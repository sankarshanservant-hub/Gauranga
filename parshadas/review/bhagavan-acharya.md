### 4. Стиль, «наш текст»
EN против OCR T (pdf 105, 168, 172–174, 181) и рабочих переводов B (079, 012, 060, 064, 040, 043): 5-словные совпадения вне цитат — переписаны:
«lived in Halisahar» → «home was Halisahar»; «married him to the daughter of Madhusudana Ghataka… had two sons» → «arranged his marriage with a
daughter… two sons were born»; «in the care of his brother-in-law» → «entrusting… to»; «younger brother, Gopala Bhattacharya, studied Vedanta in
Kashi» → перестроено; «only after Svarupa Damodara had» → «only once… had first»; «pressed his friend for two or three days» → «Two or three days
Bhagavan kept urging»; «Jagannatha is the beautiful body, and Sri Chaitanya» → «a lovely body and Sri Chaitanya its indweller»; «surrendered to the
Lord's devotees» → «gave himself up to the devotees of the Lord»; «taking it for Govardhana, ran» → «thinking it was Govardhana, rushed»; сн. 6
(«invited the Lord on a day when… as much as they served») и сн. 9 («worse than drinking poison») — перефразированы. Остальное — имена и
устойчивые сочетания. Имена — по `PERSONS.md`.
