# Chapter Eleven

*Advaita's sons: Achyutananda, Krishnadasa and Gopaladasa. Ishana's childhood*

1. Glory, glory to Sri Chaitanya! Glory to Sitanatha! Glory to Nityananda-Rama together with the devotees!
2. I shall tell briefly of the chief branches of the wish-fulfilling tree of Sri Advaita.
3. At an auspicious moment Mother Sita conceived; hearing of it, all the devotees rejoiced at heart.
4. On the full moon of the month of Vaishakha in the year 1414 of the Shaka era — the festival greatest among the festivals of the gods —
5. on that day Sita-devi gave birth to a son; the wondrous beauty of the infant enchanted everyone.
6. All said: "We have never seen such beauty! Surely some god has come to incarnate."
7. An astrologer came and, having made his calculations, said: "One of the gopis of Vraja-dhama has been born.
8. To teach the people she has taken a male form. Know for certain: from childhood he will be detached."
9. Hearing this, the devotees were filled with love and all together began the *sankirtana* of the Name.
10. By the nature of love some danced, others wept, and the Lord roared, crying: "Hari bol!"
11. Advaita's roar was like the thunder of clouds: Gauranga understood that a beloved devotee had appeared.
12. Then, for the naming of His son, the Lord in due time invited a brahmana priest.
13. The priest came and, having made his calculations, said: "This son of the Acharya is not ordinary.
14. Krishna is his mind and life, in Krishna is his bliss; therefore I name him Sri Achyutananda."
15. Hearing the name, the devotees cried "Hari!", and all the women joyfully made the "ulu-ulu" sound.
16. Sri Achyuta's love for Krishna was like that of the gopis of Vraja; the *sadhus* called him Sri Achyuta-sakhi (the girl-friend).
17. After some days, finding an auspicious moment, the Lord solemnly performed Achyuta's first rice-feeding.
18. He offered food to Madana-Gopala and, putting rice into His son's mouth, held a great festival.
19. The brahmanas, Vaishnavas and others, receiving *prasada*, clothes and cowrie shells, blessed the son.
20. When Sri Achyuta was five years old, at an auspicious moment the Lord put chalk into his hand.
21. On the very day that Sri Achyuta began his studies, my mother came to Shantipura.
22. She came and took shelter at the feet of Sri Advaita; I was then five years old.
23. The Lord in His mercy gave my mother the mantra of Krishna, and purified me by giving me the name of Hari.
24. Sita-devi, accepting me, showed me love and brought me up as her own son.
25. My mother carried out the commands of Sri Guru; some things from that time I still remember.
26. The Lord would say: "Ishana's mother is pious: in a future life she will dwell in Vaikuntha."
27. Now hear another wondrous account: how the second son of Mother Sita was born.
28. At the end of the year 1418 of the Shaka era, in the month of Madhu, on the thirteenth lunar day of the dark fortnight, at the end of the night,
29. Sita-devi gave birth to a wondrous boy of unearthly beauty, like the incarnation of a god.
30. Hear what happened at that time by the will of providence: a son was born to Sri Sri Thakurani,
31. but the infant died immediately after birth. Seeing this, Mother Shri wept aloud.
32. Mother Sita in tears said to Advaita: "My heart cannot bear my sister's grief.
33. A son had only just been born to her after so many years — and cruel fate has taken him away.
34. If I receive Your permission, this is what is in my mind: I shall give this son of mine to my sister."
35. The Lord said: "Good, good, as you wish: to relieve Shri's grief this is the best way."
36. Then Sita wiped away her tears and said: "Do not weep, do not weep, sister, be calm.
37. I truly give you this son of mine. I shall proclaim to the world that this son is yours."
38. Having said this, she placed that son in Shri's arms, and Mother Shri, giving up her grief, nursed the son at her breast.
39. No one else knows these secret events: only my mother and three others know.
40. Padmanabha Chakravarti is a vessel of the Lord's mercy: by the Lord's mercy he knows the whole truth.
41. In the morning a brahmana astrologer came and, having made his calculations, said gently:
42. "This second son of Advaitachandra has appeared to protect devotion to Krishna.
43. He who was the commander of the gods for the protection of the world of the gods — that very Six-faced One — is now Advaita's offspring."
44. Hearing this, the devotees were intoxicated with joy and, crying "Hari! Hari!", broke into dance.
45. Then in due time the Lord, inviting a priest, joyfully performed the naming ceremony.
46. The astrologer-priest, having made his calculations, said: "He will become a great scholar and conquer all.
47. He will be devoted to the service of Krishna and the Vaishnavas, and detached; therefore I name him Krishnadasa."
48. Hearing this, the devotees rejoiced still more and spent the day in the bliss of the *sankirtana* of Hari.
49. After some days, seeing auspicious signs, the Lord performed Sri Krishnadasa's first rice-feeding.
50. He first offered food to Sri Madana-Gopala and, giving His son the *maha-prasada*, performed the rite of the first rice.
51. With devotion He fed the brahmanas and Vaishnavas and generously distributed food to the blind and the destitute.
52. Distributing clothes and cowries, He courteously greeted everyone, and they, giving their blessings, went to their homes.
53. Then Sri Advaita, at an auspicious time, began Sri Krishnadasa's education.
54. Hear, all, another wondrous event: how the third son of the Lord appeared.
55. In the month of Karttika of the year 1422 of the Shaka era, on the twelfth lunar day of the bright fortnight, Sita gave birth to a son.
56. Behold the wonder of the infant right after his birth: he lay with closed eyes, as if dead.
57. Seeing this, my Lord, crying "Gaura Hari!", roared like a mighty lion.
58. The name "Gaura Hari" entered the infant's ears — and, shedding tears of love, he opened his eyes.
59. Seeing this, all in the bliss of love cried out the name of Hari, and all the women of the family made the "ulu-ulu" sound.
60. At that time a brahmana astrologer came there and, having calculated, told the truth about the newborn:
61. "This is the third son of Advaitachandra: Sri Ganesha himself abides in him.
62. He has come to destroy the obstacles on earth: seeing him, souls will obtain the treasure of devotion."
63. Hearing this, the devotees rejoiced still more and spent the day in the *sankirtana* of Hari.
64. Then, inviting a priest, the Lord entrusted him with naming the son.
65. The brahmana said: "He will be a servant of Sri Krishna; therefore I name him Sri Gopaladasa."
66. Now hear of Gopala's superhuman nature: by hearing of it a soul obtains devotion to Krishna.
67. When the devotees sang the *sankirtana* of the Name, Gopala would leave his milk and listen.
68. He shed tears and laughed merrily, again and again rolling his eyes, as if intoxicated.
69. When the *sankirtana* stopped, this state passed; then he cried loudly and at last drank his mother's milk.
70. Such was also the constant nature of Krishnadasa. This is accessible to the wise but unknown to the ignorant.
71. Of these births of the Lord's three sons I have told only briefly, for the good of the souls.
72. Placing his hope in the feet of Sri Chaitanya and Sri Advaita, Ishana Nagara tells the *Advaita-prakasha*.

---

**Translator's notes**

- V. 4: the year 1414 of the Shaka era corresponds to 1492 CE.
- Vv. 21–26: the author himself, Ishana Nagara, tells of his childhood in Advaita's house.
- V. 28: Shaka 1418 is 1496/97 CE; the month of Madhu is Chaitra.
- V. 43: the Six-faced One is Karttikeya (Skanda), the commander of the gods.
- V. 55: Shaka 1422 is 1500 CE.
