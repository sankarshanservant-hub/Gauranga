# Chapter One

*Shiva's austerities for the good of the world. The birth of Sri Advaita*

1. Glory, glory to Sri Chaitanya! Glory to Sitanatha! Glory, glory to Nityananda-Rama together with all the devotees!
2. Seeing how sinful the terrible age of Kali was, the Five-faced One (Shiva) began to ponder in his mind: "How shall I deliver the living beings?"
3. He took long counsel with Yogamaya and understood: no one but Hari can deliver the souls.
4. Having said this, Sadashiva, his mind full of eternal bliss, came to the shore of the Causal Ocean.
5. Sitting in a yogic posture, the great yogi began his austerities, and seven hundred years passed in yoga.
6. Pleased with these severe austerities, the Creator of the universe, Maha-Vishnu, revealed Himself to him.
7. Beholding Narayana face to face, the Five-faced One offered Him praises so manifold that they cannot be told.
8. Maha-Vishnu said: "You are not someone other. You and I have one soul — only the bodies are different."
9. Having said this, He embraced the Five-faced One — and the two bodies became one. Who can fathom His purpose?
10. Hear, all of you, what a great wonder took place: the body became the colour of pure gold, shining and radiant.
11. Crying "Krishna, Krishna!", the Lord gave thunderous roars. And then a voice came from the heavens — a wonder of wonders:
12. "Hear, Maha-Vishnu: in this very form descend first in the womb of Labha.
13. Afterwards I Myself shall descend in Nadia — in the house of Shachi and Jagannatha you will see Me.
14. Balarama and all the other devotees — all will take birth to deliver the souls."
15. Hearing this, Maha-Vishnu separated from Shiva, went to Shantipura and entered the womb of Labha.
16. Labha-devi was an ascetic, chaste and righteous — the wife of Kubera Acharya, who bore the title Tarka-panchanana.
17. Once Kubera, the lord of wealth, full of love, performed many austerities and repeated mantras, longing for a son like Shiva.
18. Pleased with his austerities, Shiva said, "So be it." That is why Kubera was born on earth.
19. His name was Sriman Kubera Acharya; by his righteousness and learning he became honoured by all.
20. I have no power to describe his virtues. People sing of him as a descendant of Nrisimha —
21. that Narasimha who was famous as Nariyala, of the Ojha family, perfect *shrotriyas* (knowers of the Vedas).
22. The fame of that Narasimha resounded in the three worlds; he was a great scholar in all the shastras and exceedingly discerning.
23. By the strength of his counsel King Sri Ganesha killed the Badshah of Gauda and himself became king of Gauda.
24. From the marriage of his daughter arose a (particular) caste; he lived in the land of Lauda.
25. The light of that family was Sri Kubera Acharya; at the capital he served as court pandit.
26. After his marriage many sons were born to him one after another, but all the sons died — and detachment awoke in him.
27. Then he came to beautiful Shantipura on the bank of the Ganga and lived there for some time with Labha.
28. One day Sri Kubera Tarka-panchanana recognized from Labha's appearance the signs of pregnancy.
29. He performed worship of Narayana with various offerings and fed brahmanas, the poor and the blind.
30. At that time Kubera received a letter from the king and set out for his homeland with his wife.
31. He lived in Navagrama in Lauda, where King Divya Simha ruled.
32. And so Kubera came with his wife to Navagrama, and the people of the village paid him honour.
33. Seeing the *acharya* after a long time, the king bowed to him and with joy in his heart asked after his welfare.
34. The king said: "Tell me, tell me, Tarka-panchanana, your good news — and why you were delayed so long.
35. Your company is a mine of joy for me. Without you I consider my kingdom empty."
36. The *acharya* replied: "Your Majesty, you are an ocean of mercy and are ever merciful to a poor brahmana.
37. The bank of the Ganga is holy land, a most beautiful place; to live there is the same as living in heaven.
38. My heart did not wish to leave, but I have come in obedience to your command.
39. By the mercy of God a child has again been conceived — as if the fruit of unseen destiny were taking form."
40. The king said: "The conception took place in a holy place. Truly I foresee that good will come of it.
41. Forget your former grief and call upon God: by His mercy an extraordinary child will be born."
42. So the two talked at length, and at that time a brahmana astrologer arrived.
43. The astrologer said: "Listen, venerable pandit: without doubt you will obtain a son like a god.
44. He will be long-lived, a knower of *dharma* and the shastras; I see in him the power to preach the pure *dharma*."
45. Having said this, the astrologer vanished. The king, rejoicing, looked for him but could not find him.
46. All in amazement said to one another: "Surely that was a god, an *avatara*."
47. The *acharya* was gladdened by the astrologer's words; returning home, he told Labha everything as it was.
48. Labha said: "Boundless is the glory of God. When He is merciful, no burden of sorrow remains.
49. Whoever worships Vishnu with devotion fares well in everything — so say the saints."
50. Hearing this, the *acharya*, who possessed pure knowledge, said: "Dear one, this is true, attested by the Vedas.
51. By the worship of Vishnu all the gods are worshipped; all perfections are attained and the bonds of *maya* are cut."
52. And then Kubera, with devotion, with various offerings and great pomp, performed the worship of Narayana.
53. He fed the brahmanas with Vishnu's *prasada* and gave clothing to the blind, the afflicted and the destitute.
54. Hear now a wondrous story: one day, at the end of the night, Labha Thakurani had a dream.
55. In the lotus of her heart she saw the form of Hari-Hara; all the directions were lit up by the radiance of His body.
56. In the sweetest voice He sang the *sankirtana* of Hari; raising His arms, He danced and wept, unaware of the outer world.
57. Crying "Hare Krishna!", He gave thunderous roars. Hearing this, the son of the Sun (Yama) came there.
58. Coming, Yamaraja beheld the sweetness of that form: Hari and Hara in one body, like Hara and Gauri.
59. The radiance of His body, more beautiful than millions of suns — who has the power to describe such a form?
60. On His lips "Hare Krishna," on His body the thrill of ecstasy; tears flowed ceaselessly, like the heavenly Ganga.
61. His body swayed with pure love; again and again He broke into dance, and the waves of love rose ever higher.
62. Beholding this unearthly state, the son of the Sun prostrated himself with the eight limbs of his body.
63. He offered many praises that cannot be told and, joining his palms, spoke sweet words:
64. "Listen, Lord: this is the dark age of Kali. Your descent in it is a wondrous thing.
65. By seeing You sinners will obtain deliverance — then my authority will come to an end.
66. Therefore, Lord, conceal Yourself; be merciful to Your servant and free me from this calamity."
67. Hearing this, the Lord smiled slightly and said to Yama: "Be calm, Dharmaraja, you have fallen into error.
68. You give no thought to the terrible sufferings of the sinners, yet the wise *sadhu* grieves at the grief of others.
69. You may say: the soul reaps the fruit of its own deeds. But who has the power to destroy the bonds of *karma*?
70. The soul covered by *maya* does not know its own good and considers the paltry pleasures of the external senses to be its good.
71. As a sick man suffers from eating harmful food, so one attached to the world is caught in the bonds of *karma*.
72. Especially in Kali, souls live as they please and toss about in the forest fire of terrible sufferings.
73. Seeing the unbearable torments of the souls, My mind found no rest — and at last I made this vow:
74. This great mantra destroys the bonds of *karma*; it is the means that gives rise to pure loving devotion to Krishna.
75. Teaching the souls this spiritual Name of Hari and imparting power to them, I shall deliver the sinners.
76. For this I have now taken birth. The saints will sing: 'Blessed is the age of Kali!'
77. And I have one more firm vow: I shall surely make Bhagavan Himself appear.
78. Mahaprabhu will descend with His associates and intimates, and the land will be filled with a flood of pure love.
79. But even then your authority will not cease: the blasphemers and hypocrites will not be delivered."
80. Hearing this, Yamaraja went to his own abode. The dream broke, and Labha-devi awoke and sat up.
81. She told the pandit of the wondrous dream; hearing it, he was amazed at heart.
82. Then ten months passed for the chaste Labha-devi, and on the seventh day of the month of Magha the Lord appeared.
83. On that auspicious day people bathed, gave in charity and cried out the name of Hari; all the women made the "hulu-hulu" sound.
84. Deep bliss rose in the hearts of the saints, but why — they did not understand.
85. In due time Kubera summoned an astrologer, who, having chosen, gave Him the name Kamalaksha (Lotus-eyed).
86. Behold the Lord's wonder at the age of five: He would eat no food except what had been offered to Sri Krishna.
87. At an auspicious moment the best of the twice-born put chalk in His hand, and in one month the Lord learned the whole alphabet.
88. Placing his hope in the feet of Sri Gauranga and Sri Advaita, Ishana Nagara tells the *Advaita-prakasha*.

**Translator's notes**

- *The Five-faced One* (Panchanana) is an epithet of Shiva.
- *Hari-Hara* — the combined form of Vishnu (Hari) and Shiva (Hara); according to the text, Advaita is the incarnation of Maha-Vishnu united with Shiva. In Gaudiya Vaishnava theology Advaita Acharya is Maha-Vishnu Himself, and Sadashiva is non-different from Him (cf. *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* 76; CC Adi 6).
- *Sitanatha*, "the husband of Sita," is a name of Advaita Acharya.
- V. 24: an obscure place in the Bengali original (the OCR gives *kāper*); the Hindi editor interprets it as the origin of the caste of Varendra brahmanas.
- V. 82: the Hindi edition adds a date — the bright fortnight of the month of Magha, "samvat 1491"; in the OCR the number is distorted.
