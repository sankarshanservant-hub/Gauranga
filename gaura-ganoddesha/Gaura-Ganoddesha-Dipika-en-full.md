# Sri Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika

*"The Lamp that Points Out the Associates of Gaura"*

*Kavi Karnapura (1576)*

*Translated from the Sanskrit*

*Sanskrit text, transliteration and word-for-word translation*

---

## Translator's note

The *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* is a Sanskrit work by Kavi Karnapura (Paramananda Dasa, Puridasa), the
youngest son of Shivananda Sena, one of the closest associates of Sri Chaitanya. According to its last
verse, the book was completed in Shaka 1498 (1576 CE). It enumerates the associates of Mahaprabhu, of
Nityananda and of Advaita, and states who each of them was in Krishna's pastimes in Vraja (and also in
Dvaraka, Mathura, Ayodhya and the heavens). It is the principal source of the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition
on the "former identities" of Chaitanya's associates.

**Source.** The Sanskrit text is taken from the edition of Gaudiya Vedanta Publications (2008); its text
layer, in the Kruti Dev font encoding, was converted into Unicode. **Only Kavi Karnapura's Sanskrit text
has been translated**; the edition's Hindi translation and commentary were not used and are not
reproduced. The verse numbering follows that edition (in it verses 10–22 and 194–207 are combined into
groups). Places where the text needed explanation are noted in the footnotes.

**Verification.** After the first translation the Sanskrit text was checked: conversion errors were
corrected by metre, grammar and the page images of the edition (passages that seemed incomplete were
checked in the same way, and almost all of them proved to be whole in the edition itself). The
translation of every verse was then compared afresh with the corrected text, and errors were corrected —
among them in the identifications (Shankara Ghosha and Shankara Pandita rather than "Shankha," Arjuna →
the gopi Arjuniya, Halayudha = Prabala, Gunamala = Damayanti, Vasudha and Jahnava = Varuni and Revati) and in
the chain of succession from Madhva (v. 22). The English translation was made from the Sanskrit, in
agreement with the Russian translation (same numbering and notes).

---

**Full version.** For each verse the Sanskrit text in Devanagari is given (corrected: errors of the
conversion from the Kruti Dev font have been fixed by metre, grammar and the page images of the edition),
followed by an IAST transliteration, a word-for-word translation and the translation.

## Contents

- Part One — Benediction. The Pancha-tattva. Navadvipa. The succession from Madhva. Mahaprabhu's relatives and teachers (verses 1–57)
- Part Two — Nityananda and the vyuhas. Advaita Acharya and his family. Srivasa and "those called devotees." The sannyasi associates. Puri: Prataparudra, Sarvabhauma, Ramananda (verses 58–123)
- Part Three — The cowherd friends of Vraja. Servants and musicians. The gopis: Gadadhara and Lalita, the eight sakhis and others. The manjaris and the Gosvamis of Vrindavana. Conclusion (verses 124–215)

# Part One

*Benediction. The Pancha-tattva. Navadvipa. The succession from Madhva. Mahaprabhu's relatives and teachers (verses 1–57)*

## Mangalacharana

### Verse 1

यः श्रीवृन्दावनभुवि पुरा सच्चिदानन्दसान्द्रो
गौराङ्गीभिः सदृशरुचिभिः श्यामधामा ननर्त्त।
तासां शश्वद्दृढतरपरीरम्भसम्भेदतः किम्
गौराङ्गः सन् जयति स नवद्वीपमालम्बमानः॥१॥

*yaḥ śrīvṛndāvanabhuvi purā saccidānandasāndro*
*gaurāṅgībhiḥ sadṛśarucibhiḥ śyāmadhāmā nanartta|*
*tāsāṁ śaśvaddṛḍhataraparīrambhasambhedataḥ kim*
*gaurāṅgaḥ san jayati sa navadvīpamālambamānaḥ||1||*

*Word for word:* yah — who; shri-vrindavana-bhuvi — in the land of Sri Vrindavana; pura — once; sac-cid-ananda-sandrah — the concentrated essence of being, consciousness and bliss; gaurangibhih — with golden-limbed (maidens); sadrisha-rucibhih — equal (to Him) in lustre; shyama-dhama — the abode of dark splendour; nanartta — danced; tasam — their; shashvat — eternal; dridha-tara — ever tighter; parirambha — embraces; sambhedatah — from the contact; kim — was it not (from that); gaurangah — golden-limbed; san — becoming; jayati — be glorious; sah — He; navadvipam — Navadvipa; alambamanah — resting in (dwelling in).

**1.** He who once, in the land of Vrindavana — the concentrated essence of being, consciousness and bliss, an abode of dark splendour — danced with golden-limbed maidens equal to Him in lustre; and who, perhaps because of their eternal and ever tighter embraces, Himself became golden — that Gauranga, who dwells in Navadvipa, be glorious!

### Verse 2

नमस्यामोऽस्यैव प्रियपरिजनान् वत्सलहृदः
प्रभोरद्वैतादीनपि जगदघौघक्षयकृतः।
समानप्रेमाणः समगुणगणास्तुल्यकरुणाः
स्वरूपाद्या येऽमी सरसमधुरास्तानपि नुमः॥२॥

*namasyāmo'syaiva priyaparijanān vatsalahṛdaḥ*
*prabhoradvaitādīnapi jagadaghaughakṣayakṛtaḥ|*
*samānapremāṇaḥ samaguṇagaṇāstulyakaruṇāḥ*
*svarūpādyā ye'mī sarasamadhurāstānapi numaḥ||2||*

*Word for word:* namasyamah — we bow; asya eva — of this very; priya-parijanan — to the beloved associates; vatsala-hridah — tender-hearted; prabhoh — of the Lord; advaita-adin — Advaita and the others; api — also; jagat-agha-augha-kshaya-kritah — who destroy the floods of the world's sins; samana-premanah — equal in love; sama-guna-ganah — equal in their host of qualities; tulya-karunah — equal in compassion; svarupa-adyah — Svarupa and the others; ye — who; ami — these; sarasa-madhurah — full of rasa and sweetness; tan — them; api — also; numah — we praise.

**2.** We bow to the beloved, tender-hearted associates of this Lord — Advaita and the others, who destroy the floods of the world's sins; and we praise also those — Svarupa and the rest — who are equal to them in love, equal in qualities and equal in compassion, full of *rasa* and sweetness.

### Verse 3

गुरुं नः श्रीनाथाभिधमवनिदेवान्वयविधुम्
नुमो भूषारत्नं भुव इव विभोरस्य दयितम्।
यदास्यादुन्मीलन्निरवकर-वृन्दावनरहः-
कथास्वादं लब्ध्वा जगति न जनः कोऽपि रमते॥३॥

*guruṁ naḥ śrīnāthābhidhamavanidevānvayavidhum*
*numo bhūṣāratnaṁ bhuva iva vibhorasya dayitam|*
*yadāsyādunmīlanniravakara-vṛndāvanarahaḥ-*
*kathāsvādaṁ labdhvā jagati na janaḥ ko'pi ramate||3||*

*Word for word:* gurum — the guru; nah — our; shrinatha-abhidham — named Srinatha; avani-deva-anvaya-vidhum — the moon in the line of the brahmanas ("gods of the earth"); numah — we bow to; bhusha-ratnam — a jewelled ornament; bhuvah — of the earth; iva — as it were; vibhoh — of the Lord; asya — of this; dayitam — the beloved; yat-asyat — from whose mouth; unmilan — coming forth; niravakara — pure, unalloyed (lit. "without refuse"); vrindavana-rahah-katha-asvadam — the taste of talks of the secrets of Vrindavana; labdhva — having obtained; jagati — in the world; na — not; janah ko 'pi — anyone; ramate — delights (in anything else).

**3.** We bow to our guru named Srinatha,[^01-1] a moon in the line of the brahmanas, a jewelled ornament of the earth as it were, dear to the Lord: having tasted the pure, unalloyed talks of the secret pastimes of Vrindavana that pour from his lips, no one in the world delights in anything else.

### Verse 4

पितरं श्रीशिवानन्दं सेनवंशप्रदीपकम्।
वन्देऽहं परया भक्त्या पार्षदाग्र्यं महाप्रभोः॥४॥

*pitaraṁ śrīśivānandaṁ senavaṁśapradīpakam|*
*vande'haṁ parayā bhaktyā pārṣadāgryaṁ mahāprabhoḥ||4||*

*Word for word:* pitaram — father; shri-shivanandam — Sri Shivananda; sena-vamsha-pradipakam — the lamp of the Sena family; vande — worship; aham — I; paraya bhaktya — with supreme devotion; parshada-agryam — the foremost of the associates; mahaprabhoh — of Mahaprabhu.

**4.** With supreme devotion I worship my father, Sri Shivananda, the lamp of the Sena family, foremost among the associates of Mahaprabhu.

## The sources of this book

### Verse 5

ये विख्याताः परिवाराः श्रीचैतन्यमहाप्रभोः।
नित्यानन्दाद्वैतयोश्च तेषामपि महीयसाम्।
गोपालानाञ्च पूर्वाणि नामानि यानि कानिचित्।
स्वस्वग्रन्थे स्वरूपाद्यैर्दर्शितान्यादिसूरिभिः।
विलोक्यान्यानि साधूनां मथुरौड्रनिवासिनाम्।
गौडीयानामपि मुखान्निशम्य स्वमनीषया।
विविच्याम्रेडितः कैश्चित् कैश्चित्तानि लिखाम्यहम्।
नाम्ना श्रीपरमानन्ददासः सेवितशासनः॥५॥

*ye vikhyātāḥ parivārāḥ śrīcaitanyamahāprabhoḥ|*
*nityānandādvaitayośca teṣāmapi mahīyasām|*
*gopālānāñca pūrvāṇi nāmāni yāni kānicit|*
*svasvagranthe svarūpādyairdarśitānyādisūribhiḥ|*
*vilokyānyāni sādhūnāṁ mathurauḍranivāsinām|*
*gauḍīyānāmapi mukhānniśamya svamanīṣayā|*
*vivicyāmreḍitaḥ kaiścit kaiścittāni likhāmyaham|*
*nāmnā śrīparamānandadāsaḥ sevitaśāsanaḥ||5||*

*Word for word:* ye — who; vikhyatah — famous; parivarah — associates; shri-caitanya-mahaprabhoh — of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu; nityananda-advaitayoh — of Nityananda and Advaita; ca — and; tesham — of them; api — also; mahiyasam — great; gopalanam — of the cowherds; ca — and; purvani — former; namani — names; yani kanicit — some; sva-sva-granthe — each in his own book; svarupa-adyaih — by Svarupa and others; darshitani — shown; adi-suribhih — by the first sages; vilokya — having studied; anyani — others; sadhunam — of the saints; mathura-audra-nivasinam — living in Mathura and Odra (Orissa); gaudiyanam — of the Gaudiyas; api — also; mukhat — from the lips; nishamya — having heard; sva-manishaya — by my own understanding; vivicya — having considered; amreditah — repeatedly asked; kaishcit — by some; kaishcit — some (of them); tani — them; likhami — write; aham — I; namna — by name; shri-paramananda-dasah — Sri Paramananda Dasa; sevita-shasanah — carrying out the command.

**5.** The famous associates of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, and those of the great Nityananda and Advaita too, and some of the former names of the cowherds which the first sages — Svarupa and others — pointed out each in his own book: having studied these, and having heard others from the lips of saints living in Mathura, in Odra (Orissa) and in Gauda, having considered them with my own understanding, and being repeatedly asked by some — I, named Sri Paramananda Dasa, carrying out the command given to me, write them down.

## The beginning

### Verse 6

यद्वत्पुरा कृष्णचन्द्रः पञ्चतत्त्वात्मकोऽपि सन्।
यातः प्रकटतां तद्वद्गौरः प्रकटतामियात्॥६॥

*yadvatpurā kṛṣṇacandraḥ pañcatattvātmako'pi san|*
*yātaḥ prakaṭatāṁ tadvadgauraḥ prakaṭatāmiyāt||6||*

*Word for word:* yadvat — as; pura — once; krishnacandrah — Krishnachandra; panca-tattva-atmakah — fivefold in nature; api san — being; yatah — came; prakatatam — into manifestation; tadvat — so; gaurah — Gaura; prakatatam — manifestation; iyat — attained.

**6.** As once Krishnachandra, being fivefold in nature (*pancha-tattva*), became manifest in the world, so Gaura too became manifest.

### Verse 7

स्वाभिन्नेन युतः तत्त्वं पञ्चतत्त्वमिहोच्यते।
अन्यथा तदसम्बन्धात्तत्तत्त्वं स्याच्चतुष्टयम्॥७॥

*svābhinnena yutaḥ tattvaṁ pañcatattvamihocyate|*
*anyathā tadasambandhāttattattvaṁ syāccatuṣṭayam||7||*

*Word for word:* sva-abhinnena — with what is non-different from Itself; yutah — joined; tattvam — the Truth; panca-tattvam — the pancha-tattva; iha — here; ucyate — is called; anyatha — otherwise; tat-asambandhat — through absence of connection with It; tat tattvam — that truth; syat — would be; catushtayam — a set of four.

**7.** The Truth joined with what is non-different from Itself is here called the *pancha-tattva*. Otherwise, were there no such connection, the truths would be a set of four.[^01-2]

### Verse 8

तद्भिन्नं यत्तदेवात्र तदभिन्नं विभाव्यताम्।
यतः स्वेच्छया शक्त्या कृष्णस्तादृशतां गतः॥८॥

*tadbhinnaṁ yattadevātra tadabhinnaṁ vibhāvyatām|*
*yataḥ svecchayā śaktyā kṛṣṇastādṛśatāṁ gataḥ||8||*

*Word for word:* tat-bhinnam — different from Him; yat — what; tat eva — that very thing; atra — here; tat-abhinnam — as non-different from Him; vibhavyatam — let it be understood; yatah — because; sva-icchaya — by His own will; shaktya — by potency; krishnah — Krishna; tadrishatam — such a nature; gatah — has assumed.

**8.** What is different from Him should here be understood as non-different from Him, for Krishna by His own will, by His own potency, has assumed such a form.

### Verse 9

अतः स्वरूपचरणैरुक्तं तत्त्वनिरूपणे।
उपाधिभेदात् पञ्चत्वं तत्त्वस्येह प्रदर्श्यते॥९॥

*ataḥ svarūpacaraṇairuktaṁ tattvanirūpaṇe|*
*upādhibhedāt pañcatvaṁ tattvasyeha pradarśyate||9||*

*Word for word:* atah — therefore; svarupa-caranaih — by the feet of Svarupa; uktam — it is said; tattva-nirupane — in the Tattva-nirupana ("Description of the Truth"); upadhi-bhedat — by difference of designations; pancatvam — fivefoldness; tattvasya — of the Truth; iha — here; pradarshyate — is shown.

**9.** Therefore the feet of Svarupa[^01-3] in the *Tattva-nirupana* ("Description of the Truth") have shown the fivefoldness of the one Truth according to the difference of its designations:

### Verse 10

“पञ्चतत्त्वात्मकं कृष्णं भक्तरूपस्वरूपकम्।
भक्तावतारं भक्ताख्यं नमामि भक्तशक्तिकम्॥”१०॥

*“pañcatattvātmakaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ bhaktarūpasvarūpakam|*
*bhaktāvatāraṁ bhaktākhyaṁ namāmi bhaktaśaktikam||”10||*

*Word for word:* panca-tattva-atmakam — fivefold in nature; krishnam — to Krishna; bhakta-rupa-svarupakam — the devotee in form and the devotee in essence; bhakta-avataram — the incarnation of the devotee; bhakta-akhyam — the one called a devotee; namami — I bow; bhakta-shaktikam — the potency of the devotee.

**10.** "I bow to Krishna, fivefold in nature: the devotee in form, the devotee in essence, the incarnation of the devotee, the one called a devotee, and the potency of the devotee."

### Verse 11

अस्यार्थो विवृतस्तैर्यः स संक्षिप्य विलिख्यते।
भक्तरूपो गौरचन्द्रो यतोऽसौ नन्दनन्दनः।
भक्तस्वरूपो नित्यानन्दो व्रजे यः श्रीहलायुधः।
भक्तावतार आचार्योऽद्वैतो यः श्रीसदाशिवः।
भक्ताख्याः श्रीनिवासाद्या यतस्ते भक्तरूपिणः।
भक्तशक्तिर्द्विजाग्रण्यः श्रीगदाधरपण्डितः॥११॥

*asyārtho vivṛtastairyaḥ sa saṁkṣipya vilikhyate|*
*bhaktarūpo gauracandro yato'sau nandanandanaḥ|*
*bhaktasvarūpo nityānando vraje yaḥ śrīhalāyudhaḥ|*
*bhaktāvatāra ācāryo'dvaito yaḥ śrīsadāśivaḥ|*
*bhaktākhyāḥ śrīnivāsādyā yataste bhaktarūpiṇaḥ|*
*bhaktaśaktirdvijāgraṇyaḥ śrīgadādharapaṇḍitaḥ||11||*

*Word for word:* asya — of this; arthah — the meaning; vivritah — explained; taih — by him (Svarupa); yah — which; sah — that; sankshipya — abridging; vilikhyate — is written; bhakta-rupah — the devotee in form; gauracandrah — Gaurachandra; yatah — because; asau — He; nanda-nandanah — the son of Nanda; bhakta-svarupah — the devotee in essence; nityanandah — Nityananda; vraje — in Vraja; yah — who; shri-halayudhah — Sri Halayudha; bhakta-avatarah — the incarnation of the devotee; acaryah advaitah — Advaita Acharya; yah — who; shri-sadashivah — Sri Sadashiva; bhakta-akhyah — those called devotees; shrinivasa-adyah — Srinivasa (Srivasa) and the others; yatah — because; te — they; bhakta-rupinah — having the form of devotees; bhakta-shaktih — the potency of the devotee; dvija-agranyah — the foremost of brahmanas; shri-gadadhara-panditah — Sri Gadadhara Pandita.

**11.** The meaning of this, as he explained it, I set down in brief. Gaurachandra is "the devotee in form," for He is the son of Nanda. Nityananda, who in Vraja is Sri Halayudha,[^01-4] is "the devotee in essence." Advaita Acharya, who is Sri Sadashiva, is "the incarnation of the devotee." Srivasa and the others are "those called devotees," for they have the form of devotees. And "the potency of the devotee" is the foremost of brahmanas, Sri Gadadhara Pandita.

### Verse 12

श्रीमद्विश्वम्भराद्वैतनित्यानन्दावधूतकाः।
अत्र त्रयः समुन्नेया विग्रहाः प्रभवश्च ते।
एको महाप्रभुर्ज्ञेयः श्रीचैतन्यो दयाम्बुधिः।
प्रभू द्वौ श्रीयुतौ नित्यानन्दाद्वैतौ महाशयौ।
गोस्वामिनो विग्रहाश्च ते द्विजश्च गदाधरः।
पञ्चतत्त्वात्मका एते श्रीनिवासश्च पण्डितः॥१२॥

*śrīmadviśvambharādvaitanityānandāvadhūtakāḥ|*
*atra trayaḥ samunneyā vigrahāḥ prabhavaśca te|*
*eko mahāprabhurjñeyaḥ śrīcaitanyo dayāmbudhiḥ|*
*prabhū dvau śrīyutau nityānandādvaitau mahāśayau|*
*gosvāmino vigrahāśca te dvijaśca gadādharaḥ|*
*pañcatattvātmakā ete śrīnivāsaśca paṇḍitaḥ||12||*

*Word for word:* shrimat-vishvambhara-advaita-nityananda-avadhutakah — Srimad Vishvambhara, Advaita and Nityananda Avadhuta; atra — here; trayah — three; samunneyah — are to be understood; vigrahah — as the Vigrahas; prabhavah — the Prabhus; ca — and; te — they; ekah — one; mahaprabhuh — Mahaprabhu; jneyah — is to be known; shri-caitanyah — Sri Chaitanya; daya-ambudhih — the ocean of mercy; prabhu dvau — the two Prabhus; shriyutau — glorious; nityananda-advaitau — Nityananda and Advaita; maha-ashayau — great-souled; gosvaminah — the Gosvamis; vigrahah — the Vigrahas; ca — and; te — they; dvijah — the brahmana; ca — and; gadadharah — Gadadhara; panca-tattva-atmakah — constituting the pancha-tattva; ete — these; shrinivasah — Srinivasa; ca — and; panditah — Pandita.

**12.** Srimad Vishvambhara, Advaita and Nityananda Avadhuta — these three are here to be understood as the *Vigrahas* and as the *Prabhus*. The one Mahaprabhu is Sri Chaitanya, the ocean of mercy; the two Prabhus are the glorious Nityananda and Advaita, the great-souled. They are the Gosvamis and the Vigrahas; and together with the brahmana Gadadhara and Srinivasa Pandita they constitute the *pancha-tattva*.

### Verse 13

यदुक्तं तत्र गोस्वामिश्रीस्वरूपपदाम्बुजैः।
“त्रयोऽत्र विग्रहा ज्ञेयाः प्रभवश्चात्र ते त्रयः।
एको महाप्रभुर्ज्ञेयो द्वौ प्रभू सम्मतौ सताम्॥”१३॥

*yaduktaṁ tatra gosvāmiśrīsvarūpapadāmbujaiḥ|*
*“trayo'tra vigrahā jñeyāḥ prabhavaścātra te trayaḥ|*

*Word for word:* yat — as; uktam — it is said; tatra — on this; gosvami-shri-svarupa-pada-ambujaih — by the lotus feet of Gosvami Sri Svarupa; trayah — three; atra — here; vigrahah — Vigrahas; jneyah — are to be known; prabhavah — Prabhus; ca — and; atra — here; te trayah — these three; ekah — one; mahaprabhuh — Mahaprabhu; jneyah — is acknowledged; dvau — two; prabhu — Prabhus; sammatau — accepted; satam — by the saints.

अद्वैतः कलयामास दास्यसख्ये फले उभे।
श्रीमान् रङ्गपुरी ह्येष वात्सल्ये यः समाश्रितः॥२४॥

*advaitaḥ kalayāmāsa dāsyasakhye phale ubhe|*
*śrīmān raṅgapurī hyeṣa vātsalye yaḥ samāśritaḥ||24||*

*Word for word:* advaitah — Advaita; kalayamasa — manifested; dasya-sakhye — service and friendship; phale — the fruits; ubhe — both; shriman — Sriman; rangapuri — Rangapuri; hi — and; eshah — he; vatsalye — in parental love; yah — who; samashritah — took shelter.

**24.** Advaita manifested both fruits, service and friendship; and Sriman Rangapuri took shelter in parental love.

### Verse 25

ईश्वराख्यपुरीं गौर उररीकृत्य गौरवे।
जगदाप्लावयामास प्राकृताप्राकृतात्मकम्॥२५॥

*īśvarākhyapurīṁ gaura urarīkṛtya gaurave|*
*jagadāplāvayāmāsa prākṛtāprākṛtātmakam||25||*

*Word for word:* ishvara-akhya-purim — the Puri named Ishvara; gaurah — Gaura; urarikritya — accepting; gaurave — with reverence (as guru); jagat — the world; aplavayamasa — flooded; prakrita-aprakrita-atmakam — both material and spiritual.

**25.** Gaura, accepting Ishvara Puri with reverence as His guru, flooded the world, both material and spiritual.

## Gaura, "the devotee in form"

### Verse 26

स्वीकृत्य राधिकाभावकान्तिं पूर्वसुदुष्करे।
अन्तर्बहीरसाम्भोधिः श्रीनन्दनन्दनोऽपि सन्॥२६॥

*svīkṛtya rādhikābhāvakāntiṁ pūrvasuduṣkare|*
*antarbahīrasāmbhodhiḥ śrīnandanandano'pi san||26||*

*Word for word:* svikritya — accepting; radhika-bhava-kantim — the mood and lustre of Radhika; purva-sudushkare — formerly most difficult to attain; antar-bahih — within and without; rasa-ambhodhih — an ocean of rasa; shri-nanda-nandanah — Sri Nandanandana; api san — though being.

**26.** Although He is Sri Nandanandana Himself, an ocean of *rasa* within and without, He accepted the mood and the lustre of Radhika, which had never before been attained.

### Verse 27

आद्यव्यूहोऽपि चैतन्यमविशत् यः पुरे पुरा।
विचुक्षोभ मनस्तस्य दृष्ट्वा गन्धर्वनर्त्तनम्॥२७॥

*ādyavyūho'pi caitanyamaviśat yaḥ pure purā|*
*vicukṣobha manastasya dṛṣṭvā gandharvanarttanam||27||*

*Word for word:* adya-vyuhah — the original vyuha; api — also; caitanyam — into Chaitanya; avishat — entered; yah — who (whose); pure — in the city; pura — once; vicukshobha — was stirred; manah — the mind; tasya — his; drishtva — on seeing; gandharva-narttanam — the dance of the Gandharvas.

**27.** The original *vyuha*,[^01-9] whose mind was once stirred in His city on seeing the dance of the Gandharvas, also entered into Chaitanya.

### Verse 28

द्वारकास्थोऽपि भगवानविशत् श्रीशचीसुतम्।
नानावतारः सुतरामेककालप्रभावतः॥२८॥

*dvārakāstho'pi bhagavānaviśat śrīśacīsutam|*
*nānāvatāraḥ sutarāmekakālaprabhāvataḥ||28||*

*Word for word:* dvaraka-sthah — dwelling in Dvaraka; api — also; bhagavan — Bhagavan; avishat — entered; shri-shaci-sutam — the son of Sri Shachi; nana-avatarah — the many incarnations; sutaram — surely; eka-kala-prabhavatah — by the power of simultaneity.

**28.** The Lord (Bhagavan) who dwells in Dvaraka also entered the son of Shachi, and so, by the power of simultaneity, did the many incarnations —

### Verse 29

यथा श्यामोऽविशत् कृष्णं भगवन्तं पुरा स्वयम्॥२९॥

*yathā śyāmo'viśat kṛṣṇaṁ bhagavantaṁ purā svayam||29||*

*Word for word:* yatha — as; shyamah — Shyama; avishat — entered; krishnam — Krishna; bhagavantam — Bhagavan; pura — once; svayam — Himself.

**29.** just as once Shyama[^01-10] entered into Bhagavan Krishna Himself.

### Verse 30

योगमाया-बलादेते तिष्ठन्तोऽन्यत्र यद्यपि।
तथापि प्राविशन् गौरेऽचिन्त्यलक्षणलक्षिताः॥३०॥

*yogamāyā-balādete tiṣṭhanto'nyatra yadyapi|*
*tathāpi prāviśan gaure'cintyalakṣaṇalakṣitāḥ||30||*

*Word for word:* yogamaya-balat — by the power of Yogamaya; ete — they; tishthantah — remaining; anyatra — elsewhere; yadyapi — although; tathapi — still; pravishan — entered; gaure — into Gaura; acintya-lakshana-lakshitah — marked by inconceivable signs.

**30.** Although by the power of Yogamaya they remain elsewhere as well, still they entered into Gaura — which is recognized only by inconceivable signs.

### Verse 31

यथोक्तं व्यासचरणैः प्रभासखण्डमध्यतः।
“अचिन्त्याः खलु ये भावा न तांस्तर्केण योजयेत्।” इति॥३१॥

*yathoktaṁ vyāsacaraṇaiḥ prabhāsakhaṇḍamadhyataḥ|*
*“acintyāḥ khalu ye bhāvā na tāṁstarkeṇa yojayet|” iti||31||*

*Word for word:* yatha — as; uktam — it is said; vyasa-caranaih — by the feet of Vyasa; prabhasa-khanda-madhyatah — in the Prabhasa-khanda; acintyah — inconceivable; khalu — indeed; ye — which; bhavah — truths; na — not; tan — them; tarkena — to reasoning; yojayet — one should subject; iti — thus.

**31.** As the feet of Vyasa have said in the *Prabhasa-khanda*: "Truths that are inconceivable must not be subjected to reasoning."

### Verse 32

रघुनाथं प्रविश्यापि यथा तिष्ठति भार्गवः।
एवं श्रीनारदमुखास्तिष्ठन्त्यन्येषु धामसु।
तथैव प्रभुना सार्द्धं दीव्यन्ति श्रुतिदेहवत्॥३२॥

*raghunāthaṁ praviśyāpi yathā tiṣṭhati bhārgavaḥ|*
*evaṁ śrīnāradamukhāstiṣṭhantyanyeṣu dhāmasu|*
*tathaiva prabhunā sārddhaṁ dīvyanti śrutidehavat||32||*

*Word for word:* raghunatham — into Raghunatha; pravishya api — even having entered; yatha — as; tishthati — remains (separately); bhargavah — Bhargava; evam — so; shri-narada-mukhah — Narada and the others; tishthanti — remain; anyeshu — in other; dhamasu — abodes; tatha eva — and likewise; prabhuna sarddham — together with the Lord; divyanti — sport; shruti-dehavat — like the personified shrutis.

**32.** As Bhargava,[^01-11] though he entered into Raghunatha, still remains separately, so Narada and the others remain in other abodes and at the same time sport with the Lord, like the personified *shrutis*.

### Verse 33

किन्तु यद्यद्भक्तगणा यद्यद्भावविलासिनः।
तत्तद्भावानुसारेण व्रजे तेषामभूद्गतिः॥३३॥

*kintu yadyadbhaktagaṇā yadyadbhāvavilāsinaḥ|*
*tattadbhāvānusāreṇa vraje teṣāmabhūdgatiḥ||33||*

*Word for word:* kintu — but; yad-yad-bhakta-ganah — whatever devotees; yad-yad-bhava-vilasinah — delighting in whatever mood; tat-tat-bhava-anusarena — in accordance with that mood; vraje — in Vraja; tesham — their; abhut — was; gatih — destination.

**33.** But whatever mood (*bhava*) the various devotees delighted in, in accordance with that mood they attained their destination in Vraja.

### Verse 34

गौरचन्द्रोदयेऽद्वैतं प्रति गौरवचो यथा॥
“दास्ये केचन केचन प्रणयिनः सख्ये त एवोभये
राधामाधवनिष्ठया कतिपये श्रीद्वारकाधीशितुः।
सख्यादावुभयत्र केचन परे ये वावतारान्तरे
मय्याबद्धहृदोऽखिलान् वितनवै वृन्दावनासङ्गिनः॥”३४॥

*gauracandrodaye'dvaitaṁ prati gauravaco yathā||*
*“dāsye kecana kecana praṇayinaḥ sakhye ta evobhaye*
*rādhāmādhavaniṣṭhayā katipaye śrīdvārakādhīśituḥ|*
*sakhyādāvubhayatra kecana pare ye vāvatārāntare*
*mayyābaddhahṛdo'khilān vitanavai vṛndāvanāsaṅginaḥ||”34||*

*Word for word:* gaura-candrodaye — in the Gaura-chandrodaya; advaitam prati — to Advaita; gaura-vacah — the words of Gaura; yatha — as; dasye — in service; kecana — some; kecana — some; pranayinah — loving; sakhye — in friendship; te eva — they; ubhaye — in both; radha-madhava-nishthaya — with devotion to Radha and Madhava; katipaye — some; shri-dvaraka-adhishituh — (to) the Lord of Dvaraka; sakhya-adau — in friendship and the rest; ubhayatra — towards both; kecana — some; pare — others; ye — who; va — or; avatara-antare — towards another incarnation; mayi — to Me; abaddha-hridah — with hearts bound; akhilan — all; vitanavai — I shall make; vrindavana-asanginah — devoted to Vrindavana.

**34.** As Gaura says to Advaita in the *Gaura-chandrodaya*:[^01-12] "Some are in service, some are loving in friendship, and some in both; some are devoted to Radha and Madhava, some to the Lord of Dvaraka; others in friendship and the rest towards both, and still others towards other incarnations — all of them, binding their hearts to Me, I shall make devoted to Vrindavana."

## Mahaprabhu's relatives

### Verse 35

पर्जन्यो नाम गोपाल आसीत् कृष्णपितामहः।
उपेन्द्रमिश्रः सन् जातः श्रीहट्टे सप्तपुत्रवान्॥३५॥

*parjanyo nāma gopāla āsīt kṛṣṇapitāmahaḥ|*

**35.** The cowherd named Parjanya, Krishna's grandfather, was born in Shrihatta as Upendra Mishra, the father of seven sons.

### Verse 36

महामान्याभिधा गोपी व्रजे यासीद्वरीयसी।
कृष्ण-पितामही सैव नाम्नात्र कमलावती॥३६॥

*mahāmānyābhidhā gopī vraje yāsīdvarīyasī|*

*Word for word:* mahamanya-abhidha — named Mahamanya; gopi — cowherd woman; vraje — in Vraja; ya — who; asit — was; variyasi — most eminent; krishna-pitamahi — Krishna's grandmother; sa eva — she; namna — by name; atra — here; kamalavati — Kamalavati.

**36.** The eminent cowherd woman of Vraja named Mahamanya, Krishna's grandmother, here bears the name Kamalavati.

### Verse 37

पुरा यशोदा-व्रजराजनन्दौ
वृन्दावने प्रेमरसाकरौ यौ।
शची-जगन्नाथपुरन्दराभिधौ
बभूवतुस्तौ न च संशयोऽत्र॥३७॥

*purā yaśodā-vrajarājanandau*
*vṛndāvane premarasākarau yau|*
*śacī-jagannāthapurandarābhidhau*
*babhūvatustau na ca saṁśayo'tra||37||*

*Word for word:* pura — once; yashoda-vrajaraja-nandau — Yashoda and the king of Vraja, Nanda; vrindavane — in Vrindavana; prema-rasa-akarau — mines of the rasa of love; yau — who; shaci-jagannatha-purandara-abhidhau — named Shachi and Jagannatha Purandara; babhuvatuh — became; tau — they; na ca — and there is no; samshayah — doubt; atra — in this.

**37.** Yashoda and the king of Vraja, Nanda, who once in Vrindavana were mines of the *rasa* of love, became Shachi and Jagannatha Purandara — of this there is no doubt.

### Verse 38

अमू अविशतामेव देवावदिति-कश्यपौ।
श्रीकौशल्या-दशरथौ तथा श्रीपृश्नि-तत्पती॥३८॥

*amū aviśatāmeva devāvaditi-kaśyapau|*
*śrīkauśalyā-daśarathau tathā śrīpṛśni-tatpatī||38||*

*Word for word:* amu — into these two; avishatam — entered; eva — also; devau — the divine; aditi-kashyapau — Aditi and Kashyapa; shri-kaushalya-dasharathau — Sri Kaushalya and Dasharatha; tatha — and also; shri-prishni-tat-pati — Sri Prishni and her husband.

**38.** Into them also entered the divine Aditi and Kashyapa, Sri Kaushalya and Dasharatha, and Sri Prishni and her husband.

### Verse 39

देवकी-वसुदेवौ यौ पितरौ राम-कृष्णयोः।
तावप्यमू अविशतामिति जल्पन्ति केचन।
अन्यथा राममूर्तेः श्रीविश्वरूपस्य नोद्भवः॥३९॥

*devakī-vasudevau yau pitarau rāma-kṛṣṇayoḥ|*
*tāvapyamū aviśatāmiti jalpanti kecana|*
*anyathā rāmamūrteḥ śrīviśvarūpasya nodbhavaḥ||39||*

*Word for word:* devaki-vasudevau — Devaki and Vasudeva; yau — who; pitarau — the parents; rama-krishnayoh — of Rama and Krishna; tau api — they too; amu — into these; avishatam — entered; iti — thus; jalpanti — say; kecana — some; anyatha — otherwise; rama-murteh — of the form of Rama; shri-vishvarupasya — of Sri Vishvarupa; na — no (would be); udbhavah — appearance.

**39.** Some say that Devaki and Vasudeva, the parents of Rama and Krishna, entered into them too; otherwise Sri Vishvarupa, the form of Rama, could not have appeared.

### Verse 40

रोहिणी-वसुदेवौ यौ पितरौ राम-कृष्णयोः।
पद्मावती-मुकुन्दौ तौ सन्तौ जातौ द्विजोत्तमौ।
श्रीसुमित्रा-दशरथौ तावप्यविशताममू॥४०॥

*rohiṇī-vasudevau yau pitarau rāma-kṛṣṇayoḥ|*
*padmāvatī-mukundau tau santau jātau dvijottamau|*
*śrīsumitrā-daśarathau tāvapyaviśatāmamū||40||*

*Word for word:* rohini-vasudevau — Rohini and Vasudeva; yau — who; pitarau — the parents; rama-krishnayoh — of Rama and Krishna; padmavati-mukundau — as Padmavati and Mukunda; tau — they; santau — being; jatau — were born; dvija-uttamau — the best of brahmanas; shri-sumitra-dasharathau — Sri Sumitra and Dasharatha; tau api — they too; avishatam — entered; amu — into these.

**40.** Rohini and Vasudeva, the parents of Rama and Krishna, were born as the best of brahmanas, Padmavati and Mukunda;[^01-13] into them also entered Sri Sumitra and Dasharatha.

### Verse 41

पौर्णमासी व्रजे यासीत् गोविन्दानन्दकारिणी।
आचार्य श्रील गोविन्दो गीत-पद्यादिकारकः॥४१॥

*paurṇamāsī vraje yāsīt govindānandakāriṇī|*
*ācārya śrīla govindo gīta-padyādikārakaḥ||41||*

*Word for word:* paurnamasi — Paurnamasi; vraje — in Vraja; ya — who; asit — was; govinda-ananda-karini — bringing joy to Govinda; acarya — the acharya; shrila-govindah — Srila Govinda; gita-padya-adi-karakah — the composer of songs, verses and so on.

**41.** Paurnamasi, who brought joy to Govinda in Vraja, is the *acharya* Srila Govinda, the composer of songs and verses.

### Verse 42

नाम्नाम्बिका व्रजे धात्री स्तन्यदात्री स्थिता पुरा।
सैवेयं मालिनीनाम्नी श्रीवासगृहिणीमता॥४२॥

*nāmnāmbikā vraje dhātrī stanyadātrī sthitā purā|*

*Word for word:* namna — by name; ambika — Ambika; vraje — in Vraja; dhatri — the nurse; stanya-datri — who gave the breast; sthita — was; pura — once; sa eva iyam — she is this; malini-namni — named Malini; shrivasa-grihini — the wife of Srivasa; mata — considered.

**42.** The nurse in Vraja named Ambika, who once suckled Him, is this Malini, considered the wife of Srivasa.

### Verse 43

अम्बिकायाः स्वसा यासीन्नाम्नी श्रील किलिम्बिका।
कृष्णोच्छिष्टं प्रभुञ्जाना सेयं नारायणीमता॥४३॥

*ambikāyāḥ svasā yāsīnnāmnī śrīla kilimbikā|*

*Word for word:* ambikayah — of Ambika; svasa — the sister; ya — who; asit — was; namni — by name; shrila-kilimbika — Srila Kilimbika; krishna-ucchishtam — Krishna's remnants; prabhunjana — partaking of; sa iyam — she; narayani — Narayani; mata — is considered.

**43.** Ambika's sister, named Srila Kilimbika, who partook of Krishna's remnants, is considered to be Narayani.

### Verse 44

पुरासीज्जनको राजा मिथिलाधिपतिर्महान्।
अधुना वल्लभाचार्यो भीष्मकोऽपि च सम्मतः॥४४॥

*purāsījjanako rājā mithilādhipatirmahān|*
*adhunā vallabhācāryo bhīṣmako'pi ca sammataḥ||44||*

*Word for word:* pura — once; asit — was; janakah — Janaka; raja — the king; mithila-adhipatih — lord of Mithila; mahan — great; adhuna — now; vallabha-acaryah — Vallabhacharya; bhishmakah — Bhishmaka; api ca — also; sammatah — is accepted.

**44.** He who was once the great king Janaka, lord of Mithila, is now Vallabhacharya; he is also accepted as Bhishmaka.

### Verse 45

श्रीजानकी रुक्मिणी च लक्ष्मीनाम्नी च तत्सुता।
चैतन्यचरिते व्यक्ता लक्ष्मीनाम्नी च सा यथा॥४५॥

*śrījānakī rukmiṇī ca lakṣmīnāmnī ca tatsutā|*

*Word for word:* shri-janaki — Sri Janaki; rukmini — Rukmini; ca — and; lakshmi-namni — named Lakshmi; ca — and (is); tat-suta — his daughter; caitanya-carite — in the Chaitanya-charita; vyakta — clearly named; lakshmi-namni — named Lakshmi; ca — and; sa — she; yatha — as.

**45.** Sri Janaki and Rukmini are his daughter named Lakshmi; and so she is named Lakshmi in the *Chaitanya-charita*:

### Verse 46

“सा वल्लभाचार्यसुता चलन्ती
स्नातुं सखीभिः सुरदीर्घिकायाम्।
लक्ष्मीरनेनैव कृतावतारा
प्रभोर्ययौ लोचनवर्त्म तत्र॥”४६॥

*“sā vallabhācāryasutā calantī*
*snātuṁ sakhībhiḥ suradīrghikāyām|*

**46.** "That daughter of Vallabhacharya, Lakshmi, incarnated for His sake, went with her friends to bathe in the river of the gods (the Ganga), and there came into the Lord's sight."

### Verse 47

श्रीसनातनमिश्रोऽयं पुरा सत्राजितो नृपः।
विष्णुप्रिया जगन्माता यत् कन्या भू-स्वरूपिणी॥४७॥

*śrīsanātanamiśro'yaṁ purā satrājito nṛpaḥ|*
*viṣṇupriyā jaganmātā yat kanyā bhū-svarūpiṇī||47||*

*Word for word:* shri-sanatana-mishrah — Sri Sanatana Mishra; ayam — this; pura — formerly; satrajitah nripah — King Satrajit; vishnupriya — Vishnupriya; jagan-mata — the Mother of the world; yat — whose; kanya — daughter; bhu-svarupini — the embodiment of Bhu.

**47.** Sri Sanatana Mishra is the former king Satrajit, whose daughter is Vishnupriya, the Mother of the world, the embodiment of Bhu.[^01-14]

### Verse 48

उक्ता प्रसङ्गात् कलिना श्रीचैतन्यविधूदये।
“भुवोऽंशरूपामपराञ्च विष्णुप्रियेति
वित्तां परिणीय कान्ताम्।” इत्यादि॥४८॥

*uktā prasaṅgāt kalinā śrīcaitanyavidhūdaye|*
*“bhuvo'ṁśarūpāmaparāñca viṣṇupriyeti*
*vittāṁ pariṇīya kāntām|” ityādi||48||*

*Word for word:* ukta — said; prasangat — in passing; kalina — by Kali; shri-caitanya-vidhu-udaye — in the Sri Chaitanya-chandrodaya; bhuvah — of Bhu; amsha-rupam — a partial manifestation; aparam — another; ca — and; vishnupriya iti — as "Vishnupriya"; vittam — known; pariniya — having married; kantam — beloved; iti-adi — and so on.

**48.** This was said in passing by Kali in the *Sri Chaitanya-chandrodaya*: "…and having married another beloved, a partial manifestation of Bhu, known as Vishnupriya…" and so on.

### Verse 49

विश्वामित्रोऽपि घटकः श्रीरामोद्वाहकर्मणि।
रुक्मिण्या प्रेषितो विप्रो यश्च श्रीकेशवं प्रति।
तावयं वनमाली यत्कर्मणाचार्यतां गतः॥४९॥

*viśvāmitro'pi ghaṭakaḥ śrīrāmodvāhakarmaṇi|*
*rukmiṇyā preṣito vipro yaśca śrīkeśavaṁ prati|*
*tāvayaṁ vanamālī yatkarmaṇācāryatāṁ gataḥ||49||*

*Word for word:* vishvamitrah — Vishvamitra; api — also; ghatakah — the matchmaker; shri-rama-udvaha-karmani — at the wedding of Sri Rama; rukminya — by Rukmini; preshitah — sent; viprah — the brahmana; yah ca — and who; shri-keshavam prati — to Sri Keshava; tau — these two; ayam — this; vanamali — Vanamali; yat-karmana — by which service; acaryatam — the position of acharya; gatah — attained.

**49.** Vishvamitra, who was the matchmaker at Sri Rama's wedding, and the brahmana sent by Rukmini to Sri Keshava — these two are Vanamali, who by this very service attained the position of *acharya*.[^01-15]

### Verse 50

यश्च सत्राजिता विप्रः प्रहितो माधवं प्रति।
सत्योद्वाहाय कुलकः श्रीकाशीनाथ एव सः॥५०॥

*yaśca satrājitā vipraḥ prahito mādhavaṁ prati|*
*satyodvāhāya kulakaḥ śrīkāśīnātha eva saḥ||50||*

*Word for word:* yah ca — and who; satrajita — by Satrajit; viprah — the brahmana; prahitah — sent; madhavam prati — to Madhava; satya-udvahaya — for Satya's wedding; kulakah — matchmaker; shri-kashinathah — Sri Kashinatha; eva — indeed; sah — he.

**50.** And the brahmana sent by Satrajit to Madhava as matchmaker for Satya's wedding is Sri Kashinatha.

### Verse 51

केनावान्तरभेदेन भेदं कुर्वन्ति सात्वताः।
सत्यभामाप्रकाशोऽपि जगदानन्दपण्डितः॥५१॥

*kenāvāntarabhedena bhedaṁ kurvanti sātvatāḥ|*
*satyabhāmāprakāśo'pi jagadānandapaṇḍitaḥ||51||*

*Word for word:* kena — by a certain; avantara-bhedena — secondary distinction; bhedam — distinction; kurvanti — make; satvatah — the Satvatas; satyabhama-prakashah — a manifestation of Satyabhama; api — also; jagadananda-panditah — Jagadananda Pandita.

**51.** By a certain secondary distinction, the Satvatas hold that Jagadananda Pandita is a manifestation of Satyabhama.

### Verse 52

मथुरायां यज्ञसूत्रं पुरा कृष्णाय यो मुनिः।
ददौ सान्दीपनिः सोऽभूदद्य केशवभारती॥५२॥

*mathurāyāṁ yajñasūtraṁ purā kṛṣṇāya yo muniḥ|*
*dadau sāndīpaniḥ so'bhūdadya keśavabhāratī||52||*

*Word for word:* mathurayam — in Mathura; yajna-sutram — the sacred thread; pura — once; krishnaya — to Krishna; yah — who; munih — the sage; dadau — gave; sandipanih — Sandipani; sah — he; abhut — became; adya — now; keshava-bharati — Keshava Bharati.

**52.** The sage Sandipani, who once in Mathura gave Krishna the sacred thread, has now become Keshava Bharati.

### Verse 53

पुरासीद्रघुनाथस्य यो वशिष्ठमुनिर्गुरुः।
स प्रकाशविशेषेण गङ्गादास-सुदर्शनौ॥५३॥

*purāsīdraghunāthasya yo vaśiṣṭhamunirguruḥ|*
*sa prakāśaviśeṣeṇa gaṅgādāsa-sudarśanau||53||*

*Word for word:* pura — once; asit — was; raghunathasya — of Raghunatha; yah — who; vasishtha-munih — the sage Vasishtha; guruh — guru; sah — he; prakasha-visheshena — by a special manifestation; gangadasa-sudarshanau — Gangadasa and Sudarshana.

**53.** The sage Vasishtha, once the guru of Raghunatha, became by a special manifestation Gangadasa and Sudarshana.[^01-16]

### Verse 54

वृषभानुतया ख्यातः पुरा यो व्रजमण्डले।
अधुना पुण्डरीकाख्यो विद्यानिधिमहाशयः॥५४॥

*vṛṣabhānutayā khyātaḥ purā yo vrajamaṇḍale|*
*adhunā puṇḍarīkākhyo vidyānidhimahāśayaḥ||54||*

*Word for word:* vrishabhanu-taya — as Vrishabhanu; khyatah — famous; pura — once; yah — who; vraja-mandale — in the land of Vraja; adhuna — now; pundarika-akhyah — named Pundarika; vidyanidhi-maha-ashayah — the venerable Vidyanidhi.

**54.** He who was once famous in the land of Vraja as Vrishabhanu is now the venerable Pundarika Vidyanidhi.

### Verse 55

स्वकीयभावमास्वाद्य राधाविरहकातरः।
चैतन्यः पुण्डरीकाह्वये तातावदत् स्वयम्॥५५॥

*svakīyabhāvamāsvādya rādhāvirahakātaraḥ|*
*caitanyaḥ puṇḍarīkāhvaye tātāvadat svayam||55||*

*Word for word:* svakiya-bhavam — His own mood; asvadya — tasting; radha-viraha-katarah — afflicted by separation (as) Radha; caitanyah — Chaitanya; pundarika-ahvaye — to the one called Pundarika; tata — "Father"; avadat — said; svayam — Himself.

**55.** Tasting His own mood and afflicted by separation like Radha, Chaitanya Himself called Pundarika "Father."

### Verse 56

प्रेमनिधितया ख्यातिं गौरो यस्मै ददौ सुखी।
माधवेन्द्रस्य शिष्यत्वात् गौरवञ्च सदाकरोत्॥५६॥

*premanidhitayā khyātiṁ gauro yasmai dadau sukhī|*
*mādhavendrasya śiṣyatvāt gauravañca sadākarot||56||*

*Word for word:* premanidhi-taya — as Premanidhi; khyatim — fame (name); gaurah — Gaura; yasmai — to whom; dadau — gave; sukhi — joyful; madhavendrasya — of Madhavendra; shishyatvat — because of being the disciple; gauravam — honour; ca — and; sada — always; akarot — showed.

**56.** Gaura joyfully gave him the glorious name Premanidhi, and always honoured him, because he was a disciple of Madhavendra.

### Verse 57

तत्प्रकाशविशेषोऽपि मिश्रः श्रीमाधवो मतः।
रत्नावती तु तत्पत्नी कीर्त्तिदा कीर्त्तिता बुधैः॥५७॥

*tatprakāśaviśeṣo'pi miśraḥ śrīmādhavo mataḥ|*
*ratnāvatī tu tatpatnī kīrttidā kīrttitā budhaiḥ||57||*

*Word for word:* tat-prakasha-visheshah — his (Vrishabhanu's) special manifestation; api — also; mishrah shri-madhavah — Sri Madhava Mishra; matah — is considered; ratnavati — Ratnavati; tu — and; tat-patni — his wife; kirttida — Kirtida; kirttita — is declared; budhaih — by the wise.

**57.** A special manifestation of him is considered to be Sri Madhava Mishra,[^01-17] and his wife Ratnavati is declared by the wise to be Kirtida.

# Part Two

*Nityananda and the vyuhas. Advaita Acharya and his family. Srivasa and "those called devotees." The sannyasi associates. Puri: Prataparudra, Sarvabhauma, Ramananda (verses 58–123)*

## Nityananda, "the devotee in essence"

### Verse 58

अंश-अंशिनोरभेदेन व्यूह आद्यः शचीसुतः।
बलदेवो विश्वरूपो व्यूहः सङ्कर्षणो मतः॥

*aṁśa-aṁśinorabhedena vyūha ādyaḥ śacīsutaḥ|*
*baladevo viśvarūpo vyūhaḥ saṅkarṣaṇo mataḥ||*

*Word for word:* amsha-amshinoh — of the part and the whole; abhedena — by non-difference; vyuhah — vyuha; adyah — the original; shaci-sutah — the son of Shachi; baladevah — Baladeva; vishvarupah — Vishvarupa; vyuhah — the vyuha; sankarshanah — Sankarshana; matah — is considered.

**58.** Since the part and the whole are not different, the original *vyuha* is the son of Shachi; Baladeva, the *vyuha* Sankarshana, is considered to be Vishvarupa.

### Verse 59

नित्यानन्दावधूतश्च प्रकाशेन स उच्यते॥
गौरचन्द्रोदये धर्मं प्रति वाक्यं कलेर्यथा॥

*nityānandāvadhūtaśca prakāśena sa ucyate||*
*gauracandrodaye dharmaṁ prati vākyaṁ kaleryathā||*

*Word for word:* nityananda-avadhutah — Nityananda Avadhuta; ca — and; prakashena — by manifestation; sah — He; ucyate — is called; gaura-candrodaye — in the Gaura-chandrodaya; dharmam prati — to Dharma; vakyam — the words; kaleh — of Kali; yatha — as.

**59.** And by manifestation He is also called Nityananda Avadhuta. As Kali says to Dharma in the *Gaura-chandrodaya*:

### Verse 60

“अस्याग्रजस्त्वकृतदारपरिग्रहः सन्
सङ्कर्षणः स भगवान् भुवि विश्वरूपः।
स्वीयं महः किल पुरीश्वरमापयित्वा
पूर्वं परिव्रजित एव तिरोबभूव॥” इति॥

*“asyāgrajastvakṛtadāraparigrahaḥ san*
*saṅkarṣaṇaḥ sa bhagavān bhuvi viśvarūpaḥ|*
*svīyaṁ mahaḥ kila purīśvaramāpayitvā*
*pūrvaṁ parivrajita eva tirobabhūva||” iti||*

*Word for word:* asya — His; agrajah — elder brother; tu — indeed; akrita-dara-parigrahah — who did not take a wife; san — being; sankarshanah — Sankarshana; sah — He; bhagavan — Bhagavan; bhuvi — on earth; vishvarupah — Vishvarupa; sviyam — His own; mahah — splendour; kila — indeed; puri-ishvaram — to the Puri-ishvara; apayitva — having passed on; purvam — earlier; parivrajitah — took to the wandering life; eva — indeed; tirobabhuva — disappeared; iti — thus.

**60.** "His elder brother, who never took a wife, is Bhagavan Sankarshana Himself, Vishvarupa on earth; having passed His own splendour to the Puri-ishvara,[^02-18] He earlier took to the wandering life and disappeared."

### Verse 61

“नित्यानन्दावधूतो मह इति महितम्
हन्त सङ्कर्षणं यः।” इति च॥

*“nityānandāvadhūto maha iti mahitam*
*hanta saṅkarṣaṇaṁ yaḥ|” iti ca||*

*Word for word:* nityananda-avadhutah — Nityananda Avadhuta; mahah — the splendour; iti — thus; mahitam — honoured; hanta — ah; sankarshanam — Sankarshana; yah — who (is); iti ca — and thus.

**61.** And also: "Nityananda Avadhuta, the splendour honoured [by Brahma and others], He who is Sankarshana."

### Verse 62

यदा श्रीविश्वरूपोऽयं तिरोभूतः सनातनः।
नित्यानन्दावधूतेन मिलित्वापि तदा स्थितः॥

*yadā śrīviśvarūpo'yaṁ tirobhūtaḥ sanātanaḥ|*
*nityānandāvadhūtena militvāpi tadā sthitaḥ||*

*Word for word:* yada — when; shri-vishvarupah — Sri Vishvarupa; ayam — this; tirobhutah — disappeared; sanatanah — eternal; nityananda-avadhutena — with Nityananda Avadhuta; militva — uniting; api — also; tada — then; sthitah — remained.

**62.** When this eternal Sri Vishvarupa disappeared, He, uniting with Nityananda Avadhuta, then remained in Him.

### Verse 63

ततोऽवधूतो भगवान् बलात्मा
भवन् सदा वैष्णववर्गमध्ये।
जज्वाल तिग्मांशुसहस्रतेजा
इति ब्रुवन् मे जनको ननर्त्त॥

*tato'vadhūto bhagavān balātmā*
*bhavan sadā vaiṣṇavavargamadhye|*
*jajvāla tigmāṁśusahasratejā*
*iti bruvan me janako nanartta||*

*Word for word:* tatah — therefore; avadhutah — the Avadhuta; bhagavan — divine; bala-atma — whose nature is Bala; bhavan — being; sada — always; vaishnava-varga-madhye — amid the Vaishnavas; jajvala — blazed; tigmamshu-sahasra-tejah — with the splendour of a thousand suns; iti — thus; bruvan — saying; me — my; janakah — father; nanartta — danced.

**63.** "And so the divine Avadhuta, whose nature is Bala,[^02-19] always moving among the Vaishnavas, blazed with the splendour of a thousand suns" — so my father[^02-20] said as he danced.

### Verse 64

स्वांशेन शेषेण य एव शय्या
विष्णोश्च कृष्णस्य च वासभूषा।
स्वाङ्गस्य भूषावलयादिरूपै-
र्लीलाख्यया वेद निगूढलीलाम्॥

*svāṁśena śeṣeṇa ya eva śayyā*
*viṣṇośca kṛṣṇasya ca vāsabhūṣā|*
*svāṅgasya bhūṣāvalayādirūpai-*
*rlīlākhyayā veda nigūḍhalīlām||*

*Word for word:* sva-amshena — by His own portion; sheshena — Shesha; yah — who; eva — indeed; shayya — the bed; vishnoh — of Vishnu; ca — and; krishnasya — of Krishna; ca — and; vasa-bhusha — clothing and ornament; sva-angasya — of His own body; bhusha-valaya-adi-rupaih — in the forms of ornaments, bracelets and so on; lila-akhyaya — by (the potency) called lila; veda — knows; nigudha-lilam — the hidden pastimes.

**64.** He who, by His own portion Shesha, is the bed, the clothing and the ornament of Vishnu and of Krishna, and in the form of the ornaments of His own body — bracelets and the rest — knows the hidden pastimes by the potency called *lila*.

### Verse 65

श्रीवारुणी-रेवतवंशसम्भवे
तस्य प्रिये द्वे वसुधा च जाह्नवी।
श्रीसूर्यदासस्य महात्मनः सुते
ककुद्मिरूपस्य च सूर्यतेजसः॥

*śrīvāruṇī-revatavaṁśasambhave*
*tasya priye dve vasudhā ca jāhnavī|*
*śrīsūryadāsasya mahātmanaḥ sute*
*kakudmirūpasya ca sūryatejasaḥ||*

*Word for word:* shri-varuni-revata-vamsha-sambhave — Sri Varuni and the one born in the line of Revata (Revati); tasya — His; priye — beloveds; dve — two; vasudha — Vasudha; ca — and; jahnavi — Jahnavi; shri-suryadasasya — of Sri Suryadasa; mahatmanah — great-souled; sute — daughters; kakudmi-rupasya — who is Kakudmi; ca — and; surya-tejasah — radiant as the sun.

**65.** His two beloveds, Vasudha and Jahnavi — Sri Varuni and Revati, born in the line of Revata[^02-21] — are the daughters of the great-souled Sri Suryadasa, radiant as the sun, who is Kakudmi.[^02-22]

### Verse 66

केचित् श्रीवसुधादेवीं कलावपि विवृण्वते।
अनङ्गमञ्जरीं केचिज्जाह्नवीञ्च प्रचक्षते।
उभयन्तु समीचीनं पूर्वन्यायात् सतां मतम्॥

*kecit śrīvasudhādevīṁ kalāvapi vivṛṇvate|*
*anaṅgamañjarīṁ kecijjāhnavīñca pracakṣate|*
*ubhayantu samīcīnaṁ pūrvanyāyāt satāṁ matam||*

*Word for word:* kecit — some; shri-vasudha-devim — Sri Vasudha-devi; kalau api — even in Kali; vivrinvate — declare (as Ananga-manjari); ananga-manjarim — Ananga-manjari; kecit — some; jahnavim — Jahnavi; ca — and; pracakshate — call; ubhayam — both; tu — but; samicinam — correct; purva-nyayat — according to the former rule; satam — of the saints; matam — opinion.

**66.** Some even in the age of Kali declare Sri Vasudha-devi [to be Ananga-manjari], and some say Jahnavi is Ananga-manjari. According to the former rule [of distinct manifestations], the saints hold both to be correct.

### Verse 67

सङ्कर्षणस्य यो व्यूहः पयोब्धिशायिनामकः।
स एव वीरचन्द्रोऽभूच्चैतन्याभिन्नविग्रहः॥

*saṅkarṣaṇasya yo vyūhaḥ payobdhiśāyināmakaḥ|*
*sa eva vīracandro'bhūccaitanyābhinnavigrahaḥ||*

*Word for word:* sankarshanasya — of Sankarshana; yah — who; vyuhah — the vyuha; payobdhi-shayi-namakah — named the One who lies on the Ocean of Milk; sah eva — he; virachandrah — Virachandra; abhut — became; caitanya-abhinna-vigrahah — whose form is non-different from Chaitanya.

**67.** The *vyuha* of Sankarshana, called the One who lies on the Ocean of Milk, became Virachandra, whose form is non-different from Chaitanya;

### Verse 68

अमुं प्राविशतां कार्यात् सहजौ निशठोल्मुकौ॥
मीनकेतनरामादिव्यूहः सङ्कर्षणोऽपरः॥

*amuṁ prāviśatāṁ kāryāt sahajau niśaṭholmukau||*
*mīnaketanarāmādivyūhaḥ saṅkarṣaṇo'paraḥ||*

*Word for word:* amum — into him; pravishatam — entered; karyat — for the sake of the work; sahajau — the brothers; nishatha-ulmukau — Nishatha and Ulmuka; minaketana-rama-adi-vyuhah — Minaketana Rama(dasa), the original vyuha; sankarshanah — Sankarshana; aparah — the other.

### Verse 73

## Advaita, "the incarnation of the devotee"

### Verse 78

### Verse 79

### Verse 80

### Verse 81

### Verse 82

### Verse 83

**84.** Entreated by him, Sadashiva, the lord of the gods and lord of boons, [said]: "In another birth I shall become your son."

### Verse 85

### Verse 92

### Verse 121

**121.** Therefore the skilful Ramananda day after day described to Gaurachandra the essence of devotion and love for Radha and Krishna.

### Verse 122

**123.** The best say that Arjuna, the son of Pandu, having united with the gopi Arjuniya, became Raya Ramananda.

# Part Three

*sādya nīlācalāvāsaḥ kāśīmiśraḥ prabhoḥ priyaḥ||193||*

*Word for word:* mathurayam — in Mathura; pura — once; ya — who; asit — was; sairindhri — Sairandhri; krishna-vallabha — Krishna's beloved; sa — she; adya — now; nilacala-avasah — living in Nilachala; kashi-mishrah — Kashi Mishra; prabhoh — of the Lord; priyah — dear.

**193.** Sairandhri, once Krishna's beloved in Mathura, is now Kashi Mishra, who lives in Nilachala, dear to the Lord.

### Verse 194–207

रिपवः षट् काममुख्या जिता येन वशीकृताः।

श्रीमद्भागवताचार्यो गौराङ्गात्यन्तवल्लभः॥२०३॥
सुशीलः पण्डितः श्रीमान् जीवः श्रीवल्लभात्मजः।
वाणीनाथद्विजश्चम्पहट्टवासी प्रभोः प्रियः॥२०४॥
ईशानाचार्य-कमलौ लक्ष्मीनाथाख्य-पण्डितः।
गङ्गामन्त्री जगन्नाथो मामूपाधिर्द्विजोत्तमः॥२०५॥
श्रीकण्ठाभरणोपाधिरनन्तश्चट्टवंशजः।
हस्तिगोपालनामा च रङ्गवासी च वल्लभः॥२०६॥
हर्याचार्यो गौरसङ्गी मिश्रः श्रीनयनस्तथा।
कविदत्तो रामदासश्चिरञ्जीव-सुलोचनौ॥२०७॥

*mālatī candralatikā mañjumedhā varāṅgadā|*

*hastigopālanāmā ca raṅgavāsī ca vallabhaḥ||206||*

## Conclusion

### Verse 208

केचिन्महान्तः केचित्स्युर्महान्तश्चोपपूर्वकाः।
उभयेषां गुणास्तुल्यास्तेनामी गणिता मया॥२०८॥

*kecinmahāntaḥ kecitsyurmahāntaścopapūrvakāḥ|*
*ubhayeṣāṁ guṇāstulyāstenāmī gaṇitā mayā||208||*

*Word for word:* kecit — some; mahantah — mahantas; kecit — some; syuh — are; mahantah ca upa-purvakah — mahantas with the prefix upa (upa-mahantas); ubhayesham — of both; gunah — the qualities; tulyah — equal; tena — therefore; ami — these; ganitah — listed; maya — by me.

**208.** Some of them are *mahantas*, others are *upa-mahantas*; the qualities of both are equal, and therefore I have listed them together.

### Verse 209

खण्डवासौ नरहरेः साहचर्यान्महत्तरौ।
गौराङ्गैकान्तशरणौ चिरञ्जीव-सुलोचनौ॥२०९॥

*khaṇḍavāsau narahareḥ sāhacaryānmahattarau|*
*gaurāṅgaikāntaśaraṇau cirañjīva-sulocanau||209||*

*Word for word:* khanda-vasau — living in Khanda; narahareh — of Narahari; sahacaryat — through companionship; mahattarau — greater; gauranga-ekanta-sharanau — whose one refuge is Gauranga; ciranjiva-sulocanau — Chiranjiva and Sulochana.

**209.** Chiranjiva and Sulochana, who live in Khanda, are greater through their companionship with Narahari; their one and only refuge is Gauranga.

### Verse 210

गुरोर्नाम न गृह्णीयादिति शास्त्रानुसारतः।
श्रीश्रीनाथस्य पूर्वाख्या मया न प्रकटीकृता॥२१०॥

*gurornāma na gṛhṇīyāditi śāstrānusārataḥ|*
*śrīśrīnāthasya pūrvākhyā mayā na prakaṭīkṛtā||210||*

*Word for word:* guroh — of the guru; nama — the name; na grihniyat — one should not utter; iti — thus; shastra-anusaratah — in accordance with the shastra; shri-shrinathasya — of Sri Srinatha; purva-akhya — the former name; maya — by me; na prakatikrita — has not been revealed.

**210.** In accordance with the shastra that says, "One should not utter the name of one's guru," I have not revealed the former name of Sri Srinatha.

### Verse 211

व्याचकार पारिपाट्याद्यो भागवत-संहिताम्।
कुमारहट्टे यत् कीर्त्तिः कृष्णदेवो विराजते॥२११॥

*vyācakāra pāripāṭyādyo bhāgavata-saṁhitām|*
*kumārahaṭṭe yat kīrttiḥ kṛṣṇadevo virājate||211||*

*Word for word:* vyacakara — expounded; paripatyat — with skill; yah — who; bhagavata-samhitam — the Bhagavata-samhita; kumarahatte — in Kumarahatta; yat-kirttih — whose glory; krishnadevah — Krishnadeva; virajate — shines.

**211.** He expounded the *Bhagavata-samhita* with skill; his glory is Krishnadeva, who shines in Kumarahatta.[^03-53]

### Verse 212

ये ये महान्तः क्रमभङ्गभूता-
स्ते मेऽपराधं कृपया क्षमन्ताम्।
गुणान् विनिर्णीय सतां समस्तान्
ब्रह्मेशशेषाः कथितुं न शक्ताः॥२१२॥

*ye ye mahāntaḥ kramabhaṅgabhūtā-*
*ste me'parādhaṁ kṛpayā kṣamantām|*
*guṇān vinirṇīya satāṁ samastān*
*brahmeśaśeṣāḥ kathituṁ na śaktāḥ||212||*

*Word for word:* ye ye — whatever; mahantah — mahantas; krama-bhanga-bhutah — whose order has been broken; te — they; me — my; aparadham — offence; kripaya — mercifully; kshamantam — may forgive; gunan — the qualities; vinirniya — determining; satam — of the saints; samastan — all; brahma-isha-sheshah — Brahma, Shiva and Shesha; kathitum — to tell; na shaktah — are not able.

**212.** May the *mahantas* whose order of mention has been broken mercifully forgive me this offence: even Brahma, Shiva and Shesha cannot enumerate all the qualities of the saints.

### Verse 213

मीमांसकेभ्यः शठतार्किकेभ्यो
विशेषतो हेतुरतेभ्य एषः।
गोप्यः प्रयत्नाद्रसशास्त्रविद्भ्यो
देयः सदा गौरपदाश्रयेभ्यः॥२१३॥

*mīmāṁsakebhyaḥ śaṭhatārkikebhyo*
*viśeṣato heturatebhya eṣaḥ|*
*gopyaḥ prayatnādrasaśāstravidbhyo*
*deyaḥ sadā gaurapadāśrayebhyaḥ||213||*

*Word for word:* mimamsakebhyah — from the Mimamsakas; shatha-tarkikebhyah — from deceitful logicians; visheshatah — especially; hetu-ratebhyah — from those delighting in arguments; eshah — this; gopyah — is to be hidden; prayatnat — carefully; rasa-shastra-vidbhyah — to those versed in the scriptures on rasa; deyah — is to be given; sada — always; gaura-pada-ashrayebhyah — to those who have taken shelter of Gaura's feet.

**213.** This book should be carefully kept hidden from the Mimamsakas, from deceitful logicians and especially from those who delight only in arguments; it should always be given to those versed in the scriptures on *rasa* who have taken shelter of Gaura's feet.

### Verse 214

श्रीगौरगणोद्देशदीपिका रचिता मया।
दीप्यतां परमानन्दसन्दोहभक्तवेश्मनि॥२१४॥

*śrīgauragaṇoddeśadīpikā racitā mayā|*
*dīpyatāṁ paramānandasandohabhaktaveśmani||214||*

*Word for word:* shri-gaura-gana-uddesha-dipika — the Sri Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika; racita — composed; maya — by me; dipyatam — may it shine; parama-ananda-sandoha-bhakta-veshmani — in the house of the devotees, filled with the highest bliss.

**214.** This *Sri Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* has been composed by me; may it shine in the house of the devotees, filled with the highest bliss.

### Verse 215

शाके वसुग्रहमिते मनुनैव युक्ते
ग्रन्थोऽयमाविरभवत् कतमस्य घस्रात्।
चैतन्यचन्द्रचरितामृतमग्नचित्तैः
शोध्यः समाकलितगौरगणाख्य एषः॥२१५॥

*śāke vasugrahamite manunaiva yukte*
*grantho'yamāvirabhavat katamasya ghasrāt|*
*caitanyacandracaritāmṛtamagnacittaiḥ*
*śodhyaḥ samākalitagauragaṇākhya eṣaḥ||215||*

*Word for word:* shake — in the Shaka year; vasu-graha-mite — measured by vasu (8) and graha (9); manuna — with manu (14); eva — indeed; yukte — joined; granthah — book; ayam — this; avirabhavat — appeared; katamasya — on a certain; ghasrat — day; caitanya-candra-carita-amrita-magna-cittaih — by those whose hearts are immersed in the nectar of the deeds of Chaitanya-chandra; shodhyah — to be corrected; samakalita-gaura-gana-akhyah — the enumeration of Gaura's associates; eshah — this.

**215.** In the Shaka year measured by "Vasu" and "Graha," joined with "Manu,"[^03-55] on a certain day this book appeared. May the wise, whose hearts are immersed in the nectar of the deeds of Chaitanya-chandra, correct it — this enumeration of Gaura's associates.

*Thus ends the Sri Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika, composed by Kavi Karnapura, also known as Puridasa and Paramananda Dasa.*
