# Part Two

*Nityananda and the vyuhas. Advaita Acharya and his family. Srivasa and "those called devotees." The sannyasi associates. Puri: Prataparudra, Sarvabhauma, Ramananda (verses 58–123)*

## Nityananda, "the devotee in essence"

**58.** Since the part and the whole are not different, the original *vyuha* is the son of Shachi; Baladeva, the *vyuha* Sankarshana, is considered to be Vishvarupa.

**59.** And by manifestation He is also called Nityananda Avadhuta. As Kali says to Dharma in the *Gaura-chandrodaya*:

**60.** "His elder brother, who never took a wife, is Bhagavan Sankarshana Himself, Vishvarupa on earth; having passed His own splendour to the Puri-ishvara,[^18] He earlier took to the wandering life and disappeared."

**61.** And also: "Nityananda Avadhuta, the splendour honoured [by Brahma and others], He who is Sankarshana."

**62.** When this eternal Sri Vishvarupa disappeared, He, uniting with Nityananda Avadhuta, then remained in Him.

**63.** "And so the divine Avadhuta, whose nature is Bala,[^19] always moving among the Vaishnavas, blazed with the splendour of a thousand suns" — so my father[^20] said as he danced.

**64.** He who, by His own portion Shesha, is the bed, the clothing and the ornament of Vishnu and of Krishna, and in the form of the ornaments of His own body — bracelets and the rest — knows the hidden pastimes by the potency called *lila*.

**65.** His two beloveds, Vasudha and Jahnavi — Sri Varuni and Revati, born in the line of Revata[^21] — are the daughters of the great-souled Sri Suryadasa, radiant as the sun, who is Kakudmi.[^22]

**66.** Some even in the age of Kali declare Sri Vasudha-devi [to be Ananga-manjari], and some say Jahnavi is Ananga-manjari. According to the former rule [of distinct manifestations], the saints hold both to be correct.

**67.** The *vyuha* of Sankarshana, called the One who lies on the Ocean of Milk, became Virachandra, whose form is non-different from Chaitanya;

**68.** into him, for the sake of the work, entered the brothers Nishatha and Ulmuka.[^23] Minaketana Ramadasa is the other Sankarshana, the original *vyuha*.

**69.** The Ganga, born from the foot of Vishnu, was born under her own name as the daughter of Nityananda; her husband Madhava is King Shantanu.

**70.** The third *vyuha*, Pradyumna, became the dear confidential friend (*priya-narma-sakha*) who assisted in the pastimes of Radha and Madhava in Vraja; non-different in body from Sri Chaitanya, he is Raghunandana.[^24]

**71.** The fourth *vyuha*, Aniruddha, is Vakreshvara Pandita, who gave joy to the Lord by his dancing, born of absorption in Krishna.

**72.** It was he who spoke sweet words to the feet of Chaitanya: "O merciful One, give me a thousand singers."

**73.** By a distinction of her own manifestation, Shashirekha[^25] entered into him. The appearance (*avirbhava*) of Gaurahari was in Nakula Brahmachari;

**74.** and His entering (*avesha*) is likewise to be known in the Mishra named Pradyumna.[^26] Acharya Bhagavan, the lame one, is said to be a portion (*kala*) of Gaura.

**75.** Gopinatha Acharya is to be known as Brahma, lord of the world, whom the knowers of the Tantras count in the Tantra among the nine *vyuhas*.

## Advaita, "the incarnation of the devotee"

**76.** Sadashiva, who in Vraja was a *vyuha* by being a form of empowerment (*avesha*), is Advaita Gosvami, whose form is non-different from Chaitanya.

**77.** He, having assumed the body of a cowherd boy, danced in Vraja beside Krishna, as Bhairava says in the *Shiva-tantra*:

**78.** "Once in the month of Karttika, at the great festival of lamps, Krishna, with Rama and the cowherd boys, danced with zeal.

**79.** Seeing this, my guru,[^27] longing for the mood of a cowherd, began to dance with his Beloved, whirling like a wheel.

**80.** By the grace of Sri Krishna Sadashiva became twofold: one there was Shiva himself, the other had the form of a cowherd boy."

**81.** The friend of Mahadeva, Kubera, lord of the Guhyakas, is now Kubera Pandita, His [Advaita's] father, the "sky-clad."[^28]

**82.** Once Kubera, dear to Sri Shiva, on Kailasa, frequented by the Siddhas and Sadhyas, chanted the supreme mantra of Shiva.

**83.** Then the merciful lord said, "Choose a boon," and Kubera chose: "Become my son."

**84.** Entreated by him, Sadashiva, the lord of the gods and lord of boons, [said]: "In another birth I shall become your son."

**85.** Having received this boon, he spent some time in austerity, and now, by the will of the Lord, for the sake of the work, he became the father of Advaita.

**86.** Bhagavati Yogamaya is now His wife, descended in the form of Sita; and by her manifestation, the one named Shri.[^29]

**87.** His son is Achyutananda, dear to Krishna Chaitanya; it is known that he was the beloved disciple of Srimat Pandita Gosvami.[^30]

**88.** Some say that he was formerly Karttikeya; others, knowers of *rasa*, call him the gopi named Achyuta. Both are correct, since the two are united in one. But some say that Karttikeya is Krishna Mishra, by resemblance.[^31]

**89.** [Sita's maidservants] Nandini and Jangali are to be known as Jaya and Vijaya respectively.

## Srivasa and "those called devotees"

**90.** The wise Srivasa Pandita is the former sage Narada; the best of sages named Parvata, Narada's friend, is Sriman Rama Pandita, his younger brother.

**91.** Murari Gupta is Hanuman; Angada is Sri Purandara; he who was called Sugriva is Govindananda.

**92.** He who was formerly Vibhishana is considered to be Ramachandra Puri; therefore Gaurahari said: "The cause of this is not Rama." For the sake of the work, Jatila, Radhika's mother-in-law, entered into him; and therefore Mahaprabhu then reduced His alms and so on.[^33]

**93.** The son of the sage Richika, named Brahma, a great ascetic, was born together with Prahlada [in one person] and became the one called Haridasa.

**94.** As the feet of Murari Gupta have said in the *Chaitanya-charitamrita*: the sage's son, bringing tulasi leaves in the morning

**95.** unwashed, was cursed by his father and became a *yavana*; and he became Haridasa, endowed with supreme devotion.[^34]

**96.** The eight perfections, *anima* and the rest, who were formerly in Vrindavana, these same eight took the form of devotees and were born in Gauda, as follows:

**97.** Ananta, Sukhananda, Govinda, Raghunatha, Krishnananda, Keshava, Sri Damodara and Raghava — in order, with the title "Puri" — are to be known as the eight perfections, *anima* and the rest.[^35]

**98.** The sons of Jayanti, established in celibacy and seeing all equally, the nine *bhagavatas*, who once declared the *Bhagavata-samhita*

**99.** to King Janaka, have now become *sannyasis* and always sported with the Lord Gaurahari; namely:

**100.** Sri Nrisimhananda Tirtha, Sri Satyananda Bharati, Sri Nrisimha, Chidananda and Jagannatha — the Tirthas;

**101.** Vasudeva, called Tirtha; Sri Rama; Purushottama — [also] the Avadhuta named Garuda; and Sri Gopendra, called Ashrama.

**102.** The nine treasures (*nidhis*) famed in the world — Padma, Shankha and the rest — were born here as the Lord's dear ones bearing the titles "*nidhi*," "*ratna*" and "*garbha*":

**103.** Sri Srinidhi, Sri Garbha, Kaviratna, Sudhanidhi, Vidyanidhi, Gunanidhi, Ratnabahu, the foremost of brahmanas, Sriman Acharyaratna and Sri Ratnakara Pandita.

**104.** Nilambara Chakravarti declared in an assembly the coming birth of Gaura; therefore he is called Garga.

**105.** As the father of Sri Shachi he is considered to be the cowherd Sumukha; and she who was known in Vraja as Patala is to be known as his wife.

**106.** Sri Devananda Pandita, the knower of the meaning of the Puranas, was formerly the sage Bhaguri, the pandit of Nanda's assembly.

**107.** Kashinatha, Lokanatha, Srinatha and Ramanatha — these four, devotees endowed with knowledge, are without doubt Sanaka and the others.

**108.** Since in all four names the word "*natha*" is heard, they are here called "the four Nathas," just like "the four Sanas."

**109.** He who was Vedavyasa is now Vrindavana Dasa; for the sake of the work the friend Kusumapida also entered into him.

**110.** Shuka, the son of Dvaipayana, became the Bhatta named Vallabha.[^36]

**111.** Acharya Sri Jagannatha and Gangadasa, dear to the Lord, were formerly Durvasa, dear to the gopis in Nidhuvana.

**112.** The discerning should know Acharya Chandrashekhara as Chandra (the Moon); Sriman Uddhava Dasa too is an empowered incarnation (*avesha-avatara*) of the Moon.

**113.** Therefore Chaitanya-Hari called him "the lord of the night." Srimad Vishveshvara Acharya was formerly Divakara (the Sun).

**114.** He who was formerly Vishvakarma is now Bhaskara Thakura. The mendicant Vanamali was formerly the brahmana Sudama — and so, having obtained wealth in the company of the Lord, yet regarding it as sorrow, he wandered.[^37]

**115.** The two doorkeepers of Vaikuntha, Jaya and Vijaya, have now by their own will been born as Sri Jagannatha and Madhava.

**116.** Pundarikaksha and Kumuda, famous in the realm of Vaikuntha, were born in Gauda as Govinda and Garuda, dear to the Lord.

**117.** Garuda Pandita is the Garuda known of old from the scriptures. He who was formerly named Akrura is Gopinatha Simha, so some say; [others say that] Akrura is Keshava Bharati.

## Puri

**118.** Sri Paramananda Puri was formerly Uddhava. The great king Indradyumna, who once worshipped Jagannatha, has now been born as Prataparudra, equal to Indra.

**119.** Sarvabhauma Bhattacharya was formerly the Lord of Speech (Brihaspati) in heaven.

**120.** A certain dear confidential friend [of Krishna], Arjuna, and Arjuna the son of Pandu, uniting together, became Ramananda Raya, dear to the Lord.

**121.** Therefore the skilful Ramananda day after day described to Gaurachandra the essence of devotion and love for Radha and Krishna.

**122.** Some call him Lalita, but this is accepted by only a few: for Gaura said to Bhavananda: "You are Prithapati."[^38]

**123.** The best say that Arjuna, the son of Pandu, having united with the gopi Arjuniya, became Raya Ramananda.

### Translator's notes

[^18]: According to the commentators, the "Puri-ishvara" here is Paramananda Puri, not Ishvara Puri.
[^19]: Bala is Balarama.
[^20]: Shivananda Sena, the author's father.
[^21]: Varuni and Revati are the consorts of Balarama.
[^22]: Kakudmi is the father of Revati.
[^23]: Nishatha and Ulmuka are sons of Balarama.
[^24]: Raghunandana Thakura of Shrikhanda.
[^25]: Shashirekha is a gopi friend.
[^26]: Cf. *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Antya 2): Chaitanya manifested Himself in Nakula Brahmachari (*avesha*) and in Pradyumna (Nrisimhananda) Brahmachari (*avirbhava*).
[^27]: The words are Bhairava's: "my guru" is Sadashiva.
[^28]: "Sky-clad" (*digambara*), i.e. naked, is an epithet of Shiva (in the edition, of Mahadeva); the edition notes a variant reading *vidambara* ("the best of the learned").
[^29]: Advaita's second wife, Sri-devi.
[^30]: That is, of Gadadhara Pandita. Cf. the *Advaita-prakasha*, where Achyuta is the chief hero among Advaita's sons.
[^31]: Krishna Mishra is another son of Advaita; according to the *Advaita-prakasha*, he inherited the service of Madana-Gopala.
[^33]: Cf. *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Antya 8): Ramachandra Puri's reproaches made Chaitanya cut down His food.
[^34]: Haridasa Thakura, born in a Muslim family.
[^35]: *Anima* ("becoming minute") and the rest are the eight mystic perfections of yoga.
[^36]: Vallabha Bhatta, founder of the Pushtimarga *sampradaya*.
[^37]: The edition interprets the line differently: wandering in the misery of poverty, he obtained in the Lord's company the wealth of *prema*.
[^38]: Bhavananda Raya, Ramananda's father; Prithapati, "the husband of Pritha," i.e. Pandu.
