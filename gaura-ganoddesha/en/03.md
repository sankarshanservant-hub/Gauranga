# Part Three

*The cowherd friends of Vraja. Servants and musicians. The gopis: Gadadhara and Lalita, the eight sakhis and others. The manjaris and the Gosvamis of Vrindavana. Conclusion (verses 124–215)*

**124.** "And Arjuna, the son of Pandu, at once became the gopi Arjuniya" — this is clearly stated in the *Padma Purana*, *Uttara-khanda*. Therefore the venerable Ramananda Raya is these three.[^39]

## The cowherd friends

**125.** Now the devotees from Vraja are named briefly, according to my understanding.

**126.** He who was formerly called Shridama is now the great Abhirama; it was he who carried a log that only thirty-two men could lift.

**127.** He who was formerly called Sudama is now Thakura Sundara;[^40] and the friend Vasudama is Pandita Sri Dhananjaya.

**128.** Subala, the best of the dear friends, is Gauridasa Pandita; the mighty Mahabala is Kamalakara Pippalai.

**129.** Subahu, a cowherd of Vraja, is Uddharana Datta; Mahabahu, a friend in Vraja, is Sriman Mahesha Pandita.

**130.** The former friend Stokakrishna is Sri Purushottama Dasa.

**131.** The son of Sadashiva named Nagara Purushottama, born in a family of Vaidyas, is Dama, a cowherd of Vraja.

**132.** The former friend named Arjuna is Sri Parameshvara Dasa; Lavanga, a friend in Vraja, is Kala Krishnadasa.

**133.** The brahmana Shridhara Pandita, known as Khola-vecha,[^41] was in Vraja the jester named Kusumasava.

**134.** The cowherd boy named Prabala, a friend of Balarama, who was formerly in Vraja, is now Halayudha Thakura.

**135.** Krishnachandra's friend in Vraja named Varuthapa is Rudra Pandita, dear to Gauranga.

**136.** Gandharva, a cowherd of Vraja, is Kumudananda Pandita.

## Servants, singers and musicians

**137.** The two servants in Vrindavana, Bhringara and Bhangura, were born as the Lord's servants Sri Kashishvara and Govinda.

**138.** The two servants who were formerly in Vrindavana, Raktaka and Patraka, now serve Gauranga: they are Haridasa the Elder and Haridasa the Younger.[^42]

**139.** The two, Payoda and Varida, who formerly prepared the water, are now the servants known as Ramai and Nandai.

**140.** The two singers of Vraja, Madhukantha and Madhuvrata, are Gauranga's singers Mukunda and Vasudeva Datta.

**141.** The former actor Chandramukha is Makaradhvaja Kara.

**142.** He who was formerly in Vraja the *mridanga* drummer named Sri Sudhakara is now Sri Shankara Ghosha, expert in playing the *dampha*.

**143.** Chandrahasa, the dancer of Vraja, a knower of *rasa*, is the lover of dance Sri Jagadisha Pandita.

**144.** He who in Vraja, named Maladhara, held the *venu* and *murali* flutes is now the Pandita called Vanamali, dear to Gaura.

**145.** The two famous parrots of Vrindavana, Daksha and Vichakshana, have now been born as my elder brothers, Chaitanya and Ramadasa.[^43]

## The gopis, "the potency of the devotee"

**146.** Now, according to my understanding and to what I have heard, are named those of the cowherd women who became dear to the Lord.

**147.** Sri Radha, love personified, formerly the Queen of Vrindavana, is Sri Gadadhara, dear to Gaura, called the Pandita.

**148.** As Sri Svarupa determined, he is the Lakshmi of Vraja:

**149.** "The Lakshmi who was once the beloved of Shyamasundara in Vrindavana is now the Lakshmi of Gaura's love, Sri Gadadhara Pandita."

**150.** Since Lalita follows Radha, being "Anuradhika," she too entered into him, as is said in the *Gaura-chandrodaya*:

**151.** "This too is Lalita, the friend of Radhika, and not at all this lord of brahmanas, Gadadhara; or else this Hari by His own potency has become threefold: Himself, the friend and Radhika."

**152.** Others have said that Lalita is Dhruvananda Brahmachari; by the distinction of her own manifestations this opinion too is correct.

**153.** Or else Bhagavan Gaura of His own will assumed a threefold form; therefore Sri Gadadhara Pandita is the form of Sri Radhika.

**154.** Chandrakanti, who was a manifestation of Radha's glory, is now near Gauranga as Gadadhara of the Dasa family.[^44]

**155.** Purnananda, who in Vraja was foremost among the beloveds of Baladeva, also entered into this Gadadhara for the sake of the work.

**156.** Chandravali, who once in Vraja was Krishna's supreme beloved, is now in the land of Gauda Kaviraja Sadashiva.

**157.** Sri Bhadra, on whose breast Krishna once slept in Vrindavana, is now Shankara Pandita, dear to Gauranga.

**158.** Sri Taraka and Pali, who were once in the land of Vraja, are now the Lord's dear ones Jagannatha and Sri Gopala.

**159.** Shaibya, who in Vraja was known for her fiery temper, is Damodara Pandita; for a certain purpose the goddess Sarasvati also entered into him.[^45]

**160.** Vishakha, who once in Vraja taught Radha the arts, is now Svarupa Gosvami, who delights in those same moods.

**161.** Chitra, who once in Vraja arranged Radha's hair, is now Kaviraja Sri Vanamali, dear to the Lord.

**162.** Sri Champakalata, who was the very life of Sri Radha in Vraja, is now Raghava Gosvami, who settled at Govardhana, the author of the book *Bhakti-ratna-prakasha*.

**163.** Tungavidya, who in Vraja was learned in all the shastras, is the renunciant Prabodhananda, the Sarasvati who sings of Gaura.

**164.** Indulekha, who was once Sri Radha's friend in Vraja, is Krishnadasa Brahmachari, who settled in Vrindavana.

**165.** Rangadevi is now Gadadhara Bhatta; Anantacharya Gosvami is she who was Sudevi in Vraja.

**166.** Sri Kashishvara Gosvami is the former Shashirekha in Vraja. Dhanishtha, who in Vraja gave Krishna countless delicacies, is now Raghava Pandita, dear to Gauranga.

**167.** She who in Vraja was Gunamala is Damayanti, his sister; Ratnarekha is Krishnadasa, Kalavati is Krishnananda.

**168.** Shauraseni is the skilful Narayana Vachaspati; Kaveri is Pitambara; Sukeshi is Makaradhvaja.

**169.** Madhavi is Madhavacharya, Indira is Jiva Pandita.

**170.** Sumadhura, who in Vraja was dear to Tungavidya, is Vidya Vachaspati, dear to Gaura and dear to the people of Vraja.

**171.** Balabhadra Bhattacharya is Sri Madhurekshana; Srinatha Mishra is Chitrangi; Kavichandra is Manohara.

**172.** Nandimukhi, who was in Vraja, is now Saranga Thakura. Some consider him Prahlada, but my father does not accept this.

**173.** Kalakanthi and Sukanthi, who in Vraja were in the troupe of the Gandharvas, are Ramananda Vasu and Satyaraja respectively.

**174.** She who in Vraja was Katyayani is now Sri Kanta Sena.

**175.** The overseer of Vraja named Vrinda-devi is now Sri Mukunda Dasa of Khanda, dear to the Lord.

**176.** The messenger Vira, who once in Vrindavana led all the gopis to Krishna, is now my father; she who in Vraja was Bindumati is now my mother.

**177.** Madhumati, once a dear friend in Vrindavana, is now Narahari Sarakara, dear to the Lord.

**178.** The dear friend in Vraja named Ratnavali is Gopinatha Acharya, renowned for his purity.

**179.** The flute, dear to Krishna, is Vamshi Dasa Thakura.[^48]

## The manjaris

**180.** Sri Rupa-manjari, who was once famous in Vrindavana, has now appeared, having become Rupa Gosvami.

**181.** Rati-manjari, the dearest friend of Rupa-manjari, is also called by the wise by another name, Lavanga-manjari.

**182.** She is now Sanatana, whose body is non-different from Gaura, worthy of worship by all; for the sake of the work Sanatana, the jewel among sages, also entered into him.[^49]

**183.** Shivananda Chakravarti, who settled in Vrindavana, is renowned as a manifestation of Srimati Lavanga-manjari.

**184.** Ananga-manjari is now Gopala Bhatta; but some call Bhatta Gosvami Sri Guna-manjari.

**185.** Raghunatha Bhatta is the former Raga-manjari. And he who made his dwelling in a hut by Sri Radha-kunda —

**186.** that is Raghunatha Dasa, whose former name is Rasa-manjari; some call him Srimati Rati-manjari, and others, by a difference of names, Bhanumati.

**187.** The former name of Bhugarbha Thakura is Prema-manjari; Lokanatha Gosvami is the former Sri Lila-manjari.

**188.** Kalavati, Rasollasa and Gunatunga, who were in Vraja and sang the songs composed by Sri Vishakha, are now considered to be Govinda, Madhavananda and Vasudeva respectively.[^50]

**189.** Ragalekha and Kalakeli, Radha's maidservants, are to be known as Shikhi Mahiti and his sister Madhavi respectively.

**190.** Malli, the daughter of a Pulinda, has now become Kalidasa.[^51]

**191.** Shuklambara Brahmachari, whose food the Lord Gauranga asked for and ate, was formerly the wife of a sacrificial priest. Some say that the Brahmachari was formerly a brahmana performing sacrifices.

**192.** Other wives of the sacrificial priests are Sri Jagadisha and Hiranyaka, whose food the Lord asked for and ate on Ekadashi.

**193.** Sairandhri, once Krishna's beloved in Mathura, is now Kashi Mishra, who lives in Nilachala, dear to the Lord.

**194–207.** Malati, Chandralatika, Manjumedha, Varangada, Ratnavali, Kamala, Gunachuda, Sukeshini, Karpura-manjari, Shyama-manjari, Shveta-manjari, Vilasa-manjari, Kamalekha, Mauna-manjari, Gandhonmada, Rasonmada, Chandrika, Kalabhashini, Gopali, Harini, Kali, Kalakshi, Nitya-manjari, Kalakanthi, Kurangakshi, Chandrika and Chandrashekhara — all those whom Radha has appointed to the service befitting each — having taken male forms, sported according to their own natures with Gaura and His dear ones, in order as follows: the brahmana Shubhananda; Shridhara Brahmachari; Paramananda Gupta, who composed the *Krishna-stavavali*; a certain brahmana Raghunatha, who served Gauranga alone; Kamsari Sena; the venerable Sri Jagannatha Sena; Subuddhi Mishra; Sri Harsha; Raghu Mishra, the best of brahmanas; Jitamitra — true to his name, for he conquered and subdued the six enemies headed by lust, and so Gaura named him; Srimad Bhagavatacharya, the author of the booklet *Krishna-prema-tarangini*, exceedingly dear to Gauranga; the virtuous scholar Sriman Jiva, son of Sri Vallabha; the brahmana Vaninatha of Champahatta, dear to the Lord; Ishana Acharya and Kamala; the Pandita named Lakshminatha; Gangamantri; Jagannatha, called Mamu, the best of brahmanas; Ananta of the Chatta family, called Kanthabharana; Hasti-Gopala; Vallabha of Ranga; Hari Acharya, Gaura's companion; Sri Nayana Mishra; Kavidatta; Ramadasa; Chiranjiva and Sulochana.[^52]

## Conclusion

**208.** Some of them are *mahantas*, others are *upa-mahantas*; the qualities of both are equal, and therefore I have listed them together.

**209.** Chiranjiva and Sulochana, who live in Khanda, are greater through their companionship with Narahari; their one and only refuge is Gauranga.

**210.** In accordance with the shastra that says, "One should not utter the name of one's guru," I have not revealed the former name of Sri Srinatha.

**211.** He expounded the *Bhagavata-samhita* with skill; his glory is Krishnadeva, who shines in Kumarahatta.[^53]

**212.** May the *mahantas* whose order of mention has been broken mercifully forgive me this offence: even Brahma, Shiva and Shesha cannot enumerate all the qualities of the saints.

**213.** This book should be carefully kept hidden from the Mimamsakas, from deceitful logicians and especially from those who delight only in arguments; it should always be given to those versed in the scriptures on *rasa* who have taken shelter of Gaura's feet.

**214.** This *Sri Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* has been composed by me; may it shine in the house of the devotees, filled with the highest bliss.

**215.** In the Shaka year measured by "Vasu" and "Graha," joined with "Manu,"[^55] on a certain day this book appeared. May the wise, whose hearts are immersed in the nectar of the deeds of Chaitanya-chandra, correct it — this enumeration of Gaura's associates.

*Thus ends the Sri Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika, composed by Kavi Karnapura, also known as Puridasa and Paramananda Dasa.*

### Translator's notes

[^39]: That is, three are united in Ramananda: the confidential friend Arjuna, Arjuna the Pandava, and the gopi Arjuniya (Lalita, according to some).
[^40]: Sundarananda Thakura.
[^41]: *Khola-vecha*, "seller of banana bark" (utensils made of banana leaves): the Shridhara of the *Chaitanya-bhagavata*.
[^42]: Not to be confused with Haridasa Thakura.
[^43]: Chaitanya Dasa and Ramadasa, the author's elder brothers, sons of Shivananda Sena.
[^44]: Gadadhara Dasa.
[^45]: *Chandi*, "fiery, sharp-tempered": Damodara Pandita is known for the outspokenness with which he admonished even Mahaprabhu.
[^48]: Vamshivadana Thakura, by tradition the incarnation of Krishna's flute.
[^49]: The second "Sanatana" is the sage Sanatana-kumara.
[^50]: Govinda, Madhava and Vasudeva Ghosha, the brothers who were *kirtana* singers.
[^51]: Kalidasa, the uncle of Raghunatha Dasa, known for gathering the food remnants of all the Vaishnavas.
[^52]: The edition groups verses 194–207 together; the correspondence of the gopis' names to Gaura's associates is given in the order of enumeration.
[^53]: Srinatha Pandita, the author's guru, wrote the commentary on the *Bhagavata* called *Chaitanya-mata-manjusha* and installed the Deity of Krishnadeva in Kumarahatta.
[^55]: A numerical chronogram: Vasu = 8, Graha = 9, Manu = 14; read backwards, Shaka 1498, i.e. 1576 CE.
