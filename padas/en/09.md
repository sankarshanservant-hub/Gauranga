# Vasu Ghosha: the Lord's Bhavas, Separation, Nityananda, Prayers

*Padas of Vasu (Vasudeva) Ghosha known from the *Padakalpataru* (PK), in the order of the lilas: Gaura in the
kirtana of Navadvipa, His form, His rest and the boat play on the Ganga; the Lord in Krishna's mood and in the mood of
Radha pining in separation (gaura-chandrikas to the sections of PK on impending and accomplished separation); the
devotees' longing for Gaura who has gone; praise of Nityananda; stavas and prarthanas — prayers to Gauranga. Majumdar
regards Vasu Ghosha as an eyewitness of the Navadvipa kirtanas and counts these padas among his padas on the Lord's
bhava, on Nityananda and among his stavas. The text follows PK, checked against the scans, with the manuscript
variants from PK's apparatus, the *Gaura-pada-tarangini* (GPT1 — 1st ed., 1903; GPT2 — 2nd ed., 1934) and the
*Kshanada-gita-chintamani* (KGC); the Bengali original, variants and word-for-word translation are in `bn/09.md`.*

## 1. Vasu Ghosha — "navadvīpe udaya karilā dvija-rāja…" (PK 1494)
*Gaura in the kirtana of Navadvipa: the moon risen in the darkness of Kali among the stars, His companions; grey with
dust, He sways on the waves of bhava, sings "Hari, Hari" and stands still in the tribhanga pose; at His left is
Gadadhara; remembering Vrindavana, the Lord weeps and pours a downpour of the holy name upon the world. In PK it is the
gaura-chandrika to the section "the spring rasa." Raga dhanashi or vasanta.*[^1]

**1.1.** *(Refrain)* In Navadvipa the king of the twice-born has risen[^2] —
dense is the darkness of Kali, but Gorachand shines
amid the stars — His companions.

**1.2.** In kirtana, brimming over with love, grey all over with dust,
He sways on the waves of bhava;
karatalas in His hands, He sings, "Hari, Hari!" —
and again and again stands still, bent in three places.[^3]

**1.3.** At His left is dear Gadadhara,
and on his shoulder rests the Lord's graceful arm that reaches to the knees.[^4]
Remembering Vrindavana, ceaselessly yearning,
He sheds streams of tears from His reddish eyes.

**1.4.** His eyes pour and pour like a fresh raincloud,
His teeth shine brighter than lightning.
Vasudeva Ghosha sings: to deliver the souls of the age of Kali,
He has rained a downpour of Hari's name.[^5]

[^1]: Signature: "Vasudeva Ghosha." PK: vol. 2, PDF p. 432 (printed p. 419), 3rd shakha, 27th pallava, section "অথ বাসন্তী
রাসলীলা । তদুচিত-শ্রীগৌরচন্দ্র" (gaura-chandrika to the spring rasa), no. 70 (mark "॥৭০॥১৪৯৪॥"); raga dhanashi or vasanta;
MS P-R-Sa no. 2044. GPT1 — p. 131, no. 59 (raga dhanashi). Majumdar counts PK 1494 among Vasu's padas on the Lord's
bhava.
[^2]: "Dvija-raja," "king of the twice-born," is both the moon and the best of brahmanas: Gaura is a brahmana risen
over Navadvipa like the moon.
[^3]: Tribhanga is the pose of Krishna playing the flute (bent at the neck, waist and knee): in kirtana the Lord
spontaneously assumes Krishna's form.
[^4]: Gadadhara Pandita, according to the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika*, is a manifestation of Radha; he stands at Gaura's
left as Radha stands at Krishna's left. MS "kh": "ājāno"; P-R-Sa: "ajāne" (the same).
[^5]: MS "kh": "pours" (বরিখত). P-R-Sa: "like" (যেন) instead of "brighter than" (জিনি).

## 2. Vasu Ghosha — "cala dekhi giyā gorā ati manohare…" (PK 973)
*Gaura's form in Nadia: "Come, let us go and see Gora!" — His body brighter than tested gold, His arms reaching to the
knees like golden pillars, a red cloth at His waist, a garland of malati down to His feet; the poet is ready to give
his life for Him. In PK it is the gaura-chandrika to the section "anuraga: setting out to the tryst by night." Raga
suhai.*[^6]

**2.1.** Come, let us go and see Gora, beautiful beyond words:
wondrous is Gora's form in the town of Nadia!

**2.2.** His body, tender and radiant, outshines tested gold;
who, seeing the waves of His eyes, can keep his composure?

**2.3.** His arms reach to His knees like golden pillars,
a red cloth is at His waist, broad are His hips.[^7]

**2.4.** A garland of malati sways on His neck down to His feet.
Says Vasu: "Come — I will give my life for Him!"[^8]

[^6]: Signature: "Vasu." PK: vol. 2, PDF p. 200 (printed p. 187), 3rd shakha, 12th pallava, section "ততঃ পুনশ্চ
অভিসারানুরাগঃ রাত্রৌ যথা । শ্রীগৌরচন্দ্রঃ," no. 1 (mark "॥১॥৯৭৩॥"); raga suhai; MS P-R-Sa no. 1423. The same pada stands in
PK a second time — PK 2176 (vol. 3, PDF pp. 259–260, printed pp. 248–249; 19th pallava, no. 9; MS P-R-Sa no. 2284), in
the section of padas spoken by the women of Nadia, with the ending "Says Vasu: 'I will go and offer [Him] my youth'";
so too in GPT1 (p. 161, no. 15, raga shri), among padas in nagari-bhava. Majumdar counts PK 973 among the padas on the
Lord's form and PK 2176 under nagari-bhava. The PK 973 recension is translated here; the 2176 recension belongs to
nagari-bhava: worship of Gaura in the spirit of "Gaura-nagari" is not accepted by the Gaudiya tradition (Vrindavana
Dasa, Krishnadasa Kaviraja, Bhaktisiddhanta).
[^7]: Long arms, a broad chest and broad hips are marks of a great personality (mahapurusha) according to the
samudrika-shastra; so too Vrindavana Dasa and Krishnadasa Kaviraja describe the Lord's form.
[^8]: Literally "I will give my life as nichhani" — a rite in which something precious is waved around a beloved
person to ward off misfortune and then given away; here the poet gives his very life.

## 3. Vasu Ghosha — "śutiyāche gorācā̃da śayana-mandire…" (PK 656)
*Gorachand lies in His bedchamber on a wondrous couch, languid; it is as if the Creator had strained lightning from
the clouds and put all rasa into Him; Vasudeva Ghosha looks on in joy. In PK it is the gaura-chandrika to the section
"languor after the play" (rasalasa). Raga vibhasa.*[^9]

**3.1.** Gorachand lies in His bedchamber
on a wondrous couch, on a lovely bed.

**3.2.** Languid is the body of Gora, the king of dancers —
how can I tell the beauty of His body? It cannot be told![^10]

**3.3.** As if lightning had been carefully strained from the clouds —
so much rasa did the Creator put in, fashioning Him!

**3.4.** A lovely bed, wondrous pillows —
Vasudeva Ghosha looks on with joy in his heart.

[^9]: Signature: "Vasudeva Ghosha." PK: vol. 2, PDF p. 51 (printed p. 38), 3rd shakha, section "অথ রসালস । তদুচিত-শ্রীগৌরচন্দ্র,"
no. 1 (mark "॥১॥৬৫৬॥"); raga vibhasa; MS P-R-Sa no. 1131. GPT1 — pp. 361–362, no. 53 (raga vibhasa). According to PK's
index of first lines (vol. 5), the same pada stands in PK a second time — PK 2474 (vol. 4, the section on the night and
morning plays); in the scan of vol. 4 (IA `354459`) the leaf with PK 2474–2478 is missing between PDF pp. 44
and 45, so its variants have not been checked. The catalogue wrongly linked PK 656 to the GPT pada "নাচত শচীতনয়
গৌরসুন্দর…" (VG-057). Majumdar counts PK 656 among Vasu's padas on the Lord's bhava. PK's section "rasalasa" is about the
languor of Radha and Krishna after the night's play; the gaura-chandrika shows the Lord wearied by the night's kirtana
and bhava.
[^10]: MSS "gh," "c": "Gaura lies" (শুতিয়া আছেন গৌর) instead of "Gorachand lies"; "k," "c": "in absorption" (আবেশে),
"gh": "in languor" (অলসে) instead of "languid" (আলসে; GPT1 — "আবেশে," with "আলসে" in a note); P-R-Sa: "how can I tell the
beauty of His body" (কি কব অঙ্গের শোভা), "says Vasudeva Ghosha" (কয়) instead of "looks on" (দেখে).

## 4. Vasu Ghosha — "nā jāniye gorācā̃dera kona bhāva mane…" (PK 1409)
*The boat play on the Ganga: Gorachand goes to the bank with His companions, steps into a boat with Gadadhara and
steers it Himself; everyone cries "We are sinking!" and bails out water, and, remembering the former play, floats in
the waters of prema, while the Lord, looking at Gadadhara, gently smiles. In PK it is the gaura-chandrika to the
section "the boat play" (nauka-vilasa). Raga tudi.*[^11]

**4.1.** I do not know what bhava is in Gorachand's heart:
He went with His companions to the bank of the Suradhuni.[^12]

**4.2.** Taking with Him dear Gadadhara and others,
Gaura stepped into a boat, overwhelmed by prema.

**4.3.** Becoming the helmsman Himself, He steers the boat;
"We are sinking, sinking!" all cry, and bail out the water.

**4.4.** All the companions call out, "Hari, Hari!" —
remembering the former play, some float in the waters of prema.[^13]

**4.5.** Looking into Gadadhara's face, He smiles ever so gently —
so says Vasudeva Ghosha, with jubilation in his heart.

[^11]: Signature: "Vasudeva Ghosha." PK: vol. 2, PDF p. 388 (printed p. 375), 3rd shakha, 26th pallava, section "অথ
নৌকা-বিলাস । তদুচিত শ্রীমহাপ্রভু," no. 1 (mark "॥১॥১৪০৯॥"); raga tudi; MS P-R-Sa no. 1925. GPT1 — p. 333, no. 38 (raga tudi;
among the padas on the dana-lila). Majumdar counts PK 1409 among Vasu's padas on the Lord's bhava. Compare Ramananda's
pada on the same play (PK 1417, portion 06).
[^12]: Suradhuni, "the river of the gods," is the Ganga.
[^13]: "The former play" is Krishna's play with Radha and the gopis in a boat on the Yamuna (nauka-lila): the Lord at
the helm is Krishna, Gadadhara is Radha. MSS "k," "c," P-R-Sa: "Himself" — আপনি (the same); P-R-Sa: "guides" (বহে) instead
of "steers" (বায়), "with joy" (হরিষে) instead of "with jubilation" (উল্লাসে). GPT1: "smiles to Himself" (মনে মনে হাসে).

## 5. Vasu Ghosha — "āre mora gorā dvijamaṇi…" (PK 54)
*The Lord in the mood of Krishna stricken with purvaraga: "Radha, Radha!" Gora calls, weeps and rolls on the ground,
chants Radha's name with great devotion, rivers of tears flow from His eyes, and again and again He loses
consciousness. In PK it is the gaura-chandrika to the section "Sri Krishna's purvaraga." Raga suhai, tala
jayajayanti.*[^14]

**5.1.** O my Gora, jewel of the twice-born!
"Radha, Radha!" He calls, weeps and rolls on the ground.

**5.2.** Gora chants Radha's name with great devotion,
and streams of the Suradhuni flow from His reddish eyes.[^15]

**5.3.** Again and again Gora's body rolls on the ground,
again and again, uttering Radha's name, He swoons.

**5.4.** His body is thrilled with gooseflesh, His voice falters.
Says Vasu: "Why is Gora so agitated?"

[^14]: Signature: "Vasu." PK: vol. 1, PDF p. 51 (printed p. 40), 1st shakha, section "শ্রীকৃষ্ণস্য পূর্ব্বরাগঃ । তদুচিত-শ্রীমহাপ্রভুঃ,"
no. 1 (mark "॥১॥৫৪॥"); "সুহই । জয়জয়ন্তী." GPT1 — p. 297, no. 16 (jayajayanti). Majumdar counts PK 54 among Vasu's padas on
the Lord's bhava. Usually the Lord abides in Radha's mood, but His companions also saw His mood of Krishna longing for
the gopis (cf. CC Adi 17: the Lord chants "Gopi, gopi!").
[^15]: Streams of the Suradhuni (the Ganga) are His tears. MS "kh" and GPT1: "how many Suradhunis flow…". Line 5.2 is
repeated by Radhamohana Dasa in his pada on Gaura and Gauridasa (GPT1, p. 290, no. 43).

## 6. Vasu Ghosha — "sakala bhakata meli ānande hulāhuli…" (PK 2041)
*In the morning the devotees come with joyful cries to see Gauranga, but He has not slept all night and His face is
wan; rising, He sits down again on the ground, alone, apart; the devotees are alarmed, not understanding what bhava
has risen in His heart; one washes His face, another tidies His hair. In PK it is the gaura-chandrika "on the next
day" to the section "recollections of the complete union." Raga pahida.*[^16]

**6.1.** All the devotees together, with joyful cries,
came to see Gauranga.
Gauranga is lying down, no one is near Him;
He has stayed awake all night, His face is wan.

**6.2.** *(Refrain)* Here is a great wonder:
rising, Gaura Hari sat down again on the ground,
and sits with no one beside Him.[^17]

**6.3.** Seeing this, the devotees were alarmed:
why is His face so cheerless?
All say, "Alas, alas! Nothing can be understood —
what bhava has risen in His heart today?"

**6.4.** One gently washes His face with water,
another tidies His hair.
"We know nothing: Gora is bhava embodied" —
and sad is Vasu Ghosha's face.[^18]

[^16]: Signature: "Vasu Ghosha." PK: vol. 3, PDF p. 210 (printed p. 199), 4th shakha, 16th pallava ("samriddhiman
sambhoga: recollections"), section "অথ দিনান্তরে শ্রীমদ্গৌরচন্দ্রো যথা," no. 18 (mark "॥১৮॥২০৪১॥"); raga pahida. GPT1 — p. 312,
no. 17 (pahida), just before the section on separation; in GPT2 — in the section on the antya-lila (the catalogue had
it twice: VG-080 and VG-122). The place is not named. Majumdar counts PK 2041 among Vasu's padas on the Lord's bhava.
The PK section shows Radha, on the next day, recalling the night of union with Krishna; the gaura-chandrika shows the
Lord absorbed in such recollections.
[^17]: MSS "k," "kh": "Gauranga" instead of "Gaura"; "k," "kh," "c" and GPT1: "having sat" (বসিয়া) instead of "sat." In MS "k"
there is a lacuna mark in place of the word "with joyful cries" (হুলাহুলি).
[^18]: "Bhava embodied" (bhāvera mūrati): the Lord, in whom Radha's bhava manifests in its fullness.

## 7. Vasu Ghosha — "āju kene gorācā̃dera virasa vayāna…" (PK 1598)
*The Lord in the mood of Radha foreseeing separation: Gorachand's face is cheerless, He asks, "Who has come?" and
weeps; the devotees around have fainted, not understanding the cause; crying "Hari, Hari," Gora sighs heavily. In PK it
is the gaura-chandrika to the section "impending separation." Raga suhai, tala kandarpa.*[^19]

**7.1.** Why is Gorachand's face so cheerless today?
"Who has come? Who has come?" He says, and tears stream down.[^20]

**7.2.** All around, the devotees, weeping, have fainted:
why Gauranga is so, they cannot understand.[^21]

**7.3.** To look at that face — the heart is wrung:
how many Suradhunis pour from His eyes!

**7.4.** Crying "Hari, Hari," Gora sighs heavily;
Vasu strikes his head with his hand and speaks, faltering with tears.

তদ্ভাবাক্রান্তঃ শ্রীমহাপ্রভুঃ" ("impending separation; Sri Mahaprabhu, overwhelmed by this bhava"), no. 1 (mark "॥১॥১৫৯৮॥");
"সুহই । কন্দর্পতাল"; MS P-R-Sa no. 806. GPT1 — p. 313, no. 1 (suhai, kandarpa) — the first pada of the section on
separation. Majumdar counts PK 1598 among Vasu's padas on the Lord's bhava.
[^20]: The PK section: Radha learns that Akrura has come for Krishna to take Him to Mathura; the Lord in Radha's mood
asks, "Who has come?" GPT1: "'Who has come? Who has come?' — and tears stream down" (করি instead of বলি).
[^21]: MS "k": "kena" (the same, "why"); "gh": "they cannot understand His heart" (বুঝিলু মন) instead of "cannot understand
the cause"; "k": "before Him" (সমুখ) instead of "that face" (সে মুখ); MS "kh" lacks the word "jāni" ("is wrung," literally
"I know not how"); P-R-Sa: "weep" (ঝুরে) instead of "pour" (ঝরে).

## 8. Vasu Ghosha — "roi roi jape gorā kṛṣṇa-nāma-madhu…" (PK 1634)
*The Lord in the mood of Radha separated from Krishna: weeping, Gora chants the honeyed name of Krishna; He whose feet
Shiva and Brahma do not attain sits under a tree, having left all company and all comforts; His golden body is adorned
with the treasures of bhava; seeing Him, all weep. In PK it is the gaura-chandrika to the section "accomplished
separation." Raga suhai.*[^22]

**8.1.** Weeping and weeping, Gora chants the honey of Krishna's name —
as if a spotless moon were showering nectar.[^23]

**8.2.** He whose feet Shiva and Brahma do not attain by worship
has sat down under a tree, forsaking all company.[^24]

**8.3.** Leaving all comforts, He has grown weak;
His golden body is adorned with the treasures of bhava.[^25]

**8.4.** Seeing Him, all the people weep ceaselessly;
Vasudeva Ghosha's heart can find no rest.

## 9. Vasu Ghosha — "virale vasiyā ekeśvare…" (PK 1635)
*The Lord as a sannyasi in separation: alone, in solitude, He ceaselessly chants the name of Hari; His body, once
anointed with sandal, now cares for nothing but dust; He has left the pastimes with Lakshmi and lives under a tree, has
left the flute and taken the sannyasi's staff; He knows neither day nor night. In PK it is the second gaura-chandrika
to the section "accomplished separation." Raga suhai.*[^26]

**9.1.** In solitude He sits alone
and ceaselessly chants the name of Hari.

**9.2.** His body was anointed with fragrant sandal —
now nothing pleases Him but dust.[^27]

**9.3.** The Lord has left the pastimes with Lakshmi —
now His dwelling is beneath a tree.[^28]

**9.4.** Leaving the enchanting flute from His hands,
He has taken the staff and become a sannyasi.

**9.5.** He knows neither day nor night.
Says Vasu: "The heart is breaking!"[^29]

bathes in the waters of prema. / Says Vasu: 'The heart is breaking!'"

## 10. Vasu Ghosha — "majhu mane lāgala śela…" (PK 451)
*A lament for Gaura who has turned away from His lover: a spear has pierced the heart, life has become fruitless; if
one is never again to see Gora's face, what joy is there in life? Without Gora life cannot be held. In PK it is the
gaura-chandrika to the section "repentance after a quarrel" (kalahantarita). Raga pathamanjari.*[^30]

**10.1.** A spear has pierced my heart:
Gaura has turned away from me.

**10.2.** My life has become fruitless —
a cruel Creator has sent me grief.

**10.3.** To whom shall I tell this grief?
When I begin to speak, my breast is torn apart.[^31]

**10.4.** If I am never again to see Gora's face,
what joy is there for me in life?[^32]

**10.5.** Vasudeva Ghosha sings this rasa:
without Gora life cannot be held.
