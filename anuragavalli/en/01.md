# Manjari 1. Mangalacharana and the Life of Gopala Bhatta

*Sanskrit verses of salutation; a song on the Lord's appearance in Navadvipa; obeisances to Chaitanya, Nityananda, Advaita, Gadadhara, Srivasa and the Vaishnavas; on the books of Vrindavana Dasa, Lochana Dasa and Krishnadasa Kaviraja; the author's plan — to describe his guru's branch*

**1.** *I bow to that Sri Guru by whose renowned mercy I have obtained the best of names, the mantra, the son of Shachi, Svarupa, Rupa and his elder brother, the great city of Mathura, the cowherd villages, Radha-kunda, the best of hills and — oh! — the hope of Radhika and Madhava.*[^1]

**2.** *I bow to the beautiful lotus feet of Sri Guru, to the gurus and the Vaishnavas, to Sri Rupa with his elder brother, with his companions and Raghunatha, to Jiva with them; to Krishna Chaitanya with Advaita, the Avadhuta and His associates, to the feet of Sri Radha and Krishna with Their companions, Lalita, Sri Vishakha and the others.*[^2]

**3.** *Song in raga Prema-sindhu:*
Sri Krishna Chaitanya-chandra is the son of the king of Vraja:
together with His associates of Vraja He sports eternally.
In Sri Navadvipa, by the River of the Gods,
there Prabhu appeared together with His own.

**4.** *"Gaura is born," says the Shruti; leaving His own abode in Vraja, for His own purpose He appeared with them in Gauda and made the whole world float on the waves of prema; He is the single body of the Two who are full of rasa, especially tasting…*[^3]

**5.** His endless pastimes Vrindavana Dasa
described in the *Sri Chaitanya-bhagavata*.

**6.** What remained in it in the form of sutras
Thakura Lochana told in particular:

**7.** the book *Sri Chaitanya-mangala*, full of rasa,
he revealed in the form of songs — that was his intent.

**8.** What those two did not expand in detail,
that, setting it out in detail, was spread

**9.** by Sri Krishnadasa Kaviraja Mahashaya:
his book is the *Sri Chaitanya-charitamrita*.

**10.** These books became renowned on earth;
even fools came to know the secret siddhanta of Chaitanya.

**11.** The embodiment of compassion, separation and peace, an ocean of mercy,
the friend of the low, the unfortunate and the fallen —

**12.** with a surging wave He flooded the three worlds;
there was no discrimination at all — that is the reason.

**13.** Such a Merciful One I have never seen or heard of,
by hearing of whom all hearts melt.

**14.** Together with His associates I take shelter at Mahaprabhu's feet,
bowing countless times, (asking) forgiveness for offences.

**15.** What can I say of my faults — however many I recall,
the one hope of all is the name "Purifier of the Fallen".

**16.** I bow to Nityananda Raya, Prabhu's elder brother,
whose title "Purifier of the Fallen" the three worlds sing;

**17.** by whose mercy one attains Sri Krishna Chaitanya,
who out of mercy made the land of Gauda blessed.

**18.** If anyone even once says "Nityananda" with love —
at once gooseflesh, tears, trembling, a faltering voice.

**19.** Whoever was hostile to him, to him he showed mercy —
Madhai in the town of Nadia is witness to this.

**20.** With devotion I bow to Sri Advaita Acharya-chandra,
by whose mercy one attains Chaitanya and Nityananda;

**21.** at whose call those two descended,
by whose mercy the world was delivered.

**22.** I bow to Sri Pandita Gosani, Prabhu's own shakti,
by whose mercy firm devotion to Chaitanya arises.

**23.** The devotees headed by Srivasa I praise, taking courage,
by whose breeze the three worlds became Vaishnavas.

**24.** Show mercy to me, fallen as I am, all of you:
may my heart's desire be fulfilled, may my heart melt.

**25.** Whoever takes your name even with duplicity
will not be deprived — so say the sadhus and scriptures.

**26.** In this hope I take shelter at your feet:
if you neglect me, I have no other way — this is settled.

**27.** My way, bad as it is, is you, the purifiers of the fallen;
on every side I am put to shame — this is my plea.

**28.** Whatever pleases you all, do that:
in no way can (I) be neglected.

**29.** Being low, I say this with joy in my heart,
daring to take shelter at Prabhu's lotus feet.

**30.** Firm faith in the "Purifier of the Fallen" is (my) faith;
I write without duplicity — listeners, do not laugh.

**31.** Whoever delights in hearing the *Anuraga-valli*,
his two feet are the ornament of my head.

**32.** And now hear — I shall tell further of my desire,
to tell of the path of succession of my guru.

**33.** Mahaprabhu descended in the land of Sri Gauda,
and the earth became blessed by the sight of Him;

**34.** for forty-eight years His manifest pastimes lasted,
and His own family (of associates) became endless.

**35.** In the Adi-khanda, in the tenth and eleventh chapters
and in the twelfth, this is told — listen especially:

**36.** how many branches there were on the whole circle of the earth —
this cannot be written even with a thousand mouths;

**37.** and among them the many branches in Gauda and Utkala:
these too are not counted, not written.

**38.** In those three chapters the principal persons
he wrote down, merely pointing the way.[^4]

**39.** The first twenty-four years — the pastimes in Navadvipa,
the last eighteen years — the pastimes in Nilachala,

**40.** and in the middle six years of travelling;
who can describe His wanderings everywhere?

[^1]: A verse of Raghunatha Dasa Gosvami (the closing verse of the *Mukta-charita*).
[^2]: The well-known mangalacharana verse "vande 'haṁ śrī-guroḥ śrī-yuta-pada-kamalam…" (cf. the opening of CC Adi 1.1).
[^3]: The beginning of the verse is poorly legible in the edition; the translation is approximate.
[^4]: CC Adi 10–12, the chapters on the branches of Mahaprabhu, Nityananda and Advaita.
