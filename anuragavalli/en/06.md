# Manjari 6. Return to Gauda and Again to Vrindavana

*Lokanatha entrusts Narottama to Shrinivasa; the farewell circuit of Vraja; Jiva secretly equips the travellers through the merchants of Agra; Govinda's garland; Narottama at Garanhati and his kirtana; Shrinivasa at Yajigrama: Ramachandra Kaviraja, the "second eye"; the marriage; the second journey to Vrindavana; Gopala Bhatta makes Shrinivasa adhikari of Radha-ramana*

*[p. 77]*

*Sri-raga.*

**1.** Bow down to Sri Krishna Chaitanya with His associates —
the limit of compassion, besides whom there is no other.

**2.** Begging them Himself, He distributes the highest goal to the fallen;
the name "Purifier of the fallen" has now come true.

**3.** I shall tell of one more wonder —
how Sri Thakura Mahashaya set out for the land of Gauda.

*[p. 78]*

**4.** Sri Lokanatha Gosani had long
intended to send him to Gauda.

**5.** The three things he forbade him to accept —
that (applied) only to Gauda: he knew it by insight.

**6.** Living here, (Narottama) would avoid those three things of himself,
dependent on the feet of all the Gosvamis.[^1]

**7.** Now a great love had arisen between him
and Sri Acharya Thakura — this (Lokanatha) knew at heart.

**8.** He himself loves him very much;
and so, sending him alone, he foresees various hindrances.

**9.** He knows in his mind: sooner or later, one day,
he will certainly have to go to Gauda.

**10.** Therefore, calling him to a secluded place,
he speaks his heart, moved with mercy:

**11.** "Listen, I shall tell you what I think.
Mahaprabhu spread the sankirtana;

**12.** it cannot be relished without the land of Gauda —
service to Radha and Krishna together with service to the Vaishnavas."

**13.** Thakura Mahashaya is greatly eager for kirtana,
eager to manifest service to Sri Krishna and the Vaishnavas;

**14.** this thought had always been with him, and now — from the guru's lips;
on first hearing it he was very glad.

**15.** But then, remembering the bliss of Vrindavana,
he began to speak, weeping and weeping:

*[p. 79]*

**16.** "Prabhu, here I serve you,
I see Gopala, Govinda and Gopinatha,

**17.** I live in Vrindavana; from the lips of all of you
I hear of the pastimes of Radha and Krishna and rejoice in darshana.

**18.** Let me stay for now; when my heart is ready,
I shall come at once and tell you."

**19.** Gosani says: "Since you must go in any case,
I shall be anxious if you go later.

**20.** With the Acharya you will not grieve,
and I, entrusting you to him, shall be happy."

**21.** Hearing this, he fell silent.
The next day Acharya Thakura came.

**22.** Gosani asked him for a definite answer:
"What have you decided about going to the land of Gauda?"

**23.** He says: "Having circumambulated Sri Govardhana,
the chief places of Vraja, the twelve forests,

**24.** I shall come back and certainly go to Gauda;
there is no doubt of it — that is my secret."

**25.** Hearing this, Gosani called Thakura (Narottama);
with his left hand he took Acharya Thakura's hand,

**26.** with his right — Thakura Narottama's hand,
and placed it in Acharya Thakura's hand.

**27.** With tears, faltering, he speaks sweet words:
"You will cherish my Narottama as your life.

*[p. 80]*

**28.** And he will see you as like me.
Do what pleases me."

**29.** Hearing this, both bowed down to Gosani;
Gosani raised them both and embraced them.

**30.** Thakura bowed down to Acharya Thakura;
Acharya Thakura raised him and embraced him.

**31.** Both thrilled, streams of tears from their eyes;
seeing this, Gosani felt boundless happiness.

**32.** Rising in the morning, both bathed and so on
and took leave of the Gosanis everywhere.

**33.** Sri Jiva Gosani gave them as companion
a knowledgeable Vaishnava to show them all the places.

**34.** In the evening they stopped at Sri Madhupuri;
the next morning, having bathed at Madhuvana,

**35.** having seen Talavana and Kumudavana there,
they spent that night with joyful hearts.

**36.** At dawn, having seen Bahulavana,
coming to Radha-kunda they bathed and so on.

**37.** Offering dandavat obeisances to Sri Dasa Gosani,
they rested there that night.

**38.** In order they told Gosani
the whole story; hearing it, Gosani rejoiced at heart.

**39.** In talk of Krishna (the night) passed like a moment;
rising in the morning, they bathed and performed remembrance.

*[p. 81]*

**40.** Keeping Sri Kunda on their right, Govardhana
they set out to circumambulate, their hearts overflowing.

**41.** The names always on their lips: Radha, Krishna, Govinda;
seeing the places of the pastimes and the service — what bliss!

**42.** Tears, trembling, thrills and other transformations of bhava —
how much shall I write? It is far too extensive.

**43.** Relishing the secret of each place, both
fall to the ground, absorbed (in bhava).

**44.** After a while, regaining awareness, they hurry on again,
go to another place of the pastimes and receive darshana.

**45.** In one place I have written, merely pointing the direction;
know that everywhere it was the same.

**46.** Having circumambulated Govardhana, they returned
and spent that night at the feet of Dasa Gosani.

**47.** In many ways Gosani showed them mercy —
who is there who could describe it?

**48.** How they wept at parting —
telling of that sorrow, I feel great anguish.

**49.** From there, walking on and on, they came to Paramandala[^2]
and, seeing Adi-Badri, bowed down.

**50.** Spending the night there, in the morning they went to Kamyavana
and saw all the places in order.

**51.** From there they came to Vrishabhanupura;
looking at everything, streams of tears flow from their eyes.

*[p. 82]*

**52.** At that time there was neither service nor temple there —
therefore I have not written about it.

**53.** Spending that night and seeing Prema-sarovara,
they rejoiced at the darshana of Sanketa.

**54.** Spending that night there, they went to Nandagrama,
saw the king of Vraja with his associates and bowed down.

**55.** In Pavana-sarovara they bathed and so on;
the bliss that arose cannot be told.

**56.** All around they saw the places of the pastimes;
in the morning, walking on, they came to Khadiravana.

**57.** From there they went to the village called Yavat;
seeing the places of the pastimes, they rested there.

**58.** In the morning, when they saw Kokila-vana —
the bliss that came I cannot describe.

**59.** Having seen Bathain, they see Charana-pahari;
seeing the footprints and other marks, they were very glad.

**60.** Their companion shows them from the hill
all the villages there are around.

**61.** Having seen the secret (of the pastime) there, they went to Dahi-gaon;
that night, in happy talk of Krishna, they spent there.

**62.** In the morning, going to the village of Kotimani,
they found bliss in seeing the kadamba grove.

**63.** From there, walking on, they came to Shesha-shayi;
in the kunda called Kshira-samudra they bathed and performed remembrance.

*[p. 83]*

**64.** They received the darshana of Sri Lakshmi-Narayana
and spent that night there as well.

**65.** The son of the king of Vraja performs the Shesha-shayi pastime —
telling of this, both relished happiness.

**66.** From there they came to the village of Khayra;
having seen Sanjhoi, they rested there.

**67.** Then, having seen Ujani,
they went to rest at Khelana-vana.

**68.** Then Ramaghat, Akshaya-vata,
Gopighat they saw near the Yamuna.

**69.** That day, coming to Chira-ghat, they stayed there;
the next morning they arrived at Nanda-ghat.

**70.** Having bathed and so on, they went on joyfully;
crossing the Sri Yamuna, they went to Bhadravana.

**71.** Then, having bathed at Bhandiravana,
they went to Belavana, deeply absorbed in prema.

**72.** Seeing the forest on the Yamuna's bank, they rejoiced;
seeing the beauty of Vrindavana on the far bank, they were amazed.

**73.** That day they stayed there in the joy of darshana;
the next day they rested at Lohavana.

**74.** Manasa-sarovara lies within Vrindavana;
the Yamuna flows to the north of that lake —

**75.** therefore I have not mentioned it in the account of the circuit.
In the morning they took the road along the Yamuna.

*[p. 84]*

**76.** Walking on and on, reaching the village of Raval,
seeing the birthplace of Sri Radhika —

**77.** the bliss that came could not be contained in their bodies;
they stayed right there — for prema they could not walk.

**78.** Then they set out for Gokula;
seeing its beauty, they rested at Mahavana.

**79.** There, seeing Nanda's house and the various places of the pastimes —
what happiness they felt, only they are witness.

**80.** Then at Mathura, at Vishranti — midday;
spending the day there, in the morning they go to Vrindavana.

**81.** There — the meeting with all the Gosanis.
They considered how to send them to the land of Gauda.

**82.** They would like to send them with money and provisions —
but no one accepts anything; what can be done?

**83.** Then to a merchant whose carts were going to Agra
Sri Jiva Gosani said in secret:

**84.** "Whatever books and other things Acharya Mahashaya has,
take this load and go quickly.

**85.** There, lodging him in your own house,
pay the carter whatever the cart costs.

**86.** Whatever he needs for the road,
give it all together — so that I may be pleased.

**87.** I know: do not tell him this —
knowing it comes from me, he will never accept."

*[p. 85]*

**88.** That merchant (said): "I have always prayed,
but I was never given an order to serve."

**89.** Now, receiving the order, he was filled with joy
and took on himself the charge of sending them to Gauda.

**90.** The next day he said to Acharya Thakura:
"Be merciful, come with me to Agra.

**91.** There we are many merchants;
whatever is to be decided we shall decide there."

**92.** Moved by his humility, Thakura agreed
and went and told Gosani everything.

**93.** Hearing it, Gosani was glad at heart
and then handed over all the books —

**94.** some old, some newly copied;
he had kept them ready beforehand.

**95.** Having handed over everything, in boundless bliss,
they began to think of the farewell.

**96.** Sri Acharya Thakura and Sri Thakura Mahashaya
took leave of everyone, bowing humbly.

**97.** What befell them everywhere at the leave-taking —
for sorrow I cannot describe it in full.

**98.** Mana-sarovara, Kali-hrada and the rest —
all the places they saw in the rapture of prema;

**99.** seeing the samadhis of all the Gosanis,
they wept long, rolling on the ground.

*[p. 86]*

**100.** They went to all the temples and received darshana;
at the parting both were in great agitation.

**101.** Prasada sandal, cloth, tulasi buds,
the dust of the rasa, the dust of (the Vaishnavas') feet — they filled bags.

**102.** When, at the parting, (they stood) before Sri Govinda,
they gazed fixedly at His moonlike face,

**103.** wiping away again and again the streams of tears;
who could describe that yearning?

**104.** At that moment the garland on Govinda's Sri body,
under the weight of great compassion, slipped and fell.

**105.** The pujari brought the garland and gave it to Acharya Thakura;
receiving the garland of mercy, his prema doubled.

**106.** Again and again he rises, falls, offers dandavat obeisances —
tears, trembling, thrills and the other transformations of bhava.

**107.** Clasping everyone's feet, he wept long;
the hearts of all who were there melted.

**108.** Thus some time passed;
the curtain was drawn at Govinda's door.

**109.** Then all together calmed him
and, gradually saying everything, bade him farewell.

**110.** With the greatest difficulty taking courage,
stricken with grief, they reached Agra.

**111.** There all the merchants, gathering together,
humbly hired a cart for them.

*[p. 87]*

**112.** The books are many — the baggage cannot go with them;
understanding this, they arranged everything:

**113.** whatever was needed for the journey,
clothing, vessels — all that was required on the way —

**114.** they gave everything; and then, with royal passes,
each escorted them to the border of his own domain.

**115.** Thus by degrees they came to the land of Gauda.[^3]
I shall tell briefly, as a sutra, some particulars of it.

**116.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya went to Garanhat;
there he carried out the guru's order.

**117.** He relished kirtana in countless special ways;
and the splendour of the service — how much of it can be told?

**118.** His service to the Vaishnavas and Gosanis is wonderful to hear;
practising it himself, he showed the essence of bhakti.

**119.** Acharya Thakura's disciple is Bara Kaviraja Thakura:
with him (Narottama's) love grew immensely.

**120.** The perfection of that love is impossible among (ordinary) people:
hearing of it, the hearts of all beings melt.

**121.** Following his (Narottama's) dancing and relishing,
the "Garanhati kirtana" became famous.[^4]

**122.** Ceaseless absorption in bhava, especially in kirtana —
as if prema incarnate were walking about by itself.

**123.** All the transformations of bhava of a single day —
write from birth on, and still you would not reach the end.

*[p. 88]*

**124.** Sri Acharya Thakura lived at Yajigrama;
he accepted many disciples — Sri Ramachandra Kaviraja and others.

**125.** When he made Bara Kaviraja his disciple,
he then told him this secret:

**126.** "One deeply feeling, skilled in beauty and virtues,
like you — I obtained one eye in Vrindavana.

**127.** One-eyed I have been for many days;
today the most skilful Creator has given me a second eye."[^5]

**128.** Saying this, he drew him onto his lap
and bathed him with the tears of his eyes.

**129.** Kaviraja Thakura, receiving the embrace of mercy,
lost consciousness, his heart melted with prema.

**130.** A single bhava is deep as millions of oceans —
it cannot be understood; what wise man could describe it?

**131.** Seeing it, all the bhagavatas present weep;
their bodies are filled with bliss beyond containing.

**132.** First of all he taught him all the books
and infused into that all his own power.

**133.** Beauty, virtues, Vaishnava character, the limit of learning —
the Creator joined them all in making him.

**134.** Before Sri Acharya Thakura he does not speak a word,
although he is fit to speak.

**135.** Whatever question Acharya Thakura asks,
he answers it most sweetly.

*[p. 89]*

**136.** Whatever order he receives, he never acts otherwise,
not considering his own good or harm.

**137.** "My own arm" — so the Prabhu calls him again and again,
finding occasion, with boundless satisfaction.

**138.** When one hears talk of Radha and Krishna from his lips —
not to speak of people, even stones melt.

**139.** With all the mahantas there are in Sri Gauda
he met and conversed in private.

**140.** Hearing of the passing of Sri Vishnupriya-jiu,
he wept long, beating his head.

**141.** In many ways they tried to have him marry —
Thakura Sarakara and the others;

**142.** at everyone's insistence he married.[^6]
He taught many people the books of bhakti,

**143.** living by the essence of siddhanta and the essence of rasa;
he made known to all the path of raganuga, above everything.

**144.** He carried out Sri Gosani-jiu's order;
thus he lived there for some time.

**145.** His longing for Vrindavana grew;
again, leaving everything, he set out.

**146.** By degrees he came to Sri Vrindavana;
first of all he received the darshana of Sri Bhatta Gosani.

**147.** He offered dandavat obeisances, and he embraced him;
in the rapture of prema guru and disciple both lost consciousness.

*[p. 90]*

**148.** Composing himself with difficulty, seated on his seat,
(Bhatta Gosani) asked all the news of the land of Gauda,

**149.** and gave him the darshana of Sri Radha-ramana;
seeing Him, (Shrinivasa)'s bliss and tears doubled.

**150.** Again he asked: "Have you married?"
He says: "I did not do it (of myself) — why do you ask?"[^7]

**151.** Then he received the darshana of Sri Jiva Gosani,
offered dandavat obeisances and spoke humble words with tears.

**152.** Gosani, overcome with prema, embraced him,
having found reunion after a long time,

**153.** and gave him the darshana of Sri Radha-Damodara;
both were overpowered by rapture, their hearts overflowing.

**154.** Growing calm, he again asked all the news;
Thakura told of the state of the land of Gauda:

**155.** the teaching of the scriptures of bhakti, the spreading of bhakti.
Hearing it, Gosani rejoiced at heart.

**156.** Then, seeing Sri Govinda, Gopala and Gopinatha,
(Shrinivasa) deemed his life fulfilled.

**157.** Having seen and bowed to the adhikari Gosani and everyone,
he partook of maha-prasada.

**158.** Seeing Sri Lokanatha Gosani,
he offered dandavat obeisances, absorbed in prema.

**159.** Gosani, with tears, embraced him with love;
then (Shrinivasa) tells about Sri Thakura Narottama:

*[p. 91]*

**160.** "In the service of Krishna and the Vaishnavas, in detachment from worldly things,
he fully carries out your order."

**161.** Hearing of the relishing of sankirtana, (Lokanatha) floats in bliss;
remembering his virtues, he weeps aloud.

**162.** And, meeting all the mahantas,
he spent some days in great happiness:

**163.** bathing in the Sri Yamuna, darshana of all the Thakuras,
hearing of the pastimes from the Gosanis in all the places.

**164.** The happiness of a single day I cannot tell.
Then Bhatta Gosani, showing mercy to Thakura,

**165.** said: "Having considered it in my mind, I have made you
the adhikari of Radha-ramana.

**166.** In my absence — all authority;
the whole burden of the service — is yours."

**167.** "From today I have so decided,"
he told Sri Jiva Gosani and everyone.

**168.** Hearing it, all rejoiced at heart:
"You have judged worthily, by the essence of sound reasoning."[^8]

**169.** Thus many days passed in bliss.
Meanwhile there Sri Ishvari-jiu grew anxious.

**170.** She called Sri Bara Kaviraja Thakura
and in private told him all the sorrow of her heart:

**171.** "It would be well for you to go to Vrindavana:
it is fitting to find out once how he is."

[^1]: The sense of the couplet (especially the word "parachin") is unclear; translated by sense: in Vrindavana, under the care of the Gosvamis, Narottama would not be threatened by marriage and Bengali food.
[^2]: "Paramandala" — so in the edition; apparently a village in Vraja near Adi-Badri.
[^3]: The theft of the books by the robbers of King Vira Hambira in Vishnupura, told in detail by the *Prema-vilasa* (7), the *Karnananda* (6), the *Narottama-vilasa* and the *Bhakti-ratnakara*, is not mentioned here: the journey to Gauda is described as uneventful.
[^4]: Garanhati (Garanhat) — the district of Kheturi, Narottama's homeland; "garanhati" is the style of kirtana introduced by Narottama.
[^5]: The first "eye" is Narottama, the second Ramachandra Kaviraja; the image is known only from the *Anuraga-valli*.
[^6]: Thakura Sarakara — Narahari Sarakara Thakura of Shrikhanda. Shrinivasa's marriage at the insistence of the Vaishnavas also appears in the *Prema-vilasa* (16), where news of it is brought to Gopala Bhatta in Vrindavana.
[^7]: Literally the answer is "I did not do it"; the sense is apparently "I married not of my own will but at the Vaishnavas' insistence", cf. 141–142.
[^8]: According to the *Anuraga-valli*, Gopala Bhatta makes Shrinivasa adhikari of Radha-ramana; according to the *Prema-vilasa* (25), he entrusted the service of Radha-ramana to Gopinatha.
