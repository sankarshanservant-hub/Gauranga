# Taranga 10. The Great Festival at Kheturi and Jahnava's Journey to Vrindavana

*Ekadashi at Kanchanagariya; the Vaishnavas' journey to Kheturi; Govinda's initiation, Durgadasa and the Deity of Gaura; Santosha Datta; Ramachandra's dream; the dream and initiation of Vamshidasa; the arrival of Shyamananda; Jahnava sets out from Khardaha; the reception of the guests at Kheturi; the installation of the six Deities; Narottama's kirtan; the Holi play; Jahnava's journey to Vrindavana*

**1.** Glory to the Lord of Navadvipa, Sri Gaurasundara!
Glory to Nityananda, the Lord of Ekachakra!

**2.** Glory to Sri Advaita, the ornament of Shantipura!
Glory, glory to all the devotees of the Lord!

**3.** Glory, glory to the listeners, the abode of virtues!
Now listen with kind hearts to what I shall tell.

**4.** Srinivasa Acharya Thakura came from Shrikhanda
to Yajigrama together with his own people.

**5.** With care he bestows on persons of the highest piety
the jewel of the books of the Gosvamis.

**6.** "Bhakti is supreme above all!" he proclaims, thundering, thundering;
hearing [it], the opponents of bhakti flee, subdued.

**7.** In great joy the Acharya's disciples
constantly study the books of bhakti.

**8.** All of them are discerning in all the scriptures, abodes of virtue;
seeing this, the Acharya rejoices greatly at heart.

**9.** "To Sri Gokulananda, Shridasa and the others dear [to me]
I should quickly give the mantra of initiation" — so he resolved.[^1]

**10.** In the midst of the assembly, addressing Sri Gokulananda,
he speaks most sweet words, deeply stirred:

**11.** "Sri Sarakara Thakura, Sri Dasa Gadadhara —
in separation from these two my heart was burned.[^2]

**12.** Unable to remain, I went quickly to Vrindavana;
there too I sank into an ocean of terrible sorrow.

**13.** Last Magha, on the Ekadashi of the dark fortnight,
Haridasa Acharya disappeared in Vrindavana.[^3]

**14.** At the Acharya's disappearance all the Gosvamis —
I cannot tell how stricken they became.

**15.** Keeping me a few days, they all consoled me
and very soon sent me on my way to the land of Gauda.

**16.** By their wish I came swiftly;
now what your hearts desire will come to you all.

**17.** What is there to say — it is the will of all the Prabhus:[^4]
the two brothers will be at peace in every way.

**18.** To honor the day of the Acharya's disappearance
few days remain — preparations are needed for it.

**19.** Go quickly and arrange all that is needed;
in two or four days I will come.

**20.** Do not let your hearts worry about anything:
by the Acharya's mercy everything will be accomplished."

**21.** Hearing this, Sri Gokulananda, together with his brother,
bowed down and took leave that very moment.

**22.** Ramachandra Kaviraja and all the others
went along with them for some distance.

**23.** Having spoken many sweet words to the two,
after a while they returned, each to his own lodging.

**24.** Shridasa and Gokulananda, bidding farewell to all,
came quickly to Kanchanagariya.[^5]

**25.** The people living in the village of Kanchanagariya
came to the house of Gokulananda Acharya.

**26.** Shridasa and Gokulananda, embodiments of affection,
explained everything in detail to the gathering.

**27.** Hearing [this], the eminent people everywhere
gathered provisions beyond all counting.

**28.** They set apart many separate lodgings;[^6]
having made everything ready, they told the two brothers.

**29.** Hearing [this], Sri Gokulananda Acharya and Shridasa
felt great joy in their hearts.

**30.** Seeing the preparation of so many provisions,
someone says to someone in secret:

**31.** "What all these preparations are for, I cannot understand."
Hearing this, someone says to him softly:

**32.** "Haridasa Acharya is a branch [of the tree] of Sri Mahaprabhu;
he is known everywhere, in every respect a great and noble man.

**33.** When Mahaprabhu disappeared from sight in Nilachala,
at His disappearance the three worlds became empty.

**34.** In separation from the Lord, the brahmana Haridasa Acharya
was all but lifeless — his patience did not remain.

**35.** He firmly resolved to give up his body;
I do not know by what command of the Lord he became calm.

**36.** The elder, Sri Gokulananda, and the younger, Shridasa,
he seated beside him, speaking sweet words.

**37.** He told [them] of the life of Srinivasa Acharya
and ordered them to take the mantra of initiation from him.

**38.** At the end of the night he set out for Vrindavana;
he went alone — no one was with him.

**39.** Having gone to Vrindavana, he lived in deep seclusion;
Srinivasa Acharya went there and met him.

**40.** To make Shridasa and Gokulananda his disciples
he ordered the Acharya again and again.

**41.** Srinivasa Acharya came back from Vrindavana,
and then from Gauda he went again to Vrindavana.

**42.** Last Magha Sri Haridasa Acharya
disappeared — Srinivasa heard of it on the way.

**43.** Srinivasa Acharya became greatly distressed;
Dvija Haridasa consoled him in a dream.

**44.** Having been to Vrindavana, Srinivasa came back again;
hearing of his arrival, everyone went to him.

**45.** With great affection he asked Shridasa and Gokulananda
about their welfare and told the two of them everything.

**46.** Having quickly sent the two to Kanchanagariya,
he himself is coming [here], bringing many with him.

**47.** On this auspicious day, the Ekadashi of the dark fortnight of Magha,
he will give initiation to the sons of Haridasa Acharya.

**48.** This is the day of the Acharya's disappearance;
there will be a great festival — [for it] are these great preparations.[^7]

**49.** Great bhagavatas will come here;
the ocean of the joy of sankirtana will surge.

**50.** I came to a relative's house on some business,
and so I came to hear of all this.

**51.** For as many days as this joy will be here,
for so many days I will stay here by all means."

**52.** Saying many such things, the two go off about other business.
At that time the cry of "Hari!" filled the town.

**53.** People run in all directions, unable to restrain their hearts;
seeing this, someone asks them:

**54.** "On what business are you going, and where, in such a hurry?"
Hearing this, someone says in great delight:

**55.** "Acharya Thakura has come from Yajigrama;
hearing it from people's lips, we are going for his darshana."

**56.** Hearing this, he goes, his body covered with the thrill of rapture,
and sees a great crowd at the house of Sri Gokulananda.

**57.** Having seen Sri Acharya Thakura,
everyone considers himself blessed.

**58.** All praise Shridasa and Gokulananda —
what their conduct was cannot be told.

**59.** Shridasa and Gokulananda, going forward to meet him,
overwhelmed with joy, brought the Acharya into the house.

**60.** With what honor [they] received Ramachandra Kaviraja and all the others —
who knows how to describe it?

**61.** Wherever invitations had been sent,
from each of those places the bhagavatas came.

**62.** Which Vaishnavas came from where —
that I do not describe: who could describe it?

**63.** Seeing the multitude of Vaishnavas, Gokula and Shridasa
cannot keep their hearts composed — a wondrous joy![^8]

**64.** They show [them] honor in a way that cannot be told;
seeing that endeavor, everyone feels great bliss.

**65.** The respectable people living in the village of Kanchanagariya —
all were engaged in every way at every moment.[^9]

**66.** People from other villages, with various gifts,
come from all sides, greatly jubilant.

**67.** Having seen the Sri mahantas,
someone speaks sweet words to someone:

**68.** "Since my birth I have never seen such splendor;
I had heard [of him], and now I have seen this Acharya Prabhu.

**69.** Ah, how breathtaking! What wondrous beauty of the Vaishnavas!
I think there is nothing in the world to compare with this assembly.

**70.** In my heart there is this one sorrow: staying [here] tomorrow, all of them,
having completed the ceremony, will leave in the morning.

**71.** The day after tomorrow no one [of them] will remain."
Hearing this, someone says with a smiling face:

**72.** "Tomorrow is the Ekadashi of the dark fortnight of Magha;
that is why you have reckoned so, it seems to me.

**73.** What non-Vaishnavas do on Sri Ekadashi,
these Vaishnavas cannot do.

**74.** It is the Vaishnavas who know the truth of Sri Ekadashi;
they will carefully complete the ceremony on dvadashi.[^10]

**75.** How much shall I tell of the observance of Sri Ekadashi?
Today everyone will eat rice and the rest once.

**76.** On Sri Ekadashi no one in this assembly of Vaishnavas
will take food or water.

**77.** On the day of dvadashi they will eat once —
such is the established rule of Sri Ekadashi.

**78.** I tell you in private what is on your mind:[^11]
this circle of Vaishnavas has no other rites.

**79.** On the day of dvadashi, with great care,
they will offer various foods to Krishna.

**80.** Filling divine vessels with the items of Krishna's prasada,
they will offer [them] with care to Haridasa Acharya.

**81.** Thus I have heard of many observances of the Vaishnavas;
you do not know, and so I have told you something of them."

**82.** Hearing this, the other says, "It is so, it is so!
How could a person without bhakti understand this intention?"

**83.** Having said this, his heart melted;
seeing this, the other speaks again:

**84.** "You thought that all would leave the day after tomorrow —
the day after tomorrow there will be the great festival.

**85.** Apart from today, all will stay [here] four days:
in supreme joy, behold [them] to your eyes' fill.

**86.** The mass of happiness of sankirtana, rare even for the gods,
hear it in great bliss day and night."

**87.** Thus, speaking of much with one another in private,
all float in the ocean of bhakti-rasa.

**88.** Considering themselves blessed, with jubilant hearts,
they fall prostrate at the feet of the revered Vaishnavas.

**89.** They praise Sri Gokulananda and Shridasa;
what the two do cannot be told.

**90.** On the day of Dashami the two, with their own people,
poured a rain of the nectar of prema in sankirtana.

**91.** On the day of Ekadashi — how wonderful the conduct of the two:
they will receive mantra initiation — and at this they rejoice.

**92.** Srinivasa Acharya, on the holy day of Ekadashi,
gave the two initiation into the Radha-Krishna mantra.

**93.** Having made them disciples by a wonderful rite, he rejoiced
and entrusted them to the feet of Radha-Krishna and Chaitanya.

**94.** The two fall at the feet of Srinivasa Acharya,
overwhelmed with prema, bathed in tears of bliss.

**95.** As Acharya Thakura embraces the two,
on all sides everyone raises the cry of "Hari!"

**96.** All the Vaishnavas, from the conduct of the two brothers,
obtained such joy — how can I describe it?

**97.** How on the holy Ekadashi there were holy discourses and kirtana —
that the fortunate poets will describe.

**98.** Shridasa and Gokulananda Acharya on Dvadashi
carefully prepare many kinds of foods.

**99.** [When all] was ready, they informed the Acharya;
Acharya Thakura offered the bhoga to Krishna.

**100.** Knowing that the Lord had finished His meal,
he removes the bhoga, his whole body filled with prema.

**101.** Having given achamana, he offered tambula;
at the sight of the beauty of the naivedya his heart was soothed.

**102.** In another vessel, with great care, prasada
he offered to Haridasa Acharya in solitude.

**103.** What wonder there was as he offered the bhoga —
that rapture of prema I cannot describe at all.

**104.** Knowing that the meal was finished, he gave achamana
and with care offered prasada tambula and the rest.

**105.** What joy there was then in the hearts of the Vaishnavas,
what wonderful doings — who will describe them?

**106.** Shridasa submits to Sri Acharya Thakura,
"The place has been prepared; what is your order?"

**107.** Hearing this, Sri Acharya with care seated all the Vaishnavas
in wonderful rows in a lovely place.

**108.** One after another the servers serve [the food];
the fragrance of the rice and the rest attracts every heart.

**109.** Calling out "Hari, Hari!", all the Vaishnavas
eat the prasada, overwhelmed with great joy.

**110.** Having finished the meal, all performed achamana;
whose heart would not be gladdened at the sight of it?

**111.** Everywhere there are huge crowds of people;
they eat many kinds of mahaprasada.

**112.** How many people ate — there is no counting them;
in the village of Kanchanagariya there is boundless joy.

**113.** Srinivasa Acharya Thakura, rejoicing,
ate prasada after feeding all the people.

**114.** Sri Gokulananda, Shridasa and the others, in rapture,
ate at the end the remnants on Prabhu's plate.[^12]

**115.** When the meal and all the rest were completed, all
came in great happiness to the place of sankirtana.

**116.** All of them are embodiments of bhakti, abodes of happiness;
seeing that beauty, all the people are amazed.

**117.** On all sides everyone raises the cry of "Hari!";
as the sankirtana begins, the ocean of prema surges.

**118.** The dancing, singing and music are beyond comparison;
the happiness that was in the sankirtana — who can describe it?

**119.** Thus, overwhelmed with the bliss of sankirtana,
the Vaishnavas know neither night nor day.

**120.** To part even for a moment from Srinivasa Acharya Thakura, full of prema —
I do not know what [their] hearts go through.

**121.** Four or five days they stayed in great joy;
when the time came to take leave, they lost all composure.

**122.** Lovingly consoling Shridasa and Gokulananda,
in the morning they set out from Kanchanagariya.

**123.** Telling one another of the charming deeds of the two,
the Vaishnavas went to their own homes.

**124.** How the two brothers fared in separation from the Vaishnavas —
in telling of all that, the heart breaks every time.

**125.** Srinivasa Acharya with care steadied the two
and with his people stayed [there] two to four days.

**126.** The devotion to the guru of Sri Gokulananda and Shridasa —
what power have I to describe it with a single mouth?

**127.** What took place in Kanchanagariya and other villages
I have not been able to describe here in detail.

**128.** All the fortunate ones of Kanchanagariya
satisfied their eyes, ears, minds and hearts.

**129.** The news of the great festival spread everywhere;
the Acharya with his people was overjoyed.

**130.** Although the best of acharyas held to patience,
still he was restless without his dear Narottama.

**131.** Taking with him his most experienced disciples,
he sets out quickly for the village of Sri Kheturi.

**132.** I shall give here some of the disciples' names,
by hearing which all sorrow goes far away.

**133.** Ramachandra Kaviraja, a treasury of virtues;
Shridasa, the merciful Gokulananda Acharya;

**134.** Sri Krishnavallabha, a resident of the village of Deuli;
Vyasa Acharya Chakravarti, renowned, a wealth of bhakti;[^13]

**135.** the embodiment of bhakti Sri Vallavikanta Kaviraja,
at the sight of whom the company of great unbelievers trembles;[^14]

**136.** Sri Nrisimha Kaviraja, who is a great poet,
whose brother Narayana is the best of poets;

**137.** Karnapura Kaviraja, supremely wise —
hearing his poetry, no one can remain unmoved;[^15]

**138.** Bhagavan Kaviraja, an abode of virtues,
whose brother is Rupa; Nimu and Vira of Bhaumalaya;[^16]

**139.** Sri Gokula, living at Seragarh in Panchakuta,
formerly living at Kadhai, a king of poets, unequalled in bhakti;[^17]

**140.** the best of brahmanas Ramakrishna and Kumuda — these two;[^18]
the virtues of these two brothers cannot be told;

**141.** Shyamadasa Chakravarti and Sri Ramacharana,
by worldly kinship the Acharya's two brothers-in-law;[^19]

**142.** Sri Rupa Ghataka, whose home is in Yajigrama;
Sri Gopala Dasa, a resident of Kanchanagariya.

**143.** With these disciples Acharya Thakura
went some distance from Kanchanagariya.

**144.** He says to Ramachandra with a slight smile,
"I shall go to the village of Kheturi by way of Budhari.

**145.** Your younger brother is in the village of Teliya Budhari;[^20]
who will give him the news of [our] journey?"

**146.** Ramachandra says, "There is no need to tell him:
the news that Prabhu has set out has spread everywhere."[^21]

**147.** Just then a man from Budhari,
coming very quickly, had the darshan of the Acharya.

**148.** Falling to the ground, he bows again and again;
asked about their welfare, he tells the news:

**149.** "All is auspicious, Prabhu, in seeing you.
Sri Govinda and the others are watching the road.

**150.** When Prabhu went to Vrindavana, Ramachandra went [too];
Govinda came to the village of Teliya Budhari.

**151.** He surrendered himself at Prabhu's feet
and always thinks, 'In how many days shall I see him?'

**152.** Prabhu set out from Vrindavana;
taking Ramachandra, he came to Vana-Vishnupura.

**153.** Coming to Yajigrama, he destroyed all sorrow;
in Kantaka-nagara and in Khanda there was great happiness.

**154.** Coming with his people to the village of Kanchanagariya,
he immersed everyone in the great festival.

**155.** From Kanchanagariya he set out on his way —
all this news of Prabhu has spread everywhere.

**156.** I have attained the goal of life in seeing Prabhu;
blessed is this land, to which [he] has come."

**157.** Having said much in this way, he bowed at [his] holy feet
and bowed to Ramachandra and all the others.

**158.** Taking leave, he came quickly to Budhari
and announced that Sri Acharya Prabhu was coming.

**159.** Hearing of the coming of Srinivasa Acharya,
people run from all sides to see [him].

**160.** Sri Govinda and others, with great joy in their hearts,
perform auspicious rites in every way.[^22]

**161.** Quickly they had the lodging put in order
and, going out to meet him, all brought the Acharya.

**162.** How they brought Sri Acharya to the lodging,
how all were immersed in the Acharya's beauty,

**163.** how they received the Acharya's disciples with honour,
what happiness there was in Teliya Budhari in every house,

**164.** how all kinds of provisions were prepared,
how people came and went all the time,

**165.** how prema-bhakti arose in all the people —
I have no power to describe all that in detail.

**166.** Moment by moment Govinda's joy grows ever greater;
he makes a request to his elder [brother] Ramachandra:

**167.** "Deliver me, an ignorant one, yourself:
offer [me] at the feet of Sri Acharya Prabhu."

**168.** Saying much like this, bathed in tears,
he bows at the feet of his revered elder brother.

**169.** Seeing how deeply agitated Govinda's heart was,
the most wise Ramachandra was immersed in a surge of affection.

**170.** Consoling Govinda, he went before Sri Acharya
and told him in detail what was in Govinda's heart.

**171.** Hearing this, Sri Acharya, with great joy in his heart,
gave Govinda initiation into the mantra of Radha-Krishna.

**172.** The wonderful rite by which he made Govinda his disciple,
the joy everyone felt at the hour of initiation,

**173.** the rapture of prema Govinda was in, having become a disciple —
all this the fortunate will describe in detail.

**174.** Ramachandra and Govinda, rejoicing at every moment,
together with [the whole] circle serve Sri Acharya Prabhu.

**175.** What mercy the Acharya has for these two brothers,
Ramachandra and Govinda — what power have I to tell it?

**176.** In the Acharya's mind is one [thought]: "How soon
will Sri Narottama meet Ramachandra?"

**177.** Thinking this, he again says to Ramachandra:
"It seems to me that Narottama will come here.

**178.** For many days I have had no news of him;
I sent [him] a letter with this news of mine some time ago.

**179.** How will he know that I have come here?
Let someone go quickly to where he is."

**180.** Just as he said this, a brahmana from there
came and appeared before the Acharya.

**181.** How wonderful his behaviour, how much [feeling] rises in his heart!
In great joy he gazes at the Acharya.

**182.** Seeing the beauty of the Acharya surrounded by his disciples,
he bows to the ground, his eyes full of tears of love.

**183.** Sri Acharya, seeing the brahmana, was greatly pleased
and in the sweetest words said, "Tell me who you are."

**184.** The brahmana says, "I live in the village of Kheturi;
I am the lowest of brahmanas, and my name is Durgadasa.

**185.** Sri Thakura Narottama, seeing me fallen,
lifted [me] out of the pit of the filth of worldly things.

**186.** When I heard that Prabhu had come here —
I cannot say what arose in my heart.

**187.** Telling no one, I set out in the morning;
I have reached my goal, seeing Prabhu's feet."

**188.** Hearing the brahmana's words, the Acharya, pleased,
asks for good news of Sri Narottama.

**189.** The brahmana says, "Having come from Nilachala,
he refuted the doctrines of the pashandas, revealing bhakti.

**190.** With the qualities of Sri Krishna Chaitanya, Nityananda and Advaita
he brought the fallen and the wicked into great rapture.

**191.** He made five Deities of Sri Krishna with [His] beloved
and obtained the Deity of Sri Gaura with [His] beloved.[^23]

**192.** However they tried to hide the story of how it was obtained, it could not be hidden;
how it was obtained I shall tell a little.

**193.** Near Gopalpura there is a small village;
there lives a fortunate [man] named Vipradasa.

**194.** In his house is a granary for paddy, mustard seed and so on;
no one can go there for fear of snakes.

**195.** Why the snakes have taken possession [of it], no one understands;
when mantras and herbs are applied, the snakes hiss incessantly.

**196.** I do not know what came into Sri Thakura's mind:
at daybreak he quickly went there.

**197.** Vipradasa came out and bowed at [his] feet
and very humbly asked, 'For what purpose have you come?'

**198.** [The Thakura] says to Vipradasa, 'In this paddy granary
there is something I need; that is why I have come here.'

**199.** Vipradasa, distressed, pleads:
'Please do not go near the granary: there is danger of snakes there.'

**200.** Hearing this, the Mahashaya says with a slight smile,
'Do not worry; the snakes will run away.'

**201.** Saying this, he began to open the big door of the granary —
and all saw with their own eyes the snakes vanish.

**202.** From the granary Sri Gaurasundara with His beloved
came into his arms, and [this] was visible to every eye.

**203.** Taking Sri Gaurasundara with His beloved in his arms,
Sri Thakura Mahashaya came to his dwelling.

**204.** How the sankirtana began at that time,
how prema was revealed — that cannot be told.

**205.** Sri Mahashaya's disciple is Sri Santosha Datta;[^24]
he accomplishes every task — [a man] of the greatest nobility.

**206.** He built a temple and a throne [for the Deities]
and made preparations for a great festival.

**207.** Who can know what is in Sri Mahashaya's heart?
Prabhu, he is always gazing down the road for you.

**208.** He heard the news that Prabhu had come here —
I do not know in what an ocean of happiness he drowned.

**209.** 'Today I shall cross the Padmavati and stay [there];
tomorrow at daybreak I shall come here.'"[^25]

**210.** Hearing of Narottama's deeds, Sri Acharya,
together with his own [associates], was filled with great jubilation.

**211.** He showed great favour to the brahmana Durgadasa
and made known to all the greatness of Narottama.

**212.** All were immersed in Narottama's qualities,
and the cry went up: "Tomorrow he will come here!"

**213.** The joy of the villagers is boundless;
all praise their good fortune to one another.

**214.** With this thought — "How soon will the night end?
[Then] we shall go for darshan to Ramachandra's house" —

**215.** no one is able to leave Ramachandra's house;
with great difficulty [people] spend the night in their own homes.

**216.** Ramachandra's house is full of supreme bliss:
there the Acharya delights together with his associates.

**217.** How great the Acharya's affection for Ramachandra is —
I, greatly ignorant, what power have I to tell it?

**218.** Ramachandra Kaviraja is an ocean of virtues;
his extraordinary deeds are known everywhere.

**219.** Having heard from the brahmana's mouth of Narottama's coming,
he did not reveal what arose in his heart.

**220.** Diligently completing every task,
he passed the day and the night until the second watch.

**221.** When Sri Acharya with his associates had lain down to sleep,
in solitude he contemplates Narottama's qualities.

**222.** At the mere name "Narottama" he cannot remain steady:
the hairs of his body stand on end, how much [feeling] rises in his heart!

**223.** "Why has this come over me?" — as he ponders this,
he has no wish to lie down, and sleep does not touch his eyes.

**224.** By the Lord's will sleep overcame him for a little while,
and in the guise of a dream Sri Gaurasundara appeared to him.

**225.** His beautiful body surpasses millions of Kandarpas;
what comparison is there — gold, lightning, saffron?

**226.** On His head are lovely, glossy, curling locks;
on His neck sways a forest garland that enchants the worlds.

**227.** His moonlike face surpasses the autumn moon;
how long His eyes, how matchless His glance!

**228.** Swinging His arms that reach to His knees,
with the gait of a lordly elephant He came and stood.

**229.** Seeing Gaurachandra, Ramachandra Kaviraja —
who knows what joy surges in his heart!

**230.** He fell and rolled [on the ground] at the Lord's feet;
the Lord, taking him into His embrace, bathes him with tears of love.

**231.** With a slight smile He says in the sweetest words:
"By My will you do not know yourself.

**232.** You are dear to Me, and Narottama is dear to Me;
seeing each other, you two will remember what was before.

**233.** You two will bestow My prema-bhakti
and free living beings from the cruel threefold suffering."

**234.** Saying much like this, showing great mercy,
the Lord Gaurahari disappeared.

**235.** No longer seeing the Lord, Ramachandra cannot be calm:
streams flow from his eyes like the current of a river.

**236.** Seeing his distress, the Lord again consoled [him]
and in the guise of a dream informed Srinivasa Acharya.

**237.** Who can understand the Lord's wondrous lila?
That the Lord is subject to His devotees' love is known to the whole world.

**238.** Ramachandra is utterly absorbed in the Lord's qualities;
on waking, he sees that morning has come.

**239.** Having done his morning duties, he thinks to himself:
"How soon shall I meet the Mahashaya?"

**240.** At that time a man came in great haste
and, bowing to Sri Acharya, reported:

**241.** "From the village of Kheturi across the Padmavati
Sri Thakura Mahashaya is coming here.

**242.** What a wondrous gait! His radiance is like the sun;
and those who come with him — how beautiful they all are!

**243.** I came here having seen him a short way off;
seeing him, I do not know what has happened to my heart."

**244.** The Acharya, hearing of Narottama's coming,
went forward at once with his associates.

**245.** Coming out of the house, he sees Narottama;
the moment he sees him, his heart swells with such happiness!

**246.** When Narottama bowed to Acharya Thakura,
the Acharya took him into his embrace and cannot let him go.

**247.** What wondrous bliss of prema grows in the two of them!
Seeing it, everyone was greatly amazed.

**248.** Sri Acharya Thakura introduced Thakura Narottama
to Shridasa Acharya and his other dear ones.[^26]

**249.** The wonderful meeting that took place between them —
who has the power to describe it with one mouth?

**250.** Ramachandra, beholding Narottama,
lost his composure, remembering what had been before.[^27]

**251.** It did not become especially evident — only a little;
some, even though they understood, did not make it known.

**252.** Sri Acharya, taking Narottama by the hand
and seating him in private, asks after his welfare.

**253.** Mahashaya replies with exceedingly sweet words:
"Now, by seeing you, every good thing has come about.

**254.** Prabhu ordered me to go to Gauda
[for] the service of the Deities, the service of the Vaishnavas, and Sri sankirtana.

**255.** And in this the Deities showed Their mercy,
and the temple and all the other requisites came about.

**256.** On the full moon of Phalguna the Deities
you yourself would seat upon the throne — this was in my mind.

**257.** I thought that you would come here soon;
but there has been a delay of many days."

**258.** Hearing this, the Acharya says slowly:
"Who can understand what the Lord's will is?"[^28]

**259.** Saying this, he told of his marriage
and related in detail the journey to Vrindavana and the rest.[^29]

**260.** What took place in Mahashaya's heart on hearing this —
who else has the power to understand it?

**261.** In many conversations with each other they rejoiced
and thus passed the time until the second watch of the night.

**262.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya and all the others
lay down to sleep, each in a separate place.

**263.** Sri Acharya Thakura cannot bring himself to lie down,
in anxiety over how the task is to be accomplished.

**264.** Within himself he says: "Will Mahaprabhu's dear ones
come to the village of Kheturi?

**265.** Will Gaura-raya fulfill [this] desire?"
Saying this, he is awash in the streams from both his eyes.

**266.** The Lord cannot bear His devotee's anxiety;
drawing sleep upon him, He appeared to him in the guise of a dream.

**267.** Before Srinivasa, with what sweet grace,
gently smiling, He speaks slowly:

**268.** "O Srinivasa! Do not worry at all;
quickly send letters of invitation everywhere.

**269.** Although all of them are troubled at heart,
as soon as they come here great joy will arise.

**270.** They will see My wondrous pastime with their own eyes,
they will obtain great bliss, and their desire will be fulfilled.

**271.** Every task will be accomplished without effort."
Saying this, Mahaprabhu disappeared.

**272.** No longer seeing the Lord, the Acharya is deeply distressed;
by the Lord's will he regained some composure.

**273.** When the night gave way to dawn, all gathered together
and quickly set about writing letters to every place.

**274.** Great joy pervaded Ramachandra and the others;
they prepared many letters of invitation.

**275.** The sweet verses he wrote in the letters —
on hearing them, whose composure would not vanish?

**276.** Giving the letters to fifteen most capable persons,[^30]
he sent them to Navadvipa and other places.

**277.** To where Shyamananda lives in the land of Utkala
he quickly sent a messenger with a letter.

**278.** Word spread everywhere: "On the full moon of Phalguna
there will be a great, great festival in the village of Kheturi."

**279.** In Teliya, Budhari, Bahadurpur and other
villages an ocean of bliss surged.

**280.** All sing the glories of Sri Krishna Chaitanya;
seeing this, whose mind would not be soothed?

**281.** Sri Acharya Thakura and Thakura Mahashaya,
together with their associates, think of the welfare of all.

**282.** In Ramachandra's home there is a most wondrous pastime:
a beautiful manifestation of kirtana, rare even for the gods.

**283.** How the days and nights passed, no one knew;
all were overwhelmed by the bliss of sankirtana.

**284.** Mahashaya's dear disciple Sri Gokula,
Sri Devidasa and others are matchless in every quality.[^31]

**285.** What the Acharya's state was at the playing and singing
of Sri Gokula, Devidasa and the others — who knows how to describe it?

**286.** One day the Acharya, his heart in great agitation,
told Mahashaya something in private — I do not know what.

**287.** He entrusted dear Ramachandra to Narottama;
Narottama was as if immersed in an ocean of happiness.

**288.** Who can understand this way of the Acharya's?
Having entrusted Ramachandra, he was very joyful.

**289.** Giving him Ramachandra and a few others as companions,
he sent him to Kheturi, saying, "I will come soon."

**290.** Narottama took his leave and quickly
crossed the Padmavati and went to Kheturi.

**291.** Having bid farewell to Mahashaya, Sri Acharya
stays on in the village of Budhari, unable to be at ease.

**292.** Ramachandra's younger brother, Sri Govinda, a treasury of bhakti,[^32]
is absorbed day and night in the rasa of serving the Acharya.

**293.** Seeing Govinda's devotion, Acharya Thakura
showed him the utmost mercy, beyond words.

**294.** To describe the pastimes of Sri Krishna Chaitanya
he ordered Govinda, with great joy in his heart.

**295.** By Prabhu's order he describes [them] in prose, verse and song;[^33]
hearing all of it, whose heart would not melt?

**296.** Sri Acharya was delighted by Govinda's poetry
and, praising Govinda, gave him the renowned name "Kaviraja."

**297.** He had Shridasa and his other dear ones sing these songs;
a rain of the nectar of song fell, enchanting every mind.

**298.** Wherever his dear ones were living, unknown,
he mercifully draws them all to himself.

**299.** Near Budhari is the village of Bahadurpur;
there lives the best of brahmanas, named Shyamadasa.

**300.** His younger brother is Vamshidasa Chakravarti;[^34]
the Creator fashioned him as if an embodiment of affection.

**301.** From an early age he was eager for study;
seeing his diligence, everyone is pleased.

**302.** He has the greatest attachment to Sri Krishna Chaitanya;
he constantly relishes the pastimes of Radha and Krishna.

**303.** Being uninitiated, he is very anxious at heart:
where he is to receive initiation — nothing comes to mind.

**304.** Hearing of the Acharya's arrival in the village of Budhari,
he grew very eager to see him.

**305.** Going quickly, he sees: in Sri Govinda's house
the Acharya is engaged in talk about Krishna.

**306.** On all sides he is surrounded by all his dear ones,
and all of them gaze at the Acharya's beauty.

**307.** Seeing the Acharya from afar, Vamshidasa
fell to the ground and bowed in deep humility.

**308.** Moment by moment his bliss grows beyond measure;
what arises in his mind he does not express.

**309.** After beholding Sri Acharya for some time,
he went home, wet with tears.

**310.** Seeing Vamshi's behavior, the worthy people reflect:
"He is surely a recipient of the Acharya's mercy.

**311.** With a single glance the Acharya infused power into him;
yet no one knew what was in the Acharya's mind.

**312.** Vamshidasa, dear to the Acharya, is very composed;
we cannot fathom his deep character."

**313.** Sitting in solitude, he reflects within himself:
"Will Sri Acharya Prabhu give me shelter at his feet?"

**314.** Thus pondering much, troubled at heart,
he passed the day and the night until the third watch.

**315.** At the end of the night sleep suddenly came over him,
and in the guise of a dream the Acharya comes to Vamshi.

**316.** With what wondrous grace — a charming gait;
his body, full of prema, sways.

**317.** Two long eyes, an incomparable glance;
who could keep composure, seeing the beauty of his face?

**318.** Gently smiling, he looks at Vamshi;
knowing him as his own master, Vamshi falls at his holy feet.

**319.** Overcome with affection, Acharya Thakura embraces
Vamshidasa and speaks to him in sweet words:

**320.** "A great festival will take place in the village of Kheturi;
for this Sri Narottama came to take [me with him].

**321.** I quickly sent them off
and stayed [here] for the sake of all of you.

**322.** Do not worry: when night gives way to dawn, I will make [you] my disciple
and take all of you with me to Kheturi."

**323.** Saying this, he placed his foot on Vamshi's head
and, showing great favor, vanished.

**324.** When his Prabhu vanished, [Vamshi's] heart was greatly agitated;
waking, he sees that the night is ending and dawn is breaking.

**325.** Having performed his morning duties, he went with a joyful heart
to where Sri Acharya was happily staying with his associates.

**326.** How he fell at the Acharya's feet and humbly pleaded —
whose heart would not break on hearing it?

**327.** Taking Sri Acharya Prabhu together with his associates,
he came to his own house in great joy.

**328.** Sri Acharya Prabhu, absorbed in great bliss,
gave Vamshidasa initiation into the mantra of Radha and Krishna.

**329.** By a supreme and wonderful rite he made him his disciple;
for fear of making the book too long I have not described it.

**330.** In the same way, showing favor to several others as well,
he sets out for Kheturi with his associates in great joy.

**331.** Very quickly crossing the [river] Sri Padmavati,
he sent word to the village of Kheturi.

**332.** Hearing of the arrival of Sri Acharya Thakura,
Sri Thakura Narottama was overwhelmed with joy.

**333.** Together with Ramachandra and his other dear ones
he very quickly went forward to receive him.

**334.** Sri Santosha Datta, taking his own people with him,
went ahead to the Acharya and introduced himself.

**335.** In the same way he made known his own disciples too;
all bowed at the Acharya's lotus feet.

**336.** How Sri Acharya showed mercy to everyone —
the fortunate will describe that at length.

**337.** How the dear ones met one another —
for fear of length it is not described.

**338.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, in supreme bliss,
leads Acharya Thakura to the lodging [prepared for him].

**339.** Everywhere the news rang out: "The Acharya has come!"
From all sides people run to see [him].

**340.** Seeing the Acharya with his associates,
each one joyfully praises his own good fortune.

**341.** How the Acharya cast his glance [upon them] —
that I cannot describe here in detail.

**342.** Mahashaya, leading the Acharya with his associates,
in great bliss enters a secluded dwelling.

**343.** Seeing the place, the Acharya praised [it]; to the dear ones
separate lodgings were given close by.

**344.** Sri Santosha Datta, with great joy in his heart,
had already appointed people to the lodgings.

**345.** They accomplish all the work in every way;
seeing Santosha's efforts, the Acharya is filled with contentment (santosha).[^35]

**346.** Sri Acharya with his associates soon leaves the lodging
and in great joy goes to behold the Deities.

**347.** To where they had placed the Deity of Sri Gaura with [His] beloved
Sri Thakura Mahashaya led him.

**348.** What became of Sri Acharya on seeing Mahaprabhu
cannot be described.

**349.** Seeing also the five [other] Deities,
he lost his composure; his heart overflowed with happiness.[^36]

**350.** Seeing all the supplies gathered and arranged for the great festival,
seeing the lodgings and the rest, he was supremely joyful.

**351.** Shyamananda was to come from Utkala;
seeing his delay, [the Acharya] was anxious at heart.

**352.** No sooner had he begun to speak of Sri Shyamananda's qualities
than he heard from people of his arrival.

**353.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, with a joyful heart,
going out to meet him, sees: [Shyamananda] has come to the Acharya's residence.

**354.** How they showed prema to one another —
that the greatly fortunate saw.

**355.** Sri Acharya, Narottama and Shyamananda — all three —
think within themselves about the coming of the Prabhus.[^37]

**356.** The journey of them all I tell here briefly;
I will tell it in detail in the book *Narottama-vilasa*.[^38]

**357.** In the village of Khardaha Sri Jahnava Ishvari
fixes the day to set out for Kheturi.

**358.** At that time the Lord, remaining unseen, commands:[^39]
"Delay in going to the village of Kheturi cannot be borne.

**359.** There Srinivasa and Narottama with their associates
are watching the road, waiting for all of you.

**360.** Srinivasa and Narottama are My dear servants;
I will fulfill all that I intend.

**361.** With My dear associates, manifest and unmanifest,
I will dance and sing in that wonderful sankirtana.

**362.** All will see this amazing pastime;
you will be overwhelmed — such will be the joy.

**363.** Having completed the great festival in great bliss,
I will go to Vrindavana and soon return here."

**364.** Saying this, He showed Himself and vanished;
Ishvari, overwhelmed, looks in all four directions.

**365.** The tears of bliss from her eyes cannot be checked;
at that very moment she set out for Sri Kheturi.

**366.** All this became known everywhere;
who has the power to tell what bliss arose?

**367.** Extraordinary are the ways of Sri Jahnava Ishvari;
what power have I to tell how she prepared for the journey?

**368.** The people of Khardaha and other villages
all came quickly to see [her].

**369.** Sri Jahnava Devi satisfied them all;
much as worldly custom requires, she assigned charge of everything.

**370.** What she told Sri Vasu[dha] Devi in secret,
how she took leave [of her] — who knows how to describe?

**371.** With great care consoling Ganga and Virabhadra,
she leaves Khardaha, remembering the Lord.

**372.** With her went the great devotees,
by whose sight the three worlds are purified:

**373.** Krishnadasa Sarakhel, Madhava Acharya,[^40]
Raghupati Vaidya Upadhyaya, most noble,

**374.** Sri Minaketana Ramadasa, Manohara,
Murari Chaitanya, Jnanadasa, Mahidhara,

**375.** Sri Shankara, Sri Kamalakara Pippalai,
Nrisimha Chaitanya, Jiva, Pandita Kanai,

**376.** Gauranga, Nakadi, Krishnadasa, Damodara,
Sri Parameshvari, the most learned Balarama,

**377.** Sri Mukunda, Vrindavana Dasa and others —
with all of them Ishvari goes happily.

**378.** And all the maidservants and others are on all four sides;
that unprecedented beauty destroys everyone's composure.

**379.** For some distance Sri Jahnava went without a conveyance;
[then], shrinking with modesty, she mounted a litter carried by men.

**380.** At the sight of Sri Jahnava Ishvari's journey,
in village after village, in place after place, crowds of people gather.

**381.** Nayana Bhaskara, who lived in the village of Halisahar,[^41]
also set out quickly in great joy.

**382.** The son of the lame Bhagavan, Raghunatha Acharya,
noble in all virtues, also came and joined them.[^42]

**383.** The learned men who lived in that region
all went along with Sri Ishvari.

**384.** A fortunate merchant, a servant of Nityananda,
goes along with the Lord[^43] — there is no end to that happiness.

**385.** Great crowds gathered; she came to Ambika;
Sri Chaitanya Dasa came and met her there.

**386.** He who is known everywhere and worthy in every way
is the son of Sri Vamshidasa, dear to Gaura.[^44]

**387.** At that very moment he set out with Sri Ishvari;
Sri Hridaya Chaitanya also set out —

**388.** Sri Hridayananda, expert in bestowing bhakti,
to whose love Sri Chaitanya and Nityananda are subject.[^45]

**389.** How Jahnava Ishvari fed Sri Chaitanya and Nityananda
with rice and other foods — I cannot describe.

**390.** All the devotees full of prema in the region of Ambika
set out — joy arose in their hearts.

**391.** Drawing near to Navadvipa, everyone
gazes with unblinking eyes toward Navadvipa.

**392.** Remembering the Lord's pastimes, they lose their composure
and heave long sighs like tongues of flame.

**393.** A loud wailing arose; they float in tears;
fainting, all fall to the ground.

**394.** Who can describe those wondrous states?
By the Lord's will, after some time they grew calm.

**395.** Shripati, Shrinidhi and others from Navadvipa,
in the ecstasy of prema, all came forward to receive [them].

**396.** How everyone met one another,
how they bathed in the Ganga and so on — that cannot be described.

**397.** Shripati and Shrinidhi, the two brothers of Srivasa,
taking everyone with them, enter the village of Navadvipa.

**398.** What took place at the time of entering Navadvipa —
what power have I, an ignorant one, to describe even a particle of it?

**399.** Shripati and Shrinidhi took [everyone] to [their] house;
arriving in Srivasa's courtyard, [all] were filled with great joy.[^46]

**400.** Sri Jahnava says, "Why is it that for so long
no one has come from Shantipura?"

**401.** As soon as she said this, the sons of Advaita arrived —
Sri Achyutananda and Sri Gopala, full of prema.

**402.** With Achyuta came all the bhagavatas;
who could tell all their names and qualities?

**403.** Sri Kanu Pandita, and also Dasa Narayana,
Vishnudasa Acharya, Kamadeva, Janardana,

**404.** Vanamali, Purushottama and other merciful ones —
all came and entered Srivasa's abode.

**405.** Going forward, Shripati brings everyone in;
in their meeting with one another there was great bliss.

**406.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, greatly delighted,
stayed in Navadvipa for two or three days.

**407.** In Navadvipa, Shripati, Shrinidhi and all the others
joyfully got ready to go to Kheturi.

**408.** The splendor of the gathering of the masters steals one's composure;
at the break of dawn [all] set out for Kantaka-nagara.

**409.** Together with Krishnadasa of Akaihat and others,[^47]
all arrived at Kantaka-nagara.

**410.** Yadunandana and the others, with great joy in their hearts,
went forward and brought [them] to Gauranga's abode.

**411.** At that time Sri Raghunandana of Shrikhanda arrived
with his companions — like Madana himself.

**412.** A supreme, wondrous beauty — what can it be compared to?
In their hearts was a joyful eagerness to go quickly to Kheturi.

**413.** And the coming of all the other mahantas —
who can tell of it? I shall name a few.

**414.** Together with Shivananda, the excellent brahmana Vaninatha,
Vallabha, Chaitanyadasa, Hari Acharya,[^48]

**415.** Bhagavata Acharya, and also Gopala the dancer,
Jita Mishra, Raghu Mishra, supremely merciful,

**416.** Kashinatha Pandita, and also Nayana Mishra,
Kathakata Jagannatha, the magnanimous Uddhava,[^49]

**417.** Sri Pushpa-gopala, the merciful Raghunatha,
Lakshminatha Pandita and others — abodes of virtues.

**418.** The bliss there was in the meeting of these with all those —
the fortunate ones saw it.

**419.** Coming to the place where the Lord accepted sannyasa, everyone[^50]
lost their composure — their tears could not be stopped.

**420.** What they [then] did cannot be put into words;
after some time, by the Lord's will, [all] grew calm.

**421.** On seeing Dasa Gadadhara's Gauranga,
what joy arose in their hearts — how could I tell it?

**422.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, that same day and in that very place,
cooked — how, I have no power to describe.

**423.** Having fed Gaurachandra with various preparations,
with great joy she satisfied all the mahantas.

**424.** How everyone rejoiced as they ate the rice and other dishes —
who will describe the beauty of their sitting at the meal?

**425.** Sri Yadunandana and the others, rapt in bliss,
ate last — only when Sri Ishvari had eaten.

**426.** The ocean of prema surged in Kantaka-nagara;
in Gauranga's courtyard all delight in kirtana.

**427.** Sri Yadunandana and the others, with jubilant hearts,
all got ready to go to Kheturi.

**428.** A huge throng of Vaishnavas gathered;
[they] set out from Kantaka-nagara.

**429.** Whatever villages the mahantas passed through,
the people of those villages were overwhelmed with bliss.

**430.** Teliya Budhari and the other villages are holy places;
the people in those villages are greatly fortunate.

**431.** Hearing that the masters were coming, [people] run from all sides;
having their darshana, all float in great bliss.

**432.** Seeing the people's ardor, the masters were delighted;
they understood: Srinivasa has manifested this bhakti.

**433.** That day [they] stayed in the village of Budhari;
what happiness spread there — what can be said!

**434.** The people of that land could not keep still;
lovingly they went along [with them] to the bank of the Padmavati.

**435.** Sri Santosha had arranged boats beforehand;
as soon as they arrived, [all] crossed the Padmavati.

**436.** The news of [their] coming spread through the village of Kheturi;
the people surged with joy and could not keep still.

**437.** In the village of Kheturi there were boundless myriads of people;
and how many there were in the whole region of Kheturi — there is no counting.

**438.** Children, old people and all the rest run in all directions;
no one can understand what is happening in his heart.

**439.** Meanwhile here Srinivasa, with Narottama and the others,
goes forward in supreme jubilation to receive [them].

**440.** How they brought [the guests] — that rapture of prema,
that splendor: who can describe even a particle of it?

**441.** Seeing [them], people on all sides float in tears;
they bow down to the masters, falling to the ground.

**442.** Seeing the people's ardor, a certain noble soul
speaks to someone with very sweet words:

**443.** "In this land there was not even a particle of this rare bhakti;
by the virtues of Narottama such a land has become blessed."

**444.** Speaking thus, he praises the people's good fortune;
in great bliss [all] enter the village of Kheturi.

**445.** All the Lord's beloved ones, embodiments of compassion,
rose in the midst of the village like moons.

**446.** Srinivasa, Narottama and the others, with great care,
lodged everyone in separate dwellings.

**447.** Seeing those places, all rejoiced at heart;
all arrived [there] as if in their own homes.

**448.** However many lodgings and storehouses there were,
the people assigned to them were beyond counting.

**449.** The auspicious works Sri Santosha Datta performs
with his people cannot be set down in writing.

**450.** All these accounts are an ocean of great happiness;
they will be told in detail in the *Narottama-vilasa*.

**451.** By the darshana of the Lord's associates all the people
are overwhelmed day and night, and know neither sorrow nor grief.

**452.** Even in dreams no one has any other occupation;
apart from these topics there is no other talk.

**453.** Everywhere people, with jubilant hearts,
say many sweet words to one another.

**454.** Someone says, "The festival that goes on here every day —
would we ever have seen [such a thing]? We have never even heard of the like.

**455.** We have seen the auspicious village of Sri Kheturi,
the incomparable dwellings of the holy mahantas.

**456.** O brother! The Lord's temple enchants the heart:
the Lord has not yet sat on the throne, and already there is such splendor!"

**457.** Someone says, "Tomorrow is the full moon of Phalguna:
the six Sri Deities will be seated on the throne.[^51]

**458.** Seeing the abhisheka of the Sri Deities —
what to speak of others — [even] the demigods will be enraptured.

**459.** What can I say? This is what is in my heart:
the six Sri Deities will take away the bitter sorrow.

**460.** The ocean of the happiness of sankirtana will surge;
[we] will delight in sankirtana together with the masters."

**461.** Someone says, "Fortunate is Sri Raja Santosha!
What a place for sankirtana he has built!

**462.** With what a wondrous canopy the courtyard is covered!
Hundreds of banana trees and other things adorn [it]."

**463.** Someone says, "For preparing flower garlands
and grinding sandalwood he has engaged many people."

**464.** Someone says, "Players of various instruments, dancers,
and many singers have come from many lands.

**465.** There is no end to the bards and the like.
What a wondrous clamor of people everywhere!"

**466.** Someone says, "O brother! What more is there to say?
When the night is over, my heart will be soothed.

**467.** In the morning I shall go and have the darshana of the masters;
I shall stay right there — I have no need of home.

**468.** For what happiness did I come home today to eat?" —
saying many such things, he reproached himself in sorrow.

**469.** Someone says, "O Lord! Will this sorrow not be dispelled?
Will this cruel night not pass quickly today?"

**470.** Someone says, "I think six dandas of the night remain;[^52]
otherwise would there be such a clamor of music?"

**471.** Someone says, "Look, the night has turned into a fair dawn!
The holy full moon of Phalguna attracts every heart."

**472.** Saying this, people run to where the holy temple is,
and, arriving there, they see a supreme, wondrous splendor.

**473.** From their own lodgings all the mahantas
come to the holy temple, overwhelmed with prema.

**474.** Their gait surpasses that of the king of elephants, their radiance is like the sun's;
every limb of theirs is charmingly filled with the thrill of ecstasy.

**475.** Their new garments are beautiful;
on their foreheads is tilaka, their arms and chests are marked with the names [of the Lord].

**476.** Smiling gently, gazing all around,
they enter the courtyard of the Lord's holy temple.

**477.** With joy in their hearts all sit on splendid seats;
Sri Jahnava Ishvari sits in a secluded place.

**478.** Srinivasa Acharya and Narottama Mahashaya,
seeing this splendor, swim in an ocean of happiness.

**479.** All the Lord's associates gave Srinivasa
permission [to perform] the abhisheka and the other rites.

**480.** Srinivasa, humbly bowing down to the ground,
performs the abhisheka of the Deities and the other rites.[^53]

**481.** His wonderful artistry cannot be described;
he seated the six Deities on the throne.

**482.** Whatever names the Lord had made known under the guise of a dream,
those very names of the Deities he announced:

**483.** Gauranga, Vallavikanta, Sri Vrajamohana,
Sri Krishna, Sri Radha-kanta, Sri Radha-ramana.[^54]

**484.** The beauty of the abhisheka of these six is boundless;
whoever looks even once cannot keep his composure.

**485.** The hearts of all the mahantas were filled with wonder;
they cannot stop the streams of tears of bliss from their eyes.

**486.** Watching unseen, the gods shower flowers;
attaining the highest bliss, they forget themselves.

**487.** The ceaseless clamour of cries of "Jaya, jaya!";
the sounds of various instruments steal everyone's composure.

**488.** Brahmanas recite the Vedas in the sweetest voices;
the bards describe the beauty in various ways.

**489.** The Deities are the limit of incomparable beauty;
who has such composure as to array Them in Their dress?

**490.** Srinivasa Acharya, keeping his composure with great effort,
arrayed Them in wonderful dress and gazes, filling his eyes.

**491.** Fragrant sandal paste and all the flower garlands
he took in many vessels and offered before the Lord.

**492.** Having performed the puja by a wondrous rite, in great happiness,
he performs the arati; all watch with delight.

**493.** Cries of "Jaya, jaya!" arose, the clamour of instruments;
from whoever hears that sound, all inauspiciousness goes far away.

**494.** When the arati was completed, the mahantas
in the highest bliss bow down to the ground.

**495.** Narottama, sunk in the ocean of happiness,
bows down, uttering the names of the Lords.[^55]

**496.** *Thus [it is said] in his own verse:*
*"O Gauranga! O Vallavikanta! O Sri Krishna! O Vrajamohana!*
*O Radha-ramana! O Radha! O Radha-kanta! Obeisance to You!"*[^56]

**497.** Many hundreds of people, entering the sacred courtyard,
bow down, overwhelmed at the sight of the arati.

**498.** The temple courtyard is not very spacious,
and yet countless people stay in one place.

**499.** By the Lord's will such is the power of the courtyard;
what would others understand? It is utterly imperceptible.

**500.** Hearing this account, others are amazed;
whoever saw [it] at the time of the arati, he alone knows.

**501.** Ah, I die! Having completed the wondrous arati,
the Acharya, his heart joyful, offers the bhoga.

**502.** In separate vessels, beautifully arranged,
he offered in private a bhoga of various foods.

**503.** Knowing that the meal was over, he gave achamana
and carefully had [Them] partake of tambula.

**504.** He offered flower garlands with fragrant sandal paste
and with a beautiful chamara whisk made [Them] very cool.

**505.** When the curtain over the temple door was drawn aside,
the fragrance of the Lords' bodies spread in all four directions.

**506.** By the radiance of every limb of the Lords
everyone's composure was stolen — what comparison is there for it?

**507.** Srinivasa Acharya, losing his composure completely,
comes out into the courtyard, falls to the ground and bows down.

**508.** He considers himself lowly, fearing to offend;
the humility he shows cannot be described.

**509.** As he bows again and again to the Lord's associates,
all embrace him; streams of tears of bliss flow from their eyes.

**510.** He bows at the feet of Sri Jahnava Ishvari;
she shows him boundless favour.

**511.** In the highest bliss she speaks sweet words:
"Give everyone the prasada flower garlands and sandal paste."

**512.** Hearing this, Srinivasa joyfully, in Ishvari's presence,
took the sacred garlands and sandal paste in many vessels.

**513.** In separate vessels the sacred garlands and sandal paste
he offered before the Lord's associates.

**514.** Seeing that wonder, all were delighted
and became adorned with the sacred garlands and sandal paste.

**515.** How wonderful is the beauty of the garlands and sandal paste!
The eyes of those who look cannot even blink.

**516.** Even the gods, taking human form there,
put on the sacred garlands and sandal paste; others do not know it.

**517.** The virtuous people were adorned with garlands and sandal paste —
garlands and paste whose touch destroys grief and sorrow.

**518.** Countless people put on the sacred garlands and sandal paste;
the greatly fortunate behold this wonder.

**519.** Sri Ishvari instructed Nrisimha Chaitanya,
and he put them on Srinivasa and all the others.

**520.** Sri Ishvari accepted a garland and sandal paste;
everyone's heart became greatly delighted.

**521.** How Jahnava Ishvari became at the touch
of the sacred garland and sandal paste — I cannot tell.

**522.** Looking at Narottama with a merciful glance,
[she] joyfully infused [into him] — I do not know what power.

**523.** Sri Achyutananda Prabhu, the son of Advaita,
shows Narottama great favour.

**524.** All the mahantas, who love Narottama,
gave permission to begin the sankirtana.

**525.** Narottama bows down to the ground before all;
at the beginning of the sankirtana his heart overflows with bliss.

**526.** Standing humbly in the Lord's courtyard,
he looks with a merciful glance at his own associates.

**527.** Sri Narottama's dear associates
are all expert in song, dance and instrumental music.

**528.** First of all Devidasa strikes with his hand
on the left [side] of the mardala — the sound from it is full of prema.[^57]

**529.** He brings forth sounds like syllables of nectar
and, together with Sri Vallava Dasa and others, develops [them].[^58]

**530.** Sri Gauranga Dasa and others, with joy in their hearts,
play the kamsya-tala and the rest, revealing [their] varieties.

**531.** Song is of two kinds — anibaddha and nibaddha;
the anibaddha Gokula and others perform as alapa.[^59]

**532.** In the anibaddha there are varna-nyasa and svara-alapa;[^60]
Gokula performs the alapa — the sound of his voice destroys torment.

**533.** In the alapa there are gamakas on the svaras of mandra, madhya and tara;
who, hearing that alapa, could keep his composure?

**534.** How the singers and players perform abhinaya,
and what the beauty of them all is — this cannot be described.

**535.** Narottama, surrounded by all these associates,
shines like the moon among the stars.

**536.** He is beautiful in every limb, his sweetness has no limit;
how sweet are his movements in the rapture of sankirtana!

**537.** On Sri Krishna Chaitanya, Nityananda and Advaitachandra
together with Their associates he meditates in his mind in great bliss.

**538.** Bowing again and again at the feet of all,
he performs the alapa in order to manifest a wondrous raga.

**539.** He made the ragas together with the raginis embodied;
he manifested the shrutis, svaras, gramas, murchhanas and the rest.

**540.** The sweetest sound of his voice pierces the sky;
even the most intoxicating nectar is not equal to it.

**541.** He utters the talas and pathaksharas in a beautiful rhythm,
by which the players' joy increases.

**542.** As the song and music gradually swell,
so the bliss of prema of the Lords increases.

**543.** Sri Raghunandana of Khanda, full of prema,
floats in the ocean of the happiness of sankirtana.

**544.** The sacred khol and karatala are the Lord's own wealth;
he touched them with sacred sandal paste and a flower garland.

**545.** Embracing Narottama together with his associates,
with his own hand he put the sacred garlands and sandal paste [on them].

**546.** Narottama with his associates bows down to him
and displays the perfect art of nibaddha song.

**547.** The Moon of Nadia is in the rapture of Sri Radhika's bhava:
the song, full of that bhava, is composed in a beautiful metre.

**548.** It is a mantra of attraction — what comparison can be given to it?
Singing it first, he was overwhelmed.

**549.** After that, the pastimes of Sri Radhika and Krishna
he would sing — this desire arose in his mind.[^61]

**550.** When the song of Gaura's qualities began, all lost their composure;
Sri Jahnava Ishvari floats in the waters of prema.

**551.** Sri Achyutananda Prabhu, the son of Advaita —
who knows what bliss arose in his heart.

**552.** Shripati, Shrinidhi and the other mahantas
cannot hold their bodies steady — they sway to and fro.

**553.** All gaze at Narottama with unwavering eyes;
some speak about Sri Narottama.

**554.** Someone says, "What a wondrous manifestation of song and the rest!
Ah, I die! Whose sorrow would this not destroy?"

**555.** Someone says, "Such song and music do not exist;
I do not know how Narottama manifests them."

**556.** Someone says, "Mahaprabhu used to hear from Svarupa
lofty songs with great joy in His heart.[^62]

**557.** To preserve the tradition of song and to dispel regret,
He thoroughly considered in His heart how to spread [it].

**558.** At that time He kept it in a casket of prema;
now, through Narottama, the Lord has opened it."

**559.** Someone says, "It has become manifest when the Lord is no longer seen;
how will the Lord's regret be dispelled?"[^63]

**560.** Someone says, "The Lord, who loves song and is full of His own will —
perhaps today He is sporting here in visible form.

**561.** He has relished this unprecedented singing;
so it seems to me — that is what I have said."

**562.** Someone says, "There is not the slightest doubt of this:
we shall see the Lord with His associates in this very place."

**563.** Thus they spoke of many things; no one's mind was steady:
all, drinking the nectar of the song, were deeply immersed.

**564.** What the varieties of song and the rest were like — who can describe?
The gandharvas and kinnaris, seeing it, reproach themselves.[^64]

**565.** The gods shower flowers from the sky;
mingling with the people, they achieve their own aim.

**566.** Narada and the other rishis, in invisible form,
were immersed in the ocean of the bliss of sankirtana.

**567.** Shiva, Brahma and the others are utterly immersed in the singing;
so many wishes do they make that they cannot be told.

**568.** There all the beasts, birds, snakes and the rest
became supremely overwhelmed with the bliss of the singing.

**569.** The ocean of sankirtana swells moment by moment;
on all sides people float in the tears of their eyes.

**570.** All have utterly forgotten themselves
and, like madmen, gaze about on all sides.

**571.** What can be said? From the abundance of the joy of sankirtana
Gora Raya, together with His associates, lost His composure.

**572.** As a cluster of lightning flashes appears in the clouds,
so in the cloud of sankirtana the Lord appears.

**573.** How wondrous and beautiful is His appearing!
Nityananda and Advaita are surrounded by associates.

**574.** All of them became the ornament of the place of sankirtana;
the sweetness of the Lord and His associates pervaded the three worlds.

**575.** The manifest and the unmanifest — a wonder! — together;
all know: this is the manifest pastime of the Lord.[^65]

**576.** This lila of the Lord is beyond the reach even of Brahma and the others;
the Lord with His associates sports in the sankirtana.

**577.** Supremely wonderful attire, wonderful gestures;
their beauty enchants the world — what comparison could be given?

**578.** When, forming a circle, they began a charming dance,
how the singing and music swelled — who can describe it?

**579.** Gaurachandra dances — what a wondrous creation of song!
He maddens the world with prema and pours down a rain of prema.

**580.** With a gentle, gentle smile He looks toward Narottama;
Prabhu Nityananda knows that sign of the Lord.

**581.** Nityananda, the son of Padmavati, dances;
under the weight of His steps the earth trembles ceaselessly.

**582.** Advaita Acharya dances with jubilation in His heart;
He roars like a lion in great intoxication.

**583.** Gadadhara Pandita dances, losing all composure,
having brought Srinivasa near Gaurachandra.

**584.** Srivasa Pandita dances, overwhelmed;
the dance of Murari Gupta destroys everything inauspicious.

**585.** Vakreshvara dances — no comparison can be given for him;
by this song and music his desire was fulfilled.

**586.** How sweet is the dance of Haridasa Thakura!
By the dance of Svarupa Gosani suffering goes far away.

**587.** The dancing of Gadadhara Dasa is enchanting;
Ramananda Raya dances, an ocean of rasa.

**588.** Vasudeva Sarvabhauma, Vidyavachaspati —
who, seeing the dance of these two, could keep composure?[^66]

**589.** Achyutananda, the son of Advaita, dances;
streams of bliss flow ceaselessly from his eyes.

**590.** Mukunda, Sri Narahari, Sri Raghunandana —
the way they dance cannot be described.

**591.** How absorbed in dance is Gauridasa Pandita!
Shripati and Shrinidhi dance in endless bliss.

**592.** When Govinda, Madhava and Vasu Ghosha dance,
who is there who could keep composure in his mind?

**593.** Mukunda dances, Sri Acharya Purandara,
Vasudeva Datta, Brahmachari Shuklambara.

**594.** Shriman Pandita,[^67] Yadu, Acharya Nandana;[^68]
Sri Mukunda Datta dances, Sri Madhusudana.

**595.** Srinatha and Mahesha dance, Shridhara, Shankara,
Jagadisha, Sri Yadunandana, Kashishvara.

**596.** Sri Raghunatha Bhatta dances, Rupa and Sanatana —
at the mere sight of that dance the eyes are soothed.

**597.** Sri Nakula Brahmachari dances, Dhananjaya,
the brahmana Vaninatha, Shikhi, Kanai, Vijaya.[^69]

**598.** Suryadasa and Sri Nrisimha dance in many ways;
Hridaya Chaitanya dances, drawing Shyamananda along with him.

**599.** The dear ones of Srinivasa and Sri Narottama —
countless people dance: who could count them?

**600.** The gods, gandharvas and others, mingling with the people,
dance — with what longing, with what joy of heart!

**601.** On all sides the countless people have no end;
all those fortunate ones dance in great delight.

**602.** What wondrous absorption in dance arose at the place of dancing!
In everyone's heart great bliss surges.

**603.** The time that passed in dance, song and music —
that time went unnoticed: to all it seemed but little.

**604.** Ah, how enchanting the song and music!
What a novel dance, beyond the reach even of Brahma and the others!

**605.** In what bliss Advaita and Nityananda are overwhelmed!
How Gaurachandra [shines] amid the circle of devotees!

**606.** What wondrous mercy the Lord revealed!
What a pastime this is — who can understand it?

**607.** What favor to Srinivasa and Narottama!
Together with His associates He fulfilled the desire of both.

**608.** How He, with His associates, embracing Narottama and Srinivasa,
says something to them in gentle words!

**609.** What can be said? Gora Raya, affectionate to His devotees,
finds no firmness in His heart to vanish.

**610.** Just as He had appeared with His associates in the sankirtana,
so suddenly the Lord vanished from sight.

**611.** When He vanished together with the unmanifest associates,
the manifest associates remained at the place of sankirtana.

**612.** As soon as the Lord disappeared, they regained external awareness;
that absorption of everyone passed away.

**613.** A loud wailing rose at the place of sankirtana;
all, in great distress, are drowning in tears.

**614.** Someone says, "Where has Lord Gaurachandra gone?"
Someone says, "Where are Advaita and Nityananda?"

**615.** Someone says, "Where is Sri Pandita Gadadhara?"
Someone says, "Where are Haridasa and Vakreshvara?"

**616.** Someone says, "Where have Srivasa and Murari gone?"
Someone says, "Where are Sri Mukunda and Narahari?"

**617.** Someone says, "Where are Gauridasa and Gadadhara?"
Someone says, "Where is Sri Svarupa Damodara?"

**618.** Someone says, "The Lord with His associates showed Himself to us —
where has He gone?" — and weeps, rolling on the ground.

**619.** On all sides are the anguished cries of countless people;
with their tears the earth turned to mud.

**620.** All the pashandas who had come to laugh —
even they weep, unable to restrain themselves.

**621.** All lament with their arms raised:
"Protect us, Lord Gaurahari!"

**622.** Again and again they pray at the Lord's feet
that there be no offense toward the Vaishnavas

**623.** and that they may ever drink the nectar of sankirtana.
Saying so many things, they become grey with dust.

**624.** What do I know how to tell? No one has a trace of composure;
there is no room to give a comparison for the devotees' behavior.

**625.** Shripati, Shrinidhi and the other dear devotees
say to one another, "What is this — did we see a dream?"

**626.** Someone says, "Or has a delusion come over us all?"
Someone says, "We cannot understand the Lord's will."

**627.** Saying so many things, they regained some composure;
all calmed Srinivasa and Narottama.[^70]

**628.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari says gently,
"Full mercy [has been shown] to Narottama and Srinivasa.

**629.** What the Lord ordered has come true:
together with His associates He danced in such a kirtana.

**630.** Everyone, down to the chandalas, is maddened by the Lord and His associates;
the rain of prema has dispelled everyone's suffering.

**631.** This lila of the Lord is beyond this world, full of prema."
As she said so many things, joy arose.

**632.** Delight filled the hearts of all the mahantas;
by the Lord's will their behavior became as before.

**633.** Seeing their state, Jahnava Ishvari
very softly says to Srinivasa Acharya,

**634.** "Make the arrangements to begin the phagu play."
Hearing this, he at once had phalgu and the rest brought.[^71]

**635.** Set apart in many vessels, it all looked beautiful;
seeing it, Sri Ishvari was very pleased at heart.

**636.** Srinivasa and Narottama, at Ishvari's command,
bowing to the mahantas, say gently,

**637.** "Kindly be pleased to play with phagu now."
Hearing this, all joyfully gave their consent.

**638.** Srinivasa, taking separate vessels,
joyfully set phalgu and the rest before everyone.

**639.** Flower pollen, phagu and the rest he carefully
set in a separate vessel before Ishvari.

**640.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari entered the temple
and, immersed in the bliss of prema, applied phagu to the Lord's body;[^72]

**641.** coming out of the temple, she sat on her own seat
and watches how the Lord's associates play with phagu.

**642.** Sri Achyutananda, Sri Gopala, full of prema,
Shripati, Shrinidhi, Yadu, the abode of virtues,[^73]

**643.** Sri Raghunandana and the other beloved ones of the Lord —
all were overwhelmed by prema as the phagu play began.

**644.** Someone, in great delight putting phagu on Gora's body,
cannot turn his eyes away, gazing at [His] face.

**645.** Someone, describing [His] lovely deeds in verse,
puts phagu on Sri Vallavikanta in great bliss.

**646.** In someone, as he puts phagu on Sri Vrajamohana,
an ocean of bliss surges, and he cannot keep himself steady.

**647.** Someone, having put phagu on Krishna together with Sri Radhika,
beholds that beauty, displaying gestures of every kind.

**648.** Some, displaying boundless delight,
offer phagu on the body of Sri Radha-kanta.

**649.** Some, having put phagu on Sri Radha-ramana,
smile softly, softly, with jubilant hearts.

**650.** Of that wondrous absorption in feeling in the phagu play
I cannot describe even a trace with one mouth.

**651.** How [all] lose themselves in the mutual phagu play!
How the bodies covered with phagu glitter!

**652.** How they sing songs of the Lord's phagu play!
They play various instruments — how sweet the sound!

**653.** What do I know to tell of all those wondrous ways?
Hearing the singing and music, Brahma and the others are enchanted.

**654.** Srinivasa Acharya, Narottama, Shyamananda
with their companions are beside themselves, finding great bliss.

**655.** Seeing that wondrous beauty in its sweet manner —
the bodies adorned with phalgu — what comparison can one give?

**656.** The sky and the earth became full of phagu;
on all four sides — the clamour of countless people.

**657.** By the Lord's will, in that wondrous phagu play
the gods, unseen, mingled with the people.

**658.** All, immersed in the happiness of the phagu play,
by the Lord's will grew calm at evening.

**659.** All, having seen the evening arati,
performed kirtana of the holy name for some time.

**660.** The Lord's beloved ones, oceans of great virtues,
sit in the Lord's courtyard; that sight is enchanting.

**661.** For performing Gauranga's birth abhisheka
all gave their permission to the Acharya.

**662.** Srinivasa Acharya, bowing to all to the ground,
enters the temple in great joy.

**663.** All the pujaris, with greatly jubilant hearts,
at once made ready the articles for the abhisheka.

**664.** Herbs and substances of many kinds —
they set all these provisions before the Acharya.

**665.** Acharya Thakura carefully removed Gauranga's
former dress upon the throne.

**666.** Dressing [Him] in white cloth with the utmost care,
he seated Gaurachandra on another throne.

**667.** As the rite is performed on the lunar day of Krishna's birth,
so he performs Gaurachandra's birth abhisheka.

**668.** Gaura and Krishna are one; whoever thinks of difference in this
finds no release from the torments of Yama.

**669.** Ah, ah! What a matchless festival of abhisheka!
All watch in jubilation — they cannot contain themselves.

**670.** The brahmanas chant the Vedas in sweet measure;
the bards joyfully describe the Lord's deeds.

**671.** Singers from many lands sing many songs
of the Nadia pastimes, which enchant Brahma and the others.

**672.** On all sides musicians play various instruments;
all the dancers dance in the manner of many lands.

**673.** What do I know to tell? An ocean of happiness surges;
whoever knows whatever art displays it in delight.

**674.** The rite of Gauranga's birth abhisheka
all the fortunate people behold to their eyes' content.

**675.** Someone says, "Blessed is the full moon of Phalguna!
Whoever honours this lunar day attains the moon of Nadia."

**676.** Someone says, "Is the full moon of Phalguna ever like this?
What wondrous splendour the full-moon night has!

**677.** Look, in the moonlight everything is spotless;
I cannot understand why it is brighter here."

**678.** Someone says, "To see the Lord's birth abhisheka
the moon has come here unseen and is present here."

**679.** Someone says, "What you said is true as well:
here the moons — the Lord's devotees — have risen."

**680.** Saying much like this, the people are immersed in bhakti-rasa;
seeing the beauty of the Lord's associates, they float in happiness.

**681.** How wondrous the conduct of the Lord's associates!
They sing songs of the Lord's birth abhisheka.

**682.** The Lord's abhisheka was completed;
little by little the singing swells — there is no pause in the singing.

**683.** They were immersed beyond measure in the bliss of song;
how the night passed, they know not at all.

**684.** By the Lord's will all, regaining composure,
beheld the Sri mangala-arati.

**685.** Bowing to the Lords with greatly blissful hearts,
they went each to his own place and performed the morning duties.

**686.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, in supreme joy,
performed her morning bath and daily rites.

**687.** With supreme eagerness she prepared matchless food —
rice, vegetable dishes and the rest, beyond description.

**688.** To Gauranga, Vallavikanta and the other Lords
she offered bhoga in a matchless manner.

**689.** Knowing the time, she carefully removed the bhoga;
seeing the Lords' delight, she rejoiced.

**690.** Srinivasa Acharya humbly requested
all the mahantas to see the arati.

**691.** All the mahantas, with greatly jubilant hearts,
come together into the Lord's courtyard.

**692.** What a wondrous bearing! Beautiful tilaka on the forehead,
arms and chests marked with the holy name, charming.

**693.** The new garments they wear are beautiful;
seeing the mahantas, who could keep composure?

**694.** All, having come into the Lord's courtyard,
see the Lord's arati, and their eyes are soothed.

**695.** Having completed the arati, the pujari carefully
gave everyone tulasi garlands that were prasada.

**696.** In the holy temple he completed the Lord's service
and laid the Lords down on a wondrous bed.

**697.** Having fanned [Them] with the chamara, the fan and so on, he rejoiced;
coming out of the temple, he closed the doors.

**698.** Falling to the ground, he bows to the Lord's associates;
all the mahantas kindly praise [him].

**699.** To Srinivasa and Narottama they say again and again,
"The arrangement of the Lord's service is wonderful!"

**700.** Even as they say this, how much arises in their hearts!
Who would not float in bliss seeing that conduct?

**701.** Here Sri Ishvari gave instructions to Sri Madhava;[^74]
he invited everyone to take prasada.

**702.** Hearing Madhava Acharya's sweet words,
Sri Achyuta, Shripati and the others were glad at heart.

**703.** In wonderful order all sit in a clean place;
Sri Jahnava Ishvari joyfully serves [the food].

**704.** Rice, vegetable dishes and the rest, surpassing nectar in taste,
they eat, praising, absorbed in the bliss of prema.

**705.** Savouring taste after taste, all were satisfied beyond measure;
the beauty of that meal cannot be told.

**706.** In great delight all, having performed achamana,
went each to his own lodging.

**707.** Srinivasa and the others could not transgress the command
and ate: Sri Ishvari carefully fed [them].[^75]

**708.** At last, with gladness of heart, Jahnava Ishvari
carefully partook of Sri maha-prasada.

**709.** Everyone's heart became full of great bliss;
everywhere — boundless delight in feasting.

**710.** How many people eat — there is no counting;
in the village of Sri Kheturi — an ocean of the bliss of feasting.

**711.** The Lord's associates, seeing this delight —
in all of them great happiness grows moment by moment.

**712.** The day and night of pratipada passed thus;
they decided they would set out on the second lunar day.[^76]

**713.** On the second lunar day Srinivasa, glad at heart,
humbly requests the dear associates of the Lord:

**714.** "Today in your own lodgings, quickly,
let the cooking and the rest be done — I shall watch to my eyes' content.

**715.** Whatever desire is in Santosha Datta's heart —
mercifully fulfil it."[^77]

**716.** Seeing Srinivasa's endeavour, all, delighted,
had cooking done in various ways.

**717.** Offering bhoga to Krishna, all partook of prasada;
Srinivasa and the others beheld that delight.

**718.** Santosha Datta's good fortune cannot be described:
whatever things he gave, all accepted.

**719.** Thousands upon thousands of brahmanas from many lands
he honoured, bestowing various [kind] words and things.

**720.** The singers, musicians, dancers and other people
Santosha gladdened by giving various gifts.

**721.** All the mahantas, seeing how Santosha conducted himself,
in an outpouring of affection showed [him] fitting favor.

**722.** This abundant account I have told briefly;
I shall set it out in full in the *Narottama-vilasa*.

**723.** At the end of the great festival the Lord's dear ones
were about to set out for their own lands.

**724.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari was to go to Vrindavana;
therefore, to take leave, they went to her.

**725.** What they said to one another at the time of parting —
hearing all that, hard stone splits apart.[^78]

**726.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari was utterly distraught;
she could not hold back — streams flowed from both her eyes.

**727.** The Lord's dear ones, their hearts deeply agitated,
bathed in tears, took their leave.

**728.** Gazing their fill at the Lords — Gauranga, Vallavikanta and the others,
they bow down in the courtyard.

**729.** Having taken leave, they set out from Kheturi;
the people of Kheturi village run up from all sides.

**730.** Weeping, they say so much to one another;
seeing them all, no one can remain composed.

**731.** Srinivasa Acharya cannot keep his composure;
Narottama and Ramachandra cannot be calm.

**732.** In the hearts of Shyamananda and the others the grief is boundless;
Santosha, with [his] people, is distraught at heart.

**733.** What can be said? At the departure of the Sri mahantas
a terrible grief came over the beasts and birds.

**734.** On the bank of the Padmavati a huge crowd of people gathered;
the Sri mahantas quickly boarded the boats.

**735.** Distraught, they crossed the Padmavati;
staying in the village of Budhari, in the morning [they] set out on the road.

**736.** From the bank of the Padmavati the Acharya and all the others
[and] Sri Jahnava Ishvari came to the village.

**737.** Though Ishvari was very distraught at heart,
still, consoling everyone, she calmed them.

**738.** She was soon to set out for Vrindavana;
when she made this known, all became distraught at heart.

**739.** Again, with great care, she consoled everyone
and fixed [the time of] departure for the journey to Vrindavana.

**740.** Sri Santosha Datta carefully offered various things;
showing him favor, she accepted [them].

**741.** Bowing to the Lords — Gauranga, Vallavikanta and the others —
I do not know what she said [to Them] in secret.

**742.** Taking leave before the Lord, she sets out on the road;
with her are the bhagavatas, distraught at heart:

**743.** Krishnadasa Sarakhel, Madhava Acharya,
Murari Chaitanya, Krishnadasa, the best of brahmanas,[^79]

**744.** Nrisimha Chaitanya, Balarama, Mahidhara,
Kanai, Nakadi Dasa, the handsome Gauranga,

**745.** Sri Parameshvari Dasa, Dasa Damodara,
Raghupati Vaidya, Upadhyaya Manohara,

**746.** Jnanadasa, Mukunda and all the other bhagavatas —
who could describe how great the glory of all these is?

**747.** In separation from Srinivasa Acharya, Narottama and the others,
they cannot hold their hearts: they break with grief.

**748.** Who can understand what the ways of prema are like?
How they took their leave I cannot describe.

**749.** At the time of Ishvari's departure with her people,
Govinda and others go along with her at the Acharya's command.[^80]

**750.** She set out from Kheturi, keeping her composure;
Ishvari let it be known that she would soon return.

**751.** Srinivasa Acharya and the others, by the Lord's will,
taking hold of their composure, came back to their lodgings.

**752.** The people of Kheturi village gaze at the road;
they cannot keep their composure — tears stream from their eyes.

**753.** Meditating on Sri Ishvari's feet, everyone
speaks many words of consolation to one another.

**754.** All these events I shall set out in full in the *Narottama-vilasa*:
[there] you will be able to taste prema-bhakti.

**755.** Srinivasa Acharya, together with Narottama and the others,
arrived in the Lord's courtyard.

**756.** Suddenly joy arose in the heart;
what the power of that courtyard is like cannot be told.

**757.** The courtyard where the three — Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita —
danced with Their associates, manifest and unmanifest;[^81]

**758.** the courtyard by meditation on which all obstacles are destroyed,
and by the sight of [which] one attains the supreme bliss of prema.

**759.** Glory to the sacred courtyard, which draws every heart!
Glory, glory to the village of Sri Kheturi, full of bhakti!

**760.** Acharya Thakura, with Narottama and the associates,
is overwhelmed every day in kirtana in the sacred courtyard.

**761.** One day Srinivasa Acharya, in a gentle voice,
overflowing with affection, speaks to Sri Narottama:

**762.** "Together with Shyamananda, tomorrow early in the morning, without delay,
crossing the Padmavati, I shall go to Budhari.

**763.** At Yajigrama I shall send Shyamananda on his way;
after going to Vishnupura, I shall come back to Yajigrama.

**764.** I shall send a letter with news; you too, quickly,
send me a letter about Ishvari's journey.

**765.** By whatever road Sri Ishvari will travel —
you will go along [with her], keeping to that road."[^82]

**766.** Having said many such things, in the morning, with a troubled heart,
after seeing the mangala-arati, he took his leave.

**767.** What passed between them at the time of departure —
as soon as I begin to tell of it, I do not know what my heart is doing.

**768.** This I shall describe in full in the *Narottama-vilasa*.
Crossing the Padmavati, he went to Budhari.

**769.** Here, meanwhile, Ramachandra and Sri Thakura Mahashaya[^83]
became utterly distraught in separation.

**770.** With their own people, always in the Lord's courtyard,
intoxicated with sankirtana, they do not know day from night.

**771.** Showing favor to many hundreds of pashandas,
they make [them] fit for the Lord's prema-bhakti.

**772.** Whoever has deep attachment to these accounts,
in him pure bhakti for the Lord's feet is born.

**773.** Meditating on the feet of Srinivasa Acharya,
Narahari Dasa tells the *Bhakti-ratnakara*.

*Thus in the Sri Bhakti-ratnakara ends the tenth wave, called "The Description of the Great Festival at the Abode of Sri Narottama, of Sri Jahnava's Journey to Vrindavana and Other Events".*[^84]
