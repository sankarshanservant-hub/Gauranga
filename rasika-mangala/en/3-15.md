# Pashchima-vibhaga. Lahari 15. The Description of Shyamananda's Sub-branches

*A song in glory of Shyamananda and Rasikendra; Rasika tells the brothers to name to him in order the disciples and the disciples' disciples of those to whom Shyamananda gave the shelter of his feet; they name the disciples of Damodara — the first Shyamanandis Rasamaya and Vamshidasa, those who gave themselves to Damodara with their whole families, Anandadasa called Sadavarta, and many others, — the disciples of Nagara Uddhava, Shyamarangini and Purushottama, and a multitude of other disciples of the brothers; then the principal disciples' disciples, followers of Dinashyama, Ramakrishna, Vamshi, Manohara and Mukunda: a pujari, brahmanas, bhuiyans and Bhanjas, musicians, garland-makers, and women disciples bearing the names of gopis; the Shyamanandis, like the waves of the ocean, know no one but Krishna, and whoever hears their names at once obtains prema for Krishna*

*The fifteenth lahari.*

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Glory, glory to Shyamananda-raya!*
*May I, birth after birth, worship your feet."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the ocean of mercy!
Glory, glory to Rasikendra, the son of Achyuta![^2]

**2.** Then Rasikendra gave the command to the brothers —
to count the disciples [and] the disciples' disciples of the brothers:[^3]

**3.** "Tell me of them all in order, in detail —
of all to whom Shyamananda gave the shelter of [his] feet."

**4.** Reverently [wishing] to hear the names of them all,
[he listens,] and all name [them] one by one to Rasika.[^4]

**5.** Disciples of Damodara — Rasamaya [and] Vamshidasa,
who appeared [among] the first of the Shyamanandis.[^5]

**6.** They came to have thousands and thousands of disciples;
giving [them] prema-bhakti for Krishna, they delivered [them].

**7.** With [their whole] families there gave themselves to Damodara
Madhava, Rasikananda, Sri Harichandana;[^6]

**8.** Mohanashyama, Uddhava and Ghanashyama,
Kishora, Govinda, the fortunate Haridasa;

**9.** Keshava, Krishnavallabha, Narayana Bhunya,
Mattagaja, Gokula, Bankura Krishna Bhunya;[^7]

**10.** Gokulananda, Mathura, Shrimanta, Shyamadasa,
Mathura, Ananta, Purushottama, Ramadasa;

**11.** Gokula, Gauranga, Vishnudasa, Gopidasa,
Loichhana Parichha, Mohana, Vamshidasa;[^8]

**12.** Kalindi, Radhacharana Dasa, Manohara,
Sri Rangacharana, Madhuvana, Madhukara;[^9]

**13.** Shyamamohana, the brahmana Shyamamohana Dasa,
Sri Ananta-raya, the brahmana Shyama, Yadudasa;

**14.** Akrura, Sri Hari, Kanudasa, Govardhana,
Sri Shyamavallabha, Shyamadasa, Vrindavana;

**15.** Purushottama, Akrura, Sri Radhakrishna Dasa,
Sri Kishorananda, Vrindavana, Vaishnavadasa;

**16.** Bhaktadasa, Krishnananda, Haribhakta Dasa,
Govinda, Madhuri, Sri Radhamohana Dasa;

**17.** Kanudasa, Keshava, Gopala, Govinda —
Damodara's disciples [live] in all four directions, throng upon throng.

**18.** A disciple of Sri Damodara is the famous Anandadasa,
known to the world by the name Sadavarta.[^10]

**19.** Gopivallabha, Shyamacharana, Haridasa,
Gopicharana, Shyamacharana, Gopakrishna Dasa.

**20.** Damodara's disciples seek no other refuge;
even with a hundred mouths one could not tell [of them].

**21.** Each disciple has hundreds of thousands of servants;
the disciples' disciples, the servant-disciples — who will count them?[^11]

**22.** A disciple of Nagara Uddhava is Mukundadasa:
he came to have many disciples; he lives in Bandar.[^12]

**23.** Shyamajivana Dasa is very pure in mind:
by Uddhava's mercy he attained prema-bhakti for Krishna.

**24.** A disciple of Sri Shyamarangini is Ananta Dasa;
a disciple of Purushottama is Mohana Vinoda Dasa.[^13]

**25.** A little more I shall tell briefly of the brothers' disciples,
and then I shall tell of the disciples' disciples, one by one.[^14]

**26.** The brahmana Ramadasa, Shyamadasa, Vanamali,
Krishnadasa, Gopidasa, Radha, Chandravali;[^15]

**27.** Shyamavinoda, Radhamohana, Bhudhara,
Gopinatha, Yadunatha — servants of Krishna;

**28.** Vrindavana, Mathura, Gokula, Krishnadasa,
Dvaraka, Ayodhya, Gangadasa, Gopidasa;

**29.** Purushottama, Vishnudasa, Gangarama Dasa,
Shyamasundara, Giridhara, Mohana by name;

**30.** Haridasa, Narahari, Rasika, Sundara,
Manirama, Kalurama, Ananta, Bhudhara;

**31.** Gopinatha, Yadunatha — servants of Krishna;
in Shyamananda's family the disciples are very many.[^16]

**32.** Vrindavana, Mathura, Gokula, Krishnadasa,
the brahmana Sri Vinoda Dasa, Narayana Dasa;

**33.** Manohara, Shitala, Vinoda, Shyamadasa,
the brahmana Vishnudasa, Sri Radhavallabha Dasa;

**34.** Sri Nayananandadasa, Sri Nandakishora,
Vrajamohana, Kalindi, Navina Kishora;[^17]

**35.** Krishnakishora, Krishnakalindi, Krishnananda,
Krishnabhakta, Haribhakta, Sri Paramananda;

**36.** Bhagavan, Gopala, Gauranga, Chaitanya,
Sri Radhacharana, Gopicharana — undivided [in devotion];[^18]

**37.** Govinda, Shridhara, Damodara, Nilambara,
Vasudeva, Yadavendra Dasa Shirikara;[^19]

**38.** Madhava, Govardhana, Balabhadra, Krishnadasa,
Narayanabhakta Dasa, Pitambara Dasa;

**39.** Ghanashyama, Jaladharashyama, Gopidasa,
Prasada, Akrura, Uddhava, Vaishnava Dasa;

**40.** Vrajasundara, Vrajananda, Vrajanandana,
Vrajajivana, Vrajavihari, Vrajabhushana;[^20]

**41.** Madhuvana, Subala, Sudama, Premadasa,
Harinama, Vinoda, Govinda, Shyamadasa.

**42.** This I have told briefly of the brothers' disciples:
behind one name are a hundred disciples, servants by hundreds of thousands.

**43.** I shall name the principal disciples' disciples,
making Rasika's feet the ornament of [my] head.[^21]

**44.** Dinashyama, Ramakrishna, Vamshi, Manohara,
Mukunda and the others — all the Shyamanandi followers:

**45.** I shall name the famous, worthy disciples of them all —
those for whom Krishna is wealth, life, father [and] mother.[^22]

**46.** Pujari Shricharana, Gauranga, Vinoda,
Shyamakishora, Kunja, Bhagavan, Vinoda;[^23]

**47.** Tulasi, Vihari, Radhamohana, Ananta,
Bhagavata Dasa, Gopinatha Dasa — saints;

**48.** Kanudasa, the brahmana Ramakrishna, Manohara,
Bhagirathi, Nimi, Kuladasa, Damodara;[^24]

**49.** Kanu, Vasudeva Dasa, Sri Harichandana,
Raghunatha, Vrajananda, Sri Vrajanandana;

**50.** Vrajajivana Dasa, Sri Ananta Dasa;
Ramachandra Bhunya with [his whole] community — all servants [of Krishna];[^25]

**51.** the brahmana Praharaja, the brahmana Sundara-raya,
Sri Chandana, Gajendra Bhunya, Jagata-raya;

**52.** Tilai, Shankara Bhanja, Sahani, Ananta,
Krishnacharana, Shyamasundara — pure in heart;[^26]

**53.** Lalavihari, Shyama, Rasika the vina-player,
Rasikasundara, Ananta, Shyama the garland-maker;[^27]

**54.** Vihari, Nikunja, Ghanashyama, Nidhuvana,
Govinda, Sri Hari, Vasudeva, Narayana;

**55.** Vanavihari, Shyama, Kishora, Rasananda,
Krishnaramani, Vishnudasa, Paramananda.

**56.** Ramai Shekhara is very pure in body:
Rasika's feet are constantly in his heart.[^28]

**57.** Udanda, Damodara Bhunya, Sundara-raya,
Gopala, Akrura, Hari, Uttara-raya;[^29]

**58.** Ramasena, Shyamasena, Sri Radhacharana,
Nidhuvana, Govardhana, the brahmana Vrindavana;

**59.** Ananda, Vrajavallabha, Vamshi, Bhaktadasa,
Ranabaja, Ranabhima, Manohara Dasa;[^30]

**60.** Shyamaali, Shara, Shyama, Sri Raghunandana,
Krishnavallabha, Gopivallabha, Bhima — a mahajana;[^31]

**61.** Kalindi, Keshava, Narayana, Shyamadasa,
Krishna Bhanja, Hari Bhanja, Rasamaya Dasa;[^32]

**62.** Harivallabha, Shyamavallabha, Sri Murari,
Shridhara, Purushottama Dasa, Sri Vihari;

**63.** Gopikishora, Gopicharana, Shyamaghana,
Shyamapriya, Radhapriya, Sri Krishnajivana.

**64.** The brahmana Sri Nagara Shiromani Mahashaya;
Mukunda, Mohana — the sons of Harichandana;[^33]

**65.** Vaikuntha, Vanamali, Vasudeva Dasa,
Balabhadra, Lala, Vamshi — all servants of Krishna;[^34]

**66.** Gopala, Sundara Pala, Gangadasa and others,
Ramadasa, Shyamadasa, Govinda Dasa and others;

**67.** Krishnadasa, Vamshidasa, Uddhava, Akrura,
Jagamohana, Jagabandhu, Kalandi — a brahmana;

**68.** Gopala, Sundara, Hari, Mathuramohana,
Radhagopala, Sri Radhakishora — a brahmana;[^35]

**69.** Gokula, Shyamakrishna, Sri Karunasagara,
Dinabandhu, Navaghanashyama, Manohara;

**70.** Jagatavallabha, Jayadeva, Kanurama,
Balakara Dasa, Sasagara, Balarama;[^36]

**71.** Yadunatha, Vrajanatha; Vrajarama-dasi,
Lalita, Vishakha, Chandravali, Bhadra-dasi;[^37]

**72.** Radha-dasi, Shyama-dasi, Kalandi, Tulasi,
Krishnapriya, Haripriya-dasi, Hari-dasi;

**73.** Chitrarekha, Shakuntala, Sulochana-dasi,
Mayura, Yamuna, Rambha, Sri Vallabha-dasi;

**74.** Subhadra, Vinoda, Hari-dasi, Shyamapriya,
Madhumati, Shashirekha, Sushila, Radhapriya;

**75.** Sri Rasavallabha Dasa, Govardhana Dasa,
Bhakatavatsala, Akinchana, Gopidasa.[^38]

**76.** The brahmana Ananta, the brahmana Purushottama Dasa,
the brahmana Kanu, the brahmana Rama, the brahmana Shyamadasa;

**77.** Kunjavana, Navina-madana, Vrindavana,
Ghanashyama, Jaladharashyama, Navaghana;[^39]

**78.** Giridhara, Mukunda, Sri Rangacharana,
Nidhuvana, Kripala, Gadadhara, Shricharana;[^40]

**79.** the brahmana Vamshi, the brahmana Bhakta, the brahmana Radhadasa.
The servants [and] slaves among the Shyamanandis cannot be told.[^41]

**80.** The hosts of the Shyamanandis are like the waves of the ocean:
who has the power to describe [them] all?

**81.** Briefly I have named a few — [only] the principal ones;
all of them have disciples [and] disciples' disciples by hundreds of thousands.[^42]

**82.** All the Shyamanandis, the devotees of Krishna, seek no other refuge:
none [of them] knows anyone but Krishna.

**83.** From [the time when], coming out of the womb, they fell upon the earth, [their] meditation is Krishna;
their caste [and] the treasure of their life is Krishna, and [their very] life [is Krishna].[^43]

**84.** Whoever hears the names of them all
obtains without delay the treasure of prema for Krishna.[^44]

**85.** This "Rasika-mangala" I have made known —
what the son of Achyuta, abiding in [my] heart, speaks.[^45]

**86.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Pashchima-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the fifteenth lahari, called "The Description of Shyamananda's Sub-branches."*[^46]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the heading of the lahari ("পঞ্চদশ-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 15 begins on pdf 172 (printed p. 128), under a rule after the colophon of lahari 14 (files RM-3-14-150-170). Raga Karuna-shri (*karuṇāśrī*; Purva, lahari 1, note 8; Dakshina, lahari 1, note 1). The ghosha is *jaẏa jaẏa śyāmānanda rāẏa / janame janame yena bandi tuẏā pāẏa*; "raya" (*rāẏa*) is a respectful form of address. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and the ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 175 (printed p. 131), checked against the scan: "ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-পশ্চিম-বিভাগে শ্যামানন্দোপশাখা-বর্ণননাম পঞ্চদশ-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা" (*śyāmānanda-upaśākhā-barṇana*) — "The Description of Shyamananda's Sub-branches": the "sub-branches" (*upaśākhā*) are the disciples' disciples, the branches of the second generation (cf. the "branches" of lahari 14, note 1; CC Adi 10–12). Lahari 15 comprises 1–86 (pdf 172–175). The summary line covers the whole lahari (76–86 from the OCR and scan of pdf 175, provisionally). The division of the lists into names in this lahari is in many places conjectural; most of the persons are unidentified.
[^2]: "The ocean of mercy" — *karuṇāsāgara*. "The son of Achyuta" — *acyuta-kumāra*, Rasika (lahari 14, note 87). On the author's view of Shyamananda and Rasika — Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11.
[^3]: "The brothers" — *bhāigaṇe*: Shyamananda's disciples, Rasika's spiritual brothers (lahari 14, note 8). "The disciples' disciples" — *anuśiṣya* (lahari 14, note 11). The command — cf. lahari 14, 12, 166.
[^4]: "The shelter of [his] feet" (3) — *padachāẏā*: refuge, initiation. 4a — *śunibāre śraddhā kari se sabāra nāma*: the subject is not expressed; taken as Rasika. "Name to Rasika" — *kahe … rasikera sthāna*, "tell in Rasika's presence."
[^5]: Rasamaya is the author's father, Vamshidasa (Vamshi) his uncle; together with their brother Mathura they are the grandsons of Bhima Shirikara, the first Shyamanandis, disciples of Damodara (Dakshina, lahari 4, 34–35, note 14). Damodara is the former yogi who became Shyamananda's disciple (Dakshina, lahari 1). "Who appeared [among] the first of the Shyamanandis" — *ādya śyāmānandīte yā̃hāra parakāśa* (*yā̃hāra* is singular: it may refer to Vamshidasa alone, cf. Dakshina, lahari 4, 35); it can also be read as one name, "Rasamaya Vamshidasa."
[^6]: "Gave themselves" — *bikāila*, literally "sold themselves": surrendered wholly. Rasikananda (*rasikānanda*) here is apparently a namesake of Rasika, a disciple of Damodara. Harichandana is an Oriya title of the nobility (lahari 14, note 54).
[^7]: Bhunya (*bhuñā*) — a bhuiyan, a landholder (lahari 14, note 69). Mattagaja (*mattagaja*, "an elephant in rut") is taken as a name. "Bankura" (*bā̃kuṛā*) is apparently a byname from the district of Bankura (north of Medinipur); it can also be read as two persons — "Bankura, Krishna Bhunya."
[^8]: Mathura (10) is named twice — so in the edition. Loichhana Parichha (*loichana parichā*): Parichha is an Oriya title (a steward, an official); taken as one person; cf. Laichhana (lahari 14, 159).
[^9]: Kalindi (*kālindī*, so in the scan; cf. 34, 61) is apparently the same byname as Kalandi (lahari 14, note 38; cf. 67, 72). Sri Rangacharana (*śrīrāṅgācaraṇa*, "crimson feet") — so in the scan (OCR "রাজাচরণ"); the same name occurs on pdf 175 (78).
[^10]: Sadavarta (*sadābartta* = *sadābrata*) — "a perpetual distribution of food," a house where sadhus and the poor are fed; apparently a byname of Anandadasa, who fed everyone. "Famous" — *khyātā*.
[^11]: "Hundreds of thousands" — *lakṣā lakṣā*. "The disciples' disciples, the servant-disciples" — *anu-śiṣya bhṛtya-śiṣya* (lahari 14, note 11).
[^12]: Uddhava is a disciple of Shyamananda (Purva, lahari 1, 76; BR 15, 63–65; Dakshina, lahari 11). "Nagara" (*nāgara*) is apparently a byname ("townsman," "refined, elegant"); cf. "Nagara Shiromani" (64). "In Bandar" — *bandare*, literally "in the harbour, in the trading port"; possibly the name of a village; unidentified.
[^13]: Shyamarangini — cf. Sri Shyamaranginidasa, a disciple of Shyamananda (Purva, lahari 1, 83). Purushottama is apparently a disciple of Shyamananda (Purva, lahari 1, 81, 95; BR 15, 63). "Mohana Vinoda Dasa" is one person or two (Mohana, Vinoda Dasa).
[^14]: 25 is a transition: 26–41 list further disciples of the brothers (without naming their gurus), in 42 this list is closed, and from 43 the disciples' disciples begin. "A little more I shall tell briefly" — *kichu kahiye saṃkṣepe*.
[^15]: Radha, Chandravali (*rādhā candrābalī*) — so in the scan; among men's names, apparently the names of disciples (or of women disciples, cf. 71–74).
[^16]: 31a repeats 27b, and 32a repeats 28a: so in the edition; translated as it stands. "In Shyamananda's family" — *śyāmānanda paribāre*, in his spiritual lineage.
[^17]: Sri Nayananandadasa — *śrīnaẏanānandadāsa*; the edition's footnote gives the variant *nandanandana* ("Nandanandana"). "Navina Kishora" can also be read "young Kishora."
[^18]: "Undivided [in devotion]" — *ananya*; it can also be read as a name — Ananya.
[^19]: Shirikara is a byname (Purva, lahari 1, note 39); taken as "Yadavendra Dasa Shirikara," one person; it can also be read "Yadavendra Dasa, Shirikara." Yadavendradasa is also named in Purva, lahari 1, 70.
[^20]: The names in 40 are epithets of Krishna as the Lord of Vraja (*braja-*: "the beauty of Vraja," "the bliss of Vraja," and so on), given to disciples.
[^21]: "The principal disciples' disciples" — *pradhāna pradhāna … anu-śiṣyagaṇa*. 44 names their gurus: Dinashyama and Ramakrishna are disciples of Rasika (lahari 14, 72–82, notes 41–42); Vamshi — note 5; Manohara and Mukunda are unidentified (cf. Shyama-manohara, lahari 14, note 47). "Followers" — *anucara*. 46–75 are the disciples of these teachers.
[^22]: "Famous" — *bikhyātā*. "Those for whom Krishna is wealth, life, father [and] mother" — *yā sabāra kṛṣṇadhana prāṇa pitā mātā*.
[^23]: "Pujari" — *pūjārī*, a servant of the Deity who performs puja; taken as Shricharana's byname. Kunja (*kuñja*, "grove") — so in the scan (OCR "কুণ্ড"); taken as a name. Vinoda is named twice.
[^24]: Nimi (*nimī*), Kuladasa (*kuladāsa*) — so in the scan (cut-out ×2). Bhagirathi is a man's name (or a woman disciple).
[^25]: "With [his whole] community" — *sagoṣṭhī* (lahari 14, note 83). "All servants [of Krishna]" — *sabe dāsa*; it can also be read "all [his] servants."
[^26]: Praharaja (*praharāja*) and Sahani (*sāhāni*) are Oriya titles or surnames; taken as names; it can also be read "Shankara Bhanja Sahani," one person. Tilai (*tilāi*) — cf. Telai (lahari 14, 160). Chandana — cf. lahari 14, 136. Bhanja — lahari 14, note 83. "Pure in heart" — *śuddha cita*.
[^27]: "The vina-player" — *bīṇākāra*; "the garland-maker" — *mālākāra*, apparently of the malakara (florist) caste; both words are taken as bynames of those named before them; they can also be read as separate persons.
[^28]: "Ramai Shekhara" is one person or two (Ramai, Shekhara). "Pure in body" — *śuddha kalebara*.
[^29]: Udanda — *udaṇḍa* (so in the scan); cf. Uddanda (lahari 14, 132, note 68). Uttara-raya — *uttara se rāẏa*; it can also be read "Hari Uttara-raya."
[^30]: Ranabaja (*raṇabāja*) and Ranabhima (*raṇabhīma*) — "sounding in battle," "terrible in battle": apparently martial bynames (Oriya nobility).
[^31]: 60a — *śyāmaalī śara śyāma śrīraghunandana* (so in the scan): the reading and the division are unclear; "Shyamaali" (*śyāma-alī*, "Shyama's bee"?) and "Shara" are taken as names. "Mahajana" — *mahājana*; taken as an epithet of Bhima.
[^32]: Krishna Bhanja and Hari Bhanja are of the Bhanja family (lahari 14, note 83); this Krishna Bhanja is apparently not the maharaja Krishna Bhanja-deva (Purva, lahari 1, 113). Rasamaya Dasa (*rasamaẏa dāsa*) is apparently a namesake of Rasamaya, the author's father (5); unidentified. Kalindi — note 9.
[^33]: "The brahmana Sri Nagara Shiromani" — *dbija śrīnāgara śiromaṇi*; "Shiromani" ("crown jewel") can also be read as an epithet: "Nagara, the crown jewel [of brahmanas]." Cf. Nagara Uddhava (22). Harichandana — note 6; Mukunda and Mohana are apparently the sons of a certain Harichandana.
[^34]: "All servants of Krishna" — *sabe kṛṣṇadāsa*; it can also be read "all [of them] Krishnadasas."
[^35]: "A brahmana" (67, 68) — *bhūsura* (lahari 14, note 81), *brāhmaṇa*; apparently referring to the last name of the line. Kalandi (*kālandi*) — note 9.
[^36]: 70b — *dāsa bālakara sasāgara balarāma* (so in the scan, cut-out ×2): the division is unclear; taken as "Balakara Dasa, Sasagara, Balarama."
[^37]: After Yadunatha and Vrajanatha (71a) come the women disciples (71–74): "-dasi" (*dāsī*), "maidservant," is part of the name. Many of them bear the names of gopis — Radha's friends and maidservants (Lalita, Vishakha, Chitrarekha (Chitra), Shashirekha and others), and also Chandravali and Bhadra. "Haripriya-dasi" (72) — *haripriẏā dāsī*; it can also be read "Haripriya, Dasi." Kalandi — note 9.
[^38]: "Bhakatavatsala" (*bhakatabatsala*, "loving to the devotees") and "Akinchana" (*akiñcana*, "possessing nothing") are taken as names; they can also be read as epithets of Gopidasa: "Gopidasa, loving to the devotees, possessing nothing." pdf 174 ends with a complete ॥ 75 ॥; the continuation (76–86, the author's signature and the colophon) is on pdf 175.
[^39]: The division of the list 76–79 into names is conjectural. "Brahmana" — *dbija* before a name. Navina-madana (*nabīnamadana*, "the young Madana") is taken as one name; Kunjavana, Vrindavana (cf. 14), Nidhuvana (78) are names after the groves of Vraja (cf. Madhuvana, 12, 41). Ghanashyama, Jaladharashyama — as in 39; Navaghana (*nabaghana*, "new cloud") — cf. Navaghanashyama (69).
[^40]: Sri Rangacharana — note 9 (so in the scan here as well). Kripala (*kṛpāla*, "merciful") is taken as a name. Shricharana — cf. Pujari Shricharana (46).
[^41]: "The brahmana Vamshi, the brahmana Bhakta" — *dbijabaṃśī dbijabhakta*: these may also be understood as the names "Dvijavamshi," "Dvijabhakta." "The servants [and] slaves among the Shyamanandis" — *śyāmānandī bhṛtyadāsa*.
[^42]: "The waves of the ocean" — cf. lahari 14, 58, 163. "[Only] the principal ones" — *pradhāna sbarūpe*, "as the principal ones." "Disciples' disciples" — *anuśiṣya* (note 11).
[^43]: "The devotees of Krishna" — *kārṣṇya* (so in the scan; cf. *kārṣṇa*, lahari 14, note 29; lahari 13, note 29). "Seek no other refuge" — *ananyaśaraṇa* (lahari 14, note 14). 83 — *garbha haite bhūmigata haiñā kṛṣṇadhyāna / jāti prāṇadhana yā'ra kṛṣṇa āra prāṇa*: the sense is approximate — from their very birth they meditate on Krishna; Krishna is for them caste, the treasure of life and life itself (cf. lahari 14, 67, 143: Rasika's feet are "lineage, character and caste").
