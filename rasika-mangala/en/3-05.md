# Pashchima-vibhaga. Lahari 5. The Completion of the Rasa-lila

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; following the order of the Bhagavata, the boys enact the gopis' lament for Krishna, His return, the consoling of the gopis and the dance of the rasa, and all call Rasika a partial incarnation; the next day, at the people's request, Rasika orders the rasa to be prepared again, but in the evening a storm breaks, and Shyamananda declares that the measure of the rasa is one night; in the morning a dadhi-kada is held with kirtana, in which Hridayananda dances; Rasika bows to Shyamananda, Hridayananda and the elders, embraces the Vaishnavas, sports in the waters of the Suvarnarekha, feeds and honours everyone and sends the guests off with honour; all praise Rasika, who gave prema-bhakti in every house; the author promises the listeners deliverance from the age of Kali*

*The fifth lahari.*

*Raga Kafi-bhatiyari.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "My heart, oh, pines for Shyama, [my] friend."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, renowned in the world!
Be merciful at every moment — I shall sing [his] deeds.[^2]

**2.** Thus Rasikendra manifests the rasa-lila;
seeing [it], gods and men are immersed in bliss.[^3]

**3.** The children enact the pastimes of the Sri Bhagavata —
with such movements of the body as the gopis in the dance.[^4]

**4.** All those words [of the gopis] they utter, lamenting;
all the people, seeing that dance, are spellbound.[^5]

**5.** Not finding Krishna, the gopis [are full of] wounded pride;
taking up that role, a child expounds [it].[^6]

**6.** The gopis ask everyone: "Where has Krishna gone?"
Not finding a trace [of Him], [they] lamented long.

**7.** All the virtues that Kanai showed from childhood
the gopis remember and weep — no one is conscious.[^7]

**8.** "From the poisoned water Gopala saved us,
[and] from the serpent, the rakshasa, the downpour, the irresistible wind;[^8]

**9.** from the bull demon You saved everyone.
You are not [merely] the son of Yashoda — [You are] the Lord of all![^9]

**10.** You are the Soul of all, the divine Bhagavan;
in this world [You are] the deliverance of the saintly.[^10]

**11.** Destroy the distress of the wives of Vraja:
show [us] Your moon-like face, O You who wear yellow!"[^11]

**12.** Thus, lamenting in various ways,
the wives of Vraja lamented exactly [as in the Bhagavata].[^12]

**13.** One by one the children enact all the pastimes;
seeing [this], men and women are amazed.

**14.** As the gopis poured out [their] entreaties to Krishna —
that pastime all the children show exactly.[^13]

**15.** [Prolonging] all these pastimes only a little, for sport,
[Krishna] gladdened the gopis beyond measure.[^14]

**16.** Krishna, the life of all, appears [to them];
seeing Krishna, the gopis regained [their] life.[^15]

**17.** One takes [His] hand in [her] hand, another [places His] arm on [her] shoulder;
one, with a light smile, asks for the betel chewed [by Him].[^16]

**18.** Some [He] won over by leaving nail-marks on the breast;
some [He] won over with the arrows of glances from beneath the brows.[^17]

**19.** One, biting her lip, stands at a distance;
another embraces [Him] with the greatest tenderness.[^18]

**20.** Thus the desire of all was fulfilled;
under the guise of wounded pride [the gopis] voiced something.[^19]

**21.** Hearing [these] clever words, the One who loves His devotees
dispelled all the doubts of the gopis.

**22.** Softening, the Beloved of the gopis says:
"You all never give up your crookedness.[^20]

**23.** I am always with you, in delight;
I have truly told you: [you are] My dear ones."[^21]

**24.** Having thus gladdened the gopis,
the Lord, the Lotus-eyed, began the rasa.[^22]

**25.** A beautiful sandbank, a grove on the bank of the Yamuna;
picking flowers, stringing garlands, [the gopis] adorned Gopala.

**26.** Thus Bhagavan manifests the rasa-lila:
each gopi — [and with her] Krishna, the abode of virtues.[^23]

**27.** Some gopi wins [Him] over with sweet speech,
some gopi wins [Him] over with sweet singing,

**28.** some gopi wins [Him] over with music full of flavour,
some gopi wins Damodara over with dance.[^24]

**29.** Some girl wins [Him] over with the play of her glances;
thus all the children enact many kinds of pastimes.[^25]

**30.** Holding hands, the children circle again and again;
the waist-bells [and] anklets sound without pause.[^26]

**31.** Most skilfully the children dance;
they enact that pastime in the order of the Bhagavata.

**32.** The steps [and] movements of the body cannot be described;
seeing the children's dance, all the people are spellbound.

**33.** On various instruments, with various music — in one tune;
dance, singing, movements of the body, the sweetest song.[^27]

**34.** In heaven, on earth and in the netherworlds [arose] "Glory, glory!";
staying in heaven, the gods shower flowers.

**35.** In mid-heaven the dundubhis sound again and again;
all the people were immersed in the great bliss of the rasa.[^28]

**36.** Thus [he] made the Rasa-yatra known to the world;
the son of Achyuta performs a wondrous pastime.[^29]

**37.** The splendour of Vrindavana, the pastimes of love for Krishna
the son of Achyuta made visible.[^30]

**38.** Seeing [this], all the people [were plunged] into an ocean of bliss;
all say: "Rasikendra is a partial incarnation."[^31]

**39.** Thus [he] held the rasa on a sweet night;
on the next day, expecting the rasa [again],[^32]

**40.** a multitude of people, seeing [him], all made a request:
"Today too have the rasa performed, O son of Achyuta!"

**41.** [He] was not inclined, yet at the words of all
[he] gave the order: "All you children, put on your costumes!"[^33]

**42.** Receiving the order, all dressed with care;
at that time a dreadful cloud covered the sky.

**43.** In the evening rain came with a great dreadful roar;
the earth trembles at the thunder of the cloud.

**44.** Strokes of lightning, storm and rain rage irresistibly;
until the fourth prahara the rain fell without ceasing.[^34]

**45.** Then Shyamananda Raya commanded:
"The measure [of the rasa] is one night; more is not fitting."[^35]

**46.** That word all accepted as authority;
on the morning after, they began the dadhi-kada.[^36]

**47.** Hundreds and hundreds of pots of curd with turmeric,
phagu, chuya and sandal [they] pour without measure.[^37]

**48.** Quickly bringing flowers [and] garlands of various kinds,
the crest-jewel of Rasikendras holds the dadhi-kada.[^38]

**49.** Musk, kunkuma, aragaja, saffron —
hundreds and hundreds of people, filling pots, take [them] in hand.[^39]

**50.** Some — curd [with] turmeric, some — sandal,
some — flowers, oil, chuya: hundreds and hundreds of people.

**51.** Hundreds of people offer garlands of fragrant flowers;
with garlands, sandal and the rest all were adorned.

**52.** Hundreds and hundreds of groups perform kirtana;
the sound of the mridangas [and] dundubhis touches the sky.[^40]

**53.** "Glory, glory!" — the cries of "Hari" thunder in bliss;
the circle of the earth took on the beauty of Vaikuntha.[^41]

**54.** The music, the roar of the dundubhis — most tremendous;
from the cries of countless people the earth sways.

**55.** People without count, dense cries of "Hari";
shingas, flutes, horns, many instruments are heard.[^42]

**56.** Sri Hridayananda Prabhu danced;
the third prahara came — all were amazed.[^43]

**57.** Thus [all] put the dadhi-kada on every limb;
all, holding hands, dance with various sports.

**58.** In the bliss of the taste of the rasa the fourth prahara passed;
in the evening the excellent Rasikendra brought [the festival] to completion.[^44]

**59.** Completing the sankirtana, Rasikendra Raya
fell flat with his body on the ground at Shyamananda's feet.[^45]

**60.** Then [he] bowed to Hridayananda;
the abode of virtues honoured all the elders.[^46]

**61.** With all the Vaishnavas [he] conversed;
in jubilation he embraced his companions.

**62.** Disciples, disciples of [their] disciples, and also servants [and] disciples —
then Rasikendra embraced all the people.[^47]

**63.** Having joyfully exchanged prostrations and embraces,
Rasika went to sport in the water.[^48]

**64.** The water of the Suvarnarekha is clear [and] deep;
a beautiful sandbank [and] groves adorn both banks.[^49]

**65.** Into that water Rasikendra Raya entered;
they splash water on one another, hand against hand, on the body.[^50]

**66.** Sporting in the water for a short while, [he] quickly returned;
he fed all the Vaishnavas.[^51]

**67.** [He] tore [cloth into] thousands upon thousands of kaupinas
[and], giving [them] to the multitude of Vaishnavas, took leave [of them].[^52]

**68.** With a steady [heart] he honoured all the elders
with coins, gold, sandal and the rest, garments, ornaments.[^53]

**69.** Then he honoured the brahmanas [and] sannyasis;
he sent off [with gifts] the rajas, the subjects, the residents of Vraja.[^54]

**70.** To all the [kirtana] groups he gave garments [and] ornaments;
then Rasikendra honoured his own people.[^55]

**71.** He sent off [with gifts] the dancing children:
garments, ornaments, decorations he put on [their] bodies.

**72.** Each one he sent off as befitted him;
all go away singing the virtues and fame of Rasika.

**73.** All the mahantas [and] Vaishnavas went to their own places,
reflecting in their minds on the fame and glory of Rasika.[^56]

**74.** All say: "Such a yatra we have never seen;
Rasika is not a human being — Krishnachandra is witness."[^57]

**75.** Even speaking with a hundred mouths, it cannot be told;
such a yatra [he] brought forth on earth.[^58]

**76.** Blessed, blessed is Rasikendra, blessed [his] mother [and] father;
blessed is the circle of the earth where [there was] such a yatra.

**77.** Such a yatra Rasikendra made known to the world,
by the sight of which people were delivered.

**78.** Thus Rasikendra on earth
gave prema-bhakti for Krishna in every house.

**79.** Day by day bhakti for Krishna arises more and more;
the son of Achyuta delivered tens of millions.[^59]

**80.** The holder of all virtues is Rasikendra-chandra,
whose glory gods [and] men sing.

**81.** What power have I to sing that glory?
Whatever the prince of Achyuta, dwelling in [my] heart, says —[^60]

**82.** in accordance with that I have described a little.
Listen to the Rasika-mangala, all you saintly ones.

**83.** In brief [I] have told a little of the rasa;
hearing [it], all people, cross over the [age of] Kali.[^61]

**84.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Pashchima-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the fifth lahari, called "The Completion of the Rasa-lila."*[^62]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the lahari's heading («পঞ্চম-লহরী»), in italics without a number. Lahari 5 begins on pdf 142 (printed p. 102), below a rule after the colophon of lahari 4 (files RM-3-04-065-067). The raga in the scan is *kāphi bhāṭiẏāri*: Kafi and Bhatiyari are names of ragas; here apparently a combination of the two. The ghosha reads in the scan: *jhure re prāṇa śyāmabandhu lāgiẏā*; "pines" — *jhure*, "weeps, yearns"; *re* is an exclamation. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 144 (printed p. 104), checked against the scan: «ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-পশ্চিম-বিভাগে রাসলীলা-সমাপন-নাম পঞ্চম-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা» (*rāsalīlā-samāpana*) — "The Completion of the Rasa-lila". The whole lahari is on pdf 142–144; the summary line follows the scan.
[^2]: "Renowned in the world" — *bhubanabidita*. "At every moment" — *anukṣaṇa*. "Deeds" — *carita*: life, deeds (apparently Rasika's).
[^3]: The account of the Rasa-yatra at Gopivallabhapur continues (Pashchima, laharis 2–4): following the order of the Bhagavata, the boys enact the end of the rasa-lila — the gopis' song in separation, Krishna's return and the dance of the rasa (Bhag. 10.31–33). According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, the rasa is a pastime of the Lord's internal energy, the pure prema of the gopis, having nothing in common with mundane love: kama is the desire to please one's own senses, prema is the desire to give joy to Krishna (CC, Adi 4); Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati taught that these pastimes must not be understood with the mundane mind or imitated (see further Pashchima, lahari 3, note 2; lahari 2, note 7). The boys' performance is a glorification of the Lord's pastimes, not an imitation of Krishna (Dakshina, lahari 5, note 22). "Manifests" — *kare*, lit. "performs". "Immersed" — *magana*.
[^4]: "With such movements of the body" — *tena aṅgabhaṅgī*. "As the gopis in the dance" — *yena nṛtye gopīgaṇe*.
[^5]: "All those words" — 8–11: the gopis' song in separation (Bhag. 10.31, the "Gopi-gita"). "Are spellbound" — *moha pāẏa*, lit. "fall into a swoon" (cf. Pashchima, lahari 3, note 21).
[^6]: "Wounded pride" — *abhimāna*: the hurt pride of love. "Taking up that role" — *sei aṅga dhari*, lit. "taking that part (that role)". "Expounds" — *karaẏe bākhāna*.
[^7]: Kanai is an affectionate name of Krishna. "Virtues" — *guṇa*: here, His beneficent deeds (8–9). "Remember" — *saṅariẏā* (= *smariẏā*).
[^8]: 8–11 retell the "Gopi-gita" (Bhag. 10.31.3–6). Cf. Bhag. 10.31.3: "from perishing in the poisoned water, from the serpent-demon, from rain, wind, lightning and fire, from the bull, from the son of Maya…". The poisoned water is the Yamuna poisoned by the serpent Kaliya (Bhag. 10.15–16); the serpent is Aghasura (Bhag. 10.12); the downpour is the one sent by Indra (Bhag. 10.25); the wind is the demon Trinavarta (Bhag. 10.7). "Irresistible" — *durbbāra*.
[^9]: "The bull demon" — *ṣaṇḍāsura* (in the scan the first letter looks rather like *ya*: *yaṇḍāsura*), understood as *ṣaṇḍa-asura*, "the bull asura": Arishtasura, whom Krishna slew (Bhag. 10.36; cf. 10.31.3: *vṛṣa*). "You saved" — *rākhile*. The second line — cf. Bhag. 10.31.4: "You are not merely the son of a gopi — You are the witness in the hearts of all embodied beings." "The Lord of all" — *akhila īśvara*.
[^10]: "The Soul of all" — *akhilera ātmā*. "The deliverance of the saintly" — *sādhujana-paritrāṇa*: cf. Bhagavad-gita 4.8 (*paritrāṇāya sādhūnām*).
[^11]: Cf. Bhag. 10.31.6 (*vraja-janārti-han*, "O destroyer of the distress of the people of Vraja") and 10.31.2, 10.31.6 ("show us Your face"). "Distress" — *ārata* (= *ārti*). "You who wear yellow" — *pītabāsa*: Krishna in yellow garments (cf. Bhag. 10.32.2).
[^12]: "Exactly [as in the Bhagavata]" — *se sbarūpe*, lit. "in that [very] form": the boys who portray the wives of Vraja lament just as the gopis do in the Bhagavata (cf. 14).
[^13]: "Poured out entreaties" — *nibedana kaila*, "made supplication". "As" — *yatarūpe*, lit. "in all the ways in which". "Exactly" — *sbarūpe*.
[^14]: The subject is not named; understood as Krishna (16). "Only a little" — *kṣaṇeka*, lit. "for a moment". "For sport" — *kautuke*. "Beyond measure" — *atireke*.
[^15]: Cf. Bhag. 10.32.2–3. "The life of all" — *akhilera prāṇa*. "Regained [their] life" — *pāilā parāṇa* (*parāṇa* = *prāṇa*).
[^16]: 17–19 — cf. Bhag. 10.32.4–8. "Another [places His] arm on [her] shoulder" — *keha bāhe bāhu*, lit. "someone — arm on arm"; cf. Bhag. 10.32.4: another placed His arm on her shoulder. "Chewed betel" — *carbbita tāmbūla*: cf. Bhag. 10.32.5: one received in her joined palms the betel He had chewed — the Lord's remnants (prasada). "With a light smile" — *hāsya lahu*.
[^17]: The subject is not named; understood as Krishna ("some… some" — different gopis); it can also be understood that the gopis win Krishna over (cf. 27–29). "Nail-marks" — *nakharekha*; "on the breast" — *kuce*. "The arrows of glances from beneath the brows" — *bhuru cāhani biśikhe*. The translation is exact; on the rasa as pure prema, see note 3.
[^18]: Cf. Bhag. 10.32.7: one gopi, beside herself with loving anger, bit her lips and looked at Krishna as if wounding Him with her glances. "With the greatest tenderness" — *parama senehe* (*seneha* = *sneha*).
[^19]: "Under the guise of wounded pride" — *abhimāna chale*. Cf. Bhag. 10.32.16: with feigned resentment the gopis ask Krishna who reciprocates love in what way; His answer is 10.32.17–22. "Clever words" (21) — *cāturyyabāṇī*. "The One who loves His devotees" — *bhakata-batsala*.
[^20]: "Softening" — *sadaẏa haiẏā*, "becoming full of mercy". "The Beloved of the gopis" — *gopikā-ramaṇa*. "You all never give up your crookedness" — *tomā sabā kuṭilatā nā chāṛa kakhana*: the meaning is unclear; apparently an affectionate reproach to the gopis for their trick question (20); it can also be understood as "[I] never [show] crookedness towards you". Cf. Bhag. 10.32.20–22: Krishna says that He hid in order to intensify their love and that He is unable to repay them for their devotion.
[^21]: "In delight" — *kautuke*. "Truly" — *sbarūpe*. "My dear ones" — *nija priẏa*. Cf. Bhag. 10.32.21 ("do not be angry with Me, O dear ones, your Beloved").
[^22]: Cf. Bhag. 10.33.2. "Having gladdened" — *santoṣa kariẏā*. "The Lotus-eyed" — *kamalalocana*.
[^23]: Cf. Bhag. 10.33.3: Krishna, expanding Himself, stood between each pair of gopis. "The abode of virtues" — *guṇadhāma*.
[^24]: "Full of flavour" — *rasāla*. Damodara is a name of Krishna ("bound by the waist", Bhag. 10.9).
[^25]: "Girl" — *bālā*. "With the play of her glances" — *cāhani bhaṅgīte*. "Pastimes" — *khelā*.
[^26]: "Again and again" — *ghane ghane* (cf. 35). Waist-bells — *kiṅkiṇī*; anklets — *mañjīra*. "Without pause" — *saghane*. Cf. Bhag. 10.33.6.
[^27]: "In one tune" — *eka tāna*: in harmony, in unison.
[^28]: "In mid-heaven" — *arddhasbarge*, lit. "halfway to heaven". Dundubhis are large kettledrums (Pashchima, lahari 4, note 5). Cf. Bhag. 10.33.4: kettledrums sounded in the sky and the gods rained down flowers. "Were immersed" — *majila*.
[^29]: "Made known to the world" — *karila pracāra*, lit. "spread, proclaimed". The son of Achyuta is Rasika (Purva, lahari 3, note 5); *acyuta-kumāra*.
[^30]: "Splendour" — *baibhaba*. "The pastimes of love for Krishna" — *kṛṣṇa-prema-līlā*. "Made visible" — *dṛggocara karāila*. "The son of Achyuta" — *acyutera bālā*, lit. "Achyuta's boy".
[^31]: "A partial incarnation" — *aṃśa-abatāra*: the rapturous words of the people (cf. Pashchima, lahari 2, note 28; lahari 4, 51 and note 37). According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching Rasikananda is a devotee, a disciple of Shyamananda, not an incarnation of the Lord; on how the author exalts Shyamananda and Rasika — Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11. "Into an ocean of bliss" — *ānande pāthāra*.
[^32]: "On a sweet night" — *madhura yāminī*. "Expecting the rasa [again]" — *rāsa karibāre anumāni*, lit. "supposing [there would be] a rasa held": the subject is unclear; understood as the people (40); it can also be understood as "[Rasika] intended to hold the rasa on the next day too". 39–40 form one sentence.
[^33]: "[He] was not inclined" — *mane nāhi laẏa*, lit. "the mind does not accept". "Put on your costumes" — *beśa kara*: dress for the performance (cf. Pashchima, lahari 2, 43–66).
[^34]: "Irresistibly" — *durabāra* (= *durbbāra*). "Until the fourth prahara" — *caturtha prahara*, lit. "the fourth prahara": understood as until the end of the night. A prahara is a quarter of the night or day, about three hours (Pashchima, lahari 4, note 46). "Without ceasing" — *anibāra*.
[^35]: Shyamananda Raya is Shyamananda (*rāẏa* is an honorific). "The measure [of the rasa] is one night" — *ekarātri pramāṇa*; "more is not fitting" — *āra nā yogāẏa*, lit. "more does not suit".
[^36]: "Accepted as authority" — *paramāṇa karila* (= *pramāṇa*). "On the morning after" — *tāhāra bihāna*. The dadhi-kada is a festive game in which the participants drench one another with curd mixed with turmeric and water and dance in this "mud", as at the Nandotsava (Dakshina, lahari 16, note 37).
[^37]: "Pots" — *hāṇḍī*. Phagu is a red powder (abir; Pashchima, lahari 2, note 36); chuya is a fragrant oil (Pashchima, lahari 2, note 19). "Without measure" — *apramite*.
[^38]: "Of various kinds" — *nānā bhānti*. "The crest-jewel of Rasikendras" — *rasikendra-cūṛāmaṇi*: Rasika. "Holds the dadhi-kada" — *dadhi kādā kare*.
[^39]: Kunkuma is saffron or a red powder for adornment; aragaja (*aragajā*) is a fragrant paste of sandal, aguru, musk and other substances; "saffron" — *keśara*. "Filling pots" — *hā̃ṛī pūri*.
[^40]: "Groups" — *sampradāẏa*: kirtana groups (Pashchima, lahari 2, note 17). The mridanga is a clay drum (khol). "Touches the sky" — *paraśe gagana*.
[^41]: "Thunder" — *cahala*: the meaning is approximate (in Pashchima, lahari 3, 6 the editor glosses *cahale* as "with the sound"; note 6 there). "The beauty of Vaikuntha" — *baikuṇṭhera śobhā*. "The circle of the earth" — *abanimaṇḍala*.
[^42]: "Without count" — *samuccaẏa nāhi* (cf. Pashchima, lahari 4, note 44). The shinga (*śiṅgā*) is a curved horn; "horns" — *biṣāna* (Pashchima, lahari 3, note 6). "Flutes" — *beṇu*.
[^43]: Hridayananda (Hridaya-Chaitanya) is Shyamananda's guru (Purva, lahari 1, note 13); "Prabhu" is an honorific. "Were amazed" — *camakita*. On the prahara, see note 34.
[^44]: "In the bliss of the taste of the rasa" — *rāsa-rasānande*. "In the evening" — *sandhyāte*. "Brought to completion" — *kailā pūraṇa*. "The excellent Rasikendra" — *rasikendrabara*.
[^45]: "Fell flat with his body on the ground" — *daṇḍabata kāẏa kṣiti*, lit. "[a bow like] a stick — the body to the ground". "At the feet" — *pāẏa*.
[^46]: "Elders" — *gurujana*: revered elders, teachers. "The abode of virtues" — *guṇadhāma*: Rasika.
[^47]: "Disciples of [their] disciples" — *anuśiṣya*. "Servants [and] disciples" — *bhṛtya śiṣyagaṇe*; it can also be understood as "servant-disciples". "Embraced" — *kole kaila*, lit. "took into his arms".
[^48]: "Prostrations and embraces" — *daṇḍabata kolākoli*. "To sport in the water" — *jalakeli*.
[^49]: The Suvarnarekha is the river on whose bank Gopivallabhapur stands (Pashchima, lahari 1, note 16; lahari 3, note 4). "Sandbank" — *pulina*.
[^50]: "On one another" — *eke āre*. "Hand against hand" — *hātāhāti*. "On the body" — *gāẏa*.
[^51]: "For a short while" — *kṣaṇe*. "He fed" — *bhojanādi karāila*, lit. "arranged the meal and so on".
[^52]: "Tore" — *cirilena*: tore (cloth) into strips. The kaupina is the loincloth of renunciants; in the scan *kouupinī*. "Multitude" — *samūha*. "Took leave" — *karila melānī*: sent [them] off with parting gifts.
[^53]: "With a steady [heart]" — *niścale*. "Coins" — *ṭaṅkā*: silver coins (rupees). "Elders" — *gurujana* (note 46).
[^54]: "Sent off [with gifts]" — *bidāẏa karila*: saw off with honours and presents. "The residents of Vraja" — *brajabāsī*: apparently Vaishnavas who had come to the Rasa-yatra from Vrindavana (cf. Pashchima, lahari 2, 13).
[^55]: "Groups" — *sampradā* (note 40). "His own people" — *ātmagaṇa*: relatives and his own circle.
[^56]: "Mahantas" — *mohānta*: heads of Vaishnava communities. "Fame and glory" — *yaśaḥkīrtti*.
[^57]: "Yatra" — *yātrā*: festival (the Rasa-yatra). "Rasika is not a human being" — *rasika manuṣya nahe*: the exalted praise of the people (note 31). "Krishnachandra is witness" — *kṛṣṇacandra sākṣī*: an oath formula.
[^58]: "Even speaking with a hundred mouths" — *śatamukhe kahileo*. "Brought forth" — *karila udaẏa*, lit. "caused to rise".
[^59]: "Arises" — *udaẏa*, lit. "rises". "Tens of millions" — *koṭi koṭi*, lit. "crores upon crores".
[^60]: 81–82 form one sentence. "The prince of Achyuta" — *acyuta-kuṅara* (*kuṅara* = *kumāra*): Rasika. The author attributes his inspiration to Rasika in his heart (cf. Pashchima, lahari 3, note 39).
[^61]: "Told" — *karila pracāra*. "Cross over" — *tara*: understood as an imperative; it can also be understood as "cross over" in the third person (cf. Pashchima, lahari 3, note 40). "The [age of] Kali" — *kalikāla*.
