# Purva-vibhaga. Lahari 15. The Journey from Vraja-dhama to Gauda

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Rasikendra thinks of Shyamananda without cease; in Vraja, Madana-gopala Sri Govinda at night commands Shyamananda to go to Utkala, instruct Rasika-Murari and preach prema-bhakti, and conveys the same command to him through Sri Jiva Gosvami; taking leave of Jiva and the Vaishnavas of Vraja, Shyamananda sets out with his disciples; in Agra the Mughal kotwal throws the Vaishnavas into prison, but at night an unknown person punishes him, and the kotwal begs forgiveness and serves them for a month; by way of Varanasi and Prayaga Shyamananda comes to Rohini and, learning that Rasika is at Ghantashila, goes there*

*The fifteenth lahari.*

*Raga Shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "O Hari, this time be merciful to me:*
*I hope to obtain the shade of Your feet."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the joy of all,
at whose feet the bee is Rasikendra-chandra!

**2.** Rasikendra meditates on Shyamananda without cease;
apart from Shyamananda he knows nothing else.

**3.** Shyamananda-raya could not remain in Vraja:
by Govinda's command he came to see Rasika.[^2]

**4.** I shall tell all of this in detail:
for what reason Shyamananda wandered through Utkala,

**5.** for what reason Prabhu came to deliver living beings.
Listen, listen, all, attentively, with a steady heart.

**6.** One day Shyamananda, sitting at night,
was chanting the name of Hari, full of joy.[^3]

**7.** At that time Madana-gopala Sri Govinda,
appearing before him, says: "Listen, Shyamananda!

**8.** My dearest devotee is Rasika-Murari;
instruct him in the land of Utkala.[^4]

**9.** Preach My prema-bhakti, you two;
deliver all the living beings of Utkala.[^5]

**10.** He is My very soul, most dear to Me, a dweller of Vraja;
go to the world of Utkala and show him mercy."[^6]

**11.** Hearing these words, Shyamananda, startled,
fell to the ground, his body stretched out like a stick.

**12.** Rising, he saw that there was no one in that place;
he weeps long because of separation from Krishna.

**13.** Having heard those words, Shyamananda in joy
recalled in his mind the command of Hridayananda:

**14.** "The command he gave me earlier in private —
its confirmation I have now received before my eyes.[^7]

**15.** With my own ears I heard the command from the holy mouth;
[but] how shall I leave Vraja and go to Utkala?

**16.** If I do not go, Krishna's word will be broken;
I shall surely go and see [that] jewel among men."[^8]

**17.** Night and day he ponders these words in his mind;
his heart does not consent to leave Vraja.

**18.** Then one day Madana-gopala
gave a command to Jiva Gosai face to face:

**19.** "Listen, listen, O Jiva, to what I tell you:
tell Shyamananda to go to Utkala.

**20.** Rasika-Murari is very dear to Me;
taking him [along], he will crush Utkala.[^9]

**21.** He will give My prema-bhakti to every single person."
Three times He gave him the command, to make it certain.[^10]

**22.** "He will serve My dweller of Vraja;
to Utkala the dweller of Vraja will go.

**23.** My dweller of Vraja suffers in Utkala:
no one knows [his] greatness, all have sinful hearts.

**24.** Removing the blindness of the darkness of sin, [granting] divine knowledge,
Shyamananda [and] Rasika will deliver [them]."[^11]

**25.** Hearing Krishna's words, Jiva-mahashaya
recounted everything to Shyamananda in due order:[^12]

**26.** "Listen, listen, O jewel among men:
Krishna has commanded you [to go] to the world of Utkala.

**27.** Rasika-Murari there is a person dear to Krishna;
making him your companion, deliver the living beings."

**28.** Hearing all these words from Jiva Gosai,
Shyamananda then gained conviction in his mind:

**29.** "I shall certainly go to the world of Utkala;
I shall see that Rasika-Murari, the one dear [to Krishna].

**30.** The one on whom there is such mercy of Krishna —
I shall surely meet that man."

**31.** Thus after some days Shyamananda-raya
took leave of Jiva Gosai.

**32.** Haripriya Dasa and all the other mahajanas,
the adhikaris, the dwellers of the kunjas — whoever were [there] —[^13]

**33.** taking leave of them all, Shyamananda
set out with joy in his heart.

**34.** He took with him all the scriptures of prema-bhakti;
Kishora, the young Shyamadasa of pure mind —

**35.** these three disciples [were with him], and with them one brother,
Thakura Prasadadasa, famous everywhere.[^14]

**36.** Taking these four along with him,
leaving Vraja, he came and stopped at Agra.[^15]

**37.** They all settled down inside Agra;
the kotwal of the capital, for his part, goes about on patrol.[^16]

**38.** Inside the city he sees the Vaishnavas;
seeing [them], the Mughal kotwal became very angry:

**39.** "Thieves or robbers [they may be], or sadhus — I do not know for certain;
why have they stayed fearlessly in the middle of the city?"

**40.** He gave a command to the guards: "Bring them all!";
the wicked men seized [them] and put [them] in prison.

**41.** He himself went and lay down on his bed;
suddenly someone enters that room.

**42.** Quickly lifting him together with the bed,
he dashed [him] down on the floor of that room with a crushing blow.[^17]

**43.** Throwing him to the ground, sitting on his chest, [the visitor] says to him:
"Listen, listen, you villain, sinner, evildoer!

**44.** My dear ones, having all settled down
in the middle of the city, sit and chant the name of Hari.

**45.** You seized them all and put them in prison;
today I shall destroy [you] together with all [your] family."

**46.** Suffering torment, the wicked man cries out in a terrible voice:
"My life has come up to my throat, I have fallen into calamity!"[^18]

**47.** His household came and surrounded [him] on all sides;
blood flows from his mouth, he breathes heavily.

**48.** They all lifted [him] up, put water in his mouth,
sat him up and brought him to his senses.

**49.** Then they ask him: "Why [are you] on the ground?"
Blood flows from his mouth, his eyes are closed.

**50.** Then the kotwal says: "Listen, all:
in the prison there are five vairagis.[^19]

**51.** They are not men — they are dear to Krishna;
quickly bring them to me."

**52.** Ten or twenty guards went, [carrying out] the command exactly,
[and] said: "All of you, come [with us]."

**53.** Hearing the guards' words, [Shyamananda] was startled;
feeling embarrassed in his mind, he remembered Krishna.

**54.** Then Shyamananda went to the kotwal;
seeing [him], the kotwal fell to the ground in reverent awe.

**55.** Stretched out like a stick, he fell at [his] feet:
"Forgive [my] offence — I take shelter in you.

**56.** I did not know that you are dear to Krishna;
for that offence I have suffered punishment needlessly."[^20]

**57.** The yavana praised [him] in many ways;
pleased, Shyamananda speaks:

**58.** "I beg this alms, listen, mahashaya:
you will certainly serve the Vaishnavas."

**59.** Receiving the command, the yavana ruler rejoiced;
from that day he serves sadhus.[^21]

**60.** From that [time] the joyful service of sadhus first [began]:
[even] yavanas serve at his command.[^22]

**61.** Thus the yavana kept [them] for a month
and joyfully served [them] with loving care.

**62.** [Shyamananda] held many discussions on knowledge with him;
then Shyamananda joyfully departs from there.[^23]

**63.** For some days he stayed at Varanasi [and] Prayaga;
after some days he came to the town of Rohini.[^24]

**64.** In the village he asked: "Where is Rasika?"
All said: "Surely at Ghantashila.

**65.** The mahashaya is there with his whole household."
Hearing this, Shyamananda went there.[^25]

**66.** How Shyamananda met with Rasika,
[how] on seeing each other both [were swept by] waves of prema —

**67.** of all this I shall tell something in detail;
hearing [it], the son of Nanda will feel joy.[^26]

**68.** Listen to "Rasika-mangala", all devotees of Krishna:
without delay you will obtain the treasure of prema-bhakti for Krishna.[^27]

**69.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Purva-vibhaga of "Sri Rasika-mangala" ends the fifteenth lahari, called "The Journey from Vraja-dhama to Gauda".*

[^1]: Raga Shri (*śrī*) — as in lahari 5 (note 1). The ghosha (refrain) is the same as in lahari 4 (there *tũyā pada-chāyā*, here *taba pada-chāyā*), and its translation is repeated. There is no mangala-shloka at the beginning of the lahari: after the heading, the raga and the ghosha, the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 86 (checked against the scan): "brajadhāma haite gauṛa-yātrā-nāma pañcadaśa-laharī"; Gauda here means the east in a broad sense (Bengal and the neighbouring lands): in this lahari Shyamananda goes to Utkala. Lahari 14 ends in the upper half of the same page (pdf 84).
[^2]: "The bee at whose feet" (1) — *caraṇera bhṛṅga*: one who drinks the nectar of the feet, that is, a devotee. Govinda is Krishna; further on in this lahari (pdf 85) "Madana-gopala Sri Govinda" appears to Shyamananda at night, and then conveys the same command through Sri Jiva Gosvami. According to BR, it was Sri Jiva Gosvami who sent Shyamananda to Utkala.
[^3]: "At night" — *niśite*; the edition gives in a footnote the variant *nibhṛte*, "in seclusion" (cf. 14).
[^4]: Madana-gopala Sri Govinda is Krishna (note 2); He speaks as one person (*kahe*, singular), and in 18 is called simply Madana-gopala. "In the land of Utkala" — *utkala purī*, literally "[in] the city (abode) of Utkala"; Utkala in general is meant, not Puri.
[^5]: "You two" — *dõhe*: so on the scan (the OCR and the 1997 ed. have *dehe*, "in the body"), i.e. Shyamananda and Rasika (cf. 24).
[^6]: "My very soul" — *mora ātmā*; "a dweller of Vraja" — *brajabāsi-jana*: so Krishna calls Rasikananda (as the editor of the edition also understands it; cf. 22–23). That Rasikananda is an eternal dweller of Vraja and the "soul" of Krishna is the view of the author, who exalts his hero (cf. lahari 12, note 43; lahari 14, note 11).
[^7]: Hridayananda is Hridaya Chaitanya of Ambika (Kalna), Shyamananda's guru (lahari 1, note 13). "In private" — *nibhṛte*. "Before my eyes" — *yugate*, glossed by the edition as *sākṣāte*, "directly, in person". What the guru's earlier command was the author does not say here; according to BR, Hridaya Chaitanya sent Shyamananda to Vrindavana to Sri Jiva Gosvami, who later sent him to Utkala (cf. note 2).
[^8]: "Will be broken" — *bhañjana haya*. "Jewel among men" — *puruṣa-ratana*: here Rasika; in 26 Jiva so addresses Shyamananda.
[^9]: Jiva Gosai (18) is Sri Jiva Gosvami. "He will crush Utkala" — *utkala karibe dalana*, literally "will trample, crush"; apparently he will crush the sinfulness of Utkala (cf. 23–24), i.e. conquer it with prema-bhakti.
[^10]: "To make it certain" — *pramāṇe*, "for confirmation, [as] proof". "Him" — *tā̃re* (honorific), i.e. Jiva.
[^11]: In 22–23 it is not clear who serves whom: "My dweller of Vraja" is apparently Rasika (cf. 10 and 23: "My dweller of Vraja suffers in Utkala"), and "the dweller of Vraja will go to Utkala" is apparently Shyamananda; it is also possible to read "[Rasika] will serve My dweller of Vraja", i.e. Shyamananda. "Removing the blindness of the darkness of sin" — *pāpa timirāndha chāṛāiyā*.
[^12]: "In due order" — *sandarbhe*, "coherently, in proper sequence"; *sandarbha* is also the name of Jiva Gosvami's works (the "Shat-sandarbha"), but here it is apparently used in its general sense.
[^13]: Haripriya Dasa (*haripriyā dāsa*) is unidentified; "the servants of Haripriya" is also possible. Mahajanas are revered Vaishnavas. Adhikaris (*adhikārī*) are here apparently the servants (managers) of the temples of Vraja; "dwellers of the kunjas" — *kuñjabāsī*, Vaishnavas living in the groves of Vrindavana.
[^14]: Line 34 can be divided in different ways. "These three disciples" (35) require three names; the text has *kiśora bālaka śyāmadāsa śuddhamati*: Kishora (Kishoradasa, Shyamananda's disciple; BR 15, 41), the [young] Shyamadasa and possibly a third, hidden in the words *bālaka* ("boy") or *śuddhamati* ("of pure mind"), which can also be understood as epithets; the reading is tentative. "Brother" — *bhāi*: a brother by birth or a spiritual brother (fellow devotee). "Thakura Prasadadasa" — *ṭhākura-prasāda-dāsa*; "Thakura-prasada Dasa" is also possible. The five vairagis in 50 are Shyamananda and these four.
[^15]: "These four" — *cāri vigraha*, literally "four forms (persons)"; *vigraha* here means "person". "Taking along" — *saṅgati kariyā*, as in 34 ("took the scriptures with him"). "Stopped" — *utare*, "put up, took lodging". Agra was the capital of the Mughal empire.
[^16]: A kotwal (*koṭāla*) is the chief of the city guard and police; "the kotwal of the capital" — *rājadhānī koṭāla*. "Mughal" (38) — *mogala*, i.e. a Muslim of the Mughal service nobility.
[^17]: "With a crushing blow" — *nirghāta kariñā*, "striking"; "dashed down" — *āchāṛila*. The visitor is apparently the Lord Himself: he calls the Vaishnavas "My dear ones" (44). The verb *kahena* (43) is honorific.
[^18]: "My life has come up to my throat" — *kaṇṭhāgata haila prāṇa*, i.e. "I am at my last breath". "I have fallen into calamity" — *paṛilũ pramāde*.
[^19]: Vairagis (*vairāgī*) are detached wandering Vaishnavas. The five are Shyamananda and his four companions (34–36, note 14).
[^20]: "Needlessly" — *akāraṇa*, "without cause": the kotwal seized the Vaishnavas for nothing and so brought punishment on himself; "I suffered punishment without [knowing my] fault", i.e. through ignorance, is also possible.
[^21]: A yavana (*yavana*) is a Muslim (glossary of the "Prema-vilasa"). "Ruler" — *rājana*, here the kotwal.
[^22]: "His" — *yā̃hāra* (honorific), i.e. Shyamananda's. "The joyful service of sadhus" — *sādhu sevā-pramoda*; the line can also be understood thus: "from here the joy of serving sadhus first [arose]: [for] even a yavana serves at his command".
[^23]: "With loving care" (61) — *samprīti-vidhāna*, "with loving attentiveness". "Discussions on knowledge" — *jñāna-goṣṭhī*, spiritual conversations. "Joyfully" — *raṅge*.
[^24]: Prayaga (Allahabad) is the confluence of the Ganga and the Yamuna. Rohini — *nagara rohiṇa*: Rasikananda's native village in Mallabhumi (lahari 3, note 6; in BR 15, Rayani).
[^25]: "Surely" — *sarvathā*, "in any case, certainly". "With his whole household" — *sarvārambhe*, literally "with all [his] establishment"; cf. lahari 14, 7: Rasika settled at Ghantashila "together with his family". Ghantashila — lahari 14, note 4. "Went" — *vijaya karila*, the honorific "deigned to go".
[^26]: "The son of Nanda will feel joy" — *ānanda pābe nandera nandana*; "[the listener] will obtain joy [and] the son of Nanda" is also possible.
[^27]: "Devotees of Krishna" — *kārṣṇa-jana* (lahari 10, note 13; lahari 14, note 22).
