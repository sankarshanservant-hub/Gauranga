# Purva-vibhaga. Lahari 2. Pilgrimage to the Holy Places

*A song in glory of Shyamananda and Rasikananda and an outline of what will be told; Shyamananda's birth in Dandeshvara to Krishna Mandala and Durika, his detachment and departure from home; his meeting with Hridayananda in Ambika and the name "Shyamananda"; Shyamananda's pilgrimage to the holy places, his return to Vraja and his life there in the company of Jiva Gosvami*

*The second lahari.*

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "It remains to proclaim the qualities of the moon Gauranga."*

**1.** *Song.* Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the abode of all virtues,
expert in all virtues, the life of those who have nothing!

**2.** Be merciful, Mahaprabhu — I humbly entreat —
that the glory of your virtues may appear in [my] heart.[^2]

**3.** How Prabhu came to the earth —
of that I shall tell; listen eagerly.

**4.** Let everyone say what he wishes — I have no fear of it:
the lord of my life is Rasika-shekhara.

**5.** I cannot live without singing his virtues:
Rasika-Murari has driven Vallabha mad.[^3]

**6.** I shall proclaim Rasika-deva's glory;
may good people and the learned not impute faults to me.

**7.** If one covers the moon with one's hand, it cannot be hidden:
it shows its own signs by itself.

**8.** Who has ever heard of such prema-bhakti?
[Such] has not been and will not be on the earth.

**9.** The lord of Rasika's life is the renowned Shyamananda;
through devotion to Shyamananda he became a giver of bhakti.

**10.** The Lord abides and sports in the heart of the devotee;
age after age, bestowing bhakti, He delivers the world.[^4]

**11.** All the people of Utkala were firmly set in sin —
[and] Shyamananda took birth in the form of Krishna's beloved.[^5]

**12.** His dearest devotee, the moon Rasikendra,
Shyamananda brought along with him to deliver living beings.

**13.** How the two of them subdued Utkala —
something of those stories I shall tell:

**14.** how the two were born, each in his own place;
how in detachment they wandered to the holy places;

**15.** how the two met and came together
[and] in many delightful ways gave prema-bhakti in Utkala;

**16.** how they delivered chandalas and others;
how the two became renowned in Utkala —

**17.** of all these wondrous [deeds] I shall make something known;
may the wise and learned not impute faults to me.

**18.** Now hear the secret of Shyamananda's birth,
the supreme object of worship of the Shyamanandi Vaishnavas.

**19.** Of his birth, of how he took renunciation, of his pilgrimage
I shall briefly tell something.

**20.** In the clan of the gopas [there was] Sri Krishna Mandala Mahashaya;
leaving Gauda, he made his home in Utkala.

**21.** A village called Dandeshvara is a very holy place;
in that village the Mahashaya settled.[^6]

**22.** His wife, devoted to her husband, was called Durika —
peaceful, self-controlled, forbearing, a mother of the world.

**23.** Husband and wife — both were known for their reverence for brahmanas,
devoted to every dharma, very pure of heart.

**24.** From her womb Shyamananda-raya was born;
for some time he lived in his own home.

**25.** Marriage and other enjoyments, various gifts —
thus he spent some time.

**26.** His heart was ever full of detachment out of love for Krishna;
the tears from his eyes drenched his whole body.

**27.** Absorbed in the rasa of Krishna, Prabhu does not know himself;
day and night he weeps unceasingly, calling "Krishna!"

**28.** The happiness of attachment to home he regards as equal to poison:
nothing pleases him but Krishna alone.

**29.** Prabhu tries to go away [from home],
but no one lets him go — all his kinsmen.

**30.** Then Prabhu explained everything to them all:
"Out of love for Krishna I shall go wandering.

**31.** I shall see Vrajapuri, Krishna's own abode;
when I remember it, my life will not stay in me.

**32.** Say nothing to me; listen, all of you:
I shall certainly go on pilgrimage to the holy places.

**33.** I shall certainly go round [the whole] earth."
Hearing this, his kinsmen were greatly afraid.

**34.** His kinsmen resort to various means:
in many ways they tried to keep [him].

**35.** Ocean waves are not held back by a dam of sand:
who has the power to break such detachment?

**36.** Prabhu's younger brother was Balarama Mahashaya,
peaceful, self-controlled, very pure of heart.[^7]

**37.** To him [Prabhu] handed over all household affairs,
and himself, taking renunciation, went out.

**38.** For some time Balarama remained at home;
out of love for Shyamananda his life would not stay in him.

**39.** After some days Balarama
set out on pilgrimage to the holy places in search of Shyamananda.

**40.** First of all Mahaprabhu Shyamananda-raya,
having gone to Ambuya, sees Sri Chaitanya-raya.[^8]

**41.** On seeing Nityananda he was filled with supreme bliss;
then he had the darshan of Sri Hridayananda.

**42.** Prostrate on the ground like a stick, he offers prayers;
the devotees report [to Hridayananda]: "[A] certain vairagi [has come].

**43.** He is very beautiful to look at, his form is divine;
offering praises, he keeps prostrating again and again."

**44.** Hearing [this], Hridayananda was overjoyed
and ordered: "Bring the vairagi quickly."

**45.** Seeing [him], Hridayananda rejoiced in his mind:
"This is the one who will make bhakti for Krishna manifest."

**46.** The Mahashaya asked: "Whose servant are you?
What is [your] name, for what purpose have you appeared here?"

**47.** He replied: "My name is Duhkhi Krishnadasa;
birth after birth I am your very own servant."

**48.** Hearing [this], Hridayananda Prabhu rejoiced;
giving instruction, he gave [him] the name Shyamananda.[^9]

**49.** [Hridayananda] commanded Shyamananda: "Listen, [and act] quickly:
make Vaishnavas of every house in Utkala, one after another.

**50.** By your mercy [one person] will become equal to you:
such a person has appeared in Utkala.[^10]

**51.** Taking him with you, give prema to all living beings;
Chaitanya's command is the sixteen names of "Hare Krishna."[^11]

**52.** Preach Chaitanya's prema-bhakti,
deliver all the living beings of Utkala."

**53.** Hearing [this], Shyamananda-raya was abashed:
"Everything comes true, Prabhu, by your mercy.

**54.** Be merciful to me, Prabhu, son of Subala:[^12]
in my heart I have a desire — to wander on pilgrimage to the holy places."

**55.** Having stayed there some days, he took his leave
[and] set out on pilgrimage to the holy places — Shyamananda-raya.

**56.** Listen, all, to Shyamananda's pilgrimage to the holy places,
by hearing which one obtains the treasure of love for Krishna.

**57.** First he went to Vakreshvara [and] Vaidyanatha;
to Gaya [and] Kashi, the abode of Shiva, he went quickly.[^13]

**58.** In [the month of] Magha — at Prayaga, [where] the Ganga flows south;
then, quickly reaching Mathura, he himself stayed there.[^14]

**59.** He saw the resting place on the Yamuna, Govardhana;
he sees Madana-gopala, Govinda, Vrindavana.[^15]

**60.** Wandering from kunja to kunja, he sees all the temples;
Gokula [and] the twelve forests — he saw them all.[^16]

**61.** Endowed with great renunciation, full of love for Krishna —
the servants [who were] with him cannot keep up with him.

**62.** Having stayed there some days, in his own play
he goes in whatever direction he wishes.

**63.** Joyfully seeing Hastina, the city of the Pandavas,
Prabhu very quickly reached Dvaraka.

**64.** Seeing Ranchhor-raya, [he was filled with] great bliss;
Shyamananda stayed some days in Dvaraka.[^17]

**65.** [His] renunciation is very severe; [he has] no awareness of the body;
he goes in whatever direction he wishes.

**66.** He knows neither north nor south, east nor west:
wherever the mind leads, there he goes.

**67.** All his companions search [for him] and roam from land to land;
after a day or two someone finds a trace of him.

**68.** Then he went to Sindhupura, to Kapila's place;
he came to Matsya-tirtha, Shiva-kanchi, Vishnu-kanchi.[^18]

**69.** Kurukshetra, Prithudaka, Bindu-sarovara —
Prabhu goes quickly to see Prabhasa.[^19]

**70.** With joy in his heart he roams, [heeding] neither day nor night;
in whatever direction he hears of a tirtha, that way he goes.

**71.** He takes no care to go round [the places] in order:
by his own will, as his heart pleases, he wanders.

**72.** He came to Tritakupayana-tirtha, Vishala;
he went to Brahma-tirtha, Chandra-tirtha, Pratisrota.[^20]

**73.** Having seen Prachi-Sarasvati, Naimisharanya,
Prabhu came to the city of Ayodhya and stayed [there].

**74.** The kingdom of Guhaka, [king] of the chandalas, the Sarayu, the Kaushiki;
he went to the hermitage of Paulastya, to the Gomati, the Gandaki.[^21]

**75.** Bathing in the sixteen tirthas on Mount Mahendra;
to Haridvara, the birth[place] of the Ganga, he came quickly.[^22]

**76.** He went to Badarikashrama and saw Narayana;
joyfully Prabhu sees the hermitage of Vyasa.

**77.** Incessantly he remembers the name of Krishna;
his cloth is soaked by the streams of tears from his eyes.

**78.** From there, after some days, wandering and wandering,
Prabhu came quickly to the Pampa, the Bhagirathi.

**79.** Afterwards Prabhu came to Sapta-Godavari,
to Dhenu-tirtha, to Shri-parvata, to the city of the Dravidas.

**80.** He went to the Lord of Mount Venkata, to Kamakoshthi-puri,
Kanchi, Haridvara, [to] southern Madhupuri.[^23]

**81.** He crossed the Kritamala, the Tamraparni, the Yamuna;
[visited] Mount Malaya, [and] Agastya's sacrificial ground.

**82.** He went to the abode of Chaidya, to the city of Kalinga;
he went to the southern ocean, to Sri Anantapura.[^24]

**83.** Wandering and wandering, at the lake of Pancha-apsaras
Prabhu spends his time with joy in his heart.

**84.** [The place] called Gokarna, Kulanaka, Trigartaka,
Durveshana, Arya, Nirvindhya, the abode of the Payoshni.

**85.** He went to the Reva, to the city of Mahishmati, to Malla-tirtha;
[visited] Shurparaka, Pratichiri, came to Setubandha.[^25]

**86.** In whatever direction the mind leads, in that direction he goes —
wherever he hears there is a holy place.

**87.** Whatever direction Prabhu goes, he asks no one;
what comes first, what comes after — he pays no heed to this.

**88.** Going to Dhenu-tirtha, he hears of the abduction of maya-Sita;
Avanti, Jiyada-Narasimha, the Godavari.[^26]

**89.** Devapuri, Trimalla, to the city of Kurmanatha —
in this way he keeps roaming, seeing tirtha after tirtha.[^27]

**90.** In supreme bliss Prabhu went to Nilachala:
arriving, he stayed in the city of Purushottama.

**91.** Seeing Krishna and Balarama — the lords of his life —
[he had] gooseflesh all over his body, tears flowing incessantly.

**92.** Seeing Jagabandhu, [he was filled with] great bliss and elation;
seeing [His] moonlike face, he fulfilled his desire.[^28]

**93.** Joyfully seeing all the places day and night,
conversing with all the mahantas,

**94.** having stayed [there] some days, he went to Gangasagara;
coming from there, he touched [the place of] his birth.[^29]

**95.** Then Prabhu went again to Mathura;
he stayed [there] many days in his own play.

**96.** Awaiting the appearance of [his] servant,
Prabhu incessantly wanders and sees Vrajapura.[^30]

**97.** Wandering from kunja to kunja, he sees all the places;
his voice choked with prema, tears, gooseflesh — incessantly.

**98.** Saying, "When will I attain Krishna, the lord of my life?"
in Vrindavana, at the place of the rasa[-lila], he rolls on the ground.

**99.** In renunciation, with a joyful mind, beside himself within,
he converses with all of Krishna's companions.

**100.** Jiva Gosani, Thakura Haripriya-dasa —
with all of them he constantly spent his time.[^31]

**101.** Absorbed in Krishna, he weeps incessantly,
reads the scriptures on bhakti and hears [them].

**102.** At every moment he delights in prema-bhakti —
thus Prabhu lived in Vrajapura.

**103.** The song Rasika-mangala is sweet to hear;
hearing it, all living beings, cross over [the age of] Kali.

**104.** Whoever hears of Shyamananda's pilgrimage to the holy places
is at once freed from all sins.

**105.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Purva-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the second lahari, called "Pilgrimage to the Holy Places".*

[^1]: Raga Karuna-shri — as in lahari 1 (note 8). The ghosha (refrain) is rendered approximately: "গৌরাঙ্গচাঁদের গুণ রহিল ঘোষিতে" (*gaurāṅga-cā̃dera guṇa rahila ghoṣite*), literally "the qualities of the moon Gauranga remained to be proclaimed".
[^2]: Mahaprabhu here is apparently Shyamananda (cf. 40: "Mahaprabhu Shyamananda-raya"); it may also be an address to Sri Chaitanya (cf. the ghosha). In the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition Mahaprabhu is Sri Chaitanya; the author thus exalts Shyamananda (cf. lahari 1, note 2).
[^3]: Rasika-shekhara ("crest-jewel of rasikas", 4) and Rasika-Murari are Rasikananda. Vallabha is the author himself, Gopijana-vallabha.
[^4]: Prabhu is here taken as the Lord; from the context the line may also refer to Shyamananda, whom the author exalts as descending age after age (cf. lahari 1, 9 and note 10).
[^5]: Literally "in the form of Krishna's beloved" (*kṛṣṇa-priyā-rūpe*; *priyā* is feminine), i.e. apparently Sri Radha; it can also be understood as "as one dear to Krishna". According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, Shyamananda is a devotee and servant of Radha, not Radhika Herself (see lahari 1, note 2).
[^6]: Krishna Mandala and Durika are Shyamananda's parents (cf. lahari 1, 10 and 61). Dandeshvara is Shyamananda's native village in Utkala; in BR 15 (20) it is "the village of Dandeshvara-Dharenda".
[^7]: Balarama is Shyamananda's younger brother (cf. lahari 1, 62).
[^8]: Ambuya (আম্বুয়া, *āmbuyā*) is Ambika (Kalna) and its surroundings. There Gauridasa Pandita, and after him Hridaya Chaitanya, served the Deities of Sri Chaitanya and Nityananda (cf. BR 15, 93–95); "sees Sri Chaitanya-raya" and "on seeing Nityananda" (41) refer to the darshan of these Deities. Hridayananda (41) is Hridaya Chaitanya, Shyamananda's guru (cf. lahari 1, note 13). On "Mahaprabhu" see note 2.
[^9]: Duhkhi Krishnadasa is Shyamananda's former name. Here the name "Shyamananda" is given to him by Hridayananda at initiation ("giving instruction" — *upadeśa kari*); according to BR 15 (106) and tradition, this name became known in Vrindavana when he found Sri Radha's anklet.
[^10]: "Has appeared" — *sannidhāna*. Apparently this refers to Rasikananda, Shyamananda's future disciple (his birth is told in lahari 3).
[^11]: The maha-mantra of sixteen names: "Hare Krishna Hare Krishna Krishna Krishna Hare Hare / Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare".
[^12]: Prabhu here is Hridayananda. "Son of Subala" — the spiritual descendant of Gauridasa Pandita, who in Krishna's pastimes is the cowherd boy Subala (see lahari 1, note 16); Gauridasa is Hridayananda's guru.
[^13]: Vakreshvara is a holy place of Shiva in Birbhum (West Bengal). Vaidyanatha is the shrine of Shiva at Deoghar (Jharkhand). Kashi is Varanasi; "the abode of Shiva" may also refer to all three places named.
[^14]: Magha is the month (January–February) of the Magha Mela at Prayaga. "The Ganga flows south" (*dakṣiṇa-vāhinī*) — so in the edition (and in the 1997 edition); the sense is approximate.
[^15]: The resting place is apparently Vishrama Ghat on the Yamuna in Mathura. Madana-gopala and Govinda are Deities of Vrindavana (cf. lahari 1, note 24).
[^16]: The twelve forests of Vraja.
[^17]: Hastina is Hastinapura. Ranchhor-raya is the Deity of Krishna in Dvaraka (see lahari 1, note 27).
[^18]: Sindhupura, "Kapila's place" — apparently Kapila's ashrama where the Ganga meets the ocean (Gangasagara); the 1997 edition reads "Siddhapura". Matsya-tirtha is mentioned in Sri Chaitanya's southern journey (CC, Madhya 9). Shiva-kanchi and Vishnu-kanchi are the two parts of Kanchipuram.
[^19]: The list of tirthas in 69–89 largely follows Balarama's pilgrimage (SB 10.78–79) and Sri Chaitanya's southern journey (CC, Madhya 9), but without geographical order — as the author himself says (71, 87). Names are given as in the edition (the 1997 edition agrees); some are corrupt.
[^20]: Tritakupayana is apparently the Tritakupa of SB 10.79.11. Chandra-tirtha is Chakra-tirtha in the SB. "Pratisrota" apparently comes from SB 10.78.18, where Balarama went "upstream" (*prati-srotam*) along the Sarasvati; here it is taken as a place name.
[^21]: Guhaka is Guha, king of the Nishadas (a hunting tribe) in the Ramayana, Rama's friend. The hermitage of Paulastya — in SB 10.79.10, the hermitage of Pulaha.
[^22]: "In the sixteen tirthas" — *ṣoṛaśa-tīrthete*; it may also be the name of a single tirtha. Mount Mahendra — SB 10.79.11. Haridvara is where the Ganga comes down onto the plains.
[^23]: Shri-parvata is Shri-shaila; the Lord of Mount Venkata is the Deity of Venkateshvara at Tirupati; Kamakoshthi-puri — CC, Madhya 9; Kanchi is Kanchipuram; southern Madhupuri is Madurai, "southern Mathura". "Haridvara" (*haridvārāya*) here is apparently a corruption of the words *sarid-varām* ("the best of rivers", said of the Kaveri) in SB 10.79.14. Dhenu-tirtha (79, 88) has not been identified.
[^24]: Kritamala, Tamraparni, Mount Malaya, Agastya — SB 10.79.16–17 and CC, Madhya 9; the Yamuna here is as in the edition. Chaidya is Shishupala, king of Chedi; read as in the 1997 edition ("চৈদ্যের"), the conjunct being unclear on the scan. Sri Anantapura is apparently the shrine of Ananta Padmanabha (Trivandrum; CC, Madhya 9).
[^25]: Pancha-apsaras, Gokarna, Arya, Shurparaka, Payoshni, Nirvindhya, Reva, Mahishmati — SB 10.79.18–21. Kulanaka, Trigartaka (in the SB, Kerala and Trigarta?), Durveshana, Malla-tirtha (in the SB, Manu-tirtha?) and Pratichiri have not been identified and are apparently corrupt. Setubandha is Rameshvaram.
[^26]: According to CC, Madhya 9, Sri Chaitanya heard the account of the Kurma Purana about Ravana's abduction of maya-Sita (the illusory Sita) at Rameshvaram; the author places it at Dhenu-tirtha. Avanti is Ujjain; Jiyada-Narasimha is the Deity of Nrisimha at Simhachalam (CC, Madhya 8).
[^27]: Kurmanatha is Kurma-kshetra, a place of Sri Chaitanya's southern journey (CC, Madhya 7). Devapuri and Trimalla have not been identified.
[^28]: Nilachala, the city of Purushottama, is Puri. Krishna and Balarama are Jagannatha and Baladeva; Jagabandhu ("friend of the world") is Jagannatha.
[^29]: His birthplace is Dandeshvara (Dharenda); see note 6.
[^30]: "The appearance of the servant" — *bhṛtyera prakāśa*: apparently the appearance of Rasikananda, his future disciple (cf. 50); the sense is approximate.
[^31]: Jiva Gosani is Jiva Gosvami, under whom, according to BR 15, Shyamananda studied the books on bhakti in Vrindavana (cf. 101). Thakura Haripriya-dasa has not been identified; the 1997 edition reads "Thakura Haripriya" (*ṭhākura haripriyā*). It could also be taken as an epithet of Jiva: "Thakura, servant of Haripriya (Radha)", but "with all of them" points to several persons.
