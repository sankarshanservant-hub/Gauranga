# Uttara-vibhaga. Lahari 5. The Description of the Failure of Gaurangadasi's Conspiracy through the Power of the Glory of Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; at the dadhi-kada Rasika, grey with the dust of the rasa and spattered with drops of curd, sits among his own with Krishna and Shyamananda in his heart, and the five sons of Rasamaya, as though sensing the plot, sit down around him, resolved to strike down the wicked and give their lives for Rasika; Padmanabha and Gopidasa read the letter, whose edge the elder mother holds — and in it appear verses of the Bhagavata from all twelve cantos, the siddhanta of the six shastras, the meaning of the Vedas, songs of Jayadeva and at the end three shlokas in glory of Rasika, threatening his revilers with hell; the conspirators, whispering, run off one after another, the Thakurani throws down the letter and weeps, and all acknowledge Rasika's glory; because of the incessant troubles Rasika resolves to hold the festivals at Gopivallabhapur and goes to Raja Ramachandra Dhal, and when the raja disregards his words, he leaves his land in anger, accepting not even water there, and foretells his ruin; the raja soon loses his life and many of his domains, and his son — a great Vaishnava, a disciple of Rasika — is placed on the throne; one festival of twelve days Rasika celebrates at Kusharada, in the kingdom of Patna; five such festivals took place in three villages, and twenty at Gopivallabhapur; the author promises to tell of them*

*The fifth lahari.*

*Raga Shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "O Hari, this time be merciful to me:*
*I hope to obtain the shade of Your feet."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the ocean of mercy,
at whose feet the servant is Rasika-shekhara![^2]

**2.** Thus Rasikendra, performing sankirtana,
danced much, celebrating the dadhi-kada.[^3]

**3.** His charming holy body is grey with the dust of the rasa,
and drops of curd are on his beautiful holy body.

**4.** Sandal paste, kunkuma, phagu, the pollen of flowers —
how [they] glitter on his long cheeks![^4]

**5.** His face, surpassing the moon, is exceedingly charming;
from his holy eyes tears flow in love for Krishna.

**6.** Holding Sri Nandanandana [and] the holy feet of Shyamananda
in his heart, the son of Achyuta remained.[^5]

**7.** Rasika's associates and followers together with his own people —
like a host of stars surrounding the moon.[^6]

**8.** As though knowing by a sign the plan of the wicked,
the five sons of Rasamaya sit down around Prabhu.

**9.** Within their minds the five brothers resolve:
"If anyone makes a disturbance against Prabhu,

**10.** we will strike down all these wicked ones,
and then give our lives before Sri Rasika."[^7]

**11.** Thus all together think within their minds;
all sat down carefully around Rasika.

**12.** Kings, subjects, brahmanas, sannyasis, gentle sadhus —
all the people sat around on all four sides.[^8]

**13.** At that time, bringing the letter, [they] presented [it].
Listen, all, attentively to this account.[^9]

**14.** Padmanabha [and] Gopidasa began to read;
reading the letter, they proclaimed [it] before all.

**15.** The elder mother holds the letter by one edge,
and they here, at the other edge, read [and] let [all] hear.[^10]

**16.** From the first canto to the twelfth —
ten shlokas [from each] were at the beginning of the letter.

**17.** Then — the siddhanta of the six shastras in subtle form:
they read ten or twenty shlokas, one after another.

**18.** Then in the letter the meaning of the truths of the Vedas appeared;
Padmanabha [and] Gopidasa went on reading.

**19.** Then — two or four songs of Sri Jayadeva;
on the four edges of the letter — narratives of the Bhagavata.[^11]

**20.** At the end, after that, three shlokas were written:
"Whoever reviles Murari is a sinner in this world.

**21.** Blameless is Rasika, known to all people;
the beloved of his life is Krishna; [he is] renowned in the world.[^12]

**22.** Whoever without cause lays blame on such a person
goes with all his line to a terrible hell.

**23.** One who is inseparably united with Krishna —
whoever reviles such a person is a sinner [and] a villain.[^13]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the title of the lahari ("পঞ্চম-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 5 begins on pdf 186 (printed p. 142), under a rule after the colophon of lahari 4 (files RM-4-04-001-059). Raga Shri (*śrī*; Purva, lahari 5, note 1); the ghosha is the same as in Uttara, lahari 2 (note 1), and its translation is repeated. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 187 (printed p. 143), checked against the scan: "শ্রীশ্রীরসিকানন্দ প্রভুর মহিমার গুণে গৌরাঙ্গ-দাসীর ষড়যন্ত্রের ব্যর্থতা বর্ণন" (*śrīśrīrasikānanda prabhura mahimāra guṇe gaurāṅga-dāsīra ṣaṛayantrera byarthatā barṇana*), literally "The Description of the Failure of Gaurangadasi's Conspiracy by Virtue of the Glory of Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu." Lahari 5 — 1–57 (pdf 186–187); the summary line covers the whole lahari (24–57 from the scan of pdf 187, provisional).
[^2]: As in Uttara, lahari 2, 1 (note 2): Rasika is Shyamananda's disciple.
[^3]: "Celebrating the dadhi-kada" — *dadhikādā sāri*, literally "having performed, completed the dadhi-kada": the festive drenching with curd (lahari 4, 36, 47, note 23); this is the very dadhi-sankirtana at which Gaurangadasi calls the assembly (lahari 4, 47–55). On the scan there is a stray punctuation mark after *saṃkīrttana*.
[^4]: "Grey with the dust of the rasa" (3) — *rāsadhūli-dhūsara*: with the dust of the circle of the dance. Phagu (*phāgu*) — the red powder people throw at each other at the festival (abir). "The pollen of flowers" — *phulera keśara*. "Long cheeks" — *sudīrgha kapole*.
[^5]: Sri Nandanandana — Krishna, the son of Nanda. "The son of Achyuta" — Rasika (Purva, lahari 3, note 5).
[^6]: "Associates and followers" — *sāṅgopāṅga* (Uttara, lahari 1, note 24). "His own people" — *nijagaṇa*.
[^7]: "The five sons of Rasamaya" — *rasamaẏa pañcaputra*: the author of the poem is a son of Rasamaya, a disciple of Rasikananda (the author's signature), apparently one of these five; the brothers' names are not given. "By a sign" — *iṅgite*. 9b — *yabe prabhu diẏā kare keha gaṇḍagola*: *diẏā* — "through, concerning"; understood as "against Prabhu." "Give our lives" — *prāṇa diba*: readiness to give their lives for the guru.
[^8]: "All together" (11) — *paraspara*. "Sannyasis" — *nyāsī* (so on the scan; OCR "স্যাসী"). "Gentle sadhus" — *śānta sādhujana*. "Sat" — *basilena* (so on the scan; OCR "বলিলেন").
[^9]: "Bringing the letter, presented" — *likhā āni kaila upasana*: the subject is not expressed — apparently the elder mother (lahari 4, 47–55) or her people; *upasana* — "arrival" (lahari 4, note 3), here apparently "presented."
[^10]: Padmanabha and Gopidasa — lahari 4, 55, note 30. "Proclaimed before all" (14) — *sabāsthāne karilā prakāśa*. "Holds by one edge" — *ekadike*: Gaurangadasi does not let the letter out of her hands (lahari 4, 54–55). 15b — *paṛiẏā śunaẏe ethā tā̃rā āra dike*: the sense is approximate.
[^11]: Instead of what the conspirators had written (lahari 4, 33–35, 39), the letter turns out to contain verses of the Bhagavata: "canto" — *skandha*, one of the twelve cantos of the Bhagavata; "ten shlokas" — *daśa daśa śloka*. "The siddhanta of the six shastras" — *ṣaṛśāstrera siddhānta*: of the six philosophical systems (lahari 3, note 16); "in subtle form" — *sūkṣmarūpe*. "The meaning of the truths of the Vedas" (18) — *bedatattba artha* (so on the scan; garbled in the OCR). Jayadeva — the author of the Gita-govinda; "songs" — *gāthā*. "On the four edges" — *cāridike*: where the conspirators had written the slander (lahari 4, 35, 39). This story is not in BR 15.
[^12]: "Three shlokas" — the author gives their sense in Bengali (20b–28, continuing on pdf 187); the Sanskrit text is not given. Murari — Rasika-Murari. "Blameless" — *adoṣita*. 21b — *prāṇera ballabha kṛṣṇa jagata-bikhyātā*: understood as "the beloved of his life is Krishna"; "renowned in the world" apparently refers to Rasika (the endings *-khyātā*, *-bikhyātā* are for the rhyme). "With all his line" (22) — *sabaṃśe*.
[^13]: "Inseparably united with Krishna" — *kṛṣṇera sahita yā̃ra abheda milana*, literally "whose union with Krishna is non-different": praise of Rasika. According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, the devotee is eternally distinct from Krishna, and "non-difference" is understood as complete oneness of heart and will in love and service, not as merging; Rasikananda is a devotee, a disciple of Shyamananda (Pashchima, lahari 4, note 37; on the author's attitude to Shyamananda and Rasika see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11). "Villain" — *durjjana*. pdf 186 (printed p. 142) ends with a complete ॥ 23 ॥; the words of the shlokas continue on pdf 187 (24–28).
