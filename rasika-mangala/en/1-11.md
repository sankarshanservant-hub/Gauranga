# Purva-vibhaga. Lahari 11. Description of the Wedding Preparations

*A song in glory of Achyuta's son; Achyuta announces Balabhadra's proposal to his kin, and all prepare gifts and clean the house; Balabhadra announces to his own kin that Ichchha-dei will be Rasika's wife, and praises the bridegroom; Balabhadra's sudden passing; his brother Sadashiva, true to the given word, fixes the auspicious time with astrologers, prepares the feast, adorns the house and sends men to fetch the bridegroom; Achyuta summons his kin, and they gather*

*The eleventh lahari.*

*Raga Varadi — panchali metre.*[^1]

**1.** Glory, glory to Krishna's qualities! To him who is full of all virtues —
glory, glory to Achyuta's son!
Treasure of the life of all, you have delivered all people —
of [your] glory I will compose [a song] a little.[^2]

**2.** Hearing Balabhadra's words, Achyuta, having weighed them in his mind,
spoke to everyone:
"Balabhadra Mahashaya has spoken with full certainty
about Rasika's wedding:

**3.** he has a daughter — in beauty and virtues [like] the Mother of the world;
he has given me his word [about her].
Seeing Rasika's form, he felt great happiness:
'I will surely give [him] my daughter.'[^3]

**4.** Listen, all you kinsmen: give your hearts to the wedding work,
prepare all the provisions.
The ruler of Hijli, the noble-minded Balabhadra,
who has wealth of lakhs and lakhs —

**5.** with such a man, by the Creator's arrangement,
a kinship has unexpectedly come about.
Prepare the provisions well, so that [our] dignity is upheld;
all of you together, make the effort."[^4]

**6.** Receiving Achyuta's order, all went to their places
and quickly prepared all the provisions.
What wonder in the gifts of him whose son is Rasika:
clothes and ornaments of every kind!

**7.** All the gifts and offerings, all the supplies they made ready —
of various kinds, of every sort;
the whole household cleans the house and doors,
[and] all four walls shone bright.

**8.** Then Balabhadra says, "Listen, listen, all you kinsmen,
I tell you all this news:
for Ichchha-dei, as is fitting, the bridegroom is Achyuta's son;
the Creator arranged this [match].[^5]

**9.** A most handsome bridegroom, enchanting in the three worlds —
is he Aja, Bhava or Narayana,
or Indra [and] the gods, or yogis such as Narada?
Seeing [this] boy, [one would say]: he is equal to Narayana.[^6]

**10.** Endowed with every virtue, there is none to compare [him] with;
boundless is the glory of his beauty.
Hearing the words of his Shri mouth, Brihaspati falls silent —
[he] is peerless in all the worlds.

**11.** By the good fortune of our line, or by the power of austerities,
[the Creator] has arranged such a bridegroom [for us].
Blessed, blessed is Ichchha-dei, born of a portion of Lakshmi,
whose husband is equal to Narayana!"

**12.** Calling his kinsmen, he tells them the true news:
Ichchha-dei [is the bride] of Achyuta's son.
At that time the most fortunate Balabhadra
suddenly breathed his last.[^7]

**13.** Then, after some days, Sadashiva, [true to] that word,
holding that promise as binding,
calling brahmana astrologers and reckoning all the auspicious times,
[resolved]: "I will give the daughter to Rasika."[^8]

**14.** Sadashiva, telling everyone all about it,
gave orders: "Take charge of the provisions:
curd, milk, ghee and the like, gur, guya, rice and the like;
bring cloths of many kinds.

**15.** Supplies of sweets and ghee — prepare them in great variety:
pitha, laru, bananas of every sort."
Royal dishes as gifts they prepared in many kinds,
in due order, according to their means.[^9]

**16.** They made the house and courtyard bright, laid jhunti-alipana,
drew mandalas in various forms.
Pictures of many kinds on the walls the bands of young women painted;
they adorned [all] with canopies of patanet silk.[^10]

**17.** Sadashiva Mahashaya says very humbly,
having reckoned the auspicious moment (lagna),
to two or four of his own people [and] two or four brahmanas:
"Bring the bridegroom," he says again and again.

**18.** "The bridegroom, Achyuta's son — go and bring him quickly,
having fixed the auspicious moment.
Go there with haste, bring Rasika here,
so that he enters [here] at that [appointed] time.[^11]

**19.** At Achyuta's place, all of you, speak humbly,
[asking him] to send his son:
'Having given our daughter to your son, we have taken shelter in you at heart' —
tell [him] the whole account."

**20.** Receiving Sadashiva's order, all went there
and told Achyuta [the whole] account.
Hearing all this, [Achyuta] attended to all
his kinsmen, wherever they were.[^12]

**21.** To all they gave gur and guya — to every house,
as custom prescribes.
They invited all — each and every person —
to Rasika's wedding.[^13]

**22.** Hearing [this], all the kinsfolk set out in haste
to celebrate Rasika's wedding.
Hearing, all came joyfully with [their] families,
having fitted out horses and litters in various ways.[^14]

**23.** Listen, listen, devotees of Krishna: the description of Rasika's wedding
I have composed as is fitting.
Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].[^15]

*Thus in the Purva-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the eleventh lahari, called "Description of the Wedding Preparations".*

[^1]: Raga Varadi (*barāṛī*; cf. lahari 8, note 2). "Panchali metre" (*pāñcālī chanda*) is tripadi; each stanza is set in four lines in the edition and translated likewise (cf. lahari 5, note 1). There is no ghosha and no mangala-shloka; the stanzas are numbered as in the edition.
[^2]: "Glory to Krishna's qualities" — *jaya jaya kṛṣṇaguṇa*: so in the scan and in the 1997 edition, as at the beginning of lahari 9 (note 3 there); possibly also "glory [to him who has] Krishna's qualities, full of all virtues." The address "you have delivered all people" (*uddhārile sarvajana*) is to Achyuta's son, Rasikananda.
[^3]: "The Mother of the world" — *jaganmātā*, an epithet of Lakshmi (or Durga); here, praise of the girl's beauty. "Has given me his word" — *bākyadatta kaila*: has promised to give his daughter in marriage (*vāg-dāna*, betrothal by word). 2–3 are Achyuta's words reporting Balabhadra's speech; Achyuta's speech runs to 5.
[^4]: "Kinship" — *bandhupaṇa*, relationship by marriage. "So that [our] dignity is upheld" — *mahatādi rahe yāte*, literally "so that greatness and the like remain." On Hijli and Balabhadra see lahari 10, note 21.
[^5]: "As is fitting" — *anukrama*, literally "in order, correspondingly"; the sense of the line is unclear and is translated from context. In 8–11 Balabhadra speaks to his own kin in Hijli.
[^6]: Aja is Brahma, Bhava is Shiva; Narada is the divine sage; Brihaspati (10) is the preceptor of the gods, a model of eloquence; "falls silent" — *tuni*, explained by the editor as *mauna*. The comparisons with Narayana (9, 11) and "a portion of Lakshmi" (11) are Balabhadra's rapturous praise (cf. lahari 9, note 22; lahari 10, note 35): according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching Rasikananda is a pure devotee and associate of Shyamananda, not Narayana Himself.
[^7]: "Breathed his last" — *prāṇatyāga*, literally "the giving up of the life-breath."
[^8]: Sadashiva is Balabhadra's brother (lahari 10, 86). "Holding as binding" — *pramāṇa kariyā*. "Astrologers" — *doibajña*, i.e. *daivajña*.
[^9]: Gur is unrefined cane sugar; guya (*guyā*) is areca nut; by custom gur and guya are sent to relatives with a wedding invitation (cf. 21). Pitha are rice cakes, laru are sweet balls. "Royal dishes" — *rājabhoga*.
[^10]: "Jhunti-alipana" — *jhũṭi ālipanā*: designs in rice paste on the floor and in the courtyard (in Utkala, *jhoṭi*, *jhuṭi*). "Mandalas" — *maṇḍalī*, circular designs. "On the walls" — *kā̃the*. Patanet is a fine silk cloth (lahari 10, note 24); "canopies" — *candrātapa*.
[^11]: The lagna is the auspicious moment reckoned by the astrologers; the bridegroom must arrive by it. "Two or four" (17) — *dui cāri*, i.e. a few.
[^12]: Stanza 20 ends on the next page (pdf 76). The second half of the stanza is elliptical: *śuniyā e saba kathā, bandhuvarga yathā yathā, sabākāre karila yatana* — apparently Achyuta summoned all his kin; cf. 21: the invitation sent to the kin with gur and guya.
[^13]: Gur and guya (*guṛa guyā*) — unrefined sugar and areca nut, which by custom are sent to relatives together with a wedding invitation (note 9). "As custom prescribes" — *yathāvidhi āchaye pramāṇa*.
[^14]: "With [their] families" — *sakuṭumbe*. "Litters" — *dolā*.
[^15]: "Devotees of Krishna" — *kārṣṇa-jana* (lahari 10, note 13). "The description of the wedding" — *vibhā varṇana*; the 1997 edition has *vibhā āyojana*, "the wedding preparations" (cf. the title of the lahari). The author's signature (the second half of the stanza) is given in the standard formula; in the tripadi metre it runs: "Making the holy feet of Shyamananda the ornament on his head, the son of Rasamaya sings" (cf. lahari 5, note 14).
