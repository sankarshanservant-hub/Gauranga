# Dakshina-vibhaga. Lahari 1. The Deliverance of Damodara

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Shyamananda lives at Ghantashila, holding kirtana in every house; in Rasika's house he lets little Devaki hear the sixteen names "Hare Krishna"; Rasika, recalling Krishna's command and Dayaladasi's prediction, asks for initiation and receives the mantra, and his wife Ichchha-dei receives initiation and the name Shyamadasi; in the raja's assembly Shyamananda in anger strikes the distracted Rasika with his foot, and Rasika sees in this release from bondage; Shyamananda prepares to go to Nilachala and to Vraja, tells Rasika to stay at home for a while and then come to Vraja, promising to take him along from there; on the way they come to Chakuliya, to Damodara, a learned yogi; Shyamananda reveals bhakti to him, and in the forest by the river Kharva Krishna appears to Damodara with Shyamananda on His left in the form of His beloved; Damodara with his family accepts initiation; Shyamananda departs for Nilachala and Vraja; the author says he heard all this from the lips of Damodara and Rasika*

*Glory to Sri Sri Radha and Govinda!*
*Sri Sri Rasika-mangala.*
*Dakshina-vibhaga.*
*The first lahari.*

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Rama, glory [to You]! Govinda, Rama, glory [to You]!"*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the purifier of the fallen,
the friend of all, the treasure of Rasika's life!

**2.** Thus Shyamananda, in the village of Ghantashila,
out of love for Krishna knows nothing [else] night and day.

**3.** In house after house he holds hari-sankirtana
and constantly meets with Rasika.

**4.** One day Shyamananda went to Rasika's house,
happy, with joy in his heart.

**5.** Seeing [him], Rasika with all his family fell at [his] feet;
in the house he prepared a seat and seated [him].

**6.** Washing both his feet with fragrant water,
Rasika himself wipes [them] with the finest cloth.

**7.** At that time Rasika's daughter Devaki
came into Shyamananda's presence.[^2]

**8.** Mahaprabhu asked: "Whose daughter [is this]?"
The household say: "Rasika's daughter."[^3]

**9.** Taking Devaki on his lap, Shyamananda-raya
let her hear the sixteen names "Hare Krishna."[^4]

**10.** Seeing [this] mercy to Devaki, Rasika
humbly speaks to Shyamananda:

**11.** "Listen, listen, mahashaya, kind to the devotees:
earlier Krishna gave me a firm command:

**12.** 'Your instructor is Shyamananda-raya.'
That is why you have mercifully come.[^5]

**13.** In my childhood Dayaladasi Thakurani
out of mercy herself let me hear the name.

**14.** Without guile that mother told me:
'The beloved of Krishna will meet [you] as [your] instructor.'[^6]

**15.** The guru's command [and] Krishna's command have proved true:
that is why you have unexpectedly come to me.

**16.** Instruct me in your own prema-bhakti,
by which I may attain Radha and Krishna, the lords of [my] life.

**17.** All the very names and very mantras of Radha and Krishna —
[reveal them to me] in mercy, Mahaprabhu — the truth of love for Krishna."[^7]

**18.** Having heard all Rasika's words, Shyamananda
at once gave [him] instruction — tantra [and] mantra.[^8]

**19.** Then Rasika fell at [his] lotus feet;
Shyamananda, lifting Rasika up, took [him] in his embrace.

**20.** Then Rasika went in to Ichchha-devi
[and] told [her] about Shyamananda:[^9]

**21.** "If you are truly my beloved,
then take instruction from Shyamananda.

**22.** Leaving all family, fear, shame, the customs of the family,
at once take shelter of Shyamananda."[^10]

**23.** Having heard her husband's words, Ichchha-devi, devoted to her husband, [said]:
"Whatever your path is — that is fitting for me too."

**24.** Hearing his beloved's words, Rasika joyfully [took]
camphor, betel, garlands, fragrant sandal paste,

**25.** various articles, various jewels, the finest garments;
filling trays, the companions took [them] along.[^11]

**26.** They were granted the sight of Shyamananda's feet;
Rasika, at [his] feet, tells him everything:

**27.** "Show mercy to [my whole] family, Shyamananda-raya:
birth after birth husband [and] wife are servants of your feet."

**28.** Having heard [this], Shyamananda-raya, with a joyful heart,
let Srimati Ichchha-dei hear the mantra in her ear.[^12]

**29.** He gave [her] the name Shyamadasi, famed in the world,
[and] dedicated [her] for life to the service of Krishna.

**30.** He commanded: "Shyamadasi, listen to my words:
you yourself will give the Vaishnavas food and water.

**31.** Wherever you settle by my command,
there the eight siddhis [and] nine treasures will come to you."[^13]

**32.** Having given Shyamadasi this blessing,
he granted Rasika the mercy of prema-bhakti.

**33.** The two always stay, sitting in seclusion;
filled with prema, they constantly read the tantra-granthas.[^14]

**34.** Shyamananda-raya is the embodiment of love for Krishna;
Rasika became the embodiment of prema by the mercy of [his] feet.[^15]

**35.** Thus one day, in the raja's assembly,
all are listening to the "Bhagavata" with joyful hearts.

**36.** Rasika cast a glance in another direction —
in anger Shyamananda struck [him] twice with his foot.[^16]

**37.** Having received two blows of the foot, Rasika, the crest-jewel [of rasikas],
fell to the ground, prostrate like a stick.

**38.** "Today my karma, both auspicious and inauspicious, is destroyed:
twice Shyamananda-raya struck [me] with his foot.

**39.** Today release from the bonds of material existence has come:
by the blow I have obtained both feet of Prabhu.[^17]

**40.** From today the darkness is destroyed" —
so he tells everyone, with tears in his eyes.

**41.** Seeing Rasika's devotion, Shyamananda-raya,
pressing [him] to his chest, weeps — Prabhu cannot restrain himself.

**42.** Thus Shyamananda spent some days,
always rejoicing together with Rasika.

**43.** Shyamananda said: "I will go to Jagannatha,
and from there I will go quickly to Vraja."[^18]

**44.** Hearing [this], Rasika says to Shyamananda:
"Take me with you to Vraja."

**45.** He commanded Rasika: "Listen to [my] words:
without you all [your] family will be sorrowful at heart.

**46.** Stay at home calmly for some time,
and afterwards you will come to Vraja — [so] I tell you.

**47.** First I go to Vraja — I tell [you] for certain;
and from Vraja, taking you [with me], I will go there."[^19]

**48.** Rasika accepted this command as binding;
from there Shyamananda set out.

**49.** For some distance Rasika goes with him;
he expounded books on bhakti and love for Krishna.[^20]

**50.** Coming to the village of Chakuliya, the two entered
the house of Sri Damodara Dasa Gosani.[^21]

**51.** I will tell in detail of the instruction given to him —
why Damodara received mercy.

**52.** The mahashaya practises yoga most intensely;
in his heart he ceaselessly contemplates the knowledge of yoga.

**53.** Most steadfast, an excellent scholar, of fathomless greatness —
Rasika knows the weight of his learning.

**54.** From childhood the two delight in learning;
by Shyamananda's mercy [he] became manifest [as a devotee].[^22]

**55.** Thus Shyamananda, in Damodara's house,
taking Rasika with him, speaks to him of the truth of prema.

**56.** He spoke to him of the truth of Sankhya [and] the truth of knowledge,
and then began to preach prema-bhakti for Krishna.[^23]

**57.** Hearing from Shyamananda of the greatness of bhakti,
Damodara began to extol knowledge.

**58.** Then Shyamananda explained both truths
[and] revealed the subtle bhakti within jnana-yoga.[^24]

**59.** In all of these [we] find the evidence of the scriptures about Krishna:
the subtle truth of the scriptures speaks of the marks of bhakti.

**60.** He revealed ninefold bhakti according to the scriptures
[and] its sixty-four limbs — in due order.[^25]

**61.** Among them the highest is prema-bhakti for Krishna —
[that] mood in which the young wives of Vraja are Krishna's beloveds.

**62.** And all else concerning the truth of prema he told him,
examining the truth of the Vedas, the truth of the scriptures [and] the tantras.

**63.** Damodara heard everything, one point after another,
and then fixed his mind on love for Krishna.

**64.** Rasika told Damodara in detail:
"Leaving everything, worship the feet of Shyamananda.

**65.** With all my family I have sold myself to these feet;
you too accept initiation (diksha) into the mantra of Krishna."[^26]

**66.** Then Damodara says to Rasika:
"Certainly I will sell myself to the feet of this Prabhu.

**67.** When I see some manifestation of his,
[then] with all my family I will become the servant of this Prabhu."[^27]

**68.** Thus for some days in Damodara's house
Shyamananda stayed, [absorbed] in his own pastimes.

**69.** One day, after the meal and so on, Shyamananda
sits with Rasika in the joy of [talks about] Krishna.[^28]

**70.** Damodara anointed [his] body with camphor and sandal;
Rasika, with joy in his heart, serves betel.

**71.** From there Damodara went quickly
into the forest — for breath practice.[^29]

**72.** There is a river there named Kharva;
Damodara came to that place.[^30]

**73.** A dense forest, a divine, delightful place;
Damodara saw with his own eyes:

**74.** suddenly in that place he beholds a wish-fulfilling tree,
a throne of gems, a city of jewels;[^31]

**75.** a youthful kishora form, dark and beautiful,
bent in three places, graceful, holding a flute and a peacock feather,

**76.** clad in yellow garments, in a captivating dress;
at His left side he saw Shyamananda.

**77.** Seeing the two present on the jewelled throne —
Shyamananda in his own form offering betel —[^32]

**78.** seeing Shyamananda-raya in the form of Krishna's beloved,
Damodara in amazement fell at [their] feet.[^33]

**79.** Tears of bliss, hair standing on end — it cannot be described,
[as he] saw with his own eyes the form of the two.

**80.** Rising, Sri Damodara weeps —
[and] the son of Nanda disappeared.

**81.** Where did the breath practice and the thoughts of yoga go!
Intoxicated with prema, he quickly set off home.[^34]

**82.** At home he sees Shyamananda with Rasika:
the two are sitting [talking] of Krishna in great delight.

**83.** Seeing Shyamananda from afar, Damodara
joyfully fell to the ground, his body prostrate like a stick.

**84.** In the highest joy Shyamananda embraced [him]
and, showing Damodara mercy, speaks fondly:

**85.** "The form that you saw with your own eyes —
leaving everything, meditate on that form."

**86.** Having heard Prabhu's words, Damodara says:
"If you show mercy, [you are my] shelter [and] brother.[^35]

**87.** I cannot understand anything of your greatness;
who in the three worlds will understand your artful play?[^36]

**88.** Be merciful to me, Prabhu, son of Durika:
with all my family I have sold myself to your feet."

**89.** Having heard Damodara's words, Shyamananda,
with joy in his heart, gave [him] initiation (diksha) into the mantra of Krishna.

**90.** Then his two wives [and] his fortunate mother —
the whole family, with pure minds, sold themselves at [his] feet.

**91.** For some days Shyamananda stayed there;
the three of them constantly speak of Krishna.

**92.** All the tantras, mantras, scriptures, [all that is] full of bhakti and prema,
he mercifully told Rasika [and] Damodara.

**93.** First of all these two disciples, great souls,
became manifest by Shyamananda's mercy.

**94.** Earlier Netrananda, Kishora, Haridasa became famed;
then Rasika [and] Damodara became renowned in the world.[^37]

**95.** Then Shyamananda-raya went to Nilachala;
staying [there] some days, he went to the Mathura region.[^38]

**96.** Waiting for Rasika in Vraja,
he ceaselessly wanders from forest to forest.

**97.** Listen, all, to this account of the instruction —
words from the lips of Damodara [and] Rasika.

**98.** With faith [I] questioned the three prabhus;
whatever they told me out of mercy —[^39]

**99.** that account ceaselessly awakens in my heart;
now I have made [it] known, receiving the command on my head.

**100.** Whatever the son of Achyuta told me —
just so I have composed [this account of his] glory.[^40]

**101.** Let the learned and noble not hold faults in this against me;
listen to "Rasika-mangala", all you devotees of Krishna.

**102.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Dakshina-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the first lahari, called "The Deliverance of Damodara".*

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the heading of a new part of the poem, as at the beginning of the Purva-vibhaga (Purva, lahari 1); they are given in italics without a number. The Dakshina-vibhaga begins on a new page (pdf 90) after the colophon of the last, 16th lahari of the Purva-vibhaga (pdf 89). The raga Karuna-shri and the ghosha (refrain) are the same as in the Purva-vibhaga, lahari 1 (note 8), and the translation of the ghosha is repeated. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 93 (checked against the scan): "দামোদর-উদ্ধারনাম প্রথম-লহরী" (*dāmodara-uddhāra*). Damodara is the yogi whom Shyamananda turned to bhakti (cf. BR 15, 55–58).
[^2]: Devaki is Rasikananda's daughter; the author offers obeisances to her in the Purva-vibhaga, lahari 1, 109.
[^3]: Mahaprabhu here is Shyamananda (cf. Purva, lahari 2, note 2); likewise in 17. In the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition Mahaprabhu is Sri Chaitanya.
[^4]: The sixteen names are the maha-mantra "Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare / Hare Rama, Hare Rama, Rama Rama, Hare Hare" (cf. the Kali-santarana Upanishad). This is not diksha, only the mercy of letting her hear the holy name (cf. Purva, lahari 7, note 15).
[^5]: On Krishna's command see Purva, lahari 14 (Krishna appears to Rasika and declares that Shyamananda will be his instructor). "Instructor" — *upadeśa-kartā*, here the one who gives initiation (the diksha-guru). "Have come" — *karilā vijaya*, the respectful "have been pleased to arrive."
[^6]: Dayaladasi Thakurani is the saintly Vaishnavi who let Rasika hear the holy name in infancy (Purva, lahari 4, note 18; lahari 7, 22–49, notes 10, 11, 15, 16); the text has *dayāla dāsī*. "Mother" is a respectful address to her (Purva, lahari 7, note 11). "Without guile" — *niramāyā*, "sincerely" (Purva, lahari 14, note 21). "The beloved of Krishna" — *kṛṣṇa-priyā* (feminine), i.e., Shyamananda: thus the author exalts him; see the theological explanation in the Purva-vibhaga, lahari 14, note 11, and lahari 1, note 2. In lahari 7 (48–49) Dayaladasi calls the future instructor Rasika's "lord of life," without this epithet.
[^7]: "The very names and very mantras" — *nija-nāma nija-mantra*: apparently the names and mantras belonging to Radha and Krishna Themselves; it can also be read "your own." In 18 (pdf 91) Shyamananda gives Rasika "tantra and mantra" — initiation.
[^8]: "Tantra [and] mantra" — *tantra mantra upadeśa*: initiation (diksha) into the mantra according to the sacred manuals (tantras, agamas); cf. BR 15, 47: Shyamananda gave Rasika initiation into the mantra of Radha and Krishna. "Instruction" (*upadeśa*) here, as in 12, 14, 16, means initiation.
[^9]: Ichchha-devi — *icchādevī* (20, 23); in 28 *icchādei*, as in the Purva-vibhaga (lahari 10, note 26): Rasika's wife, daughter of Balabhadra, the governor of Hijli. Shyamadasi (29) is her initiated name (Purva, lahari 1, note 47).
[^10]: "Truly" — *paramāṇa*, "indeed, certainly". "Customs of the family" — *kulācāra*. "Take shelter" — *śaraṇa paśaha*, literally "enter the shelter". "At once" — *tatakāla* (= *tatkāla*).
[^11]: "Companions" — *sakhīgaṇa*, Ichchha-devi's companions; with the verb *lailena* (honorific) the line can also be taken as "[she] took her companions along". "Filling trays" — *thāli puri*.
[^12]: "Let her hear the mantra in her ear" — the rite of diksha: the guru speaks the mantra into the disciple's ear. Srimati is a respectful title for a woman.
[^13]: The eight siddhis are the mystic perfections of yoga (anima, laghima and others); the nine treasures (nava-nidhi) are the treasures of Kubera. Here they stand for abundance: wherever Shyamadasi lives, she will have all she needs to feed the Vaishnavas (30). For a pure devotee they are no goal in themselves.
[^14]: "The two" — *dõhe*: apparently Shyamananda and Rasika (cf. 34); possibly Rasika and Shyamadasi. "In seclusion" — *nigame* (Purva, lahari 16, note 21). "Tantra-granthas" — *tantragrantha*: here apparently Vaishnava manuals of worship (agamas, Pancharatra); cf. 18, 62, 92.
[^15]: "By the mercy of [his] feet" — *rasika-caraṇa-kṛpāya* (so on the scan, hyphenated): taken as "Rasika [became the embodiment of prema] by the mercy of [Shyamananda's] feet"; grammatically "by the mercy of Rasika's feet" is also possible.
[^16]: "Struck him twice with his foot" — *mārilena dui lātha*, literally "gave two kicks" (so on the scan; the OCR has *lākha*). The author presents the guru's anger as mercy: Rasika sees in the blow the destruction of karma and liberation (38–40), since he has been touched by the guru's feet. This episode is not in BR 15; cf. BR 15, 52: "Shyamananda tested Murari".
[^17]: "Release from the bonds of material existence" — *bhava-bandha-vimocana*. "By the blow" — *nirghāte*, "by a blow; certainly". "Darkness" (40) — *timira*, the darkness of ignorance.
[^18]: "To Jagannatha" — to Puri (Nilachala; cf. 95).
[^19]: "There" — *tathāya*: apparently to Utkala; cf. lahari 2, 64: Shyamananda, having met Rasika in Vraja, sets out with him for Utkala. The line can also be taken as "[returning] from Vraja, I will take you there [to Vraja]". "Calmly" (46) — *niścale*.
[^20]: The subject of the second line is not named: apparently Shyamananda expounds [to Rasika] on the way; possibly Rasika. "Expounded" — *bākhāna* (= *vyākhyāna*).
[^21]: Chakuliya — *cākuliyā*; apparently present-day Chakulia in East Singhbhum district (Jharkhand), east of Ghatshila. Sri Damodara Dasa Gosani is the former yogi who became Shyamananda's disciple (BR 15, 55–58); the author bows to him in the Purva-vibhaga, lahari 1, 72–74 (note 37) and 96.
[^22]: "The two" — Rasika and Damodara: apparently friends from childhood (cf. 53; Purva, lahari 1, 74). "[He] became manifest" — *hailā parakāśa*, "was revealed, came forth"; the subject is not named — apparently Damodara [as a devotee]; cf. 93, where the same expression is used of the two disciples. Also possible: "[their learning] became manifest".
[^23]: Sankhya — a philosophical system (darshana) (Purva, lahari 16, note 22); "knowledge" — *jñāna*, the path of knowledge leading to the impersonal Brahman.
[^24]: "Both truths" — *dui tattva*: apparently jnana and bhakti. "The subtle bhakti within jnana-yoga" — *jñānayoga-madhye bhakti sūkṣma*: according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, knowledge and yoga bear fruit only through an admixture of bhakti, whereas pure bhakti does not depend on them (SB 10.14.4; CC, Madhya 22).
[^25]: Ninefold bhakti — hearing, chanting, remembering and so on (SB 7.5.23–24); the sixty-four limbs (angas) of sadhana-bhakti — according to the "Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu" (1.2) and CC, Madhya 22. "In due order" — *nikhila-yugate*, glossed by the editor as *saṅgati-mata*, "accordingly, properly".
[^26]: "Sold myself" — *bikāyinu*: gave myself over completely, became a servant; likewise in 66, 88, 90. "These feet" — Shyamananda's feet.
[^27]: "Some manifestation of his" — *ihāra prakāśa*: some display of his [spiritual] power. Damodara's condition is fulfilled in 74–79.
[^28]: "In the joy of [talks about] Krishna" — *kṛṣṇera sānande*, literally "in the joy of Krishna": so on the scan and in the 1997 edition; cf. 82.
[^29]: "Breath practice" — *pavana-sādhana* (in 81 *pavana-abhyāsa*): pranayama, the control of the breath in yoga.
[^30]: Kharva — *kharbbā* (so on the scan; the 1997 edition has *kharbba*); the river is not identified. "Came" — *uttarilā*, "arrived; crossed".
[^31]: The wish-fulfilling tree (kalpa-vriksha), the throne and the city of jewels are features of the spiritual Vrindavana (cf. "Brahma-samhita" 5.29, 5.56: abodes of chintamani stones amid wish-fulfilling trees).
[^32]: "In his own form" — *nija veśe*, literally "in his own dress", i.e. in the form of Shyamananda; offering betel is service to Krishna. The 1997 edition is corrupt here.
[^33]: "In the form of Krishna's beloved" — *kṛṣṇapriyā-rūpa* (the edition misprints *śyānānanda* for *śyāmānanda*); the place at Krishna's left (76) is Sri Radha's. Thus the author exalts Shyamananda (cf. note 6). According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, Shyamananda is a devotee and servant of Radha, a disciple of Hridaya Chaitanya, not Radhika Herself (Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11). BR 15 (55–58) does not describe Damodara's vision: there Shyamananda, showing him mercy, "immersed him in bhakti-rasa".
[^34]: "Intoxicated with prema" — *premamaye mattā*: so on the scan (the feminine ending apparently for the rhyme with *cintā*).
[^35]: "Shelter [and] brother" — *śaraṇa sodara*: clear on the scan (and so in the 1997 edition); the meaning is unclear. Taken as an address to Shyamananda; possibly "be [my] shelter, like a brother born of the same mother".
[^36]: "Artful play" — *cāturī*, "skill, ingenuity".
[^37]: Netrananda, Kishora, Haridasa — early disciples of Shyamananda. Kishora is apparently Kishoradasa (BR 15, 41, 60); Netrananda — cf. Purva, lahari 1, note 36; Haridasa is not identified. The 1997 edition prints "কিশোর-হরিদাস": possibly one person, Kishora-Haridasa. "First" (93) — apparently the first in these parts (in Utkala), since earlier disciples are named just after.
[^38]: Nilachala — Puri (cf. 43). The Mathura region — *mathurā-maṇḍala*, Vraja.
[^39]: "Questioned" — *śudhāila*: a third-person form; taken as the author's words (cf. "me" in the second line and 99–100); the subject may be someone else. The three prabhus are apparently Shyamananda, Rasika and Damodara (cf. 97).
[^40]: The son of Achyuta is Rasikananda. "Composed" — *granthana*, literally "strung together, woven". "Devotees of Krishna" (101) — *kārṣṇa-jana* (Purva, lahari 10, note 13; lahari 14, note 22).
