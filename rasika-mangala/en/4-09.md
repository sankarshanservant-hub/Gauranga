# Uttara-vibhaga. Lahari 9. The Description of the Sinking of the Boat in the Midst of the Vaitarani River and of the Rescue from It of the Devotees and the Book Srimad-Bhagavatam

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Rasika travels to Nilachala with great devotees, amid cries of "Hari!", singing, flutes and vinas; thousands of people gather to see him, many become his disciples, and all Utkala takes exclusive shelter in Krishna; at Dhamnagar Rasika leaves his palanquin and goes on foot; at Jajpur he bathes in the Vaitarani at the Ashvamedha ghat, sees Varahanatha, holds kirtan, dances and gives gifts to the brahmanas; the next day, on the flooded river, the wind and waves capsize the boat carrying two or three hundred people, but where a twenty-cubit pole finds no bottom the water becomes knee-deep for all, and Rasika turns the boat over; people on the banks lament the drowned, while others say that in Rasika's boat no one will perish; boats sent out pick everyone up; the manuscript of the Bhagavata is missing, the brahmana Radhamadhava finds it in the river, but neither he nor dozens of people can lift it out of the water, while Rasika lifts it into the boat with his hand, lighter than a flower; the author asks the learned not to hold against him faults in the order of the narrative*

*The ninth lahari.*

*Raga Shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Rama, glory [to You]! Govinda, Rama, glory [to You]!"*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the life of [his] beloved!
Be merciful, so that I may compose [his] glory.[^2]

**2.** At this time Rasikendra goes from road to road;
with him are great, great, exalted bhagavatas.

**3.** Along the road they go, again and again making the sound of Hari's name;
some sing, some play the flute [and] the vina.[^3]

**4.** Thousands upon thousands of people come to see;
with cries of "Hari!" they come from all sides.

**5.** Seeing Rasika's form, all in joy
gaze upon [his] lotus feet.

**6.** Seeing [his] form, the men and women were enchanted;
hearing Rasika's words, they gladden their ears.

**7.** Like bees, people surrounded [him] to see;
no one's heart consents to leave [him] and go.

**8.** How sweet [his] words, how sweet his converse!
He won everyone over by the radiance of his glance.[^4]

**9.** Many became [his] disciples as he went along the road;
becoming devoted to Krishna alone, they worship Krishna with pure hearts.

**10.** Seeing Rasika, all gave their minds to Krishna;
the land of Utkala took exclusive shelter.[^5]

**11.** Prabhu entered Dhamnagar;
from that place he gave up the palanquin.[^6]

**12.** On foot goes Rasika-shekhara;
at last he arrived at the town of Jajpur.

**13.** Bathing in the Sri Vaitarani — at the Ashvamedha ghat;
on its bank he saw Varahanatha.[^7]

**14.** That day he held kirtan at that place;
the son of Achyuta danced much.

**15.** Hundreds upon hundreds of streams of tears flow from [his] eyes;
seeing [this], the men and women were amazed.

**16.** Prabhu gave many goods to the brahmanas;
from there, the next day, he set out.

**17.** He entered the river at Jajpur;
the river was especially full from the flood.[^8]

**18.** Seeing the waves, the men and women tremble;
a boat came up and stopped close by.

**19.** Two or three hundred people sit [in it] at once;
Rasika-shekhara went and sat in the boat.

**20.** Remembering Krishna, taking his close ones with him,
ten or twenty mahantas joyfully went and sat [in it].[^9]

**21.** Steering the boat, the helmsman took [it] some distance;
from the force of the wind the boat pitches to and fro.

**22.** In the strong wind wave upon wave rises;
the boat, having gone [a little], came to the middle of the river.

**23.** The helmsman in great terror could not control [it];
the boat pitches to and fro without end, beyond measure.

**24.** In distress they remember Krishna:
"In this danger protect [us], son of Achyuta!

**25.** Apart from you there is no other protector;
in this terrible peril, Prabhu, save [us]!"

**26.** Calling out, Prabhu says to all:
"Do not fear; remember Krishna."

**27.** Even as he spoke, the boat capsized;
all fell and remained inside the boat.

**28.** Some people fell outside the boat —
Rasikendra-chudamani with his close ones.

**29.** The river is a bottomless sea; in its very middle
the men and women fell into the water.[^10]

**30.** In the boundless sea the water became knee-deep;
all the people stood up in the river.

**31.** Standing on firm ground, Prabhu quickly seized the boat,
lifted [and] turned the boat over — the men and women rise up.[^11]

**32.** Frantic with fear, all quickly rose;
all joyfully sing Rasika's qualities:

**33.** "Blessed, blessed is Rasikendra, the ocean of compassion!
We were sinking in the water in the middle of the river.

**34.** In the midst of the bottomless sea [the water] became thigh-deep!
Blessed is Rasikendra-chandra, the ocean of mercy!

**35.** Through such an impassable [place] you carried all of us across:
in the bottomless water of the sea we stood.[^12]

**36.** To deliver [the people of] the terrible [age of] Kali — Rasika the avatara!
In this terrible water he carried all of us across.[^13]

**37.** Prabhu, the breaker of fear, the friend of suffering people,
whose vow is to be a fortress of refuge, the ocean of mercy!"[^14]

**38.** Thus all the men and women offer praise;
Rasika's ingenuity cannot be understood at all.

**39.** Seeing the boat sinking, all the people on both banks
roll on the ground [and] weep — they cannot be held back.[^15]

**40.** All say: "Not one will survive now!
They have sunk in the middle of the river — protect [them], Narayana!

**41.** With huge waves the river devours both banks;
great is the roar of the waves under the blows of the wind.

**42.** In such waves hundreds of thousands (?) have sunk —
for nothing all the living beings have drowned [and] perished."[^16]

**43.** Some say: "In the boat in which Rasika travels
not one will perish — by his mercy.

**44.** He who holds to his name in the hour of great danger
is not touched by misfortune: he crosses over with ease.

**45.** Tiger, bear, elephant, lion, rhinoceros — flee;
when Rasika is remembered, none comes near.[^17]

**46.** That very Prabhu himself travels in the boat with them.
Have no fear at all," [they] said to everyone.

**47.** Fathomless is the glory of Rasikendra-chudamani:
he himself was born from a part of Narayana.[^18]

**48.** Whose vow is to deliver the fallen, the life of all,
Prabhu, the crusher of the pashandis, the son of Achyuta!

**49.** All the sadhus began to offer praise;
all [together] sent out two or four boats.

**50.** [The boatmen] saw all the people standing in the middle of the river;
all, taking the boats, came up [to them].

**51.** They lower a bamboo pole of twenty cubits — they find no bottom;
in that very place Rasika-shekhara is standing.[^19]

**52.** Two or three hundred people, all the sadhus —
all are standing in water up to the thigh.

**53.** Seeing [this], all the boatmen were amazed;
they lifted all into the boats, taking [them] by the hand.

**54.** Carefully they lifted out everyone's belongings;
they saw that no one had the Sri Bhagavata.[^20]

**55.** Amazed, Prabhu asks everyone;
the brahmana Radhamadhava leapt into the water.[^21]

**56.** He swam a good distance in the strong current;
suddenly the chest came into his hand.

**57.** Diving, he brought up the box within the water;
not a single drop of water had touched the book.[^22]

**58.** Seeing that the book was not [there], Prabhu grew anxious;
Prabhu himself wants to leap into the water.

**59.** Hearing Prabhu's words, all the companions —
ten or twenty — quickly leapt into the water in the middle [of the river].

**60.** From afar Sri Radhamadhava called:
"Quickly bring the boat here, all of you!

**61.** Though I have found the book, I cannot swim [with it]."
At that moment the helmsman arrives with the boat.

**62.** Ten or twenty companions gathered there;
they lift the book from the water — it will not rise at all.

**63.** All the people who were sitting in the boat
seized [it] together [and] pulled.

**64.** However much strength each of them had,
they could not lift [it] — the book floats away.

**65.** Amazed, all say to Rasika:
"Hundreds upon hundreds of people set to lifting the book,

**66.** [yet] we cannot raise [it] from the water even by a finger's breadth;
the water roars in the wind, the boat is being carried away."[^23]

**67.** Hearing, Prabhu in distress says to the helmsman:
"Hold the boat in the current — where the book is floating."[^24]

**68.** Quickly the helmsman brought the boat beside the book;
Prabhu, coming close, saw the book floating in the water.

**69.** Stretching out his hand from the boat, he takes hold of the book;
quickly he lifted the book into the boat.

**70.** That which hundreds upon hundreds of people could not lift
rose into Prabhu's hand lighter than a flower.[^25]

**71.** Seeing [this], all the companions are amazed;
by Sri Krishna's mercy this was resolved.

**72.** The pastimes are a boundless sea: who can know [them]?
[I tell] the little that is revealed to me by Rasika's mercy.

**73.** May the learned sadhus not take to heart
any faults in the order [of the narrative], and may they listen with love.[^26]

**74.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Uttara-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the ninth lahari, called "The Description of the Sinking of the Boat in the Midst of the Vaitarani River and of the Rescue from It of the Devotees and the Book Srimad-Bhagavatam."*[^27]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the title of the lahari ("নবম-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 9 begins on pdf 193 (printed p. 149) below a rule after the colophon of lahari 8 (files RM-4-08-001-016, RM-4-08-017-021). Raga Shri (*śrī*; Purva, lahari 5, note 1); the ghosha is the same as in Purva, lahari 1, and Dakshina, lahari 1, and its translation is repeated. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and the ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 195 (printed p. 151), checked against the scan: "বৈতরণী-নদীমধ্যে নৌকাডুবি এবং তাহা হইতে ভক্তগণ ও শ্রীমদ্ভাগবত-গ্রন্থ-উদ্ধার-বর্ণন" (*baitaraṇī-nadīmadhye naukāḍubi ebaṃ tāhā haite bhaktagaṇa o śrīmadbhāgabata-grantha-uddhāra-barṇana*). Lahari 9 comprises 1–74 (pdf 193–195), all in this file.
[^2]: "The life of [his] beloved" — *śyāmānanda-ballabhajībana*: understood as "Shyamananda, the life of [his] beloved [disciple]," i.e. Rasika (cf. lahari 8, 1: "the life of Rasika"); "the life of the beloved [devotees]" is also possible. The second line recurs at the beginning of many laharis; "compose [his] glory" — *yaśaḥ … racana*.
[^3]: "Exalted bhagavatas" (2) — *parama bhāgabata*, great devotees. "From road to road" — *pathe pathe*. "Making the sound of Hari's name" (3) — *haridhbani kari'*; likewise in 4. The vina is a stringed instrument.
[^4]: "By the radiance of his glance" — *cāhani prakāśa*.
[^5]: "Devoted to Krishna alone" (9) — *ananya*. "The land of Utkala" (10) — *utkalabhubana*: Orissa. "Exclusive shelter" — *ananyaśaraṇa*: shelter in Krishna alone.
[^6]: Dhamnagar (*dhāmanagara*) is a place in Orissa (now Bhadrak district), on the way from Medinipur to Puri, north of Jajpur. "Palanquin" — *sukapāla* (so on the scan; in Purva, lahari 12 — *sukupāla*, note 6). From here Rasika goes on foot (12; cf. lahari 10, 3–4).
[^7]: Jajpur (*jājapura*; Yajapura) is an ancient town and tirtha on the Vaitarani river in Orissa. Vaitarani — *boitaraṇī* (so on the scan, cut-out ×2; an Oriya spelling; in the colophon *baitaraṇī*), a sacred river of Orissa. The Ashvamedha ghat (*aśbamedhaghāṭa*) is a ghat on the Vaitarani at Jajpur; according to tradition Brahma performed ashvamedha sacrifices there (the Dashashvamedha ghat). Varahanatha (*barāhanātha*) is the Deity of Lord Varaha at Jajpur, on the bank of the Vaitarani.
[^8]: "The river at Jajpur" — *jājapura nadī*: the Vaitarani (colophon). "Flood" — *banyā*. "Came up and stopped close by" (18) — *nikaṭe haila upasana*: *upasana* — "came near, arrived" (Uttara, lahari 4, note 3); likewise in 22, 50.
[^9]: "His close ones" — *ātmagaṇa*. "Ten or twenty" — *daśabiśa*: "a score or so." Mahantas (*mohānta*) are senior Vaishnavas, heads of communities. "The helmsman" (21, 23, 61, 67–68) — *kāṇḍārī*; "the boatmen" (53) — *kāṇḍāriẏā*. "From the force of the wind" (21) — *pabanera bhaẏe*, literally "from fear of the wind." "Pitches to and fro" — *ṭalamala kare*.
[^10]: "A bottomless sea" — *agādha samudra*: so the author calls the flooded river; likewise in 30, 34–35.
[^11]: "Remained inside the boat" (27) — apparently under the capsized boat: Rasika, lifting it (31), frees them. "The water became knee-deep" (30) — *eka hā̃ṭu*, literally "one knee"; in 34, 52 — "thigh-deep" (*eka uru*). "Standing on firm ground" (31) — *sthale*: on the riverbed, which had become firm ground. "Lifted [and] turned over" — *tuliẏā phelila*.
[^12]: "We stood" — *ubhā* (the editor glosses it "উভা—দণ্ডায়মান," "standing"; likewise in 50).
[^13]: "Rasika the avatara" — *rasika-abatāra*: so those who were saved glorify Rasika. In the Gaudiya Vaishnava understanding Rasikananda is not an avatara of the Lord but an eternal associate and dear devotee, a disciple of Shyamananda; *abatāra* here is in the broad sense of "one who has descended [into the world]" (Purva, lahari 6, note 11). On the author's view of Shyamananda and Rasika, see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11. "Carried across" — *pāra kaila*: both across the river and, by implication, across [the ocean of] Kali; cf. 44.
[^14]: "The breaker of fear" — *ātaṅkabhañjana*. "Whose vow is to be a fortress of refuge" — *śaraṇa-pañjara-bānā* (lahari 8, notes 2, 11).
[^15]: "The boat sinking" — *nāẏa ḍubā*: from the banks they saw the boat capsize. "They cannot be held back" — *nā yāẏa dharaṇa*.
[^16]: *lākhāni* — clear on the scan (cut-out ×2), the sense is unclear; understood tentatively from *lākh*, "a hundred thousand" (an exaggeration of the people on the bank); another meaning is possible. "For nothing" — *akāraṇe*. "Perished" — *marila*: so the people on the bank think; in fact no one perished (43, 52).
[^17]: "Travels" (43) — *bijaẏe*, said respectfully of a procession. "With ease" (44) — *hele*. "Rhinoceros" (45) — *gaṇḍā*; "flee" — *bhāga* (so on the scan, cut-out ×2): understood as *bhāg*, "flight"; the sense is approximate. "None comes near" — *nāhi āse lāga*. Verse 46 is understood as the continuation of the speech of "some" from 43.
[^18]: "Born from a part of Narayana" — *nārāẏaṇa-aṃśe janma*: the author's rapturous praise; see note 13 (Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11).
[^19]: "Whose vow is to deliver the fallen" (48) — *patitatāraṇa-bānā*. "Pashandis" — *pāṣaṇḍa*, unbelievers. "Sent out" (49) — the subject is "all [the sadhus]," apparently from the bank. "A bamboo pole of twenty cubits" (51) — *kuṛi hāta bā̃śa*: a bamboo pole for sounding the depth; "they find no bottom" — *nāi pāẏa sthala*.
[^20]: "The Sri Bhagavata" — the manuscript of the Srimad-Bhagavatam (in the colophon *śrīmadbhāgabata-grantha*) that Rasika was carrying with him; on Rasika's love for the Bhagavata, see Purva, lahari 8.
[^21]: The brahmana Radhamadhava (*dbija rādhāmādhaba*) is one of Rasika's companions; a Radhamadhava is also named in the list of Rasika's disciples (Pashchima, lahari 14, 147) — possibly the same person. The second line of 55 is apparently the answer to Prabhu's question: it was the brahmana Radhamadhava who leapt into the water [to search for the book].
[^22]: "The chest" (56) — *sinduka*; "the box" (57) — *peṛi*: the box in which the manuscript lay. "Brought up the box within the water" — *ḍubiẏā ānila peṛi jalera bhitara*: understood to mean that he raised the box from the depths but could not get out with it (60–61). "Seeing that the book was not [there]" (58) — in the boat.
[^23]: "It will not rise at all" (62) — *nā uṭhe sarbbathā*. "Seized [it] … [and] pulled" (63) — *ākarṣiẏā … dharila*. "Set to" (65) — *lāge*. "Even by a finger's breadth" (66) — *aṅguṣṭheka*, literally "by one thumb['s breadth]." "The boat is being carried away" — *palāila tarī*, literally "the boat ran away."
[^24]: "In the current" — *khare*: the editor glosses it "খর—স্রোত," "current." The line can also be understood as "hold the boat [against] the current where the book is floating."
[^25]: "Lighter than a flower" — *phula haite uśbāsa*: the editor glosses it "উশ্বাস—হালকা," "light."
[^26]: "This was resolved" (71) — *tāhā haila samādhāna*. "Is revealed" (72) — *sphure*. "Faults in the order [of the narrative]" (73) — *anukrama-doṣa*: the author asks not to be faulted if events are not told in sequence. "The learned sadhus" — *supaṇḍita sādhujana*.
[^27]: The colophon from the scan (pdf 195). After the colophon, lahari 10 begins on the same page (files RM-4-10-001-004).
