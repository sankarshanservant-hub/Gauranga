# Uttara-vibhaga. Lahari 13. The Ending of the Drought in Shekharabhumi

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Rasika comes to Shekharabhumi, and the king, honouring him as a second Narayana, begs for help: for three years there has been no rain, and for want of water the subjects are leaving for other villages; Rasika bids him hold a great festival and gather the sadhus; during the sankirtana and the feasting of the sadhus he commands King Indra to send rain, and clouds cover the sky over Shekhara: a downpour fills the ponds and reservoirs, and in the month of Magha the rivers flood; the king recognizes Rasika as a second Narayana; the author glorifies Rasika, who never stays at home even for a moment but travels to deliver living beings, weeps with love for Krishna, speaks of Krishna to the pandits and spreads sankirtana and prema-bhakti in every land of Utkala*

*The thirteenth lahari.*

*Raga Varadi.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "O treasury of mercy, O compassionate Shyama!*
*Turn Your attention to the fallen, afflicted people."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the ocean of mercy,
whose beloved follower is Rasikendra-chandra!

**2.** From there the Prabhu went to Shekharabhumi;
arriving, the Prabhu stayed at the king's palace.[^2]

**3.** The king with his whole clan rejoiced;
he honoured Rasika as a second Narayana.[^3]

**4.** Falling at [his] feet, the king makes a plea:
"For three years no rain has fallen here.

**5.** What offence has been committed against the sadhus?
What have the guru, Krishna [and] the sadhus resolved [against us]?

**6.** That is why no rain falls in this village;
for want of water the king [and] subjects are exhausted.

**7.** Great dread has seized all the subjects;
for want of water all are leaving [the village] for other villages.[^4]

**8.** This is [my] plea, Prabhu, at your feet:
command King Indra [that] he send rain."[^5]

**9.** Hearing the king's words, Rasikendra says:
"Hold a great festival, gather the sadhus in crowds.

**10.** When the festival begins, rain will come."
Receiving the command, [the king] began the supreme sacrifice.[^6]

**11.** Day and night they began the sankirtana;
with care they honoured the guru, Krishna, the sadhus [and] the brahmanas.

**12.** The sadhus partake of food of six tastes;
on every side all remember Krishna.[^7]

**13.** Rasikendra commanded King Indra:
"Send abundant rain on all four sides of the village."

**14.** By [that] command, suddenly the clouds,
encircling the bounds of Shekhara, covered the sky.

**15.** A terrible downpour fell [through all] the fourth prahara;
ponds, reservoirs [and] embankment tanks quickly filled.

**16.** In the month of Magha the rivers, canals and other [waters] flooded;
the clouds, vying with one another, sent much rain.[^8]

**17.** Such is the command of Rasikendra-chandra:
gods, asuras, men [and] beasts carry [it] out with all their might.

**18.** All were amazed, seeing [this] manifestation;
all Rasika's servants rejoiced.[^9]

**19.** The king honoured [his] feet in many ways;
he knew Rasikendra to be a second Narayana:

**20.** "To deliver living beings the Prabhu has taken birth;
by great fortune I have seen this treasure — [his] feet."[^10]

**21.** Thus Rasikendra in every land, land after land,
in [all] four directions made devotion to Krishna manifest.

**22.** Not for a moment does he stay in his own house;
for the deliverance of living beings he wanders.

**23.** In every land he spread sankirtana;
granting prema-bhakti for Krishna, he delivered [people].

**24.** Rasikendra constantly weeps with love for Krishna;
in hundreds of streams [tears] flow from Rasika's eyes.

**25.** Over his whole body — pulaka, his speech is always faltering;
the people of the three worlds are enchanted, seeing [his] form.

**26.** The eight sattvika-bhavas are always manifest;
in love for Krishna he constantly delights in [its] play.[^11]

**27.** In whatever direction the Prabhu goes, all [go] in that direction;
all the dharmas always move with Rasika.

**28.** Rasikendra speaks of Krishna to all people;
surrounding [him] on every side, crowds of pandits listen.

**29.** Hearing the speech of [his] holy mouth, all are overwhelmed;
no one is able to answer [him].

**30.** Hearing the words of the holy mouth, like nectar —
hearing that sweet speech, [all] find solace of heart.[^12]

**31.** Weeping and weeping, he tells of Krishna;
hearing [it], the pandits roll on the ground.

**32.** He delivered the three worlds, granting prema-bhakti;
in love for Krishna he always wanders, roaming [everywhere].

**33.** To whatever land the Prabhu Rasika-shekhara comes,
all [there] gain countless treasures, intoxicated with joy.

**34.** No one lets [him] go, [all] hold [him] back with care;
when the Prabhu leaves that place, all are dying with weeping.

**35.** Thus all the lands constantly think
of Rasika's feet, in remembrance [and] meditation.

**36.** Thus he delivered the earth —
the son of Achyuta, [possessed] of boundless beauty [and] virtues.[^13]

**37.** He spread an unheard-of devotion to Krishna;
for all, Krishna alone [became] the refuge.

**38.** All people became filled with prema-bhakti for Krishna;
the evil desires of all people were destroyed.[^14]

**39.** Glory, glory to Rasika-shekhara, the jewel of virtues,
by the touch of whose feet the earth rejoices!

**40.** He performed many pastimes in the land of Utkala —
they cannot be described, even if one describes with a hundred mouths.

**41.** By his grace I have told a little;
all listen to the "Rasika-mangala" with attention.

**42.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Uttara-vibhaga of the "Sri Rasika-mangala" ends the thirteenth lahari, called "The Ending of the Drought in Shekharabhumi."*[^15]

[^2]: "Rasikendra-chandra" (1) — "the moon of Rasikendras," Rasika. "Beloved follower" — *priẏa anucara*. "From there" (2) — from Nagpur (lahari 12, 64). Shekharabhumi (*śekharabhūmi*; in 14, "Shekhara") — apparently Shikharbhum, the domain of the Shikhara rajas (Panchakot, in the present Purulia district, West Bengal); the identification is tentative, and the king is not named. "Stayed" — *utarilā*; "at the palace" — *bāṛīte*, "at the house."
[^3]: "As a second Narayana" (3) — *dbitī nārāẏaṇa sama*; likewise in 19; in 20 the king says: "To deliver living beings the Prabhu has taken birth." This is rapturous praise: according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching Rasika is a devotee, a disciple of Shyamananda, not an incarnation (lahari 10, note 14; lahari 12, notes 8, 14); on the author's attitude to Shyamananda and Rasika — Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11. "With his whole clan" — *sagoṣṭhī sahite*.
[^4]: "Three years" (4) — *tina sambatsara*. "Against the sadhus" (5) — *sādhujana-sthāne*: the king thinks the drought is the result of an offence against sadhus. "Resolved" — *kibā karilena mane*, literally "what have [they] set in mind." "Exhausted" (6) — *bikala*. "Leaving [the village]" (7) — *chāṛi'* (so on the scan; the OCR is garbled).
[^5]: "King Indra" (8, 13) — *indrarāja*: Indra, the king of heaven, who commands the rain. Indra and the other demigods are servants of the Lord; that Indra carries out Rasika's command (13–17; cf. lahari 11, 74) is the author's way of glorifying the power of a devotee (note 3; lahari 12, note 8). "Send rain" — *kare bariṣaṇe*.
[^6]: "In crowds" (9) — *bṛnda bṛnda*. "The supreme sacrifice" (10) — *parameṣṭi* (so on the scan, crop ×2): understood as *parama iṣṭi*, "supreme sacrifice (worship)," i.e. the festival itself (11–12); *parameṣṭhī* is also a name of Brahma, which does not fit here. The reading and sense are unclear. The subject is not named — apparently the king.
[^7]: "Food of six tastes" (12) — *ṣaṛarasa* (cf. lahari 12, 57). "Partake" — *bhojana kariẏā* (so on the scan; the OCR is garbled). "Remember" — *saṅaraṇa*.
[^8]: "Suddenly" (14) — *ācambite*. "The bounds of Shekhara" — *śekharera sīmā* (note 2). "The fourth prahara" (15) — *caturtha prahara*: a prahara is about three hours; possibly "[all] four praharas," i.e. a whole day. "Reservoirs" — *taṛāga*; "embankment tanks" — *bānda* (*bā̃dh*, a pond or reservoir with an embankment). Magha (16) — January–February, the dry season. "Canals" — *khāla*. "Vying with one another" — *bādābādi* (lahari 11, note 3).
[^9]: "With all their might" (17) — *prāṇapaṇe*. "Manifestation" (18) — *parakāśa*.
[^10]: "In many ways" (19) — *bahurūpe*. "This treasure — [his] feet" (20) — *e caraṇadhana* (so on the scan; the OCR is garbled). On "has taken birth" — note 3.
[^11]: "Not for a moment" (22) — *eka tila*, literally "not for a sesame seed." "In his own house" — *āpana mandire*. Pulaka (25) — the hairs standing on end in ecstasy. "Faltering speech" — *gada gada bāṇī*. The eight sattvika-bhavas (26) — lahari 6, note 26. "Delights in [its] play" — *karena bilāsa*.
[^12]: "All the dharmas" (27) — *sarbba dharma*: understood as "all virtues [and] righteousness." "Overwhelmed" (29) — *jara jara* (lahari 10, note 12). "Find solace of heart" (30) — *juṛāẏa parāṇa*, literally "the life is cooled (soothed)."
[^13]: "Roll on the ground" (31) — *loṭāẏa dharaṇī*. "Countless treasures" (33) — *koṭī nidhi*, literally "ten million treasures." "With care" (34) — *yatna kari'* (so on the scan; the OCR is garbled). "Are dying with weeping" — *mare sabe jhuri*: figuratively, they pine with grief. "Think" (35) — *bhābe*; "in remembrance [and] meditation" — *smaraṇe dhiẏāne*. "The son of Achyuta" (36) — *acyuta-kumāra*, Rasika.
[^14]: "Unheard-of" (37) — *asambhaba*, literally "impossible." "Krishna alone [became] the refuge" — *ananyaśaraṇa kṛṣṇe*. "Evil desires" (38) — *durbbāsanā*.
[^15]: The colophon is on pdf 204 (printed p. 160), checked against the scan (note 1). Lahari 13 ends on pdf 204 (॥42॥ — the author's signature); on the same page, below a rule, lahari 14 begins (files RM-4-14-001-014).
