# Pashchima-vibhaga. Lahari 14. The Description of Shyamananda's Branches

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; in separation from Shyamananda, Rasika gathers the devotees of Krishna to hold the twelfth-day festival and appoints the first aradhana in Govindapur; he tells the brothers to invite all the Shyamanandis, to decide who will be adhikari of the community and of Sri Govinda's service, and to name to him all of Shyamananda's disciples; the devotees, praising Rasika's three sons and daughter, choose his eldest son, Radhananda, full of prema for Krishna; Rasika embraces him, ties a cloth on his head and entrusts to him the service of Sri Govinda; the brothers say the Shyamanandis are countless as the waves of the ocean; the author lists Rasika's disciples — brahmanas, kings, renunciants — among them Ramakrishna, who crossed a river on a crocodile, Gokula Dasa, who manifested bhakti in Kendujhar, and Govinda Bhattacharya, who preached in Bengal, — and says they cannot be counted*

*The fourteenth lahari.*

*Raga Kaushika.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Glory, O glory to Rama and Krishna!*
*O Murari, O Murari, O Murari!"*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the purifier of the fallen,
the own dear treasure of the life of Rasika-chandra![^2]

**2.** In separation from Shyamananda, Rasika-shekhara
is constantly absorbed in ecstatic feeling; [his] body is overwhelmed.[^3]

**3.** Taking all the devotees of Krishna, he sat to deliberate
on holding the twelfth-day festival.[^4]

**4.** Govindapur was fixed as the place [of the festival]:
there — the first aradhana, the great festival.[^5]

**5.** "This great festival [should be] known to the world;
it is fitting to bring all the communities of Shyamanandis."[^6]

**6.** Rasikendra says: "I know nothing —
[my] meditation and remembrance are Shyamananda's feet.

**7.** Mad with prema for Krishna, I have spent all this time;
who lives where — I do not know them."[^7]

**8.** Rasika gave the command to all the brothers:
"Invite all the Shyamanandis, every one.[^8]

**9.** Who will be the adhikari in the Shyamanandi community?
Whom will you make [it] — deliberate on this.

**10.** The service of Sri Govinda and the great festival —
understand that this is [a duty we have] accepted, and consider [it].[^9]

**11.** For I shall wander, carrying out Prabhu's work;
[arrange it] so that the service here goes on in due order.[^10]

**12.** Tell me the names of Shyamananda's disciples,
the disciples of [their] disciples, the servant-disciples — all who are in Utkala.[^11]

**13.** Kings, subjects, mahantas, all the renunciants —
who is whose disciple — distinguish them all one by one.[^12]

**14.** In what family each was born, where each lives —
having ascertained [this], tell of the Shyamanandi servants.[^13]

**15.** What love each has for Krishna,
who seeks no other refuge, who has prema-bhakti;

**16.** who has affection for the company of sadhus, in whose heart is kindness,
who is firmly [devoted] to the guru — tell in detail.[^14]

**17.** Describe them to me one by one;
entrust to me the Shyamanandi community.

**18.** I shall write to all, as befits [each];
all those people will come to the great festival."[^15]

**19.** All said: "Prabhu, you are detached;
you have appeared in order to give prema to the destitute.[^16]

**20.** Appoint one adhikari equal to yourself;
entrust to him the service of Sri Govinda."[^17]

**21.** Then Rasikendra gave the command to all;
Rasika, [gathering everyone] in a circle, sat to deliberate:

**22.** "The one on whom all agree — he shall be [chosen];
quickly deliberate, come to a decision [and] bring [him]."[^18]

**23.** All the devotees of Krishna, gathering, deliberated:
Rasika has three sons, supremely magnanimous.

**24.** These three brothers, born of one mother, enchant the world;
by their glance the three worlds were delivered.[^19]

**25.** And he has one daughter, supremely steadfast,
an abode that has taken refuge in prema for Krishna, skilled in rasa.[^20]

**26.** All these names and [the account of their] glory
I proclaim only to purify myself.[^21]

**27.** The eldest son is Radhananda, great in wisdom;
Krishnagati — [his] mind and speech are incomparable;

**28.** [the third] is named Radhakrishna Dasa, an abode of prema for Krishna;
the daughter is named Vrindavati — she whose life is Krishna.[^22]

**29.** Now I shall tell the boundless glory of Radhananda —
of him to whom this authority is fitting.

**30.** All understand only a trace of Radhananda's glory:
unceasingly [they] see his striving towards Krishna.[^23]

**31.** On Krishna is [his] attachment, on Krishna his mind, in Krishna his abiding;
within and without, Krishna dwells in him.

**32.** When he falls asleep, he plays with Krishna;
when he wakes, in separation he weeps.

**33.** Weeping and weeping, he sees the form of Radha and Krishna;
immersed, he plunges into a well of bliss.[^24]

**34.** At times he smiles softly, ever so slightly;
he sinks and rises in the ocean of the nectar of prema for Krishna.

**35.** Like a blazing fire in the midst of the forest of worldly existence [is he];
seeing [him], the hordes of pashandis are consumed by burning.[^25]

**36.** Hunger, thirst and [the like] do not touch [his] body;
his mind, maddened with prema for Krishna, is overflowing with love.[^26]

**37.** He always has the strength to give Krishna and to take Krishna;
reassuring the destitute, he bestows prema.

**38.** Feeding [a person] the fruit of prema, he fills his belly;
his grief, pain [and] burning [suffering] he reduces to ashes.

**39.** Having eaten the fruit of prema, that one, maddened,
dances, sings, weeps, crying, "Krishna! Ha, ha, ha!"[^27]

**40.** All those devotees of Krishna, seeing all this,
firmly decided: he is the worthy one.

**41.** All went and said before Rasikendra:
"Worthy to be adhikari is Radhananda, the greatly fortunate."

**42.** They told of all his virtues;
hearing this, Prabhu was pleased, his eyes filled with tears.

**43.** Prabhu said to all: "Call [him] and bring [him]."
All went to bring him, rejoicing.

**44.** They told him everything in his presence;
hearing it, he was delighted, joyful at heart.[^28]

**45.** At the command [they] rose and quickly set out;
all the devotees of Krishna came before Prabhu.[^29]

**46.** Seeing Prabhu, [Radhananda] began to prostrate himself;
saying, "Rise, rise," Prabhu took [him] into his embrace.

**47.** Embracing [him] again, Rasikendra-raya
began to speak: "Dear one, listen quickly.

**48.** Before, you had Shyamananda's command;
now all the devotees of Krishna have surrounded you.[^30]

**49.** I too [give] you, dear one, this authority:
govern Shyamananda's community."[^31]

**50.** Prostrating himself again, he fell to the ground;
the devotees of Krishna tell Rasika to give [him] the cloth.

**51.** With everyone's consent Rasikendra-raya
quickly tied the cloth on Radhananda's head.[^32]

**52.** Having tied the cloth, Rasikendra walked around [him];
with prema in his heart Prabhu says, "Hari, Hari!"

**53.** He entrusted the service of Sri Govinda to Radhananda;
earnestly he told [him] to preach prema-bhakti.[^33]

**54.** Then Rasikendra again, to everyone,
spoke sweet words with love:

**55.** "All of you, devotees of Krishna, are supremely magnanimous;
you are able to give bhakti for Krishna to all and to receive [it].[^34]

**56.** Count the names of the Shyamanandi Vaishnavas
and bring [them to me], dear ones, with care."

**57.** Hearing Rasika's command, the brothers say:
"The communities of the Shyamanandis cannot be told.[^35]

**58.** The Shyamanandis are like the waves of the ocean;
the devotees of Krishna, the Shyamanandis, cannot be counted.

**59.** We shall name the chief ones in [your] presence;
all together will tell what they know, as is fitting."

**60.** Before, I described the disciples of Shyamananda;
now I shall tell of his disciples, servants [and their] disciples.

**61.** Briefly I shall relate something about them;
all you Shyamanandis, devotees of Krishna, listen attentively.[^36]

**62.** Motionless, Prabhu, the son of Achyuta, listens;
with great joy all the devotees of Krishna listen.

**63.** At the beginning of the book I offered all the obeisances:
all the disciples of Shyamananda I [named] one by one.

**64.** Now the brothers earnestly tell of the disciples;
the son of Achyuta listens [to them] with undivided mind.[^37]

**65.** I shall tell in detail of Rasika's worthy disciples,
praising the firmness of each one's bhakti, such as it is.

**66.** The first disciple is the brahmana Kalandi Bhaktadasa,
whose true abode is Rasika's feet.[^38]

**67.** Sri Shyamagopala Dasa, very pure of mind,
for whom Rasika-shekhara is lineage, character and caste.

**68.** He is the son of Kashinatha, renowned in the world,
very eloquent, intelligent, one who says what is fitting.

**69.** His mother, Govindadasi, is Rasika's servant;
by Rasika's mercy [she] is renowned [for knowing] the truth about the Vaishnavas.[^39]

**70.** In a village called Sri Jangha, a truly divine place,
lived a fortunate [man] named Ramadasa.

**71.** His wife, named Draupadi, was devoted to her husband;
she was born in the clan of the shishta-karanas, renowned [to all].[^40]

**72.** From her womb was born Dinashyama Dasa;
from childhood Rasika was manifest in his heart.[^41]

**73.** The disciple of Rasika, an embodiment of prema:
whatever Rasika commands, he unfailingly does.

**74.** Night and day he always meditates on Rasikendra;
at Rasika's feet he offered [his] caste [and] life.

**75.** He made many disciples in the land of Utkala;
all the elders honour [him] as equal to a guru.

**76.** Dinashyama Dasa is very dear to the Vaishnavas;
he always delights in the pastimes of prema for Krishna.

**77.** He brought much joy upon the earth;
giving prema-bhakti to all, he delivered [them].

**78.** The glory of Dinashyama cannot be told —
of him to whom [Rasika] entrusted his own prema-bhakti.

**79.** The brahmana Ramakrishna Dasa, very pure of mind,
who has no other goal than Rasikendra,

**80.** made many disciples in the land of Utkala;
all people sing of him as the blessing of the world.

**81.** For sport he sits on the backs of tigers and crocodiles;
by Rasika's mercy he fears no one.

**82.** Mounting a crocodile, he crosses the river —
Ramakrishna Mahashaya, the deliverer of the fallen.[^42]

**83.** Rasika's disciple is the renowned Narayana Dasa;
pure of heart, he knows no one but Krishna.

**84.** The brahmana Paramananda, very pure of heart,
by Rasika's mercy became a great scholar.

**85.** In a brahmana family was born Goura-gopala;
he knows nothing but Rasikendra.[^43]

**86.** The brahmana Gopinatha, a renunciant mahashaya,
in whose heart Rasikendra [abides] unceasingly,

**87.** knows nothing but prema-bhakti for Krishna;
all his time was spent with Rasika.

**88.** Krishna's meals, offerings of six tastes,
Gopinatha, pure in conduct, cooks.[^44]

**89.** Prema-ankura Dasa is Rasika's servant;
among his servants and [his] servants' servants the kadamba blossomed.[^45]

**90.** Rasika's disciple from childhood, Sri Gokula Dasa,
manifested bhakti in the land of Kendujhar.

**91.** In the forest land the mahashaya made many disciples;
they know nothing but Rasikendra.[^46]

**92.** Shyama-manohara Dasa, very pure of mind,
who has no other goal than Rasika's feet —

**93.** earlier I made his greatness known;
a great scholar, he delivered all people.[^47]

**94.** Maharaja Vaidyanatha is a great mahajana;
with body, mind and words he firmly takes refuge in Rasika.

**95.** He left his body in the land of Utkala;
in Vrindavana he saw all the sadhus.[^48]

**96.** Chhota-raya [and] Rautra are very pure of mind;
they have no other goal than Rasikendra.

**97.** Both are very mighty, embodiments of prema;
seeing their deeds, all attained bhakti.[^49]

**98.** Shyamadasa [and] Mohana are Prabhu's own servants;
with the songs of Jayadeva they enchant everyone.[^50]

**99.** The brahmana Govinda Bhattacharya Mahashaya,
in whose heart Rasikendra-chandra always [abides],

**100.** preached bhakti for Hari in Vanga;
hundreds and hundreds of brahmanas became his disciples.[^51]

**101.** Rasika's disciple is the fortunate brahmana Dube:
he knows nothing other than Rasikendra-chandra.

**102.** Tarkalankara Bhattacharya Shyamasundara —
he to whom Rasika-shekhara gave prema-bhakti.

**103.** Rasika's disciple is the brahmana Sundara-raya,
a mahashaya, prema-bhakti for Krishna embodied.[^52]

**104.** The best of brahmanas, the renunciant Sri Mohana Dasa,
from birth lived together with Rasika.

**105.** Leaving house, wife, sons, all his family,
the son of Rama went around [the whole] earth.[^53]

**106.** Rasika's servant is Mangaraja Harichandana;
Gopinatha Dasa Pattanayaka is a mahajana.[^54]

**107.** Radhavinoda Dasa, Kalandi Bhagavan,
Paramananda, Manohara, Kanu, Krishnanama,

**108.** Krishnacharana, the brahmana Achyuta, Shricharana,
Gokulananda, Govinda — those who took refuge in Rasika.[^55]

**109.** The brahmana Govinda Dasa, Rasika's servant —
with prema for Krishna, night and day his body is overwhelmed.

**110.** Rasika's disciple is Kalandi, the best of brahmanas,
whose true home is Rasika's feet.

**111.** Akrura, Gopala, Hari, Sri Tulasi-dasi,
Raja Mitra, Chitrasena, Suvarna Vayasi.[^56]

**112.** The brahmana Govinda Dasa, Krishnabhakta Dasa,
Vrajamohana, the brahmana Shyamamohana Dasa.

**113.** Sri Gopala Acharya, Kalandi Dharambara,
in whose heart unceasingly is the best one, Rasikendra;

**114.** his sons — Radhamohana, Bhudhara,
Sri Radhagovinda, Radhakrishna, the best of brahmanas —

**115.** with all their family became Rasika's disciples;
they know nothing but Rasikendra.[^57]

**116.** Great in steadfastness, an embodiment of prema, Sri Govinda Dasa,
a disciple of Rasika, lives in Ghantashila.

**117.** He made many disciples in the land of Bhanjabhum;
he especially flooded [it] with prema for Krishna.[^58]

**118.** Rasika's disciple is Gangadasa Mahashaya,
an embodiment of prema, the son of Shridhara;

**119.** Daityari, Shyamasundara, Shyamamohana,
Shyamadasa, Bhagavan — six brothers.

**120.** All [of them] are Rasika's nephews, his servants;
for them Rasikendra-raya is caste, life [and] wealth.[^59]

**121.** Vrindavana-kishora is Rasika's servant;
together with [his] community he spoke [of] Krishna-tattva.[^60]

**122.** Chintamani [and] Vihari are very fortunate:
for them Rasikendra-chudamani is caste, wealth [and] life.[^61]

**123.** Achyuta, Shyamakishora, Vrindavana Dasa,
Shrirama, Bali, Vamana, Jagu, Shyamadasa.[^62]

**124.** All Rasika's disciples seek no other refuge;
unceasingly [they] serve the guru, Krishna and the sadhus.

**125.** Ghanashyama Dasa, Sri Beraya Vishnudasa,
in whose hearts Rasika's feet are manifest.[^63]

**126.** Bahubalindra, Sri Shyama, Rasika Dasa,
Chandra, Bhanu and others are Rasika's own servants.[^64]

**127.** The brahmana Gopimohana, Shyamamohana Dasa,
in whose hearts Rasika's feet dwell.

**128.** Vrajamohana, the renunciant Shyamarasika,
Sakhishyama, Gokula — very experienced.[^65]

**129.** In the land of Hijli is Vaikuntha Dasa Mahashaya,
in whose heart is Rasikendra-chudamani.

**130.** Unceasingly he serves hundreds and hundreds of sadhus;
selling himself, [he] is firm in the service of the sadhus.[^66]

**131.** Lala, Purushottama, Shyamakishora, Yugala,
Akrura, Shyamasundara, Vamshi, Manohara.[^67]

**132.** Sadashiva Pattanayaka, the sadhu Uddanda,
Krishnananda Harichandana — very formidable.[^68]

**133.** The brahmana Jivadasa Bhunya, Raghunatha Dasa,
Krishnadasa Bhunya and others are Rasika's servants.[^69]

**134.** Gajendra, Mathuradasa are very pure of mind;
they have no other goal than Rasikendra.

**135.** Madhusudana, Dvarakananda Mahashaya,
in whose heart Rasikendra [abides] unceasingly.

**136.** Raja Ramachandra, Chitreshvara, Sri Chandana —
all with body, mind and words take refuge in Rasika.

**137.** Madho, Manohara, Niranjana Mahashaya;
Uddhava, Hari, Keshava — the sons of Bhima.[^70]

**138.** Shyamasundara, Vrindavana — the sons of Vamshi;
all with their whole being take refuge in Rasika.[^71]

**139.** The brahmana Radhavallabha, the son of Purushottama;
princes are Rasika's disciples, crowd upon crowd.

**140.** The two — Radhavallabha [and] Shyamadasa:
in Gahamagarh [they have] hundreds of thousands of disciples.[^72]

**141.** The brahmana Shyamasundara is a very great mahajana:
he cooks the bhoga for Krishna at Rasika's.[^73]

**142.** The brahmana Radhamohana, Uddhava, Bhagavan,
Nilambara, Vanamali, Ramadasa, Shyama.

**143.** Krishnananda Bhunya is very pure of mind:
for him Rasika's feet are lineage, character and caste.

**144.** Gopala Bhunya, Krishnananda Harichandana,
Gopala, Madhava, Raja Keshobanai.[^74]

**145.** The brahmana Sri Radhamohana is a great scholar,
Rasika's disciple, full of prema, with an intoxicated heart.

**146.** Radhavallabha Dasa, the son of Vallabha,
is Rasika's servant, and also Vrindavana Dasa.[^75]

**147.** Radhamadhava, Shyamasundara, Anuraga;
Rasika's disciple is Vanamali, the greatly fortunate.

**148.** Mukunda, Paramananda, Kanu, Bhagavan,
Agata, Mohana and others — servants, as is attested.[^76]

**149.** Gangadasa, Keshava and others, Sri Chandrashekhara,
Shyamasundara, Vrajamohana — the best of brahmanas.

**150.** Radhamohana, Bhaktadasa, Purushottama,
Shyamadasa who lives under a tree, Vrajamohana.[^77]

**151.** Akrura, Mohanananda, Nandana, Balaka,
Manohara, Sri Radhavinoda — manifest before the eyes.[^78]

**152.** Krishnananda, Krishna-jivana, Bhudhara,
Sri Gopala Dasa, Radhacharana, Akrura.

**153.** Mohana, Vrajamohana, Shyamadasa and others,
Yadava, Shyamamohana — greatly renowned.

**154.** Sri Vinoda Dasa, Chintamani Dasa — famous,
expert in music, very, very [skilled in] poetry.[^79]

**155.** The brahmana Sri Murali Dasa, the brahmana Sri Gopala;
disciples of Rasika — Dvijadasa, Sri Dayala.[^80]

**156.** Shyamadasa, Harinarayana Mahashaya,
in whose heart is Rasikendra-chudamani.

**157.** The brahmana Gopimohana Dasa, Shyamamohana,
the brahmana Yadunatha — people dear to Rasika.

**158.** Nilashyama Dasa, Gopimohana, Akrura,
Ghanashyama, Ramadasa, Govinda — a brahmana.[^81]

**159.** Jayadeva Dasa, Laichhana, Krishnadasa,
Sri Viravara Deu, Shankara, Kanudasa.[^82]

**160.** Sri Govinda Bhanja, Jagannatha, Krishnadasa,
Shyama Bhanja, Telai, Shripati, Ramadasa.

**161.** Mithi Bhanja with [his whole] community, Gopala Bhanja-raya,
Shyamadasa, Kishora and Madha-raya.[^83]

**162.** Radhakrishna Dasa is Rasika's own servant.
[Among] women — throng upon throng of disciples, beyond measure.[^84]

**163.** Rasika's servants are [like] the waves of the ocean:
who has such power as to count [them]?

**164.** I have considered ten or twenty principal ones
[and] briefly named a few — those whose names I know.

**165.** Behind each name are hundreds and hundreds of disciples;
there is no counting all of Rasika's disciples.[^85]

**166.** Now I shall tell briefly of the brothers' disciples —
the disciples, the disciples' disciples, the servant-disciples — one by one.[^86]

**167.** In the Pashchima-vibhaga I have proclaimed this —
whatever the son of Achyuta makes me speak.[^87]

**168.** Rasika's disciples seek no other refuge;
taking refuge in them, one obtains the treasure of prema for Krishna.[^88]

**169.** I have made the "Rasika-mangala" known a little;
listen, all, attentively, filled with joy.

**170.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Pashchima-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the fourteenth lahari, called "The Description of Shyamananda's Branches."*[^89]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the title of the lahari ("চতুর্দ্দশ-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 14 begins on pdf 167 (printed p. 123), below a rule after the colophon of lahari 13 (files RM-3-13-060-075). Raga Kaushika (*kauśika*; Purva, lahari 3, note 1). The ghosha — *jaẏa re jaẏa rāmakṛṣṇa murāre, o murāre o murāre* — is the same as in Purva, lahari 3: Rama and Krishna are Balarama and Krishna; Murari ("the enemy of Mura") is a name of Krishna which the author also applies to Rasika-Murari. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 172 (printed p. 128), checked against the scan: "ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-পশ্চিম-বিভাগে শ্যামানন্দ-শাখাবর্ণন-নাম চতুর্দ্দশ-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা" (*śyāmānanda-śākhā-barṇana*) — "The Description of Shyamananda's Branches": the "branches" are disciples and their disciples (cf. the branches of the Chaitanya tree, CC Adi 10–12). Lahari 14 comprises 1–170 (pdf 167–172). The summary line covers the whole lahari (45–170 from the OCR of pdf 169–172, provisionally).
[^2]: "The purifier of the fallen" — *patita-pābana*. "The own dear treasure of the life" — *nija priẏa prāṇadhana*: Shyamananda is Rasika's guru. On the author's attitude to Shyamananda and Rasika, see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11.
[^3]: "Absorbed in ecstatic feeling" — *bhābābeśe*. "Overwhelmed" — *jara jara* (Pashchima, lahari 12, note 16).
[^4]: "The twelfth-day festival" — *duẏādaśa mahotsaba* (*duẏādaśa* = *dbādaśa*): apparently a festival in memory of the departed held on the twelfth day after his departure; it may also be understood as "twelve festivals." "Devotees of Krishna" — *kārṣṇajana* (Purva, lahari 10, note 13).
[^5]: Govindapur — cf. Sri Govindapur (lahari 13, 15, 19; note 8). "Was fixed" — *karila niścaẏa*; the subject is not expressed (Rasika with the devotees). "The first aradhana" — *ādya ārādhanā*: apparently the first festival in Shyamananda's memory (on the aradhana festival, lahari 13, note 8). 5 is taken as Rasika's words (cf. 6).
[^6]: "It is fitting to bring" — *ānibā ucita*. "Communities" — *goṣṭhī* (Pashchima, lahari 12, note 20).
[^7]: "Meditation and remembrance" — *dhyāna saṅaraṇa* (*saṅaraṇa* = *smaraṇa*). "I have spent all this time" — *gela eta kāle*, "so much time has passed": absorbed in prema, Rasika does not know all of Shyamananda's disciples or where they live.
[^8]: "The brothers" — *bhāigaṇe*: apparently godbrothers, disciples of Shyamananda. "Every one" — *sarbajane*.
[^9]: "Adhikari" (9) — *adhikārī*: a successor, the head of the community, to whom the service of the Deity is entrusted (cf. 20, 29). 10b — *ihā aṅgīkṛta bujhi kara anubhaba*: the sense is approximate.
[^10]: "Carrying out Prabhu's work" — *prabhukārya anbeṣiẏā*, literally "seeking out Prabhu's work": Shyamananda's mission. "In due order" — *subandhāna haña*, "being well arranged."
[^11]: "The disciples of [their] disciples, the servant-disciples" — *anuśiṣya bhṛtyaśiṣya* (Pashchima, lahari 12, note 17).
[^12]: "Mahantas" — *mohānta*, abbots, teachers. "Renunciants" — *udāsīna*, detached wanderers. "Distinguish one by one" — *kara bhinna bhinna*.
[^13]: "Tell of the Shyamanandi servants" — *kaha śyāmānandī dāsa*; it may also be understood as an address: "tell, O Shyamanandi servants."
[^14]: "Love" (15) — *suprīti*. "Seeks no other refuge" — *ananya-śaraṇa*. "Firmly [devoted] to the guru" — *gurute niścita*.
[^15]: "Entrust to me the community" (17) — *goṣṭhī more kara samarpaṇa*: apparently "hand over to me [the list of] the community." "I shall write" — *kariba lekhana*: apparently he will send letters of invitation.
[^16]: "Detached" — *udāsa*: Rasika does not cling to the world (cf. his wish to wander, 11). "You have appeared" — *haẏecha prakāśa*: the disciples speak of Rasika as one who has appeared in the world for the sake of mercy; on the author's attitude to Rasika, see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11. "The destitute" — *akiñcane*, "those who have nothing."
