# Pashchima-vibhaga. Lahari 4. The Description of the Rasa-yatra

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; at Sri Gopivallabhapur, as in a second Vrindavana, Rasika continues the Rasa-yatra, and the boys, in the order of the Bhagavata, represent how Krishna hides from the proud gopis, taking one gopi away with Him, how the gopis search for Him through the forests, questioning the trees, in their absorption enact His pastimes, find the chosen one whom He has left, and all together sing of Him; all say Rasika was born of a portion of Narayana; when Rasika comes from his house to the place of the rasa, a cobra bites him on the foot; he tells no one anything and all night watches the rasa in bliss, the snake perishes in the crowd, trampled to dust, and in the morning two fangs are taken out of his foot, and the poison vanishes without any charms; the author glorifies Rasika and promises prema-bhakti to the hearers of the Rasika-mangala*

*The fourth lahari.*

*Raga Narayani-gauda.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "My Krishna is a treasury of virtues,*
*the refuge of the helpless, the utmost limit of mercy."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the deliverer of all!
Be merciful, so that I may compose [a song of your] glory.[^2]

**2.** Thus at the place [called] Sri Gopivallabhapur
[Rasika] manifested the rasa, as though in a second Vrindavana.[^3]

**3.** What beauty of the circle [of the rasa] — it cannot be told!
What dance and song, what Krishna-sankirtana!

**4.** What majesty in the dance of all the boys!
What attire, what ornaments on all the people![^4]

**5.** What harmonious music — a wondrous tale!
What sweet sound, enchanting the world!

**6.** What dundubhi drums, thundering mightily!
What a jewelled throne, what a splendid tree![^5]

**7.** Thus [he] manifested the Rasa-yatra;
Rasikendra, the jewel, ever performs [his] pastimes.[^6]

**8.** Thus all became intoxicated with the taste of the rasa;
all the people were immersed in bliss without end.[^7]

**9.** The children, holding each other's hands, circle closely,
as though the women of Vraja were sporting around Krishna.[^8]

**10.** In manifold happiness [they] passed [the time] in the taste of the great rasa;
one after another the children dance in jubilation.[^9]

**11.** At times, playfully, they tell the pastimes of the Bhagavata —
how Krishna [and] the wives of Vraja played.[^10]

**12.** Those movements of the body the children reproduce playfully;
seeing [it], the people of the three worlds are amazed.

**13.** At times the children, in the mood of the gopis, say everything [as it was]:
"We have completely subdued Krishna."[^11]

**14.** Hearing of the gopis' pride, Krishna disappeared;
after a moment the gopis realized: "Where has Krishna gone?"[^12]

**15.** Not seeing Krishna, the gopis lost consciousness;
all began to search [for Him] diligently.

**16.** One fortunate gopi named Vrindavati
Krishna led away, making [her His] companion.[^13]

**17.** Seeking the footprints of the two,
all the gopis, searching, wander from forest to forest.[^14]

**18.** Krishna's gait, smile, glance, speech —
remembering them again and again, the gopis weep aloud.[^15]

**19.** Thinking of those pastimes full of the taste of the rasa, the young [gopis]
wander through the forest in love, deeply afflicted.[^16]

**20.** In the forests of ashvattha, kurubaka, ashoka,
in the groves of naga, punnaga, champaka, tulasi[^17]

**21.** [they] asked: "Have you seen the Lord of our life on this path?
He has gone, leaving us all, making [us] helpless."

**22.** Mallika, malati, jati, yuthi — from forest to forest —
all [the gopis] say: "Have you seen Narayana on this path?"[^18]

**23.** Going into the mango grove, the gopis ask each [tree];
in the forests of piyala, panasa, ashana, pomegranate:

**24.** "Have you seen the son of Nanda going along this path?"
Asking, [they] looked along the path for Krishna, the treasure of [their] life.[^19]

**25.** In the forests of jambu, bel, kadamba, bakula — from forest to forest,
on the bank of the Yamuna the gopis search from forest to forest.

**26.** The gopis ask everyone: "Where has Krishna gone?
Tell me, all of you, have you seen my son of Nanda?"[^20]

**27.** Saying [this] again and again, the gopis lost consciousness;
becoming one in spirit with Him, all the gopis search [for Him].[^21]

**28.** All the pastimes that Bhagavan performed in Vraja
the gopis enact, [their] consciousness filled with Krishna.[^22]

**29.** One says: "I destroyed Putana";
some gopi sucks the breast, holding on firmly.[^23]

**30.** "I overturned the cart," says someone;
some gopi, rolling on the ground, cries.

**31.** One says: "I slew Trinavarta";
another says: "I destroyed the wicked Baka."

**32.** "I am Krishna," says some gopi;
the flute sounds, a gentle smile, an unhurried gait.[^24]

**33.** Some gopi, turning her garment over her left hand,
calls everyone and says: "I have lifted Mandara!

**34.** What can King Indra, lord of the gods, do —
a terrible storm [and] downpour, as much as his power [allows]?"[^25]

**35.** Another gopi says: "I am the Subduer of Kaliya;
my name is Destroyer of the wicked, Slayer of the evil."

**36.** Another gopi, putting her hand to her mouth, calls everyone:
"I have destroyed the forest fire — look, every one of you!"[^26]

**37.** One gopi, tying her garment at her waist, cries out:
"Playfully I broke the yamala-arjuna [trees]!"[^27]

**38.** Another says: "Blessed is the life of that gopi
whom the Lord of [our] life has taken away [with Him]!"[^28]

**39.** "Beside the footprints of Krishna are the footprints of that gopi;
blessed is the gopi — she has subdued the son of Nanda!

**40.** [He] whose feet Lakshmi ever holds in her heart —
[that] divine Narayana sported with the gopi in seclusion."[^29]

**41.** Thus, seeking [Krishna and enacting] Krishna's pastimes,
all the cowherd women wander from forest to forest.[^30]

**42.** Then the gopi whom Krishna had taken away,
thinking of what lay deep in her heart, said to Krishna:[^31]

**43.** "I cannot walk; listen, Narayana."
Krishna says: "Sit on [My] shoulder, O gopi."

**44.** Going a little way, Krishna disappeared;
the gopi fell face down on that [very] spot.[^32]

**45.** Lamenting, she weeps, uttering Krishna's name;
hearing the weeping, all came and joined [her].[^33]

**46.** With body, mind and speech becoming one in spirit with Him,
the gopis sing of Krishna's virtues, remembering [Him].[^34]

**47.** Thus all the children, with manifold delight,
[enacted] the pastimes one by one in the order of the Bhagavata.

**48.** That gait, that posture, that conduct,
the movements of the body [they] show wondrously.[^35]

**49.** All the children are most skilled in dance;
seeing [them], all the assembly is amazed.

**50.** All the pastimes that Krishna [and] the gopis performed in the taste of the rasa,
all the children reproduce in every detail.[^36]

**51.** All say: "Vrindavana has become visible before our eyes;
the son of Achyuta was born of a portion of Narayana."[^37]

**52.** Blessed is the fortune of the earth, blessed is this place,
where Rasikendra-chandra dwelt![^38]

**53.** At this time Rasikendra-chandra, from his house,
with joy in his heart came to the place of the rasa.[^39]

**54.** There was a cobra there;
that snake bit Rasika on the foot.[^40]

**55.** The most wicked [snake] bit with tremendous force;
two fangs broke off and remained in the flesh.[^41]

**56.** A stream of blood began to flow from the foot;
amazed, [Rasika] remembered Krishna.[^42]

**57.** His companions brought lamps [and] look:
in the press of the people the snake had given up its life.[^43]

**58.** People without number; by the blows of [their] feet
the snake's body, turned to dust, was scattered in all directions.[^44]

**59.** Rasikendra-mahashaya went on to the place of the rasa,
saying nothing to anyone, [though] the poison [was] burning.[^45]

**60.** At the place of the rasa he remains in great bliss, in joy [in] Krishna;
four praharas passed — [and] no suffering at all.[^46]

**61.** All night, rejoicing in the sight of the rasa,
the son of Achyuta delights in the dance [and] song.[^47]

**62.** In the morning all saw on the foot:
[there were] two fangs, and blood was flowing without stopping.

**63.** All carefully drew out the fangs;
Rasika's pastime amazes the three worlds.[^48]

**64.** He let no one charm away [the poison] — [and] the poison vanished;
seeing [this] manifestation of Rasika, all are amazed.[^49]

**65.** The greatness of Rasika cannot be told at all;
"[He is] a part of Narayana," all the people sing.[^50]

**66.** Listen to the Rasika-mangala, all friends:
without delay you will obtain the treasure of prema-bhakti for Krishna.[^51]

**67.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Pashchima-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the fourth lahari, called "The Description of the Rasa-yatra."*[^52]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the lahari's heading («চতুর্থ-লহরী»), in italics without a number. Lahari 4 begins on a new page — pdf 140 (printed p. 100; the number is not printed), after the colophon of lahari 3 on pdf 139 (files RM-3-03-032-065). The raga in the scan is *nārānī gauṛā*, i.e. Narayani-gauda (Dakshina, lahari 5, note 1); the ghosha is the same as in Dakshina, laharis 5 and 16, and its translation is repeated. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 142 (printed p. 102), checked against the scan: «ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-পশ্চিম-বিভাগে রাসযাত্রা-বর্ণন-নাম চতুর্থ-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা» (*rāsayātrā-barṇana*) — "The Description of the Rasa-yatra". The summary line covers the whole lahari (65–67 from the scan of pdf 142, provisionally).
[^2]: "The deliverer of all" — *akhilatāraṇa*. The second line is as in Dakshina, lahari 16, 1.
[^3]: "Manifested" — *prakāśilā*; the subject is not named; understood as Rasika (cf. 7 and lahari 3). The account continues of the Rasa-yatra opened on the spring full moon of Vaishakha (Pashchima 2, note 4). On the rasa-lila and the Rasa-yatra, see Pashchima 2, note 7, and Pashchima 3, note 2: according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, the rasa is a manifestation of the gopis' pure prema, in which there is not a trace of mundane desire (kama; cf. CC, Adi 4); Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati warned against any mundane understanding of these pastimes. The boys represent the pastimes of Krishna and the gopis as a sacred performance — a glorification of the Lord's pastimes, not an imitation of Krishna (Dakshina, lahari 5, note 22).
[^4]: "Majesty" — *garimā*. "On all the people" — *sarbbajanā*: it can also be understood as "on all the participants".
[^5]: Dundubhis are large kettledrums. "Splendid tree" — *tarubara*, "best of trees": the wish-fulfilling tree in the middle of the circle of the rasa (Pashchima 1, 59–61, note 41).
[^6]: "Rasikendra, the jewel" — *rasikendramaṇi*. "Performs [his] pastimes" — *karena bilāsa*.
[^7]: "Intoxicated" — *bhora* (*bhor*): "drunk, absorbed". "Without end" — *nāi ora*.
[^8]: "Closely" — *ghane*. "Women of Vraja" — *brajāṅganā*. "Sporting" — *biharaṇe*.
[^9]: "Passed [the time]" — *bañcilena*. "One after another" — *eke āre*.
[^10]: "Playfully" — *kouutuke* (= *kautuke*). "Wives of Vraja" — *brajabadhū*: the gopis.
[^11]: "Say everything [as it was]" — *kahe sarbbakathā*. "Completely" — *sarbbathā*. Cf. Bhag. 10.29.47–48: the gopis became proud, and Krishna disappeared.
[^12]: "Realized" — *jānilā*, lit. "knew, understood": understood as "noticed that Krishna had gone".
[^13]: Vrindavati (*bṛndābatī*; so in the scan) — in the Bhagavata this gopi is not named (Bhag. 10.30.28: "She has truly worshipped (*ārādhitaḥ*) Him"); according to the Gaudiya Vaishnava interpretation she is Sri Radha. The name "Vrindavati" for her is unusual; perhaps the author so calls Radha as the mistress of Vrindavana. "Fortunate" — *bhāgyabatī*. "Making [her His] companion" — *kariẏā saṃhati*.
[^14]: Cf. Bhag. 10.30.25–28: the gopis find Krishna's footprints and beside them the footprints of the chosen gopi. "Wander" — *bharame* (= *bhrame*).
[^15]: Cf. Bhag. 10.30.2. "Remembering" — *samari* (= *smari*).
[^16]: "Those pastimes full of the taste of the rasa" — *rāsa-rasa bihāra*. "The young [gopis]" — *yubatī*. "In love" — *anurāge* (anuraga — ever-increasing loving attachment). "Deeply afflicted" — *ati duḥkhamati*.
[^17]: 20–25 — cf. Bhag. 10.30.5–9: the gopis ask the trees and creepers about Krishna. Ashvattha — the sacred fig; kurubaka — a shrub with red flowers; ashoka — a tree with red flowers; naga (nagakeshara) and punnaga — trees with fragrant flowers; champaka — a tree with golden fragrant flowers; tulasi — the sacred plant dear to Krishna.
[^18]: Mallika, malati, jati, yuthi (*mallikā, mālatī, jāti, yūthī*) are kinds of jasmine. Narayana — so the gopis in the author's text call Krishna (cf. 40, 43).
[^19]: Piyala (*piẏāla*, *priyāla*) — a tree with edible fruit; panasa (*panasa*) — the jackfruit tree; ashana (*aśana*) — a forest tree; "pomegranate" — *dāṛimba*. "Asking, looked" — *puchiẏe dekhila*: it can also be understood as "asked: have [you] seen…".
[^20]: "My son of Nanda" — *nandabālā*, lit. "Nanda's boy".
[^21]: "Becoming one in spirit with Him" — *tadātmikā*: Bhag. 10.30.3, 43 (*tad-ātmikāḥ*) — the gopis, wholly absorbed in Krishna, begin to behave as He does.
[^22]: "[Their] consciousness filled with Krishna" — *kṛṣṇamaẏa jñāna*. 29–37 — cf. Bhag. 10.30.14–23: the gopis in their absorption enact Krishna's pastimes (one as Putana, another as Krishna sucking her breast, and so on).
[^23]: Putana — the rakshasi who came to kill the infant Krishna with her poisoned breast (Bhag. 10.6). "Some gopi" — *kona gopā* (so in the scan; *gopā* = gopi; likewise in 23). The gopis speak in Krishna's person, in the masculine.
[^24]: "The flute sounds" — *beṇu sphure*. "A gentle smile" — *mandasmita*. "An unhurried gait" — *manthara gamana*.
[^25]: 33–34: "Mandara" — *mandara*: the mountain with which the ocean was churned; here apparently instead of Govardhana, which Krishna lifted to protect Vraja from Indra's downpour (Bhag. 10.25; cf. 10.30.20: a gopi raised the edge of her garment, as Krishna the hill). "Turning… over" — *pālaṭi*, lit. "turning over". Indra is the king of the gods (*surapati*).
[^26]: Kaliya — the serpent subdued by Krishna in the Yamuna (Bhag. 10.16). "Putting her hand to her mouth" — *mukhe kara diẏā*: Krishna swallowed the forest fire (Bhag. 10.19). "Every one of you" — *pratyeke*, lit. "each one"; below the line the editor gives the variant reading *pratyakṣe*, "before your eyes" (not translated).
[^27]: Yamala-arjuna — the two arjuna trees that the little Krishna, tied by Yashoda to a mortar, uprooted (Bhag. 10.10). "Tying her garment at her waist" — *kaṭite basana bā̃dhi*. "Cries out" — *ḍāke*, lit. "calls".
[^28]: 38–40 — cf. Bhag. 10.30.28–34. "The Lord of [our] life" — *prāṇanātha*.
[^29]: Lakshmi — the consort of Narayana, the goddess of fortune; even she longs for Krishna's feet. "Ever" — *anukṣaṇa*. "Sported… in seclusion" — *ekānte ramila*. The first line is understood as referring to Narayana, that is, Krishna.
[^30]: "Seeking [Krishna and enacting] Krishna's pastimes" — *kṛṣṇalīlā anbeṣaṇa kari*, lit. "searching out Krishna's pastimes"; understood in the light of 27–37. "Cowherd women" — *gopanārī*. "Wander" — *bhrami bule*.
[^31]: 42–45 — cf. Bhag. 10.30.36–40: the chosen gopi, becoming proud, asks Krishna to carry her, and He disappears. "What lay deep in her heart" — *marmmakathā*, lit. "a word [from] the depths of the heart".
[^32]: "Face down" — *mukha māṛi* (so in the scan), lit. "pressing the face": understood as "fell on her face"; the reading is unclear.
[^33]: "Weeping" — *kāndanā*. "Joined [her]" — *milena*.
[^34]: Cf. Bhag. 10.30.43–44: the gopis, one in spirit with Krishna (*tad-ātmikāḥ*), sing of His virtues. "With body, mind and speech" — *kāẏamanobākye*.
[^35]: The first line is as in lahari 3, 32. "Wondrously" — *ati bilakṣaṇa*, "extraordinarily".
[^36]: "In the taste of the rasa" — *rāsarase*. "In every detail" — *aśeṣa-biśeṣe*.
[^37]: "Has become visible before our eyes" — *sākṣāta haila*. "The son of Achyuta" is Rasika (Purva, lahari 3, note 5). "Was born of a portion of Narayana" — *nārāẏaṇa-aṃśe janma*: the rapturous words of the onlookers (cf. lahari 3, 61: "a second Narayana"). According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, Rasikananda is a devotee and associate, not an incarnation of the Lord (Dakshina, lahari 5, note 26; Purva, lahari 1, note 69); his guru Shyamananda is a servant of Radha and Krishna (Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11).
[^38]: "Blessed is the fortune of the earth" — *dhanya bhāgya pṛthibīra*. "Dwelt" — *biśrāma karilā*, lit. "rested, had his abode". Rasikendra-chandra is Rasika ("the moon Rasikendra").
[^39]: "Came" — *bije kaila*: respectful, of a ceremonial arrival (Pashchima 2, note 5). "From his house" — *gṛha haite*. 53–64 — an account not found in BR 15.
[^40]: "Cobra" — *gokṣura* (*gokhurā*), the spectacled cobra; *nāga* — snake. "One" — *ekai*. "Snake" — *sarapa* (= *sarpa*).
[^41]: "The most wicked" — *duṣṭabara*, lit. "the best of the wicked". "With tremendous force" — *mahāteje*.
[^42]: "Amazed" — *camatkāra haiẏā*. "Remembered Krishna" — *kaila kṛṣṇa-saṅaraṇe*. The subject is not named; understood as Rasika.
[^43]: "Lamps" — *deuṭī* (torches, lamps). "In the press of the people" — *manuṣya-gahale* (so in the scan): *gahala* is understood as "crowd, throng"; the reading is unclear.
[^44]: "People without number" — *samuccaẏa nāhi loka* (cf. lahari 3, 5). "By the blows of [their] feet" — *caraṇera ghāte*. "In all directions" — *cāribhite*.
[^45]: Mahashaya is a respectful title. "[Though] the poison [was] burning" — *biṣa tejomaẏa*, lit. "the poison — fiery (potent)".
[^46]: "In joy [in] Krishna" — *kṛṣṇa-sukhe*. A prahara is a quarter of the night or day, about three hours: four praharas are the whole night.
[^47]: "All night" — *cira niśi*. "Delights" — *biharena*.
[^48]: "Carefully" — *kariẏā yatana*. "Amazes the three worlds" — *camatkāra tribhubana*.
[^49]: "Charm away" — *jhāṛite* (so in the scan; the OCR has «বাড়িতে»): *jhāṛā* is the folk healer's "removal" of poison by incantations and fanning. "Manifestation" — *prakāśa*: Rasika's manifested power. The author describes a miracle, exalting Rasika; this account is not in BR 15.
[^50]: Continuation of lahari 4 (its beginning is on pdf 140–141, files RM-3-04-001-064); pdf 142 (printed p. 102) begins with ॥ 65 ॥. "Cannot be told at all" — *kichu kahana nā yāẏa*. "A part of Narayana" — *nārāẏaṇa-aṃśa*: the rapturous words of the people, as in 51; according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching Rasikananda is a devotee and associate, not an incarnation of the Lord (note 37).
[^51]: "Friends" — *bandhugaṇa*. "Without delay" — *abilambe*. "The treasure of prema-bhakti for Krishna" — *kṛṣṇapremabhakti-dhana*.
[^52]: The colophon is on pdf 142 (printed p. 102), checked against the scan: «ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-পশ্চিম-বিভাগে রাসযাত্রা-বর্ণন-নাম চতুর্থ-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা» (*rāsayātrā-barṇana*) — "The Description of the Rasa-yatra". Lahari 5 begins on the same page, below a rule (files RM-3-05-001-084).
