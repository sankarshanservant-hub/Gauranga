# Uttara-vibhaga. Lahari 16. The Absorption of Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu into the Holy Form of Sri Sri Kshirachora Gopinatha-jiu at Remuna

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; the author begins the account of Rasika's departure: in Phalguna, on the day of pratipad after Shiva-chaturdashi, he disappeared and went to Vrindavana; resolving to go to Krishna, Rasika comes to Banshda, where a small thorn pricks his foot and a fever begins; he asks that a place be made for him at the feet of Gopala-raya at Remuna; around him sankirtana resounds day and night, thousands of sadhus are fed, kings, subjects and disciples gather, and every place where he rests becomes greater than Vaikuntha; when they wish to carry him to Gopiballavpur, the palanquin grows heavy and will not leave the ground, and at his command all set out for Remuna; there Rasika enters the temple alone, and a brahmana sees him enter the body of Gopala; the palanquin is empty; those close to him, keeping the secret, screen the place with cloth and before Gopala raise a samadhi with clothes, ornaments, garlands and fragrances; the author glorifies Rasika, who age after age accepts a body for the sake of living beings, names his poem — four vibhagas, sixty-four laharis — asks the learned to forgive its faults, praises Rasika, the husband of Shyamadasi, and relates that he began the poem on Vasanta-panchami in the ninth anka of Gajapati Balabhadra and completed it two years and eight months later, on a Sunday, on the bright panchami of the month Kanya in the twelfth anka*

*The sixteenth lahari.*

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "It remains to proclaim the qualities of the moon Gauranga."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the treasury of mercy —
master, family, wealth [and] life of Sri Rasika-deva![^2]

**2.** Now hear everything about how [he] departed.
Prabhu descended [into the world] from a part of Narayana.[^3]

**3.** On the day of pratipad after the close of Shiva-chaturdashi,
in Phalguna, Rasikendra departed.[^4]

**4.** Listen, everyone: I shall tell in detail
how the son of Achyuta departed.

**5.** Leaving all his associates, intimates and those who had taken shelter [of him],
becoming unmanifest, Prabhu went to Vrindavana.[^5]

**6.** Of all the lilas Prabhu performed on earth
I have briefly described a little, placing the command on my head.[^6]

**7.** That Rasika-shekhara is the very form of the bliss of kirtan:
day and night he dances unceasingly in sankirtana.

**8.** [Before] the Deities, in the temples, in all the servants' homes —
everywhere he dances in waves of bliss.[^7]

**9.** Resolving to go to Krishna,
the son of Achyuta came to Banshda.[^8]

**10.** A small thorn pricked [his] holy foot;
on that pretext a fever appeared in his body.[^9]

**11.** Prabhu tells those close to him the pain of his heart:
"I shall certainly go to Krishna.

**12.** At Remuna, at the feet of Sri Gopala-raya —
there, without fail, make a place for me."[^10]

**13.** Day and night, unceasingly, in the joy of sankirtana,
all his companions, surrounding [him], always sing the Name.

**14.** Thousands upon thousands of sadhus are fed;
life found relief: a festival every moment.[^11]

**15.** Kings, subjects, disciples of disciples, servants, disciples —
all, hearing [of it], came to see [him].

**16.** Cries of "Hari!", sankirtana, shouts of "Jaya!",
vina, venu, mridanga, mandira, karatalas,

**17.** the sound of conches, shinga, venu, rabab, murali;
on all sides waves of bliss and joyful cries.[^12]

**18.** All those places became greater than Vaikuntha —
on the roads and at the landings, wherever Rasikendra rested.

**19.** Then, seeing [his] body grown powerless, all
endeavoured to carry [him] to Gopiballavpur.

**20.** All, supporting [him], seated [him] in a palanquin;
no one can lift it — Prabhu had become heavy.[^13]

**21.** Hundreds of people lift that palanquin —
all could not raise [it] from the ground.

**22.** Again Prabhu commanded: "Take [me] to Remuna."
Thrilled and astonished, all at once rose up.[^14]

**23.** On the way the earth sways with the bliss of sankirtana;
cries of "Hari!", "Jaya, jaya!" — all over the earth.

**24.** Leaving Sarata, Prabhu came to Remuna;
there, going alone, Prabhu entered the temple.[^15]

**25.** The brahmana just saw [him] entering the temple —
he disappeared in the body of Gopala.[^16]

**26.** The twice-born says: "Where has Rasika-shekhara gone?
I saw him go inside the temple."

**27.** In the temple the twice-born saw there was no one there:
he had entered wholly into the body of Gopala.

**28.** The brahmana, overwhelmed with love, tells everyone:
"Rasika Thakura has been absorbed into the body of Gopala."[^17]

**29.** Then all entered that place;
all the people see: there is no one in the palanquin.

**30.** Astonished, all did not make [it] known;
quickly they put up a cloth screen on all four sides.[^18]

**31.** Clothes, ornaments, garlands — as many as there were —
with sandal and kunkuma they laid out a seat [for him].

**32.** With aguru, kasturi, chuya and sandal
they established the samadhi there in front of Gopala.[^19]

**33.** Prabhu disappeared together with his body —
he entered the holy form of Gopala.[^20]

**34.** Apart from those close to him, no one knows this;
of [those who] saw with their own eyes, no one made it known.

**35.** To keep [it] from the people they did many things;
the whole world sees [it all] as an illusion.[^21]

**36.** Thus they made the samadhi in that place;
all this truth the sadhus know.

**37.** Rasika's glory is beyond the reach [even] of Indra and the other [gods];
like an illusion, he bears a body [of seeming] birth and death.[^22]

**38.** Boundless are the beauty and qualities of the moon Rasikendra,
whose qualities Aja, Bhava [and] the hosts of gods sing.[^23]

**39.** Descending to the earth for a few days,
he delivered all living beings by giving prema-bhakti.

**40.** The bhakti that Rasika-shekhara established and left behind —
holding to that bhakti, cross over, all people.[^24]

**41.** Age after age, for the sake of living beings he accepts a body;
this quality Gopijana-vallabha Dasa sings.[^25]

**42.** Rasika's glory cannot be told even in part —
[he] on whom Indra and the [other gods], the lords of sages and [others] meditate.

**43.** I am poor, wretched, fallen, dull-witted;
what can I know of the glory of Rasika-deva?

**44.** I have composed some description of [his] nature —
what the son of Achyuta, dwelling in the heart, makes [me] say.[^26]

**45.** From birth I have served the feet of Rasikendra;
whatever Prabhu by [his] grace makes me say,

**46.** in accordance with that I have described [his] glory;
in this the learned and good will find no fault.[^27]

**47.** In the fourth vibhaga I have composed the book —
the holy glory of Shyamananda [and] Rasika.

**48.** A book of four vibhagas, sixty-four laharis —
what Rasika-murari spoke, dwelling in the heart.[^28]

**49.** In accordance with that I have spread the book;
hearing it with joy, cross over, the whole world.

**50.** Even if there are faults, read it correcting [them];
let all sing Rasika's glory with joy.[^29]

**51.** Do not think this a human lila:
the son of Achyuta was born from a part of Narayana.[^30]

**52.** Hearing his qualities, cross over, dancing and singing;
hear the *Rasika-mangala* with joy.

**53.** Glory, glory to Rasikendra, friend of the destitute!
Glory, glory to Thakura Gosai, the ocean of happiness!

**54.** Glory, glory to Rasika-murari, the abode of qualities!
Glory, glory to Thakura Gosani, dear [as] life![^31]

**55.** Glory, glory to the giver of prema-bhakti to all people!
Glory, glory to the protector of the poor, the lowly [and] the wretched!

**56.** Glory, glory to the shining moon of the line of Shyamananda!
Glory, glory to Rasikendra, who captivates every heart!

**57.** Glory, glory to the husband of Shyamadasi Thakurani!
Apart from those feet Vallabha has no [other] refuge.[^32]

**58.** [In the reign of] Sri Balabhadra, gajapati in the land of Orissa,
in the ninth anka, on Vasanta-panchami, in the month of Makara,

**59.** having received the command, [I] began on that day,
keeping Rasika's feet especially in [my] heart.[^33]

**60.** For two years and eight months, in that meditation,
I composed [the account of] Rasika's glory.

**61.** On a Sunday the book was completed —
in the twelfth anka, [in the month of] Kanya, on the fifth [lunar day] of the bright fortnight.[^34]

**62.** Hear the *Rasika-mangala*, all friends:
without delay you will gain the treasure of prema-bhakti for Krishna.

**63.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Uttara-vibhaga of the "Sri Rasika-mangala" ends the sixteenth lahari, called "The Absorption of Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu into the Holy Form of Sri Sri Kshirachora Gopinatha-jiu at Remuna."*[^35]

*The book is complete.*

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the lahari's title ("ষোড়শ-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 16 begins on the lower half of pdf 211 (printed p. 165), below the rule after the colophon of lahari 15 (files RM-4-15-001-057, RM-4-15-058-069). Raga Karuna-shri (*karuṇāśrī*) and the ghosha are the same as in lahari 14 (note 1); the translation of the ghosha is repeated. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 215 (printed p. 167), checked against the scan: "ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গলে উত্তরবিভাগে শ্রীশ্রীরসিকানন্দপ্রভুর রেমুণাতে শ্রীশ্রীক্ষীরচোরাগোপীনাথজীউর শ্রীঅঙ্গে লীন নাম ষোড়শলহরী সম্পূর্ণা" (*… remuṇāte śrīśrīkṣīracorāgopīnāthajīura śrīaṅge līna*); "absorption" — *līna* (notes 16–17); "holy form" — *śrīaṅga*. Kshirachora Gopinatha ("Gopinatha who stole the thickened milk") is the Deity at Remuna who stole thickened milk for Madhavendra Puri (CC, Madhya 4; here — lahari 15, 54–56, note 25); in the text of the lahari the author calls Him Gopala and Gopala-raya. Jiu (*jīu*) is a respectful form of address to the Deity. Lahari 16 is 1–63 (pdf 211, 212, 215): pdf 213 is a plate (a photograph of Rasikananda's samadhi-mandira in the courtyard of the Kshirachora Gopinatha temple at Remuna, with the publisher's caption), pdf 214 a blank page; not translated. This is the last lahari of the poem.
[^2]: "Treasury of mercy" — *karuṇānidhāna*. "Master, family, wealth [and] life" — *prabhu jāti dhana prāṇa*: Shyamananda is everything to Rasika.
[^3]: "How [he] departed" — *gamanera … sab byabahāra*, lit. "all the circumstances of the departure." "Descended [into the world] from a part of Narayana" — *nārāẏaṇa-aṃśe … haila abatāra* (cf. 51): the author's rapturous praise; in the Gaudiya Vaishnava understanding Rasikananda is not an avatara of the Lord but an eternal associate and dear devotee, a disciple of Shyamananda (Uttara, lahari 9, notes 13 and 18; Purva, lahari 6, note 11). On the author's view of Shyamananda and Rasika, see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11.
[^4]: Shiva-chaturdashi is Shivaratri, the fourteenth lunar day of the dark fortnight; pratipad is the first lunar day [of the following fortnight]. "Departed" — *karilā gamane*, lit. "made his departure." The author does not name the year.
[^5]: "Associates, intimates and those who had taken shelter [of him]" — *sāṅgopāṅgāśraẏagaṇa*: *sāṅgopāṅga* — "associates and intimates," *āśraẏa* — those who had found shelter with him. "Becoming unmanifest" — *aprakaṭa haiẏā*. "Went to Vrindavana" — to Krishna's eternal abode (cf. lahari 14: the resolve to return to Krishna in Vrindavana).
[^6]: "Placing the command on my head" — *ājñā kari śire*: apparently the command to describe Rasika's life (cf. 44–45, 59).
[^7]: "The Deities" — *deba*; "in all the servants' homes" — *yata sebaka mandire*: *mandira* means both "temple" and "house."
[^8]: Banshda (*bā̃śadā*) — apparently a village on Rasika's way; not identified (cf. Sarata, 24). "Came" — *prabeśilā*, lit. "entered." "Son of Achyuta" — the scan has the misprint *acuta*.
[^9]: "On that pretext" — *tāhe bhara kari*, lit. "leaning on that": the thorn is only the occasion for the departure.
[^10]: "Place" — *āsana*, lit. "seat, throne": here the place of his samadhi (cf. 31–32; lahari 15, 52, 57, note 23). Gopala-raya is Kshirachora Gopinatha at Remuna (note 1). "Without fail" — *niśce* (so in the scan).
[^11]: "Life found relief" — *juṛila jībana*, lit. "life was cooled (soothed)," i.e. all found joy; it may also be understood of Rasika himself.
[^12]: Vina — a stringed instrument; venu and murali — flutes; mridanga — a clay drum; mandira and karatalas — hand cymbals; shinga (*śiṅgā*) — a horn; rabab (*rabāba*) — a plucked stringed instrument. "Joyful cries" — *hulahulī*: the jubilant ululation of women.
[^13]: "Palanquin" — *sukapāla* (so in the scan, also 21 and 29; cf. *sukupāla*, Purva, lahari 12, note 6).
[^14]: "Rose up" — *uṭhilā*: apparently all rose [to set out, lifting the palanquin]; it may also be read "[the palanquin] rose." "Thrilled" — *pulake*, lit. "with hair standing on end [from joy]"; "astonished" — *camakite*.
[^15]: Sarata (*sāratā*) — apparently a village on the way to Remuna; not identified. Remuna is a village near Balasore (Orissa) where the temple of Kshirachora Gopinatha stands (CC, Madhya 4). "Going alone" — *ekā giẏā*.
[^16]: "He disappeared in the body of Gopala" — *rahilena antarddhāna gopāla-aṅgete*, lit. "he remained hidden in the body of Gopala." The tradition that Rasikananda entered the Deity of Kshirachora Gopinatha at Remuna is preserved by his followers; his samadhi stands in the temple courtyard (photograph on the plate, pdf 213). In the Gaudiya Vaishnava understanding this is the disappearance (departure) of a pure devotee who by the Lord's will leaves the visible world and continues his eternal service to Krishna in His abode; "absorption" (*līna*, 28) does not mean merging into God and loss of individuality (Vaishnavas reject sayujya-mukti).
[^17]: "Has been absorbed" — *līna* (note 16). "Overwhelmed with love" — *premete byākula*. "The twice-born" (26–27) — *dbija*, a brahmana; "the brahmana" (28) — *bipra*.
[^18]: "Did not make [it] known" — *nā kaila prakāśa*. "A cloth screen" — *lugāra kāṇḍāri* (so in the scan): *lugā* — "cloth, garment"; *kāṇḍāri* is taken as "enclosure, screen"; the sense is conjectural.
[^19]: Kunkuma — a red powder; aguru — fragrant aloe wood; kasturi — musk; chuya (*cuẏā*) — a fragrant essence. "Laid out a seat [for him]" (31) — *āsana pātila*, lit. "spread a seat" (note 10). A samadhi is the resting place of a saint (lahari 15, note 23); according to this account no body lies in it — Rasika disappeared "together with his body" (33), and clothes, ornaments and garlands were placed in the samadhi (31). "In front of Gopala" — *gopāla-agrete*.
[^20]: "Together with his body" — *sadeha sahite*; "disappeared" — *antarddhāna hailā*; "holy form" — *śrīaṅga* (note 16).
[^21]: "To keep [it] from the people" — *loka bhāṇḍibāre*, lit. "to mislead the people": apparently the samadhi was raised so that outsiders would think Prabhu had left his body like an ordinary man. "As an illusion" — *kuhakera mata*: *kuhaka* — "conjuring trick, illusion"; the world sees only the outward appearance (cf. 37).
[^22]: "Beyond the reach" — *agocare*. "Bears a body [of seeming] birth and death" — *kuhaka sadṛśa janma-mṛtyu-deha dhare*, lit. "like an illusion, bears a body of birth and death": Rasika's birth and departure only seem ordinary (cf. 35, 51). In the Gaudiya Vaishnava understanding the Lord's associates come into the world and leave it by His will; their departure is a disappearance.
[^23]: Aja — Brahma, Bhava — Shiva (Purva, lahari 9, note 22). "The moon Rasikendra" — *rasikendra-candra*.
[^24]: "Cross over" — *tarahu*, lit. "cross [the ocean of worldly existence]"; likewise *tara* in 49 and 52.
[^25]: "Accepts a body" — *lahe deha bāsa*, lit. "accepts residence in a body" (note 3). Gopijana-vallabha Dasa is the author (Purva, lahari 1, note 58).
[^26]: "Nature" — *sbabhāba-barṇanā*, "a description of the nature (character) [of Rasika]." "Makes [me] say" — *bolāẏa*. The son of Achyuta is Rasika.
[^27]: "Will find no fault" — *doṣa nāhi la'be*, lit. "will not count it a fault."
[^28]: "The fourth vibhaga" (47) is the Uttara-vibhaga. "Holy glory" — *puṇya yaśaḥkīrtti* (the scan has *yaśaḥkīrtta*). Four vibhagas of sixteen laharis make sixty-four: thus the author sums up the whole poem. Rasika-murari is Rasikananda.
[^29]: "Read it correcting [them]" — *śuddha kariẏā paṛibe*.
[^30]: "Born from a part of Narayana" — *nārāẏaṇa-aṃśe janma*: see note 3.
[^31]: Thakura Gosai, Thakura Gosani (*ṭhākura gõsāi*, *ṭhākura gosāñi*) — respectful names of Rasika. "Dear [as] life" — *priẏa prāṇa*.
[^32]: Shyamadasi Thakurani is Rasikananda's wife (Purva, lahari 1, note 47). Vallabha is the author himself, Gopijana-vallabha (Purva, lahari 1, note 59).
[^33]: 58–61 give the date of the poem's composition. Balabhadra is the gajapati (king) of Orissa, apparently Balabhadra-deva of Khurda (17th century). Anka (*aṅka*) is the Orissan reckoning of regnal years in which certain numbers are skipped, so the difference between anka numbers does not equal the number of years elapsed; no conversion to the Common Era is attempted here. Vasanta-panchami is the fifth lunar day of the bright fortnight of the month Magha; "in the month of Makara" — *makramāse* (so in the scan): the solar month Makara (January–February). "Having received the command" — *ājñā pāñā*: apparently the same command as in 6. "[I] began" — *ārambha karila* (a third-person form used for the first person, cf. 60). "Especially" — *biśeṣe*.
[^34]: "[In the month of] Kanya" — *kainya* (so in the scan): the solar month Kanya (September–October). "On the fifth [lunar day] of the bright fortnight" — *pañcamī śuklapakṣe*. In sum: begun on Vasanta-panchami in the 9th anka of Balabhadra, completed on a Sunday, on the bright panchami of the month Kanya in the 12th anka — by the author's account two years and eight months later (60).
[^35]: For the title see note 1. After the colophon come the line "গ্রন্থ সম্পূর্ণ" ("The book is complete") and a vignette: here the poem ends (pdf 215, printed p. 167). There is no separate general colophon of the *Rasika-mangala*; the date of composition is given in 58–61 (notes 33–34). pdf 216 is a blank page; from pdf 217 begins the publisher's appendix (পরিশিষ্ট), not translated.
