# Dakshina-vibhaga. Lahari 2. Sri Rasika's Darshan of Vraja-mandala

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Shyamananda stays in Vraja; Shyamadasi, by the order of Sadashiva Dasa, is staying in Hijli, while Rasika lives at Taniya, in the house of Ananta; Rasika tells her that he is leaving for Vraja, and when she begs him to take her along, he tells her to serve the sadhus at home and promises to take her later; through the forest country Rasika hastens to Vraja: in Mathura he sees Krishna's birthplace, in Vrindavana Madana-gopala and Govinda, and goes round the twelve forests; at Govardhana Krishna appears to him in the form of a Vrajavasi and commands him to return to Utkala and give bhakti, saying that Shyamananda is waiting for him in Mathura; in Mathura Rasika meets Shyamananda and asks to be allowed to stay in Vraja, but Shyamananda reminds him of the command given at Govardhana; amazed, Rasika obeys, and together they go to Utkala; at Segala Vishnudasa with his family becomes a disciple and receives the name Dasa Rasamaya; Rasika returns home*

*The second lahari.*

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Where shall I go to find Shyamananda, my life?"*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the life of all [the world]!
Be merciful to me: [let] me sing [your] virtues every moment.[^2]

**2.** Thus, having shown mercy to Rasika [and] Damodara,
Shyamananda stayed in Sri Vraja-mandala.

**3.** At that time, by the order of Sadashiva Dasa,
Shyamadasi Thakurani came there.[^3]

**4.** For some days she stayed in the Hijli country;
again people went to bring [her].[^4]

**5.** Meanwhile, after some days, Rasika-shekhara
came to Taniya, to the house of Ananta.[^5]

**6.** Rasika stayed there for some days;
at that time the Thakurani came to that place.

**7.** For some days they stayed, rapt in talks about Krishna;
Prabhu joyfully says to Shyamadasi:[^6]

**8.** "I will go to Vraja — I tell [you] for certain;
you go and live in the home of [our] relatives."

**9.** Hearing [this], the Thakurani became very sad:
"Whatever is your will, Prabhu — what can I say?

**10.** Making me leave home, you brought [me] here,
and now you go, leaving [me] alone — what am I to do?

**11.** If there is compassion [in you], take me with you" —
so she said humbly to Rasika.

**12.** Hearing [this], Rasika says to Shyamadasi:
"Service to the sadhus gives the fruit of [pilgrimage to] millions of tirthas.[^7]

**13.** So serve the sadhus, staying at home;
[when] I, having seen Sri Vraja, come back once —[^8]

**14.** then I shall certainly take you [with me]."
[Thus] he firmly agreed with Thakurani.

**15.** Then Shyama Thakurani went home;
Rasikendra took leave of everyone.[^9]

**16.** Through the forest country he went by the road to Ayodhya;
full of loving longing, he went swiftly to Vraja.[^10]

**17.** First Rasika came to the city of Mathura;
seeing Krishna's birthplace, he [sheds] streams of tears.

**18.** Then he arrived at Vrindavana-dhama;
with care he beholds Madana-gopala [and] Govinda.[^11]

**19.** Wandering from kunja to kunja, he sees all the temples;
one by one he greets all the adhikaris.[^12]

**20.** Seeing the bank of the Yamuna, his mind rejoiced;
going round Vrindavana-puri, he looks [at it] again and again.

**21.** Staying there some days with joy in his heart,
Sri Rasikananda went to see Vraja.

**22.** He views all the twelve forests one by one —
where Krishna playfully performed which lila:

**23.** Bhadravana, Loha, Shrivana, Bhandiravana,
Mahavana, Talavana, the forest of Khadira,

**24.** Bahula, Kamoda, Kamya, Madhu, Vrindavana —
and all the upavanas, as many as there are.[^13]

**25.** He saw all the places, wandering and wandering,
constantly weeping, absorbed in Krishna.

**26.** His mind maddened with renunciation, no outward awareness;
he roams from forest to forest, viewing the places of the lilas.

**27.** Seeing Govardhana hill, he lost consciousness;
then he had darshana of Sri Gopala-raya.[^14]

**28.** That day he stayed at Govardhana;
sitting in seclusion, he remembers Krishna.[^15]

**29.** At that time Krishna, taking the form of a resident of Vraja,
came and gave Rasika darshana:[^16]

**30.** "Listen, Rasika, to My words:
go quickly to the land of Utkala.

**31.** Give My bhakti to all living beings, gladdening [them],
so that they may serve My resident of Vraja with a pure heart.[^17]

**32.** My Shyamananda is waiting for you;
go to Mathura and see his feet."[^18]

**33.** Hearing all these words, Rasika looked up —
and with his own eyes saw Krishna in the form of a resident of Vraja.

**34.** Seeing [that] enchanting form, Rasika fainted
and fell to the ground, clasping [His] feet.

**35.** Rising, he saw: there was no one in that place;
he wept much in separation.

**36.** Having heard with his own ears of Shyamananda in Mathura,
he set out swiftly for Shyamananda.[^19]

**37.** Then Rasikendra, having seen Govardhana,
went round Vraja and came to Vrindavana.

**38.** For three days he stayed there unrecognized;
wandering, he viewed all the places where Krishna's lilas [took place].

**39.** At Govardhana he had received darshana of Krishna —
[and] constantly he weeps in the ecstasy of prema.

**40.** All over his body the hairs stand on end, [like] a kadamba flower;
tears flow from his eyes without cease.[^20]

**41.** Sweat, trembling, a faltering voice, rapid breathing;
Rasika rolls on the ground, unable to restrain his words:

**42.** "Krishna, lord of my life, leaving me, where have You gone?
How shall I live without seeing the boy of Nanda?"

**43.** The eight sattvika-bhavas appear in his body;
constantly he delights in love for Krishna.[^21]

**44.** He knows neither night nor day, there is no outward awareness;
he always meditates on Krishna, the lord of [his] life.

**45.** There is neither hunger nor thirst: he always thinks of that form;
his heart is in great longing, contemplating that form.

**46.** At that time Shyamananda, for the sake of darshana,
went from Vrindavana to Mathura.

**47.** In Mathura [he] joyfully beheld Keshava;
in that [very] place [Rasika] received darshana of Shyamananda.[^22]

**48.** Seeing each other, both were enraptured at heart;
Rasika fell at Shyamananda's feet.

**49.** Shyamananda-raya swiftly took [him] into his embrace;
in prema both their voices falter, tears flow.

**50.** Wiping Rasika's face, Shyamananda-raya
took him and seated him near [his own] seat.

**51.** With jubilation in his heart he asked about everything:
"I have been especially waiting for you.

**52.** It is good [that] I have seen [you]: you have come to Vrindavana.
Now go to your own home."

**53.** Hearing [this], Rasika says: "Listen, Prabhu, to [my] words:
I am thinking of staying a few days in Vraja.

**54.** I have not yet properly seen the whole land of Vraja,
hearing whose name I could find no rest at home.

**55.** How shall I go, leaving that land?
Allow me to stay a few days in Vrindavana."[^23]

**56.** Hearing [this], Shyamananda speaks sweet words:
"Without you [your] household there will suffer.

**57.** Everyone will lay all kinds of blame on me;
go, my child, do not make trouble.[^24]

**58.** You [and] I have the command to go to Utkala:
we shall give prema-bhakti for Krishna in every home.

**59.** 'Serve My sadhus with a pure heart' —
[so said] Krishna, whom you saw face to face in the form of a resident of Vraja.[^25]

**60.** He who spoke to you at Govardhana —
how will you disobey His command?"

**61.** Hearing [this], Rasika was deeply astonished:
"Truly, Shyamananda is an avatara dear to Krishna![^26]

**62.** I received darshana in seclusion, alone,
[yet] here he has told me all the details."[^27]

**63.** Then Rasika says to Shyamananda:
"Whatever your command, Prabhu, that is [my] rule."[^28]

**64.** Hearing [this], Shyamananda, greatly delighted,
set out for Utkala, taking Rasika with him.

**65.** By the forest road the two came [back] swiftly;
by way of Nagpur they arrived at Segala.[^29]

**66.** [There] lives a fortunate [man] named Vishnudasa;
coming to his house, Prabhu rested.

**67.** That mahashaya with all his family became a disciple;
[Prabhu] ordered his name [to be] "Dasa Rasamaya."[^30]

**68.** After some days they came swiftly from there
and entered Rasika's house.

**69.** Seeing Rasika, all [were plunged] in an ocean of joy;
[the news of] Shyamananda [and] Rasikendra spread [everywhere].[^31]

**70.** Rising [like the sun] to destroy the blinding darkness of Utkala,
the son of Achyuta came together with Shyamananda.

**71.** For the sake of the mercy of Rasikendra, the crest-jewel [of rasikas],
I have briefly described [his] glory.[^32]

**72.** Learned and good people, do not impute faults [to me] in this;
listen to the Rasika-mangala, all you people of Krishna.[^33]

**73.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus ends the second lahari in the Dakshina-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala, called "Sri Rasika's Darshan of Vraja-mandala."*[^34]

[^1]: Raga Karuna-shri — as in lahari 1 (note 1) and in the Purva-vibhaga, lahari 1 (note 8); the ghosha (refrain) is new: "কোথা গেলে পাব শ্যামানন্দ জীবন আমার" (*kothā gele pāba śyāmānanda jīvana āmāra*); it is repeated in lahari 3. There is no mangala-shloka: after the heading, raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. Lahari 1 ends with its colophon at the top of pdf 93; lahari 2 begins on the same page. The title follows the colophon on pdf 95 (checked against the scan): "ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-দক্ষিণবিভাগে শ্রীরসিকের ব্রজমণ্ডল-দর্শননাম দ্বিতীয়-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা" (*śrīrasikera brajamaṇḍala-darśana*).
[^2]: "The life of all [the world]" — *akhila-jīvana* (Purva, lahari 16, note 2). "Sing every moment" — *guṇa gāi anukṣaṇa*, literally "[may] I sing [your] virtues at every moment".
[^3]: Sadashiva Dasa — apparently Sadashiva, the brother of Balabhadra, the governor of Hijli (Purva, lahari 10, 86; lahari 11, note 8), i.e. Shyamadasi's uncle (Ichchha-dei is Balabhadra's daughter). "There" — *tathāya*: apparently to Hijli (4), to her native place. Thakurani — a respectful "lady". Shyamadasi is Rasika's wife (lahari 1, 28–32).
[^4]: Hijli — Purva, lahari 10, note 21. Who sent the people is not said; apparently Rasika (cf. 10).
[^5]: Taniya — *taniyā* (clear on the scan, cut-out ×2.5); location not identified. Ananta — not identified (cf. Ananta-raya, to whom the author bows in the Purva-vibhaga, lahari 1, 105). Rasika-shekhara — "crown of rasikas", an epithet of Rasika.
[^6]: Prabhu here is Rasika (as also in 9).
[^7]: "The fruit of [pilgrimage to] millions of tirthas" — *koṭi tīrtha-phala*; cf. SB 1.13.10: devotees are themselves living holy places.
[^8]: The sentence continues in 14 (pdf 94): "...then I will surely take you [with me]."
[^9]: Shyama Thakurani is Shyamadasi, Rasika's wife (*śyāmā ṭhākurāṇī*, in short). "Firmly agreed" — *satya karila nirṇaya*, lit. "settled the promise." "Took leave" — *karila melānī*.
[^10]: "The forest country" — *banabhūmi*: apparently the wooded lands west of Medinipur (now Jharkhand), through which the roads from Utkala to the north ran; likewise in 65. "By the road to Ayodhya" — *ayodhyāra pathe*: possibly past the Ayodhya hills (now Purulia district, West Bengal) on the forest road westward, or else by the northern route through Ayodhya (Awadh) to Vraja.
[^11]: Madana-gopala and Govinda are Deities of Vrindavana (Purva, lahari 1, note 24). "Beholds" — *dekhe*, "sees," here: receives darshana.
[^12]: Kunjas are the groves of Vrindavana. Adhikaris — here apparently the servitors (managers) of the temples (Purva, lahari 15, note 13).
[^13]: The twelve forests of Vraja (23–24): Bhadravana, Lohavana, Shrivana (Bilvavana), Bhandiravana, Mahavana, Talavana, Khadiravana, Bahulavana, Kamoda (usually Kumudavana), Kamyavana, Madhuvana, Vrindavana; the text has *loha*, *śrībana*, *kāmoda*, *madhu* in short. Upavanas are the lesser groves of Vraja.
[^14]: "Lost consciousness" — *harila cetana*, lit. "[it] stole [his] consciousness." Sri Gopala-raya is apparently the Gopala found by Madhavendra Puri on Govardhana hill (CC, Madhya 4; cf. Purva, lahari 1, note 26).
[^15]: "In seclusion" — *nigame* (Purva, lahari 16, note 21); likewise in 62.
[^16]: "Taking the form of a resident of Vraja" — *brajabāsi-rūpa haiñā*: Krishna appears to Rasika as a simple Vrajavasi (cf. 33, 59). For the vision of Krishna commanding Rasika to go to Shyamananda cf. Purva, lahari 14; BR 15 has no pilgrimage of Rasika to Vraja.
[^17]: "Gladdening [them]" — *ānandite*. "My resident of Vraja" — *mora brajabāsī*: apparently Shyamananda (cf. 32: "My Shyamananda"); cf. Purva, lahari 15, note 11, where the same expression is ambiguous.
[^18]: Krishna calls Shyamananda "His own" and sends Rasika to him; on how the author exalts Shyamananda see Purva, lahari 1, note 2, and lahari 14, note 11.
[^19]: The order of events in 36–47 is not quite clear: in 36 Rasika hastens to Shyamananda, but in 37–38 he goes round Vraja and stays three days in Vrindavana, and the meeting in Mathura takes place only in 46–48. "Having heard with his own ears" — *śuniyā śravaṇe*, i.e. apparently from Krishna's lips (32). The darshana at Govardhana in 39 is the same as in 29–33.
[^20]: Hairs standing on end in ecstasy (*pulaka*) are compared to the kadamba flower, covered with bristling stamens.
[^21]: The eight sattvika-bhavas (*aṣṭa sāttvika-bhāva*) are stupor, perspiration, hairs standing on end, faltering of the voice, trembling, change of complexion, tears and loss of consciousness (Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu 2.3.16). "In his body" — *se śrī-aṅge*, lit. "in that holy body."
[^22]: Keshava is Keshava-raya (Keshavadeva), the Deity of Krishna in Mathura (Purva, lahari 1, note 26). The subject in 47a is not named: Keshava is apparently seen by Shyamananda (46), and darshana of Shyamananda is received by Rasika; it can also be taken that Rasika sees Keshava. "Received" — *pā'la*.
[^23]: "I am thinking of staying" — *rahibāre anumāni*. "Whose name" — *yā̃hāra nāma* (respectful pronoun), i.e. the name of the land of Vraja.
[^24]: "Do not make trouble" — *nā kara jañjāla*, "do not create confusion, bother."
[^25]: 59a — *mora sādhujana-sebā kara śuddhacite*: apparently Shyamananda is repeating Krishna's command (cf. 31), hence "My sadhus"; it can also be taken as Shyamananda's own words ("my sadhus"). Line 59b is taken as a continuation: "Krishna, whom you saw face to face (*dekhile sākṣāte*) in the form of a resident of Vraja."
[^26]: "An avatara dear to Krishna" — *kṛṣṇapriya śyāmānanda avatāra*; it can also be taken as "the incarnation of Krishna's beloved" (cf. *kṛṣṇa-priyā*, Dakshina, lahari 1, note 6): thus the author exalts Shyamananda (Purva, lahari 1, note 2; lahari 14, note 11). According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching Shyamananda is a devotee and servant of Radha, a disciple of Hridaya Chaitanya, not an avatara. Rasika is amazed that Shyamananda knows of the darshana at Govardhana (62).
[^27]: "In seclusion" — *nigame* (cf. 28). "He has told" — *kahilena* (respectful): Shyamananda.
[^28]: "That is [my] rule" — *sei paramāṇe*, lit. "that [is the] measure (authority)."
[^29]: "Came [back]" — *āilā*. Nagpur is apparently Chota Nagpur (the plateau in present Jharkhand), through whose forest lands the roads from northern India to Utkala ran. Segala (*segalā*) — the place is not identified; the edition gives in a footnote the variant *semulā*.
[^30]: Vishnudasa is not identified. "Prabhu" (66) is Shyamananda or Rasika (cf. note 6); whose disciple Vishnudasa became is not quite clear. The name "Dasa Rasamaya" — *dāsa rasamaya*, i.e. Rasamayadasa; the author's father Rasamaya bore the same name (Purva, lahari 1, 135, 141, notes 46, 56), but whether this is the same person the author does not say here.
[^31]: "Spread [everywhere]" — *haila paracāra*: Shyamananda and Rasikendra became known.
[^32]: "For the sake of the mercy" — *kṛpāra kāraṇa*; it can also be taken as "by the mercy of Rasikendra." "Crest-jewel" — *cūṛāmaṇi*.
[^33]: "People of Krishna" — *kārṣṇa-jana*; read from the scan (a superscript repha), garbled in the OCR (Purva, lahari 1, note 66).
[^34]: The colophon is on pdf 95 (checked against the scan); on the title see note 1. Lower on the same page lahari 3 begins.
