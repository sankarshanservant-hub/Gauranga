# Uttara-vibhaga. Lahari 15. The Telling to the Disciples of the Departure to Goloka, and the Command to Build the Samadhi at Gopala's Place in Remuna

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; in seclusion Rasika discloses to those close to him that the command to depart has definitely been given him and that he will not remain in the terrible Kali, when the yavanas will grow strong and people abandon righteousness; he recounts his life: eighteen years of secret bhajan from birth, initiation from Shyamananda and nineteen years and seven months of wandering with him, and after Shyamananda's departure twenty-four years and nine months of service by his command, the yearly festivals and twelve-day aradhanas in Govindapur, Shyamasundarpur, Kusharada and Gopivallabhapur — sixty-two years and four months in all; only his wish for a month-long festival, another pilgrimage to Vrindavana and the feeding of Vaishnavas on the Ganga has gone unfulfilled; he asks that his samadhi be made in Remuna, at the place of Gopala, who stole kshira for Madhavendra Puri; those close to him weep in dread, and Rasika consoles them; ill omens appear — earthquakes, a rain of blood, strife — all beings grieve, and the author promises to tell of Rasika's disappearance*

*The fifteenth lahari.*

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Having placed a treasure in [my] hands, O Creator,*
*why have you deprived [me of it]?"*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the life of Rasika,
who delivered the whole circle of the earth![^2]

**2.** At this time Rasikendra, sitting in seclusion,
tells those close to him all that is in his heart:

**3.** "The command has definitely been given me to depart;
no longer shall I remain in this terrible [age of] Kali.[^3]

**4.** The great Kali will manifest on earth,
the yavanas will grow strong in the land of Bharata.

**5.** All people will abandon righteous conduct;
all such conduct I shall see no more.[^4]

**6.** When I came from Gopivallabhapur,
I petitioned before Govinda:

**7.** 'In the manifest [world] I have served You all these days;
now, showing mercy, take [me] to [Your] own abode.'[^5]

**8.** Having come to earth, [I] performed bhajan of Krishna:
for eighteen years [I] performed bhajan in secret.[^6]

**9.** Ceaselessly streams of tears flow from my eyes;
in the mood of separation from Krishna I wander from forest to forest.

**10.** Living at home, [I] gave no thought to home,
intoxicated with the rasa of love for Krishna, pondering [Him] on and on.

**11.** [I] served the guru, Krishna [and] the sadhus in secret;
apart from Krishna, I see nothing else with my eyes.[^7]

**12.** [I] cared nothing for bed, food or sleep;
for eighteen years [I] performed bhajan of Krishna.

**13.** At this time Shyamananda came:
the tantra, the mantra of Krishna [and] initiation he gave [me] in the ear.[^8]

**14.** In the bliss of love for Krishna [he] flooded the three worlds;
for twenty years [I] wandered together [with him].

**15.** [I] gladdened kings [and] subjects in land after land;
[I] made all lands brim over with love for Krishna.

**16.** Ceaselessly, in the bliss of Krishna, in the ocean of prema,
all enjoyed pastimes with Shyamananda.[^9]

**17.** Night and day [I] knew nothing but Krishna;
[I] preached service to the guru, Krishna [and] the sadhus.

**18.** Service to the ashvattha, the tulasi, the dhatri, to the guru [and] the brahmanas,
Sri Harivasara and the rest, service to the Vrajavasis.[^10]

**19.** All bhakti, all dharma that there is according to the shastras —
[I] accomplished it all together with Shyamananda.

**20.** [I] spread sankirtana in all lands,
wandering with Shyamananda from land to land.

**21.** Thus for nineteen years and seven months
[I] manifested Krishna's pastimes on earth.[^11]

**22.** Then Prabhu departed to his own abode;
knowing [me] to be a servant, he entrusted the service to me.

**23.** For twenty-four years and nine months [I] bore the burden of service:
by Shyamananda's command [I] accepted [it].[^12]

**24.** All righteous conduct, all the burden of service [I carried out];
the treasure of loving devotion to Krishna [I] preached.

**25.** In all lands [I] gave prema-bhakti to every house;
in the homes of all [I] performed the pastime of sankirtana.

**26.** In all houses, in every one, [I] arranged service to the sadhus;
sixteenfold service to the guru, Krishna, the sadhus [and] the brahmanas.[^13]

**27.** Processions, festival days, great festivals — year after year;
service to the guru, Krishna [and] the sadhus — every year.

**28.** The guru [and] the paratpara-[guru], and Chaitanya Gosani,
Advaita and the other mahajanas, and the godbrothers —

**29.** the aradhana of all [of them] every year
[I] celebrated with great festivals, in joy [and] delight.[^14]

**30.** Two [festivals] of twelve [days] [I] held in Govindapur,
three [festivals] of twelve [days] in Shyamasundarpur,

**31.** one [festival] of twelve [days] [I] held in the village of Kusharada:
these six [festivals] of twelve [days] [I] held in various places.

**32.** In Sri Gopivallabhapur — nineteen [festivals] of twelve [days]:
for nineteen years [I] tasted the rasa of Krishna's bliss.[^15]

**33.** The sixty-four limbs of bhakti [I] practised always;
all brimmed over with love for Krishna.

**34.** [By] the eighteen Puranas, the Sri Bhagavata and the rest
[I] revealed the bhajan of Krishna as prescribed.

**35.** Thus exclusive bhajan of Sri Krishna,
love for Krishna [I] revealed in the land of Bharata.[^16]

**36.** For twenty-four years and nine months in this way
[I] revealed the bhajan of Krishna, one thing after another.

**37.** Thus for sixty-two years and four months
[I] manifested the lila of the bhajan of Krishna.

**38.** In supreme bliss [I] passed the days;
[I] filled the whole world with love for Krishna.[^17]

**39.** [I] saw with my eyes [the world] brimming over with love for Krishna;
may I with these eyes look [on nothing else] any more.

**40.** Thus for sixty-two years [I] played;
now I shall go [and] see Krishna's own pastimes.[^18]

**41.** The command of Krishna has been given me: 'Come to [your] own abode.'
This is what I have told you in secret.

**42.** Birth after birth you all are those close to me;
therefore I have told [you] everything in detail.

**43.** Certainly I shall go to Krishna;
[only] one regret has remained in my mind.[^19]

**44.** [As] in the seventh wave of the *Shyamananda-rasarnava* —
a month-long great festival with the feeding of sweets, in joy;

**45.** to see the abode of Vrindavana once more;
the feeding of Vaishnavas at a temple on the bank of the Ganga —

**46.** this wish of mine has not been fulfilled.
The command to depart has been given — who will break it?[^20]

**47.** The command of Prabhu I never break:
certainly I shall go to Krishna.

**48.** For one who is born, the body certainly disappears;
birth after birth Bhagavan performs this lila.[^21]

**49.** From my heart I have told all this to you;
fulfil one request of mine with care.

**50.** To Nilachala, to the bank of the Ganga, to the abode of Vrindavana
I cannot set out quickly.

**51.** Now I am well — I say [this] to all;
[but] it is unknown [when] — be sure to perform [the rite] for the body.[^22]

**52.** Listen to my words, all you who are close to me:
make my samadhi at Gopala's place.[^23]

**53.** Chaitanya Gosani gave the command with [His own] mouth;
Gopala is my very own: this I know directly.[^24]

**54.** Madhavendra Puri and all the mahajanas
worshipped the feet of that Gopala in Remuna.

**55.** He is greatly subject to His devotees: stealing kshira,
He brought [it] and with love gave [it] to Madhavendra Puri.

**56.** Since then, calling [Him] Gopala Kshirachora,
all people serve [Him], seeing [His] manifestation.[^25]

**57.** My samadhi all will see in that place:
to all [of you] I have told the truth, sitting in seclusion."[^26]

**58.** Hearing this, all those close to him, in great dismay,
roll on the ground and weep, their eyes full of tears.[^27]

**59.** All the companions wept much;
he consoles everyone with sweet words.

**60.** "Do not take anything to heart, all you companions" —
embracing everyone by the neck, he weeps again and again.[^28]

**61.** Thus, having experienced the human lila,
he wished to conceal [it].[^29]

**62.** Earthquakes, a rain of blood, quarrels and strife
appeared in every home all over the earth.

**63.** Unfortunate the earth has now become:
Rasikendra has resolved to go to Goloka.[^30]

**64.** The earth wept much in secret —
all the various living beings, unmoving and moving.[^31]

**65.** The creatures of the waters and the forests all grew sorrowful;
in separation from Rasika grief arose in all.

**66.** Fear seized all the men and women;
all say: "Rasikendra will depart."

**67.** Thus, removing the burden of the earth's sorrow,
he spread the name of Krishna in the world.[^32]

**68.** Now I shall make known the account of [his] disappearance;
listen, everyone, to the *Rasika-mangala* with attention.[^33]

**69.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Uttara-vibhaga of the "Sri Rasika-mangala" ends the fifteenth lahari, called "The Telling to the Disciples of the Departure to Goloka, and the Command to Build the Samadhi at Gopala's Place in Remuna."*[^34]

[^2]: "The life of Rasika" (1) — *rasikera prāṇa*. "Who delivered the whole circle of the earth" — *sarbba abanimaṇḍala kaila paritrāṇa*. "In seclusion" (2) — *nigame* (lahari 14, note 13). "Those close to him" — *ātmagaṇe*.
[^3]: From 3 to 57 is Rasika's speech to those close to him. In it Rasika speaks of himself now in the first person (3, 5–7, 9 *bhrami*, 11 *nā dekhi*, 22 *more*, 39–57), now with third-person verbs (8, 10, 12, 14–21, 23–38: *kaila*, *bhajila*, *sādhila*), as of another (cf. lahari 14, 73, note 33); in the EN the subject is supplied as "[I]." "Has definitely been given" — *niścaya hailā ājñā*. "The terrible [age of] Kali" — *ghora kali* (lahari 14, 55, 60).
[^4]: "The great Kali" (4) — *mahākali*. Yavanas — foreigners, here apparently Muslims (lahari 14, note 22). "Righteous conduct" (5) — *dharmmabyabahāra*. "All such conduct I shall see no more" — *e sakala ācaraṇa nā dekhiba āra*: apparently he does not wish to witness the decline (4–5a); it can also be read "I shall see no more such (righteous) conduct."
[^5]: "When I came from Gopivallabhapur" (6) — *yakhana āsi' gopīballabhapura haite*. "Before Govinda" — *gobinda-sākṣāte*: apparently before the Deity of Govinda; where is not said (possibly in Govindapur, cf. 30). "In the manifest [world]" (7) — *prakaṭe*: in the manifest lila. "All these days" — *etadina*. "To [Your] own abode" — *nijadhāme*.
[^6]: "Having come to earth" (8) — *bhūmigata haiẏā*: from birth (lahari 14, 57, note 24). "Eighteen years" — *aṣṭādaśa batsara*; likewise 12 (*āṭhāra*): from birth to initiation from Shyamananda (13). The reckoning of years in 8–40: 18 years (8, 12) + 19 years 7 months with Shyamananda (21; in 14, "twenty years," a round number) + 24 years 9 months after his departure (23, 36) = 62 years 4 months (37; 40 and lahari 14, 56 — "sixty-two years"). This agrees with the years of Rasikananda's life (1590–1652). "In secret" — *saṅgopane*; likewise 11.
[^7]: "Flow" (9) — *galaẏe*. "In the mood of separation from Krishna" — *kṛṣṇera biraha bhābe*. "I wander from forest to forest" — *bhrami bane bane*. "Gave no thought to home" (10) — *gṛhajñāna nā karila cite*, lit. "kept no thought of home in mind." "Pondering [Him] on and on" — *bhābite bhābite*.
[^8]: "Cared nothing" (12) — *nā kaila ādara*. "Came" (13) — *karila gamane*. "The tantra, the mantra of Krishna [and] initiation he gave [me] in the ear" — *kṛṣṇa tantra mantra dīkṣā karāila karṇe*: initiation in which the guru speaks the mantra into the disciple's ear. On Rasika's meeting with Shyamananda and his initiation — the Purva-vibhaga; cf. the *Bhakti-ratnakara*, taranga 15.
[^9]: "Flooded the three worlds" (14) — *bhāsāilā tribhubana*, lit. "made [them] float." "Twenty years" — *biṃśati batsara* (note 6). "Wandered together [with him]" — *kaila saṅge biharaṇa*. "Gladdened" (15) — *pramoda karila* (lahari 14, note 19). "Made ... brim over" — *ḍhalaḍhala kaila* (lahari 14, note 18). "All" (16) — *sabe*: all [the companions]. "Enjoyed pastimes" — *karila bihāre*.
[^10]: "Night and day" (17) — *niśi diśi*. Ashvattha, tulasi, dhatri (18) — sacred plants that are worshipped (Purva, lahari 6). Sri Harivasara (*śrīharibāsara*) — "the day of Hari," Ekadashi, the fast in honour of the Lord. "Service to the Vrajavasis" — *brajabāsī sebā*: service to the residents of Vraja.
[^11]: "According to the shastras" (19) — *śāstramate*. "Accomplished" — *sādhila*. "Nineteen years and seven months" (21) — *uniśa batsara sāta māsa* (note 6). "Krishna's pastimes" — *kṛṣṇakhelā*.
[^12]: "Prabhu departed to his own abode" (22) — *prabhu bijaẏa karila nijadhāme*: Shyamananda's departure (Pashchima, lahari 13). "Knowing [me] to be a servant" — *bhṛtya jāni'*. "Twenty-four years and nine months" (23) — *cabbiśa bachara naẏa māsa*; likewise 36 (note 6). "The burden of service" — *sebābhāra*. "Accepted" — *karila aṅgīkāra*.
[^13]: "All righteous conduct" (24) — *yata dharmma ācaraṇa*. "In the homes of all" (25) — *sabāra mandire*: *mandira* — "house" or "temple." "Sixteenfold service" (26) — *ṣaṛ-daśa sebā* (so on the scan; *ṣaṛ-daśa* = *ṣoḍaśa*, "sixteen"): apparently worship with sixteen offerings (*ṣoḍaśa-upacāra*; Purva, lahari 8, note 23).
[^14]: "Processions, festival days, great festivals" (27) — *yātrā parbba mahotsaba*. The edition's footnote to 27 gives the variant *ārādhanā yātrā kaila*. "The guru [and] the paratpara-[guru]" (28) — *guru parātpara*: understood as the guru and the senior gurus of the succession (*parama-guru*, *parātpara-guru*); it can also be read "the guru, highest of the high." "Mahajanas" — *mahājana*. "Godbrothers" — *guru bhāi*. "Aradhana" (29) — *ārādhanā*: yearly worship on memorial days (Uttara, lahari 2). "In joy [and] delight" — *ānanda kutuhale*.
[^15]: "[Festival] of twelve [days]" (30–32) — *duẏādaśa*, *dbādaśa*: the yearly twelve-day aradhana of Shyamananda (lahari 5, notes 28–29; lahari 14, note 18). Here: two in Govindapur, three in Shyamasundarpur, one in Kusharada, six in all (31), and nineteen in Gopivallabhapur (32); in lahari 5, 49–51 — "five in three villages" and "twenty" in Gopivallabhapur. The total in both places is twenty-five, i.e. one festival a year for the almost twenty-five years after Shyamananda's departure (23). "Tasted the rasa of Krishna's bliss" (32) — *kaila kṛṣṇānandarasa*.
[^16]: "The sixty-four limbs of bhakti" (33) — *catuḥṣaṣṭi bhakti-aṅga*: the sixty-four practices of bhakti (*Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu* 1.2; CC, Madhya 22). "All brimmed over" — *ḍhalāḍhali hailā sabāẏa*. "As prescribed" (34) — *yathābidhi*. "Exclusive bhajan" (35) — *ananya ... bhajana*. "In the land of Bharata" — *bhāratabhubana*.
[^17]: "One thing after another" (36) — *eke eke*. "Sixty-two years and four months" (37) — *bāṣaṭṭi batsara cāri māsa* (so on the scan; note 6). "The lila of the bhajan" — *bhajanalīlā*. "Passed the days" (38) — *dina karila haraṇa*.
[^18]: "Saw" (39) — *dekhila* (third person). "May I with these eyes look [on nothing else] any more" — *e naẏane āra yena nā kari darśane*: having seen the world full of love for Krishna, he wishes to see nothing else (cf. lahari 14, 55). "Played" (40) — *kaila khelā*. "Krishna's own pastimes" — *kṛṣṇera nija līlā*: Krishna's eternal pastimes in His abode.
[^19]: "Come to [your] own abode" (41) — *esa nijadhāme* (cf. lahari 14, 75–77). "You" — *tomāre*, singular: apparently addressed to one of those close to him (cf. lahari 14, 72: "to you two"); in 42 again "you all." "Those close to me" (42) — *ātmagaṇa*. "Regret" (43) — *saṃśaya*, lit. "doubt," here an unfulfilled wish (44–46).
[^20]: *Shyamananda-rasarnava* (44) — *śyāmānanda rasārṇaba*, lit. "the ocean of Shyamananda's rasa": apparently the biography *Sri Shyamananda-rasarnava*, which the edition's preface ascribes to Krishnadasa, the author of the *Shyamananda-prakasha*; the "seventh wave" (*saptama-taraṅga*) is its chapter; its contents have not been checked. It can also be read figuratively: "in the seventh wave of the ocean of Shyamananda's rasa." The sense of 44 is apparently that Rasika wished to hold a month-long festival with the feeding of sweets, as described there. "In joy" — *raṅge*. "Once more" (45) — *āra bāra*. "The feeding of Vaishnavas at a temple on the bank of the Ganga" — *gaṅgātīre debālaẏe baiṣṇaba bhojana*. "Has not been fulfilled" (46) — *nā haila sāṅga*. "Who will break it?" — *ke karibe bhaṅga*.
[^21]: "Prabhu" (47) — here the Lord who gave the command. "For one who is born, the body certainly disappears" (48) — *janmile abaśya haẏa deha antarddhāna*: *antarddhāna* — "vanishing, disappearance." "Bhagavan performs this lila" — apparently the Lord, descending age after age, Himself displays birth and departure as lila (cf. *Bhagavad-gita* 4.6–9), and His associates come and go with Him. According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching the appearance and disappearance of the Lord and His pure devotees are not a compelled birth and end of the body under the rule of karma but their lila.
[^22]: "From my heart" (49) — *marame*. "Request" — *nibedana*. 50 — *śīghrate nā pāri tathā karite praẏāṇa*: apparently Rasika will not have time to go to the holy places (Nilachala, the Ganga, Vrindavana) where Vaishnavas wish to leave the body, and therefore asks for a samadhi in Remuna (52). "Now I am well" (51) — *ekṣaṇe āchiẏe bhāla*. "It is unknown [when]" — *nā jāniẏe*. "Be sure to perform [the rite] for the body" — *abaśya karaẏe deha-karmme*: *deha-karma* is understood as the funeral rite; the sense is approximate.
[^23]: "Samadhi" (52) — *samādhi*: a tomb, the place where a Vaishnava's body is laid to rest (the bodies of saintly Vaishnavas are usually not cremated but interred). "At Gopala's place" — *gopālera sthāne*: in Remuna (54; colophon). The edition's footnote gives the variant *gopīnātha*: the Deity of Remuna is known as Kshirachora Gopinatha (CC, Madhya 4); Rasikananda's samadhi is in Remuna, at the temple of Kshirachora Gopinatha.
[^24]: "Gave the command with [His own] mouth" (53) — *ājñā karilena mukhe*. "Gopala is my very own" — *nije gopāla āmāra*: the sense is unclear; understood as Rasika's words: Gopala is his own Lord; it can also be read as Sri Chaitanya's words ("Gopala is My very own"). What command Sri Chaitanya gave, and to whom, is not said (Rasika was born after Sri Chaitanya's disappearance). "Directly" — *pratyakṣe*.
[^25]: "Mahajanas" (54) — *mahājana*. Madhavendra Puri and Sri Chaitanya visited Remuna (CC, Madhya 4; Madhya 16). "Subject to His devotees" (55) — *bhaktabaśa*. According to CC, Madhya 4, Gopinatha hid for Madhavendra Puri a pot of kshira (sweet thickened milk) offered to Him, and in a dream told the pujari to give it to Puri; since then He has been called Kshirachora, "the thief of kshira." 55b — *dilena āniẏā preme mādhabendra purī*: understood with *mādhabendra purī* as the recipient; it can also be read "Madhavendra Puri brought [it] and with love gave [it]," but according to the CC it was the pujari who brought the kshira to Puri. Gopala Kshirachora — *kṣīracorā gopāla* (56). "[His] manifestation" — *prakāśa*: His manifest mercy (or the Deity Himself).
[^26]: "The truth" (57) — *tattba*. "In seclusion" — *nigame*. Here Rasika's speech ends; pdf 210 ends with the complete ॥57॥ and pdf 211 begins with ॥58॥ — no couplet carries over. The continuation of lahari 15 (58–69, the author's signature and the colophon) is on pdf 211 (the next portion).
[^27]: Continuation of lahari 15 (it begins on pdf 209, printed p. 163; title and 1–57 in files RM-4-15-001-057). pdf 210 ends with the complete ॥57॥, pdf 211 (printed p. 165) begins with the complete ॥58॥ — no couplet runs over. "In great dismay" — *mahātrāse*, lit. "in great fear." "Roll on the ground" — *bhūmite loṭāñā*.
[^28]: "Do not take anything to heart" — *kichu mane nā karibe*, lit. "keep nothing in mind": apparently "do not grieve [over my departure]." "Again and again" — *ghane ghane*.
[^29]: "Having experienced the human lila" — *mānuṣika līlā anubhaba*; the line may also be read "thus [ended] the manifestation of the human lila." "Conceal" — *saṅgopana karibāre*: to hide [the lila] from the world's eyes, i.e. to depart. "He wished" — *haila anurāga*, lit. "an inclination arose."
[^30]: 62–63 — ill omens before Rasika's departure; cf. the omens Yudhishthira sees when Krishna leaves the earth (*Bhagavata Purana* 1.14). "Unfortunate" — *hatabhāgī*. "Now" — *etadine*, lit. "by this day." Goloka is Krishna's highest abode. "Has resolved" — *mane kaila*.
[^31]: "In secret" — *nigame* (Uttara, lahari 14, note 13: "in seclusion"); here apparently "hiddenly, inwardly." "All the various living beings…" — *nānā jība sthābara jaṅgama jībagaṇe*: the subject of the second line is the same weeping ones.
