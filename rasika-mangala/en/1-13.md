# Purva-vibhaga. Lahari 13. Description of the Mood of Renunciation

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; after the wedding Rasika lives at home, relishing the "Bhagavata" in the company of devotees; in seclusion he chants the name of Hari, weeps, rolls on the ground and calls on Krishna as his only refuge; his family search for him in the forests and bring him home; he regards his home as poison and wishes to leave, taking renunciation; Achyuta urges him to stay, and Rasika replies that only Krishna, His name, His lilas, Vrindavana and devotion are real, and calls on his father to worship Krishna*

*The thirteenth lahari.*

*Raga Varadi — panchali metre.*[^1]

**1.** Glory to Shyamananda, the joy of all [the world]!
Be merciful to me,
so that "Rasika-mangala", a wave of bliss,
I may sing day after day.[^2]

**2.** Having celebrated the wedding and so on, Rasika-Murari
lives at home, [passing the time] in various ways.
In the taste of the "Bhagavata" he always delights
in the company of rasikas.[^3]

**3.** On some days, sitting in seclusion,
he constantly chants the name of Hari.
The tears from his eyes flow in a hundred streams;
he is aware of neither night nor day.[^4]

**4.** Saying "Krishna, Krishna!", rolling on the ground,
he weeps in agitation:
"Krishna is the Lord of my life; I have no other refuge";
his loosened hair he does not tie up.[^5]

**5.** In the rapture of feeling, with a faltering voice,
[he repeats] "Krishna, Krishna" again and again.
Over his whole body the hairs stand on end; the eight sattvika [bhavas]
arise as he remembers Krishna.[^6]

**6.** In a loud voice he speaks, humbly:
"Krishna is the treasure of my life.
Krishna is my mother, Krishna is my father,
Krishna is [my] caste and life.

**7.** Krishna is my Destroyer, Krishna is my Creator,
Krishna is my Maintainer.
Krishna is son and wealth; Krishna is friend;
Krishna is my Protector.[^7]

**8.** Apart from Krishna I have no one else;
I have offered [my] life to Him."
Saying such words, greatly distraught,
he cries aloud, "Krishna!"

**9.** With eyes full [of tears] he weeps incessantly,
remembering Krishna's qualities.
At meals and in sleep he knows nothing else,
meditating on Krishna's name.

**10.** He does not stay at home, he eats nothing,
he sits in seclusion all the time.
The household search [for him] incessantly,
roaming from forest to forest.

**11.** Wandering and wandering, the one sent sees a wonder:
Rasika rolling on the ground.
Lifting [him], taking him on his lap, [he] wipes with his cloth
[his] body, grey with dust.[^8]

**12.** Taking him home, bathing him,
[they feed him] dishes of six tastes and so on.
Thus day after day he plays in the forests,
wandering about, searching especially [hard].[^9]

**13.** Day after day Achyuta's son
performs lilas in various ways.
On nothing but Krishna does he fix his meditation;
he regards worldly life as false.[^10]

**14.** His own home he regards as poison,
[and so] his wife, children, kinsmen.
Abandoning everything, taking renunciation,
he wishes again and again to leave.[^11]

**15.** Learning [of this], Achyuta speaks, explaining everything:
"Listen, Rasika, my child!
Stay at home — I will give you everything,
whatever you want, whatever you wish."[^12]

**16.** Hearing his father's words, Rasika says:
"Listen, father, to [my] words.
All the wealth of worldly life I see to be false;
truth is in Krishna: [this is what is] certain.[^13]

**17.** True is the treasure — Krishna; true are Krishna's people;
true are Krishna's lilas;
true is Vrindavana, true are the gopis,
true is the son of Nanda.[^14]

**18.** True is sankirtana, true is the name of Krishna,
true is the guru, [true is] devotion to Krishna.
Listen, my father: this is the essence of the Vedas —
let everyone turn their mind to Krishna.

**19.** Worship Krishna, father — this is the view of the shastras;
Krishna is the life of all.
Brahma and the others, Narada, Shiva, Shuka, Indra
know no one but Krishna."[^15]

**20.** Hearing these words, all the people
think in their minds: "Krishna is the truth."
Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].[^16]

*Thus in the Purva-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the thirteenth lahari, called "Description of the Mood of Renunciation".*

[^1]: Raga Varadi (*barāṛī*) — panchali metre (*pāñcālī chanda*), i.e. tripadi, as in lahari 11 (note 1); each stanza is set in four lines in the edition and translated likewise. There is no ghosha and no mangala-shloka; the stanzas are numbered as in the edition. The title of the lahari follows the colophon on pdf 82 (checked against the scan): "iti śrīrasika-maṅgala pūrva-vibhāge vairāgya-bhāva-varṇana-nāma trayodaśa-laharī sampūrṇa" (*vairāgya-bhāva-varṇana*). Lahari 13 begins on a new page (pdf 81) after the colophon of lahari 12 on pdf 80.
[^2]: "The joy of all [the world]" — *akhila ānanda*. "A wave of bliss" — *ānanda-kallola*.
[^3]: Rasika-Murari is Rasikananda. "In the taste of the 'Bhagavata'" — *bhāgavata-rase*: so in the scan; the 1997 edition has *bhāgavata raṅge*, "in the delight of the 'Bhagavata'". "Rasikas" — *rasika-jana*, connoisseurs of rasa, devotees able to relish it.
[^4]: "In seclusion" — *nigame* (also 10): the meaning is taken from the context (lahari 12, note 68). "He is aware of neither night nor day" — *niśi diśi nāhi jāna*.
[^5]: "Rolling on the ground" — *bhūme gaṛi buli*. "Loosened" — *luṭhila*, according to the edition's gloss, "loose, dishevelled".
[^6]: "With a faltering voice" — *gada gada bhāṣe*. "The hairs stand on end" — *pulaka*. The eight sattvika bhavas (*aṣṭa sāttvika*) — lahari 6, note 13.
[^7]: "Destroyer, Creator, Maintainer" — *hartā*, *kartā*, *pālayitā*: the three functions of dissolution, creation and maintenance; that is, Krishna is everything to him. "Friend" — *bandhu-jita*: so in the scan and in the 1997 edition; possibly for *bandhu-hita*, "friend and benefactor"; the meaning of *jita* is unclear. "Protector" — *rakṣitā*.
[^8]: "The one sent" — *dūta*, a servant sent out to search. "With his cloth" — *nicole*, with his upper garment or wrap.
[^9]: Six tastes — lahari 3, note 13. "Plays in the forests" — *khele bane bane*; "wandering about, searching especially [hard]" — *cāhiyā bule viśeṣe*, apparently searching for Krishna; the rendering is tentative.
[^10]: "Son" — *bālā*, lit. "boy". "Does he fix his meditation" — *kare dhiyāna*, "meditates".
[^11]: "Home" — *sadana*, according to the edition's gloss, "house". "His wife, children, kinsmen" — *dārā-suta-bandhu-gaṇa*, a set expression for family ties (Rasika has no children yet). "Renunciation" — *vairāgya* (cf. the title of the lahari).
[^12]: "My child" — *bāchā*, an affectionate address to a son. "Whatever you wish" — *tomāra icchā*. Achyuta's conversation with his son continues on the next page (pdf 82, 16–20).
[^13]: "The wealth of worldly life" — *saṃsāra vaibhava*. "Truth is in Krishna: [this is what is] certain" — *satya kṛṣṇe paramāṇa*; *paramāṇa* (= *pramāṇa*) means "proof, measure"; possibly also "truth is proven [only] in Krishna".
[^14]: "Krishna's people" — *kṛṣṇa-jana*, His devotees. "The son of Nanda" — *nandera bālā*, lit. "Nanda's boy", that is, Krishna.
[^15]: Shuka is Shukadeva Gosvami, the narrator of the Bhagavata; Narada is the divine sage. "Know no one but Krishna" — *kṛṣṇa bine nāhi jāna*; the author uses *jāna* ("knows") the same way in 3 (note 4). Possibly also *jān* (Persian "life"): "without Krishna [they] have no life" (cf. the rhyme *prāṇa*, "life", in the first line).
[^16]: The author's signature (the second half of the stanza) is given in the common formula; here it is in tripadi metre and reads differently: "Shyamananda's feet are all the wealth of the son of Rasamaya" (*śyāmānanda-pada, sakala sampada, rasamayera nandane*); cf. lahari 5, note 14; lahari 11, note 15.
