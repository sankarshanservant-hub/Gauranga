# Purva-vibhaga. Lahari 1. Obeisances to the Vaishnavas

*Mangala-shlokas to Shyamananda, Rasikananda and the Gosvamis; a song of obeisance to the guru Shyamananda and his wives, to Sri Chaitanya, Nityananda, Advaita and their associates, to the holy places, to Shyamananda's disciples and Rasikananda's companions; the author's humility and the fruit of hearing the Rasika-mangala*

*Glory to Sri Sri Radha and Govinda!*
*Sri Sri Rasika-mangala.*
*Purva-vibhaga.*
*The first lahari.*
*Glory to Sri Sri Rasikananda-deva!*

**S1.** *How many, how many Puranas and Itihasas are there not in the world! Yet in them*
*nowhere did Krishna Himself write anything, except in the Gita-govinda.*
*Likewise among devotees nowhere is a mark in the form of a dot inscribed by one's own hand —*
*only for Sri Shyamananda did Srimati Radhika Herself joyfully make [it].*[^1][^2][^3]

**S2.** *A treasury of deep bliss, an ocean of grace, a treasury of the beauty of the three worlds,*
*a treasury and abode of the nectar of the rasa of full prema, a treasury of the Lakshmi of good fortune,*
*the one great treasury of the afflicted, a treasury of tenderness,*[^4] *a treasury of the pastimes of compassion —*
*all glory to Shyamananda, the treasury of mercy, whose mind is filled with sweetness!*

**S3.** *Bestower of deep bliss, exalter of rasa, a mine of the bhava of Sri Krishna,*
*giver of peace to the heart, destroyer of darkness, bringer of good to the host of devotees,*
*cutter of sorrow, prolonger of happiness, maker of the wealth of compassion,*
*deliverer of the lowly — to Prabhu Rasikananda, [like] the sun, I bow.*

**S4.** *O Sri Sanatana Prabhu, ocean of compassion!*
*O Rupa, whose merciful glance is the only [refuge] of the wretched!*
*O pair of Bhattas! O wise Raghunatha Dasa!*
*O Sri Jiva! Quickly show mercy to me, whose mind is dull.*[^5]

**S5.** *I worship the two lotus feet of Sri Shyamananda-deva,*
*by meditating on which people attain prema-bhakti for Hari.*

**S6.** *I worship the two feet of Rasikendra — the highest good,*
*the support of the essence of all sweetness, the highest festival.*[^6]

**S7.** *His face is the moon, his speech nectar, Bharati is in his throat;*
*his beauty is Lakshmi, his sweet smile is lovely — a row of kunda flowers;*
*his teeth are pearls, his pair of eyes a pair of bees, his arms two lotus stalks:*
*may people serve this Sri Murari like a chintamani gem.*[^7]

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^8]
*Ghosha: "Rama, glory [to You]! Govinda, Rama, glory [to You]!"*

**1.** *Song.* I worship the feet of Sri Guru — Shyamananda-raya,
by whose mercy attachment to Krishna arose.

**2.** By his mercy comes release from the bonds of worldly existence;
by his mercy all people became full of bhakti.

**3.** Such is Shyamananda: by the touch of his feet
the people of the three worlds float in the rasa of prema-bhakti.

**4.** To poor, lowly, miserable people he showed great mercy;
out of compassion he won over the three worlds.

**5.** The moon of the cowherd clan, shining forth in Utkala,[^9]
he destroyed the darkness of sin by giving prema-bhakti.

**6.** Prabhu is an ocean of bliss, a sea of mercy;
surpassing the three worlds, the beauty of his body is charming.

**7.** Prabhu is a sea of prema, an ocean of nectar;
fortunate in every way, a treasury of tens of millions and hundreds of thousands.

**8.** He dispels the torment of the three worlds;
at his compassionate words everyone's heart melts.

**9.** The crest-jewel of all sweetness is Shyamananda;
age after age he performs pastimes, having descended [into the world].[^10]

**10.** Be merciful to me, Prabhu, son of Durika:[^11]
I shall describe the glory of your dear devotee.

**11.** Then I worship the wives of the guru — the three Thakuranis,[^12]
by whose mercy I know prema-bhakti for Krishna.

**12.** Prema for Krishna embodied, bhakti itself —
she is praised in the world as a disciple of Hridayananda.[^13]

**13.** Show your favour, wife of Shyamananda,
so that with my mouth I may proclaim the glory of Rasika.

**14.** I worship Sri Krishna Chaitanya, Svayam Bhagavan,
who gave prema-bhakti to all living beings.

**15.** Descending age after age, the son of Shachi,
destroying the wicked, protects the saintly.

**16.** Seeing the dreadful [age of] Kali, full of compassion for the living beings,
He took birth in Navadvipa, bringing His associates and companions with Him.

**17.** Sri Chaitanya-raya, to whom the destitute are dear as life,
on whom Brahma, Shiva and Purandara meditate —

**18.** be merciful to me, son of Jagannatha:
I shall describe something of the Rasika-mangala.

**19.** Then I worshipped Nityananda-Balarama,
whose incomparable beauty surpasses tens of millions of Kamadevas.

**20.** The poor, the lowly — every single person, down to the chandalas —
by giving them bhakti for Krishna He delivered the three worlds.

**21.** Bowing down, I worship Shachi and Jagannatha;
I worship Hadai Pandita and Padmavati.

**22.** I worship Lakshmi and Vishnupriya, the two Thakuranis;
I worship Vasudha and Jahnava, the wives of the elder brother.[^14]

**23.** With devotion I worship Advaita Acharya,
by whose mercy bhakti for Chaitanya arises.

**24.** With joy I have now worshipped Sita Thakurani,
bhakti itself in the descent of Sri Chaitanya.

**25.** I worship Sri Achyutananda, the son of Advaita;
I worship all the household of Sita Thakurani.

**26.** I worship Virabhadra-raya, radiant in body,[^15]
whose manifestation is famed throughout the earth.

**27.** Together with their households I worship all the companions,
so that Rasika's glory may shine forth in my heart.

**28.** With joy I worshipped Ramai and Sundarananda,
whose greatness is manifest on earth.

**29.** I worship Gauridasa Thakura — Subala-raya;
everyone sings of him as dear to Nityananda.

**30.** The whole world calls him an intimate friend (priya-narma-sakha);
in his line the Vaishnava Shyamananda appeared.[^16]

**31.** If that Prabhu grants [me] his mercy,
then I shall spread the Rasika-mangala.

**32.** With respect I worship Uddharana Datta;
I worship Premeshvara, a follower of Chaitanya.[^17]

**33.** With humble entreaty I worship Murari Thakura;
with devotion I worshipped Kamalakara.

**34.** I worship the two, Purushottama and Manohara;
I worshipped the feet of Kaliya Krishnadasa.[^18]

**35.** I worshipped the eight Giris, most dear to Chaitanya;
I worshipped the eight Puris, the elders of all.

**36.** I worshipped the eight Bharatis, great mahajanas,
who had Vishvambhara accept sannyasa.[^19]

**37.** I worship the eight boys, followers of Chaitanya;
I worship the sixty-four mahantas, all the companions.[^20]

**38.** With great joy I worship the guru's line;
I worship Balarama, Bada Thakura, Haridasa.[^21]

**39.** I worship Govinda Gosvami, Thakura Mahesha;
especially I worship Durlabha Thakurani.[^22]

**40.** I worship Hridayananda, who brought light to the line;[^23]
I worshipped the feet of all [his] servants and all the household.

**41.** Together with their associates and companions I worship [them], the abode of all virtues;
I worship all the Vaishnavas, bowing down [to them].

**42.** I worship all the brahmanas, the best of all sannyasis;
I worship the seven seas, the earth with all that moves and does not move.

**43.** Among these I joyfully worshipped the holy places,
by hearing of which bhakti for Krishna becomes manifest.

**44.** I worship Sri Vrindavana, Madana-gopala,
Sri Govinda, Gopinatha, Sri Banka-vihara.[^24]

**45.** I worship Radha-vallabha, the graceful Thakura;[^25]
I worship Kalindi-Yamuna, all of Vraja-pura.

**46.** I worship Gokula, Mathura, Sri Keshava-raya,
by hearing of whom all sins are destroyed.[^26]

**47.** I worship Yadava-raya, the lord of Gokula;
I worshipped Gopala-raya, the holder of Govardhana.

**48.** Then I worshipped Dvaraka and Ranchhor-raya:[^27]
that abode of Krishna is greater than Vaikuntha.

**49.** I worship Badarikashrama and Nara-Narayana;
I worship the Gandaki, the Gomati, Naimisharanya.

**50.** I worship Prabhasa, Pushkara, the tirtha of the Godavari;
I worship the Narmada, the Sarasvati, the Sindhu, the Kaveri.

**51.** I worshipped Ayodhya and Kurukshetra, holy abodes;
I worship Setubandha and every place where Hari [resides].

**52.** I worshipped Hastinapura, the home of the Pandavas,
where Sri Krishna stays for the sake of His devotees.

**53.** I worship Kanchi and Avantika, most holy places:
age after age the seven cities are the abodes of Hari.[^28]

**54.** I worship Sri Purushottama, the lord of Nilachala,[^29]
Gaya, the Ganga, Varanasi, Prayaga and the rest.

**55.** I worship the Bhagirathi and Navadvipa, the great place,
the abode of Sri Chaitanyachandra Mahaprabhu.

**56.** I worship Gangasagara, famed throughout the world;
I worship holy Navadvipa and Tamralipta.[^30]

**57.** I worshipped the three places — heaven, earth and the netherworld;
among them I worshipped all the holy abodes.

**58.** Be merciful to me, all of you — I bow down [to you] —
so that I may expound the Rasika-mangala.

**59.** Briefly I shall speak of two or four of the elders;
by Rasika's mercy I worship the feet of them all.

**60.** I worship Gopiramana, the adhikari [of the service] of Chaitanya;
I worship Sri Rama Thakura, possessed of every virtue.[^31]

**61.** I worshipped the two, Krishnananda and Dvarika;
I worship Achyuta and Bhavani, dear to Krishna.[^32]

**62.** I worship Prasada Thakura and Balaramadasa,
who lived together with Shyamananda's younger brother.[^33]

**63.** Manohara, full of bhava, renounced, devoted to Krishna;
I worship the feet of Kishora the teacher.

**64.** I worship Sri Tulasidasa, the guru of sankirtana,
who from his birth lived together with Rasika.[^34]

**65.** At the festival of sankirtana he receives the first worship:
Rasika honours him with cloth and ornaments.

**66.** When Rasika cannot manage to water Tulasi,
he offers [the water] at the feet of Tulasi[dasa] and drinks it with a joyful heart.[^35]

**67.** I worship all the elders, holding their feet,
so that I may sing Rasika's praise at every moment.

**68.** I worship the feet of all the Vaishnavas of Shyamananda;
[I name] ten or twenty of the chief ones — this is a brief account.

**69.** Do not hold against me any fault of order:
in the course of the book I shall tell of everything.

**70.** I worship Yadavendradasa, the elder [brother] of Nityananda;[^36]
I worship Sri Kishora and Sri Balakadasa.

**71.** Vaishnavadasa, Gopinathadasa, Manohara;
I worship Damodara-prabhu, the servant of Krishna.[^37]

**72.** His voice choked with prema, his eyes filled with tears,
in the bliss of Krishna he weeps constantly night and day.

**73.** Damodara knows nothing but Krishna;
he made very many disciples who took the one sole shelter.

**74.** His union with Rasika is undivided;
to such a Damodara, a jewel among men, I bow.

**75.** With joy I worshipped the brahmana Govindadasa;
I worshipped Gopala, Balabhadra, Haridasa.[^38]

**76.** I worshipped Govinda, the mahajana of Vrindavana;
I worship the feet of Shyamasundara and Uddhava.

**77.** I worship the two, Shyamadasa and Jagannatha;
I worship the feet of Kaviraja Balabhadra.

**78.** With devotion I worship Chintamanidasa;
I worship Sri Radhavallabhadasa, pure of mind.

**79.** Anantadasa, Raghunathadasa of Mathura;
I worship the brahmana Padmanabha and Gangadharadasa.

**80.** I worship Sri Radhamohana, the brahmana Shirikara;[^39]
with respect I worship merciful Kanudasa.

**81.** With reverence I worshipped Govindadasa and Bhudhara;
I worship Radhacharana and Purushottama, the best of brahmanas.

**82.** I worship Ananta, Radhavallabha, Radhadhara,
Gokula, Krishna-smarana, the brahmana Damodara.[^40]

**83.** I worship Sri Shyamaranginidasa, the best of sadhus;
I worship Sri Shyamatarangi, a brahmana, the best of sadhus.

**84.** Abhaya, Ramagovinda — I worshipped them all;
Ananda, Mathurashyama, pure in body.

**85.** I worship Madhuvanadasa, a companion of Krishna;
each one of them has hundreds and hundreds of disciples, very many.

**86.** I worship Sri Anandananda, a worthy brahmana;
I worshipped the descendants of Divakara, kind of heart.[^41]

**87.** With great joy I worship Sri Mathuradasa of the gopas;
with joy I worshipped Mathuradasa of Gauda.[^42]

**88.** Jagannathadasa, Radhavallabha, Bhudhara,
Ramadasa, Sri Chaitanyadasa, the best of brahmanas —

**89.** their feet I worship, rejoicing;
I worship Sri Krishnadasa and Gopaladasa.

**90.** I worship Mukunda-bhupati, a servant of Shyamananda,
whose poetic gift is manifest in all four directions.[^43]

**91.** I worship Sri Keshava Shiromani, greatly steadfast;
I worship Sachuda Sri Jagannatha, full of sattva.[^44]

**92.** With joy I worshipped Bhrigu and Sri Purushottama;
I worshipped Bhudeva and Sri Chaitanyadasa.

**93.** I worship the vaidya Sri Gopaladasa, the fortunate;
I worship the brahmanas Shyama, Rasika, Govinda.

**94.** Madana-mohanadasa, the brahmana Gadadhara;
I worship the brahmana Balabhadra and Vamshi, the best of brahmanas.

**95.** I worship the brahmana Purushottama, greatly fortunate,
for whom Prabhu Shyamananda is caste, wealth and life.

**96.** I worship the brahmana Damodara, a servant of Shyamananda,
whose true abode is at Shyamananda's holy feet.

**97.** With all his family he gave himself over to Shyamananda;
he knows nothing but guru, Krishna and the sadhus.

**98.** I worship Sri Mathuradasa, a great mahajana:
all [his] wealth and people he surrendered to Shyamananda.

**99.** A dear disciple of Shyamananda, prema-bhakti embodied —
for him Prabhu Shyamananda is family, character and caste.

**100.** The brahmana Haridasa, Vanamali, the best of brahmanas,
Radhakrishna, Dharambara, the vaishya Narayana;

**101.** I worshipped Gauranga, Purushottama, Madhava;
I worship the brahmana Gopala, Manohara, Bhudeva.

**102.** I worship Govinda Bhattacharya, who lives in Vanga:
in Vanga he made [the glory of] Shyamananda manifest.[^45]

**103.** I worship Sri Kishoradasa and Kanudasa,
Sri Mathuradasa of the gopas, Rasamayadasa.[^46]

**104.** I worship Sri Gaurangadasa and Manoharadasa;
I worship all the Shyamanandis, wherever each one lives.

**105.** I worship Nilambaradasa and Sri Ananta-raya;
then I worshipped the worthy Sanatana.

**106.** With joy I worshipped Thakura Vishnudasa,
who constantly delights [in the company of] Rasika.

**107.** Then I worshipped Shyamadasi Thakurani,
Rasika's wife, prema-bhakti embodied.[^47]

**108.** A disciple of Shyamananda, devoted to her husband, mother of the world;
from her birth her service to Govinda has been known to the world.

**109.** Then I worship Sri Devaki, Rasika's daughter,
a disciple of Shyamananda: the eldest sister, she is [our] mother.

**110.** I worship Radhananda Thakura, Rasika's son,
a dear disciple of Shyamananda, endowed with every virtue.[^48]

**111.** Absorbed in Krishna, his heart is enchanted by the rasa of prema;
the streams from his eyes drench his whole body.

**112.** He is full of all the shastras, a great scholar,
skilled in music, known to the world.

**113.** I worshipped Sri Krishna Bhanja-deva, the maharaja,
who with firm faith serves and worships Shyamananda's feet.[^49]

**114.** A dear disciple of Shyamananda, the moon that illumines his line,
in whose land is the bliss of great festivals of service to Krishna.

**115.** A king wholly devoted [to Krishna], known to the world,
dedicated to Hari-nama, always practising [it].

**116.** In his heart dwell the sixty-four limbs of bhakti;[^50]
everyone proclaims him devoted to the brahmanas.

**117.** Is it Parikshit, Ambarisha, Sanaka and his brothers
who took birth in the form of bhakti for Krishna? He has won renown.[^51]

**118.** By the strength of his piety the best of kings, mighty and formidable:
hostile kings come and become servants at his feet.

**119.** Such a king, a devotee of Krishna — be merciful to me:
I shall sing Rasika's glory, sitting steadfast.

**120.** With devotion I worship Krishnanandadasa,
who has no other refuge than Shyamananda.

**121.** I worship the chaste Vrindavati, Rasika's daughter,
humble and patient, whom the world praises.

**122.** With joy I worshipped Krishnagati, pure of mind,
Rasika's middle son, renowned in the world.

**123.** Mad with prema for Krishna, he does not know day from night;
such is Krishnagati, whose life's treasure is Krishna.

**124.** Rasika's youngest son is Radhakrishnadasa,
dear to Shyamananda; [Krishna] is manifest in his pure heart.[^52]

**125.** Rasika's son, filled with the bliss of prema,
kind to all living beings — I worship those feet.

**126.** Prasada, Gopala, Govinda, Ramadasa,
Madhava, Kishora, Radhamohana — those servants;

**127.** and I worshipped the feet of Purushottamadasa;
the gopa Ankura — both are servants of Sri Shyamananda.[^53]

**128.** Krishnadasa Dariya, Radhavallabhadasa —
the servants among the Shyamanandis cannot be counted.[^54]

**129.** I worship Jagannatha, the servant of Achyuta's son;[^55]
I worship Ananta, Sridhara and Kashinatha.

**130.** Then I worshipped Nilambara Shirikara,
Kapileshvara, Gangadasa — all the companions.

**131.** I worship Sri Shyamagopala, a great mahajana;
I worshipped the two, Chintamani and Vihari.

**132.** Dinashyama, Ramakrishna, Shyama, Manohara,
Gopinatha, Vaidyanatha — all the companions.

**133.** The Shyamanandis are numberless — how many names shall I take?
One by one I bow down to them all.

**134.** Be merciful to me, all of you, accept me:
I shall proclaim something of Rasika's glory.

**135.** Rolling at his feet, I worship my father Rasamaya;
then I worshipped my mother, devoted to her husband.

**136.** Husband and wife, both, and five sons —
all took shelter at Rasika's feet.[^56]

**137.** I worshipped my paternal uncle, Vamshi Mathuradasa,
who is renowned among the first Shyamanandis.[^57]

**138.** I worship with devotion all the elders,
pure of mind, in my mother's line and my father's line.

**139.** In the line of the gopas we all were born;
Shyamananda's two feet are [our] family, character and caste.

**140.** Gopijana-vallabha, Haricharanadasa,
Madhava, Rasikananda, Kishoradasa —

**141.** the five brothers, sons of Sri Rasamaya,
whose caste, wealth and life is Achyuta's son.[^58]

**142.** Vallabha's son is the renowned Radhavallabha,
whose mother and father is Rasikendra, the crest-jewel [of devotees].[^59]

**143.** Together with their families they are Rasika's servants;
they constantly abide with Rasika.

**144.** As once upon the Pandavas and other poor, suffering people
Krishna kept His watchful gaze without cease,[^60]

**145.** [so,] by the power of the feet of Rasika, the devotee of Krishna,
no sorrow touches [them] or their families.

**146.** Apart from Rasikendra they know nothing of all this —
puja, meditation, austerity, japa, the eightfold sadhana.[^61]

**147.** With their whole being they serve Rasika;
regarding them as servants, he protects them all.

**148.** As Krishna, the friend of the poor, is a shelter and a shield,
so still more is [His] devotee a shelter, [like] one's own brother.[^62]

**149.** Thus the whole [of our] family abides at Rasika's feet
constantly — be it night, be it day.

**150.** Rasika's paternal uncle, Tulasi Thakura,
every year urgently commands [me]:[^63]

**151.** "Seeing the prema for Krishna throughout the land of Utkala,
describe Rasika's glory!"

**152.** Prabhu is embarrassed on hearing his own virtues —
fearing that embarrassment, I did not make [it] known.[^64]

**153.** At that time Rasika-shekhara of Berhapal,
laughing playfully, answered everyone:[^65]

**154.** "There will be a Shyamanandi who is so fortunate —
[who it is,] all the Shyamanandis devoted to Krishna decide.[^66]

**155.** So that all people may sing of this whole community,
it is good if someone describes [it] thus."

**156.** Having received that encouragement, I took the command upon my head,
quickly honouring Rasika's feet, [placing them] upon my head.

**157.** Remembering the lotus feet of Sri Krishna,
I shall describe something of Rasika's glory.

**158.** Gopijana-vallabha, a servant of Sri Shyamananda,
has dared to make something known.

**159.** The glory of a devotee is a boundless, fathomless ocean:
Brahma, Shiva, Indra and the others cannot find its limit.

**160.** The greatness of a devotee is many times greater than Krishna['s]:
[Krishna] constantly goes about [after His devotees] in hope of the dust of their feet.[^67]

**161.** Such is Rasika-Murari, most dear to Krishna:
even with ten million mouths I could not tell his virtues.

**162.** I am utterly poor and lowly, wretched, in a sorry plight;
whatever is spoken — it is Rasika, the lord of [my] life, who speaks it.

**163.** Who can tell of the boundless ocean of [his] pastimes?
Whatever appears to me by Shyamananda's mercy,

**164.** I, relying on his command, shall make known —
something of Rasika-deva's holy glory.

**165.** Without intelligence, without learning, I am of wicked mind;
what could I know of Rasika-deva's holy glory?

**166.** All the Shyamanandis devoted to Krishna commanded me
to proclaim Rasika-deva's glory.

**167.** Being a tiny person, I am very bold:
show favour, all of you, that [my] heart's wish may be fulfilled.

**168.** I shall describe something — a description of [his] nature —
as the son of Achyuta, like a magician, makes [me] dance.[^68]

**169.** Do not mind any faults in the order [of the narrative];
may sadhus and learned people listen with affection.

**170.** I shall tell something — the Rasika-mangala;
in the three worlds fortunate people will hear [it].

**171.** Devotees of Sri Krishna, each abiding in his own place,
hear the Sri Rasika-mangala day and night.

**172.** To hear it is a joy to the ears, to sing it is full of rasa;
merely by hearing it, one easily crosses [the ocean of] worldly existence.

**173.** To destroy the terrible darkness of Kali, the impenetrable gloom,
[he] appeared in the form of a devotee.[^69]

**174.** As by hearing Krishna's qualities one crosses [the ocean of] worldly existence,
so merely by hearing a devotee's qualities one overcomes the three times.

**175.** Whoever even once hears this with his ears,
in that very moment millions upon millions of great sins are destroyed.

**176.** Release from all bonds and prema-bhakti come [to one]
who hears the holy praise — the Rasika-mangala.

**177.** To the poor [it gives] wealth, to the childless a son;
hearing the Rasika-mangala takes away grief and sorrow.

**178.** Supreme, exclusive bhakti arises at once [in one]
who reads [it] with love.

**179.** All living beings suffer from the bite of Time —
recite, all of you, the mantra — the Rasika-mangala!

**180.** Whoever reads or hears [it] is not devoured by the wheel of Time;
at once the noose of the bondage of worldly existence is destroyed.

**181.** Effortlessly [he gains] wife, sons and every strength,
wealth, people, prema-bhakti, the highest good.

**182.** Let no one disregard [it], saying, "It is composed in the common tongue" —
a venomous snake never at any time gives up its poison.[^70]

**183.** Listen attentively, all of you, leaving other talk;
having heard, destroy the pain of the bondage of worldly existence.

**184.** Especially for the Shyamanandi Vaishnavas [this] is life:
Rasikendra-chudamani is their caste, life and wealth.

**185.** Those who hear his qualities with faith
attain Rasika's feet without delay.

**186.** In Patala the Nagas hear [it];
in heaven the devas hear, on earth the sadhus.[^71]

**187.** Sing with your own mouths the qualities of Krishna's devotee:
the four Vedas sing that Bhagavan is subject to His devotees.[^72]

**188.** O you most wise, all of you, do not impute any faults [to me];
setting aside [thought of] all faults, listen with joy.[^73]

**189.** Women, men and others, be they children or old people —
whoever hears [it] with whatever desire,

**190.** merely by hearing, the desire is completely fulfilled:
wealth, grain, sons, grandsons, fame, a home full of prosperity.

**191.** Release from all bonds [and] prema-bhakti come
merely by hearing the praise of Rasika.

**192.** The Purva-vibhaga is supremely full of rasa:
having heard it, all living beings, cross over [the age of] Kali.

**193.** Hear the Rasika-mangala, all friends:
without delay you will obtain the treasure of prema-bhakti for Krishna.

**194.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Purva-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the first lahari, called "Obeisances to the Vaishnavas".*

[^1]: The mangala-shlokas at the beginning of the lahari are numbered separately in the edition (॥ 1 ॥–॥ 7 ॥), and from the song (after the raga and the ghosha, at the word "গীত") the numbering starts again from 1 and runs to the end of the lahari (॥ 194 ॥, pdf 44). To keep the numbers distinct, the shlokas are labelled S1–S7; the numbers 1, 2… are the edition's.
