# Dakshina-vibhaga. Lahari 11. The Curse Laid on Shyamadasi for an Offence in Krishna's Service

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; after the Dola-yatra Harabola, the yavana ruler, seeing the festival, calls Shyamananda Narayana, holds a three-day festival of sankirtana at Alamganj in Medinipur and worships him as Ishvara; Shyamananda accepts many disciples in Dharenda; at the request of Rasamaya, Vamshi and Bhima Shirikara he enters married life and in Greater Balarampur takes as his wife Shyamapriya, the daughter of Jagannatha; Rasika returns home; one day Shyamadasi, while preparing vegetables for the offering to the Deity, leaves her work to feed her crying first-born son Vrajananda, and Rasika in anger says that her sons will not remain with her; so six sons one after another leave this world, and by his mercy three remain — Radhananda, Krishnagati and Radhakrishnadasa; Hridayananda comes to Dharenda and orders Shyamananda, Rasika and Damodara to give everyone in Utkala Chaitanya's prema-bhakti and the name Hare Krishna*

*The eleventh lahari.*

*Raga Mallar.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Crusher of the Daityas, enemy of the Daityas."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, mercy incarnate!
Giving prema-bhakti, he delivered the living beings.

**2.** Then, having celebrated the Dola-yatra, Mahashaya
bade farewell there to all the Vaishnavas.

**3.** At this time the yavana ruler of that region,
named Harabola — a villain, a very wicked man —[^2]

**4.** came and saw the dola festival with his own eyes;
seeing this wonder, the yavana ruler says:

**5.** "Not a man — this great person is Narayana!
I have beheld his feet."[^3]

**6.** Hearing [this], Shyamananda says to Rasika:
"Come, let us go and see the yavana ruler."

**7.** Prabhu went [and] saw the yavana ruler;
seeing [him], the villainous yavana showed [him] great honour.[^4]

**8.** That very yavana says to Shyamananda:
"Hold a great festival here; listen, good sir.

**9.** All the provisions I shall give — [you] have no concern at all;
all the Hindu adhikaris I shall send off [with gifts]."[^5]

**10.** "Go to the house and take care of all the provisions,
so that the sadhus may eat happily."

**11.** In Medinipur there is the place Alamganj —
there at last the great festival was arranged.[^6]

**12.** For three days [and] three nights, in great bliss,
sankirtana [and] the cry of "Hari" sounded on all four sides.

**13.** That very yavana was greatly delighted;
without pause he watches the sankirtana.

**14.** Great faith arose in him towards Shyamananda:
calling [him] Ishvara, he firmly began to worship [him].[^7]

**15.** Such is the might of Shyamananda [and] Rasika:
even a yavana chants his name.[^8]

**16.** From there Shyamananda set out
and, coming to Dharenda, arrived [there].

**17.** Shyamananda accepted many disciples there:
Chintamani, Madhuvana, Mathura, Mukunda,

**18.** Shyamasundara and the fortunate Narasimha,
Kanudasa, Hiradhara, the fortunate Kanu,[^9]

**19.** Uddhava, Akrura and others — how many names shall I take?
Many in that village Shyamananda made [his] disciples.[^10]

**20.** Then Rasamaya, Vamshi, Bhima Shirikara,
joining their palms, say to Shyamananda:[^11]

**21.** "Fulfil the word of us all;
we make a request — if you will not reject it.

**22.** You have long wandered through the holy places;
now, Prabhu, live a household life for a while.

**23.** If you give the order, I shall find [you] a bride."
Hearing [this], Shyamananda became somewhat afraid.[^12]

**24.** "Very well — as you all decide."
From there Shyamananda set out.

**25.** At that very place he took leave of Rasika
[and] went to Greater Balarampur.[^13]

**26.** There lives the fortunate Jagannatha;
he gave his daughter to Shyamananda.

**27.** Her name is Shyamapriya, she is very beautiful;
in beauty [and] virtues a portion of Lakshmi, enchanting the world.[^14]

**28.** Joyfully holding a festival of sankirtana,
Shyamananda married Shyamapriya.

**29.** Having married, [he] sent the bride to Dharenda;
for some days she stayed in Chintamani's house.[^15]

**30.** Then Shyamananda came to Radhanagar;
there for some days he first set up [his own] house.[^16]

**31.** Rasikendra then went to his own place;
without pause he serves guru, Krishna and the sadhus.

**32.** Every day Shyamadasi Thakurani at home
serves the Thakura lovingly, without pause.

**33.** One day, for the Thakura's bhoga,
the mother sat down diligently to cut the vegetables.[^17]

**34.** At that time her son was lying in the cradle;
the son began to cry, distressed by hunger.[^18]

**35.** His name was Vrajananda, his form most charming:
the first son, Rasika's excellent child.

**36.** Hearing the crying, the mother grew anxious,
left the vegetables, came and took her son on her lap.

**37.** She gives her own son milk to drink;
at that time Rasika arrived at that place.

**38.** Seeing with his own eyes that no one was cutting the vegetables,
seeing the delay in the bhoga, with angry words —

**39.** in anger Rasika said: "Listen, Shyamadasi!
Leaving Krishna's service, what are you doing, sitting [here]?"

**40.** Shyamadasi said to Rasika:
"The child was crying hard because of hunger.

**41.** When I have given [him] milk, I shall make the offering."
In anger Rasika says: "Listen, listen again and again!

**42.** In ignorance you have left the service of Krishna, the lord of life;
taking the son of maya on your lap, you have sat down with care.

**43.** Leaving the service of Krishna, the lord of my life,
you are bewildered in delusion because of maya.

**44.** Leaving love for Krishna, you have given great love to your son."
In great anger Rasikendra says to her:[^19]

**45.** "Whenever Krishna's service is broken even for a moment,
however many sons you have, they will not remain with you.[^20]

**46.** The one whom you raise without offence —
that son will remain on earth: I have told [you] the reason."

**47.** All were astonished on hearing those words:
by the word of [his] order six sons were struck down.[^21]

**48.** Service to guru, Krishna and the sadhus goes on [there] without pause;
whenever, even for a moment, [Rasika] sees a lapse with his own eyes,

**49.** then he orders that son to go;
thus, year after year — six sons.[^22]

**50.** Parted from her sons, Shyamadasi Thakurani,
the mother of the world, grieved greatly.

**51.** Then Prabhu, out of mercy — the jewel of compassion and virtues —
out of mercy kept three sons on earth:[^23]

**52.** Radhananda, Krishnagati, Radhakrishnadasa;
without pause they delight in the bliss of Krishna.[^24]

**53.** Before guru, Krishna and the sadhus they are free of offence;
they are embodiments of prema, most pure in mind.

**54.** Thus in Sri Gopivallabhapur
Rasika-chandra ever joyfully abides.[^25]

**55.** At that time Sri Hridayananda Adhikari,
Prabhu, came and arrived at the town of Dharinda.[^26]

**56.** Hearing of the manifest [greatness] of Shyamananda [and] Rasika,
he came to see [them], bringing his associates.

**57.** Staying at Dharenda, he quickly sends people
to bring Shyamananda, Rasika, Damodara.[^27]

**58.** Hearing the order, the three Prabhus came in haste
[and] received the darshan of Adhikari Thakura.

**59.** Seeing [this] circle, Sri Hridayananda was pleased;
embracing [them], he ordered: "Listen, Shyamananda:

**60.** Chaitanya's prema-bhakti, the name 'Hare Krishna' —
give them to all living beings in Utkala.[^28]

**61.** Seeing this circle, I have become very glad:
without pause make devotion to Krishna manifest."

**62.** He showed great mercy to Rasika;
for some days he stayed in the company of all.

**63.** Then Adhikari Prabhu went to his own land;
Shyamananda especially presented [him] with many gifts.

**64.** Having received great joy, Sri Hridayananda departed;
seeing [him] off, Shyamananda went a good distance.[^29]

**65.** Having taken leave [of him], all quickly returned
[and] arrived at the house of Rasamaya [and] Vamshi.[^30]

**66.** In Rasamaya's house — Shyamananda's meal;
for some days the three stayed there.

**67.** Day by day he showed mercy to all living beings.
I have described a little of the Rasika-mangala — [his] nature.[^31]

**68.** Listen, all, attentively, leaving other talk;
by listening, destroy the pain of the fear of worldly existence.

**69.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Dakshina-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the eleventh lahari, called "The Curse Laid on Shyamadasi for an Offence in Krishna's Service."*[^32]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the lahari's heading ("একাদশ-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 11 begins on the lower half of pdf 114, after the colophon of lahari 10 (files RM-2-10-001-073). Raga Mallar (*mollāra*, so in the scan); the ghosha is the same as in lahari 4 (note 1). There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 116 (checked against the scan): "কৃষ্ণসেবাপরাধে শ্যামদাসী-প্রতি অভিশাপপ্রদান-নাম একাদশ-লহরী" (*kṛṣṇasebāparādhe śyāmadāsī-prati abhiśāpa-pradāna*). The summary line covers the whole lahari (1–7 from the scan; 8–69 from the OCR of the full edition, provisionally): Alamganj (*ālamagañja*), Shyamapriya (*śyāmapriẏā*), Vrajananda (*brajānanda*), Krishnagati (*kṛṣṇagati*) should be checked when those couplets are translated.
[^2]: The yavana ruler (*yabana rājana*) — a Muslim ruler or nobleman of "that region" (of Barakola). Harabola — *harabolā*, so in the scan; in the edition's table of contents — "হরিবোলা" (*haribolā*). Not identified. "Named" — *bali*, lit. "called".
[^3]: These are the yavana's words; "this great person" (*mahājana*) is apparently Shyamananda (cf. 6–8). Thus the author exalts Shyamananda (cf. Dakshina, lahari 8, note 3, on Rasika being honoured as Ishvara); according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching Shyamananda is a devotee and servant of Radha, not the Lord Himself (Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11).
[^4]: "Prabhu" — Shyamananda, apparently together with Rasika (6). "Showed great honour" — *bahu mānya kailā*. Couplet 7 ends at the bottom of pdf 114; pdf 115 begins with ॥ 8 ॥.
[^5]: "[You] have no concern at all" — *nāhi kichu dāẏa* (cf. lahari 9, note 6). "Hindu adhikaris" — *hindu adhikārī*: apparently the yavana ruler's term for the Hindu teachers and temple servants, the guests of the festival; "send off [with gifts]" — *kariba bidāẏa*, "dismiss with honour" (cf. 2; lahari 10, 70–71). It can also be understood as "I shall dismiss all the Hindu officials." The yavana's words run from 8 to 10.
[^6]: Medinipur — a town in West Bengal (cf. the Purva-vibhaga); Alamganj (*ālamagañja*) — apparently a quarter of Medinipur. "At last" — *nidāna*; below the line the editor gives the variant *bihāna* ("in the morning"; not translated). The festival was apparently held at the yavana's expense (8–10).
[^7]: "Calling [him] Ishvara" — *īśvara baliẏā*: this is how the yavana sees Shyamananda (cf. 5 and note 3). According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching the guru is honoured as the Lord's most intimate servant, not as the Lord Himself (Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11). "Firmly" — *niścaẏa*; the 1997 edition has *niścala*, "unwaveringly."
[^8]: "Might" — *paratāpa* (= *pratāpa*). "His name" — *yā̃ra nāma*: apparently Shyamananda's name. The yavana is Harabola (3, note 2).
[^9]: Chintamani, Uddhava, Akrura and Madhuvana are also named in BR 15 (63–65) among Shyamananda's disciples; Chintamani appears again below, 29. Mathura is possibly Rasamaya's brother (Dakshina, lahari 4, 34–35). The others are not identified. "Kanu" (18) may be the same Kanudasa or another person. "Fortunate" — *bhāgyabān*; the second time the scan has the misprint "তাগ্যবান্".
[^10]: "Many in that village Shyamananda made [his] disciples" — *bahuśiṣya śyāmānanda karila se grāma*, lit. "Shyamananda made that village [a village] of many disciples."
[^11]: Rasamaya is the author's father, Vamshi his brother; Bhima Shirikara is the ruler of Dharenda converted by Rasika (Dakshina, lahari 5; Shirikara — as in Purva, lahari 1, note 39).
[^12]: "Live a household life" — *karaha saṃsāra*. "I shall find [you] a bride" — *kanyā … kariba sañjāta*, lit. "I shall make a maiden appear." BR 15 says nothing of Shyamananda's marriage; the Rasika-mangala tells of it here (26–29) and further on (lahari 12, 12).
[^13]: Greater Balarampur — *baṛa balarāmapura* (lahari 8, note 16). Jagannatha (26) is possibly the same disciple of Shyamananda at Balarampur (lahari 8, note 18).
[^14]: "A portion of Lakshmi" — *lakṣmī aṃśe*: poetic praise, usual in describing a bride.
[^15]: "Married" — *bibhā karilena* (28), "having married" — *bibhā kari*. "Bride" — *kanyā*. Chintamani is Shyamananda's disciple at Dharenda (17). Dharenda is spelled here *dhārandā*.
[^16]: Radhanagar (*rādhānagara*) is not identified. "First set up [his own] house" — *gṛha … prathame karilā*; the sense is approximate.
[^17]: "The Thakura" — *ṭhākura*: the Deity served in Rasika's house (cf. lahari 7, 6: Sri Gopivallabha-raya with the Thakurani). Bhoga is the food cooked to be offered to the Deity. "The mother" — *mātā*: Shyamadasi, "the mother of the world" (50; Purva, lahari 1, 108). "To cut the vegetables" — *śañcā karibāre*: according to the editor's gloss below the line (*śañcā*, the cutting of vegetables; the gloss is not translated).
[^18]: "In the cradle" — *dulīra upare* (so in the scan; the OCR of the 1997 edition has "দুগীর"): *dulī* is a hanging cradle. Vrajananda (35) is Rasika's first-born; in the Purva-vibhaga (lahari 1, 110, note 48) Radhananda is called the eldest son — the eldest of those who remained (51–52).
[^19]: "I shall make the offering" (41) — *kari upahāra* (the 1997 edition has *upacāra*, "I shall perform the service"). "The son of maya" (42) — *māẏā-putra*: a son as a bond of worldly attachment. The author calls Rasika's words (44–46) a curse (see the colophon) and presents it as the consequence of aparadha — an offence in Krishna's service: according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, service to the Deity stands above family attachments, a lapse in it is an offence, and the coming and going of every living being is in the Lord's hands. This episode is not in BR 15.
[^20]: "Even for a moment" — *pala mātra*: a pala is a measure of time (about 24 seconds). "Will not remain with you" — *nā rahibe saṅga*. "Without offence" (46) — *nir-aparādhe*, i.e. without lapses in Krishna's service.
[^21]: "By the word of [his] order" — *ājñā pramāṇe*. "Were struck down" — *hata hailā* (cf. lahari 9, note 8): six sons departed from life, each one when Rasika noticed a lapse in the service (48–49).
[^22]: "A lapse" (48) — *kruṭī* (= *truṭi*). "He orders that son to go" — *ājñā karena yāite se nandana*.
[^23]: "Prabhu" — here apparently Rasika. "On earth" — *dharaṇī*.
[^24]: Radhananda, Krishnagati, Radhakrishnadasa — Rasika's sons (Purva, lahari 1, 110, 122, 124; note 48).
[^25]: Gopivallabhapur — a village on the Suvarnarekha, the chief place of Rasika's service (Dakshina, lahari 3).
[^26]: Hridayananda — Hridaya Chaitanya, Shyamananda's guru, servant of the Deities at Ambika-Kalna (Purva, lahari 1, note 13; BR 15, 94). "Adhikari" (*adhikārī*) — the title of the head of a temple's service. Dharinda (*dhārindā*) is Dharenda (57).
[^27]: Damodara — the learned yogi of Chakulia, Shyamananda's disciple (Dakshina, lahari 1). "The three Prabhus" (58) — Shyamananda, Rasika and Damodara.
[^28]: "Embracing" (59) — *kole kari*, lit. "taking on the lap (in the arms)." According to BR 15 (101) Shyamananda settled in Utkala at the order of the Gosvami; here the order to preach in Utkala is given by Hridayananda.
[^29]: "Seeing [him] off" — *anubraje*: by custom a guest, all the more a guru, is accompanied for some distance.
[^30]: Rasamaya and Vamshi are brothers (note 11); their house is at Dharenda (lahari 9, note 12).
[^31]: "[His] nature" — *sbabhābe*, i.e. a description of Rasika's nature (cf. lahari 10, 72: *sbabhāba barṇana*); the 1997 edition has *prabhābe*, "greatness."
[^32]: The colophon is on pdf 116 (checked against the scan): "ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-দক্ষিণ-বিভাগে কৃষ্ণসেবাপরাধে শ্যামদাসী-প্রতি অভিশাপপ্রদান-নাম একাদশ-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা" (*kṛṣṇasebāparādhe śyāmadāsī-prati abhiśāpa-pradāna*, lit. "The Pronouncing of a Curse on Shyamadasi for an Offence in Krishna's Service"); the 1997 edition omits Shyamadasi's name in the colophon. Lahari 12 begins on pdf 117 (files RM-2-12-001-030).
