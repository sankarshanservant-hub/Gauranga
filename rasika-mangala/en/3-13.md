# Pashchima-vibhaga. Lahari 13. The Description of Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu's Separation at the Disappearance of Sri Sri Shyamananda Prabhu

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Shyamananda and Rasika give prema in all lands; in Ambika Adhikari Thakura departs, and Shyamananda, weeping for Hridayananda, says he will no longer remain on earth; in the month of Phalguna an aradhana festival is held in Shyamasundarpur, and the whole community gathers; in Govindapur, after Damodara's departure, a festival is held in his memory and again the day of Adhikari Gosani is kept; Shyamananda tells Rasika that he has fulfilled the Lord's command to deliver, together with him, the living beings of Utkala, and that Krishna bids him depart; in Nrisimhapur, in the house of Uddanda-raya, he is ill for four months, refuses treatment and in place of medicine orders unceasing sankirtana; when Rasika begs leave to go to Vrindavana, he tells him to remain in Utkala, preach and care for all, places his feet on his head, ties a cloth on him with his own hand, applies tilaka and gives him all authority in the community; in Shaka 1552, on the first day of the dark fortnight of Ashadha after the Snana-yatra, amid the sound of sankirtana, Shyamananda departs; Rasika falls unconscious and laments for him; the disciples console him, but he is inconsolable; in that village, by the rules of the shastras, the place of his departure is established; the author promises to tell of the festivals and asks pardon for faults in the order of the narrative*

*The thirteenth lahari.*

*Raga Narayani-gauda.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "My Krishna, treasury of virtues,*
*refuge of the helpless, the utmost limit of mercy."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the life of Rasika!
Glory, glory to Rasikendra, the son of Achyuta![^2]

**2.** Thus Shyamananda and Rasikendra-chandra
gave prema in all lands, with joy in their hearts.

**3.** At that time, after some days, in the land of Ambuya
Sri Adhikari Thakura attained Goloka.[^3]

**4.** After some days a servant [came] to Shyamananda
and told him everything that [had happened] there.

**5.** Hearing it, Shyamananda wept much;
he sent people to bring Rasika.

**6.** Rasikendra came as soon as he received the command;
Shyamananda said to Rasika:[^4]

**7.** "No longer shall I remain on the earth:
separation from Hridayananda tears [my] heart apart."[^5]

**8.** Shyamananda wept for a long time;
the whole community grew very sad.

**9.** He told Rasika everything:
"I shall hold a great festival in Shyamasundarpur.

**10.** Proclaim this word in all lands;
bring provisions from everywhere in every way."[^6]

**11.** Having received the command, Rasikendra set out;
he sent servants in all four directions.

**12.** Having received the command, [they] brought many provisions there;
at Rasika's command many people came.

**13.** In [the month of] Phalguna the great festival was held;
all the communities of Shyamananda's servants came.[^7]

**14.** There was great joy in Shyamasundarpur;
who could describe that happiness even with a hundred mouths?

**15.** Having held the aradhana festival that year,
Shyamananda went to Sri Govindapur.[^8]

**16.** After some days Damodara departed from this world;
hearing [of it], Shyamananda Prabhu was stricken to the heart.[^9]

**17.** Shyamananda gave a command to Rasika
[and said]: "Damodara has shown [me] the way — I see [it] in my heart."[^10]

**18.** [Rasika] held the aradhana festival in his [memory],
and again the day of Adhikari Gosani.[^11]

**19.** In Govindapur that festival was held;
before all the Shyamanandis Prabhu made [his will] known.[^12]

**20.** Seating Rasika beside him, he speaks softly:
"Listen, listen, O child, Rasika-shekhara!

**21.** Long ago Prabhu Bhagavan gave me the command:
'Taking Rasika, deliver the living beings.'[^13]

**22.** By that command I preached bhakti for Krishna
and delivered all the living beings of Utkala.

**23.** The whole world is intoxicated with prema for Krishna;
now you, taking them all [into your care], live [with them] in joy.[^14]

**24.** Krishna's command has come for me to go;
surely I shall no longer remain on earth."

**25.** Having said this, he entered Nrisimhapur:
taking Rasika with him, Prabhu went there.

**26.** At that time Shyamananda fell ill;
they all stayed in the house of Uddanda-raya.[^15]

**27.** Shyamananda-raya fell very gravely ill;
for four months he remained there with the community.

**28.** Ten or twenty physicians came and treated him;
each gave whatever medicine he wished.

**29.** The physicians treated [him] in every way;
day and night Rasika sat [by him] without sleep.

**30.** In no way did [his] body recover;
Shyamananda himself said to everyone:

**31.** "There is Krishna's command — I shall certainly go;
make no vain efforts; listen, all of you.

**32.** Hold sankirtana day and night;
unceasingly, sadhus, speak of Krishna.

**33.** Vina, venu, rabab, murali — many instruments:
this is the medicine; in this, I tell you, is [the whole] truth."[^16]

**34.** Hearing this, Rasika grew very sad;
in a voice choked with feeling he said to Prabhu:

**35.** "Command me, Prabhu, to go to Vrindavana:
how shall I keep my life in separation from you?"

**36.** Rasika-shekhara was deeply distressed;
hearing him, Shyamananda answered him:

**37.** "All the Shyamanandis who were born in Utkala —
with them live in joy for some time longer.

**38.** By my command remain in the land of Utkala;
know in your heart that you are always in Vrindavana.[^17]

**39.** For some time longer preach bhakti for Krishna;
fill the world to overflowing with prema for Krishna.[^18]

**40.** From house to house diligently [arrange] service to the sadhus;
give prema-bhakti for Krishna to all people, to each one.

**41.** [Uphold] what the shastras establish — shruti, smriti, the Bhagavata:
service to the guru, to Krishna and to the sadhus, and the brahmanas;

**42.** care for all people on earth."
He placed [his] lotus feet on Rasika's head.[^19]

**43.** Tying a cloth [on him] with his own hand, Shyamananda
with joy in his heart applied tilaka to his forehead.[^20]

**44.** Himself joining his palms, he spoke to all —
thus in Shyamananda's community he performed the anointing:[^21]

**45.** "All will abide by Rasika's command;
Rasikendra will care for all.

**46.** Let no one break Rasika's command:
whoever turns away from Rasika is no companion of mine."

**47.** He gave all authority to Rasika-shekhara,
entrusted everything to the son of Achyuta.

**48.** All the while the sound of sankirtana rises on every side;
looking at Shyamananda, everyone grew anxious.

**49.** In Shaka 1552 — such is the date —
Prabhu departed to Krishna.[^22]

**50.** [It was at] the Deity's Snana-yatra, at the close of the full moon,
on the first lunar day of the dark fortnight, at the entry of [the month of] Ashadha.[^23]

**51.** Cries of "Hari!", the sound of conches, the sound of sankirtana —
shouts of "Jaya!" reached the vault of the sky.

**52.** At that very moment Prabhu disappeared [from the world's sight].
Hearing [of it], the whole community lost their senses.[^24]

**53.** Rasika fell to the ground unconscious;
his companions lift him, hold him up, sprinkle water on his face.

**54.** His body was drowned in the streams of tears from his eyes;
lamenting, Rasika-shekhara says:

**55.** "When I was eighteen years old,
then Shyamananda-svami appeared to me.

**56.** For twenty years I served [his] feet;
now, leaving [me] alone, Prabhu has gone to his own abode.[^25]

**57.** He by whose touch [people] obtained prema-bhakti for Krishna,
by the sight of whom all became pure of mind;

**58.** by whose mercy ignorance was dispelled,
by whose favour [came] release from the bonds of worldly existence;

**59.** by whose grace Hunas, Pulindas, mlecchas and others,
leaving their own occupations, went mad with prema for Krishna —[^26]

**60.** such a Prabhu has gone, leaving [me] — I shall not see [him] again;
now my whole world has become empty.[^27]

**61.** Who will now be kind to me, [loving me] with a father's tenderness?
With whom shall I wander, roaming from land to land?

**62.** With whom shall I go round the holy places?
Who will take me with him to Vrindavana?[^28]

**63.** No more shall I see those two feet!"
Saying this, Rasikendra fell to the ground.

**64.** All the devotees of Krishna, the Shyamanandis who were with [him],
consoling [him], all speak [to him] in many ways.[^29]

**65.** No one's consolation consoles Prabhu;
day and night streams flow from his two eyes.

**66.** Rasika's love cannot be described:
from separation from Shyamananda [his] heart breaks.

**67.** Even with millions of mouths it cannot be told —
he, hearing of whose love, a stone melts.[^30]

**68.** In that village they established the place of [his] disappearance,
according to the rules of the Vedas, [following] the testimony of the smriti-shastras.[^31]

**69.** Now I shall tell of the great festivals and the rest,
hearing of which one never again meets with sin.[^32]

**70.** Who can know the lilas, [boundless] as the waves of the ocean?
By Rasika's mercy I have told a little.[^33]

**71.** In the Pashchima-vibhaga I have composed this —
what Prabhu, the son of Achyuta, makes me speak.

**72.** Do not keep in mind any faults in the order [of the narrative];
may the learned sadhus listen with love.[^34]

**73.** The lord of everyone's life is Rasikendra-chandra;
listen, all, to his virtues, filled with joy.

**74.** Listen to the "Rasika-mangala," all devotees of Krishna:
without delay you will obtain the treasure of prema-bhakti for Krishna.

**75.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Pashchima-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the thirteenth lahari, called "The Description of Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu's Separation at the Disappearance of Sri Sri Shyamananda Prabhu."*[^35]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the title of the lahari ("ত্রয়োদশ-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 13 begins on pdf 163 (printed p. 121), below a rule after the colophon of lahari 12 (files RM-3-12-078-078). Raga Narayani-gauda (*nārāni gauṛā*, so in the scan; Purva, lahari 7, note 1); the ghosha is the same as in lahari 12. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 167 (printed p. 123): "ইতি শ্রীরসিকমঙ্গল-পশ্চিম-বিভাগে শ্রীশ্রীশ্যামানন্দ প্রভুর তিরোভাবে শ্রীশ্রীরসিকানন্দ-প্রভুর বিরহ-বর্ণন-নাম ত্রয়োদশ-লহরী সম্পূর্ণা" (*tirobhābe … biraha-barṇana*) — "The Description of Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu's Separation at the Disappearance of Sri Sri Shyamananda Prabhu". Lahari 13 comprises 1–75 (pdf 163–164, 167; pdf 165 is an inserted plate, pdf 166 is blank). The summary line covers the whole lahari (60–75 from the OCR of pdf 167, provisionally).
[^2]: "The life of Rasika" — *rasika-jībana*: Shyamananda, Rasika's guru. "The son of Achyuta" — *acyuta-nandana* (Pashchima, lahari 5, note 60).
[^3]: Ambuya (*āmbuyā*, so in the scan; the OCR is garbled) — Ambika (Kalna) and its surroundings (Purva, lahari 2, note 8; the editor understands it likewise). "Land" — *maluk* (*mulk*), "domain, region." Sri Adhikari Thakura is Hridayananda (Hridaya Chaitanya), Shyamananda's guru, servant of the Deities at Ambika-Kalna (7; Dakshina, lahari 11, note 26). "Attained Goloka" — *pāilā goloke*: so the author speaks of a Vaishnava's departure — a return to Krishna's eternal abode.
[^4]: "As soon as he received the command" — *ājñā prabeśite*, lit. "on the command's entering (arriving)"; it may also be understood as "to receive the command." "Said to Rasika" — *rasika-agrete*, lit. "before Rasika."
[^5]: "On the earth" — *abanimaṇḍale*, "on the circle of the earth." Hridayananda is Sri Adhikari Thakura (3).
[^6]: Shyamasundarpur — the village given by the raja of Ghantashila (lahari 12, 6–7). "Provisions" — *sambhāra*: everything needed for the festival.
[^7]: Phalguna — the month of February–March. "Was held" — *karila prakāśa*, lit. "manifested." "All the communities of Shyamananda's servants" — *saba goṣṭhī śyāmānanda dāsa*; it may also be understood as "all the communities [and] servants of Shyamananda."
[^8]: The aradhana festival (*ārādhanā mahotsaba*, lit. "festival of worship") — apparently a festival in memory of a departed Vaishnava, here Hridayananda (3–9; cf. 18). "Went" — *bije kaila* (*bijaya*, the "progress" of a holy person). Sri Govindapur is apparently Little Govindapur, where Shyamananda lived (lahari 12, 10, note 10), or the Sri Govindapur given by the bhuiyan Bhimadhana (Dakshina, lahari 12, note 7).
[^9]: Damodara — the learned yogi of Chakulia, Shyamananda's disciple (Dakshina, lahari 1, note 21; Dakshina, lahari 11, note 27); in lahari 12, 28 he is "Damodara Gosani." "Departed from this world" — *haila antardhāna*, "disappeared." "Stricken to the heart" — *ākula parāṇa*, "his soul in turmoil."
[^10]: "Has shown the way" — *patha kāṛāilā*; the editor glosses *kāṛāilā* as *dekhāilā*, "showed." Understood: Damodara went first, showing the way by which Shyamananda too will soon depart (cf. 24); the sense is approximate. "Gave a command to Rasika" — *ājñā kaila … rasikera sthāne*: apparently to hold the festival (18).
[^11]: "In his [memory]" — *tāhāra*, i.e. in memory of Damodara. "The day of Adhikari Gosani" — apparently the anniversary of Hridayananda's departure (3; cf. 15). The subject of "held" is not expressed; understood as Rasika, by Shyamananda's command (17).
[^12]: Shyamanandis (*śyāmānandī*) — Shyamananda's followers. "Made [his will] known" — *prakāśilā*, "revealed, announced"; understood: Shyamananda announces his impending departure (20–24).
[^13]: "Prabhu Bhagavan" is Krishna: in Vraja, Madana-gopala Sri Govinda commanded Shyamananda to go to Utkala and instruct Rasika-Murari (Purva, lahari 15, 7–8, 18–20). "Deliver" — *kara … paritrāṇa*.
[^14]: "Taking them all" — *e sabāre laiẏā*. "Live [with them] in joy" — *karaha bihāra*, lit. "perform vihara" (strolling, sport, wandering); likewise in 37 (*biharaṇa*).
[^15]: Nrisimhapur — Dakshina, lahari 16, note 24. Uddanda-raya (*udaṇḍa rāẏa*) — the bhuiyan of Nrisimhapur, a disciple of Shyamananda (Dakshina, lahari 16). "Fell ill" — *asustha hailā*.
[^16]: The vina is a stringed instrument; the venu and murali are flutes; the rabab is a bowed (or plucked) stringed instrument. "Medicine" — *auṣadha* (in the scan *a-uṣadha*). "In this, I tell you, is [the whole] truth" — *ithe kahilāma tattba*.
[^17]: "Know in your heart that you are always in Vrindavana" — *manete jāniha sadā ācha bṛndābane*: serving in Utkala by the guru's command, Rasika inwardly abides in Vrindavana.
[^18]: "Fill the world to overflowing" — *ḍhalāḍhali karaha saṃsāra*: *ḍhalāḍhali* is "swaying, spilling over"; it may also be understood as "let the world sway (in rapture) with prema for Krishna."
[^19]: 41–42a form one instruction: *śruti-smṛti-bhāgabata śāstra-paramāṇa / gurukṛṣṇa-sādhusebā āra dbijagaṇa / sarbajana pālana karaha bhūmaṇḍale*; the verb "care for" (*pālana karaha*) apparently governs the whole list. "What the shastras establish" — *śāstra-paramāṇa*, "the testimony of scripture." "Placed his feet on his head" — a sign of blessing and of bestowing grace.
[^20]: "Tying a cloth [on him]" — *bastra bāndhi'*: apparently he tied a cloth (a turban) on his head, a sign of succession. "To his forehead" — *māthe*, "on the head, on the forehead."
[^21]: "Anointing" — *ṭīkā*: the mark (on the forehead) by which a successor or ruler is confirmed; *ṭīkā se sārilā*, "he performed that tika." Shyamananda hands over to Rasika the leadership of the community (45–47). On the author's view of Shyamananda and Rasika, see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11.
[^22]: Shaka 1552 is 1630 CE. "Such is the date" — *se pramāṇa*, "that is the evidence." "Departed to Krishna" — *kṛṣṇera sannidhe … karilā praẏāṇa*, "set out to Krishna" (*sannidhe*, "into the presence"; so in the scan, the OCR is garbled). On Shyamananda as an associate of the Lord, see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11.
[^23]: The Snana-yatra (*deba-snānayātrā*, "the bathing festival of the Deity") — the bathing of Jagannatha on the full moon of the month of Jyaishtha. "The first lunar day of the dark fortnight" — *kṛṣṇa-pratipada tithi*: the first day after the full moon; when months are reckoned from the full moon, the beginning of Ashadha (June–July). In the scan, lines 49–50 and 52a are underlined by hand (a reader's marks); the text is clear.
[^24]: "Disappeared" — *hailā antardhāna*, "became hidden": the departure of an associate of the Lord is a disappearance, not an ordinary passing. "Lost their senses" — *harilā jñāna*, lit. "lost consciousness."
[^25]: Rasikananda was born in 1590 (Purva, lahari 4, note 8); eighteen years and twenty years of service (55–56) agree approximately with the date 1630 (49). "Appeared to me" — *darśana dilā*, "gave darshan" (Purva, lahari 16). "To his own abode" — *nija dhāme*.
[^26]: Hunas, Pulindas, mlecchas — *hūṇa-pulinda-mlecchādi* (reading from the scan; the OCR is garbled): foreigners and forest tribes (Pashchima, lahari 6, note 11; Pashchima, lahari 10, note 23). "Their own occupations" — *nija karma*, caste and worldly occupations. The sentence continues in 60 (pdf 167): "Such a Prabhu…".
[^27]: The continuation of the sentence in 57–59: "He by whose touch… — such a Prabhu…". "Has gone, leaving [me]" — *chāṛi' gelā*. "Has become empty" — *śūnya bhela* (*bhela*, "became").
[^28]: "With a father's tenderness" — *bātsalya kariyā*: Shyamananda, the guru, loved Rasika as a father loves a son. "Wander, roaming" — *buliba bhramiyā*. "Go round the holy places" — *tīrtha-paryaṭana*.
[^29]: Shyamanandis — note 12. "Devotees of Krishna" — *kārṣṇa* (Purva, lahari 10, note 13).
[^30]: "Love" — *anurāga*, loving attachment (likewise in 67). "A stone melts" — *pāṣāṇa milāẏa*, literally "a stone dissolves."
[^31]: "The place of [his] disappearance" — *tirodhāna-āsana*, literally "the seat of disappearance": apparently a samadhi, the memorial place where the transcendental body of a saint rests. "In that village" — apparently Nrisimhapur, where Shyamananda departed (25–26, 52). The subject is not expressed. "According to the rules of the Vedas, [following] the testimony of the smriti-shastras" — *bedabidhi smṛtiśāstra kariyā pramāṇe*.
[^32]: "The great festivals and the rest" — *mahotsaba ādi*: the festivals in Shyamananda's memory, told in the following laharis (14–16). "One never again meets with sin" — *pāpa sane dekhā nahe āra*.
[^33]: "The lilas, [boundless] as the waves of the ocean" — *samudra-taraṅga-līlā*; cf. *laharī*, "wave," the name of the poem's chapters. "Have told" — *kailũ paracāre*.
[^34]: "The son of Achyuta" is Rasika (note 2). "Makes me speak" — *more bolāẏa*: the author ascribes his words to Rasika. "Faults in the order [of the narrative]" — *anukrama doṣa*: departures from the sequence of events. "With love" — *suprīte* (so in the scan; the OCR is garbled).
