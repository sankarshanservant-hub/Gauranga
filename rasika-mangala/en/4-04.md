# Uttara-vibhaga. Lahari 4. The Description of the Conspiracy against Rasikananda Prabhu Owing to Gaurangadasi's Unwillingness that the Three Thakuranis Live Together

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Rasika comes to Gopivallabhapur and hears that Uddanda, for his offence, has perished with all his line, while the people glorify Rasika; he ceremonially brings Shyamapriya Thakurani and Vrindavanachandra to Shyamasundarpur, settles the three Thakuranis there together and celebrates the third twelve-day festival, to which kings, subjects, mahantas, Vrajavasis, Gaudiyas and the whole Shyamanandi community gather; but the Thakuranis do not get along, they quarrel and abuse Rasika, and he only laughs; Shyamapriya asks him to build three houses, but Rasika holds firmly to the guru's order; the angered elder Thakurani, Gaurangadasi, secretly conspires with Vidyutmala, Harikara and others: they write a forged letter in Rasika's name to Shyamapriya about poisoning Gaurangadasi, and she keeps it at her breast until the day of the dadhi-kada; some of the conspirators want to take Rasika's life on the spot, others to drive him away; during the dadhi-sankirtana Gaurangadasi demands judgement before the whole assembly and bids Padmanabha and Gopidasa read the letter; the author glorifies Rasika, by whose word poison becomes nectar*

*The fourth lahari.*

*Raga Kaushika.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "Glory, O glory to Rama and Krishna!*
*O Murari, O Murari, O Murari!"*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the purifier of all!
Be merciful, that I may compose [a song of your] glory.[^2]

**2.** From there Rasikendra set out
[and] arrived at Sri Gopivallabhapur.[^3]

**3.** There he heard that Uddanda had lost his life:
he had perished together with all his line.[^4]

**4.** Where did the wealth, the people, all the family go?
Because of the offence everything went to ruin.

**5.** Seeing the manifestation [of the power] of Rasika, all the people —
all the kings [and] subjects — were amazed.

**6.** All say: "Rasikendra is a second Narayana:
at his touch the wicked were pacified.[^5]

**7.** His word is heeded by Huns, Pulindas and the like,
his word is heeded by mlecchas, Pukkashas and the like;[^6]

**8.** his word is carried out by wicked kings,
his command is not transgressed by devas and asuras.

**9.** Krishna gave him (Uddanda) the punishment he deserved" —
all speak to one another of Rasika's glory.

**10.** Having crushed [his] pride, Rasikendra ceremonially brought
Shyamapriya Thakurani [and] Vrindavanachandra —

**11.** to Sri Shyamasundarpur he ceremonially brought [them];
the three Thakuranis he settled there together.[^7]

**12.** There he celebrated the third festival of twelve [days];
from all four directions he had an incredible [abundance of] provisions brought.[^8]

**13.** A great many kings [and] subjects came there;
all the mahantas, as many as [there were], came.

**14.** Vrajavasis, Gaudiyas — all the saints, as many as [there were] —
came; all the Shyamanandi kinsfolk came.[^9]

**15.** No one can transgress Rasikendra's command:
all came to see the festival.

**16.** Many kirtaniyas came there:
in the midst of the forest the world of Vaikuntha arose.[^10]

**17.** Whatever Prabhu, the son of Achyuta, has in mind —
the impossible at once comes to pass.

**18.** The lila that Prabhu performs by his own will
neither Brahma, nor Shiva, nor King Indra can break.[^11]

**19.** Thus he celebrated two [festivals] of twelve [days] there;
[but] among the three Thakuranis there was no harmony at all.[^12]

**20.** Constantly they quarrel without cause;
all three abuse the son of Achyuta.

**21.** Prabhu says nothing to anyone — hearing, he laughs;
yet they quarrel all the same, day and night.[^13]

**22.** Shyamapriya Thakurani is most composed;
she knows all truths [and] is exceedingly calm.[^14]

**23.** Having reflected, she speaks gently to Rasika:
"Quickly set up three houses in this village.

**24.** Together [we] cannot pass our days — I tell you for certain;
I do not know what more [will come] through you."[^15]

**25.** The unyielding Nagara, Prabhu would not even listen:
"We shall live together — the guru's order [is] the warrant.[^16]

**26.** Whoever, breaking the order by a single step, goes away,
to that person the Shyamanandis will not go."[^17]

**27.** These words Prabhu spoke firmly and resolutely;
the elder Thakurani, hearing [them], grew angry in her heart.[^18]

**28.** At the time of the third festival of twelve [days],
taking two or four [people], she confers in secret:[^19]

**29.** Vidyutmala, Nava-Gauranga, Balarama,
Sri Keshavananda, Harikara, Vishnurama,

**30.** Kalandi, Radhajivana — all the wicked ones,
[and] disciples' disciples, servant-disciples — all who were with them.[^20]

**31.** The elder Thakurani, deliberating with all, [says]:
"I will not let Rasika come here.

**32.** Let Shyamapriya [and] Yamuna go to their own places;
devise a plan so that I alone may remain in this place."[^21]

**33.** Hearing [this], Harikara says: "There is a better plan:
write a letter in Rasika's name,

**34.** as though he had written to mother Shyamapriya
that he will make Gaurangadasi drink poison

**35.** [and] will try by all sorts of means to kill her —
write all such words all over the letter.[^22]

**36.** At the festival, at the sankirtana, on the day of the dadhi-kada,
we will hold an assembly [and] seat all the saints:[^23]

**37.** kings, subjects, mahajanas, mahantas,
the Shyamanandi community, and sannyasis [and] brahmanas as well.

**38.** This letter we will bring [and] present in the midst of the assembly:
all, hearing [it], will revile Rasika.

**39.** On this pretext we will not let him come here again."
On the four edges of the sheet they wrote all this.

**40.** Wrapping the letter in silk cloth, they tied it carefully;
the elder Thakurani hid [it] in the garment she wore.[^24]

**41.** Keeping the letter with her as dearer than life,
day and night she carries it at her breast.

**42.** Here, meanwhile, Rasikendra-raya, intoxicated with the festival,
knows nothing but service to the guru, to Krishna [and] to the sadhus.

**43.** And here all the wicked ones confer in secret
how to bring Rasika-shekhara to disgrace.[^25]

**44.** Someone says: "When everyone hears the letter,
in an instant we will take Rasika's life on the spot."

**45.** Someone says: "Then and there we will strike with a yamadhara."
Someone says: "[So long as] he no longer stays here."[^26]

**46.** Thus all the wicked ones confer;
Rasika's people know nothing of all this.

**47.** All were joyfully performing the dadhi-sankirtana;
at that time the elder mother, before everyone,

**48.** summoning all with words not to be refused, [says]:
"Let all the people judge without guile.[^27]

**49.** I make one request of everyone:
all of you, listen with a firm mind to a letter.

**50.** Having heard the letter, by a proper judgement of righteousness
impose a fitting punishment — I say to all.[^28]

**51.** See: here are [all] the devotees of Utkala;
let all the people, without stirring, listen to the letter.

**52.** All of you continually lay the burden of blame on me:
'You have ruined Shyamananda's house.'

**53.** [So] hear, all of you, what my faults and merits are:
he writes to Shyamapriya [that she should] kill me.[^29]

**54.** From the day I received this letter
I have kept it carefully day and night myself.

**55.** I have no one I trust enough to put [it] in [their] hands:
Padmanabha [and] Gopidasa, read [it] before everyone."[^30]

**56.** Rasika's lila amazes the three worlds:
by his word poison becomes nectar.

**57.** Remembrance of him frees one from the bonds of worldly existence —
[and] such ignorant people would disgrace him![^31]

**58.** Listen, everyone — I shall tell what happened next:
the "Rasika-mangala" is supremely deep.

**59.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].[^32]

*Thus in the Uttara-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the fourth lahari, called "The Description of the Conspiracy against Rasikananda Prabhu Owing to Gaurangadasi's Unwillingness that the Three Thakuranis Live Together."*

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the title of the lahari ("চতুর্থ-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 4 begins on pdf 184 (printed p. 140), under a rule after the colophon of lahari 3 (files RM-4-03-…). Raga Kaushika (*kauṣika*, so on the scan; Purva, lahari 3, note 1); the ghosha is the same as in Purva, lahari 3 (there without the word "ghosha"), and its translation is repeated: Rama and Krishna are Balarama and Krishna; Murari is a name of Krishna, which the author also applies to Rasika-Murari. There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 186 (printed p. 142), checked against the scan: "তিন ঠাকুরাণীকে একত্র স্থাপনে গৌরাঙ্গ-দাসীর অনিচ্ছাহেতু রসিকানন্দ প্রভুর বিরুদ্ধে ষড়যন্ত্র-বর্ণন" (*tina ṭhākurāṇīke ekatra sthāpane gaurāṅga-dāsīra anicchāhetu rasikānanda prabhura biruddhe ṣaṛayantra-barṇana*), literally "The Description of the Conspiracy against Rasikananda Prabhu by Reason of Gaurangadasi's Unwillingness [to Allow] the Three Thakuranis to Be Settled Together." Lahari 4 — 1–59 (pdf 184–186).
[^2]: "The purifier of all" — *akhila-pābana*. 1b — *kṛpā kara yaśaḥ yena kariẏe racana*, literally "be merciful, that I may compose the glory."
[^3]: "From there" — from Hijli (lahari 3). "Arrived" — *upasana* (Uttara, lahari 1, 58, note 31): apparently *upasanna*, "arrived."
[^4]: Uddanda — Uddanda the bhuiyan, who refused to give up Vrindavanachandra (lahari 3, 3–6; cf. Uddanda-raya — Dakshina, lahari 16). In lahari 3 (6) Rasika said that if Shyamananda had truly given him the order, Uddanda would not remain on earth. "With all his line" — *sabaṃśa sahita*. "Offence" (4) — *aparādha*.
[^5]: "A second Narayana" — *dbitī nārāẏaṇa*: the enthusiastic words of the people (cf. Pashchima, lahari 3, 61). According to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, Rasikananda is a devotee and associate, a disciple of Shyamananda, not an incarnation of the Lord (Pashchima, lahari 4, note 37); on the author's attitude to Shyamananda and Rasika see Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11. Verses 17–18 are to be understood likewise. "The wicked" — *duṣṭagaṇa*.
[^6]: Huns, Pulindas, Pukkashas (*pukaśa*), mlecchas — "low" and foreign peoples; cf. the Bhagavata (2.4.18): the Kiratas, Huns, Andhras, Pulindas, Pukkashas and others are purified by taking refuge in the Lord's devotees. "Heed his word" — *bacana śune*, literally "hear his word": obey.
[^7]: "Having crushed pride" — *garba dhbaṃsi*: the pride of Uddanda. "Ceremonially brought" — *bije karāila*: the ceremonial transfer of a Deity (Uttara, lahari 2, 49, note 28). Shyamapriya Thakurani is Shyamananda's wife (Dakshina, lahari 11, 26–29); Vrindavanachandra is a Deity of Shyamananda (Pashchima, lahari 11, note 9). Shyamasundarpur — Pashchima, lahari 12, note 8. The three Thakuranis are Shyamapriya, Yamuna and Gaurangadasi (34; Uttara, lahari 2, 48, 53–54, notes 27, 30; Pashchima, lahari 12, 11, note 11): so Shyamananda had ordered (Uttara, lahari 2, 47–54).
[^8]: "The third festival of twelve [days]" — *tṛtīẏa dbādaśa mahotsaba*: apparently the third annual twelve-day aradhana of Shyamananda (Uttara, lahari 1, note 11; lahari 2, note 5); cf. 19 and 28. "Incredible [abundance]" — *asambhaba*.
[^9]: Vrajavasis — the residents of Vraja; Gaudiyas (*gouṛiẏā*, so on the scan) — Vaishnavas from Gauda (Bengal). "The Shyamanandi kinsfolk" — *śyāmānandī saba ātmagaṇa*: the whole community of Shyamananda.
[^10]: Kirtaniyas (*kīrtanīẏā*) — kirtana singers (Uttara, lahari 1, note 10). "The world of Vaikuntha" — *baikuṇṭha-bhubana*.
[^11]: "The impossible comes to pass" (17) — *aghaṭana-ghaṭana*. "The son of Achyuta" — Rasika (Purva, lahari 3, note 5). "King Indra" — *indra-rāẏa*. On 17–18 see note 5.
[^12]: "Two [festivals] of twelve [days]" — *dui duẏādaśa*: apparently two annual festivals in memory of Shyamananda held at Shyamasundarpur; how this fits with the "third" festival (12, 28) is unclear. "There was no harmony at all" — *prīti nā haila sarbathā*, literally "there was no love whatever."
[^13]: "Quarrel" — *kandala kare*. "All three" (20) — *tina jane*.
[^14]: "Most composed" — *baṛa gabhīratā*, literally "great depth." "Knows all truths" — *sarba tattbajñāta*. "Exceedingly calm" — *atība dhīratā*.
[^15]: "Gently" — *sudhāmaẏa*, "[with words] full of nectar." "Houses" — *bāṛī*, homesteads. 24b — *nā jānihe tomā diẏā āra kibā haẏa*: the sense is unclear; understood as Shyamapriya's fear that harm will come of Rasika's insistence; it can also be understood as "I do not know what else can be done through you."
[^16]: "The unyielding Nagara" — *haṭhiẏā nāgara*: *haṭhiẏā* — "stubborn, unyielding," *nāgara* — "the refined, skilful [lover]," an epithet of Krishna; here of Rasika (cf. lahari 3, 24: *haṭhiẏā nāgaracandra*). "The guru's order" — Shyamananda's order (Uttara, lahari 2, 47–54). "Warrant" — *paramāṇe* (= *pramāṇe*).
[^17]: "By a single step" — *eka bhoṛā*: the editor explains in a footnote: *bhoṛā* — "step" (*pada*). The sense: whoever deviates even slightly from the order and leaves will be shunned by the Shyamanandi community.
[^18]: "Firmly and resolutely" — *dṛṛha bhālamate*. "The elder Thakurani" — *baṛa ṭhākurāṇī*, literally "the great Thakurani": according to the colophons of laharis 4 and 5, Gaurangadasi (cf. 34); further on she is also "the elder mother" (*baṛa mātā*, 47).
[^19]: "Two or four" — *dui cāri*: a few. "In secret" — *nigame* (cf. 43: *gupte*).
[^20]: Vidyutmala Thakurani — Pashchima, lahari 2, note 6 (there she comes to the Rasa-yatra). "Nava-Gauranga" — *naba gouraṅga* (so on the scan, crop ×2): it can also be understood as two names — "Nava, Gauranga." Keshavananda, Harikara, Vishnurama, Kalandi (*kālandī*, so on the scan; cf. the name Kalindi in Pashchima, lahari 15), Radhajivana, Balarama — not identified. "Disciples' disciples, servant-disciples" — *anuśiṣya bhṛtyaśiṣya*.
[^21]: "To their own places" — *yathāsthāne*. Yamuna Thakurani — Dakshina, lahari 16, 37, note 22.
[^22]: "Letter" — *lipi* (33), *likhā*, *patra* (39). "In Rasika's name" — *rasikera nāme*. "To mother Shyamapriya" — *mātā śyāmapriẏā sthāne*. "Her" (35) — *ihāre*: Gaurangadasi. "All over the letter" — *likhā cāribhite*, literally "on the four sides of the letter"; cf. 39: *patra cāri dhāre*.
[^23]: Dadhi-kada (*dadhikādā*) — the festive drenching with curd at the close of a festival (Uttara, lahari 1, 5, note 4); cf. 47: "dadhi-sankirtana." "We will hold an assembly" — *sabhā kari*.
[^24]: "Silk cloth" — *paṭabastra*. "In the garment she wore" — *pindhilā basane*.
[^25]: "Bring to disgrace" — *biṛambana* (also in 57): "to put to shame, expose to disgrace."
[^26]: "In an instant" (44) — *eka tile*. "On the spot" — *ṭhāẏa*. Yamadhara (*yamadhara*) — the editor explains in a footnote: "a kind of weapon"; apparently the *jamadhar*, the katar dagger.
[^27]: "The elder mother" — *baṛa mātā*: Gaurangadasi (note 18). "With words not to be refused" — *alaṅghya bacane*: imperiously, so that none could fail to come. 48b — *niṣkapaṭe bicāra karaẏe sarbajane*: understood as the beginning of her speech.
[^28]: "By a proper judgement of righteousness" — *yathocita sadharma bicāre* (on the scan *sadharmma*, in the OCR *dharma*). "Impose" — *karibe*.
[^29]: "What my faults and merits are" — *āmāra ye doṣaguṇa*: "[here is] what my faults are." "He" — Rasika, in whose name the forged letter was written (33–35).
[^30]: "I have no one I trust" — *pratyaẏa nāhika mora*. Padmanabha [and] Gopidasa — *padmanābha gopīdāsa*: it can also be understood as one person, "Padmanabha Gopidasa" (in lahari 5, 14, 18 the verb with them is in the honorific form, which does not distinguish number); not identified (cf. the brahmana Padmanabha among Shyamananda's disciples — Purva, lahari 1, 79).
[^31]: "Poison becomes nectar" (56) — *garala amṛta haẏa*: a hint at the outcome (lahari 5). "The bonds of worldly existence" — *bhababandha*. "Ignorant people" — *ajñagaṇa*.
[^32]: "Supremely deep" (58) — *ati parama gahana*. The author's signature (59) and the colophon are on pdf 186 (printed p. 142), checked against the scan; the title is as in the heading (note 1). Lahari 4 — 1–59 (pdf 184–186), complete. Below on the same page, under a rule, lahari 5 begins (files RM-4-05-001-023).
