# Uttara-vibhaga. Lahari 14. The Receiving by Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu, in a Dream, of the Command of Sri Sri Bhagavan to Depart to Krishna's Abode, at Bhograi

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Rasika visits Kendubilva, the place of Jayadeva, Vishnupur and Ambuya, goes round the holy places on both banks of the Ganga and every year goes to Nilachala for the Ratha-yatra; in every land he spreads the talk of Krishna, service to the guru, Krishna, the sadhus and the brahmanas, and kirtan, and on the road Sri Chaitanya travelled he performs worship; his daily and nightly routine is described by dandas; every year he celebrates twelve festivals, and all Utkala takes refuge in Krishna; one day in seclusion Rasika reflects that Chaitanya, Nityananda, Advaita and their associates have disappeared, and Shyamananda and the mahantas too have departed, and resolves to return to his own abode, to Krishna in Vrindavana; he reveals this to those close to him and tells two of them — Gopijanavallabha and Tulasi — that at Bhograi after sankirtana, as he was pondering a book with closed eyes, the youthful Shyama with a flute appeared to him and bade him depart, and when he hesitated, angrily repeated the command three times; they fall to the ground, and Rasika, embracing them, says that he will always remain with them*

*The fourteenth lahari.*

*Raga Karuna-shri.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "It remains to proclaim the qualities of the moon Gauranga."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the ocean of mercy,
whose own servant from birth is the son of Achyuta![^2]

**2.** At that time Rasikendra [passed] through Kendubilva;
seeing the place of Jayadeva, he became absorbed in bhava.

**3.** Viewing Vishnupur with delight for some days,
Rasika came to Ambuya to bathe in the Ganga.[^3]

**4.** He wanders, visiting the holy places on both banks of the Ganga;
he sees Sri Chaitanya-chand [and] Nityananda-raya.

**5.** All the holy places of the mahantas that were on both banks
Rasika-shekhara viewed with joy in his heart.[^4]

**6.** From there Rasikendra came in haste [to Nilachala];
every year he sees Jagannatha on the chariot.

**7.** Every year, going to the abode of Nilachala,
the son of Achyuta performs many pastimes.

**8.** Every year he made festival pilgrimages;
for twelve months [of the year] he always wanders, renowned in the world.[^5]

**9.** In every land he joyfully spread the talk of Krishna,
service to the guru, Krishna [and] the sadhus, the "Bhagavata" [and] the "Gita."

**10.** Service to the brahmanas, service to the sadhus, the spreading of kirtan —
in every land he widely spread this dharma.

**11.** Love for Krishna, the sixty-four [limbs] of bhakti and the rest
Rasika-Murari makes manifest in every land.[^6]

**12.** Not for one day does he know anything other than Krishna;
night and day Rasika always meditates on Krishna.

**13.** In the houses of all [his] disciples, [their] disciples' disciples [and] brothers
Rasikendra always dances in sankirtana.[^7]

**14.** [On the road] by which Sri Chaitanya-chandra went to Jagannatha and back —
on that road Rasikendra performs worship in many ways.[^8]

**15.** Somewhere he worships, somewhere he rolls [on the ground],
somewhere in sankirtana tears flow.[^9]

**16.** Service to Krishna, worship of Krishna, sankirtana of Krishna;
ceaselessly he listens to talks about Krishna.

**17.** In the morning — darshan of the mangala-arati;
for two dandas Prabhu meets with the Vaishnavas.[^10]

**18.** Then for four dandas he reads stotras, [scriptures] and so on;
bathing, worship of the Deity, remembrance and so on — four dandas.

**19.** Then two dandas — meditation in great absorption in bhava;
then four dandas — immersed in the rasa of the *Bhagavata*.

**20.** Then for two dandas he has the sadhus fed;
then for six dandas he hears the Puranas.

**21.** Then six dandas — with his circle, in the rasa of song:
Jayadeva's *Gita-govinda* and other works, in endless variety.[^11]

**22.** Thus he spends the day in the rasa of Krishna;
as evening comes, Prabhu beholds the arati.

**23.** For two dandas of the night he converses with the Vaishnavas;
eight dandas of the night — the delight of kirtan.

**24.** Then six dandas of the night — a gathering for music:
various instruments, various songs, wondrous tales.

**25.** From various lands skilled people come and stay [with him];
joyfully Prabhu listens to the singing of all.[^12]

**26.** Two dandas at night — the feeding of the sadhus;
together with the sadhus he joyfully eats.

**27.** Then for six dandas of the night he reads the *Bhagavata*,
sitting on his asana in seclusion, entirely alone.

**28.** Then for six dandas of the night he chants the Hari-nama;
resting in yoga-nidra, he ever meditates on Krishna.[^13]

**29.** Day and night, in every moment when there is leisure —
ceaselessly the Hari-nama, with eyes full of tears.

**30.** Always he chants the Hari-nama, holding the knots in his hand;
on roads and at landings he wanders, chanting the Hari-nama.[^14]

**31.** Day and night there is no rest [for him], not for a moment;
apart from Krishna, Rasika knows nothing.

**32.** Ceaselessly his mind is intoxicated with the rasa of sankirtana;
ceaselessly he explains to all the truth about Krishna.

**33.** Ceaselessly he travels for the sake of living beings;
freeing all from ignorance, he bestowed prema [on them].

**34.** He destroyed the evil desires of all people;
all people became intoxicated with prema-bhakti for Krishna.

**35.** To all the wicked, as many as there were in the whole world,
he proclaimed prema-bhakti in their hearts.

**36.** The moon of Rasikendras, whose vow is to destroy adharma,
establishes dharma with joy in his heart,

**37.** as Krishna, descending age after age,
establishes the sadhus, destroying the wicked.[^15]

**38.** Such a mahashaya, the crown jewel of Rasikendras,
destroying their sins, made everyone a sadhu.[^16]

**39.** Many lilas he performed in the world;
he distributed love for Krishna in every single home.

**40.** The mantra of Krishna, the tantra of Krishna, initiation into [the mantra of] Krishna and so on,
the essence [and] meaning of the Vedas and shastras that are well known —

**41.** with the consent of all the shastras, drawing out [their] essence,
he preached exclusive bhakti for Krishna.

**42.** All the bhakti that the scriptures have described for the good of living beings —
all that bhakti the son of Achyuta revealed.[^17]

**43.** Nothing more remained [to be done] in accomplishing bhakti;
the whole world became filled with love for Krishna.

**44.** The forest land of Utkala overflowed [with prema];
all understood: all [else] is false, Krishna is the true Lord.

**45.** Twelve festivals every year
Rasika-shekhara celebrated in great bliss.[^18]

**46.** All the kings and subjects there are in Orissa —
disciples, disciples of disciples [and] servants [of his] in every home.

**47.** Day and night they hold festivals everywhere;
with all their might they serve guru, Krishna [and] the sadhus.

**48.** Nothing remained with which to gladden [the world further]:
all in the land of Utkala had taken exclusive shelter [of Krishna].[^19]

**49.** One day Rasika-mahashaya, sitting in seclusion,
reflected in his heart:

**50.** by the word of all the sadhus [he] came to Bharata;
dispelling ignorance, [he] made all people sadhus.[^20]

**51.** [He] sees: the whole world has taken exclusive shelter [of Krishna];
in the hearts of children, the old [and] the young — the preaching [of] Krishna.[^21]

**52.** Women, men, and even the low-born [and] the Pukkashas,
Hunas, Pulindas and others, mlecchas, yavanas, rakshasas,

**53.** brahmanas, kshatriyas, vaishyas, shudras and others, as many [as there are] —
all, having taken exclusive shelter, are intoxicated with love for Krishna.[^22]

**54.** Apart from the jubilant clamour [of the name] of Krishna, nothing else is heard;
the whole world floats in the bliss of love for Krishna.

**55.** "I do not know what the terrible [age of] Kali will do at some time;
apart from this happiness, I see nothing else with my eyes."[^23]

**56.** Service to the guru, Krishna [and] the sadhus, festivals [and] processions
for sixty-two years [he] performed — renowned in the world.

**57.** Having come to earth, [he] took the shelter of Krishna;
in the bliss of love for Krishna he passed the time.[^24]

**58.** "Chaitanya Gosani, Nityananda, Advaita and others —
all have disappeared, [and with Them] Their associates, intimates, those who took refuge [in Them] and others.[^25]

**59.** Now [both] Shyamananda Prabhu [and] the host of mahantas —
before my eyes they have all departed.

**60.** Now, I understand, the terrible [age of] Kali will manifest:
all the sadhus have left [their] pastimes on earth.[^26]

**61.** I too shall go to my own place;
I shall go [and] see with my eyes Sri Nandanandana —

**62.** the Lord of Sri Radhika's life, Bhagavan Himself;
Krishna together with the maidens of Vraja I shall see with my eyes:

**63.** in Vrindavana, on the bank of the Yamuna, in the forest groves,
at the foot of the wish-fulfilling tree, Krishna on a throne of jewels —

**64.** bent in three places, graceful, Shyama, all-enchanting,
the youthful adolescent — between Radha and Chandravali.

**65.** Throng after throng the maidens of Vraja serve [Him] always;
the land [there] is chintamani, [that] abode cannot be described.[^27]

**66.** In such an abode I shall enjoy pastimes with Krishna;
I shall leave this body — this earthly dwelling — this very day."[^28]

**67.** Thus in seclusion [he] ponders all this in his mind;
the son of Achyuta disclosed [it] to those close to him.[^29]

**68.** Hearing this, all [felt as if] struck on the head by a thunderbolt;
rolling on the ground, they weep before Prabhu.[^30]

**69.** Prabhu says: "Gopijanavallabha [and] Tulasi!
More than life I love you all.[^31]

**70.** Birth after birth you all are dear to me;
many pastimes [you have] performed [with me] in the land of Utkala.

**71.** Whatever plans there were in my mind —
without your company I carry [them] out no longer.[^32]

**72.** Therefore I tell one thing to you two:
today, sitting in seclusion, I was pondering a book.

**73.** After the sankirtana, in that place, at Bhograi,
pondering the book, [I] was sitting with closed eyes.[^33]

**74.** A form [the colour] of a fresh cloud, a youthful adolescent,
in His hand an enchanting flute, beautiful to behold.

**75.** Coming before me, He spoke a word:
'The command [is given]: Rasikendra, depart!'[^34]

**76.** Absorbed in the taste of pondering the book, I delayed;
then, growing angry, He spoke a word:

**77.** 'You do not heed the command, Rasika-mahashaya!
To Krishna's abode, to your own place, set out!'[^35]

**78.** Commanding thus, He vanished,
and I, as if in a dream-trance, seemed to part with life.

**79.** This command was given to me at the end of the night:
again and again, three times He commanded insistently."[^36]

**80.** Hearing these words, both fell to the ground;
smiling, the jewel of Rasikendras speaks the truth [to them].

**81.** Embracing [them], Prabhu says: "Listen, you two:
I am always with you."[^37]

**82.** Nothing can be described of Rasika's life:
having descended, he purified the earth.

**83.** He delivered the three worlds by the mercy of [his] feet —
Rasikendra-raya, whose vow is to be a sheltering enclosure.[^38]

**84.** [His] pastimes are a boundless ocean, they cannot be described;
in brief I have described [them] by the mercy of his feet.

**85.** Listen to the "Rasika-mangala," all you sadhus:
without delay you will obtain the treasure of loving devotion to Krishna.[^39]

**86.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Uttara-vibhaga of the "Sri Rasika-mangala" ends the fourteenth lahari, called "The Receiving by Sri Sri Rasikananda Prabhu, in a Dream, of the Command of Sri Sri Bhagavan to Depart to Krishna's Abode, at Bhograi."*[^40]

"Their associates, intimates, those who took refuge [in Them]" — *sāṅgopāṅgāśraya*: *sāṅgopāṅga*, lit. "with limbs and sub-limbs," i.e. with the closest associates and their followers; *āśraya* — those who took refuge [in Them]; the same expression is in lahari 16, 5.
[^26]: "The host of mahantas" (59) — *mohāntera gaṇa*. "Before my eyes" — *dekhite dekhite*, lit. "while [I] was watching," i.e. one after another and quickly. On Shyamananda's departure — Pashchima, lahari 13. "Have departed" — *karilā gamana*. "Pastimes on earth" (60) — *pṛthibī-bilāsa*.
[^27]: "My own place" (61) — *āpanā sthāne*: the eternal abode (cf. 77: *nijasthāne*). Nandanandana — "the son of Nanda," Krishna. "The Lord of Sri Radhika's life" (62) — *śrīrādhikā-prāṇapati*; "Bhagavan Himself" — *sbaẏaṃ bhagabān*. "The maidens of Vraja" — *brajāṅganā*. "On the bank of the Yamuna, in the forest groves" (63) — *yamunā pulina kuñjabane*. "Bent in three places" (64) — *tribhaṅga*. Chandravali — the leader of the group of gopis rivalling Radha; pre-eminence among the gopis belongs to Radha (cf. 62). "Throng after throng" (65) — *yūtha yūtha*: a yutha is a group of gopis under a leader. "The land is chintamani" — *bhūmi cintāmaṇi*: the land of Vrindavana is made of wish-fulfilling chintamani stones; cf. *Brahma-samhita* 5.56 (*bhūmiś cintāmaṇi-gaṇa-mayī*), which also names the wish-fulfilling trees (63).
[^28]: "I shall enjoy pastimes" (66) — *kariba bilāsa*. "I shall leave this body" — *chāṛiba e deha* (so in the text). "This earthly dwelling" — *pṛthibīnibāsa*: understood as in apposition to "body"; it can also be read "I shall leave ... my life on earth." In the Gaudiya Vaishnava understanding the departure of an associate of the Lord is not an end under the rule of karma but a passage into the eternal (unmanifest) lila. "This very day" — *āji*.
[^29]: "In seclusion" (67) — *nigame* (note 13). "To those close to him" — *ātmagaṇe*. The son of Achyuta is Rasika.
[^30]: "Struck on the head by a thunderbolt" (68) — *muṇḍe haila bajrāghāta*. "Rolling" — *luṭhiẏā*. "Before Prabhu" — *prabhura sākṣāta*.
[^31]: "Gopijanavallabha [and] Tulasi" (69) — *gopījanaballabhatulasī*, a single compound; understood as two names (cf. 72, 80, 81: "you two," "both"). Gopijanavallabha is possibly the author himself, Gopijana-vallabha Dasa, the son of Rasamaya; Tulasi is apparently Thakura Tulasi (Tulasidasa), the kirtan singer (Uttara, lahari 2, note 15; Purva, lahari 1, note 34). "More than life" — *prāṇa haite*.
[^32]: "Birth after birth" (70) — *janme janme*. "Many pastimes [you have] performed [with me]" — *aneka karilā khelā*: *karilā* is taken as a second-person form; it can also be read "[I] performed." "In the land of Utkala" — *utkala-bhubana*. "Plans" (71) — *bicāra*. "Without your company I carry [them] out no longer" — *tomā sabā saṅga binā nā kariẏe āra*: i.e. he does nothing without sharing it with them — and therefore discloses the command to them.
[^33]: "One thing" (72) — *eka kathā*. "Pondering a book" — *puṃthi cintā kari*: apparently a sacred book he was reading (which one is not said). "After the sankirtana" (73) — *saṃkīrttana upānte*. "In that place, at Bhograi" — *se bhogarāi sthāne* (note 1). "[I] was sitting" — *baise*, lit. "sits" (third person): Rasika speaks of himself as of another. "With closed eyes" — *mudritanaẏane*.
[^34]: "[The colour] of a fresh cloud" (74) — *nabaghanaśyāma*; "a youthful adolescent" — *nabīna kiśora*: Krishna (the colophon speaks of "the command of Sri Sri Bhagavan"). "Flute" — *bā̃śī*. "The command [is given]" (75) — *ājñā hailā*.
[^35]: "I delayed" (76) — *karilũ helana*, lit. "I neglected, disregarded." "You do not heed the command" (77) — *ājñā nāhi māna*. "Mahashaya" — *mahāśaẏa*. "Set out" — *karaha bijaẏa*: of a ceremonial departure (lahari 2, note 28).
[^36]: "He vanished" (78) — *haila antarddhāna*. "As if in a dream-trance, seemed to part with life" — *yena sbapnābeśe tejinu parāṇa*: i.e. from the shock he as it were lost consciousness; hence the colophon's "in a dream" (*sbapnābeśe*). "At the end of the night" (79) — *niśi śeṣe*: apparently the vision came in the night after the sankirtana (72–73); "today" (72) — on the same day. "Three times He commanded" — *tina ājñā karila*, lit. "gave three commands"; "insistently" — *biśeṣe*.
[^37]: "Both" (80) — *dõhā*. "Smiling" — *hāsiẏā* (so on the scan; the OCR has *hāniẏā*). "The truth" — *tattba*. "The jewel of Rasikendras" — *rasikendramaṇi*. "Embracing [them]" (81) — *kole kari'*, "taking into his arms." "Always" — *anukṣaṇa*, "every moment": a promise to remain with those close to him even after his departure.
[^38]: From 82 — the author's words. "Of Rasika's life" — *rasika-caritra*. "Having descended" — *abatīrṇa haiẏā*: the author's enthusiastic praise (on the author's attitude to Shyamananda and Rasika — Purva, lahari 1, note 2; Purva, lahari 14, note 11; cf. note 15). "He delivered the three worlds" (83) — *uddhārila tina loka*. "Whose vow is to be a sheltering enclosure" — *śaraṇapañjarabānā*: *śaraṇa-pañjara* — "a cage (enclosure) of refuge," i.e. a sure protection for those who take shelter; *bānā* — "vow, sign" (note 15). Raya — *rāẏa*, an honorific, "lord."
[^39]: "A boundless ocean" (84) — *apāra samudra*. "In brief" — *saṃkṣepe*. "By the mercy of his feet" — *tā̃ra caraṇakṛpāẏa*. "Without delay" (85) — *abilambe*. "The treasure of loving devotion to Krishna" — *kṛṣṇapremabhaktidhana*.
