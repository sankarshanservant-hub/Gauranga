# Dakshina-vibhaga. Lahari 14. The Examination of the Truth of the Jiva and of Abhidheya

*A song in glory of Shyamananda; Rasika continues to instruct the raja and the three brothers: once born and touching the earth, the jiva by maya forgets Krishna and in childhood, youth, maturity and old age does not worship Him, wandering through eighty-four lakhs of species; Krishna is the Lord and father of all, and the four varnas were born from Him; the human body is rare and fleeting, its end is certain, kinsmen are strangers, pride ruins; Rasika bids them chant the name without delay, serve the sadhus and Vaishnavas while staying at home, take shelter of a guru wholly surrendered to Krishna, of whatever caste he may be, and never take instruction from one who is not devoted to Krishna, even if he is learned in the Vedas; the panditas cannot refute him, all give up the worship of other gods and surrender to Krishna; the author promises to tell how they gave up the killing of living beings*

*The fourteenth lahari.*

*Raga Varadi.*[^1]
*Ghosha: "O ocean of mercy, O merciful Shyama!*
*Pay heed to fallen and wretched people."*

**1.** Glory, glory to Shyamananda, the son of Durika!
Glory, glory to the life of Rasika, the treasure of [his] life![^2]

**2.** Thus Rasika speaks to the raja;
motionless, with affection, the three brothers listen.

**3.** He told of the jiva within the womb:
[there] the jiva meditates on Narayana without cease.

**4.** The jiva who comes forth from the womb onto the earth —
[that] jiva forgets everything by the Lord's maya.[^3]

**5.** Subject to the Lord, the jiva wanders by the fruits of karma;
as soon as he touched the earth, all [his] knowledge was stolen away.[^4]

**6.** In childhood the jiva wanders senseless,
having forgotten Krishna, the lord of his life.

**7.** Then in the age of pauganda, for so many days,
leaving Krishna's feet, the jiva is intoxicated with the taste of learning.[^5]

**8.** In the age of kishora the jiva is intoxicated with pride;
he does not worship Krishna's feet, absorbed in ignorance.

**9.** Then, after some days, comes the time of [mature] age;
blinded by various sense objects, he does not worship Krishna.[^6]

**10.** Then, after some days, old age enters the jiva;
not worshipping Krishna, the jiva suffers various afflictions.

**11.** In this way [go] the appearance and dissolution of the jiva;
not worshipping Krishna, he wanders through various species.

**12.** For eighty-four lakhs [of births] the jiva wanders through various species;
he does not worship Narayana, does not hear the words of the sadhus.

**13.** I shall tell in detail of the eighty-four lakhs [of births] of the jiva;
I shall tell the essential meaning of the shastras — listen attentively.[^7]

**14.** Twenty lakhs [of births] the jiva wanders as the immovable and the like —
I speak in accordance with the shastras, as is well known.

**15.** Then [he] becomes an aquatic: nine lakhs of births;
when one does not worship Krishna, all this comes about.

**16.** Eleven lakhs he wanders in various species of worms;
birth after birth he suffers, not knowing Krishna.

**17.** Then ten lakhs are births in the species of birds;
he wanders on the wheel of maya, not knowing Krishna the Lord.

**18.** Thirty lakhs he wanders in various species of animals;
not worshipping Krishna, the living being suffers much.

**19.** Then four lakhs of births as a human being:
[he] wanders in various low births, not knowing Krishna.[^8]

**20.** Then a hundred births, known as brahminhood;
not worshipping Krishna, [the jiva] is bewildered by false learning.[^9]

**21.** The brahmana reads various shastras, [remaining] senseless:
he does not accept, does not worship the feet of Sri Krishna.

**22.** Krishna is the Lord of all, the father of all;
they do not recognise Krishna the father, being bewildered.

**23.** From Krishna's mouth the brahmanas were born;
from Krishna's arms came the birth of the kshatriyas;

**24.** from Krishna's thighs came the birth of the vaishyas;
and the shudras were born from Krishna's feet.[^10]

**25.** His own father, Krishna, the jiva forgets by maya;
thus birth after birth he suffers much.

**26.** Exceedingly rare is this human body;
in a moment it perishes, unsteady as lightning.[^11]

**27.** Only then is this human body called rare
when [one] has love for Krishna [and] for the company of sadhus.[^12]

**28.** If one keeps the company of sadhus, one will attain Narayana;
without worship this body gets [only] much toil.

**29.** All that I have told of the jiva's birth and dying —
without worshipping Krishna [he] sets out on his wanderings again.

**30.** The end — now, or in a hundred years?
Together with the body its dying is born — such is the judgement of the shastras.[^13]

**31.** Having obtained such a body, why do they neglect [it]?
To cross the ocean of existence, Krishna's name is the raft.[^14]

**32.** The city of Kritanta has drawn near;
in the grip of Time, day by day life and strength depart.[^15]

**33.** Therefore make Narayana's name your provision [for the road];
the end is certain — this is the truth; meditate on Krishna.[^16]

**34.** Sing of Krishna, worship Krishna, remember Krishna;
be merciful to all living beings, serve the Vaishnavas.

**35.** Performing Krishna-sankirtana, take Hari's name;
with reverence hear of the host of Krishna's qualities.

**36.** Constantly serve the sadhus with a glad heart,
without guile, in the company of sadhus, in an abundance of prema.[^17]

**37.** Staying in your own home, worship Narayana;
with prema serve the sadhus, [perform] Krishna-sankirtana.

**38.** Gladly give food and water to guests;
leaving everything, worship Krishna, purified in mind.

**39.** Time always walks alongside the jiva;
child, youth [or] old man — that cannot be determined.

**40.** Some on the lap, some in the hand, some in the mouth [it holds]:
thus is the jiva subject to Time.[^18]

**41.** Mother, father, wife, son, friend, brother —
no one is one's own; know that all are strangers.[^19]

**42.** The one you call brother — he it is who will set fire to [your] mouth;
love Krishna, O living being — why perish in sorrow?[^20]

**43.** The pride of wealth, the pride of learning, the pride of youth,
the pride of family, the pride of kingdom and whatever [other] possession —

**44.** beguiled by these, do not perish in falsehood;
with firm attachment worship Narayana's feet.[^21]

**45.** Without worshipping Narayana there is no deliverance;
in the Vedas, shastras [and] Puranas the essence is devotion to Krishna.

**46.** True is Krishna, true is Krishna — know this for certain;
knowing all [else] to be false, take shelter of Krishna.[^22]

**47.** Blessed is that devotee of Krishna, [though] he live [but] five days;
what use is it, not worshipping Krishna, [to live] millions of kalpas?[^23]

**48.** Leaving everything, Prabhu, take shelter of Krishna;
child, youth [or] old man — do not be deluded.[^24]

**49.** There is no fixed age for worshipping Krishna:
Dhruva, Prahlada, Shuka, Hanu — from their very childhood.[^25]

**50.** Without delay worship Krishna, taking pains;
at the guru's place hear the narrations about Krishna.

**51.** As long as the jiva has not received [the mantra] in his ear from the guru,
know for certain: that person is called an animal.[^26]

**52.** All that [is offered] by his hand is like nectar,
but otherwise water is [as] urine: such is the testimony of the shastras.[^27]

**53.** Be he versed in the four Vedas, an ascetic in his conduct —
even leaving the body in a holy place, he still does not attain deliverance.

**54.** Shraddha and all else that he performs — all [leads] downward,
as long as he does not take shelter of Krishna the Lord.[^28]

**55.** Take shelter of a guru wholly surrendered [to Krishna],
of the guru who releases from the bonds of existence.

**56.** With your whole being take shelter at the guru's feet;
receiving instruction, enter Krishna's shelter.

**57.** [Be he] a brahmana of noble family, [versed] in the four Vedas and six shastras,
a sannyasi, an ascetic, shining with great knowledge —

**58.** whoever is not a devotee of Krishna wholly surrendered [to Him],
from him never take instruction.[^29]

**59.** If [a man] is wholly surrendered [to Krishna], of whatever caste he may be,
[and] with his whole being has taken shelter of Krishna —

**60.** take shelter of such a guru firmly:
by the mercy of such a guru the agitation of the mind departs.

**61.** With faith worship the feet of such a guru —
then without delay you will obtain the treasure of devotion to Krishna.[^30]

**62.** Thus Rasika speaks to the raja,
[citing] the testimony of the six shastras, the Bhagavata [and] the Nigama.[^31]

**63.** Then the assembly of panditas listened [to him]:
who has the power to refute such [words]?

**64.** All accepted Rasika's words as true;
abandoning ignorance, all began to meditate on Krishna.

**65.** Abandoning the worship of various other gods, all the people
with their whole being took shelter of Krishna.[^32]

**66.** How they gave up the killing of living beings and the like —
that I shall tell in detail; listen, everyone.[^33]

**67.** Listen to the Rasika-mangala, all friends:
without delay you will obtain the treasure of prema-bhakti for Krishna.

**68.** Adorning himself with Shyamananda's feet,
the son of Rasamaya joyfully composed [this].

*Thus in the Dakshina-vibhaga of the Sri Rasika-mangala ends the fourteenth lahari, called "The Examination of the Truth of the Jiva and of Abhidheya."*[^34]

[^1]: The lines before the raga are the lahari's heading ("চতুর্দশ-লহরী"), in italics without a number. Lahari 14 begins on the lower half of pdf 121 (printed p. 83), after the colophon of lahari 13 (files RM-2-13-051-075). Raga Varadi (*barāṛī*); the ghosha is the same as in laharis 7 and 10 (lahari 7, note 1). There is no mangala-shloka: after the raga and ghosha the song begins with ॥ 1 ॥. The title follows the colophon on pdf 123 (checked against the scan): "জীবতত্ত্ব ও অভিধেয়-তত্ত্ববিচারনাম চতুর্দশ-লহরী" (*jībatattva o abhidheya-tattva-bicāra*); the 1997 edition reads the same. Jiva-tattva is the truth of the jiva (lahari 13, note 22). Abhidheya is the means of attaining the goal, i.e., the practice of bhakti (cf. sambandha, abhidheya, prayojana — CC, Madhya 20–25). The whole lahari is on pdf 121–123; the summary line follows the scans.
[^2]: Durika is Shyamananda's mother (Purva, lahari 1, note 11). "The life of Rasika, the treasure of [his] life" — *rasika-jībana prāṇadhana* (cf. lahari 7, 1).
[^3]: Jiva (*jība*) — as in lahari 13 (50–71; lahari 13, note 22). "Comes forth onto the earth" — *bhūmigata haẏa*. In the 1997 edition the first line reads "তাহাতে সেই জীব…" ("then that jiva…").
[^4]: "As soon as he touched the earth" — *pṛthbī paraśite*: once born and fallen onto the ground, the infant loses the memory of his prayers in the womb (cf. Bhagavatam 3.31). "Wanders by the fruits of karma" — *phire karmmaphale*.
[^5]: Pauganda is the age from five to ten, kishora (8) from ten to fifteen. "Intoxicated with the taste of learning" — *matta bidyārase*: absorbed in study for worldly ends. "With pride" (8) — *made*.
[^6]: "The time of [mature] age" — *baẏasa samaẏa*, lit. "the time of age".
[^7]: The count of species (14–19): immovable — 20 lakhs, aquatic — 9, worms — 11, birds — 10, animals — 30, humans — 4; in all 84 lakhs (8,400,000), as in the verse usually cited from the Padma Purana (*jalajā nava-lakṣāṇi…*). A lakh is one hundred thousand. The hundred births as a brahmana (20) are the author's addition beyond this count.
[^8]: "Low births" — *antyaja-yoni*: births outside the four varnas.
[^9]: "Brahminhood" — *brahmaṇya*. "False learning" — *kubidyā*.
[^10]: Cf. Bhagavatam 11.5.2 and the Purusha-sukta (Rigveda 10.90): from the mouth, arms, thighs and feet of the Purusha the four varnas were born.
[^11]: "Perishes" — *bhaṅgura*; "unsteady as lightning" — *taṛita-asthira*. Cf. Bhagavatam 11.9.29: human birth is rare and impermanent, yet it grants the highest goal.
[^12]: "Love" — *senaha* (= *sneha*; so the editor explains below the line; the gloss is not translated). The line may also be understood as "has love for Krishna in the company of sadhus."
[^13]: "The end", "dying" — *maraṇa*, *mṛtyu*. "Together with the body its dying is born" — *deha saṅge mṛtyu jāta*; cf. Bhagavad-gita 2.27.
[^14]: "Raft" — *bhelā*.
[^15]: "The city of Kritanta" — *kṛtānta-nagara*: the abode of Yama, lord of the netherworld (Kritanta, "He who makes an end"). "In the grip of Time" — *kālagraste*. "Life and strength" — *āẏurbbalā*; so the editor explains below the line (*āẏuḥ ebaṃ parākrama*; the gloss is not translated).
[^16]: "Provision [for the road]" — *sambala*.
[^17]: "In an abundance of prema" — *prema atireke*.
[^18]: *keha kole keha hāte keha-i mukhete*: understood as a figure of Time (Kala) holding the jivas — some on its lap, some in its hand, some already in its mouth; the sense is approximate. "Thus" — *hena-i yugate* (*yugati* = *yukti*, "manner"); it can also be understood as "in such a yuga".
[^19]: "Wife" — *strīri* (so in the edition).
[^20]: "Will set fire to [your] mouth" — *sei poṛe mukhe*: the rite of mukhagni — at the funeral pyre a son or brother touches fire to the mouth of the one who has left the body. "Perish" — *mara*, lit. "die".
[^21]: "Possession" — *sampada*. "Do not perish in falsehood" — *nā mara mithyāẏa*, lit. "do not die by falsehood (in vain)". "With firm attachment" — *dṛṛha anurāge*.
[^22]: "All [else] is false" — *saba mithyā*: cf. lahari 13, 9 and note 5.
[^23]: "Five days" — *pā̃ca dina*: i.e., a very short time. "Millions of kalpas" — *koṭi kalpe*; a kalpa is a day of Brahma.
[^24]: "Prabhu" — Rasika's address to the raja. "Do not be deluded" — *nā kara bharama*: do not think that the time for serving Krishna has not yet come (cf. 49).
[^25]: Dhruva (Bhagavatam 4.8–9), Prahlada (Bhagavatam 7.5–9), Shuka(deva) and Hanu(man) were devotees from childhood. "Hanu" — *hanu*, Hanuman.
[^26]: "Has not received [the mantra] in his ear from the guru" — *gurukarṇa … nāhi haẏa*, lit. "there has been no guru-ear": has not received initiation (the guru speaks the mantra into the disciple's ear). "Is called an animal" — *paśu bali*.
[^27]: "By his hand" — *tā'ra hāte*: by the hand of the initiated. "But otherwise water is [as] urine" — *tā nā ha'le jala mūtra*: the offering of the uninitiated is impure.
[^28]: "Leaving the body" — *maile*, lit. "on dying": it was held that departing this life in a holy place (Kashi and others) grants liberation by itself. Shraddha is the memorial rite for the ancestors. "[Leads] downward" — *adhogati*. "Krishna the Lord" — *kṛṣṇapati*.
[^29]: "Devotee of Krishna" — *kārṣṇa*: the scan has "কাষ্ণ´", with the reph shifted to the right (cf. *kārṣṇa-jana*, Purva, lahari 10, note 13); the OCR of the 1997 edition has "কানা" (garbled). "Wholly surrendered" — *ananya-śaraṇa*. Cf. the verse of the Padma Purana cited in the Hari-bhakti-vilasa (first vilasa): a brahmana expert in the six duties, mantras and tantras cannot be a guru if he is not a Vaishnava, while a Vaishnava, even one born among dog-eaters, can.
[^30]: 55–61: the guru is defined not by caste but by devotion to Krishna (59); cf. CC, Madhya 8: "Whether a brahmana, a sannyasi or a shudra — whoever knows the science of Krishna, he is a guru."
[^31]: "Nigama" — the Vedas. "The six shastras" — lahari 12, note 12. "Testimony" — *paramāṇe* (= *pramāṇe*).
[^32]: "The worship of various other gods" — *nānā debāntara-pūjā*: thus Rasika's instruction at the beginning of lahari 13 is fulfilled (6; lahari 13, note 4).
[^33]: "The killing of living beings" — *jībahatyā*: apparently animal sacrifice; the account is in lahari 15 (pdf 124).
[^34]: The colophon is on pdf 123 (checked against the scan); on the title see note 1. Lahari 15 begins on pdf 124.
