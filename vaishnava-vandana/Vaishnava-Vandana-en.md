# The Vaishnava-vandanas

*"Praises of the Vaishnavas": lists of the associates of Sri Chaitanya*

*The Sanskrit vandana attributed to Jiva Gosvami; the* Vaishnava-vandana *and* Vaishnavabhidhana *of Devakinandana Dasa*

*Translated from the Sanskrit and Bengali*

---

## Translator's note

**What a Vaishnava-vandana is.** This is the name of verse lists of Vaishnavas — above all the associates
of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, Nityananda and Advaita — which devotees recited in the morning: "Whoever, rising
at dawn, reads the Vaishnava-vandana will never know torment." Unlike the biographies, the vandanas tell no
story; they give names — sometimes a single word, sometimes a short characterisation: who someone was in
Krishna-lila, what he was famous for, whom the Lord especially loved. They therefore complement Kavi
Karnapura's *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* (1576), which matches the Lord's associates with their forms in Vraja,
and the lists of the "branches of the tree of devotion" in the *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Adi 9–12). Some
facts are known only from the vandanas: about brahmacharis among the associates at Navadvipa, about many
sannyasis around the Lord, about the deeds of Nityananda's associates.

**Part 1. The Sanskrit vandana attributed to Jiva Gosvami** (153 verses). The author several times calls himself
"Jiva" and signs at the end: "I, Jiva, have completed it"; the colophon calls the vandana "following the
sampradaya of Madhva". It is not in the list of Jiva's works in the *Bhakti-ratnakara*; only two manuscripts
are known, and the attribution remains disputed. B. Majumdar, who published the text in an appendix to his
book *Sri-Chaitanya-chariter upadan* (1938/39; 2nd ed. 1959), inclined to think it was Jiva's own and that
the Bengali vandanas followed it. There is no public-domain edition of this text; only the old Sanskrit text
has been translated, from two independent OCR copies of Majumdar's two editions, without his notes. Corrupt
places are noted in the footnotes.

**Part 2. Devakinandana Dasa, the Vaishnava-vandana** (147 couplets) — the most widespread Bengali vandana.
Devakinandana was a disciple of Purushottama Dasa, the son of Sadashiva Kaviraja among Nityananda's associates,
and so wrote in the sixteenth century. The translation follows the edition of Atulakrishna Gosvami (Calcutta,
1910), where Devakinandana's vandana is printed together with others. The numbering of couplets agrees with
Majumdar's; two couplets bracketed by the editor are numbered 16a and 42a. Raga lines and refrains (dhuya)
are not numbered. Devakinandana's "great" vandana, according to Majumdar, survives only in manuscript and has
not been found in a public-domain edition.

**Part 3. Devakinandana Kaviraja, the Vaishnavabhidhana** — a Sanskrit list of names (53 verses), following
almost exactly the order of the Bengali vandana; from the same 1910 edition.

**Not translated** are two other vandanas of the 1910 edition — the "First vandana" on Nityananda's associates
and a vandana in tripadi, both attributed to Vrindavana Dasa (see `CATALOG.md`).

**Principles.** Only the text of the vandanas is translated — without the original or a word-for-word
rendering. The Russian and English translations were made in parallel, with the same numbering and the same
notes. The notes say briefly who each associate is (according to the *Gaura-ganoddesha* — GGD, by our
numbering; the *Chaitanya-charitamrita* — CC; the *Chaitanya-bhagavata* — CB) and mark divergences between
the vandanas, from GGD and from Majumdar's conclusions. Where a vandana differs from what Gaudiya Vaishnavas
accept (for example, a different identification of an associate), the translation is exact and the
explanation is given in a note. At the end of the book there is a **consolidated index of persons**: where
each associate is mentioned in the three texts and in GGD.

# Part 1. The Sanskrit *Vaishnava-vandana* attributed to Jiva Gosvami

*Introduction; Gaura and His family; Nityananda, His consorts and descendants; Madhavendra, Advaita and His line; the associates at Navadvipa; the sannyasis; the Gosvamis of Vrindavana; associates in Gauda and Orissa; Nityananda's followers; conclusion: the succession from Madhavendra Puri (verses 1–153)*

## Introduction

**1.** He whose elder brother is the glorious Sanatana, like unto the Eternal, and whose younger brother is Sri Vallabha — that Sri Rupa is the true refuge of Jiva.[^01-1]

**2.** Bhagavan, the son of Sri Shachi — Krishna — descended in the age of Kali with all the avataras and their devotees: the Lord absorbed in their various moods.

**3.** "He who utters the syllables 'Krishna' and whose lustre is not dark, accompanied by His parts, intimates, weapons and associates — in the age of Kali the intelligent worship Him with sacrifices consisting chiefly of sankirtana."[^01-2]

**4.** The one God is the supreme Krishnachandra; He is the One who bears the name Krishna Chaitanya; and this God Nityananda is His own form (svarupa). Dividing Himself in two […], may He do [good] to us.[^01-3]

**5.** His dear ones, Advaita and the others, are His very self; the second [after Him is Nityananda]; His many principal energies are glorious Rupa and the others. The Lord born of Shachi is a wide-spreading tree of prema: may It give shade to those scorched by the heat [of worldly existence].

**6.** To praise them and to remember them bestows every perfection. And this [praise] is made by a certain jiva — Jiva, who does not know which of them comes first and which after.[^01-4]

**7.** Forgive my offences, O great souls whose hearts are with Krishna — saints who see no faults in others, eager to show mercy to the wretched.

**8.** As you are here, as [the knowers] truly know you, so may Bhagavan make [me] speak, impelled by His command.

## Gaura and His family

**9.** I praise Shachi and Jagannatha, who are Yashoda and Nanda;[^01-5] both their sons are Vishvarupa and the divine Vishvambhara.

**10.** Then I praise Vishvarupa, king among sannyasis, called Shankararanya — the wondrous elder brother of Chaitanya.[^01-6]

**11.** I praise Sri Gaurachandra, whose body is made of rasa, the abode of a treasury of compassion: taking the mood of Radhika in order to relish Sri Hari, to deliver the hosts of souls stained by the filth of Kali and devoid of every [good] disposition, He joyfully appeared with throngs of His associates in Sri Navadvipa.[^01-7]

**12.** I praise the goddess Lakshmipriya, and then Vishnupriya; then the divine Gadadhara, who is truly the Lord's second body.[^01-8]

**13.** He is the disciple of Vidyanidhi, a mine of the rasa of devotion to the Lord; this is the steadfast Gadadhara, beloved of all the devotees.

## Nityananda and His line

**14.** I praise Padmavati and her husband Haddipa Pandita:[^01-9] the merciful Nityananda became their son.

**15.** I praise the divine Nityananda — Balabhadra, the Lord Himself, the root of bliss, the fearless one, the deliverer of the world, the guru.

**16.** He is both purusha and prakriti, by the distinction of outer and inner: in different bodies He serves Sri Krishna.[^01-10]

**17.** I praise Sri Vasudha-devi, beloved of Nityananda Prabhu, daughter of Sri Suryadasa, awakened by the power of the Lord.[^01-11]

**18.** I praise Sri Jahnavi-devi, the disciple of Sri Puri-Ishvara, whom the knowers of secrets call Ananga-manjari.[^01-12]

**19.** When the Lord had taken sannyasa and gone away, by His command she serves Nityananda — His very own form — with supreme love, firm in her vow.[^01-13]

**20.** Ever drawn by separation, the mistress went to Vrindavana; when she wished to see Gopinatha, He pulled the knot of her garment.

**21.** And the goddess whose knot had been pulled said to Him — the dawning of rasa: "Soon I will come to You — to the place beside Your feet."[^01-14]

**22.** I praise Lord Virachandra — Hari, the Lord Sri Chaitanya, appearing a second time, the deliverer of the three worlds,[^01-15]

**23.** devoted to the Vedic dharma and yet detached, free from egotism, without pride, self-controlled — the servant of Jahnavi here.[^01-16]

**24.** The daughter of Nityananda Prabhu, whose nature is melted in Radha and Krishna, the wife of Madhava Acharya, whose form is being, consciousness and bliss,

**25.** the chief — Sri Prema-manjari, the excellent mother of the worlds — I praise Ganga, the giver of prema, the purifier of the three worlds.[^01-17]

**26.** This Ganga, the disciple of Jahnavi, together with the purifying lords […], purifies the three worlds.[^01-18]

**27.** [I praise] Madhava, the ornament of the twice-born, who appeared wishing to take as wife Ganga, who had descended [to earth] — like Madhava in form, his body full of rasa — named Prema.[^01-19]

**28.** A disciple of Ishvara Puri, gone to the far shore of all the darshanas, foremost among the devotees of Vishnu, adorned with a host of good qualities,

**29.** the wise one examined them and, setting aside karma and knowledge, established the truth of love for Krishna — an ocean of mercy.

## Madhavendra, Advaita and His line

**30.** The ornament of the ancient line of ascetics, lord of sages, devoted to the first guru-Lord — I praise Sri Madhavendra, who made devotion to Hari manifest.[^01-20]

**31.** I praise Advaita, the merciful, the supremely compassionate — the very abode of Shambhu in person: by His austerities the son of Sri Shachi was brought here together with His associates.[^01-21]

**32.** [I praise] His beloved named Sita — the primal energy of Kailasa, mother of the three worlds: pleased with her, Sri Jagannatha bestowed His grace on the three worlds.[^01-22]

**33.** Among His sons, the one called Achyutananda — him I praise, supreme bliss, the beloved of Krishna Chaitanya;

**34.** the one called Achyuta, who knows the truth of Sri Krishna Chaitanya, servant of the steadfast Sri Gadadhara, an ocean of good qualities.

**35.** Those followers of Sri Advaita and [His] sons who wholly worshipped with devotion the merciful Sri Chaitanya-Hari, the son of Shachi, as the Lord of all — [are worthy of praise]. But many others were struck down by God — them we do not honour; save Achyuta, they are abandoned: I pass them over.[^01-23]

## The associates at Navadvipa

**36.** I praise Srivasa — Narada; Malini — the [Lord's] second mother; then Narayani-devi, who partook of the nectar of His lips.[^01-24]

**37.** I praise Narayani's son — Vrindavana Dasa, the excellent, who described the qualities of Sri Nityananda and Chaitanya.[^01-25]

**38.** I praise Haridasa — the abode of Brahma, who made the holy name of Hari manifest — and Jagadananda Pandita, another form of Vani.[^01-26]

**39.** Then I praise Gopinatha, who offered praise to Chaitanya;[^01-27] then Murari Gupta, the great soul, Hanuman.[^01-28]

**40.** I praise Sri Chandrashekhara, ever cool as the moon; Acharyaratna; Govinda and Garuda, whose minds are with Gaura.[^01-29]

**41.** I praise also Mukunda Datta, the great soul, maddened by singing the pure qualities of Sri Krishna, praised by the kinnaras.[^01-30]

**42.** I praise Vasudeva Datta, full of greatness: by the touch of the breeze from his body one at once attains prema.[^01-31]

**43.** Damodara and Pitambara, Jagannatha, Shankara and Narayana — these five, free from desires, I praise — saints, great souls.[^01-32]

**44.** I praise Nilambara Chakravarti, renowned as the Lord's maternal grandfather, who cast the horoscope that contained what was to come.[^01-33]

**45.** I praise Sri Rama Pandita, devoted to the welfare of all beings,[^01-34] and Narayana Gupta, the great soul, the one abode of virtues.[^01-35]

**46.** I praise Gangadasa, who lived in Navadvipa, the supreme guru, and Sri Vishnudasa, and the one called Sri Sudarshana.[^01-36]

**47.** I praise Sadashiva, Vidyanidhi and Shrigarbha, Shrinidhi, Buddhimanta and the excellent Srila Shuklambara —

**48.** these six great souls, brahmacharis full of love;[^01-37] and Sri Ramadasa, and Kavichandra, an ocean of mercy.[^01-38]

**49.** I praise Vijaya the scribe,[^01-39] and the pure Acharya Ratneshvara,[^01-40] and the generous Shridhara, the renowned,[^01-41] and Vanamali together with his son.[^01-42]

**50.** I praise Halayudha and Vasudeva, the pure, whose hearts are with Sri Chaitanya, and Ishana Dasa, the object of Shachi-devi's affection.[^01-43]

**51.** With humility I praise Shriman and Sanjaya, the merciful: their mark is supreme bliss, their hearts are given to Chaitanya.[^01-44]

**52.** Garuda, Kashishvara, Jagadisha and Gangadasa, both, the sweet Krishnananda I praise, and the excellent Rama-Mukunda.[^01-45]

**53.** I praise Vallabha Acharya, whose daughter Lakshmi captivates the heart: rich in virtues, he gave her to the son of Shachi, the bridegroom.[^01-46]

**54.** Then I praise Sanatana Pandita, rich in virtues, who gave his daughter Vishnupriya to the son of Shachi.

**55.** I praise the brahmana Kashinatha, and Vanamali Acharya, who conceived and arranged the marriage of Lakshmi-devi.[^01-47]

## The sannyasis

**56.** Then I praise Ishvara Puri: accepting him as His guru, the Lord, called Chaitanya, considered Himself blessed.[^01-48]

**57.** I praise also Sri Keshava Bharati, honoured by the sannyasis, from whom Mahaprabhu took the renunciation of dharma.[^01-49]

**58.** Then I praise Ramachandra Puri, [he] to whom the Lord always yielded;[^01-50] [I praise] Sri Puri Paramananda, called Uddhava, dear to Hari.[^01-51]

**59.** Then I praise Damodara Puri, like unto Satyabhama;[^01-52] I praise Narasimha Tirtha, then Sukhananda Puri;

**60.** then the one called Govindananda, and Brahmananda Puri; the one called Nrisimhananda, and Satyananda Bharati.

**61.** I praise Garuda Avadhuta, endowed with wondrous prema; then I praise Vishnu Puri, who composed the *Bhakti-ratnavali*;[^01-53]

**62.** and Brahmananda Svarupa, then Krishnananda Puri; Sri Raghava Puri I praise with devotion and supreme joy;

**63.** I praise Vishveshvarananda, then Sri Keshava Puri; I praise then Anubhavananda and Chidananda, pure of heart.[^01-54]

## The Gosvamis of Vrindavana

**64.** I praise the two masters — Rupa and Sanatana, supreme bliss, detached and merciful, dwelling in Vrindavana.

**65.** Perfumed by even a trace of the fragrance of their lotus feet, may I, named Jiva, serve them here, birth after birth.[^01-55]

**66.** Sri Rupa, endowed with the Lord's power, examining all the shastras, established the supreme truth — love for Krishna — an ocean of mercy.[^01-56]

**67.** Sanatana, seeking nothing for himself, composed the *Hari-bhakti-vilasa* and other works — what devotees ought to do — under the name of Gopala Bhatta.[^01-57]

**68.** And the wise Gopala Bhatta, living near Sanatana, devoted to the qualities of Hari, happily spent his days and nights here;

**69.** constantly hearing what he told of the Lord's form and qualities, he truly considered himself blessed in every way.[^01-58]

**70.** I praise Raghunatha Dasa, who dwells at Radha-kunda, who knows the whole truth of Chaitanya, the best, who has given up every other feeling.[^01-59]

**71.** I praise the Gosvami named Raghava, who sports at Govardhana, a great soul, wandering [there] in a special mood.[^01-60]

**72.** With humility I praise Raghunatha Bhatta, teacher of the *Sri Bhagavata*,[^01-61] Lokanatha Gosvami and the pure Bhugarbha Thakura.[^01-62]

**73.** With joy I praise the pure Prabodhananda Sarasvati, by whom the *Chandramrita* was composed, whose disciple is Gopala Bhatta.[^01-63]

**74.** Then I praise Kashishvara,[^01-64] then Shuddha Sarasvati,[^01-65] and then Raghavananda, imbued with the mood of Nityananda,[^01-66]

**75.** in whose house the son of Padmavati, in play, joined a kadamba flower to a pomegranate tree.[^01-67]

## The associates in Gauda and Orissa

**76.** I praise Purandara — here truly equal to Angada: a certain best of brahmanas saw his tail in his house.[^01-68]

**77.** I praise the best of Mishras — Kashi, dwelling in Utkala, the spotless: in his abode lived Gaurahari, worshipped by his devotion.[^01-69]

**78.** Then I praise Vaninatha, whose life is Sri Jagannatha;[^01-70] then I praise Ramananda, full of all the marks of devotion:

**79.** from the cloud of his mouth the merciful Chaitanya rained upon the earth the nectar — a multitude of truths about devotion to Him.[^01-71]

**80.** Then I praise Vakreshvara, the heart of the Lord, who is attained with the greatest difficulty: in his presence the Lord performed kirtana in the bliss of prema.[^01-72]

**81.** I praise that Sugriva Mishra — Govinda, the best of the twice-born, whose greatness in bhakti-yoga is renowned on earth:[^01-73]

**82.** so that the Lord might go to the forest [of Vrindavana], he built from the land of Sri Navadvipa across the whole land of Gauda a bridge made of mind.[^01-74]

**83.** I praise here Gadadhara Dasa — the daughter of Vrishabhanu, whose body is non-different from Sri Krishna, the embodiment of mahabhava.[^01-75]

**84.** I praise Sadashiva the vaidya: at his mere touch even a stone at once melts — what then of the living![^01-76]

**85.** I praise Shivananda Sena, devoted to firm faith and peace, who knows nothing but the Lord's feet.[^01-77]

**86.** I praise that Mukunda Dasa whose son is Raghunandana — truly Kama, the husband of Rati — the one who fed Gopala.[^01-78]

**87.** The devotion of Sri Mukunda Dasa is sung by people to this day: seeing a peacock feather, drawn away by love for Krishna,

**88.** he at once lost his senses, immersed in supreme bliss, and, unaware of the outer world, fell down from his high seat.[^01-79]

**89.** With devotion I praise Narahari Dasa, who gave the play of his feelings to Chaitanya, called Madhumati — pure, blessed, seeing nothing but Krishna.[^01-80]

**90.** And this Raghunandana is most worthy, the disciple of Narahari, righteous, honoured; saintly in character from childhood, pure, his heart cleansed by devotion.

**91.** I praise then Raghunatha Dasa,[^01-81] Purandara Acharya, generous in deeds, and Sri Krishnadasa, slave of Hari's feet, gentle, merciful, loving the people of Bhagavan.[^01-82]

**92.** I praise Paramananda Pandita, the Lord's fellow student,[^01-83] and Devananda Pandita, the reciter of the *Sri Bhagavata*.[^01-84]

**93.** I praise Acharyaratna, who knows the inmost essence of prema,[^01-85] and Govinda, Madhavananda and Vasu Ghosha — treasuries of virtue.[^01-86]

**94.** I praise the Dasa named Purushottama, endowed with majesty, who made the karavi flower in his ears smell like a lotus.[^01-87]

**95.** I praise Abhirama Dasa, who on earth is called Shridama: a log that only many could lift he playfully made into a flute.[^01-88]

**96.** I praise Sri Sundarananda, the form of the cowherd Sudama: his disciple gave the name of Hari here even to tigers.[^01-89]

**97.** I praise also Sri Gauridasa — the cowherd called Subala: led by him, Advaita Thakura [came] to Utkala, to supreme bliss.[^01-90]

**98.** By him the Deities of Sri Chaitanya and Nityananda were made manifest in person: by the sight of these Deities the bonds of karma are at once destroyed.[^01-91]

**99.** Then I praise Parameshvara Thakura, the self-luminous: dancing, he made the jackals hear the name of Hari.[^01-92]

**100.** Then I praise Pippalai, overwhelmed by a childlike mood — I praise Kamalakara Dasa, whose bliss is sankirtana.[^01-93]

**101.** I praise the one called Purushottama Tirtha, the crown of rasikas;[^01-94] then I praise Kaliya Krishnadasa, overwhelmed by prema alone.[^01-95]

**102.** I praise Sharnga Thakura, who revealed his greatness: he gave snakes places in his own dwelling.[^01-96]

**103.** Then I praise Makaradhvaja, beautiful, the one abode of virtues, who always sings kirtana to Krishna near the Lord.[^01-97]

**104.** Then Bhagavatacharya,[^01-98] Sri Kaviraja Mishra,[^01-99] and then Ananta Acharya, who lived at Navadvipa.[^01-100]

**105.** I praise the Pandita named Madhu[^01-101] and the one called Govinda Acharya, who zealously described the secret of Radha and Krishna.[^01-102]

**106.** Then I praise Sarvabhauma Bhattacharya — Brihaspati;[^01-103] then Prataparudra, by whom the Lord's six arms were seen.[^01-104]

**107.** I praise the brahmana Raghunatha, the physician Sri Vishnudasa; I praise also his brother — Vanamali Dasa;[^01-105]

**108.** Vipradasa of Utkala and then the brahmana Haridasa: by them Hari, the son of Sri Shachi, was won over by love.[^01-106]

**109.** I praise Kanai Khutiya, a treasury of the rasa of love for Krishna, whose two auspicious sons are Jagannatha and Balarama.[^01-107]

**110.** I praise Jagannatha, at whose singing the trees here weep, losing themselves, and the compassionate Balarama the Oriya, to whom Baladeva and Jagannatha are subject.[^01-108]

**111.** With devotion I praise the Thakura named Govindananda: for the Lord's sake he built a bridge made of mind.[^01-109]

**112.** Then I praise Kashishvara, the one called Sri Simheshvara, and Shivananda Pandita, and then Chandaneshvara.[^01-110]

**113.** With the highest feeling I praise Madhava Pattanayaka; then I praise Hari Bhatta and Baladeva Mahiti;

**114.** then Subuddhi Mishra and Shrinatha, the best of Mishras; I praise Sri Tulasi Mishra and Kashinatha Mahiti.[^01-111]

**115.** Ramananda, first in the Vasu line, together with his family;[^01-112] both Purushottama Brahmachari and the Pandita named Madhu,[^01-113]

**116.** servants of the Lord Sri Chaitanya, merciful great souls. All these are full of great compassion, seeking nothing for themselves.

**117.** I praise the brahmana Ramachandra and Shridhara Pandita, generous in virtues;[^01-114] I praise Yadu Kavichandra and Dhananjaya Pandita, who gave away his wealth:[^01-115]

**118.** renowned is his renunciation — he gave all he had to the Lord, and this magnanimous pandita himself took only a pot [for alms] and a loincloth.[^01-116]

**119.** [I praise] Pandita Sri Jagannatha, then Acharya Lakshmana; then I praise Krishnadasa and Pandita Suryadasa.[^01-117]

**120.** Then I praise Krishna's flute — Vamshivadana Thakura;[^01-118] Murari Chaitanya Dasa, who played with pythons terrible as Yama.[^01-119]

**121.** I praise Jagannatha Sena, Paramananda Gupta,[^01-120] the boy called Ramadasa[^01-121] and then Kavichandra.

**122.** I praise Sri Vallabhacharya,[^01-122] then Kamsari Sena; then I praise Bhaskara — the form of Vishvakarma.[^01-123]

**123.** I praise Balarama Dasa, specially distinguished as a teacher of song, who serves Nityananda Prabhu — supreme bliss.[^01-124]

**124.** I praise Mahesha Pandita, overwhelmed by madness for Krishna;[^01-125] I praise the dancer, the Pandita — the one called Jagadisha Pandita;[^01-126]

**125.** and Thakura Krishnadasa, wholly devoted to Nityananda, who with devotion kept the divine Nityananda in his house.

**126.** Gauridasa went there, took his Lord, spoke [a word] and led Him away: who else is so devoted to Him, so absorbed [in Him]?

**127.** Who can describe the greatness of Sri Krishnadasa's prema? From separation from Nityananda he was as one mad for seven months;

**128.** giving him His sight once more, He Himself calmed him.[^01-127] I praise then the best of avadhutas — the one called Paramananda.[^01-128]

**129.** [I praise] Anadi Gangadasa[^01-129] and Pandita Vilasi; I praise the Dasa named Sri Yadunatha, sweet of heart.[^01-130]

**130.** I praise Sri Purushottama Tirtha, Jagannatha and the one called Rama, Raghunatha Tirtha, the blessed Upendra Ashrama and Hariharananda;[^01-131]

**131.** I praise Vasudeva Tirtha, then Srila Ananta Puri; and Mukunda Kaviraja, and then Rajiva Pandita.[^01-132]

**132.** I praise Sri Jiva Pandita, rich in all virtues, whose devotion to the feet of Sri Chaitanya-chandra is spotless.[^01-133]

**133.** I praise the one called Shishu Krishnadasa, nurtured by Sri Nityananda, whose body is full of happiness, blessed and pure.[^01-134]

**134.** I praise Uddharana Datta, who together with Nityananda wandered through all the holy places — pure of soul, seeking nothing for himself.[^01-135]

**135.** I praise Sri Madhava Acharya, who composed the *Krishna-mangala*,[^01-136] Nrisimha Chaitanya Dasa,[^01-137] and then Krishnadasa.

**136.** I praise Sri Shankara Ghosha, the auspicious, best of those who own nothing: by playing the dampha this divine [devotee] delighted the son of Shachi.[^01-138]

**137.** Again and again I praise the Vaishnavas and their feet; [I praise] Shivananda Chakravarti[^01-139] and the one called Sri Narayana.

## Conclusion

**138.** To praise each of them and likewise to utter their names — of those who shine with special qualities and are endowed with endless virtues,

**139.** of the great-souled Vaishnavas, whose truth I do not know — is equal to [uttering] the name of Tirthapada[^01-140] and is the supreme cause of purification.

**140.** Madhavendra has many disciples, spread over the earth: chief among them are Advaita, the bestowers of good, and [those headed by] Sankarshana Puri;

**141.** then those headed by Ishvara Puri, some headed by Govinda, those headed by Sri Paramananda Puri — the purifiers of the world.[^01-141]

**142.** The disciples of Ishvara Puri are Gaurachandra and Jahnavi; the disciple of Sankarshana Puri is Nityananda Prabhu Himself.[^01-142]

**143.** All who descended [into the world] as former devotees of Chaitanya-chandra received the mercy of Madhavendra through one door or another.

**144.** The one called Madhavendra Puri is the first devotee and guru; his host are the servants of Krishna Chaitanya, the givers of bhakti.[^01-143]

**145.** Some [came] through Advaita, others through Sita, through the son of Padmavati, likewise through Jahnavi;

**146.** some through Gadadhara, likewise through Sri Rupa; some through Sanatana, some through Haridasa;

**147.** some from Raghunatha Dasa, some through Vakreshvara, some through Kashishvara, and likewise through Narahari;

**148.** some here through Ramananda, others through Sarvabhauma; and likewise other devotees here are servants of others.

**149.** Therefore they took refuge in Sri Krishna Chaitanya Himself, worshipped by all, the guru of the world, who in person comprises all these forms.

**150.** The devotees whom Krishna brought down here — His eternal associates — those who are praised and named [here] for special reasons —

**151.** servants, energies, and also parts and personal forms: the distinction among them is to be learnt from the *Sri Bhagavatamrita*.[^01-144]

**152.** And those who, seeing the distribution of prema, came here longing for it — they too are worthy of reverence: they are purified by the touch of devotion to the Supreme.

**153.** This *Vaishnava-vandana*, which gives happiness and brings the fulfilment of all aims — a reckoning of the glorious sampradaya of Madhva, bestowing devotion to Sri Krishna, full of the qualities of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, [arranged] according to the order of His devotees — I, Jiva, have completed and, having made it, laid at [their] feet.[^01-145]

*Thus ends the* Vaishnava-vandana *of the devotees of Chaitanya, following the sampradaya of Madhva, composed by Sri Jiva Gosvami. Obeisance to Sri Chaitanya-chandra! Obeisance to Sri Nityananda-chandra! Obeisance to Sri Advaita-chandra!*

[^01-144]: The *Laghu-bhagavatamrita* of Rupa Gosvami, which describes the kinds of manifestation of the Lord and His associates.
[^01-145]: The author's last signature (see note to v. 1). The words "according to the order of His devotees" are an uncertain reading. The colophon calls the vandana "following the sampradaya of Madhva", as does GGD 22.

# Part 2. Devakinandana Dasa, *Vaishnava-vandana*

*A Bengali vandana (payar) with ragas and refrains: introduction; Gaura, Nityananda, Advaita and their families; the associates at Navadvipa; the sannyasis; the Gosvamis of Vrindavana; associates in Gauda and Orissa; the author's guru Purushottama; Nityananda's associates; conclusion (pp. 21–36 of the 1910 edition; couplets 1–147)*

*[p. 21]*

*Raga abhira.*

*Refrain:* Gorachand is my life, Gorachand is my wealth: spreading the net of prema, Gora has caught the whole world.

**1.** Humbly, holding a straw between my teeth,
I make my plea at the feet of the guru and the Vaishnavas.

**2.** How many Vaishnavas [came] with the descent of Sri Krishna Chaitanya and Nityananda —
who can tell them all?

**3.** Even the gods cannot comprehend the power of the Vaishnavas —
and who am I? A child of little understanding.

**4.** Great is the longing of my tongue and the desire of my mind —
that is why I wish to compose a Vaishnava-vandana.

**5.** Whatever I say, I say by the grace of the guru and the Vaishnavas;
do not count it an offence of mine if I break the order.[^02-1]

**6.** I praise Shachi and Jagannatha Mishra Purandara,
whose sons are Vishvarupa and Vishvambhara.

**7.** I shall praise Vishvarupa — blessed is he, blessed! —
Chaitanya's elder brother, named Sri Shankararanya.

**8.** I shall praise Mahaprabhu Sri Krishna Chaitanya,
who descended to purify the fallen — blessed is He, blessed!

**9.** I praise Lakshmi Thakurani and Vishnupriya,
and I extol Gadadhara Pandita Gosani.

**10.** I praise Padmavati-devi and Hadai Pandita,
whose son is Nityananda, wondrous in His deeds.

*[p. 22]*

**11.** I praise the merciful Thakura Sri Nityananda,
from whom come dancing, singing and joy for all.

**12.** I praise the two Thakuranis — Vasudha and Jahnava,
whose son is Virabhadra, extolled in the world.[^02-2]

**13.** With attention I shall praise Sri Virabhadra Gosani,
by whose conduct the whole world is won.

*Raga bhatiyali.*

*Refrain:* Gora Gosani is the descent that purifies the fallen. Blessed is Gora's descent, the crown-jewel of sannyasis: so beautiful a name is heard nowhere else.

**14.** With attention I praise first of all Madhavendra Puri —
the first descent on the path of devotion to Vishnu.[^02-3]

**15.** I praise Acharya Gosani — Advaita, the Lord,
who brought Mahaprabhu into the world.

**16.** With all my heart I praise Sita Thakurani,
I praise her sons — Achyutananda and the others.[^02-4]

**16a.** (Pundarika Vidyanidhi, the crest-jewel of devotees:
uttering his name, the Lord Himself wept.[^02-5])

**17.** I shall praise Sri Srinivasa Thakura Pandita,
renowned throughout the world as Narada.[^02-6]

**18.** With devotion I shall praise Malini Thakurani,
whom Gauranga with His own mouth called mother.

**19.** With attention I shall praise Sri Narayani-devi,
whom the Lord Himself called "alabati".[^02-7]

*[p. 23]*

**20.** I praise Haridasa Thakura, foremost among renunciants:
he gave children treats to make them say the name of Hari.[^02-8]

**21.** I praise Gopinatha Thakura, renowned in the world,
counted as Brahma for his praise of the Lord.[^02-9]

**22.** I shall praise Murari Gupta, mighty in devotion,
who in a former descent was called Hanuman.

**23.** I praise Sri Chandrashekhara, cool as the moon;
I praise Acharyaratna, whose fame is spotless.[^02-10]

**24.** I praise Govinda and Garuda, whose greatness is boundless,
whose right is devotion to Gaura's feet.[^02-11]

**25.** I shall praise the ambashtha[^02-12] named Sri Mukunda Datta,
whose singing surpassed the gandharvas.

**26.** With deep and pure feeling I praise Vasudeva Datta,
whom the Lord kept near Him in Utkala.[^02-13]

**27.** I praise Damodara Pandita, most dispassionate,
I praise Pitambara, his elder brother.

**28.** I praise Sri Jagannatha, Shankara and Narayana:
great renunciants are these five brothers.[^02-14]

**29.** I praise the great soul Nilambara Chakravarti,
who promptly foretold the Lord's future.[^02-15]

**30.** I praise Sri Rama Pandita and Gupta Narayana;
I praise the teachers — Vishnu, Gangadasa and Sudarshana.[^02-16]

**31.** I praise Sadashiva, and Shrigarbha, and Shrinidhi,
I praise Buddhimanta Khan and Vidyanidhi.

*[p. 24]*

**32.** I shall praise the brahmachari Shuklambara,
to whom the Lord granted the boon of His own prema-bhakti.[^02-17]

**33.** I praise Nandana Acharya and Vijaya the scribe,
I praise the great soul Ramadasa Kavichandra.[^02-18]

**34.** I praise Shridhara Pandita, called Kholaveca,
with whom the Lord was ever in playful quarrel.[^02-19]

**35.** I praise the beggar Vanamali together with his son:
he unexpectedly beheld the Lord's manifestation.[^02-20]

**36.** I praise, with respect, Halayudha Thakura,
I shall extol Sri Vasudeva Bhadra.[^02-21]

**37.** I shall praise Ishana Dasa with joined palms —
Shachi Thakurani loved him dearly.[^02-22]

**38.** I praise Jagadisha and Shriman, Sanjaya,
with humility I praise Garuda and Kashishvara.[^02-23]

**39.** I shall extol Gangadasa and Krishnananda,
with joy I praise Sri Rama and Mukunda.[^02-24]

**40.** I praise Vallabha Acharya, known to all the world,
whose daughter is Sri Lakshmi Thakurani Herself.

**41.** With joy I praise Sanatana Mishra,
whose blessed daughter is Vishnupriya Thakurani.

**42.** I praise Acharya Vanamali and the brahmana Kashinatha,
by whose efforts Mahaprabhu's marriage was arranged.[^02-25]

**42a.** (All who were at the Lord's wedding festival —
to the lotus feet of them all I bow unceasingly.[^02-26])

*[p. 25]*

*Raga suhai.*

*Refrain:* A beautiful descent is the descent of Sri Gauranga: such an ocean of mercy there is nowhere else.

**43.** With attention I praise Gosani Ishvara Puri,
from whom the Lord, teaching the world, took initiation.

**44.** I praise Keshava Bharati — the sage Sandipani:
the Lord Himself made him His guru.[^02-27]

**45.** I shall praise the feet of Sri Ramachandra Puri:
the Lord said of him that he belonged to Sri Rama's host.[^02-28]

**46.** I praise Paramananda Puri — in nature Uddhava,
I praise Damodara Puri — in the mood of Satyabhama.[^02-29]

**47.** I praise Narasimha Tirtha and Sukhananda Puri,
I praise Sri Govinda Puri and Brahmananda Puri.

**48.** I praise Nrisimha Puri and Satyananda Bharati,
I shall praise Garuda Avadhuta, great of mind.

**49.** With care I praise Vishnu Puri Gosani,
compiler of the *Vishnu-bhakti-ratnavali*.[^02-30]

**50.** With great devotion I praise Brahmananda Svarupa,
I praise Krishnananda Puri and Sri Raghava Puri.

**51.** I praise Vishveshvarananda, known to all,
whose faith in Mahaprabhu's feet is especially firm.

**52.** I praise Sri Keshava Puri and Anubhavananda […].[^02-31]

*[p. 26]*

**53.** I praise the two great souls — Rupa and Sanatana,
who established [the holy places of] the land of Vrindavana.[^02-32]

**54.** I praise Sri Jiva Gosani, accepted by all,
who, establishing the siddhanta, preserved the foundations of bhakti.[^02-33]

**55.** I praise Raghunatha Dasa, who dwells at Radha-kunda,
I praise Raghava Gosani, who sports at Govardhana.[^02-34]

**56.** I shall praise Gopala Bhatta in Vrindavana:
he abides ever together with Sanatana and Rupa.

**57.** With a concentrated mind I shall praise Raghunatha Bhatta Gosani,
the teacher of the *Sri Bhagavata* in Vrindavana.

**58.** I praise Lokanatha Thakura and Bhugarbha Thakura,
abundant in mercy to deliver souls.[^02-35]

**59.** With one heart I praise Kashishvara Gosani,
especially renowned in the Mathura-mandala.[^02-36]

**60.** I praise Shuddha Sarasvati, purest in form,
whose devotion to the Lord's feet is spotless.

**61.** With care I praise Prabodhananda Gosani,
who described the qualities of Mahaprabhu.[^02-37]

**62.** I praise Jagadananda Pandita — Sarasvati herself,
for whom Mahaprabhu had the highest love.[^02-38]

**63.** I praise the great soul, Pandita Raghava,
whose glory is manifest in the village of Panihati.

**64.** I praise Purandara Pandita — in valour Angada:
a brahmana saw his tail in his house.[^02-39]

*[p. 27]*

**65.** I praise Kashi Mishra, in whose abode [lived] the Lord;
with attention I shall praise Vaninatha Pattanayaka.

**66.** I praise Sri Pradyumna Mishra and Raya Bhavananda,
I praise Kalanidhi, Sudhanidhi and Gopinatha.[^02-40]

**67.** I praise Raya Ramananda, of great spiritual standing,
whom the Lord esteemed as the rarest treasure of knowledge.[^02-41]

**68.** I praise Vakreshvara Pandita with his divine body:
within — the splendour of Krishna, without — golden Gauranga.[^02-42]

**69.** I shall praise Sugriva Mishra — Sri Govinda:
for the Lord's sake he [built] a bridge in his mind.[^02-43]

**70.** With reverence I shall praise Gadadhara Dasa too,
whose greatness is especially manifest in Vrindavana.[^02-44]

**71.** With one heart I praise Sadashiva Kaviraja:
ceaselessly mad with prema, he is unaware of the outer world.

**72.** I praise Shivananda Sena, whose body is prema,
whose caste, life and wealth are Gora's two feet.

**73.** Chaitanya Dasa, Ramadasa and Karnapura —
the three sons of Shivananda I shall praise abundantly.[^02-45]

**74.** I shall praise Mukunda Dasa, pure of heart through bhava:
seeing a peacock feather, he lost consciousness.[^02-46]

**75.** I praise Narahari Dasa — the abode of prema,
in whose heart Gauranga sports unceasingly.

**76.** I praise Sri Raghunandana, saintly in character,
whose form and nature enchant the world.

*[p. 28]*

**77.** I praise Raghunatha Dasa, full of the nectar of prema,
by whose character all people are won.[^02-47]

**78.** I praise Acharya Purandara and Pandita Devananda,
I praise Sri Acharyachandra, full of prema for Gaura.[^02-48]

**79.** I praise Krishnadasa Thakura of Akaihat,[^02-49]
I praise Paramananda Puri — the Lord's fellow student.[^02-50]

**80.** With attention I shall praise Sri Govinda Ghosha,
whose name the Lord Himself made true.

**81.** I shall praise Madhava Ghosha — the object of the Lord's love,
to whom the Lord granted a voice that never breaks.[^02-51]

**82.** With attention I shall praise Sri Vasudeva Ghosha,
whose songs about Gaura have no end.[^02-52]

**83.** With respect I shall praise Thakura Sri Abhirama:
a log for sixteen bearers he holds as a flute.[^02-53]

**84.** With great hope I shall praise Sundarananda Thakura:
he made kadamba flowers bloom on a lemon tree.[^02-54]

**85.** With attention I shall praise Parameshvara Dasa Thakura:
at the place of sankirtana he made the jackals chant the name.[^02-55]

**86.** With respect I shall praise Vamshivadana Thakura:
Gadadhara Dasa made [him] a descent of the flute.[^02-56]

**87.** I praise my chosen master named Sri Purushottama[^02-57] —
who can describe his incomparable qualities?

**88.** Even one who has no good qualities he shows mercy to
by the power of his natural compassion.

*[p. 29]*

**89.** In his seventh year he had the madness of love for Krishna;
his dancing enchants the world, his power is unfathomable.

**90.** Seizing Gauridasa the kirtaniya by the hair,
he imparted his own power and made him sing the praise of Nityananda.[^02-58]

**91.** Gadadhara Dasa and Sri Govinda Ghosha [are witnesses]:
at his manifestation the Lord found joy.[^02-59]

**92.** Abhisheka with a hundred and eight pitchers of Ganga water;
omniscience from his very childhood.[^02-60]

**93.** The karavi flower that was in his ear
before the whole assembly came to smell like a lotus.[^02-61]

**94.** Through his name all Vaishnavas attain perfection;
his body is the happiness of prema embodied.

**95.** With great devotion I praise Kaliya Krishnadasa,
in divine sacred thread and garments, bearing the splendour of Krishna.[^02-62]

**96.** I praise Kamalakara Pippalai, sporting in bhava,
who said to the Lord: "Take the stick, give me the flute."[^02-63]

**97.** I praise Ratnakara's son named Purushottama,
who lives in Nadia, an abode of divine splendour.[^02-64]

**98.** With attention I praise Uddharana Datta:
together with Nityananda he went round all the holy places.[^02-65]

**99.** I praise Gauridasa Pandita, who carried out the Lord's will:
he took Acharya Gosani to Utkala.[^02-66]

**100.** I praise Purushottama Pandita, sporting [in bhava], wise:
the Lord gave him the place of Acharya Gosani.[^02-67]

*[p. 30]*

**101.** With one heart I shall praise Saranga Dasa,
I praise Makaradhvaja Kara, the Lord's singer.[^02-68]

*Raga barari.*

*Refrain:* Gora Gosani is the descent that purifies the fallen: by Your mercy all souls are delivered.

**102.** I praise Kaviraja Mishra, Bhagavatacharya,
I praise Sri Madhu Pandita and Ananta Acharya.[^02-69]

**103.** I praise Govinda Acharya, endowed with every virtue,
who composed the wondrous dhamali of Radha and Krishna.[^02-70]

**104.** I praise Sarvabhauma — in character Brihaspati:
at the Lord's manifestation a wondrous poetic gift opened in him.[^02-71]

**105.** I praise Prataparudra Raya, in fame equal to Indra:
the Lord revealed to him the six-armed form.[^02-72]

**106.** I praise the brahmana Yadunatha and Vipradasa the Oriya,
I praise the brahmana Haridasa and the vaidya Vishnudasa,

**107.** at whose songs the Lord rejoiced especially;
I praise his brother — Sri Vanamali Dasa.[^02-73]

**108.** Disguising himself, he entered into the play of the gopis;
there is no describing how his prema showed itself.

**109.** I praise Kanai Khutiya, known to all the world,
whose two sons are Jagannatha and Balarama.[^02-74]

**110.** I praise the great soul, Balarama Dasa the Oriya,
to whom Jagannatha and Balarama are subject.

*[p. 31]*

**111.** I praise Jagannatha Dasa, master of music,
by the rasa of whose singing Jagannatha is enchanted.[^02-75]

**112.** I shall praise Shivananda Pandita and Kashishvara,
I shall praise Chandaneshvara and Simheshvara.

**113.** I shall praise Subuddhi Mishra and Sri Shrinatha Mishra,
I praise Tulasi Mishra and Kashinatha Mahiti.

**114.** I praise Sri Hari Bhatta and Balarama Mahiti,
I praise the Pattanayaka named Madhava, the steadfast.[^02-76]

**115.** With care I shall praise Ramananda of the Vasu line:
in his family they know none but Gaura.[^02-77]

**116.** I shall praise the brahmachari named Sri Purushottama,
with great devotion I praise Sri Madhu Pandita.

**117.** I praise Shrikara Pandita and the brahmana Ramachandra,
I praise Yadu Kavichandra, full of every happiness.[^02-78]

**118.** I praise the renunciant Pandita Dhananjaya, sporting [in bhava]:
giving everything to the Lord, he took a pot in his hand.[^02-79]

**119.** I praise Jagannatha Pandita and Acharya Lakshmana,
I praise Krishnadasa Pandita, purest of heart.

**120.** I praise Suryadasa Pandita, known to the world:
his two daughters are Vasudha and Jahnava.

**121.** With attention I praise Murari Chaitanya Dasa,
whose wondrous deeds are like those of Prahlada.[^02-80]

**122.** I praise Paramananda Gupta, Jagannatha Sena,
Kavichandra, Mukunda, and with them the boy Rama.[^02-81]

*[p. 32]*

**123.** I praise Sri Kamsari Sena, Sri Vallabha Sena,[^02-82]
I praise Bhaskara Thakura, [in whom is] the greatness of Vishvakarma.

**124.** I praise the maker of songs Sri Balarama Dasa,
whose faith in Nityananda-chandra is beyond words.

**125.** I praise Mahesha Pandita, a great madman [of love],
I praise Jagadisha Pandita, the lover of dance.

**126.** I praise Vrindavana Dasa, the son of Narayani,
who brought to light the *Chaitanya-mangala*.[^02-83]

**127.** I shall praise Thakura Krishnadasa of Badagachhi,
whose faith is in Nityananda, in the bliss of prema.[^02-84]

**128.** With one heart I praise Paramananda Avadhuta:
ceaselessly mad, he is unaware of the outer world.

**129.** I shall praise Anadi Gangadasa Pandita,
I praise Yadunatha Dasa, sweet in character.

**130.** I praise Purushottama Puri and Jagannatha Tirtha,
I praise Sri Rama Tirtha and Raghunatha Puri.[^02-85]

**131.** I praise Vasudeva Tirtha and Upendra Ashrama,
I shall praise Ananta Puri and Hariharananda.

**132.** I praise Mukunda Kaviraja, spotless in character,
I shall praise the blissful Sri Jiva Pandita.

**133.** I shall extol the one called Shishu Krishnadasa:
nurtured by the Lord, he is an abode of divine splendour.[^02-86]

**134.** I praise Madhava Acharya, cool with poetry,
whose composed song is the *Sri Krishna-mangala*.

*[p. 35]*

**135.** Krishnadasa, the younger brother of Gauridasa Pandita,
I shall praise, and Nrisimha, and Sri Chaitanya Dasa.[^02-87]

**136.** (With faith I praise Raghunatha Bhatta,
I praise Sri Ramachandra Dasa of the divine eyes.[^02-88])

**137.** I praise Sri Shankara Ghosha, of a poor man's ways,
who by playing the dampha won the Lord's love.[^02-89]

**138.** In supreme joy I praise Acharya Madhava:
as the fruit of devotion he became the husband of Ganga-devi.[^02-90]

**139.** I praise Narayana Paidari and Shivananda Chakravarti[^02-91] —
there is no end to praising the Vaishnavas!

**140.** How many Vaishnavas there are in this descent — beyond counting:
their endless glory cannot be told.

**141.** Endless are the Vaishnavas, endless their glory:
there is no one who could measure it.

**142.** Have I wit enough to praise them?
Even the gods cannot express the purity of the Vaishnavas.

**143.** The teacher of all is the Vaishnava Thakura;
he is far beyond hearing, sight, mind and speech.[^02-92]

**144.** I take refuge at the feet of the guru and the Vaishnavas:
briefly I have said something in this Vaishnava-vandana.

**145.** Whoever reads and hears the Vaishnava-vandana,
the filth in his heart is removed and his mind becomes pure.

**146.** Whoever, rising at dawn, reads the Vaishnava-vandana
will never know any torment.

*[p. 36]*

**147.** He will gain that prema-bhakti which even the gods find hard to attain —
with this longing speaks Devakinandana Dasa.

*Thus ends the* Vaishnava-vandana *composed by Sri Devakinandana Dasa.*

# Part 3. Devakinandana Kaviraja, *Sri Vaishnavabhidhana*

*A Sanskrit list of the names of Vaishnavas — "The Names of the Vaishnavas": a brief recapitulation of the same author's Bengali vandana (pp. 56–60 of the 1910 edition; verses 1–53)*

*[p. 56]*

**1.** Having first bowed to my guru Sri Purushottama,[^03-1] whose merciful glance has purified the earth — a wish-fulfilling tree that grants every desire —

**2.** and to all the Vaishnavas — full of great power, of great fortune, great purifiers of the fallen, mahabhagavatas, forms of Vishnu —

**3.** [I bow] then to the renowned Shachi and Jagannatha, brahmanas in form, the parents of Sri Vishvarupa and Sri Vishvambhara;

**4.** to the blessed Vishvarupa, the great soul, bearing the name Shankararanya, in the form of the elder brother of Sri Krishna Chaitanya-chandra;

**5.** to Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu — the Lord of Gadadhara's life, the husband of Lakshmi and Vishnupriya, prema and mercy embodied in person;

**6.** and likewise to Padmavati and Srimat Mukunda,[^03-2] best of brahmanas — the parents, of incomparable good fortune, of Nityananda Svarupa;

**7.** to Srimat Nityananda-chandra — the husband of Vasudha and Jahnavi, the father of Sri Virabhadra, the crusher of all the godless.

**8.** Though I am by nature insignificant, unintelligent and a child myself, a fool in describing the endless glory of the endless Vaishnavas,

**9.** yet from the greed of my tongue and the great agitation of my heart I make a certain remembrance — the endless names of the Vaishnavas.

**10.** And this too I, the insignificant one, say to all: [you] in whom virtues arise, do not count against me faults against the order.

**11.** Sri Madhava Puri,[^03-3] Srila Advaita Acharya, and likewise Achyuta, Gopinatha, Srinivasa, Govinda, Chandrashekhara,

*[p. 57]*

**12.** Haridasa, Sri Murari Gupta, and likewise Narayana, Mukunda, Vasudeva, Sri Damodara Pandita,

**13.** Pitambara, Jagannatha, Sri Narayana and Shankara, Sri Rama Pandita, and likewise Nilambara Chakravarti,

**14.** Gangadasa, the brahmana Vishnu, Sri Sudarshana Pandita, and likewise Vidyanidhi, Buddhimanta, Srila Sadashiva,

**15.** Shrigarbha, Shrinidhi, Shuklambara, Shridhara Pandita, Kavichandra, Ramadasa, Vanamali, Halayudha,

**16.** Vijaya, Nandana Acharya, Ishana, Garudadhvaja, Jagadisha, Sanjaya, Shriman, and likewise Kashishvara,

**17.** Gangadasa, Vasudeva Bhadra, Rama and Mukunda, Sri Vallabha, best of acharyas, Mishra Srila Sanatana,

**18.** Acharya Vanamali and Kashinatha, best of brahmanas, the Puri bearing the name Ishvara, Srimat Keshava Bharati,

**19.** the Puri named Paramananda, Damodara Svarupa,[^03-4] the Tirtha named Nrisimha, and likewise Ramachandra Puri,

**20.** and Brahmananda Puri, Sri Satyananda Bharati, Srimat Sukhananda Puri, and likewise Sri Govinda Puri,

**21.** the divine Garuda Avadhuta, the Puri named Raghava, Brahmananda Svarupa, the Puri Sriyuta Keshava,

**22.** Srimat Vishnu Puri, the great soul Vishveshvarananda, the one whose name is Sri Sacchidananda, and likewise Anubhavananda,

**23.** Srimat Krishnananda Puri, Nrisimhananda Bharati, the divine one named Kashishvara, Hanuman,[^03-5] Sri Sanatana,

*[p. 58]*

**24.** Rupa, Jiva, Sri Prabodhananda, Shuddha Sarasvati, the one bearing the name Raghunatha Dasa, and likewise Gopala Bhatta,

**25.** Raghunatha, Lokanatha, Srimat the one named Bhugarbha, Raghava, Jagadananda Pandita, Sri Purandara,

**26.** Kashi Mishra, Raya Ramananda, the brahmana Vakreshvara, Vaninatha Pattanayaka, and likewise Govindananda,

**27.** Sadashiva Kaviraja, Gadadhara of the Dasa family, Sri Shivananda Sena and Sri Mukunda, best of physicians,

**28.** Srimat Narahari, and likewise Srila Raghunandana, Raghunatha Dasa the vaidya, Upadhyaya Madhusudana,

**29.** Devananda, best of brahmanas, Srila Acharya Purandara, Sriyuta Acharyachandra, Sri Krishnadasa Pandita,

**30.** the [Lord's] fellow student Paramananda, and likewise Srimat Srishtidhara,[^03-6] Govinda, Madhava and Vasudeva, bearing the name Ghosha,

**31.** Srila Sri Ramadasa and Sri Sundarananda, Sri Parameshvara and my supreme one — Srila Sri Purushottama,[^03-7]

**32.** Sri Krishnadasa, Sri Gauridasa, Sri Kamalakara, Sri Vamshivadana Dasa, who revealed the song of the flute,

**33.** Srimat Uddharana and Srila the brahmana Sri Purushottama, Kaviraja Mishra, the best [of all], Madhusudana Pandita,

**34.** Srimat Bhagavatacharya, and likewise Govinda Acharya, Sri Sarvabhauma, and likewise Sriyuta Nandana Acharya,

**35.** Srimat Prataparudra, Yadunatha […],[^03-8] the brahmana Haridasa, Srila Saranga, Makaradhvaja,

*[p. 59]*

**36.** Sri Vrindavana Dasa, the Pandita named Jagadisha, Pradyumna Mishra, Tapana Acharya, and likewise Sri Bhagavan,[^03-9]

**37.** Sri Vipradasa the Oriya, the vaidya Sri Vishnudasa, the vaidya Vanamali Dasa, Haridasa, Gadadhara,

**38.** Sri Krishnadasa the Oriya, Sri Kashishvara Pandita, Balarama Dasa and Jagannatha Dasa, Sri Chandaneshvara,

**39.** Simheshvara, Shivananda, Balarama Mahiti,[^03-10] Subuddhi Mishra, Tulasi Mishra, the one called Shrinatha,

**40.** Kashinatha, Hari Bhatta, Madhava Pattanayaka, Ramananda Vasu, the brahmachari Sri Purushottama,

**41.** the brahmana Sri Ramachandra, Srimat Shrikara Pandita, Yadunatha Kavichandra, Pandita Sri Dhananjaya,

**42.** Acharya Sri Jagannatha, Sri Suryadasa Pandita, Srila Sri Lakshmana Acharya, and likewise Sri Krishna Acharya,

**43.** Chaitanya Dasa, the physician Paramananda Gupta, Sri Jagannatha and Kamsari — the Senas, Sriyuta Bhaskara,

**44.** Kavichandra, Sri Mukunda, Sri Rama, Vallabha Sena, Sriyuta the one named Balarama Dasa, Mahesha Pandita,

**45.** Paramananda Avadhuta, Sri Gangadasa Pandita, Kaviraja Sri Mukunda,[^03-11] Sri Jiva Pandita,

**46.** Chiranjiva, Krishnadasa, the boy named Krishnadasa, the excellent Yadunatha Dasa, Sri Krishnadasa Pandita,

**47.** Rama Tirtha, Krishna Tirtha, the Puri Sri Purushottama, Srimat Jagannatha Tirtha, and likewise Raghunatha Puri,

*[p. 60]*

**48.** Sri Vasudeva Tirtha and Srila the one named Upendra Ashrama, the Puri named Ananta, Hariharananda Bharati,

**49.** Srimat Nrisimha Chaitanya, Srimat Acharya Madhava, Shankara, Madhavananda Acharya, Dasa Sanatana, Shivananda Chakravarti, the brahmana Narayana and others.

**50.** Whoever remembers them at dawn or hears [their names] with devotion will at no time come to torment — this is certain.

**51.** The person who, remembering them again and again, bows down will never commit an offence at the feet of the Vaishnavas.

**52.** By merely remembering them he attains the position of a Vaishnava and bhakti, sweet with the nectar of prema, hard to attain even for the gods.

**53.** The Vaishnava is above all; he surpasses all the Vedas; and he is far beyond hearing, sight and mind.

*Thus ends the* Vaishnavabhidhana *composed by Sri Devakinandana Kaviraja.*

**Obeisance to the Vaishnavas** — wish-fulfilling trees, oceans of mercy, purifiers of the fallen — obeisance, obeisance!

To those devoted to the name of Hari and wholly given to devotion to Hari — whether their conduct be bad or good — to them obeisance, obeisance for ever![^03-12]

*Offered to Sri Krishna.*
