# Part 2. Devakinandana Dasa, *Vaishnava-vandana*

*A Bengali vandana (payar) with ragas and refrains: introduction; Gaura, Nityananda, Advaita and their families; the associates at Navadvipa; the sannyasis; the Gosvamis of Vrindavana; associates in Gauda and Orissa; the author's guru Purushottama; Nityananda's associates; conclusion (pp. 21–36 of the 1910 edition; couplets 1–147)*

*[p. 21]*

*Raga abhira.*

*Refrain:* Gorachand is my life, Gorachand is my wealth: spreading the net of prema, Gora has caught the whole world.

**1.** Humbly, holding a straw between my teeth,
I make my plea at the feet of the guru and the Vaishnavas.

**2.** How many Vaishnavas [came] with the descent of Sri Krishna Chaitanya and Nityananda —
who can tell them all?

**3.** Even the gods cannot comprehend the power of the Vaishnavas —
and who am I? A child of little understanding.

**4.** Great is the longing of my tongue and the desire of my mind —
that is why I wish to compose a Vaishnava-vandana.

**5.** Whatever I say, I say by the grace of the guru and the Vaishnavas;
do not count it an offence of mine if I break the order.[^1]

**6.** I praise Shachi and Jagannatha Mishra Purandara,
whose sons are Vishvarupa and Vishvambhara.

**7.** I shall praise Vishvarupa — blessed is he, blessed! —
Chaitanya's elder brother, named Sri Shankararanya.

**8.** I shall praise Mahaprabhu Sri Krishna Chaitanya,
who descended to purify the fallen — blessed is He, blessed!

**9.** I praise Lakshmi Thakurani and Vishnupriya,
and I extol Gadadhara Pandita Gosani.

**10.** I praise Padmavati-devi and Hadai Pandita,
whose son is Nityananda, wondrous in His deeds.

*[p. 22]*

**11.** I praise the merciful Thakura Sri Nityananda,
from whom come dancing, singing and joy for all.

**12.** I praise the two Thakuranis — Vasudha and Jahnava,
whose son is Virabhadra, extolled in the world.[^2]

**13.** With attention I shall praise Sri Virabhadra Gosani,
by whose conduct the whole world is won.

*Raga bhatiyali.*

*Refrain:* Gora Gosani is the descent that purifies the fallen. Blessed is Gora's descent, the crown-jewel of sannyasis: so beautiful a name is heard nowhere else.

**14.** With attention I praise first of all Madhavendra Puri —
the first descent on the path of devotion to Vishnu.[^3]

**15.** I praise Acharya Gosani — Advaita, the Lord,
who brought Mahaprabhu into the world.

**16.** With all my heart I praise Sita Thakurani,
I praise her sons — Achyutananda and the others.[^4]

**16a.** (Pundarika Vidyanidhi, the crest-jewel of devotees:
uttering his name, the Lord Himself wept.[^5])

**17.** I shall praise Sri Srinivasa Thakura Pandita,
renowned throughout the world as Narada.[^6]

**18.** With devotion I shall praise Malini Thakurani,
whom Gauranga with His own mouth called mother.

**19.** With attention I shall praise Sri Narayani-devi,
whom the Lord Himself called "alabati".[^7]

*[p. 23]*

**20.** I praise Haridasa Thakura, foremost among renunciants:
he gave children treats to make them say the name of Hari.[^8]

**21.** I praise Gopinatha Thakura, renowned in the world,
counted as Brahma for his praise of the Lord.[^9]

**22.** I shall praise Murari Gupta, mighty in devotion,
who in a former descent was called Hanuman.

**23.** I praise Sri Chandrashekhara, cool as the moon;
I praise Acharyaratna, whose fame is spotless.[^10]

**24.** I praise Govinda and Garuda, whose greatness is boundless,
whose right is devotion to Gaura's feet.[^11]

**25.** I shall praise the ambashtha[^12] named Sri Mukunda Datta,
whose singing surpassed the gandharvas.

**26.** With deep and pure feeling I praise Vasudeva Datta,
whom the Lord kept near Him in Utkala.[^13]

**27.** I praise Damodara Pandita, most dispassionate,
I praise Pitambara, his elder brother.

**28.** I praise Sri Jagannatha, Shankara and Narayana:
great renunciants are these five brothers.[^14]

**29.** I praise the great soul Nilambara Chakravarti,
who promptly foretold the Lord's future.[^15]

**30.** I praise Sri Rama Pandita and Gupta Narayana;
I praise the teachers — Vishnu, Gangadasa and Sudarshana.[^16]

**31.** I praise Sadashiva, and Shrigarbha, and Shrinidhi,
I praise Buddhimanta Khan and Vidyanidhi.

*[p. 24]*

**32.** I shall praise the brahmachari Shuklambara,
to whom the Lord granted the boon of His own prema-bhakti.[^17]

**33.** I praise Nandana Acharya and Vijaya the scribe,
I praise the great soul Ramadasa Kavichandra.[^18]

**34.** I praise Shridhara Pandita, called Kholaveca,
with whom the Lord was ever in playful quarrel.[^19]

**35.** I praise the beggar Vanamali together with his son:
he unexpectedly beheld the Lord's manifestation.[^20]

**36.** I praise, with respect, Halayudha Thakura,
I shall extol Sri Vasudeva Bhadra.[^21]

**37.** I shall praise Ishana Dasa with joined palms —
Shachi Thakurani loved him dearly.[^22]

**38.** I praise Jagadisha and Shriman, Sanjaya,
with humility I praise Garuda and Kashishvara.[^23]

**39.** I shall extol Gangadasa and Krishnananda,
with joy I praise Sri Rama and Mukunda.[^24]

**40.** I praise Vallabha Acharya, known to all the world,
whose daughter is Sri Lakshmi Thakurani Herself.

**41.** With joy I praise Sanatana Mishra,
whose blessed daughter is Vishnupriya Thakurani.

**42.** I praise Acharya Vanamali and the brahmana Kashinatha,
by whose efforts Mahaprabhu's marriage was arranged.[^25]

**42a.** (All who were at the Lord's wedding festival —
to the lotus feet of them all I bow unceasingly.[^26])

*[p. 25]*

*Raga suhai.*

*Refrain:* A beautiful descent is the descent of Sri Gauranga: such an ocean of mercy there is nowhere else.

**43.** With attention I praise Gosani Ishvara Puri,
from whom the Lord, teaching the world, took initiation.

**44.** I praise Keshava Bharati — the sage Sandipani:
the Lord Himself made him His guru.[^27]

**45.** I shall praise the feet of Sri Ramachandra Puri:
the Lord said of him that he belonged to Sri Rama's host.[^28]

**46.** I praise Paramananda Puri — in nature Uddhava,
I praise Damodara Puri — in the mood of Satyabhama.[^29]

**47.** I praise Narasimha Tirtha and Sukhananda Puri,
I praise Sri Govinda Puri and Brahmananda Puri.

**48.** I praise Nrisimha Puri and Satyananda Bharati,
I shall praise Garuda Avadhuta, great of mind.

**49.** With care I praise Vishnu Puri Gosani,
compiler of the *Vishnu-bhakti-ratnavali*.[^30]

**50.** With great devotion I praise Brahmananda Svarupa,
I praise Krishnananda Puri and Sri Raghava Puri.

**51.** I praise Vishveshvarananda, known to all,
whose faith in Mahaprabhu's feet is especially firm.

**52.** I praise Sri Keshava Puri and Anubhavananda […].[^31]

*[p. 26]*

**53.** I praise the two great souls — Rupa and Sanatana,
who established [the holy places of] the land of Vrindavana.[^32]

**54.** I praise Sri Jiva Gosani, accepted by all,
who, establishing the siddhanta, preserved the foundations of bhakti.[^33]

**55.** I praise Raghunatha Dasa, who dwells at Radha-kunda,
I praise Raghava Gosani, who sports at Govardhana.[^34]

**56.** I shall praise Gopala Bhatta in Vrindavana:
he abides ever together with Sanatana and Rupa.

**57.** With a concentrated mind I shall praise Raghunatha Bhatta Gosani,
the teacher of the *Sri Bhagavata* in Vrindavana.

**58.** I praise Lokanatha Thakura and Bhugarbha Thakura,
abundant in mercy to deliver souls.[^35]

**59.** With one heart I praise Kashishvara Gosani,
especially renowned in the Mathura-mandala.[^36]

**60.** I praise Shuddha Sarasvati, purest in form,
whose devotion to the Lord's feet is spotless.

**61.** With care I praise Prabodhananda Gosani,
who described the qualities of Mahaprabhu.[^37]

**62.** I praise Jagadananda Pandita — Sarasvati herself,
for whom Mahaprabhu had the highest love.[^38]

**63.** I praise the great soul, Pandita Raghava,
whose glory is manifest in the village of Panihati.

**64.** I praise Purandara Pandita — in valour Angada:
a brahmana saw his tail in his house.[^39]

*[p. 27]*

**65.** I praise Kashi Mishra, in whose abode [lived] the Lord;
with attention I shall praise Vaninatha Pattanayaka.

**66.** I praise Sri Pradyumna Mishra and Raya Bhavananda,
I praise Kalanidhi, Sudhanidhi and Gopinatha.[^40]

**67.** I praise Raya Ramananda, of great spiritual standing,
whom the Lord esteemed as the rarest treasure of knowledge.[^41]

**68.** I praise Vakreshvara Pandita with his divine body:
within — the splendour of Krishna, without — golden Gauranga.[^42]

**69.** I shall praise Sugriva Mishra — Sri Govinda:
for the Lord's sake he [built] a bridge in his mind.[^43]

**70.** With reverence I shall praise Gadadhara Dasa too,
whose greatness is especially manifest in Vrindavana.[^44]

**71.** With one heart I praise Sadashiva Kaviraja:
ceaselessly mad with prema, he is unaware of the outer world.

**72.** I praise Shivananda Sena, whose body is prema,
whose caste, life and wealth are Gora's two feet.

**73.** Chaitanya Dasa, Ramadasa and Karnapura —
the three sons of Shivananda I shall praise abundantly.[^45]

**74.** I shall praise Mukunda Dasa, pure of heart through bhava:
seeing a peacock feather, he lost consciousness.[^46]

**75.** I praise Narahari Dasa — the abode of prema,
in whose heart Gauranga sports unceasingly.

**76.** I praise Sri Raghunandana, saintly in character,
whose form and nature enchant the world.

*[p. 28]*

**77.** I praise Raghunatha Dasa, full of the nectar of prema,
by whose character all people are won.[^47]

**78.** I praise Acharya Purandara and Pandita Devananda,
I praise Sri Acharyachandra, full of prema for Gaura.[^48]

**79.** I praise Krishnadasa Thakura of Akaihat,[^49]
I praise Paramananda Puri — the Lord's fellow student.[^50]

**80.** With attention I shall praise Sri Govinda Ghosha,
whose name the Lord Himself made true.

**81.** I shall praise Madhava Ghosha — the object of the Lord's love,
to whom the Lord granted a voice that never breaks.[^51]

**82.** With attention I shall praise Sri Vasudeva Ghosha,
whose songs about Gaura have no end.[^52]

**83.** With respect I shall praise Thakura Sri Abhirama:
a log for sixteen bearers he holds as a flute.[^53]

**84.** With great hope I shall praise Sundarananda Thakura:
he made kadamba flowers bloom on a lemon tree.[^54]

**85.** With attention I shall praise Parameshvara Dasa Thakura:
at the place of sankirtana he made the jackals chant the name.[^55]

**86.** With respect I shall praise Vamshivadana Thakura:
Gadadhara Dasa made [him] a descent of the flute.[^56]

### Translator's notes

[^1]: Cf. the apology for the order in Jiva's vandana (Part 1, v. 6) and in GGD 212.
[^2]: Here, unlike Jiva's vandana (Part 1, vv. 22–23), Virabhadra (Virachandra) is called the son of Vasudha and Jahnava, i.e. of Nityananda.
[^3]: Madhavendra Puri is "the first sprout of the tree of prema" (CC Adi 9); GGD 22–25.
[^4]: According to the manuscripts of 1654 and 1702 (Majumdar) this reads "I praise her son Sri Achyutananda": these manuscripts name no other son of Advaita; cf. Part 1, vv. 33–35.
[^5]: The editor puts this couplet in brackets (apparently it is not in all manuscripts). GGD 54–56: Pundarika Vidyanidhi is Vrishabhanu; the Lord called him "father" and wept remembering him (CB Madhya 7).
[^6]: GGD 90.
[^7]: The meaning of the word "alabati" (ālabāṭī) here is unclear; it may be connected with Narayani's receiving the remnants of the Lord's meal (cf. Part 1, v. 36).
[^8]: GGD 93–95 (Brahma and Prahlada).
[^9]: Apparently Gopinatha Acharya (GGD 75 — Brahma); see Part 1, note to v. 39.
[^10]: See Part 1, note to v. 40: Acharyaratna is according to the CC a title of Chandrashekhara, but the vandanas and GGD 103 name him separately.
[^11]: GGD 116–117.
[^12]: Ambashtha — the caste of vaidyas (physicians).
[^13]: On Vasudeva Datta in Puri — CC Madhya 11, 15. GGD 140 (the singer Madhuvrata).
[^14]: Cf. Part 1, v. 43 and note.
[^15]: GGD 104 (Garga).
[^16]: On "Gupta Narayana" see Part 1, note to v. 45. Nimai's teachers — Part 1, v. 46.
[^17]: CB Madhya: the Lord ate rice from the beggar's bag of the brahmachari Shuklambara; GGD 191.
[^18]: Cf. Part 1, vv. 48–49: in Jiva, in place of Nandana Acharya, there is Ratneshvara Acharya, and Ramadasa and Kavichandra are apparently two persons.
[^19]: CB Adi-khanda — Nimai's playful quarrels with Shridhara about the price of banana leaves and about his poverty; GGD 133.
[^20]: Cf. Part 1, v. 49 and note (GGD 114).
[^21]: GGD 134 (Halayudha). "Vasudeva Bhadra" — cf. *Vaishnavabhidhana* (Part 3, v. 17).
[^22]: Ishana, the servant in the Lord's household, who served Shachi and Vishnupriya.
[^23]: Cf. Part 1, vv. 51–52.
[^24]: Cf. Part 1, v. 52 and note.
[^25]: GGD 49–50; cf. Part 1, v. 55.
[^26]: The editor puts this couplet in brackets; Majumdar does not count it.
[^27]: GGD 52: Keshava Bharati is the sage Sandipani, Krishna's guru.
[^28]: Cf. GGD 92 and Part 1, note to v. 58.
[^29]: GGD 118 (Paramananda Puri — Uddhava); on Damodara Puri — Part 1, note to v. 59.
[^30]: Vishnu Puri's *Bhakti-ratnavali*; see Part 1, v. 61.
[^31]: The second line of the couplet is missing in the edition (a blank space in the scan). Cf. Part 1, v. 63: "Anubhavananda and Chidananda".
[^32]: Rupa and Sanatana uncovered the lost holy places of Vrindavana at the Lord's command (CC Madhya 23 and elsewhere).
[^33]: In Jiva's vandana Jiva himself is naturally not praised; here his name is present.
[^34]: Cf. Part 1, vv. 70–71 and notes.
[^35]: At this point Devakinandana's "great" vandana (according to Majumdar) adds Vaishnavas of other sampradayas (Vishnusvami, Hit Harivamsha, Surdas and others).
[^36]: Kashishvara Gosvami (GGD 166), who served the Deity of Govinda in Vrindavana.
[^37]: In the *Sri Chaitanya-chandramrita* (`../chaitanya-chandramrita/`); GGD 163.
[^38]: According to GGD 51 Jagadananda is a manifestation of Satyabhama; "Sarasvati" — so also in Jiva (Part 1, v. 38).
[^39]: GGD 91 (Angada); Part 1, v. 76.
[^40]: According to Majumdar this couplet is not in the old manuscripts: the names Kalanidhi and Sudhanidhi (brothers of Ramananda Raya) occur only in the CC (Madhya 10), and the couplet was apparently added from it. Pradyumna Mishra — CC Antya 5; Bhavananda Raya, Ramananda's father (GGD 122); Gopinatha is Gopinatha Pattanayaka, Ramananda's brother (CC Antya 9).
[^41]: CC Madhya 8; GGD 120–124.
[^42]: I.e. in Vakreshvara both Krishna and Gaura are manifest. GGD 71–73.
[^43]: See Part 1, vv. 81–82 and 111 and notes.
[^44]: Cf. Part 1, v. 83; GGD 154–155.
[^45]: Shivananda Sena and his sons Chaitanya Dasa, Ramadasa and Kavi Karnapura (Paramananda Dasa), the author of GGD (GGD 4, 145). According to Majumdar this couplet is not in the old manuscripts.
[^46]: CC Madhya 15; Part 1, vv. 87–88.
[^47]: Probably the same Raghunatha (the vaidya) as in Part 1, v. 91.
[^48]: Acharyachandra — cf. *Vaishnavabhidhana* (Part 3, v. 29). At this place Jiva has Acharyaratna (Part 1, v. 93; Majumdar notes this too).
[^49]: Krishnadasa of Akaihat, an associate of Nityananda (named in the lists of the branches of the tree of devotion, CC Adi).
[^50]: Cf. Part 1, v. 92 (Paramananda Pandita).
[^51]: The Ghosha brothers, kirtana singers (GGD 188). "A voice that never breaks" is the Lord's gift to Madhava Ghosha's singing.
[^52]: Vasudeva Ghosha's padas about Gaura — see `../padas/`.
[^53]: GGD 126: "a log that only thirty-two men could lift"; Jiva (Part 1, v. 95) — "many".
[^54]: CB Antya 5 describes this miracle in Raghava's house at Panihati as performed by Nityananda; so too Jiva (Part 1, v. 75).
[^55]: GGD 132; Part 1, v. 99.
[^56]: I.e., apparently, Gadadhara Dasa revealed that Vamshivadana is the incarnation of Krishna's flute (GGD 179). The line is unclear.
