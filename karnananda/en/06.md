# Niryasa 6. Prabhu's Vow

*Nine jewel-verses on Srinivasa's mission; in payar: the news of Mahaprabhu's departure, the dream and the order to go to Vrindavana; the news of the departure of Rupa and Sanatana and their appearance in a dream; initiation from Gopala Bhatta*

**1.** Nine verses:
*"Here Bhagavan, producing the pure truth of the Satvatas, revealed it through one of His shaktis named Sri Rupa; through another of His shaktis, manifesting in a pure brahmana family the one named Sri Srinivasa, Sri Prabhu Himself wound up His lila in Nilachala. (1)*
*Sri Srinivasa Prabhu, who had resolved to go to Sri Purushottama, hearing from people's lips of the departure of Chaitanya, the ocean of mercy, fainted again and again from floods of grief; then merciful Bhagavan, seeing the devotee's pain, gave him in a dream this most wondrous command: (2)*
*'You have been born by Me as My own shakti; go quickly to Sri Vrindavana: there are the blessed Sri Rupa, Jiva and the others; I have already ordered them to entrust to you the jewels of their books; accept them without doubt and teach the people in Gauda.' (3)*
*Receiving this command and turning his whole heart to it, Sri Srinivasa again set his mind on seeing the beauty of the groves of Sri Vrindavana; but hearing there of the departure of the Gosvamis, crying 'Alas, alas!' in grief, his heart distraught, he fell unconscious in the middle of the road. (4)*
*In a dream Sri Rupa and the others, together with Srila Sanatana, said to him: 'This is no time for you to grieve, for Gopala Bhatta is there; therefore accept (from him) the best mantra and all the books composed by us, go to the land of Gauda, spread our teaching and instruct the Vaishnavas.' (5)*
*His mind flooded with the nectar of that command, he entered Vrindavana and with devotion received from Gopala Bhatta Prabhu the whole truth of the mantra; expert in understanding service to him, sent by that glorious one with abundant prema to go to Gauda, he eagerly replied: (6)*
*'If by your grace those connected with me will attain the lotus feet of Radha and Krishna, then I shall go; otherwise why should I go?' Hearing all this, the best of the Gosvamis in great joy went with it to Govinda. (7)*
*(Govinda said:) 'The command of those whose souls are wholly absorbed in meditation on Sri Govinda's lotus feet will bear fruit through shelter in Sri Srinivasa: in order to give (these books) he has been brought by Me to earth. Therefore let him go to Gauda; why this worry of yours here?' (8)*
*Having drunk this nectar of command issuing from the moon-like lips of Sri Govinda, bowing to the assembly of Gosvamis with a glad heart and circumambulating them, taking with devotion the collection of books and setting out eagerly for the land of Gauda — may Sri Srinivasa Prabhu, the sole treasury of compassion, be ever victorious! (9)"*[^1]

**2.** To reveal the pure lila of Vraja in Gauda,
(the Lord), as His heart desired, empowered Sri Rupa.

**3.** Manifesting one shakti, empowering Sri Rupa,
He revealed the books with great joy.

**4.** To reveal in Gauda what was in His heart,
Gaura had in His heart the desire to distribute it.

**5.** Most wondrous was the shakti Gaura manifested —
who can understand the mind of Chaitanya?

**6.** In Nilachala Mahaprabhu's manifest pastimes were going on;
the desire arose in (Srinivasa's) heart to see His holy feet.

**7.** Leaving everything, Prabhu (Srinivasa) set out,
fixing his mind on the shelter of the holy feet.

**8.** With desire in his heart he went on and on —
and on the way he received the news that Prabhu had disappeared.

**9.** As soon as he heard it, he fell unconscious to the ground;
who can describe that ocean of grief?

**10.** Again and again Prabhu (Srinivasa) faints, now unconscious,
now wailing and weeping.

**11.** Then Mahaprabhu, seeing the devotee's grief,
came before him and began to speak:

**12.** He comforted him much, placing His foot on his head,
and began to speak sweetly:

**13.** "You are My own shakti, listen:
give up grief and go quickly to Vrindavana.

**14.** Where Sri Rupa and Sanatana live,
there they have expanded the books on the pastimes of Radha and Krishna.

**15.** Taking all those books, reveal them in Gauda
and distribute them with joy in your heart."

**16.** Receiving the command, the nectar of His words,
he set out, bowing to Mahaprabhu's feet.

**17.** Then he went to Sri Vrindavana:
"I shall see the beauty of its groves with my own eyes."

**18.** Coming to Sri Mathura-mandala, he halted
and there heard that the two brothers had disappeared.

**19.** As soon as he heard it, Prabhu fell, striking the ground,
and with cries lamented greatly:

**20.** "If I have not been able to see the two brothers,
what further purpose is there in life?"

**21.** Firmly resolving thus in his heart,
he fell unconscious at the foot of a tree.

**22.** Then the two brothers, seeing the devotee's grief,
came, greatly pleased, to appear to him.

**23.** Placing their feet on his head, they say to Prabhu:
"Look at us, look your fill."

**24.** Seeing with his own eyes the beauty of Sri Rupa and Sanatana,
he felt a bliss that cannot be told.

**25.** The two brothers, rejoicing, say:
"Through you the lowly, the wretched and the wicked will be delivered.

**26.** Give up grief and go quickly;
take shelter at the feet of Sri Bhatta Gosani.

**27.** From him you will receive initiation into the mantra;
through that is our mercy — what more can we say?

**28.** With the mass of books you will go to Gauda
and deliver the living beings smitten by Kali."

**29.** Receiving the command, these nectarean words,
he went to Vrindavana as revealed.

**30.** Coming there, he saw the feet of Srila Gosvami,[^2]
fell to the ground and offered many praises:

**31.** "Be merciful to me, Prabhu, filled with compassion;
bring me to the goal, Prabhu, out of mercy."

**32.** He told Prabhu the whole command of the two brothers,
and the Gosani understood why he had come.

**33.** Hearing this, the Gosvami was boundlessly glad:
gooseflesh rose all over his body, tears flowed from his eyes.

**34.** "Listen, Srinivasa, you are my life:
to see you I have held on to life.

**35.** You are the life of my life;
for you Mahaprabhu gave this treasure.

**36.** Look here: a letter written by Mahaprabhu's holy hand —
I have kept it carefully for you.[^3]

**37.** Look your fill at Prabhu's handwriting:
your good fortune, my son, is beyond words.

**38.** And look further: the seat on which Mahaprabhu sat,
and the cord — He carefully sent them to me.

**39.** Sitting on this seat given by Mahaprabhu,
I shall give you initiation into the mantra in great bliss."

**40.** Sitting on the seat, he gave him initiation into the mantra,
and then, giving him the collection of books, taught him.

**41.** When my Prabhu had become expert in the books,
seeing this, all the Gosanis were pleased.

**42.** They ordered: "Go to Gauda —
this is the order of Sri Rupa, there is no doubt."

**43.** Sri Jiva says: "Listen, Acharya Mahashaya:
know for certain — this is Mahaprabhu's order.

**44.** Once, for your sake, Mahaprabhu
sent a letter from Nilachala."

**45.** Seeing the letter, my Prabhu began to weep
and, weeping and weeping, fell into thought.

**46.** ("He who is born as the embodiment of prema, named Srinivasa —
I am not to see him: the Creator has left Me without hope.")[^4]

**47.** (So) the Gosani mercifully told me;
(and further:) "Having entrusted all the books to Srinivasa, (say to him):

**48.** 'Take these books and go to Gauda';
this is Mahaprabhu's order — accept the collection of books."

**49.** Then my Prabhu began to speak:
"In my heart was the desire always to remain with Prabhu,[^5]

**50.** to live in Vrindavana and serve Prabhu;
how, leaving this, shall I go to Gauda?

**51.** But the guru's order is stronger — it cannot be otherwise."
And he made known the desire of his heart:

**52.** "If I am indeed to go to Gauda,
then gladly give me this order:

**53.** whoever, Prabhu, accepts a connection with me
will attain the feet of Radha and Krishna.

**54.** Give this order all together, out of mercy;
otherwise I shall not go — listen attentively."

**55.** Hearing this, all the Gosanis were boundlessly glad,
streams of prema flowed unceasingly from their eyes.

**56.** All the Gosanis together, coming to Govinda,
entreated Him with joined palms:

**57.** Sri Bhatta Gosani and Sri Dasa Raghunatha,
Sri Jiva Gosani and Raghunatha Bhatta,

**58.** Lokanatha Gosani and Bhugarbha Thakura —
all prayed much to Sri Govinda,

**59.** meditating in their hearts on Sri Govinda's two feet:
"Out of mercy give Srinivasa this order:

**60.** whoever, Prabhu, accepts a connection with him
will attain the feet of Radha and Krishna."

**61.** All gladly made this request;
hearing it, Sri Govinda gave the command:[^6]

**62.** "To taste rasa I descended in Gauda
and tasted rasa in many ways.

**63.** Know the reason for which I descended:
I flooded all people, giving them the treasure of prema.

**64.** By My shakti his birth has been manifested:
he was born as the embodiment of prema, and his name is Srinivasa.

**65.** Whoever accepts in his heart a connection with him
will attain the feet of Radha and Krishna."

**66.** Receiving the nectar of command from the moon-like lips of Sri Govinda,
all together heard it with straining ears:

**67.** "Send him quickly to Gauda:
let him go, taking the collection of books."

**68.** Then my Prabhu, circumambulating all,
fell to the ground and wept much, crying aloud.

**69.** In everyone's heart the ocean of bliss swelled:
who can describe the bliss that came upon them?

**70.** My Prabhu, having received the nectar of Sri Govinda's command,
described, gazing at Sri Govinda's moon-like face:

**71.** This is the pada, in raga Suhai:
With what chisel was this moon-face carved, friend, who carved those two eyes?
As I look, my heart feels so strange — my very heart is witness to it. (1)
Who, friend, carefully cut the jewel and set it in (His) ear?
Together with my mind, my five life-breaths — I have become a yogini, meditating on Him. (2)
Above the nose shines the gajamukta pearl, set in gold on either side:
who is this — a crescent moon with lightning, smiling from behind a cloud? (3)
On the lovely forehead shines — what a lovely tilaka, friend! — and over it a row of curls;
deep in my heart it sparkles, friend — like a row of bees on the moon. (4)
That noose of Cupid — the tilt of His crown-chuda, friend — where did He learn it?
I have not seen it to my heart's content, friend — that is the great pain of my heart. (5)
With what sweet rasa His words are filled, friend — if only one could take them in one's hand!
If the Creator had made them so, friend, I would break off piece after piece and eat them. (6)
His arms are like the trunks of the best of elephants, friend, the curve of His arms, and at their tips red hingul;
the bird of youth, friend, dies of thirst, begging for the rasa of His touch. (7)
Who, as if anointing with nectar, adorned His forehead with a sandal tilaka?
Gazing at the moon-face, how can I hold my heart, how can my soul live? (8)
The tinkling of the anklets on His feet surpasses the khanjana bird's chirping, friend; His gait is slow as a rutting elephant's.
In the flood of nectarean rasa Srinivasa has drowned, friend: the Creator has fashioned an ocean of prema. (9)[^7]

**72.** Having relished this, all wept with their arms round each other's necks;
who can describe the bliss that came upon them?

**73.** My Prabhu greeted all as befitted:
he bowed down like a rod, overflowing with prema.

**74.** Some he embraced, to others he bowed;
mercy came upon all, according to their worth.

[^1]: The nine Sanskrit verses (the "necklace of nine jewels", 5.184) were apparently composed by the author himself or taken from a Sanskrit biography; the text in the edition is corrupt in places (especially vv. 7–8), translated by sense. The editor glosses v. 8: "as the giver of these books, this Srinivasa has been brought to earth".
[^2]: Gopala Bhatta Gosvami.
[^3]: The mention of a letter in Mahaprabhu's own hand is peculiar to the *Karnananda*; in the *Prema-vilasa* and the *Bhakti-ratnakara* the Lord sends Gopala Bhatta His seat and cord (cf. 5.137–140).
[^4]: Probably words from Mahaprabhu's letter (36) which Srinivasa recalls or reads; the change of speaker is not marked in the original.
[^5]: Here "Prabhu" is Srinivasa's guru, Gopala Bhatta.
[^6]: Govinda is the Deity Govindadeva in Vrindavana; His command is given as the speech of Krishna Himself, who descended in Gauda as Mahaprabhu (62–63).
[^7]: A pada of Srinivasa (signed "Srinivasa" in the 9th stanza) describing the form of Govindadeva in the mood of a gopi. The portrayal of the gopis' feelings is transcendental prema, not mundane passion (cf. the note to 1.318).
