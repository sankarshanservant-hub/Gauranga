# Niryasa 4. Sri Ramachandra Kaviraja's Instruction to Maharaja Sri Vira Hambira

*King Vira Hambira begs Srinivasa for mercy; Srinivasa entrusts him to Ramachandra Kaviraja, who expounds to him sadhana-bhakti according to the Gosvamis: service of the Vaishnavas and of Tulasi, the qualities of a Vaishnava, the limbs of bhakti, raganuga-bhakti, the Shikshashtaka, the siddha-deha and the prayers of Rupa and Raghunatha for the service of the manjaris, the supremacy of parakiya, of Vrindavana, the Yamuna and Sri Radha, the eternal lila*

**1.** Glory, glory to Mahaprabhu Sri Krishna Chaitanya,
the purifier of the fallen, apart from whom there is no other!

**2.** Hear with care another account
that I heard from the lips of my mistress.

**3.** The king, having returned to his home,
meditated in his heart on the virtues of Ramachandra.

**4.** The king's heart is always overflowing — he thinks to himself,
day and night meditating on Ramachandra's virtues:

**5.** "There is no jewel on earth like Ramachandra —
this I have understood with all my heart."

**6.** Having reflected, he resolved firmly:
"If I should have the good fortune to hear of sadhana from his lips…"

**7.** Then the king came to Prabhu's house
and bowed many times, prostrate on the ground.

**8.** Going to Srimati, he bowed to her,
then went to Ramachandra and bowed to him.

**9.** Near Prabhu the king, most humble,
with joined palms speaks with humility:

**10.** "You have descended to deliver the fallen;
be merciful and accept me.

**11.** With a straw between my teeth I beg, Prabhu, be merciful:
do not despise me, wretched and low.

**12.** If out of mercy you have given me the shade of your feet,
do not reject me, scorched by the threefold miseries.

**13.** So much of my life has passed in vain;
seeing Ramachandra, my heart has become pure.

**14.** I know nothing of the goal and the means;
by your own goodness be merciful, jewel of virtues.

**15.** The little I heard from the lips of Vyasa
has gladdened my heart."

**16.** The king says: "Prabhu, you are merciful;
show me mercy, filled with compassion.

**17.** You are an ocean of mercy, the purifier of the fallen;
be merciful, Prabhu, I take shelter.

**18.** Accept me, Prabhu, as your own."
Saying this, the king fell to the ground.

**19.** Then Prabhu himself carefully raised him
and out of mercy embraced him tightly.

**20.** "The goal and the means according to the teaching of the Gosvamis
Ramachandra will clearly explain to you."

**21.** Saying this, Prabhu called Ramachandra
and, taking the king by the hand, entrusted him to him:

**22.** "Listen, Ramachandra, do this:
accept him as a younger brother."

**23.** Hearing this, Ramachandra said, "As you command —
I shall tell him of Krishna in particular."

**24.** The king again bowed to Ramachandra
and humbly offered him many praises.

**25.** Seeing this, Prabhu was glad
and, pleased, says to the king:

**26.** "Listen, listen, O king, with one mind:
Rupa and Sanatana have been merciful to you.

**27.** In order to show you mercy,
Mahaprabhu in the form of books entered your house.

**28.** You, maharaja, are most fortunate:
there is no fortune on earth equal to yours.

**29.** These books are a great jewel, supremely radiant:
when they entered (your house), your heart became pure.

**30.** Understand for yourself what you were —
and to such a person they showed mercy, infusing shakti.

**31.** My Prabhu[^1] and Sri Rupa and Sanatana
joyfully showed you mercy.

**32.** The six Gosvamis, in order to accept you,
under the pretext of theft showed you full mercy."[^2]

**33.** Hearing this, the maharaja's heart overflowed:
his body was covered with gooseflesh, his eyes filled with tears.

**34.** Faltering with prema, he speaks broken words,
weeping aloud, rolling on the ground.

**35.** Then Prabhu carefully raised him
and, rejoicing, out of mercy embraced him tightly.

**36.** Again taking the king by the hand, with a joyful heart
he entrusted him to Ramachandra.

**37.** Again and again Prabhu says in great eagerness:
"Tell him the goal and the means according to the teaching of the Gosvamis.

**38.** And let him hear one thing more —
why the books of the Gosvamis came to him."

**39.** Ramachandra, receiving Prabhu's order, at once
spoke to the king with a joyful heart:

**40.** "What shall I tell you of sadhana —
the mercy of the Gosvamis has come to you in full.

**41.** Whoever takes shelter at my Prabhu's feet,
to him Rupa and Sanatana show mercy first.

**42.** To preach the books in Gauda,
Prabhu brought them from Vraja with great effort.

**43.** Prabhu came to Gauda having taken a vow;
the reason for that vow I shall tell in particular later.

**44.** All the Gosvamis, loving you,
took up residence in your house in the form of books.

**45.** So great is Prabhu's mercy upon you —
who can tell the limit of your good fortune?

**46.** First of all the Gosvamis came to your house —
by that your heart has become pure.

**47.** You are most fortunate — understand this in your heart;
who can describe your greatness, brother?

**48.** Now I shall tell you for certain,
if your heart is ready to hear of the limbs of sadhana:

**49.** serve the Vaishnavas and serve Tulasi,
and without effort you will attain Krishna's feet.

**50.** See: my Prabhu's dharma is the service of the Vaishnavas;
he placed it even above the service of the Sri Vigraha.[^3]

**51.** Therefore this is Prabhu's certain dharma:
serve the Vaishnavas with a joyful heart.

**52.** Serve the Vaishnavas exclusively:
drink charanamrita[^4] and eat prasada,

**53.** the dust of the Vaishnavas' feet is the ornament of the head,
sincerely and constantly keep the company of Vaishnavas.

**54.** Serve the Vaishnavas without committing offences,
and without effort you will attain Krishna — I tell you.

**55.** Even a small offence against a Vaishnava
obstructs the prema even of a great devotee full of prema.

**56.** The power of a Vaishnava can give and take away Krishna;
serve such Vaishnavas, brother, with great eagerness.

**57.** Krishna and Krishna's devotee are equal in virtues —
of this there is proof in the words of the Purana,

**58.** in the *Srimad-Bhagavatam*:
*"In one who has unalloyed devotion to Bhagavan all the gods reside with all good qualities; whence great qualities in one who is not devoted to Hari and runs after fancies towards the external and transient?"*[^5]

**59.** All these great qualities are in the body of a Vaishnava:
all Krishna's qualities pass into His devotee.

**60.** These qualities are the marks of a Vaishnava;
I shall tell a little of them for my own purification.

**61.** Merciful, hostile to none, truthful, equal,
faultless, self-controlled, gentle, pure, possessionless,

**62.** benefactor of all, peaceful, with Krishna as his only shelter,
free of worldly desire, free of worldly endeavour, steady, conqueror of the six foes,

**63.** eating moderately, never careless, honouring others, himself without pride,
grave, compassionate, friendly, wise, expert, silent.[^6]

**64.** To awaken Krishna-prema, this is the principal limb:
therefore, leaving everything, keep the company of Vaishnavas.

**65.** Giving up bad company — this is the conduct of a Vaishnava;
all this is the essence I have told you.

**66.** So, brother, I have told you of the service of Vaishnavas;
now I shall tell you of the service of Tulasi.

**67.** Whoever serves Tulasi in nine ways
becomes a vessel of Krishna's mercy:

**68.** see Tulasi, touch her and meditate on her —
always do this attentively;

**69.** utter Tulasi's name and bow to her,
hear Tulasi's name unceasingly;

**70.** plant Tulasi, water Tulasi,
always, unceasingly worship Tulasi.

**71.** Whoever serves Tulasi in these nine ways —
who can describe his greatness and virtues?

**72.** Sri Krishna will surely love him,
and having obtained that love he will dwell with Krishna.

**73.** The proof of this:
*"Seen, touched, meditated upon, glorified, bowed to, heard of, planted, watered and daily worshipped — auspicious Tulasi… Those who day after day serve the goddess Tulasi in these nine ways dwell in the abode of Hari for thousands of millions of yugas."*[^7]

**74.** Hearing all this, the king rejoiced at heart
and made a request at Ramachandra's feet:

**75.** "The sixty-four kinds of bhakti and all the other means —
my heart longs to hear of them."

**76.** Ramachandra says: "Brother, fix your heart
and listen with joy, filling your ears.

**77.** Hear, O king, of bhakti as sadhana,
by hearing of which one obtains the treasure of Krishna-prema.

**78.** Hearing and the other acts are its essential characteristic;
by its secondary characteristic the treasure of prema is born from it.[^8]

**79.** Eternally perfect Krishna-prema is never the product of sadhana:
in a heart purified by hearing and the rest it awakens.

**80.** Sadhana-bhakti is of two kinds:
vaidhi-bhakti and raganuga-bhakti.

**81.** When one without raga worships by the injunctions of scripture,
that is called vaidhi-bhakti, conduct according to scripture.

**82.** Sadhana-bhakti is manifold, it has many limbs;
in brief I shall say something of them:

**83.** service of the guru, initiation, shelter at the guru's feet,
following the path of the sadhus, inquiry and learning of true dharma,

**84.** worship of Krishna, renouncing enjoyment for Krishna's sake,
fasting on Ekadashi, accepting only what is needed to live,

**85.** worship of cows, brahmanas, Vaishnavas, the amalaki and the ashvattha,
avoiding offences in service and against the name — whoever is able;

**86.** not taking many disciples, not associating with non-Vaishnavas,
not studying many books if this is not a limb of bhakti;

**87.** being equal in loss and gain, not succumbing to grief and the like,
not blaspheming other scriptures and other gods;

**88.** not listening to worldly talk and blasphemy of Vaishnavas,
causing no anxiety to any living being by mind or word;

**89.** remembrance, worship, offering prayers and sankirtana,
service, friendship, attendance, surrender of self;

**90.** making petitions, bowing down like a rod, singing before (the Deity),
rising to greet, following behind, residing at a holy place and temple;

**91.** reciting hymns, japa, sankirtana, circumambulation,
partaking of mahaprasada, garlands, incense and scents, delightful (after offering);

**92.** seeing the Sri Murti, arati, festivals,
service of what is His — with gifts for His love, meditation, all this;

**93.** His own Tulasi, the Vaishnava, Mathura and the *Bhagavata* —
the service of these four is most pleasing to Krishna;

**94.** all endeavours for Krishna's mercy,
celebrating His appearance and other festivals with devotees,

**95.** complete surrender, the vow of Kartika and other vows —
these are the sixty-four limbs, of supreme greatness.[^9]

**96.** The company of sadhus, nama-sankirtana, hearing the *Bhagavata*,
living in Mathura, serving the Sri Murti with faith —

**97.** of all sadhanas these are the principal limbs:
even slight contact with these five gives birth to Krishna-prema.

**98.** I have described the limbs of the sadhana of vaidhi-bhakti,
by hearing of which the treasure of prema is born in the heart."

**99.** Then the king, having heard of the limbs of sadhana-bhakti,
humbly addressed Ramachandra:

**100.** "I have heard of sadhana-bhakti with its many limbs;
now my heart wishes to hear of the bhakti of the raganuga path."

**101.** Then Ramachandra, rejoicing at heart,
smiling, says to the king:

**102.** "Listen, listen, brother, about raganuga-bhakti:
as soon as you heard of it, your heart was filled with longing.

**103.** Raganuga-bhakti is the essence of all goals;
I have not the power to explain it completely.

**104.** I shall say only a little — listen attentively,
hear, O king, the characteristics of raganuga-bhakti.

**105.** Hearing, kirtana and the rest (the Gosvamis) described as vaidhi-bhakti,
but they also placed them within raganuga-bhakti.

**106.** This is the certain teaching of the Gosvamis:
through vaidhi-bhakti one comes to raga-bhakti.

**107.** Whoever, having heard of the greatness of hearing and kirtana,
performs them, accepting the injunction of scripture —

**108.** that is why the Gosvamis described vaidhi-bhakti;
why (hearing and kirtana) are limbs of raga, I shall tell — listen.

**109.** Without hearing and kirtana there is no raga-bhakti;
I shall tell the reason with certainty.

**110.** What to speak of others — Radha Thakurani,
the limit of sweetness, a mine of jewel-like virtues,

**111.** worshipful to all, best of all, the object of everyone's worship,
whose beauty and other qualities even Krishna cannot fathom[^10] —

**112.** if She suddenly hears the name of Krishna,
as soon as She hears it the lovely One begins to tremble.

**113.** Suddenly She loses control of Herself;
who can describe the waves of manifold bhavas?

**114.** Worshipful to all, best of all, the object of everyone's worship,
whose excellent qualities Krishna cannot fully know —

**115.** gooseflesh rises over Her whole body, Her limbs grow limp,
and how many more waves of bhava rise in Her!

**116.** Can I describe the bhavas pervading Her whole body —
bhava, hava and all the sattvika and vyabhichari bhavas?

**117.** In the waves of bhava Her body has no rest:
as soon as She hears Krishna's name, She loses all composure.

**118.** She wishes for many mouths to chant Krishna's name,
wishes for millions of ears to hear that name.

**119.** Who can describe the virtue of the maddening name,
which can make the lifeless living?

**120.** Krishna's name makes the living lifeless —
such is the virtue of the name that it draws all the senses.

**121.** Whoever conceives a longing for such nectar of Krishna's name
abandons worldly dharma and the Vedas and worships Krishna.

**122.** What do I know to tell of such an all-powerful name?
From the lips of Sri Rupa flows a stream of nectar:

**123.** in every syllable flows the essence of sweetness;
such a wondrous verse the Gosani proclaimed.

**124.** In the *Vidagdha-madhava* Sri Rupa composed this verse:
*"Dancing on the lips, it creates the desire for many mouths; entering the ears, it awakens the longing for millions of ears; dwelling in the courtyard of the heart, it conquers the activity of all the senses. I do not know of how many nectars these two syllables 'Krish-na' are made!"*[^11]

**125.** And in the *Stavavali*, in the hymn *Premambhoja-maranda*, Sri Dasa Gosvami says:
*"(Radha,) whose braid is Her hidden pique and contrariness, radiant with the tilaka of good fortune, whose ears delight in the earrings of hearing Krishna's name and fame."*[^12]

**126.** Hidden pique and contrariness are Her braid,
the tilaka of good fortune the charming essence of Her beauty;

**127.** Krishna's name, qualities and fame are the earrings in Her ears,
Krishna's name, qualities and fame the stream of Her words.

**128.** Gaurachandra Himself, taking up that bhava of Radha,
tasted Krishna's name with bliss.

**129.** Of this there is a verse of Sri Rupa Gosvami in the *Stavamala*:
*"His tongue quivers as He loudly chants 'Hare Krishna'; His hand is beautiful with a girdle-string of knots for counting the names; His eyes are wide, His arms, like long bolts, move playfully — will that Chaitanya again come before my eyes?"*[^13]

**130.** Sri Krishna Chaitanya is the son of the king of Vraja Himself:
He tasted the nectar of the name in many ways.

**131.** Such a name of Krishna, O king, chant unceasingly —
by it the essence of sweetness is attained.

**132.** And hear further the verses of Mahaprabhu's *Shikshashtaka*,
which destroy the darkness of the heart like rising moonlight.

**133.** Prabhu always relished them with Svarupa and the others;
by hearing them the heart becomes most pure.

**134.** That *Shikshashtaka*, brother, I shall tell you:
string it on the thread of faith and wear it on your heart.

**135.** This is pure raga-bhakti, I tell you for certain:
by hearing it prema is born in the heart.

**136.** Prabhu says: "Hear, Svarupa and Ramananda Raya:
nama-sankirtana is the supreme means in the age of Kali.

**137.** Whoever in Kali worships Krishna by the sacrifice of sankirtana
is truly intelligent — he attains Krishna's feet."

**138.** Of this there is a verse in the *Srimad-Bhagavatam*, canto 11, chapter 5, verse 32:
*"Him who in syllables is 'Krishna' but in lustre not dark, with His parts, associates, weapons and companions, the intelligent worship by sacrifices consisting chiefly of sankirtana."*[^14]

**139.** By nama-sankirtana all anarthas are destroyed,
all happiness dawns, and the jubilation of Krishna-prema.

**140.** Of this there is Sri Mahaprabhu's verse in the *Padyavali*:
*"Victory to Sri Krishna-sankirtana: it cleanses the mirror of the heart, extinguishes the great forest fire of material existence, spreads the moonlight that opens the lily of the highest good, and is the life of the bride of knowledge; it swells the ocean of bliss, gives at every step the taste of full nectar, and bathes the whole self."*[^15]

**141.** By sankirtana sin and samsara are destroyed;
one attains Krishna and immersion in the ocean of the nectar of service.

**142.** Sorrow and humility arose — (Prabhu) recites His own verse,
by hearing the meaning of which all sorrow and grief depart:

**143.** in the *Padyavali*, His own verse:
*"You have manifested many names and invested in them all Your power; You have set no fixed time for remembering them. Such is Your mercy, O Bhagavan — and such is my misfortune that no attachment for them has arisen in me."*

**144.** "Many people have many kinds of desires —
out of mercy He spread many names.

**145.** Eating, lying down, wherever one is, one chants the name:
there are no rules of place and time, and it gives all perfection.

**146.** All power He distributed among the names —
yet by my ill fate I have no attachment to the name.

**147.** How one should take the name so that prema is born —
hear the characteristics of that, Svarupa and Ramaraya":

**148.** in the *Padyavali*, His own verse:
*"Humbler than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, seeking no honour and honouring others, one should always sing of Hari."*

**149.** "Though exalted, he considers himself lower than grass;
he is tolerant in two ways, like a tree:

**150.** a tree, even when cut, says nothing,
and even drying up and dying asks no one for water;

**151.** whoever asks, to him it gives its wealth,
it bears heat and rain and protects others.

**152.** Though exalted, the Vaishnava will be without pride;
he will honour all living beings, knowing that Krishna dwells in them.

**153.** Whoever, having become such, takes Krishna's name,
in him prema for Krishna's feet is born."

**154.** As He spoke, Prabhu's humility grew,
and He began to beg Krishna for pure bhakti.

**155.** Such is the nature of prema: where there is the bond of prema,
one thinks, "I have not even a scent of bhakti for Krishna."

**156.** In the *Padyavali*, His own verse:
*"Not wealth, not followers, not beautiful poetry do I desire, O Lord of the world: in birth after birth may I have causeless devotion to You, O Ishvara."*

**157.** "I ask for neither wealth, nor followers, nor beautiful poetry:
Krishna, out of mercy give me pure bhakti."

**158.** In great humility He again begs for the gift of dasya-bhakti,
regarding Himself as a conditioned soul in samsara:[^16]

**159.** in the *Padyavali*, His own verse:
*"O son of Nanda, think of me, Your servant, fallen into the terrible ocean of existence, as a particle of dust at Your lotus feet."*

**160.** "I am Your eternal servant, but, forgetting You,
I have fallen into the ocean of existence, bound by maya.

**161.** Out of mercy make me like a particle of dust at Your feet,
and I, Your servant, shall serve You."

**162.** Again great longing and humility arose:
He begs Krishna for nama-sankirtana with prema:

**163.** in the *Padyavali*, His own verse:
*"When will my eyes be wet with streams of tears, my voice choked with emotion and my body covered with gooseflesh as I chant Your name?"*

**164.** "Without the treasure of prema a poor life is in vain;
making me Your servant, give me the treasure of prema as my wage."

**165.** Absorbed in another rasa, separation manifested;
anxiety, sorrow and humility — He speaks in delirium:

**166.** in the *Padyavali*, His own verse:
*"A moment has become a yuga for me, my eyes the rainy season, the whole world is empty in separation from Govinda."*

**167.** "In anxiety the day does not pass — a moment is like a yuga,
both eyes rain tears like the clouds of the rainy season.

**168.** In separation from Govinda the three worlds have become empty;
I burn as in a fire of husks, yet life does not leave me."

**169.** Krishna, to test (Radha), became indifferent;
all the sakhis say, "Ignore Krishna!"

**170.** As Radha reflected on this, in Her pure heart
the natural disposition of prema awoke.

**171.** Joy, longing, humility, boldness, meekness —
so many bhavas arose in one place.

**172.** With so many bhavas Radha's mind grew agitated,
and before the sakhis She boldly recited a verse.

**173.** In that same bhava Prabhu uttered that verse,
and, uttering it, became the same Himself.

**174.** In the *Padyavali*, His own verse:
*"Let Him embrace me, devoted to His feet, or trample me, or break my heart by not showing Himself — let that rake do as He pleases: He, and no other, is the lord of my life."*[^17]

**175.** In this verse there is a great wealth of meaning;
I tell it briefly — it has no end.

**176.** Here it is, set to a raga:
I am a maidservant at Krishna's feet, He is the treasure-house of the happiness of rasa: embracing me, He makes me His own.
Whether or not He grants me His sight, tormenting my body and mind, still He is the lord of my life. (1)
Friend, hear what my heart has firmly decided:
whether He loves me or gives me pain, my life's lord is Krishna and no other. (Refrain)
Leaving other women, He, subject to me in body and mind, displays my good fortune:
causing them all pain, He sports with me, showing this to those women. (2)
Or else He is a rake, deceitful, bold and false: gathering other women,
to cause me heartache He sports with them before my eyes — and still He is the lord of my life. (3)[^18]

**177.** The meanings of these and all the other verses
(Prabhu) relished together with Svarupa and the others.

**178.** Thus Prabhu, absorbed in each of these bhavas,
relished delirious speech (pralapa), uttering each of these verses.

**179.** Earlier, composing eight verses, He taught the people;
the meaning of those eight verses He relished Himself.

**180.** Whoever reads and hears the verses of Prabhu's *Shikshashtaka*,
his Krishna-prema-bhakti grows day by day.

**181.** Although Prabhu is as deep as millions of oceans,
at the rising of the moon of many bhavas He grows restless.

**182.** Whatever verses there are in Jayadeva, in the *Bhagavata*,
in Raya's drama and in the *Karnamrita* —

**183.** in the corresponding bhava He recites those verses,
and, absorbed in the corresponding bhava, relishes them.

**184.** For twelve years, in such a state, day and night,
He relished the rasa of Krishna with Svarupa and the others.

**185.** What can I say of the glory of hearing and the rest,
in which a stream of nectar always flows?

**186.** One whose mind is absorbed in pure raga
knows this — no one else knows it.

**187.** Hearing, kirtana and the rest are the essence of raga-bhakti;
for raganuga devotees this is the essence of their work.

**188.** Ragatmika-bhakti, the principal, is in the residents of Vraja;
the bhakti of the devotees who follow them is called raganuga.

**189.** Intense thirst for the Beloved is the essential characteristic of raga;
absorption in the Beloved is called its secondary characteristic.

**190.** Bhakti full of raga is called raganuga;
hearing of it, some fortunate soul becomes eager for it.

**191.** Out of that eagerness he follows the bhava of the residents of Vraja;
the nature of raganuga is that it does not depend on the arguments of scripture.

**192.** Of this in the *Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu*, Eastern Division, second wave:
*"The bhakti clearly manifest in the residents of Vraja is called ragatmika; the bhakti that follows it is called raganuga."* *"When, on hearing of the sweetness of their bhavas, the intelligence longs for it without depending on scripture or argument — that is the sign of the birth of eagerness (lobha)."*[^19]

**193.** It has two kinds of sadhana, external and internal:
externally, in the body of the practitioner, he hears and chants,

**194.** and in the mind, contemplating his own siddha-deha,
day and night he remembers the feet of Radha and Krishna.

**195.** Following the one whose bhava he has taken as his shelter,
he serves unceasingly, his mind turned inward.

**196.** Of this in the *Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu*, Eastern Division, second wave, the words of Sri Rupa Gosvami:
*"One who longs for that bhava should serve both in the form of a practitioner and in the perfected form, following the residents of Vraja."*[^20]

**197.** So deep is this bhava that it cannot be told;
the minds of all of us cannot enter it.

**198.** Once in Vraja, when Krishna, Bhagavan Himself,
entered into Radha's pure bhava,

**199.** accepting the bhava and lustre of Sri Radhika,
He descended in Navadvipa to taste it.

**200.** Which insignificant living being could enter
into that wondrous bhava and describe it?

**201.** Kaviraja Gosani, knowing its essence,
described it clearly in his book:

**202.** absorbed in the bhava of a maidservant, Vrajendra-nandana
relished it in the mood of following.

**203.** He described this at length in the *Antya-lila* —
look at those pastimes carefully:

**204.** in the seventeenth and eighteenth chapters
he depicted it — relish it!

**205.** Prabhu lay in a state like a tortoise;[^21]
what bhava He relished in that —

**206.** when Svarupa Gosani came and brought Him back to consciousness,
He told Svarupa what was in His heart.

**207.** As soon as He regained consciousness, His hands and feet came out,
and His body became as before, as it should be.

**208.** Rising, Prabhu sat up, looks this way and that,
and asks Svarupa, "Where have you brought Me?

**209.** Hearing the sound of the flute, I went to Vrindavana
and saw Vrajendra-nandana playing the flute in the pasture.

**210.** With the signal of the flute He summoned Radha to the bower-house
and went to the kunja to sport.

**211.** I followed behind Him;
the tinkling of His ornaments captivated My ears.

**212.** He laughed and joked with the gopis,
and My ears rejoiced to hear His voice and words.

**213.** Why have you brought Me back to give Me pain in vain?
Having found Krishna's pastimes, I could not see them through."

**214.** In the eighteenth chapter is the water-pastime:
what bhava He displayed in it!

**215.** Beholding this water-pastime,
He, entering it with His mind, sees many delights:

**216.** "Seeing the Kalindi, I went to Vrindavana
and saw Vrajendra-nandana sporting in the water.

**217.** Together with Radhika and the other gopis
He sports in the water of the Yamuna with great delight.

**218.** Standing on the bank with the sakhis, I watch;
one sakhi shows Me the delights of the water-play."

**219.** So Prabhu spoke to Svarupa in His transport,
revealing what was in His heart.

**220.** What Sri Krishna Chaitanya relished
is known only to Svarupa and the others (His intimates);

**221.** apart from Svarupa and the rest, it is known to no one —
so the author (of the CC) says with certainty.[^22]

**222.** And one thing more — hear it attentively,
O king, give up envy and listen.

**223.** When Sri Rupa Manjari was before Sri Radha,
she made this prayer before Her.

**224.** Of this, in the *Stavamala*, in the *Chatu-pushpanjali*, the words of Sri Rupa Gosvami:
*"When, O Bimboshthi, will the son of the lord of Vraja snatch and eat the betel that I am offering to Your lotus face? When, O beautiful goddess, will You order this (maidservant) to arrange Your curling locks, disordered in play?"*[^23]

**225.** Its meaning:
"Sri Radha Bimboshthi, when shall I, having prepared betel with fragrant camphor,
offer it to Your lips?

**226.** I shall joyfully place it in Your mouth,
and the son of the king of Vraja will snatch it and eat it.

**227.** Taking the rolled betel from my mistress's lips,
He will chew it and attain even greater bliss.

**228.** Be merciful, be gracious to me —
when shall I see this to my eyes' content?

**229.** O goddess, when You, in the transport of play,
weary with sport, grow tired,

**230.** I shall joyfully set right what was disordered in play —
when shall I arrange the mass of Your hair?"

**231.** These confidential words (Ramachandra) told the king;
hearing them, the king was greatly pleased.

**232.** Again Ramachandra says: "Listen, O king:
this is a most confidential, beautiful word.

**233.** Those who are eternally perfect — such is their work;
understand this in your heart.

**234.** All of Sri Radha's eternal companions —
even for them such a bhava is very hard to attain.

**235.** She who always serves in the form of a manjari —
in the state of a practitioner the same always appears to her.

**236.** Therefore, though perfect, she performs sadhana,
and so she revealed it to her devotees.

**237.** Whoever follows her has such a way;
having found love, perform such sadhana.

**238.** Now hear the words of prayer of Dasa Gosani:
in the body of a practitioner he always acts as a perfected one.

**239.** Having seen Radha in his cherished (spiritual) body,
he prays for the service of Sri Radha's feet:

**240.** 'Listen, goddess: to become a maidservant of Your holy feet —
that is my constant desire.

**241.** The sakhis who accompany You are equal to You;
before the bhava of such a sakhi I always bow.

**242.** Therefore this is my plea to Your feet:
out of mercy grant me the service of Your feet.

**243.** My constant desire is the service of Your feet;
apart from this, never give me anything else.'

**244.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Vilapa-kusumanjali*, verse 16 — the words of Sri Dasa Gosvami:
*"Apart from the excellent service of Your lotus feet, O goddess, I never ask for anything else. To friendship with You — obeisance, obeisance for ever; but for service to You may I have a taste, a taste in truth."*[^24]

**245.** Hear, brother, another wondrous account —
this is a most, most firm teaching of the Gosvamis.

**246.** Sri Rupa Manjari, on seeing Radha's lake[^25] —
what bhava rises in the heart on seeing it!

**247.** 'Listen, goddess: when Your lake
came before my eyes,

**248.** then there came into the path of my eyes
the lovely lotus-eyed One — I beheld Her face to face.

**249.** From then on a longing was born in my heart:
I wished to become a maidservant at Your lotus feet.

**250.** Sri Rupa Manjari anointed both my eyes
(with collyrium) in Vrindavana.

**251.** From then on, O (maidservant of) Sri Vrindavaneshvari,
I wish to apply red lac to Your holy feet.

**252.** If ever You show this mercy,
I shall serve, having received Your command.'"

**253.** Ramachandra says: "Listen, O king,
hear attentively a most wondrous word:

**254.** if in your heart a longing has arisen
to serve Radha and Krishna in Vrindavana,

**255.** if (you wish) to serve Their feet with raga
and to become a vessel of the mercy of the Two,

**256.** if (you wish) birth after birth to live in Sri Vraja-mandala,
serving abundantly and most purely —

**257.** then the feet of Svarupa, Rupa Gosani, Sanatana
with all their companions, and of Gopala Bhatta —

**258.** he whose heart is fixed in faith in their feet
is the one who serves the feet of the Two.

**259.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Vilapa-kusumanjali*, verses 15 and 14:
*"O lotus-eyed One! When Your lake, shining with clusters of lotuses over which thirsty bees rejoice, filled with sweet water, appeared before my eyes, at that very moment there arose in me the longing to serve You alone."* *"Since a certain manjari named Rupa in the land of Vraja, alas, illumined (anointed) my eyes, ever since then, O queen of Vrindavana, there has arisen in me an intense desire to see the red lac on Your lotus feet."*[^26]

**260.** In the *Stavavali*, in the *Manah-shiksha*, verse 3:
*"O mind, listen: if you wish to live with raga in the land of Vraja birth after birth, and if you wish to serve the Divine Couple directly, then always clearly, with prema, remember and bow to Svarupa, Sri Rupa with his companions, and his elder brother."*[^27]

**261.** If one serves, together with Rupa,
Radha, helpless in the battle of love, and the Holder of the Hill,

**262.** then one obtains direct service in Vraja:
this treasure is given only to those who have taken shelter of him.

**263.** Worship of Radha and Krishna, always taking the name,
meditation on the Two and nama-sankirtana,

**264.** many obeisances — always, with joy in your heart:
unceasingly and freely perform this service.

**265.** Strictly keeping to the drinking of these five nectars,
always serve Mount Govardhana with joy.

**266.** Becoming a follower of Sri Rupa together with her group,
serve the Two, immersing your mind in Them.

**267.** In the *Stavavali*, in the *Manah-shiksha*, verse 11:
*"O mind, in order to obtain in Vraja the direct service of Radha, helpless in the battle of love, and the Holder of the Hill together with Their companions — together with Sri Rupa, duly drinking every day this fivefold nectar: Their worship, Their names, meditation, hearing and obeisances — serve Govardhana."*[^28]

**268.** Sri Rupa Manjari and Sri Guna Manjari —
there is no comparison for their equal sweetness.

**269.** Sri Rupa Manjari, filled with love for Sri Guna Manjari,
prayed on her behalf:

**270.** when the sweet festival began,
many beauties of Vraja surrounded Krishna;

**271.** how much laughter and joking, how much loveliness and sweetness!
In the many delights of the play they forgot themselves.

**272.** Sri Radha Sudhamukhi, radiant with mirth,
greatly pleased, sends Sri Krishna —

**273.** sends Him with the corner of Her eye:
"Look, over there Guna Manjari is hiding —

**274.** go and kiss her face!"
"When shall I see this fun to my eyes' content?"

**275.** Of this in the *Stavamala*, in the *Utkalika-vallari*, verse 46:
*"O son of the lord of Vraja, when shall I see You, at the sweet festival, amid the throng of companions, sent by the glance of my Mistress bright with smiles, kissing the face of Guna Manjari who has hidden herself?"*[^29]

**276.** Firm in this bhava, Sri Dasa Gosani
wrote of it there in his own book.

**277.** At the end of the *Vishakhananda-stava* he wrote —
and within it is this word, the highest extract:

**278.** *Stavavali*, *Vishakhananda-stotra*, verse 134:
*"This garland of verses has been strung by one who serves only the dust of the lotus feet of Srimad Rupa; let those who have taken shelter of him smell (its fragrance)."*[^30]

**279.** One who serves the dust of the feet of Srimad Rupa —
someone strung these verses.

**280.** With joy he strung this garland of verses —
who will catch the enchanting fragrance of the garland?

**281.** Whoever has taken shelter of Sri Rupa catches that fragrance;
there is no other means of catching it.

**282.** Therefore the Gosani, knowing this in his heart,
wrote it clearly with joy.

**283.** Taking on his head the order of Sri Rupa and Sanatana,
he took up residence on the bank of Radha-kunda.

**284.** Of this:
*"Living on the bank of Radha-kunda by the order of Rupa and his brother…"* etc.[^31]

**285.** Making rules for himself, the Gosani lived there;
and this became his unchanging rule.

**286.** Who can write of Raghunatha's countless virtues?
Raghunatha's rules were like a line carved in stone.

**287.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Sva-niyama-dashaka*, verse 1:
*"For the guru, the mantra, the name, the feet of the best of lords born from Shachi's womb, for Svarupa, for Sri Rupa with his companions and his elder brother, for the king of hills (Govardhana), the lake of Gandharva (Radha-kunda), Madhu-puri (Mathura), the forests of Vraja, Vraja itself, the devotees and the dwellers of the cowherd villages — may I have supreme attachment."*[^32]

**288.** Its meaning:
"Sri Guru, the mantra and the name of Krishna,
the most rasa-filled form of Chaitanya, the abode of virtues,

**289.** Svarupa Gosani and Sri Rupa Gosani
with their companions, and his elder brother,

**290.** the king of hills and the lake of Gandharvi,
the region of Sri Mathura and the land of Vrindavana,

**291.** Sri Vraja-mandala and the devotees of Vraja —
for all these is my supreme faith and attachment."

**292.** Keep all these words deep in your heart:
whoever is without this belongs to another teaching.

**293.** This parakiya-lila is most, most profound:
it is inaccessible to unfortunate people.

**294.** If there is a longing to attain this bhava,
serve your own Prabhu, keeping the rules.

**295.** Sri Kaviraja Gosani, knowing the essence,
described it clearly in his book:

**296.** "In the bhava of parakiya is the highest extract of rasa;
apart from Vraja it is found nowhere."[^33]

**297.** This parakiya-lila is approved by Rupa —
this truth, brother, I have told you with certainty.

**298.** Which lila Mahaprabhu relished
is known only to Svarupa and the others (His intimates).

**299.** Prabhu always longed for the rasa of parakiya —
otherwise why did His heart rejoice at an ordinary verse?

**300.** Of this in the *Chaitanya-charitamrita*, Madhya-khanda, chapter 1:
*"He who stole my maidenhood is the same who is now my husband; these are the same spring nights, the same fragrance of blossoming malati and the mature breezes from the kadambas; and I am the same — yet my heart longs for the love-play there, under the vetasi tree on the bank of the Reva."*[^34]

**301.** In the dance (Prabhu) recites this verse again and again;
apart from Svarupa no one understands its meaning.

**302.** By providence Sri Rupa Gosani came to Nilachala;
hearing the verse, he at once grasped its intent.

**303.** Sri Rupa understood Prabhu's deep bhava
and, knowing Prabhu's heart, wrote a verse.

**304.** (Its sense:) "See, once, in youth,
the Two sported joyfully in the vetasi grove.

**305.** By the will of fate They were married,
but from the marriage that happiness did not arise.

**306.** After the marriage They met again,
but did not taste the former happiness in it.

**307.** Before, the Two had the special bhava of parakiya" —
that is why Prabhu was transported by this verse.[^35]

**308.** No one knows the inner thoughts of Mahaprabhu:
Sri Rupa Gosvami, knowing them, revealed them.

**309.** Of this in the *Chaitanya-charitamrita*, Madhya-khanda, chapter 1 — a verse of Sri Rupa:
*"Friend, this is the same beloved Krishna, met at Kurukshetra, and I am the same Radha, and this is the same happiness of our meeting; yet my mind longs for the forest on the bank of the Kalindi, where the fifth note of the sweet flute plays in the depths (of the grove)."*[^36]

**310.** "You are the same, I am the same, and the meeting new —
and yet Vrindavana steals my mind.

**311.** Of the happiness I tasted with You in Vrindavana
there is not a particle of that sweetness here.

**312.** The same Radha, the same Krishna, the same Vrindavana —
every moment I long for a speedy meeting there."

**313.** Without Vrindavana there is no bhava of parakiya:
if the meeting is elsewhere, there is not that happiness.

**314.** Therefore this bhava dwells only in Vraja:
the abode of Vrindavana is infinitely dear to Krishna."

**315.** When Ramachandra had said all this,
the joy in the king's heart increased.

**316.** The king humbly says to Ramachandra:
"Which abode is the best? Tell me in detail.

**317.** Among the countless millions of universes, which abode is the best?
In which abode does Krishna always reside?

**318.** Tell me all this, sir."
Then Ramachandra, filled with kindness, says:

**319.** "In the *Varaha*[^37] it is said:
*'In the countless millions of universes, arising from the countless (expansions) of the three gunas, Brahma, Vishnu and Maheshvara are fractions of millions of millions of parts of His part.'*

**320.** The Supreme Lord is Krishna, Bhagavan Himself,
the source of all avataras, the chief cause of all causes;

**321.** in the countless Vaikunthas are His countless avataras,
He is the support of all the countless universes.

**322.** Vrajendra-nandana, whose body is being, consciousness and bliss,
possesses all majesty, all powers, and is wholly complete.

**323.** In the *Brahma-samhita* it is said:
*'The Supreme Lord is Krishna, His form is being, consciousness and bliss; without beginning, He is the beginning of all, Govinda, the cause of all causes.'*[^38]

**324.** In Vrindavana He is the transcendental, ever-youthful Madana,
who is worshipped with the kama-gayatri and the kama-bija;

**325.** men, women, the unmoving and the moving —
He attracts the hearts of all; He is the Enchanter of Cupid himself.

**326.** Whoever worships Him with this pure bhava
easily attains Vrajendra-nandana.[^39]

**327.** In the *Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu*, Eastern Division, verse 1:
*'Victory to the Moon, the Beloved of Radha, the embodiment of the nectar of all rasas, whose spreading radiance has eclipsed the star-gopis Taraka and Pali, and who has captivated Shyama and Lalita!'*[^40]

**328.** And in the *Varaha* it is said:
*'Imperishable, eternal, blissful, unchanging is the abode of Govinda, not different from Govinda's body, complete — the shelter of the happiness of Brahman. Where the supreme majesty of Brahman eternally resides in Vrindavana — that, O goddess, is the region of Mathura, and within it especially Vrindavana. More secret than the secret, lovely in its midst lies Vrindavana: the fullness of the happiness and majesty of Brahman, eternal, unchanging bliss. Vaikuntha and the other (abodes) are parts of its parts; Vrindavana itself is on earth.'*[^41]

**329.** By the word "Brahman" I mean Sri Krishna, Bhagavan Himself,
possessor of all opulence, whose eternal abode is Goloka,

**330.** whose bliss is eternal, imperishable, unchanging,
who is full of the six opulences and surrounded by His associates.

**331.** Krishna Himself — and the abode itself; it cannot be otherwise:
Vrindavana itself is on earth — what doubt is there?

**332.** Vaikuntha and the other abodes are its parts;
Vrindavana itself on earth is the crown of all.

**333.** By the word "Goloka" I mean the town of Gokula:
Gokula is called Goloka — so it has been stated in detail.

**334.** The other Goloka is the splendour (vaibhava) of Gokula;
hear the evidence of this.

**335.** In the *Laghu-bhagavatamrita*, in the chapter on the abodes, verse 72:
*'That which is called Goloka is the splendour of Gokula.'*[^42]"

**336.** The king asks: "What are called the six opulences?"
Then Ramachandra gives him the evidence:

**337.** "In the *Bhagavatamrita* it is said:
*'Manifold wondrous sweetness, depth, majesty, might, generosity and steadfastness — these are called the six opulences.'*

**338.** Manifold is His wondrous sweetness and depth;
there is no end to His might, majesty, generosity and steadfastness.

**339.** And it is said:
*'Fullness of majesty, might, fame, beauty, knowledge and renunciation — these six are called "bhaga".'*[^43]

**340.** Fullness of majesty and fullness of might,
fullness of fame, beauty, knowledge and renunciation — certainly."

**341.** Again the king says to Sri Ramachandra:
"Tell me lovingly about this:

**342.** I wish to hear of the greatness of the Ganga and the Yamuna —
which of them has greater virtue? Tell me for certain.

**343.** I have heard that Krishna is worshipped by all;
now I wish to hear of the greatness of Sri Radhika.

**344.** Krishna's svakiya-lila and parakiya —
tell me about all this in detail."

**345.** Hearing this, Ramachandra, with joy in his heart,
began to tell of it in detail:

**346.** "Listen, O king, you have asked a great question:
this is a most holy and pure account.

**347.** However much the greatness of the Ganga is praised in the scriptures,
the fame of the Yamuna is ten million times greater.

**348.** This is known from the scriptures, and not otherwise:
it is certainly in the words of the Puranas.

**349.** Both banks of the Yamuna are lovely:
laid with pure gold, with precious rubies.

**350.** A mere touch of such a Yamuna
equals ten million Gangas in merit — so I tell you.

**351.** What more shall I say, brother, of the greatness of the Yamuna,
in which the son of the king of Vraja eternally sports!

**352.** Of this:
*'There both banks are lovely, made of pure gold; and even a drop that has touched her is declared ten million times superior to the Ganga.'*[^44]

**353.** And now hear of the greatness of Sri Radha,
to which Krishna Himself finds no limit.

**354.** Sri Radha is a mine of jewel-like virtues,
whose greatness all the scriptures extol.

**355.** Krishna finds no shore to the ocean of Sri Radha's virtues;
what can I, senseless and worthless, say of Her virtues?

**356.** To all the goddesses in the countless millions of universes
She is the ornament of the head.

**357.** In the *Brihad-Gautamiya-tantra* — in the *Charitamrita*, Adi-khanda, chapter 4:
*"The goddess Radhika is declared to be full of Krishna, the supreme deity, containing all the Lakshmis, all splendour, the supreme Enchantress."*[^45]

**358.** Krishna's beloveds, it is seen, are of three kinds:
some are the Lakshmis, others the queens,

**359.** and (the third) the beauties of Vraja, the essence of all beloveds;
all the beloveds expand from Sri Radha.

**360.** As Krishna, descending, manifests the avataras,
so from Radha, the source of parts, the three groups expand.

**361.** The Lakshmis are Her partial vaibhava-vilasa manifestations,
the queens Her vaibhava-prakasha manifestations.

**362.** The maidens of Vraja, differing in form and nature,
are Her kaya-vyuhas (bodily expansions), the cause of rasa.

**363.** Without many beloveds there is no jubilation of rasa:
for the help of the lila there are many manifestations.

**364.** "Devi" means the shining one, the supreme beauty,
or the place of Krishna's play and worship, the city where He dwells.

**365.** Or: prema full of rasa is Krishna's very nature;
His shakti is one with Him.

**366.** She worships Krishna by fulfilling His desires;
therefore the Puranas extol Her as "Radhika".

**367.** In the Tenth Canto, chapter 30, verse 23:
*"Surely by Her the Lord Hari, Bhagavan, has been worshipped, since Govinda, pleased, left us and took Her to a secluded place."*[^46]

**368.** Therefore She is worshipped by all, the supreme deity,
the protectress of all, the mother of the whole world.

**369.** "All the Lakshmis" — I have already spoken of this:
all the Lakshmis exist by virtue of Her.

**370.** Or: the word "kanti" means all of Krishna's desires:
all Krishna's desires rest in Radha;

**371.** Radhika fulfils Krishna's desires —
this is the meaning of the words "all splendour".

**372.** Krishna enchants the world — and She enchants Him;
therefore the Thakurani is the highest of all.

**373.** As Krishna is the original Person, Bhagavan Himself,
so Radha is the source of all the shaktis; this is the evidence of scripture.

**374.** Such is Radha, Krishna's beloved, the limit of virtues,
whose virtues shine unceasingly in Krishna's heart.

**375.** Durga and the other (energies) of the three gunas are fractions of millions of Her parts;
Radha, the beloved of Sri Krishna, is the crown of all.

**376.** In the *Varaha* it is said:
*"His beloved is the original Prakriti, Radhika, His dear one; Durga and the others, consisting of the three gunas, are fractions of millions of millions of Her parts."*[^47]

**377.** The crown of all bhavas is mahabhava;
all the other bhavas rest on that bhava.

**378.** She in whose body such mahabhava dwells —
a bhava that never dwells in any other abode;

**379.** whose heart, senses and mind are permeated with mahabhava,
in whose heart Krishna always shines;

**380.** Krishnamayi — Krishna is within Her and without:
wherever Her glance falls, there Krishna shines.

**381.** Sri Radha Thakurani is the very embodiment of mahabhava,
a mine of all virtues, the crown of Krishna's beloveds.

**382.** In svakiya mahabhava never resides:
its eternal dwelling is in the bhava of parakiya.[^48]

**383.** This bhava of parakiya dwells in Vrindavana,
where the jubilation of rasa rises unceasingly.

**384.** Of the embodiment of mahabhava Sri Dasa Gosani
wrote there, in the *Premambhoja-maranda*:

**385.** in the hymn *Premambhoja-maranda*:
*"(Radha,) whose form is manifested from the shining chintamani-gem of mahabhava, radiant with the excellent unguents of the fragrance of Her friends' love."*[^49]

**386.** Beginning with this, in all his verses
the Gosani described this bhava, each one separately.

**387.** The essence of hladini is prema; the essence of prema is bhava;
the summit of bhava is mahabhava.

**388.** In the *Ujjvala-nilamani*, in the chapter on Radha, verse 2:
*"She is the embodiment of mahabhava, surpassing all in virtues."*[^50]

**389.** Her body is the very nature of prema, permeated with prema;
the best of Sri Krishna's beloveds — known to the world.

**390.** In the *Brahma-samhita* it is said:
*"I worship the original Person Govinda, who, together with those who are permeated with the rasa of bliss and consciousness — His own forms, His parts — dwells in Goloka, being the Soul of all."*[^51]

**391.** The chintamani-gem of mahabhava is Radha's very nature;
Lalita and the other sakhis are Her kaya-vyuhas.

**392.** Krishna's affection for Radha is a fragrant unguent,
by which Her body is most fragrant and radiant in colour.

**393.** Her first bath is in the stream of the nectar of compassion,
the middle bath in the stream of the nectar of youth,

**394.** and after that a bath in the stream of the nectar of loveliness;
Her bashfulness is the dark silk sari She wears;

**395.** love for Krishna is Her second, red garment;
love with pique is the bodice covering Her breast;

**396.** beauty is kunkuma, Her friends' love sandal,
the radiance of Her smile camphor: with these three Her body is anointed.[^52]

**397.** Krishna's radiant rasa is abundant musk:
with that musk Her body is painted.

**398.** Hidden pique and contrariness are Her braid,
the quality of being dhira and adhira the silk veil on Her body;

**399.** raga is red betel, by which Her lips shine,
the crookedness of prema the collyrium on both Her eyes;

**400.** the blazing sattvika bhavas, jealousy and the other sanchari bhavas —
with these bhava-ornaments Radha's body is filled.

**401.** She is adorned with twenty bhavas — kilakinchita and the rest,
a garland of flowers — the rows of Her virtues — covers Her whole body.

**402.** The tilaka of beauty shines on Her lovely forehead,
prema-vaichittya, a jewelled necklace, trembles on Her breast.

**403.** The state of middle youth is Her hand resting on a friend's shoulder,
the sakhis around Her are thoughts turned to Krishna's pastimes.

**404.** In the house of Her body's fragrance is the couch of pride:
seated on it, She always thinks of Krishna's company.

**405.** Krishna's name, qualities and fame are the earrings in Her ears,
Krishna's name, qualities and fame the stream of Her words.

**406.** She makes Krishna drink the honey of the rasa of Shyama,
unceasingly fulfilling all of Krishna's desires.

**407.** Satyabhama longs for Her good fortune,
from Her the beauties of Vraja learn the arts and play;

**408.** Lakshmi and Parvati long for Her beauty,
Arundhati longs for Her fidelity;

**409.** Krishna finds no end to Her excellent virtues —
what insignificant being could count them?[^53]

**410.** Of this:
*"The crowning ornament-manjari on the body of the totality of good fortune, She shines among the doe-eyed beauties up to Vaikuntha; She is the one ocean of the nectar of bliss, the one city of the nectar of cleverness, the one creeper of the nectar of sweetness, the one casket of jewel-like virtues."*[^54]

**411.** The Creator fashioned the one ocean of the nectar of bliss,
made Radha a city of cleverness;

**412.** did not the Creator fashion the one creeper of sweetness,
did He not make the one casket of jewel-like virtues?

**413.** Whoever does not worship the dust of Radha's lotus feet
will never know the rasa of supreme sweetness.

**414.** Vrindavana is the land marked by Sri Radha's footprints:
whoever has not taken shelter there will not obtain the treasure.

**415.** Whoever does not converse with the sadhus
whose hearts are deep in Radha's bhava —

**416.** he will never plunge into the ocean of Shyama:
this I have said for certain, there is no doubt.

**417.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Sankalpa-prakasha-stotra*, verse 1:
*"Without worshipping the dust of Radha's lotus feet, without taking shelter of Vrindavana marked by Her feet, without conversing with those whose hearts are deep in Her bhava — how can one plunge into the rasa of the ocean of Shyama?"*[^55]

**418.** Throughout the universe the enchanting name of Radha
always and unceasingly shines.

**419.** Radha's virtues are those in the Agamas and Nigamas;
Narada and the other sages chant Her name.

**420.** Whoever, neglecting the lotus feet of such a Radha,
constantly desires to worship Govinda,

**421.** whoever does not worship Radha but seeks only Krishna —
he is a great hypocrite, proud, most foolish.

**422.** May I never live near him;
know for certain: that is a fallen place.

**423.** May I never dwell in such a place,
may my mind never for a moment turn to it.

**424.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Sva-niyama-dashaka*, verse 6:
*"Whoever, neglecting Gandharva, sung by the sages headed by the vina-player (Narada), the expert one, called by the Vedas the dearest to Him, hypocritically worships Govinda alone — to his wretched dwelling I shall not go even for a moment: this is my vow."*[^56]

**425.** In the universe shines the fame of Radha's name;
in the hearts of the sadhus it always shines.

**426.** Whoever has surely moistened his heart (by association) with Radha's people
and firmly worships Krishna together with Radha —

**427.** to him I bow with prema;
this is my constant, unceasing desire.

**428.** Washing his two lotus feet,
I drink (that water) and then hold it on my head.

**429.** This is my daily, invariable rule;
may Radha's companions be merciful to me!

**430.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Sva-niyama-dashaka*, verse 7:
*"Whoever in the universe, moistened by the shining name 'Radha', bowed with prema, worships Krishna together with Her — washing his lotus feet and joyfully drinking that water, I daily and invariably carry it on my head."*[^57]

**431.** Having settled all this, Sri Dasa Gosani
made rules for himself and settled there, on the bank of the kunda.

**432.** With him were Krishnadasa and Lokanatha Gosani;
day and night — unceasing talk of Krishna.

**433.** At that time the book called *Gopala-champu*
they all relished together unceasingly.

**434.** Relishing it, they rejoiced greatly at heart;
but very hard to grasp is the intent of its verses:

**435.** by the outward meaning it is understood as svakiya,
but the inner meaning is parakiya alone.

**436.** Not understanding Sri Jiva's deep heart,
outsiders explain it as svakiya;

**437.** but the inner meaning of the book points to parakiya.
They were all immersed in bliss, relishing this.[^58]

**438.** The parakiya-lila — this place is Vrindavana;
leaving it, (Raghunatha) went to no other abode.

**439.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Sva-niyama-dashaka*, verse 2:
*"Nor to any other holy place, even one graced by the form of Hari, (shall I go)…"* etc.[^59]

**440.** "This Vrindavana is my sadhana and bhajana;
to leave my body in this place is my rule.

**441.** Milk born in Vraja is my food,
the leaves of the trees of Vraja are my clothing.

**442.** By these I shall live, giving up pride,
staying at Sri Kunda or at Mount Govardhana.

**443.** By the lake of Radha's prema, surely here
I wish to die: such is my desire.

**444.** May Sri Jiva be before me,
and Sri Krishnadasa and Lokanatha Gosani:

**445.** before them all I shall leave my body —
when, to my great fortune, will such a state come to me?"

**446.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Sva-niyama-dashaka*, verse 9:
*"Sustaining my life without pride, by rule, with things born in Vraja — milk for food, clothing, vessels and the rest — I live at the kunda of the Mistress and by the best of hills; and in due time I shall die by the dear lake, before Jiva and the others."*[^60]

**447.** Kaviraja Gosani, knowing the essence of the *Champu*,
wrote in his book of the establishment of the eternal lila.

**448.** The book named *Sri Gopala-champu* is a mighty hero:
in it is established the eternal lila, full of the rasa of Vraja.

**449.** By the words "full of rasa" I mean the eternal parakiya;
hold this carefully in your heart.

**450.** Whoever knows this rasa-lila as eternal, eternal,
attains the pure Vrajendra-nandana.

**451.** Whatever lila (is manifest), that is eternal — it is not otherwise:
manifestation and non-manifestation are only the arrangement of the lila.

**452.** Krishna, independent, plays unceasingly;
manifesting the lila, He showed the eternal lila in it.

**453.** Of this:
*"In the manifest and unmanifest (lila) it is eternally the same, in the forest and in the cowherd village: herding cows with friends — but without the slaying of asuras."*[^61]

**454.** Herding cows with friends and the other pastimes —
only in the eternal lila there is no slaying of asuras.

**455.** Between the eternal lila and (the manifest) lila this alone is the difference;
I have told you this — it is most holy.

**456.** I have explained the rasa of the eternal lila and the rest:
in it the mother, father and friends abide eternally.

**457.** There is an example of this — listen, O king;
I shall give the evidence, the word of scripture.

**458.** When Krishna came to Vrindavana from another abode,
He always sported with His eternal associates.

**459.** Sending Drona, Dhara and the others to Vaikuntha,
He always sports, filled with joy.

**460.** Drona, Dhara and the others, partial manifestations of Nanda and the rest,
appeared, coming into the crown of Vraja;

**461.** but with the best of the best, these (eternal) friends,
the eternal lila shines and abides in Vraja.

**462.** I have told you the essence of this eternal lila;
even Ananta cannot tell it in full.

**463.** I, worthless and low, cannot grasp the lila —
what can I say of that lila, highest of the high?

**464.** Of this in the *Laghu-bhagavatamrita*, in the section on the manifest and unmanifest lila, verses 61–62:
*"Drona and the others, who descended as parts of the lord of Vraja and the rest, Krishna has now sent to Vaikuntha; but with the residents of Gokula, dearer than the dearest, Hari always sports in Vrindavana."*[^62]

**465.** All these limbs of sadhana I have told in essence;
who could tell them in full?

**466.** The eternal lila and the rest, manifold,
the truth of Krishna, the truth of Radha and the truth of the lila,

**467.** ashraya and alambana, uddipana and the rest,
the distinctions of rati, and among them samartha — above all —

**468.** all the conclusions (siddhantas) that were in the conversation with Ramananda Raya
he told the king fully and in detail;

**469.** all the conclusions that (the Lord) told Sri Sanatana
he told the king one after another.[^63]

**470.** Then the king, bowing to Ramachandra,
began to speak humbly.

**471.** Having received the instruction, the maharaja rejoiced at heart
and very softly began to say something.

**472.** This narrative of the *Karnananda* is an extract of nectar:
at its touch on the ear, the joy of prema is born in the devotees.

**473.** The daughter of Sri Acharya Prabhu is Srila Hemalata:
has not the Creator fashioned a wish-fulfilling creeper of prema?

**474.** Her two lotus feet sport in my heart;
Yadunandana Dasa tells the rasa of the *Karnananda*.

*Thus ends the fourth niryasa of the* Sri Karnananda, *called "Sri Ramachandra Kaviraja's Instruction to Maharaja Sri Vira Hambira".*
