# Niryasa 7. The Question of the Passing of Krishnadasa Kaviraja and Raghunatha Dasa

*The author asks Hemalata: the* Prema-vilasa *says that the Kaviraja died of grief at the news of the theft of the books, and Raghunatha Dasa even earlier, whereas the Kaviraja himself writes that Raghunatha is alive; Hemalata relates Srinivasa's answer to Ramachandra: Raghunatha, bound by his vow, could not depart before his time, while the Kaviraja, hearing the command of Rupa and Sanatana, returned to his body*

**1.** Glory, glory to Mahaprabhu, the deliverance of the fallen!
Glory, glory to Nityananda, the treasury of compassion!

**2.** Glory, glory to the Lord of Sita, Ishvara Advaita!
Glory, glory to Prabhu's associates headed by Srivasa!

**3.** Glory, glory to Sri Svarupa Gosani Damodara!
Glory, glory to Sri Ramananda, the mine of rasa!

**4.** Glory to Rupa and Sanatana, purifiers of the fallen!
Glory, glory to the feet of Sri Gopala Bhatta!

**5.** Glory to Raghunatha Bhatta and Sri Dasa Gosani!
Glory, glory for ever to Sri Jiva Gosani!

**6.** Glory to Sri Acharya Prabhu, the ocean of mercy!
Glory, glory to Ramachandra together with his brother!

**7.** Glory to the Vaishnava Gosanis, purifiers of the fallen!
With a straw between my teeth I beg: give me this wealth —

**8.** with longing to attain the feet of Sri Acharya Prabhu;
out of mercy fulfil this desire of mine.

**9.** Listen, listen, devotees, to my request,
hear a most holy account.

**10.** Having heard the book, Prabhu[^1] was pleased
and, with a softened heart, showed much mercy:

**11.** "Listen, listen, son, I tell you:
in your heart is the shining of my Prabhu's feet."

**12.** Then I, clasping Srimati's two feet,
bowed many times, rolling on the ground:

**13.** "Listen, listen, Prabhu, be merciful to me:
there is a great doubt in my heart.

**14.** If out of mercy you dispel the doubt…"
The words of her holy lips soothe the ears.

**15.** Prabhu says: "Tell me what doubt — I shall listen."
Then I spoke these words to Prabhu's feet:

**16.** "At Jahnava's command Nityananda Dasa
composed the *Prema-vilasa* on Prabhu's life.[^2]

**17.** When Prabhu came to Gauda with the books,
there he wrote these words in particular:

**18.** that the Kaviraja, hearing the news of the theft of the books,
leapt up, went and fell into the Kunda;[^3]

**19.** most detached at heart, he could not contain himself:
'Alas, alas, such grief cannot be borne!'

**20.** And Sri Dasa Gosvami had left his body even earlier.
Hearing this, doubt arose in my heart.

**21.** Srila Kaviraja Gosani in his own book
wrote in the summaries — I have copied them one by one:

**22.** 'May Sri Raghunatha Dasa abide' — so he wrote.[^4]
With great doubt I submitted to your feet:

**23.** Raghunatha disappeared before the Kaviraja —
yet in the summary the great one wrote this word;

**24.** did the Kaviraja depart before Raghunatha?
'When will the Gosani come before my eyes?' —

**25.** such words the Gosani wrote more than once.
The doubt in my heart grew beyond measure;

**26.** with great doubt I submitted to your feet:
out of mercy, Prabhu, dispel the doubt."

**27.** Hearing this, the Thakurani rejoiced greatly at heart
and began to speak sweetly:

**28.** "Listen, son: I heard earlier from Prabhu's[^5] lips —
Ramachandra asked Prabhu about this very thing,

**29.** and the answer Prabhu gave him
made Ramachandra very happy.

**30.** Being nearby, I heard those words;
all those words I shall tell you in full.

**31.** Prabhu says: 'Ramachandra, hear the word:
I shall tell you something wondrous — listen.

**32.** Who could write down the endless virtues of Raghunatha?
Raghunatha's rules are like a line on stone.

**33.** The Gosvami's vow is most firm and certain:
whatever vow he made cannot be broken.

**34.** In separation from Sri Rupa the Gosani was afflicted at heart;
almost blind, he stayed on the bank of Radha-kunda.

**35.** In great separation the Gosani was afflicted at heart,
thinking unceasingly how to leave his body.

**36.** At that time the news of the theft of the books arrived:
in great grief (the Kaviraja) rose, weeping:[^6]

**37.** "Alas, alas, what has happened — what a great calamity!" —
so he repeated in grief again and again.

**38.** Then that Gosvami could not hold himself;
holding in his heart the lotus feet of Raghunatha,

**39.** he was as if attaining his siddha-deha[^7] —
and the grief in Dasa Gosvami's heart knew no bounds.

**40.** Thus among all the residents of Radha-kunda
the grief in their hearts greatly increased.

**41.** Then the two brothers, Rupa and Sanatana,
were greatly troubled at heart.

**42.** Knowing the firmness of Raghunatha's vow,
the two Gosvamis speak, calling the Kaviraja:

**43.** "For this purpose is the writing of Prabhu, the Jagad-guru:[^8]
to entrust to Srinivasa the great treasure of the books.

**44.** For this purpose Chaitanya Gosani revealed the books,
infusing shakti into me;

**45.** to distribute them in Gauda — shakti in Srinivasa:
this is the reason, this is Mahaprabhu's command.

**46.** The command of Prabhu, the crown of the all-knowing, is stronger:
who has the power to act otherwise?

**47.** Why do you leave your body in vain from grief?
The books will be found — this I tell you.

**48.** Serve Raghunatha for some days more;
afterwards you will come again into my group."

**49.** Drinking the nectar of the two brothers' command,
the Kaviraja again regained consciousness in his body.

**50.** All the gods in the sky gave their command (by a heavenly voice);
seeing the Kaviraja's return (to life), all are deep in thought:

**51.** "How could Raghunatha's vow be broken?" —
all together pondered this unceasingly.

**52.** The Gosvami's writing is like a line on stone:
who has the power to refute it?

**53.** Of this in the *Stavavali*, in the *Sva-niyama-dashaka*, verse 9:
*"Sustaining my life without pride, by rule, with things born in Vraja — milk for food, clothing, vessels and the rest — I live at the kunda of the Mistress and by the best of hills; and in due time I shall die by the dear lake, before Jiva and the others"* etc.[^9]

**54.** "Milk born in Vraja is my food, the leaves of Vraja's trees my clothing;
by these I shall live, giving up pride.

**55.** At Sri Kunda or at Mount Govardhana —
I shall surely die on the bank of Radha-kunda.

**56.** This is a most firm rule, most difficult:
Sri Jiva will be before me,

**57.** and Sri Krishnadasa and Gosani Lokanatha."
Knowing this, suddenly a heavenly voice resounded;

**58.** all heard it with their own ears:
"Listen, listen, Kaviraja, I tell you:
soon you will receive the news that the books have been found."

[^1]: Hemalata.
[^2]: That is, Srinivasa's life; cf. 6.120–121.
[^3]: *Prema-vilasa* 13: Krishnadasa Kaviraja, hearing of the theft of the books, died of grief at Radha-kunda (cf. ev-krishnadasa-kaviraja-departure).
[^4]: The opening blessing in the CC (the summaries, suchakas, of the chapters, e.g. Antya 1) — *bhūyāt śrī-raghunātha-dāsaḥ* ("may Raghunatha Dasa abide"): so when the Kaviraja was completing the CC, Raghunatha was alive. The author's doubt is a real contradiction between the *Prema-vilasa* and the CC.
[^5]: Srinivasa.
[^6]: The subject is not named; from the sense of what follows (38–49), it is Krishnadasa Kaviraja.
[^7]: That is, left his body. This account is an attempt to reconcile the *Prema-vilasa* with the CC: the Kaviraja supposedly died of grief, but at the command of Rupa and Sanatana returned to his body in order to serve Raghunatha. This reconciliation is found only in the *Karnananda*.
[^8]: That is, Mahaprabhu. Rupa and Sanatana speak here from the eternal abode, appearing to the Kaviraja.
[^9]: Raghunatha Dasa's *Sva-niyama-dashaka*, v. 9 (cf. 4.446). Raghunatha's vow was to die before the eyes of Jiva, Krishnadasa and Lokanatha; therefore Krishnadasa could not depart before him.
