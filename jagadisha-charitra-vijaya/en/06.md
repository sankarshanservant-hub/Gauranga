# Varna 6. The Description of Sri Jagadisha's Coming to Sri Navadvipa

*Grown old, Kamalaksha and Bhagyavati ask their son to show them the way to Krishna's feet; Jagadisha instructs them in hearing and chanting according to the "Bhagavata", reads the "Bhagavata" to them, and they depart for the abode of Vishnu; Duhkhini is consoled; the shraddha in a tulasi grove before the shalagrama-shila, pindas of maha-prasada and the feeding of Vaishnava brahmanas; grieving for people turned away from Krishna, Jagadisha resolves to live on the bank of the Ganga; Duhkhini's consent, the farewell to the neighbours, Mahesha goes with them; on the seventh day of the journey they reach the Ganga, and Jagadisha settles in Navadvipa, where the Lord is to appear; the author's signature*

*The sixth varna*

**1.** Countless are your wondrous deeds, Prabhu;
they are known among the saintly.

**2.** Having heard all those accounts from the mouths of the saintly,
for my own purification I write down a little with great joy.

**3.** Thus Jagadisha performs various pastimes;
seeing [this], Kamalaksha floats in an ocean of bliss.

**4.** One day Bhagyavati says to her husband:
"Hear my words, O Bandya of great mind.

**5.** My son Jagadisha is full of every virtue;
so too the daughter-in-law is an abode of virtues.

**6.** The Creator has given [us] a son and a daughter-in-law after our heart;
this has become the very limit of worldly happiness.

**7.** Old age has come upon you and me;
now consider how we may cross over [the ocean of] existence.

**8.** The mind is always sunk in worldly pleasures;
not even once does remembrance of Krishna's feet arise.

**9.** Without remembrance of Sri Krishna the time passes in vain;
how will remembrance of Krishna come — what means shall we take?"

**10.** Hearing this, Kamalaksha was greatly pleased
and began to say this to Bhagyavati:

**11.** "Listen, dear one: blessed, blessed is your life —
you have made me remember Sri Krishna.

**12.** I too think within myself all the time:
how can the mind be bound to Sri Krishna's feet?

**13.** Come, let us both go and ask Jagadisha
by what way the feet of Sri Krishna are attained."

**14.** Having resolved thus, the two, in great joy,
went to Jagadisha and began to speak:

**15.** "Listen, my son: we have both grown old;
how may we now attain the feet of Sri Krishna?

**16.** Tell us both the means:
we both shall act according to it."

**17.** Hearing this, Jagadisha was delighted
and, becoming attentive, began to speak thus:

**18.** "Listen, father and mother, to my words:
there are endless practices of sadhana for attaining Krishna.

**19.** But all of them are extremely difficult:
in the Kali-yuga man is unable to accomplish them.

**20.** There is one sadhana that is supremely easy,
by which the feet of Sri Krishna are attained with ease.

**21.** When in the Dvapara-yuga Sri Krishna descended,
Brahma came to Dvaraka and offered Him praise.

**22.** In that praise there is one verse;
to that verse people pay no attention.

**23.** Whoever performs sadhana according to that verse
easily crosses over [the ocean of] existence and attains the feet of Sri Krishna.

*Tatha hi, in the Srimad Bhagavata, in the Eleventh Canto, the words of Sri Brahma to Sri Krishna:*

**24.** *Your deeds, O Lord — the saintly people in the Kali-yuga,
hearing and chanting them, will easily cross beyond darkness.*[^1]

**25.** Brahma says: 'Listen, Krishna, I make a humble submission:
Your descent is for the deliverance of living beings.

**26.** The deeds You have performed after descending —
by constantly hearing and chanting them,

**27.** all the saintly people in the Kali-yuga will without effort
cross over [the ocean of] existence and attain Your feet.'

**28.** Brahma has declared this supreme sadhana;
practise it, both of you, and you will attain the feet of Sri Krishna.

**29.** I submit something more; listen, father and mother:
again the "Bhagavata" speaks of sadhana.

*Tatha hi, in the same place, in the Second Canto:*

**30.** *Whoever constantly hears with faith and chants His deeds —
into his heart, in no long time, Bhagavan enters.*[^2]

**31.** Whoever hears with faith of Sri Krishna's deeds
and, being diligent, performs the sankirtana of the Name —

**32.** into his heart in a short time
Bhagavan enters; of this there is no doubt."

**33.** Jagadisha says: "This the 'Bhagavata' declares
to be the supreme means of sadhana in the Kali-yuga.

**34.** You too, with firm faith in these words,
hear of Sri Krishna's deeds and say 'Hari, Hari!'

**35.** With the mood of bhakti always hear and chant,
and without effort you both will attain the feet of Sri Krishna."

**36.** Hearing this, his father and mother said to him:
"Tell us of Sri Krishna."

**37.** Then Prabhu Jagadisha [began] to recite to them both
the Srimad Bhagavata, explaining [it] in many ways.

**38.** Thus, having heard all the verses,
both, their life fulfilled, departed for the abode of Vishnu.

**39.** In separation from his father and mother the Pandita Thakura,
losing all composure, grieved greatly.

**40.** At home Duhkhini Devi weeps aloud;
who has the power to describe that lament?

**41.** After some time the Prabhu regained his composure
and began to speak to Duhkhini Devi:

**42.** "Listen, dear one: this world is fashioned by maya;
know for certain that everything in it is perishable.

**43.** By Krishna's maya the birth, life and dissolution of living beings
come about in the course of time, according to each one's own karma.

**44.** Thus the living being continually comes and goes in this world
as long as it does not obtain the company of Krishna's devotees.

**45.** By the mercy of Krishna's devotee comes bhakti for Krishna;
when there is bhakti for Krishna, all misfortune is dispelled.

**46.** Consider, dear one, in your own heart:
who else is as merciful as Krishna?

**47.** If one utters His name on any pretext whatever,
even a great sinner is liberated — so say the Puranas.

**48.** Ajamila is the witness to this: his own son
he called, saying 'Narayana!', at the time of dying.

**49.** By that he attained the abode of Vishnu.
Who can say how great is the fruit of Krishna's name?

*Tatha hi, in the Srimad Bhagavata:*

**50.** *Even Ajamila, dying, uttering the name of Hari addressed to his son,
attained the abode; how much more one who utters [it] with faith!*[^3]

**51.** My father and mother are associates of Krishna:
of their own will they took birth upon the earth.

**52.** In many ways the two worshipped Sri Krishna;
when their time was complete, they went to the abode of Sri Krishna.

**53.** Never grieve for them;
giving up grief, worship Sri Krishna.

**54.** Radha and Krishna are the original father and mother of all;
I have told [you] this essence of all truths.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Narada-pancharatra:*

**55.** *Sri Krishna is the father of the worlds, the mother of the world is Radhika.*[^4]

**56.** Thus Jagadisha consoled [her];
hearing [him], Duhkhini Devi became calm.

**57.** Then the Prabhu, gathering various provisions,
invited the brahmanas and worthy people.

**58.** The Prabhu considers within himself:
"I shall perform the shraddha and the other rites in a tulasi grove."

**59.** The shraddha should by all means be performed in a tulasi grove;
the injunction for this is in the Skanda Purana.

*Tatha hi, in the same, in the dialogue of Brahma and Narada:*

**60.** *Where the shade born of a tulasi grove falls, O twice-born,
there the shraddha should be offered for the satisfaction of the forefathers.*

**61.** *Where a multitude of tulasi seeds falls, O Narada,
a pinda offered there is an imperishable gift to the forefathers.*

**62.** On the eleventh day the Prabhu, in the tulasi grove,
with a joyful heart prepared the place for the shraddha.

**63.** Around it he arranged excellent seats
for the brahmanas and worthy people to sit.

**64.** The brahmanas and worthy people arrived,
and all sat down on the seats, each as befitted him.

**65.** Having performed according to rule the rites proper to the time,
the Prabhu came and sat down to perform the shraddha.

**66.** Having the shalagrama-shila brought there,
the Prabhu first worshipped it on behalf of the forefathers.

**67.** On behalf of the forefathers one should worship Sri Krishna;
the proof of this is the word of the Puranas.

*Tatha hi, in the same:*

**68.** *Men who have worshipped Keshava on behalf of the forefathers,
leaving behind the torments of hell, attain liberation, O great sage.*[^5]

**69.** *Blessed in the world are those men, especially in the age of Kali,
who daily worship Hari for the sake of the forefathers, O sage.*

**70.** *What [use] are many pindas, the shraddha at Gaya and the rest, O sage, [to those]
by whom Hari is worshipped with bhakti day by day for the sake of the forefathers?*

**71.** After this the Prabhu chose the family priest, the purohita,
and attentively honoured the brahmanas.

**72.** Then, in that place, brahmanas devoted to Vishnu
recite the Gita and the Srimad Bhagavata.

**73.** A circle of Vaishnavas with khols and karatalas
sings of Sri Krishna's pastimes, full of supreme rasa.

**74.** There Jagadisha gave various gifts
and began the shraddha near the shalagrama.

*Tatha hi, in the same, in the dialogue of Sri Shiva and Skanda:*

**75.** *The man who performs the shraddha near a shalagrama —
his forefathers remain in heaven, satisfied, for a hundred kalpas.*

**76.** *Forefathers once satisfied by a pinda before a shalagrama-shila
dwell [there], and there is no counting [that time].*

**77.** Having performed the shraddha rite, at the end he offered the pindas
of Sri maha-prasada, as there is proof for it.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Padma Purana:*

**78.** *With food offered to Vishnu one should honour the other gods,
and it should also be given to the forefathers — it yields an imperishable [fruit].*

**79.** In this way the Prabhu completed the shraddha rite;
as dakshina he gave gold coins to the priest.

**80.** Brahmanas marked with the sign of the conch and wearing the urdhva-pundra
the Prabhu feeds in every way.

**81.** Whoever brings home a Vaishnava brahmana whose body is marked with the conch
and feeds him —

**82.** his food Bhagavan Himself eats
together with the forefathers — so say the Puranas.

*Tatha hi, in the Brahma Purana, in the dialogue of Brahma and Narada:*

**83.** *In whose house a brahmana whose body is marked with the conch eats —
that food Keshava Himself eats together with the forefathers.*

**84.** Whoever at a shraddha feeds a brahmana wearing the urdhva-pundra,
with him the forefathers are pleased for millions of kalpas.

*Tatha hi, in the Padma Purana, in the dialogue of Shiva and Parvati:*

**85.** *Whoever at a shraddha feeds a brahmana wearing the urdhva-pundra, O dear one —
his forefathers are pleased for millions of kalpas; there is no doubt.*

**86.** Krishna says: "Brahmanas wearing the urdhva-pundra —
in whose house they eat food,

**87.** forty generations of his family from hell
I will surely deliver — know this for certain."

*Tatha hi, in the same, the words of Sri Bhagavan:*

**88.** *In whose house a brahmana wearing the urdhva-pundra eats food —
forty generations of his family I lift out of hell.*

**89.** His relatives, household and neighbours —
all of them Jagadisha, a treasury of virtues, fed.

**90.** To the brahmanas he gave cloth, money, metal vessels and so on;
with sweet words the Prabhu satisfied everyone.

**91.** After that Jagadisha held a mahotsava:
he fed the Vaishnavas with various dishes.

**92.** In this way Jagadisha, with great joy in his heart,
completed the shraddha rite with sankirtana of the Name.

**93.** I have described it only briefly, like a sutra;
may the devotees not hold this against [me] as an offence.

**94.** After this Jagadisha, together with Duhkhini,
lives at home, always engaged in bhajana of Krishna:

**95.** hearing and chanting Krishna's name, qualities and pastimes,
meditation on Krishna's lotus feet, worship and offering prayers.

**96.** In this way the Prabhu practises the limbs of bhakti,
and Duhkhini always serves the Prabhu.

**97.** But seeing all the people turned away from Krishna,
the Prabhu is extremely sorrowful at heart.

**98.** The Prabhu decided: "I will not remain in this land;
I shall find out what Duhkhini thinks."

**99.** One day, at night, in the bedchamber
the Prabhu is spending [time] playfully with Duhkhini.

**100.** At that time the Prabhu says: "Listen, Duhkhini:
my father and mother have gone to the other world.

**101.** None of my kinsfolk are here;
therefore I will by no means remain in this land.

**102.** Your father and mother are living;
I shall leave you in their care and go."

**103.** Hearing these words, the Devi says in sorrow:
"By my fate, Prabhu, you have given this command.

**104.** I have worshipped your lotus feet with great care —
why then have you still not accepted me?

**105.** What offence have I committed at [your] holy feet,
that you will leave me and go elsewhere?"

**106.** Having said this, the Devi fell silent in sorrow.
Hearing her, Prabhu Jagadisha began to speak:

**107.** "Hear my words; do not be downcast:
you and I are one soul, only the bodies are different.[^6]

**108.** Whoever sees a difference between you and me
will never be worthy of my mercy.

**109.** The wish has arisen in me not to stay in this land;
tell me plainly what wish is in your heart."

**110.** Duhkhini says: "Prabhu, I am not separate [from you]:
wherever you yourself keep [me], there I shall stay."

**111.** When Duhkhini had spoken these words,
Prabhu Jagadisha began to speak:

**112.** "Listen, dear one, I shall tell you the essence of this truth;
reflect on it within your mind.

**113.** After many births the human body is obtained;
whoever in it is intelligent worships Krishna.

**114.** One should now dwell in that place
by living in which one obtains bhakti for Krishna.

**115.** The Naradiya Purana says this:
one should by all means live on the bank of the Bhagirathi.

*Tatha hi, in the same:*

**116.** *What [use] is the eightfold yoga, what are austerities, what are sacrifices?
Living by the Ganga alone is the cause of the knowledge of Brahman.*[^7]

**117.** In the Kali-yuga sacrifice, yoga and austerity are nothing;
by merely living on the bank of the Ganga the knowledge of Brahman arises.

**118.** By the word "Brahman" I mean Krishna; knowledge of the truth about Him
comes from living by the Ganga — so say the Puranas.

**119.** The glory of the Ganga is that she is called the water that has washed Krishna's feet;
Shiva, holding her on his head, knows the limit of the truth about her.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Skanda Purana:*

**120.** *The greatness of the water that has washed [Vishnu's] feet is known to the god Shankara,
who holds on his head the Ganga fallen from the feet of Vishnu.*

**121.** Listen, I say more: in all the works of the Kali-yuga —
in almost all of them — there are hundreds upon hundreds of obstacles.

**122.** The name of Hari and bathing in the Ganga are free from hindrance:
in them there is no obstacle — so say the Puranas.

*Tatha hi, in the same, in the dialogue of Sri Brahma and Narada:*

**123.** *In the age of Kali all works are subject to hindrance, O great sage;
bathing in the Ganga and the name of Hari — these two are free from hindrance.*

**124.** So I have told you the whole truth;
leaving this land, dear one, let us go to the bank of the Ganga."

**125.** The Devi says: "Hear my answer, Prabhu:
whatever your command, that is my duty."

**126.** Hearing this, the Prabhu, pleased, says to her:
"I will surely settle on the bank of the Ganga.

**127.** Since those words have found a place in your heart,
I understand that Krishna's mercy has come upon you."

**128.** Duhkhini says: "I do not know what is good and what is bad;
my only hope is your lotus feet.

**129.** Wherever you go, there I shall surely go;
always keep Duhkhini at [your] crimson feet."

**130.** Knowing for certain the disposition of Duhkhini's mind,
the Prabhu was filled at heart with exceeding joy.

**131.** One day Jagadisha, in his own house,
called together his neighbours.

**132.** At the Prabhu's call all came and assembled;
the Prabhu humbly began to speak to them all:

**133.** "The wish to live on the bank of the Ganga has arisen in [my] heart;
be merciful, all of you, and give me [your] leave."

**134.** When Prabhu Jagadisha spoke these words,
the brahmanas, hearing them, became very sad.

**135.** With joined palms the brahmanas say before the Prabhu:
"Tell us, Prabhu, what means [of deliverance] will there be for us?"

**136.** The Prabhu says: "Do not be anxious;
constantly perform the sankirtana of Krishna's name.

**137.** Whomever you see, instruct him about Krishna;
in this way you will deliver this land."

**138.** All were in great sorrow at the separation from the Prabhu;
with nectarean words the Prabhu comforted them all.

**139.** Having received their leave, the Prabhu stayed some days more;
one day, at night, he says to Duhkhini:

**140.** "Tomorrow is an auspicious hour; the day is very good for setting out;
dear one, let us go from this land to the bank of the Ganga."

**141.** When Prabhu Jagadisha had said this,
Duhkhini Devi, hearing it, was delighted.

**142.** The Devi says: "Now my good fortune has come:
the Prabhu will take me with him to the bank of the Ganga.

**143.** There must be no delay now — let us go quickly.
Prabhu, tell this to Mahesha Pandita."

**144.** Then the Prabhu called his brother Mahesha
and told him that he was going to the bank of the Ganga.

**145.** Mahesha says: "I am your servant,
bound to your command, never independent.

**146.** Whatever your command, Prabhu, that I shall do;
wherever you tell me to live, there I shall live."

**147.** Hearing this from Mahesha's mouth,
the Prabhu embraced Mahesha with great happiness.

**148.** Rising in the morning, he set out together with Duhkhini;
Mahesha Pandita went behind him.

**149.** Leaving his native land, Jagadisha goes to the bank of the Ganga;
seeing this, the villagers are greatly grieved.

**150.** All weep aloud and lament,
saying: "Abandoning us, where are you [going]?"

**151.** Thus all the people of the village weep;
the Prabhu consoles them all with sweet words:

**152.** "I am not apart from you all for even a moment;
give me leave — I shall go and see the Ganga.

**153.** When one sees the Ganga, the mind turns to Krishna;
for this reason I go, leaving my home."

**154.** In this way Jagadisha makes them all understand;
all, grieved at heart, go to their own homes.

**155.** Having consoled them all, the Prabhu sets out from there;
Duhkhini and Mahesha follow behind him.

**156.** As they walked on and on, the sixth day of the journey came;
on the seventh day they arrived at the bank of the Ganga.

**157.** Having arrived, Prabhu Jagadisha reflects within himself:
"I have come to the bank of the Ganga — in what place shall I live?"

**158.** Having bathed in the Ganga, the Prabhu thinks within himself:
"Krishnachandra will descend in the Kali-yuga;

**159.** the Lord will be of golden complexion — so says the 'Bhagavata'.
[Would that] I might live in that place, if [it] were known for certain."[^8]

**160.** As he was thinking thus, the Prabhu came to know [it] in his mind,
and at that very moment he set out joyfully for Navadvipa.

**161.** Coming to the village of Navadvipa, he was filled with delight,
and in that place, with great happiness, he took up residence.[^9]

**162.** In this way Jagadisha came to Nadia,
and, having come to that land, he preached bhakti for Krishna.

**163.** Thus the Prabhu made his dwelling in Navadvipa;
by hearing of this, bhakti for Gaura's feet arises.

**164.** Prabhu Bhagavatananda is a mine of prema for Gaura;
day and night he is absorbed [in the company of] Gaura's devotees.

**165.** Above all, the life of Prabhu Jagadisha
he greatly loves to hear and chant.[^10]

**166.** By the strength of his command, placing hope in his feet,
this Ananda Dasa describes Jagadisha's pastimes.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the sixth varna, called "The Description of Sri Jagadisha's Coming to Sri Navadvipa".*
