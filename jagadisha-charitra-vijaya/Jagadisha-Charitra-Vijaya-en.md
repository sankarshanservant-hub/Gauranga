# Sri Jagadisha-charitra-vijaya

*"The Victory of Jagadisha's Life"*

*A poem by Ananda Dasa, Bengali, 18th century. Twelve varnas*

*Translated from the Bengali into English*

---

## Translator's Note

The *Jagadisha-charitra-vijaya* is the only separate life of **Jagadisha Pandita**, associate of Sri Chaitanya and Nityananda, the "dancer" (Nritya-vinodi), who founded the service of Sri Jagannatha and Sri Gaura-Gopala in the village of Yashoda (Yatra) near Chakdaha. The *Chaitanya-charitamrita* and *Chaitanya-bhagavata* only mention him; his birth, childhood, marriage to Duhkhini, settling in Navadvipa, the coming of Jagannatha to Yashoda, the visit of Mahaprabhu and Nityananda there after the sannyasa, the appearance of Gaura-Gopala, and Jagadisha's disciples and descendants are known from this text alone.

**The author.** Ananda Dasa is sixth in the line of disciples from Jagadisha (Jagadisha → Raghunatha Acharya → Bhagavatananda → Premananda → Radhacharana → Ananda Dasa). He wrote, he says, at the order of Bhagavatananda, who appeared to him in a dream, and he transmits the traditions of his line and of the Yashoda shripata.

**Source.** The translation follows the edition of Kishori Dasa Babaji (1st ed., 1402 B.S., c. 1996), printed from a manuscript dated 1737 Shaka (1815/16 CE); the beginning of the first varna is lost in the manuscript. Couplets are numbered within each varna; Sanskrit shlokas and the "tathahi…" introductions are translated in place. The editor's preface, survey and footnotes were neither translated nor used. Uncertain readings are listed in a separate list of questions and in the notes.

**The author and Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching.** The translation renders the text exactly; where the author differs from other sources or his words could be misread, the notes give the Gaudiya Vaishnava position according to Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.
- Ananda Dasa identifies Jagadisha Pandita of Yashoda, the "dancer" (in the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika*, 143, Chandrahasa of Vraja), with the Jagadisha of Navadvipa from whom, together with Hiranya, the child Nimai begged the Ekadashi offering. Other sources apparently speak of two different associates.
- At the end the author calls Jagadisha the sakhi Kalavati in Vraja (12.45), which differs from the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika*.
- The argument for a formless Brahman in varna 4 is the opponent's, refuted by Jagadisha; Brahman is the effulgence of the Lord's body (*Brahma-samhita* 5.40).
- The words "the guru is Ishvara Himself" (11.89) are understood by the *Gurvashtaka*: the guru is the Lord's dearest servant.
- The Deity whom the craftsman manifests at Mahaprabhu's order is the Lord Himself (10.88–91: whoever sees a difference between the Lord, His devotee and His Deity goes to hell).
- The departure of Jagadisha's three sons (9.106) is not a punishment but their entry into the Lord.

Reliability: a late life from the descendants of the line; its chronology and some details are not confirmed by early sources, but the local traditions of Yashoda are preserved only here.

## Varna 1. Mangalacharana

*The beginning of the varna is lost; homage to Chaitanya and Nityananda; Advaita — his roar and the Lord's descent; Gadadhara and Srivasa; Mahaprabhu's call to sing the Name of Hari; the glory of Jagadisha Pandita; the author's line of gurus — Raghunatha Acharya, Bhagavatananda (how he received his name), Premananda, Radhacharana; homage to the Gosvamis, the sixty-four mahantas and all Vaishnavas; the author's signature*

*Sri Sri Krishna Chaitanya is the refuge.*
*Sri Jagadisha-charita-vijaya.*
*The first varna.*
*Mangalacharana.*

**1.** …I attentively offer homage,[^01-1]
if there be mercy on me, foolish, poor and lowly.

**2.** Glory, glory to the son of Shachi, glory to the Merciful One!
Glory to Nityananda Raya, who descended as His prakasha!

*Tatha hi:*

**3.** *I worship Nityananda, from whose ears hang pearls, who in the form of Chaitanya's elder brother sanctified the face of the earth.*

**4.** Glory to Prabhu Nityananda, the abode of mercy![^01-2]
With devotion in my heart I worship His lotus feet.

**5.** With a concentrated mind I have bowed to Chaitanya and Nitai:
see — there is no one else to deliver the living beings.

**6.** Glory, glory to the merciful Sri Krishna Chaitanya!
Glory to Nityananda Raya, the son of Padmavati!

**7.** Glory to Advaita Acharya, the king of devotees,
who spread prema-bhakti upon the earth!

*Tatha hi:*

**8.** *I worship Acharya Advaita, the Lord who descended as a devotee: having understood the disposition of His mind, Chaitanya descended to the earth.*

**9.** Prabhu Advaita Acharya is the avatara as a devotee;
bowing my head, I worship His feet.

**10.** The avatara as a devotee is the Lord's non-different own form;
in many ways He destroys the darkness in the minds of living beings.

**11.** For the deliverance of living beings He gives a thunderous roar;
at His roar the whole world was astonished.

**12.** Night and day Prabhu ever roars;
at that roar the son of the king of Vraja appeared.

**13.** Having appeared, the Lord gave Him an order;
receiving that order, He understood in His heart:

**14.** "In Navadvipa, in Shachi's house, [My] descent will take place."
Knowing this, Prabhu Advaita was filled with boundless bliss.

**15.** "Glory, glory to the son of Shachi, Sri Krishna Chaitanya!
After so many days the Lord has made Me blessed.

**16.** Blessed, blessed am I — My birth has borne fruit;
I consider Myself blessed, having received the strength of [His] mercy."

**17.** Thus Prabhu Advaita, by giving His roar,
Himself brought about the descent of Chaitanya.

*Tatha hi:*

**18.** *I worship Gadadhara together with Srivasa Pandita — the two vessels of Chaitanya's prema, who descended as [His] devotee and [His] shakti.*

**19.** Glory, glory to Gadadhara, the shakti of Chaitanya!
To his lotus feet I offer obeisances and praise.

**20.** Srivasa Pandita is a dear devotee of Chaitanya;
see: his life is known throughout the world.

**21.** Prostrating myself before those lotus feet, [I pray:]
"Prabhu, reveal the message of the truth to me, foolish and worthless."

**22.** Prabhu Sri Krishna Chaitanya, comprising the five tattvas,
has made His descent — blessed is the Kali-yuga!

**23.** "The dharma of the Kali-yuga is the sankirtana of the Name of Hari:
raise your arms, call out 'Hari!' and dance!

**24.** Hold in your heart the maha-mantra, the Name of Hari, as the essence:
apart from the Name of Hari, living beings have no other way."

**25.** Speaking these words in a loud voice,
Mahaprabhu Himself, entreating, gives the Name of Hari to all without distinction.

**26.** In this way He gave the order to His own devotees;
receiving the order, they all distributed the Name of Hari.

**27.** Glory, glory to Sri Chaitanya, the ocean of mercy!
In His mercy He delivered all the fallen.

**28.** One branch of the Lord is Jagadisha Pandita;
see: his life is known throughout the world.

*Tatha hi:*

**29.** *I worship Prabhu Jagadisha, the friend of Duhkhini, maddened by prema for Chaitanya, grieved only by the sufferings [of others].*[^01-3]

**30.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha! I have taken refuge [in him]:
in your mercy grant me your crimson feet.

**31.** I am foolish and utterly low, and know nothing of bhajana;
be merciful to me, O treasure of Duhkhini's life!

**32.** Prabhu is at every moment intoxicated with prema for Chaitanya;
day and night he says: "Gaura is the treasure of my life!"

**33.** Raising his arms, Prabhu speaks these words:
"O living beings, with a concentrated mind take refuge in Chaitanya!

**34.** Worship Chaitanya, take up the name of Chaitanya —
by worshipping Chaitanya all will attain deliverance!"

**35.** He himself ever meditates on Chaitanya's feet;
calling out "Chaitanya!", Prabhu dances.

**36.** Now he dances and sings, now he runs with his face turned upward,
now, falling in the dust, he rolls on the ground.

**37.** "Sri Chaitanya, Sri Chaitanya is the treasure of my life!" —
saying this, he is beside himself with prema, be it night or day.

**38.** If he sees someone poor, lowly, unhappy or distressed,
he himself offers him the jewel of Chaitanya's name.

**39.** Be merciful, Jagadisha, protector of the world,
whose servant is Prabhu Raghunatha Acharya!

**40.** What power have I to describe his praise?
Only for my own purification do I humbly say a little.[^01-4]

*Tatha hi:*

**41.** *I worship the two feet of Raghunatha, the highest good, [him] whose name is the receptacle of the essence of all sweetness, who in the heart is everything.*[^01-5]

**42.** Prabhu Raghunatha Acharya is an ocean of mercy:
in his mercy he delivered all the fallen.

**43.** His name is the receptacle of the essence of all sweetness;
by his mercy bliss grows in the heart.

**44.** The pair of his feet is the highest good;
to those feet I bow, relying on the strength of devotion.

**45.** Whoever worships Raghunatha's feet with devotion,
he surely fulfils that one's heart's desire.

**46.** A branch of Sri Raghunatha is Bhagavatananda,
whose mercy destroys the bonds of worldly existence for living beings.

*Tatha hi:*

**47.** *We worship him, the desired one — Srimad Bhagavatananda, the abode of bliss and virtues, the ocean of mercy.*

**48.** Glory, glory to Bhagavatananda Mahashaya!
Be merciful to me, the fallen one, O merciful Prabhu!

**49.** Prabhu values virtues; he is the eyes of the blind;
to people without knowledge Prabhu gives knowledge of the truth.

**50.** His boundless mercy cannot be described:
by his mercy the desires of the heart are fulfilled.

**51.** Apart from those feet I know nothing else;
millions of times I prostrate myself at his feet.

**52.** What can I, a fool, know of his life?
Formerly he was known by the name Sri Krishna.[^01-6]

**53.** He recited the "Bhagavata" right before the sri-murti;
hearing his recitation, the Lord was greatly pleased.

**54.** Hearing from his lips the nectar of the "Bhagavata",
[the Lord] manifested Himself visibly from the sri-murti.

**55.** At his words the Lord felt great joy
and, bringing His own cloth, placed it on his head.

**56.** Seeing this, the devotees of Gaura rejoiced,
and all of them gave him the name Bhagavatananda.

**57.** I, an insignificant being, am writing of all these events —
forgive me this fault in your mercy.

**58.** I write the story of the mahantas from my heart's fond desire —
in your mercy forgive me this offence.

**59.** I do not understand what I write — I am always bewildered;
whatever I write, I write only by the strength of your order.

**60.** Prabhu, one of your branches is Srila Premananda,
by whose mercy living beings attain the highest bliss.

*Tatha hi:*

**61.** *I worship Premananda, who bestows divine knowledge on people covered by ignorance, whose heart is filled with compassion.*

**62.** Glory, glory to the venerable Gosani Premananda,
Prabhu, the ocean of the nectar of prema, full of all virtues!

**63.** People covered by ignorance dwell in the well of worldly existence:
filling their bellies, they pass their time somehow or other.

**64.** Like those blind from birth, they know nothing;
they cannot tell the proper from the improper; night and day are the same to them.[^01-7]

**65.** As a motherless child wanders about in sorrow,
so living beings come and go in vain in worldly existence.

**66.** To all these living beings Prabhu gave divine knowledge
and in his mercy carried them across the river of worldly existence.

**67.** The fear of worldly existence has troubled my mind;
bowing to your feet, I have taken refuge.

**68.** I bow to your branch, Radhacharana Mahashaya,
by whose mercy living beings have bliss in their hearts.

*Tatha hi:*

**69.** *O Radhacharana, ocean of mercy, I, the lowly one, worship you: show mercy to me, worthless and blind, fallen into the cesspit of worldly existence.*

**70.** Glory to Prabhu Sri Radhacharana, the ocean of mercy!
For the deliverance of living beings you are [their] friend.

**71.** Living beings come and go in the cesspit of worldly existence;
in that well of existence, in great darkness, I too remain.

**72.** I am foolish and utterly ill-behaved, my mind ever wicked;
I am poor, lowly and base, and moreover devoid of bhajana.

**73.** Show mercy, Prabhu — I have bowed to your feet;
deliver me from worldly existence — I have taken refuge.

*Tatha hi:*

**74.** *I worship that Radhika, clad in a cloud-coloured garment, whose radiance is [like] a steady streak of lightning, auspicious in form, dear to Sri Lakshmi.*[^01-8]

**75.** The radiance of the feet surpasses steady lightning,
the beauty of the moons of the toenails surpasses the moon.

**76.** Above them is a cloud-coloured garment, wondrously beautiful,
lovely to behold, worked with gold.

**77.** With a joyful heart I have bowed to her feet;
I have no power to describe that form.

**78.** In Vrajapura is the queen of Vraja, Radha Thakurani;
I know that this [Prabhu] is the embodiment of a part of her.[^01-9]

**79.** My remembrance, contemplation, vandana and bhajana
are to ever perform archana to those crimson feet.

**80.** I am foolish, dull of mind and utterly ill-behaved;
apart from those feet I have no refuge.

**81.** In offering praise I cannot tell merits from faults —
do not be angry at all with this foolish servant.

**82.** I have offered a little praise to my own line of gurus;
now I shall glorify the Lord's devotees.[^01-10]

*Tatha hi:*

**83.** I diligently worship the beautiful feet of Sri Rupa, by which the fear of worldly existence goes far away.
Whoever serves those feet attains Sri Chaitanya:
show mercy to me, the ill-behaved.

**84.** Glory, glory to Sanatana, the treasure of Chaitanya's life, renowned in this world!
Bowing my head, I worship the pair of his feet —
perhaps he will show me favour.

**85.** Glory to Raghunatha Bhatta! Make me your own, worthless, devoid of bhajana and sadhana.
Worshipping your lotus feet, [may] I likewise give up the objects of the senses —
show mercy to me, foolish and worthless.

**86.** Glory to Sri Jiva Gosani! I pray to you: give this fallen one the shade of your feet.
I have bowed to those feet — purify my mind,
show mercy by casting your merciful glance.

**87.** Glory to Sri Gopala Bhatta! Mighty is the ocean of maya, and I am drowning in it.
Remove that maya, so that I may behold Vrajapura,
having given up the craving for the objects of the senses.[^01-11]

**88.** Glory to Raghunatha Dasa! May I become your servant — such is the desire of my heart.
Those feet take away sorrow, and before them I am poor and lowly —
I am a prisoner of the maya of samsara.

**89.** From this maya of samsara drag me out, O Lord of the poor — there is no way but those feet!
I worship your two feet: remove the bad objects of the senses —
I pray at those feet.

**90.** Glory to the sixty-four mahantas, the limit of whose virtues even the gods cannot see![^01-12]
My mind is very wicked, yet with all diligence
I have bowed to their feet.

**91.** To all the residents of Navadvipa, the devotees of Sri Mahaprabhu, I bow, holding a straw between my teeth.
Forgiving my offences, may all be gracious
and fulfil my wish.

**92.** The Vaishnavas who live in Vrindavana — I bow to their feet.
All the residents of Nilachala, the constant devotees of the Lord —
to them I bow with a concentrated mind.

**93.** The servants of the servants of Mahaprabhu live in land after land — in the feet of them all I place my hope:
may I, birth after birth, attain those feet,
may I become a servant of the servants of the Lord's devotees.

**94.** I bow to all the Vaishnavas — I have no power, and my intelligence is not subtle;
as far as my understanding goes I have praised all the Vaishnavas —
be kind and show mercy.

**95.** The feet of Bhagavatananda are my wealth and fine treasure; his order came to me from above.
According to that Prabhu's wish, the life of Sri Jagadisha
has been described by Ananda Chandra Dasa.[^01-13]

*Thus, in the "Charitra-vijaya" of Sri Jagadisha Pandita, ends the first varna, called "Mangalacharana".*

[^01-1]: The beginning of the varna is missing in the edition: the first leaf of the base manuscript is lost, and the text begins in the middle of a line ("…আমি বন্দি সাবধানে", *…āmi bandi sābadhāne*).

[^01-2]: The edition reads "করুনা সন্ম" (*karunā sanma*); taken as *karuṇā-sadma*, "abode of mercy" (rhyming with "পাদপদ্ম").

[^01-3]: A play on words: *duḥkhinī-bāndhava* is "the friend of Duhkhini" (Jagadisha's wife) and "the friend of the suffering [soul]". *Duḥkhaika-kātaram* — lit. "distressed only by suffering"; cf. 38.

[^01-4]: "কিছু করি পরিহার" (*kichu kari parihāra*): *parihāra* is taken in its Old Bengali sense of "humble entreaty, apology"; the rendering is tentative.

[^01-5]: The second line of the shloka is corrupt in the edition: "সর্ব্বমাধুর্য্য সারা নামাধী‹ং› হৃদয়েযৎ সর্বং" (*sarva-mādhurya-sārā nāmādhī‹ṁ› hṛdaye yat sarvaṁ*); it is translated by sense, following the Bengali paraphrase in 43.

[^01-6]: That is, Bhagavatananda's former name was Sri Krishna; the name "Bhagavatananda" ("the bliss of the 'Bhagavata'") was given to him for his recitation of the "Bhagavata" (53–56). To which Deity he recited is not said.

[^01-7]: *Janama-āndhuyā* (জনম আন্ধুয়া) — "blind from birth".

[^01-8]: The shloka is partly corrupt in the edition: "স্থিরবিদ্যুন্মতাকান্তিমম্বুদাম্বরধারিনীং। রাধিকাং শম্বরূপাং তাং শ্রীললক্ষীপ্রিয়াং ভজে" — "-মতা-" is taken as *-latā-* ("streak of lightning"), *śambarūpāṁ* as *śaṁ-rūpāṁ* ("auspicious in form"), *śrīla-lakṣmī-priyāṁ* as "dear to Sri Lakshmi"; the last two readings are unclear. The Bengali paraphrase (75–77) speaks of the radiance of the feet, the toenails and the garment.

[^01-9]: "তাঁর অংশ স্বরূপিনী ইহা আমি জানি" — *aṁśa-svarūpiṇī* (feminine), "the embodiment of her part". From the context (68–80: the homage to Radhacharana, into which the shloka on Radhika is inserted) the author apparently honours his guru Radhacharana as a partial manifestation of Sri Radha — in his eternal identity as Radha's maidservant (hence the feminine). The rendering "this [Prabhu]" is an interpretation; the original has "this" (*ihā*).

[^01-10]: The author's line of gurus (28–80): Jagadisha Pandita → Raghunatha Acharya → Bhagavatananda → Premananda → Radhacharana → Ananda Dasa. There follows homage to the Gosvamis and all Vaishnavas; "tatha hi" here introduces not a shloka but Bengali stanzas in tripadi.

[^01-11]: The edition reads "দেখিবেন ব্রজপুর" (*dekhibena*, lit. "he will see"); taken as "[that I may] see". "তিয়া গিয়া" — *tiyāgiyā*, "having given up".

[^01-12]: The sixty-four mahantas (*cauṣaṭṭi mahānta*) — the traditional Bengali Vaishnava count of the great associates of Sri Chaitanya.

[^01-13]: An expanded form of the author's signature; here he calls himself Ananda Chandra Dasa. "From above" — *pratyādeśa*, an order given in a dream or vision; the author tells of this dream at the beginning of varna 2.


## Varna 2. The Description of the Birth and the Annaprashana

*In a dream Bhagavatananda bids the author describe Jagadisha's life; Kamalaksha and Bhagyavati receive from Narayana the boon of a Vaishnava son; Jagadisha's birth on Bhima Ekadashi; the festival; the annaprashana and the naming*

*The second varna*

**1.** *Having received in a dream the supreme command of Srimad Bhagavatananda,
I describe the life of Sri Jagadisha.*[^02-1]

**2.** Glory to Prabhu Bhagavatananda, full of mercy!
Be kind and show mercy to me, a wretch.

**3.** My birth has become fortunate and fruitful:
therefore I have seen those rosy feet.

**4.** On the twenty-ninth [of the month] of Bhadra I was overcome by sleep;
at that time I saw a wondrous form.

**5.** The beauty of those feet surpassed gold;
I saw the radiance of the holy body, surpassing millions of suns.

**6.** I saw the beautiful face — [beside it] the moon seemed stained.
That great personage appeared before me.

**7.** Smiling, he speaks sweet words to me:
"Describe the life of Jagadisha."

**8.** "I am a fool — what shall I describe?" I thought in my heart.
My heart grew timid with fear; words would not come.

**9.** Seeing my fear, that jewel of a man says to me:
"Ananda, never be afraid in your heart.[^02-2]

**10.** Know for certain: I am Bhagavatananda.
You will surely carry out my command.

**11.** Through your mouth I myself will describe [it];
the devotees will surely accept [it]."

**12.** Mercifully the Prabhu gave me this command —
and at that very moment my sleep was broken.

**13.** Awake, I no longer saw that form;
my mind grew eager to carry out the command.

**14.** Like one mad, I write in the book an account of myself;[^02-3]
I do not know good from bad — the Prabhu's command is my strength.

**15.** One who becomes a devotee of Sri Jagadisha
will surely accept this book.

**16.** What will others understand of this book's narration?
He will understand for whom Jagadisha is the treasure of life.

*Tatha hi:*

**17.** *Hear, then, of the form, birth and deeds
of the great-souled Sri Jagadisha Pandita, described by me.*

**18.** Glory, glory to the devotees of Sri Mahaprabhu!
Hear the account of Jagadisha's life.

**19.** Be kind and show mercy to me, a fool,
so that the book's description may flow with ease.

**20.** In the eastern country there lived a twice-born named Kamalaksha —
a Bandya of Gayaghar, a descendant of Bhatta Narayana.[^02-4]

**21.** His wife was chaste and wholly devoted to her husband;
her renowned name was Srimati Bhagyavati.

**22.** One equal to Bhagyavati is not seen in the world:
day and night she performs the worship and service of Vishnu.

**23.** Kamalaksha Bandya was wholly devoted to Vishnu;
with body, mind and speech he served Vishnu.

**24.** Apart from the truth of bhakti to Vishnu, no words were on his lips;
in taking the name of Vishnu he abode in great happiness.

**25.** With the mood of bhakti the two worshipped Vishnu;
their bhakti pleased Narayana.

**26.** Graciously the Lord said to them both:
"Whatever you desire — take it as a boon."

**27.** The Bandya says: "If You will give a boon to one who has nothing,
let firm bhakti abide at Your feet.

**28.** Wealth, sons, worldly things do not attract my mind.
Do not bind me, poor and wretched, in the net of maya."

**29.** The Lord says: "Keep no doubt at all in your mind:
know for certain that you both are dear to Me."

**30.** The Bandya says: "Lord, if it is Your mercy,
then give me this boon, O merciful one:

**31.** devoted to Vishnu, learned in all the shastras —
let a son be born to me from Bhagyavati's womb."

**32.** Hearing this, the Lord speaks gracious words to him:
"Your son will be the crown of Vaishnavas."

**33.** Hearing this, Bhagyavati was filled with joy:
the Lord had declared that the son would be a Vaishnava.

**34.** The Devi thrilled all over; [tears] streamed from both her eyes.
She says: "A Vaishnava will come into my womb!

**35.** What fortune: my birth has become fruitful.
Surely in a former life I did some good deed.

**36.** By the fruit of that merit this good fortune will be mine:
the Lord has declared that the son will be a Vaishnava."

**37.** With a happy heart the chaste lady serves Vishnu:
by hearing, chanting, remembering, worship and prayer.

**38.** Thus in various ways she served Sri Vishnu;
after many days the Devi conceived.

**39.** One, two — in due order ten months were completed;
all that the injunctions and custom require was done.

**40.** Looking at Bhagyavati, the Bandya rejoices in his heart:
her body is like a mass of great effulgence.

**41.** Seeing [this] wonder, the Bandya reflects:
"Surely some great [soul] has come within her."

**42.** With a happy heart Kamalaksha worshipped Vishnu;
at an auspicious moment Bhagyavati gave birth to a son.

**43.** In the month of Magha, in the bright fortnight, on the lunar day of Ekadashi,
known among people as Bhima Ekadashi,[^02-5]

**44.** at the end of the day, when the sun had set,
the dusk (godhuli) passed and the time of evening came.

**45.** At that hour people perform the evening worship
and play karatalas, mridangas and other instruments.

**46.** Lighting incense and lamps, people worship Vishnu;
in every house is the sound of the conches and bells of arati.[^02-6]

**47.** On the night of Ekadashi, the day of Sri Hari, people
loudly sing the names "Hare Krishna."

**48.** Under an auspicious lagna, with auspicious planets, in an auspicious house and sign,
Jagadisha appeared — the treasure-store of all virtues.

**49.** The people keeping the Ekadashi vow are full of bliss;
at that hour Bhagyavati gave birth to a son.

**50.** Wondrously beautiful is the form of that child;
Bhagyavati gazes [at him] with unwavering eyes.

**51.** The beautiful face is like a dancing moon;
the brows reach to the ears, the eyes are large.

**52.** The nose surpasses the sesame flower, the lips are a lovely bimba;
a pair of long ears, a charming forehead.

**53.** The two arms are like lotus stalks without thorns;
the chest is broad, the navel like a lake.

**54.** The waist is very slender, the hips are broad;[^02-7]
seeing his thighs, the elephant's trunk was put to shame.

**55.** The two lotus feet surpass the land-lotus;
on them are five and five holy toes, perfectly pure.

**56.** The nails of those toes are [like] the rising of the moon:
they filled with light the lying-in room, where there was no lamp.[^02-8]

**57.** His lovely yellow hue surpasses turmeric and champaka;
on the child's body all auspicious marks are manifest.

**58.** Seeing [this], Bhagyavati Devi called the Bandya;
coming, Kamalaksha Bandya looks at his son's face.

**59.** Seeing the son's radiance, he was amazed in his mind:
"A great personage has appeared from Bhagyavati's womb."

**60.** Seeing on the child's body the signs of a portion of Ishvara,
the brahmana thrilled all over and became very happy.

**61.** Wealth, grain and whatever else was in his house
he gave away to the brahmanas with love in his heart.

**62.** The singers, dancers and all others who came
the Bandya satisfied with gifts of money.

**63.** In Kamalaksha's house there was a great festival;
hearing of it, the neighbours came to see.

**64.** Seeing the child's form, [all] were filled with delight;
in bliss all the people give [him] their blessings.

**65.** All together they joyfully brought presents;
Kamalaksha distributed all of it to the brahmanas.

**66.** Bhagyavati, taking her son on her lap,
gazes at him constantly without blinking.

**67.** According to custom, twenty-one days passed;
having bathed, mother and son came into the house.

**68.** Having brought wife and son into the house, the worthy Bandya
gave many gifts with a joyful heart.

**69.** Having gained a son, they grow happier day by day;
both worship Vishnu with the greatest care.

**70.** Thus gradually the fifth month passed,
and the sixth month [of the child's life] began.

**71.** Resolving to perform the annaprashana for their son,
they fixed a day at an auspicious hour in the month of Ashadha.

**72.** Holding their son in their arms, the two, lying down,
consider what name to give the son:

**73.** "The form of our son enchants the world;
it seems he will deliver the world.

**74.** The signs of greatness are imprinted on his body;
therefore it is fitting to give him the name Jagadisha."[^02-9]

**75.** Having decided this, they were filled with great happiness;
rising in the morning, they invited many brahmanas.

**76.** According to the rules of the shastras they performed the holy annaprashana;
having given the name Jagadisha, they rejoiced at heart.

**77.** With the brahmanas they held a great festival;
for the son's welfare they gave gifts of many kinds.

**78.** I have no power to describe this in detail;
by the Prabhu's mercy I describe it only briefly, like a sutra.

**79.** As the moon of the second day gradually becomes full,
so little by little Jagadisha grew.

**80.** I am a fool, of bad conduct, utterly wretched;
[my] description is wayward, the book is not beautiful.

**81.** At the feet of the Lord's devotees I beg pardon:
do not hold against me any offence [I have committed] in this.

**82.** In a dream Prabhu Bhagavatananda commanded:
"Ananda, describe the life of Jagadisha."

**83.** By the strength of that command, placing hope in those feet,
the servant of his servants describes Jagadisha's life.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the second varna, called "The Description of the Birth and the Annaprashana".*

## Varna 3. The Description of the Childhood Pastimes and the Upanayana

*The infant Jagadisha repeats Krishna's name from his first words, plays at sankirtana with the neighbours' children and worships a clay form of Krishna; his parents recognize in him an associate of the Lord; the "chalk in the hand" rite, a prayer to Krishna for bhakti, mastery of all the shastras and of the "Bhagavata" by the age of eight; the upanayana in his ninth year; praise of Jagadisha's mercy and the author's signature*

*The third varna*

**1.** *Him who in childhood, while still speaking half-words, [showed] the power of the japa of Krishna's name —
that Srimad Jagadisha of wondrous deeds I worship.*[^03-1]

**2.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, the ocean of mercy!
Show mercy, Prabhu: I am very wretched.

**3.** When in childhood the Prabhu's speech began to come,
he took to uttering constantly the names of Radha and Krishna.

**4.** Babbling half-words, he utters the sweetest name of Krishna;
hearing [this], Bhagyavati Devi was filled with abundant joy.

**5.** Calling Kamalaksha, the chaste lady in great bliss says:
"Your son is by no means an ordinary man.

**6.** From the child's mouth come words of Krishna all the time;
he is an associate of the Lord — this I have understood fully.

**7.** When he is hungry, he always says, 'Krishna, Krishna';
even as he falls asleep, [his] tongue takes the name of Krishna.

**8.** Saying 'Krishna, Krishna, Krishna!' he weeps;
and at times, hearing the name of Krishna, he stops crying.

**9.** Hearing the name of Krishna, his face breaks into smiles;
in the bliss of Krishna's name at times he dances.

**10.** He calls many sons of the neighbouring twice-born
and constantly plays the game of Krishna-sankirtana.

**11.** Out of clay he shapes an image (murti) of Krishna
and worships it with various forest flowers.

**12.** Taking sweets and other food from me,
he steadies his mind and offers [them] to Krishna.

**13.** Thus day and night he abides in feeling for Krishna.
Know for certain: he is one of the Lord's associates."

**14.** Hearing this, the Bandya says to her: "Listen, wife:
I know that [our] son is an associate of the Lord.

**15.** Raise him with care;
by this we both shall attain the goal of life."

**16.** Thus the two, with great joy at heart,
raise their son day and night.

**17.** I bow down at the feet of these two,
whose son is my Prabhu Jagadisha.

**18.** The childhood pastimes of Sri Jagadisha, as if in a sutra,
for my own purification I describe briefly.

**19.** When the Prabhu reached the age of five years,
his father, choosing an auspicious day, placed chalk in his hand.[^03-2]

**20.** Remembering Sri Krishna, he began his learning;
by the sixth month the Prabhu had mastered writing.

**21.** Then, after some days, the Prabhu began his reading;
[but] he gave his mind to the lotus feet of Sri Krishna.

**22.** At his father's insistence he writes and reads,
but this becomes an obstacle to remembering Krishna's feet.

**23.** Pained by the obstacles to his bhajana,
the Prabhu prays thus to the feet of Sri Krishna:

**24.** "Lord, by Your mercy always arrange this:
that I may give up maya and worship Your feet.

**25.** Do not bind me with the maya of worldly things;
do not deceive me, Lord, by giving me various kinds of learning."

**26.** Thus he always worships Krishna;
staying near his guru, he does not study.

**27.** [Yet] when the guru asks about the lesson,
by Krishna's mercy he recites all that [was to be] learned.

**28.** Grammar, lexicons, literature, drama,
alankara, smriti, nyaya, the darshanas, "sulyaka",[^03-3]

**29.** the agamas, the nigamas — and he also studied Vedanta;
a knower of all the shastras, no one finds the limit of his [learning].

**30.** The four Vedas and fourteen shastras he learned as if in play;[^03-4]
all the ancient Puranas and the rest he read.

**31.** Having thus studied various books,
at last he reads the Srimad Bhagavata.

**32.** In it he found the highest message of the truth about Krishna;
on the reading of that book the Prabhu fixed his mind.

**33.** Regarding the book as Krishna's own form, he worships it;[^03-5]
in the Prabhu's heart there is no thought of other books.

**34.** Thus Jagadisha accomplished all [this]:
in his eighth year he completed his studies.

**35.** Taking his guru's leave, he came to his own home
and with full ceremony bowed at the feet of his father and mother.

**36.** Looking at their son's face, both rejoice at heart;
taking the son on their laps, they kiss [him] with affection.

**37.** Bringing various sweets, they placed them beside their son;
seeing the excellent food, the Prabhu was glad.

**38.** With a happy mind he offers [it] to Sri Krishna;
Krishna's prasada he gave first to his mother.

**39.** A little at a time he distributed it to other people,
and he himself ate a little of the maha-prasada.

**40.** Seeing his son's conduct, Kamalaksha Bandya
together with his own people became greatly joyful.

**41.** When the son reached the age of nine years,
[his father] resolved in his mind: "Now I shall give him the sacred thread."

**42.** Gathering his own kinsfolk, he fixed the day;
the brahmana set about collecting all that was needed.

**43.** For Jagadisha's auspicious rite, by Krishna's mercy,[^03-6]
from who knows where the things needed came and presented themselves.

**44.** Thus provisions of many kinds were gathered;
he invited all his relatives and kinsmen by marriage.

**45.** He invited brahmanas from various lands;
all of them came on the day of the upanayana.

**46.** The brahmanas came and assembled;
Jagadisha received the sacred thread.

**47.** Everyone was treated to various dishes;
Kamalaksha gratified everyone as befitted each.

**48.** How the Prabhu's upanayana took place
I have written briefly, fearing to make the book too long.

**49.** The Prabhu, wearing the sacred thread, displayed such sweetness —
seeing it, the eyes are soothed.
Whoever sees that loveliness becomes free of worldly attachment
and without effort attains Krishna's feet.

**50.** Whoever worships those feet is a supreme devotee;
there is none as fortunate as he.
Jagadisha, by the power of mercy, wandering through Gauda-mandala,
gives bhakti to anyone and everyone.

**51.** Setting aside all the shastras, describing the greatness of bhakti,
he made the living beings take up bhakti to Krishna.
Thus all the living beings, becoming followers of the Prabhu,
obtained the strength for the bhajana of Krishna.

**52.** One to whom Jagadisha is merciful crosses [the ocean of] existence with ease;
Vrindavana becomes attainable for him.
Therefore I say: by no means fall into the delusion of worldly things —
take the shelter of his feet.

**53.** Worship those lotus feet — these bonds of existence will fall away;
have no doubt at all about this.
By the power of those feet you will cross the ocean of maya —
know these words as certain.

**54.** All the virtues of Jagadisha — to set them down in a book
I have no such power.
The mind bound up in worldly things is not still for a moment;
at all times it is seized by maya.

**55.** Wickedness is maya's nature: she has made [me] take up a wicked dharma;
she raises opposition on the path of bhakti.
O mind, be attentive: maya is very powerful —
[beware] lest she ever drown [you].[^03-7]

**56.** Binding my mind to Jagadisha's lotus feet,
casting away every other hope,
being happy myself, to write the Prabhu's virtues in a book —
this became my desire.

**57.** When such was my mind, in a dream a command was given
by my Prabhu Bhagavatananda.
In accordance with that Prabhu's order, the life of Jagadisha
was composed by this Ananda Dasa.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the third varna, called "The Description of the Childhood Pastimes and the Upanayana".*

## Varna 4. The Account of the Discussion of Brahman

*In the eastern country Jagadisha teaches the shastras to brahmanas' sons and makes them Vaishnavas; the pandit Vidyanidhi Bhattacharya, who holds that Brahman is formless, comes to debate with him; the story of Hanuman's doubt and the voice from the effulgent abode; the testimony of the Bhagavata, the Brahma-vaivarta Purana, the Bhagavad-gita and the Padma Purana; Vidyanidhi takes shelter of Jagadisha and attains Krishna-prema*

*The fourth varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, glory to the merciful one!
Be kind and show mercy to me, a wretch.

**2.** I am a man poor, lowly and bad, sunk in base worldly pursuits;
fallen, destitute, and an utter wretch besides.

**3.** In a dream I received Bhagavatananda's command;
by the strength of that command, Prabhu, I sing your virtues.

**4.** Mercifully be pleased with me, a fallen one,
so that somehow the description of [your] life may be completed.

**5.** The pastimes Prabhu performed while living in the eastern country —
all those pastimes I have written down in this book.

**6.** When the holy upanayana had been performed, Prabhu with a joyful heart
began to teach various shastras to the sons of brahmanas.

**7.** The brahmanas' sons whom Prabhu taught the shastras,
having come to know the truth of bhakti, all became teachers.

**8.** Thus many brahmanas from various lands,[^04-1]
coming to Prabhu, study [under him].

**9.** One studies grammar and lexicons;
another studies the science of literature (sahitya-shastra).

**10.** Some study smriti and logic, some Vedanta;
some fortunate one studies shruti, the Vedas and the agamas.

**11.** Thus the disciples study various shastras;
by Prabhu's mercy everyone's mind is at Krishna's feet.

**12.** After the various shastras, at the end he teaches the Puranas;
some study the Sri Bhagavata.

**13.** Learning from it the determination of all truths,
by Prabhu's mercy all became Vaishnavas.

**14.** Thus Jagadisha teaches his disciples;
seeing [this], mother and father rejoiced at heart.

**15.** The Bandya says: "Bhagyavati, this is your good fortune:
therefore this saintly son was born from your womb."

**16.** Bhagyavati says: "My lord, by your mercy
a son devoted to Krishna was born from my womb."[^04-2]

**17.** Kamalaksha and Bhagyavati, sitting in their house,
always speak of their son's virtues with joy at heart.

**18.** Meanwhile Jagadisha here teaches his disciples
and with his disciples performs Hari-sankirtana.

**19.** Rising early in the morning, Prabhu performs sankirtana —
and at that time a brahmana's son came.

**20.** He was a man of great learning, expert in every branch of knowledge;
the learned of all lands yielded to him.

**21.** Having heard with his own ears of Prabhu's learning,
he came into Prabhu's presence to debate the shastras.

**22.** His renowned name was Vidyanidhi Bhattacharya;
his gift of poetry equalled that of Kalidasa.[^04-3]

**23.** Having defeated the pandits of all lands,
he came and appeared where Jagadisha was.

**24.** Coming, he raised discussion of various shastras:
smriti, logic, Vedanta and the rest — all he debated.

**25.** In the debate on all the shastras he was defeated;
then he raised the question of the inquiry into Brahman.

**26.** Vidyanidhi says: "Brahman is formless."
Prabhu says: "You do not know that Brahman indeed has form."

**27.** Vidyanidhi says: "I know Brahman to be formless:
all the shastras declare It formless.

*Tatha hi, in the shastra:*

**28.** *'[Brahman] is spotless, formless, Its form is effulgence; all the gods are Its parts' —
so Rama said to Hanuman long ago.*[^04-4]

**29.** In the Treta-yuga Ramachandra, having bound the ocean,
performed worship, installing a Shiva-linga.[^04-5]

**30.** Seeing this, Hanuman reflects:
'Whom does Rama, the complete Brahman, worship?

**31.** I know only this: Ramachandra is the complete Brahman.
Now I surmise that there must be another Brahman.'

**32.** This doubt arose in Hanuman's mind;
Ramachandra, the Indweller, knew of it.

**33.** With a smiling face Bhagavan says to Hanuman:
'What are you pondering in your heart, child Hanuman?'

**34.** Hanuman says: 'Lord, You are above all;
whom You worship, this I cannot understand.

**35.** I know: Ramachandra is the Maker, above all,
the beginningless Purusha, the sustainer of all the universes.

**36.** Whom then do You worship? — a doubt has arisen;
this lila of Yours I could not understand.

**37.** Whom does the merciful Lord worship?
Be kind and tell me, have pity.'

**38.** Ramachandra says: 'Child, hear the essence:
the root of all is the effulgent, formless Brahman.

**39.** Its part is Sadashiva, who comprises all the gods;
know for certain: him I worship.'

**40.** Ramachandra — an avatara, Bhagavan in full —
Himself said that the original Brahman is formless.

**41.** According to which shastra do you speak of another Brahman?
Set it out in detail before me."

**42.** Jagadisha says: "You explain to me:
by what was Hanuman's doubt removed?"

**43.** The brahmana says: "Of that I know nothing at all;
I accept only the effulgent, formless Brahman.

**44.** If you know it, then declare:
what is the form of the complete Brahman, Bhagavan?"

**45.** Then Jagadisha says: "Hear, then, the essence:
that effulgent Brahman is the vibhuti of Sri Rama.[^04-6]

**46.** In the very story you have brought before me
lies its true conclusion (siddhanta).

**47.** Sri Rama said that there is an effulgent Brahman;
hearing this, doubt arose in Hanuman's mind.

**48.** To dispel that doubt, Hanuman
went to where the effulgent abode is.

**49.** Going there, he asked: 'Listen, effulgent one,
tell me the truth about yourself.

**50.** I am the son of the Wind, the servant of Sri Rama;
I have come here to know the truth about you.

**51.** I adjure you by Rama: do not deceive me;
set out the whole truth about yourself.'

**52.** As soon as he said this, from the effulgent abode
a voice issued forth thus:

**53.** *'That Rama whose servant you are, from whom the three gunas arose,
from whom creation and the rest come forth — I am only His supreme vibhuti.*

**54.** That Rama whose servant you are, O son of the Wind —
from Him the three gunas arose.

**55.** Creation and all the rest come from Him;
know for certain: I am His vibhuti.

**56.** Raghuvara called Himself Brahman:
He is the master of the world, the Ishvara of all.

**57.** To test your mind He beguiled you;
deceived by the ruse, you have come here.

**58.** Go quickly and take hold of Sri Rama's feet —
this is my request to you.

**59.** Doubt arose in you: whom does Rama worship?
Rama Himself worshipped Himself.'

**60.** Hearing the voice from heaven, the great Hanuman,
delighted at heart, reflects:

**61.** 'This effulgent Brahman is the vibhuti of Sri Rama;
Sri Rama is the supreme Brahman: I have grasped the essence.'

**62.** Having thus become certain, the heroic lord of monkeys
came back from there with great speed.

**63.** Coming, the hero bowed down at Sri Rama's feet;
the Lord, taking hold of Hanuman, embraced him.

**64.** Then the hero Hanuman, before Sri Rama,
with joined palms began to offer prayers:

**65.** 'I have committed an offence at Your feet;
forgive the fault — I am Your foolish servant.

**66.** I am poor and lowly, an animal, a monkey by birth;
be merciful to me, a fallen one, O ocean of compassion!

**67.** Through the confusion of my mind I could not understand;
it has gone away, Lord, by Your compassion.'

**68.** Thus Sri Hanuman's error was removed:
he understood that the formless is Sri Rama's vibhuti.

**69.** You are a pandit, yet you do not know Ishvara;
accept that the effulgence of Ishvara's body is Brahman.[^04-7]

**70.** In the four yugas Bhagavan has four avataras;
the proof of this is in the Srimad Bhagavata.

*Tatha hi, in the Tenth Canto:*

**71.** *He has taken forms according to the yugas and formerly had three colours —
white, red and yellow; now He has assumed the black.*[^04-8]

**72.** In the four yugas the Lord descends as a complete avatara;
the effulgent Brahman is the radiance of that Lord's body.

**73.** In the Dvapara-yuga Sri Krishna descends as the complete Brahman;
sattva, rajas and tamas arose through His portions.

**74.** That Lord is all that is, the sustainer of all;
apart from Him the world has no other Maker.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Brahma-vaivarta Purana, the words of Sri Mahadeva to Sri Parvati:*

**75.** *From Brahma down to a blade of grass, all is false, O Parvati;
worship the true, supreme Brahman — the Lord of Radha, who is beyond the three gunas.*[^04-9]

**76.** Shiva says: 'Gauri, hear my words:
with undivided mind always worship Sri Krishna's feet.

**77.** The Lord, the Master of Radhika, is beyond the three gunas;
apart from the feet of that Lord there is no other refuge.

**78.** From Brahma down to a blade of grass, all is impermanent;
Sri Krishna, eternity, knowledge and bliss, is the supreme Brahman, the truth.

**79.** Whoever leaves such a Krishnachandra and worships another
is bewildered by maya and steeped in utter ignorance.'

**80.** Shiva — lord of the three worlds, god of the gods —
says that Krishna is Brahman, the Maker of all.

**81.** Leaving such a Brahman, you call Brahman formless;
I know: your learning lies in invented shastras."

**82.** Hearing this, the brahmana bowed his head;
Jagadisha again began to speak to him:

**83.** "Listen, brahmana: Vedavyasa wrote in the Purana
what Parvati told King Suratha.[^04-10]

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Brahma-vaivarta Purana, in the Prakriti-khanda:*

**84.** *From Brahma down to a blade of grass, all is perishable;
eternal and true is the supreme Brahman — Krishna, beyond the gunas, the Infallible.*

**85.** From Brahma down to a blade of grass, all is perishable;
eternal and true is the supreme Brahman — Krishna, the Lord of all.

**86.** All that you see in the universe is the making of maya;
from Krishna's maya come Brahma and the other gods.

**87.** Krishna, beyond the gunas, the Infallible, is the father of all,
the Creator of all, the Destroyer of all, the Protector of all.

**88.** Parvati, O brahmana, told Suratha this truth;
the Devi instructed [him] to worship Sri Krishna.[^04-11]

**89.** The king says: 'Mother, I do not know Krishna;
you are the maker of the universe, the mother of the three gunas.'

**90.** The Devi says: 'Listen attentively, O king:
what you have said is not untrue.

*Tatha hi, in the same place:*

**91.** *I am the original of Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and the rest, higher than the highest,
with qualities and beyond qualities, the best, ever acting by my own will.*

**92.** *I am manifold: by a portion and by maya I am all women;
and I, O king, was created by Krishna through the play of His eyebrows.*

**93.** *Through the play of His eyebrows He created the great Virat among purushas,
in the pores of whose skin the universes eternally abide.*[^04-12]

**94.** I am the original of Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and the rest,
with qualities and beyond qualities, higher than the highest, acting by my own will.

**95.** Through kalas and amshas I take many forms;
all women are my own form.

**96.** Such as I am, Vamshidhara created me
through the play of His eyebrows — know this, O best of kings.

**97.** This you have heard; now I shall tell you something more:
the Virat Purusha is an avatara of Maha-Vishnu.

**98.** Him Krishna created through the play of His eyebrows;
in the pores of His skin this universe ever abides.'

**99.** The conversation that took place between Parvati and King Suratha —
that conversation I have now told you.

**100.** I shall tell something more, best of brahmanas — you listen:
what Arjuna said to Sri Krishna.

*Tatha hi, in the Srimad Bhagavad-gita, in the eleventh chapter:*

**101.** *You are the original God, the ancient Purusha, You are the supreme refuge of this universe.
You are the knower and the knowable, and the supreme abode; by You the universe is pervaded, O You of endless forms.*[^04-13]

**102.** *You are Vayu, Yama, Agni, Varuna, the moon, Prajapati and the great-grandsire.
Obeisance, obeisance to You a thousand times, and again, and yet again — obeisance, obeisance to You!*

**103.** *Obeisance to You from in front and from behind, obeisance to You from every side, O All!
Infinite is Your might, immeasurable Your valour; You pervade all, and therefore You are all.*

**104.** The original Purusha of all the gods, the Ancient One,
You are the place where this universe dissolves.[^04-14]

**105.** You are the support of the world, the one thing to be known;
You are that very cause, manifest before the eyes in form.[^04-15]

**106.** You alone have pervaded this universe;
bearer of endless forms, You are beyond perception.

**107.** Vayu, Yama, Agni, Varuna, the lord of the night;
the father of Brahma's father — who can understand Your ways?[^04-16]

**108.** Obeisance, obeisance, O Mahaprabhu, obeisance again and again,
thousands upon thousands of times, again and again — obeisance.[^04-17]

**109.** Your power is immeasurable, no one can measure it;
You abide in all beings, within and without.

**110.** Gold is one — the difference lies in the shaping of many forms:
You are all forms, as the Veda says."

**111.** This is what Arjuna said in the Bharata;
hearing it, the brahmana's mind was turned.

**112.** He bows down to Jagadisha [prostrate] with eight limbs
and says: "Deliver me, placing your feet on my head."

**113.** Prabhu says: "Why do you speak to me in this way?
Worship Sri Krishna, leaving the formless.

**114.** Apart from Krishna the living being has no other refuge;
with one mind worship His feet."

**115.** The brahmana says: "I have understood: you are a part of Krishna.[^04-18]
Be merciful: I take shelter of you.

**116.** By your mercy my evil-mindedness has been shattered;
Prabhu, teach me what is to be done and what is not.

**117.** I have no knowledge of the truth, I am wicked;
your descent is to make the living beings know the truth."

*Tatha hi:*

**118.** *To those whose minds are drawn by sense objects he gives knowledge of the truth;
Sri Jagadisha, the very form of Ishvara, I worship day and night.*

**119.** "All the living beings whose minds are drawn by sense objects
have fallen into the well of worldly existence.

**120.** Taking pity on those beings, Prabhu,
you delivered them, giving knowledge of the truth.

**121.** You are the very form of Ishvara, a treasure-house of mercy;
have mercy: [let me] serve your feet without cease.

**122.** Bring me to the goal of life, giving me the shade of your feet;
in compassion make me know the truth of Sri Krishna.

**123.** Be merciful, Jagadisha, deliverer of the world:
apart from your lotus feet there is no other refuge."

**124.** Saying this, the brahmana fell at Prabhu's feet;
Prabhu embraced him with a merciful heart.

**125.** Comforting him, Prabhu seated him at his side
and speaks to him words for his good in the sweetest tones.

**126.** Prabhu says: "Listen, O son of a brahmana:
whoever, born a human being, does not worship Krishna —

**127.** his birth is wasted, in vain he comes and goes;
I count such a fallen wretch among the beasts."

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Padma Purana:*

**128.** *Even a chandala devoted to bhakti for Vishnu is the best of sages;
and a twice-born devoid of bhakti for Vishnu is lower than a dog-eater.*

**129.** A chandala becomes the best if he is a devotee of Krishna;
a brahmana is called low if he does not worship Krishna.

**130.** Thus in the Uttara-khanda the great Shiva,
the lord, tells Parvati the truth of Krishna.

**131.** Hearing this, the brahmana's son says:
"Be merciful to me: my days are passing in vain."

**132.** Then Jagadisha Prabhu showed great mercy:
he explained to him all the teaching on the truth of Krishna.

**133.** Having obtained Krishna-prema and bhakti, that best of the twice-born,
his life fulfilled, went to his own home.

**134.** In this way Jagadisha, establishing bhakti,
delivered all worldly people.

**135.** The life of Jagadisha is made of millions of oceans of nectar;
were it described in detail, the book would grow beyond measure.

**136.** For fear of enlarging the book, I have written briefly;
the discussion of Brahman I have described here in brief.

**137.** Srila Bhagavatananda Prabhu is full of mercy;
this Ananda places hope in his feet.

**138.** In a dream that Prabhu commanded [me] by a revelation (pratyadesha);
by the strength of his command I have described a little.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the fourth varna, called "The Account of the Discussion of Brahman".*

## Varna 5. The Description of Sri Jagadisha's Wedding

*The birth of Mahesha, Jagadisha's younger brother; the fame of Jagadisha's learning; the poor brahmana Tapana asks Kamalaksha to take his daughter Duhkhini as Jagadisha's wife; the casting of horoscopes and the betrothal; the pre-wedding rites in both houses; the bridegroom's procession; the pandits' dispute in the assembly at Tapana's house and Jagadisha's prayer for Krishna's descent; the kanyadana; the night in the bride's house and the return home; the author's signature*

*The fifth varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, glory to the merciful one!
Show mercy to me, a sinner, becoming kind.

**2.** Thus the Prabhu constantly expounded the shastras;
seeing [this], the venerable Bandya rejoiced at heart.

**3.** Kamalaksha and Bhagyavati, happy in mind,
constantly sit apart and speak of their son's virtues.

**4.** Both, happy in mind, serve Sri Vishnu;
hearing their son praised, they are glad at heart.

**5.** Thus to Bhagyavati, after some time,
by Krishna's mercy another son was born.

**6.** With love they named him Mahesha —
him whom the three worlds call mad with prema for Krishna.[^05-1]

**7.** In character he became like Jagadisha;
seeing [this], his father and mother were greatly pleased.

**8.** Prabhu Jagadisha taught him the shastras,
and Mahesha came to have virtues equal to his [own].

**9.** Having gained two sons, Kamalaksha and Bhagyavati
were both in great happiness, joyful in mind.

**10.** The Prabhu's learning became known everywhere;
all say: "Jagadisha is a supreme pandita."

**11.** Panditas from every land come and debate [with him];
learning the truth about Krishna, they all become Vaishnavas.

**12.** Thus, delighting in learning, revealing the explanation of bhakti,
he reads the Gita and the Bhagavata.
Seeing his many virtues, all the people of that village
come and gather around him every day.

**13.** The Prabhu pleases everyone with kind words, as befits each,
and at this everyone rejoices.
Someone says to Kamalaksha: "Get your son married:
the time of his youth has come."

**14.** Hearing this, the best of the twice-born says: "All of you, give your blessings
that my son may remain in well-being.
What is ordained by the Lord, what will be — that must be [done];
for that reason I am making efforts."

**15.** In this way all the villagers come [to him],
won over by Jagadisha's virtues.
By providence, one day there
arrived a brahmana named Tapana.

**16.** Of a noble family, very gentle, living in that same land,
a devotee of Vishnu, wholly given to bhakti.
Kamalaksha, seeing him, with great respect
offered him a seat.

**17.** Prabhu Jagadisha, seeing [him], became supremely happy,
rose and bowed down to him.
Recognizing him as a Vaishnava, the Prabhu holds ishta-goshthi [with him] —
he spoke much about Krishna.

**18.** Seeing Jagadisha's virtues, becoming supremely happy,
the jewel of the twice-born says to Kamalaksha:
"You are greatly fortunate: your son is a great soul;
I take him to be like a god."

**19.** Hearing from the brahmana's mouth his son's fame, Kamalaksha rejoiced greatly
and says to him:
"By the strength of the mercy of you all, every perfection [comes] with ease —
this is my conviction.

**20.** With whom brahmanas are angry, with him Krishna is not pleased —
so it is proclaimed in the Puranas.
That there be no offence [of mine] before a brahmana —
this I take care to avoid."

**21.** The brahmana says: "Listen: your son is a knower of dharma,
in whom from an early age there is good conduct.
As the fruit of worshipping Vishnu you obtained this son;
through him [you] will cross [the ocean of] existence."

**22.** Kamalaksha says to him: "What children have you?
Tell me in detail, wise one."
The brahmana says: "One daughter; besides her there is no other;
on her account I am sorely afflicted.

**23.** Seeing that she has reached the age of a bride, neither food nor water pleases [me];
I am not able to give her in marriage.
For one who has no means, even to die is a blessing:
in this world he is greatly despised.

**24.** Is there any such friend, to whom I might go,
[who] would deliver [me] from the burden of a daughter?
In the forest fire of the burden of a daughter my mind is always burning;
because of it I have become greatly distressed.

**25.** Having considered in my heart, I have come to your house,
knowing your nature, moist with compassion.
Save me in this calamity: command Jagadisha —
let him accept my daughter.

**26.** Then [my] caste and family will remain; otherwise I am ruined —
I cannot discharge my worldly duty.
I have no means at all — only the giving away of my daughter:
understanding this, you will give me your answer."

**27.** Hearing his words, taking to heart the brahmana's welfare,
Kamalaksha asks him:
"What is your daughter's name? I shall make a calculation and see;
then I can give [you] assurance."

**28.** The brahmana says: "O abode of virtues, the daughter's name is Duhkhini:
I gave it after examining [her] rashi and lagna.
This is what I think: Jagadisha is a mine of virtues;
marrying him, she will become happy."[^05-2]

**29.** Then the Bandya, laying out the chalk, calculates the rashi, gana and jati —
the signs of union of son and daughter-in-law.
Having calculated, the twice-born learned: like the sun and the lotus
is the wondrous match of the two.[^05-3]

**30.** Knowing all the auspicious signs, he says: "Listen, Tapana:
this I accept.
The wedding of Jagadisha with your daughter —
the match is firmly settled.

**31.** Whatever will be required of me I shall give in full;
now do not worry any more."
Then, fixing an auspicious lagna, the brahmana went to his own home,
his body filled with joy.

**32.** Going home, he speaks lovingly to the brahmani:
"Listen, dear one, my afflicted beauty:[^05-4]
today I have arranged a match for [our] daughter Duhkhini —
here I have come, having fixed the lagna.

**33.** Nearby is the house of the Bandyaghati of Gayaghar,
an excellent twice-born named Kamalaksha.[^05-5]
Jagadisha is his son, endowed with every good quality,
charming, supremely handsome.

**34.** His mother is Bhagyavati; you will be able to recognize her, chaste one:
you have seen her many times.
That twice-born Kamalaksha, foremost among the compassionate of heart,
showed mercy on seeing [my] distress."

**35.** The whole story from beginning to end, all that had happened there,
he told to his wife.
Hearing all this, the mother of Duhkhini Devi
forgot herself in joy.

**36.** Then, after some days, in accordance with that lagna,
seeing a day with an auspicious moment,
Kamalaksha, king of the twice-born, invited everyone —
the society of his own kinsfolk.

**37.** Bhagyavati with respect brought all the brahmanas' wives
joyfully to her house.
Coming, the brahmana women, joyful in mind,
anoint Jagadisha with turmeric.

**38.** They give [him] the "aiburo-bhat", as before a wedding
is the custom among people;[^05-6]
with various foods and drinks the brahmanas and good people
they feed in many ways.

**39.** After this the twice-born all with respect
invite Prabhu Jagadisha [to their homes].
According to rule the brahmanas honour the Prabhu
with cloth, garlands and various gifts.

**40.** Then the Bandya, joyful, arranging a splendid assembly,
brought his own friends.
Entering the assembly, in rows as befitted each,
all the brahmanas and good people sit.

**41.** With fragrant garlands and sandal paste he honoured everyone
and humbly offered betel nut and pan.
All the gathering of brahmanas, all delighted,
rose and went off to their homes.

**42.** All the articles for the adhivasa, clothes, so many ornaments,
he sent to Tapana's house.[^05-7]
Seeing [them], greatly satisfied, that pure-minded twice-born
with great joy took everything into [his] house.

**43.** Here the twice-born Kamalaksha the auspicious rite of nandimukha
carefully completed.[^05-8]
With snacks, dishes, food and drink his relatives, kinsfolk
and [the whole] assembly he honoured as befitted.

**44.** Then a barber came, sat down and shaved [the bridegroom],
and dresses the bridegroom as is proper.
Then Prabhu Jagadisha puts on the splendid attire of a bridegroom —
clothes and ornaments, the beauty of his body;
a fine, charming topor the Prabhu puts on his head,
on his forehead a tilaka that captivates the mind.[^05-9]

**45.** Then Kamalaksha Bandya, bringing many kinds of instruments,
holds a varied auspicious festival.
Khamak, tamak, bheri, madal, sanai, turi,
damama and jay-dhol resound.[^05-10]

**46.** They sing in pairs and [strike] the kanshi, some [play] the vina, some the flute —
playing, they walk ahead.[^05-11]
They play with raybansh staves, [sound] the tali, rejoice with cries of "Jaya!" —
rockets, danka drums, chamaras, banners.[^05-12]

**47.** Dancing girls, musicians and bhats sing songs and dance;
jesters clown and pull faces.
"Svasti, svasti!" [people] call out; the twice-born — the sound of the Vedas;
they proclaim "Jaya, jaya!" and auspicious [words].

**48.** The women perform auspicious rites according to family custom
to the ululation and the sound of conches.
Remembering Sri Hari in his mind, mounted a splendid conveyance
Prabhu Jagadisha at the auspicious moment.

**49.** Taking along the purohita and all his kinsfolk and relatives,
Kamalaksha in supreme joy
sets out to marry his son — with much merriment and tumult,
with various poems, in special joy.

**50.** Lighting torches, they hold them up and make various illuminations;
[they let off] all kinds of fireworks.
Joyfully going round the town, the bridegroom's procession in great merriment
moves on — the people [go] with a great clamour.

**51.** Over there, in Tapana's house, seated in the assembly
are all the brahmanas and good people.
All the members of the procession came and arrived,
when the assembly was [already] present.

**52.** Seeing [them], the twice-born Tapana, leaving his own seat,
stood before the assembly.
Bowing to everyone, he offers many kinds of praise
and with humility pleased them all.

**53.** Each according to what he was, greeting him fittingly,
he seated on an excellent seat.
Each, as befitted him, sits in his own place,
according to the rules of courteous salutation.

**54.** The ghatakas sat down in a crowd, covering their foreheads with tilaka marks,
and hold much discussion of lineages.
All read out genealogies, beating their hands again and again —
a din arose: "jhan, jhan."[^05-13]

**55.** The adhyapakas, the bhattacharyas, all the honourable panditas,
seated, discuss topics of the shastras.
And then, as they went on speaking, over the understanding of word and meaning
a dispute grew there.

**56.** One plays a trick, another fills out his conclusion,
yet another says: "No, no!"
Again someone says: "Why, that is not so:
there is a double meaning — listen, I will tell you."

**57.** Speaking loudly at him, another comes forward,
displaying his learning with arrogance.
In such a loud uproar — the words "ghatatva", "patatva";
no one can defeat anyone.[^05-14]

**58.** Some determine Brahman, some refute [it];
some say "it is", some — "it is not".
All the Mayavadis expound the bhashya on Vedanta;
Sankhya, Nyaya — whatever comes [to mind] to whom.

**59.** Opinions invented outside the shastras, prose, verse, all kinds of metres —
all recite various compositions.
Intoxicated with pride of learning, rising from their seats,
they talk of all sorts of things.[^05-15]

**60.** The old panditas, addressing another,
[try] to say: "O Vidyavagisha!" —
but at the word "O" they cough, "khoh, khoh" says the throat,
and "Vidyavagisha" stays in the mind.

**61.** As they speak, their heads shake — and still they do not settle down,
in their absorption in past cultivation of learning.
They have no strength, [yet] they are proud: lest anyone call [them] small —
such is the conceit in their every hope.

**62.** The lord of panditas, Prabhu Jagadisha, seeing [this],
remembered Hari in his mind.
He says: "Krishna, in how many days will You descend
and deliver all these ill-minded people?"[^05-16]

**63.** Thus the host of twice-born debate various shastras —
in the assembly, the semblance of a storm.
No one desists; frenzied in debate,
extremely restless, irrepressible.

**64.** The auspicious lagna has arrived; knowing [this], Tapana Pandita
says with respect to the brahmanas:
"Listen, all you gentlemen: the lagna of the wedding is passing;
if you permit, I shall perform the kanyadana."

**65.** Hearing Tapana's words, with cries of "Good, good!"
[all] calmed down and sat on their seats.
Thereupon the twice-born Tapana, performing the varana of Sri Guru,
performed the varana of the purohita.[^05-17]

**66.** All the other brahmanas with splendid garlands and sandal paste
he worshipped and took [their] permission.
Sri Tapana, jewel of the twice-born, bringing his daughter Duhkhini,
in the midst of the assembly makes obeisance.

**67.** Uttering Vedic mantras, taking Jagadisha's hand,
at the auspicious lagna he gave away his daughter.
Then, performing again the women's rites and completing the kushandika,
they carry out [all] the rites according to rule.[^05-18]

**68.** Auspicious instruments sound, the women raise a clamour,
the brahmanas sound the Vedas.
Joyfully welcoming the son-in-law with the daughter according to the rite,
Tapana's brahmani took [them] into the house.

**69.** With Duhkhini Devi on his left, the crest-jewel of [all] couples,
Srila Jagadisha sat.[^05-19]
How charming, how beautiful [his] golden-hued body!
Seeing [it], everyone became joyful.

**70.** All the young women lose their hearts at the sight [of him] —
such is the splendour of [his] delightful form.
The ladies make merry with the Prabhu:
under various pretexts — laughter and jests.

**71.** Understanding what the occasion called for, the Prabhu too, in the same way,
[replies] wittily to all.
Thus in such talk with the women
the night passed in conversation.

**72.** Rising at dawn, then, in a festival of joy and auspiciousness,
Kamalaksha, taking his son and daughter-in-law,
together with his companions, in joyful array and merriment,
arrived at his own home.

**73.** Seeing [them], Bhagyavati Devi, the chaste lady, quickly rose and came out
and joyfully took the bride on her lap.
Holding her son's lotus hand, together [with them] the brahmana's wife
brought [them] into her own chambers.[^05-20]

**74.** The brahmana women came and merrily give wedding gifts,
all together give their blessings.
Placing paddy and durva grass on [their] heads, all praising [them],
they went off to their own homes.

**75.** Whatever family customs there are after a wedding —
they gradually completed them all.
Kamalaksha and Bhagyavati, together with their son and daughter-in-law,
thus lived on in household life.

**76.** This auspicious wedding of Sri Jagadisha I
have described in brief.
My Vaishnava Thakuras, forgive [my] offences;
never take as a fault [whatever is amiss] in this.

**77.** Srila Bhagavatananda is the root of supreme bliss;
placing hope in his lotus feet,
the Prabhu's wedding pastime, as if in a sutra, was described
by the poor, lowly Ananda Dasa.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the fifth varna, called "The Description of Sri Jagadisha's Wedding".*

[^05-6]: The edition reads «আই বড়ভাওদেব» (*āi baṛabhāodeba*); taken as «আইবড়ভাত দেন» (*āibaṛabhāt den*), "they give the aiburo-bhat" — the farewell meal that relatives and neighbours give a bridegroom or bride before the wedding (cf. 39).

[^05-7]: Adhivasa — the rite of consecration on the eve of a wedding; the articles for it are sent from the bridegroom's house to the bride's.

[^05-8]: Nandimukha (nandimukha-shraddha) — an offering to the ancestors before an auspicious rite, including a wedding.

[^05-9]: Topor (টোপর) — the tall conical headdress of a Bengali bridegroom. In the edition this unit has nine half-lines before «॥»: the first triplet ("Then a barber came … as is proper") has no rhyming partner — perhaps a line has been lost.

[^05-10]: Tamak, bheri, madal, damama and jay-dhol are drums and kettledrums; the turi is a trumpet; the sanai is an oboe-like wind instrument; the khamak is a plucked instrument with a string stretched over a small drum.

[^05-11]: «যোড় গাই» (*yoṛ gāi*) — the reading is unclear; taken as "they sing in pairs". Kanshi (কাঁশী) — a gong or cymbal of bell-metal; the vina is a stringed instrument.

[^05-12]: Raybansh (রায় বাঁশ) — a long bamboo staff skilfully twirled in a procession; tali (টালী) — apparently small cymbals or hand-clapping. «বান» (*bān*) is taken as «বাণ» (*bāṇ*), "rockets, fireworks"; the danka is a large drum; chamaras are yak-tail whisks.

[^05-13]: Ghatakas — matchmaker-genealogists, keepers of the pedigrees of brahmana families; at a wedding they recite the genealogies (*vaṁśāvalī*) of bride and bridegroom.

[^05-14]: The edition reads «খটত্ব পটত্ব» (*khaṭatva paṭatva*); taken as «ঘটত্ব পটত্ব» (*ghaṭatva paṭatva*), "pot-ness, cloth-ness" — stock terms of Navya-nyaya that became a byword for scholastic hair-splitting.

[^05-15]: «নিরাসনে» (*nirāsane*, "without a seat") — taken as "rising from their seats".

[^05-16]: Cf. Advaita, who called on Krishna to descend for the deliverance of the living beings (CC Adi 3; CB Adi 2).

[^05-17]: The edition reads «ধনী» (*dhanī*); taken as «ধ্বনি» (*dhvani*), "cry". Varana — the ceremonial engaging and honouring of the guru and the officiating priest (purohita) with gifts before a rite.

[^05-18]: The women's rites (*strī-ācāra*) — wedding customs performed by the women. Kushandika — the wedding fire sacrifice (homa).

[^05-19]: The edition reads «স্ববাগে» (*svabāge*); taken as «স্ববামে» (*svabāme*), "on his own left": at a Bengali wedding the bride sits to the left of the bridegroom.

[^05-20]: The edition reads «সমিভ্যারে» (*samibhyāre*); taken as «সমভিব্যাহারে» (*samabhivyāhāre*), "together, accompanied by".


## Varna 6. The Description of Sri Jagadisha's Coming to Sri Navadvipa

*Grown old, Kamalaksha and Bhagyavati ask their son to show them the way to Krishna's feet; Jagadisha instructs them in hearing and chanting according to the "Bhagavata", reads the "Bhagavata" to them, and they depart for the abode of Vishnu; Duhkhini is consoled; the shraddha in a tulasi grove before the shalagrama-shila, pindas of maha-prasada and the feeding of Vaishnava brahmanas; grieving for people turned away from Krishna, Jagadisha resolves to live on the bank of the Ganga; Duhkhini's consent, the farewell to the neighbours, Mahesha goes with them; on the seventh day of the journey they reach the Ganga, and Jagadisha settles in Navadvipa, where the Lord is to appear; the author's signature*

*The sixth varna*

**1.** Countless are your wondrous deeds, Prabhu;
they are known among the saintly.

**2.** Having heard all those accounts from the mouths of the saintly,
for my own purification I write down a little with great joy.

**3.** Thus Jagadisha performs various pastimes;
seeing [this], Kamalaksha floats in an ocean of bliss.

**4.** One day Bhagyavati says to her husband:
"Hear my words, O Bandya of great mind.

**5.** My son Jagadisha is full of every virtue;
so too the daughter-in-law is an abode of virtues.

**6.** The Creator has given [us] a son and a daughter-in-law after our heart;
this has become the very limit of worldly happiness.

**7.** Old age has come upon you and me;
now consider how we may cross over [the ocean of] existence.

**8.** The mind is always sunk in worldly pleasures;
not even once does remembrance of Krishna's feet arise.

**9.** Without remembrance of Sri Krishna the time passes in vain;
how will remembrance of Krishna come — what means shall we take?"

**10.** Hearing this, Kamalaksha was greatly pleased
and began to say this to Bhagyavati:

**11.** "Listen, dear one: blessed, blessed is your life —
you have made me remember Sri Krishna.

**12.** I too think within myself all the time:
how can the mind be bound to Sri Krishna's feet?

**13.** Come, let us both go and ask Jagadisha
by what way the feet of Sri Krishna are attained."

**14.** Having resolved thus, the two, in great joy,
went to Jagadisha and began to speak:

**15.** "Listen, my son: we have both grown old;
how may we now attain the feet of Sri Krishna?

**16.** Tell us both the means:
we both shall act according to it."

**17.** Hearing this, Jagadisha was delighted
and, becoming attentive, began to speak thus:

**18.** "Listen, father and mother, to my words:
there are endless practices of sadhana for attaining Krishna.

**19.** But all of them are extremely difficult:
in the Kali-yuga man is unable to accomplish them.

**20.** There is one sadhana that is supremely easy,
by which the feet of Sri Krishna are attained with ease.

**21.** When in the Dvapara-yuga Sri Krishna descended,
Brahma came to Dvaraka and offered Him praise.

**22.** In that praise there is one verse;
to that verse people pay no attention.

**23.** Whoever performs sadhana according to that verse
easily crosses over [the ocean of] existence and attains the feet of Sri Krishna.

*Tatha hi, in the Srimad Bhagavata, in the Eleventh Canto, the words of Sri Brahma to Sri Krishna:*

**24.** *Your deeds, O Lord — the saintly people in the Kali-yuga,
hearing and chanting them, will easily cross beyond darkness.*[^06-1]

**25.** Brahma says: 'Listen, Krishna, I make a humble submission:
Your descent is for the deliverance of living beings.

**26.** The deeds You have performed after descending —
by constantly hearing and chanting them,

**27.** all the saintly people in the Kali-yuga will without effort
cross over [the ocean of] existence and attain Your feet.'

**28.** Brahma has declared this supreme sadhana;
practise it, both of you, and you will attain the feet of Sri Krishna.

**29.** I submit something more; listen, father and mother:
again the "Bhagavata" speaks of sadhana.

*Tatha hi, in the same place, in the Second Canto:*

**30.** *Whoever constantly hears with faith and chants His deeds —
into his heart, in no long time, Bhagavan enters.*[^06-2]

**31.** Whoever hears with faith of Sri Krishna's deeds
and, being diligent, performs the sankirtana of the Name —

**32.** into his heart in a short time
Bhagavan enters; of this there is no doubt."

**33.** Jagadisha says: "This the 'Bhagavata' declares
to be the supreme means of sadhana in the Kali-yuga.

**34.** You too, with firm faith in these words,
hear of Sri Krishna's deeds and say 'Hari, Hari!'

**35.** With the mood of bhakti always hear and chant,
and without effort you both will attain the feet of Sri Krishna."

**36.** Hearing this, his father and mother said to him:
"Tell us of Sri Krishna."

**37.** Then Prabhu Jagadisha [began] to recite to them both
the Srimad Bhagavata, explaining [it] in many ways.

**38.** Thus, having heard all the verses,
both, their life fulfilled, departed for the abode of Vishnu.

**39.** In separation from his father and mother the Pandita Thakura,
losing all composure, grieved greatly.

**40.** At home Duhkhini Devi weeps aloud;
who has the power to describe that lament?

**41.** After some time the Prabhu regained his composure
and began to speak to Duhkhini Devi:

**42.** "Listen, dear one: this world is fashioned by maya;
know for certain that everything in it is perishable.

**43.** By Krishna's maya the birth, life and dissolution of living beings
come about in the course of time, according to each one's own karma.

**44.** Thus the living being continually comes and goes in this world
as long as it does not obtain the company of Krishna's devotees.

**45.** By the mercy of Krishna's devotee comes bhakti for Krishna;
when there is bhakti for Krishna, all misfortune is dispelled.

**46.** Consider, dear one, in your own heart:
who else is as merciful as Krishna?

**47.** If one utters His name on any pretext whatever,
even a great sinner is liberated — so say the Puranas.

**48.** Ajamila is the witness to this: his own son
he called, saying 'Narayana!', at the time of dying.

**49.** By that he attained the abode of Vishnu.
Who can say how great is the fruit of Krishna's name?

*Tatha hi, in the Srimad Bhagavata:*

**50.** *Even Ajamila, dying, uttering the name of Hari addressed to his son,
attained the abode; how much more one who utters [it] with faith!*[^06-3]

**51.** My father and mother are associates of Krishna:
of their own will they took birth upon the earth.

**52.** In many ways the two worshipped Sri Krishna;
when their time was complete, they went to the abode of Sri Krishna.

**53.** Never grieve for them;
giving up grief, worship Sri Krishna.

**54.** Radha and Krishna are the original father and mother of all;
I have told [you] this essence of all truths.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Narada-pancharatra:*

**55.** *Sri Krishna is the father of the worlds, the mother of the world is Radhika.*[^06-4]

**56.** Thus Jagadisha consoled [her];
hearing [him], Duhkhini Devi became calm.

**57.** Then the Prabhu, gathering various provisions,
invited the brahmanas and worthy people.

**58.** The Prabhu considers within himself:
"I shall perform the shraddha and the other rites in a tulasi grove."

**59.** The shraddha should by all means be performed in a tulasi grove;
the injunction for this is in the Skanda Purana.

*Tatha hi, in the same, in the dialogue of Brahma and Narada:*

**60.** *Where the shade born of a tulasi grove falls, O twice-born,
there the shraddha should be offered for the satisfaction of the forefathers.*

**61.** *Where a multitude of tulasi seeds falls, O Narada,
a pinda offered there is an imperishable gift to the forefathers.*

**62.** On the eleventh day the Prabhu, in the tulasi grove,
with a joyful heart prepared the place for the shraddha.

**63.** Around it he arranged excellent seats
for the brahmanas and worthy people to sit.

**64.** The brahmanas and worthy people arrived,
and all sat down on the seats, each as befitted him.

**65.** Having performed according to rule the rites proper to the time,
the Prabhu came and sat down to perform the shraddha.

**66.** Having the shalagrama-shila brought there,
the Prabhu first worshipped it on behalf of the forefathers.

**67.** On behalf of the forefathers one should worship Sri Krishna;
the proof of this is the word of the Puranas.

*Tatha hi, in the same:*

**68.** *Men who have worshipped Keshava on behalf of the forefathers,
leaving behind the torments of hell, attain liberation, O great sage.*[^06-5]

**69.** *Blessed in the world are those men, especially in the age of Kali,
who daily worship Hari for the sake of the forefathers, O sage.*

**70.** *What [use] are many pindas, the shraddha at Gaya and the rest, O sage, [to those]
by whom Hari is worshipped with bhakti day by day for the sake of the forefathers?*

**71.** After this the Prabhu chose the family priest, the purohita,
and attentively honoured the brahmanas.

**72.** Then, in that place, brahmanas devoted to Vishnu
recite the Gita and the Srimad Bhagavata.

**73.** A circle of Vaishnavas with khols and karatalas
sings of Sri Krishna's pastimes, full of supreme rasa.

**74.** There Jagadisha gave various gifts
and began the shraddha near the shalagrama.

*Tatha hi, in the same, in the dialogue of Sri Shiva and Skanda:*

**75.** *The man who performs the shraddha near a shalagrama —
his forefathers remain in heaven, satisfied, for a hundred kalpas.*

**76.** *Forefathers once satisfied by a pinda before a shalagrama-shila
dwell [there], and there is no counting [that time].*

**77.** Having performed the shraddha rite, at the end he offered the pindas
of Sri maha-prasada, as there is proof for it.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Padma Purana:*

**78.** *With food offered to Vishnu one should honour the other gods,
and it should also be given to the forefathers — it yields an imperishable [fruit].*

**79.** In this way the Prabhu completed the shraddha rite;
as dakshina he gave gold coins to the priest.

**80.** Brahmanas marked with the sign of the conch and wearing the urdhva-pundra
the Prabhu feeds in every way.

**81.** Whoever brings home a Vaishnava brahmana whose body is marked with the conch
and feeds him —

**82.** his food Bhagavan Himself eats
together with the forefathers — so say the Puranas.

*Tatha hi, in the Brahma Purana, in the dialogue of Brahma and Narada:*

**83.** *In whose house a brahmana whose body is marked with the conch eats —
that food Keshava Himself eats together with the forefathers.*

**84.** Whoever at a shraddha feeds a brahmana wearing the urdhva-pundra,
with him the forefathers are pleased for millions of kalpas.

*Tatha hi, in the Padma Purana, in the dialogue of Shiva and Parvati:*

**85.** *Whoever at a shraddha feeds a brahmana wearing the urdhva-pundra, O dear one —
his forefathers are pleased for millions of kalpas; there is no doubt.*

**86.** Krishna says: "Brahmanas wearing the urdhva-pundra —
in whose house they eat food,

**87.** forty generations of his family from hell
I will surely deliver — know this for certain."

*Tatha hi, in the same, the words of Sri Bhagavan:*

**88.** *In whose house a brahmana wearing the urdhva-pundra eats food —
forty generations of his family I lift out of hell.*

**89.** His relatives, household and neighbours —
all of them Jagadisha, a treasury of virtues, fed.

**90.** To the brahmanas he gave cloth, money, metal vessels and so on;
with sweet words the Prabhu satisfied everyone.

**91.** After that Jagadisha held a mahotsava:
he fed the Vaishnavas with various dishes.

**92.** In this way Jagadisha, with great joy in his heart,
completed the shraddha rite with sankirtana of the Name.

**93.** I have described it only briefly, like a sutra;
may the devotees not hold this against [me] as an offence.

**94.** After this Jagadisha, together with Duhkhini,
lives at home, always engaged in bhajana of Krishna:

**95.** hearing and chanting Krishna's name, qualities and pastimes,
meditation on Krishna's lotus feet, worship and offering prayers.

**96.** In this way the Prabhu practises the limbs of bhakti,
and Duhkhini always serves the Prabhu.

**97.** But seeing all the people turned away from Krishna,
the Prabhu is extremely sorrowful at heart.

**98.** The Prabhu decided: "I will not remain in this land;
I shall find out what Duhkhini thinks."

**99.** One day, at night, in the bedchamber
the Prabhu is spending [time] playfully with Duhkhini.

**100.** At that time the Prabhu says: "Listen, Duhkhini:
my father and mother have gone to the other world.

**101.** None of my kinsfolk are here;
therefore I will by no means remain in this land.

**102.** Your father and mother are living;
I shall leave you in their care and go."

**103.** Hearing these words, the Devi says in sorrow:
"By my fate, Prabhu, you have given this command.

**104.** I have worshipped your lotus feet with great care —
why then have you still not accepted me?

**105.** What offence have I committed at [your] holy feet,
that you will leave me and go elsewhere?"

**106.** Having said this, the Devi fell silent in sorrow.
Hearing her, Prabhu Jagadisha began to speak:

**107.** "Hear my words; do not be downcast:
you and I are one soul, only the bodies are different.[^06-6]

**108.** Whoever sees a difference between you and me
will never be worthy of my mercy.

**109.** The wish has arisen in me not to stay in this land;
tell me plainly what wish is in your heart."

**110.** Duhkhini says: "Prabhu, I am not separate [from you]:
wherever you yourself keep [me], there I shall stay."

**111.** When Duhkhini had spoken these words,
Prabhu Jagadisha began to speak:

**112.** "Listen, dear one, I shall tell you the essence of this truth;
reflect on it within your mind.

**113.** After many births the human body is obtained;
whoever in it is intelligent worships Krishna.

**114.** One should now dwell in that place
by living in which one obtains bhakti for Krishna.

**115.** The Naradiya Purana says this:
one should by all means live on the bank of the Bhagirathi.

*Tatha hi, in the same:*

**116.** *What [use] is the eightfold yoga, what are austerities, what are sacrifices?
Living by the Ganga alone is the cause of the knowledge of Brahman.*[^06-7]

**117.** In the Kali-yuga sacrifice, yoga and austerity are nothing;
by merely living on the bank of the Ganga the knowledge of Brahman arises.

**118.** By the word "Brahman" I mean Krishna; knowledge of the truth about Him
comes from living by the Ganga — so say the Puranas.

**119.** The glory of the Ganga is that she is called the water that has washed Krishna's feet;
Shiva, holding her on his head, knows the limit of the truth about her.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Skanda Purana:*

**120.** *The greatness of the water that has washed [Vishnu's] feet is known to the god Shankara,
who holds on his head the Ganga fallen from the feet of Vishnu.*

**121.** Listen, I say more: in all the works of the Kali-yuga —
in almost all of them — there are hundreds upon hundreds of obstacles.

**122.** The name of Hari and bathing in the Ganga are free from hindrance:
in them there is no obstacle — so say the Puranas.

*Tatha hi, in the same, in the dialogue of Sri Brahma and Narada:*

**123.** *In the age of Kali all works are subject to hindrance, O great sage;
bathing in the Ganga and the name of Hari — these two are free from hindrance.*

**124.** So I have told you the whole truth;
leaving this land, dear one, let us go to the bank of the Ganga."

**125.** The Devi says: "Hear my answer, Prabhu:
whatever your command, that is my duty."

**126.** Hearing this, the Prabhu, pleased, says to her:
"I will surely settle on the bank of the Ganga.

**127.** Since those words have found a place in your heart,
I understand that Krishna's mercy has come upon you."

**128.** Duhkhini says: "I do not know what is good and what is bad;
my only hope is your lotus feet.

**129.** Wherever you go, there I shall surely go;
always keep Duhkhini at [your] crimson feet."

**130.** Knowing for certain the disposition of Duhkhini's mind,
the Prabhu was filled at heart with exceeding joy.

**131.** One day Jagadisha, in his own house,
called together his neighbours.

**132.** At the Prabhu's call all came and assembled;
the Prabhu humbly began to speak to them all:

**133.** "The wish to live on the bank of the Ganga has arisen in [my] heart;
be merciful, all of you, and give me [your] leave."

**134.** When Prabhu Jagadisha spoke these words,
the brahmanas, hearing them, became very sad.

**135.** With joined palms the brahmanas say before the Prabhu:
"Tell us, Prabhu, what means [of deliverance] will there be for us?"

**136.** The Prabhu says: "Do not be anxious;
constantly perform the sankirtana of Krishna's name.

**137.** Whomever you see, instruct him about Krishna;
in this way you will deliver this land."

**138.** All were in great sorrow at the separation from the Prabhu;
with nectarean words the Prabhu comforted them all.

**139.** Having received their leave, the Prabhu stayed some days more;
one day, at night, he says to Duhkhini:

**140.** "Tomorrow is an auspicious hour; the day is very good for setting out;
dear one, let us go from this land to the bank of the Ganga."

**141.** When Prabhu Jagadisha had said this,
Duhkhini Devi, hearing it, was delighted.

**142.** The Devi says: "Now my good fortune has come:
the Prabhu will take me with him to the bank of the Ganga.

**143.** There must be no delay now — let us go quickly.
Prabhu, tell this to Mahesha Pandita."

**144.** Then the Prabhu called his brother Mahesha
and told him that he was going to the bank of the Ganga.

**145.** Mahesha says: "I am your servant,
bound to your command, never independent.

**146.** Whatever your command, Prabhu, that I shall do;
wherever you tell me to live, there I shall live."

**147.** Hearing this from Mahesha's mouth,
the Prabhu embraced Mahesha with great happiness.

**148.** Rising in the morning, he set out together with Duhkhini;
Mahesha Pandita went behind him.

**149.** Leaving his native land, Jagadisha goes to the bank of the Ganga;
seeing this, the villagers are greatly grieved.

**150.** All weep aloud and lament,
saying: "Abandoning us, where are you [going]?"

**151.** Thus all the people of the village weep;
the Prabhu consoles them all with sweet words:

**152.** "I am not apart from you all for even a moment;
give me leave — I shall go and see the Ganga.

**153.** When one sees the Ganga, the mind turns to Krishna;
for this reason I go, leaving my home."

**154.** In this way Jagadisha makes them all understand;
all, grieved at heart, go to their own homes.

**155.** Having consoled them all, the Prabhu sets out from there;
Duhkhini and Mahesha follow behind him.

**156.** As they walked on and on, the sixth day of the journey came;
on the seventh day they arrived at the bank of the Ganga.

**157.** Having arrived, Prabhu Jagadisha reflects within himself:
"I have come to the bank of the Ganga — in what place shall I live?"

**158.** Having bathed in the Ganga, the Prabhu thinks within himself:
"Krishnachandra will descend in the Kali-yuga;

**159.** the Lord will be of golden complexion — so says the 'Bhagavata'.
[Would that] I might live in that place, if [it] were known for certain."[^06-8]

**160.** As he was thinking thus, the Prabhu came to know [it] in his mind,
and at that very moment he set out joyfully for Navadvipa.

**161.** Coming to the village of Navadvipa, he was filled with delight,
and in that place, with great happiness, he took up residence.[^06-9]

**162.** In this way Jagadisha came to Nadia,
and, having come to that land, he preached bhakti for Krishna.

**163.** Thus the Prabhu made his dwelling in Navadvipa;
by hearing of this, bhakti for Gaura's feet arises.

**164.** Prabhu Bhagavatananda is a mine of prema for Gaura;
day and night he is absorbed [in the company of] Gaura's devotees.

**165.** Above all, the life of Prabhu Jagadisha
he greatly loves to hear and chant.[^06-10]

**166.** By the strength of his command, placing hope in his feet,
this Ananda Dasa describes Jagadisha's pastimes.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the sixth varna, called "The Description of Sri Jagadisha's Coming to Sri Navadvipa".*

[^06-10]: The edition reads «প্রভুর রচিত» (*prabhur racita*, "composed by the Prabhu"); taken as «প্রভুর চরিত» (*prabhur carita*), "the life of the Prabhu".


## Varna 7. The Meeting of Sri Jagadisha with Sri Mahaprabhu

*Jagadisha lives in Navadvipa next to Jagannatha Mishra and prays to Krishna to descend; the Lord's appearance in Shachi's house; Duhkhini nurses the infant Nimai; Jagadisha and Hiranya Bhagavata keep Ekadashi to call Krishna down; the weeping Nimai demands their offering to Vishnu; on another Ekadashi the Lord eats the offering during Jagadisha's puja and reveals Himself to him; Duhkhini sees the four-armed form; the Lord calls them His associates from Dvapara; Jagadisha dances in prema; the kirtanas at Srivasa's house and the subduing of the Kazi; with the Lord's permission Jagadisha leaves Duhkhini with Mahesha and sets out for Nilachala; the author's signature*

*The seventh varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, glory to the ocean of mercy!
You are the message of deliverance for the living beings, the friend of the lowly.

**2.** Thus Prabhu Jagadisha in Navadvipa
took up residence near Jagannatha Mishra.[^07-1]

**3.** The wife of Mishra Thakura is Shachi Thakurani;
Duhkhini became very dear to her.

**4.** Thus Jagadisha, living in that place,
began to worship Krishna with body, words and mind.

**5.** The Prabhu worships Krishna with joy in his heart;
Srimati Duhkhini serves him.

**6.** The mind of Duhkhini Devi is wholly devoted to Krishna;
seeing [this], Prabhu Jagadisha is glad at heart.

**7.** Thus Jagadisha lives in Nadia;
seeing people turned away from Sri Krishna, he suffers.

**8.** Becoming compassionate towards all those living beings,
he ponders a means: how will they be saved?

**9.** Jagadisha says: "Krishna, descend
and deliver all these people who are turned away [from You].

**10.** Let all worship Your lotus feet —
this is my desire in body, mind and words."

**11.** Thus Jagadisha performs worship
and prays for the deliverance of the living beings.

**12.** In this way many days passed,
and Sri Jagadisha's desire was fulfilled.

**13.** To fulfil the desire of His devotee, the merciful Lord
Krishnachandra rose in Shachi's house.

**14.** Covering His black complexion, He became golden:
in that complexion He took on [the lustre of] jambunada gold.[^07-2]

**15.** The pastimes of that Lord are countless and boundless;
they are [described] in detail in the Chaitanya-charitamrita.

**16.** I have no power to describe them;
forgive me [this] offence, devotees of Gaura.

**17.** In Shachi's house Chaitanya Gosani descended;
His father gave Him the name Nimai.

**18.** Nimai's mother and Thakurani Duhkhini
are very happy on seeing each other.[^07-3]

**19.** The love between them is like that of two sisters born of one mother:
whoever does not know [them] thinks just that.

**20.** Shachi's son grew up supremely beautiful;
seeing [Him], Duhkhini rejoices boundlessly at heart.

**21.** Shachi Devi entrusted her son to Duhkhini
and with great affection began to say to her:

**22.** "This is my son, beautiful Nimai;
always keep Him close to you."

**23.** Hearing this from Shachi's holy lips, [Duhkhini] Devi
in supreme bliss took Nimai on her lap.

**24.** And so Duhkhini Devi, taking Nimai,
fondles and rears [Him], happy at heart.

**25.** Thus, her mind filled with tenderness for Nimai,
she rears Him, thinking Him an ordinary child.

**26.** [There was] a great-minded [brahmana] named Hiranya Bhagavata,
a supreme devotee of Krishna, living in Navadvipa.

**27.** With him Jagadisha, in the joy of prema,
constantly remains in talks about Krishna.

**28.** The two have one mind and one and the same bhajana;
both with love perform the worship of Sri Krishna.

**29.** For the sake of worldly pleasures the world is full of craving;
bewildered by maya, the living beings do not worship Krishna.

**30.** If they are admonished, no one listens;
to the one who admonishes they speak harsh words.

**31.** Thus, deluded by Vishnu's maya,
all the living beings remain, having forgotten Krishna.

**32.** For their sake both ponder:
in what way will they all be delivered?

**33.** "If Krishna comes and descends,
then the deliverance of all these living beings will take place.

**34.** Sri Krishna will come and deliver the living beings —
then indeed all these living beings will be delivered."

**35.** "We will bring Sri Krishna" — such is the desire of both;
they consider how [this] desire may be fulfilled.

**36.** Pondering and pondering, [they] remembered a verse,
and, reflecting on that verse, they found joy.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Brihan-naradiya Purana:*

**37.** *The vow called Ekadashi bestows the fruit of all desires;
brahmanas must keep it by all means — [it] is the cause of Vishnu's pleasure.*[^07-4]

**38.** Whoever among men keeps this vow of Ekadashi,
all his desires are fulfilled.

**39.** [It is] the duty of a brahmana, for the sake of Vishnu's pleasure;
from this all desires are fulfilled.

**40.** Having considered thus, both rejoiced
and with great bliss began [to keep] the vow of Ekadashi.

**41.** With devotion both keep the vow of Ekadashi;
its procedure is according to the shastras.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Vishnu Purana:*

**42.** *[Keeping] the Ekadashi of both fortnights, without food, concentrated,
having bathed according to the rules, in clean clothes, with senses controlled,*

**43.** *having worshipped Vishnu according to the rules with faith, deeply concentrated —
with flowers, fragrances, and likewise incense, lamps, excellent naivedya,*

**44.** *with many kinds of gifts, japa, homa and circumambulations,
with various hymns, with charming dance, song and music,*

**45.** *with prostrations like a rod, and likewise with excellent cries of "jaya" —
having thus worshipped [Him] according to the rules, let him keep vigil at night;*

**46.** *let one devoted to Vishnu tell narrations or sing songs —
[such] a man reaches the supreme abode of Vishnu: of this there is no doubt.*[^07-5]

**47.** In this way both keep the vow of Ekadashi
and worship Krishna according to the rules of the shastras.

**48.** With bows and praises both pray:
"Krishna, fulfil the desire of us both.

**49.** O Lord, come Yourself, descend,
and deliver the living beings bewildered by maya.

**50.** Let all give up being pashandas and become Vaishnavas;
let all know, O Lord, Your glory.

**51.** Let the living beings worship no one but You;
become the wealth and life of all the living beings.

**52.** O Lord, deliver the living beings, coming Yourself;
make the living beings happy by giving [them] bhakti for Yourself.

**53.** This, O Lord, is the desire of us both:
O Lord, deliver all the living beings."

**54.** Having prayed thus to the feet of Sri Krishna,
both keep the vow of Ekadashi with joy in their hearts.

**55.** Seeing the firmness of both in the vow, Gaura Raya,
the Lord, seeks a means to meet the two.

**56.** One day Mahaprabhu is weeping;
His mother comforts [Him], but He will not be calmed.

**57.** Before His father and mother He shows wilfulness:
the Lord asks for just what cannot be had.

**58.** Whatever the Lord asks for, His father and mother give;
and still Mahaprabhu weeps.

**59.** For this there is only one remedy:
on hearing the name of Hari, the Lord weeps no more.

**60.** To comfort the Lord, all the men and women
loudly sing "Hare Krishna, Hare Rama".

**61.** And still Mahaprabhu does not calm down;
He tells no reason, but only weeps.

**62.** Shachi Devi says: "Child, why are You crying?"
The Lord says: "A fit has come upon Me just now.[^07-6]

**63.** All of you, find out [what it is] and treat it;
then, calmed down, I will play."

**64.** Hearing this, everyone treats [Him],
and still Mahaprabhu weeps.

**65.** Mishra says: "Child Vishvambhara, what do You want?
That very thing I will give, child — stop crying."

**66.** The Lord says: "If you want to make Me well,
then go quickly to the house of two brahmanas.

**67.** Jagadisha Pandita [and] Hiranya Bhagavata —
the two brahmanas are keeping the vow of Ekadashi today.

**68.** They have prepared naivedya to worship Vishnu;
if I get to eat it, I will cry no more."

**69.** Hearing these words from the boy's mouth,
Mishra's wife laments greatly.[^07-7]

**70.** Hearing [of this], those two brahmanas came quickly,
brought Vishnu's naivedya and placed it before the Lord.

**71.** Having eaten it, Mahaprabhu became calm;
that this was Sri Krishna Himself, they did not know.

**72.** Then the Lord thought in His heart:
"I will make Myself known to Jagadisha.[^07-8]

**73.** Again on an Ekadashi day I will eat the naivedya,
and on that day I will let him see Me."

**74.** In this way some days passed,
and then Mahaprabhu met Jagadisha.

**75.** On another Ekadashi day the Lord considered:
"Now Jagadisha has begun the worship of Krishna.

**76.** At this time I shall go there" —
having considered [thus], Mahaprabhu set out at once.

**77.** Arriving, He saw Jagadisha Pandita:
in the worship of Krishna he had forgotten the outer world.

**78.** Many articles of naivedya of various kinds —
fruits, roots, sweets and the like — beyond counting.

**79.** Fixing his mind on Krishna, Jagadisha offers [them];
seeing [this], Mahaprabhu became filled with supreme bliss.

**80.** Receiving the devotee's articles, Sri Gauranga Raya joyfully
displayed there something of a child's restlessness:

**81.** as if unknowingly, He ate a little of the naivedya.
At that moment the Pandita's meditation was broken.

**82.** Jagadisha says: "What have You done, Nimai?
You have eaten the naivedya — the worship of Vishnu has not been done."

**83.** The Lord says: "Jagadisha, listen to [My] words:
come and worship My feet as [the feet of] Krishna."

**84.** Jagadisha sees that this son of Shachi
is Sri Krishna Himself — of this there is no doubt.

**85.** Prostrating on the ground with all eight limbs, he bows down
and again and again offers praises with joined palms.

**86.** The Lord says: "You are an old brahmana, a venerable man;
it is not fitting for you to bow to Me.

**87.** You are an aged pandita, all-knowing,
and by bowing to Me you bring misfortune on Me."

**88.** Jagadisha says: "O Lord, deceive me no more:
I have seen with my own eyes the marks on Your feet."

*Tatha hi:*

**89.** *The lotus feet which Brahma and the others, constantly meditating, have not attained —
You, O wondrous One, I have seen with my own eyes by my good fortune.*

**90.** Brahma, Bhava and the other gods, always absorbed in meditation,
constantly long to attain those feet.

**91.** By the good fortune of many births, one after another,
today I have seen that pair of feet with my own eyes.

**92.** For so many days I, wretched one, did not recognize [You];
today my body has become fruitful.

**93.** Since You, O Lord, have shown mercy to a low sinner,
mercifully give me the shade of Your feet upon my head.

**94.** To Your devotee You have given Your pair of feet;
now You will never be able to trick [him]."

**95.** Thus Jagadisha offers various praises
and says: "Forgive me [my] offence."

**96.** At that time Duhkhini Devi came there,
and, coming, she saw the holy feet of Mahaprabhu.

**97.** On the soles shine the marks of the flag, the thunderbolt and the elephant goad;
in the four arms are the conch, disc, club and lotus.

**98.** On the chest a garland of forest flowers, on the hips a dhara;
the forehead framed by curls, above the form a chuda.[^07-9]

**99.** Seeing such beauty, Duhkhini fainted;
after a moment she came to her senses.

**100.** With tears in her eyes the Devi says to the Lord:
"At last, O Lord, You have become kind to me.

**101.** At last You have made my birth fruitful:
forgiving my offence, You have let me see You.

**102.** I reared [You], thinking You an ordinary child;
forgive me this offence, Narayana.

**103.** I am a woman, ignorant, I have no strength at all;
O Lord, give [me] firm bhakti for Your lotus feet."

**104.** Thus Duhkhini Devi, offering many praises,
bowed down, [falling] like a rod, holding the Lord's feet.

**105.** Jagadisha [and] Duhkhini bowed to the Lord's feet;
mercifully the Lord began to speak to them both:

**106.** "You two were formerly My associates,
and now you have taken birth as devotees.

**107.** In the same way the associates [of My lila in] Dvapara
have been born in the form of devotees in various places.

**108.** I have met with you before all the others,
and later I will meet with all the devotees.[^07-10]

**109.** All together we shall practise the bhagavata-dharma
and spread the sankirtana of the name of Hari.

**110.** In the age of Kali the living beings are intoxicated with worldly things;
by giving the name of Hari, I will deliver them all."

**111.** Hearing Gauranga's words, Pandita Thakura
found abundant bliss in his mind.

**112.** Intoxicated with prema, Jagadisha began to dance;
then Mahaprabhu calmed him.

**113.** In this way Jagadisha met the Lord
and together with his wife began to serve His feet.

**114.** Listen with faith, all you devotees,
[to the account of] Jagadisha's meeting with Mahaprabhu.

**115.** After some time the Lord, with His devotees,
performs kirtana filled with supreme bliss.

**116.** Gathering at night in the house of Srivasa Pandita,
all cry "Hari, Hari!", clapping their hands.

**117.** Then Mahaprabhu manifested the sankirtana;
because of it there was a great clash with the Kazi.

**118.** Having crushed the Kazi, the Lord performs sankirtana
and joyfully enjoys His pastimes with His circle of devotees.[^07-11]

**119.** For the deliverance of the pashandas the Lord Gaurahari
ponders in His mind day and night.

**120.** He considered in His mind the means for this;
that news Jagadisha came to know in his heart:

**121.** the Lord, leaving Navadvipa and becoming a sannyasi,
will live at Jagannatha's together with the devotees.

**122.** Knowing [this], Jagadisha conceived a desire in his mind:
"I will manifest the service of Sri Jagannatha.[^07-12]

**123.** Leaving Navadvipa, I will live in another place.
How shall I obtain Gauranga's command for this?"

**124.** Gaura, the wish-fulfilling tree for the desires of His devotees, the jewel of virtues,
called Jagadisha and Himself gave the command.

**125.** The Lord says: "Jagadisha, listen to [My] words:
go — your wish will be fulfilled."

**126.** Receiving the command, Jagadisha became very happy;
he called Mahesha Pandita and began to speak:

**127.** "I will go to Niladri to see Jagannatha;
I leave Duhkhini in your house."[^07-13]

**128.** Having thus reassured his own brother,
he set out for Nilachala with joy in his heart.

**129.** Whoever hears this account with faith
obtains Chaitanya's feet by Jagadisha's mercy.

**130.** Placing hope in the feet of Prabhu Bhagavatananda,
Ananda Dasa tells of Jagadisha's pastimes.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the seventh varna, called "The Meeting of Sri Jagadisha with Sri Mahaprabhu".*

## Varna 8. The Account of Sri Jagadisha's Residence in the Village of Yashoda and the Manifestation of Service to Sri Jagannatha

*At Mahaprabhu's command Jagadisha, leaving Duhkhini with Mahesha, sets out for Nilachala; the journey in prema; Gopinatha at Remuna, Sakshi-gopala at Cuttack; the Nilachakra, Athara-nala and the approach to the temple by prostrations; the darshana of Jagannatha and Jagadisha's prayer; Jagadisha asks for Jagannatha's body kept at the temple's "Vaikuntha", and Jagannatha promises to ride on his back; the king's permission; Jagadisha brings Jagannatha to Yashoda (Jatra) and manifests His service; Mahesha and Duhkhini move there; the pilgrims; the local king cannot lift the Deity, offers land and receives instruction; the author's signature*

*The eighth varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha! Being kind to me,
bestow mercy, [casting on me] a merciful glance.
Happy at heart, I write your virtues in a book —
mercifully bring [it] to completion.

**2.** All the pastimes that Jagadisha thus performed —
who can count them?
What I hear from the mouths of saints, that alone I describe a little,
in order to purify my own heart.

**3.** Mahaprabhu gave the command, and Sri Jagadisha set out
to see the kshetra of Jagannatha.
Choked with the rasa of prema, bowing, he takes each step;
streams of prema flow from both his eyes.

**4.** Loudly raising the cry of "Hari!", the crown-jewel of Vaishnavas
set out in great happiness.
Having consoled Duhkhini and left [her] in Mahesha's house,
he went towards the south.

**5.** On and on he walks along the road, ever singing Gaura's virtues,
not resting even for a moment.
In his mind — always meditation on Krishna; his ears hear talks of Krishna;
his tongue always takes Krishna's name.

**6.** In this way Sri Pandita, greatly delighted,[^08-1]
walks along the road, absorbed in prema.
All the toll-keepers at the river crossings, seeing the Prabhu, join their palms;
no one raises any obstacle [for him].

**7.** The Prabhu walks on happily; the temples in place after place
he visits, absorbed in prema.
He knows no sorrow at all, ever happy in the name of Krishna;
the weariness of the road never comes over him.

**8.** Sri Jagadisha Pandita, joyful at heart,
in due course came to Remuna.
There, seeing Sri Gopinatha, he fell prostrate
and with a happy heart offered himself to Him.[^08-2]

**9.** Humbly he prays: "O merciful One, be merciful,
be gracious to me,
so that by Your grace, going to Nilachala,
I may obtain the darshana of Jagannatha."

**10.** Having prayed thus, he spent that night
in the abode of Lord Gopinatha.
Rising in the morning, Sri Pandita, having bowed to Sri Gopinatha,
went on with happiness in his heart.

**11.** Intoxicated with prema he walks on, like a crazy baul;
people take him for a madman and laugh.
But whoever is most fortunate recognizes the Prabhu,
offers praises and floats in the bliss of prema.

**12.** Such is the Prabhu's way: equal kindness to all;
he takes the virtues and has no eye for faults.
Bearing Gaura's command on his head, he goes to the city of Nilachala,
singing the names and virtues of Gauranga.

**13.** Thus Sri Pandita arrived at Cuttack,
and there he saw Sakshi-gopala.[^08-3]
Seeing the form of Sri Gopala, he became supremely happy
and offered [Him] many obeisances and praises.

**14.** Staying there two days and seeing the service of the eight periods of the day,
the Prabhu became greatly delighted.
Coming to Tulasi-chura, Jagadisha, the abode of virtues,
beheld the Nilachakra.[^08-4]

**15.** The bliss in the Prabhu's mind on seeing the Nilachakra —
who has the power to tell it?
His body trembling with prema, he dances, making so many playful gestures;
streams of prema flow from both his eyes.

**16.** At every step [falling] flat like a rod, he bows hundreds and hundreds of times,
offers praises in prose and verse.[^08-5]
Crying "Gaura! Jagannatha!" he dances with both arms raised
and forgets himself in the bliss of prema.

**17.** Thus, absorbed in prema, coming to Athara-nala,
he danced much and sang kirtana.[^08-6]
From there, in great happiness, in the delight of prema for Krishna,
he began to prostrate himself with eight limbs.

**18.** Jagadisha, the vessel of prema — the Prabhu walks one step
and again prostrates himself with eight limbs.
Thus step by step, as his heart desired,
he goes on, bowing down again and again.

**19.** Thus, coming to Nilachala in great eagerness,
he beheld Jagannatha.
Trembling with prema, rolling on the ground,
he fell prostrate in the mood of bhakti.

**20.** Bowing to Sri Jagannatha, he offers praises with joined palms,
gazing [at Him] with a joyful heart.
His body is heavy with the rasa of prema, his eyes are full of tears,
no words come forth from his holy mouth.

**21.** Thus for some time he gazed [at Him];
after that, external awareness returned.
Then, happy at heart, standing facing east,
he offered many praises thus:

**22.** "O Krishna, full of mercy, have mercy on me;
You are the wealth and life of the destitute.
Out of fear of Time my heart is always trembling;
mercifully deliver me.

**23.** I, a tiny soul, born upon the earth,
am bound in the net of maya.
You are the maker of the world, the one sustainer of the universe;
forgiving my offences, You have shown Yourself.

**24.** Govinda, son of Nanda, lifting Sri Govardhana,
You mercifully protected Gokula.
Lord of the life of the gopis, together with the cowherd boys
You performed hidden pastimes in Vrajapura.

**25.** Friend of the lowly, be kind — You bear the name "the Merciful";
You, Lord, are the life of those who have nothing.
I am bound by evil sense objects; granting [me] Your lotus feet,
Lord, deliver me.

**26.** I am a person without bhakti, I have no bhajana or sadhana —
how shall I cross this samsara?
Being merciful, granting [me] bhakti-yoga to Yourself,
make me Your servant and keep me at Your holy feet."

**27.** Thus Sri Pandita offered many hymns,
and Jagannatha became pleased.
He says to the Pandita: "Take a boon, whatever you wish:
I have appeared before you in person."

**28.** Seeing the Lord's form, radiant as a sapphire,
Jagadisha joined both his palms.
His eyes filling with tears, his heart overwhelmed with prema,
he prostrated himself with eight limbs.

**29.** "That body of Yours which lies at the place [called] Vaikuntha,
in the northern part of the Sri Mandira —
if I receive Your mercy, I shall take that form
and manifest [Your] service in the land of Gauda."[^08-7]

**30.** Then the Lord gave His command — He gave the command to Jagadisha:
"I accept [your request].
Go alone, taking My body,
to whatever place you wish."

**31.** Hearing this, Sri Jagadisha Pandita rejoiced
and began to speak humbly:
"A form as heavy as a load for three carrying-poles — that I should have the strength to carry it —
how can this be possible?[^08-8]

**32.** And the king's men — why would they let [me] go?
These two hesitations are in my mind.
Though You, Lord, have given the command, still I feel fear in my mind;
make this lowly person fearless."

**33.** Hearing this, Jagannatha, the Lord of the life of the destitute,
says to him kindly:
"The king's envoys will not hinder [you]; I will go on your back;
take [Me] and go without fear."

**34.** Rising in the morning, Sri Pandita, greatly delighted,
took his morning bath in the waters of the ocean.
At that moment a king's servant, coming near him,
made this submission:

**35.** With many obeisances and praises [he said]: "The king has given permission
to take away the body [of Jagannatha]."
Hearing this, he was happy: all sorrow went far away,
and his bliss became boundless.

**36.** Tying it as a load upon his back, the body of Jagannatha
Thakura Pandita took up.
That account is full of nectar; by the command of Bhagavata[nanda]
this Ananda has described [it] a little.[^08-9]

**37.** Taking the body of Jagannatha, the Pandita
walks the road in prema, filled with joy.

**38.** Day and night singing the names of Gaura and Krishna,
in due course he arrived at the village of Sri Yashoda (Jatra).

**39.** Having come there, the Pandita regained external awareness
and made a plea: "Jagannatha, stay here."

**40.** Jagannatha accepted his words
and gave this command: "You serve Me."

**41.** Receiving Sri Jagannatha's command, the Pandita
became very joyful in his mind.

**42.** Mahesha Pandita and Duhkhini Thakurani —
he himself brought both of them to that place.

**43.** Jagadisha manifested the service of Jagannatha;
hearing of it, the people of that land came to see.

**44.** All the people say: "This land has become blessed:
a brahmana has brought Jagannatha and serves [Him].

**45.** To see Jagannatha in Nilachala
one must spend money and toil on the road.

**46.** Without effort we shall have darshana in this place;
this good brahmana has delivered us all."

**47.** In this way all the people come eagerly,
take darshana and go away happy in the bliss of prema.

**48.** Jagannatha's arrival became widely famous;
hearing of it, the king of that land came to see.

**49.** Coming and seeing [Him], the maharaja was astonished
and then reasoned within his mind:

**50.** "I see that this body is a load for three carrying-poles,[^08-10]
[yet] the brahmana brought it on his back alone.

**51.** Whether it is true or false I cannot understand; my mind is confused.
Or did he make Jagannatha out of shola?"[^08-11]

**52.** Thinking thus, that king took hold of Jagannatha;
he made great effort, yet could not lift [Him].

**53.** Astonished, the king bowed down
and, coming from there, clasped the Prabhu's feet.

**54.** The king says: "Forgive my offence, O merciful one:
I have taken shelter of you, Prabhu."

**55.** Jagadisha says: "You are the ruler of the kingdom;
it is not proper for you to praise me."

**56.** The king says: "Prabhu, hide behind maya no more[^08-12];
mercifully grant [me] the shade of your feet.

**57.** I have come to know your greatness well:
you have descended to deliver this land.

**58.** Casting a merciful glance upon me, Prabhu,
accept a few of my possessions.

**59.** Then all my offences will be forgiven;
if the offence is not forgiven, I shall surely perish."

**60.** Seeing him distressed, Pandita Thakura
showed him abundant mercy.

**61.** Jagadisha says: "King, whatever you wish —
for your satisfaction I will accept [it]."

**62.** The king says: "Prabhu, this is my request:
accept some land in my kingdom."

**63.** The Prabhu says: "I live in your kingdom;
I live on an allowance, and you are the master of the kingdom."

**64.** The king says: "For the sake of Jagannatha's service
accept fertile, tax-free land.

**65.** Staying in this place and serving Jagannatha,
deliver all the fallen worldly people."

**66.** The Prabhu was pleased by the king's humility,
and the Prabhu accepted the land given by the king.

**67.** Again that king, clasping the Prabhu's feet,
makes a plea for his own welfare.

**68.** The king says: "I have fallen into this well of samsara;
in what way shall I be delivered from it?

**69.** Mercifully tell me the means for that;
I have taken refuge at your reddish feet."

**70.** Seeing the king's earnest longing, Prabhu Jagadisha
joyfully gave instruction.

**71.** The Prabhu says: "King, listen to my words:
care for your subjects with love, as for sons.

**72.** In your own kingdom establish bhakti for Krishna;
without effort you will attain the feet of Govinda."

**73.** Receiving instruction from the Prabhu, the best of kings
offered the Prabhu many obeisances and praises.

**74.** Then that maharaja, taking the Prabhu's leave,
went to his own abode, joyful.

**75.** Going home, he carried out the Prabhu's instruction:
he established bhakti for Krishna in his own kingdom.

**76.** Here, meanwhile, Prabhu Jagadisha in his own home
serves Sri Jagannatha with a joyful heart.

**77.** The way he performs the service of Jagannatha —
who has the power to describe it?

**78.** Sri Jagadisha's virtues cannot be described:
Jagannatha was won over by his service.

**79.** How Jagannatha came to the village of Sri Yashoda
I have described in brief.

**80.** Sri Jagadisha's pastimes are like an ocean of nectar:
because of his good character Jagannatha showed [him] mercy.

**81.** Pandita Thakura is the Prabhu of my Prabhu;
without his feet I have no refuge.

**82.** A tiny soul, I describe his pastimes;
all of you, mercifully forgive [my] offences.

**83.** Lowly, sinful am I, of very wicked intent;
I do not know what I write, I do not understand my own heart.

**84.** As Prabhu Bhagavatananda commanded,
so, in accordance with that command, Ananda composed [this].

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the eighth varna, called "The Account of Sri Jagadisha's Residence in the Village of Yashoda and the Manifestation of Service to Sri Jagannatha".*

## Varna 9. The Account of Sri Mahaprabhu's Coming to the Village of Yashoda

*Invocation of Jagadisha; Jagadisha lives in Yashoda, serving Jagannatha; the marriage of Mahesha, who stays in his father-in-law's house; three sons are born to Jagadisha; the Lord's sannyasa at Katwa and His coming to Shantipura; the Lord remembers Jagadisha and comes to Yashoda by night together with Nityananda; Jagadisha shows Them Jagannatha; the Lord asks Duhkhini for paramanna, and a cow herself gives her milk; Nityananda asks for bhekut fish with mangoes — Jagadisha finds mangoes in the month of Pausha, and the Yamuna brings him a fish; Duhkhini, in prema, stirs the boiling paramanna with her hand, and the hand of the Lord burns; Nityananda accepts Mahesha; Jagadisha's son defiles what was prepared for the Lords, and Jagadisha asks the Lord to take away his three sons — they leave their bodies and merge into the body of Gauranga; the meal of the Lords, mahaprasada and the night kirtana; the author's signature*

*The ninth varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, glory to the merciful one!
Be kind and show mercy to me, a sinner.

**2.** I describe your life, being an insignificant creature:
Prabhu, mercifully complete this book.

**3.** Bhagavatananda gave me the order in a dream;
by the power of that order I describe your lila.

**4.** I am an ignorant sinner, I know nothing;
whatever words Bhagavata[nanda] makes me speak, those I speak.[^09-1]

**5.** Thus Jagadisha abode in Yashoda:
serving Jagannatha, he settled [there].

**6.** Serving Jagannatha with body, words and mind,
he performs the duties of a householder according to the injunctions of the shastras.

**7.** In this way Jagadisha lived in Yashoda.
Duhkhini said: "Get Mahesha married."

**8.** Then Pandita Thakura pondered in his heart:
how can the marriage of his brother Mahesha be arranged?

**9.** While he was thinking this over, some days passed;
by providence a brahmana came to Yashoda.

**10.** That brahmana has only one daughter,
and he is anxious about her marriage.

**11.** Coming to the Pandita, he makes a request:
"Give my daughter in marriage to your brother."

**12.** Jagadisha first ascertained his kulinya,
and then the Prabhu accepted his words.[^09-2]

**13.** After this that brahmana, before Jagadisha,
makes a certain request with humble words:

**14.** "Mercifully give me permission
that Mahesha may live in my house.

**15.** As long as life remains in my body,
so long let Mahesha live in my house."

**16.** Jagadisha gave him permission for this;
having fixed the auspicious hour, that brahmana set off for his home.

**17.** Srila Jagadisha married off his brother;
Pandita Mahesha stayed in his father-in-law's house.

**18.** In time three sons were born to Jagadisha;
joy arose in the mind of Duhkhini Devi.

**19.** Thus the Prabhu remains in the householder ashrama.
Mahaprabhu accepted sannyasa at Katwa,

**20.** and together with Nityananda the Lord came to Shantipura;
coming there, the devotees met with the Lord.[^09-3]

**21.** At that very moment it arose in Mahaprabhu's heart:
"In Yashoda Jagadisha is remembering Me."

**22.** Drawn by Jagadisha's prema, Gaurachandra,
attaining the highest bliss, says to Nityananda:

**23.** "Listen, Shripada: taking You along with Me,
today I shall certainly go to the village of Yashoda."

**24.** Nityananda says: "Who lives there,
to whose home You, Prabhu, will go?"

**25.** The Lord says: "There [lives] the foremost of mahantas,
by whose prema Jagannatha Bhagavan is conquered.

**26.** His name is known everywhere in the world:
the foremost of Vaishnavas, Sri Jagadisha Pandita.

**27.** In My childhood I met with him;
receiving My order, he took up the service of Jagannatha.[^09-4]

**28.** By My order he settled in Yashoda;
he knew beforehand of My sannyasa.

**29.** Therefore today I shall certainly go there;
be gracious and come there [with Me]."

**30.** Nityananda says: "Prabhu, by Your order
I shall certainly go to Jagadisha's home."

**31.** Saying this, the two Lords set out together
and reached Yashoda when a prahara of the night had passed.[^09-5]

**32.** Arriving, the Two went towards Jagadisha's house;
the Pandita knew of it in his heart.

**33.** In great joy he went far out to meet [Them]
and bowed down to Both, offering many praises.

**34.** Coming into the house, he washed the feet of Both
and brought excellent seats for [Them] to sit on.

**35.** The Lord says: "Jagadisha, I have heard
that you brought Jagannatha and serve [Him].[^09-6]

**36.** Take Me and let Me see Him —
let My eyes today attain their purpose."

**37.** Jagadisha says: "Prabhu, You are Jagannatha;
mercifully You have made me, a sinner, Your own."

**38.** Placing the two Lords in front, the Pandita went [behind Them]
and quickly opened the door of the shri-mandira.

**39.** Seeing Jagannatha, [They] offered many obeisances and praises;
in the rapture of prema the two Lords danced at length.

**40.** Jagadisha, bringing the Two to his own house,
respectfully seated [Them] on excellent seats.

**41.** Duhkhini Thakurani came and bowed down;
seeing the two Lords, she attained great happiness.

**42.** With tears in her eyes the devi offers various praises;
Mahaprabhu addressed her, calling her mother:

**43.** "Listen, mother Duhkhini," says Mahaprabhu,
"today I shall eat hot paramanna.[^09-7]

**44.** My stomach is burning with the fire of hunger;
you, mother, quickly cook paramanna."

**45.** Hearing the Lord's words, Duhkhini Thakurani
at once became anxious in her heart:

**46.** "Where shall I get milk so late at night?
How shall I carry out the Lord's order?"

**47.** In her own house there is a cow;
Duhkhini quickly went to her.

**48.** Weeping and weeping, she says: "Mother, listen,
this is my request to you:

**49.** Mahaprabhu has come here
and has ordered: 'I shall eat paramanna.'

**50.** Where shall I find milk so late at night?
That is why I have come to you, mother.

**51.** If you mercifully give milk,
then I will cook paramanna for the Lord."

**52.** Hearing this, that cow said to her:
"Today my birth as a cow has attained its purpose.

**53.** Quickly bring a vessel and hold it to me —
as much milk as you want, that much I will give.

**54.** Mahaprabhu will drink my milk —
what greater fortune than this could I have?"

**55.** Hearing this, [Duhkhini] brought a vessel and held it there,
and that cow gave half a maund of milk into it.[^09-8]

**56.** Taking that milk, the devi, with a joyful heart,
went to cook for the Lord.

**57.** Meanwhile Prabhu Nityananda, filled with great joy,
gave an order to Jagadisha:

**58.** "I shall eat bhekut fish with mangoes;
go quickly [and make] the arrangements for it."[^09-9]

**59.** Hearing this, Jagadisha reflects in his mind:
"Where shall I get mangoes in the month of Pausha?"

**60.** Meditating in his heart on the lotus feet of Nityananda,
he went and arrived at a mango grove.

**61.** Holding a light, he searches every tree —
and sees: on one tree there are five gandas of mangoes.[^09-10]

**62.** Bringing them, the Pandita gave [them] to Duhkhini
and set off for the bank of the Yamuna for a fish.

**63.** To the Yamuna he speaks humbly:
"Mother, give me one bhekut fish.

**64.** Today Prabhu Nityananda, being most kind,
has come to my home.

**65.** The Prabhu will eat bhekut fish —
for that reason I have come to you."

**66.** Saying this, Jagadisha stood there
and began to recite a hymn to the Yamuna.

**67.** At that moment the Daughter of the Sun, by a wave,
brought a fish near him and placed it on the bank.[^09-11]

**68.** Taking the fish, Jagadisha came home
and gave it to Duhkhini.

**69.** Having given the fish, Jagadisha came to the Lords
and sat [with Them] conversing about the lilas of Krishna.

**70.** Here Duhkhini Devi, with a joyful mind,
carefully cooks for the two Lords.

**71.** Mahaprabhu will eat payasa —
that is why she cooks, overwhelmed by prema.

**72.** In the rapture of prema Sri Duhkhini forgot the external world
and began to stir the paramanna with her hand.

**73.** Meanwhile Jagadisha, before Mahaprabhu,
speaking of Krishna, is immersed in the happiness of supreme bliss.

**74.** The Lord says: "Jagadisha, listen:
why is My hand burning?"

**75.** Hearing this, Jagadisha became very distressed
and began to think how [the Lord] might be made well.

**76.** Seeing him troubled, Sri Gauranga Raya
ordered: "Go and look in the kitchen.

**77.** Then you will learn the reason for the burning of the hand;
quickly put it right, so that I may be well."

**78.** Hearing this, Jagadisha hurriedly went there
and sees Duhkhini cooking, overwhelmed by prema.

**79.** The Thakurani is stirring the paramanna with her hand.
Seeing [this], Jagadisha says: "What are you doing, Duhkhini?

**80.** Why are you stirring the fiery payasa with your own hand?
Don't, don't, dear — let go of it quickly!"

**81.** Hearing this, the devi came to external awareness
and, abashed, began to speak:

**82.** "I was as if asleep, I was aware of nothing;
but be that as it may, my hand feels no pain."

**83.** Jagadisha says: "Why should you feel pain?
This pain Mahaprabhu has taken upon Himself."

**84.** Hearing this, the devi, grieved, washed her hand
and began to stir the paramanna with a stick.

**85.** For the two Lords the cooking was done separately;
in great happiness the two Lords ate.

**86.** Having fed [Them], Jagadisha was happy
and laid the Two down on an excellent bed.

**87.** When the two Lords had lain down happily,
Duhkhi[ni] and Jagadisha served [Their] feet.[^09-12]

**88.** Rising in the morning, the two Lords performed Their morning duties
and, through Duhkhini's care, stayed [there] that day.

**89.** By providence Mahesha came that day;
Prabhu Nityananda accepted him.[^09-13]

**90.** Chaitanya and Nitai are the two avatara brothers;
Jagadisha and Mahesha are sold to Them both.

**91.** The two brothers, very joyful at heart,
served the two Lords in many ways.

**92.** Rising in the morning, the two Lords wished to leave;
Mahesha then made every effort to keep [Them].

**93.** Seeing Mahesha's entreaty, Gauranga and Nitai
did not leave that day and stayed there.

**94.** Taking pains for the service of the Lords,
the two brothers gathered many kinds of foodstuffs.

**95.** They prepared [them] in the purest vessels.
At that time Jagadisha's son came there.

**96.** In childish restlessness he played about there in many ways
and, having chewed sugarcane, threw [it] into those foodstuffs.[^09-14]

**97.** Seeing this, Jagadisha was grieved
and, overcome with anger, began to speak:

**98.** "A householder desires offspring,
thinking: 'A son will deliver me.'

**99.** But [this holds] only if the son is devoted to Krishna —
then it is so; such is the word of the shastras.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Narada-pancharatra, the words of Sri Vedavyasa to Sri Shukadeva:*

**100.** *He is [truly] a son who becomes a devotee of Krishna, a giver of happiness and blessing:
by his mere birth he purifies, as if in play, a hundred [generations] of men.*[^09-15]

**101.** But if someone's son is not a Vaishnava,
then all his dharma and all his works are in vain.

**102.** My three sons have turned away from Gaura:
if they live, I shall have great sorrow."

**103.** Saying this, he came to Mahaprabhu
and, clasping the Lord's feet, began to speak:

**104.** "King Indradyumna had eighteen sons —
by Your grace You destroyed them all.[^09-16]

**105.** In the same way destroy my sons —
this is my prayer at Your feet."

**106.** Then, through Jagadisha's anger, the three noble [sons],
leaving their bodies, merged into the body of Gauranga.[^09-17]

**107.** Duhkhini Thakurani, perceiving this,
serving Gauranga, cast off her grief.

**108.** Hearing those words, Pandita Jagadisha,
filled with joy, danced in the rapture of prema.

**109.** Then [they] gathered various foodstuffs,
and Duhkhini herself cooked them.

**110.** The two Lords ate in great bliss,
and then the devotees received mahaprasada.

**111.** Having served the Lords, Jagadisha was happy;
all night he sat and performed sankirtana of the Name.

**112.** Placing hope in the feet of Prabhu Bhagavatananda,
Ananda Dasa tells of Jagadisha's pastimes.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the ninth varna, called "The Account of Sri Mahaprabhu's Coming to the Village of Yashoda".*

## Varna 10. The Account of the Manifestation of the Deity Sri Gaura-Gopala in the Village of Sri Yashoda

*Invocation of Jagadisha; in the morning Mahaprabhu and Nityananda prepare to leave; Duhkhini faints at the separation, and the Lord tells her to install His image, promising always to dwell in it and to call her mother; the Lords go to Advaita at Shantipura; by the Lord's order a craftsman comes to Yashoda and in a single night manifests the Deity — the Lord as an infant, as He used to crawl in Duhkhini's house in Nadia — and departs; in the morning Duhkhini finds Nimai crying at the door and nurses Him; Jagadisha seats the Deity on the throne in Jagannatha's temple; the Lord appears to Jagadisha in the form of Krishna and to Duhkhini as a child; at Shantipura the Lord learns of the manifestation of His image and with Nityananda comes again to Yashoda; the Deity Sri Gaura-Gopala hides; Duhkhini cooks a meal, and the Lord asks for a third portion; the Lord's teaching on the oneness of Himself, His devotee and His Deity; the joint meal of Gaura, Nityananda and the Deity; Nityananda sees Gaura-Gopala at Duhkhini's breast and glorifies her; the Lord lets Duhkhini choose which of His forms will remain in her house, and she takes Gopala; the Lords depart; glorification of Jagadisha and Duhkhini; the author's self-abasement and signature*

*The tenth varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, glory to the merciful one!
Be kind and show mercy to me, a sinner.

**2.** Thus the Pandita, having passed the night awake,
came in the morning and bowed to the Lord's feet.

**3.** Then Mahaprabhu and Prabhu Nityananda,
Both joyful, performed Their morning duties.

**4.** Then the two Lords wished to depart,
[and the Lord] speaks sweet words to Duhkhini Devi.

**5.** Mahaprabhu says: "Mother, I make a request to you:
now I shall go to Nilachala."[^10-1]

**6.** When Gauranga spoke these words,
Duhkhini Devi, hearing [them], fainted.

**7.** After some time the devi regained consciousness
and, weeping, began to speak before the Lord:

**8.** "Be pleased and show me this mercy:
let me die before Your eyes.

**9.** How shall I live, Prabhu, not seeing You?
Hearing of Your going, my heart breaks.

**10.** What can I say, Prabhu? You act as You will:
whatever You wish will surely come to be.

**11.** But mercifully give me instruction:
how shall I keep my life in separation from You?"

**12.** Having said this, the devi began to weep.
Mahaprabhu mercifully said to her:

**13.** "Listen, mother Duhkhini, to My words:
install My image.

**14.** In that image I shall always dwell,
and I shall call you mother.

**15.** In whatever way you desire, whenever you desire, —
then I shall fulfil that desire."

**16.** Having thus consoled Duhkhini
and embraced Jagadisha, the Two set out.

**17.** Then the two Lords came to Shantipura
and, having come, stayed happily in Advaita's house.[^10-2]

**18.** [Meanwhile] at Yashoda Jagadisha in supreme joy
manifests the image of Sri Mahaprabhu.[^10-3]

**19.** How that image was installed —
of that I shall tell a little.

**20.** There was the Lord's command to manifest the Deity,
and the Pandita was constantly anxious about it.

**21.** By the arrangement of providence, in the evening someone came
and, bowing to the Pandita, addressed [him]:

**22.** "Listen, sir: by caste I am a craftsman.
I have come here by Mahaprabhu's command.[^10-4]

**23.** Gauranga Sri Hari commanded me:
'Go quickly to the village of Yashoda.

**24.** There lives a certain brahmana pandita,
named Jagadisha, famed in the world.

**25.** His wife, the chaste Duhkhini Thakurani —
she will worship My image.

**26.** Therefore go quickly to Jagadisha
and manifest My image.'

**27.** Mahaprabhu gave me this command;
to carry out the Lord's command I have come here."

**28.** When the craftsman had told all this,
Jagadisha began to dance in prema.

**29.** Jagadisha was greatly delighted;
hearing [it], Duhkhini rejoiced in her mind.

**30.** With devotion and care they lodged the craftsman,
and that very night the craftsman manifested the sri-murti.

**31.** As the Lord was in childhood, when [He lived] in the town of Nadia
and crawled on His knees playing in Duhkhini's house,[^10-5] —

**32.** manifesting [by his art] an image in that form,
the craftsman left [it] at the door and went to his own place.

**33.** In the morning Duhkhini Devi opened the door
and saw Nimai crying at the door.

**34.** Hastily Duhkhini took that Deity onto her lap
and, giving Him the breast, comforted [Him].

**35.** Immersed in the ocean of prema for Gaura, Duhkhini
does not herself know where she is or what she is doing.

**36.** Jagadisha came and saw that sri-murti;
seeing [it], in prema he prostrated himself with eight limbs.

**37.** Seeing that Duhkhini was overwhelmed with prema,
he himself tells her what had happened before:

**38.** "Mahaprabhu commanded that the Deity be manifested;
know for certain that this is that [His] image."

**39.** Having said this, Jagadisha tries to bring her round,
yet the devi does not return to external awareness.

**40.** Then Jagadisha, taking that Deity in his arms,
went joyfully to Jagannatha's temple.

**41.** There he seated the Lord on a jewelled throne
and with his own hands rendered Him fitting service.

**42.** In this way Jagadisha serves,
while Duhkhini, in parental love, sees [Him] in the form of a child.

**43.** When Jagadisha performs service,
Mahaprabhu then assumes the form of Krishna.

**44.** And when Duhkhini is alone in the house,
then the Lord plays in the form of a child.

**45.** The Lord is a wish-fulfilling tree; knowing the feelings of His devotees,
He Himself fulfils the desires of both.

**46.** Who else can understand the mercy of Gauranga?
Thus He performs His lilas in Jagadisha's house.

**47.** Here, at Shantipura, in Advaita's house,
[the Lord] always passes the time joyfully with the devotees.

**48.** Learning that [His] image had become manifest in Yashoda,
the Lord felt delight in His mind.

**49.** Mahaprabhu says: "Listen, Nityananda Raya:
My Deity has become manifest in Yashoda."

**50.** Nityananda says: "Prabhu, You are an ocean of mercy;
to fulfil the desires of devotees is Your nature.

**51.** Jagadisha Pandita is Your dear devotee;
You have made his glory manifest to the world.

**52.** Jagadisha Pandita is supremely fortunate:
You, Bhagavan, have come under the sway of his prema.

**53.** How vast is Jagadisha Pandita's good fortune:
sitting at home, he constantly sees Your feet!"

**54.** Then the Lord humbly said to Him:
"Be kind, let us go to Jagadisha's abode.

**55.** What My image that has appeared there is like —
going there, I shall certainly see it."

**56.** Receiving the command, that very moment Nityananda Raya
went there together with Mahaprabhu.

**57.** The two Lords set out in supreme delight
and, arriving, happily entered Jagadisha's house.

**58.** Pandita Thakura, seeing the two Lords,
fell at [Their] feet, prostrate like a rod.

**59.** Joining his palms, he offered many praises;
the two Lords embraced the Pandita.

**60.** Then Jagadisha, bringing fragrant water,
washed the lotus feet of Both.

**61.** There the Pandita saw Gaura Bhagavan:
sitting on Duhkhini's lap, He sucks at the breast.

**62.** [The Pandita] announced there the arrival of the two Lords,
and Sri Gaura-Gopala began to speak to her:

**63.** "Hide Me inside the house
and with joy in your heart serve the two Lords."

**64.** Having said this to her, Sri Gaura-Gopala,
the Lord, at that moment assumed the form of the Deity.

**65.** Having secretly hidden Gopala, Duhkhini
herself came to the two Lords.

**66.** Coming, the devi bowed down, prostrate like a rod,
and, standing, with tears in her eyes gazes at [Their] form.

**67.** The Lord asked her whether all was well.
Joining her palms, Duhkhini Devi replies:

**68.** "By the grace of Your feet all is well;
no sorrow weighs upon my heart.

**69.** Remembrance of Your feet removes all sorrows.
What sorrow have I, Prabhu, — You are in my house!"

**70.** The Lord was pleased with Duhkhini's words
and began to speak to her with sweet words.

**71.** The Lord says: "Listen, mother, go into the house:
I am hungry — cook quickly."

**72.** Then Sri Duhkhini Devi, hearing the Lord's words,
herself went into the house to cook.

**73.** Thakura Pandita brought many kinds of provisions,
and the devi joyfully began to cook.

**74.** Rice, vegetable dishes and various sweets,
pishtakas and the like she cooked, and paramanna too.[^10-6]

**75.** Cleaning the place, she set out two seats
and, arranging two meals, placed [them] there.

**76.** Then Jagadisha, with great joy in his heart,
made his request before the two Lords:

**77.** "Prabhu, Duhkhini has finished cooking;
be merciful, come and eat, both of You."

**78.** Hearing this, Mahaprabhu and Prabhu Nityananda
joyfully go to eat.

**79.** Seeing two meals, the Lord Gaurahari says:
"Bring and arrange one more meal."

**80.** Hearing this, a seat was placed in that spot,
and one more meal was arranged, brought and set there.

**81.** Seeing this, Mahaprabhu rejoiced in His mind
and speaks some sweet words to Jagadisha:

**82.** "I have heard all the people saying to one another
that a certain craftsman came to your house

**83.** and fashioned an image of Me;
you worship Him, regarding Him as Me.

**84.** Duhkhini tends and cherishes Him,
and He calls Duhkhini mother."

**85.** Hearing this, Jagadisha and Sri Duhkhini
make no reply: both fell silent.

**86.** Seeing them both abashed, the Lord Gaura Raya,
the Lord, began to speak without any pretence:

**87.** "You have both done well; have no worry at all.
I pervade everything; I am present everywhere.[^10-7]

**88.** In wood, in stone or in clay —
in whatever a devotee worships Me with devotion,

**89.** in that, by the power of the devotee's devotion, I abide;
I fulfil the desires of devotees — in this there is nothing else.

**90.** Whoever says that there is a difference
between Me, My devotee and My Deity

**91.** surely goes to hell.
Without guile I have told you the essence of the truth.[^10-8]

**92.** Therefore, giving up all worry,
worship My Deity, regarding Him as Me.

**93.** Bring My Deity — I shall see [Him]:
we three shall eat in one place."

**94.** Hearing this, Jagadisha became exceedingly happy;
he brought the Lord's image and seated [Him] there.

**95.** The two Lords at the sides, Nityananda in the middle:
he looks at the faces of the two Lords, filled with bliss.

**96.** Nityananda Raya, ever intoxicated with prema for Gaura,
now looks towards the Lord Gauranga,

**97.** now again gazes at [His] image;
seeing no difference at all, his mind is full of supreme bliss.

**98.** Seeing the two Mahaprabhus together,
he, well pleased, says to Jagadisha:[^10-9]

**99.** "Blessed, blessed are you, Jagadisha, I tell you:
two Gauras have appeared in your house!

**100.** Who has the power to understand your greatness?
Only he understands it who has firm devotion to Gaura's feet."

**101.** Having said this, Sri Nityananda Himself
took hold of Jagadisha and embraced [him] in prema.

**102.** Having embraced [him], He speaks in sweet words:
"Only now has your good fortune become known.

**103.** Which [of Them] is the original form Himself, which the manifested one?
The two are one; there is no difference at all, that is certain."

**104.** Hearing this, Sri Gaura-Gopala speaks these words:
"Leave other talk aside, Prabhu, and eat."

**105.** Then the two Mahaprabhus and Nityananda —
all three ate joyfully.

**106.** Having rinsed Their mouths, the three sat on the seats;
Jagadisha brought fragrant paste and flowers and applied [them] to [Their] bodies.

**107.** Then he laid the three to rest in three places
and in turn served [Their] feet.

**108.** Then the three great Lords fell asleep,
and Jagadisha went and honoured the mahaprasada.

**109.** Then he himself sat down to nama-sankirtana,
while Duhkhini remained in meditation on Gauranga's feet.

**110.** Then Gaura-Gopala woke from sleep;
calling for His mother, He cries and plays all sorts of pranks.

**111.** Hearing this, Duhkhini Devi went there
and, taking Gopala on her lap, gave Him the breast.

**112.** At this time Nityananda woke from sleep
and saw Gaura-Gopala on Duhkhini's lap.

**113.** Nityananda began to speak to Duhkhini:
"Mother, you have made your human birth fruitful.

**114.** The mother of the Lord Gauranga is Shachi Thakurani:
until now we knew only this.

**115.** Now I have seen: Gaura is your son.[^10-10]
Your good fortune, mother, cannot be put into words.

**116.** Blessed, blessed is Mahaprabhu Sri Krishna Chaitanya!
Who else will understand this hidden lila of Yours?

**117.** Blessed, blessed is Jagadisha, blessed is Sri Duhkhini!
The earth will be filled with your fame."

**118.** In this way [He] praised Duhkhini.
At that time the moon set, and the night passed.

**119.** Mahaprabhu rose and called out "Hari!" —
and Gaura at once got up from Duhkhini's lap.

**120.** Then the three Lords rose and performed Their morning duties,
and Mahaprabhu asked Duhkhini for leave to go.

**121.** Mahaprabhu says: "Mother, be glad at heart
and give Me leave: I shall go to Nilachala.

**122.** I am one Gaura, and this is Gaura-Gopala:
in two forms I am present in your house at the same time.

**123.** Whichever form you wish, keep that one here;
the other form will go on pilgrimage to the holy places.

**124.** Let Gopala go, and I shall stay in your house,
or I shall go, and you keep Gopala."[^10-11]

**125.** These words Mahaprabhu spoke to Duhkhini.
Hearing them, the devi took Gopala onto her lap.

**126.** Seeing [this], Mahaprabhu says: "Listen, Duhkhini:
now I set out on pilgrimage to the holy places."

**127.** Having said this, Mahaprabhu and Prabhu Nityananda,
having consoled Duhkhini, joyfully depart.

**128.** Glory, glory to Mahaprabhu Sri Krishna Chaitanya!
Glory to the merciful Prabhu Nityananda — He is blessed!

**129.** For the fulfilment of the devotees' desire is the manifestation of the Two;
They fulfilled the longing of Duhkhini Devi.

**130.** Blessed is my Prabhu Jagadisha, the jewel of virtues!
Blessed is Duhkhini Thakurani, the purifier of the world!

**131.** Gaurachandra descended in the city of Navadvipa —
[and it is they] in whose house He abides again in the form of a prakasha.

**132.** Jagadisha and Duhkhini are the Lord's associates;
at the feet of those two I bow again and again.

**133.** Having described all these lilas, being an insignificant soul,
[I pray:] Prabhu, out of compassion forgive [my] offences.

**134.** I am utterly destitute, my mind ever wicked;
I am poor, low and fallen, and moreover devoid of bhajana.[^10-12]

**135.** I have no power to describe, my mind is not pure:
it is Bhagavatananda's mercy that makes [me] describe.

**136.** That Prabhu mercifully makes me write:
by my own power this description would not come about.

**137.** Bhagavatananda is the master of my master;
may I serve his two feet birth after birth.[^10-13]

**138.** Hoping in his mind to obtain the dust of his feet,
Ananda has described the manifestation of Gaura-Gopala.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the tenth varna, called "The Account of the Manifestation of the Deity Sri Gaura-Gopala in the Village of Sri Yashoda".*

[^10-2]: Shantipura is the place where Advaita Acharya lived. On the Lord's stay after sannyasa in Advaita's house at Shantipura see CC Madhya 3; see also varna 9, note 3.

[^10-3]: «প্রতি মূর্ত্তি» (*prati-mūrti*, 13, 18 and below) — "image", "likeness": a Deity in which, by the Lord's word (14), He Himself dwells. From 62 on this Deity is called Sri Gaura-Gopala. The author tells of His manifestation as a miracle (20–34): a craftsman sent by the Lord manifests the Deity in a single night and departs.

[^10-4]: «জাতিতে ভাস্কর» (*jātite bhāskara*) — "a craftsman by caste (jati)". The craftsman's name is not given.

[^10-5]: Cf. varna 7 (Duhkhini nurses the little Nimai in Navadvipa) and varna 6, note 9: the author apparently identifies Jagadisha Pandita of Yashoda with the Jagadisha of the pair "Jagadisha and Hiranya", the Lord's neighbours in Navadvipa in His childhood.

[^10-6]: Pishtaka (পিষ্টক, *piṣṭaka*) — cakes made of rice flour. Paramanna — rice cooked in milk with sugar (see varna 9, note 7).

[^10-7]: The edition reads «সর্বত্র ব্যবকর আমি» (*sarvatra byabakara āmi*); the reading is unclear. Taken as «ব্যাপক» (*byāpaka*), "all-pervading".

[^10-8]: Cf. the well-known verse cited as words of the Padma Purana: *arcye viṣṇau śilā-dhīr guruṣu nara-matir vaiṣṇave jāti-buddhiḥ… yasya vā nārakī saḥ* — "One who regards the Deity of Vishnu as stone, the guru as an ordinary man, a Vaishnava as belonging to a caste… is a dweller in hell."

[^10-9]: «দুই মহাপ্রভু» (*dui mahāprabhu*, 98, 105) — the Lord and His Deity Sri Gaura-Gopala; in 108 «তিন মহাপ্রভু» (*tina mahāprabhu*, "the three great Lords") — together with Nityananda.

[^10-10]: Nityananda's words praise Duhkhini's vatsalya-prema (Gaura-Gopala at her breast). Shachi-devi remains the mother of Mahaprabhu; these words do not diminish her position.

[^10-11]: In the edition the punctuation in 124–126 is shifted («…তব ঘরে।» / «…গোপালেরে।» / «…কহিলা ॥» / «…লইলা।» / «…দুঃখিনী ॥» / «…আমি।»); the couplets are arranged by rhyme (ঘরে/গোপালেরে, কহিলা/লইলা, দুঃখিনী/আমি).

[^10-12]: «সদামন্দ মন» (*sadāmanda mana*) is taken as «সদা মন্দ মন», "a mind ever wicked"; «হীনাধাম» (*hīnādhāma*) as «হীন অধম», "low, fallen".

[^10-13]: The line of disciples: Jagadisha → Raghunatha Acharya → Bhagavatananda → Premananda → Radhacharana → Ananda Dasa; the author's guru is Radhacharana. "The master of my master" is as in the text. The author's signature (138) is a variant of the formula.


## Varna 11. The Description of Sri Raghunatha Acharya's Initiation and Instruction

*Invocation of Jagadisha; from Yashoda the Lord comes to Shantipura, to Advaita, and announces to the devotees that He is going to Nilachala; Nityananda and all the devotees go with Him; on the way the Lord finds no joy in the kirtana until He tells Nityananda to bring Jagadisha; Jagadisha dances at the head of the procession and receives the name Sri Nritya-vinodi; the Lord in Puri, Sarvabhauma becomes His devotee; the Lord's journey through the south and return to Nilachala; the Lord sends Nityananda with Jagadisha, Ramadasa, Gadadhara and others to Gauda to deliver the fallen; He tells Bhagavan Acharya to live as a householder, foretells the birth of his son Raghunatha and tells him to give the boy to Jagadisha; the author's signature; Nityananda and Jagadisha in Gauda; Raghunatha is born to Khanja Bhagavan, and he entrusts his son to Jagadisha; Jagadisha raises Raghunatha, gives him the sacred thread, teaches him the shastras and initiates him into the mantra of Radha-Krishna; Kamalakanta of Durgapur takes initiation from Jagadisha; Raghunatha asks to be taught the truth about Krishna and about bhakti; Jagadisha's instruction, which he heard from Mahaprabhu: Krishna is the complete Brahman, Chaitanya is Krishna descended in a golden form to give the yuga-dharma of sankirtana; bhakti is obtained through the association of devotees and is performed in nine ways; the guru and the siddha-deha; the way to Vrindavana; Jagadisha also tells of the truth about Gauranga; the author's self-abasement and signature*

*The eleventh varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, the lord of the devotees!
Show mercy to me, an evildoer.
Carry [me] across maya by your own goodness —
this I pray again and again.

**2.** However many your deeds may be — to set them down in a book
I have no power at all.
Following the command given in a dream, I have undertaken to describe [them] —
bring the book to completion yourself.

**3.** From the village of Sri Yashoda, taking Nityananda with Him,
Gauranga came to Shantipura.
Coming to Advaita's house, He summoned the devotees
and with His holy mouth speaks to them all:

**4.** "Listen, devotees, I tell you: in My mind there is this wish —
I shall go to Nilachala.
Whoever wishes for darshana, come with Me —
this is My request."[^11-1]

**5.** Nityananda says joyfully: "I shall go with You —
what greater fortune could there be than this?
I shall go with Prabhu — I say this with a firm mind;
may all of them wish the same."

**6.** Then the devotees of Gaura, attaining the highest bliss,
made a request to the Lord:
"We shall go with You; we shall not remain apart from Your feet —
this is the word of all of us."

**7.** Hearing this, Sri Chaitanya said: "Blessed, blessed [are you]:
you have fulfilled My desire."
Remembering Sri Hari, Gaurahari set out,
showing honour to all.

**8.** All the devotees together set out at once,
joyfully performing dance and sankirtana.
But the Lord's mind is troubled, [He] is not glad:
again and again the rhythm breaks.

**9.** Then Sri Gauranga Raya reflects in His mind:
"I have not told this to Jagadisha.
I understand: for this reason My heart is not glad,
and in the sankirtana I find no happiness."

**10.** Having reflected thus, Mahaprabhu Gaurahari
gave a command to Nityananda:
"I set out joyfully for Niladri without telling Jagadisha —
that is why My happiness has been broken.

**11.** Do this task, fulfil My word:
bring the Pandita here."
Receiving the Lord's sign, Nityananda, rejoicing,
at once had the Pandita brought.

**12.** Coming, Sri Pandita prostrated himself with all eight limbs
and fell at the Lord's feet.
In the happiness of seeing the Lord he forgets himself,
and no words come from his mouth.

**13.** Mahaprabhu, seeing him, became supremely happy
and in the bliss of prema embraced him.
To give Jagadisha happiness, with a beaming face
the Lord speaks sweet words:

**14.** "Taking the devotees with Me, I set out for Nilachala
without telling you.
We go performing sankirtana, but in it I find no happiness;
then I considered in My heart:

**15.** 'I shall call Jagadisha and take him with Me —
then the journey will be happy.'
Now you have come, and I am happy:
go in front, dancing.

**16.** All My devotees love you;
seeing you, all rejoice in their minds.
Go in front, dancing a lovely dance —
then the devotees will be happy."

**17.** Mahaprabhu gave the command, and Sri Jagadisha went,
dancing with many joyous movements.
All the Vaishnavas, performing sankirtana of the name of Hari,
went along with Jagadisha.

**18.** Seeing the beauty of that dance, which captivated everyone's mind,
[they] gave [him] the name Sri Nritya-vinodi.
Hearing this, he dances with a joyful mind, [giving himself] to the play,
ceaselessly intoxicated in the rapture of prema.[^11-2]

**19.** Thus, dancing with the Lord in great joy,
Thakura Pandita went along.
Performing various lilas on the way, they came and entered the Kshetra —
the Lord together with the devotees.

**20.** Seeing Jagannatha in the Kshetra, all became happy
and remained with Gauranga.
The Lord, Sri Shachinandana, the treasure of the devotees' life,
sports in the joy of sankirtana.[^11-3]

**21.** Then the Lord Gaurahari, staying in the city of Nilachala,
made Sarvabhauma His own.
Sarvabhauma Bhattacharya, the eldest and best of panditas,
became the Lord's supreme devotee.

**22.** Leaving the devotees with Prabhu Nityananda,
the Lord set out on pilgrimage to the holy places.
Going to the southern country and manifesting sankirtana,
He delivered those who had turned away [from God].

**23.** Coming again to Nilachala, Mahaprabhu, the moon Gaura,
met His own devotees.
The devotees, having gained the Lord, were immersed in the highest bliss,
and all remained in Nilachala.[^11-4]

**24.** One day Gaurachandra, taking Nityananda with Him,
sitting alone in a secluded place,
took Nityananda by the hand and lovingly
speaks in various ways of the desire of His mind:

**25.** "Listen, brother Nityananda: I made this vow —
to deliver all the fallen.
But, following My mother's command, I remain in the city of Nilachala,
and that vow has come to nothing.

**26.** I beg You: going to the land of Gauda,
give bhakti to all.
Let the fallen be delivered, let My vow stand —
fulfil this word of Mine.

**27.** Go quickly and take with You
Jagadisha, who is as dear to Me as life,
and also Your companions Ramadasa, Gadadhara
and all the other best of devotees."[^11-5]

**28.** At this time, to see Mahaprabhu,
Bhagavan Acharya entered that place.[^11-6]
Seeing Gaura and Nityananda, he became supremely happy
and bowed down, prostrating himself with all eight limbs.

**29.** Mahaprabhu says to him: "O Acharya, full of virtues,
go to your homeland with Nityananda.
Fulfil My word: live in the householder ashrama,
constantly [engaged] in talks about Sri Krishna.

**30.** Within a year a good son will be born to you —
give him the name Sri Raghunatha.
Having entrusted him to Jagadisha Pandita,
come and stay with Me.

**31.** Jagadisha, full of affection, will raise him;
when he becomes worthy, he will give him initiation into the mantra,
will teach him in various ways all the books of the shastras on bhakti
and will reveal to him the whole truth about Krishna.

**32.** Raghunatha's sons will receive the mantra of Sri Krishna
from Raghunatha.
This is My word: whoever is born in your line,
all will be the family of Jagadisha."[^11-7]

**33.** Accepting the Lord's command as authoritative, Acharya Sri Bhagavan
set out for Gauda, rejoicing in prema.
Sri Jagadisha Pandita, together with Sri Bhagavan,
went with Nityananda.

**34.** Then Prabhu Nityananda, the root of the world's bliss,
joyfully taking the devotees with Him,
placing Gaura's word on His head and performing sankirtana of the Name,
came to Sri Gauda-mandala.

**35.** Worshipping the two feet of Prabhu Bhagavatananda,
with effort, in the mood of devotion,
by the command of that Prabhu, [given in a dream,] this lowly Ananda Dasa
describes this book.

**36.** Jagadisha Pandita, together with Prabhu Nityananda,
came and arrived in Sri Gauda-mandala.

**37.** Coming to their homeland, the devotees, each to his own house,
went and began the sankirtana of the name of Hari.

**38.** [Accepting] the command of Sri Gauranga Mahaprabhu as authoritative,
Khanja Bhagavan began to live in the householder ashrama.

**39.** In great bliss Bhagavan Acharya Thakura,
staying in the householder ashrama, performs abundant service of bhakti.

**40.** By the Lord's mercy a son was born to him;
having gained a son, Bhagavan rejoiced in his heart.

**41.** As Mahaprabhu had earlier commanded him,
so he gave his son the name Raghunatha.

**42.** By Mahaprabhu's command Khanja Mahashaya
brought Raghunatha and entrusts him to Jagadisha.

**43.** Humbly Khanja says: "Listen, Pandita:
you are a dear devotee of the Lord, known throughout the world.

**44.** To you I have entrusted Raghunatha;
take care of him in every way.

**45.** Teach him with care;
seeing that the proper time has come, give him the sacred thread.

**46.** You will give him initiation into the mantra of Radha-Krishna
and reveal to him the whole truth about Krishna and the truth about bhakti.

**47.** Now I am going to Mahaprabhu;
Raghunatha, entrusted [to you], has become yours."

**48.** Jagadisha says: "Leave off [this] humility before me;
Raghunatha is surely as dear to me as life."

**49.** Then Khanja [went] to Nilachala and met the Lord,
and Jagadisha raised Raghunatha.

**50.** After some time he gave him the sacred thread
and himself taught him all the shastras.

**51.** Mercifully he gave him initiation into the mantra of Radha-Krishna
and commanded: "Always say: 'Gauranga! Sri Hari!'"

**52.** In this way Raghunatha lives in Yashoda,
always skilful in the service of Jagadisha.

**53.** At this time a worthy man came there —
a resident of Durgapur named Kamalakanta.[^11-8]

**54.** Coming, he surrendered himself to Jagadisha,
and Jagadisha mercifully gave him initiation into the mantra.

**55.** For the sake of instruction he stays some days with Prabhu
and serves Prabhu with body, words and mind.

**56.** Raghunatha Acharya and Kamalakanta Dasa —
these two are servants of Jagadisha Pandita.

**57.** One day Raghunatha, before Prabhu Jagadisha,
joining both palms, makes a request:

**58.** "I know nothing of the truth about Krishna or the truth about bhakti;
mercifully reveal [it] yourself to me, a sinner."

**59.** Hearing Raghunatha's words, Thakura Pandita,
rejoicing, began to speak to him:

**60.** "After many births the living being obtains a human body;
this human body is the means of attaining Brahman.

**61.** No one knows the real truth about Ishvara:
each according to his own view accepts this one or that as Brahman.

**62.** Seeing this, Bhagavan manifests His own truth
from yuga to yuga, descending as an avatara.

**63.** In the Satya, Treta and Dvapara yugas, Ishvara mercifully
descends, assuming a white, a red and a dark complexion.

**64.** Thus in order, in the three yugas, He manifests His own truth,
instructing in the yuga-dharma — meditation, yajna and puja.[^11-9]

**65.** Practising one dharma or another, living beings
are liberated and all go to the abode of Vaikuntha.

**66.** But in the Kali-yuga there is something special:
Bhagavan Krishna Himself descends.

**67.** Assuming a yellow complexion, the Lord speaks of His own truth
and manifests the yuga-dharma — the sankirtana of the name of Hari.

**68.** Practising this dharma, all the saintly
attain the feet of Sri Krishna in the abode of Vrindavana.

**69.** That Lord of yellow complexion is Krishna Chaitanya:
He is the limit of the supreme truth; there is none other than Him.

**70.** The teaching of that Lord is higher than the highest;
follow that alone, give no regard to any other.

*Tatha hi:*

**71.** *The object of worship is Bhagavan, the son of the lord of Vraja; His abode is Vrindavana; the charming mode of worship is that which was conceived among the wives of Vraja; the scripture is the spotless Purana Bhagavata, able to bestow prema: such is the teaching of Gaura Mahaprabhu, and therefore regard is for it, not for another.*[^11-10]

**72.** That Lord Gaurachandra is my Ishvara;
what He commanded is the essence of truth.

**73.** That truth I shall now tell you;
hear it with the highest faith.

**74.** Formerly, one day, at Mahaprabhu's feet
I made a request in order to know the truth.

**75.** The instruction that Mahaprabhu then gave,
I shall tell you in detail.

**76.** The Lord said: 'The complete, eternal Brahman
is the son of Nanda, whose form is eternity, knowledge and bliss.'

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Krama-samhita:*

**77.** *The supreme Lord is Krishna, whose form is eternity, knowledge and bliss; without beginning, He is the beginning [of all]; He is Govinda, the cause of all causes.*[^11-11]

*And in the Srimad Bhagavatam:*

**78.** *Oh, the fortune, oh, the fortune of the cowherd Nanda and the residents of Vraja: their friend is the supreme bliss, the complete, eternal Brahman!*[^11-12]

**79.** The Lord said: '[Such] is the brahma-tattva — the limit of knowledge.
Now hear a little of the glory of bhakti for Krishna.

**80.** The son of the king of Vraja, unattainable even for Brahma and the others —
by the power of bhakti the devotee attains His feet.

*Tatha hi, in the Srimad Bhagavatam, in the Eleventh Canto, the words of Sri Bhagavan to Sri Uddhava:*

**81.** *Neither yoga, nor sankhya, nor dharma, O Uddhava, nor study of the Vedas, nor austerity, nor renunciation wins Me as does powerful bhakti for Me.*[^11-13]

**82.** Such bhakti is obtained from the association of devotees;
and the association of devotees the living being obtains through former pious deeds.

*Tatha hi, in the Sri Brihan-naradiya Purana:*

**83.** *Bhakti is born from the association of the devotees of Bhagavan; and association with them is obtained by people through pious deeds accumulated before.*

*And in the Sri Bhavartha-dipika:*

**84.** *Without the mercy of the Vaishnavas there is no bhakti for Krishna; considering thus, let bhakti be shown to His devotees.*[^11-14]

**85.** This bhakti for Krishna is of nine kinds;
in the words of Prahlada it is proclaimed.

*Tatha hi, in the Srimad Bhagavatam, in the Seventh Canto, the words of Sri Prahlada:*

**86.** *Hearing and chanting [the name and deeds] of Vishnu, remembering, serving the feet, worship [of the Deity], offering prayers, service, friendship and offering oneself —*

**87.** *if a person performs this ninefold bhakti, offered to Vishnu, directly for Bhagavan, that I consider the highest learning.*[^11-15]

**88.** Thus I have told the characteristics of bhakti for Krishna;
now hear of the means of attaining Vrindavana.

**89.** From the standpoint of siddhanta, the guru is Ishvara Himself;
from the standpoint of upasana, the guru is an associate of Krishna.[^11-16]

**90.** Taking shelter at the feet of the gurudeva, the associate of Krishna,
one should receive the mantra.

**91.** With body, mind and words one should serve the gurudeva
and practise the nine kinds of bhakti.

**92.** With the outer body one should perform this sadhana,
and within, meditate on the siddha-deha.[^11-17]

**93.** Dasya, sakhya, vatsalya and madhurya:
of these four bhavas, whatever bhava the guru has,

**94.** with that same mood one's own mind will be imbued;
together with the guru, in Vrindavana, [one should] serve Sri Krishna.

**95.** When by the guru's mercy the sadhana ripens,
you will surely attain in Vraja the son of the king of Vraja.'

**96.** This instruction the Lord Gaurahari gave;
by His command I follow this dharma.

**97.** You too act in this way —
without difficulty you will attain in Vraja the son of the king of Vraja."

**98.** When Prabhu Jagadisha had said this,
Raghunatha, hearing it, rejoiced exceedingly.

**99.** Offering many praises, he again made a request:
"Prabhu, let me hear the truth about Gauranga."

**100.** Hearing this, the Pandita rejoiced in his mind
and told him the truth about Gauranga in detail.

**101.** That truth about Gauranga which Prabhu told,
I have not written down for fear of the book growing too large.

**102.** I am an insignificant creature, and it is endless and boundless:
I have no power to describe it in detail.

**103.** At the feet of the saints I beg pardon:
do not count any offence against me in this.

**104.** This book is not described by me:
it came about by the command of Bhagavata[nanda].[^11-18]

**105.** What do I know, what do I understand? Prabhu himself, of his own accord,
coming in a dream, gave the command to me, a fool.

**106.** By the power of that Prabhu's command
the poor and lowly Ananda Dasa made this book manifest.

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the eleventh varna, called "The Description of Sri Raghunatha Acharya's Initiation and Instruction".*

## Varna 12. The Account of the Disappearance of Sri Jagadisha Pandita

*Invocation of Jagadisha; Raghunatha Acharya settles in Malipara and spreads devotion to Hari, Kamalakanta Dasa of Durgapur makes bhakti manifest in his own country, and Jagadisha's disciples establish sankirtana; a daughter, Rasamanjari, and a son, Ramabhadra, are born to Jagadisha and Duhkhini; Jagadisha seeks a match for his daughter with Gopala-vallabha, the son of Madhava Acharya and of Gangadevi, Nityananda's daughter, at Jirat; Madhava Acharya consults Gangadevi and comes to Yashoda; the weddings of the daughter and the son; Jagadisha infuses his power into his son Ramabhadra; Jagadisha's disappearance on the bright third tithi of the month of Pausha and his departure to Vrindavana; in Vraja he is Kalavati-jiu; an appeal to Jagadisha's disciples and the fruit of hearing; a list of the contents of the twelve varnas; the author's self-abasement and a shloka; a song of prayer to Gauranga; glorification of Chaitanya, Nityananda, Advaita, Srinivasa, Gadadhara and the six Gosvamis; entreaty to the devotees and to Bhagavatananda; the author's signature; the closing shloka and the date of the manuscript*

*The twelfth varna*

**1.** Glory, glory to Jagadisha, the ocean of mercy!
Mercifully deliver me, a sinner.

**2.** Thus Raghunatha Acharya Mahashaya,
having received initiation and instruction, went to his own home.

**3.** His dwelling was in the village of Malipara;
there he established devotion to Hari.[^12-1]

**4.** Sri Kamalakanta Dasa, a resident of Durgapur,
going to his own country, made bhakti manifest [there].[^12-2]

**5.** Thus the disciples of Sri Jagadisha
all established the sankirtana of Hari.

**6.** Meanwhile Prabhu Jagadisha, remaining in Yashoda,
delivered many souls by giving them devotion to Krishna.

**7.** Thus many people became devotees of Krishna;
by the power of kirtana the minds of all were purified.

**8.** At this the Pandita was filled with joy
and lived in his own home, happy at heart.

**9.** After some time, by the mercy of Mahaprabhu,
one daughter and one son were born to the Pandita.[^12-3]

**10.** The daughter was given the name Sri Rasamanjari,
and the son received the name Sri Ramabhadra.

**11.** Having obtained a daughter and a son, Duhkhini, joyful at heart,
serves the feet of Gauranga with firm devotion.

**12.** After some time their daughter reached the age for marriage,
and Prabhu began to ponder over her wedding.[^12-4]

**13.** As he pondered and pondered, he remembered
that at the shripat of Jirat dwells Ganga Gosvami.[^12-5]

**14.** She is the daughter of Prabhu Nityananda;
throughout the three worlds she is highly honoured.

**15.** Her husband is Sri Madhava Acharya Mahashaya,
noble in every respect, both in worldly dealings and in the highest goal.

**16.** His son is named Gopala-vallabha;
"To him I shall give my daughter," [Jagadisha] decided.[^12-6]

**17.** Having settled this in his mind, the Thakura Pandita
quickly sent a brahmana to Jirat.

**18.** That brahmana, going to Sri Madhava Acharya,
tells him in detail what is in the Pandita's mind.

**19.** Hearing this, Sri Madhava Acharya Mahashaya,
with joy in his heart, reflects:

**20.** "To marry my son to Jagadisha's daughter —
this I have firmly decided."

**21.** Having reflected thus, he entered the inner apartments
and told Sri Gangadevi all that had happened:

**22.** "Jagadisha Pandita is a mahanta of great mind;
he has a daughter endowed with every good quality.

**23.** For the sake of her marriage with my son
he has sent a brahmana to me.

**24.** Tell me what your wish is:
I want to send the brahmana back quickly."

**25.** Hearing this, Gangadevi said to him:
"Whatever your wish is, to that I give my consent.

**26.** But if you yourself ask what is in my mind —
I know that Jagadisha is a venerable mahanta.

**27.** If he gives his daughter to my son,
that is what should be done — such is my decision."

**28.** Hearing [this], Sri Madhava Acharya was pleased
and, taking that brahmana with him, came to Yashoda.

**29.** Learning [of this], Jagadisha went out to meet him and received him,
and seated him on a wonderful seat.

**30.** The two sat and conversed for some time about the Lord;
then Jagadisha speaks these words to him:

**31.** "Your son Gopivallabha Mahashaya —
to him I shall give my daughter: such is my decision.

**32.** Tell me what should be done about it —
this is my request to you."

**33.** Hearing this, Sri Madhava Acharya says to him:
"I shall tell you what is in my mind:

**34.** to marry my son to your daughter —
such is my decision."

**35.** Having spoken thus, the two then fixed the auspicious hour,
and on an auspicious day the Pandita gave his daughter in marriage.

**36.** Some time after that the Pandita Thakura
with great joy arranged his son's wedding.

**37.** Having married his daughter and his son, the Thakura Pandita
joyfully serves Jagannatha.

**38.** Thus Jagadisha follows the duties of a householder
and, giving the name of Hari, delivers many souls.

**39.** Into his own son Ramabhadra he infused his power (shakti),
and he, giving bhakti, delivered many souls.

**40.** If all that were described, the book would become very long;
for this reason I have written down [only] selected parts of it.[^12-7]

**41.** I bow to his lotus feet —
may there be no offence on my part in this.

**42.** Thus Sri Jagadisha, having delivered the souls,
disappeared, meditating on the feet of Gaura.[^12-8]

**43.** In the month of Pausha, in the bright fortnight, on the day of the third tithi,
he disappeared and went to Vrindavana.

**44.** Who has the power to describe the life of Jagadisha,
who is famed in Vraja under the name Kalavati-jiu?[^12-9]

**45.** To assist in the lilas of Gauranga,
he descended in a male form in the house of a brahmana.

**46.** Having accomplished his task, he went again to Vrindavana;
a small part of his lilas is described in this book.

**47.** To all who have taken shelter of Prabhu Jagadisha
I make this request:

**48.** hear this life of Jagadisha
and with devotion worship the feet of Jagadisha.

**49.** One to whom Jagadisha shows compassion
attains Vrindavana without difficulty.

**50.** The lilas of Prabhu Jagadisha are endless and boundless:
I have no power to describe them in full.

**51.** Prabhu Bhagavatananda gave the order in a dream;
by his order this description in the form of a sutra came about.[^12-10]

**52.** Merely by hearing this, all sins are destroyed,
and residence in the abode of Vrindavana is attained without difficulty.

**53.** Now I shall tell of Jagadisha's lilas in order:
in the first varna — the mangalacharana.[^12-11]

**54.** In the second varna — Prabhu's birth and annaprashana,
in the third varna — the description of the upanayana.

**55.** In the fourth varna — how Prabhu taught the shastras
and refuted the wrong understanding of a brahmana who held that [God] is formless.

**56.** In the fifth varna — the description of Prabhu's wedding,
and there too the birth of Mahesha Pandita is recorded.

**57.** In the sixth — the disappearance of Prabhu's father and mother;
Prabhu performed the shraddha and other rites by the injunctions of the shastras.

**58.** Leaving the eastern country, the brahmana went to Navadvipa
and settled there near the Ganga.

**59.** In the seventh — the meeting with Mahaprabhu:
Mahaprabhu ate the offering for Vishnu.[^12-12]

**60.** In the eighth Jagadisha Pandita Mahashaya
went to Niladri by the Lord's order.

**61.** Bringing from there the body of Jagannatha,
he himself manifested [His] service in Yashoda.[^12-13]

**62.** In the ninth — Gauranga's coming to Yashoda:
the Lord, having asked for it Himself, ate payasa.

**63.** Prabhu Nityananda asked for a meal of whatever He wished,
and the Pandita fed [Him] according to [His] wish.

**64.** In the tenth — the account of the installation of Gaura-Gopala
and of the Lord's coming again to the Pandita's house.

**65.** In the eleventh Mahaprabhu, filled with joy,
went to Sri Kshetra, taking the Pandita with Him.

**66.** There too the birth of Raghunatha Acharya is described,
and how Jagadisha gave him initiation and instruction.

**67.** A certain worthy man named Kamalakanta Dasa
took shelter of Jagadisha's feet.

**68.** In the twelfth — Sri Jagadisha, having performed various lilas
and delivered many fallen souls, disappeared.

**69.** In twelve varnas the book has been completed:
I have described [everything] only briefly, in the form of a sutra.

**70.** Jagadisha's lilas are filled with millions of oceans of nectar;
fortunate is he who relishes them.

**71.** I am an insignificant soul, a low sinner:
O Vaishnava Thakura, forgive [my] offences.

**72.** I am extremely dull-witted, greedy for sense objects;
I wander about ever maddened, under the sway of maya.

**73.** I do not know good from bad — I am a fool;
for my own purification I offer this praise.

**74.** Out of compassion Mahaprabhu descended
and delivered the greatest of sinners.

**75.** By Gauranga's mercy His sadhus appeared,
and they delivered a multitude of souls.

**76.** I am the most foolish of the low, and moreover I do not know bhajana:
how should I know how to sing the Lord's praise?

**77.** I have no discernment of good and bad, my mind is not pure:
I have no power to sing the Lord's praise.

**78.** As the Lord's dear devotees have described [it],
following that, I offer a little praise.

*Tatha hi:*

**79.** *"I am not skilled, not virtuous, and no knower of the shastras;
I have no good qualities, I am not wise and not versed in dharma.
Me, incapable of anything, fallen, afflicted — O Lord, O Friend, alas, alas! —
O Delight of Shachi's heart, protect, protect!"*[^12-14]

**80.** Have mercy, Gorachand, ocean of compassion!
I am exceedingly sinful, and You are the deliverer of the fallen,
the friend of the humble, the destitute and those who have nothing.[^12-15]

**81.** I am a sinner with evil desires, my mind is unsteady,
day and night it is agitated among sense objects.
Devoid of bhakti, utterly wretched, devoid of bhajana and sadhana —
therefore my heart is in great fear.

**82.** Never have I been skilled, there is no virtue in me,
and moreover I am not versed in the shastras.
Ever immersed in evil objects, never have I been wise,
nor am I versed in dharma.

**83.** In the three worlds there is no one as sinful as I —
therefore all disregard [me].
Thinking of this, my heart ever trembles and quakes:
in no way do I see protection [for myself].

**84.** Consider and see: there is no end to my sins,
my mind is skilled in evil deeds.
You bear the name of the Merciful One, Prabhu, beautiful Gauranga —
this time protect, protect me!

**85.** Descending to the earth, You delivered many sinners —
but in that, I think, there is no glory [for You].
If, however, You, Prabhu, can deliver me, a low sinner,
then [Your] glory will remain in the three worlds.

**86.** Having delivered many sinners, You did not look at me —
because of this there is fear in my mind:
You bear the name Patita-pavana, Prabhu, abode of virtues —
lest a stain fall on [that] name!

**87.** I am an insignificant, wretched soul; absorbed in sense objects,
I did not worship Your feet.
But You, Prabhu, are an ocean of mercy, the friend of low people:
this word is proclaimed everywhere.

**88.** You are the Sustainer of countless universes, You are the father of all,
all living beings are Your children.
If by ill fate a son goes away elsewhere,
the father never abandons him.

**89.** All the living beings in the universe You delivered in various ways,
no one's ill fate remained:
by the power of Your compassion they all easily
crossed the ocean of Your maya.

**90.** Listen, Prabhu Gaurahari, this I pray:
my birth is within [that same] universe.
If, then, I remain lying [in it], suffering the torments of the three miseries,
it must be according to [my] past karma.

**91.** Descending in the Kali-yuga, You made a vow:
"I shall deliver the greatest of sinners."
In my mind there is this sorrow: because of me, it seems,
Your vow has not been kept.

**92.** Or else at the time of [Your] descent You made such a vow:
"All living beings except me
I shall easily deliver, I shall give [them] krishna-prema and bhakti" —
for this reason I have been left out.

**93.** But in this there is no fault [of Yours]; listen, merciful Gaura,
I pray [to You] again and again:
I have described this book — whatever faults have occurred in it,
forgive me for them.[^12-16]

**94.** Not knowing good from bad, I describe the lilas of Your branch,
being an exceedingly insignificant soul.
Mercifully bring it about
that the devotees accept this book.[^12-17]

**95.** Whatever may fall to my lot — I give no thought to that at all,
in my mind there is only one desire:
the book came about with great labour by the command of Bhagavata[nanda] —
may the devotees accept it.[^12-18]

**96.** Glory, glory to Sri Chaitanya! Glory to blessed Nityananda!
Glory, glory to Sri Advaitachandra!
Glory to Srinivasa, glory to Gadadhara, full of prema!
Glory, glory to the host of Gaura's devotees![^12-19]

**97.** Glory to Rupa and Sanatana, whose life's treasure is Gaura!
Glory, glory to Bhatta Raghunatha!
Glory to Sri Jiva Gosani — there is no one as merciful as he:
he makes the fallen his own.

**98.** Glory to Sri Gopala Bhatta, full of supreme compassion,
whose life is Sri Radharamana,
whose disciple Srinivasa, bringing the books to light,
saved the souls by the gift of bhakti.

**99.** Glory to Raghunatha Dasa, Gauranga's dear servant,
ever intoxicated with singing Gaura's virtues,
from whom Sri Rupa Gosvami and others, day and night, without cease,
hear of Gaura's virtues.

**100.** The countless mahantas, whose life's treasure
is Shachi's darling Gaurahari —
placing their holy feet upon my head,
I humbly make only this entreaty:

**101.** may all their devotees, with joy,
accept this book.
Then the life of Jagadisha will spread everywhere:
whoever hears [it] will gain benefit.

**102.** I am a person devoid of intelligence: this book, this description,
could never have come about from me.
If, then, the description has come about and I have put the letters together —
[it is] by the order of Prabhu Bhagavata[nanda].

**103.** What that Prabhu said, that I wrote in the book;
it was completed by his wish.
If now the devotees accept the book,
then [my] labour will be fruitful.

**104.** Devotees of Gaura, oceans of mercy, out of compassion
all of you place your feet upon my head.
Then, easily, by the power of the mercy of you all,
I shall cross this ocean of worldly existence.

**105.** Prabhu Bhagavatananda, root of the world's joy,
free [me] from the fear of worldly existence.
Fallen into the ocean of existence, I call [to you] again and again:
this time save [me].

**106.** Descending to the earth by Gauranga's order,
you delivered many sinners.
To so low a person as I you appeared in a dream —
shall I ever again obtain [your] darshana?

**107.** As the order was [given] then, so the book was composed
by the humble and wretched Ananda Dasa.
I ask for nothing more: [let me] ever sing Gaura's virtues —
fulfil this desire.[^12-20]

*Thus in the Charitra-vijaya of Sri Jagadisha Pandita ends the twelfth varna, called "The Account of the Disappearance of Sri Jagadisha Pandita".*[^12-21]

**108.** *One who constantly hears with devotion the narrations about Gaura's devotees
becomes dear to Gaurachandra — of this there is no doubt.*[^12-22]

[^12-1]: Malipara (মালীপাড়া, *mālīpāḍā*) — the village where Raghunatha Acharya settled, the son of Bhagavan Acharya Khanja and a disciple of Jagadisha (varna 11).

[^12-2]: Kamalakanta Dasa of Durgapur — a disciple of Jagadisha (varna 11, note 8). «দেশে গিয়া» (*deśe giyā*) — "going to [his own] country".

[^12-3]: Cf. varna 9, 104–106: there Jagadisha's three earlier sons, at his own request, left their bodies and merged into the body of Gauranga. Here a daughter, Rasamanjari, and a son, Ramabhadra, are born (10).

[^12-4]: «কতদিনে দুহিকার কন্যা কাল হৈল» (*katadine duhikāra kanyā-kāla haila*) — the reading «দুহিকার» is unclear; taken as «দুঁহাকার» (*duṅhākāra*), "of the two of them" (Jagadisha and Duhkhini); «কন্যা কাল» — "the maiden's time", the age for marriage.

[^12-5]: Shripat (শ্রীপাট, *śrīpāṭa*) — the holy residence of a Vaishnava. Jirat (জিরাট, *jirāṭa*) — the shripat of Ganga, Nityananda's daughter; "Ganga Gosvami" (গঙ্গাগোস্বামী, *gaṅgā-gosvāmī*) is as in the text; in 21 and 25 she is called Gangadevi. Her husband Madhava Acharya (15) is a disciple and son-in-law of Nityananda.

[^12-6]: The son of Madhava Acharya and Gangadevi is named in the text in two ways: «গোপাল বল্লভ» (*gopāla ballabha*, Gopala-vallabha, 16) and «গোপীবল্লভ» (*gopīballabha*, Gopivallabha, 31); translated as in the text.

[^12-7]: «এ কারণে লিখিলুঁ বি·শষ তাহার» (*e kāraṇe likhiluṅ biśeṣa tāhāra*) — one letter is damaged in the edition; taken as «বিশেষ» (*biśeṣa*), "particular, selected". Possibly «না» (*nā*) has dropped out: "for this reason I have not written it down in detail" (cf. varna 11, 101).

[^12-8]: «অন্তর্দ্ধান» (*antardhāna*) — "disappearance": so Vaishnavas call the departure of the Lord's associate from the manifest lila. According to the author, Jagadisha went to Vrindavana (43, 46). In the second line of 43 the rhyme is imperfect (দিন / বৃন্দাবনে).

[^12-9]: «জীউ» (*jīu*) — the respectful "-ji". According to GGD 143, Jagadisha Pandita in Krishna-lila is Chandrahasa, a dancer of Vraja; Kalavati in GGD 188 is one of the sakhis who sang Vishakha's songs, and she is identified there with another associate. The identification of Jagadisha with Kalavati is the author's; cf. 45: "descended in a male form".

[^12-10]: «সূত্র বর্ণন» (*sūtra varṇana*) — "a description in the form of a sutra", i.e. briefly, only the essentials (cf. 69). On Bhagavatananda's order in a dream — varna 11, 104–106.

[^12-11]: In 53–68 the author lists the contents of the twelve varnas; cf. their colophons. In 54 «জন্মান্ন প্রাশন» (*janmānna prāśana*) — "birth and annaprashana".

[^12-12]: Cf. varna 7 and varna 6, note 9: in the CB and CC the episode of the offering for Vishnu on Ekadashi is connected with the Jagadisha of the pair "Jagadisha and Hiranya".

[^12-13]: Niladri — Puri. "Body" (কলেবর, *kalevara*) — the body of the Deity Jagannatha, brought by Jagadisha (varna 8, note 7).

[^12-14]: The source of the shloka has not been established. «সর্বাক্ষমং পতিত মার্ত্তম» is taken as *sarvākṣamaṁ patitam ārtam*. Stanza 82 paraphrases the first half of the shloka.

[^12-15]: After this stanza the edition has «ধ্রু» (*dhru*, "dhruva") — the refrain of the song; stanzas 81–107 are tripadi. «কৃপাকর» (*kṛpākara*) is taken as «কৃপা কর» (*kṛpā kara*), "have mercy"; Gorachand (গোরাচান্দ, *gorācānda*) — Gaurachandra.

[^12-16]: In the edition the punctuation in 93 is shifted («…বারে বারে ॥» / «…আমারে।»); the stanza is arranged by rhyme (বারে বারে / আমারে).

[^12-17]: «তব শাখা লীলা» (*taba śākhā līlā*) — "the lilas of Your branch": Jagadisha is a branch of the tree of Sri Chaitanya; in the CC Jagadisha Pandita is named among the branches of Nityananda (Adi 11).

[^12-18]: «ভাগবত» (*bhāgavata*, 95, 102) — Bhagavatananda, who commanded the author in a dream to write the book (cf. 51, 105–106; varna 11, note 18).

[^12-19]: Srinivasa (শ্রীনিবাস, *śrīnivāsa*) in 96 is Srivasa Pandita (in GGD 12, "Srinivasa Pandita"), named alongside Chaitanya, Nityananda, Advaita and Gadadhara; in 98 — Srinivasa Acharya, the disciple of Gopala Bhatta. [^12-20]: The author's signature is a variant of the formula.

[^12-21]: Before the colophon the edition has «পুষ্পিকাঃ» (*puṣpikāḥ*, "colophon"), apparently an editor's label. In the colophon «অন্তর্দ্ধান কথনং» (*antardhāna kathanaṁ*) — "the account of the disappearance".
