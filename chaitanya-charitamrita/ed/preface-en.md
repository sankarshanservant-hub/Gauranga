# Preface

Śrīla Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī’s *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta* is the principal biography of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and the summit of Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava theology. The author, a disciple of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī, wrote it in Vṛndāvana at the request of the Vaiṣṇavas, when he was already very old. According to the verse with which many manuscripts close, he completed it in the Śaka year 1537 (1615 CE). The book has three parts. The *Ādi-līlā* establishes the truth about the Lord and His associates and tells of His first twenty-four years in Navadvīpa. The *Madhya-līlā* follows His travels after He accepted *sannyāsa*. The *Antya-līlā* describes His last years in Purī, when, absorbed in the mood of Śrīmatī Rādhikā, He tasted the divine madness of separation from Kṛṣṇa. In the *Śrī Caitanya-bhāgavata* Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura described above all the pastimes of Navadvīpa. Kavirāja Gosvāmī revealed what he had left untold: the inner purpose of the Lord’s descent and the taste of His *prema*.

## The translation of the verses

The English translation of the verses and the word-for-word glosses were made directly from the Bengali, following the text of the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the *Anubhāṣya*. We aimed at a plain, dignified English that stays close to the original. Where the text of the edition differs from other manuscripts in a way that affects the meaning, a note says so.

The companion Russian edition gives the translation of Vṛndāvana Candra dāsa, revised verse by verse against the same edition. Chapters 16–19 of the *Antya-līlā*, which he did not translate, were rendered in 2026 in his style. For the first verse of the *Śikṣāṣṭaka* (Antya 20.12) the Russian edition gives the poetic rendering of Śrīla Bhakti Rakṣaka Śrīdhara Deva-Gosvāmī Mahārāja, a disciple of Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura.

## The commentaries

The complete edition contains two classical commentaries, translated here into English for the first time.

The ***Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*** (“The Stream of Nectar”) is by Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura and was published in the 1890s. It summarises each chapter and explains the difficult verses for a wide circle of readers.

The ***Anubhāṣya*** is by his son and successor, Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura. It first appeared in 1915, and the author later enlarged it for the fourth edition published in his lifetime by the Gauḍīya Maṭha (1928–29). Besides its explanations, it supplies section headings, the construction of the Sanskrit verses, and a wealth of references to scripture. The word of Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta is the final authority in this edition: disputed points of translation were settled by it.

The text and the numbering of the verses follow the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition of the 1928–29 line. The commentaries are translated in full, without abridgement. Where they quote the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, the quotation is given in the translation of this edition. Where a commentator understands a verse differently from the translation, a note points this out.

## On reading

The translation was checked through the eyes of a Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇava. The Lord is never subject to *māyā*. The Deity is not an image but the Lord Himself. The love of the *gopīs* is not worldly passion but the purest *prema*. The divine madness of Śrī Caitanya is not an illness but the highest expression of that love. The passing of the Lord and His associates from this world is called their departure, not their death.

**On the words “māyā” and “illusion”.** Wherever the book says that the Lord or His associates “forgot”, “did not know”, or were “under illusion” or “*māyā*”, what is meant is *yogamāyā*, the Lord’s own internal potency. It serves His pastimes by veiling His majesty from Him and His eternal associates, so that their love may be full and sweet. The Lord and His associates are never subject to the external *māyā* that covers the conditioned souls. This note applies to every such passage and is not repeated in the footnotes.

Bengali and Sanskrit words are given in standard diacritical transliteration. Names and terms are kept uniform throughout the book.

This edition was prepared with a prayer for the mercy of the Vaiṣṇavas. We ask forgiveness for its shortcomings. May it help the reader come to know Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu and His associates more closely.
