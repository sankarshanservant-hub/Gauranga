“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 19  
**The Ravings of Separation, the Rubbing of His Face against the Walls, and Other Pastimes**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে তং কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যং মাতৃভক্তশিরোমণিম্ ৷  
প্রলপ্য মুখসংঘর্ষী মধুদ্যানে ললাস যঃ ॥ ১ ॥

vande taṁ kṛṣṇa-caitanyaṁ  
I bow down  to Him  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  
mātṛ-bhakta-śiromaṇim  
of those devoted to their mothers  the crown jewel  
pralapya mukha-saṅgharṣī  
raving  His face  rubbing  
madhudyāne lalāsa yaḥ  
in the spring garden  sported  who

(1) I bow down to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the crown jewel of all who are devoted to their mothers — to Him who, raving in His madness, rubbed His face against the walls and sported in the spring garden.[^1][^2]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এইমতে মহাপ্রভু কৃষ্ণপ্রেমাবেশে ৷  
উন্মাদ-প্রলাপ করে রাত্রি-দিবসে ॥ ৩ ॥

ei-mate mahāprabhu kṛṣṇa-premāveśe  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  for Kṛṣṇa  absorbed in love  
unmāda-pralāpa kare rātri-divase  
mad  ravings  makes  night  and day

(3) In this way Mahāprabhu, absorbed in love for Kṛṣṇa, raved night and day in divine madness.

প্রভুর অত্যন্ত প্রিয় পণ্ডিত-জগদানন্দ ৷  
যাহার চরিত্রে প্রভু পায়েন আনন্দ ॥ ৪ ॥

prabhura atyanta priya paṇḍita-jagadānanda  
to the Lord  very  dear  Paṇḍita  Jagadānanda  
yāhāra caritre prabhu pāyena ānanda  
in whose  conduct  the Lord  finds  joy

(4) Jagadānanda Paṇḍita was very dear to the Lord, who delighted in all his ways.

প্রতিবৎসর প্রভু তাঁরে পাঠান নদীয়াতে ৷  
বিচ্ছেদ-দুঃখিতা জানি’ জননী আশ্বাসিতে ॥ ৫ ॥

prati-vatsara prabhu tām̐re pāṭhāna nadīyāte  
every  year  the Lord  him  sends  to Nadia  
viccheda-duḥkhitā jāni’ jananī āśvāsite  
by separation  grieved  knowing  His mother  to console

(5) Every year the Lord sent him to Nadia to console His mother, for He knew how she grieved in separation from Him.

নদীয়া চলহ, মাতারে কহিহ নমস্কার ৷  
আমার নামে পাদপদ্ম ধরিহ তাঁহার ॥ ৬ ॥

nadīyā calaha, mātāre kahiha namaskāra  
to Nadia  go  to Mother  tell  My obeisances  
āmāra nāme pāda-padma dhariha tām̐hāra  
in My  name  the lotus feet  touch  her

(6) “Go to Nadia and convey My obeisances to Mother. Touch her lotus feet on My behalf.

কহিহ তাঁহারে — ‘তুমি করহ স্মরণ ৷  
নিত্য আসি’ তোমার বন্দিয়ে চরণ ॥ ৭ ॥

kahiha tām̐hāre — ‘tumi karaha smaraṇa  
tell  her  you  please remember  
nitya āsi’ tomāra vandiye caraṇa  
every day  coming  your  I worship  feet

(7) Tell her: ‘Please remember — every day I come and bow at your feet.

যে-দিনে তোমার ইচ্ছা করাইতে ভোজন ৷  
সে-দিনে অবশ্য আমি করিয়ে ভক্ষণ ॥ ৮ ॥

ye-dine tomāra icchā karāite bhojana  
on whatever  day  your  wish  to feed (Me)  
se-dine avaśya āmi kariye bhakṣaṇa  
on that  day  surely  I  do  eat

(8) Whenever you wish to feed Me, on that day I surely come and eat.

তোমার সেবা ছাড়ি’ আমি করিলুঁ সন্ন্যাস ৷  
‘বাউল’ হঞা আমি কৈলুঁ ধর্ম্মনাশ ॥ ৯ ॥

tomāra sevā chāḍi’ āmi karilum̐ sannyāsa  
your  service  giving up  I  took  sannyāsa  
‘bāula’ hañā āmi kailum̐ dharma-nāśa  
a madman  becoming  I  made  of duty  ruin

(9) I gave up serving you and took *sannyāsa*. I became a madman and abandoned My duty.

এই অপরাধ তুমি না লইহ আমার ৷  
তোমার অধীন আমি — পুত্র সে তোমার ॥ ১০ ॥

ei aparādha tumi nā laiha āmāra  
this  offence  you  not  take  My  
tomāra adhīna āmi — putra se tomāra  
on you  dependent  I  son  indeed  your

(10) Do not hold this offence against Me. I am dependent on you — I am your son.

নীলাচলে আছি আমি তোমার আজ্ঞাতে ৷  
যাবৎ জীব, তাবৎ আমি নারিব ছাড়িতে ॥ ১১ ॥

nīlācale āchi āmi tomāra ājñāte  
in Nīlācala  stay  I  your  by order  
yāvat jība, tāvat āmi nāriba chāḍite  
as long as  I live  so long  I  cannot  leave

(11) I stay in Nīlācala by your order, and as long as I live I cannot leave it.’ ”

গোপ-লীলায় পাইলা যেই প্রসাদ-বসনে ৷  
মাতারে পাঠান তাহা পুরীর বচনে ॥ ১২ ॥

gopa-līlāya pāilā yei prasāda-vasane  
in the cowherd  pastime  received  which  prasāda  cloth  
mātāre pāṭhāna tāhā purīra vacane  
to Mother  sends  that  of Purī  at the word

(12) At the request of Paramānanda Purī, the Lord would send His mother the cloth *prasāda* He had received at the festival of Jagannātha’s cowherd pastime.[^3]

জগন্নাথের উত্তম প্রসাদ আনিয়া যতনে ৷  
মাতারে পৃথক্ পাঠান, আর ভক্তগণে ॥ ১৩ ॥

jagannāthera uttama prasāda āniyā yatane  
Jagannātha’s  best  prasāda  bringing  with care  
mātāre pṛthak pāṭhāna, āra bhakta-gaṇe  
to Mother  separately  sends  and  to the devotees

(13) He also took care to obtain the best of Jagannātha’s *prasāda*, and He sent it separately to His mother and to the devotees.

মাতৃভক্তগণের প্রভু হন শিরোমণি ৷  
সন্ন্যাস করিয়া সদা সেবেন জননী ॥ ১৪ ॥

mātṛ-bhakta-gaṇera prabhu hana śiromaṇi  
of those devoted to their mothers  the Lord  is  the crown jewel  
sannyāsa kariyā sadā sevena jananī  
sannyāsa  having taken  always  serves  His mother

(14) The Lord is the crown jewel of all who are devoted to their mothers. Even after taking *sannyāsa*, He always served His mother.[^4]

জগদানন্দ নদীয়া গিয়া মাতারে মিলিলা ৷  
প্রভুর যত নিবেদন, সকল কহিলা ॥ ১৫ ॥

jagadānanda nadīyā giyā mātāre mililā  
Jagadānanda  to Nadia  going  with Mother  met  
prabhura yata nivedana, sakala kahilā  
the Lord’s  as many  messages  all  told

(15) Arriving in Nadia, Jagadānanda met Mother Śacī and gave her all the Lord’s messages.

আচার্য্যাদি ভক্তগণে মিলিলা প্রসাদ দিয়া ৷  
মাতা-ঠাঞি আজ্ঞা লইলা মাসেক রহিয়া ॥ ১৬ ॥

ācāryādi bhakta-gaṇe mililā prasāda diyā  
the Ācārya and other  devotees  met  prasāda  giving  
mātā-ṭhāñi ājñā lailā māseka rahiyā  
from Mother  leave  took  a month  staying

(16) He also visited Advaita Ācārya and the other devotees and gave them *prasāda*. After staying a month, he took leave of Mother Śacī.

আচার্য্যের ঠাঞি গিয়া আজ্ঞা মাগিলা ৷  
আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি প্রভুরে সন্দেশ কহিলা ॥ ১৭ ॥

ācāryera ṭhāñi giyā ājñā māgilā  
of the Ācārya  to  going  leave  asked  
ācārya-gosāñi prabhure sandeśa kahilā  
Ācārya  Gosāñi  for the Lord  a message  gave

(17) He went to the Ācārya to take leave of him, and Ācārya Gosāñi gave him a message for the Lord.

তরজা-প্রহেলী আচার্য্য কহেন ঠারে ঠোরে ৷  
প্রভুমাত্র বুঝেন, কেহ বুঝিতে না পারে ॥ ১৮ ॥

tarajā-prahelī ācārya kahena ṭhāre ṭhore  
a tarjā  riddle  the Ācārya  speaks  in hints  
prabhu-mātra bujhena, keha bujhite nā pāre  
the Lord  only  understands  anyone  understand  not  can

(18) The Ācārya spoke in riddles and veiled hints (*tarjā-prahelī*), which only the Lord could understand; no one else could.[^5]

প্রভুরে কহিহ আমার কোটি নমস্কার ৷  
এই নিবেদন তাঁর চরণে আমার ॥ ১৯ ॥

prabhure kahiha āmāra koṭi namaskāra  
to the Lord  tell  my  ten million  obeisances  
ei nivedana tām̐ra caraṇe āmāra  
this  submission  His  at the feet  my

(19) “Convey my countless obeisances to the Lord, and lay this submission of mine at His feet:

বাউলকে কহিহ, — লোক হইল বাউল ৷  
বাউলকে কহিহ, — হাটে না বিকায় চাউল ॥ ২০ ॥

bāulake kahiha, — loka haila bāula  
to the madman  tell  people  have become  mad  
bāulake kahiha, — hāṭe nā vikāya cāula  
to the madman  tell  in the market  not  sells  rice

(20) Tell the madman (*bāul*) that the people have gone mad. Tell the madman that rice no longer sells in the market.[^6]

বাউলকে কহিহ, — কাযে নাহিক আউল ৷  
বাউলকে কহিহ, — ইহা কহিয়াছে বাউল ॥ ২১ ॥

bāulake kahiha, — kāye nāhika āula  
to the madman  tell  in work  is not  the āul  
bāulake kahiha, — ihā kahiyāche bāula  
to the madman  tell  this  has said  a madman

(21) Tell the madman that the *āul* — the one mad with love — is no longer engaged in worldly affairs. Tell the madman that a madman has said this.”[^7]

এত শুনি’ জগদানন্দ হাসিতে লাগিলা ৷  
নীলাচলে আসি’ তবে প্রভুরে কহিলা ॥ ২২ ॥

eta śuni’ jagadānanda hāsite lāgilā  
this  hearing  Jagadānanda  to laugh  began  
nīlācale āsi’ tabe prabhure kahilā  
to Nīlācala  coming  then  to the Lord  told

(22) Hearing this, Jagadānanda began to laugh. Then he returned to Nīlācala and told the Lord everything.

তরজা শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু ঈষৎ হাসিলা ৷  
‘তাঁর যেই আজ্ঞা’ — বলি’ মৌন ধরিলা ॥ ২৩ ॥

tarajā śuni’ mahāprabhu īṣat hāsilā  
the tarjā  hearing  Mahāprabhu  slightly  smiled  
‘tām̐ra yei ājñā’ — bali’ mauna dharilā  
his  whatever  order  saying  silence  kept

(23) Mahāprabhu heard the *tarjā*, smiled slightly and said, “That is his order.” Then He fell silent.

জানিয়া স্বরূপ গোসাঞি প্রভুরে পুছিল ৷  
‘এই তরজার অর্থ বুঝিতে নারিল’ ॥ ২৪ ॥

jāniyā svarūpa gosāñi prabhure puchila  
learning (of this)  Svarūpa  Gosāñi  the Lord  asked  
‘ei tarajāra artha bujhite nārila’  
this  of the tarjā  the meaning  to understand  I could not

(24) Learning of this, Svarūpa Gosāñi asked the Lord, “I could not understand the meaning of this *tarjā*.”[^8]

প্রভু কহেন, — ‘আচার্য্য হয় পূজক প্রবল ৷  
আগম-শাস্ত্রের বিধি-বিধানে কুশল ॥ ২৫ ॥

prabhu kahena, — ‘ācārya haya pūjaka prabala  
the Lord  says  the Ācārya  is  a worshipper  great  
āgama-śāstrera vidhi-vidhāne kuśala  
of the Āgama  scriptures  in the rules  and regulations  expert

(25) The Lord said, “The Ācārya is a great worshipper, expert in the rules and regulations of the Āgama scriptures.[^9]

উপাসনা লাগি’ দেবের করেন আবাহন ৷  
পূজা লাগি’ কত কাল করেন নিরোধন ॥ ২৬ ॥

upāsanā lāgi’ devera karena āvāhana  
for worship  the Deity  makes  invocation  
pūjā lāgi’ kata kāla karena nirodhana  
for worship  some  time  makes  keeping (Him) present

(26) For worship he invokes the Deity, and keeps Him present as long as the *pūjā* lasts.[^10]

পূজা নির্ব্বাহণ হৈলে পাছে করেন বিসর্জ্জন ৷  
তরজার না জানি অর্থ কিবা তাঁর মন ॥ ২৭ ॥

pūjā nirvāhaṇa haile pāche karena visarjana  
the worship  completion  when  afterwards  makes  sending away  
tarajāra nā jāni artha kibā tām̐ra mana  
of the tarjā  not  I know  the meaning  what  his  mind

(27) When the *pūjā* is over, he bids Him depart. I know neither the meaning of this *tarjā* nor what is in his mind.

মহাযোগেশ্বর আচার্য্য — তরজাতে সমর্থ ৷  
আমিহ বুঝিতে নারি তরজার অর্থ’ ॥ ২৮ ॥

mahā-yogeśvara ācārya — tarajāte samartha  
a great  master of mystic power  the Ācārya  in tarjās  skilled  
āmiha bujhite nāri tarajāra artha’  
even I  to understand  cannot  of the tarjā  the meaning

(28) The Ācārya is a great master of yoga, skilled in veiled sayings. Even I cannot fathom the meaning of his *tarjā*.”

শুনিয়া বিস্মিত হইলা সব ভক্তগণ ৷  
স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি কিছু হইলা বিমন ॥ ২৯ ॥

śuniyā vismita hailā saba bhakta-gaṇa  
hearing  astonished  became  all  the devotees  
svarūpa-gosāñi kichu hailā vimana  
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  somewhat  became  downcast

(29) Hearing this, all the devotees were astonished, and Svarūpa Gosāñi became somewhat downcast.

সেই দিন হৈতে প্রভুর আর দশা হইল ৷  
কৃষ্ণের বিরহ-দশা দ্বিগুণ বাড়িল ॥ ৩০ ॥

sei dina haite prabhura āra daśā haila  
that  day  from  of the Lord  another  state  arose  
kṛṣṇera viraha-daśā dviguṇa bāḍila  
of Kṛṣṇa  separation’s  state  doubly  increased

(30) From that day the Lord’s condition changed: His feeling of separation from Kṛṣṇa grew twice as strong.

উন্মাদ-প্রলাপ-চেষ্টা করে রাত্রি-দিনে ৷  
রাধা-ভাবাবেশে বিরহ বাড়ে অনুক্ষণে ॥ ৩১ ॥

unmāda-pralāpa-ceṣṭā kare rātri-dine  
of madness  ravings  behaviour  makes  night  and day  
rādhā-bhāvāveśe viraha bāḍe anukṣaṇe  
in Rādhā’s  feeling  absorbed  separation  grows  every moment

(31) Night and day He behaved and raved like one mad. Absorbed in the feelings of Rādhā, He felt the separation grow keener every moment.

আচম্বিতে স্ফুরে কৃষ্ণের মথুরা-গমন ৷  
উদ্ঘূর্ণা-দশা হৈল উন্মাদ লক্ষণ ॥ ৩২ ॥

ācambite sphure kṛṣṇera mathurā-gamana  
suddenly  appears  Kṛṣṇa’s  to Mathurā  departure  
udghūrṇā-daśā haila unmāda lakṣaṇa  
of udghūrṇā  the state  arose  of madness  a symptom

(32) Suddenly Kṛṣṇa’s departure for Mathurā rose before Him, and He was seized by *udghūrṇā*, a symptom of divine madness.

রামানন্দের গলা ধরি’ করেন প্রলাপন ৷  
স্বরূপে পুছেন জানি’ নিজ-সখীগণ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

rāmānandera galā dhari’ karena pralāpana  
Rāmānanda’s  neck  holding  makes  ravings  
svarūpe puchena jāni’ nija-sakhī-gaṇa  
Svarūpa  asks  taking (him) as  His own  friends

(33) Clasping Rāmānanda round the neck, He raved, and He questioned Svarūpa, taking him for one of His own friends (*sakhīs*).

পূর্ব্বে যেন বিশাখারে রাধিকা পুছিলা ৷  
সেই শ্লোক পড়ি’ প্রলাপ করিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৩৪ ॥

pūrve yena viśākhāre rādhikā puchilā  
long ago  as  Viśākhā  Rādhikā  asked  
sei śloka paḍi’ pralāpa karite lāgilā  
that  verse  reciting  ravings  to make  began

(34) Reciting the verse with which Rādhikā once questioned Viśākhā, He began to rave:

ক্ব নন্দকুলচন্দ্রমাঃ ক্ব শিখিচন্দ্রকালঙ্কৃতিঃ  
ক্ব মন্দ্রমুরলীরবঃ ক্ব নু সুরেন্দ্রনীলদ্যুতিঃ ৷  
ক্ব রাসরসতাণ্ডবী ক্ব সখি জীবরক্ষৌষধি-  
নিধির্ম্মম সুহৃত্তমঃ ক্ব বত হন্ত হা ধিগ্বিধিম্ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kva nanda-kula-candramāḥ kva śikhi-candrakālaṅkṛtiḥ  
where  of Nanda’s  dynasty  the moon  where  with peacock feathers  adorned  
kva mandra-muralī-ravaḥ kva nu surendra-nīla-dyutiḥ  
where  deep  of the flute  the sound  where  indeed  of the sapphire  the lustre  
kva rāsa-rasa-tāṇḍavī kva sakhi jīva-rakṣauṣadhi-  
where  in the rāsa  with rapture  dancing  where  O friend  of life  the saving medicine (-)  
nidhir mama suhṛt-tamaḥ kva bata hanta hā dhig vidhim  
treasure  My  dearest friend  where  alas  alas  ah  fie on  the Creator

(35) “O friend, where is the moon of Nanda’s line? Where is He who is adorned with a peacock feather? Where is He whose flute sounds so deep, whose lustre is like the sapphire’s? Where is He who dances, enraptured by the taste of the *rāsa*? Where is He — the medicine that saves My life, My treasure, My dearest friend? Alas, alas! Fie on the Creator!” (Lalita-mādhava, 3.25)[^11]

ব্রজেন্দ্রকুল — দুগ্ধসিন্ধু, কৃষ্ণ — তাহে পূর্ণ ইন্দু,  
জন্মি’ কৈলা জগৎ উজোর ৷  
কান্ত্যমৃত যেবা পিয়ে, নিরন্তর পিয়া জিয়ে,  
ব্রজ-জনের নয়ন-চকোর ॥ ৩৬ ॥

vrajendra-kula — dugdha-sindhu,  
of the king of Vraja  the dynasty  the ocean of milk  
kṛṣṇa — tāhe pūrṇa indu,  
Kṛṣṇa  in it  the full  moon  
janmi’ kailā jagat ujora  
being born  made  the world  illumined  
kānty-amṛta yebā piye,  
of lustre  the nectar  who  drink  
nirantara piyā jiye,  
constantly  drinking  live  
vraja-janera nayana-cakora  
of Vraja’s  people  the eyes  cakora birds

(36) “The line of Vraja’s king is an ocean of milk, and Kṛṣṇa is the full moon within it: by His birth He has lit up the world. The eyes of the people of Vraja are *cakora* birds: they drink the nectar of His lustre constantly, and only by drinking it do they live.[^12]

সখি হে, কোথা কৃষ্ণ, করাহ দরশন ৷  
ক্ষণেকে যাহার মুখ, না দেখিলে ফাটে বুক,  
শীঘ্র দেখাহ, না রহে জীবন ॥ ৩৭ ॥

sakhi he, kothā kṛṣṇa, karāha daraśana  
O friend  O  where  Kṛṣṇa  let (Me) see  
kṣaṇeke yāhāra mukha,  
for a moment  whose  face  
nā dekhile phāṭe buka,  
not  seeing  breaks  the heart  
śīghra dekhāha, nā rahe jīvana  
quickly  show  not  remains  life

(37) O friend, where is Kṛṣṇa? Let Me see Him! If I do not see His face even for a moment, My heart breaks. Show Him to Me quickly, or My life will not remain.[^13]

এই ব্রজের রমণী, কামার্কতপ্ত-কুমুদিনী,  
নিজ-করামৃত দিয়া দান ৷  
প্রফুল্লিত করে যেই, কাঁহা মোর চন্দ্র সেই,  
দেখাহ, সখি, রাখ মোর প্রাণ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

ei vrajera ramaṇī,  
these  of Vraja  the women  
kāmārka-tapta-kumudinī,  
by the sun of desire  scorched  night lotuses  
nija-karāmṛta diyā dāna  
own  of the rays (hands)  the nectar  giving  as a gift  
praphullita kare yei,  
blossoming  makes  who  
kām̐hā mora candra sei,  
where  My  moon  that  
dekhāha, sakhi, rākha mora prāṇa  
show  O friend  save  My  life

(38) The women of Vraja are night-blooming lotuses scorched by the sun of loving desire (*kāma*). Where is that moon of Mine who makes them blossom with the gift of the nectar of His rays — His hands? O friend, show Him to Me and save My life![^14]

কাঁহা সে চূড়ার ঠাম, শিখিপিঞ্ছের উড়ান,  
নব মেঘে যেন ইন্দ্রধনু ৷  
পীতাম্বর — তড়িদ্দ্যুতি, মুক্তামালা — বকপাঁতি,  
নবাম্বুদ জিনি’ শ্যামতনু ॥ ৩৯ ॥

kām̐hā se cūḍāra ṭhāma,  
where  that  of the crown  the beauty  
śikhi-piñchera uḍāna,  
of the peacock feathers  the waving  
nava meghe yena indra-dhanu  
in a new cloud  like  a rainbow  
pītāmbara — taḍid-dyuti,  
the yellow garment  lightning’s  flash  
muktā-mālā — baka-pām̐ti,  
the pearl  necklace  a line of herons  
navāmbuda jini’ śyāma-tanu  
a new cloud  surpassing  the dark body

(39) Where is the beauty of His crown, with its waving peacock feathers, like a rainbow in a fresh cloud? His yellow garment is a flash of lightning, His pearl necklace a line of herons, and His dark body outshines a new rain cloud.

একবার যার নয়নে লাগে, সদা তার হৃদয়ে জাগে,  
কৃষ্ণতনু — যেন আম্র-আঠা ৷  
নারী-মনে পশি’ যায়, যত্নে নাহি বাহিরায়,  
তনু নহে, — সেয়াকুলের কাঁটা ॥ ৪০ ॥

eka-bāra yāra nayane lāge,  
once  whose  in the eyes  catches  
sadā tāra hṛdaye jāge,  
always  his  in the heart  stays awake  
kṛṣṇa-tanu — yena āmra-āṭhā  
Kṛṣṇa’s  body  like  mango  sap  
nārī-mane paśi’ yāya,  
of women  into the mind  entering  goes  
yatne nāhi bāhirāya,  
with effort  not  comes out  
tanu nahe, — seyā-kulera kām̐ṭā  
a body  is not  of the seyākul  a thorn

(40) Once Kṛṣṇa’s body has met someone’s eyes, it lives on in that heart for ever, clinging like the sap of the mango tree. It enters a woman’s mind and will not come out, however hard she tries. It is not a body — it is a thorn of the *seyākul* bush![^15]

জিনিয়া তমালদ্যুতি, ইন্দ্রনীল-সম কান্তি,  
সে কান্তিতে জগৎ মাতায় ৷  
শৃঙ্গার-রস-সার-ছানি’ তাতে চন্দ্র-জ্যোৎস্না সানি’,  
জানি’ বিধি নিরমিলা তায় ॥ ৪১ ॥

jiniyā tamāla-dyuti,  
surpassing  the tamāla’s  lustre  
indranīla-sama kānti,  
to the sapphire  equal  the lustre  
se kāntite jagat mātāya  
by that  lustre  the world  maddens  
śṛṅgāra-rasa-sāra-chāni’,  
of amorous  rasa  the essence  straining  
tāte candra-jyotsnā sāni’,  
with it  of the moon  the light  mixing  
jāni’ vidhi niramilā tāya  
knowing  the Creator  fashioned  Him

(41) His lustre, like a sapphire’s, outshines the dark *tamāla* tree, and with it He maddens the world. With all his skill the Creator fashioned Him: he strained out the very essence of the *rasa* of love and mixed it with moonlight.[^16]

কাঁহা সে মুরলীধ্বনি, নবাম্বুদ-গর্জ্জিত জিনি’,  
জগৎ আকর্ষে শ্রবণে যাহার ৷  
উঠি’ ধায় ব্রজ-জন, তৃষিত চাতকগণ,  
আসি’ পিয়ে কান্ত্যমৃত-ধার ॥ ৪২ ॥

kām̐hā se muralī-dhvani,  
where  that  of the flute  the sound  
navāmbuda-garjita jini’,  
of a new cloud  the thunder  surpassing  
jagat ākarṣe śravaṇe yāhāra  
the world  attracts  to the hearing  of which  
uṭhi’ dhāya vraja-jana,  
rising  run  of Vraja  the people  
tṛṣita cātaka-gaṇa,  
thirsty  cātaka birds  
āsi’ piye kānty-amṛta-dhāra  
coming  drink  of lustre  the nectar  streams

(42) Where is the sound of that flute which surpasses the thunder of a fresh cloud and draws the whole world to hear it? At its sound the people of Vraja rise and run like thirsty *cātaka* birds, and come to drink the streams of the nectar of His lustre.

মোর সেই কলানিধি, প্রাণরক্ষা-মহৌষধি,  
সখি মোর তেঁহো সুহৃত্তম ৷  
দেহ জীয়ে তাঁহা বিনে, ধিক্ এই জীবনে,  
বিধি করে এত বিড়ম্বন ! ॥ ৪৩ ॥

mora sei kalā-nidhi,  
My  that  treasury of arts (the moon)  
prāṇa-rakṣā-mahauṣadhi,  
of life  saving  the great medicine  
sakhi mora tem̐ho suhṛttama  
O friend  My  He  dearest friend  
deha jīye tām̐hā vine,  
the body  lives  Him  without  
dhik ei jīvane,  
fie  on this  life  
vidhi kare eta viḍambana!  
the Creator  makes  such  mockery

(43) He is My moon, the treasury of all arts, the great medicine that saves My life. O friend, He is My dearest friend. Yet My body lives on without Him — fie on such a life! What a mockery the Creator has made of Me![^17]

‘যে-জন জীতে নাহি চায়, তারে কেনে জীয়ায়’,  
বিধিপ্রতি উঠে ক্রোধ-শোক ৷  
বিধিরে করে ভর্ৎসন, কৃষ্ণে দেন ওলাহন,  
পড়ি’ ভাগবতের এক শ্লোক ॥ ৪৪ ॥

‘ye-jana jīte nāhi cāya,  
who  a person  to live  not  wants  
tāre kene jīyāya’,  
him  why  makes live  
vidhi-prati uṭhe krodha-śoka  
the Creator  against  rises  anger  and grief  
vidhire kare bhartsana,  
the Creator  makes  rebuke  
kṛṣṇe dena olāhana,  
Kṛṣṇa  gives  reproach  
paḍi’ bhāgavatera eka śloka  
reciting  of the Bhāgavatam  one  verse

(44) “Why does he make one live who does not want to live?” — so anger and grief rose in Him against the Creator. Reciting a verse from the *Bhāgavatam*, He rebuked the Creator and reproached Kṛṣṇa:

অহো বিধাতস্তব ন ক্বচিদ্দয়া  
সংযোজ্য মৈত্র্য্যা প্রণয়েন দেহিনঃ ৷  
তাংশ্চাকৃতার্থান্ বিযুনঙ্ক্ষ্যপার্থকং  
বিচেষ্টিতং তেঽর্ভকচেষ্টিতং যথা ॥ ৪৫ ॥

aho vidhātas tava na kvacid dayā  
O  Creator  your  not  anywhere  mercy  
saṁyojya maitryā praṇayena dehinaḥ  
having joined  by friendship  by love  embodied beings  
tāṁś cākṛtārthān viyunaṅkṣy apārthakaṁ  
them  and  unfulfilled  you separate  pointlessly  
viceṣṭitaṁ te ’rbhaka-ceṣṭitaṁ yathā  
the doings  your  of a child  the doings  like

(45) “O Creator, there is no mercy in you anywhere! You join embodied beings in friendship and love, and then, before their desires are fulfilled, you pointlessly separate them. Your doings are like the play of a child.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.39.19)[^18]

না জানিস্ প্রেম-মর্ম্ম, ব্যর্থ করিস্ পরিশ্রম,  
তোর চেষ্টা — বালক-সমান ৷  
তোর যদি লাগ্ পাইয়ে, তবে তোরে শিক্ষা দিয়ে,  
এমন যেন না করিস্ বিধান ॥ ৪৬ ॥

nā jānis prema-marma,  
not  you know  of love  the heart  
vyartha karis pariśrama,  
in vain  you make  labour  
tora ceṣṭā — bālaka-samāna  
your  doings  a child  like  
tora yadi lāg pāiye,  
you  if  hold of  I get  
tabe tore śikṣā diye,  
then  to you  a lesson  I will give  
emana yena nā karis vidhāna  
such  that  not  you make  arrangement

(46) “You do not know the heart of love, and you labour in vain. Your doings are like a child’s. If I can only get hold of you, I will teach you such a lesson that you will never arrange such things again![^19]

অরে বিধি, তুই বড়ই নিষ্ঠুর ৷  
অন্যোঽন্য দুর্লভ জন, প্রেমে করাঞা সম্মিলন,  
‘অকৃতার্থান্’ কেনে করিস্ দূর ? ॥ ৪৭ ॥

are vidhi, tui baḍai niṣṭhura  
O  Creator  you  very  cruel  
anyo’nya durlabha jana,  
for each other  hard to win  people  
preme karāñā sammilana,  
in love  making  union  
‘akṛtārthān’ kene karis dūra?  
unfulfilled  why  you make  far apart

(47) O Creator, you are very cruel! You bring together in love those who are so hard for each other to win. Why then do you part them before their desires are fulfilled?[^20]

অরে বিধি অকরুণ, দেখাঞা কৃষ্ণানন,  
নেত্র-মন লোভাইলা মোর ৷  
ক্ষণেকে করিতে পান, কাড়ি’ নিলা অন্যস্থান,  
পাপ কৈলি ‘দত্ত-অপহার’ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

are vidhi akaruṇa,  
O  Creator  merciless  
dekhāñā kṛṣṇānana,  
showing  Kṛṣṇa’s face  
netra-mana lobhāilā mora  
eyes  mind  you tempted  My  
kṣaṇeke karite pāna,  
for a moment  to make  drinking  
kāḍi’ nilā anya-sthāna,  
snatching  you took  to another  place  
pāpa kaili ‘datta-apahāra’  
sin  you committed  of the given  taking back

(48) Merciless Creator! You showed Me Kṛṣṇa’s face and stirred a thirst in My eyes and mind. But I had drunk of it for only a moment when you snatched it away and carried it off. You have committed the sin of taking back what you gave.[^21]

‘অক্রূর করে তোর দোষ, আমায় কেনে কর রোষ’,  
ইহা যদি কহ ‘দুরাচার’ ৷  
তুই অক্রূর-মূর্ত্তি ধরি’, কৃষ্ণ নিলি চুরি করি’,  
অন্যের নহে ঐছে ব্যবহার ॥ ৪৯ ॥

‘akrūra kare tora doṣa,  
Akrūra  makes  your  offence  
āmāya kene kara roṣa’,  
to me  why  you make  anger  
ihā yadi kaha ‘durācāra’  
this  if  you say  O villain  
tui akrūra-mūrti dhari’,  
you  Akrūra’s  form  assuming  
kṛṣṇa nili curi kari’,  
Kṛṣṇa  you took  theft  making  
anyera nahe aiche vyavahāra  
of another  is not  such  behaviour

(49) If you say, villain, ‘It is Akrūra who has wronged You — why are You angry with me?’ — then know that it was you who took the form of Akrūra and stole Kṛṣṇa away. No one else behaves like this.

আপনার কর্ম্ম-দোষ, তোরে কিবা করি রোষ,  
তোর আমার সম্বন্ধ বিদূর ৷  
যে আমার প্রাণনাথ, একত্র রহি যাঁর সাথ,  
সেই কৃষ্ণ হইলা নিষ্ঠুর ! ॥ ৫০ ॥

āpanāra karma-doṣa,  
My own  of deeds  the fault  
tore kibā kari roṣa,  
with you  why  I make  anger  
tora āmāra sambandha vidūra  
your  My  relation  far distant  
ye āmāra prāṇa-nātha,  
who  My  of life  the Lord  
ekatra rahi yām̐ra sātha,  
together  I lived  with whom  in company  
sei kṛṣṇa hailā niṣṭhura!  
that  Kṛṣṇa  became  cruel

(50) But it is the fault of My own deeds — why should I be angry with you? There is nothing between you and Me. It is Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of My life, from whom I was never apart, who has become so cruel!

সব ত্যজি’ ভজি যাঁরে, সেই আপনা-হাতে মারে,  
নারীবধে কৃষ্ণের নাহি ভয় ৷  
তাঁর লাগি’ আমি মরি, উলটি’ না চাহে হরি,  
ক্ষণমাত্রে ভাঙ্গিল প্রণয় ॥ ৫১ ॥

saba tyaji’ bhaji yām̐re,  
all  giving up  I serve  whom  
sei āpanā-hāte māre,  
He  with His own  hand  kills  
nārī-vadhe kṛṣṇera nāhi bhaya  
in killing  a woman  Kṛṣṇa’s  is not  fear  
tām̐ra lāgi’ āmi mari,  
Him  for  I  die  
ulaṭi’ nā cāhe hari,  
turning back  not  looks  Hari  
kṣaṇa-mātre bhāṅgila praṇaya  
in a moment  broke  the love

(51) He whom I serve, having renounced everything, kills Me with His own hand. Kṛṣṇa has no fear of killing a woman. I am dying for Him, yet Hari will not even turn back to look at Me. In a moment He has broken our love.[^22]

কৃষ্ণে কেনে করি রোষ, আপন দুর্দ্দৈব-দোষ,  
পাকিল মোর এই পাপফল ৷  
যে কৃষ্ণ — মোর প্রেমাধীন, তারে কৈল উদাসীন,  
এই মোর অভাগ্য প্রবল ॥ ৫২ ॥

kṛṣṇe kene kari roṣa,  
with Kṛṣṇa  why  I make  anger  
āpana durdaiva-doṣa,  
My own  ill fate’s  the fault  
pākila mora ei pāpa-phala  
has ripened  My  this  of sin  the fruit  
ye kṛṣṇa — mora premādhīna,  
who  Kṛṣṇa  My  to love subject  
tāre kaila udāsīna,  
Him  made  indifferent  
ei mora abhāgya prabala  
this  My  misfortune  mighty

(52) But why should I be angry with Kṛṣṇa? It is the fault of My own ill fate: the fruit of My sins has ripened. Kṛṣṇa is subject to My love, yet My mighty misfortune has made Him indifferent to Me!”

এইমত গৌর-রায়, বিষাদে করে হায় হায়,  
‘হাহা কৃষ্ণ, তুমি গেলা কতি ?’  
গোপীভাব হৃদয়ে, তার বাক্য বিলাপয়ে,  
‘গোবিন্দ দামোদর মাধবেতি’ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

ei-mata gaura-rāya,  
in this way  Gaura  Rāya  
viṣāde kare hāya hāya,  
in grief  makes  alas  alas  
‘hāhā kṛṣṇa, tumi gelā kati?’  
alas  Kṛṣṇa  You  went  where  
gopī-bhāva hṛdaye,  
of the gopīs  the feeling  in the heart  
tāra vākya vilāpaye,  
their  words  lamenting  
‘govinda dāmodara mādhaveti’  
Govinda  Dāmodara  Mādhava (thus)

(53) In this way Gaura Rāya cried in grief, “Alas! Alas! O Kṛṣṇa, where have You gone?” With the feelings of the *gopīs* in His heart, He lamented in their words: “O Govinda! O Dāmodara! O Mādhava!”

তবে স্বরূপ-রামরায়, করি’ নানা উপায়,  
মহাপ্রভুর করে আশ্বাসন ৷  
গায়েন মঙ্গল-গীত, প্রভুর ফিরাইলা চিত,  
প্রভুর কিছু স্থির হৈল মন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

tabe svarūpa-rāma-rāya,  
then  Svarūpa  Rāma  Rāya  
kari’ nānā upāya,  
making  various  means  
mahāprabhura kare āśvāsana  
of Mahāprabhu  make  consoling  
gāyena maṅgala-gīta,  
they sing  auspicious  songs  
prabhura phirāilā cita,  
the Lord’s  turned  mind  
prabhura kichu sthira haila mana  
the Lord’s  somewhat  calm  became  mind

(54) Then Svarūpa and Rāmānanda Rāya tried in various ways to console Mahāprabhu. They sang auspicious songs to turn His mind, and the Lord grew somewhat calmer.[^23]

এইমত প্রলাপিতে অর্দ্ধরাত্রি গেল ৷  
গম্ভীরাতে স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি প্রভুরে শোয়াইল ॥ ৫৫ ॥

ei-mata pralāpite ardha-rātri gela  
in this way  in raving  half  the night  passed  
gambhīrāte svarūpa-gosāñi prabhure śoyāila  
in the Gambhīrā  Svarūpa  Gosāñi  the Lord  put to bed

(55) In this way half the night passed in His ravings. Then Svarūpa Gosāñi put the Lord to bed in the Gambhīrā.

প্রভুরে শোয়াঞা রামানন্দ গেলা ঘরে ৷  
স্বরূপ, গোবিন্দ শুইলা গম্ভীরার দ্বারে ॥ ৫৬ ॥

prabhure śoyāñā rāmānanda gelā ghare  
the Lord  having put to bed  Rāmānanda  went  home  
svarūpa, govinda śuilā gambhīrāra dvāre  
Svarūpa  Govinda  lay down  of the Gambhīrā  at the door

(56) After putting the Lord to bed, Rāmānanda went home, and Svarūpa and Govinda lay down at the door of the Gambhīrā.

প্রেমাবেশে মহাপ্রভুর গর-গর মন ৷  
নামসঙ্কীর্ত্তন করি’ করেন জাগরণ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

premāveśe mahāprabhura gara-gara mana  
absorbed in love  of Mahāprabhu  overflowing  the mind  
nāma-saṅkīrtana kari’ karena jāgaraṇa  
of the name  saṅkīrtana  doing  makes  waking

(57) Mahāprabhu’s mind was overflowing with love, and He stayed awake chanting the holy name.

বিরহে ব্যাকুল প্রভু উদ্বেগে উঠিলা ৷  
গম্ভীরার ভিত্ত্যে মুখ ঘষিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৫৮ ॥

virahe vyākula prabhu udvege uṭhilā  
in separation  agitated  the Lord  in distress  rose  
gambhīrāra bhittye mukha ghaṣite lāgilā  
of the Gambhīrā  against the walls  His face  to rub  began

(58) Agitated by separation, the Lord rose in distress and began to rub His face against the walls of the Gambhīrā.[^24]

মুখে, গণ্ডে, নাকে ক্ষত হইল অপার ৷  
ভাবাবেশে না জানেন প্রভু, পড়ে রক্তধার ॥ ৫৯ ॥

mukhe, gaṇḍe, nāke kṣata haila apāra  
on His face  cheeks  nose  wounds  became  countless  
bhāvāveśe nā jānena prabhu, paḍe rakta-dhāra  
absorbed in feeling  not  knows  the Lord  falls  of blood  a stream

(59) His face, cheeks and nose were covered with countless wounds. Absorbed in His feelings, the Lord was unaware of it, and the blood kept flowing.

সর্ব্বরাত্রি করেন ভাবে মুখ সংঘর্ষণ ৷  
গোঁ-গোঁ-শব্দ করেন, — স্বরূপ শুনিলা তখন ॥ ৬০ ॥

sarva-rātri karena bhāve mukha saṅgharṣaṇa  
all  night  makes  in ecstasy  of the face  rubbing  
gom̐-gom̐-śabda karena, — svarūpa śunilā takhana  
goṅ-goṅ  sounds  makes  Svarūpa  heard  then

(60) All night, in the grip of His feelings, He rubbed His face against the walls and made a low, muffled moaning — goṅ-goṅ. Svarūpa heard it.

দীপ জ্বালি’ ঘরে গেলা, দেখি’ প্রভুর মুখ ৷  
স্বরূপ, গোবিন্দ দুঁহার হৈল বড় দুঃখ ॥ ৬১ ॥

dīpa jvāli’ ghare gelā, dekhi’ prabhura mukha  
a lamp  lighting  into the room  went  seeing  the Lord’s  face  
svarūpa, govinda dum̐hāra haila baḍa duḥkha  
Svarūpa  Govinda  of both  arose  great  sorrow

(61) With Govinda he lit a lamp, and they went into the room. When they saw the Lord’s face, both were deeply grieved.

প্রভুরে শয্যাতে আনি’ শয়ন করাইলা ৷  
‘কাঁহে কৈলা এই তুমি ?’ — স্বরূপ পুছিলা ॥ ৬২ ॥

prabhure śayyāte āni’ śayana karāilā  
the Lord  to the bed  bringing  to lie down  made  
‘kām̐he kailā ei tumi?’ — svarūpa puchilā  
why  did  this  You  Svarūpa  asked

(62) They brought the Lord to His bed and made Him lie down, and Svarūpa asked, “Why did You do this?”

প্রভু কহেন, — উদ্বেগে ঘরে না পারি রহিতে ৷  
দ্বার চাহি’ ফিরি’ শীঘ্র বাহির হইতে ॥ ৬৩ ॥

prabhu kahena, — udvege ghare nā pāri rahite  
the Lord  says  in distress  in the room  not  I can  remain  
dvāra cāhi’ phiri’ śīghra bāhira haite  
the door  seeking  I wandered  quickly  outside  to go

(63) The Lord said, “In My distress I could not stay in the room. I wandered about looking for the door, eager to get out.

দ্বার নাহি’ পাঞা মুখ লাগে চারিভিতে ৷  
ক্ষত হয়, রক্ত পড়ে, না পাই যাইতে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

dvāra nāhi’ pāñā mukha lāge cāri-bhite  
the door  not  finding  the face  strikes  on all sides  
kṣata haya, rakta paḍe, nā pāi yāite  
wounds  come  blood  falls  not  I can  go

(64) But I could not find the door, and on every side My face struck against the walls. So I was hurt and bleeding, yet I could not get out.”

উন্মাদ-দশায় প্রভুর স্থির নহে মন ৷  
যেই করে, যেই বোলে, — উন্মাদ-লক্ষণ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

unmāda-daśāya prabhura sthira nahe mana  
in the state of madness  the Lord’s  steady  is not  mind  
yei kare, yei bole, — unmāda-lakṣaṇa  
whatever  He does  whatever  He says  of madness  a symptom

(65) In the state of divine madness the Lord’s mind was never steady. Whatever He did and whatever He said was a symptom of that madness.

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি তবে চিন্তা পাইলা মনে ৷  
ভক্তগণ লঞা বিচার কৈলা আর দিনে ॥ ৬৬ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi tabe cintā pāilā mane  
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  then  anxiety  felt  in his mind  
bhakta-gaṇa lañā vicāra kailā āra dine  
the devotees  gathering  counsel  held  on the next  day

(66) Then Svarūpa Gosāñi became anxious, and the next day he gathered the devotees and took counsel with them.[^25]

সব ভক্ত মেলি’ তবে প্রভুরে সাধিল ৷  
শঙ্কর-পণ্ডিতে প্রভুর সঙ্গে শোয়াইল ॥ ৬৭ ॥

saba bhakta meli’ tabe prabhure sādhila  
all  the devotees  together  then  the Lord  entreated  
śaṅkara-paṇḍite prabhura saṅge śoyāila  
Śaṅkara  Paṇḍita  the Lord’s  with  made sleep

(67) All the devotees together entreated the Lord, and they had Śaṅkara Paṇḍita sleep beside Him.

প্রভু-পাদতলে শঙ্কর করেন শয়ন ৷  
প্রভু তাঁর উপর করেন পাদ-প্রসারণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

prabhu-pāda-tale śaṅkara karena śayana  
the Lord’s  feet  at the base  Śaṅkara  makes  lying  
prabhu tām̐ra upara karena pāda-prasāraṇa  
the Lord  him  upon  makes  of the feet  stretching

(68) Śaṅkara would lie at Mahāprabhu’s feet, and the Lord would stretch His legs out across him.

‘প্রভু-পাদোপাধান’ বলি’ তাঁর নাম হইল ৷  
পূর্ব্বে বিদুরে যেন শ্রীশুক বর্ণিল ॥ ৬৯ ॥

‘prabhu-pādopādhāna’ bali’ tām̐ra nāma haila  
the Lord’s  feet’s pillow  calling  his  name  became  
pūrve vidure yena śrī-śuka varṇila  
long ago  of Vidura  as  Śrī  Śuka  described

(69) So he became known as “the pillow for the Lord’s feet,” just as Śrī Śuka once described Vidura:[^26]

ইতি ব্রুবাণং বিদুরং বিনীতং  
সহস্রশীর্ষ্ণশ্চরণোপাধানম্ ৷  
প্রহৃষ্টরোমা ভগবৎকথায়াং  
প্রণীয়মানো মুনিরভ্যচষ্ট ॥ ৭০ ॥

iti bruvāṇaṁ viduraṁ vinītaṁ  
thus  speaking  Vidura  humble  
sahasra-śīrṣṇaś caraṇopādhānam  
of the thousand-headed  of the feet  the pillow  
prahṛṣṭa-romā bhagavat-kathāyāṁ  
thrilled  with hairs  in talk of Bhagavān  
praṇīyamāno munir abhyacaṣṭa  
being urged  the sage  began to speak

(70) “So spoke the humble Vidura, the pillow for the feet of the thousand-headed Lord; and the sage Maitreya, urged on by him to speak of Bhagavān, began, the hairs of his body standing on end with joy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.13.5)[^27]

শঙ্কর করেন প্রভুর পাদ-সম্বাহন ৷  
ঘুমাঞা পড়েন, তৈছে করেন শয়ন ॥ ৭১ ॥

śaṅkara karena prabhura pāda-samvāhana  
Śaṅkara  makes  the Lord’s  feet  massaging  
ghumāñā paḍena, taiche karena śayana  
falling asleep  he drops  so  makes  lying

(71) Śaṅkara would massage the Lord’s feet, fall asleep and lie there just as he was.

উঘাড় অঙ্গে পড়িয়া শঙ্কর নিদ্রা যায় ৷  
প্রভু উঠি’ আপন কাঁথা তাহারে জড়ায় ॥ ৭২ ॥

ughāḍa aṅge paḍiyā śaṅkara nidrā yāya  
uncovered  in body  lying  Śaṅkara  sleep  goes  
prabhu uṭhi’ āpana kām̐thā tāhāre jaḍāya  
the Lord  rising  His own  quilt  him  wraps

(72) When Śaṅkara lay asleep with his body uncovered, the Lord would rise and wrap him in His own quilt.

নিরন্তর ঘুমায় শঙ্কর শীঘ্র চেতন ৷  
বসি’ পাদ চাপি’ করে রাত্রি জাগরণ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

nirantara ghumāya śaṅkara śīghra cetana  
constantly  sleeps  Śaṅkara  quickly  awake  
vasi’ pāda cāpi’ kare rātri jāgaraṇa  
sitting  the feet  pressing  makes  of the night  waking

(73) Śaṅkara would keep falling asleep but quickly wake again, and then he would sit up, press the Lord’s feet, and stay awake through the night.

তাঁর ভয়ে নারেন প্রভু বাহিরে যাইতে ৷  
তাঁর ভয়ে নারেন ভিত্ত্যে মুখাব্জ ঘষিতে ॥ ৭৪ ॥

tām̐ra bhaye nārena prabhu bāhire yāite  
of him  for fear  cannot  the Lord  outside  go  
tām̐ra bhaye nārena bhittye mukhābja ghaṣite  
of him  for fear  cannot  against the wall  His lotus face  rub

(74) For fear of disturbing him, the Lord could not go out, nor could He rub His lotus face against the walls.[^28]

এই লীলা মহাপ্রভুর রঘুনাথ-দাস ৷  
চৈতন্যস্তবকল্পবৃক্ষে কৈরাছেন প্রকাশ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

ei līlā mahāprabhura raghunātha-dāsa  
this  pastime  of Mahāprabhu  Raghunātha dāsa  
caitanya-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe kairāchena prakāśa  
in the Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa  has  described

(75) Raghunātha dāsa has described this pastime of Mahāprabhu in his *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*:

স্বকীয়স্য প্রাণার্ব্বুদসদৃশ-গোষ্ঠস্য বিরহাৎ  
প্রলাপানুন্মাদাৎ সততমতি কুর্ব্বন্ বিকলধীঃ ৷  
দধদ্ভিত্তৌ শশ্বদ্বদনবিধুঘর্ষেণ রুধিরং  
ক্ষতোত্থং গৌরাঙ্গো হৃদয় উদয়ন্মাং মদয়তি ॥ ৭৬ ॥

svakīyasya prāṇārbuda-sadṛśa-goṣṭhasya virahāt  
from His own  millions of lives  equal to  from Vraja  from separation  
pralāpān unmādāt satatam ati kurvan vikala-dhīḥ  
ravings  from madness  constantly  much  making  bewildered in mind  
dadhad bhittau śaśvad vadana-vidhu-gharṣeṇa rudhiraṁ  
bearing  on the wall  incessantly  by the rubbing of His moonlike face  blood  
kṣatotthaṁ gaurāṅgo hṛdaya udayan māṁ madayati  
from wounds arisen  Gaurāṅga  in my heart  rising  me  maddens

(76) “In separation from His own Vraja, dear to Him as millions of lives, He raved constantly in His madness, His mind bewildered. Again and again He rubbed His moonlike face against the wall, and it was covered with blood from the wounds. That Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, 6)[^29]

এইমত মহাপ্রভু রাত্রি দিবসে ৷  
প্রেমসিন্ধু-মগ্ন রহে, কভু ডুবে, ভাসে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu rātri divase  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  night  and day  
prema-sindhu-magna rahe, kabhu ḍube, bhāse  
in the ocean of love  immersed  remains  now  sinks  floats

(77) In this way, night and day, Mahāprabhu remained immersed in the ocean of love, now sinking, now rising to the surface.

এককালে বৈশাখের পৌর্ণমাসী-দিনে ৷  
রাত্রিকালে মহাপ্রভু চলিলা উদ্যানে ॥ ৭৮ ॥

eka-kāle vaiśākhera paurṇamāsī-dine  
one  time  of Vaiśākha  on the full-moon  day  
rātri-kāle mahāprabhu calilā udyāne  
at night  Mahāprabhu  went  to a garden

(78) Once, on the full-moon day of the month of Vaiśākha, Mahāprabhu went to a garden at night.[^30]

‘জগন্নাথবল্লভ’ নাম উদ্যান প্রধানে ৷  
প্রবেশ করিলা প্রভু লঞা ভক্তগণে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

‘jagannātha-vallabha’ nāma udyāna pradhāne  
Jagannātha-vallabha  named  of gardens  the foremost  
praveśa karilā prabhu lañā bhakta-gaṇe  
entry  made  the Lord  taking  the devotees

(79) Taking the devotees with Him, the Lord entered the finest of gardens, called Jagannātha-vallabha.

প্রফুল্লিত বৃক্ষ-বল্লী, — যেন বৃন্দাবন ৷  
শুক, শারী, পিক, ভৃঙ্গ করে আলাপন ॥ ৮০ ॥

praphullita vṛkṣa-vallī, — yena vṛndāvana  
blossoming  trees  creepers  like  Vṛndāvana  
śuka, śārī, pika, bhṛṅga kare ālāpana  
parrots  female parrots  cuckoos  bees  make  conversation

(80) The trees and creepers were in full bloom, as in Vṛndāvana, and the parrots, *śuka* and *śārī*, the cuckoos and the bees were calling to one another.

পুষ্পগন্ধ লঞা বহে মলয়-পবন ৷  
‘গুরু’ হঞা তরুলতায় শিখায় নাচন ॥ ৮১ ॥

puṣpa-gandha lañā vahe malaya-pavana  
of flowers  the fragrance  carrying  blows  the Malaya  breeze  
‘guru’ hañā taru-latāya śikhāya nācana  
a teacher  becoming  the trees and creepers  teaches  dancing

(81) Carrying the fragrance of the flowers, the breeze from the Malaya hills blew, and like a dancing master it taught the trees and creepers to dance.[^31]

পূর্ণচন্দ্র-চন্দ্রিকায় পরম উজ্জ্বল ৷  
তরুলতাদি জ্যোৎস্নায় করে ঝলমল ॥ ৮২ ॥

pūrṇa-candra-candrikāya parama ujjvala  
of the full moon  by the light  supremely  bright  
taru-latādi jyotsnāya kare jhalamala  
the trees  creepers and all  in the moonlight  make  glittering

(82) Everything shone brightly in the light of the full moon; the trees, the creepers and all else glittered in the moonlight.

ছয় ঋতুগণ যাঁহা বসন্ত প্রধান ৷  
দেখি’ আনন্দিত হৈলা গৌর ভগবান্ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

chaya ṛtu-gaṇa yām̐hā vasanta pradhāna  
the six  seasons  where  spring  foremost  
dekhi’ ānandita hailā gaura bhagavān  
seeing  delighted  became  Gaura  Bhagavān

(83) All six seasons were present there, with spring foremost among them. Seeing this, Gaura Bhagavān was delighted.

ললিত লবঙ্গলতা পদ গাওয়াঞা ৷  
নৃত্য করি’ বুলেন প্রভু নিজগণ লঞা ॥ ৮৪ ॥

lalita lavaṅgalatā pada gāoyāñā  
lalita  lavaṅga-latā  the song  having (them) sing  
nṛtya kari’ bulena prabhu nija-gaṇa lañā  
dance  doing  roams  the Lord  His own associates  with

(84) He had His associates sing the song “Lalita-lavaṅga-latā”, and He danced and roamed about with them.[^32]

[^1]: So in the edition: «মধুদ্যানে» (*madhudyāne*); by the rule of sandhi (*madhu* + *udyāna*) — «মধূদ্যানে», as in the *Anubhāṣya*. The sense is the same: “in the spring garden”.

[^2]: Heading in the edition: obeisance to the Lord, full of matchless tenderness towards His devotee-mother and overwhelmed by *mahābhāva* in the Jagannātha-vallabha garden. *Anubhāṣya*: the *madhūdyāna* is the grove of spring pastimes called Jagannātha-vallabha. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: He rubbed His face against the walls of the house, and He sported in the spring garden to show His longing for love of Kṛṣṇa.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: the cloth *prasāda* from Śrī Jagannātha’s cowherd dress (*gopa-veśa*). Heading in the edition: at the request of Paramānanda Purī the Lord sends Śacīdevī cloth and *prasāda* in Navadvīpa.

[^4]: Heading in the edition: Bhagavān is subject to transcendental parental love (*vātsalya-prema*). *Anubhāṣya*: the mother gave this body, reared and nourished it; to devote it to the service of Kṛṣṇa is itself the purest and best service to one’s mother.

[^5]: Heading in the edition: through the Paṇḍita, Śrī Advaita Prabhu sends Mahāprabhu a riddle (*prahelikā*).

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the people have gone mad with love (prema), and in the market of love there is no longer any place to sell rice — that is, love; the *āul*, the *bāul* mad with love, is no longer engaged in worldly affairs; and Advaita, himself mad with love, has said this. The meaning: the purpose for which the Lord appeared has been fulfilled; now let it be as the Lord wishes. Heading in the edition: a hint that the purpose of Mahāprabhu’s descent has been fulfilled and that it is time to conclude His pastimes. *Anubhāṣya*: there is a variant reading, “the people have become *āul*”; *āul* means “overcome [by feeling]”, that is, full of love, and also “possessing nothing”, “afflicted”.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: some explain *kāye nāhika āula* thus: in the work of preaching love there is no longer any disorder.

[^8]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *jāniyā* (“learning [of this]”). Other editions read *jāniyāo*, “though he knew [the meaning]”: “Though Svarūpa Gosāñi knew it, he still asked.”

[^9]: Heading in the edition: the Lord explains the riddle by a hint.

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *āvāhana* — inviting the Deity before worship; *nirodhana* — keeping the Deity present as long as the worship lasts; *visarjana* (27) — when the worship is over, the Deity is sent to another place.

[^11]: Heading in the edition: Śrī Rādhā, in separation from Kṛṣṇa, asks Viśākhā where Kṛṣṇa is to be found (*citra-jalpa*).

[^12]: Heading in the edition: the meaning of the verse — Śrī Rādhā, tormented by separation from Kṛṣṇa, describes the qualities of Kṛṣṇa, the life of the people of Vraja; to any melody (*yathā-rāga*).

[^13]: In the edition this verse is marked as the refrain of the song (ধ্রু).

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: the gopīs’ loving desire (*kāma*) is the desire to meet Kṛṣṇa; their hearts, scorched by it, are like night-blooming lotuses under a burning sun, and Kṛṣṇacandra, the moon, gives them the nectar of His own rays — or hands (*kara*).

[^15]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the nature of the *seyākul* thorn is that once it catches, it hardly lets go. *Anubhāṣya*: mango sap, once it sticks, is hard to remove.

[^16]: So in the edition: «জানি’» — a participle, “knowing”: the Creator, knowing his art, fashioned Him. Without the apostrophe («জানি», “I know”) the line would read: “surely the Creator fashioned Him”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *chāni’* — straining; *sāni’* — mixing.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: *kalā-nidhi* — the abode of the sixty-four arts, and in the other sense the moon, full in all its sixteen digits (*kalās*); *viḍambana* — deception.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: the lament of the gopīs of Vraja when they heard that Akrūra had come to Vraja to take Rāma and Kṛṣṇa to Mathurā.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pariśrama* — the labour of creation and so on.

[^20]: In the edition this verse is marked as the refrain of the song (ধ্রু).

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: *datta-apahāra* — to take back what one has given; it is one of the sins that require atonement (*prāyaścitta*).

[^22]: Heading in the edition: reproaches to Kṛṣṇa in loving anger (*praṇaya-roṣa*).

[^23]: Heading in the edition: Svarūpa consoles the Lord with songs in keeping with His mood.

[^24]: Heading in the edition: the Lord’s divine madness — the rubbing of His face against the walls (*udghūrṇā*).

[^25]: Heading in the edition: after taking counsel with the devotees, Svarūpa chooses Śaṅkara Paṇḍita to be the pillow for the Lord’s feet.

[^26]: Heading in the edition: Śaṅkara’s service to Bhagavān — like Vidura’s service in the Dvāpara age.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: Vidura asked Maitreya about the deeds of Svāyambhuva Manu and Śatarūpā, and in reply Maitreya began to speak of Hari. “The pillow for the feet” is one on whose lap Kṛṣṇa lovingly stretches out His feet: it is said that Kṛṣṇa came to Vidura’s house, laid both feet on his lap and fell asleep (see the *Sārārtha-darśinī* commentary).

[^28]: Heading in the edition: with Śaṅkara near, the Lord’s madness subsides.

[^29]: Heading in the edition: the Lord in separation from Kṛṣṇa, raving in divine madness. *Anubhāṣya*: *goṣṭha* is Vraja, dear to the Lord as countless lives; *dadhat* — “bearing”: the Lord’s face bore the blood from its wounds.

[^30]: Heading in the edition: one day, in the Jagannātha-vallabha garden, the Lord, overwhelmed by *mahābhāva*, shows the ten kinds of *citra-jalpa*.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: the breeze itself carried the fragrance of the flowers, and besides, like a dancing master, it taught the trees and creepers to dance.

[^32]: “Lalita-lavaṅga-latā” is Jayadeva’s spring song from the *Gīta-govinda* (first canto), about Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes with the gopīs.
