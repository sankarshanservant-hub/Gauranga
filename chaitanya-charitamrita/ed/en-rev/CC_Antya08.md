“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 8  
**The Reduction of the Meals**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

তং বন্দে কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যং রামচন্দ্রপুরীভয়াৎ ৷  
লৌকিকাহারতঃ স্বং যো ভিক্ষান্নং সমকোচয়ৎ ॥ ১ ॥

taṁ vande kṛṣṇa-caitanyaṁ  
Him  I worship  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  
rāmacandra-purī-bhayāt  
of Rāmacandra Purī  out of fear  
laukikāhārataḥ svaṁ yo  
than the ordinary food  His own  who  
bhikṣānnaṁ samakocayat  
alms-food  reduced

(1) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who, out of fear of Rāmacandra Purī, reduced His own meals below the ordinary measure.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য করুণাসিন্ধু-অবতার ৷  
ব্রহ্মা-শিবাদিক ভজে চরণ যাঁহার ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya karuṇā-sindhu-avatāra  
glory  glory  to Śrī  Caitanya  of mercy  the ocean  the descent  
brahmā-śivādika bhaje caraṇa yām̐hāra  
Brahmā,  Śiva and the rest  worship  the feet  whose

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya, the descent of the ocean of mercy, whose feet Brahmā, Śiva and the other gods worship!

জয় জয় শ্রীবাসাদি যত ভক্তগণ ৷  
শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য প্রভু — যাঁর প্রাণধন ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya śrīvāsādi yata bhakta-gaṇa  
glory  glory  to Śrīvāsa and the rest  all  the devotees  
śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya prabhu — yām̐ra prāṇa-dhana  
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  the Lord  whose  of life  the treasure

(3) Glory, glory to Śrīvāsa and to all the devotees, whose life’s treasure is the Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya![^2]

এইমত গৌরচন্দ্র নিজভক্ত-সঙ্গে ৷  
নীলাচলে ক্রীড়া করে কৃষ্ণপ্রেমতরঙ্গে ॥ ৪ ॥

ei-mata gauracandra nija-bhakta-saṅge  
in this way  Gauracandra  His own  devotees  with  
nīlācale krīḍā kare kṛṣṇa-prema-taraṅge  
in Nīlācala  plays  makes  of love for Kṛṣṇa  in the waves

(4) In this way Gauracandra enjoyed His pastimes in Nīlācala with His own devotees, amid the waves of love for Kṛṣṇa.

হেনকালে রামচন্দ্রপুরী-গোসাঞি আইলা ৷  
পরমানন্দ-পুরীরে আর প্রভুরে মিলিলা ॥ ৫ ॥

hena-kāle rāmacandra-purī-gosāñi āilā  
at this  time  Rāmacandra  Purī  Gosāñi  came  
paramānanda-purīre āra prabhure mililā  
Paramānanda  Purī  and  the Lord  met

(5) At that time Rāmacandra Purī Gosāñi came and met Paramānanda Purī and the Lord.[^3]

পরমানন্দ-পুরী কৈল চরণ বন্দন ৷  
পুরী-গোসাঞি কৈল তাঁরে দৃঢ় আলিঙ্গন ॥ ৬ ॥

paramānanda-purī kaila caraṇa vandana  
Paramānanda  Purī  did  at the feet  homage  
purī-gosāñi kaila tām̐re dṛḍha āliṅgana  
Purī  Gosāñi  gave  him  a firm  embrace

(6) Paramānanda Purī paid homage at his feet, and Purī Gosāñi embraced him firmly.

মহাপ্রভু কৈলা তাঁরে দণ্ডবৎ নতি ৷  
আলিঙ্গন করি’ তেঁহো কৈল কৃষ্ণস্মৃতি ॥ ৭ ॥

mahāprabhu kailā tām̐re daṇḍavat nati  
Mahāprabhu  made  to him  prostrate  obeisance  
āliṅgana kari’ tem̐ho kaila kṛṣṇa-smṛti  
embrace  giving  he  made  of Kṛṣṇa  remembrance

(7) Mahāprabhu offered him prostrate obeisance; he embraced the Lord and remembered Kṛṣṇa.[^4]

তিনজনে ইষ্টগোষ্ঠী কৈলা কতক্ষণ ৷  
জগদানন্দ-পণ্ডিত তাঁরে কৈলা নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৮ ॥

tina-jane iṣṭa-goṣṭhī kailā kata-kṣaṇa  
the three  persons  about the Lord  conversation  held  for some  time  
jagadānanda-paṇḍita tām̐re kailā nimantraṇa  
Jagadānanda  Paṇḍita  him  invited

(8) For some time the three of them spoke together about the Lord. Then Jagadānanda Paṇḍita invited him for a meal.

জগন্নাথের প্রসাদ আনিলা ভিক্ষার লাগিয়া ৷  
যথেষ্ট ভিক্ষা করিলা তেঁহো নিন্দার লাগিয়া ॥ ৯ ॥

jagannāthera prasāda ānilā bhikṣāra lāgiyā  
Jagannātha’s  prasāda  he brought  of the meal  for the sake  
yatheṣṭa bhikṣā karilā tem̐ho nindāra lāgiyā  
as much as he liked  meal  ate  he  of fault-finding  for the sake

(9) Jagadānanda brought Jagannātha’s prasāda for the meal, and Rāmacandra Purī ate as much as he liked, only to find fault afterwards.[^5]

ভিক্ষা করি’ কহে পুরী, — শুন, জগদানন্দ ৷  
অবশেষ প্রসাদ তুমি করহ ভক্ষণ ॥ ১০ ॥

bhikṣā kari’ kahe purī, — śuna, jagadānanda  
meal  having taken  says  Purī  listen  Jagadānanda  
avaśeṣa prasāda tumi karaha bhakṣaṇa  
the remnants  of prasāda  you  do  eat

(10) When he had eaten, the Purī said, “Listen, Jagadānanda: you eat the prasāda that remains.”

আগ্রহ করিয়া তাঁরে বসি’ খাওয়াইল ৷  
আপনে আগ্রহ করি’ পরিবেশন কৈল ॥ ১১ ॥

āgraha kariyā tām̐re vasi’ khāoyāila  
insistence  showing  him  having seated  fed  
āpane āgraha kari’ pariveśana kaila  
himself  insistence  showing  serving  did

(11) He insisted on seating Jagadānanda and feeding him, and eagerly served the food himself.

আগ্রহ করিয়া পুনঃ পুনঃ খাওয়াইল ৷  
আচমন কৈলে নিন্দা করিতে লাগিল ॥ ১২ ॥

āgraha kariyā punaḥ punaḥ khāoyāila  
insistence  showing  again  and again  fed  
ācamana kaile nindā karite lāgila  
rinsing of the mouth  when he did  fault-finding  to do  began

(12) Again and again he pressed him to eat; and when Jagadānanda had rinsed his mouth, he began to find fault:

শুনি, চৈতন্যগণ করে বহুত ভক্ষণ ৷  
‘সত্য’ সেই বাক্য, — সাক্ষাৎ দেখিলুঁ এখন ॥ ১৩ ॥

śuni, caitanya-gaṇa kare bahuta bhakṣaṇa  
I heard  Caitanya’s  followers  make  much  eating  
‘satya’ sei vākya, — sākṣāt dekhilum̐ ekhana  
‘true’  that  saying  with my own eyes  I have seen  now

(13) “I had heard that Caitanya’s followers eat a great deal. That report is true: now I have seen it with my own eyes.

সন্ন্যাসীরে এত খাওয়াঞা করে ধর্ম্ম নাশ ৷  
বৈরাগী হঞা এত খায়, বৈরাগ্যের নাহি ‘ভাস’ ॥ ১৪ ॥

sannyāsīre eta khāoyāñā kare dharma nāśa  
a sannyāsī  so much  feeding  they make  of dharma  ruin  
vairāgī hañā eta khāya, vairāgyera nāhi ‘bhāsa’  
renunciants  being  so much  they eat  of renunciation  there is not  ‘a semblance’

(14) Feeding a sannyāsī so much, they ruin his dharma; and though they are renunciants, they eat so much that they have not even a semblance of renunciation!”[^6]

এই ত’ স্বভাব তাঁর আগ্রহ করিয়া ৷  
পিছে নিন্দা করে, আগে বহুত খাওয়াঞা ॥ ১৫ ॥

ei ta’ svabhāva tām̐ra āgraha kariyā  
this  indeed  the nature  his  insistence  showing  
piche nindā kare, āge bahuta khāoyāñā  
afterwards  fault-finding  makes  first  much  having fed

(15) This was his nature: first he would insist on feeding someone a great deal, and afterwards he would find fault with him.

পূর্ব্বে যবে মাধবেন্দ্রপুরী করেন অন্তর্দ্ধান ৷  
রামচন্দ্রপুরী তবে আইলা তাঁর স্থান ॥ ১৬ ॥

pūrve yabe mādhavendra purī karena antarddhāna  
formerly  when  Mādhavendra  Purī  made  departure from the world  
rāmacandra-purī tabe āilā tām̐ra sthāna  
Rāmacandra  Purī  then  came  his  to the place

(16) Formerly, when Mādhavendra Purī was departing from this world, Rāmacandra Purī came to him.[^7]

পুরী-গোসাঞি করেন কৃষ্ণনাম-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
মথুরা না পাইনু বলি’ করেন ক্রন্দন ॥ ১৭ ॥

purī-gosāñi karena kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṅkīrtana  
Purī  Gosāñi  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s  name  chanting  
mathurā nā pāinu bali’ karena krandana  
Mathurā  not  I have attained  saying  makes  weeping

(17) Purī Gosāñi was chanting the name of Kṛṣṇa, and weeping, “I have not attained Mathurā!”[^8]

রামচন্দ্রপুরী তবে উপদেশে তাঁরে ৷  
শিষ্য হঞা গুরুকে কহে, ভয় নাহি করে ॥ ১৮ ॥

rāmacandra-purī tabe upadeśe tām̐re  
Rāmacandra  Purī  then  instructs  him  
śiṣya hañā guruke kahe, bhaya nāhi kare  
disciple  being  to the guru  he speaks  fear  not  does

(18) Then Rāmacandra Purī instructed him. Though a disciple, he spoke to his guru without fear:

তুমি — পূর্ণ ব্রহ্মানন্দ, করহ স্মরণ ৷  
ব্রহ্মবিৎ হঞা কেনে করহ রোদন ? ॥ ১৯ ॥

tumi — pūrṇa brahmānanda, karaha smaraṇa  
you  full  the bliss of Brahman  do  remember  
brahmavit hañā kene karaha rodana?  
knower of Brahman  being  why  do  weeping

(19) “You are full of the bliss of Brahman — remember it! You are a knower of Brahman: why do you weep?”[^9]

শুনি’ মাধবেন্দ্র-মনে ক্রোধ উপজিল ৷  
দূর, দূর, পাপিষ্ঠ বলি’ ভর্ৎসনা করিল ॥ ২০ ॥

śuni’ mādhavendra-mane krodha upajila  
hearing  in Mādhavendra’s mind  anger  arose  
dūra, dūra, pāpiṣṭha bali’ bhartsanā karila  
away  away  most sinful one  saying  rebuke  made

(20) Hearing this, Mādhavendra Purī grew angry. “Away, away, you sinner!” he cried, and rebuked him.[^10]

‘কৃষ্ণ-কৃপা’ না পাইনু, না পাইনু ‘মথুরা’ ৷  
আপন-দুঃখে মরোঁ, — এই দিতে আইল জ্বালা ॥ ২১ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa-kṛpā’ nā pāinu, nā pāinu ‘mathurā’  
‘Kṛṣṇa’s mercy’  not  I have attained  not  I have attained  ‘Mathurā’  
āpana-duḥkhe marom̐, — ei dite āila jvālā  
in my own  grief  I am dying  this one  to give  came  burning pain

(21) “I have not attained Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, I have not attained Mathurā. I am leaving this world in my own grief, and this one has come to add burning pain!

মোরে মুখ না দেখাবি তুই, যাও যথি-তথি ৷  
তোরে দেখি’ মৈলে, মোর হবে অসদ্গতি ॥ ২২ ॥

more mukha nā dekhābi tui, yāo yathi-tathi  
to me  face  not  you shall show  you  go  wherever  
tore dekhi’ maile, mora habe asad-gati  
you  seeing  if I die  my  will be  an evil  end

(22) Never show me your face again; go wherever you like! If I leave this world while looking at you, I shall come to an evil end.

কৃষ্ণ না পাইনু মরোঁ আপনার দুঃখে ৷  
মোরে ‘ব্রহ্ম’ উপদেশে এই ছার মূর্খে ॥ ২৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa nā pāinu marom̐ āpanāra duḥkhe  
Kṛṣṇa  not  I have attained  I am dying  in my own  grief  
more ‘brahma’ upadeśe ei chāra mūrkhe  
to me  ‘Brahman’  teaches  this  wretched  fool

(23) I am leaving this world in my own grief, not having attained Kṛṣṇa, and this wretched fool teaches me about Brahman!”

এই যে শ্রীমাধবেন্দ্রপাদ উপেক্ষা করিল ৷  
সেই অপরাধে ইঁহার ‘বাসনা’ জন্মিল ॥ ২৪ ॥

ei ye śrī-mādhavendrapāda upekṣā karila  
this  that  Śrī  Mādhavendrapāda  disregard  made  
sei aparādhe im̐hāra ‘vāsanā’ janmila  
by that  offence  his  ‘worldly desires’  were born

(24) Śrī Mādhavendrapāda thus disregarded him, and through that offence worldly desires (*vāsanā*) were born in Rāmacandra Purī.[^11]

শুষ্ক-ব্রহ্মজ্ঞানী, নাহি কৃষ্ণের ‘সম্বন্ধ’ ৷  
সর্ব্বলোকের নিন্দা করে, নিন্দাতে নির্ব্বন্ধ ॥ ২৫ ॥

śuṣka-brahma-jñānī, nāhi kṛṣṇera ‘sambandha’  
dry  Brahman  knower  there is not  with Kṛṣṇa  ‘relationship’  
sarva-lokera nindā kare, nindāte nirbandha  
of all  people  fault-finding  makes  in fault-finding  stubbornly fixed

(25) A dry knower of Brahman, he had no relationship with Kṛṣṇa. He found fault with everyone and clung stubbornly to fault-finding.[^12]

ঈশ্বরপুরী করেন শ্রীপাদ-সেবন ৷  
স্বহস্তে করেন মলমূত্রাদি মার্জ্জন ॥ ২৬ ॥

īśvara-purī karena śrīpāda-sevana  
Īśvara  Purī  does  to Śrīpāda  service  
svahaste karena malamūtrādi mārjana  
with his own hands  does  stool, urine and the rest  cleaning

(26) Īśvara Purī, however, served Śrīpāda Mādhavendra Purī, cleaning away his stool and urine with his own hands.[^13]

নিরন্তর কৃষ্ণনাম করায় স্মরণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম, কৃষ্ণলীলা শুনায় অনুক্ষণ ॥ ২৭ ॥

nirantara kṛṣṇa-nāma karāya smaraṇa  
constantly  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  makes him  remember  
kṛṣṇa-nāma, kṛṣṇa-līlā śunāya anukṣaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa’s  name,  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes  makes him hear  every moment

(27) He constantly helped him remember the name of Kṛṣṇa, and at every moment let him hear Kṛṣṇa’s name and Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.

তুষ্ট হঞা পুরী তাঁরে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ৷  
বর দিলা, — কৃষ্ণে তোমার হউক প্রেমধন ॥ ২৮ ॥

tuṣṭa hañā purī tām̐re kailā āliṅgana  
pleased  being  Purī  him  gave  an embrace  
vara dilā, — kṛṣṇe tomāra hauka prema-dhana  
blessing  gave  for Kṛṣṇa  your  may there be  of love  the treasure

(28) Pleased with him, Purī Gosāñi embraced him and gave him a blessing: “May you have the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa!”

সেই হৈতে ঈশ্বরপুরী — ‘প্রেমের সাগর’ ৷  
রামচন্দ্রপুরী হৈল সর্ব্বনিন্দাকর ॥ ২৯ ॥

sei haite īśvara-purī — ‘premera sāgara’  
that  from  Īśvara  Purī  ‘of love  an ocean’  
rāmacandra-purī haila sarva-nindākara  
Rāmacandra  Purī  became  of everyone  a fault-finder

(29) From then on Īśvara Purī was an ocean of love, while Rāmacandra Purī became one who found fault with everyone.

মহদনুগ্রহ-নিগ্রহের ‘সাক্ষী’ দুইজনে ৷  
এই দুইদ্বারে শিখাইলা জগজনে ॥ ৩০ ॥

mahad-anugraha-nigrahera ‘sākṣī’ dui-jane  
of a great soul’s  mercy  punishment  ‘witnesses’  the two  persons  
ei dui-dvāre śikhāilā jaga-jane  
these  two  through  taught  the world’s  people

(30) The two of them are witnesses of a great soul’s mercy and punishment; through these two he taught the people of the world.[^14]

জগদ্গুরু মাধবেন্দ্র করি’ প্রেম দান ৷  
এই শ্লোক পড়ি’ তেঁহো করিলা অন্তর্দ্ধান ॥ ৩১ ॥

jagad-guru mādhavendra kari’ prema dāna  
of the world  guru  Mādhavendra  making  of love  a gift  
ei śloka paḍi’ tem̐ho karilā antarddhāna  
this  verse  reciting  he  made  departure from the world

(31) Having given the gift of love, Mādhavendra, the guru of the world, recited this verse and departed from this world:

অয়ি দীনদয়ার্দ্র নাথ হে  
মথুরানাথ কদাবলোক্যসে ৷  
হৃদয়ং ত্বদলোককাতরং  
দয়িত ভ্রাম্যতি কিং করোম্যহম্ ॥ ৩২ ॥

ayi dīna-dayārdra nātha he  
O  melting with compassion for the wretched  Lord  O  
mathurā-nātha kadāvalokyase  
of Mathurā  Lord  when shall You be seen  
hṛdayaṁ tvad-aloka-kātaraṁ  
heart  at not seeing You  stricken  
dayita bhrāmyati kiṁ karomy aham  
O Beloved  wanders  what  do  I

(32) “O Lord, melting with compassion for the wretched! O Lord of Mathurā, when shall I see You? My heart, stricken at not seeing You, has lost all rest. O Beloved, what am I to do?” (Padyāvalī, 330)[^15]

এই শ্লোকে কৃষ্ণপ্রেম করে উপদেশ ৷  
কৃষ্ণের বিরহে, ভক্তের ভাববিশেষ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ei śloke kṛṣṇa-prema kare upadeśa  
in this  verse  love for Kṛṣṇa  makes  teaching  
kṛṣṇera virahe, bhaktera bhāva-viśeṣa  
of Kṛṣṇa  in separation  of the devotee  bhāva  special

(33) This verse teaches love for Kṛṣṇa: the special feeling (*bhāva-viśeṣa*) of a devotee in separation from Kṛṣṇa.[^16]

পৃথিবীতে রোপণ করি’ গেলা প্রেমাঙ্কুর ৷  
সেই প্রেমাঙ্কুরের বৃক্ষ — চৈতন্যঠাকুর ॥ ৩৪ ॥

pṛthivīte ropaṇa kari’ gelā premāṅkura  
on the earth  planting  making  went  of love  the sprout  
sei premāṅkurera vṛkṣa — caitanya-ṭhākura  
that  of love  of the sprout  the tree  Caitanya  Ṭhākura

(34) He planted the sprout of love on the earth and departed. Śrī Caitanya Ṭhākura is the tree that grew from that sprout of love.[^17]

প্রস্তাবে কহিলুঁ পুরী-গোসাঞির নির্য্যাণ ৷  
যেই ইহা শুনে, সেই বড় ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

prastāve kahilum̐ purī-gosāñira niryāṇa  
in this context  I have told  Purī  Gosāñi’s  departure  
yei ihā śune, sei baḍa bhāgyavān  
whoever  this  hears  he  very  fortunate

(35) In this context I have told of Purī Gosāñi’s departure from this world. Whoever hears of it is greatly fortunate.[^18]

রামচন্দ্রপুরী ঐছে রহিলা নীলাচলে ৷  
বিরক্ত স্বভাব, কভু রহে কোন স্থলে ॥ ৩৬ ॥

rāmacandra-purī aiche rahilā nīlācale  
Rāmacandra  Purī  thus  stayed  in Nīlācala  
virakta svabhāva, kabhu rahe kona sthale  
renounced  by nature  sometimes  stays  in some  place

(36) So Rāmacandra Purī stayed on in Nīlācala. Renounced by nature, he would stay now in one place, now in another.[^19]

অনিমন্ত্রণ ভিক্ষা করে, নাহিক নির্ণয় ৷  
অন্যের ভিক্ষার স্থিতির লয়েন নিশ্চয় ॥ ৩৭ ॥

animantraṇa bhikṣā kare, nāhika nirṇaya  
uninvited  meal  takes  there is no  fixed rule  
anyera bhikṣāra sthitira layena niścaya  
of others’  meals  the arrangement  takes  account

(37) He took his meals uninvited, with no fixed rule, and he kept account of how others took their meals.[^20]

প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণে লাগে কৌড়ি চারি পণ ৷  
কভু কাশীশ্বর, গোবিন্দ খান তিন জন ॥ ৩৮ ॥

prabhura nimantraṇe lāge kauḍi cāri paṇa  
the Lord’s  for the invitation  is needed  cowries  four  paṇas  
kabhu kāśīśvara, govinda khāna tina jana  
sometimes  Kāśīśvara  Govinda  eat  three  persons

(38) To invite the Lord for a meal cost four paṇas of cowries, for sometimes Kāśīśvara and Govinda ate as well, three persons in all.[^21]

প্রত্যহ প্রভুর ভিক্ষা ইতি-উতি হয় ৷  
কেহ যদি মূল্য আনে, চারিপণ-নির্ণয় ॥ ৩৯ ॥

pratyaha prabhura bhikṣā iti-uti haya  
daily  the Lord’s  meal  here and there  is  
keha yadi mūlya āne, cāri-paṇa-nirṇaya  
someone  if  the price  brings  four  paṇas  fixed

(39) Every day the Lord took His meal at one place or another. If someone brought money for it, the amount was fixed at four paṇas.

প্রভুর স্থিতি, রীতি, ভিক্ষা, শয়ন, প্রয়াণ ৷  
রামচন্দ্রপুরী করে সর্ব্বানুসন্ধান ॥ ৪০ ॥

prabhura sthiti, rīti, bhikṣā, śayana, prayāṇa  
the Lord’s  stay  ways  meals  sleep  going out  
rāmacandra-purī kare sarvānusandhāna  
Rāmacandra  Purī  makes  all  inquiry

(40) Rāmacandra Purī inquired into everything about the Lord: where He stayed, His ways, His meals, His sleep and His going out.

প্রভুর যতেক গুণ স্পর্শিতে নারিল ৷  
ছিদ্র চাহি’ বুলে, কাঁহা ছিদ্র না পাইল ॥ ৪১ ॥

prabhura yateka guṇa sparśite nārila  
the Lord’s  all  qualities  to touch  could not  
chidra cāhi’ bule, kām̐hā chidra nā pāila  
faults  seeking  he goes about  anywhere  fault  not  found

(41) He could not touch any of the Lord’s virtues. He went about seeking faults, but found no fault anywhere.

সন্ন্যাসী হঞা করে মিষ্টান্ন ভক্ষণ ৷  
এই ভোগে হয় কৈছে ইন্দ্রিয়-বারণ ? ॥ ৪২ ॥

sannyāsī hañā kare miṣṭānna bhakṣaṇa  
a sannyāsī  being  makes  of sweets  eating  
ei bhoge haya kaiche indriya-vāraṇa?  
by this  enjoyment  is  how  of the senses  control

(42) “He is a sannyāsī, yet He eats sweets! With such enjoyment, how can there be control of the senses?”

এই নিন্দা করি’ কহে সর্ব্বলোক-স্থানে ৷  
প্রভুরে দেখিতেহ অবশ্য আইসে প্রতিদিনে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

ei nindā kari’ kahe sarva-loka-sthāne  
this  fault-finding  making  he speaks  of all  people  in the presence  
prabhure dekhiteha avaśya āise prati-dine  
the Lord  to see  still  he comes  every  day

(43) Finding this fault, he spoke of it before everyone; and yet he came every day to see the Lord.

প্রভু গুরুবুদ্ধ্যে করেন সম্ভ্রম, সম্মান ৷  
তেঁহো ছিদ্র চাহি’ বুলে, — এই তার কাম ॥ ৪৪ ॥

prabhu guru-buddhye karena sambhrama, sammāna  
the Lord  as a guru  regarding him  makes  reverence  honour  
tem̐ho chidra cāhi’ bule, — ei tāra kāma  
he  faults  seeking  goes about  this  his  occupation

(44) Regarding him as a guru, the Lord showed him reverence and honour, but he went about looking for faults — that was his occupation.

যত নিন্দা করে তাহা প্রভু সব জানে ৷  
তথাপি আদর করে বড়ই সম্ভ্রমে ॥ ৪৫ ॥

yata nindā kare tāhā prabhu saba jāne  
whatever  fault-finding  he makes  that  the Lord  all  knows  
tathāpi ādara kare baḍai sambhrame  
still  respect  makes  very great  with reverence

(45) The Lord knew all the fault-finding he did, yet He still treated him with great respect and reverence.

একদিন প্রাতঃকালে আইলা প্রভুর ঘর ৷  
পিপীলিকা দেখি’ কিছু কহেন উত্তর ॥ ৪৬ ॥

ekadina prātaḥ-kāle āilā prabhura ghara  
one day  in the morning  came  of the Lord  to the house  
pipīlikā dekhi’ kichu kahena uttara  
ants  seeing  something  says  in reply

(46) One morning he came to the Lord’s house. Seeing ants there, he made a remark:

রাত্রাবত্র ঐক্ষবমাসীৎ,  
তেন পিপীলিকাঃ সঞ্চরন্তি ৷ অহো !  
বিরক্তানাং সন্ন্যাসিনামিয়মিন্দ্রিয়-  
লালসেতি ব্রুবন্নুত্থায় গতঃ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

rātrāv atra aikṣavam āsīt,  
at night  here  sugarcane molasses  was  
tena pipīlikāḥ sañcaranti. aho!  
therefore  ants  are moving about  alas  
viraktānāṁ sannyāsinām iyam indriya-  
of renounced  sannyāsīs  this  for the senses  
lālaseti bruvannutthāya gataḥ  
craving  so  saying  getting up  he went

(47) “Last night there was sugarcane molasses here; that is why the ants are crawling about. Alas! Such is the craving of the senses in renounced sannyāsīs!” Saying this, he got up and left.[^22]

প্রভু পরম্পরায় নিন্দা কৈরাছেন শ্রবণ ৷  
এবে সাক্ষাৎ শুনিলেন ‘কল্পিত’ নিন্দন ॥ ৪৮ ॥

prabhu paramparāya nindā kairāchena śravaṇa  
the Lord  through others  fault-finding  had  heard  
ebe sākṣāt śunilena ‘kalpita’ nindana  
now  directly  He heard  ‘invented’  blame

(48) The Lord had heard of his fault-finding through others; now He heard the invented blame with His own ears.[^23]

সহজেই পিপীলিকা সর্ব্বত্র বেড়ায় ৷  
তাহাতে তর্ক উঠাঞা দোষ লাগায় ॥ ৪৯ ॥

sahajei pipīlikā sarvatra beḍāya  
naturally  ants  everywhere  wander  
tāhāte tarka uṭhāñā doṣa lāgāya  
in that  argument  raising  a fault  he attaches

(49) Ants naturally wander about everywhere, yet he raised an argument from it and attached a fault to the Lord.

শুনি’ তাহা প্রভুর সঙ্কোচ-ভয় মনে ৷  
গোবিন্দে বোলাঞা কিছু কহেন বচনে ॥ ৫০ ॥

śuni’ tāhā prabhura saṅkoca-bhaya mane  
hearing  that  of the Lord  embarrassment  fear  in the mind  
govinde bolāñā kichu kahena vacane  
Govinda  calling  some  says  words

(50) Hearing this, the Lord felt embarrassment and fear in His mind. He called Govinda and spoke to him:[^24]

আজি হৈতে ভিক্ষা আমার এই ত’ নিয়ম ৷  
পিণ্ডাভোগের এক চৌঠি, পাঁচগণ্ডার ব্যঞ্জন ॥ ৫১ ॥

āji haite bhikṣā āmāra ei ta’ niyama  
today  from  meal  My  this  indeed  rule  
piṇḍā-bhogera eka cauṭhi, pām̐ca-gaṇḍāra vyañjana  
of the piṇḍā-bhoga  one  quarter  of five  gaṇḍās’ worth  vegetables

(51) “From today this is the rule for My meals: a quarter-pot of the rice offered on Jagannātha’s altar (*piṇḍā-bhoga*), and vegetables worth five gaṇḍās.[^25]

ইহা বই অধিক আর কিছু না লইবা ৷  
অধিক আনিলে আমা এথা না দেখিবা ॥ ৫২ ॥

ihā bai adhika āra kichu nā laibā  
this  except  more  other  anything  not  you shall take  
adhika ānile āmā ethā nā dekhibā  
more  if you bring  Me  here  not  you shall see

(52) Take nothing more than this. If you bring more, you will not see Me here again.”

সকল বৈষ্ণবে গোবিন্দ কহে এই বাত্ ৷  
শুনি’ সবার মাথে যৈছে হৈল বজ্রাঘাত ॥ ৫৩ ॥

sakala vaiṣṇave govinda kahe ei bāt  
to all  the Vaiṣṇavas  Govinda  tells  this  news  
śuni’ sabāra māthe yaiche haila vajrāghāta  
hearing  of everyone  on the head  as if  there was  a thunderbolt’s stroke

(53) Govinda told all the Vaiṣṇavas this news. Hearing it, they felt as if a thunderbolt had struck their heads.

রামচন্দ্রপুরীকে সবায় দেয় তিরস্কার ৷  
এই পাপিষ্ঠ আসি’ প্রাণ লইল সবার ॥ ৫৪ ॥

rāmacandra-purīke sabāya deya tiraskāra  
Rāmacandra  Purī  everyone  gives  rebuke  
ei pāpiṣṭha āsi’ prāṇa laila sabāra  
this  great sinner  coming  life  has taken  of all

(54) Everyone reproached Rāmacandra Purī: “This sinner has come here and taken the life of us all!”

সেইদিন একবিপ্র কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
এক-চৌঠি ভাত, পাঁচ-গণ্ডার ব্যঞ্জন ॥ ৫৫ ॥

এই মাত্র গোবিন্দ কৈল অঙ্গীকার ৷  
মাথায় ঘা মারে বিপ্র, করে হাহাকার ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sei-dina eka-vipra kaila nimantraṇa  
that  day  a  brāhmaṇa  made  invitation  
eka-cauṭhi bhāta, pām̐ca-gaṇḍāra vyañjana  
one  quarter-pot  rice  of five  gaṇḍās’ worth  vegetables  
ei mātra govinda kaila aṅgīkāra  
this  only  Govinda  made  acceptance  
māthāya ghā māre vipra, kare hāhākāra  
on his head  blows  strikes  the brāhmaṇa  makes  lamentation

(55–56) That day a brāhmaṇa invited the Lord. Govinda accepted only a quarter-pot of rice and vegetables worth five gaṇḍās, and the brāhmaṇa struck his head and cried out in grief.

সেই ভাত-ব্যঞ্জন প্রভু অর্দ্ধেক খাইল ৷  
যে কিছু রহিল, তাহা গোবিন্দ পাইল ॥ ৫৭ ॥

sei bhāta-vyañjana prabhu arddheka khāila  
that  rice  vegetables  the Lord  half  ate  
ye kichu rahila, tāhā govinda pāila  
whatever  remained  that  Govinda  received

(57) The Lord ate half of that rice and vegetables, and whatever remained Govinda received.

অর্দ্ধাশন করেন প্রভু, গোবিন্দ অর্দ্ধাশন ৷  
সব ভক্তগণ তবে ছাড়িল ভোজন ॥ ৫৮ ॥

arddhāśana karena prabhu, govinda arddhāśana  
half a meal  takes  the Lord  Govinda  half a meal  
saba bhakta-gaṇa tabe chāḍila bhojana  
all  the devotees  then  gave up  eating

(58) The Lord ate half a meal and Govinda half a meal, and then all the devotees gave up eating.[^26]

গোবিন্দ-কাশীশ্বরে প্রভুর কৈলা আজ্ঞাপন ৷  
দুঁহে অন্যত্র মাগি’ কর উদর ভরণ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

govinda-kāśīśvare prabhura kailā ājñāpana  
to Govinda  Kāśīśvara  the Lord’s  was made  order  
dum̐he anyatra māgi’ kara udara bharaṇa  
you two  elsewhere  begging  do  of the belly  filling

(59) The Lord ordered Govinda and Kāśīśvara, “You two beg elsewhere and fill your stomachs.”

এইরূপ মহাদুঃখে দিন কত গেল ৷  
শুনি’ রামচন্দ্রপুরী প্রভু-পাশ আইল ॥ ৬০ ॥

ei-rūpa mahā-duḥkhe dina kata gela  
in this way  in great  grief  days  some  passed  
śuni’ rāmacandra-purī prabhu-pāśa āila  
hearing  Rāmacandra  Purī  the Lord  near  came

(60) Some days passed in this great grief. Hearing of it, Rāmacandra Purī came to the Lord.

প্রণাম করি’ প্রভু কৈলা চরণ বন্দন ৷  
প্রভুরে কহয়ে কিছু হাসিয়া বচন ॥ ৬১ ॥

praṇāma kari’ prabhu kailā caraṇa vandana  
obeisance  making  the Lord  did  at the feet  homage  
prabhure kahaye kichu hāsiyā vacana  
to the Lord  says  some  smiling  words

(61) The Lord bowed down and paid homage at his feet, and the Purī, smiling, spoke to the Lord:[^27]

সন্ন্যাসীর ধর্ম্ম নহে ‘ইন্দ্রিয়-তর্পণ’ ৷  
যৈছে তৈছে করে মাত্র উদর ভরণ ॥ ৬২ ॥

sannyāsīra dharma nahe ‘indriya-tarpaṇa’  
a sannyāsī’s  dharma  is not  ‘of the senses  gratification’  
yaiche taiche kare mātra udara bharaṇa  
somehow or other  makes  only  of the belly  filling

(62) “A sannyāsī’s dharma is not to gratify the senses. He just fills his stomach somehow or other.[^28]

তোমারে ক্ষীণ দেখি, শুনি, — কর অর্দ্ধাশন ৷  
এই ‘শুষ্ক-বৈরাগ্য’ নহে সন্ন্যাসীর ‘ধর্ম্ম’ ॥ ৬৩ ॥

tomāre kṣīṇa dekhi, śuni, — kara arddhāśana  
You  thin  I see  I hear  You make  half-eating  
ei ‘śuṣka-vairāgya’ nahe sannyāsīra ‘dharma’  
this  ‘dry  renunciation’  is not  a sannyāsī’s  ‘dharma’

(63) I see that You have grown thin, and I hear You eat only half Your meals. This dry renunciation is not a sannyāsī’s dharma.

যথাযোগ্য উদর ভরে, না করে ‘বিষয়’ ভোগ ৷  
সন্ন্যাসীর তবে সিদ্ধ হয় জ্ঞানযোগ ॥ ৬৪ ॥

yathā-yogya udara bhare, nā kare ‘viṣaya’ bhoga  
as is fitting  the belly  fills  not  makes  ‘sense objects’  enjoyment  
sannyāsīra tabe siddha haya jñāna-yoga  
of a sannyāsī  then  perfected  becomes  of knowledge  yoga

(64) He fills his stomach as much as is fitting and does not enjoy sense objects. Then the sannyāsī’s *jñāna-yoga* is perfected.

নাত্যশ্নতোঽপি যোগোঽস্তি ন চৈকান্তমনশ্নতঃ ৷  
ন চাতিস্বপ্নশীলস্য জাগ্রতো নৈব চার্জ্জুন ॥ ৬৫ ॥

nāty-aśnato ’pi yogo ’sti  
not  of one who eats too much  even  yoga  is  
na caikāntam anaśnataḥ  
not  and  entirely  of one who does not eat  
na cāti-svapna-śīlasya  
not  and  too much  sleep  of one given to  
jāgrato naiva cārjuna  
of one who keeps awake  not  indeed  and  O Arjuna

(65) ‘O Arjuna, there is no yoga for one who eats too much, nor for one who does not eat at all, nor for one given to too much sleep, nor for one who stays awake too long.’ (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.16)

যুক্তাহারবিহারস্য যুক্তচেষ্টস্য কর্ম্মসু ৷  
যুক্তস্বপ্নাববোধস্য যোগো ভবতি দুঃখহা ॥ ৬৬ ॥

yuktāhāra-vihārasya  
of one regulated in eating and recreation  
yukta-ceṣṭasya karmasu  
regulated  in effort  in actions  
yukta-svapnāvabodhasya  
regulated in sleep and waking  
yogo bhavati duḥkha-hā  
yoga  becomes  of sorrows  the destroyer

(66) ‘For one who is regulated in eating and recreation, regulated in his efforts at work, and regulated in sleep and waking, yoga becomes the destroyer of all sorrow.’” (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.17)

প্রভু কহে, — অজ্ঞ বালক মুই ‘শিষ্য’ তোমার ৷  
মোরে শিক্ষা দেহ’, — এই ভাগ্য আমার ॥ ৬৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — ajña bālaka mui ‘śiṣya’ tomāra  
the Lord  says  ignorant  boy  I  ‘disciple’  your  
more śikṣā deha’, — ei bhāgya āmāra  
to Me  instruction  you give  this  fortune  My

(67) The Lord said, “I am an ignorant boy, your disciple. That you instruct Me is My good fortune.”[^29]

এত শুনি’ রামচন্দ্রপুরী উঠি’ গেলা ৷  
ভক্তগণ অর্দ্ধাশন করে, — গোসাঞি শুনিলা ॥ ৬৮ ॥

eta śuni’ rāmacandra-purī uṭhi’ gelā  
this  hearing  Rāmacandra  Purī  getting up  went  
bhakta-gaṇa arddhāśana kare, — gosāñi śunilā  
the devotees  half-eating  make  the Gosāñi  heard

(68) Hearing this, Rāmacandra Purī got up and left. The Gosāñi heard that the devotees were eating only half their meals.

আর দিন ভক্তগণ-সহ পরমানন্দপুরী ৷  
প্রভু-পাশে নিবেদিলা দৈন্য-বিনয় করি’ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

āra dina bhakta-gaṇa-saha paramānanda-purī  
another  day  the devotees  with  Paramānanda  Purī  
prabhu-pāśe nivedilā dainya-vinaya kari’  
to the Lord  near  submitted  humility  courtesy  making

(69) On another day Paramānanda Purī, with the devotees, humbly and courteously submitted to the Lord:

রামচন্দ্রপুরী হয় নিন্দুক-স্বভাব ৷  
তার বোলে অন্ন ছাড়ি’ কিবা হবে লাভ ? ॥ ৭০ ॥

rāmacandra-purī haya ninduka-svabhāva  
Rāmacandra  Purī  is  of a fault-finder  the nature  
tāra bole anna chāḍi’ kibā habe lābha?  
his  at the word  food  giving up  what  will be  gain

(70) “Rāmacandra Purī has the nature of a fault-finder. What will be gained by giving up food at his word?[^30]

পুরীর স্বভাব, — যথেষ্ট আহার করাঞা ৷  
যে না খায়, তারে খাওয়ায় যতন করিয়া ॥ ৭১ ॥

purīra svabhāva, — yatheṣṭa āhāra karāñā  
of the Purī  the nature  as much as one likes  food  having made eat  
ye nā khāya, tāre khāoyāya yatana kariyā  
who  not  eats  him  he feeds  effort  making

(71) It is the Purī’s nature to make people eat as much as they like; whoever does not eat, he feeds with great effort.

খাওয়াঞা পুনঃ তারে করয়ে নিন্দন ৷  
এত অন্ন খাও, — তোমার কত আছে ধন ? ॥ ৭২ ॥

khāoyāñā punaḥ tāre karaye nindana  
having fed  then  him  makes  fault-finding  
eta anna khāo, — tomāra kata āche dhana?  
so much  food  you eat  your  how much  is there  wealth

(72) Having fed him, he then finds fault with him: ‘You eat so much food — how much wealth do you have?

সন্ন্যাসীকে এত খাওয়াঞা কর ধর্ম্ম নাশ !  
অতএব জানিনু, — তোমার কিছু নাহি ‘ভাস’ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

sannyāsīke eta khāoyāñā kara dharma nāśa!  
a sannyāsī  so much  feeding  you make  of dharma  ruin  
ataeva jāninu, — tomāra kichu nāhi ‘bhāsa’  
therefore  I know  your  any  there is not  ‘semblance’

(73) By feeding a sannyāsī so much, you ruin his dharma! Now I see that you have not even a semblance of piety.’[^31]

কে কৈছে ব্যবহারে, কেবা কৈছে খায় ৷  
এই অনুসন্ধান তেঁহো করয় সদায় ॥ ৭৪ ॥

ke kaiche vyavahāre, kebā kaiche khāya  
who  how  in behaviour  who  how  eats  
ei anusandhāna tem̐ho karaya sadāya  
this  inquiry  he  makes  always

(74) How each person behaves and how each one eats — he is always inquiring into this.

শাস্ত্রে যেই দুই ধর্ম্ম কৈরাছে বর্জ্জন ৷  
সেই কর্ম্ম নিরন্তর ইঁহার করণ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

śāstre yei dui dharma kairāche varjana  
in the scriptures  which  two  duties  have made  rejection  
sei karma nirantara im̐hāra karaṇa  
that  activity  constantly  his  doing

(75) The two things that the scriptures forbid are what he does constantly.

পরস্বভাবকর্ম্মাণি ন প্রশংসেন্ন গর্হয়েৎ ৷  
বিশ্বমেকাত্মকং পশ্যন্ প্রকৃত্যা পুরুষেণ চ ॥ ৭৬ ॥

para-svabhāva-karmāṇi  
of others  the nature  and actions  
na praśaṁsen na garhayet  
not  one should praise  not  one should blame  
viśvam ekātmakaṁ paśyan  
the universe  of one nature  seeing  
prakṛtyā puruṣeṇa ca  
with prakṛti  with the puruṣa  and

(76) ‘One should neither praise nor blame the nature and actions of others, but see the universe as one, a union of prakṛti and the puruṣa.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.28.1)[^32]

তার মধ্যে পূর্ব্ববিধি ‘প্রশংসা’ ছাড়িয়া ৷  
পরবিধি ‘নিন্দা’ করে ‘বলিষ্ঠ’ জানিয়া ॥ ৭৭ ॥

tāra madhye pūrva-vidhi ‘praśaṁsā’ chāḍiyā  
of them  among  the earlier injunction  ‘praise’  giving up  
para-vidhi ‘nindā’ kare ‘baliṣṭha’ jāniyā  
the later injunction  ‘blame’  he does  ‘stronger’  knowing it

(77) Of the two, he gives up the earlier injunction, praise, and practises the later one, blame, knowing that the later one is ‘stronger’.[^33]

পূর্ব্বপরয়োর্মধ্যে পরবিধির্বলবান্ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

pūrva-parayor madhye para-vidhir balavān  
of the earlier  and later  between  the later injunction  stronger

(78) ‘Between an earlier and a later injunction, the later injunction is stronger.’ (a maxim of Nyāya)[^34]

যাঁহা গুণ শত আছে, তাহা না করে গ্রহণ ৷  
গুণমধ্যে ছলে করে দোষ-আরোপণ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

yām̐hā guṇa śata āche, tāhā nā kare grahaṇa  
where  virtues  a hundred  are  those  not  makes  acceptance  
guṇa-madhye chale kare doṣa-āropaṇa  
virtues  among  by deceit  makes  of faults  imputation

(79) Where there are a hundred virtues, he does not accept them; among the virtues he deceitfully imputes faults.

ইঁহার স্বভাব ইঁহা কহিতে না যুয়ায় ৷  
তথাপি কহিয়ে কিছু মর্ম্ম-দুঃখ পায় ॥ ৮০ ॥

im̐hāra svabhāva im̐hā kahite nā yuyāya  
his  nature  this  to tell  not  is fitting  
tathāpi kahiye kichu marma-duḥkha pāya  
still  I tell  something  heart-felt  pain  receiving

(80) It is not fitting even to speak of his nature. Still I tell You something of it, for it pains us to the heart.[^35]

ইঁহার বচনে কেনে অন্ন ত্যাগ কর ?  
পূর্ব্ববৎ নিমন্ত্রণ মান’, — সবার বোল ধর ॥ ৮১ ॥

im̐hāra vacane kene anna tyāga kara?  
his  at the word  why  food  renunciation  You make  
pūrvavat nimantraṇa māna’, — sabāra bola dhara  
as before  invitations  accept  of all  the request  heed

(81) Why do You give up food at his word? Accept invitations as before and heed the request of us all.”

প্রভু কহে, — সবে কেনে পুরীরে কর রোষ ?  
‘সহজ’ ধর্ম্ম কহে তেঁহো, তাঁর কিবা দোষ ? ॥ ৮২ ॥

prabhu kahe, — sabe kene purīre kara roṣa?  
the Lord  says  all of you  why  with the Purī  make  anger  
‘sahaja’ dharma kahe tem̐ho, tām̐ra kibā doṣa?  
‘natural’  dharma  speaks  he  his  what  fault

(82) The Lord said, “Why are you all angry with the Purī? He speaks the natural dharma of a sannyāsī. What fault is there in him?

যতি হঞা জিহ্বা-লাম্পট্য, — অত্যন্ত অন্যায় ৷  
যতির ধর্ম্ম, — প্রাণ রাখিতে আহারমাত্র খায় ॥ ৮৩ ॥

yati hañā jihvā-lāmpaṭya, — atyanta anyāya  
a renunciant  being  of the tongue  greed  very  improper  
yatira dharma, — prāṇa rākhite āhāra-mātra khāya  
a renunciant’s  dharma  life  to maintain  food  only  he eats

(83) For a renunciant to be greedy of the tongue is very improper. A renunciant’s dharma is to eat only as much food as keeps him alive.”[^36]

তবে সবে মেলি’ প্রভুরে বহু যত্ন কৈলা ৷  
সবার আগ্রহে প্রভু অর্দ্ধেক রাখিলা ॥ ৮৪ ॥

tabe sabe meli’ prabhure bahu yatna kailā  
then  all  together  the Lord  much  entreaty  made  
sabāra āgrahe prabhu arddheka rākhilā  
of all  at the insistence  the Lord  half  kept

(84) Then all of them together entreated the Lord earnestly, and at everyone’s insistence the Lord agreed to take half of His former measure.

দুইপণ কৌড়ি লাগে প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণে ৷  
কভু দুইজন ভোক্তা, কভু তিনজনে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

duipaṇa kauḍi lāge prabhura nimantraṇe  
two paṇas  of cowries  is needed  the Lord’s  for the invitation  
kabhu dui-jana bhoktā, kabhu tina-jane  
sometimes  two  persons  eaters  sometimes  three  persons

(85) To invite the Lord now cost two paṇas of cowries. Sometimes two persons ate, sometimes three.

অভোজ্যান্ন বিপ্র যদি করেন নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
প্রসাদ-মূল্য লইতে লাগে কৌড়ি দুইপণ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

abhojyānna vipra yadi karena nimantraṇa  
whose cooked food may not be eaten  a brāhmaṇa  if  makes  invitation  
prasāda-mūlya laite lāge kauḍi duipaṇa  
prasāda  the price  to take  is needed  cowries  two paṇas

(86) If a brāhmaṇa whose cooked food could not be accepted invited the Lord, two paṇas of cowries were needed to buy prasāda.

ভোজ্যান্ন বিপ্র যদি নিমন্ত্রণ করে ৷  
কিছু ‘প্রসাদ’ আনে, কিছু পাক করে ঘরে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

bhojyānna vipra yadi nimantraṇa kare  
whose cooked food may be eaten  a brāhmaṇa  if  invitation  makes  
kichu ‘prasāda’ āne, kichu pāka kare ghare  
some  ‘prasāda’  brings  some  cooking  makes  at home

(87) If a brāhmaṇa whose cooked food could be accepted invited Him, he would bring some prasāda and cook some at home.

পণ্ডিত-গোসাঞি, ভগবান্-আচার্য্য, সার্ব্বভৌম ৷  
নিমন্ত্রণের দিনে যদি করে নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৮৮ ॥

paṇḍita-gosāñi, bhagavān-ācārya, sārvabhauma  
Paṇḍita  Gosāñi  Bhagavān  Ācārya  Sārvabhauma  
nimantraṇera dine yadi kare nimantraṇa  
of invitation  on the day  if  make  invitation

(88) But when Paṇḍita Gosāñi, Bhagavān Ācārya or Sārvabhauma invited Him,[^37]

তাঁ-সবার ইচ্ছায় প্রভু করেন ভোজন ৷  
তাঁহা প্রভুর স্বাতন্ত্র্য নাই, যৈছে তাঁর মন ॥ ৮৯ ॥

tām̐-sabāra icchāya prabhu karena bhojana  
of them all  by the wish  the Lord  takes  meal  
tām̐hā prabhura svātantrya nāi, yaiche tām̐ra mana  
there  the Lord’s  independence  is not  as  their  mind

(89) the Lord ate as they wished. In their homes He had no independence: all was as they desired.

ভক্তগণে সুখ দিতে প্রভুর ‘অবতার’ ৷  
যাঁহা যৈছে যোগ্য, তাঁহা করেন ব্যবহার ॥ ৯০ ॥

bhakta-gaṇe sukha dite prabhura ‘avatāra’  
to the devotees  happiness  to give  the Lord’s  ‘descent’  
yām̐hā yaiche yogya, tām̐hā karena vyavahāra  
where  how  fitting  there  He makes  conduct

(90) The Lord descended to give happiness to His devotees, and He acted as was fitting in each place and situation.[^38]

কভু লৌকিক রীতি, — যেন ‘ইতর’ জন ৷  
কভু স্বতন্ত্র, করেন ‘ঐশ্বর্য্য’ প্রকটন ॥ ৯১ ॥

kabhu laukika rīti, — yena ‘itara’ jana  
sometimes  worldly  ways  as if  ‘ordinary’  person  
kabhu svatantra, karena ‘aiśvarya’ prakaṭana  
sometimes  independent  makes  ‘divine majesty’  manifestation

(91) Sometimes He followed worldly ways, like an ordinary person; sometimes, being independent, He displayed His divine majesty (*aiśvarya*).[^39]

কভু রামচন্দ্রপুরীর হয় ভৃত্যপ্রায় ৷  
কভু তারে নাহি মানে, দেখে তৃণ-প্রায় ॥ ৯২ ॥

kabhu rāmacandra-purīra haya bhṛtya-prāya  
sometimes  of Rāmacandra  Purī  is  a servant  almost  
kabhu tāre nāhi māne, dekhe tṛṇa-prāya  
sometimes  him  not  regards  sees  a straw  like

(92) Sometimes He was almost like a servant of Rāmacandra Purī; sometimes He paid him no regard and looked on him as a straw.[^40]

ঈশ্বর-চরিত্র প্রভুর — বুদ্ধির অগোচর ৷  
যবে যেই করেন, সেই সব — মনোহর ॥ ৯৩ ॥

īśvara-caritra prabhura — buddhira agocara  
divine  conduct  of the Lord  of the intellect  beyond the reach  
yabe yei karena, sei saba — manohara  
when  whatever  He does  that  all  enchanting

(93) The Lord’s divine conduct is beyond the reach of the intellect. Whatever He does at any time is all enchanting.[^41]

এইমত রামচন্দ্রপুরী নীলাচলে ৷  
দিন কত রহি’ গেলা ‘তীর্থ’ করিবারে ॥ ৯৪ ॥

ei-mata rāmacandra-purī nīlācale  
in this way  Rāmacandra  Purī  in Nīlācala  
dina kata rahi’ gelā ‘tīrtha’ karibāre  
days  some  staying  went  ‘pilgrimage’  to make

(94) In this way Rāmacandra Purī stayed some days in Nīlācala and then left on pilgrimage.

তেঁহো গেলে প্রভুর গণ হৈল হরষিত ৷  
শিরের পাথর যেন পড়িল আচম্বিত ॥ ৯৫ ॥

tem̐ho gele prabhura gaṇa haila haraṣita  
he  when gone  the Lord’s  associates  became  delighted  
śirera pāthara yena paḍila ācambita  
from the head  a stone  as if  fell  suddenly

(95) When he had gone, the Lord’s associates were delighted, as if a stone had suddenly fallen from their heads.

স্বচ্ছন্দে নিমন্ত্রণ, প্রভুর কীর্ত্তন-নর্ত্তন ৷  
স্বচ্ছন্দে করেন সবে প্রসাদ ভোজন ॥ ৯৬ ॥

svacchande nimantraṇa, prabhura kīrtana-nartana  
freely  invitations  the Lord’s  kīrtana  dancing  
svacchande karena sabe prasāda bhojana  
freely  make  all  prasāda  eating

(96) Freely the Lord again accepted invitations and freely He sang kīrtana and danced; freely everyone again ate prasāda.

গুরু উপেক্ষা কৈলে, ঐছে ফল হয় ৷  
ক্রমে ঈশ্বরপর্য্যন্ত অপরাধে ঠেকয় ॥ ৯৭ ॥

guru upekṣā kaile, aiche phala haya  
the guru  disregard  if he makes  such  result  is  
krame īśvara-paryanta aparādhe ṭhekaya  
gradually  the Lord  even up to  in offences  one becomes caught

(97) When the guru disregards someone, such is the result: gradually he comes to offend even the Lord.[^42]

যদ্যপি গুরুবুদ্ধ্যে প্রভু তার দোষ না লইল ৷  
তার ফলদ্বারা লোকে শিক্ষা করাইল ॥ ৯৮ ॥

yadyapi guru-buddhye prabhu tāra doṣa nā laila  
although  as a guru  regarding him  the Lord  his  fault  not  took  
tāra phala-dvārā loke śikṣā karāila  
of it  the fruit  by means of  the people  teaching  gave

(98) Although the Lord, regarding him as a guru, did not take offence at his faults, through their fruit He taught the people.[^43]

চৈতন্যচরিত্র — যেন অমৃতের পূর ৷  
শুনিতে শ্রবণে মনে লাগয়ে মধুর ॥ ৯৯ ॥

caitanya-caritra — yena amṛtera pūra  
Caitanya’s  conduct  like  of nectar  a flood  
śunite śravaṇe mane lāgaye madhura  
in hearing  to the ears  to the mind  feels  sweet

(99) Śrī Caitanya’s conduct is like a flood of nectar; when heard, it is sweet to the ears and to the mind.

চৈতন্যচরিত্র লিখি, শুন একমনে ৷  
অনায়াসে পাবে প্রেম শ্রীকৃষ্ণচরণে ॥ ১০০ ॥

caitanya-caritra likhi, śuna eka-mane  
Caitanya’s  conduct  I write  hear  with one mind  
anāyāse pābe prema śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇe  
easily  you will attain  love  of Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  at the feet

(100) I write of Śrī Caitanya’s conduct: hear it with undivided attention, and you will easily attain love for the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১০১ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(101) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.[^44]

---

[^1]: Heading in the edition: obeisance to the Lord, who out of fear of Rāmacandra Purī reduced His meals. *Anubhāṣya*: this fear was outward and worldly, roused by the words of Rāmacandra Purī, a blasphemer of Hari, the guru and the Vaiṣṇavas; the Lord’s meals were moderate even before, yet He cut them below the measure people consider ordinary.

[^2]: The first line is the same as Ādi 8.4; translated as there. The *Anubhāṣya* gives here another reading of two verses: “Glory, glory to Nityānanda, the moon among avadhūtas, who bound the world with the noose of prema! Glory, glory to Śrī Advaita, the descent of Īśvara, who called Kṛṣṇa down and delivered the world!”

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Rāmacandra Purī was a disciple of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, and so Mahāprabhu and Paramānanda Purī honoured him. *Anubhāṣya*: by nature he was envious and hostile to Hari, the guru and the Vaiṣṇavas, but he wore the dress of a sannyāsī, and only for that did people call him “Gosāñi”: in those days the title was given to those who had left home, not by caste or birth.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya*: taking Mahāprabhu for a follower of Īśvara Purī, Rāmacandra Purī embraced Him and remembered Kṛṣṇa, as is customary among Vaiṣṇava sannyāsīs: when greeted, they reply “*oṁ namo bhagavate nārāyaṇāya*”; a sannyāsī is not to bless living beings or bow to them.

[^5]: Heading in the edition: having eaten more than his fill, he blames the one who feeds him.

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *bhāsa* — at least a semblance (*ābhāsa*). Heading in the edition: he blames the associates of Gaura, truly endowed with pure renunciation, as if they lacked it.

[^7]: Heading in the edition: the past of Rāmacandra Purī, rejected by his guru; he comes when his gurudeva, Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, is departing from this world.

[^8]: Heading in the edition: Śrī Mādhavendra Purī chants of Kṛṣṇa in the transcendental rasa of separation (*vipralambha*).

[^9]: Heading in the edition: Rāmacandra opposes the spiritual pastimes (*cid-vilāsa*).

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: Mādhavendra Purī was suffering in separation from Kṛṣṇa — transcendental *vipralambha* had appeared in him; but Rāmacandra, reasoning in a worldly way and taking his guru for a mortal, thought he grieved over some worldly want and hastened to bring him to the realisation of impersonal Brahman. Seeing the folly of his disciple and his disrespect for the guru, Mādhavendra Purī rejected him and drove him away.

[^11]: Heading in the edition: from the offence of disrespect for the guru arises the desire for sense enjoyment, that is, worldly desires. The *Anubhāṣya* quotes the *Bhagavat-pariśiṣṭa*: even those liberated in this life fall back into worldly desires if they offend the Lord. The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* explains *vāsanā* otherwise — as the desire for dry knowledge, from which blasphemy of the devotees is born.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: *nirbandha* — a stubborn attachment to blaming others. Impersonalists, weak in the knowledge of one’s relationship with the Lord (*sambandha-jñāna*), cannot accept a relationship with Kṛṣṇa and blame the devotees and their service; a soul falls into such ignorance when he offends the feet of the guru or of a devotee.

[^13]: Śrī Īśvara Purī is the Guru of Mahāprabhu. *Anubhāṣya*: *śrīpāda-sevana* — service to Śrī Mādhavendra Purīpāda. Heading in the edition: the undivided devotion of Śrīla Īśvara Purī to his guru.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: from the great soul, Śrīla Mādhavendra Purī, Īśvara Purī received abundant mercy (*anugraha*), and Rāmacandra Purī only punishment (*nigraha*).

[^15]: The same verse as Madhya 4.197; translated as there. Here it is spoken by Mādhavendra Purī himself. The source follows the heading in the edition of this chapter: “the words of Mādhavendra Purī, given in the *Padyāvalī* as number 330” (the heading to Madhya 4.197 gives number 400); the *Anubhāṣya* refers to Madhya 4.197. Heading in the edition (to 31): Mādhavendra Gosvāmī departs from the manifest world in the transcendental state of separation.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: *bhāva-viśeṣa* — the manifestation of the feeling of separation (*vipralambha*). Among the *prākṛta-sahajiyās*, practitioners commit all kinds of excesses in the name of *sambhoga* (union) and so obstruct the realisation of the true nature of separation.

[^17]: Heading in the edition: Mādhavendra is the sprout of the wish-fulfilling tree of love for Kṛṣṇa; Śrī Caitanya Himself is the main tree, grown from that sprout and spread wide.

[^18]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *niryāṇa* — departure from the manifest world (*aprakaṭa*). Heading in the edition: hearing of Mādhavendra’s departure, living beings learn the service born of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

[^19]: Heading in the edition: the dry renunciation of Rāmacandra Purī.

[^20]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: he found out how others took their meals — what and how much they ate. *Anubhāṣya*: he spent his days keeping account of where another sannyāsī took alms, how much, where he lived and so on.

[^21]: A paṇa is eighty cowries. Heading in the edition: how the Lord took His meals each day; with Him ate Govinda and Kāśīśvara, disciples of Śrī Īśvara Purī.

[^22]: *Aikṣava* — molasses from sugarcane (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*). *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu is Bhagavān Himself, beyond all material qualities; finding no flaw in Him and seeing no sweets in His house, Rāmacandra guessed from the ants that molasses had been there at night, so as to mention some flaw and thereby exalt himself.

[^23]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *kalpita nindana* — a falsely imputed charge.

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*: hearing Himself blamed, the Lord, in the role of ācārya, the guru of the world, feels fear and shame.

[^25]: *Anubhāṣya*: Jagannātha’s prasāda — rice — is received in earthen pots; a quarter of a pot of the usual measure is called *ek-cauṭhi*. A gaṇḍā is four cowries.

[^26]: The Lord ate half of the appointed quarter-pot, and Govinda the rest (57). Heading in the edition (to 57): the devotees give up food and drink.

[^27]: Heading in the edition: the Lord, in the role of an ācārya who gives honour to others (*mānada*), always honours Rāmacandra.

[^28]: Heading in the edition: he teaches the Lord the dharma of a renunciant (*yati-dharma*).

[^29]: Heading in the edition: the Lord’s humble words — an example of the dharma of one who seeks no honour (*amānī*).

[^30]: Heading in the edition (to 69): the devotees headed by Paramānanda Purī ask the Lord to take the proper measure of food and censure before Him the nature and conduct of Rāmacandra Purī.

[^31]: *Bhāsa* — “a semblance, a shadow” (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* to 14), as in 14: there is not even a semblance of dharma in you.

[^32]: *Ekātmakam* — “one in nature” (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*). *Anubhāṣya*: thus Śrī Bhagavān describes to Uddhava the conduct of a pure jñānī; the verse speaks of praise and blame of the nature and acts of others meant to harm them.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: in verse 76 the earlier injunction is “do not praise”, the later “do not blame”; the later is stronger, so praising people is no great sin, but blaming them must be avoided at all costs. Rāmacandra kept the earlier and not the later; the verse may be read as mockery.

[^34]: The source follows the heading in the edition: “a maxim of Nyāya” (*nyāya-vacana*).

[^35]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *iṅhā kahite*, “to speak of this” (another reading: *iṅhā karite*, “to act so”). *Anubhāṣya*: *pāya* — “having felt” (the heart’s pain).

[^36]: Heading in the edition (to 82): the Lord, guru of the world and teacher of the people, sets down the rules of the renunciant’s dharma; though He is Īśvara Himself, He regards Himself as a jīva bound by the rules of renunciation and teaches detachment.

[^37]: Heading in the edition: Bhagavān, subject to His devotees, eats in the homes of Gadādhara, Bhagavān Ācārya and Sārvabhauma.

[^38]: Heading in the edition: the purpose of the Lord’s descent and the way He acts.

[^39]: Heading in the edition: sometimes the Lord behaves like a worldly jīva and so deceives, and sometimes, as the Supreme Lord, He bestows full mercy.

[^40]: Heading in the edition: sometimes He shows Rāmacandra Purī worldly honour, and sometimes He holds him of no account.

[^41]: Heading in the edition: all that the inconceivable Īśvara does is eternal, beneficial and beautiful.

[^42]: Heading in the edition: when, for disrespect towards the guru, the guru disregards a jīva, the fruit is enmity towards Viṣṇu, that is, godlessness (*pāṣaṇḍatā*).

[^43]: *Anubhāṣya*: through the conduct of the offender Rāmacandra the Lord teaches the world.

[^44]: The colophon verse is the same as Antya 2.172; translated as there.
