“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 9  
**The Pilgrimage to the Holy Places of the South**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

নানামতগ্রাহগ্রস্তান্ দাক্ষিণাত্যজনদ্বিপান্ ৷  
কৃপারিণা বিমুচ্যৈতান্ গৌরশ্চক্রে স বৈষ্ণবান্ ॥ ১ ॥

nānā-mata-grāha-grastān  
of various  doctrines  by the crocodiles  seized  
dākṣiṇātya-jana-dvipān  
of the South  the people  elephants  
kṛpāriṇā vimucyaitān  
with the disc of mercy  freeing  them  
gauraś cakre sa vaiṣṇavān  
Gaura  made  He  Vaiṣṇavas

(1) The people of the South were like elephants seized by the crocodiles of various doctrines. Gaura freed them with the disc of His mercy and made them Vaiṣṇavas.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

দক্ষিণগমন প্রভুর অতি বিলক্ষণ ৷  
সহস্র সহস্র তীর্থ কৈল দরশন ॥ ৩ ॥

dakṣiṇa-gamana prabhura ati vilakṣaṇa  
to the south  the journey  of the Lord  most  extraordinary  
sahasra sahasra tīrtha kaila daraśana  
thousands  upon thousands  of holy places  He made  visit

(3) The Lord’s journey to the south was most extraordinary: He visited thousands upon thousands of holy places.

সেই সব তীর্থ স্পর্শি, মহাতীর্থ কৈল ৷  
সেই ছলে সেই দেশের লোক নিস্তারিল ॥ ৪ ॥

sei saba tīrtha sparśi, mahā-tīrtha kaila  
those  all  holy places  touching  great holy places  He made  
sei chale sei deśera loka nistārila  
under that  pretext  of that  land  the people  He delivered

(4) By touching all those holy places He made them great holy places, and under that pretext He delivered the people of that land.

সেইসব তীর্থের ক্রম করিতে না পারি ৷  
দক্ষিণ-বামে তীর্থ-গমন হয় ফেরাফেরি ॥ ৫ ॥

seisaba tīrthera krama karite nā pāri  
of those  holy places  the order  to arrange  not  I am able  
dakṣiṇa-vāme tīrtha-gamana haya pherāpheri  
right and left  to the holy places  the journey  was  back and forth

(5) I cannot set those holy places in order, for His journey to them went right and left, back and forth.[^3]

অতএব নাম-মাত্র করিয়ে গণন ৷  
কহিতে না পারি তার যথা অনুক্রম ॥ ৬ ॥

ataeva nāma-mātra kariye gaṇana  
therefore  the names  only  I make  enumeration  
kahite nā pāri tāra yathā anukrama  
to describe  not  I am able  their  proper  sequence

(6) Therefore I simply list their names; I cannot describe them in their proper sequence.

পূর্ব্ববৎ পথে যাইতে যে পায় দরশন ৷  
যেই গ্রামে যায়, সে গ্রামের যত জন ॥ ৭ ॥

সবেই বৈষ্ণব হয়, কহে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
অন্য গ্রাম নিস্তারয়ে সেই ‘বৈষ্ণব’ করি’ ॥ ৮ ॥

pūrvavat pathe yāite ye pāya daraśana  
as before  on the road  going  whoever  received  sight  
yei grāme yāya, se grāmera yata jana  
to whichever  village  He went  of that  village  all  the people  
sabei vaiṣṇava haya, kahe ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’  
all  Vaiṣṇavas  become  say  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Hari”  
anya grāma nistāraye sei ‘vaiṣṇava’ kari’  
other  villages  deliver  them  Vaiṣṇavas  making

(7–8) As before, whoever saw Him on the road and all the people of every village He came to became Vaiṣṇavas and chanted “Kṛṣṇa” and “Hari.” They in turn delivered other villages, making their people Vaiṣṇavas.

দক্ষিণ দেশের লোক অনেক প্রকার ৷  
কেহ জ্ঞানী, কেহ কর্ম্মী, পাষণ্ডী অপার ॥ ৯ ॥

dakṣiṇa deśera loka aneka prakāra  
of the south  of the land  the people  of many  kinds  
keha jñānī, keha karmī, pāṣaṇḍī apāra  
some  jñānīs  some  karmīs  pāṣaṇḍīs  countless

(9) The people of the South were of many kinds: some were *jñānīs*, some were *karmīs*, and the unbelievers were countless.[^4]

সেই সব লোক প্রভুর দর্শনপ্রভাবে ৷  
নিজ-নিজ-মত ছাড়ি’ হইল বৈষ্ণবে ॥ ১০ ॥

sei saba loka prabhura darśana-prabhāve  
those  all  people  of the Lord  of the sight  by the power  
nija-nija-mata chāḍi’ haila vaiṣṇave  
their  own  doctrines  giving up  became  Vaiṣṇavas

(10) By the power of seeing the Lord, all those people gave up their own doctrines and became Vaiṣṇavas.

বৈষ্ণবের মধ্যে রাম-উপাসক সব ৷  
কেহ ‘তত্ত্ববাদী’, কেহ হয় ‘শ্রীবৈষ্ণব’ ॥ ১১ ॥

vaiṣṇavera madhye rāma-upāsaka saba  
the Vaiṣṇavas  among  of Rāma  worshipers  all  
keha ‘tattvavādī’, keha haya ‘śrī-vaiṣṇava’  
some  Tattvavādīs  some  were  Śrī Vaiṣṇavas

(11) Among the Vaiṣṇavas, all were worshipers of Rāma: some were Tattvavādīs, and some were Śrī Vaiṣṇavas.

সেই সব বৈষ্ণব মহাপ্রভুর দর্শনে ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-উপাসক হৈল, লয় কৃষ্ণনামে ॥ ১২ ॥

sei saba vaiṣṇava mahāprabhura darśane  
those  all  Vaiṣṇavas  of Mahāprabhu  upon seeing  
kṛṣṇa-upāsaka haila, laya kṛṣṇa-nāme  
of Kṛṣṇa  worshipers  became  take  the name of Kṛṣṇa

(12) Upon seeing Mahāprabhu, all those Vaiṣṇavas became worshipers of Kṛṣṇa and took up the name of Kṛṣṇa.

রাম! রাঘব! রাম! রাঘব! রাম! রাঘব! পাহি মাম্ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ! কেশব! কৃষ্ণ! কেশব! কৃষ্ণ! কেশব! রক্ষ মাম্ ॥ ১৩ ॥

rāma! rāghava! rāma! rāghava! rāma! rāghava! pāhi mām  
Rāma  descendant of Raghu  Rāma  descendant of Raghu  Rāma  descendant of Raghu  save  me  
kṛṣṇa! keśava! kṛṣṇa! keśava! kṛṣṇa! keśava! rakṣa mām  
Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  protect  me

(13) “Rāma! Rāghava! Rāma! Rāghava! Rāma! Rāghava! Save me! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Protect me!”

এই শ্লোক পথে পড়ি’ করিলা প্রয়াণ ৷  
গৌতমী-গঙ্গায় যাই’ কৈল গঙ্গাস্নান ॥ ১৪ ॥

ei śloka pathe paḍi’ karilā prayāṇa  
this  verse  on the way  reciting  He made  the journey  
gautamī-gaṅgāya yāi’ kaila gaṅgā-snāna  
at Gautamī-gaṅgā  arriving  He took  a bath in the Gaṅgā

(14) Reciting this verse along the way, the Lord journeyed on. He came to Gautamī-gaṅgā and bathed in the Gaṅgā there.

মল্লিকার্জ্জুন-তীর্থে যাই’ মহেশ দেখিল ৷  
তাঁহা সব লোকে কৃষ্ণনাম লওয়াইল ॥ ১৫ ॥

mallikārjuna-tīrthe yāi’ maheśa dekhila  
to Mallikārjuna-tīrtha  going  Maheśa  He saw  
tām̐hā saba loke kṛṣṇa-nāma laoyāila  
there  all  the people  the name of Kṛṣṇa  He made take

(15) He went to Mallikārjuna-tīrtha and saw Maheśa (Śiva). There He made all the people take the name of Kṛṣṇa.

রামদাস মহাদেবে করিল দরশন ৷  
অহোবল-নৃসিংহেরে করিলা গমন ॥ ১৬ ॥

rāmadāsa mahādeve karila daraśana  
the servant of Rāma  Mahādeva  He made  sight  
ahovala-nṛsiṁhere karilā gamana  
to Ahobala-nṛsiṁha  He made  going

(16) He had *darśana* of Mahādeva, the servant of Rāma, and then went to Ahovala-nṛsiṁha.

নৃসিংহ দেখিয়া তাঁরে কৈল নতি-স্তুতি ৷  
সিদ্ধবট গেলা যাঁহা মূর্ত্তি সীতাপতি ॥ ১৭ ॥

nṛsiṁha dekhiyā tām̐re kaila nati-stuti  
Nṛsiṁha  seeing  to Him  He made  obeisances and prayers  
siddhavaṭa gelā yām̐hā mūrti sītāpati  
to Siddhavaṭa  He went  where  the Deity  of Sītā’s Lord

(17) Seeing Nṛsiṁha, He offered Him obeisances and prayers. Then He went to Siddhavaṭa, where the Deity of Sītā’s Lord is.

রঘুনাথ দেখি’ কৈল প্রণতি স্তবন ৷  
তাঁহা এক বিপ্র প্রভুর কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১৮ ॥

raghunātha dekhi’ kaila praṇati stavana  
Raghunātha  seeing  He made  obeisances  prayers  
tām̐hā eka vipra prabhura kaila nimantraṇa  
there  a  brāhmaṇa  the Lord  made  invitation

(18) Seeing Raghunātha, He offered obeisances and prayers. There a brāhmaṇa invited the Lord to take His meal.

সেই বিপ্র রামনাম নিরন্তর লয় ৷  
‘রাম’ ‘রাম’ বিনা অন্য বাণী না কহয় ॥ ১৯ ॥

sei vipra rāma-nāma nirantara laya  
that  brāhmaṇa  the name of Rāma  constantly  takes  
‘rāma’ ‘rāma’ vinā anya vāṇī nā kahaya  
“Rāma”  “Rāma”  except  other  words  not  he speaks

(19) That brāhmaṇa chanted the name of Rāma constantly. Apart from “Rāma, Rāma,” he spoke no other word.

সেই দিন তাঁর ঘরে রহি’ ভিক্ষা করি’ ৷  
তাঁরে কৃপা করি’ আগে চলিলা গৌরহরি ॥ ২০ ॥

sei dina tām̐ra ghare rahi’ bhikṣā kari’  
that  day  in his  house  staying  alms  accepting  
tām̐re kṛpā kari’ āge calilā gaurahari  
to him  mercy  showing  onward  went  Gaurahari

(20) Gaurahari stayed in his house that day and accepted His meal there. Then, after blessing him, He went on.

স্কন্দক্ষেত্র-তীর্থে কৈল স্কন্দ দরশন ৷  
ত্রিমঠ আইলা, তাঁহা দেখি’ ত্রিবিক্রম ॥ ২১ ॥

skanda-kṣetra-tīrthe kaila skanda daraśana  
at Skanda-kṣetra  He made  of Skanda  sight  
trimaṭha āilā, tām̐hā dekhi’ trivikrama  
to Trimaṭha  He came  there  seeing  Trivikrama

(21) At Skanda-kṣetra He had darśana of Skanda. Then He came to Trimaṭha and saw Trivikrama there.

পুনঃ সিদ্ধবট আইলা সেই বিপ্র ঘরে ৷  
সেই বিপ্র কৃষ্ণনাম লয় নিরন্তরে ॥ ২২ ॥

punaḥ siddhavaṭa āilā sei vipra-ghare  
again  to Siddhavaṭa  He came  of that  brāhmaṇa  to the house  
sei vipra kṛṣṇa-nāma laya nirantare  
that  brāhmaṇa  the name of Kṛṣṇa  takes  constantly

(22) He came back to Siddhavaṭa, to the house of that brāhmaṇa, and now the brāhmaṇa was constantly chanting the name of Kṛṣṇa.

ভিক্ষা করি’ মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে প্রশ্ন কৈল ৷  
‘কহ বিপ্র, এই তোমার কোন্ দশা হৈল ॥ ২৩ ॥

bhikṣā kari’ mahāprabhu tām̐re praśna kaila  
meal  taking  Mahāprabhu  to him  question  put  
‘kaha vipra, ei tomāra kon daśā haila  
tell  brāhmaṇa  this  your  what  state  has come

(23) After taking His meal, Mahāprabhu asked him, “Tell Me, brāhmaṇa, what state has come over you?

পূর্ব্বে তুমি নিরন্তর লৈতে রামনাম ৷  
এবে কেনে নিরন্তর লও কৃষ্ণনাম’ ॥ ২৪ ॥

pūrve tumi nirantara laite rāma-nāma  
before  you  constantly  used to take  the name of Rāma  
ebe kene nirantara lao kṛṣṇa-nāma’  
now  why  constantly  you take  the name of Kṛṣṇa

(24) Before, you chanted the name of Rāma constantly. Why do you now constantly chant the name of Kṛṣṇa?”

বিপ্র বলে, — এই তোমার দর্শন-প্রভাবে ৷  
তোমা দেখি’ গেল মোর আজন্ম-স্বভাবে ॥ ২৫ ॥

vipra bale, — ei tomāra darśana-prabhāve  
the brāhmaṇa  says  this  of You  of the sight  by the power  
tomā dekhi’ gela mora ājanma-svabhāve  
You  seeing  went away  my  from birth  nature

(25) The brāhmaṇa said, “This is by the power of seeing You. When I saw You, the nature I had from birth left me.

বাল্যাবধি রামনাম গ্রহণ আমার ৷  
তোমা দেখি’ কৃষ্ণনাম আইল একবার ॥ ২৬ ॥

bālyāvadhi rāma-nāma-grahaṇa āmāra  
from childhood  of the name of Rāma  the taking  mine  
tomā dekhi’ kṛṣṇa-nāma āila eka-bāra  
You  seeing  the name of Kṛṣṇa  came  once

(26) From childhood I have chanted the name of Rāma. When I saw You, the name of Kṛṣṇa came to me once.

সেই হৈতে কৃষ্ণনাম জিহ্বাতে বসিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম স্ফুরে, রামনাম দূরে গেলা ॥ ২৭ ॥

sei haite kṛṣṇa-nāma jihvāte vasilā  
from that  time  the name of Kṛṣṇa  on my tongue  sat  
kṛṣṇa-nāma sphure, rāma-nāma dūre gelā  
the name of Kṛṣṇa  appears  the name of Rāma  far  went away

(27) Since then the name of Kṛṣṇa has dwelt on my tongue: it is Kṛṣṇa’s name that now comes forth, and the name of Rāma has gone far away.

বাল্যকাল হৈতে মোর স্বভাব এক হয় ৷  
নামের মহিমা শাস্ত্র করিয়ে সঞ্চয় ॥ ২৮ ॥

bālya-kāla haite mora svabhāva eka haya  
from childhood  time  my  nature  one  is  
nāmera mahimā-śāstra kariye sañcaya  
of the name  of the glory  of the scriptures  I make  collecting

(28) From childhood I have had one habit: I collect what the scriptures say about the glory of the holy name.

রমন্তে যোগীনোঽনন্তে সত্যানন্দে চিদাত্মনি ৷  
ইতি রামপদেনাসৌ পরং ব্রহ্মাভিধীয়তে ॥ ২৯ ॥

ramante yogīno ’nante  
take delight  the yogīs  in the Infinite  
satyānande cid-ātmani  
in true bliss  in the spiritual self  
iti rāma-padenāsau  
thus  by the word “Rāma”  this  
paraṁ brahmābhidhīyate  
the Supreme  Brahman  is called

(29) “The yogīs delight (*ramante*) in the Infinite, in true bliss, in the spiritual Self; therefore the Supreme Brahman is called ‘Rāma.’ ” (Padma Purāṇa, Śrī Rāmacandra’s Śata-nāma-stotra, 8)[^5]

কৃষির্ভূবাচকঃ শব্দো ণশ্চ নির্বৃতিবাচকঃ ৷  
তয়োরৈক্যং পরং ব্রহ্ম কৃষ্ণ ইত্যভিধীয়তে ॥ ৩০ ॥

kṛṣir bhū-vācakaḥ śabdo  
“kṛṣ”  being  denoting  the sound  
ṇaś ca nirvṛti-vācakaḥ  
“ṇa”  and  bliss  denoting  
tayor aikyaṁ paraṁ brahma  
of the two  the union  the Supreme  Brahman  
kṛṣṇa ity abhidhīyate  
Kṛṣṇa  thus  is called

(30) “The sound ‘kṛṣ’ denotes being, and ‘ṇa’ denotes bliss. Their union, the Supreme Brahman, is called ‘Kṛṣṇa.’ ” (Mahābhārata, Udyoga-parva, 71.4)[^6]

পরংব্রহ্ম দুইনাম সমান হইল ৷  
পুনঃ আর শাস্ত্রে কিছু বিশেষ পাইল ॥ ৩১ ॥

paraṁ brahma dui-nāma samāna haila  
of the Supreme  Brahman  the two names  equal  turned out  
punaḥ āra śāstre kichu viśeṣa pāila  
again  further  in the scriptures  some  distinction  I found

(31) Both names belong to the Supreme Brahman and so are equal. But in the scriptures I found a further distinction.

রাম রামেতি রামেতি রমে রামে মনোরমে ৷  
সহস্রনামভিস্তুল্যং রামনাম বরাননে ॥ ৩২ ॥

rāma rāmeti rāmeti  
“Rāma  Rāma  Rāma” (chanting)  
rame rāme manorame  
I take delight  in Rāma  enchanting  
sahasra-nāmabhis tulyaṁ  
to the thousand names  equal  
rāma-nāma varānane  
the name of Rāma  O fair-faced one

(32) “O fair-faced one, chanting ‘Rāma, Rāma, Rāma,’ I take delight in the enchanting Rāma. The name of Rāma is equal to the thousand names (of Viṣṇu).” (Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 72.335)

সহস্রনাম্নাং পুণ্যানাং ত্রিরাবৃত্ত্যা তু যৎ ফলম্ ৷  
একাবৃত্ত্যা তু কৃষ্ণস্য নামৈকং তৎ প্রযচ্ছতি ॥ ৩৩ ॥

sahasra-nāmnāṁ puṇyānāṁ  
of the thousand names  holy  
trir-āvṛttyā tu yat phalam  
by three repetitions  indeed  what  result  
ekāvṛttyā tu kṛṣṇasya  
by one repetition  indeed  of Kṛṣṇa  
nāmaikaṁ tat prayacchati  
the name alone  that  gives

(33) “The result obtained by reciting the thousand holy names (of Viṣṇu) three times is given by a single utterance of one name of Kṛṣṇa.” (Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa)

এই বাক্যে কৃষ্ণনামের মহিমা অপার ৷  
তথাপি লইতে নারি, শুন হেতু তার ॥ ৩৪ ॥

ei vākye kṛṣṇa-nāmera mahimā apāra  
in this  statement  of the name of Kṛṣṇa  the glory  boundless  
tathāpi laite nāri, śuna hetu tāra  
still  to take  I could not  hear  the reason  for that

(34) This statement shows the boundless glory of the name of Kṛṣṇa. Still I could not chant it; hear the reason.

ইষ্টদেব রাম, তাঁর নামে সুখ পাই ৷  
সুখ পাঞা রামনাম রাত্রিদিন গাই ॥ ৩৫ ॥

iṣṭa-deva rāma, tām̐ra nāme sukha pāi  
my chosen Deity  Rāma  His  in the name  happiness  I find  
sukha pāñā rāma-nāma rātri-dina gāi  
happiness  finding  the name of Rāma  night and day  I sing

(35) Rāma is my chosen Deity, and I found happiness in His name; finding that happiness, I sang the name of Rāma night and day.

তোমার দর্শনে যবে কৃষ্ণনাম আইল ৷  
তাহার মহিমা তবে হৃদয়ে লাগিল ॥ ৩৬ ॥

tomāra darśane yabe kṛṣṇa-nāma āila  
of You  at the sight  when  the name of Kṛṣṇa  came  
tāhāra mahimā tabe hṛdaye lāgila  
its  glory  then  in my heart  took hold

(36) When the name of Kṛṣṇa came to me at the sight of You, its glory took hold of my heart.

সেই কৃষ্ণ তুমি — ইহা সাক্ষাৎ নির্দ্ধারিল ৷  
এত কহি’ বিপ্র প্রভুর চরণে পড়িল ॥ ৩৭ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa tumi — ihā sākṣāt nirddhārila  
that  Kṛṣṇa  You  this  directly  I have ascertained  
eta kahi’ vipra prabhura caraṇe paḍila  
so  saying  the brāhmaṇa  of the Lord  at the feet  fell

(37) I have clearly understood that You are that very Kṛṣṇa.” Saying this, the brāhmaṇa fell at the Lord’s feet.

তাঁরে কৃপা করি’ প্রভু চলিলা আর দিনে ৷  
বৃদ্ধকাশী আসি’ কৈল শিব দরশনে ॥ ৩৮ ॥

tām̐re kṛpā kari’ prabhu calilā āra dine  
to him  mercy  showing  the Lord  departed  the next  day  
vṛddhakāśī āsi’ kaila śiva daraśane  
to Vṛddhakāśī  coming  He made  of Śiva  sight

(38) After showing him mercy, the Lord set out the next day. He came to Vṛddhakāśī and had darśana of Śiva.

তাঁহা হৈতে চলি’ আগে গেলা এক গ্রামে ৷  
ব্রাহ্মণ-সমাজ তাঁহা, করিল বিশ্রামে ॥ ৩৯ ॥

tām̐hā haite cali’ āge gelā eka grāme  
from there  setting out  further  He went  to a  village  
brāhmaṇa-samāja tām̐hā, karila viśrāme  
of brāhmaṇas  a community  there  He made  rest

(39) Going on from there, He came to a village where there was a community of brāhmaṇas, and He rested there.

প্রভুর প্রভাবে লোক আইল দরশনে ৷  
লক্ষার্ব্বুদ লোক আইসে, না যায় গণনে ॥ ৪০ ॥

prabhura prabhāve loka āila daraśane  
of the Lord  by the influence  people  came  for sight  
lakṣārbuda loka āise, nā yāya gaṇane  
hundreds of thousands and millions  of people  come  not  can be  counted

(40) Drawn by the Lord’s power, people came to see Him — hundreds of thousands, millions, beyond counting.

গোসাঞির সৌন্দর্য্য দেখি’ তাতে প্রেমাবেশ ৷  
সবে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহে, ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হৈল সর্ব্বদেশ ॥ ৪১ ॥

gosāñira saundarya dekhi’ tāte premāveśa  
of the Gosāñi  the beauty  seeing  in it  absorption in love  
sabe ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahe, ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila sarva-deśa  
all  “Kṛṣṇa”  say  Vaiṣṇava  became  the whole  land

(41) Seeing the Lord’s beauty and His absorption in love, everyone said “Kṛṣṇa,” and the whole land became Vaiṣṇava.

তার্কিক-মীমাংসক, যত মায়াবাদিগণ ৷  
সাংখ্য, পাতঞ্জল, স্মৃতি, পুরাণ, আগম ॥ ৪২ ॥

tārkika-mīmāṁsaka, yata māyāvādi-gaṇa  
logicians  Mīmāṁsakas  all  Māyāvādīs  
sāṅkhya, pātañjala, smṛti, purāṇa, āgama  
Sāṅkhya  Pātañjala  smṛti  Purāṇa  Āgama

(42) There were logicians and Mīmāṁsakas, all the Māyāvādīs, and followers of Sāṅkhya and Pātañjala (yoga), of the smṛtis, the Purāṇas and the Āgamas.[^7]

নিজ-নিজ-শাস্ত্রোদ্গ্রাহে সবাই প্রচণ্ড ৷  
সর্ব্ব মত দুষি’ প্রভু করে খণ্ড খণ্ড ॥ ৪৩ ॥

nija-nija-śāstrodgrāhe sabāi pracaṇḍa  
of their own  scriptures  in insisting  all  fierce  
sarva mata duṣi’ prabhu kare khaṇḍa khaṇḍa  
all  doctrines  finding fault  the Lord  makes  to pieces  to pieces

(43) All of them were fierce in insisting on their own scriptures, but the Lord exposed the faults of every doctrine and broke them to pieces.

সর্ব্বত্র স্থাপয় প্রভু বৈষ্ণবসিদ্ধান্তে ৷  
প্রভুর সিদ্ধান্ত কেহ না পারে খণ্ডিতে ॥ ৪৪ ॥

sarvatra sthāpaya prabhu vaiṣṇava-siddhānte  
everywhere  establishes  the Lord  the Vaiṣṇava conclusion  
prabhura siddhānta keha nā pāre khaṇḍite  
of the Lord  the conclusion  anyone  not  is able  to refute

(44) Everywhere the Lord established the Vaiṣṇava siddhānta, and no one could refute His conclusions.

হারি’ হারি’ প্রভুমতে করেন প্রবেশ ৷  
এইমতে ‘বৈষ্ণব’ করিল দক্ষিণ দেশ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

hāri’ hāri’ prabhu-mate karena praveśa  
defeated  defeated  into the Lord’s doctrine  they make  entry  
ei-mate ‘vaiṣṇava’ karila dakṣiṇa deśa  
in this way  Vaiṣṇava  He made  the southern  land

(45) Defeated again and again, they entered into the Lord’s doctrine. In this way He made the South Vaiṣṇava.

পাষণ্ডী আইল যত পাণ্ডিত্য শুনিয়া ৷  
গর্ব্ব করি’ আইল সঙ্গে শিষ্যগণ লঞা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

pāṣaṇḍī āila yata pāṇḍitya śuniyā  
the pāṣaṇḍīs  came  all  of His learning  hearing  
garva kari’ āila saṅge śiṣya-gaṇa lañā  
pride  showing  they came  with them  disciples  bringing

(46) Hearing of His learning, all the unbelievers came; full of pride, they brought their disciples with them.

বৌদ্ধাচার্য্য মহা-পণ্ডিত নিজ নব মতে ৷  
প্রভুর আগেতে উদ্গ্রাহ করি লাগিলা বলিতে ॥ ৪৭ ॥

bauddhācārya mahā-paṇḍita nija nava mate  
a Buddhist ācārya  great  scholar  in his own  nine  principles  
prabhura āgete udgrāha kari lāgilā balite  
of the Lord  before  insistence  showing  began  to speak

(47) A Buddhist ācārya, a great scholar in his own nine principles, began to argue them insistently before the Lord.

যদ্যপি অসম্ভাষ্য বৌদ্ধ অযুক্ত দেখিতে ৷  
তথাপি বলিলা প্রভু গর্ব্ব খণ্ডাইতে ॥ ৪৮ ॥

yadyapi asambhāṣya bauddha ayukta dekhite  
although  not to be spoken with  the Buddhists  improper  to look at  
tathāpi balilā prabhu garva khaṇḍāite  
still  spoke  the Lord  the pride  to break

(48) Although the Buddhists are not to be spoken with, and it is improper even to look at them, the Lord spoke in order to break their pride.[^8]

তর্ক প্রধান বৌদ্ধ-শাস্ত্র ‘নব মতে’ ৷  
তর্কেই খণ্ডিল প্রভু, না পারে স্থাপিতে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

tarka-pradhāna bauddha-śāstra ‘nava mate’  
on logic  based  Buddhist  scriptures  “nine  principles”  
tarkei khaṇḍila prabhu, nā pāre sthāpite  
by logic itself  refuted  the Lord  not  they can  establish

(49) The Buddhist scriptures, with their “nine principles,” rest on logic, so the Lord refuted them by logic alone, and they could not uphold them.[^9][^10]

বৌদ্ধাচার্য্য ‘নব প্রশ্ন’ সব উঠাইল ৷  
দৃঢ় যুক্তি-তর্কে প্রভু খণ্ড খণ্ড কৈল ॥ ৫০ ॥

bauddhācārya ‘nava praśna’ saba uṭhāila  
the Buddhist ācārya  “nine  propositions”  all  raised  
dṛḍha yukti-tarke prabhu khaṇḍa khaṇḍa kaila  
with firm  reasoning and logic  the Lord  to pieces  to pieces  broke

(50) The Buddhist ācārya put forward all nine propositions, and the Lord broke them to pieces with firm reasoning and logic.

দার্শনিক পণ্ডিত সবাই পাইল পরাজয় ৷  
লোকে হাস্য করে, বৌদ্ধ পাইল লজ্জা-ভয় ॥ ৫১ ॥

dārśanika paṇḍita sabāi pāila parājaya  
the philosophers  scholars  all  suffered  defeat  
loke hāsya kare, bauddha pāila lajjā-bhaya  
people  laughter  raise  the Buddhists  felt  shame and fear

(51) All the scholar-philosophers were defeated. People laughed, and the Buddhists felt shame and fear.

প্রভুকে বৈষ্ণব জানি’ বৌদ্ধ ঘরে গেল ৷  
সকল বৌদ্ধ মিলি’ তবে কুমন্ত্রণা কৈল ॥ ৫২ ॥

prabhuke vaiṣṇava jāni’ bauddha ghare gela  
the Lord  a Vaiṣṇava  knowing  the Buddhists  home  went  
sakala bauddha mili’ tabe ku-mantraṇā kaila  
all  Buddhists  gathering  then  an evil plot  made

(52) Knowing the Lord to be a Vaiṣṇava, the Buddhists went home. Then all of them gathered and made an evil plot.

অপবিত্র অন্ন এক থালিতে ভরিয়া ৷  
প্রভু-আগে নিল ‘মহাপ্রসাদ’ বলিয়া ॥ ৫৩ ॥

apavitra anna eka thālite bhariyā  
impure  food  a  on a plate  filling  
prabhu-āge nila ‘mahā-prasāda’ baliyā  
of the Lord  before  they brought  “mahā-prasāda”  calling it

(53) They filled a plate with impure food and brought it before the Lord, calling it “mahā-prasāda.”

হেনকালে মহাকায় এক পক্ষী আইল ৷  
ওষ্ঠে করি’ থালি-সহ অন্ন লঞা গেল ॥ ৫৪ ॥

hena-kāle mahā-kāya eka pakṣī āila  
at that time  huge  a  bird  came  
oṣṭhe kari’ thāli-saha anna lañā gela  
in its beak  taking  the plate together with  the food  carrying off  went

(54) Just then a huge bird came, took the plate with the food in its beak and carried it off.

বৌদ্ধগণের উপরে অন্ন পড়ে অমেধ্য হঞা ৷  
বৌদ্ধাচার্য্যের মাথায় থালি পড়িল বাজিয়া ॥ ৫৫ ॥

bauddha-gaṇera upare anna paḍe amedhya haiyā  
of the Buddhists  upon  the food  falls  impure  being  
bauddhācāryera māthāya thāli paḍila bājiyā  
of the Buddhist ācārya  on the head  the plate  fell  with a clang

(55) The impure food fell upon the Buddhists, and the plate fell with a clang on the head of the Buddhist ācārya.

তেরছে পড়িল থালি, — মাথা কাটি’ গেল ৷  
মূর্চ্ছিত হঞা আচার্য্য ভূমিতে পড়িল ॥ ৫৬ ॥

terache paḍila thāli, — māthā kāṭi’ gela  
edgewise  fell  the plate  the head  it cut  
mūrcchita hañā ācārya bhūmite paḍila  
unconscious  becoming  the ācārya  to the ground  fell

(56) The plate fell edgewise and cut his head, and the ācārya fell to the ground unconscious.

হাহাকার করি’ কান্দে সব শিষ্যগণ ৷  
সবে আসি’ প্রভু-পদে লইল শরণ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

hāhākāra kari’ kānde saba śiṣya-gaṇa  
cries of lamentation  raising  weep  all  the disciples  
sabe āsi’ prabhu-pade laila śaraṇa  
all  coming  at the Lord’s feet  took  shelter

(57) All the disciples cried out in lamentation and wept. They came and took shelter at the Lord’s feet:

তুমি ত’ ঈশ্বর সাক্ষাৎ, ক্ষম অপরাধ ৷  
জীয়াও আমার গুরু, করহ প্রসাদ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

tumi ta’ īśvara sākṣāt, kṣama aparādha  
You  indeed  the Lord  Himself  forgive  the offense  
jīyāo āmāra guru, karaha prasāda  
bring back to life  our  guru  show  mercy

(58) “You are the Lord Himself. Forgive our offense. Bring our guru back to life — be merciful!”

প্রভু কহে, সবে কহ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
গুরুকর্ণে কহ কৃষ্ণনাম উচ্চ করি’ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

prabhu kahe, sabe kaha ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’  
the Lord  says  all  say  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Hari”  
guru-karṇe kaha kṛṣṇa-nāma ucca kari’  
of the guru  into the ear  say  the name of Kṛṣṇa  loudly  doing

(59) The Lord said, “All of you say ‘Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, Hari!’ Say the name of Kṛṣṇa loudly into your guru’s ear.

তোমা-সবার ‘গুরু’ তবে পাইবে চেতন ৷  
সব বৌদ্ধ মিলি’ করে কৃষ্ণসঙ্কীর্ত্তন ॥ ৬০ ॥

tomā-sabāra ‘guru’ tabe pāibe cetana  
your  guru  then  will regain  consciousness  
saba bauddha mili’ kare kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana  
all  the Buddhists  together  perform  the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa

(60) Then your guru will regain consciousness.” All the Buddhists together performed the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name.

গুরু-কর্ণে কহে সবে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘রাম’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
চেতন পাঞা আচার্য্য বলে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ৬১ ॥

guru-karṇe kahe sabe ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘rāma’ ‘hari’  
of the guru  into the ear  say  all  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Rāma”  “Hari”  
cetana pāñā ācārya bale ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
consciousness  regaining  the ācārya  says  “Hari”  “Hari”

(61) All of them said “Kṛṣṇa! Rāma! Hari!” into their guru’s ear. Regaining consciousness, the ācārya said, “Hari! Hari!”

কৃষ্ণ বলি’ আচার্য্য প্রভুরে করেন বিনয় ৷  
দেখিয়া সকল লোক হইল বিস্ময় ॥ ৬২ ॥

kṛṣṇa bali’ ācārya prabhure karena vinaya  
“Kṛṣṇa”  saying  the ācārya  to the Lord  shows  humility  
dekhiyā sakala loka haila vismaya  
seeing (this)  all  the people  were  amazed

(62) Saying “Kṛṣṇa,” the ācārya humbly prayed to the Lord. Seeing this, all the people were amazed.

এইরূপে কৌতুক করি’ শচীর নন্দন ৷  
অন্তর্দ্ধান কৈল, কেহ না পায় দর্শন ॥ ৬৩ ॥

ei-rūpe kautuka kari’ śacīra nandana  
in this way  sport  making  of Śacī  the son  
antarddhāna kaila, keha nā pāya darśana  
disappearance  made  no one  not  gets  sight (of Him)

(63) Having played in this way, the son of Śacī disappeared, and no one could see Him.

মহাপ্রভু চলি’ আইলা ত্রিপতী-ত্রিমল্লে ৷  
চতুর্ভুজ মূর্ত্তি দেখি’ ব্যেঙ্কটাদ্র্যে চলে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

mahāprabhu cali’ āilā tripatī-trimalle  
Mahāprabhu  setting out  came  to Tripati-Trimalla  
catur-bhuja mūrti dekhi’ vyeṅkaṭādrye cale  
four-armed  Deity  seeing  to Veṅkaṭādri  goes

(64) Mahāprabhu went on and came to Tripati and Trimalla. Seeing the four-armed Deity, He went on to Veṅkaṭādri.

ত্রিপতি আসিয়া কৈল শ্রীরাম দরশন ৷  
রঘুনাথ-আগে কৈল প্রণাম স্তবন ॥ ৬৫ ॥

tripati āsiyā kaila śrī-rāma daraśana  
to Tripati  coming  He made  of Śrī Rāma  sight  
raghunātha-āge kaila praṇāma stavana  
Raghunātha  before  He made  obeisances  prayers

(65) Coming to Tripati, He had darśana of Śrī Rāma, and before Raghunātha He offered obeisances and prayers.

স্বপ্রভাবে লোক-সবার করাঞা বিস্ময় ৷  
পানা-নৃসিংহে আইলা প্রভু দয়াময় ॥ ৬৬ ॥

sva-prabhāve loka-sabāra karāñā vismaya  
by His own influence  all the people  making  amazed  
pānā-nṛsiṁhe āilā prabhu dayā-maya  
to Pānā-nṛsiṁha  came  the Lord  all-merciful

(66) Amazing all the people by His influence, the all-merciful Lord came to Pānā-nṛsiṁha.

নৃসিংহে প্রণতি-স্তুতি প্রেমাবেশে কৈল ৷  
প্রভুর প্রভাবে লোক চমৎকার হৈল ॥ ৬৭ ॥

nṛsiṁhe praṇati-stuti premāveśe kaila  
to Nṛsiṁha  obeisances and prayers  absorbed in love  He made  
prabhura prabhāve loka camatkāra haila  
of the Lord  by the influence  the people  amazed  became

(67) Absorbed in love, He offered obeisances and prayers to Nṛsiṁha, and the people were astonished at the Lord’s influence.

শিবকাঞ্চী আসিয়া কৈল শিব দরশন ৷  
প্রভাবে ‘বৈষ্ণব’ কৈল সব শৈবগণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

śiva-kāñcī āsiyā kaila śiva daraśana  
to Śiva-kāñcī  coming  He made  of Śiva  sight  
prabhāve ‘vaiṣṇava’ kaila saba śaiva-gaṇa  
by His influence  Vaiṣṇavas  He made  all  the Śaivas

(68) Coming to Śiva-kāñcī, He had darśana of Śiva, and by His influence He made all the Śaivas Vaiṣṇavas.

বিষ্ণুকাঞ্চী আসি’ দেখিল লক্ষ্মী-নারায়ণ ৷  
প্রণাম করিয়া কৈল বহুত স্তবন ॥ ৬৯ ॥

viṣṇu-kāñcī āsi’ dekhila lakṣmī-nārāyaṇa  
to Viṣṇu-kāñcī  coming  He saw  Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa  
praṇāma kariyā kaila bahuta stavana  
obeisance  making  He made  many  prayers

(69) Coming to Viṣṇu-kāñcī, He saw Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa, bowed down and offered many prayers.

প্রেমাবেশে নৃত্য-গীত বহুত করিল ৷  
দিন-দুই রহি’ লোকে ‘কৃষ্ণভক্ত’ কৈল ॥ ৭০ ॥

premāveśe nṛtya-gīta bahuta karila  
absorbed in love  dancing and singing  much  He did  
dina-dui rahi’ loke ‘kṛṣṇa-bhakta’ kaila  
two days  staying  the people  devotees of Kṛṣṇa  He made

(70) Absorbed in love, He danced and sang a great deal. He stayed there two days and made the people devotees of Kṛṣṇa.

ত্রিমলয় দেখি’ গেলা ত্রিকালহস্তী-স্থানে ৷  
মহাদেব দেখি’ তাঁরে করিল প্রণামে ॥ ৭১ ॥

trimalaya dekhi’ gelā trikāla-hastī-sthāne  
Trimalaya  seeing  He went  to Trikālahastī  the place  
mahādeva dekhi’ tām̐re karila praṇāme  
Mahādeva  seeing  to him  He made  obeisances

(71) After seeing Trimalaya, He went to Trikālahastī. There He saw Mahādeva and bowed down to him.

পক্ষীতীর্থ দেখি’ কৈল শিব দরশন ৷  
বৃদ্ধকোল-তীর্থে তবে করিলা গমন ॥ ৭২ ॥

pakṣī-tīrtha dekhi’ kaila śiva daraśana  
Pakṣī-tīrtha  seeing  He made  of Śiva  sight  
vṛddhakola-tīrthe tabe karilā gamana  
to Vṛddhakola-tīrtha  then  He made  going

(72) At Pakṣī-tīrtha He had darśana of Śiva, and then He went to Vṛddhakola-tīrtha.

শ্বেতবরাহ দেখি’ তাঁরে নমস্করি’ ৷  
পীতাম্বর-শিব-স্থানে গেলা গৌরহরি ॥ ৭৩ ॥

śveta-varāha dekhi’ tām̐re namaskari’  
Śveta-varāha  seeing  to Him  bowing down  
pītāmbara-śiva-sthāne gelā gaurahari  
to Pītāmbara Śiva’s  place  went  Gaurahari

(73) There He saw Śveta-varāha and bowed down to Him. Then Gaurahari went to the place of Śiva Pītāmbara.

শিয়ালী ভৈরবী দেবী করি’ দরশন ৷  
কাবেরীর তীরে আইলা শচীর নন্দন ॥ ৭৪ ॥

śiyālī bhairavī devī kari’ daraśana  
Śiyālī  Bhairavī  of the goddess  making  sight  
kāverīra tīre āilā śacīra nandana  
of the Kāverī  to the bank  came  of Śacī  the son

(74) After seeing the goddess Śiyālī-bhairavī, the son of Śacī came to the bank of the Kāverī.

গো-সমাজে শিব দেখি’ আইলা বেদাবন ৷  
মহাদেব দেখি’ তাঁরে করিলা বন্দন ॥ ৭৫ ॥

go-samāje śiva dekhi’ āilā vedāvana  
at Go-samāja  Śiva  seeing  He came  to Vedāvana  
mahādeva dekhi’ tām̐re karilā vandana  
Mahādeva  seeing  to him  He made  homage

(75) At Go-samāja He saw Śiva, and then He came to Vedāvana. There He saw Mahādeva and paid him homage.

অমৃতলিঙ্গ-শিব দেখি’ বন্দন করিল ৷  
সব শিবালয়ে শৈব ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হইল ॥ ৭৬ ॥

amṛtaliṅga-śiva dekhi’ vandana karila  
Amṛta-liṅga Śiva  seeing  homage  He made  
saba śivālaye śaiva ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila  
in all  the temples of Śiva  the Śaivas  Vaiṣṇavas  became

(76) Seeing Śiva Amṛta-liṅga, He paid him homage. In all the temples of Śiva, the Śaivas became Vaiṣṇavas.

দেবস্থানে আসি’ কৈল বিষ্ণু দরশন ৷  
শ্রী-বৈষ্ণবের সঙ্গে তাঁহা গোষ্ঠী অনুক্ষণ ॥ ৭৭ ॥

deva-sthāne āsi’ kaila viṣṇu daraśana  
to Devasthāna  coming  He made  of Viṣṇu  sight  
śrī-vaiṣṇavera saṅge tām̐hā goṣṭhī anukṣaṇa  
the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas  with  there  discussions  constantly

(77) Coming to Devasthāna, He had darśana of Viṣṇu, and there He was constantly in discussion with the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas.

কুম্ভকর্ণ-কপালে দেখি’ সরোবর ৷  
শিব-ক্ষেত্রে শিব দেখে গৌরাঙ্গসুন্দর ॥ ৭৮ ॥

kumbhakarṇa-kapāle dekhi’ sarovara  
at Kumbhakarṇa-kapāla  seeing  the lake  
śiva-kṣetre śiva dekhe gaurāṅga-sundara  
at Śiva-kṣetra  Śiva  sees  Gaurāṅga-sundara

(78) At Kumbhakarṇa-kapāla the beautiful Gaurāṅga saw the lake, and at Śiva-kṣetra He saw Śiva.

পাপনাশনে বিষ্ণু কৈল দরশন ৷  
শ্রীরঙ্গক্ষেত্রে তবে করিলা গমন ॥ ৭৯ ॥

pāpa-nāśane viṣṇu kaila daraśana  
at Pāpanāśana  of Viṣṇu  He made  sight  
śrī-raṅga-kṣetre tabe karilā gamana  
to Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra  then  He made  going

(79) At Pāpanāśana He had darśana of Viṣṇu, and then He went to Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra.

কাবেরীতে স্নান করি’ দেখি’ রঙ্গনাথ ৷  
স্তুতি-প্রণতি করি’ মানিলা কৃতার্থ ॥ ৮০ ॥

kāverīte snāna kari’ dekhi’ raṅganātha  
in the Kāverī  bath  taking  seeing  Raṅganātha  
stuti-praṇati kari’ mānilā kṛtārtha  
prayers and obeisances  making  considered (Himself)  fulfilled

(80) He bathed in the Kāverī and saw Raṅganātha. Offering prayers and obeisances, He felt His purpose fulfilled.

প্রেমাবেশে কৈল বহুত গান নর্ত্তন ৷  
‘দেখি’ চমৎকার হৈল সব লোকের মন ॥ ৮১ ॥

premāveśe kaila bahuta gāna nartana  
absorbed in love  He did  much  singing  dancing  
‘dekhi’ camatkāra haila saba lokera mana  
seeing (this)  amazed  became  of all  the people  the minds

(81) Absorbed in love, He sang and danced a great deal, and all the people who saw it were amazed.

শ্রী-বৈষ্ণব এক, — ‘ব্যেঙ্কট ভট্ট’ নাম ৷  
প্রভুরে নিমন্ত্রণ কৈল করিয়া সম্মান ॥ ৮২ ॥

śrī-vaiṣṇava eka, — ‘vyeṅkaṭa bhaṭṭa’ nāma  
a Śrī Vaiṣṇava  one  “Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa”  by name  
prabhure nimantraṇa kaila kariyā sammāna  
the Lord  invitation  made  showing  respect

(82) A Śrī Vaiṣṇava named Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa respectfully invited the Lord.

নিজ-ঘরে লঞা কৈল পাদপ্রক্ষালন ৷  
সেই জল লঞা কৈল সবংশে ভক্ষণ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

nija-ghare lañā kaila pāda-prakṣālana  
to his own house  bringing  he made  washing of the feet  
sei jala lañā kaila sa-vaṁśe bhakṣaṇa  
that  water  taking  he made  with his whole family  drinking

(83) He brought the Lord to his house and washed His feet, and he and his whole family drank that water.

ভিক্ষা করাঞা কিছু কৈল নিবেদন ৷  
চাতুর্ম্মাস্য আসি’ প্রভু, হৈল উপসন্ন ॥ ৮৪ ॥

bhikṣā karāñā kichu kaila nivedana  
meal  having given  something  he made  request  
cāturmāsya āsi’ prabhu, haila upasanna  
Cāturmāsya  coming  O Lord  has become  near

(84) After serving Him a meal, he made a request: “O Lord, Cāturmāsya has come and is at hand.

চাতুর্ম্মাস্যে কৃপা করি’ রহ মোর ঘরে ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথা কহি’ কৃপায় উদ্ধার’ আমারে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

cāturmāsye kṛpā kari’ raha mora ghare  
during Cāturmāsya  mercy  showing  stay  in my  house  
kṛṣṇa-kathā kahi’ kṛpāya uddhāra’ āmāre  
about Kṛṣṇa  talks  speaking  mercifully  deliver  me

(85) Be merciful and stay in my house for Cāturmāsya. Speak about Kṛṣṇa and mercifully deliver me.”

তাঁর ঘরে রহিলা প্রভু কৃষ্ণকথা রসে ৷  
ভট্টসঙ্গে গোঙাইল সুখে চারিমাসে ॥ ৮৬ ॥

tām̐ra ghare rahilā prabhu kṛṣṇa-kathā-rase  
in his  house  stayed  the Lord  in talks about Kṛṣṇa  with relish  
bhaṭṭa-saṅge goṅāila sukhe cāri māse  
with the Bhaṭṭa  spent  happily  four  months

(86) The Lord stayed in his house, relishing talks about Kṛṣṇa, and happily spent the four months with the Bhaṭṭa.

কাবেরীতে স্নান করি’ শ্রীরঙ্গ দর্শন ৷  
প্রতিদিন প্রেমাবেশে করেন নর্ত্তন ॥ ৮৭ ॥

kāverīte snāna kari’ śrī-raṅga darśana  
in the Kāverī  bath  taking  of Śrī Raṅga  sight  
pratidina premāveśe karena nartana  
every day  absorbed in love  He performs  dancing

(87) Every day He bathed in the Kāverī, had darśana of Śrī Raṅga and danced, absorbed in love.

সৌন্দর্য্যাদি প্রেমাবেশ দেখি’ সর্ব্বলোক ৷  
দেখিবারে আইসে, দেখে, খণ্ডে দুঃখ-শোক ॥ ৮৮ ॥

saundaryādi premāveśa dekhi’ sarva-loka  
beauty and the rest  absorption in love  seeing  all the people  
dekhibāre āise, dekhe, khaṇḍe duḥkha-śoka  
to see  they come  they see  is destroyed  sorrow and grief

(88) Everyone who saw His beauty and the rest and His absorption in love came to see Him; they saw Him, and their sorrow and grief were destroyed.

লক্ষ লক্ষ লোক আইল নানা-দেশ হৈতে ৷  
সবে কৃষ্ণনাম কহে প্রভুকে দেখিতে ॥ ৮৯ ॥

lakṣa lakṣa loka āila nānā-deśa haite  
hundreds  of thousands  of people  came  from various  lands  
sabe kṛṣṇa-nāma kahe prabhuke dekhite  
all  the name of Kṛṣṇa  say  the Lord  on seeing

(89) Hundreds of thousands of people came from various lands, and on seeing the Lord all of them chanted the name of Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণনাম বিনা কেহ নাহি কহে আর ৷  
সবে কৃষ্ণভক্ত হৈল, — লোকে চমৎকার ॥ ৯০ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nāma vinā keha nāhi kahe āra  
the name of Kṛṣṇa  except  no one  not  says  anything else  
sabe kṛṣṇa-bhakta haila, — loke camatkāra  
all  devotees of Kṛṣṇa  became  the people  amazed

(90) No one spoke anything but the name of Kṛṣṇa. Everyone became a devotee of Kṛṣṇa, and the people were amazed.

শ্রীরঙ্গক্ষেত্রে বৈসে যত বৈষ্ণব-ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
এক এক দিন সবে কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৯১ ॥

śrī-raṅga-kṣetre vaise yata vaiṣṇava-brāhmaṇa  
in Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra  dwell  all  the Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇas  
eka eka dina sabe kaila nimantraṇa  
one  day each  all  made  invitation

(91) All the Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇas living in Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra invited Him, each for one day.

এক এক দিনে চাতুর্ম্মাস্য পূর্ণ হৈল ৷  
কতক ব্রাহ্মণ ভিক্ষা দিতে না পাইল ॥ ৯২ ॥

eka eka dine cāturmāsya pūrṇa haila  
one  day each  the Cāturmāsya  complete  became  
kataka brāhmaṇa bhikṣā dite nā pāila  
some  brāhmaṇas  meal  to give  not  got (the chance)

(92) One day each, and the Cāturmāsya came to an end; some brāhmaṇas never got the chance to serve Him a meal.

সেই ক্ষেত্রে রহে এক বৈষ্ণব-ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
দেবালয়ে আসি’ করে গীতা আবর্ত্তন ॥ ৯৩ ॥

sei kṣetre rahe eka vaiṣṇava-brāhmaṇa  
in that  holy place  lives  a  Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇa  
devālaye āsi’ kare gītā āvartana  
to the temple  coming  he makes  of the Gītā  recitation

(93) In that holy place lived a Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇa who would come to the temple and recite the Gītā.

অষ্টাদশাধ্যায় পড়ে আনন্দ-আবেশে ৷  
অশুদ্ধ পড়েন, লোক করে উপহাসে ॥ ৯৪ ॥

aṣṭādaśādhyāya paḍe ānanda-āveśe  
the eighteen chapters  he reads  by bliss  overwhelmed  
aśuddha paḍena, loka kare upahāse  
incorrectly  he reads  people  make  mockery

(94) Overwhelmed with bliss, he read all eighteen chapters; he read them incorrectly, and people mocked him.

কেহ হাসে, কেহ নিন্দে, তাহা নাহি মানে ৷  
আবিষ্ট হঞা গীতা পড়ে আনন্দিত-মনে ॥ ৯৫ ॥

keha hāse, keha ninde, tāhā nāhi māne  
some  laugh  some  censure  that  not  he heeds  
āviṣṭa hañā gītā paḍe ānandita-mane  
absorbed  being  the Gītā  he reads  with a joyful  mind

(95) Some laughed and some censured him, but he paid no heed. Absorbed, he read the Gītā with a joyful heart.

পুলকাশ্রু, কম্প, স্বেদ, — যাবৎ পঠন ৷  
দেখি’ আনন্দিত হৈল মহাপ্রভুর মন ॥ ৯৬ ॥

pulakāśru, kampa, sveda, — yāvat paṭhana  
hair standing on end  tears  trembling  sweat  as long as  the reading  
dekhi’ ānandita haila mahāprabhura mana  
seeing  joyful  became  of Mahāprabhu  the mind

(96) As long as he read, his hair stood on end, and he shed tears, trembled and perspired. Seeing this, Mahāprabhu was delighted.

মহাপ্রভু পুছিল তাঁরে, শুন মহাশয় ৷  
কোন্ অর্থ জানি’ তোমার এত সুখ হয় ॥ ৯৭ ॥

mahāprabhu puchila tām̐re, śuna mahāśaya  
Mahāprabhu  asked  him  listen  good sir  
kon artha jāni’ tomāra eta sukha haya  
what  meaning  knowing  to you  such  happiness  comes

(97) Mahāprabhu asked him, “Listen, good sir: what meaning do you understand that gives you such happiness?”

বিপ্র কহে, — মূর্খ আমি, শব্দার্থ না জানি ৷  
শুদ্ধাশুদ্ধ গীতা পড়ি, গুরু-আজ্ঞা মানি’ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

vipra kahe, — mūrkha āmi, śabdārtha nā jāni  
the brāhmaṇa  says  a fool  I  the meaning of the words  not  I know  
śuddhāśuddha gītā paḍi, guru-ājñā māni’  
correctly or incorrectly  the Gītā  I read  of my guru  the order  obeying

(98) The brāhmaṇa said, “I am a fool and do not know the meaning of the words. Correctly or incorrectly, I read the Gītā in obedience to my guru’s order.

অর্জ্জুনের রথে কৃষ্ণ হয় রজ্জুধর ৷  
বসিয়াছেন তাতে, — যেন শ্যামল সুন্দর ॥ ৯৯ ॥

arjunera rathe kṛṣṇa haya rajjudhara  
of Arjuna  on the chariot  Kṛṣṇa  is  the holder of the reins  
vasiyāchena tāte, — yena śyāmala sundara  
He is seated  on it  like  a dark  beautiful one

(99) On Arjuna’s chariot Kṛṣṇa holds the reins. He is seated there, a dark and beautiful form.

অর্জ্জুনেরে কহিলেন হিত-উপদেশ ৷  
তাঁরে দেখি’ হয় মোর আনন্দ-আবেশ ॥ ১০০ ॥

arjunere kahilena hita-upadeśa  
to Arjuna  He spoke  beneficial  instructions  
tām̐re dekhi’ haya mora ānanda-āveśa  
Him  seeing  comes  my  by bliss  being overwhelmed

(100) He spoke beneficial instructions to Arjuna. Seeing Him, I am overwhelmed with bliss.

যাবৎ পড়োঁ, তাবৎ পাঙ তাঁর দরশন ৷  
এই লাগি’ গীতা-পাঠ না ছাড়ে মোর মন ॥ ১০১ ॥

yāvat paḍom̐, tāvat pāṅa tām̐ra daraśana  
as long as  I read  so long  I get  His  sight  
ei lāgi’ gītā-pāṭha nā chāḍe mora mana  
for this  reason  the reading of the Gītā  not  gives up  my  mind

(101) As long as I read, I see Him. That is why my mind will not give up reading the Gītā.”

প্রভু কহে, — গীতা-পাঠে তোমারই অধিকার ৷  
তুমি সে জানহ এই গীতার অর্থ-সার ॥ ১০২ ॥

prabhu kahe, — gītā-pāṭhe tomārai adhikāra  
the Lord  says  for reading the Gītā  yours alone  the qualification  
tumi se jānaha ei gītāra artha-sāra  
you  indeed  know  of this  Gītā  of the meaning  the essence

(102) The Lord said, “You alone are truly qualified to read the Gītā; you know the essence of its meaning.”

এত বলি’ সেই বিপ্রে কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷  
প্রভু-পদ ধরি’ বিপ্র করেন রোদন ॥ ১০৩ ॥

eta bali’ sei vipre kaila āliṅgana  
so  saying  that  brāhmaṇa  He made  embrace  
prabhu-pada dhari’ vipra karena rodana  
of the Lord  the feet  clasping  the brāhmaṇa  makes  weeping

(103) Saying this, the Lord embraced the brāhmaṇa, and the brāhmaṇa clasped the Lord’s feet and wept.

তোমা দেখি’ তাহা হৈতে দ্বিগুণ সুখ হয় ৷  
সেই কৃষ্ণ তুমি, — হেন মোর মনে লয় ॥ ১০৪ ॥

tomā dekhi’ tāhā haite dvi-guṇa sukha haya  
You  seeing  than that  than  double  happiness  comes  
sei kṛṣṇa tumi, — hena mora mane laya  
that  Kṛṣṇa  You  so  to my  mind  it seems

(104) “Seeing You, my happiness is twice as great. I feel that You are that very Kṛṣṇa.”

কৃষ্ণস্ফূর্ত্ত্যে তাঁর মন হঞাছে নির্ম্মল ৷  
অতএব প্রভুর তত্ত্ব জানিল সকল ॥ ১০৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-sphūrtye tām̐ra mana hañāche nirmala  
by the manifestation of Kṛṣṇa  his  mind  had become  pure  
ataeva prabhura tattva jānila sakala  
therefore  of the Lord  the truth  he knew  all

(105) His mind had become pure through Kṛṣṇa’s manifestation in his heart; therefore he understood the whole truth about the Lord.

তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে করাইল শিক্ষণ ৷  
এই বাত্ কাঁহা না করিহ প্রকাশন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu tām̐re karāila śikṣaṇa  
then  Mahāprabhu  to him  gave  instruction  
ei bāt kām̐hā nā kariha prakāśana  
this  word  anywhere  not  do  disclosure

(106) Then Mahāprabhu instructed him: “Do not reveal this matter to anyone.”

সেই বিপ্র মহাপ্রভুর বড় ভক্ত হৈল ৷  
চারি মাস প্রভু-সঙ্গ কভু না ছাড়িল ॥ ১০৭ ॥

sei vipra mahāprabhura baḍa bhakta haila  
that  brāhmaṇa  of Mahāprabhu  a great  devotee  became  
cāri māsa prabhu-saṅga kabhu nā chāḍila  
four  months  the Lord’s company  ever  not  he left

(107) That brāhmaṇa became a great devotee of Mahāprabhu, and for four months he never left the Lord’s company.

এইমত ভট্টগৃহে রহে গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
নিরন্তর ভট্ট-সঙ্গে কৃষ্ণকথানন্দ ॥ ১০৮ ॥

ei-mata bhaṭṭa-gṛhe rahe gauracandra  
in this way  in the Bhaṭṭa’s house  lives  Gauracandra  
nirantara bhaṭṭa-saṅge kṛṣṇa-kathānanda  
constantly  with the Bhaṭṭa  bliss of talks about Kṛṣṇa

(108) In this way Gauracandra lived in the Bhaṭṭa’s house, constantly enjoying the bliss of talks about Kṛṣṇa with him.

‘শ্রী-বৈষ্ণব’-ভট্ট সেবে লক্ষ্মী নারায়ণ ৷  
তাঁর ভক্তি দেখি’ প্রভুর তুষ্ট হৈল মন ॥ ১০৯ ॥

‘śrī-vaiṣṇava’ bhaṭṭa seve lakṣmī nārāyaṇa  
a Śrī Vaiṣṇava  the Bhaṭṭa  serves  Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa  
tām̐ra bhakti dekhi’ prabhura tuṣṭa haila mana  
his  devotion  seeing  of the Lord  pleased  became  the mind

(109) Being a Śrī Vaiṣṇava, the Bhaṭṭa served Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa. Seeing his devotion, the Lord was pleased at heart.

নিরন্তর তাঁর সঙ্গে হৈল সখ্যভাব ৷  
হাস্য-পরিহাসে দুঁহে সখ্যের স্বভাব ॥ ১১০ ॥

nirantara tām̐ra saṅge haila sakhya-bhāva  
constantly  with him  arose  a friendly feeling  
hāsya-parihāse dum̐he sakhyera svabhāva  
in jokes and teasing  of the two  of friendship  the nature

(110) Being constantly together, they came to feel like friends, and, as friends do, the two joked and teased each other.

প্রভু কহে, — ভট্ট, তোমার লক্ষ্মী-ঠাকুরাণী ৷  
কান্ত-বক্ষঃস্থিতা, পতিব্রতা শিরোমণি ॥ ১১১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — bhaṭṭa, tomāra lakṣmī-ṭhākurāṇī  
the Lord  says  Bhaṭṭa  your  Lakṣmī the goddess  
kānta-vakṣaḥ-sthitā, pativratā śiromaṇi  
dwelling on her husband’s chest  of chaste women  the crown jewel

(111) The Lord said, “Bhaṭṭa, your goddess Lakṣmī dwells on the chest of her husband; she is the crown jewel of chaste women.

আমার ঠাকুর কৃষ্ণ — গোপ, গো-চারক ৷  
সাধ্বী হঞা কেনে চাহে তাঁহার সঙ্গম ॥ ১১২ ॥

āmāra ṭhākura kṛṣṇa — gopa, go-cāraka  
My  Lord  Kṛṣṇa  a cowherd  tending cows  
sādhvī hañā kene cāhe tām̐hāra saṅgama  
chaste  being  why  desires  His  company

(112) But My Lord Kṛṣṇa is a cowherd who tends cows. Why should a chaste lady desire His company?

এই লাগি’ সুখভোগ ছাড়ি’ চিরকাল ৷  
ব্রত-নিয়ম করি’ তপ করিল অপার ॥ ১১৩ ॥

ei lāgi’ sukha-bhoga chāḍi’ cira-kāla  
for this  reason  enjoyment of happiness  giving up  for a long time  
vrata-niyama kari’ tapa karila apāra  
vows and rules  observing  austerities  she performed  boundless

(113) For this she gave up the enjoyment of happiness and for a long time kept vows and rules and performed boundless austerities.

কস্যানুভাবোঽস্য ন দেব বিদ্মহে  
তবাঙ্ঘ্রিরেণুস্পর্শাধিকারঃ ৷  
যদ্বাঞ্ছয়া শ্রীর্ললনাচরত্তপো  
বিহায় কামান্ সুচিরং ধৃতব্রতা ॥ ১১৪ ॥

kasyānubhāvo ’sya na deva vidmahe  
of what  merit  of this one  not  O Lord  we know  
tavāṅghri-reṇu-sparśādhikāraḥ  
of Your feet  of the dust  of the touch  the right  
yad-vāñchayā śrīr lalanācarat tapo  
desiring which  Śrī  the best of women  performed  austerity  
vihāya kāmān su-ciraṁ dhṛta-vratā  
giving up  desires  for a very long time  holding vows

(114) “O Lord, we do not know by what merit this one has gained the right to touch the dust of Your feet. Desiring it, Śrī, the best of women, gave up all desires, kept vows and performed austerities for a very long time.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.16.36)

ভট্ট কহে, কৃষ্ণ-নারায়ণ — একই স্বরূপ ৷  
কৃষ্ণেতে অধিক লীলা-বৈদগ্ধ্যাদিরূপ ॥ ১১৫ ॥

bhaṭṭa kahe, kṛṣṇa-nārāyaṇa — ekai svarūpa  
the Bhaṭṭa  says  Kṛṣṇa and Nārāyaṇa  one and the same  form  
kṛṣṇete adhika līlā-vaidagdhyādi-rūpa  
in Kṛṣṇa  more  of pastimes  of cleverness and the rest  features

(115) The Bhaṭṭa said, “Kṛṣṇa and Nārāyaṇa are one and the same form, but in Kṛṣṇa there is more playfulness, more artfulness in His pastimes, and so on.

তাঁর স্পর্শে নাহি যায় পতিব্রতা-ধর্ম্ম ৷  
কৌতুকে লক্ষ্মী চাহেন কৃষ্ণের সঙ্গম ॥ ১১৬ ॥

tām̐ra sparśe nāhi yāya pativratā-dharma  
His  by the touch  not  is lost  of chastity  the duty  
kautuke lakṣmī cāhena kṛṣṇera saṅgama  
out of playful curiosity  Lakṣmī  desires  Kṛṣṇa’s  company

(116) His touch does not violate her chastity. Lakṣmī desires Kṛṣṇa’s company out of playful curiosity.

সিদ্ধান্ততস্ত্বভেদেঽপি শ্রীশ-কৃষ্ণস্বরূপয়োঃ ৷  
রসেনোৎকৃষ্যতে কৃষ্ণরূপমেষা রসস্থিতিঃ ॥ ১১৭ ॥

siddhāntatas tv abhede ’pi  
by siddhānta  indeed  in non-difference  although  
śrīśa-kṛṣṇa-svarūpayoḥ  
of the Lord of Śrī  of Kṛṣṇa  of the forms  
rasenotkṛṣyate kṛṣṇa-  
by rasa  excels  of Kṛṣṇa  
rūpam eṣā rasa-sthitiḥ  
the form  such  of rasa  the nature

(117) “By siddhānta there is no difference between the forms of the Lord of Śrī and of Kṛṣṇa. Yet in rasa the form of Kṛṣṇa excels: such is the nature of rasa.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.59)

কৃষ্ণসঙ্গে পতিব্রতা-ধর্ম্ম নহে নাশ ৷  
অধিক লাভ পাইয়ে, আর রাস-বিলাস ॥ ১১৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa-saṅge pativratā-dharma nahe nāśa  
in Kṛṣṇa’s company  the duty of chastity  is not  destroyed  
adhika lābha pāiye, āra rāsa-vilāsa  
greater  gain  she obtains  moreover  enjoyment of the rāsa dance

(118) In Kṛṣṇa’s company the duty of chastity is not destroyed; rather she gains more — the enjoyment of the rāsa dance.

বিনোদিনী লক্ষ্মীর হয় কৃষ্ণে অভিলাষ ৷  
ইহাতে কি দোষ, কেনে কর পরিহাস ॥ ১১৯ ॥

vinodinī lakṣmīra haya kṛṣṇe abhilāṣa  
pleasure-loving  of Lakṣmī  there is  for Kṛṣṇa  desire  
ihāte ki doṣa, kene kara parihāsa  
in this  what  fault  why  You make  mockery

(119) If the playful Lakṣmī longs for Kṛṣṇa, what fault is there in that? Why do You laugh at it?”

প্রভু কহে, — দোষ নাহি, ইহা আমি জানি ৷  
রাস না পাইল লক্ষ্মী, শাস্ত্রে ইহা শুনি ॥ ১২০ ॥

prabhu kahe, — doṣa nāhi, ihā āmi jāni  
the Lord  says  fault  there is none  this  I  know  
rāsa nā pāila lakṣmī, śāstre ihā śuni  
the rāsa  not  obtained  Lakṣmī  in the scriptures  this  I hear

(120) The Lord said, “I know there is no fault in it. But I hear from the scriptures that Lakṣmī did not attain the rāsa dance.

নায়ং শ্রিয়োঽঙ্গ উ নিতান্তরতেঃ প্রসাদঃ  
স্বর্যোষিতাং নলিনগন্ধরুচাং কুতোঽন্যাঃ ৷  
রাসোৎসবেঽস্য ভুজদণ্ডগৃহীতকণ্ঠ-  
লব্ধাশিষাং য উদগাদ্ব্রজসুন্দরীণাম্ ॥ ১২১ ॥

nāyaṁ śriyo ’ṅga u nitānta-rateḥ prasādaḥ  
not  this  of Śrī  O  indeed  wholly attached  favor  
svar-yoṣitāṁ nalina-gandha-rucāṁ kuto ’nyāḥ  
of the celestial women  of lotuses  the fragrance  having the luster  how then  others  
rāsotsave ’sya bhuja-daṇḍa-gṛhīta-kaṇṭha-  
at the festival of rāsa  His  by the arms  embraced  around the neck  
labdhāśiṣāṁ ya udagād vraja-sundarīṇām  
who obtained their desire  which  arose  of Vraja  the beautiful women

(121) “At the festival of the rāsa dance He embraced the beautiful women of Vraja around their necks with His arms, and they attained their heart’s desire. Such favor was not granted even to Śrī, who is wholly devoted to Him, nor to the celestial women, fragrant and radiant like lotuses — what then of others?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.47.60)

লক্ষ্মী কেনে না পাইল, ইহার কি কারণ ৷  
তপ করি’ কৈছে কৃষ্ণ পাইল শ্রুতিগণ ॥ ১২২ ॥

lakṣmī kene nā pāila, ihāra ki kāraṇa  
Lakṣmī  why  not  obtained  of this  what  the reason  
tapa kari’ kaiche kṛṣṇa pāila śruti-gaṇa  
austerities  performing  how  Kṛṣṇa  attained  the Śrutis

(122) Why did Lakṣmī not attain it? What is the reason? And how did the Śrutis attain Kṛṣṇa by performing austerities?

নিভৃতমরুন্মনোঽক্ষদৃঢ়যোগযুজো হৃদি য-  
ন্মুনয় উপাসতে তদরয়োঽপি যযুঃ স্মরণাৎ ৷  
স্ত্রিয় উরগেন্দ্র-ভোগভুজদণ্ডবিষক্ত-ধিয়ো  
বয়মপি তে সমাঃ সমদৃশোঽঙ্ঘ্রিসরোজসুধাঃ ॥ ১২৩ ॥

nibhṛta-marun-mano-’kṣa-dṛḍha-yoga-yujo hṛdi yan-  
who have restrained  breath  mind  senses  in firm  yoga  engaged  in the heart  which  
munaya upāsate tad arayo ’pi yayuḥ smaraṇāt  
the sages  worship  that  the enemies  even  attained  by remembering  
striya uragendra-bhoga-bhuja-daṇḍa-viṣakta-dhiyo  
the women  of the king of serpents  like the coils  to the arms  attached  in mind  
vayam api te samāḥ samadṛśo ’ṅghri-saroja-sudhāḥ  
we  also  of You  equal  who see all equally  of the lotus feet  the nectar

(123) “That which the sages worship in the heart, having restrained breath, mind and senses in firm yoga, Your enemies attained simply by remembering You. The women whose minds were fixed on Your arms, like the coils of the king of serpents, attained the nectar of Your lotus feet; and we, the Śrutis, are equal to them, for You see all equally.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.23)

শ্রুতি পায়, লক্ষ্মী না পায়, ইথে কি কারণ ৷  
ভট্ট কহে, — ইহা প্রবেশিতে নারে মোর মন ॥ ১২৪ ॥

śruti pāya, lakṣmī nā pāya, ithe ki kāraṇa  
the Śrutis  obtain  Lakṣmī  not  obtains  in this  what  the reason  
bhaṭṭa kahe, — ihā praveśite nāre mora mana  
the Bhaṭṭa  says  into this  to enter  cannot  my  mind

(124) The Śrutis attained it, but Lakṣmī did not — what is the reason?” The Bhaṭṭa said, “My mind cannot enter into this.

আমি জীব, — ক্ষুদ্রবুদ্ধি, সহজে অস্থির ৷  
ঈশ্বরের লীলা — কোটিসমুদ্র-গম্ভীর ॥ ১২৫ ॥

āmi jīva, — kṣudra-buddhi, sahaje asthira  
I  a jīva  of small intelligence  by nature  unsteady  
īśvarera līlā — koṭi-samudra-gambhīra  
of the Lord  the pastimes  of ten million oceans  the depth

(125) I am a jīva of small intelligence, unsteady by nature, while the Lord’s pastimes are as deep as ten million oceans.

তুমি সাক্ষাৎ সেই কৃষ্ণ, জান নিজকর্ম্ম ৷  
যারে জানাহ, সেই জানে তোমার লীলামর্ম্ম ॥ ১২৬ ॥

tumi sākṣāt sei kṛṣṇa, jāna nija-karma  
You  directly  that  Kṛṣṇa  You know  Your own deeds  
yāre jānāha, sei jāne tomāra līlā-marma  
to whom  You reveal  he  knows  of Your  pastimes  the secret

(126) You are that very Kṛṣṇa, and You know Your own deeds. Only one to whom You reveal it knows the secret of Your pastimes.”

প্রভু কহে, — কৃষ্ণের এক সজীব লক্ষণ ৷  
স্বমাধুর্য্যে সর্ব্ব চিত্ত করে আকর্ষণ ॥ ১২৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — kṛṣṇera eka sajīva lakṣaṇa  
the Lord  says  of Kṛṣṇa  one  living  characteristic  
sva-mādhurye sarva citta kare ākarṣaṇa  
by His own sweetness  of all  the hearts  He does  attraction

(127) The Lord said, “Kṛṣṇa has one living characteristic, ever active: by His own sweetness He attracts every heart.

ব্রজলোকের ভাবে পাইয়ে তাঁহার চরণ ৷  
তাঁরে ঈশ্বর করি’ নাহি জানে ব্রজজন ॥ ১২৮ ॥

vraja-lokera bhāve pāiye tām̐hāra caraṇa  
of the people of Vraja  by the feeling  one attains  His  feet  
tām̐re īśvara kari’ nāhi jāne vraja-jana  
Him  as the Lord  considering  not  know  the people of Vraja

(128) His feet are attained by following the feelings of the people of Vraja, and the people of Vraja do not know Him as the Lord.

কেহ তাঁরে পুত্র-জ্ঞানে উদুখলে বান্ধে ৷  
কেহ সখা-জ্ঞানে জিনি’ চড়ে তাঁর কান্ধে ॥ ১২৯ ॥

keha tām̐re putra-jñāne udukhale bāndhe  
someone  Him  as a son  regarding  to a mortar  binds  
keha sakhā-jñāne jini’ caḍe tām̐ra kāndhe  
someone  as a friend  regarding  defeating  climbs  on His  shoulders

(129) One, regarding Him as her son, ties Him to a mortar; another, regarding Him as a friend, defeats Him and climbs onto His shoulders.

‘ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন’ বলি’ তাঁরে জানে ব্রজজন ৷  
ঐশ্বর্য্যজ্ঞানে নাহি কোন সম্বন্ধ-মানন ॥ ১৩০ ॥

‘vrajendra-nandana’ bali’ tām̐re jāne vraja-jana  
as the son of Vraja’s king  calling  Him  know  the people of Vraja  
aiśvarya-jñāne nāhi kona sambandha-mānana  
in awareness of majesty  no  any  relationship  acknowledgment

(130) The people of Vraja know Him as the son of the king of Vraja; they acknowledge no relationship with Him based on awareness of His majesty.

ব্রজলোকের ভাবে যেই করয়ে ভজন ৷  
সেই ব্রজে পায় শুদ্ধ ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ১৩১ ॥

vraja-lokera bhāve yei karaye bhajana  
of the people of Vraja  in the mood  whoever  performs  worship  
sei vraje pāya śuddha vrajendra-nandana  
he  in Vraja  attains  pure  the son of Vraja’s king

(131) Whoever worships in the mood of the people of Vraja attains in Vraja the son of the king of Vraja in His pure form.

নায়ং সুখাপো ভগবান্ দেহিনাং গোপিকাসুতঃ ৷  
জ্ঞানিনাং চাত্মভূতানাং যথা ভক্তিমতামিহ ॥ ১৩২ ॥

nāyaṁ sukhāpo bhagavān  
not  this  easily attained  Bhagavān  
dehināṁ gopikā-sutaḥ  
for the embodied  of the gopī  the son  
jñānināṁ cātma-bhūtānāṁ  
for the jñānīs  and  for those who realized the self  
yathā bhakti-matām iha  
as  for those who have devotion  here

(132) “This Bhagavān, the son of the gopī, is not so easily attained by the embodied, nor by jñānīs who have realized the self, as He is by those who have devotion.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.9.21)

শ্রুতিগণ গোপীগণের অনুগত হঞা ৷  
ব্রজেশ্বরীসুত ভজে গোপীভাব লঞা ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

śruti-gaṇa gopī-gaṇera anugata hañā  
the Śrutis  of the gopīs  followers  becoming  
vrajeśvarī-suta bhaje gopī-bhāva lañā  
the son of Vraja’s queen  they worship  the mood of the gopīs  taking

(133) The Śrutis became followers of the gopīs and worshiped the son of the queen of Vraja, taking on the mood of the gopīs.

বাহ্যান্তরে গোপীদেহ ব্রজে যবে পাইল ৷  
সেই দেহে কৃষ্ণসঙ্গে রাসক্রীড়া কৈল ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

bāhyāntare gopī-deha vraje yabe pāila  
outwardly and inwardly  gopīs’ bodies  in Vraja  when  they obtained  
sei dehe kṛṣṇa-saṅge rāsa-krīḍā kaila  
in those  bodies  with Kṛṣṇa  the rāsa play  they performed

(134) When in Vraja they obtained the bodies of gopīs, outwardly and inwardly, in those bodies they enjoyed the rāsa dance with Kṛṣṇa.

গোপজাতি কৃষ্ণ, গোপী — প্রেয়সী তাঁহার ৷  
দেবী বা অন্য স্ত্রী কৃষ্ণ না করে অঙ্গীকার ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

gopa-jāti kṛṣṇa, gopī — preyasī tām̐hāra  
of the cowherd caste  Kṛṣṇa  the gopīs  the beloveds  His  
devī vā anya strī kṛṣṇa nā kare aṅgīkāra  
a goddess  or  other  woman  Kṛṣṇa  not  does  acceptance

(135) Kṛṣṇa belongs to the cowherd folk, and the gopīs are His beloveds. Kṛṣṇa does not accept a goddess or any other woman.

লক্ষ্মী চাহে সেই দেহে কৃষ্ণের সঙ্গম ৷  
গোপিকা-অনুগা হঞা না কৈল ভজন ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

lakṣmī cāhe sei dehe kṛṣṇera saṅgama  
Lakṣmī  desires  in that  body  of Kṛṣṇa  the company  
gopikā-anugā hañā nā kaila bhajana  
of the gopīs  a follower  becoming  not  did  worship

(136) Lakṣmī desires Kṛṣṇa’s company in that same body of hers; she did not worship Him as a follower of the gopīs.

অন্য দেহে না পাইয়ে রাসবিলাস ৷  
অতএব ‘নায়ং’ শ্লোক কহে বেদব্যাস ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

anya dehe nā pāiye rāsa-vilāsa  
in another  body  not  one attains  the rāsa play  
ataeva ‘nāyaṁ’ śloka kahe veda-vyāsa  
therefore  “nāyaṁ”  the verse  speaks  Vedavyāsa

(137) In any other body one does not attain the rāsa play. That is why Vedavyāsa spoke the verse beginning “nāyaṁ.”

পূর্ব্বে ভট্টের মনে এক ছিল অভিমান ৷  
‘শ্রীনারায়ণ’ হয়েন স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

pūrve bhaṭṭera mane eka chila abhimāna  
before  of the Bhaṭṭa  in the mind  one  there was  pride (conviction)  
‘śrī-nārāyaṇa’ hayena svayaṁ bhagavān  
Śrī Nārāyaṇa  is  Himself  Bhagavān

(138) Before this, there was a proud conviction in the Bhaṭṭa’s mind that Śrī Nārāyaṇa is Svayam Bhagavān,

তাঁহার ভজন সর্ব্বোপরি-কক্ষা হয় ৷  
শ্রী-বৈষ্ণবে’র ভজন এই সর্ব্বোপরি হয় ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

tām̐hāra bhajana sarvopari-kakṣā haya  
to Him  worship  above all  in rank  is  
śrī-vaiṣṇave’ra bhajana ei sarvopari haya  
of the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas  worship  this  above all  is

(139) and that worship of Him is of the highest order, so the worship of the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas is the highest of all.

এই তাঁর গর্ব্ব প্রভু করিতে খণ্ডন ৷  
পরিহাসদ্বারে উঠায় এতেক বচন ॥ ১৪০ ॥

ei tām̐ra garva prabhu karite khaṇḍana  
this  his  pride  the Lord  in order  to break  
parihāsa-dvāre uṭhāya eteka vacana  
by way of a joke  raises  such  words

(140) To break this pride of his, the Lord spoke all these words in jest.

প্রভু কহে, — ভট্ট, তুমি না করিহ সংশয় ৷  
‘স্বয়ং ভগবান্’ কৃষ্ণ এই ত’ নিশ্চয় ॥ ১৪১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — bhaṭṭa, tumi nā kariha saṁśaya  
the Lord  says  Bhaṭṭa  you  not  have  doubt  
‘svayaṁ bhagavān’ kṛṣṇa ei ta’ niścaya  
Svayam Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  this  indeed  certain

(141) The Lord said, “Bhaṭṭa, do not doubt it: it is certain that Kṛṣṇa is Svayam Bhagavān.

কৃষ্ণের বিলাস মূর্ত্তি — শ্রীনারায়ণ ৷  
অতএব লক্ষ্মী-আদ্যের হরে তেঁহ মন ॥ ১৪২ ॥

kṛṣṇera vilāsa-mūrti — śrī-nārāyaṇa  
of Kṛṣṇa  the form for pastimes  Śrī Nārāyaṇa  
ataeva lakṣmī-ādyera hare tem̐ha mana  
therefore  of Lakṣmī and others  captivates  He  the minds

(142) Śrī Nārāyaṇa is Kṛṣṇa’s form for pastimes (vilāsa-mūrti); that is why He captivates the minds of Lakṣmī and others.

এতে চাংশকলাঃ পুংসঃ কৃষ্ণস্তু ভগবান্ স্বয়ম্ ৷  
ইন্দ্রারিব্যাকুলং লোকং মৃড়য়ন্তি যুগে যুগে ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

ete cāṁśa-kalāḥ puṁsaḥ  
these  and  portions  and parts  of the Puruṣa  
kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam  
Kṛṣṇa  but  Bhagavān  Himself  
indrāri-vyākulaṁ lokaṁ  
by the enemies of Indra  troubled  the world  
mṛḍayanti yuge yuge  
make happy  in age  after age

(143) “All these (avatāras) are portions (*aṁśa*) and parts of portions (*kalā*) of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself. Age after age They bring happiness to the world when it is troubled by the enemies of Indra.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.28)

নারায়ণ হৈতে কৃষ্ণের অসাধারণ গুণ ৷  
অতএব লক্ষ্মীর কৃষ্ণে তৃষ্ণা অনুক্ষণ ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

nārāyaṇa haite kṛṣṇera asādhāraṇa guṇa  
of Nārāyaṇa  than  in Kṛṣṇa  extraordinary  qualities  
ataeva lakṣmīra kṛṣṇe tṛṣṇā anukṣaṇa  
therefore  of Lakṣmī  for Kṛṣṇa  thirst  constant

(144) Kṛṣṇa has extraordinary qualities beyond Nārāyaṇa’s; that is why Lakṣmī constantly thirsts for Kṛṣṇa.

তুমি যে পড়িলা শ্লোক, সে হয় প্রমাণ ৷  
সেই শ্লোকে আইসে ‘কৃষ্ণ — স্বয়ং ভগবান্’ ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

tumi ye paḍilā śloka, se haya pramāṇa  
you  which  recited  the verse  it  is  the proof  
sei śloke āise ‘kṛṣṇa — svayaṁ bhagavān’  
in that  verse  it comes out  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān

(145) The verse you recited is itself the proof: that verse shows that Kṛṣṇa is Svayam Bhagavān.

সিদ্ধান্ততস্ত্বভেদেঽপি শ্রীশ-কৃষ্ণস্বরূপয়োঃ ৷  
রসেনোৎকৃষ্যতে কৃষ্ণরূপমেষা রসস্থিতিঃ ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

siddhāntatas tv abhede ’pi  
by siddhānta  indeed  in non-difference  although  
śrīśa-kṛṣṇa-svarūpayoḥ  
of the Lord of Śrī  of Kṛṣṇa  of the forms  
rasenotkṛṣyate kṛṣṇa-  
by rasa  excels  of Kṛṣṇa  
rūpam eṣā rasa-sthitiḥ  
the form  such  of rasa  the nature

(146) “By siddhānta there is no difference between the forms of the Lord of Śrī and of Kṛṣṇa. Yet in rasa the form of Kṛṣṇa excels: such is the nature of rasa.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.59)

‘স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ’ হরে লক্ষ্মীর মন ৷  
গোপীকার মন হরিতে নারে ‘নারায়ণ’ ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

‘svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa’ hare lakṣmīra mana  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  captivates  Lakṣmī’s  mind  
gopikāra mana harite nāre ‘nārāyaṇa’  
of the gopīs  the mind  to captivate  is not able  Nārāyaṇa

(147) Kṛṣṇa, Svayam Bhagavān, captivates the mind of Lakṣmī, but Nārāyaṇa cannot captivate the minds of the gopīs.

নারায়ণের কা কথা, শ্রীকৃষ্ণ আপনে ৷  
গোপিকারে হাস্য করাইতে হয় ‘নারায়ণে’ ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

nārāyaṇera kā kathā, śrī-kṛṣṇa āpane  
of Nārāyaṇa  what  to speak  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  Himself  
gopikāre hāsya karāite haya ‘nārāyaṇe’  
the gopīs  laugh  to make  becomes  Nārāyaṇa

(148) What to speak of Nārāyaṇa? Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself becomes Nārāyaṇa to make the gopīs laugh.

‘চতুর্ভুজ-মূর্ত্তি’ দেখায় গোপীগণের আগে ৷  
সেই ‘কৃষ্ণে’ গোপিকার নহে অনুরাগে ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

‘catur-bhuja-mūrti’ dekhāya gopī-gaṇera āge  
a four-armed form  He shows  of the gopīs  before  
sei ‘kṛṣṇe’ gopikāra nahe anurāge  
for that  Kṛṣṇa  of the gopīs  there is no  loving attachment

(149) He shows a four-armed form before the gopīs, but the gopīs feel no loving attachment for that “Kṛṣṇa.”

গোপীনাং পশুপেন্দ্রনন্দনজুষো ভাবস্য কস্তাং কৃতী  
বিজ্ঞাতুং ক্ষমতে দুরূহপদবীসঞ্চারিণঃ প্রক্রিয়াম্ ৷  
আবিষ্কুর্ব্বতি বৈষ্ণবীমপি তনুং তস্মিন্ ভুজৈর্জিষ্ণুভি-  
র্যাসাং হন্ত চতুর্ভিরদ্ভুতরুচিং রাগোদয়ঃ কুঞ্চতি ॥ ১৫০ ॥

gopīnāṁ paśupendra-nandana-juṣo bhāvasya kas tāṁ kṛtī  
of the gopīs  serving the son of the king of herdsmen  of the love  what  that  wise man  
vijñātuṁ kṣamate durūha-padavī-sañcāriṇaḥ prakriyām  
to understand  is able  moving on an unfathomable path  the working  
āviṣkurvati vaiṣṇavīm api tanuṁ tasmin bhujair jiṣṇubhir  
when He reveals  of Viṣṇu  even  a form  in Him  with arms  victorious  
yāsāṁ hanta caturbhir adbhuta-ruciṁ rāgodayaḥ kuñcati  
whose  alas  four  of wondrous splendour  the rise of love  shrinks

(150) “What wise man can fathom the ways of the gopīs’ love for the son of the cowherd king — a love that moves on a path beyond comprehension? For even when He reveals a form of Viṣṇu, wondrous in its splendour, with four victorious arms, their love, alas, shrinks back.” (Lalita-mādhava, 6.14)

এত কহি’ প্রভু তাঁর গর্ব্ব চূর্ণ করিয়া ৷  
তাঁরে সুখ দিতে কহে সিদ্ধান্ত ফিরাইয়া ॥ ১৫১ ॥

eta kahi’ prabhu tām̐ra garva cūrṇa kariyā  
so  saying  the Lord  his  pride  to dust  reducing  
tām̐re sukha dite kahe siddhānta phirāiyā  
to him  happiness  to give  speaks  the conclusion  turning around

(151) Speaking thus, the Lord crushed his pride. Then, to make him happy, He turned the argument around and said:

দুঃখ না ভাবিহ, ভট্ট, কৈলুঁ পরিহাস ৷  
শাস্ত্রসিদ্ধান্ত শুন, যাতে বৈষ্ণব-বিশ্বাস ॥ ১৫২ ॥

duḥkha nā bhāviha, bhaṭṭa, kailum̐ parihāsa  
sorrow  not  take to heart  Bhaṭṭa  I made  a joke  
śāstra-siddhānta śuna, yāte vaiṣṇava-viśvāsa  
the conclusion of the scriptures  hear  on which  the faith of the Vaiṣṇavas

(152) “Do not be sad, Bhaṭṭa; I was only joking. Now hear the conclusion of the scriptures on which the Vaiṣṇavas’ faith rests.

কৃষ্ণ-নারায়ণ, যৈছে একই স্বরূপ ৷  
গোপী-লক্ষ্মী-ভেদ নাহি হয় একরূপ ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nārāyaṇa, yaiche ekai svarūpa  
Kṛṣṇa and Nārāyaṇa  just as  one and the same  form  
gopī-lakṣmī-bheda nāhi haya eka-rūpa  
between the gopīs and Lakṣmī  difference  there is no  they are  of one form

(153) Just as Kṛṣṇa and Nārāyaṇa are one and the same form, so there is no difference between the gopīs and Lakṣmī: they are one in form.

একই বিগ্রহে করে নানাকার রূপ ৷  
গোপী-লক্ষ্মী-ভেদ নাহি, জানিহ ‘স্বরূপ’ ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

ekai vigrahe kare nānākāra rūpa  
in one and the same  form  He assumes  various  appearances  
gopī-lakṣmī-bheda nāhi, jāniha ‘svarūpa’  
between the gopīs and Lakṣmī  difference  there is no  know  the essential nature

(154) One and the same form assumes various appearances. Know the truth: there is no difference between the gopīs and Lakṣmī.

গোপীদ্বারে লক্ষ্মী করে কৃষ্ণসঙ্গাস্বাদ ৷  
ঈশ্বরত্বে ভেদ মানিলে হয় অপরাধ ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

gopī-dvāre lakṣmī kare kṛṣṇa-saṅgāsvāda  
through the gopīs  Lakṣmī  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s company  tasting  
īśvaratve bheda mānile haya aparādha  
in Godhood  difference  if one admits  there is  an offense

(155) Through the gopīs, Lakṣmī tastes the joy of Kṛṣṇa’s company. To see any difference in Their Godhood is an offense.

এক ঈশ্বর — ভক্তের ধ্যান-অনুরূপ ৷  
একই বিগ্রহে করে নানাকার রূপ ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

eka īśvara — bhaktera dhyāna-anurūpa  
one  Lord  of the devotee  to the meditation  according  
ekai vigrahe kare nānākāra rūpa  
in one and the same  form  He assumes  various  appearances

(156) The Lord is one; according to each devotee’s meditation, one and the same form assumes various appearances.

মণির্যথা বিভাগেন নীলপীতাদিভির্যুতঃ ৷  
রূপভেদমবাপ্নোতি ধ্যানভেদাত্তথাচ্যুতঃ ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

maṇir yathā vibhāgena  
a gem  as  by the difference (of setting)  
nīla-pītādibhir yutaḥ  
with blue  yellow and so on  joined  
rūpa-bhedam avāpnoti  
difference of form  attains  
dhyāna-bhedāt tathācyutaḥ  
by difference of meditation  so  Acyuta

(157) “As a gem joined with blue, yellow and other colors appears different, so Acyuta takes different forms according to the meditation of the worshiper.” (Śrī Nārada-pañcarātra)

ভট্ট কহে, — কাঁহা আমি জীব পামর ৷  
কাঁহা তুমি সেই কৃষ্ণ, — সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

bhaṭṭa kahe, — kām̐hā āmi jīva pāmara  
the Bhaṭṭa  says  where  I  a jīva  fallen  
kām̐hā tumi sei kṛṣṇa, — sākṣāt īśvara  
where  You  that  Kṛṣṇa  directly  the Lord

(158) The Bhaṭṭa said, “How far apart we are: I, a fallen jīva, and You, that very Kṛṣṇa, the Lord Himself!

অগাধ ঈশ্বর-লীলা কিছুই না জানি ৷  
তুমি যেই কহ, সেই সত্য করি’ মানি ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

agādha īśvara-līlā kichui nā jāni  
fathomless  the Lord’s pastimes  nothing  not  I know  
tumi yei kaha, sei satya kari’ māni  
You  what  say  that  as truth  regarding  I accept

(159) I know nothing of the Lord’s fathomless pastimes. Whatever You say, I accept as the truth.

মোরে পূর্ণ কৃপা কৈল লক্ষ্মী-নারায়ণ ৷  
তাঁর কৃপায় পাইনু তোমার চরণ-দরশন ॥ ১৬০ ॥

more pūrṇa kṛpā kaila lakṣmī-nārāyaṇa  
to me  full  mercy  showed  Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa  
tām̐ra kṛpāya pāinu tomāra caraṇa-daraśana  
Their  by the mercy  I received  of Your  feet  sight

(160) Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa have shown me Their full mercy; by Their mercy I have been granted the sight of Your feet.

কৃপা করি’ কহিলে মোরে কৃষ্ণের মহিমা ৷  
যাঁর রূপ-গুণৈশ্বর্য্যের কেহ না পায় সীমা ॥ ১৬১ ॥

kṛpā kari’ kahile more kṛṣṇera mahimā  
mercy  showing  You told  me  of Kṛṣṇa  the greatness  
yām̐ra rūpa-guṇaiśvaryera keha nā pāya sīmā  
whose  beauty  qualities  majesty  no one  not  finds  limit

(161) In Your mercy You have told me of the greatness of Kṛṣṇa, whose beauty, qualities and majesty no one can measure.

এবে সে জানিনু কৃষ্ণভক্তি সর্ব্বোপরি ৷  
কৃতার্থ করিলে, মোরে কহিলে কৃপা করি’ ॥ ১৬২ ॥

ebe se jāninu kṛṣṇa-bhakti sarvopari  
now  indeed  I have learned  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  above all  
kṛtārtha karile, more kahile kṛpā kari’  
fulfilled  You made (me)  to me  You told  mercy  showing

(162) Now I have understood that devotion to Kṛṣṇa is above all. You have fulfilled my life by mercifully telling me this.”

এত বলি’ ভট্ট পড়িলা প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
কৃপা করি’ প্রভু তাঁরে কৈলা আলিঙ্গনে ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

eta bali’ bhaṭṭa paḍilā prabhura caraṇe  
so  saying  the Bhaṭṭa  fell  of the Lord  at the feet  
kṛpā kari’ prabhu tām̐re kailā āliṅgane  
mercy  showing  the Lord  him  took  in His embrace

(163) Saying this, the Bhaṭṭa fell at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord mercifully embraced him.

চাতুর্ম্মাস্য পূর্ণ হৈল, ভট্ট-আজ্ঞা লঞা ৷  
দক্ষিণ চলিলা প্রভু শ্রীরঙ্গ দেখিয়া ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

cāturmāsya pūrṇa haila, bhaṭṭa-ājñā lañā  
the Cāturmāsya  complete  became  of the Bhaṭṭa  permission  taking  
dakṣiṇa calilā prabhu śrī-raṅga dekhiyā  
south  went  the Lord  Śrī Raṅga  seeing

(164) When the Cāturmāsya was over, the Lord took the Bhaṭṭa’s leave, saw Śrī Raṅga and went on to the south.

সঙ্গেতে চলিলা ভট্ট, না যায় ভবনে ৷  
তাঁরে বিদায় দিলা প্রভু অনেক যতনে ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

saṅgete calilā bhaṭṭa, nā yāya bhavane  
along (with Him)  went  the Bhaṭṭa  not  goes  home  
tām̐re vidāya dilā prabhu aneka yatane  
him  sent back  the Lord  with great  effort

(165) The Bhaṭṭa went along with Him and would not go home; only with great effort did the Lord send him back.

প্রভুর বিয়োগে ভট্ট হৈল অচেতন ৷  
এই রঙ্গলীলা করে শচীর নন্দন ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

prabhura viyoge bhaṭṭa haila acetana  
of the Lord  in separation  the Bhaṭṭa  became  unconscious  
ei raṅga-līlā kare śacīra nandana  
such  playful pastimes  performs  of Śacī  the son

(166) In separation from the Lord, the Bhaṭṭa fell unconscious. Such are the playful pastimes of Śacī’s son.

ঋষভ-পর্ব্বতে চলি’ আইলা গৌরহরি ৷  
নারায়ণ দেখিলা তাঁহা নতিস্তুতি করি’ ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

ṛṣabha-parvate cali’ āilā gaurahari  
to Ṛṣabha hill  setting out  came  Gaurahari  
nārāyaṇa dekhilā tām̐hā nati-stuti kari’  
Nārāyaṇa  He saw  there  obeisances and prayers  offering

(167) Gaurahari went on and came to Ṛṣabha hill. There He saw Nārāyaṇa and offered obeisances and prayers.

পরমানন্দপুরী তাঁহা রহে চতুর্ম্মাস ৷  
শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু গেলা পুরী-গোসাঞির পাশ ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

paramānanda-purī tām̐hā rahe catur-māsa  
Paramānanda Purī  there  stays  for four months  
śuni’ mahāprabhu gelā purī-gosāñira pāśa  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  went  Purī Gosāñi  to

(168) Paramānanda Purī was staying there for the four months. Hearing this, Mahāprabhu went to Purī Gosāñi.

পুরী-গোসাঞির প্রভু কৈল চরণ বন্দন ৷  
প্রেমে পুরী-গোসাঞি তাঁরে কৈল আলিঙ্গন ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

purī-gosāñira prabhu kaila caraṇa vandana  
to Purī Gosāñi  the Lord  made  to the feet  homage  
preme purī-gosāñi tām̐re kaila āliṅgana  
with love  Purī Gosāñi  Him  took  in his embrace

(169) The Lord bowed at the feet of Purī Gosāñi, and Purī Gosāñi lovingly embraced Him.

তিনদিন প্রেমে দোঁহে কৃষ্ণকথা-রঙ্গে ৷  
সেই বিপ্র-ঘরে দোঁহে রহে একসঙ্গে ॥ ১৭০ ॥

tina dina preme dom̐he kṛṣṇa-kathā-raṅge  
three  days  in love  both  in the joy of talks about Kṛṣṇa  
sei vipra-ghare dom̐he rahe eka-saṅge  
in that  brāhmaṇa’s house  both  stay  together

(170) For three days the two of them stayed together in that brāhmaṇa’s house, rejoicing in loving talks about Kṛṣṇa.

পুরী-গোসাঞি বলে, — আমি যাব পুরুষোত্তমে ৷  
পুরুষোত্তম দেখি’ গৌড়ে যাব গঙ্গাস্নানে ॥ ১৭১ ॥

purī-gosāñi bale, — āmi yāba puruṣottame  
Purī Gosāñi  says  I  shall go  to Puruṣottama  
puruṣottama dekhi’ gauḍe yāba gaṅgā-snāne  
Puruṣottama  seeing  to Gauḍa  I shall go  for a bath in the Gaṅgā

(171) Purī Gosāñi said, “I shall go to Puruṣottama. After seeing Puruṣottama, I shall go to Gauḍa to bathe in the Gaṅgā.”

প্রভু কহে, — তুমি পুনঃ আইস নীলাচলে ৷  
আমি সেতুবন্ধ হৈতে আসিব অল্পকালে ॥ ১৭২ ॥

prabhu kahe, — tumi punaḥ āisa nīlācale  
the Lord  says  you  again  come  to Nīlācala  
āmi setubandha haite āsiba alpa-kāle  
I  from Setubandha  shall come back  in a short time

(172) The Lord said, “Please come back to Nīlācala. I shall return from Setubandha before long.

তোমার নিকটে রহি, — হেন বাঞ্ছা হয় ৷  
নীলাচলে আসিবে মোরে হঞা সদয় ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

tomāra nikaṭe rahi, — hena vāñchā haya  
your  in the company  to stay  such  desire  there is  
nīlācale āsibe more hañā sadaya  
to Nīlācala  you will come  to Me  being  kind

(173) I wish to stay in your company, so be kind to Me and come to Nīlācala.”

এত বলি’ তাঁর ঠাঞি এই আজ্ঞা লঞা ৷  
দক্ষিণে চলিলা প্রভু হরষিত হঞা ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

eta bali’ tām̐ra ṭhāñi ei ājñā lañā  
so  saying  from him  this  permission  taking  
dakṣiṇe calilā prabhu haraṣita hañā  
to the south  went  the Lord  delighted  being

(174) Having said this and taken his leave, the Lord went on to the south in great joy.

পরমানন্দপুরী তবে চলিলা নীলাচলে ৷  
মহাপ্রভু চলি’ তবে আইলা শ্রীশৈলে ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

paramānanda purī tabe calilā nīlācale  
Paramānanda  Purī  then  went  to Nīlācala  
mahāprabhu cali’ tabe āilā śrī-śaile  
Mahāprabhu  setting out  then  came  to Śrī Śaila

(175) Paramānanda Purī then set out for Nīlācala, and Mahāprabhu went on and came to Śrī Śaila.

শিব-দুর্গা রহে তাঁহা ব্রাহ্মণের বেশে ৷  
মহাপ্রভু দেখি’ দোঁহার হইল উল্লাসে ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

śiva-durgā rahe tām̐hā brāhmaṇera veśe  
Śiva and Durgā  live  there  of brāhmaṇas  in the guise  
mahāprabhu dekhi’ dom̐hāra haila ullāse  
Mahāprabhu  seeing  of the two  arose  joy

(176) Śiva and Durgā lived there in the guise of a brāhmaṇa couple. Seeing Mahāprabhu, both were overjoyed.

তিন দিন ভিক্ষা দিল করি’ নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
নিভৃতে বসি’ গুপ্তবার্ত্তা কহে দুইজন ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

tina dina bhikṣā dila kari’ nimantraṇa  
three  days  meal  they gave  making  invitation  
nibhṛte vasi’ gupta-vārtā kahe dui-jana  
in seclusion  sitting  confidential talks  hold  the two

(177) They invited Him and served Him meals for three days, and the two sat in seclusion and spoke of confidential matters.

তাঁর সঙ্গে মহাপ্রভু করি’ ইষ্টগোষ্ঠী ৷  
আজ্ঞা লঞা আইলা তবে পুরী কামকোষ্ঠী ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

tām̐ra saṅge mahāprabhu kari’ iṣṭa-goṣṭhī  
with him  Mahāprabhu  holding  spiritual talks  
ājñā lañā āilā tabe purī kāmakoṣṭhī  
permission  taking  came  then  to Kāmakoṣṭhī-purī

(178) After holding spiritual discussions with him, Mahāprabhu took his leave and came to Kāmakoṣṭhī-purī.

দক্ষিণ-মথুরা আইলা কামকোষ্ঠী হৈতে ৷  
তাঁহা দেখা হৈল এক ব্রাহ্মণ-সহিতে ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

dakṣiṇa-mathurā āilā kāmakoṣṭhī haite  
to Southern Mathurā  He came  from Kāmakoṣṭhī  
tām̐hā dekhā haila eka brāhmaṇa-sahite  
there  a meeting  took place  with a  brāhmaṇa

(179) From Kāmakoṣṭhī He came to Southern Mathurā, and there He met a brāhmaṇa.

সেই বিপ্র মহাপ্রভুকে কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
রামভক্ত সেই বিপ্র — বিরক্ত মহাজন ॥ ১৮০ ॥

sei vipra mahāprabhuke kaila nimantraṇa  
that  brāhmaṇa  Mahāprabhu  made  invitation  
rāma-bhakta sei vipra — virakta mahājana  
a devotee of Rāma  that  brāhmaṇa  a renounced  great soul

(180) That brāhmaṇa invited Mahāprabhu. He was a devotee of Rāma, a renounced great soul.

কৃতমালায় স্নান করি’ আইলা তাঁর ঘরে ৷  
ভিক্ষা কি দিবেন বিপ্র, — পাক নাহি করে ॥ ১৮১ ॥

kṛtamālāya snāna kari’ āilā tām̐ra ghare  
in the Kṛtamālā  bath  taking  He came  to his  house  
bhikṣā ki dibena vipra, — pāka nāhi kare  
meal  what  will give  the brāhmaṇa  cooking  not  he does

(181) After bathing in the Kṛtamālā, the Lord came to his house. But what meal could the brāhmaṇa serve? He had not cooked.

মহাপ্রভু কহে তাঁরে, — শুন মহাশয় ৷  
মধ্যাহ্ন হৈল, কেনে পাক নাহি হয় ॥ ১৮২ ॥

mahāprabhu kahe tām̐re, — śuna mahāśaya  
Mahāprabhu  says  to him  listen  good sir  
madhyāhna haila, kene pāka nāhi haya  
midday  has come  why  cooking  there is not

(182) Mahāprabhu said to him, “Listen, good sir: it is already midday. Why has nothing been cooked?”

[^1]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the people of the South are like Gajendra, and the crocodiles are Buddhism, Jainism, Māyāvāda and other doctrines.

[^2]: The same verse as Ādi 4.2; translation as there.

[^3]: Heading in the edition: the Lord went this way and that across the South, so the author’s account does not keep to the geographical order and gives only the general direction of the journey.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *pāṣaṇḍīs* (unbelievers) are the *jñānīs* and the followers of *karma-vāda*, the opponents of pure devotion.

[^5]: The verse explains the name “Rāma” by its derivation: from the root *ram*, “to delight” (*ramante*, “they delight”).

[^6]: Source as in the edition: a verse of the *Mahābhārata* (Udyoga-parva, 71.4) quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī.

[^7]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the logicians (*tārkikas*) are the Naiyāyikas, followers of Gautama, and the Vaiśeṣikas, followers of Kaṇāda; the Mīmāṁsakas follow Jaimini; the Māyāvādīs, Śaṅkara; Sāṅkhya is the teaching of Kapila; Pātañjala is the yoga-śāstra; the smṛtis are the twenty dharma-śāstra *saṁhitās* of Manu, Atri and others; the Purāṇas are the eighteen *mahāpurāṇas* and eighteen *upapurāṇas*; the Āgamas are the tantras.

[^8]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: one who opposes the Vedas and has turned away from bhakti is not to be spoken with; and of one who has seen such an unbeliever the śāstra says, “let him enter the water with his clothes on.”

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Buddhist śāstras do not follow the path of *śruti*, Vedic revelation, and so the Lord refutes them by logic alone.

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Buddhists have two paths, the *hīnayāna* and the *mahāyāna*, and both start from nine tenets: the universe is beginningless and so has no Īśvara; the world is unreal; the truth is the “I”; rebirth and the other world are real; Buddha alone is the way to know the truth; *nirvāṇa* is the highest truth; Buddhist philosophy alone is true; the Vedas were composed by men; the Buddhist life is the practice of true dharma — compassion and the like.
