“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 6  
**The Deliverance of Sārvabhauma**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

নৌমি তং গৌরচন্দ্রং যঃ কুতর্ক-কর্কশাশয়ম্ ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌমং সর্ব্বভূমা ভক্তিভূমানমাচরৎ ॥ ১ ॥

naumi taṁ gauracandraṁ yaḥ  
I bow down  to that  Gauracandra  who  
kutarka-karkaśāśayam  
by false arguments  whose heart was hardened  
sārvabhaumaṁ sarva-bhūmā  
Sārvabhauma  the Lord of all  
bhakti-bhūmānam ācarat  
of devotion  the great abode  made

(1) I bow down to Gauracandra, the Lord of all, who turned Sārvabhauma, his heart hardened by false arguments, into a great abode of devotion.[^1]

জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaurabhaktavṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

আবেশে চলিলা প্রভু জগন্নাথ-মন্দিরে ৷  
জগন্নাথ দেখি’ প্রেমে হইলা অস্থিরে ॥ ৩ ॥

āveśe calilā prabhu jagannātha-mandire  
in ecstasy  went  the Lord  to Jagannātha’s temple  
jagannātha dekhi’ preme hailā asthire  
Jagannātha  seeing  in love  He became  restless

(3) Absorbed in ecstasy, the Lord went to the temple of Jagannātha, and when He saw Jagannātha He was overwhelmed with love.

জগন্নাথ আলিঙ্গিতে চলিলা ধাঞা ৷  
মন্দিরে পড়িলা প্রেমে আবিষ্ট হঞা ॥ ৪ ॥

jagannātha āliṅgite calilā dhāñā  
Jagannātha  to embrace  He went  running  
mandire paḍilā preme āviṣṭa hañā  
in the temple  He fell  with love  overwhelmed  being

(4) He ran forward to embrace Jagannātha, but, overwhelmed with love, He fell down in the temple.

দৈবে সার্ব্বভৌম তাঁহাকে করে দরশন ৷  
পড়িছা মারিতে তেঁহো কৈল নিবারণ ॥ ৫ ॥

daive sārvabhauma tām̐hāke kare daraśana  
by providence  Sārvabhauma  Him  sees  
paḍichā mārite tem̐ho kaila nivāraṇa  
the temple superintendent  from striking  he  made  prevention

(5) By providence Sārvabhauma saw Him there, and he stopped the temple superintendent (*paḍichā*) from striking Him.[^3]

প্রভুর সৌন্দর্য্য আর প্রেমের বিকার ৷  
দেখি’ সার্ব্বভৌম হৈলা বিস্মিত অপার ॥ ৬ ॥

prabhura saundarya āra premera vikāra  
the Lord’s  beauty  and  of love  the transformations  
dekhi’ sārvabhauma hailā vismita apāra  
seeing  Sārvabhauma  became  amazed  boundlessly

(6) Seeing the Lord’s beauty and the transformations of love in His body, Sārvabhauma was amazed beyond measure.

বহুক্ষণে চৈতন্য নহে, ভোগের কাল হৈল ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম মনে তবে উপায় চিন্তিল ॥ ৭ ॥

bahu-kṣaṇe caitanya nahe, bhogera kāla haila  
for a long time  consciousness  is not  of the offering  the time  came  
sārvabhauma mane tabe upāya cintila  
Sārvabhauma  in his mind  then  a means  considered

(7) For a long time the Lord did not regain consciousness, and the time came to offer food to Jagannātha. Then Sārvabhauma considered what to do.

শিষ্য পড়িছা-দ্বারা নিল বহাঞা ৷  
ঘরে আনি’ পবিত্র-স্থানে রাখিল শোয়াঞা ॥ ৮ ॥

śiṣya paḍichā-dvārā nila vahāñā  
by disciples  and temple superintendents  had (Him)  carried  
ghare āni’ pavitra-sthāne rākhila śoyāñā  
to his house  bringing  in a pure  place  he kept (Him)  lying

(8) With the help of his disciples and the temple superintendents he had Him carried to his house and laid Him down in a pure place.[^4]

শ্বাস-প্রশ্বাস নাহি উদর-স্পন্দন ৷  
দেখিয়া চিন্তিত হৈল ভট্টাচার্য্যের মন ॥ ৯ ॥

śvāsa-praśvāsa nāhi udara-spandana  
breathing in and out  there is not  of the abdomen  movement  
dekhiyā cintita haila bhaṭṭācāryera mana  
seeing  anxious  became  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind

(9) There was no breath and no movement of the abdomen; seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious.

সূক্ষ্ম তুলা আনি’ নাসা-অগ্রেতে ধরিল ৷  
ঈষৎ চলয়ে তুলা দেখি’ ধৈর্য্য হৈল ॥ ১০ ॥

sūkṣma tulā āni’ nāsā-agrete dharila  
fine  cotton  bringing  at the tip of the nostrils  he held  
īṣat calaye tulā dekhi’ dhairya haila  
slightly  moves  the cotton  seeing  composure  came

(10) He brought a fine wisp of cotton and held it before the Lord’s nostrils, and when he saw the cotton stir slightly, he grew calmer.

বসি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য মনে করেন বিচার ৷  
এই কৃষ্ণ-মহাপ্রেমের সাত্ত্বিক বিকার ॥ ১১ ॥

vasi’ bhaṭṭācārya mane karena vicāra  
sitting  the Bhaṭṭācārya  in his mind  makes  reflection  
ei kṛṣṇa-mahāpremera sāttvika vikāra  
this  of great love for Kṛṣṇa  the sāttvika  transformation

(11) Sitting there, the Bhaṭṭācārya reflected: “This is a *sāttvika* transformation of great love for Kṛṣṇa.

‘সূদ্দীপ্ত সাত্ত্বিক’ এই নাম যে ‘প্রলয়’ ৷  
নিত্যসিদ্ধ ভক্তে সে ‘সূদ্দীপ্ত ভাব’ হয় ॥ ১২ ॥

‘sūddīpta sāttvika’ ei nāma ye ‘pralaya’  
sūddīpta  sāttvika  this  the name  which  pralaya  
nitya-siddha bhakte se ‘sūddīpta bhāva’ haya  
in eternally  perfect  devotees  that  sūddīpta  bhāva  occurs

(12) This swoon, called *pralaya*, is a *sūddīpta sāttvika*, the ecstatic symptom at its most blazing; such *sūddīpta bhāva* appears only in eternally perfect devotees.[^5]

‘অধিরূঢ়-মহাভাব’ যাঁর, তাঁর এ বিকার ৷  
মনুষ্যের দেহে দেখি, — বড় চমৎকার ॥ ১৩ ॥

‘adhirūḍha-mahābhāva’ yām̐ra, tām̐ra e vikāra  
adhirūḍha-mahābhāva  in whom  his  this  transformation  
manuṣyera dehe dekhi, — baḍa camatkāra  
of a human being  in the body  I see  very  astonishing

(13) This transformation belongs to one who has *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva*. That I should see it in a human body is astonishing indeed!”[^6]

এত চিন্তি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য আছেন বসিয়া ৷  
নিত্যানন্দাদি সিংহদ্বারে উত্তরিল গিয়া ॥ ১৪ ॥

eta cinti’ bhaṭṭācārya āchena vasiyā  
thus  thinking  the Bhaṭṭācārya  remains  sitting  
nityānandādi siṁha-dvāre uttarila giyā  
Nityānanda and the others  at the Lion Gate  arrived  going

(14) While the Bhaṭṭācārya sat there thinking this, Nityānanda and the others arrived at the Lion Gate.

তাঁহা শুনি’ লোকে কহে অন্যোন্যে বাত্ ৷  
এত সন্ন্যাসী আসি’ দেখি’ জগন্নাথ ॥ ১৫ ॥

tām̐hā śuni’ loke kahe anyonye bāt  
there  hearing  people  speak  among themselves  the talk  
eta sannyāsī āsi’ dekhi’ jagannātha  
such a  sannyāsī  coming  seeing  Jagannātha

(15) There they heard people talking among themselves: “Such a sannyāsī came here and saw Jagannātha.[^7]

মূর্চ্ছিত হৈল, চেতন না হয় শরীরে ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম লঞা গেলা আপনার ঘরে ॥ ১৬ ॥

mūrcchita haila, cetana nā haya śarīre  
unconscious  He became  consciousness  not  is  in the body  
sārvabhauma lañā gelā āpanāra ghare  
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house

(16) He fell unconscious and showed no sign of life, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.”

শুনি’ সবে জানিলা এই মহাপ্রভুর কার্য্য ৷  
হেনকালে আইলা তাঁহা গোপীনাথাচার্য্য ॥ ১৭ ॥

śuni’ sabe jānilā ei mahāprabhura kārya  
hearing  all  understood  this  Mahāprabhu’s  doing  
hena-kāle āilā tām̐hā gopīnāthācārya  
at that time  came  there  Gopīnātha Ācārya

(17) Hearing this, they all understood that it was Mahāprabhu’s doing. Just then Gopīnātha Ācārya came there.

নদীয়া-নিবাসী, বিশারদের জামাতা ৷  
মহাপ্রভুর ভক্ত তেঁহো প্রভুর তত্ত্বজ্ঞাতা ॥ ১৮ ॥

nadīyā-nivāsī, viśāradera jāmātā  
of Nadīyā  a resident  of Viśārada  the son-in-law  
mahāprabhura bhakta tem̐ho prabhura tattva-jñātā  
Mahāprabhu’s  devotee  he  of the Lord  the truth  knowing

(18) He was a resident of Nadīyā and the son-in-law of Viśārada, a devotee of Mahāprabhu who knew the truth about the Lord.[^8]

মুকুন্দ-সহিত পূর্ব্বে আছে পরিচয় ৷  
মুকুন্দ দেখিয়া তাঁর হইল বিস্ময় ॥ ১৯ ॥

mukunda-sahita pūrve āche paricaya  
with Mukunda  previously  there is  acquaintance  
mukunda dekhiyā tām̐ra haila vismaya  
Mukunda  seeing  his  became  surprise

(19) He had known Mukunda before, and he was surprised to see him there.

মুকুন্দ তাঁহারে দেখি’ কৈল নমস্কার ৷  
তেঁহো আলিঙ্গিয়া পুছে প্রভুর সমাচার ॥ ২০ ॥

mukunda tām̐hāre dekhi’ kaila namaskāra  
Mukunda  him  seeing  made  obeisance  
tem̐ho āliṅgiyā puche prabhura samācāra  
he  embracing (him)  asks  of the Lord  the news

(20) On seeing him, Mukunda bowed down, and Gopīnātha embraced him and asked for news of the Lord.

মুকুন্দ কহে, — প্রভুর ইঁহা হৈল আগমনে ৷  
আমি-সব আসিয়াছি মহাপ্রভুর সনে ॥ ২১ ॥

mukunda kahe, — prabhura im̐hā haila āgamane  
Mukunda  says  of the Lord  here  has been  the coming  
āmi-saba āsiyāchi mahāprabhura sane  
we all  have come  Mahāprabhu  with

(21) Mukunda said, “The Lord has come here, and we have all come with Mahāprabhu.”

নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞিকে আচার্য্য কৈল নমস্কার ৷  
সবে মেলি’ পুছে প্রভুর বার্ত্তা বার বার ॥ ২২ ॥

nityānanda-gosāñike ācārya kaila namaskāra  
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Ācārya  made  obeisance  
sabe meli’ puche prabhura vārtā bāra bāra  
all  meeting  he asks  of the Lord  news  again  and again

(22) The Ācārya bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi and, meeting them all, asked again and again for news of the Lord.

মুকুন্দ কহে, — মহাপ্রভু সন্ন্যাস করিয়া ৷  
নীলাচলে আইলা সঙ্গে আমা-সবা লঞা ॥ ২৩ ॥

mukunda kahe, — mahāprabhu sannyāsa kariyā  
Mukunda  says  Mahāprabhu  sannyāsa  having taken  
nīlācale āilā saṅge āmā-sabā lañā  
to Nīlācala  came  with (Him)  us all  taking

(23) Mukunda said, “After taking sannyāsa, Mahāprabhu came to Nīlācala and brought us all with Him.

আমা-সবা ছাড়ি’ আগে গেলা দরশনে ৷  
আমি-সব পাছে আইলাঙ তাঁর অন্বেষণে ॥ ২৪ ॥

āmā-sabā chāḍi’ āge gelā daraśane  
us all  leaving  ahead  He went  for darśana  
āmi-saba pāche āilāṅa tām̐ra anveṣaṇe  
we all  behind  came  of Him  in search

(24) He left us and went on ahead for darśana, and we have followed in search of Him.

অন্যোন্যে লোকের মুখে যে কথা শুনিল ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম-গৃহে প্রভু, — অনুমান কৈল ॥ ২৫ ॥

anyonye lokera mukhe ye kathā śunila  
among themselves  of the people  from the mouths  what  talk  we heard  
sārvabhauma-gṛhe prabhu, — anumāna kaila  
in Sārvabhauma’s house  the Lord  inference  we made

(25) From what we heard people saying among themselves, we have gathered that the Lord is in Sārvabhauma’s house.

ঈশ্বর-দর্শনে প্রভু প্রেমে অচেতন ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম লঞা গেলা আপন-ভবন ॥ ২৬ ॥

īśvara-darśane prabhu preme acetana  
of the Lord  at the sight  the Lord  in love  unconscious  
sārvabhauma lañā gelā āpana-bhavana  
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house

(26) At the sight of Jagannātha the Lord fell unconscious in love, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.

তোমার মিলনে যবে আমার হৈল মন ৷  
দৈবে সেই ক্ষণে পাইলুঁ তোমার দরশন ॥ ২৭ ॥

tomāra milane yabe āmāra haila mana  
with you  for meeting  when  my  was  mind  
daive sei kṣaṇe pāilum̐ tomāra daraśana  
by providence  at that  moment  I got  your  sight

(27) Just as I was wishing to meet you, by providence I saw you at that very moment.

চল, সবে যাই সার্ব্বভৌমের ভবন ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ পাছে করিব ঈশ্বর দর্শন ॥ ২৮ ॥

cala, sabe yāi sārvabhaumera bhavana  
come  all  let us go  Sārvabhauma’s  to the house  
prabhu dekhi’ pāche kariba īśvara darśana  
the Lord  seeing  afterwards  we shall have  of the Lord  darśana

(28) Come, let us all go to Sārvabhauma’s house. After seeing the Lord, we shall have darśana of Jagannātha.”[^9]

এত শুনি’ গোপীনাথ সবারে লঞা ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম-ঘরে গেলা হরষিত হঞা ॥ ২৯ ॥

eta śuni’ gopīnātha sabāre lañā  
this  hearing  Gopīnātha  all  taking  
sārvabhauma-ghare gelā haraṣita hañā  
to Sārvabhauma’s house  went  delighted  being

(29) Hearing this, Gopīnātha joyfully took them all to Sārvabhauma’s house.

সার্ব্বভৌম-স্থানে গিয়া প্রভুকে দেখিল ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ আচার্য্যের দুঃখ-হর্ষ হৈল ॥ ৩০ ॥

sārvabhauma-sthāne giyā prabhuke dekhila  
to Sārvabhauma’s place  going  the Lord  he saw  
prabhu dekhi’ ācāryera duḥkha-harṣa haila  
the Lord  seeing  of the Ācārya  sorrow and joy  arose

(30) When he came to Sārvabhauma’s house and saw the Lord, the Ācārya felt both sorrow and joy.

সার্ব্বভৌমে জানাঞা সবা নিল অভ্যন্তরে ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞিরে তেঁহো কৈল নমস্কারে ॥ ৩১ ॥

sārvabhaume jānāñā sabā nila abhyantare  
Sārvabhauma  informing  all  he took  inside  
nityānanda-gosāñire tem̐ho kaila namaskāre  
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  he  made  obeisance

(31) The Ācārya informed Sārvabhauma and brought them all inside, and Sārvabhauma bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi.

সবা-সহিত যথাযোগ্য করিল মিলন ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ সবার হৈল হরষিত মন ॥ ৩২ ॥

sabā-sahita yathā-yogya karila milana  
all  with  as befitting  he made  meeting  
prabhu dekhi’ sabāra haila haraṣita mana  
the Lord  seeing  of all  became  joyful  the minds

(32) He greeted each of them as was fitting, and all rejoiced at the sight of the Lord.

সার্ব্বভৌম পাঠাইল সবা দর্শন করিতে ৷  
‘চন্দনেশ্বর’ নিজপুত্র দিল সবার সাথে ॥ ৩৩ ॥

sārvabhauma pāṭhāila sabā darśana karite  
Sārvabhauma  sent  all  darśana  to have  
‘candaneśvara’ nija-putra dila sabāra sāthe  
Candaneśvara  his own son  he gave  of all  along with

(33) Sārvabhauma sent them all for darśana and gave them his own son, Candaneśvara, to go with them.

জগন্নাথ দেখি’ সবার হৈল আনন্দ ৷  
ভাবেতে আবিষ্ট হৈলা প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

jagannātha dekhi’ sabāra haila ānanda  
Jagannātha  seeing  of all  there was  joy  
bhāvete āviṣṭa hailā prabhu nityānanda  
in ecstasy  absorbed  became  Prabhu  Nityānanda

(34) Seeing Jagannātha, everyone was filled with joy, and Nityānanda Prabhu was overcome with ecstasy.

সবে মেলি’ ধরি’ তাঁরে সুস্থির করিল ৷  
ঈশ্বর-সেবক মালা-প্রসাদ আনি’ দিল ॥ ৩৫ ॥

sabe meli’ dhari’ tām̐re susthira karila  
all  together  holding  Him  steady  made  
īśvara-sevaka mālā-prasāda āni’ dila  
of the Lord  the servant  garland-prasāda  bringing  gave

(35) Together they held Him and steadied Him, and a servant of Jagannātha brought them His garland and prasāda.

প্রসাদ পাঞা সবে হৈলা আনন্দিত মনে ৷  
পুনরপি আইলা সবে মহাপ্রভুর স্থানে ॥ ৩৬ ॥

prasāda pāñā sabe hailā ānandita mane  
prasāda  receiving  all  became  joyful  in mind  
punarapi āilā sabe mahāprabhura sthāne  
once again  came  all  Mahāprabhu’s  to the place

(36) Receiving the prasāda, they all rejoiced, and they came back to Mahāprabhu.

উচ্চ করি’ করে সবে নাম-সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
তৃতীয় প্রহরে হৈল প্রভুর চেতন ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ucca kari’ kare sabe nāma-saṁkīrtana  
loudly  making  perform  all  of the name  saṁkīrtana  
tṛtīya prahare haila prabhura cetana  
in the third  watch  came  of the Lord  consciousness

(37) They all chanted the holy name loudly together, and in the third *prahara* of the day, after noon, the Lord regained consciousness.

হুঙ্কার করিয়া উঠে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলি’ ৷  
আনন্দে সার্ব্বভৌম তাঁর লৈল পদধূলি ॥ ৩৮ ॥

huṅkāra kariyā uṭhe ‘hari’ ‘hari’ bali’  
a roar  making  He rises  Hari  Hari  saying  
ānande sārvabhauma tām̐ra laila pada-dhūli  
in joy  Sārvabhauma  His  took  the dust of the feet

(38) With a roar He rose up, crying, “Hari! Hari!”, and Sārvabhauma joyfully took the dust of His feet.

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — শীঘ্র করহ মধ্যাহ্ন ৷  
মুঞি ভিক্ষা দিমু আজি মহা-প্রসাদান্ন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe, — śīghra karaha madhyāhna  
Sārvabhauma  says  quickly  perform  the midday duties  
muñi bhikṣā dimu āji mahā-prasādānna  
I  alms  shall give  today  mahā-prasāda

(39) Sārvabhauma said, “Quickly finish Your midday duties. Today I shall offer You mahā-prasāda as alms.”

সমুদ্রস্নান করি’ প্রভু শীঘ্র আইলা ৷  
চরণ পাখালি, প্রভু আসনে বসিলা ॥ ৪০ ॥

samudra-snāna kari’ prabhu śīghra āilā  
in the sea  bath  having taken  the Lord  quickly  came  
caraṇa pākhāli, prabhu āsane vasilā  
the feet  washing  the Lord  on a seat  sat

(40) The Lord bathed in the sea and quickly returned. He washed His feet and sat down on His seat.

বহুত প্রসাদ সার্ব্বভৌম আনাইল ৷  
তবে মহাপ্রভু সুখে ভোজন করিল ॥ ৪১ ॥

bahuta prasāda sārvabhauma ānāila  
much  prasāda  Sārvabhauma  had brought  
tabe mahāprabhu sukhe bhojana karila  
then  Mahāprabhu  happily  eating  did

(41) Sārvabhauma had a great quantity of prasāda brought, and Mahāprabhu happily took His meal.

সুবর্ণ-থালাতে অন্ন উত্তম ব্যঞ্জন ৷  
ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে প্রভু করেন ভোজন ॥ ৪২ ॥

suvarṇa-thālāte anna uttama vyañjana  
on golden plates  rice  excellent  vegetables  
bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge prabhu karena bhojana  
with the devotees  the Lord  does  eating

(42) Rice and excellent vegetable dishes were served on golden plates, and the Lord ate together with His devotees.

সার্ব্বভৌম পরিবেশন করেন আপনে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — মোরে দেহ লাফ্রা-ব্যঞ্জনে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

sārvabhauma pariveśana karena āpane  
Sārvabhauma  serving  does  himself  
prabhu kahe, — more deha lāphrā-vyañjane  
the Lord  says  to Me  give  lāphrā vegetables

(43) Sārvabhauma served them himself. The Lord said, “Give Me the *lāphrā*, the plain mixed vegetables,[^10]

পীঠা-পানা দেহ তুমি ইঁহা-সবাকারে ৷  
সবে ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে যুড়ি’ দুই করে ॥ ৪৪ ॥

pīṭhā-pānā deha tumi im̐hā-sabākāre  
cakes and sweet drinks  give  you  to all these  
sabe bhaṭṭācārya kahe yuḍi’ dui kare  
to all  the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  joining  two  hands

(44) and give the cakes and sweet drinks to all of them.” With folded hands the Bhaṭṭācārya said to them all:

জগন্নাথ কৈছে করিয়াছেন ভোজন ৷  
আজি সব মহাপ্রসাদ কর আস্বাদন ॥ ৪৫ ॥

jagannātha kaiche kariyāchena bhojana  
Jagannātha  as  has done  eating  
āji saba mahāprasāda kara āsvādana  
today  all  mahā-prasāda  do  tasting

(45) Today you must all relish the mahā-prasāda, just as Jagannātha has eaten it.”

এত বলি’ পীঠা-পানা সব খাওয়াইলা ৷  
ভিক্ষা করাঞা আচমন করাইলা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

eta bali’ pīṭhā-pānā saba khāoyāilā  
so  saying  cakes and sweet drinks  all  he fed  
bhikṣā karāñā ācamana karāilā  
the meal  having given  rinsing of the mouth  he had them do

(46) Saying this, he fed them all the cakes and sweet drinks, and when they had eaten he gave them water to rinse their mouths.

আজ্ঞা মাগি’ গোপীনাথ আচার্য্যকে লঞা ৷  
প্রভুর নিকটে আইলা ভোজন করিয়া ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ājñā māgi’ gopīnātha ācāryake lañā  
permission  asking  Gopīnātha  Ācārya  taking  
prabhura nikaṭe āilā bhojana kariyā  
of the Lord  near  came  eating  having done

(47) Then, taking leave, he went with Gopīnātha Ācārya to eat, and after eating he came back to the Lord.

‘নমো নারায়ণায়’ বলি’ নমস্কার কৈল ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণে মতিরস্তু’ বলি’ গোসাঞি কহিল ॥ ৪৮ ॥

‘namo nārāyaṇāya’ bali’ namaskāra kaila  
namo nārāyaṇāya  saying  obeisance  he made  
‘kṛṣṇe matir astu’ bali’ gosāñi kahila  
on Kṛṣṇa  the mind  may be  saying  Gosāñi  spoke

(48) Saying, “*Namo nārāyaṇāya*”, he bowed down to the Lord, and Gosāñi replied, “*Kṛṣṇe matir astu* — may your mind be fixed on Kṛṣṇa!”[^11]

শুনি’ সার্ব্বভৌম মনে বিচার করিল ৷  
বৈষ্ণব-সন্ন্যাসী ইঁহো, বচনে জানিল ॥ ৪৯ ॥

śuni’ sārvabhauma mane vicāra karila  
hearing  Sārvabhauma  in his mind  consideration  made  
vaiṣṇava-sannyāsī im̐ho, vacane jānila  
a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī  He  by His words  he knew

(49) Hearing this, Sārvabhauma reflected, and from His words he understood that He was a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī.

গোপীনাথ আচার্য্যেরে কহে সার্ব্বভৌম ৷  
গোসাঞির জানিতে চাহি কাঁহা পূর্ব্বাশ্রম ॥ ৫০ ॥

gopīnātha ācāryere kahe sārvabhauma  
to Gopīnātha  Ācārya  says  Sārvabhauma  
gosāñira jānite cāhi kām̐hā pūrvāśrama  
of Gosāñi  to know  I want  what  the former āśrama

(50) Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya, “I would like to know about Gosāñi’s life before He took sannyāsa.”[^12]

গোপীনাথাচার্য্য কহে, — নবদ্বীপে ঘর ৷  
‘জগন্নাথ’ — নাম, পদবী — ‘মিশ্র পুরন্দর’ ॥ ৫১ ॥

gopīnāthācārya kahe, — navadvīpe ghara  
Gopīnātha Ācārya  says  in Navadvīpa  home  
‘jagannātha’ — nāma, padavī — ‘miśra purandara’  
Jagannātha  name  title  Miśra Purandara

(51) Gopīnātha Ācārya said, “His home is in Navadvīpa. There lived a man named Jagannātha, whose title was Miśra Purandara.

‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ — নাম ইঁহার, তাঁর ইঁহো পুত্র ৷  
নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্ত্তীর হয়েন দৌহিত্র ॥ ৫২ ॥

‘viśvambhara’ — nāma im̐hāra, tām̐ra im̐ho putra  
Viśvambhara  name  His  his  He  son  
nīlāmbara cakravartīra hayena dauhitra  
of Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī  He is  the daughter’s son

(52) This is his son, named Viśvambhara. He is also the grandson of Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, his daughter’s son.”

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্ত্তী ৷  
বিশারদের সমাধ্যায়ী, — এই তাঁর খ্যাতি ॥ ৫৩ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe, — nīlāmbara cakravartī  
Sārvabhauma  says  Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī  
viśāradera samādhyāyī, — ei tām̐ra khyāti  
of Viśārada  a fellow student  this  his  fame

(53) Sārvabhauma said, “Nīlāmbara Cakravartī is known as a fellow student of Viśārada.

‘মিশ্র পুরন্দর’ তাঁর মান্য, হেন জানি ৷  
পিতার সম্বন্ধে দোঁহাকে পূজ্য করি’ মানি ॥ ৫৪ ॥

‘miśra purandara’ tām̐ra mānya, hena jāni  
Miśra Purandara  by him  respected  this  I know  
pitāra sambandhe dom̐hāke pūjya kari’ māni  
of my father  through the connection  both  worthy of honour  making  I regard

(54) Miśra Purandara, I know, was respected by him. Because of their bond with my father, I honour them both.”

নদীয়া-সম্বন্ধে সার্ব্বভৌম হৃষ্ট হৈলা ৷  
প্রীত হঞা গোসাঞিরে কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

nadīyā-sambandhe sārvabhauma hṛṣṭa hailā  
with Nadīyā  through the connection  Sārvabhauma  glad  became  
prīta hañā gosāñire kahite lāgilā  
pleased  being  to Gosāñi  to speak  began

(55) Sārvabhauma was glad to find this connection with Nadīyā, and in his pleasure he began to speak to Gosāñi.

সহজেই পূজ্য তুমি, আরে ত’ সন্ন্যাস ৷  
অতএব হঙ তোমার আমি নিজ-দাস ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sahajei pūjya tumi, āre ta’ sannyāsa  
naturally  worthy of worship  You  moreover  indeed  sannyāsa  
ataeva haṅa tomāra āmi nija-dāsa  
therefore  am  Your  I  own servant

(56) “You are worthy of honour by Your very nature, and on top of that You are a sannyāsī. Therefore I am Your own servant.”[^13]

শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু কৈল শ্রীবিষ্ণু স্মরণ ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যে কহে কিছু বিনয়-বচন ॥ ৫৭ ॥

śuni’ mahāprabhu kaila śrī viṣṇu smaraṇa  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  made  of Śrī Viṣṇu  remembrance  
bhaṭṭācārye kahe kichu vinaya-vacana  
to the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  some  humble words

(57) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu remembered Śrī Viṣṇu and spoke some humble words to the Bhaṭṭācārya.[^14]

তুমি জগদ্গুরু — সর্ব্বলোক-হিতকর্ত্তা ৷  
বেদান্ত পড়াও, সন্ন্যাসীর উপকর্ত্তা ॥ ৫৮ ॥

tumi jagad-guru — sarvaloka-hita-kartā  
you  the teacher of the world  of all people  the benefactor  
vedānta paḍāo, sannyāsīra upakartā  
Vedānta  you teach  of sannyāsīs  the helper

(58) “You are the teacher of the whole world and the benefactor of all people. You teach Vedānta, and you are a helper of sannyāsīs.

আমি বালক-সন্ন্যাসী — ভাল-মন্দ নাহি জানি ৷  
তোমার আশ্রয় নিলুঁ, গুরু করি’ মানি ॥ ৫৯ ॥

āmi bālaka-sannyāsī — bhāla-manda nāhi jāni  
I  a boy sannyāsī  good and bad  not  know  
tomāra āśraya nilum̐, guru kari’ māni  
your  shelter  I have taken  guru  making  I regard

(59) I am a young sannyāsī who does not know good from bad. I have taken shelter of you, and I regard you as My guru.

তোমার সঙ্গ লাগি’ মোর ইঁহা আগমন ৷  
সর্ব্বপ্রকারে করিবে আমায় পালন ॥ ৬০ ॥

tomāra saṅga lāgi’ mora im̐hā āgamana  
your  association  for the sake of  My  here  coming  
sarva-prakāre karibe āmāya pālana  
in every  way  you will  Me  protect

(60) I have come here for your company alone. Please take care of Me in every way.

---

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to Gaura, who conquered Sārvabhauma”. *Anubhāṣya*: *sarva-bhūmā* — He whose greatness surpasses all that is not Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Paramātmā Gauracandra; *kutarka* — false arguments, misconceptions about the nature of the jīva and its activity; *bhakti-bhūmānam ācarat* — He made him a vessel full of pure bhakti, that is, a servant of His feet. The verse plays on the words *sārvabhauma* (“lord of all the earth”), *sarva-bhūmā* and *bhakti-bhūmā*.

[^2]: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *paḍichā* is a temple officer, a kind of overseer; this *paḍichā* was a disciple of Sārvabhauma. Heading in the edition: “Sārvabhauma happens to see the Lord and saves Him from a blow”.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya*: Sārvabhauma then lived south of the temple, in Bālukhaṇḍa, on the bank of the Mārkaṇḍeya-saras pond; the house is now known as the Gaṅgāmātā Maṭha.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* and the *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi*): the *sāttvika* symptoms may be “smouldering”, “flaming”, “burning” or “blazing” (*uddīpta*); *sūddīpta* is a special kind of the blazing, when all the *sāttvika* symptoms, multiplied many times over, reach the highest limit of prema. An eternally perfect (*nitya-siddha*) devotee is an eternal associate of the Lord.

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya*: *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva* is beyond the reach even of the queens of Dvārakā — only the gopīs of Vraja experience it; its highest stage, *mādana*, is found in Śrī Rādhikā alone. Heading in the edition: “Unworldly *mahābhāva* in the Lord’s body”.

[^7]: Heading in the edition: “Nityānanda and His companions come from Āṭhāranālā to Purī and hear from the people that the Lord is in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s house”.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: Maheśvara Viśārada, a fellow student of Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, lived in Vidyānagara near Samudragaḍa; his sons were Madhusūdana Vācaspati and Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma, and his son-in-law was Gopīnātha Ācārya, the husband of Sārvabhauma’s sister.

[^9]: Heading in the edition: “Love for the Lord is greater than for Jagannātha”.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: *lāphrā-vyañjana* is a vegetable dish in which various vegetables are stewed together and seasoned with cumin, pepper and mustard.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: it is customary to greet sannyāsīs with the words *oṁ namo nārāyaṇāya*. The smṛti rule for sannyāsīs is to give neither blessings nor obeisances, but a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī knows himself to be a servant of Kṛṣṇa and so gives everyone the compassionate blessing, “May your mind be fixed on Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet.”

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pūrvāśrama* — the name by which He was known and the place where He lived as a householder, before taking sannyāsa.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: by the excellence of Your nature You are worthy of my worship, and by Your outward āśrama, as a sannyāsī, of the worship of householders like us; so I am Your servant.

[^14]: Heading in the edition: “The humility of the Lord, who honours others (*mānada-dharma*)”.
