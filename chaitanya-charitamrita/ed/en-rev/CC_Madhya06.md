“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 6  
**The Deliverance of Sārvabhauma**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

নৌমি তং গৌরচন্দ্রং যঃ কুতর্ক-কর্কশাশয়ম্ ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌমং সর্ব্বভূমা ভক্তিভূমানমাচরৎ ॥ ১ ॥

naumi taṁ gauracandraṁ yaḥ  
I bow down  to that  Gauracandra  who  
kutarka-karkaśāśayam  
by false arguments  whose heart was hardened  
sārvabhaumaṁ sarva-bhūmā  
Sārvabhauma  the Lord of all  
bhakti-bhūmānam ācarat  
of devotion  the great abode  made

(1) I bow down to Gauracandra, the Lord of all, who turned Sārvabhauma, his heart hardened by false arguments, into a great abode of devotion.[^1]

জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaurabhaktavṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

আবেশে চলিলা প্রভু জগন্নাথ-মন্দিরে ৷  
জগন্নাথ দেখি’ প্রেমে হইলা অস্থিরে ॥ ৩ ॥

āveśe calilā prabhu jagannātha-mandire  
in ecstasy  went  the Lord  to Jagannātha’s temple  
jagannātha dekhi’ preme hailā asthire  
Jagannātha  seeing  in love  He became  restless

(3) Absorbed in ecstasy, the Lord went to the temple of Jagannātha, and when He saw Jagannātha, He grew restless with love.

জগন্নাথ আলিঙ্গিতে চলিলা ধাঞা ৷  
মন্দিরে পড়িলা প্রেমে আবিষ্ট হঞা ॥ ৪ ॥

jagannātha āliṅgite calilā dhāñā  
Jagannātha  to embrace  He went  running  
mandire paḍilā preme āviṣṭa hañā  
in the temple  He fell  with love  overwhelmed  being

(4) He ran forward to embrace Jagannātha, but, overwhelmed with love, He fell down in the temple.

দৈবে সার্ব্বভৌম তাঁহাকে করে দরশন ৷  
পড়িছা মারিতে তেঁহো কৈল নিবারণ ॥ ৫ ॥

daive sārvabhauma tām̐hāke kare daraśana  
by providence  Sārvabhauma  Him  sees  
paḍichā mārite tem̐ho kaila nivāraṇa  
the temple superintendent  from striking  he  made  prevention

(5) By providence Sārvabhauma saw Him there, and he stopped the temple superintendent (*paḍichā*) from striking Him.[^3]

প্রভুর সৌন্দর্য্য আর প্রেমের বিকার ৷  
দেখি’ সার্ব্বভৌম হৈলা বিস্মিত অপার ॥ ৬ ॥

prabhura saundarya āra premera vikāra  
the Lord’s  beauty  and  of love  the transformations  
dekhi’ sārvabhauma hailā vismita apāra  
seeing  Sārvabhauma  became  amazed  boundlessly

(6) Seeing the Lord’s beauty and the transformations of love in His body, Sārvabhauma was amazed beyond measure.

বহুক্ষণে চৈতন্য নহে, ভোগের কাল হৈল ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম মনে তবে উপায় চিন্তিল ॥ ৭ ॥

bahu-kṣaṇe caitanya nahe, bhogera kāla haila  
for a long time  consciousness  is not  of the offering  the time  came  
sārvabhauma mane tabe upāya cintila  
Sārvabhauma  in his mind  then  a means  considered

(7) For a long time the Lord did not regain consciousness, and the time came to offer food to Jagannātha. Then Sārvabhauma considered what to do.

শিষ্য পড়িছা-দ্বারা নিল বহাঞা ৷  
ঘরে আনি’ পবিত্র-স্থানে রাখিল শোয়াঞা ॥ ৮ ॥

śiṣya paḍichā-dvārā nila vahāñā  
by disciples  and temple superintendents  had (Him)  carried  
ghare āni’ pavitra-sthāne rākhila śoyāñā  
to his house  bringing  in a pure  place  he kept (Him)  lying

(8) With the help of his disciples and the temple superintendents he had Him carried to his house and laid Him down in a pure place.[^4]

শ্বাস-প্রশ্বাস নাহি উদর-স্পন্দন ৷  
দেখিয়া চিন্তিত হৈল ভট্টাচার্য্যের মন ॥ ৯ ॥

śvāsa-praśvāsa nāhi udara-spandana  
breathing in and out  there is not  of the abdomen  movement  
dekhiyā cintita haila bhaṭṭācāryera mana  
seeing  anxious  became  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind

(9) There was no breath and no movement of the abdomen; seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious.

সূক্ষ্ম তুলা আনি’ নাসা-অগ্রেতে ধরিল ৷  
ঈষৎ চলয়ে তুলা দেখি’ ধৈর্য্য হৈল ॥ ১০ ॥

sūkṣma tulā āni’ nāsā-agrete dharila  
fine  cotton  bringing  at the tip of the nostrils  he held  
īṣat calaye tulā dekhi’ dhairya haila  
slightly  moves  the cotton  seeing  composure  came

(10) He brought a fine wisp of cotton and held it before the Lord’s nostrils, and when he saw the cotton stir slightly, he grew calmer.

বসি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য মনে করেন বিচার ৷  
এই কৃষ্ণ-মহাপ্রেমের সাত্ত্বিক বিকার ॥ ১১ ॥

vasi’ bhaṭṭācārya mane karena vicāra  
sitting  the Bhaṭṭācārya  in his mind  makes  reflection  
ei kṛṣṇa-mahāpremera sāttvika vikāra  
this  of great love for Kṛṣṇa  the sāttvika  transformation

(11) Sitting there, the Bhaṭṭācārya reflected: “This is a *sāttvika* transformation of great love for Kṛṣṇa.

‘সূদ্দীপ্ত সাত্ত্বিক’ এই নাম যে ‘প্রলয়’ ৷  
নিত্যসিদ্ধ ভক্তে সে ‘সূদ্দীপ্ত ভাব’ হয় ॥ ১২ ॥

‘sūddīpta sāttvika’ ei nāma ye ‘pralaya’  
sūddīpta  sāttvika  this  the name  which  pralaya  
nitya-siddha bhakte se ‘sūddīpta bhāva’ haya  
in eternally  perfect  devotees  that  sūddīpta  bhāva  occurs

(12) This swoon, called *pralaya*, is *sūddīpta sāttvika*, ecstasy at its most blazing, and such blazing ecstasy (*sūddīpta bhāva*) is found only in eternally perfect devotees.[^5]

‘অধিরূঢ়-মহাভাব’ যাঁর, তাঁর এ বিকার ৷  
মনুষ্যের দেহে দেখি, — বড় চমৎকার ॥ ১৩ ॥

‘adhirūḍha-mahābhāva’ yām̐ra, tām̐ra e vikāra  
adhirūḍha-mahābhāva  in whom  his  this  transformation  
manuṣyera dehe dekhi, — baḍa camatkāra  
of a human being  in the body  I see  very  astonishing

(13) This transformation belongs to one who has *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva*. That I should see it in a human body is astonishing indeed!”[^6]

এত চিন্তি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য আছেন বসিয়া ৷  
নিত্যানন্দাদি সিংহদ্বারে উত্তরিল গিয়া ॥ ১৪ ॥

eta cinti’ bhaṭṭācārya āchena vasiyā  
thus  thinking  the Bhaṭṭācārya  remains  sitting  
nityānandādi siṁha-dvāre uttarila giyā  
Nityānanda and the others  at the Lion Gate  arrived  going

(14) While the Bhaṭṭācārya sat there thinking this, Nityānanda and the others arrived at the Lion Gate.

তাঁহা শুনি’ লোকে কহে অন্যোন্যে বাত্ ৷  
এত সন্ন্যাসী আসি’ দেখি’ জগন্নাথ ॥ ১৫ ॥

tām̐hā śuni’ loke kahe anyonye bāt  
there  hearing  people  speak  among themselves  the talk  
eta sannyāsī āsi’ dekhi’ jagannātha  
such a  sannyāsī  coming  seeing  Jagannātha

(15) There they heard people saying to one another, “A sannyāsī came here and saw Jagannātha.[^7]

মূর্চ্ছিত হৈল, চেতন না হয় শরীরে ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম লঞা গেলা আপনার ঘরে ॥ ১৬ ॥

mūrcchita haila, cetana nā haya śarīre  
unconscious  He became  consciousness  not  is  in the body  
sārvabhauma lañā gelā āpanāra ghare  
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house

(16) He fell unconscious and showed no sign of life, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.”

শুনি’ সবে জানিলা এই মহাপ্রভুর কার্য্য ৷  
হেনকালে আইলা তাঁহা গোপীনাথাচার্য্য ॥ ১৭ ॥

śuni’ sabe jānilā ei mahāprabhura kārya  
hearing  all  understood  this  Mahāprabhu’s  doing  
hena-kāle āilā tām̐hā gopīnāthācārya  
at that time  came  there  Gopīnātha Ācārya

(17) Hearing this, they all understood that it was Mahāprabhu’s doing. Just then Gopīnātha Ācārya came there.

নদীয়া-নিবাসী, বিশারদের জামাতা ৷  
মহাপ্রভুর ভক্ত তেঁহো প্রভুর তত্ত্বজ্ঞাতা ॥ ১৮ ॥

nadīyā-nivāsī, viśāradera jāmātā  
of Nadīyā  a resident  of Viśārada  the son-in-law  
mahāprabhura bhakta tem̐ho prabhura tattva-jñātā  
Mahāprabhu’s  devotee  he  of the Lord  the truth  knowing

(18) He was a resident of Nadīyā and the son-in-law of Viśārada. A devotee of Mahāprabhu, he knew the truth about the Lord.[^8]

মুকুন্দ-সহিত পূর্ব্বে আছে পরিচয় ৷  
মুকুন্দ দেখিয়া তাঁর হইল বিস্ময় ॥ ১৯ ॥

mukunda-sahita pūrve āche paricaya  
with Mukunda  previously  there is  acquaintance  
mukunda dekhiyā tām̐ra haila vismaya  
Mukunda  seeing  his  became  surprise

(19) He had known Mukunda before, and he was surprised to see him there.

মুকুন্দ তাঁহারে দেখি’ কৈল নমস্কার ৷  
তেঁহো আলিঙ্গিয়া পুছে প্রভুর সমাচার ॥ ২০ ॥

mukunda tām̐hāre dekhi’ kaila namaskāra  
Mukunda  him  seeing  made  obeisance  
tem̐ho āliṅgiyā puche prabhura samācāra  
he  embracing (him)  asks  of the Lord  the news

(20) On seeing him, Mukunda bowed down, and Gopīnātha embraced him and asked for news of the Lord.

মুকুন্দ কহে, — প্রভুর ইঁহা হৈল আগমনে ৷  
আমি-সব আসিয়াছি মহাপ্রভুর সনে ॥ ২১ ॥

mukunda kahe, — prabhura im̐hā haila āgamane  
Mukunda  says  of the Lord  here  has been  the coming  
āmi-saba āsiyāchi mahāprabhura sane  
we all  have come  Mahāprabhu  with

(21) Mukunda said, “The Lord has come here, and we have all come with Mahāprabhu.”

নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞিকে আচার্য্য কৈল নমস্কার ৷  
সবে মেলি’ পুছে প্রভুর বার্ত্তা বার বার ॥ ২২ ॥

nityānanda-gosāñike ācārya kaila namaskāra  
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Ācārya  made  obeisance  
sabe meli’ puche prabhura vārtā bāra bāra  
all  meeting  he asks  of the Lord  news  again  and again

(22) The Ācārya bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi and, meeting them all, asked again and again for news of the Lord.

মুকুন্দ কহে, — মহাপ্রভু সন্ন্যাস করিয়া ৷  
নীলাচলে আইলা সঙ্গে আমা-সবা লঞা ॥ ২৩ ॥

mukunda kahe, — mahāprabhu sannyāsa kariyā  
Mukunda  says  Mahāprabhu  sannyāsa  having taken  
nīlācale āilā saṅge āmā-sabā lañā  
to Nīlācala  came  with (Him)  us all  taking

(23) Mukunda said, “After taking sannyāsa, Mahāprabhu came to Nīlācala and brought us all with Him.

আমা-সবা ছাড়ি’ আগে গেলা দরশনে ৷  
আমি-সব পাছে আইলাঙ তাঁর অন্বেষণে ॥ ২৪ ॥

āmā-sabā chāḍi’ āge gelā daraśane  
us all  leaving  ahead  He went  for darśana  
āmi-saba pāche āilāṅa tām̐ra anveṣaṇe  
we all  behind  came  of Him  in search

(24) He left us and went on ahead for darśana, and we have followed in search of Him.

অন্যোন্যে লোকের মুখে যে কথা শুনিল ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম-গৃহে প্রভু, — অনুমান কৈল ॥ ২৫ ॥

anyonye lokera mukhe ye kathā śunila  
among themselves  of the people  from the mouths  what  talk  we heard  
sārvabhauma-gṛhe prabhu, — anumāna kaila  
in Sārvabhauma’s house  the Lord  inference  we made

(25) From what we heard people saying among themselves, we have gathered that the Lord is in Sārvabhauma’s house.

ঈশ্বর-দর্শনে প্রভু প্রেমে অচেতন ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম লঞা গেলা আপন-ভবন ॥ ২৬ ॥

īśvara-darśane prabhu preme acetana  
of the Lord  at the sight  the Lord  in love  unconscious  
sārvabhauma lañā gelā āpana-bhavana  
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house

(26) At the sight of Jagannātha the Lord fell unconscious in love, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.

তোমার মিলনে যবে আমার হৈল মন ৷  
দৈবে সেই ক্ষণে পাইলুঁ তোমার দরশন ॥ ২৭ ॥

tomāra milane yabe āmāra haila mana  
with you  for meeting  when  my  was  mind  
daive sei kṣaṇe pāilum̐ tomāra daraśana  
by providence  at that  moment  I got  your  sight

(27) Just as I was wishing to meet you, by providence I saw you at that very moment.

চল, সবে যাই সার্ব্বভৌমের ভবন ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ পাছে করিব ঈশ্বর দর্শন ॥ ২৮ ॥

cala, sabe yāi sārvabhaumera bhavana  
come  all  let us go  Sārvabhauma’s  to the house  
prabhu dekhi’ pāche kariba īśvara darśana  
the Lord  seeing  afterwards  we shall have  of the Lord  darśana

(28) Come, let us all go to Sārvabhauma’s house. After seeing the Lord, we shall have darśana of Jagannātha.”[^9]

এত শুনি’ গোপীনাথ সবারে লঞা ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম-ঘরে গেলা হরষিত হঞা ॥ ২৯ ॥

eta śuni’ gopīnātha sabāre lañā  
this  hearing  Gopīnātha  all  taking  
sārvabhauma-ghare gelā haraṣita hañā  
to Sārvabhauma’s house  went  delighted  being

(29) Hearing this, Gopīnātha joyfully took them all to Sārvabhauma’s house.

সার্ব্বভৌম-স্থানে গিয়া প্রভুকে দেখিল ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ আচার্য্যের দুঃখ-হর্ষ হৈল ॥ ৩০ ॥

sārvabhauma-sthāne giyā prabhuke dekhila  
to Sārvabhauma’s place  going  the Lord  he saw  
prabhu dekhi’ ācāryera duḥkha-harṣa haila  
the Lord  seeing  of the Ācārya  sorrow and joy  arose

(30) When he came to Sārvabhauma’s house and saw the Lord, the Ācārya felt both sorrow and joy.

সার্ব্বভৌমে জানাঞা সবা নিল অভ্যন্তরে ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞিরে তেঁহো কৈল নমস্কারে ॥ ৩১ ॥

sārvabhaume jānāñā sabā nila abhyantare  
Sārvabhauma  informing  all  he took  inside  
nityānanda-gosāñire tem̐ho kaila namaskāre  
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  he  made  obeisance

(31) The Ācārya informed Sārvabhauma and brought them all inside, and the master of the house bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi.

সবা-সহিত যথাযোগ্য করিল মিলন ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ সবার হৈল হরষিত মন ॥ ৩২ ॥

sabā-sahita yathā-yogya karila milana  
all  with  as befitting  he made  meeting  
prabhu dekhi’ sabāra haila haraṣita mana  
the Lord  seeing  of all  became  joyful  the minds

(32) He greeted each of them as was fitting, and all rejoiced at the sight of the Lord.

সার্ব্বভৌম পাঠাইল সবা দর্শন করিতে ৷  
‘চন্দনেশ্বর’ নিজপুত্র দিল সবার সাথে ॥ ৩৩ ॥

sārvabhauma pāṭhāila sabā darśana karite  
Sārvabhauma  sent  all  darśana  to have  
‘candaneśvara’ nija-putra dila sabāra sāthe  
Candaneśvara  his own son  he gave  of all  along with

(33) Sārvabhauma sent them all for darśana and gave them his own son, Candaneśvara, to go with them.

জগন্নাথ দেখি’ সবার হৈল আনন্দ ৷  
ভাবেতে আবিষ্ট হৈলা প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

jagannātha dekhi’ sabāra haila ānanda  
Jagannātha  seeing  of all  there was  joy  
bhāvete āviṣṭa hailā prabhu nityānanda  
in ecstasy  absorbed  became  Prabhu  Nityānanda

(34) Seeing Jagannātha, everyone was filled with joy, and Nityānanda Prabhu was overcome with ecstasy.

সবে মেলি’ ধরি’ তাঁরে সুস্থির করিল ৷  
ঈশ্বর-সেবক মালা-প্রসাদ আনি’ দিল ॥ ৩৫ ॥

sabe meli’ dhari’ tām̐re susthira karila  
all  together  holding  Him  steady  made  
īśvara-sevaka mālā-prasāda āni’ dila  
of the Lord  the servant  garland-prasāda  bringing  gave

(35) Together they held Him and steadied Him, and a servant of Jagannātha brought them His garland and prasāda.

প্রসাদ পাঞা সবে হৈলা আনন্দিত মনে ৷  
পুনরপি আইলা সবে মহাপ্রভুর স্থানে ॥ ৩৬ ॥

prasāda pāñā sabe hailā ānandita mane  
prasāda  receiving  all  became  joyful  in mind  
punarapi āilā sabe mahāprabhura sthāne  
once again  came  all  Mahāprabhu’s  to the place

(36) Receiving the prasāda, they all rejoiced, and they came back to Mahāprabhu.

উচ্চ করি’ করে সবে নাম-সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
তৃতীয় প্রহরে হৈল প্রভুর চেতন ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ucca kari’ kare sabe nāma-saṁkīrtana  
loudly  making  perform  all  of the name  saṁkīrtana  
tṛtīya prahare haila prabhura cetana  
in the third  watch  came  of the Lord  consciousness

(37) They all chanted the holy name loudly together, and in the third *prahara* of the day, after noon, the Lord regained consciousness.

হুঙ্কার করিয়া উঠে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলি’ ৷  
আনন্দে সার্ব্বভৌম তাঁর লৈল পদধূলি ॥ ৩৮ ॥

huṅkāra kariyā uṭhe ‘hari’ ‘hari’ bali’  
a roar  making  He rises  Hari  Hari  saying  
ānande sārvabhauma tām̐ra laila pada-dhūli  
in joy  Sārvabhauma  His  took  the dust of the feet

(38) With a roar He rose up, crying, “Hari! Hari!”, and Sārvabhauma joyfully took the dust of His feet.

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — শীঘ্র করহ মধ্যাহ্ন ৷  
মুঞি ভিক্ষা দিমু আজি মহা-প্রসাদান্ন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe, — śīghra karaha madhyāhna  
Sārvabhauma  says  quickly  perform  the midday duties  
muñi bhikṣā dimu āji mahā-prasādānna  
I  alms  shall give  today  mahā-prasāda

(39) Sārvabhauma said, “Quickly finish Your midday duties. Today I shall offer You mahā-prasāda as alms.”

সমুদ্রস্নান করি’ প্রভু শীঘ্র আইলা ৷  
চরণ পাখালি, প্রভু আসনে বসিলা ॥ ৪০ ॥

samudra-snāna kari’ prabhu śīghra āilā  
in the sea  bath  having taken  the Lord  quickly  came  
caraṇa pākhāli, prabhu āsane vasilā  
the feet  washing  the Lord  on a seat  sat

(40) The Lord bathed in the sea and quickly returned. He washed His feet and sat down on His seat.

বহুত প্রসাদ সার্ব্বভৌম আনাইল ৷  
তবে মহাপ্রভু সুখে ভোজন করিল ॥ ৪১ ॥

bahuta prasāda sārvabhauma ānāila  
much  prasāda  Sārvabhauma  had brought  
tabe mahāprabhu sukhe bhojana karila  
then  Mahāprabhu  happily  eating  did

(41) Sārvabhauma had a great quantity of prasāda brought, and Mahāprabhu happily took His meal.

সুবর্ণ-থালাতে অন্ন উত্তম ব্যঞ্জন ৷  
ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে প্রভু করেন ভোজন ॥ ৪২ ॥

suvarṇa-thālāte anna uttama vyañjana  
on golden plates  rice  excellent  vegetables  
bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge prabhu karena bhojana  
with the devotees  the Lord  does  eating

(42) Rice and excellent vegetable dishes were served on golden plates, and the Lord ate together with His devotees.

সার্ব্বভৌম পরিবেশন করেন আপনে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — মোরে দেহ লাফ্রা-ব্যঞ্জনে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

sārvabhauma pariveśana karena āpane  
Sārvabhauma  serving  does  himself  
prabhu kahe, — more deha lāphrā-vyañjane  
the Lord  says  to Me  give  lāphrā vegetables

(43) Sārvabhauma served them himself. The Lord said, “Give Me the *lāphrā*, the plain mixed vegetables,[^10]

পীঠা-পানা দেহ তুমি ইঁহা-সবাকারে ৷  
সবে ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে যুড়ি’ দুই করে ॥ ৪৪ ॥

pīṭhā-pānā deha tumi im̐hā-sabākāre  
cakes and sweet drinks  give  you  to all these  
sabe bhaṭṭācārya kahe yuḍi’ dui kare  
to all  the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  joining  two  hands

(44) and give the cakes and sweet drinks to all of them.” With folded hands the Bhaṭṭācārya said to them all:

জগন্নাথ কৈছে করিয়াছেন ভোজন ৷  
আজি সব মহাপ্রসাদ কর আস্বাদন ॥ ৪৫ ॥

jagannātha kaiche kariyāchena bhojana  
Jagannātha  as  has done  eating  
āji saba mahāprasāda kara āsvādana  
today  all  mahā-prasāda  do  tasting

(45) “Today you must all relish the mahā-prasāda, just as Jagannātha has eaten it.”

এত বলি’ পীঠা-পানা সব খাওয়াইলা ৷  
ভিক্ষা করাঞা আচমন করাইলা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

eta bali’ pīṭhā-pānā saba khāoyāilā  
so  saying  cakes and sweet drinks  all  he fed  
bhikṣā karāñā ācamana karāilā  
the meal  having given  rinsing of the mouth  he had them do

(46) Saying this, he fed them all the cakes and sweet drinks, and when they had eaten he gave them water to rinse their mouths.

আজ্ঞা মাগি’ গোপীনাথ আচার্য্যকে লঞা ৷  
প্রভুর নিকটে আইলা ভোজন করিয়া ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ājñā māgi’ gopīnātha ācāryake lañā  
permission  asking  Gopīnātha  Ācārya  taking  
prabhura nikaṭe āilā bhojana kariyā  
of the Lord  near  came  eating  having done

(47) Then, taking leave, he went with Gopīnātha Ācārya to eat, and after eating he came back to the Lord.

‘নমো নারায়ণায়’ বলি’ নমস্কার কৈল ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণে মতিরস্তু’ বলি’ গোসাঞি কহিল ॥ ৪৮ ॥

‘namo nārāyaṇāya’ bali’ namaskāra kaila  
namo nārāyaṇāya  saying  obeisance  he made  
‘kṛṣṇe matir astu’ bali’ gosāñi kahila  
on Kṛṣṇa  the mind  may be  saying  Gosāñi  spoke

(48) Saying, “*Namo nārāyaṇāya*”, he bowed down to the Lord, and Gosāñi replied, “*Kṛṣṇe matir astu* — may your mind be fixed on Kṛṣṇa!”[^11]

শুনি’ সার্ব্বভৌম মনে বিচার করিল ৷  
বৈষ্ণব-সন্ন্যাসী ইঁহো, বচনে জানিল ॥ ৪৯ ॥

śuni’ sārvabhauma mane vicāra karila  
hearing  Sārvabhauma  in his mind  consideration  made  
vaiṣṇava-sannyāsī im̐ho, vacane jānila  
a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī  He  by His words  he knew

(49) Hearing this, Sārvabhauma reflected, and from His words he understood that He was a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī.

গোপীনাথ আচার্য্যেরে কহে সার্ব্বভৌম ৷  
গোসাঞির জানিতে চাহি কাঁহা পূর্ব্বাশ্রম ॥ ৫০ ॥

gopīnātha ācāryere kahe sārvabhauma  
to Gopīnātha  Ācārya  says  Sārvabhauma  
gosāñira jānite cāhi kām̐hā pūrvāśrama  
of Gosāñi  to know  I want  what  the former āśrama

(50) Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya, “I would like to know about Gosāñi’s life before He took sannyāsa.”[^12]

গোপীনাথাচার্য্য কহে, — নবদ্বীপে ঘর ৷  
‘জগন্নাথ’ — নাম, পদবী — ‘মিশ্র পুরন্দর’ ॥ ৫১ ॥

gopīnāthācārya kahe, — navadvīpe ghara  
Gopīnātha Ācārya  says  in Navadvīpa  home  
‘jagannātha’ — nāma, padavī — ‘miśra purandara’  
Jagannātha  name  title  Miśra Purandara

(51) Gopīnātha Ācārya said, “His home is in Navadvīpa. There lived a man named Jagannātha, whose title was Miśra Purandara.

‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ — নাম ইঁহার, তাঁর ইঁহো পুত্র ৷  
নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্ত্তীর হয়েন দৌহিত্র ॥ ৫২ ॥

‘viśvambhara’ — nāma im̐hāra, tām̐ra im̐ho putra  
Viśvambhara  name  His  his  He  son  
nīlāmbara cakravartīra hayena dauhitra  
of Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī  He is  the daughter’s son

(52) This is his son, named Viśvambhara. He is also the grandson of Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, his daughter’s son.”

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্ত্তী ৷  
বিশারদের সমাধ্যায়ী, — এই তাঁর খ্যাতি ॥ ৫৩ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe, — nīlāmbara cakravartī  
Sārvabhauma  says  Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī  
viśāradera samādhyāyī, — ei tām̐ra khyāti  
of Viśārada  a fellow student  this  his  fame

(53) Sārvabhauma said, “Nīlāmbara Cakravartī is known as a fellow student of Viśārada.

‘মিশ্র পুরন্দর’ তাঁর মান্য, হেন জানি ৷  
পিতার সম্বন্ধে দোঁহাকে পূজ্য করি’ মানি ॥ ৫৪ ॥

‘miśra purandara’ tām̐ra mānya, hena jāni  
Miśra Purandara  by him  respected  this  I know  
pitāra sambandhe dom̐hāke pūjya kari’ māni  
of my father  through the connection  both  worthy of honour  making  I regard

(54) Miśra Purandara, I know, was honoured by my father, Viśārada, and for his sake I revere them both.”

নদীয়া-সম্বন্ধে সার্ব্বভৌম হৃষ্ট হৈলা ৷  
প্রীত হঞা গোসাঞিরে কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

nadīyā-sambandhe sārvabhauma hṛṣṭa hailā  
with Nadīyā  through the connection  Sārvabhauma  glad  became  
prīta hañā gosāñire kahite lāgilā  
pleased  being  to Gosāñi  to speak  began

(55) Sārvabhauma was glad to find this connection with Nadīyā, and in his pleasure he began to speak to Gosāñi.

সহজেই পূজ্য তুমি, আরে ত’ সন্ন্যাস ৷  
অতএব হঙ তোমার আমি নিজ-দাস ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sahajei pūjya tumi, āre ta’ sannyāsa  
naturally  worthy of worship  You  moreover  indeed  sannyāsa  
ataeva haṅa tomāra āmi nija-dāsa  
therefore  am  Your  I  own servant

(56) “You are worthy of honour by Your very nature, and on top of that You are a sannyāsī. Therefore I am Your own servant.”[^13]

শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু কৈল শ্রীবিষ্ণু স্মরণ ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যে কহে কিছু বিনয়-বচন ॥ ৫৭ ॥

śuni’ mahāprabhu kaila śrī viṣṇu smaraṇa  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  made  of Śrī Viṣṇu  remembrance  
bhaṭṭācārye kahe kichu vinaya-vacana  
to the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  some  humble words

(57) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu remembered Śrī Viṣṇu and spoke some humble words to the Bhaṭṭācārya.[^14]

তুমি জগদ্গুরু — সর্ব্বলোক-হিতকর্ত্তা ৷  
বেদান্ত পড়াও, সন্ন্যাসীর উপকর্ত্তা ॥ ৫৮ ॥

tumi jagad-guru — sarvaloka-hita-kartā  
you  the teacher of the world  of all people  the benefactor  
vedānta paḍāo, sannyāsīra upakartā  
Vedānta  you teach  of sannyāsīs  the helper

(58) “You are the teacher of the whole world and the benefactor of all people. You teach Vedānta, and you are a helper of sannyāsīs.

আমি বালক-সন্ন্যাসী — ভাল-মন্দ নাহি জানি ৷  
তোমার আশ্রয় নিলুঁ, গুরু করি’ মানি ॥ ৫৯ ॥

āmi bālaka-sannyāsī — bhāla-manda nāhi jāni  
I  a boy sannyāsī  good and bad  not  know  
tomāra āśraya nilum̐, guru kari’ māni  
your  shelter  I have taken  guru  making  I regard

(59) I am a young sannyāsī who does not know good from bad. I have taken shelter of you, and I regard you as My Guru.

তোমার সঙ্গ লাগি’ মোর ইঁহা আগমন ৷  
সর্ব্বপ্রকারে করিবে আমায় পালন ॥ ৬০ ॥

tomāra saṅga lāgi’ mora im̐hā āgamana  
your  association  for the sake of  My  here  coming  
sarva-prakāre karibe āmāya pālana  
in every  way  you will  Me  protect

(60) I have come here for your company alone. Please take care of Me in every way.

আজি যে হৈল আমার বড়ই বিপত্তি ৷  
তাহা হৈতে কৈলে তুমি আমার অব্যাহতি ॥ ৬১ ॥

āji ye haila āmāra baḍai vipatti  
today  which  happened  My  great  danger  
tāhā haite kaile tumi āmāra avyāhati  
from that  made  you  My  rescue

(61) Today I fell into great danger, and you rescued Me from it.”[^15]

ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে, — একলে তুমি না যাইহ দর্শনে ৷  
আমার সঙ্গে যাবে, কিম্বা আমার লোক-সনে ॥ ৬২ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya kahe, — ekale tumi nā yāiha darśane  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  alone  You  not  go  for darśana  
āmāra saṅge yābe, kimbā āmāra loka-sane  
me  with  You will go  or  my  men  with

(62) The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Do not go alone for darśana. Go with me or with my men.”

প্রভু কহে, — মন্দির ভিতরে না যাইব ৷  
গরুড়ের পাশে রহি’ দর্শন করিব ॥ ৬৩ ॥

prabhu kahe, — mandira bhitare nā yāiba  
the Lord  says  the temple  inside  not  I shall go  
garuḍera pāśe rahi’ darśana kariba  
of Garuḍa  beside  staying  darśana  I shall have

(63) The Lord said, “I shall not go inside the temple. I shall stand beside the Garuḍa pillar and take darśana from there.”

গোপীনাথাচার্য্যকে কহে সার্ব্বভৌম ৷  
তুমি গোসাঞিরে লঞা করাইহ দর্শন ॥ ৬৪ ॥

gopīnāthācāryake kahe sārvabhauma  
to Gopīnātha Ācārya  says  Sārvabhauma  
tumi gosāñire lañā karāiha darśana  
you  Gosāñi  taking  have Him take  darśana

(64) Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya, “Take Gosāñi for darśana.

আমার মাতৃস্বসা-গৃহ — নির্জ্জন স্থান ৷  
তাঁহা বাসা দেহ, কর সর্ব্ব সমাধান ॥ ৬৫ ॥

āmāra mātṛ-svasā-gṛha — nirjana sthāna  
my  mother’s sister’s house  a secluded  place  
tām̐hā vāsā deha, kara sarva samādhāna  
there  lodging  give  make  all  arrangements

(65) My maternal aunt’s house is a secluded place. Give Him lodging there and see to everything He needs.”

গোপীনাথ প্রভু লঞা তাঁহা বাসা দিল ৷  
জলপাত্র আদি সর্ব্ব সমাধান কৈল ॥ ৬৬ ॥

gopīnātha prabhu lañā tām̐hā vāsā dila  
Gopīnātha  the Lord  taking  there  lodging  gave  
jala-pātra ādi sarva samādhāna kaila  
water pot  and so on  all  arrangements  made

(66) Gopīnātha took the Lord there and gave Him lodging, and he arranged everything for Him — a water pot and all the rest.

আর দিন গোপীনাথ প্রভু স্থানে গিয়া ৷  
শয্যোত্থান দরশন করাইল লঞা ॥ ৬৭ ॥

āra dina gopīnātha prabhu sthāne giyā  
the next  day  Gopīnātha  of the Lord  to the place  going  
śayyotthāna daraśana karāila lañā  
of the rising from bed  darśana  had Him take  taking (Him)

(67) The next day Gopīnātha went to the Lord and took Him to see Jagannātha rise from His bed.

মুকুন্দদত্ত লঞা আইলা সার্ব্বভৌম স্থানে ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম কিছু তাঁরে বলিলা বচনে ॥ ৬৮ ॥

mukunda-datta lañā āilā sārvabhauma sthāne  
Mukunda Datta  taking  came  Sārvabhauma’s  to the place  
sārvabhauma kichu tām̐re balilā vacane  
Sārvabhauma  something  to him  said  in words

(68) Then he came with Mukunda Datta to Sārvabhauma, and the Bhaṭṭācārya said to him:

প্রকৃতি-বিনীত, সন্ন্যাসী দেখিতে সুন্দর ৷  
আমার বহুপ্রীতি বাড়ে ইঁহার উপর ॥ ৬৯ ॥

prakṛti-vinīta, sannyāsī dekhite sundara  
by nature  humble  the sannyāsī  to see  beautiful  
āmāra bahu-prīti bāḍe im̐hāra upara  
my  great love  increases  Him  for

(69) “This sannyāsī is humble by nature and beautiful to see, and my affection for Him keeps growing.[^16]

কোন্ সম্প্রদায়ে সন্ন্যাস কর‌্যাছেন গ্রহণ ৷  
কি নাম ইঁহার, শুনিতে হয় মন ॥ ৭০ ॥

kon sampradāye sannyāsa karyāchena grahaṇa  
in which  sampradāya  sannyāsa  has He made  acceptance  
ki nāma im̐hāra, śunite haya mana  
what  name  His  to hear  there is  desire

(70) In which sampradāya has He taken sannyāsa? And what is His name? I would like to hear.”

গোপীনাথ কহে, — ইঁহার নাম শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
গুরু ইঁহার কেশব-ভারতী মহাধন্য ॥ ৭১ ॥

gopīnātha kahe, — im̐hāra nāma śrī kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
Gopīnātha  says  His  name  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
guru im̐hāra keśava-bhāratī mahā-dhanya  
guru  His  Keśava Bhāratī  greatly  blessed

(71) Gopīnātha said, “His name is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and His guru is the greatly blessed Keśava Bhāratī.”

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — ইঁহার নাম সর্ব্বোত্তম ৷  
ভারতী-সম্প্রদায় এই, — হয়েন মধ্যম ॥ ৭২ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe, — im̐hāra nāma sarvottama  
Sārvabhauma  says  His  name  most excellent  
bhāratī-sampradāya ei, — hayena madhyama  
the Bhāratī sampradāya  this  is  middling

(72) Sārvabhauma said, “His name is excellent, but the Bhāratī sampradāya is of middling rank.”[^17]

গোপীনাথ কহে, — ইঁহার নাহি বাহ্যাপেক্ষা ৷  
অতএব বড় সম্প্রদায়ের নাহিক অপেক্ষা ॥ ৭৩ ॥

gopīnātha kahe, — im̐hāra nāhi bāhyāpekṣā  
Gopīnātha  says  His  there is not  regard for externals  
ataeva baḍa sampradāyera nāhika apekṣā  
therefore  of a great  sampradāya  there is not  need

(73) Gopīnātha said, “He has no regard for externals, so He has no need of a higher sampradāya.”[^18]

ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে, — ইঁহার প্রৌঢ় যৌবন ৷  
কেমনে সন্ন্যাস-ধর্ম্ম হবেক রক্ষণ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya kahe, — im̐hāra prauḍha yauvana  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  His  full  youth  
kemane sannyāsa-dharma habeka rakṣaṇa  
how  of sannyāsa  the duty  will be  kept

(74) The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “He is in the full bloom of youth. How will He keep the vows of sannyāsa?

নিরন্তর ইঁহাকে বেদান্ত শুনাইব ৷  
বৈরাগ্য-অদ্বৈত-মার্গে প্রবেশ করাইব ॥ ৭৫ ॥

nirantara im̐hāke vedānta śunāiba  
constantly  to Him  Vedānta  I shall teach  
vairāgya-advaita-mārge praveśa karāiba  
of renunciation  of non-duality  on the path  entering  I shall cause

(75) I shall teach Him Vedānta constantly and lead Him onto the path of renunciation and non-dualism.[^19]

কহেন যদি, পুনরপি যোগপট্ট দিয়া ৷  
সংস্কার করিয়ে উত্তম-সম্প্রদায়ে আনিয়া ॥ ৭৬ ॥

kahena yadi, punarapi yogapaṭṭa diyā  
He says  if  again  the sannyāsa cloth  giving  
saṁskāra kariye uttama-sampradāye āniyā  
initiation  I shall perform  into the higher  sampradāya  bringing

(76) If He agrees, I shall give Him the sannyāsa cloth (*yoga-paṭṭa*) once again, perform the rites, and bring Him into a higher sampradāya.”[^20]

শুনি’ গোপীনাথ মুকুন্দ, দুঁহে, দুঃখী হৈলা ৷  
গোপীনাথাচার্য্য কিছু কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৭৭ ॥

śuni’ gopīnātha mukunda, dum̐he, duḥkhī hailā  
hearing  Gopīnātha  Mukunda  both  sorrowful  became  
gopīnāthācārya kichu kahite lāgilā  
Gopīnātha Ācārya  something  to say  began

(77) Hearing this, Gopīnātha and Mukunda were both saddened, and Gopīnātha Ācārya began to speak.

ভট্টাচার্য্য, তুমি ইঁহার না জান মহিমা ৷  
ভগবত্তা-লক্ষণের ইঁহাতেই সীমা ॥ ৭৮ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya, tumi im̐hāra nā jāna mahimā  
Bhaṭṭācārya  you  His  not  know  greatness  
bhagavattā-lakṣaṇera im̐hātei sīmā  
of Godhood  of the signs  in Him alone  the limit

(78) “Bhaṭṭācārya, you do not know His greatness. In Him the signs of Godhood reach their utmost limit.

তাহাতে বিখ্যাত ইঁহো পরম-ঈশ্বর ৷  
অজ্ঞ-স্থানে কিছু নহে বিজ্ঞের গোচর ॥ ৭৯ ॥

tāhāte vikhyāta im̐ho parama-īśvara  
therefore  renowned  He  the Supreme Lord  
ajña-sthāne kichu nahe vijñera gocara  
of the ignorant  in the presence  anything  is not  of the wise  perceptible

(79) That is why He is renowned as the Supreme Lord. But what the wise perceive lies beyond the reach of the ignorant.”

শিষ্যগণ কহে, — ঈশ্বর কহ কোন্ প্রমাণে ৷  
আচার্য্য কহে, — বিজ্ঞমত ঈশ্বর-লক্ষণে ॥ ৮০ ॥

śiṣya-gaṇa kahe, — īśvara kaha kon pramāṇe  
the disciples  say  the Lord  you say  by what  proof  
ācārya kahe, — vijña-mata īśvara-lakṣaṇe  
the Ācārya  says  the opinion of the wise  of the Lord  on the signs

(80) The disciples said, “By what proof do you call Him the Lord?” The Ācārya said, “By the signs of the Lord as the wise define them.”[^21]

শিষ্য কহে, — ঈশ্বর-তত্ত্ব সাধি অনুমানে ৷  
আচার্য্য কহে, — অনুমানে নহে ঈশ্বরজ্ঞানে ॥ ৮১ ॥

śiṣya kahe, — īśvara-tattva sādhi anumāne  
a disciple  says  of the Lord  the truth  we establish  by inference  
ācārya kahe, — anumāne nahe īśvarajñāne  
the Ācārya  says  by inference  is not  knowledge of the Lord

(81) A disciple said, “We establish the truth of the Lord by inference.” The Ācārya replied, “The Lord is not known by inference.[^22]

অনুমান প্রমাণ নহে ঈশ্বরতত্ত্ব জ্ঞানে ৷  
কৃপা বিনা ঈশ্বরেরে কেহ নাহি জানে ॥ ৮২ ॥

anumāna pramāṇa nahe īśvara-tattva jñāne  
inference  proof  is not  of the Lord  of the truth  in knowing  
kṛpā vinā īśvarere keha nāhi jāne  
mercy  without  the Lord  anyone  not  knows

(82) Inference is no proof where the truth of the Lord is concerned; without His mercy no one can know Him.

ঈশ্বরের কৃপা-লেশ হয় ত’ যাহারে ৷  
সেই ত’ ঈশ্বর-তত্ত্ব জানিবারে পারে ॥ ৮৩ ॥

īśvarera kṛpā-leśa haya ta’ yāhāre  
of the Lord  of mercy  a particle  there is  indeed  on whom  
sei ta’ īśvara-tattva jānibāre pāre  
he  indeed  of the Lord  the truth  to know  is able

(83) Only one who has received even a particle of the Lord’s mercy can know the truth of the Lord.

অথাপি তে দেব পদাম্বুজদ্বয়-  
প্রসাদ-লেশানুগৃহীত এব হি ৷  
জানাতি তত্ত্বং ভগবন্মহিম্নো  
ন চান্য একোঽপি চিরং বিচিন্বন্ ॥ ৮৪ ॥

athāpi te deva padāmbuja-dvaya-  
yet  Your  O Lord  of lotus feet  of the pair  
prasāda-leśānugṛhīta eva hi  
of the grace  by a trace  favoured  only  indeed  
jānāti tattvaṁ bhagavan-mahimno  
knows  the truth  of Bhagavān’s  greatness  
na cānya eko ’pi ciraṁ vicinvan  
not  and  another  one  even  long  speculating

(84) “O Lord, only one who is favoured with even a trace of the grace of Your two lotus feet can know the truth of Your greatness as Bhagavān; no one else can know it, however long he may search.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.29)[^23]

যদ্যপি জগদ্গুরু তুমি — শাস্ত্র-জ্ঞানবান্ ৷  
পৃথিবীতে নাহি পণ্ডিত তোমার সমান ॥ ৮৫ ॥

ঈশ্বরের কৃপা-লেশ নাহিক তোমাতে ৷  
অতএব ঈশ্বরতত্ত্ব না পার জানিতে ॥ ৮৬ ॥

yadyapi jagad-guru tumi — śāstra-jñānavān  
although  the teacher of the world  you  of scripture  possessing knowledge  
pṛthivīte nāhi paṇḍita tomāra samāna  
on earth  there is not  a scholar  you  equal to  
īśvarera kṛpā-leśa nāhika tomāte  
of the Lord  of mercy  a particle  there is not  on you  
ataeva īśvara-tattva nā pāra jānite  
therefore  of the Lord  the truth  not  you are able  to know

(85–86) Although you are the teacher of the world, learned in the scriptures, and there is no scholar on earth equal to you, not a particle of the Lord’s mercy has come to you. That is why you cannot know the truth of the Lord.[^24]

তোমার নাহিক দোষ, শাস্ত্রে এই কহে ৷  
পাণ্ডিত্যাদ্যে ঈশ্বরতত্ত্ব-জ্ঞান কভু নহে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

tomāra nāhika doṣa, śāstre ei kahe  
your  there is not  fault  in scripture  this  it says  
pāṇḍityādye īśvara-tattva-jñāna kabhu nahe  
by scholarship and the like  of the Lord  of the truth  knowledge  ever  is not

(87) It is not your fault; the scriptures themselves say that the truth of the Lord can never be known by scholarship and the like.”[^25]

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — আচার্য্য, কহ সাবধানে ৷  
তোমাতে ঈশ্বর-কৃপা ইথে কি প্রমাণে ॥ ৮৮ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe, — ācārya, kaha sāvadhāne  
Sārvabhauma  says  Ācārya  speak  carefully  
tomāte īśvara-kṛpā ithe ki pramāṇe  
on you  the Lord’s mercy  for this  what  proof

(88) Sārvabhauma said, “Ācārya, speak with care. What proof is there that the Lord’s mercy is upon you?”[^26]

আচার্য্য কহে, — বস্তুবিষয়ে হয় বস্তু-জ্ঞান ৷  
বস্তুতত্ত্ব-জ্ঞান হয় কৃপাতে, — প্রমাণ ॥ ৮৯ ॥

ācārya kahe, — vastu-viṣaye haya vastu-jñāna  
the Ācārya  says  in regard to the Reality  there is  knowledge of the Reality  
vastu-tattva-jñāna haya kṛpāte, — pramāṇa  
of the Reality  knowledge of the truth  comes  by mercy  the proof

(89) The Ācārya said, “Knowledge of the Reality comes from the Reality Itself: Its truth is known only by Its mercy, and that is the proof.[^27]

ইঁহার শরীরে সব ঈশ্বর-লক্ষণ ৷  
মহা-প্রেমাবেশ তুমি পাঞাছ দর্শন ॥ ৯০ ॥

im̐hāra śarīre saba īśvara-lakṣaṇa  
His  in the body  all  of the Lord  the signs  
mahā-premāveśa tumi pāñācha darśana  
great  absorption in love  you  have got  sight

(90) All the signs of the Lord are present in His body, and you yourself have seen His great absorption in love.

তবু ত’ ঈশ্বর-জ্ঞান না হয় তোমার ৷  
ঈশ্বরের মায়া, — এই বলি ব্যবহার ॥ ৯১ ॥

tabu ta’ īśvara-jñāna nā haya tomāra  
still  indeed  knowledge of the Lord  not  is  yours  
īśvarera māyā, — ei bali vyavahāra  
of the Lord  māyā  this  saying  usage

(91) Even so, you do not know Him as the Lord, and you take all this for the Lord’s māyā, mere worldly behaviour.[^28]

দেখিলে না দেখে তাঁরে বহির্ম্মুখ জন ৷  
শুনি’ হাসি’ সার্ব্বভৌম বলিল বচন ॥ ৯২ ॥

dekhile nā dekhe tām̐re bahirmukha jana  
seeing  not  sees  Him  the averse  person  
śuni’ hāsi’ sārvabhauma balila vacana  
hearing  smiling  Sārvabhauma  spoke  words

(92) A person turned away from God sees Him and yet does not see Him.” Hearing this, Sārvabhauma smiled and said:

ইষ্টগোষ্ঠী বিচার করি, না করিহ রোষ ৷  
শাস্ত্রদৃষ্ট্যে কহি, কিছু না লইহ দোষ ॥ ৯৩ ॥

iṣṭa-goṣṭhī vicāra kari, nā kariha roṣa  
friendly  discussion  consideration  we make  not  do  anger  
śāstra-dṛṣṭye kahi, kichu nā laiha doṣa  
by the vision of scripture  I speak  at all  not  take  offence

(93) “We are only discussing this together in search of the truth; do not be angry. I speak from the standpoint of scripture, so take no offence.[^29]

মহা-ভাগবত হয় চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ৷  
এই কলিকালে বিষ্ণুর অবতার নাই ॥ ৯৪ ॥

mahā-bhāgavata haya caitanya-gosāñi  
a great  devotee  is  Caitanya Gosāñi  
ei kali-kāle viṣṇura avatāra nāi  
in this  age of Kali  Viṣṇu’s  avatāra  there is not

(94) Caitanya Gosāñi is a great devotee, but in this age of Kali there is no avatāra of Viṣṇu.

অতএব ‘ত্রিযুগ’ করি’ কহি বিষ্ণুনাম ৷  
কলিযুগে অবতার নাহি, — শাস্ত্রজ্ঞান ॥ ৯৫ ॥

ataeva ‘tri-yuga’ kari’ kahi viṣṇunāma  
therefore  Triyuga  making  they call  Viṣṇu’s name  
kali-yuge avatāra nāhi, — śāstra-jñāna  
in the Kali-yuga  avatāra  there is not  the knowledge of scripture

(95) That is why Viṣṇu is called Triyuga, ‘He who appears in three ages’. The scriptures teach that there is no avatāra in the Kali-yuga.”[^30]

শুনিয়া আচার্য্য কহে দুঃখী হঞা মনে ৷  
শাস্ত্রজ্ঞ হঞা তুমি কর অভিমানে ॥ ৯৬ ॥

śuniyā ācārya kahe duḥkhī hañā mane  
hearing  the Ācārya  says  sorrowful  being  in mind  
śāstra-jña hañā tumi kara abhimāne  
learned in scripture  being  you  make  pride

(96) Hearing this, the Ācārya grew sad at heart and said, “You are learned in the scriptures, and you are proud of it.

ভাগবত-ভারত দুই — শাস্ত্রের প্রধান ৷  
সেই দুইগ্রন্থ-বাক্যে নাহি অবধান ॥ ৯৭ ॥

bhāgavata-bhārata dui — śāstrera pradhāna  
the Bhāgavata  the Bhārata  the two  of the scriptures  the foremost  
sei dui-grantha-vākye nāhi avadhāna  
of those  two books  to the words  there is not  attention

(97) The *Bhāgavata* and the *Mahābhārata* are the foremost of all scriptures, yet you pay no attention to the words of these two books.[^31]

সেই দুই কহে কলিতে সাক্ষাৎ-অবতার ৷  
তুমি কহ, — কলিতে নাহি বিষ্ণুর প্রচার ॥ ৯৮ ॥

sei dui kahe kalite sākṣāt-avatāra  
those  two  say  in Kali  a direct avatāra  
tumi kaha, — kalite nāhi viṣṇura pracāra  
you  say  in Kali  there is not  of Viṣṇu  appearance

(98) Both of them speak of a direct avatāra in the age of Kali, and yet you say that Viṣṇu does not appear in Kali.

কলিকালে লীলাবতার না করে ভগবান্ ৷  
অতএব ‘ত্রিযুগ’ করি’ কহি তার নাম ॥ ৯৯ ॥

kali-kāle līlāvatāra nā kare bhagavān  
in the age of Kali  a līlā-avatāra  not  makes  Bhagavān  
ataeva ‘tri-yuga’ kari’ kahi tāra nāma  
therefore  Triyuga  making  they call  His  name

(99) It is because in the age of Kali Bhagavān does not appear as a *līlā-avatāra* that He is called Triyuga.[^32]

প্রতিযুগে করেন কৃষ্ণ যুগ-অবতার ৷  
তর্কনিষ্ঠ হৃদয় তোমার নাহিক বিচার ॥ ১০০ ॥

pratiyuge karena kṛṣṇa yuga-avatāra  
in every age  makes  Kṛṣṇa  a yuga-avatāra  
tarka-niṣṭha hṛdaya tomāra nāhika vicāra  
devoted to logic  heart  your  there is not  discernment

(100) But in every age Kṛṣṇa appears as the *yuga-avatāra*. Your heart is given over to logic, and so you have no discernment.

আসন্ বর্ণাস্ত্রয়ো হ্যস্য গৃহ্ণতোঽনুযুগং তনূঃ ৷  
শুক্লো রক্তস্তথা পীত ইদানীং কৃষ্ণতাং গতঃ ॥ ১০১ ॥

āsan varṇās trayo hy asya  
there were  colours  three  indeed  of Him  
gṛhṇato ’nu-yugaṁ tanūḥ  
accepting  in each age  bodies  
śuklo raktas tathā pīta  
white  red  likewise  yellow  
idānīṁ kṛṣṇatāṁ gataḥ  
now  blackness  has attained

(101) “Your son, who accepts a body in every age, has appeared in three colours — white, red and yellow; now He has become dark (*kṛṣṇa*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.13)[^33]

ইতি দ্বাপর উর্ব্বীশ স্তুবন্তি জগদীশ্বরম্ ৷  
নানাতন্ত্রবিধানেন কলাবপি তথা শৃণু ॥ ১০২ ॥

iti dvāpara urv-īśa  
thus  in Dvāpara  O lord of the earth  
stuvanti jagad-īśvaram  
they praise  the Lord of the universe  
nānā-tantra-vidhānena  
of various  tantras  by the rules  
kalāv api tathā śṛṇu  
in Kali  also  how  hear

(102) “Thus, O king, people praise the Lord of the universe in the Dvāpara age. Now hear how they praise Him in the age of Kali as well, following the rules of various tantras.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.31)[^34]

কৃষ্ণবর্ণং ত্বিষাঽকৃষ্ণং সাঙ্গোপাঙ্গাস্ত্রপার্ষদম্ ৷  
যজ্ঞৈঃ সংকীর্ত্তনপ্রায়ৈর্যজন্তি হি সুমেধসঃ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa-varṇaṁ tviṣā ’kṛṣṇaṁ  
Kṛṣṇa  describing (or: with the syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’)  by lustre  not dark  
sāṅgopāṅgāstra-pārṣadam  
with limbs,  sub-limbs,  weapons  and associates  
yajñaiḥ saṁkīrtana-prāyair  
by sacrifices  in which saṅkīrtana  predominates  
yajanti hi su-medhasaḥ  
they worship  indeed  the intelligent

(103) “With sacrifices in which congregational chanting predominates, the wise worship the Lord who ever sings of Kṛṣṇa and whose lustre is not dark. He is always surrounded by His intimate companions (*aṅga*), His devotees (*upāṅga*), His weapons and His associates (*pārṣada*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.32)[^35]

সুবর্ণবর্ণো হেমাঙ্গো বরাঙ্গশ্চন্দনাঙ্গদী ৷  
সন্ন্যাসকৃচ্ছমঃ শান্তো নিষ্ঠা-শান্তি-পরায়ণঃ ॥ ১০৪ ॥

suvarṇa-varṇo hemāṅgo  
of golden  colour  with limbs of gold  
varāṅgaś candanāṅgadī  
with beautiful limbs  wearing sandal armlets  
sannyāsa-kṛc chamaḥ śānto  
sannyāsa  taking  self-restrained  peaceful  
niṣṭhā-śānti-parāyaṇaḥ  
to steadfastness and peace  devoted

(104) “He is of golden colour, His limbs are like gold, His form is beautiful, and He wears armlets of sandal. He has taken sannyāsa, He is self-restrained and tranquil, and His highest refuge is steadfastness and peace.” (Mahābhārata, Dāna-dharma, 149; Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma, 92 and 75)[^36]

তোমার আগে এত কথার নাহি প্রয়োজন ৷  
উষর-ভূমিতে যেন বীজের রোপণ ॥ ১০৫ ॥

tomāra āge eta kathāra nāhi prayojana  
you  before  these  words  there is not  need  
uṣara-bhūmite yena bījera ropaṇa  
on barren  ground  like  of seeds  the sowing

(105) “But there is no use saying all this to you; it is like sowing seeds on barren ground.[^37]

তোমার উপরে তাঁর কৃপা যবে হবে ৷  
এসব সিদ্ধান্ত তবে তুমিহ কহিবে ॥ ১০৬ ॥

tomāra upare tām̐ra kṛpā yabe habe  
on you  His  mercy  when  will be  
esaba siddhānta tabe tumiha kahibe  
these  conclusions  then  you too  will speak

(106) When His mercy comes upon you, then you too will speak these same conclusions.[^38]

তোমার যে শিষ্য কহে কুতর্ক, নানা-বাদ ৷  
ইহার কি দোষ, — এই মায়ার প্রসাদ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

tomāra ye śiṣya kahe kutarka, nānā-vāda  
your  which  disciples  speak  false arguments  various  doctrines  
ihāra ki doṣa, — ei māyāra prasāda  
their  what  fault  this  of māyā  the gift

(107) As for your disciples, who put forward false arguments and all kinds of doctrines — what fault is it of theirs? This is the gift of māyā.

যচ্ছক্তয়ো বদতাং বাদিনাং বৈ,  
বিবাদ-সংবাদ-ভুবো ভবন্তি ৷  
কুর্ব্বন্তি চৈষাং মুহুরাত্মমোহং,  
তস্মৈ নমোঽনন্তগুণায় ভূম্নে ॥ ১০৮ ॥

yac-chaktayo vadatāṁ vādināṁ vai  
whose potencies  of those who speak  of disputants  indeed  
vivāda-saṁvāda-bhuvo bhavanti  
of disagreement  of agreement  the grounds  become  
kurvanti caiṣāṁ muhur ātma-mohaṁ  
they make  and  their  repeatedly  about the self  bewilderment  
tasmai namo ’nanta-guṇāya bhūmne  
to Him  obeisance  of endless qualities  to the Greatest

(108) “I bow down to the Infinite, the Lord of endless qualities, whose potencies become the ground of both agreement and dispute among those who argue, and bewilder them again and again about the self.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 6.4.31)[^39]

যুক্তঞ্চ সন্তি সর্ব্বত্র ভাষন্তে ব্রাহ্মণা যথা ৷  
মায়াং মদীয়ামুদ্গৃহ্য বদতাং কিং নু দুর্ঘটম্ ॥ ১০৯ ॥

yuktaṁ ca santi sarvatra  
reasonable  and  they are present  everywhere  
bhāṣante brāhmaṇā yathā  
speak  the brāhmaṇas  as  
māyāṁ madīyām udgṛhya  
māyā  My  taking up  
vadatāṁ kiṁ nu durghaṭam  
of those who speak  what  indeed  is impossible

(109) “What the brāhmaṇas say is reasonable, for the elements are present in one another everywhere. And when people speak relying on My māyā, what is there they cannot assert?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.22.4)

তবে ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে, যাহ গোসাঞির স্থানে ৷  
আমার নামে গণ-সহিত কর নিমন্ত্রণে ॥ ১১০ ॥

tabe bhaṭṭācārya kahe, yāha gosāñira sthāne  
then  the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  go  to Gosāñi’s  place  
āmāra nāme gaṇa-sahita kara nimantraṇe  
in my  name  with His companions  make  invitation

(110) Then the Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Go to Gosāñi and invite Him in my name, together with His companions.

প্রসাদ আনি’ তাঁরে করাহ আগে ভিক্ষা ৷  
পশ্চাৎ আসি’ আমারে করাইহ শিক্ষা ॥ ১১১ ॥

prasāda āni’ tām̐re karāha āge bhikṣā  
prasāda  bringing  Him  have Him take  first  the meal  
paścāt āsi’ āmāre karāiha śikṣā  
afterwards  coming  me  you may  instruct

(111) Bring Him prasāda and serve Him His meal first. Afterwards come back and teach me.”

আচার্য্য — ভগিনীপতি, শ্যালক — ভট্টাচার্য্য ৷  
নিন্দা-স্তুতি-হাস্যে শিক্ষা করা’ন আচার্য্য ॥ ১১২ ॥

ācārya — bhaginī-pati, śyālaka — bhaṭṭācārya  
the Ācārya  the sister’s husband  the brother-in-law  the Bhaṭṭācārya  
nindā-stuti-hāsye śikṣā karā’na ācārya  
by censure, praise and jest  instruction  gives  the Ācārya

(112) The Ācārya was the husband of the Bhaṭṭācārya’s sister, and so he would instruct his brother-in-law with censure, praise and jest.

আচার্য্যের সিদ্ধান্তে মুকুন্দের হৈল সন্তোষ ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যের বাক্যে মনে হৈল দুঃখ রোষ ॥ ১১৩ ॥

ācāryera siddhānte mukundera haila santoṣa  
of the Ācārya  by the conclusions  of Mukunda  there was  satisfaction  
bhaṭṭācāryera vākye mane haila duḥkha roṣa  
of the Bhaṭṭācārya  by the words  in the mind  there was  sorrow  anger

(113) Mukunda was pleased with the Ācārya’s conclusions, but the Bhaṭṭācārya’s words filled him with sorrow and anger.

গোসাঞির স্থানে আচার্য্য কৈল আগমন ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যের নামে তাঁরে কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১১৪ ॥

gosāñira sthāne ācārya kaila āgamana  
to Gosāñi’s  place  the Ācārya  made  coming  
bhaṭṭācāryera nāme tām̐re kaila nimantraṇa  
in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  name  Him  made  invitation

(114) The Ācārya went to Gosāñi and invited Him in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s name.

মুকুন্দ-সহিত কহে ভট্টাচার্য্যের কথা ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যের নিন্দা করে, মনে পাঞা ব্যথা ॥ ১১৫ ॥

mukunda-sahita kahe bhaṭṭācāryera kathā  
with Mukunda  he tells  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  words  
bhaṭṭācāryera nindā kare, mane pāñā vyathā  
of the Bhaṭṭācārya  censure  he makes  in mind  getting  pain

(115) Together with Mukunda he told the Lord what the Bhaṭṭācārya had said, and, pained at heart, he spoke critically of the Bhaṭṭācārya.

শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু কহে ঐছে মৎ কহ ৷  
আমা প্রতি ভট্টাচার্য্যের হয় অনুগ্রহ ॥ ১১৬ ॥

śuni’ mahāprabhu kahe aiche mat kaha  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  says  so  do not  speak  
āmā prati bhaṭṭācāryera haya anugraha  
Me  towards  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  is  kindness

(116) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu said, “Do not speak like that. The Bhaṭṭācārya is kind to Me.

আমার সন্ন্যাস-ধর্ম্ম চাহেন রাখিতে ৷  
বাৎসল্যে করুণা করেন, কি দোষ ইহাতে ॥ ১১৭ ॥

āmāra sannyāsa-dharma cāhena rākhite  
My  of sannyāsa  the duty  he wants  to protect  
vātsalye karuṇā karena, ki doṣa ihāte  
in parental affection  mercy  he shows  what  fault  in this

(117) He wants to protect My vows of sannyāsa, and out of fatherly affection he shows Me compassion. What fault is there in that?”

আর দিন মহাপ্রভু ভট্টাচার্য্য-সনে ৷  
আনন্দে করিলা জগন্নাথ দরশনে ॥ ১১৮ ॥

āra dina mahāprabhu bhaṭṭācārya-sane  
the next  day  Mahāprabhu  the Bhaṭṭācārya  with  
ānande karilā jagannātha daraśane  
joyfully  made  of Jagannātha  darśana

(118) The next day Mahāprabhu joyfully took darśana of Jagannātha together with the Bhaṭṭācārya.

ভট্টাচার্য্য-সঙ্গে তাঁর মন্দিরে আইলা ৷  
প্রভুরে আসন দিয়া আপনে বসিলা ॥ ১১৯ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya-saṅge tām̐ra mandire āilā  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  together with  his  to the house  He came  
prabhure āsana diyā āpane vasilā  
to the Lord  a seat  giving  himself  sat

(119) From there they went together to the Bhaṭṭācārya’s house, where he offered the Lord a seat and then sat down himself.[^40]

বেদান্ত পড়াইতে তবে আরম্ভ করিলা ৷  
স্নেহ-ভক্তি করি’ কিছু প্রভুরে কহিলা ॥ ১২০ ॥

vedānta paḍāite tabe ārambha karilā  
Vedānta  to teach  then  beginning  he made  
sneha-bhakti kari’ kichu prabhure kahilā  
affection and devotion  showing  something  to the Lord  he said

(120) Then he began to teach Vedānta, and with affection and reverence he said to the Lord:

বেদান্ত-শ্রবণ, — এই সন্ন্যাসীর ধর্ম্ম ৷  
নিরন্তর কর তুমি বেদান্ত শ্রবণ ॥ ১২১ ॥

vedānta-śravaṇa, — ei sannyāsīra dharma  
hearing Vedānta  this  of a sannyāsī  the duty  
nirantara kara tumi vedānta śravaṇa  
constantly  do  You  of Vedānta  hearing

(121) “To hear Vedānta is the duty of a sannyāsī. You should hear Vedānta constantly.”[^41]

প্রভু কহে, — মোরে তুমি কর অনুগ্রহ ৷  
সেই সে কর্ত্তব্য, তুমি যেই মোরে কহ ॥ ১২২ ॥

prabhu kahe, — more tumi kara anugraha  
the Lord  says  to Me  you  show  kindness  
sei se kartavya, tumi yei more kaha  
that  alone  the duty  you  whatever  to Me  say

(122) The Lord said, “You are kind to Me. Whatever you tell Me, that is My duty.”

সপ্তদিন পর্য্যন্ত ঐছে করেন শ্রবণে ৷  
ভাল-মন্দ নাহি কহে, বসি’ মাত্র শুনে ॥ ১২৩ ॥

saptadina paryanta aiche karena śravaṇe  
seven days  for  thus  He does  hearing  
bhāla-manda nāhi kahe, vasi’ mātra śune  
good or bad  not  He says  sitting  only  He listens

(123) For seven days He listened in this way. He said nothing, good or bad, but only sat and listened.[^42]

অষ্টম-দিবসে তাঁরে পুছে সার্ব্বভৌম ৷  
সাত দিন কর তুমি বেদান্ত শ্রবণ ॥ ১২৪ ॥

aṣṭama-divase tām̐re puche sārvabhauma  
on the eighth  day  Him  asks  Sārvabhauma  
sāta dina kara tumi vedānta śravaṇa  
seven  days  do  You  of Vedānta  hearing

(124) On the eighth day Sārvabhauma asked Him, “For seven days You have been hearing Vedānta.

ভালমন্দ নাহি কহ, রহ মৌন ধরি’ ৷  
বুঝ, কি না বুঝ, — ইহা জানিতে না পারি ॥ ১২৫ ॥

bhāla-manda nāhi kaha, raha mauna dhari’  
good or bad  not  You say  You remain  silence  keeping  
bujha, ki nā bujha, — ihā jānite nā pāri  
You understand  or  not  You understand  this  to know  not  I can

(125) Yet You say nothing, good or bad, but remain silent, and I cannot tell whether You understand or not.”

প্রভু কহে, — মূর্খ আমি, নাহি অধ্যয়ন ৷  
তোমার আজ্ঞাতে মাত্র করিয়ে শ্রবণ ॥ ১২৬ ॥

prabhu kahe, — mūrkha āmi, nāhi adhyayana  
the Lord  says  a fool  I  there is not  study  
tomāra ājñāte mātra kariye śravaṇa  
your  by the order  only  I do  hearing

(126) The Lord said, “I am a fool and have never studied. I listen only because you tell Me to.

সন্ন্যাসীর ধর্ম্ম লাগি’ শ্রবণ মাত্র করি ৷  
তুমি যেই অর্থ কর, বুঝিতে না পারি ॥ ১২৭ ॥

sannyāsīra dharma lāgi’ śravaṇa mātra kari  
of a sannyāsī  the duty  for the sake of  hearing  only  I do  
tumi yei artha kara, bujhite nā pāri  
you  whatever  meaning  give  to understand  not  I can

(127) I listen simply as a sannyāsī’s duty requires, but I cannot understand the meaning you give.”

ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে, — ‘না বুঝি’, হেন জ্ঞান যার ৷  
বুঝিবার লাগি’ সেহ পুছে পুনর্ব্বার ॥ ১২৮ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya kahe, — ‘nā bujhi’, hena jñāna yāra  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  not  I understand  such  awareness  whose  
bujhibāra lāgi’ seha puche punarbāra  
to understand  for the sake of  he  asks  again

(128) The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “One who knows that he does not understand asks again in order to understand.

তুমি শুনি’ শুনি’ রহ মৌন মাত্র ধরি’ ৷  
হৃদয়ে কি আছে তোমার, বুঝিতে না পারি ॥ ১২৯ ॥

tumi śuni’ śuni’ raha mauna mātra dhari’  
You  hearing  hearing  remain  silence  only  keeping  
hṛdaye ki āche tomāra, bujhite nā pāri  
in the heart  what  is  Your  to understand  not  I can

(129) But You listen and listen and only keep silent. I cannot tell what is in Your heart.”

প্রভু কহে, — সূত্রের অর্থ বুঝিয়ে নির্ম্মল ৷  
তোমার ব্যাখ্যা শুনি’ মন হয় ত’ বিকল ॥ ১৩০ ॥

prabhu kahe, — sūtrera artha bujhiye nirmala  
the Lord  says  of the sūtras  the meaning  I understand  clear  
tomāra vyākhyā śuni’ mana haya ta’ vikala  
your  explanation  hearing  the mind  becomes  indeed  agitated

(130) The Lord said, “The meaning of the sūtras is clear to Me, but when I hear your explanation, My mind is troubled.

সূত্রের অর্থ ভাষ্য কহে প্রকাশিয়া ৷  
তুমি, ভাষ্য কহ — সূত্রের অর্থ আচ্ছাদিয়া ॥ ১৩১ ॥

sūtrera artha bhāṣya kahe prakāśiyā  
of the sūtra  the meaning  the commentary  speaks  revealing  
tumi, bhāṣya kaha — sūtrera artha ācchādiyā  
you  commentary  speak  of the sūtra  the meaning  covering

(131) A commentary (*bhāṣya*) should bring the meaning of a sūtra to light, but your commentary only covers it.

সূত্রের মুখ্য অর্থ না করহ ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
কল্পনার্থে তুমি তাহা কর আচ্ছাদন ॥ ১৩২ ॥

sūtrera mukhya artha nā karaha vyākhyāna  
of the sūtras  the primary  meaning  not  you do  explanation  
kalpanārthe tumi tāhā kara ācchādana  
by imagined meanings  you  it  do  covering

(132) You do not explain the primary meaning of the sūtras; you cover it with meanings of your own imagining.[^43]

উপনিষদ্-শব্দে যেই মুখ্য অর্থ হয় ৷  
সেই অর্থ মুখ্য, — ব্যাসসূত্রে সব কয় ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

upaniṣad-śabde yei mukhya artha haya  
of the Upaniṣads  in the words  which  primary  meaning  is  
sei artha mukhya, — vyāsa-sūtre saba kaya  
that  meaning  primary  in Vyāsa’s sūtras  all  is said

(133) Whatever is the primary meaning of the words of the Upaniṣads, that is the principal meaning, and Vyāsa’s sūtras state it all.

মুখ্যার্থ ছাড়িয়া কর গৌণার্থ কল্পনা ৷  
‘অভিধা’-বৃত্তি ছাড়ি’ কর শব্দের ‘লক্ষণা’ ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

mukhyārtha chāḍiyā kara gauṇārtha kalpanā  
the primary meaning  abandoning  you make  of a secondary meaning  invention  
‘abhidhā’-vṛtti chāḍi’ kara śabdera ‘lakṣaṇā’  
abhidhā  function  giving up  you make  of words  lakṣaṇā

(134) Abandoning the primary meaning, you invent secondary meanings. Giving up the direct sense of words (*abhidhā*), you read them indirectly (*lakṣaṇā*).[^44]

প্রমাণের মধ্যে শ্রুতি প্রমাণ — প্রধান ৷  
শ্রুতি যে মুখ্যার্থ কহে, সেই সে প্রমাণ ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

pramāṇera madhye śruti pramāṇa — pradhāna  
of proofs  among  śruti  proof  the chief  
śruti ye mukhyārtha kahe, sei se pramāṇa  
śruti  what  primary meaning  speaks  that  alone  proof

(135) Among all proofs the śruti is the chief, and what the śruti says in its primary meaning — that alone is proof.[^45]

জীবের অস্থি-বিষ্ঠা দুই, — শঙ্খ-গোময় ৷  
শ্রুতি-বাক্যে সেই দুই মহা-পবিত্র হয় ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

jīvera asthi-viṣṭhā dui, — śaṅkha-gomaya  
of a living being  bone and dung  the two  conch and cow dung  
śruti-vākye sei dui mahā-pavitra haya  
by the words of śruti  those  two  very pure  are

(136) Conchshell and cow dung are the bone and the dung of living beings, yet by the word of the śruti these two are supremely pure.

স্বতঃপ্রমাণ বেদ সত্য যেই কয় ৷  
‘লক্ষণা’ করিলে স্বতঃপ্রামাণ্য-হানি হয় ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

svataḥ-pramāṇa veda satya yei kaya  
self-evident  the Vedas  truth  whatever  speak  
‘lakṣaṇā’ karile svataḥ-prāmāṇya-hāni haya  
lakṣaṇā  if one makes  of self-evidence  loss  occurs

(137) The Vedas are self-evident, and what they say is truth. If they are read indirectly (*lakṣaṇā*), their self-evident authority is lost.[^46]

ব্যাস-সূত্রের অর্থ — যৈছে সূর্য্যের কিরণ ৷  
স্বকল্পিত ভাষ্য-মেঘে করে আচ্ছাদন ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

vyāsa-sūtrera artha — yaiche sūryera kiraṇa  
of Vyāsa’s sūtras  the meaning  like  of the sun  the rays  
sva-kalpita bhāṣya-meghe kare ācchādana  
self-imagined  by clouds of commentary  one makes  covering

(138) The meaning of Vyāsa’s sūtras is like the rays of the sun, and you cover it with the clouds of a commentary of your own invention.

বেদ-পুরাণে কহে ব্রহ্ম-নিরূপণ ৷  
সেই ব্রহ্ম — বৃহদ্বস্তু, ঈশ্বর-লক্ষণ ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

veda-purāṇe kahe brahma-nirūpaṇa  
in the Vedas and Purāṇas  they speak  of Brahman  the description  
sei brahma — bṛhad-vastu, īśvara-lakṣaṇa  
that  Brahman  the Great Reality  of the Lord  the signs

(139) The Vedas and Purāṇas describe Brahman, and that Brahman is the Great Reality (*bṛhad-vastu*), bearing all the signs of the Lord.

সর্ব্বৈশ্বর্য্যপরিপূর্ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তাঁরে নিরাকার করি’ করহ ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ১৪০ ॥

sarvaiśvarya-paripūrṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
full of all opulences  Himself  Bhagavān  
tām̐re nirākāra kari’ karaha vyākhyāna  
Him  formless  making  you do  explanation

(140) He is Svayaṁ Bhagavān, full of all opulences, yet you explain Him as formless.[^47]

‘নির্ব্বিশেষ’ তাঁরে কহে যেই শ্রুতিগণ ৷  
‘প্রাকৃত’ নিষেধি করে ‘অপ্রাকৃত’ স্থাপন ॥ ১৪১ ॥

‘nirviśeṣa’ tām̐re kahe yei śruti-gaṇa  
featureless  Him  call  which  the śrutis  
‘prākṛta’ niṣedhi kare ‘aprākṛta’ sthāpana  
the material  rejecting  they make  of the non-material  establishment

(141) Those śrutis that call Him featureless (*nirviśeṣa*) reject what is material and establish what is beyond matter.

যা যা শ্রুতির্জল্পতি নির্ব্বিশেষং সা সাভিধত্তে সবিশেষমেব ৷  
বিচারযোগে সতি হন্ত তাসাং প্রায়ো বলীয়ঃ সবিশেষমেব ॥ ১৪২ ॥

yā yā śrutir jalpati nirviśeṣaṁ  
whichever  whichever  śruti  speaks  of the featureless  
sā sābhidhatte sa-viśeṣam eva  
that  that denotes  the one with features  only  
vicāra-yoge sati hanta tāsāṁ  
of reflection  on application  being  indeed  of them  
prāyo balīyaḥ sa-viśeṣam eva  
as a rule  stronger  the one with features  alone

(142) “Whatever śruti speaks of the featureless directly denotes Him who has features; and when such texts are carefully examined, the sense ‘with features’ almost always proves the stronger.” (Hayaśīrṣa-pañcarātra, quoted in the Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 6.21)[^48]

ব্রহ্ম হৈতে জন্মে বিশ্ব, ব্রহ্মেতে জীবয় ৷  
সেই ব্রহ্মে পুনরপি হয়ে যায় লয় ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

brahma haite janme viśva, brahmete jīvaya  
from Brahman  is born  the universe  in Brahman  it lives  
sei brahme punarapi haye yāya laya  
into that  Brahman  again  becoming  it goes  dissolution

(143) The universe is born from Brahman and lives in Brahman, and into that same Brahman it dissolves again.[^49]

‘অপাদান’, ‘করণ’, ‘অধিকরণ’-কারক তিন ৷  
ভগবানের সবিশেষে এই তিন চিহ্ন ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

‘apādāna’, ‘karaṇa’, ‘adhikaraṇa’-kāraka tina  
“apādāna” (source)  “karaṇa” (instrument)  “adhikaraṇa” (locus) — cases  three  
bhagavānera saviśeṣe ei tina cihna  
of Bhagavān  in having features  these  three  signs

(144) Source, instrument and abode — these three cases (*apādāna*, *karaṇa* and *adhikaraṇa*) are the three signs that Bhagavān has features.[^50]

ভগবান্ অনেক হৈতে যবে কৈল মন ৷  
প্রাকৃত-শক্তিতে তবে কৈল বিলোকন ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

bhagavān aneka haite yabe kaila mana  
the Lord  many  to become  when  He made  His mind  
prākṛta-śaktite tabe kaila vilokana  
upon the material energy  then  He cast  a glance

(145) When Bhagavān resolved to become many, He cast His glance upon the material energy.

সে কালে নাহি জন্মে ‘প্রাকৃত’ মন-নয়ন ৷  
অতএব ‘অপ্রাকৃত’ ব্রহ্মের নেত্র-মন ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

se kāle nāhi janme ‘prākṛta’ mana-nayana  
at that  time  not  are born  material  mind and eyes  
ataeva ‘aprākṛta’ brahmera netra-mana  
therefore  non-material  of Brahman  the eyes and mind

(146) At that time no material mind or eyes had yet come into being; therefore the eyes and mind of Brahman are not material.[^51]

ব্রহ্ম-শব্দে কহে পূর্ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ, — শাস্ত্রের প্রমাণ ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

brahma-śabde kahe pūrṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
by the word “Brahman”  is meant  the complete  Svayaṁ  Bhagavān  
svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa, — śāstrera pramāṇa  
Svayaṁ  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of scripture  the evidence

(147) The word “Brahman” denotes the complete Svayaṁ Bhagavān, and Svayaṁ Bhagavān is Kṛṣṇa — such is the testimony of scripture.

বেদের নিগূঢ় অর্থ বুঝন না হয় ৷  
পুরাণ-বাক্যে সেই অর্থ করয় নিশ্চয় ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

vedera nigūḍha artha bujhana nā haya  
of the Vedas  the hidden  meaning  understanding  not  is  
purāṇa-vākye sei artha karaya niścaya  
by the words of the Purāṇas  that  meaning  is made  certain

(148) The hidden meaning of the Vedas is not easily understood, and the words of the Purāṇas make that meaning certain.

অহো ভাগ্যমহো ভাগ্যং নন্দগোপব্রজৌকসাম্ ৷  
যন্মিত্রং পরমানন্দং পূর্ণং ব্রহ্ম সনাতনম্ ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

aho bhāgyam aho bhāgyaṁ  
oh  the fortune  oh  the fortune  
nanda-gopa-vrajaukasām  
of Nanda  the cowherds  the residents of Vraja  
yan-mitraṁ paramānandaṁ  
whose friend  the supreme bliss  
pūrṇaṁ brahma sanātanam  
the complete  Brahman  eternal

(149) “Oh, the fortune, oh, the fortune of Nanda, the cowherds and all the residents of Vraja, whose friend is the supreme bliss, the complete and eternal Brahman!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.32)[^52]

‘অপাণি-পাদ’-শ্রুতি বর্জ্জে ‘প্রাকৃত’ পাণি-চরণ ৷  
পুনঃ কহে, — শীঘ্র চলে, করে সর্ব্ব গ্রহণ ॥ ১৫০ ॥

‘apāṇi-pāda’-śruti varje ‘prākṛta’ pāṇi-caraṇa  
the “apāṇi-pāda” śruti  rejects  material  hands and feet  
punaḥ kahe, — śīghra cale, kare sarva grahaṇa  
again  it says  swiftly  He moves  He makes  of everything  acceptance

(150) The śruti text “apāṇi-pāda” (“without hands and feet”) denies Him material hands and feet; yet it says again that He moves swiftly and accepts all things.

অতএব শ্রুতি কহে, ব্রহ্ম — সবিশেষ ৷  
‘মুখ্য’ ছাড়ি’ ‘লক্ষণা’তে মানে নির্ব্বিশেষ ॥ ১৫১ ॥

ataeva śruti kahe, brahma — saviśeṣa  
therefore  the śruti  says  Brahman  with features  
‘mukhya’ chāḍi’ ‘lakṣaṇā’te māne nirviśeṣa  
the primary meaning  abandoning  by the indirect reading  they take  as featureless

(151) Therefore the śruti declares that Brahman has features; but the Māyāvādīs abandon the primary meaning and, by an indirect reading, hold Him to be featureless.[^53]

ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যপূর্ণানন্দ-বিগ্রহ যাঁহার ৷  
হেন-ভগবানে তুমি কহ নিরাকার ॥ ১৫২ ॥

ṣaḍaiśvaryapūrṇānanda-vigraha yām̐hāra  
full of the six opulences, of bliss  the form  whose  
hena-bhagavāne tumi kaha nirākāra  
such a Bhagavān  you  call  formless

(152) His form is bliss itself, full of the six opulences — and such a Bhagavān you call formless!

স্বাভাবিক তিন শক্তি যেই ব্রহ্মে হয় ৷  
‘নিঃশক্তিক’ করি’ তাঁরে করহ নিশ্চয় ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

svābhāvika tina śakti yei brahme haya  
natural  three  potencies  which  in Brahman  are  
‘niḥśaktika’ kari’ tām̐re karaha niścaya  
“without potency”  making  Him  you make  your conclusion

(153) Brahman has three natural potencies, yet you conclude that He has no potency at all.[^54]

বিষ্ণুশক্তিঃ পরা প্রোক্তা ক্ষেত্রজ্ঞাখ্যা তথাপরা ৷  
অবিদ্যা কর্ম্মসংজ্ঞান্যা তৃতীয়া শক্তিরিষ্যতে ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

viṣṇu-śaktiḥ parā proktā  
Viṣṇu’s potency  superior  is declared  
kṣetra-jñākhyā tathāparā  
called the knower of the field  likewise superior  
avidyā karma-saṁjñānyā  
called ignorance and karma  another  
tṛtīyā śaktir iṣyate  
a third  potency  is held to be

(154) “The potency of Viṣṇu is declared to be superior; the potency called the knower of the field (*kṣetrajña*) is likewise superior; and the other, called ignorance and karma, is held to be a third potency.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 6.7.61)[^55]

যয়া ক্ষেত্রজ্ঞশক্তিঃ সা বেষ্টিতা নৃপ সর্ব্বগা ৷  
সংসারতাপানখিলানবাপ্নোত্যত্র সন্ততান্ ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

yayā kṣetra-jña-śaktiḥ sā  
by which  the knower of the field  potency  that  
veṣṭitā nṛpa sarva-gā  
enveloped  O king  all-pervading  
saṁsāra-tāpān akhilān  
of worldly existence  the miseries  all  
avāpnoty atra santatān  
obtains  here  continual

(155) “O king, the potency called the knower of the field can enter both spirit and matter; yet here, enveloped by that ignorance, it suffers without end all the miseries of worldly existence.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 6.7.62)[^56]

তয়া তিরোহিতত্বাচ্চ শক্তিঃ ক্ষেত্রজ্ঞসংজ্ঞিতা ৷  
সর্ব্বভূতেষু ভূপাল তারতম্যেন বর্ত্ততে ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

tayā tirohitatvāc ca  
by it  because of being covered  and  
śaktiḥ kṣetra-jña-saṁjñitā  
the potency  the knower of the field  called  
sarva-bhūteṣu bhū-pāla  
in all beings  O king  
tāratamyena vartate  
in varying degrees  exists

(156) “Because it is covered by that ignorance, O king, the potency called the knower of the field exists in all beings in varying degrees.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 6.7.63)[^57]

হ্লাদিনী সন্ধিনী সম্বিৎ ত্বয়্যেকা সর্ব্বসংশ্রয়ে ৷  
হ্লাদতাপকরী মিশ্রা ত্বয়ি নো গুণ-বর্জ্জিতে ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

hlādinī sandhinī samvit  
hlādinī  sandhinī  samvit  
tvayy ekā sarva-saṁśraye  
in You  one  of all  the shelter  
hlāda-tāpa-karī miśrā  
of joy  of pain  causing  mixed  
tvayi no guṇa-varjite  
in You  not  of the guṇas  devoid

(157) “O Lord, You are the basis of all, and in You Hlādinī, Sandhinī and Samvit are one potency. But that which causes joy, pain and a mixture of the two is not in You, for You are beyond the guṇas.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 1.12.69)[^58]

সচ্চিদানন্দময় হয় ঈশ্বর-স্বরূপ ৷  
তিন অংশে চিচ্ছক্তি হয় তিন রূপ ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

sac-cid-ānanda-maya haya īśvara-svarūpa  
made of eternity, knowledge and bliss  is  the Lord’s  own nature  
tina aṁśe cic-chakti haya tina rūpa  
in three  aspects  the spiritual potency  is  three  forms

(158) The Lord’s own nature is made of eternity, knowledge and bliss, and in these three aspects His spiritual potency takes three forms.

আনন্দাংশে ‘হ্লাদিনী’, সদংশে ‘সন্ধিনী’ ৷  
চিদংশে ‘সম্বিৎ’, যারে কৃষ্ণজ্ঞান মানি ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

ānandāṁśe ‘hlādinī’, sad-aṁśe ‘sandhinī’  
in the aspect of bliss  hlādinī  in the aspect of being  sandhinī  
cid-aṁśe ‘samvit’, yāre kṛṣṇa-jñāna māni  
in the aspect of consciousness  samvit  which  knowledge of Kṛṣṇa  we hold

(159) In the aspect of bliss She is Hlādinī, in the aspect of being Sandhinī, and in the aspect of consciousness Samvit, which we regard as knowledge of Kṛṣṇa.[^59]

অন্তরঙ্গা — চিচ্ছক্তি, তটস্থা — জীবশক্তি ৷  
বহিরঙ্গা — মায়া, — তিনে করে প্রেমভক্তি ॥ ১৬০ ॥

antaraṅgā — cic-chakti, taṭasthā — jīva-śakti  
internal  the spiritual potency  marginal  the potency of the jīvas  
bahiraṅgā — māyā, — tine kare prema-bhakti  
external  māyā  all three  render  loving devotion

(160) The spiritual potency is internal, the potency of the jīvas is marginal, and māyā is external — and all three render loving service.

ষড়্বিধ ঐশ্বর্য্য — প্রভুর চিচ্ছক্তি বিলাস ৷  
হেন শক্তি নাহি মান, — পরম সাহস ॥ ১৬১ ॥

ṣaḍ-vidha aiśvarya — prabhura cic-chakti vilāsa  
of six  kinds  opulence  the Lord’s  spiritual potency’s  play  
hena śakti nāhi māna, — parama sāhasa  
such  potency  not  you accept  utmost  audacity

(161) The six kinds of opulence are the play of the Lord’s spiritual potency. That you do not accept such a potency is the height of audacity.

‘মায়াধীশ’-‘মায়াবশ’ ঈশ্বরে জীবে ভেদ ৷  
হেন-জীবে ঈশ্বর-সহ কহ ত’ অভেদ ॥ ১৬২ ॥

‘māyādhīśa’-‘māyāvaśa’ īśvare jīve bheda  
“the master of māyā”  “subject to māyā”  between the Lord  and the jīva  the difference  
hena-jīve īśvara-saha kaha ta’ abheda  
such a jīva  with the Lord  you call  indeed  non-different

(162) The Lord is the master of māyā, and the jīva is subject to māyā — this is the difference between them. Yet you call such a jīva one with the Lord.

গীতাশাস্ত্রে জীবরূপ ‘শক্তি’ করি’ মানে ৷  
হেন জীবে ‘অভেদ’ কহ-ঈশ্বরের সনে ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

gītā-śāstre jīva-rūpa ‘śakti’ kari’ māne  
in the Gītā  the jīva  as  a “potency”  is accepted  
hena jīve ‘abheda’ kaha-īśvarera sane  
such  a jīva  “non-different”  you call  from the Lord  with

(163) In the Gītā the jīva is accepted as a potency, yet you call such a jīva non-different from the Lord.[^60]

ভূমিরাপোঽনলো বায়ুঃ খং মনো বুদ্ধিরেব চ ৷  
অহংকার ইতীয়ং মে ভিন্না প্রকৃতিরষ্টধা ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

bhūmir āpo ’nalo vāyuḥ  
earth  water  fire  air  
khaṁ mano buddhir eva ca  
ether  mind  intelligence  indeed  and  
ahaṁkāra itīyaṁ me  
false ego  thus this  My  
bhinnā prakṛtir aṣṭadhā  
separated  nature  eightfold

(164) “Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intelligence and false ego — these eight make up My separated nature.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.4)[^61]

অপরেয়মিতস্ত্বন্যাং প্রকৃতিং বিদ্ধি মে পরাম্ ৷  
জীবভূতাং মহাবাহো যয়েদং ধার্য্যতে জগৎ ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

apareyam itas tv anyāṁ  
inferior  this  beyond this  but  another  
prakṛtiṁ viddhi me parām  
nature  know  My  superior  
jīva-bhūtāṁ mahā-bāho  
consisting of jīvas  O mighty-armed  
yayedaṁ dhāryate jagat  
by which  this  is taken up  the world

(165) “This nature is inferior. But know, O mighty-armed, that I have another, superior nature, consisting of the jīvas, who take up this world for their own enjoyment.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.5)[^62]

ঈশ্বরের শ্রীবিগ্রহ সচ্চিদানন্দাকার ৷  
সে-বিগ্রহে কহ সত্ত্বগুণের বিকার ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

īśvarera śrī vigraha sac-cid-ānandākāra  
the Lord’s  sacred  form  of eternity, knowledge and bliss in shape  
se-vigrahe kaha sattva-guṇera vikāra  
that form  you call  of the mode of goodness  a transformation

(166) The Lord’s sacred form is made of eternity, knowledge and bliss, yet you call that form a transformation of the mode of goodness.

শ্রীবিগ্রহ যে না মানে, সেই ত’ পাষণ্ড ৷  
অস্পৃশ্য, অদৃশ্য, সেই হয় যমদণ্ড্য ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

śrī vigraha ye nā māne, sei ta’ pāṣaṇḍa  
the sacred  form  who  not  accepts  he  indeed  godless  
aspṛśya, adṛśya, sei haya yama-daṇḍya  
untouchable  not to be looked at  he  becomes  punishable by Yama

(167) Whoever does not accept the Lord’s sacred form is godless (*pāṣaṇḍī*). He is not to be touched or even looked at, and Yama will punish him.[^63]

বেদ না মানিয়া বৌদ্ধ হয় ত’ নাস্তিক ৷  
বেদাশ্রয় নাস্তিক্য-বাদ বৌদ্ধকে অধিক ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

veda nā māniyā bauddha haya ta’ nāstika  
the Vedas  not  accepting  the Buddhist  is  indeed  an unbeliever  
vedāśraya nāstikya-vāda bauddhake adhika  
sheltered in the Vedas  atheistic doctrine  than the Buddhist  worse

(168) The Buddhist, who does not accept the Vedas, is an unbeliever (*nāstika*); but an atheistic doctrine that takes shelter in the Vedas is worse than Buddhism.[^64]

জীবের নিস্তার লাগি’ সূত্র কৈল ব্যাস ৷  
মায়াবাদি-ভাষ্য শুনিলে হয় সর্ব্বনাশ ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

jīvera nistāra lāgi’ sūtra kaila vyāsa  
of the jīvas  the deliverance  for  the sūtras  composed  Vyāsa  
māyāvādi-bhāṣya śunile haya sarva-nāśa  
the Māyāvādī commentary  if one hears  there is  utter ruin

(169) Vyāsa composed the sūtras for the deliverance of the jīvas, but whoever hears the Māyāvādī commentary on them is utterly ruined.

‘পরিণাম-বাদ’ ব্যাসসূত্রের সম্মত ৷  
অচিন্ত্যশক্তি ঈশ্বর জগদ্রূপে পরিণত ॥ ১৭০ ॥

‘pariṇāma-vāda’ vyāsasūtrera sammata  
the doctrine of transformation  by Vyāsa’s sūtras  is accepted  
acintya-śakti īśvara jagad-rūpe pariṇata  
of inconceivable potency  the Lord  in the form of the world  is transformed

(170) Vyāsa’s sūtras accept the doctrine of transformation (*pariṇāma-vāda*): by His inconceivable potency the Lord takes the form of the world.[^65]

মণি যৈছে অবিকৃতে প্রসবে হেমভার ৷  
জগদ্রূপ হয় ঈশ্বর, তবু অবিকার ॥ ১৭১ ॥

maṇi yaiche avikṛte prasabe hema-bhāra  
a gem  as  unchanged  brings forth  heaps of gold  
jagad-rūpa haya īśvara, tabu avikāra  
the form of the world  becomes  the Lord  still  unchanged

(171) As the *cintāmaṇi* gem, itself unchanged, brings forth heaps of gold, so the Lord becomes the world and yet remains unchanged.[^66]

ব্যাস — ভ্রান্ত বলি’ সেই সূত্রে দোষ দিয়া ৷  
‘বিবর্ত্তবাদ’ স্থাপিয়াছে কল্পনা করিয়া ॥ ১৭২ ॥

vyāsa — bhrānta bali’ sei sūtre doṣa diyā  
Vyāsa  is mistaken  saying  to those  sūtras  fault  ascribing  
‘vivarta-vāda’ sthāpiyāche kalpanā kariyā  
the doctrine of illusion  he has established  fancy  making

(172) Calling Vyāsa mistaken and finding fault with his sūtras, the Māyāvādī has set up the doctrine of illusion (*vivarta-vāda*) by his own invention.[^67]

জীবের দেহে আত্মবুদ্ধি, — সেই মিথ্যা হয় ৷  
জগৎ যে মিথ্যা নহে, নশ্বর মাত্র হয় ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

jīvera dehe ātma-buddhi, — sei mithyā haya  
of the jīva  in the body  the notion of self  that  false  is  
jagat ye mithyā nahe, naśvara mātra haya  
the world  but  false  is not  perishable  only  is

(173) What is false is the jīva’s sense of self in the body. The world is not false — it is only perishable.

‘প্রণব’ যে মহাবাক্য — ঈশ্বরের মূর্ত্তি ৷  
প্রণব হৈতে সর্ব্ববেদ, জগতে উৎপত্তি ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

‘praṇava’ ye mahāvākya — īśvarera mūrti  
praṇava  which  the great statement  of the Lord  the form  
praṇava haite sarva-veda, jagate utpatti  
from praṇava  all the Vedas  to the world  origin

(174) The *praṇava* (Oṁ), which is the great statement (*mahā-vākya*), is the form of the Lord. From the praṇava come all the Vedas and the world.[^68]

‘তত্ত্বমসি’ — জীব-হেতু প্রাদেশিক বাক্য ৷  
প্রণব না মানি’ তারে কহে মহাবাক্য ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

‘tat tvam asi’ — jīva-hetu prādeśika vākya  
“tat tvam asi”  concerning the jīva  partial  statement  
praṇava nā māni’ tāre kahe mahā-vākya  
the praṇava  not  accepting  it  he calls  the great statement

(175) *Tat tvam asi* is only a partial statement, spoken of the jīva; yet the Māyāvādī, not accepting the praṇava, calls that the great statement.”[^69]

এইমতে কল্পিত ভাষ্যে শত দোষ দিল ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য পূর্ব্বপক্ষ অপার করিল ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

ei-mate kalpita bhāṣye śata doṣa dila  
thus  in the fanciful  commentary  hundreds of  faults  He showed  
bhaṭṭācārya pūrva-pakṣa apāra karila  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  objections  without end  made

(176) In this way the Lord pointed out hundreds of faults in that fanciful commentary, and the Bhaṭṭācārya raised countless objections.

বিতণ্ডা, ছল, নিগ্রহাদি অনেক উঠাইল ৷  
সব খণ্ডি’ প্রভু নিজ-মত সে স্থাপিল ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

vitaṇḍā, chala, nigrahādi aneka uṭhāila  
captious wrangling  quibbles  points of defeat and the like  many  he raised  
saba khaṇḍi’ prabhu nija-mata se sthāpila  
all  refuting  the Lord  His own view  that  established

(177) He brought up much captious wrangling (*vitaṇḍā*), quibbling (*chala*), points of defeat (*nigraha*) and the like, but the Lord refuted them all and established His own view.[^70]

ভগবান্ — ‘সম্বন্ধ’, ভক্তি — ‘অভিধেয়’ হয় ৷  
প্রেম — ‘প্রয়োজন’, বেদে তিন বস্তু কয় ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

bhagavān — ‘sambandha’, bhakti — ‘abhidheya’ haya  
Bhagavān  sambandha  bhakti  abhidheya  is  
prema — ‘prayojana’, vede tina vastu kaya  
love  prayojana  in the Vedas  three  realities  are stated

(178) “Bhagavān is the relationship (*sambandha*), bhakti is the means (*abhidheya*), and love is the goal (*prayojana*) — these three truths the Vedas declare.[^71]

আর যে যে-কিছু কহে, সকলেই কল্পনা ৷  
স্বতঃপ্রমাণ বেদ-বাক্যে না করিয়ে লক্ষণা ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

āra ye ye-kichu kahe, sakalei kalpanā  
else  whatever  anything  is said  all of it  fancy  
svataḥ-pramāṇa veda-vākye nā kariye lakṣaṇā  
self-evident  in the words of the Veda  not  I apply  an indirect reading

(179) Whatever else anyone says is all fancy. To the self-evident words of the Veda I do not apply an indirect reading (*lakṣaṇā*).[^72]

আচার্য্যের দোষ নাহি, ঈশ্বর-আজ্ঞা হৈল ৷  
অতএব কল্পনা করি’ নাস্তিক-শাস্ত্র কৈল ॥ ১৮০ ॥

ācāryera doṣa nāhi, īśvara-ājñā haila  
of the Ācārya  fault  there is not  the Lord’s order  there was  
ataeva kalpanā kari’ nāstika-śāstra kaila  
therefore  fancy  making  an atheistic scripture  he made

(180) The Ācārya (Śaṅkara) is not at fault: it was the Lord’s order. That is why he invented an atheistic scripture.”[^73]

স্বাগমৈঃ কল্পিতৈস্ত্বঞ্চ জনান্ মদ্বিমুখান্ কুরু ৷  
মাঞ্চ গোপয় যেন স্যাৎ সৃষ্টিরেষোত্তরোত্তরা ॥ ১৮১ ॥

svāgamaiḥ kalpitais tvaṁ ca  
by your own scriptures  imagined  you  and  
janān mad-vimukhān kuru  
people  averse to Me  make  
māṁ ca gopaya yena syāt  
Me  and  hide  so that  may be  
sṛṣṭir eṣottarottarā  
creation  this  ever increasing

(181) “By scriptures of your own imagining turn people away from Me, and hide Me, so that this creation may go on increasing more and more.” (Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 62.31)[^74]

মায়াবাদমসচ্ছাস্ত্রং প্রচ্ছন্নং বৌদ্ধমুচ্যতে ৷  
ময়ৈব বিহিতং দেবি কলৌ ব্রাহ্মণ-মূর্ত্তিনা ॥ ১৮২ ॥

māyāvādam asac-chāstraṁ  
Māyāvāda  a false scripture  
pracchannaṁ bauddham ucyate  
disguised  Buddhism  is called  
mayaiva vihitaṁ devi  
by me  indeed  set forth  O Devī  
kalau brāhmaṇa-mūrtinā  
in Kali  in the form of a brāhmaṇa

(182) “Māyāvāda is a false scripture; it is called Buddhism in disguise. It was I, O Devī, who set it forth in the age of Kali in the form of a brāhmaṇa.” (Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 25.7)[^75]

শুনি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য হৈল পরম বিস্মিত ৷  
মুখে না নিঃসরে বাণী, হইলা স্তম্ভিত ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

śuni’ bhaṭṭācārya haila parama vismita  
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya  became  utterly  astonished  
mukhe nā niḥsare vāṇī, hailā stambhita  
from his mouth  not  comes out  a word  he became  stunned

(183) Hearing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was utterly astonished. Not a word left his lips; he sat stunned.

প্রভু কহে, — ভট্টাচার্য্য, না কর বিস্ময় ৷  
ভগবানে ভক্তি — পরম-পুরুষার্থ হয় ॥ ১৮৪ ॥

prabhu kahe, — bhaṭṭācārya, nā kara vismaya  
the Lord  says  Bhaṭṭācārya  do not  make  wonder  
bhagavāne bhakti — parama-puruṣārtha haya  
to Bhagavān  devotion  the highest goal of life  is

(184) The Lord said, “Do not be amazed, Bhaṭṭācārya. Devotion to Bhagavān is the highest goal of human life.

‘আত্মারাম’ পর্য্যন্ত করে ঈশ্বর ভজন ৷  
ঐছে অচিন্ত্য ভগবানের গুণগণ ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

‘ātmārāma’ paryanta kare īśvara bhajana  
the ātmārāmas  even  do  of the Lord  worship  
aiche acintya bhagavānera guṇa-gaṇa  
such  inconceivable  Bhagavān’s  qualities

(185) Even the *ātmārāmas*, who delight in the self, worship the Lord — so inconceivable are the qualities of Bhagavān.”

আত্মারামাশ্চ মুনয়ো নির্গ্রন্থা অপ্যুরুক্রমে ৷  
কুর্ব্বন্ত্যহৈতুকীং ভক্তিমিত্থম্ভূতগুণো হরিঃ ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

ātmārāmāś ca munayo  
delighting in the ātmā  and  the sages  
nirgranthā apy urukrame  
free from knots  even  to Urukrama  
kurvanty ahaitukīṁ bhaktim  
perform  causeless  devotion  
ittham-bhūta-guṇo hariḥ  
of such  qualities  is Hari

(186) “Even sages who delight in the ātmā and are free from the knots of the heart render causeless devotion to Urukrama, for such are the qualities of Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.10)[^76]

শুনি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে, — শুন, মহাশয় ৷  
এই শ্লোকের অর্থ শুনিতে বাঞ্ছা হয় ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

śuni’ bhaṭṭācārya kahe, — śuna, mahāśaya  
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  listen  sir  
ei ślokera artha śunite vāñchā haya  
of this  verse  the meaning  to hear  desire  there is

(187) Hearing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Listen, my dear sir (*mahāśaya*): I wish to hear the meaning of this verse.”

প্রভু কহে, — তুমি কি অর্থ কর, তাহা শুনি ৷  
পাছে আমি করিব অর্থ, যেবা কিছু জানি ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

prabhu kahe, — tumi ki artha kara, tāhā śuni  
the Lord  says  you  what  meaning  make  that  I will hear  
pāche āmi kariba artha, yebā kichu jāni  
afterwards  I  will make  meaning  whatever  little  I know

(188) The Lord said, “First let Me hear what meaning you give it. Afterwards I shall explain it, as far as I know.”

শুনি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য শ্লোক করিল ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
তর্কশাস্ত্র-মত উঠায় বিবিধ বিধান ॥ ১৮৯ ॥

śuni’ bhaṭṭācārya śloka karila vyākhyāna  
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya  of the verse  made  an explanation  
tarka-śāstra-mata uṭhāya vividha vidhāna  
according to logic  raises  various  propositions

(189) At this the Bhaṭṭācārya explained the verse, raising various propositions in the manner of the science of logic.

নববিধ অর্থ কৈল শাস্ত্রমত লঞা ৷  
শুনি’ প্রভু কহে কিছু ঈষৎ হাসিয়া ॥ ১৯০ ॥

nava-vidha artha kaila śāstra-mata lañā  
of nine kinds  meanings  he gave  scriptural views  taking  
śuni’ prabhu kahe kichu īṣat hāsiyā  
hearing  the Lord  says  something  slightly  smiling

(190) He gave nine different meanings on the authority of scripture. Hearing them, the Lord smiled slightly and said:

‘ভট্টাচার্য্য’, জানি, — তুমি সাক্ষাৎ বৃহস্পতি ৷  
শাস্ত্রব্যাখ্যা করিতে ঐছে কারো নাহি শক্তি ॥ ১৯১ ॥

‘bhaṭṭācārya’, jāni, — tumi sākṣāt bṛhaspati  
Bhaṭṭācārya  I know  you  the very  Bṛhaspati  
śāstra-vyākhyā karite aiche kāro nāhi śakti  
of scripture  explanation  to make  like this  no one’s  there is not  power

(191) “Bhaṭṭācārya, I know that you are Bṛhaspati himself. No one else has the power to explain scripture like this.

কিন্তু তুমি অর্থ কৈলে পাণ্ডিত্য-প্রতিভায় ৷  
ইহা বই শ্লোকের আছে আরো অভিপ্রায় ॥ ১৯২ ॥

kintu tumi artha kaile pāṇḍitya-pratibhāya  
but  you  meaning  made  by the brilliance of scholarship  
ihā vai ślokera āche āro abhiprāya  
this  besides  of the verse  there is  further  intent

(192) But you have explained it by the brilliance of your scholarship; beyond this, the verse has another intent.”

ভট্টাচার্য্যের প্রার্থনাতে প্রভু ব্যাখ্যা কৈল ৷  
তাঁর নব অর্থ মধ্যে এক না ছুঁইল ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

bhaṭṭācāryera prārthanāte prabhu vyākhyā kaila  
the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  at the request  the Lord  explanation  made  
tām̐ra nava artha madhye eka nā chum̐ila  
his  nine  meanings  among  one  not  He touched

(193) At the Bhaṭṭācārya’s request the Lord explained the verse, without touching even one of his nine meanings.

আত্মারামাশ্চ-শ্লোকে ‘একাদশ’ পদ হয় ৷  
পৃথক্ পৃথক্ কৈল পদের অর্থ নিশ্চয় ॥ ১৯৪ ॥

ātmārāmāś ca-śloke ‘ekādaśa’ pada haya  
in the “ātmārāmāś ca” verse  eleven  words  there are  
pṛthak pṛthak kaila padera artha niścaya  
separately  separately  He made  of the words  the meaning  certain

(194) The verse “ātmārāmāś ca” has eleven words, and He established the meaning of each word separately.

তত্তৎপদ-প্রাধান্যে ‘আত্মারাম’ মিলাঞা ৷  
অষ্টাদশ অর্থ কৈল অভিপ্রায় লঞা ॥ ১৯৫ ॥

tat-tat-pada-prādhānye ‘ātmārāma’ milāñā  
to each word  giving prominence  with “ātmārāma”  combining  
aṣṭādaśa artha kaila abhiprāya lañā  
eighteen  meanings  He made  the intent  taking

(195) Giving prominence to each word in turn and combining it with “ātmārāma”, He brought out eighteen meanings according to the verse’s intent.[^77]

ভগবান্, তাঁর শক্তি, তাঁর গুণগণ ৷  
অচিন্ত্য প্রভাব তিনের না যায় কথন ॥ ১৯৬ ॥

bhagavān, tām̐ra śakti, tām̐ra guṇa-gaṇa  
Bhagavān  His  potency  His  qualities  
acintya prabhāva tinera nā yāya kathana  
inconceivable  power  of the three  not  can be  described

(196) “Bhagavān, His potency and His qualities — the inconceivable power of these three cannot be described.

অন্য যত সাধ্য-সাধন করি’ আচ্ছাদন ৷  
এই তিনে হরে সিদ্ধ-সাধকের মন ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

anya yata sādhya-sādhana kari’ ācchādana  
other  all  goals and means  making  eclipse  
ei tine hare siddha-sādhakera mana  
these  three  steal  of the perfected and the practitioners  the minds

(197) Eclipsing every other goal and means, these three steal the hearts of the perfected and of those who still practise.”[^78]

সনকাদি-শুকদেব তাহাতে প্রমাণ ৷  
এইমত নানা অর্থ করেন ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ১৯৮ ॥

sanakādi-śukadeva tāhāte pramāṇa  
Sanaka and the others, Śukadeva  of this  the proof  
ei-mata nānā artha karena vyākhyāna  
in this way  various  meanings  He makes  explanation

(198) Sanaka and the other Kumāras, and Śukadeva, are proof of this. In this way the Lord explained many meanings.[^79]

শুনি’ ভট্টাচার্য্যের মনে হৈল চমৎকার ৷  
প্রভুকে কৃষ্ণ জানি’ করে আপনা ধিক্কার ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

śuni’ bhaṭṭācāryera mane haila camatkāra  
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  in the mind  there was  wonder  
prabhuke kṛṣṇa jāni’ kare āpanā dhikkāra  
the Lord  as Kṛṣṇa  knowing  he does  himself  reproach

(199) Hearing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was struck with wonder. Knowing the Lord to be Kṛṣṇa, he began to reproach himself:

ইঁহো ত’ সাক্ষাৎ কৃষ্ণ, — মুঞি না জানিয়া ৷  
মহা-অপরাধ কৈনু গর্ব্বিত হইয়া ॥ ২০০ ॥

im̐ho ta’ sākṣāt kṛṣṇa, — muñi nā jāniyā  
He  indeed  directly  Kṛṣṇa  I  not  knowing  
mahā-aparādha kainu garvita haiyā  
a great offence  I committed  proud  being

(200) “He is Kṛṣṇa Himself, and I, not knowing it, have committed a great offence in my pride.”

আত্মনিন্দা করি’ লৈল প্রভুর শরণ ৷  
কৃপা করিবারে তবে প্রভুর হৈল মন ॥ ২০১ ॥

ātma-nindā kari’ laila prabhura śaraṇa  
self-reproach  making  he took  of the Lord  shelter  
kṛpā karibāre tabe prabhura haila mana  
mercy  to show  then  of the Lord  arose  the mind

(201) Reproaching himself, he took shelter of the Lord, and the Lord then resolved to show him mercy.

নিজ-রূপ প্রভু তাঁরে করাইল দর্শন ৷  
চতুর্ভুজ-রূপ প্রভু হইলা তখন ॥ ২০২ ॥

nija-rūpa prabhu tām̐re karāila darśana  
His own form  the Lord  to him  caused  to see  
catur-bhuja-rūpa prabhu hailā takhana  
four-armed in form  the Lord  became  then

(202) The Lord showed him His own form: He became four-armed.

দেখাইল তাঁরে আগে চতুর্ভুজ-রূপ ৷  
পাছে শ্যাম-বংশীমুখ স্বকীয় স্বরূপ ॥ ২০৩ ॥

dekhāila tām̐re āge catur-bhuja-rūpa  
He showed  to him  first  the four-armed form  
pāche śyāma-vaṁśī-mukha svakīya svarūpa  
then  dark  with the flute at the lips  His very own  form

(203) First He showed him the four-armed form, and then His very own form — dark, with the flute at His lips.[^80]

দেখি’ সার্ব্বভৌম দণ্ডবৎ করি’ পড়ি’ ৷  
পুনঃ উঠি’ স্তুতি করে দুই কর যুড়ি’ ॥ ২০৪ ॥

dekhi’ sārvabhauma daṇḍavat kari’ paḍi’  
seeing  Sārvabhauma  obeisance  making  falling down  
punaḥ uṭhi’ stuti kare dui kara yuḍi’  
again  rising  prayers  he offers  two  hands  joining

(204) Seeing this, Sārvabhauma fell down flat in obeisance; then he rose and, with folded hands, offered prayers.

প্রভুর কৃপায় তাঁর স্ফুরিল সব তত্ত্ব ৷  
নাম-প্রেমদান-আদি বর্ণেন মহত্ত্ব ॥ ২০৫ ॥

prabhura kṛpāya tām̐ra sphurila saba tattva  
by the Lord’s  mercy  to him  were revealed  all  truths  
nāma-prema-dāna-ādi varṇena mahattva  
of the holy name  of love  the giving  and so on  he describes  the greatness

(205) By the Lord’s mercy all truths were revealed to him, and he described the glory of the holy name, of the gift of love and of much else.

শত শ্লোক কৈল এক দণ্ড না যাইতে ৷  
বৃহস্পতি তৈছে শ্লোক না পারে করিতে ॥ ২০৬ ॥

śata śloka kaila eka daṇḍa nā yāite  
a hundred  verses  he composed  one  daṇḍa  not  passing  
bṛhaspati taiche śloka nā pāre karite  
Bṛhaspati  such  verses  not  can  compose

(206) Within a single *daṇḍa* (twenty-four minutes) he composed a hundred verses — verses such as even Bṛhaspati could not compose.[^81]

শুনি’ সুখে প্রভু তাঁরে কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য প্রেমাবেশে হৈল অচেতন ॥ ২০৭ ॥

śuni’ sukhe prabhu tām̐re kaila āliṅgana  
hearing  with joy  the Lord  him  made  an embrace  
bhaṭṭācārya premāveśe haila acetana  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  in the rapture of love  became  unconscious

(207) Hearing them, the Lord joyfully embraced him, and the Bhaṭṭācārya, overwhelmed by love, lost consciousness.

অশ্রু, স্তম্ভ, পুলক, স্বেদ, কম্প থরহরি ৷  
নাচে, গায়, কান্দে, পড়ে প্রভু-পদ ধরি’ ॥ ২০৮ ॥

aśru, stambha, pulaka, sveda, kampa tharahari  
tears  stupor  thrill  sweat  trembling  violent  
nāce, gāya, kānde, paḍe prabhu-pada dhari’  
he dances  sings  weeps  falls  the Lord’s feet  clasping

(208) Tears, stupor, thrill, sweat and violent trembling came over him. He danced, sang and wept, and fell down clasping the Lord’s feet.

দেখি’ গোপীনাথাচার্য্য হরষিত-মন ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যের নৃত্য দেখি’ হাসে প্রভুর গণ ॥ ২০৯ ॥

dekhi’ gopīnāthācārya haraṣita-mana  
seeing  Gopīnāthācārya  delighted at heart  
bhaṭṭācāryera nṛtya dekhi’ hāse prabhura gaṇa  
the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  dance  seeing  laugh  the Lord’s  companions

(209) Seeing this, Gopīnātha Ācārya was delighted at heart, and the Lord’s companions laughed to see the Bhaṭṭācārya dance.

গোপীনাথাচার্য্য কহে মহাপ্রভুর প্রতি ৷  
সেই ভট্টাচার্য্যের তুমি কৈলে এই গতি ॥ ২১০ ॥

gopīnāthācārya kahe mahāprabhura prati  
Gopīnāthācārya  says  Mahāprabhu  to  
sei bhaṭṭācāryera tumi kaile ei gati  
that very  Bhaṭṭācārya’s  You  have made  this  state

(210) Gopīnātha Ācārya said to Mahāprabhu, “It is that very Bhaṭṭācārya whom You have brought to such a state!”

প্রভু কহে, — তুমি ভক্ত, তোমার সঙ্গ হৈতে ৷  
জগন্নাথ ইঁহারে কৃপা কৈল ভালমতে ॥ ২১১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — tumi bhakta, tomāra saṅga haite  
the Lord  says  you  a devotee  your  association  from  
jagannātha im̐hāre kṛpā kaila bhāla-mate  
Jagannātha  to him  mercy  showed  fully

(211) The Lord said, “You are a devotee, and through association with you Jagannātha has shown him full mercy.”

তবে ভট্টাচার্য্যে প্রভু সুস্থির করিল ৷  
স্থির হঞা ভট্টাচার্য্য বহু স্তুতি কৈল ॥ ২১২ ॥

tabe bhaṭṭācārye prabhu susthira karila  
then  the Bhaṭṭācārya  the Lord  calm  made  
sthira hañā bhaṭṭācārya bahu stuti kaila  
calm  becoming  the Bhaṭṭācārya  many  prayers  made

(212) Then the Lord calmed the Bhaṭṭācārya, and once composed, he offered many prayers.

জগৎ নিস্তারিলে তুমি, — সেহ অল্পকার্য্য ৷  
আমা উদ্ধারিলে তুমি, — এ শক্তি আশ্চর্য্য ॥ ২১৩ ॥

jagat nistārile tumi, — seha alpa-kārya  
the world  have delivered  You  that  a small deed  
āmā uddhārile tumi, — e śakti āścarya  
me  have delivered  You  this  power  wonderful

(213) “That You have delivered the world is a small deed; that You have delivered me — that is a wondrous power!

তর্ক-শাস্ত্রে জড় আমি, যৈছে লৌহপিণ্ড ৷  
আমা দ্রবাইলে তুমি, প্রতাপ প্রচণ্ড ॥ ২১৪ ॥

tarka-śāstre jaḍa āmi, yaiche lauha-piṇḍa  
by logic  dulled  I  like  a lump of iron  
āmā dravāile tumi, pratāpa pracaṇḍa  
me  have melted  You  power  mighty

(214) Logic had made me as hard and dull as a lump of iron, yet You have melted me — mighty is Your power!”

স্তুতি শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু নিজ-বাসা আইলা ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য আচার্য্য-দ্বারে ভিক্ষা করাইলা ॥ ২১৫ ॥

stuti śuni’ mahāprabhu nija-vāsā āilā  
the prayers  hearing  Mahāprabhu  to His own lodging  came  
bhaṭṭācārya ācārya-dvāre bhikṣā karāilā  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  through the Ācārya  a meal  arranged

(215) Having heard these prayers, Mahāprabhu returned to His lodging, and the Bhaṭṭācārya had a meal served to Him through Gopīnātha Ācārya.

আর দিন প্রভু গেলা জগন্নাথ-দরশনে ৷  
দর্শন করিলা জগন্নাথ-শয্যোত্থানে ॥ ২১৬ ॥

āra dina prabhu gelā jagannātha-daraśane  
the next  day  the Lord  went  to see Jagannātha  
darśana karilā jagannātha-śayyotthāne  
darśana  He had  at Jagannātha’s rising from bed

(216) The next day the Lord went to see Jagannātha and had *darśana* of Him as He rose from His bed.

পূজারী আনিয়া মালা-প্রসাদান্ন দিলা ৷  
প্রসাদান্ন-মালা পাঞা প্রভু হর্ষ হৈলা ॥ ২১৭ ॥

pūjārī āniyā mālā-prasādānna dilā  
the pūjārī  bringing  garland and prasāda  gave  
prasādānna-mālā pāñā prabhu harṣa hailā  
the prasāda and garland  receiving  the Lord  joyful  became

(217) The pūjārī brought Him a garland and prasāda, and receiving them, the Lord was delighted.

সেই প্রসাদান্ন-মালা অঞ্চলে বান্ধিয়া ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যের ঘরে আইলা ত্বরাযুক্ত হঞা ॥ ২১৮ ॥

sei prasādānna-mālā añcale bāndhiyā  
that  prasāda and garland  in the end of His cloth  tying  
bhaṭṭācāryera ghare āilā tvarāyukta hañā  
the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  to the house  He came  in haste  being

(218) Tying the prasāda and garland in the end of His cloth, He hurried to the Bhaṭṭācārya’s house.

অরুণোদয়-কালে হৈল প্রভুর আগমন ৷  
সেইকালে ভট্টাচার্য্যের হৈল জাগরণ ॥ ২১৯ ॥

aruṇodaya-kāle haila prabhura āgamana  
at dawn  there was  the Lord’s  arrival  
sei-kāle bhaṭṭācāryera haila jāgaraṇa  
at that time  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  there was  waking

(219) The Lord arrived at dawn, just as the Bhaṭṭācārya was waking.[^82]

‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ স্ফুট কহি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য জাগিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম শুনি’ প্রভুর আনন্দ বাড়িলা ॥ ২২০ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ sphuṭa kahi’ bhaṭṭācārya jāgilā  
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  clearly  saying  the Bhaṭṭācārya  awoke  
kṛṣṇa-nāma śuni’ prabhura ānanda bāḍilā  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  hearing  the Lord’s  joy  increased

(220) As he woke, the Bhaṭṭācārya called out clearly, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”, and when the Lord heard the name of Kṛṣṇa, His joy grew.

বাহিরে প্রভুর তেঁহো পাইল দরশন ৷  
আস্তে-ব্যস্তে আসি’ কৈল চরণ বন্দন ॥ ২২১ ॥

bāhire prabhura tem̐ho pāila daraśana  
outside  of the Lord  he  obtained  sight  
āste-vyaste āsi’ kaila caraṇa vandana  
hurriedly  coming  he made  to the feet  obeisance

(221) Seeing the Lord outside, he hastened to Him and bowed at His feet.

বসিতে আসন দিয়া দুঁহেতে বসিলা ৷  
প্রসাদান্ন খুলি’ প্রভু তাঁর হাতে দিলা ॥ ২২২ ॥

vasite āsana diyā dum̐hete vasilā  
to sit  a seat  giving  both  sat down  
prasādānna khuli’ prabhu tām̐ra hāte dilā  
the prasāda  untying  the Lord  his  in the hand  gave

(222) He offered the Lord a seat, and the two sat down together. Untying the prasāda, the Lord placed it in his hand.

প্রসাদান্ন পাঞা ভট্টাচার্য্যের আনন্দ হৈল ৷  
স্নান, সন্ধ্যা, দন্তধাবন যদ্যপি না কৈল ॥ ২২৩ ॥

prasādānna pāñā bhaṭṭācāryera ānanda haila  
the prasāda  receiving  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  joy  there was  
snāna, sandhyā, danta-dhāvana yadyapi nā kaila  
bath  morning prayers  cleaning the teeth  although  not  he had done

(223) The Bhaṭṭācārya was overjoyed to receive the prasāda, though he had not yet bathed, said his morning prayers or even cleaned his teeth.

চৈতন্য-প্রসাদে মনের সব জাড্য গেল ৷  
এই শ্লোক পড়ি’ অন্ন ভক্ষণ করিল ॥ ২২৪ ॥

caitanya-prasāde manera saba jādya gela  
by Caitanya’s grace  of the mind  all  dullness  went  
ei śloka paḍi’ anna bhakṣaṇa karila  
this  verse  reciting  the food  he ate

(224) By Caitanya’s grace all the dullness of his mind was gone. Reciting these verses, he ate the prasāda:

শুষ্কং পর্য্যুষিতং বাপি নীতং বা দূরদেশতঃ ৷  
প্রাপ্তিমাত্রেণ ভোক্তব্যং নাত্র কালবিচারণা ॥ ২২৫ ॥

śuṣkaṁ paryuṣitaṁ vāpi  
dried up  stale  or even  
nītaṁ vā dūra-deśataḥ  
brought  or  from a distant land  
prāpti-mātreṇa bhoktavyaṁ  
as soon as received  should be eaten  
nātra kāla-vicāraṇā  
not here  of time  consideration

(225) “Even if it is dry, stale or brought from a distant land, it should be eaten as soon as it is received. Here there is no consideration of time.” (Padma Purāṇa)[^83]

ন দেশনিয়মস্তত্র ন কালনিয়মস্তথা ৷  
প্রাপ্তমন্নং দ্রুতং শিষ্টৈর্ভোক্তব্যং হরিরব্রবীৎ ॥ ২২৬ ॥

na deśa-niyamas tatra  
no  of place  rule  in this  
na kāla-niyamas tathā  
no  of time  rule  likewise  
prāptam annaṁ drutaṁ śiṣṭair  
received  food  quickly  by the cultured  
bhoktavyaṁ harir abravīt  
should be eaten  Hari  said

(226) “For this there is no rule of place and no rule of time. The cultured should eat such food as soon as it is received — so Hari has said.” (Padma Purāṇa)[^84]

দেখিয়া আনন্দ হৈল মহাপ্রভুর মন ৷  
প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা প্রভু কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ॥ ২২৭ ॥

dekhiyā ānanda haila mahāprabhura mana  
seeing  joyful  became  Mahāprabhu’s  mind  
premāviṣṭa hañā prabhu kailā āliṅgana  
absorbed in love  becoming  the Lord  made  an embrace

(227) Mahāprabhu’s heart filled with joy at the sight, and overwhelmed with love, He embraced him.

দুইজনে ধরি’ দুঁহে করেন নর্ত্তন ৷  
প্রভু-ভৃত্য দুঁহা স্পর্শে, দোঁহার ফুলে মন ॥ ২২৮ ॥

dui-jane dhari’ dum̐he karena nartana  
the two  holding  each other  perform  dancing  
prabhu-bhṛtya dum̐hā sparśe, dom̐hāra phule mana  
the Lord and the servant  both  by the touch  of both  blossom  hearts

(228) Holding each other, the two danced. Lord and servant touched each other, and the hearts of both blossomed.

স্বেদ-কম্প-অশ্রু দুঁহে আনন্দে ভাসিলা ৷  
প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা প্রভু কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ২২৯ ॥

sveda-kampa-aśru dum̐he ānande bhāsilā  
sweat  trembling  tears  both  in bliss  floated  
premāviṣṭa hañā prabhu kahite lāgilā  
absorbed in love  becoming  the Lord  to speak  began

(229) Bathed in sweat, trembling and in tears, both floated in bliss. Filled with love, the Lord began to speak:

আজি মুঞি অনায়াসে জিনিনু ত্রিভুবন ৷  
আজি মুঞি করিনু বৈকুণ্ঠ আরোহণ ॥ ২৩০ ॥

āji muñi anāyāse jininu tribhuvana  
today  I  without effort  have conquered  the three worlds  
āji muñi karinu vaikuṇṭha ārohaṇa  
today  I  have made  to Vaikuṇṭha  the ascent

(230) “Today I have conquered the three worlds without effort. Today I have ascended to Vaikuṇṭha.

আজি মোর পূর্ণ হৈল সর্ব্ব অভিলাষ ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌমের হৈল মহাপ্রসাদে বিশ্বাস ॥ ২৩১ ॥

āji mora pūrṇa haila sarva abhilāṣa  
today  My  fulfilled  were  all  desires  
sārvabhaumera haila mahā-prasāde viśvāsa  
Sārvabhauma’s  there has come  in mahā-prasāda  faith

(231) Today all My desires are fulfilled, for Sārvabhauma has come to have faith in mahā-prasāda.

আজি তুমি নিষ্কপটে হৈলা কৃষ্ণাশ্রয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণ আজি নিষ্কপটে তোমা হইয়া সদয় ॥ ২৩২ ॥

āji tumi niṣkapaṭe hailā kṛṣṇāśraya  
today  you  sincerely  have become  sheltered in Kṛṣṇa  
kṛṣṇa āji niṣkapaṭe tomā haiyā sadaya  
Kṛṣṇa  today  sincerely  to you  having become  kind

(232) Today you have taken shelter of Kṛṣṇa without guile, and today Kṛṣṇa has shown you His mercy without reserve.

আজি সে খণ্ডিল তোমার দেহাদি-বন্ধন ৷  
আজি তুমি ছিন্ন কৈলে মায়ার বন্ধন ॥ ২৩৩ ॥

āji se khaṇḍila tomāra dehādi-bandhana  
today  that  has been cut  your  bondage to the body and the rest  
āji tumi chinna kaile māyāra bandhana  
today  you  cut  have made  of māyā  the bonds

(233) Today your bondage to the body and all that goes with it has been cut away; today you have cut the bonds of māyā.

আজি কৃষ্ণপ্রাপ্তি-যোগ্য হৈল তোমার মন ৷  
বেদ-ধর্ম্ম লঙ্ঘি’ কৈলে প্রসাদ ভক্ষণ ॥ ২৩৪ ॥

āji kṛṣṇa-prāpti-yogya haila tomāra mana  
today  fit to attain Kṛṣṇa  has become  your  mind  
veda-dharma laṅghi’ kaile prasāda bhakṣaṇa  
the Vedic rules  overstepping  you have done  of prasāda  eating

(234) Today your mind has become fit to attain Kṛṣṇa, for you have eaten prasāda, setting aside the Vedic rules.”

যেষাং স এষ ভগবান্ দয়য়েদনন্তঃ  
সর্ব্বাত্মনাশ্রিতপদো যদি নির্ব্যলীকম্ ৷  
তে দুস্তরামতিতরন্তি চ দেবমায়াং  
নৈষাং মমাহমিতিধীঃ শ্বশৃগালভক্ষ্যে ॥ ২৩৫ ॥

yeṣāṁ sa eṣa bhagavān dayayed anantaḥ  
to whom  He  this  the Lord  may show mercy  the Unlimited  
sarvātmanāśrita-pado yadi nirvyalīkam  
with all their being  who have taken shelter of His feet  if  without deceit  
te dustarām atitaranti ca deva-māyāṁ  
they  insurmountable  cross over  and  the divine māyā  
naiṣāṁ mamāham iti dhīḥ śva-śṛgāla-bhakṣye  
not of them  “mine”, “I”  thus  the notion  in what is food for dogs and jackals

(235) “Those to whom the unlimited Lord shows His mercy — who have taken shelter of His feet with all their being and without deceit — cross over the insurmountable divine māyā, and they no longer think of the body, food for dogs and jackals, as ‘I’ and ‘mine’.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.7.42)[^85]

এত কহি’ মহাপ্রভু আইলা নিজ-স্থানে ৷  
সেই হৈতে ভট্টাচার্য্যের খণ্ডিল অভিমানে ॥ ২৩৬ ॥

eta kahi’ mahāprabhu āilā nija-sthāne  
so much  saying  Mahāprabhu  came  to His own place  
sei haite bhaṭṭācāryera khaṇḍila abhimāne  
from that  time  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  was broken  pride

(236) Having said this, Mahāprabhu returned to His own place, and from that time the Bhaṭṭācārya’s pride was broken.

চৈতন্য-চরণ বিনা নাহি জানে আন ৷  
ভক্তি বিনা শাস্ত্রের অন্য না করে ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ২৩৭ ॥

caitanya-caraṇa vinā nāhi jāne āna  
Caitanya’s feet  except  not  he knows  anything else  
bhakti vinā śāstrera anya nā kare vyākhyāna  
bhakti  except  of scripture  other  not  he makes  explanation

(237) He knew nothing but Caitanya’s feet, and he explained scripture as teaching nothing but bhakti.

গোপীনাথাচার্য্য তাঁর বৈষ্ণবতা দেখিয়া ৷  
‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলি’ নাচে হাতে তালি দিয়া ॥ ২৩৮ ॥

gopīnāthācārya tām̐ra vaiṣṇavatā dekhiyā  
Gopīnāthācārya  his  Vaiṣṇava devotion  seeing  
‘hari’ ‘hari’ bali’ nāce hāte tāli diyā  
“Hari”  “Hari”  calling  dances  hands  clapping

(238) Seeing his devotion as a Vaiṣṇava, Gopīnātha Ācārya danced, clapping his hands and calling, “Hari! Hari!”

আর দিন ভট্টাচার্য্য আইলা দর্শনে ৷  
জগন্নাথ না দেখি’ আইলা প্রভু-স্থানে ॥ ২৩৯ ॥

āra dina bhaṭṭācārya āilā darśane  
the next  day  the Bhaṭṭācārya  came  for darśana  
jagannātha nā dekhi’ āilā prabhu-sthāne  
Jagannātha  not  seeing  came  to the Lord’s place

(239) The next day the Bhaṭṭācārya set out for darśana, but before seeing Jagannātha he went to the Lord.[^86]

দণ্ডবৎ করি’ কৈল বহুবিধ স্তুতি ৷  
দৈন্য করি’ কহে নিজ-পূর্ব্ব দুর্ম্মতি ॥ ২৪০ ॥

daṇḍavat kari’ kaila bahu-vidha stuti  
obeisance  making  he offered  many kinds of  prayers  
dainya kari’ kahe nija-pūrva durmati  
humility  showing  he tells  his own former  perversity

(240) Falling flat in obeisance, he offered many prayers, and in humility he spoke of his former perverse thinking.

ভক্তিসাধন-শ্রেষ্ঠ শুনিতে হৈল মন ৷  
প্রভু উপদেশ কৈল নাম-সংকীর্ত্তন ॥ ২৪১ ॥

bhakti-sādhana-śreṣṭha śunite haila mana  
of bhakti  of practice  the best  to hear  arose  desire  
prabhu upadeśa kaila nāma-saṁkīrtana  
the Lord  instruction  gave  the congregational chanting of the name

(241) He wished to hear which is the best practice of bhakti, and the Lord taught him the chanting of the holy name (*nāma-saṅkīrtana*):[^87]

হরের্নাম হরের্নাম হরের্নামৈব কেবলম্ ৷  
কলৌ নাস্ত্যেব নাস্ত্যেব নাস্ত্যেব গতিরন্যথা ॥ ২৪২ ॥

harer nāma harer nāma  
of Hari  the name  of Hari  the name  
harer nāmaiva kevalam  
of Hari  the name  indeed  only  
kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva  
in Kali  there is not  indeed  there is not  indeed  
nāsty eva gatir anyathā  
there is not  indeed  way  other

(242) “The name of Hari, the name of Hari, the name of Hari alone! In the age of Kali there is no other way, there is no other way, there is no other way.” (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa, 38.126)[^88]

এই শ্লোকের অর্থ শুনাইল করিয়া বিস্তার ৷  
শুনি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য-মনে হৈল চমৎকার ॥ ২৪৩ ॥

ei ślokera artha śunāila kariyā vistāra  
of this  verse  the meaning  He told  making  at length  
śuni’ bhaṭṭācārya-mane haila camatkāra  
hearing  in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s mind  there was  wonder

(243) The Lord explained the meaning of this verse at length, and hearing it, the Bhaṭṭācārya was filled with wonder.

গোপীনাথাচার্য্য বলে, — ‘আমি পূর্ব্বে যে কহিল ৷  
শুন, ভট্টাচার্য্য, তোমার সেই ত’ হইল’ ॥ ২৪৪ ॥

gopīnāthācārya bale, — ‘āmi pūrve ye kahila  
Gopīnāthācārya  says  I  before  what  said  
śuna, bhaṭṭācārya, tomāra sei ta’ haila’  
listen  Bhaṭṭācārya  to you  that  indeed  has happened

(244) Gopīnātha Ācārya said, “Listen, Bhaṭṭācārya: what I told you before has now happened to you.”

ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে তাঁরে করি’ নমস্কারে ৷  
তোমার সম্বন্ধে প্রভু কৃপা কৈল মোরে ॥ ২৪৫ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya kahe tām̐re kari’ namaskāre  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  to him  making  obeisance  
tomāra sambandhe prabhu kṛpā kaila more  
your  through the connection  the Lord  mercy  showed  to me

(245) Bowing to him, the Bhaṭṭācārya said, “It is through my connection with you that the Lord has shown me mercy.

তুমি — মহা-ভাগবত, আমি — তর্ক-অন্ধে ৷  
প্রভু কৃপা কৈল মোরে তোমার সম্বন্ধে ॥ ২৪৬ ॥

tumi — mahābhāgavata, āmi — tarka-andhe  
you  a great devotee  I  blinded by logic  
prabhu kṛpā kaila more tomāra sambandhe  
the Lord  mercy  showed  to me  your  through the connection

(246) You are a great devotee (*mahā-bhāgavata*), and I am blinded by logic; the Lord has shown me mercy through my connection with you.”

বিনয় শুনি’ তুষ্ট্যে প্রভু কৈল আলিঙ্গন ৷  
কহিল, — যাঞা করহ ঈশ্বর দরশন ॥ ২৪৭ ॥

vinaya śuni’ tuṣṭye prabhu kaila āliṅgana  
the humility  hearing  pleased  the Lord  made  an embrace  
kahila, — yāñā karaha īśvara daraśana  
He said  going  have  of the Lord  darśana

(247) Pleased to hear his humble words, the Lord embraced him and said, “Go now and have darśana of the Lord.”[^89]

জগদানন্দ-দামোদর, — দুই সঙ্গে লঞা ৷  
ঘরে আইল ভট্টাচার্য্য জগন্নাথ দেখিয়া ॥ ২৪৮ ॥

jagadānanda-dāmodara, — dui saṅge lañā  
Jagadānanda and Dāmodara  the two  with him  taking  
ghare āila bhaṭṭācārya jagannātha dekhiyā  
home  came  the Bhaṭṭācārya  Jagannātha  having seen

(248) Taking Jagadānanda and Dāmodara with him, the Bhaṭṭācārya saw Jagannātha and returned home.

উত্তম উত্তম প্রসাদ বহুত আনিলা ৷  
নিজবিপ্র-হাতে দুই জনা সঙ্গে দিলা ॥ ২৪৯ ॥

uttama uttama prasāda bahuta ānilā  
the finest  the finest  prasāda  much  he brought  
nija-vipra-hāte dui janā saṅge dilā  
in the hands of his own brāhmaṇa  two  persons  with  he gave

(249) He brought a great deal of the very best prasāda and sent it by his own brāhmaṇa, together with those two.

নিজ কৃত দুই শ্লোক লিখিয়া তালপাতে ৷  
প্রভুকে দিহ’ বলি’ দিল জগদানন্দ-হাতে ॥ ২৫০ ॥

nija kṛta dui śloka likhiyā tāla-pāte  
by himself  composed  two  verses  writing  on a palm leaf  
prabhuke diha’ bali’ dila jagadānanda-hāte  
to the Lord  give  saying  he gave  into Jagadānanda’s hand

(250) He wrote two verses of his own on a palm leaf and gave it into Jagadānanda’s hand, saying, “Give this to the Lord.”

প্রভু-স্থানে আইলা দুঁহে প্রসাদ-পত্রী লঞা ৷  
মুকুন্দ দত্ত পত্রী নিল তাঁর হাতে পাঞা ॥ ২৫১ ॥

prabhu-sthāne āilā dum̐he prasāda-patrī lañā  
to the Lord’s place  came  both  prasāda and letter  taking  
mukunda datta patrī nila tām̐ra hāte pāñā  
Mukunda  Datta  the letter  took  his  in the hand  finding

(251) The two came to the Lord with the prasāda and the letter, and Mukunda Datta, finding the letter in Jagadānanda’s hand, took it.[^90]

দুই শ্লোক বাহির-ভিতে লিখিয়া রাখিল ৷  
তবে জগদানন্দ পত্রী প্রভুকে লঞা দিল ॥ ২৫২ ॥

dui śloka bāhira-bhite likhiyā rākhila  
two  verses  on the outer wall  writing  he kept  
tabe jagadānanda patrī prabhuke lañā dila  
then  Jagadānanda  the letter  to the Lord  taking  gave

(252) He copied the two verses onto the outer wall; then Jagadānanda took the letter and gave it to the Lord.

প্রভু শ্লোক পড়ি’ পত্র ছিণ্ডিয়া ফেলিল ৷  
ভিত্যে দেখি’ ভক্ত সব শ্লোক কণ্ঠে কৈল ॥ ২৫৩ ॥

prabhu śloka paḍi’ patra chiṇḍiyā phelila  
the Lord  the verses  reading  the leaf  tearing  threw away  
bhitye dekhi’ bhakta saba śloka kaṇṭhe kaila  
on the wall  seeing  the devotees  all  the verses  by heart  made

(253) The Lord read the verses, tore up the leaf and threw it away, but the devotees saw them on the wall and all learned them by heart.

বৈরাগ্য-বিদ্যা-নিজভক্তিযোগ-  
শিক্ষার্থমেকঃ পুরুষঃ পুরাণঃ ৷  
শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যশরীরধারী  
কৃপাম্বুধির্যস্তমহং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ২৫৪ ॥

vairāgya-vidyā-nija-bhakti-yoga-  
detachment  knowledge  His own  devotional  yoga  
śikṣārtham ekaḥ puruṣaḥ purāṇaḥ  
to teach  the one  Person  primeval  
śrī kṛṣṇa-caitanya-śarīra-dhārī  
Śrī  the form of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  assuming  
kṛpāmbudhir yas tam ahaṁ prapadye  
the ocean of mercy  who  to Him  I  surrender

(254) “I take refuge in the one primeval Person, the ocean of mercy, who has assumed the form of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya to teach detachment, knowledge and devotion to Himself.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 6.74)[^91]

কালান্নষ্টং ভক্তিযোগং নিজং যঃ  
প্রাদুষ্কর্ত্তুং কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যনামা ৷  
আবির্ভূতস্তস্য পাদারবিন্দে  
গাঢ়ং গাঢ়ং লীয়তাং চিত্তভৃঙ্গঃ ॥ ২৫৫ ॥

kālān naṣṭaṁ bhakti-yogaṁ nijaṁ yaḥ  
by time  lost  devotional yoga  His own  who  
prāduṣkartuṁ kṛṣṇa-caitanya-nāmā  
to revive  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  by name  
āvirbhūtas tasya pādāravinde  
appeared  His  at the lotus feet  
gāḍhaṁ gāḍhaṁ līyatāṁ citta-bhṛṅgaḥ  
deeply  deeply  may be absorbed  the bee of the mind

(255) “He appeared under the name Kṛṣṇa Caitanya to revive His own devotional yoga, which had been lost through time. May the bee of my mind sink deep, deep into His lotus feet.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 6.75)[^92]

এই দুই শ্লোক — ভক্তকণ্ঠে মণিহার ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌমের কীর্ত্তি ঘোষে ঢক্কাবাদ্যাকার ॥ ২৫৬ ॥

ei dui śloka — bhakta-kaṇṭhe maṇi-hāra  
these  two  verses  on the devotees’ necks  a jewelled necklace  
sārvabhaumera kīrti ghoṣe ḍhakkā-vādyākāra  
Sārvabhauma’s  fame  proclaim  like the beat of a great drum

(256) These two verses are a jewelled necklace on the devotees’ necks, and like the beat of a great drum (*ḍhakkā*) they proclaim Sārvabhauma’s glory.

সার্ব্বভৌম হৈলা প্রভুর ভক্ত একজন ৷  
মহাপ্রভুর সেবা-বিনা নাহি অন্য মন ॥ ২৫৭ ॥

sārvabhauma hailā prabhura bhakta eka-jana  
Sārvabhauma  became  of the Lord  a devotee  one  
mahāprabhura sevā-vinā nāhi anya mana  
Mahāprabhu’s  service  except  there is not  other  thought

(257) Sārvabhauma became the Lord’s devotee, and he had no thought for anything but serving Mahāprabhu.

‘শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য শচীসূত গুণধাম’ ৷  
এই ধ্যান, এই জপ, লয় এই নাম ॥ ২৫৮ ॥

‘śrī kṛṣṇa-caitanya śacī-sūta guṇa-dhāma’  
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the son of Śacī  the abode of virtues  
ei dhyāna, ei japa, laya ei nāma  
this  meditation  this  japa  he takes  this  name

(258) “Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the son of Śacī, the abode of all virtues” — this was his meditation, this his japa; this was the name he chanted.

একদিন সার্ব্বভৌম প্রভু-আগে আইলা ৷  
নমস্কার করি’ শ্লোক পড়িতে লাগিলা ॥ ২৫৯ ॥

ekadina sārvabhauma prabhu-āge āilā  
one day  Sārvabhauma  before the Lord  came  
namaskāra kari’ śloka paḍite lāgilā  
obeisance  making  a verse  to recite  he began

(259) One day Sārvabhauma came before the Lord, offered obeisance and began to recite a verse.

ভাগবতের ‘ব্রহ্ম-স্তবে’র শ্লোক পড়িলা ৷  
শ্লোক-শেষে দুই অক্ষর-পাঠ ফিরাইলা ॥ ২৬০ ॥

bhāgavatera ‘brahma-stave’ra śloka paḍilā  
of the Bhāgavatam  of Brahmā’s prayers  a verse  he recited  
śloka-śeṣe dui akṣara-pāṭha phirāilā  
at the end of the verse  two  syllables  reading  he changed

(260) He recited a verse from Brahmā’s prayers in the *Bhāgavatam*, but at the end of the verse he changed the reading of two syllables:

তত্তেঽনুকম্পাং সুসমীক্ষমাণো ভুঞ্জান এবাত্মকৃতং বিপাকম্ ৷  
হৃদ্বাগ্বপুর্ভির্বিদধন্নমস্তে জীবেত যো ভক্তিপদে স দায়ভাক্ ॥ ২৬১ ॥

tat te ’nukampāṁ su-samīkṣamāṇo  
therefore  Your  mercy  earnestly awaiting  
bhuñjāna evātma-kṛtaṁ vipākam  
enduring  indeed  self-made  the fruits  
hṛd-vāg-vapurbhir vidadhan namas te  
with heart, speech and body  offering  obeisance  to You  
jīveta yo bhakti-pade sa dāya-bhāk  
lives  who  to the position of devotion  he  is heir

(261) “Therefore one who lives earnestly awaiting Your mercy, enduring the fruits of his own deeds and offering You obeisance with heart, words and body, is an heir to the position of devotion.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.8)[^93]

প্রভু কহে, ‘মুক্তিপদে’ — ইহা পাঠ হয় ৷  
‘ভক্তিপদে’ কেনে পড়, কি তোমার আশয় ॥ ২৬২ ॥

prabhu kahe, ‘mukti-pade’ — ihā pāṭha haya  
the Lord  says  “mukti-pade”  this  the reading  is  
‘bhakti-pade’ kene paḍa, ki tomāra āśaya  
“bhakti-pade”  why  you read  what  your  intention

(262) The Lord said, “The reading is ‘mukti-pade’. Why do you read ‘bhakti-pade’? What do you mean by it?”

ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে, — ‘ভক্তি’-সম নহে মুক্তি-ফল ৷  
ভগবদ্ভক্তিবিমুখের হয় দণ্ড কেবল ॥ ২৬৩ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya kahe, — ‘bhakti’-sama nahe mukti-phala  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  to bhakti  equal  is not  the fruit of liberation  
bhagavad-bhakti-vimukhera haya daṇḍa kevala  
of those averse to devotion to the Lord  it is  punishment  only

(263) The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “The fruit of liberation is not equal to bhakti. For those averse to devotion to Bhagavān it is only a punishment.[^94]

কৃষ্ণের বিগ্রহ যেই সত্য নাহি মানে ৷  
যেই নিন্দা-যুদ্ধাদিক করে তাঁর সনে ॥ ২৬৪ ॥

সেই দুইর দণ্ড — হয় ‘ব্রহ্মসাযুজ্য-মুক্তি’ ৷  
তার মুক্তি ফল নহে, যেই করে ভক্তি ॥ ২৬৫ ॥

kṛṣṇera vigraha yei satya nāhi māne  
Kṛṣṇa’s  form  who  as real  not  accepts  
yei nindā-yuddhādika kare tām̐ra sane  
who  blasphemy, war and the like  does  Him  with  
sei duira daṇḍa — haya ‘brahma-sāyujya-mukti’  
of these  two  the punishment  is  liberation by merging in Brahman  
tāra mukti phala nahe, yei kare bhakti  
his  liberation  fruit  is not  who  does  bhakti

(264–265) Those who do not accept Kṛṣṇa’s form as real, and those who blaspheme Him, fight against Him and the like — for both of these the punishment is liberation by merging in Brahman (*brahma-sāyujya-mukti*). But for one who practises bhakti, liberation is not the fruit.

যদ্যপি মুক্তি হয় এই পঞ্চপ্রকার ৷  
সালোক্য-সামীপ্য-সারূপ্য-সাষ্টি-সাযুজ্য আর ॥ ২৬৬ ॥

yadyapi mukti haya ei pañca-prakāra  
although  liberation  is  of these  five kinds  
sālokya-sāmīpya-sārūpya-sāṣṭi-sāyujya āra  
sālokya, sāmīpya, sārūpya, sārṣṭi, sāyujya  and

(266) Although liberation is of five kinds — *sālokya*, *sāmīpya*, *sārūpya*, *sārṣṭi* and *sāyujya* —

‘সালোক্যাদি’ চারি যদি হয় সেবা-দ্বার ৷  
তবু কদাচিৎ ভক্ত করে অঙ্গীকার ॥ ২৬৭ ॥

‘sālokyādi’ cāri yadi haya sevā-dvāra  
sālokya and the rest  four  if  are  a door to service  
tabu kadācit bhakta kare aṅgīkāra  
still  occasionally  the devotee  makes  acceptance

(267) if the four beginning with *sālokya* become a door to service, a devotee may still sometimes accept them.[^95]

‘সাযুজ্য’ শুনিতে ভক্তের হয় ঘৃণা-ভয় ৷  
নরক বাঞ্ছয়ে, তবু সাযুজ্য না লয় ॥ ২৬৮ ॥

‘sāyujya’ śunite bhaktera haya ghṛṇā-bhaya  
of “sāyujya”  hearing  the devotee’s  there is  disgust and fear  
naraka vāñchaye, tabu sāyujya nā laya  
hell  he desires  yet  sāyujya  not  he accepts

(268) But the devotee feels disgust and fear at the very word “sāyujya”. He would rather wish for hell than accept *sāyujya*.

ব্রহ্মে, ঈশ্বরে সাযুজ্য দুই ত’ প্রকার ৷  
ব্রহ্ম-সাযুজ্য হৈতে ঈশ্বর-সাযুজ্য ধিক্কার ॥ ২৬৯ ॥

brahme, īśvare sāyujya dui ta’ prakāra  
in Brahman  in the Lord  merging  of two  indeed  kinds  
brahma-sāyujya haite īśvara-sāyujya dhikkāra  
merging in Brahman  than  merging in the Lord  more contemptible

(269) *Sāyujya* is of two kinds — merging in Brahman and merging in the Lord — and merging in the Lord is even more contemptible than merging in Brahman.”[^96]

সালোক্য-সাষ্টি-সামীপ্য-সারূপ্যৈকত্বমপ্যুত ৷  
দীয়মানং ন গৃহ্ণন্তি বিনা মৎ-সেবনং জনাঃ ॥ ২৭০ ॥

sālokya-sāṣṭi-sāmīpya-  
residence in My world  equal opulence  nearness  
sārūpyaikatvam apy uta  
likeness of form  oneness  even  and  
dīyamānaṁ na gṛhṇanti  
being given  not  accept  
vinā mat-sevanaṁ janāḥ  
without  My service  the people

(270) “My devotees accept nothing but service to Me — not even when they are offered residence in My world, opulence equal to Mine, nearness to Me, a form like Mine, or even oneness with Me.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.29.13)[^97]

প্রভু কহে, — ‘মুক্তিপদে’র আর অর্থ হয় ৷  
মুক্তিপদ-শব্দে ‘সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর’ কহয় ॥ ২৭১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — ‘mukti-pade’ra āra artha haya  
the Lord  says  of “mukti-pade”  another  meaning  there is  
mukti-pada-śabde ‘sākṣāt īśvara’ kahaya  
by the word “mukti-pada”  directly  the Lord  is meant

(271) The Lord said, “‘Mukti-pade’ has another meaning: the word ‘mukti-pada’ denotes the Lord Himself.

মুক্তি পদে যাঁর, সেই ‘মুক্তিপদ’ হয় ৷  
কিম্বা নবম পদার্থ ‘মুক্তির’ সমাশ্রয় ॥ ২৭২ ॥

mukti pade yām̐ra, sei ‘mukti-pada’ haya  
liberation  at the feet  whose  He  “mukti-pada”  is  
kimvā navama padārtha ‘muktira’ samāśraya  
or  the ninth  subject  of mukti  the shelter

(272) He at whose feet liberation lies is ‘mukti-pada’; or else it means the shelter of mukti, the ninth subject of the *Bhāgavatam*.[^98]

দুই-অর্থে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি, কেনে পাঠ ফিরি ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — ও-পাঠ কহিতে না পারি ॥ ২৭৩ ॥

dui-arthe ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahi, kene pāṭha phiri  
in both meanings  Kṛṣṇa  I say  why  the reading  change  
sārvabhauma kahe, — o-pāṭha kahite nā pāri  
Sārvabhauma  says  that reading  to say  not  I can

(273) In both meanings it names Kṛṣṇa, so why change the reading?” Sārvabhauma said, “I cannot bring myself to say that reading.

যদ্যপি তোমার অর্থ এই শব্দে কয় ৷  
তথাপি ‘আশ্লিষ্য-দোষে’ কহন না যায় ॥ ২৭৪ ॥

yadyapi tomāra artha ei śabde kaya  
although  Your  meaning  this  word  expresses  
tathāpi ‘āśliṣya-doṣe’ kahana nā yāya  
still  by the fault of ambiguity  saying  not  is possible

(274) Although this word does convey Your meaning, still, because of the fault of ambiguity (*āśliṣya-doṣa*), it cannot be said.[^99]

যদ্যপি ‘মুক্তি’-শব্দের হয় পঞ্চ বৃত্তি ৷  
‘রূঢ়িবৃত্ত্যে’ কহে তবু ‘সাযুজ্যে’ প্রতীতি ॥ ২৭৫ ॥

yadyapi ‘mukti’-śabdera haya pañca vṛtti  
although  of the word “mukti”  there are  five  senses  
‘rūḍhi-vṛttye’ kahe tabu ‘sāyujye’ pratīti  
by conventional usage  it says  still  sāyujya  the sense

(275) Although the word ‘mukti’ has five senses, in its conventional usage (*rūḍhi-vṛtti*) it still conveys *sāyujya*.[^100]

মুক্তি-শব্দ কহিতে মনে হয় ঘৃণা-ত্রাস ৷  
ভক্তি-শব্দ কহিতে মনে হয় ত’ উল্লাস ॥ ২৭৬ ॥

mukti-śabda kahite mane haya ghṛṇā-trāsa  
the word “mukti”  saying  in the mind  there is  disgust and dread  
bhakti-śabda kahite mane haya ta’ ullāsa  
the word “bhakti”  saying  in the mind  there is  indeed  delight

(276) When I say the word ‘mukti’, my mind feels disgust and dread; when I say the word ‘bhakti’, it fills with delight.”

শুনিয়া হাসেন প্রভু আনন্দিত-মনে ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যে কৈল প্রভু দৃঢ় আলিঙ্গনে ॥ ২৭৭ ॥

śuniyā hāsena prabhu ānandita-mane  
hearing  laughs  the Lord  joyful at heart  
bhaṭṭācārye kaila prabhu dṛḍha āliṅgane  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  made  the Lord  a firm  embrace

(277) Hearing this, the Lord laughed, joyful at heart, and embraced the Bhaṭṭācārya firmly.

যেই ভট্টাচার্য্য পড়ে পড়ায় মায়াবাদে ৷  
তাঁর ঐছে বাক্য স্ফুরে চৈতন্য-প্রসাদে ॥ ২৭৮ ॥

yei bhaṭṭācārya paḍe paḍāya māyāvāde  
which  Bhaṭṭācārya  studies  teaches  Māyāvāda  
tām̐ra aiche vākya sphure caitanya-prasāde  
his  such  words  manifest  by Caitanya’s grace

(278) The Bhaṭṭācārya had once studied and taught Māyāvāda, yet by Caitanya’s grace such words now sprang from his lips.

লোহাকে যাবৎ স্পর্শি’ হেম নাহি করে ৷  
তাবৎ স্পর্শমণি কেহ চিনিতে না পারে ॥ ২৭৯ ॥

lohāke yāvat sparśi’ hema nāhi kare  
iron  as long as  touching  gold  not  it makes  
tāvat sparśa-maṇi keha cinite nā pāre  
so long  the touchstone  anyone  to recognise  not  can

(279) Until a touchstone turns iron into gold by its touch, no one can recognise it as a touchstone.[^101]

ভট্টাচার্য্যের বৈষ্ণবতা দেখি’ সর্ব্বজন ৷  
প্রভুকে জানিল — ‘সাক্ষাৎ ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন’ ॥ ২৮০ ॥

bhaṭṭācāryera vaiṣṇavatā dekhi’ sarva-jana  
the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  Vaiṣṇavism  seeing  all the people  
prabhuke jānila — ‘sākṣāt vrajendra-nandana’  
the Lord  they knew  directly  the son of the king of Vraja

(280) Seeing the Bhaṭṭācārya become a Vaiṣṇava, everyone knew the Lord to be the son of the king of Vraja Himself.

কাশীমিশ্র-আদি যত নীলাচলবাসী ৷  
শরণ লইল সবে প্রভু-পদে আসি’ ॥ ২৮১ ॥

kāśī-miśra-ādi yata nīlācala-vāsī  
Kāśī Miśra and the others  all  residents of Nīlācala  
śaraṇa la-ila sabe prabhu-pade āsi’  
shelter  took  all  at the Lord’s feet  coming

(281) Kāśī Miśra and all the other residents of Nīlācala came and took shelter at the Lord’s feet.

সেই সব কথা আগে করিব বর্ণন ৷  
এবে কহি প্রভুর দক্ষিণ-যাত্রা-বিবরণ ॥ ২৮২ ॥

sei saba kathā āge kariba varṇana  
those  all  matters  later  I shall make  description  
ebe kahi prabhura dakṣiṇa-yātrā-vivaraṇa  
now  I tell  the Lord’s  of the southern journey  the account

(282) All this I shall describe later; now I tell the story of the Lord’s journey to the south.

সার্ব্বভৌম করে যৈছে প্রভুর সেবন ৷  
যৈছে পরিপাটী করে ভিক্ষা-নির্ব্বাহণ ॥ ২৮৩ ॥

sārvabhauma kare yaiche prabhura sevana  
Sārvabhauma  does  how  the Lord’s  service  
yaiche paripāṭī kare bhikṣā-nirvāhaṇa  
how  splendidly  he does  of meals  arranging

(283) How Sārvabhauma served the Lord, and how splendidly he arranged His meals —

বিস্তারিয়া আগে তাহা করিব বর্ণন ৷  
এই মহাপ্রভুর লীলা, সার্ব্বভৌম-মিলন ॥ ২৮৪ ॥

ইহা যেই শ্রদ্ধা করি’ করয়ে শ্রবণ ৷  
জ্ঞান-কর্ম্মপাশ হৈতে হয় বিমোচন ॥ ২৮৫ ॥

vistāriyā āge tāhā kariba varṇana  
in detail  later  that  I shall make  description  
ei mahāprabhura līlā, sārvabhauma-milana  
this  Mahāprabhu’s  pastime  the meeting with Sārvabhauma  
ihā yei śraddhā kari’ karaye śravaṇa  
this  whoever  faith  having  does  hearing  
jñāna-karma-pāśa haite haya vimocana  
of jñāna and karma  the snare  from  there is  release

(284–285) all this I shall describe in detail later. This is Mahāprabhu’s pastime of meeting with Sārvabhauma; whoever hears it with faith is freed from the snares of *jñāna* and *karma*.

শ্রদ্ধায় চৈতন্যলীলা শুনে যেই জন ৷  
অচিরে মিলয়ে তাঁরে চৈতন্যচরণ ॥ ২৮৬ ॥

śraddhāya caitanya-līlā śune yei jana  
with faith  Caitanya’s pastimes  hears  who  person  
acire milaye tām̐re caitanya-caraṇa  
soon  are attained  by him  Caitanya’s feet

(286) Whoever hears Caitanya’s pastimes with faith soon attains His feet.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ২৮৭ ॥

śrī rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(287) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

---

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to Gaura, who conquered Sārvabhauma”. *Anubhāṣya*: *sarva-bhūmā* — He whose greatness surpasses all that is not Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Paramātmā Gauracandra; *kutarka* — false arguments, misconceptions about the nature of the jīva and its activity; *bhakti-bhūmānam ācarat* — He made him a vessel full of pure bhakti, that is, a servant of His feet. The verse plays on the words *sārvabhauma* (“lord of all the earth”), *sarva-bhūmā* and *bhakti-bhūmā*.

[^2]: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *paḍichā* is a temple officer, a kind of overseer; this *paḍichā* was a disciple of Sārvabhauma. Heading in the edition: “Sārvabhauma happens to see the Lord and saves Him from a blow”.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya*: Sārvabhauma then lived south of the temple, in Bālukhaṇḍa, on the bank of the Mārkaṇḍeya-saras pond; the house is now known as the Gaṅgāmātā Maṭha.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* and the *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi*): the *sāttvika* symptoms may be “smouldering”, “flaming”, “burning” or “blazing” (*uddīpta*); *sūddīpta* is a special kind of the blazing, when all the *sāttvika* symptoms, multiplied many times over, reach the highest limit of prema. An eternally perfect (*nitya-siddha*) devotee is an eternal associate of the Lord.

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya*: *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva* is beyond the reach even of the queens of Dvārakā — only the gopīs of Vraja experience it; its highest stage, *mādana*, is found in Śrī Rādhikā alone. Heading in the edition: “Unworldly *mahābhāva* in the Lord’s body”.

[^7]: Heading in the edition: “Nityānanda and His companions come from Āṭhāranālā to Purī and hear from the people that the Lord is in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s house”.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: Maheśvara Viśārada, a fellow student of Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, lived in Vidyānagara near Samudragaḍa; his sons were Madhusūdana Vācaspati and Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma, and his son-in-law was Gopīnātha Ācārya, the husband of Sārvabhauma’s sister.

[^9]: Heading in the edition: “Love for the Lord is greater than for Jagannātha”.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: *lāphrā-vyañjana* is a vegetable dish in which various vegetables are stewed together and seasoned with cumin, pepper and mustard.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: it is customary to greet sannyāsīs with the words *oṁ namo nārāyaṇāya*. The smṛti rule for sannyāsīs is to give neither blessings nor obeisances, but a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī knows himself to be a servant of Kṛṣṇa and so gives everyone the compassionate blessing, “May your mind be fixed on Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet.”

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pūrvāśrama* — the name by which He was known and the place where He lived as a householder, before taking sannyāsa.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: by the excellence of Your nature You are worthy of my worship, and by Your outward āśrama, as a sannyāsī, of the worship of householders like us; so I am Your servant.

[^14]: Heading in the edition: “The humility of the Lord, who honours others (*mānada-dharma*)”.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: on seeing Śrī Jagannātha I lost consciousness; you took care of Me and brought Me back from the inner state to the outer.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: although Mahāprabhu had every right of a true sannyāsī, in humility He thought it proper to be known by the name of a *brahmacārī*, a disciple of a sannyāsī, and so showed the very pattern of natural humility.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: of the ten names of sannyāsīs established by Śaṅkara, in the Śṛṅgerī Maṭha the title “Sarasvatī” is held the highest, “Bhāratī” middling and “Purī” the lowest. Sārvabhauma means that the name “Śrī Kṛṣṇa” is the best of names (“Caitanya” being His *brahmacārī* name), but He did not enter the highest, Sarasvatī, sampradāya.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: one who is proud of his position seeks a high rank, but one who wishes to serve Hari humbly, owning nothing, does not disdain even the Bhāratī sampradāya.

[^19]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: I shall lead Him onto the path of exclusive non-dualism (*kevalādvaita*), founded on knowledge, for which this world of māyā is worthless.

[^20]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *yoga-paṭṭa* is a special garment of sannyāsīs. *Anubhāṣya*: if He wishes to enter the higher Sarasvatī sampradāya, I shall arrange for a sannyāsī of that sampradāya to give Him the *yoga-paṭṭa* and the other articles of renunciation once again.

[^21]: Heading in the edition: “On the path of argument Bhagavān is not attained; on the path of *śrauta* (hearing) He is easily attained”.

[^22]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the disciples reason after the Nyāya: as from smoke on a mountain one infers fire, so from the world, which must have a cause, one infers the Lord as the cause of the universe.

[^23]: The prayer of Brahmā. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: those who search for a long time, reasoning about the scriptures by inference, do not come to know this truth.

[^24]: Heading in the edition: “Though Gopīnātha honours others, seeing the Bhaṭṭācārya’s godlessness he shows him no deference”.

[^25]: The *Anubhāṣya* quotes *Kaṭha Upaniṣad*, 1.2.23: “This Self is not attained by exposition, nor by intellect, nor by much learning; He is attained by the one whom He Himself chooses, and to him He reveals His own form.”

[^26]: Heading in the edition: “Sārvabhauma’s false argument”.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Reality (*vastu*) and its potency are one, so knowledge of the Reality comes from the Reality alone; the undivided Reality cannot be known by divided knowledge — only its mercy reveals it.

[^28]: So according to the *Anubhāṣya*: you have seen Bhagavān’s great absorption in love, yet you did not recognise the Lord in this unworldly Person full of love, and you regard even such a pastime of His as māyic, mere worldly behaviour. The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* and the heading in the edition (“To see the evident signs of the Lord and still not believe in Him — this is the play of māyā”) also allow the reading: covered by the Lord’s māyā, you could not recognise Him as the Lord.

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya*: *iṣṭa-goṣṭhī* — a circle of people gathered for a desired end; *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: I reason as those do who seek the desired truth.

[^30]: The *Anubhāṣya* quotes *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, 7.9.38: “You protect dharma according to the ages, but in Kali You come hidden, and so You are Triyuga”; Śrīdhara Svāmī: “In Kali You are hidden and do not act [as in the other ages]; therefore You are known as appearing in three ages.”

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: see the *Anubhāṣya* on Ādi 3.49 and the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* on Ādi 3.51.

[^32]: *Anubhāṣya*: a *līlā-avatāra* is an avatāra whose pastimes are full of wonders, effortless, ever new and dependent on His will alone (Matsya, Kūrma, Rāma, Nṛsiṁha, Vāmana, Varāha and others; the *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta* counts twenty-five).

[^33]: The same verse as Ādi 3.36; translation as there. The words of Garga Muni to Nanda Mahārāja. Heading in the edition: “The avatāras of four colours in the four ages”.

[^34]: *Anubhāṣya*: the sage Karabhājana answers Nimi, the king of Videha; the tantras are the *Pañcarātra* and other Sātvata tantras.

[^35]: The same verse as Ādi 3.51; translation as there.

[^36]: The same verse as Ādi 3.49; translation as there.

[^37]: Heading in the edition: “Gopīnātha disregards the Bhaṭṭācārya and does not take him seriously”.

[^38]: Heading in the edition: “Knowledge of Bhagavān’s greatness comes only by Bhagavān’s mercy”.

[^39]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is the secret prayer *Haṁsa-guhya*, offered by Prajāpati Dakṣa to Śrī Hari; the potencies spoken of are the external ones, māyā and *avidyā*.

[^40]: *Tāṁra mandire* — “to his house”; heading in the edition: “After seeing Jagannātha together with Sārvabhauma, the Lord goes to his house”.

[^41]: *Anubhāṣya*: Vedānta here is Śaṅkara’s *Śārīraka-bhāṣya* on the *Brahma-sūtra*, which establishes a Brahman without attributes; to delight always in the sayings of Vedānta is the dharma of a sannyāsī.

[^42]: Heading in the edition: “For seven days the Lord hears Vedānta from Sārvabhauma’s lips and keeps silent, not accepting the Māyāvāda commentary”.

[^43]: *Anubhāṣya*: you do not explain the primary meaning of Vyāsa’s sūtras, which is obtained through the direct sense of words (*abhidhā-vṛtti*), but cover it and invent an imaginary meaning by *lakṣaṇā*.

[^44]: Heading in the edition (at 131): “In its direct sense the meaning of the *Brahma-sūtra* is simple; the indirect sense, resting on conjecture, covers it”.

[^45]: *Anubhāṣya*: see Śrī Jīva’s *Tattva-sandarbha* (10–11) and the commentaries on the sūtras *śāstra-yonitvāt*, *tarkāpratiṣṭhānāt* and *śrutes tu śabda-mūlatvāt*; Śrī Jīva (*Sarva-saṁvādinī*): the root proof is sound (*śabda*) alone — words free from error, inattention, the wish to deceive and the imperfection of the senses.

[^46]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: to interpret the Vedic sayings by *lakṣaṇā* is to subject them to inference and so destroy their self-evidence.

[^47]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: by its greatness Brahman bears the signs of the Lord, and when the Lord is seen in the fullness of all His opulences, that Brahman proves to be Bhagavān Himself.

[^48]: The reference follows the heading in the edition: “words of the *Hayaśīrṣa-pañcarātra*, quoted in the sixth act of the *Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* as its twenty-first verse”. *Anubhāṣya*: *nirviśeṣa* — Brahman without attributes, pure consciousness alone; *saviśeṣa* — possessing name, form, qualities and pastimes; *abhidhatte* — denotes in the direct sense (*abhidhā-vṛtti*). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: both aspects of Bhagavān are eternal, but it is the one with features that is experienced in the world, and so it is the stronger.

[^49]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this is the sense of the śruti *yato vā imāni bhūtāni jāyante* (*Taittirīya Upaniṣad*, Bhṛgu-vallī, 1). Heading in the edition: “In every case-relation it is Śrī Bhagavān who is meant”.

[^50]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Supreme Brahman has three signs — He is the ablative, the instrumental and the locative case — and by these three eternal signs it is seen that Bhagavān eternally has features. *Anubhāṣya*: see Śrīdhara Svāmī’s commentary on *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, 6.4.30.

[^51]: *Anubhāṣya*: at the moment of that glance material eyes did not yet exist; so Brahman with features had an eternal spiritual mind with which He thought of creation and eternal spiritual eyes with which He looked upon prakṛti.

[^52]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is the prayer of Brahmā after Śrī Kṛṣṇa had crushed his pride; *pūrṇam* — undivided, *sanātanam* — eternally manifest.

[^53]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Māyāvādī abandons the direct sense (*abhidhā-vṛtti*) and through *lakṣaṇā* sets up the doctrine of featurelessness; yet even this “featurelessness” serves only to distinguish Brahman from material features.

[^54]: *Anubhāṣya*: the pure monists hold potency to be a passing state born of ignorance, but the three potencies abide in Brahman eternally. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: these three natural potencies are accepted in many statements of scripture that go back to the śruti “His supreme potency is heard to be manifold” (*Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad*, 6.8).

[^55]: The verse is Ādi 7.119; the translation follows it.

[^56]: *Anubhāṣya*: *sarva-gā* — able to enter both spirit and matter; *kṣetra-jña-śakti* — the potency called the jīva; *yayā* — by *avidyā*, the māyā that turns one away from Bhagavān; *atra* — here, in saṁsāra, the realm of Devī. The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* takes *sarva-gā* as “all-knowing”.

[^57]: *Anubhāṣya*: because of *avidyā* the jīva potency is reckoned now higher, now lower. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: removed from the working of the spiritual potency, the jīva enters the wheel of karma and attains now a high, now a low position.

[^58]: The verse is Ādi 4.63; the translation follows it. Here the second quarter reads *sarva-saṁśraye* (“the shelter of all”), in Ādi 4.63 *sarva-saṁsthitau* (“the basis of all”); the sense is the same.

[^59]: Compare Ādi 4.62. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this samvit is knowledge related to Kṛṣṇa.

[^60]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: since the *Gītā* calls the jīva a potency, one must also accept the oneness of the jīva with the Lord, according to the Vedānta saying “the potency and the possessor of potency are not different”; this inconceivable oneness and difference (*acintya-bhedābheda*) is the secret of the truth of the Lord and the jīva.

[^61]: *Anubhāṣya*: *bhinnā prakṛtiḥ* — the potency called external; mind and intelligence are named separately because they are the chief among the elements. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: these eight are manifestations of the inferior (*aparā*) potency; the jīva is distinct from them.

[^62]: The verse is Ādi 7.118; the translation follows it.

[^63]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *pāṣaṇḍī* is one who takes the eternal form of Bhagavān, full of form, qualities and pastimes, to be a transformation of material goodness and therefore does not serve Him; devotees do not touch him or look at him.

[^64]: Heading in the edition: “The Māyāvādī follows the Vedas in word, but in truth he is a hidden Buddhist”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: a secret enemy who comes in the guise of a friend is far more dangerous than an open one.

[^65]: Heading in the edition: “The *Brahma-sūtra* intends the doctrine of the transformation of potency (*śakti-pariṇāma-vāda*)”. *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 7.121–133.

[^66]: *Anubhāṣya*: by the power within it the gem produces something other than itself — gold — and remains a gem; so the Lord transforms His māyā-śakti into the changing world of the guṇas, while His own nature stays unchanged.

[^67]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śaṅkarācārya feared that if *pariṇāma-vāda* were accepted, the sūtra *janmādy asya yataḥ* would be called faulty and its author Vyāsa mistaken; so he put forward invented arguments and declared *vivarta-vāda* to be the truth.

[^68]: *Anubhāṣya*: the praṇava is the form of the Lord in His name (*nāma-vigraha*); it alone is the *mahā-vākya*.

[^69]: *Anubhāṣya*: this saying is addressed to the bewildered jīva, so that it may not identify itself with the body; the Māyāvādīs, instead of taking shelter of the name, *oṁkāra*, put forward *tat tvam asi*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the saying explains the spiritual nature of the jīva, but it is not a *mahā-vākya*.

[^70]: *Anubhāṣya*: *vitaṇḍā* — arguing only to refute another’s view without establishing one’s own; *chala* — a refutation that ascribes to a word some invented object in place of its real sense; *nigraha* — the defeat of the opposing side.

[^71]: Compare Ādi 7.146. Heading in the edition: “The Lord establishes the true teaching of the Vedas”.

[^72]: Some editions read *veda-vākye karena lakṣaṇā* (“he gives the self-evident words of the Veda an indirect reading”); the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition has *veda-vākye nā kariye lakṣaṇā*, and the translation follows it.

[^73]: Heading in the edition: “By the Lord’s command Śaṅkara bewilders the asuras”. The *Anubhāṣya* quotes the Lord’s words from the *Kūrma Purāṇa* (Pūrva-bhāga, 16.115–117): to keep the world from sinners who reject the Vedas, Rudra, at Mādhava’s bidding, composed bewildering scriptures.

[^74]: The reference follows the heading in the edition. *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord is speaking to Śiva; *sva-āgamaiḥ kalpitaiḥ* — by his own tantras and other books, fallen from the truth; *sṛṣṭir uttarottarā* — so that the inclination to saṁsāra may keep growing.

[^75]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pracchannam* — hypocritically occupied with discussing the Vedas, yet opposed to the path of śruti; *brāhmaṇa-mūrtinā* — in the body named Śaṅkara, born in the land of Malabar.

[^76]: *Anubhāṣya*: *ātmārāmāḥ* — those who delight in the ātmā, that is, in Bhagavān, whose nature is to play with Kṛṣṇa; *nirgranthāḥ* — free from the knot of desires born in the heart; *ahaitukī* — free from other desires, not covered by karma, jñāna and the like; *itthaṁ-bhūta-guṇaḥ* — Hari has the quality of attracting jīvas in every condition, liberated or not, and by His mercy frees those devoted to Māyāvāda from their doctrines.

[^77]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: eleven meanings for the eleven words of the verse, and seven more when *ātmārāma* is joined to each of the seven chief words — *munayaḥ*, *nirgranthāḥ*, *urukrama*, *ahaitukī*, *bhakti*, *guṇa* and *hari*; eighteen in all. *Anubhāṣya*: see Madhya 24.

[^78]: *Anubhāṣya*: eclipsing all the relationships and means invented by jñānīs, karmīs and those with other desires, these three realities — Bhagavān of inconceivable power, His potency and His qualities — steal the minds of both sādhakas and siddhas.

[^79]: *Anubhāṣya*: so Kṛṣṇa attracted the liberated sages, Sanaka and the others, and Śukadeva; see Madhya 24.112–116, 17.139 and *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, 3.15.43.

[^80]: Heading in the edition (for 202–203): “The Lord shows first the four-armed form, then the two-armed form”.

[^81]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is Śrīla Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya’s book *Suśloka-śataka*.

[^82]: *Anubhāṣya*: *aruṇodaya* is the period of four daṇḍas (about an hour and a half) before sunrise.

[^83]: The source follows the heading in the edition above verse 225. *Anubhāṣya*: *śuṣkam* — without juice, *paryuṣitam* — cooked several days before; Kṛṣṇa’s prasāda is to be honoured as soon as it is received.

[^84]: *Anubhāṣya*: *śiṣṭaiḥ* — by the Vaiṣṇavas; in honouring prasāda one takes no account of place, time or interruption.

[^85]: The last line follows the *Anubhāṣya*: *eṣām* — those who have surrendered have no thought of “I” and “mine” for the body, fit only to be eaten by animals. The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* reads it otherwise: to those who think “I” and “mine” of the body Bhagavān does not show mercy. *Anubhāṣya*: these are Brahmā’s words to Nārada on the Lord’s māyā and the glory of the devotees; *sarvātmanā* — with one’s whole being, not with a part of it.

[^86]: Heading in the edition: “The Bhaṭṭācārya’s love for the Lord surpasses even his love for Jagannātha”.

[^87]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Sārvabhauma asked which of the sixty-four limbs of sādhana-bhakti is the best, and Mahāprabhu answered, “Best of all is *nāma-saṅkīrtana* alone.”

[^88]: The verse is Ādi 7.76 and 17.21; the translation follows it.

[^89]: In some editions the order is *karaha yāñā*; the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition has *yāñā karaha*.

[^90]: Heading in the edition: “Even before the leaf reached the Lord, Mukunda kept a copy of the two verses”.

[^91]: Heading in the edition (for 254–255): “two verses composed by Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya and quoted in the *Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka*, act 6, 74”. *Anubhāṣya*: *vairāgya* — detachment from all but Kṛṣṇa; *vidyā* — realisation of the Supreme Lord; *nija-bhakti-yoga* — service to His name, form, qualities and pastimes; He is the ocean of mercy because even to those attached to matter He gives the prema of Vraja, fit only for the most exalted liberated souls.

[^92]: *Anubhāṣya*: *kālāt* — by the law of time, because other desires, karma and jñāna prevailed; *nijam* — full of Kṛṣṇa’s name, form, qualities and pastimes.

[^94]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the highest fruit of bhakti is prema-bhakti alone; mukti is not its fruit; for one averse to bhakti, *sāyujya-mukti* is only a kind of punishment.

[^95]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: these four are not so blameworthy, for they are like a door to service; yet the devotee of Kṛṣṇa does not accept even them, for birth after birth he desires only bhakti to Kṛṣṇa.

[^96]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *brahma-sāyujya* is the highest goal for the Māyāvādī Vedāntists, *īśvara-sāyujya* — *kaivalya* — that of Patañjali’s teaching; the second is even more to be shunned, for there meditation on the Lord with features yields a fruit far removed from Him.

[^98]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: He at whose feet mukti lies is Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the “tenth” subject; or else the ninth subject is mukti, and He who is its shelter is Śrī Kṛṣṇa. *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 2.91–92.

[^99]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *āśliṣya-doṣa* — the fault by which a word may be taken in two ways, so that its direct sense suffers somewhat.

[^100]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *rūḍhi-vṛtti* is the direct sense (*mukhya-vṛtti*).
