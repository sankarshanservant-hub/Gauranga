“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 6  
**Determining the Truth of Śrī Advaita**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে তং শ্রীমদদ্বৈতাচার্য্যমদ্ভুতচেষ্টিতম্ ৷  
যস্য প্রসাদাদজ্ঞোঽপি তৎস্বরূপং নিরূপয়েৎ ॥ ১ ॥

vande taṁ śrīmad-advaitā-  
I bow  to Him  Śrī Advaita-  
cāryam adbhuta-ceṣṭitam  
Ācārya  wondrous  (whose) deeds  
yasya prasādād ajño ’pi  
by whose  grace  an ignorant one  even  
tat-svarūpaṁ nirūpayet  
His  true nature  may describe

(1) I bow to Śrī Advaita Ācārya, whose deeds are wondrous. By His grace even an ignorant person may describe His true nature.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

পঞ্চ শ্লোকে কহিল শ্রীনিত্যানন্দ-তত্ত্ব ৷  
শ্লোকদ্বয়ে কহি অদ্বৈতাচার্য্যের মহত্ত্ব ॥ ৩ ॥

pañca śloke kahila śrī-nityānanda-tattva  
in five  verses  I have told  of Śrī Nityānanda  the truth  
śloka-dvaye kahi advaitācāryera mahattva  
in two verses  I tell  of Advaita Ācārya  the greatness

(3) In five verses I have told the truth of Śrī Nityānanda; now in two verses I tell the greatness of Advaita Ācārya.

মহাবিষ্ণুর্জগৎকর্ত্তা মায়য়া যঃ সৃজত্যদঃ ৷  
তস্যাবতার এবায়মদ্বৈতাচার্য্য ঈশ্বরঃ ॥ ৪ ॥

mahā-viṣṇur jagat-kartā  
Mahā-Viṣṇu  of the world  the maker  
māyayā yaḥ sṛjaty adaḥ  
by māyā  who  creates  this (world)  
tasyāvatāra evāyam  
His  avatāra  indeed  this  
advaitācārya īśvaraḥ  
Advaita Ācārya  the Lord

(4) Mahā-Viṣṇu is the maker of the world, who creates it through māyā; and this Lord Advaita Ācārya is His avatāra.[^1]

অদ্বৈতং হরিণাদ্বৈতাদাচার্য্যং ভক্তিশংসনাৎ ৷  
ভক্তাবতারমীশং তমদ্বৈতাচার্য্যমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ৫ ॥

advaitaṁ hariṇādvaitād  
Advaita  from Hari  being non-different  
ācāryaṁ bhakti-śaṁsanāt  
Ācārya  bhakti  from teaching  
bhaktāvatāram īśaṁ tam  
as a devotee descended  the Lord  Him  
advaitācāryam āśraye  
Advaita Ācārya  I take shelter of

(5) He is called Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, and Ācārya because He teaches bhakti. I take shelter of Him, the Lord Advaita Ācārya, who has descended as a devotee.[^2]

অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্য গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর ৷  
যাঁহার মহিমা নহে জীবের গোচর ॥ ৬ ॥

advaita-ācārya gosāñi sākṣāt īśvara  
Advaita  Ācārya  Gosāñi  Himself  the Lord  
yām̐hāra mahimā nahe jīvera gocara  
whose  greatness  not  of the jīva  within the range

(6) Advaita Ācārya Gosāñi is the Lord Himself; His greatness lies beyond the reach of the jīva.

মহাবিষ্ণু সৃষ্টি করেন জগদাদি কার্য্য ৷  
তাঁর অবতার সাক্ষাৎ অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য ॥ ৭ ॥

mahā-viṣṇu sṛṣṭi karena jagad-ādi kārya  
Mahā-Viṣṇu  creation  performs  of the world  and other  works  
tām̐ra avatāra sākṣāt advaita ācārya  
His  avatāra  direct  Advaita  Ācārya

(7) Mahā-Viṣṇu performs the creation of the world and its other works, and Advaita Ācārya is His direct avatāra.

যে পুরুষ সৃষ্টি-স্থিতি করেন মায়ায় ৷  
অনন্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ড সৃষ্টি করেন লীলায় ॥ ৮ ॥

ye puruṣa sṛṣṭi-sthiti karena māyāya  
which  Puruṣa  creation and maintenance  performs  through māyā  
ananta brahmāṇḍa sṛṣṭi karena līlāya  
endless  universes  creation  performs  in play

(8) That Puruṣa creates and maintains the world through māyā, and in His play He creates endless universes.

ইচ্ছায় অনন্ত মূর্ত্তি করেন প্রকাশ ৷  
এক এক মূর্ত্ত্যে করেন ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে প্রবেশ ॥ ৯ ॥

icchāya ananta mūrti karena prakāśa  
by His will  endless  forms  He makes  manifest  
eka eka mūrtye karena brahmāṇḍe praveśa  
in one  one  form (each)  He makes  into a universe  entrance

(9) By His will He manifests endless forms, and in one form each He enters every universe.

সে পুরুষের অংশ — অদ্বৈত, নাহি কিছু ভেদ ৷  
শরীর-বিশেষ তাঁর — নাহিক বিচ্ছেদ ॥ ১০ ॥

se puruṣera aṁśa — advaita, nāhi kichu bheda  
of that  Puruṣa  a part  Advaita  there is not  any  difference  
śarīra-viśeṣa tām̐ra — nāhika viccheda  
a particular body  His  there is no  separation

(10) Advaita is a part (*aṁśa*) of that Puruṣa, and there is no difference between Them at all: He is only another body of the Puruṣa, and the two are never apart.[^3]

সহায় করেন তাঁর লইয়া ‘প্রধান’ ৷  
কোটি ব্রহ্মাণ্ড করেন ইচ্ছায় নির্ম্মাণ ॥ ১১ ॥

sahāya karena tām̐ra laiyā ‘pradhāna’  
help  He gives  to Him  taking  pradhāna  
koṭi brahmāṇḍa karena icchāya nirmāṇa  
millions  of universes  He makes  by His will  the fashioning

(11) Taking pradhāna, the material basis of the world, Advaita assists the Puruṣa, and by His will He fashions millions of universes.[^4]

জগৎ-মঙ্গল অদ্বৈত, মঙ্গল-গুণধাম ৷  
মঙ্গল-চরিত্র সদা, ‘মঙ্গল’ যাঁর নাম ॥ ১২ ॥

jagat-maṅgala advaita, maṅgala-guṇa-dhāma  
of the world  the good  Advaita  of auspicious  qualities  the abode  
maṅgala-caritra sadā, ‘maṅgala’ yām̐ra nāma  
auspicious  conduct  always  ‘Maṅgala’  whose  name

(12) Advaita is the good of the whole world and the abode of all auspicious qualities. His conduct is ever auspicious, and His name is Maṅgala, “the Auspicious”.[^5]

কোটি অংশ, কোটি শক্তি, কোটি অবতার ৷  
এত লঞা সৃজে পুরুষ সকল সংসার ॥ ১৩ ॥

koṭi aṁśa, koṭi śakti, koṭi avatāra  
millions  of parts  millions  of potencies  millions  of avatāras  
eta lañā sṛje puruṣa sakala saṁsāra  
so many  taking  creates  the Puruṣa  the whole  world

(13) Taking millions of parts, millions of potencies and millions of avatāras, the Puruṣa creates the whole world.

মায়া যৈছে দুই অংশ — ‘নিমিত্ত’, ‘উপাদান’ ৷  
মায়া — ‘নিমিত্ত’-হেতু, উপাদান — ‘প্রধান’ ॥ ১৪ ॥

māyā yaiche dui aṁśa — ‘nimitta’, ‘upādāna’  
māyā  as  two  aspects  efficient  material  
māyā — ‘nimitta’-hetu, upādāna — ‘pradhāna’  
māyā  the efficient  cause  the material  pradhāna

(14) Māyā has two aspects, the efficient cause (*nimitta*) and the material cause (*upādāna*): as the efficient cause it is called māyā, and as the material cause, pradhāna.

পুরুষ ঈশ্বর ঐছে দ্বিমূর্ত্তি হইয়া ৷  
বিশ্ব-সৃষ্টি করে ‘নিমিত্ত’ ‘উপাদান’ লঞা ॥ ১৫ ॥

puruṣa īśvara aiche dvi-mūrti haiyā  
the Puruṣa  the Lord  likewise  two forms  becoming  
viśva-sṛṣṭi kare ‘nimitta’ ‘upādāna’ lañā  
of the universe  creation  performs  the efficient  the material  taking

(15) In the same way the Lord, the Puruṣa, assumes two forms and creates the universe with these two causes, the efficient and the material.

আপনে পুরুষ — বিশ্বের ‘নিমিত্ত’-কারণ ৷  
অদ্বৈত-রূপে ‘উপাদান’ হন নারায়ণ ॥ ১৬ ॥

āpane puruṣa — viśvera ‘nimitta’-kāraṇa  
Himself  the Puruṣa  of the world  the efficient  cause  
advaita-rūpe ‘upādāna’ hana nārāyaṇa  
of Advaita  in the form  the material cause  becomes  Nārāyaṇa

(16) The Puruṣa Himself is the efficient cause of the world, and in the form of Advaita, Nārāyaṇa becomes its material cause.

‘নিমিত্তাংশে’ করে তেঁহো মায়াতে ঈক্ষণ ৷  
‘উপাদান’ অদ্বৈত করেন ব্রহ্মাণ্ড-সৃজন ॥ ১৭ ॥

‘nimittāṁśe’ kare tem̐ho māyāte īkṣaṇa  
in the efficient aspect  performs  He  upon māyā  the glance  
‘upādāna’ advaita karena brahmāṇḍa-sṛjana  
as the material cause  Advaita  performs  of the universe  the creation

(17) As the efficient cause He casts His glance upon māyā; as the material cause, Advaita creates the universe.

যদ্যপি সাংখ্য মানে, ‘প্রধান’ — কারণ ৷  
জড় হইতে কভু নহে জগৎ-সৃজন ॥ ১৮ ॥

yadyapi sāṅkhya māne, ‘pradhāna’ — kāraṇa  
although  Sāṅkhya  holds  pradhāna  the cause  
jaḍa haite kabhu nahe jagat-sṛjana  
from inert matter  never  is  of the world  the creation

(18) Although Sāṅkhya holds pradhāna to be the cause, the world can never come into being from inert matter.[^6]

নিজ সৃষ্টিশক্তি প্রভু সঞ্চারে প্রধানে ৷  
ঈশ্বরের শক্ত্যে তবে হয়ে ত’ নির্ম্মাণে ॥ ১৯ ॥

nija sṛṣṭi-śakti prabhu sañcāre pradhāne  
His own  creative power  the Lord  infuses  into pradhāna  
īśvarera śaktye tabe haye ta’ nirmāṇe  
of the Lord  by the power  then  takes place  indeed  the fashioning

(19) The Lord infuses His own creative power into pradhāna, and then, by the Lord’s power, the world is fashioned.

অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্য — কোটিব্রহ্মাণ্ডের কর্ত্তা ৷  
আর এক এক মূর্ত্ত্যে ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের ভর্ত্তা ॥ ২০ ॥

advaita-ācārya — koṭi-brahmāṇḍera kartā  
Advaita  Ācārya  of millions of universes  the creator  
āra eka eka mūrtye brahmāṇḍera bhartā  
and  in one  one  form (each)  of the universe  the sustainer

(20) Advaita Ācārya is the creator of millions of universes, and in a form of His within each of them He is its sustainer.[^7]

সেই নারায়ণের মুখ্য অঙ্গ, — অদ্বৈত ৷  
‘অঙ্গ’ শব্দে অংশ করি’ কহে ভাগবত ॥ ২১ ॥

sei nārāyaṇera mukhya aṅga, — advaita  
of that  Nārāyaṇa  the principal  limb  Advaita  
‘aṅga’ śabde aṁśa kari’ kahe bhāgavata  
‘aṅga’  by the word  part  meaning  says  the Bhāgavata

(21) Advaita is the principal limb (*aṅga*) of Śrī Nārāyaṇa; and in the *Bhāgavatam* the word ‘aṅga’ means ‘aṁśa’, a partial manifestation.[^8]

নারায়ণস্ত্বং ন হি সর্ব্বদেহিনা-  
মাত্মাস্যধীশাখিললোকসাক্ষী ৷  
নারায়ণোঽঙ্গং নরভূজলায়না-  
ত্তচ্চাপি সত্যং ন তবৈব মায়া ॥ ২২ ॥

nārāyaṇas tvaṁ na hi sarva-dehinām  
Nārāyaṇa  You  not  indeed  of all  the embodied  
ātmāsy adhīśākhila-loka-sākṣī  
the Self  You are  O Lord  of all  worlds  the witness  
nārāyaṇo ’ṅgaṁ nara-bhū-jalāyanāt  
Nārāyaṇa  (Your) limb  from Nara  born  waters  abode  
tac cāpi satyaṁ na tavaiva māyā  
that  also  true  not  Your  indeed  māyā

(22) “Are You not Nārāyaṇa? For You are the Self of all embodied beings, O Lord, and the witness of all the worlds. Nārāyaṇa is Your portion — so called because His abode is the waters born of Nara; and that too is true, not Your māyā.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.14)[^9]

ঈশ্বরের ‘অঙ্গ’, অংশ — চিদানন্দময় ৷  
মায়ার সম্বন্ধ নাহি, এই শ্লোকে কয় ॥ ২৩ ॥

īśvarera ‘aṅga’, aṁśa — cid-ānanda-maya  
of the Lord  the aṅga  the aṁśa  full of consciousness and bliss  
māyāra sambandha nāhi, ei śloke kaya  
with māyā  connection  there is not  this  verse  says

(23) This verse says that the Lord’s aṅga, His aṁśa, is full of consciousness and bliss and has no connection with māyā.[^10]

‘অংশ’ না কহিয়া, কেনে কহ তাঁরে ‘অঙ্গ’ ৷  
‘অংশ’ হৈতে ‘অঙ্গ,’ যাতে হয় অন্তরঙ্গ ॥ ২৪ ॥

‘aṁśa’ nā kahiyā, kene kaha tām̐re ‘aṅga’  
‘aṁśa’  not  saying  why  do you call  Him  ‘aṅga’  
‘aṁśa’ haite ‘aṅga,’ yāte haya antaraṅga  
than ‘aṁśa’  ‘aṅga’  because  is  more intimate

(24) Why call Him ‘aṅga’ and not ‘aṁśa’? Because ‘aṅga’, a limb, is closer and more intimate than ‘aṁśa’, a part.

মহাবিষ্ণুর অংশ — অদ্বৈত গুণধাম ৷  
ঈশ্বরে অভেদ, তেঞি ‘অদ্বৈত’ পূর্ণ নাম ॥ ২৫ ॥

mahā-viṣṇura aṁśa — advaita guṇa-dhāma  
of Mahā-Viṣṇu  a part  Advaita  of qualities  the abode  
īśvare abheda, teñi ‘advaita’ pūrṇa nāma  
from the Lord  non-different  therefore  ‘Advaita’  full  name

(25) Advaita, the abode of all good qualities, is a part of Mahā-Viṣṇu. He is non-different from the Lord, and so the name Advaita, “Non-dual”, fits Him in full.[^11]

পূর্ব্বে যৈছে কৈল সর্ব্ব বিশ্বের-সৃজন ৷  
অবতরি’ কৈল এবে ভক্তি-প্রবর্ত্তন ॥ ২৬ ॥

pūrve yaiche kaila sarva viśvera-sṛjana  
before  as  He performed  of the whole  universe  the creation  
avatari’ kaila ebe bhakti-pravartana  
having descended  He performed  now  of bhakti  the establishment

(26) As once He created the whole universe, so now, having descended, He has established bhakti.

জীব নিস্তারিল কৃষ্ণভক্তি করি’ দান ৷  
গীতা-ভাগবতে কৈল ভক্তির ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ২৭ ॥

jīva nistārila kṛṣṇa-bhakti kari’ dāna  
the jīvas  He delivered  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  making  a gift  
gītā-bhāgavate kaila bhaktira vyākhyāna  
in the Gītā and Bhāgavata  He gave  of bhakti  the explanation

(27) He delivered the jīvas by giving them devotion to Kṛṣṇa, and He explained the *Gītā* and the *Bhāgavatam* in the light of bhakti.

ভক্তি-উপদেশ বিনু তাঁর নাহি কার্য্য ৷  
অতএব নাম হৈল ‘অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য’ ॥ ২৮ ॥

bhakti-upadeśa vinu tām̐ra nāhi kārya  
bhakti  teaching  except  His  there is no  work  
ataeva nāma haila ‘advaita ācārya’  
therefore  name  became  ‘Advaita Ācārya’

(28) He has no work but teaching bhakti; therefore His name became Advaita Ācārya.[^12]

বৈষ্ণবের গুরু তেঁহো জগতের আর্য্য ৷  
দুইনাম-মিলনে হৈল ‘অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্য’ ॥ ২৯ ॥

vaiṣṇavera guru tem̐ho jagatera ārya  
of the Vaiṣṇavas  the guru  He  of the world  the venerable  
dui-nāma-milane haila ‘advaita-ācārya’  
two names  by joining  became  ‘Advaita Ācārya’

(29) He is the guru of the Vaiṣṇavas and the venerable one of the whole world. Joined together, these two names became Advaita Ācārya.

কমল-নয়নের তেঁহো, যাতে ‘অঙ্গ’, ‘অংশ’ ৷  
‘কমলাক্ষ’ বলি’ ধরে নাম অবতংস ॥ ৩০ ॥

kamala-nayanera tem̐ho, yāte ‘aṅga’, ‘aṁśa’  
of the Lotus-eyed  He  because  ‘aṅga’  ‘aṁśa’  
‘kamalākṣa’ bali’ dhare nāma avataṁsa  
‘Kamalākṣa’  calling  He bears  the name  as a crown-jewel

(30) Because He is the limb and the part of the Lotus-eyed Lord, He bears the beautiful name Kamalākṣa, “Lotus-eyed”.[^13]

[^1]: The same verse as Ādi 1.12. The edition ascribes verses 6.4–6.5 to the diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara («শ্রীস্বরূপগোস্বামি-কড়চায় শ্লোক»; heading: “explanation of the twelfth and thirteenth of the first fourteen verses of the Ādi”). *Anubhāṣya*: Mahā-Viṣṇu, the maker of the world, is the shelter of the efficient cause (*nimitta*), and His avatāra, the Lord Advaita Ācārya, is the shelter of the material cause (*upādāna*).

[^2]: The same verse as Ādi 1.13. *Anubhāṣya*: He is Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, from the Viṣṇu-tattva; He is Ācārya because He instructs others in bhajana.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: one and the same māyā is called *pradhāna* as the material cause and *māyā* as the efficient cause. According to these two functions Mahā-Viṣṇu abides in two forms: in one, abiding in prakṛti, He is the efficient cause of the world — the form of Viṣṇu; in the other, abiding in pradhāna, He is Advaita in the form of Rudra. Therefore Advaita is in no way different from the Puruṣa; only Their bodies differ.

[^4]: The edition’s heading: “The Deity who presides over pradhāna, the material cause of the world.”

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Advaita is Mahā-Viṣṇu, and He is the Ācārya; His life, turned towards service, brings good to all; He is no material being and no shelter of the material guṇas. There were people of demoniac nature, bewildered by māyā and devoid of bhakti, who could not understand Him as the non-dual Viṣṇu and, clinging to the doctrine of bare non-duality (*kevalādvaita*), only pretended to follow Him; by His māyā the Ācārya let them feed their pride — and even this punishment of the faithless is nothing but auspicious conduct. Some say that Śrī Advaita’s other name was Maṅgala.

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya* (on 6.15) examines the Sāṅkhya doctrine at length and, following the *Vedānta-sūtra* (2.2.1–10) and Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa’s *Govinda-bhāṣya*, shows that inert pradhāna can be neither the material nor the efficient cause of the world, for a varied creation is impossible without a conscious principle. It adds that the Kapila who founded the Sāṅkhya opposed to the Vedas is not Lord Kapila, the son of Kardama and avatāra of Vāsudeva, but another — a jīva of the line of Agni, bewildered by māyā. On 6.18–6.19 the *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 5.58–5.66 and Madhya 20.259–261, 271, 276.

[^7]: The edition’s heading: “The two forms of Advaita Prabhu.” The form in which the Puruṣa enters each universe (6.9) is Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu.

[^8]: The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 3.67 (“‘aṅga’ means ‘aṁśa’”).

[^9]: The same verse as Ādi 2.30 and 3.68 (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “see Ādi 2.30”, where the *Anubhāṣya* explains the three meanings of the name Nārāyaṇa). Here the author cites it for the word *aṅgam*, “part”: Nārāyaṇa is Your *aṅga*, that is, Your *aṁśa* (6.21).

[^10]: The edition’s heading: “Whether aṅga or aṁśa, beyond māyā.” The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 3.69–3.70.

[^11]: The edition’s heading: “The meaning of the name ‘Advaita’.” *Pūrṇa nāma* — “a full name”, a name true in every respect.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya* (on 6.26–6.28): although Advaita Prabhu is Viṣṇu-tattva, the One who is served, He has no work but the good of the souls — awakening in them the desire to serve. Had He shown His pastimes only as the One who is served, the pleasure-seeking people of the world would have imitated Him and become godless adherents of bare non-duality, or worshippers of themselves as God; seeing this, He displayed the pastime of a servant of Viṣṇu, and that is His being the Ācārya. As for the wicked who defile the holy place and dress of the Ācārya and, instead of serving the Lord, dance the dance of sense-pleasure, the Ācārya utterly rejects them.

[^13]: The edition’s heading: “The meaning of the name ‘Kamalākṣa’.” *Avataṁsa* — “a crown, an ornament”: Kamalākṣa is the loveliest of names.
