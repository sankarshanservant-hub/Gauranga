“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 15  
**The Lord’s Pastime in the Garden**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

দুর্গমে কৃষ্ণভাবাব্ধৌ নিমগ্নোন্মগ্নচেতসা ৷  
গৌরেণ হরিণা প্রেমমর্য্যাদা ভূরি দর্শিতা ॥ ১ ॥

durgame kṛṣṇa-bhāvābdhau  
in the unfathomable  of Kṛṣṇa  in the ocean of feelings  
nimagnonmagna-cetasā  
whose heart sank and rose  
gaureṇa hariṇā prema-  
by Gaura  Hari  of prema  
maryādā bhūri darśitā  
the greatness  abundantly  was shown

(1) In the unfathomable ocean of feelings for Kṛṣṇa, Gaurahari’s heart now sank and now rose; and so He revealed the greatness of prema in all its fullness.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য অধীশ্বর ৷  
জয় নিত্যানন্দ পূর্ণানন্দ-কলেবর ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya adhīśvara  
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the Supreme Lord  
jaya nityānanda pūrṇānanda-kalevara  
glory  to Nityānanda  full of bliss  whose body

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the Supreme Lord! Glory to Nityānanda, whose body is full of bliss!

জয়াদ্বৈতাচার্য্য কৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-প্রিয়তম ৷  
জয় শ্রীবাস-আদি প্রভুর ভক্তগণ ॥ ৩ ॥

jayādvaitācārya kṛṣṇa-caitanya-priyatama  
glory to Advaitācārya  to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  most dear  
jaya śrīvāsa-ādi prabhura bhakta-gaṇa  
glory  to Śrīvāsa  and the others  the Lord’s  devotees

(3) Glory to Advaitācārya, most dear to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Glory to Śrīvāsa and all the Lord’s devotees!

এইমত মহাপ্রভু রাত্রি-দিবসে ৷  
আত্মস্ফূর্ত্তি নাহি কৃষ্ণভাবাবেশে ॥ ৪ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu rātri-divase  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  night  and day  
ātma-sphūrti nāhi kṛṣṇa-bhāvāveśe  
of Himself  awareness  none  absorbed in love for Kṛṣṇa

(4) In this way, night and day, Mahāprabhu was so absorbed in love for Kṛṣṇa that He had no awareness of Himself.

কভু ভাবে মগ্ন, কভু অর্দ্ধ-বাহ্যস্ফূর্ত্তি ৷  
কভু বাহ্যস্ফূর্ত্তি, — তিন রীতে প্রভুস্থিতি ॥ ৫ ॥

kabhu bhāve magna, kabhu arddha-bāhya-sphūrti  
sometimes  in feeling  immersed  sometimes  half  external  awareness  
kabhu bāhya-sphūrti, — tina rīte prabhu-sthiti  
sometimes  external  awareness  in three  ways  the Lord’s  state

(5) Sometimes He was immersed in feeling, sometimes half aware of the outer world, sometimes fully aware of it: the Lord passed through these three states.

স্নান, দর্শন, ভোজন দেহ-স্বভাবে হয় ৷  
কুমারের চাক যেন সতত ফিরয় ॥ ৬ ॥

snāna, darśana, bhojana deha-svabhāve haya  
bathing  seeing the Lord  eating  by the body’s  nature  happen  
kumārera cāka yena satata phiraya  
of the potter  the wheel  as  constantly  turns

(6) Bathing, going to see Lord Jagannātha and eating went on of themselves, by the body’s own nature, as a potter’s wheel keeps turning.[^2]

একদিন করেন প্রভু জগন্নাথ দরশন ৷  
জগন্নাথে দেখে সাক্ষাৎ ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ৭ ॥

ekadina karena prabhu jagannātha daraśana  
one day  makes  the Lord  of Jagannātha  the seeing  
jagannāthe dekhe sākṣāt vrajendra-nandana  
in Jagannātha  He sees  directly  the son of the king of Vraja

(7) One day, when the Lord went to see Lord Jagannātha, He beheld in Him the son of the king of Vraja Himself.

একবারে স্ফুরে প্রভুর কৃষ্ণের পঞ্চগুণ ৷  
পঞ্চগুণে করে পঞ্চেন্দ্রিয় আকর্ষণ ॥ ৮ ॥

eka-bāre sphure prabhura kṛṣṇera pañca-guṇa  
at once  appear  to the Lord  of Kṛṣṇa  five  qualities  
pañca-guṇe kare pañcendriya ākarṣaṇa  
the five  qualities  make  of the five senses  attraction

(8) At once the five qualities of Kṛṣṇa appeared to the Lord, and they drew His five senses to themselves.[^3]

একমন পঞ্চদিকে পঞ্চগুণ টানে ৷  
টানাটানি প্রভুর মন হৈল অগেয়ানে ॥ ৯ ॥

ekamana pañca-dike pañca-guṇa ṭāne  
one mind  in five  directions  five  qualities  pull  
ṭānāṭāni prabhura mana haila ageyāne  
by the tugging  the Lord’s  mind  became  unconscious

(9) The five qualities pulled His one mind in five directions, and, torn this way and that, the Lord lost consciousness.

হেনকালে ঈশ্বরের উপলভোগ সরিল ৷  
ভক্তগণ মহাপ্রভুরে ঘরে লঞা আইল ॥ ১০ ॥

hena-kāle īśvarera upala-bhoga sarila  
at that time  of the Lord  the upala-bhoga  ended  
bhakta-gaṇa mahāprabhure ghare lañā āila  
the devotees  Mahāprabhu  home  taking  came

(10) At that time Lord Jagannātha’s *upala-bhoga* offering came to an end, and the devotees brought Mahāprabhu home.

স্বরূপ, রামানন্দ, — এই দুইজন লঞা ৷  
বিলাপ করেন দুঁহার কণ্ঠেতে ধরিয়া ॥ ১১ ॥

svarūpa, rāmānanda, — ei dui-jana lañā  
Svarūpa  Rāmānanda  these  two  persons  with  
vilāpa karena dum̐hāra kaṇṭhete dhariyā  
lament  He makes  of both  the necks  holding

(11) With these two, Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, He lamented, His arms around their necks.

কৃষ্ণের বিয়োগে রাধার উৎকণ্ঠিত মন ৷  
বিশাখারে কহে আপন উৎকণ্ঠা-কারণ ॥ ১২ ॥

kṛṣṇera viyoge rādhāra utkaṇṭhita mana  
of Kṛṣṇa  in separation  Rādhā’s  anxious  mind  
viśākhāre kahe āpana utkaṇṭhā-kāraṇa  
to Viśākhā  She tells  Her own  of the longing  the cause

(12) In separation from Kṛṣṇa, Rādhā’s mind was restless with longing, and She told Viśākhā the cause of it.

সেই শ্লোক পড়ি’ আপনে করে মনস্তাপ ৷  
শ্লোকের অর্থ শুনায় দুঁহারে করিয়া বিলাপ ॥ ১৩ ॥

sei śloka paḍi’ āpane kare manastāpa  
that  verse  reciting  Himself  makes  heartache  
ślokera artha śunāya dum̐hāre kariyā vilāpa  
of the verse  the meaning  makes hear  to the two  making  lament

(13) Reciting that verse Himself, the Lord poured out the anguish of His heart, and lamenting, He explained its meaning to the two of them.

সৌন্দর্য্যামৃতসিন্ধুভঙ্গললনা-চিত্তাদ্রিসংপ্লাবকঃ  
কর্ণানন্দিসনর্ম্মরম্যবচনঃ কোটীন্দুশীতাঙ্গকঃ ৷  
সৌরভ্যামৃতসংপ্লবাবৃতজগৎ পীযূষরম্যাধরঃ  
শ্রীগোপেন্দ্রসুতঃ স কর্ষতি বলাৎ  
পঞ্চেন্দ্রিয়াণ্যালি মে ॥ ১৪ ॥

saundaryāmṛta-sindhu-bhaṅga-  
of beauty  of the nectar  of the ocean  by the waves  
lalanā-cittādri-saṁplāvakaḥ  
of women  of the hearts  the hills  who floods  
karṇānandi-sanarma-ramya-  
delighting the ears  jesting  charming  
vacanaḥ koṭīndu-śītāṅgakaḥ  
whose words  than millions of moons  cooler  whose body  
saurabhyāmṛta-saṁplavāvṛta-  
of fragrance  of nectar  by the flood  covered  
jagat pīyūṣa-ramyādharaḥ  
the world  than nectar  lovelier  whose lips  
śrī-gopendra-sutaḥ sa karṣati  
Śrī  of the king of the cowherds  the son  He  draws  
balāt pañcendriyāṇy āli me  
by force  five senses  O friend  My

(14) “O friend, with the waves of the nectar ocean of His beauty He floods the hills of women’s hearts; His playful, charming words delight the ear; His body is cooler than millions of moons; with a nectar flood of fragrance He has covered the world; His lips are lovelier than nectar — that son of the king of the cowherds draws all five of My senses by force.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 8.3)[^4]

কৃষ্ণ-রূপ-শব্দ-স্পর্শ, সৌরভ্য-অধর-রস,  
যার মাধুর্য্য কহন না যায় ৷  
দেখি’ লোভে পঞ্চজন, এক অশ্ব — মোর মন,  
চড়ি’ পঞ্চ পাঁচদিকে ধায় ॥ ১৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-rūpa-śabda-sparśa, saurabhya-adhara-rasa,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  form  sound  touch  fragrance  of the lips  taste  
yāra mādhurya kahana nā yāya  
whose  sweetness  to describe  not  is possible  
dekhi’ lobhe pañcajana,  
seeing  in greed  the five  
eka aśva — mora mana,  
one  horse  My  mind  
caḍi’ pañca pām̐ca-dike dhāya  
mounting  the five  in five directions  run

(15) “Kṛṣṇa’s form, His voice, His touch, His fragrance and the taste of His lips — their sweetness cannot be described. Seeing them, the five, in their greed, mount a single horse — My mind — and ride off in five directions.

সখি হে, শুন মোর দুঃখের কারণ ৷  
মোর পঞ্চেন্দ্রিয়গণ, মহা-লম্পট দস্যুগণ,  
সবে কহে, — হর’ পরধন ॥ ১৬ ॥

sakhi he, śuna mora duḥkhera kāraṇa  
O friend  ah  hear  of My  sorrow  the cause  
mora pañcendriya-gaṇa,  
My  five senses  
mahā-lampaṭa dasyu-gaṇa,  
great  greedy ones  robbers  
sabe kahe, — hara’ para-dhana  
all  say  steal  another’s wealth

(16) O friend, hear the cause of My sorrow. My five senses are utterly greedy robbers: they all say, ‘Steal another’s wealth!’[^5]

এক অশ্ব একক্ষণে, পাঁচ পাঁচ দিকে টানে,  
এক মন কোন্ দিকে ধায় ?  
এককালে সবে টানে, গেল ঘোড়ার পরাণে,  
এই দুঃখ সহন না যায় ॥ ১৭ ॥

eka aśva eka-kṣaṇe,  
one  horse  at one moment  
pām̐ca pām̐ca dike ṭāne,  
the five  in five  directions  pull  
eka mana kon dike dhāya?  
one  mind  which  way  shall run  
eka-kāle sabe ṭāne,  
at one time  all  pull  
gela ghoḍāra parāṇe,  
goes  of the horse  the life  
ei duḥkha sahana nā yāya  
this  pain  to bear  not  is possible

(17) One horse, and at one moment the five pull it in five directions — which way is the one mind to run? When all pull at once, the horse’s life departs. I cannot bear this pain.

ইন্দ্রিয়ে না করি রোষ, ইঁহা-সবার কাঁহা দোষ,  
কৃষ্ণরূপাদির মহা আকর্ষণ ৷  
রূপাদি পাঁচ পাঁচে টানে, গেল ঘোড়ার পরাণে,  
মোর দেহে না রহে জীবন ॥ ১৮ ॥

indriye nā kari roṣa,  
the senses  not  I make  anger  
im̐hā-sabāra kām̐hā doṣa,  
of these all  where  the fault  
kṛṣṇa-rūpādira mahā ākarṣaṇa  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  form and the rest  great  attraction  
rūpādi pām̐ca pām̐ce ṭāne,  
form and the rest  the five  the five  pull  
gela ghoḍāra parāṇe,  
goes  of the horse  the life  
mora dehe nā rahe jīvana  
in My  body  not  remains  life

(18) I am not angry with My senses — what fault is theirs? Kṛṣṇa’s form and the rest attract with great force. The five — His form and the rest — pull the five senses; the horse’s life is ebbing, and life will not stay in My body.

কৃষ্ণরূপামৃতসিন্ধু, তাহার তরঙ্গ-বিন্দু,  
একবিন্দু জগৎ ডুবায় ৷  
ত্রিজগতে যত নারী, তার চিত্ত-উচ্চগিরি,  
তাহা ডুবাই আগে উঠি’ ধায় ॥ ১৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa-rūpāmṛta-sindhu,  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  beauty  of nectar  the ocean  
tāhāra taraṅga-bindu,  
of it  of a wave  a drop  
eka-bindu jagat ḍubāya  
one  drop  the world  drowns  
trijagate yata nārī,  
in the three worlds  as many as  women  
tāra citta-ucca-giri,  
their  hearts  high  mountains  
tāhā ḍubāi āge uṭhi’ dhāya  
those  drowning  further  rising  rushes

(19) The beauty of Kṛṣṇa is an ocean of nectar; a single drop of its waves drowns the world. The hearts of all the women in the three worlds are high mountains: it drowns them and surges on beyond.

কৃষ্ণের বচন মাধুরী, নানা-রস-নর্ম্মধারী,  
তার অন্যায় কথন না যায় ৷  
জগতের নারীর কাণে, মাধুরী গুণে বান্ধি’ টানে,  
টানাটানি কাণের প্রাণ যায় ॥ ২০ ॥

kṛṣṇera vacana mādhurī,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  of the words  the sweetness  
nānā-rasa-narma-dhārī,  
of various  rasas  jests  bearing  
tāra anyāya kathana nā yāya  
its  injustice  to describe  not  is possible  
jagatera nārīra kāṇe,  
of the world’s  women  into the ears  
mādhurī guṇe bāndhi’ ṭāne,  
sweetness  with the rope (quality)  binding  pulls  
ṭānāṭāni kāṇera prāṇa yāya  
by the tugging  of the ears  the life  departs

(20) The sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s words, full of jests in many moods, is unjust beyond telling. Entering the ears of the world’s women, it binds them with the rope of its sweetness and pulls, and in that tug-of-war the ears lose their very life.[^6]

কৃষ্ণ-অঙ্গ সুশীতল, কি কহিমু তার বল,  
ছটায় জিনে কোটীন্দু চন্দন ৷  
সশৈল নারীর বক্ষ, তাহা আকর্ষিতে দক্ষ,  
আকর্ষয়ে নারীগণ-মন ॥ ২১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-aṅga suśītala,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  body  very cool  
ki kahimu tāra bala,  
what  shall I say  its  power  
chaṭāya jine koṭīndu candana  
by its lustre  conquers  millions of moons  sandal  
saśaila nārīra vakṣa,  
mountain-like  of women  the breasts  
tāhā ākarṣite dakṣa,  
those  in drawing  skilled  
ākarṣaye nārī-gaṇa-mana  
draws  of the women  the minds

(21) Kṛṣṇa’s body is so cool — what can I say of its power? With its cool rays it surpasses millions of moons and sandal paste. Skilled at drawing the mountain-like breasts of women, it draws their minds as well.

কৃষ্ণাঙ্গ — সৌরভ্যভর, মৃগমদ মনোহর,  
নীলোৎপলের হরে গর্ব্ব-ধন ৷  
জগৎ-নারীর নাসা, তার ভিতর পাতে বাসা,  
নারীগণে করে আকর্ষণ ॥ ২২ ॥

kṛṣṇāṅga — saurabhya-bhara,  
Kṛṣṇa’s body  of fragrance  full  
mṛga-mada manohara,  
musk  charming  
nīlotpalera hare garva-dhana  
of the blue lotus  steals  of pride  the wealth  
jagat-nārīra nāsā,  
of the world’s  women  the nostrils  
tāra bhitara pāte vāsā,  
of them  inside  makes  a dwelling  
nārī-gaṇe kare ākarṣaṇa  
the women  makes  attraction

(22) Kṛṣṇa’s body is laden with fragrance; it humbles charming musk and robs the blue lotus of its wealth of pride. It makes its home in the nostrils of the world’s women and draws them to itself.

কৃষ্ণের অধরামৃত, তাতে কর্পূর-মন্দস্মিত,  
স্ব-মাধুর্য্যে হরে নারীর মন ৷  
অন্যত্র ছাড়ায় লোভ, না পাইলে মনে ক্ষোভ,  
ব্রজনারীগণের মূলধন ॥ ২৩ ॥

kṛṣṇera adharāmṛta,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  the nectar of the lips  
tāte karpūra-manda-smita,  
with it  camphor  gentle  smile  
sva-mādhurye hare nārīra mana  
by its own  sweetness  steals  of women  the mind  
anyatra chāḍāya lobha,  
elsewhere  takes away  the longing  
nā pāile mane kṣobha,  
not  having obtained  in the mind  agitation  
vraja-nārī-gaṇera mūla-dhana  
of Vraja’s  women  the principal wealth

(23) The nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips, with the camphor of His gentle smile, steals women’s minds by its own sweetness. It takes away all longing for anything else; not obtaining it, the mind is in turmoil. It is the chief wealth of the women of Vraja.”

এত কহি’ গৌরহরি, দুইজনার কণ্ঠ ধরি’,  
কহে, — শুন, স্বরূপ-রামরায় ৷  
কাঁহা কঁরো, কাঁহা যাঙ, কাঁহা গেলে কৃষ্ণ পাঙ,  
দুঁহে মোরে কহ সে উপায় ॥ ২৪ ॥

eta kahi’ gaurahari,  
so  saying  Gaurahari  
dui-janāra kaṇṭha dhari’,  
of the two  the necks  holding  
kahe, — śuna, svarūpa-rāmarāya  
says  hear  Svarūpa  Rāmarāya  
kām̐hā kam̐ro, kām̐hā yāṅa,  
what  shall I do  where  shall I go  
kām̐hā gele kṛṣṇa pāṅa,  
where  going  Kṛṣṇa  shall I find  
dum̐he more kaha se upāya  
you two  to Me  tell  that  means

(24) Saying this, Gaurahari threw His arms around the necks of the two and said, “Listen, Svarūpa and Rāmarāya: what shall I do, where shall I go? Where can I go to find Kṛṣṇa? Both of you, tell Me how!”

এইমত গৌরপ্রভু প্রতিদিনে-দিনে ৷  
বিলাপ করেন স্বরূপ-রামানন্দ-সনে ॥ ২৫ ॥

ei-mata gaura-prabhu prati dine-dine  
in this way  Gaura  the Lord  every  day after day  
vilāpa karena svarūpa-rāmānanda-sane  
lament  makes  with Svarūpa  and Rāmānanda

(25) In this way, day after day, the Lord Gaura lamented with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda.

সেই দুইজন প্রভুরে করে আশ্বাসন ৷  
স্বরূপ গায়, রায় করে শ্লোক পঠন ॥ ২৬ ॥

sei dui-jana prabhure kare āśvāsana  
those  two  persons  the Lord  make  consolation  
svarūpa gāya, rāya kare śloka paṭhana  
Svarūpa  sings  Rāya  makes  of verses  recitation

(26) Those two consoled the Lord: Svarūpa sang songs, and Rāya recited verses.

কর্ণামৃত, বিদ্যাপতি, শ্রীগীতগোবিন্দ ৷  
ইহার শ্লোক-গীতে প্রভুর করায় আনন্দ ॥ ২৭ ॥

karṇāmṛta, vidyāpati, śrī-gīta-govinda  
Karṇāmṛta  Vidyāpati  Śrī Gīta-govinda  
ihāra śloka-gīte prabhura karāya ānanda  
of these  in verses and songs  to the Lord  give  joy

(27) With the verses and songs of the *Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta*, of Vidyāpati and of the *Śrī Gīta-govinda* they gave the Lord joy.

একদিন মহাপ্রভু সমুদ্র যাইতে ৷  
পুষ্পের উদ্যান তথা দেখেন আচম্বিতে ॥ ২৮ ॥

ekadina mahāprabhu samudra yāite  
one day  Mahāprabhu  to the sea  going  
puṣpera udyāna tathā dekhena ācambite  
of flowers  a garden  there  sees  suddenly

(28) One day, on His way to the sea, Mahāprabhu suddenly saw a flower garden.

বৃন্দাবন-ভ্রমে তাঁহা পশিলা ধাঞা ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে বুলে তাঁহা কৃষ্ণ অন্বেষিয়া ॥ ২৯ ॥

vṛndāvana-bhrame tām̐hā paśilā dhāñā  
Vṛndāvana  mistaking it for  there  entered  running  
premāveśe bule tām̐hā kṛṣṇa anveṣiyā  
absorbed in prema  wanders  there  Kṛṣṇa  searching for

(29) Taking it for Vṛndāvana, He ran into it, and absorbed in prema He wandered there searching for Kṛṣṇa,

রাসে রাধা লঞা কৃষ্ণ অন্তর্দ্ধান কৈলা ৷  
পাছে সখীগণ যৈছে চাহি’ বেড়াইলা ॥ ৩০ ॥

rāse rādhā lañā kṛṣṇa antarddhāna kailā  
in the rāsa  Rādhā  taking  Kṛṣṇa  disappearance  made  
pāche sakhī-gaṇa yaiche cāhi’ beḍāilā  
afterwards  the gopīs  as  searching  wandered

(30) just as, when Kṛṣṇa disappeared from the rāsa dance taking Rādhā with Him, the gopīs afterwards wandered about searching for Him.

সেই ভাবাবেশে প্রভু প্রতি-তরুলতা ৷  
শ্লোক পড়ি’ পড়ি’ চাহি’ বুলে যথা তথা ॥ ৩১ ॥

sei bhāvāveśe prabhu prati-taru-latā  
in that  mood absorbed  the Lord  each  tree  creeper  
śloka paḍi’ paḍi’ cāhi’ bule yathā tathā  
verses  reciting  reciting  searching  wanders  here  there

(31) Absorbed in that mood, the Lord wandered here and there searching, reciting verse after verse to each tree and creeper:

চূতপ্রিয়ালপনসাসনকোবিদার-  
জম্ব্বর্কবিল্ববকুলাম্রকদম্বনীপাঃ ৷  
যেঽন্যে পরার্থভবকা যমুনোপকূলাঃ  
শংসন্তু কৃষ্ণপদবীং রহিতাত্মনাং নঃ ॥ ৩২ ॥

cūta-priyāla-panasāsana-kovidāra-  
cūta  priyāla  panasa  āsana  kovidāra  
jambv-arka-bilva-bakulāmra-kadamba-nīpāḥ  
jambu  arka  bilva  bakula  mango  kadamba  nīpa  
ye ’nye parārtha-bhavakā yamunopakūlāḥ  
who  other  for others’ good  born  on the Yamunā’s banks  
śaṁsantu kṛṣṇa-padavīṁ rahitātmanāṁ naḥ  
tell  of Kṛṣṇa  the path  who have lost our selves  to us

(32) “O *cūta*, *priyāla*, *panasa*, *āsana* and *kovidāra*, *jambu*, *arka*, *bilva*, *bakula*, mango, kadamba and *nīpa*, and all you other trees on the banks of the Yamunā, whose life is for the good of others — tell us, who have lost our very selves, which way Kṛṣṇa went!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.9)

কচ্চিত্তুলসি কল্যাণি গোবিন্দচরণপ্রিয়ে ৷  
সহ ত্বালিকুলৈর্বিভ্রদ্দৃষ্টস্তেঽতিপ্রিয়োঽচ্যুতঃ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

kaccit tulasi kalyāṇi  
perhaps  O tulasī  O auspicious one  
govinda-caraṇa-priye  
to Govinda’s  feet  dear  
saha tvāli-kulair bibhrad  
with  you  by swarms of bees  bearing  
dṛṣṭas te ’ti-priyo ’cyutaḥ  
seen  your  very dear  Acyuta

(33) “O auspicious tulasī, dear to Govinda’s feet! Have you seen your most beloved Acyuta pass by, wearing you, with swarms of bees around Him?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.7)

মালত্যদর্শি বঃ কচ্চিন্মল্লিকে জাতি যূথিকে ৷  
প্রীতিং বো জনয়ন্ যাতঃ করস্পর্শেন মাধবঃ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

mālaty adarśi vaḥ kaccin  
O mālatī  was seen  by you  perhaps  
mallike jāti yūthike  
O mallikā  O jāti  O yūthikā  
prītiṁ vo janayan yātaḥ  
joy  your  producing  gone  
kara-sparśena mādhavaḥ  
by the touch of His hand  Mādhava

(34) “O *mālatī*, O *mallikā*, O *jāti*, O *yūthikā*! Have you seen Mādhava pass by, giving you joy with the touch of His hand?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.8)

আম্র, পনস, পিয়াল, জম্বু, কোবিদার ৷  
তীর্থবাসী সবে, কর পর-উপকার ॥ ৩৫ ॥

āmra, panasa, piyāla, jambu, kovidāra  
O mango  panasa  piyāla  jambu  kovidāra  
tīrtha-vāsī sabe, kara para-upakāra  
of a holy place  dwellers  all  do  to others  good

(35) “O mango, *panasa*, *piyāla*, *jambu* and *kovidāra*! You all dwell in a holy place — so do good to others.[^7]

---

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: the ocean of feelings for Kṛṣṇa is unfathomable even for sages headed by Brahmā, for their knowledge rests on the senses. Heading in the edition: the Lord is immersed in the ocean of the mahābhāva of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

[^2]: *Anubhāṣya*: a potter’s wheel goes on turning by the force of the first push, and the potter need not keep his hand on it; so the Lord’s bodily actions went on naturally even when He had no awareness of the outer world. The comparison fits the daily life of the highest mahā-bhāgavata as long as he remains in this world.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: His form draws the eyes, His song the ears, His fragrance the nose, His taste the tongue and His touch the skin; all five transcendental qualities of Kṛṣṇa appeared at once to the Lord’s five transcendental senses.

[^4]: Heading in the edition: Śrī Rādhā’s words to Viśākhā.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: drawn by Kṛṣṇa, the senses conspire like five robbers: “Come, let us steal this wealth that belongs to another — the horse of the mind.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the senses know that Kṛṣṇa is another’s man, and still each seeks to steal its own object in Him.

[^6]: *Guṇa* means both “quality” and “rope”: sweetness binds the ears as with a rope. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇa’s playful speech treats women so unjustly that words cannot tell it.

[^7]: Heading in the edition: the meaning of the verse; the gopīs, wholly absorbed in Kṛṣṇa and separated from Him, search for Him and lament.
