“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 5  
**Determining the Truth of Śrī Nityānanda**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দেঽনন্তাদ্ভুতৈশ্বর্য্যং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দমীশ্বরম্ ৷  
যস্যেচ্ছয়া তৎস্বরূপমজ্ঞেনাপি নিরূপ্যতে ॥ ১ ॥

vande ’nantādbhutaiśvaryaṁ  
I bow  of endless  wondrous  opulence (possessed)  
śrī-nityānandam īśvaram  
Śrī Nityānanda  the Lord  
yasyecchayā tat-svarūpam  
by whose  will  His  true nature  
ajñenāpi nirūpyate  
by an ignorant one  even  is described

(1) I bow to the Lord Śrī Nityānanda, whose divine opulence is endless and wondrous. By His will even a fool can come to know and describe His true nature.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এই ষট্শ্লোকে কহিল কৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-মহিমা ৷  
পঞ্চশ্লোকে কহি নিত্যানন্দতত্ত্ব-সীমা ॥ ৩ ॥

ei ṣaṭ-śloke kahila kṛṣṇa-caitanya-mahimā  
in these  six  verses  I have told  of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the glory  
pañca-śloke kahi nityānanda-tattva-sīmā  
in five  verses  I tell  of the truth of Nityānanda  the full extent

(3) In these six verses I have told of the glory of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya; in five verses I now tell the whole truth of Nityānanda.

সর্ব্ব-অবতারী কৃষ্ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তাঁহার দ্বিতীয় দেহ শ্রীবলরাম ॥ ৪ ॥

sarva-avatārī kṛṣṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
of all  avatāras the source  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān  
tām̐hāra dvitīya deha śrī-balarāma  
His  second  body  Śrī Balarāma

(4) Kṛṣṇa, the source of all avatāras, is Bhagavān Himself, and Śrī Balarāma is His second body.

একই স্বরূপ দোঁহে, ভিন্নমাত্র কায় ৷  
আদ্য কায়ব্যূহ, কৃষ্ণলীলার সহায় ॥ ৫ ॥

eka-i svarūpa dom̐he, bhinna-mātra kāya  
one and the same  nature  of the two  different only  the bodies  
ādya kāya-vyūha, kṛṣṇa-līlāra sahāya  
the first  bodily expansion  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  the helper

(5) The two are one in nature; only Their bodies differ. Balarāma is the first expansion of Kṛṣṇa’s body and His helper in His pastimes.

সেই কৃষ্ণ — নবদ্বীপে শ্রীচৈতন্যচন্দ্র ৷  
সেই বলরাম — সঙ্গে শ্রীনিত্যানন্দ ॥ ৬ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa — navadvīpe śrī-caitanya-candra  
that  Kṛṣṇa  in Navadvīpa  Śrī Caitanyacandra  
sei balarāma — saṅge śrī-nityānanda  
that  Balarāma  with Him  Śrī Nityānanda

(6) That Kṛṣṇa is Śrī Caitanyacandra in Navadvīpa, and that Balarāma is Śrī Nityānanda, who is with Him.

সঙ্কর্ষণঃ কারণতোয়শায়ী  
গর্ভোদশায়ী চ পয়োঽব্ধিশায়ী ৷  
শেষশ্চ যস্যাংশকলাঃ স নিত্যা-  
নন্দাখ্যরামঃ শরণং মমাস্তু ॥ ৭ ॥

saṅkarṣaṇaḥ kāraṇa-toya-śāyī  
Saṅkarṣaṇa  Kāraṇodakaśāyī  
garbhoda-śāyī ca payo ’bdhi-śāyī  
Garbhodakaśāyī  and  Kṣīrodakaśāyī  
śeṣaś ca yasyāṁśa-kalāḥ sa nityā-  
and Śeṣa  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-  
nandākhya-rāmaḥ śaraṇaṁ mamāstu  
-nanda named  Rāma  refuge  my  may be

(7) “Saṅkarṣaṇa, the Lord who lies in the Causal Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Garbha Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Ocean of Milk, and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions: may that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my refuge.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^2]

শ্রীবলরাম গোসাঞি মূল-সঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
পঞ্চরূপ ধরি’ করেন কৃষ্ণের সেবন ॥ ৮ ॥

śrī-balarāma gosāñi mūla-saṅkarṣaṇa  
Śrī Balarāma  Gosāñi  the original  Saṅkarṣaṇa  
pañca-rūpa dhari’ karena kṛṣṇera sevana  
five  forms  assuming  He performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  service

(8) Śrī Balarāma Gosāñi is the original Saṅkarṣaṇa. Assuming five forms, He serves Kṛṣṇa.

আপনে করেন কৃষ্ণলীলার সহায় ৷  
সৃষ্টিলীলা-কার্য্য করে ধরি’ চারি কায় ॥ ৯ ॥

āpane karena kṛṣṇa-līlāra sahāya  
Himself  He performs  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  assistance  
sṛṣṭi-līlā-kārya kare dhari’ cāri kāya  
of the pastime of creation  the work  He does  assuming  four  bodies

(9) He Himself assists Kṛṣṇa in His pastimes, and in four other bodies He carries out the work of creation.

সৃষ্ট্যাদিক সেবা, তাঁর আজ্ঞার পালন ৷  
‘শেষ’-রূপে করে কৃষ্ণের বিবিধ সেবন ॥ ১০ ॥

sṛṣṭy-ādika sevā, tām̐ra ājñāra pālana  
creation  and the rest  service  His  order’s  carrying out  
‘śeṣa’-rūpe kare kṛṣṇera vividha sevana  
Śeṣa  in the form of  He performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  manifold  service

(10) His work of creation and the rest is service done at Kṛṣṇa’s command; and as Śeṣa He serves Kṛṣṇa in many ways.

সর্ব্বরূপে আস্বাদয়ে কৃষ্ণ-সেবানন্দ ৷  
সেই বলরাম — গৌরসঙ্গে নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ১১ ॥

sarva-rūpe āsvādaye kṛṣṇa-sevānanda  
in all  forms  He tastes  of Kṛṣṇa’s service  the bliss  
sei balarāma — gaura-saṅge nityānanda  
that  Balarāma  Gaura’s companion  Nityānanda

(11) In all these forms He tastes the bliss of serving Kṛṣṇa. And that same Balarāma is Nityānanda, the companion of Gaura.

সপ্তম শ্লোকের অর্থ করি চারিশ্লোক ৷  
যাতে নিত্যানন্দতত্ত্ব জানে সর্ব্বলোক ॥ ১২ ॥

saptama ślokera artha kari cāriśloka  
of the seventh  verse  the meaning  I make  in four verses  
yāte nityānanda-tattva jāne sarvaloka  
by which  of Nityānanda  the truth  know  all people

(12) I shall explain the meaning of the seventh verse in four verses, so that all may know the truth of Nityānanda.[^3]

মায়াতীতে ব্যাপিবৈকুণ্ঠলোকে  
পূর্ণৈশ্বর্য্যে শ্রীচতুর্ব্যূহমধ্যে ৷  
রূপং যস্যোদ্ভাতি সঙ্কর্ষণাখ্যং  
তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১৩ ॥

māyātīte vyāpi-vaikuṇṭha-loke  
beyond māyā  in all-pervading  Vaikuṇṭha  world  
pūrṇaiśvarye śrī-catur-vyūha-madhye  
full of opulence  amid the fourfold expansion  
rūpaṁ yasyodbhāti saṅkarṣaṇākhyaṁ  
form  whose  shines forth  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(13) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma, whose form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines amid the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha) in the all-pervading world of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence and beyond māyā.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^4]

প্রকৃতির পার ‘পরব্যোম’-নামে ধাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণবিগ্রহ যৈছে বিভূত্যাদি-গুণবান্ ॥ ১৪ ॥

prakṛtira pāra ‘paravyoma’-nāme dhāma  
material nature’s  beyond  Paravyoma  named  an abode  
kṛṣṇa-vigraha yaiche vibhūty-ādi-guṇavān  
Kṛṣṇa’s  form  just as  with opulence  and other  qualities endowed

(14) Beyond material nature lies the abode called Paravyoma. Like Kṛṣṇa’s own form, it is endowed with every opulence and every divine quality.

সর্ব্বগ, অনন্ত ব্রহ্ম-বৈকুণ্ঠাদি ধাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণ-অবতারের তাহাঞি বিশ্রাম ॥ ১৫ ॥

sarvaga, ananta brahma-vaikuṇṭhādi dhāma  
all-pervading  endless  Brahman  Vaikuṇṭha  and others  abodes  
kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-avatārera tāhāñi viśrāma  
of Kṛṣṇa  of Kṛṣṇa’s avatāras  there  the dwelling

(15) There lie endless, all-pervading abodes — Brahman, Vaikuṇṭha and others — and in them Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras dwell.[^5]

তাহার উপরিভাগে ‘কৃষ্ণলোক’ খ্যাতি ৷  
দ্বারকা-মথুরা-গোকুল — ত্রিবিধত্বে স্থিতি ॥ ১৬ ॥

tāhāra upari-bhāge ‘kṛṣṇa-loka’ khyāti  
its  in the upper  part  Kṛṣṇaloka  renowned  
dvārakā-mathurā-gokula — tri-vidhatve sthiti  
Dvārakā  Mathurā  Gokula  in three divisions  it abides

(16) In its highest region is the realm known as Kṛṣṇaloka, which abides in three divisions — Dvārakā, Mathurā and Gokula.

সর্ব্বোপরি শ্রীগোকুল — ব্রজলোকধাম ৷  
শ্রীগোলোক, শ্বেতদ্বীপ, বৃন্দাবন-নাম ॥ ১৭ ॥

sarvopari śrī-gokula — vrajaloka-dhāma  
above all  Śrī Gokula  the abode of the world of Vraja  
śrī-goloka, śvetadvīpa, vṛndāvana-nāma  
Śrī Goloka  Śvetadvīpa  Vṛndāvana  named

(17) Above all is Śrī Gokula, the abode of Vraja, which is also called Śrī Goloka, Śvetadvīpa and Vṛndāvana.

সর্ব্বগ, অনন্ত, বিভু, কৃষ্ণতনুসম ৷  
উপর্য্যধো ব্যাপিয়াছে, নাহিক নিয়ম ॥ ১৮ ॥

sarvaga, ananta, vibhu, kṛṣṇa-tanu-sama  
all-pervading  endless  vast  like Kṛṣṇa’s body  
upary-adho vyāpiyāche, nāhika niyama  
above and below  it pervades  there is no  limit

(18) Like Kṛṣṇa’s body, it is all-pervading, endless and vast; it extends above and below without any bound.[^6]

ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে প্রকাশ তাঁর কৃষ্ণের ইচ্ছায় ৷  
একই স্বরূপ তাঁর, নাহি দুই কায় ॥ ১৯ ॥

brahmāṇḍe prakāśa tām̐ra kṛṣṇera icchāya  
in the universe  manifestation  its  by Kṛṣṇa’s  will  
eka-i svarūpa tām̐ra, nāhi dui kāya  
one and the same  nature  its  there are not  two  bodies

(19) By Kṛṣṇa’s will it is manifest within the universe, yet it remains one and the same: it does not have two bodies.

চিন্তামণি-ভূমি, কল্পবৃক্ষময় বন ৷  
চর্ম্মচক্ষে দেখে তাঁরে প্রপঞ্চের সম ॥ ২০ ॥

cintāmaṇi-bhūmi, kalpa-vṛkṣa-maya vana  
of cintāmaṇi  the land  of wish-fulfilling trees full  forests  
carma-cakṣe dekhe tām̐re prapañcera sama  
with eyes of flesh  one sees  it  the material world  like

(20) Its land is cintāmaṇi, its forests are full of wish-fulfilling trees; yet eyes of flesh see it as no different from the material world.[^7]

প্রেমনেত্রে দেখে তাঁর স্বরূপ-প্রকাশ ৷  
গোপ-গোপীসঙ্গে যাঁহা কৃষ্ণের বিলাস ॥ ২১ ॥

prema-netre dekhe tām̐ra svarūpa-prakāśa  
with eyes of love  one sees  its  true form’s  manifestation  
gopa-gopī-saṅge yām̐hā kṛṣṇera vilāsa  
with the cowherd boys  and girls  together  where  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes

(21) With eyes of love one sees it as it truly is — the place where Kṛṣṇa sports with the cowherd boys and girls.

চিন্তামণিপ্রকরসদ্মসু কল্পবৃক্ষ-  
লক্ষাবৃতেষু সুরভীরভিপালয়ন্তম্ ৷  
লক্ষ্মীসহস্রশতসম্ভ্রমসেব্যমানং  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ২২ ॥

cintāmaṇi-prakara-sadmasu kalpa-vṛkṣa-  
of cintāmaṇi gems  of clusters  in abodes  wish-fulfilling trees  
lakṣāvṛteṣu surabhīr abhipālayantam  
by millions  surrounded  the surabhi cows  tending  
lakṣmī-sahasra-śata-sambhrama-sevyamānaṁ  
by Lakṣmīs  thousands  hundreds  with reverence  being served  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(22) “I worship Govinda, the original Person, who tends the surabhi cows in abodes built of cintāmaṇi gems and surrounded by millions of wish-fulfilling trees, and who is served with reverence by hundreds of thousands of Lakṣmīs.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.29)[^8]

মথুরা-দ্বারকায় নিজরূপ প্রকাশিয়া ৷  
নানা-রূপে বিলসয়ে চতুর্ব্যূহ হৈঞা ॥ ২৩ ॥

mathurā-dvārakāya nija-rūpa prakāśiyā  
in Mathurā  Dvārakā  His own  form  manifesting  
nānā-rūpe vilasaye catur-vyūha haiñā  
in many  forms  He sports  the catur-vyūha  becoming

(23) In Mathurā and Dvārakā He manifests His own form and, becoming the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha), sports in many forms.

বাসুদেব-সঙ্কর্ষণ-প্রদ্যুম্নানিরুদ্ধ ৷  
সর্ব্বচতুর্ব্যূহ-অংশী, তুরীয়, বিশুদ্ধ ॥ ২৪ ॥

vāsudeva-saṅkarṣaṇa-pradyumnāniruddha  
Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha  
sarva-catur-vyūha-aṁśī, turīya, viśuddha  
of all  catur-vyūhas  the source  transcendent  pure

(24) Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha are the source of every catur-vyūha; They are transcendent (*turīya*) and pure.

এই তিন লোকে কৃষ্ণ কেবল-লীলাময় ৷  
নিজগণ লঞা খেলে অনন্ত সময় ॥ ২৫ ॥

ei tina loke kṛṣṇa kevala-līlā-maya  
in these  three  abodes  Kṛṣṇa  wholly  of pastimes full  
nija-gaṇa lañā khele ananta samaya  
His own  associates  taking  He plays  endless  time

(25) In these three abodes Kṛṣṇa is wholly given to His pastimes; there He plays with His own associates without end.

পরব্যোম-মধ্যে করি’ স্বরূপপ্রকাশ ৷  
নারায়ণরূপে করেন বিবিধ বিলাস ॥ ২৬ ॥

para-vyoma-madhye kari’ svarūpa prakāśa  
of Paravyoma  in the midst  making  of His own form  manifestation  
nārāyaṇa-rūpe karena vividha vilāsa  
of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  He performs  various  pastimes

(26) In the midst of Paravyoma He manifests His own form and, as Nārāyaṇa, enjoys pastimes of many kinds.

স্বরূপবিগ্রহ কৃষ্ণের কেবল দ্বিভুজ ৷  
নারায়ণরূপে সেই তনু চতুর্ভুজ ॥ ২৭ ॥

svarūpa-vigraha kṛṣṇera kevala dvi-bhuja  
own  form  Kṛṣṇa’s  only  two-armed  
nārāyaṇa-rūpe sei tanu catur-bhuja  
of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  that  body  four-armed

(27) Kṛṣṇa’s own form has only two arms; in the form of Nārāyaṇa that same body has four.

শঙ্খ-চক্র-গদা-পদ্ম, মহৈশ্বর্য্যময় ৷  
শ্রী-ভূ-নীলা-শক্তি যাঁর চরণ সেবয় ॥ ২৮ ॥

śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-padma, mahaiśvarya-maya  
conch  disc  club  lotus  of great majesty full  
śrī-bhū-nīlā-śakti yām̐ra caraṇa sevaya  
Śrī  Bhū  Nīlā  potencies  whose  feet  serve

(28) He holds the conch, disc, club and lotus and is full of supreme majesty; His feet are served by His potencies Śrī, Bhū and Nīlā.[^9]

যদ্যপি কেবল তাঁর ক্রীড়ামাত্র ধর্ম্ম ৷  
তথাপি জীবেরে কৃপায় করে এক কর্ম্ম ॥ ২৯ ॥

yadyapi kevala tām̐ra krīḍā-mātra dharma  
although  only  His  play  merely  nature  
tathāpi jīvere kṛpāya kare eka karma  
still  to the jīvas  out of mercy  He does  one  work

(29) Though His nature is simply to play, out of mercy to the jīvas He does one work:

সালোক্য-সামীপ্য-সার্ষ্টি-সারূপ্য-প্রকার ৷  
চারি মুক্তি দিয়া করে জীবেরে নিস্তার ॥ ৩০ ॥

sālokya-sāmīpya-sārṣṭi-sārūpya-prakāra  
sālokya  sāmīpya  sārṣṭi  sārūpya  kinds  
cāri mukti diyā kare jīvere nistāra  
four  liberations  giving  He makes  of the jīvas  deliverance

(30) Granting the four kinds of liberation — sālokya, sāmīpya, sārṣṭi and sārūpya — He delivers the jīvas.

ব্রহ্মসাযুজ্য-মুক্তের তাঁহা নাহি গতি ৷  
বৈকুণ্ঠ-বাহিরে হয় তা-সবার স্থিতি ॥ ৩১ ॥

brahma-sāyujya-muktera tām̐hā nāhi gati  
in Brahman  by merging  of the liberated  there  there is no  access  
vaikuṇṭha-bāhire haya tā-sabāra sthiti  
Vaikuṇṭha  outside  is  of them all  the dwelling

(31) Those liberated by merging into Brahman have no access there; they all remain outside it.

বৈকুণ্ঠ-বাহিরে এক জ্যোতির্ম্ময় মণ্ডল ৷  
কৃষ্ণের অঙ্গের প্রভা, পরম উজ্জ্বল ॥ ৩২ ॥

vaikuṇṭha-bāhire eka jyotir-maya maṇḍala  
Vaikuṇṭha  outside  one  luminous  sphere  
kṛṣṇera aṅgera prabhā, parama ujjvala  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  body  the radiance  supremely  bright

(32) Outside Vaikuṇṭha lies a sphere of light — the radiance of Kṛṣṇa’s body, supremely bright.

‘সিদ্ধলোক’ নাম তার প্রকৃতির পার ৷  
চিৎস্বরূপ, তাঁহা নাহি চিচ্ছক্তি-বিকার ॥ ৩৩ ॥

‘siddha-loka’ nāma tāra prakṛtira pāra  
Siddhaloka  name  its  material nature  beyond  
cit-svarūpa, tām̐hā nāhi cic-chakti-vikāra  
spiritual  in nature  there  there are not  of the spiritual potency  transformations

(33) It is called Siddhaloka and lies beyond material nature. It is spiritual, yet the spiritual potency displays no variety there.

সূর্য্যমণ্ডল যেন বাহিরে নির্ব্বিশেষ ৷  
ভিতরে সূর্য্যের রথ-আদি সবিশেষ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

sūrya-maṇḍala yena bāhire nirviśeṣa  
the sun’s  disc  as  outside  without variety  
bhitare sūryera ratha-ādi saviśeṣa  
within  the sun’s  chariot  and the rest  full of variety

(34) It is like the disc of the sun: from outside it is a featureless glow, but within are the sun’s chariot and all its variety.

কামাদ্দ্বেষাৎ ভয়াৎ স্নেহাৎ যথা ভক্ত্যেশ্বরে মনঃ ৷  
আবেশ্য তদঘং হিত্বা বহবস্তদ্গতিং গতাঃ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kāmād dveṣāt bhayāt snehāt  
from desire  from enmity  from fear  from affection  
yathā bhaktyeśvare manaḥ  
as  by devotion  on the Lord  the mind  
āveśya tad aghaṁ hitvā  
fixing  that  sin  casting off  
bahavas tad gatiṁ gatāḥ  
many  His  destination  have attained

(35) “Many have fixed their minds on the Lord — some by devotion, others by loving desire (*kāma*), enmity, fear or affection — and, casting off the sin in those feelings, have attained Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.1.29)[^10]

যদরীণাং প্রিয়াণাঞ্চ প্রাপ্যমেকমিবোদিতম্ ৷  
তদ্ব্রহ্মকৃষ্ণয়োরৈক্যাৎ কিরণার্কোপমা-জুষোঃ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

yad arīṇāṁ priyāṇāṁ ca  
which  of the enemies  of the beloved  and  
prāpyam ekam ivoditam  
the attainment  one  as if  is spoken of  
tad brahma-kṛṣṇayor aikyāt  
that  of Brahman and Kṛṣṇa  from the oneness  
kiraṇārkopamā-juṣoḥ  
like the rays and the sun  of those two

(36) “The goal of the Lord’s enemies and that of His beloved ones are sometimes spoken of as one, because Brahman and Kṛṣṇa are one, as the rays are one with the sun.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.278)[^11]

তৈছে পরব্যোমে নানা চিচ্ছক্তিবিলাস ৷  
নির্ব্বিশেষ জ্যোতির্বিম্ব বাহিরে প্রকাশ ॥ ৩৭ ॥

taiche para-vyome nānā cic-chakti-vilāsa  
so  in Paravyoma  manifold  of the spiritual potency  pastimes  
nirviśeṣa jyotir-bimba bāhire prakāśa  
featureless  of light  a sphere  outside  is manifest

(37) So in Paravyoma there are manifold pastimes of the spiritual potency, while outside it a featureless sphere of light is manifest.

নির্ব্বিশেষ-ব্রহ্ম সেই কেবল জ্যোতির্ম্ময় ৷  
সায়ুজ্যের অধিকারী তাঁহা পায় লয় ॥ ৩৮ ॥

nirviśeṣa-brahma sei kevala jyotir-maya  
featureless  Brahman  that  only  luminous  
sāyujyera adhikārī tām̐hā pāya laya  
for sāyujya  the qualified  there  attain  absorption

(38) That featureless Brahman is light alone, and those who are fit for *sāyujya* are absorbed in it.

সিদ্ধলোকস্তু তমসঃ পারে যত্র বসন্তি হি ৷  
সিদ্ধা ব্রহ্মসুখে মগ্না দৈত্যাশ্চ হরিণা হতাঃ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

siddha-lokas tu tamasaḥ  
Siddhaloka  but  darkness  
pāre yatra vasanti hi  
beyond  where  dwell  indeed  
siddhā brahma-sukhe magnā  
the siddhas  in the bliss of Brahman  immersed  
daityāś ca hariṇā hatāḥ  
the demons  and  by Hari  slain

(39) “Beyond darkness lies Siddhaloka, where dwell the siddhas, immersed in the bliss of Brahman, and the demons slain by Hari.” (Brahmāṇḍa-purāṇa; Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.280)[^12]

সেই পরব্যোমে নারায়ণের চারি পাশে ৷  
দ্বারকায় চতুর্ব্যূহ দ্বিতীয় প্রকাশে ॥ ৪০ ॥

sei para-vyome nārāyaṇera cāri pāśe  
in that  Paravyoma  of Nārāyaṇa  on four  sides  
dvārakāya catur-vyūha dvitīya prakāśe  
of Dvārakā  the catur-vyūha  in a second  manifestation

(40) In that Paravyoma, on the four sides of Nārāyaṇa, the catur-vyūha of Dvārakā is present in a second manifestation.

বাসুদেব-সঙ্কর্ষণ-প্রদ্যুম্নানিরুদ্ধ ৷  
‘দ্বিতীয় চতুর্ব্যূহ’ এই — তুরীয়, বিশুদ্ধ ॥ ৪১ ॥

vāsudeva-saṅkarṣaṇa-pradyumnāniruddha  
Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha  
‘dvitīya catur-vyūha’ ei — turīya, viśuddha  
the second  catur-vyūha  this  transcendent  pure

(41) Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha — this is the second catur-vyūha, transcendent and pure.

তাঁহা যে রামের রূপ — মহাসঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
চিচ্ছক্তি-আশ্রয় তিঁহো, কারণের কারণ ॥ ৪২ ॥

tām̐hā ye rāmera rūpa — mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa  
there  which  of Rāma  form  Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  
cic-chakti-āśraya tim̐ho, kāraṇera kāraṇa  
of the spiritual potency  the shelter  He  of causes  the cause

(42) There the form of Rāma is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa. He is the shelter of the spiritual potency, the cause of all causes.

চিচ্ছক্তিবিলাস এক — ‘শুদ্ধসত্ত্ব’-নাম ৷  
শুদ্ধসত্ত্বময় যত বৈকুণ্ঠাদি-ধাম ॥ ৪৩ ॥

cic-chakti-vilāsa eka — ‘śuddha-sattva’-nāma  
of the spiritual potency  a manifestation  one  śuddha-sattva  named  
śuddha-sattva-maya yata vaikuṇṭhādi-dhāma  
of śuddha-sattva made  all  Vaikuṇṭha and other  abodes

(43) One manifestation of the spiritual potency is called *śuddha-sattva*, pure spiritual existence, and of it all the abodes of Vaikuṇṭha are made.

ষড়্বিধৈশ্বর্য্য তাঁহা সকল চিন্ময় ৷  
সঙ্কর্ষণের বিভূতি সব, জানিহ নিশ্চয় ॥ ৪৪ ॥

ṣaḍ-vidhaiśvarya tām̐hā sakala cinmaya  
of six kinds  opulence  there  all  spiritual  
saṅkarṣaṇera vibhūti saba, jāniha niścaya  
of Saṅkarṣaṇa  the splendour  all  know  for certain

(44) The six kinds of opulence there are all spiritual; know for certain that all of them are the splendour of Saṅkarṣaṇa.

‘জীব’ নাম তটস্থাখ্য এক শক্তি হয় ৷  
মহাসঙ্কর্ষণ — সব জীবের আশ্রয় ॥ ৪৫ ॥

‘jīva’ nāma taṭasthākhya eka śakti haya  
jīva  named  marginal called  one  potency  there is  
mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa — saba jīvera āśraya  
Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  of all  jīvas  the shelter

(45) There is a potency called *jīva*, known as the marginal (*taṭasthā*) potency; and Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of all jīvas.

যাঁহা হৈতে বিশ্বোৎপত্তি, যাঁহাতে প্রলয় ৷  
সেই পুরুষের সঙ্কর্ষণ সমাশ্রয় ॥ ৪৬ ॥

yām̐hā haite viśvotpatti, yām̐hāte pralaya  
from whom  of the universe  the origin  in whom  the dissolution  
sei puruṣera saṅkarṣaṇa samāśraya  
of that  Puruṣa  Saṅkarṣaṇa  the shelter

(46) Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of the Puruṣa from whom the universe arises and into whom it dissolves.

সর্ব্বাশ্রয়, সর্ব্বাদ্ভুত, ঐশ্বর্য্য অপার ৷  
‘অনন্ত’ কহিতে নারে মহিমা যাঁহার ॥ ৪৭ ॥

sarvāśraya, sarvādbhuta, aiśvarya apāra  
of all  the shelter  all-wondrous  opulence  boundless  
‘ananta’ kahite nāre mahimā yām̐hāra  
Ananta  to describe  cannot  the glory  whose

(47) He is the shelter of all, wondrous in every way, His opulence boundless; even Ananta cannot describe His glory.

তুরীয়, বিশুদ্ধসত্ত্ব, ‘সঙ্কর্ষণ’ নাম ৷  
তিঁহো যাঁর অংশ, সেই নিত্যানন্দ-রাম ॥ ৪৮ ॥

turīya, viśuddha-sattva, ‘saṅkarṣaṇa’ nāma  
transcendent  pure sattva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named  
tim̐ho yām̐ra aṁśa, sei nityānanda-rāma  
He  whose  part  that  Nityānanda-Rāma

(48) The transcendent Saṅkarṣaṇa, pure spiritual existence (*viśuddha-sattva*), is a part of Nityānanda-Rāma.[^13]

অষ্টম শ্লোকের কৈল সংক্ষেপ বিবরণ ৷  
নবম শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ৪৯ ॥

aṣṭama ślokera kaila saṅkṣepa vivaraṇa  
of the eighth  verse  I gave  a brief  explanation  
navama ślokera artha śuna diyā mana  
of the ninth  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  the mind

(49) Thus I have briefly explained the eighth verse. Now hear attentively the meaning of the ninth.

মায়াভর্ত্তাজাণ্ডসঙ্ঘাশ্রয়াঙ্গঃ  
শেতে সাক্ষাৎ কারণাম্ভোধিমধ্যে ৷  
যস্যৈকাংশঃ শ্রীপুমানাদিদেব-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৫০ ॥

māyā-bhartājāṇḍa-saṅghāśrayāṅgaḥ  
māyā’s  master  of universes  multitudes  shelter  whose body  
śete sākṣāt kāraṇāmbhodhi-madhye  
lies  in person  of the Causal  Ocean  in the midst  
yasyaikāṁśaḥ śrī-pumān ādi-devas  
whose  one part  the Puruṣa  the original  Lord  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(50) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of Him is the original Lord, the Puruṣa, the master of māyā, who lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean, His body the shelter of countless universes.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^14]

বৈকুণ্ঠ-বাহিরে যেই জ্যোতির্ময় ধাম ৷  
তাহার বাহিরে ‘কারণার্ণব’ নাম ॥ ৫১ ॥

vaikuṇṭha-bāhire yei jyotir-maya dhāma  
Vaikuṇṭha  outside  which  luminous  abode  
tāhāra bāhire ‘kāraṇārṇava’ nāma  
of it  outside  Kāraṇārṇava  named

(51) Outside Vaikuṇṭha is the luminous abode, and beyond it lies what is called the Kāraṇārṇava, the Causal Ocean.

বৈকুণ্ঠ বেড়িয়া এক আছে জলনিধি ৷  
অনন্ত, অপার — তার নাহিক অবধি ॥ ৫২ ॥

vaikuṇṭha beḍiyā eka āche jala-nidhi  
Vaikuṇṭha  encircling  one  there is  an ocean  
ananta, apāra — tāra nāhika avadhi  
endless  shoreless  its  there is no  limit

(52) Encircling Vaikuṇṭha is an ocean, endless and shoreless; it has no bound.

বৈকুণ্ঠের পৃথিব্যাদি সকল চিন্ময় ৷  
মায়িক ভূতের তথি জন্ম নাহি হয় ॥ ৫৩ ॥

vaikuṇṭhera pṛthivy-ādi sakala cinmaya  
of Vaikuṇṭha  earth  and the rest  all  spiritual  
māyika bhūtera tathi janma nāhi haya  
material  of the elements  there  birth  not  is

(53) In Vaikuṇṭha earth and all the elements are spiritual; material elements never arise there.

চিন্ময়-জল সেই পরম-কারণ ৷  
যার এক কণা গঙ্গা পতিতপাবন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

cinmaya-jala sei parama-kāraṇa  
spiritual  water  that  the supreme  cause  
yāra eka kaṇā gaṅgā patita-pāvana  
of which  one  drop  the Gaṅgā  of the fallen  the purifier

(54) That spiritual water is the supreme cause; a single drop of it is the Gaṅgā, purifier of the fallen.

সেই ত’ কারণার্ণবে সেই সঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
আপনার এক অংশে করেন শয়ন ॥ ৫৫ ॥

sei ta’ kāraṇārṇave sei saṅkarṣaṇa  
in that  very  Causal Ocean  that  Saṅkarṣaṇa  
āpanāra eka aṁśe karena śayana  
His own  in one  part  He makes  lying down

(55) In that Causal Ocean the same Saṅkarṣaṇa lies in the form of one of His parts.

মহৎস্রষ্টা পুরুষ, তিঁহো জগৎ-কারণ ৷  
আদ্য-অবতার করে মায়ার দরশন ॥ ৫৬ ॥

mahat-sraṣṭā puruṣa, tim̐ho jagat-kāraṇa  
of the mahat  the creator  Puruṣa  He  of the world  the cause  
ādya-avatāra kare māyāra daraśana  
the first  avatāra  makes  of māyā  the glance

(56) He is the Puruṣa, creator of the *mahat-tattva*, the cause of the world. This first avatāra casts His glance upon māyā.

মায়াশক্তি রহে কারণাব্ধির বাহিরে ৷  
কারণ-সমুদ্র মায়া পরশিতে নারে ॥ ৫৭ ॥

māyā-śakti rahe kāraṇābdhira bāhire  
māyā  potency  remains  the Causal Ocean  outside  
kāraṇa-samudra māyā paraśite nāre  
the Causal  Ocean  māyā  to touch  cannot

(57) The potency māyā stays outside the Causal Ocean; māyā cannot touch its waters.

সেই ত’ মায়ার দুই-বিধ অবস্থিতি ৷  
জগতের উপাদান ‘প্রধান’, ‘প্রকৃতি’ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

sei ta’ māyāra dui-vidha avasthiti  
of that  very  māyā  twofold  state  
jagatera upādāna ‘pradhāna’, ‘prakṛti’  
of the world  the material cause  pradhāna  prakṛti

(58) That māyā exists in two ways; as the material cause of the world it is called *pradhāna* or *prakṛti*.[^15]

জগৎকারণ নহে প্রকৃতি জড়রূপা ৷  
শক্তি সঞ্চারিয়া তারে কৃষ্ণ করে কৃপা ॥ ৫৯ ॥

jagat-kāraṇa nahe prakṛti jaḍa-rūpā  
of the world  the cause  is not  prakṛti  inert  in nature  
śakti sañcāriyā tāre kṛṣṇa kare kṛpā  
potency  infusing  into her  Kṛṣṇa  shows  mercy

(59) Inert prakṛti is not the cause of the world; Kṛṣṇa shows her mercy by infusing her with His potency.

কৃষ্ণশক্ত্যে প্রকৃতি হয় গৌণ কারণ ৷  
অগ্নিশক্ত্যে লৌহ যৈছে করয়ে জারণ ॥ ৬০ ॥

kṛṣṇa-śaktye prakṛti haya gauṇa kāraṇa  
by Kṛṣṇa’s  potency  prakṛti  becomes  the secondary  cause  
agni-śaktye lauha yaiche karaye jāraṇa  
by fire’s  power  iron  as  does  burning

(60) By Kṛṣṇa’s potency prakṛti becomes a secondary cause, just as iron burns by the power of fire.

অতএব কৃষ্ণ মূল-জগৎকারণ ৷  
প্রকৃতি — কারণ, যৈছে অজাগলস্তন ॥ ৬১ ॥

ataeva kṛṣṇa mūla-jagat-kāraṇa  
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  the original  of the world  cause  
prakṛti — kāraṇa, yaiche ajā-gala-stana  
prakṛti  the cause  as  of a goat  on the neck  the teats

(61) Therefore Kṛṣṇa is the original cause of the world. To call prakṛti the cause is like taking the teats on a goat’s neck for an udder.

মায়া-অংশে কহি তারে নিমিত্ত-কারণ ৷  
সেহ নহে, যাতে কর্ত্তা-হেতু — নারায়ণ ॥ ৬২ ॥

māyā-aṁśe kahi tāre nimitta-kāraṇa  
in the māyā  aspect  I call  her  the efficient  cause  
seha nahe, yāte kartā-hetu — nārāyaṇa  
even that  is not so  because  the agent  cause  Nārāyaṇa

(62) In her aspect as māyā I call her the efficient cause of the world; but even that is not so, for the true maker and cause is Nārāyaṇa.[^16]

ঘটের নিমিত্ত-হেতু যৈছে কুম্ভকার ৷  
তৈছে জগতের কর্ত্তা — পুরুষাবতার ॥ ৬৩ ॥

ghaṭera nimitta-hetu yaiche kumbhakāra  
of the pot  efficient  cause  as  the potter  
taiche jagatera kartā — puruṣāvatāra  
so  of the world  the maker  the Puruṣa-avatāra

(63) As the potter is the efficient cause of the pot, so the Puruṣa-avatāra is the maker of the world.

কৃষ্ণ — কর্ত্তা, মায়া তাঁর করেন সহায় ৷  
ঘটের কারণ — চক্র-দণ্ডাদি উপায় ॥ ৬৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa — kartā, māyā tām̐ra karena sahāya  
Kṛṣṇa  the maker  māyā  to Him  gives  help  
ghaṭera kāraṇa — cakra-daṇḍādi upāya  
of the pot  cause  wheel  stick  and other  instruments

(64) Kṛṣṇa is the maker, and māyā only helps Him, as the wheel, the stick and other tools are causes of the pot.

দূর হৈতে পুরুষ করে মায়াতে অবধান ৷  
জীবরূপ বীর্য্য তাতে করেন আধান ॥ ৬৫ ॥

dūra haite puruṣa kare māyāte avadhāna  
from afar  the Puruṣa  makes  on māyā  a glance  
jīva-rūpa vīrya tāte karena ādhāna  
in the form of jīvas  the seed  into her  He makes  placing

(65) From afar the Puruṣa glances upon māyā and places in her the seed — the jīvas.

এক অঙ্গাভাসে করে মায়াতে মিলন ৷  
মায়া হৈতে জন্মে তবে ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের গণ ॥ ৬৬ ॥

eka aṅgābhāse kare māyāte milana  
by one  semblance of His body  makes  with māyā  union  
māyā haite janme tabe brahmāṇḍera gaṇa  
from māyā  are born  then  of universes  the multitudes

(66) By a mere semblance of His body He unites with māyā, and from māyā the hosts of universes are then born.[^17]

অগণ্য, অনন্ত যত অণ্ড-সন্নিবেশ ৷  
ততরূপে পুরুষ করে সবাতে প্রবেশ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

agaṇya, ananta yata aṇḍa-sanniveśa  
countless  endless  as many  universes’  arrangements  
tata-rūpe puruṣa kare sabāte praveśa  
in so many  forms  the Puruṣa  makes  into all  entrance

(67) Into all those countless, endless universes the Puruṣa enters, taking as many forms.

পুরুষ-নাসাতে যবে বাহিরায় শ্বাস ৷  
নিশ্বাস সহিতে হয় ব্রহ্মাণ্ড-প্রকাশ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

puruṣa-nāsāte yabe bāhirāya śvāsa  
the Puruṣa’s  from the nostrils  when  goes out  the breath  
niśvāsa sahite haya brahmāṇḍa-prakāśa  
the exhalation  together with  becomes  of universes  the manifestation

(68) When the breath goes out of the Puruṣa’s nostrils, the universes become manifest with His exhalation.

পুনরপি শ্বাস যবে প্রবেশে অন্তরে ৷  
শ্বাস-সহ ব্রহ্মাণ্ড পৈশে পুরুষ-শরীরে ॥ ৬৯ ॥

punarapi śvāsa yabe praveśe antare  
again  the breath  when  enters  within  
śvāsa-saha brahmāṇḍa paiśe puruṣa-śarīre  
with the breath  the universes  enter  of the Puruṣa  into the body

(69) And when the breath enters again, the universes enter the Puruṣa’s body with it.

গবাক্ষের রন্ধ্রে যেন ত্রসরেণু চলে ৷  
পুরুষের লোমকূপে ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের জালে ॥ ৭০ ॥

gavākṣera randhre yena trasareṇu cale  
of a window  through the holes  as  specks of dust  move  
puruṣera loma-kūpe brahmāṇḍera jāle  
of the Puruṣa  in the pores  of universes  the multitudes

(70) As specks of dust move through the holes of a lattice window, so hosts of universes move through the pores of the Puruṣa’s skin.

যস্যৈকনিশ্বসিতকালমথাবলম্ব্য  
জীবন্তি লোমবিলজা জগদণ্ডনাথাঃ ৷  
বিষ্ণুর্মহান্ স ইহ যস্য কলাবিশেষো  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ৭১ ॥

yasyaika-niśvasita-kālam athāvalambya  
whose  one  exhalation’s  span  then  depending on  
jīvanti loma-vila-jā jagad-aṇḍa-nāthāḥ  
live  from the pores  born  of the universes  the lords  
viṣṇur mahān sa iha yasya kalā-viśeṣo  
Viṣṇu  the Great  He  here  whose  kalā  particular  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(71) “The lords of the universes, born from His pores, abide for the span of one of His breaths. That Mahā-Viṣṇu is a particular kalā of Govinda, the original Person, whom I worship.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.48)[^18]

ক্বাহং তমো-মহদহং-খ-চরাগ্নিবার্ভূ-  
সংবেষ্টিতাণ্ডঘট-সপ্তবিতস্তিকায়ঃ ৷  
ক্বেদৃগ্বিধাঽবিগণিতাণ্ডপরাণুচর্য্যা-  
বাতাধ্বরোমবিবরস্য চ তে মহিত্বম্ ॥ ৭২ ॥

kvāhaṁ tamo-mahad-ahaṁ-kha-carāgni-vār-bhū-  
where  I  by darkness  the mahat  false ego  ether  air  fire  water  earth  
saṁveṣṭitāṇḍa-ghaṭa-sapta-vitasti-kāyaḥ  
enclosed  universe  in the pot  seven  spans  of body  
kvedṛg-vidhā ’vigaṇitāṇḍa-parāṇu-caryā-  
where  such  countless  universes  like atoms  moving  
vātādhva-roma-vivarasya ca te mahitvam  
through the windows  the pores of the skin  and  Your  greatness

(72) “Where am I, a creature with a body seven spans tall, enclosed in the pot of a universe wrapped in prakṛti, the mahat, false ego, ether, air, fire, water and earth? And where is Your greatness, when countless such universes drift like atoms through the pores of Your skin, as through the openings of a lattice?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.11)[^19]

অংশের অংশ যেই, ‘কলা’ তার নাম ৷  
গোবিন্দের প্রতিমূর্ত্তি শ্রীবলরাম ॥ ৭৩ ॥

aṁśera aṁśa yei, ‘kalā’ tāra nāma  
of a part  the part  which  kalā  its  name  
govindera pratimūrti śrī-balarāma  
of Govinda  the second body  Śrī Balarāma

(73) A part of a part is called a *kalā*. Śrī Balarāma is Govinda’s second body.[^20]

তাঁর এক স্বরূপ — শ্রীমহাসঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
তাঁর অংশ ‘পুরুষ’ হয় কলাতে গণন ॥ ৭৪ ॥

tām̐ra eka svarūpa — śrī-mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa  
His  one  form  Śrī Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  
tām̐ra aṁśa ‘puruṣa’ haya kalāte gaṇana  
His  part  the Puruṣa  is  among the kalās  counted

(74) One of His forms is Śrī Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa, and Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa’s part, the Puruṣa, is counted as a *kalā*.

যাঁহাকে ত’ কলা কহি, তিঁহো মহাবিষ্ণু ৷  
মহাপুরুষাবতারী, সেহো সর্ব্বজিষ্ণু ॥ ৭৫ ॥

yām̐hāke ta’ kalā kahi, tim̐ho mahā-viṣṇu  
whom  indeed  kalā  I call  He  Mahā-Viṣṇu  
mahā-puruṣāvatārī, seho sarva-jiṣṇu  
of the Mahā-Puruṣa avatāras the source  He  all-conquering

(75) He whom I call a kalā is Mahā-Viṣṇu. He is the source of the Puruṣa-avatāras, ever victorious.

গর্ভোদ-ক্ষীরোদ-শায়ী দোঁহে ‘পুরুষ’ নাম ৷  
সেই দুই, যাঁর অংশ, — বিষ্ণু, বিশ্বধাম ॥ ৭৬ ॥

garbhoda-kṣīroda-śāyī dom̐he ‘puruṣa’ nāma  
in the Garbha  Kṣīra  lying  both  Puruṣa  named  
sei dui, yām̐ra aṁśa, — viṣṇu, viśvadhāma  
those  two  whose  parts  Viṣṇu  abode of the universes

(76) The Lords who lie in the Garbha and Kṣīra oceans are both called Puruṣas, and He whose parts They are is Viṣṇu, the abode of all the universes.

বিষ্ণোস্তু ত্রীণি রূপাণি পুরুষাখ্যান্যথো বিদুঃ ৷  
একন্তুমহতঃ স্রষ্টৃ দ্বিতীয়ং ত্বণ্ডসংস্থিতম্ ৷  
তৃতীয়ং সর্ব্বভূতস্থং তানি জ্ঞাত্বা বিমুচ্যতে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

viṣṇos tu trīṇi rūpāṇi  
of Viṣṇu  but  three  forms  
puruṣākhyāny atho viduḥ  
called Puruṣas  then  they know  
ekaṁ tu mahataḥ sraṣṭṛ  
one  but  of the mahat  the creator  
dvitīyaṁ tv aṇḍa-saṁsthitam  
the second  but  in the universe  abiding  
tṛtīyaṁ sarva-bhūta-sthaṁ  
the third  in all  beings  abiding  
tāni jñātvā vimucyate  
them  knowing  one is liberated

(77) “Viṣṇu has three forms called Puruṣas: the first creates the mahat, the second abides within the universe, and the third dwells in all beings. Knowing Them, one is set free.” (Sātvata-tantra; Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, Pūrva-khaṇḍa, 2.9)

যদ্যপি কহিয়ে তাঁরে কৃষ্ণের ‘কলা’ করি ৷  
মৎস্যকূর্ম্মাদ্যবতারের তিঁহো অবতারী ॥ ৭৮ ॥

yadyapi kahiye tām̐re kṛṣṇera ‘kalā’ kari  
although  I call  Him  Kṛṣṇa’s  kalā  making  
matsya-kūrmādy-avatārera tim̐ho avatārī  
of Matsya  Kūrma  and other  avatāras  He  the source

(78) Though I call Him a kalā of Kṛṣṇa, He is the source of Matsya, Kūrma and the other avatāras.

এতে চাংশকলাঃ পুংসঃ কৃষ্ণস্তু ভগবান্ স্বয়ম্ ৷  
ইন্দ্রারিব্যাকুলং লোকং মৃড়য়ন্তি যুগে যুগে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

ete cāṁśa-kalāḥ puṁsaḥ  
these  and  portions  and parts  of the Puruṣa  
kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam  
Kṛṣṇa  but  Bhagavān  Himself  
indrāri-vyākulaṁ lokaṁ  
by the enemies of Indra  troubled  the world  
mṛḍayanti yuge yuge  
make happy  in age  after age

(79) “All these (avatāras) are portions (*aṁśa*) and parts of portions (*kalā*) of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself. Age after age They bring happiness to the world when it is troubled by the enemies of Indra.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.28)[^21][^22]

সেই পুরুষ সৃষ্টি-স্থিতি-প্রলয়ের কর্ত্তা ৷  
নানা অবতার করে, জগতের ভর্ত্তা ॥ ৮০ ॥

sei puruṣa sṛṣṭi-sthiti-pralayera kartā  
that  Puruṣa  of creation  maintenance  dissolution  the doer  
nānā avatāra kare, jagatera bhartā  
many  avatāras  makes  of the world  the sustainer

(80) That Puruṣa creates, maintains and dissolves the world; He sends forth many avatāras and sustains the world.

সৃষ্ট্যাদি-নিমিত্তে যেই অংশের অবধান ৷  
সেই ত’ অংশেরে কহি ‘অবতার’ নাম ॥ ৮১ ॥

sṛṣṭy-ādi-nimitte yei aṁśera avadhāna  
creation  and the rest  for the sake of  which  part’s  descent  
sei ta’ aṁśere kahi ‘avatāra’ nāma  
that  very  part  I call  avatāra  by name

(81) The part that descends for creation and the rest I call by the name *avatāra*.

আদ্যাবতার, মহাপুরুষ, ভগবান্ ৷  
সর্ব্ব-অবতার-বীজ, সর্ব্বাশ্রয়-ধাম ॥ ৮২ ॥

ādyāvatāra, mahā-puruṣa, bhagavān  
the first avatāra  the Mahā-Puruṣa  Bhagavān  
sarva-avatāra-bīja, sarvāśraya-dhāma  
of all  avatāras  the seed  of all  the shelter  the abode

(82) He is the first avatāra, the Mahā-Puruṣa, Bhagavān — the seed of all avatāras, the abode and shelter of all.

আদ্যোঽবতারঃ পুরুষঃ পরস্য  
কালঃ স্বভাবঃ সদসন্মনশ্চ ৷  
দ্রব্যং বিকারো গুণ ইন্দ্রিয়াণি  
বিরাট্ স্বরাট্ স্থাস্নু চরিষ্ণু ভূম্নঃ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

ādyo ’vatāraḥ puruṣaḥ parasya  
the first  avatāra  the Puruṣa  of the Supreme  
kālaḥ svabhāvaḥ sad-asan manaś ca  
time  nature  cause and effect  the mind  and  
dravyaṁ vikāro guṇa indriyāṇi  
the elements  false ego  the guṇas  the senses  
virāṭ svarāṭ sthāsnu cariṣṇu bhūmnaḥ  
the universal body  the self-luminous  the unmoving  the moving  of the Great One

(83) “The Puruṣa is the first avatāra of the Supreme. Time, nature, prakṛti as cause and effect, the mind, the elements, false ego, the guṇas, the senses, the universal body (*virāṭ*) and the Vairāja (*svarāṭ*), and all beings, moving and unmoving — all these are the splendour of the Great One.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.6.42)[^23]

জগৃহে পৌরুষং রূপং ভগবান্মহদাদিভিঃ ৷  
সম্ভূতং ষোড়শকলমাদৌ লোকসিসৃক্ষয়া ॥ ৮৪ ॥

jagṛhe pauruṣaṁ rūpaṁ  
accepted  of the Puruṣa  form  
bhagavān mahad-ādibhiḥ  
Bhagavān  with the mahat  and the rest  
sambhūtaṁ ṣoḍaśa-kalam  
composed  of sixteen  parts  
ādau loka-sisṛkṣayā  
in the beginning  the worlds  desiring to create

(84) “In the beginning, desiring to create the worlds, Bhagavān accepted the form of the Puruṣa, joined with the mahat and the other principles and possessed of sixteen potencies (*kalā*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.1)[^24]

যদ্যপি সর্ব্বাশ্রয় তিঁহো, তাঁহাতে সংসার ৷  
অন্তরাত্মা-রূপে তিঁহো জগৎ-আধার ॥ ৮৫ ॥

yadyapi sarvāśraya tim̐ho, tām̐hāte saṁsāra  
although  of all the shelter  He  in Him  the world  
antarātmā-rūpe tim̐ho jagat-ādhāra  
the Supersoul  in the form of  He  of the world  the support

(85) He is the shelter of all, and the world rests in Him; as the Supersoul He is the support of the world.

প্রকৃতি-সহিতে তাঁর উভয় সম্বন্ধ ৷  
তথাপি প্রকৃতি-সহ নাহি স্পর্শগন্ধ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

prakṛti-sahite tām̐ra ubhaya sambandha  
with prakṛti  His  twofold  relation  
tathāpi prakṛti-saha nāhi sparśa-gandha  
yet  with prakṛti  there is not  of touch  a scent

(86) He has a twofold relation with prakṛti, and yet there is not even a trace of contact between Him and prakṛti.

এতদীশনমীশস্য প্রকৃতিস্থোঽপি তদ্গুণৈঃ ৷  
ন যুজ্যতে সদাত্মস্থৈর্যথা বুদ্ধিস্তদাশ্রয়া ॥ ৮৭ ॥

etad īśanam īśasya  
this  the lordship  of the Lord  
prakṛti-stho ’pi tad-guṇaiḥ  
in material nature  abiding  though  by its qualities  
na yujyate sadātma-sthair  
not  is affected  ever  abiding in Him  
yathā buddhis tad-āśrayā  
as  the intelligence  sheltered in Him

(87) “This is the lordship of the Lord: though He abides within material nature, He is never affected by its qualities — just as the intelligence of those who have taken shelter of Him is not affected.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.11.38)[^25]

এইমত গীতাতেহো পুনঃ পুনঃ কয় ৷  
সর্ব্বদা ঈশ্বর-তত্ত্ব অচিন্ত্যশক্তি হয় ॥ ৮৮ ॥

ei mata gītāteho punaḥ punaḥ kaya  
in this way  in the Gītā also  again  again  it says  
sarvadā īśvara-tattva acintya-śakti haya  
always  of the Lord  the truth  of inconceivable potency  is

(88) So the Gītā too declares again and again that the truth of the Lord is always endowed with inconceivable potency:

আমি ত’ জগতে বসি, জগৎ আমাতে ৷  
না আমি জগতে বসি, না আমা জগতে ॥ ৮৯ ॥

āmi ta’ jagate vasi, jagat āmāte  
I  indeed  in the world  abide  the world  in Me  
nā āmi jagate vasi, nā āmā jagate  
not  I  in the world  abide  not  in Me  the world

(89) “I abide in the world, and the world is in Me; and yet I do not abide in the world, nor is the world in Me.

অচিন্ত্য ঐশ্বর্য্য এই জানিহ আমার ৷  
এই ত’ গীতার অর্থ কৈল পরচার ॥ ৯০ ॥

acintya aiśvarya ei jāniha āmāra  
inconceivable  opulence  this  know  My  
ei ta’ gītāra artha kaila paracāra  
this  indeed  of the Gītā  the meaning  is made  proclaimed

(90) Know this to be My inconceivable power.” Such is the meaning the Gītā proclaims.

সেই ত’ পুরুষ যাঁর ‘অংশ’ ধরে নাম ৷  
চৈতন্যের সঙ্গে সেই নিত্যানন্দ-রাম ॥ ৯১ ॥

sei ta’ puruṣa yām̐ra ‘aṁśa’ dhare nāma  
that  very  Puruṣa  whose  part  bears  the name  
caitanyera saṅge sei nityānanda-rāma  
of Caitanya  the companion  that  Nityānanda-Rāma

(91) He of whom that Puruṣa is called a part is Nityānanda-Rāma, the companion of Caitanya.

এই ত’ নবম শ্লোকের অর্থ-বিবরণ ৷  
দশম শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ৯২ ॥

ei ta’ navama ślokera artha-vivaraṇa  
this  indeed  of the ninth  verse  of the meaning  the explanation  
daśama ślokera artha śuna diyā mana  
of the tenth  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  the mind

(92) Such is the explanation of the ninth verse. Now hear attentively the meaning of the tenth.

যস্যাংশাংশঃ শ্রীল-গর্ভোদশায়ী  
যন্নাভ্যব্জং লোকসঙ্ঘাতনালম্ ৷  
লোকস্রষ্টুঃ সূতিকাধামধাতু-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৯৩ ॥

yasyāṁśāṁśaḥ śrīla-garbhoda-śāyī  
whose  part of a part  the blessed  Garbhodakaśāyī  
yan-nābhy-abjaṁ loka-saṅghāta-nālam  
whose  navel-lotus  of the worlds  totality  as its stem  
loka-sraṣṭuḥ sūtikā-dhāma dhātus  
of the creator of the worlds  of birth  the place  of Brahmā  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(93) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of His part is the blessed Garbhodakaśāyī, whose navel-lotus, with all the worlds as its stem, is the birthplace of Brahmā, the creator of the worlds.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^26]

সেই ত’ পুরুষ অনন্তব্রহ্মাণ্ড সৃজিয়া ৷  
সব অণ্ডে প্রবেশিলা বহু-মূর্ত্তি হঞা ॥ ৯৪ ॥

sei ta’ puruṣa ananta-brahmāṇḍa sṛjiyā  
that  very  Puruṣa  endless universes  having created  
saba aṇḍe praveśilā bahu-mūrti hañā  
into all  universes  entered  many  forms  becoming

(94) Having created endless universes, that Puruṣa entered them all, becoming many forms.

ভিতরে প্রবেশি’ দেখে সব অন্ধকার ৷  
রহিতে নাহিক স্থান করিল বিচার ॥ ৯৫ ॥

bhitare praveśi’ dekhe saba andhakāra  
within  having entered  sees  all  darkness  
rahite nāhika sthāna karila vicāra  
to stay  there is no  place  made  reflection

(95) Entering, He saw only darkness everywhere, and finding no place to stay, He reflected.

নিজাঙ্গ-স্বেদজল করিল সৃজন ৷  
সেই জলে কৈল অর্দ্ধ-ব্রহ্মাণ্ড ভরণ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

nijāṅga-sveda-jala karila sṛjana  
of His own body  the sweat-water  made  creation  
sei jale kaila ardha-brahmāṇḍa bharaṇa  
with that  water  made  half the universe  filling

(96) From the perspiration of His own body He created water and with it filled half the universe.

ব্রহ্মাণ্ড-প্রমাণ পঞ্চাশৎকোটি-যোজন ৷  
আয়াম, বিস্তার, দুই হয় এক সম ॥ ৯৭ ॥

brahmāṇḍa-pramāṇa pañcāśat-koṭi-yojana  
of the universe  the measure  fifty  crores  of yojanas  
āyāma, vistāra, dui haya eka sama  
length  breadth  the two  are  one  equal

(97) The universe measures fifty crores of yojanas, equal in length and breadth.

জলে ভরি’ অর্দ্ধ তাঁহা কৈল নিজ-বাস ৷  
আর অর্দ্ধে কৈল চৌদ্দভুবন প্রকাশ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

jale bhari’ ardha tām̐hā kaila nija-vāsa  
with water  filling  half  there  made  His own  dwelling  
āra ardhe kaila caudda-bhuvana prakāśa  
the other  in the half  made  of the fourteen worlds  manifestation

(98) Filling half with water, He made His dwelling there; in the other half He manifested the fourteen worlds.

তাঁহাই প্রকট কৈল বৈকুণ্ঠ নিজ-ধাম ৷  
শেষ-শয়ন-জলে করিল বিশ্রাম ॥ ৯৯ ॥

tām̐hāi prakaṭa kaila vaikuṇṭha nija-dhāma  
there  manifest  made  Vaikuṇṭha  His own  abode  
śeṣa-śayana-jale karila viśrāma  
Śeṣa  as bed  on the water  made  rest

(99) There He manifested Vaikuṇṭha, His own abode, and rested on the water with Śeṣa as His bed.

অনন্তশয়্যাতে তাঁহা করিল শয়ন ৷  
সহস্র মস্তক তাঁর সহস্র বদন ॥ ১০০ ॥

সহস্র-চরণ-হস্ত, সহস্র-নয়ন ৷  
সর্ব্ব-অবতার-বীজ, জগৎ-কারণ ॥ ১০১ ॥

ananta-śayyāte tām̐hā karila śayana  
on Ananta  the bed  there  made  lying down  
sahasra mastaka tām̐ra sahasra vadana  
a thousand  heads  His  a thousand  faces  
sahasra-caraṇa-hasta, sahasra-nayana  
a thousand  feet  hands  a thousand  eyes  
sarva-avatāra-bīja, jagat-kāraṇa  
of all  avatāras  the seed  of the world  the cause

(100–101) There He lay on Ananta as His bed. He has a thousand heads and a thousand faces, a thousand hands and feet and a thousand eyes. He is the seed of all avatāras and the cause of the world.[^27]

তাঁর নাভিপদ্ম হৈতে উঠিল এক পদ্ম ৷  
সেই পদ্মে হৈল ব্রহ্মার জন্ম-সদ্ম ॥ ১০২ ॥

tām̐ra nābhi-padma haite uṭhila eka padma  
His  from the navel-lotus  arose  one  lotus  
sei padme haila brahmāra janma-sadma  
that  lotus  became  of Brahmā  birth  the abode

(102) From His navel-lotus a lotus arose, and that lotus became the birthplace of Brahmā.

সেই পদ্মনালে হৈল চৌদ্দভুবন ৷  
তেঁহো ব্রহ্মা হঞা সৃষ্টি করিল সৃজন ॥ ১০৩ ॥

sei padma-nāle haila caudda-bhuvana  
in that  lotus  stem  came to be  the fourteen  worlds  
tem̐ho brahmā hañā sṛṣṭi karila sṛjana  
He  Brahmā  becoming  creation  made  bringing forth

(103) In the stem of that lotus the fourteen worlds came to be, and He, becoming Brahmā, brought forth the creation.

বিষ্ণুরূপ হঞা করে জগৎ পালনে ৷  
গুণাতীত-বিষ্ণু স্পর্শ নাহি মায়া-গুণে ॥ ১০৪ ॥

viṣṇu-rūpa hañā kare jagat pālane  
Viṣṇu’s  form  taking  makes  of the world  maintenance  
guṇātīta-viṣṇu sparśa nāhi māyā-guṇe  
beyond the guṇas  Viṣṇu  touch  there is no  of māyā  with the guṇas

(104) Taking the form of Viṣṇu, He maintains the world. Viṣṇu is beyond the guṇas; the guṇas of māyā never touch Him.

রুদ্ররূপ ধরি’ করে জগৎ সংহার ৷  
সৃষ্টি-স্থিতি-প্রলয় — ইচ্ছায় যাঁহার ॥ ১০৫ ॥

rudra-rūpa dhari’ kare jagat saṁhāra  
Rudra’s  form  assuming  makes  of the world  destruction  
sṛṣṭi-sthiti-pralaya — icchāya yām̐hāra  
creation  maintenance  dissolution  by the will  of whom

(105) Assuming the form of Rudra, He destroys the world. Creation, maintenance and dissolution all take place by His will.

হিরণ্যগর্ভ, অন্তর্য্যামী, জগৎ-কারণ ৷  
যাঁর অংশে করি’ করে বিরাট-কল্পন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

hiraṇya-garbha, antaryāmī, jagat-kāraṇa  
Hiraṇyagarbha  the Indweller  of the world  the cause  
yām̐ra aṁśe kari’ kare virāṭa-kalpana  
whom  as a part  considering  make  of the universal form  the conception

(106) He is Hiraṇyagarbha, the Indweller, the cause of the world; and it is a part of Him that is conceived as the universal form (*virāṭ*).

হেন নারায়ণ, — যাঁর অংশের অংশ ৷  
সেই প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ — সর্ব্ব-অবতংস ॥ ১০৭ ॥

hena nārāyaṇa, — yām̐ra aṁśera aṁśa  
such  Nārāyaṇa  of whose  part  a part  
sei prabhu nityānanda — sarva-avataṁsa  
that  Lord  Nityānanda  of all  the crown

(107) Great as Nārāyaṇa is, He is only a part of a part of Lord Nityānanda, the crown of all avatāras.

দশম শ্লোকের অর্থ কৈল বিবরণ ৷  
একাদশ শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ১০৮ ॥

daśama ślokera artha kaila vivaraṇa  
of the tenth  verse  the meaning  I made  explanation  
ekādaśa ślokera artha śuna diyā mana  
of the eleventh  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  the mind

(108) I have explained the meaning of the tenth verse; now hear attentively the meaning of the eleventh.

যস্যাংশাংশাংশঃ পরাত্মাখিলানাং  
পোষ্টা বিষ্ণুর্ভাতি দুগ্ধাব্ধিশায়ী ৷  
ক্ষৌণীভর্ত্তা যৎকলা সোঽপ্যনন্ত-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১০৯ ॥

yasyāṁśāṁśāṁśaḥ parātmākhilānāṁ  
whose  part of a part of a part  the Supersoul  of all  
poṣṭā viṣṇur bhāti dugdhābdhi-śāyī  
the maintainer  Viṣṇu  appears  lying on the Ocean of Milk  
kṣauṇī-bhartā yat-kalā so ’py anantas  
of the earth  the upholder  whose part  He  also  Ananta  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(109) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of a part of His part is Viṣṇu who lies on the Ocean of Milk, the Supersoul and maintainer of all beings; and Ananta, who upholds the earth, is a portion of that Viṣṇu.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^28]

নারায়ণের নাভিনাল-মধ্যেতে ধরণী ৷  
ধরণীর মধ্যে সপ্ত সমুদ্র যে গণি ॥ ১১০ ॥

nārāyaṇera nābhi-nāla-madhyete dharaṇī  
of Nārāyaṇa  navel  stem  within  the earth  
dharaṇīra madhye sapta samudra ye gaṇi  
of the earth  within  seven  oceans  which  are counted

(110) Within the stem of Nārāyaṇa’s navel-lotus is the earth, and within the earth are counted seven oceans.[^29]

তাঁহা ক্ষীরোদধি-মধ্যে ‘শ্বেতদ্বীপ’ নাম ৷  
পালয়িতা বিষ্ণু, — তাঁর সেই নিজ ধাম ॥ ১১১ ॥

tām̐hā kṣīrodadhi-madhye ‘śvetadvīpa’ nāma  
there  of the Milk Ocean  in the midst  Śvetadvīpa  named  
pālayitā viṣṇu, — tām̐ra sei nija dhāma  
the maintainer  Viṣṇu  His  that  own  abode

(111) There, in the midst of the Ocean of Milk, is the island called Śvetadvīpa, the abode of Viṣṇu the maintainer.

সকল জীবের তিঁহো হয়ে অন্তর্য্যামী ৷  
জগৎ-পালক তিঁহো জগতের স্বামী ॥ ১১২ ॥

sakala jīvera tim̐ho haye antaryāmī  
of all  beings  He  is  the Indweller  
jagat-pālaka tim̐ho jagatera svāmī  
of the world  the maintainer  He  of the world  the master

(112) He is the Indweller of all beings, the maintainer of the world and its master.

যুগ-মন্বন্তরে ধরি’ নানা অবতার ৷  
ধর্ম্ম সংস্থাপন করে, অধর্ম্ম সংহার ॥ ১১৩ ॥

yuga-manvantare dhari’ nānā avatāra  
in the yugas  manvantaras  taking  many  avatāras  
dharma saṁsthāpana kare, adharma saṁhāra  
of dharma  establishing  makes  of adharma  destruction

(113) In each yuga and manvantara He takes many avatāras, establishing dharma and destroying adharma.

দেবগণে না পায় যাঁহার দরশন ৷  
ক্ষীরোদকতীরে যাই’ করেন স্তবন ॥ ১১৪ ॥

deva-gaṇe nā pāya yām̐hāra daraśana  
the gods  not  obtain  whose  sight  
kṣīrodaka-tīre yāi’ karena stavana  
of the Milk Ocean  to the shore  going  make  praise

(114) Even the gods cannot see Him; they go to the shore of the Ocean of Milk and offer Him prayers.

তবে অবতরি’ করে জগৎ পালন ৷  
অনন্ত বৈভব তাঁর নাহিক গণন ॥ ১১৫ ॥

tabe avatari’ kare jagat pālana  
then  descending  makes  of the world  protection  
ananta vaibhava tām̐ra nāhika gaṇana  
endless  splendour  His  there is no  counting

(115) Then He descends and protects the world. His splendour is endless, beyond all counting.

সেই বিষ্ণু হয় যাঁর অংশাংশের অংশ ৷  
সেই প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ — সর্ব্ব-অবতংস ॥ ১১৬ ॥

sei viṣṇu haya yām̐ra aṁśāṁśera aṁśa  
that  Viṣṇu  is  of whose  part of a part  a part  
sei prabhu nityānanda — sarva-avataṁsa  
that  Lord  Nityānanda  of all  the crown

(116) And that Viṣṇu is a part of a part of a part of Lord Nityānanda, the crown of all avatāras.

সেই বিষ্ণু ‘শেষ’ রূপে ধরেন ধরণী ৷  
কাঁহা আছে মহী, শিরে, হেন নাহি জানি ॥ ১১৭ ॥

sei viṣṇu ‘śeṣa’ rūpe dharena dharaṇī  
that  Viṣṇu  Śeṣa  in the form  holds  the earth  
kām̐hā āche mahī, śire, hena nāhi jāni  
where  is  the earth  on the head  this  not  knows

(117) That Viṣṇu, in the form of Śeṣa, holds the earth, and does not even know where on His heads it lies.

সহস্র বিস্তীর্ণ যাঁর ফণার মণ্ডল ৷  
সূর্য্য জিনি’ মণিগণ করে ঝলমল ॥ ১১৮ ॥

sahasra vistīrṇa yām̐ra phaṇāra maṇḍala  
a thousand  broad  whose  of hoods  the circle  
sūrya jini’ maṇi-gaṇa kare jhala-mala  
the sun  surpassing  gems  multitude  make  glittering

(118) On the circle of His thousand broad hoods hosts of gems glitter, outshining the sun.

পঞ্চাশৎকোটি-যোজন পৃথিবী বিস্তার ৷  
যাঁর একফণে রহে সর্ষপ-আকার ॥ ১১৯ ॥

pañcāśat-koṭi-yojana pṛthivī vistāra  
fifty  crores  of yojanas  the earth’s  extent  
yām̐ra eka-phaṇe rahe sarṣapa-ākāra  
whose  on one hood  remains  of a mustard seed  the size

(119) The earth, fifty crores of yojanas wide, rests on one of His hoods like a mustard seed.

সেই ত’ ‘অনন্ত’ ‘শেষ’ — ভক্ত-অবতার ৷  
ঈশ্বরের সেবা বিনা নাহি জানে আর ॥ ১২০ ॥

sei ta’ ‘ananta’ ‘śeṣa’ — bhakta-avatāra  
that  very  Ananta  Śeṣa  devotee  avatāra  
īśvarera sevā vinā nāhi jāne āra  
of the Lord  service  except  not  knows  other

(120) That Ananta Śeṣa is an avatāra in the form of a devotee; He knows nothing but service to the Lord.

সহস্র-বদনে করে কৃষ্ণগুণ গান ৷  
নিরবধি গুণ গান, অন্ত নাহি পা’ন ॥ ১২১ ॥

sahasra-vadane kare kṛṣṇa-guṇa gāna  
with a thousand  mouths  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  singing  
niravadhi guṇa gāna, anta nāhi pā’na  
without end  qualities  He sings  end  not  finds

(121) With His thousand mouths He sings of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities; He sings without cease and never reaches their end.

সনকাদি ভাগবত শুনে যার মুখে ৷  
ভগবানের গুণ কহে, ভাসে প্রেমসুখে ॥ ১২২ ॥

sanakādi bhāgavata śune yāra mukhe  
Sanaka and the others  the Bhāgavata  hear  from whose  mouth  
bhagavānera guṇa kahe, bhāse prema-sukhe  
of Bhagavān  the qualities  He tells  He floats  in the joy of love

(122) From His mouth Sanaka and the other Kumāras hear the *Bhāgavatam*, while He, awash in the joy of love, tells of the qualities of Bhagavān.

ছত্র, পাদুকা, শয়্যা, উপাধান, বসন ৷  
আরাম, আবাস, যজ্ঞসূত্র, সিংহাসন ॥ ১২৩ ॥

chatra, pādukā, śayyā, upādhāna, vasana  
umbrella  sandals  bed  pillow  garments  
ārāma, āvāsa, yajña-sūtra, siṁhāsana  
resting place  dwelling  sacred thread  throne

(123) He becomes umbrella, sandals, bed, pillow, garments, resting place, dwelling, sacred thread and throne.

এত মূর্ত্তি-ভেদ করি’ কৃষ্ণসেবা করে ৷  
কৃষ্ণের শেষতা পাঞা ‘শেষ’ নাম ধরে ॥ ১২৪ ॥

eta mūrti-bheda kari’ kṛṣṇa-sevā kare  
so many  forms  different  assuming  Kṛṣṇa’s service  He does  
kṛṣṇera śeṣatā pāñā ‘śeṣa’ nāma dhare  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  service  the utmost limit  reaching  Śeṣa  the name  He bears

(124) Assuming all these different forms He serves Kṛṣṇa; and because He has reached the utmost limit of service to Kṛṣṇa, He bears the name Śeṣa.[^30]

সেই ত’ অনন্ত, যাঁর কহি এক কলা ৷  
হেন প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ, কে জানে তাঁর খেলা ॥ ১২৫ ॥

sei ta’ ananta, yām̐ra kahi eka kalā  
that  very  Ananta  of whom  I call  one  kalā  
hena prabhu nityānanda, ke jāne tām̐ra khelā  
such  Lord  Nityānanda  who  knows  His  play

(125) That Ananta I call but one kalā of Him. Such is Lord Nityānanda — who can know His play?

এসব প্রমাণে জানি নিত্যানন্দতত্ত্বসীমা ৷  
তাঁহাকে ‘অনন্ত’ কহি, কি তাঁর মহিমা ॥ ১২৬ ॥

esaba pramāṇe jāni nityānanda-tattva-sīmā  
by all these  proofs  I know  of Nityānanda’s truth  the full extent  
tām̐hāke ‘ananta’ kahi, ki tām̐ra mahimā  
Him  Ananta  I call  what  His  glory

(126) By all these proofs I know the full truth of Nityānanda. If I call Him Ananta, what glory is that to Him?

অথবা ভক্তের বাক্য মানি সত্য করি’ ৷  
সকল সম্ভবে তাঁতে, যাতে অবতারী ॥ ১২৭ ॥

athavā bhaktera vākya māni satya kari’  
or else  of the devotees  the words  accept  true  considering  
sakala sambhave tām̐te, yāte avatārī  
all  is possible  in Him  since  the source of avatāras

(127) Or else, accepting the words of the devotees as true, I say: everything is possible in Him, for He is the source of the avatāras.

অবতার-অবতারী — অভেদ, যে জানে ৷  
পূর্ব্বে যৈছে কৃষ্ণকে কেহো কাহো করি’ মানে ॥ ১২৮ ॥

avatāra-avatārī — abheda, ye jāne  
avatāra  the source of avatāras  non-different  who  knows  
pūrve yaiche kṛṣṇake keho kāho kari’ māne  
before  as  Kṛṣṇa  someone  as someone  considering  accepted

(128) One who knows that the avatāras and their source are non-different speaks in this way — just as in the past people saw in Kṛṣṇa this or that avatāra:[^31]

কেহো কহে, কৃষ্ণ সাক্ষাৎ নরনারায়ণ ৷  
কেহো কহে, কৃষ্ণ হয় সাক্ষাৎ বামন ॥ ১২৯ ॥

keho kahe, kṛṣṇa sākṣāt nara-nārāyaṇa  
someone  says  Kṛṣṇa  directly  Nara-Nārāyaṇa  
keho kahe, kṛṣṇa haya sākṣāt vāmana  
someone  says  Kṛṣṇa  is  directly  Vāmana

(129) some said that Kṛṣṇa was Nara-Nārāyaṇa Himself, others that He was Vāmana Himself,

কেহো কহে, কৃষ্ণ ক্ষীরোদশায়ী-অবতার ৷  
অসম্ভব নহে, সত্য বচন সবার ॥ ১৩০ ॥

keho kahe, kṛṣṇa kṣīroda-śāyī-avatāra  
someone  says  Kṛṣṇa  of Kṣīrodaśāyī  an avatāra  
asambhava nahe, satya vacana sabāra  
impossible  it is not  true  the words  of all

(130) and still others that He was an avatāra of Kṣīrodaśāyī. None of this is impossible: the words of all are true.

কৃষ্ণ যবে অবতরে সর্ব্বাংশ-আশ্রয় ৷  
সর্ব্বাংশ আসি’ তবে কৃষ্ণেতে মিলয় ॥ ১৩১ ॥

kṛṣṇa yabe avatare sarvāṁśa-āśraya  
Kṛṣṇa  when  descends  of all portions  the shelter  
sarvāṁśa āsi’ tabe kṛṣṇete milaya  
all portions  coming  then  in Kṛṣṇa  unite

(131) When Kṛṣṇa, the shelter of all portions, descends, all His portions come and unite in Him.

যেই যেই রূপে জানে, সেই তাহা কহে ৷  
সকল সম্ভবে কৃষ্ণে, কিছু মিথ্যা নহে ॥ ১৩২ ॥

yei yei rūpe jāne, sei tāhā kahe  
in whatever  whatever  form  knows  he  that  says  
sakala sambhave kṛṣṇe, kichu mithyā nahe  
all  is possible  in Kṛṣṇa  nothing  false  is

(132) So each speaks of Him in the form in which he knows Him. Everything is possible in Kṛṣṇa; none of it is false.

অতএব শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য গোসাঞি ৷  
সর্ব্ব-অবতার-লীলা করি’ সবারে দেখাই ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

ataeva śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya gosāñi  
therefore  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  Gosāñi  
sarva-avatāra-līlā kari’ sabāre dekhāi  
of all  avatāras  the pastimes  performing  to all  showed

(133) Therefore Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gosāñi performed the pastimes of all the avatāras and showed them to everyone.

এইরূপে নিত্যানন্দ ‘অনন্ত’ প্রকাশ ৷  
সেইভাবে — কহে মুঞি চৈতন্যের দাস ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

ei-rūpe nityānanda ‘ananta’ prakāśa  
in this way  Nityānanda’s  Ananta  manifestation  
sei-bhāve — kahe muñi caitanyera dāsa  
in that mood  says  I  of Caitanya  servant

(134) In this way Nityānanda is manifest as Ananta, and in that same mood He says, “I am Caitanya’s servant.”

কভু গুরু, কভু সখা, কভু ভৃত্য-লীলা ৷  
পূর্ব্বে যেন তিনভাবে ব্রজে কৈল খেলা ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

kabhu guru, kabhu sakhā, kabhu bhṛtya-līlā  
sometimes  guru  sometimes  friend  sometimes  servant’s pastime  
pūrve yena tina-bhāve vraje kaila khelā  
before  as  in three moods  in Vraja  made  play

(135) Sometimes He plays the guru, sometimes the friend, sometimes the servant — just as He once played in Vraja in these three moods.

বৃষ হঞা কৃষ্ণসনে মাথামাথি রণ ৷  
কভু কৃষ্ণ করে তাঁর পাদ সম্বাহন ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

vṛṣa hañā kṛṣṇa-sane māthā-māthi raṇa  
a bull  becoming  with Kṛṣṇa  head to head  fight  
kabhu kṛṣṇa kare tām̐ra pāda samvāhana  
sometimes  Kṛṣṇa  makes  His  feet  massaging

(136) Becoming a bull, He would fight with Kṛṣṇa head to head; and sometimes Kṛṣṇa would massage His feet.

আপনাকে ভৃত্য করি’ কৃষ্ণে প্রভু জানে ৷  
কৃষ্ণের কলার কলা আপনাকে মানে ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

āpanāke bhṛtya kari’ kṛṣṇe prabhu jāne  
Himself  a servant  considering  Kṛṣṇa  as Master  knows  
kṛṣṇera kalāra kalā āpanāke māne  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  kalā  a kalā  Himself  He considers

(137) He thinks of Himself as a servant and knows Kṛṣṇa as His Master; He considers Himself but a kalā of Kṛṣṇa’s kalā.

বৃষায়মাণৌ নর্দ্দন্তৌ যুয়ুধাতে পরস্পরম্ ৷  
অনুকৃত্য রুতৈর্জন্তূংশ্চেরতুঃ প্রাকৃতৌ যথা ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

vṛṣāyamāṇau nardantau  
behaving as bulls  bellowing  
yuyudhāte parasparam  
they fought  with each other  
anukṛtya rutair jantūṁś  
imitating  with cries  animals  
ceratuḥ prākṛtau yathā  
they roamed  ordinary (children)  like

(138) “Pretending to be bulls, the two would bellow and fight with each other; imitating the cries of animals, They roamed about like ordinary children.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.11.40)

ক্বচিৎ ক্রীড়া-পরিশ্রান্তং গোপোৎসঙ্গোপবর্হণম্ ৷  
স্বয়ং বিশ্রাময়ত্যার্য্যং পাদসম্বাহনাদিভিঃ ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

kvacit krīḍā-pariśrāntaṁ  
sometimes  by play  wearied  
gopotsaṅgopabarhaṇam  
of a cowherd boy  the lap  as His pillow  
svayaṁ viśrāmayaty āryaṁ  
Himself  gives rest  to the elder  
pāda-saṁvāhanādibhiḥ  
of the feet  by massaging  and so on

(139) “Sometimes, when His elder brother lay tired from play with His head on a cowherd boy’s lap, Kṛṣṇa Himself would give Him rest by massaging His feet and serving Him in other ways.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.15.14)

কেয়ং বা কুত আয়াতা দৈবী বা নার্য্যুতাসুরী ৷  
প্রায়ো মায়াস্তু মে ভর্ত্তুর্নান্যা মেঽপি বিমোহিনী ॥ ১৪০ ॥

keyaṁ vā kuta āyātā  
who is she  or  from where  has come  
daivī vā nāry utāsurī  
divine  or  human  or  demonic  
prāyo māyāstu me bhartur  
most likely  māyā  it must be  of My  Master  
nānyā me ’pi vimohinī  
no other  Me  even  bewilders

(140) (Baladeva said in wonder:) “Who is she, and where has she come from? Is she divine, human or demonic? Most likely she is the māyā of My Master, for no other can bewilder even Me.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.13.37)[^32]

যস্যাঙ্ঘ্রিপঙ্কজরজোঽখিললোক-পালৈ-  
র্মৌল্যুত্তমৈর্ধৃতমুপাসিত-তীর্থতীর্থম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মা ভবোঽহমপি যস্য কলাঃ কলায়াঃ  
শ্রীশ্চোদ্বহেম চিরমস্য নৃপাসনং ক্ব ॥ ১৪১ ॥

yasyāṅghri-paṅkaja-rajo ’khila-loka-pālair  
whose  feet  lotus  the dust  of all  the worlds  by the guardians  
mauly-uttamair dhṛtam upāsita-tīrtha-tīrtham  
on heads  best  borne  the worshipped  of holy places  the holy place  
brahmā bhavo ’ham api yasya kalāḥ kalāyāḥ  
Brahmā  Śiva  I  also  whose  parts  of a part  
śrīś codvahema ciram asya nṛpāsanaṁ kva  
Śrī  and  we bear  ever  His  royal throne  what

(141) (Baladeva said:) “The guardians of all the worlds bear on their crowned heads the dust of His lotus feet, the holiest of holy places; and Brahmā, Śiva, Lakṣmī and I Myself, mere parts of His part, carry it on our heads forever. What then is a king’s throne to Him?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.68.37)[^33]

একলা ঈশ্বর কৃষ্ণ, আর সব ভৃত্য ৷  
যারে যৈছে নাচায়, সে তৈছে করে নৃত্য ॥ ১৪২ ॥

ekalā īśvara kṛṣṇa, āra saba bhṛtya  
alone  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa  the rest  all  servants  
yāre yaiche nācāya, se taiche kare nṛtya  
whom  as  He makes dance  he  so  makes  dance

(142) Kṛṣṇa alone is the Lord; all others are His servants. As He makes each one dance, so each one dances.

এই মত চৈতন্যগোসাঞি একলা ঈশ্বর ৷  
আর সব পারিষদ, কেহ বা কিঙ্কর ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

ei mata caitanyagosāñi ekalā īśvara  
in this way  Caitanya Gosāñi  alone  the Lord  
āra saba pāriṣada, keha vā kiṅkara  
the rest  all  associates  some  or  servants

(143) In the same way Caitanya Gosāñi alone is the Lord; all the rest are His associates or His servants.

গুরুবর্গ, — নিত্যানন্দ, অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য ৷  
শ্রীবাসাদি, আর যত — লঘু, সম, আর্য্য ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

সবে পারিষদ, সবে লীলার সহায় ৷  
সবা লঞা নিজ-কার্য্য সাধে গৌর-রায় ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

guru-varga, — nityānanda, advaita ācārya  
the elders  Nityānanda  Advaita  Ācārya  
śrīvāsādi, āra yata — laghu, sama, ārya  
Śrīvāsa and others  and  all  junior  equal  senior  
sabe pāriṣada, sabe līlāra sahāya  
all  associates  all  of the pastimes  helpers  
sabā lañā nija-kārya sādhe gaura-rāya  
all  taking  His own  work  accomplishes  Gaura-rāya

(144–145) Nityānanda, Advaita Ācārya, Śrīvāsa and others play the part of His elders and gurus, and all the rest are His juniors, equals and seniors. All are His associates and helpers in His pastimes, and with them all Gaura-rāya accomplishes His purpose.

অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য, নিত্যানন্দ, — দুই অঙ্গ ৷  
দুইজন লঞা প্রভুর যত কিছু রঙ্গ ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

advaita ācārya, nityānanda, — dui aṅga  
Advaita  Ācārya  Nityānanda  two  limbs  
dui-jana lañā prabhura yata kichu raṅga  
the two  taking  of the Lord  all  whatever  pastimes

(146) Advaita Ācārya and Nityānanda are His two limbs (*aṅga*); with these two the Lord performs all His pastimes.[^34]

অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর ৷  
প্রভু, গুরু করি’ মানে, তিঁহো ত’ কিঙ্কর ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

advaita-ācārya-gosāñi sākṣāt īśvara  
Advaita  Ācārya  Gosāñi  directly  the Lord  
prabhu, guru kari’ māne, tim̐ho ta’ kiṅkara  
the Lord  as guru  considering  honours  He  indeed  a servant

(147) Advaita Ācārya Gosāñi is the Lord Himself. The Lord honours Him as a guru, yet He is His servant.[^35]

আচার্য্য-গোসাঞির তত্ত্ব না যায় কথন ৷  
কৃষ্ণ অবতারিয়া যেঁহো তারিল ভুবন ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

ācārya-gosāñira tattva nā yāya kathana  
of the Ācārya Gosāñi  the truth  not  can be  told  
kṛṣṇa avatāriyā yem̐ho tārila bhuvana  
Kṛṣṇa  making descend  who  delivered  the world

(148) The truth of Ācārya Gosāñi cannot be told: it was He who brought Kṛṣṇa down and delivered the world.

নিত্যানন্দস্বরূপ পূর্ব্বে হইয়া লক্ষ্মণ ৷  
লঘুভ্রাতা হৈয়া করে রামের সেবন ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

nityānanda-svarūpa pūrve haiyā lakṣmaṇa  
Nityānanda-svarūpa  before  becoming  Lakṣmaṇa  
laghu-bhrātā haiyā kare rāmera sevana  
younger  brother  becoming  makes  Rāma’s  service

(149) Nityānanda Svarūpa, appearing before as Lakṣmaṇa, served Rāma as His younger brother.[^36]

রামের চরিত্র সব, — দুঃখের কারণ ৷  
স্বতন্ত্র লীলায় দুঃখ সহেন লক্ষ্মণ ॥ ১৫০ ॥

rāmera caritra saba, — duḥkhera kāraṇa  
of Rāma  the deeds  all  of suffering  the cause  
svatantra līlāya duḥkha sahena lakṣmaṇa  
independent  in pastime  suffering  endures  Lakṣmaṇa

(150) Everything Rāma did brought Him suffering, and Lakṣmaṇa, of His own free will, bore that suffering with Him.

নিষেধ করিতে নারে, যাতে ছোট ভাই ৷  
মৌন ধরি’ রহে লক্ষ্মণ মনে দুঃখ পাই’ ॥ ১৫১ ॥

niṣedha karite nāre, yāte choṭa bhāi  
objection  to make  cannot  since  younger  brother  
mauna dhari’ rahe lakṣmaṇa mane duḥkha pāi’  
silence  keeping  remains  Lakṣmaṇa  in the mind  sorrow  feeling

(151) Being the younger brother, He could not object; so Lakṣmaṇa kept silent and grieved within.

কৃষ্ণ-অবতারে জ্যেষ্ঠ হৈলা সেবার কারণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণকে করাইল নানা সুখ আস্বাদন ॥ ১৫২ ॥

kṛṣṇa-avatāre jyeṣṭha hailā sevāra kāraṇa  
in Kṛṣṇa’s  descent  the elder  He became  of service  for the sake  
kṛṣṇake karāila nānā sukha āsvādana  
to Kṛṣṇa  He gave  many  joys  to taste

(152) In Kṛṣṇa’s descent He became the elder brother for the sake of service, and He gave Kṛṣṇa many joys to taste.

রাম-লক্ষ্মণ — কৃষ্ণ-রামের অংশবিশেষ ৷  
অবতার-কালে দোঁহে দোঁহাতে প্রবেশ ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

rāma-lakṣmaṇa — kṛṣṇa-rāmera aṁśa-viśeṣa  
Rāma  Lakṣmaṇa  of Kṛṣṇa and Rāma  particular portions  
avatāra-kāle dom̐he dom̐hāte praveśa  
of descent  at the time  the two  into the two  entering

(153) Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa are particular portions of Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma, and when Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma descend, Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa enter into Them.[^37]

সেই অংশ লঞা জ্যেষ্ঠ-কনিষ্ঠাভিমান ৷  
অংশাংশী রূপে শাস্ত্রে করয়ে ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

sei aṁśa lañā jyeṣṭha-kaniṣṭhābhimāna  
those  portions  taking  of elder  and younger  the sense  
aṁśāṁśī rūpe śāstre karaye vyākhyāna  
part and whole  in the form  in the scriptures  is made  explanation

(154) Together with those portions They take the roles of elder and younger brother, and the scriptures describe Them as the whole and His portion.

রামাদিমূর্ত্তিষু কলানিয়মেন তিষ্ঠন্  
নানাবতারমকরোদ্ভুবনেষু কিন্তু ৷  
কৃষ্ণঃ স্বয়ং সমভবৎ পরমঃ পুমান্ যো  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

rāmādi-mūrtiṣu kalā-niyamena tiṣṭhan  
in Rāma  and others  forms  by kalās  in order  abiding  
nānāvatāram akarod bhuvaneṣu kintu  
many avatāras  made  in the worlds  but  
kṛṣṇaḥ svayaṁ samabhavat paramaḥ pumān yo  
as Kṛṣṇa  Himself  appeared  the Supreme  Person  who  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(155) “I worship Govinda, the original Person, who, abiding through His kalās in Rāma and other forms, manifested many avatāras in the worlds, but who Himself, the Supreme Person, appeared as Kṛṣṇa.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.39)[^38]

শ্রীচৈতন্য — সেই কৃষ্ণ, নিত্যানন্দ — রাম ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ পূর্ণ করে চৈতন্যের কাম ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

śrī-caitanya — sei kṛṣṇa, nityānanda — rāma  
Śrī Caitanya  that  Kṛṣṇa  Nityānanda  Rāma  
nityānanda pūrṇa kare caitanyera kāma  
Nityānanda  fulfilment  makes  of Caitanya’s  desire

(156) Śrī Caitanya is that Kṛṣṇa, and Nityānanda is Rāma. Nityānanda fulfils all of Caitanya’s desires.

নিত্যানন্দ-মহিমা-সিন্ধু অনন্ত, অপার ৷  
এক কণা স্পর্শি মাত্র, — সে কৃপা তাঁহার ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

nityānanda-mahimā-sindhu ananta, apāra  
of Nityānanda’s glory  the ocean  endless  shoreless  
eka kaṇā sparśi mātra, — se kṛpā tām̐hāra  
one  drop  I touch  only  that  mercy  His

(157) The ocean of Nityānanda’s glory is endless and shoreless. I touch but a single drop of it — and that only by His mercy.

আর এক শুন তাঁর কৃপার মহিমা ৷  
অধম জীবেরে চড়াইল ঊর্দ্ধ্ব সীমা ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

āra eka śuna tām̐ra kṛpāra mahimā  
one more  hear  His  of mercy  the greatness  
adhama jīvere caḍāila ūrddhva sīmā  
a fallen  soul  He raised  to the highest  limit

(158) Hear one more glory of His mercy: He raised a fallen soul to the very highest.[^39]

বেদগুহ্য কথা এই অয়োগ্য কহিতে ৷  
তথাপি কহিয়ে তাঁর কৃপা প্রকাশিতে ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

veda-guhya kathā ei ayogya kahite  
like the Vedas  secret  story  this  unfit  to tell  
tathāpi kahiye tām̐ra kṛpā prakāśite  
still  I tell  His  mercy  to reveal

(159) This story is as secret as the Vedas and not fit to be told; still I tell it to reveal His mercy.

উল্লাস-উপরি লেখোঁ তোমার প্রসাদ ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ প্রভু, মোর ক্ষম অপরাধ ॥ ১৬০ ॥

ullāsa-upari lekhom̐ tomāra prasāda  
in overflowing joy  I write  of Your  grace  
nityānanda prabhu, mora kṣama aparādha  
Nityānanda  Prabhu  my  forgive  offence

(160) In overflowing joy I write of Your grace. O Nityānanda Prabhu, forgive my offence!

অবধূত গোসাঞির এক ভৃত্য প্রেমধাম ৷  
মীনকেতন রামদাস হয় তাঁর নাম ॥ ১৬১ ॥

avadhūta gosāñira eka bhṛtya prema-dhāma  
of the Avadhūta  Gosāñi  one  servant  abode of love  
mīnaketana rāmadāsa haya tām̐ra nāma  
Mīnaketana  Rāmadāsa  is  his  name

(161) Avadhūta Gosāñi had a servant who was an abode of love; his name was Mīnaketana Rāmadāsa.[^40]

আমার আলয়ে অহোরাত্র-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
তাহাতে আইলা তিঁহো পাঞা নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১৬২ ॥

āmāra ālaye aho-rātra-saṅkīrtana  
in my  house  day and night  saṅkīrtana  
tāhāte āilā tim̐ho pāñā nimantraṇa  
there  came  he  receiving  an invitation

(162) In my house there was saṅkīrtana day and night, and he came there by invitation.

মহাপ্রেমময় তিঁহো বসিলা অঙ্গনে ৷  
সকল বৈষ্ণব তাঁর বন্দিলা চরণে ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

mahā-prema-maya tim̐ho vasilā aṅgane  
with great  love  filled  he  sat  in the courtyard  
sakala vaiṣṇava tām̐ra vandilā caraṇe  
all  Vaiṣṇavas  his  bowed down  at the feet

(163) Full of great love, he sat down in the courtyard, and all the Vaiṣṇavas bowed at his feet.

নমস্কার করিতে, কা’র উপরেতে চড়ে ৷  
প্রেমে কা’রে বংশী মারে, কাহাকে চাপড়ে ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

namaskāra karite, kā’ra uparete caḍe  
as they bowed  on whom  upon  he climbs  
preme kā’re vaṁśī māre, kāhāke cāpaḍe  
in love  whom  with the flute  he strikes  whom  he slaps

(164) As they bowed, he would climb on the back of one, and in his love strike another with his flute or slap yet another.

যে নয়ন দেখিতে অশ্রু হয় মনে যার ৷  
সেই নেত্রে অবিচ্ছিন্ন বহে অশ্রুধার ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

ye nayana dekhite aśru haya mane yāra  
which  eyes  on seeing  tears  come  in the mind  of whom  
sei netre avicchinna vahe aśru-dhāra  
from those  eyes  unbroken  flows  of tears  a stream

(165) From those eyes, the very sight of which brings tears to one’s heart, flowed an unbroken stream of tears.

কভু কোন অঙ্গে দেখি পুলক-কদম্ব ৷  
এক অঙ্গে জাড়্য তাঁর, আর অঙ্গে কম্প ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

kabhu kona aṅge dekhi pulaka-kadamba  
sometimes  on some  limb  I see  hairs on end  like kadamba  
eka aṅge jāḍya tām̐ra, āra aṅge kampa  
on one  limb  stiffness  his  on another  limb  trembling

(166) At times I saw the hair on some limb stand on end like kadamba blossoms; one limb would stiffen while another trembled.

নিত্যানন্দ বলি’ যবে করেন হুঙ্কার ৷  
তাহা দেখি’ লোকের হয় মহা-চমৎকার ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

nityānanda bali’ yabe karena huṅkāra  
Nityānanda  saying  when  makes  a loud cry  
tāhā dekhi’ lokera haya mahā-camatkāra  
that  seeing  of the people  is  great  wonder

(167) When he roared out “Nityānanda!”, all who saw it were filled with wonder.

গুণার্ণব মিশ্র নামে এক বিপ্র আর্য্য ৷  
শ্রীমূর্ত্তি-নিকটে তেঁহো করে সেবা-কার্য্য ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

guṇārṇava miśra nāme eka vipra ārya  
Guṇārṇava  Miśra  by name  a  brāhmaṇa  respectable  
śrī-mūrti-nikaṭe tem̐ho kare sevā-kārya  
the Deity  near  he  does  of service  the work

(168) A respectable brāhmaṇa named Guṇārṇava Miśra was serving the Deity there.

অঙ্গনে বসিয়া তেঁহো না কৈল সম্ভাষ ৷  
তাহা দেখি’ ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা বলে রামদাস ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

aṅgane vasiyā tem̐ho nā kaila sambhāṣa  
in the courtyard  sitting (to him)  he  not  made  greeting  
tāhā dekhi’ kruddha hañā bale rāmadāsa  
that  seeing  angry  becoming  says  Rāmadāsa

(169) Rāmadāsa sat in the courtyard, but the brāhmaṇa did not greet him; seeing this, Mīnaketana said angrily:[^41]

এই ত’ দ্বিতীয় সূত রোমহরষণ ৷  
বলদেব দেখি’ যে না কৈল প্রত্যুদ্গম ॥ ১৭০ ॥

ei ta’ dvitīya sūta romaharaṣaṇa  
this  indeed  a second  Sūta  Romaharṣaṇa  
baladeva dekhi’ ye nā kaila pratyudgama  
Baladeva  seeing  who  not  made  rising to greet

(170) “Here is a second Romaharṣaṇa Sūta, who did not rise to greet Baladeva when he saw Him!”[^42]

এত বলি’ নাচে গায়, করয়ে সন্তোষ ৷  
কৃষ্ণকার্য্য করে বিপ্র — না করিল রোষ ॥ ১৭১ ॥

eta bali’ nāce gāya, karaye santoṣa  
so  saying  he dances  sings  makes  rejoicing  
kṛṣṇa-kārya kare vipra — nā karila roṣa  
Kṛṣṇa’s work  does  the brāhmaṇa  not  showed  anger

(171) Having said this, he danced and sang and rejoiced; but the brāhmaṇa went on with his service to Kṛṣṇa and showed no anger.

উৎসবান্তে গেলা তিঁহো করিয়া প্রসাদ ৷  
মোর ভ্রাতা-সনে তাঁর কিছু হৈল বাদ ॥ ১৭২ ॥

utsavānte gelā tim̐ho kariyā prasāda  
at the festival’s end  went  he  having given  blessings  
mora bhrātā-sane tām̐ra kichu haila vāda  
my  brother  with  his  some  arose  dispute

(172) At the end of the festival he was leaving, having given his blessings, when a dispute arose between him and my brother.

চৈতন্য-প্রভুতে তাঁর সুদৃঢ় বিশ্বাস ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-প্রতি তাঁর বিশ্বাস-আভাস ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

caitanya-prabhute tām̐ra sudṛḍha viśvāsa  
in Caitanya Prabhu  his  firm  faith  
nityānanda-prati tām̐ra viśvāsa-ābhāsa  
towards Nityānanda  his  of faith  a semblance

(173) My brother had firm faith in Caitanya Prabhu, but towards Nityānanda only a semblance of faith.

ইহা জানি’ রামদাসের দুঃখ হৈল মনে ৷  
তবে ত’ ভ্রাতারে আমি করিনু ভর্ৎসনে ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

ihā jāni’ rāmadāsera duḥkha haila mane  
this  knowing  Rāmadāsa’s  sorrow  was  in the mind  
tabe ta’ bhrātāre āmi karinu bhartsane  
then  indeed  the brother  I  made  rebuke

(174) Knowing this, Rāmadāsa was grieved at heart, and so I rebuked my brother:

দুই ভাই একতনু — সমান-প্রকাশ ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ না মান’, তোমার হবে সর্ব্বনাশ ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

dui bhāi eka-tanu — samāna-prakāśa  
two  brothers  one body  equal manifestation  
nityānanda nā māna’, tomāra habe sarva-nāśa  
Nityānanda  not  you accept  your  will be  total  ruin

(175) “The two brothers are one body, equally manifest. If you do not accept Nityānanda, you will be utterly ruined.

একেতে বিশ্বাস, অন্যে না কর সম্মান ৷  
‘অর্ধকুক্কুটী-ন্যায়’ তোমার প্রমাণ ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

ekete viśvāsa, anye nā kara sammāna  
in one  faith  to the other  not  you give  respect  
‘ardha-kukkuṭī-nyāya’ tomāra pramāṇa  
of half a hen  the logic  your  standard

(176) You have faith in one but show the other no respect: your standard is the logic of half a hen.

কিংবা, দোঁহা না মানিঞা হও ত’ পাষণ্ড ৷  
একে মানি’ আরে না মানি, — এইমত ভণ্ড ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

kiṁvā, dom̐hā nā māniñā hao ta’ pāṣaṇḍa  
or else  both  not  accepting  be  then  an atheist  
eke māni’ āre nā māni, — ei-mata bhaṇḍa  
one  accepting  the other  not  accepting  this kind of  hypocrite

(177) If you accept neither, you are an atheist; and to accept one and not the other is hypocrisy.”[^43]

ক্রুদ্ধ হৈয়া বংশী ভাঙ্গি’ চলে রামদাস ৷  
তৎকালে আমার ভ্রাতার হৈল সর্ব্বনাশ ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

kruddha haiyā vaṁśī bhāṅgi’ cale rāmadāsa  
angry  becoming  the flute  breaking  goes away  Rāmadāsa  
tat-kāle āmāra bhrātāra haila sarva-nāśa  
at that moment  my  brother’s  came about  total  ruin

(178) In anger Rāmadāsa broke his flute and went away, and at that very moment my brother was ruined.

এই ত’ কহিল তাঁর সেবক-প্রভাব ৷  
আর এক কহি তাঁর দয়ার স্বভাব ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

ei ta’ kahila tām̐ra sevaka-prabhāva  
thus  indeed  I have told  His  servant’s  power  
āra eka kahi tām̐ra dayāra svabhāva  
one more  I tell  His  mercy’s  nature

(179) Thus I have told of the power of His servant. Now I tell of something more — the nature of His mercy.

ভাইকে ভর্ৎসিনু মুঞি, লঞা এই গুণ ৷  
সেই রাত্রে প্রভু মোরে দিলা দরশন ॥ ১৮০ ॥

bhāike bhartsinu muñi, lañā ei guṇa  
the brother  I rebuked  I  taking  this  as a virtue  
sei rātre prabhu more dilā daraśana  
that  night  the Lord  to me  gave  sight

(180) Because I had rebuked my brother — and He counted this as a virtue — that night the Lord appeared to me.

নৈহাটি-নিকটে ‘ঝামটপুর’ নামে গ্রাম ৷  
তাঁহা স্বপ্নে দেখা দিলা নিত্যানন্দ-রাম ॥ ১৮১ ॥

naihāṭi-nikaṭe ‘jhāmaṭapura’ nāme grāma  
Naihāṭi  near  Jhāmaṭapura  named  a village  
tām̐hā svapne dekhā dilā nityānanda-rāma  
there  in a dream  showed Himself  Nityānanda-Rāma

(181) In a village called Jhāmaṭapura, near Naihāṭi, Nityānanda-Rāma appeared to me in a dream.[^44]

দণ্ডবৎ হৈয়া আমি পড়িনু পায়েতে ৷  
নিজপাদপদ্ম প্রভু দিলা মোর মাথে ॥ ১৮২ ॥

daṇḍavat haiyā āmi paḍinu pāyete  
like a rod  becoming  I  fell  at the feet  
nija-pāda-padma prabhu dilā mora māthe  
His own  lotus foot  the Lord  placed  my  on the head

(182) I fell flat like a rod at His feet, and the Lord placed His lotus foot upon my head.

‘উঠ’, ‘উঠ’ বলি’ মোরে বলে বার বার ৷  
উঠি’ তাঁর রূপ দেখি’ হৈনু চমৎকার ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

‘uṭha’, ‘uṭha’ bali’ more bale bāra bāra  
rise  rise  saying  to me  He says  again  again  
uṭhi’ tām̐ra rūpa dekhi’ hainu camatkāra  
rising  His  form  seeing  I became  astonished

(183) Again and again He said to me, “Rise, rise!” I rose, and seeing His form I was struck with wonder.

শ্যাম-চিক্কণ কান্তি, প্রকাণ্ড শরীর ৷  
সাক্ষাৎ কন্দর্প, যৈছে মহামল্ল-বীর ॥ ১৮৪ ॥

śyāma-cikkaṇa kānti, prakāṇḍa śarīra  
dark  glossy  lustre  huge  body  
sākṣāt kandarpa, yaiche mahā-malla-vīra  
very  Kandarpa  like  a great wrestler  hero

(184) His huge body had a glossy dark lustre. He was as beautiful as Kandarpa, the god of love, in person, and had the bearing of a great champion wrestler.

সুবলিত হস্ত, পদ, কমল-লোচন ৷  
পট্টবস্ত্র শিরে, পট্টবস্ত্র পরিধান ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

suvalita hasta, pada, kamala-locana  
shapely  hands  feet  lotus  eyes  
paṭṭa-vastra śire, paṭṭa-vastra paridhāna  
silk  cloth  on the head  silk  cloth  garment

(185) His hands and feet were finely shaped, His eyes like lotuses; He wore silk on His head and silk as His garment.

সুবর্ণ-কুণ্ডল কর্ণে, স্বর্ণাঙ্গদ-বালা ৷  
পায়েতে নূপুর বাজে, কণ্ঠে পুষ্পমালা ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

suvarṇa-kuṇḍala karṇe, svarṇāṅgada-vālā  
golden  earrings  in the ears  golden armlets  bracelets  
pāyete nūpura bāje, kaṇṭhe puṣpa-mālā  
on the feet  ankle-bells  ring  on the neck  a flower garland

(186) Golden earrings hung in His ears, golden armlets and bracelets adorned His arms; ankle-bells rang on His feet, and a garland of flowers lay on His neck.

চন্দন লেপিত অঙ্গ, তিলক সুঠাম ৷  
মত্ত-গজ জিনি’ মদ-মন্থর পয়ান ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

candana lepita aṅga, tilaka suṭhāma  
with sandal  anointed  the body  tilaka  graceful  
matta-gaja jini’ mada-manthara payāna  
a mad  elephant  surpassing  with intoxication  slow  gait

(187) His body was anointed with sandal and graced with a lovely tilaka; His slow, intoxicated gait surpassed that of a maddened elephant.

কোটিচন্দ্র-জিনি’ মুখ উজ্জ্বল-বরণ ৷  
দাড়িম্ব-বীজ-সম দন্তে তাম্বূল-চর্ব্বণ ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

koṭi-candra-jini’ mukha ujjvala-varaṇa  
millions of moons  surpassing  face  bright  in colour  
dāḍimba-bīja-sama dante tāmbūla-carvaṇa  
of pomegranate  seeds  like  in the teeth  betel  chewing

(188) His bright face outshone millions of moons; as He chewed betel, His teeth looked like pomegranate seeds.[^45]

প্রেমে মত্ত অঙ্গ ডাহিনে বামে দোলে ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ বলিয়া গম্ভীর বোল বলে ॥ ১৮৯ ॥

preme matta aṅga ḍāhine vāme dole  
with love  intoxicated  body  to the right  to the left  sways  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ baliyā gambhīra bola bale  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  saying  deep  words  speaks

(189) Intoxicated with love, His body swayed to right and left, and in a deep voice He called, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”

রাঙ্গা-যষ্টি-হস্তে দোলে যেন মত্ত সিংহ ৷  
চারি পাশে বেড়ি আছে চরণেতে ভৃঙ্গ ॥ ১৯০ ॥

rāṅgā-yaṣṭi-haste dole yena matta siṁha  
red  staff  in the hand  sways  like  a mad  lion  
cāri pāśe veḍi āche caraṇete bhṛṅga  
four  sides  surrounding  are  at the feet  bees

(190) With a red staff in His hand He swayed like a maddened lion, and bees hovered all around His feet.

পারিষদগণে দেখি’ সব গোপ-বেশে ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহে সবে সপ্রেম আবেশে ॥ ১৯১ ॥

pāriṣada-gaṇe dekhi’ saba gopa-veśe  
the associates  I see  all  in cowherd  dress  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahe sabe saprema āveśe  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  say  all  with love  in rapture

(191) I saw His associates, all dressed as cowherds, all calling “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” in loving rapture.

শিঙ্গা বাঁশী বাজায় কেহ, কেহ নাচে গায় ৷  
সেবক যোগায় তাম্বূল, চামর ঢুলায় ॥ ১৯২ ॥

śiṅgā vām̐śī bājāya keha, keha nāce gāya  
horns  flutes  plays  someone  someone  dances  sings  
sevaka yogāya tāmbūla, cāmara ḍhulāya  
a servant  offers  betel  a cāmara  waves

(192) Some played horns and flutes, some danced and sang; one servant offered betel, another waved a cāmara.

নিত্যানন্দ-স্বরূপের দেখিয়া বৈভব ৷  
কিবা রূপ, গুণ, লীলা — অলৌকিক সব ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

nityānanda-svarūpera dekhiyā vaibhava  
of Nityānanda-svarūpa  seeing  the splendour  
kibā rūpa, guṇa, līlā — alaukika saba  
what  form  qualities  pastimes  unearthly  all

(193) I beheld the splendour of Nityānanda Svarūpa — what form, what qualities, what pastimes, all beyond this world!

আনন্দে বিহ্বল আমি, কিছু নাহি জানি ৷  
তবে হাসি’ প্রভু মোরে কহিলেন বাণী ॥ ১৯৪ ॥

ānande vihvala āmi, kichu nāhi jāni  
with bliss  overwhelmed  I  anything  not  know  
tabe hāsi’ prabhu more kahilena vāṇī  
then  smiling  the Lord  to me  spoke  words

(194) Overwhelmed with bliss, I knew nothing at all. Then, smiling, the Lord spoke to me:

আরে আরে কৃষ্ণদাস, না করহ ভয় ৷  
বৃন্দাবনে যাহ, — তাঁহা সর্ব্ব লভ্য হয় ॥ ১৯৫ ॥

āre āre kṛṣṇadāsa, nā karaha bhaya  
O  O  Kṛṣṇadāsa  not  make  fear  
vṛndāvane yāha, — tām̐hā sarva labhya haya  
to Vṛndāvana  go  there  all  attainable  is

(195) “O Kṛṣṇadāsa, Kṛṣṇadāsa, do not be afraid! Go to Vṛndāvana: there everything can be attained.”

এত বলি’ প্রেরিলা মোরে হাতসান দিয়া ৷  
অন্তর্দ্ধান কৈল প্রভু নিজগণ লঞা ॥ ১৯৬ ॥

eta bali’ prerilā more hātasāna diyā  
so  saying  He sent  me  a touch of the hand  giving  
antardhāna kaila prabhu nija-gaṇa lañā  
disappearance  made  the Lord  His own  associates  taking

(196) Saying this, He touched me with His hand and sent me forth; and the Lord disappeared with His associates.[^46]

মূর্চ্ছিত হইয়া মুঞি পড়িনু ভূমিতে ৷  
স্বপ্নভঙ্গ হৈল, দেখি, হঞাছে প্রভাতে ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

mūrcchita haiyā muñi paḍinu bhūmite  
unconscious  becoming  I  fell  on the ground  
svapna-bhaṅga haila, dekhi, hañāche prabhāte  
of the dream  breaking  came  I see  it has become  morning

(197) I fell senseless to the ground; my dream broke, and I saw that morning had come.

কি দেখিনু কি শুনিনু, করিয়ে বিচার ৷  
প্রভু-আজ্ঞা হৈল বৃন্দাবন যাইবার ॥ ১৯৮ ॥

ki dekhinu ki śuninu, kariye vicāra  
what  I saw  what  I heard  I make  reflection  
prabhu-ājñā haila vṛndāvana yāibāra  
the Lord’s  order  was  to Vṛndāvana  to go

(198) I reflected on what I had seen and heard: the Lord had ordered me to go to Vṛndāvana.

সেইক্ষণে বৃন্দাবনে করিনু গমন ৷  
প্রভুর কৃপাতে সুখে আইনু বৃন্দাবন ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

sei kṣaṇe vṛndāvane karinu gamana  
at that  moment  to Vṛndāvana  I made  departure  
prabhura kṛpāte sukhe āinu vṛndāvana  
the Lord’s  by mercy  happily  I came  to Vṛndāvana

(199) At that very moment I set out, and by the Lord’s mercy I reached Vṛndāvana in happiness.

জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ, নিত্যানন্দ-রাম ৷  
যাঁহার কৃপাতে পাইনু বৃন্দাবন-ধাম ॥ ২০০ ॥

jaya jaya nityānanda, nityānanda-rāma  
glory  glory  Nityānanda  Nityānanda-Rāma  
yām̐hāra kṛpāte pāinu vṛndāvana-dhāma  
whose  by mercy  I attained  the abode of Vṛndāvana

(200) Glory, glory to Nityānanda, to Nityānanda-Rāma, by whose mercy I attained the abode of Vṛndāvana!

জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ, জয় কৃপাময় ৷  
যাঁহা হৈতে পাইনু রূপ-সনাতনাশ্রয় ॥ ২০১ ॥

jaya jaya nityānanda, jaya kṛpā-maya  
glory  glory  Nityānanda  glory  full of mercy  
yām̐hā haite pāinu rūpa-sanātanāśraya  
from whom  I attained  Rūpa  Sanātana  the shelter

(201) Glory, glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Him who is full of mercy, through whom I attained the shelter of Rūpa and Sanātana!

যাঁহা হৈতে পাইনু রঘুনাথ-মহাশয় ৷  
যাঁহা হৈতে পাইনু শ্রীস্বরূপ-আশ্রয় ॥ ২০২ ॥

yām̐hā haite pāinu raghunātha-mahāśaya  
from whom  I attained  Raghunātha  the great soul  
yām̐hā haite pāinu śrī-svarūpa-āśraya  
from whom  I attained  Śrī Svarūpa’s  shelter

(202) Through Him I attained the great soul Raghunātha; through Him I attained the shelter of Śrī Svarūpa.

সনাতন-কৃপায় পাইনু ভক্তির সিদ্ধান্ত ৷  
শ্রীরূপ-কৃপায় পাইনু ভক্তিরসপ্রান্ত ॥ ২০৩ ॥

sanātana-kṛpāya pāinu bhaktira siddhānta  
by Sanātana’s  mercy  I attained  of bhakti  the conclusions  
śrī-rūpa-kṛpāya pāinu bhakti-rasa-prānta  
by Śrī Rūpa’s  mercy  I attained  of bhakti-rasa  the nearness

(203) By Sanātana’s mercy I came to know the conclusions of bhakti; by Śrī Rūpa’s mercy I came near to bhakti-rasa.[^47]

জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ-চরণারবিন্দ ৷  
যাঁহা হৈতে পাইনু শ্রীরাধাগোবিন্দ ॥ ২০৪ ॥

jaya jaya nityānanda-caraṇāravinda  
glory  glory  of Nityānanda  to the lotus feet  
yām̐hā haite pāinu śrī-rādhā-govinda  
from which  I attained  Śrī Rādhā-Govinda

(204) Glory, glory to the lotus feet of Nityānanda, through which I attained Śrī Rādhā-Govinda!

জগাই মাধাই হৈতে মুঞি সে পাপিষ্ঠ ৷  
পুরীষের কীট হৈতে মুঞি সে লঘিষ্ঠ ॥ ২০৫ ॥

jagāi mādhāi haite muñi se pāpiṣṭha  
Jagāi  Mādhāi  than  I  am  more sinful  
purīṣera kīṭa haite muñi se laghiṣṭha  
of excrement  the worm  than  I  am  lower

(205) I am more sinful than Jagāi and Mādhāi, lower than a worm in excrement.

মোর নাম শুনে যেই তার পুণ্য ক্ষয় ৷  
মোর নাম লয় যেই তার পাপ হয় ॥ ২০৬ ॥

mora nāma śune yei tāra puṇya kṣaya  
my  name  hears  whoever  his  piety  is destroyed  
mora nāma laya yei tāra pāpa haya  
my  name  takes  whoever  his  sin  there is

(206) Whoever hears my name loses his piety; whoever utters my name commits sin.

এমন নির্ঘৃণ মোরে কেবা কৃপা করে ৷  
এক-নিত্যানন্দ বিনু জগৎ ভিতরে ॥ ২০৭ ॥

emana nirghṛṇa more kebā kṛpā kare  
such  despicable  me  who  mercy  shows  
eka-nityānanda vinu jagat bhitare  
one  Nityānanda  except  of the world  within

(207) Who in all the world but Nityānanda alone would show mercy to one as despicable as I?

প্রেমে মত্ত নিত্যানন্দ কৃপা-অবতার ৷  
উত্তম, অধম, কিছু না করে বিচার ॥ ২০৮ ॥

preme matta nityānanda kṛpā-avatāra  
with love  intoxicated  Nityānanda  of mercy  the avatāra  
uttama, adhama, kichu nā kare vicāra  
high  low  any  not  makes  distinction

(208) Intoxicated with love, Nityānanda is the avatāra of mercy; He makes no distinction between the high and the low.

যে আগে পড়য়ে, তারে করয়ে নিস্তার ৷  
অতএব নিস্তারিলা মো-হেন দুরাচার ॥ ২০৯ ॥

ye āge paḍaye, tāre karaye nistāra  
who  before (Him)  falls  him  He makes  deliverance  
ataeva nistārilā mo-hena durācāra  
therefore  He delivered  like me  a sinner

(209) He delivers whoever falls before Him; and so He delivered even a sinner like me.

মো-পাপিষ্ঠে আনিলেন শ্রীবৃন্দাবন ৷  
মো-হেন অধমে দিলা শ্রীরূপচরণ ॥ ২১০ ॥

mo-pāpiṣṭhe ānilena śrī-vṛndāvana  
me most sinful  He brought  to Śrī Vṛndāvana  
mo-hena adhame dilā śrī-rūpa-caraṇa  
like me  to the lowly  He gave  Śrī Rūpa’s  feet

(210) He brought me, the most sinful, to Śrī Vṛndāvana, and to one as low as I He gave the feet of Śrī Rūpa.

শ্রীমদনগোপাল-শ্রীগোবিন্দ-দরশন ৷  
কহিবার যোগ্য নহে এসব কথন ॥ ২১১ ॥

śrī-madana-gopāla-śrī-govinda-daraśana  
Śrī Madana-Gopāla’s  Śrī Govinda’s  sight  
kahibāra yogya nahe esaba kathana  
to tell  fit  is not  all this  account

(211) The sight of Śrī Madana-Gopāla and Śrī Govinda — all this is not fit to be told.

বৃন্দাবন-পুরন্দর শ্রীমদনগোপাল ৷  
রাসবিলাসী সাক্ষাৎ ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ॥ ২১২ ॥

vṛndāvana-purandara śrī-madana-gopāla  
of Vṛndāvana  the lord  Śrī Madana-Gopāla  
rāsa-vilāsī sākṣāt vrajendra-kumāra  
the enjoyer of the rāsa  directly  of the king of Vraja  the son

(212) Śrī Madana-Gopāla is the lord of Vṛndāvana, the enjoyer of the rāsa dance, the son of the king of Vraja Himself.

শ্রীরাধা-ললিতা-সঙ্গে রাস-বিলাস ৷  
মন্মথ-মন্মথরূপে যাঁহার প্রকাশ ॥ ২১৩ ॥

śrī-rādhā-lalitā-saṅge rāsa-vilāsa  
with Śrī Rādhā  Lalitā  together  the rāsa  pastime  
manmatha-manmatha-rūpe yām̐hāra prakāśa  
of Cupid  the enchanter  in the form  whose  manifestation

(213) With Śrī Rādhā and Lalitā He dances the rāsa, appearing in a form that enchants even Cupid.

তাসামাবিরভূচ্ছৌরিঃ স্ময়মানমুখাম্বুজঃ ৷  
পীতাম্বরধরঃ স্রগ্বী সাক্ষান্মন্মথমন্মথঃ ॥ ২১৪ ॥

tāsām āvirabhūc chauriḥ  
among them  appeared  Śauri  
smayamāna-mukhāmbujaḥ  
smiling  lotus-faced  
pītāmbara-dharaḥ sragvī  
yellow garments  wearing  garlanded  
sākṣān manmatha-manmathaḥ  
directly  of Cupid  the enchanter

(214) “Among them appeared Śauri (Kṛṣṇa), His lotus face smiling, wearing yellow garments and a garland — the very enchanter of Cupid.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.32.2)

স্বমাধুর্য্যে লোকের মন করে আকর্ষণ ৷  
দুই পাশে রাধা-ললিতা করেন সেবন ॥ ২১৫ ॥

sva-mādhurye lokera mana kare ākarṣaṇa  
by His own  sweetness  of people  the minds  makes  attraction  
dui pāśe rādhā-lalitā karena sevana  
on both  sides  Rādhā  Lalitā  render  service

(215) By His own sweetness He draws the minds of all, and on either side Rādhā and Lalitā serve Him.

নিত্যানন্দ-দয়া মোরে তাঁরে দেখাইল ৷  
শ্রীরাধা-মদনমোহনে প্রভু করি’ দিল ॥ ২১৬ ॥

nityānanda-dayā more tām̐re dekhāila  
Nityānanda’s  mercy  to me  Him  showed  
śrī-rādhā-madana-mohane prabhu kari’ dila  
Śrī Rādhā-Madanamohana  as Lord  making  gave

(216) Nityānanda’s mercy showed Him to me and gave me Śrī Rādhā and Madanamohana as my Lady and Lord.

মো-অধমে দিল শ্রীগোবিন্দ দরশন ৷  
কহিবার কথা নহে অকথ্য-কথন ॥ ২১৭ ॥

mo-adhame dila śrī-govinda daraśana  
to me  the lowly  gave  of Śrī Govinda  the sight  
kahibāra kathā nahe akathya-kathana  
to tell  a matter  is not  the indescribable  telling

(217) To me, so lowly, He gave the sight of Śrī Govinda. It is not a thing to be told; it is beyond all telling.

বৃন্দাবনে যোগপীঠে কল্পতরু-বনে ৷  
রত্নমণ্ডপ, তাহে রত্নসিংহাসনে ॥ ২১৮ ॥

শ্রীগোবিন্দ বসিয়াছেন ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ৷  
মাধুর্য্য প্রকাশি’ করেন জগৎ মোহন ॥ ২১৯ ॥

vṛndāvane yoga-pīṭhe kalpa-taru-vane  
in Vṛndāvana  at Yogapīṭha  of wish-fulfilling trees  in the grove  
ratna-maṇḍapa, tāhe ratna-siṁhāsane  
a jewelled  pavilion  there  on a jewelled  throne  
śrī-govinda vasiyāchena vrajendra-nandana  
Śrī Govinda  is seated  of the king of Vraja  the son  
mādhurya prakāśi’ karena jagat mohana  
sweetness  manifesting  makes  of the world  enchantment

(218–219) In Vṛndāvana, at Yogapīṭha, in a grove of wish-fulfilling trees, there is a pavilion of jewels, and there on a jewelled throne sits Śrī Govinda, the son of the king of Vraja. Revealing His sweetness, He enchants the whole world.

বাম-পার্শ্বে শ্রীরাধিকা সখীগণ-সঙ্গে ৷  
রাসাদিক-লীলা প্রভু করে কত রঙ্গে ॥ ২২০ ॥

vāma-pārśve śrī-rādhikā sakhī-gaṇa-saṅge  
on the left side  Śrī Rādhikā  with Her friends  together  
rāsādika-līlā prabhu kare kata raṅge  
the rāsa  and other  pastimes  the Lord  makes  with how many  joys

(220) On His left is Śrī Rādhikā with Her friends, and with them the Lord delights in the rāsa and so many other pastimes.

যাঁর ধ্যান নিজ-লোকে করে পদ্মাসন ৷  
অষ্টাদশাক্ষর-মন্ত্রে করে উপাসন ॥ ২২১ ॥

yām̐ra dhyāna nija-loke kare padmāsana  
on whom  meditation  in his own  world  makes  the lotus-seated  
aṣṭādaśākṣara-mantre kare upāsana  
with the eighteen-syllable  mantra  makes  worship

(221) In his own world Brahmā, who sits on the lotus, meditates on Him and worships Him with the eighteen-syllable mantra.[^48]

চৌদ্দভুবনে যাঁর সবে করে ধ্যান ৷  
বৈকুণ্ঠাদি-পুরে যাঁর লীলাগুণ গান ॥ ২২২ ॥

caudda-bhuvane yām̐ra sabe kare dhyāna  
in the fourteen  worlds  on whom  all  make  meditation  
vaikuṇṭhādi-pure yām̐ra līlā-guṇa gāna  
in Vaikuṇṭha and other  abodes  whose  pastimes  qualities  singing

(222) All the fourteen worlds meditate on Him, and in Vaikuṇṭha and the other abodes they sing of His pastimes and qualities.

যাঁর মাধুরীতে করে লক্ষ্মী আকর্ষণ ৷  
রূপগোসাঞি করিয়াছেন সে-রূপ বর্ণন ॥ ২২৩ ॥

yām̐ra mādhurīte kare lakṣmī ākarṣaṇa  
whose  by the sweetness  makes  Lakṣmī  attraction  
rūpa-gosāñi kariyāchena se-rūpa varṇana  
Rūpa  Gosāñi  has made  of that form  description

(223) His sweetness attracts even Lakṣmī. Rūpa Gosāñi has described that form:

স্মেরাং ভঙ্গীত্রয়পরিচিতাং সাচিবিস্তীর্ণদৃষ্টিং  
বংশীন্যস্তাধরকিশলয়ামুজ্জ্বলাং চন্দ্রকেণ ৷  
গোবিন্দাখ্যাং হরিতনুমিতঃ কেশিতীর্থোপকণ্ঠে  
মা প্রেক্ষিষ্ঠাস্তব যদি সখে বন্ধুসঙ্গেঽস্তি রঙ্গঃ ॥ ২২৪ ॥

smerāṁ bhaṅgī-traya-paricitāṁ sāci-vistīrṇa-dṛṣṭiṁ  
smiling  by three bends  marked  sidelong  wide  glance  
vaṁśī-nyastādhara-kiśalayām ujjvalāṁ candrakeṇa  
on the flute  placed  lips  tender as shoots  radiant  with a peacock feather  
govindākhyāṁ hari-tanum itaḥ keśi-tīrthopakaṇṭhe  
Govinda  named  Hari’s  form  here  Keśī-tīrtha  near  
mā prekṣiṣṭhās tava yadi sakhe bandhu-saṅge ’sti raṅgaḥ  
do not  look  of you  if  O friend  for relatives’ company  there is  fondness

(224) “O friend, if you still care for the company of your relatives, do not look at that form of Hari called Govinda, standing here by Keśī-tīrtha: He smiles, His body bends in three places, His wide glance falls sidelong, His lips, tender as new shoots, rest upon the flute, and a peacock feather makes Him radiant.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.239)[^49]

সাক্ষাৎ ব্রজেন্দ্রসুত ইথে নাহি আন ৷  
যেবা অজ্ঞে করে তাঁরে প্রতিমা-হেন জ্ঞান ॥ ২২৫ ॥

sākṣāt vrajendra-suta ithe nāhi āna  
directly  of the king of Vraja  the son  in this  there is no  other  
yebā ajñe kare tām̐re pratimā-hena jñāna  
whoever  the fool  makes  of Him  like an image  the notion

(225) He is the son of the king of Vraja Himself — of this there is no doubt. The fool who thinks of Him as a mere image[^50]

সেই অপরাধে তার নাহিক নিস্তার ৷  
ঘোর নরকেতে পড়ে, কি বলিব আর ॥ ২২৬ ॥

sei aparādhe tāra nāhika nistāra  
for that  offence  his  there is no  deliverance  
ghora narakete paḍe, ki baliba āra  
terrible  into hell  falls  what  shall I say  more

(226) has no deliverance for that offence; he falls into a terrible hell. What more can I say?

হেন যে গোবিন্দ প্রভু, পাইনু যাঁহা হৈতে ৷  
তাঁহার চরণ-কৃপা কে পারে বর্ণিতে ॥ ২২৭ ॥

hena ye govinda prabhu, pāinu yām̐hā haite  
such  that  Govinda  the Lord  I attained  from whom  
tām̐hāra caraṇa-kṛpā ke pāre varṇite  
His  feet’s  mercy  who  can  describe

(227) Through Him I attained such a Lord as Govinda — who can describe the mercy of His feet?

বৃন্দাবনে বৈসে যত বৈষ্ণব-মণ্ডল ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম-পরায়ণ, পরম-মঙ্গল ॥ ২২৮ ॥

vṛndāvane vaise yata vaiṣṇava-maṇḍala  
in Vṛndāvana  dwell  all  Vaiṣṇava  circles  
kṛṣṇa-nāma-parāyaṇa, parama-maṅgala  
to Kṛṣṇa’s name  devoted  supremely  auspicious

(228) All the circles of Vaiṣṇavas who dwell in Vṛndāvana are devoted to the name of Kṛṣṇa and are supremely auspicious.

যাঁর প্রাণধন — নিত্যানন্দ-শ্রীচৈতন্য ৷  
রাধাকৃষ্ণ-ভক্তি বিনে নাহি জানে অন্য ॥ ২২৯ ॥

yām̐ra prāṇa-dhana — nityānanda-śrī-caitanya  
whose  of life  the treasure  Nityānanda-Śrī Caitanya  
rādhā-kṛṣṇa-bhakti vine nāhi jāne anya  
to Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa  devotion  except  not  know  anything else

(229) Nityānanda and Śrī Caitanya are the treasure of their lives; they know nothing but devotion to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

সে বৈষ্ণবের পদরেণু, তার পদছায়া ৷  
অধমেরে দিল প্রভুনিত্যানন্দ-দয়া ॥ ২৩০ ॥

se vaiṣṇavera pada-reṇu, tāra pada-chāyā  
of those  Vaiṣṇavas  the dust of the feet  their  feet’s shade  
adhamere dila prabhu-nityānanda-dayā  
to the lowly one  gave  of Lord Nityānanda  the mercy

(230) The dust of those Vaiṣṇavas’ feet and the shelter of their shade — this the mercy of Lord Nityānanda gave to me, the lowly one.

‘তাঁহা সর্ব্ব লভ্য হয়’ — প্রভুর বচন ৷  
সেই সূত্র, এই তার কৈল বিবরণ ॥ ২৩১ ॥

‘tām̐hā sarva labhya haya’ — prabhura vacana  
there  all  attainable  is  the Lord’s  words  
sei sūtra, ei tāra kaila vivaraṇa  
that  the sūtra  this  of it  I have made  the explanation

(231) “There everything can be attained” — these words of the Lord are the sūtra, and this is my explanation of it.[^51]

সে সব পাইনু আমি বৃন্দাবনে আয় ৷  
সেই সব লভ্য এই প্রভুর কৃপায় ॥ ২৩২ ॥

se saba pāinu āmi vṛndāvane āya  
that  all  attained  I  to Vṛndāvana  coming  
sei saba labhya ei prabhura kṛpāya  
that  all  attained  by this  Lord’s  mercy

(232) All this I attained when I came to Vṛndāvana, and all of it I attained by the mercy of this Lord.

আপনার কথা লিখি নির্লজ্জ হইয়া ৷  
নিত্যানন্দগুণে লেখায় উন্মত্ত করিয়া ॥ ২৩৩ ॥

āpanāra kathā likhi nirlajja haiyā  
my own  story  I write  shameless  becoming  
nityānanda-guṇe lekhāya unmatta kariyā  
Nityānanda’s qualities  make me write  mad  making

(233) Shamelessly I write my own story: Nityānanda’s qualities have made me mad and make me write.

নিত্যানন্দ-প্রভুর গুণ-মহিমা অপার ৷  
‘সহস্রবদনে’ শেষ নাহি পায় যাঁর ॥ ২৩৪ ॥

nityānanda-prabhura guṇa-mahimā apāra  
of Nityānanda Prabhu’s  of qualities  the glory  boundless  
‘sahasra-vadane’ śeṣa nāhi pāya yām̐ra  
with a thousand mouths  Śeṣa (the end)  not  finds  of which

(234) The glory of Nityānanda Prabhu’s qualities is boundless: even Śeṣa with His thousand mouths cannot reach its end.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যাঁর আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ২৩৫ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yām̐ra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(235) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^2]: *Anubhāṣya*: Saṅkarṣaṇa is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa of the spiritual sky (Paravyoma); Kāraṇodakaśāyī is the original Puruṣa-avatāra; Garbhodakaśāyī is the second Puruṣa, Hiraṇyagarbha, the Viṣṇu of the whole universe (*samaṣṭi*); Kṣīrodakaśāyī is the third Puruṣa, the Viṣṇu of each being (*vyaṣṭi*); Śeṣa is Ananta. The edition ascribes the verse to the diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara. The same verse as Ādi 1.7.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: what is said in the seventh verse is explained in the eighth, ninth, tenth and eleventh verses of the invocation (Ādi 1.8–1.11), which the author now quotes and expounds (5.13, 5.50, 5.93, 5.109).

[^4]: The same verse as Ādi 1.8.

[^5]: The edition’s heading: “Brahman and the worlds above it, the abodes of Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in that realm are the all-pervading, endless abode of Brahman and the abodes of Vaikuṇṭha; in all of them dwell Kṛṣṇa Himself and all His avatāras.

[^6]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «নাহিক নিয়ম» (another reading: «নাহি তাঁর সীমা»).

[^7]: The edition’s heading: “To the eye of enjoyment it looks like the material world, but to the eye of devotion it is the spiritual land of cintāmaṇi, the field of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: even in the Vraja manifest on earth the land is cintāmaṇi and the forests are of wish-fulfilling trees.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lakṣmīs here are the gopīs, the beloved cowherd maidens of Vraja (*lakṣmyaḥ gopa-rāmāḥ*); *abhipālayantam* — “tending them in every way, as befits a cowherd”.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: in some Bengali manuscripts “Līlā-śakti” is read in place of “Nīlā”; these three potencies dwell in Vaikuṇṭha beside Nārāyaṇa.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is Nārada’s answer to Yudhiṣṭhira’s question how Śiśupāla, who hated Kṛṣṇa, attained *sāyujya*: by *kāma* — the gopīs; by enmity — Dantavakra and Śiśupāla; by fear — Kaṁsa; by affection — the Pāṇḍavas. *Tad agham* is the sin born of desire and the other feelings; *tad-gatim* — the various kinds of liberation. The gopīs’ *kāma* Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī calls prema: “The pure love of the gopīs came to be called *kāma*” (*Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*).

[^11]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: where the scriptures say that the Lord’s enemies and His beloved ones attain the same, they speak only of the oneness of Brahman, which is like the rays, and Kṛṣṇa, who is like the sun. In truth His beloved ones attain the variety of Vaikuṇṭha, and His enemies Siddhaloka, where there are no pastimes.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: *tamasaḥ pāre* — beyond the three guṇas; the siddhas are those perfected in the knowledge of featureless Brahman and in *kaivalya-yoga*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: there dwell the Māyāvādīs, immersed in the bliss of Brahman, and asuras such as Kaṁsa, slain by the Lord; the yogīs of Patañjali’s school who attain *kaivalya* also reach that world.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: the text reads *aṁśa* (“part”), the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* *aṅga*; both readings mean the same. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa is spiritual *viśuddha-sattva*; He is the *aṅga*, that is, the *prakāśa*, of Nityānanda-Rāma.

[^14]: The same verse as Ādi 1.9.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya* and *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the external potency has two parts. The efficient cause (*nimitta*), the guṇas, is called māyā; the material cause (*upādāna*) is *pradhāna*, which is also called *prakṛti* (the first is spoken of in 5.62).

[^16]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Nārāyaṇa is the principal efficient cause of the world, as the potter is of the pot; māyā is a secondary cause, like the potter’s wheel and stick (5.63–5.64).

[^17]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *aṅgābhāsa* is only a semblance of bodily contact, not a real union with māyā.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: the lords of the universes are the *samaṣṭi*-Viṣṇus and others; *jīvanti* — “they are manifest, they abide”.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is one of the prayers Brahmā offered to Kṛṣṇa after stealing the calves and cowherd boys, to atone for his offence; *tamaḥ* is unmanifest prakṛti.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pratimūrti* — “second body” (see 5.4–5.5).

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: *ete* — “the avatāras named before”; *aṁśāḥ* — portions of the Puruṣa-avatāra, *kalāḥ* — portions of portions; *mṛḍayanti*, “make happy”, refers to these avatāras. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “Rāma, Nṛsiṁha and others are portions or parts of the Puruṣa; They protect the world oppressed by demons.”

[^22]: The same verse as Ādi 2.67.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: Brahmā is telling Nārada of the splendour of Kāraṇārṇavaśāyī: *kāla* — that which sets the guṇas in motion; *svabhāva* — their transformation; *sad-asat* — prakṛti as cause and effect; *manaḥ* — the mahat-tattva; *dravya* — the subtle and gross elements; *vikāra* — false ego; *virāṭ* — the collective body of the universe; *svarāṭ* — the Vairāja; *sthāsnu*, *cariṣṇu* — unmoving and moving bodies; all this is His splendour (*vibhūti*).

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*: Bhagavān here is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa; “the mahat and the rest” are the mahat, false ego, the five great elements, the eleven senses and the five *tanmātras*; the sixteen *kalās* are the fullness of power for creation — in the *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, Śrī, Bhū, Kīrti, Ilā, Līlā, Kānti, Vidyā and nine potencies headed by Vimalā.

[^25]: The same verse as Ādi 2.55.

[^26]: The same verse as Ādi 1.10. *Anubhāṣya*: Garbhodakaśāyī is the second Puruṣa; the stem of the lotus is the support of the fourteen worlds; the lotus is the birthplace of Brahmā.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Bhāgavatam* (1.3.4) and the Puruṣa-sūkta (“The Puruṣa has a thousand heads, a thousand eyes, a thousand feet”): the thousand-headed one is Garbhodakaśāyī Himself, not Ananta on whom He lies. The edition’s heading to 5.101: “He who is praised in the Puruṣa-sūkta of the Ṛg-veda.”

[^28]: The same verse as Ādi 1.11. *Anubhāṣya*: Ananta, the upholder of the earth, is a *kalā* of Kṣīrodakaśāyī, a part of His part.

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Siddhānta-śiromaṇi*): the seven oceans are of salt water, milk, curd, ghee, sugar-cane juice, wine and fresh water; south of the salt ocean is the Ocean of Milk, where Vāsudeva dwells.

[^30]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *śeṣatā* — “the last, utmost limit of service”. *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Bhāgavatam* (10.2.8): “My abode called Śeṣa.”

[^31]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: as Kṛṣṇa was once equated with Vāmana and others, so those who make no distinction call Nityānanda “Ananta”; since devotees have said so, it is not false: in the highest truth everything is possible.

[^32]: The edition’s heading: “Baladeva’s wonder at the sight of Kṛṣṇa’s yogamāyā.” *Anubhāṣya*: when Brahmā had stolen the calves and the cowherd boys, Kṛṣṇa Himself became them and went on with His pastimes; one day Baladeva, seeing the unusual love of the cows, understood this and was amazed.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Kauravas spoke insultingly of Kṛṣṇa and tried to win Baladeva to their side; angered, Baladeva answered them with these words. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “Brahmā, Śiva, I, Baladeva, and Lakṣmī — some of us are His parts, some parts of His parts.”

[^34]: *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 3.71 (Nityānanda and Advaita are the two *aṅgas* of Śrī Caitanya).

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu honoured Advaita Ācārya as one of His elders, but Advaita regarded Himself as Śrī Caitanya’s servant. He was a contemporary and friend of Mahāprabhu’s father; Śrī Īśvara Purī and Śrī Advaita were both disciples of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, and since Mahāprabhu accepted Īśvara Purī as His initiating guru, Advaita was a godbrother of His guru and worthy of respect.

[^36]: *Anubhāṣya*: among the *daṇḍī* sannyāsīs of the ten orders (*daśanāmī*), a brahmacārī receives one of four names — *svarūpa*, *ānanda*, *prakāśa* or *caitanya*. During His pilgrimage Nityānanda Prabhu stayed with a sannyāsī whose title was *tīrtha* or *āśrama*, and so His brahmacārī name was “Nityānanda-svarūpa”.

[^37]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*): Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa, Bharata and Śatrughna are avatāras of Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha; the *Padma-purāṇa* calls Rāma Nārāyaṇa, and Lakṣmaṇa, Bharata and Śatrughna Śeṣa, the Disc and the Conch.

[^38]: *Anubhāṣya*: *kalā-niyamena* — “as part and part of a part”; He abides in the forms of Rāma and others, manifesting them as avatāras for particular purposes (*naimittika*), but He Himself appeared as Kṛṣṇa.

[^39]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads «অধম জীবেরে চড়াইল ঊর্দ্ধ্ব সীমা» (“He raised a fallen soul to the highest limit”), with the heading “The greatness of Nityānanda’s mercy shown by the author’s own story.” Other editions read «যৈছে চড়াইল» (“how He raised…”).

[^40]: *Anubhāṣya*: *avadhūta* — Śrīdhara Svāmī (on *Bhāgavatam*, 3.1.19) explains the word as “one whose body bears no mark of ritual purification”: Nityānanda, eternally perfect and beyond varṇāśrama, bore none of its signs and was absorbed in the mood of Vraja. On Mīnaketana Rāmadāsa see Ādi 11.53.

[^41]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «বসিয়া» (“sitting”; another reading: «আসিয়া», “having come”). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Guṇārṇava Miśra, the servant of the Deity, did not greet Nityānanda’s servant who was sitting in the courtyard.

[^42]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* and *Anubhāṣya* (*Bhāgavatam*, 10.78.22–28): in Naimiṣāraṇya Romaharṣaṇa, a disciple of Vyāsa, did not rise to greet Baladeva and was slain by Him; Guṇārṇava Miśra, who had no special regard for Nityānanda, acted just as improperly. The devotees do not blame Mīnaketana for this anger.

[^43]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “the logic of half a hen” is to take one half of a hen as old and the other as young; so too, by accepting one of the undivided Caitanya and Nityānanda and rejecting the other, you show both unbelief and hypocrisy.

[^44]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Jhāmaṭapura is a village near Naihāṭi, two krośas north of Kāṭoyā; Kavirāja Gosvāmī lived there, and a Deity of Mahāprabhu is now worshipped there.

[^45]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «দন্তে» (“in the teeth”; another reading: «দন্ত»).

[^46]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *hātasāna* — “a touch of the hand”.

[^47]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *bhakti-rasa-prānta* — “only nearness to bhakti-rasa”. *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Sanātana is the ācārya of the conclusions of bhakti, Śrī Rūpa the ācārya of bhakti-rasa (cf. Antya 4).

[^48]: *Anubhāṣya*: Brahmā, seated on the lotus, together with the inhabitants of Brahmaloka meditates on Govinda, the Deity worshipped with the eighteen-syllable mantra, on whom the dwellers of all fourteen worlds meditate.

[^49]: *Anubhāṣya*: in the guise of a prohibition the verse says that this form, the abode of supreme beauty, must certainly be seen: once its sweetness is tasted, all else seems worthless. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: having seen Śrī Govinda, one loses the taste for everything else.

[^50]: The edition’s heading: “To see the transcendental Deity as earthly stone, wood or metal is a great offence.” *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Bhakti-sandarbha* (286): true worshippers see the Deity as the Supreme Lord Himself, for the idea of a difference destroys bhakti; and the *Padma-purāṇa*: one who sees stone in the Deity of Viṣṇu is called bound for hell.

[^51]: *Anubhāṣya*: Nitāi’s merciful command (5.195) is the main sūtra by which the *Caritāmṛta* was written in Vṛndāvana, the land of the Vaiṣṇavas.
