“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 5  
**Determining the Truth of Śrī Nityānanda**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দেঽনন্তাদ্ভুতৈশ্বর্য্যং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দমীশ্বরম্ ৷  
যস্যেচ্ছয়া তৎস্বরূপমজ্ঞেনাপি নিরূপ্যতে ॥ ১ ॥

vande ’nantādbhutaiśvaryaṁ  
I bow  of endless  wondrous  opulence (possessed)  
śrī-nityānandam īśvaram  
Śrī Nityānanda  the Lord  
yasyecchayā tat-svarūpam  
by whose  will  His  true nature  
ajñenāpi nirūpyate  
by an ignorant one  even  is described

(1) I bow to the Lord Śrī Nityānanda, whose divine opulence is endless and wondrous. By His will even a fool can come to know and describe His true nature.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এই ষট্শ্লোকে কহিল কৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-মহিমা ৷  
পঞ্চশ্লোকে কহি নিত্যানন্দতত্ত্ব-সীমা ॥ ৩ ॥

ei ṣaṭ-śloke kahila kṛṣṇa-caitanya-mahimā  
in these  six  verses  I have told  of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the glory  
pañca-śloke kahi nityānanda-tattva-sīmā  
in five  verses  I tell  of the truth of Nityānanda  the full extent

(3) In these six verses I have told the glory of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya; in five verses I now tell the whole truth of Nityānanda.

সর্ব্ব-অবতারী কৃষ্ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তাঁহার দ্বিতীয় দেহ শ্রীবলরাম ॥ ৪ ॥

sarva-avatārī kṛṣṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
of all  avatāras the source  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān  
tām̐hāra dvitīya deha śrī-balarāma  
His  second  body  Śrī Balarāma

(4) Kṛṣṇa, the source of all avatāras, is Bhagavān Himself, and Śrī Balarāma is His second body.

একই স্বরূপ দোঁহে, ভিন্নমাত্র কায় ৷  
আদ্য কায়ব্যূহ, কৃষ্ণলীলার সহায় ॥ ৫ ॥

eka-i svarūpa dom̐he, bhinna-mātra kāya  
one and the same  nature  of the two  different only  the bodies  
ādya kāya-vyūha, kṛṣṇa-līlāra sahāya  
the first  bodily expansion  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  the helper

(5) The two are one in nature; only Their bodies differ. Balarāma is the first expansion of Kṛṣṇa’s body and His helper in His pastimes.

সেই কৃষ্ণ — নবদ্বীপে শ্রীচৈতন্যচন্দ্র ৷  
সেই বলরাম — সঙ্গে শ্রীনিত্যানন্দ ॥ ৬ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa — navadvīpe śrī-caitanya-candra  
that  Kṛṣṇa  in Navadvīpa  Śrī Caitanyacandra  
sei balarāma — saṅge śrī-nityānanda  
that  Balarāma  with Him  Śrī Nityānanda

(6) That Kṛṣṇa is Śrī Caitanyacandra in Navadvīpa, and that Balarāma is Śrī Nityānanda, who is with Him.

সঙ্কর্ষণঃ কারণতোয়শায়ী  
গর্ভোদশায়ী চ পয়োঽব্ধিশায়ী ৷  
শেষশ্চ যস্যাংশকলাঃ স নিত্যা-  
নন্দাখ্যরামঃ শরণং মমাস্তু ॥ ৭ ॥

saṅkarṣaṇaḥ kāraṇa-toya-śāyī  
Saṅkarṣaṇa  Kāraṇodakaśāyī  
garbhoda-śāyī ca payo ’bdhi-śāyī  
Garbhodakaśāyī  and  Kṣīrodakaśāyī  
śeṣaś ca yasyāṁśa-kalāḥ sa nityā-  
and Śeṣa  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-  
nandākhya-rāmaḥ śaraṇaṁ mamāstu  
-nanda named  Rāma  refuge  my  may be

(7) “Saṅkarṣaṇa, the Lord who lies in the Causal Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Garbha Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Ocean of Milk, and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions: may that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my refuge.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^2]

শ্রীবলরাম গোসাঞি মূল-সঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
পঞ্চরূপ ধরি’ করেন কৃষ্ণের সেবন ॥ ৮ ॥

śrī-balarāma gosāñi mūla-saṅkarṣaṇa  
Śrī Balarāma  Gosāñi  the original  Saṅkarṣaṇa  
pañca-rūpa dhari’ karena kṛṣṇera sevana  
five  forms  assuming  He performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  service

(8) Śrī Balarāma Gosāñi is the original Saṅkarṣaṇa. Assuming five forms, He serves Kṛṣṇa.

আপনে করেন কৃষ্ণলীলার সহায় ৷  
সৃষ্টিলীলা-কার্য্য করে ধরি’ চারি কায় ॥ ৯ ॥

āpane karena kṛṣṇa-līlāra sahāya  
Himself  He performs  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  assistance  
sṛṣṭi-līlā-kārya kare dhari’ cāri kāya  
of the pastime of creation  the work  He does  assuming  four  bodies

(9) He Himself assists Kṛṣṇa in His pastimes, and in four other bodies He does the work of the pastime of creation.

সৃষ্ট্যাদিক সেবা, তাঁর আজ্ঞার পালন ৷  
‘শেষ’-রূপে করে কৃষ্ণের বিবিধ সেবন ॥ ১০ ॥

sṛṣṭy-ādika sevā, tām̐ra ājñāra pālana  
creation  and the rest  service  His  order’s  carrying out  
‘śeṣa’-rūpe kare kṛṣṇera vividha sevana  
Śeṣa  in the form of  He performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  manifold  service

(10) His service of creation and the rest is the carrying out of Kṛṣṇa’s order; and as Śeṣa He serves Kṛṣṇa in many ways.

সর্ব্বরূপে আস্বাদয়ে কৃষ্ণ-সেবানন্দ ৷  
সেই বলরাম — গৌরসঙ্গে নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ১১ ॥

sarva-rūpe āsvādaye kṛṣṇa-sevānanda  
in all  forms  He tastes  of Kṛṣṇa’s service  the bliss  
sei balarāma — gaura-saṅge nityānanda  
that  Balarāma  Gaura’s companion  Nityānanda

(11) In all these forms He tastes the bliss of serving Kṛṣṇa. And that same Balarāma is Nityānanda, the companion of Gaura.

সপ্তম শ্লোকের অর্থ করি চারিশ্লোক ৷  
যাতে নিত্যানন্দতত্ত্ব জানে সর্ব্বলোক ॥ ১২ ॥

saptama ślokera artha kari cāriśloka  
of the seventh  verse  the meaning  I make  in four verses  
yāte nityānanda-tattva jāne sarvaloka  
by which  of Nityānanda  the truth  know  all people

(12) The meaning of the seventh verse I shall explain in four verses, so that all people may know the truth of Nityānanda.[^3]

মায়াতীতে ব্যাপিবৈকুণ্ঠলোকে  
পূর্ণৈশ্বর্য্যে শ্রীচতুর্ব্যূহমধ্যে ৷  
রূপং যস্যোদ্ভাতি সঙ্কর্ষণাখ্যং  
তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১৩ ॥

māyātīte vyāpi-vaikuṇṭha-loke  
beyond māyā  in all-pervading  Vaikuṇṭha  world  
pūrṇaiśvarye śrī-catur-vyūha-madhye  
full of opulence  amid the fourfold expansion  
rūpaṁ yasyodbhāti saṅkarṣaṇākhyaṁ  
form  whose  shines forth  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(13) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma, whose form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines amid the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha) in the all-pervading world of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence and beyond māyā.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^4]

প্রকৃতির পার ‘পরব্যোম’-নামে ধাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণবিগ্রহ যৈছে বিভূত্যাদি-গুণবান্ ॥ ১৪ ॥

prakṛtira pāra ‘paravyoma’-nāme dhāma  
material nature’s  beyond  Paravyoma  named  an abode  
kṛṣṇa-vigraha yaiche vibhūty-ādi-guṇavān  
Kṛṣṇa’s  form  just as  with opulence  and other  qualities endowed

(14) Beyond material nature lies the abode called Paravyoma. Like Kṛṣṇa’s own form, it is endowed with every opulence and every divine quality.

সর্ব্বগ, অনন্ত ব্রহ্ম-বৈকুণ্ঠাদি ধাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণ-অবতারের তাহাঞি বিশ্রাম ॥ ১৫ ॥

sarvaga, ananta brahma-vaikuṇṭhādi dhāma  
all-pervading  endless  Brahman  Vaikuṇṭha  and others  abodes  
kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-avatārera tāhāñi viśrāma  
of Kṛṣṇa  of Kṛṣṇa’s avatāras  there  the dwelling

(15) There lie endless, all-pervading abodes — Brahman, Vaikuṇṭha and others — and in them Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras dwell.[^5]

তাহার উপরিভাগে ‘কৃষ্ণলোক’ খ্যাতি ৷  
দ্বারকা-মথুরা-গোকুল — ত্রিবিধত্বে স্থিতি ॥ ১৬ ॥

tāhāra upari-bhāge ‘kṛṣṇa-loka’ khyāti  
its  in the upper  part  Kṛṣṇaloka  renowned  
dvārakā-mathurā-gokula — tri-vidhatve sthiti  
Dvārakā  Mathurā  Gokula  in three divisions  it abides

(16) In its highest region is the realm known as Kṛṣṇaloka, which abides in three divisions — Dvārakā, Mathurā and Gokula.

সর্ব্বোপরি শ্রীগোকুল — ব্রজলোকধাম ৷  
শ্রীগোলোক, শ্বেতদ্বীপ, বৃন্দাবন-নাম ॥ ১৭ ॥

sarvopari śrī-gokula — vrajaloka-dhāma  
above all  Śrī Gokula  the abode of the world of Vraja  
śrī-goloka, śvetadvīpa, vṛndāvana-nāma  
Śrī Goloka  Śvetadvīpa  Vṛndāvana  named

(17) Above all is Śrī Gokula, the abode of Vraja, which is also called Śrī Goloka, Śvetadvīpa and Vṛndāvana.

সর্ব্বগ, অনন্ত, বিভু, কৃষ্ণতনুসম ৷  
উপর্য্যধো ব্যাপিয়াছে, নাহিক নিয়ম ॥ ১৮ ॥

sarvaga, ananta, vibhu, kṛṣṇa-tanu-sama  
all-pervading  endless  vast  like Kṛṣṇa’s body  
upary-adho vyāpiyāche, nāhika niyama  
above and below  it pervades  there is no  limit

(18) Like Kṛṣṇa’s body, it is all-pervading, endless and vast; it extends above and below without any bound.[^6]

ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে প্রকাশ তাঁর কৃষ্ণের ইচ্ছায় ৷  
একই স্বরূপ তাঁর, নাহি দুই কায় ॥ ১৯ ॥

brahmāṇḍe prakāśa tām̐ra kṛṣṇera icchāya  
in the universe  manifestation  its  by Kṛṣṇa’s  will  
eka-i svarūpa tām̐ra, nāhi dui kāya  
one and the same  nature  its  there are not  two  bodies

(19) By Kṛṣṇa’s will it is manifest within the universe, yet it remains one and the same: it has not two bodies.

চিন্তামণি-ভূমি, কল্পবৃক্ষময় বন ৷  
চর্ম্মচক্ষে দেখে তাঁরে প্রপঞ্চের সম ॥ ২০ ॥

cintāmaṇi-bhūmi, kalpa-vṛkṣa-maya vana  
of cintāmaṇi  the land  of wish-fulfilling trees full  forests  
carma-cakṣe dekhe tām̐re prapañcera sama  
with eyes of flesh  one sees  it  the material world  like

(20) Its land is cintāmaṇi, its forests are full of wish-fulfilling trees; yet eyes of flesh see it as no different from the material world.[^7]

প্রেমনেত্রে দেখে তাঁর স্বরূপ-প্রকাশ ৷  
গোপ-গোপীসঙ্গে যাঁহা কৃষ্ণের বিলাস ॥ ২১ ॥

prema-netre dekhe tām̐ra svarūpa-prakāśa  
with eyes of love  one sees  its  true form’s  manifestation  
gopa-gopī-saṅge yām̐hā kṛṣṇera vilāsa  
with the cowherd boys  and girls  together  where  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes

(21) With eyes of love one sees it as it truly is — the place where Kṛṣṇa sports with the cowherd boys and girls.

চিন্তামণিপ্রকরসদ্মসু কল্পবৃক্ষ-  
লক্ষাবৃতেষু সুরভীরভিপালয়ন্তম্ ৷  
লক্ষ্মীসহস্রশতসম্ভ্রমসেব্যমানং  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ২২ ॥

cintāmaṇi-prakara-sadmasu kalpa-vṛkṣa-  
of cintāmaṇi gems  of clusters  in abodes  wish-fulfilling trees  
lakṣāvṛteṣu surabhīr abhipālayantam  
by millions  surrounded  the surabhi cows  tending  
lakṣmī-sahasra-śata-sambhrama-sevyamānaṁ  
by Lakṣmīs  thousands  hundreds  with reverence  being served  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(22) “I worship Govinda, the original Person, who tends the surabhi cows in abodes built of cintāmaṇi gems and surrounded by millions of wish-fulfilling trees, and who is served with reverence by hundreds of thousands of Lakṣmīs.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.29)[^8]

মথুরা-দ্বারকায় নিজরূপ প্রকাশিয়া ৷  
নানা-রূপে বিলসয়ে চতুর্ব্যূহ হৈঞা ॥ ২৩ ॥

mathurā-dvārakāya nija-rūpa prakāśiyā  
in Mathurā  Dvārakā  His own  form  manifesting  
nānā-rūpe vilasaye catur-vyūha haiñā  
in many  forms  He sports  the catur-vyūha  becoming

(23) In Mathurā and Dvārakā He manifests His own form and, becoming the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha), sports in many forms.

বাসুদেব-সঙ্কর্ষণ-প্রদ্যুম্নানিরুদ্ধ ৷  
সর্ব্বচতুর্ব্যূহ-অংশী, তুরীয়, বিশুদ্ধ ॥ ২৪ ॥

vāsudeva-saṅkarṣaṇa-pradyumnāniruddha  
Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha  
sarva-catur-vyūha-aṁśī, turīya, viśuddha  
of all  catur-vyūhas  the source  transcendent  pure

(24) Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha are the source of every catur-vyūha; They are transcendent (*turīya*) and pure.

এই তিন লোকে কৃষ্ণ কেবল-লীলাময় ৷  
নিজগণ লঞা খেলে অনন্ত সময় ॥ ২৫ ॥

ei tina loke kṛṣṇa kevala-līlā-maya  
in these  three  abodes  Kṛṣṇa  wholly  of pastimes full  
nija-gaṇa lañā khele ananta samaya  
His own  associates  taking  He plays  endless  time

(25) In these three abodes Kṛṣṇa is wholly given to His pastimes; with His own associates He plays there for endless time.

পরব্যোম-মধ্যে করি’ স্বরূপপ্রকাশ ৷  
নারায়ণরূপে করেন বিবিধ বিলাস ॥ ২৬ ॥

para-vyoma-madhye kari’ svarūpa prakāśa  
of Paravyoma  in the midst  making  of His own form  manifestation  
nārāyaṇa-rūpe karena vividha vilāsa  
of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  He performs  various  pastimes

(26) In the midst of Paravyoma He manifests His own form and, as Nārāyaṇa, enjoys pastimes of many kinds.

স্বরূপবিগ্রহ কৃষ্ণের কেবল দ্বিভুজ ৷  
নারায়ণরূপে সেই তনু চতুর্ভুজ ॥ ২৭ ॥

svarūpa-vigraha kṛṣṇera kevala dvi-bhuja  
own  form  Kṛṣṇa’s  only  two-armed  
nārāyaṇa-rūpe sei tanu catur-bhuja  
of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  that  body  four-armed

(27) Kṛṣṇa’s own form has only two arms; in the form of Nārāyaṇa that same body has four.

শঙ্খ-চক্র-গদা-পদ্ম, মহৈশ্বর্য্যময় ৷  
শ্রী-ভূ-নীলা-শক্তি যাঁর চরণ সেবয় ॥ ২৮ ॥

śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-padma, mahaiśvarya-maya  
conch  disc  club  lotus  of great majesty full  
śrī-bhū-nīlā-śakti yām̐ra caraṇa sevaya  
Śrī  Bhū  Nīlā  potencies  whose  feet  serve

(28) He holds the conch, disc, club and lotus and is full of supreme majesty; His feet are served by His potencies Śrī, Bhū and Nīlā.[^9]

যদ্যপি কেবল তাঁর ক্রীড়ামাত্র ধর্ম্ম ৷  
তথাপি জীবেরে কৃপায় করে এক কর্ম্ম ॥ ২৯ ॥

yadyapi kevala tām̐ra krīḍā-mātra dharma  
although  only  His  play  merely  nature  
tathāpi jīvere kṛpāya kare eka karma  
still  to the jīvas  out of mercy  He does  one  work

(29) Though His nature is simply to play, yet out of mercy to the jīvas He does one work.

সালোক্য-সামীপ্য-সার্ষ্টি-সারূপ্য-প্রকার ৷  
চারি মুক্তি দিয়া করে জীবেরে নিস্তার ॥ ৩০ ॥

sālokya-sāmīpya-sārṣṭi-sārūpya-prakāra  
sālokya  sāmīpya  sārṣṭi  sārūpya  kinds  
cāri mukti diyā kare jīvere nistāra  
four  liberations  giving  He makes  of the jīvas  deliverance

(30) Granting the four kinds of liberation — sālokya, sāmīpya, sārṣṭi and sārūpya — He delivers the jīvas.

ব্রহ্মসাযুজ্য-মুক্তের তাঁহা নাহি গতি ৷  
বৈকুণ্ঠ-বাহিরে হয় তা-সবার স্থিতি ॥ ৩১ ॥

brahma-sāyujya-muktera tām̐hā nāhi gati  
in Brahman  by merging  of the liberated  there  there is no  access  
vaikuṇṭha-bāhire haya tā-sabāra sthiti  
Vaikuṇṭha  outside  is  of them all  the dwelling

(31) Those liberated by merging into Brahman have no access there; they all dwell outside Vaikuṇṭha.

বৈকুণ্ঠ-বাহিরে এক জ্যোতির্ম্ময় মণ্ডল ৷  
কৃষ্ণের অঙ্গের প্রভা, পরম উজ্জ্বল ॥ ৩২ ॥

vaikuṇṭha-bāhire eka jyotir-maya maṇḍala  
Vaikuṇṭha  outside  one  luminous  sphere  
kṛṣṇera aṅgera prabhā, parama ujjvala  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  body  the radiance  supremely  bright

(32) Outside Vaikuṇṭha lies a sphere of light — the radiance of Kṛṣṇa’s body, supremely bright.

‘সিদ্ধলোক’ নাম তার প্রকৃতির পার ৷  
চিৎস্বরূপ, তাঁহা নাহি চিচ্ছক্তি-বিকার ॥ ৩৩ ॥

‘siddha-loka’ nāma tāra prakṛtira pāra  
Siddhaloka  name  its  material nature  beyond  
cit-svarūpa, tām̐hā nāhi cic-chakti-vikāra  
spiritual  in nature  there  there are not  of the spiritual potency  transformations

(33) It is called Siddhaloka and lies beyond material nature. It is spiritual, yet the spiritual potency displays no variety there.

সূর্য্যমণ্ডল যেন বাহিরে নির্ব্বিশেষ ৷  
ভিতরে সূর্য্যের রথ-আদি সবিশেষ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

sūrya-maṇḍala yena bāhire nirviśeṣa  
the sun’s  disc  as  outside  without variety  
bhitare sūryera ratha-ādi saviśeṣa  
within  the sun’s  chariot  and the rest  full of variety

(34) It is like the disc of the sun: from outside it is a featureless glow, but within are the sun’s chariot and all its variety.

কামাদ্দ্বেষাৎ ভয়াৎ স্নেহাৎ যথা ভক্ত্যেশ্বরে মনঃ ৷  
আবেশ্য তদঘং হিত্বা বহবস্তদ্গতিং গতাঃ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kāmād dveṣāt bhayāt snehāt  
from desire  from enmity  from fear  from affection  
yathā bhaktyeśvare manaḥ  
as  by devotion  on the Lord  the mind  
āveśya tad aghaṁ hitvā  
fixing  that  sin  casting off  
bahavas tad gatiṁ gatāḥ  
many  His  destination  have attained

(35) “Many have fixed their minds on the Lord — some by devotion, others by desire (*kāma*), enmity, fear or affection — and, casting off the sin in those feelings, have attained Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.1.29)[^10]

যদরীণাং প্রিয়াণাঞ্চ প্রাপ্যমেকমিবোদিতম্ ৷  
তদ্ব্রহ্মকৃষ্ণয়োরৈক্যাৎ কিরণার্কোপমা-জুষোঃ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

yad arīṇāṁ priyāṇāṁ ca  
which  of the enemies  of the beloved  and  
prāpyam ekam ivoditam  
the attainment  one  as if  is spoken of  
tad brahma-kṛṣṇayor aikyāt  
that  of Brahman and Kṛṣṇa  from the oneness  
kiraṇārkopamā-juṣoḥ  
like the rays and the sun  of those two

(36) “That the goal of the Lord’s enemies and of His beloved ones is spoken of as if it were one is because Brahman and Kṛṣṇa are one, as the rays are one with the sun.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.278)[^11]

তৈছে পরব্যোমে নানা চিচ্ছক্তিবিলাস ৷  
নির্ব্বিশেষ জ্যোতির্বিম্ব বাহিরে প্রকাশ ॥ ৩৭ ॥

taiche para-vyome nānā cic-chakti-vilāsa  
so  in Paravyoma  manifold  of the spiritual potency  pastimes  
nirviśeṣa jyotir-bimba bāhire prakāśa  
featureless  of light  a sphere  outside  is manifest

(37) So in Paravyoma there are manifold pastimes of the spiritual potency, while outside it a featureless sphere of light is manifest.

নির্ব্বিশেষ-ব্রহ্ম সেই কেবল জ্যোতির্ম্ময় ৷  
সায়ুজ্যের অধিকারী তাঁহা পায় লয় ॥ ৩৮ ॥

nirviśeṣa-brahma sei kevala jyotir-maya  
featureless  Brahman  that  only  luminous  
sāyujyera adhikārī tām̐hā pāya laya  
for sāyujya  the qualified  there  attain  absorption

(38) That featureless Brahman is light alone, and those who are fit for *sāyujya* are absorbed in it.

সিদ্ধলোকস্তু তমসঃ পারে যত্র বসন্তি হি ৷  
সিদ্ধা ব্রহ্মসুখে মগ্না দৈত্যাশ্চ হরিণা হতাঃ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

siddha-lokas tu tamasaḥ  
Siddhaloka  but  darkness  
pāre yatra vasanti hi  
beyond  where  dwell  indeed  
siddhā brahma-sukhe magnā  
the siddhas  in the bliss of Brahman  immersed  
daityāś ca hariṇā hatāḥ  
the demons  and  by Hari  slain

(39) “Beyond darkness lies Siddhaloka, where dwell the siddhas, immersed in the bliss of Brahman, and the demons slain by Hari.” (Brahmāṇḍa-purāṇa; Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.280)[^12]

সেই পরব্যোমে নারায়ণের চারি পাশে ৷  
দ্বারকায় চতুর্ব্যূহ দ্বিতীয় প্রকাশে ॥ ৪০ ॥

sei para-vyome nārāyaṇera cāri pāśe  
in that  Paravyoma  of Nārāyaṇa  on four  sides  
dvārakāya catur-vyūha dvitīya prakāśe  
of Dvārakā  the catur-vyūha  in a second  manifestation

(40) In that Paravyoma, on the four sides of Nārāyaṇa, the catur-vyūha of Dvārakā is present in a second manifestation.

বাসুদেব-সঙ্কর্ষণ-প্রদ্যুম্নানিরুদ্ধ ৷  
‘দ্বিতীয় চতুর্ব্যূহ’ এই — তুরীয়, বিশুদ্ধ ॥ ৪১ ॥

vāsudeva-saṅkarṣaṇa-pradyumnāniruddha  
Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha  
‘dvitīya catur-vyūha’ ei — turīya, viśuddha  
the second  catur-vyūha  this  transcendent  pure

(41) Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha — this is the second catur-vyūha, transcendent and pure.

তাঁহা যে রামের রূপ — মহাসঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
চিচ্ছক্তি-আশ্রয় তিঁহো, কারণের কারণ ॥ ৪২ ॥

tām̐hā ye rāmera rūpa — mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa  
there  which  of Rāma  form  Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  
cic-chakti-āśraya tim̐ho, kāraṇera kāraṇa  
of the spiritual potency  the shelter  He  of causes  the cause

(42) There the form of Rāma is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa. He is the shelter of the spiritual potency, the cause of all causes.

চিচ্ছক্তিবিলাস এক — ‘শুদ্ধসত্ত্ব’-নাম ৷  
শুদ্ধসত্ত্বময় যত বৈকুণ্ঠাদি-ধাম ॥ ৪৩ ॥

cic-chakti-vilāsa eka — ‘śuddha-sattva’-nāma  
of the spiritual potency  a manifestation  one  śuddha-sattva  named  
śuddha-sattva-maya yata vaikuṇṭhādi-dhāma  
of śuddha-sattva made  all  Vaikuṇṭha and other  abodes

(43) One manifestation of the spiritual potency is called *śuddha-sattva*, pure spiritual existence, and of it all the abodes of Vaikuṇṭha are made.

ষড়্বিধৈশ্বর্য্য তাঁহা সকল চিন্ময় ৷  
সঙ্কর্ষণের বিভূতি সব, জানিহ নিশ্চয় ॥ ৪৪ ॥

ṣaḍ-vidhaiśvarya tām̐hā sakala cinmaya  
of six kinds  opulence  there  all  spiritual  
saṅkarṣaṇera vibhūti saba, jāniha niścaya  
of Saṅkarṣaṇa  the splendour  all  know  for certain

(44) The six kinds of opulence there are all spiritual; know for certain that all of them are the splendour of Saṅkarṣaṇa.

‘জীব’ নাম তটস্থাখ্য এক শক্তি হয় ৷  
মহাসঙ্কর্ষণ — সব জীবের আশ্রয় ॥ ৪৫ ॥

‘jīva’ nāma taṭasthākhya eka śakti haya  
jīva  named  marginal called  one  potency  there is  
mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa — saba jīvera āśraya  
Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  of all  jīvas  the shelter

(45) There is a potency called *jīva*, known as the marginal (*taṭasthā*) potency; and Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of all jīvas.

যাঁহা হৈতে বিশ্বোৎপত্তি, যাঁহাতে প্রলয় ৷  
সেই পুরুষের সঙ্কর্ষণ সমাশ্রয় ॥ ৪৬ ॥

yām̐hā haite viśvotpatti, yām̐hāte pralaya  
from whom  of the universe  the origin  in whom  the dissolution  
sei puruṣera saṅkarṣaṇa samāśraya  
of that  Puruṣa  Saṅkarṣaṇa  the shelter

(46) Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of the Puruṣa from whom the universe arises and into whom it dissolves.

সর্ব্বাশ্রয়, সর্ব্বাদ্ভুত, ঐশ্বর্য্য অপার ৷  
‘অনন্ত’ কহিতে নারে মহিমা যাঁহার ॥ ৪৭ ॥

sarvāśraya, sarvādbhuta, aiśvarya apāra  
of all  the shelter  all-wondrous  opulence  boundless  
‘ananta’ kahite nāre mahimā yām̐hāra  
Ananta  to describe  cannot  the glory  whose

(47) He is the shelter of all, wondrous in every way, His opulence boundless; even Ananta cannot describe His glory.

তুরীয়, বিশুদ্ধসত্ত্ব, ‘সঙ্কর্ষণ’ নাম ৷  
তিঁহো যাঁর অংশ, সেই নিত্যানন্দ-রাম ॥ ৪৮ ॥

turīya, viśuddha-sattva, ‘saṅkarṣaṇa’ nāma  
transcendent  pure sattva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named  
tim̐ho yām̐ra aṁśa, sei nityānanda-rāma  
He  whose  part  that  Nityānanda-Rāma

(48) The transcendent Saṅkarṣaṇa, pure spiritual existence (*viśuddha-sattva*), is a part of Nityānanda-Rāma.[^13]

অষ্টম শ্লোকের কৈল সংক্ষেপ বিবরণ ৷  
নবম শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ৪৯ ॥

aṣṭama ślokera kaila saṅkṣepa vivaraṇa  
of the eighth  verse  I gave  a brief  explanation  
navama ślokera artha śuna diyā mana  
of the ninth  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  the mind

(49) Thus I have briefly explained the eighth verse. Now hear attentively the meaning of the ninth.

মায়াভর্ত্তাজাণ্ডসঙ্ঘাশ্রয়াঙ্গঃ  
শেতে সাক্ষাৎ কারণাম্ভোধিমধ্যে ৷  
যস্যৈকাংশঃ শ্রীপুমানাদিদেব-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৫০ ॥

māyā-bhartājāṇḍa-saṅghāśrayāṅgaḥ  
māyā’s  master  of universes  multitudes  shelter  whose body  
śete sākṣāt kāraṇāmbhodhi-madhye  
lies  in person  of the Causal  Ocean  in the midst  
yasyaikāṁśaḥ śrī-pumān ādi-devas  
whose  one part  the Puruṣa  the original  Lord  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(50) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of Him is the original Lord, the Puruṣa, the master of māyā, who lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean, His body the shelter of countless universes.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^14]

বৈকুণ্ঠ-বাহিরে যেই জ্যোতির্ময় ধাম ৷  
তাহার বাহিরে ‘কারণার্ণব’ নাম ॥ ৫১ ॥

vaikuṇṭha-bāhire yei jyotir-maya dhāma  
Vaikuṇṭha  outside  which  luminous  abode  
tāhāra bāhire ‘kāraṇārṇava’ nāma  
of it  outside  Kāraṇārṇava  named

(51) Outside Vaikuṇṭha is the luminous abode, and beyond it lies what is called the Kāraṇārṇava, the Causal Ocean.

বৈকুণ্ঠ বেড়িয়া এক আছে জলনিধি ৷  
অনন্ত, অপার — তার নাহিক অবধি ॥ ৫২ ॥

vaikuṇṭha beḍiyā eka āche jala-nidhi  
Vaikuṇṭha  encircling  one  there is  an ocean  
ananta, apāra — tāra nāhika avadhi  
endless  shoreless  its  there is no  limit

(52) Encircling Vaikuṇṭha is an ocean, endless and shoreless; it has no bound.

বৈকুণ্ঠের পৃথিব্যাদি সকল চিন্ময় ৷  
মায়িক ভূতের তথি জন্ম নাহি হয় ॥ ৫৩ ॥

vaikuṇṭhera pṛthivy-ādi sakala cinmaya  
of Vaikuṇṭha  earth  and the rest  all  spiritual  
māyika bhūtera tathi janma nāhi haya  
material  of the elements  there  birth  not  is

(53) In Vaikuṇṭha earth and all the elements are spiritual; material elements never arise there.

চিন্ময়-জল সেই পরম-কারণ ৷  
যার এক কণা গঙ্গা পতিতপাবন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

cinmaya-jala sei parama-kāraṇa  
spiritual  water  that  the supreme  cause  
yāra eka kaṇā gaṅgā patita-pāvana  
of which  one  drop  the Gaṅgā  of the fallen  the purifier

(54) That spiritual water is the supreme cause; a single drop of it is the Gaṅgā, purifier of the fallen.

সেই ত’ কারণার্ণবে সেই সঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
আপনার এক অংশে করেন শয়ন ॥ ৫৫ ॥

sei ta’ kāraṇārṇave sei saṅkarṣaṇa  
in that  very  Causal Ocean  that  Saṅkarṣaṇa  
āpanāra eka aṁśe karena śayana  
His own  in one  part  He makes  lying down

(55) In that Causal Ocean the same Saṅkarṣaṇa lies in one part of Himself.

মহৎস্রষ্টা পুরুষ, তিঁহো জগৎ-কারণ ৷  
আদ্য-অবতার করে মায়ার দরশন ॥ ৫৬ ॥

mahat-sraṣṭā puruṣa, tim̐ho jagat-kāraṇa  
of the mahat  the creator  Puruṣa  He  of the world  the cause  
ādya-avatāra kare māyāra daraśana  
the first  avatāra  makes  of māyā  the glance

(56) He is the Puruṣa, creator of the *mahat-tattva*, the cause of the world. This first avatāra casts His glance upon māyā.

মায়াশক্তি রহে কারণাব্ধির বাহিরে ৷  
কারণ-সমুদ্র মায়া পরশিতে নারে ॥ ৫৭ ॥

māyā-śakti rahe kāraṇābdhira bāhire  
māyā  potency  remains  the Causal Ocean  outside  
kāraṇa-samudra māyā paraśite nāre  
the Causal  Ocean  māyā  to touch  cannot

(57) The potency māyā stays outside the Causal Ocean; māyā cannot touch its waters.

সেই ত’ মায়ার দুই-বিধ অবস্থিতি ৷  
জগতের উপাদান ‘প্রধান’, ‘প্রকৃতি’ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

sei ta’ māyāra dui-vidha avasthiti  
of that  very  māyā  twofold  state  
jagatera upādāna ‘pradhāna’, ‘prakṛti’  
of the world  the material cause  pradhāna  prakṛti

(58) That māyā exists in two ways; as the material cause of the world it is called *pradhāna* or *prakṛti*.[^15]

জগৎকারণ নহে প্রকৃতি জড়রূপা ৷  
শক্তি সঞ্চারিয়া তারে কৃষ্ণ করে কৃপা ॥ ৫৯ ॥

jagat-kāraṇa nahe prakṛti jaḍa-rūpā  
of the world  the cause  is not  prakṛti  inert  in nature  
śakti sañcāriyā tāre kṛṣṇa kare kṛpā  
potency  infusing  into her  Kṛṣṇa  shows  mercy

(59) Inert prakṛti is not the cause of the world; Kṛṣṇa shows her mercy by infusing her with His potency.

কৃষ্ণশক্ত্যে প্রকৃতি হয় গৌণ কারণ ৷  
অগ্নিশক্ত্যে লৌহ যৈছে করয়ে জারণ ॥ ৬০ ॥

kṛṣṇa-śaktye prakṛti haya gauṇa kāraṇa  
by Kṛṣṇa’s  potency  prakṛti  becomes  the secondary  cause  
agni-śaktye lauha yaiche karaye jāraṇa  
by fire’s  power  iron  as  does  burning

(60) By Kṛṣṇa’s potency prakṛti becomes a secondary cause, just as iron burns by the power of fire.

অতএব কৃষ্ণ মূল-জগৎকারণ ৷  
প্রকৃতি — কারণ, যৈছে অজাগলস্তন ॥ ৬১ ॥

ataeva kṛṣṇa mūla-jagat-kāraṇa  
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  the original  of the world  cause  
prakṛti — kāraṇa, yaiche ajā-gala-stana  
prakṛti  the cause  as  of a goat  on the neck  the teats

(61) Therefore Kṛṣṇa is the original cause of the world. To call prakṛti the cause is like taking the teats on a goat’s neck for an udder.

মায়া-অংশে কহি তারে নিমিত্ত-কারণ ৷  
সেহ নহে, যাতে কর্ত্তা-হেতু — নারায়ণ ॥ ৬২ ॥

māyā-aṁśe kahi tāre nimitta-kāraṇa  
in the māyā  aspect  I call  her  the efficient  cause  
seha nahe, yāte kartā-hetu — nārāyaṇa  
even that  is not so  because  the agent  cause  Nārāyaṇa

(62) In her aspect as māyā I call her the efficient cause of the world; but even that is not so, for the true maker and cause is Nārāyaṇa.[^16]

ঘটের নিমিত্ত-হেতু যৈছে কুম্ভকার ৷  
তৈছে জগতের কর্ত্তা — পুরুষাবতার ॥ ৬৩ ॥

ghaṭera nimitta-hetu yaiche kumbhakāra  
of the pot  efficient  cause  as  the potter  
taiche jagatera kartā — puruṣāvatāra  
so  of the world  the maker  the Puruṣa-avatāra

(63) As the potter is the efficient cause of the pot, so the Puruṣa-avatāra is the maker of the world.

কৃষ্ণ — কর্ত্তা, মায়া তাঁর করেন সহায় ৷  
ঘটের কারণ — চক্র-দণ্ডাদি উপায় ॥ ৬৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa — kartā, māyā tām̐ra karena sahāya  
Kṛṣṇa  the maker  māyā  to Him  gives  help  
ghaṭera kāraṇa — cakra-daṇḍādi upāya  
of the pot  cause  wheel  stick  and other  instruments

(64) Kṛṣṇa is the maker, and māyā only helps Him, as the wheel, the stick and other tools are causes of the pot.

দূর হৈতে পুরুষ করে মায়াতে অবধান ৷  
জীবরূপ বীর্য্য তাতে করেন আধান ॥ ৬৫ ॥

dūra haite puruṣa kare māyāte avadhāna  
from afar  the Puruṣa  makes  on māyā  a glance  
jīva-rūpa vīrya tāte karena ādhāna  
in the form of jīvas  the seed  into her  He makes  placing

(65) From afar the Puruṣa glances upon māyā and places in her the seed — the jīvas.

এক অঙ্গাভাসে করে মায়াতে মিলন ৷  
মায়া হৈতে জন্মে তবে ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের গণ ॥ ৬৬ ॥

eka aṅgābhāse kare māyāte milana  
by one  semblance of His body  makes  with māyā  union  
māyā haite janme tabe brahmāṇḍera gaṇa  
from māyā  are born  then  of universes  the multitudes

(66) By a mere semblance of His body He unites with māyā, and from māyā the hosts of universes are then born.[^17]

অগণ্য, অনন্ত যত অণ্ড-সন্নিবেশ ৷  
ততরূপে পুরুষ করে সবাতে প্রবেশ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

agaṇya, ananta yata aṇḍa-sanniveśa  
countless  endless  as many  universes’  arrangements  
tata-rūpe puruṣa kare sabāte praveśa  
in so many  forms  the Puruṣa  makes  into all  entrance

(67) Into all those countless, endless universes the Puruṣa enters, taking as many forms.

পুরুষ-নাসাতে যবে বাহিরায় শ্বাস ৷  
নিশ্বাস সহিতে হয় ব্রহ্মাণ্ড-প্রকাশ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

puruṣa-nāsāte yabe bāhirāya śvāsa  
the Puruṣa’s  from the nostrils  when  goes out  the breath  
niśvāsa sahite haya brahmāṇḍa-prakāśa  
the exhalation  together with  becomes  of universes  the manifestation

(68) When the breath goes out of the Puruṣa’s nostrils, the universes become manifest with His exhalation.

পুনরপি শ্বাস যবে প্রবেশে অন্তরে ৷  
শ্বাস-সহ ব্রহ্মাণ্ড পৈশে পুরুষ-শরীরে ॥ ৬৯ ॥

punarapi śvāsa yabe praveśe antare  
again  the breath  when  enters  within  
śvāsa-saha brahmāṇḍa paiśe puruṣa-śarīre  
with the breath  the universes  enter  of the Puruṣa  into the body

(69) And when the breath enters again, the universes enter the Puruṣa’s body with it.

গবাক্ষের রন্ধ্রে যেন ত্রসরেণু চলে ৷  
পুরুষের লোমকূপে ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের জালে ॥ ৭০ ॥

gavākṣera randhre yena trasareṇu cale  
of a window  through the holes  as  specks of dust  move  
puruṣera loma-kūpe brahmāṇḍera jāle  
of the Puruṣa  in the pores  of universes  the multitudes

(70) As specks of dust move through the holes of a lattice window, so hosts of universes move through the pores of the Puruṣa’s skin.

যস্যৈকনিশ্বসিতকালমথাবলম্ব্য  
জীবন্তি লোমবিলজা জগদণ্ডনাথাঃ ৷  
বিষ্ণুর্মহান্ স ইহ যস্য কলাবিশেষো  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ৭১ ॥

yasyaika-niśvasita-kālam athāvalambya  
whose  one  exhalation’s  span  then  depending on  
jīvanti loma-vila-jā jagad-aṇḍa-nāthāḥ  
live  from the pores  born  of the universes  the lords  
viṣṇur mahān sa iha yasya kalā-viśeṣo  
Viṣṇu  the Great  He  here  whose  kalā  particular  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(71) “The lords of the universes, born from His pores, abide for the span of one of His breaths. That Mahā-Viṣṇu is a particular kalā of Govinda, the original Person, whom I worship.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.48)[^18]

ক্বাহং তমো-মহদহং-খ-চরাগ্নিবার্ভূ-  
সংবেষ্টিতাণ্ডঘট-সপ্তবিতস্তিকায়ঃ ৷  
ক্বেদৃগ্বিধাঽবিগণিতাণ্ডপরাণুচর্য্যা-  
বাতাধ্বরোমবিবরস্য চ তে মহিত্বম্ ॥ ৭২ ॥

kvāhaṁ tamo-mahad-ahaṁ-kha-carāgni-vār-bhū-  
where  I  by darkness  the mahat  false ego  ether  air  fire  water  earth  
saṁveṣṭitāṇḍa-ghaṭa-sapta-vitasti-kāyaḥ  
enclosed  universe  in the pot  seven  spans  of body  
kvedṛg-vidhā ’vigaṇitāṇḍa-parāṇu-caryā-  
where  such  countless  universes  like atoms  moving  
vātādhva-roma-vivarasya ca te mahitvam  
through the windows  the pores of the skin  and  Your  greatness

(72) “Where am I, a creature with a body seven spans tall, enclosed in the pot of a universe wrapped in prakṛti, the mahat, false ego, ether, air, fire, water and earth? And where is Your greatness, when countless such universes drift like atoms through the pores of Your skin, as through the openings of a lattice?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.11)[^19]

অংশের অংশ যেই, ‘কলা’ তার নাম ৷  
গোবিন্দের প্রতিমূর্ত্তি শ্রীবলরাম ॥ ৭৩ ॥

aṁśera aṁśa yei, ‘kalā’ tāra nāma  
of a part  the part  which  kalā  its  name  
govindera pratimūrti śrī-balarāma  
of Govinda  the second body  Śrī Balarāma

(73) A part of a part is called a *kalā*. Śrī Balarāma is Govinda’s second body.[^20]

তাঁর এক স্বরূপ — শ্রীমহাসঙ্কর্ষণ ৷  
তাঁর অংশ ‘পুরুষ’ হয় কলাতে গণন ॥ ৭৪ ॥

tām̐ra eka svarūpa — śrī-mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa  
His  one  form  Śrī Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  
tām̐ra aṁśa ‘puruṣa’ haya kalāte gaṇana  
His  part  the Puruṣa  is  among the kalās  counted

(74) One of His forms is Śrī Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa, and Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa’s part, the Puruṣa, is counted as a *kalā*.

যাঁহাকে ত’ কলা কহি, তিঁহো মহাবিষ্ণু ৷  
মহাপুরুষাবতারী, সেহো সর্ব্বজিষ্ণু ॥ ৭৫ ॥

yām̐hāke ta’ kalā kahi, tim̐ho mahā-viṣṇu  
whom  indeed  kalā  I call  He  Mahā-Viṣṇu  
mahā-puruṣāvatārī, seho sarva-jiṣṇu  
of the Mahā-Puruṣa avatāras the source  He  all-conquering

(75) He whom I call a kalā is Mahā-Viṣṇu. He is the source of the Puruṣa-avatāras, ever victorious.

গর্ভোদ-ক্ষীরোদ-শায়ী দোঁহে ‘পুরুষ’ নাম ৷  
সেই দুই, যাঁর অংশ, — বিষ্ণু, বিশ্বধাম ॥ ৭৬ ॥

garbhoda-kṣīroda-śāyī dom̐he ‘puruṣa’ nāma  
in the Garbha  Kṣīra  lying  both  Puruṣa  named  
sei dui, yām̐ra aṁśa, — viṣṇu, viśvadhāma  
those  two  whose  parts  Viṣṇu  abode of the universes

(76) The Lords who lie in the Garbha and Kṣīra oceans are both called Puruṣas, and He whose parts They are is Viṣṇu, the abode of all the universes.

বিষ্ণোস্তু ত্রীণি রূপাণি পুরুষাখ্যান্যথো বিদুঃ ৷  
একন্তুমহতঃ স্রষ্টৃ দ্বিতীয়ং ত্বণ্ডসংস্থিতম্ ৷  
তৃতীয়ং সর্ব্বভূতস্থং তানি জ্ঞাত্বা বিমুচ্যতে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

viṣṇos tu trīṇi rūpāṇi  
of Viṣṇu  but  three  forms  
puruṣākhyāny atho viduḥ  
called Puruṣas  then  they know  
ekaṁ tu mahataḥ sraṣṭṛ  
one  but  of the mahat  the creator  
dvitīyaṁ tv aṇḍa-saṁsthitam  
the second  but  in the universe  abiding  
tṛtīyaṁ sarva-bhūta-sthaṁ  
the third  in all  beings  abiding  
tāni jñātvā vimucyate  
them  knowing  one is liberated

(77) “Viṣṇu has three forms called Puruṣas: the first creates the mahat, the second abides within the universe, and the third dwells in all beings. Knowing Them, one is set free.” (Sātvata-tantra; Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, Pūrva-khaṇḍa, 2.9)

যদ্যপি কহিয়ে তাঁরে কৃষ্ণের ‘কলা’ করি ৷  
মৎস্যকূর্ম্মাদ্যবতারের তিঁহো অবতারী ॥ ৭৮ ॥

yadyapi kahiye tām̐re kṛṣṇera ‘kalā’ kari  
although  I call  Him  Kṛṣṇa’s  kalā  making  
matsya-kūrmādy-avatārera tim̐ho avatārī  
of Matsya  Kūrma  and other  avatāras  He  the source

(78) Though I call Him a kalā of Kṛṣṇa, He is the source of Matsya, Kūrma and the other avatāras.

এতে চাংশকলাঃ পুংসঃ কৃষ্ণস্তু ভগবান্ স্বয়ম্ ৷  
ইন্দ্রারিব্যাকুলং লোকং মৃড়য়ন্তি যুগে যুগে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

ete cāṁśa-kalāḥ puṁsaḥ  
these  and  portions  and parts  of the Puruṣa  
kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam  
Kṛṣṇa  but  Bhagavān  Himself  
indrāri-vyākulaṁ lokaṁ  
by the enemies of Indra  troubled  the world  
mṛḍayanti yuge yuge  
make happy  in age  after age

(79) “All these (avatāras) are portions (*aṁśa*) and parts of portions (*kalā*) of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself. Age after age They bring happiness to the world when it is troubled by the enemies of Indra.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.28)[^21][^22]

সেই পুরুষ সৃষ্টি-স্থিতি-প্রলয়ের কর্ত্তা ৷  
নানা অবতার করে, জগতের ভর্ত্তা ॥ ৮০ ॥

sei puruṣa sṛṣṭi-sthiti-pralayera kartā  
that  Puruṣa  of creation  maintenance  dissolution  the doer  
nānā avatāra kare, jagatera bhartā  
many  avatāras  makes  of the world  the sustainer

(80) That Puruṣa creates, maintains and dissolves the world; He sends forth many avatāras and sustains the world.

সৃষ্ট্যাদি-নিমিত্তে যেই অংশের অবধান ৷  
সেই ত’ অংশেরে কহি ‘অবতার’ নাম ॥ ৮১ ॥

sṛṣṭy-ādi-nimitte yei aṁśera avadhāna  
creation  and the rest  for the sake of  which  part’s  descent  
sei ta’ aṁśere kahi ‘avatāra’ nāma  
that  very  part  I call  avatāra  by name

(81) The part that descends for creation and the rest I call by the name *avatāra*.

আদ্যাবতার, মহাপুরুষ, ভগবান্ ৷  
সর্ব্ব-অবতার-বীজ, সর্ব্বাশ্রয়-ধাম ॥ ৮২ ॥

ādyāvatāra, mahā-puruṣa, bhagavān  
the first avatāra  the Mahā-Puruṣa  Bhagavān  
sarva-avatāra-bīja, sarvāśraya-dhāma  
of all  avatāras  the seed  of all  the shelter  the abode

(82) He is the first avatāra, the Mahā-Puruṣa, Bhagavān — the seed of all avatāras, the abode and shelter of all.

আদ্যোঽবতারঃ পুরুষঃ পরস্য  
কালঃ স্বভাবঃ সদসন্মনশ্চ ৷  
দ্রব্যং বিকারো গুণ ইন্দ্রিয়াণি  
বিরাট্ স্বরাট্ স্থাস্নু চরিষ্ণু ভূম্নঃ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

ādyo ’vatāraḥ puruṣaḥ parasya  
the first  avatāra  the Puruṣa  of the Supreme  
kālaḥ svabhāvaḥ sad-asan manaś ca  
time  nature  cause and effect  the mind  and  
dravyaṁ vikāro guṇa indriyāṇi  
the elements  false ego  the guṇas  the senses  
virāṭ svarāṭ sthāsnu cariṣṇu bhūmnaḥ  
the universal body  the self-luminous  the unmoving  the moving  of the Great One

(83) “The Puruṣa is the first avatāra of the Supreme. Time, nature, prakṛti as cause and effect, the mind, the elements, false ego, the guṇas, the senses, the universal body (*virāṭ*) and the Vairāja (*svarāṭ*), and all beings, moving and unmoving — all these are the splendour of the Great One.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.6.42)[^23]

জগৃহে পৌরুষং রূপং ভগবান্মহদাদিভিঃ ৷  
সম্ভূতং ষোড়শকলমাদৌ লোকসিসৃক্ষয়া ॥ ৮৪ ॥

jagṛhe pauruṣaṁ rūpaṁ  
accepted  of the Puruṣa  form  
bhagavān mahad-ādibhiḥ  
Bhagavān  with the mahat  and the rest  
sambhūtaṁ ṣoḍaśa-kalam  
composed  of sixteen  parts  
ādau loka-sisṛkṣayā  
in the beginning  the worlds  desiring to create

(84) “In the beginning, desiring to create the worlds, Bhagavān accepted the form of the Puruṣa, joined with the mahat and the other principles and possessed of sixteen potencies (*kalā*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.1)[^24]

যদ্যপি সর্ব্বাশ্রয় তিঁহো, তাঁহাতে সংসার ৷  
অন্তরাত্মা-রূপে তিঁহো জগৎ-আধার ॥ ৮৫ ॥

yadyapi sarvāśraya tim̐ho, tām̐hāte saṁsāra  
although  of all the shelter  He  in Him  the world  
antarātmā-rūpe tim̐ho jagat-ādhāra  
the Supersoul  in the form of  He  of the world  the support

(85) He is the shelter of all, and the world rests in Him; as the Supersoul He is the support of the world.

প্রকৃতি-সহিতে তাঁর উভয় সম্বন্ধ ৷  
তথাপি প্রকৃতি-সহ নাহি স্পর্শগন্ধ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

prakṛti-sahite tām̐ra ubhaya sambandha  
with prakṛti  His  twofold  relation  
tathāpi prakṛti-saha nāhi sparśa-gandha  
yet  with prakṛti  there is not  of touch  a scent

(86) He has a twofold relation with prakṛti, and yet there is not even a trace of contact between Him and prakṛti.

এতদীশনমীশস্য প্রকৃতিস্থোঽপি তদ্গুণৈঃ ৷  
ন যুজ্যতে সদাত্মস্থৈর্যথা বুদ্ধিস্তদাশ্রয়া ॥ ৮৭ ॥

etad īśanam īśasya  
this  the lordship  of the Lord  
prakṛti-stho ’pi tad-guṇaiḥ  
in material nature  abiding  though  by its qualities  
na yujyate sadātma-sthair  
not  is affected  ever  abiding in Him  
yathā buddhis tad-āśrayā  
as  the intelligence  sheltered in Him

(87) “This is the lordship of the Lord: though He abides within material nature, He is never affected by its qualities — just as the intelligence of those who have taken shelter of Him is not affected.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.11.38)[^25]

এইমত গীতাতেহো পুনঃ পুনঃ কয় ৷  
সর্ব্বদা ঈশ্বর-তত্ত্ব অচিন্ত্যশক্তি হয় ॥ ৮৮ ॥

ei mata gītāteho punaḥ punaḥ kaya  
in this way  in the Gītā also  again  again  it says  
sarvadā īśvara-tattva acintya-śakti haya  
always  of the Lord  the truth  of inconceivable potency  is

(88) So the Gītā too declares again and again that the truth of the Lord is always endowed with inconceivable potency:

আমি ত’ জগতে বসি, জগৎ আমাতে ৷  
না আমি জগতে বসি, না আমা জগতে ॥ ৮৯ ॥

āmi ta’ jagate vasi, jagat āmāte  
I  indeed  in the world  abide  the world  in Me  
nā āmi jagate vasi, nā āmā jagate  
not  I  in the world  abide  not  in Me  the world

(89) “I abide in the world, and the world is in Me; and yet I do not abide in the world, nor is the world in Me.

অচিন্ত্য ঐশ্বর্য্য এই জানিহ আমার ৷  
এই ত’ গীতার অর্থ কৈল পরচার ॥ ৯০ ॥

acintya aiśvarya ei jāniha āmāra  
inconceivable  opulence  this  know  My  
ei ta’ gītāra artha kaila paracāra  
this  indeed  of the Gītā  the meaning  is made  proclaimed

(90) Know this to be My inconceivable power.” Such is the meaning the Gītā proclaims.

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: *ajñena* — “by one who does not know the scriptures”, that is, by the author himself; *nirūpyate* — “is described”: by Nityānanda’s mercy even such a person can describe the truth about Him.

[^2]: *Anubhāṣya*: Saṅkarṣaṇa is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa of the spiritual sky (Paravyoma); Kāraṇodakaśāyī is the original Puruṣa-avatāra; Garbhodakaśāyī is the second Puruṣa, Hiraṇyagarbha, the Viṣṇu of the whole universe (*samaṣṭi*); Kṣīrodakaśāyī is the third Puruṣa, the Viṣṇu of each being (*vyaṣṭi*); Śeṣa is Ananta. The edition ascribes the verse to the diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara. The same verse as Ādi 1.7.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: what is said in the seventh verse is explained in the eighth, ninth, tenth and eleventh verses of the invocation (Ādi 1.8–1.11), which the author now quotes and expounds (5.13, 5.50, 5.93, 5.109).

[^4]: The same verse as Ādi 1.8.

[^5]: The edition’s heading: “Brahman and the worlds above it, the abodes of Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in that realm are the all-pervading, endless abode of Brahman and the abodes of Vaikuṇṭha; in all of them dwell Kṛṣṇa Himself and all His avatāras.

[^6]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «নাহিক নিয়ম» (another reading: «নাহি তাঁর সীমা»).

[^7]: The edition’s heading: “To the eye of enjoyment it looks like the material world, but to the eye of devotion it is the spiritual land of cintāmaṇi, the field of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: even in the Vraja manifest on earth the land is cintāmaṇi and the forests are of wish-fulfilling trees.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lakṣmīs here are the gopīs, the beloved cowherd maidens of Vraja (*lakṣmyaḥ gopa-rāmāḥ*); *abhipālayantam* — “tending them in every way, as befits a cowherd”.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: in some Bengali manuscripts “Līlā-śakti” is read in place of “Nīlā”; these three potencies dwell in Vaikuṇṭha beside Nārāyaṇa.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is Nārada’s answer to Yudhiṣṭhira’s question how Śiśupāla, who hated Kṛṣṇa, attained *sāyujya*: by *kāma* — the gopīs; by enmity — Dantavakra and Śiśupāla; by fear — Kaṁsa; by affection — the Pāṇḍavas. *Tad agham* is the sin born of desire and the other feelings; *tad-gatim* — the various kinds of liberation. The gopīs’ *kāma* Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī calls prema: “The pure love of the gopīs came to be called *kāma*” (*Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*).

[^11]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: where the scriptures say that the Lord’s enemies and His beloved ones attain the same, they speak only of the oneness of Brahman, which is like the rays, and Kṛṣṇa, who is like the sun. In truth His beloved ones attain the variety of Vaikuṇṭha, and His enemies Siddhaloka, where there are no pastimes.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: *tamasaḥ pāre* — beyond the three guṇas; the siddhas are those perfected in the knowledge of featureless Brahman and in *kaivalya-yoga*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: there dwell the Māyāvādīs, immersed in the bliss of Brahman, and asuras such as Kaṁsa, slain by the Lord; the yogīs of Patañjali’s school who attain *kaivalya* also reach that world.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: the text reads *aṁśa* (“part”), the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* *aṅga*; both readings mean the same. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa is spiritual *viśuddha-sattva*; He is the *aṅga*, that is, the *prakāśa*, of Nityānanda-Rāma.

[^14]: The same verse as Ādi 1.9.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya* and *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the external potency has two parts. The efficient cause (*nimitta*), the guṇas, is called māyā; the material cause (*upādāna*) is *pradhāna*, which is also called *prakṛti* (the first is spoken of in 5.62).

[^16]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Nārāyaṇa is the principal efficient cause of the world, as the potter is of the pot; māyā is a secondary cause, like the potter’s wheel and stick (5.63–5.64).

[^17]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *aṅgābhāsa* is only a semblance of bodily contact, not a real union with māyā.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: the lords of the universes are the *samaṣṭi*-Viṣṇus and others; *jīvanti* — “they are manifest, they abide”.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is one of the prayers Brahmā offered to Kṛṣṇa after stealing the calves and cowherd boys, to atone for his offence; *tamaḥ* is unmanifest prakṛti.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pratimūrti* — “second body” (see 5.4–5.5).

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: *ete* — “the avatāras named before”; *aṁśāḥ* — portions of the Puruṣa-avatāra, *kalāḥ* — portions of portions; *mṛḍayanti*, “make happy”, refers to these avatāras. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “Rāma, Nṛsiṁha and others are portions or parts of the Puruṣa; They protect the world oppressed by demons.”

[^22]: The same verse as Ādi 2.67.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: Brahmā is telling Nārada of the splendour of Kāraṇārṇavaśāyī: *kāla* — that which sets the guṇas in motion; *svabhāva* — their transformation; *sad-asat* — prakṛti as cause and effect; *manaḥ* — the mahat-tattva; *dravya* — the subtle and gross elements; *vikāra* — false ego; *virāṭ* — the collective body of the universe; *svarāṭ* — the Vairāja; *sthāsnu*, *cariṣṇu* — unmoving and moving bodies; all this is His splendour (*vibhūti*).

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*: Bhagavān here is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa; “the mahat and the rest” are the mahat, false ego, the five great elements, the eleven senses and the five *tanmātras*; the sixteen *kalās* are the fullness of power for creation — in the *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, Śrī, Bhū, Kīrti, Ilā, Līlā, Kānti, Vidyā and nine potencies headed by Vimalā.

[^25]: The same verse as Ādi 2.55.
