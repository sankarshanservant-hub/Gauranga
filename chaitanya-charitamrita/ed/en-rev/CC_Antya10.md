“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 10  
**Relishing the Devotees’ Offerings**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যং ভক্তানুগ্রহকারকম্ ৷  
যেন কেনাপি সন্তুষ্টং ভক্তদত্তেন শ্রদ্ধয়া ॥ ১ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanyaṁ  
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
bhaktānugraha-kārakam  
who bestows grace on His devotees  
yena kenāpi santuṣṭaṁ  
with whatever  even  pleased  
bhakta-dattena śraddhayā  
given by a devotee  with faith

(1) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who bestows grace on His devotees and is pleased with whatever a devotee offers Him with faith.[^1]

জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

বর্ষান্তরে সব ভক্ত প্রভুরে দেখিতে ৷  
পরম-আনন্দে সবে নীলাচল যাইতে ॥ ৩ ॥

varṣāntare saba bhakta prabhure dekhite  
in the next year  all  the devotees  the Lord  to see  
parama-ānande sabe nīlācala yāite  
in great joy  all  to Nīlācala  to go

(3) The next year all the devotees set out in great joy for Nīlācala to see the Lord.

অদ্বৈতআচার্য্য-গোসাঞি-সর্ব্ব-অগ্রগণ্য ৷  
আচার্য্যরত্ন, আচার্য্যনিধি, শ্রীবাস আদি ধন্য ॥ ৪ ॥

advaitācārya-gosāñi-sarva-agra-gaṇya  
Advaitācārya Gosāñi  of all  the foremost  
ācāryaratna, ācāryanidhi, śrīvāsa ādi dhanya  
Ācāryaratna  Ācāryanidhi  Śrīvāsa  and others  blessed

(4) Foremost of all was Advaitācārya Gosāñi; with him came Ācāryaratna, Ācāryanidhi, Śrīvāsa and other blessed devotees.[^3]

যদ্যপি প্রভুর আজ্ঞা গৌড়ে রহিতে ৷  
তথাপি নিত্যানন্দ প্রেমে চলিলা দেখিতে ॥ ৫ ॥

yadyapi prabhura ājñā gauḍe rahite  
although  the Lord’s  order  in Gauḍa  to remain  
tathāpi nityānanda preme calilā dekhite  
still  Nityānanda  in love  set out  to see (Him)

(5) Although the Lord had ordered Him to remain in Gauḍa, Nityānanda, out of love, set out to see Him.

অনুরাগের লক্ষণ এই, — ‘বিধি’ নাহি মানে ৷  
তাঁর আজ্ঞা ভাঙ্গে তাঁর সঙ্গের কারণে ॥ ৬ ॥

anurāgera lakṣaṇa ei, — ‘vidhi’ nāhi māne  
of anurāga  the sign  this  rules  not  heeds  
tām̐ra ājñā bhāṅge tām̐ra saṅgera kāraṇe  
His  order  breaks  His  company  for the sake of

(6) This is the sign of *anurāga* (deep, spontaneous love): it does not heed rules (*vidhi*). For the sake of His company it breaks His order.

রাসে যৈছে ঘর যাইতে গোপীরে আজ্ঞা দিলা ৷  
তাঁর আজ্ঞা ভাঙ্গি’ তাঁর সঙ্গে সে রহিলা ॥ ৭ ॥

rāse yaiche ghara yāite gopīre ājñā dilā  
in the rāsa  as  home  to go  to the gopīs  an order  He gave  
tām̐ra ājñā bhāṅgi’ tām̐ra saṅge se rahilā  
His  order  breaking  with Him  they  remained

(7) So it was in the *rāsa* dance: He told the gopīs to go home, but they broke His order and stayed with Him.

আজ্ঞা-পালনে কৃষ্ণের যৈছে পরিতোষ ৷  
প্রেমে আজ্ঞা ভাঙ্গিলে হয় কোটি-সুখ-পোষ ॥ ৮ ॥

ājñā-pālane kṛṣṇera yaiche paritoṣa  
in the keeping of His order  Kṛṣṇa’s  whatever  satisfaction  
preme ājñā bhāṅgile haya koṭi-sukha-poṣa  
in love  the order  if broken  there is  a millionfold nourishment of joy

(8) Kṛṣṇa is pleased when His order is kept; but when it is broken out of love, His joy is nourished a millionfold.

বাসুদেব-দত্ত, মুরারি-গুপ্ত, গঙ্গাদাস ৷  
শ্রীমান্-সেন, শ্রীমান্-পণ্ডিত, অকিঞ্চন কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৯ ॥

মুরারি, গরুড়-পণ্ডিত, বুদ্ধিমন্ত-খাঁন ৷  
সঞ্জয়-পুরুষোত্তম, পণ্ডিত-ভগবান্ ॥ ১০ ॥

শুক্লাম্বর, নৃসিংহানন্দ আর যত জন ৷  
সবাই চলিলা, নাম না যায় লিখন ॥ ১১ ॥

vāsudeva-datta, murāri-gupta, gaṅgādāsa  
Vāsudeva Datta  Murāri Gupta  Gaṅgādāsa  
śrīmān-sena, śrīmān-paṇḍita, akiñcana kṛṣṇadāsa  
Śrīmān Sena  Śrīmān Paṇḍita  Akiñcana  Kṛṣṇadāsa  
murāri, garuḍa-paṇḍita, buddhimanta-khām̐na  
Murāri  Garuḍa Paṇḍita  Buddhimanta Khān  
sañjaya-puruṣottama, paṇḍita-bhagavān  
Sañjaya Puruṣottama  Paṇḍita Bhagavān  
śuklāmbara, nṛsiṁhānanda āra yata jana  
Śuklāmbara  Nṛsiṁhānanda  and  as many  people (as there were)  
sabāi calilā, nāma nā yāya likhana  
all  set out  the names  not  can be  written

(9–11) Vāsudeva Datta, Murāri Gupta, Gaṅgādāsa, Śrīmān Sena, Śrīmān Paṇḍita, Akiñcana Kṛṣṇadāsa, Murāri, Garuḍa Paṇḍita, Buddhimanta Khān, Sañjaya Puruṣottama, Bhagavān Paṇḍita, Śuklāmbara, Nṛsiṁhānanda and all the others set out. Their names cannot all be written down.

কুলীনগ্রামী, খণ্ডবাসী মিলিলা আসিয়া ৷  
শিবানন্দ-সেন আইলা সবারে লঞা ॥ ১২ ॥

kulīna-grāmī, khaṇḍa-vāsī mililā āsiyā  
the people of Kulīnagrāma  the residents of Khaṇḍa  joined  coming  
śivānanda-sena āilā sabāre lañā  
Śivānanda Sena  came  all  bringing

(12) The people of Kulīnagrāma and the residents of Khaṇḍa came and joined them, and Śivānanda Sena came, bringing everyone along.

রাঘব-পণ্ডিত চলে ঝালি সাজাইয়া ৷  
দময়ন্তী যত দ্রব্য দিয়াছে করিয়া ॥ ১৩ ॥

rāghava-paṇḍita cale jhāli sājāiyā  
Rāghava Paṇḍita  goes  the jhālis  having packed  
damayantī yata dravya diyāche kariyā  
Damayantī  all  the things  had given  having prepared

(13) Rāghava Paṇḍita set out with his *jhālis* (hampers), packed with all the things that Damayantī had prepared and given him.

নানা অপূর্ব্ব ভক্ষ্যদ্রব্য প্রভুর যোগ্য ভোগ ৷  
বৎসরেক প্রভু যাহা করেন উপযোগ ॥ ১৪ ॥

nānā apūrva bhakṣya-dravya prabhura yogya bhoga  
various  wonderful  foods  for the Lord  worthy  offerings  
vatsareka prabhu yāhā karena upayoga  
for a whole year  the Lord  which  makes  use of

(14) There were many wonderful foods, offerings worthy of the Lord, enough for Him to partake of for a whole year.

আম্র-কাশন্দি, আদা-ঝাল-কাশন্দি-নাম ৷  
নেম্বু-আদা-আম্রকলি বিবিধ বিধান ॥ ১৫ ॥

আম্সি, আমখণ্ড, তৈলাম্র, আমসত্তা ৷  
যত্ন করি’ গুণ্ডা করি’ পুরাণ শুক্তা ॥ ১৬ ॥

āmra-kāśandi, ādā-jhāla-kāśandi-nāma  
mango kāśandi  ginger and chili kāśandi  so named  
nembu-ādā-āmra-kali vividha vidhāna  
lime and ginger with mango buds  various  preparations  
āmsi, āma-khaṇḍa, tailāmra, āma-sattā  
āmsi  āma-khaṇḍa  tailāmra  āma-sattā  
yatna kari’ guṇḍā kari’ purāṇa śuktā  
with care  making  powder  making  dried  śuktā

(15–16) There were mango *kāśandi* and the relishes called ginger and chili *kāśandi*, lime and ginger with mango buds, and other preparations; *āmsi*, *āma-khaṇḍa*, *tailāmra* (mango pickle in mustard oil) and *āma-sattā*; and dried *śuktā* (bitter greens), carefully ground into powder.[^4]

‘শুক্তা’ বলি’ অবজ্ঞা না করিহ চিত্তে ৷  
শুক্তায় যে সুখ হয়, নহে পঞ্চামৃতে ॥ ১৭ ॥

‘śuktā’ bali’ avajñā nā kariha citte  
śuktā  calling it  contempt  do not  feel  in your mind  
śuktāya ye sukha haya, nahe pañcāmṛte  
in śuktā  what  joy  there is  is not  in pañcāmṛta

(17) Do not look down on it because it is only *śuktā*: the joy found in *śuktā* is not found even in *pañcāmṛta*.[^5]

ভাবগ্রাহী মহাপ্রভু স্নেহমাত্র লয় ৷  
শুক্তাপাতা-কাশন্দিতে মহাসুখ হয় ॥ ১৮ ॥

bhāva-grāhī mahāprabhu sneha-mātra laya  
who accepts the feeling  Mahāprabhu  the affection  alone  accepts  
śuktā-pātā-kāśandite mahā-sukha haya  
in śuktā leaves and kāśandi  great joy  there is

(18) Mahāprabhu receives the feeling (*bhāva-grāhī*); He takes only the affection. So the *śuktā* leaves and *kāśandi* gave Him great joy.[^6]

‘মনুষ্য’-বুদ্ধি দময়ন্তী করে প্রভুর পায় ৷  
গুরু-ভোজনে উদরে কভু ‘আম’ হঞা যায় ॥ ১৯ ॥

‘manuṣya’-buddhi damayantī kare prabhura pāya  
human  regard  Damayantī  holds  the Lord’s  toward the feet  
guru-bhojane udare kabhu ‘āma’ hañā yāya  
from heavy eating  in the stomach  sometimes  āma  becoming  goes

(19) Damayantī, in her affection, regarded the Lord as a human being (*manuṣya-buddhi*): “From heavy food, *āma* (indigestion) may sometimes form in His stomach.[^7]

শুক্তা খাইলে সেই আম হইবেক নাশ ৷  
সেই স্নেহ মনে ভাবি’ প্রভুর উল্লাস ॥ ২০ ॥

śuktā khāile sei āma haibeka nāśa  
śuktā  if He eats  that  āma  will be  destroyed  
sei sneha mane bhāvi’ prabhura ullāsa  
that  affection  in His mind  considering  the Lord’s  delight

(20) If He eats *śuktā*, that *āma* will be cured.” Thinking of her affection, the Lord was delighted.

প্রিয়েণ সংগ্রথ্য বিপক্ষ-সন্নিধাব্  
উপাহিতাং বক্ষসি পীবর-স্তনী ৷  
স্রজং ন কাচিদ্ বিজহৌ জলাবিলাং  
বসন্তি হি প্রেম্ণি গুণা ন বস্তুনি ॥ ২১ ॥

priyeṇa saṁgrathya vipakṣa-sannidhāv  
by her beloved  strung  in the presence of her rivals  
upāhitāṁ vakṣasi pīvara-stanī  
placed  on her breast  the full-breasted (woman)  
srajaṁ na kācid vijahau jalāvilāṁ  
the garland  not  a certain (woman)  cast off  soiled with water  
vasanti hi premṇi guṇā na vastuni  
dwell  for  in love  virtues  not  in the thing

(21) “Her beloved strung a garland himself and placed it on her breast in the presence of her rivals; and though it was soiled with water, the full-bosomed maiden did not cast it off — for virtues dwell in love, not in the thing.” (Kirātārjunīya, 8.20)[^8]

ধনিয়া-মৌহরীর তণ্ডুল গুণ্ডা করিয়া ৷  
নাড়ু বান্ধিয়াছে চিনি-পাক করিয়া ॥ ২২ ॥

dhaniyā-mauharīra taṇḍula guṇḍā kariyā  
of coriander and anise  the seeds  powder  making  
nāḍu bāndhiyāche cini-pāka kariyā  
nāḍus  she has shaped  sugar syrup  cooking

(22) She ground coriander and anise seeds into powder, cooked it in sugar syrup and shaped it into *nāḍus* (sweet balls).

শুণ্ঠীখণ্ড, নাড়ু, আর আমপিত্তহর ৷  
পৃথক্ পৃথক্ বান্ধি’ বস্ত্রের কুথলী-ভিতর ॥ ২৩ ॥

śuṇṭhī-khaṇḍa, nāḍu, āra āma-pitta-hara  
dry ginger with sugar  nāḍus  and  remedies for āma and bile  
pṛthak pṛthak bāndhi’ vastrera kuthalī-bhitara  
separately  separately  tying  of cloth  in little bags

(23) Dry ginger with sugar, *nāḍus*, and remedies for *āma* and bile she tied up separately in little cloth bags.[^9]

কোলিশুণ্ঠি কোলিচূর্ণ কোলিখণ্ড আর ৷  
কত নাম লইব, আর শতপ্রকার ‘আচার’ ॥ ২৪ ॥

koli-śuṇṭhi koli-cūrṇa koli-khaṇḍa āra  
dried kul berries  kul powder  kul with sugar  and  
kata nāma laiba, āra śata-prakāra ‘ācāra’  
how many  names  shall I list  and  of a hundred kinds  pickles

(24) Dried *kul* berries (*koli-śuṇṭhi*), *kul* powder, *kul* with sugar — how many names shall I list? — and a hundred kinds of pickles (*ācāra*).

নারিকেল-খণ্ড আর নাড়ু গঙ্গাজলি ৷  
চিরস্থায়ী খণ্ডবিকার করিলা সকলি ॥ ২৫ ॥

nārikela-khaṇḍa āra nāḍu gaṅgā-jali  
coconut sweets  and  nāḍus  gaṅgājali  
cira-sthāyī khaṇḍa-vikāra karilā sakali  
long-lasting  sugar sweets  she made  all

(25) Coconut sweets and *gaṅgājali nāḍus* (white sweet balls) — she made every kind of sugar sweet that would keep a long time.

চিরস্থায়ী ক্ষীরসার, মণ্ডাদি-বিকার ৷  
অমৃত-কর্পূর আদি অনেক প্রকার ॥ ২৬ ॥

cirasthāyī kṣīrasāra, maṇḍādi-vikāra  
long-lasting  kṣīrasāra  maṇḍā and other  preparations  
amṛta-karpūra ādi aneka prakāra  
amṛta-karpūra  and others  many  kinds

(26) There were also long-lasting *kṣīrasāra* (thickened cream), *maṇḍā* and other milk sweets, *amṛta-karpūra* and many more besides.

শালিকাচটি-ধান্যের ‘আতপ’ চিঁড়া করি’ ৷  
নূতন-বস্ত্রের বড় কুথলী সব ভরি’ ॥ ২৭ ॥

śālikācaṭi-dhānyera ‘ātapa’ cim̐ḍā kari’  
of śālikācaṭi paddy  sun-dried  flat rice  making  
nūtana-vastrera baḍa kuthalī saba bhari’  
of new cloth  big  bags  all  filling

(27) From *śālikācaṭi* paddy she made sun-dried (*ātapa*) flat rice and filled big bags of new cloth with it.

কতেক চিঁড়া হুড়ুম্ করি’ ঘৃতেতে ভাজিয়া ৷  
চিনি-পাকে নাড়ু কৈলা কর্পূরাদি দিয়া ॥ ২৮ ॥

kateka cim̐ḍā huḍum kari’ ghṛtete bhājiyā  
some  flat rice  huḍum  making  in ghee  frying  
cini-pāke nāḍu kailā karpūrādi diyā  
in sugar syrup  nāḍus  she made  camphor and so on  adding

(28) Some of the flat rice she made into *huḍum* (puffed rice) and fried it in ghee; then, adding camphor and other spices, she made *nāḍus* of it in sugar syrup.

শালি-ধান্যের তণ্ডুল-ভাজা চূর্ণ করিয়া ৷  
ঘৃতসিক্ত চূর্ণ কৈলা চিনি-পাক দিয়া ॥ ২৯ ॥

কর্পূর, মরিচ, লবঙ্গ, এলাচি, রসবাস ৷  
চূর্ণ দিয়া নাড়ু কৈলা পরম সুবাস ॥ ৩০ ॥

śāli-dhānyera taṇḍula-bhājā cūrṇa kariyā  
of śāli paddy  fried rice grains  powder  making  
ghṛta-sikta cūrṇa kailā cini-pāka diyā  
moistened with ghee  the powder  she made  sugar syrup  adding  
karpūra, marica, lavaṅga, elāci, rasavāsa  
camphor  black pepper  cloves  cardamom  rasavāsa  
cūrṇa diyā nāḍu kailā parama suvāsa  
the powder  adding  nāḍus  she made  most  fragrant

(29–30) She fried grains of *śāli* rice and ground them to powder, moistened the powder with ghee and cooked it with sugar syrup. Then, adding powdered camphor, black pepper, cloves, cardamom and *rasavāsa* (aromatic spice), she made most fragrant *nāḍus*.

শালি-ধান্যের খই পুনঃ ঘৃতেতে ভাজিয়া ৷  
চিনি-পাক উখ্ড়া কৈলা কর্পূরাদি দিয়া ॥ ৩১ ॥

śāli-dhānyera khai punaḥ ghṛtete bhājiyā  
of śāli paddy  khai  again  in ghee  frying  
cini-pāka ukhḍā kailā karpūrādi diyā  
in sugar syrup  ukhḍā  she made  camphor and so on  adding

(31) Again, she fried *khai* (popped rice) of *śāli* paddy in ghee and made *ukhḍā* in sugar syrup, adding camphor and other spices.[^10]

ফুট্কলাই চূর্ণ করি’ ঘৃতে ভাজাইল ৷  
চিনি-পাকে কর্পূর দিয়া নাড়ু কৈল ॥ ৩২ ॥

phuṭkalāi cūrṇa kari’ ghṛte bhājāila  
phuṭkalāi  powder  making  in ghee  she fried  
cini-pāke karpūra diyā nāḍu kaila  
in sugar syrup  camphor  adding  nāḍus  she made

(32) She ground *phuṭkalāi* (fried peas) to powder and fried it in ghee, and with sugar syrup and camphor made *nāḍus* of it.

কহিতে না জানি নাম এ-জন্মে যাহার ৷  
ঐছে নানা ভক্ষ্যদ্রব্য সহস্রপ্রকার ॥ ৩৩ ॥

kahite nā jāni nāma e-janme yāhāra  
to tell  not  I know  the names  in this life  of which  
aiche nānā bhakṣya-dravya sahasra-prakāra  
such  various  foods  of a thousand  kinds

(33) There were a thousand kinds of such foods, whose names I could not tell in this lifetime.

রাঘবের আজ্ঞা, আর করেন দময়ন্তী ৷  
দুঁহার প্রভুতে স্নেহ পরম-ভকতি ॥ ৩৪ ॥

rāghavera ājñā, āra karena damayantī  
Rāghava’s  order  and  carries out  Damayantī  
dum̐hāra prabhute sneha parama-bhakati  
of both  for the Lord  affection  supreme devotion

(34) Rāghava gave the orders, and Damayantī carried them out. Both had deep affection and supreme devotion for the Lord.

গঙ্গা-মৃত্তিকা আনি’ বস্ত্রেতে ছানিয়া ৷  
পাঁচ কুড়ি করিয়া দিলা গন্ধদ্রব্য দিয়া ॥ ৩৫ ॥

gaṅgā-mṛttikā āni’ vastrete chāniyā  
Gaṅgā clay  bringing  through cloth  straining  
pām̐ca kuḍi kariyā dilā gandha-dravya diyā  
pāṁc  kuḍi  making  she gave  fragrant substances  adding

(35) She brought clay from the Gaṅgā, strained it through cloth, mixed in fragrant substances and made it into *pāṁc-kuḍi* (thin wafers).[^11]

পাতল মৃৎপাত্রে চন্দনাদি ভরি’ ৷  
আর সব বস্তু ভরে বস্ত্রের কুথলী ॥ ৩৬ ॥

pātala mṛt-pātre candanādi bhari’  
thin  clay  pots  sandalwood and so on  filling  
āra saba vastu bhare vastrera kuthalī  
other  all  things  she fills  of cloth  little bags

(36) She filled thin clay pots with sandalwood paste and other things, and packed everything else in little cloth bags.[^12]

সামান্য ঝালি হৈতে দ্বিগুণ ঝালি কৈলা ৷  
পরিপাটি করি’ সব ঝালি ভরাইলা ॥ ৩৭ ॥

sāmānya jhāli haite dviguṇa jhāli kailā  
ordinary  jhālis  than  twice as big  jhālis  she made  
paripāṭi kari’ saba jhāli bharāilā  
neatly  making  all  the jhālis  she had filled

(37) She had *jhālis* made twice the size of ordinary ones, and had them all packed neatly.

ঝালি বান্ধি’ মোহর দিলা আগ্রহ করিয়া ৷  
তিন বোঝারি ঝালি বহে ক্রম করিয়া ॥ ৩৮ ॥

jhāli bāndhi’ mohara dilā āgraha kariyā  
the jhālis  tying  a seal  she gave  with care  
tina bojhāri jhāli vahe krama kariyā  
three  porters  the jhālis  carry  in turn  doing

(38) She tied up the *jhālis* and carefully sealed them. Three porters carried them, taking turns.

সংক্ষেপে কহিলুঁ এই ঝালির বিচার ৷  
‘রাঘবের ঝালি’ বলি’ বিখ্যাতি যাহার ॥ ৩৯ ॥

saṁkṣepe kahilum̐ ei jhālira vicāra  
briefly  I have told  these  jhālis’  account  
‘rāghavera jhāli’ bali’ vikhyāti yāhāra  
Rāghava’s jhālis  as  fame  of which

(39) I have briefly described these *jhālis*, which became famous as “Rāghava’s *jhālis*.”

ঝালির উপর ‘মুনসিব’ মকরধ্বজ-কর ৷  
প্রাণরূপে ঝালি রাখে হঞা তৎপর ॥ ৪০ ॥

jhālira upara ‘munsiba’ makaradhvaja-kara  
the jhālis  over  munsib  Makaradhvaja Kara  
prāṇa-rūpe jhāli rākhe hañā tatpara  
like his life  the jhālis  keeps  being  vigilant

(40) Makaradhvaja Kara was the *munsib* (steward) of the *jhālis*; he guarded them vigilantly, as if they were his very life.[^13]

এইমতে বৈষ্ণব সব নীলাচলে আইলা ৷  
দৈবে জগন্নাথের সে দিন জল-লীলা ॥ ৪১ ॥

ei-mate vaiṣṇava saba nīlācale āilā  
in this way  the Vaiṣṇavas  all  to Nīlācala  came  
daive jagannāthera se dina jala-līlā  
by providence  Jagannātha’s  that  day  water pastime

(41) In this way all the Vaiṣṇavas came to Nīlācala. As providence would have it, that day was Jagannātha’s water pastime.

নরেন্দ্রের জলে ‘গোবিন্দ’ নৌকাতে চড়িয়া ৷  
জলক্রীড়া করে সবভক্তগণ লঞা ॥ ৪২ ॥

narendrera jale ‘govinda’ naukāte caḍiyā  
of Narendra  on the water  Govinda  a boat  boarding  
jala-krīḍā kare sababhakta-gaṇa lañā  
water sports  performs  all the devotees  with

(42) On the waters of Narendra Lake, Śrī Govinda boarded a boat and sported in the water with all the devotees.[^14]

সেইকালে মহাপ্রভু ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে ৷  
নরেন্দ্রে আইলা দেখিতে জলকেলি রঙ্গে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

sei-kāle mahāprabhu bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge  
at that time  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees  
narendre āilā dekhite jala-keli raṅge  
to Narendra  came  to see  the water play  joyfully

(43) At that time Mahāprabhu came joyfully to Narendra with His devotees to watch the water play.

সেইকালে সব গৌড়ের ভক্তগণ ৷  
নরেন্দ্রেতে প্রভু-সঙ্গে হইল মিলন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

sei-kāle saba gauḍera bhakta-gaṇa  
at that time  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees  
narendrete prabhu-saṅge haila milana  
at Narendra  with the Lord  was  meeting

(44) At that very time all the devotees of Gauḍa met the Lord at Narendra.

ভক্তগণ পড়ে আসি’ প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
উঠাঞা প্রভু সবারে কৈলা আলিঙ্গনে ॥ ৪৫ ॥

bhakta-gaṇa paḍe āsi’ prabhura caraṇe  
the devotees  fall  coming  at the Lord’s  feet  
uṭhāñā prabhu sabāre kailā āliṅgane  
raising them  the Lord  all  embraced

(45) The devotees came and fell at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord raised them all and embraced them.

গৌড়ীয়-সম্প্রদায় সব করেন কীর্ত্তন ৷  
প্রভুর মিলনে উঠে প্রেমের ক্রন্দন ॥ ৪৬ ॥

gauḍīya-sampradāya saba karena kīrtana  
the Gauḍīya  party  all  perform  kīrtana  
prabhura milane uṭhe premera krandana  
at the Lord’s  meeting  rises  of love  weeping

(46) The whole Gauḍīya party sang kīrtana, and on meeting the Lord they wept with love.

জলক্রীড়া, বাদ্য, গীত, নর্ত্তন, কীর্ত্তন ৷  
মহাকোলাহল তীরে, সলিলে খেলন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

jala-krīḍā, vādya, gīta, nartana, kīrtana  
water sports  music  singing  dancing  kīrtana  
mahā-kolāhala tīre, salile khelana  
a great tumult  on the shore  in the water  play

(47) Water sports, music, singing, dancing and kīrtana — a great tumult on the shore and play in the water.

গৌড়ীয়া-সঙ্কীর্ত্তনে আর রোদন মিলিয়া ৷  
মহাকোলাহল-শব্দ হৈল ব্রহ্মাণ্ড ভরিয়া ॥ ৪৮ ॥

gauḍīyā-saṅkīrtane āra rodana miliyā  
of the Gauḍīyas  saṅkīrtana  and  weeping  mingling  
mahākolāhala-śabda haila brahmāṇḍa bhariyā  
the sound of a great tumult  arose  the universe  filling

(48) The Gauḍīyas’ saṅkīrtana and their weeping mingled, and the sound of that great tumult filled the universe.

সব ভক্ত লঞা প্রভু নামিলেন জলে ৷  
সবা লঞা জলক্রীড়া করেন কুতূহলে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

saba bhakta lañā prabhu nāmilena jale  
all  the devotees  taking  the Lord  entered  the water  
sabā lañā jala-krīḍā karena kutūhale  
all  with  water sports  performs  delightedly

(49) The Lord entered the water with all the devotees and sported with them all in delight.

প্রভুর এই জলক্রীড়া দাস-বৃন্দাবন ৷  
‘চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে’ বিস্তারি’ কৈরাছেন বর্ণন ॥ ৫০ ॥

prabhura ei jala-krīḍā dāsa-vṛndāvana  
the Lord’s  this  water sport  Vṛndāvana dāsa  
‘caitanya-maṅgale’ vistāri’ kairāchena varṇana  
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  at length  has made  description

(50) Vṛndāvana dāsa has described these water sports of the Lord at length in his *Caitanya-maṅgala* (the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*).[^15]

পুনঃ ইঁহা বর্ণিলে পুনরুক্তি হয় ৷  
ব্যর্থ লিখন হয়, মোর গ্রন্থ বাড়য় ॥ ৫১ ॥

punaḥ im̐hā varṇile punarukti haya  
again  this  if I describe  repetition  there is  
vyartha likhana haya, mora grantha bāḍaya  
useless  writing  is  my  book  enlarges

(51) If I describe them here again, it will be repetition — useless writing that only makes my book longer.

জললীলা করি’ গোবিন্দ চলিলা আলয় ৷  
নিজগণ লঞা প্রভু গেলা দেবালয় ॥ ৫২ ॥

jala-līlā kari’ govinda calilā ālaya  
water pastime  performing  Govinda  went  to His abode  
nija-gaṇa lañā prabhu gelā devālaya  
His own associates  with  the Lord  went  to the temple

(52) After the water pastime Govinda returned to His abode, and the Lord went to the temple with His associates.

জগন্নাথ দেখি’ পুনঃ নিজ ঘরে-আইলা ৷  
প্রসাদ আনাঞা ভক্তগণে খাওয়াইলা ॥ ৫৩ ॥

jagannātha dekhi’ punaḥ nija ghare-āilā  
Jagannātha  seeing  again  His own  to the residence came  
prasāda ānāñā bhakta-gaṇe khāoyāilā  
prasāda  having brought  the devotees  He fed

(53) After seeing Jagannātha, He returned to His residence, had prasāda brought and fed the devotees.

ইষ্টগোষ্ঠী সবা লঞা কতক্ষণ কৈলা ৷  
নিজ-নিজ-পূর্ব্ব-বাসায় সবায় পাঠাইলা ॥ ৫৪ ॥

iṣṭa-goṣṭhī sabā lañā kata-kṣaṇa kailā  
a discussion  all  with  for some time  He held  
nija-nija-pūrva-vāsāya sabāya pāṭhāilā  
to their own  former lodgings  everyone  He sent

(54) For some time He talked with them all of Kṛṣṇa (*iṣṭa-goṣṭhī*), and then sent everyone to the lodgings each had occupied before.

গোবিন্দ-ঠাঞি রাঘব ঝালি সমর্পিলা ৷  
ভোজন-গৃহের কোণে ঝালি রাখিলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

govinda-ṭhāñi rāghava jhāli samarpilā  
to Govinda  Rāghava  the jhālis  handed over  
bhojana-gṛhera koṇe jhāli rākhilā  
of the dining room  in a corner  the jhālis  were kept

(55) Rāghava handed the *jhālis* over to Govinda, and they were kept in a corner of the dining room.

পূর্ব্ব-বৎসরের ঝালি আজাড় করিয়া ৷  
দ্রব্য ভরিবারে রাখে অন্য গৃহে লঞা ॥ ৫৬ ॥

pūrva-vatsarera jhāli ājāḍa kariyā  
of the previous year  the jhālis  empty  making  
dravya bharibāre rākhe anya gṛhe lañā  
things  for filling  keeps  in another room  taking

(56) Govinda emptied the previous year’s *jhālis* and took them to another room, keeping them there to be filled with provisions.

আর দিন মহাপ্রভু নিজগণ লঞা ৷  
জগন্নাথ দেখিলেন শয়্যোত্থানে যাঞা ॥ ৫৭ ॥

āra dina mahāprabhu nija-gaṇa lañā  
next  day  Mahāprabhu  His associates  with  
jagannātha dekhilena śayyotthāne yāñā  
Jagannātha  saw  at the rising from bed  going

(57) The next day Mahāprabhu went with His associates to see Jagannātha at His rising from bed.

বেড়া-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন তাঁহা আরম্ভ করিলা ৷  
সাত-সম্প্রদায় তবে গাইতে লাগিলা ॥ ৫৮ ॥

beḍā-saṅkīrtana tām̐hā ārambha karilā  
encircling saṅkīrtana  there  beginning  He made  
sāta-sampradāya tabe gāite lāgilā  
seven  parties  then  to sing  began

(58) There He began the *beḍā-saṅkīrtana* (the kīrtana around the temple), and seven parties began to sing.

সাত-সম্প্রদায়ে নৃত্য করে সাত জন ৷  
অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্য, আর প্রভু-নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

sāta-sampradāye nṛtya kare sāta jana  
in the seven parties  dance  perform  seven  people  
advaita-ācārya, āra prabhu-nityānanda  
Advaita Ācārya  and  Prabhu Nityānanda

(59) In the seven parties seven persons danced: Advaita Ācārya and Prabhu Nityānanda,

বক্রেশ্বর, অচ্যুতানন্দ, পণ্ডিত-শ্রীবাস ৷  
সত্যরাজ-খাঁন, আর নরহরিদাস ॥ ৬০ ॥

vakreśvara, acyutānanda, paṇḍita-śrīvāsa  
Vakreśvara  Acyutānanda  Paṇḍita Śrīvāsa  
satyarāja-khām̐na, āra narahari-dāsa  
Satyarāja Khān  and  Narahari dāsa

(60) Vakreśvara, Acyutānanda, Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, Satyarāja Khān and Narahari dāsa.

সাত-সম্প্রদায়ে প্রভু করেন ভ্রমণ ৷  
মোর সম্প্রদায়ে প্রভু — ঐছে সবার মন ॥ ৬১ ॥

sāta-sampradāye prabhu karena bhramaṇa  
among the seven parties  the Lord  makes  wandering  
mora sampradāye prabhu — aiche sabāra mana  
in my  party  the Lord  so  everyone’s  mind

(61) The Lord moved among the seven parties, and each one thought, “The Lord is in my party.”[^16]

সঙ্কীর্ত্তন কোলাহলে আকাশ ভেদিল ৷  
সব জগন্নাথবাসী দেখিতে আইল ॥ ৬২ ॥

saṅkīrtana kolāhale ākāśa bhedila  
saṅkīrtana  by the tumult  the sky  was pierced  
saba jagannātha-vāsī dekhite āila  
all  the residents of Jagannātha’s town  to see  came

(62) The tumult of the saṅkīrtana pierced the sky, and all the residents of Jagannātha’s town came to see.

রাজা আসি’ দূরে দেখে নিজগণ লঞা ৷  
রাজপত্নী সব দেখে অট্টালী চড়িয়া ॥ ৬৩ ॥

rājā āsi’ dūre dekhe nija-gaṇa lañā  
the king  coming  from afar  watches  his retinue  with  
rāja-patnī saba dekhe aṭṭālī caḍiyā  
the queens  all  watch  the upper storey  climbing

(63) The king came with his retinue and watched from a distance, and all the queens watched from the upper storey of the palace.

কীর্ত্তন-আবেশে পৃথিবী করে টলমল ৷  
‘হরিধ্বনি’ করে লোক, হৈল কোলাহল ॥ ৬৪ ॥

kīrtana-āveśe pṛthivī kare ṭalamala  
in the ecstasy of kīrtana  the earth  makes  trembling  
‘hari-dhvani’ kare loka, haila kolāhala  
the sound of Hari  make  the people  there was  a tumult

(64) In the ecstasy of the kīrtana the earth trembled. The people called out “Hari!” and there was a great tumult.[^17]

এইমত কতক্ষণ করাইলা কীর্ত্তন ৷  
আপনে নাচিতে তবে প্রভুর হৈল মন ॥ ৬৫ ॥

ei-mata kata-kṣaṇa karāilā kīrtana  
in this way  for some time  He had performed  kīrtana  
āpane nācite tabe prabhura haila mana  
Himself  to dance  then  the Lord’s  arose  wish

(65) In this way He had the kīrtana performed for some time; then the Lord wished to dance Himself.

সাত-দিকে সাত-সম্প্রদায় গায়, বাজায় ৷  
মধ্যে মহাপ্রেমাবেশে নাচে গৌর-রায় ॥ ৬৬ ॥

sāta-dike sāta-sampradāya gāya, bājāya  
on seven sides  seven parties  sing  play  
madhye mahā-premāveśe nāce gaura-rāya  
in the middle  in the ecstasy of great love  dances  Gaura Rāya

(66) On seven sides the seven parties sang and played, and in the middle Gaura Rāya danced, absorbed in great love.

ওড়িয়া-পদ মহাপ্রভুর মনে স্মৃতি হৈল ৷  
স্বরূপেরে সেই পদ গাইতে আজ্ঞা দিল ॥ ৬৭ ॥

oḍiyā-pada mahāprabhura mane smṛti haila  
an Oḍiyā line  Mahāprabhu’s  in the mind  remembrance  arose  
svarūpere sei pada gāite ājñā dila  
to Svarūpa  that  line  to sing  order  He gave

(67) A line of an Oḍiyā song came to Mahāprabhu’s mind, and He told Svarūpa to sing it:

‘জগমোহন-পরিমুণ্ডা যাউ’ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

‘jagamohana-pari-muṇḍā yāu’  
the Jagamohana  to  head  may be offered

(68) “Let my head be offered at the Jagamohana!”[^18]

এই পদে নৃত্য করেন আপন-আবেশে ৷  
সবলোক চৌদিকে প্রভুর প্রেমে ভাসে ॥ ৬৯ ॥

ei pade nṛtya karena āpana-āveśe  
to this  line  dance  He performs  in His own absorption  
sabaloka caudike prabhura preme bhāse  
all the people  around  in the Lord’s  love  float

(69) To this line He danced, absorbed in His own feeling, and all the people around floated in the Lord’s love.[^19]

‘বোল্’ ‘বোল্’ বলেন প্রভু শ্রীবাহু তুলিয়া ৷  
হরিধ্বনি করে লোক আনন্দে ভাসিয়া ॥ ৭০ ॥

‘bol’ ‘bol’ balena prabhu śrī-bāhu tuliyā  
say  say  says  the Lord  His holy arms  raising  
hari-dhvani kare loka ānande bhāsiyā  
the sound of Hari  make  the people  in bliss  floating

(70) Raising His holy arms, the Lord called, “Say it! Say it!” and the people, floating in bliss, called out “Hari!”

প্রভু পড়ি’ মূর্চ্ছা যায়, শ্বাস নাহি আর ৷  
আচম্বিতে উঠে প্রভু করিয়া হুঙ্কার ॥ ৭১ ॥

prabhu paḍi’ mūrcchā yāya, śvāsa nāhi āra  
the Lord  falling  unconscious  goes  breath  not  any more  
ācambite uṭhe prabhu kariyā huṅkāra  
suddenly  rises  the Lord  making  a roar

(71) The Lord fell and lost consciousness; He was no longer breathing. Then suddenly He rose with a roar.

সঘন পুলক, — যেন শিমুলের তরু ৷  
কভু প্রফুল্লিত অঙ্গ, কভু হয় সরু ॥ ৭২ ॥

saghana pulaka, — yena śimulera taru  
dense  bristling of the hair  like  of a śimula  tree  
kabhu praphullita aṅga, kabhu haya saru  
sometimes  swollen  the body  sometimes  becomes  thin

(72) His hair bristled thickly, like the thorns of a *śimula* tree. At times His body swelled; at times it grew thin.

প্রতি রোমে হয় প্রস্বেদ, রক্তোদ্গম ৷  
‘জজ’ ‘গগ’ ‘পরি’ ‘মুমু’ — গদ্গদ বচন ॥ ৭৩ ॥

prati rome haya prasveda, raktodgama  
from every  pore  comes  perspiration  oozing of blood  
‘jaja’ ‘gaga’ ‘pari’ ‘mumu’ — gadgada vacana  
‘jaja’  ‘gaga’  ‘pari’  ‘mumu’  faltering  speech

(73) From every pore came perspiration and blood, and His faltering voice uttered only “jaja,” “gaga,” “pari,” “mumu.”

এক এক দন্ত সব পৃথক্ পৃথক্ নড়ে ৷  
ঐছে নড়ে দন্ত, — যেন ভূমে খসি’ পড়ে ॥ ৭৪ ॥

eka eka danta saba pṛthak pṛthak naḍe  
one  one  tooth  all  separately  separately  shake  
aiche naḍe danta, — yena bhūme khasi’ paḍe  
so  shake  the teeth  as if  to the ground  coming loose  fall

(74) Each of His teeth shook separately; they shook as if they would come loose and fall to the ground.

ক্ষণে ক্ষণে বাড়ে প্রভুর আনন্দ-আবেশ ৷  
তৃতীয় প্রহর হইল, নৃত্য নহে শেষ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kṣaṇe kṣaṇe bāḍe prabhura ānanda-āveśa  
moment  by moment  grows  the Lord’s  ecstasy of bliss  
tṛtīya prahara haila, nṛtya nahe śeṣa  
the third  prahara  came  the dance  is not  ended

(75) Moment by moment the Lord’s blissful ecstasy grew. The third *prahara* of the day came, and still the dance did not end.

সব লোকের উথলিল আনন্দ-সাগর ৷  
সব লোক পাসরিল দেহ-আত্ম-ঘর ॥ ৭৬ ॥

saba lokera uthalila ānanda-sāgara  
of all  the people  surged  the ocean of bliss  
saba loka pāsarila deha-ātma-ghara  
all  the people  forgot  body  self  home

(76) The ocean of bliss surged in everyone, and all the people forgot body, self and home.

তবে নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু সৃজিলা উপায় ৷  
ক্রমে-ক্রমে কীর্ত্তনীয়া রাখিল সবায় ॥ ৭৭ ॥

tabe nityānandaprabhu sṛjilā upāya  
then  Nityānanda Prabhu  devised  a means  
krame-krame kīrtanīyā rākhila sabāya  
gradually  the singers  stopped  all

(77) Then Nityānanda Prabhu devised a means: one by one He stopped all the singers.

প্রধান প্রধান যেবা হয় সম্প্রদায় ৷  
স্বরূপের সঙ্গে সেই মন্দস্বর গায় ॥ ৭৮ ॥

pradhāna pradhāna yebā haya sampradāya  
principal  principal  whichever  were  the parties  
svarūpera saṅge sei manda-svara gāya  
with Svarūpa  they  in a soft voice  sing

(78) Only the principal parties went on singing with Svarūpa, softly.

কোলাহল নাহি, প্রভুর কিছু বাহ্য হৈল ৷  
তবে নিত্যানন্দ সবার শ্রম জানাইল ॥ ৭৯ ॥

kolāhala nāhi, prabhura kichu bāhya haila  
tumult  there was no  the Lord’s  some  outer awareness  came  
tabe nityānanda sabāra śrama jānāila  
then  Nityānanda  everyone’s  fatigue  made known

(79) When the tumult died down, the Lord regained some outer awareness. Then Nityānanda told Him how tired everyone was.[^20]

ভক্তশ্রম জানি’ কৈলা কীর্ত্তন সমাপন ৷  
সবা লঞা আসি’ কৈলা সমুদ্রে স্নপন ॥ ৮০ ॥

bhakta-śrama jāni’ kailā kīrtana samāpana  
the devotees’ fatigue  knowing  He made  of the kīrtana  conclusion  
sabā lañā āsi’ kailā samudre snapana  
all  with  coming  He made  in the sea  bathing

(80) Knowing that the devotees were tired, He brought the kīrtana to an end, went with them all and bathed in the sea.

সব লঞা প্রভু কৈলা প্রসাদ ভোজন ৷  
সবারে বিদায় দিলা করিতে শয়ন ॥ ৮১ ॥

saba lañā prabhu kailā prasāda bhojana  
all  with  the Lord  made  prasāda  eating  
sabāre vidāya dilā karite śayana  
to all  leave  He gave  to take  rest

(81) The Lord took prasāda with them all and then sent everyone off to rest.

গম্ভীরার দ্বারে করেন আপনে শয়ন ৷  
গোবিন্দ আসিয়া করে পাদ-সম্বাহন ॥ ৮২ ॥

gambhīrāra dvāre karena āpane śayana  
of the Gambhīrā  at the door  makes  Himself  lying down  
govinda āsiyā kare pāda-samvāhana  
Govinda  coming  does  massaging of the feet

(82) He Himself lay down at the door of the Gambhīrā, and Govinda came to massage His feet.

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: *yena kenāpi* — with anything at all, even the simplest thing, if a devotee offers it with faith.

[^2]: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (and Antya 3.2); translation as there.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: Ācāryaratna is Candraśekhara; Ācāryanidhi is Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi, also called Premanidhi.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya*: *tailāmra* is mango pickle kept in mustard oil. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *purāṇa śuktā* — dried bitter jute greens.

[^5]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *śuktāya ye sukha haya, nahe pañcāmṛte*; another reading is *sukutāya ye sukha prabhura, tāhā nahe pañcāmṛte* (“the Lord’s joy in *sukutā* is not found in *pañcāmṛta*”).

[^6]: Edition heading: the transcendental Bhagavān accepts the feeling. *Anubhāṣya*: *bhāva* here is a transcendental, causeless movement of the heart, made of pure goodness and aimed at pleasing Kṛṣṇa — not a mundane feeling that seeks its own pleasure.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: to see Him as a human being (*manuṣya-buddhi*) is the consciousness of the residents of Gauḍa and Vraja: their hearts, made of pure goodness, know nothing of His majesty, and they know Gaura-Kṛṣṇa in His human form as won over by their pure love alone. *Āma* is an acid-bilious disorder that comes from indigestion.

[^8]: Edition heading: a verse from Bhāravi’s *Kirātārjunīya* (canto 8, verse 20); only what is offered with love is full of virtue, and in a gift of love outer faults and merits are not weighed. *Anubhāṣya*: only a thing offered with love has virtue; any other, even a good one, is without it — and love has no need to test the thing.

[^9]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *śuṇṭhī-khaṇḍa, nāḍu, āra āma-pitta-hara* — three separate things. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *kuthalī* — little bags.

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *ukhḍā* — the small husked grains, like *khai*, that remain after the large *khai* have been picked out.

[^11]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *pāṁc kuḍi*; according to the *Anubhāṣya*, other readings are *pākauḍi* and *pāṁpaḍi* (thin wafers), or *parpaṭī*.

[^12]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *candanādi* (“sandalwood and other things”); another reading is *sandhānādi* (“pickles and the like”).

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: *munsib* (Arabic *munsif*) — overseer, steward; Makaradhvaja Kara was a resident of Pānihāṭi, a devotee of Gaura and a follower of Rāghava Paṇḍita.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya* (on verse 52): in the temple of Jagannātha resides Śrī Govindadeva, the *vijaya-mūrti* (the Deity who goes out in procession); it is He who goes to Narendra for the water pastimes.

[^15]: *Caitanya-maṅgala* was the first title of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya, chapter 9.

[^16]: Edition heading: the Lord manifests His great majesty (*aiśvarya*).

[^17]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *kīrtana-āveśe* (“in the ecstasy of kīrtana”); another reading, noted in the *Anubhāṣya*, is *kīrtana-āṭope* (“by the force of the kīrtana”).

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Jagamohana is the hall (*nāṭya-mandira*) of Śrī Jagannātha; *pari* — “to”, *muṇḍā* — “head”, *yāu* — “may it be offered”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the place in the Jagamohana where the devotees dance is called *parimaṇḍala*, and *parimuṇḍā* is the Oḍiyā corruption of that word; the line is not given in full, so its sense is not clear — it only points to some special feeling.

[^19]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *āpana-āveśe* (“absorbed in His own feeling”); another reading is *parama-āveśe* (“in great absorption”).

[^20]: Edition heading: the Lord comes to a half-external state (*ardha-bāhya*).
