# Editor’s Note

The verses of the «Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta» are translated here from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha
edition with the commentaries «Anubhāṣya» of Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura and «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»
of Śrīla Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura, and in parallel with the Russian translation of Vṛndāvana Candra dāsa (revised in
2026). Explanations drawn from the commentaries are given in short editorial footnotes, not inside the translation.

**On the words “māyā” and “illusion”.** Wherever this book says that the Lord or His associates “forgot”, “did not
know”, or were “under illusion” or “māyā”, what is meant is **yogamāyā** — the Lord’s own internal potency, which
serves His pastimes by veiling His majesty from Him and His eternal associates so that their love may be full and
sweet. The Lord and His associates are never subject to the external māyā that covers the conditioned souls. This
note applies to every such passage and is not repeated in the footnotes.
