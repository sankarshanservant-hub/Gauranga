“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 1  
**The Auspicious Invocation: Obeisance to the Guru and Others**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে গুরূনীশভক্তানীশমীশাবতারকান্ ৷  
তৎপ্রকাশাংশ্চ তচ্চক্তীঃ কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যসংজ্ঞকম্ ॥ ১ ॥

vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān  
I worship  the gurus,  the Lord’s devotees  
īśam īśāvatārakān  
the Lord,  the Lord’s avatāras  
tat-prakāśāṁś ca tac-chaktīḥ  
His manifestations  and  His potencies  
kṛṣṇa-caitanya-saṁjñakam  
Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  named

(1) I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, His avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies — and the Lord Himself, who is known as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.[^1]

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যনিত্যানন্দৌ সহোদিতৌ ৷  
গৌড়োদয়ে পুষ্পবন্তৌ চিত্রৌ শন্দৌ তমোনুদৌ ॥ ২ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
nityānandau sahoditau  
and Nityānanda  who rose together  
gauḍodaye puṣpavantau  
on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and moon  
citrau śan-dau tamo-nudau  
wonderful  bestowing good  dispelling darkness

(2) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Śrī Nityānanda, who are like a wonderful sun and moon. They have risen together on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa, dispelling the darkness and bestowing good on all.

যদদ্বৈতং ব্রহ্মোপনিষদি তদপ্যস্য তনুভা  
য আত্মান্তর্য্যামী পুরুষ ইতি সোঽস্যাংশবিভবঃ ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যৈঃ পূর্ণো য ইহ ভগবান্ স স্বয়ময়ং  
ন চৈতন্যাৎ কৃষ্ণাজ্জগতি পরতত্ত্বং পরমিহ ॥ ৩ ॥

yad advaitaṁ brahmopaniṣadi tad apy asya tanu-bhā  
which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  also  His  bodily  effulgence  
ya ātmāntaryāmī puruṣa iti so ’syāṁśa-vibhavaḥ  
who  the Supersoul  the Indweller  the Puruṣa  thus  He  His  portion  manifestation  
ṣaḍ-aiśvaryaiḥ pūrṇo ya iha bhagavān sa svayam ayaṁ  
with six  opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one  
na caitanyāt kṛṣṇāj jagati para-tattvaṁ param iha  
not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  supreme  truth  higher  here

(3) There is no Truth in the world higher than Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. That which the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is the effulgence of His body; the Puruṣa, the Supersoul who dwells within every heart, is His partial manifestation; and Bhagavān, full in all six opulences, is He Himself.[^2]

অনর্পিতচরীং চিরাৎ করুণয়াবতীর্ণঃ কলৌ  
সমর্পয়িতুমুন্নতোজ্জ্বলরসাং স্বভক্তিশ্রিয়ম্ ৷  
হরিঃ পুরটসুন্দরদ্যুতিকদম্বসন্দীপিতঃ  
সদা হৃদয়কন্দরে স্ফুরতু বঃ শচীনন্দনঃ ॥ ৪ ॥

anarpita-carīṁ cirāt karuṇayāvatīrṇaḥ kalau  
not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in Kali  
samarpayitum unnatojjvala-rasāṁ sva-bhakti-śriyam  
to bestow  of the most exalted radiant rasa  devotion to Himself  the treasure  
hariḥ puraṭa-sundara-dyuti-kadamba-sandīpitaḥ  
Hari  golden  beautiful  lustre  with a mass  illumined  
sadā hṛdaya-kandare sphuratu vaḥ śacī-nandanaḥ  
always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  Śacī’s  son

(4) “May the Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, illumined by a mass of lustre more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the inmost cave of your hearts. Out of His mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a very long time — the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted, radiant *ujjvala-rasa*.” (Vidagdha-mādhava, 1.2)[^3]

রাধা কৃষ্ণপ্রণয়বিকৃতির্হ্লাদিনীশক্তিরস্মা-  
দেকাত্মানাবপি ভুবি পুরা দেহভেদং গতৌ তৌ ৷  
চৈতন্যাখ্যং প্রকটমধুনা তদ্দ্বয়ং চৈক্যমাপ্তং  
রাধাভাবদ্যুতিসুবলিতং নৌমি কৃষ্ণস্বরূপম্ ॥ ৫ ॥

rādhā kṛṣṇa-praṇaya-vikṛtir hlādinī śaktir asmād  
Rādhā  of Kṛṣṇa’s  love  the transformation  the pleasure  potency  therefore  
ekātmānāv api bhuvi purā deha-bhedaṁ gatau tau  
one in self  although  in the world  of old  bodily  distinction  attained  the Two  
caitanyākhyaṁ prakaṭam adhunā tad-dvayaṁ caikyam āptaṁ  
Caitanya named  manifest  now  that  pair  and oneness  attained  
rādhā-bhāva-dyuti-suvalitaṁ naumi kṛṣṇa-svarūpam  
with Rādhā’s  sentiment  and lustre  adorned  I bow to  Kṛṣṇa  Himself

(5) “Śrī Rādhā is the transformation of Kṛṣṇa’s love, His pleasure potency, *hlādinī-śakti*. Therefore, though Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in Self, from time without beginning They have appeared in the world in two different forms. Now these Two have become one and are manifest as Śrī Caitanya. I bow to Him, Kṛṣṇa Himself, adorned with the sentiment and the lustre of Śrī Rādhā.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^4]

শ্রীরাধায়াঃ প্রণয়মহিমা কীদৃশো বানয়ৈবা-  
স্বাদ্যো যেনাদ্ভুতমধুরিমা কীদৃশো বা মদীয়ঃ ৷  
সৌখ্যঞ্চাস্যা মদনুভবতঃ কীদৃশং বেতি লোভা-  
ত্তদ্ভাবাঢ়্যঃ সমজনি শচীগর্ভসিন্ধৌ হরীন্দুঃ ॥ ৬ ॥

śrī-rādhāyāḥ praṇaya-mahimā kīdṛśo vānayaivā-  
Śrī  Rādhā’s  love’s  greatness  of what kind  or  by Her alone  
svādyo yenādbhuta-madhurimā kīdṛśo vā madīyaḥ  
to be tasted  by which  wondrous  sweetness  of what kind  or  My  
saukhyaṁ cāsyā mad-anubhavataḥ kīdṛśaṁ veti lobhāt  
happiness  and Her  from experiencing Me  of what kind  or thus  out of longing  
tad-bhāvāḍhyaḥ samajani śacī-garbha-sindhau harīnduḥ  
with Her sentiment rich  was born  in Śacī’s womb  ocean  the moon Hari

(6) “What is the greatness of Śrī Rādhā’s love? What is My own wondrous sweetness, which She alone tastes through that love? And what is the happiness She feels in experiencing Me?” Longing to know this, the moon Hari, rich with Her sentiment, was born from the ocean of Śacī’s womb. (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^5]

সঙ্কর্ষণঃ কারণতোয়শায়ী  
গর্ভোদশায়ী চ পয়োঽব্ধিশায়ী ৷  
শেষশ্চ যস্যাংশকলাঃ স নিত্যা-  
নন্দাখ্যরামঃ শরণং মমাস্তু ॥ ৭ ॥

saṅkarṣaṇaḥ kāraṇa-toya-śāyī  
Saṅkarṣaṇa  Kāraṇodakaśāyī  
garbhoda-śāyī ca payo’bdhi-śāyī  
Garbhodakaśāyī  and  Kṣīrodakaśāyī  
śeṣaś ca yasyāṁśa-kalāḥ sa nityā-  
and Śeṣa  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-  
nandākhya-rāmaḥ śaraṇaṁ mamāstu  
-nanda named  Rāma  refuge  my  may be

(7) “Saṅkarṣaṇa, the Lord who lies in the Causal Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Garbha Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Ocean of Milk, and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions: may that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my refuge.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^6]

মায়াতীতে ব্যাপিবৈকুণ্ঠলোকে  
পূর্ণৈশ্বর্য্যে শ্রীচতুর্ব্যূহমধ্যে ৷  
রূপং যস্যোদ্ভাতি সঙ্কর্ষণাখ্যং  
তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৮ ॥

māyātīte vyāpi-vaikuṇṭha-loke  
beyond māyā  in all-pervading  Vaikuṇṭha  world  
pūrṇaiśvarye śrī-catur-vyūha-madhye  
full of opulence  amid the fourfold expansion  
rūpaṁ yasyodbhāti saṅkarṣaṇākhyaṁ  
form  whose  shines forth  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(8) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma, whose form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines amid the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha) in the all-pervading world of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence and beyond māyā.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)

মায়াভর্ত্তাজাণ্ডসঙ্ঘাশ্রয়াঙ্গঃ  
শেতে সাক্ষাৎ কারণাম্ভোধিমধ্যে ৷  
যস্যৈকাংশঃ শ্রীপুমানদিদেব-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৯ ॥

māyā-bhartājāṇḍa-saṅghāśrayāṅgaḥ  
māyā’s  master  of universes  multitudes  shelter  whose body  
śete sākṣāt kāraṇāmbhodhi-madhye  
lies  in person  of the Causal  Ocean  in the midst  
yasyaikāṁśaḥ śrī-pumān ādi-devas  
whose  one part  the Puruṣa  the original  Lord  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(9) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of Him is the original Lord, the Puruṣa, the master of māyā, who lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean, His body the shelter of countless universes.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)

যস্যাংশাংশঃ শ্রীল-গর্ভোদশায়ী  
যন্নাভ্যব্জং লোকসঙ্ঘাতনালম্ ৷  
লোকস্রষ্টুঃ সূতিকাধামধাতু-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১০ ॥

yasyāṁśāṁśaḥ śrīla-garbhoda-śāyī  
whose  part of a part  the blessed  Garbhodakaśāyī  
yan-nābhy-abjaṁ loka-saṅghāta-nālam  
whose  navel-lotus  of the worlds  totality  as its stem  
loka-sraṣṭuḥ sūtikā-dhāma dhātus  
of the creator of the worlds  of birth  the place  of Brahmā  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(10) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of His part is the blessed Garbhodakaśāyī, whose navel-lotus, with all the worlds as its stem, is the birthplace of Brahmā, the creator of the worlds.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^7]

যস্যাংশাংশাংশঃ পরাত্মাখিলানাং  
পোষ্টা বিষ্ণুর্ভাতি দুগ্ধাব্ধিশায়ী ৷  
ক্ষৌণীভর্ত্তা যৎকলা সোঽপ্যনন্ত-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১১ ॥

yasyāṁśāṁśāṁśaḥ parātmākhilānāṁ  
whose  part of a part of a part  the Supersoul  of all  
poṣṭā viṣṇur bhāti dugdhābdhi-śāyī  
the maintainer  Viṣṇu  appears  lying on the Ocean of Milk  
kṣauṇī-bhartā yat-kalā so ’py anantas  
of the earth  the upholder  whose part  He  also  Ananta  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(11) “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of a part of His part is Viṣṇu who lies on the Ocean of Milk, the Supersoul and maintainer of all beings; and Ananta, who upholds the earth, is a portion of that Viṣṇu.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^8]

মহাবিষ্ণুর্জগৎকর্ত্তা মায়য়া যঃ সৃজত্যদঃ ৷  
তস্যাবতার এবায়মদ্বৈতাচার্য্য ঈশ্বরঃ ॥ ১২ ॥

mahā-viṣṇur jagat-kartā  
Mahā-Viṣṇu  of the world  the maker  
māyayā yaḥ sṛjaty adaḥ  
by māyā  who  creates  this (world)  
tasyāvatāra evāyam  
His  avatāra  indeed  this  
advaitācārya īśvaraḥ  
Advaita Ācārya  the Lord

(12) Mahā-Viṣṇu is the maker of the world, who creates it through māyā; and this Lord Advaita Ācārya is His avatāra.[^9]

অদ্বৈতং হরিণাদ্বৈতাদচার্য্যং ভক্তিশংসনাৎ ৷  
ভক্তাবতারমীশং তমদ্বৈতাচার্য্যমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ১৩ ॥

advaitaṁ hariṇādvaitād  
Advaita  from Hari  being non-different  
ācāryaṁ bhakti-śaṁsanāt  
Ācārya  bhakti  from teaching  
bhaktāvatāram īśaṁ tam  
as a devotee descended  the Lord  Him  
advaitācāryam āśraye  
Advaita Ācārya  I take shelter of

(13) He is called Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, and Ācārya because He teaches bhakti. I take shelter of Him, the Lord Advaita Ācārya, who has descended as a devotee.

পঞ্চতত্ত্বাত্মকং কৃষ্ণং ভক্তরূপস্বরূপকম্ ৷  
ভক্তাবতারং ভক্তাখ্যং নমামি ভক্তশক্তিকম্ ॥ ১৪ ॥

pañca-tattvātmakaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ  
of five truths  composed  Kṛṣṇa  
bhakta-rūpa-svarūpakam  
devotee in form  devotee in essence  
bhaktāvatāraṁ bhaktākhyaṁ  
the devotee-avatāra  called a devotee  
namāmi bhakta-śaktikam  
I bow to  the devotional potency

(14) “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who is manifest as five truths — the Pañca-tattva: as a devotee in form and a devotee in essence, as the incarnation of a devotee, as the one called a devotee, and as the potency of devotion.” (Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī)[^10]

জয়তাং সুরতৌ পঙ্গোর্মম মন্দমতের্গতী ৷  
মৎসর্ব্বস্বপদাম্ভোজৌ রাধামদনমোহনৌ ॥ ১৫ ॥

jayatāṁ suratau paṅgor  
glory  to the merciful (Pair)  of the lame  
mama manda-mater gatī  
of me  of dull mind  the refuge  
mat-sarvasva-padāmbhojau  
whose lotus feet are my all  
rādhā-madana-mohanau  
Rādhā  and Madana-mohana

(15) All glory to the merciful Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Madana-mohana! I am lame and dull of mind, and They are my only refuge; Their lotus feet are all I have.[^11]

দীব্যদ্বৃন্দারণ্যকল্পদ্রুমাধঃ-  
শ্রীমদ্রত্নাগারসিংহাসনস্থৌ ৷  
শ্রীশ্রীরাধা-শ্রীলগোবিন্দদেবৌ  
প্রেষ্ঠালীভিঃ সেব্যমানৌ স্মরামি ॥ ১৬ ॥

dīvyad-vṛndāraṇya-kalpa-drumādhaḥ-  
shining  in Vṛndāvana forest  wish-fulfilling  tree  beneath  
śrīmad-ratnāgāra-siṁhāsana-sthau  
in a beautiful  jewel temple  on a lion-throne  seated  
śrī śrī rādhā-śrīla-govinda-devau  
Śrī Śrī  Rādhā  and Śrīla  Govinda  the Deities  
preṣṭhālībhiḥ sevyamānau smarāmi  
by Their dearest friends  being served  I remember

(16) I remember Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Śrī Govindadeva, seated on a throne in a beautiful temple of jewels beneath a wish-fulfilling tree in the radiant forest of Vṛndāvana, served by Their dearest companions.[^12]

শ্রীমান্ রাসরসারম্ভী বংশীবটতটস্থিতঃ ৷  
কর্ষন্ বেণুস্বনৈর্গোপীর্গোপীনাথঃ শ্রিয়েঽস্তু নঃ ॥ ১৭ ॥

śrīmān rāsa-rasārambhī  
the beautiful  of the rāsa dance’s  sweetness  the initiator  
vaṁśīvaṭa-taṭa-sthitaḥ  
of Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing  
karṣan veṇu-svanair gopīr  
drawing  with the flute’s  sounds  the gopīs  
gopī-nāthaḥ śriye ’stu naḥ  
of the gopīs  the Lord  for good  may be  ours

(17) May the beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha, who begins the sweet rāsa dance, be our good fortune. Standing on the bank by Vaṁśīvaṭa, He draws the gopīs to Himself with the sounds of His flute.[^13]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ১৮ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host

(18) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এই তিন ঠাকুর গৌড়ীয়াকে করিয়াছেন আত্মসাৎ ৷  
এ তিনের চরণ বন্দোঁ, তিনে মোর নাথ ॥ ১৯ ॥

ei tina ṭhākura gauḍīyāke kariyāchena ātmasāt  
these  three  Deities  the Gauḍīyas  have made  Their own  
e tinera caraṇa vandom̐, tine mora nātha  
of these  three  the feet  I worship;  the Three  my  Lords

(19) These three Deities have made the Gauḍīya devotees Their own. I worship Their feet: these Three are my Lords.[^14]

গ্রন্থের আরম্ভে করি মঙ্গলাচরণ ৷  
গুরু, বৈষ্ণব, ভগবান্ — তিনের স্মরণ ॥ ২০ ॥

granthera ārambhe kari maṅgalācaraṇa  
of the book  at the beginning  I make  the auspicious invocation  
guru, vaiṣṇava, bhagavān — tinera smaraṇa  
guru,  Vaiṣṇava,  Bhagavān —  of the three  remembrance

(20) At the beginning of the book I make the auspicious invocation — the remembrance of three: the Guru, the Vaiṣṇavas and Bhagavān.

তিনের স্মরণে হয় বিঘ্নবিনাশন ৷  
অনায়াসে হয় নিজ বাঞ্চিতপূরণ ॥ ২১ ॥

tinera smaraṇe haya vighna-vināśana  
of the three  by remembrance  comes  of obstacles  destruction  
anāyāse haya nija vāñcita-pūraṇa  
easily  comes  one’s own  desire’s  fulfilment

(21) Remembering these three, one overcomes all obstacles and easily attains one’s heart’s desire.

সে মঙ্গলাচরণ হয় ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
বস্তুনির্দ্দেশ, আশীর্ব্বাদ, নমস্কার ॥ ২২ ॥

se maṅgalācaraṇa haya tri-vidha prakāra  
that  invocation  is  of three  kinds  
vastu-nirdeśa, āśīrvāda, namaskāra  
indication of the subject,  benediction,  obeisance

(22) This invocation is of three kinds: indicating the subject, giving blessing and offering obeisance.

প্রথম দুই শ্লোকে ইষ্টদেব-নমস্কার ৷  
সামান্য-বিশেষ-রূপে দুই ত’ প্রকার ॥ ২৩ ॥

prathama dui śloke iṣṭa-deva-namaskāra  
in the first  two  verses  to the worshipful Lord  obeisance  
sāmānya-viśeṣa-rūpe dui ta’ prakāra  
general  and specific  in form  two  indeed  kinds

(23) In the first two verses is the obeisance to the worshipful Lord, in two ways — general and specific.

তৃতীয় শ্লোকেতে করি বস্তুর নির্দ্দেশ ৷  
যাহা হৈতে জানি পরতত্ত্বের উদ্দেশ ॥ ২৪ ॥

tṛtīya ślokete kari vastura nirdeśa  
in the third  verse  I make  of the subject  indication  
yāhā haite jāni para-tattvera uddeśa  
by which  is known  of the Supreme Truth  the indication

(24) In the third verse I indicate the subject, through which the Supreme Truth is made known.

চতুর্থ শ্লোকেতে করি জগতে আশীর্ব্বাদ ৷  
সর্ব্বত্র মাগিয়ে কৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-প্রসাদ ॥ ২৫ ॥

caturtha ślokete kari jagate āśīrvāda  
in the fourth  verse  I give  to the world  blessing  
sarvatra māgiye kṛṣṇa-caitanya-prasāda  
everywhere  I beg for  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s  grace

(25) In the fourth verse I bless the world, praying that the grace of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya may reach all.

সেই শ্লোকে কহি বাহ্যাবতার-কারণ ৷  
পঞ্চ ষষ্ঠ শ্লোকে কহি মূল-প্রয়োজন ॥ ২৬ ॥

sei śloke kahi bāhyāvatāra-kāraṇa  
in that  verse  I tell  of the external descent  the cause  
pañca ṣaṣṭha śloke kahi mūla-prayojana  
in the fifth  and sixth  verses  I tell  the root  purpose

(26) In that verse I also tell the external reason for His descent; in the fifth and sixth verses I tell its root purpose.

এই ছয় শ্লোকে কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যের তত্ত্ব ৷  
আর পঞ্চ শ্লোকে নিত্যানন্দের মহত্ত্ব ॥ ২৭ ॥

ei chaya śloke kṛṣṇa caitanyera tattva  
in these  six  verses  of Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  the truth  
āra pañca śloke nityānandera mahattva  
and in five  verses  of Nityānanda  the greatness

(27) In these six verses is the truth of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and in the next five, the greatness of Śrī Nityānanda.

আর দুই শ্লোকে অদ্বৈত-তত্ত্বাখ্যান ৷  
আর এক শ্লোকে পঞ্চতত্ত্বের ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ২৮ ॥

āra dui śloke advaita-tattvākhyāna  
then in two  verses  of Advaita  the truth  the account  
āra eka śloke pañca-tattvera vyākhyāna  
then in one  verse  of the Pañca-tattva  the explanation

(28) Then two verses tell the truth of Advaita, and one verse explains the Pañca-tattva.

এই চৌদ্দশ্লোকে করি মঙ্গলাচরণ ৷  
তহিঁ মধ্যে কহি সব বস্তুনিরূপণ ॥ ২৯ ॥

ei caudda śloke kari maṅgalācaraṇa  
in these  fourteen  verses  I make  the invocation  
tahim̐ madhye kahi saba vastu-nirūpaṇa  
within  them  I tell  the whole  subject’s  description

(29) In these fourteen verses I make the auspicious invocation, and within them I describe the whole subject.

সব শ্রোতা-বৈষ্ণবেরে করি’ নমস্কার ৷  
এই সব শ্লোকের করি অর্থ-বিচার ॥ ৩০ ॥

saba śrotā-vaiṣṇavere kari’ namaskāra  
to all  listening  Vaiṣṇavas  offering  obeisance  
ei saba ślokera kari artha-vicāra  
of all these  verses  I make  of the meaning  consideration

(30) Offering obeisance to all the Vaiṣṇavas who hear this, I shall now consider the meaning of all these verses.

সকল বৈষ্ণব, শুন করি’ একমন ৷  
চৈতন্য-কৃষ্ণের শাস্ত্র যে মত নিরূপণ ॥ ৩১ ॥

sakala vaiṣṇava, śuna kari’ eka-mana  
all  Vaiṣṇavas,  hear  making  one mind  
caitanya-kṛṣṇera śāstra ye mata nirūpaṇa  
of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the scriptures  as  in view  establish

(31) O Vaiṣṇavas, hear with one-pointed mind how the scriptures establish the truth of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa.[^15]

কৃষ্ণ, গুরুদ্বয়, ভক্ত, অবতার, প্রকাশ ৷  
শক্তি, — এই ছয় রূপে করেন বিলাস ॥ ৩২ ॥

kṛṣṇa, gurudvaya, bhakta, avatāra, prakāśa  
Kṛṣṇa,  the two gurus,  devotees,  avatāras,  manifestations  
śakti, — ei chaya rūpe karena vilāsa  
potencies —  in these  six  forms  He enjoys

(32) Kṛṣṇa, the two kinds of guru, the devotees, the avatāras, the manifestations and the potencies — in these six forms He enjoys His pastimes.[^16]

এই ছয় তত্ত্বের করি চরণ বন্দন ৷  
প্রথমে সামান্যে করি মঙ্গলাচরণ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ei chaya tattvera kari caraṇa vandana  
of these  six  truths  I make  to the feet  worship  
prathame sāmānye kari maṅgalācaraṇa  
first  in general  I make  the invocation

(33) I worship the feet of these six truths; first I make the invocation in general.

বন্দে গুরূনীশভক্তানীশমীশাবতারকান্ ৷  
তৎপ্রকাশাংশ্চ তচ্চক্তীঃ কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যসংজ্ঞকম্ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān  
I worship  the gurus,  the Lord’s devotees  
īśam īśāvatārakān  
the Lord,  the Lord’s avatāras  
tat-prakāśāṁś ca tac-chaktīḥ  
His manifestations  and  His potencies  
kṛṣṇa-caitanya-saṁjñakam  
Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  named

(34) I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, His avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies — and the Lord Himself, who is known as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.

মন্ত্রগুরু আর যত শিক্ষাগুরুগণ ৷  
তাঁহার চরণ আগে করিয়ে বন্দন ॥ ৩৫ ॥

mantra-guru āra yata śikṣā-guru-gaṇa  
mantra-guru  and  all  instructing  gurus  
tām̐hāra caraṇa āge kariye vandana  
their  feet  first  I make  obeisance

(35) First I offer obeisance at the feet of my mantra-guru and of all my instructing gurus.[^17]

শ্রীরূপ, সনাতন, ভট্ট রঘুনাথ ৷  
শ্রীজীব, গোপালভট্ট, দাস রঘুনাথ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

śrī-rūpa, sanātana, bhaṭṭa raghunātha  
Śrī  Rūpa,  Sanātana,  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha  
śrī-jīva, gopāla-bhaṭṭa, dāsa raghunātha  
Śrī  Jīva,  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa,  Dāsa  Raghunātha

(36) Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha Dāsa —

এই ছয় গুরু — শিক্ষাগুরু যে আমার ৷  
তাঁ’ সবার পাদপদ্মে কোটি নমস্কার ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ei chaya guru — śikṣā-guru ye āmāra  
these  six  gurus —  instructing gurus  who are  mine  
tām̐’ sabāra pāda-padme koṭi namaskāra  
of all of them  at the lotus feet  ten million  obeisances

(37) these six are my instructing gurus; at the lotus feet of them all I offer millions of obeisances.

ভগবানের ভক্ত যত শ্রীবাস প্রধান ৷  
তাঁহার চরণপদ্মে সহস্র প্রণাম ॥ ৩৮ ॥

bhagavānera bhakta yata śrīvāsa pradhāna  
of the Lord  devotees  all  Śrīvāsa  the chief  
tām̐hāra caraṇa-padme sahasra praṇāma  
their  at the lotus feet  a thousand  obeisances

(38) To all the devotees of the Lord, of whom Śrīvāsa is the chief, I offer thousands of obeisances at their lotus feet.

অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য — প্রভুর অংশ-অবতার ৷  
তাঁর পাদপদ্মে কোটি প্রণতি আমার ॥ ৩৯ ॥

advaita ācārya — prabhura aṁśa-avatāra  
Advaita  Ācārya —  of the Lord  partial  avatāra  
tām̐ra pāda-padme koṭi praṇati āmāra  
at His  lotus feet  ten million  obeisances  mine

(39) Advaita Ācārya is a partial avatāra of the Lord; at His lotus feet I offer millions of obeisances.

নিত্যানন্দরায় — প্রভুর স্বরূপপ্রকাশ ৷  
তাঁর পাদপদ্ম বন্দো য়াঁর মুঞি দাস ॥ ৪০ ॥

nityānanda-rāya — prabhura svarūpa-prakāśa  
Nityānanda  Rāya —  of the Lord  own-form  manifestation  
tām̐ra pāda-padma vando yām̐ra muñi dāsa  
His  lotus feet  I worship  whose  I  servant

(40) Śrī Nityānanda Rāya is the Lord’s own direct manifestation (*svarūpa-prakāśa*). I worship His lotus feet: I am His servant.

গদাধর পণ্ডিতাদি — প্রভুর নিজশক্তি ৷  
তাঁ-সবার চরণে মোর সহস্র প্রণতি ॥ ৪১ ॥

gadādhara paṇḍitādi — prabhura nija-śakti  
Gadādhara  Paṇḍita and others —  of the Lord  own  potencies  
tām̐-sabāra caraṇe mora sahasra praṇati  
of all of them  at the feet  my  thousand  obeisances

(41) Gadādhara Paṇḍita and the others are the Lord’s own potencies; at the feet of them all I offer thousands of obeisances.

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য প্রভু স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তাঁহার পদারবিন্দে অনন্ত প্রণাম ॥ ৪২ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya prabhu svayaṁ bhagavān  
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  Prabhu  Himself  Bhagavān  
tām̐hāra padāravinde ananta praṇāma  
His  at the lotus feet  endless  obeisances

(42) Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is Bhagavān Himself; at His lotus feet I offer endless obeisances.

সাবরণে প্রভুরে করিয়া নমস্কার ৷  
এই ছয় তেঁহো যৈছে — করিয়ে বিচার ॥ ৪৩ ॥

sāvaraṇe prabhure kariyā namaskāra  
with His entourage  to the Lord  having made  obeisance  
ei chaya tem̐ho yaiche — kariye vicāra  
these  six  He  how —  I make  consideration

(43) Having bowed to the Lord together with His entourage, I shall now consider how He Himself is these six.[^18]

যদ্যপি আমার গুরু — চৈতন্যের দাস ৷  
তথাপি জানিয়ে আমি তাঁহার প্রকাশ ॥ ৪৪ ॥

yadyapi āmāra guru — caitanyera dāsa  
although  my  guru —  of Caitanya  a servant  
tathāpi jāniye āmi tām̐hāra prakāśa  
still  know  I  His  manifestation

(44) Although my Guru is a servant of Śrī Caitanya, I know him to be His manifestation.[^19]

গুরু কৃষ্ণরূপ হন শাস্ত্রের প্রমাণে ৷  
গুরুরূপে কৃষ্ণ কৃপা করেন ভক্তগণে ॥ ৪৫ ॥

guru kṛṣṇa-rūpa hana śāstrera pramāṇe  
the guru  Kṛṣṇa’s form  is  of scripture  by the evidence  
guru-rūpe kṛṣṇa kṛpā karena bhakta-gaṇe  
in the form of the guru  Kṛṣṇa  mercy  shows  to the devotees

(45) On the evidence of scripture the guru is a form of Kṛṣṇa: in the form of the guru, Kṛṣṇa bestows His mercy on the devotees.

আচার্য্যং মাং বিজানীয়ান্নাবমন্যেত কর্হিচিৎ ৷  
ন মর্ত্ত্যবুদ্ধ্যাসূয়েত সর্ব্বদেবময়ো গুরুঃ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

ācāryaṁ māṁ vijānīyān  
the ācārya  Me  one should know  
nāvamanyeta karhicit  
one should not disrespect  ever  
na martya-buddhyāsūyeta  
not  as a mortal  should envy  
sarva-deva-mayo guruḥ  
of all gods  composed  the guru

(46) “Know the ācārya to be Me. Never show him disrespect, and do not envy him, thinking him an ordinary mortal, for the Guru embodies all the gods.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.17.27)[^20]

শিক্ষাগুরুকে ত’ জানি কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ ৷  
অন্তর্য্যামী, ভক্তশ্রেষ্ঠ — এই দুই রূপ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

śikṣā-guruke ta’ jāni kṛṣṇera svarūpa  
the instructing guru  indeed  I know  Kṛṣṇa’s  own form  
antaryāmī, bhakta-śreṣṭha — ei dui rūpa  
the Indweller,  the best of devotees —  these  two  forms

(47) I know the instructing Guru to be Kṛṣṇa’s own form, in two aspects: the Indweller within, and the best of devotees.[^21]

নৈবোপয়ন্ত্যপচিতিং কবয়স্তবেশ  
ব্রহ্মায়ুষাপি কৃতমৃদ্ধমুদঃ স্মরন্তঃ ৷  
যোঽন্তর্বহিস্তনুভৃতামশুভং বিধুন্ব-  
ন্নাচার্য্য-চৈত্ত্যবপুষা স্বগতিং ব্যনক্তি ॥ ৪৮ ॥

naivopayanty apacitiṁ kavayas taveśa  
not  attain  repayment  the wise  Your,  O Lord  
brahmāyuṣāpi kṛtam ṛddha-mudaḥ smarantaḥ  
with Brahmā’s lifespan  even  Your deeds  with growing joy  remembering  
yo ’ntar bahis tanu-bhṛtām aśubhaṁ vidhunvann  
who  within  without  of the embodied  the inauspicious  dispelling  
ācārya-caitya-vapuṣā sva-gatiṁ vyanakti  
as ācārya  and Supersoul  in forms  Your own path  reveal

(48) “O Lord! Even if the wise lived as long as Brahmā and remembered with ever-growing joy all that You have done for them, they could not repay their debt to You. For, dispelling all that is inauspicious in embodied souls, You come to them from without as the ācārya and from within as the Supersoul, and reveal to them the way to Yourself.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.29.6)[^22]

তেষাং সততযুক্তানাং ভজতাং প্রীতিপূর্ব্বকম্ ৷  
দদামি বুদ্ধিযোগং তং যেন মামুপয়ান্তি তে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

teṣāṁ satata-yuktānāṁ  
to them  ever  longing (for Me)  
bhajatāṁ prīti-pūrvakam  
worshipping  with love  
dadāmi buddhi-yogaṁ taṁ  
I give  understanding  that  
yena mām upayānti te  
by which  to Me  come  they

(49) “To those who are ever devoted to Me and worship Me with love, I give the understanding that brings them to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 10.10)[^23]

যথা ভগবান্ ব্রহ্মণে স্বয়মুপদিশ্যানুভাবিতবান্ ॥ ৫০ ॥

yathā bhagavān brahmaṇe  
as  the Lord  to Brahmā  
svayam upadiśyānubhāvitavān  
Himself  having instructed  made him realise

(50) “Thus the Lord Himself instructed Brahmā and made him realise it.”

জ্ঞানং পরমগুহ্যং মে যদ্বিজ্ঞান-সমন্বিতম্ ৷  
সরহস্যং তদঙ্গঞ্চ গৃহাণ গদিতং ময়া ॥ ৫১ ॥

jñānaṁ parama-guhyaṁ me  
knowledge  most  confidential  of Me  
yad vijñāna-samanvitam  
which  with realisation  endowed  
sa-rahasyaṁ tad-aṅgaṁ ca  
with its secret  and its  limb  also  
gṛhāṇa gaditaṁ mayā  
receive  spoken  by Me

(51) “Receive from Me, as I speak it, the supremely confidential knowledge of Myself, endowed with realisation, together with its secret and that secret’s integral part.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.9.31)[^24]

যাবানহং যথাভাবো যদ্রূপগুণকর্ম্মকঃ ৷  
তথৈব তত্ত্ববিজ্ঞানমস্তু তে মদনুগ্রহাৎ ॥ ৫২ ॥

yāvān ahaṁ yathā-bhāvo  
as great as  I am,  in what being  
yad-rūpa-guṇa-karmakaḥ  
with what forms  qualities  and deeds  
tathaiva tattva-vijñānam  
just so  the true  realisation  
astu te mad-anugrahāt  
may be  yours  by My grace

(52) “By My grace may you truly realise Me as I am — My being, My forms, My qualities and My deeds.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.9.32)

অহমেবাসমেবাগ্রে নান্যৎ যৎ সদসৎপরম্ ৷  
পশ্চাদহং যদেতচ্চ যোঽবশিষ্যেত সোঽস্ম্যহম্ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

aham evāsam evāgre  
I  alone  was  indeed  before (creation)  
nānyat yat sad-asat-param  
nothing else  which  effect  cause  beyond  
paścād ahaṁ yad etac ca  
afterwards  I  what  this (world)  also  
yo ‘vaśiṣyeta so ‘smy aham  
what  remains  that  am  I

(53) “Before creation I alone existed; there was nothing else — neither effect, nor cause, nor That which is beyond them. After creation this whole world is not other than Me, and what remains after its dissolution is also I.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.9.33)[^25]

ঋতেঽর্থং যৎ প্রতীয়েত ন প্রতীয়েত চাত্মনি ৷  
তদ্বিদ্যাদত্মনো মায়াং যথাভাসো যথা তমঃ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

ṛte ’rthaṁ yat pratīyeta  
without  the reality  what  may appear  
na pratīyeta cātmani  
not  may appear  and  in Me  
tad vidyād ātmano māyāṁ  
that  one should know  My  māyā  
yathābhāso yathā tamaḥ  
as  the reflection  as  the darkness

(54) “Whatever appears to exist apart from Me, the Reality, and does not appear in Me, know that to be My māyā, like a reflection and like darkness.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.9.34)[^26]

যথা মহান্তি ভূতানি ভূতেষূচ্চাবচেষ্বনু ৷  
প্রবিষ্টান্যপ্রবিষ্টানি তথা তেষু ন তেষ্বহম্ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

yathā mahānti bhūtāni  
as  the great  elements  
bhūteṣūccāvaceṣv anu  
in beings  great and small  after  
praviṣṭāny apraviṣṭāni  
entered  not entered  
tathā teṣu na teṣv aham  
so  in them  not  in them  I

(55) “As the great elements enter all beings, great and small, and yet remain outside them, so I am within all that is created and yet beyond it.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.9.35)[^27]

এতাবদেব জিজ্ঞাস্যং তত্ত্বজিজ্ঞাসুনাত্মনঃ ৷  
অন্বয়-ব্যতিরেকাভ্যাং যৎ স্যাৎ সর্ব্বত্র সর্ব্বদা ॥ ৫৬ ॥

etāvad eva jijñāsyaṁ  
so much  indeed  to be inquired  
tattva-jijñāsunātmanaḥ  
by one seeking the truth  of the Self  
anvaya-vyatirekābhyāṁ  
positively  and negatively  
yat syāt sarvatra sarvadā  
which  may be  everywhere  always

(56) “This much must be inquired into by one who seeks the truth of the Self: That which exists everywhere and always, both directly and indirectly.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.9.36)[^28]

চিন্তামণির্জয়তি সোমগিরির্গুরুর্মে  
শিক্ষাগুরুশ্চ ভগবান্ শিখিপিঞ্ছমৌলিঃ ৷  
যৎপাদকল্পতরুপল্লবশেখরেষু  
লীলাস্বয়ম্বররসং লভতে জয়শ্রীঃ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

cintāmaṇir jayati somagirir gurur me  
Cintāmaṇi  glory  Somagiri  guru  my  
śikṣā-guruś ca bhagavān śikhi-piñcha-mauliḥ  
instructing guru  and  the Lord  with peacock feather  crowned  
yat-pāda-kalpataru-pallava-śekhareṣu  
whose  feet  wish-tree  sprouts  at the tips  
līlā-svayaṁvara-rasaṁ labhate jayaśrīḥ  
in pastime  self-choice  the rasa  attains  Jayaśrī

(57) “Glory to Cintāmaṇi and to Somagiri, my guru! Glory to my instructing guru, the Lord whose crown is a peacock feather! At the tips of His feet, like the tender shoots of a wish-fulfilling tree, Jayaśrī (Śrī Rādhā) tastes the joy of choosing Him as Her own.” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 1)[^29]

জীবে সাক্ষাৎ নাহি তাতে গুরু চৈত্যরূপে ৷  
শিক্ষাগুরু হয় কৃষ্ণ মহান্তস্বরূপে ॥ ৫৮ ॥

jīve sākṣāt nāhi tāte guru caitya-rūpe  
to the soul  directly  not (seen)  therefore  guru  as the Indweller  
śikṣā-guru haya kṛṣṇa mahānta-svarūpe  
instructing guru  is  Kṛṣṇa  as a great devotee  in form

(58) The soul cannot see Kṛṣṇa directly; therefore He is the guru within as the Indweller, and becomes the instructing guru in the form of a great devotee.

ততো দুঃসঙ্গমুৎসৃজ্য সৎসু সজ্জেত বুদ্ধিমান্ ৷  
সন্ত এবাস্য ছিন্দন্তি মনোব্যাসঙ্গমুক্তিভিঃ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

tato duḥsaṅgam utsṛjya  
therefore  bad company  having given up  
satsu sajjeta buddhi-mān  
with the saintly  should associate  the wise  
santa evāsya chindanti  
the saints  alone  his  cut  
mano-vyāsaṅgam uktibhiḥ  
the mind’s  attachment  by their words

(59) “Therefore the wise should give up bad company and associate with the saintly, for the saints alone, by their words, cut through the attachments of his mind.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.26.26)

সতাং প্রসঙ্গান্মম বীর্য্যসংবিদো  
ভবন্তি হৃৎকর্ণরসায়নাঃ কথাঃ ৷  
তজ্জোষণাদাশ্বপবর্গবর্ত্মনি  
শ্রদ্ধা রতির্ভক্তিরনুক্রমিষ্যতি ॥ ৬০ ॥

satāṁ prasaṅgān mama vīrya-saṁvido  
of the saints  in the company  My  prowess  revealing  
bhavanti hṛt-karṇa-rasāyanāḥ kathāḥ  
become  to heart and ear  delightful  talks  
taj-joṣaṇād āśv apavarga-vartmani  
by relishing them  quickly  on the path of liberation  
śraddhā ratir bhaktir anukramiṣyati  
faith,  attraction,  love  will follow in turn

(60) (Lord Kapila to His mother Devahūti:) “In the company of the saints there are talks revealing My power, delightful to the heart and to the ear. By relishing them one quickly comes to faith in Me, the path of liberation, and after it to attraction (*rati*) and love (*prema-bhakti*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.25)[^30]

ঈশ্বরস্বরূপ ভক্ত তাঁর অধিষ্ঠান ৷  
ভক্তের হৃদয়ে কৃষ্ণের সতত বিশ্রাম ॥ ৬১ ॥

īśvara-svarūpa bhakta tām̐ra adhiṣṭhāna  
of the Lord  own form  the devotee  His  abode  
bhaktera hṛdaye kṛṣṇera satata viśrāma  
of the devotee  in the heart  of Kṛṣṇa  always  resting place

(61) The devotee is the Lord’s own form, His abode: in the devotee’s heart Kṛṣṇa always rests.[^31]

সাধবো হৃদয়ং মহ্যং সাধূনাং হৃদয়ম্ত্বহম্ ৷  
মদন্যত্তে ন জানন্তি নাহং তেভ্যো মনাগপি ॥ ৬২ ॥

sādhavo hṛdayaṁ mahyaṁ  
the saints  heart  My  
sādhūnāṁ hṛdayaṁ tv aham  
of the saints  heart  indeed  I  
mad-anyat te na jānanti  
other than Me  they  not  know  
nāhaṁ tebhyo manāg api  
not  I  than them  even slightly

(62) “The saints are My heart, and I am the heart of the saints. They know no one but Me, and I know no one but them.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.4.68)

ভবদ্বিধা ভাগবতাস্তীর্থভূতাঃ স্বয়ং প্রভো ৷  
তীর্থীকুর্ব্বন্তি তীর্থানি স্বান্তঃস্থেন গদাভৃতা ॥ ৬৩ ॥

bhavad-vidhā bhāgavatās  
like you  devotees  
tīrtha-bhūtāḥ svayaṁ prabho  
holy places  being  yourselves,  O lord  
tīrthī-kurvanti tīrthāni  
make holy  the holy places  
svāntaḥ-sthena gadā-bhṛtā  
by Him within  who bears the club

(63) “Devotees such as you are themselves holy places, my lord. By the Lord who dwells within your hearts, you make even the holy places holy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.13.10)[^32]

সেই ভক্তগণ হয় দ্বিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
পারিষদ্গণ এক, সাধকগণ আর ॥ ৬৪ ॥

sei bhakta-gaṇa haya dvi-vidha prakāra  
those  devotees  are  of two  kinds  
pāriṣad-gaṇa eka, sādhaka-gaṇa āra  
associates  one,  practitioners  other

(64) These devotees are of two kinds: the Lord’s eternal associates and those who practise devotion.

ঈশ্বরের অবতার এ-তিন প্রকার ৷  
অংশ-অবতার, আর গুণ-অবতার ॥ ৬৫ ॥

শক্ত্যাবেশ-অবতার — তৃতীয় এমত ৷  
অংশ-অবতার — পুরুষ-মৎস্যাদিক যত ॥ ৬৬ ॥

īśvarera avatāra e-tina prakāra  
of the Lord  avatāras  these three  kinds  
aṁśa-avatāra, āra guṇa-avatāra  
partial avatāras,  then  guṇa-avatāras  
śaktyāveśa-avatāra — tṛtīya e-mata  
empowered avatāras —  the third  thus  
aṁśa-avatāra — puruṣa-matsyādika yata  
partial avatāras —  the Puruṣas,  Matsya and others  all

(65–66) The Lord’s avatāras are of three kinds: partial avatāras, guṇa-avatāras, and, third, empowered avatāras. The partial avatāras are the Puruṣas, Matsya and the others.

ব্রহ্মা বিষ্ণু শিব — তিন গুণাবতারে গণি ৷  
শক্ত্যবেশ — সনকাদি, পৃথু, ব্যাসমুনি ॥ ৬৭ ॥

brahmā viṣṇu śiva — tina guṇāvatāre gaṇi  
Brahmā,  Viṣṇu,  Śiva —  three  as guṇa-avatāras  are counted  
śakty-āveśa — sanakādi, pṛthu, vyāsa-muni  
empowered —  the Kumāras,  Pṛthu,  Vyāsa  the sage

(67) Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva are counted as the three guṇa-avatāras; the Kumāras, Pṛthu and the sage Vyāsa are empowered avatāras.

দুইরূপে হয় ভগবানের প্রকাশ ৷  
একে ত’ প্রকাশ হয়, আরে ত’ বিলাস ॥ ৬৮ ॥

dui-rūpe haya bhagavānera prakāśa  
in two forms  is  of the Lord  manifestation  
eke ta’ prakāśa haya, āre ta’ vilāsa  
one  indeed  prakāśa  is,  the other  vilāsa

(68) The Lord manifests Himself in two ways: one is *prakāśa*, the other *vilāsa*.

একই বিগ্রহ যদি হয় বহুরূপ ৷  
আকারে ত’ ভেদ নাহি, একই স্বরূপ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

মহিষী-বিবাহে, যৈছে যৈছে কৈল রাস ৷  
ইহাকে কহিয়ে কৃষ্ণের মুখ্য ‘প্রকাশ’ ॥ ৭০ ॥

eka-i vigraha yadi haya bahu-rūpa  
one and the same  person  if  becomes  many forms  
ākāre ta’ bheda nāhi, eka-i svarūpa  
in appearance  no  difference,  one  identity  
mahiṣī-vivāhe, yaiche yaiche kaila rāsa  
at the queens’ marriage,  as  as  He did  the rāsa  
ihāke kahiye kṛṣṇera mukhya ‘prakāśa’  
this  is called  Kṛṣṇa’s  chief  prakāśa

(69–70) When one Person appears in many forms, alike in appearance and one in identity — as at the marriage of the queens or in the rāsa dance — this is called Kṛṣṇa’s chief *prakāśa*.

চিত্রং বতৈতদেকেন বপুষা যুগপৎ পৃথক্ ৷  
গৃহেষু দ্ব্যষ্টসাহস্রং স্ত্রিয় এক উদাবহৎ ॥ ৭১ ॥

citraṁ bataitad ekena  
wonderful  indeed  this  with one  
vapuṣā yugapat pṛthak  
body  at once  separately  
gṛheṣu dvy-aṣṭa-sāhasraṁ  
in palaces  sixteen thousand  
striya eka udāvahat  
women  the One  married

(71) “How wonderful! The one Kṛṣṇa, in one and the same body, married sixteen thousand princesses at once, each in her own palace.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.69.2)

রাসোৎসবঃ সংপ্রবৃত্তো গোপীমণ্ডলমণ্ডিতঃ ৷  
যোগেশ্বরেণ কৃষ্ণেন তাসাং মধ্যে দ্বয়োর্দ্বয়োঃ ॥ ৭২ ॥

rāsotsavaḥ sampravṛtto  
the rāsa festival  began  
gopī-maṇḍala-maṇḍitaḥ  
with circles of gopīs  adorned  
yogeśvareṇa kṛṣṇena  
by the Master of yoga  Kṛṣṇa  
tāsāṁ madhye dvayor dvayoḥ  
of them  between  each two

(72) “The festival of the rāsa dance began, adorned with circles of gopīs; Kṛṣṇa, the Master of yoga, stood between each two of them —” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.3)

প্রবিষ্টেন গৃহীতানাং কণ্ঠে স্বনিকটং স্ত্রিয়ঃ ৷  
যং মন্যেরন্নভস্তাবদ্বিমানশতসঙ্কুলম্ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

দিবৌকসাং সদারাণামত্যৌৎসুক্যভৃতাত্মনাম্ ৷  
ততো দুন্দুভয়ো নেদুর্নিপেতুঃ পুষ্পবৃষ্টয়ঃ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

praviṣṭena gṛhītānāṁ  
having entered,  of those embraced  
kaṇṭhe sva-nikaṭaṁ striyaḥ  
by the neck,  beside her  the women  
yaṁ manyeran nabhas tāvad  
whom  would think;  the sky  at once  
vimāna-śata-saṅkulam  
with hundreds of celestial cars  crowded  
divaukasāṁ sa-dārāṇām  
of the gods  with their wives  
atyautsukya-bhṛtātmanām  
with intense  eagerness  filled in mind  
tato dundubhayo nedur  
then  the drums  sounded  
nipetuḥ puṣpa-vṛṣṭayaḥ  
fell  showers of flowers

(73–74) “— having entered between them and embraced each by the neck, so that every girl thought Him beside her alone. At once the sky was crowded with hundreds of celestial cars of the gods and their wives, their hearts filled with intense eagerness. Then the drums sounded and showers of flowers fell.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.3–4)[^33]

অনেকত্র প্রকটতা রূপস্যৈকস্য যৈকদা ৷  
সর্ব্বথা তৎস্বরূপৈব স প্রকাশ ইতীর্য্যতে ॥ ৭৫ ॥

anekatra prakaṭatā  
in many places  manifestation  
rūpasyaikasya yaikadā  
of form  one  which  at one time  
sarvathā tat-svarūpaiva  
in every way  identical in nature  
sa prakāśa itīryate  
that  prakāśa  is called

(75) “When one form appears at one time in many places, identical in every way, that is called *prakāśa*.” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.21)

একই বিগ্রহ যদি আকারে হয় আন ৷  
অনেক প্রকাশ হয়, ‘বিলাস’ তার নাম ॥ ৭৬ ॥

eka-i vigraha yadi ākāre haya āna  
one and the same  person  if  in appearance  is  different  
aneka prakāśa haya, ‘vilāsa’ tāra nāma  
many  manifestations  are,  vilāsa  its  name

(76) But when one and the same Person appears in many forms that differ in appearance, that is called *vilāsa*.

স্বরূপমন্যাকারং যত্তস্য ভাতি বিলাসতঃ ৷  
প্রায়েণাত্মসমং শক্ত্যা স বিলাসো নিগদ্যতে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

svarūpam anyākāraṁ yat  
His own form  of different shape  which  
tasya bhāti vilāsataḥ  
of Him  appears  for pastimes  
prāyeṇātma-samaṁ śaktyā  
almost  equal to Him  by His potency  
sa vilāso nigadyate  
that  vilāsa  is called

(77) “When by His potency His own form appears for His pastimes in a different shape, almost equal to Him, it is called *vilāsa*.” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.15)[^34]

যৈছে বলদেব, পরব্যোমে নারায়ণ ৷  
যৈছে বাসুদেব প্রদ্যুম্নাদি সঙ্কর্ষণ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

yaiche baladeva, paravyome nārāyaṇa  
as  Baladeva,  in the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  
yaiche vāsudeva pradyumnādi saṅkarṣaṇa  
as  Vāsudeva,  Pradyumna and others,  Saṅkarṣaṇa

(78) Examples are Baladeva, Nārāyaṇa in the spiritual sky, and Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and the others.[^35]

ঈশ্বরের শক্তি হয় ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
এক লক্ষ্মীগণ, পুরে মহিষীগণ আর ॥ ৭৯ ॥

ব্রজে গোপীগণ আর সবাতে প্রধান ৷  
ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন যা’তে স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ৮০ ॥

īśvarera śakti haya trividha prakāra  
of the Lord  potencies  are  of three  kinds  
eka lakṣmī-gaṇa, pure mahiṣī-gaṇa āra  
one  the Lakṣmīs,  in the city  the queens  another  
vraje gopī-gaṇa āra sabāte pradhāna  
in Vraja  the gopīs  and  of all  the chief  
vrajendra-nandana yā’te svayaṁ bhagavān  
Vraja’s king’s  son  since in them  Himself  Bhagavān

(79–80) The Lord’s potencies are of three kinds: the Lakṣmīs in Vaikuṇṭha, the queens in Dvārakā, and the gopīs in Vraja. The gopīs are the foremost of all, for with them the son of the king of Vraja is Bhagavān Himself.[^36]

স্বয়ংরূপ কৃষ্ণের কায়ব্যূহ — তাঁর সম ৷  
ভক্ত সহিতে হয় তাঁহার আবরণ ॥ ৮১ ॥

svayaṁ-rūpa kṛṣṇera kāya-vyūha — tām̐ra sama  
Kṛṣṇa’s original form’s  bodily expansions —  His  equals  
bhakta sahite haya tām̐hāra āvaraṇa  
the devotees  together with  are  His  entourage

(81) The expansions of the body of Kṛṣṇa’s original form are equal to Him, and together with the devotees they form His entourage.[^37]

ভক্ত আদি ক্রমে কৈল সবার বন্দন ৷  
এ-সবার বন্দন সর্ব্ব শুভের কারণ ॥ ৮২ ॥

bhakta ādi krame kaila sabāra vandana  
devotees  and the rest  in order  I made  of all  worship  
e-sabāra vandana sarva śubhera kāraṇa  
of these all  worship  of every  good  the cause

(82) Thus, beginning with the devotees, I have worshipped them all in order; worship of them all is the source of every good.

প্রথম শ্লোকে সামান্য মঙ্গলাচরণ ৷  
দ্বিতীয় শ্লোকেতে করি বিশেষ বন্দন ॥ ৮৩ ॥

prathama śloke sāmānya maṅgalācaraṇa  
in the first  verse  general  invocation  
dvitīya ślokete kari viśeṣa vandana  
in the second  verse  I make  specific  worship

(83) In the first verse is the general invocation; in the second I offer specific worship.

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যনিত্যানন্দৌ সহোদিতৌ ৷  
গৌড়োদয়ে পুষ্পবন্তৌ চিত্রৌ শন্দৌ তমোনুদৌ ॥ ৮৪ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
nityānandau sahoditau  
and Nityānanda  who rose together  
gauḍodaye puṣpavantau  
on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and moon  
citrau śan-dau tamo-nudau  
wonderful  bestowing good  dispelling darkness

(84) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Śrī Nityānanda, who are like a wonderful sun and moon. They have risen together on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa, dispelling the darkness and bestowing good on all.

ব্রজে যে বিহরে পূর্ব্বে কৃষ্ণ-বলরাম ৷  
কোটীসূর্য্যচন্দ্র জিনি’ দোঁহার নিজধাম ॥ ৮৫ ॥

সেই দুই জগতেরে হইয়ে সদয় ৷  
গৌড়দেশে পূর্ব্বশৈলে করিল উদয় ॥ ৮৬ ॥

vraje ye vihare pūrve kṛṣṇa-balarāma  
in Vraja  who  sported  before  Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma  
koṭī-sūrya-candra jini’ dom̐hāra nija-dhāma  
millions of suns and moons  surpassing  of both  own  effulgence  
sei dui jagatere haiye sadaya  
those  Two  to the world  becoming  compassionate  
gauḍadeśe pūrva-śaile karila udaya  
in Gauḍa land  on the eastern mountain  have risen

(85–86) Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma, who sported in Vraja long ago, Their radiance surpassing millions of suns and moons, have now, out of compassion for the world, risen on the eastern horizon in the land of Gauḍa.[^38]

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য আর প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
যাঁহার প্রকাশে সর্ব্ব জগৎ আনন্দ ॥ ৮৭ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya āra prabhu nityānanda  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  and  Prabhu  Nityānanda  
yām̐hāra prakāśe sarva jagat ānanda  
whose  by the appearance  the whole  world  joyful

(87) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Prabhu Nityānanda — by Their appearance the whole world is filled with joy.

সূর্য্যচন্দ্র হরে যৈছে সব অন্ধকার ৷  
বস্তু প্রকাশিয়া করে ধর্ম্মের প্রচার ॥ ৮৮ ॥

এইমত দুই ভাই জীবের অজ্ঞান-  
তমোনাশ করি’ কৈল তত্ত্ববস্তু-দান ॥ ৮৯ ॥

sūrya-candra hare yaiche saba andhakāra  
the sun and moon  remove  as  all  darkness  
vastu prakāśiyā kare dharmera pracāra  
things  revealing  make  of duty  the spreading  
ei mata dui bhāi jīvera ajñāna-  
in this way  the two  brothers  of the soul  of ignorance  
tamo-nāśa kari’ kaila tattva-vastu-dāna  
the darkness  destroying  made  of the Truth  the gift

(88–89) As the sun and moon remove all darkness and, revealing things, set the world about its duties, so these two Brothers dispelled the darkness of the soul’s ignorance and gave it the gift of the Truth.

অজ্ঞান-তমের নাম কহিয়ে ‘কৈতব’ ৷   
ধর্ম্ম-অর্থ-কাম-বাঞ্ছা আদি এই সব ॥ ৯০ ॥

ajñāna-tamera nāma kahiye ‘kaitava’  
of ignorance  of the darkness  the name  I call  ‘deceit’  
dharma-artha-kāma-vāñchā ādi ei saba  
religiosity,  wealth,  pleasure  desire  and so on  these  all

(90) I call this darkness of ignorance *kaitava*, deceit: it is the desire for religious merit, wealth, pleasure and the rest.[^39]

ধর্ম্মঃ প্রোজ্ঝিতকৈতবোঽত্র পরমো নির্ম্মৎসরাণাং সতাং  
বেদ্যং বাস্তবমত্র বস্তু শিবদং তাপত্রয়োন্মূলনম্ ৷  
শ্রীমদ্ভাগবতে মহামুনিকৃতে কিংবাপরৈরীশ্বরঃ  
সদ্যো হৃদ্যবরুধ্যতেঽত্র কৃতিভিঃ শুশ্রূষুভিস্তৎক্ষণাৎ ॥ ৯১ ॥

dharmaḥ projjhita-kaitavo ‘tra paramo nirmatsarāṇāṁ satāṁ  
dharma  wholly-cast-out  deceit  here  supreme  of the non-envious  of the saints  
vedyaṁ vāstavam atra vastu śiva-daṁ tāpa-trayonmūlanam  
to be known  real  here  substance  bestowing good  the threefold miseries  uprooting  
śrīmad-bhāgavate mahā-muni-kṛte kiṁ vā parair īśvaraḥ  
in the beautiful Bhāgavata  by the great sage  composed  what  by others?  the Lord  
sadyo hṛdy avarudhyate ‘tra kṛtibhiḥ śuśrūṣubhis tat-kṣaṇāt  
at once  in the heart  is captured  here  by the pious  who wish to hear  that very moment

(91) “In this beautiful *Bhāgavatam*, composed by the great sage, is taught the supreme dharma of the saintly who are free from envy, from which all deceit has been cast out. Here is known the true Reality, which bestows good and uproots the threefold miseries. What need is there of other scriptures? As soon as the pious wish to hear it, the Lord is at once captured within their hearts.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.2)[^40]

তার মধ্যে মোক্ষবাঞ্ছা কৈতব-প্রধান ৷  
যাহা হৈতে কৃষ্ণভক্তি হয় অন্তর্দ্ধান ॥ ৯২ ॥

tāra madhye mokṣa-vāñchā kaitava-pradhāna  
of them  among  liberation’s  desire  deceit’s  chief  
yāhā haite kṛṣṇa-bhakti haya antardhāna  
from which  Kṛṣṇa  devotion  is  disappearance

(92) Among them the chief deceit is the desire for liberation, by which devotion to Kṛṣṇa disappears.

“প্র-শব্দেন মোক্ষাভিসন্ধিরপি নিরস্তঃ” ইতি ॥ ৯৩ ॥

“pra-śabdena mokṣābhisandhir api nirastaḥ” iti  
by the word ‘pra’  of liberation  the intention  also  is excluded  thus

(93) “By the prefix *pra* even the intention of liberation is excluded.”[^41]

কৃষ্ণভক্তির বাধক — যত শুভাশুভ কর্ম্ম ৷  
সেই এক জীবের অজ্ঞানতমো-ধর্ম্ম ॥ ৯৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhaktira bādhaka — yata śubhāśubha karma  
to Kṛṣṇa-bhakti  obstacles —  all  good and bad  deeds  
sei eka jīvera ajñāna-tamo-dharma  
that  one  of the soul  of ignorance  darkness’  nature

(94) All good and bad deeds that obstruct devotion to Kṛṣṇa also belong to the soul’s darkness of ignorance.

যাঁহার প্রসাদে এই তমো হয় নাশ ৷  
তমো নাশ করি’ করে তত্ত্বের প্রকাশ ॥ ৯৫ ॥

yām̐hāra prasāde ei tamo haya nāśa  
whose  by the grace  this  darkness  is  destroyed  
tamo nāśa kari’ kare tattvera prakāśa  
darkness  destroying  make  of the Truth  the revelation

(95) By Their grace this darkness is destroyed, and, destroying it, They reveal the Truth.

তত্ত্ববস্তু — কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণভক্তি, প্রেমরূপ ৷  
নামসংকীর্ত্তন — সর্ব্ব আনন্দস্বরূপ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

tattva-vastu — kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-bhakti, prema-rūpa  
the Truth —  Kṛṣṇa,  Kṛṣṇa-bhakti  in the form of love  
nāma-saṅkīrtana — sarva ānanda-svarūpa  
the Name’s congregational chanting —  all  bliss  embodiment

(96) The Truth is Kṛṣṇa, devotion to Kṛṣṇa in the form of pure love (prema), and the congregational chanting of the Holy Name, the embodiment of all bliss.

সূর্য্য চন্দ্র বাহিরের তমঃ সে বিনাশে ৷  
বহির্ব্বস্তু ঘট পট আদি সে প্রকাশে ॥ ৯৭ ॥

sūrya candra bāhirera tamaḥ se vināśe  
the sun  the moon  external  darkness  they  destroy  
bahir-vastu ghaṭa paṭa ādi se prakāśe  
external things  pots  cloth  and so on  they  reveal

(97) The sun and moon dispel the outer darkness and reveal outer things — pots, cloth and the like.

দুই ভাই হৃদয়ের ক্ষালি’ অন্ধকার ৷  
দুই ভাগবত-সঙ্গে করান সাক্ষাৎকার ॥ ৯৮ ॥

dui bhāi hṛdayera kṣāli’ andhakāra  
the two  brothers  of the heart  washing away  the darkness  
dui bhāgavata-saṅge karāna sākṣātkāra  
two  bhāgavatas  with  bring about  meeting

(98) But the two Brothers wash away the darkness of the heart and bring one to meet the two *bhāgavatas*.

এক ভাগবত বড় — ভাগবতশাস্ত্র ৷  
আর ভাগবত — ভক্ত ভক্তি-রস-পাত্র ॥ ৯৯ ॥

eka bhāgavata baḍa — bhāgavata-śāstra  
one  bhāgavata  great —  the Bhāgavata  scripture  
āra bhāgavata — bhakta bhakti-rasa-pātra  
another  bhāgavata —  the devotee  of devotion’s  rasa  the vessel

(99) One *bhāgavata* is the great scripture *Bhāgavatam*; the other *bhāgavata* is the devotee, the vessel of the rasa of devotion.

দুই ভাগবত দ্বারা দিয়া ভক্তিরস ৷  
তাঁহার হৃদয়ে তাঁর প্রেমে হয় বশ ॥ ১০০ ॥

dui bhāgavata dvārā diyā bhakti-rasa  
two  bhāgavatas  through  giving  devotion’s rasa  
tām̐hāra hṛdaye tām̐ra preme haya vaśa  
his  in the heart  by his  love  is  subdued

(100) Through these two *bhāgavatas* They give the rasa of devotion, and the Lord, dwelling in the devotee’s heart, is held captive by his love.[^42]

এক অদ্ভুত — সমকালে দোঁহার প্রকাশ ৷  
আর অদ্ভুত — চিত্তগুহার তমঃ করে নাশ ॥ ১০১ ॥

eka adbhuta — sama-kāle dom̐hāra prakāśa  
one  wonder —  at the same time  of both  the appearance  
āra adbhuta — citta-guhāra tamaḥ kare nāśa  
another  wonder —  of the heart’s cave  the darkness  make  destruction

(101) One wonder is that the Two appeared at the same time; the other wonder is that They destroy the darkness in the cave of the heart.

এই চন্দ্র সূর্য্য দুই পরম সদয় ৷  
জগতের ভাগ্যে গৌড়ে করিল উদয় ॥ ১০২ ॥

ei candra sūrya dui parama sadaya  
this  moon  sun  both  supremely  kind  
jagatera bhāgye gauḍe karila udaya  
of the world  for the fortune  in Gauḍa  have  risen

(102) This moon and sun, supremely kind, have risen in Gauḍa for the good fortune of the world.

সেই দুই প্রভুর করি চরণ বন্দন ৷  
যাঁহা হইতে বিঘ্ননাশ অভীষ্টপূরণ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

sei dui prabhura kari caraṇa vandana  
of these  two  Lords  I make  of the feet  worship  
yām̐hā haite vighna-nāśa abhīṣṭa-pūraṇa  
from which  obstacles’  destruction,  desires’  fulfilment

(103) I worship the feet of these two Lords, from whom come the destruction of obstacles and the fulfilment of desire.

এই দুই শ্লোকে কৈল মঙ্গল বন্দন ৷  
তৃতীয় শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন সর্ব্বজন ॥ ১০৪ ॥

ei dui śloke kaila maṅgala vandana  
in these  two  verses  I made  auspicious  worship  
tṛtīya ślokera artha śuna sarva-jana  
of the third  verse  the meaning  hear  all  people

(104) In these two verses I have made the auspicious worship; now, all of you, hear the meaning of the third verse.

বক্তব্য-বাহুল্য, গ্রন্থ-বিস্তারের ডরে ৷  
বিস্তারি’ না বর্ণি সারার্থ কহি অল্পাক্ষরে ॥ ১০৫ ॥

vaktavya-bāhulya, grantha-vistārera ḍare  
of what must be said  the abundance,  of the book  the expansion  in fear  
vistāri’ nā varṇi sārārtha kahi alpākṣare  
in detail  not  I describe;  the essence  I tell  in few words

(105) Fearing the abundance of what could be said and the swelling of the book, I do not describe it at length; I tell the essence in few words.

‘মিতঞ্চ সারঞ্চ বচো হি বাগ্মিতা’ ইতি ॥ ১০৬ ॥

‘mitañ ca sārañ ca vaco hi vāgmitā’ iti  
measured  and  essential  and  speech  indeed  eloquence  thus

(106) “Speech that is measured and essential is true eloquence.”

শুনিলে খণ্ডিবে চিত্তের অজ্ঞানাদি দোষ ৷  
কৃষ্ণে গাঢ় প্রেম হবে, পাইবে সন্তোষ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

śunile khaṇḍibe cittera ajñānādi doṣa  
by hearing  will be cut  of the heart  ignorance and other  faults  
kṛṣṇe gāḍha prema habe, pāibe santoṣa  
for Kṛṣṇa  deep  love  will come,  will obtain  satisfaction

(107) By hearing this, one’s heart will be freed from ignorance and other faults; deep love for Kṛṣṇa will arise, and one will find true satisfaction.[^43]

শ্রীচৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ-অদ্বৈত-মহত্ত্ব ৷  
তাঁর ভক্ত-ভক্তি-নাম-প্রেমরসতত্ত্ব ॥ ১০৮ ॥

ভিন্ন ভিন্ন লিখিয়াছি করিয়া বিচার ৷  
শুনিলে জানিবে সব বস্তুতত্ত্বসার ॥ ১০৯ ॥

śrī-caitanya-nityānanda-advaita-mahattva  
Śrī  Caitanya’s,  Nityānanda’s,  Advaita’s  greatness  
tām̐ra bhakta-bhakti-nāma-prema-rasa-tattva  
Their  devotees,  devotion,  Name,  of love’s rasa  the truth  
bhinna bhinna likhiyāchi kariyā vicāra  
separately  separately  I have written  having considered  
śunile jānibe saba vastu-tattva-sāra  
by hearing  will know  all  of the Truth  the essence

(108–109) The greatness of Śrī Caitanya, Nityānanda and Advaita, Their devotees, devotion, the Holy Name and the truth of the rasa of prema — I have written of each separately, after careful consideration. Whoever hears it will know the essence of all Truth.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১১০ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(110) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

---

[^1]: According to the *Anubhāṣya* (on 1.34): the gurus are Śrī Nityānanda, Śrī Raghunātha, Śrī Rūpa and others; the devotees — Śrīvāsa and others; the avatāras — Śrī Advaita and others; the manifestations — Śrī Nityānanda and others; the potencies — Śrī Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Jagadānanda and others. “Known as Kṛṣṇa Caitanya” qualifies the Lord Himself (*īśam*). The verse is explained in 1.32–1.82.

[^2]: *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 2.5): Brahman is the radiance of His transcendental body, and the Paramātmā a particular manifestation of His majesty; both are only two aspects of His form, perceived according to the capacity of the seer — not the fullness of the Lord, as Bhagavān is.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 3.4): *unnatojjvala-rasa* is “the exalted *ujjvala-rasa*, that is, *śṛṅgāra-rasa*” — the amorous love of Vraja, which in the gopīs is *parakīyā*; hence not “conjugal”. *Sva-bhakti-śrī* is “the beauty of prema for Him”. Why this gift was withheld for so long is explained by Kavirāja Gosvāmī in Ādi 3.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 4.55): Rādhā is “the transformation of Kṛṣṇa’s love — the *hlādinī-śakti* in the form of the play of prema”; the Two are “of one Self”; Their two forms are the *viṣaya* and the *āśraya* of love, “from beginningless time” (*purā*); in Śrī Caitanya — “Kṛṣṇa within, Gaura without”.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 4.230): *mad-anubhavataḥ* — “from experiencing Me”: Rādhā’s happiness comes from relishing Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness.

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 5.7): Saṅkarṣaṇa is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa of the spiritual sky; then the three Puruṣa-avatāras; Śeṣa is Ananta. Ascribed in the edition to the diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 5.7, 5.93): Garbhodakaśāyī is the second Puruṣa, Hiraṇyagarbha, the Viṣṇu of the whole universe (*samaṣṭi*); the stem is the fourteen worlds.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 5.109): Ananta, upholder of the earth, is a *kalā* of Kṣīrodakaśāyī.

[^9]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (on Ādi 6.10): one and the same māyā is the material cause as *pradhāna* and the efficient cause as *māyā*; Mahā-Viṣṇu creates through both.

[^10]: According to the *Anubhāṣya* (on Ādi 7.6): the devotee in form is Śrī Gaurāṅga, who tastes His own sweetness; the devotee in essence (in the form of His brother) is Śrī Nityānanda; the devotee-avatāra is Śrī Advaita; the devotee is Śrīvāsa and others; the potency is Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Rāmānanda and others. Ascribed in the edition to Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: *manda-mati* — “a mind absorbed in the world, feeble, unable to follow other paths such as karma or jñāna”; *suratau* — “merciful”, or “deeply attached to each other”.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: *preṣṭhālī* — Lalitā and the other *priya-narma-sakhīs*, attended by Śrī Rūpa-mañjarī and others.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: the flute “loosens every desire in the gopīs other than for Kṛṣṇa and draws them from their homes with the rope of prema”; *śriye* — “for the wealth of prema”.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: Madana-mohana, Govinda and Gopīnātha are the presiding Deities of *sambandha*, *abhidheya* and *prayojana* taught by Śrī Caitanya.

[^15]: Bengali text as in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «শাস্ত্র যে মত নিরূপণ».

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: *guru-dvaya* — the initiating (*dīkṣā*) and the instructing (*śikṣā*) guru; both are one guru-tattva and equally worshipful for the disciple.

[^17]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the singular «তাঁহার» is used because both kinds of guru are one tattva; another reading has «তা-সবার» (“of all of them”).

[^18]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *āvaraṇa* — “the devotees surrounding the Lord on all sides”.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: all except Mahāprabhu are His servants; the guru is the *āśraya-vigraha*, the abode of service, and is the Lord’s manifestation as His servant, never to be regarded as the *viṣaya* (the object of worship).

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: *mām* — “My dearest one”; *martya-buddhyā* — “with a mind bounded by time, place and circumstance”.

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: the instructing guru is twofold — the *caitya-guru*, who gives discrimination from within, and the *mahānta-guru*, the great devotee.

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: *apaciti* — “a return of the favour, repayment”; *aśubha* — “absorption in what is not Kṛṣṇa”; *sva-gati* — “the way to Him, the position of His associate”.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: this understanding (*buddhi-yoga*) cannot be gained by oneself or from anyone else — the Lord alone gives it.

[^24]: According to the *Anubhāṣya*: the knowledge is *sambandha*; the secret (*rahasya*) is prema-bhakti; its limb (*aṅga*) is sādhana-bhakti — hearing and the rest; all this the Lord speaks out of mercy, though Brahmā did not ask.

[^25]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “neither *sat*, nor *asat*, nor even the indescribable impersonal Brahman existed apart from Me.” *Anubhāṣya*: “I” is the Lord’s personal form, not the impersonal Brahman; the world is “not other than Me”.

[^26]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the reflection (*ābhāsa*) is the likeness of the spiritual world; darkness is what lies far from it. *Anubhāṣya*: the māyā meant here is twofold — *jīva-māyā* and *guṇa-māyā*.

[^27]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “I enter everything as the Paramātmā, yet remain eternally apart as Bhagavān, the only object of the devotees’ love.” The *Anubhāṣya* also reads *na teṣu* as *nateṣu* — “in those who bow to Me”: within their hearts and before their eyes He reveals His beauty.

[^28]: *Anubhāṣya*: *anvaya-vyatireka* — injunctions and prohibitions; also union and separation.

[^29]: According to the *Anubhāṣya*: Cintāmaṇi is the guru who showed the path (*śravaṇa-guru*), Somagiri the mantra-guru, and Kṛṣṇa the *caitya-śikṣā-guru*; *jayaśrī* is Vṛndāvaneśvarī; *līlā-svayaṁvara-rasa* — the joy of choosing Him out of deep love. The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* takes “cintāmaṇi” as an epithet of Somagiri.

[^30]: *Anubhāṣya*: *apavarga-vartmani* — “in Hari, in whom ignorance ceases”; *rati* is bhāva, *bhakti* is prema.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: the devotee belongs to the category of potency (*śakti*), not of the possessor of potency; Kṛṣṇa abides in him as in His dwelling.

[^32]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: they purify the places of pilgrimage defiled by the sins of those who visit them.

[^33]: The Bengali reading of 1.74 follows the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition, «অত্যৌৎসুক্যভৃতাত্মনাম্», which the *Anubhāṣya* glosses: “their minds greatly agitated by the eagerness to see”.

[^34]: *Anubhāṣya*: *anyākāram* — “with a different arrangement of the limbs”; *prāyeṇātma-samam* — “equal to the original form, but with some difference in qualities”.

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: Baladeva is *svayaṁ-prakāśa*, Nārāyaṇa a *prābhava-vilāsa*.

[^36]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “His companions in Vraja are the *svarūpa-śakti* itself.”

[^37]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *kāya-vyūha* — “the expansion of His own body”; the entourage and the One it surrounds are one when seen through *acintya-bhedābheda*, and so the six truths of 1.32 are one.

[^38]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *nija-dhāma* — “Their effulgence”; *pūrva-śaile* — “in Gauḍa as the eastern mountain of sunrise, on the eastern bank of the Ganges”.

[^39]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads «…বাঞ্ছা আদি এই সব» (“and so on”). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (on 1.91): *kaitava* is the desire for *dharma*, *artha*, *kāma* and *mokṣa*; the chief of them is the desire for liberation (1.92).

[^40]: The verse says only «শ্রীমদ্ভাগবতে মহামুনিকৃতে» (*śrīmad-bhāgavate mahā-muni-kṛte*), “in the beautiful *Bhāgavata* composed by the great sage”; the sage is not named. The *Anubhāṣya* explains: *śrī-nārāyaṇa-mahā-muni-racite* — “composed by the great sage Śrī Nārāyaṇa”; the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *Bhāgavatam* was first composed by Śrī Nārāyaṇa in the form of four verses (*catuḥ-ślokī*). The translation therefore names no one, as the verse does not. *Projjhita-kaitava* — free from the desire for the fruits of dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa.

[^41]: Śrīdhara Svāmī’s comment on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.1.2 (heading in the edition).

[^42]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “They have become subject to the love of the soul.”

[^43]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in place of «কৃষ্ণে গাঢ় প্রেম হবে» another reading has «সর্ব্বতত্ত্ব জ্ঞান হইবে» (“one will know all truths”).
