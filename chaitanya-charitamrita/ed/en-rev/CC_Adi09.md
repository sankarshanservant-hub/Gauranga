“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 9  
**The Description of the Wish-fulfilling Tree of Bhakti**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

তং শ্রীমৎকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যদেবং বন্দে জগদ্গুরুম্ ৷  
যস্যানুকম্পয়া শ্বাপি মহাব্ধিং সন্তরেৎ সুখম্ ॥ ১ ॥

taṁ śrīmat-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
Him  glorious  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya-  
devaṁ vande jagad-gurum  
the Lord  I worship  the guru of the world  
yasyānukampayā śvāpi  
by whose mercy  even a dog  
mahābdhiṁ santaret sukham  
the great ocean  can cross  easily

(1) I worship the glorious Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the guru of the whole world, by whose mercy even a dog can easily swim across the great ocean.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
জয় জয়াদ্বৈত জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya gauracandra  
glory  glory  to Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  Gauracandra  
jaya jayādvaita jaya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory to Advaita  glory  glory  to Nityānanda

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! Glory, glory to Advaita, and glory, glory to Nityānanda!

জয় জয় শ্রীবাসাদি গৌরভক্তগণ ৷  
সর্ব্বাভীষ্ট-পূর্ত্তি-হেতু যাঁহার স্মরণ ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya śrīvāsādi gaura-bhakta-gaṇa  
glory  glory  to Śrīvāsa and the others  Gaura’s devotees  
sarvābhīṣṭa-pūrti-hetu yām̐hāra smaraṇa  
of all desires  fulfilment  the cause  whose  remembrance

(3) Glory, glory to Śrīvāsa and all the devotees of Gaura, the remembrance of whom fulfils every desire!

শ্রীরূপ, সনাতন, ভট্ট রঘুনাথ ৷  
শ্রীজীব, গোপালভট্ট, দাস রঘুনাথ ॥ ৪ ॥

śrī-rūpa, sanātana, bhaṭṭa raghunātha  
Śrī  Rūpa  Sanātana  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha  
śrī-jīva, gopāla-bhaṭṭa, dāsa raghunātha  
Śrī  Jīva  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa  Dāsa  Raghunātha

(4) I remember also Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha Dāsa.

এসব-প্রসাদে লিখি চৈতন্য-লীলাগুণ ৷  
জানি বা না জানি, করি আপন-শোধন ॥ ৫ ॥

e-saba-prasāde likhi caitanya-līlā-guṇa  
of all these  by the mercy  I write  Caitanya’s  pastimes  qualities  
jāni vā nā jāni, kari āpana-śodhana  
I know  or  not  I know  I do  my own  purification

(5) By the mercy of them all I write of the pastimes and qualities of Śrī Caitanya. Whether I understand them or not, I do it for my own purification.

মালাকারঃ স্বয়ং কৃষ্ণ-প্রেমামরতরুঃ স্বয়ম্ ৷  
দাতা ভোক্তা তৎফলানাং যস্তং চৈতন্যমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ৬ ॥

mālākāraḥ svayaṁ kṛṣṇa-  
the gardener  Himself  Kṛṣṇa-  
premāmara-taruḥ svayam  
the immortal tree of love  Himself  
dātā bhoktā tat-phalānāṁ  
the giver  the enjoyer  of its fruits  
yas taṁ caitanyam āśraye  
who  Him  Caitanya  I take shelter of

(6) I take shelter of Śrī Caitanya, who is Himself the gardener and Himself the immortal tree of love for Kṛṣṇa, and who both gives away its fruits and enjoys them.[^2]

প্রভু কহে, আমি ‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ নাম ধরি ৷  
নাম সার্থক হয়, যদি প্রেমে বিশ্ব ভরি ॥ ৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, āmi ‘viśvambhara’ nāma dhari  
the Lord  said  I  ‘Viśvambhara’  the name  bear  
nāma sārthaka haya, yadi preme viśva bhari  
the name  meaningful  becomes  if  with love  the universe  I fill

(7) The Lord said to Himself: “I bear the name Viśvambhara, ‘He who fills the universe’. My name will be true if I fill the universe with love.”[^3]

এত চিন্তি’ লৈলা প্রভু মালাকার-ধর্ম্ম ৷  
নবদ্বীপে আরম্ভিলা ফলোদ্যান-কর্ম্ম ॥ ৮ ॥

eta cinti’ lailā prabhu mālākāra-dharma  
thus  thinking  took  the Lord  of a gardener  the duty  
navadvīpe ārambhilā phalodyāna-karma  
in Navadvīpa  began  of a fruit garden  the work

(8) Thinking thus, the Lord took up the work of a gardener and began to lay out an orchard in Navadvīpa.

শ্রীচৈতন্য মালাকার পৃথিবীতে আনি’ ৷  
ভক্তি-কল্পতরু রোপিলা সিঞ্চি’ ইচ্ছা-পানি ॥ ৯ ॥

śrī-caitanya mālākāra pṛthivīte āni’  
Śrī  Caitanya  the gardener  to the earth  bringing  
bhakti-kalpataru ropilā siñci’ icchā-pāni  
the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti  planted  watering  with the water of His will

(9) Bringing it to the earth, the gardener Śrī Caitanya planted the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti and watered it with the water of His will.

জয় শ্রীমাধবপুরী কৃষ্ণপ্রেমপূর ৷  
ভক্তিকল্পতরুর তেঁহো প্রথম অঙ্কুর ॥ ১০ ॥

jaya śrī mādhavapurī kṛṣṇa-prema-pūra  
glory  to Śrī Mādhava Purī  with love for Kṛṣṇa  brimming  
bhakti-kalpatarura tem̐ho prathama aṅkura  
of the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti  he  the first  sprout

(10) Glory to Śrī Mādhava Purī, brimming with love for Kṛṣṇa! He is the first sprout of the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti.[^4]

শ্রীঈশ্বরপুরী-রূপে অঙ্কুর পুষ্ট হৈল ৷  
আপনে চৈতন্যমালী স্কন্ধ উপজিল ॥ ১১ ॥

śrī-īśvarapurī-rūpe aṅkura puṣṭa haila  
Śrī Īśvara Purī  in the form of  the sprout  strong  became  
āpane caitanya-mālī skandha upajila  
Himself  Caitanya the gardener  the trunk  brought forth

(11) In the form of Śrī Īśvara Purī the sprout grew strong, and then the gardener, Śrī Caitanya Himself, became the trunk of the tree.[^5]

নিজাচিন্ত্যশক্ত্যে মালী হঞা স্কন্ধ হয় ৷  
সকল শাখার সেই স্কন্ধ মূলাশ্রয় ॥ ১২ ॥

nijācintya-śaktye mālī hañā skandha haya  
by His own inconceivable power  the gardener  being  the trunk  becomes  
sakala śākhāra sei skandha mūlāśraya  
of all  the branches  that  trunk  the original support

(12) By His own inconceivable power the gardener became the trunk, and that trunk is the original support of all the branches.

পরমানন্দ পুরী, আর কেশব ভারতী ৷  
ব্রহ্মানন্দ পুরী, আর ব্রহ্মানন্দ ভারতী ॥ ১৩ ॥

বিষ্ণুপুরী, কেশবপুরী, পুরী কৃষ্ণানন্দ ৷  
শ্রীনৃসিংহতীর্থ, আর পুরী সুখানন্দ ॥ ১৪ ॥

এই নব মূল নিকসিল বৃক্ষমূলে ৷  
এই নব মূলে বৃক্ষ করিল নিশ্চলে ॥ ১৫ ॥

paramānanda purī, āra keśava bhāratī  
Paramānanda  Purī  and  Keśava  Bhāratī  
brahmānanda purī, āra brahmānanda bhāratī  
Brahmānanda  Purī  and  Brahmānanda  Bhāratī  
viṣṇu-purī, keśava-purī, purī kṛṣṇānanda  
Viṣṇu Purī  Keśava Purī  Purī  Kṛṣṇānanda  
śrī-nṛsiṁhatīrtha, āra purī sukhānanda  
Śrī Nṛsiṁha Tīrtha  and  Purī  Sukhānanda  
ei nava mūla nikasila vṛkṣa-mūle  
these  nine  roots  sprang up  at the base of the tree  
ei nava mūle vṛkṣa karila niścale  
on these  nine  roots  the tree  made  immovable

(13–15) At the base of the tree nine roots sprang up: Paramānanda Purī and Keśava Bhāratī, Brahmānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī, Viṣṇu Purī, Keśava Purī, Kṛṣṇānanda Purī, Śrī Nṛsiṁha Tīrtha and Sukhānanda Purī. On them the tree stood immovable.[^6]

মধ্যমূল পরমানন্দ পুরী মহাধীর ৷  
এই নব মূলে বৃক্ষ করিল সুস্থির ॥ ১৬ ॥

madhya-mūla paramānanda purī mahā-dhīra  
the central root  Paramānanda  Purī  greatly steadfast  
ei nava mūle vṛkṣa karila susthira  
on these  nine  roots  the tree  made  firm

(16) The central root is Paramānanda Purī, a man of great steadfastness, and on these nine roots the tree stood secure.[^7]

স্কন্ধের উপরে বহু শাখা নিকসিল ৷  
উপরি উপরি শাখা অসংখ্য হইল ॥ ১৭ ॥

skandhera upare bahu śākhā nikasila  
of the trunk  above  many  branches  sprang out  
upari upari śākhā asaṅkhya haila  
higher  and higher  branches  countless  became

(17) From the trunk many branches grew, and as they rose higher and higher, they became countless.

বিশ বিশ শাখা করি’ এক এক মণ্ডল ৷  
মহা-মহা-শাখা ছাইল ব্রহ্মাণ্ড সকল ॥ ১৮ ॥

biśa biśa śākhā kari’ eka eka maṇḍala  
twenty  twenty  branches  making  one  one  circle  
mahā-mahā-śākhā chāila brahmāṇḍa sakala  
great  great  branches  covered  universes  all

(18) Every twenty branches formed a circle of their own, and these mighty boughs covered all the universes.

একৈক শাখাতে উপশাখা শত শত ৷  
যত উপজিল শাখা কে গণিবে কত ॥ ১৯ ॥

ekaika śākhāte upaśākhā śata śata  
on each  branch  sub-branches  hundreds  hundreds  
yata upajila śākhā ke gaṇibe kata  
as many as  sprang up  branches  who  will count  how many

(19) On every branch there are hundreds of smaller branches. Who can count all the branches that have grown?

মুখ্য মুখ্য শাখাগণের নাম-গণন ৷  
আগে ত’ করিব, শুন বৃক্ষের বর্ণন ॥ ২০ ॥

mukhya mukhya śākhā-gaṇera nāma-gaṇana  
chief  chief  of the branches  of the names  the counting  
āge ta’ kariba, śuna vṛkṣera varṇana  
further on  indeed  I shall do  hear  of the tree  the description

(20) I shall list the names of the chief branches further on; now hear the description of the tree.[^8]

বৃক্ষের উপরে শাখা হৈল দুই স্কন্ধ ৷  
এক ‘অদ্বৈত’ নাম, আর ‘নিত্যানন্দ’ ॥ ২১ ॥

vṛkṣera upare śākhā haila dui skandha  
of the tree  above  branches  became  two  trunks  
eka ‘advaita’ nāma, āra ‘nityānanda’  
one  ‘Advaita’  named  the other  ‘Nityānanda’

(21) Higher up, the tree branched into two trunks: one is named Advaita, and the other Nityānanda.[^9]

সেই দুইস্কন্ধে শাখা যত উপজিল ৷  
তার উপশাখাগণে জগৎ ছাইল ॥ ২২ ॥

sei dui-skandhe śākhā yata upajila  
on those  two trunks  branches  as many as  sprang up  
tāra upaśākhā-gaṇe jagat chāila  
their  sub-branches  the world  covered

(22) Many branches sprang from those two trunks, and their offshoots covered the world.[^10]

বড় শাখা, উপশাখা, তার উপশাখা ৷  
জগৎ ব্যাপিল তার কে করিবে লেখা ॥ ২৩ ॥

baḍa śākhā, upaśākhā, tāra upaśākhā  
great  branches  sub-branches  their  sub-branches  
jagat vyāpila tāra ke karibe lekhā  
the world  pervaded  of them  who  will make  a record

(23) Great branches, smaller branches and their offshoots filled the world. Who could ever write them all down?

শিষ্য, প্রশিষ্য, আর উপশিষ্যগণ ৷  
জগৎ ব্যাপিল তার নাহিক গণন ॥ ২৪ ॥

śiṣya, praśiṣya, āra upaśiṣya-gaṇa  
disciples  grand-disciples  and  later disciples  
jagat vyāpila tāra nāhika gaṇana  
the world  pervaded  of them  there is no  counting

(24) Disciples, the disciples of disciples and their followers spread over the world beyond all counting.

উড়ুম্বর-বৃক্ষ যেন ফলে সর্ব্ব অঙ্গে ৷  
এইমত ভক্তিবৃক্ষে সর্ব্বত্র ফল লাগে ॥ ২৫ ॥

uḍumbara-vṛkṣa yena phale sarva aṅge  
the udumbara tree  as  bears fruit  on all  limbs  
ei mata bhakti-vṛkṣe sarvatra phala lāge  
in this way  on the tree of bhakti  everywhere  fruit  appears

(25) As the udumbara fig bears fruit on all its limbs, so the tree of bhakti bears fruit everywhere.

মূলস্কন্ধের শাখা-উপশাখাগণে ৷  
লাগিল যে প্রেমফল, — অমৃতকে জিনে ॥ ২৬ ॥

mūla-skandhera śākhā-upaśākhā-gaṇe  
of the main trunk  on the branches and sub-branches  
lāgila ye prema-phala, — amṛtake jine  
grew  which  the fruit of love  nectar  surpasses

(26) On the branches and offshoots of the main trunk grew the fruit of love, sweeter than nectar.

পাকিল যে প্রেমফল অমৃত-মধুর ৷  
বিলায় চৈতন্যমালী, নাহি লয় মূল ॥ ২৭ ॥

pākila ye prema-phala amṛta-madhura  
ripened  which  the fruit of love  nectar-sweet  
vilāya caitanya-mālī, nāhi laya mūla  
distributes  Caitanya the gardener  not  takes  price

(27) When the fruit of love ripened, sweet as nectar, the gardener Caitanya gave it away and asked no price.

ত্রিজগতে যত আছে ধন-রত্নমণি ৷  
একফলের মূল্য করি’ তাহা নাহি গণি ॥ ২৮ ॥

tri-jagate yata āche dhana-ratnamaṇi  
in the three worlds  as much as  there is  wealth  jewels  gems  
eka-phalera mūlya kari’ tāhā nāhi gaṇi  
of one fruit  the price  making  that  not  I count

(28) All the wealth and jewels of the three worlds I would not count as the price of a single fruit.

মাগে বা না মাগে কেহ, পাত্র বা অপাত্র ৷  
ইহার বিচার নাহি জানে, দেয় মাত্র ॥ ২৯ ॥

māge vā nā māge keha, pātra vā apātra  
asks  or  not  asks  anyone  worthy  or  unworthy  
ihāra vicāra nāhi jāne, deya mātra  
of this  consideration  not  He knows  He gives  simply

(29) Whether one asks or not, whether one is worthy or unworthy, He does not consider; He simply gives.

অঞ্জলি অঞ্জলি ভরি’ ফেলে চতুর্দ্দিশে ৷  
দরিদ্র কুড়াঞা খায়, মালাকার হাসে ॥ ৩০ ॥

añjali añjali bhari’ phele caturdiśe  
handful  handful  filling  He throws  in the four directions  
daridra kuḍāñā khāya, mālākāra hāse  
the poor  gathering  eat  the gardener  smiles

(30) Filling His hands again and again, He scatters the fruit in all directions; the poor gather it up and eat, and the gardener smiles.

মালাকার কহে, — শুন, বৃক্ষ-পরিবার ৷  
মূলশাখা-উপশাখা যতেক প্রকার ॥ ৩১ ॥

mālākāra kahe, — śuna, vṛkṣa-parivāra  
the gardener  says  listen  of the tree  family  
mūlaśākhā-upaśākhā yateka prakāra  
main branches  sub-branches  as many  kinds

(31) The gardener said: “Listen, O family of the tree, all you main branches and offshoots of every kind!

অলৌকিক বৃক্ষ করে সর্ব্বেন্দ্রিয়-কর্ম্ম ৷  
স্থাবর হইয়া ধরে জঙ্গমের ধর্ম্ম ॥ ৩২ ॥

alaukika vṛkṣa kare sarvendriya-karma  
supramundane  tree  performs  of all the senses  the work  
sthāvara haiyā dhare jaṅgamera dharma  
immovable  being  holds  of the moving  the nature

(32) This wondrous tree is wholly spiritual and performs the work of all the senses. Though it is a tree and cannot move, it acts as a living, moving being.[^11]

এ বৃক্ষের অঙ্গ হয় সব সচেতন ৷  
বাড়িয়া ব্যাপিল সবে সকল ভুবন ॥ ৩৩ ॥

e vṛkṣera aṅga haya saba sa-cetana  
of this  tree  limbs  are  all  conscious  
bāḍiyā vyāpila sabe sakala bhuvana  
growing  pervaded  all  all  the worlds

(33) All the limbs of this tree are conscious, and as they grow, they spread through all the worlds.

একলা মালাকার আমি কাহাঁ কাহাঁ যাব ৷  
একলা বা কত ফল পাড়িয়া বিলাব ॥ ৩৪ ॥

ekalā mālākāra āmi kāhām̐ kāhām̐ yāba  
alone  gardener  I  where  where  shall go  
ekalā vā kata phala pāḍiyā vilāba  
alone  or  how many  fruits  picking  shall distribute

(34) I am a lone gardener. How many places can I go to? How many fruits can I pick and give away by Myself?

একলা উঠাঞা দিতে হয় পরিশ্রম ৷  
কেহ পায়, কেহ না পায়, রহে মনে ভ্রম ॥ ৩৫ ॥

ekalā uṭhāñā dite haya pariśrama  
alone  picking up  to give  is  labour  
keha pāya, keha nā pāya, rahe mane bhrama  
someone  gets  someone  not  gets  remains  in the mind  doubt

(35) It is hard labour to pick them and give them alone, and I fear that some will receive them and some will not.

অতএব আমি আজ্ঞা দিলুঁ সবাকারে ৷  
যাঁহা তাঁহা প্রেমফল দেহ’ যারে তারে ॥ ৩৬ ॥

ataeva āmi ājñā dilum̐ sabākāre  
therefore  I  order  have given  to all  
yām̐hā tām̐hā prema-phala deha’ yāre tāre  
anywhere  the fruit of love  give  to anyone

(36) Therefore I give this order to all of you: wherever you are, give the fruit of love to anyone and everyone.[^12]

একলা মালাকার আমি কত ফল খাব ৷  
না দিয়া বা এই ফল আর কি করিব ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ekalā mālākāra āmi kata phala khāba  
alone  gardener  I  how many  fruits  shall eat  
nā diyā vā ei phala āra ki kariba  
not  giving  or  this  fruit  else  what  shall I do

(37) I am a lone gardener. How many fruits can I eat? And if I do not give this fruit away, what else shall I do with it?

আত্ম-ইচ্ছামৃতে বৃক্ষ সিঞ্চি নিরন্তর ৷  
তাহাতে অসংখ্য ফল বৃক্ষের উপর ॥ ৩৮ ॥

ātma-icchāmṛte vṛkṣa siñci nirantara  
of My own desire  with the nectar  the tree  I water  constantly  
tāhāte asaṅkhya phala vṛkṣera upara  
by that  countless  fruits  of the tree  upon

(38) I water the tree unceasingly with the nectar of My own desire, and so it bears countless fruits.

অতএব সব ফল দেহ’ যারে তারে ৷  
খাইয়া হউক্ লোক অজর অমরে ॥ ৩৯ ॥

ataeva saba phala deha’ yāre tāre  
therefore  all  fruits  give  to anyone  
khāiyā hauk loka ajara amare  
eating  may become  people  ageless  immortal

(39) Therefore give all the fruits to anyone and everyone. Let people eat them and be free from old age and death.

জগৎ ব্যাপিয়া মোর হবে পুণ্য খ্যাতি ৷  
সুখী হইয়া লোক মোর গাহিবেক কীর্ত্তি ॥ ৪০ ॥

jagat vyāpiyā mora habe puṇya khyāti  
the world  pervading  My  will be  holy  fame  
sukhī haiyā loka mora gāhibeka kīrti  
happy  becoming  people  My  will sing  glory

(40) My holy fame will spread through the world, and people, made happy, will sing My praises.

ভারত-ভূমিতে হৈল মনুষ্য-জন্ম যার ৷  
জন্ম সার্থক করি’ কর পর-উপকার ॥ ৪১ ॥

bhārata-bhūmite haila manuṣya-janma yāra  
of Bhārata  in the land  has been  human  birth  whose  
janma sārthaka kari’ kara para-upakāra  
birth  fruitful  making  do  others’  welfare

(41) Let everyone who has taken human birth in the land of Bhārata make that birth fruitful by working for the good of others.[^13]

এতাবজ্জন্মসাফল্যং দেহিনামিহ দেহিষু ৷  
প্রাণৈরর্থৈর্ধিয়া বাচা শ্রেয় আচরণং সদা ॥ ৪২ ॥

etāvaj janma-sāphalyaṁ  
this much  of birth  the success  
dehinām iha dehiṣu  
of the embodied  here  towards the embodied  
prāṇair arthair dhiyā vācā  
with life  with wealth  with intelligence  with words  
śreya ācaraṇaṁ sadā  
the good  doing  always

(42) ‘This alone is the true success of birth for embodied beings in this world: always to do good to others with one’s life, wealth, intelligence and words.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.22.35)[^14]

প্রাণিনামুপকারায় যদেবেহ পরত্র চ ৷  
কর্ম্মণা মনসা বাচা তদেব মতিমান্ ভজেৎ ॥ ৪৩ ॥

prāṇinām upakārāya  
of living beings  for the benefit  
yad eveha paratra ca  
whatever  indeed  here  in the next  and  
karmaṇā manasā vācā  
by deeds  by mind  by words  
tad eva mati-mān bhajet  
that  alone  the wise  should practise

(43) ‘By deeds, thoughts and words the wise should do only that which brings good to living beings, in this world and the next.’ (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 3.12.45)[^15]

মালী মনুষ্য আমার নাহি রাজ্য-ধন ৷  
ফল-ফুল দিয়া করি’ পুণ্য উপার্জ্জন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

mālī manuṣya āmāra nāhi rājya-dhana  
a gardener  a man  My  not  kingdom  wealth  
phala-phula diyā kari’ puṇya upārjana  
fruits  flowers  giving  doing  merit  earning

(44) I am a gardener, a man with no kingdom and no riches. By giving away fruits and flowers I earn merit.

মালী হঞা বৃক্ষ হইলাঙ এই ত’ ইচ্ছাতে ৷  
সর্ব্বপ্রাণীর উপকার হয় বৃক্ষ হৈতে ॥ ৪৫ ॥

mālī hañā vṛkṣa hailāṅa ei ta’ icchāte  
a gardener  being  the tree  I became  this  indeed  by the wish  
sarva-prāṇīra upakāra haya vṛkṣa haite  
of all living beings  benefit  is  the tree  from

(45) I am the gardener, and yet I have become the tree, for that was My wish: a tree benefits every living being.[^16]

অহো এষাং বরং জন্ম সর্ব্বপ্রাণ্যুপজীবনম্ ৷  
সুজনস্যেব যেষাং বৈ বিমুখা যান্তি নার্থিনঃ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

aho eṣāṁ varaṁ janma  
ah  of these  excellent  birth  
sarva-prāṇy-upajīvanam  
of all living beings  the sustenance  
su-janasyeva yeṣāṁ vai  
of a good man  as  from whom  indeed  
vimukhā yānti nārthinaḥ  
disappointed  go away  not  petitioners

(46) ‘Ah, how excellent is the birth of these trees, which sustains all living beings! Like a good man, they never let one who asks of them go away disappointed.’” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.22.33)[^17]

এই আজ্ঞা কৈল যদি চৈতন্য-মালাকার ৷  
পরম আনন্দ পাইল বৃক্ষ-পরিবার ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ei ājñā kaila yadi caitanya-mālākāra  
this  order  gave  when  Caitanya  the gardener  
parama ānanda pāila vṛkṣa-parivāra  
supreme  joy  attained  of the tree  the family

(47) When the gardener Caitanya gave this order, the whole family of the tree was filled with joy.

যে যাঁহা তাঁহা দান করে প্রেমফল ৷  
ফলাস্বাদে মত্ত লোক হইল সকল ॥ ৪৮ ॥

ye yām̐hā tām̐hā dāna kare prema-phala  
whoever  wherever  there  gift  makes  the fruit of love  
phalāsvāde matta loka haila sakala  
by tasting the fruit  intoxicated  people  became  all

(48) Each branch, wherever it was, began to give away the fruit of love, and all the people who tasted it became intoxicated.[^18]

মহা-মাদক প্রেমফল পেট ভরি’ খায় ৷  
মাতিল সকল লোক — হাসে, নাচে, গায় ॥ ৪৯ ॥

mahā-mādaka prema-phala peṭa bhari’ khāya  
greatly  intoxicating  the fruit of love  belly  filling  they eat  
mātila sakala loka — hāse, nāce, gāya  
maddened  all  people  laugh  dance  sing

(49) People ate their fill of this most intoxicating fruit of love, and, maddened by it, they all laughed, danced and sang.

কেহ গড়াগড়ি যায়, কেহ ত’ হুঙ্কার ৷  
দেখি’ আনন্দিত হঞা হাসে মালাকার ॥ ৫০ ॥

keha gaḍāgaḍi yāya, keha ta’ huṅkāra  
someone  rolling  goes  someone  indeed  roaring  
dekhi’ ānandita hañā hāse mālākāra  
seeing  delighted  becoming  smiles  the gardener

(50) Some rolled on the ground, some roared, and seeing this, the gardener smiled with delight.

এই মালাকার খায় এই প্রেমফল ৷  
নিরবধি মত্ত রহে, বিবশ-বিহ্বল ॥ ৫১ ॥

ei mālākāra khāya ei prema-phala  
this  gardener  eats  this  fruit of love  
niravadhi matta rahe, vivaśa-vihvala  
ever  intoxicated  remains  helpless  overwhelmed

(51) The gardener Himself eats this fruit of love and remains ever intoxicated, helpless and overwhelmed.[^19]

সর্ব্বলোকে মত্ত কৈলা আপন-সমান ৷  
প্রেমে মত্ত লোক বিনা নাহি দেখি আন ॥ ৫২ ॥

sarva-loke matta kailā āpana-samāna  
all people  intoxicated  made  His own  equal  
preme matta loka vinā nāhi dekhi āna  
with love  intoxicated  people  except  not  I see  other

(52) He made all people as intoxicated as Himself; I see no one who is not mad with love.

যে যে পূর্ব্বে নিন্দা কৈল, বলি’ মাতোয়াল ৷  
সেই ফল খায়, নাচে, বলে — ভাল ভাল ॥ ৫৩ ॥

ye ye pūrve nindā kaila, bali’ mātoyāla  
who  who  before  blame  made  saying  drunkard  
sei phala khāya, nāce, bale — bhāla bhāla  
that  fruit  eat  dance  say  good  good

(53) Even those who once reviled Him, calling Him a drunkard, ate that fruit and danced, crying, “Wonderful, wonderful!”[^20]

এই ত’ কহিলুঁ প্রেমফল-বিতরণ ৷  
এবে শুন, ফলদাতা যে যে শাখাগণ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

ei ta’ kahilum̐ prema-phala-vitaraṇa  
this  indeed  I have told  of the fruit of love  the distribution  
ebe śuna, phala-dātā ye ye śākhā-gaṇa  
now  hear  the givers of fruit  which  which  branches

(54) Thus I have told of the distribution of the fruit of love. Now hear of the branches that give the fruit.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৫৫ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(55) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “By Gaura’s mercy the impossible becomes possible.” *Anubhāṣya*: *jagad-guru* — the guru of all the worlds, worthy of everyone’s worship.

[^2]: Heading in the edition: “The gardener is Mahāprabhu Himself.” *Anubhāṣya*: *amara-taru* — the unfading, imperishable tree, that is, the wish-fulfilling tree (*kalpa-vṛkṣa*); its fruits are the fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa.

[^3]: Heading in the edition: “Why He became the gardener: so that His being the Lord of *abhidheya*, the means to the goal, should bear fruit.”

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śrī Mādhava Purī is Mādhavendra Purī, a renowned sannyāsī of the sampradāya of Śrī Madhvācārya; Śrī Caitanya is the disciple of his disciple. Before him there was no clear sign of prema-bhakti in the Madhva sampradāya, and his verse *ayi dīna-dayārdra nātha* holds, as a seed, what Mahāprabhu was to teach.

[^5]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* and *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Īśvara Purī, the Guru who gave Mahāprabhu His mantra, was born into a brāhmaṇa family in Kumārahaṭṭa (Hālisahar) and was the dearest disciple of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, whom he served with complete devotion (Antya 8.26–29). Before initiating Mahāprabhu in Gayā he lived for some months in Navadvīpa in the house of Gopīnātha Ācārya and read Him his own *Kṛṣṇa-līlāmṛta* (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 7). When Mahāprabhu came to Kumārahaṭṭa, He took earth from the place of Īśvara Purī’s birth, teaching everyone devotion to the Guru.

[^6]: Heading in the edition: “Nine sannyāsīs — nine roots.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the sannyāsīs named Purī are related by succession to Śrī Īśvara Purī, and those named Bhāratī to Keśava Bhāratī, who gave Mahāprabhu sannyāsa. *Anubhāṣya*: Keśava Bhāratī belonged to the Bhāratī branch of the orders of sannyāsa founded by Śaṅkara; some say he had received a mantra from Śrī Mādhavendra Purī and was a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī. In Śaka 1432 (1510) he gave sannyāsa to Nimāi Paṇḍita in Kāṭoyā. Brahmānanda Purī sang with Mahāprabhu in the kīrtanas of Navadvīpa and came with Him to Nīlācala. Brahmānanda Bhāratī wore a deerskin; when Mahāprabhu came to him and pretended not to recognise him, he exchanged it for saffron cloth.

[^7]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *ei nava mūle* (“on these nine roots”); some other editions read *aṣṭa dike aṣṭa mūla* (“eight roots in the eight directions”). *Anubhāṣya*: Paramānanda Purī, a brāhmaṇa of Tirhut and a disciple of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, was especially dear to Mahāprabhu; together with Svarūpa Dāmodara he was always at His side in Nīlācala (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 3 and 11). The *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* names him as Uddhava.

[^8]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *nāma-gaṇana* (“the counting of the names”); some other editions read *nāma agaṇana* (“the names are countless”). The branches are named in Ādi 10–12.

[^9]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *vṛkṣera upare śākhā haila dui skandha* (“above, the branches of the tree became two trunks”); some other editions read *śākhāra upare haila vṛkṣa dui skandha*. Heading in the edition: “On either side of the main trunk, two trunks — Nitāi and Advaita.”

[^10]: Heading in the edition: “Branches and offshoots — the succession of disciples and their disciples.”

[^11]: Heading in the edition: “Every part of the Caitanya-tree is full of consciousness, and by tasting its conscious fruit the insentient jīva awakens to consciousness.”

[^12]: Heading in the edition (to 34–38): “Seeing that spreading the Name and prema alone is impossible, He orders everyone to give them without discrimination.”

[^13]: Heading in the edition: “Everyone born as a human being in the land of Bhārata is duty-bound to show people eternal mercy — to turn them towards Kṛṣṇa.” *Anubhāṣya*: to be born as a human being in holy Bhāratavarṣa, the best of lands, is to receive a body for the true and eternal good of the world; in this lies the success of such a birth.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Kṛṣṇa spoke these words to His cowherd friends when, after stealing the gopīs’ garments, He had gone far with them and, resting beneath the trees, saw the trees’ patience and readiness to serve others. The good (*śreyas*) here is eternal good: to free the jīva from aversion to the Lord and turn him towards Him; this is the highest expression of eternal mercy. Heading in the edition: “The highest mercy and the highest good is to turn the jīva towards devotion to Kṛṣṇa by deeds, thoughts and words.”

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: the benefit (*upakāra*) is eternal good; what one should do is awaken in living beings the pious merit (*sukṛti*) that turns them towards devotion to the Lord.

[^16]: Heading in the edition: “Seeing the causeless mercy of the tree, He wished to become the original wish-fulfilling tree Himself.”

[^17]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *sarva-prāṇy-upajīvanam* (“a birth that sustains all living beings”); some other editions read *upajīvinām*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇa says this addressing the trees: they are the sustenance of all beings and their birth is fruitful; no petitioner leaves them empty-handed — the trees behave like good people.

[^18]: Heading in the edition: “The fruit of prema is given regardless of qualification (*adhikāra*).”

[^19]: Heading in the edition (to 51–52): “By giving the jīvas love for Kṛṣṇa like His own, He makes them mahā-bhāgavatas.”

[^20]: Heading in the edition: “Even the lowly blasphemers are delivered by receiving the fruit of love for Kṛṣṇa.”
