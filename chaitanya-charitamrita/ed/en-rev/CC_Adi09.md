“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 9  
**The Description of the Wish-fulfilling Tree of Bhakti**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

তং শ্রীমৎকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যদেবং বন্দে জগদ্গুরুম্ ৷  
যস্যানুকম্পয়া শ্বাপি মহাব্ধিং সন্তরেৎ সুখম্ ॥ ১ ॥

taṁ śrīmat-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
Him  glorious  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya-  
devaṁ vande jagad-gurum  
the Lord  I worship  the guru of the world  
yasyānukampayā śvāpi  
by whose mercy  even a dog  
mahābdhiṁ santaret sukham  
the great ocean  can cross  easily

(1) I worship the glorious Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the guru of the whole world, by whose mercy even a dog can easily swim across the great ocean.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
জয় জয়াদ্বৈত জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya gauracandra  
glory  glory  to Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  Gauracandra  
jaya jayādvaita jaya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory to Advaita  glory  glory  to Nityānanda

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! Glory, glory to Advaita, and glory, glory to Nityānanda!

জয় জয় শ্রীবাসাদি গৌরভক্তগণ ৷  
সর্ব্বাভীষ্ট-পূর্ত্তি-হেতু যাঁহার স্মরণ ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya śrīvāsādi gaura-bhakta-gaṇa  
glory  glory  to Śrīvāsa and the others  Gaura’s devotees  
sarvābhīṣṭa-pūrti-hetu yām̐hāra smaraṇa  
of all desires  fulfilment  the cause  whose  remembrance

(3) Glory, glory to Śrīvāsa and all the devotees of Gaura, the remembrance of whom fulfils every desire!

শ্রীরূপ, সনাতন, ভট্ট রঘুনাথ ৷  
শ্রীজীব, গোপালভট্ট, দাস রঘুনাথ ॥ ৪ ॥

śrī-rūpa, sanātana, bhaṭṭa raghunātha  
Śrī  Rūpa  Sanātana  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha  
śrī-jīva, gopāla-bhaṭṭa, dāsa raghunātha  
Śrī  Jīva  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa  Dāsa  Raghunātha

(4) I remember also Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha Dāsa.

এসব-প্রসাদে লিখি চৈতন্য-লীলাগুণ ৷  
জানি বা না জানি, করি আপন-শোধন ॥ ৫ ॥

e-saba-prasāde likhi caitanya-līlā-guṇa  
of all these  by the mercy  I write  Caitanya’s  pastimes  qualities  
jāni vā nā jāni, kari āpana-śodhana  
I know  or  not  I know  I do  my own  purification

(5) By the mercy of them all I write of the pastimes and qualities of Śrī Caitanya. Whether I understand them or not, I write for my own purification.

মালাকারঃ স্বয়ং কৃষ্ণ-প্রেমামরতরুঃ স্বয়ম্ ৷  
দাতা ভোক্তা তৎফলানাং যস্তং চৈতন্যমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ৬ ॥

mālākāraḥ svayaṁ kṛṣṇa-  
the gardener  Himself  Kṛṣṇa-  
premāmara-taruḥ svayam  
the immortal tree of love  Himself  
dātā bhoktā tat-phalānāṁ  
the giver  the enjoyer  of its fruits  
yas taṁ caitanyam āśraye  
who  Him  Caitanya  I take shelter of

(6) I take shelter of Śrī Caitanya, who is Himself the gardener and Himself the immortal tree of love for Kṛṣṇa, and who both gives away its fruits and enjoys them.[^2]

প্রভু কহে, আমি ‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ নাম ধরি ৷  
নাম সার্থক হয়, যদি প্রেমে বিশ্ব ভরি ॥ ৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, āmi ‘viśvambhara’ nāma dhari  
the Lord  said  I  ‘Viśvambhara’  the name  bear  
nāma sārthaka haya, yadi preme viśva bhari  
the name  meaningful  becomes  if  with love  the universe  I fill

(7) The Lord said to Himself: “I bear the name Viśvambhara, ‘He who fills the universe’. My name will be true if I fill the universe with love.”[^3]

এত চিন্তি’ লৈলা প্রভু মালাকার-ধর্ম্ম ৷  
নবদ্বীপে আরম্ভিলা ফলোদ্যান-কর্ম্ম ॥ ৮ ॥

eta cinti’ lailā prabhu mālākāra-dharma  
thus  thinking  took  the Lord  of a gardener  the duty  
navadvīpe ārambhilā phalodyāna-karma  
in Navadvīpa  began  of a fruit garden  the work

(8) Thinking thus, the Lord took up the work of a gardener and began to lay out an orchard in Navadvīpa.

শ্রীচৈতন্য মালাকার পৃথিবীতে আনি’ ৷  
ভক্তি-কল্পতরু রোপিলা সিঞ্চি’ ইচ্ছা-পানি ॥ ৯ ॥

śrī-caitanya mālākāra pṛthivīte āni’  
Śrī  Caitanya  the gardener  to the earth  bringing  
bhakti-kalpataru ropilā siñci’ icchā-pāni  
the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti  planted  watering  with the water of His will

(9) Bringing it to the earth, the gardener Śrī Caitanya planted the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti and watered it with the water of His will.

জয় শ্রীমাধবপুরী কৃষ্ণপ্রেমপূর ৷  
ভক্তিকল্পতরুর তেঁহো প্রথম অঙ্কুর ॥ ১০ ॥

jaya śrī mādhavapurī kṛṣṇa-prema-pūra  
glory  to Śrī Mādhava Purī  with love for Kṛṣṇa  brimming  
bhakti-kalpatarura tem̐ho prathama aṅkura  
of the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti  he  the first  sprout

(10) Glory to Śrī Mādhava Purī, brimming with love for Kṛṣṇa! He is the first sprout of the wish-fulfilling tree of bhakti.[^4]

শ্রীঈশ্বরপুরী-রূপে অঙ্কুর পুষ্ট হৈল ৷  
আপনে চৈতন্যমালী স্কন্ধ উপজিল ॥ ১১ ॥

śrī-īśvarapurī-rūpe aṅkura puṣṭa haila  
Śrī Īśvara Purī  in the form of  the sprout  strong  became  
āpane caitanya-mālī skandha upajila  
Himself  Caitanya the gardener  the trunk  brought forth

(11) In the form of Śrī Īśvara Purī the sprout grew strong, and then the gardener, Śrī Caitanya Himself, became the trunk of the tree.[^5]

নিজাচিন্ত্যশক্ত্যে মালী হঞা স্কন্ধ হয় ৷  
সকল শাখার সেই স্কন্ধ মূলাশ্রয় ॥ ১২ ॥

nijācintya-śaktye mālī hañā skandha haya  
by His own inconceivable power  the gardener  being  the trunk  becomes  
sakala śākhāra sei skandha mūlāśraya  
of all  the branches  that  trunk  the original support

(12) By His own inconceivable power the gardener became the trunk, and that trunk is the original support of all the branches.

পরমানন্দ পুরী, আর কেশব ভারতী ৷  
ব্রহ্মানন্দ পুরী, আর ব্রহ্মানন্দ ভারতী ॥ ১৩ ॥

বিষ্ণুপুরী, কেশবপুরী, পুরী কৃষ্ণানন্দ ৷  
শ্রীনৃসিংহতীর্থ, আর পুরী সুখানন্দ ॥ ১৪ ॥

এই নব মূল নিকসিল বৃক্ষমূলে ৷  
এই নব মূলে বৃক্ষ করিল নিশ্চলে ॥ ১৫ ॥

paramānanda purī, āra keśava bhāratī  
Paramānanda  Purī  and  Keśava  Bhāratī  
brahmānanda purī, āra brahmānanda bhāratī  
Brahmānanda  Purī  and  Brahmānanda  Bhāratī  
viṣṇu-purī, keśava-purī, purī kṛṣṇānanda  
Viṣṇu Purī  Keśava Purī  Purī  Kṛṣṇānanda  
śrī-nṛsiṁhatīrtha, āra purī sukhānanda  
Śrī Nṛsiṁha Tīrtha  and  Purī  Sukhānanda  
ei nava mūla nikasila vṛkṣa-mūle  
these  nine  roots  sprang up  at the base of the tree  
ei nava mūle vṛkṣa karila niścale  
on these  nine  roots  the tree  made  immovable

(13–15) At the base of the tree nine roots sprang up: Paramānanda Purī and Keśava Bhāratī, Brahmānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī, Viṣṇu Purī, Keśava Purī, Kṛṣṇānanda Purī, Śrī Nṛsiṁha Tīrtha and Sukhānanda Purī. On these nine roots the tree became immovable.[^6]

মধ্যমূল পরমানন্দ পুরী মহাধীর ৷  
এই নব মূলে বৃক্ষ করিল সুস্থির ॥ ১৬ ॥

madhya-mūla paramānanda purī mahā-dhīra  
the central root  Paramānanda  Purī  greatly steadfast  
ei nava mūle vṛkṣa karila susthira  
on these  nine  roots  the tree  made  firm

(16) The central root is Paramānanda Purī, a man of great steadfastness, and on these nine roots the tree stands secure.[^7]

স্কন্ধের উপরে বহু শাখা নিকসিল ৷  
উপরি উপরি শাখা অসংখ্য হইল ॥ ১৭ ॥

skandhera upare bahu śākhā nikasila  
of the trunk  above  many  branches  sprang out  
upari upari śākhā asaṅkhya haila  
higher  and higher  branches  countless  became

(17) From the trunk many branches grew, and as they rose higher and higher, the branches became countless.

বিশ বিশ শাখা করি’ এক এক মণ্ডল ৷  
মহা-মহা-শাখা ছাইল ব্রহ্মাণ্ড সকল ॥ ১৮ ॥

biśa biśa śākhā kari’ eka eka maṇḍala  
twenty  twenty  branches  making  one  one  circle  
mahā-mahā-śākhā chāila brahmāṇḍa sakala  
great  great  branches  covered  universes  all

(18) Every twenty branches formed a circle of their own, and these mighty boughs covered all the universes.

একৈক শাখাতে উপশাখা শত শত ৷  
যত উপজিল শাখা কে গণিবে কত ॥ ১৯ ॥

ekaika śākhāte upaśākhā śata śata  
on each  branch  sub-branches  hundreds  hundreds  
yata upajila śākhā ke gaṇibe kata  
as many as  sprang up  branches  who  will count  how many

(19) On every branch there are hundreds of smaller branches. Who can count all the branches that have grown?

মুখ্য মুখ্য শাখাগণের নাম-গণন ৷  
আগে ত’ করিব, শুন বৃক্ষের বর্ণন ॥ ২০ ॥

mukhya mukhya śākhā-gaṇera nāma-gaṇana  
chief  chief  of the branches  of the names  the counting  
āge ta’ kariba, śuna vṛkṣera varṇana  
further on  indeed  I shall do  hear  of the tree  the description

(20) I shall list the names of the chief branches further on; now hear the description of the tree.[^8]

বৃক্ষের উপরে শাখা হৈল দুই স্কন্ধ ৷  
এক ‘অদ্বৈত’ নাম, আর ‘নিত্যানন্দ’ ॥ ২১ ॥

vṛkṣera upare śākhā haila dui skandha  
of the tree  above  branches  became  two  trunks  
eka ‘advaita’ nāma, āra ‘nityānanda’  
one  ‘Advaita’  named  the other  ‘Nityānanda’

(21) Higher up, the tree branched into two trunks: one is named Advaita, and the other Nityānanda.[^9]

সেই দুইস্কন্ধে শাখা যত উপজিল ৷  
তার উপশাখাগণে জগৎ ছাইল ॥ ২২ ॥

sei dui-skandhe śākhā yata upajila  
on those  two trunks  branches  as many as  sprang up  
tāra upaśākhā-gaṇe jagat chāila  
their  sub-branches  the world  covered

(22) Many branches sprang from those two trunks, and their offshoots covered the world.[^10]

বড় শাখা, উপশাখা, তার উপশাখা ৷  
জগৎ ব্যাপিল তার কে করিবে লেখা ॥ ২৩ ॥

baḍa śākhā, upaśākhā, tāra upaśākhā  
great  branches  sub-branches  their  sub-branches  
jagat vyāpila tāra ke karibe lekhā  
the world  pervaded  of them  who  will make  a record

(23) Great branches, smaller branches and their offshoots filled the world. Who could ever write them all down?

শিষ্য, প্রশিষ্য, আর উপশিষ্যগণ ৷  
জগৎ ব্যাপিল তার নাহিক গণন ॥ ২৪ ॥

śiṣya, praśiṣya, āra upaśiṣya-gaṇa  
disciples  grand-disciples  and  later disciples  
jagat vyāpila tāra nāhika gaṇana  
the world  pervaded  of them  there is no  counting

(24) Disciples, the disciples of disciples and their followers spread over the world beyond all counting.

উড়ুম্বর-বৃক্ষ যেন ফলে সর্ব্ব অঙ্গে ৷  
এইমত ভক্তিবৃক্ষে সর্ব্বত্র ফল লাগে ॥ ২৫ ॥

uḍumbara-vṛkṣa yena phale sarva aṅge  
the udumbara tree  as  bears fruit  on all  limbs  
ei mata bhakti-vṛkṣe sarvatra phala lāge  
in this way  on the tree of bhakti  everywhere  fruit  appears

(25) As the udumbara fig bears fruit on all its limbs, so the tree of bhakti bears fruit everywhere.

মূলস্কন্ধের শাখা-উপশাখাগণে ৷  
লাগিল যে প্রেমফল, — অমৃতকে জিনে ॥ ২৬ ॥

mūla-skandhera śākhā-upaśākhā-gaṇe  
of the main trunk  on the branches and sub-branches  
lāgila ye prema-phala, — amṛtake jine  
grew  which  the fruit of love  nectar  surpasses

(26) On the branches and offshoots of the main trunk grew the fruit of love, sweeter than nectar.

পাকিল যে প্রেমফল অমৃত-মধুর ৷  
বিলায় চৈতন্যমালী, নাহি লয় মূল ॥ ২৭ ॥

pākila ye prema-phala amṛta-madhura  
ripened  which  the fruit of love  nectar-sweet  
vilāya caitanya-mālī, nāhi laya mūla  
distributes  Caitanya the gardener  not  takes  price

(27) When the fruit of love ripened, sweet as nectar, the gardener Caitanya gave it away and asked no price.

ত্রিজগতে যত আছে ধন-রত্নমণি ৷  
একফলের মূল্য করি’ তাহা নাহি গণি ॥ ২৮ ॥

tri-jagate yata āche dhana-ratnamaṇi  
in the three worlds  as much as  there is  wealth  jewels  gems  
eka-phalera mūlya kari’ tāhā nāhi gaṇi  
of one fruit  the price  making  that  not  I count

(28) All the wealth and jewels of the three worlds I would not count as the price of a single fruit.

মাগে বা না মাগে কেহ, পাত্র বা অপাত্র ৷  
ইহার বিচার নাহি জানে, দেয় মাত্র ॥ ২৯ ॥

māge vā nā māge keha, pātra vā apātra  
asks  or  not  asks  anyone  worthy  or  unworthy  
ihāra vicāra nāhi jāne, deya mātra  
of this  consideration  not  He knows  He gives  simply

(29) Whether one asks or not, whether one is worthy or unworthy, He does not consider; He simply gives.

অঞ্জলি অঞ্জলি ভরি’ ফেলে চতুর্দ্দিশে ৷  
দরিদ্র কুড়াঞা খায়, মালাকার হাসে ॥ ৩০ ॥

añjali añjali bhari’ phele caturdiśe  
handful  handful  filling  He throws  in the four directions  
daridra kuḍāñā khāya, mālākāra hāse  
the poor  gathering  eat  the gardener  smiles

(30) Filling His hands again and again, He scatters the fruit in all directions; the poor gather it up and eat, and the gardener smiles.

---

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “By Gaura’s mercy the impossible becomes possible.” *Anubhāṣya*: *jagad-guru* — the guru of all the worlds, worthy of everyone’s worship.

[^2]: Heading in the edition: “The gardener is Mahāprabhu Himself.” *Anubhāṣya*: *amara-taru* — the unfading, imperishable tree, that is, the wish-fulfilling tree (*kalpa-vṛkṣa*); its fruits are the fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa.

[^3]: Heading in the edition: “Why He became the gardener: so that His being the Lord of *abhidheya*, the means to the goal, should bear fruit.”

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śrī Mādhava Purī is Mādhavendra Purī, a renowned sannyāsī of the sampradāya of Śrī Madhvācārya; Śrī Caitanya is the disciple of his disciple. Before him there was no clear sign of prema-bhakti in the Madhva sampradāya, and his verse *ayi dīna-dayārdra nātha* holds, as a seed, what Mahāprabhu was to teach.

[^5]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* and *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Īśvara Purī, the Guru who gave Mahāprabhu His mantra, was born into a brāhmaṇa family in Kumārahaṭṭa (Hālisahar) and was the dearest disciple of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, whom he served with complete devotion (Antya 8.26–29). Before initiating Mahāprabhu in Gayā he lived for some months in Navadvīpa in the house of Gopīnātha Ācārya and read Him his own *Kṛṣṇa-līlāmṛta* (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 7). When Mahāprabhu came to Kumārahaṭṭa, He took earth from the place of Īśvara Purī’s birth, teaching everyone devotion to the Guru.

[^6]: Heading in the edition: “Nine sannyāsīs — nine roots.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the sannyāsīs named Purī are related by succession to Śrī Īśvara Purī, and those named Bhāratī to Keśava Bhāratī, who gave Mahāprabhu sannyāsa. *Anubhāṣya*: Keśava Bhāratī belonged to the Bhāratī branch of the orders of sannyāsa founded by Śaṅkara; some say he had received a mantra from Śrī Mādhavendra Purī and was a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī. In Śaka 1432 (1510) he gave sannyāsa to Nimāi Paṇḍita in Kāṭoyā. Brahmānanda Purī sang with Mahāprabhu in the kīrtanas of Navadvīpa and came with Him to Nīlācala. Brahmānanda Bhāratī wore a deerskin; when Mahāprabhu came to him and pretended not to recognise him, he exchanged it for saffron cloth.

The *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* names him as Uddhava.
