“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 2  
**A Brief Account of the Antya-līlā: The Ravings of Love’s Madness**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বিচ্ছেদেঽস্মিন্ প্রভোরন্ত্যলীলা-সূত্রানুবর্ণনে ৷  
গৌরস্য কৃষ্ণবিচ্ছেদপ্রলাপাদ্যনুবর্ণ্যতে ॥ ১ ॥

vicchede ’smin prabhor antya-  
in chapter  this  of the Lord  final  
līlā-sūtrānuvarṇane  
pastimes  sūtras  in describing  
gaurasya kṛṣṇa-viccheda-  
of Gaura  from Kṛṣṇa  in separation  
pralāpādy anuvarṇyate  
raving and the rest  is described

(1) “In this chapter, as I set out in brief the Lord’s final pastimes, I describe Gaura’s raving (*pralāpa*) and His other states in separation from Kṛṣṇa.”[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

শেষ যে রহিল প্রভুর দ্বাদশ বৎসর ৷  
কৃষ্ণের বিয়োগ-স্ফূর্ত্তি হয় নিরন্তর ॥ ৩ ॥

śeṣa ye rahila prabhura dvādaśa vatsara  
the last  which  remained  of the Lord  twelve  years  
kṛṣṇera viyoga-sphūrti haya nirantara  
of Kṛṣṇa  separation  the feeling  was  constant

(3) Through the last twelve years that remained to Him, the Lord felt His separation from Kṛṣṇa without cease.

শ্রীরাধিকার চেষ্টা যেন উদ্ধব-দর্শনে ৷  
এইমত দশা প্রভুর হয় রাত্রি-দিনে ॥ ৪ ॥

śrī-rādhikāra ceṣṭā yena uddhava-darśane  
of Śrī Rādhikā  the behaviour  as  on seeing Uddhava  
ei mata daśā prabhura haya rātri-dine  
such  a state  of the Lord  was  night and day

(4) Night and day the Lord was in the very state of Śrī Rādhikā when She met Uddhava.

নিরন্তর হয় প্রভুর বিরহ-উন্মাদ ৷  
ভ্রমময় চেষ্টা সদা, প্রলাপময় বাদ ॥ ৫ ॥

nirantara haya prabhura viraha-unmāda  
constant  was  of the Lord  separation  madness  
bhrama-maya ceṣṭā sadā, pralāpa-maya vāda  
bewildered  actions  always  raving  speech

(5) The Lord was constantly in the divine madness of separation: He always acted as one bewildered, and all His speech was raving.[^2]

রোমকূপে রক্তোদ্গম, দন্ত সব হালে ৷  
ক্ষণে অঙ্গ ক্ষীণ হয়, ক্ষণে অঙ্গ ফুলে ॥ ৬ ॥

roma-kūpe raktodgama, danta saba hāle  
from the pores  blood oozing  teeth  all  loosened  
kṣaṇe aṅga kṣīṇa haya, kṣaṇe aṅga phule  
at one moment  the body  thin  becomes  at another  the body  swells

(6) Blood oozed from His pores, and all His teeth shook. At one moment His body grew thin, at another it swelled.

গম্ভীরা-ভিতরে রাত্রে নাহি নিদ্রা-লব ৷  
ভিত্তে মুখ-শির ঘষে, ক্ষত হয় সব ॥ ৭ ॥

gambhīrā-bhitare rātre nāhi nidrā-lava  
within the Gambhīrā  at night  not  of sleep  a particle  
bhitte mukha-śira ghaṣe, kṣata haya saba  
against the wall  face and head  rubs  wounds  become  all over

(7) Inside the Gambhīrā He did not sleep a moment at night. He rubbed His face and head against the wall, and they were covered with wounds.[^3]

তিন দ্বারে কপাট, প্রভু যায়েন বাহিরে ৷  
কভু সিংহদ্বারে পড়ে, কভু সিন্ধুনীরে ॥ ৮ ॥

tina dvāre kapāṭa, prabhu yāyena bāhire  
on three  doors  bolts  the Lord  goes  outside  
kabhu siṁha-dvāre paḍe, kabhu sindhu-nīre  
sometimes  at the Lion Gate  falls  sometimes  in the waters of the sea

(8) Though the three doors were bolted, the Lord would go outside. Sometimes He fell at the Lion Gate, sometimes into the waters of the sea.

চটক পর্ব্বত দেখি’ ‘গোবর্দ্ধন’ ভ্রমে ৷  
ধাঞা চলে আর্ত্তনাদ করিয়া ক্রন্দনে ॥ ৯ ॥

caṭaka parvata dekhi’ ‘govarddhana’ bhrame  
Caṭaka  hill  seeing  for Govardhana  mistaking  
dhāñā cale ārta-nāda kariyā krandane  
running  goes  cries of anguish  making  weeping

(9) The Caṭaka hill seemed to Him to be Govardhana, and He ran towards it, weeping and crying out in anguish.[^4]

উপবনোদ্যান দেখি’ বৃন্দাবন-জ্ঞান ৷  
তাঁহা যাই’ নাচে, গায়, ক্ষণে মূর্চ্ছা যা’ন ॥ ১০ ॥

upavanodyāna dekhi’ vṛndāvana-jñāna  
groves and gardens  seeing  as Vṛndāvana  conceiving  
tām̐hā yāi’ nāce, gāya, kṣaṇe mūrcchā yā’na  
there  going  dances  sings  at times  unconscious  falls

(10) The groves and gardens He took for Vṛndāvana; He would go there to dance and sing, and at times He fell unconscious.

কাঁহা নাহি শুনি যেই ভাবের বিকার ৷  
সেই ভাব হয় প্রভুর শরীরে প্রচার ॥ ১১ ॥

kām̐hā nāhi śuni yei bhāvera vikāra  
nowhere  not  heard  which  of ecstasy  transformations  
sei bhāva haya prabhura śarīre pracāra  
those  ecstasies  became  in the Lord’s  body  manifest

(11) Transformations of feeling never heard of anywhere showed themselves in the Lord’s body.

হস্তপদের সন্ধি সব বিতস্তি-প্রমাণে ৷  
সন্ধি ছাড়ি’ ভিন্ন হয়ে, চর্ম্ম রহে স্থানে ॥ ১২ ॥

hasta-padera sandhi saba vitasti-pramāṇe  
of hands and feet  the joints  all  a span  in length  
sandhi chāḍi’ bhinna haye, carma rahe sthāne  
joints  leaving  separate  became  the skin  remained  in place

(12) All the joints of His arms and legs came apart by a span; they left their sockets, and only the skin held them in place.[^5]

হস্ত, পদ, শির, সব শরীর-ভিতরে ৷  
প্রবিষ্ট হয় — কূর্ম্মরূপ দেখিয়ে প্রভুরে ॥ ১৩ ॥

hasta, pada, śira, saba śarīra-bhitare  
hands  feet  head  all  the body  within  
praviṣṭa haya — kūrma-rūpa dekhiye prabhure  
withdrawn  became  a tortoise’s form  was seen  in the Lord

(13) His hands, feet and head all withdrew into His body, and the Lord was seen in the form of a tortoise.

এইমত অদ্ভুত-ভাব শরীরে প্রকাশ ৷  
মনেতে শূন্যতা, বাক্যে হাহা-হুতাশ ॥ ১৪ ॥

ei mata adbhuta-bhāva śarīre prakāśa  
in this way  wondrous  ecstasies  in the body  manifest  
manete śūnyatā, vākye hā-hā-hutāśa  
in the mind  emptiness  in words  alas  despair

(14) So wondrous were the feelings that showed themselves in His body. His heart felt empty, and all His words were lament and despair:

কাঁহা মোর প্রাণনাথ মুরলীবদন ৷  
কাঁহা করোঁ, কাঁহা পাঙ ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ১৫ ॥

kām̐hā mora prāṇa-nātha muralī-vadana  
where  My  of life  Lord  with the flute at His lips  
kām̐hā karom̐, kām̐hā pāṅa vrajendranandana  
what  shall I do  where  shall I find  the son of the king of Vraja

(15) “Where is the Lord of My life, with the flute at His lips? What shall I do? Where shall I find the son of the king of Vraja?

কাহারে কহিব, কেবা জানে মোর দুঃখ ৷  
ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন বিনা ফাটে মোর বুক ॥ ১৬ ॥

kāhāre kahiba, kebā jāne mora duḥkha  
to whom  shall I tell  who  knows  My  sorrow  
vrajendranandana vinā phāṭe mora buka  
the son of the king of Vraja  without  bursts  My  heart

(16) To whom can I speak? Who knows My sorrow? Without the son of the king of Vraja My heart is breaking.”

এইমত বিলাপ করে বিহ্বল অন্তর ৷  
রায়ের নাটক-শ্লোক পড়ে নিরন্তর ॥ ১৭ ॥

ei mata vilāpa kare vihvala antara  
thus  lamentation  makes  bewildered  at heart  
rāyera nāṭaka-śloka paḍe nirantara  
of Rāya  from the drama  verses  recites  constantly

(17) Thus He lamented, bewildered at heart, and constantly recited verses from Rāmānanda Rāya’s drama:

প্রেমচ্ছেদরুজোঽবগচ্ছতি হরির্নায়ং ন চ প্রেম বা  
স্থানাস্থানমবৈতি নাপি মদনো জানাতি নো দুর্ব্বলাঃ ৷  
অন্যো বেদ ন চান্যদুঃখমখিলং নো জীবনং বাশ্রবং  
দ্বিত্রাণ্যেব দিনানি যৌবনমিদং হা হা বিধেঃ কা গতিঃ ॥ ১৮ ॥

prema-ccheda-rujo ’vagacchati harir nāyaṁ na ca prema vā  
love’s  breaking  the pains  knows  Hari  not  this  not  and  love  either  
sthānāsthānam avaiti nāpi madano jānāti no durbalāḥ  
proper and improper place  knows  nor even  Madana  knows  us  weak  
anyo veda na cānya-duḥkham akhilaṁ no jīvanaṁ vāśravaṁ  
another  knows  not  and  another’s  sorrow  all  our  life  is  not obedient  
dvi-trāṇy eva dināni yauvanam idaṁ hā hā vidheḥ kā gatiḥ  
two or three  only  days  youth  this  alas  alas  of the Creator  what  is the way

(18) (Śrī Rādhā laments:) “This Hari does not know the pains we suffer when love is broken; nor does love know where it should and should not strike; nor does even Madana know how weak we are. No one knows another’s sorrow in full; our very life is not in our control, and this youth lasts only two or three days. Alas, alas, O Creator, what is to become of us?” (Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka, 3.9)[^6]

উপজিল প্রেমাঙ্কুর, ভাঙ্গিল যে দুঃখ-পূর,  
কৃষ্ণ তাহা নাহি করে পান ৷  
বাহিরে নাগররাজ, ভিতরে শঠের কাজ,  
পরনারী বধে সাবধান ॥ ১৯ ॥

upajila premāṅkura, bhāṅgila ye duḥkha-pūra,  
sprouted  of love the sprout  broke (it)  that  of sorrow  flood  
kṛṣṇa tāhā nāhi kare pāna  
Kṛṣṇa  it  not  does  drink  
bāhire nāgara-rāja, bhitare śaṭhera kāja,  
outwardly  king of lovers  inwardly  of a cheat  the work  
para-nārī vadhe sāvadhāna  
others’ women  in killing  intent

(19) “In My heart the sprout of love came up, but a flood of sorrow broke it, and Kṛṣṇa pays it no heed. Outwardly He is the king of lovers, but within He does the work of a cheat, intent on killing other men’s wives.[^7]

সখি হে, না বুঝিয়ে বিধির বিধান ৷  
সুখ লাগি’ কৈলুঁ প্রীতি, হৈল বিপরীত গতি,  
এবে যায়, না রহে পরাণ ॥ ২০ ॥

sakhi he, nā bujhiye vidhira vidhāna  
O friend  not  I understand  the Creator’s  ordinance  
sukha lāgi’ kailum̐ prīti, haila viparīta gati,  
happiness  for the sake of  I loved  came out  contrary  the course  
ebe yāya, nā rahe parāṇa  
now  goes  not  stays  life

(20) O friend, I do not understand the Creator’s ordinance. I loved for the sake of happiness, but everything turned out the opposite. Now life is leaving Me; it will not stay.

কুটিল প্রেমা অগেয়ান, নাহি জানে স্থানাস্থান,  
ভাল-মন্দ নারে বিচারিতে ৷  
ক্রূর শঠের গুণডোরে, হাতে-গলে বান্ধি’ মোরে,  
রাখিয়াছে, নারি’ উকাশিতে ॥ ২১ ॥

kuṭila premā ageyāna, nāhi jāne sthānāsthāna,  
crooked  love  senseless  not  knows  proper and improper place  
bhāla-manda nāre vicārite  
good and bad  cannot  consider  
krūra śaṭhera guṇa-ḍore, hāte-gale bāndhi’ more,  
of the cruel  cheat  with the rope of qualities  hands and neck  binding  Me  
rākhiyāche, nāri’ ukāśite  
keeps  unable  to get free

(21) Love is crooked and senseless: it does not know where it should strike and where not, nor can it tell good from bad. With the rope of that cruel cheat’s qualities it has bound My hands and neck, and I cannot get free.[^8]

যে মদন তনুহীন, পরদ্রোহে পরবীণ,  
পাঁচ বাণ সন্ধে অনুক্ষণ ৷  
অবলার শরীরে, বিন্ধি’ কৈল জরজরে,  
দুঃখ দেয়, না লয় জীবন ॥ ২২ ॥

ye madana tanu-hīna, para-drohe paravīṇa,  
that  Madana  bodiless  in harming others  expert  
pām̐ca bāṇa sandhe anukṣaṇa  
five  arrows  fixes  constantly  
abalāra śarīre, vindhi’ kaila jarajare,  
of a helpless woman  into the body  piercing  has made  riddled  
duḥkha deya, nā laya jīvana  
pain  gives  not  takes  life

(22) That bodiless Madana, so skilled in harming others, constantly fixes his five arrows to the bow. He has pierced the body of a helpless woman through and through: he gives her pain but does not take her life.

অন্যের যে দুঃখ মনে, অন্যে তাহা নাহি জানে,  
সত্য এই শাস্ত্রের বিচার ৷  
অন্য জন কাঁহা লিখি, না জানয়ে প্রাণসখী,  
যাতে কহে ধৈর্য্য ধরিবার ॥ ২৩ ॥

anyera ye duḥkha mane, anye tāhā nāhi jāne,  
of another  which  sorrow  in the heart  another  that  not  knows  
satya ei śāstrera vicāra  
true  this  of scripture  the verdict  
anya jana kām̐hā likhi, nā jānaye prāṇa-sakhī,  
of other  people  why  write  not  knows  the friend dear as life  
yāte kahe dhairya dharibāra  
since  says  patience  to hold

(23) What sorrow lies in another’s heart, no one else knows: this verdict of scripture is true. Why speak of others? Even My friend, dear to Me as life, does not know it, for she only tells Me to be patient.

‘কৃষ্ণ — কৃপা-পারাবার, কভু করিবেন অঙ্গীকার’,  
সখি, তোর এ ব্যর্থ বচন ৷  
জীবের জীবন চঞ্চল, যেন পদ্মপত্রের জল,  
তত দিন জীবে কোন্ জন ॥ ২৪ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa — kṛpā-pārāvāra, kabhu karibena aṅgīkāra’  
Kṛṣṇa  of mercy  the ocean  some day  will accept  
sakhi, tora e vyartha vacana  
friend  your  these  useless  words  
jīvera jīvana cañcala, yena padma-patrera jala,  
of the living being  the life  unsteady  like  on a lotus leaf  water  
tata dina jīve kon jana  
so many  days  will live  which  person

(24) ‘Kṛṣṇa is an ocean of mercy; some day He will accept You’ — friend, these words of yours are empty. A living being’s life is as unsteady as water on a lotus leaf: who will live long enough?

শত বৎসর পর্য্যন্ত, জীবের জীবন অন্ত,  
এই বাক্য কহ না বিচারি’ ৷  
নারীর যৌবন-ধন, যারে কৃষ্ণ করে মন,  
সে যৌবন — দিন দুই-চারি ॥ ২৫ ॥

śata vatsara paryanta, jīvera jīvana anta,  
a hundred  years  up to  of the living being  life’s  end  
ei vākya kaha nā vicāri’  
this  statement  you make  not  considering  
nārīra yauvana-dhana, yāre kṛṣṇa kare mana,  
of a woman  youth’s  wealth  on which  Kṛṣṇa  sets  His mind  
se yauvana — dina dui-cāri  
that  youth  days  two or four

(25) You say without thinking that a living being’s life lasts a hundred years. But the wealth of a woman’s youth, on which Kṛṣṇa sets His mind, lasts only a few short days.

অগ্নি যৈছে নিজ-ধাম, দেখাইয়া অভিরাম,  
পতঙ্গীরে আকর্ষিয়া মারে ৷  
কৃষ্ণ ঐছে নিজ-গুণ, দেখাইয়া হরে মন,  
পাছে দুঃখ-সমুদ্রেতে ডারে ॥ ২৬ ॥

agni yaiche nija-dhāma, dekhāiyā abhirāma,  
fire  as  its own  light  showing  charming  
pataṅgīre ākarṣiyā māre  
the moth  attracting  kills  
kṛṣṇa aiche nija-guṇa, dekhāiyā hare mana,  
Kṛṣṇa  so  His own  qualities  showing  steals  the mind  
pāche duḥkha-samudrete ḍāre  
afterwards  of sorrow  into the ocean  casts

(26) As fire shows its charming light, draws the moth and kills it, so Kṛṣṇa shows His qualities and steals the heart, and then casts it into an ocean of sorrow.”

এতেক বিলাপ করি’, বিষাদে শ্রীগৌরহরি,  
উঘাড়িয়া দুঃখের কপাট ৷  
ভাবের তরঙ্গ-বলে, নানারূপে মন চলে,  
আর এক শ্লোক কৈল পাঠ ॥ ২৭ ॥

eteka vilāpa kari’, viṣāde śrī-gaurahari,  
so much  lament  making  in dejection  Śrī Gaurahari  
ughāḍiyā duḥkhera kapāṭa  
opening  of sorrow  the door  
bhāvera taraṅga-bale, nānārūpe mana cale,  
of ecstasy  of the waves  by the force  in many ways  the mind  moves  
āra eka śloka kaila pāṭha  
another  one  verse  made  recitation

(27) Lamenting so much in His dejection, Śrī Gaurahari opened the door of His sorrow. Tossed by the mighty waves of feeling, His mind turned this way and that, and He recited another verse:

শ্রীকৃষ্ণরূপাদিনিষেবণং বিনা  
ব্যর্থানি মেঽহান্যখিলেন্দ্রিয়াণ্যলম্ ৷  
পাষাণশুষ্কেন্ধনভারকাণ্যহো  
বিভর্ম্মি বা তানি কথং হতত্রপঃ ॥ ২৮ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-rūpādi-niṣevaṇaṁ vinā  
of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  form and the rest  service  without  
vyarthāni me ’hāny akhilendriyāṇy alam  
useless  My  days  all senses  utterly  
pāṣāṇa-śuṣkendhana-bhārakāṇy aho  
of stone  dry wood  burdens  alas  
bibharmi vā tāni kathaṁ hata-trapaḥ  
I bear  how  them  shameless

(28) “Without serving Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s form and all else that is His, My days and all My senses are utterly useless. Alas, how can I, shameless, go on bearing these senses, burdens of stone and dry wood?[^9]

বংশীগানামৃত-ধাম, লাবণ্যামৃত-জন্মস্থান,  
যে না দেখে সে-চাঁদ বদন ৷  
সে নয়নে কিবা কাজ, পড়ুক্ তার মুণ্ডে বাজ,  
সে নয়ন রহে কি কারণ ॥ ২৯ ॥

vaṁśī-gānāmṛta-dhāma, lāvaṇyāmṛta-janma-sthāna,  
of the flute song’s nectar  abode  of beauty’s nectar  birthplace  
ye nā dekhe se-cām̐da vadana  
who  not  sees  that moon-like  face  
se nayane kibā kāja, paḍuk tāra muṇḍe vāja,  
of those  eyes  what  use  let fall  his  on the head  a thunderbolt  
se nayana rahe ki kāraṇa  
those  eyes  remain  for what  reason

(29) That moon-like face is the abode of the nectar of His flute song and the birthplace of the nectar of beauty. What use are the eyes of one who does not see it? Let a thunderbolt fall on his head! Why do such eyes remain?[^10]

সখি হে, শুন, মোর হত বিধিবল ৷  
মোর বপু-চিত্ত-মন, সকল ইন্দ্রিয়গণ,  
কৃষ্ণ বিনা সকল বিফল ॥ ৩০ ॥ধ্রু॥

sakhi he, śuna, mora hata vidhi-bala  
O friend  hear  My  struck  destiny’s power  
mora vapu-citta-mana, sakala indriya-gaṇa,  
My  body heart mind  all  senses  
kṛṣṇa vinā sakala viphala  
Kṛṣṇa  without  all  fruitless

(30) O friend, hear Me: My fortune is struck down. My body, heart and mind, and all My senses — without Kṛṣṇa all are fruitless.

কৃষ্ণের মধুর বাণী, অমৃতের তরঙ্গিণী,  
তার প্রবেশ নাহি যে শ্রবণে ৷  
কাণাকড়ি-ছিদ্র সম, জানিহ সে শ্রবণ,  
তার জন্ম হৈল অকারণে ॥ ৩১ ॥

kṛṣṇera madhura vāṇī, amṛtera taraṅgiṇī,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  sweet  words  of nectar  a river  
tāra praveśa nāhi ye śravaṇe  
their  entry  is not  into which  ears  
kāṇākaḍi-chidra sama, jāniha se śravaṇa,  
of a holed cowrie shell  a hole  like  know  those  ears  
tāra janma haila akāraṇe  
their  birth  was  in vain

(31) Kṛṣṇa’s sweet words are a river of nectar. Know that the ears they do not enter are like the hole in a broken cowrie shell: their birth was in vain.

কৃষ্ণের অধরামৃত, কৃষ্ণ-গুণ-চরিত,  
সুধাসার-স্বাদু-বিনিন্দন ৷  
তার স্বাদ যে না জানে, জন্মিয়া না মৈল কেনে,  
সে রসনা ভেক-জিহ্বা সম ॥ ৩২ ॥

kṛṣṇera adharāmṛta, kṛṣṇa-guṇa-carita,  
of Kṛṣṇa  the nectar of the lips  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  and deeds  
sudhā-sāra-svādu-vinindana  
of the essence of nectar  the taste  surpassing  
tāra svāda ye nā jāne, janmiyā nā maila kene,  
their  taste  who  not  knows  being born  not  died  why  
se rasanā bheka-jihvā sama  
that  tongue  a frog’s tongue  like

(32) The nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips, and Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and deeds, surpass in taste the very essence of nectar. Why did one who does not know their taste not die at birth? That tongue is like a frog’s tongue.

মৃগমদ-নীলোৎপল, মিলনে যে পরিমল,  
যেই হরে তার গর্ব্ব-মান ৷  
হেন কৃষ্ণ-অঙ্গ-গন্ধ, যার নাহি সে সম্বন্ধ,  
সেই নাসা ভস্ত্রার সমান ॥ ৩৩ ॥

mṛga-mada nīlotpala, milane ye parimala,  
of musk  and the blue lotus  in mingling  which  fragrance  
yei hare tāra garva-māna  
that  steals  its  pride  
hena kṛṣṇa-aṅga-gandha, yāra nāhi se sambandha,  
such  Kṛṣṇa’s  body’s  fragrance  whose  not  that  connection  
sei nāsā bhastrāra samāna  
that  nose  a bellows  like

(33) The fragrance of Kṛṣṇa’s body steals the pride even of the scent born from mingling musk and the blue lotus. The nose that has no connection with that fragrance is like a bellows.

কৃষ্ণ-কর-পদতল, কোটিচন্দ্র-সুশীতল,  
তার স্পর্শ যেন স্পর্শমণি ৷  
তার স্পর্শ নাহি যার, সে যাউক্ ছারখার,  
সেই বপু লৌহ-সম জানি ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kara-pada-tala, koṭi-candra-suśītala,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  hands  and feet  the palms and soles  than millions of moons  cooler  
tāra sparśa yena sparśa-maṇi  
their  touch  like  a touchstone  
tāra sparśa nāhi yāra, se yāuk chārakhāra,  
their  touch  not  whose  he  let go  to ruin  
sei vapu lauha-sama jāni  
that  body  like iron  I know

(34) The palms of Kṛṣṇa’s hands and the soles of His feet are cooler than millions of moons, and their touch is like a touchstone. Let one who has not known that touch go to ruin: I know his body is like iron.”[^11]

করি’ এত বিলাপন, প্রভু শ্রীশচীনন্দন,  
উঘাড়িয়া হৃদয়ের শোক ৷  
দৈন্য-নির্ব্বেদ-বিষাদে, হৃদয়ের অবসাদে,  
পুনরপি পড়ে এক শ্লোক ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kari’ eta vilāpana, prabhu śrīśacīnandana,  
making  so much  lament  the Lord  Śrī Śacīnandana  
ughāḍiyā hṛdayera śoka  
opening  of the heart  the grief  
dainya-nirveda-viṣāde, hṛdayera avasāde,  
in humility  despondency  dejection  of the heart  in exhaustion  
punarapi paḍe eka śloka  
again  recites  one  verse

(35) Lamenting so much, the Lord, Śrī Śacīnandana, laid open the grief of His heart. In humility, despondency and dejection, His heart exhausted, He recited another verse:

যদা যাতো দৈবান্মধুরিপুরসৌ লোচনপথং  
তদাস্মাকং চেতো মদনহতকেনাহৃতমভূৎ ৷  
পুনর্যস্মিন্নেষ ক্ষণমপি দৃশোরেতি পদবীং  
বিধাস্যামস্তস্মিন্নখিলঘটিকা রত্নখচিতাঃ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

yadā yāto daivān madhu-ripur asau locana-pathaṁ  
when  came  by fortune  the enemy of Madhu  this  of the eyes  on the path  
tadāsmākaṁ ceto madana-hatakenāhṛtam abhūt  
then  our  mind  by wretched Madana  stolen  was  
punar yasminn eṣa kṣaṇam api dṛśor eti padavīṁ  
again  in which  He  a moment  even  of the eyes  comes  on the path  
vidhāsyāmas tasminn akhila-ghaṭikā ratna-khacitāḥ  
we shall make  in it  all the ghaṭikās  with jewels  studded

(36) “When by good fortune Madhu’s enemy came within our sight, our mind was stolen by that wretched Madana. When He comes again into our sight even for a moment, we shall stud all the *ghaṭikās* of that time with jewels.” (Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka, 3.11)

যে কালে বা স্বপনে, দেখিনু বংশীবদনে,  
সেই কালে আইলা দুই বৈরী ৷  
‘আনন্দ’ আর ‘মদন’, হরি’ নিল মোর মন,  
দেখিতে না পাইলুঁ নেত্র ভরি’ ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ye kāle vā svapane, dekhinu vaṁśī-vadane,  
at the time  or  as in a dream  I saw  the one with the flute at His lips  
sei kāle āilā dui vairī  
at that  time  came  two  enemies  
‘ānanda’ āra ‘madana’, hari’ nila mora mana,  
bliss  and  Madana  stealing  took  My  mind  
dekhite nā pāilum̐ netra bhari’  
to see  not  I could  eyes  filling

(37) “Whenever I saw Him with the flute at His lips, even in a dream, two enemies came at once — Bliss and Madana. They stole My mind, and I could not see Him to My eyes’ content.

পুনঃ যদি কোন ক্ষণ, করায় কৃষ্ণ দরশন,  
তবে সেই ঘটী-ক্ষণ-পল ৷  
দিয়া মাল্যচন্দন, নানা রত্ন-আভরণ,  
অলঙ্কৃত করিমু সকল ॥ ৩৮ ॥

punaḥ yadi kona kṣaṇa, karāya kṛṣṇa daraśana  
again  if  some  moment  grants  Kṛṣṇa’s  sight  
tabe sei ghaṭī-kṣaṇa-pala  
then  those  ghaṭīs  kṣaṇas  and palas  
diyā mālya-candana, nānā ratna-ābharaṇa,  
giving  garlands  and sandal  various  jewelled  ornaments  
alaṅkṛta karimu sakala  
adorned  I shall make  all

(38) If some moment ever again grants Me the sight of Kṛṣṇa, then I shall adorn all those *ghaṭīs*, *kṣaṇas* and *palas* with garlands and sandal paste and many jewelled ornaments.”

ক্ষণে বাহ্য হৈল মন, আগে দেখে দুই জন,  
তাঁরে পুছে, — আমি না চৈতন্য ?  
স্বপ্নপ্রায় কি দেখিনু, কিবা আমি প্রলাপিনু,  
তোমরা কিছু শুনিয়াছ দৈন্য ? ॥ ৩৯ ॥

kṣaṇe bāhya haila mana, āge dekhe dui jana,  
in a moment  external  became  the mind  before  sees  two  persons  
tām̐re puche, — āmi nā caitanya?  
to them  asks  I  not  Caitanya (conscious)  
svapna-prāya ki dekhinu, kibā āmi pralāpinu,  
like a dream  what  I saw  what  I  raved  
tomarā kichu śuniyācha dainya?  
you  anything  heard  of humble words

(39) In a moment His mind came to external consciousness, and seeing two persons before Him, He asked them: “Am I not Caitanya? What did I see, as though in a dream? What did I say in My raving? Did you hear any words of misery?[^12]

শুন, মোর প্রাণের বান্ধব ৷  
নাহি কৃষ্ণ-প্রেমধন, দরিদ্র মোর জীবন,  
দেহেন্দ্রিয় বৃথা মোর সব ॥ ৪০ ॥

śuna, mora prāṇera bāndhava  
hear  My  of life  friend  
nāhi kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana, daridra mora jīvana,  
not  for Kṛṣṇa  love’s wealth  poor  My  life  
dehendriya vṛthā mora saba  
body and senses  useless  My  all

(40) Hear Me, friend of My life: I have no wealth of love for Kṛṣṇa. My life is poor, and My body and senses are all useless.”

পুনঃ কহে, — হায় হায়, শুন, স্বরূপ-রামরায়,  
এই মোর হৃদয় নিশ্চয় ৷  
শুনি, করহ বিচার, হয়, নয় — কহ সার,  
এত বলি’ শ্লোক উচ্চারয় ॥ ৪১ ॥

punaḥ kahe, — hāya hāya, śuna, svarūpa-rāmarāya,  
again  says  alas  alas  hear  Svarūpa and Rāmarāya  
ei mora hṛdaya niścaya  
this  My  heart’s  conviction  
śuni, karaha vicāra, haya, naya — kaha sāra,  
having heard  consider  is  is not  tell  the truth  
eta bali’ śloka uccāraya  
so  saying  a verse  utters

(41) Again He said: “Alas, alas! Hear, Svarūpa and Rāmarāya, what My heart is sure of. Having heard it, consider and tell Me truly whether it is so or not.” Saying this, He uttered a verse:

কইঅবরহিঅং পেম্মং ণ হি হোই মাণুসে লোএ ৷  
জই হোই কস্স বিরহো বিরহে হোন্তম্মি কো জীঅই ॥ ৪২ ॥

ka-i-avarahi-aṁ pemmaṁ ṇa hi hoi māṇuse loe  
free of deceit  love  not  indeed  is  in the human  world  
ja-i hoi kassa viraho virahe hontammi ko jīa-i  
if  it is  whose  separation  in separation  occurring  who  lives

(42) “Love free of deceit does not exist in the human world. If it exists, how can anyone be separated? And if separation comes, who can live?”[^13]

অকৈতব কৃষ্ণপ্রেম, যেন জাম্বূনদ-হেম,  
সেই প্রেমা নৃলোকে না হয় ৷  
যদি হয় তার যোগ, না হয় তবে বিয়োগ,  
বিয়োগ হৈলে কেহ না জীয়য় ॥ ৪৩ ॥

akaitava kṛṣṇa-prema, yena jāmbūnada-hema,  
without deceit  love for Kṛṣṇa  like  of the Jāmbū river  gold  
sei premā nṛloke nā haya  
that  love  in the human world  not  exists  
yadi haya tāra yoga, nā haya tabe viyoga,  
if  is  with it  union  not  is  then  separation  
viyoga haile keha nā jīyaya  
separation  occurring  anyone  not  lives

(43) “Love for Kṛṣṇa without deceit is like the gold of the Jāmbū river: such love does not exist in the human world. If one is joined with it, there is no separation; and if separation comes, no one lives.”

এত কহি’ শচীসুত, শ্লোক পড়ে অদ্ভুত,  
শুনে দুঁহে এক-মন হঞা ৷  
আপন-হৃদয়-কাজ, কহিতে বাসিয়ে লাজ,  
তবু কহি লাজবীজ খাঞা ॥ ৪৪ ॥

eta kahi’ śacī-suta, śloka paḍe adbhuta,  
so  saying  the son of Śacī  a verse  recites  wondrous  
śune dum̐he eka-mana hañā  
hear  both  of one  mind  becoming  
āpana-hṛdaya-kāja, kahite vāsiye lāja,  
of My own  heart’s  affairs  to tell  I feel  shame  
tabu kahi lāja-bīja khāñā  
still  I tell  shame’s seed  eating

(44) Having said this, the son of Śacī recited a wondrous verse, and the two listened with one mind: “I feel ashamed to tell what is in My heart, yet I will speak, casting shame aside:[^14]

ন প্রেমগন্ধোঽস্তি দরাপি মে হরৌ  
ক্রন্দামি সৌভাগ্যভরং প্রকাশিতুম্ ৷  
বংশীবিলাস্যাননলোকনং বিনা  
বিভর্ম্মি যৎ প্রাণপতঙ্গকান্ বৃথা ॥ ৪৫ ॥

na prema-gandho ’sti darāpi me harau  
not  of love a trace  is  even slightly  in Me  for Hari  
krandāmi saubhāgya-bharaṁ prakāśitum  
I weep  of good fortune  the greatness  to display  
vaṁśī-vilāsy-ānana-lokanaṁ vinā  
of Him who plays the flute  of the face  without seeing  
bibharmi yat prāṇa-pataṅgakān vṛthā  
I sustain  that  the insects of My life  in vain

(45) There is not even a slight trace of love for Hari in Me. I weep only to display how great My good fortune is. Without seeing the face of Him who plays the flute, I sustain in vain this insect-like life of Mine.[^15]

দূরে শুদ্ধপ্রেমগন্ধ, কপট প্রেমের বন্ধ,  
সেহ মোর নাহি কৃষ্ণ-পায় ৷  
তবে যে করি ক্রন্দন, স্বসৌভাগ্য প্রখ্যাপন,  
করি, ইহা জানিহ নিশ্চয় ॥ ৪৬ ॥

dūre śuddha-prema-gandha, kapaṭa premera bandha,  
far  of pure love  the scent  of feigned  love  the bond  
seha mora nāhi kṛṣṇa-pāya  
even that  of Mine  is not  at Kṛṣṇa’s feet  
tabe ye kari krandana, sva-saubhāgya prakhyāpana,  
yet  that  I make  weeping  my good fortune’s  display  
kari, ihā jāniha niścaya  
I make  this  know  for certain

(46) Far from Me is even the scent of pure love; I do not have even a bond of feigned love at Kṛṣṇa’s feet. If I weep, I do it to display My good fortune — know this for certain.

যাতে বংশীধ্বনি-সুখ, না দেখি’ সে চাঁদ মুখ,  
যদ্যপি নাহিক ‘আলম্বন’ ৷  
নিজ-দেহে করি প্রীতি, কেবল কামের রীতি,  
প্রাণ-কীটেরে করিয়ে ধারণ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

yāte vaṁśī-dhvani-sukha, nā dekhi’ se cām̐da mukha,  
in which  the flute sound’s  joy  not  seeing  that  moon-like  face  
yadyapi nāhika ‘ālambana’  
although  there is not  support (ālambana)  
nija-dehe kari prīti, kevala kāmera rīti,  
My own body  I make  love  only  of kāma  the way  
prāṇa-kīṭere kariye dhāraṇa  
the insect of life  I make  maintenance

(47) Without seeing that moon-like face, in which lies the joy of the flute’s sound, I have no support (*ālambana*) for My life; still I love My own body. This is simply the way of *kāma*, desire for oneself: I maintain this insect of a life.[^16]

কৃষ্ণপ্রেমা সুনির্ম্মল, যেন শুদ্ধগঙ্গাজল,  
সেই প্রেমা — অমৃতের সিন্ধু ৷  
নির্ম্মল সে অনুরাগে, না লুকায় অন্য দাগে,  
শুক্লবস্ত্রে যৈছে মসীবিন্দু ॥ ৪৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa-premā sunirmala, yena śuddha-gaṅgā-jala,  
love for Kṛṣṇa  spotless  like  pure  Ganges  water  
sei premā — amṛtera sindhu  
that  love  of nectar  an ocean  
nirmala se anurāge, nā lukāya anya dāge,  
spotless  in that  attachment (anurāga)  not  hides  another  stain  
śukla-vastre yaiche masī-bindu  
on white cloth  like  an ink  drop

(48) Love for Kṛṣṇa is spotless, like the pure water of the Ganges; that love is an ocean of nectar. In that spotless attachment (*anurāga*) no other stain can hide, just as a drop of ink cannot hide on white cloth.[^17]

শুদ্ধপ্রেম-সুখসিন্ধু, পাই তার এক বিন্দু,  
সেই বিন্দু জগৎ ডুবায় ৷  
কহিবার যোগ্য নয়, তথাপি বাউলে কয়,  
কহিলে বা কেবা পাতিয়ায় ॥ ৪৯ ॥

śuddha-prema-sukha-sindhu, pāi tāra eka bindu,  
pure love’s  happiness  ocean  if I get  its  one  drop  
sei bindu jagat ḍubāya  
that  drop  the world  drowns  
kahibāra yogya naya, tathāpi bāule kaya,  
to tell  fit  not  yet  the madman  speaks  
kahile vā kebā pātiyāya  
when told  even  who  believes

(49) Pure love is an ocean of joy; if one gets a single drop of it, that drop drowns the world. It is not fit to be told, yet the madman speaks — and when it is told, who believes it?”

এইমত দিনে-দিনে, স্বরূপ-রামানন্দ-সনে,  
নিজ-ভাব করেন বিদিত ৷  
বাহ্যে বিষজ্বালা হয়, ভিতরে আনন্দময়,  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেমা অদ্ভুত চরিত ॥ ৫০ ॥

ei mata dine-dine, svarūpa-rāmānanda-sane,  
in this way  day after day  Svarūpa and Rāmānanda  with  
nija-bhāva karena vidita  
His own  feelings  makes  known  
bāhye viṣa-jvālā haya, bhitare ānanda-maya,  
outwardly  poison’s burning  is  within  blissful  
kṛṣṇa-premā adbhuta carita  
love for Kṛṣṇa  wondrous  nature

(50) In this way, day after day, He revealed His feelings to Svarūpa and Rāmānanda. Outwardly there was the burning of poison, but within was bliss: such is the wondrous nature of love for Kṛṣṇa.

এই প্রেমা-আস্বাদন, তপ্ত-ইক্ষু-চর্ব্বণ,  
মুখ জ্বলে, না যায় ত্যজন ৷  
সেই প্রেমা যাঁর মনে, তার বিক্রম সেই জানে,  
বিষামৃতে একত্র মিলন ॥ ৫১ ॥

ei premā-āsvādana, tapta-ikṣu-carvaṇa,  
this  love’s  tasting  hot  sugarcane  chewing  
mukha jvale, nā yāya tyajana  
the mouth  burns  not  can be  given up  
sei premā yām̐ra mane, tāra vikrama sei jāne,  
that  love  in whose  heart  its  power  he alone  knows  
viṣāmṛte ekatra milana  
of poison and nectar  in one  meeting

(51) Tasting this love is like chewing hot sugarcane: the mouth burns, yet one cannot give it up. Only he in whose heart that love lives knows its power — poison and nectar meeting in one.

পীড়াভির্নবকালকূট-কটুতাগর্ব্বস্য নির্ব্বাসনো  
নিঃস্যন্দেন মুদাং সুধা-মধুরিমাহঙ্কারসঙ্কোচনঃ ৷  
প্রেমা সুন্দরি নন্দনন্দনপরো জাগর্ত্তি যস্যান্তরে  
জ্ঞায়ন্তে স্ফুটমস্য বক্রমধুরাস্তেনৈব বিক্রান্তয়ঃ ॥ ৫২ ॥

pīḍābhir nava-kāla-kūṭa-kaṭutā-garvasya nirvāsano  
by its pains  of fresh  kālakūṭa poison  pungency’s  pride  banishing  
niḥsyandena mudāṁ sudhā-madhurimāhaṅkāra-saṅkocanaḥ  
by the outpouring  of joys  of nectar  sweetness’s  conceit  humbling  
premā sundari nanda-nandana-paro jāgarti yasyāntare  
love  O beautiful one  for Nanda’s son  directed  awakens  in whose  heart  
jñāyante sphuṭam asya vakra-madhurās tenaiva vikrāntayaḥ  
are known  clearly  its  crooked and sweet  by him  alone  powers

(52) “O beautiful one, when love for Nanda’s son awakens in someone’s heart, its pains drive out the pride that fresh *kālakūṭa* poison takes in its sting, and its outpouring of joys humbles the boast of nectar in its sweetness. Its crooked and sweet powers are clearly known to that person alone.” (Vidagdha-mādhava, 2.18)[^18]

যে কালে দেখে জগন্নাথ, শ্রীরাম-সুভদ্রা-সাথ,  
তবে জানে — আইলাম কুরুক্ষেত্র ৷  
সফল হৈল জীবন, দেখিলুঁ পদ্মলোচন,  
জুড়াইল তনু-মন-নেত্র ॥ ৫৩ ॥

ye kāle dekhe jagannātha, śrīrāma-subhadrā-sātha,  
when  sees  Jagannātha  with Śrī Rāma and Subhadrā  
tabe jāne — āilāma kurukṣetra  
then  thinks  I have come  to Kurukṣetra  
saphala haila jīvana, dekhilum̐ padma-locana,  
fruitful  has become  life  I have seen  the lotus-eyed One  
juḍāila tanu-mana-netra  
are soothed  body  mind  eyes

(53) When He saw Jagannātha with Śrī Balarāma and Subhadrā, He thought: “I have come to Kurukṣetra! My life has borne fruit: I have seen the lotus-eyed One, and My body, mind and eyes are soothed.”

গরুড়ের সন্নিধানে, রহি’ করে দরশনে,  
সে আনন্দের কি কহিব ব’লে ৷  
গরুড়-স্তম্ভের তলে, আছে এক নিম্ন খালে,  
সে খাল ভরিল অশ্রুজলে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

garuḍera sannidhāne, rahi’ kare daraśane,  
of Garuḍa  in the presence  staying  takes  darśana  
se ānandera ki kahiba ba’le  
that  bliss  what  shall I tell  in words  
garuḍa-stambhera tale, āche eka nimna khāle,  
of the Garuḍa pillar  at the base  is  a  low  channel  
se khāla bharila aśru-jale  
that  channel  filled  with tears

(54) Standing near Garuḍa, He took darśana of the Lord. What can I say of that bliss? At the base of the Garuḍa pillar there was a low channel in the floor, and it filled with His tears.[^19]

তাঁহা হৈতে ঘরে আসি’, মাটীর উপরে বসি’,  
নখে করে পৃথিবী লিখন ৷  
হা-হা কাঁহা বৃন্দাবন, কাঁহা গোপেন্দ্রনন্দন,  
কাঁহা সেই বংশীবদন ॥ ৫৫ ॥

tām̐hā haite ghare āsi’, māṭīra upare vasi’,  
from there  home  coming  the ground  on  sitting  
nakhe kare pṛthivī likhana  
with His nails  makes  on the earth  writing  
hā-hā kām̐hā vṛndāvana, kām̐hā gopendra-nandana,  
alas  where  is Vṛndāvana  where  the son of the king of cowherds  
kām̐hā sei vaṁśī-vadana  
where  that  one with the flute at His lips

(55) Returning home from there, He would sit on the ground and write on the earth with His nails: “Alas, where is Vṛndāvana? Where is the son of the king of the cowherds? Where is He with the flute at His lips?

কাঁহা সে ত্রিভঙ্গঠাম, কাঁহা সেই বেণুগান,  
কাঁহা সেই যমুনা-পুলিন ৷  
কাঁহা সে রাসবিলাস, কাঁহা নৃত্যগীত-হাস,  
কাঁহা প্রভু মদনমোহন ॥ ৫৬ ॥

kām̐hā se tri-bhaṅga-ṭhāma, kām̐hā sei veṇu-gāna,  
where  that  threefold-bending  stance  where  that  flute  song  
kām̐hā sei yamunā-pulina  
where  that  Yamunā’s  bank  
kām̐hā se rāsa-vilāsa, kām̐hā nṛtya-gīta-hāsa,  
where  that  rāsa  play  where  dancing  singing  laughter  
kām̐hā prabhu madana-mohana  
where  the Lord  Madana-mohana

(56) Where is that stance, bending in three places? Where is that song of the flute? Where is that bank of the Yamunā? Where is that play of the rāsa? Where are the dancing, the singing and the laughter? Where is My Lord, Madana-mohana?”

উঠিল নানা ভাবোদ্বেগ, মনে হৈল উদ্বেগ,  
ক্ষণমাত্র নারে গোঙাইতে ৷  
প্রবল বিরহানলে, ধৈর্য্য হৈল টলমলে,  
নানা শ্লোক লাগিলা পড়িতে ॥ ৫৭ ॥

uṭhila nānā bhāvodvega, mane haila udvega,  
arose  various  ecstasies’ agitation  in the mind  was  anxiety  
kṣaṇa-mātra nāre goṅāite  
for a moment  only  cannot  pass time  
prabala virahānale, dhairya haila ṭalamale,  
in the strong  fire of separation  composure  became  shaken  
nānā śloka lāgilā paḍite  
various  verses  began  to recite

(57) Many feelings surged up in turmoil, and His mind grew anxious; He could not endure a single moment. In the fierce fire of separation His composure tottered, and He began to recite various verses:

অমূন্যধন্যানি দিনান্তরাণি  
হরে ত্বদালোকনমন্তরেণ ৷  
অনাথবন্ধো করুণৈকসিন্ধো  
হা হন্ত হা হন্ত কথং নয়ামি ॥ ৫৮ ॥

amūny adhanyāni dināntarāṇi  
these  unfortunate  days and nights  
hare tvad-ālokanam antareṇa  
O Hari  Your sight  without  
anātha-bandho karuṇaika-sindho  
O friend of the helpless  of compassion the sole ocean  
hā hanta hā hanta kathaṁ nayāmi  
alas  alas  alas  alas  how  shall I pass

(58) “O Hari, O friend of the helpless, O sole ocean of compassion! Alas, alas, how shall I pass these unfortunate days and nights without seeing You?” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 41)[^20]

তোমার দর্শন-বিনে, অধন্য এ রাত্রি-দিনে,  
এই কাল না যায় কাটন ৷  
তুমি অনাথের বন্ধু, অপার করুণাসিন্ধু,  
কৃপা করি’ দেহ দরশন ॥ ৫৯ ॥

tomāra darśana-vine, adhanya e rātri-dine,  
Your  sight  without  unfortunate  these  nights and days  
ei kāla nā yāya kāṭana  
this  time  not  can  pass  
tumi anāthera bandhu, apāra karuṇā-sindhu,  
You  of the helpless  the friend  boundless  of compassion ocean  
kṛpā kari’ deha daraśana  
mercy  showing  give  sight

(59) “Without seeing You, these nights and days are unfortunate; this time will not pass. You are the friend of the helpless, a boundless ocean of compassion: be merciful and let Me see You.”

উঠিল ভাব-চাপল, মন হইল চঞ্চল,  
ভাবের গতি বুঝন না যায় ৷  
অদর্শনে পোড়ে মন, কেমনে পাব দরশন,  
কৃষ্ণ-ঠাঞি পুছেন উপায় ॥ ৬০ ॥

uṭhila bhāva-cāpala, mana haila cañcala,  
arose  ecstasy’s  restlessness  the mind  became  unsteady  
bhāvera gati bujhana nā yāya  
of ecstasy  the course  to understand  not  is possible  
adarśane poḍe mana, kemane pāba daraśana,  
without seeing  burns  the mind  how  shall I get  sight  
kṛṣṇa-ṭhāñi puchena upāya  
of Kṛṣṇa  He asks  the means

(60) Restless feeling arose and His mind grew unsteady; the course of such feeling cannot be understood. His mind burned from not seeing Kṛṣṇa, and He asked Kṛṣṇa Himself how He might see Him:

ত্বচ্ছৈশবং ত্রিভুবনাদ্ভুতমিত্যবেহি  
মচ্চাপলঞ্চ তব বা মম বাধিগম্যম্ ৷  
তৎ কিং করোমি বিরলং মুরলীবিলাসি  
মুগ্ধং মুখাম্বুজমুদীক্ষিতুমীক্ষণাভ্যাম্ ॥ ৬১ ॥

tvac-chaiśavaṁ tri-bhuvanādbhutam ity avehi  
Your boyhood  of the three worlds a wonder  so  know  
mac-cāpalaṁ ca tava vā mama vādhigamyam  
My restlessness  and  to You  or  to Me  alone  known  
tat kiṁ karomi viralaṁ muralī-vilāsi  
so  what  shall I do  in solitude  O flute-player  
mugdhaṁ mukhāmbujam udīkṣitum īkṣaṇābhyām  
charming  lotus face  to behold  with My two eyes

(61) “O flute-player, know that Your boyish charm and My restlessness are wonders of the three worlds, known only to You and Me. What then shall I do to gaze on Your charming lotus face with My own eyes, when We are alone?” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 32)[^21]

তোমার মাধুরী-বল, তাতে মোর চাপল,  
এই দুই, তুমি আমি জানি ৷  
কাঁহা করোঁ কাঁহা যাঙ, কাঁহা গেলে তোমা পাঙ,  
তাহা মোরে কহ ত’ আপনি ॥ ৬২ ॥

tomāra mādhurī-bala, tāte mora cāpala,  
Your  sweetness’s  power  by it  My  restlessness  
ei dui, tumi āmi jāni  
these  two  You (and)  I  know  
kām̐hā karom̐ kām̐hā yāṅa, kām̐hā gele tomā pāṅa,  
what  shall I do  where  shall I go  where  going  You  shall I find  
tāhā more kaha ta’ āpani  
that  to Me  tell  indeed  Yourself

(62) “The power of Your sweetness, and My restlessness because of it — these two only You and I know. What shall I do? Where shall I go? Where can I go to find You? Tell Me this Yourself!”

নানা-ভাবের প্রাবল্য, হৈল সন্ধি-শাবল্য,  
ভাবে-ভাবে হৈল মহারণ ৷  
ঔৎসুক্য, চাপল্য, দৈন্য, রোষামর্ষ আদি সৈন্য,  
প্রেমোন্মাদ — সবার কারণ ॥ ৬৩ ॥

nānā-bhāvera prābalya, haila sandhi-śābalya,  
of various  ecstasies  the force  arose  junction and mingling  
bhāve-bhāve haila mahā-raṇa  
ecstasy with ecstasy  there was  a great battle  
autsukya, cāpalya, dainya, roṣāmarṣa ādi sainya,  
eagerness  restlessness  humility  anger and indignation  and other  soldiers  
premonmāda — sabāra kāraṇa  
the madness of love  of all  the cause

(63) Under the force of many feelings they met and mingled (*sandhi* and *śābalya*), and feeling fought feeling in a great battle. Eagerness, restlessness, humility, anger, indignation and others were the soldiers, and the madness of love was the cause of it all.[^22]

মত্তগজ ভাবগণ, প্রভুর দেহ — ইক্ষুবন,  
গজযুদ্ধে বনের দলন ৷  
প্রভুর হৈল দিব্যোন্মাদ, তনুমনের অবসাদ,  
ভাবাবেশে করে সম্বোধন ॥ ৬৪ ॥

matta-gaja bhāva-gaṇa, prabhura deha — ikṣu-vana,  
maddened elephants  the ecstasies  the Lord’s  body  a sugarcane field  
gaja-yuddhe vanera dalana  
in the elephants’ fight  of the field  the trampling  
prabhura haila divyonmāda, tanu-manera avasāda,  
to the Lord  came  divine madness  of body and mind  exhaustion  
bhāvāveśe kare sambodhana  
in ecstatic absorption  makes  address

(64) His feelings were maddened elephants and the Lord’s body a field of sugarcane: in the elephants’ battle the field was trampled. Divine madness came upon the Lord, His body and mind were exhausted, and, absorbed in His feelings, He cried out:[^23]

হে দেব, হে দয়িত, হে ভুবনৈকবন্ধো,  
হে কৃষ্ণ, হে চপল, হে করুণৈকসিন্ধো ৷  
হে নাথ, হে রমণ, হে নয়নাভিরাম,  
হা হা কদা নু ভবিতাসি পদং দৃশোর্মে ॥ ৬৫ ॥

he deva, he dayita, he bhuvanaika-bandho,  
O God  O Beloved  O sole Friend of the world  
he kṛṣṇa, he capala, he karuṇaika-sindho,  
O Kṛṣṇa  O Fickle One  O sole Ocean of compassion  
he nātha, he ramaṇa, he nayanābhirāma  
O Lord  O Lover  O Delight of the eyes  
hā hā kadā nu bhavitāsi padaṁ dṛśor me  
alas  alas  when  indeed  will You be  on the path  of the eyes  of Mine

(65) “O God, O Beloved, O sole Friend of the world! O Kṛṣṇa, O Fickle One, O sole Ocean of compassion! O Lord, O Lover, O Delight of the eyes! Alas, alas, when will You again come before My eyes?” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 40)[^24]

উন্মাদের লক্ষণ, করায় কৃষ্ণ-স্ফুরণ,  
ভাবাবেশে উঠে প্রণয়-মান ৷  
সোল্লুণ্ঠ-বচন-রীতি, মান, গর্ব্ব, ব্যাজ-স্তুতি,  
কভু নিন্দা, কভু বা সম্মান ॥ ৬৬ ॥

unmādera lakṣaṇa, karāya kṛṣṇa-sphuraṇa,  
of madness  the signs  cause  Kṛṣṇa’s  appearance  
bhāvāveśe uṭhe praṇaya-māna  
in ecstatic absorption  arises  loving  pique  
solluṇṭha-vacana-rīti, māna, garva, vyāja-stuti,  
of mocking  words  the manner  pique  pride  veiled praise  
kabhu nindā, kabhu vā sammāna  
sometimes  censure  sometimes  even  honour

(66) The signs of madness made Kṛṣṇa appear before Him, and, absorbed in feeling, He was seized by loving pique (*praṇaya-māna*). His words turned mocking, sulky and proud, with praise in the guise of blame: now He censured, now He honoured.[^25]

তুমি দেব — ক্রীড়া-রত, ভুবনের নারী যত,  
তাহে কর অভীষ্ট ক্রীড়ন ৷  
তুমি মোর দয়িত, তাতে বৈস মোর চিত,  
মোর ভাগ্যে কৈলে আগমন ॥ ৬৭ ॥

tumi deva — krīḍā-rata, bhuvanera nārī yata,  
You  God  in play  absorbed  of the world  women  all  
tāhe kara abhīṣṭa krīḍana  
with them  You make  the play You desire  
tumi mora dayita, tāte vaisa mora cita,  
You  My  Beloved  therefore  sit  in My  heart  
mora bhāgye kaile āgamana  
by My  good fortune  You have made  Your coming

(67) “You are God (*deva*), absorbed in play: with all the women of the world You play as You like. You are My Beloved (*dayita*): so sit within My heart, since by My good fortune You have come.

ভুবনের নারীগণ, সবা’ কর আকর্ষণ,  
তাঁহা কর সব সমাধান ৷  
তুমি কৃষ্ণ — চিত্ত হর, ঐছে কোন্ পামর,  
তোমারে বা কেবা করে মান ॥ ৬৮ ॥

bhuvanera nārī-gaṇa, sabā’ kara ākarṣaṇa,  
of the world  the women  all  You make  attraction  
tām̐hā kara saba samādhāna  
there  You make  for all  satisfaction  
tumi kṛṣṇa — citta hara, aiche kon pāmara,  
You  Kṛṣṇa  hearts  You steal  such  what  rogue  
tomāre vā kebā kare māna  
You  indeed  who  makes  honour

(68) You attract all the women of the world and satisfy them all. You are Kṛṣṇa, the stealer of hearts — what a rogue! Who would ever honour You?

তোমার চপল মতি, একত্র না হয় স্থিতি,  
তাতে তোমার নাহি কিছু দোষ ৷  
তুমি ত’ করুণাসিন্ধু, আমার পরাণ-বন্ধু,  
তোমায় নাহি মোর কভু রোষ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

tomāra capala mati, ekatra nā haya sthiti,  
Your  fickle  mind  in one place  not  is  staying  
tāte tomāra nāhi kichu doṣa  
in that  Yours  is not  any  fault  
tumi ta’ karuṇā-sindhu, āmāra parāṇa-bandhu,  
You  indeed  compassion’s ocean  My  life’s  friend  
tomāya nāhi mora kabhu roṣa  
at You  is not  My  ever  anger

(69) Your mind is fickle; it never stays in one place — but that is no fault of Yours. You are an ocean of compassion, the friend of My life; I am never angry with You.

তুমি নাথ, — ব্রজপ্রাণ, ব্রজের কর পরিত্রাণ,  
বহুকার্য্যে নাহি অবকাশ ৷  
তুমি আমার রমণ, সুখ দিতে আগমন,  
এ তোমার বৈদগ্ধ্য-বিলাস ॥ ৭০ ॥

tumi nātha, — vraja-prāṇa, vrajera kara paritrāṇa,  
You  Lord  Vraja’s life  Vraja  You make  protection  
bahu kārye nāhi avakāśa  
with many tasks  is not  leisure  
tumi āmāra ramaṇa, sukha dite āgamana,  
You  My  Lover  happiness  to give  coming  
e tomāra vaidagdhya-vilāsa  
this  Your  cleverness’s  play

(70) You are the Lord (*nātha*), the life of Vraja; You protect Vraja, and with so many tasks You have no leisure. You are My Lover (*ramaṇa*); You come to give Me happiness — this is the play of Your cleverness.

মোর বাক্য নিন্দা মানি’, কৃষ্ণ ছাড়ি’ গেলা জানি,  
শুন, মোর এ স্তুতি-বচন ৷  
নয়নের অভিরাম, তুমি মোর ধন-প্রাণ,  
হাহা পুনঃ দেহ দরশন ॥ ৭১ ॥

mora vākya nindā māni’, kṛṣṇa chāḍi’ gelā jāni,  
My  words  as censure  taking  Kṛṣṇa  leaving  went  I know  
śuna, mora e stuti-vacana  
hear  My  this  praise  speech  
nayanera abhirāma, tumi mora dhana-prāṇa,  
of the eyes  delight  You  My  wealth and life  
hāhā punaḥ deha daraśana  
alas  again  give  sight

(71) Taking My words as censure, Kṛṣṇa has left Me, I know. Hear these words of praise: You are the delight of My eyes, My wealth and My life. Alas, show Yourself to Me again!”

স্তম্ভ, কম্প, প্রস্বেদ, বৈবর্ণ্য, অশ্রু, স্বরভেদ,  
দেহ হৈল পুলকে ব্যাপিত ৷  
হাসে, কান্দে, নাচে, গায়, উঠি’ ইতি-উতি ধায়,  
ক্ষণে ভূমে পড়িয়া মূর্চ্ছিত ॥ ৭২ ॥

stambha, kampa, prasveda, vaivarṇya, aśru, svara-bheda,  
stupor  trembling  perspiration  pallor  tears  faltering voice  
deha haila pulake vyāpita  
the body  became  with bristling hair  pervaded  
hāse, kānde, nāce, gāya, uṭhi’ iti-uti dhāya,  
laughs  weeps  dances  sings  rising  here and there  runs  
kṣaṇe bhūme paḍiyā mūrcchita  
at times  on the ground  falling  unconscious

(72) Stupor, trembling, perspiration, pallor, tears and a faltering voice appeared, and His body was covered with bristling hair. He laughed, wept, danced and sang; rising, He ran here and there, and at times fell unconscious to the ground.

মূর্চ্ছায় হৈল সাক্ষাৎকার, উঠি’ করে হুহুঙ্কার,  
কহে, — এই আইলা মহাশয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের মাধুরী-গুণে, নানা ভ্রম হয় মনে,  
শ্লোক পড়ি’ করয়ে নিশ্চয় ॥ ৭৩ ॥

mūrcchāya haila sākṣātkāra, uṭhi’ kare huhuṅkāra,  
in the swoon  there was  a meeting face to face  rising  makes  a loud roar  
kahe, — ei āilā mahāśaya  
says  here  has come  the Great One  
kṛṣṇera mādhurī-guṇe, nānā bhrama haya mane,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  by sweetness and qualities  various  bewilderment  is  in the mind  
śloka paḍi’ karaye niścaya  
a verse  reciting  makes  certain

(73) In His swoon He met Kṛṣṇa face to face. Rising, He gave a loud roar and said: “Here He has come, the Great One!” Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness and qualities filled His mind with many bewildered fancies, and by reciting a verse He made sure of what He saw:

মারঃ স্বয়ং নু মধুরদ্যুতিমণ্ডলং নু  
মাধুর্য্যমেব নু মনোনয়নামৃতং নু ৷  
বেণীমৃজো নু মম জীবিতবল্লভো নু  
কৃষ্ণোঽয়মভ্যুদয়তে মম লোচনায় ॥ ৭৪ ॥

māraḥ svayaṁ nu madhura-dyuti-maṇḍalaṁ nu  
Cupid  himself  is it  of sweet  radiance  an orb  is it  
mādhuryam eva nu mano-nayanāmṛtaṁ nu  
sweetness  itself  is it  for mind and eyes  nectar  is it  
veṇī-mṛjo nu mama jīvita-vallabho nu  
the loosener of braids  is it  of My  life  the beloved  is it  
kṛṣṇo ’yam abhyudayate mama locanāya  
Kṛṣṇa  this  rises  to My  eyes

(74) “Is this the god of love himself? Is it an orb of sweet radiance? Is it sweetness itself, nectar for the mind and eyes? Is it the One who loosens the braids, the beloved of My life? It is Kṛṣṇa who rises before My eyes!” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 68)[^26]

কিবা এই সাক্ষাৎ কাম, দ্যুতিবিম্ব-মূর্ত্তিমান্,  
কি মাধুর্য্য স্বয়ং মূর্ত্তিমন্ত ৷  
কিবা মনো-নেত্রোৎসব, কিবা প্রাণবল্লভ,  
সত্য কৃষ্ণ আইলা নেত্রানন্দ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kibā ei sākṣāt kāma, dyuti-bimba-mūrtimān,  
is it  this  in person  Kāma  of radiance’s orb  the embodiment  
ki mādhurya svayaṁ mūrtimanta  
is it  sweetness  itself  embodied  
kibā mano-netrotsava, kibā prāṇa-vallabha,  
is it  of mind and eyes the festival  is it  of life the beloved  
satya kṛṣṇa āilā netrānanda  
truly  Kṛṣṇa  has come  the joy of the eyes

(75) “Is this Kāma in person, an orb of radiance embodied? Is it sweetness itself in bodily form? Is it a feast for My mind and eyes? Is it the beloved of My life? Truly Kṛṣṇa, the joy of My eyes, has come!”

গুরু — নানা ভাবগণ, শিষ্য — প্রভুর তনু-মন,  
নানা রীতে সতত নাচায় ৷  
নির্ব্বেদ, বিষাদ, দৈন্য, চাপল্য, হর্ষ, ধৈর্য্য, মন্যু,  
এই নৃত্যে প্রভুর কাল যায় ॥ ৭৬ ॥

guru — nānā bhāva-gaṇa, śiṣya — prabhura tanu-mana,  
the guru  various  ecstasies  the disciple  the Lord’s  body and mind  
nānā rīte satata nācāya  
in various  ways  constantly  make dance  
nirveda, viṣāda, dainya, cāpalya, harṣa, dhairya, manyu,  
despondency  dejection  humility  restlessness  joy  composure  anger  
ei nṛtye prabhura kāla yāya  
in this  dance  the Lord’s  time  passes

(76) His many feelings were the guru, His body and mind the disciple, and in endless ways the guru made the disciple dance. Despondency, dejection, humility, restlessness, joy, composure and anger — in this dance the Lord’s time passed.[^27]

চণ্ডীদাস, বিদ্যাপতি, রায়ের নাটক-গীতি,  
কর্ণামৃত, শ্রীগীতগোবিন্দ ৷  
স্বরূপ-রামানন্দ-সনে, মহাপ্রভু রাত্রি-দিনে,  
গায়, শুনে — পরম আনন্দ ॥ ৭৭ ॥

caṇḍīdāsa, vidyāpati, rāyera nāṭaka-gīti,  
Caṇḍīdāsa  Vidyāpati  of Rāya  the drama’s  songs  
karṇāmṛta, śrī-gīta-govinda  
the Karṇāmṛta  the Śrī Gīta-govinda  
svarūpa-rāmānanda-sane, mahāprabhu rātri-dine,  
Svarūpa and Rāmānanda  with  Mahāprabhu  night and day  
gāya, śune — parama ānanda  
sings  hears  supreme  bliss

(77) With Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, Mahāprabhu night and day sang and heard the songs of Caṇḍīdāsa and Vidyāpati, Rāya’s drama, the *Karṇāmṛta* and the *Śrī Gīta-govinda* in supreme bliss.[^28]

পুরীর বাৎসল্য মুখ্য, রামানন্দের শুদ্ধসখ্য,  
গোবিন্দাদ্যের শুদ্ধদাস্যরস ৷  
গদাধর, জগদানন্দ, স্বরূপের (মুখ্য) রসানন্দ,  
এই চারিভাবে প্রভু বশ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

purīra vātsalya mukhya, rāmānandera śuddha-sakhya,  
of Purī  parental love  chief  of Rāmānanda  pure friendship  
govindādyera śuddha-dāsya-rasa  
of Govinda and others  pure servitude’s rasa  
gadādhara, jagadānanda, svarūpera (mukhya) rasānanda,  
Gadādhara  Jagadānanda  of Svarūpa  (the chief)  rasa’s bliss  
ei cāri-bhāve prabhu vaśa  
by these  four moods  the Lord  controlled

(78) Purī’s love was chiefly parental; Rāmānanda’s was pure friendship; Govinda and others had the rasa of pure servitude; and Gadādhara, Jagadānanda and Svarūpa knew the bliss of the chief rasa. By these four moods the Lord was held captive.[^29]

লীলাশুক-মত্ত জন, তাঁর হয় ভাবোদ্গম,  
ঈশ্বরে সে, — কি ইহা বিস্ময় ৷  
তাহে মুখ্য-রসাশ্রয়, হইয়াছেন মহাশয়,  
তাতে হয় সর্ব্বভাবোদয় ॥ ৭৯ ॥

līlāśuka-matta jana, tām̐ra haya bhāvodgama,  
Līlāśuka  a mortal  man  in him  arises  ecstasy  
īśvare se, — ki ihā vismaya  
in the Lord  that  what  in this  wonder  
tāhe mukhya-rasāśraya, haiyāchena mahāśaya,  
moreover  of the chief rasa the abode  has become  the Great One  
tāte haya sarva-bhāvodaya  
therefore  is  of all ecstasies the rising

(79) Even in Līlāśuka, a mortal man, such feelings arose; what wonder, then, if they arose in the Lord? Moreover, the Great One has become the abode (*āśraya*) of the chief rasa, and so every feeling rises in Him.[^30]

পূর্ব্বে ব্রজবিলাসে, যেই তিন অভিলাষে,  
যত্নেহ আস্বাদ না হৈল ৷  
শ্রীরাধার ভাবসার, আপনে করি’ অঙ্গীকার,  
সেই তিন বস্তু আস্বাদিল ॥ ৮০ ॥

pūrve vraja-vilāse, yei tina abhilāṣe,  
formerly  in the Vraja pastimes  which  three  desires  
yatneha āsvāda nā haila  
even with effort  tasted  not  were  
śrī-rādhāra bhāva-sāra, āpane kari’ aṅgīkāra,  
of Śrī Rādhā  the essence of feeling  Himself  making  acceptance  
sei tina vastu āsvādila  
those  three  things  He tasted

(80) In His former pastimes in Vraja there were three things He longed for and could not taste, however He tried. Now, accepting the essence of Śrī Rādhā’s feeling, He tasted those three Himself.[^31]

আপনে করি’ আস্বাদনে, শিখাইল ভক্তগণে,  
প্রেমচিন্তামণির প্রভু ধনী ৷  
নাহি জানে স্থানাস্থান, যারে তারে কৈল দান,  
মহাপ্রভু — দাতা-শিরোমণি ॥ ৮১ ॥

āpane kari’ āsvādane, śikhāila bhakta-gaṇe,  
Himself  making  tasting  He taught  the devotees  
prema-cintāmaṇira prabhu dhanī  
of the touchstone of love  the Lord  is rich  
nāhi jāne sthānāsthāna, yāre tāre kaila dāna,  
not  knows  proper and improper place  to whomever  made  a gift  
mahāprabhu — dātā-śiromaṇi  
Mahāprabhu  of givers  the crest-jewel

(81) Tasting it Himself, He taught His devotees. The Lord is rich with the touchstone of love; without regard for who was worthy and who was not, He gave it to everyone. Mahāprabhu is the crest-jewel of givers.[^32]

এই গুপ্তভাব-সিন্ধু, ব্রহ্মা না পায় এক বিন্দু,  
হেন ধন বিলাইল সংসারে ৷  
ঐছে দয়ালু অবতার, ঐছে দাতা নাহি আর,  
গুণ কেহ নারে বর্ণিবারে ॥ ৮২ ॥

ei gupta-bhāva-sindhu, brahmā nā pāya eka bindu,  
this  of secret moods  ocean  Brahmā  not  gets  one  drop  
hena dhana vilāila saṁsāre  
such  wealth  He distributed  in the world  
aiche dayālu avatāra, aiche dātā nāhi āra,  
such  merciful  avatāra  such  giver  is not  another  
guṇa keha nāre varṇibāre  
qualities  anyone  cannot  describe

(82) Of this ocean of secret moods Brahmā does not obtain a single drop, yet the Lord distributed such wealth throughout the world. There is no other avatāra so merciful, no other such giver; no one can describe His qualities.

কহিবার কথা নয়, কহিলে কেহ না বুঝয়,  
ঐছে চিত্র চৈতন্যের রঙ্গ ৷  
সেই সে বুঝিতে পারে, চৈতন্যের কৃপা যাঁরে,  
হয় তাঁর দাসানুদাস-সঙ্গ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

kahibāra kathā naya, kahile keha nā bujhaya,  
of telling  a matter  not  if told  anyone  not  understands  
aiche citra caitanyera raṅga  
so  wonderful  Caitanya’s  play  
sei se bujhite pāre, caitanyera kṛpā yām̐re,  
he alone  to understand  can  Caitanya’s  mercy  on whom  
haya tām̐ra dāsānudāsa-saṅga  
is  his  of the servants of the servants  the company

(83) This is not a matter to be told; if it is told, no one understands — so wonderful is Caitanya’s play. Only he can understand it on whom Caitanya’s mercy rests and who has the company of the servants of His servants.[^33]

চৈতন্যলীলা-রত্ন-সার, স্বরূপের ভাণ্ডার,  
তেঁহো থুইল রঘুনাথের কণ্ঠে ৷  
তাঁহা কিছু যে শুনিলুঁ, তাহা ইহা বিস্তারিলুঁ,  
ভক্তগণে দিলুঁ এই ভেটে ॥ ৮৪ ॥

caitanya-līlā-ratna-sāra, svarūpera bhāṇḍāra,  
of Caitanya’s pastimes  of jewels  the essence  of Svarūpa  the treasury  
tem̐ho thuila raghunāthera kaṇṭhe  
he  placed  in Raghunātha’s  throat  
tām̐hā kichu ye śunilum̐, tāhā ihā vistārilum̐,  
from him  something  which  I heard  that  here  I expanded  
bhakta-gaṇe dilum̐ ei bheṭe  
to the devotees  I gave  this  as a gift

(84) Svarūpa held in his treasury the jewel-like essence of Caitanya’s pastimes, and he placed it in Raghunātha’s throat. The little I heard from Raghunātha I have expanded here, and I give it to the devotees as a gift.[^34]

যদি কেহ হেন কয়, গ্রন্থ কৈল শ্লোকময়,  
ইতর-জনে নারিবে বুঝিতে ৷  
প্রভুর যেই আচরণ, সেই করি বর্ণন,  
সর্ব্ব-চিত্ত নারি আরাধিতে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

yadi keha hena kaya, grantha kaila śloka-maya,  
if  someone  so  says  the book  made  full of Sanskrit verses  
itara-jane nāribe bujhite  
common people  cannot  understand  
prabhura yei ācaraṇa, sei kari varṇana,  
the Lord’s  which  conduct  that  I make  description  
sarva-citta nāri ārādhite  
of all  the hearts  I cannot  please

(85) If someone says, “The book is full of Sanskrit verses; ordinary people will not be able to understand it,” I reply: I describe the Lord’s conduct as it was; I cannot please everyone’s heart.[^35]

নাহি কাঁহা সবিরোধ, নাহি কাঁহা অনুরোধ,  
সহজ বস্তু করি বিবরণ ৷  
যদি হয় রাগোদ্দেশ, তাঁহা হয়ে আবেশ,  
সহজ বস্তু না যায় লিখন ॥ ৮৬ ॥

nāhi kām̐hā savirodha, nāhi kām̐hā anurodha,  
not  anywhere  contradiction  not  anywhere  deference  
sahaja vastu kari vivaraṇa  
the natural  truth  I make  description  
yadi haya rāgoddeśa, tām̐hā haye āveśa,  
if  there is  partiality’s aim  in that  arises  absorption  
sahaja vastu nā yāya likhana  
the natural  truth  not  can  be written

(86) Nowhere here is there contradiction, and nowhere have I deferred to others’ views: I describe the natural truth (*sahaja vastu*). For if the heart is bent by partiality, it becomes absorbed in that, and the natural truth can no longer be written.[^36]

যেবা নাহি বুঝে কেহ, শুনিতে শুনিতে সেহ,  
কি অদ্ভুত চৈতন্যচরিত ৷  
কৃষ্ণে উপজিবে প্রীতি, জানিবে রসের রীতি,  
শুনিলেই বড় হয় হিত ॥ ৮৭ ॥

yebā nāhi bujhe keha, śunite śunite seha,  
whoever  not  understands  anyone  hearing  hearing  he too  
ki adbhuta caitanya-carita  
how  wonderful  Caitanya’s life  
kṛṣṇe upajibe prīti, jānibe rasera rīti,  
for Kṛṣṇa  will arise  love  he will know  rasa’s  ways  
śunilei baḍa haya hita  
by merely hearing  great  comes  benefit

(87) Even one who does not understand will, by hearing again and again how wonderful is Caitanya’s life, come to love Kṛṣṇa and know the ways of *rasa*. Simply by hearing, great good comes.

ভাগবত — শ্লোকময়, টীকা তার সংস্কৃত হয়,  
তবু কৈছে বুঝে ত্রিভুবন ৷  
ইঁহা শ্লোক দুই-চারি, তার ব্যাখ্যা ভাষা করি,  
কেনে না বুঝিবে সর্ব্বজন ॥ ৮৮ ॥

bhāgavata — śloka-maya, ṭīkā tāra saṁskṛta haya,  
the Bhāgavata  full of verses  commentaries  its  Sanskrit  are  
tabu kaiche bujhe tri-bhuvana  
still  how  understand  the three worlds  
im̐hā śloka dui-cāri, tāra vyākhyā bhāṣā kari,  
here  verses  two or four  their  explanation  in the vernacular  made  
kene nā bujhibe sarva-jana  
why  not  will understand  all people

(88) The *Bhāgavata* is full of Sanskrit verses, and its commentaries are in Sanskrit, yet the three worlds understand it. Here there are only a few verses, explained in the vernacular — why should everyone not understand?

শেষ-লীলার সূত্রগণ, কৈলুঁ কিছু বিবরণ,  
ইঁহা বিস্তারিতে চিত্ত হয় ৷  
থাকে যদি আয়ুঃ-শেষ, বিস্তারিব লীলা-শেষ,  
যদি মহাপ্রভুর কৃপা হয় ॥ ৮৯ ॥

śeṣa-līlāra sūtra-gaṇa, kailum̐ kichu vivaraṇa,  
of the final pastimes  the sūtras  I made  some  description  
im̐hā vistārite citta haya  
this  to expand  the heart  wishes  
thāke yadi āyuḥ-śeṣa, vistāriba līlā-śeṣa,  
remains  if  of life a remainder  I shall expand  of the pastimes  the rest  
yadi mahāprabhura kṛpā haya  
if  Mahāprabhu’s  mercy  comes

(89) I have described something of the *sūtras* of the final pastimes, and my heart wishes to expand them. If some life remains to me and Mahāprabhu’s mercy comes, I shall describe the rest of the pastimes in detail.

আমি বৃদ্ধ জরাতুর, লিখিতে কাঁপয়ে কর,  
মনে কিছু স্মরণ না হয় ৷  
না দেখিয়ে নয়নে, না শুনয়ে শ্রবণে,  
তবু লিখি, — এ বড় বিস্ময় ॥ ৯০ ॥

āmi vṛddha jarātura, likhite kām̐paye kara,  
I  old  infirm  in writing  trembles  the hand  
mane kichu smaraṇa nā haya  
in the mind  anything  memory  not  is  
nā dekhiye nayane, nā śunaye śravaṇe,  
not  I see  with my eyes  not  I hear  with my ears  
tabu likhi, — e baḍa vismaya  
still  I write  this  great  wonder

(90) I am old and infirm; my hand trembles as I write, and my mind remembers nothing. My eyes do not see, my ears do not hear, yet I write — this is a great wonder.

এই অন্ত্যলীলা-সার, সূত্রমধ্যে বিস্তার,  
করি’ কিছু করিলুঁ বর্ণন ৷  
ইহা-মধ্যে মরি যবে, বর্ণিতে না পারি তবে,  
এই লীলা ভক্তগণ-ধন ॥ ৯১ ॥

ei antya-līlā-sāra, sūtra-madhye vistāra,  
this  of the final pastimes  essence  within the sūtras  in detail  
kari’ kichu karilum̐ varṇana  
making  some  I have made  description  
ihā-madhye mari yabe, varṇite nā pāri tabe,  
in the meantime  I die  if  to describe  not  I can  then  
ei līlā bhakta-gaṇa-dhana  
these  pastimes  of the devotees  the wealth

(91) Within the *sūtras* I have described something of this essence of the final pastimes, expanding it a little. If I die before I can describe it, these pastimes will remain the wealth of the devotees.

সংক্ষেপে এই সূত্র কৈল, যেই ইঁহা না লিখিল,  
আগে তাহা করিব বিচার ৷  
যদি তত দিন জিয়ে, মহাপ্রভুর কৃপা হয়ে,  
ইচ্ছা ভরি’ করিব বিস্তার ॥ ৯২ ॥

saṅkṣepe ei sūtra kaila, yei im̐hā nā likhila,  
in brief  these  sūtras  I made  what  here  not  I wrote  
āge tāhā kariba vicāra  
later  that  I shall make  consideration  
yadi tata dina jiye, mahāprabhura kṛpā haye,  
if  so many  days  I live  Mahāprabhu’s  mercy  comes  
icchā bhari’ kariba vistāra  
to my heart’s content  I shall make  expansion

(92) I have made these *sūtras* in brief; what I have not written here I shall consider later. If I live so long and Mahāprabhu’s mercy comes, I shall expand it to my heart’s content.

ছোট বড় ভক্তগণ, বন্দোঁ সবার চরণ,  
সবে মোরে করহ সন্তোষ ৷  
স্বরূপ-গোসাঞির মত, রূপ-রঘুনাথ জানে যত,  
তাই লিখি, নাহি মোর দোষ ॥ ৯৩ ॥

choṭa baḍa bhakta-gaṇa, vandom̐ sabāra caraṇa,  
small  great  devotees  I worship  of all  the feet  
sabe more karaha santoṣa  
all  me  make  pleased  
svarūpa-gosāñira mata, rūpa-raghunātha jāne yata,  
of Svarūpa Gosāñi  the view  Rūpa and Raghunātha  know  as much as  
tāi likhi, nāhi mora doṣa  
that  I write  is not  my  fault

(93) Devotees great and small, I worship the feet of you all; may you all be pleased with me. I write what Svarūpa Gosāñi held and what Rūpa and Raghunātha know; the fault is not mine.[^37]

শ্রীচৈতন্য, নিত্যানন্দ, অদ্বৈতাদি ভক্তবৃন্দ,  
শিরে ধরি সবার চরণ ৷  
স্বরূপ, রূপ, সনাতন, রঘুনাথের শ্রীচরণ,  
ধূলি করোঁ মস্তকে ভূষণ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

śrī caitanya, nityānanda, advaitādi bhakta-vṛnda,  
Śrī Caitanya  Nityānanda  Advaita and other  devotees  
śire dhari sabāra caraṇa  
on my head  I hold  of all  the feet  
svarūpa, rūpa, sanātana, raghunāthera śrī-caraṇa,  
of Svarūpa  Rūpa  Sanātana  Raghunātha  the holy feet’s  
dhūli karom̐ mastake bhūṣaṇa  
dust  I make  for my head  an ornament

(94) I hold on my head the feet of Śrī Caitanya, Nityānanda, Advaita and all the devotees. The dust of the holy feet of Svarūpa, Rūpa, Sanātana and Raghunātha I make the ornament of my head.

পাঞা যাঁর আজ্ঞা-ধন, ব্রজের বৈষ্ণবগণ,  
বন্দোঁ তাঁর মুখ্য হরিদাস ৷  
চৈতন্যবিলাস-সিন্ধু- কল্লোলের এক বিন্দু,  
তার কণা কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৯৫ ॥

pāñā yām̐ra ājñā-dhana, vrajera vaiṣṇava-gaṇa,  
having received  whose  order-wealth  of Vraja  the Vaiṣṇavas  
vandom̐ tām̐ra mukhya haridāsa  
I worship  their  chief  Haridāsa  
caitanya-vilāsa-sindhu- kallolera eka bindu,  
of Caitanya’s pastimes’ ocean  of a wave  one  drop  
tāra kaṇā kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
of it  a particle  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(95) The Vaiṣṇavas of Vraja gave me the treasure of their command, and I bow to their chief, Haridāsa. The ocean of Caitanya’s pastimes has a wave, the wave a drop; of that drop Kṛṣṇadāsa tells a particle.[^38]

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: the final pastimes are the Lord’s life in sannyāsa; Gaura is the Lord who has taken on the feelings of the gopīs; *pralāpa* is the speech of madness born of separation from the Beloved. Heading in the edition: “The Lord’s raving and other states in separation from Kṛṣṇa”.

[^2]: *Anubhāṣya*: bewildered actions — *udghūrṇā*; raving speech — the ten kinds of mad speech, beginning with *citra-jalpa*.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Gambhīrā is the small inner room behind the veranda and the hall.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the sand hills on the seashore are called *caṭaka-parvatas*; there is a large one between the Guṇḍicā temple and the sea, and Mahāprabhu often ran there, taking it for Govardhana.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: the bones in the joints come apart, the skin alone stays in place, and the joint stretches to the length of a span (*vitasti*).

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya*: the pain is that of love broken off — Kṛṣṇa has broken the bond, and the gopīs are tormented by the sickness of separation; *āśravam* — “nothing but suffering”, or “in another’s power”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: another reading is *vidhe* (“O Creator!”).

[^7]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the sprout of love came up when She met Kṛṣṇa; in separation it was wounded, and now a stream of sorrow flows. Kṛṣṇa alone can heal it, yet He makes no effort to protect the sprout.

[^8]: Bengali *guṇa* means both “quality” and “rope”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: it is love itself that binds Rādhā with the rope of the qualities of that cruel cheat, Kṛṣṇa.

[^9]: Heading in the edition: “A song of despondency (*nirveda*) that reveals deep love for Kṛṣṇa”; the verse is called the words of the Gosvāmipāda (*gosvāmi-pādokta-śloka*). *Anubhāṣya*: shameless — because She still clings to this living body that does not taste Kṛṣṇa.

[^10]: For verses 29–34 the *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Bhāgavatam* (2.3.17–24): ears that do not hear of the Lord’s deeds are empty holes; a tongue that does not sing of Him is a frog’s tongue; one who has never smelt the tulasī from His feet is a corpse that breathes. Heading in the edition (29–34): without service to Kṛṣṇa the body and senses are fruitless — the futility of eyes, ears, tongue, nose and skin.

[^11]: The touchstone (*sparśa-maṇi*) turns iron into gold at a touch.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: “The Lord’s mahābhāva — *citra-jalpa*; in outward consciousness He laments before Svarūpa and Rāmānanda”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the two were Svarūpa Dāmodara and Rāya Rāmānanda; seeing them, the Lord came partly to outward consciousness, set aside the feeling that He was Rādhā, and asked, “Am I not that very Caitanya?” The Bengali *caitanya* also means “consciousness”: “Am I not conscious?”

[^13]: Heading in the edition: a Prakrit verse cited in the *Vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī* on *Bhāgavatam* 10.31.1. *Anubhāṣya*: love without deceit — without the pretended religion of dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa and the rest.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: *lāja-bīja khāñā* (lit. “having eaten the seed of shame”) — casting off all shame.

[^15]: Heading in the edition: a verse of Śrī Mahāprabhu Himself (*śrī-śrī-mahāprabhu-pādokta-śloka*). *Anubhāṣya*: I weep — I shed tears of joy, as though I had prema, to display My great good fortune.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: *ālambana* (support) is the meeting of the one served (*viṣaya*) and the servant (*āśraya*); the longing to see Kṛṣṇa’s face shows that this support is absent, and then life is kept up in vain, for the sake of *kāma*, desire for oneself.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: the anurāga of pure love for Kṛṣṇa is like white cloth, and the absence of anurāga is like a drop of ink: against it, it shows at once.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: these are the words of Paurṇamāsī to Nāndīmukhī.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Garuḍa pillar stands before Śrī Jagannātha’s temple, at the far end of the *jagamohana*; behind it, in the floor, was a low channel, and it filled with the Lord’s tears of love.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: the helpless are the gopīs, tormented by separation; Hari is He who steals their bodies, minds and words; the sole ocean of compassion — for no one but Kṛṣṇa has the wealth of sweetness and prema with which to pity them.

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: Your *śaiśava* and My *cāpala* (restlessness) are wondrous in all three worlds, and known to the two of Us alone; *viralam* — “in solitude”, or “seldom seen”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “the sweetness of Your *śaiśava*” (lit. “childhood”).

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*): *sandhi* is the joining of two feelings, alike or different; *śābalya*, their clash with one another; *autsukya* — eagerness, *cāpalya* — restlessness, *dainya* — humility, *roṣa* — anger, *amarṣa* — indignation.

[^23]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *divyonmāda* is a wondrous state of prema in the *mohana* phase of bhāva, resembling delusion.

[^24]: The names of this verse are explained in order in verses 67–71. *Anubhāṣya*: sole Friend of the world — sole guardian of the land of Vraja; Fickle One — He who acts as He pleases; Lover — He who gives joy to the gopīs.

[^25]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *solluṇṭha* — censure in words of praise. *Anubhāṣya*: *praṇaya* is love untouched even by the scent of reverent awe; *māna* is affection at its height which, to hide itself, outwardly takes on a contrary look.

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: the particle *nu* expresses conjecture — “can it be…?”

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: as a guru, keeping his pupils under strict discipline, teaches them the arts, so the feelings in Mahāprabhu’s heart became His guru and made His body and mind dance in many ways.

[^28]: *Anubhāṣya*: Rāya’s drama is the *Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka*; the songs are verses from the works of Caṇḍīdāsa, Vidyāpati, Rāmānanda Rāya, Bilvamaṅgala and Jayadeva.

[^29]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Purī is Śrī Paramānanda Purī; the chief rasa is madhura-rasa. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja Paramānanda Purī is Uddhava, and Rāmānanda is Arjuna (or Viśākhā); through these four moods the Lord accepted their worship, their company and their service, and was bound to them.

[^30]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Līlāśuka is Śrī Bilvamaṅgala Gosvāmī, a brāhmaṇa of the South named Śilhaṇa Miśra; renouncing the world at the words of the courtesan Cintāmaṇi, he later received bhakti by the grace of Kṛṣṇa and the Vaiṣṇavas and wrote the *Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta*; seeing his madness of love, people called him Līlāśuka. The chief rasa is madhura-rasa.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 4.230 — the greatness of Śrī Rādhā’s love, His own sweetness that Her love tastes, and the happiness She feels in it.

[^32]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: like an earthly cintāmaṇi, the cintāmaṇi of prema brings forth other cintāmaṇis and yet remains whole in the Lord’s treasury; and the devotees, from the cintāmaṇi the Lord gave them, spread countless millions of them through the world.

[^33]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: ordinary people have no claim on this truth of feelings that follow Rādhā; told to the unworthy, it is distorted into the perverted moods of the sahajiyās, bāuls and the like.

[^34]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Svarūpa Gosvāmī set down the Lord’s last pastimes in the sūtras of his diary (*kaḍacā*) and placed them in the throat of Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī — had him learn them by heart; so the diary was never written as a separate book, and this *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* is its essence.

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: describing the Lord’s conduct as it was, the author does not seek the praise of the followers of various doctrines, and for fear of their censure he neither omits, adds, hides nor “corrects” anything.

[^36]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: for the jīva the truth of rāga is natural (*sahaja*), the way of reasoning is not; if rāga is turned towards another doctrine or the conclusions of logic, one is absorbed in them and loses impartiality, and then the nature innate in the jīva cannot be described. *Anubhāṣya*: one who gains the direction of rāga will, absorbed in it, easily grasp these descriptions; the natural reality is lived by the followers of rāga and cannot be conveyed in writing. In another reading, *rāga-dveṣa*: one who has left the service of Kṛṣṇa and is held by attachment and aversion to other things cannot describe the love for Kṛṣṇa innate in the pure soul.

[^37]: *Anubhāṣya*: devotees great and small — those who know bhajana, those who practise it, and those initiated into the name of Kṛṣṇa. Fearing that a quarrelsome neophyte who fancies himself a rasika may think he writes of siddhānta out of learning and love of argument, the author says humbly that he has no independence: he writes only what he learnt from Śrī Rūpa, Raghunātha and Svarūpa Dāmodara, to whose feet he is sold.

[^38]: Haridāsa Paṇḍita, the servant of Govindadeva in Vṛndāvana, at whose request the book was written (Ādi 8.54–65, 72; *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 8.49–60). Heading in the edition (94–95): “Obeisance to the Pañca-tattva, the gurus and Haridāsa Paṇḍita”.
