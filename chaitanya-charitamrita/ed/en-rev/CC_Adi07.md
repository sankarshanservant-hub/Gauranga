“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 7  
**Setting Forth the Account of the Pañca-tattva**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

অগত্যেকগতিং নত্বা হীনার্থাধিকসাধকম্ ৷  
শ্রীচৈতন্যং লিখ্যতেঽস্য প্রেমভক্তিবদান্যতা ॥ ১ ॥

agaty-eka-gatiṁ natvā  
of the shelterless  the only shelter  having bowed  
hīnārthādhika-sādhakam  
to the deprived  the highest  who grants  
śrī-caitanyaṁ likhyate ’sya  
to Śrī Caitanya  is written  His  
prema-bhakti-vadānyatā  
in giving prema-bhakti  magnanimity

(1) Bowing to Śrī Caitanya, the only refuge of those who have no other refuge, who gives the highest treasure to those bereft of every good, I write of the magnanimity with which He bestows love and devotion for Kṛṣṇa.[^1]

জয় জয় মহাপ্রভু শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
তাঁহার চরণাশ্রিত সেই বড় ধন্য ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya mahāprabhu śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
glory  glory  to Mahāprabhu  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  
tām̐hāra caraṇāśrita sei baḍa dhanya  
His  at the feet sheltered  he  greatly  blessed

(2) Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever takes shelter at His feet is truly blessed.

পূর্ব্বে গুর্ব্বাদি ছয় তত্ত্বে কৈল নমস্কার ৷  
গুরুতত্ত্ব কহিয়াছি, এবে পাঁচের বিচার ॥ ৩ ॥

pūrve gurvādi chaya tattve kaila namaskāra  
earlier  the guru and the rest  six  to the truths  I made  obeisance  
guru-tattva kahiyāchi, ebe pām̐cera vicāra  
the truth of the guru  I have told  now  of the five  the consideration

(3) At the beginning I offered obeisance to the six truths, headed by the guru. I have spoken of the truth of the guru; now I shall consider the other five.[^2]

পঞ্চতত্ত্ব অবতীর্ণ চৈতন্যের সঙ্গে ৷  
পঞ্চতত্ত্ব লঞা করেন সংকীর্ত্তন-রঙ্গে ॥ ৪ ॥

pañca-tattva avatīrṇa caitanyera saṅge  
the five truths  descended  Caitanya  with  
pañca-tattva lañā karena saṅkīrtana-raṅge  
the five truths  taking  He performs  of saṅkīrtana  in the joy

(4) The five truths descended together with Śrī Caitanya, and with them He delights in saṅkīrtana.

পঞ্চতত্ত্ব — এক-বস্তু, নাহি কিছু ভেদ ৷  
রস আস্বাদিতে তত্ত্ব বিবিধ বিভেদ ॥ ৫ ॥

pañca-tattva — eka-vastu, nāhi kichu bheda  
the five truths  one substance  there is not  any  difference  
rasa āsvādite tattva vividha vibheda  
rasa  to taste  the truth  manifold  distinctions

(5) The five truths are one substance; there is no difference between them at all. Yet to taste rasa, the one truth appears in different forms.[^3]

পঞ্চতত্ত্বাত্মকং কৃষ্ণং ভক্তরূপস্বরূপকম্ ৷  
ভক্তাবতারং ভক্তাখ্যং নমামি ভক্তশক্তিকম্ ॥ ৬ ॥

pañca-tattvātmakaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ  
of five truths  composed  Kṛṣṇa  
bhakta-rūpa-svarūpakam  
devotee in form  devotee in essence  
bhaktāvatāraṁ bhaktākhyaṁ  
the devotee-avatāra  called a devotee  
namāmi bhakta-śaktikam  
I bow to  the devotional potency

(6) “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who is manifest as five truths — the Pañca-tattva: as a devotee in form and a devotee in essence, as the incarnation of a devotee, as the one called a devotee, and as the potency of devotion.” (Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī)[^4]

স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ একলে ঈশ্বর ৷  
অদ্বিতীয়, নন্দাত্মজ, রসিক-শেখর ॥ ৭ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa ekale īśvara  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  the one  Lord  
advitīya, nandātmaja, rasika-śekhara  
without a second  Nanda’s son  of rasikas  the crown

(7) Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself, the one Lord of all. He has no equal; He is the son of Nanda and the crown of all who relish rasa.

রাসাদি-বিলাসী, ব্রজললনা-নাগর ৷  
আর যত সব দেখ, — তাঁর পরিকর ॥ ৮ ॥

rāsādi-vilāsī, vrajalalanā-nāgara  
of the rāsa and other pastimes the enjoyer  of the maidens of Vraja  the lover  
āra yata saba dekha, — tām̐ra parikara  
others  as many  all  you see  His  associates

(8) He enjoys the rāsa dance and His other pastimes and is the beloved of the maidens of Vraja; all the others you see are His associates.

সেই কৃষ্ণ অবতীর্ণ শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
সেই পরিকরগণ সঙ্গে সব ধন্য ॥ ৯ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa avatīrṇa śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
sei parikara-gaṇa saṅge saba dhanya  
those  associates  with Him  all  blessed

(9) That same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and with Him have come all those associates, every one of them blessed.

একলা ঈশ্বর-তত্ত্ব চৈতন্য-ঈশ্বর ৷  
ভক্তভাবময় তাঁর শুদ্ধ কলেবর ॥ ১০ ॥

ekalā īśvara-tattva caitanya-īśvara  
alone  the Lord-truth  Caitanya  the Lord  
bhakta-bhāvamaya tām̐ra śuddha kalevara  
of a devotee’s sentiment full  His  pure  body

(10) Lord Caitanya alone is the Supreme Lord, yet His pure, spiritual body is filled with the sentiment of a devotee.[^5]

কৃষ্ণমাধুর্য্যের এক অদ্ভুত স্বভাব ৷  
আপনা আস্বাদিতে কৃষ্ণ করে ভক্তভাব ॥ ১১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-mādhuryera eka adbhuta svabhāva  
of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  one  wondrous  property  
āpanā āsvādite kṛṣṇa kare bhakta-bhāva  
Himself  to taste  Kṛṣṇa  takes on  a devotee’s sentiment

(11) Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness has one wondrous property: to taste Himself, Kṛṣṇa takes on the sentiment of His own devotee.[^6]

ইথে ভক্তভাব ধরে চৈতন্য গোসাঞি ৷  
‘ভক্তস্বরূপ’ তাঁর নিত্যানন্দ-ভাই ॥ ১২ ॥

ithe bhakta-bhāva dhare caitanya gosāñi  
therefore  a devotee’s sentiment  takes  Caitanya  Gosāñi  
‘bhakta-svarūpa’ tām̐ra nityānanda-bhāi  
‘devotee in essence’  His  Nityānanda  the brother

(12) Therefore Caitanya Gosāñi takes on the sentiment of a devotee, and His brother Nityānanda is the devotee in essence (*bhakta-svarūpa*).

‘ভক্ত-অবতার’ তাঁর আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি ৷  
এই তিন তত্ত্ব সবে প্রভু করি’ গাই ॥ ১৩ ॥

‘bhakta-avatāra’ tām̐ra ācārya-gosāñi  
‘the devotee-avatāra’  His  the Ācārya  Gosāñi  
ei tina tattva sabe prabhu kari’ gāi  
these  three  truths  all  as Lord  holding  we sing

(13) Advaita Ācārya is His incarnation as a devotee (*bhakta-avatāra*). These three truths we all glorify as the Lord — as Prabhu.

এক মহাপ্রভু, আর প্রভু দুইজন ৷  
দুই প্রভু সেবে মহাপ্রভুর চরণ ॥ ১৪ ॥

eka mahāprabhu, āra prabhu duijana  
one  Mahāprabhu  and  Prabhus  two  
dui prabhu seve mahāprabhura caraṇa  
the two  Prabhus  serve  Mahāprabhu’s  feet

(14) One is Mahāprabhu, and the other two are Prabhus; the two Prabhus serve the feet of Mahāprabhu.

এই তিন তত্ত্ব, — ‘সর্ব্বারাধ্য’ করি’ মানি ৷  
চতুর্থ যে ভক্ততত্ত্ব — ‘আরাধক’ করি’ জানি ॥ ১৫ ॥

ei tina tattva, — ‘sarvārādhya’ kari’ māni  
these  three  truths  ‘worshipable by all’  as  we accept  
caturtha ye bhakta-tattva — ‘ārādhaka’ kari’ jāni  
the fourth  which  the devotee-truth  ‘worshipper’  as  we know

(15) These three truths we accept as worshipable by all; the fourth, the truth of the devotee, we know as the worshipper.[^7]

শ্রীবাসাদি যত কোটি কোটি ভক্তগণ ৷  
‘শুদ্ধভক্ত’-তত্ত্বমধ্যে তাঁ-সবার গণন ॥ ১৬ ॥

śrīvāsādi yata koṭi koṭi bhakta-gaṇa  
Śrīvāsa and others  all  millions  millions  devotees  
‘śuddha-bhakta’-tattva-madhye tām̐-sabāra gaṇana  
‘pure devotee’-truth  within  of them all  the counting

(16) The countless millions of devotees headed by Śrīvāsa are all counted among the pure devotees (*śuddha-bhakta*).

গদাধর-পণ্ডিতাদি প্রভুর ‘শক্তি’-অবতার ৷  
‘অন্তরঙ্গ-ভক্ত’ করি’ গণন যাঁহার ॥ ১৭ ॥

gadādhara-paṇḍitādi prabhura ‘śakti’-avatāra  
Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others  the Lord’s  ‘potency’-avatāras  
‘antaraṅga-bhakta’ kari’ gaṇana yām̐hāra  
‘intimate devotees’  as  counting  of whom

(17) Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others are incarnations of the Lord’s potency (*śakti*); they are counted as His intimate devotees.[^8]

যাঁ-সবা লঞা প্রভুর নিত্য বিহার ৷  
যাঁ-সবা লঞা প্রভুর কীর্ত্তন প্রচার ॥ ১৮ ॥

যাঁ-সবা লঞা করেন প্রেম আস্বাদন ৷  
যাঁ-সবা লঞা দান করে প্রেমধন ॥ ১৯ ॥

yām̐-sabā lañā prabhura nitya vihāra  
with whom all  taking  the Lord’s  eternal  pastimes  
yām̐-sabā lañā prabhura kīrtana pracāra  
with whom all  taking  the Lord’s  of kīrtana  spreading  
yām̐-sabā lañā karena prema āsvādana  
with whom all  taking  He does  of prema  tasting  
yām̐-sabā lañā dāna kare prema-dhana  
with whom all  taking  giving  He makes  prema’s treasure

(18–19) With them the Lord enjoys His eternal pastimes, with them He spreads the kīrtana, with them He tastes prema, and with them He gives away the treasure of prema.[^9]

সেই পঞ্চতত্ত্ব মিলি’ পৃথিবী আসিয়া ৷  
পূর্ব্ব-প্রেমভাণ্ডারের মুদ্রা উঘাড়িয়া ॥ ২০ ॥

পাঁচে মিলি’ লুটে প্রেম, করে আস্বাদন ৷  
যত যত পিয়ে, তৃষ্ণা বাড়ে অনুক্ষণ ॥ ২১ ॥

sei pañca-tattva mili’ pṛthivī āsiyā  
those  five truths  together  to the earth  coming  
pūrva-premabhāṇḍārera mudrā ughāḍiyā  
of the ancient storehouse of prema  the seal  breaking open  
pām̐ce mili’ luṭe prema, kare āsvādana  
the five  together  plunder  prema  do  tasting  
yata yata piye, tṛṣṇā bāḍe anukṣaṇa  
as much  as much  they drink  thirst  grows  every moment

(20–21) Coming to earth together, the five truths broke the seal of the ancient storehouse of prema. Together they plunder that love and taste it, and the more they drink, the more their thirst grows at every moment.[^10]

পুনঃ পুনঃ পিয়াইয়া হয় মহামত্ত ৷  
নাচে, কান্দে, হাসে, গায়, যৈছে মদমত্ত ॥ ২২ ॥

punaḥ punaḥ piyāiyā haya mahāmatta  
again  again  giving to drink  become  greatly maddened  
nāce, kānde, hāse, gāya, yaiche mada-matta  
they dance  weep  laugh  sing  like  the intoxicated

(22) Again and again they give it to others to drink and grow wild with love; they dance, weep, laugh and sing as though intoxicated.

পাত্রাপাত্র-বিচার নাহি, নাহি স্থানাস্থান ৷  
যেই যাঁহা পায়, তাঁহা করে প্রেমদান ॥ ২৩ ॥

pātrāpātra-vicāra nāhi, nāhi sthānāsthāna  
fit and unfit  consideration  there is not  there is not  proper place and improper  
yei yām̐hā pāya, tām̐hā kare prema-dāna  
whoever  wherever  is found  there  they make  of prema  the gift

(23) They make no distinction between the fit and the unfit, nor between a proper place and an improper one: wherever they find anyone, there they give prema.

লুটিয়া, খাইয়া, দিয়া, ভাণ্ডার উজাড়ে ৷  
আশ্চর্য্য ভাণ্ডার, প্রেম শতগুণ বাড়ে ॥ ২৪ ॥

luṭiyā, khāiyā, diyā, bhāṇḍāra ujāḍe  
plundering  eating  giving  the storehouse  they empty  
āścarya bhāṇḍāra, prema śata-guṇa bāḍe  
wondrous  storehouse  prema  a hundredfold  grows

(24) Plundering, tasting and giving, they empty the storehouse — yet this storehouse is wondrous: the prema in it grows a hundredfold.

উছলিল প্রেমবন্যা চৌদিকে বেড়ায় ৷  
স্ত্রী, বৃদ্ধ, বালক, যুবা, সবারে ডুবায় ॥ ২৫ ॥

uchalila prema-vanyā caudike veḍāya  
surged  of prema the flood  on all sides  spreads  
strī, vṛddha, bālaka, yuvā, sabāre ḍubāya  
women  old men  boys  youths  all  it drowns

(25) The flood of prema surged and spread on every side, drowning everyone — women and old men, boys and youths.

সজ্জন, দুর্জ্জন, পঙ্গু, জড়, অন্ধগণ ৷  
প্রেমবন্যায় ডুবাইল জগতের জন ॥ ২৬ ॥

saj-jana, durjana, paṅgu, jaḍa, andha-gaṇa  
the good  the wicked  the lame  the dull  the blind  
prema-vanyāya ḍubāila jagatera jana  
in the flood of prema  drowned  of the world  the people

(26) The good and the wicked, the lame, the dull and the blind — the flood of prema drowned all the people of the world.

জগৎ ডুবিল, জীবের হইল বীজ নাশ ৷  
তাহা দেখি’ পাঁচ জনের পরম উল্লাস ॥ ২৭ ॥

jagat ḍubila, jīvera haila bīja nāśa  
the world  drowned  of the jīvas  became  the seed  destroyed  
tāhā dekhi’ pām̐ca janera parama ullāsa  
that  seeing  of the five  persons  supreme  joy

(27) The world was drowned, and the seed of the jīvas’ bondage was destroyed. Seeing this, the five felt the highest joy.[^11]

যত যত প্রেমবৃষ্টি করে পঞ্চজন ৷  
তত তত বাড়ে জল, ব্যাপে ত্রিভুবন ॥ ২৮ ॥

yata yata prema-vṛṣṭi kare pañca-jana  
as much  as much  rain of prema  make  the five persons  
tata tata bāḍe jala, vyāpe tri-bhuvana  
so much  so much  rises  the water  pervades  the three worlds

(28) The more the five pour down the rain of prema, the higher the waters rise, until they fill the three worlds.

মায়াবাদী, কর্ম্মনিষ্ঠ, কুতার্কিকগণ ৷  
নিন্দক, পাষণ্ডী, যত পড়ুয়া অধম ॥ ২৯ ॥

সেই সব মহাদক্ষ ধাঞা পলাইল ৷  
সেই বন্যা তা-সবারে ছুঁইতে নারিল ॥ ৩০ ॥

māyāvādī, karma-niṣṭha, kutārkika-gaṇa  
Māyāvādīs  those devoted to karma  the false logicians  
nindaka, pāṣaṇḍī, yata paḍuyā adhama  
blasphemers  pāṣaṇḍīs  all  students  the lowest  
sei saba mahādakṣa dhāñā palāila  
they  all  very clever  running  fled  
sei vanyā tā-sabāre chum̐ite nārila  
that  flood  them all  to touch  could not

(29–30) But the Māyāvādīs, those devoted to fruitive work, the false logicians, the blasphemers, the pāṣaṇḍīs and the lowest of students — all these, being very clever, ran away, and the flood could not touch them.[^12]

তাহা দেখি’ মহাপ্রভু করেন চিন্তন ৷  
জগৎ ডুবাইতে আমি করিলুঁ যতন ॥ ৩১ ॥

কেহ কেহ এড়াইল, প্রতিজ্ঞা হইল ভঙ্গ ৷  
তা-সবা ডুবাইতে পাতিব কিছু রঙ্গ ॥ ৩২ ॥

tāhā dekhi’ mahāprabhu karena cintana  
that  seeing  Mahāprabhu  makes  reflection  
jagat ḍubāite āmi karilum̐ yatana  
the world  to drown  I  made  effort  
keha keha eḍāila, pratijñā haila bhaṅga  
some  some  escaped  the vow  became  broken  
tā-sabā ḍubāite pātiba kichu raṅga  
them all  to drown  I shall set  some  play

(31–32) Seeing this, Mahāprabhu reflected: “I tried to drown the whole world, but some have escaped, and My vow has been broken. To drown them too, I shall set some play in motion.”

---

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: *agaty-eka-gati* — “the one refuge of those who have no other shelter”; *hīnārthādhika-sādhaka* — “He who grants to those deprived of the supreme good (*paramārtha*) something greater still — the fifth goal of life, love for Kṛṣṇa”.

[^2]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in the first chapter the truth of the guru was described — as the initiating and the instructing guru; the six truths are those of the verse *vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān* (Ādi 1.1), and now the remaining five are examined.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: in substance the five truths are one, without duality, but for the sake of the pastimes and the tasting of rasa the one truth is fivefold. The devotee in form, the devotee in essence and the incarnation of a devotee are the Lord Himself, His *prakāśa* and His *aṁśa* (*viṣṇu-tattva*); the potency of devotion and the pure devotee are potencies non-different from Him, who serve Him. Without either the worshipped or the worshipper there would be no pastime of tasting rasa.

[^4]: According to the *Anubhāṣya*: the devotee in form is Śrī Gaurāṅga, who tastes His own sweetness; the devotee in essence (in the form of His brother) is Śrī Nityānanda; the devotee-avatāra is Śrī Advaita; the one called a devotee is Śrīvāsa and others, established in *śānta*, *dāsya* and the other rasas; the potency is Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Rāmānanda and others. The same verse as Ādi 1.14; ascribed in the edition to Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī.

[^5]: The edition’s heading: “Though He is the Lord of all, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is full of the mood of one who is subordinate.” *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Caitanya is the one Supreme Lord of all conscious beings (*nityo nityānām…*); though non-different from the son of the king of Vraja, He takes a form full of the mood of serving Kṛṣṇa. That form must not be thought of as a body for worldly enjoyment or counted among the jīvas of this world: it is manifest in pure *sattva* (*viśuddha-sattva*) and is revealed to a heart illumined by it.

[^6]: The edition’s heading: “To taste His own sweetness, Kṛṣṇa descends as Gaura — ‘in the form of a devotee’.” *Anubhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇa, the abode of all sweetness, though He is Himself the object of love (*viṣaya*), takes on the sentiment of its subject (*āśraya*) and tastes the joy of serving Himself; yet Śrī Caitanya is not merely a form of the *āśraya*: He is the original Lord Himself (*svayaṁ-rūpa*).

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Mahāprabhu is the highest Truth, above all, while Śrī Nityānanda and Śrī Advaita are *īśvara-tattvas* subordinate to Him; all three are worshipable by everyone else. The fourth truth is the pure devotees, the fifth the intimate (*antaraṅga*) devotees; both are worshippers. The two worshipable truths, though the last two worship them, are themselves engaged in the service of Śrī Gaurāṅga.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: the truths of *śakti* — the intimate devotees — abide in *madhura-rasa*, which includes *vātsalya*, *sakhya* and *dāsya*; the servants of Śrī Nityānanda and Śrī Advaita, the pure devotees, usually abide in *vātsalya*, *sakhya*, *dāsya* and *śānta*. When their love for Śrī Gaurasundara grows intense, they take shelter of *madhura-rasa* under the guidance of the intimate devotees. Pure devotees are those who, free from other desires, do not cover their service to Kṛṣṇa with *karma* or *jñāna*; the intimate devotees are those wholly given to *madhura-rasa* alone.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: “with them” — with all four: with His *prakāśa* (Śrī Nityānanda), with the avatāra of the Puruṣa (Śrī Advaita), with the intimate devotees and with the pure devotees. The edition’s heading: “With the four truths — the Lord’s pastimes, preaching, tasting and giving.”

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the storehouse of prema is the pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. It had come into the world before, but it was locked and sealed; in the descent of Śrī Caitanya the Pañca-tattva broke the seal, threw open the doors and tasted prema, plundering it.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: in the Lord’s *jīva-śakti*, together with the inclination towards Kṛṣṇa, there lies hidden the seed of the desire to enjoy in turning away from Him. As a seed in flooded earth can put forth no shoot, so in the flood of prema the seed of every desire other than service to Kṛṣṇa perished. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: there perished the seed of the bondage of *avidyā* — the soul’s forgetfulness that it is Kṛṣṇa’s servant.

[^12]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Māyāvādīs are the sannyāsīs Prakāśānanda and others; those devoted to karma — Devānanda and other karmīs without bhakti, the *smārtas*, for whom karma and its fruits are the chief goal; the false logicians — Sārvabhauma and other godless logicians; the blasphemers — the one whom the Lord drove away with a stick, Gopāla Cāpāla and others who reviled the Lord and His devotees; the pāṣaṇḍīs — those who place other gods on a level with the Lord; the lowest of students — those for whom learning is only a cause for argument and not a way to God.
