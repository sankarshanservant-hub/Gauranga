“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 7  
**Setting Forth the Account of the Pañca-tattva**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

অগত্যেকগতিং নত্বা হীনার্থাধিকসাধকম্ ৷  
শ্রীচৈতন্যং লিখ্যতেঽস্য প্রেমভক্তিবদান্যতা ॥ ১ ॥

agaty-eka-gatiṁ natvā  
of the shelterless  the only shelter  having bowed  
hīnārthādhika-sādhakam  
to the deprived  the highest  who grants  
śrī-caitanyaṁ likhyate ’sya  
to Śrī Caitanya  is written  His  
prema-bhakti-vadānyatā  
in giving prema-bhakti  magnanimity

(1) Bowing to Śrī Caitanya, the only refuge of those who have no other refuge, who gives the highest treasure to those bereft of every good, I write of the magnanimity with which He bestows love and devotion for Kṛṣṇa.[^1]

জয় জয় মহাপ্রভু শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
তাঁহার চরণাশ্রিত সেই বড় ধন্য ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya mahāprabhu śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
glory  glory  to Mahāprabhu  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  
tām̐hāra caraṇāśrita sei baḍa dhanya  
His  at the feet sheltered  he  greatly  blessed

(2) Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever takes shelter at His feet is truly blessed.

পূর্ব্বে গুর্ব্বাদি ছয় তত্ত্বে কৈল নমস্কার ৷  
গুরুতত্ত্ব কহিয়াছি, এবে পাঁচের বিচার ॥ ৩ ॥

pūrve gurvādi chaya tattve kaila namaskāra  
earlier  the guru and the rest  six  to the truths  I made  obeisance  
guru-tattva kahiyāchi, ebe pām̐cera vicāra  
the truth of the guru  I have told  now  of the five  the consideration

(3) At the beginning I offered obeisance to the six truths, headed by the guru. I have spoken of the truth of the guru; now I shall consider the other five.[^2]

পঞ্চতত্ত্ব অবতীর্ণ চৈতন্যের সঙ্গে ৷  
পঞ্চতত্ত্ব লঞা করেন সংকীর্ত্তন-রঙ্গে ॥ ৪ ॥

pañca-tattva avatīrṇa caitanyera saṅge  
the five truths  descended  Caitanya  with  
pañca-tattva lañā karena saṅkīrtana-raṅge  
the five truths  taking  He performs  of saṅkīrtana  in the joy

(4) The five truths descended together with Śrī Caitanya, and with them He delights in saṅkīrtana.

পঞ্চতত্ত্ব — এক-বস্তু, নাহি কিছু ভেদ ৷  
রস আস্বাদিতে তত্ত্ব বিবিধ বিভেদ ॥ ৫ ॥

pañca-tattva — eka-vastu, nāhi kichu bheda  
the five truths  one substance  there is not  any  difference  
rasa āsvādite tattva vividha vibheda  
rasa  to taste  the truth  manifold  distinctions

(5) The five truths are one substance; there is no difference between them at all. Yet to taste rasa, the one truth appears in different forms.[^3]

পঞ্চতত্ত্বাত্মকং কৃষ্ণং ভক্তরূপস্বরূপকম্ ৷  
ভক্তাবতারং ভক্তাখ্যং নমামি ভক্তশক্তিকম্ ॥ ৬ ॥

pañca-tattvātmakaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ  
of five truths  composed  Kṛṣṇa  
bhakta-rūpa-svarūpakam  
devotee in form  devotee in essence  
bhaktāvatāraṁ bhaktākhyaṁ  
the devotee-avatāra  called a devotee  
namāmi bhakta-śaktikam  
I bow to  the devotional potency

(6) “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who is manifest as five truths — the Pañca-tattva: as a devotee in form and a devotee in essence, as the incarnation of a devotee, as the one called a devotee, and as the potency of devotion.” (Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī)[^4]

স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ একলে ঈশ্বর ৷  
অদ্বিতীয়, নন্দাত্মজ, রসিক-শেখর ॥ ৭ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa ekale īśvara  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  the one  Lord  
advitīya, nandātmaja, rasika-śekhara  
without a second  Nanda’s son  of rasikas  the crown

(7) Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself, the one Lord of all. He has no equal; He is the son of Nanda and the crown of all who relish rasa.

রাসাদি-বিলাসী, ব্রজললনা-নাগর ৷  
আর যত সব দেখ, — তাঁর পরিকর ॥ ৮ ॥

rāsādi-vilāsī, vrajalalanā-nāgara  
of the rāsa and other pastimes the enjoyer  of the maidens of Vraja  the lover  
āra yata saba dekha, — tām̐ra parikara  
others  as many  all  you see  His  associates

(8) He enjoys the rāsa dance and His other pastimes and is the beloved of the maidens of Vraja; all the others you see are His associates.

সেই কৃষ্ণ অবতীর্ণ শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
সেই পরিকরগণ সঙ্গে সব ধন্য ॥ ৯ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa avatīrṇa śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
sei parikara-gaṇa saṅge saba dhanya  
those  associates  with Him  all  blessed

(9) That same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and with Him have come all those associates, every one of them blessed.

একলা ঈশ্বর-তত্ত্ব চৈতন্য-ঈশ্বর ৷  
ভক্তভাবময় তাঁর শুদ্ধ কলেবর ॥ ১০ ॥

ekalā īśvara-tattva caitanya-īśvara  
alone  the Lord-truth  Caitanya  the Lord  
bhakta-bhāvamaya tām̐ra śuddha kalevara  
of a devotee’s sentiment full  His  pure  body

(10) Lord Caitanya alone is the Supreme Lord, yet His pure, spiritual body is filled with the sentiment of a devotee.[^5]

কৃষ্ণমাধুর্য্যের এক অদ্ভুত স্বভাব ৷  
আপনা আস্বাদিতে কৃষ্ণ করে ভক্তভাব ॥ ১১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-mādhuryera eka adbhuta svabhāva  
of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  one  wondrous  property  
āpanā āsvādite kṛṣṇa kare bhakta-bhāva  
Himself  to taste  Kṛṣṇa  takes on  a devotee’s sentiment

(11) Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness has one wondrous property: to taste Himself, Kṛṣṇa takes on the sentiment of His own devotee.[^6]

ইথে ভক্তভাব ধরে চৈতন্য গোসাঞি ৷  
‘ভক্তস্বরূপ’ তাঁর নিত্যানন্দ-ভাই ॥ ১২ ॥

ithe bhakta-bhāva dhare caitanya gosāñi  
therefore  a devotee’s sentiment  takes  Caitanya  Gosāñi  
‘bhakta-svarūpa’ tām̐ra nityānanda-bhāi  
‘devotee in essence’  His  Nityānanda  the brother

(12) Therefore Caitanya Gosāñi takes on the sentiment of a devotee, and His brother Nityānanda is the devotee in essence (*bhakta-svarūpa*).

‘ভক্ত-অবতার’ তাঁর আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি ৷  
এই তিন তত্ত্ব সবে প্রভু করি’ গাই ॥ ১৩ ॥

‘bhakta-avatāra’ tām̐ra ācārya-gosāñi  
‘the devotee-avatāra’  His  the Ācārya  Gosāñi  
ei tina tattva sabe prabhu kari’ gāi  
these  three  truths  all  as Lord  holding  we sing

(13) Advaita Ācārya is His incarnation as a devotee (*bhakta-avatāra*). These three truths we all glorify as the Lord — as Prabhu.

এক মহাপ্রভু, আর প্রভু দুইজন ৷  
দুই প্রভু সেবে মহাপ্রভুর চরণ ॥ ১৪ ॥

eka mahāprabhu, āra prabhu duijana  
one  Mahāprabhu  and  Prabhus  two  
dui prabhu seve mahāprabhura caraṇa  
the two  Prabhus  serve  Mahāprabhu’s  feet

(14) One is Mahāprabhu, and the other two are Prabhus; the two Prabhus serve the feet of Mahāprabhu.

এই তিন তত্ত্ব, — ‘সর্ব্বারাধ্য’ করি’ মানি ৷  
চতুর্থ যে ভক্ততত্ত্ব — ‘আরাধক’ করি’ জানি ॥ ১৫ ॥

ei tina tattva, — ‘sarvārādhya’ kari’ māni  
these  three  truths  ‘worshipable by all’  as  we accept  
caturtha ye bhakta-tattva — ‘ārādhaka’ kari’ jāni  
the fourth  which  the devotee-truth  ‘worshipper’  as  we know

(15) These three truths we accept as worshipable by all; the fourth, the truth of the devotee, we know as the worshipper.[^7]

শ্রীবাসাদি যত কোটি কোটি ভক্তগণ ৷  
‘শুদ্ধভক্ত’-তত্ত্বমধ্যে তাঁ-সবার গণন ॥ ১৬ ॥

śrīvāsādi yata koṭi koṭi bhakta-gaṇa  
Śrīvāsa and others  all  millions  millions  devotees  
‘śuddha-bhakta’-tattva-madhye tām̐-sabāra gaṇana  
‘pure devotee’-truth  within  of them all  the counting

(16) The countless millions of devotees headed by Śrīvāsa are all counted among the pure devotees (*śuddha-bhakta*).

গদাধর-পণ্ডিতাদি প্রভুর ‘শক্তি’-অবতার ৷  
‘অন্তরঙ্গ-ভক্ত’ করি’ গণন যাঁহার ॥ ১৭ ॥

gadādhara-paṇḍitādi prabhura ‘śakti’-avatāra  
Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others  the Lord’s  ‘potency’-avatāras  
‘antaraṅga-bhakta’ kari’ gaṇana yām̐hāra  
‘intimate devotees’  as  counting  of whom

(17) Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others are incarnations of the Lord’s potency (*śakti*); they are counted as His intimate devotees.[^8]

যাঁ-সবা লঞা প্রভুর নিত্য বিহার ৷  
যাঁ-সবা লঞা প্রভুর কীর্ত্তন প্রচার ॥ ১৮ ॥

যাঁ-সবা লঞা করেন প্রেম আস্বাদন ৷  
যাঁ-সবা লঞা দান করে প্রেমধন ॥ ১৯ ॥

yām̐-sabā lañā prabhura nitya vihāra  
with whom all  taking  the Lord’s  eternal  pastimes  
yām̐-sabā lañā prabhura kīrtana pracāra  
with whom all  taking  the Lord’s  of kīrtana  spreading  
yām̐-sabā lañā karena prema āsvādana  
with whom all  taking  He does  of prema  tasting  
yām̐-sabā lañā dāna kare prema-dhana  
with whom all  taking  giving  He makes  prema’s treasure

(18–19) With them the Lord enjoys His eternal pastimes, with them He spreads the kīrtana, with them He tastes prema, and with them He gives away the treasure of prema.[^9]

সেই পঞ্চতত্ত্ব মিলি’ পৃথিবী আসিয়া ৷  
পূর্ব্ব-প্রেমভাণ্ডারের মুদ্রা উঘাড়িয়া ॥ ২০ ॥

পাঁচে মিলি’ লুটে প্রেম, করে আস্বাদন ৷  
যত যত পিয়ে, তৃষ্ণা বাড়ে অনুক্ষণ ॥ ২১ ॥

sei pañca-tattva mili’ pṛthivī āsiyā  
those  five truths  together  to the earth  coming  
pūrva-premabhāṇḍārera mudrā ughāḍiyā  
of the ancient storehouse of prema  the seal  breaking open  
pām̐ce mili’ luṭe prema, kare āsvādana  
the five  together  plunder  prema  do  tasting  
yata yata piye, tṛṣṇā bāḍe anukṣaṇa  
as much  as much  they drink  thirst  grows  every moment

(20–21) Coming to earth together, the five truths broke the seal of the ancient storehouse of prema. Together they plunder that love and taste it, and the more they drink, the more their thirst grows at every moment.[^10]

পুনঃ পুনঃ পিয়াইয়া হয় মহামত্ত ৷  
নাচে, কান্দে, হাসে, গায়, যৈছে মদমত্ত ॥ ২২ ॥

punaḥ punaḥ piyāiyā haya mahāmatta  
again  again  giving to drink  become  greatly maddened  
nāce, kānde, hāse, gāya, yaiche mada-matta  
they dance  weep  laugh  sing  like  the intoxicated

(22) Again and again they give it to others to drink and grow wild with love; they dance, weep, laugh and sing as though intoxicated.

পাত্রাপাত্র-বিচার নাহি, নাহি স্থানাস্থান ৷  
যেই যাঁহা পায়, তাঁহা করে প্রেমদান ॥ ২৩ ॥

pātrāpātra-vicāra nāhi, nāhi sthānāsthāna  
fit and unfit  consideration  there is not  there is not  proper place and improper  
yei yām̐hā pāya, tām̐hā kare prema-dāna  
whoever  wherever  is found  there  they make  of prema  the gift

(23) They make no distinction between the fit and the unfit, nor between a proper place and an improper one: wherever they find anyone, there they give prema.

লুটিয়া, খাইয়া, দিয়া, ভাণ্ডার উজাড়ে ৷  
আশ্চর্য্য ভাণ্ডার, প্রেম শতগুণ বাড়ে ॥ ২৪ ॥

luṭiyā, khāiyā, diyā, bhāṇḍāra ujāḍe  
plundering  eating  giving  the storehouse  they empty  
āścarya bhāṇḍāra, prema śata-guṇa bāḍe  
wondrous  storehouse  prema  a hundredfold  grows

(24) Plundering, tasting and giving, they empty the storehouse — yet this storehouse is wondrous: the prema in it grows a hundredfold.

উছলিল প্রেমবন্যা চৌদিকে বেড়ায় ৷  
স্ত্রী, বৃদ্ধ, বালক, যুবা, সবারে ডুবায় ॥ ২৫ ॥

uchalila prema-vanyā caudike veḍāya  
surged  of prema the flood  on all sides  spreads  
strī, vṛddha, bālaka, yuvā, sabāre ḍubāya  
women  old men  boys  youths  all  it drowns

(25) The flood of prema surged and spread on every side, drowning everyone — women and old men, boys and youths.

সজ্জন, দুর্জ্জন, পঙ্গু, জড়, অন্ধগণ ৷  
প্রেমবন্যায় ডুবাইল জগতের জন ॥ ২৬ ॥

saj-jana, durjana, paṅgu, jaḍa, andha-gaṇa  
the good  the wicked  the lame  the dull  the blind  
prema-vanyāya ḍubāila jagatera jana  
in the flood of prema  drowned  of the world  the people

(26) The good and the wicked, the lame, the dull and the blind — the flood of prema drowned all the people of the world.

জগৎ ডুবিল, জীবের হইল বীজ নাশ ৷  
তাহা দেখি’ পাঁচ জনের পরম উল্লাস ॥ ২৭ ॥

jagat ḍubila, jīvera haila bīja nāśa  
the world  drowned  of the jīvas  became  the seed  destroyed  
tāhā dekhi’ pām̐ca janera parama ullāsa  
that  seeing  of the five  persons  supreme  joy

(27) The world was drowned, and the seed of the jīvas’ bondage was destroyed. Seeing this, the five felt the highest joy.[^11]

যত যত প্রেমবৃষ্টি করে পঞ্চজন ৷  
তত তত বাড়ে জল, ব্যাপে ত্রিভুবন ॥ ২৮ ॥

yata yata prema-vṛṣṭi kare pañca-jana  
as much  as much  rain of prema  make  the five persons  
tata tata bāḍe jala, vyāpe tri-bhuvana  
so much  so much  rises  the water  pervades  the three worlds

(28) The more the five pour down the rain of prema, the higher the waters rise, until they fill the three worlds.

মায়াবাদী, কর্ম্মনিষ্ঠ, কুতার্কিকগণ ৷  
নিন্দক, পাষণ্ডী, যত পড়ুয়া অধম ॥ ২৯ ॥

সেই সব মহাদক্ষ ধাঞা পলাইল ৷  
সেই বন্যা তা-সবারে ছুঁইতে নারিল ॥ ৩০ ॥

māyāvādī, karma-niṣṭha, kutārkika-gaṇa  
Māyāvādīs  those devoted to karma  the false logicians  
nindaka, pāṣaṇḍī, yata paḍuyā adhama  
blasphemers  pāṣaṇḍīs  all  students  the lowest  
sei saba mahādakṣa dhāñā palāila  
they  all  very clever  running  fled  
sei vanyā tā-sabāre chum̐ite nārila  
that  flood  them all  to touch  could not

(29–30) But the Māyāvādīs, those devoted to fruitive work, the false logicians, the blasphemers, the pāṣaṇḍīs and the lowest of students — all these, being very clever, ran away, and the flood could not touch them.[^12]

তাহা দেখি’ মহাপ্রভু করেন চিন্তন ৷  
জগৎ ডুবাইতে আমি করিলুঁ যতন ॥ ৩১ ॥

কেহ কেহ এড়াইল, প্রতিজ্ঞা হইল ভঙ্গ ৷  
তা-সবা ডুবাইতে পাতিব কিছু রঙ্গ ॥ ৩২ ॥

tāhā dekhi’ mahāprabhu karena cintana  
that  seeing  Mahāprabhu  makes  reflection  
jagat ḍubāite āmi karilum̐ yatana  
the world  to drown  I  made  effort  
keha keha eḍāila, pratijñā haila bhaṅga  
some  some  escaped  the vow  became  broken  
tā-sabā ḍubāite pātiba kichu raṅga  
them all  to drown  I shall set  some  play

(31–32) Seeing this, Mahāprabhu reflected: “I tried to drown the whole world, but some have escaped, and My vow has been broken. To drown them too, I shall set some play in motion.”

এত বলি’ মনে কিছু করিয়া বিচার ৷  
সন্ন্যাস-আশ্রম প্রভু কৈলা অঙ্গীকার ॥ ৩৩ ॥

eta bali’ mane kichu kariyā vicāra  
thus  saying  in the mind  some  making  deliberation  
sannyāsa-āśrama prabhu kailā aṅgīkāra  
the sannyāsa order  the Lord  made  acceptance

(33) Saying this and deliberating within Himself, the Lord accepted the order of sannyāsa.

চব্বিশ বৎসর ছিলা গৃহস্থ-আশ্রমে ৷  
পঞ্চবিংশতি বর্ষে কৈল যতিধর্ম্মে ॥ ৩৪ ॥

cabbiśa vatsara chilā gṛhastha-āśrame  
twenty-four  years  He remained  in the householder order  
pañca-viṁśati varṣe kaila yati-dharme  
in the twenty-fifth  year  He took  to the renunciant’s dharma

(34) For twenty-four years He lived as a householder; in His twenty-fifth year He took up the dharma of a renunciant.[^13]

সন্ন্যাস করিয়া প্রভু কৈলা আকর্ষণ ৷  
যতেক পালাঞাছিল তার্কিকাদি গণ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

sannyāsa kariyā prabhu kailā ākarṣaṇa  
sannyāsa  having taken  the Lord  made  attraction  
yateka pālāñāchila tārkikādi gaṇa  
all who  had fled  the logicians and the rest

(35) Having taken sannyāsa, the Lord drew to Himself all who had fled — the logicians and the rest.

পড়ুয়া, পাষণ্ডী, কর্ম্মী, নিন্দকাদি যত ৷  
তারা আসি’ প্রভু-পায় হয় অবনত ॥ ৩৬ ॥

paḍuyā, pāṣaṇḍī, karmī, nindakādi yata  
students  pāṣaṇḍīs  karmīs  blasphemers and others  all  
tārā āsi’ prabhu-pāya haya avanata  
they  coming  at the Lord’s feet  become  bowed down

(36) Students, pāṣaṇḍīs, karmīs, blasphemers and all the others came and bowed down at the Lord’s feet.

অপরাধ ক্ষমাইল, ডুবিল প্রেমজলে ৷  
কেবা এড়াইবে প্রভুর প্রেম-মহাজালে ॥ ৩৭ ॥

aparādha kṣamāila, ḍubila prema-jale  
offences  He forgave  they sank  in the waters of prema  
kebā eḍāibe prabhura prema-mahājāle  
who  will escape  the Lord’s  in the great net of prema

(37) He forgave their offences, and they sank into the waters of prema. Who can escape the Lord’s great net of love?

সবা নিস্তারিতে প্রভু কৃপা-অবতার ৷  
সবা নিস্তারিতে করে চাতুরী অপার ॥ ৩৮ ॥

sabā nistārite prabhu kṛpā-avatāra  
all  to deliver  the Lord  the avatāra of mercy  
sabā nistārite kare cāturī apāra  
all  to deliver  He uses  stratagems  boundless

(38) The Lord is the avatāra of mercy who came to deliver everyone, and to deliver everyone He uses boundless stratagems.

তবে নিজ ভক্ত কৈল যত ম্লেচ্ছ আদি ৷  
সবে এড়াইল মাত্র কাশীর মায়াবাদী ॥ ৩৯ ॥

tabe nija bhakta kaila yata mleccha ādi  
then  His own  devotees  He made  all  mlecchas  and others  
sabe eḍāila mātra kāśīra māyāvādī  
all  escaped  only  of Kāśī  the Māyāvādīs

(39) He made His devotees of all — even the mlecchas and others; only the Māyāvādīs of Kāśī escaped.

বৃন্দাবন যাইতে প্রভু রহিলা কাশীতে ৷  
মায়াবাদিগণ তাঁরে লাগিলা নিন্দিতে ॥ ৪০ ॥

vṛndāvana yāite prabhu rahilā kāśīte  
to Vṛndāvana  going  the Lord  stayed  in Kāśī  
māyāvādi-gaṇa tām̐re lāgilā nindite  
the Māyāvādīs  Him  began  to blaspheme

(40) On His way to Vṛndāvana the Lord stayed in Kāśī, and the Māyāvādīs began to blaspheme Him:

সন্ন্যাসী হইয়া করেন গায়ন, নাচন ৷  
না করে বেদান্ত শ্রবণ, করে সংকীর্ত্তন ॥ ৪১ ॥

sannyāsī haiyā karena gāyana, nācana  
a sannyāsī  being  He does  singing  dancing  
nā kare vedānta śravaṇa, kare saṅkīrtana  
not  does  of Vedānta  hearing  does  saṅkīrtana

(41) “Though a sannyāsī, He sings and dances. He does not hear Vedānta, but performs saṅkīrtana.[^14]

মূর্খ সন্ন্যাসী নিজ-ধর্ম্ম নাহি জানে ৷  
ভাবুক হইয়া ফেরে ভাবুকের সনে ॥ ৪২ ॥

mūrkha sannyāsī nija-dharma nāhi jāne  
a foolish  sannyāsī  his own dharma  not  knows  
bhāvuka haiyā phere bhāvukera sane  
sentimental  being  He wanders  with the sentimentalists  together

(42) This foolish sannyāsī does not know His own dharma; a sentimentalist Himself, He wanders about with sentimentalists.”

এ সব শুনিয়া প্রভু হাসে মনে মনে ৷  
উপেক্ষা করিয়া কারো না কৈল সম্ভাষণে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

e saba śuniyā prabhu hāse mane mane  
this  all  hearing  the Lord  smiles  in the mind  in the mind  
upekṣā kariyā kāro nā kaila sambhāṣaṇe  
disregard  making  to anyone  not  made  conversation

(43) Hearing all this, the Lord smiled within, and, paying no heed, He spoke with none of them.

উপেক্ষা করিয়া কৈল মথুরা গমন ৷  
মথুরা দেখিয়া পুনঃ কৈল আগমন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

upekṣā kariyā kaila mathurā gamana  
disregard  making  He made  to Mathurā  going  
mathurā dekhiyā punaḥ kaila āgamana  
Mathurā  having seen  again  He made  return

(44) Paying them no heed, He went on to Mathurā, and after seeing Mathurā He came back again.

কাশীতে লেখক শূদ্র-শ্রীচন্দ্রশেখর ৷  
তাঁর ঘরে রহিলা প্রভু স্বতন্ত্র ঈশ্বর ॥ ৪৫ ॥

kāśīte lekhaka śūdra-śrīcandraśekhara  
in Kāśī  the scribe  the śūdra  Śrī Candraśekhara  
tām̐ra ghare rahilā prabhu svatantra īśvara  
his  in the house  stayed  the Lord  the independent  Īśvara

(45) In Kāśī lived the scribe Śrī Candraśekhara, a śūdra; in his house the Lord stayed, for He is the independent Lord.[^15]

তপন-মিশ্রের ঘরে ভিক্ষা-নির্ব্বাহণ ৷  
সন্ন্যাসীর সঙ্গে নাহি মানে নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

tapana-miśrera ghare bhikṣā-nirvāhaṇa  
of Tapana Miśra  in the house  alms  taking  
sannyāsīra saṅge nāhi māne nimantraṇa  
of sannyāsīs  in company  not  accepts  invitation

(46) He took His meals at the house of Tapana Miśra and accepted no invitation in the company of the sannyāsīs.

সনাতন গোসাঞি আসি’ তাঁহাই মিলিলা ৷  
তাঁর শিক্ষা লাগি’ প্রভু দু-মাস রহিলা ॥ ৪৭ ॥

sanātana gosāñi āsi’ tām̐hāi mililā  
Sanātana  Gosāñi  coming  there  met  
tām̐ra śikṣā lāgi’ prabhu du-māsa rahilā  
his  instruction  for the sake of  the Lord  two months  stayed

(47) Sanātana Gosāñi came and met Him there, and to instruct him the Lord stayed two months.

তাঁরে শিক্ষাইল সব বৈষ্ণবের ধর্ম্ম ৷  
শ্রীভাগবত-আদি শাস্ত্রের যত গূঢ় মর্ম্ম ॥ ৪৮ ॥

tām̐re śikṣāila saba vaiṣṇavera dharma  
him  He taught  all  of the Vaiṣṇavas  the dharma  
śrī bhāgavata-ādi śāstrera yata gūḍha marma  
Śrī Bhāgavata and other  of the scriptures  all  secret  heart

(48) He taught him the whole dharma of the Vaiṣṇavas and all the hidden heart of the *Bhāgavata* and the other scriptures.

ইতিমধ্যে চন্দ্রশেখর, মিশ্র-তপন ৷  
দুঃখী হঞা প্রভু-পায় কৈল নিবেদন ॥ ৪৯ ॥

itimadhye candraśekhara, miśra-tapana  
meanwhile  Candraśekhara  Miśra Tapana  
duḥkhī hañā prabhu-pāya kaila nivedana  
grieved  being  at the Lord’s feet  made  petition

(49) Meanwhile Candraśekhara and Tapana Miśra, deeply grieved, came to the Lord’s feet with a petition:

কতেক শুনিব প্রভু তোমার নিন্দন!  
না পারি সহিতে, এবে ছাড়িব জীবন ॥ ৫০ ॥

kateka śuniba prabhu tomāra nindana!  
how much  shall we hear  O Lord  of You  the blasphemy  
nā pāri sahite, ebe chāḍiba jīvana  
not  we can  bear  now  we shall give up  life

(50) “How much longer must we hear You blasphemed, O Lord? We cannot bear it; we would rather give up our lives.

তোমাকে নিন্দয়ে যত সন্ন্যাসীর গণ ৷  
শুনিতে না পারি, ফাটে হৃদয়-শ্রবণ ॥ ৫১ ॥

tomāke nindaye yata sannyāsīra gaṇa  
You  blaspheme  all  of sannyāsīs  the company  
śunite nā pāri, phāṭe hṛdaya-śravaṇa  
to hear  not  we can  bursts  heart and ears

(51) All the sannyāsīs revile You, and we cannot listen to it: our hearts and ears are bursting.”

ইহা শুনি’ রহে প্রভু ঈষৎ হাসিয়া ৷  
সেইকালে এক বিপ্র মিলিল আসিয়া ॥ ৫২ ॥

ihā śuni’ rahe prabhu īṣat hāsiyā  
this  hearing  remains  the Lord  slightly  smiling  
sei kāle eka vipra milila āsiyā  
at that  time  one  brāhmaṇa  met  coming

(52) Hearing this, the Lord only smiled a little. Just then a brāhmaṇa came to meet Him.

আসি’ নিবেদন করে চরণে ধরিয়া ৷  
এক বস্তু মাগোঁ, দেহ, প্রসন্ন হইয়া ॥ ৫৩ ॥

āsi’ nivedana kare caraṇe dhariyā  
coming  petition  makes  the feet  clasping  
eka vastu māgom̐, deha, prasanna haiyā  
one  thing  I beg  give  pleased  being

(53) He came and, clasping the Lord’s feet, made his petition: “I beg one thing of You; be pleased and grant it.

সকল সন্ন্যাসী মুঞি কৈনু নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
তুমি যদি আইস, পূর্ণ হয় মোর মন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

sakala sannyāsī muñi kainu nimantraṇa  
all  sannyāsīs  I  have made  invitation  
tumi yadi āisa, pūrṇa haya mora mana  
You  if  come  fulfilled  becomes  my  heart

(54) I have invited all the sannyāsīs; if You come too, my heart’s desire will be fulfilled.

না যাহ সন্ন্যাসী-গোষ্ঠী, ইহা আমি জানি ৷  
মোরে অনুগ্রহ কর নিমন্ত্রণ মানি’ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

nā yāha sannyāsī-goṣṭhī, ihā āmi jāni  
not  You go  sannyāsī-gatherings  this  I  know  
more anugraha kara nimantraṇa māni’  
to me  mercy  show  the invitation  accepting

(55) I know that You do not go to gatherings of sannyāsīs; yet be merciful to me and accept my invitation.”

প্রভু হাসি’ নিমন্ত্রণ কৈল অঙ্গীকার ৷  
সন্ন্যাসীরে কৃপা লাগি’ এ ভঙ্গী তাঁহার ॥ ৫৬ ॥

prabhu hāsi’ nimantraṇa kaila aṅgīkāra  
the Lord  smiling  the invitation  made  acceptance  
sannyāsīre kṛpā lāgi’ e bhaṅgī tām̐hāra  
to the sannyāsīs  mercy  for the sake of  this  gesture  His

(56) Smiling, the Lord accepted the invitation; He made this gesture to show mercy to the sannyāsīs.

সে বিপ্র জানেন, প্রভু না যা’ন কা’র ঘরে ৷  
তাঁহার প্রেরণায় তাঁরে অত্যাগ্রহ করে ॥ ৫৭ ॥

se vipra jānena, prabhu nā yā’na kā’ra ghare  
that  brāhmaṇa  knows  the Lord  not  goes  to anyone’s  house  
tām̐hāra preraṇāya tām̐re atyāgraha kare  
by His  inspiration  Him  great insistence  makes

(57) The brāhmaṇa knew that the Lord does not go to anyone’s house, but, inspired by the Lord Himself, he pressed Him with great insistence.[^16]

আর দিনে গেলা প্রভু সে বিপ্র-ভবনে ৷  
দেখিলেন, বসিয়াছেন সন্ন্যাসীর গণে ॥ ৫৮ ॥

āra dine gelā prabhu se vipra-bhavane  
on the next  day  went  the Lord  that  brāhmaṇa’s  house  
dekhilena, vasiyāchena sannyāsīra gaṇe  
He saw  are seated  of sannyāsīs  the company

(58) The next day the Lord went to the brāhmaṇa’s house and saw the sannyāsīs all seated there.

সবা নমস্করি’ গেলা পাদ-প্রক্ষালনে ৷  
পাদ প্রক্ষালিয়া বসিল সেই স্থানে ॥ ৫৯ ॥

sabā namaskari’ gelā pāda-prakṣālane  
to all  bowing  He went  to wash the feet  
pāda prakṣāliyā vasila sei sthāne  
the feet  having washed  sat  in that  place

(59) Bowing to them all, He went to wash His feet, and when He had washed them He sat down in that very place.

বসিয়া করিলা কিছু ঐশ্বর্য্য প্রকাশ ৷  
মহাতেজোময় বপু কোটীসূর্য্যাভাস ॥ ৬০ ॥

vasiyā karilā kichu aiśvarya prakāśa  
sitting  He made  some  of majesty  display  
mahātejomaya vapu koṭī-sūryābhāsa  
greatly effulgent  body  of millions of suns  the splendour

(60) As He sat there, He revealed a little of His majesty: His body shone with great effulgence, with the splendour of millions of suns.[^17]

প্রভাবে আকর্ষিল সব সন্ন্যাসীর মন ৷  
উঠিলা সন্ন্যাসী সব ছাড়িয়া আসন ॥ ৬১ ॥

prabhāve ākarṣila saba sannyāsīra mana  
by the splendour  He attracted  all  of the sannyāsīs  the minds  
uṭhilā sannyāsī saba chāḍiyā āsana  
rose  the sannyāsīs  all  leaving  their seats

(61) That splendour drew the minds of all the sannyāsīs, and they all rose from their seats.

প্রকাশানন্দ-নামে এক সন্ন্যাসী প্রধান ৷  
প্রভুকে কহিল কিছু করিয়া সম্মান ॥ ৬২ ॥

prakāśānanda-nāme eka sannyāsī pradhāna  
Prakāśānanda  by name  one  sannyāsī  chief  
prabhuke kahila kichu kariyā sammāna  
to the Lord  spoke  something  showing  respect

(62) The chief among the sannyāsīs, Prakāśānanda by name, addressed the Lord with respect:[^18]

ইহাঁ আইস গোসাঞি, শুনহ শ্রীপাদ ৷  
অপবিত্র স্থানে বৈস, কিবা অবসাদ ॥ ৬৩ ॥

ihām̐ āisa gosāñi, śunaha śrīpāda  
here  come  Gosāñi  listen  Śrīpāda  
apavitra sthāne vaisa, kibā avasāda  
in an impure  place  You sit  what  dejection

(63) “Come here, Gosāñi; listen, Śrīpāda. You are sitting in an impure place — why are You so downcast?”

প্রভু কহে, — আমি হই হীন-সম্প্রদায় ৷  
তোমা-সবার সম্প্রদায়ে বসিতে না যুয়ায় ॥ ৬৪ ॥

prabhu kahe, — āmi hai hīna-sampradāya  
the Lord  says  I  am  of a lower sampradāya  
tomā-sabāra sampradāye vasite nā yuyāya  
of you all  in the assembly  to sit  not  befits

(64) The Lord replied, “I belong to a lower sampradāya; it is not fitting for Me to sit in your assembly.”[^19]

আপনে প্রকাশানন্দ হাতেতে ধরিয়া ৷  
বসাইল সভামধ্যে সম্মান করিয়া ॥ ৬৫ ॥

āpane prakāśānanda hātete dhariyā  
himself  Prakāśānanda  by the hand  taking  
vasāila sabhā-madhye sammāna kariyā  
seated  in the midst of the assembly  respect  showing

(65) Then Prakāśānanda himself took Him by the hand and, with respect, seated Him in the midst of the assembly.

পুছিল, তোমার নাম ‘শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য’ ৷  
কেশব-ভারতীর শিষ্য, তাতে তুমি ধন্য ॥ ৬৬ ॥

puchila, tomāra nāma ‘śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya’  
he asked  Your  name  ‘Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’  
keśava-bhāratīra śiṣya, tāte tumi dhanya  
of Keśava Bhāratī  the disciple  therefore  You  are glorious

(66) He asked, “Your name is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, is it not? You are a disciple of Keśava Bhāratī, and so You are worthy of glory.

সাম্প্রদায়িক সন্ন্যাসী তুমি, রহ এই গ্রামে ৷  
কি কারণে আমা-সবার না কর দর্শনে ॥ ৬৭ ॥

sāmpradāyika sannyāsī tumi, raha ei grāme  
of the sampradāya  a sannyāsī  You  You live  in this  town  
ki kāraṇe āmā-sabāra nā kara darśane  
for what  reason  us all  not  You make  seeing

(67) You are a sannyāsī of our own sampradāya and You live in this town; why then do You not come to see us?

সন্ন্যাসী হইয়া কর নর্ত্তন-গায়ন ৷  
ভাবুক সব সঙ্গে লঞা করহ কীর্ত্তন ॥ ৬৮ ॥

sannyāsī haiyā kara nartana-gāyana  
a sannyāsī  being  You do  dancing and singing  
bhāvuka saba saṅge lañā karaha kīrtana  
sentimentalists  all  with  taking  You do  kīrtana

(68) Though You are a sannyāsī, You dance and sing, and You take all these sentimentalists with You and perform kīrtana.

বেদান্ত-পঠন, ধ্যান, — সন্ন্যাসীর ধর্ম্ম ৷  
তাহা ছাড়ি’ কর কেনে ভাবুকের কর্ম্ম ॥ ৬৯ ॥

vedānta-paṭhana, dhyāna, — sannyāsīra dharma  
Vedānta-study  meditation  of a sannyāsī  the dharma  
tāhā chāḍi’ kara kene bhāvukera karma  
that  abandoning  You do  why  of sentimentalists  the work

(69) The study of Vedānta and meditation are the dharma of a sannyāsī; why do You abandon them and do the work of sentimentalists?

প্রভাবে দেখিয়ে তোমা সাক্ষাৎ নারায়ণ ৷  
হীনাচার কর কেনে, ইথে কি কারণ ॥ ৭০ ॥

prabhāve dekhiye tomā sākṣāt nārāyaṇa  
by Your splendour  we see  You  directly  Nārāyaṇa  
hīnācāra kara kene, ithe ki kāraṇa  
low conduct  You do  why  in this  what  reason

(70) From Your splendour we see that You are Nārāyaṇa Himself; why then do You behave so lowly? What is the reason?”

প্রভু কহে, — শুন, শ্রীপাদ, ইহার কারণ ৷  
গুরু মোরে মূর্খ দেখি’ করিল শাসন ॥ ৭১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — śuna, śrīpāda, ihāra kāraṇa  
the Lord  says  listen  Śrīpāda  of this  the reason  
guru more mūrkha dekhi’ karila śāsana  
the guru  Me  a fool  seeing  made  chastisement

(71) The Lord said, “Listen, Śrīpāda, to the reason for this. My guru saw that I was a fool, and he chastised Me:[^20]

মূর্খ তুমি, তোমার নাহি বেদান্তাধিকার ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণমন্ত্র’ জপ’ সদা, — এই মন্ত্রসার ॥ ৭২ ॥

mūrkha tumi, tomāra nāhi vedāntādhikāra  
a fool  You are  Your  there is not  qualification for Vedānta  
‘kṛṣṇa-mantra’ japa’ sadā, — ei mantra-sāra  
‘the Kṛṣṇa-mantra’  chant  always  this  of mantras the essence

(72) ‘You are a fool; You are not qualified for Vedānta. Always chant the Kṛṣṇa-mantra — it is the essence of all mantras.

কৃষ্ণমন্ত্র হৈতে হবে সংসার-মোচন ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম হৈতে পাবে কৃষ্ণের চরণ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa-mantra haite habe saṁsāra-mocana  
the Kṛṣṇa-mantra  from  will be  from saṁsāra  release  
kṛṣṇa-nāma haite pābe kṛṣṇera caraṇa  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  from  You will attain  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(73) From the Kṛṣṇa-mantra will come release from saṁsāra, and by Kṛṣṇa’s name You will attain Kṛṣṇa’s feet.[^21]

নাম বিনা কলিকালে নাহি আর ধর্ম্ম ৷  
সর্ব্বমন্ত্রসার নাম, — এই শাস্ত্রমর্ম্ম ॥ ৭৪ ॥

nāma vinā kali-kāle nāhi āra dharma  
the name  without  in the age of Kali  there is not  other  dharma  
sarva-mantra-sāra nāma, — ei śāstra-marma  
of all mantras the essence  the name  this  of scripture the heart

(74) In the age of Kali there is no dharma other than the name; the name is the essence of all mantras — this is the heart of the scriptures.’

এত বলি’ এক শ্লোক শিখাইল মোরে ৷  
কণ্ঠে করি’ এই শ্লোক করিহ বিচারে ॥ ৭৫ ॥

eta bali’ eka śloka śikhāila more  
thus  saying  one  verse  he taught  Me  
kaṇṭhe kari’ ei śloka kariha vicāre  
in the throat  keeping  this  verse  make  reflection

(75) Saying this, he taught Me a verse: ‘Keep this verse on your lips and reflect on it.’

হরের্নাম হরের্নাম হরের্নামৈব কেবলম্ ৷  
কলৌ নাস্ত্যেব নাস্ত্যেব নাস্ত্যেব গতিরন্যথা ॥ ৭৬ ॥

harer nāma harer nāma  
of Hari  the name  of Hari  the name  
harer nāmaiva kevalam  
of Hari  the name  indeed  only  
kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva  
in Kali  there is not  indeed  there is not  indeed  
nāsty eva gatir anyathā  
there is not  indeed  way  other

(76) “The name of Hari, the name of Hari, the name of Hari alone! In the age of Kali there is no other way, there is no other way, there is no other way.” (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa, 38.126)[^22]

এই আজ্ঞা পাঞা নাম লই অনুক্ষণ ৷  
নাম লৈতে লৈতে মোর ভ্রান্ত হৈল মন ॥ ৭৭ ॥

ei ājñā pāñā nāma lai anukṣaṇa  
this  order  receiving  the name  I take  constantly  
nāma laite laite mora bhrānta haila mana  
the name  taking  taking  My  bewildered  became  mind

(77) Receiving this order, I took the name constantly, and as I chanted on and on, My mind grew bewildered.

ধৈর্য্য ধরিতে নারি, হৈলাম উন্মত্ত ৷  
হাসি, কান্দি, নাচি, গাই, যৈছে মদমত্ত ॥ ৭৮ ॥

dhairya dharite nāri, hailāma unmatta  
composure  to hold  I cannot  I became  mad  
hāsi, kāndi, nāci, gāi, yaiche madamatta  
I laugh  I weep  I dance  I sing  like  one intoxicated

(78) I could no longer keep My composure; I became mad. I laughed and wept, I danced and sang like one intoxicated.

তবে ধৈর্য্য ধরি’ মনে করিলাম বিচার ৷  
কৃষ্ণনামে জ্ঞানাচ্ছন্ন হইল আমার ॥ ৭৯ ॥

tabe dhairya dhari’ mane karilāma vicāra  
then  composure  holding  in the mind  I made  consideration  
kṛṣṇa-nāme jñānācchanna haila āmāra  
by Kṛṣṇa’s name  knowledge-covered  became  My

(79) Then, gathering My composure, I reflected: “Kṛṣṇa’s name has covered all My knowledge.

পাগল হইলাঙ আমি, ধৈর্য্য নাহি মনে ৷  
এত চিন্তি’ নিবেদিলাম গুরুর চরণে ॥ ৮০ ॥

pāgala hailāṅa āmi, dhairya nāhi mane  
mad  have become  I  composure  there is not  in the mind  
eta cinti’ nivedilāma gurura caraṇe  
thus  thinking  I submitted  at the guru’s  feet

(80) I have gone mad; there is no composure left in My mind.” Thinking this, I came to the feet of My guru and said:

কিবা মন্ত্র দিলা, গোসাঞি, কিবা তার বল ৷  
জপিতে জপিতে মন্ত্র করিল পাগল ॥ ৮১ ॥

kibā mantra dilā, gosāñi, kibā tāra bala  
what  mantra  you gave  Gosāñi  what  its  power  
japite japite mantra karila pāgala  
chanting  chanting  the mantra  made  mad

(81) ‘What mantra have you given Me, Gosāñi? What power it has! Chanting and chanting, the mantra has made Me mad.

হাসায়, নাচায়, মোরে করায় ক্রন্দন ৷  
এত শুনি’ গুরু মোরে বলিলা বচন ॥ ৮২ ॥

hāsāya, nācāya, more karāya krandana  
it makes laugh  makes dance  Me  makes  weep  
eta śuni’ guru more balilā vacana  
this  hearing  the guru  to Me  spoke  words

(82) It makes Me laugh, it makes Me dance, it makes Me weep.’ Hearing this, My guru spoke these words to Me:

কৃষ্ণনাম-মহামন্ত্রের এই ত’ স্বভাব ৷  
যেই জপে, তার কৃষ্ণে উপজয়ে ভাব ॥ ৮৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nāma-mahā-mantrera ei ta’ svabhāva  
of the great mantra of Kṛṣṇa’s name  this  indeed  the nature  
yei jape, tāra kṛṣṇe upajaye bhāva  
whoever  chants  his  for Kṛṣṇa  arises  bhāva

(83) ‘Such is the nature of the great mantra, Kṛṣṇa’s name: whoever chants it, in him bhāva for Kṛṣṇa awakens.[^23]

কৃষ্ণবিষয়ক প্রেমা — পরম পুরুষার্থ ৷  
যার আগে তৃণতুল্য চারি পুরুষার্থ ॥ ৮৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-viṣayaka premā — parama puruṣārtha  
for Kṛṣṇa  prema  the supreme  goal of life  
yāra āge tṛṇa-tulya cāri puruṣārtha  
before which  like straw  the four  goals of life

(84) Prema for Kṛṣṇa is the supreme goal of life; before it the four goals of life are like straw.

পঞ্চম পুরুষার্থ — প্রেমানন্দামৃতসিন্ধু ৷  
ব্রহ্মাদি আনন্দ যার নহে এক বিন্দু ॥ ৮৫ ॥

pañcama puruṣārtha — premānandāmṛta-sindhu  
the fifth  goal of life  an ocean of the nectar of the bliss of prema  
brahmādi ānanda yāra nahe eka bindu  
of Brahman and the rest  the bliss  of which  is not  one  drop

(85) The fifth goal of life is an ocean of the nectar of the bliss of prema; the bliss of Brahman and all the rest is not equal to a single drop of it.[^24]

কৃষ্ণনামের ফল — ‘প্রেমা’, সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
ভাগ্যে সেই প্রেমা তোমায় করিল উদয় ॥ ৮৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nāmera phala — ‘premā’, sarva-śāstre kaya  
of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the fruit  ‘prema’  in all scriptures  it is said  
bhāgye sei premā tomāya karila udaya  
by good fortune  that  prema  in You  has made  dawning

(86) All the scriptures say that the fruit of Kṛṣṇa’s name is prema, and by good fortune that prema has dawned in You.

প্রেমার স্বভাবে করে চিত্ত-তনু ক্ষোভ ৷  
কৃষ্ণের চরণ-প্রাপ্ত্যে উপজায় লোভ ॥ ৮৭ ॥

premāra svabhāve kare citta-tanu kṣobha  
by prema’s  nature  it makes  of mind and body  agitation  
kṛṣṇera caraṇa-prāptye upajāya lobha  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  for the attainment  arises  greed

(87) It is the nature of prema to stir both mind and body and to awaken a burning longing to attain Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

প্রেমার স্বভাবে ভক্ত হাসে, কান্দে, গায় ৷  
উন্মত্ত হইয়া নাচে, ইতি-উতি ধায় ॥ ৮৮ ॥

premāra svabhāve bhakta hāse, kānde, gāya  
by prema’s  nature  the devotee  laughs  weeps  sings  
unmatta haiyā nāce, iti-uti dhāya  
mad  becoming  dances  here and there  runs

(88) By the nature of prema the devotee laughs, weeps and sings; as if mad, he dances and runs here and there.[^25]

স্বেদ, কম্প, রোমাঞ্চাশ্রু, গদ্গদ, বৈবর্ণ্য ৷  
উন্মাদ, বিষাদ, ধৈর্য্য, গর্ব্ব, হর্ষ, দৈন্য ॥ ৮৯ ॥

এত ভাবে প্রেমা ভক্তগণেরে নাচায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের আনন্দামৃতসাগরে ভাসায় ॥ ৯০ ॥

sveda, kampa, romāñcāśru, gadgada, vaivarṇya  
perspiration  trembling  hair standing on end and tears  faltering voice  pallor  
unmāda, viṣāda, dhairya, garva, harṣa, dainya  
madness  despondency  steadiness  pride  joy  humility  
eta bhāve premā bhakta-gaṇere nācāya  
with so many  feelings  prema  the devotees  makes dance  
kṛṣṇera ānandāmṛta-sāgare bhāsāya  
in Kṛṣṇa’s  ocean of the nectar of bliss  makes float

(89–90) Perspiration, trembling, hair standing on end, tears, a faltering voice and change of colour; madness, despondency, steadiness, pride, joy and humility — with all these feelings prema makes the devotees dance and sets them afloat on the ocean of the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s bliss.

ভাল হৈল, পাইলে তুমি পরমপুরুষার্থ ৷  
তোমার প্রেমেতে আমি হৈলাঙ কৃতার্থ ॥ ৯১ ॥

bhāla haila, pāile tumi parama-puruṣārtha  
good  it is  You have attained  You  the supreme goal of life  
tomāra premete āmi hailāṅa kṛtārtha  
by Your  prema  I  have become  fulfilled

(91) It is good that You have attained the supreme goal of life; by Your prema my own life has been fulfilled.

নাচ, গাও, ভক্তসঙ্গে কর সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম উপদেশি’ তার’ সর্ব্বজন ॥ ৯২ ॥

nāca, gāo, bhakta-saṅge kara saṅkīrtana  
dance  sing  with the devotees  perform  saṅkīrtana  
kṛṣṇa-nāma upadeśi’ tāra’ sarva-jana  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  teaching  deliver  all people

(92) Dance, sing and perform saṅkīrtana with the devotees; teach Kṛṣṇa’s name and deliver all people.’[^26]

এত বলি’ এক শ্লোক শিখাইল মোরে ৷  
ভাগবতের সার এই বলে বারে বারে ॥ ৯৩ ॥

eta bali’ eka śloka śikhāila more  
thus  saying  one  verse  he taught  Me  
bhāgavatera sāra ei bale vāre vāre  
of the Bhāgavata  the essence  this  he says  again  again

(93) Saying this, he taught Me a verse and told Me again and again: ‘This is the essence of the *Bhāgavata*.’

এবংব্রতঃ স্বপ্রিয়নামকীর্ত্ত্যা-  
জাতানুরাগো দ্রুতচিত্ত উচ্চৈঃ ৷  
হসত্যথো রোদিতি রৌতি গায়-  
ত্যুন্মাদবন্নৃত্যতি লোকবাহ্যঃ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

evaṁ-vrataḥ sva-priya-nāma-kīrtyā  
one with such a vow  by the chanting of the names of his beloved  
jātānurāgo druta-citta uccaiḥ  
in whom love has arisen  with melted heart  loudly  
hasaty atho roditi rauti gāyaty  
laughs  then  weeps  cries out  sings  
unmāda-van nṛtyati loka-bāhyaḥ  
like a madman  dances  beyond the world

(94) “One who has taken this vow, chanting the names of his beloved Lord, finds love awakening within him; his heart melts, and, heedless of the world, he laughs aloud, weeps, cries out, sings and dances like a madman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.40)[^27]

এই তাঁর বাক্যে আমি দৃঢ় বিশ্বাস ধরি’ ৷  
নিরন্তর কৃষ্ণনাম সংকীর্ত্তন করি ॥ ৯৫ ॥

ei tām̐ra vākye āmi dṛḍha viśvāsa dhari’  
this  his  in the words  I  firm  faith  holding  
nirantara kṛṣṇa-nāma saṅkīrtana kari  
constantly  Kṛṣṇa’s name  saṅkīrtana  I do

(95) With firm faith in these words of his, I constantly perform the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name.

সেই কৃষ্ণনাম কভু গাওয়ায়, নাচায় ৷  
গাহি, নাচি নাহি আমি আপন-ইচ্ছায় ॥ ৯৬ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa-nāma kabhu gāoyāya, nācāya  
that  Kṛṣṇa’s name  at times  makes sing  makes dance  
gāhi, nāci nāhi āmi āpana-icchāya  
I sing  I dance  not  I  by My own will

(96) That name of Kṛṣṇa sometimes makes Me sing and dance; I do not sing and dance of My own will.[^28]

কৃষ্ণনামে যে আনন্দসিন্ধু-আস্বাদন ৷  
ব্রহ্মানন্দ তার আগে খাতোদক-সম ॥ ৯৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nāme ye ānanda-sindhu-āsvādana  
in Kṛṣṇa’s name  which  of the ocean of bliss  the tasting  
brahmānanda tāra āge khātodaka-sama  
the bliss of Brahman  of that  before  like water in a ditch

(97) Before the ocean of bliss tasted in Kṛṣṇa’s name, the bliss of Brahman is like water in a ditch.

ত্বৎসাক্ষাৎকরণাহ্লাদ-বিশুদ্ধাব্ধিস্থিতস্য মে ৷  
সুখানি গোষ্পদায়ন্তে ব্রাহ্মাণ্যপি জগদ্গুরো ॥ ৯৮ ॥

tvat-sākṣāt-karaṇāhlāda-  
from seeing You face to face  the joy  
viśuddhābdhi-sthitasya me  
in the pure ocean  situated  for me  
sukhāni goṣ-padāyante  
joys  become like a calf’s hoofprint  
brāhmāṇy api jagad-guro  
of Brahman  even  O Guru of the world

(98) “O Guru of the world, now that I am immersed in the pure ocean of joy that comes from seeing You face to face, all other joys — even the bliss of Brahman — seem to me like the water in a calf’s hoofprint.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 14.36)

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: *agaty-eka-gati* — “the one refuge of those who have no other shelter”; *hīnārthādhika-sādhaka* — “He who grants to those deprived of the supreme good (*paramārtha*) something greater still — the fifth goal of life, love for Kṛṣṇa”.

[^2]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in the first chapter the truth of the guru was described — as the initiating and the instructing guru; the six truths are those of the verse *vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān* (Ādi 1.1), and now the remaining five are examined.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: in substance the five truths are one, without duality, but for the sake of the pastimes and the tasting of rasa the one truth is fivefold. The devotee in form, the devotee in essence and the incarnation of a devotee are the Lord Himself, His *prakāśa* and His *aṁśa* (*viṣṇu-tattva*); the potency of devotion and the pure devotee are potencies non-different from Him, who serve Him. Without either the worshipped or the worshipper there would be no pastime of tasting rasa.

[^4]: According to the *Anubhāṣya*: the devotee in form is Śrī Gaurāṅga, who tastes His own sweetness; the devotee in essence (in the form of His brother) is Śrī Nityānanda; the devotee-avatāra is Śrī Advaita; the one called a devotee is Śrīvāsa and others, established in *śānta*, *dāsya* and the other rasas; the potency is Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Rāmānanda and others. The same verse as Ādi 1.14; ascribed in the edition to Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī.

[^5]: The edition’s heading: “Though He is the Lord of all, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is full of the mood of one who is subordinate.” *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Caitanya is the one Supreme Lord of all conscious beings (*nityo nityānām…*); though non-different from the son of the king of Vraja, He takes a form full of the mood of serving Kṛṣṇa. That form must not be thought of as a body for worldly enjoyment or counted among the jīvas of this world: it is manifest in pure *sattva* (*viśuddha-sattva*) and is revealed to a heart illumined by it.

[^6]: The edition’s heading: “To taste His own sweetness, Kṛṣṇa descends as Gaura — ‘in the form of a devotee’.” *Anubhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇa, the abode of all sweetness, though He is Himself the object of love (*viṣaya*), takes on the sentiment of its subject (*āśraya*) and tastes the joy of serving Himself; yet Śrī Caitanya is not merely a form of the *āśraya*: He is the original Lord Himself (*svayaṁ-rūpa*).

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Mahāprabhu is the highest Truth, above all, while Śrī Nityānanda and Śrī Advaita are *īśvara-tattvas* subordinate to Him; all three are worshipable by everyone else. The fourth truth is the pure devotees, the fifth the intimate (*antaraṅga*) devotees; both are worshippers. The two worshipable truths, though the last two worship them, are themselves engaged in the service of Śrī Gaurāṅga.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: the truths of *śakti* — the intimate devotees — abide in *madhura-rasa*, which includes *vātsalya*, *sakhya* and *dāsya*; the servants of Śrī Nityānanda and Śrī Advaita, the pure devotees, usually abide in *vātsalya*, *sakhya*, *dāsya* and *śānta*. When their love for Śrī Gaurasundara grows intense, they take shelter of *madhura-rasa* under the guidance of the intimate devotees. Pure devotees are those who, free from other desires, do not cover their service to Kṛṣṇa with *karma* or *jñāna*; the intimate devotees are those wholly given to *madhura-rasa* alone.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: “with them” — with all four: with His *prakāśa* (Śrī Nityānanda), with the avatāra of the Puruṣa (Śrī Advaita), with the intimate devotees and with the pure devotees. The edition’s heading: “With the four truths — the Lord’s pastimes, preaching, tasting and giving.”

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the storehouse of prema is the pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. It had come into the world before, but it was locked and sealed; in the descent of Śrī Caitanya the Pañca-tattva broke the seal, threw open the doors and tasted prema, plundering it.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: in the Lord’s *jīva-śakti*, together with the inclination towards Kṛṣṇa, there lies hidden the seed of the desire to enjoy in turning away from Him. As a seed in flooded earth can put forth no shoot, so in the flood of prema the seed of every desire other than service to Kṛṣṇa perished. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: there perished the seed of the bondage of *avidyā* — the soul’s forgetfulness that it is Kṛṣṇa’s servant.

[^12]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Māyāvādīs are the sannyāsīs Prakāśānanda and others; those devoted to karma — Devānanda and other karmīs without bhakti, the *smārtas*, for whom karma and its fruits are the chief goal; the false logicians — Sārvabhauma and other godless logicians; the blasphemers — the one whom the Lord drove away with a stick, Gopāla Cāpāla and others who reviled the Lord and His devotees; the pāṣaṇḍīs — those who place other gods on a level with the Lord; the lowest of students — those for whom learning is only a cause for argument and not a way to God.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Mahāprabhu accepted sannyāsa in Śaka 1432, in the month of Māgha, during the bright fortnight, at Katwa, from the *daṇḍi-svāmī* Śrī Keśava Bhāratī of the school of Śrī Śaṅkara (under the Śṛṅgerī Maṭha in the south).

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: by the rule of the *smṛti*, a sannyāsī must give up singing, dancing and playing music and always study Vedānta; seeing that Mahāprabhu did not hear Śaṅkara’s Māyāvāda but danced in love, absorbed in singing of Kṛṣṇa, the sannyāsīs of Kāśī took Him to be ignorant of the dharma of sannyāsa.

[^15]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Candraśekhara was a *vaidya*, and so by varṇa a śūdra. It is not proper for a sannyāsī to spend the night in a śūdra’s house, but Mahāprabhu stayed with him out of mercy, for He is the independent Lord: before His mercy brāhmaṇa and śūdra are equal.

[^16]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: wishing to show mercy to the sannyāsīs, the Lord Himself moved the brāhmaṇa’s heart, and so he begged with such insistence.

[^17]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «কোটীসূর্য্যাভাস» (another reading: «কোটিসূর্য্যাভাস»); the sense is the same.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya* (on 7.149): Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī was then the leader of the sannyāsīs of Śaṅkara’s school in Kāśī. Some mistakenly identify him with Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī, the author of the *Caitanya-candrāmṛta* and the *Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi*, but they are different persons.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: of the ten names of sannyāsīs instituted by Śaṅkarācārya, “Tīrtha”, “Āśrama” and “Sarasvatī” are held higher than the rest. Mahāprabhu had taken sannyāsa in the “Bhāratī” line and therefore called Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī’s school the higher one. In this way He showed the humility of a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī, who seeks no honour for himself and gives honour to others.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Caitanya, Himself the sole object of the Vedānta, teaches who is fit to study it: one who is humbler than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, who seeks no honour and gives honour to others. In calling Himself a fool, He, the Lord of the fourteen worlds, shows the humility of a disciple; the real fool is one who judges the object of the Vedānta by the mind and senses.

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-sandarbha*, 284): the mantras too are names of the Lord, adorned with the word *namaḥ* and the like, and the sages have placed a special power in them; the name alone, depending on nothing else, is able to give the highest goal of life. Initiation is needed to curb the impure tendencies of those whose minds are attached to the body and restless.

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: in Satya-yuga the way is meditation, in Tretā sacrifice, in Dvāpara the worship of the Deity; in Kali there is none of these — there is only one way, the name of Hari; all other means are fruitless in Kali.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya* (on 7.74): “Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare / Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare — these sixteen names destroy the impurity of Kali; no higher means is to be found in all the Vedas” (*Kali-santaraṇa Upaniṣad*). On 7.83: the mantras of the Pañcarātra are called simply mantras, while the names of the Lord are called the *mahā-mantra*.

[^24]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «ব্রহ্মাদি আনন্দ» (“the bliss of Brahman and the rest”; the heading: “Prema, which puts liberation and the rest to shame, is the fifth goal of life”). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: even the bliss of Brahman, the first stage of liberation, cannot be compared with a single drop of prema. Other editions read «মোক্ষাদি» (“liberation and the rest”).

[^25]: *Anubhāṣya*: when those without prema display laughter, tears, dancing and singing, it is only a sign of their misfortune; one who performs bhajana must reject altogether any artificial agitation of body and senses. The laughter, tears, singing, dancing and longing born of bhāva and prema come when the natural function of the soul awakens; they are the guileless movements of one turned towards service. The presumption of one who, without prema, assumes the high station of a devotee brings only harm into the world.

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: to those whom Gurudeva sees to be qualified he gives the right to dance, sing and perform saṅkīrtana with like-minded devotees, and they, following in his steps, serve the deliverance of the world; the unqualified should chant Kṛṣṇa’s name in solitude.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: these words were spoken by Kavi, one of the nine Yogendras, to King Nimi. *Evaṁ-vrataḥ* — one whose vow is the service of hearing, chanting and the like; *druta-cittaḥ* — with a heart full of longing; *loka-bāhyaḥ* — indifferent to people’s laughter, blame or praise.

[^28]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Nāma Prabhu Himself, by His own will, moved Śrī Gaurasundara to sing and dance; Gaurasundara did not chant the name as though it were a thing of this world. Those who, driven by restless senses, make the name their plaything and try to lord it over Him, reap only the fruits of karma instead of bhajana.
