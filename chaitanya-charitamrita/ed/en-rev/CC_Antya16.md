“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 16  
**Grace to Kālidāsa, and the Ravings of Madness in Separation**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যং কৃষ্ণভাবামৃতং হি যঃ ৷  
আস্বাদ্যাস্বাদয়ন্ ভক্তান্ প্রেমদীক্ষামশিক্ষয়ৎ ॥ ১ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanyaṁ  
I bow down  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
kṛṣṇa-bhāvāmṛtaṁ hi yaḥ  
of Kṛṣṇa  of feelings  the nectar  indeed  who  
āsvādyāsvādayan bhaktān  
having tasted  making taste  the devotees  
prema-dīkṣām aśikṣayat  
of prema  the initiation  taught

(1) I bow down to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who Himself tasted the nectar of feelings for Kṛṣṇa and gave it to His devotees to taste; so He taught them the path of divine love.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এইমত মহাপ্রভু রহেন নীলাচলে ৷  
ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে সদা প্রেম-বিহ্বলে ॥ ৩ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu rahena nīlācale  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  stays  in Nīlācala  
bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge sadā prema-vihvale  
the devotees  with  always  by love  overwhelmed

(3) In this way Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala with His devotees, always overwhelmed by love.

বর্ষান্তরে আইলা সব গৌড়ের ভক্তগণ ৷  
পূর্ব্ববৎ আসি’ কৈল প্রভুর মিলন ॥ ৪ ॥

varṣāntare āilā saba gauḍera bhakta-gaṇa  
the next year  came  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees  
pūrvavat āsi’ kaila prabhura milana  
as before  coming  made  with the Lord  meeting

(4) The next year all the devotees of Gauḍa came again and, as before, met the Lord.

তাঁ-সবার সঙ্গে আইল কালিদাস নাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম বিনা তেঁহো নাহি জানে আন ॥ ৫ ॥

tām̐-sabāra saṅge āila kālidāsa nāma  
them all  with  came  Kālidāsa  by name  
kṛṣṇa-nāma vinā tem̐ho nāhi jāne āna  
Kṛṣṇa’s  name  except  he  not  knew  anything else

(5) With them came a devotee named Kālidāsa, who knew nothing but the name of Kṛṣṇa.

মহাভাগবত তেঁহো সরল উদার ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম-‘সঙ্কেতে’ চালায় ব্যবহার ॥ ৬ ॥

mahā-bhāgavata tem̐ho sarala udāra  
a great  devotee  he  simple  generous  
kṛṣṇa-nāma-‘saṅkete’ cālāya vyavahāra  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  name  by the signs  conducted  his dealings

(6) He was a great devotee, simple and generous, and even in his everyday dealings the name of Kṛṣṇa served him as a sign.[^2]

কৌতুকেতে তেঁহো যদি পাশক খেলায় ৷  
‘হরেকৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরেকৃষ্ণ’ করি’ পাশক চালায় ॥ ৭ ॥

kautukete tem̐ho yadi pāśaka khelāya  
in fun  he  if  at dice  played  
‘hare-kṛṣṇa’ ‘hare-kṛṣṇa’ kari’ pāśaka cālāya  
Hare Kṛṣṇa  Hare Kṛṣṇa  saying  the dice  threw

(7) When he played at dice for fun, he would cast them saying, “Hare Kṛṣṇa! Hare Kṛṣṇa!”[^3]

রঘুনাথ-দাসের তেঁহো হয় জ্ঞাতি-খুড়া ৷  
বৈষ্ণবের উচ্ছিষ্ট খাইতে তেঁহো হৈল বুড়া ॥ ৮ ॥

raghunātha-dāsera tem̐ho haya jñāti-khuḍā  
of Raghunātha  dāsa  he  was  a kinsman  uncle  
vaiṣṇavera ucchiṣṭa khāite tem̐ho haila buḍā  
of Vaiṣṇavas  the remnants  eating  he  became  old

(8) He was Raghunātha dāsa’s uncle by kinship, and he had grown old taking the remnants of Vaiṣṇavas.

গৌড়দেশে হয় যত বৈষ্ণবের গণ ৷  
সবার উচ্ছিষ্ট তেঁহো করিল ভোজন ॥ ৯ ॥

gauḍa-deśe haya yata vaiṣṇavera gaṇa  
in the land of Gauḍa  are  as many  of Vaiṣṇavas  as  
sabāra ucchiṣṭa tem̐ho karila bhojana  
of all  the remnants  he  did  eat

(9) He took the remnants of all the Vaiṣṇavas in Gauḍa.

ব্রাহ্মণ-বৈষ্ণব যত — ছোট, বড় হয় ৷  
উত্তম-বস্তু ভেট লঞা তাঁর ঠাঞি যায় ॥ ১০ ॥

brāhmaṇa-vaiṣṇava yata — choṭa, baḍa haya  
brāhmaṇa  Vaiṣṇavas  as many as  small  great  are  
uttama-vastu bheṭa lañā tām̐ra ṭhāñi yāya  
the best  things  as gifts  taking  to them  goes

(10) He would go to every Vaiṣṇava of brāhmaṇa birth, great and small, bringing the finest things as gifts.

তাঁর ঠাঞি শেষ-পাত্র লয়েন মাগিয়া ৷  
কাঁহা না পায়, তবে রহে লুকাঞা ॥ ১১ ॥

tām̐ra ṭhāñi śeṣa-pātra layena māgiyā  
from them  the plates of remnants  takes  begging  
kām̐hā nā pāya, tabe rahe lukāñā  
wherever  not  gets  then  stays  hiding

(11) He would beg them for the plates with their remnants, and where he was refused, he would hide nearby.

ভোজন করিলে পাত্র ফেলাঞা যায় ৷  
লুকাঞা সেই পাত্র আনি’ চাটি’ খায় ॥ ১২ ॥

bhojana karile pātra phelāñā yāya  
eating  having done  the plate  throwing away  goes  
lukāñā sei pātra āni’ cāṭi’ khāya  
secretly  that  plate  bringing  licking  eats

(12) When a Vaiṣṇava had eaten and thrown his plate away, Kālidāsa would secretly take it up and lick it clean.

শূদ্র-বৈষ্ণবের ঘরে যায় ভেট লঞা ৷  
এইমত তাঁর উচ্ছিষ্ট খায় লুকাঞা ॥ ১৩ ॥

śūdra-vaiṣṇavera ghare yāya bheṭa lañā  
of śūdra  Vaiṣṇavas  to the homes  goes  gifts  taking  
ei-mata tām̐ra ucchiṣṭa khāya lukāñā  
in this way  their  remnants  eats  secretly

(13) He went with gifts to the homes of Vaiṣṇavas of śūdra birth as well, and in the same way secretly took their remnants.

ভূঁইমালি-জাতি, ‘বৈষ্ণব’ — ‘ঝড়ু’ তাঁর নাম ৷  
আম্রফল লঞা তেঁহো গেলা তাঁর স্থান ॥ ১৪ ॥

bhūm̐imāli-jāti, ‘vaiṣṇava’ — ‘jhaḍu’ tām̐ra nāma  
bhūṅimāli  by caste  a Vaiṣṇava  Jhaḍu  his  name  
āmra-phala lañā tem̐ho gelā tām̐ra sthāna  
mangoes  taking  he  went  to his  place

(14) There was a Vaiṣṇava named Jhaḍu, of the *bhūṅimāli* caste. Kālidāsa went to his home, taking mangoes with him.[^4]

আম্র ভেট দিয়া তাঁর চরণ বন্দিলা ৷  
তাঁর পত্নীরে তবে নমস্কার কৈলা ॥ ১৫ ॥

āmra bheṭa diyā tām̐ra caraṇa vandilā  
the mangoes  as a gift  giving  his  feet  he honoured  
tām̐ra patnīre tabe namaskāra kailā  
his  wife  then  obeisance  made

(15) He presented the mangoes, bowed at his feet, and then paid his respects to his wife.

পত্নী-সহিত তেঁহো আছেন বসিয়া ৷  
বহু সম্মান কৈলা কালিদাসেরে দেখিয়া ॥ ১৬ ॥

patnī-sahita tem̐ho āchena vasiyā  
his wife  with  he  was  seated  
bahu sammāna kailā kālidāsere dekhiyā  
much  honour  showed  Kālidāsa  seeing

(16) Jhaḍu Ṭhākura was sitting with his wife; seeing Kālidāsa, he received him with great respect.

ইষ্টগোষ্ঠী কতক্ষণ করি’ তাঁর সনে ৷  
ঝড়ু-ঠাকুর কহে তাঁরে মধুর বচনে ॥ ১৭ ॥

iṣṭagoṣṭhī kata-kṣaṇa kari’ tām̐ra sane  
friendly talk  for some time  having made  with him  
jhaḍu-ṭhākura kahe tām̐re madhura vacane  
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  says  to him  in sweet  words

(17) After talking with him a while, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura said to him sweetly:

আমি — নীচজাতি, তুমি, — অতিথি সর্ব্বোত্তম ৷  
কোন্‌প্রকারে করিমু তোমার সেবন ? ॥ ১৮ ॥

āmi — nīca-jāti, tumi, — atithi sarvottama  
I  low  born  you  guest  the most excellent  
kon-prakāre karimu tomāra sevana?  
in what  way  shall I do  you  service

(18) “I am of low birth, and you are a most honoured guest. How shall I serve you?

আজ্ঞা দেহ’, — ব্রাহ্মণ-ঘরে অন্ন লঞা দিয়ে ৷  
তাঁহা তুমি প্রসাদ পাও, তবে আমি জীয়ে ॥ ১৯ ॥

ājñā deha’, — brāhmaṇa-ghare anna lañā diye  
permission  give  a brāhmaṇa’s  to the house  provisions  taking  I will give  
tām̐hā tumi prasāda pāo, tabe āmi jīye  
there  you  prasāda  take  then  I  shall live

(19) Give me leave to take provisions to a brāhmaṇa’s house. There you can take *prasāda*, and that will give me life.”

কালিদাস কহে, — ঠাকুর, কৃপা কর মোরে ৷  
তোমার দর্শনে আইনু মুই পতিত পামরে ॥ ২০ ॥

kālidāsa kahe, — ṭhākura, kṛpā kara more  
Kālidāsa  says  Ṭhākura  mercy  show  to me  
tomāra darśane āinu mui patita pāmare  
your  for the sight  have come  I  fallen  sinner

(20) Kālidāsa said, “Ṭhākura, be merciful to me! I, a fallen sinner, have come to see you.

পবিত্র হইনু মুই পাইনু দরশন ৷  
কৃতার্থ হইনু, মোর সফল জীবন ॥ ২১ ॥

pavitra hainu mui pāinu daraśana  
pure  have become  I  have gained  the sight  
kṛtārtha hainu, mora saphala jīvana  
fulfilled  I have become  my  successful  life

(21) By seeing you I have become pure. My purpose is fulfilled, and my life has borne its fruit.

এক বাঞ্ছা হয়, — যদি কৃপা করি’ কর ৷  
পাদরজ দেহ’, পাদ মোর মাথে ধর ॥ ২২ ॥

eka vāñchā haya, — yadi kṛpā kari’ kara  
one  desire  there is  if  mercy  showing  you do  
pāda-raja deha’, pāda mora māthe dhara  
of the feet  the dust  give  your foot  my  on the head  place

(22) I have one desire, if you will be merciful: give me the dust of your feet, and place your foot on my head.”

ঠাকুর কহে, — ঐছে বাত্ কহিতে না যুয়ায় ৷  
আমি — নীচজাতি, তুমি — সুসজ্জন রায় ॥ ২৩ ॥

ṭhākura kahe, — aiche bāt kahite nā yuyāya  
the Ṭhākura  says  such  words  to speak  not  is fitting  
āmi — nīca-jāti, tumi — susajjana rāya  
I  low  born  you  a noble  gentleman

(23) The Ṭhākura said, “It is not fitting to say such things. I am of low birth, and you are a noble gentleman.”

তবে কালিদাস শ্লোক পড়ি’ শুনাইল ৷  
শুনি’ ঝড়ু-ঠাকুরের বড় সুখ হইল ॥ ২৪ ॥

tabe kālidāsa śloka paḍi’ śunāila  
then  Kālidāsa  verses  reciting  made him hear  
śuni’ jhaḍu-ṭhākurera baḍa sukha haila  
hearing  Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura’s  great  joy  was

(24) Then Kālidāsa recited verses for him, and hearing them, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura was greatly pleased.

ন মেঽভক্তশ্চতুর্বেদী মদ্ভক্তঃ শ্বপচঃ প্রিয়ঃ ৷  
তস্মৈ দেয়ং ততো গ্রাহ্যং স চ পূজ্যো যথা হ্যহম্ ॥ ২৫ ॥

na me ’bhaktaś catur-vedī  
not  to Me  a nondevotee  a knower of the four Vedas  
mad-bhaktaḥ śva-pacaḥ priyaḥ  
My devotee  a dog-eater  dear  
tasmai deyaṁ tato grāhyaṁ  
to him  should be given  from him  should be accepted  
sa ca pūjyo yathā hy aham  
he  and  is to be worshipped  as  indeed  I

(25) “Even a knower of the four Vedas is not dear to Me if he is not devoted to Me, but My devotee is dear to Me even if he was born a dog-eater. One should give to him and accept from him; he is to be worshipped just as I am.” (Itihāsa-samuccaya, quoted in Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 10.91)[^5]

বিপ্রাদ্দ্বিষড়্গুণযুতাদরবিন্দনাভ-  
পাদারবিন্দবিমুখাৎ শ্বপচং বরিষ্ঠম্ ৷  
মন্যে তদর্পিতমনোবচনেহিতার্থ-  
প্রাণং পুনাতি স কুলং ন তু ভূরিমানঃ ॥ ২৬ ॥

viprād dvi-ṣaḍ-guṇa-yutād aravinda-nābha-  
than a brāhmaṇa  twelve  qualities  endowed with  of the lotus-naveled Lord  
pādāravinda-vimukhāt śva-pacaṁ variṣṭham  
from the lotus feet  averse  a dog-eater  better  
manye tad-arpita-mano-vacanehitārtha-  
I consider  to Him offered  mind  words  deeds  wealth  
prāṇaṁ punāti sa kulaṁ na tu bhūri-mānaḥ  
and life  purifies  he  his family  not  but  one full of pride

(26) “I consider a dog-eater whose mind, words, deeds, wealth and very life are offered to the Lord of the lotus navel better than a brāhmaṇa endowed with the twelve qualities who has turned away from His lotus feet. Such a devotee purifies his whole family; the brāhmaṇa swollen with pride cannot.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.10)[^6]

অহো বত শ্বপচোঽতো গরীয়ান্  
যজ্জিহ্বাগ্রে বর্ত্ততে নাম তুভ্যম্ ৷  
তেপুস্তপস্তে জুহুবুঃ সস্নুরার্য্যা  
ব্রহ্মানূচুর্নাম গৃণন্তি যে তে ॥ ২৭ ॥

aho bata śva-paco ’to garīyān  
oh  how wonderful  a dog-eater  therefore  most worthy  
yaj-jihvāgre vartate nāma tubhyam  
on the tip of whose tongue  is present  the name  Your  
tepus tapas te juhuvuḥ sasnur āryā  
have performed  austerities  they  have offered sacrifice  have bathed (in all holy waters)  noble ones  
brahmānūcur nāma gṛṇanti ye te  
have recited the Vedas  the name  utter  who  Your

(27) “Oh, how wonderful! Even a dog-eater is worthy of the highest honour if Your name is on the tip of his tongue. Those who utter Your name have already performed austerities and offered sacrifices; they have bathed in all the holy waters, they are truly noble, and they have studied the Vedas.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.33.7)[^7]

শুনি’ ঠাকুর কহে, — শাস্ত্র এই সত্য হয় ৷  
সেই নীচ নহে, — যাঁতে কৃষ্ণভক্তি কয় ॥ ২৮ ॥

śuni’ ṭhākura kahe, — śāstra ei satya haya  
hearing  the Ṭhākura  says  scripture  this  true  is  
sei nīca nahe, — yām̐te kṛṣṇa-bhakti kaya  
he  low  is not  in whom  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is spoken of

(28) Hearing this, the Ṭhākura said, “What scripture says is true: one in whom there is devotion to Kṛṣṇa is not low.[^8][^9]

আমি — নীচজাতি, আমার নাহি কৃষ্ণ ভক্তি ৷  
অন্য ঐছে হয়, আমায় নাহি ঐছে শক্তি ॥ ২৯ ॥

āmi — nīca-jāti, āmāra nāhi kṛṣṇa-bhakti  
I  low  born  my  is no  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  
anya aiche haya, āmāya nāhi aiche śakti  
others  such  are  in me  is no  such  power

(29) But I am of low birth, and I have no devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Others may be so blessed, but I have no such power.”[^10]

তাঁরে নমস্করি’ কালিদাস বিদায় মাগিলা ৷  
ঝড়ু-ঠাকুর তবে তাঁর অনুব্রজি’ আইলা ॥ ৩০ ॥

tām̐re namaskari’ kālidāsa vidāya māgilā  
to him  bowing  Kālidāsa  leave  asked  
jhaḍu-ṭhākura tabe tām̐ra anuvraji’ āilā  
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  then  him  following  went

(30) Bowing to him, Kālidāsa asked leave to go, and Jhaḍu Ṭhākura walked after him to see him off.

তাঁরে বিদায় দিয়া ঠাকুর যদি ঘরে আইল ৷  
তাঁর চরণ-চিহ্ন যেই ঠাঞি পড়িল ॥ ৩১ ॥

tām̐re vidāya diyā ṭhākura yadi ghare āila  
him  leave  giving  the Ṭhākura  when  home  came  
tām̐ra caraṇa-cihna yei ṭhāñi paḍila  
his  feet’s  marks  where  fell

(31) When the Ṭhākura had seen him off and returned home, Kālidāsa went to the spot where Jhaḍu’s footprints lay.

সেই ধূলি লঞা কালিদাস সর্ব্বাঙ্গে লেপিলা ৷  
তাঁর নিকট একস্থানে লুকাঞা রহিলা ॥ ৩২ ॥

sei dhūli lañā kālidāsa sarvāṅge lepilā  
that  dust  taking  Kālidāsa  all over his body  smeared  
tām̐ra nikaṭa eka-sthāne lukāñā rahilā  
him  near  in one place  hiding  stayed

(32) Kālidāsa took that dust and smeared it all over his body, and then he hid near Jhaḍu’s house.

ঝড়ু-ঠাকুর ঘর যাই’ দেখি’ আম্রফল ৷  
মানসেই কৃষ্ণচন্দ্রে অর্পিলা সকল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

jhaḍu-ṭhākura ghara yāi’ dekhi’ āmra-phala  
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  home  going  seeing  the mangoes  
mānasei kṛṣṇa-candre arpilā sakala  
in his mind alone  to Kṛṣṇacandra  offered  all

(33) On reaching home Jhaḍu Ṭhākura saw the mangoes and offered them all to Kṛṣṇacandra within his mind.[^11]

কলার পাটুয়া-খোলা হৈতে আম্র নিকাশিয়া ৷  
তাঁর পত্নী তাঁরে দেন, খায়েন চূষিয়া ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kalāra pāṭuyā-kholā haite āmra nikāśiyā  
banana  leaves  bark  from  the mangoes  taking out  
tām̐ra patnī tām̐re dena, khāyena cūṣiyā  
his  wife  to him  gives  he eats  sucking

(34) His wife took the mangoes out of their wrappings of banana leaf and bark and handed them to him, and he ate them, sucking out the juice.[^12]

চূষি’ চূষি’ চোষা আঁঠি ফেলিলা পাটুয়াতে ৷  
তাঁরে খাওয়াঞা তাঁর পত্নী খায় পশ্চাতে ॥ ৩৫ ॥

cūṣi’ cūṣi’ coṣā ām̐ṭhi phelilā pāṭuyāte  
sucking  sucking  the sucked  stones  threw  into the wrappings  
tām̐re khāoyāñā tām̐ra patnī khāya paścāte  
him  having fed  his  wife  eats  afterwards

(35) When he had sucked the stones, he threw them into the wrappings; and when his wife had fed him, she ate after him.[^13]

আঁঠি-চোষা সেই পাটুয়া-খোলাতে ভরিয়া ৷  
বাহিরে উচ্ছিষ্ট-গর্ত্তে ফেলাইলা লঞা ॥ ৩৬ ॥

ām̐ṭhi-coṣā sei pāṭuyā-kholāte bhariyā  
stones  sucked  those  wrappings  filling  
bāhire ucchiṣṭa-garte phelāilā lañā  
outside  into the pit for remnants  threw  taking

(36) She filled the wrappings with the sucked stones, carried them outside and threw them into the pit for remnants.

সেই খোলা, আঁঠি, চোকলা চূষে কালিদাস ৷  
চূষিতে চূষিতে হয় প্রেমেতে উল্লাস ॥ ৩৭ ॥

sei kholā, ām̐ṭhi, cokalā cūṣe kālidāsa  
those  wrappings  stones  skins  sucks  Kālidāsa  
cūṣite cūṣite haya premete ullāsa  
sucking  sucking  comes  in love  jubilation

(37) Kālidāsa sucked those wrappings, stones and skins, and as he went on, he grew ever more jubilant with divine love.[^14]

এইমত যত বৈষ্ণব বৈসে গৌড়দেশে ৷  
কালিদাস ঐছে সবার নিলা অবশেষে ॥ ৩৮ ॥

ei-mata yata vaiṣṇava vaise gauḍa-deśe  
in this way  as many  Vaiṣṇavas  live  in the land of Gauḍa  
kālidāsa aiche sabāra nilā avaśeṣe  
Kālidāsa  thus  of all  took  the remnants

(38) In this way Kālidāsa honoured the remnants of all the Vaiṣṇavas who lived in the land of Gauḍa.

[^2]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in his worldly dealings he used the name of Kṛṣṇa as a conventional sign (*saṅketa*).

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: if a soul full of *anarthas* does not follow Kālidāsa’s firm devotion to the holy name, but for the pleasure of the senses imitates this outward play of his and grows addicted to dice, its bondage to Kali will only increase its bent for sin (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.17.38–41). And its outward imitation of chanting, which prompts it to sin in reliance on the power of the name, will end only in offence to the name (*nāmāparādha*).

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the bhūṅimālis are a caste like the haḍḍis. *Anubhāṣya*: the homes of Kālidāsa and Jhaḍu Ṭhākura were in the village of Bhedo (Bhaduyā), about three miles south of Kṛṣṇapura, the birthplace of Śrīla Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī.

[^5]: The same verse as Madhya 19.50 and 20.58; translation as there. Heading in the edition here: “Words of the Lord in the *Itihāsa-samuccaya*” (at Madhya 19.50: *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa* 10.91, words of the *Itihāsa-samuccaya*). The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Madhya 19.50.

[^6]: The same verse as Antya 4.69 and Madhya 20.59; translation as there. The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Madhya 20.59.

[^7]: The same verse as Madhya 11.192 and 19.72; translation as there. The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Madhya 11.192.

[^8]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *śāstra ei satya haya / sei nīca nahe, — yāṅte kṛṣṇa-bhakti kaya*. Other editions read *śāstra ei satya kaya / sei śreṣṭha, aiche yāṅte kṛṣṇa-bhakti haya*, “the scriptures speak this truth: the best is one in whom there is devotion to Kṛṣṇa.”

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Mahābhārata*, the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, the *Padma-purāṇa* and many other scriptures make it clear that transcendental brahminhood belongs eternally to a Vaiṣṇava. Therefore one who has gained devotion to Kṛṣṇa, even if born in a low family, can no longer be of low caste.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: the words “I have no devotion” show only Jhaḍu Ṭhākura’s magnanimity, worthy of a Vaiṣṇava, and his freedom from pride. Great devotees do not identify with the body, and by nature they speak with the humility of pure devotees: “All the devotees of Kṛṣṇa stand high; I alone have no devotion and am born in a low family.”

[^11]: Heading in the edition: Jhaḍu Ṭhākura, the guru of all brāhmaṇas, worships within his mind the Deity revealed in the mind, and then eats the mangoes as Kṛṣṇa’s remnants.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pāṭuyā-kholā* — leaves and bark; *nikāśiyā* — taking out.

[^13]: Heading in the edition: the wife of a Vaiṣṇava honours the remnants of her Vaiṣṇava husband’s meal.

[^14]: Heading in the edition: the most fortunate Kālidāsa, in great bliss, honours the remnants of a Vaiṣṇava’s meal, seeing them as transcendental.
