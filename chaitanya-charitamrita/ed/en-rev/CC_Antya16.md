“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 16  
**Grace to Kālidāsa, and the Ravings of Madness in Separation**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the Anubhāṣya, 2026)

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যং কৃষ্ণভাবামৃতং হি যঃ ৷  
আস্বাদ্যাস্বাদয়ন্ ভক্তান্ প্রেমদীক্ষামশিক্ষয়ৎ ॥ ১ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanyaṁ  
I bow down  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
kṛṣṇa-bhāvāmṛtaṁ hi yaḥ  
of Kṛṣṇa  of feelings  the nectar  indeed  who  
āsvādyāsvādayan bhaktān  
having tasted  making taste  the devotees  
prema-dīkṣām aśikṣayat  
of prema  the initiation  taught

(1) I bow down to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who Himself tasted the nectar of feelings for Kṛṣṇa and gave it to His devotees to taste; so He taught them the path of divine love.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এইমত মহাপ্রভু রহেন নীলাচলে ৷  
ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে সদা প্রেম-বিহ্বলে ॥ ৩ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu rahena nīlācale  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  stays  in Nīlācala  
bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge sadā prema-vihvale  
the devotees  with  always  by love  overwhelmed

(3) In this way Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala with His devotees, always overwhelmed by love.

বর্ষান্তরে আইলা সব গৌড়ের ভক্তগণ ৷  
পূর্ব্ববৎ আসি’ কৈল প্রভুর মিলন ॥ ৪ ॥

varṣāntare āilā saba gauḍera bhakta-gaṇa  
the next year  came  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees  
pūrvavat āsi’ kaila prabhura milana  
as before  coming  made  with the Lord  meeting

(4) The next year all the devotees of Gauḍa came again and, as before, met the Lord.

তাঁ-সবার সঙ্গে আইল কালিদাস নাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম বিনা তেঁহো নাহি জানে আন ॥ ৫ ॥

tām̐-sabāra saṅge āila kālidāsa nāma  
them all  with  came  Kālidāsa  by name  
kṛṣṇa-nāma vinā tem̐ho nāhi jāne āna  
Kṛṣṇa’s  name  except  he  not  knew  anything else

(5) With them came a devotee named Kālidāsa, who knew nothing but the name of Kṛṣṇa.

মহাভাগবত তেঁহো সরল উদার ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম-‘সঙ্কেতে’ চালায় ব্যবহার ॥ ৬ ॥

mahā-bhāgavata tem̐ho sarala udāra  
a great  devotee  he  simple  generous  
kṛṣṇa-nāma-‘saṅkete’ cālāya vyavahāra  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  name  by the signs  conducted  his dealings

(6) He was a great devotee, simple and generous, and even in his everyday dealings the name of Kṛṣṇa served him as a sign.[^2]

কৌতুকেতে তেঁহো যদি পাশক খেলায় ৷  
‘হরেকৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরেকৃষ্ণ’ করি’ পাশক চালায় ॥ ৭ ॥

kautukete tem̐ho yadi pāśaka khelāya  
in fun  he  if  at dice  played  
‘hare-kṛṣṇa’ ‘hare-kṛṣṇa’ kari’ pāśaka cālāya  
Hare Kṛṣṇa  Hare Kṛṣṇa  saying  the dice  threw

(7) When he played at dice for fun, he would cast them saying, “Hare Kṛṣṇa! Hare Kṛṣṇa!”[^3]

রঘুনাথ-দাসের তেঁহো হয় জ্ঞাতি-খুড়া ৷  
বৈষ্ণবের উচ্ছিষ্ট খাইতে তেঁহো হৈল বুড়া ॥ ৮ ॥

raghunātha-dāsera tem̐ho haya jñāti-khuḍā  
of Raghunātha  dāsa  he  was  a kinsman  uncle  
vaiṣṇavera ucchiṣṭa khāite tem̐ho haila buḍā  
of Vaiṣṇavas  the remnants  eating  he  became  old

(8) He was Raghunātha dāsa’s uncle within the clan, and he had grown old honouring the remnants of Vaiṣṇavas.

গৌড়দেশে হয় যত বৈষ্ণবের গণ ৷  
সবার উচ্ছিষ্ট তেঁহো করিল ভোজন ॥ ৯ ॥

gauḍa-deśe haya yata vaiṣṇavera gaṇa  
in the land of Gauḍa  are  as many  of Vaiṣṇavas  as  
sabāra ucchiṣṭa tem̐ho karila bhojana  
of all  the remnants  he  did  eat

(9) There was no Vaiṣṇava in Gauḍa whose remnants he had not taken.

ব্রাহ্মণ-বৈষ্ণব যত — ছোট, বড় হয় ৷  
উত্তম-বস্তু ভেট লঞা তাঁর ঠাঞি যায় ॥ ১০ ॥

brāhmaṇa-vaiṣṇava yata — choṭa, baḍa haya  
brāhmaṇa  Vaiṣṇavas  as many as  small  great  are  
uttama-vastu bheṭa lañā tām̐ra ṭhāñi yāya  
the best  things  as gifts  taking  to them  goes

(10) He would go to every Vaiṣṇava of brāhmaṇa birth, great and small, bringing the finest things as gifts.

তাঁর ঠাঞি শেষ-পাত্র লয়েন মাগিয়া ৷  
কাঁহা না পায়, তবে রহে লুকাঞা ॥ ১১ ॥

tām̐ra ṭhāñi śeṣa-pātra layena māgiyā  
from them  the plates of remnants  takes  begging  
kām̐hā nā pāya, tabe rahe lukāñā  
wherever  not  gets  then  stays  hiding

(11) He would beg them for the plates with their remnants, and where he was refused, he would hide nearby.

ভোজন করিলে পাত্র ফেলাঞা যায় ৷  
লুকাঞা সেই পাত্র আনি’ চাটি’ খায় ॥ ১২ ॥

bhojana karile pātra phelāñā yāya  
eating  having done  the plate  throwing away  goes  
lukāñā sei pātra āni’ cāṭi’ khāya  
secretly  that  plate  bringing  licking  eats

(12) When a Vaiṣṇava had eaten and thrown his plate away, Kālidāsa would secretly take it up and lick it clean.

শূদ্র-বৈষ্ণবের ঘরে যায় ভেট লঞা ৷  
এইমত তাঁর উচ্ছিষ্ট খায় লুকাঞা ॥ ১৩ ॥

śūdra-vaiṣṇavera ghare yāya bheṭa lañā  
of śūdra  Vaiṣṇavas  to the homes  goes  gifts  taking  
ei-mata tām̐ra ucchiṣṭa khāya lukāñā  
in this way  their  remnants  eats  secretly

(13) He went with gifts to the homes of Vaiṣṇavas of śūdra birth as well, and in the same way secretly took their remnants.

ভূঁইমালি-জাতি, ‘বৈষ্ণব’ — ‘ঝড়ু’ তাঁর নাম ৷  
আম্রফল লঞা তেঁহো গেলা তাঁর স্থান ॥ ১৪ ॥

bhūm̐imāli-jāti, ‘vaiṣṇava’ — ‘jhaḍu’ tām̐ra nāma  
bhūṅimāli  by caste  a Vaiṣṇava  Jhaḍu  his  name  
āmra-phala lañā tem̐ho gelā tām̐ra sthāna  
mangoes  taking  he  went  to his  place

(14) There was a Vaiṣṇava named Jhaḍu, of the *bhūṅimāli* caste. Kālidāsa went to his home, taking mangoes with him.[^4]

আম্র ভেট দিয়া তাঁর চরণ বন্দিলা ৷  
তাঁর পত্নীরে তবে নমস্কার কৈলা ॥ ১৫ ॥

āmra bheṭa diyā tām̐ra caraṇa vandilā  
the mangoes  as a gift  giving  his  feet  he honoured  
tām̐ra patnīre tabe namaskāra kailā  
his  wife  then  obeisance  made

(15) He presented the mangoes, bowed at his feet, and then paid his respects to his wife.

পত্নী-সহিত তেঁহো আছেন বসিয়া ৷  
বহু সম্মান কৈলা কালিদাসেরে দেখিয়া ॥ ১৬ ॥

patnī-sahita tem̐ho āchena vasiyā  
his wife  with  he  was  seated  
bahu sammāna kailā kālidāsere dekhiyā  
much  honour  showed  Kālidāsa  seeing

(16) Jhaḍu Ṭhākura was sitting with his wife; seeing Kālidāsa, he received him with great respect.

ইষ্টগোষ্ঠী কতক্ষণ করি’ তাঁর সনে ৷  
ঝড়ু-ঠাকুর কহে তাঁরে মধুর বচনে ॥ ১৭ ॥

iṣṭagoṣṭhī kata-kṣaṇa kari’ tām̐ra sane  
friendly talk  for some time  having made  with him  
jhaḍu-ṭhākura kahe tām̐re madhura vacane  
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  says  to him  in sweet  words

(17) After talking with him a while, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura said to him sweetly:

আমি — নীচজাতি, তুমি, — অতিথি সর্ব্বোত্তম ৷  
কোন্‌প্রকারে করিমু তোমার সেবন ? ॥ ১৮ ॥

āmi — nīca-jāti, tumi, — atithi sarvottama  
I  low  born  you  guest  the most excellent  
kon-prakāre karimu tomāra sevana?  
in what  way  shall I do  you  service

(18) “I am of low birth, and you are a most honoured guest. How shall I serve you?

আজ্ঞা দেহ’, — ব্রাহ্মণ-ঘরে অন্ন লঞা দিয়ে ৷  
তাঁহা তুমি প্রসাদ পাও, তবে আমি জীয়ে ॥ ১৯ ॥

ājñā deha’, — brāhmaṇa-ghare anna lañā diye  
permission  give  a brāhmaṇa’s  to the house  provisions  taking  I will give  
tām̐hā tumi prasāda pāo, tabe āmi jīye  
there  you  prasāda  take  then  I  shall live

(19) Give me leave to take provisions to a brāhmaṇa’s house. There you can take *prasāda*, and that will give me life.”

কালিদাস কহে, — ঠাকুর, কৃপা কর মোরে ৷  
তোমার দর্শনে আইনু মুই পতিত পামরে ॥ ২০ ॥

kālidāsa kahe, — ṭhākura, kṛpā kara more  
Kālidāsa  says  Ṭhākura  mercy  show  to me  
tomāra darśane āinu mui patita pāmare  
your  for the sight  have come  I  fallen  sinner

(20) Kālidāsa said, “Ṭhākura, be merciful to me! I, a fallen sinner, have come to see you.

পবিত্র হইনু মুই পাইনু দরশন ৷  
কৃতার্থ হইনু, মোর সফল জীবন ॥ ২১ ॥

pavitra hainu mui pāinu daraśana  
pure  have become  I  have gained  the sight  
kṛtārtha hainu, mora saphala jīvana  
fulfilled  I have become  my  successful  life

(21) By seeing you I have become pure. My purpose is fulfilled, and my life has borne its fruit.

এক বাঞ্ছা হয়, — যদি কৃপা করি’ কর ৷  
পাদরজ দেহ’, পাদ মোর মাথে ধর ॥ ২২ ॥

eka vāñchā haya, — yadi kṛpā kari’ kara  
one  desire  there is  if  mercy  showing  you do  
pāda-raja deha’, pāda mora māthe dhara  
of the feet  the dust  give  your foot  my  on the head  place

(22) I have one desire, if you will be merciful: give me the dust of your feet, and place your foot on my head.”

ঠাকুর কহে, — ঐছে বাত্ কহিতে না যুয়ায় ৷  
আমি — নীচজাতি, তুমি — সুসজ্জন রায় ॥ ২৩ ॥

ṭhākura kahe, — aiche bāt kahite nā yuyāya  
the Ṭhākura  says  such  words  to speak  not  is fitting  
āmi — nīca-jāti, tumi — susajjana rāya  
I  low  born  you  a noble  gentleman

(23) The Ṭhākura said, “It is not fitting to say such things. I am of low birth, and you are a noble gentleman.”

তবে কালিদাস শ্লোক পড়ি’ শুনাইল ৷  
শুনি’ ঝড়ু-ঠাকুরের বড় সুখ হইল ॥ ২৪ ॥

tabe kālidāsa śloka paḍi’ śunāila  
then  Kālidāsa  verses  reciting  made him hear  
śuni’ jhaḍu-ṭhākurera baḍa sukha haila  
hearing  Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura’s  great  joy  was

(24) Then Kālidāsa recited verses for him, and hearing them, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura was greatly pleased.

ন মেঽভক্তশ্চতুর্বেদী মদ্ভক্তঃ শ্বপচঃ প্রিয়ঃ ৷  
তস্মৈ দেয়ং ততো গ্রাহ্যং স চ পূজ্যো যথা হ্যহম্ ॥ ২৫ ॥

na me ’bhaktaś catur-vedī  
not  to Me  a nondevotee  a knower of the four Vedas  
mad-bhaktaḥ śva-pacaḥ priyaḥ  
My devotee  a dog-eater  dear  
tasmai deyaṁ tato grāhyaṁ  
to him  should be given  from him  should be accepted  
sa ca pūjyo yathā hy aham  
he  and  is to be worshipped  as  indeed  I

(25) “Even a knower of the four Vedas is not dear to Me if he is not devoted to Me, but My devotee is dear to Me even if he was born a dog-eater. One should give to him and accept from him; he is to be worshipped just as I am.” (Itihāsa-samuccaya, quoted in Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 10.91)[^5]

বিপ্রাদ্দ্বিষড়্গুণযুতাদরবিন্দনাভ-  
পাদারবিন্দবিমুখাৎ শ্বপচং বরিষ্ঠম্ ৷  
মন্যে তদর্পিতমনোবচনেহিতার্থ-  
প্রাণং পুনাতি স কুলং ন তু ভূরিমানঃ ॥ ২৬ ॥

viprād dvi-ṣaḍ-guṇa-yutād aravinda-nābha-  
than a brāhmaṇa  twelve  qualities  endowed with  of the lotus-naveled Lord  
pādāravinda-vimukhāt śva-pacaṁ variṣṭham  
from the lotus feet  averse  a dog-eater  better  
manye tad-arpita-mano-vacanehitārtha-  
I consider  to Him offered  mind  words  deeds  wealth  
prāṇaṁ punāti sa kulaṁ na tu bhūri-mānaḥ  
and life  purifies  he  his family  not  but  one full of pride

(26) “I consider a dog-eater whose mind, words, deeds, wealth and very life are offered to the Lord of the lotus navel better than a brāhmaṇa endowed with the twelve qualities who has turned away from His lotus feet. Such a devotee purifies his whole family; the brāhmaṇa swollen with pride cannot.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.10)[^6]

অহো বত শ্বপচোঽতো গরীয়ান্  
যজ্জিহ্বাগ্রে বর্ত্ততে নাম তুভ্যম্ ৷  
তেপুস্তপস্তে জুহুবুঃ সস্নুরার্য্যা  
ব্রহ্মানূচুর্নাম গৃণন্তি যে তে ॥ ২৭ ॥

aho bata śva-paco ’to garīyān  
oh  how wonderful  a dog-eater  therefore  most worthy  
yaj-jihvāgre vartate nāma tubhyam  
on the tip of whose tongue  is present  the name  Your  
tepus tapas te juhuvuḥ sasnur āryā  
have performed  austerities  they  have offered sacrifice  have bathed (in all holy waters)  noble ones  
brahmānūcur nāma gṛṇanti ye te  
have recited the Vedas  the name  utter  who  Your

(27) “Oh, how wonderful! Even a dog-eater is worthy of the highest honour if Your name is on the tip of his tongue. Those who utter Your name have already performed austerities and offered sacrifices; they have bathed in all the holy waters, they are truly noble, and they have studied the Vedas.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.33.7)[^7]

শুনি’ ঠাকুর কহে, — শাস্ত্র এই সত্য হয় ৷  
সেই নীচ নহে, — যাঁতে কৃষ্ণভক্তি কয় ॥ ২৮ ॥

śuni’ ṭhākura kahe, — śāstra ei satya haya  
hearing  the Ṭhākura  says  scripture  this  true  is  
sei nīca nahe, — yām̐te kṛṣṇa-bhakti kaya  
he  low  is not  in whom  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is spoken of

(28) Hearing this, the Ṭhākura said, “What scripture says is true: one in whom there is devotion to Kṛṣṇa is not low.[^8][^9]

আমি — নীচজাতি, আমার নাহি কৃষ্ণ ভক্তি ৷  
অন্য ঐছে হয়, আমায় নাহি ঐছে শক্তি ॥ ২৯ ॥

āmi — nīca-jāti, āmāra nāhi kṛṣṇa-bhakti  
I  low  born  my  is no  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  
anya aiche haya, āmāya nāhi aiche śakti  
others  such  are  in me  is no  such  power

(29) But I am of low birth, and I have no devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Others may be so blessed, but I have no such power.”[^10]

তাঁরে নমস্করি’ কালিদাস বিদায় মাগিলা ৷  
ঝড়ু-ঠাকুর তবে তাঁর অনুব্রজি’ আইলা ॥ ৩০ ॥

tām̐re namaskari’ kālidāsa vidāya māgilā  
to him  bowing  Kālidāsa  leave  asked  
jhaḍu-ṭhākura tabe tām̐ra anuvraji’ āilā  
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  then  him  following  went

(30) Bowing to him, Kālidāsa asked leave to go, and Jhaḍu Ṭhākura walked after him to see him off.

তাঁরে বিদায় দিয়া ঠাকুর যদি ঘরে আইল ৷  
তাঁর চরণ-চিহ্ন যেই ঠাঞি পড়িল ॥ ৩১ ॥

tām̐re vidāya diyā ṭhākura yadi ghare āila  
him  leave  giving  the Ṭhākura  when  home  came  
tām̐ra caraṇa-cihna yei ṭhāñi paḍila  
his  feet’s  marks  where  fell

(31) When the Ṭhākura had seen him off and returned home, Kālidāsa went to the spot where Jhaḍu’s footprints lay.

সেই ধূলি লঞা কালিদাস সর্ব্বাঙ্গে লেপিলা ৷  
তাঁর নিকট একস্থানে লুকাঞা রহিলা ॥ ৩২ ॥

sei dhūli lañā kālidāsa sarvāṅge lepilā  
that  dust  taking  Kālidāsa  all over his body  smeared  
tām̐ra nikaṭa eka-sthāne lukāñā rahilā  
him  near  in one place  hiding  stayed

(32) He gathered up that dust, smeared it all over his body, and then hid near Jhaḍu’s house.

ঝড়ু-ঠাকুর ঘর যাই’ দেখি’ আম্রফল ৷  
মানসেই কৃষ্ণচন্দ্রে অর্পিলা সকল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

jhaḍu-ṭhākura ghara yāi’ dekhi’ āmra-phala  
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  home  going  seeing  the mangoes  
mānasei kṛṣṇa-candre arpilā sakala  
in his mind alone  to Kṛṣṇacandra  offered  all

(33) Entering the house, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura saw the mangoes and offered them all to Kṛṣṇacandra within his mind.[^11]

কলার পাটুয়া-খোলা হৈতে আম্র নিকাশিয়া ৷  
তাঁর পত্নী তাঁরে দেন, খায়েন চূষিয়া ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kalāra pāṭuyā-kholā haite āmra nikāśiyā  
banana  leaves  bark  from  the mangoes  taking out  
tām̐ra patnī tām̐re dena, khāyena cūṣiyā  
his  wife  to him  gives  he eats  sucking

(34) His wife took the mangoes out of their wrappings of banana leaf and bark and handed them to him, and he ate them, sucking out the juice.[^12]

চূষি’ চূষি’ চোষা আঁঠি ফেলিলা পাটুয়াতে ৷  
তাঁরে খাওয়াঞা তাঁর পত্নী খায় পশ্চাতে ॥ ৩৫ ॥

cūṣi’ cūṣi’ coṣā ām̐ṭhi phelilā pāṭuyāte  
sucking  sucking  the sucked  stones  threw  into the wrappings  
tām̐re khāoyāñā tām̐ra patnī khāya paścāte  
him  having fed  his  wife  eats  afterwards

(35) He sucked the stones and threw them into the wrappings; and when his wife had fed him, she ate after him.[^13]

আঁঠি-চোষা সেই পাটুয়া-খোলাতে ভরিয়া ৷  
বাহিরে উচ্ছিষ্ট-গর্ত্তে ফেলাইলা লঞা ॥ ৩৬ ॥

ām̐ṭhi-coṣā sei pāṭuyā-kholāte bhariyā  
stones  sucked  those  wrappings  filling  
bāhire ucchiṣṭa-garte phelāilā lañā  
outside  into the pit for remnants  threw  taking

(36) She filled the wrappings with the sucked stones, carried them outside and threw them into the pit for remnants.

সেই খোলা, আঁঠি, চোকলা চূষে কালিদাস ৷  
চূষিতে চূষিতে হয় প্রেমেতে উল্লাস ॥ ৩৭ ॥

sei kholā, ām̐ṭhi, cokalā cūṣe kālidāsa  
those  wrappings  stones  skins  sucks  Kālidāsa  
cūṣite cūṣite haya premete ullāsa  
sucking  sucking  comes  in love  jubilation

(37) Kālidāsa sucked those wrappings, stones and skins, and as he went on, he grew ever more jubilant with divine love.[^14]

এইমত যত বৈষ্ণব বৈসে গৌড়দেশে ৷  
কালিদাস ঐছে সবার নিলা অবশেষে ॥ ৩৮ ॥

ei-mata yata vaiṣṇava vaise gauḍa-deśe  
in this way  as many  Vaiṣṇavas  live  in the land of Gauḍa  
kālidāsa aiche sabāra nilā avaśeṣe  
Kālidāsa  thus  of all  took  the remnants

(38) In this way Kālidāsa honoured the remnants of all the Vaiṣṇavas who lived in the land of Gauḍa.

সেই কালিদাস যবে নীলাচলে আইলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভু তাঁর উপর মহাকৃপা কৈলা ॥ ৩৯ ॥

sei kālidāsa yabe nīlācale āilā  
that  Kālidāsa  when  to Nīlācala  came  
mahāprabhu tām̐ra upara mahā-kṛpā kailā  
Mahāprabhu  on him  great  mercy  showed

(39) When Kālidāsa came to Nīlācala, Mahāprabhu showed him great mercy.

প্রতিদিন প্রভু যদি যান দরশনে ৷  
জল-করঙ্গ লঞা গোবিন্দ যায় প্রভু-সনে ॥ ৪০ ॥

prati-dina prabhu yadi yāna daraśane  
every  day  the Lord  when  goes  for darśana  
jala-karaṅga lañā govinda yāya prabhu-sane  
water  pot  taking  Govinda  goes  with the Lord

(40) Every day, when the Lord went to see Lord Jagannātha, Govinda accompanied Him with His waterpot.

সিংহদ্বারের উত্তরদিকে কপাটের আড়ে ৷  
বাইশ ‘পহাচ’-তলে আছে এক নিম্ন গাড়ে ॥ ৪১ ॥

siṁha-dvārera uttara-dike kapāṭera āḍe  
of the Siṁha-dvāra  on the north  side  of the gate  behind  
bāiśa ‘pahāca’-tale āche eka nimna gāḍe  
twenty-two  steps  at the foot  is  one  low  pit

(41) On the north side of the Siṁha-dvāra, sheltered behind the gate, at the foot of the twenty-two steps, there was a small pit.[^15]

সেই গাড়ে করেন প্রভু পাদ-প্রক্ষালনে ৷  
তবে করিবারে যায় ঈশ্বর-দরশনে ॥ ৪২ ॥

sei gāḍe karena prabhu pāda-prakṣālane  
in that  pit  does  the Lord  feet  washing  
tabe karibāre yāya īśvara-daraśane  
then  to do  goes  of the Lord  darśana

(42) In that pit the Lord would wash His feet, and then He would go to see Lord Jagannātha.

গোবিন্দেরে মহাপ্রভু কৈরাছে নিয়ম ৷  
‘মোর পাদজল যেন না লয় কোন জন’ ॥ ৪৩ ॥

govindere mahāprabhu kairāche niyama  
for Govinda  Mahāprabhu  has made  a rule  
‘mora pāda-jala yena nā laya kona jana’  
My  feet  water  that  not  takes  any  person

(43) Mahāprabhu had given Govinda a rule: “See that no one takes the water that washes My feet.”[^16]

প্রাণিমাত্র লইতে না পায় সেই জল ৷  
অন্তরঙ্গ ভক্ত লয় করি’ কোন ছল ॥ ৪৪ ॥

prāṇi-mātra laite nā pāya sei jala  
living beings  any  to take  not  can  that  water  
antaraṅga bhakta laya kari’ kona chala  
intimate  devotees  take  making  some  pretext

(44) No living being could take that water; only intimate devotees contrived to get it, on one pretext or another.

একদিন প্রভু তাঁহা পাদ প্রক্ষালিতে ৷  
কালিদাস আসি’ তাঁহা পাতিলেন হাতে ॥ ৪৫ ॥

eka-dina prabhu tām̐hā pāda prakṣālite  
one  day  the Lord  there  feet  to wash  
kālidāsa āsi’ tām̐hā pātilena hāte  
Kālidāsa  coming  there  held out  his hands

(45) One day, as the Lord was washing His feet there, Kālidāsa came and held out his cupped hands.

এক অঞ্জলি, দুই অঞ্জলি, তিন অঞ্জলি পিলা ৷  
তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে নিষেধ করিলা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

eka añjali, dui añjali, tina añjali pilā  
one  palmful  two  palmfuls  three  palmfuls  he drank  
tabe mahāprabhu tām̐re niṣedha karilā  
then  Mahāprabhu  him  prohibition  made

(46) He drank one palmful, two palmfuls, three palmfuls, and then Mahāprabhu stopped him:

অতপর আর না করিহ পুনর্ব্বার ৷  
এতাবতা বাঞ্ছা-পূরণ করিলুঁ তোমার ॥ ৪৭ ॥

atapara āra nā kariha punar-bāra  
from now on  more  not  do  again  
etāvatā vāñchā-pūraṇa karilum̐ tomāra  
thus far  of your desire  the fulfilment  I have made  your

(47) “From now on, do not do this again. With this much I have fulfilled your desire.”

সর্ব্বজ্ঞ-শিরোমণি চৈতন্য ঈশ্বর ৷  
বৈষ্ণবে তাঁহার বিশ্বাস, জানেন অন্তর ॥ ৪৮ ॥

sarvajña-śiromaṇi caitanya īśvara  
of the omniscient  the crest jewel  Caitanya  the Lord  
vaiṣṇave tām̐hāra viśvāsa, jānena antara  
in Vaiṣṇavas  his  faith  knows  the heart

(48) Lord Caitanya, crest jewel of the all-knowing, knew Kālidāsa’s heart and his faith in the Vaiṣṇavas.

সেই গুণ লঞা প্রভু তাঁরে তুষ্ট হইলা ৷  
অন্যের দুর্ল্লভ প্রসাদ তাঁহারে করিলা ॥ ৪৯ ॥

sei guṇa lañā prabhu tām̐re tuṣṭa hailā  
that  quality  on account of  the Lord  with him  pleased  became  
anyera durllabha prasāda tām̐hāre karilā  
to others  rare  mercy  to him  showed

(49) Pleased with him for that quality, the Lord showed him a mercy rarely granted to others.[^17]

বাইশ ‘পহাচ’-পাছে উপর দক্ষিণ-দিকে ৷  
এক নৃসিংহ-মূর্ত্তি আছেন উঠিতে বামভাগে ॥ ৫০ ॥

bāiśa ‘pahāca’-pāche upara dakṣiṇa-dike  
twenty-two  steps  behind  above  on the south  side  
eka nṛsiṁha-mūrti āchena uṭhite vāma-bhāge  
one  Nṛsiṁha  Deity  is present  going up  on the left side

(50) Beyond the twenty-two steps, at the top on the south side, on the left as one goes up, stands a Deity of Lord Nṛsiṁha.

প্রতিদিন তাঁরে প্রভু করেন নমস্কার ৷  
নমস্করি’ এই শ্লোক পড়ে বারবার ॥ ৫১ ॥

prati-dina tām̐re prabhu karena namaskāra  
every  day  to Him  the Lord  makes  obeisance  
namaskari’ ei śloka paḍe bāra-bāra  
bowing  this  verse  recites  again and again

(51) Every day the Lord would bow down to Him and recite this verse again and again:

নমস্তে নরসিংহায় প্রহ্লাদাহ্লাদদায়িনে ৷  
হিরণ্যকশিপোর্ব্বক্ষঃশিলাটঙ্ক-নখালয়ে ॥ ৫২ ॥

namas te nara-siṁhāya  
obeisance  to You  to Narasiṁha  
prahlādāhlāda-dāyine  
to Prahlāda  joy  who gives  
hiraṇyakaśipor vakṣaḥ-  
of Hiraṇyakaśipu  the chest  
śilā-ṭaṅka-nakhālaye  
the stone  chisel  whose nails

(52) “Obeisance to You, Lord Narasiṁha, who give joy to Prahlāda, and whose nails are a chisel on the stone of Hiraṇyakaśipu’s chest.” (Nṛsiṁha-purāṇa)[^18]

ইতো নৃসিংহঃ পরতো নৃসিংহো  
যতো যতো যামি ততো নৃসিংহঃ ৷  
বহির্নৃসিংহো হৃদয়ে নৃসিংহো  
নৃসিংহমাদিং শরণং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

ito nṛsiṁhaḥ parato nṛsiṁho  
here  Nṛsiṁha  there  Nṛsiṁha  
yato yato yāmi tato nṛsiṁhaḥ  
wherever  wherever  I go  there  Nṛsiṁha  
bahir nṛsiṁho hṛdaye nṛsiṁho  
outside  Nṛsiṁha  in the heart  Nṛsiṁha  
nṛsiṁham ādiṁ śaraṇaṁ prapadye  
in Nṛsiṁha  the original  shelter  I take

(53) “Nṛsiṁha is here, Nṛsiṁha is there; wherever I go, there is Nṛsiṁha. Nṛsiṁha is outside, Nṛsiṁha is in the heart. I take shelter of Nṛsiṁha, the original Lord.” (Nṛsiṁha-purāṇa)[^19]

তবে প্রভু করিলা জগন্নাথ দরশন ৷  
ঘরে আসি’ করিলা মধ্যাহ্ন-ভোজন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

tabe prabhu karilā jagannātha daraśana  
then  the Lord  made  of Jagannātha  darśana  
ghare āsi’ karilā madhyāhna-bhojana  
home  coming  made  the midday  meal

(54) Then the Lord had *darśana* of Jagannātha, and on returning home He took His midday meal.

বহির্দ্বারে আছে কালিদাস প্রত্যাশা করিয়া ৷  
গোবিন্দেরে ঠারে প্রভু কহেন জানিয়া ॥ ৫৫ ॥

bahir-dvāre āche kālidāsa pratyāśā kariyā  
at the outer door  is  Kālidāsa  hoping  waiting  
govindere ṭhāre prabhu kahena jāniyā  
to Govinda  by a sign  the Lord  speaks  knowing

(55) Kālidāsa was waiting hopefully at the outer door. Knowing this, the Lord gave Govinda a sign.

প্রভুর ইঙ্গিতে গোবিন্দ সব জানে ৷  
কালিদাসেরে দিল প্রভুর শেষপাত্র-দানে ॥ ৫৬ ॥

prabhura iṅgite govinda saba jāne  
the Lord’s  sign  Govinda  all  understands  
kālidāsere dila prabhura śeṣa-pātra-dāne  
to Kālidāsa  gave  the Lord’s  plate of remnants  as a gift

(56) Govinda understood all the Lord’s signs and gave Kālidāsa the plate with the Lord’s remnants.

বৈষ্ণবের শেষ-ভক্ষণের এতেক মহিমা ৷  
কালিদাসে পাওয়াইল প্রভুর কৃপা-সীমা ॥ ৫৭ ॥

vaiṣṇavera śeṣa-bhakṣaṇera eteka mahimā  
of Vaiṣṇavas  remnants  of eating  such  greatness  
kālidāse pāoyāila prabhura kṛpā-sīmā  
to Kālidāsa  made obtain  the Lord’s  mercy  the limit

(57) Such is the greatness of the remnants of Vaiṣṇavas: by taking them Kālidāsa received the Lord’s mercy in its fullest measure.

তাতে ‘বৈষ্ণবের ঝুটা’ খাও ছাড়ি’ ঘৃণা-লাজ ৷  
যাহা হইতে পাইবা বাঞ্ছিত সব কাজ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

tāte ‘vaiṣṇavera jhuṭā’ khāo chāḍi’ ghṛṇā-lāja  
therefore  Vaiṣṇavas’  leftovers  eat  giving up  disgust  shame  
yāhā haite pāibā vāñchita saba kāja  
which  from  you will obtain  desired  all  aims

(58) Therefore, casting off disgust and shame, take the remnants (*jhuṭā*) of Vaiṣṇavas; through them you will obtain all you desire.

কৃষ্ণের উচ্ছিষ্ট হয় ‘মহাপ্রসাদ’ নাম ৷  
‘ভক্তশেষ’ হৈলে ‘মহা-মহাপ্রসাদাখ্যান’ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

kṛṣṇera ucchiṣṭa haya ‘mahā-prasāda’ nāma  
of Kṛṣṇa  the remnants  are  mahā-prasāda  named  
‘bhakta-śeṣa’ haile ‘mahā-mahā-prasādākhyāna’  
devotee’s  remnants  if they become  mahā-mahā-prasāda  called

(59) The remnants of Kṛṣṇa are called *mahā-prasāda*; when they become the remnants of a devotee, they are called *mahā-mahā-prasāda*.

ভক্তপদধূলি আর ভক্তপদ-জল ৷  
ভক্তভুক্ত-শেষ, — এই তিন সাধনের বল ॥ ৬০ ॥

bhakta-pada-dhūli āra bhakta-pada-jala  
devotee’s  feet  dust  and  devotee’s  feet  water  
bhakta-bhukta-śeṣa, — ei tina sādhanera bala  
by the devotee  eaten  remnants  these  three  of practice  the strength

(60) The dust of a devotee’s feet, the water that has washed his feet, and the remnants of what he has eaten — these three are the strength of spiritual practice.[^20]

এই তিন-সেবা হৈতে কৃষ্ণপ্রেমা হয় ৷  
পুনঃ পুনঃ সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে ফুকারিয়া কয় ॥ ৬১ ॥

ei tina-sevā haite kṛṣṇa-premā haya  
these  three  serving  from  for Kṛṣṇa  love  arises  
punaḥ punaḥ sarva-śāstre phukāriyā kaya  
again  again  in all  scriptures  loudly  they declare

(61) By serving these three one attains love for Kṛṣṇa; all the scriptures declare this loudly again and again.

তাতে বার বার কহি, — শুন ভক্তগণ ৷  
বিশ্বাস করিয়া কর এ-তিন সেবন ॥ ৬২ ॥

tāte bāra bāra kahi, — śuna bhakta-gaṇa  
therefore  again  again  I say  hear  devotees  
viśvāsa kariyā kara e-tina sevana  
faith  having  do  these three  service

(62) Therefore I tell you over and over: listen, O devotees! Serve these three with faith.

তিন হৈতে কৃষ্ণনাম-প্রেমের উল্লাস ৷  
কৃষ্ণের প্রসাদ, তাতে ‘সাক্ষী’ কালিদাস ॥ ৬৩ ॥

tina haite kṛṣṇa-nāma-premera ullāsa  
the three  from  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  of love  jubilation  
kṛṣṇera prasāda, tāte ‘sākṣī’ kālidāsa  
Kṛṣṇa’s  grace  of that  witness  Kālidāsa

(63) These three bring the joy of Kṛṣṇa’s name and of love for Him, and through them one gains Kṛṣṇa’s grace. Kālidāsa is the witness to this.

নীলাচলে মহাপ্রভু রহে এইমতে ৷  
কালিদাসে মহাকৃপা কৈলা অলক্ষিতে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

nīlācale mahāprabhu rahe ei-mate  
in Nīlācala  Mahāprabhu  stays  in this way  
kālidāse mahā-kṛpā kailā alakṣite  
to Kālidāsa  great  mercy  showed  unnoticed

(64) In this way Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala, and without anyone noticing He showed Kālidāsa great mercy.

সে বৎসর শিবানন্দ পত্নী লঞা আইলা ৷  
‘পুরীদাস’-ছোটপুত্রে সঙ্গেতে আনিলা ॥ ৬৫ ॥

se vatsara śivānanda patnī lañā āilā  
that  year  Śivānanda  his wife  taking  came  
‘purī-dāsa’-choṭa-putre saṅgete ānilā  
Purī dāsa  his youngest  son  with him  brought

(65) That year Śivānanda came with his wife and brought with him his youngest son, Purī dāsa.[^21]

পুত্র সঙ্গে লঞা তেঁহো আইলা প্রভু-স্থানে ৷  
পুত্রেরে করাইলা প্রভুর চরণ বন্দনে ॥ ৬৬ ॥

putra saṅge lañā tem̐ho āilā prabhu-sthāne  
son  with  taking  he  came  to the Lord’s place  
putrere karāilā prabhura caraṇa vandane  
the son  he made  the Lord’s  feet  honour

(66) Taking his son, he came to the Lord and had the boy bow at His feet.

‘কৃষ্ণ কহ’ বলি’ প্রভু বলেন বার বার ৷  
তবু কৃষ্ণনাম বালক না করে উচ্চার ॥ ৬৭ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa kaha’ bali’ prabhu balena bāra bāra  
Kṛṣṇa  say  saying  the Lord  says  again  again  
tabu kṛṣṇa-nāma bālaka nā kare uccāra  
still  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  the boy  not  makes  utterance

(67) Again and again the Lord said, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!” But the boy stayed silent and would not utter the name of Kṛṣṇa.

শিবানন্দ বালকেরে বহু যত্ন করিলা ৷  
তবু সেই বালক কৃষ্ণনাম না কহিলা ॥ ৬৮ ॥

śivānanda bālakere bahu yatna karilā  
Śivānanda  with the boy  much  effort  made  
tabu sei bālaka kṛṣṇa-nāma nā kahilā  
still  that  boy  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  not  said

(68) Śivānanda did all he could to persuade the boy, but still the boy did not say the name of Kṛṣṇa.

প্রভু কহে, — আমি নাম জগতে লওয়াইলুঁ ৷  
স্থাবরে-পর্য্যন্ত কৃষ্ণনাম কহাইলুঁ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

prabhu kahe, — āmi nāma jagate laoyāilum̐  
the Lord  says  I  the name  the world  made take  
sthāvare-paryanta kṛṣṇa-nāma kahāilum̐  
the unmoving  even  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  I made say

(69) The Lord said, “I have made the whole world chant the holy name; I have made even trees and stones say the name of Kṛṣṇa.

ইহারে নারিলুঁ কৃষ্ণনাম কহাইতে !  
শুনিয়া স্বরূপগোসাঞি লাগিলা কহিতে ॥ ৭০ ॥

ihāre nārilum̐ kṛṣṇa-nāma kahāite!  
this one  I could not  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  make say  
śuniyā svarūpa-gosāñi lāgilā kahite  
hearing  Svarūpa  Gosāñi  began  to speak

(70) Yet I could not make this boy say the name of Kṛṣṇa!” Hearing this, Svarūpa Gosāñi said:

তুমি কৃষ্ণনাম-মন্ত্র কৈলা উপদেশে ৷  
মন্ত্র পাঞা কার আগে না করে প্রকাশে ॥ ৭১ ॥

tumi kṛṣṇa-nāma-mantra kailā upadeśe  
You  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  mantra  made  instruction  
mantra pāñā kāra āge nā kare prakāśe  
the mantra  having received  anyone  before  not  makes  disclosure

(71) “You have instructed him in the mantra of Kṛṣṇa’s name. Having received the mantra, he will not disclose it to anyone.[^22]

মনে মনে জপে, মুখে না করে আখ্যান ৷  
এই ইহার মনঃকথা — করি অনুমান ॥ ৭২ ॥

mane mane jape, mukhe nā kare ākhyāna  
in the mind  in the mind  he chants  with the mouth  not  makes  utterance  
ei ihāra manaḥ-kathā — kari anumāna  
this  his  mind’s  thought  I make  guess

(72) He chants it silently, within, but does not speak it aloud. That, I think, is his intention.”

আর দিন কহেন প্রভু, — ‘পড়, পুরীদাস’ ৷  
এই শ্লোক করি’ তেঁহো করিলা প্রকাশ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

āra dina kahena prabhu, — ‘paḍa, purī-dāsa’  
another  day  says  the Lord  recite  Purī dāsa  
ei śloka kari’ tem̐ho karilā prakāśa  
this  verse  composing  he  made  manifest

(73) Another day the Lord said, “Recite, Purī dāsa!” and the boy composed and recited this verse:

শ্রবসোঃ কুবলয়মক্ষ্ণো রঞ্জনমুরসো মহেন্দ্রমণিদাম ৷  
বৃন্দাবনরমণীনাং মণ্ডনমখিলং হরির্জ্জয়তি ॥ ৭৪ ॥

śravasoḥ kuvalayam akṣṇor añjanam  
in the ears  a blue lotus  for the eyes  collyrium  
uraso mahendra-maṇi-dāma  
for the chest  of great sapphires  a garland  
vṛndāvana-ramaṇīnāṁ maṇḍanam  
of Vṛndāvana’s  beautiful girls  the ornament  
akhilaṁ harir jayati  
complete  Hari  is glorious

(74) “Glory to Hari! He is all the adornment of the girls of Vṛndāvana: the blue lotus at their ears, the collyrium of their eyes, the necklace of great sapphires upon their breast.” (Āryā-śataka, 1)[^23]

সাত-বৎসরের শিশু, নাহি অধ্যয়ন ৷  
ঐছে শ্লোক করে, — লোকে চমৎকার মন ॥ ৭৫ ॥

sāta-vatsarera śiśu, nāhi adhyayana  
seven  years  a child  no  study  
aiche śloka kare, — loke camatkāra mana  
such  verse  makes  people  amazed  minds

(75) He was a child of seven, who had not studied, yet he composed such a verse! Everyone was amazed.

চৈতন্যপ্রভুর এই কৃপার মহিমা ৷  
ব্রহ্মাদি-দেব যার নাহি পায় সীমা ॥ ৭৬ ॥

caitanya-prabhura ei kṛpāra mahimā  
Caitanya  Prabhu’s  this  of mercy  greatness  
brahmādi-deva yāra nāhi pāya sīmā  
Brahmā and the other  gods  of which  not  find  the limit

(76) Such is the greatness of Caitanya Prabhu’s mercy; even Brahmā and the other gods cannot find its limit.

ভক্তগণ প্রভু-সঙ্গে রহে চারিমাসে ৷  
প্রভু আজ্ঞা দিলা সবে গেলা গৌড়দেশে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

bhakta-gaṇa prabhu-saṅge rahe cāri-māse  
the devotees  with the Lord  stayed  four months  
prabhu ājñā dilā sabe gelā gauḍa-deśe  
the Lord  leave  gave  all  went  to the land of Gauḍa

(77) The devotees stayed with the Lord for four months; then He gave them leave, and they all went back to Gauḍa.

তাঁ-সবার সঙ্গে প্রভুর ছিল বাহ্যজ্ঞান ৷  
তাঁরা গেলে পুনঃ হৈলা উন্মাদ প্রধান ॥ ৭৮ ॥

tām̐-sabāra saṅge prabhura chila bāhya-jñāna  
them all  with  the Lord’s  was  external  awareness  
tām̐rā gele punaḥ hailā unmāda pradhāna  
they  gone  again  became  madness  dominant

(78) In their company the Lord kept His external awareness, but when they had gone, His divine madness again prevailed.[^24]

রাত্রি-দিনে স্ফুরে কৃষ্ণের রূপ-গন্ধ-রস ৷  
সাক্ষাদনুভবে, — যেন কৃষ্ণ-উপস্পর্শ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

rātri-dine sphure kṛṣṇera rūpa-gandha-rasa  
night and day  appear  Kṛṣṇa’s  form  fragrance  taste  
sākṣād-anubhave, — yena kṛṣṇa-upasparśa  
directly  experiencing  as if  Kṛṣṇa’s  touch

(79) Night and day Kṛṣṇa’s form, fragrance and taste appeared to Him; He experienced them directly, as if Kṛṣṇa were touching Him.[^25]

একদিন প্রভু গেলা জগন্নাথ-দরশনে ৷  
সিংহদ্বারে দলই আসি’ করিল বন্দনে ॥ ৮০ ॥

eka-dina prabhu gelā jagannātha-daraśane  
one  day  the Lord  went  for Jagannātha’s  darśana  
siṁha-dvāre dalai āsi’ karila vandane  
at the Siṁha-dvāra  the doorkeeper  coming  made  obeisance

(80) One day, when the Lord went to see Jagannātha, the doorkeeper came up to Him at the Siṁha-dvāra and bowed down.[^26]

তারে বলে, — ‘কোথা কৃষ্ণ, মোর প্রাণনাথ ?  
মোরে কৃষ্ণ দেখাও’ বলি’ ধরে তার হাত ॥ ৮১ ॥

tāre bale, — ‘kothā kṛṣṇa, mora prāṇa-nātha?  
to him  says  where  Kṛṣṇa  My  life’s  Lord  
more kṛṣṇa dekhāo’ bali’ dhare tāra hāta  
to Me  Kṛṣṇa  show  saying  takes  his  hand

(81) The Lord said to him, “Where is Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of My life? Show Me Kṛṣṇa!” and took him by the hand.

সেহ কহে, — ‘ইঁহা হয় ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ৷  
আইস তুমি মোর সঙ্গে, করাঙ দরশন’ ॥ ৮২ ॥

seha kahe, — ‘im̐hā haya vrajendra-nandana  
he  says  here  is  of the king of Vraja  the son  
āisa tumi mora saṅge, karāṅa daraśana’  
come  You  me  with  I will make  see

(82) The doorkeeper said, “The son of the king of Vraja is here. Come with me and I will show Him to You.”

‘তুমি মোর সখা, দেখাহ, — কাঁহা প্রাণনাথ ?’  
এত বলি’ জগমোহন গেলা ধরি’ তার হাত ॥ ৮৩ ॥

‘tumi mora sakhā, dekhāha, — kām̐hā prāṇa-nātha?’  
you  My  friend  show  where  life’s  Lord  
eta bali’ jagamohana gelā dhari’ tāra hāta  
this  saying  to the jagamohana  went  holding  his  hand

(83) “You are My friend. Show Me where the Lord of My life is!” Saying this, the Lord went into the *jagamohana*, holding the man’s hand.

সেহ বলে, — ‘এই দেখ শ্রীপুরুষোত্তম ৷  
নেত্র ভরিয়া তুমি করহ দরশন’ ॥ ৮৪ ॥

seha bale, — ‘ei dekha śrī-puruṣottama  
he  says  here  see  Śrī Puruṣottama  
netra bhariyā tumi karaha daraśana’  
the eyes  filling  You  make  darśana

(84) The doorkeeper said, “Here is Śrī Puruṣottama. Fill Your eyes with the sight of Him.”

গরুড়ের পাছে রহি’ করেন দরশন ৷  
দেখেন, — জগন্নাথ হয় মুরলীবদন ॥ ৮৫ ॥

garuḍera pāche rahi’ karena daraśana  
of Garuḍa  behind  staying  He makes  darśana  
dekhena, — jagannātha haya muralī-vadana  
He sees  Jagannātha  is  with the flute at His lips

(85) Standing behind the Garuḍa pillar, the Lord gazed, and He saw that Jagannātha was Kṛṣṇa with the flute at His lips.

এই লীলা নিজ গ্রন্থে রঘুনাথ-দাস ৷  
চৈতন্যস্তবকল্পবৃক্ষে কৈরাছেন প্রকাশ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

ei līlā nija-granthe raghunātha-dāsa  
this  pastime  in his own  book  Raghunātha  dāsa  
caitanya-stava-kalpa-vṛkṣe kairāchena prakāśa  
in the Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa  has  described

(86) Raghunātha dāsa has described this pastime in his own book, the *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*:

ক্ব মে কান্তঃ কৃষ্ণস্ত্বরিতমিহ তং লোকয় সখে  
ত্বমেবেতি দ্বারাধিপমভিবদন্নুন্মদ ইব ৷  
দ্রুতং গচ্ছ দ্রষ্টুং প্রিয়মিতি তদুক্তেন ধৃত-তদ্-  
ভুজান্তর্গৌরাঙ্গো হৃদয় উদয়ন্মাং মদয়তি ॥ ৮৭ ॥

kva me kāntaḥ kṛṣṇas tvaritam iha taṁ lokaya sakhe  
where  My  beloved  Kṛṣṇa  quickly  here  Him  show  friend  
tvam eveti dvārādhipam abhivadann unmada iva  
you  alone  so  to the doorkeeper  saying  mad  as if  
drutaṁ gaccha draṣṭuṁ priyam iti tad-uktena dhṛta-tad-  
quickly  go  to see  the Beloved  so  by his words  holding  his  
bhujāntar gaurāṅgo hṛdaya udayan māṁ madayati  
hand  Gaurāṅga  in my heart  rising  me  maddens

(87) “‘Friend, where is My beloved Kṛṣṇa? Show Him to Me here, quickly — only you can!’ So, as if mad, Gaurāṅga spoke to the doorkeeper; and when the man said, ‘Come quickly and see Your Beloved,’ He took hold of his hand. That Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, 7)[^27]

হেনকালে ‘গোপাল-বল্লভ’-ভোগ লাগাইল ৷  
শঙ্খ-ঘণ্টা-আদি সহ আরতি বাজিল ॥ ৮৮ ॥

hena-kāle ‘gopāla-vallabha’-bhoga lāgāila  
at that time  gopāla-vallabha  offering  was placed  
śaṅkha-ghaṇṭā-ādi saha ārati bājila  
conch  bells  and so on  with  the ārati  sounded

(88) At that time the *gopāla-vallabha* offering was placed before Jagannātha, and the *ārati* began to the sound of conchshells, bells and other instruments.[^28]

ভোগ সরিলে জগন্নাথের সেবকগণ ৷  
প্রসাদ লঞা প্রভু-ঠাঞি কৈল আগমন ॥ ৮৯ ॥

bhoga sarile jagannāthera sevaka-gaṇa  
the offering  being over  Jagannātha’s  servants  
prasāda lañā prabhu-ṭhāñi kaila āgamana  
prasāda  taking  to the Lord  made  coming

(89) When the offering was over, Jagannātha’s servants came to the Lord with *prasāda*.

মালা পরাঞা প্রসাদ দিল প্রভুর হাতে ৷  
আস্বাদ দূরে রহু, যার গন্ধে মন মাতে ॥ ৯০ ॥

mālā parāñā prasāda dila prabhura hāte  
a garland  putting on  prasāda  gave  the Lord’s  into the hands  
āsvāda dūre rahu, yāra gandhe mana māte  
the taste  far  let it remain  whose  by the fragrance  the mind  is intoxicated

(90) Placing a garland on Him, they put the *prasāda* into the Lord’s hands. Let alone its taste — its very fragrance intoxicated the mind.

বহুমূল্য প্রসাদ সেই বস্তু সর্ব্বোত্তম ৷  
তার অল্প খাওয়াইতে সেবক করিল যতন ॥ ৯১ ॥

bahu-mūlya prasāda sei vastu sarvottama  
very valuable  prasāda  that  the thing  the most excellent  
tāra alpa khāoyāite sevaka karila yatana  
of it  a little  to make eat  the servant  made  effort

(91) That *prasāda* was very precious, the finest of all things, and the servant did his best to have the Lord taste a little of it.

তার অল্প লঞা প্রভু জিহ্বাতে যদি দিলা ৷  
আর সব গোবিন্দের আঁচলে বান্ধিলা ॥ ৯২ ॥

tāra alpa lañā prabhu jihvāte yadi dilā  
of it  a little  taking  the Lord  on the tongue  when  put  
āra saba govindera ām̐cale bāndhilā  
the rest  all  Govinda’s  in the cloth’s end  tied

(92) The Lord took a little of it and put it on His tongue; all the rest He tied in the end of Govinda’s cloth.

কোটিঅমৃত-স্বাদ পাঞা প্রভুর চমৎকার ৷  
সর্ব্বাঙ্গে পুলক, নেত্রে বহে অশ্রুধার ॥ ৯৩ ॥

koṭiamṛta-svāda pāñā prabhura camatkāra  
of millions of nectars  the taste  getting  the Lord’s  wonder  
sarvāṅge pulaka, netre vahe aśru-dhāra  
on all the limbs  thrill  from the eyes  flows  of tears  a stream

(93) Tasting what was sweeter than millions of nectars, the Lord was struck with wonder. His hair stood on end all over His body, and streams of tears flowed from His eyes.

‘এই দ্রব্যে এত স্বাদ কাঁহা হইতে আইল ?  
কৃষ্ণের অধরামৃত ইথে সঞ্চারিল’ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

‘ei dravye eta svāda kām̐hā haite āila?  
in this  food  such  taste  from where  has come  
kṛṣṇera adharāmṛta ithe sañcārila’  
of Kṛṣṇa  the lip-nectar  into it  has entered

(94) “How has this food come by such a taste? The nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips has entered it!”

এই বুদ্ধ্যে মহাপ্রভুর প্রেমাবেশ হৈল ৷  
জগন্নাথের সেবক দেখি’ সম্বরণ কৈল ॥ ৯৫ ॥

ei buddhye mahāprabhura premāveśa haila  
by this  thought  Mahāprabhu’s  absorption in love  was  
jagannāthera sevaka dekhi’ samvaraṇa kaila  
of Jagannātha  the servant  seeing  restraint  made

(95) At this thought Mahāprabhu was overcome by love, but seeing Jagannātha’s servant, He restrained Himself.[^29]

‘সুকৃতি-লভ্য ফেলা-লব’ — বলেন বারবার ৷  
ঈশ্বর-সেবক পুছে, — ‘কি অর্থ ইহার’ ? ॥ ৯৬ ॥

‘sukṛti-labhya phelā-lava’ — balena bāra-bāra  
by sukṛti  to be gained  of the remnants  a particle  He says  again and again  
īśvara-sevaka puche, — ‘ki artha ihāra’?  
the Lord’s  servant  asks  what  meaning  of this

(96) Again and again He said, “A particle of the remnants, gained by *sukṛti*!” Jagannātha’s servant asked, “What does this mean?”[^30]

প্রভু কহে, — এই যে দিলা কৃষ্ণাধরামৃত ৷  
ব্রহ্মাদি-দুর্ল্লভ এই নিন্দয়ে ‘অমৃত’ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — ei ye dilā kṛṣṇādharāmṛta  
the Lord  says  this  that  you have given  Kṛṣṇa’s lip-nectar  
brahmādi-durllabha ei nindaye ‘amṛta’  
for Brahmā and others  rare  this  puts to shame  nectar

(97) The Lord said, “What you have given Me is the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips. Even Brahmā and the gods can hardly obtain it, and it puts nectar itself to shame.

কৃষ্ণের যে ভুক্ত-শেষ, তার ‘ফেলা’-নাম ৷  
তার এক ‘লব’ যে পায়, সেই ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

kṛṣṇera ye bhukta-śeṣa, tāra ‘phelā’-nāma  
Kṛṣṇa’s  which  eaten  remnants  of that  phelā  the name  
tāra eka ‘lava’ ye pāya, sei bhāgyavān  
of it  one  particle  who  obtains  he  fortunate

(98) The remnants of what Kṛṣṇa has eaten are called *phelā*. Whoever obtains even a particle of it is fortunate.

সামান্য ভাগ্য হৈতে তার প্রাপ্তি নাহি হয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের যাঁতে পূর্ণ কৃপা, সেই তাহা পায় ॥ ৯৯ ॥

sāmānya bhāgya haite tāra prāpti nāhi haya  
ordinary  fortune  from  its  attainment  not  is  
kṛṣṇera yām̐te pūrṇa kṛpā, sei tāhā pāya  
Kṛṣṇa’s  on whom  full  mercy  he  it  obtains

(99) It cannot be attained by ordinary good fortune. Only one on whom Kṛṣṇa’s full mercy rests obtains it.[^31]

‘সুকৃতি’-শব্দে কহে ‘কৃষ্ণকৃপা-হেতু পুণ্য’ ৷  
যেই যাঁর হয়, ‘ফেলা’ পায় সেই ধন্য ॥ ১০০ ॥

‘sukṛti’-śabde kahe ‘kṛṣṇa-kṛpā-hetu puṇya’  
sukṛti  by the word  is meant  of Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  the cause  piety  
yei yām̐ra haya, ‘phelā’ pāya sei dhanya  
this  whose  is  phelā  obtains  he  blessed

(100) The word *sukṛti* means the piety that is the cause of Kṛṣṇa’s mercy. Whoever has it obtains *phelā* and is blessed.”[^32]

এত বলি’ প্রভু তা-সবারে বিদায় দিলা ৷  
উপল-ভোগ দেখিয়া প্রভু নিজ-বাসা আইলা ॥ ১০১ ॥

eta bali’ prabhu tā-sabāre vidāya dilā  
this  having said  the Lord  them all  leave  gave  
upala-bhoga dekhiyā prabhu nija-vāsā āilā  
the upala-bhoga  seeing  the Lord  to His own  residence  came

(101) Having said this, the Lord gave the servants leave to go, and after seeing the *upala-bhoga* offering He returned to His residence.

মধ্যাহ্ন করিয়া কৈলা ভিক্ষা নির্ব্বাহণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণাধরামৃত সদা অন্তরে স্মরণ ॥ ১০২ ॥

madhyāhna kariyā kailā bhikṣā nirvāhaṇa  
the midday duties  having done  made  the meal  completion  
kṛṣṇādharāmṛta sadā antare smaraṇa  
Kṛṣṇa’s lip-nectar  always  in the heart  remembrance

(102) He performed His midday duties and finished His meal, but all the while He remembered the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips within His heart.

বাহ্য-কৃত্য করেন, প্রেমে গরগর মন ৷  
কষ্টে সম্বরণ করেন, আবেশ সঘন ॥ ১০৩ ॥

bāhya-kṛtya karena, preme garagara mana  
external  duties  He does  with love  overflowing  the mind  
kaṣṭe samvaraṇa karena, āveśa saghana  
with difficulty  restraint  He makes  absorption  intense

(103) He carried out His outward duties, but His mind was overflowing with love; His absorption was intense, and He restrained it only with great difficulty.

সন্ধ্যা-কৃত্য করি’ পুনঃ নিজগণ-সঙ্গে ৷  
নিভৃতে বসিলা নানা-কৃষ্ণকথা-রঙ্গে ॥ ১০৪ ॥

sandhyā-kṛtya kari’ punaḥ nija-gaṇa-saṅge  
evening  duties  having done  again  His own  associates  with  
nibhṛte vasilā nānā-kṛṣṇa-kathā-raṅge  
in seclusion  sat  of various  Kṛṣṇa  talks  in the delight

(104) After His evening duties He again sat in seclusion with His own associates, delighting in many talks about Kṛṣṇa.

প্রভুর ইঙ্গিতে গোবিন্দ প্রসাদ আনিলা ৷  
পুরী-ভারতীরে প্রভু কিছু পাঠাইলা ॥ ১০৫ ॥

prabhura iṅgite govinda prasāda ānilā  
the Lord’s  at the sign  Govinda  the prasāda  brought  
purī-bhāratīre prabhu kichu pāṭhāilā  
to Purī  and Bhāratī  the Lord  some  sent

(105) At the Lord’s sign Govinda brought the *prasāda*, and the Lord sent some to Paramānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī.

রামানন্দ-সার্ব্বভৌম-স্বরূপাদি-গণে ৷  
সবারে প্রসাদ দিল করিয়া বণ্টনে ॥ ১০৬ ॥

rāmānanda-sārvabhauma-svarūpādi-gaṇe  
to Rāmānanda  Sārvabhauma  Svarūpa  and the others  
sabāre prasāda dila kariyā vaṇṭane  
to all  prasāda  gave  having made  distribution

(106) He divided the *prasāda* and gave it to all — Rāmānanda, Sārvabhauma, Svarūpa and the others.

প্রসাদের সৌরভ্য-মাধুর্য্য করি’ আস্বাদন ৷  
অলৌকিক আস্বাদে সবার বিস্ময় হৈল মন ॥ ১০৭ ॥

prasādera saurabhya-mādhurya kari’ āsvādana  
of the prasāda  the fragrance  the sweetness  having made  tasting  
alaukika āsvāde sabāra vismaya haila mana  
at the unearthly  taste  of all  amazed  became  the minds

(107) When they savoured the fragrance and sweetness of the *prasāda*, all were amazed at its unearthly taste.

প্রভু কহে, — এই সব হয় ‘প্রাকৃত’ দ্রব্য ৷  
ঐক্ষব, কর্পূর, মরিচ, এলাইচ, লবঙ্গ, গব্য ॥ ১০৮ ॥

রসবাস, গুড়ত্বক-আদি যত সব ৷  
‘প্রাকৃত’ বস্তুর স্বাদ সবার অনুভব ॥ ১০৯ ॥

prabhu kahe, — ei saba haya ‘prākṛta’ dravya  
the Lord  says  these  all  are  material  substances  
aikṣava, karpūra, marica, elāica, lavaṅga, gavya  
sugar  camphor  black pepper  cardamom  clove  ghee  
rasavāsa, guḍatvaka-ādi yata saba  
spices  cinnamon  and so on  as many  as all  
‘prākṛta’ vastura svāda sabāra anubhava  
material  things’  taste  of all  the experience

(108–109) The Lord said, “All these are material substances — sugar, camphor, black pepper, cardamom, cloves, ghee, spices, cinnamon and the rest. Everyone knows the taste of material things.[^33]

এই দ্রব্যে এত আস্বাদ, গন্ধ লোকাতীত ৷  
আস্বাদ করিয়া দেখ, — সবার প্রতীত ॥ ১১০ ॥

ei dravye eta āsvāda, gandha lokātīta  
in these  substances  such  taste  fragrance  beyond this world  
āsvāda kariyā dekha, — sabāra pratīta  
tasting  see  of all  conviction

(110) Yet these substances have a taste and a fragrance beyond this world. Taste them and see for yourselves.

আস্বাদ দূরে রহু, যার গন্ধে মাতে মন ৷  
আপনা বিনা অন্য মাধুর্য্য করায় বিস্মরণ ॥ ১১১ ॥

āsvāda dūre rahu, yāra gandhe māte mana  
the taste  far  let it remain  whose  by the fragrance  is intoxicated  the mind  
āpanā vinā anya mādhurya karāya vismaraṇa  
itself  except  other  sweetness  makes  forget

(111) Let alone its taste — its fragrance alone intoxicates the mind and makes one forget every sweetness but its own.

তাতে এই দ্রব্যে কৃষ্ণাধর-স্পর্শ হৈল ৷  
অধরের গুণ সব ইহাতে সঞ্চারিল ॥ ১১২ ॥

tāte ei dravye kṛṣṇādhara-sparśa haila  
therefore  this  food  Kṛṣṇa’s lips’  touch  received  
adharera guṇa saba ihāte sañcārila  
of the lips  the qualities  all  into it  have passed

(112) Therefore Kṛṣṇa’s lips must have touched this food, and all the qualities of His lips have passed into it.

অলৌকিক-গন্ধ-স্বাদ, অন্য-বিস্মারণ ৷  
মহা-মাদক হয় এই কৃষ্ণাধরের গুণ ॥ ১১৩ ॥

alaukika-gandha-svāda, anya-vismāraṇa  
unearthly  fragrance  taste  of others  forgetting  
mahā-mādaka haya ei kṛṣṇādharera guṇa  
great  intoxicating  are  these  of Kṛṣṇa’s lips  qualities

(113) An unearthly fragrance and taste, and the power to make one forget everything else — such are the greatly intoxicating qualities of Kṛṣṇa’s lips.[^34]

অনেক ‘সুকৃতে’ ইহা হঞাছে সম্প্রাপ্তি ৷  
সবে এই আস্বাদ কর করি’ মহাভক্তি ॥ ১১৪ ॥

aneka ‘sukṛte’ ihā hañāche samprāpti  
by many  sukṛtis  this  has been  obtained  
sabe ei āsvāda kara kari’ mahā-bhakti  
all  this  tasting  do  with  great devotion

(114) We have gained this through much *sukṛti*. Now all of you, taste it with great devotion.”

হরিধ্বনি করি’ সবে কৈলা আস্বাদন ৷  
আস্বাদিতে প্রেমে মত্ত হইল সবার মন ॥ ১১৫ ॥

hari-dhvani kari’ sabe kailā āsvādana  
Hari  calling  all  made  tasting  
āsvādite preme matta haila sabāra mana  
while tasting  with love  mad  became  of all  the minds

(115) Calling out the name of Hari, they all tasted it, and as they did so their minds grew mad with love.

প্রেমাবেশে মহাপ্রভু যবে আজ্ঞা দিলা ৷  
রামানন্দ-রায় শ্লোক পড়িতে লাগিলা ॥ ১১৬ ॥

premāveśe mahāprabhu yabe ājñā dilā  
absorbed in love  Mahāprabhu  when  order  gave  
rāmānanda-rāya śloka paḍite lāgilā  
Rāmānanda Rāya  verses  to recite  began

(116) When Mahāprabhu, absorbed in love, gave the order, Rāmānanda Rāya began to recite verses.

সুরতবর্দ্ধনং শোকনাশনং স্বরিতবেণুনা সুষ্ঠুচুম্বিতম্ ৷  
ইতররাগবিস্মারণং নৃণাং বিতর বীর নস্তেঽধরামৃতম্ ॥ ১১৭ ॥

surata-vardhanaṁ śoka-nāśanaṁ  
union  increasing  grief  destroying  
svarita-veṇunā suṣṭhu-cumbitam  
by the sounding  flute  well  kissed  
itara-rāga-vismāraṇaṁ nṛṇāṁ  
other  attachments  making forget  of men  
vitara vīra nas te ’dharāmṛtam  
bestow  O hero  on us  Your  lip-nectar

(117) “O hero, grant us the nectar of Your lips. It deepens the longing for union and destroys grief; Your sounding flute kisses it tenderly, and it makes men forget every other attachment.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.14)[^35]

শ্লোক শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু মহাতুষ্ট হৈলা ৷  
রাধার উৎকণ্ঠা-শ্লোক পড়িতে লাগিলা ॥ ১১৮ ॥

śloka śuni’ mahāprabhu mahā-tuṣṭa hailā  
the verse  hearing  Mahāprabhu  greatly  pleased  became  
rādhāra utkaṇṭhā-śloka paḍite lāgilā  
of Rādhā  longing  verse  to recite  began

(118) Mahāprabhu was greatly pleased to hear it, and He began to recite a verse of Rādhā’s eager longing:

ব্রজাতুলকুলাঙ্গনেতর-রসালিতৃষ্ণাহর-  
প্রদীব্যদধরামৃতঃ সুকৃতিলভ্য-ফেলা-লবঃ ৷  
সুধাজিদহিবল্লিকা-সুদলবীটিকা-চর্ব্বিতঃ  
স মে মদনমোহনঃ সখি তনোতি জিহ্বা-স্পৃহাম্ ॥ ১১৯ ॥

vrajātula-kulāṅganetara-rasāli-tṛṣṇā-hara-  
of Vraja  of the incomparable  noble ladies  other  tastes  the thirst  removing  
pradīvyad-adharāmṛtaḥ sukṛti-labhya-phelā-lavaḥ  
shining  whose lip-nectar  by sukṛti  to be gained  of the remnants  a particle  
sudhā-jid-ahivallikā-sudala-vīṭikā-carvitaḥ  
than nectar  surpassing  of betel  fine leaves  rolls  chewing  
sa me madana-mohanaḥ sakhi tanoti jihvā-spṛhām  
He  My  Madana-mohana  O friend  increases  of the tongue  the craving

(119) “O friend, the shining nectar of His lips takes from the incomparable noble ladies of Vraja all thirst for any other taste; a particle of His remnants is gained only by *sukṛti*; and He chews rolls of fine betel leaves sweeter than nectar. That Madana-mohana of Mine deepens the craving of My tongue.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 8.8)[^36]

এত কহি’ গৌরপ্রভু ভাবাবিষ্ট হঞা ৷  
দুই শ্লোকের অর্থ করে প্রলাপ করিয়া ॥ ১২০ ॥

eta kahi’ gaura-prabhu bhāvāviṣṭa hañā  
this  having said  Gaura  Prabhu  absorbed in feeling  becoming  
dui ślokera artha kare pralāpa kariyā  
of the two  verses  the meaning  makes  raving  doing

(120) Having said this, Gaura Prabhu, absorbed in feeling, explained the meaning of the two verses in raving words (*pralāpa*).

তনু-মন করায় ক্ষোভ, বাড়ায় সুরত-লোভ,  
হর্ষ-শোকাদি-ভার বিনাশয় ৷  
পাসরায় অন্য রস, জগৎ করে আত্মবশ,  
লজ্জা, ধর্ম্ম, ধৈর্য্য করে ক্ষয় ॥ ১২১ ॥

নাগর, শুন তোমার অধর-চরিত ৷  
মাতায় নারীর মন, জিহ্বা করে আকর্ষণ,  
বিচারিতে সব বিপরীত ॥ ১২২ ॥ধ্রু॥

tanu-mana karāya kṣobha,  
body  mind  makes  agitation  
bāḍāya surata-lobha,  
increases  for union  the longing  
harṣa-śokādi-bhāra vināśaya  
of joy  grief  and so on  the burden  destroys  
pāsarāya anya rasa,  
makes forget  other  tastes  
jagat kare ātma-vaśa,  
the world  makes  its own  subject  
lajjā, dharma, dhairya kare kṣaya  
shame  duty  composure  makes  destruction  
nāgara, śuna tomāra adhara-carita  
O lover  hear  Your  of the lips  conduct  
mātāya nārīra mana,  
intoxicates  of women  the mind  
jihvā kare ākarṣaṇa,  
the tongue  makes  attraction  
vicārite saba viparīta  
on reflection  all  contrary

(121–122) “O Beloved, hear the ways of Your lips! They stir the body and mind, increase the longing for union, and take away the burden of joy, grief and all the rest. They make one forget every other taste, bring the whole world under their sway, and destroy shame, duty and composure. They intoxicate the minds of women and draw them on, kindling the craving of the tongue. Yet when I think it over, everything about them is contrary.[^37]

আছুক নারীর কাষ, কহিতে বাসিয়ে লাজ,  
তোমার অধর বড় ধৃষ্ট-রায় ৷  
পুরুষে করে আকর্ষণ, আপনা পিয়াইতে মন,  
অন্যরস সব পাসরায় ॥ ১২৩ ॥

āchuka nārīra kāṣa,  
let alone  of women  the matter  
kahite vāsiye lāja,  
to say  I feel  shame  
tomāra adhara baḍa dhṛṣṭa-rāya  
Your  lips  very  bold  lord  
puruṣe kare ākarṣaṇa,  
a man  they make  attraction  
āpanā piyāite mana,  
themselves  to give to drink  desire  
anya-rasa saba pāsarāya  
other  tastes  all  make forget

(123) Let alone women — I am ashamed even to say it: Your lips are the boldest of masters. They draw even a man to them, longing to give themselves to him to drink, and make him forget every other taste.[^38]

সচেতন রহু দূরে, অচেতন সচেতন করে,  
তোমার অধর — বড় বাজিকর ৷  
তোমার বেণু শুষ্কেন্ধন, তার জন্মায় ইন্দ্রিয়-মন,  
তারে আপনা পিয়ায় নিরন্তর ॥ ১২৪ ॥

sacetana rahu dūre,  
the conscious  let remain  far  
acetana sacetana kare,  
the unconscious  conscious  they make  
tomāra adhara — baḍa vājikara  
Your  lips  a great  magician  
tomāra veṇu śuṣkendhana,  
Your  flute  dry fuel  
tāra janmāya indriya-mana,  
in it  they produce  senses  mind  
tāre āpanā piyāya nirantara  
to it  themselves  give to drink  constantly

(124) Let alone the conscious — Your lips make even the unconscious conscious; they are a great magician! Your flute is nothing but dry firewood, yet they give it senses and a mind and let it drink of them ceaselessly.[^39]

বেণু ধৃষ্ট-পুরুষ হঞা, পুরুষাধর পিয়া পিয়া,  
গোপীগণে জানায় নিজ পান ৷  
‘ওহে, শুন, গোপীগণ, বলে পিঙো তোমার ধন,  
তোমার যদি থাকে অভিমান ॥ ১২৫ ॥

veṇu dhṛṣṭa-puruṣa hañā,  
the flute  a bold  man  being  
puruṣādhara piyā piyā,  
a man’s lips  drinking  drinking  
gopī-gaṇe jānāya nija pāna  
to the gopīs  announces  its own  drinking  
‘ohe, śuna, gopī-gaṇa,  
oh  hear  gopīs  
bale piṅo tomāra dhana,  
by force  I drink  your  wealth  
tomāra yadi thāke abhimāna  
yours  if  there is  pride

(125) The flute, a brazen man, drinks and drinks the lips of a man and tells the gopīs of its drinking: ‘Listen, O gopīs! I am drinking your wealth by force. If you have any pride,

তবে মোরে ক্রোধ করি’, লজ্জা, ভয়, ধর্ম্ম, ছাড়ি’,  
ছাড়ি’ দিমু, কর আসি’ পান ৷  
নহে পিমু নিরন্তর, তোমায় মোর নাহিক ডর,  
অন্যে দেখোঁ তৃণের সমান ॥ ১২৬ ॥

tabe more krodha kari’,  
then  at me  anger  making  
lajjā bhaya, dharma, chāḍi’,  
shame  fear  duty  giving up  
chāḍi’ dimu, kara āsi’ pāna  
I will give up  do  coming  drinking  
nahe pimu nirantara,  
otherwise  I will drink  constantly  
tomāya mora nāhika ḍara,  
of you  my  is no  fear  
anye dekhom̐ tṛṇera samāna  
others  I see  to straw  equal

(126) then be angry with me, cast off shame, fear and duty, and I will yield it to you: come and drink. Otherwise I shall drink it without end. I have no fear of you, and all others I count as straw.’

অধরামৃত নিজ-স্বরে, সঞ্চারিয়া সেই বলে,  
আকর্ষয় ত্রিজগৎ-জন ৷  
আমরা ধর্ম্ম ভয় করি’, রহি’ যদি ধৈর্য্য ধরি’,  
তবে আমায় করে বিড়ম্বন ॥ ১২৭ ॥

adharāmṛta nija-svare,  
lip-nectar  with its own sound  
sañcāriyā sei bale,  
mixing  by that  force  
ākarṣaya trijagat-jana  
attracts  of the three worlds  the people  
āmarā dharma bhaya kari’,  
we  of duty  fear  having  
rahi’ yadi dhairya dhari’,  
remain  if  composure  holding  
tabe āmāya kare viḍambana  
then  us  it makes  mockery

(127) The flute mixes the nectar of Your lips with its own sound, and by that power it draws the people of the three worlds. And if we, fearing for our duty, try to keep our composure, it makes a mockery of us.

নীবি খসায় গুরু-আগে, লজ্জা-ধর্ম্ম করায় ত্যাগে,  
কেশে ধরি’ যেন লঞা যায় ৷  
আনি’ করায় তোমার দাসী, শুনি’ লোক করে হাসি,  
এইমত নারীরে নাচায় ॥ ১২৮ ॥

nīvi khasāya guru-āge,  
the waist-knot  loosens  before the elders  
lajjā-dharma karāya tyāge,  
shame  duty  makes  give up  
keśe dhari’ yena lañā yāya  
by the hair  seizing  as if  taking  goes  
āni’ karāya tomāra dāsī,  
bringing  makes  Your  maidservants  
śuni’ loka kare hāsi,  
hearing  people  make  laughter  
ei-mata nārīre nācāya  
in this way  women  makes dance

(128) Before our elders it loosens the knots at our waists and makes us abandon shame and duty. As if seizing us by the hair, it leads us away, brings us to You and makes us Your maidservants, and people laugh to hear of it. This is how it makes women dance!

শুষ্ক বাঁশের লাঠিখান, এত করে অপমান,  
এই দশা করিল, গোসাঞি ৷  
না সহি’ কি করিতে পারি, তাহে রহি মৌন ধরি’,  
চোরার মাকে ডাকি’ কান্দিতে নাই ॥ ১২৯ ॥

śuṣka bām̐śera lāṭhikhāna,  
dry  of bamboo  a stick  
eta kare apamāna,  
so much  makes  insult  
ei daśā karila, gosāñi  
this  state  has made  a master  
nā sahi’ ki karite pāri,  
not  bearing  what  to do  can we  
tāhe rahi mauna dhari’,  
so  we remain  silence  keeping  
corāra māke ḍāki’ kāndite nāi  
of the thief  the mother  calling  to weep  not

(129) A stick of dry bamboo heaps such insults on us! Like a master, it has brought us to this state. What can we do but bear it? So we keep silent: when a thief is punished, his mother cannot call out and weep.[^40]

অধরের এই রীতি, আর শুন কুনীতি,  
সে অধর-সনে যার মেলা ৷  
সেই ভক্ষ্য-ভোজ্য-পান, হয় অমৃত-সমান,  
নাম তার হয় ‘কৃষ্ণ ফেলা’ ॥ ১৩০ ॥

adharera ei rīti,  
of the lips  this  the way  
āra śuna kunīti,  
further  hear  the bad custom  
se adhara-sane yāra melā  
those  lips  with  which  meeting  
sei bhakṣya-bhojya-pāna,  
that  food  edible  drink  
haya amṛta-samāna,  
becomes  nectar  equal  
nāma tāra haya ‘kṛṣṇa phelā’  
the name  its  is  Kṛṣṇa  phelā

(130) Such is the way of Your lips. Now hear the bad custom of whatever meets those lips: any food or drink they touch becomes like nectar and is called ‘Kṛṣṇa’s *phelā*’.[^41]

সে ফেলার এক লব, না পায় দেবতা সব,  
এ দম্ভে কেবা পাতিয়ায় ?  
বহুজন্ম পুণ্য করে, তবে ‘সুকৃতি’ নাম ধরে,  
সে ‘সুকৃতে’ তার লব পায় ॥ ১৩১ ॥

se phelāra eka lava,  
that  phelā’s  one  particle  
nā pāya devatā saba,  
not  obtain  the gods  all  
e dambhe kebā pātiyāya?  
this  boast  who  would believe  
bahu-janma puṇya kare,  
many  births  pious acts  does  
tabe ‘sukṛti’ nāma dhare,  
then  sukṛti  the name  takes  
se ‘sukṛte’ tāra lava pāya  
by that  sukṛti  its  particle  obtains

(131) Not one of the gods can obtain a particle of that *phelā* — who would believe such a boast? Only piety gathered over many births bears the name *sukṛti*, and by that *sukṛti* one obtains a particle of it.

কৃষ্ণ যে খায় তাম্বূল, কহে তার নাহি মূল,  
তাহে আর দম্ভ পরিপাটী ৷  
তার যেবা উদগার, তারে কয় ‘অমৃতসার’,  
গোপীর মুখ করে ‘আলবাটী’ ॥ ১৩২ ॥

kṛṣṇa ye khāya tāmbūla,  
Kṛṣṇa  which  chews  betel  
kahe tāra nāhi mūla,  
they say  its  is no  price  
tāhe āra dambha paripāṭī  
to that  further  of boasting  the artfulness  
tāra yebā udagāra,  
its  whatever  chewed remnant  
tāre kaya ‘amṛta-sāra’,  
that  they call  nectar’s  essence  
gopīra mukha kare ‘ālabāṭī’  
the gopīs’  mouths  they make  a bowl for saliva

(132) The betel Kṛṣṇa chews is said to be beyond price, and on top of that comes this artful boast: what He has chewed they call ‘the essence of nectar’, and they make the gopīs’ mouths its cup, its *ālabāṭī*.[^42]

এসব — তোমার কুটিনাটি, ছাড় এই পরিপাটী,  
বেণুদ্বারে কাঁহে হর’ প্রাণ ৷  
আপনার হাসি লাগি’, নহ নারীর বধভাগী,  
দেহ’ নিজাধরামৃত দান ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

e-saba — tomāra kuṭināṭi,  
all this  Your  wiles  
chāḍa ei paripāṭī,  
give up  this  artfulness  
veṇu-dvāre kām̐he hara’ prāṇa  
by the flute  why  You steal  lives  
āpanāra hāsi lāgi’,  
for Your own  amusement  for the sake of  
naha nārīra vadha-bhāgī,  
do not be  of women  the killing  sharer  
deha’ nijādharāmṛta dāna  
give  Your own lip-nectar  as a gift

(133) All this is Your trickery. Give up this artfulness! Why do You steal our lives with Your flute? Do not, for Your own amusement, make Yourself guilty of killing women. Give us the gift of the nectar of Your lips.”[^43]

কহিতে কহিতে প্রভুর মন ফিরি’ গেল ৷  
ক্রোধ মন শান্ত হৈল, উৎকণ্ঠা বাড়িল ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

kahite kahite prabhura mana phiri’ gela  
speaking  speaking  the Lord’s  mind  turned  went  
krodha mana śānta haila, utkaṇṭhā bāḍila  
angry  mind  calm  became  longing  increased

(134) As He spoke, the Lord’s mood changed: His angry mind grew calm, and His longing increased.

পরম দুর্ল্লভ এই কৃষ্ণাধরামৃত ৷  
তাহা যেই পায়, তার সফল জীবিত ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

parama durllabha ei kṛṣṇādharāmṛta  
supremely  rare  this  Kṛṣṇa’s lip-nectar  
tāhā yei pāya, tāra saphala jīvita  
it  whoever  obtains  his  successful  life

(135) “This nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips is supremely rare. Whoever obtains it has found the fruit of life.

যোগ্য হঞা কেহ করিতে না পায় পান ৷  
তথাপি সে নির্লজ্জ, বৃথা ধরে প্রাণ ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

yogya hañā keha karite nā pāya pāna  
worthy  being  someone  to do  not  gets  drinking  
tathāpi se nirlajja, vṛthā dhare prāṇa  
still  he  shameless  in vain  holds  life

(136) If one who is worthy cannot drink it and still clings to life, he is shameless and lives in vain.

অযোগ্য হঞা তাহা কেহ সদা পান করে ৷  
যোগ্য জন নাহি পায়, লোভে মাত্র মরে ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

ayogya hañā tāhā keha sadā pāna kare  
unworthy  being  it  someone  always  drinking  does  
yogya jana nāhi pāya, lobhe mātra mare  
worthy  person  not  gets  from longing  only  dies

(137) The unworthy drink it constantly, while the worthy do not get it and only die of longing.

তাতে জানি, — কোন তপস্যার আছে বল ৷  
অযোগ্যেরে দেওয়ায় কৃষ্ণাধরামৃত-ফল ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

tāte jāni, — kona tapasyāra āche bala  
therefore  I know  some  austerity’s  is  power  
ayogyere deoyāya kṛṣṇādharāmṛta-phala  
to the unworthy  gives  Kṛṣṇa’s lip-nectar  the fruit

(138) So I know there is power in some austerity: as its fruit, the unworthy are given the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips.

‘কহ রাম-রায়, কিছু শুনিতে হয় মন’ ৷  
ভাব জানি’ পড়ে রায় গোপীর বচন ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

‘kaha rāma-rāya, kichu śunite haya mana’  
tell  Rāma Rāya  something  to hear  is  My mind  
bhāva jāni’ paḍe rāya gopīra vacana  
the mood  knowing  recites  Rāya  of the gopīs  the words

(139) Tell Me something, Rāmarāya — My mind longs to hear.” Knowing His mood, Rāmānanda recited the words of the gopīs:

গোপ্যঃ কিমাচরদয়ং কুশলং স্ম বেণু-  
র্দামোদরাধরসুধামপি গোপিকানাম্ ৷  
ভুঙ্ক্তে স্বয়ং যদবশিষ্টরসং হ্রদিন্যো  
হৃষ্যত্ত্বচোঽশ্রু মুমুচুস্তরবো যথার্য্যাঃ ॥ ১৪০ ॥

gopyaḥ kim ācarad ayaṁ kuśalaṁ sma veṇur  
O gopīs  what  performed  this  good deed  indeed  flute  
dāmodarādhara-sudhām api gopikānām  
of Dāmodara  of the lips  the nectar  even  of the gopīs  
bhuṅkte svayaṁ yad avaśiṣṭa-rasaṁ hradinyo  
enjoys  itself  of which  the left-over  taste  the rivers  
hṛṣyat-tvaco ’śru mumucus taravo yathāryāḥ  
thrilled  in skin  tears  shed  the trees  like  the elders

(140) “O gopīs, what good deed did this flute perform, that it enjoys of its own accord the nectar of Dāmodara’s lips, which belongs to the gopīs, leaving only a taste of it behind? The rivers that nourished it thrill with joy, their lotuses like hair standing on end, and the trees shed tears, like elders of a family rejoicing in a descendant.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.9)[^44]

এই শ্লোক শুনি’ প্রভু ভাবাবিষ্ট হঞা ৷  
উৎকণ্ঠাতে অর্থ করে প্রলাপ করিয়া ॥ ১৪১ ॥

ei śloka śuni’ prabhu bhāvāviṣṭa hañā  
this  verse  hearing  the Lord  absorbed in feeling  becoming  
utkaṇṭhāte artha kare pralāpa kariyā  
in longing  the meaning  makes  raving  doing

(141) Hearing this verse, the Lord became absorbed in feeling, and in His longing He explained its meaning in raving words.

অহো ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন, ব্রজের কোন কন্যাগণ,  
অবশ্য করিব পরিণয় ৷  
সে-সম্বন্ধে গোপীগণ, যারে জানে নিজধন,  
সে সুধা অন্যের লভ্য নয় ॥ ১৪২ ॥

aho vrajendra-nandana,  
ah  of the king of Vraja  the son  
vrajera kona kanyā-gaṇa,  
of Vraja  some  maidens  
avaśya kariba pariṇaya  
certainly  will make  marriage  
se-sambandhe gopī-gaṇa,  
by that  relation  the gopīs  
yāre jāne nija-dhana,  
which  know  their own wealth  
se sudhā anyera labhya naya  
that  nectar  for others  attainable  is not

(142) “Ah, the son of the king of Vraja will certainly marry some maidens of Vraja. Because of that bond the gopīs know this nectar as their own wealth, and it is not for others to attain.[^45]

গোপীগণ, কহ সব করিয়া বিচারে ৷  
কোন্ তীর্থ, কোন্ তপ, কোন্ সিদ্ধমন্ত্র-জপ,  
এই বেণু কৈল জন্মান্তরে ? ॥ ১৪৩ ॥ধ্রু॥

gopī-gaṇa, kaha saba kariyā vicāre  
O gopīs  tell  all  making  consideration  
kon tīrtha, kon tapa,  
which  holy place  which  austerity  
kon siddha-mantra-japa,  
which  perfected  mantra  chanting  
ei veṇu kaila janmāntare?  
this  flute  did  in a former birth

(143) O gopīs, think it over together and tell Me: what holy place, what austerity, what chanting of a perfect mantra did this flute perform in a former birth?[^46]

হেন কৃষ্ণাধর-সুধা, যে কৈল অমৃত-মুদা,  
যার আশায় গোপী ধরে প্রাণ ৷  
এই বেণু অযোগ্য অতি, স্থাবর ‘পুরুষ জাতি’,  
সেই সুধা সদা করে পান ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

hena kṛṣṇādhara-sudhā,  
such  Kṛṣṇa’s lips’  nectar  
ye kaila amṛta-mudā,  
which  made  nectar  eclipsed  
yāra āśāya gopī dhare prāṇa  
of which  in the hope  the gopīs  hold  life  
ei veṇu ayogya ati,  
this  flute  unworthy  very  
sthāvara ‘puruṣa jāti’,  
unmoving  of the male  sex  
sei sudhā sadā kare pāna  
that  nectar  always  makes  drinking

(144) Such is the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips: it has eclipsed nectar itself, and only in the hope of it do the gopīs keep their lives. Yet this flute, utterly unworthy, an unmoving thing, and of the male sex besides, drinks that nectar all the time.[^47]

যার ধন, না কহে তারে, পান করে বলাৎকারে,  
পিতে তারে ডাকিয়া জানায় ৷  
তার তপস্যার ফল, দেখ ইহার ভাগ্য-বল,  
ইহার উচ্ছিষ্ট মহাজনে খায় ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

yāra dhana, nā kahe tāre,  
whose  wealth  not  tells  them  
pāna kare balātkāre,  
drinking  makes  by force  
pite tāre ḍākiyā jānāya  
to drink  them  calling  announces  
tāra tapasyāra phala,  
its  austerity’s  the fruit  
dekha ihāra bhāgya-bala,  
see  its  fortune’s  power  
ihāra ucchiṣṭa mahā-jane khāya  
its  remnants  the great  eat

(145) Without asking those to whom this wealth belongs, it drinks it by force and loudly calls them to come and drink. See the fruit of its austerity, the power of its good fortune: even the great partake of its remnants.[^48]

মানসগঙ্গা, কালিন্দী, ভুবন-পাবনী নদী,  
কৃষ্ণ যদি তাতে করে স্নান ৷  
বেণু-ঝুটাধর রস, হঞা লোভে পরবশ,  
সেই কালে হর্ষে করে পান ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

mānasa-gaṅgā, kālindī,  
the Mānasa-gaṅgā  the Kālindī  
bhuvana-pāvanī nadī,  
the world  purifying  rivers  
kṛṣṇa yadi tāte kare snāna  
Kṛṣṇa  when  in them  makes  bath  
veṇu-jhuṭādhara-rasa,  
of the flute  left over  of the lips  the taste  
hañā lobhe paravaśa,  
being  by longing  overpowered  
sei kāle harṣe kare pāna  
at that  time  with joy  make  drinking

(146) The Mānasa-gaṅgā and the Kālindī are rivers that purify the world. When Kṛṣṇa bathes in them, they are overpowered by longing and joyfully drink the taste of His lips left over by the flute.

এ-ত নদী রহু দূরে, বৃক্ষ সব তার তীরে,  
তপ করে পর-উপকারী ৷  
নদীর শেষ-রস পাঞা, মূলদ্বারে আকর্ষিয়া,  
কেনে পিয়ে, বুঝিতে না পারি ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

e-ta nadī rahu dūre,  
these  rivers  let remain  far  
vṛkṣa saba tāra tīre,  
trees  all  their  on the banks  
tapa kare para-upakārī  
austerity  perform  to others  beneficent  
nadīra śeṣa-rasa pāñā,  
of the rivers  the left-over  taste  getting  
mūla-dvāre ākarṣiyā,  
by the roots  drawing up  
kene piye, bujhite nā pāri  
why  they drink  to understand  not  I can

(147) But let alone the rivers! The trees on their banks perform austerities and do good to others; yet they take the taste the rivers have left, draw it up through their roots and drink it — why, I cannot understand.[^49][^50]

নিজাঙ্কুরে পুলকিত, পুষ্পে হাস্য বিকসিত,  
মধু-মিষে বহে অশ্রুধার ৷  
বেণুরে মানি’ নিজ জাতি, আর্য্যের যেন পুত্র-নাতি,  
‘বৈষ্ণব’ হৈলে আনন্দ-বিকার ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

nijāṅkure pulakita,  
with their own shoots  thrilled  
puṣpe hāsya vikasita,  
in flowers  laughter  blossoming  
madhu-miṣe vahe aśru-dhāra  
of honey  in the guise  flow  of tears  streams  
veṇure māni’ nija jāti,  
the flute  regarding  as their own  kin  
āryera yena putra-nāti,  
of a noble man  as  sons  grandsons  
‘vaiṣṇava’ haile ānanda-vikāra  
Vaiṣṇavas  becoming  of bliss  transformation

(148) Their new shoots are their hair standing on end, their flowers are blossoming laughter, and in the guise of honey streams of tears flow from them. They regard the flute as their own kin and are overcome with bliss, as a noble man is when his sons and grandsons become Vaiṣṇavas.

বেণুর তপ জানি যবে, সেই তপ করি তবে,  
এ — অযোগ্য, আমরা — যোগ্যা নারী ৷  
যা না পাঞা দুঃখে মরি, অযোগ্য পিয়ে সইতে নারি,  
তাহা লাগি’ তপস্যা বিচারি ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

veṇura tapa jāni yabe,  
the flute’s  austerity  we know  when  
sei tapa kari tabe,  
that  austerity  we will do  then  
e — ayogya, āmarā — yogyā nārī  
it  unworthy  we  worthy  women  
yā nā pāñā duḥkhe mari,  
what  not  getting  in grief  we die  
ayogya piye saite nāri,  
the unworthy  drinks  to bear  we cannot  
tāhā lāgi’ tapasyā vicāri  
that  for the sake of  austerity  we consider

(149) When we know what austerity the flute performed, we will perform the same. It is unworthy, and we are worthy women. We are dying of grief for want of what the unworthy one drinks, and we cannot bear it. That is why we are pondering its austerity.”

এতেক বিলাপ করি’, প্রেমাবেশে গৌরহরি,  
সঙ্গে লঞা স্বরূপ-রামরায় ৷  
কভু নাচে, কভু গায়, ভাবাবেশে মূর্চ্ছা যায়,  
এইরূপে রাত্রি দিন-যায় ॥ ১৫০ ॥

eteka vilāpa kari’,  
so much  lamentation  making  
premāveśe gaurahari,  
absorbed in love  Gaurahari  
saṅge lañā svarūpa-rāma-rāya  
with  taking  Svarūpa  Rāma Rāya  
kabhu nāce, kabhu gāya,  
sometimes  dances  sometimes  sings  
bhāvāveśe mūrcchā yāya,  
in absorption of feeling  into a swoon  goes  
ei-rūpe rātri dina-yāya  
in this way  night  day  passes

(150) Thus Gaurahari lamented, absorbed in love. With Svarūpa and Rāmarāya at His side He sometimes danced, sometimes sang, and sometimes, overcome by feeling, fell into a swoon. In this way His nights and days passed.[^51]

স্বরূপ, রূপ, সনাতন, রঘুনাথের শ্রীচরণ,  
শিরে ধরি’ করি যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত, অমৃত হৈতে পরামৃত,  
গায় দীনহীন কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১৫১ ॥

svarūpa, rūpa, sanātana,  
of Svarūpa  Rūpa  Sanātana  
raghunāthera śrī-caraṇa,  
of Raghunātha  the holy feet  
śire dhari’ kari yāra āśa  
on my head  placing  I make  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta,  
the Caitanya-caritāmṛta  
amṛta haite parāmṛta,  
than nectar  surpassing nectar  
gāya dīna-hīna kṛṣṇadāsa  
sings  lowly  and fallen  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(151) Placing on my head the holy feet of Svarūpa, Rūpa, Sanātana and Raghunātha, and hoping for their mercy, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, lowly and fallen, sing the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, a nectar surpassing nectar.
