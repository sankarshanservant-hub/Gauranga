“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 3  
**The Description of the Acceptance of Sannyāsa and the Feast in Advaita’s House**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

ন্যাসং বিধায়োৎপ্রণয়োঽথ গৌরো  
বৃন্দাবনং গন্তুমনা ভ্রমাদ্ যঃ ৷  
রাঢ়ে ভ্রমন্ শান্তিপুরীময়িত্বা  
ললাস ভক্তৈরিহ তং নতো ’স্মি ॥ ১ ॥

nyāsaṁ vidhāyotpraṇayo ’tha gauro  
sannyāsa  having accepted  full of love  then  Gaura  
vṛndāvanaṁ gantu-manā bhramād yaḥ  
to Vṛndāvana  intent on going  in bewilderment  who  
rāḍhe bhraman śānti-purīm ayitvā  
in Rāḍha  wandering  Śāntipura  having reached  
lalāsa bhaktair iha taṁ nato ’smi  
enjoyed  with the devotees  here  to Him  bowed  am I

(1) After accepting sannyāsa, Gaura, overflowing with love, longed to go to Vṛndāvana; but, lost to the outer world, He wandered through Rāḍha. Then He came to Śāntipura and rejoiced there with His devotees. I bow down to Him.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

চব্বিশ বৎসর শেষ যেই মাঘ-মাস ৷  
তার শুক্লপক্ষে প্রভু করিলা সন্ন্যাস ॥ ৩ ॥

cabbiśa vatsara śeṣa yei māgha-māsa  
of the twenty-fourth  year  at the end  which  the month of Māgha  
tāra śukla-pakṣe prabhu karilā sannyāsa  
in its  bright fortnight  the Lord  took  sannyāsa

(3) At the close of His twenty-fourth year, in the bright fortnight of the month of Māgha, the Lord accepted *sannyāsa*.[^3]

সন্ন্যাস করি’ প্রেমাবেশে চলিলা বৃন্দাবন ৷  
রাঢ়-দেশে তিন দিন করিলা ভ্রমণ ॥ ৪ ॥

sannyāsa kari’ premāveśe calilā vṛndāvana  
sannyāsa  having accepted  absorbed in love  set out  for Vṛndāvana  
rāḍha-deśe tina dina karilā bhramaṇa  
in the land of Rāḍha  three  days  did  wandering

(4) After accepting sannyāsa, absorbed in love, He set out for Vṛndāvana, but for three days He wandered in the land of Rāḍha.[^4]

এই শ্লোক পড়ি’ প্রভু ভাবের আবেশে ৷  
ভ্রমিতে পবিত্র কৈল সব রাঢ়-দেশে ॥ ৫ ॥

ei śloka paḍi’ prabhu bhāvera āveśe  
this  verse  reciting  the Lord  of ecstasy  in absorption  
bhramite pavitra kaila saba rāḍha-deśe  
by wandering  pure  made  all  the land of Rāḍha

(5) Reciting this verse in the rapture of His feelings, the Lord wandered on and purified the whole land of Rāḍha:

এতাং সমাস্থায় পরাত্মনিষ্ঠা-  
মুপাসিতাং পূর্ব্বতমৈর্মহদ্ভিঃ ৷  
অহং তরিষ্যামি দুরন্তপারং  
তমো মুকুন্দাঙ্ঘ্রিনিষেবয়ৈব ॥ ৬ ॥

etāṁ sama āsthāya parātma-niṣṭhām  
this  fully  having taken up  devotion to the Supreme Self  
upāsitāṁ pūrvatamair mahadbhiḥ  
worshipped  by the most ancient  great souls  
ahaṁ tariṣyāmi duranta-pāraṁ  
I  shall cross  whose far shore is hard to reach  
tamo mukundāṅghri-niṣevayaiva  
the darkness  of Mukunda’s feet  by service  alone

(6) “I shall take up this devotion to the Supreme Self (*parātma-niṣṭhā*), which the great souls of old embraced; and by service to the feet of Mukunda alone I shall cross the darkness whose far shore is so hard to reach.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.23.57)[^5]

প্রভু কহে, — সাধু এই ভিক্ষুর বচন ৷  
মুকুন্দ-সেবন-ব্রত কৈল নির্দ্ধারণ ॥ ৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — sādhu ei bhikṣura vacana  
the Lord  said  excellent  these  of the mendicant  words  
mukunda-sevana-vrata kaila nirddhāraṇa  
to Mukunda  of service  the vow  has made  firm resolve

(7) The Lord said, “How excellent are this mendicant’s words! He has firmly resolved on the vow of serving Mukunda.

পরাত্মনিষ্ঠা-মাত্র বেষ-ধারণ ৷  
মুকুন্দ-সেবায় হয় সংসার-তারণ ॥ ৮ ॥

parātma-niṣṭhā-mātra veṣa-dhāraṇa  
devotion to the Supreme Self  only for  the dress  wearing  
mukunda-sevāya haya saṁsāra-tāraṇa  
by service to Mukunda  comes about  from saṁsāra  deliverance

(8) The dress of renunciation is worn only for the sake of devotion to the Supreme Self, and it is service to Mukunda that delivers one from saṁsāra.[^6]

সেই বেশ কৈল, এবে বৃন্দাবন গিয়া ৷  
কৃষ্ণনিষেবণ করি’ নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥ ৯ ॥

sei veśa kaila, ebe vṛndāvana giyā  
that  dress  I have taken  now  to Vṛndāvana  having gone  
kṛṣṇa-niṣevaṇa kari’ nibhṛte vasiyā  
Kṛṣṇa  service  doing  in solitude  sitting

(9) I have taken that dress; now I shall go to Vṛndāvana and serve Kṛṣṇa, settled in solitude.”

এত বলি’ চলে প্রভু প্রেমোন্মাদের চিহ্ন ৷  
দিক্-বিদিক্-জ্ঞান নাহি, কিবা রাত্রি-দিন ॥ ১০ ॥

eta bali’ cale prabhu premonmādera cihna  
thus  saying  goes  the Lord  of love’s madness  with the signs  
dik-vidik-jñāna nāhi, kibā rātri-dina  
of directions  awareness  none  whether  night or day

(10) Saying this, the Lord went on, showing the signs of love’s madness. He had no sense of direction and did not know whether it was night or day.

নিত্যানন্দ, আচার্য্যরত্ন, মুকুন্দ, — তিন জন ৷  
প্রভু-পাছে-পাছে তিনে করেন গমন ॥ ১১ ॥

nityānanda, ācāryaratna, mukunda, — tina jana  
Nityānanda  Ācāryaratna  Mukunda  three  persons  
prabhu-pāche-pāche tine karena gamana  
the Lord  behind  behind  the three  make  going

(11) Nityānanda, Ācāryaratna and Mukunda — these three followed close behind the Lord.

যেই যেই প্রভু দেখে, সেই সেই লোক ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে ‘হরি’ বলে, খণ্ডে দুঃখ-শোক ॥ ১২ ॥

yei yei prabhu dekhe, sei sei loka  
whoever  whoever  the Lord  sees  those  those  people  
premāveśe ‘hari’ bale, khaṇḍe duḥkha-śoka  
in ecstatic love  “Hari”  say  is cut off  sorrow and grief

(12) Whoever saw the Lord cried out “Hari!”, overcome with love, and all their sorrow and grief were cut away.

গোপ-বালক সব প্রভুকে দেখিয়া ৷  
‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলি’ ডাকে উচ্চ করিয়া ॥ ১৩ ॥

gopa-bālaka saba prabhuke dekhiyā  
cowherd  boys  all  the Lord  seeing  
‘hari’ ‘hari’ bali’ ḍāke ucca kariyā  
“Hari”  “Hari”  saying  call  loudly  making

(13) All the cowherd boys, on seeing the Lord, called out loudly, “Hari! Hari!”

শুনি’ তা-সবার নিকট গেলা গৌরহরি ৷  
‘বল’ ‘বল’ বলে সবার শিরে হস্ত ধরি’ ॥ ১৪ ॥

śuni’ tā-sabāra nikaṭa gelā gaurahari  
hearing  them all  near  went  Gaurahari  
‘bala’ ‘bala’ bale sabāra śire hasta dhari’  
“say it”  “say it”  says  of all  on the heads  hand  placing

(14) Hearing this, Gaurahari went up to them, placed His hand on each one’s head and said, “Say it! Say it!”

তা’-সবার স্তুতি করে, — তোমরা ভাগ্যবান্ ৷  
কৃতার্থ করিলে মোরে শুনাঞা হরিনাম ॥ ১৫ ॥

tā’-sabāra stuti kare, — tomarā bhāgyavān  
them all  praise  does  you  are fortunate  
kṛtārtha karile more śunāñā hari-nāma  
fulfilled  have made  Me  by making Me hear  the name of Hari

(15) He praised them all: “You are fortunate! You have fulfilled My life by letting Me hear the name of Hari.”

গুপ্তে তা’-সবাকে আনি’ ঠাকুর নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
শিখাইল সবাকারে করিয়া প্রবন্ধ ॥ ১৬ ॥

gupte tā’-sabāke āni’ ṭhākura nityānanda  
secretly  them all  bringing  Ṭhākura  Nityānanda  
śikhāila sabākāre kariyā prabandha  
instructed  all of them  having made  a plan

(16) Meanwhile Ṭhākura Nityānanda gathered the boys in secret and, having made up a fitting tale, taught them all what to say:[^7]

বৃন্দাবন পথ প্রভু পুছেন তোমারে ৷  
গঙ্গাতীর-পথ তবে দেখাইহ তাঁরে ॥ ১৭ ॥

vṛndāvana patha prabhu puchena tomāre  
of Vṛndāvana  the way  the Lord  asks  you  
gaṅgā-tīra-patha tabe dekhāiha tām̐re  
along the bank of the Ganges  the path  then  show  Him

(17) “When the Lord asks you the way to Vṛndāvana, show Him the path along the bank of the Gaṅgā.”

তবে প্রভু পুছিলেন, — ‘শুন, শিশুগণ ৷  
কহ দেখি, কোন্ পথে যাব বৃন্দাবন’ ॥ ১৮ ॥

tabe prabhu puchilena, — ‘śuna, śiśu-gaṇa  
then  the Lord  asked  listen  children  
kaha dekhi, kon pathe yāba vṛndāvana’  
tell  please  by which  path  shall I go  to Vṛndāvana

(18) Then the Lord asked, “Listen, children! Tell Me, which way should I take to Vṛndāvana?”

শিশু সব গঙ্গাতীরপথ দেখাইল ৷  
সেই পথে আবেশে প্রভু গমন করিল ॥ ১৯ ॥

śiśu saba gaṅgātīrapatha dekhāila  
the children  all  the path along the Ganges’ bank  showed  
sei pathe āveśe prabhu gamana karila  
that  by path  in absorption  the Lord  going  made

(19) The children showed Him the path along the bank of the Gaṅgā, and the Lord, absorbed in rapture, went that way.[^8]

আচার্য্যরত্নেরে কহে নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞি ৷  
শীঘ্র যাহ তুমি অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্যের ঠাঞি ॥ ২০ ॥

ācāryaratnere kahe nityānanda-gosāñi  
to Ācāryaratna  says  Nityānanda Gosāñi  
śīghra yāha tumi advaita-ācāryera ṭhāñi  
quickly  go  you  of Advaita Ācārya  to the place

(20) Nityānanda Gosāñi said to Ācāryaratna, “Go quickly to Advaita Ācārya.[^9]

প্রভু লয়ে যাব আমি তাঁহার মন্দিরে ৷  
সাবধানে রহেন যেন নৌকা লঞা তীরে ॥ ২১ ॥

prabhu laye yāba āmi tām̐hāra mandire  
the Lord  taking  shall go  I  His  to the house  
sāvadhāne rahena yena naukā lañā tīre  
ready  let Him stay  so that  a boat  having taken  on the bank

(21) I shall bring the Lord to His house. Let Him wait ready on the bank with a boat.

তবে নবদ্বীপে তুমি করিহ গমন ৷  
শচী-মাতা লঞা আইস, আর ভক্তগণ ॥ ২২ ॥

tabe navadvīpe tumi kariha gamana  
then  to Navadvīpa  you  make  going  
śacī-mātā lañā āisa, āra bhakta-gaṇa  
Mother Śacī  taking  come  and  the devotees

(22) Then go to Navadvīpa and come back with Mother Śacī and the other devotees.”[^10]

তাঁরে পাঠাইয়া নিত্যানন্দ মহাশয় ৷  
মহাপ্রভুর আগে আসি’ দিল পরিচয় ॥ ২৩ ॥

tām̐re pāṭhāiyā nityānanda mahāśaya  
him  having sent  Nityānanda  the great soul  
mahāprabhura āge āsi’ dila paricaya  
of Mahāprabhu  before  coming  gave  introduction

(23) Having sent him off, the great-hearted Nityānanda came before Mahāprabhu and made Himself known.

প্রভু কহে, — শ্রীপাদ, তোমার কোথাকে গমন ৷  
শ্রীপাদ কহে, তোমার সঙ্গে যাব বৃন্দাবন ॥ ২৪ ॥

prabhu kahe, — śrīpāda, tomāra kothāke gamana  
the Lord  says  Śrīpāda  Your  where  going  
śrīpāda kahe, tomāra saṅge yāba vṛndāvana  
Śrīpāda  says  You  with  shall go  to Vṛndāvana

(24) The Lord said, “Śrīpāda, where are You going?” Śrīpāda answered, “I am going with You to Vṛndāvana.”

প্রভু কহে, কত দূরে আছে বৃন্দাবন ৷  
তিঁহো কহেন, — কর এই যমুনা দরশন ॥ ২৫ ॥

prabhu kahe, kata dūre āche vṛndāvana  
the Lord  says  how  far  is  Vṛndāvana  
tim̐ho kahena, — kara ei yamunā daraśana  
He  says  take  this  of the Yamunā  sight

(25) The Lord asked, “How far is Vṛndāvana?” Nityānanda replied, “Look, here is the Yamunā!”

এত বলি’ আনিল তাঁরে গঙ্গা-সন্নিধানে ৷  
আবেশে প্রভুর হৈল গঙ্গারে যমুনা-জ্ঞানে ॥ ২৬ ॥

eta bali’ ānila tām̐re gaṅgā-sannidhāne  
thus  saying  brought  Him  to the Ganges’  vicinity  
āveśe prabhura haila gaṅgāre yamunā-jñāne  
in ecstasy  the Lord’s  arose  the Ganges  as the Yamunā perceiving

(26) With these words He brought Mahāprabhu to the Gaṅgā, and in His rapture the Lord saw the Gaṅgā as the Yamunā.[^11]

অহো ভাগ্য, যমুনারে পাইলুঁ দরশন ৷  
এত বলি’ যমুনার করেন স্তবন ॥ ২৭ ॥

aho bhāgya, yamunāre pāilum̐ daraśana  
oh  fortune  of the Yamunā  I have obtained  sight  
eta bali’ yamunāra karena stavana  
thus  saying  of the Yamunā  makes  praise

(27) “Oh, what fortune! I have seen the Yamunā!” Saying this, He began to sing her praises:

চিদানন্দভানোঃ সদা নন্দসূনোঃ  
পরপ্রেমপাত্রী দ্রবব্রহ্মগাত্রী ৷  
অঘানাং লবিত্রী জগৎক্ষেমধাত্রী  
পবিত্রীক্রিয়ান্নো বপুর্মিত্রপুত্রী ॥ ২৮ ॥

cid-ānanda-bhānoḥ sadā nanda-sūnoḥ  
of consciousness and bliss  of the sun  always  of Nanda’s son  
para-prema-pātrī drava-brahma-gātrī  
of supreme love  the object  liquid Brahman  whose body  
aghānāṁ lavitrī jagat-kṣema-dhātrī  
of sins  the cutter  of the world  welfare  bestower  
pavitrī-kriyān no vapur mitra-putrī  
may she purify  our  bodies  of the sun  the daughter

(28) “She is ever the object of the supreme love of Nanda’s son, the sun of consciousness and bliss. Her body is Brahman become water; she cuts away all sins and brings good to the world. May the daughter of the Sun purify our bodies!” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 5.13)[^12]

এত বলি’ নমস্করি’ কৈল গঙ্গাস্নান ৷  
এক কৌপীন, নাহি দ্বিতীয় পরিধান ॥ ২৯ ॥

eta bali’ namaskari’ kaila gaṅgā-snāna  
thus  saying  bowing down  did  bath in the Ganges  
eka kaupīna, nāhi dvitīya paridhāna  
one  kaupīna  not  second  garment

(29) Saying this, He bowed down and bathed in the Gaṅgā. He had only a single *kaupīna* and no other cloth.

হেনকালে আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি নৌকাতে চড়িঞা ৷  
আইল নূতন কৌপীন-বহির্ব্বাস লঞা ॥ ৩০ ॥

hena kāle ācārya-gosāñi naukāte caḍiñā  
at such  a time  Ācārya Gosāñi  in a boat  having boarded  
āila nūtana kaupīna-bahirvāsa lañā  
came  new  kaupīna and outer cloth  bringing

(30) Just then Ācārya Gosāñi arrived by boat, bringing a new *kaupīna* and outer cloth.

আগে আচার্য্য আসি’ রহিলা নমস্কার করি’ ৷  
আচার্য্য দেখি’ বলে প্রভু মনে সংশয় করি’ ॥ ৩১ ॥

āge ācārya āsi’ rahilā namaskāra kari’  
in front  the Ācārya  coming  stood  obeisance  having made  
ācārya dekhi’ bale prabhu mane saṁśaya kari’  
the Ācārya  seeing  says  the Lord  in mind  doubt  feeling

(31) The Ācārya came before Him, bowed down and stood waiting. At the sight of the Ācārya the Lord was puzzled at heart, and He said:[^13]

তুমি ত’ আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি, এথা কেনে আইলা ৷  
আমি বৃন্দাবনে, তুমি কেমতে জানিলা ॥ ৩২ ॥

tumi ta’ ācārya-gosāñi, ethā kene āilā  
You  surely  Ācārya Gosāñi  here  why  have come  
āmi vṛndāvane, tumi ke-mate jānilā  
I  in Vṛndāvana  You  how  knew

(32) “You are Ācārya Gosāñi, aren’t You? Why have You come here? How did You know that I was in Vṛndāvana?”

আচার্য্য কহে, — তুমি যাঁহা, সেই বৃন্দাবন ৷  
মোর ভাগ্যে গঙ্গাতীরে তোমার আগমন ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ācārya kahe, — tumi yām̐hā, sei vṛndāvana  
the Ācārya  says  You  wherever  that  is Vṛndāvana  
mora bhāgye gaṅgā-tīre tomāra āgamana  
by My  fortune  to the Ganges’ bank  Your  coming

(33) The Ācārya said, “Wherever You are, that is Vṛndāvana. It is My good fortune that You have come to the bank of the Gaṅgā.”

প্রভু কহে, — নিত্যানন্দ আমারে বঞ্চিলা ৷  
গঙ্গাকে আনিয়া মোরে যমুনা কহিলা ॥ ৩৪ ॥

prabhu kahe, — nityānanda āmāre vañcilā  
the Lord  says  Nityānanda  Me  has deceived  
gaṅgāke āniyā more yamunā kahilā  
to the Ganges  bringing  to Me  the Yamunā  called it

(34) The Lord said, “Nityānanda has deceived Me! He brought Me to the Gaṅgā and told Me it was the Yamunā.”[^14]

আচার্য্য কহে, মিথ্যা নহে শ্রীপাদ-বচন ৷  
যমুনাতে স্নান তুমি করিলা এখন ॥ ৩৫ ॥

ācārya kahe, mithyā nahe śrīpāda-vacana  
the Ācārya  says  false  is not  Śrīpāda’s words  
yamunāte snāna tumi karilā ekhana  
in the Yamunā  bath  You  took  just now

(35) The Ācārya said, “Śrīpāda’s words are not false. Just now You did bathe in the Yamunā.

গঙ্গায় যমুনা বহে হঞা একধার ৷  
পশ্চিমে যমুনা বহে, পূর্ব্বে গঙ্গাধার ॥ ৩৬ ॥

gaṅgāya yamunā vahe hañā eka-dhāra  
in the Ganges  the Yamunā  flows  becoming  one stream  
paścime yamunā vahe, pūrve gaṅgā-dhāra  
on the west  the Yamunā  flows  on the east  the Ganges’ stream

(36) Here the Yamunā flows in the Gaṅgā, and the two run as one stream: on the west flows the Yamunā, on the east the Gaṅgā.

পশ্চিমধারে যমুনা বহে, তাঁহা কৈলে স্নান ৷  
আর্দ্র কৌপীন ছাড়ি’ শুষ্ক কর পরিধান ॥ ৩৭ ॥

paścima-dhāre yamunā vahe, tām̐hā kaile snāna  
in the western stream  the Yamunā  flows  there  You took  bath  
ārdra kaupīna chāḍi’ śuṣka kara paridhāna  
wet  kaupīna  giving up  dry  make  wearing

(37) The Yamunā flows in the western stream, and that is where You bathed. Now take off Your wet *kaupīna* and put on a dry one.

প্রেমাবেশে তিন দিন আছ উপবাস ৷  
আজি মোর ঘরে ভিক্ষা, চল মোর বাস ॥ ৩৮ ॥

premāveśe tina dina ācha upavāsa  
in ecstatic love  three  days  You have been  fasting  
āji mora ghare bhikṣā, cala mora vāsa  
today  in My  house  alms  come  to My  dwelling

(38) Absorbed in love, You have been fasting for three days. Today take Your alms in My house; come home with Me.

এক মুষ্টি অন্ন মুঞি করিয়াছোঁ পাক ৷  
শুখারুখা ব্যঞ্জন কৈলুঁ, সূপ আর শাক ॥ ৩৯ ॥

eka muṣṭi anna muñi kariyāchom̐ pāka  
one  handful  of rice  I  have done  cooking  
śukhārukhā vyañjana kailum̐, sūpa āra śāka  
plain  vegetables  I made  dāl  and  greens

(39) I have cooked just a handful of rice and made some plain vegetables, dāl and greens.”[^15]

এত বলি’ নৌকায় চড়াঞা নিল নিজ-ঘর ৷  
পাদপ্রক্ষালন কৈল আনন্দ-অন্তর ॥ ৪০ ॥

eta bali’ naukāya caḍāñā nila nija-ghara  
thus  saying  in the boat  seating  took  to His own house  
pāda-prakṣālana kaila ānanda-antara  
of the feet  washing  did  joyful at heart

(40) Saying this, He seated the Lord in the boat and took Him to His own house, where, joyful at heart, He washed the Lord’s feet.

প্রথমে পাক করিয়াছেন আচার্য্যাণী ৷  
বিষ্ণু-সমর্পণ কৈল আচার্য্য আপনি ॥ ৪১ ॥

prathame pāka kariyāchena ācāryāṇī  
first  the cooking  had done  the Ācārya’s wife  
viṣṇu-samarpaṇa kaila ācārya āpani  
to Viṣṇu  offering  did  the Ācārya  Himself

(41) The Ācārya’s wife had done the cooking beforehand, and the Ācārya Himself offered the food to Viṣṇu.[^16]

তিন ঠাঞি ভোগ বাড়াইল সম করি’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণের ভোগ বাড়াইল ধাতু-পাত্রোপরি ॥ ৪২ ॥

tina ṭhāñi bhoga bāḍāila sama kari’  
in three  places  the food  served  equal  making  
kṛṣṇera bhoga bāḍāila dhātu-pātropari  
Kṛṣṇa’s  offering  served  on a metal plate

(42) The food was set out in three equal portions, and Kṛṣṇa’s offering was placed on a metal plate.[^17]

বত্তিশা-আঠিয়া-কলার আঙ্গটিয়া পাতে ৷  
দুই ঠাঞি ভোগ বাড়াইল ভালমতে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

battiśā-āṭhiyā-kalāra āṅgaṭiyā pāte  
of the battiśā-āṭhiyā  banana  whole  on leaves  
dui ṭhāñi bhoga bāḍāila bhāla mate  
in two  places  the food  served  in a fine  manner

(43) The other two portions were set out nicely on whole, uncut leaves of the *battiśā-āṭhiyā* banana.[^18]

মধ্যে পীত-ঘৃতসিক্ত শাল্যন্নের স্তূপ ৷  
চারিদিকে ব্যঞ্জন-ডোঙ্গা, আর মুদ্গসূপ ॥ ৪৪ ॥

madhye pīta-ghṛta-sikta śāly-annera stūpa  
in the middle  with yellow ghee soaked  of fine rice  a heap  
cāri-dike vyañjana-ḍoṅgā, āra mudga-sūpa  
on four sides  vegetable cups  and  mung dāl

(44) In the middle of each was a heap of fine *śāli* rice soaked in yellow ghee, and all around it stood leaf cups of vegetables and mung dāl.

সার্দ্দ্রক, বাস্তুক-শাক বিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
পটোল, কুষ্মাণ্ড-বড়ি, মানকচু আর ॥ ৪৫ ॥

sārdraka, vāstuka-śāka vividha prakāra  
sārdraka  vāstuka greens  various  kinds  
paṭola, kuṣmāṇḍa-baḍi, mānakacu āra  
paṭola  pumpkin with baḍi  mānakacu  and

(45) There was *sārdraka*, various kinds of *vāstuka* greens, *paṭola*, pumpkin with *baḍi*, and *mānakacu*.[^19]

চই-মরিচ-সুখ্ত দিয়া সব ফল-মূলে ৷  
অমৃতনিন্দক পঞ্চবিধ তিক্ত-ঝালে ॥ ৪৬ ॥

ca-i-marica-sukhta diyā saba phala-mūle  
cai and pepper sukhta  adding  to all  fruits and roots  
amṛta-nindaka pañca-vidha tikta-jhāle  
nectar-defeating  five kinds  bitter and pungent

(46) All the fruits and roots, seasoned with *cai*, black pepper and *sukhta*, became five kinds of bitter and pungent dishes that put nectar to shame.[^20]

কোমল নিম্বপত্র সহ ভাজা বার্ত্তাকী ৷  
পটোল-ফুলবড়ি-ভাজা, কুষ্মাণ্ড-মানচাকি ॥ ৪৭ ॥

komala nimba-patra saha bhājā vārtākī  
tender  nim leaves  with  fried  eggplant  
paṭola-phula-baḍi-bhājā, kuṣmāṇḍa-mānacāki  
paṭola with phula-baḍi fried  pumpkin mānacāki

(47) There was eggplant fried with tender *nim* leaves, *paṭola* fried with *phula-baḍi*, and pumpkin *mānacāki*.

নারিকেল-শস্য, ছানা, শর্করা মধুর ৷  
মোচাঘণ্ট, দুগ্ধকুষ্মাণ্ড, সকল প্রচুর ॥ ৪৮ ॥

nārikela-śasya, chānā, śarkarā madhura  
coconut pulp  curd  with sugar  sweet  
mocā-ghaṇṭa, dugdha-kuṣmāṇḍa, sakala pracura  
banana-flower ghaṇṭa  pumpkin in milk  all  in abundance

(48) There was coconut pulp, fresh curd (*chānā*) sweetened with sugar, a *ghaṇṭa* of banana flowers and pumpkin cooked in milk — all in abundance.

মধুরাম্লবড়া, অম্লাদি পাঁচ-ছয় ৷  
সকল ব্যঞ্জন কৈল লোকে যত হয় ॥ ৪৯ ॥

madhurāmla-baḍā, amlādi pām̐ca-chaya  
sweet-and-sour baḍās  sour dishes and others  five or six  
sakala vyañjana kaila loke yata haya  
all  vegetables  made  among people  as many as  exist

(49) There were sweet-and-sour *baḍās* and five or six sour dishes. Every vegetable dish known in the world was made.[^21]

মুদ্গবড়া, মাষবড়া কলাবড়া মিষ্ট ৷  
ক্ষীরপুলী, নারিকেল, যত পীঠা ইষ্ট ॥ ৫০ ॥

mudgabaḍā, māṣabaḍā kalābaḍā miṣṭa  
mung baḍās  urad baḍās  banana baḍās  sweet  
kṣīrapulī, nārikela, yata pīṭhā iṣṭa  
kṣīrapulī  coconut  all  cakes  favourite

(50) There were *baḍās* of mung and of urad, sweet banana *baḍās*, *kṣīrapulī*, coconut, and every kind of cake one could wish for.[^22]

বত্তিশা-আঠিয়া-কলার ডোঙ্গা বড় বড় ৷  
চলে হালে নাহি, — ডোঙ্গা অতি বড় দড় ॥ ৫১ ॥

battiśā-āṭhiyā-kalāra ḍoṅgā baḍa baḍa  
of the battiśā-āṭhiyā  banana  the cups  large  large  
cale hāle nāhi, — ḍoṅgā ati baḍa daḍa  
move  shake  not  cups  very  large  strong

(51) The cups made of *battiśā-āṭhiyā* banana leaves were very large and strong, so that they did not tip or wobble.

পঞ্চাশ পঞ্চাশ ডোঙ্গা ব্যঞ্জনে পূড়িঞা ৷  
তিন ভোগের আসে পাসে রাখিল ধরিঞা ॥ ৫২ ॥

pañcāśa pañcāśa ḍoṅgā vyañjane pūḍiñā  
fifty  fifty  cups  with vegetables  filling  
tina bhogera āse pāse rākhila dhariñā  
of the three  offerings  around  on the sides  placed  arranging

(52) Around each of the three offerings they set fifty such cups filled with vegetables.

সঘৃত-পায়স নব মৃৎকুণ্ডিকা ভরিঞা ৷  
তিন পাত্রে ঘনাবর্ত্ত-দুগ্ধ রাখে ত’ ধরিঞা ॥ ৫৩ ॥

saghṛta-pāyasa nava mṛt-kuṇḍikā bhariñā  
with ghee sweet rice  new  earthen pots  filling  
tina pātre ghanāvarta-dugdha rākhe ta’ dhariñā  
in three  vessels  thickened milk  they place  indeed  arranging

(53) Sweet rice with ghee filled new earthen pots, and thickened milk was set out in three vessels.

দুগ্ধ-চিড়া-কলা আর দুগ্ধ-লক্লকী ৷  
যতেক করিল, তাহা কহিতে না শকি ॥ ৫৪ ॥

dugdha-ciḍā-kalā āra dugdha-laklakī  
milk with flat rice and banana  and  milk laklakī  
yateka karila, tāhā kahite nā śaki  
however much  they made  that  to describe  not  I am able

(54) There was flat rice with banana in milk, and milk *laklakī*. I cannot describe all that they made.[^23]

দুই পাশে ধরিল সব মৃৎকুণ্ডিকা ভরি’ ৷  
চাঁপাকলা-দধি-সন্দেশ কহিতে না পারি ॥ ৫৫ ॥

dui pāśe dharila saba mṛt-kuṇḍikā bhari’  
on two  sides  placed  all  earthen pots  filling  
cām̐pākalā-dadhi-sandeśa kahite nā pāri  
campaka bananas, yogurt, sandeśa  to describe  not  I can

(55) On both sides stood earthen pots filled with *cā̃pā* bananas, yogurt and *sandeśa* — I cannot describe it all.

অন্ন-ব্যঞ্জন-উপরি দিল তুলসীমঞ্জরী ৷  
তিন জলপাত্রে সুবাসিত জল ভরি’ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

anna-vyañjana-upari dila tulasī-mañjarī  
over the rice and vegetables  placed  tulasī buds  
tina jala-pātre suvāsita jala bhari’  
three  water pots  with scented  water  filling

(56) On top of the rice and vegetables they placed tulasī buds, and three water pots were filled with scented water.[^24]

তিন শুভ্রপীঠ, তার উপরি বসন ৷  
কৃষ্ণের ভোগ সাক্ষাৎ কৃষ্ণে করাইল ভোজন ॥ ৫৭ ॥

tina śubhra-pīṭha, tāra upari vasana  
three  white seats  them  upon  cloth  
kṛṣṇera bhoga sākṣāt kṛṣṇe karāila bhojana  
Kṛṣṇa’s  food  directly  to Kṛṣṇa  made to eat

(57) There were three white seats covered with cloth. The Ācārya fed Kṛṣṇa’s portion to Kṛṣṇa Himself.[^25]

আরতির কালে দুই প্রভু বোলাইল ৷  
প্রভু-সঙ্গে সবে আসি’ আরতি দেখিল ॥ ৫৮ ॥

āratira kāle dui prabhu bolāila  
of the ārati  at the time  the two  Lords  called  
prabhu-saṅge sabe āsi’ ārati dekhila  
with the Lords  all  coming  the ārati  saw

(58) At the time of ārati the Ācārya called the two Lords, and everyone came with Them to see the ārati.

আরতি করিয়া কৃষ্ণে করা’ল শয়ন ৷  
আচার্য্য আসি’ প্রভুরে তবে কৈলা নিবেদন ॥ ৫৯ ॥

ārati kariyā kṛṣṇe karā’la śayana  
ārati  having done  Kṛṣṇa  made to lie down  
ācārya āsi’ prabhure tabe kailā nivedana  
the Ācārya  coming  to the Lord  then  made  a request

(59) After the ārati, Kṛṣṇa was laid down to rest, and then the Ācārya came and made a request to the Lord:

গৃহের ভিতরে প্রভু করুন গমন ৷  
দুই ভাই আইলা তবে করিতে ভোজন ॥ ৬০ ॥

gṛhera bhitare prabhu karuna gamana  
of the house  inside  the Lord  may make  going  
dui bhāi āilā tabe karite bhojana  
the two  brothers  came  then  to take  the meal

(60) “May the Lord come inside the house.” Then the two Brothers went in to take Their meal.

মুকুন্দ, হরিদাস, — দুই প্রভু বোলাইল ৷  
যোড়হাতে দুইজন কহিতে লাগিল ॥ ৬১ ॥

mukunda, haridāsa, — dui prabhu bolāila  
Mukunda  Haridāsa  the two  Lords  called  
yoḍa-hāte dui-jana kahite lāgila  
with joined palms  the two  to speak  began

(61) The two Lords called Mukunda and Haridāsa, but the two of them, with joined palms, began to speak.

মুকুন্দ বলে, মোর কিছু কৃত্য নাহি সরে ৷  
পাছে মুঞি প্রসাদ পামু, তুমি যাহ ঘরে ॥ ৬২ ॥

mukunda bale, mora kichu kṛtya nāhi sare  
Mukunda  says  my  some  duty  not  is finished  
pāche muñi prasāda pāmu, tumi yāha ghare  
later  I  prasāda  shall take  You  go  into the house

(62) Mukunda said, “Some of my duties are not yet finished. I shall take prasāda later; You go inside.”

হরিদাস বলে, মুঞি পাপিষ্ঠ অধম ৷  
বাহিরে একমুষ্টি পাছে করিমু ভোজন ॥ ৬৩ ॥

haridāsa bale, muñi pāpiṣṭha adhama  
Haridāsa  says  I  most sinful  lowly  
bāhire eka muṣṭi pāche karimu bhojana  
outside  one  handful  later  shall make  meal

(63) Haridāsa said, “I am most sinful and lowly. Later I shall eat a handful outside.”

দুই প্রভুরে লঞা আচার্য্য গেলা ভিতর-ঘরে ৷  
প্রসাদ দেখিয়া প্রভুর আনন্দ অন্তরে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

dui prabhure lañā ācārya gelā bhitara-ghare  
the two  Lords  taking  the Ācārya  went  to the inner room  
prasāda dekhiyā prabhura ānanda antare  
the prasāda  seeing  of the Lord  joy  in the heart

(64) The Ācārya took the two Lords into the inner room. Seeing the prasāda, the Lord felt joy in His heart.

ঐছে অন্ন যে কৃষ্ণকে করায় ভোজন ৷  
জন্মে জন্মে শিরে ধরোঁ তাঁহার চরণ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

aiche anna ye kṛṣṇake karāya bhojana  
such  food  who  to Kṛṣṇa  makes  eat  
janme janme śire dharom̐ tām̐hāra caraṇa  
birth  after birth  on My head  I hold  his  feet

(65) “Whoever feeds Kṛṣṇa such food — birth after birth I would hold his feet upon My head.”

প্রভু জানে, তিন ভোগ — কৃষ্ণের নৈবেদ্য ৷  
আচার্য্যের মনঃ-কথা নহে প্রভুর বেদ্য ॥ ৬৬ ॥

prabhu jāne, tina bhoga — kṛṣṇera naivedya  
the Lord  knows  three  offerings  Kṛṣṇa’s  naivedya  
ācāryera manaḥ-kathā nahe prabhura vedya  
of the Ācārya  the mind’s  intention  not  to the Lord  known

(66) The Lord took all three portions to be food already offered to Kṛṣṇa, for He did not know what the Ācārya had in mind.[^26]

প্রভু বলে, বৈস তুমি করিতে ভোজন ৷  
আচার্য্য কহে, আমি করিব পরিবেশন ॥ ৬৭ ॥

prabhu bale, vaisa tumi karite bhojana  
the Lord  says  sit  You  to take  the meal  
ācārya kahe, āmi kariba pariveśana  
the Ācārya  says  I  shall do  serving

(67) “You too sit down and eat,” said the Lord. “I shall serve,” replied the Ācārya.[^27]

কোন্ স্থানে বসিব, আর আন দুই পাত ৷  
অল্প করি’ তাহে আনি’ দেহ ব্যঞ্জন-ভাত ॥ ৬৮ ॥

kon sthāne vasiba, āra āna dui pāta  
in which  place  shall I sit  another  bring  two  leaves  
alpa kari’ tāhe āni’ deha vyañjana-bhāta  
little  making  on them  bringing  give  vegetables and rice

(68) The Lord said, “Where shall I sit? Bring two more leaves, and put just a little rice and vegetables on them.”[^28]

আচার্য্য কহে, বৈস দোঁহে পিণ্ডার উপরে ৷  
এত বলি হাতে ধরি’ বসাইল দুঁহারে ॥ ৬৯ ॥

ācārya kahe, vaisa dom̐he piṇḍāra upare  
the Ācārya  says  sit  both  the seats  upon  
eta bali hāte dhari’ vasāila dum̐hāre  
thus  saying  by the hands  taking  seated  both

(69) The Ācārya said, “Both of You, sit on these seats,” and saying this, He took Them by the hand and seated Them.

প্রভু কহে, সন্ন্যাসীর ভক্ষ্য নহে উপকরণ ৷  
ইহা খাইলে কৈছে হবে ইন্দ্রিয়-বারণ ॥ ৭০ ॥

prabhu kahe, sannyāsīra bhakṣya nahe upakaraṇa  
the Lord  says  of a sannyāsī  food  is not  such fare  
ihā khāile kaiche habe indriya-vāraṇa  
this  eating  how  will be  of the senses  restraint

(70) The Lord said, “Such fare is not fit food for a sannyāsī. If he eats this, how will he restrain his senses?”[^29]

আচার্য্য কহে, ছাড় তুমি আপনার চুরি ৷  
আমি জানি তোমার সন্ন্যাসের ভারিভুরি ॥ ৭১ ॥

ācārya kahe, chāḍa tumi āpanāra curi  
the Ācārya  says  give up  You  Your own  concealment  
āmi jāni tomāra sannyāsera bhāri-bhuri  
I  know  of Your  sannyāsa  the pretence

(71) The Ācārya said, “Stop hiding who You are. I know the secret of Your sannyāsa.[^30]

ভোজন করহ, ছাড় বচন-চাতুরী ৷  
প্রভু কহে, এত অন্ন খাইতে না পারি ॥ ৭২ ॥

bhojana karaha, chāḍa vacana-cāturī  
the meal  take  give up  of words  cleverness  
prabhu kahe, eta anna khāite nā pāri  
the Lord  says  so much  food  to eat  not  I can

(72) Eat, and stop Your clever talk.” “I cannot eat so much,” said the Lord.

আচার্য্য বলে, অকপটে করহ আহার ৷  
যদি খাইতে না পার, রহিবেক আর ॥ ৭৩ ॥

ācārya bale, akapaṭe karaha āhāra  
the Ācārya  says  without pretence  take  food  
yadi khāite nā pāra, rahibeka āra  
if  to eat  not  You can  will remain  the rest

(73) The Ācārya said, “Eat without pretence. If You cannot eat it all, the rest can stay.”[^31]

প্রভু বলে, এত অন্ন নারিব খাইতে ৷  
সন্ন্যাসীর ধর্ম্ম নহে উচ্ছিষ্ট রাখিতে ॥ ৭৪ ॥

prabhu bale, eta anna nāriba khāite  
the Lord  says  so much  food  I shall not be able  to eat  
sannyāsīra dharma nahe ucchiṣṭa rākhite  
of a sannyāsī  the duty  is not  remnants  to leave

(74) The Lord said, “I shall not be able to eat so much, and it is not a sannyāsī’s dharma to leave remnants.”[^32]

আচার্য্য বলে, নীলাচলে খাও চৌয়ান্নবার ৷  
একবারে অন্ন খাও শত শত ভার ॥ ৭৫ ॥

ācārya bale, nīlācale khāo cauyānna-bāra  
the Ācārya  says  in Nīlācala  You eat  fifty-four times  
eka-bāre anna khāo śata śata bhāra  
at one time  food  You eat  hundreds  hundreds  loads

(75) The Ācārya said, “In Nīlācala You eat fifty-four times, and each time You eat hundreds and hundreds of loads of food.

তিন জনার ভক্ষ্যপিণ্ড — তোমার একগ্রাস ৷  
তার লেখায় এই অন্ন নহে পঞ্চগ্রাস ॥ ৭৬ ॥

tina janāra bhakṣya-piṇḍa — tomāra eka grāsa  
of three  persons  the food portion  Your  one  mouthful  
tāra lekhāya ei anna nahe pañca-grāsa  
by that  reckoning  this  food  is not  five mouthfuls

(76) What three people eat is a single mouthful for You. By that reckoning this food is not even five mouthfuls.

মোর ভাগ্যে, মোর ঘরে, তোমার আগমন ৷  
ছাড়হ চাতুরী, প্রভু, করহ ভোজন ॥ ৭৭ ॥

mora bhāgye, mora ghare, tomāra āgamana  
by My  fortune  to My  house  Your  coming  
chāḍaha cāturī, prabhu, karaha bhojana  
give up  cleverness  Lord  take  the meal

(77) By My good fortune You have come to My house. Give up Your cleverness, Lord, and eat.”

এত বলি, জল দিল দুই গোসাঞির হাতে ৷  
হাসিয়া লাগিলা দুঁহে ভোজন করিতে ॥ ৭৮ ॥

eta bali, jala dila dui gosāñira hāte  
thus  saying  water  gave  of the two  Gosāñis  on the hands  
hāsiyā lāgilā dum̐he bhojana karite  
smiling  began  both  meal  to take

(78) Saying this, He poured water on the hands of the two Gosāñis, and with a smile the two of Them began to eat.

নিত্যানন্দ কহে, কৈলুঁ তিন উপবাস ৷  
আজি পারণা করিতে বড় ছিল আশ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

nityānanda kahe, kailum̐ tina upavāsa  
Nityānanda  says  I did  three  fasts  
āji pāraṇā karite baḍa chila āśa  
today  breaking the fast  to do  great  was  hope

(79) Nityānanda said, “I have fasted for three days, and I had great hope of breaking My fast today.

আজি উপবাস হৈল আচার্য্য-নিমন্ত্রণে ৷  
অর্দ্ধপেট না ভরিল এই গ্রাসেক অন্নে ॥ ৮০ ॥

āji upavāsa haila ācārya-nimantraṇe  
today  fasting  has come  by the Ācārya’s invitation  
ardha-peṭa nā bharila ei grāseka anne  
half the belly  not  has filled  this  mouthful of  food

(80) But today, by the Ācārya’s invitation, it has turned into a fast: this mouthful of food has not filled even half My belly.”

আচার্য্য কহে, তুমি তৈথিক সন্ন্যাসী ৷  
কভু ফল-মূল খাও, কভু উপবাসী ॥ ৮১ ॥

ācārya kahe, tumi taithika sannyāsī  
the Ācārya  says  You  a pilgrim  sannyāsī  
kabhu phala-mūla khāo, kabhu upavāsī  
sometimes  fruits and roots  You eat  sometimes  fasting

(81) The Ācārya said, “You are a pilgrim sannyāsī: sometimes You eat fruits and roots, sometimes You fast.[^33]

দরিদ্রব্রাহ্মণ ঘরে যে পাইলা মুষ্ট্যেক অন্ন ৷  
ইহাতে সন্তুষ্ট হও, ছাড় লোভ-মন ॥ ৮২ ॥

daridrabrāhmaṇa ghare ye pāilā muṣṭy-eka anna  
in a poor brāhmaṇa’s  house  what  You have got  a handful of  food  
ihāte santuṣṭa hao, chāḍa lobha-mana  
with this  satisfied  be  give up  a greedy mind

(82) Be satisfied with the handful of food You have received in a poor brāhmaṇa’s house, and give up Your greed.”

নিত্যানন্দ বলে, যবে কৈলে নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
তত দিতে চাহ, যত করিয়ে ভোজন ॥ ৮৩ ॥

nityānanda bale, yabe kaile nimantraṇa  
Nityānanda  says  since  You made  an invitation  
tata dite cāha, yata kariye bhojana  
so much  to give  You must  as much as  I eat

(83) Nityānanda said, “Since You invited Me, You must give Me as much as I eat.”

শুনি, নিত্যানন্দের কথা ঠাকুর অদ্বৈত ৷  
কহেন তাঁহারে কিছু পাইয়া পিরীত ॥ ৮৪ ॥

śuni, nityānandera kathā ṭhākura advaita  
hearing  Nityānanda’s  words  Ṭhākura  Advaita  
kahena tām̐hāre kichu pāiyā pirīta  
speaks  to Him  some  feeling  affection

(84) Hearing Nityānanda’s words, Ṭhākura Advaita answered Him fondly:

ভ্রষ্ট অবধূত তুমি, উদর ভরিতে ৷  
সন্ন্যাস লইয়াছ, বুঝি, ব্রাহ্মণ দণ্ডিতে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

bhraṣṭa avadhūta tumi, udara bharite  
fallen  avadhūta  You  the belly  to fill  
sannyāsa laiyācha, bujhi, brāhmaṇa daṇḍite  
sannyāsa  You have taken  I think  brāhmaṇas  to punish

(85) “You are a fallen avadhūta! You took sannyāsa just to fill Your belly — to punish brāhmaṇas, I think.[^34]

তুমি খেতে পার দশ-বিশ মানের অন্ন ৷  
আমি তাহা কাঁহা পাব, দরিদ্র ব্রাহ্মণ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

tumi khete pāra daśa-viśa mānera anna  
You  to eat  can  ten or twenty  māns of  food  
āmi tāhā kām̐hā pāba, daridra brāhmaṇa  
I  that  where  shall get  a poor  brāhmaṇa

(86) You can eat ten or twenty *māns* of rice. Where shall I, a poor brāhmaṇa, get that much?[^35]

যে পাঞাছ মুষ্ট্যেক অন্ন, তাহা খাঞা উঠ ৷  
পাগলামি না করিহ, না ছড়াইও ঝুট ॥ ৮৭ ॥

ye pāñācha muṣṭy-eka anna, tāhā khāñā uṭha  
what  You have got  a handful of  food  that  eating  get up  
pāgalāmi nā kariha, nā chaḍāio jhuṭa  
madness  not  do  not  scatter  leftovers

(87) Eat the handful of food You have been given and get up. Don’t act like a madman, and don’t scatter Your leftovers about!”

এই মত হাস্যরসে করেন ভোজন ৷  
অর্দ্ধ-অর্দ্ধ খাঞা প্রভু ছাড়েন ব্যঞ্জন ॥ ৮৮ ॥

ei mata hāsya-rase karena bhojana  
in this  way  in a joking mood  They take  the meal  
ardha-ardha khāñā prabhu chāḍena vyañjana  
half  half  eating  the Lord  leaves  the vegetables

(88) In this way They ate, joking and laughing. The Lord would eat half of each vegetable dish and leave the rest.

সেই ব্যঞ্জন আচার্য্য পুনঃ করেন পূরণ ৷  
এই মত পুনঃ পুনঃ পরিবেশে ব্যঞ্জন ॥ ৮৯ ॥

sei vyañjana ācārya punaḥ karena pūraṇa  
those  vegetables  the Ācārya  again  makes  filling  
ei mata punaḥ punaḥ pariveśe vyañjana  
in this  way  again  again  serves  the vegetables

(89) The Ācārya would fill that dish again, and so He served the vegetables again and again.

দোনা ব্যঞ্জনে ভরি’ করেন প্রার্থন ৷  
প্রভু বলেন, আর কত করিব ভোজন ॥ ৯০ ॥

donā vyañjane bhari’ karena prārthana  
cup  with vegetables  filling  makes  entreaty  
prabhu balena, āra kata kariba bhojana  
the Lord  says  more  how much  shall I make  eating

(90) Filling the cup with vegetables, He would entreat the Lord to eat. The Lord said, “How much more am I to eat?”

আচার্য্য কহে, যে দিয়াছি, তাহা না ছাড়িবা ৷  
এখন যে দিয়ে, তার অর্দ্ধেক খাইবা ॥ ৯১ ॥

ācārya kahe, ye diyāchi, tāhā nā chāḍibā  
the Ācārya  says  what  I have given  that  not  leave  
ekhana ye diye, tāra ardheka khāibā  
now  what  I give  of that  half  eat

(91) The Ācārya said, “Do not leave what I have already given You. Of what I give You now, eat half.”

নানা যত্ন-দৈন্যে প্রভুর করাইল ভোজন ৷  
আচার্য্যের ইচ্ছা প্রভু করিল পূরণ ॥ ৯২ ॥

nānā yatna-dainye prabhura karāila bhojana  
with various  efforts and humility  the Lord  made  eat  
ācāryera icchā prabhu karila pūraṇa  
the Ācārya’s  desire  the Lord  made  fulfilled

(92) With many efforts and humble pleas the Ācārya fed the Lord, and the Lord fulfilled His wish.

নিত্যানন্দ কহে, আমার পেট না ভরিল ৷  
লঞা যাহ, তোর অন্ন কিছু না খাইল ॥ ৯৩ ॥

nityānanda kahe — āmāra peṭa nā bharila  
Nityānanda  says  My  belly  not  is filled  
lañā yāha, tora anna kichu nā khāila  
taking  go away  your  food  any  not  I have eaten

(93) Nityānanda said, “My belly is not full! Take it away — I have not eaten any of Your food.”

এত বলি’ একগ্রাস অন্ন হাতে লঞা ৷  
উঝালি’ ফেলিল আগে যেন ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা ॥ ৯৪ ॥

eta bali’ ekagrāsa anna hāte lañā  
thus  saying  a mouthful of  rice  in His hand  taking  
ujhāli’ phelila āge yena kruddha hañā  
tossing  threw  in front  as if  angry  becoming

(94) Saying this, He took up a handful of rice and flung it down before Him, as if in anger.

ভাত দুই-চারি লাগে আচার্য্যের অঙ্গে ৷  
ভাত গায়ে লঞা আচার্য্য নাচে বহুরঙ্গে ॥ ৯৫ ॥

bhāta dui-cāri lāge ācāryera aṅge  
grains of rice  two or four  touched  the Ācārya’s  body  
bhāta gāye lañā ācārya nāce bahuraṅge  
the rice  on His body  bearing  the Ācārya  dances  in many ways

(95) A few grains of rice struck the Ācārya’s body, and with the rice still on Him He danced about in delight.[^36]

অবধূতের ঝুটা লাগিল মোর অঙ্গে ৷  
পরম পবিত্র মোরে কৈল এই ঢঙ্গে ॥ ৯৬ ॥

avadhūtera jhuṭā lāgila mora aṅge  
of the avadhūta  the leftovers  have touched  My  body  
parama pavitra more kaila ei ḍhaṅge  
supremely  pure  Me  has made  in this  way

(96) “The leftovers of the avadhūta have touched My body! In this way He has made Me supremely pure.[^37]

তোরে নিমন্ত্রণ করি’ পাইনু তার ফল ৷  
তোর জাতি-কুল নাহি, সহজে পাগল ॥ ৯৭ ॥

tore nimantraṇa kari’ pāinu tāra phala  
you  invitation  having made  I have got  its  fruit  
tora jāti-kula nāhi, sahaje pāgala  
your  caste and family  none  by nature  a madman

(97) I invited You, and I have reaped the fruit of it! You have no caste or family — You are a madman by nature.[^38]

আপনার সম মোরে করিবার তরে ৷  
ঝুটা দিলে, বিপ্র বলি’ ভয় না করিলে ॥ ৯৮ ॥

āpanāra sama more karibāra tare  
Your own  equal  Me  to make  for the sake of  
jhuṭā dile, vipra bali’ bhaya nā karile  
leftovers  you gave  a brāhmaṇa  considering  fear  not  you felt

(98) To make Me just like You, You threw Your leftovers at Me, without any fear that I am a brāhmaṇa.”[^39]

নিত্যানন্দ বলে, — এই কৃষ্ণের প্রসাদ ৷  
ইহাকে ‘ঝুটা’ কহিলে, কৈলে অপরাধ ॥ ৯৯ ॥

nityānanda bale, — ei kṛṣṇera prasāda  
Nityānanda  says  this  Kṛṣṇa’s  prasāda  
ihāke ‘jhuṭā’ kahile, kaile aparādha  
it  ‘leftovers’  having called  You committed  an offence

(99) Nityānanda said, “This is Kṛṣṇa’s prasāda! By calling it ‘leftovers’ You have committed an offence.[^40]

শতেক সন্ন্যাসী যদি করাহ ভোজন ৷  
তবে এই অপরাধ হইবে খণ্ডন ॥ ১০০ ॥

śateka sannyāsī yadi karāha bhojana  
a hundred  sannyāsīs  if  You feed  
tabe ei aparādha haibe khaṇḍana  
then  this  offence  will be  cancelled

(100) If You feed a hundred sannyāsīs, then this offence will be cancelled.”

আচার্য্য কহে, না করিব সন্ন্যাসী-নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
সন্ন্যাসী নাশিল মোর সব স্মৃতি-ধর্ম্ম ॥ ১০১ ॥

ācārya kahe, nā kariba sannyāsī-nimantraṇa  
the Ācārya  says  not  I shall make  sannyāsī invitation  
sannyāsī nāśila mora saba smṛti-dharma  
a sannyāsī  has destroyed  My  all  smṛti-dharma

(101) The Ācārya said, “I shall never again invite a sannyāsī! A sannyāsī has ruined all My *smṛti-dharma*.”[^41]

এত বলি’ দুই জনে করাইল আচমন ৷  
উত্তম শয্যাতে লইয়া করাইল শয়ন ॥ ১০২ ॥

eta bali’ dui jane karāila ācamana  
thus  saying  the two  persons  made to rinse  
uttama śayyāte la-iyā karāila śayana  
on an excellent  bed  taking  made to lie down

(102) Saying this, He had the two Lords rinse Their hands and mouths, and then laid Them down to rest on a fine bed.

লবঙ্গ এলাচী-বীজ — উত্তম রসবাস ৷  
তুলসী-মঞ্জরী সহ দিল মুখবাস ॥ ১০৩ ॥

lavaṅga elācī-bīja — uttama rasa-vāsa  
cloves  cardamom seeds  excellent  fragrance  
tulasī-mañjarī saha dila mukha-vāsa  
tulasī buds  with  gave  mouth-freshener

(103) He gave Them cloves and cardamom seeds with tulasī buds as a fragrant mouth-freshener.

সুগন্ধি চন্দনে লিপ্ত কৈল কলেবর ৷  
সুগন্ধি পুষ্পমালা আনি’ দিল হৃদয়-উপর ॥ ১০৪ ॥

sugandhi candane lipta kaila kalevara  
fragrant  with sandalwood  anointed  made  the bodies  
sugandhi puṣpa-mālā āni’ dila hṛdaya-upara  
fragrant  flower garlands  bringing  placed  on the chest

(104) He anointed Their bodies with fragrant sandalwood paste, and brought garlands of sweet-scented flowers and placed them on Their chests.

আচার্য্য করিতে চাহে পাদ-সম্বাহন ৷  
সঙ্কুচিত হঞা প্রভু বলেন বচন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

ācārya karite cāhe pāda-samvāhana  
the Ācārya  to do  wishes  feet  massaging  
saṅkucita hañā prabhu balena vacana  
embarrassed  becoming  the Lord  speaks  words

(105) The Ācārya wished to massage the Lord’s feet, but the Lord, abashed, said:

বহুত নাচাইলে তুমি, ছাড় নাচান ৷  
মুকুন্দ-হরিদাস লইয়া করহ ভোজন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

bahuta nācāile tumi, chāḍa nācāna  
much  You have made Me dance  You  give up  making Me dance  
mukunda-haridāsa laiyā karaha bhojana  
Mukunda and Haridāsa  taking  take  Your meal

(106) “You have made Me dance a great deal; now stop making Me dance. Go and eat with Mukunda and Haridāsa.”[^42]

তবে ত’ আচার্য্য সঙ্গে লঞা দুই জনে ৷  
করিল ভোজন, ইচ্ছা যে আছিল মনে ॥ ১০৭ ॥

tabe ta’ ācārya saṅge lañā dui jane  
then  indeed  the Ācārya  with Him  taking  the two  persons  
karila bhojana, icchā ye āchila mane  
did  meal  the desire  which  was  in the mind

(107) Then the Ācārya took the two of them with Him and ate, as He had wished in His heart.

শান্তিপুরের লোক শুনি’ প্রভুর আগমন ৷  
দেখিতে আইলা লোক প্রভুর চরণ ॥ ১০৮ ॥

śāntipurera loka śuni’ prabhura āgamana  
of Śāntipura  the people  hearing  of the Lord  the arrival  
dekhite āilā loka prabhura caraṇa  
to see  came  the people  the Lord’s  feet

(108) Hearing that the Lord had come, the people of Śāntipura came to see His feet.

‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলে লোক আনন্দিত হঞা ৷  
চমৎকার পাইল প্রভুর সৌন্দর্য্য দেখিঞা ॥ ১০৯ ॥

‘hari’ ‘hari’ bale loka ānandita hañā  
“Hari”  “Hari”  say  the people  joyful  becoming  
camatkāra pāila prabhura saundarya dekhiñā  
wonder  felt  the Lord’s  beauty  seeing

(109) The people joyfully called out “Hari! Hari!”, and seeing the Lord’s beauty, they were struck with wonder.

গৌর-দেহ-কান্তি সূর্য্য জিনিয়া উজ্জ্বল ৷  
অরুণ-বস্ত্রকান্তি তাহে করে ঝলমল ॥ ১১০ ॥

gaura-deha-kānti sūrya jiniyā ujjvala  
of the golden body  the radiance  the sun  surpassing  bright  
aruṇa-vastra-kānti tāhe kare jhala-mala  
of the saffron cloth  the radiance  on it  makes  glitter

(110) The radiance of His golden body outshone the sun, and over it shimmered the glow of His cloth, red as the dawn.

[^2]: The same verse as Ādi 4.2; translation as there.

[^3]: The same verse as Madhya 1.16; translation as there.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the name Rāḍha comes from *rāṣṭra*; the land of Gauḍa on the western bank of the Gaṅgā is called Rāḍha-deśa, also Pauṇḍra-deśa.

[^5]: The song of the mendicant brāhmaṇa of Avantī. *Anubhāṣya*: devotion to the Supreme Self is the natural bhajana that follows when all *anarthas* are gone; the dress of the fourth āśrama belongs to the outward marks of a Vaiṣṇava, and those who wear it are freed from saṁsāra by serving Mukunda. The Lord sang this song of the *tridaṇḍi* mendicant to show that His single *daṇḍa* held all four.

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the meaning of the sannyāsa dress is devotion to the Supreme Self, which rejects attachment to the material self.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: *prabandha* — a well-composed story.

[^8]: Heading in the edition: “At Nitāi’s word they show the Lord the way to Navadvīpa”.

[^9]: Heading in the edition: Nitāi sends Candraśekhara to Śāntipura so that everything needed may be arranged and the news of the Lord’s coming spread everywhere.

[^10]: The edition reads *āra bhakta-gaṇa* (“and the other devotees”); another reading is *saba bhakta-gaṇa* (“all the devotees”).

[^11]: Heading in the edition: “The Gaṅgā awakens in Mahāprabhu the remembrance of the Yamunā”.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: the words of the *Padma Purāṇa*, quoted in the *Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* (5.13). *Anubhāṣya*: *cid-ānanda-bhānu* — Kṛṣṇa, who reveals knowledge and love (*saṁvit* and *prīti*); *drava-brahma-gātrī* — whose form is spiritual water; *aghānām* — of offences; the body is purified by divine knowledge.

[^13]: Heading in the edition: “Seeing Advaita, the Lord hesitates”.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: *gaṅgāke* (“to the Gaṅgā”) — a form of the speech of Rāḍha, where the author lived before taking sannyāsa: there the locative ending *-e* is still replaced by *-ke*.

[^15]: Heading in the edition: fearing the Lord might refuse, Advaita describes the meal at His house as the simplest fare.

[^16]: Heading in the edition: “Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī cooks, and the Ācārya Himself offers the bhoga”.

[^17]: Heading in the edition: the offering is set out on three plates — for three: the two Lords and Kṛṣṇa.

[^18]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *āṭhiyā* is a kind of banana tree that bears a bunch of thirty-two clusters; *āṅgaṭiyā* — a whole, untorn banana leaf.

[^19]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition prints *sādraka* — a dropped *repha* (a printing defect); the reading *sārdraka* is kept.

[^20]: *Cai* — the pungent stems of a pepper vine; *sukhta* — a bitter Bengali vegetable dish.

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: *loke* — in the world.

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: *iṣṭa* — needed, desired.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: in verses 45–55 the author shows his fine knowledge of the art of cooking.

[^24]: Heading in the edition: tulasī on the offering, and with it water for *ācamana*.

[^25]: *Anubhāṣya*: “to Kṛṣṇa Himself” (*sākṣāt kṛṣṇe*) — that is, to Śrīman Mahāprabhu. The two portions on banana leaves had not been offered (*Anubhāṣya* to verse 66).

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: Advaita Himself offered to Kṛṣṇa only the portion on the metal plate; the two portions on banana leaves, meant for Mahāprabhu and Nityānanda, remained unoffered. The Ācārya kept this in mind and did not reveal it to Mahāprabhu.

[^27]: The edition reads *vaisa tumi karite bhojana* (“You sit down and eat”); another reading is *tine kariye bhojana* (“let the three of us eat”).

[^28]: *Anubhāṣya*: seeing how much food there was, the Lord asked for two more plates with only a little rice and vegetables on them.

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya*: *upakaraṇa* — dāl, vegetables and the like, which make rice easy to eat. A sannyāsī has no right to such tasty dishes: enjoying what pleases the senses strengthens the urge to enjoy. So the Lord told Raghunātha dāsa, “Do not eat fine food and do not wear fine clothes” (Antya 6.236).

[^30]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *bhāribhuri* — a secret.

[^31]: The edition reads *rahibeka āra* (“the rest will remain”); another reading is *pāte rahibeka* (“will remain on the leaf”).

[^32]: *Anubhāṣya*: a sannyāsī may not leave remnants on his plate — Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.18.19: “…and let him eat all that remains, leaving nothing.”

[^33]: The edition reads *taithika* (so also the *Anubhāṣya*); another reading is *tairthika*. *Anubhāṣya*: Nityānanda, though Himself an *avadhūta*, plays the part of a *bahūdaka* sannyāsī who wanders to the holy places.

[^34]: *Anubhāṣya*: *bhraṣṭa* — “fallen” in the eyes of the worldly society of the smārtas, that is, beyond rules and prohibitions: praise in the guise of blame. All these words of Advaita are a joke. The highest stage of sannyāsa is that of the *paramahaṁsa*, also called *avadhūta*.

[^35]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: a *mān* is a measure of four *sers*.

[^36]: Heading in the edition: the remnants thrown by Nitāi touched Advaita’s body, and Advaita danced, overflowing with love.

[^37]: *Anubhāṣya*: by the rules of the smārtas Advaita should have become impure at the touch of these remnants, but in truth He became supremely pure: the remnants of a Vaiṣṇava, a *paramahaṁsa*, are *mahā-mahā-prasāda*. *Avadhūta* — one who does not care for his body.

[^38]: Heading in the edition: “In the guise of blame Advaita glorifies Nityānanda”. *Anubhāṣya*: “mad by nature” — devoted to the dharma of the *paramahaṁsas*, which is natural to the soul, and every moment intoxicated with serving Kṛṣṇa with all the senses.

[^39]: *Anubhāṣya*: “like You” — a perfected Vaiṣṇava, a *paramahaṁsa* beyond rules and prohibitions; such souls are never ruled by fear of the worldly smārta society.

[^40]: *Anubhāṣya*: to see *mahā-prasāda* as ordinary rice and dāl is an offence (*Bṛhad-viṣṇu-purāṇa*).

[^41]: Heading in the edition: “A Vaiṣṇava’s sannyāsa sets aside the rules of the smārtas”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *smṛti-dharma* — the dharma of the smārtas.

[^42]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord, a sannyāsī, saw that He was given a fine bed, cloves, cardamom, sandalwood paste and a garland, and that Advaita even wished to massage His feet; so He said, “You have made Me dance a great deal.”
