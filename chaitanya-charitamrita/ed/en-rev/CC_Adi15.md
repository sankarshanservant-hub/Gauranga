“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 15  
**A Brief Description of the Boyhood Pastimes**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

কুমনাঃ সুমনস্ত্বং হি যাতি যস্য পদাব্জয়োঃ ৷  
সুমনোঽর্পণমাত্রেণ তং চৈতন্যপ্রভুং ভজে ॥ ১ ॥

ku-manāḥ su-manastvaṁ hi  
one of evil mind  goodness of mind  surely  
yāti yasya padābjayoḥ  
attains  at whose  lotus feet  
su-mano-’rpaṇa-mātreṇa  
of flowers  by the offering  merely  
taṁ caitanya-prabhuṁ bhaje  
to that  Caitanya Prabhu  I worship

(1) “I worship Caitanya Prabhu. The mere offering of flowers at His lotus feet turns even an evil mind into a good one.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 7.1)[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র, জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra, jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

পৌগণ্ড-লীলার সূত্র করিয়ে গণন ৷  
পৌগণ্ড-বয়সে প্রভুর মুখ্য অধ্যয়ন ॥ ৩ ॥

paugaṇḍa-līlāra sūtra kariye gaṇana  
of the boyhood  pastimes  an outline  I make  enumeration  
paugaṇḍa-vayase prabhura mukhya adhyayana  
in the boyhood  years  the Lord’s  chief (pastime)  study

(3) Now I shall give a brief account of the Lord’s boyhood pastimes. In those years His chief occupation was study.[^2]

পৌগণ্ডলীলা চৈতন্যকৃষ্ণস্যাতিসুবিস্তৃতা ৷  
বিদ্যারম্ভমুখা পাণিগ্রহণান্তা মনোহরা ॥ ৪ ॥

paugaṇḍa-līlā caitanya-  
the boyhood  pastimes  of Caitanya-  
kṛṣṇasyāti-suvistṛtā  
Kṛṣṇa  very extensive  
vidyārambha-mukhā pāṇi-  
with the beginning of learning  as their start  hand-  
grahaṇāntā mano-harā  
taking (marriage)  as their end  enchanting

(4) “The boyhood pastimes of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa are vast and enchanting. They begin with His first lessons and end with His marriage.”[^3]

গঙ্গাদাস পণ্ডিত-স্থানে পড়েন ব্যাকরণ ৷  
শ্রবণ-মাত্রে কণ্ঠে কৈল সূত্রবৃত্তিগণ ॥ ৫ ॥

gaṅgādāsa paṇḍita-sthāne paḍena vyākaraṇa  
Gaṅgādāsa  Paṇḍita’s  at the place  He studies  grammar  
śravaṇa-mātre kaṇṭhe kaila sūtra-vṛtti-gaṇa  
by hearing  merely  in His throat  He held  the sūtras and commentaries  all

(5) He studied grammar with Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita, and as soon as He heard the sūtras and their commentaries, He knew them all by heart.[^4]

অল্পকালে হৈলা পঞ্জী-টীকাতে প্রবীণ ৷  
চিরকালের পড়ুয়া জিনে হইয়া নবীন ॥ ৬ ॥

alpa-kāle hailā pañjī-ṭīkāte pravīṇa  
in a short time  He became  in the Pañjī-ṭīkā  expert  
cira-kālera paḍuyā jine haiyā navīna  
of long standing  the students  He defeats  being  new

(6) Within a short time He mastered the *Pañjī-ṭīkā*, and, young as He was, He defeated students who had studied for years.[^5]

অধ্যয়ন-লীলা প্রভুর দাস-বৃন্দাবন ৷  
‘চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে’ কৈল বিস্তারিত বর্ণন ॥ ৭ ॥

adhyayana-līlā prabhura dāsa-vṛndāvana  
of study  the pastimes  of the Lord  Vṛndāvana dāsa  
‘caitanya-maṅgale’ kaila vistārita varṇana  
in the ‘Caitanya-maṅgala’  made  detailed  description

(7) In the *Caitanya-maṅgala* Vṛndāvana dāsa has described in detail the Lord’s pastimes as a student.[^6]

এক দিন মাতার পদে করিয়া প্রণাম ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — মাতা, মোরে দেহ এক দান ॥ ৮ ॥

eka dina mātāra pade kariyā praṇāma  
one  day  at His mother’s  feet  making  obeisance  
prabhu kahe, — mātā, more deha eka dāna  
the Lord  says  —  Mother,  to Me  give  one  gift

(8) One day the Lord bowed at His mother’s feet and said, “Mother, grant Me one gift.”

মাতা বলে, — তাই দিব, যা তুমি মাগিবে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — একাদশীতে অন্ন না খাইবে ॥ ৯ ॥

mātā bale, — tāi diba, yā tumi māgibe  
the mother  says  —  that  I will give  whatever  You  will ask  
prabhu kahe, — ekādaśīte anna nā khāibe  
the Lord  says  —  on Ekādaśī  grain  not  you will eat

(9) “I will give You whatever You ask,” said His mother, and the Lord replied, “Do not eat grain on Ekādaśī.”[^7]

শচী কহে, — না খাইব, ভালই কহিলা ৷  
সেই হৈতে একাদশী করিতে লাগিলা ॥ ১০ ॥

śacī kahe, — nā khāiba, bhālai kahilā  
Śacī  says  —  not  I will eat,  well indeed  You have spoken  
sei haite ekādaśī karite lāgilā  
from then on  Ekādaśī  to observe  she began

(10) Śacī said, “I will not eat it. You have spoken well.” And from that day on she observed Ekādaśī.

তবে মিশ্র বিশ্বরূপের দেখিয়া যৌবন ৷  
কন্যা মাগি’ বিবাহ দিতে কৈল মন ॥ ১১ ॥

tabe miśra viśvarūpera dekhiyā yauvana  
then  Miśra  of Viśvarūpa  seeing  the youth  
kanyā māgi’ vivāha dite kaila mana  
a bride  seeking,  marriage  to give  made  up his mind

(11) Then Miśra, seeing that Viśvarūpa had come of age, resolved to find Him a bride and see Him married.

বিশ্বরূপ শুনি’ ঘর ছাড়ি’ পলাইলা ৷  
সন্ন্যাস করিয়া তীর্থ করিবারে গেলা ॥ ১২ ॥

viśvarūpa śuni’ ghara chāḍi’ palāilā  
Viśvarūpa  hearing,  home  leaving  fled  
sannyāsa kariyā tīrtha karibāre gelā  
sannyāsa  taking,  pilgrimage  to make  He went

(12) When Viśvarūpa heard of this, He fled from home. He took sannyāsa and set out on pilgrimage to the holy places.

শুনি’ শচী-মিশ্রের দুঃখী হৈল মন ৷  
তবে প্রভু মাতা-পিতার কৈল আশ্বাসন ॥ ১৩ ॥

śuni’ śacī-miśrera duḥkhī haila mana  
hearing,  of Śacī and Miśra  sorrowful  became  the hearts  
tabe prabhu mātā-pitāra kaila āśvāsana  
then  the Lord  mother and father  made  consolation

(13) The news filled the hearts of Śacī and Miśra with sorrow, and the Lord set about consoling His mother and father.

ভাল হৈল, — বিশ্বরূপ সন্ন্যাস করিল ৷  
পিতৃকূল মাতৃকূল, — দুই উদ্ধারিল ॥ ১৪ ॥

bhāla haila, — viśvarūpa sannyāsa karila  
good  it is  —  Viśvarūpa  sannyāsa  has taken  
pitṛkūla mātṛkūla, — dui uddhārila  
the father’s line,  the mother’s line  —  both  He has delivered

(14) “It is well that Viśvarūpa has taken sannyāsa: He has delivered both His father’s line and His mother’s.[^8]

আমি ত’ করিব তোমা’ দুঁহার সেবন ৷  
শুনিয়া সন্তুষ্ট হৈল পিতামাতার মন ॥ ১৫ ॥

āmi ta’ kariba tomā’ dum̐hāra sevana  
I  indeed  will do  of you  both  service  
śuniyā santuṣṭa haila pitāmātāra mana  
hearing  content  became  of the father and mother  the hearts

(15) “And I Myself will serve you both.” Hearing this, His father and mother were content at heart.

একদিন নৈবেদ্য-তাম্বূল খাইয়া ৷  
ভূমিতে পড়িলা প্রভু অচেতন হঞা ॥ ১৬ ॥

ekadina naivedya-tāmbūla khāiyā  
one day  offered  betel  having eaten  
bhūmite paḍilā prabhu acetana hañā  
to the ground  fell  the Lord  unconscious  becoming

(16) One day, after chewing betel that had been offered to the Deity, the Lord fell to the ground unconscious.

আস্তে-ব্যস্তে পিতা-মাতা মুখে দিল পানি ৷  
সুস্থ হঞা কহে প্রভু অপূর্ব্ব কাহিনী ॥ ১৭ ॥

āste-vyaste pitā-mātā mukhe dila pāni  
in haste  father and mother  on His face  put  water  
sustha hañā kahe prabhu apūrva kāhinī  
recovering  tells  the Lord  a wondrous  tale

(17) His father and mother hastily sprinkled water on His face, and when He came to Himself, the Lord told them a wondrous tale.

এথা হৈতে বিশ্বরূপ মোরে লঞা গেলা ৷  
সন্ন্যাস করহ তুমি’ আমারে কহিলা ॥ ১৮ ॥

ethā haite viśvarūpa more lañā gelā  
from here  Viśvarūpa  Me  taking  went  
sannyāsa karaha tumi’ āmāre kahilā  
sannyāsa  take  You  to Me  He said

(18) “Viśvarūpa took Me away from here and said to Me, ‘You must take sannyāsa.’

আমি কহি, — আমার অনাথ পিতা-মাতা ৷  
আমি বালক, — সন্ন্যাসের কিবা জানি কথা ॥ ১৯ ॥

āmi kahi, — āmāra anātha pitā-mātā  
I  said  —  My  helpless  father and mother  
āmi bālaka, — sannyāsera kibā jāni kathā  
I  am a boy  —  of sannyāsa  what  do I know  the matter

(19) “I answered, ‘My father and mother have no one to look after them, and I am still a boy — what do I know of sannyāsa?

গৃহস্থ হইয়া করি পিতৃ-মাতৃ-সেবন ৷  
ইহাতে সন্তুষ্ট হবেন লক্ষ্মী-নারায়ণ ॥ ২০ ॥

gṛhastha haiyā kari pitṛ-mātṛ-sevana  
a householder  being  I will do  father’s and mother’s  service  
ihāte santuṣṭa habena lakṣmī-nārāyaṇa  
by this  pleased  will be  Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa

(20) “ ‘As a householder I shall serve My father and mother, and this will please Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa.’

তবে বিশ্বরূপ ইঁহা পাঠাইল মোরে ৷  
মাতাকে কহিলা কোটি কোটি নমস্কারে ॥ ২১ ॥

tabe viśvarūpa im̐hā pāṭhāila more  
then  Viśvarūpa  here  sent  Me  
mātāke kahilā koṭi koṭi namaskāre  
to Mother  He sent word  millions  upon millions  of obeisances

(21) “Then Viśvarūpa sent Me back here and asked Me to convey His obeisances to Mother, millions upon millions of them.”

এইমত নানা লীলা করে গৌরহরি ৷  
কি কারণে লীলা, — ইহা বুঝিতে না পারি ॥ ২২ ॥

ei mata nānā līlā kare gaurahari  
in this way  various  pastimes  does  Gaurahari  
ki kāraṇe līlā, — ihā bujhite nā pāri  
for what  reason  the pastimes  —  this  to understand  not  I can

(22) Thus Gaurahari revels in many pastimes, and why He does so, I cannot understand.

কতদিন রহি’ মিশ্র গেলা পরলোক ৷  
মাতা-পুত্র দুঁহার বাড়িল হৃদি শোক ॥ ২৩ ॥

kata dina rahi’ miśra gelā para-loka  
some  days  remaining,  Miśra  went  to the next world  
mātā-putra dum̐hāra bāḍila hṛdi śoka  
mother and son  of both  grew  in the heart  grief

(23) Some time later Miśra left this world, and grief welled up in the hearts of mother and son.[^9]

বন্ধু-বান্ধব আসি’ দুঁহা প্রবোধিল ৷  
পিতৃক্রিয়া বিধিমতে ঈশ্বর করিল ॥ ২৪ ॥

bandhu-bāndhava āsi’ dum̐hā prabodhila  
friends and kinsmen  coming  both  consoled  
pitṛ-kriyā vidhi-mate īśvara karila  
the rites for the father  according to the injunctions  the Lord  performed

(24) Friends and kinsmen came and consoled them both, and the Lord performed His father’s funeral rites as the scriptures enjoin.

কত দিনে প্রভু চিত্তে করিলা চিন্তন ৷  
গৃহস্থ হইলাম, এবে চাহি গৃহধর্ম্ম ॥ ২৫ ॥

kata dine prabhu citte karilā cintana  
after some  days  the Lord  in His mind  made  reflection  
gṛhastha hailāma, ebe cāhi gṛha-dharma  
a householder  I have become,  now  I want  household dharma

(25) After a while the Lord thought to Himself, “I have become a householder; now I must take up the duties of household life.

গৃহিণী বিনা গৃহধর্ম্ম না হয় শোভন ৷  
এত চিন্তি, বিবাহ করিতে হৈল মন ॥ ২৬ ॥

gṛhiṇī vinā gṛha-dharma nā haya śobhana  
a wife  without  household dharma  not  is  graceful  
eta cinti, vivāha karite haila mana  
thus  thinking,  marriage  to make  turned  His mind

(26) “Yet without a wife household life has no grace.” Thinking thus, He set His mind on marriage.

ন গৃহং গৃহমিত্যাহুর্গৃহিণী গৃহমুচ্যতে ৷  
তয়া হি সহিতঃ সর্ব্বান্ পুরুষার্থান্ সমশ্নুতে ॥ ২৭ ॥

na gṛhaṁ gṛham ity āhur  
not  the house  a home  thus  they call  
gṛhiṇī gṛham ucyate  
the wife  the home  is called  
tayā hi sahitaḥ sarvān  
with her  indeed  together  all  
puruṣārthān samaśnute  
the goals of life  one attains

(27) “It is not the house that is called the home; the wife is called the home. Together with her one attains all the goals of life.”[^10]

দৈবে এক দিন প্রভু পড়িয়া আসিতে ৷  
বল্লভাচার্য্যের কন্যা দেখে গঙ্গা-পথে ॥ ২৮ ॥

daive eka dina prabhu paḍiyā āsite  
by providence  one  day  the Lord  from study  while returning  
vallabhācāryera kanyā dekhe gaṅgā-pathe  
Vallabhācārya’s  daughter  He sees  on the path to the Gaṅgā

(28) One day, by providence, as the Lord was coming home from His studies, He saw the daughter of Vallabhācārya on the way to the Gaṅgā.

পূর্ব্বসিদ্ধ ভাব দুঁহার উদয় করিলা ৷  
দৈবে বনমালী ঘটক শচী-স্থানে আইলা ॥ ২৯ ॥

pūrva-siddha bhāva dum̐hāra udaya karilā  
perfect from of old  the feelings  of both  arising  made  
daive vanamālī ghaṭaka śacī-sthāne āilā  
by providence  Vanamālī  the matchmaker  to Śacī’s house  came

(29) The love that had been theirs from of old awoke in them both, and, as providence would have it, Vanamālī the matchmaker came to Śacī’s house.[^11]

শচীর ইঙ্গিতে সম্বন্ধ করিল ঘটন ৷  
লক্ষ্মীকে বিবাহ কৈল শচীর নন্দন ॥ ৩০ ॥

śacīra iṅgite sambandha karila ghaṭana  
at Śacī’s  sign  the match  made  arrangement  
lakṣmīke vivāha kaila śacīra nandana  
Lakṣmī  married  did  Śacī’s  son

(30) At a sign from Śacī he arranged the match, and her son married Lakṣmī.

বিস্তারিয়া বর্ণিলা তাহা বৃন্দাবন-দাস ৷  
এই ত’ পৌগণ্ড-লীলার সূত্র প্রকাশ ॥ ৩১ ॥

vistāriyā varṇilā tāhā vṛndāvana-dāsa  
in detail  described  that  Vṛndāvana dāsa  
ei ta’ paugaṇḍa-līlāra sūtra prakāśa  
this  indeed  of the boyhood pastimes  the outline  presentation

(31) Vṛndāvana dāsa has described all this in detail; here I have set out the boyhood pastimes in outline.[^12]

পৌগণ্ড-লীলায় লীলা বহু ত’ প্রকার ৷  
বৃন্দাবন-দাস ইহাঁ করিয়াছেন বিস্তার ॥ ৩২ ॥

paugaṇḍa-līlāya līlā bahu ta’ prakāra  
in the boyhood pastimes  pastimes  many  indeed  kinds  
vṛndāvana-dāsa ihām̐ kariyāchena vistāra  
Vṛndāvana dāsa  them  has made  elaboration

(32) The Lord’s boyhood holds pastimes of many kinds, and Vṛndāvana dāsa has told them at length.

অতএব দিঙমাত্র ইহাঁ দেখাইল ৷  
‘চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে’ সর্ব্বলোকে খ্যাতি হৈল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ataeva diṅmātra ihām̐ dekhāila  
therefore  a mere glimpse  here  I have shown  
‘caitanya-maṅgale’ sarva-loke khyāti haila  
through the ‘Caitanya-maṅgala’  among all people  fame  came about

(33) Therefore I have given here no more than a glimpse of them; through the *Caitanya-maṅgala* they have become famous throughout the world.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৩৪ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(34) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

---

[^1]: A play on words: *sumanas* means both “flower” and “good mind”. Heading in the edition: “By the worship of Gaura even the evil-minded gain a good mind.” *Anubhāṣya*: an evil mind is one absorbed in anything other than Kṛṣṇa; a good mind is free of other desires, uncovered by knowledge and karma, and wholly given to the service of Kṛṣṇa.

[^2]: *Anubhāṣya* (on 15.4): *paugaṇḍa*, boyhood, is the period from the fifth year of life to the tenth. Heading in the edition: “Among the boyhood pastimes, study is the chief.”

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa is Viśvambhara, the Lord whose form is one with Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord first received His elementary schooling from Viṣṇu and Sudarśana, and then studied grammar with Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita.

[^5]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *Pañjī-ṭīkā* was a well-known commentary on grammar, and Mahāprabhu wrote His own notes (*ṭippanī*) on it.

[^6]: *Caitanya-maṅgala* is the original title of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (cf. Ādi 8.33–35). *Anubhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 4 and 6–10.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: in the *Bhakti-sandarbha* (299) Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī cites the *Skanda Purāṇa* and the *Agni Purāṇa*: to eat on Ekādaśī is a grave sin, and the fast on that day is the great vow of the Vaiṣṇavas, binding even on those who are not Vaiṣṇavas. Vaiṣṇavas never eat anything but *mahā-prasāda*, and so for them the Ekādaśī fast means giving up grains, even as *mahā-prasāda*.

[^8]: Heading in the edition: “When a son takes sannyāsa for the worship of Kṛṣṇa, the lines of his mother and father are delivered.”

[^9]: Heading in the edition: “Miśra’s disappearance from the world of men.” The *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Ādi 8): Miśra’s eternally perfect body passed from sight; the Lord wept bitterly for him, as Śrī Rāma wept at the passing of Daśaratha; to tell of this at length is painful, and so the author tells it briefly.

[^10]: In the edition the verse is headed “[A saying of smṛti (of the Lord?)]”; the *Anubhāṣya* refers to the *Mahābhārata* (Śānti-parva, 144.6). The goals of life (*puruṣārtha*), according to the *Anubhāṣya*, are dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa and the rest. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: it is not the dwelling but the wife that is called the home; together with the wife one enjoys all the goals of life.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: Vanamālī the matchmaker was a brāhmaṇa of Navadvīpa who arranged the Lord’s marriage. *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (49): Viśvāmitra, the matchmaker at Śrī Rāma’s wedding, and the brāhmaṇa whom Rukmiṇī sent to Śrī Kṛṣṇa are both now Vanamālī, who for this service came to be called Ācārya.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 10; Ādi 8 there describes at greater length the Lord’s sacred-thread ceremony (*upanayana*) and the gold He gave His mother.
