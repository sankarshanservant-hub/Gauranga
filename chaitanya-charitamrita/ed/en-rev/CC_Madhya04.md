“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 4  
**Relishing the Life of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

যস্মৈ দাতুং চোরয়ন্ ক্ষীরভাণ্ডং  
গোপীনাথঃ ক্ষীরচোরাভিধোঽভূৎ ৷  
শ্রীগোপালঃ প্রাদুরাসীদ্বশঃ সন্  
যৎপ্রেম্ণা তং মাধবেন্দ্রং নতোঽস্মি ॥ ১ ॥

yasmai dātuṁ corayan kṣīra-bhāṇḍaṁ  
to whom  to give  stealing  of sweet rice  the pot  
gopīnāthaḥ kṣīra-corābhidho ’bhūt  
Gopīnātha  Kṣīra-corā  named  became  
śrī gopālaḥ prādurāsīd vaśaḥ san  
Śrī Gopāla  appeared  conquered  being  
yat-premṇā taṁ mādhavendraṁ nato ’smi  
by whose love  to that  Mādhavendra  bowed down  am I

(1) I bow down to Mādhavendra Purī, for whose sake Gopīnātha stole a pot of sweet rice and so became known as Kṣīra-corā, “the thief of sweet rice”, and conquered by whose love Śrī Gopāla revealed Himself.[^1]

জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

নীলাদ্রিগমন, জগন্নাথ-দরশন ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম ভট্টাচার্য্য-প্রভুর মিলন ॥ ৩ ॥

এ সকল লীলা শ্রীদাস বৃন্দাবন ৷  
বিস্তারি’ বর্ণিয়াছেন উত্তম বর্ণন ॥ ৪ ॥

nīlādri-gamana, jagannātha-daraśana  
to Nīlādri  the coming  of Jagannātha  the sight  
sārvabhauma bhaṭṭācārya-prabhura milana  
of Sārvabhauma  Bhaṭṭācārya and the Lord  the meeting  
e sakala līlā śrīdāsa vṛndāvana  
these  all  pastimes  Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa  
vistāri’ varṇiyāchena uttama varṇana  
in detail  has described  in excellent  description

(3–4) The Lord’s coming to Nīlādri, His seeing Jagannātha, His meeting with Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya—all these pastimes Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa has described in detail, and his description is excellent.[^3]

সহজে বিচিত্র মধুর চৈতন্য-বিহার ৷  
বৃন্দাবনদাস-মুখে অমৃতের ধার ॥ ৫ ॥

sahaje vicitra madhura caitanya-vihāra  
by nature  wonderful  sweet  Caitanya’s  pastimes  
vṛndāvanadāsa-mukhe amṛtera dhāra  
Vṛndāvana dāsa’s  from the mouth  of nectar  a stream

(5) The pastimes of Caitanya are by their very nature wonderful and sweet, and from the lips of Vṛndāvana dāsa they flow as a stream of nectar.

অতএব তাহা বর্ণিলে হয় পুনরুক্তি ৷  
দম্ভ করি’ বর্ণি যদি নাহি তৈছে শক্তি ॥ ৬ ॥

ataeva tāhā varṇile haya punarukti  
therefore  them  if I describe  there will be  repetition  
dambha kari’ varṇi yadi nāhi taiche śakti  
pride  showing  I describe  if  there is not  such  power

(6) Therefore to describe them again would be mere repetition; and should I presume to describe them out of pride, I have no such power.

চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে যাহা করিল বর্ণন ৷  
সূত্ররূপে সেই লীলা করিয়ে সূচন ॥ ৭ ॥

caitanya-maṅgale yāhā karila varṇana  
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  what  he made  description  
sūtra-rūpe sei līlā kariye sūcana  
in the form of a summary  those  pastimes  I make  mention

(7) So I shall only touch briefly upon the pastimes he described in the *Caitanya-maṅgala*.[^4]

তাঁর সূত্রে আছে, তেঁহ না কৈল বর্ণন ৷  
যথা কথঞ্চিৎ করি’ সেই লীলা কথন ॥ ৮ ॥

tām̐ra sūtre āche, tem̐ha nā kaila varṇana  
in his  summary  they are  he  not  made  description  
yathā kathañcit kari’ sei līlā kathana  
as  best I can  making  of those  pastimes  the telling

(8) As best I can, I shall tell those pastimes he mentioned in his summary but did not describe.

অতএব তাঁর পায়ে করি নমস্কার ৷  
তাঁর পায় অপরাধ না হউক্ আমার ॥ ৯ ॥

ataeva tām̐ra pāye kari namaskāra  
therefore  at his  feet  I make  obeisance  
tām̐ra pāya aparādha nā hauk āmāra  
at his  feet  offence  not  may there be  my

(9) Therefore I bow down at his feet, praying that I may commit no offence at his feet.

এইমত মহাপ্রভু চলিলা নীলাচলে ৷  
চারিভক্ত সঙ্গে কৃষ্ণকীর্ত্তন কুতূহলে ॥ ১০ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu calilā nīlācale  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  went  to Nīlācala  
cāri bhakta saṅge kṛṣṇa-kīrtana kutūhale  
four  devotees  with  of Kṛṣṇa  kīrtana  joyfully

(10) In this way Mahāprabhu went on towards Nīlācala with four devotees, joyfully singing the kīrtana of Kṛṣṇa.

ভিক্ষা লাগি’ একদিন এক গ্রাম গিয়া ৷  
আপনে অনেক অন্ন আনিল মাগিয়া ॥ ১১ ॥

bhikṣā lāgi’ ekadina eka grāma giyā  
alms  for  one day  to a  village  going  
āpane aneka anna ānila māgiyā  
Himself  much  food  brought  having begged

(11) One day He went to a village for alms and Himself begged and brought back a great deal of food.

পথে বড় বড় দানী বিঘ্ন নাহি করে ৷  
তা’ সবারে কৃপা করি’ আইলা রেমুণারে ॥ ১২ ॥

pathe baḍa baḍa dānī vighna nāhi kare  
on the way  great  great  toll-keepers  hindrance  not  make  
tā’ sabāre kṛpā kari’ āilā remuṇāre  
on them  all  mercy  bestowing  He came  to Remuṇā

(12) On the way the many powerful ferrymen at the crossings did not hinder Him; bestowing His mercy on them all, He came to Remuṇā.[^5]

রেমুণাতে গোপীনাথ পরম-মোহন ৷  
ভক্তি করি’ কৈল প্রভু তাঁর দরশন ॥ ১৩ ॥

remuṇāte gopīnātha parama-mohana  
in Remuṇā  Gopīnātha  supremely  enchanting  
bhakti kari’ kaila prabhu tām̐ra daraśana  
devotion  with  took  the Lord  His  darśana

(13) In Remuṇā the Lord, full of devotion, took darśana of the supremely enchanting Gopīnātha.[^6]

তাঁর পাদপদ্ম নিকট প্রণাম করিতে ৷  
তাঁর পুষ্প-চূড়া পড়িল প্রভুর মাথাতে ॥ ১৪ ॥

tām̐ra pāda-padma nikaṭa praṇāma karite  
His  lotus feet  near  obeisance  while making  
tām̐ra puṣpa-cūḍā paḍila prabhura māthāte  
His  flower crown  fell  the Lord’s  on the head

(14) As the Lord bowed down near His lotus feet, Gopīnātha’s crown of flowers fell onto His head.

চূড়া পাঞা মহাপ্রভুর আনন্দিত মন ৷  
বহু নৃত্যগীত কৈল লঞা ভক্তগণ ॥ ১৫ ॥

cūḍā pāñā mahāprabhura ānandita mana  
the crown  receiving  Mahāprabhu’s  delighted  mind  
bahu nṛtya-gīta kaila lañā bhakta-gaṇa  
much  dancing and singing  did  taking along  the devotees

(15) Receiving the crown, Mahāprabhu was filled with joy, and with His devotees He danced and sang at length.

প্রভুর প্রভাব দেখি’ প্রেম রূপ-গুণ ৷  
বিস্মিত হইলা গোপীনাথের দাসগণ ॥ ১৬ ॥

prabhura prabhāva dekhi’ prema rūpa-guṇa  
the Lord’s  majesty  seeing  love  beauty and qualities  
vismita hailā gopīnāthera dāsa-gaṇa  
astonished  became  of Gopīnātha  the servants

(16) Seeing the Lord’s majesty, His love, His beauty and His qualities, the servants of Gopīnātha were struck with wonder.

নানারূপে প্রীত্যে কৈল প্রভুর সেবন ৷  
সেই রাত্রি তাঁহা প্রভু করিলা বঞ্চন ॥ ১৭ ॥

nānā-rūpe prītye kaila prabhura sevana  
in many ways  with love  did  the Lord’s  service  
sei rātri tām̐hā prabhu karilā vañcana  
that  night  there  the Lord  spent

(17) Lovingly they served the Lord in many ways, and that night the Lord stayed there.

মহাপ্রসাদ-ক্ষীর-লোভে রহিলা প্রভু তথা ৷  
পূর্ব্বে ঈশ্বরপুরী তাঁরে কহিয়াছেন কথা ॥ ১৮ ॥

mahāprasāda-kṣīra-lobhe rahilā prabhu tathā  
for the mahā-prasāda sweet rice  out of eagerness  stayed  the Lord  there  
pūrve īśvara-purī tām̐re kahiyāchena kathā  
before  Īśvara Purī  to Him  had told  the story

(18) The Lord stayed there out of eagerness for the mahā-prasāda of sweet rice, for Īśvara Purī had once told Him its story.[^7]

‘ক্ষীরচোরা গোপীনাথ’ প্রসিদ্ধ তাঁর নাম ৷  
ভক্তগণে কহে প্রভু সেই ত’ আখ্যান ॥ ১৯ ॥

‘kṣīra-corā gopīnātha’ prasiddha tām̐ra nāma  
Kṣīra-corā  Gopīnātha  famous  His  name  
bhakta-gaṇe kahe prabhu sei ta’ ākhyāna  
to the devotees  tells  the Lord  that  very  story

(19) His famous name is “Kṣīra-corā Gopīnātha”, and the Lord told the devotees the story behind it.

পূর্ব্বে মাধবপুরী লাগি’ ক্ষীর কৈল চুরি ৷  
অতএব নাম হৈল ‘ক্ষীরচোরা হরি’ ॥ ২০ ॥

pūrve mādhava-purī lāgi’ kṣīra kaila curi  
formerly  Mādhava Purī  for the sake of  sweet rice  did  theft  
ataeva nāma haila ‘kṣīra-corā hari’  
therefore  the name  became  Kṣīra-corā Hari

(20) Long ago He stole sweet rice for Mādhava Purī, and so He came to be called “Kṣīra-corā Hari”, Hari the thief of sweet rice.[^8][^9]

পূর্ব্বে শ্রীমাধব-পুরী আইলা বৃন্দাবন ৷  
ভ্রমিতে ভ্রমিতে গেলা যথা গোবর্দ্ধন ॥ ২১ ॥

pūrve śrī-mādhava-purī āilā vṛndāvana  
formerly  Śrī Mādhava Purī  came  to Vṛndāvana  
bhramite bhramite gelā yathā govarddhana  
wandering  wandering  went  where  Govardhana

(21) Once Śrī Mādhava Purī came to Vṛndāvana, and wandering on and on, he reached Govardhana.

প্রেমে মত্ত, — নাহি তাঁর রাত্রিদিন-জ্ঞান ৷  
ক্ষণে উঠে, ক্ষণে পড়ে, নাহি স্থানাস্থান ॥ ২২ ॥

preme matta, — nāhi tām̐ra rātridina-jñāna  
with love  maddened  not  his  of night and day  awareness  
kṣaṇe uṭhe, kṣaṇe paḍe, nāhi sthānāsthāna  
now  rises  now  falls  no  sense of place

(22) Mad with love, he could not tell night from day. Now he would rise, now he would fall, with no thought of where he was.

শৈল পরিক্রমা করি’ গোবিন্দকুণ্ডে আসি’ ৷  
স্নান করি’ বৃক্ষতলে আছে সন্ধ্যায় বসি’ ॥ ২৩ ॥

śaila parikramā kari’ govinda-kuṇḍe āsi’  
the hill  circumambulation  making  to Govinda-kuṇḍa  coming  
snāna kari’ vṛkṣa-tale āche sandhyāya vasi’  
bath  taking  beneath a tree  was  in the evening  seated

(23) After walking around the hill, he came to Govinda-kuṇḍa and bathed there, and in the evening he sat beneath a tree.[^10]

গোপ-বালক এক দুগ্ধ-ভাণ্ড লঞা ৷  
আসি’ আগে ধরি’ কিছু বলিল হাসিয়া ॥ ২৪ ॥

gopa-bālaka eka dugdha-bhāṇḍa lañā  
a cowherd boy  one  milk  pot  carrying  
āsi’ āge dhari’ kichu balila hāsiyā  
coming  before him  holding it  something  said  smiling

(24) A cowherd boy came carrying a pot of milk. Holding it out before him, He smiled and said:

পুরী, এই দুগ্ধ লঞা কর তুমি পান ৷  
মাগি’ কেনে নাহি খাও, কিবা কর ধ্যান ॥ ২৫ ॥

purī, ei dugdha lañā kara tumi pāna  
Purī  this  milk  taking  do  you  drinking  
māgi’ kene nāhi khāo, kibā kara dhyāna  
having begged  why  not  you eat  what  do you  meditate on

(25) “Purī, take this milk and drink it. Why do you not beg for food and eat? What are you meditating on?”

বালকের সৌন্দর্য্যে পুরীর হইল সন্তোষ ৷  
তাহার মধুর-বাক্যে গেল ভোগ-শোষ ॥ ২৬ ॥

bālakera saundarye purīra haila santoṣa  
the boy’s  by the beauty  Purī’s  became  delight  
tāhāra madhura-vākye gela bhoga-śoṣa  
His  by the sweet words  went away  hunger and thirst

(26) Purī was delighted by the boy’s beauty, and at His sweet words his hunger and thirst vanished.[^11]

পুরী কহে, — কে তুমি, কাঁহা তোমার বাস ৷  
কেমতে জানিলে, আমি করি উপবাস ॥ ২৭ ॥

purī kahe, — ke tumi, kām̐hā tomāra vāsa  
Purī  said  who  You  where  Your  dwelling  
ke-mate jānile, āmi kari upavāsa  
how  did You know  I  keep  a fast

(27) Purī said, “Who are You? Where do You live? How did You know that I was fasting?”

বালক কহে, — গোপ আমি, এই গ্রামে বসি ৷  
আমার গ্রামেতে কেহ না রহে উপবাসী ॥ ২৮ ॥

bālaka kahe, — gopa āmi, ei grāme vasi  
the boy  said  a cowherd  I  in this  village  live  
āmāra grāmete keha nā rahe upavāsī  
My  in the village  anyone  not  remains  fasting

(28) The boy said, “I am a cowherd and live in this village. In My village no one goes hungry.[^12]

কেহ অন্ন মাগি’ খায়, কেহ দুগ্ধাহার ৷  
অযাচক-জনে আমি দিয়ে ত’ আহার ॥ ২৯ ॥

keha anna māgi’ khāya, keha dugdhāhāra  
some  food  begging  eat  some  live on milk  
ayācaka-jane āmi diye ta’ āhāra  
to one who does not beg  I  give  indeed  food

(29) Some beg for food and eat, and some live on milk; but to one who does not beg I Myself give food.

জল নিতে স্ত্রীগণ তোমারে দেখি’ গেল ৷  
স্ত্রীগণ দুগ্ধ দিয়া আমারে পাঠাইল ॥ ৩০ ॥

jala nite strī-gaṇa tomāre dekhi’ gela  
water  to fetch  the women  you  seeing  went  
strī-gaṇa dugdha diyā āmāre pāṭhāila  
the women  milk  giving  Me  sent

(30) The women who came to fetch water saw you, and they have given Me milk and sent Me to you.

গোদোহন করিতে চাহি, শীঘ্র আমি যাব ৷  
পুনঃ আসি’ আমি এই ভাণ্ড লইব ॥ ৩১ ॥

go-dohana karite cāhi, śīghra āmi yāba  
cow-milking  to do  I want  quickly  I  shall go  
punaḥ āsi’ āmi ei bhāṇḍa laiba  
again  coming  I  this  pot  shall take

(31) I must go now to milk the cows, but I will come back and take this pot.”

এত বলি’ গেলা বালক না দেখিয়ে আর ৷  
মাধব-পুরীর চিত্তে হইল চমৎকার ॥ ৩২ ॥

eta bali’ gelā bālaka nā dekhiye āra  
this  saying  went  the boy  not  was seen  any more  
mādhava-purīra citte haila camatkāra  
Mādhava Purī’s  in the heart  arose  wonder

(32) Saying this, the boy went away and was seen no more, and Mādhava Purī’s heart was filled with wonder.[^13]

দুগ্ধ পান করি’ ভাণ্ড ধুঞা রাখিল ৷  
বাট দেখে, সে বালক পুনঃ না আইল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

dugdha pāna kari’ bhāṇḍa dhuñā rākhila  
milk  drinking  having done  the pot  washing  set aside  
bāṭa dekhe, se bālaka punaḥ nā āila  
the path  he watches  that  boy  again  not  came

(33) He drank the milk, washed the pot and set it aside. He kept watching the path, but the boy did not come back.[^14]

বসি’ নাম লয় পুরী, নাহি নিদ্রা হয় ৷  
শেষ রাত্রে তন্দ্রা হৈল, — বাহ্যবৃত্তি-লয় ॥ ৩৪ ॥

vasi’ nāma laya purī, nāhi nidrā haya  
sitting  the Name  takes  Purī  no  sleep  comes  
śeṣa rātre tandrā haila, — bāhya-vṛtti-laya  
at the end  of the night  a trance  came  of the outward senses  the stilling

(34) Purī sat chanting the Name, and no sleep came to him. At the end of the night a trance came over him, and his outward senses were stilled.[^15]

স্বপ্নে দেখে, সেই বালক সম্মুখে আসিঞা ৷  
এক কুঞ্জে লঞা গেল হাতেতে ধরিঞা ॥ ৩৫ ॥

svapne dekhe, sei bālaka sammukhe āsiñā  
in a dream  he sees  that  boy  before him  coming  
eka kuñje lañā gela hātete dhariñā  
to a  grove  taking  went  by the hand  holding

(35) In a dream he saw that same boy come before him, take him by the hand and lead him into a grove.

কুঞ্জ দেখাঞা কহে, — আমি এই কুঞ্জে রই ৷  
শীত-বৃষ্টি-বাতাগ্নিতে মহা-দুঃখ পাই ॥ ৩৬ ॥

kuñja dekhāñā kahe, — āmi ei kuñje rai  
the grove  showing  He says  I  in this  grove  stay  
śīta-vṛṣṭi-vātāgnite mahā-duḥkha pāi  
from cold, rain, wind and heat  great suffering  I get

(36) Showing him the grove, He said, “I stay in this grove, and I suffer greatly from cold and rain, wind and heat.

গ্রামের লোক আনি’ আমা কাঢ় কুঞ্জ হৈতে ৷  
পর্ব্বত-উপরি লঞা রাখ ভালমতে ॥ ৩৭ ॥

grāmera loka āni’ āmā kāḍha kuñja haite  
of the village  the people  bringing  Me  take out  the grove  from  
parvata-upari lañā rākha bhāla-mate  
on top of the hill  taking  keep  properly

(37) Bring the people of the village and take Me out of the grove. Carry Me up the hill and keep Me there properly.[^16]

এক মঠ করি’ তাঁহা করহ স্থাপন ৷  
বহু শীতল জলে কর শ্রীঅঙ্গ মার্জ্জন ॥ ৩৮ ॥

eka maṭha kari’ tām̐hā karaha sthāpana  
a  temple  making  there  do  installation  
bahu śītala jale kara śrī-aṅga mārjana  
with much  cool  water  do  of the holy body  bathing

(38) Build a temple there and install Me in it, and bathe My body with plenty of cool water.

বহুদিন তোমার পথ করি নিরীক্ষণ ৷  
কবে আসি’ মাধব আমা করিবে সেবন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

bahu-dina tomāra patha kari nirīkṣaṇa  
for many days  your  path  I do  watching  
kabe āsi’ mādhava āmā karibe sevana  
when  coming  Mādhava  Me  will do  service

(39) For many days I have been watching the path for you, wondering when Mādhava would come and serve Me.[^17]

তোমার প্রেমবশে করি’ সেবা অঙ্গীকার ৷  
দর্শন দিয়া নিস্তারিব সকল সংসার ॥ ৪০ ॥

tomāra prema-vaśe kari’ sevā aṅgīkāra  
your  conquered by love  making  of service  acceptance  
darśana diyā nistāriba sakala saṁsāra  
darśana  giving  I shall deliver  all  the world

(40) Conquered by your love, I shall accept your service, and by giving My darśana I shall deliver the whole world.

‘শ্রীগোপাল’ নাম মোর, — গোবর্দ্ধনধারী ৷  
বজ্রের স্থাপিত, আমি ইঁহা অধিকারী ॥ ৪১ ॥

‘śrī-gopāla’ nāma mora, — govarddhana-dhārī  
Śrī Gopāla  name  My  the Holder of Govardhana  
vajrera sthāpita, āmi im̐hā adhikārī  
by Vajra  installed  I  here  the master

(41) My name is Śrī Gopāla; I am the Holder of Govardhana. I was installed by Vajra, and I am the master here.[^18]

শৈল-উপরি হৈতে আমা কুঞ্জে লুকাঞা ৷  
ম্লেচ্ছ-ভয়ে সেবক মোর গেল পলাঞা ॥ ৪২ ॥

śaila-upari haite āmā kuñje lukāñā  
the top of the hill  from  Me  in the grove  hiding  
mleccha-bhaye sevaka mora gela palāñā  
of the mlecchas  out of fear  servant  My  went  fleeing

(42) In fear of the *mlecchas* My servant brought Me down from the top of the hill, hid Me in this grove and fled.

সেই হৈতে রহি আমি এই কুঞ্জ-স্থানে ৷  
ভাল, আইলা তুমি, আমা কাঢ় সাবধানে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

sei haite rahi āmi ei kuñja-sthāne  
that  since  stay  I  in this  grove  
bhāla, āilā tumi, āmā kāḍha sāvadhāne  
good  have come  you  Me  take out  carefully

(43) Since then I have stayed in this grove. It is good that you have come; take Me out carefully.”

এত বলি’, যেই বালক অন্তর্দ্ধান হৈল ৷  
জাগিয়া মাধবপুরী বিচার করিল ॥ ৪৪ ॥

eta bali’, yei bālaka antarddhāna haila  
this  saying  that  boy  disappeared  
jāgiyā mādhava-purī vicāra karila  
waking  Mādhava Purī  reflection  made

(44) Saying this, the boy disappeared. Mādhava Purī woke up and began to reflect.

শ্রীকৃষ্ণকে দেখিনু মুঞি নারিনু চিনিতে ৷  
এত বলি, প্রেমাবেশে পড়িলা ভূমিতে ॥ ৪৫ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇake dekhinu muñi nārinu cinite  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa  saw  I  could not  recognise  
eta bali, premāveśe paḍilā bhūmite  
this  saying  in the rapture of love  fell  to the ground

(45) “I saw Śrī Kṛṣṇa, but I could not recognise Him!” Saying this, he fell to the ground, overwhelmed with love.

ক্ষণেক রোদন করি’ মন কৈল স্থির ৷  
আজ্ঞা-পালন লাগি’ হইলা সুস্থির ॥ ৪৬ ॥

kṣaṇeka rodana kari’ mana kaila sthira  
for a while  weeping  doing  mind  made  steady  
ājñā-pālana lāgi’ ha-ilā susthira  
the order’s fulfilment  for the sake of  became  composed

(46) He wept for a while, then steadied his mind and became composed in order to carry out the command.

প্রাতঃস্নান করি’ পুরী গ্রামমধ্যে গেলা ৷  
সব লোক একত্র করি’ কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৪৭ ॥

prātaḥ-snāna kari’ purī grāma-madhye gelā  
morning bath  taking  Purī  into the village  went  
saba loka ekatra kari’ kahite lāgilā  
all  people  together  gathering  to speak  began

(47) After his morning bath Purī went into the village, gathered all the people together and said:[^19]

গ্রামের ঈশ্বর তোমার — গোবর্দ্ধনধারী ৷  
কুঞ্জে আছে, চল, তাঁরে বাহির যে করি ॥ ৪৮ ॥

grāmera īśvara tomāra — govarddhana-dhārī  
of the village  the Lord  your  the Holder of Govardhana  
kuñje āche, cala, tām̐re bāhira ye kari  
in the grove  He is  come  Him  out  then  let us bring

(48) “The Lord of your village, the Holder of Govardhana, is in the grove. Come, let us bring Him out.

কুঠারি কোদাল লহ দ্বার করিতে ৷  
অত্যন্ত নিবিড় কুঞ্জ, — নারি প্রবেশিতে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

kuṭhāri kodāla laha dvāra karite  
axes  spades  take  a way  to make  
atyanta niviḍa kuñja, — nāri praveśite  
very  dense  the grove  we cannot  enter

(49) Take axes and spades to clear a way, for the grove is so dense that we cannot get in.”

শুনি’ লোক তাঁর সঙ্গে চলিলা হরিষে ৷  
কুঞ্জ কাটি’ দ্বার করি’ করিলা প্রবেশে ॥ ৫০ ॥

śuni’ loka tām̐ra saṅge calilā hariṣe  
hearing  the people  him  with  went  joyfully  
kuñja kāṭi’ dvāra kari’ karilā praveśe  
the grove  cutting  a way  making  made  entry

(50) Hearing this, the people joyfully went with him. Cutting through the thicket, they made a way and entered.

ঠাকুর দেখিল মাটী-তৃণে আচ্ছাদিত ৷  
দেখি’ সব লোক হৈল আনন্দে বিস্মিত ॥ ৫১ ॥

ṭhākura dekhila māṭī-tṛṇe ācchādita  
the Deity  they saw  with earth and grass  covered  
dekhi’ saba loka haila ānande vismita  
seeing  all  people  became  with joy  amazed

(51) They found the Deity covered with earth and grass, and at the sight all the people were amazed and overjoyed.

আবরণ দূর করি’ করিল চিহ্নিতে ৷  
মহা-ভারি ঠাকুর — কেহ নারে চালাইতে ॥ ৫২ ॥

āvaraṇa dūra kari’ karila cihnite  
the covering  away  putting  made  visible  
mahā-bhāri ṭhākura — keha nāre cālāite  
very heavy  the Deity  no one  could  move

(52) They cleared away the covering and the Deity came into view. He was immensely heavy, and no one could move Him.

মহা-মহা-বলিষ্ঠ লোক একত্র করিঞা ৷  
পর্ব্বত-উপরি গেল পুরী ঠাকুর লঞা ॥ ৫৩ ॥

mahā-mahā-baliṣṭha loka ekatra kariñā  
very very strong  men  together  gathering  
parvata-upari gela purī ṭhākura lañā  
to the top of the hill  went  Purī  the Deity  taking

(53) Gathering the very strongest men, Purī took the Deity up to the top of the hill.

পাথরের সিংহাসনে ঠাকুর বসাইল ৷  
বড় এক পাথর পৃষ্ঠে অবলম্ব দিল ॥ ৫৪ ॥

pātharera siṁhāsane ṭhākura vasāila  
of stone  on a throne  the Deity  they seated  
baḍa eka pāthara pṛṣṭhe avalamba dila  
big  one  stone  behind  as support  they gave

(54) They seated the Deity on a throne of stone, and set a large stone behind Him for support.

গ্রামের ব্রাহ্মণ সব নবঘট লঞা ৷  
গোবিন্দ-কুণ্ডের জল আনিল ছানিঞা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

grāmera brāhmaṇa saba nava ghaṭa lañā  
of the village  the brāhmaṇas  all  new  pots  taking  
govinda-kuṇḍera jala ānila chāniñā  
of Govinda-kuṇḍa  water  brought  straining

(55) All the brāhmaṇas of the village took new pots and brought water from Govinda-kuṇḍa, straining it.[^20]

নবশতঘট জল কৈল উপনীত ৷  
নানা বাদ্য-ভেরী বাজে, স্ত্রীগণে গায় গীত ॥ ৫৬ ॥

nava śata-ghaṭa jala kaila upanīta  
nine  hundred pots  of water  made  brought  
nānā vādya-bherī bāje, strī-gaṇe gāya gīta  
various  instruments and drums  sound  the women  sing  songs

(56) Nine hundred pots of water were brought. Instruments and drums of every kind resounded, and the women sang.

কেহ গায়, কেহ নাচে, মহোৎসব হৈল ৷  
দধি, দুগ্ধ, ঘৃত আইল গ্রামে যত ছিল ॥ ৫৭ ॥

keha gāya, keha nāce, mahotsava haila  
some  sing  some  dance  a great festival  there was  
dadhi, dugdha, ghṛta āila grāme yata chila  
yogurt  milk  ghee  came  in the village  as much as  there was

(57) Some sang and some danced; it became a great festival. All the yogurt, milk and ghee in the village was brought.

ভোগ-সামগ্রী আইল সন্দেশাদি যত ৷  
নানা উপহার, তাহা কহিতে পারি কত ॥ ৫৮ ॥

bhoga-sāmagrī āila sandeśādi yata  
foodstuffs for the offering  came  sandeśa and the rest  all  
nānā upahāra, tāhā kahite pāri kata  
various  gifts  them  to describe  can I  how many

(58) Foodstuffs for the offering came, *sandeśa* and every other sweet, and gifts of so many kinds that I cannot describe them all.

তুলসী আদি, পুষ্প, বস্ত্র আইল অনেক ৷  
আপনে মাধবপুরী কৈল অভিষেক ॥ ৫৯ ॥

tulasī ādi, puṣpa, vastra āila aneka  
tulasī  and so on  flowers  cloth  came  in abundance  
āpane mādhava-purī kaila abhiṣeka  
himself  Mādhava Purī  performed  the abhiṣeka

(59) Tulasī, flowers and cloth came in abundance, and Mādhava Purī himself performed the bathing ceremony (*abhiṣeka*).

অঙ্গমলা দূর করি’ করাইল স্নান ৷  
বহু তৈল দিয়া কৈল শ্রীঅঙ্গ চিক্কণ ॥ ৬০ ॥

aṅgamalā dūra kari’ karāila snāna  
the dirt of the body  away  removing  had Him  bathed  
bahu taila diyā kaila śrī-aṅga cikkaṇa  
much  oil  applying  made  the holy body  glossy

(60) Removing the dirt from His body, he bathed Him, and with plenty of oil he made the holy body glisten.[^21]

পঞ্চগব্য, পঞ্চামৃতে স্নান করাঞা ৷  
মহাস্নান করাইল শত ঘট দিঞা ॥ ৬১ ॥

pañca-gavya, pañcāmṛte snāna karāñā  
with pañca-gavya  with pañcāmṛta  bath  giving  
mahā-snāna karāila śata ghaṭa diñā  
the great bath  he performed  hundred  pots  giving

(61) He bathed Him with *pañca-gavya* and *pañcāmṛta*, and then performed the great bath (*mahā-snāna*) with a hundred pots of water.[^22]

পুনঃ তৈল দিয়া কৈল শ্রীঅঙ্গ চিক্কণ ৷  
শঙ্খ-গন্ধোদকে কৈল স্নান সমাধান ॥ ৬২ ॥

punaḥ taila diyā kaila śrī-aṅga cikkaṇa  
again  oil  applying  made  the holy body  glossy  
śaṅkha-gandhodake kaila snāna samādhāna  
with fragrant water from a conch  made  of the bath  completion

(62) Again he made the holy body glisten with oil, and completed the bath with fragrant water poured from a conch.[^23]

শ্রীঅঙ্গ মার্জ্জন করি’ বস্ত্র পরাইল ৷  
চন্দন, তুলসী, পুষ্প-মালা অঙ্গে দিল ॥ ৬৩ ॥

śrī-aṅga mārjana kari’ vastra parāila  
the holy body  wiping  having done  cloth  dressed  
candana, tulasī, puṣpa-mālā aṅge dila  
sandal  tulasī  flower garlands  on the body  placed

(63) Wiping the holy body dry, he dressed Him and adorned His body with sandal paste, tulasī and garlands of flowers.

ধূপ, দীপ, করি’ নানা ভোগ লাগাইল ৷  
দধি-দুগ্ধ-সন্দেশাদি যত কিছু আইল ॥ ৬৪ ॥

dhūpa, dīpa, kari’ nānā bhoga lāgāila  
incense  lamp  offering  various  foods  offered  
dadhi-dugdha-sandeśādi yata kichu āila  
yogurt, milk, sandeśa and the rest  whatever  all  had come

(64) After offering incense and a lamp, he offered many kinds of food—yogurt, milk, sweets and everything else that had been brought.

সুবাসিত জল নবপাত্রে সমর্পিল ৷  
আচমন দিয়া সে তাম্বূল নিবেদিল ॥ ৬৫ ॥

suvāsita jala nava-pātre samarpila  
fragrant  water  in new vessels  offered  
ācamana diyā se tāmbūla nivedila  
ācamana  giving  then  tāmbūla  offered

(65) He offered fragrant water in new vessels, and after giving water to rinse the mouth (*ācamana*), he offered betel (*tāmbūla*).

আরাত্রিক করি’ কৈল বহুত স্তবন ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ করি’ কৈল আত্ম-সমর্পণ ॥ ৬৬ ॥

ārātrika kari’ kaila bahuta stavana  
ārati  performing  made  many  prayers  
daṇḍavat kari’ kaila ātma-samarpaṇa  
prostration  making  made  self-surrender

(66) After the *ārati* he offered many prayers, and falling flat like a rod, he surrendered himself.

গ্রামের যতেক তণ্ডুল, দালি, গোধূম-চূর্ণ ৷  
সকল আনিয়া দিল পর্ব্বত হৈল পূর্ণ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

grāmera yateka taṇḍula, dāli, godhūma-cūrṇa  
of the village  all the  rice  dāl  wheat flour  
sakala āniyā dila parvata haila pūrṇa  
all  bringing  they gave  the hill  became  full

(67) All the rice, *dāl* and wheat flour in the village was brought and given, until the hilltop was full.

কুম্ভকার ঘরে ছিল যে মৃদ্ভাজন ৷  
সব আনাইল প্রাতে চড়িল রন্ধন ॥ ৬৮ ॥

kumbhakāra ghare chila ye mṛd-bhājana  
of the potters  in the houses  were  which  earthen pots  
saba ānāila prāte caḍila randhana  
all  had brought  in the morning  began  cooking

(68) All the earthen pots in the potters’ houses were brought, and in the morning the cooking began.

দশবিপ্র অন্ন রান্ধি’ করে এক স্তূপ ৷  
জনা-পাঁচ রান্ধে ব্যঞ্জনাদি নানা সূপ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

daśa-vipra anna rāndhi’ kare eka stūpa  
ten brāhmaṇas  rice  cooking  make  a  heap  
janā-pām̐ca rāndhe vyañjanādi nānā sūpa  
five people  cook  vegetables and the rest  various  soups

(69) Ten brāhmaṇas cooked rice and piled it into a great heap, while some five others cooked vegetable dishes and many kinds of soup.[^24]

বন্য শাক-ফল-মূলে বিবিধ ব্যঞ্জন ৷  
কেহ বড়া-বড়ি-কড়ি করে বিপ্রগণ ॥ ৭০ ॥

vanya śāka-phala-mūle vividha vyañjana  
wild  from greens, fruits and roots  various  vegetable dishes  
keha baḍā-baḍi-kaḍi kare vipra-gaṇa  
some  baḍā, baḍi and kaḍi  make  the brāhmaṇas

(70) From wild greens, fruits and roots they made dishes of every kind, and some of the brāhmaṇas made *baḍā*, *baḍi* and *kaḍi*.

জনা পাঁচ সাত রুটি করে রাশি রাশি ৷  
অন্ন-ব্যঞ্জন সব রহে ঘৃতে ভাসি’ ॥ ৭১ ॥

janā pām̐ca sāta ruṭi kare rāśi rāśi  
people  five  seven  flatbreads  make  heaps  upon heaps  
anna-vyañjana saba rahe ghṛte bhāsi’  
rice and vegetables  all  remain  in ghee  floating

(71) Five or seven people made flatbreads (*ruṭi*) in heaps upon heaps, and all the rice and vegetables were swimming in ghee.

নববস্ত্র পাতি’ তাহে পলাশের পাত ৷  
রান্ধি’ রান্ধি’ তার উপর রাশি কৈল ভাত ॥ ৭২ ॥

nava-vastra pāti’ tāhe palāśera pāta  
new cloth  spreading  on it  of palāśa  leaves  
rāndhi’ rāndhi’ tāra upara rāśi kaila bhāta  
cooking  cooking  on it  on top  a heap  made  the rice

(72) They spread new cloth and laid *palāśa* leaves on it, and as the rice was cooked, they heaped it up on top.

তার পাশে রুটি-রাশির পর্ব্বত হইল ৷  
সূপ-আদি-ব্যঞ্জন-ভাণ্ড চৌদিকে ধরিল ॥ ৭৩ ॥

tāra pāśe ruṭi-rāśira parvata haila  
its  beside  of heaped flatbreads  a mountain  there was  
sūpa-ādi-vyañjana-bhāṇḍa caudike dharila  
of soups and other vegetable dishes  the pots  all around  they placed

(73) Beside it rose a mountain of flatbreads, and pots of soup and vegetables were set all around.

তার পাশে দধি, দুগ্ধ, মাঠা শিখরিণী ৷  
পায়স, মথনি, সর পাশে ধরি’ আনি’ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

tāra pāśe dadhi, dugdha, māṭhā śikhariṇī  
its  beside  yogurt  milk  buttermilk  śikhariṇī  
pāyasa, mathani, sara pāśe dhari’ āni’  
sweet rice  fresh butter  cream  beside  placing  having brought

(74) Beside them they brought and set out yogurt, milk, buttermilk, *śikhariṇī*, sweet rice, fresh butter and cream.[^25]

হেনমতে অন্নকূট করিল সাজন ৷  
পুরী-গোসাঞি গোপালেরে কৈল সমর্পণ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

hena-mate anna-kūṭa karila sājana  
in this way  the Annakūṭa  made  arrangement  
purī-gosāñi gopālere kaila samarpaṇa  
Purī Gosāñi  to Gopāla  made  offering

(75) In this way the Annakūṭa, the mountain of food, was arranged, and Purī Gosāñi offered it to Gopāla.[^26]

অনেক ঘট ভরি’ দিল সুবাসিত জল ৷  
বহুদিনের ক্ষুধায় গোপাল খাইল সকল ॥ ৭৬ ॥

aneka ghaṭa bhari’ dila suvāsita jala  
many  pots  filling  gave  fragrant  water  
bahu-dinera kṣudhāya gopāla khāila sakala  
of many days  from hunger  Gopāla  ate  everything

(76) Many pots were filled with fragrant water and offered, and Gopāla, hungry after so many days, ate everything.

যদ্যপি গোপাল সব অন্ন-ব্যঞ্জন খাইল ৷  
তাঁর হস্ত-স্পর্শে পুনঃ তেমনি হইল ॥ ৭৭ ॥

yadyapi gopāla saba anna-vyañjana khāila  
although  Gopāla  all  rice and vegetables  ate  
tām̐ra hasta-sparśe punaḥ temani haila  
His  by the touch of the hand  again  as before  it became

(77) Although Gopāla ate all the rice and vegetables, by the touch of His hand everything became full again, just as before.[^27]

ইহা অনুভব কৈল মাধব-গোসাঞি ৷  
তাঁর ঠাঞি গোপালের লুকান কিছু নাই ॥ ৭৮ ॥

ihā anubhava kaila mādhava-gosāñi  
this  perception  had  Mādhava Gosāñi  
tām̐ra ṭhāñi gopālera lukāna kichu nāi  
from him  of Gopāla  hidden  anything  is not

(78) Mādhava Gosāñi perceived this, for nothing of Gopāla’s is hidden from him.[^28]

একদিনের উদ্যোগে ঐছে মহোৎসব কৈল ৷  
গোপাল-প্রভাবে হয়, অন্যে না জানিল ॥ ৭৯ ॥

eka-dinera udyoge aiche mahotsava kaila  
of one day  by the preparation  such  a festival  held  
gopāla-prabhāve haya, anye nā jānila  
by Gopāla’s power  it happens  others  not  knew

(79) Such a festival was held after only a single day’s preparation. It came about by Gopāla’s power, but no one else knew it.

আচমন দিয়া দিল বিড়ক-সঞ্চয় ৷  
আরতি করিল, লোকে করে জয় জয় ॥ ৮০ ॥

ācamana diyā dila viḍaka-sañcaya  
ācamana  giving  offered  of betel rolls  a store  
ārati karila, loke kare jaya jaya  
ārati  performed  the people  cry  jaya  jaya

(80) After giving water to rinse the mouth, he offered a store of betel rolls; then he performed *ārati*, and the people cried, “Jaya! Jaya!”[^29]

শয্যা করাইল, নুতন খাট আনাঞা ৷  
নববস্ত্র আনি’ তার উপরে পাতিয়া ॥ ৮১ ॥

śayyā karāila, nutana khāṭa ānāñā  
a bed  had made  new  cot  having brought  
nava vastra āni’ tāra upare pātiyā  
new  cloth  bringing  on it  on top  spreading

(81) He had a bed prepared: a new cot was brought, and new cloth was spread over it.[^30]

তৃণ-টাটি দিয়া চারি দিক্ আবরিল ৷  
উপরেতে এক টাটি দিয়া আচ্ছাদিল ॥ ৮২ ॥

tṛṇa-ṭāṭi diyā cāri dik āvarila  
grass screens  with  four  sides  enclosed  
uparete eka ṭāṭi diyā ācchādila  
above  one  screen  putting  covered

(82) The four sides were enclosed with screens of grass, and one screen was placed on top as a roof.

পুরী-গোসাঞি আজ্ঞা দিল সকল ব্রাহ্মণে ৷  
আ-বাল-বৃদ্ধ গ্রামের লোক করাহ ভোজনে ॥ ৮৩ ॥

purī-gosāñi ājñā dila sakala brāhmaṇe  
Purī Gosāñi  order  gave  to all  the brāhmaṇas  
ā-bāla-vṛddha grāmera loka karāha bhojane  
from children to the old  of the village  the people  make eat

(83) Purī Gosāñi told all the brāhmaṇas, “Feed all the people of the village, from the children to the old.”

সবে বসি’ ক্রমে ক্রমে ভোজন করিল ৷  
ব্রাহ্মণ-ব্রাহ্মণীগণে আগে খাওয়াইল ॥ ৮৪ ॥

sabe vasi’ krame krame bhojana karila  
all  sitting  in turn  in turn  meal  had  
brāhmaṇa-brāhmaṇī-gaṇe āge khāoyāila  
the brāhmaṇas and their wives  first  they fed

(84) Everyone sat down and ate in turn; the brāhmaṇas and their wives were fed first.

অন্য গ্রামের লোক যত দেখিতে আইল ৷  
গোপাল দেখিয়া সেহ প্রসাদ পাইল ॥ ৮৫ ॥

anya grāmera loka yata dekhite āila  
of other  villages  the people  all who  to see  came  
gopāla dekhiyā seha prasāda pāila  
Gopāla  seeing  they too  prasāda  received

(85) Everyone who came from other villages to look on saw Gopāla and received *prasāda* as well.

দেখিয়া পুরীর প্রভাব লোকে চমৎকার ৷  
পূর্ব্ব অন্নকূট যেন হৈল সাক্ষাৎকার ॥ ৮৬ ॥

dekhiyā purīra prabhāva loke camatkāra  
seeing  Purī’s  power  the people  amazed  
pūrva annakūṭa yena haila sākṣātkāra  
former  Annakūṭa  as if  became  visible

(86) Seeing Purī’s power, the people were amazed: it was as if the Annakūṭa of old had come before their eyes.[^31]

সকল ব্রাহ্মণে পুরী বৈষ্ণব করিল ৷  
সেই সেই সেবা-মধ্যে সবা নিয়োজিল ॥ ৮৭ ॥

sakala brāhmaṇe purī vaiṣṇava karila  
all  the brāhmaṇas  Purī  Vaiṣṇavas  made  
sei sei sevā-madhye sabā niyojila  
in this  and that  service  all  engaged

(87) Purī made all the brāhmaṇas Vaiṣṇavas and engaged each of them in one service or another.[^32]

পুনঃ দিন-শেষে প্রভুর করাইল উত্থান ৷  
কিছু ভোগ লাগাইল করাইল জলপান ॥ ৮৮ ॥

punaḥ dina-śeṣe prabhura karāila utthāna  
again  at the end of the day  the Lord’s  had  rising  
kichu bhoga lāgāila karāila jala-pāna  
a little  food  offered  gave  water to drink

(88) At the close of the day he had the Lord woken again, offered Him a little food and gave Him water to drink.

গোপাল প্রকট হৈল, — দেশে শব্দ হৈল ৷  
আশ-পাশ-গ্রামের লোক দেখিতে আইল ॥ ৮৯ ॥

gopāla prakaṭa haila, — deśe śabda haila  
Gopāla  has appeared  in the land  the word  spread  
āśa-pāśa-grāmera loka dekhite āila  
of the surrounding villages  the people  to see  came

(89) The word spread through the land: “Gopāla has appeared!” And people from all the villages around came to see Him.

একেক দিন একেক গ্রামে লইল মাগিঞা ৷  
অন্নকূট করে সবে হরষিত হঞা ॥ ৯০ ॥

ekeka dina ekeka grāme laila māgiñā  
each  day  each  village  took  asking  
anna-kūṭa kare sabe haraṣita hañā  
Annakūṭa  hold  all  joyful  becoming

(90) Each village asked for a day of its own, and all of them joyfully held the Annakūṭa.

রাত্রিকালে ঠাকুরের করাইয়া শয়ন ৷  
পুরী গোসাঞি কৈল কিছু গব্য ভোজন ॥ ৯১ ॥

rātri-kāle ṭhākura karāiyā śayana  
at night  the Deity  having made  lie down  
purī gosāñi kaila kichu gavya bhojana  
Purī  Gosāñi  took  a little  milk  food

(91) At night, after putting the Deity to rest, Purī Gosāñi would take a little milk.[^33]

প্রাতঃকালে পুনঃ তৈছে করিল সেবন ৷  
অন্ন লঞা এক গ্রামের আইল লোকগণ ॥ ৯২ ॥

prātaḥ-kāle punaḥ taiche karila sevana  
in the morning  again  likewise  did  service  
anna lañā eka grāmera āila loka-gaṇa  
food  taking  of one  village  came  the people

(92) In the morning the service went on again in the same way, and the people of another village came, bringing food.

অন্ন, ঘৃত, দধি, দুগ্ধ, — গ্রামে যত ছিল ৷  
গোপালের আগে লোক আনিঞা ধরিল ॥ ৯৩ ॥

anna, ghṛta, dadhi, dugdha, — grāme yata chila  
grain  ghee  yogurt  milk  in the village  as much as  there was  
gopālera āge loka āniñā dharila  
of Gopāla  before  the people  bringing  placed

(93) The people brought all the grain, ghee, yogurt and milk there was in their village and set it before Gopāla.

পূর্ব্বদিন-প্রায় ব্রাহ্মণ করিল রন্ধন ৷  
তৈছে অন্নকূট গোপাল করিল ভোজন ॥ ৯৪ ॥

pūrva-dina-prāya brāhmaṇa karila randhana  
as on the day before  the brāhmaṇas  did  cooking  
taiche anna-kūṭa gopāla karila bhojana  
likewise  Annakūṭa  Gopāla  made  eating

(94) The brāhmaṇas cooked as on the day before, and Gopāla ate the Annakūṭa in the same way.

ব্রজবাসী লোকের কৃষ্ণে সহজ-প্রীতি ৷  
গোপালের সহজ-প্রীতি ব্রজবাসী-প্রতি ॥ ৯৫ ॥

vraja-vāsī lokera kṛṣṇe sahaja-prīti  
of Vraja  the people’s  for Kṛṣṇa  natural love  
gopālera sahaja-prīti vraja-vāsī-prati  
Gopāla’s  natural love  for the Vrajavāsīs

(95) The people of Vraja have a natural love for Kṛṣṇa, and Gopāla has a natural love for the people of Vraja.[^34]

মহাপ্রসাদ খাইল আসিয়া সব লোক ৷  
গোপাল দেখিয়া সবার খণ্ডে দুঃখ-শোক ॥ ৯৬ ॥

mahā-prasāda khāila āsiyā saba loka  
mahā-prasāda  ate  coming  all  the people  
gopāla dekhiyā sabāra khaṇḍe duḥkha-śoka  
Gopāla  seeing  of all  are dispelled  sorrow and grief

(96) Everyone came and ate the *mahā-prasāda*, and seeing Gopāla, they were all freed from sorrow and grief.

আশ-পাশ ব্রজভূমের যত লোক সব ৷  
এক এক দিন সবে করে মহোৎসব ॥ ৯৭ ॥

āśa-pāśa vraja-bhūmera yata loka saba  
surrounding  of the land of Vraja  as many  people  all  
eka eka dina sabe kare mahotsava  
one  by one  day  all  hold  a great festival

(97) All the people of the surrounding land of Vraja held the great festival, each on a day of their own.

গোপাল-প্রকট শুনি’ নানা দেশ হৈতে ৷  
নানা দ্রব্য লঞা লোক লাগিল আসিতে ॥ ৯৮ ॥

gopāla-prakaṭa śuni’ nānā deśa haite  
Gopāla’s appearance  hearing  various  lands  from  
nānā dravya lañā loka lāgila āsite  
various  goods  bringing  people  began  to come

(98) Hearing that Gopāla had appeared, people began to come from many lands, bringing all kinds of gifts.

মথুরার লোক সব বড় বড় ধনী ৷  
ভক্তি করি’ নানা দ্রব্য ভেট দেয় আনি’ ॥ ৯৯ ॥

mathurāra loka saba baḍa baḍa dhanī  
of Mathurā  the people  all  great  great  rich  
bhakti kari’ nānā dravya bheṭa deya āni’  
devotion  with  various  goods  as gifts  give  bringing

(99) The people of Mathurā, all of them very wealthy, brought many kinds of things and offered them with devotion.

স্বর্ণ, রৌপ্য, বস্ত্র, গন্ধ, ভক্ষ্য-উপহার ৷  
অসংখ্য আইসে, নিত্য বাড়িল ভাণ্ডার ॥ ১০০ ॥

svarṇa, raupya, vastra, gandha, bhakṣya-upahāra  
gold  silver  cloth  fragrances  gifts of food  
asaṅkhya āise, nitya bāḍila bhāṇḍāra  
countless  come  daily  grew  the storehouse

(100) Gold, silver, cloth, fragrances and gifts of food came in countless numbers, and the storehouse grew day by day.

এক মহাধনী ক্ষত্রিয় করাইল মন্দির ৷  
কেহ পাক-ভাণ্ডার কৈল, কেহ ত’ প্রাচীর ॥ ১০১ ॥

eka mahā-dhanī kṣatriya karāila mandira  
one  very wealthy  kṣatriya  had built  a temple  
keha pāka-bhāṇḍāra kaila, keha ta’ prācīra  
someone  kitchen and storehouse  made  someone  indeed  a wall

(101) A very wealthy *kṣatriya* had a temple built; someone built the kitchen and storehouse, and someone else the surrounding wall.

এক এক ব্রজবাসী এক এক গাভী দিল ৷  
দশসহস্র গাভী গোপালের হৈল ॥ ১০২ ॥

eka eka vraja-vāsī eka eka gābhī dila  
each  Vrajavāsī  one each  cow  gave  
daśa-sahasra gābhī gopālera haila  
ten thousand  cows  Gopāla’s  became

(102) Every Vrajavāsī gave a cow, and Gopāla came to have ten thousand cows.

গৌড় হইতে আইলা দুই বৈরাগী ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
পুরী-গোসাঞি রাখিল তারে করিয়া যতন ॥ ১০৩ ॥

gauḍa haite āilā dui vairāgī brāhmaṇa  
Gauḍa  from  came  two  renounced  brāhmaṇas  
purī-gosāñi rākhila tāre kariyā yatana  
Purī Gosāñi  kept  them  showing  care

(103) Two renounced brāhmaṇas came from Gauḍa, and Purī Gosāñi kept them with him, caring for them affectionately.[^35]

সেই দুই শিষ্য করি’ সেবা সমর্পিল ৷  
রাজ-সেবা হয়, — পুরীর আনন্দ বাড়িল ॥ ১০৪ ॥

sei dui śiṣya kari’ sevā samarpila  
those  two  disciples  making  service  entrusted  
rāja-sevā haya, — purīra ānanda bāḍila  
royal service  takes place  Purī’s  joy  grew

(104) He made those two his disciples and entrusted the service to them. The Deity was now served in royal style, and Purī’s joy grew.

এইমত বৎসর দুই করিল সেবন ৷  
একদিন পুরী-গোসাঞি দেখিল স্বপন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

ei-mata vatsara dui karila sevana  
in this way  years  two  did  service  
ekadina purī-gosāñi dekhila svapana  
one day  Purī Gosāñi  saw  a dream

(105) In this way he served for two years. Then one day Purī Gosāñi had a dream.

গোপাল কহে, পুরী আমার তাপ নাহি যায় ৷  
মলয়জ-চন্দন লেপ’, তবে সে জুড়ায় ॥ ১০৬ ॥

gopāla kahe, purī āmāra tāpa nāhi yāya  
Gopāla  says  Purī  My  heat  not  goes away  
malayaja-candana lepa’, tabe se juḍāya  
Malaya sandalwood  smear  then  it  will cool

(106) Gopāla said, “Purī, My heat does not go away. Smear Me with sandalwood from the Malaya hills, and then it will be cooled.[^36]

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to Mādhavendra Purī, the servant of ‘Kṣīra-corā Gopīnātha’”. *Anubhāṣya*: Gopīnātha — the famous Deity of the village of Remuṇā; Śrī Gopāla, the Holder of Govardhana — the Deity installed by Vajra; Mādhavendra Purī — the disciple of Lakṣmīpati, a guru of the Madhva sampradāya.

[^2]: The same verse as Ādi 4.2, except that the first line here has *gauracandra* in place of *śrī-caitanya*; translation as there, with that name.

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya-khaṇḍa, chapters 2 and 3.

[^4]: *Caitanya-maṅgala* is the original title of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (cf. Ādi 8.33–35).

[^5]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *dānī* — the ferrymen at the river crossings (to verse 153: those who take the toll for the crossing). *Anubhāṣya*: in Remuṇā dwells the Deity of Gopīnātha, and the samādhi of Rasikānanda Prabhu, the servant of Śyāmānanda Prabhu, is still there.

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Remuṇā is a village five miles west of Bāleśvara; there Kṣīra-corā Gopīnātha resides.

[^7]: Heading in the edition: the Lord stays there to honour the *kṣīra-prasāda*, which, as He had heard from His guru, awakens the memory of Kṛṣṇa’s love for His devotees.

[^8]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *mādhava-purī lāgi’*, without the genitive ending.

[^9]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mādhava Purī is Mādhavendra Purī.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: *śaila* — the hill of Govardhana, eight *krośas* from Mathurā.

[^11]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *bhoga-śoṣa* — the desire to eat.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: “The boy hides who He is”.

[^13]: Heading in the edition: “Having given the milk, the boy disappears”.

[^14]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *bāṭa* — the path, a word of the language of Utkala.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: *bāhya-vṛtti-laya* — he was absorbed in the *samādhi* of bhakti; this was not ordinary sleep.

[^16]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *kāḍha* — take out; *maṭha* — a temple.

[^17]: Heading in the edition: “Bhagavān waits for His devotee”.

[^18]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Vajra was the son of Aniruddha, Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s grandson; the Pāṇḍavas brought him from Dvārakā and made him king in Mathurā, and Śrī Gopāla, the Holder of Govardhana, is one of the Deities he installed.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya* (to verses 47–169): all this effort is for Kṛṣṇa’s pleasure; it is not an attempt to enjoy material objects.

[^20]: *Nava* here means “new” (cf. *nava-pātre*, 65; *nava-vastra*, 72, 81); the nine hundred pots are in verse 56.

[^21]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *aṅgamalā*, “the dirt of the body”; another reading is *amaṅgalā*, “what is inauspicious”. *Anubhāṣya*: following the *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*, the dirt is removed by rubbing with pulse flour and with brushes, and then the Lord is carefully anointed with fine fragrant oils (*abhyaṅga*).

[^22]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *pañca-gavya* — milk, yogurt, ghee, cow’s urine and cow dung; *pañcāmṛta* — yogurt, milk, ghee, honey and sugar. *Anubhāṣya*: according to the *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*, in the *mahā-snāna* the Deity is bathed with ghee, two thousand *palas* each of ghee and of water.

[^23]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *śaṅkha-gandhodaka* — water poured into a conch and made fragrant with flowers and sandal.

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya* (to verses 69–75): here we see how skilled the author, Śrīla Kavirāja Gosvāmī, is in every kind of cooking.

[^25]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *māṭhā* — buttermilk; *śikhariṇī* is made by mixing yogurt, milk, sugar, camphor and pepper; *mathani* — fresh butter (*navanīta* and *haiyaṅgava*).

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: *annakūṭa* — a mountain of rice (*anna*); *kūṭa* — a fort, a peak, a mountain.

[^27]: Heading in the edition: “Though Gopāla ate all the offering, at the touch of His hand everything became full again”.

[^28]: Heading in the edition: “The pastimes of Bhagavān are open only to the devotee”. *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 3.86–89.

[^29]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *viḍaka* — rolls of betel; *sañcaya* — a store.

[^30]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *nūtana*.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Annakūṭa of old — at the end of the Dvāpara-yuga, on Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s advice, the cowherds gave up the worship of Indra and honoured the cows, the brāhmaṇas and Govardhana Hill, and Kṛṣṇa, assuming another form and saying “I am the hill”, ate the abundant offerings (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.24). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: just such a great Annakūṭa as at that worship of Govardhana Mādhavendra Purī also held.

[^32]: Heading in the edition: “By Purī’s mercy the brāhmaṇas became Vaiṣṇavas”.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: *gavya* — milk.

[^34]: Heading in the edition: “The natural love of the people of Vraja and Kṛṣṇa for one another”.

[^35]: Heading in the edition: “Purī shows mercy to two renounced brāhmaṇas and entrusts the service to them”.

[^36]: *Anubhāṣya*: *malayaja* — born in the land of Malaya (Malabar, the southern part of the Western Ghats, also called Candana-giri, “the sandal mountain”); the word *malayaja* also means sandalwood itself. Heading in the edition: “In a dream Gopāla tells Purī that He wants sandal”.
