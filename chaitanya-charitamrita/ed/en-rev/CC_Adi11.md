“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 11  
**The Description of the Branches of the Nityānanda Trunk**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

নিত্যানন্দপদাম্ভোজ-ভৃঙ্গান্ প্রেমমধুন্মদান্ ৷  
নত্বাখিলান্ তেষু মুখ্যা লিখ্যন্তে কতিচিন্ময়া ॥ ১ ॥

nityānanda-padāmbhoja-  
of Nityānanda  of the feet  lotus-  
bhṛṅgān prema-madhunmadān  
the bees  by the honey of love  maddened  
natvākhilān teṣu mukhyā  
having bowed to  all  among them  the chief  
likhyante katicin mayā  
are written of  a few  by me

(1) I bow to all the devotees who, like bees at the lotus feet of Nityānanda, are maddened by the honey of love, and I shall write of a few, the foremost among them.

জয় জয় মহাপ্রভু শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
তাঁহার চরণাশ্রিত যেই, সেই ধন্য ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya mahāprabhu śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
glory  glory  Mahāprabhu  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  
tām̐hāra caraṇāśrita yei, sei dhanya  
His  sheltered at the feet  whoever,  he  is blessed

(2) Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever has taken shelter at His feet is truly blessed.

জয় জয় শ্রীঅদ্বৈত, জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয় জয় মহাপ্রভুর সর্ব্বভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-advaita, jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Advaita,  glory  to Nityānanda  
jaya jaya mahāprabhura sarva-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory  glory  of Mahāprabhu  to all the devotees

(3) Glory, glory to Śrī Advaita! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory, glory to all the devotees of Mahāprabhu!

তস্য শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-সৎপ্রেমামরশাখিনঃ ৷  
ঊর্দ্ধ্বস্কন্ধাবধূতেন্দোঃ শাখারূপান্ গণান্নুমঃ ॥ ৪ ॥

tasya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
of that  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya-  
sat-premāmara-śākhinaḥ  
eternal love  undying  tree  
ūrddhva-skandhāvadhūtendoḥ  
the high  trunk  the Avadhūta  moon  
śākhā-rūpān gaṇān numaḥ  
as branches  the devotees  we bow to

(4) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the undying tree of eternal love, and its tall trunk is the Avadhūta, Śrī Nityānanda, beautiful as the moon. I bow to His devotees, the branches of that trunk.[^1]

শ্রীনিত্যানন্দ-বৃক্ষের স্কন্ধ-গুরুতর ৷  
তাহাতে জন্মিল শাখা-প্রশাখা বিস্তর ॥ ৫ ॥

śrī-nityānanda-vṛkṣera skandha-gurutara  
Śrī  Nityānanda  of the tree  trunk  mighty  
tāhāte janmila śākhā-praśākhā vistara  
on it  grew  branches  offshoots  in abundance

(5) Śrī Nityānanda is the mighty trunk of the tree, and on it grew branches and offshoots in great number.[^2]

মালাকারের ইচ্ছা-জলে বাড়ে শাখাগণ ৷  
প্রেম-ফুল-ফলে ভরি’ ছাইল ভুবন ॥ ৬ ॥

mālākārera icchā-jale bāḍe śākhā-gaṇa  
the gardener’s  by the water of will  grow  the branches  
prema-phula-phale bhari’ chāila bhuvana  
with flowers and fruits of love  filled  covered  the world

(6) The branches grow by the water of the gardener’s will. Laden with the flowers and fruits of love, they have covered the world.[^3]

অসংখ্য অনন্ত গণ কে করু গণন ৷  
আপনা শোধিতে কহি মুখ্য মুখ্য জন ॥ ৭ ॥

asaṅkhya ananta gaṇa ke karu gaṇana  
countless  endless  devotees  who  can  count  
āpanā śodhite kahi mukhya mukhya jana  
myself  to purify  I tell  the chief  chief  persons

(7) The devotees are numberless, without end — who could count them all? For my own purification I shall name only the foremost.

শ্রীবীরভদ্র গোসাঞি — স্কন্ধ-মহাশাখা ৷  
তাঁর উপশাখা যত, অসংখ্য তার লেখা ॥ ৮ ॥

śrī-vīrabhadra gosāñi — skandha-mahāśākhā  
Śrī  Vīrabhadra  Gosāñi  of the trunk  the great branch  
tām̐ra upaśākhā yata, asaṅkhya tāra lekhā  
His  offshoots  as many,  countless  their  record

(8) Śrī Vīrabhadra Gosāñi is the great branch of the trunk, and the offshoots that spring from Him are beyond counting.[^4]

ঈশ্বর হইয়া কহায় মহা-ভাগবত ৷  
বেদধর্ম্মাতীত হঞা বেদধর্ম্মে রত ॥ ৯ ॥

īśvara haiyā kahāya mahā-bhāgavata  
the Lord  being  has Himself called  a great devotee  
veda-dharmātīta hañā veda-dharme rata  
beyond Vedic dharma  being  in Vedic dharma  engaged

(9) Though He is the Lord, He calls Himself a great devotee; though He is beyond the dharma of the Vedas, He keeps faithfully to it.[^5]

অন্তরে ঈশ্বর-চেষ্টা, বাহিরে নির্দ্দম্ভ ৷  
চৈতন্যভক্তি-মণ্ডপে তেঁহো মূলস্তম্ভ ॥ ১০ ॥

antare īśvara-ceṣṭā, bāhire nirdambha  
within  the Lord’s  conduct,  outwardly  without pride  
caitanya-bhakti-maṇḍape tem̐ho mūla-stambha  
of devotion to Caitanya  in the hall  He  the main pillar

(10) Within, He acts as the Lord; outwardly, He shows no pride. He is the main pillar in the hall of devotion to Śrī Caitanya.

অদ্যাপি যাঁহার কৃপা-মহিমা হইতে ৷  
চৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ গায় সকল জগতে ॥ ১১ ॥

adyāpi yām̐hāra kṛpā-mahimā haite  
even today  whose  of mercy  by the glory  
caitanya-nityānanda gāya sakala jagate  
Caitanya-Nityānanda  sings  the whole  world

(11) Even today, through the glory of His mercy, the whole world sings of Caitanya and Nityānanda.

সেই বীরভদ্র-গোসাঞির চরণ — শরণ ৷  
যাঁহার প্রসাদে হয় অভীষ্ট-পূরণ ॥ ১২ ॥

sei vīrabhadra-gosāñira caraṇa — śaraṇa  
that  Vīrabhadra  Gosāñi’s  feet  —  my shelter  
yām̐hāra prasāde haya abhīṣṭa-pūraṇa  
whose  by the grace  comes  desires’  fulfilment

(12) The feet of that Vīrabhadra Gosāñi are my shelter, for by His grace every desire is fulfilled.

শ্রীরামদাস আর, গদাধর দাস ৷  
চৈতন্য-গোসাঞির ভক্ত রহে তাঁর পাশ ॥ ১৩ ॥

śrī-rāmadāsa āra, gadādhara dāsa  
Śrī  Rāmadāsa  and  Gadādhara  dāsa  
caitanya-gosāñira bhakta rahe tām̐ra pāśa  
of Caitanya Gosāñi  devotees  stayed  his  at the side

(13) Śrī Rāmadāsa and Gadādhara dāsa, devotees of Caitanya Gosāñi, stayed at Nityānanda’s side.[^6]

নিত্যানন্দে আজ্ঞা দিল যবে গৌড়ে যাইতে ৷  
মহাপ্রভু এই দুই দিলা তাঁর সাথে ॥ ১৪ ॥

nityānande ājñā dila yabe gauḍe yāite  
to Nityānanda  the order  gave  when  to Gauḍa  to go  
mahāprabhu ei dui dilā tām̐ra sāthe  
Mahāprabhu  these  two  gave  Him  with

(14) When Mahāprabhu told Nityānanda to go to Gauḍa, He sent these two with Him.

অতএব দুইগণে দুঁহার গণন ৷  
মাধব-বাসুদেব ঘোষের এই বিবরণ ॥ ১৫ ॥

ataeva dui-gaṇe dum̐hāra gaṇana  
therefore  in both groups  of both  the counting  
mādhava-vāsudeva ghoṣera ei vivaraṇa  
of Mādhava  Vāsudeva  Ghoṣa  the same  account

(15) Therefore they are counted in both groups, and so it is with Mādhava and Vāsudeva Ghoṣa.[^7]

রামদাস — মুখ্যশাখা, সখ্য-প্রেমরাশি ৷  
ষোলসাঙ্গের কাষ্ঠ যেই তুলি’ কৈল বাঁশী ॥ ১৬ ॥

rāmadāsa — mukhya-śākhā, sakhya-prema-rāśi  
Rāmadāsa  —  a chief branch,  of friendly love  a treasure  
ṣolasāṅgera kāṣṭha yei tuli’ kaila bām̐śī  
of sixteen bearers  the log  which  lifting  made  a flute

(16) Rāmadāsa, a chief branch, was a treasury of loving friendship. He lifted a log that it took sixteen men to carry and made a flute of it.[^8]

গদাধর দাস গোপীভাবে পূর্ণানন্দ ৷  
যাঁর ঘরে দানকেলি কৈল নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ১৭ ॥

gadādhara dāsa gopībhāve pūrṇānanda  
Gadādhara  dāsa  in the mood of a gopī  full of bliss  
yām̐ra ghare dānakeli kaila nityānanda  
whose  in the house  the toll pastime  performed  Nityānanda

(17) Gadādhara dāsa was full of bliss in the mood of a gopī, and in his house Nityānanda revealed the *dāna-keli* pastime.[^9]

শ্রীমাধব ঘোষ — মুখ্য কীর্ত্তনীয়াগণে ৷  
নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু নৃত্য করে যাঁর গানে ॥ ১৮ ॥

śrī-mādhava ghoṣa — mukhya kīrtanīyā-gaṇe  
Śrī  Mādhava  Ghoṣa  —  foremost  among kīrtana singers  
nityānanda-prabhu nṛtya kare yām̐ra gāne  
Nityānanda Prabhu  dance  does  whose  to the singing

(18) Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa was foremost among the kīrtana singers, and Nityānanda Prabhu would dance to his singing.

বাসুদেব গীতে করে প্রভুর বর্ণনে ৷  
কাষ্ঠ-পাষাণ-দ্রবে যাহার শ্রবণে ॥ ১৯ ॥

vāsudeva gīte kare prabhura varṇane  
Vāsudeva  in songs  makes  of the Lord  description  
kāṣṭha-pāṣāṇa-drave yāhāra śravaṇe  
wood  stone  melt  whose  on hearing

(19) When Vāsudeva sang of the Lord, even wood and stone would melt to hear him.[^10]

মুরারি-চৈতন্যদাসের অলৌকিক লীলা ৷  
ব্যাঘ্র-গালে চড় মারে, সর্প-সনে খেলা ॥ ২০ ॥

murāri-caitanya-dāsera alaukika līlā  
of Murāri-caitanya dāsa  wondrous  pastimes  
vyāghra-gāle caḍa māre, sarpa-sane khelā  
the tiger  on the cheek  slap  strikes,  with snakes  play

(20) Wondrous were the pastimes of Murāri-caitanya dāsa: he would slap a tiger on the cheek and play with snakes.[^11]

নিত্যানন্দের গণ যত, — সব ব্রজসখা ৷  
শৃঙ্গ-বেত্র-গোপবেশ, শিরে শিখিপাখা ॥ ২১ ॥

nityānandera gaṇa yata, — saba vraja-sakhā  
of Nityānanda  the associates  as many  —  all  friends of Vraja  
śṛṅga-vetra-gopaveśa, śire śikhi-pākhā  
horn  staff  cowherd dress,  on the head  peacock feather

(21) All of Nityānanda’s associates are cowherd friends from Vraja. Each carries a horn and a staff and wears the dress of a cowherd, with a peacock feather on his head.[^12]

রঘুনাথ বৈদ্য উপাধ্যায় মহাশয় ৷  
যাঁহার দর্শনে কৃষ্ণপ্রেমভক্তি হয় ॥ ২২ ॥

raghunātha vaidya upādhyāya mahāśaya  
Raghunātha  Vaidya  Upādhyāya  a great soul  
yām̐hāra darśane kṛṣṇa-prema-bhakti haya  
whose  at the sight  love and devotion for Kṛṣṇa  arises

(22) Raghunātha Vaidya Upādhyāya was a great soul: the very sight of him awakened loving devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

সুন্দরানন্দ — নিত্যানন্দের শাখা, ভৃত্য মর্ম্ম ৷  
যাঁর সঙ্গে নিত্যানন্দ করে ব্রজনর্ম্ম ॥ ২৩ ॥

sundarānanda — nityānandera śākhā, bhṛtya marma  
Sundarānanda  —  Nityānanda’s  branch,  servant  intimate  
yām̐ra saṅge nityānanda kare vraja-narma  
whom  with  Nityānanda  enjoys  the playful pastimes of Vraja

(23) Sundarānanda, a branch of Nityānanda, was His intimate servant, and with him Nityānanda enjoyed the playful pastimes of Vraja.[^13]

কমলাকর পিপ্পলাই — অলৌকিক রীত ৷  
অলৌকিক প্রেম তাঁর ভুবনে বিদিত ॥ ২৪ ॥

kamalākara pippalāi — alaukika rīta  
Kamalākara  Pippalāi  —  extraordinary  ways  
alaukika prema tām̐ra bhuvane vidita  
extraordinary  love  his  in the world  known

(24) Kamalākara Pippalāi was extraordinary in his ways, and his extraordinary love is known throughout the world.[^14]

সূর্য্যদাস সরখেল, তাঁর ভাই কৃষ্ণদাস ৷  
নিত্যানন্দে দৃঢ় বিশ্বাস, প্রেমের নিবাস ॥ ২৫ ॥

sūryadāsa sarakhela, tām̐ra bhāi kṛṣṇadāsa  
Sūryadāsa  Sarakhela,  his  brother  Kṛṣṇadāsa  
nityānande dṛḍha viśvāsa, premera nivāsa  
in Nityānanda  firm  faith,  of love  the abode

(25) Sūryadāsa Sarakhela and his brother Kṛṣṇadāsa had firm faith in Nityānanda and were abodes of love.[^15]

শ্রীগৌরীদাস পণ্ডিতে প্রেমোদ্দণ্ডভক্তি ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেমা দিতে, নিতে, ধরে মহাশক্তি ॥ ২৬ ॥

śrīgaurīdāsa paṇḍite premoddaṇḍa-bhakti  
Śrī  Gaurīdāsa  in Paṇḍita  devotion of soaring love  
kṛṣṇa-premā dite, nite, dhare mahāśakti  
love for Kṛṣṇa  to give,  to take,  holds  great power

(26) Śrī Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita’s devotion soared with prema; he had great power both to give and to receive love for Kṛṣṇa.[^16]

নিত্যানন্দে সমর্পিল জাতি-কুল-পাঁতি ৷  
শ্রীচৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দে করি’ প্রাণপতি ॥ ২৭ ॥

nityānande samarpila jāti-kula-pām̐ti  
to Nityānanda  surrendered  caste  family  place at the common meal  
śrī-caitanya-nityānande kari’ prāṇapati  
Śrī  Caitanya-Nityānanda  making  the Lords of his life

(27) Gaurīdāsa surrendered to Nityānanda his caste, his family and his place in his community, and made Śrī Caitanya and Nityānanda the Lords of his life.[^17]

নিত্যানন্দ প্রভুর প্রিয় — পণ্ডিত পুরন্দর ৷  
প্রেমার্ণব-মধ্যে ফিরে যৈছন মন্দর ॥ ২৮ ॥

nityānanda prabhura priya — paṇḍita purandara  
of Nityānanda  Prabhu  dear  —  Paṇḍita  Purandara  
premārṇava-madhye phire yaichana mandara  
the ocean of love  within  moves  like  Mandara

(28) Purandara Paṇḍita was dear to Nityānanda Prabhu; he moved through the ocean of love like Mount Mandara.[^18]

পরমেশ্বরদাস — নিত্যানন্দৈক-শরণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তি পায়, তাঁরে যে করে স্মরণ ॥ ২৯ ॥

parameśvara-dāsa — nityānandaika-śaraṇa  
Parameśvara dāsa  —  Nityānanda  his sole shelter  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti pāya, tām̐re ye kare smaraṇa  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  attains,  him  who  does  remember

(29) Parameśvara dāsa took shelter in Nityānanda alone; whoever remembers him attains devotion to Kṛṣṇa.[^19]

শ্রীজগদীশ পণ্ডিত হয় জগৎ-পাবন ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেমামৃত বর্ষে, যেন বর্ষা ঘন ॥ ৩০ ॥

śrī jagadīśa paṇḍita haya jagat-pāvana  
Śrī  Jagadīśa  Paṇḍita  is  of the world  the purifier  
kṛṣṇa-premāmṛta varṣe, yena varṣā ghana  
the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa  rains,  like  rain  heavy

(30) Śrī Jagadīśa Paṇḍita purifies the whole world; like a heavy monsoon rain, he pours down the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa.[^20]

নিত্যানন্দ-প্রিয়ভৃত্য পণ্ডিত ধনঞ্জয় ৷  
অত্যন্ত বিরক্ত, সদা কৃষ্ণপ্রেমময় ॥ ৩১ ॥

nityānanda-priya-bhṛtya paṇḍita dhanañjaya  
of Nityānanda  dear  servant  Paṇḍita  Dhanañjaya  
atyanta virakta, sadā kṛṣṇa-premamaya  
extremely  detached,  always  full of love for Kṛṣṇa

(31) Dhanañjaya Paṇḍita, a dear servant of Nityānanda, was utterly detached and always full of love for Kṛṣṇa.[^21]

মহেশ পণ্ডিত — ব্রজের উদার গোপাল ৷  
ঢক্কাবাদ্যে নৃত্য করে প্রেমে মাতোয়াল ॥ ৩২ ॥

maheśa paṇḍita — vrajera udāra gopāla  
Maheśa  Paṇḍita  —  of Vraja  the generous  cowherd boy  
ḍhakkā-vādye nṛtya kare preme mātoyāla  
to the kettledrum  dance  does  in love  intoxicated

(32) Maheśa Paṇḍita is a generous cowherd boy of Vraja; to the beat of the kettledrum he would dance, drunk with love.[^22]

নবদ্বীপে পুরুষোত্তম পণ্ডিত মহাশয় ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-নামে যাঁর মহোন্মাদ হয় ॥ ৩৩ ॥

navadvīpe puruṣottama paṇḍita mahāśaya  
in Navadvīpa  Puruṣottama  Paṇḍita  a great soul  
nityānanda-nāme yām̐ra mahonmāda haya  
at Nityānanda’s name  whose  great madness  comes

(33) Puruṣottama Paṇḍita of Navadvīpa was a great soul; at the very name of Nityānanda he was seized by the great madness of love.[^23]

বলরাম দাস — কৃষ্ণপ্রেমরসাস্বাদী ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-নামে হয় পরম উন্মাদী ॥ ৩৪ ॥

balarāma dāsa — kṛṣṇa-prema-rasāsvādī  
Balarāma  dāsa  —  the rasa of love for Kṛṣṇa  tasting  
nityānanda-nāme haya parama unmādī  
at Nityānanda’s name  becomes  supremely  mad

(34) Balarāma dāsa tasted the rasa of love for Kṛṣṇa, and at the name of Nityānanda he was utterly beside himself.[^24]

মহাভাগবত যদুনাথ কবিচন্দ্র ৷  
যাঁহার হৃদয়ে নৃত্য করে নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

mahā-bhāgavata yadunātha kavicandra  
the great devotee  Yadunātha  Kavicandra  
yām̐hāra hṛdaye nṛtya kare nityānanda  
whose  in the heart  dance  does  Nityānanda

(35) Yadunātha Kavicandra was a great devotee, in whose heart Nityānanda dances.[^25]

রাঢ়ে যাঁর জন্ম কৃষ্ণদাস দ্বিজবর ৷  
শ্রীনিত্যানন্দের তিঁহো পরম কিঙ্কর ॥ ৩৬ ॥

rāḍhe yām̐ra janma kṛṣṇadāsa dvija-vara  
in Rāḍha  whose  birth  Kṛṣṇadāsa  the best of brāhmaṇas  
śrī-nityānandera tim̐ho parama kiṅkara  
of Śrī Nityānanda  he  the foremost  servant

(36) Kṛṣṇadāsa, an excellent brāhmaṇa born in Rāḍha, was a foremost servant of Śrī Nityānanda.

কালা-কৃষ্ণদাস বড় বৈষ্ণবপ্রধান ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-চন্দ্র বিনা নাহি জানে আন ॥ ৩৭ ॥

kālā-kṛṣṇadāsa baḍa vaiṣṇava-pradhāna  
Kālā  Kṛṣṇadāsa  great  among Vaiṣṇavas  foremost  
nityānanda-candra vinā nāhi jāne āna  
Nityānanda  the moon  but  not  knows  another

(37) Kālā Kṛṣṇadāsa was a great Vaiṣṇava, among the foremost; he knew no one but the moonlike Nityānanda.[^26]

শ্রীসদাশিব কবিরাজ — বড় মহাশয় ৷  
শ্রীপুরুষোত্তমদাস — তাঁহার তনয় ॥ ৩৮ ॥

śrī-sadāśiva kavirāja — baḍa mahāśaya  
Śrī  Sadāśiva  Kavirāja  —  great  soul  
śrī-puruṣottama-dāsa — tām̐hāra tanaya  
Śrī  Puruṣottama  dāsa  —  his  son

(38) Śrī Sadāśiva Kavirāja was a great soul, and Śrī Puruṣottama dāsa was his son.[^27]

আজন্ম নিমগ্ন নিত্যানন্দের চরণে ৷  
নিরন্তর বাল্য-লীলা করে কৃষ্ণ-সনে ॥ ৩৯ ॥

ājanma nimagna nityānandera caraṇe  
from birth  immersed  in Nityānanda’s  feet  
nirantara bālya-līlā kare kṛṣṇa-sane  
constantly  childhood pastimes  does  Kṛṣṇa  with

(39) From his birth he was absorbed in the feet of Nityānanda, and he ceaselessly played childhood games with Kṛṣṇa.[^28]

তাঁর পুত্র — মহাশয় শ্রীকানু ঠাকুর ৷  
যাঁর দেহে রহে কৃষ্ণ-প্রেমামৃতপূর ॥ ৪০ ॥

tām̐ra putra — mahāśaya śrī-kānu ṭhākura  
his  son  —  the great soul  Śrī  Kānu  Ṭhākura  
yām̐ra dehe rahe kṛṣṇa-premāmṛta-pūra  
whose  in the body  dwells  of the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa  a flood

(40) His son was the great soul Śrī Kānu Ṭhākura, whose body overflowed with the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa.[^29]

মহাভাগবত-শ্রেষ্ঠ দত্ত উদ্ধারণ ৷  
সর্ব্বভাবে সেবে নিত্যানন্দের চরণ ॥ ৪১ ॥

mahā-bhāgavata-śreṣṭha datta uddhāraṇa  
of great devotees  the best  Datta  Uddhāraṇa  
sarva-bhāve seve nityānandera caraṇa  
in every way  serves  Nityānanda’s  feet

(41) Uddhāraṇa Datta, the best of great devotees, served the feet of Nityānanda in every way.[^30]

আচার্য্য বৈষ্ণবানন্দ ভক্তি-অধিকারী ৷  
পূর্ব্বে নাম ছিল যাঁর ‘রঘুনাথ পুরী’ ॥ ৪২ ॥

ācārya vaiṣṇavānanda bhakti-adhikārī  
Ācārya  Vaiṣṇavānanda  of devotion  the possessor  
pūrve nāma chila yām̐ra ‘raghunātha purī’  
formerly  name  was  whose  ‘Raghunātha  Purī’

(42) Ācārya Vaiṣṇavānanda was rich in devotion; his former name was Raghunātha Purī.[^31]

বিষ্ণুদাস, নন্দন, গঙ্গাদাস — তিন ভাই ৷  
পূর্ব্বে যাঁর ঘরে ছিল ঠাকুর নিতাই ॥ ৪৩ ॥

viṣṇudāsa, nandana, gaṅgādāsa — tina bhāi  
Viṣṇudāsa,  Nandana,  Gaṅgādāsa  —  three  brothers  
pūrve yām̐ra ghare chila ṭhākura nitāi  
formerly  whose  in the house  stayed  Ṭhākura  Nitāi

(43) Viṣṇudāsa, Nandana and Gaṅgādāsa were three brothers, in whose house Ṭhākura Nitāi once stayed.[^32]

নিত্যানন্দভৃত্য — পরমানন্দ উপাধ্যায় ৷  
শ্রীজীব পণ্ডিত নিত্যানন্দ গুণ গায় ॥ ৪৪ ॥

nityānanda-bhṛtya — paramānanda upādhyāya  
Nityānanda’s servant  —  Paramānanda  Upādhyāya  
śrī-jīva paṇḍita nityānanda guṇa gāya  
Śrī  Jīva  Paṇḍita  Nityānanda’s  qualities  sings

(44) Paramānanda Upādhyāya was a servant of Nityānanda, and Śrī Jīva Paṇḍita sang of Nityānanda’s virtues.[^33]

পরমানন্দ গুপ্ত — কৃষ্ণভক্ত মহামতি ৷  
পূর্ব্বে যাঁর ঘরে নিত্যানন্দের বসতি ॥ ৪৫ ॥

paramānanda gupta — kṛṣṇa-bhakta mahāmati  
Paramānanda  Gupta  —  Kṛṣṇa’s devotee  great-minded  
pūrve yām̐ra ghare nityānandera vasati  
formerly  whose  in the house  Nityānanda’s  dwelling

(45) Paramānanda Gupta was a devotee of Kṛṣṇa, great in wisdom; Nityānanda once lived in his house.[^34]

নারায়ণ, কৃষ্ণদাস, আর মনোহর ৷  
দেবানন্দ — চারি ভাই নিতাই-কিঙ্কর ॥ ৪৬ ॥

nārāyaṇa, kṛṣṇadāsa, āra manohara  
Nārāyaṇa,  Kṛṣṇadāsa,  and  Manohara  
devānanda — cāri bhāi nitāi-kiṅkara  
Devānanda  —  four  brothers  Nitāi’s servants

(46) Nārāyaṇa, Kṛṣṇadāsa, Manohara and Devānanda — these four brothers were servants of Nitāi.

হোড় কৃষ্ণদাস — নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু-প্রাণ ৷  
শ্রীনিত্যানন্দ-পদ বিনা নাহি জানে আন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

hoḍa kṛṣṇadāsa — nityānanda-prabhu-prāṇa  
Hoḍa  Kṛṣṇadāsa  —  Nityānanda Prabhu  his life  
śrī-nityānanda-pada vinā nāhi jāne āna  
Śrī  Nityānanda’s  feet  but  not  knows  another

(47) To Hoḍa Kṛṣṇadāsa, Nityānanda Prabhu was life itself; he knew nothing but the feet of Śrī Nityānanda.[^35]

নকড়ি, মুকুন্দ, সূর্য্য, মাধব, শ্রীধর ৷  
রামানন্দ বসু, জগন্নাথ, মহীধর ॥ ৪৮ ॥

nakaḍi, mukunda, sūrya, mādhava, śrīdhara  
Nakaḍi,  Mukunda,  Sūrya,  Mādhava,  Śrīdhara  
rāmānanda vasu, jagannātha, mahīdhara  
Rāmānanda  Vasu,  Jagannātha,  Mahīdhara

(48) Among Nityānanda’s devotees were also Nakaḍi, Mukunda, Sūrya, Mādhava, Śrīdhara, Rāmānanda Vasu, Jagannātha and Mahīdhara;[^36]

শ্রীমন্ত, গোকুলদাস, হরিহরানন্দ ৷  
শিবাই, নন্দাই, অবধূত পরমানন্দ ॥ ৪৯ ॥

śrīmanta, gokula-dāsa, hariharānanda  
Śrīmanta,  Gokuladāsa,  Hariharānanda  
śivāi, nandāi, avadhūta paramānanda  
Śivāi,  Nandāi,  the Avadhūta  Paramānanda

(49) Śrīmanta, Gokuladāsa, Hariharānanda, Śivāi, Nandāi and Paramānanda the Avadhūta;

বসন্ত, নবনী হোড়, গোপাল, সনাতন ৷  
বিষ্ণাই হাজরা, কৃষ্ণানন্দ, সুলোচন ॥ ৫০ ॥

vasanta, navanī hoḍa, gopāla, sanātana  
Vasanta,  Navanī  Hoḍa,  Gopāla,  Sanātana  
viṣṇāi hājarā, kṛṣṇānanda, sulocana  
Viṣṇāi  Hājarā,  Kṛṣṇānanda,  Sulocana

(50) Vasanta, Navanī Hoḍa, Gopāla, Sanātana, Viṣṇāi Hājarā, Kṛṣṇānanda and Sulocana;[^37]

কংসারি সেন, রামসেন, রামচন্দ্র কবিরাজ ৷  
গোবিন্দ, শ্রীরঙ্গ, মুকুন্দ, তিন কবিরাজ ॥ ৫১ ॥

kaṁsāri sena, rāmasena, rāmacandra kavirāja  
Kaṁsāri  Sena,  Rāmasena,  Rāmacandra  Kavirāja  
govinda, śrīraṅga, mukunda, tina kavirāja  
Govinda,  Śrīraṅga,  Mukunda,  three  kavirājas

(51) Kaṁsāri Sena, Rāmasena and Rāmacandra Kavirāja, and the three physicians — Govinda, Śrīraṅga and Mukunda;[^38]

পীতাম্বর, মাধবাচার্য্য, দাস দামোদর ৷  
শঙ্কর, মুকুন্দ, জ্ঞানদাস, মনোহর ॥ ৫২ ॥

pītāmbara, mādhavācārya, dāsa dāmodara  
Pītāmbara,  Mādhavācārya,  Dāsa  Dāmodara  
śaṅkara, mukunda, jñāna-dāsa, manohara  
Śaṅkara,  Mukunda,  Jñānadāsa,  Manohara

(52) Pītāmbara, Mādhavācārya, Dāmodara dāsa, Śaṅkara, Mukunda, Jñānadāsa and Manohara;

নর্ত্তক গোপাল, রামভদ্র, গৌরাঙ্গদাস ৷  
নৃসিংহচৈতন্য, মীনকেতন রামদাস ॥ ৫৩ ॥

nartaka gopāla, rāmabhadra, gaurāṅga-dāsa  
the dancer  Gopāla,  Rāmabhadra,  Gaurāṅgadāsa  
nṛsiṁha-caitanya, mīnaketana rāmadāsa  
Nṛsiṁha-caitanya,  Mīnaketana  Rāmadāsa

(53) Gopāla the dancer, Rāmabhadra, Gaurāṅgadāsa, Nṛsiṁha-caitanya and Mīnaketana Rāmadāsa.

বৃন্দাবনদাস — নারায়ণীর নন্দন ৷  
‘চৈতন্য-মঙ্গল’ যেঁহো করিল রচন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

vṛndāvana-dāsa — nārāyaṇīra nandana  
Vṛndāvana  dāsa  —  Nārāyaṇī’s  son  
‘caitanya-maṅgala’ yem̐ho karila racana  
‘Caitanya-maṅgala’  who  composed

(54) Vṛndāvana dāsa, the son of Nārāyaṇī, composed the *Caitanya-maṅgala*.[^39]

ভাগবতে কৃষ্ণলীলা বর্ণিলা বেদব্যাস ৷  
চৈতন্য-লীলাতে ব্যাস — বৃন্দাবন দাস ॥ ৫৫ ॥

bhāgavate kṛṣṇalīlā varṇilā vedavyāsa  
in the Bhāgavata  Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  described  Vedavyāsa  
caitanya-līlāte vyāsa — vṛndāvana dāsa  
in Caitanya’s pastimes  the Vyāsa  —  Vṛndāvana  dāsa

(55) Veda-vyāsa described the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa in the *Bhāgavata*; the Vyāsa of Caitanya’s pastimes is Vṛndāvana dāsa.

সর্ব্বশাখা-শ্রেষ্ঠ বীরভদ্র গোসাঞি ৷  
তাঁর উপশাখা যত, তার অন্ত নাই ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sarvaśākhā-śreṣṭha vīrabhadra gosāñi  
of all branches  the best  Vīrabhadra  Gosāñi  
tām̐ra upaśākhā yata, tāra anta nāi  
His  offshoots  as many,  of them  end  there is not

(56) Vīrabhadra Gosāñi is the best of all the branches, and His offshoots are without end.

অনন্ত নিত্যানন্দগণ — কে করু গণন ৷  
আত্মপবিত্রতা-হেতু লিখিলাঙ কত জন ॥ ৫৭ ॥

ananta nityānanda-gaṇa — ke karu gaṇana  
endless  Nityānanda’s devotees  —  who  can  count  
ātma-pavitratā-hetu likhilāṅa kata jana  
self-purification  for the sake of  I have written of  some  persons

(57) Nityānanda’s devotees are endless — who could count them? I have written of some of them for my own purification.

এই সর্ব্বশাখা পূর্ণ — পক্ব-প্রেমফলে ৷  
যারে দেখে, তারে দিয়া ভাসাইল সকলে ॥ ৫৮ ॥

ei sarva-śākhā pūrṇa — pakva-prema-phale  
these  all  branches  full  —  with ripe fruits of love  
yāre dekhe, tāre diyā bhāsāila sakale  
whom  they see,  to him  giving  flooded  everyone

(58) All these branches are laden with the ripe fruits of love. They gave them to whomever they met and flooded all with love.[^40]

অনর্গল প্রেম সবার, চেষ্টা অনর্গল ৷  
প্রেম দিতে, কৃষ্ণ দিতে (সবে) ধরে মহাবল ॥ ৫৯ ॥

anargala prema sabāra, ceṣṭā anargala  
unbounded  love  of all,  endeavour  unbounded  
prema dite, kṛṣṇa dite (sabe) dhare mahābala  
love  to give,  Kṛṣṇa  to give  (all)  hold  great power

(59) Their love knows no bounds, nor does their striving; each of them has great power to give love and to give Kṛṣṇa Himself.

সংক্ষেপে কহিলাঙ এই নিত্যানন্দ-গণ ৷  
যাঁহার অবধি না পায় ‘সহস্র-বদন’ ॥ ৬০ ॥

saṅkṣepe kahilāṅ ei nityānanda-gaṇa  
in brief  I have told  these  Nityānanda’s devotees  
yām̐hāra avadhi nā pāya ‘sahasra-vadana’  
whose  limit  not  reaches  ‘the thousand-mouthed’

(60) Thus I have told in brief of Nityānanda’s devotees, whose limit even the thousand-mouthed Ananta cannot reach.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৬১ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(61) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “The description of the branches of the Nityānanda trunk.” *Anubhāṣya*: the eternal, imperishable tree of love is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Himself, who bears the name Gaura; the tall trunk of that tree is Nityānanda Prabhu, the moon; His associates are the branches of that trunk.

[^2]: Heading in the edition: “By Mahāprabhu’s will the branches of Nityānanda grow and become pre-eminent.”

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the gardener is Śrī Mahāprabhu.

[^4]: Heading in the edition: “The branch of Śrī Vīracandra Gosāñi.” *Anubhāṣya*: Vīrabhadra was the son of Śrī Nityānanda and Vasudhā and the disciple of Jāhnavā Mātā. According to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (67), the vyūha of Saṅkarṣaṇa who lies on the ocean of milk appeared as Vīracandra, non-different from Śrī Caitanya.

[^5]: Heading in the edition: “His glory: though Viṣṇu Himself, He acts as a Vaiṣṇava.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Vīracandra Prabhu is the vyūha of Śrī Saṅkarṣaṇa who lies on the ocean of milk; though He is the Lord Himself, He regarded Himself as a Vaiṣṇava.

[^6]: “His side” is Nityānanda’s: by verse 14, Mahāprabhu sent these two to Gauḍa with Him; so too the edition’s heading to verse 14: “Together with Nitāi both preach in Gauḍa.” Heading in the edition: “Ṭhākura Abhirāma (the first of the twelve gopālas) and Dāsa Gadādhara.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Rāmadāsa is Abhirāma dāsa. *Anubhāṣya*: Abhirāma, whose very life was Nityānanda, is Śrīdāma of Vraja (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 126); at Nityānanda’s command he became an ācārya and preacher of bhakti, and love for Kṛṣṇa awoke in anyone he struck with his whip “Jayamaṅgala”. On Gadādhara dāsa see Ādi 10.53.

[^7]: Heading in the edition: “Mādhava and Vāsu Ghoṣa Ṭhākura.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (on 14–15): when Mahāprabhu sent Nityānanda to Gauḍa, He gave Him Rāmadāsa and Gadādhara dāsa as companions; that is why they are named both among Mahāprabhu’s devotees and among Nityānanda’s. The same is true of Mādhava and Vāsu Ghoṣa.

[^8]: The *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (126), quoted in the *Anubhāṣya* on 11.13, speaks of a log that only thirty-two men could carry. Heading in the edition: “The pastime of Abhirāma.”

[^9]: *Dāna-keli*, “the toll pastime”: Kṛṣṇa and His friends block the gopīs’ way and demand a “toll” from them. Heading in the edition: “The wondrous deeds of Dāsa Gadādhara.”

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya* (on 15): into the collections of Vāsu Ghoṣa’s songs the *prākṛta-sahajiyās* have inserted many songs of “Gaura the lover of the women of Nadia” (*gaura-nāgarī*), pleasing to their senses. Vāsu Ghoṣa, who tasted the rasa of separation, never wrote such songs and never could have; the sādhaka should reject them.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*, quoting the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 5): oblivious of his body, Caitanya dāsa would chase tigers through the forest and ride on them, take a huge python into his lap, and stay under water for two or three days. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: he was born at Sar-Vṛndāvanapura (Bardhamān district), and on coming to Modadruma (Māmgāchi) in Navadvīpa he received the name Śārṅga Murāri-caitanya dāsa.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: “Nitāi’s associates are pure devotees, the friends of Vraja.” *Anubhāṣya*: all who took shelter of Nityānanda Prabhu belong to the sakhya-rasa of Vraja and wear the dress of cowherd boys. His consort, Īśvarī Jāhnavā Mātā, is Anaṅga-mañjarī, the younger sister of Śrīmatī Rādhikā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 66), and the devotees who took shelter of her are also counted among Nityānanda’s associates.

[^13]: By the heading in the edition, the second of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Sudāma (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 127); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6) calls him “an ocean of the rasa of love, the chief of Nityānanda’s companions”.

[^14]: By the heading in the edition, the third of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Mahābala (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 128); he installed the Deity of Jagannātha at Māhesh, and his descendants serve Him.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: Sūryadāsa Sarakhela lived at Śāligrāma near Navadvīpa; he received the title “Sarakhela” in the service of the Muslim ruler of Gauḍa. His daughters Vasudhā and Jāhnavā are the consorts of Śrī Nityānanda (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 65). Kṛṣṇadāsa Sarakhela was the younger brother of Sūryadāsa and of Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita.

[^16]: By the heading in the edition, the fourth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Subala (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 128); the brother of Sūryadāsa Sarakhela, he moved from Śāligrāma to Ambikā-Kālnā on the bank of the Gaṅgā.

[^17]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *pā̐ti* is the common meal (*paṅkti-bhojana*), the right to sit and eat in one row with one’s caste. Heading in the edition: “Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita, whose life is Gaura and Nitāi.”

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*, quoting the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 5): at Khaḍadaha, at the home of Purandara Paṇḍita, Nityānanda danced tirelessly, and Purandara, frenzied with love, climbed a tree, roared like a lion and leapt down crying, “I am Aṅgada!”

[^19]: By the heading in the edition, the fifth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Arjuna (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 132); he accompanied Jāhnavā Mātā to the festival at Kheturi and at her command installed the Deities of Śrī Rādhā-Gopīnātha at Āṭpur. The *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā* says that he made even jackals utter the holy name.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: his śrīpāṭa is Yaśaḍā near Cākdaha; at Mahāprabhu’s command he preached the holy name in Nīlācala, and from there he brought a Deity of Jagannātha, whom he installed at Yaśaḍā.

[^21]: By the heading in the edition, the sixth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Vasudāma (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 127); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6) calls him “a wonderful mahānta, in whose heart Nityānanda ever dwells”. His chief śrīpāṭa is Śītalagrāma near Kāṭoyā.

[^22]: By the heading in the edition, the seventh of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Mahābāhu (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 129); he was with Nityānanda at the festival in Pānihāṭi and then went with Him to Saptagrāma.

[^23]: By the heading in the edition, the eighth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Stoka-kṛṣṇa (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 130); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6) says he was “born in Navadvīpa, the intimate great servant of Nityānanda”.

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*, quoting the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6): “Balarāma dāsa, drunk with the rasa of love — even a breeze from him destroys all sins.”

[^25]: *Anubhāṣya* (from the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*): Yadunātha Kavicandra was the son of Ratnagarbha Ācārya, a friend of Nityānanda’s father from the same village; his brothers were Kṛṣṇānanda and Jīva (11.44, 11.50).

[^26]: By the heading in the edition, the ninth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Lavaṅga (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 132); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6): “Kāliyā Kṛṣṇadāsa is famed in the three worlds; whoever remembers him attains Gauracandra.” His śrīpāṭa is Ākāihāṭ near Kāṭoyā.

[^27]: By the heading in the edition, Puruṣottama is the tenth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: Sadāśiva Kavirāja was formerly, in Vraja, Candrāvalī, Kṛṣṇa’s beloved (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 156); his son is “Nāgara” Puruṣottama, the cowherd boy Dāma of Vraja (ibid., 131); Sadāśiva’s father was Kaṁsāri Sena (11.51). Rarely does one find, as in this family, four generations of perfected devotees of Gaura: Kaṁsāri Sena, Sadāśiva, Puruṣottama and Kānu Ṭhākura.

[^28]: Heading in the edition: “‘Nāgara’ Puruṣottama.”

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya*: some count Kānu Ṭhākura among the twelve gopālas. According to his family tradition, his mother left the world soon after his birth, and Nityānanda Prabhu took the infant to Khaḍadaha and named him Śiśu Kṛṣṇadāsa; in Vṛndāvana, Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī and the people of Vraja gave him the name Ṭhākura Kānāi. His śrīpāṭa is Bodhkhānā (Yaśohar district).

[^30]: By the heading in the edition, the eleventh of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Subāhu (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 129). According to the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 5), Nityānanda stayed in his house at Saptagrāma on the bank of the Triveṇī, and through Uddhāraṇa the whole community of merchants (*vaṇiks*) was purified.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (97) names Raghunātha Purī among the eight Purīs who embody the eight mystic perfections (*aṇimā* and the rest).

[^32]: *Anubhāṣya*: the three brothers were brāhmaṇas of Navadvīpa; Viṣṇudāsa and Gaṅgādāsa lived for some time with Mahāprabhu in Nīlācala (Ādi 10.151). In the house of Nandana Ācārya, Mahāprabhu and Advaita once hid, and Nityānanda, on coming to Navadvīpa, stayed there for some time.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: Jīva Paṇḍita was the middle son of Ratnagarbha Ācārya (11.35); in Vraja he is Indirā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 169).

[^34]: *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Mañjumedhā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 194, 199); he composed a garland of hymns to Kṛṣṇa.

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: Hoḍa Kṛṣṇadāsa is Rājā Kṛṣṇadāsa of Baḍagāchi, the son of Hari Hoḍa; at his initiative Śrī Nityānanda’s wedding took place in nearby Śāligrāma. The *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6): “the pious Kṛṣṇadāsa of Baḍagāchi, in whose house Nityānanda showed His pastimes”.

[^36]: By the heading in the edition, Śrīdhara is the twelfth of the twelve gopālas.

[^37]: *Anubhāṣya*: Navanī Hoḍa is the son of Hoḍa Kṛṣṇadāsa (11.47); Kṛṣṇānanda is the brother of Yadunātha Kavicandra (11.35).

[^38]: *Anubhāṣya*: Kaṁsāri Sena is the father of Sadāśiva Kavirāja (11.38), in Vraja Ratnāvalī (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 194, 200). Rāmacandra Kavirāja was the son of Cirañjīva and Sunandā of Śrīkhaṇḍa, a disciple of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya and the dear friend of Narottama Ṭhākura, who prayed for his company birth after birth. Govinda Kavirāja was his younger brother, also a disciple of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya, the author of the play *Saṅgīta-mādhava* and of the *Gītāmṛta*; both received the title “Kavirāja” from Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī.

[^39]: *Caitanya-maṅgala* was the first name of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (see Ādi 8.33–35). *Anubhāṣya*: according to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (109), Vṛndāvana dāsa was formerly Vedavyāsa, and Kṛṣṇa’s friend Kusumāpīḍa also dwells in him. He was the son of Nārāyaṇī, the niece of Śrīvāsa, and the author of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*.

[^40]: Heading in the edition: “By giving love for Kṛṣṇa they deliver the world.”
