“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 13  
**The Description of the Great Festival of the Lord’s Appearance**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

স প্রসীদতু চৈতন্যদেবো যস্য প্রসাদতঃ ৷  
তল্লীলাবর্ণনে যোগ্যঃ সদ্যঃ স্যাদধমোঽপ্যয়ম্ ॥ ১ ॥

sa prasīdatu caitanya-  
He  may be gracious  Caitanya-  
devo yasya prasādataḥ  
deva  whose  by the grace  
tal-līlā-varṇane yogyaḥ  
in describing His pastimes  fit  
sadyaḥ syād adhamo ’py ayam  
at once  may become  fallen  even  this one

(1) May Śrī Caitanyadeva be gracious to me, for by His grace even one as fallen as I at once becomes fit to describe His pastimes.

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya gauracandra  
glory  glory  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  Gauracandra  
jayādvaitacandra jaya jaya nityānanda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  glory  Nityānanda

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory, glory to Nityānanda!

জয় জয় গদাধর জয় শ্রীনিবাস ৷  
জয় মুকুন্দ বাসুদেব জয় হরিদাস ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya gadādhara jaya śrīnivāsa  
glory  glory  Gadādhara  glory  Śrīnivāsa  
jaya mukunda vāsudeva jaya haridāsa  
glory  Mukunda  Vāsudeva  glory  Haridāsa

(3) Glory, glory to Gadādhara, and glory to Śrīvāsa! Glory to Mukunda and Vāsudeva, and glory to Haridāsa!

জয় দামোদর-স্বরূপ জয় মুরারি গুপ্ত ৷  
এই সব চন্দ্রোদয়ে তমঃ কৈল লুপ্ত ॥ ৪ ॥

jaya dāmodara-svarūpa jaya murāri gupta  
glory  Dāmodara-svarūpa  glory  Murāri  Gupta  
ei saba candrodaye tamaḥ kaila lupta  
these  all  by the moons’ rising  darkness  made  vanish

(4) Glory to Svarūpa Dāmodara, and glory to Murāri Gupta! These moons have risen and driven all darkness away.

জয় শ্রীচৈতন্যচন্দ্রের ভক্ত পূর্ণচন্দ্রগণ ৷  
সবার প্রেম-জ্যোৎস্নায় উজ্জ্বল ত্রিভুবন ॥ ৫ ॥

jaya śrī-caitanyacandrera bhakta pūrṇacandra-gaṇa  
glory  of Śrī  Caitanyacandra  the devotees  full moons  
sabāra prema-jyotsnāya ujjvala tri-bhuvana  
of all  by the moonlight of love  bright  the three worlds

(5) Glory to the devotees of Śrī Caitanyacandra, who are full moons! The moonlight of their love makes the three worlds shine.[^1]

এই ত’ কহিল গ্রন্থারম্ভে মুখবন্ধ ৷  
এবে কহি চৈতন্য-লীলাক্রম-অনুবন্ধ ॥ ৬ ॥

ei ta’ kahila granthārambhe mukha-bandha  
this  indeed  I have told  at the book’s beginning  the preface  
ebe kahi caitanya-līlā-krama-anubandha  
now  I tell  of Caitanya’s pastimes  the sequence  in order

(6) Thus I have given the preface at the beginning of the book. Now I shall tell the pastimes of Śrī Caitanya in their proper order.

প্রথমে ত’ সূত্ররূপে করিয়ে গণন ৷  
পাছে তাহা বিস্তারি করিব বিবরণ ॥ ৭ ॥

prathame ta’ sūtra-rūpe kariye gaṇana  
first  indeed  in the form of sūtras  I make  the count  
pāche tāhā vistāri kariba vivaraṇa  
afterwards  that  expanding  I shall make  the description

(7) First I shall list them briefly, as in sūtras; afterwards I shall describe them in detail.

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য নবদ্বীপে অবতরি ৷  
আটচল্লিশ বৎসর প্রকট বিহরি ॥ ৮ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya navadvīpe avatari  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  in Navadvīpa  descending  
āṭa-calliśa vatsara prakaṭa vihari  
forty-eight  years  manifest  sporting

(8) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya descended in Navadvīpa, and for forty-eight years He enjoyed His pastimes before the eyes of the world.

চৌদ্দশত সাত শকে জন্মের প্রমাণ ৷  
চৌদ্দশত পঞ্চান্নে হইল অন্তর্দ্ধান ॥ ৯ ॥

caudda-śata sāta śake janmera pramāṇa  
fourteen hundred  seven  in the Śaka year  of birth  the record  
caudda-śata pañcānne haila antarddhāna  
in fourteen hundred  fifty-five  took place  the disappearance

(9) His appearance is recorded in Śaka 1407 (1486), and He disappeared from the world in 1455 (1533).

চব্বিশ বৎসর প্রভু কৈল গৃহবাস ৷  
নিরন্তর কৈল তাহে কীর্ত্তন-বিলাস ॥ ১০ ॥

cabbiśa vatsara prabhu kaila gṛha-vāsa  
twenty-four  years  the Lord  made  household life  
nirantara kaila tāhe kīrtana-vilāsa  
constantly  made  there  the delight of kīrtana

(10) For twenty-four years the Lord lived as a householder, and all that time He delighted in kīrtana.

চব্বিশ বৎসর-শেষে করিয়া সন্ন্যাস ৷  
আর চব্বিশ বৎসর কৈল নীলাচলে বাস ॥ ১১ ॥

cabbiśa vatsara-śeṣe kariyā sannyāsa  
of the twenty-fourth  year  at the end  accepting  sannyāsa  
āra cabbiśa vatsara kaila nīlācale vāsa  
another  twenty-four  years  made  in Nīlācala  residence

(11) At the end of His twenty-fourth year He accepted sannyāsa, and for another twenty-four years He lived in Nīlācala.

তার মধ্যে ছয় বৎসর — গমনাগমন ৷  
কভু দক্ষিণ, কভু গৌড়, কভু বৃন্দাবন ॥ ১২ ॥

tāra madhye chaya vatsara — gamanāgamana  
of them  within  six  years  —  going and coming  
kabhu dakṣiṇa, kabhu gauḍa, kabhu vṛndāvana  
sometimes  the south,  sometimes  Gauḍa,  sometimes  Vṛndāvana

(12) Six of those years He spent journeying — now to the south, now to Gauḍa, now to Vṛndāvana.

অষ্টাদশ বৎসর রহিলা নীলাচলে ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেম-লীলামৃতে ভাসা’ল সকলে ॥ ১৩ ॥

aṣṭādaśa vatsara rahilā nīlācale  
eighteen  years  remained  in Nīlācala  
kṛṣṇa-prema-līlāmṛte bhāsā’la sakale  
in the nectar of the pastimes of love for Kṛṣṇa  flooded  everyone

(13) For eighteen years He stayed in Nīlācala, flooding everyone with the nectar of His pastimes of love for Kṛṣṇa.

গার্হস্থ্যে প্রভুর লীলা — ‘আদি’-লীলাখ্যান ৷  
‘মধ্য’-‘অন্ত্য’-নামে — শেষলীলার দুই নাম ॥ ১৪ ॥

gārhasthye prabhura līlā — ‘ādi’-līlākhyāna  
in household life  the Lord’s  pastimes  —  ‘ādi’-līlā  is the name  
‘madhya’-‘antya’-nāme — śeṣa-līlāra dui nāma  
‘madhya’  ‘antya’  by name  —  of the later pastimes  two  names

(14) The Lord’s pastimes as a householder are called the Ādi-līlā, and His later pastimes bear two names, Madhya and Antya.

আদিলীলা-মধ্যে প্রভুর যতেক চরিত ৷  
সূত্ররূপে মুরারি গুপ্ত করিলা গ্রথিত ॥ ১৫ ॥

ādi-līlā-madhye prabhura yateka carita  
the Ādi-līlā  within  the Lord’s  all  deeds  
sūtra-rūpe murāri gupta karilā grathita  
in the form of sūtras  Murāri Gupta  made  strung together

(15) Murāri Gupta strung together, in the form of sūtras, all the Lord’s deeds in the Ādi-līlā.

প্রভুর যে-শেষ-লীলা স্বরূপ-দামোদর ৷  
সূত্র করি’ গ্রন্থিলেন গ্রন্থের ভিতর ॥ ১৬ ॥

prabhura ye-śeṣa-līlā svarūpa-dāmodara  
the Lord’s  which  later pastimes  Svarūpa-dāmodara  
sūtra kari’ granthilena granthera bhitara  
sūtras  making  strung  the book  within

(16) Svarūpa Dāmodara set down the Lord’s later pastimes in brief notes, and gathered them in his book.[^2]

এই দুই জনের সূত্র দেখিয়া শুনিয়া ৷  
বর্ণনা করেন বৈষ্ণব ক্রম যে করিয়া ॥ ১৭ ॥

ei dui janera sūtra dekhiyā śuniyā  
these  two  persons’  sūtras  seeing  hearing  
varṇanā karena vaiṣṇava krama ye kariyā  
description  make  the Vaiṣṇavas  the sequence  which  keeping

(17) Reading and hearing the notes of these two, the Vaiṣṇavas describe the Lord’s pastimes in their order.[^3]

বাল্য, পৌগণ্ড, কৈশোর, যৌবন, — চারি ভেদ ৷  
অতএব আদিখণ্ডে লীলা চারি ভেদ ॥ ১৮ ॥

bālya, paugaṇḍa, kaiśora, yauvana, — cāri bheda  
childhood,  boyhood,  early youth,  youth,  —  four  divisions  
ataeva ādi-khaṇḍe līlā cāri bheda  
therefore  in the first part  the pastimes  four  divisions

(18) Childhood (*bālya*), boyhood (*paugaṇḍa*), early youth (*kaiśora*) and youth (*yauvana*) — these are the four ages, and so the pastimes of the first part fall into four divisions.

সর্ব্বসদ্গুণপূর্ণাং তাং বন্দে ফাল্গুনপূর্ণিমাম্ ৷  
যস্যাং শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যোঽবতীর্ণঃ কৃষ্ণনামভিঃ ॥ ১৯ ॥

sarva-sad-guṇa-pūrṇāṁ tāṁ  
with all  good  qualities  full  that  
vande phālguna-pūrṇimām  
I bow to  the Phālguna  full moon  
yasyāṁ śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanyo  
on which  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  
’vatīrṇaḥ kṛṣṇa-nāmabhiḥ  
descended  with the names of Kṛṣṇa

(19) I bow to that full moon of Phālguna, rich in every good quality, on which Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya descended together with the names of Kṛṣṇa.[^4]

ফাল্গুনপূর্ণিমা-সন্ধ্যায় প্রভুর জন্মোদয় ৷  
সেইকালে দৈবযোগে চন্দ্রের গ্রহণ হয় ॥ ২০ ॥

phālguna-pūrṇimā-sandhyāya prabhura janmodaya  
on the Phālguna full moon  in the evening  the Lord’s  birth  arose  
sei-kāle daiva-yoge candrera grahaṇa haya  
at that  time  by providence  of the moon  eclipse  takes place

(20) The Lord appeared on the evening of the Phālguna full moon, and at that very hour, as providence would have it, the moon was eclipsed.

‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলে লোক হরষিত হঞা ৷  
জন্মিলা চৈতন্যপ্রভু ‘নাম’ জন্মাইয়া ॥ ২১ ॥

‘hari’ ‘hari’ bale loka haraṣita hañā  
‘Hari’  ‘Hari’  say  the people  joyful  becoming  
janmilā caitanya-prabhu ‘nāma’ janmāiyā  
was born  Caitanya Prabhu  the Name  causing to be born

(21) The people were joyfully calling out, “Hari! Hari!” — and Caitanya Prabhu took birth, having first brought the Name to birth.

জন্ম-বাল্য-পৌগণ্ড-কৈশোর-যুবাকালে ৷  
হরিনাম লওয়াইলা প্রভু নানা-ছলে ॥ ২২ ॥

janma-bālya-paugaṇḍa-kaiśora-yuvā-kāle  
at birth,  in childhood,  boyhood,  early youth,  youth  
hari-nāma laoyāilā prabhu nānā-chale  
the name of Hari  made take  the Lord  by various  pretexts

(22) At His birth, in childhood, boyhood, early youth and youth, the Lord found one pretext or another to make people take the name of Hari.

বাল্যভাব-ছলে প্রভু করেন ক্রন্দন ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ নাম শুনি’ রহয়ে রোদন ॥ ২৩ ॥

bālya-bhāva-chale prabhu karena krandana  
of a child’s mood  on the pretext  the Lord  does  crying  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’ nāma śuni’ rahaye rodana  
‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Hari’  the names  hearing  stops  weeping

(23) On the pretext of being a baby the Lord would cry, and the moment He heard the names “Kṛṣṇa” and “Hari”, He stopped weeping.[^5]

অতএব ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলে নারীগণ ৷  
দেখিতে আইসে যেবা সর্ব্ব-বন্ধুজন ॥ ২৪ ॥

ataeva ‘hari’ ‘hari’ bale nārīgaṇa  
therefore  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’  say  the women  
dekhite āise yebā sarva-bandhu jana  
to see  come  who  all  friends and kin

(24) So all the women among His friends and kin who came to see Him would chant, “Hari! Hari!”

‘গৌরহরি’ বলি’ তারে হাসে সর্ব্ব নারী ৷  
অতএব হৈল তাঁর নাম ‘গৌরহরি’ ॥ ২৫ ॥

‘gaurahari’ bali’ tāre hāse sarva nārī  
‘Gaurahari’  calling  Him  laugh  all  the women  
ataeva haila tām̐ra nāma ‘gaurahari’  
therefore  became  His  name  ‘Gaurahari’

(25) Laughing, the women all called Him “Gaurahari”, and so Gaurahari became His name.

বাল্য-বয়স — যাবৎ হাতে খড়ি দিল ৷  
পৌগণ্ড-বয়স — যাবৎ বিবাহ না কৈল ॥ ২৬ ॥

bālya-vayasa — yāvat hāte khaḍi dila  
childhood  age  —  until  in His hand  chalk  was given  
paugaṇḍa-vayasa — yāvat vivāha nā kaila  
boyhood  age  —  until  marriage  not  was made

(26) His childhood lasted until the chalk was put in His hand for His first lesson; His boyhood, until He married.

বিবাহ করিলে হৈল নবীন যৌবন ৷  
সর্ব্বত্র লওয়াইল প্রভু নাম-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ॥ ২৭ ॥

vivāha karile haila navīna yauvana  
marriage  having made  began  fresh  youth  
sarvatra laoyāila prabhu nāma-saṅkīrtana  
everywhere  made take  the Lord  the saṅkīrtana of the name

(27) With His marriage began the bloom of youth, and everywhere the Lord led people to chant the name together.

পৌগণ্ড-বয়সে পড়েন, পড়ান শিষ্যগণে ৷  
সর্ব্বত্র করেন কৃষ্ণনামের ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ২৮ ॥

paugaṇḍa-vayase paḍena, paḍāna śiṣyagaṇe  
in boyhood  age  He studies,  teaches  the pupils  
sarvatra karena kṛṣṇa-nāmera vyākhyāne  
everywhere  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the explanation

(28) In His boyhood He studied and also taught pupils, and everywhere He explained the name of Kṛṣṇa.

সূত্র-বৃত্তি-টীকায় কৃষ্ণনামের তাৎপর্য্য ৷  
শিষ্যের প্রতীত হয়, — সবার আশ্চর্য্য ॥ ২৯ ॥

sūtra-vṛtti-ṭīkāya kṛṣṇanāmera tātparya  
in sūtra,  gloss,  commentary  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the purport  
śiṣyera pratīta haya, — sabāra āścarya  
to the pupils  clear  becomes,  —  to all  a wonder

(29) He showed that the purport of every sūtra, gloss and commentary was the name of Kṛṣṇa, and His pupils came to see it plainly — a wonder to all.[^6]

যারে দেখে, তারে কহে, — কহ কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-নামে ভাসাইল নবদ্বীপ-গ্রাম ॥ ৩০ ॥

yāre dekhe, tāre kahe, — kaha kṛṣṇa-nāma  
whom  He sees,  to him  says  —  say  Kṛṣṇa’s name  
kṛṣṇa-nāme bhāsāila navadvīpa-grāma  
with Kṛṣṇa’s name  flooded  Navadvīpa  town

(30) Whomever He met He would tell, “Chant the name of Kṛṣṇa!” — and He flooded the town of Navadvīpa with Kṛṣṇa’s name.

কিশোর-বয়সে আরম্ভিলা সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
রাত্র-দিনে প্রেমে নৃত্য, সঙ্গে ভক্তগণ ॥ ৩১ ॥

kiśora-vayase ārambhilā saṁkīrtana  
in early youth  age  began  saṅkīrtana  
rātra-dine preme nṛtya, saṅge bhakta-gaṇa  
night and day  in love  dancing,  with  the devotees

(31) In His early youth He began the saṅkīrtana, dancing in love night and day together with His devotees.

নগরে নগরে ভ্রমে কীর্ত্তন করিয়া ৷  
ভাসাইল ত্রিভুবন প্রেমভক্তি দিয়া ॥ ৩২ ॥

nagare nagare bhrame kīrtana kariyā  
from town  to town  wanders  kīrtana  doing  
bhāsāila tri-bhuvana prema-bhakti diyā  
flooded  the three worlds  love and devotion  giving

(32) He wandered from town to town singing kīrtana, and flooded the three worlds with loving devotion.

চব্বিশ বৎসর ঐছে নবদ্বীপ-গ্রামে ৷  
লওয়াইলা সর্ব্বলোকে কৃষ্ণপ্রেম-নামে ॥ ৩৩ ॥

cabbiśa vatsara aiche navadvīpa-grāme  
twenty-four  years  thus  in Navadvīpa  town  
laoyāilā sarva-loke kṛṣṇa-prema-nāme  
made take  all people  to love for Kṛṣṇa and the name

(33) Thus for twenty-four years in Navadvīpa He led everyone to the name of Kṛṣṇa and to love for Him.

চব্বিশ বৎসর ছিলা করিয়া সন্ন্যাস ৷  
ভক্তগণ লঞা কৈলা নীলাচলে বাস ॥ ৩৪ ॥

cabbiśa vatsara chilā kariyā sannyāsa  
twenty-four  years  He stayed  having accepted  sannyāsa  
bhakta-gaṇa lañā kailā nīlācale vāsa  
the devotees  taking along  made  in Nīlācala  residence

(34) After accepting sannyāsa He remained twenty-four years more, living with His devotees in Nīlācala.

তার মধ্যে নীলাচলে ছয় বৎসর ৷  
নৃত্য, গীত, প্রেমভক্তি-দান নিরন্তর ॥ ৩৫ ॥

tāra madhye nīlācale chaya vatsara  
of them  within  in Nīlācala  six  years  
nṛtya, gīta, premabhakti-dāna nirantara  
dancing,  singing,  the gift of loving devotion  unceasing

(35) Of those years in Nīlācala, six He spent in ceaseless dancing and singing, giving away loving devotion.[^7]

সেতুবন্ধ, আর গৌড়-ব্যাপি বৃন্দাবন ৷  
প্রেম-নাম প্রচারিয়া করিলা ভ্রমণ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

setubandha, āra gauḍa-vyāpi vṛndāvana  
Setubandha,  and  pervading Gauḍa  Vṛndāvana  
prema-nāma pracāriyā karilā bhramaṇa  
love and the name  preaching  made  journeying

(36) He journeyed as far as Setubandha in the south, through Gauḍa and on to Vṛndāvana, spreading the holy name and love wherever He went.

এই ‘মধ্যলীলা’ নাম — লীলা-মুখ্যধাম ৷  
শেষ অষ্টাদশ বর্ষ — ‘অন্ত্যলীলা’ নাম ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ei ‘madhya-līlā’ nāma — līlā-mukhyadhāma  
this  ‘madhya-līlā’  by name  —  of the pastimes  the chief abode  
śeṣa aṣṭādaśa varṣa — ‘antya-līlā’ nāma  
the last  eighteen  years  —  ‘antya-līlā’  by name

(37) These years are called the Madhya-līlā, the very heart of His pastimes; the last eighteen years are called the Antya-līlā.

তার মধ্যে ছয় বৎসর ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে ৷  
প্রেমভক্তি লওয়াইল নৃত্যগীত-রঙ্গে ॥ ৩৮ ॥

tāra madhye chaya vatsara bhaktagaṇa-saṅge  
of them  within  six  years  with the devotees  
prema-bhakti laoyāila nṛtya-gīta-raṅge  
loving devotion  made take  in the joy of dancing and singing

(38) For six of those years, in the joy of dancing and singing with His devotees, He led people to loving devotion.

দ্বাদশ বৎসর শেষ রহিলা নীলাচলে ৷  
প্রেমাবস্থা শিখাইলা আস্বাদন-চ্ছলে ॥ ৩৯ ॥

dvādaśa vatsara śeṣa rahilā nīlācale  
twelve  years  the last  remained  in Nīlācala  
premāvasthā śikhāilā āsvādana-cchale  
the states of love  taught  on the pretext of tasting

(39) The last twelve years He stayed in Nīlācala, and, tasting love Himself, He taught everyone its highest states.[^8]

রাত্রি-দিবসে কৃষ্ণবিরহ-স্ফুরণ ৷  
উন্মাদের চেষ্টা করে প্রলাপ-বচন ॥ ৪০ ॥

rātri-divase kṛṣṇa-viraha-sphuraṇa  
night  and day  of separation from Kṛṣṇa  the welling up  
unmādera ceṣṭā kare pralāpa-vacana  
of a madman  the ways  makes,  incoherent  words

(40) Night and day the pain of separation from Kṛṣṇa welled up in Him; He behaved like one mad and spoke in broken, raving words.

শ্রীরাধার প্রলাপ যৈছে উদ্ধব-দর্শনে ৷  
সেইমত উন্মাদ-প্রলাপ করে রাত্রিদিনে ॥ ৪১ ॥

śrī-rādhāra pralāpa yaiche uddhava-darśane  
Śrī Rādhā’s  raving  as  on seeing Uddhava  
seimata unmāda-pralāpa kare rātridine  
just so  mad  raving  makes  night and day

(41) Just as Śrī Rādhā raved on seeing Uddhava, so He raved in His madness night and day.

বিদ্যাপতি, জয়দেব, চণ্ডীদাসের গীত ৷  
আস্বাদেন রামানন্দ-স্বরূপ-সহিত ॥ ৪২ ॥

vidyāpati, jayadeva, caṇḍīdāsera gīta  
Vidyāpati,  Jayadeva,  of Caṇḍīdāsa  the songs  
āsvādena rāmānanda-svarūpa-sahita  
He relishes  Rāmānanda  Svarūpa  together with

(42) With Rāmānanda and Svarūpa He relished the songs of Vidyāpati, Jayadeva and Caṇḍīdāsa.[^9]

কৃষ্ণের বিয়োগে যত প্রেম-চেষ্টিত ৷  
আস্বাদিয়া পূর্ণ কৈল আপন-বাঞ্ছিত ॥ ৪৩ ॥

kṛṣṇera viyoge yata prema-ceṣṭita  
of Kṛṣṇa  in separation  all  the ways of love  
āsvādiyā pūrṇa kaila āpana-vāñchita  
tasting  fulfilled  made  His own  desire

(43) Tasting all the ways of love in separation from Kṛṣṇa, He fulfilled His own desire.

অনন্ত চৈতন্যলীলা ক্ষুদ্র জীব হঞা ৷  
কে বলিতে পারে, তাহা বিস্তার করিয়া ॥ ৪৪ ॥

ananta caitanya-līlā kṣudra jīva hañā  
endless  Caitanya’s pastimes;  a tiny  soul  being  
ke balite pāre, tāhā vistāra kariyā  
who  to tell  can,  them  in full  making

(44) Śrī Caitanya’s pastimes are endless. How could a tiny soul tell them in full?

সূত্র করি’ গণে যদি আপনে অনন্ত ৷  
সহস্র-বদনে তিঁহো নাহি পায় অন্ত ॥ ৪৫ ॥

sūtra kari’ gaṇe yadi āpane ananta  
in sūtras  making  counts  if  Himself  Ananta  
sahasra-vadane tim̐ho nāhi pāya anta  
with a thousand  mouths  He  not  reaches  the end

(45) Even if Ananta Himself were to count them in brief, with all His thousand mouths He would never reach the end.

দামোদর-স্বরূপ, আর গুপ্ত মুরারি ৷  
মুখ্যমুখ্যলীলা সূত্রে লিখিয়াছে বিচারি’ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

dāmodara-svarūpa, āra gupta murāri  
Dāmodara-svarūpa,  and  Gupta  Murāri  
mukhya-mukhya-līlā sūtre likhiyāche vicāri’  
the chief and foremost  pastimes  in sūtras  have written  after weighing

(46) Svarūpa Dāmodara and Murāri Gupta weighed them carefully and wrote down the chief pastimes in brief.

সেই অনুসারে লিখি লীলা-সূত্রগণ ৷  
বিস্তারি’ বর্ণিয়াছেন তাহা দাস-বৃন্দাবন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

sei anusāre likhi līlā-sūtragaṇa  
that  following  I write  the sūtras of the pastimes  
vistāri’ varṇiyāchena tāhā dāsa-vṛndāvana  
in detail  has described  them  Dāsa-Vṛndāvana

(47) Following them, I write these pastimes in brief, and Vṛndāvana Dāsa has described them in detail.[^10]

চৈতন্য-লীলার ব্যাস, — দাস বৃন্দাবন ৷  
মধুর করিয়া লীলা করিলা রচন ॥ ৪৮ ॥

caitanya-līlāra vyāsa, — dāsa vṛndāvana  
of Caitanya’s pastimes  the Vyāsa  —  Dāsa  Vṛndāvana  
madhura kariyā līlā karilā racana  
sweet  making  the pastimes  made  composition

(48) Vṛndāvana Dāsa is the Vyāsa of Śrī Caitanya’s pastimes, and he set them down with great sweetness.

গ্রন্থ-বিস্তার-ভয়ে ছাড়িলা যে যে স্থানে ৷  
সেই সেই স্থানে কিছু করিব ব্যাখানে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

grantha-vistāra-bhaye chāḍilā ye ye sthāne  
of the book  the expansion  fearing  left out  which  which  places  
sei sei sthāne kichu kariba vyākhāne  
those  those  places  some  I shall make  explanation

(49) Fearing that his book would grow too long, he left out certain parts, and of these I shall say something.

প্রভুর লীলামৃত তিঁহো কৈল আস্বাদন ৷  
তাঁর ভুক্ত-শেষ কিছু করিয়ে চর্ব্বণ ॥ ৫০ ॥

prabhura līlāmṛta tim̐ho kaila āsvādana  
the Lord’s  nectar of pastimes  he  made  tasting  
tām̐ra bhukta-śeṣa kichu kariye carvaṇa  
his  food’s remnants  some  I make  chewing

(50) He has tasted the nectar of the Lord’s pastimes; I only chew a little of what he has left.

আদিলীলা-সূত্র লিখি, শুন, ভক্তগণ ৷  
সংক্ষেপে লিখিয়ে সম্যক্ না যায় লিখন ॥ ৫১ ॥

ādi-līlā-sūtra likhi, śuna, bhakta-gaṇa  
the Ādi-līlā sūtras  I write,  hear,  devotees  
saṁkṣepe likhiye samyak nā yāya likhana  
in brief  I write;  fully  not  goes  writing

(51) Listen, O devotees: I write the Ādi-līlā in brief, for it cannot be written in full.

কোন বাঞ্ছা পূরণ লাগি’ ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হৈতে মনে করিলা বিচার ॥ ৫২ ॥

kona vāñchā pūraṇa lāgi’ vrajendra-kumāra  
a certain  desire  fulfilment  for the sake of  the son of the king of Vraja  
avatīrṇa haite mane karilā vicāra  
to descend  in His mind  made  consideration

(52) To fulfil a certain desire, the son of the king of Vraja resolved in His mind to descend.

আগে অবতারিলা যে যে গুরু-পরিবার ৷  
সংক্ষেপে কহিয়ে, কহা না যায় বিস্তার ॥ ৫৩ ॥

āge avatārilā ye ye guru-parivāra  
first  caused to descend  which  which  the family of elders  
saṁkṣepe kahiye, kahā nā yāya vistāra  
in brief  I tell;  to tell  not  goes  in full

(53) First He sent down His elders and kin. I shall name them in brief, for they cannot all be told in full.

শ্রীশচী-জগন্নাথ, শ্রীমাধবপুরী ৷  
কেশব ভারতী, আর শ্রীঈশ্বর পুরী ॥ ৫৪ ॥

অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য, আর পণ্ডিত শ্রীবাস ৷  
আচার্য্যরত্ন, বিদ্যানিধি, ঠাকুর হরিদাস ॥ ৫৫ ॥

śrī-śacī-jagannātha, śrī-mādhava-purī  
Śrī Śacī  Jagannātha,  Śrī Mādhava Purī  
keśava bhāratī, āra śrī-īśvara purī  
Keśava  Bhāratī,  and  Śrī Īśvara  Purī  
advaita ācārya, āra paṇḍita śrīvāsa  
Advaita  Ācārya,  and  Paṇḍita  Śrīvāsa  
ācāryaratna, vidyānidhi, ṭhākura haridāsa  
Ācāryaratna,  Vidyānidhi,  Ṭhākura  Haridāsa

(54–55) Śrī Śacī and Jagannātha, Śrī Mādhava Purī, Keśava Bhāratī and Śrī Īśvara Purī, Advaita Ācārya and Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, Ācāryaratna, Vidyānidhi and Ṭhākura Haridāsa.

শ্রীহট্ট-নিবাসী শ্রীউপেন্দ্রমিশ্র-নাম ৷  
বৈষ্ণব, পণ্ডিত, ধনী, সদ্গুণ-প্রধান ॥ ৫৬ ॥

śrīhaṭṭa-nivāsī śrī-upendra-miśra-nāma  
of Śrīhaṭṭa  resident  Śrī Upendra Miśra  by name  
vaiṣṇava, paṇḍita, dhanī, sad-guṇa-pradhāna  
a Vaiṣṇava,  learned,  wealthy,  in good qualities  foremost

(56) In Śrīhaṭṭa lived Śrī Upendra Miśra — a Vaiṣṇava, learned and wealthy, and rich in every virtue.[^11]

সপ্ত মিশ্র তাঁর পুত্র — সপ্ত ঋষীশ্বর ৷  
কংসারি, পরমানন্দ, পদ্মনাভ, সর্ব্বেশ্বর ॥ ৫৭ ॥

জগন্নাথ, জনার্দ্দন, ত্রৈলোক্যনাথ ৷  
নদীয়াতে গঙ্গাবাস কৈল জগন্নাথ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

sapta miśra tām̐ra putra — sapta ṛṣīśvara  
seven  Miśras  his  sons  —  seven  great sages  
kaṁsāri, paramānanda, padmanābha, sarveśvara  
Kaṁsāri,  Paramānanda,  Padmanābha,  Sarveśvara  
jagannātha, janārdana, trailokyanātha  
Jagannātha,  Janārdana,  Trailokyanātha  
nadīyāte gaṅgā-vāsa kaila jagannātha  
in Nadīyā  by the Gaṅgā  residence  made  Jagannātha

(57–58) His seven sons, the seven Miśras, were like seven great sages: Kaṁsāri, Paramānanda, Padmanābha, Sarveśvara, Jagannātha, Janārdana and Trailokyanātha. Jagannātha made his home by the Gaṅgā in Nadīyā.

জগন্নাথ মিশ্রবর — পদবী ‘পুরন্দর’ ৷  
নন্দ-বসুদেব পূর্ব্বে সদ্গুণ-সাগর ॥ ৫৯ ॥

jagannātha miśravara — padavī ‘purandara’  
Jagannātha  the best of the Miśras  —  title  ‘Purandara’  
nanda-vasudeva pūrve sadguṇa-sāgara  
Nanda  Vasudeva  formerly,  of good qualities  an ocean

(59) Jagannātha, the best of the Miśras, bore the title Purandara. This ocean of virtues had once been Nanda and Vasudeva.[^12]

তাঁর পত্নী ‘শচী’-নাম, পতিব্রতা সতী ৷  
যাঁর পিতা ‘নীলাম্বর’ নাম চক্রবর্ত্তী ॥ ৬০ ॥

tām̐ra patnī ‘śacī’-nāma, pativratā satī  
his  wife  Śacī  by name,  devoted to her husband,  chaste  
yām̐ra pitā ‘nīlāmbara’ nāma cakravartī  
whose  father  Nīlāmbara  by name  Cakravartī

(60) His wife, named Śacī, was chaste and wholly devoted to her husband; her father was Nīlāmbara Cakravartī.[^13]

রাঢ়দেশে জন্মিলা ঠাকুর নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
গঙ্গাদাস পণ্ডিত, গুপ্ত মুরারি, মুকুন্দ ॥ ৬১ ॥

rāḍhadeśe janmilā ṭhākura nityānanda  
in the Rāḍha country  were born  Ṭhākura  Nityānanda  
gaṅgādāsa paṇḍita, gupta murāri, mukunda  
Gaṅgādāsa  Paṇḍita,  Gupta  Murāri,  Mukunda

(61) In the land of Rāḍha were born Ṭhākura Nityānanda, Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita, Murāri Gupta and Mukunda.

অসংখ্য ভক্তের করাইলা অবতার ৷  
শেষে অবতীর্ণ হৈলা ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ॥ ৬২ ॥

asaṁkhya bhaktera karāilā avatāra  
countless  devotees’  caused  descent  
śeṣe avatīrṇa hailā vrajendra-kumāra  
at last  descended  became  the son of the king of Vraja

(62) He caused countless devotees to descend, and last of all the son of the king of Vraja descended Himself.

প্রভুর আবির্ভাবপূর্ব্বে যত বৈষ্ণবগণ ৷  
অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্যের স্থানে করেন গমন ॥ ৬৩ ॥

prabhura āvirbhāva-pūrve yata vaiṣṇava-gaṇa  
the Lord’s  appearance  before  all  the Vaiṣṇavas  
advaita-ācāryera sthāne karena gamana  
of Advaita  Ācārya  to the place  make  going

(63) Before the Lord appeared, all the Vaiṣṇavas would gather at the home of Advaita Ācārya.

গীতা-ভাগবত কহে আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি ৷  
জ্ঞান-কর্ম্ম নিন্দি’ করে ভক্তির বড়াই ॥ ৬৪ ॥

gītā-bhāgavata kahe ācārya-gosāñi  
the Gītā  the Bhāgavata  speaks  Ācārya  Gosāñi  
jñāna-karma nindi’ kare bhaktira baḍāi  
knowledge  work  decrying  makes  of bhakti  the glory

(64) There Ācārya Gosāñi expounded the *Gītā* and the *Bhāgavata*, decrying the paths of knowledge and fruitive work and extolling bhakti.

সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কহে কৃষ্ণভক্তির ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
জ্ঞান, যোগ, তপো-ধর্ম্ম নাহি মানে আন ॥ ৬৫ ॥

sarva-śāstre kahe kṛṣṇa-bhaktira vyākhyāna  
in all scriptures  speaks  of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the explanation  
jñāna, yoga, tapo-dharma nāhi māne āna  
knowledge,  yoga,  austerity,  dharma  not  accepts  other

(65) He showed that all the scriptures speak of devotion to Kṛṣṇa, and would accept no other path — not knowledge, yoga, austerity or ritual dharma.[^14]

তাঁর সঙ্গে আনন্দ করে বৈষ্ণবের গণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথা, কৃষ্ণপূজা, নামসংকীর্ত্তন ॥ ৬৬ ॥

tām̐ra saṅge ānanda kare vaiṣṇavera gaṇa  
His  company  in  joy  make  the Vaiṣṇavas’  host  
kṛṣṇa-kathā, kṛṣṇa-pūjā, nāma-saṁkīrtana  
talk of Kṛṣṇa,  worship of Kṛṣṇa,  the saṅkīrtana of the name

(66) In His company the Vaiṣṇavas delighted in talks of Kṛṣṇa, worship of Kṛṣṇa and the saṅkīrtana of the holy name.

কিন্তু সর্ব্বলোক দেখি’ কৃষ্ণবহির্ম্মুখ ৷  
বিষয়ে নিমগ্ন লোক দেখি’ পায় দুঃখ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

kintu sarva-loka dekhi’ kṛṣṇa-bahirmukha  
but  all people  seeing  turned away from Kṛṣṇa  
viṣaye nimagna loka dekhi’ pāya duḥkha  
in sense objects  sunk  people  seeing  gets  sorrow

(67) But when He saw that all people had turned away from Kṛṣṇa and were sunk in worldly things, He grieved.

লোকের নিস্তার-হেতু করেন চিন্তন ৷  
কেমতে এ সর্ব্বলোকের হইবে তারণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

lokera nistāra-hetu karena cintana  
of the people  deliverance  for  makes  thinking  
kemate e sarva-lokera haibe tāraṇa  
how  these  all people’s  will be  deliverance

(68) He pondered how they might be delivered: how could all these people be saved?

কৃষ্ণ অবতরি’ করেন ভক্তির বিস্তার ৷  
তবে ত’ সকল লোকের হইবে নিস্তার ॥ ৬৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa avatari’ karena bhaktira vistāra  
Kṛṣṇa  descending  makes  of bhakti  the spreading  
tabe ta’ sakala lokera haibe nistāra  
then  indeed  all  people’s  will be  deliverance

(69) “If Kṛṣṇa descends and spreads bhakti, then indeed all people will be delivered.”

কৃষ্ণ অবতারিতে আচার্য্য প্রতিজ্ঞা করিয়া ৷  
কৃষ্ণপূজা করে তুলসী-গঙ্গাজল দিয়া ॥ ৭০ ॥

kṛṣṇa avatārite ācārya pratijñā kariyā  
Kṛṣṇa  to bring down  the Ācārya  a vow  making  
kṛṣṇa-pūjā kare tulasī-gaṅgājala diyā  
worship of Kṛṣṇa  does  tulasī  Gaṅgā water  offering

(70) Vowing to bring Kṛṣṇa down, the Ācārya worshipped Him with tulasī and Gaṅgā water.

কৃষ্ণের আহ্বান করে সঘন হুঙ্কার ৷  
হুঙ্কারে আকৃষ্ট হৈলা ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ॥ ৭১ ॥

kṛṣṇera āhvāna kare saghana huṅkāra  
Kṛṣṇa’s  calling  does  with repeated  roars  
huṅkāre ākṛṣṭa hailā vrajendra-kumāra  
by the roars  attracted  became  the son of the king of Vraja

(71) He called to Kṛṣṇa with roar upon roar, and those roars drew the son of the king of Vraja.[^15]

জগন্নাথমিশ্র-পত্নী শচীর উদরে ৷  
অষ্ট কন্যা ক্রমে হৈল, জন্মি’ জন্মি’ মরে ॥ ৭২ ॥

jagannātha-miśra-patnī śacīra udare  
Jagannātha Miśra’s  wife  Śacī’s  in the womb  
aṣṭa kanyā krame haila, janmi’ janmi’ mare  
eight  daughters  one after another  came,  being born  being born  die

(72) Eight daughters were born one after another to Śacī, the wife of Jagannātha Miśra, and each died soon after her birth.

অপত্য-বিরহে মিশ্রের দুঃখী হৈল মন ৷  
পুত্র লাগি’ আরাধিল বিষ্ণুর চরণ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

apatya-virahe miśrera duḥkhī haila mana  
children’s  loss  in  Miśra’s  sorrowful  became  mind  
putra lāgi’ ārādhila viṣṇura caraṇa  
a son  for  worshipped  Viṣṇu’s  feet

(73) Grieving at the loss of his children, Miśra worshipped the feet of Viṣṇu, praying for a son.

তবে পুত্র জনমিল ‘বিশ্বরূপ’ নাম ৷  
মহা-গুণবান্ তেঁহ — ‘বলদেব’-ধাম ॥ ৭৪ ॥

tabe putra janamila ‘viśvarūpa’ nāma  
then  a son  was born  ‘Viśvarūpa’  by name  
mahā-guṇavān tem̐ha — ‘baladeva’-dhāma  
greatly  virtuous  He  —  the abode of ‘Baladeva’

(74) Then a son was born, named Viśvarūpa. He was rich in great virtues, a manifestation of Baladeva.

বলদেব-প্রকাশ — পরব্যোমে ‘সঙ্কর্ষণ’ ৷  
তেঁহ — বিশ্বের উপাদান-নিমিত্ত-কারণ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

baladeva-prakāśa — para-vyome ‘saṅkarṣaṇa’  
Baladeva’s  manifestation  —  in the spiritual sky  ‘Saṅkarṣaṇa’  
tem̐ha — viśvera upādāna-nimitta-kāraṇa  
He  —  of the universe  the material  efficient  cause

(75) Baladeva’s manifestation in the spiritual sky is Saṅkarṣaṇa, and He is both the material and the efficient cause of the universe.[^16]

তাঁহা বই বিশ্বে কিছু নাহি দেখি আর ৷  
অতএব ‘বিশ্বরূপ’ নাম যে তাঁহার ॥ ৭৬ ॥

tām̐hā bai viśve kichu nāhi dekhi āra  
Him  apart from  in the universe  anything  not  I see  else  
ataeva ‘viśvarūpa’ nāma ye tām̐hāra  
therefore  ‘Viśvarūpa’  the name  indeed  His

(76) Apart from Him I see nothing in the universe, and so He is called Viśvarūpa, “He whose form is the universe”.

নৈতচ্চিত্রং ভগবতি হ্যনন্তে জগদীশ্বরে ৷  
ওতং প্রোতমিদং যস্মিন্ তন্তুষ্বঙ্গ যথা পটঃ ॥ ৭৭ ॥

naitac citraṁ bhagavati  
not  this  wonderful  in Bhagavān  
hy anante jagad-īśvare  
indeed  in the unlimited  in the Lord of the universe  
otaṁ protam idaṁ yasmin  
woven lengthwise  crosswise  this  in whom  
tantuṣv aṅga yathā paṭaḥ  
on threads  O dear one  as  cloth

(77) “O King, this is no wonder in Bhagavān, the unlimited Lord of the universe, in whom this whole world is woven, warp and weft, like cloth upon its threads.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.15.35)[^17]

অতএব প্রভু তাঁরে বলে, ‘বড় ভাই’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ, বলরাম দুই — চৈতন্য, নিতাই ॥ ৭৮ ॥

ataeva prabhu tām̐re bale, ‘baḍa bhāi’  
therefore  the Lord  Him  calls  ‘elder  brother’  
kṛṣṇa, balarāma dui — caitanya, nitāi  
Kṛṣṇa,  Balarāma  the two  —  Caitanya,  Nitāi

(78) That is why the Lord called Him His elder brother. Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma — these two are Caitanya and Nitāi.[^18]

পুত্র পাঞা দম্পতি হৈলা আনন্দিত মন ৷  
বিশেষে সেবন করে গোবিন্দচরণ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

putra pāñā dampati hailā ānandita mana  
a son  obtaining  the couple  became  joyful  at heart  
viśeṣe sevana kare govinda-caraṇa  
especially  service  do  of Govinda’s  feet

(79) Blessed with a son, the couple rejoiced and served the feet of Govinda with still greater devotion.

চৌদ্দশত ছয় শকে শেষ মাঘ-মাসে ৷  
জগন্নাথ-শচীর দেহে কৃষ্ণের প্রবেশে ॥ ৮০ ॥

caudda-śata chaya śake śeṣa māgha-māse  
fourteen hundred  six  in the Śaka year  at the end  in the month of Māgha  
jagannātha-śacīra dehe kṛṣṇera praveśe  
of Jagannātha  Śacī  into the bodies  of Kṛṣṇa  the entering

(80) At the end of the month of Māgha in Śaka 1406, Kṛṣṇa entered the bodies of Jagannātha and Śacī.[^19]

মিশ্র কহে শচী-স্থানে, — দেখি অন্য রীত ৷  
জ্যোতির্ম্ময় দেহ, গেহ লক্ষ্মী-অধিষ্ঠিত ॥ ৮১ ॥

miśra kahe śacī-sthāne, — dekhi anya rīta  
Miśra  says  to Śacī  —  I see  a different  way  
jyotirmaya deha, geha lakṣmī-adhiṣṭhita  
effulgent  the body,  the house  by Lakṣmī  presided over

(81) Miśra said to Śacī, “I see something strange. Your body is radiant, and our house has become the abode of Lakṣmī.

যাঁহা তাঁহা সর্ব্বলোক করয়ে সম্মান ৷  
ঘরে পাঠাইয়া দেয় ধন, বস্ত্র, ধান ॥ ৮২ ॥

yām̐hā tām̐hā sarva-loka karaye sammāna  
wherever  wherever  all people  do  honour  
ghare pāṭhāiyā deya dhana, vastra, dhāna  
to the house  sending  give  wealth,  cloth,  rice

(82) Wherever I go, people honour me, and they send wealth, clothes and rice to our house.”

শচী কহে, — মুঞি দেখোঁ আকাশ-উপরে ৷  
দিব্যমূর্ত্তি লোক আসি’ স্তুতি হেন করে ॥ ৮৩ ॥

śacī kahe, — muñi dekhom̐ ākāśa-upare  
Śacī  says  —  I  see  in the sky  above  
divya-mūrti loka āsi’ stuti hena kare  
of divine forms  beings  coming  praises  thus  make

(83) Śacī replied, “And I see beings of divine form appear in the sky above and offer prayers.”

জগন্নাথ মিশ্র কহে, — স্বপ্ন যে দেখিল ৷  
জ্যোতির্ম্ময়-ধাম মোর হৃদয়ে পশিল ॥ ৮৪ ॥

jagannātha miśra kahe, — svapna ye dekhila  
Jagannātha  Miśra  says  —  a dream  which  I saw  
jyotirmaya-dhāma mora hṛdaye paśila  
an effulgent  abode  my  into the heart  entered

(84) Jagannātha Miśra said, “I had a dream: a radiant abode entered my heart.

আমার হৃদয় হৈতে তোমার হৃদয়ে ৷  
হেন বুঝি, জন্মিবেন কোন মহাশয়ে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

āmāra hṛdaya haite tomāra hṛdaye  
my  heart  from  into your  heart  
hena bujhi, janmibena kona mahāśaye  
so  I understand,  will be born  some  great soul

(85) From my heart it passed into yours. So I understand that some great soul is to be born.”

এত বলি’ দুঁহে রহে হরষিত হঞা ৷  
শালগ্রাম সেবা করে বিশেষ করিয়া ॥ ৮৬ ॥

eta bali’ dum̐he rahe haraṣita hañā  
thus  saying  the two  remain  joyful  becoming  
śālagrāma sevā kare viśeṣa kariyā  
the śālagrāma  service  do  special  making

(86) Speaking thus, the two were filled with joy, and they served the śālagrāma with special care.

হৈতে হৈতে হৈল গর্ভ ত্রয়োদশ মাস ৷  
তথাপি ভূমিষ্ঠ নহে, — মিশ্রের হৈল ত্রাস ॥ ৮৭ ॥

haite haite haila garbha trayodaśa māsa  
going on  going on  became  the pregnancy  thirteen  months  
tathāpi bhūmiṣṭha nahe, — miśrera haila trāsa  
even so  born  not  —  Miśra’s  became  fear

(87) Month followed month until the pregnancy reached its thirteenth month; still the child was not born, and Miśra grew afraid.

নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্ত্তী কহিল গণিয়া ৷  
এই মাসে পুত্র হবে শুভক্ষণ পাঞা ॥ ৮৮ ॥

nīlāmbara cakravartī kahila gaṇiyā  
Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī  said  calculating  
ei māse putra habe śubha-kṣaṇa pāñā  
in this  month  a son  will be  an auspicious moment  obtaining

(88) Nīlāmbara Cakravartī made his calculations and said, “A son will be born this month, at an auspicious moment.”

চৌদ্দশত সাত শকে মাস যে ফাল্গুন ৷  
পৌর্ণমাসীর সন্ধ্যাকালে হৈলে শুভক্ষণ ॥ ৮৯ ॥

caudda-śata sāta śake māsa ye phālguna  
fourteen hundred  seven  in the Śaka year  the month  which  Phālguna  
paurṇamāsīra sandhyā-kāle haile śubha-kṣaṇa  
of the full moon  at evening time  came  the auspicious moment

(89) In Śaka 1407, in the month of Phālguna, the auspicious moment came on the evening of the full moon.

সিংহ-রাশি, সিংহ-লগ্ন, উচ্চ গ্রহগণ ৷  
ষড়্বর্গ, অষ্টবর্গ, সর্ব্ব সুলক্ষণ ॥ ৯০ ॥

siṁha-rāśi, siṁha-lagna, ucca graha-gaṇa  
Leo  the sign,  Leo  the ascendant,  exalted  the planets  
ṣaḍ-varga, aṣṭa-varga, sarva sulakṣaṇa  
the six divisions,  the eight divisions,  all  auspicious signs

(90) The moon was in Leo, Leo was rising and the planets were exalted; the six divisions and the eight divisions all showed auspicious signs.[^20]

অ-কলঙ্ক গৌরচন্দ্র দিলা দরশন ৷  
স-কলঙ্ক চন্দ্রে আর কোন্ প্রয়োজন ॥ ৯১ ॥

a-kalaṅka gauracandra dilā daraśana  
spotless  Gauracandra  gave  sight of Himself  
sa-kalaṅka candre āra kon prayojana  
spotted  in the moon  further  what  need

(91) Once the spotless moon Gauracandra had shown Himself, what further need was there of the spotted moon?

এত জানি’ চন্দ্রে রাহু করিলা গ্রহণ ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’নামে ভাসে ত্রিভুবন ॥ ৯২ ॥

eta jāni’ candre rāhu karilā grahaṇa  
this  knowing  the moon  Rāhu  made  seizing  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’nāme bhāse tri-bhuvana  
‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Hari’  with the names  floats  the three worlds

(92) Knowing this, Rāhu seized the moon, and the three worlds were flooded with the names “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Hari!”

জয় জয় ধ্বনি হৈল সকল ভুবন ৷  
চমৎকার হৈয়া লোক ভাবে মনে মন ॥ ৯৩ ॥

jaya jaya dhvani haila sakala bhuvana  
‘glory  glory’  the sound  became  in all  the worlds  
camatkāra haiyā loka bhāve mane mana  
amazed  becoming  the people  think  within  their minds

(93) Cries of “Jaya! Jaya!” rang through all the worlds, and the people, amazed, wondered within themselves.

জগৎ ভরিয়া লোক বলে — ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
সেইক্ষণে গৌরকৃষ্ণ ভূমে অবতরি ॥ ৯৪ ॥

jagat bhariyā loka bale — ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
the world  filling  the people  say  —  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’  
sei-kṣaṇe gaurakṛṣṇa bhūme avatari  
at that moment  Gaura-Kṛṣṇa  on the earth  descended

(94) The whole world was filled with people crying, “Hari! Hari!” — and at that very moment Gaura-Kṛṣṇa descended to the earth.

প্রসন্ন হইল সব জগতের মন ৷  
‘হরি’ বলি’ হিন্দুকে হাস্য করয়ে যবন ॥ ৯৫ ॥

prasanna haila saba jagatera mana  
glad  became  all  of the world  the mind  
‘hari’ bali’ hinduke hāsya karaye yavana  
‘Hari’  saying  at the Hindus  mockery  makes  the Muslim

(95) The whole world was glad at heart; even the Muslims, mocking the Hindus, cried out, “Hari!”[^21]

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “The rays of *hari-bhajana* shining from the devotee-moons destroy the darkness of the living beings’ ignorance.”

[^2]: Heading in the edition to 15–16: Murāri Gupta strung together the Ādi-līlā in his *Caitanya-carita*, and Svarūpa Dāmodara the later pastimes in his *Kaḍacā* (diary).

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śrī Murāri Gupta’s notes on the Ādi-līlā still survive; the Vaiṣṇavas read them, and they heard the notes of Śrī Svarūpa Gosvāmī’s diary from the lips of Śrī Raghunātha Dāsa Gosvāmī — and so they describe the Lord’s pastimes.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya*: on this full moon Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya — the Lord of Goloka, the original source of all avatāras, in whom Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one — descended together with the names of Kṛṣṇa from His own abode, the Gaura-prakoṣṭha, into the earthly Navadvīpa; and the full moon itself is no worldly day but a transcendental goddess of the lunar day (*tithi*), who descended into time to serve Him. The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* cites a verse: “In the twenty-eighth age of Vaivasvata Manu, in the year fourteen hundred and seven, on the lovely bank of the Bhāgīrathī, from the great ocean of Śacī’s womb, on a full moon seized by Rāhu, Gaurāṅga appeared.”

[^5]: Heading in the edition: “Crying on the pretext of making people chant the name — and quieting at the name alone.”

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: while teaching His pupils the sūtras of grammar with their *vṛtti* and *ṭīkā*, the Lord taught them the purport of Kṛṣṇa’s name; following that teaching, the Gosvāmīs (Śrī Jīva) later composed two grammars, the *Laghu-* and the *Bṛhad-Harināmāmṛta-vyākaraṇa*, through which one gains both a knowledge of words and devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

[^7]: Heading in the edition to 34–36: “Of the last twenty-four years in Nīlācala, six were spent preaching the name and prema from the ocean to the Himalayas.”

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: one in whom prema has awakened dwells in the transcendental rasa of separation (*vipralambha*), which nourishes the rasa of union (*sambhoga*); without separation union finds no fullness. This state of love Śrī Gaurasundara taught the souls of the world, in order to taste it Himself.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: Vidyāpati, Jayadeva and Caṇḍīdāsa sang of the transcendental rasa of separation, and the Lord, absorbed in the heart of Śrī Rādhā, relished their songs with His two most intimate devotees, Svarūpa Dāmodara and Rāmānanda Rāya. But for those who identify with the body, who look in these songs for worldly poetry, or who, like the *prākṛta-sahajiyās*, claim to follow *rāgānugā* in order to please their senses, any discussion of the pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa is strictly forbidden: they would take those transcendental pastimes for worldly love and ruin themselves.

[^10]: Heading in the edition: “On the basis of those notes Ṭhākura Vṛndāvana Dāsa described the pastimes of Gaura.”

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 35): the cowherd Parjanya, Kṛṣṇa’s grandfather, appeared in Śrīhaṭṭa as Upendra Miśra, the father of seven sons; he lived in the village of Ḍhākā-dakṣiṇa.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: “Who Jagannātha was in the avatāra of Kṛṣṇa.”

[^16]: Heading in the edition: “Viśvarūpa is Mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa of Vaikuṇṭha.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Viśvarūpa is the avatāra of Mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa of the spiritual sky. *Anubhāṣya*: Viśvarūpa, the elder brother of Śrī Gaurahari, took sannyāsa before marriage, receiving the name Śaṅkarāraṇya, and in Śaka 1431 passed from view at Pandharpur; He is both the material and the efficient cause of the universe. According to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (58–59), Viśvarūpa is the *vyūha* Saṅkarṣaṇa, while Nityānanda Avadhūta is Baladeva Himself, the Balarāma of Vraja.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śukadeva says this to Parīkṣit about Baladeva’s slaying of the demon Dhenuka.

[^18]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahā-saṅkarṣaṇa, the material and the efficient cause, pervades the universe through and through, and therefore Mahāprabhu calls Viśvarūpa His elder brother; but the Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma of Kṛṣṇa’s own abode are Caitanya and Nitāi, and so Nityānanda Prabhu is the original Saṅkarṣaṇa, Baladeva Himself.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya* to 80–86: Jagannātha and Śacī are the Lord’s eternal associates (*nitya-siddha*); their hearts and bodies are made of pure goodness (*śuddha-sattva*) and are not like those of ordinary living beings. Pure goodness is called *vasudeva*, and in it Vāsudeva, who eternally enjoys His spiritual pastimes, becomes manifest. The union of Jagannātha and Śacī and the conception in Śacī’s womb were not like the carnal union and pregnancy of worldly people; even to think so is an offence. According to the *Bhāgavatam* (10.2.16–18, with the comments of Śrīdhara Svāmī and Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa) and the *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, Kṛṣṇa first became manifest in the heart of Vasudeva, and from his heart in the heart of Devakī; so too Śrī Gaurasundara appeared in the hearts of Miśra and Śacī, wholly absorbed as they were in serving the feet of Govinda (13.79).

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *ṣaḍ-varga* are the six divisions of a sign — *kṣetra*, *horā*, *drekkāṇa*, *navāṁśa*, *dvādaśāṁśa* and *triṁśāṁśa*; by them Cakravartī determined the auspicious signs. The *aṣṭa-varga* is reckoned from the positions of the planets, as taught in the *Bṛhaj-jātaka* and other books; by this reckoning too he found Mahāprabhu’s signs auspicious.
