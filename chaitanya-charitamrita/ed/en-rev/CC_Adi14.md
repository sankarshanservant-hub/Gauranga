“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 14  
**A Brief Description of the Childhood Pastimes**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

কথঞ্চন স্মৃতে যস্মিন্ দুষ্করং সুকরং ভবেৎ ৷  
বিস্মৃতে বিপরীতং স্যাৎ শ্রীচৈতন্যমমুং ভজে ॥ ১ ॥

kathañcana smṛte yasmin  
in any way  being remembered  whom  
duṣkaraṁ sukaraṁ bhavet  
the difficult  easy  becomes  
vismṛte viparītaṁ syāt  
being forgotten  the reverse  becomes  
śrī-caitanyam amuṁ bhaje  
Śrī Caitanya  Him  I worship

(1) “I worship Śrī Caitanya. Remember Him in any way at all, and the difficult becomes easy; forget Him, and the easy becomes difficult.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 20.1)

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য, জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র, জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya, jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra, jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

প্রভুর কহিল এই জন্মলীলা-সূত্র ৷  
যশোদা-নন্দন যৈছে হৈল শচীপুত্র ॥ ৩ ॥

prabhura kahila ei janmalīlā-sūtra  
of the Lord  I have told  this  of the birth-pastime  the outline  
yaśodā-nandana yaiche haila śacī-putra  
of Yaśodā  the son  how  became  Śacī’s son

(3) Thus I have given an outline of the Lord’s birth, telling how the son of Yaśodā became the son of Śacī.

সংক্ষেপে কহিল জন্মলীলা-অনুক্রম ৷  
এবে কহি বাল্যলীলা-সূত্রের গণন ॥ ৪ ॥

saṅkṣepe kahila janma-līlā-anukrama  
in brief  I have told  of the birth-pastimes  the sequence  
ebe kahi bālyalīlā-sūtrera gaṇana  
now  I tell  of the childhood pastimes’  outline  the account

(4) I have told the pastimes of His birth briefly and in order; now I shall name, one by one, the chief events of His childhood.

বন্দে চৈতন্যকৃষ্ণস্য বাল্যলীলাং মনোহরাম্ ৷  
লৌকিকীমপি তামীশ-চেষ্টয়া বলিতান্তরাম্ ॥ ৫ ॥

vande caitanya-kṛṣṇasya  
I worship  of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  
bālya-līlāṁ mano-harām  
the childhood pastimes  enchanting  
laukikīm api tām īśa-  
worldly  though  them  by the Lord’s  
ceṣṭayā valitāntarām  
acts  inwardly imbued

(5) I worship the enchanting childhood pastimes of Kṛṣṇa, who came as Caitanya. Though they seem like the doings of an ordinary human child, within they are interwoven with the acts of the Lord Himself.[^1]

বাল্যলীলায় আগে প্রভুর উত্তান শয়ন ৷  
পিতা-মাতায় দেখাইল চিহ্ন চরণ ॥ ৬ ॥

bālya-līlāya āge prabhura uttāna śayana  
in the childhood pastimes  first  the Lord’s  face-up  lying  
pitā-mātāya dekhāila cihna caraṇa  
to father and mother  He showed  the marks  of the feet

(6) At the very start of His childhood pastimes, as Śrī Caitanya lay on His back, He showed His father and mother the divine marks on His feet.[^2]

গৃহে দুই জন দেখি’ লঘুপদ-চিহ্ন ৷  
তাহাতেই ধ্বজ, বজ্র, শঙ্খ, চক্র, মীন ॥ ৭ ॥

gṛhe dui jana dekhi’ laghupada-cihna  
in the house  the two  seeing  of small feet  the prints  
tāhātei dhvaja, vajra, śaṅkha, cakra, mīna  
in them indeed  flag,  thunderbolt,  conch,  disc,  fish

(7) In the house the two of them saw the prints of tiny feet, and in them a flag, a thunderbolt, a conch, a disc and a fish.

দেখিয়া দোঁহার চিত্তে জন্মিল বিস্ময় ৷  
কার পদচিহ্ন ঘরে, না পায় নিশ্চয় ॥ ৮ ॥

dekhiyā dom̐hāra citte janmila vismaya  
seeing  of both  in the hearts  arose  wonder  
kāra pada-cihna ghare, nā pāya niścaya  
whose  footprints  in the house  not  they find  certainty

(8) At the sight both were filled with wonder, unable to tell whose footprints these could be.

মিশ্র কহে, — বালগোপাল আছে শিলা-সঙ্গে ৷  
তিঁহো মূর্ত্তি-হঞা খেলে, জানি, ঘরে রঙ্গে ॥ ৯ ॥

miśra kahe, — bālagopāla āche śilā-saṅge  
Miśra  says  —  the child Gopāla  is  with the śilā  
tim̐ho mūrti-hañā khele, jāni, ghare raṅge  
He  taking a form  plays,  I think,  in the house  for His delight

(9) Miśra said, “The child Gopāla is present in our *śālagrāma-śilā*. I think He takes on a form and plays about the house for His own delight.”

সেই ক্ষণে জাগি’ নিমাই করয়ে ক্রন্দন ৷  
অঙ্কে লঞা শচী তাঁরে পিয়াইল স্তন ॥ ১০ ॥

sei kṣaṇe jāgi’ nimāi karaye krandana  
at that  moment  waking  Nimāi  begins  crying  
aṅke lañā śacī tām̐re piyāila stana  
on her lap  taking  Śacī  to Him  gave to drink  the breast

(10) At that moment Nimāi woke and began to cry. Śacī took Him on her lap and nursed Him.

স্তন পিয়াইতে পুত্রের চরণ দেখিল ৷  
সেই চিহ্ন পায়ে দেখি’ — মিশ্রে বোলাইল ॥ ১১ ॥

stana piyāite putrera caraṇa dekhila  
the breast  while giving  of her son  the feet  she saw  
sei cihna pāye dekhi’ — miśre bolāila  
those  marks  on His feet  seeing  —  Miśra  she called

(11) As she nursed Him, she looked at her son’s feet, and when she saw those same marks on them, she called Miśra.

দেখিয়া মিশ্রের হৈল আনন্দিত মতি ৷  
গুপ্তে বোলাইল নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্ত্তী ॥ ১২ ॥

dekhiyā miśrera haila ānandita mati  
seeing  Miśra’s  became  joyful  mind  
gupte bolāila nīlāmbara cakravartī  
secretly  he called  Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī

(12) The sight filled Miśra’s heart with joy, and he quietly sent for Nīlāmbara Cakravartī.

চিহ্ন দেখি’ চক্রবর্ত্তী বলেন হাসিয়া ৷  
লগ্ন গণি’ পূর্ব্বে আমি রাখিয়াছি লিখিয়া ॥ ১৩ ॥

cihna dekhi’ cakravartī balena hāsiyā  
the marks  seeing  Cakravartī  says  smiling  
lagna gaṇi’ pūrve āmi rākhiyāchi likhiyā  
the birth-chart  reckoning  earlier  I  have kept  written down

(13) Seeing the marks, Cakravartī smiled and said, “When I cast His birth chart, I foresaw this and wrote it down.

বত্রিশ লক্ষণ — মহাপুরুষ-ভূষণ ৷  
এই শিশু-অঙ্গে দেখি সে সব লক্ষণ ॥ ১৪ ॥

batriśa lakṣaṇa — mahāpuruṣa-bhūṣaṇa  
thirty-two  marks  —  of a great personality  the ornaments  
ei śiśu-aṅge dekhi se saba lakṣaṇa  
on this  child’s body  I see  those  all  marks

(14) Thirty-two marks adorn a great personality, and I see every one of them on this child’s body.

পঞ্চদীর্ঘঃ পঞ্চসূক্ষ্মঃ সপ্তরক্তঃ ষড়ুন্নতঃ ৷  
ত্রিহ্রস্ব-পৃথু-গম্ভীরো দ্বাত্রিংশল্লক্ষণো মহান্ ॥ ১৫ ॥

pañca-dīrghaḥ pañca-sūkṣmaḥ  
five long  five fine  
sapta-raktaḥ ṣaḍ-unnataḥ  
seven red  six raised  
tri-hrasva-pṛthu-gambhīro  
three short, broad, deep  
dvātriṁśal-lakṣaṇo mahān  
thirty-two-marked  great

(15) “A great personality bears thirty-two marks: five parts of his body are long, five fine, seven reddish and six raised; three are short, three broad and three deep.” (Sāmudrika, 3)[^3]

নারায়ণের চিহ্নযুক্ত শ্রীহস্ত-চরণ ৷  
এই শিশু সর্ব্ব লোকে করিবে তারণ ॥ ১৬ ॥

nārāyaṇera cihna-yukta śrī-hasta-caraṇa  
of Nārāyaṇa  bearing the marks  His holy  hands and feet  
ei śiśu sarva loke karibe tāraṇa  
this  child  all  people  will  deliver

(16) His hands and feet bear the marks of Nārāyaṇa. This child will deliver all people.

এই ত’ করিবে বৈষ্ণব-ধর্ম্মের প্রচার ৷  
ইহা হৈতে হবে দুই কুলের নিস্তার ॥ ১৭ ॥

ei ta’ karibe vaiṣṇava-dharmera pracāra  
this one  indeed  will make  of Vaiṣṇava  dharma  the spreading  
ihā haite habe dui kulera nistāra  
from Him  will be  of the two  families  the deliverance

(17) He will spread the Vaiṣṇava dharma far and wide, and through Him both His families will be delivered.[^4]

মহোৎসব কর, সব বোলাহ ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
আজি দিন ভাল, — করিব নাম-করণ ॥ ১৮ ॥

mahotsava kara, saba bolāha brāhmaṇa  
a great festival  hold,  all  call  the brāhmaṇas  
āji dina bhāla, — kariba nāma-karaṇa  
today  the day  is good  —  I shall perform  the naming

(18) Hold a great festival and invite all the brāhmaṇas. Today is an auspicious day; I shall perform the naming ceremony.

সর্ব্বলোকে করিবে এই ধারণ, পোষণ ৷  
‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ নাম ইহার, — এই ত’ কারণ ॥ ১৯ ॥

sarva-loke karibe ei dhāraṇa, poṣaṇa  
all  people  will  this one  uphold,  nourish  
‘viśvambhara’ nāma ihāra, — ei ta’ kāraṇa  
‘Viśvambhara’  the name  His  —  this  indeed  the reason

(19) He will uphold and nourish all people, and that is why His name is Viśvambhara.”[^5]

শুনি’ শচী-মিশ্রের মনে আনন্দ বাড়িল ৷  
ব্রাহ্মণ-ব্রাহ্মণী আনি’ মহোৎসব কৈল ॥ ২০ ॥

śuni’ śacī-miśrera mane ānanda bāḍila  
hearing  of Śacī and Miśra  in the minds  joy  grew  
brāhmaṇa-brāhmaṇī āni’ mahotsava kaila  
brāhmaṇas and their wives  bringing  a great festival  they held

(20) Hearing this, Śacī and Miśra rejoiced all the more. They invited the brāhmaṇas and their wives and held a great festival.

তবে কত দিনে প্রভুর জানু-চংক্রমণ ৷  
নানা চমৎকার তথা করাইল দর্শন ॥ ২১ ॥

tabe kata dine prabhura jānu-caṁkramaṇa  
then  some  days later  the Lord’s  on the knees  moving  
nānā camatkāra tathā karāila darśana  
various  wonders  there  He made  to be seen

(21) Some days later the Lord began to crawl on His knees, and He showed many wonders.[^6]

ক্রন্দনের ছলে বলাইল হরিনাম ৷  
নারী সব ‘হরি’ বলে, — হাসে গৌরধাম ॥ ২২ ॥

krandanera chale balāila hari-nāma  
of crying  on the pretext  He made say  the name of Hari  
nārī saba ‘hari’ bale, — hāse gaura-dhāma  
the women  all  ‘Hari’  say  —  laughs  the golden One

(22) On the pretext of crying He made everyone chant the name of Hari: all the women would call out “Hari!”, and the golden Lord would laugh.[^7]

তবে কত দিনে কৈল পদ-চংক্রমণ ৷  
শিশুগণে মিলি’ কৈল বিবিধ খেলন ॥ ২৩ ॥

tabe kata dine kaila pada-caṅkramaṇa  
then  some  days later  He did  on the feet  walking  
śiśu-gaṇe mili’ kaila vividha khelana  
with the children  joining  He did  various  games

(23) Later still He began to walk, and He played all sorts of games with the other children.

একদিন শচী খই-সন্দেশ আনিয়া ৷  
বাটা ভরি’ দিয়া বলে — খাও ত’ বসিয়া ॥ ২৪ ॥

ekadina śacī khai-sandeśa āniyā  
one day  Śacī  puffed rice and sweets  bringing  
bāṭā bhari’ diyā bale — khāo ta’ basiyā  
a dish  filling  giving  says  —  eat  now  sitting down

(24) One day Śacī brought puffed rice and sweets, filled a dish, and gave it to Him, saying, “Sit down and eat.”

এত বলি’ গেলা শচী গৃহে কর্ম্ম করিতে ৷  
লুকাঞা লাগিলা শিশু মৃত্তিকা খাইতে ॥ ২৫ ॥

eta bali’ gelā śacī gṛhe karma karite  
so  saying  went  Śacī  into the house  work  to do  
lukāñā lāgilā śiśu mṛttikā khāite  
hiding  began  the child  earth  to eat

(25) With that, Śacī went into the house to do her work, and the child began to eat earth on the sly.[^8]

দেখি’ শচী ধাঞা আইলা করি’ ‘হায়’ ‘হায়’ ৷  
মাটি কাড়ি’ লঞা বলে মাটি কেনে খায় ॥ ২৬ ॥

dekhi’ śacī dhāñā āilā kari’ ‘hāya’ ‘hāya’  
seeing  Śacī  running  came  crying  ‘alas!’  ‘alas!’  
māṭi kāḍi’ lañā bale māṭi kene khāya  
the earth  snatching  taking  says  earth  why  He eats

(26) When Śacī saw it, she came running and cried out, “Oh dear, oh dear!” She snatched the earth away and said, “Why are You eating earth?”

কান্দিয়া বলেন শিশু — কেনে কর রোষ ৷  
তুমি মাটি খাইতে দিলে মোর কিবা দোষ ॥ ২৭ ॥

kāndiyā balena śiśu — kene kara roṣa  
weeping  says  the child  —  why  you make  anger  
tumi māṭi khāite dile mora kibā doṣa  
you  earth  to eat  gave,  My  what  fault

(27) The child wept and said, “Why are you angry? You gave Me earth to eat — how is that My fault?

খই-সন্দেশ-অন্ন, যতেক — মাটির বিকার ৷  
ইহ মাটি, সেহ মাটি, কি ভেদ-বিচার ॥ ২৮ ॥

khai-sandeśa-anna, yateka — māṭira vikāra  
puffed rice, sweets, food,  all that  —  of earth  transformations  
iha māṭi, seha māṭi, ki bheda-vicāra  
this  earth,  that  earth,  what  of difference  consideration

(28) Puffed rice, sweets and all food are only earth in another form. This is earth and that is earth; why make any distinction?[^9]

মাটি — দেহ, মাটি — ভক্ষ্য, দেখহ বিচারি’ ৷  
অবিচারে দেহ দোষ, কি বলিতে পারি ॥ ২৯ ॥

māṭi — deha, māṭi — bhakṣya, dekhaha vicāri’  
earth  —  the body,  earth  —  food,  look  considering  
avicāre deha doṣa, ki balite pāri  
without consideration  you give  blame,  what  to say  can I

(29) The body is earth, and food is earth — think it over and see. You blame Me without thinking; what can I say?”

অন্তরে বিস্মিত শচী বলিল তাহারে ৷  
মাটি খাইতে জ্ঞানযোগ কে শিখাল তোরে ॥ ৩০ ॥

antare vismita śacī balila tāhāre  
inwardly  astonished  Śacī  said  to Him  
māṭi khāite jñāna-yoga ke śikhāla tore  
earth  for eating  this jñāna-yoga  who  taught  You

(30) Inwardly astonished, Śacī said to Him, “Who taught You such *jñāna-yoga*, just so You could eat earth?

মাটির বিকার অন্ন খাইলে দেহ-পুষ্টি হয় ৷  
মাটি খাইলে রোগ হয়, দেহ যায় ক্ষয় ॥ ৩১ ॥

māṭira vikāra anna khāile deha-puṣṭi haya  
of earth  the transformation  food  if eaten  of the body  nourishment  is  
māṭi khāile roga haya, deha yāya kṣaya  
earth  if eaten  disease  comes,  the body  goes  to decay

(31) Food is earth transformed, and when we eat it, it nourishes the body. But eat earth itself, and disease sets in and the body wastes away.

মাটির বিকার ঘটে পানি ভরি’ আনি ৷  
মাটি-পিণ্ডে ধরি যবে, শোষি’ যায় পানি ॥ ৩২ ॥

māṭira vikāra ghaṭe pāni bhari’ āni  
of earth  the transformation  in a pot  water  filling  I bring  
māṭi-piṇḍe dhari yabe, śoṣi’ yāya pāni  
in a lump of earth  I hold  when,  soaking  goes  the water

(32) In a pot, which is earth transformed, I fetch water; but try to hold it in a lump of earth, and the water soaks away.”

আত্ম লুকাইতে প্রভু বলিলা তাঁহারে ৷  
আগে কেন ইহা মাতা না শিখালে মোরে ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ātma lukāite prabhu balilā tām̐hāre  
Himself  to hide  the Lord  said  to her  
āge kena ihā mātā nā śikhāle more  
before  why  this  mother  not  taught  Me

(33) To keep Himself hidden, the Lord said to her, “Mother, why did you not teach Me this before?

এবে সে জানিলাঙ, আর মাটি না খাইব ৷  
ক্ষুধা লাগে যবে, তবে তোমার স্তন পিব ॥ ৩৪ ॥

ebe se jānilāṅa, āra māṭi nā khāiba  
now  indeed  I know,  any more  earth  not  I will eat  
kṣudhā lāge yabe, tabe tomāra stana piba  
hunger  comes  when,  then  your  breast  I will drink

(34) Now that I know, I will not eat earth any more. When I am hungry, I will drink at your breast.”

এত বলি’ জননীর কোলেতে চড়িয়া ৷  
স্তন পান করে প্রভু ঈষৎ হাসিয়া ॥ ৩৫ ॥

eta bali’ jananīra kolete caḍiyā  
so  saying  of the mother  onto the lap  climbing  
stana pāna kare prabhu īṣat hāsiyā  
the breast  drinking  does  the Lord  slightly  smiling

(35) Saying this, the Lord climbed onto His mother’s lap and, with a little smile, began to nurse.

এই মতে নানা-ছলে ঐশ্বর্য্য দেখায় ৷  
বাল্যভাব প্রকটিয়া পশ্চাৎ লুকায় ॥ ৩৬ ॥

ei mate nānā-chale aiśvarya dekhāya  
in this way  on various  pretexts  majesty  He shows  
bālya-bhāva prakaṭiyā paścāt lukāya  
the child’s mood  displaying  afterwards  He hides

(36) Thus, on one pretext or another, He would show His majesty, and afterwards, slipping back into the ways of a child, He would hide it again.

অতিথি-বিপ্রের অন্ন খাইল তিনবার ৷  
পাছে গুপ্তে সেই বিপ্রে করিল নিস্তার ॥ ৩৭ ॥

atithi-viprera anna khāila tina-bāra  
of a guest  brāhmaṇa’s  food  He ate  three times  
pāche gupte sei vipre karila nistāra  
afterwards  in secret  that  brāhmaṇa  He made  delivered

(37) Three times He ate the food a brāhmaṇa guest had cooked, and afterwards, in secret, He delivered him.[^10]

চোরে লঞা গেল প্রভুকে বাহিরে পাইয়া ৷  
তার স্কন্ধে চড়ি’ আইলা তারে ভুলাইয়া ॥ ৩৮ ॥

core lañā gela prabhuke bāhire pāiyā  
thieves  taking  went off  the Lord  outside  finding  
tāra skandhe caḍi’ āilā tāre bhulāiyā  
on their  shoulders  riding  He came back  them  bewildering

(38) Another time some thieves found the Lord outside and carried Him off; but He bewildered them and came home riding on their shoulders.[^11]

ব্যাধি-ছলে জগদীশ-হিরণ্য-সদনে ৷  
বিষ্ণু-নৈবেদ্য খাইল একাদশী-দিনে ॥ ৩৯ ॥

vyādhi-chale jagadīśa-hiraṇya-sadane  
of illness  on the pretext  of Jagadīśa and Hiraṇya  in the house  
viṣṇu-naivedya khāila ekādaśī-dine  
Viṣṇu’s  offering  He ate  on Ekādaśī  day

(39) On the day of Ekādaśī, pretending to be ill, He ate the food offered to Viṣṇu in the house of Jagadīśa and Hiraṇya.[^12]

শিশুগণ লয়ে পাড়া-পড়সীর ঘরে ৷  
চুরি করি’ দ্রব্য খায়, মারে বালকেরে ॥ ৪০ ॥

śiśugaṇa laye pāḍā-paḍasīra ghare  
the children  taking  of the neighbours  into the houses  
curi kari’ dravya khāya, māre bālakere  
stealing  things  He eats,  beats  the boys

(40) He would take the children into the neighbours’ houses and steal food to eat, and He would beat the other boys.[^13]

শিশু সব শচী-স্থানে কৈল নিবেদন ৷  
শুনি’ শচী পুত্রে কিছু দিলা ওলাহন ॥ ৪১ ॥

śiśu saba śacī-sthāne kaila nivedana  
the children  all  in Śacī’s presence  made  complaint  
śuni’ śacī putre kichu dilā olāhana  
hearing  Śacī  her son  a little  gave  a scolding

(41) The children all complained to Śacī, and when she heard them, she scolded her son a little:

কেনে চুরি কর, কেনে মারহ শিশুরে ৷  
কেনে পর-ঘরে যাহ, কি বা নাহি ঘরে ॥ ৪২ ॥

kene curi kara, kene māraha śiśure  
why  stealing  You do,  why  You beat  the children  
kene para-ghare yāha, ki vā nāhi ghare  
why  to others’ houses  You go,  what  or  is not  at home

(42) “Why do You steal? Why do You beat the other children? Why do You go into other people’s houses? What do You lack at home?”

শুনি’ ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা প্রভু ঘর-ভিতর যাঞা ৷  
ঘরে যত ভাণ্ড ছিল, ফেলিল ভাঙ্গিয়া ॥ ৪৩ ॥

śuni’ kruddha hañā prabhu ghara-bhitara yāñā  
hearing  angry  becoming  the Lord  inside the house  going  
ghare yata bhāṇḍa chila, phelila bhāṅgiyā  
in the house  all the  pots  there were,  He threw down  breaking

(43) Hearing this, the Lord grew angry, went into the house, and smashed every pot in it.

তবে শচী কোলে করি’ করাইল সন্তোষ ৷  
লজ্জিত হইলা প্রভু জানি’ নিজ দোষ ॥ ৪৪ ॥

tabe śacī kole kari’ karāila santoṣa  
then  Śacī  on her lap  taking  made  content  
lajjita hailā prabhu jāni’ nija doṣa  
ashamed  became  the Lord  knowing  His own  fault

(44) Then Śacī took Him on her lap and comforted Him, and the Lord, knowing He was at fault, felt ashamed.

কভু মৃদুহস্তে কৈল মাতাকে তাড়ন ৷  
মাতাকে মূর্চ্ছিতা দেখি, করয়ে ক্রন্দন ॥ ৪৫ ॥

kabhu mṛdu-haste kaila mātāke tāḍana  
once  with soft hands  He did  His mother  striking  
mātāke mūrcchitā dekhi, karaye krandana  
His mother  fainted  seeing,  begins  crying

(45) Once He struck His mother with His soft little hands, and when He saw her faint, He began to cry.

নারীগণ কহে, — নারিকেল দেহ আনি’ ৷  
তবে সুস্থ হইবেন তোমার জননী ॥ ৪৬ ॥

nārīgaṇa kahe, — nārikela deha āni’  
the women  say  —  a coconut  give  bringing  
tabe sustha haibena tomāra jananī  
then  well  will be  Your  mother

(46) The women said, “Bring her a coconut, and then Your mother will recover.”[^14]

বাহিরে যাঞা আনিলেন দুই নারিকেল ৷  
দেখিয়া অপূর্ব্ব হৈলা বিস্মিত সকল ॥ ৪৭ ॥

bāhire yāñā ānilena dui nārikela  
outside  going  He brought  two  coconuts  
dekhiyā apūrva hailā vismita sakala  
seeing  the marvel  became  astonished  all

(47) He went out and brought back two coconuts, and everyone was astonished at the marvel.

কভু শিশু-সঙ্গে স্নান করিল গঙ্গাতে ৷  
কন্যাগণ আইলা তাঁহা দেবতা পূজিতে ॥ ৪৮ ॥

kabhu śiśu-saṅge snāna karila gaṅgāte  
sometimes  with the children  bath  He took  in the Ganges  
kanyāgaṇa āilā tām̐hā devatā pūjite  
the girls  came  there  the gods  to worship

(48) Sometimes He bathed in the Ganges with the other children, and the girls would come there to worship the gods.

গঙ্গাস্নান করি’ পূজা করিতে লাগিলা ৷  
কন্যাগণ-মধ্যে প্রভু আসিয়া বসিলা ॥ ৪৯ ॥

gaṅgā-snāna kari’ pūjā karite lāgilā  
bath in the Ganges  having taken  worship  to do  they began  
kanyāgaṇa-madhye prabhu āsiyā basilā  
of the girls  in the midst  the Lord  coming  sat down

(49) When they had bathed in the Ganges and begun their worship, the Lord would come and sit down among them.

কন্যারে কহে, — আমা পূজ, আমি দিব বর ৷  
গঙ্গা-দুর্গা — দাসী মোর, মহেশ — কিঙ্কর ॥ ৫০ ॥

kanyāre kahe, — āmā pūja, āmi diba vara  
to the girls  He says  —  Me  worship,  I  will give  a boon  
gaṅgā-durgā — dāsī mora, maheśa — kiṅkara  
Gaṅgā and Durgā  —  maidservants  Mine,  Maheśa  —  servant

(50) He would say to the girls, “Worship Me, and I will grant you a boon. Gaṅgā and Durgā are My maidservants, and Maheśa is My servant.”[^15]

আপনি চন্দন পরি’ পরেন ফুলমালা ৷  
নৈবেদ্য কাড়িয়া খা’ন — সন্দেশ, চাল, কলা ॥ ৫১ ॥

āpani candana pari’ parena phula-mālā  
Himself  sandal paste  putting on  He puts on  flower garlands  
naivedya kāḍiyā khā’na — sandeśa, cāla, kalā  
the offerings  snatching  He eats  —  sweets,  rice,  bananas

(51) He would smear Himself with sandal paste and put on the flower garlands, then snatch the offerings and eat them — sweets, rice and bananas.

ক্রোধে কন্যাগণ কহে — শুন, হে নিমাঞি ৷  
গ্রাম-সম্বন্ধে হও তুমি আমা-সবার ভাই ॥ ৫২ ॥

krodhe kanyāgaṇa kahe — śuna, he nimāñi  
in anger  the girls  say  —  listen,  O  Nimāi  
grāma-sambandhe hao tumi āmā-sabāra bhāi  
by village  ties  are  You  of us  all  the brother

(52) The girls said angrily, “Listen, Nimāi! By village ties You are a brother to us all.

আমা-সবার পক্ষে ইহা কহিতে না যুয়ায় ৷  
না লহ দেবতা-সজ্জ, না কর অন্যায় ॥ ৫৩ ॥

āmā-sabāra pakṣe ihā kahite nā yuyāya  
of us all  on the part  this  to say  not  it is fitting  
nā laha devatā-sajja, nā kara anyāya  
do not  take  the gods’  offerings,  do not  do  wrong

(53) It is not proper for us to say this to You, but do not take the gods’ offerings, and do not misbehave!”

প্রভু কহে, — তোমা-সবাকে দিলাঙ এই বর ৷  
তোমা-সবার ভর্ত্তা হবে পরম সুন্দর ॥ ৫৪ ॥

prabhu kahe, — tomā-sabāke dilāṅa ei vara  
the Lord  says  —  to you all  I give  this  boon  
tomā-sabāra bhartā habe parama sundara  
of you all  the husband  will be  supremely  handsome

(54) The Lord said, “I give you all this boon: each of you will have a most handsome husband.

পণ্ডিত, বিদগ্ধ, যুবা, ধনধান্যবান্ ৷  
সাত সাত পুত্র হবে — চিরায়ু, মতিমান্ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

paṇḍita, vidagdha, yuvā, dhana-dhānyavān  
learned,  refined,  young,  rich in wealth and grain  
sāta sāta putra habe — cirāyu, matimān  
seven  seven  sons  there will be  —  long-lived,  wise

(55) He will be learned and refined, young and rich in wealth and grain, and each of you will have seven sons, long-lived and wise.”

বর শুনি’ কন্যাগণের অন্তরে সন্তোষ ৷  
বাহিরে ভর্ৎসন করে করি’ মিথ্যা রোষ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

vara śuni’ kanyā-gaṇera antare santoṣa  
the boon  hearing  of the girls  in the hearts  delight  
bāhire bhartsana kare kari’ mithyā roṣa  
outwardly  scolding  they do  making  false  anger

(56) Hearing this boon, the girls were delighted at heart, but outwardly they scolded Him with feigned anger.

কোন কন্যা পলাইল নৈবেদ্য লইয়া ৷  
তারে ডাকি’ কহে প্রভু সক্রোধ হইয়া ॥ ৫৭ ॥

kona kanyā palāila naivedya laiyā  
some  girl  ran away  the offering  taking  
tāre ḍāki’ kahe prabhu sakrodha haiyā  
her  calling  says  the Lord  angry  becoming

(57) If some girl ran off with her offering, the Lord would call after her angrily:

যদি নৈবেদ্য না দেহ হইয়া কৃপণী ৷  
বুড়া ভর্ত্তা হবে, আর চারি সতিনী ॥ ৫৮ ॥

yadi naivedya nā deha haiyā kṛpaṇī  
if  the offering  not  you give  being  miserly  
buḍā bhartā habe, āra cāri satinī  
old  husband  will be,  and  four  co-wives

(58) “If you are stingy and will not give Me your offering, you will get an old husband — and four co-wives!”

ইহা শুনি’ তা-সবার মনে হৈল ভয় ৷  
কোন কিছু জানে, কিবা দেবাবিষ্ট হয় ॥ ৫৯ ॥

ihā śuni’ tā-sabāra mane haila bhaya  
this  hearing  of them all  in the minds  arose  fear  
kona kichu jāne, kibā devāviṣṭa haya  
something  some  He knows,  or  by a god possessed  He is

(59) The girls grew frightened at these words: “Perhaps He knows something, or perhaps some god has entered Him.”

আনিয়া নৈবেদ্য তারা সম্মুখে ধরিল ৷  
খাইয়া নৈবেদ্য তারে ইষ্টবর দিল ॥ ৬০ ॥

āniyā naivedya tārā sammukhe dharila  
bringing  the offering  they  before Him  held  
khāiyā naivedya tāre iṣṭavara dila  
eating  the offering  to them  the desired boon  He gave

(60) So they brought their offerings and held them out before Him, and when He had eaten, He gave them the boon they longed for.

এইমত চাপল্য সব লোকেরে দেখায় ৷  
দুঃখ কারো মনে নহে, সবে সুখ পায় ॥ ৬১ ॥

ei mata cāpalya saba lokere dekhāya  
in this way  naughtiness  to all  people  He shows  
duḥkha kāro mane nahe, sabe sukha pāya  
sorrow  anyone’s  in the mind  is not,  all  happiness  get

(61) In this way He showed His mischief to everyone, yet no one was ever unhappy; it brought them all joy.

একদিন বল্লভাচার্য্য-কন্যা ‘লক্ষ্মী’ নাম ৷  
দেবতা পূজিতে আইল করি’ গঙ্গাস্নান ॥ ৬২ ॥

ekadina vallabhācārya-kanyā ‘lakṣmī’ nāma  
one day  Vallabhācārya’s daughter  ‘Lakṣmī’  by name  
devatā pūjite āila kari’ gaṅgā-snāna  
the gods  to worship  came  having taken  a bath in the Ganges

(62) One day Vallabhācārya’s daughter, Lakṣmī by name, bathed in the Ganges and came to worship the gods.[^16]

তাঁরে দেখি’ প্রভুর হৈল সাভিলাষ মন ৷  
লক্ষ্মী চিত্তে সুখ পাইল প্রভুর দর্শন ॥ ৬৩ ॥

tām̐re dekhi’ prabhura haila sābhilāṣa mana  
her  seeing  the Lord’s  became  longing  mind  
lakṣmī citte sukha pāila prabhura darśana  
Lakṣmī  in her heart  joy  felt  the Lord’s  at the sight

(63) When the Lord saw her, His heart was drawn to her; and at the sight of the Lord, Lakṣmī felt joy within.

সাহজিক প্রীতি দুঁহার করিল উদয় ৷  
বাল্যভাবে ছন্ন তনু হইল নিশ্চয় ॥ ৬৪ ॥

sāhajika prīti dum̐hāra karila udaya  
natural  love  of both  made  arising  
bālya-bhāve channa tanu haila niścaya  
in the childhood mood  hidden  the form  became  certainly

(64) A natural love awoke in them both, though the mood of childhood still veiled its true form.[^17]

দুঁহা দেখি’ দুঁহার চিত্তে হইল উল্লাস ৷  
দেবপূজা-ছলে কৈল দুঁহে পরকাশ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

dum̐hā dekhi’ dum̐hāra citte haila ullāsa  
each other  seeing  of both  in the hearts  arose  delight  
deva-pūjā-chale kaila dum̐he parakāśa  
of worshipping the gods  on the pretext  did  both  revealing

(65) Seeing each other, both rejoiced at heart, and on the pretext of worshipping the gods they let their feelings show.

প্রভু কহে, — ‘আমা’ পূজ, আমি — মহেশ্বর ৷  
আমারে পূজিলে পাবে অভীপ্সিত বর ॥ ৬৬ ॥

prabhu kahe, — ‘āmā’ pūja, āmi — maheśvara  
the Lord  says  —  Me  worship,  I  —  the Supreme Lord  
āmāre pūjile pābe abhīpsita vara  
Me  worshipping  you will get  the desired  boon

(66) The Lord said, “Worship Me, for I am Maheśvara, the Supreme Lord. If you worship Me, you will receive the boon you desire.”

লক্ষ্মী তাঁর অঙ্গে দিল স-পুষ্প চন্দন ৷  
মল্লিকার মালা দিয়া করিল বন্দন ॥ ৬৭ ॥

lakṣmī tām̐ra aṅge dila sa-puṣpa candana  
Lakṣmī  His  on the body  placed  with flowers  sandal paste  
mallikāra mālā diyā karila vandana  
of mallikā  a garland  giving  she made  obeisance

(67) Lakṣmī placed sandal paste and flowers on His body, offered Him a garland of mallikā jasmine, and bowed to Him.

প্রভু তাঁর পূজা পাঞা হাসিতে লাগিল ৷  
শ্লোক পড়ি’ তাঁর ভাব অঙ্গীকার কৈল ॥ ৬৮ ॥

prabhu tām̐ra pūjā pāñā hāsite lāgila  
the Lord  her  worship  receiving  to smile  began  
śloka paḍi’ tām̐ra bhāva aṅgīkāra kaila  
a verse  reciting  her  feeling  He accepted

(68) Receiving her worship, the Lord began to smile, and by reciting a verse He accepted the sentiment of her heart.

সঙ্কল্পো বিদিতঃ সাধ্ব্যো ভবতীনাং মদর্চ্চনম্ ৷  
ময়ানুমোদিতঃ সোঽসৌ সত্যো ভবিতুমর্হতি ॥ ৬৯ ॥

saṅkalpo viditaḥ sādhvyo  
the resolve  is known  O chaste ones  
bhavatīnāṁ mad-arcanam  
your  worship of Me  
mayānumoditaḥ so ’sau  
by Me  approved  that  very  
satyo bhavitum arhati  
true  to become  deserves

(69) “O chaste ones, I know your resolve to worship Me. I accept it, and it deserves to come true.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.22.25)[^18]

এইমত লীলা দুঁহে করি’ গেলা ঘরে ৷  
গম্ভীর চৈতন্য-লীলা কে বুঝিতে পারে ॥ ৭০ ॥

ei-mata līlā dum̐he kari’ gelā ghare  
in this way  pastime  the two  having played  went  home  
gambhīra caitanya-līlā ke bujhite pāre  
deep  Caitanya’s pastimes  who  to understand  is able

(70) After this pastime the two of them went home. Who can fathom the deep pastimes of Caitanya?

চৈতন্য চাপল্য দেখি’ প্রেমে সর্ব্ব জন ৷  
শচী-জগন্নাথে দেখি’ দেন ওলাহন ॥ ৭১ ॥

caitanya cāpalya dekhi’ preme sarva jana  
Caitanya’s  naughtiness  seeing  out of love  all  people  
śacī-jagannāthe dekhi’ dena olāhana  
Śacī and Jagannātha  meeting  give  complaints

(71) Seeing Caitanya’s mischief, everyone, out of love for Him, would come to Śacī and Jagannātha with complaints.

একদিন শচী-দেবী পুত্রেরে ভর্ৎসিয়া ৷  
ধরিবারে গেলা পুত্রে, গেলা পলাইয়া ॥ ৭২ ॥

ekadina śacī-devī putrere bhartsiyā  
one day  Śacī-devī  her son  having scolded  
dharibāre gelā putra, gelā palāiyā  
to catch  went  her son,  He went  running away

(72) One day Śacī-devī scolded her son and went to catch Him, but He ran away.

উচ্ছিষ্ট-গর্ত্তে ত্যক্ত-হাণ্ডীর উপর ৷  
বসিয়াছেন সুখে প্রভু দেব-বিশ্বম্ভর ॥ ৭৩ ॥

ucchiṣṭa-garte tyakta-hāṇḍīra upara  
in the refuse pit  of the discarded  pots  on top  
basiyāchena sukhe prabhu deva-viśvambhara  
is seated  happily  the Lord  divine  Viśvambhara

(73) Then the Lord Viśvambhara, upholder of the universe, sat down contentedly on the cast-off pots in the refuse pit.[^19]

শচী আসি’ কহে, — কেনে অশুচি ছুঁইলা ৷  
গঙ্গাস্নান কর যাই’ — অপবিত্র হইলা ॥ ৭৪ ॥

śacī āsi’ kahe, — kene aśuci chum̐ilā  
Śacī  coming  says  —  why  the impure  You touched  
gaṅgā-snāna kara yāi’ — apavitra hailā  
bath in the Ganges  take  going  —  unclean  You have become

(74) Śacī came and said, “Why have You touched these unclean things? Go and bathe in the Ganges — You have made Yourself impure.”

ইহা শুনি’ মাতাকে কহিল ব্রহ্মজ্ঞান ৷  
বিস্মিতা হইয়া মাতা করাইল স্নান ॥ ৭৫ ॥

ihā śuni’ mātāke kahila brahma-jñāna  
this  hearing  to His mother  He spoke  knowledge of Brahman  
vismitā haiyā mātā karāila snāna  
astonished  becoming  the mother  made Him  bathe

(75) In reply He spoke to His mother of *brahma-jñāna*, spiritual knowledge. She was astonished, but still she made Him bathe.[^20]

কভু পুত্রসঙ্গে শচী করিলা শয়ন ৷  
দেখে, দিব্যলোক আসি’ ভরিল ভবন ॥ ৭৬ ॥

kabhu putrasaṅge śacī karilā śayana  
once  with her son  Śacī  lay down  to rest  
dekhe, divyaloka āsi’ bharila bhavana  
she sees  divine beings  coming  filled  the house

(76) Once, as Śacī lay resting with her son, she saw that divine beings had come and filled the house.

শচী বলে, — যাহ, পুত্র, বোলাহ বাপেরে ৷  
মাতৃ-আজ্ঞা পাইয়া প্রভু চলিলা বাহিরে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

śacī bale, — yāha, putra, bolāha bāpere  
Śacī  says  —  go,  son,  call  Your father  
mātṛ-ājñā pāiyā prabhu calilā bāhire  
the mother’s  order  receiving  the Lord  went  outside

(77) Śacī said, “Go, my son, and call Your father.” At His mother’s bidding the Lord went outside.

চলিতে চরণে নূপুর বাজে ঝন্ঝন্ ৷  
শুনি’ চমকিত হৈল পিতামাতার মন ॥ ৭৮ ॥

calite caraṇe nūpura bāje jhanjhan  
as He walked  on the feet  anklets  ring  jhan-jhan  
śuni’ camakita haila pitāmātāra mana  
hearing  startled  became  of father and mother  the minds

(78) As He walked, anklets jingled on His feet, and His father and mother were startled to hear them.

মিশ্র কহে, — এই বড় অদ্ভুত কাহিনী ৷  
শিশুর শূন্যপদে কেনে নূপুরের ধ্বনি ॥ ৭৯ ॥

miśra kahe, — ei baḍa adbhuta kāhinī  
Miśra  says  —  this  very  wonderful  happening  
śiśura śūnya-pade kene nūpurera dhvani  
of the child  on bare feet  why  of anklets  the sound

(79) Miśra said, “This is a great wonder! Why do we hear anklets on the child’s bare feet?”

শচী কহে, — আর এক অদ্ভুত দেখিল ৷  
দিব্য দিব্য লোক আসি’ অঙ্গন ভরিল ॥ ৮০ ॥

śacī kahe, — āra eka adbhuta dekhila  
Śacī  says  —  another  one  wonder  I saw  
divya divya loka āsi’ aṅgana bharila  
divine  divine  beings  coming  the courtyard  filled

(80) Śacī said, “I saw another wonder too: radiant divine beings came and filled the courtyard.

কিবা কোলাহল করে, বুঝিতে না পারি ৷  
কাহাকে বা স্তুতি করে, অনুমান করি ॥ ৮১ ॥

kibā kolāhala kare, bujhite nā pāri  
what  clamour  they make,  to understand  not  I can  
kāhāke vā stuti kare, anumāna kari  
whom  or  praises  they offer,  a guess  I make

(81) They raised such a clamour that I could not make out what they said; whom they were praising I can only guess.”

মিশ্র বলে, — কিছু হউক, চিন্তা কিছু নাই ৷  
বিশ্বম্ভরের কুশল হউক, — এই মাত্র চাই ॥ ৮২ ॥

miśra bale, — kichu hauka, cintā kichu nāi  
Miśra  says  —  whatever  may be,  worry  any  there is not  
viśvambharera kuśala hauka, — ei mātra cāi  
Viśvambhara’s  welfare  may be  —  this  only  I want

(82) Miśra said, “Whatever it may be, there is nothing to worry about. All I want is for Viśvambhara to be well.”

একদিন মিশ্র পুত্রের চাপল্য দেখিয়া ৷  
ধর্ম্ম-শিক্ষা দিল বহু ভর্ৎসনা করিয়া ॥ ৮৩ ॥

ekadina miśra putrera cāpalya dekhiyā  
one day  Miśra  his son’s  naughtiness  seeing  
dharma-śikṣā dila bahu bhartsanā kariyā  
instruction in dharma  gave  much  scolding  doing

(83) One day, seeing his son’s mischief, Miśra scolded Him severely and taught Him what dharma requires.

রাত্রে স্বপ্ন দেখে, — এক আসি’ ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
মিশ্রেরে কহয়ে কিছু সরোষ বচন ॥ ৮৪ ॥

rātre svapna dekhe, — eka āsi’ brāhmaṇa  
at night  a dream  he sees  —  one  coming  brāhmaṇa  
miśrere kahaye kichu sa-roṣa vacana  
to Miśra  speaks  some  angry  words

(84) At night he dreamt that a brāhmaṇa came and spoke to him angrily:

মিশ্র, তুমি পুত্রের তত্ত্ব কিছুই না জান ৷  
ভর্ৎসন-তাড়ন কর, — পুত্র করি’ মান ॥ ৮৫ ॥

miśra, tumi putrera tattva kichui nā jāna  
Miśra,  you  of your son  the truth  at all  not  know  
bhartsana-tāḍana kara, — putra kari’ māna  
scolding and beating  you do  —  a son  considering  regarding

(85) “Miśra, you know nothing of who your son truly is. You scold and beat Him because you think of Him as merely your son.”

মিশ্র কহে, — দেব, সিদ্ধ, মুনি কেনে নয় ৷  
যে সে বড় হউক, এবে আমার তনয় ॥ ৮৬ ॥

miśra kahe, — deva, siddha, muni kene naya  
Miśra  says  —  a god,  a perfected one,  a sage  why  not  
ye se baḍa hauka, ebe āmāra tanaya  
whoever  great  may He be,  now  my  son

(86) Miśra said, “Be He a god, a perfected being or a sage — however great He may be, now He is my son.

পুত্রের লালন-শিক্ষা — পিতার স্বধর্ম্ম ৷  
আমি না শিখালে, কৈছে জানিবে ধর্ম্ম-মর্ম্ম ॥ ৮৭ ॥

putrera lālana-śikṣā — pitāra sva-dharma  
of a son  the upbringing and teaching  —  of a father  the own dharma  
āmi nā śikhāle kaiche jānibe dharma-marma  
I  not  if teach,  how  will He know  of dharma  the heart

(87) To raise and teach his son is a father’s own dharma. If I do not teach Him, how will He come to know the heart of dharma?”

বিপ্র কহে, — এই যদি দৈব-সিদ্ধ হয় ৷  
স্বতঃসিদ্ধজ্ঞান তবে শিক্ষা ব্যর্থ হয় ॥ ৮৮ ॥

vipra kahe, — ei yadi daiva-siddha haya  
the brāhmaṇa  says  —  He  if  divinely perfect  is  
svataḥ-siddha-jñāna tabe śikṣā vyartha haya  
self-perfect knowledge,  then  teaching  useless  is

(88) The brāhmaṇa said, “If He is divine and perfect by nature, His knowledge is complete in itself, and teaching Him is in vain.”[^21]

মিশ্র কহে, — পুত্র কেনে নহে নারায়ণ ৷  
তথাপিহ পিতার ধর্ম্ম — পুত্রকে শিক্ষণ ॥ ৮৯ ॥

miśra kahe, — putra kene nahe nārāyaṇa  
Miśra  says  —  my son  why  not be  Nārāyaṇa  
tathāpiha pitāra dharma — putrake śikṣaṇa  
even so  a father’s  dharma  —  his son’s  teaching

(89) Miśra said, “My son may well be Nārāyaṇa; even so, it is a father’s dharma to teach his son.”

এইমতে দুঁহে করেন ধর্ম্মের বিচার ৷  
শুদ্ধবাৎসল্য মিশ্রের, নাহি জানে আর ॥ ৯০ ॥

ei-mate dum̐he karena dharmera vicāra  
in this way  the two  do  of dharma  discussion  
śuddha-vātsalya miśrera, nāhi jāne āra  
pure parental love  Miśra’s,  not  he knows  anything else

(90) So the two of them discussed dharma. Miśra’s parental love was pure; he knew nothing else.[^22]

এত শুনি’ দ্বিজ গেলা হঞা আনন্দিত ৷  
মিশ্র জাগিয়া হইলা পরম বিস্মিত ॥ ৯১ ॥

eta śuni’ dvija gelā hañā ānandita  
so  hearing  the brāhmaṇa  went away  becoming  pleased  
miśra jāgiyā hailā parama vismita  
Miśra  waking  became  supremely  astonished

(91) At this the brāhmaṇa went away well pleased, and Miśra awoke in utter astonishment.

বন্ধু-বান্ধব-স্থানে স্বপ্ন কহিল ৷  
শুনিয়া সকল লোক বিস্মিত হইল ॥ ৯২ ॥

bandhu-bāndhava-sthāne svapna kahila  
of friends and kinsmen  in the presence  the dream  he told  
śuniyā sakala loka vismita haila  
hearing  all  the people  astonished  became

(92) He told the dream to his friends and kinsmen, and all who heard it marvelled.

এইমত শিশুলীলা করে গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
দিনে দিনে পিতা-মাতার বাড়িল আনন্দ ॥ ৯৩ ॥

ei mata śiśu-līlā kare gauracandra  
in this way  childhood pastimes  does  Gauracandra  
dine dine pitā-mātāra bāḍila ānanda  
day by day  of father and mother  grew  the joy

(93) Thus Gauracandra played His childhood pastimes, and day by day His parents’ joy grew.

কত দিনে মিশ্র পুত্রের হাতে খড়ি দিল ৷  
অল্প দিনে দ্বাদশ-ফলা অক্ষর শিখিল ॥ ৯৪ ॥

kata dine miśra putrera hāte khaḍi dila  
some  days later  Miśra  his son’s  in the hand  chalk  placed  
alpa dine dvādaśa-phalā akṣara śikhila  
in a few  days  with the twelve phalās  the letters  He learnt

(94) Some days later Miśra placed chalk in his son’s hand, and within a few days He had learnt all the letters with their twelve *phalās*.[^23]

বাল্যলীলা-সূত্র এই কহিল অনুক্রম ৷  
ইহা বিস্তারিয়াছেন দাস-বৃন্দাবন ॥ ৯৫ ॥

bālya-līlā-sūtra ei kahila anukrama  
of the childhood pastimes  outline  this  I have told  in order  
ihā vistāriyāchena dāsa-vṛndāvana  
this  has elaborated  dāsa  Vṛndāvana

(95) This, in order, is the outline of His childhood pastimes; Vṛndāvana dāsa has described them at length.[^24]

অতএব বাল্যলীলা সংক্ষেপে সূত্র কৈল ৷  
পুনরুক্তি-ভয়ে বিস্তারিয়া না কহিল ॥ ৯৬ ॥

ataeva bālya-līlā saṅkṣepe sūtra kaila  
therefore  the childhood pastimes  in brief  outline  I made  
punar-ukti-bhaye vistāriyā nā kahila  
of repetition  for fear  at length  not  I told

(96) Therefore I have outlined the childhood pastimes briefly and, for fear of repeating him, have not told them in detail.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৯৭ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(97) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “Though Gaura’s pastimes look human, they are not of this world (*aprākṛta*).” *Anubhāṣya*: *laukikīm api* — though they resemble the doings of people in this world; *īśa-ceṣṭayā valitāntarām* — “inwardly joined with the acts of the Lord”, with His supernatural endeavours. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the childhood pastimes look worldly, yet the acts of God are woven into them.

[^2]: Heading in the edition to 6–7: “The marks of the conch, disc, flag, thunderbolt and fish on His soles.” *Anubhāṣya*: *uttāna* means lying face upwards, on one’s back; another reading is *utthāna*, “rising”: the baby tried to stand on His feet and, not yet used to it, tumbled down like any child.

[^3]: Heading in the edition: “The thirty-two marks of a great personality”; the source is the *Sāmudrika*, verse 3. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the long are the nose, arms, jaw, eyes and knees; the fine — skin, hair, finger joints, teeth and body hair; the reddish — the corners of the eyes, the soles, palms, palate, lower and upper lip, and nails; the raised — chest, shoulders, nails, nose, waist and face; the short — neck, shanks and generative organ; the broad — waist, forehead and chest; the deep — navel, voice and character (*sattva*). One who bears these thirty-two marks is a *mahāpuruṣa*, a great personality.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the two families are His father’s and His mother’s.

[^5]: The *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Ādi 3): “At His birth the whole world grew well again, as when Nārāyaṇa once upheld the earth. Therefore His name is Śrī Viśvambhara.” The name Viśvambhara also occurs in the *Atharva-veda* (book 2, *anuvāka* 3, *prapāṭhaka* 3, mantra 16.5): “O Viśvambhara, uphold and protect me with all that sustains the world, *svāhā*.”

[^6]: Heading in the edition: “Supernatural acts displayed.” *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 3): the beautiful Lord crawled about on His knees, and little bells tinkled at His waist. One day a snake was gliding through the courtyard, and the Lord, at play like a child, caught hold of it. The snake coiled itself up, and He lay down on top of it; then the snake left Him and slipped away.

[^7]: Heading in the edition: “At the name of Hari His crying stops.” *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 3): the lotus-eyed Lord would cry until He heard the name of Hari. Everyone understood the sign, and as soon as He began to cry they would clap their hands and sing “Hari!” — and so, just as He wished, He made them all chant the holy name without cease.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: this episode is one of those that Vṛndāvana dāsa left out of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* and that the author, as he promised in Ādi 13.49, now supplies.

[^9]: Headings in the edition: “Nimāi’s philosophical reply” (v. 27), “All is a transformation of clay” (v. 28), “The difference between a substance and its transformations, or between the favourable and the unfavourable” (v. 31). *Anubhāṣya* on 27–33: to take things for enjoyment is an activity of inert (*acit*) nature, and there is no service of Hari in it. The followers of the impersonal (*nirviśeṣa*) doctrine mistakenly hold that what is favourable to Kṛṣṇa’s service is of one kind with what is unfavourable. That such a notion is a crude, unripe error of worldly thought — that this “earthen” impersonal reasoning is barren — Mahāprabhu showed through His mother’s words, by the simple example of clay and the pot.

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: a wandering brāhmaṇa came as a guest to the house of Śrī Jagannātha Miśra. Miśra gave him all he needed for cooking, and he prepared a meal; but as soon as he offered it in meditation to Gopāla, Nimāi came and began to eat it. The brāhmaṇa cooked again, and the same thing happened. The third time he cooked when everyone in the house was asleep, and again Nimāi came and began to eat. The brāhmaṇa cried out in despair, and then Nimāi said, “O brāhmaṇa, when I was Yaśodā’s darling in Vraja, this happened to you before. Now too, drawn by your devotion, I have shown Myself to you out of mercy.” Seeing his worshipful Lord, the brāhmaṇa, enraptured with great love, honoured the remnants of the prasāda, and the Lord forbade him to reveal this hidden pastime. *Anubhāṣya*: see the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 3.

[^11]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu, still very small and decked in gold ornaments, was playing outside the gate. Two thieves lifted Him onto their shoulders and carried Him off, promising Him sweets, but they meant to kill the child in the forest and take His ornaments. Mahāprabhu, however, spread His *māyā* and led them astray, and with Him on their shoulders they arrived at the gate of His own house. There the relatives who had been running about searching for Him saw them; the thieves set the child down and fled, and He was carried with great care into Śacī’s courtyard. *Anubhāṣya*: see the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 3.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: “On Ekādaśī He eats the offering to Viṣṇu from the house of Hiraṇya and Jagadīśa.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on Ekādaśī an offering for Viṣṇu was being prepared in the house of Jagadīśa and Hiraṇya Paṇḍita. Mahāprabhu wanted to taste it and sent His father there. Hiraṇya and Jagadīśa were amazed: “Today is Ekādaśī, and an offering for Viṣṇu is being cooked in our house — how could so small a child know of it? Surely there is a Vaiṣṇava power in Him.” And they sent the food to the child. Mahāprabhu had asked for it on the pretext of illness, saying that if He ate the offering to Viṣṇu His illness would pass. He fed it to the other children, ate some Himself, and so was “cured”. Their house lay about a *krośa* south-east of Miśra’s, and a child could not have known what was happening there. *Anubhāṣya*: see the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 4.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 3–4): every day, just for fun, He would steal food from the neighbours — in one house He drank the milk, in another He ate the rice, and where He found nothing He broke the pots; and if there was a baby in the house, He made it cry.

[^14]: Heading in the edition to 45–47: “He strikes His mother and, seeing her swoon, brings coconuts that were hard to find.” The *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Caitanya-maṅgala* of Locana dāsa (Ādi): a radiant woman, smiling, took Viśvambhara by the chin and said, “Bring Your mother two coconuts, and then Śacī, Your mother, will live.” Viśvambhara was delighted and at once brought a pair of coconuts.

[^15]: *Vara* means both “boon” and “bridegroom”; the girls worshipped the gods to win a good husband (cf. 54–55, 58).

[^16]: Heading in the edition: “Meeting with Lakṣmī-devī, Vallabha’s daughter.” *Anubhāṣya* on 62–68: Vallabhācārya was a royal paṇḍita living in Navadvīpa. *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (44): “Formerly he was the great Janaka, king of Mithilā; now he is Vallabhācārya, and he is held to be Bhīṣmaka as well.” Śrī Gaurahari’s first marriage was to his daughter, Lakṣmīpriyā-devī. *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (45): Śrī Jānakī and Rukmiṇī appeared in Caitanya’s pastimes as his daughter named Lakṣmī; and another verse there tells how Vallabhācārya’s daughter Lakṣmī, who had descended with the Lord, went with her friends to bathe in the heavenly river and there came before His eyes.

[^17]: Heading in the edition: “The eternal, natural love of the two, and their delight.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Lakṣmī is the Lord’s eternal consort, and the Lord is Lakṣmī’s eternal husband; their eternal love is therefore *sāhajika*, inborn, and here it appeared veiled by the mood of childhood.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: these are Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s words to the gopīs who had kept the vow to Kātyāyanī longing to attain Him; after returning their garments, He saw their desire. *Mad-arcanam* is worship meant to attain Me; *saṅkalpa*, the heart’s cherished wish, which out of shyness they had left unspoken; *anumodita*, “accepted”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “O chaste ones, I have understood the purpose of your worship, and it gives Me great joy; your wish deserves to be fulfilled.”

[^19]: Heading in the edition: “Nimāi sits on the discarded pots.” The *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Ādi 7): “The Lord sat Himself down, as if on a throne, on the pots thrown away after the offering to Viṣṇu. His mother came, saw Him and cried, ‘Oh dear, my child, You must not sit here!’ But the Lord said, ‘Everywhere I see one, without a second. There is no defilement at all in these pots. Pots in which food is cooked for Viṣṇu are never impure; their touch makes any other place pure.’”

[^20]: Heading in the edition: “The son instructs His mother in the knowledge of Brahman (*brahma-jñāna*).” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord said, “Mother, ‘defiled’ and ‘undefiled’ are only human notions; there is no truth in them. In these pots you cooked for Viṣṇu and offered the food to Him — how can they ever be defiled? And the soul is eternally pure — how can ‘defiled’ and ‘pure’ apply to it?” Hearing such teaching, His mother was astonished and made Him bathe.

[^21]: Heading in the edition to 88–89: “The brāhmaṇa’s words and Miśra’s reply.” *Anubhāṣya*: seeing that Miśra took Nimāi for an untaught child and wished to teach Him so as to awaken knowledge in Him, the brāhmaṇa said: “Your son is an eternally perfect divinity, yet you mistake His eternal, natural knowledge for a child’s folly and set about teaching Him. That is in vain, and it is not proper for you.” Here the *Anubhāṣya* also refers to Antya 4.174–176 and cites the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* (11.28.4; 11.19.45; 11.21.3): in the transcendent there is no distinction of “good” and “bad” — such distinctions belong to the duality of the mind; “pure” and “impure” are prescribed among things of one nature only for the sake of dharma, worldly dealings and the upkeep of life.

[^22]: Heading in the edition: “Miśra’s pure parental love for the Lord.” *Anubhāṣya*: *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* (Western Division, Fourth Wave): the abiding sentiment (*sthāyi-bhāva*) of parental affection, nourished by *vibhāva* and the other elements, is called *vatsala-rasa*. And the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* (10.8.45): Yaśodā looked upon as her own son Hari, whose glory is sung by the three Vedas, the Upaniṣads, Sāṅkhya, Yoga and the Sātvata scriptures.

[^23]: Heading in the edition: “Chalk is placed in Nimāi’s hand.” *Anubhāṣya*: the twelve *phalās* are the signs by which other sounds are joined to a Bengali letter: *repha*, *ṇa*, *na*, *ma*, *ya*, *ra*, *la*, *va*, *ṛ*, *ṝ*, *ḷ* and *ḹ*.

[^24]: Heading in the edition: “These chief events of the childhood pastimes are described in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*.”
