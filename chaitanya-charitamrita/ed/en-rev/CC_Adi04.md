“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 4  
**An Account of the Root Purpose of Caitanya’s Descent**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

শ্রীচৈতন্যপ্রসাদেন তদ্রূপস্য বিনির্ণয়ম্ ৷  
বালোঽপি কুরুতে শাস্ত্রং দৃষ্ট্বা ব্রজবিলাসিনঃ ॥ ১ ॥

śrī-caitanya-prasādena  
of Śrī Caitanya  by the grace  
tad-rūpasya vinirṇayam  
of His  form  the ascertainment  
bālo ’pi kurute śāstraṁ  
a child  even  makes  scripture  
dṛṣṭvā vraja-vilāsinaḥ  
having seen  of the Enjoyer in Vraja

(1) By the grace of Śrī Caitanya even a child, looking into the scriptures, can ascertain the true form of Him who sports in Vraja.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

চতুর্থ শ্লোকের অর্থ কৈল বিবরণ ৷  
পঞ্চম শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ৩ ॥

caturtha ślokera artha kaila vivaraṇa  
of the fourth  verse  of the meaning  I have made  the explanation  
pañcama ślokera artha śuna diyā mana  
of the fifth  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  the mind

(3) I have explained the meaning of the fourth verse. Now hear with attention the meaning of the fifth.

মূল-শ্লোকের অর্থ করিতে প্রকাশ ৷  
অর্থ লাগাইতে আগে কহিয়ে আভাস ॥ ৪ ॥

mūla-ślokera artha karite prakāśa  
of the root  verse  the meaning  in order to make  manifest  
artha lāgāite āge kahiye ābhāsa  
the meaning  in order to apply  first  I speak  an outline

(4) To bring out the meaning of the root verse, I shall first give an outline before applying it.

চতুর্থ শ্লোকের অর্থ এই কৈল সার ৷  
প্রেম-নাম প্রচারিতে এই অবতার ॥ ৫ ॥

caturtha ślokera artha ei kaila sāra  
of the fourth  verse  of the meaning  this  I have made  the essence  
prema-nāma pracārite ei avatāra  
love and the name  to spread  this  descent

(5) The essence of the fourth verse, as I have explained it, is this: the Lord descended to spread divine love and the holy name.

সত্য এই হেতু, কিন্তু এতো বহিরঙ্গ ৷  
আর এক হেতু, শুন, আছে অন্তরঙ্গ ॥ ৬ ॥

satya ei hetu, kintu eto bahiraṅga  
true  this  reason  but  this indeed  external  
āra eka hetu, śuna, āche antaraṅga  
another  one  reason  hear  there is  internal

(6) This reason is true, but it is external. There is another reason, an inner one — hear it.[^2]

পূর্ব্বে হেন পৃথিবীর ভার হরিবারে ৷  
কৃষ্ণ অবতীর্ণ হৈলা শাস্ত্রেতে প্রচারে ॥ ৭ ॥

pūrve hena pṛthivīra bhāra haribāre  
formerly  so  of the earth  the burden  to remove  
kṛṣṇa avatīrṇa hailā śāstrete pracāre  
Kṛṣṇa  descended  became  in the scriptures  they proclaim

(7) The scriptures proclaim that in the past Kṛṣṇa descended to remove the burden of the earth.[^3]

স্বয়ং ভগবানের কর্ম্ম নহে ভারহরণ ৷  
স্থিতিকর্ত্তা বিষ্ণু করেন জগৎ-পালন ॥ ৮ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavānera karma nahe bhāra-haraṇa  
of Bhagavān Himself  the work  is not  the removal of the burden  
sthiti-kartā viṣṇu karena jagat-pālana  
the maintainer  Viṣṇu  does  of the world  the protection

(8) But removing the burden is not the work of Bhagavān Himself. It is Viṣṇu, the maintainer, who protects the world.[^4]

কিন্তু কৃষ্ণের যেই হয় অবতার-কাল ৷  
ভারহরণ-কাল তাতে হইল মিশাল ॥ ৯ ॥

kintu kṛṣṇera yei haya avatāra-kāla  
but  of Kṛṣṇa  which  is  of descent  the time  
bhāra-haraṇa-kāla tāte haila miśāla  
of the removal of the burden  the time  with it  became  mixed

(9) But the time of Kṛṣṇa’s descent coincided with the time for removing that burden.

পূর্ণ ভগবান্ অবতরে যেই কালে ৷  
আর সব অবতার তাঁতে আসি’ মিলে ॥ ১০ ॥

pūrṇa bhagavān avatare yei kāle  
the complete  Bhagavān  descends  at which  time  
āra saba avatāra tām̐te āsi’ mile  
other  all  avatāras  in Him  coming  meet

(10) When the complete Bhagavān descends, all the other avatāras come and meet in Him.

নারায়ণ, চতুর্ব্যূহ, মৎস্যাদ্যবতার ৷  
যুগ-মন্বন্তরাবতার, যত আছে আর ॥ ১১ ॥

সবে আসি’ কৃষ্ণ-অঙ্গে হয় অবতীর্ণ ৷  
ঐছে অবতরে কৃষ্ণ ভগবান্ পূর্ণ ॥ ১২ ॥

nārāyaṇa, catur-vyūha, matsyādy-avatāra  
Nārāyaṇa,  the Catur-vyūha,  Matsya and the other  avatāras  
yuga-manvantarāvatāra, yata āche āra  
of the yugas  of the manvantaras  the avatāras  as many as  there are  others  
sabe āsi’ kṛṣṇa-aṅge haya avatīrṇa  
all  coming  in Kṛṣṇa’s  body  become  descended  
aiche avatare kṛṣṇa bhagavān pūrṇa  
thus  descends  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān  complete

(11–12) Nārāyaṇa, the Catur-vyūha, Matsya and the other avatāras, the avatāras of the yugas and the manvantaras, and as many others as there are — all come and descend within the body of Kṛṣṇa. So does Kṛṣṇa, the complete Bhagavān, descend.[^5]

অতএব বিষ্ণু তখন কৃষ্ণের শরীরে ৷  
বিষ্ণুদ্বারে কৃষ্ণ করে অসুর-সংহারে ॥ ১৩ ॥

ataeva viṣṇu takhana kṛṣṇera śarīre  
therefore  Viṣṇu  then  of Kṛṣṇa  in the body  
viṣṇu-dvāre kṛṣṇa kare asura-saṁhāre  
through Viṣṇu  Kṛṣṇa  makes  of the demons  the slaying

(13) Therefore Viṣṇu was then within the body of Kṛṣṇa, and through Viṣṇu Kṛṣṇa slew the demons.

আনুষঙ্গ-কর্ম্ম এই অসুর-মারণ ৷  
যে লাগি’ অবতার, কহি সে মূল কারণ ॥ ১৪ ॥

ānuṣaṅga-karma ei asura-māraṇa  
secondary  work  this  of the demons  the killing  
ye lāgi’ avatāra, kahi se mūla kāraṇa  
for which  the descent  I tell  that  root  cause

(14) The killing of the demons is a secondary work. Now I shall tell the root cause for which He descends.

প্রেমরস-নির্য্যাস করিতে আস্বাদন ৷  
রাগমার্গ-ভক্তি লোকে করিতে প্রচারণ ॥ ১৫ ॥

রসিক-শেখর কৃষ্ণ পরমকরুণ ৷  
এই দুই হেতু হৈতে ইচ্ছার উদ্গম ॥ ১৬ ॥

prema-rasa-niryāsa karite āsvādana  
of the rasa of love  the essence  in order to make  tasting  
rāga-mārga-bhakti loke karite pracāraṇa  
of the path of rāga  devotion  in the world  in order to make  spreading  
rasika-śekhara kṛṣṇa parama-karuṇa  
of the relishers of rasa  the crown  Kṛṣṇa  supremely merciful  
ei dui hetu haite icchāra udgama  
these  two  reasons  from  of the desire  the rising

(15–16) To taste the very essence of the rasa of love, and to spread in the world devotion on the path of rāga (*rāga-mārga*) — from these two reasons the desire arose in Kṛṣṇa, the crown of all who relish rasa, who is supremely merciful.[^6]

ঐশ্বর্য্য-জ্ঞানেতে সব জগৎ মিশ্রিত ৷  
ঐশ্বর্য্য-শিথিল-প্রেমে নাহি মোর প্রীত ॥ ১৭ ॥

aiśvarya-jñānete saba jagat miśrita  
of majesty  with the awareness  the whole  world  is mixed  
aiśvarya-śithila-preme nāhi mora prīta  
by majesty  slackened  in love  is not  My  pleasure

(17) The whole world is permeated by the awareness of My majesty, and love slackened by awe gives Me no joy.[^7]

আমারে ঈশ্বর মানে, আপনাকে হীন ৷  
তার প্রেমে বশ আমি না হই অধীন ॥ ১৮ ॥

āmāre īśvara māne, āpanāke hīna  
Me  as the Lord  considers  himself  as low  
tāra preme vaśa āmi nā hai adhīna  
his  by love  subdued  I  not  become  dependent

(18) One who regards Me as the Lord and himself as lowly — by his love I am not subdued; I do not become dependent on him.

আমাকে ত’ যে যে ভক্ত ভজে যেই ভাবে ৷  
তারে সে সে ভাবে ভজি, — এ মোর স্বভাবে ॥ ১৯ ॥

āmāke ta’ ye ye bhakta bhaje yei bhāve  
to Me  indeed  whichever  whichever  devotee  worships  in which  mood  
tāre se se bhāve bhaji, — e mora svabhāve  
him  in that  in that  mood  I worship  this  My  nature

(19) In whatever mood a devotee worships Me, in that very mood I reciprocate with him. Such is My nature.

যে যথা মাং প্রপদ্যন্তে তাংস্তথৈব ভজাম্যহম্ ৷  
মম বর্ত্মানুবর্ত্তন্তে মনুষ্যাঃ পার্থ সর্ব্বশঃ ॥ ২০ ॥

ye yathā māṁ prapadyante  
who  as  to Me  surrender  
tāṁs tathaiva bhajāmy aham  
them  in that same way  I reward  I  
mama vartmānuvartante  
My  path  follow  
manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ  
men  O Pārtha  in every way

(20) “As people surrender to Me, so do I reward them. O Pārtha, all men follow My path in every way.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 4.11)[^8]

মোর পুত্র, মোর সখা, মোর প্রাণপতি ৷  
এইভাবে যেই মোরে করে শুদ্ধভক্তি ॥ ২১ ॥

আপনাকে বড় মানে, আমারে সম-হীন ৷  
সেই ভাবে হই আমি তাহার অধীন ॥ ২২ ॥

mora putra, mora sakhā, mora prāṇa-pati  
my  son  my  friend  my  of life  lord  
ei-bhāve yei more kare śuddha-bhakti  
in this mood  whoever  to Me  makes  pure devotion  
āpanāke baḍa māne, āmāre sama-hīna  
himself  great  considers  Me  equal or lower  
sei bhāve hai āmi tāhāra adhīna  
in that  mood  become  I  his  dependent

(21–22) Whoever renders Me pure devotion in the mood “He is my son, my friend, the lord of my life”, considering himself great and Me equal or lower — in that mood I become subject to him.[^9]

ময়ি ভক্তির্হি ভূতানামমৃতত্বায় কল্পতে ৷  
দিষ্ট্যা যদাসীন্মৎস্নেহো ভবতীনাং মদাপনঃ ॥ ২৩ ॥

mayi bhaktir hi bhūtānām  
to Me  devotion  indeed  of beings  
amṛtatvāya kalpate  
for immortality  is fit  
diṣṭyā yad āsīn mat-sneho  
by good fortune  which  arose  for Me  the love  
bhavatīnāṁ mad-āpanaḥ  
of you  bringing Me

(23) “Devotion to Me brings living beings to immortality. And how fortunate it is that you have love for Me — for it brings you to Me!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.82.44)[^10]

মাতা মোরে পুত্ত্রভাবে করেন বন্ধন ৷  
অতিহীন-জ্ঞানে করে লালন পালন ॥ ২৪ ॥

mātā more putra-bhāve karena bandhana  
mother  Me  in the mood of a son  makes  binding  
atihīna-jñāne kare lālana pālana  
utterly helpless  thinking  does  fondling  caring

(24) My mother binds Me in the mood of a son; thinking Me utterly helpless, she fondles and cares for Me.

সখা শুদ্ধসখ্যে করে স্কন্ধে আরোহণ ৷  
তুমি কোন্ বড় লোক, — তুমি আমি সম ॥ ২৫ ॥

sakhā śuddha-sakhye kare skandhe ārohaṇa  
a friend  in pure friendship  makes  on the shoulders  climbing  
tumi kon baḍa loka, — tumi āmi sama  
You  what  great  person  You  I  equal

(25) My friend, in pure friendship, climbs on My shoulders and says, “What great man are You? You and I are equal!”

প্রিয়া যদি মান করি’ করয়ে ভর্ৎসন ৷  
বেদ স্তুতি হৈতে হরে সেই মোর মন ॥ ২৬ ॥

priyā yadi māna kari’ karaye bhartsana  
the beloved  if  pique  showing  makes  rebuke  
veda stuti haite hare sei mora mana  
of the Vedas  the hymns  than  steals  that  My  mind

(26) When My beloved, in her pique (*māna*), rebukes Me, that steals My heart more than the hymns of the Vedas.[^11]

এই শুদ্ধাভক্তি লঞা করিমু অবতার ৷  
করিব বিবিধবিধ অদ্ভুত বিহার ॥ ২৭ ॥

বৈকুণ্ঠাদ্যে নাহি যে যে লীলার প্রচার ৷  
সে সে লীলা করিব, যাতে মোর চমৎকার ॥ ২৮ ॥

ei śuddhā-bhakti lañā karimu avatāra  
this  pure devotion  taking  I shall make  descent  
kariba vividha-vidha adbhuta vihāra  
I shall make  of many kinds  wondrous  pastimes  
vaikuṇṭhādye nāhi ye ye līlāra pracāra  
in Vaikuṇṭha and elsewhere  there is not  which  which  of pastimes  the display  
se se līlā kariba, yāte mora camatkāra  
those  those  pastimes  I shall perform  in which  My  wonder

(27–28) Taking this pure devotion, I shall descend and perform many kinds of wondrous pastimes. I shall perform pastimes such as are not shown even in Vaikuṇṭha — pastimes that astonish even Me.[^12]

মো-বিষয়ে গোপীগণের উপপতি-ভাবে ৷  
যোগমায়া করিবেক আপনপ্রভাবে ॥ ২৯ ॥

mo-viṣaye gopī-gaṇera upapati-bhāve  
toward Me  of the gopīs  in the mood of a paramour (*upapati*)  
yoga-māyā karibeka āpana-prabhāve  
Yogamāyā  will make  by her own  power

(29) By her own power Yogamāyā will awaken in the gopīs a love for Me as a lover who is not their husband (*upapati*).[^13]

আমিহ না জানি তাহা, না জানে গোপীগণ ৷  
দুঁহার রূপগুণে দুঁহার নিত্য হরে মন ॥ ৩০ ॥

āmiha nā jāni tāhā, nā jāne gopī-gaṇa  
I too  not  know  that  not  know  the gopīs  
dum̐hāra rūpa-guṇe dum̐hāra nitya hare mana  
of the two  by beauty and qualities  of the two  always  steals  the mind

(30) Neither I nor the gopīs will know of it, for each of us will ever steal the other’s heart with beauty and virtues.[^14]

ধর্ম্ম ছাড়ি’ রাগে দুঁহে করয়ে মিলন ৷  
কভু মিলে, কভু না মিলে, — দৈবের ঘটন ॥ ৩১ ॥

dharma chāḍi’ rāge dum̐he karaye milana  
dharma  leaving  in rāga  the two  make  meeting  
kabhu mile, kabhu nā mile, — daivera ghaṭana  
sometimes  meet  sometimes  not  meet  of destiny  the arrangement

(31) Leaving dharma behind, the two will meet in love (*rāga*) — sometimes meeting, sometimes not, as destiny arranges.

এই সব রসনির্য্যাস করিব আস্বাদ ৷  
এই দ্বারে করিব সব ভক্তেরে প্রসাদ ॥ ৩২ ॥

ei saba rasa-niryāsa kariba āsvāda  
these  all  of rasa  essences  I shall make  tasting  
ei dvāre kariba saba bhaktere prasāda  
by this  means  I shall make  to all  devotees  grace

(32) I shall taste the essence of all these rasas, and by this means I shall show grace to all My devotees.

ব্রজের নির্ম্মল রাগ শুনি’ ভক্তগণ ৷  
রাগমার্গে ভজে যেন ছাড়ি’ ধর্ম্ম-কর্ম্ম ॥ ৩৩ ॥

vrajera nirmala rāga śuni’ bhakta-gaṇa  
of Vraja  the pure  love  hearing  the devotees  
rāga-mārge bhaje yena chāḍi’ dharma-karma  
on the path of rāga  worship  so that  leaving  religious duties

(33) Hearing of the pure love (*rāga*) of Vraja, the devotees will worship Me on the path of rāga, leaving aside their religious duties (*dharma-karma*).[^15]

অনুগ্রহায় ভক্তানাং মানুষং দেহমাশ্রিতঃ ৷  
ভজতে তাদৃশীঃ ক্রীড়া যাঃ শ্রুত্বা তৎপরো ভবেৎ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

anugrahāya bhaktānāṁ  
for the favour  of the devotees  
mānuṣaṁ deham āśritaḥ  
a human  body  having taken  
bhajate tādṛśīḥ krīḍā  
performs  such  pastimes  
yāḥ śrutvā tat-paro bhavet  
which  having heard  devoted to Him  one must become

(34) “To show favour to His devotees, Kṛṣṇa takes a body like a human one and performs such pastimes that whoever hears of them must become wholly devoted to Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.36)[^16]

‘ভবেৎ’ ক্রিয়া বিধিলিঙ্, সেই ইহা কয় ৷  
কর্ত্তব্য অবশ্য এই, অন্যথা প্রত্যবায় ॥ ৩৫ ॥

‘bhavet’ kriyā vidhiliṅ, sei ihā kaya  
‘bhavet’  the verb  injunctive (*vidhi-liṅ*)  that  here  says  
kartavya avaśya ei, anyathā pratyavāya  
a duty  certainly  this  otherwise  a fault

(35) The verb *bhavet* is in the injunctive mood (*vidhi-liṅ*), and so it says here: this is certainly a duty, and to neglect it is a fault.[^17]

এই বাঞ্ছা যৈছে কৃষ্ণপ্রাকট্য-কারণ ৷  
অসুরসংহার — আনুষঙ্গ প্রয়োজন ॥ ৩৬ ॥

এই মত চৈতন্য-কৃষ্ণ পূর্ণ ভগবান্ ৷  
যুগধর্ম্মপ্রবর্ত্তন নহে তাঁর কাম ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ei vāñchā yaiche kṛṣṇa-prākaṭya-kāraṇa  
this  desire  as  of Kṛṣṇa’s  appearance  the cause  
asura-saṁhāra — ānuṣaṅga prayojana  
of the demons  the slaying  a secondary  purpose  
ei mata caitanya-kṛṣṇa pūrṇa bhagavān  
in the same way  Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the complete  Bhagavān  
yuga-dharma-pravartana nahe tām̐ra kāma  
of the yuga-dharma  the inauguration  is not  His  desire

(36–37) As this desire was the cause of Kṛṣṇa’s appearance and the slaying of the demons only a secondary purpose, so too Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa is the complete Bhagavān, and to inaugurate the religion of the age (*yuga-dharma*) is not His own desire.[^18]

কোন কারণে যবে হৈল অবতারে মন ৷  
যুগধর্ম্ম-কাল হৈল সে কালে মিলন ॥ ৩৮ ॥

kona kāraṇe yabe haila avatāre mana  
for some  reason  when  arose  to descend  the mind  
yuga-dharma-kāla haila se kāle milana  
of the yuga-dharma  the time  became  with that  time  coinciding

(38) When for some reason He resolved to descend, the time of the *yuga-dharma* coincided with it.[^19]

দুই হেতু অবতরি’ লঞা ভক্তগণ ৷  
আপনে আস্বাদে প্রেম-নাম-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

dui hetu avatari’ lañā bhakta-gaṇa  
for two  reasons  descending  taking  the devotees  
āpane āsvāde prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana  
Himself  tastes  love  of the name  the congregational chanting

(39) Descending for these two reasons, together with His devotees He Himself tastes prema and the congregational chanting of the name.

সেই দ্বারে আচণ্ডালে কীর্ত্তন সঞ্চারে ৷  
নাম-প্রেমমালা গাঁথি’ পরাইল সংসারে ॥ ৪০ ॥

sei dvāre ācaṇḍāle kīrtana sañcāre  
by that  means  even to the outcastes  kīrtana  He spreads  
nāma-prema-mālā gām̐thi’ parāila saṁsāre  
of the name  of love  a garland  weaving  He put on  the world

(40) By this means He spread kīrtana even among the outcastes; weaving a garland of the holy name and divine love, He placed it around the whole world.

এইমত ভক্তভাব করি’ অঙ্গীকার ৷  
আপনি আচরি’ ভক্তি করিল প্রচার ॥ ৪১ ॥

ei-mata bhakta-bhāva kari’ aṅgīkāra  
in this way  of a devotee  the mood  making  acceptance  
āpani ācari’ bhakti karila pracāra  
Himself  practising  devotion  made  preaching

(41) Thus, accepting the mood of a devotee, He preached devotion by practising it Himself.[^20]

দাস্য, সখ্য, বাৎসল্য, আর যে শৃঙ্গার ৷  
চারি প্রেম, চতুর্বিধ ভক্তই আধার ॥ ৪২ ॥

dāsya, sakhya, vātsalya, āra ye śṛṅgāra  
servitude,  friendship,  parental love,  and  which  the love of the beloved  
cāri prema, catur-vidha bhaktai ādhāra  
four  loves  of four kinds  devotees  the vessel

(42) Servitude, friendship, parental love and the love of the beloved (*śṛṅgāra*) — these are the four kinds of prema, and the four kinds of devotees are their vessels.[^21]

নিজ নিজ ভাব সবে শ্রেষ্ঠ করি’ মানে ৷  
নিজভাবে করে কৃষ্ণসুখ-আস্বাদনে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

nija nija bhāva sabe śreṣṭha kari’ māne  
own  own  mood  all  best  making  consider  
nija-bhāve kare kṛṣṇa-sukha-āsvādane  
in their own mood  make  with Kṛṣṇa  of happiness  the tasting

(43) Each considers his own mood the best, and in that mood he tastes happiness with Kṛṣṇa.

তটস্থ হইয়া হৃদি বিচার যদি করি ৷  
সব রস হৈতে শৃঙ্গারে অধিক মাধুরী ॥ ৪৪ ॥

taṭastha haiyā hṛdi vicāra yadi kari  
impartial  becoming  in the heart  consideration  if  I make  
saba rasa haite śṛṅgāre adhika mādhurī  
all  rasas  than  in śṛṅgāra  greater  sweetness

(44) But if one considers it in one’s heart without partiality, the sweetness of *śṛṅgāra*, the love of the beloved, surpasses that of all the other rasas.[^22]

যথোত্তরমসৌ স্বাদুবিশেষোল্লাসময়্যপি ৷  
রতির্বাসনয়া স্বাদ্বী ভাসতে কাপি কস্যচিৎ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

yathottaram asau svādu-  
in ascending order  this  
viśeṣollāsamayy api  
of special taste  full of delight  although  
ratir vāsanayā svādvī  
love  by inclination  sweet  
bhāsate kāpi kasyacit  
appears  some one  to someone

(45) “Although love (*rati*), rising from one kind to the next, grows ever sweeter and more joyful, by the inclination of the heart a particular one appears sweetest to a particular devotee.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.5.38)[^23]

অতএব মধুর রস কহি তার নাম ৷  
স্বকীয়া-পরকীয়া-রূপে দ্বিবিধ সংস্থান ॥ ৪৬ ॥

ataeva madhura rasa kahi tāra nāma  
therefore  madhura  rasa  I call  its  name  
svakīyā-parakīyā-rūpe dvi-vidha saṁsthāna  
svakīyā  parakīyā  in the form  twofold  existence

(46) Therefore I call it *madhura-rasa*, the sweet rasa. It exists in two forms: *svakīyā*, conjugal love, and *parakīyā*, love that is not conjugal.[^24]

পরকীয়া-ভাবে অতি রসের উল্লাস ৷  
ব্রজ বিনা ইহার অন্যত্র নাহি বাস ॥ ৪৭ ॥

parakīyā-bhāve ati rasera ullāsa  
in the parakīyā mood  greatest  of rasa  the delight  
vraja vinā ihāra anyatra nāhi vāsa  
Vraja  except  of it  elsewhere  is not  the abode

(47) In the mood of *parakīyā* rasa rejoices most of all, and nowhere but in Vraja does it dwell.[^25]

ব্রজবধূগণের এই ভাব নিরবধি ৷  
তার মধ্যে শ্রীরাধার ভাবের অবধি ॥ ৪৮ ॥

vraja-vadhū-gaṇera ei bhāva niravadhi  
of Vraja  of the young women  this  mood  boundless  
tāra madhye śrī-rādhāra bhāvera avadhi  
of them  among  of Śrī Rādhā  of the mood  the summit

(48) In the young women of Vraja this mood is boundless, and among them it reaches its summit in Śrī Rādhā.[^26]

প্রৌঢ়-নির্ম্মলভাব প্রেম সর্ব্বোত্তম ৷  
কৃষ্ণের মাধুর্য্যরস-আস্বাদ-কারণ ॥ ৪৯ ॥

prauḍha-nirmala-bhāva prema sarvottama  
mature  pure  of mood  love  the highest of all  
kṛṣṇera mādhurya-rasa-āsvāda-kāraṇa  
of Kṛṣṇa  of sweetness  of the rasa  of the tasting  the cause

(49) Her love, mature and pure in feeling, is the highest of all; it is through this love that the sweetness of Kṛṣṇa is tasted in its fullness.[^27]

অতএব সেই ভাব অঙ্গীকার করি’ ৷  
সাধিলেন নিজ বাঞ্ছা গৌরাঙ্গ-শ্রীহরি ॥ ৫০ ॥

ataeva sei bhāva aṅgīkāra kari’  
therefore  that  mood  acceptance  making  
sādhilena nija vāñchā gaurāṅga-śrī-hari  
fulfilled  His own  desire  Gaurāṅga  Śrī Hari

(50) Therefore Śrī Hari, appearing as Gaurāṅga, accepted that mood and fulfilled His own desire.[^28]

সুরেশানাং দুর্গং গতিরতিশয়েনোপনিষদাং  
মুনীনাং সর্ব্বস্বং প্রণতপটলীনাং মধুরিমা ৷  
বিনির্য্যাসঃ প্রেম্ণো নিখিলপশুপালাম্বুজদৃশাং  
স চৈতন্যঃ কিং মে পুনরপি দৃশোর্যাস্যতি পদম্ ॥ ৫১ ॥

sureśānāṁ durgaṁ gatir atiśayenopaniṣadāṁ  
of the lords of the gods  the inaccessible refuge  the goal  supremely  of the Upaniṣads  
munīnāṁ sarva-svaṁ praṇata-paṭalīnāṁ madhurimā  
of the sages  the all-in-all  of the bowing hosts  the sweetness  
viniryāsaḥ premṇo nikhila-paśu-pālāmbuja-dṛśāṁ  
the essence  of love  of all  the cowherd girls  lotus-eyed  
sa caitanyaḥ kiṁ me punar api dṛśor yāsyati padam  
that  Caitanya  will  of my  again  even  of the eyes  come  to the path

(51) “He is the refuge hard to reach even for the lords of the gods, the supreme goal of the Upaniṣads, the all-in-all of the sages, the sweetness of the hosts who bow to Him, and the very essence of the love of the lotus-eyed cowherd girls of Vraja. Will that Caitanya ever again come before my eyes?” (Stava-mālā, the first Caitanyāṣṭaka, 2)[^29]

অপারং কস্যাপি প্রণয়িজনবৃন্দস্য কুতুকী  
রসস্তোমং হৃত্বা মধুরমুপভোক্তুং কমপি যঃ ৷  
রুচং স্বামাবব্রে দ্যুতিমিহ তদীয়াং প্রকটয়ন্  
স দেবশ্চৈতন্যাকৃতিরতিতরাং নঃ কৃপয়তু ॥ ৫২ ॥

apāraṁ kasyāpi praṇayi-jana-vṛndasya kutukī  
boundless  of a certain  of loving  persons  of the host  eager for delight  
rasa-stomaṁ hṛtvā madhuram upabhoktuṁ kam api yaḥ  
of rasa  the mass  having stolen  sweet  to relish  indescribable  who  
rucaṁ svām āvavre dyutim iha tadīyāṁ prakaṭayan  
lustre  His own  covered  radiance  here  Hers  revealing  
sa devaś caitanyākṛtir atitarāṁ naḥ kṛpayatu  
that  Lord  in the form of Caitanya  exceedingly  to us  may He be merciful

(52) “The Lord, eager for delight, stole from one among the host of His beloveds (Śrī Rādhā) Her boundless, indescribably sweet wealth of rasa, that He might relish it Himself. For this He covered His own lustre and revealed here Her radiance. May that Lord, in the form of Caitanya, be boundlessly merciful to us!” (Stava-mālā, the second Caitanyāṣṭaka, 3)[^30]

ভাবগ্রহণের হেতু করিল ধর্ম্ম স্থাপন ৷  
তার মুখ্য হেতু কহি, শুন সর্ব্বজন ॥ ৫৩ ॥

bhāva-grahaṇera hetu karila dharma sthāpana  
of the mood  of accepting  for the sake  made  of dharma  the establishment  
tāra mukhya hetu kahi, śuna sarva-jana  
of it  the chief  reason  I tell  hear  all people

(53) It was to accept that mood that He established dharma. Now I shall tell its chief reason — listen, everyone.[^31]

মূল হেতু আগে শ্লোকের কৈল আভাস ৷  
এবে কহি সেই শ্লোকের অর্থ প্রকাশ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

mūla hetu āge ślokera kaila ābhāsa  
the root  reason  before  of the verse  I made  an outline  
ebe kahi sei ślokera artha prakāśa  
now  I tell  that  of the verse  of the meaning  the revelation

(54) Up to now I have given an outline of the verse to point to the root reason. Now I shall reveal the meaning of that verse.

রাধা কৃষ্ণপ্রণয়বিকৃতির্হ্লাদিনী শক্তিরস্মা-  
দেকাত্মানাবপি ভুবি পুরা দেহভেদং গতৌ তৌ ৷  
চৈতন্যাখ্যং প্রকটমধুনা তদ্দ্বয়ং চৈক্যমাপ্তং  
রাধাভাবদ্যুতিসুবলিতং নৌমি কৃষ্ণস্বরূপম্ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

rādhā kṛṣṇa-praṇaya-vikṛtir hlādinī śaktir asmād  
Rādhā  of Kṛṣṇa’s  love  the transformation  the pleasure  potency  therefore  
ekātmānāv api bhuvi purā deha-bhedaṁ gatau tau  
one in self  although  in the world  of old  bodily  distinction  attained  the Two  
caitanyākhyaṁ prakaṭam adhunā tad-dvayaṁ caikyam āptaṁ  
Caitanya named  manifest  now  that  pair  and oneness  attained  
rādhā-bhāva-dyuti-suvalitaṁ naumi kṛṣṇa-svarūpam  
with Rādhā’s  sentiment  and lustre  adorned  I bow to  Kṛṣṇa  Himself

(55) “Śrī Rādhā is the transformation of Kṛṣṇa’s love, His pleasure potency, *hlādinī-śakti*. Therefore, though Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in Self, from time without beginning They have appeared in the world in two different forms. Now these Two have become one and are manifest as Śrī Caitanya. I bow to Him, Kṛṣṇa Himself, adorned with the sentiment and the lustre of Śrī Rādhā.” (from the notes, *kaḍacā*, of Svarūpa Dāmodara)[^32]

রাধাকৃষ্ণ এক আত্মা, দুই দেহ ধরি’ ৷  
অন্যোন্যে বিলসে রস আস্বাদন করি’ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

rādhā-kṛṣṇa eka ātmā, dui deha dhari’  
Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa  one  self  two  bodies  assuming  
anyonye vilase rasa āsvādana kari’  
with each other  sport  rasa  tasting  making

(56) Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one Self; assuming two bodies, They sport with each other, tasting rasa.[^33]

সেই দুই এক এবে চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ৷  
ভাব আস্বাদিতে দোঁহে হৈলা একঠাঞি ॥ ৫৭ ॥

sei dui eka ebe caitanya-gosāñi  
those  two  one  now  Caitanya  Gosāñi  
bhāva āsvādite dom̐he hailā eka-ṭhāñi  
the mood  to taste  the Two  became  in one place

(57) Now those Two have become one — Caitanya Gosāñi; to taste that mood (*bhāva*) the Two have come together in one.[^34]

ইথি লাগি’ আগে করি তার বিবরণ ৷  
যাহা হৈতে হয় গৌরের মহিমা-কথন ॥ ৫৮ ॥

ithi lāgi’ āge kari tāra vivaraṇa  
for this  reason  first  I make  of that  the description  
yāhā haite haya gaurera mahimā-kathana  
from which  comes  of Gaura  of the glory  the telling

(58) For this reason I shall first describe that truth, for from it comes the telling of Gaura’s glory.[^35]

রাধিকা হয়েন কৃষ্ণের প্রণয়-বিকার ৷  
স্বরূপশক্তি — ‘হ্লাদিনী’ নাম যাঁহার ॥ ৫৯ ॥

rādhikā hayena kṛṣṇera praṇaya-vikāra  
Rādhikā  is  of Kṛṣṇa  of love  the transformation  
svarūpa-śakti — ‘hlādinī’ nāma yām̐hāra  
the potency of His own nature  ‘hlādinī’  name  whose

(59) Rādhikā is the transformation of Kṛṣṇa’s love; She is the potency of His own nature (*svarūpa-śakti*) whose name is Hlādinī.[^36]

হ্লাদিনী করায় কৃষ্ণে আনন্দাস্বাদন ৷  
হ্লাদিনীর দ্বারা করে ভক্তের পোষণ ॥ ৬০ ॥

hlādinī karāya kṛṣṇe ānandāsvādana  
hlādinī  makes  Kṛṣṇa  bliss  taste  
hlādinīra dvārā kare bhaktera poṣaṇa  
of hlādinī  by means  makes  of the devotees  the nourishment

(60) Hlādinī makes Kṛṣṇa taste bliss, and through Hlādinī He nourishes His devotees.[^37]

সচ্চিদানন্দ, পূর্ণ, কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ ৷  
একই চিচ্ছক্তি তাঁর ধরে তিন রূপ ॥ ৬১ ॥

sac-cid-ānanda, pūrṇa, kṛṣṇera svarūpa  
eternity-knowledge-bliss  complete  of Kṛṣṇa  the nature  
ekai cic-chakti tām̐ra dhare tina rūpa  
one only  spiritual potency  His  takes  three  forms

(61) Kṛṣṇa’s nature is eternity, knowledge and bliss; He is complete. And His one spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*) takes three forms:[^38]

আনন্দাংশে হ্লাদিনী, সদংশে সন্ধিনী ৷  
চিদংশে সম্বিৎ — যারে জ্ঞান করি’ মানি ॥ ৬২ ॥

ānandāṁśe hlādinī, sad-aṁśe sandhinī  
in the aspect of bliss  hlādinī  in the aspect of being  sandhinī  
cid-aṁśe samvit — yāre jñāna kari’ māni  
in the aspect of consciousness  samvit  which  as knowledge  making  we regard

(62) In the aspect of bliss She is Hlādinī, in the aspect of being Sandhinī, and in the aspect of consciousness Samvit, which we know as knowledge.[^39]

হ্লাদিনী সন্ধিনী সম্বিৎ ত্বয়্যেকা সর্ব্বসংস্থিতৌ ৷  
হ্লাদতাপকরী মিশ্রা ত্বয়ি নো গুণবর্জ্জিতে ॥ ৬৩ ॥

hlādinī sandhinī samvit  
hlādinī  sandhinī  samvit  
tvayy ekā sarva-saṁsthitau  
in You  one  of all  the basis  
hlāda-tāpa-karī miśrā  
of joy  of pain  causing  mixed  
tvayi no guṇa-varjite  
in You  not  of the guṇas  devoid

(63) “O Lord, You are the basis of all, and in You Hlādinī, Sandhinī and Samvit are one potency. But that which causes joy, pain and a mixture of the two is not in You, for You are beyond the guṇas.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 1.12.69)[^40]

সন্ধিনীর সার অংশ — ‘শুদ্ধসত্ত্ব’ নাম ৷  
ভগবানের সত্তা হয় যাহাতে বিশ্রাম ॥ ৬৪ ॥

sandhinīra sāra aṁśa — ‘śuddha-sattva’ nāma  
of sandhinī  the essential  part  ‘pure being’  name  
bhagavānera sattā haya yāhāte viśrāma  
of Bhagavān  the existence  has  in which  rest

(64) The essential part of Sandhinī is called *śuddha-sattva*, pure being; in it the existence of Bhagavān rests.[^41]

মাতা, পিতা, স্থান, গৃহ, শয়্যাসন আর ৷  
এ সব কৃষ্ণের শুদ্ধসত্ত্বের বিকার ॥ ৬৫ ॥

mātā, pitā, sthāna, gṛha, śayyāsana āra  
mother  father  abode  house  bed and seat  and others  
e saba kṛṣṇera śuddha-sattvera vikāra  
these  all  of Kṛṣṇa  of pure being  transformations

(65) His mother and father, His abode, His house, His bed and seat and all else — all these are transformations of Kṛṣṇa’s *śuddha-sattva*, pure transcendental being.[^42]

সত্ত্বং বিশুদ্ধং বসুদেবশব্দিতং  
যদীয়তে তত্র পুমানপাবৃতঃ ৷  
সত্ত্বে চ তস্মিন্ ভগবান্ বাসুদেবো  
হ্যধোক্ষজো মে মনসা বিধীয়তে ॥ ৬৬ ॥

sattvaṁ viśuddhaṁ vasudeva-śabditaṁ  
being  pure  by the name Vasudeva  called  
yad īyate tatra pumān apāvṛtaḥ  
because  is revealed  there  the Person  unveiled  
sattve ca tasmin bhagavān vāsudevo  
in being  and  that  Bhagavān  Vāsudeva  
hy adhokṣajo me manasā vidhīyate  
indeed  beyond the senses  by me  with the mind  is worshipped

(66) “Pure being (*viśuddha-sattva*) is called *vasudeva*, for in it the Supreme Person is revealed unveiled. And in that being I worship with my mind Lord Vāsudeva, who is beyond the senses (Adhokṣaja).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 4.3.23)[^43]

কৃষ্ণে ভগবত্তা-জ্ঞান — সংবিতের সার ৷  
ব্রহ্মজ্ঞানাদিক সব তার পরিবার ॥ ৬৭ ॥

kṛṣṇe bhagavattā-jñāna — saṁvitera sāra  
in Kṛṣṇa  of Godhood  the knowledge  of samvit  the essence  
brahma-jñānādika saba tāra parivāra  
of Brahman  knowledge and the rest  all  its  retinue

(67) The knowledge that Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān is the essence of Samvit; the knowledge of Brahman and all the rest are only its retinue.[^44]

হ্লাদিনীর সার ‘প্রেম’, প্রেমসার ‘ভাব’ ৷  
ভাবের পরমকাষ্ঠা, নাম ‘মহাভাব’ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

hlādinīra sāra ‘prema’, prema-sāra ‘bhāva’  
of hlādinī  the essence  ‘prema’  of prema the essence  ‘bhāva’  
bhāvera parama-kāṣṭhā, nāma ‘mahā-bhāva’  
of bhāva  the highest limit  name  ‘mahābhāva’

(68) The essence of Hlādinī is prema, divine love; the essence of prema is *bhāva*; and the highest limit of *bhāva* is called *mahābhāva*.[^45]

মহাভাবস্বরূপা শ্রীরাধা-ঠাকুরাণী ৷  
সর্ব্বগুণখনি কৃষ্ণকান্তাশিরোমণি ॥ ৬৯ ॥

mahābhāva-svarūpā śrī-rādhā-ṭhākurāṇī  
of mahābhāva  the embodiment  Śrī Rādhā  Ṭhākurāṇī  
sarva-guṇa-khani kṛṣṇa-kāntā-śiromaṇi  
of all  virtues  the mine  of Kṛṣṇa’s  beloveds  the crest-jewel

(69) Śrī Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī is the embodiment of *mahābhāva*. She is a mine of all virtues and the crest-jewel of Kṛṣṇa’s beloveds.[^46]

তয়োরপ্যুভয়োর্মধ্যে রাধিকা সর্ব্বথাধিকা ৷  
মহাভাবস্বরূপেয়ং গুণৈরতিবরীয়সী ॥ ৭০ ॥

tayor apy ubhayor madhye  
of these two  even  both  among  
rādhikā sarvathādhikā  
Rādhikā  in every way  superior  
mahābhāva-svarūpeyaṁ  
of mahābhāva  the embodiment  She  
guṇair ativarīyasī  
in virtues  far surpassing

(70) “Of the two, Rādhikā is superior in every way. She is the embodiment of *mahābhāva*, and in virtues She surpasses all.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi, the chapter on Śrī Rādhā, 2)[^47]

কৃষ্ণপ্রেমভাবিত যাঁর চিত্তেন্দ্রিয়-কায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-নিজশক্তি রাধা ক্রীড়ার সহায় ॥ ৭১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-prema-bhāvita yām̐ra cittendriya-kāya  
by love for Kṛṣṇa  steeped  whose  mind  senses  body  
kṛṣṇa-nija-śakti rādhā krīḍāra sahāya  
Kṛṣṇa’s  own potency  Rādhā  of the play  the helper

(71) Her mind, senses and body are steeped in love for Kṛṣṇa; Rādhā is Kṛṣṇa’s own potency, the helper in His play.[^48]

আনন্দচিন্ময়রসপ্রতিভাবিতাভি-  
স্তাভির্য এব নিজরূপতয়া কলাভিঃ ৷  
গোলোক এব নিবসত্যখিলাত্মভূতো  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ৭২ ॥

ānanda-cinmaya-rasa-pratibhāvitābhis  
with blissful  spiritual  rasa  imbued  
tābhir ya eva nija-rūpatayā kalābhiḥ  
with them  who  indeed  as His own form  with the portions (of potency)  
goloka eva nivasaty akhilātma-bhūto  
in Goloka  indeed  dwells  of all  the soul  being  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(72) “I worship Govinda, the original Person, the Soul of all. He dwells eternally in Goloka with the gopīs, His potencies (*kalās*), who are one with Him in nature and imbued with blissful spiritual rasa.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.37)[^49]

কৃষ্ণেরে করায় যৈছে রস আস্বাদন ৷  
ক্রীড়ার সহায় যৈছে, শুন বিবরণ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

kṛṣṇere karāya yaiche rasa āsvādana  
Kṛṣṇa  makes  how  rasa  taste  
krīḍāra sahāya yaiche, śuna vivaraṇa  
of the play  the helper  how  hear  the description

(73) Now hear how She makes Kṛṣṇa taste rasa and how She helps Him in His play.

কৃষ্ণকান্তাগণ দেখি ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
এক লক্ষ্মীগণ, পুরে মহিষীগণ আর ॥ ৭৪ ॥

ব্রজাঙ্গনা-রূপ, আর কান্তাগণ-সার ৷  
শ্রীরাধিকা হৈতে কান্তাগণের বিস্তার ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kāntā-gaṇa dekhi tri-vidha prakāra  
of Kṛṣṇa  beloveds  the host  we see  of three  kinds  
eka lakṣmī-gaṇa, pure mahiṣī-gaṇa āra  
first  the Lakṣmīs  in the city  the queens  and  
vrajāṅganā-rūpa, āra kāntā-gaṇa-sāra  
of the Vraja girls  the form  and  of beloveds  the essence  
śrī-rādhikā haite kāntā-gaṇera vistāra  
from Śrī Rādhikā  of the beloveds  the expansion

(74–75) Kṛṣṇa’s beloveds are of three kinds: the Lakṣmīs, the queens in His city, and the girls of Vraja, who are the essence of all beloveds. All of them expand from Śrī Rādhikā.[^50]

অবতারী কৃষ্ণ যৈছে করে অবতার ৷  
অংশিনী রাধা হৈতে তিন গণের বিস্তার ॥ ৭৬ ॥

avatārī kṛṣṇa yaiche kare avatāra  
the source of avatāras  Kṛṣṇa  as  makes  avatāras  
aṁśinī rādhā haite tina gaṇera vistāra  
the source of parts  Rādhā  from  of three  groups  the expansion

(76) As Kṛṣṇa, the source of all avatāras, manifests the avatāras, so the three groups expand from Rādhā, the source of all parts.[^51]

বৈভবগণ যেন তাঁর অঙ্গ-বিভূতি ৷  
বিম্ব-প্রতিবিম্ব-রূপ মহিষীর ততি ॥ ৭৭ ॥

vaibhava-gaṇa yena tām̐ra aṅga-vibhūti  
the vaibhavas  as it were  Her  of the body  splendour  
bimba-pratibimba-rūpa mahiṣīra tati  
original  reflection  in the form  of the queens  the host

(77) The Lakṣmīs are, as it were, the splendour of Her body, and the queens are Her reflections (*bimba-pratibimba*).[^52]

লক্ষ্মীগণ তাঁর বৈভব-বিলাসাংশরূপ ৷  
মহিষীগণ প্রাভব-প্রকাশস্বরূপ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

lakṣmī-gaṇa tām̐ra vaibhava-vilāsāṁśa-rūpa  
the Lakṣmīs  Her  of the vaibhava-vilāsa  parts  the form  
mahiṣī-gaṇa prābhava-prakāśa-svarūpa  
the queens  of the prābhava-prakāśa  the nature

(78) The Lakṣmīs are partial manifestations (*aṁśas*) of Her *vaibhava-vilāsa*, and the queens are Her *prābhava-prakāśas*.[^53]

আকার-স্বরূপ-ভেদে ব্রজদেবীগণ ৷  
কায়ব্যূহরূপ তাঁর রসের কারণ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

ākāra-svarūpa-bhede vraja-devī-gaṇa  
of form  of nature  by difference  of Vraja  the goddesses  
kāya-vyūha-rūpa tām̐ra rasera kāraṇa  
of the body  expansion  the form  Her  of rasa  for the sake

(79) The goddesses of Vraja, differing in form and nature, are expansions of Her own body (*kāya-vyūha*), for the sake of rasa.[^54]

বহু কান্তা বিনা নহে রসের উল্লাস ৷  
লীলার সহায় লাগি’ বহু ত’ প্রকাশ ॥ ৮০ ॥

bahu kāntā vinā nahe rasera ullāsa  
many  beloveds  without  is not  of rasa  the delight  
līlāra sahāya lāgi’ bahu ta’ prakāśa  
of the play  the help  for the sake  many  indeed  manifestations

(80) Without many beloveds rasa does not rejoice; therefore, to help in the play, She has many manifestations.[^55]

তার মধ্যে ব্রজে নানা ভাব-রস-ভেদে ৷  
কৃষ্ণকে করায় রাসাদিক-লীলাস্বাদে ॥ ৮১ ॥

tāra madhye vraje nānā bhāva-rasa-bhede  
of them  among  in Vraja  various  of moods  of rasas  by the differences  
kṛṣṇake karāya rāsādika-līlāsvāde  
Kṛṣṇa  make  the rāsa and other  pastimes  taste

(81) Among them the girls of Vraja, differing in moods and rasas, make Kṛṣṇa taste the *rāsa* dance and His other pastimes.[^56]

গোবিন্দানন্দিনী, রাধা, গোবিন্দমোহিনী ৷  
গোবিন্দসর্ব্বস্ব, সর্ব্বকান্তা-শিরোমণি ॥ ৮২ ॥

govindānandinī, rādhā, govinda-mohinī  
Govinda’s delight  Rādhā  Govinda’s enchantress  
govinda-sarvasva, sarva-kāntā-śiromaṇi  
Govinda’s all-in-all  of all  beloveds  the crest-jewel

(82) Rādhā is the delight of Govinda and the enchantress of Govinda; She is Govinda’s all-in-all and the crest-jewel of all His beloveds.[^57]

দেবী কৃষ্ণময়ী প্রোক্তা রাধিকা পরদেবতা ৷  
সর্ব্বলক্ষ্মীময়ী সর্ব্বকান্তিঃ সম্মোহিনী পরা ॥ ৮৩ ॥

devī kṛṣṇa-mayī proktā  
the goddess  full of Kṛṣṇa  is declared  
rādhikā para-devatā  
Rādhikā  the supreme  deity  
sarva-lakṣmī-mayī sarva-  
all  Lakṣmīs  containing  all  
kāntiḥ sammohinī parā  
splendour  the enchantress  supreme

(83) “The goddess (*devī*) Rādhikā is declared to be full of Kṛṣṇa (*kṛṣṇa-mayī*). She is the supreme deity; all the Lakṣmīs are within Her; She is the whole of splendour (*sarva-kānti*) and the supreme enchantress.” (Bṛhad-gautamīya-tantra)[^58]

‘দেবী’ কহি দ্যোতমানা, পরমা সুন্দরী ৷  
কিংবা, কৃষ্ণপূজা-ক্রীড়ার বসতি নগরী ॥ ৮৪ ॥

‘devī’ kahi dyotamānā, paramā sundarī  
‘devī’  means  shining  supremely  beautiful  
kiṁvā, kṛṣṇa-pūjā-krīḍāra vasati nagarī  
or  of Kṛṣṇa’s  worship  of the play  the dwelling  city

(84) *Devī* means “shining and supremely beautiful”, or else “the city where Kṛṣṇa’s worship and play abide”.[^59]

কৃষ্ণময়ী — কৃষ্ণ যাঁর ভিতরে বাহিরে ৷  
যাঁহা যাঁহা নেত্র পড়ে, তাঁহা কৃষ্ণ স্ফুরে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-mayī — kṛṣṇa yām̐ra bhitare bāhire  
full of Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  whose  within  without  
yām̐hā yām̐hā netra paḍe, tām̐hā kṛṣṇa sphure  
wherever  wherever  the eyes  fall  there  Kṛṣṇa  appears

(85) *Kṛṣṇa-mayī* — Kṛṣṇa is within Her and without; wherever Her eyes fall, there Kṛṣṇa appears.[^60]

কিম্বা, প্রেমরসময় কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ ৷  
তাঁর শক্তি তাঁর সহ হয় একরূপ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

kimbā, prema-rasa-maya kṛṣṇera svarūpa  
or  full of the rasa of love  of Kṛṣṇa  the nature  
tām̐ra śakti tām̐ra saha haya eka-rūpa  
His  potency  with Him  is  one

(86) Or else: Kṛṣṇa’s very nature is full of the rasa of love, and His potency is one with Him.[^61]

কৃষ্ণবাঞ্ছা-পূর্ত্তিরূপ করে আরাধনে ৷  
অতএব ‘রাধিকা’ নাম পুরাণে বাখানে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa-vāñchā-pūrti-rūpa kare ārādhane  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  desires  of the fulfilment  in the form  makes  worship  
ataeva ‘rādhikā’ nāma purāṇe vākhāne  
therefore  ‘Rādhikā’  name  in the Purāṇas  they explain

(87) Her worship of Kṛṣṇa consists in fulfilling His desires; therefore the Purāṇas explain Her name as Rādhikā.[^62]

অনয়ারাধিতো নূনং ভগবান্ হরিরীশ্বরঃ ৷  
যন্নো বিহায় গোবিন্দঃ প্রীতো যামনয়দ্রহঃ ॥ ৮৮ ॥

anayārādhito nūnaṁ  
by Her  worshipped  surely  
bhagavān harir īśvaraḥ  
Bhagavān  Hari  the Lord  
yan no vihāya govindaḥ  
since  us  leaving  Govinda  
prīto yām anayad rahaḥ  
pleased  whom  led  to a secret place

(88) “Surely the Lord, Bhagavān Hari, has been worshipped by Her, for Govinda, pleased, has left us and led Her away to a secluded place.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.28)[^63]

অতএব সর্ব্বপূজ্যা, পরম-দেবতা ৷  
সর্ব্বপালিকা, সর্ব্ব-জগতের মাতা ॥ ৮৯ ॥

ataeva sarva-pūjyā, parama-devatā  
therefore  worshipped by all  the supreme  deity  
sarva-pālikā, sarva-jagatera mātā  
the protectress of all  of all  the world  the mother

(89) Therefore She is worshipped by all; She is the supreme deity, the protectress of all and the mother of the whole world.[^64]

‘সর্ব্বলক্ষ্মী’ শব্দ পূর্ব্বে করিয়াছি ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
সর্ব্বলক্ষ্মীগণের তিঁহো হন অধিষ্ঠান ॥ ৯০ ॥

‘sarva-lakṣmī’ śabda pūrve kariyāchi vyākhyāna  
‘all Lakṣmīs’  the word  before  I have made  the explanation  
sarva-lakṣmī-gaṇera tim̐ho hana adhiṣṭhāna  
of all  the Lakṣmīs  She  is  the abode

(90) I have already explained the word *sarva-lakṣmī*: She is the abode of all the Lakṣmīs.[^65]

কিম্বা, ‘সর্ব্বলক্ষ্মী’ — কৃষ্ণের ষড়্বিধ ঐশ্বর্য্য ৷  
তাঁর অধিষ্ঠাত্রী শক্তি — সর্ব্বশক্তিবর্য্য ॥ ৯১ ॥

kimbā, ‘sarva-lakṣmī’ — kṛṣṇera ṣaḍ-vidha aiśvarya  
or  ‘all Lakṣmīs’  Kṛṣṇa’s  sixfold  opulence  
tām̐ra adhiṣṭhātrī śakti — sarva-śakti-varya  
its  presiding  potency  of all  potencies  the best

(91) Or *sarva-lakṣmī* means Kṛṣṇa’s six kinds of opulence, and She is the potency that presides over them, the best of all His potencies.[^66]

সর্ব্ব-সৌন্দর্য্য-কান্তি বৈসয়ে যাঁহাতে ৷  
সর্ব্বলক্ষ্মীগণের শোভা হয় যাঁহা হৈতে ॥ ৯২ ॥

sarva-saundarya-kānti vaisaye yām̐hāte  
of all  beauty  the splendour  dwells  in whom  
sarva-lakṣmī-gaṇera śobhā haya yām̐hā haite  
of all  the Lakṣmīs  the beauty  comes  from whom

(92) In Her dwells the splendour of all beauty, and from Her comes the beauty of all the Lakṣmīs.

কিংবা ‘কান্তি’ শব্দে কৃষ্ণের সব ইচ্ছা কহে ৷  
কৃষ্ণের সকল বাঞ্ছা রাধাতেই রহে ॥ ৯৩ ॥

kiṁvā ‘kānti’ śabde kṛṣṇera saba icchā kahe  
or  ‘kānti’  by the word  Kṛṣṇa’s  all  desires  they mean  
kṛṣṇera sakala vāñchā rādhātei rahe  
Kṛṣṇa’s  all  desires  in Rādhā alone  abide

(93) Or the word *kānti* may mean all of Kṛṣṇa’s desires, and all of Kṛṣṇa’s desires abide in Rādhā alone.[^67]

রাধিকা করেন কৃষ্ণের বাঞ্ছিত পূরণ ৷  
‘সর্ব্বকান্তি’ শব্দের এই অর্থ বিবরণ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

rādhikā karena kṛṣṇera vāñchita pūraṇa  
Rādhikā  makes  of Kṛṣṇa  of the desired  the fulfilment  
‘sarva-kānti’ śabdera ei artha vivaraṇa  
‘all splendour’  of the word  this  of the meaning  the explanation

(94) Rādhikā fulfils whatever Kṛṣṇa desires. This is the meaning of the word *sarva-kānti*.

জগৎমোহন কৃষ্ণ, তাঁহার মোহিনী ৷  
অতএব সমস্তের পরা ঠাকুরাণী ॥ ৯৫ ॥

jagat-mohana kṛṣṇa, tām̐hāra mohinī  
the enchanter of the world  Kṛṣṇa  His  enchantress  
ataeva samastera parā ṭhākurāṇī  
therefore  of all  the supreme  Mistress

(95) Kṛṣṇa enchants the world, and She enchants Him; therefore She is the supreme Mistress of all.[^68]

রাধা — পূর্ণশক্তি, কৃষ্ণ — পূর্ণশক্তিমান্ ৷  
দুই বস্তু ভেদ নাই, শাস্ত্র-পরমাণ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

rādhā — pūrṇa-śakti, kṛṣṇa — pūrṇa-śaktimān  
Rādhā  the full potency  Kṛṣṇa  the full possessor of potency  
dui vastu bheda nāi, śāstra-paramāṇa  
two  realities  difference  there is not  of scripture  the evidence

(96) Rādhā is the full potency, and Kṛṣṇa the full possessor of potency. The scriptures bear witness that there is no difference between the two.[^69]

মৃগমদ, তার গন্ধ — যৈছে অবিচ্ছেদ ৷  
অগ্নি, জ্বালাতে — যৈছে কভু নাহি ভেদ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

mṛgamada, tāra gandha — yaiche aviccheda  
musk  its  fragrance  as  inseparable  
agni, jvālāte — yaiche kabhu nāhi bheda  
fire  and its heat  as  ever  there is not  difference

(97) As musk and its fragrance are inseparable, as fire and its heat are never different,[^70]

রাধাকৃষ্ণ ঐছে সদা একই স্বরূপ ৷  
লীলারস আস্বাদিতে ধরে দুইরূপ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

rādhā-kṛṣṇa aiche sadā ekai svarūpa  
Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa  so  always  one  essence  
līlā-rasa āsvādite dhare dui-rūpa  
of the pastimes  the rasa  to taste  assume  two forms

(98) so Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are always one essence; to taste the rasa of Their pastimes They assume two forms.[^71]

প্রেমভক্তি শিখাইতে আপনে অবতরি’ ৷  
রাধা-ভাব-কান্তি দুই অঙ্গীকার করি’ ॥ ৯৯ ॥

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যরূপে কৈল অবতার ৷  
এই ত’ পঞ্চম শ্লোকের অর্থ পরচার ॥ ১০০ ॥

prema-bhakti śikhāite āpane avatari’  
loving devotion  to teach  Himself  descending  
rādhā-bhāva-kānti dui aṅgīkāra kari’  
Rādhā’s  mood  lustre  both  acceptance  making  
śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-rūpe kaila avatāra  
in the form of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  made  descent  
ei ta’ pañcama ślokera artha paracāra  
this  indeed  of the fifth  verse  of the meaning  the explanation

(99–100) To teach loving devotion, He Himself descended, accepting both the mood and the lustre of Rādhā, and appeared in the form of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. This is the explanation of the meaning of the fifth verse.[^72]

ষষ্ঠ শ্লোকের অর্থ করিতে প্রকাশ ৷  
প্রথমে কহিয়ে সেই শ্লোকের আভাস ॥ ১০১ ॥

ṣaṣṭha ślokera artha karite prakāśa  
of the sixth  verse  the meaning  in order to make  manifest  
prathame kahiye sei ślokera ābhāsa  
first  I speak  that  of the verse  an outline

(101) To bring out the meaning of the sixth verse, I shall first give an outline of it.[^73]

অবতরি’ প্রভু প্রচারিল সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
এহো বাহ্য হেতু, পূর্ব্বে করিয়াছি সূচন ॥ ১০২ ॥

avatari’ prabhu pracārila saṅkīrtana  
descending  the Lord  preached  saṅkīrtana  
eho bāhya hetu, pūrve kariyāchi sūcana  
this  external  reason  before  I have made  indication

(102) The Lord descended and preached *saṅkīrtana*; but that, as I have indicated before, is an external reason.[^74]

অবতারের আর এক আছে মুখ্যবীজ ৷  
রসিকশেখর কৃষ্ণের সেই কার্য্য নিজ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

avatārera āra eka āche mukhya-bīja  
of the descent  yet  one  there is  the chief seed  
rasika-śekhara kṛṣṇera sei kārya nija  
of the crown of rasikas  Kṛṣṇa’s  that  work  own

(103) There is another, chief seed of the descent: it is the very own work of Kṛṣṇa, the crown of all who relish rasa.[^75]

অতি গূঢ় হেতু সেই ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
দামোদর-স্বরূপ হৈতে যাহার প্রচার ॥ ১০৪ ॥

ati gūḍha hetu sei tri-vidha prakāra  
very  hidden  reason  that  of three  kinds  
dāmodara-svarūpa haite yāhāra pracāra  
from Dāmodara-svarūpa  of which  the making known

(104) That most hidden reason is threefold, and it was made known by Svarūpa Dāmodara.[^76]

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি — প্রভুর অতি অন্তরঙ্গ ৷  
তাহাতে জানেন প্রভুর এসব প্রসঙ্গ ॥ ১০৫ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi — prabhura ati antaraṅga  
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  of the Lord  very  intimate  
tāhāte jānena prabhura esaba prasaṅga  
therefore  knows  of the Lord  these  matters

(105) Svarūpa Gosāñi is the Lord’s most intimate associate, and so he knows all these matters of the Lord.[^77]

রাধিকার ভাব-মূর্ত্তি প্রভুর অন্তর ৷  
সেইভাবে সুখ-দুঃখ উঠে নিরন্তর ॥ ১০৬ ॥

rādhikāra bhāva-mūrti prabhura antara  
of Rādhikā  of the mood  the embodiment  of the Lord  the heart  
sei-bhāve sukha-duḥkha uṭhe nirantara  
in that mood  joy  sorrow  arise  incessantly

(106) The Lord’s heart is the very embodiment of Rādhikā’s mood, and in that mood joy and sorrow arise in Him incessantly.[^78]

শেষলীলায় প্রভুর কৃষ্ণবিরহ-উন্মাদ ৷  
ভ্রমময় চেষ্টা, সদা প্রলাপময়-বাদ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

śeṣa-līlāya prabhura kṛṣṇa-viraha-unmāda  
in the last pastimes  of the Lord  separation from Kṛṣṇa  madness  
bhrama-maya ceṣṭā, sadā pralāpa-maya-vāda  
of bewilderment  full  actions  always  of delirium  full  speech

(107) In His last pastimes the Lord was mad with separation from Kṛṣṇa: His actions were full of bewilderment, and His speech was always full of delirium.[^79]

রাধিকার ভাব যৈছে উদ্ধবদর্শনে ৷  
সেই ভাবে মত্ত প্রভু রহে রাত্রি-দিনে ॥ ১০৮ ॥

rādhikāra bhāva yaiche uddhava-darśane  
of Rādhikā  the mood  as  on seeing Uddhava  
sei bhāve matta prabhu rahe rātri-dine  
in that  mood  intoxicated  the Lord  remains  night and day

(108) As Rādhikā felt on seeing Uddhava, so the Lord remained, intoxicated with that mood, day and night.[^80]

রাত্রে প্রলাপ করে স্বরূপের কণ্ঠ ধরি’ ৷  
আবেশে আপন ভাব কহয়ে উঘাড়ি’ ॥ ১০৯ ॥

rātre pralāpa kare svarūpera kaṇṭha dhari’  
at night  delirium  makes  of Svarūpa  the neck  holding  
āveśe āpana bhāva kahaye ughāḍi’  
in absorption  His own  moods  speaks  laying bare

(109) At night, holding Svarūpa by the neck, He spoke in delirium, and in His absorption He laid bare His own feelings.[^81]

যবে যেই ভাব উঠে প্রভুর অন্তর ৷  
সেই গীত-শ্লোকে সুখ দেন দামোদর ॥ ১১০ ॥

yabe yei bhāva uṭhe prabhura antara  
when  which  mood  arises  of the Lord  in the heart  
sei gīta-śloke sukha dena dāmodara  
with that  song  verse  joy  gives  Dāmodara

(110) Whatever mood arose in the Lord’s heart, Dāmodara gave Him joy with a song or a verse that suited it.

এবে কার্য্য নাহি কিছু এসব বিচারে ৷  
আগে ইহা বিবরিব করিয়া বিস্তারে ॥ ১১১ ॥

ebe kārya nāhi kichu esaba vicāre  
now  need  there is not  any  of these  in the consideration  
āge ihā vivariba kariyā vistāre  
later  this  I shall describe  making  in detail

(111) There is no need to consider all this now; later I shall describe it in detail.[^82]

পূর্ব্বে ব্রজে কৃষ্ণের ত্রিবিধ বয়োধর্ম্ম ৷  
কৌমার, পৌগণ্ড, আর কৈশোর অতিমর্ম্ম ॥ ১১২ ॥

pūrve vraje kṛṣṇera tri-vidha vayo-dharma  
formerly  in Vraja  of Kṛṣṇa  threefold  of age  characteristic  
kaumāra, paugaṇḍa, āra kaiśora atimarma  
childhood  boyhood  and  youth  most essential

(112) Formerly in Vraja Kṛṣṇa had three ages — childhood, boyhood and youth — and youth was the most essential of them.[^83]

বাৎসল্য-আবেশে কৈল কৌমার সফল ৷  
পৌগণ্ড সফল কৈল লঞা সখাবল ॥ ১১৩ ॥

vātsalya-āveśe kaila kaumāra saphala  
of parental love  in the absorption  made  childhood  fruitful  
paugaṇḍa saphala kaila lañā sakhāvala  
boyhood  fruitful  made  taking  the host of friends

(113) In the absorption of parental love He made His childhood fruitful, and with the host of His friends He made His boyhood fruitful.

রাধিকাদি লঞা কৈল রাসাদি-বিলাস ৷  
বাঞ্ছা ভরি’ আস্বাদিল রসের নির্য্যাস ॥ ১১৪ ॥

rādhikādi lañā kaila rāsādi-vilāsa  
Rādhikā and others  taking  made  the rāsa and other  pastimes  
vāñchā bhari’ āsvādila rasera niryāsa  
the desire  filling  tasted  of rasa  the essence

(114) And with Rādhikā and the other gopīs He performed the *rāsa* dance and other pastimes, and tasted the very essence of rasa to His heart’s content.[^84]

কৈশোর-বয়সে কাম, জগৎসকল ৷  
রাসাদি-লীলায় তিন করিল সফল ॥ ১১৫ ॥

kaiśora-vayase kāma, jagat-sakala  
in the age of youth  Kāma  the world  whole  
rāsādi-līlāya tina karila saphala  
by the rāsa and other  pastimes  the three  made  fruitful

(115) In His youth, by the *rāsa* dance and other pastimes, He made three things fruitful: His youth, Kāma (the god of love), and the whole world.[^85]

সোঽপি কৈশোরক-বয়ো মানয়ন্মধুসূদনঃ ৷  
রেমে স্ত্রীরত্নকূটস্থঃ ক্ষপাসু ক্ষপিতাহিতঃ ॥ ১১৬ ॥

so ’pi kaiśoraka-vayo  
He  too  youthful  age  
mānayan madhusūdanaḥ  
honouring  Madhusūdana  
reme strī-ratna-kūṭa-sthaḥ  
sported  of jewel-like women  amid the host  standing  
kṣapāsu kṣapitāhitaḥ  
at night  the destroyer  of the inauspicious

(116) “And Madhusūdana, honouring His youth, sported on autumn nights amid a host of jewel-like girls — He who destroys all that is inauspicious.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 5.13.59)[^86]

বাচা সূচিতশর্ব্বরীরতিকলাপ্রাগল্ভ্যয়া রাধিকাং  
ব্রীড়াকুঞ্চিতলোচনাং বিরচয়ন্নগ্রে সখীনামসৌ ৷  
তদ্বক্ষোরুহচিত্রকেলিমকরীপাণ্ডিত্যপারং গতঃ  
কৈশোরং সফলীকরোতি কলয়ন্কুঞ্জে বিহারং হরিঃ ॥ ১১৭ ॥

vācā sūcita-śarvarī-rati-kalā-prāgalbhyayā rādhikāṁ  
by speech  hinting  of the night’s  love  arts  at the boldness  Rādhikā  
vrīḍā-kuñcita-locanāṁ viracayann agre sakhīnām asau  
with shyness  closed  eyes  making  before  the friends  He  
tad-vakṣo-ruha-citra-keli-makarī-pāṇḍitya-pāraṁ gataḥ  
on Her  breasts  of the picture  of playful  makarīs  of the skill  the limit  having reached  
kaiśoraṁ saphalī-karoti kalayan kuñje vihāraṁ hariḥ  
youth  fruitful  makes  performing  in the bower  pastimes  Hari

(117) “By words that hinted boldly at the arts of love of the night before, He made Rādhikā close Her eyes in shyness before Her friends; then, drawing playful *makarīs* on Her breasts, He showed the utmost skill. So Hari, sporting in the bowers, makes His youth fruitful.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.231)[^87]

হরিরেষ ন চেদবাতরিষ্যম্  
মথুরায়াং মধুরাক্ষি রাধিকা চ ৷  
অভবিষ্যদিয়ং বৃথা বিসৃষ্টি-  
র্মকরাঙ্কস্তু বিশেষতস্তদাত্র ॥ ১১৮ ॥

harir eṣa na ced avātariṣyam  
Hari  this  not  if  had descended  
mathurāyāṁ madhurākṣi rādhikā ca  
in Mathurā  O sweet-eyed one  Rādhikā  and  
abhaviṣyad iyaṁ vṛthā visṛṣṭir  
would have been  this  in vain  creation  
makarāṅkas tu viśeṣatas tadātra  
Kāma  but  especially  then  here

(118) “O sweet-eyed one, had this Hari and Rādhikā not descended in Mathurā, all creation would have been in vain — and above all the god of love.” (Vidagdha-mādhava, 7.3)[^88]

এইমত পূর্ব্বে কৃষ্ণ রসের সদন ৷  
যদ্যপি করিল রস-নির্য্যাস-চর্ব্বণ ॥ ১১৯ ॥

তথাপি নহিল তিন বাঞ্ছিত পূরণ ৷  
তাহা আস্বাদিতে যদি করিল যতন ॥ ১২০ ॥

ei mata pūrve kṛṣṇa rasera sadana  
in this way  formerly  Kṛṣṇa  of rasa  the abode  
yadyapi karila rasa-niryāsa-carvaṇa  
although  made  of rasa  of the essence  the relishing  
tathāpi nahila tina vāñchita pūraṇa  
still  was not  three  of desires  the fulfilment  
tāhā āsvādite yadi karila yatana  
them  to taste  though  made  effort

(119–120) Thus formerly Kṛṣṇa, the abode of rasa, relished the very essence of rasa; yet three of His desires remained unfulfilled, though He tried to taste them.[^89]

তাঁহার প্রথম বাঞ্ছা করিয়ে ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
কৃষ্ণ কহে, — আমি হই রসের নিদান ॥ ১২১ ॥

tām̐hāra prathama vāñchā kariye vyākhyāna  
His  first  desire  I make  explanation  
kṛṣṇa kahe, — āmi hai rasera nidāna  
Kṛṣṇa  says  I  am  of rasa  the source

(121) I shall explain His first desire. Kṛṣṇa says: “I am the source of rasa.[^90]

পূর্ণানন্দময় আমি চিন্ময় পূর্ণতত্ত্ব ৷  
রাধিকার প্রেমে আমা করায় উন্মত্ত ॥ ১২২ ॥

pūrṇānanda-maya āmi cin-maya pūrṇa-tattva  
full of complete bliss  I  spiritual  the complete truth  
rādhikāra preme āmā karāya unmatta  
Rādhikā’s  by the love  Me  makes  mad

(122) I am full of complete bliss, the spiritual and complete Truth; yet the love of Rādhikā makes Me mad.[^91]

না জানি রাধার প্রেমে আছে কত বল ৷  
যে বলে আমারে করে সর্ব্বদা বিহ্বল ॥ ১২৩ ॥

nā jāni rādhāra preme āche kata bala  
not  I know  Rādhā’s  in the love  there is  how much  strength  
ye bale āmāre kare sarvadā vihvala  
by which  strength  Me  makes  always  overwhelmed

(123) I do not know how much strength there is in Rādhā’s love, by which She ever overwhelms Me.

রাধিকা প্রেমগুরু, আমি শিষ্য নট ৷  
সদা আমা নানা নৃত্যে নাচায় উদ্ভট ॥ ১২৪ ॥

rādhikā prema-guru, āmi śiṣya naṭa  
Rādhikā  the teacher of love  I  the disciple  a dancer  
sadā āmā nānā nṛtye nācāya udbhaṭa  
always  Me  various  in dances  makes dance  unprecedented

(124) Rādhikā is My teacher in love, and I am Her disciple, a dancer: She always makes Me dance new and unheard-of dances.”

কস্মাদ্বৃন্দে প্রিয়সখি হরেঃ পাদমূলাৎ কুতোঽসৌ  
কুণ্ডারণ্যে কিমিহ কুরুতে নৃত্যশিক্ষাং গুরুঃ কঃ ৷  
তং ত্বন্মূর্ত্তিঃ প্রতিতরুলতাং দিগ্বিদিক্ষু স্ফুরন্তী  
শৈলূষীব ভ্রমতি পরিতো নর্ত্তয়ন্তী স্বপশ্চাৎ ॥ ১২৫ ॥

kasmād vṛnde priya-sakhi hareḥ pāda-mūlāt kuto ’sau  
from where  O Vṛndā  dear friend  of Hari  from the feet  where  He  
kuṇḍāraṇye kim iha kurute nṛtya-śikṣāṁ guruḥ kaḥ  
in the forest by the kuṇḍa  what  here  does  of dance  the learning  the teacher  who  
taṁ tvan-mūrtiḥ prati-taru-latāṁ dig-vidikṣu sphurantī  
Him  Your  image  in every  tree  creeper  in the quarters and between  appearing  
śailūṣīva bhramati parito nartayantī sva-paścāt  
like a dancer  wanders  all around  making dance  behind itself

(125) “ ‘Where have you come from, Vṛndā, dear friend?’ ‘From the feet of Hari.’ ‘And where is He?’ ‘In the forest by Rādhā-kuṇḍa.’ ‘What is He doing there?’ ‘Learning to dance.’ ‘And who is His teacher?’ ‘Your own image, Rādhā: appearing in every tree and creeper, in every quarter of the sky, it wanders all around like a skilled dancer and makes Kṛṣṇa dance behind it.’ ” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 8.77)[^92]

নিজ-প্রেমাস্বাদে মোর হয় যে আহ্লাদ ৷  
তাহা হইতে কোটিগুণ রাধা-প্রেমাস্বাদ ॥ ১২৬ ॥

nija-premāsvāde mora haya ye āhlāda  
in tasting His own love  My  is  which  joy  
tāhā haite koṭi-guṇa rādhā-premāsvāda  
that  than  ten million times  Rādhā’s  tasting of love

(126) “The joy I feel in tasting My own love is great, but Rādhā’s joy in tasting Her love is ten million times greater.[^93]

আমি যৈছে পরস্পর বিরুদ্ধধর্ম্মাশ্রয় ৷  
রাধাপ্রেম তৈছে সদা বিরুদ্ধধর্ম্মময় ॥ ১২৭ ॥

āmi yaiche paraspara viruddha-dharmāśraya  
I  as  mutually  of opposite qualities  the shelter  
rādhā-prema taiche sadā viruddha-dharma-maya  
Rādhā’s  love  so  always  of opposite qualities  full

(127) As I am the shelter of mutually opposite qualities, so Rādhā’s love is always full of opposite qualities.[^94]

রাধা-প্রেমা বিভু — যার বাড়িতে নাহি ঠাঞি ৷  
তথাপি সে ক্ষণে ক্ষণে বাড়য়ে সদাই ॥ ১২৮ ॥

rādhā-premā vibhu — yāra bāḍite nāhi ṭhāñi  
Rādhā’s  love  all-pervading  for which  to grow  there is not  room  
tathāpi se kṣaṇe kṣaṇe bāḍaye sadāi  
still  it  moment  by moment  grows  always

(128) Rādhā’s love is all-pervading, with no room left to grow; yet it grows at every moment.[^95]

যাহা বই গুরুবস্তু নাহি সুনিশ্চিত ৷  
তথাপি গুরুর ধর্ম্ম গৌরব-বর্জ্জিত ॥ ১২৯ ॥

yāhā vai guru vastu nāhi suniścita  
which  besides  great  thing  there is not  surely  
tathāpi gurura dharma gaurava-varjita  
still  of the great  the quality  of pride  devoid

(129) Surely there is nothing greater than it, and yet it is free of the pride that belongs to greatness.

যাহা বই সুনির্ম্মল দ্বিতীয় নাহি আর ৷  
তথাপি সর্ব্বদা বাম্য, বক্র ব্যবহার ॥ ১৩০ ॥

yāhā bai sunirmala dvitīya nāhi āra  
which  besides  most pure  second  there is not  other  
tathāpi sarvadā vāmya, vakra vyavahāra  
still  always  contrariness  crooked  behaviour

(130) There is nothing else so pure as it, and yet it is always contrary and crooked in its ways.

বিভুরপি কলয়ন্ সদাভিবৃদ্ধিং  
গুরুরপি গৌরবচর্য্যয়া বিহীনঃ ৷  
মুহুরুপচিতবক্রিমাপি শুদ্ধো  
জয়তি মুরদ্বিষি রাধিকানুরাগঃ ॥ ১৩১ ॥

vibhur api kalayan sadābhivṛddhiṁ  
all-pervading  though  making  constant  growth  
gurur api gaurava-caryayā vihīnaḥ  
great  though  of pride  of conduct  devoid  
muhur upacita-vakrimāpi śuddho  
again and again  increased  crookedness  though  pure  
jayati mura-dviṣi rādhikānurāgaḥ  
glory be  for the enemy of Mura  of Rādhikā  the love

(131) “All glory to Rādhikā’s love for Kṛṣṇa, the enemy of Mura! All-pervading, it grows at every moment; great, it is free of pride; again and again it grows more crooked, and yet it is pure.” (Dāna-keli-kaumudī, 2)[^96]

সেই প্রেমার রাধিকা পরম ‘আশ্রয়’ ৷  
সেই প্রেমার আমি হই কেবল ‘বিষয়’ ॥ ১৩২ ॥

sei premāra rādhikā parama ‘āśraya’  
of that  love  Rādhikā  the supreme  ‘shelter’  
sei premāra āmi hai kevala ‘viṣaya’  
of that  love  I  am  only  ‘object’

(132) Rādhikā is the supreme *āśraya* of that love — the one in whom it dwells; and I am only its *viṣaya* — the One toward whom it flows.[^97]

বিষয়জাতীয় সুখ আমার আস্বাদ ৷  
আমা হৈতে কোটিগুণ আশ্রয়ের আহ্লাদ ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

viṣaya-jātīya sukha āmāra āsvāda  
of the object’s  kind  happiness  My  tasting  
āmā haite koṭi-guṇa āśrayera āhlāda  
Me  than  ten million times  of the shelter  the joy

(133) The happiness I taste is that of the *viṣaya*; the joy of the *āśraya* is ten million times greater than Mine.[^98]

আশ্রয়জাতীয় সুখ পাইতে মন ধায় ৷  
যত্নে আস্বাদিতে নারি, কি করি উপায় ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

āśraya-jātīya sukha pāite mana dhāya  
of the shelter’s  kind  happiness  to obtain  the mind  runs  
yatne āsvādite nāri, ki kari upāya  
by effort  to taste  I cannot  what  shall I do  means

(134) My mind runs after the happiness of the *āśraya*, but try as I may, I cannot taste it. What means can I find?[^99]

কভু যদি এই প্রেমার হইয়ে আশ্রয় ৷  
তবে এই প্রেমানন্দের অনুভব হয় ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

kabhu yadi ei premāra haiye āśraya  
ever  if  of this  love  I become  the shelter  
tabe ei premānandera anubhava haya  
then  of this  love’s  bliss  the experience  will be

(135) If ever I become the *āśraya* of this love, then I shall know the bliss of this love.”

এত চিন্তি’ রহে কৃষ্ণ পরমকৌতুকী ৷  
হৃদয়ে বাড়য়ে প্রেম-লোভ ধক্ধকি ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

eta cinti’ rahe kṛṣṇa parama-kautukī  
so  thinking  remains  Kṛṣṇa  supremely eager for wonder  
hṛdaye bāḍaye prema-lobha dhakdhaki  
in the heart  grows  for love  the greed  blazing

(136) Thinking thus, Kṛṣṇa, ever eager for wonder, remains; and in His heart the longing for that love blazes ever higher.

এই এক, শুন আর লোভের প্রকার ৷  
স্বমাধুর্য্য দেখি’ কৃষ্ণ করেন বিচার ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

ei eka, śuna āra lobhera prakāra  
this  one  hear  another  of greed  kind  
sva-mādhurya dekhi’ kṛṣṇa karena vicāra  
His own  sweetness  seeing  Kṛṣṇa  makes  reflection

(137) That is one. Now hear of another kind of longing. Seeing His own sweetness, Kṛṣṇa reflects:[^100]

অদ্ভুত, অনন্ত, পূর্ণ মোর মধুরিমা ৷  
ত্রিজগতে ইহার কেহ নাহি পায় সীমা ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

adbhuta, ananta, pūrṇa mora madhurimā  
wondrous  endless  complete  My  sweetness  
tri-jagate ihāra keha nāhi pāya sīmā  
in the three worlds  of it  anyone  not  finds  the limit

(138) “Wondrous, endless and complete is My sweetness; in the three worlds no one finds its limit.[^101]

এই প্রেমদ্বারে নিত্য রাধিকা একলি ৷  
আমার মাধুর্য্যামৃত আস্বাদে সকলি ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

ei prema-dvāre nitya rādhikā ekali  
this  love  through  always  Rādhikā  alone  
āmāra mādhuryāmṛta āsvāde sakali  
My  of sweetness  the nectar  tastes  all

(139) Through this love Rādhikā alone ever tastes all the nectar of My sweetness.

যদ্যপি নির্ম্মল রাধার সৎপ্রেমদর্পণ ৷  
তথাপি স্বচ্ছতা তার বাঢ়ে ক্ষণে ক্ষণ ॥ ১৪০ ॥

yadyapi nirmala rādhāra sat-prema-darpaṇa  
although  pure  Rādhā’s  of true  love  the mirror  
tathāpi svacchatā tāra bāḍhe kṣaṇe kṣaṇa  
still  clearness  its  grows  moment  by moment

(140) Though the mirror of Rādhā’s true love is spotless, its clearness grows at every moment.[^102]

আমার মাধুর্য্য নাহি বাঢ়িতে অবকাশে ৷  
এ-দর্পণের আগে নব নব রূপে ভাসে ॥ ১৪১ ॥

āmāra mādhurya nāhi bāḍhite avakāśe  
My  sweetness  there is not  to grow  room  
e-darpaṇera āge nava nava rūpe bhāse  
this mirror  before  new  new  in forms  shines

(141) My sweetness has no room to grow, and yet before this mirror it shines in ever new forms.

মন্মাধুর্য্য রাধার প্রেম — দোঁহে হোড় করি’ ৷  
ক্ষণে ক্ষণে বাড়ে দোঁহে, কেহ নাহি হারি ॥ ১৪২ ॥

man-mādhurya rādhāra prema — dom̐he hoḍa kari’  
My sweetness  Rādhā’s  love  the two  competition  making  
kṣaṇe kṣaṇe bāḍe dom̐he, keha nāhi hāri  
moment  by moment  grow  the two  either  not  loses

(142) My sweetness and Rādhā’s love vie with each other; at every moment both grow, and neither is defeated.[^103]

আমার মাধুর্য্য নিত্য নব নব হয় ৷  
স্ব-স্ব-প্রেম-অনুরূপ ভক্তে আস্বাদয় ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

āmāra mādhurya nitya nava nava haya  
My  sweetness  always  new  new  is  
sva-sva-prema-anurūpa bhakte āsvādaya  
own  own  love  according to  devotees  taste

(143) My sweetness is ever new, and devotees taste it each according to his own love.

দর্পণাদ্যে দেখি’ যদি আপন মাধুরী ৷  
আস্বাদিতে হয় লোভ, আস্বাদিতে নারি ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

darpaṇādye dekhi’ yadi āpana mādhurī  
in a mirror and the like  seeing  if  My own  sweetness  
āsvādite haya lobha, āsvādite nāri  
to taste  arises  greed  to taste  I cannot

(144) When I see My own sweetness in a mirror or the like, a longing to taste it arises, but I cannot taste it.

বিচার করিয়ে যদি আস্বাদ-উপায় ৷  
রাধিকাস্বরূপ হইতে তবে মন ধায় ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

vicāra kariye yadi āsvāda-upāya  
consideration  I make  if  of tasting  the means  
rādhikā-svarūpa haite tabe mana dhāya  
Rādhikā in nature  to become  then  the mind  runs

(145) And when I consider how I might taste it, My mind runs to become Rādhikā Herself.”[^104]

অপরিকলিতপূর্ব্বঃ কশ্চমৎকারকারী  
স্ফুরতি মম গরীয়ানেষ মাধুর্য্যপূরঃ ৷  
অয়মহমপি হন্ত প্রেক্ষ্য যং লুব্ধচেতাঃ  
সরভসমুপভোক্তুং কাময়ে রাধিকেব ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

aparikalita-pūrvaḥ kaś camatkāra-kārī  
never experienced  before  some  wonder-working  
sphurati mama garīyān eṣa mādhurya-pūraḥ  
appears  My  greater  this  of sweetness  flood  
ayam aham api hanta prekṣya yaṁ lubdha-cetāḥ  
this  I  even  alas  seeing  which  with greedy heart  
sarabhasam upabhoktuṁ kāmaye rādhikeva  
eagerly  to enjoy  I desire  like Rādhikā

(146) “What never-before-known, wonder-working flood of My sweetness, surpassing all, appears here! Alas, seeing it, even I long with an eager heart to enjoy it, like Rādhikā.” (Lalita-mādhava, 8.34)[^105]

কৃষ্ণমাধুর্য্যের এক স্বাভাবিক বল ৷  
কৃষ্ণআদি নরনারী করয়ে চঞ্চল ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa-mādhuryera eka svābhāvika bala  
of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  one  natural  power  
kṛṣṇa-ādi nara-nārī karaye cañcala  
Kṛṣṇa and others  men  women  makes  restless

(147) Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness has a natural power: it makes all men and women restless, beginning with Kṛṣṇa Himself.[^106]

শ্রবণে, দর্শনে আকর্ষয়ে সর্ব্বমন ৷  
আপনা আস্বাদিতে কৃষ্ণ করেন যতন ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

śravaṇe, darśane ākarṣaye sarva-mana  
by hearing  by seeing  attracts  all  minds  
āpanā āsvādite kṛṣṇa karena yatana  
Himself  to taste  Kṛṣṇa  makes  effort

(148) Heard of or seen, it draws every heart, and Kṛṣṇa Himself strives to taste it.

এ মাধুর্য্যামৃত সদা যেই পান করে ৷  
তৃষ্ণাশান্তি নহে, তৃষ্ণা বাঢ়ে নিরন্তরে ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

e mādhuryāmṛta sadā yei pāna kare  
this  of sweetness  the nectar  always  whoever  drinking  makes  
tṛṣṇā-śānti nahe, tṛṣṇā bāḍhe nirantare  
of thirst  the quenching  is not  the thirst  grows  incessantly

(149) Whoever drinks this nectar of sweetness finds his thirst never quenched; the thirst only grows without end.[^107]

অতৃপ্ত হইয়া করে বিধিরে নিন্দন ৷  
অবিদগ্ধ বিধি ভাল না জানে সৃজন ॥ ১৫০ ॥

atṛpta haiyā kare vidhire nindana  
unsatisfied  becoming  makes  of the Creator  blame  
avidagdha vidhi bhāla nā jāne sṛjana  
unskilled  the Creator  well  not  knows  creation

(150) Unsatisfied, he blames the Creator: “The unskilled Creator does not know how to create well!

কোটি নেত্র নাহি দিল, সবে দিল দুই ৷  
তাহাতে নিমেষ, — কৃষ্ণ কি দেখিব মুঞি ॥ ১৫১ ॥

koṭi netra nāhi dila, sabe dila dui  
ten million  eyes  not  gave  only  gave  two  
tāhāte nimeṣa, — kṛṣṇa ki dekhiba muñi  
in them  blinking  Kṛṣṇa  how  shall see  I

(151) He did not give me ten million eyes — he gave only two, and even those blink. How shall I see Kṛṣṇa?”

গোপ্যশ্চ কৃষ্ণমুপলভ্য চিরাদভীষ্টং  
যৎপ্রেক্ষণে দৃশিষু পক্ষ্মকৃতং শপন্তি ৷  
দৃগ্ভির্হৃদীকৃতমলং পরিরভ্য সর্ব্বা-  
স্তদ্ভাবমাপুরপি নিত্যযুজাং দুরাপম্ ॥ ১৫২ ॥

gopyaś ca kṛṣṇam upalabhya cirād abhīṣṭaṁ  
the gopīs  and  Kṛṣṇa  having obtained  after long  the desired  
yat-prekṣaṇe dṛśiṣu pakṣma-kṛtaṁ śapanti  
in seeing whom  in the eyes  the maker of the eyelids  they curse  
dṛgbhir hṛdī-kṛtam alaṁ parirabhya sarvās  
by the eyes  placed in the heart  fully  embracing  all  
tad-bhāvam āpur api nitya-yujāṁ durāpam  
that state  attained  even  of those ever-united  hard to attain

(152) “Having at last, after long separation, obtained their beloved Kṛṣṇa — at whom they gazed cursing the maker of eyelids — the gopīs through their eyes placed Him in their hearts and embraced Him there to their fill. So they all attained a state that even perfected yogīs can hardly reach.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.82.39)[^108]

অটতি যদ্ভবানহ্নি কাননং  
ত্রুটির্যুগায়তে ত্বামপশ্যতাম্ ৷  
কুটিলকুন্তলং শ্রীমুখঞ্চ তে  
জড় উদীক্ষতাং পক্ষ্মকৃদ্দৃশাম্ ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

aṭati yad bhavān ahni kānanaṁ  
wander  when  You  by day  the forest  
truṭir yugāyate tvām apaśyatām  
a moment  becomes a yuga  You  for those not seeing  
kuṭila-kuntalaṁ śrī-mukhaṁ ca te  
with curling  locks  beautiful face  and  Your  
jaḍa udīkṣatāṁ pakṣma-kṛd dṛśām  
a fool  for those gazing  the maker of eyelids  of the eyes

(153) “When by day You wander in the forest, a moment without You becomes an age for us. And as we gaze upon Your beautiful face framed in curling locks, we see what a fool was the maker of our eyelids.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.15)[^109]

কৃষ্ণাবলোকন বিনা নেত্রে ফল নাহি আন ৷  
যেই জন কৃষ্ণ দেখে, সেই ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

kṛṣṇāvalokana vinā netre phala nāhi āna  
of Kṛṣṇa  the seeing  without  for the eyes  fruit  there is not  other  
yei jana kṛṣṇa dekhe, sei bhāgyavān  
which  person  Kṛṣṇa  sees  that one  fortunate

(154) Without seeing Kṛṣṇa the eyes have no other fruit; whoever sees Kṛṣṇa is fortunate indeed.[^110]

অক্ষণ্বতাং ফলমিদং ন পরং বিদামঃ  
সখ্যঃ পশূননুবিবেশয়তোর্বয়স্যৈঃ ৷  
বক্ত্রং ব্রজেশসুতয়োরনুবেণুজুষ্টং  
যৈর্বা নিপীতমনুরক্ত কটাক্ষমোক্ষম্ ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

akṣaṇvatāṁ phalam idaṁ na paraṁ vidāmaḥ  
of those with eyes  the fruit  this  not  other  we know  
sakhyaḥ paśūn anuviveśayator vayasyaiḥ  
O friends  the cattle  driving into the forest  with companions  
vaktraṁ vrajeśa-sutayor anuveṇu-juṣṭaṁ  
the faces  of the two sons of Vraja’s lord  with the flute  graced  
yair vā nipītam anurakta kaṭākṣa-mokṣam  
by whom  or  drunk  loving  glances  casting

(155) “O friends, we know no other fruit for the eyes than this: to drink in the faces of the two sons of Vraja’s lord as They drive the cows into the forest with Their friends, playing Their flutes and casting loving glances.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.7)[^111]

গোপ্যস্তপঃ কিমচরন্ যদমুষ্য রূপং  
লাবণ্যসারমসমোর্ধ্বমনন্যসিদ্ধম্ ৷  
দৃগ্ভিঃ পিবন্ত্যনুসবাভিনবং দুরাপ-  
মেকান্তধাম যশসঃ শ্রিয় ঐশ্বরস্য ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

gopyas tapaḥ kim acaran yad amuṣya rūpaṁ  
the gopīs  austerity  what  performed  since  His  form  
lāvaṇya-sāram asamordhvam ananya-siddham  
of loveliness  the essence  unequalled and unsurpassed  not perfected by anything else  
dṛgbhiḥ pibanty anusavābhinavaṁ durāpam  
with the eyes  drink  at every moment new  hard to attain  
ekānta-dhāma yaśasaḥ śriya aiśvarasya  
the sole abode  of fame  of beauty  of majesty

(156) “What austerities did the gopīs perform, that with their eyes they drink His form — the essence of loveliness, unequalled and unsurpassed, perfect in itself, new at every moment, hard to attain, the sole abode of fame, beauty and majesty?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.44.14)[^112]

অপূর্ব্ব মাধুরী কৃষ্ণের, অপূর্ব্ব তার বল ৷  
যাহার শ্রবণে মন হয় টলমল ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

apūrva mādhurī kṛṣṇera, apūrva tāra bala  
unprecedented  sweetness  of Kṛṣṇa  unprecedented  its  power  
yāhāra śravaṇe mana haya ṭalamala  
of which  on hearing  the mind  becomes  unsteady

(157) Unprecedented is Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness, and unprecedented its power: merely hearing of it, the mind trembles.

কৃষ্ণের মাধুর্য্যে কৃষ্ণে উপজয় লোভ ৷  
সম্যক্ আস্বাদিতে নারে, মনে রহে ক্ষোভ ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

kṛṣṇera mādhurye kṛṣṇe upajaya lobha  
of Kṛṣṇa  by the sweetness  in Kṛṣṇa  arises  greed  
samyak āsvādite nāre, mane rahe kṣobha  
fully  to taste  He cannot  in the mind  remains  regret

(158) Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness awakens longing in Kṛṣṇa Himself; He cannot taste it fully, and sorrow remains in His heart.

এই ত’ দ্বিতীয় হেতুর কহিল বিবরণ ৷  
তৃতীয় হেতুর এবে শুনহ লক্ষণ ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

ei ta’ dvitīya hetura kahila vivaraṇa  
this  indeed  of the second  reason  I have told  the description  
tṛtīya hetura ebe śunaha lakṣaṇa  
of the third  reason  now  hear  the characteristics

(159) So I have described the second reason. Now hear the nature of the third.[^113]

অত্যন্তনিগূঢ় এই রসের সিদ্ধান্ত ৷  
স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি মাত্র জানেন একান্ত ॥ ১৬০ ॥

atyanta-nigūḍha ei rasera siddhānta  
extremely  hidden  this  of rasa  the conclusion  
svarūpa-gosāñi mātra jānena ekānta  
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  only  knows  fully

(160) This conclusion about rasa is most deeply hidden; Svarūpa Gosāñi alone knows it fully.[^114]

যেবা কেহ অন্য জানে, সেহো তাঁহা হৈতে ৷  
চৈতন্য-গোসাঞির তেঁহ অত্যন্ত মর্ম্ম যাতে ॥ ১৬১ ॥

yebā keha anya jāne, seho tām̐hā haite  
if  anyone  other  knows  that too  from him  
caitanya-gosāñira tem̐ha atyanta marma yāte  
of Caitanya Gosāñi  he  the very  heart  since

(161) If anyone else knows it, he knows it from him, for he is the very heart of Caitanya Gosāñi.

গোপীগণের প্রেমের ‘রূঢ়ভাব’ নাম ৷  
বিশুদ্ধ নির্ম্মল প্রেম, কভু নহে কাম ॥ ১৬২ ॥

gopī-gaṇera premera ‘rūḍha-bhāva’ nāma  
of the gopīs  of the love  ‘rūḍha-bhāva’  the name  
viśuddha nirmala prema, kabhu nahe kāma  
most pure  spotless  love  ever  is not  lust

(162) The love of the gopīs is called *rūḍha-bhāva*. It is pure and spotless prema, never lust (*kāma*).[^115]

প্রেমৈব গোপরামানাং কাম ইত্যগমৎ প্রথাম্ ৷  
ইত্যুদ্ধবাদয়োঽপ্যেতং বাঞ্ছন্তি ভগবৎপ্রিয়াঃ ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

premaiva gopa-rāmānāṁ  
love itself  of the cowherd women  
kāma ity agamat prathām  
as kāma  so  attained  fame  
ity uddhavādayo ’py etaṁ  
therefore  Uddhava and others  even  it  
vāñchanti bhagavat-priyāḥ  
desire  dear to the Lord

(163) “The love of the cowherd women alone, though it is prema, became famed under the name *kāma*; and so even Uddhava and others dear to the Lord long for it.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.285)[^116]

কাম, প্রেম, — দোঁহাকার বিভিন্ন লক্ষণ ৷  
লৌহ আর হেম যৈছে স্বরূপে বিলক্ষণ ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

kāma, prema, — dom̐hākāra vibhinna lakṣaṇa  
lust  love  of the two  different  characteristics  
lauha āra hema yaiche svarūpe vilakṣaṇa  
iron  and  gold  as  in nature  distinct

(164) Lust and love have different characteristics, as iron and gold differ in their very nature.[^117]

আত্মেন্দ্রিয়প্রীতি-বাঞ্ছা — তারে বলি ‘কাম’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণেন্দ্রিয়প্রীতি-ইচ্ছা ধরে ‘প্রেম’ নাম ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

ātmendriya-prīti-vāñchā — tāre bali ‘kāma’  
of one’s own senses  of pleasure  the desire  that  I call  ‘lust’  
kṛṣṇendriya-prīti-icchā dhare ‘prema’ nāma  
of Kṛṣṇa’s senses  of pleasure  the wish  bears  ‘love’  the name

(165) The desire to please one’s own senses I call lust (*kāma*); the wish to please Kṛṣṇa’s senses bears the name of prema.[^118]

কামের তাৎপর্য্য — নিজ সম্ভোগ কেবল ৷  
কৃষ্ণসুখ তাৎপর্য্য মাত্র প্রেম ত’ প্রবল ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

kāmera tātparya — nija sambhoga kevala  
of lust  the aim  one’s own  enjoyment  only  
kṛṣṇa-sukha tātparya mātra prema ta’ prabala  
Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  the aim  only  love  indeed  mighty

(166) The aim of lust is only one’s own enjoyment; the aim of love is only Kṛṣṇa’s happiness, and so love is mighty.[^119]

লোকধর্ম্ম, বেদধর্ম্ম, দেহধর্ম্ম, কর্ম্ম ৷  
লজ্জা, ধৈর্য্য, দেহসুখ, আত্মসুখ-মর্ম্ম ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

দুস্ত্যজ্য আর্য্যপথ, নিজ পরিজন ৷  
স্বজনে করয়ে যত তাড়ন-ভর্ৎসন ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

সর্ব্বত্যাগ করি’ করে কৃষ্ণের ভজন ৷  
কৃষ্ণসুখহেতু করে প্রেম-সেবন ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

loka-dharma, veda-dharma, deha-dharma, karma  
social custom  Vedic dharma  bodily needs  work  
lajjā, dhairya, deha-sukha, ātma-sukha-marma  
shame  patience  bodily pleasure  of self-happiness  the essence  
dustyajya ārya-patha, nija parijana  
hard to give up  the path of the noble  own  family  
sva-jane karaye yata tāḍana-bhartsana  
the relatives  make  whatever  beating  rebuke  
sarva-tyāga kari’ kare kṛṣṇera bhajana  
of all  renunciation  making  they make  of Kṛṣṇa  worship  
kṛṣṇa-sukha-hetu kare prema-sevana  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  for the sake  they make  loving  service

(167–169) Social custom and Vedic religion, the needs of the body and their duties, shame and restraint, bodily pleasure and the very core of their own happiness, the path of the noble (*ārya-patha*), so hard to give up, their own families, and all the beatings and rebukes of their kin — giving up all this, they worship Kṛṣṇa, and render loving service for His happiness alone.[^120]

ইহাকে কহিয়ে কৃষ্ণে দৃঢ় অনুরাগ ৷  
স্বচ্ছ ধৌতবস্ত্রে যৈছে নাহি কোন দাগ ॥ ১৭০ ॥

ihāke kahiye kṛṣṇe dṛḍha anurāga  
this  they call  for Kṛṣṇa  firm  attachment  
svaccha dhauta-vastre yaiche nāhi kona dāga  
clean  washed  on cloth  as  there is not  any  stain

(170) This is called firm attachment (*anurāga*) to Kṛṣṇa: it is as clean as washed cloth without a single stain.

অতএব কাম-প্রেমে বহুত অন্তর ৷  
কাম — অন্ধতমঃ, প্রেম — নির্ম্মল ভাস্কর ॥ ১৭১ ॥

ataeva kāma-preme bahuta antara  
therefore  between lust and love  great  difference  
kāma — andha-tamaḥ, prema — nirmala bhāskara  
lust  blind darkness  love  the spotless  sun

(171) Therefore there is a vast difference between lust and love: lust is blind darkness, and love the spotless sun.[^121]

অতএব গোপীগণের নাহি কামগন্ধ ৷  
কৃষ্ণসুখ লাগি মাত্র, কৃষ্ণ সে সম্বন্ধ ॥ ১৭২ ॥

ataeva gopī-gaṇera nāhi kāma-gandha  
therefore  of the gopīs  there is not  of lust  the scent  
kṛṣṇa-sukha lāgi mātra, kṛṣṇa se sambandha  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  for the sake  only  with Kṛṣṇa  that  relation

(172) Therefore in the gopīs there is not even a trace of lust; their bond with Kṛṣṇa is for His happiness alone.[^122]

যত্তে সুজাতচরণাম্বুরুহং স্তনেষু  
ভীতাঃ শনৈঃ প্রিয় দধীমহি কর্ক্কশেষু ৷  
তেনাটবীমটসি তদ্ব্যথতে ন কিংস্বিৎ  
কূর্পাদিভির্ভ্রমতি ধীর্ভবদায়ুষাং নঃ ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

yat te sujāta-caraṇāmburuhaṁ staneṣu  
which  Your  tender  lotus feet  on the breasts  
bhītāḥ śanaiḥ priya dadhīmahi karkaśeṣu  
fearful  gently  O dear  we place  hard  
tenāṭavīm aṭasi tad vyathate na kiṁ svit  
with them  the forest  You wander  they  are pained  not  whether  
kūrpādibhir bhramati dhīr bhavad-āyuṣāṁ naḥ  
by fine stones and such  whirls  the mind  of whom You are the life  our

(173) “O dear one, Your tender lotus feet we place gently on our hard breasts, afraid to hurt them — and with those feet You wander through the forest! Are they not hurt by the sharp pebbles? You are our very life, and our minds are reeling.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.19)[^123]

আত্ম-সুখ-দুঃখে গোপীর নাহিক বিচার ৷  
কৃষ্ণসুখহেতু করে সব ব্যবহার ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

ātma-sukha-duḥkhe gopīra nāhika vicāra  
of one’s own  happiness  sorrow  of the gopīs  there is not  consideration  
kṛṣṇa-sukha-hetu kare saba vyavahāra  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  for the sake  they do  all  actions

(174) The gopīs give no thought to their own happiness or sorrow; all they do is for Kṛṣṇa’s happiness.[^124]

কৃষ্ণলাগি’ আর সব করি’ পরিত্যাগ ৷  
কৃষ্ণসুখহেতু করে শুদ্ধ অনুরাগ ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-lāgi’ āra saba kari’ parityāga  
for Kṛṣṇa  the rest  all  making  renunciation  
kṛṣṇa-sukha-hetu kare śuddha anurāga  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  for the sake  they make  pure  attachment

(175) For Kṛṣṇa they have given up everything else, and their pure attachment is for His happiness alone.

এবং মদর্থোজ্ঝিতলোকবেদ-  
স্বানাং হি বো ময়্যনুবৃত্তয়েঽবলাঃ ৷  
ময়া পরোক্ষং ভজতা তিরোহিতং  
মাসূয়িতুং মার্হথ তৎ প্রিয়ং প্রিয়াঃ ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

evaṁ mad-arthojjhita-loka-veda-  
thus  for My sake  having abandoned  social  Vedic (law)  
svānāṁ hi vo mayy anuvṛttaye ’balāḥ  
and kinsmen  indeed  of you  to Me  for the attachment  O weak ones  
mayā parokṣaṁ bhajatā tirohitaṁ  
by Me  invisibly  loving  disappeared  
māsūyituṁ mārhatha tat priyaṁ priyāḥ  
Me  to blame  you should not  that  dear one  O dear ones

(176) “O dear ones, O tender ones! For My sake you have abandoned the laws of society and of the Vedas, and even your kinsmen. I disappeared only that your love for Me might grow still stronger — yet even unseen I went on loving you. So, beloved ones, do not be displeased with Him who is dear to you.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.32.21)[^125]

কৃষ্ণের প্রতিজ্ঞা এক আছে পূর্ব্ব হৈতে ৷  
যে যৈছে ভজে, কৃষ্ণ তারে ভজে তৈছে ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

kṛṣṇera pratijñā eka āche pūrva haite  
Kṛṣṇa’s  promise  one  there is  from before  
ye yaiche bhaje, kṛṣṇa tāre bhaje taiche  
who  as  worships  Kṛṣṇa  him  worships  so

(177) Kṛṣṇa has a promise from of old: as one worships Him, so does He reciprocate.[^126]

যে যথা মাং প্রপদ্যন্তে তাংস্তথৈব ভজাম্যহম্ ৷  
মম বর্ত্মানুবর্ত্তন্তে মনুষ্যাঃ পার্থ সর্ব্বশঃ ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

ye yathā māṁ prapadyante  
who  as  to Me  surrender  
tāṁs tathaiva bhajāmy aham  
them  in that same way  I reward  I  
mama vartmānuvartante  
My  path  follow  
manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ  
men  O Pārtha  in every way

(178) “As people surrender to Me, so do I reward them. O Pārtha, all men follow My path in every way.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 4.11)[^127]

সে প্রতিজ্ঞা ভঙ্গ হৈল গোপীর ভজনে ৷  
তাহাতে প্রমাণ কৃষ্ণ-শ্রীমুখবচনে ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

se pratijñā bhaṅga haila gopīra bhajane  
that  promise  broken  was  by the gopīs’  worship  
tāhāte pramāṇa kṛṣṇa-śrī-mukha-vacane  
of that  the proof  in Kṛṣṇa’s  holy mouth’s  words

(179) That promise was broken by the gopīs’ worship, and the proof is in Kṛṣṇa’s own words:[^128]

ন পারয়েঽহং নিরবদ্যসংয়ুজাং  
স্বসাধুকৃত্যং বিবুধায়ুষাপি বঃ ৷  
যা মাভজন্ দুর্জ্জয়গেহশৃঙ্খলাঃ  
সংবৃশ্চ্য তদ্বঃ প্রতিযাতু সাধুনা ॥ ১৮০ ॥

na pāraye ’haṁ niravadya-saṁyujāṁ  
not  am able  I  of the faultlessly united  
sva-sādhu-kṛtyaṁ vibudhāyuṣāpi vaḥ  
own good deed  in a god’s lifetime  even  to you  
yā mābhajan durjaya-geha-śṛṅkhalāḥ  
who  Me  worshipped  hard to conquer  of home  the fetters  
saṁvṛścya tad vaḥ pratiyātu sādhunā  
completely cutting  that  your  let it be requited  by the good deed

(180) “O gopīs, your union with Me is faultless. Even in a lifetime of the gods I could not repay you for what you have done: you cut the fetters of home, so hard to break, and came to Me. So let your own good deed be your reward.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.32.22)[^129]

তবে যে দেখিয়ে গোপীর নিজদেহে প্রীত ৷  
সেহো ত’ কৃষ্ণের লাগি, জানিহ নিশ্চিত ॥ ১৮১ ॥

tabe ye dekhiye gopīra nija-dehe prīta  
then  that  we see  of the gopīs  for their own body  love  
seho ta’ kṛṣṇera lāgi, jāniha niścita  
that  indeed  of Kṛṣṇa  for the sake  know  certainly

(181) And if we see that the gopīs care for their own bodies, know for certain that this too is for Kṛṣṇa.[^130]

এই দেহ কৈলুঁ আমি কৃষ্ণে সমর্পণ ৷  
তাঁর ধন তাঁর এই সম্ভোগ-কারণ ॥ ১৮২ ॥

ei deha kailum̐ āmi kṛṣṇe samarpaṇa  
this  body  have made  I  to Kṛṣṇa  offering  
tām̐ra dhana tām̐ra ei sambhoga-kāraṇa  
His  property  His  this  of enjoyment  the means

(182) “This body I have offered to Kṛṣṇa. It is His property, the means of His enjoyment.

এদেহ-দর্শন-স্পর্শে কৃষ্ণ-সন্তোষণ ৷  
এই লাগি’ করে অঙ্গের মার্জ্জন-ভূষণ ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

edeha-darśana-sparśe kṛṣṇa-santoṣaṇa  
of this body  by seeing  touching  Kṛṣṇa’s  pleasure  
ei lāgi’ kare aṅgera mārjana-bhūṣaṇa  
for this  sake  they make  of the body  cleansing  adorning

(183) Seeing and touching this body gives Kṛṣṇa pleasure.” For this reason they cleanse and adorn their bodies.

নিজাঙ্গমপি যা গোপ্যো মমেতি সমুপাসতে ৷  
তাভ্যঃ পরং ন মে পার্থ নিগূঢ়প্রেমভাজনম্ ॥ ১৮৪ ॥

nijāṅgam api yā gopyo  
own body  even  who  the gopīs  
mameti samupāsate  
as Mine  so  carefully tend  
tābhyaḥ paraṁ na me pārtha  
than them  higher  not  of Me  O Pārtha  
nigūḍha-prema-bhājanam  
of hidden  love  the vessel

(184) “O Pārtha, the gopīs tend even their own bodies, for they regard them as Mine. No one is so secretly dear to Me as they are.” (Ādi Purāṇa; Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 40)[^131]

আর এক অদ্ভুত গোপীভাবের স্বভাব ৷  
বুদ্ধির গোচর নহে যাহার প্রভাব ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

āra eka adbhuta gopī-bhāvera svabhāva  
yet  one  wondrous  of the gopīs’ mood  the nature  
buddhira gocara nahe yāhāra prabhāva  
of the intellect  in the range  is not  of which  the power

(185) There is another wondrous trait of the gopīs’ love, whose power lies beyond the reach of the intellect.[^132]

গোপীগণ করেন যবে কৃষ্ণ দরশন ৷  
সুখবাঞ্ছা নাহি, সুখ হয় কোটিগুণ ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

gopī-gaṇa karena yabe kṛṣṇa daraśana  
the gopīs  make  when  of Kṛṣṇa  the sight  
sukha-vāñchā nāhi, sukha haya koṭi-guṇa  
of happiness  the desire  there is not  happiness  becomes  ten million times

(186) When the gopīs see Kṛṣṇa, they have no desire for happiness, and yet their happiness grows ten million times.

গোপিকা-দর্শনে কৃষ্ণের যে আনন্দ হয় ৷  
তাহা হৈতে কোটিগুণ গোপী আস্বাদয় ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

gopikā-darśane kṛṣṇera ye ānanda haya  
on seeing the gopīs  of Kṛṣṇa  which  joy  is  
tāhā haite koṭi-guṇa gopī āsvādaya  
that  than  ten million times  the gopīs  taste

(187) The joy Kṛṣṇa feels on seeing the gopīs — the gopīs taste ten million times more.

তাঁ-সবার নাহি নিজসুখ-অনুরোধ ৷  
তথাপি বাঢ়য়ে সুখ, পড়িল বিরোধ ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

tām̐-sabāra nāhi nija-sukha-anurodha  
of them all  there is not  own happiness  the concern  
tathāpi bāḍhaye sukha, paḍila virodha  
still  grows  happiness  arose  a contradiction

(188) They have no concern for their own happiness, and still their happiness grows — here is a contradiction.

এ বিরোধের এক মাত্র দেখি সমাধান ৷  
গোপিকার সুখে কৃষ্ণসুখ-পর্য্যবসান ॥ ১৮৯ ॥

e virodhera eka mātra dekhi samādhāna  
of this  contradiction  one  only  I see  solution  
gopikāra sukhe kṛṣṇa-sukha-paryavasāna  
of the gopīs  in the happiness  in Kṛṣṇa’s happiness  the culmination

(189) I see only one solution to this contradiction: the gopīs’ happiness finds its fulfilment in Kṛṣṇa’s happiness.[^133]

গোপিকা-দর্শনে কৃষ্ণের বাঢ়ে প্রফুল্লতা ৷  
সে মাধুর্য্য বাঢ়ে যার নাহিক সমতা ॥ ১৯০ ॥

gopikā-darśane kṛṣṇera bāḍhe praphullatā  
on seeing the gopīs  of Kṛṣṇa  grows  delight  
se mādhurya bāḍhe yāra nāhika samatā  
that  sweetness  grows  of which  there is not  equal

(190) On seeing the gopīs Kṛṣṇa’s delight grows, and His sweetness, which has no equal, grows too.

আমার দর্শনে কৃষ্ণ পাইল এত সুখ ৷  
এই সুখে গোপীর প্রফুল্ল অঙ্গমুখ ॥ ১৯১ ॥

āmāra darśane kṛṣṇa pāila eta sukha  
me  on seeing  Kṛṣṇa  received  so much  happiness  
ei sukhe gopīra praphulla aṅga-mukha  
by this  happiness  of the gopīs  blossom  bodies  faces

(191) “On seeing me Kṛṣṇa felt such happiness!” — with this happiness the gopīs’ faces and bodies blossom.

গোপী-শোভা দেখি’ কৃষ্ণের শোভা বাঢ়ে যত ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-শোভা দেখি’ গোপীর শোভা বাঢ়ে তত ॥ ১৯২ ॥

gopī-śobhā dekhi’ kṛṣṇera śobhā bāḍhe yata  
the gopīs’ beauty  seeing  of Kṛṣṇa  the beauty  grows  as much  
kṛṣṇa-śobhā dekhi’ gopīra śobhā bāḍhe tata  
Kṛṣṇa’s beauty  seeing  of the gopīs  the beauty  grows  so much

(192) The more Kṛṣṇa’s beauty grows on seeing the gopīs’ beauty, the more the gopīs’ beauty grows on seeing His.

এইমত পরস্পর পড়ে হুড়াহুড়ি ৷  
পরস্পর বাঢ়ে, কেহ মুখ নাহি মুড়ি ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

ei-mata paraspara paḍe huḍāhuḍi  
in this way  mutually  arises  a contest  
paraspara bāḍhe, keha mukha nāhi muḍi  
mutually  grow  neither  the face  not  turns away

(193) So a contest arises between them; both grow together, and neither turns away.

কিন্তু কৃষ্ণের সুখ হয় গোপী-রূপ-গুণে ৷  
তাঁর সুখে সুখবৃদ্ধি হয়ে গোপীগণে ॥ ১৯৪ ॥

kintu kṛṣṇera sukha haya gopī-rūpa-guṇe  
but  of Kṛṣṇa  the happiness  is  by the gopīs’ beauty and virtues  
tām̐ra sukhe sukha-vṛddhi haye gopī-gaṇe  
by His  happiness  of happiness  the increase  is  in the gopīs

(194) But Kṛṣṇa’s happiness comes from the gopīs’ beauty and virtues, and the gopīs’ happiness grows by His happiness.[^134]

অতএব সেই সুখ কৃষ্ণ-সুখ পোষে ৷  
এই হেতু গোপী-প্রেমে নাহি কাম-দোষে ॥ ১৯৫ ॥

ataeva sei sukha kṛṣṇa-sukha poṣe  
therefore  that  happiness  Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  nourishes  
ei hetu gopī-preme nāhi kāma-doṣe  
this  for the reason  in the gopīs’ love  there is not  of lust  fault

(195) Therefore their happiness only nourishes Kṛṣṇa’s happiness, and so in the gopīs’ love there is no fault of lust.[^135]

উপেত্য পথি সুন্দরীততিভিরাভিরভ্যর্চ্চিতং  
স্মিতাঙ্কুরকরম্বিতৈর্নটদপাঙ্গভঙ্গীশতৈঃ ৷  
স্তনস্তবকসঞ্চরন্নয়নচঞ্চরীকাঞ্চলং  
ব্রজে বিজয়িনং ভজে বিপিনদেশতঃ কেশবম্ ॥ ১৯৬ ॥

upetya pathi sundarī-tatibhir ābhir abhyarcitaṁ  
approaching  on the road  of beauties  by the rows  by these  worshipped  
smitāṅkura-karambitair naṭad-apāṅga-bhaṅgī-śataiḥ  
with buds of smiles  mingled  by the dancing  side-glances  in hundreds of turns  
stana-stavaka-sañcaran-nayana-cañcarīkāñcalaṁ  
on the clusters of the breasts  moving  of the eyes  bee-like  corners  
vraje vijayinaṁ bhaje vipina-deśataḥ keśavam  
to Vraja  returning victorious  I worship  from the forest  Keśava

(196) “I worship Keśava, returning victorious from the forest to Vraja. Rows of beautiful girls come out on the road to worship Him with hundreds of dancing side-glances mingled with budding smiles, and the corners of His eyes, like bees, wander over the flower-clusters of their breasts.” (Stava-mālā, Keśavāṣṭaka, 8)[^136]

আর এক গোপীপ্রেমের স্বাভাবিক চিহ্ন ৷  
যে প্রকারে হয় প্রেম কামগন্ধহীন ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

āra eka gopī-premera svābhāvika cihna  
yet  one  of the gopīs’ love  natural  sign  
ye prakāre haya prema kāma-gandha-hīna  
by which  way  is  love  of lust’s scent  devoid

(197) There is another natural sign of the gopīs’ love, which shows that it has no trace of lust.[^137]

গোপীপ্রেমে করে কৃষ্ণমাধুর্য্যের পুষ্টি ৷  
মাধুর্য্য বাঢ়ায় প্রেম হঞা মহাতুষ্টি ॥ ১৯৮ ॥

gopī-preme kare kṛṣṇa-mādhuryera puṣṭi  
the gopīs’ love  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  the nourishment  
mādhurya bāḍhāya prema hañā mahā-tuṣṭi  
the sweetness  increases  love  becoming  great satisfaction

(198) The gopīs’ love nourishes Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness, and that sweetness in turn makes their love grow, to their great delight.[^138]

প্রীতিবিষয়ানন্দে তদাশ্রয়ানন্দ ৷  
তাঁহা নাহি নিজসুখবাঞ্ছার সম্বন্ধ ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

prīti-viṣayānande tad-āśrayānanda  
of love  of the object  in the bliss  its  of the shelter  the bliss  
tām̐hā nāhi nija-sukha-vāñchāra sambandha  
there  there is not  own happiness  of the desire  connection

(199) The bliss of the *āśraya* of love lies in the bliss of its *viṣaya*; there is no connection with the desire for one’s own happiness.[^139]

নিরুপাধি প্রেম যাঁহা, তাঁহা এই রীতি ৷  
প্রীতিবিষয়সুখে আশ্রয়ের প্রীতি ॥ ২০০ ॥

nirupādhi prema yām̐hā, tām̐hā ei rīti  
unconditional  love  where  there  this  law  
prīti-viṣaya-sukhe āśrayera prīti  
of love  of the object  in the happiness  of the shelter  the love

(200) Wherever love is unconditional, this is the law: the joy of the *āśraya* lies in the happiness of the *viṣaya*.[^140]

নিজ-প্রেমানন্দে কৃষ্ণ-সেবানন্দ বাধে ৷  
সে আনন্দের প্রতি ভক্তের হয় মহাক্রোধে ॥ ২০১ ॥

nija-premānande kṛṣṇa-sevānanda bādhe  
by own  love’s bliss  of Kṛṣṇa’s  service’s bliss  is hindered  
se ānandera prati bhaktera haya mahā-krodhe  
that  bliss  toward  of the devotee  is  great anger

(201) If the bliss of one’s own love hinders the bliss of serving Kṛṣṇa, the devotee is filled with great anger at that bliss.[^141]

অঙ্গস্তম্ভারম্ভমুত্তুঙ্গয়ন্তং  
প্রেমানন্দং দারুকো নাভ্যনন্দৎ ৷  
কংসারাতের্বীজনে যেন সাক্ষা-  
দক্ষোদীয়ানন্তরায়ো ব্যধায়ি ॥ ২০২ ॥

aṅga-stambhārambham uttuṅgayantaṁ  
of the body  stupor  the onset  intensifying  
premānandaṁ dāruko nābhyanandat  
the bliss of love  Dāruka  not  welcomed  
kaṁsārāter vījane yena sākṣād  
of the enemy of Kaṁsa  in the fanning  by which  directly  
akṣodīyān antarāyo vyadhāyi  
no small  obstacle  was created

(202) “Dāruka did not welcome the bliss of love that brought on a stupor of his body, for it put no small obstacle in the way of his fanning Kṛṣṇa, the enemy of Kaṁsa.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 3.2.62)[^142]

[^1]: In the edition the verse is headed “By the grace of Gaura, the nature of Śrī Kṛṣṇa ascertained”. *Anubhāṣya*: “a child” — one who knows nothing; “His form” — the form of Gaura, who is not different from Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa; “the Enjoyer in Vraja” — the son of the king of Vraja; “ascertainment” — pointing out the truth (*tattva*).

[^2]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «এতো» (another reading: «এহো», “this too”); the sense is the same. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Gaurāṅga descended to preach bhakti and the name of Kṛṣṇa; that reason is true, but it is external, not the hidden one.

[^3]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «হেন» (“so”); another reading has «যেন» (“as”); the sense is the same.

[^4]: In the edition the verse is headed: “Viṣṇu’s work is the deliverance of the saints and the destruction of the wicked; it is not the work of Kṛṣṇa Himself.”

[^5]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Catur-vyūha are Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha; Matsya and the others are partial avatāras (*aṁśāvatāra*). All the avatāras are parts of the complete Bhagavān and take shelter in Him, and so Viṣṇu, the maintainer of the world, was then present within the form of Kṛṣṇa.

[^6]: In the edition the verse is headed: “Viṣṇu descends to preach *vidhi-bhakti*; Kṛṣṇa comes as Gaura to preach *rāga-bhakti*.”

[^7]: The same verse as Ādi 3.16 (the *Anubhāṣya* refers to it). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in love born of the awareness of majesty prema slackens, and Kṛṣṇa takes no pleasure in it; one who thinks himself lowly and Him the Lord loves Him with that awareness, and to such love He is not subject.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord speaks this verse about the cause of His manifest pastimes; “I reward” — “I show favour”; “My path” — the path of perfection. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “O Pārtha, in whatever way one worships Me, in that way I become attainable to him; all men follow the path shown by Me.”

[^9]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: pure devotion (*śuddha-bhakti*) is the cultivation of Kṛṣṇa with a favourable intent, free of other desires and uncovered by knowledge and fruitive work. *Anubhāṣya*: devotion mixed with other desires, knowledge, work or yoga is “mixed devotion” (*viddha-bhakti*); devotion by rule (*vaidhī*) assists pure devotion, but the word *śuddha-bhakti* points to the service of the *rāgānugā* path — the devotion of Goloka, full of rāga, while *vaidhī-bhakti*, full of reverence, belongs to Vaikuṇṭha.

[^10]: Kṛṣṇa’s words to the gopīs at Kurukṣetra (Samanta-pañcaka), where, at a solar eclipse, both the Yādavas from Dvārakā and Nanda Mahārāja with all Vraja had come. *Anubhāṣya*: “immortality” — eternal service as the Lord’s associate; their love “brings Me” — grants direct meeting with Him; “by good fortune” — “by My good fortune”.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: the rebuke with which the lover (*āśraya*), in pure attachment, chides the Beloved (*viṣaya*) is a sign of utmost, exclusive love; where the Beloved is honoured as worshipful, natural love slackens, and so such rebuke stands higher than the awe-filled praise of the Vedas.

[^12]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the pastimes not found in Vaikuṇṭha and the other worlds Kṛṣṇa will show in this descent, and in them He Himself will be astonished. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vaikuṇṭha and the other realms beyond māyā the Lord’s pastimes do not give Him Himself the ever-new joy of wonder; the excellence of the pastimes of Goloka, where He lives for His own joy, He wishes to show in the world to devotees in whom the sense of majesty prevails.

[^13]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Yogamāyā is Kṛṣṇa’s own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*); by His will and by her inconceivable power she awakens in the hearts of His eternal beloveds, the gopīs, the mood of *upapati* toward Him. *Anubhāṣya*: the tendency to *parakīyā* service, in which the lover, drawn by a sweetness (*mādhurya*) that surpasses majesty, passes beyond all ordained reverence, comes from Yogamāyā; this working of the spiritual (*cinmayī*) māyā is unknown even to the Beloved Himself, and is possible only through Yogamāyā, the embodiment of His grace.

[^14]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: for the nourishment of rasa My inconceivable potency will cover My omniscience and give rise to a wondrous rasa, and the gopīs, though they are the Lord’s own potency, will not know it either.

[^15]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the pure rāga that Kṛṣṇa will show in Vraja is the means by which He gives rasa to all devotees: hearing of it, they will leave *dharma-karma* and worship Him on the path of rāga.

[^16]: In the edition the verse is headed: “Only the liberated soul, perfected on the path of *rāgānugā*, is fit to hear the transcendental *rāsa-līlā*.” *Anubhāṣya*: this is Śukadeva’s answer to Parīkṣit’s question about the truth and the need of Kṛṣṇa’s *parakīyā* pastimes; “a human body” — the supreme, transcendental body that resembles a human one; “having heard” — even an outsider, gaining faith in the Lord, must become devoted to Kṛṣṇa’s service. One who, not understanding the transcendental nature of these pastimes, takes “such pastimes” for worldly enjoyment only distorts their meaning (as the *prākṛta-sahajiyās* do); the principal sense of the word “such” is transcendental love (*rati*).

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: seeing an injunction in the word *bhavet*, let no one think that the path of *rāgānugā*, open only to those who have gained a taste (*ruci*), is a path of rules open to anyone, even to one full of *anarthas* and seeking enjoyment. Injunctions and prohibitions belong to this world; in Goloka-Vṛndāvana there are none — there all who have taken shelter, moved by longing, seek only one thing: Kṛṣṇa’s pleasure.
