“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 17  
**The Journey to Śrī Vṛndāvana**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

গচ্ছন্ বৃন্দাবনং গৌরো  
ব্যাঘ্রেভৈণখগান্ বনে ৷  
প্রেমোন্মত্তান্ সহোন্নৃত্যান্  
বিদধে কৃষ্ণজল্পিনঃ ॥ ১ ॥

gacchan vṛndāvanaṁ gauro  
going  to Vṛndāvana  Gaura  
vyāghrebhaiṇa-khagān vane  
tigers, elephants, deer and birds  in the forest  
premonmattān sahonnṛtyān  
maddened with love  dancing along with Him  
vidadhe kṛṣṇa-jalpinaḥ  
made  chanting “Kṛṣṇa”

(1) As Gaura went to Vṛndāvana, He made the tigers, elephants, deer and birds of the forest mad with love: they danced along with Him and chanted the name of Kṛṣṇa.[^1]

জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

শরৎকাল হৈল, প্রভুর চলিতে হৈল মতি ৷  
রামানন্দ-স্বরূপ-সঙ্গে নিভৃতে যুকতি ॥ ৩ ॥

śarat-kāla haila, prabhura calite haila mati  
autumn  came  the Lord’s  to set out  arose  the intention  
rāmānanda-svarūpa-saṅge nibhṛte yukati  
with Rāmānanda and Svarūpa  in private  consultation

(3) When autumn came, the Lord resolved to set out, and He took counsel in private with Rāmānanda and Svarūpa.

মোর সহায় কর যদি, তুমি-দুই জন ৷  
তবে আমি যাঞা দেখি শ্রীবৃন্দাবন ॥ ৪ ॥

mora sahāya kara yadi, tumi-dui jana  
to Me  help  you give  if  you two  persons  
tabe āmi yāñā dekhi śrī-vṛndāvana  
then  I  going  may see  Śrī Vṛndāvana

(4) “If you two will help Me, then I can go and see Śrī Vṛndāvana.

রাত্রে উঠি’ বনপথে পলাঞা যাব ৷  
একাকী যাইব, কাঁহো সঙ্গে না লইব ॥ ৫ ॥

rātre uṭhi’ vana-pathe palāñā yāba  
at night  rising  by the forest path  slipping away  I shall go  
ekākī yāiba, kām̐ho saṅge nā laiba  
alone  I shall go  anyone  with Me  not  I shall take

(5) I shall rise at night and slip away by the forest path. I shall go alone and take no one with Me.

কেহ যদি সঙ্গ লইতে পাছে উঠি’ ধায় ৷  
সবারে রাখিবা, যেন কেহ নাহি যায় ॥ ৬ ॥

keha yadi saṅga laite pāche uṭhi’ dhāya  
anyone  if  company  to keep  after Me  rising  runs  
sabāre rākhibā, yena keha nāhi yāya  
all  you will hold back  so that  anyone  not  goes

(6) If anyone gets up and runs after Me to keep Me company, hold them all back, so that no one follows.

প্রসন্ন হঞা আজ্ঞা দিবা, না মানিবা ‘দুঃখ’ ৷  
তোমা-সবার ‘সুখে’ পথে হবে মোর ‘সুখ’ ॥ ৭ ॥

prasanna hañā ājñā dibā, nā mānibā ‘duḥkha’  
pleased  being  leave  give  not  consider it  a sorrow  
tomā-sabāra ‘sukhe’ pathe habe mora ‘sukha’  
of you all  in the happiness  on the road  will be  My  happiness

(7) Give Me leave gladly, and do not take it as a sorrow. If you are all happy, I shall be happy on the road.”

দুইজন কহে, — তুমি ঈশ্বর ‘স্বতন্ত্র’ ৷  
যেই ইচ্ছা, সেই করিবা, নহ ‘পরতন্ত্র’ ॥ ৮ ॥

dui-jana kahe, — tumi īśvara ‘svatantra’  
the two  say  —  You  the Lord  independent  
yei icchā, sei karibā, naha ‘paratantra’  
whatever  You wish  that  You will do  You are not  dependent

(8) The two of them said, “You are the independent Lord. Whatever You wish, You will do; You depend on no one.

কিন্তু আমা-দুঁহার শুন এক নিবেদনে ৷  
তোমার সুখে আমার সুখ — কহিলা আপনে ॥ ৯ ॥

kintu āmā-dum̐hāra śuna eka nivedane  
but  of us two  hear  one  request  
tomāra sukhe āmāra sukha — kahilā āpane  
in your  happiness  My  happiness  —  You said  Yourself

(9) But hear one request from the two of us. You Yourself have said, ‘In your happiness is My happiness.’

আমা-দুঁহার মনে তবে বড় ‘সুখ’ হয় ৷  
এক নিবেদন যদি ধর, দয়াময় ॥ ১০ ॥

āmā-dum̐hāra mane tabe baḍa ‘sukha’ haya  
of us two  in the mind  then  great  happiness  will be  
eka nivedana yadi dhara, dayāmaya  
one  request  if  You accept  O merciful one

(10) Our hearts will be very happy, O merciful Lord, if You grant us one request.

‘উত্তম ব্রাহ্মণ’ এক সঙ্গে অবশ্য চাহি ৷  
ভিক্ষা করি’ ভিক্ষা দিবে, যাবে পাত্র বহি’ ॥ ১১ ॥

‘uttama brāhmaṇa’ eka saṅge avaśya cāhi  
a worthy  brāhmaṇa  one  with You  certainly  we want  
bhikṣā kari’ bhikṣā dibe, yābe pātra vahi’  
alms  collecting  meals  he will give  he will go  the waterpot  carrying

(11) We certainly want one good brāhmaṇa to go with You. He will collect alms and give You Your meals, and he will carry Your waterpot.

বনপথে যাইতে নাহি ‘ভোজ্যান্ন’ ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
আজ্ঞা কর, — সঙ্গে চলুক বিপ্র একজন ॥ ১২ ॥

vana-pathe yāite nāhi ‘bhojyānna’ brāhmaṇa  
on the forest path  going  there is no  whose food may be accepted  brāhmaṇa  
ājñā kara, — saṅge caluka vipra eka-jana  
give the order  —  with You  let go  a brāhmaṇa  one

(12) On the forest path there is no brāhmaṇa whose food You could accept. Permit one brāhmaṇa to go with You.”

প্রভু কহে, — নিজ-সঙ্গী কাঁহো না লইব ৷  
একজনে নিলে, আনের মনে দুঃখ হইব ॥ ১৩ ॥

prabhu kahe, — nija-saṅgī kām̐ho nā laiba  
the Lord  says  —  of My own companions  anyone  not  I shall take  
eka-jane nile, ānera mane duḥkha haiba  
one person  if I take  of the others  in the mind  sorrow  will be

(13) The Lord said, “I shall not take any of My own companions. If I take one, the others will be unhappy.

নূতন সঙ্গী হইবেক, — স্নিগ্ধ যাঁর মন ৷  
ঐছে যবে পাই, তবে লই ‘এক’ জন ॥ ১৪ ॥

nūtana saṅgī haibeka, — snigdha yām̐ra mana  
new  the companion  should be  —  loving  whose  mind  
aiche yabe pāi, tabe lai ‘eka’ jana  
such  when  I find  then  I take  one  person

(14) Let My companion be someone new, whose heart is full of love. If I find such a person, I will take that one alone.”[^3]

স্বরূপ কহে, — এই বলভদ্র-ভট্টাচার্য্য ৷  
তোমাতে সুস্নিগ্ধ বড়, পণ্ডিত, সাধু, আর্য্য ॥ ১৫ ॥

svarūpa kahe, — ei balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārya  
Svarūpa  says  —  here is  Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya  
tomāte su-snigdha baḍa, paṇḍita, sādhu, ārya  
toward You  affectionate  very  learned  saintly  noble

(15) Svarūpa said, “Here is Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya. He is full of love for You, and he is learned, saintly and noble.

প্রথমেই তোমা-সঙ্গে আইলা গৌড় হৈতে ৷  
ইঁহার ইচ্ছা আছে ‘সর্ব্বতীর্থ’ করিতে ॥ ১৬ ॥

prathamei tomā-saṅge āilā gauḍa haite  
at the very first  with You  he came  Gauḍa  from  
im̐hāra icchā āche ‘sarva-tīrtha’ karite  
his  desire  is  all the holy places  to visit

(16) He came with You from Gauḍa at the very start, and he wishes to visit all the holy places.[^4]

ইঁহার সঙ্গে আছে বিপ্র এক ‘ভৃত্য’ ৷  
ইঁহো পথে করিবেন সেবাভিক্ষা-কৃত্য ॥ ১৭ ॥

im̐hāra saṅge āche vipra eka ‘bhṛtya’  
with him  together  there is  a brāhmaṇa  one  servant  
im̐ho pathe karibena sevābhikṣā-kṛtya  
he  on the road  will perform  the duties of service and meals

(17) With him is a brāhmaṇa servant, and on the road the Bhaṭṭācārya himself will serve You and see to Your meals.

ইঁহারে সঙ্গে লহ যদি, সবার হয় ‘সুখ’ ৷  
বন-পথে যাইতে তোমার নহিবে কোন ‘দুঃখ’ ॥ ১৮ ॥

im̐hāre saṅge laha yadi, sabāra haya ‘sukha’  
him  with You  You take  if  of everyone  is  happiness  
vana-pathe yāite tomāra nahibe kona ‘duḥkha’  
on the forest path  going  Your  will not be  any  hardship

(18) If You take him with You, everyone will be happy, and You will meet no hardship on the forest path.

সেই বিপ্র বহি’ নিবে বস্ত্রাম্বুভাজন ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য ভিক্ষা দিবে করি’ ভিক্ষাটন ॥ ১৯ ॥

sei vipra vahi’ nibe vastrāmbu-bhājana  
that  brāhmaṇa  carrying  will take  the cloth and the waterpot  
bhaṭṭācārya bhikṣā dibe kari’ bhikṣāṭana  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  meals  will give  doing  the rounds for alms

(19) That brāhmaṇa will carry Your cloth and waterpot, and the Bhaṭṭācārya will go begging alms and give You Your meals.”

তাঁহার বচন প্রভু অঙ্গীকার কৈল ৷  
বলভদ্র-ভট্টাচার্য্যে সঙ্গে করি’ নিল ॥ ২০ ॥

tām̐hāra vacana prabhu aṅgīkāra kaila  
his  words  the Lord  acceptance  made  
balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārye saṅge kari’ nila  
Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya  as a companion  making  took

(20) The Lord accepted Svarūpa’s words and took Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya with Him.

পূর্ব্বরাত্রে জগন্নাথ দেখি’ ‘আজ্ঞা’ লঞা ৷  
শেষ-রাত্রে উঠি’ প্রভু চলিলা লুকাঞা ॥ ২১ ॥

pūrva-rātre jagannātha dekhi’ ‘ājñā’ lañā  
the night before  Jagannātha  seeing  permission  taking  
śeṣa-rātre uṭhi’ prabhu calilā lukāñā  
at the end of the night  rising  the Lord  set out  in secret

(21) The night before, He saw Jagannātha and received His permission. Then, rising at the end of the night, the Lord set out in secret.

প্রাতঃকালে ভক্তগণ প্রভু না দেখিয়া ৷  
অন্বেষণ করি’ ফিরে ব্যাকুল হঞা ॥ ২২ ॥

prātaḥ-kāle bhakta-gaṇa prabhu nā dekhiyā  
in the morning  the devotees  the Lord  not  seeing  
anveṣaṇa kari’ phire vyākula hañā  
search  making  wander about  distressed  becoming

(22) In the morning the devotees could not find the Lord, and they went about searching for Him in distress.

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি সবায় কৈল নিবারণ ৷  
নিবৃত্ত হঞা রহে সবে, জানি’ প্রভুর মন ॥ ২৩ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi sabāya kaila nivāraṇa  
Svarūpa Gosāñi  everyone  made  restraint  
nivṛtta hañā rahe sabe, jāni’ prabhura mana  
desisting  becoming  remain  all  knowing  the Lord’s  mind

(23) Svarūpa Gosāñi held them all back, and knowing the Lord’s mind, they all gave up the search.

প্রসিদ্ধ পথ ছাড়ি’ প্রভু উপপথে চলিলা ৷  
‘কটক’ ডাহিনে করি’ বনে প্রবেশিলা ॥ ২৪ ॥

prasiddha patha chāḍi’ prabhu upapathe calilā  
the well-known  road  leaving  the Lord  by a side path  went  
‘kaṭaka’ ḍāhine kari’ vane praveśilā  
Kaṭaka  on the right  keeping  into the forest  He entered

(24) Leaving the main road, the Lord went by a side path. Keeping Kaṭaka on His right, He entered the forest.

নির্জ্জন-বনে চলে প্রভু কৃষ্ণনাম লঞা ৷  
হস্তী-ব্যাঘ্র পথ ছাড়ে প্রভুরে দেখিয়া ॥ ২৫ ॥

nirjana-vane cale prabhu kṛṣṇa-nāma lañā  
through the lonely forest  goes  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa’s name  taking  
hastī-vyāghra patha chāḍe prabhure dekhiyā  
elephants and tigers  the path  leave  the Lord  seeing

(25) The Lord went through the lonely forest chanting Kṛṣṇa’s name, and elephants and tigers left the path when they saw Him.

পালে-পালে ব্যাঘ্র, হস্তী, গণ্ডার, শূকরগণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে আবেশে প্রভু করিলা গমন ॥ ২৬ ॥

pāle-pāle vyāghra, hastī, gaṇḍāra, śūkara-gaṇa  
herd upon herd  tigers  elephants  rhinoceroses  boars  
tāra madhye āveśe prabhu karilā gamana  
of them  in the midst  in ecstasy  the Lord  made  His way

(26) There were herds of tigers, elephants, rhinoceroses and boars, and the Lord, absorbed in ecstasy, made His way through their midst.

দেখি’ ভট্টাচার্য্যের মনে হয় মহাভয় ৷  
প্রভুর প্রতাপে তারা এক পাশ হয় ॥ ২৭ ॥

dekhi’ bhaṭṭācāryera mane haya mahā-bhaya  
seeing  in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind  arises  great fear  
prabhura pratāpe tārā eka pāśa haya  
by the Lord’s  power  they  to one  side  move

(27) Seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was terrified, but by the Lord’s power the animals stood aside.

একদিন পথে ব্যাঘ্র করিয়াছে শয়ন ৷  
আবেশে তার গায়ে প্রভুর লাগিল চরণ ॥ ২৮ ॥

ekadina pathe vyāghra kariyāche śayana  
one day  on the path  a tiger  had made  lying down  
āveśe tāra gāye prabhura lāgila caraṇa  
in ecstasy  its  against the body  the Lord’s  touched  foot

(28) One day a tiger was lying on the path, and the Lord, lost in ecstasy, touched its body with His foot.

প্রভু কহে, — কহ ‘কৃষ্ণ’, ব্যাঘ্র উঠিল ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি’ ব্যাঘ্র নাচিতে লাগিল ॥ ২৯ ॥

prabhu kahe, — kaha ‘kṛṣṇa’, vyāghra uṭhila  
the Lord  says  —  say  “Kṛṣṇa”  the tiger  rose  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahi’ vyāghra nācite lāgila  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  saying  the tiger  to dance  began

(29) The Lord said, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!” The tiger rose and began to dance, saying, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”

আর দিনে মহাপ্রভু করে নদীস্নান ৷  
মত্তহস্তীযূথ আইল করিতে জলপান ॥ ৩০ ॥

āra dine mahāprabhu kare nadī-snāna  
on another  day  Mahāprabhu  takes  a bath in the river  
matta-hastī-yūtha āila karite jala-pāna  
a herd of maddened elephants  came  to do  drinking of water

(30) On another day, while Mahāprabhu was bathing in a river, a herd of maddened elephants came to drink.

প্রভু জলে কৃত্য করেন, আগে হস্তী আইলা ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ কহ’ বলি’ প্রভু জল ফেলি’ মারিলা ॥ ৩১ ॥

prabhu jale kṛtya karena, āge hastī āilā  
the Lord  in the water  His rites  performs  before Him  the elephants  came  
‘kṛṣṇa kaha’ bali’ prabhu jala pheli’ mārilā  
“Kṛṣṇa  say”  saying  the Lord  water  throwing  splashed

(31) As the Lord performed His rites in the water, the elephants came before Him. Crying, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!”, the Lord splashed water at them.[^5]

সেই-জল-বিন্দু-কণা লাগে যাঁর গায় ৷  
সেই ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহে, প্রেমে নাচে, গায় ॥ ৩২ ॥

sei-jala-bindu-kaṇā lāge yām̐ra gāya  
of that water drops sprays  touch  whose  body  
sei ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahe, preme nāce, gāya  
he  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  says  in love  dances  sings

(32) Whoever was touched by a spray of those drops cried, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” and danced and sang in love.

কেহ ভূমে পড়ে, কেহ করয়ে চিৎকার ৷  
দেখি’ ভট্টাচার্য্যের মনে হয় চমৎকার ॥ ৩৩ ॥

keha bhūme paḍe, keha karaye citkāra  
some  on the ground  fall  some  make  loud cries  
dekhi’ bhaṭṭācāryera mane haya camatkāra  
seeing  in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind  arises  wonder

(33) Some fell to the ground, and some trumpeted loudly. Seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was struck with wonder.

পথে যাইতে করে প্রভু উচ্চ-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
মধুর কণ্ঠধ্বনি শুনি’ আইসে মৃগীগণ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

pathe yāite kare prabhu ucca-saṅkīrtana  
on the road  going  does  the Lord  loud saṅkīrtana  
madhura kaṇṭha-dhvani śuni’ āise mṛgī-gaṇa  
sweet  the sound of His voice  hearing  come  the does

(34) As He went along the road, the Lord would sing the holy name aloud, and hearing His sweet voice, the does came to Him.

ডাহিনে-বামে ধ্বনি শুনি’ যায় প্রভু-সঙ্গে ৷  
প্রভু তার অঙ্গ মুছে, শ্লোক পড়ে রঙ্গে ॥ ৩৫ ॥

ḍāhine-vāme dhvani śuni’ yāya prabhu-saṅge  
right and left  the sound  hearing  go  with the Lord  
prabhu tāra aṅga muche, śloka paḍe raṅge  
the Lord  their  bodies  strokes  a verse  recites  joyfully

(35) Listening to the sound, they walked with the Lord on His right and left. The Lord stroked them and joyfully recited a verse:

ধন্যাঃ স্ম মূঢ়মতয়োঽপি হরিণ্য এতা  
যা নন্দনন্দনমুপাত্ত-বিচিত্রবেশম্ ৷  
আকর্ণ্য বেণুরণিতং সহকৃষ্ণসারাঃ  
পূজাং দধুর্বিরচিতাং প্রণয়াবলোকৈঃ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

dhanyāḥ sma mūḍha-matayo ’pi hariṇya etā  
blessed  indeed  foolish-minded  though  does  these  
yā nanda-nandanam upātta-vicitraveśam  
who  to the son of Nanda  who had put on wondrous attire  
ākarṇya veṇu-raṇitaṁ saha-kṛṣṇa-sārāḥ  
hearing  the sound of the flute  together with the black bucks  
pūjāṁ dadhur viracitāṁ praṇayāvalokaiḥ  
worship  offered  arranged  with loving glances

(36) “How blessed are these does! Foolish though they are, on hearing the flute, they and their mates, the black bucks, have offered the wondrously attired son of Nanda a worship of loving glances.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.11)[^6]

হেনকালে ব্যাঘ্র তথা আইল পাঁচ-সাত ৷  
ব্যাঘ্র-মৃগী মিলি’ চলে মহাপ্রভুর সাথ ॥ ৩৭ ॥

hena-kāle vyāghra tathā āila pām̐ca-sāta  
at that time  tigers  there  came  five or seven  
vyāghra-mṛgī mili’ cale mahāprabhura sātha  
tigers and does  together  go  Mahāprabhu’s  company

(37) Just then five or seven tigers came there, and tigers and does walked together with Mahāprabhu.

দেখি’ মহাপ্রভুর ‘বৃন্দাবন’-স্মৃতি হৈল ৷  
বৃন্দাবন-গুণ-বর্ণন শ্লোক পড়িল ॥ ৩৮ ॥

dekhi’ mahāprabhura ‘vṛndāvana’-smṛti haila  
seeing  in Mahāprabhu  of Vṛndāvana  remembrance  arose  
vṛndāvana-guṇa-varṇana śloka paḍila  
Vṛndāvana’s  qualities  describing  a verse  He recited

(38) Seeing this, Mahāprabhu remembered Vṛndāvana, and He recited a verse describing its glories:

যত্র নৈসর্গদুর্ব্বৈরাঃ সহাসন্ নৃ-মৃগাদয়ঃ ৷  
মিত্রাণীবাজিতাবাস-দ্রুত-রুট্তর্ষণাদিকম্ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

yatra naisarga-durvairāḥ  
where  by nature bitter enemies  
sahāsan nṛ-mṛgādayaḥ  
lived together  men, beasts and others  
mitrāṇīvājitāvāsa-  
like friends  the abode of the Unconquered  
druta-ruṭ-tarṣaṇādikam  
fled away  anger, thirst and the like

(39) “There, in the abode of the Unconquered Lord, men and beasts, enemies by nature, lived together like friends, and anger, thirst and the like had fled away.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.13.60)[^7]

‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহ করি’ প্রভু যবে বলিল ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি’ ব্যাঘ্র-মৃগ নাচিতে লাগিল ॥ ৪০ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kaha kari’ prabhu yabe balila  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  say  thus  the Lord  when  said  
‘kṛṣṇa’ kahi’ vyāghra-mṛga nācite lāgila  
Kṛṣṇa  saying  the tigers and deer  to dance  began

(40) When the Lord said, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!’” the tigers and deer began to dance, saying, “Kṛṣṇa!”

নাচে, কান্দে ব্যাঘ্রগণ মৃগীগণ-সঙ্গে ৷  
বলভদ্র-ভট্টাচার্য্য দেখে অপূর্ব্ব-রঙ্গে ॥ ৪১ ॥

nāce, kānde vyāghra-gaṇa mṛgī-gaṇa-saṅge  
dance  weep  the tigers  together with the does  
balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārya dekhe apūrva-raṅge  
Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya  watches  with wonder at the unprecedented sport

(41) The tigers danced and wept together with the does, and Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya watched, amazed at this wondrous play.

ব্যাঘ্র-মৃগ অন্যোন্যে করে আলিঙ্গন ৷  
মুখে মুখ দিয়া করে অন্যোন্যে চুম্বন ॥ ৪২ ॥

vyāghra-mṛga anyonye kare āliṅgana  
tigers and deer  one another  do  embracing  
mukhe mukha diyā kare anyonye cumbana  
to mouth  mouth  putting  do  one another  kissing

(42) The tigers and deer embraced one another, and putting mouth to mouth, they kissed.

কৌতুক দেখিয়া প্রভু হাসিতে লাগিলা ৷  
তা-সবাকে তাঁহা ছাড়ি’ আগে চলি’ গেলা ॥ ৪৩ ॥

kautuka dekhiyā prabhu hāsite lāgilā  
the fun  seeing  the Lord  to laugh  began  
tā-sabāke tām̐hā chāḍi’ āge cali’ gelā  
them all  there  leaving  ahead  going  He went

(43) Seeing this delightful scene, the Lord began to laugh. Then He left them all there and went on ahead.

ময়ূরাদি পক্ষিগণ প্রভুরে দেখিয়া ৷  
সঙ্গে চলে, ‘কৃষ্ণ’ বলি’ নাচে মত্ত হঞা ॥ ৪৪ ॥

mayūrādi pakṣi-gaṇa prabhure dekhiyā  
peacocks and other  birds  the Lord  seeing  
saṅge cale, ‘kṛṣṇa’ bali’ nāce matta hañā  
along with Him  go  Kṛṣṇa  saying  dance  maddened  becoming

(44) At the sight of the Lord, peacocks and other birds went along with Him, crying “Kṛṣṇa” and dancing as if mad.

‘হরিবোল’ বলি’ প্রভু করে উচ্চধ্বনি ৷  
বৃক্ষলতা — প্রফুল্লিত, সেই ধ্বনি শুনি’ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

‘hari-bola’ bali’ prabhu kare ucca-dhvani  
“Hari bol”  saying  the Lord  makes  a loud call  
vṛkṣa-latā — praphullita, sei dhvani śuni’  
trees and creepers  —  rejoice  that  call  hearing

(45) The Lord called aloud, “Hari bol!” and the trees and creepers rejoiced to hear it.

‘ঝারিখণ্ডে’ স্থাবর-জঙ্গম আছে যত ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম দিয়া কৈল প্রেমেতে উন্মত্ত ॥ ৪৬ ॥

‘jhārikhaṇḍe’ sthāvara-jaṅgama āche yata  
in Jhārikhaṇḍa  the stationary and the moving  are  as many as  
kṛṣṇa-nāma diyā kaila premete unmatta  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  giving  He made  with love  mad

(46) Giving Kṛṣṇa’s name to all the moving and nonmoving beings of Jhārikhaṇḍa, He made them mad with love.[^8]

যেই গ্রাম দিয়া যান, যাঁহা করেন স্থিতি ৷  
সে-সব গ্রামের লোকের হয় ‘প্রেমভক্তি’ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

yei grāma diyā yāna, yām̐hā karena sthiti  
which  village  through  He goes  where  He makes  a stay  
se-saba grāmera lokera haya ‘prema-bhakti’  
of all those  villages  in the people  arises  loving devotion

(47) In every village He passed through or stayed in, the people gained loving devotion, *prema-bhakti*.

কেহ যদি তাঁর মুখে শুনে কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
তাঁর মুখে আন শুনে, তাঁর মুখে আন ॥ ৪৮ ॥

সবে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ বলি’ নাচে, কান্দে, হাসে ৷  
পরম্পরায় ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হইল সর্ব্বদেশে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

keha yadi tām̐ra mukhe śune kṛṣṇa-nāma  
anyone  if  His  from the mouth  hears  Kṛṣṇa’s name  
tām̐ra mukhe āna śune, tām̐ra mukhe āna  
his  from the mouth  another  hears  his  from the mouth  another  
sabe ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’ bali’ nāce, kānde, hāse  
all  Kṛṣṇa  Hari  saying  dance  weep  laugh  
paramparāya ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila sarva-deśe  
in succession  Vaiṣṇavas  became  in every land

(48–49) If anyone heard Kṛṣṇa’s name from His mouth, another heard it from that person’s mouth, and yet another from his. Saying “Kṛṣṇa!” and “Hari!”, they all danced, wept and laughed, and so, one from another, people became Vaiṣṇavas in every land.

যদ্যপি প্রভু লোক-সংঘট্টের ত্রাসে ৷  
প্রেম ‘গুপ্ত’ করেন, বাহিরে না প্রকাশে ॥ ৫০ ॥

yadyapi prabhu loka-saṅghaṭṭera trāse  
although  the Lord  of crowds of people  in fear  
prema ‘gupta’ karena, bāhire nā prakāśe  
His love  hidden  keeps  outwardly  not  shows

(50) Although the Lord, wary of crowds, kept His love hidden and did not show it outwardly,[^9]

তথাপি তাঁর দর্শন-শ্রবণ-প্রভাবে ৷  
সকল দেশের লোক হৈল ‘বৈষ্ণবে’ ॥ ৫১ ॥

tathāpi tām̐ra darśana-śravaṇa-prabhāve  
still  His  of seeing and hearing  by the power  
sakala deśera loka haila ‘vaiṣṇave’  
of all  lands  the people  became  Vaiṣṇavas

(51) still, by the power of seeing and hearing Him, the people of every land became Vaiṣṇavas.

গৌড়, বঙ্গ, উৎকল, দক্ষিণ-দেশ গিয়া ৷  
লোকের নিস্তার কৈল আপনে ভ্রমিয়া ॥ ৫২ ॥

gauḍa, baṅga, utkala, dakṣiṇa-deśa giyā  
to Gauḍa  Vaṅga  Utkala  the southern lands  going  
lokera nistāra kaila āpane bhramiyā  
of the people  deliverance  He brought  Himself  wandering

(52) Going to Gauḍa, Vaṅga, Utkala and the South, He Himself wandered there and delivered the people.

মথুরা যাইবার ছলে আসেন ঝারিখণ্ড ৷  
ভিন্নপ্রায় লোক তাঁহা পরম-পাষণ্ড ॥ ৫৩ ॥

mathurā yāibāra chale āsena jhārikhaṇḍa  
to Mathurā  going  on the pretext  He comes  to Jhārikhaṇḍa  
bhinna-prāya loka tām̐hā parama-pāṣaṇḍa  
almost savage  the people  there  great unbelievers

(53) Ostensibly on His way to Mathurā, He came to Jhārikhaṇḍa, where the people were half-savage and the worst of unbelievers.[^10]

নাম-প্রেম দিয়া কৈল সবার নিস্তার ৷  
চৈতন্যের গূঢ়লীলা বুঝিতে শক্তি কার ॥ ৫৪ ॥

nāma-prema diyā kaila sabāra nistāra  
the name and love  giving  He made  of all  the deliverance  
caitanyera gūḍha-līlā bujhite śakti kāra  
Caitanya’s  hidden pastimes  to understand  the power  whose

(54) Giving them the holy name and love, He delivered them all. Who has the power to understand the hidden pastimes of Caitanya?

বন দেখি’ ভ্রম হয় এই ‘বৃন্দাবন’ ৷  
শৈল দেখি’ মনে হয় এই ‘গোবর্দ্ধন’ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

vana dekhi’ bhrama haya ei ‘vṛndāvana’  
a forest  seeing  the illusion  arises  this  Vṛndāvana  
śaila dekhi’ mane haya ei ‘govardhana’  
a hill  seeing  in the mind  arises  this  Govardhana

(55) Seeing a forest, He would take it for Vṛndāvana; seeing a hill, He would think it Govardhana.

যাঁহা নদী দেখে, তাঁহা মানয়ে ‘কালিন্দী’ ৷  
মহাপ্রেমাবেশে নাচে প্রভু পড়ে কান্দি’ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

yām̐hā nadī dekhe, tām̐hā mānaye ‘kālindī’  
wherever  a river  He sees  there  He considers  Kālindī  
mahā-premāveśe nāce prabhu paḍe kāndi’  
in a great trance of love  dances  the Lord  falls  weeping

(56) Wherever He saw a river, He took it for the Kālindī. Overwhelmed by love, the Lord danced and fell down weeping.

পথে যাইতে ভট্টাচার্য্য শাক-মূল-ফল ৷  
যাঁহা যেই পায়েন, তাঁহা লয়েন সকল ॥ ৫৭ ॥

pathe yāite bhaṭṭācārya śāka-mūla-phala  
on the road  going  the Bhaṭṭācārya  greens, roots and fruits  
yām̐hā yei pāyena, tām̐hā layena sakala  
wherever  whatever  he finds  there  takes  all

(57) As they went along the road, the Bhaṭṭācārya gathered whatever greens, roots and fruits he could find.

যে-গ্রামে রহেন প্রভু, তথায় ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
পাঁচ-সাত জন আসি’ করে নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

ye-grāme rahena prabhu, tathāya brāhmaṇa  
in whatever village  stays  the Lord  there  brāhmaṇas  
pām̐ca-sāta jana āsi’ kare nimantraṇa  
five or seven  persons  coming  make  an invitation

(58) In whatever village the Lord stayed, five or seven brāhmaṇas would come and invite Him.

কেহ অন্ন আনি’ দেয় ভট্টাচার্য্য-স্থানে ৷  
কেহ দুগ্ধ, দধি, কেহ ঘৃত, খণ্ড আনে ॥ ৫৯ ॥

keha anna āni’ deya bhaṭṭācārya-sthāne  
someone  grain  bringing  gives  of the Bhaṭṭācārya  to the place  
keha dugdha, dadhi, keha ghṛta, khaṇḍa āne  
someone  milk  yogurt  someone  ghee  sugar  brings

(59) Some brought grain and left it with the Bhaṭṭācārya; some brought milk and yogurt, and some ghee and sugar.

যাঁহা বিপ্র নাহি, তাঁহা ‘শূদ্রমহাজন’ ৷  
আসি’ সবে ভট্টাচার্য্যে করে নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৬০ ॥

yām̐hā vipra nāhi, tām̐hā ‘śūdra-mahājana’  
where  brāhmaṇas  there are none  there  respectable śūdras  
āsi’ sabe bhaṭṭācārye kare nimantraṇa  
coming  all  the Bhaṭṭācārya  make  invitation

(60) Where there were no brāhmaṇas, respectable men of the *śūdra* community came and invited the Bhaṭṭācārya.[^11]

ভট্টাচার্য্য পাক করে বন্য-ব্যঞ্জন ৷  
বন্য-ব্যঞ্জনে প্রভুর আনন্দিত মন ॥ ৬১ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya pāka kare vanya-vyañjana  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  cooking  does  of forest vegetables  
vanya-vyañjane prabhura ānandita mana  
with the forest vegetables  the Lord’s  delighted  mind

(61) The Bhaṭṭācārya cooked vegetables from the forest, and the Lord was delighted with them.

দুই-চারি দিনের অন্ন রাখেন সংহতি ৷  
যাঁহা শূন্য বন, লোকের নাহিক বসতি ॥ ৬২ ॥

তাঁহা সেই অন্ন ভট্টাচার্য্য করে পাক ৷  
ফল-মূলে ব্যঞ্জন করে, বন্য নানা শাক ॥ ৬৩ ॥

dui-cāri dinera anna rākhena saṁhati  
for two or four  days  grain  he keeps  with him  
yām̐hā śūnya vana, lokera nāhika vasati  
where  empty  forest  of people  there is no  dwelling  
tām̐hā sei anna bhaṭṭācārya kare pāka  
there  that  grain  the Bhaṭṭācārya  does  cooking  
phala-mūle vyañjana kare, vanya nānā śāka  
from fruits and roots  vegetable dishes  makes  of the forest  various  greens

(62–63) He kept with him grain for two or four days. Where the forest was empty and no one lived, the Bhaṭṭācārya cooked that grain and made dishes from fruits, roots and many kinds of forest greens.

পরম সন্তোষ প্রভুর বন্য-ভোজনে ৷  
মহাসুখ পান, যে-দিন রহেন নির্জ্জনে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

parama santoṣa prabhura vanya-bhojane  
supreme  satisfaction  of the Lord  in the forest fare  
mahā-sukha pāna, ye-dina rahena nirjane  
great happiness  He finds  on the day  He remains  in solitude

(64) The Lord was perfectly content with the forest fare, and He was happiest on the days He spent in solitude.

ভট্টাচার্য্য সেবা করে, স্নেহে যৈছে ‘দাস’ ৷  
তাঁর বিপ্র বহে জলপাত্র-বহির্ব্বাস ॥ ৬৫ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya sevā kare, snehe yaiche ‘dāsa’  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  service  does  with affection  like  a servant  
tām̐ra vipra vahe jala-pātra-bahirvāsa  
his  brāhmaṇa  carries  the waterpot and the outer cloth

(65) The Bhaṭṭācārya served Him lovingly, like a servant, and his brāhmaṇa carried the waterpot and the outer cloth.

নির্ঝরেতে উষ্ণোদকে স্নান তিনবার ৷  
দুইসন্ধ্যা অগ্নিতাপ কাষ্ঠের অপার ॥ ৬৬ ॥

nirjharete uṣṇodake snāna tina-bāra  
in the springs  in warm water  a bath  three times  
dui-sandhyā agni-tāpa kāṣṭhera apāra  
at both twilights  the warmth of a fire  of wood  without limit

(66) He bathed three times a day in the warm water of springs, and at dawn and dusk He warmed Himself at a fire, for there was no end of firewood.[^12]

নিরন্তর প্রেমাবেশে নির্জ্জনে গমন ৷  
সুখ অনুভবি’ প্রভু কহেন বচন ॥ ৬৭ ॥

nirantara premāveśe nirjane gamana  
constantly  in the ecstasy of love  in solitude  travelling  
sukha anubhavi’ prabhu kahena vacana  
happiness  feeling  the Lord  speaks  words

(67) Travelling in solitude, He was constantly absorbed in love. Feeling this happiness, the Lord said:

শুন, ভট্টাচার্য্য, — আমি গেলাঙ বহু-দেশ ৷  
বনপথে দুঃখের কাঁহা নাহি পাই লেশ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

śuna, bhaṭṭācārya, — āmi gelāṅa bahu-deśa  
listen  Bhaṭṭācārya  —  I  have gone  through many lands  
vana-pathe duḥkhera kām̐hā nāhi pāi leśa  
on the forest path  of hardship  anywhere  not  I find  a trace

(68) “Listen, Bhaṭṭācārya. I have travelled through many lands, but on this forest path I find not a trace of hardship anywhere.

কৃষ্ণ — কৃপালু, আমায় বহুত কৃপা কৈলা ৷  
বনপথে আনি’ আমায় বড় সুখ দিলা ॥ ৬৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa — kṛpālu, āmāya bahuta kṛpā kailā  
Kṛṣṇa  —  merciful  to Me  great  mercy  He showed  
vana-pathe āni’ āmāya baḍa sukha dilā  
by the forest path  bringing  to Me  great  happiness  He gave

(69) Kṛṣṇa is merciful. He has shown Me great mercy: bringing Me by the forest path, He has given Me great happiness.

পূর্ব্বে বৃন্দাবন যাইতে করিলাঙ বিচার ৷  
মাতা, গঙ্গা, ভক্তগণে দেখিব একবার ॥ ৭০ ॥

pūrve vṛndāvana yāite karilāṅa vicāra  
earlier  to Vṛndāvana  going  I made  a plan  
mātā, gaṅgā, bhakta-gaṇe dekhiba eka-bāra  
My mother  the Gaṅgā  the devotees  I shall see  once

(70) Earlier, when I set out for Vṛndāvana, I thought, ‘I shall see My mother, the Gaṅgā and the devotees once more.

ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে অবশ্য করিব মিলন ৷  
ভক্তগণে সঙ্গে লঞা যাব ‘বৃন্দাবন’ ॥ ৭১ ॥

bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge avaśya kariba milana  
with the devotees  surely  I shall make  a meeting  
bhakta-gaṇe saṅge lañā yāba ‘vṛndāvana’  
the devotees  with Me  taking  I shall go  to Vṛndāvana

(71) I shall surely meet the devotees, and I shall take them with Me to Vṛndāvana.’

এত ভাবি’ গৌড়দেশে করিলুঁ গমন ৷  
মাতা, গঙ্গা, ভক্তে দেখি’ সুখী হৈল মন ॥ ৭২ ॥

eta bhāvi’ gauḍa-deśe karilum̐ gamana  
so  thinking  to Gauḍa  I made  My way  
mātā, gaṅgā, bhakte dekhi’ sukhī haila mana  
My mother  the Gaṅgā  the devotees  seeing  happy  became  My mind

(72) With this in mind I went to Gauḍa, and My heart was happy to see My mother, the Gaṅgā and the devotees.

ভক্তগণে লঞা তবে চলিলাঙ রঙ্গে ৷  
লক্ষকোটি লোক তাঁহা হৈল আমা-সঙ্গে ॥ ৭৩ ॥

bhakta-gaṇe lañā tabe calilāṅa raṅge  
the devotees  taking  then  I set out  joyfully  
lakṣa-koṭi loka tām̐hā haila āmā-saṅge  
hundreds of thousands  of people  there  became  with Me

(73) Then I joyfully set out with the devotees, and hundreds of thousands of people there joined Me.

সনাতন-মুখে কৃষ্ণ আমা শিখাইলা ৷  
তাহা বিঘ্ন করি’ বনপথে লঞা আইলা ॥ ৭৪ ॥

sanātana-mukhe kṛṣṇa āmā śikhāilā  
of Sanātana  through the mouth  Kṛṣṇa  Me  taught  
tāhā vighna kari’ vana-pathe lañā āilā  
to that  obstacle  making  by the forest path  taking Me  He brought

(74) Kṛṣṇa taught Me through Sanātana’s mouth. He put an end to that journey and brought Me by the forest path.

কৃপার সমুদ্র, দীন-হীনে দয়াময় ৷  
কৃষ্ণকৃপা বিনা কোন ‘সুখ’ নাহি হয় ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kṛpāra samudra, dīna-hīne dayāmaya  
of mercy  the ocean  to the poor and fallen  compassionate  
kṛṣṇa-kṛpā vinā kona ‘sukha’ nāhi haya  
Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  without  any  happiness  not  there is

(75) He is an ocean of mercy, compassionate to the poor and the fallen. Without Kṛṣṇa’s mercy there is no happiness at all.”

ভট্টাচার্য্যে আলিঙ্গিয়া তাঁহারে কহিল ৷  
তোমার প্রসাদে এত সুখ পাইল ॥ ৭৬ ॥

bhaṭṭācārye āliṅgiyā tām̐hāre kahila  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  embracing  to him  He said  
tomāra prasāde eta sukha pāila  
by your  grace  so much  happiness  I have found

(76) Embracing the Bhaṭṭācārya, He said to him, “By your kindness I have found so much happiness.”

তেঁহো কহেন, — তুমি ‘কৃষ্ণ’, তুমি ‘দয়াময়’ ৷  
অধম জীব মুঞি, মোরে হইলা সদয় ॥ ৭৭ ॥

tem̐ho kahena, — tumi ‘kṛṣṇa’, tumi ‘dayāmaya’  
he  says  —  You  are Kṛṣṇa  You  are merciful  
adhama jīva muñi, more hailā sadaya  
a fallen  soul  I  to me  You have been  kind

(77) He replied, “You are Kṛṣṇa! You are merciful! I am a fallen soul, yet You have been kind to me.

মুঞি ছার, মোরে তুমি সঙ্গে লঞা আইলা ৷  
কৃপা করি’ মোর হাতে ‘প্রভু’ ভিক্ষা কৈলা ॥ ৭৮ ॥

muñi chāra, more tumi saṅge lañā āilā  
I  am insignificant  me  You  with You  taking  have come  
kṛpā kari’ mora hāte ‘prabhu’ bhikṣā kailā  
mercy  showing  from my  hand  the Lord  meals  has taken

(78) I am worthless, yet You have brought me with You. In Your mercy, my Lord, You have taken Your meals from my hand.

অধম-কাকেরে কৈলা গরুড়-সমান ৷  
‘স্বতন্ত্র ঈশ্বর’ তুমি — স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

adhama-kākere kailā garuḍa-samāna  
a lowly crow  You have made  equal to Garuḍa  
‘svatantra īśvara’ tumi — svayaṁ bhagavān  
the independent  Lord  You  —  Svayam  Bhagavān

(79) You have made a lowly crow equal to Garuḍa. You are the independent Lord, *Svayam Bhagavān* Himself.”

মূকং করোতি বাচালং পঙ্গুং লঙ্ঘয়তে গিরিম্ ৷  
যৎকৃপা তমহং বন্দে পরমানন্দ-মাধবম্ ॥ ৮০ ॥

mūkaṁ karoti vācālaṁ  
the mute  makes  eloquent  
paṅguṁ laṅghayate girim  
the lame  makes cross  a mountain  
yat-kṛpā tam ahaṁ vande  
whose mercy  to Him  I  bow down  
paramānanda-mādhavam  
Mādhava who is the supreme bliss

(80) “I bow down to Mādhava, the supreme bliss, whose mercy makes the mute eloquent and lets the lame cross a mountain.” (Śrīdhara Svāmī, *Bhāvārtha-dīpikā* on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.1)[^13]

এইমত বলভদ্র করেন স্তবন ৷  
প্রেমসেবা করি’ তুষ্ট কৈল প্রভুর মন ॥ ৮১ ॥

ei-mata balabhadra karena stavana  
thus  Balabhadra  offers  praise  
prema-sevā kari’ tuṣṭa kaila prabhura mana  
loving service  rendering  pleased  he made  the Lord’s  mind

(81) In this way Balabhadra offered praise, and by his loving service he pleased the Lord.

এইমত নানা-সুখে প্রভু আইলা ‘কাশী’ ৷  
মধ্যাহ্ন-স্নান কৈল মণিকর্ণিকায় আসি’ ॥ ৮২ ॥

ei-mata nānā-sukhe prabhu āilā ‘kāśī’  
thus  in many joys  the Lord  came  to Kāśī  
madhyāhna-snāna kaila maṇikarṇikāya āsi’  
His midday bath  He took  at Maṇikarṇikā  coming

(82) So the Lord came joyfully to Kāśī, and there He took His midday bath at Maṇikarṇikā.[^14]

সেইকালে তপনমিশ্র করে গঙ্গাস্নান ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ হৈল তাঁর কিছু বিস্ময় জ্ঞান ॥ ৮৩ ॥

sei-kāle tapana-miśra kare gaṅgā-snāna  
at that time  Tapana Miśra  takes  a bath in the Gaṅgā  
prabhu dekhi’ haila tām̐ra kichu vismaya jñāna  
the Lord  seeing  arose  in him  some  wonder  feeling

(83) At that time Tapana Miśra was bathing in the Gaṅgā, and he was surprised to see the Lord there.

‘পূর্ব্বে শুনিয়াছি প্রভু কর্য্যাছেন সন্ন্যাস’ ৷  
নিশ্চয় করিয়া, হৈল হৃদয়ে উল্লাস ॥ ৮৪ ॥

‘pūrve śuniyāchi prabhu karyāchena sannyāsa’  
earlier  I have heard  the Lord  has taken  sannyāsa  
niścaya kariyā, haila hṛdaye ullāsa  
certainty  making  arose  in his heart  joy

(84) “I heard earlier that the Lord had taken *sannyāsa*,” he thought, and when he was sure it was He, his heart filled with joy.

প্রভুর চরণ ধরি’ করেন রোদন ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে উঠাঞা কৈল আলিঙ্গন ॥ ৮৫ ॥

prabhura caraṇa dhari’ karena rodana  
the Lord’s  feet  clasping  he does  weeping  
prabhu tām̐re uṭhāñā kaila āliṅgana  
the Lord  him  raising  made  embrace

(85) Clasping the Lord’s feet, he wept, and the Lord raised him up and embraced him.

প্রভু লঞা গেলা বিশ্বেশ্বর-দরশনে ৷  
তবে আসি’ দেখে বিন্দুমাধব চরণে ॥ ৮৬ ॥

prabhu lañā gelā viśveśvara-daraśane  
the Lord  taking  he went  to the darśana of Viśveśvara  
tabe āsi’ dekhe bindu-mādhava caraṇe  
then  coming  sees  Bindu-mādhava’s  feet

(86) He took the Lord to see Viśveśvara, and then they came and saw the feet of Bindu-mādhava.

ঘরে লঞা আইলা প্রভুকে আনন্দিত হঞা ৷  
সেবা করি’ নৃত্য করে বস্ত্র উড়াঞা ॥ ৮৭ ॥

ghare lañā āilā prabhuke ānandita hañā  
home  taking  he brought  the Lord  joyful  becoming  
sevā kari’ nṛtya kare vastra uḍāñā  
service  rendering  dance  he does  his cloth  waving

(87) Full of joy, he brought the Lord home, served Him, and danced, waving his cloth.

প্রভুর চরণোদক সবংশে কৈল পান ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যের পূজা কৈল করিয়া সম্মান ॥ ৮৮ ॥

prabhura caraṇodaka savaṁśe kaila pāna  
of the Lord  the water from His feet  with all his family  he made  drinking  
bhaṭṭācāryera pūjā kaila kariyā sammāna  
of the Bhaṭṭācārya  worship  he made  showing  honour

(88) With all his family he drank the water that had washed the Lord’s feet, and he honoured the Bhaṭṭācārya with worship.

প্রভুরে নিমন্ত্রণ করি’ ঘরে ভিক্ষা দিল ৷  
বলভদ্র-ভট্টাচার্য্যে পাক করাইল ॥ ৮৯ ॥

prabhure nimantraṇa kari’ ghare bhikṣā dila  
the Lord  invitation  making  at home  meals  he gave  
balabhadra-bhaṭṭācārye pāka karāila  
Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya  cooking  he had do

(89) He invited the Lord and fed Him in his home, having Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya do the cooking.

ভিক্ষা করি’ মহাপ্রভু করিলা শয়ন ৷  
মিশ্রপুত্র রঘু করে পাদ-সম্বাহন ॥ ৯০ ॥

bhikṣā kari’ mahāprabhu karilā śayana  
meal  taking  Mahāprabhu  made  lying down  
miśra-putra raghu kare pāda-samvāhana  
Miśra’s son  Raghu  does  massaging of the feet

(90) After His meal Mahāprabhu lay down to rest, and Miśra’s son Raghu massaged His feet.[^15]

প্রভুর ‘শেষান্ন’ মিশ্র সবংশে খাইল ৷  
‘প্রভু আইলা’ শুনি’ চন্দ্রশেখর আইল ॥ ৯১ ॥

prabhura ‘śeṣānna’ miśra savaṁśe khāila  
the Lord’s  remnants of food  Miśra  with all his family  ate  
‘prabhu āilā’ śuni’ candraśekhara āila  
the Lord  has come  hearing  Candraśekhara  came

(91) Miśra and all his family ate the Lord’s remnants. Hearing that the Lord had come, Candraśekhara came too.

মিশ্রের সখা তেঁহো প্রভুর পূর্ব্ব দাস ৷  
বৈদ্যজাতি, লিখনবৃত্তি, বারাণসী-বাস ॥ ৯২ ॥

miśrera sakhā tem̐ho prabhura pūrva dāsa  
Miśra’s  friend  he  the Lord’s  old  servant  
vaidya-jāti, likhana-vṛtti, vārāṇasī-vāsa  
a vaidya by caste  a scribe by profession  living in Vārāṇasī

(92) He was Miśra’s friend and an old servant of the Lord. A *vaidya* by caste and a scribe by profession, he lived in Vārāṇasī.

আসি’ প্রভু-পদে পড়ি’ করেন রোদন ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে কৃপায় উঠি’ কৈল আলিঙ্গন ॥ ৯৩ ॥

āsi’ prabhu-pade paḍi’ karena rodana  
coming  at the Lord’s feet  falling  he does  weeping  
prabhu tām̐re kṛpāya uṭhi’ kaila āliṅgana  
the Lord  him  mercifully  rising  made  embrace

(93) He came, fell at the Lord’s feet and wept, and the Lord rose and mercifully embraced him.

চন্দ্রশেখর কহে, — প্রভু, বড় কৃপা কৈলা ৷  
আপনে আসিয়া ভৃত্যে দরশন দিলা ॥ ৯৪ ॥

candraśekhara kahe, — prabhu, baḍa kṛpā kailā  
Candraśekhara  says  —  O Lord  great  mercy  You have shown  
āpane āsiyā bhṛtye daraśana dilā  
Yourself  coming  to Your servant  darśana  You gave

(94) Candraśekhara said, “Lord, You have shown me great mercy: You came Yourself to give Your servant Your darśana.

আপন-‘প্রারব্ধে’ বসি’ বারাণসী-স্থানে ৷  
‘মায়া’, ‘ব্রহ্ম’ শব্দ বিনা নাহি শুনি কানে ॥ ৯৫ ॥

āpana-‘prārabdhe’ vasi’ vārāṇasī-sthāne  
by my own prārabdha  living  in Vārāṇasī  
‘māyā’, ‘brahma’ śabda vinā nāhi śuni kāne  
māyā  Brahman  words  except  not  I hear  with my ears

(95) By my own *prārabdha*, the karma now bearing fruit, I live in Vārāṇasī, and my ears hear nothing but the words ‘*māyā*’ and ‘Brahman.’[^16]

ষড়্দর্শন-ব্যাখ্যা বিনা কথা নাহি এথা ৷  
মিশ্র কৃপা করি’ মোরে শুনান কৃষ্ণকথা ॥ ৯৬ ॥

ṣaḍ-darśana-vyākhyā vinā kathā nāhi ethā  
of the six philosophies the explanation  except  talk  there is no  here  
miśra kṛpā kari’ more śunāna kṛṣṇa-kathā  
Miśra  mercy  showing  to me  makes hear  talks of Kṛṣṇa

(96) Here there is no talk but explanations of the six philosophies; but Miśra, in his kindness, lets me hear talks of Kṛṣṇa.[^17]

নিরন্তর দুঁহে চিন্তি তোমার চরণ ৷  
‘সর্ব্বজ্ঞ ঈশ্বর’ তুমি দিলা দরশন ॥ ৯৭ ॥

nirantara dum̐he cinti tomāra caraṇa  
constantly  we two  meditate  on Your  feet  
‘sarvajña īśvara’ tumi dilā daraśana  
the omniscient  Lord  You  have given  darśana

(97) We two constantly meditate on Your feet, and You, the omniscient Lord, have given us Your darśana.

শুনি, — ‘মহাপ্রভু’ যাবেন শ্রীবৃন্দাবনে ৷  
দিন কত রহি’ তার’ ভৃত্য দুইজনে ॥ ৯৮ ॥

śuni, — ‘mahāprabhu’ yābena śrī-vṛndāvane  
I hear  —  Mahāprabhu  will go  to Śrī Vṛndāvana  
dina kata rahi’ tāra’ bhṛtya dui-jane  
days  a few  staying  deliver  servants  two

(98) I hear that Mahāprabhu is going to Śrī Vṛndāvana. Stay a few days and deliver Your two servants.”[^18]

মিশ্র কহে, — প্রভু, যাবৎ কাশীতে রহিবা ৷  
মোর নিমন্ত্রণ বিনা অন্য না মানিবা ॥ ৯৯ ॥

miśra kahe, — prabhu, yāvat kāśīte rahibā  
Miśra  says  —  O Lord  as long as  in Kāśī  You stay  
mora nimantraṇa vinā anya nā mānibā  
my  invitation  except  any other  not  You will accept

(99) Miśra said, “Lord, as long as You stay in Kāśī, please accept no invitation but mine.”

এইমত মহাপ্রভু দুই ভৃত্যের বশে ৷  
ইচ্ছা নাহি, তবু তথা রহিলা দিন-দশে ॥ ১০০ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu dui bhṛtyera vaśe  
thus  Mahāprabhu  of two  servants  under the control  
icchā nāhi, tabu tathā rahilā dina-daśe  
desire  there was none  yet  there  He stayed  for ten days

(100) So Mahāprabhu, yielding to His two servants, stayed there ten days, though He had no wish to.[^19]

মহারাষ্ট্রীয় বিপ্র আইসে প্রভু দেখিবারে ৷  
প্রভুর রূপ-প্রেম দেখি’ হয় চমৎকারে ॥ ১০১ ॥

mahārāṣṭrīya vipra āise prabhu dekhibāre  
a Mahārāṣṭrian  brāhmaṇa  comes  the Lord  to see  
prabhura rūpa-prema dekhi’ haya camatkāre  
the Lord’s  beauty and love  seeing  becomes  amazed

(101) A brāhmaṇa from Mahārāṣṭra would come to see the Lord, and he marvelled at His beauty and His love.

বিপ্র সব নিমন্ত্রয়, প্রভু নাহি মানে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — আজি মোর হঞাছে নিমন্ত্রণে ॥ ১০২ ॥

vipra saba nimantraya, prabhu nāhi māne  
the brāhmaṇas  all  invite  the Lord  not  accepts  
prabhu kahe, — āji mora hañāche nimantraṇe  
the Lord  says  —  today  My  there has been  invitation

(102) All the brāhmaṇas invited Him, but the Lord would not accept. “Today I already have an invitation,” He would say.[^20]

এইমত প্রতিদিন করেন বঞ্চন ৷  
সন্ন্যাসীর সঙ্গ-ভয়ে না মানেন নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

ei-mata prati-dina karena vañcana  
thus  every day  He does  evading  
sannyāsīra saṅga-bhaye nā mānena nimantraṇa  
of the sannyāsīs  of the company  in fear  not  He accepts  invitations

(103) In this way He evaded them every day. Wary of the company of the *sannyāsīs*, He accepted no invitations.[^21]

প্রকাশানন্দ শ্রীপাদ সভাতে বসিয়া ৷  
‘বেদান্ত’ পড়ান বহু শিষ্যগণ লঞা ॥ ১০৪ ॥

prakāśānanda śrīpāda sabhāte vasiyā  
Prakāśānanda  Śrīpāda  in the assembly  sitting  
‘vedānta’ paḍāna bahu śiṣya-gaṇa lañā  
Vedānta  teaches  many  disciples  taking

(104) Prakāśānanda Śrīpāda sat in his assembly and taught Vedānta to his many disciples.[^22]

এক বিপ্র দেখি’ আইলা প্রভুর ব্যবহার ৷  
প্রকাশানন্দ-আগে কহে চরিত্র তাঁহার ॥ ১০৫ ॥

eka vipra dekhi’ āilā prabhura vyavahāra  
one  brāhmaṇa  having seen  came  the Lord’s  conduct  
prakāśānanda-āge kahe caritra tām̐hāra  
before Prakāśānanda  tells  the character  His

(105) One brāhmaṇa who had seen the Lord’s conduct came and told Prakāśānanda about Him.

এক সন্ন্যাসী আইলা জগন্নাথ হৈতে ৷  
তাঁহার মহিমা-প্রতাপ না পারি বর্ণিতে ॥ ১০৬ ॥

eka sannyāsī āilā jagannātha haite  
one  sannyāsī  has come  Jagannātha  from  
tām̐hāra mahimā-pratāpa nā pāri varṇite  
His  glory and power  not  I can  describe

(106) “A *sannyāsī* has come from Jagannātha Purī. I cannot describe His glory and power.

সকল দেখিয়ে তাঁতে অদ্ভুত-কথন ৷  
প্রকাণ্ড-শরীর, শুদ্ধকাঞ্চন-বরণ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

sakala dekhiye tām̐te adbhuta-kathana  
everything  I see  in Him  wonderful to tell  
prakāṇḍa-śarīra, śuddha-kāñcana-varaṇa  
a tall body  the colour of pure gold

(107) Everything I see in Him is wonderful to tell: His body is tall and powerful, and His complexion is like pure gold.

আজানুলম্বিত ভুজ, কমল-নয়ন ৷  
যত কিছু ঈশ্বরের সর্ব্ব সল্লক্ষণ ॥ ১০৮ ॥

ājānu-lambita bhuja, kamala-nayana  
reaching to the knees  arms  lotus eyes  
yata kichu īśvarera sarva sal-lakṣaṇa  
whatever  there are  of the Lord  all  auspicious marks

(108) His arms reach to His knees, His eyes are like lotuses, and He bears every auspicious mark of the Lord.

তাহা দেখি’ জ্ঞান হয় — ‘এই নারায়ণ’ ৷  
যেই তাঁরে দেখে, করে কৃষ্ণসংকীর্ত্তন ॥ ১০৯ ॥

tāhā dekhi’ jñāna haya — ‘ei nārāyaṇa’  
that  seeing  the understanding  arises  —  this  is Nārāyaṇa  
yei tām̐re dekhe, kare kṛṣṇa-saṁkīrtana  
whoever  Him  sees  does  the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa

(109) Seeing these marks, one understands, ‘This is Nārāyaṇa.’ Whoever sees Him begins to chant the names of Kṛṣṇa.

‘মহাভাগবত’-লক্ষণ শুনি ভাগবতে ৷  
সে সব লক্ষণ প্রকট দেখিয়ে তাঁহাতে ॥ ১১০ ॥

‘mahā-bhāgavata’-lakṣaṇa śuni bhāgavate  
of a mahā-bhāgavata the marks  I hear  in the Bhāgavata  
se saba lakṣaṇa prakaṭa dekhiye tām̐hāte  
those  all  marks  manifest  I see  in Him

(110) I hear in the *Bhāgavata* the marks of a *mahā-bhāgavata*, a great devotee, and I see all those marks plainly in Him.

‘নিরন্তর কৃষ্ণনাম’ জিহ্বা তাঁর গায় ৷  
দুই-নেত্রে অশ্রু বহে গঙ্গাধারা-প্রায় ॥ ১১১ ॥

‘nirantara kṛṣṇa-nāma’ jihvā tām̐ra gāya  
unceasingly  Kṛṣṇa’s name  tongue  His  sings  
dui-netre aśru vahe gaṅgā-dhārā-prāya  
from His two eyes  tears  flow  like the streams of the Gaṅgā

(111) His tongue sings Kṛṣṇa’s name unceasingly, and tears flow from His eyes like the streams of the Gaṅgā.

ক্ষণে নাচে, হাসে, গায়, করয়ে ক্রন্দন ৷  
ক্ষণে হুহুঙ্কার করে, — সিংহের গর্জ্জন ॥ ১১২ ॥

kṣaṇe nāce, hāse, gāya, karaye krandana  
at times  He dances  laughs  sings  does  weeping  
kṣaṇe huhuṅkāra kare, — siṁhera garjana  
at times  loud roars  He makes  —  of a lion  the roaring

(112) At times He dances, laughs, sings and weeps; at times He roars like a lion.

জগৎমঙ্গল তাঁর ‘কৃষ্ণচৈতন্য’-নাম ৷  
নাম, রূপ, গুণ তাঁর, সব — অনুপম ॥ ১১৩ ॥

jagat-maṅgala tām̐ra ‘kṛṣṇa-caitanya’-nāma  
auspicious for the world  His  name “Kṛṣṇa Caitanya”  
nāma, rūpa, guṇa tām̐ra, saba — anupama  
name  form  qualities  His  all  —  incomparable

(113) His name, ‘Kṛṣṇa Caitanya,’ brings good to the whole world. His name, form and qualities are all beyond compare.

দেখিলে সে জানি তাঁর ‘ঈশ্বরের রীতি’ ৷  
অলৌকিক কথা শুনি’ কে করে প্রতীতি ? ॥ ১১৪ ॥

dekhile se jāni tām̐ra ‘īśvarera rīti’  
seeing  then  one knows  His  of the Lord  ways  
alaukika kathā śuni’ ke kare pratīti?  
uncommon  account  hearing  who  will make  belief

(114) When you see Him, you know that His ways are the ways of the Lord. But who would believe such an extraordinary account just by hearing it?”

শুনিয়া প্রকাশানন্দ বহুত হাসিলা ৷  
বিপ্রে উপহাস করি’ কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ১১৫ ॥

śuniyā prakāśānanda bahuta hāsilā  
hearing  Prakāśānanda  much  laughed  
vipre upahāsa kari’ kahite lāgilā  
at the brāhmaṇa  mockery  making  to speak  began

(115) Hearing this, Prakāśānanda laughed heartily, and mocking the brāhmaṇa, he said:

শুনিয়াছি গৌড়দেশের সন্ন্যাসী — ‘ভাবুক’ ৷  
কেশব-ভারতী-শিষ্য, লোকপ্রতারক ॥ ১১৬ ॥

śuniyāchi gauḍa-deśera sannyāsī — ‘bhāvuka’  
I have heard  of Gauḍa  the sannyāsī  —  a sentimentalist  
keśava-bhāratī-śiṣya, loka-pratāraka  
a disciple of Keśava Bhāratī  a deceiver of people

(116) “I have heard of Him — the sentimental *sannyāsī* from Gauḍa, a disciple of Keśava Bhāratī and a deceiver of people.[^23]

‘চৈতন্য’-নাম তাঁর, ভাবুকগণ লঞা ৷  
দেশে-দেশে, গ্রামে-গ্রামে বুলে নাচাঞা ॥ ১১৭ ॥

‘caitanya’-nāma tām̐ra, bhāvuka-gaṇa lañā  
Caitanya  name  His  sentimentalists  taking  
deśe-deśe, grāme-grāme bule nācāñā  
from land to land  from village to village  wanders  making them dance

(117) His name is Caitanya. He goes about with His sentimentalists from land to land and village to village, making them dance.

যেই তাঁরে দেখে, সেই ঈশ্বর করি’ কহে ৷  
ঐছে মোহন-বিদ্যা — যে দেখে, সে মোহে ॥ ১১৮ ॥

yei tām̐re dekhe, sei īśvara kari’ kahe  
whoever  Him  sees  he  God  taking Him for  says  
aiche mohana-vidyā — ye dekhe, se mohe  
such  the art of enchanting  whoever  sees  he  is bewildered

(118) “Whoever sees Him declares Him to be God. Such is His art of enchantment: whoever looks at Him falls under its spell.

সার্ব্বভৌম ভট্টাচার্য্য — পণ্ডিত প্রবল ৷  
শুনি’ চৈতন্যের সঙ্গে হইল পাগল ॥ ১১৯ ॥

sārvabhauma bhaṭṭācārya — paṇḍita prabala  
Sārvabhauma  Bhaṭṭācārya  a scholar  formidable  
śuni’ caitanyera saṅge haila pāgala  
I have heard  Caitanya’s  in the company  became  mad

(119) “Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya is a formidable scholar, yet I hear that in Caitanya’s company he has gone mad.

‘সন্ন্যাসী’ — নাম-মাত্র, মহা-ইন্দ্রজালী ৷  
‘কাশীপুরে’ না বিকাবে তাঁর ভাবকালি ॥ ১২০ ॥

‘sannyāsī’ — nāma-mātra, mahā-indrajālī  
a sannyāsī  in name only  a great conjurer  
‘kāśīpure’ nā vikābe tām̐ra bhāvakāli  
in Kāśī  not  will sell  His  wares of emotion

(120) “He is a *sannyāsī* in name only; in fact He is a great conjurer. But here in Kāśī His wares of emotion will not sell.[^24]

‘বেদান্ত’ শ্রবণ কর, না যাইহ তাঁর পাশ ৷  
উচ্ছৃঙ্খল-লোক-সঙ্গে দুইলোক-নাশ ॥ ১২১ ॥

‘vedānta’ śravaṇa kara, nā yāiha tām̐ra pāśa  
Vedānta  hearing  do  do not  go  His  to the side  
ucchṛṅkhala-loka-saṅge dui-loka-nāśa  
of unruly people  in the company  of both worlds  ruin

(121) “Keep hearing Vedānta and do not go near Him. The company of unruly people ruins both this world and the next.”[^25]

এত শুনি’ সেই বিপ্র মহাদুঃখ পাইলা ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি’ তথা হৈতে উঠি’ গেলা ॥ ১২২ ॥

eta śuni’ sei vipra mahā-duḥkha pāilā  
this  hearing  that  brāhmaṇa  great pain  felt  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahi’ tathā haite uṭhi’ gelā  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  saying  there  from  rising  went

(122) Hearing this, the brāhmaṇa was deeply pained. Saying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” he rose and left that place.

প্রভুর দরশনে শুদ্ধ হঞাছে তাঁর মন ৷  
প্রভু-আগে দুঃখী হঞা কহে বিবরণ ॥ ১২৩ ॥

prabhura daraśane śuddha hañāche tām̐ra mana  
of the Lord  by the sight  pure  had become  his  mind  
prabhu-āge duḥkhī hañā kahe vivaraṇa  
before the Lord  sorrowful  being  tells  the account

(123) His mind had been purified by seeing the Lord. Sorrowfully he came before the Lord and told Him everything.

শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু তবে ঈষৎ হাসিলা ৷  
পুনরপি সেই বিপ্র প্রভুরে পুছিলা ॥ ১২৪ ॥

śuni’ mahāprabhu tabe īṣat hāsilā  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  then  slightly  smiled  
punarapi sei vipra prabhure puchilā  
again  that  brāhmaṇa  the Lord  asked

(124) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu smiled slightly, and the brāhmaṇa asked the Lord again:

তার আগে যবে আমি তোমার নাম লইল ৷  
সেহ তোমার নাম জানে, — আপনে কহিল ॥ ১২৫ ॥

tāra āge yabe āmi tomāra nāma laila  
him  before  when  I  Your  name  took  
seha tomāra nāma jāne, — āpane kahila  
he  Your  name  knows  himself  said

(125) “When I spoke Your name before him, he himself said that he knew Your name.

তোমার ‘দোষ’ কহিতে করে নামের উচ্চার ৷  
‘চৈতন্য’ ‘চৈতন্য’ করি’ কহে তিনবার ॥ ১২৬ ॥

tomāra ‘doṣa’ kahite kare nāmera uccāra  
Your  faults  in telling  makes  of the name  utterance  
‘caitanya’ ‘caitanya’ kari’ kahe tina-bāra  
Caitanya  Caitanya  thus  says  three times

(126) “While speaking of Your faults he uttered Your name, saying ‘Caitanya, Caitanya’ three times.

তিনবারে ‘কৃষ্ণনাম’ না আইল তার মুখে ৷  
‘অবজ্ঞা’তে নাম লয়, শুনি’ পাই দুঃখে ॥ ১২৭ ॥

tina-bāre ‘kṛṣṇa-nāma’ nā āila tāra mukhe  
in three times  the name of Kṛṣṇa  not  came  his  to the mouth  
‘avajñā’te nāma laya, śuni’ pāi duḥkhe  
with contempt  the name  he takes  hearing  I feel  pain

(127) “But in all three times the name of Kṛṣṇa never came to his lips. He takes Your name with contempt, and hearing it pains me.[^26]

ইহার কারণ মোরে কহ কৃপা করি’ ৷  
তোমা দেখি’ মুখ মোর বলে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ ॥” ॥ ১২৮ ॥

ihāra kāraṇa more kaha kṛpā kari’  
of this  the reason  to me  tell  mercy  showing  
tomā dekhi’ mukha mora bale ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’”  
You  seeing  mouth  my  says  Kṛṣṇa  Hari

(128) “Please be kind and tell me the reason for this. When I see You, my mouth says ‘Kṛṣṇa!’ and ‘Hari!’ ”

প্রভু কহে, — মায়াবাদী কৃষ্ণে অপরাধী ৷  
‘ব্রহ্ম’, ‘আত্মা’, ‘চৈতন্য’ কহে নিরবধি ॥ ১২৯ ॥

prabhu kahe, — māyāvādī kṛṣṇe aparādhī  
the Lord  says  Māyāvādīs  to Kṛṣṇa  are offenders  
‘brahma’, ‘ātmā’, ‘caitanya’ kahe niravadhi  
Brahman  ātmā  caitanya  they say  incessantly

(129) The Lord said, “The Māyāvādīs are offenders against Kṛṣṇa. They incessantly say ‘Brahman,’ ‘ātmā,’ ‘caitanya’ (consciousness).[^27]

অতএব তার মুখে না আইসে কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণনাম’, ‘কৃষ্ণস্বরূপ’ — দুইত ‘সমান’ ॥ ১৩০ ॥

ataeva tāra mukhe nā āise kṛṣṇa-nāma  
therefore  their  to the mouth  not  comes  the name of Kṛṣṇa  
‘kṛṣṇa-nāma’, ‘kṛṣṇa-svarūpa’ — duita ‘samāna’  
the name of Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa’s own form  both are  equal

(130) “Therefore the name of Kṛṣṇa does not come to their lips. The name of Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa’s own form are one and the same.

‘নাম’, ‘বিগ্রহ’, ‘স্বরূপ’ — তিন একরূপ ৷  
তিনে ‘ভেদ’ নাহি, — তিন ‘চিদানন্দ-রূপ’ ॥ ১৩১ ॥

‘nāma’, ‘vigraha’, ‘svarūpa’ — tina eka-rūpa  
the name  the Deity form  the svarūpa  the three  one in form  
tine ‘bheda’ nāhi, — tina ‘cid-ānanda-rūpa’  
among the three  difference  there is not  the three  are forms of consciousness and bliss

(131) “The name, the form (*vigraha*) and the essential nature (*svarūpa*) are one. There is no difference among the three: all three are forms of consciousness and bliss.

দেহ-দেহীর, নাম-নামীর কৃষ্ণে নাহি ‘ভেদ’ ৷  
জীবের ধর্ম্ম — নাম-দেহ-স্বরূপে ‘বিভেদ’ ॥ ১৩২ ॥

deha-dehīra, nāma-nāmīra kṛṣṇe nāhi ‘bheda’  
of body and embodied  of name and named  in Kṛṣṇa  there is not  difference  
jīvera dharma — nāma-deha-svarūpe ‘vibheda’  
of the jīva  the nature  in name  body  and self  difference

(132) “In Kṛṣṇa there is no difference between the body and its possessor, or between the name and the one named. It is the jīva’s nature that his name, body and true self are different.

নাম চিন্তামণিঃ কৃষ্ণশ্চৈতন্যরসবিগ্রহঃ ৷  
পূর্ণঃ শুদ্ধো নিত্যমুক্তোঽভিন্নত্বান্নামনামিনোঃ ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

nāma cintāmaṇiḥ kṛṣṇaś  
the name  a wish-fulfilling gem  is Kṛṣṇa  
caitanya-rasa-vigrahaḥ  
of consciousness  of rasa  the embodiment  
pūrṇaḥ śuddho nitya-mukto  
complete  pure  eternally  liberated  
’bhinnatvān nāma-nāminoḥ  
because of non-difference  of the name  and the named

(133) “The name is a wish-fulfilling gem; it is Kṛṣṇa Himself, the embodiment of consciousness and rasa — complete, pure and eternally liberated, for the name and the named are not different.” (Padma Purāṇa; Viṣṇu-dharmottara)[^28]

অতএব কৃষ্ণের ‘নাম’, ‘দেহ’, ‘বিলাস’ ৷  
প্রাকৃতেন্দ্রিয়-গ্রাহ্য নহে, হয় স্বপ্রকাশ ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

ataeva kṛṣṇera ‘nāma’, ‘deha’, ‘vilāsa’  
therefore  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  body  pastimes  
prākṛtendriya-grāhya nahe, haya sva-prakāśa  
by material senses  graspable  are not  they are  self-manifest

(134) “Therefore Kṛṣṇa’s name, His body and His pastimes cannot be grasped by the material senses; they manifest themselves.

‘কৃষ্ণনাম’, ‘কৃষ্ণগুণ’, ‘কৃষ্ণলীলা’-বৃন্দ ৷  
কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ সম, — সব চিদানন্দ ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa-nāma’, ‘kṛṣṇa-guṇa’, ‘kṛṣṇa-līlā’-vṛnda  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  all  
kṛṣṇera svarūpa sama, — saba cid-ānanda  
Kṛṣṇa’s  svarūpa  equal to  all  consciousness and bliss

(135) “Kṛṣṇa’s name, His qualities and all His pastimes are equal to Kṛṣṇa’s own form: all of them are consciousness and bliss.

অতঃ শ্রীকৃষ্ণনামাদি ন ভবেদ্গ্রাহ্যমিন্দ্রিয়ৈঃ ৷  
সেবোন্মুখে হি জিহ্বাদৌ স্বয়মেব স্ফুরত্যদঃ ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

ataḥ śrī-kṛṣṇa-nāmādi  
therefore  Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name and the rest  
na bhaved grāhyam indriyaiḥ  
not  can be  grasped  by the senses  
sevonmukhe hi jihvādau  
when turned toward service  indeed  on the tongue and the rest  
svayam eva sphuraty adaḥ  
of itself  alone  manifests  this

(136) “Therefore Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name, form, qualities and pastimes cannot be grasped by the material senses. When one is turned toward service, they reveal themselves of their own accord, beginning on the tongue.” (Padma Purāṇa; cited in Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.234)

ব্রহ্মানন্দ হৈতে পূর্ণানন্দ লীলারস ৷  
ব্রহ্মজ্ঞানী আকর্ষিয়া করে আত্মবশ ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

brahmānanda haite pūrṇānanda līlā-rasa  
than the bliss of Brahman  more than  full bliss  of the pastimes  the rasa  
brahma-jñānī ākarṣiyā kare ātma-vaśa  
the knower of Brahman  attracting  makes  subject to itself

(137) “The rasa of His pastimes is fuller bliss than the bliss of Brahman. It attracts even the knower of Brahman and makes him its own.

স্বসুখনিভৃতচেতাস্তদ্ব্যুদস্তান্যভাবো-  
ঽপ্যজিতরুচিরলীলাকৃষ্টসারস্তদীয়ম্ ৷  
ব্যতনুত কৃপয়া যস্তত্ত্বদীপং পুরাণং  
তমখিলবৃজিনঘ্নং ব্যাসসূনুং নতোঽস্মি ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

sva-sukha-nibhṛta-cetās tad vyudastānya-bhāvo  
in his own happiness  filled  whose mind  therefore  having cast off other  feelings  
’py ajita-rucira-līlākṛṣṭa-sāras tadīyam  
yet  by the Unconquerable One’s  charming  pastimes  attracted  whose heart  about Him  
vyatanuta kṛpayā yas tattva-dīpaṁ purāṇaṁ  
composed  out of mercy  who  of the truth  the lamp  the Purāṇa  
tam akhila-vṛjina-ghnaṁ vyāsa-sūnuṁ nato ’smi  
to him  of all  sins  the destroyer  to the son of Vyāsa  bowed am I

(138) “His mind was filled with the happiness of the self, and so he had cast off every other feeling; yet his heart was drawn by the charming pastimes of the Unconquerable Lord, and in his mercy he brought forth the lamp of truth, the Purāṇa that tells of Him. To him, the son of Vyāsa, destroyer of all sins, I bow down.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 12.12.69)[^29]

ব্রহ্মানন্দ হৈতে পূর্ণানন্দ কৃষ্ণগুণ ৷  
অতএব আকর্ষয়ে আত্মারামের মন ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

brahmānanda haite pūrṇānanda kṛṣṇa-guṇa  
than the bliss of Brahman  more than  full bliss  are Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  
ataeva ākarṣaye ātmārāmera mana  
therefore  they attract  of the ātmārāma  the mind

(139) “Kṛṣṇa’s qualities are fuller bliss than the bliss of Brahman. Therefore they attract the mind even of one who delights in the self.

আত্মারামাশ্চ মুনয়ো নির্গ্রন্থা অপ্যুরুক্রমে ৷  
কুর্ব্বন্ত্যহৈতুকীং ভক্তিমিত্থম্ভূতগুণো হরিঃ ॥ ১৪০ ॥

ātmārāmāś ca munayo  
delighting in the ātmā  and  the sages  
nirgranthā apy urukrame  
free from knots  even  to Urukrama  
kurvanty ahaitukīṁ bhaktim  
perform  causeless  devotion  
ittham-bhūta-guṇo hariḥ  
of such  qualities  is Hari

(140) “Even sages who delight in the ātmā and are free from the knots of the heart render causeless devotion to Urukrama, for such are the qualities of Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.10)[^30]

এই সব রহু — কৃষ্ণচরণ-সম্বন্ধে ৷  
আত্মারামের মন হরে তুলসীর গন্ধে ॥ ১৪১ ॥

ei saba rahu — kṛṣṇa-caraṇa-sambandhe  
these  all  let them be  with Kṛṣṇa’s feet  in connection  
ātmārāmera mana hare tulasīra gandhe  
of the ātmārāma  the mind  steals  of tulasī  the fragrance

(141) “But leave all this aside: the very fragrance of tulasī that has touched Kṛṣṇa’s feet steals the mind of one who delights in the self.

তস্যারবিন্দনয়নস্য পদারবিন্দ-  
কিঞ্জল্কমিশ্রতুলসীমকরন্দবায়ুঃ ৷  
অন্তর্গতঃ স্ববিবরেণ চকার তেষাং  
সংক্ষোভমক্ষরজুষামপি চিত্ততন্বোঃ ॥ ১৪২ ॥

tasyāravinda-nayanasya padāravinda-  
of Him  the lotus-eyed  of the lotus feet  
kiñjalka-miśra-tulasī-makaranda-vāyuḥ  
with the filaments  mixed  of tulasī  of the nectar  the breeze  
antar-gataḥ sva-vivareṇa cakāra teṣāṁ  
having entered within  through its own passage  caused  in them  
saṅkṣobham akṣara-juṣām api citta-tanvoḥ  
agitation  devoted to the imperishable Brahman  even  of mind and body

(142) “The breeze, carrying the fragrance of the tulasī mixed with the filaments of the lotus feet of the lotus-eyed Lord, entered within them through their nostrils and stirred both mind and body, though they were devoted to the imperishable Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.15.43)

অতএব ‘কৃষ্ণনাম’ না আইসে তার মুখে ৷  
মায়াবাদি-গণ, যাতে মহা-বহির্ম্মুখে ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

ataeva ‘kṛṣṇa-nāma’ nā āise tāra mukhe  
therefore  the name of Kṛṣṇa  not  comes  their  to the mouth  
māyāvādi-gaṇa, yāte mahā-bahirmukhe  
the Māyāvādīs  since  are utterly averse

(143) “Therefore the name of Kṛṣṇa does not come to the lips of the Māyāvādīs, for they are utterly averse to the Lord.

ভাবকালি বেচিতে আমি আইলাঙ কাশীপুরে ৷  
গ্রাহক নাহি, না বিকায়, লঞা যাব ঘরে ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

bhāvakāli vecite āmi āilāṅa kāśīpure  
wares of emotion  to sell  I  came  to Kāśī  
grāhaka nāhi, nā vikāya, lañā yāba ghare  
buyer  there is none  not  it sells  taking it  I shall go  home

(144) “I came to Kāśī to sell My wares of emotion, but there is no buyer; they do not sell, so I shall have to take them back home.

ভারি বোঝা লঞা আইলাঙ, কেমনে লঞা যাব ?  
অল্প-স্বল্প-মূল্য পাইলে, এথাই বেচিব ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

bhāri bojhā lañā āilāṅa, kemane lañā yāba?  
heavy  load  bringing  I came  how  taking it  shall I go  
alpa-svalpa-mūlya pāile, ethāi veciba  
a small  a trifling  price  if I get  right here  I shall sell

(145) “I came with a heavy load — how can I carry it back? If I get even a small price, I will sell it right here.”[^31]

এত বলি’ সেই বিপ্রে আত্মসাৎ করি’ ৷  
প্রাতে উঠি’ মথুরা চলিলা গৌরহরি ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

eta bali’ sei vipre ātmasāt kari’  
thus  saying  that  brāhmaṇa  His own  making  
prāte uṭhi’ mathurā calilā gaurahari  
in the morning  rising  to Mathurā  set out  Gaurahari

(146) Saying this, Gaurahari made that brāhmaṇa His own, and rising early the next morning He set out for Mathurā.

সেই তিন সঙ্গে চলে, প্রভু নিষেধিল ৷  
দূর হৈতে তিনজনে ঘরে পাঠাইল ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

sei tina saṅge cale, prabhu niṣedhila  
those  three  with Him  go  the Lord  forbade  
dūra haite tina-jane ghare pāṭhāila  
from a distance  from  the three  home  He sent

(147) Those three followed Him, but the Lord forbade them, and from some way along the road He sent all three back home.

প্রভুর বিরহে তিনে একত্র মিলিয়া ৷  
প্রভুগুণ গান করে প্রেমে মত্ত হঞা ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

prabhura virahe tine ekatra miliyā  
of the Lord  in separation  the three  together  meeting  
prabhu-guṇa gāna kare preme matta hañā  
the Lord’s qualities  singing  do  with love  maddened  becoming

(148) In separation from the Lord the three would meet together and sing of His qualities, maddened with love.

‘প্রয়াগ’ আসিয়া প্রভু কৈল নদীস্নান ৷  
‘মাধব’ দেখিয়া প্রেমে কৈল নৃত্যগান ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

‘prayāga’ āsiyā prabhu kaila nadī-snāna  
to Prayāga  coming  the Lord  performed  bathing in the river  
‘mādhava’ dekhiyā preme kaila nṛtya-gāna  
Mādhava  seeing  in love  performed  dancing and singing

(149) Coming to Prayāga, the Lord bathed in the river, and on seeing Mādhava He danced and sang in love.[^32]

যমুনা দেখিয়া প্রেমে পড়ে ঝাঁপ দিয়া ৷  
আস্তে ব্যস্তে ভট্টাচার্য্য উঠায় ধরিয়া ॥ ১৫০ ॥

yamunā dekhiyā preme paḍe jhām̐pa diyā  
the Yamunā  seeing  in love  He falls  a leap  making  
āste vyaste bhaṭṭācārya uṭhāya dhariyā  
hastily  hurriedly  the Bhaṭṭācārya  lifts Him up  catching hold

(150) Seeing the Yamunā, He leapt into it in love, and the Bhaṭṭācārya hurriedly caught hold of Him and lifted Him out.

এইমত তিনদিন প্রয়াগে রহিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-নাম-প্রেম দিয়া লোক নিস্তারিলা ॥ ১৫১ ॥

ei-mata tina-dina prayāge rahilā  
in this way  three days  in Prayāga  He stayed  
kṛṣṇa-nāma-prema diyā loka nistārilā  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  and love  giving  the people  He delivered

(151) In this way He stayed three days in Prayāga, delivering the people by giving them the name of Kṛṣṇa and love for Him.

‘মথুরা’ চলিতে পথে যথা রহি’ যায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-নাম-প্রেম দিয়া লোকেরে নাচায় ॥ ১৫২ ॥

‘mathurā’ calite pathe yathā rahi’ yāya  
to Mathurā  going  on the way  wherever  stopping  He goes  
kṛṣṇa-nāma-prema diyā lokere nācāya  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  and love  giving  the people  He makes dance

(152) Wherever He stopped on the way to Mathurā, He gave the people the name of Kṛṣṇa and love for Him and made them dance.

পূর্ব্বে যেন ‘দক্ষিণ’ যাইতে লোক নিস্তারিলা ৷  
‘পশ্চিম’-দেশে তৈছে সব ‘বৈষ্ণব’ করিলা ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

pūrve yena ‘dakṣiṇa’ yāite loka nistārilā  
before  as  south  going  the people  He delivered  
‘paścima’-deśe taiche saba ‘vaiṣṇava’ karilā  
in the western lands  likewise  all  Vaiṣṇavas  He made

(153) As He had delivered the people before when He went to the south, so in the western lands He made everyone a Vaiṣṇava.

পথে যাঁহা যাঁহা হয় যমুনা-দর্শন ৷  
তাঁহা ঝাঁপ দিয়া পড়ে প্রেমে অচেতন ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

pathe yām̐hā yām̐hā haya yamunā-darśana  
on the way  wherever  wherever  there was  a sight of the Yamunā  
tām̐hā jhām̐pa diyā paḍe preme acetana  
there  a leap  making  He falls  in love  unconscious

(154) Wherever He caught sight of the Yamunā along the way, He leapt into it and fell unconscious in love.

মথুরা-নিকটে আইলা, মথুরা দেখিয়া ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ হঞা পড়ে প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

mathurā-nikaṭe āilā, mathurā dekhiyā  
near Mathurā  He came  Mathurā  seeing  
daṇḍavat hañā paḍe premāviṣṭa hañā  
like a rod  becoming  He falls  absorbed in love  being

(155) When He drew near Mathurā and saw the city, He fell down flat like a rod, overwhelmed with love.

মথুরা আসিয়া কৈলা, ‘বিশ্রাম-তীর্থে’ স্নান ৷  
‘জন্মস্থানে’ ‘কেশব’ দেখি’ করিলা প্রণাম ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

mathurā āsiyā kailā, ‘viśrāma-tīrthe’ snāna  
to Mathurā  coming  He performed  at Viśrāma-tīrtha  bathing  
‘janma-sthāne’ ‘keśava’ dekhi’ karilā praṇāma  
at the birthplace  Keśava  seeing  He offered  obeisance

(156) Arriving in Mathurā, He bathed at Viśrāma-tīrtha, and at the birthplace He saw Keśava and bowed down to Him.

প্রেমাবেশে নাচে, গায়, সঘনে হুঙ্কার ৷  
প্রভুর প্রেমাবেশ দেখি’ লোকে চমৎকার ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

premāveśe nāce, gāya, saghane huṅkāra  
in the rapture of love  He dances  sings  again and again  roars  
prabhura premāveśa dekhi’ loke camatkāra  
the Lord’s  rapture of love  seeing  the people  are astonished

(157) Rapt in love, He danced, sang and roared again and again, and the people were astonished to see the Lord’s rapture of love.

একবিপ্র পড়ে প্রভুর চরণ ধরিয়া ৷  
প্রভু-সঙ্গে নৃত্য করে প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

eka-vipra paḍe prabhura caraṇa dhariyā  
a brāhmaṇa  falls  the Lord’s  feet  clasping  
prabhu-saṅge nṛtya kare premāviṣṭa hañā  
with the Lord  dancing  does  absorbed in love  becoming

(158) A brāhmaṇa fell down and clasped the Lord’s feet, and then, overwhelmed with love, he danced with the Lord.

দুঁহে প্রেমে নৃত্য করি’ করে কোলাকুলি ৷  
‘হরি’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহে দুঁহে বলি’ বাহু তুলি ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

dum̐he preme nṛtya kari’ kare kolākuli  
both  in love  dancing  doing  they make  embraces  
‘hari’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahe dum̐he bali’ bāhu tuli  
Hari  Kṛṣṇa  say  both  saying  arms  raising

(159) Dancing in love, the two embraced each other, and with arms raised they both called out, “Hari! Kṛṣṇa!”

লোক ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলে, কোলাহল হৈল ৷  
‘কেশব’-সেবক প্রভুকে মালা পরাইল ॥ ১৬০ ॥

loka ‘hari’ ‘hari’ bale, kolāhala haila  
the people  Hari  Hari  say  a tumult  arose  
‘keśava’-sevaka prabhuke mālā parāila  
Keśava’s servant  on the Lord  a garland  placed

(160) The people cried, “Hari! Hari!” and a great tumult arose. Keśava’s servant placed a garland on the Lord.

লোকে কহে প্রভু দেখি’ হঞা বিস্ময় ৷  
ঐছে হেন প্রেম ‘লৌকিক’ কভু নয় ॥ ১৬১ ॥

loke kahe prabhu dekhi’ hañā vismaya  
the people  say  the Lord  seeing  becoming  astonished  
aiche hena prema ‘laukika’ kabhu naya  
such  this kind of  love  worldly  ever  is not

(161) Seeing the Lord, the people were amazed and said, “Love such as this is never of this world.

যাঁহার দর্শনে লোকে প্রেমে মত্ত হঞা ৷  
হাসে, কান্দে, নাচে, গায়, কৃষ্ণনাম লঞা ॥ ১৬২ ॥

yām̐hāra darśane loke preme matta hañā  
whose  at the sight  the people  with love  maddened  becoming  
hāse, kānde, nāce, gāya, kṛṣṇa-nāma lañā  
laugh  weep  dance  sing  the name of Kṛṣṇa  taking

(162) “At the sight of Him people become mad with love: they laugh, weep, dance and sing, chanting the name of Kṛṣṇa.

সর্ব্বথা নিশ্চিত, ইঁহো — কৃষ্ণ-অবতার ৷  
মথুরা আইল লোকের করিতে নিস্তার ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

sarvathā niścita, im̐ho — kṛṣṇa-avatāra  
in every way  certain  He is  an avatāra of Kṛṣṇa  
mathurā āila lokera karite nistāra  
to Mathurā  He has come  of the people  to accomplish  deliverance

(163) “It is certain in every way that He is an avatāra of Kṛṣṇa. He has come to Mathurā to deliver the people.”

তবে মহাপ্রভু সেই ব্রাহ্মণে লঞা ৷  
তাঁহারে পুছিলা কিছু নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu sei brāhmaṇe lañā  
then  Mahāprabhu  that  brāhmaṇa  taking  
tām̐hāre puchilā kichu nibhṛte vasiyā  
him  asked  something  in private  sitting

(164) Then Mahāprabhu took that brāhmaṇa aside, sat with him in private and asked him:

‘আর্য্য, সরল তুমি — বৃদ্ধ ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
কাঁহা হৈতে পাইলে তুমি এই প্রেমধন ? ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

‘ārya, sarala tumi — vṛddha brāhmaṇa  
noble  simple  you are  an aged  brāhmaṇa  
kām̐hā haite pāile tumi ei prema-dhana?  
where  from  did you get  you  this  treasure of love

(165) “You are noble and simple-hearted, an aged brāhmaṇa. Where did you get this treasure of love?”

বিপ্র কহে, — শ্রীপাদ মাধবেন্দ্র-পুরী ৷  
ভ্রমিতে ভ্রমিতে আইলা মথুরা-নগরী ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

vipra kahe, — śrīpāda mādhavendra-purī  
the brāhmaṇa  says  Śrīpāda  Mādhavendra Purī  
bhramite bhramite āilā mathurā-nagarī  
wandering  wandering  came  to the city of Mathurā

(166) The brāhmaṇa said, “Śrīpāda Mādhavendra Purī came to the city of Mathurā in the course of his wanderings.

কৃপা করি’ তেঁহো মোর নিলয়ে আইলা ৷  
মোরে শিষ্য করি’ মোর হাতে ‘ভিক্ষা’ কৈলা ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

kṛpā kari’ tem̐ho mora nilaye āilā  
mercy  showing  he  my  to the dwelling  came  
more śiṣya kari’ mora hāte ‘bhikṣā’ kailā  
me  a disciple  making  from my  hands  alms  he took

(167) “Out of kindness he came to my home. He made me his disciple and accepted his meal (*bhikṣā*) from my hands.

গোপাল প্রকট করি’ সেবা কৈল ‘মহাশয়’ ৷  
অদ্যাপিহ তাঁহার সেবা ‘গোবর্দ্ধনে’ হয় ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

gopāla prakaṭa kari’ sevā kaila ‘mahāśaya’  
Gopāla  manifest  making  service  performed  the great soul  
adyāpiha tām̐hāra sevā ‘govardhane’ haya  
to this day  His  service  at Govardhana  goes on

(168) “That great soul brought Gopāla to light and served Him, and to this day Gopāla is served at Govardhana.”

শুনি’ প্রভু কৈল তাঁর চরণ বন্দন ৷  
ভয় পাঞা প্রভু-পায় পড়িলা ব্রাহ্মণ ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

śuni’ prabhu kaila tām̐ra caraṇa vandana  
hearing  the Lord  made  his  to the feet  obeisance  
bhaya pāñā prabhu-pāya paḍilā brāhmaṇa  
fear  feeling  at the Lord’s feet  fell  the brāhmaṇa

(169) Hearing this, the Lord bowed down at the brāhmaṇa’s feet, and the brāhmaṇa in fear fell at the Lord’s feet.

প্রভু কহে, — তুমি ‘গুরু’, আমি ‘শিষ্য’-প্রায় ৷  
‘গুরু’ হঞা, ‘শিষ্যে’ নমস্কার না যুয়ায় ॥ ১৭০ ॥

prabhu kahe, — tumi ‘guru’, āmi ‘śiṣya’-prāya  
the Lord  says  you  are guru  I  like a disciple  
‘guru’ hañā, ‘śiṣye’ namaskāra nā yuyāya  
guru  being  to a disciple  obeisance  not  is proper

(170) The Lord said, “You are in the position of a guru, and I am like your disciple. It is not proper for a guru to bow down to a disciple.”

শুনিয়া বিস্মিত বিপ্র কহে ভয় পাঞা ৷  
ঐছে বাত্ কহ কেনে সন্ন্যাসী হঞা ॥ ১৭১ ॥

śuniyā vismita vipra kahe bhaya pāñā  
hearing  astonished  the brāhmaṇa  says  fear  feeling  
aiche bāt kaha kene sannyāsī hañā  
such  words  You speak  why  a sannyāsī  being

(171) Hearing this, the brāhmaṇa was astonished and said in fear, “You are a *sannyāsī* — why do You speak like this?

কিন্তু তোমার প্রেম দেখি’ মনে অনুমানি ৷  
মাধবেন্দ্র-পুরীর ‘সম্বন্ধ’ ধর, জানি ॥ ১৭২ ॥

kintu tomāra prema dekhi’ mane anumāni  
but  Your  love  seeing  in my mind  I infer  
mādhavendra-purīra ‘sambandha’ dhara, jāni  
with Mādhavendra Purī  a connection  You have  I know

(172) “But seeing Your love, I infer that You have some connection with Mādhavendra Purī; I am sure of it.

কৃষ্ণপ্রেমা তাঁহা, যাঁহা তাঁহার ‘সম্বন্ধ’ ৷  
তাঁহা বিনা এই প্রেমার কাঁহা নাহি গন্ধ ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa-premā tām̐hā, yām̐hā tām̐hāra ‘sambandha’  
love for Kṛṣṇa  is there  where  his  connection  
tām̐hā vinā ei premāra kām̐hā nāhi gandha  
him  without  of this  love  anywhere  there is not  the scent

(173) “Love for Kṛṣṇa is found where there is a connection with him. Without him there is not even a scent of such love anywhere.”

তবে ভট্টাচার্য্য তারে ‘সম্বন্ধ’ কহিল ৷  
শুনি’ আনন্দিত বিপ্র নাচিতে লাগিল ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

tabe bhaṭṭācārya tāre ‘sambandha’ kahila  
then  the Bhaṭṭācārya  to him  the connection  told  
śuni’ ānandita vipra nācite lāgila  
hearing  overjoyed  the brāhmaṇa  to dance  began

(174) Then the Bhaṭṭācārya told him of the connection, and hearing it the brāhmaṇa began to dance with joy.

তবে বিপ্র প্রভুরে লঞা আইলা নিজ-ঘরে ৷  
আপন-ইচ্ছায় প্রভুর নানা সেবা করে ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

tabe vipra prabhure lañā āilā nija-ghare  
then  the brāhmaṇa  the Lord  taking  came  to his own home  
āpana-icchāya prabhura nānā sevā kare  
of his own accord  for the Lord  various  service  does

(175) Then the brāhmaṇa took the Lord to his own home and of his own accord rendered Him many kinds of service.

ভিক্ষা লাগি’ ভট্টাচার্য্যে করাইল রন্ধন ৷  
তবে মহাপ্রভু হাসি’ বলিলা বচন ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

bhikṣā lāgi’ bhaṭṭācārye karāila randhana  
the meal  for the sake of  the Bhaṭṭācārya  he had  cook  
tabe mahāprabhu hāsi’ balilā vacana  
then  Mahāprabhu  smiling  spoke  words

(176) He had the Bhaṭṭācārya cook for the Lord’s meal, but Mahāprabhu smiled and said:

পুরী-গোসাঞি তোমার ঘরে কর্য্যাছেন ভিক্ষা ৷  
মোরে তুমি ভিক্ষা দেহ’, — এই মোর ‘শিক্ষা’ ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

“purī-gosāñi tomāra ghare karyāchena bhikṣā  
Purī Gosāñi  your  in the house  has taken  alms  
more tumi bhikṣā deha’, — ei mora ‘śikṣā’”  
to Me  you  alms  give  this  My  teaching

(177) “Purī Gosāñi has taken his meals in your house. You yourself give Me My meal — this is My instruction.

যদ্যদাচরতি শ্রেষ্ঠস্তত্তদেবেতরো জনঃ ৷  
স যৎ প্রমাণং কুরুতে লোকস্তদনুবর্ত্ততে ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

yad yad ācarati śreṣṭhas  
whatever  whatever  does  the best  
tat tad evetaro janaḥ  
that  that  indeed  other  people  
sa yat pramāṇaṁ kurute  
he  whatever  standard  sets  
lokas tad anuvartate  
the world  that  follows

(178) “Whatever the best of men does, others do the same; whatever standard he sets, the world follows.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 3.21)[^33]

যদ্যপি ‘সনোড়িয়া’ হয় সেইত ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
সনোড়িয়া-ঘরে সন্ন্যাসী না করে ভোজন ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

yadyapi ‘sanoḍiyā’ haya seita brāhmaṇa  
although  a Sanoḍiyā  was  that very  brāhmaṇa  
sanoḍiyā-ghare sannyāsī nā kare bhojana  
in a Sanoḍiyā’s house  a sannyāsī  not  does  eat

(179) That brāhmaṇa was a Sanoḍiyā, and *sannyāsīs* do not eat in a Sanoḍiyā’s house.[^34]

তথাপি পুরী দেখি’ তাঁর ‘বৈষ্ণব’-আচার ৷  
‘শিষ্য’ করি’ তাঁর ভিক্ষা কৈল অঙ্গীকার ॥ ১৮০ ॥

tathāpi purī dekhi’ tām̐ra ‘vaiṣṇava’-ācāra  
nevertheless  Purī  seeing  his  Vaiṣṇava conduct  
‘śiṣya’ kari’ tām̐ra bhikṣā kaila aṅgīkāra  
a disciple  making him  his  meal  made  acceptance

(180) Nevertheless, seeing his Vaiṣṇava conduct, Purī made him his disciple and accepted meals from him.

মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে যদি ‘ভিক্ষা’ মাগিল ৷  
দৈন্য করি’ সেই বিপ্র কহিতে লাগিল ॥ ১৮১ ॥

mahāprabhu tām̐re yadi ‘bhikṣā’ māgila  
Mahāprabhu  of him  when  a meal  asked  
dainya kari’ sei vipra kahite lāgila  
humility  showing  that  brāhmaṇa  to speak  began

(181) When Mahāprabhu asked him for a meal, the brāhmaṇa humbly began to say:

তোমারে ‘ভিক্ষা’ দিব, বড় ভাগ্য সে আমার ৷  
তুমি — ঈশ্বর, নাহি তোমার বিধি-ব্যবহার ॥ ১৮২ ॥

tomāre ‘bhikṣā’ diba, baḍa bhāgya se āmāra  
to You  a meal  I shall give  great  fortune  that is  my  
tumi — īśvara, nāhi tomāra vidhi-vyavahāra  
You  are the Lord  there is not  Your  rule-bound conduct

(182) “To give You a meal is my great good fortune. You are the Lord; You are not bound by rules of conduct.

‘মূর্খ’-লোক করিবেক তোমার নিন্দন ৷  
সহিতে না পারিমু সেই ‘দুষ্টে’র বচন ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

‘mūrkha’-loka karibeka tomāra nindana  
foolish people  will make  Your  censure  
sahite nā pārimu sei ‘duṣṭe’ra vacana  
to bear  not  I shall be able  of those  wicked men  the words

(183) “But foolish people will censure You, and I will not be able to bear the words of those wicked people.”[^35]

প্রভু কহে, — শ্রুতি, স্মৃতি, যত ঋষিগণ ৷  
সবে ‘এক’-মত নহে, ভিন্ন ভিন্ন ধর্ম্ম ॥ ১৮৪ ॥

prabhu kahe, — śruti, smṛti, yata ṛṣi-gaṇa  
the Lord  says  śruti  smṛti  all  the sages  
sabe ‘eka’-mata nahe, bhinna bhinna dharma  
all  of one opinion  are not  different  different  dharmas

(184) The Lord said, “The śruti, the smṛti and all the sages are not of one opinion; they teach many different kinds of dharma.

ধর্ম্ম-স্থাপন-হেতু সাধুর ব্যবহার ৷  
পুরী-গোসাঞির যে আচরণ, সেই ধর্ম্ম সার ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

dharma-sthāpana-hetu sādhura vyavahāra  
of dharma  establishing  for the sake of  of the sādhu  the conduct  
purī-gosāñira ye ācaraṇa, sei dharma sāra  
of Purī Gosāñi  which  conduct  that  of dharma  the essence

(185) “The conduct of a sādhu is what establishes dharma, and the conduct of Purī Gosāñi is the essence of dharma.[^36]

তর্কোঽপ্রতিষ্ঠঃ শ্রুতয়ো বিভিন্না  
নাসাবৃষির্যস্য মতং ন ভিন্নম্ ৷  
ধর্ম্মস্য তত্ত্বং নিহিতং গুহায়াং  
মহাজনো যেন গতঃ স পন্থাঃ ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

tarko ’pratiṣṭhaḥ śrutayo vibhinnā  
argument  is unsettled  the śrutis  are various  
nāsāv ṛṣir yasya mataṁ na bhinnam  
there is no such  sage  whose  opinion  not  differs  
dharmasya tattvaṁ nihitaṁ guhāyāṁ  
of dharma  the truth  is hidden  in a cave  
mahājano yena gataḥ sa panthāḥ  
the great soul  by which  has gone  that  is the path

(186) “Argument is inconclusive, the śrutis differ from one another, and there is no sage whose opinion does not differ from the others. The truth of dharma is hidden in the cave of the heart; the path is the one by which the great souls have gone.” (Mahābhārata, Vana-parva, 313.117)[^37]

তবে সেই বিপ্র প্রভুকে ভিক্ষা করাইল ৷  
মধুপুরীর লোক-সব প্রভুকে দেখিতে আইল ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

tabe sei vipra prabhuke bhikṣā karāila  
then  that  brāhmaṇa  the Lord  a meal  had Him take  
madhu-purīra loka-saba prabhuke dekhite āila  
of Madhupurī  all the people  the Lord  to see  came

(187) Then the brāhmaṇa served the Lord His meal, and all the people of Madhupurī (Mathurā) came to see the Lord.

লক্ষ-সংখ্য লোক আইসে, নাহিক গণন ৷  
বাহির হঞা প্রভু দিল দরশন ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

lakṣa-saṅkhya loka āise, nāhika gaṇana  
by the hundred thousand  people  come  there is no  counting  
bāhira hañā prabhu dila daraśana  
outside  coming  the Lord  gave  His sight

(188) People came by the hundreds of thousands, beyond counting, and the Lord came out of the house and showed Himself to them.

বাহু তুলি’ বলে প্রভু ‘হরিবোল’-ধ্বনি ৷  
প্রেমে মত্ত নাচে লোক করি’ হরিধ্বনি ॥ ১৮৯ ॥

bāhu tuli’ bale prabhu ‘hari-bola’-dhvani  
arms  raising  utters  the Lord  “Haribol”  the sound  
preme matta nāce loka kari’ hari-dhvani  
with love  maddened  dance  the people  making  the sound of Hari

(189) Raising His arms, the Lord called out, “Haribol!” and the people danced, mad with love, calling out the name of Hari.

যমুনার ‘চব্বিশ ঘাটে’ প্রভু কৈল স্নান ৷  
সেই বিপ্র প্রভুকে দেখায় তীর্থস্থান ॥ ১৯০ ॥

yamunāra ‘cabbiśa ghāṭe’ prabhu kaila snāna  
of the Yamunā  at the twenty-four ghāṭs  the Lord  took  bath  
sei vipra prabhuke dekhāya tīrtha-sthāna  
that  brāhmaṇa  to the Lord  shows  the holy places

(190) The Lord bathed at the twenty-four ghāṭs of the Yamunā, and the brāhmaṇa showed Him the holy places.

স্বয়ম্ভু, বিশ্রাম, দীর্ঘবিষ্ণু, ভূতেশ্বর ৷  
মহাবিদ্যা, গোকর্ণাদি দেখিলা, বিস্তর ॥ ১৯১ ॥

svayambhu, viśrāma, dīrgha-viṣṇu, bhūteśvara  
Svayambhu  Viśrāma  Dīrgha-viṣṇu  Bhūteśvara  
mahāvidyā, gokarṇādi dekhilā, vistara  
Mahāvidyā  Gokarṇa and others  He saw  in great number

(191) He saw Svayambhu, Viśrāma, Dīrgha-viṣṇu, Bhūteśvara, Mahāvidyā, Gokarṇa and many other places.

‘বন’ দেখিবারে যদি প্রভুর মন হৈল ৷  
সেইত ব্রাহ্মণে প্রভু সঙ্গেতে লইল ॥ ১৯২ ॥

‘vana’ dekhibāre yadi prabhura mana haila  
the forests  to see  when  the Lord’s  mind  was  
seita brāhmaṇe prabhu saṅgete laila  
that very  brāhmaṇa  the Lord  with Him  took

(192) When the Lord wished to see the forests, He took that same brāhmaṇa with Him.[^38]

মধুবন, তাল, কুমুদ, বহুলা-বন গেলা ৷  
তাঁহা তাঁহা স্নান করি’ প্রেমাবিষ্ট হৈলা ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

madhu-vana, tāla, kumuda, bahulā-vana gelā  
Madhuvana  Tāla  Kumuda  Bahulā-vana  He went to  
tām̐hā tām̐hā snāna kari’ premāviṣṭa hailā  
there  there  bathing  doing  absorbed in love  He became

(193) He went to Madhuvana, Tālavana, Kumudavana and Bahulāvana, bathing in each place and becoming overwhelmed with love.

পথে গাভীঘটা চরে প্রভুরে দেখিয়া ৷  
প্রভুকে বেড়য় আসি’ হুঙ্কার করিয়া ॥ ১৯৪ ॥

pathe gābhī-ghaṭā care prabhure dekhiyā  
on the way  a herd of cows  grazing  the Lord  seeing  
prabhuke beḍaya āsi’ huṅkāra kariyā  
the Lord  surround  coming  lowing  making

(194) On the way, a herd of grazing cows saw the Lord, came up lowing loudly and surrounded Him.

গাভী দেখি’ স্তব্ধ প্রভু প্রেমের তরঙ্গে ৷  
বাৎসল্যে গাভী প্রভুর চাটে সব-অঙ্গে ॥ ১৯৫ ॥

gābhī dekhi’ stabdha prabhu premera taraṅge  
the cows  seeing  stunned  the Lord  of love  in the waves  
vātsalye gābhī prabhura cāṭe saba-aṅge  
with motherly affection  the cows  the Lord’s  lick  whole body

(195) At the sight of the cows the Lord stood stunned in waves of love, and with motherly affection the cows licked His whole body.

সুস্থ হঞা প্রভু করে অঙ্গ-কণ্ডূয়ন ৷  
প্রভু-সঙ্গে চলে, নাহি ছাড়ে ধেনুগণ ॥ ১৯৬ ॥

sustha hañā prabhu kare aṅga-kaṇḍūyana  
composed  becoming  the Lord  does  scratching of the bodies  
prabhu-saṅge cale, nāhi chāḍe dhenu-gaṇa  
with the Lord  go  not  leave  the cows

(196) Coming to Himself, the Lord scratched the cows’ bodies, and they walked along with Him and would not leave Him.

কষ্টে-সৃষ্ট্যে ধেনু সব রাখিল গোয়াল ৷  
প্রভুকণ্ঠধ্বনি শুনি’ আইসে মৃগীপাল ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

kaṣṭe-sṛṣṭye dhenu saba rākhila goyāla  
with difficulty  with great effort  the cows  all  held back  the cowherd  
prabhu-kaṇṭha-dhvani śuni’ āise mṛgī-pāla  
the sound of the Lord’s voice  hearing  come  herds of does

(197) With great difficulty the cowherd held all the cows back. Then herds of does, hearing the sound of the Lord’s voice, came to Him.

মৃগ-মৃগী মুখ দেখি’ প্রভু-অঙ্গ চাটে ৷  
ভয় নাহি করে, সঙ্গে যায় বাটে-বাটে ॥ ১৯৮ ॥

mṛga-mṛgī mukha dekhi’ prabhu-aṅga cāṭe  
the bucks and does  His face  seeing  the Lord’s body  lick  
bhaya nāhi kare, saṅge yāya vāṭe-vāṭe  
fear  not  feel  with Him  go  along the paths

(198) Looking at His face, the bucks and does licked the Lord’s body. Without any fear they followed Him along the paths.

শুক, পিক, ভৃঙ্গ প্রভুরে দেখি’ ‘পঞ্চম’ গায় ৷  
শিখিগণ নৃত্য করি’ প্রভু-আগে যায় ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

śuka, pika, bhṛṅga prabhure dekhi’ ‘pañcama’ gāya  
parrots  cuckoos  bees  the Lord  seeing  in the fifth note  sing  
śikhi-gaṇa nṛtya kari’ prabhu-āge yāya  
the peacocks  dancing  doing  before the Lord  go

(199) Seeing the Lord, the parrots, cuckoos and bees sang in the fifth note (*pañcama*), and the peacocks went dancing before Him.

প্রভু দেখি’ বৃন্দাবনের বৃক্ষ-লতাগণে ৷  
অঙ্কুর-পুলক, মধু-অশ্রু বরিষণে ॥ ২০০ ॥

prabhu dekhi’ vṛndāvanera vṛkṣa-latā-gaṇe  
the Lord  seeing  of Vṛndāvana  the trees and creepers  
aṅkura-pulaka, madhu-aśru variṣaṇe  
sprouts  as hair standing on end  honey  as tears  shower

(200) Seeing the Lord, the trees and creepers of Vṛndāvana put forth sprouts like hair standing on end in ecstasy, and they rained honey like tears.

ফুল-ফল ভরি’ ডাল পড়ে প্রভু-পায় ৷  
বন্ধু দেখি’ বন্ধু যেন ‘ভেট’ লঞা যায় ॥ ২০১ ॥

phula-phala bhari’ ḍāla paḍe prabhu-pāya  
with flowers and fruits  laden  branches  fall  at the Lord’s feet  
bandhu dekhi’ bandhu yena ‘bheṭa’ lañā yāya  
a friend  seeing  a friend  as if  a gift  taking  goes

(201) Their branches, laden with flowers and fruits, bent down to the Lord’s feet, as a friend comes with a gift on seeing a friend.

প্রভু দেখি’ বৃন্দাবনের স্থাবর-জঙ্গম ৷  
আনন্দিত, বন্ধু যেন দেখে বন্ধুগণ ॥ ২০২ ॥

prabhu dekhi’ vṛndāvanera sthāvara-jaṅgama  
the Lord  seeing  of Vṛndāvana  the unmoving and moving beings  
ānandita, bandhu yena dekhe bandhu-gaṇa  
are joyful  a friend  as if  sees  friends

(202) Seeing the Lord, all the moving and unmoving beings of Vṛndāvana rejoiced, as a friend rejoices on seeing his friends.

তা-সবার প্রীতি দেখি’ প্রভু ভাবাবেশে ৷  
সবা-সনে ক্রীড়া করে হঞা তার বশে ॥ ২০৩ ॥

tā-sabāra prīti dekhi’ prabhu bhāvāveśe  
of them all  the love  seeing  the Lord  in rapture  
sabā-sane krīḍā kare hañā tāra vaśe  
with all  play  does  becoming  their  subject to

(203) Seeing the love of all of them, the Lord, rapt in ecstasy, played with them all, as if He were in their power.

প্রতি বৃক্ষ-লতা প্রভু করেন আলিঙ্গন ৷  
পুষ্পাদি ধ্যানে করেন কৃষ্ণে সমর্পণ ॥ ২০৪ ॥

prati vṛkṣa-latā prabhu karena āliṅgana  
every  tree and creeper  the Lord  makes  embrace  
puṣpādi dhyāne karena kṛṣṇe samarpaṇa  
flowers and the rest  in meditation  He makes  to Kṛṣṇa  offering

(204) The Lord embraced every tree and creeper, and in meditation He offered their flowers and other gifts to Kṛṣṇa.

অশ্রু-কম্প-পুলক-প্রেমে শরীর অস্থিরে ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ বল, ‘কৃষ্ণ’ বল, বলে উচ্চৈঃস্বরে ॥ ২০৫ ॥

aśru-kampa-pulaka-preme śarīra asthire  
tears  trembling  thrills  from love  His body  restless  
‘kṛṣṇa’ bala, ‘kṛṣṇa’ bala, bale uccaiḥsvare  
Kṛṣṇa  say  Kṛṣṇa  say  He says  in a loud voice

(205) His body was restless with the tears, trembling and thrills of love, and He called out in a loud voice, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’! Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!”

স্থাবর-জঙ্গম মিলি’ করে কৃষ্ণধ্বনি ৷  
প্রভুর গম্ভীর-স্বরে যেন প্রতিধ্বনি ॥ ২০৬ ॥

sthāvara-jaṅgama mili’ kare kṛṣṇa-dhvani  
the unmoving and moving beings  together  make  the sound of Kṛṣṇa  
prabhura gambhīra-svare yena prati-dhvani  
to the Lord’s  deep voice  as if  an echo

(206) All the moving and unmoving beings together cried out “Kṛṣṇa!”, as if echoing the Lord’s deep voice.

মৃগের গলা ধরি’ প্রভু করেন রোদনে ৷  
মৃগের পুলক অঙ্গে, অশ্রু নয়নে ॥ ২০৭ ॥

mṛgera galā dhari’ prabhu karena rodane  
of a deer  the neck  embracing  the Lord  makes  weeping  
mṛgera pulaka aṅge, aśru nayane  
the deer’s  thrill  on the body  tears  in the eyes

(207) Embracing a deer around the neck, the Lord wept, and the deer’s body thrilled, and tears filled its eyes.

বৃক্ষডালে শুক-শারী দিল দরশন ৷  
তাহা দেখি’ প্রভুর কিছু শুনিতে হৈল মন ॥ ২০৮ ॥

vṛkṣa-ḍāle śuka-śārī dila daraśana  
on the branch of a tree  a male and a female parrot  gave  sight of themselves  
tāhā dekhi’ prabhura kichu śunite haila mana  
that  seeing  in the Lord  something  to hear  arose  a wish

(208) On the branch of a tree a male and a female parrot appeared, and seeing them, the Lord wished to hear them say something.

শুক-শারিকা প্রভুর হাতে উড়ি’ পড়ে ৷  
প্রভুকে শুনাঞা কৃষ্ণের গুণ-শ্লোক পড়ে ॥ ২০৯ ॥

śuka-śārikā prabhura hāte uḍi’ paḍe  
the male and female parrots  the Lord’s  onto the hand  flying  alight  
prabhuke śunāñā kṛṣṇera guṇa-śloka paḍe  
for the Lord  to hear  Kṛṣṇa’s  of qualities  a verse  recite

(209) The parrots flew down onto the Lord’s hand, and for the Lord to hear they began reciting verses about Kṛṣṇa’s qualities.

সৌন্দর্য্যং ললনালিধৈর্য্যদলনং লীলা রমাস্তম্ভিনী  
বীর্য্যং কন্দুকিতাদ্রিবর্য্যমমলাঃ পারে পরার্দ্ধং গুণাঃ ৷  
শীলং সর্ব্বজনানুরঞ্জনমহো যস্যায়মস্মৎপ্রভু-  
র্বিশ্বং বিশ্বজনীনকীর্ত্তিরবতাৎ কৃষ্ণো জগন্মোহনঃ ॥ ২১০ ॥

saundaryaṁ lalanāli-dhairya-dalanaṁ līlā ramā-stambhinī  
beauty  of the rows of young women  the composure  crushing  pastimes  Ramā  astonishing  
vīryaṁ kandukitādri-varyam amalāḥ pāre parārdhaṁ guṇāḥ  
prowess  turning into a ball  the mountain  best  spotless  beyond  a parārdha  qualities  
śīlaṁ sarva-janānurañjanam aho yasyāyam asmat-prabhur  
character  all people  delighting  aho  whose  this  our Lord  
viśvaṁ viśva-janīna-kīrtir avatāt kṛṣṇo jagan-mohanaḥ  
the universe  for all the world beneficial  whose fame  may protect  Kṛṣṇa  the enchanter of the world

(210) (The male parrot recited:) “His beauty shatters the composure of the young women; His pastimes astonish Ramā (Lakṣmī); His strength turns the best of mountains into a ball; His spotless qualities are beyond all counting; His character delights all people. Ah, may our Lord Kṛṣṇa, the enchanter of the world, whose fame is a blessing for the whole world, protect the universe!” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 13.29)

শুক-মুখে শুনি’ তবে কৃষ্ণের বর্ণন ৷  
শারিকা পড়য়ে তবে রাধিকা-বর্ণন ॥ ২১১ ॥

śuka-mukhe śuni’ tabe kṛṣṇera varṇana  
from the parrot’s mouth  hearing  then  of Kṛṣṇa  the description  
śārikā paḍaye tabe rādhikā-varṇana  
the female parrot  recites  then  of Rādhikā  a description

(211) Having heard the description of Kṛṣṇa from the mouth of the male parrot, the female parrot then recited a description of Rādhikā:

শ্রীরাধিকায়াঃ প্রিয়তা স্বরূপতা  
সুশীলতা নর্ত্তনগানচাতুরী ৷  
গুণালিসম্পৎ কবিতা চ রাজতে  
জগন্মনোমোহন-চিত্তমোহিনী ॥ ২১২ ॥

śrī-rādhikāyāḥ priyatā svarūpatā  
of Śrī Rādhikā  the affection  the beauty of Her own form  
su-śīlatā nartana-gāna-cāturī  
the good character  in dancing  singing  the skill  
guṇāli-sampat kavitā ca rājate  
of hosts of qualities  the wealth  poetry  and  shine  
jagan-mano-mohana-citta-mohinī  
of the world’s  minds  of the enchanter  the heart  enchanting

(212) “Śrī Rādhikā’s affection, the beauty of Her own form, Her good character, Her skill in dancing and singing, Her wealth of qualities and Her poetry shine forth; they enchant the heart of Him who enchants the minds of the whole world.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 13.30)[^39]

পুনঃ শুক কহে, — কৃষ্ণ ‘মদনমোহন’ ৷  
তবে আর শ্লোক শুক করিল পঠন ॥ ২১৩ ॥

punaḥ śuka kahe, — kṛṣṇa ‘madana-mohana’  
again  the parrot  says  Kṛṣṇa  is Madana-mohana  
tabe āra śloka śuka karila paṭhana  
then  another  verse  the parrot  made  recitation

(213) Again the male parrot said, “Kṛṣṇa is Madana-mohana, the enchanter of Cupid,” and recited another verse:

বংশীধারী জগন্নারী-চিত্তহারী স শারিকে ৷  
বিহারী গোপনারীভির্জীয়ান্মদনমোহনঃ ॥ ২১৪ ॥

vaṁśī-dhārī jagan-nārī-  
the holder of the flute  of the world’s women  
citta-hārī sa śārike  
the stealer of the hearts  He  O śārī  
vihārī gopa-nārībhir  
who sports  with the cowherd women  
jīyān madana-mohanaḥ  
may He be victorious  the enchanter of Cupid

(214) “O Śārikā, He who holds the flute and steals the hearts of the women of the world, who sports with the cowherd maidens — may that Madana-mohana be victorious!” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 13.31)

পুনঃ শারী কহে শুকে করি’ পরিহাস ৷  
তাহা শুনি’ প্রভুর হৈল বিস্ময়-প্রেমোল্লাস ॥ ২১৫ ॥

punaḥ śārī kahe śuke kari’ parihāsa  
again  the female parrot  says  to the male  making  a jest  
tāhā śuni’ prabhura haila vismaya-premollāsa  
that  hearing  in the Lord  arose  wonder  and the joy of love

(215) Then the female parrot spoke to the male parrot in jest, and hearing her, the Lord was filled with wonder and the joy of love:

রাধা-সঙ্গে যদা ভাতি, তদা ‘মদনমোহনঃ’ ৷  
অন্যথা বিশ্বমোহোঽপি স্বয়ং ‘মদনমোহিতঃ’ ॥ ২১৬ ॥

rādhā-saṅge yadā bhāti  
with Rādhā  when  He shines  
tadā ‘madana-mohanaḥ’  
then  He is “Madana-mohana”  
anyathā viśva-moho ’pi  
otherwise  the enchanter of the universe  though  
svayaṁ ‘madana-mohitaḥ’  
Himself  is “madana-mohita”

(216) “When He shines with Rādhā, then He is ‘Madana-mohana,’ the enchanter of Cupid. Otherwise, though He enchants the universe, He Himself is ‘madana-mohita,’ enchanted by Cupid.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 13.32)[^40]

শুক-শারী উড়ি’ পুনঃ গেল বৃক্ষডালে ৷  
ময়ূরের নৃত্য প্রভু দেখে কুতূহলে ॥ ২১৭ ॥

śuka-śārī uḍi’ punaḥ gela vṛkṣa-ḍāle  
the male and female parrots  flying up  again  went  to the branch of a tree  
mayūrera nṛtya prabhu dekhe kutūhale  
of the peacocks  the dance  the Lord  watches  with eagerness

(217) The parrots flew back up to the branch of the tree, and the Lord eagerly watched the dancing of the peacocks.

ময়ূরের কণ্ঠ দেখি’ প্রভুর কৃষ্ণকান্তি-স্মৃতি হৈল ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে মহাপ্রভু ভূমিতে পড়িল ॥ ২১৮ ॥

mayūrera kaṇṭha dekhi’ prabhura kṛṣṇa-kānti-smṛti haila  
of the peacocks  the necks  seeing  in the Lord  of Kṛṣṇa’s luster  the remembrance  arose  
premāveśe mahāprabhu bhūmite paḍila  
in the rapture of love  Mahāprabhu  to the ground  fell

(218) Seeing the necks of the peacocks, the Lord remembered the luster of Kṛṣṇa’s body, and Mahāprabhu fell to the ground in the rapture of love.

প্রভুরে মূর্চ্ছিত দেখি’ সেই ত’ ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য-সঙ্গে করে প্রভুর সন্তর্পণ ॥ ২১৯ ॥

prabhure mūrcchita dekhi’ sei ta’ brāhmaṇa  
the Lord  unconscious  seeing  that  very  brāhmaṇa  
bhaṭṭācārya-saṅge kare prabhura santarpaṇa  
with the Bhaṭṭācārya  does  the Lord’s  reviving

(219) Seeing the Lord unconscious, the brāhmaṇa and the Bhaṭṭācārya tended Him to revive Him.

আস্তে-ব্যস্তে মহাপ্রভুর লঞা বহির্ব্বাস ৷  
জলসেক করে অঙ্গে, বস্ত্রের বাতাস ॥ ২২০ ॥

āste-vyaste mahāprabhura lañā bahirvāsa  
hastily  hurriedly  Mahāprabhu’s  taking  outer cloth  
jala-seka kare aṅge, vastrera vātāsa  
sprinkling of water  they do  on the body  with the cloth  fanning

(220) Hurriedly they took Mahāprabhu’s outer cloth, sprinkled His body with water and fanned Him with the cloth.

প্রভু-কর্ণে কৃষ্ণনাম কহে উচ্চ করি’ ৷  
চেতন পাঞা প্রভু যা’ন গড়াগড়ি ॥ ২২১ ॥

prabhu-karṇe kṛṣṇa-nāma kahe ucca kari’  
in the Lord’s ear  the name of Kṛṣṇa  they say  loud  making it  
cetana pāñā prabhu yā’na gaḍāgaḍi  
consciousness  regaining  the Lord  goes  rolling on the ground

(221) They spoke the name of Kṛṣṇa loudly in the Lord’s ear, and regaining consciousness, the Lord rolled about on the ground.

কণ্টক-দুর্গম বনে অঙ্গ ক্ষত হৈল ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য কোলে করি’ প্রভুরে সুস্থ কৈল ॥ ২২২ ॥

kaṇṭaka-durgama vane aṅga kṣata haila  
with thorns  impassable  in the forest  His body  wounded  became  
bhaṭṭācārya kole kari’ prabhure sustha kaila  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  on his lap  taking  the Lord  calm  made

(222) In that forest, impassable with thorns, His body was scratched and cut. The Bhaṭṭācārya took the Lord on his lap and calmed Him.

কৃষ্ণাবেশে প্রভুর প্রেমে গরগর মন ৷  
‘বোল্’ ‘বোল্’ করি’ উঠি’ করেন নর্ত্তন ॥ ২২৩ ॥

kṛṣṇāveśe prabhura preme garagara mana  
absorbed in Kṛṣṇa  the Lord’s  with love  overwhelmed  mind  
‘bol’ ‘bol’ kari’ uṭhi’ karena nartana  
say  say  crying  rising  He does  dancing

(223) Absorbed in Kṛṣṇa, the Lord’s mind was overwhelmed with love. Crying “Say it! Say it!” He got up and began to dance.

ভট্টাচার্য্য, সেই বিপ্র কৃষ্ণনাম গায় ৷  
নাচিতে নাচিতে পথে প্রভু চলি’ যায় ॥ ২২৪ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya, sei vipra kṛṣṇa-nāma gāya  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  that  brāhmaṇa  the name of Kṛṣṇa  sing  
nācite nācite pathe prabhu cali’ yāya  
dancing  dancing  along the path  the Lord  goes  on

(224) The Bhaṭṭācārya and the brāhmaṇa sang the name of Kṛṣṇa, and the Lord went on along the path, dancing all the way.

প্রভুর প্রেমাবেশ দেখি’ ব্রাহ্মণ — বিস্মিত ৷  
প্রভুর রক্ষা লাগি’ বিপ্র হইলা চিন্তিত ॥ ২২৫ ॥

prabhura premāveśa dekhi’ brāhmaṇa — vismita  
of the Lord  the rapture of love  seeing  the brāhmaṇa  is amazed  
prabhura rakṣā lāgi’ vipra hailā cintita  
of the Lord  protection  for the sake of  the brāhmaṇa  became  anxious

(225) The brāhmaṇa was amazed to see the Lord’s rapture of love, and he grew anxious about how to keep the Lord safe.

নীলাচলে ছিলা যৈছে প্রেমাবেশ মন ৷  
বৃন্দাবন যাইতে পথে হৈল শত-গুণ ॥ ২২৬ ॥

nīlācale chilā yaiche premāveśa mana  
in Nīlācala  was  as  absorbed in love  His mind  
vṛndāvana yāite pathe haila śata-guṇa  
to Vṛndāvana  going  on the way  it became  a hundredfold

(226) The absorption in love His mind had known in Nīlācala became a hundred times greater on the way to Vṛndāvana.

সহস্রগুণ প্রেম বাড়ে মথুরা-দরশনে ৷  
লক্ষগুণ প্রেম বাড়ে, ভ্রমেন যবে বনে ॥ ২২৭ ॥

sahasra-guṇa prema bāḍe mathurā-daraśane  
a thousandfold  love  increases  at the sight of Mathurā  
lakṣa-guṇa prema bāḍe, bhramena yabe vane  
a hundred-thousandfold  love  increases  He wanders  when  in the forests

(227) At the sight of Mathurā His love increased a thousandfold, and when He wandered in the forests it increased a hundred-thousandfold.

অন্য দেশে প্রেম উছলে ‘বৃন্দাবন’-নামে ৷  
সাক্ষাৎ ভ্রময়ে এবে সেই বৃন্দাবনে ॥ ২২৮ ॥

প্রেমে গরগর মন রাত্রি-দিবসে ৷  
স্নান-ভিক্ষাদি-নির্ব্বাহ করেন অভ্যাসে ॥ ২২৯ ॥

anya deśe prema uchale ‘vṛndāvana’-nāme  
in other  lands  love  overflows  at the name of Vṛndāvana  
sākṣāt bhramaye ebe sei vṛndāvane  
in person  He wanders  now  in that very  Vṛndāvana  
preme garagara mana rātri-divase  
with love  overwhelmed  His mind  night and day  
snāna-bhikṣādi-nirvāha karena abhyāse  
bathing  eating and the rest  carrying out  He does  by habit

(228–229) In other lands His love would overflow at the very name of Vṛndāvana, and now He was wandering in Vṛndāvana itself. His mind was overwhelmed with love night and day; He bathed, ate and did everything else only out of habit.

এইমত প্রেম, যাবৎ ভ্রমিল ‘বার’ বন ৷  
একত্র লিখিলুঁ, সর্ব্বত্র না যায় বর্ণন ॥ ২৩০ ॥

ei-mata prema, yāvat bhramila ‘bāra’ vana  
such  love  as long as  He wandered  the twelve  forests  
ekatra likhilum̐, sarvatra nā yāya varṇana  
in one place  I have written  everywhere  not  can be  the description

(230) Such was His love all the while He wandered through the twelve forests. I have described it in one place; it cannot be described everywhere.

বৃন্দাবনে হৈল প্রভুর যতেক প্রেমের বিকার ৷  
কোটি-গ্রন্থে ‘অনন্ত’ লিখেন তাহার বিস্তার ॥ ২৩১ ॥

vṛndāvane haila prabhura yateka premera vikāra  
in Vṛndāvana  there were  the Lord’s  all the  of love  transformations  
koṭi-granthe ‘ananta’ likhena tāhāra vistāra  
in millions of books  Ananta  writes  their  details

(231) Ananta writes in millions of books about all the transformations of love the Lord showed in Vṛndāvana.

তবু লিখিবারে নারে তার এক কণ ৷  
উদ্দেশ করিতে করি দিগ্দরশন ॥ ২৩২ ॥

tabu likhibāre nāre tāra eka kaṇa  
still  to write  He cannot  of it  one  particle  
uddeśa karite kari dig-daraśana  
a pointer  to give  I make  an indication of direction

(232) Yet even He cannot describe a particle of them. To give some indication, I only point the way.

জগৎ ভাসিল চৈতন্যলীলার পাথারে ৷  
যাঁর যত শক্তি, তত পাথারে সাঁতারে ॥ ২৩৩ ॥

jagat bhāsila caitanya-līlāra pāthāre  
the world  was flooded  of Caitanya’s pastimes  in the ocean  
yām̐ra yata śakti, tata pāthāre sām̐tāre  
whose  as much  strength  so much  in the ocean  swims

(233) The world was flooded by the ocean of Caitanya’s pastimes; everyone swims in that ocean as far as his strength allows.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ২৩৪ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(234) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^9]: Edition heading: “Though before outsiders the Lord hides the signs of His love, people gain bhakti merely by seeing Him and hearing Him chant the name.”

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: *bhinna-prāya* — living apart from cultured society, that is, “almost savage”. Edition heading: “In Jhārikhaṇḍa the Lord delivers even people wholly turned away from Kṛṣṇa.”

By the rules of Śaṅkara’s sannyāsīs alms may be accepted only in the house of a brāhmaṇa by birth, but Mahāprabhu looked not at birth, only at whether a man was a Vaiṣṇava. Edition heading: “The Lord is served by brāhmaṇas by initiation.”

[^12]: Edition heading: “The Lord bathes in springs three times a day and keeps off the cold with a wood fire.”

[^13]: *Paramānanda-mādhava*, “Mādhava, the supreme bliss.” *Anubhāṣya*: the words name both Śrī Paramānanda Svāmī of the Viṣṇusvāmī line and Bhagavān Himself, the worshipful Lord of Śrīdhara Svāmī; the mute becomes eloquent in chanting of Kṛṣṇa, and the lame crosses the mountain, doing even the impossible in Kṛṣṇa’s service. Edition heading: the sixth verse of Śrīdhara Svāmī’s invocation at the start of his *Bhāvārtha-dīpikā* on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.1.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: Kāśī, also called Vārāṇasī or Avimukta, is a most ancient city, named after the rivers Asi and Varuṇā. The name of the Maṇikarṇikā ghāṭa is explained in different ways: here a jewel (*maṇi*) fell from the ear (*karṇa*) of Viṣṇu — or, some say, of Śiva; or here Viśvanātha places in the ear of the dying the *tāraka-brahma*, the name of Rāma.

[^15]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this is Raghunātha, Tapana Miśra’s son, later renowned as Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: in Kāśī, chief holy place of the Śaivas and of the worshippers of five deities, there is no talk of serving Hari, and for a devotee of Gaura it is no place to live; so Candraśekhara bitterly says he lives there by his *prārabdha*, for past sins. This is self-reproach: a pure devotee may speak of himself so, but he does not reap the fruits of good and bad deeds; even *sādhana-bhakti* destroys sin, its seed and ignorance. Edition heading: “Kāśī, where there is no talk of serving Hari, is the dwelling of the dry Māyāvādīs.”

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: the six *darśanas* are Vaiśeṣika (of the sage Kaṇāda), Nyāya (Gotama), Yoga (Patañjali), Sāṅkhya (Kapila), Pūrva- or Karma-mīmāṁsā (Jaimini) and Uttara- or Brahma-mīmāṁsā, that is, Vedānta (Vedavyāsa).

[^18]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the two servants are Candraśekhara and Tapana Miśra.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: Madhya 1.239 says that this time the Lord stayed four days in Tapana Miśra’s house in Kāśī. Edition heading: “Bhagavān is subject to His devotees.”

[^20]: Edition heading: “Māyāvādī brāhmaṇas, not Vaiṣṇavas, are unfit to give the Lord His meals.”

[^21]: Edition heading: “The Lord, playing the part of an ācārya, avoids the company of the Māyāvādīs.”

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: Prakāśānanda was an *ekadaṇḍī sannyāsī* of Śaṅkara’s line who lived in Kāśī in Mahāprabhu’s time; the Lord Himself speaks of him in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Madhya 3 and 20): explaining the Vedas, he rejects His form (*vigraha*) and calls His body, pastimes and abode false. He is in no way the same person as the *tridaṇḍī-svāmī* Śrī Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī, a follower of Rāmānuja from Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra, the guru and uncle of Śrīla Gopāla Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: *bhāvuka*, “sentimentalist”: so the Māyāvādī Prakāśānanda calls the Lord, for he confuses Mahāprabhu’s transcendental *bhāva* with the artificial, short-lived fervour of those who follow a religion of the mind (*mano-dharma*); even the pure devotee’s spiritual chanting, dancing and music he puts on a level with worldly song and dance. Edition heading: “Prakāśānanda spews the poison of his Māyāvāda.”

[^24]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *bhāvakāli* — the ways of a *bhāvuka*, a sentimentalist. The Lord takes up this image in verses 144–145.

[^25]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the unruly are those who have broken the bonds of scriptural injunctions.

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: *doṣa* — blasphemy. Names such as ‘Brahman’, ‘Caitanya’, ‘Ātmā’, ‘Paramātmā’ and ‘Īśvara’, as the Māyāvādī understands them, point only in part to Kṛṣṇa’s majesty, never to His generosity and sweetness. The Māyāvādīs do not believe that the name, form, qualities and pastimes of the Absolute are spiritual, and so they are great offenders; one who seeks the highest good should not hear talk of the highest goal from their lips.

[^27]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Māyāvādī calls the form of Bhagavān “a form made of *māyā*” and holds His name, form, qualities and pastimes to be non-eternal. Rejecting Kṛṣṇa’s principal names (*mukhya-nāma*), he utters secondary ones (*gauṇa-nāma*) — ‘Brahman’, ‘Ātmā’, ‘Caitanya’. Even if “Kṛṣṇa” or “Govinda” escapes his lips, the name of Kṛṣṇa still does not come from his mouth, for he takes it to be an ordinary, mundane word.

[^30]: The same verse as Madhya 6.186; translation as there.
