“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 8  
**An Account of the Vaiṣṇavas’ Command to Compose the Book**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে চৈতন্যদেবং তং ভগবন্তং যদিচ্ছয়া ৷  
প্রসভং নর্ত্ত্যতে চিত্রং লেখরঙ্গে জড়োঽপ্যয়ম্ ॥ ১ ॥

vande caitanya-devaṁ taṁ  
I worship  Caitanya-deva  Him  
bhagavantaṁ yad-icchayā  
the Lord  by whose will  
prasabhaṁ nartyate citraṁ  
suddenly  is made to dance  wondrously  
lekha-raṅge jaḍo ’py ayam  
on the stage of writing  dull  though  this one

(1) I worship Lord Caitanya-deva, by whose will even this dull fool has suddenly been set dancing, wondrously, on the stage of writing.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
জয় জয় পরমানন্দ জয় নিত্যানন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya gauracandra  
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  Gauracandra  
jaya jaya paramānanda jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to the supremely blissful  glory  to Nityānanda

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! Glory, glory to Nityānanda, full of the highest bliss!

জয় জয়াদ্বৈতাচার্য্য জয় কৃপাময় ৷  
জয় জয় গদাধর পণ্ডিত মহাশয় ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jayādvaita ācārya jaya kṛpāmaya  
glory  glory  to Advaita Ācārya  glory  to the all-merciful  
jaya jaya gadādhara paṇḍita mahāśaya  
glory  glory  to Gadādhara  Paṇḍita  the noble-hearted

(3) Glory, glory to Advaita Ācārya, glory to the all-merciful! Glory, glory to the noble-hearted Gadādhara Paṇḍita!

জয় জয় শ্রীবাসাদি যত ভক্তগণ ৷  
প্রণত হইয়া বন্দোঁ সবার চরণ ॥ ৪ ॥

jaya jaya śrīvāsādi yata bhakta-gaṇa  
glory  glory  to Śrīvāsa and the rest  all  the devotees  
praṇata haiyā vandom̐ sabāra caraṇa  
bowed down  being  I worship  of all  the feet

(4) Glory, glory to Śrīvāsa and to all the devotees! Bowing low, I worship the feet of them all.

মূক কবিত্ব করে যাঁ-সবার স্মরণে ৷  
পঙ্গু গিরি লঙ্ঘে, অন্ধ দেখে তারাগণে ॥ ৫ ॥

mūka kavitva kare yām̐-sabāra smaraṇe  
the mute  poetry  makes  of whom all  by remembrance  
paṅgu giri laṅghe, andha dekhe tārā-gaṇe  
the lame  mountains  crosses  the blind  sees  the stars

(5) By remembering them the mute become poets, the lame cross mountains and the blind see the stars.

এ-সব না মানে যেই পণ্ডিতসকল ৷  
তা-সবার বিদ্যাপাঠ ভেক-কোলাহল ॥ ৬ ॥

e-saba nā māne yei paṇḍita sakala  
all this  not  accept  who  scholars  all  
tā-sabāra vidyā-pāṭha bheka-kolāhala  
of them all  the study of learning  the croaking of frogs

(6) Scholars who do not accept all this — their learning is the croaking of frogs.

এই সব না মানে যেবা, করে কৃষ্ণভক্তি ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-কৃপা নাহি তারে, নাহি তার গতি ॥ ৭ ॥

ei saba nā māne yebā, kare kṛṣṇa-bhakti  
all this  not  accepts  whoever  practises  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  
kṛṣṇa-kṛpā nāhi tāre, nāhi tāra gati  
Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  is not  on him  there is not  his  goal

(7) Whoever practises devotion to Kṛṣṇa yet does not accept all this receives no mercy from Kṛṣṇa and reaches no goal.[^2]

পূর্ব্বে যেন জরাসন্ধ-আদি রাজাগণ ৷  
বেদ-ধর্ম্ম করি’ করে বিষ্ণুর পূজন ॥ ৮ ॥

pūrve yena jarāsandha-ādi rājā-gaṇa  
formerly  as  Jarāsandha and other  kings  
veda-dharma kari’ kare viṣṇura pūjana  
Vedic dharma  performing  do  of Viṣṇu  worship

(8) Long ago Jarāsandha and other kings followed the Vedic dharma and worshipped Viṣṇu.

কৃষ্ণ নাহি মানে, তাতে দৈত্য করি’ মানি ৷  
চৈতন্য না মানিলে তৈছে দৈত্য তারে জানি ॥ ৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa nāhi māne, tāte daitya kari’ māni  
Kṛṣṇa  not  they accepted  therefore  as demons  we regard  
caitanya nā mānile taiche daitya tāre jāni  
Caitanya  not  accepting  likewise  a demon  him  we know

(9) But they did not accept Kṛṣṇa, and so we regard them as demons; and whoever does not accept Caitanya we know to be a demon in the same way.[^3]

মোরে না মানিলে সব লোক হবে নাশ ৷  
ইথে লাগি’ কৃপার্দ্র প্রভু করিল সন্ন্যাস ॥ ১০ ॥

more nā mānile saba loka habe nāśa  
Me  not  accepting  all  people  will be  lost  
ithe lāgi’ kṛpārdra prabhu karila sannyāsa  
for this  reason  melting with mercy  the Lord  accepted  sannyāsa

(10) “If they do not accept Me, all people will be lost.” For this reason the Lord, His heart melting with mercy, accepted sannyāsa.

সন্ন্যাসী-বুদ্ধ্যে মোরে করিবে নমস্কার ৷  
তথাপি খণ্ডিবে দুঃখ, পাইবে নিস্তার ॥ ১১ ॥

sannyāsī-buddhye more karibe namaskāra  
thinking Me a sannyāsī  to Me  they will make  obeisance  
tathāpi khaṇḍibe duḥkha, pāibe nistāra  
even so  will be cut  misery  they will attain  deliverance

(11) “Even if they bow to Me thinking Me an ordinary sannyāsī, their misery will be cut away and they will find deliverance.”[^4]

হেন কৃপাময় চৈতন্য না ভজে যেই জন ৷  
সর্ব্বোত্তম হইলেও তারে অসুরে গণন ॥ ১২ ॥

hena kṛpāmaya caitanya nā bhaje yei jana  
such  merciful  Caitanya  not  worships  who  a person  
sarvottama haileo tāre asure gaṇana  
the highest of all  even being  him  among the asuras  is counted

(12) Whoever does not worship Caitanya, so full of mercy, is counted among the asuras, though he be the greatest of men.[^5]

অতএব পুনঃ কহোঁ ঊর্ধ্ব বাহু হঞা ৷  
চৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ ভজ কুতর্ক ছাড়িয়া ॥ ১৩ ॥

ataeva punaḥ kahom̐ ūrdhva bāhu hañā  
therefore  again  I say  upraised  arms  with  
caitanya-nityānanda bhaja kutarka chāḍiyā  
Caitanya and Nityānanda  worship  false argument  giving up

(13) Therefore, with arms upraised, I say again: give up false argument and worship Caitanya and Nityānanda!

যদি বা তার্কিক কহে, — তর্ক সে প্রমাণ ৷  
তর্কশাস্ত্রে সিদ্ধ যেই, সেই সেব্যমান ॥ ১৪ ॥

yadi vā tārkika kahe, — tarka se pramāṇa  
if  or  a logician  says  logic  that  is the proof  
tarka-śāstre siddha yei, sei sevyamāna  
in the science of logic  established  whatever  that  is to be worshipped

(14) But a logician may say: “Logic is the proof. Whatever the science of logic establishes is what deserves worship.”

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-দয়া করহ বিচার ৷  
বিচার করিলে চিত্তে পাবে চমৎকার ॥ ১৫ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-dayā karaha vicāra  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s  mercy  make  consideration  
vicāra karile citte pābe camatkāra  
consideration  having made  in the heart  you will find  wonder

(15) Then weigh the mercy of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya; weigh it well, and your heart will be struck with wonder.[^6]

বহু জন্ম করে যদি শ্রবণ, কীর্ত্তন ৷  
তবু ত’ না পায় কৃষ্ণপদে প্রেমধন ॥ ১৬ ॥

bahu janma kare yadi śravaṇa, kīrtana  
many  births  does  if  hearing  chanting  
tabu ta’ nā pāya kṛṣṇa-pade prema-dhana  
still  indeed  not  obtains  at Kṛṣṇa’s feet  the treasure of love

(16) One may hear and chant for many births, and still not obtain the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa’s feet.[^7]

জ্ঞানতঃ সুলভা মুক্তির্ভুক্তির্যজ্ঞাদিপুণ্যতঃ ৷  
সেয়ং সাধনসাহস্রৈর্হরিভক্তিঃ সুদুর্ল্লভা ॥ ১৭ ॥

jñānataḥ su-labhā muktir  
through knowledge  easily obtained  liberation  
bhuktir yajñādi-puṇyataḥ  
enjoyment  through the merit of sacrifice and the like  
seyaṁ sādhana-sāhasrair  
this very  by thousands of practices  
hari-bhaktiḥ su-durlabhā  
devotion to Hari  very hard to attain

(17) “Liberation is easily won through knowledge, and enjoyment through the merit of sacrifices and other pious works; but devotion to Hari is hard to attain even by thousands of spiritual practices.” (Tantra; also Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.1.36)[^8]

কৃষ্ণ যদি ছুটে ভক্তে ভুক্তি মুক্তি দিয়া ৷  
কভু ভক্তি না দেন রাখেন লুকাইয়া ॥ ১৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa yadi chuṭe bhakte bhukti mukti diyā  
Kṛṣṇa  if  slips away  to the devotee  enjoyment  liberation  giving  
kabhu bhakti nā dena rākhena lukāiyā  
ever  devotion  not  He gives  He keeps  hiding

(18) Kṛṣṇa may slip away from a devotee by giving him enjoyment or liberation, but devotion He never gives: He keeps it hidden.[^9]

রাজন্ পতির্গুরুরলং ভবতাং যদূনাং  
দৈবং প্রিয়ঃ কুলপতিঃ ক্ব চ কিঙ্করো বঃ ৷  
অস্ত্বেবমঙ্গ ভগবান্ ভজতাং মুকুন্দো  
মুক্তিং দদাতি কর্হিচিৎ স্ম ন ভক্তিযোগম্ ॥ ১৯ ॥

rājan patir gurur alaṁ bhavatāṁ yadūnāṁ  
O king  master  guru  indeed  of you  of the Yadus  
daivaṁ priyaḥ kula-patiḥ kva ca kiṅkaro vaḥ  
worshipped Deity  dear one  head of the family  sometimes  and  servant  of you  
astv evam aṅga bhagavān bhajatāṁ mukundo  
let it be  so  dear one  the Lord  to those who worship  Mukunda  
muktiṁ dadāti karhicit sma na bhakti-yogam  
liberation  gives  at any time  indeed  not  bhakti-yoga

(19) (Śukadeva said:) “O King, Mukunda is the master and guru of you and of the Yadus, your worshipped Deity, your dearest friend and the head of your family — and at times He even becomes your servant. So it is, dear one; yet to those who worship Him the Lord gives liberation, but never bhakti-yoga.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.6.18)[^10]

হেন প্রেম শ্রীচৈতন্য দিলা যথা তথা ৷  
জগাই মাধাই পর্য্যন্ত — অন্যের কা কথা ॥ ২০ ॥

hena prema śrī-caitanya dilā yathā tathā  
such  love  Śrī Caitanya  gave  here and there  
jagāi mādhāi paryanta — anyera kā kathā  
Jagāi  Mādhāi  even to  of others  what  talk

(20) Such love Śrī Caitanya gave everywhere, even to Jagāi and Mādhāi — what then of others?[^11]

স্বতন্ত্র-ঈশ্বর প্রেম — নিগূঢ় ভাণ্ডার ৷  
বিলাইল যারে তারে, না কৈল বিচার ॥ ২১ ॥

svatantra-īśvara prema — nigūḍha-bhāṇḍāra  
the independent Lord  love  a hidden  storehouse  
bilāila yāre tāre, nā kaila vicāra  
gave away  to whomever  to anyone  not  He made  discrimination

(21) The independent Lord gave away prema, that hidden storehouse, to anyone at all, and made no distinctions.

অদ্যাপিহ দেখ চৈতন্য-নাম যেই লয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-প্রেমে পুলকাশ্রু-বিহ্বল সে হয় ॥ ২২ ॥

adyāpiha dekha caitanya-nāma yei laya  
even today  see  Caitanya’s name  whoever  takes  
kṛṣṇa-preme pulakāśru-vihvala se haya  
in love for Kṛṣṇa  with thrills and tears  overwhelmed  he  becomes

(22) See it even today: whoever takes the name of Caitanya is overwhelmed with love for Kṛṣṇa, his body thrilling and his eyes streaming with tears.[^12]

‘নিত্যানন্দ’ বলিতে হয় কৃষ্ণপ্রেমোদয় ৷  
আউলায় সকল অঙ্গ, অশ্রু-গঙ্গা বয় ॥ ২৩ ॥

‘nityānanda’ balite haya kṛṣṇa-premodaya  
‘Nityānanda’  on saying  there is  the rising of love for Kṛṣṇa  
āulāya sakala aṅga, aśru-gaṅgā vaya  
are thrown into turmoil  all  the limbs  a Ganges of tears  flows

(23) At the name “Nityānanda” love for Kṛṣṇa awakens; every limb is shaken, and a Ganges of tears flows.

‘কৃষ্ণনাম’ করে অপরাধের বিচার ৷  
কৃষ্ণ বলিলে অপরাধীর না হয় বিকার ॥ ২৪ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa-nāma’ kare aparādhera vicāra  
the name of Kṛṣṇa  makes  of offences  consideration  
kṛṣṇa balile aparādhīra nā haya vikāra  
‘Kṛṣṇa’  on saying  of the offender  not  there is  transformation

(24) The name of Kṛṣṇa takes account of offences: when an offender says “Kṛṣṇa”, no transformation of love takes place in him.[^13]

তদশ্মসারং হৃদয়ং বতেদং  
যদ্গৃহ্যমাণৈর্হরিনামধেয়ৈঃ ৷  
ন বিক্রিয়েতাথ যদা বিকারো  
নেত্রে জলং গাত্ররুহেষু হর্ষঃ ॥ ২৫ ॥

tad aśma-sāraṁ hṛdayaṁ batedaṁ  
that  hard as stone  heart  alas  this  
yad gṛhyamāṇair hari-nāma-dheyaiḥ  
which  being taken  by the names of Hari  
na vikriyetātha yadā vikāro  
is not  changed  but  when  there is transformation  
netre jalaṁ gātra-ruheṣu harṣaḥ  
in the eyes  water  in the hairs of the body  joy

(25) “Alas, that heart is hard as stone which does not change when the names of Hari are chanted; for when the heart is changed, tears fill the eyes and the hairs of the body stand on end in joy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.24)[^14]

‘এক’ কৃষ্ণনামে করে সর্ব্বপাপ নাশ ৷  
প্রেমের কারণ ভক্তি করেন প্রকাশ ॥ ২৬ ॥

‘eka’ kṛṣṇa-nāme kare sarva-pāpa nāśa  
‘one’  by the name of Kṛṣṇa  is made  of all sins  destruction  
premera kāraṇa bhakti karena prakāśa  
of love  the cause  devotion  He makes  manifest

(26) A single name of Kṛṣṇa destroys all sins and reveals devotion, from which love is born.[^15]

প্রেমের উদয়ে হয় প্রেমের বিকার ৷  
স্বেদ-কম্প-পুলকাদি গদ্গদাশ্রুধার ॥ ২৭ ॥

premera udaye haya premera vikāra  
of love  at the rising  there are  of love  the transformations  
sveda-kampa-pulakādi gadgadāśrudhāra  
sweat  trembling  thrilling of the hairs and the rest  faltering voice  streams of tears

(27) When love dawns, its transformations appear: sweat and trembling, the hairs standing on end, a faltering voice and streams of tears.

অনায়াসে ভবক্ষয়, কৃষ্ণের সেবন ৷  
এক কৃষ্ণনামের ফলে পাই এত ধন ॥ ২৮ ॥

anāyāse bhava-kṣaya, kṛṣṇera sevana  
effortlessly  the end of worldly existence  of Kṛṣṇa  service  
eka kṛṣṇa-nāmera phale pāi eta dhana  
of one  name of Kṛṣṇa  as the fruit  one gains  such  wealth

(28) Worldly existence ends without effort, and one is given the service of Kṛṣṇa: such is the wealth that a single name of Kṛṣṇa brings.

হেন কৃষ্ণনাম যদি লয় বহুবার ৷  
তবু যদি প্রেম নহে, নহে অশ্রুধার ॥ ২৯ ॥

তবে জানি, অপরাধ তাহাতে প্রচুর ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম-বীজ তাহে না করে অঙ্কুর ॥ ৩০ ॥

hena kṛṣṇa-nāma yadi laya bahu-bāra  
such  the name of Kṛṣṇa  if  one takes  many  times  
tabu yadi prema nahe, nahe aśrudhāra  
still  if  love  is not  are not  streams of tears  
tabe jāni, aparādha tāhāte pracura  
then  I know  offences  in him  abundant  
kṛṣṇa-nāma-bīja tāhe nā kare aṅkura  
the seed of the name of Kṛṣṇa  there  not  makes  a sprout

(29–30) If one takes such a name of Kṛṣṇa again and again, and still love does not come, nor streams of tears, then I know that his offences are many: the seed of the name of Kṛṣṇa does not sprout in him.[^16]

চৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দে নাহি এসব বিচার ৷  
নাম লৈতে প্রেম দেন, বহে অশ্রুধার ॥ ৩১ ॥

caitanya-nityānande nāhi esaba vicāra  
in Caitanya and Nityānanda  there is not  all such  consideration  
nāma laite prema dena, vahe aśrudhāra  
the name  on taking  love  They give  flow  streams of tears

(31) With Caitanya and Nityānanda there is no such weighing of offences: as soon as one takes Their names They give love, and streams of tears flow.[^17]

স্বতন্ত্র ঈশ্বর প্রভু অত্যন্ত উদার ৷  
তাঁরে না ভজিলে কভু না হয় নিস্তার ॥ ৩২ ॥

svatantra īśvara prabhu atyanta udāra  
independent  the Lord  the Master  exceedingly  magnanimous  
tām̐re nā bhajile kabhu nā haya nistāra  
Him  not  worshipping  ever  not  there is  deliverance

(32) The Lord is independent and supremely magnanimous; without worshipping Him there is never any deliverance.[^18]

ওরে মূঢ় লোক, শুন চৈতন্যমঙ্গল ৷  
চৈতন্য-মহিমা যাতে জানিবে সকল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ore mūḍha loka, śuna caitanya-maṅgala  
O  foolish  people  hear  the Caitanya-maṅgala  
caitanya-mahimā yāte jānibe sakala  
Caitanya’s glory  in which  you will know  all

(33) O foolish people, hear the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, and from it you will know all the glory of Caitanya![^19]

কৃষ্ণলীলা ভাগবতে কহে বেদব্যাস ৷  
চৈতন্য-লীলার ব্যাস — বৃন্দাবন দাস ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-līlā bhāgavate kahe veda-vyāsa  
Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  in the Bhāgavata  tells  Veda-vyāsa  
caitanya-līlāra vyāsa — vṛndāvana dāsa  
of Caitanya’s pastimes  the Vyāsa  Vṛndāvana dāsa

(34) Veda-vyāsa told the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa in the *Bhāgavata*; the Vyāsa of Caitanya’s pastimes is Vṛndāvana dāsa.

বৃন্দাবন দাস কৈল ‘চৈতন্যমঙ্গল’ ৷  
যাঁহার শ্রবণে নাশে সর্ব্ব অমঙ্গল ॥ ৩৫ ॥

vṛndāvana dāsa kaila ‘caitanya-maṅgala’  
Vṛndāvana  dāsa  composed  the Caitanya-maṅgala  
yām̐hāra śravaṇe nāśe sarva amaṅgala  
of which  by hearing  is destroyed  all  inauspiciousness

(35) Vṛndāvana dāsa composed the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, and by hearing it all that is inauspicious is destroyed.

চৈতন্য-নিতাইর যাতে জানিয়ে মহিমা ৷  
যাতে জানি কৃষ্ণভক্তিসিদ্ধান্তের সীমা ॥ ৩৬ ॥

caitanya-nitāira yāte jāniye mahimā  
of Caitanya and Nitāi  in which  one knows  the glory  
yāte jāni kṛṣṇa-bhakti-siddhāntera sīmā  
in which  I know  of the conclusions of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the utmost limit

(36) In it one comes to know the glory of Caitanya and Nitāi, and in it I find the utmost reach of the conclusions of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.[^20]

ভাগবতে যত ভক্তিসিদ্ধান্তের সার ৷  
লিখিয়াছেন ইঁহা জানি’ করিয়া উদ্ধার ॥ ৩৭ ॥

bhāgavate yata bhakti-siddhāntera sāra  
in the Bhāgavata  all  of the conclusions of devotion  the essence  
likhiyāchena im̐hā jāni’ kariyā uddhāra  
he has written  this  knowing  making  extraction

(37) Knowing all the essence of the conclusions of devotion in the *Bhāgavata*, he drew it out and set it down in writing.

‘চৈতন্যমঙ্গল’ শুনে যদি পাষণ্ডী, যবন ৷  
সেহ মহা-বৈষ্ণব হয় ততক্ষণ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

‘caitanya-maṅgala’ śune yadi pāṣaṇḍī, yavana  
the Caitanya-maṅgala  hears  if  an unbeliever  a yavana  
seha mahā-vaiṣṇava haya tata-kṣaṇa  
he too  a great Vaiṣṇava  becomes  at that moment

(38) If even an unbeliever or a yavana hears the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, he becomes a great Vaiṣṇava at that very moment.

মনুষ্যে রচিতে নারে ঐছে গ্রন্থ ধন্য ৷  
বৃন্দাবনদাস-মুখে বক্তা শ্রীচৈতন্য ॥ ৩৯ ॥

manuṣye racite nāre aiche grantha dhanya  
a human being  to compose  cannot  such  a book  blessed  
vṛndāvana-dāsa-mukhe vaktā śrī-caitanya  
through the mouth of Vṛndāvana dāsa  the speaker  Śrī Caitanya

(39) No human being could compose so blessed a book: it is Śrī Caitanya who speaks through the mouth of Vṛndāvana dāsa.

বৃন্দাবনদাস-পদে কোটি নমস্কার ৷  
ঐছে গ্রন্থ করি’ তেঁহো তারিলা সংসার ॥ ৪০ ॥

vṛndāvana-dāsa-pade koṭi namaskāra  
at the feet of Vṛndāvana dāsa  millions  of obeisances  
aiche grantha kari’ tem̐ho tārilā saṁsāra  
such  a book  making  he  delivered  the world

(40) Millions of obeisances at the feet of Vṛndāvana dāsa! By writing such a book he delivered the world.

নারায়ণী — চৈতন্যের উচ্ছিষ্ট-ভাজন ৷  
তাঁর গর্ভে জন্মিলা শ্রীদাস-বৃন্দাবন ॥ ৪১ ॥

nārāyaṇī — caitanyera ucchiṣṭa-bhājana  
Nārāyaṇī  of Caitanya  the remnants  partaker  
tām̐ra garbhe janmilā śrī-dāsa-vṛndāvana  
her  in the womb  was born  Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa

(41) Nārāyaṇī partook of Caitanya’s remnants, and from her womb Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa was born.[^21]

তাঁর কি অদ্ভুত চৈতন্যচরিত-বর্ণন ৷  
যাহার শ্রবণে শুদ্ধ কৈল ত্রিভুবন ॥ ৪২ ॥

tām̐ra ki adbhuta caitanya-carita-varṇana  
his  what  wonderful  of Caitanya’s deeds  description  
yāhāra śravaṇe śuddha kaila tri-bhuvana  
of which  by the hearing  pure  were made  the three worlds

(42) How wonderful is his description of Caitanya’s deeds! By hearing it the three worlds were purified.

অতএব ভজ, লোক, চৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
খণ্ডিবে সংসার-দুঃখ, পাবে প্রেমানন্দ ॥ ৪৩ ॥

ataeva bhaja, loka, caitanya-nityānanda  
therefore  worship  O people  Caitanya and Nityānanda  
khaṇḍibe saṁsāra-duḥkha, pābe premānanda  
will be cut  the misery of worldly life  you will attain  the bliss of love

(43) Therefore, O people, worship Caitanya and Nityānanda! The misery of worldly life will be cut away, and you will attain the bliss of love.

বৃন্দাবন-দাস কৈল ‘চৈতন্য-মঙ্গল’ ৷  
তাহাতে চৈতন্য-লীলা বর্ণিল সকল ॥ ৪৪ ॥

vṛndāvana-dāsa kaila ‘caitanya-maṅgala’  
Vṛndāvana dāsa  composed  the Caitanya-maṅgala  
tāhāte caitanya-līlā varṇila sakala  
in it  Caitanya’s pastimes  he described  all

(44) Vṛndāvana dāsa composed the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, and in it he told all the pastimes of Caitanya.

সূত্র করি’ সব লীলা করিল গ্রন্থন ৷  
পাছে বিস্তারিয়া তাহার কৈল বিবরণ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

sūtra kari’ saba līlā karila granthana  
as a summary  making  all  the pastimes  he composed  
pāche vistāriyā tāhāra kaila vivaraṇa  
afterwards  expanding  of them  he made  the description

(45) First he set down all the pastimes in brief, as a summary, and afterwards described them at length.

চৈতন্যচন্দ্রের লীলা অনন্ত অপার ৷  
বর্ণিতে বর্ণিতে গ্রন্থ হইল বিস্তার ॥ ৪৬ ॥

caitanya-candrera līlā ananta apāra  
of Caitanya-candra  the pastimes  endless  boundless  
varṇite varṇite grantha haila vistāra  
describing  describing  the book  became  large

(46) The pastimes of Caitanya-candra are endless and boundless, and as he went on describing them the book grew long.

বিস্তার দেখিয়া কিছু সঙ্কোচ হৈল মন ৷  
সূত্রধৃত কোন লীলা না কৈল বর্ণন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

vistāra dekhiyā kichu saṅkoca haila mana  
the length  seeing  somewhat  hesitant  became  his mind  
sūtra-dhṛta kona līlā nā kaila varṇana  
held in the summary  some  pastimes  not  he made  description

(47) Seeing its length, he grew somewhat hesitant, and some of the pastimes held in the summary he did not describe.[^22]

নিত্যানন্দ-লীলা-বর্ণনে হইল আবেশ ৷  
চৈতন্যের শেষ-লীলা রহিল অবশেষ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

nityānanda-līlā-varṇane haila āveśa  
in describing Nityānanda’s pastimes  there was  absorption  
caitanyera śeṣa-līlā rahila avaśeṣa  
of Caitanya  the final pastimes  remained  left over

(48) He became absorbed in describing the pastimes of Nityānanda, and Caitanya’s final pastimes were left untold.

সেই সব লীলার শুনিতে বিবরণ ৷  
বৃন্দাবনবাসী ভক্তের উৎকণ্ঠিত মন ॥ ৪৯ ॥

sei saba līlāra śunite vivaraṇa  
of those  all  pastimes  to hear  the description  
vṛndāvana-vāsī bhaktera utkaṇṭhita mana  
the residents of Vṛndāvana  of the devotees  eager  the hearts

(49) The devotees of Vṛndāvana longed to hear the account of all those pastimes.

বৃন্দাবনে কল্প-দ্রুমে সুবর্ণ-সদন ৷  
মহা-যোগপীঠ তাহাঁ, রত্ন-সিংহাসন ॥ ৫০ ॥

vṛndāvane kalpa-drume suvarṇa-sadana  
in Vṛndāvana  at a wish-fulfilling tree  a golden  dwelling  
mahā-yogapīṭha tāhām̐, ratna-siṁhāsana  
the great  Yogapīṭha  there  a jewelled throne

(50) In Vṛndāvana, beneath a wish-fulfilling tree, stands a golden temple; there is the great Yogapīṭha, and in it a jewelled throne.

তাতে বসি’ আছে সদা ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ৷  
‘শ্রীগোবিন্দ-দেব’ নাম সাক্ষাৎ মদন ॥ ৫১ ॥

tāte vasi’ āche sadā vrajendra-nandana  
on it  seated  is  always  the son of the king of Vraja  
‘śrī-govinda-deva’ nāma sākṣāt madana  
Śrī Govinda-deva  by name  in person  Madana

(51) On it the son of the king of Vraja is ever seated: His name is Śrī Govinda-deva, the very god of love.

রাজ-সেবা হয় তাঁহা বিচিত্র প্রকার ৷  
দিব্য সামগ্রী, দিব্য বস্ত্র-অলঙ্কার ॥ ৫২ ॥

rāja-sevā haya tām̐hā vicitra prakāra  
royal service  is  there  of many  kinds  
divya sāmagrī, divya vastra-alaṅkāra  
divine  articles  divine  clothes and ornaments

(52) There He is served like a king in many wonderful ways, with divine offerings, divine garments and ornaments.

সহস্র সেবক সেবা করে অনুক্ষণ ৷  
সহস্র-বদনে সেবা না যায় বর্ণন ॥ ৫৩ ॥

sahasra sevaka sevā kare anukṣaṇa  
a thousand  servants  service  render  constantly  
sahasra-vadane sevā nā yāya varṇana  
with a thousand mouths  the service  not  can be  described

(53) A thousand servants serve Him constantly; that service could not be described even with a thousand mouths.

সেবার অধ্যক্ষ — শ্রীপণ্ডিত হরিদাস ৷  
তাঁর যশঃ-গুণ সর্ব্বজগতে প্রকাশ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

sevāra adhyakṣa — śrī-paṇḍita haridāsa  
of the service  the superintendent  Śrī Paṇḍita  Haridāsa  
tām̐ra yaśaḥ-guṇa sarva-jagate prakāśa  
his  fame and virtues  in all the world  are known

(54) The superintendent of that service is Śrī Haridāsa Paṇḍita, whose fame and virtues are known throughout the world.

সুশীল, সহিষ্ণু, শান্ত, বদান্য, গম্ভীর ৷  
মধুর-বচন, মধুর-চেষ্টা, মহাধীর ॥ ৫৫ ॥

suśīla, sahiṣṇu, śānta, vadānya, gambhīra  
good-natured  tolerant  peaceful  generous  grave  
madhura-vacana, madhura-ceṣṭā, mahā-dhīra  
sweet in speech  sweet in conduct  supremely steady

(55) He is gentle, patient, serene, generous and grave, sweet in speech and sweet in conduct, and steady beyond measure.

সবার সম্মান-কর্ত্তা, করেন সবার হিত ৷  
কৌটিল্য-মাৎসর্য্য-হিংসা শূন্য তাঁর চিত ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sabāra sammāna-kartā, karena sabāra hita  
to all  giving respect  he does  of all  the good  
kauṭilya-mātsarya-hiṁsā śūnya tām̐ra cita  
crookedness  envy  malice  empty of  his  heart

(56) He honours everyone and works for the good of all; his heart is free of crookedness, envy and malice.

কৃষ্ণের যে সাধারণ সদ্গুণ পঞ্চাশ ৷  
সে সব গুণের তাঁর শরীরে নিবাস ॥ ৫৭ ॥

kṛṣṇera ye sādhāraṇa sad-guṇa pañcāśa  
of Kṛṣṇa  which  common  good qualities  fifty  
se saba guṇera tām̐ra śarīre nivāsa  
those  all  of qualities  his  in the body  is the dwelling

(57) The fifty good qualities of Kṛṣṇa that are shared in part by others all dwell in his person.[^23]

যস্যাস্তি ভক্তির্ভগবত্যকিঞ্চনা  
সর্ব্বৈর্গুণৈস্তত্র সমাসতে সুরাঃ ৷  
হরাবভক্তস্য কুতো মহদ্-গুণা  
মনোরথেনাসতি ধাবতো বহিঃ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

yasyāsti bhaktir bhagavaty akiñcanā  
in whom  there is  devotion  to the Lord  free of desire  
sarvair guṇais tatra samāsate surāḥ  
with all  qualities  there  dwell  the gods  
harāv abhaktasya kuto mahad-guṇā  
to Hari  of one not devoted  whence  great qualities  
mano-rathenāsati dhāvato bahiḥ  
by the chariot of the mind  toward the unreal  running  outward

(58) “In one whose devotion to the Lord is free of desire all the gods dwell, and with them every virtue. But whence can great qualities come to one who is not devoted to Hari? Driven by his desires, his mind is forever running outward after what is passing.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.18.12)[^24]

---

[^1]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: by the will of Lord Caitanya-deva I, a fool like a painted puppet, have suddenly begun this dance of writing a book. *Anubhāṣya*: *jaḍaḥ* — “like one without sense”; *lekha-raṅge* — “in the play of composing this book”; *prasabham* — “suddenly”.

[^2]: Edition heading to 6–8: “To worship Gaura or Kṛṣṇa without accepting the glory of the Pañca-tattva, with a mind that separates Them, is a grave offence.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *ei saba* — “this Pañca-tattva”; those who practise devotion to Kṛṣṇa without accepting it receive no mercy from Kṛṣṇa.

[^3]: Edition heading: “Devotion to Gaura without devotion to Kṛṣṇa, and devotion to Kṛṣṇa without devotion to Gaura, is not devotion.” *Anubhāṣya*: Jarāsandha and the others worshipped Viṣṇu with Vedic mantras, but because they rejected Kṛṣṇa, the original Lord Himself, out of enmity or indifference, their worship ended in the dharma of the asuras. So too, when a jīva tries to worship Viṣṇu while forgetting his eternal service to Śrī Caitanya, that is only the same dharma of the asuras, not Vaiṣṇavism.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya* to 10–11: Śrī Caitanya-candra is non-different from Śrī Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa. Seeing that people in their ignorance judged His pastimes by their own worldly attachments and could not understand Him, Śrī Gaurahari took the vow of renunciation for their sake before the eyes of the world, and so showed mercy to the unwise.

[^5]: Edition heading: “Not to be devoted to the magnanimous Gaura is the nature of an asura.” *Anubhāṣya*: whoever cannot grasp even this mercy of Śrī Caitanya — His call to the jīvas, “Worship Kṛṣṇa” — is an asura, not a Vaiṣṇava, however much the world may honour him. Worship of Caitanya that rejects the worship of Kṛṣṇa lacks His mercy and is an invention of the age of Kali; it is just as asuric to revere one of the six truths while slighting another, or to see Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya as merely the best of mortals, different from Kṛṣṇa.

[^6]: Edition heading: “Gaura’s mercy is more wonderful than any other mercy.” *Anubhāṣya* to 14–15: logic rests on the worldly senses, which are weak and unfit to grasp the Lord, and so it often passes falsehood off as proven truth. Yet even one who relies only on perception and inference, if he compares every kind of mercy, will find that the mercy of Śrī Gaurahari is found in nothing created, nor in the Creator, nor in the avatāras, nor even in the Source of the avatāras, Kṛṣṇa.

[^7]: Edition heading: “While offences remain, countless hearing and chanting are fruitless.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this is said of one who commits the ten offences against the holy name (see 8.24). *Anubhāṣya*: whoever hears and chants without taking shelter at Śrī Caitanya’s feet will not attain love for Kṛṣṇa even in many births; but whoever, following His teaching, is humbler than grass and more tolerant than a tree, seeks no honour and gives honour to others, is freed from the ten offences, chants the name constantly and attains prema.

[^8]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: liberation comes easily through knowledge, and the pleasures of heaven through pious works, but devotion to Hari is not easily attained even by thousands of practices: something more is needed — service to a pure devotee and knowledge of one’s relationship with the Supreme Truth (*sambandha-jñāna*). The reference is confirmed by the edition.

[^9]: Edition heading: “Until bhāva (rati) has awakened in the jīva, Kṛṣṇa grants liberation; when He sees rati, He grants loving devotion.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: if devotees hope for enjoyment and liberation, Kṛṣṇa hides pure devotion from them, grants them these and leaves them (*chuṭe* — “goes away, leaving them”).

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: these are Śukadeva’s words to Parīkṣit in the account of Ṛṣabhadeva. *Daivam* — “the Deity you worship”; *priyaḥ* — “your very self”; *kva ca* — “sometimes, as when He acted as a messenger”; *bhajatām* — “to those who worship Him with desires (*sakāma-bhaktas*)”: to them He grants liberation, but He never gives bhakti-yoga. Edition heading: “Without a relationship of rasa with Kṛṣṇa one gains only liberation.” The edition misprints *kulavatiḥ*; the *Anubhāṣya* explains *kula-patiḥ*.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: even sinners and wrongdoers like Jagāi and Mādhāi, once they receive Gaura’s mercy, give up their sins and one day attain love for Kṛṣṇa.

[^12]: Edition heading to 22–23: “Love for Kṛṣṇa awakens through service to Gaura and Nitāi.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* to 21–22: the wonder of Śrī Caitanya’s descent is that to anyone who comes near Him He, the independent Lord, gives the hidden storehouse of prema without asking whether he is worthy or not. Offender or not, whoever surrenders and calls on Him — “O Gaurāṅga! O Kṛṣṇa Caitanya!” — is overwhelmed with the thrills and tears of love for Kṛṣṇa.

[^13]: The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* lists, after the *Padma Purāṇa*, the ten offences against the holy name: (1) blaspheming the saints; (2) thinking the names of Śiva and other gods independent of Viṣṇu; (3) disregarding the guru; (4) reviling the śruti and the scriptures that follow it; (5) thinking the glory of the name an exaggeration; (6) interpreting the name in one’s own way, as a fancy; (7) sinning on the strength of the name; (8) equating the name with other pious acts; (9) instructing the faithless and the averse in the name; (10) having no love for the name even after hearing its glory. While these offences remain, Kṛṣṇa shows no mercy, and the offender has no true transformations of love. The *Anubhāṣya* explains each of them.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: these are Śaunaka’s words to Sūta. *Aśma-sāram* — a heart as hard as stone because of offences against the holy name. Yet the heart of a deep mahā-bhāgavata may melt at the name with no outward tears or thrills, while those who imitate for the sake of honour may show false tears and thrills without a trace of feeling; so a show of melting in beginners inclined to enjoyment, even when they chant the name abundantly, is itself a sign of offence. Edition heading: “Without the desire to please Kṛṣṇa, the feelings in an offender’s stony heart are not pure; they are only an imitation.”

[^15]: Edition heading: “The self-manifest Lord, the Name, rising on the tongue, bestows love for Kṛṣṇa.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the devotion that awakens prema is *sādhana-bhakti*; it is this that the holy name reveals.

[^16]: Edition heading to 29: “The countless hearing and chanting of one who offends the holy name are fruitless.”

[^17]: Edition heading: “With Gaura and Nitāi, and with Their names, offences are not weighed.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: whoever takes shelter of Caitanya and Nityānanda with faith has his past offences washed away in a moment, and They give prema as soon as the name of Kṛṣṇa begins to rise on his lips. *Anubhāṣya*: an offender who chants the name of Kṛṣṇa does not obtain the fruit of the name, prema; but one who chants the names of Gaura and Nityānanda, even while still an offender, is freed from offences in the very act of chanting and obtains that fruit. The name of Kṛṣṇa and the name of Gaura are equally non-different from the One they name; but Kṛṣṇa’s magnanimity is shown to the liberated, while that of Gaura and Nityānanda reaches even those who still have *anarthas*.

[^18]: Edition heading: “There is no other way than to worship the magnanimous Gaura.” *Anubhāṣya*: “worshipping Śrī Caitanya” does not mean worshipping Gaura while leaving aside Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa; those who bypass Svarūpa, Rūpa, Raghunātha and the other ācāryas closest to Śrī Caitanya and invent their own worship of Gaura are never delivered.

[^19]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *Caitanya-maṅgala* is the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* of Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura of the village of Denuḍ (Bardhamān district). The book was first called *Caitanya-maṅgala*; it is said that when Locana dāsa Ṭhākura began to sing his own *Caitanya-maṅgala*, Vṛndāvana dāsa renamed his book. Edition heading to 33–35: “The highest good of the jīva lies in hearing the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* of Ṭhākura Vṛndāvana dāsa, the incarnation of Vyāsa.”

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* is the treasury of the conclusions of bhakti, but it is so vast that many cannot take in its essence; that essence Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura set down in his *Śrī Caitanya-bhāgavata*. Only those who know the conclusions of devotion can truly understand the glory of Nitāi-Gaura, for without right understanding (*siddhānta*) the goddess of Devotion cannot be served.

[^21]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Nārāyaṇī was the daughter of Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita’s brother; as a child she received the remnants of Mahāprabhu’s meal after kīrtana. *Anubhāṣya*: in the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* Kavi-karṇapūra writes that Nārāyaṇī is Kilimbikā, the sister of Kṛṣṇa’s nurse Ambikā, who ate Kṛṣṇa’s remnants. Ṭhākura Vṛndāvana is known through his mother, who received Śrī Gaurasundara’s remnants and mercy; his ancestry on his father’s side is not given, for a Vaiṣṇava needs no introduction through his forefathers.

[^22]: Edition heading: “But for fear of enlarging the book he did not wish to expand the summary.”

[^23]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the fifty common qualities of Kṛṣṇa are listed in the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* (Southern Division, First Wave: *ayaṁ netā suramyāṅgaḥ…*).

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śukadeva speaks these words to Parīkṣit while describing the qualities of the great devotee Prahlāda. *Akiñcanā* — “free of desire”; *samāsate* — “dwell eternally”; *asati* — “in the passing happiness of sense objects”; *mahad-guṇāḥ* — the virtues of the great, such as knowledge and detachment. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in one who has pure devotion to Kṛṣṇa the gods abide with all their qualities; but the mind of one without devotion to Hari always runs after what is external and unreal, and great qualities are impossible for him.
