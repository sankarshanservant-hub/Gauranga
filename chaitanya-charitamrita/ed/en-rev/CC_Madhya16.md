“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 16  
**The Journey to Gauḍa Once Again**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the Anubhāṣya, 2026)

গৌড়োদ্যানং গৌরমেঘঃ সিঞ্চন্স্বালোকনামৃতৈঃ ৷  
ভবাগ্নিদগ্ধজনতা-বীরুধঃ সমজীবয়ৎ ॥ ১ ॥

gauḍodyānaṁ gaura-meghaḥ  
the garden of Gauḍa  the Gaura-cloud  
siñcan svālokanāmṛtaiḥ  
sprinkling  with the nectar of His glances  
bhavāgni-dagdha-janatā-  
by the fire of worldly existence  scorched  of the people  
vīrudhaḥ samajīvayat  
the creepers  revived

(1) Sprinkling the garden of Gauḍa with the nectar of His glances, the cloud that is Gaura revived the people — creepers scorched by the fire of worldly existence.[^1]

জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

প্রভুর হইল ইচ্ছা যাইতে বৃন্দাবন ৷  
শুনিয়া প্রতাপরুদ্র হইলা বিমন ॥ ৩ ॥

prabhura haila icchā yāite vṛndāvana  
of the Lord  arose  the desire  to go  to Vṛndāvana  
śuniyā pratāparudra hailā vimana  
hearing  Pratāparudra  became  downcast

(3) The Lord conceived the desire to go to Vṛndāvana, and when Pratāparudra heard of it, he was downcast.

সার্ব্বভৌম, রামানন্দ, আনি’ দুই জন ৷  
দুঁহাকে কহেন রাজা বিনয়-বচন ॥ ৪ ॥

sārvabhauma, rāmānanda, āni’ dui jana  
Sārvabhauma  Rāmānanda  summoning  the two  persons  
dum̐hāke kahena rājā vinaya-vacana  
to both  speaks  the king  humble  words

(4) The king summoned the two of them, Sārvabhauma and Rāmānanda, and spoke to them humbly:

নীলাদ্রি ছাড়ি’ প্রভুর মন অন্যত্র যাইতে ৷  
তোমরা করহ যত্ন তাঁহারে রাখিতে ॥ ৫ ॥

nīlādri chāḍi’ prabhura mana anyatra yāite  
Nīlādri  leaving  of the Lord  the mind  elsewhere  to go  
tomarā karaha yatna tām̐hāre rākhite  
you  make  effort  Him  to keep

(5) “The Lord has a mind to leave Nīlādri and go elsewhere. Please make every effort to keep Him here.

তাঁহা বিনা এই রাজ্য মোরে নাহি ভায় ৷  
গোসাঞি রাখিতে করহ নানা উপায় ॥ ৬ ॥

tām̐hā vinā ei rājya more nāhi bhāya  
Him  without  this  kingdom  to me  not  is pleasing  
gosāñi rākhite karaha nānā upāya  
the Gosāñi  to keep  employ  various  means

(6) Without Him this kingdom gives me no pleasure. Use every means to keep the Gosāñi here.”

রামানন্দ, সার্ব্বভৌম, দুইজনা-স্থানে ৷  
তবে যুক্তি করে প্রভু — ‘যাব বৃন্দাবনে’ ॥ ৭ ॥

rāmānanda, sārvabhauma, dui-janā-sthāne  
Rāmānanda  Sārvabhauma  with the two  
tabe yukti kare prabhu — ‘yāba vṛndāvane’  
then  counsel  takes  the Lord  —  “I will go  to Vṛndāvana”

(7) Then the Lord took counsel with the two of them, Rāmānanda and Sārvabhauma: “I will go to Vṛndāvana.”

দুঁহে কহে, — রথযাত্রা কর দরশন ৷  
কার্ত্তিক আইলে, তবে করিহ গমন ॥ ৮ ॥

dum̐he kahe, — ratha-yātrā kara daraśana  
the two  say  —  the Ratha-yātrā  do  seeing  
kārtika āile, tabe kariha gamana  
Kārtika  when it comes  then  make  departure

(8) Both of them said, “See the Ratha-yātrā first; when Kārtika comes, then You may go.”

কার্ত্তিক আইলে কহে, — এবে মহা-শীত ৷  
দোলযাত্রা দেখি’ যাও, — এই ভাল রীত ॥ ৯ ॥

kārtika āile kahe, — ebe mahā-śīta  
Kārtika  when it came  they say  —  now  great cold  
dola-yātrā dekhi’ yāo, — ei bhāla rīta  
the Dola-yātrā  having seen  go  —  this  good  way

(9) When Kārtika came, they said, “Now it is bitterly cold. See the Dola-yātrā and then go — that is the proper way.”

আজি-কালি করি’ উঠায় বিবিধ উপায় ৷  
যাইতে সম্মতি না দেয় বিচ্ছেদের ভয় ॥ ১০ ॥

āji-kāli kari’ uṭhāya vividha upāya  
today–tomorrow  saying  raise  various  pretexts  
yāite sammati nā deya vicchedera bhaya  
to going  consent  not  give  of separation  out of fear

(10) Saying “today, tomorrow,” they raised one pretext after another; afraid of separation, they would not consent to His going.

যদ্যপি স্বতন্ত্র প্রভু নহে নিবারণ ৷  
ভক্ত-ইচ্ছা বিনা প্রভু না করে গমন ॥ ১১ ॥

yadyapi svatantra prabhu nahe nivāraṇa  
although  independent  the Lord  not  is to be stopped  
bhakta-icchā vinā prabhu nā kare gamana  
the devotees’ wish  without  the Lord  not  makes  departure

(11) Although the Lord is independent and no one can stop Him, He would not leave without the consent of His devotees.

তৃতীয় বৎসরে সব গৌড়ের ভক্তগণ ৷  
নীলাচলে চলিতে সবার হৈল মন ॥ ১২ ॥

tṛtīya vatsare saba gauḍera bhakta-gaṇa  
in the third  year  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees  
nīlācale calite sabāra haila mana  
to Nīlācala  to go  of all  arose  the wish

(12) In the third year all the devotees of Gauḍa resolved to go to Nīlācala.

সবে মেলি’ গেলা অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্যের পাশে ৷  
প্রভু দেখিতে আচার্য্য চলিলা উল্লাসে ॥ ১৩ ॥

sabe meli’ gelā advaita-ācāryera pāśe  
all  gathering  went  Advaita Ācārya’s  to the side  
prabhu dekhite ācārya calilā ullāse  
the Lord  to see  the Ācārya  set out  joyfully

(13) They all gathered and went to Advaita Ācārya, and the Ācārya joyfully set out to see the Lord.

যদ্যপি প্রভুর আজ্ঞা গৌড়েতে রহিতে ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-প্রভুকে প্রেমভক্তি-প্রকাশিতে ॥ ১৪ ॥

তথাপি চলিলা মহাপ্রভুরে দেখিতে ৷  
নিত্যানন্দের প্রেম-চেষ্টা কে পারে বুঝিতে ॥ ১৫ ॥

yadyapi prabhura ājñā gauḍete rahite  
although  of the Lord  the order  in Gauḍa  to stay  
nityānanda-prabhuke prema-bhakti-prakāśite  
to Nityānanda Prabhu  prema-bhakti  to make manifest  
tathāpi calilā mahāprabhure dekhite  
nevertheless  set out  Mahāprabhu  to see  
nityānandera prema-ceṣṭā ke pāre bujhite  
Nityānanda’s  loving dealings  who  can  understand

(14–15) Although the Lord had ordered Nityānanda Prabhu to stay in Gauḍa and reveal prema-bhakti, Nityānanda set out all the same to see Mahāprabhu. Who can understand the ways of His love?

আচার্য্যরত্ন, বিদ্যানিধি, শ্রীবাস, রামাই ৷  
বাসুদেব, মুরারি, গোবিন্দাদি তিন-ভাই ॥ ১৬ ॥

রাঘব-পণ্ডিত নিজ-ঝালি সাজাঞা ৷  
কুলীন-গ্রামবাসী চলে পট্টডোরী লঞা ॥ ১৭ ॥

ācāryaratna, vidyānidhi, śrīvāsa, rāmāi  
Ācāryaratna  Vidyānidhi  Śrīvāsa  Rāmāi  
vāsudeva, murāri, govindādi tina-bhāi  
Vāsudeva  Murāri  Govinda and the others  the three brothers  
rāghava-paṇḍita nija-jhāli sājāñā  
Rāghava Paṇḍita  his own bags  having prepared  
kulīna-grāma-vāsī cale paṭṭa-ḍorī lañā  
the residents of Kulīna-grāma  go  silk ropes  taking

(16–17) Ācāryaratna, Vidyānidhi, Śrīvāsa, Rāmāi, Vāsudeva, Murāri, and Govinda with his two brothers set out. Rāghava Paṇḍita packed his bags (*jhāli*) for the journey, and the residents of Kulīna-grāma came bringing silk ropes.[^3]

খণ্ডবাসী নরহরি, শ্রীরঘুনন্দন ৷  
সর্ব্ব-ভক্ত চলে, তার কে করে গণন ॥ ১৮ ॥

khaṇḍa-vāsī narahari, śrī-raghunandana  
the resident of Khaṇḍa  Narahari  Śrī Raghunandana  
sarva-bhakta cale, tāra ke kare gaṇana  
all the devotees  go  of them  who  makes  a count

(18) Narahari of Khaṇḍa and Śrī Raghunandana came too. All the devotees set out — who can count them?

শিবানন্দ-সেন করে ঘাটি সমাধান ৷  
সবারে পালন করি’ সুখে লঞা যান ॥ ১৯ ॥

śivānanda-sena kare ghāṭi samādhāna  
Śivānanda Sena  does  at the toll stations  the settling  
sabāre pālana kari’ sukhe lañā yāna  
all  care  taking  happily  leading  goes

(19) Śivānanda Sena settled the tolls at the crossings; taking care of everyone, he led them happily along.

সবার সর্ব্বকার্য্য করেন, দেন বাসা-স্থান ৷  
শিবানন্দ জানে উড়িয়া-পথের সন্ধান ॥ ২০ ॥

sabāra sarva-kārya karena, dena vāsā-sthāna  
of all  all the affairs  he does  gives  lodging places  
śivānanda jāne uḍiyā-pathera sandhāna  
Śivānanda  knows  of the Oḍiyā roads  the details

(20) He attended to everyone’s needs and found them places to stay, for Śivānanda knew the roads through Orissa well.

সে বৎসর প্রভু দেখিতে সব ঠাকুরাণী ৷  
চলিলা আচার্য্য-সঙ্গে অচ্যুত-জননী ॥ ২১ ॥

se vatsara prabhu dekhite saba ṭhākurāṇī  
that  year  the Lord  to see  all  the ladies  
calilā ācārya-saṅge acyuta-jananī  
went  with the Ācārya  Acyuta’s  mother

(21) That year all the ladies went to see the Lord. Acyuta’s mother went with the Ācārya.

শ্রীবাস-পণ্ডিত-সঙ্গে চলিলা মালিনী ৷  
শিবানন্দ-সঙ্গে চলে তাঁহার গৃহিণী ॥ ২২ ॥

śrīvāsa-paṇḍita-saṅge calilā mālinī  
with Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita  went  Mālinī  
śivānanda-saṅge cale tām̐hāra gṛhiṇī  
with Śivānanda  goes  his  wife

(22) Mālinī went with Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, and Śivānanda’s wife went with him.

শিবানন্দের বালক, নাম — চৈতন্য-দাস ৷  
তিঁহো চলিয়াছে প্রভুরে দেখিতে উল্লাস ॥ ২৩ ॥

śivānandera bālaka, nāma — caitanya-dāsa  
Śivānanda’s  son  name  —  Caitanya dāsa  
tim̐ho caliyāche prabhure dekhite ullāsa  
he  set out  the Lord  to see  joyfully

(23) Śivānanda’s son, named Caitanya dāsa, also set out joyfully to see the Lord.

আচার্য্যরত্ন-সঙ্গে চলে তাঁহার গৃহিণী ৷  
তাঁহার প্রেমের কথা কহিতে না জানি ॥ ২৪ ॥

ācāryaratna-saṅge cale tām̐hāra gṛhiṇī  
with Ācāryaratna  goes  his  wife  
tām̐hāra premera kathā kahite nā jāni  
of that one  of the love  the account  to tell  not  I know how

(24) Ācāryaratna’s wife went with him. I do not know how to describe her love.[^4]

সব ঠাকুরাণী মহাপ্রভুকে ভিক্ষা দিতে ৷  
প্রভুর নানা প্রিয় দ্রব্য নিল ঘর হৈতে ॥ ২৫ ॥

saba ṭhākurāṇī mahāprabhuke bhikṣā dite  
all  the ladies  to Mahāprabhu  a meal  to give  
prabhura nānā priya dravya nila ghara haite  
of the Lord  various  favourite  foods  took  from home

(25) To offer Mahāprabhu a meal, all the ladies brought from home many of the Lord’s favourite foods.

শিবানন্দ-সেন করে সব সমাধান ৷  
ঘাটিয়াল প্রবোধি’ দেন সবারে বাসা-স্থান ॥ ২৬ ॥

śivānanda-sena kare saba samādhāna  
Śivānanda Sena  does  all  the arranging  
ghāṭiyāla prabodhi’ dena sabāre vāsā-sthāna  
the toll collectors  pacifying  gives  to all  lodging places

(26) Śivānanda Sena arranged everything. He pacified the toll collectors (*ghāṭiyāla*) and found lodgings for everyone.

ভক্ষ্য দিয়া করেন সবার সর্ব্বত্র পালনে ৷  
পরম আনন্দে যান প্রভুর দরশনে ॥ ২৭ ॥

bhakṣya diyā karena sabāra sarvatra pālane  
food  giving  does  of all  everywhere  the care  
parama ānande yāna prabhura daraśane  
in supreme  bliss  goes  of the Lord  to the sight

(27) Everywhere he looked after them all and gave them food, and he went on in supreme bliss to see the Lord.

রেমুণায় আসিয়া কৈল গোপীনাথ দরশন ৷  
আচার্য্য করিল তাঁহা কীর্ত্তন, নর্ত্তন ॥ ২৮ ॥

remuṇāya āsiyā kaila gopīnātha daraśana  
to Remuṇā  coming  they made  of Gopīnātha  darśana  
ācārya karila tām̐hā kīrtana, nartana  
the Ācārya  did  there  kīrtana  dancing

(28) Coming to Remuṇā, they had darśana of Gopīnātha, and there the Ācārya sang and danced.

নিত্যানন্দের পরিচয় সব-লোক-সনে ৷  
বহুত সম্মান আসি’ কৈল সেবকগণে ॥ ২৯ ॥

nityānandera paricaya saba-loka-sane  
Nityānanda’s  acquaintance  with all the people  
bahuta sammāna āsi’ kaila sevaka-gaṇe  
great  honour  coming  showed  the servants

(29) Nityānanda was known to all the people there, and the temple servants came and showed Him great honour.

সেই রাত্রি সব মহান্ত তাহাঞি রহিলা ৷  
বার ক্ষীর আনি’ আগে সেবক ধরিলা ॥ ৩০ ॥

sei rātri saba mahānta tāhāñi rahilā  
that  night  all  the great souls  right there  stayed  
bāra kṣīra āni’ āge sevaka dharilā  
twelve  pots of kṣīra  bringing  before  the servant  placed

(30) All those great souls stayed there that night, and a servant brought twelve pots of *kṣīra* (sweet rice) and set them before the devotees.

ক্ষীর বাঁটি’ সবারে দিল প্রভু-নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
ক্ষীর-প্রসাদ পাঞা সবার বাড়িল আনন্দ ॥ ৩১ ॥

kṣīra bām̐ṭi’ sabāre dila prabhu-nityānanda  
the kṣīra  dividing  to all  gave  Nityānanda Prabhu  
kṣīra-prasāda pāñā sabāra bāḍila ānanda  
the kṣīra prasāda  receiving  of all  increased  the bliss

(31) Nityānanda Prabhu divided the *kṣīra* and gave it to everyone, and receiving that *kṣīra-prasāda*, they were all filled with joy.

মাধবপুরীর কথা, গোপাল-স্থাপন ৷  
তাঁহারে গোপাল যৈছে মাগিল চন্দন ॥ ৩২ ॥

mādhava-purīra kathā, gopāla-sthāpana  
of Mādhava Purī  the story  of Gopāla  the installation  
tām̐hāre gopāla yaiche māgila candana  
from him  Gopāla  how  asked for  sandalwood

(32) The story of Mādhava Purī, the installation of Gopāla, how Gopāla asked him for sandalwood,[^5]

তাঁর লাগি’ গোপীনাথ ক্ষীর চুরি কৈল ৷  
মহাপ্রভুর মুখে আগে এ কথা শুনিল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

tām̐ra lāgi’ gopīnātha kṣīra curi kaila  
him  for the sake of  Gopīnātha  the kṣīra  theft  made  
mahāprabhura mukhe āge e kathā śunila  
of Mahāprabhu  from the mouth  before  this  story  they had heard

(33) and how Gopīnātha stole *kṣīra* for his sake — they had heard this story earlier from Mahāprabhu’s own mouth.

সেই কথা সবার মধ্যে কহে নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
শুনিয়া বৈষ্ণবমনে বাড়িল আনন্দ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

sei kathā sabāra madhye kahe nityānanda  
that  story  all  among  tells  Nityānanda  
śuniyā vaiṣṇavamane bāḍila ānanda  
hearing  in the hearts of the Vaiṣṇavas  increased  the bliss

(34) Now Nityānanda told that story among them all, and the Vaiṣṇavas’ hearts swelled with joy as they listened.

এইমত চলি’ চলি’ কটক আইলা ৷  
সাক্ষীগোপাল দেখি’ সবে সে দিন রহিলা ॥ ৩৫ ॥

ei mata cali’ cali’ kaṭaka āilā  
in this way  going on  going on  to Kaṭaka  they came  
sākṣī-gopāla dekhi’ sabe se dina rahilā  
Sākṣi-gopāla  seeing  all  that  day  stayed

(35) Travelling on in this way, they came to Kaṭaka. There they saw Sākṣi-gopāla and stayed that day.

সাক্ষীগোপালের কথা কহে নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
শুনিয়া বৈষ্ণব-মনে বাড়িল আনন্দ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

sākṣī-gopālera kathā kahe nityānanda  
of Sākṣi-gopāla  the story  tells  Nityānanda  
śuniyā vaiṣṇava-mane bāḍila ānanda  
hearing  in the hearts of the Vaiṣṇavas  increased  the bliss

(36) Nityānanda told the story of Sākṣi-gopāla, and the Vaiṣṇavas’ hearts swelled with joy as they listened.

প্রভুকে মিলিতে সবার উৎকণ্ঠা অন্তরে ৷  
শীঘ্র করি’ আইলা সবে শ্রীনীলাচলে ॥ ৩৭ ॥

prabhuke milite sabāra utkaṇṭhā antare  
the Lord  to meet  of all  eagerness  in the heart  
śīghra kari’ āilā sabe śrī-nīlācale  
quickly  making  came  all  to Śrī Nīlācala

(37) Their hearts were all eager to meet the Lord, so they hurried on to Śrī Nīlācala.

আঠারনালাকে আইলা গোসাঞি শুনিয়া ৷  
দুইমালা পাঠাইলা গোবিন্দ হাতে দিয়া ॥ ৩৮ ॥

āṭhāranālāke āilā gosāñi śuniyā  
at Āṭhāranālā  they have come  the Gosāñi  hearing  
duimālā pāṭhāilā govinda hāte diyā  
two garlands  sent  Govinda’s  in the hand  placing

(38) Hearing that they had reached Āṭhāranālā, the Gosāñi placed two garlands in Govinda’s hands and sent him to them.

দুই মালা গোবিন্দ দুই জনে পরাইল ৷  
অদ্বৈত, অবধূত-গোসাঞি বড় সুখ পাইল ॥ ৩৯ ॥

dui mālā govinda dui jane parāila  
the two  garlands  Govinda  on the two  persons  put  
advaita, avadhūta-gosāñi baḍa sukha pāila  
Advaita  the Avadhūta Gosāñi  great  happiness  felt

(39) Govinda put the two garlands on the two of them, and Advaita and the Avadhūta Gosāñi (Nityānanda) were overjoyed.

তাহাঞি আরম্ভ কৈল কৃষ্ণ-সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
নাচিতে নাচিতে চলি’ আইলা দুইজন ॥ ৪০ ॥

tāhāñi ārambha kaila kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana  
right there  beginning  made  of Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana  
nācite nācite cali’ āilā dui-jana  
dancing  dancing  going  came  the two

(40) Right there they began Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana, and the two of them came on, dancing all the way.

পুনঃ মালা দিয়া স্বরূপাদি নিজগণে ৷  
আগু বাড়ি’ পাঠাইল শচীর নন্দনে ॥ ৪১ ॥

punaḥ mālā diyā svarūpādi nija-gaṇe  
again  garlands  giving  to Svarūpa and the others  to His own men  
āgu bāḍi’ pāṭhāila śacīra nandane  
ahead  sending forward  sent  of Śacī  by the son

(41) Then Śacī’s son gave garlands to Svarūpa and His other associates and sent them ahead.

নরেন্দ্র আসিয়া তাঁহা সবারে মিলিলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভুর দত্ত মালা সবারে পরাইলা ॥ ৪২ ॥

narendra āsiyā tām̐hā sabāre mililā  
to Narendra  coming  there  with all  they met  
mahāprabhura datta mālā sabāre parāilā  
by Mahāprabhu  given  the garlands  on all  they put

(42) Coming to Narendra, they met everyone there and put Mahāprabhu’s garlands on them all.

সিংহদ্বার-নিকটে আইলা শুনি’ গৌররায় ৷  
আপনে আসিয়া প্রভু মিলিলা সবায় ॥ ৪৩ ॥

siṁha-dvāra-nikaṭe āilā śuni’ gaurarāya  
near the Siṁha-dvāra  they have come  hearing  Gaurarāya  
āpane āsiyā prabhu mililā sabāya  
Himself  coming  the Lord  met  with all

(43) When Gaurarāya heard that they had reached the Siṁha-dvāra, the Lord came Himself and met them all.

সবা লঞা কৈল জগন্নাথ-দরশন ৷  
সবা লঞা আইলা পুনঃ আপন-ভবন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

sabā lañā kaila jagannātha-daraśana  
all  taking  He made  Jagannātha-darśana  
sabā lañā āilā punaḥ āpana-bhavana  
all  taking  came  again  to His own residence

(44) Taking them all with Him, He had darśana of Jagannātha, and then He brought them all back to His own residence.

বাণীনাথ, কাশীমিশ্র প্রসাদ আনিল ৷  
স্বহস্তে সবারে প্রভু প্রসাদ খাওয়াইল ॥ ৪৫ ॥

vāṇīnātha, kāśī-miśra prasāda ānila  
Vāṇīnātha  Kāśī Miśra  prasāda  brought  
svahaste sabāre prabhu prasāda khāoyāila  
with His own hand  all  the Lord  prasāda  fed

(45) Vāṇīnātha and Kāśī Miśra brought prasāda, and the Lord served it to everyone with His own hand.

পূর্ব্ব-বৎসরে যাঁর যেই বাসা-স্থান ৷  
তাঁহা সবা পাঠাঞা করাইল বিশ্রাম ॥ ৪৬ ॥

pūrva-vatsare yām̐ra yei vāsā-sthāna  
in the previous year  whose  whatever  lodging place  
tām̐hā sabā pāṭhāñā karāila viśrāma  
there  all  sending  made rest

(46) He sent each of them to the lodging they had had the year before and let them rest.

এইমত ভক্তগণ রহিলা চারি মাস ৷  
প্রভুর সহিত করে কীর্ত্তন-বিলাস ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ei mata bhakta-gaṇa rahilā cāri māsa  
in this way  the devotees  stayed  four  months  
prabhura sahita kare kīrtana-vilāsa  
the Lord  with  do  the pastimes of kīrtana

(47) In this way the devotees stayed four months, enjoying the pastimes of kīrtana with the Lord.

পূর্ব্ববৎ রথযাত্রা-কাল যবে আইল ৷  
সবা লঞা গুণ্ডিচা-মন্দির প্রক্ষালিল ॥ ৪৮ ॥

pūrvavat ratha-yātrā-kāla yabe āila  
as before  the time of the Ratha-yātrā  when  came  
sabā lañā guṇḍicā-mandira prakṣālila  
all  taking  the Guṇḍicā temple  He washed

(48) When the time of the Ratha-yātrā came, He took them all, as before, and washed the Guṇḍicā temple.

কুলীনগ্রামী পট্টডোরী জগন্নাথে দিল ৷  
পূর্ব্ববৎ রথ-অগ্রে নর্ত্তন করিল ॥ ৪৯ ॥

kulīna-grāmī paṭṭa-ḍorī jagannāthe dila  
the residents of Kulīna-grāma  silk ropes  to Jagannātha  gave  
pūrvavat ratha-agre nartana karila  
as before  in front of the chariot  dancing  He did

(49) The residents of Kulīna-grāma gave silk ropes to Jagannātha, and as before He danced in front of the chariot.

বহু নৃত্য করি’ পুনঃ চলিল উদ্যানে ৷  
বাপী-তীরে তাঁহা যাই’ করিল বিশ্রামে ॥ ৫০ ॥

bahu nṛtya kari’ punaḥ calila udyāne  
much  dancing  having done  then  went  to the garden  
vāpī-tīre tām̐hā yāi’ karila viśrāme  
on the bank of the pond  there  going  made  rest

(50) After dancing a great deal He went to the garden, and there He rested on the bank of the pond.

রাঢ়ী এক বিপ্র তিঁহো — নিত্যানন্দ-দাস ৷  
মহা-ভাগ্যবান্ তিঁহো, নাম — কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৫১ ॥

rāḍhī eka vipra tim̐ho — nityānanda-dāsa  
from Rāḍha  one  brāhmaṇa  he  —  a servant of Nityānanda  
mahā-bhāgyavān tim̐ho, nāma — kṛṣṇadāsa  
most fortunate  he  name  —  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(51) There was a most fortunate brāhmaṇa from Rāḍha named Kṛṣṇadāsa, a servant of Nityānanda.

ঘট ভরি’ প্রভুর তিঁহো অভিষেক কৈল ৷  
তাঁর অভিষেকে প্রভু মহা-তৃপ্ত হৈল ॥ ৫২ ॥

ghaṭa bhari’ prabhura tim̐ho abhiṣeka kaila  
a pitcher  filling  of the Lord  he  bathing  did  
tām̐ra abhiṣeke prabhu mahā-tṛpta haila  
his  by the bathing  the Lord  greatly pleased  became

(52) He filled a pitcher and bathed the Lord, and the Lord was greatly pleased.

বলগণ্ডি-ভোগের বহু প্রসাদ আইল ৷  
সবা সঙ্গে মহাপ্রভু প্রসাদ খাইল ॥ ৫৩ ॥

balagaṇḍi-bhogera bahu prasāda āila  
of the Balagaṇḍi offering  much  prasāda  came  
sabā saṅge mahāprabhu prasāda khāila  
all  with  Mahāprabhu  prasāda  ate

(53) A great deal of prasāda came from the Balagaṇḍi offering, and Mahāprabhu honoured it with them all.

পূর্ব্ববৎ রথযাত্রা কৈল দরশন ৷  
হেরাপঞ্চমী-যাত্রা দেখে লঞা ভক্তগণ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

pūrvavat ratha-yātrā kaila daraśana  
as before  the Ratha-yātrā  He made  seeing  
herā-pañcamī-yātrā dekhe lañā bhakta-gaṇa  
the Herā-pañcamī festival  sees  taking  the devotees

(54) As before, He watched the Ratha-yātrā, and with His devotees He saw the festival of Herā-pañcamī.

আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি প্রভুর কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে কৈল যৈছে ঝড়-বরিষণ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

ācārya-gosāñi prabhura kaila nimantraṇa  
Ācārya Gosāñi  of the Lord  made  invitation  
tāra madhye kaila yaiche jhaḍa-variṣaṇa  
that  in the midst of  made  how  a storm of rain

(55) Ācārya Gosāñi invited the Lord, and in the midst of it He brought on a storm of wind and rain.[^6]

বিস্তারি’ বর্ণিয়াছেন দাস-বৃন্দাবন ৷  
শ্রীবাস প্রভুরে তবে কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

vistāri’ varṇiyāchena dāsa-vṛndāvana  
in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa  
śrīvāsa prabhure tabe kaila nimantraṇa  
Śrīvāsa  the Lord  then  made  invitation

(56) Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail. Then Śrīvāsa invited the Lord.[^7]

প্রভুর প্রিয়-ব্যঞ্জন সব রান্ধেন মালিনী ৷  
‘ভক্ত্যে দাসী’-অভিমান, ‘স্নেহেতে জননী’ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

prabhura priya-vyañjana saba rāndhena mālinī  
the Lord’s  favourite dishes  all  cooks  Mālinī  
‘bhaktye dāsī’-abhimāna, ‘snehete jananī’  
“in devotion  a maidservant” — such self-conception  “in affection  a mother”

(57) Mālinī cooked all the Lord’s favourite dishes. In devotion she thought of herself as His maidservant; in affection she was His mother.

আচার্য্যরত্ন-আদি যত মুখ্য ভক্তগণ ৷  
মধ্যে মধ্যে প্রভুরে করেন নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

ācāryaratna-ādi yata mukhya bhakta-gaṇa  
Ācāryaratna and the others  as many  chief  devotees  
madhye madhye prabhure karena nimantraṇa  
from time  to time  the Lord  make  invitation

(58) Ācāryaratna and the other leading devotees invited the Lord from time to time.

চাতুর্ম্মাস্য-অন্তে পুনঃ নিত্যানন্দে লঞা ৷  
কিবা যুক্তি করে নিত্য নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥ ৫৯ ॥

cāturmāsya-ante punaḥ nityānande lañā  
at the end of Cāturmāsya  again  Nityānanda  taking  
kibā yukti kare nitya nibhṛte vasiyā  
some kind of  counsel  holds  daily  in private  sitting

(59) At the end of Cāturmāsya the Lord would again take Nityānanda aside every day and confer with Him in private.

আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি প্রভুকে কহে ঠারে-ঠোরে ৷  
আচার্য্য তর্জ্জা পড়ে, কেহ বুঝিতে না পারে ॥ ৬০ ॥

ācārya-gosāñi prabhuke kahe ṭhāre-ṭhore  
Ācārya Gosāñi  to the Lord  speaks  in hints  
ācārya tarjā paḍe, keha bujhite nā pāre  
the Ācārya  riddles  recites  anyone  to understand  not  is able

(60) Ācārya Gosāñi spoke to the Lord in hints, reciting riddles (*tarjā*) that no one could understand.[^8]

তাঁর মুখ দেখি’ হাসে শচীর নন্দন ৷  
অঙ্গীকার জানি’ আচার্য্য করেন নর্ত্তন ॥ ৬১ ॥

tām̐ra mukha dekhi’ hāse śacīra nandana  
his  face  seeing  smiles  of Śacī  the son  
aṅgīkāra jāni’ ācārya karena nartana  
acceptance  knowing  the Ācārya  does  dancing

(61) Śacī’s son looked at Advaita’s face and smiled, and the Ācārya, taking this as the Lord’s assent, began to dance.

কিবা প্রার্থনা, কিবা আজ্ঞা — কেহ না বুঝিল ৷  
আলিঙ্গন করি’ প্রভু তাঁরে বিদায় দিল ॥ ৬২ ॥

kibā prārthanā, kibā ājñā — keha nā bujhila  
whether  a prayer  whether  an order  —  anyone  not  understood  
āliṅgana kari’ prabhu tām̐re vidāya dila  
embrace  making  the Lord  him  leave  gave

(62) No one understood whether it was a prayer or an instruction. Then the Lord embraced Him and bade Him farewell.[^9]

নিত্যানন্দ কহে প্রভু, — শুনহ, শ্রীপাদ ৷  
এই আমি মাগি, তুমি করহ প্রসাদ ॥ ৬৩ ॥

nityānanda kahe prabhu, — śunaha, śrīpāda  
to Nityānanda  says  the Lord  —  listen  Śrīpāda  
ei āmi māgi, tumi karaha prasāda  
this  I  beg  You  grant  the favour

(63) The Lord said to Nityānanda, “Listen, Śrīpāda: I beg this of You — grant Me this favour.

প্রতিবর্ষ নীলাচলে তুমি না আসিবা ৷  
গৌড়ে রহি’ মোর ইচ্ছা সফল করিবা ॥ ৬৪ ॥

prati-varṣa nīlācale tumi nā āsibā  
every year  to Nīlācala  You  not  should come  
gauḍe rahi’ mora icchā saphala karibā  
in Gauḍa  staying  My  desire  fulfilled  should make

(64) Do not come to Nīlācala every year. Stay in Gauḍa and fulfil My desire.

তাঁহা সিদ্ধি করে — হেন অন্যে না দেখিয়ে ৷  
আমার ‘দুষ্কর’ কর্ম্ম, তোমা হৈতে হয়ে ॥ ৬৫ ॥

tām̐hā siddhi kare — hena anye nā dekhiye  
that  fulfilment  does  —  such  another  not  I see  
āmāra ‘duṣkara’ karma, tomā haite haye  
My  difficult  work  You  from  is accomplished

(65) I see no one else who could accomplish it. My difficult work can be done only by You.”

নিত্যানন্দ কহে, — আমি ‘দেহ’ তুমি ‘প্রাণ’ ৷  
‘দেহ’ ‘প্রাণ’ ভিন্ন নহে, — এই ত’ প্রমাণ ॥ ৬৬ ॥

nityānanda kahe, — āmi ‘deha’ tumi ‘prāṇa’  
Nityānanda  says  —  I  the body  You  the life  
‘deha’ ‘prāṇa’ bhinna nahe, — ei ta’ pramāṇa  
the body  the life  different  not are  —  this  indeed  the proof

(66) Nityānanda said, “I am the body and You are the life. Body and life are not separate; that is beyond doubt.[^10]

অচিন্ত্যশক্ত্যে কর তুমি তাহার ঘটন ৷  
যে করাহ, সেই করি, নাহিক নিয়ম ॥ ৬৭ ॥

acintya-śaktye kara tumi tāhāra ghaṭana  
by inconceivable potency  bring about  You  of that  the accomplishment  
ye karāha, sei kari, nāhika niyama  
whatever  You make Me do  that  I do  there is no  rule

(67) Yet by Your inconceivable potency You keep them apart. Whatever You have Me do, that I do; there is no rule here.”

তাঁরে বিদায় দিল প্রভু করি’ আলিঙ্গন ৷  
এইমত বিদায় দিল সব ভক্তগণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

tām̐re vidāya dila prabhu kari’ āliṅgana  
Him  leave  gave  the Lord  making  an embrace  
ei mata vidāya dila saba bhakta-gaṇa  
in this way  leave  gave  all  the devotees

(68) The Lord embraced Him and bade Him farewell, and in the same way He bade farewell to all the devotees.

কুলীনগ্রামী পূর্ব্ববৎ কৈল নিবেদন ৷  
প্রভু, আজ্ঞা কর, — আমার কর্তব্য সাধন ॥ ৬৯ ॥

kulīna-grāmī pūrvavat kaila nivedana  
the resident of Kulīna-grāma  as before  made  a submission  
prabhu, ājñā kara, — āmāra kartavya sādhana  
Lord  an order  give  —  my  duty  practice

(69) As before, a resident of Kulīna-grāma submitted, “Lord, please instruct me: what is my duty, my practice?”

প্রভু কহে, — বৈষ্ণব সেবা, নাম-সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
দুই কর, শীঘ্র পাবে শ্রীকৃষ্ণ-চরণ ॥ ৭০ ॥

prabhu kahe, — vaiṣṇava sevā, nāma-saṅkīrtana  
the Lord  says  —  of the Vaiṣṇavas  service  saṅkīrtana of the name  
dui kara, śīghra pābe śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇa  
two  do  quickly  you will obtain  the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa

(70) The Lord said, “Serve the Vaiṣṇavas and chant the holy name in saṅkīrtana. Do these two, and you will quickly attain the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”

তেঁহো কহে, — কে বৈষ্ণব, কি তাঁর লক্ষণ ?  
তবে হাসি’ কহে প্রভু জানি’ তাঁর মন ॥ ৭১ ॥

tem̐ho kahe, — ke vaiṣṇava, ki tām̐ra lakṣaṇa?  
he  says  —  who  a Vaiṣṇava  what  his  symptom  
tabe hāsi’ kahe prabhu jāni’ tām̐ra mana  
then  smiling  says  the Lord  knowing  his  mind

(71) He asked, “Who is a Vaiṣṇava? What are his marks?” Then the Lord, knowing his mind, smiled and said,

কৃষ্ণনাম নিরন্তর যাঁহার বদনে ৷  
সেই বৈষ্ণব-শ্রেষ্ঠ, ভজ তাঁহার চরণে ॥ ৭২ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nāma nirantara yām̐hāra vadane  
the name of Kṛṣṇa  constantly  whose  in the mouth  
sei vaiṣṇava-śreṣṭha, bhaja tām̐hāra caraṇe  
he  the best of the Vaiṣṇavas  worship  his  feet

(72) “One on whose lips the name of Kṛṣṇa is always present is the best of Vaiṣṇavas. Worship his feet.”[^11]

বর্ষান্তরে পুনঃ তাঁরা ঐছে প্রশ্ন কৈল ৷  
‘বৈষ্ণবের তারতম্য’ প্রভু শিখাইল ॥ ৭৩ ॥

varṣāntare punaḥ tām̐rā aiche praśna kaila  
after a year  again  they  such  a question  asked  
‘vaiṣṇavera tāratamya’ prabhu śikhāila  
of the Vaiṣṇavas  the gradation  the Lord  taught

(73) A year later they asked the same question again, and the Lord taught them the gradation of Vaiṣṇavas:

যাঁহার দর্শনে মুখে আইসে কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
তাঁহারে জানিহ তুমি ‘বৈষ্ণব-প্রধান’ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

yām̐hāra darśane mukhe āise kṛṣṇa-nāma  
whose  at the sight  to the mouth  comes  the name of Kṛṣṇa  
tām̐hāre jāniha tumi ‘vaiṣṇava-pradhāna’  
him  know  you  the foremost Vaiṣṇava

(74) “One whose very sight brings the name of Kṛṣṇa to the lips — know him to be the foremost Vaiṣṇava.”[^12]

ক্রম করি’ কহে প্রভু ‘বৈষ্ণব’-লক্ষণ ৷  
‘বৈষ্ণব’, ‘বৈষ্ণবতর’, আর ‘বৈষ্ণবতম’ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

krama kari’ kahe prabhu ‘vaiṣṇava’-lakṣaṇa  
in order  making  speaks  the Lord  of a Vaiṣṇava  the marks  
‘vaiṣṇava’, ‘vaiṣṇavatara’, āra ‘vaiṣṇavatama’  
Vaiṣṇava  a higher Vaiṣṇava  and  the highest Vaiṣṇava

(75) Step by step the Lord described the marks of a Vaiṣṇava: the Vaiṣṇava, the higher Vaiṣṇava (*vaiṣṇavatara*) and the highest Vaiṣṇava (*vaiṣṇavatama*).[^13]

এইমত সব বৈষ্ণব গৌড়ে চলিলা ৷  
বিদ্যানিধি সে বৎসর নীলাদ্রি রহিলা ॥ ৭৬ ॥

ei mata saba vaiṣṇava gauḍe calilā  
in this way  all  the Vaiṣṇavas  to Gauḍa  went  
vidyānidhi se vatsara nīlādri rahilā  
Vidyānidhi  that  year  in Nīlādri  stayed

(76) In this way all the Vaiṣṇavas returned to Gauḍa. That year Vidyānidhi stayed on in Nīlādri.

স্বরূপ-সহিত তাঁর হয় সখ্য-প্রীতি ৷  
দুই-জনায় কৃষ্ণ-কথায় একত্রই স্থিতি ॥ ৭৭ ॥

svarūpa-sahita tām̐ra haya sakhya-prīti  
with Svarūpa  his  is  friendly affection  
dui-janāya kṛṣṇa-kathāya ekatrai sthiti  
of the two  in talks of Kṛṣṇa  together  the abiding

(77) He had a loving friendship with Svarūpa, and the two of them stayed together, absorbed in talks about Kṛṣṇa.

গদাধর-পণ্ডিতে তিঁহো পুনঃ মন্ত্র দিল ৷  
ওড়ন-ষষ্ঠীর দিনে যাত্রা যে দেখিল ॥ ৭৮ ॥

gadādhara-paṇḍite tim̐ho punaḥ mantra dila  
to Gadādhara Paṇḍita  he  again  mantra  gave  
oḍana-ṣaṣṭhīra dine yātrā ye dekhila  
of Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī  on the day  the festival  which  he saw

(78) He gave the mantra to Gadādhara Paṇḍita a second time, and he saw the festival on the day of Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī.

জগন্নাথ পরেন তথা ‘মাড়ুয়া’ বসন ৷  
দেখিয়া সঘৃণ হৈল বিদ্যানিধির মন ॥ ৭৯ ॥

jagannātha parena tathā ‘māḍuyā’ vasana  
Jagannātha  wears  there  starched  cloth  
dekhiyā saghṛṇa haila vidyānidhira mana  
seeing  disgusted  became  Vidyānidhi’s  mind

(79) There Jagannātha is dressed in starched (*māḍuyā*) cloth, and when Vidyānidhi saw this, he felt disgust.[^14]

সেই রাত্র্যে জগন্নাথ-বলাই আসিয়া ৷  
দুই-ভাই চড়া’ন তাঁরে হাসিয়া হাসিয়া ॥ ৮০ ॥

sei rātrye jagannātha-balāi āsiyā  
that  night  Jagannātha and Balāi  coming  
dui-bhāi caḍā’na tām̐re hāsiyā hāsiyā  
the two brothers  slap  him  laughing  laughing

(80) That night Jagannātha and Balāi came to him, and the two brothers slapped him, laughing all the while.

গাল ফুলিল, আচার্য্য অন্তরে উল্লাস ৷  
বিস্তারি’ বর্ণিয়াছেন বৃন্দাবন-দাস ॥ ৮১ ॥

gāla phulila, ācārya antare ullāsa  
the cheeks  swelled  the Ācārya’s  in the heart  joy  
vistāri’ varṇiyāchena vṛndāvana dāsa  
in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana  dāsa

(81) His cheeks swelled, but the Ācārya (Vidyānidhi) rejoiced at heart. Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail.[^15]

এইমত প্রত্যব্দ আইসে গৌড়ের ভক্তগণ ৷  
প্রভু-সঙ্গে রহি’ করে যাত্রা-দরশন ॥ ৮২ ॥

ei mata pratyabda āise gauḍera bhakta-gaṇa  
in this way  every year  come  of Gauḍa  the devotees  
prabhu-saṅge rahi’ kare yātrā-daraśana  
with the Lord  staying  do  of the festival  seeing

(82) In this way the devotees of Gauḍa came every year, stayed with the Lord and saw the festival.

তার মধ্যে যে যে বর্ষে আছয়ে বিশেষ ৷  
বিস্তারিয়া আগে তাহা কহিব নিঃশেষ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

tāra madhye ye ye varṣe āchaye viśeṣa  
that  among  in which  which  years  there is  something special  
vistāriyā āge tāhā kahiba niḥśeṣa  
in detail  later  that  I shall tell  fully

(83) Whatever was special in particular years, I shall relate fully and in detail later on.

এইমত মহাপ্রভুর চারি বৎসর গেল ৷  
দক্ষিণ যাঞা আসিতে দুই বৎসর লাগিল ॥ ৮৪ ॥

ei mata mahāprabhura cāri vatsara gela  
in this way  of Mahāprabhu  four  years  passed  
dakṣiṇa yāñā āsite dui vatsara lāgila  
to the south  going  to come back  two  years  it took

(84) So four years of Mahāprabhu’s life passed. Going south and coming back took two years.

আর দুই বৎসর চাহে বৃন্দাবন যাইতে ৷  
রামানন্দ-হঠে প্রভু না পারে চলিতে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

āra dui vatsara cāhe vṛndāvana yāite  
another  two  years  He wants  to Vṛndāvana  to go  
rāmānanda-haṭhe prabhu nā pāre calite  
through Rāmānanda’s insistence  the Lord  not  can  set out

(85) For another two years He wanted to go to Vṛndāvana, but because of Rāmānanda’s insistence the Lord could not set out.

পঞ্চম বৎসরে গৌড়ের ভক্তগণ আইলা ৷  
রথ দেখি’ না রহিলা, গৌড়েরে চলিলা ॥ ৮৬ ॥

pañcama vatsare gauḍera bhakta-gaṇa āilā  
in the fifth  year  of Gauḍa  the devotees  came  
ratha dekhi’ nā rahilā, gauḍere calilā  
the chariot  seeing  not  stayed  to Gauḍa  went

(86) In the fifth year the devotees of Gauḍa came; they saw the Ratha-yātrā, but they did not stay and returned to Gauḍa.

তবে প্রভু সার্ব্বভৌম-রামানন্দ-স্থানে ৷  
আলিঙ্গন করি’ কহে মধুর-বচনে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

tabe prabhu sārvabhauma-rāmānanda-sthāne  
then  the Lord  to Sārvabhauma and Rāmānanda  
āliṅgana kari’ kahe madhura-vacane  
embrace  making  speaks  with sweet words

(87) Then the Lord embraced Sārvabhauma and Rāmānanda and spoke to them sweetly:

বহুত উৎকণ্ঠা মোর যাইতে বৃন্দাবন ৷  
তোমার হঠে দুই বৎসর না কৈলুঁ গমন ॥ ৮৮ ॥

bahuta utkaṇṭhā mora yāite vṛndāvana  
great  eagerness  Mine  to go  to Vṛndāvana  
tomāra haṭhe dui vatsara nā kailum̐ gamana  
your  through insistence  two  years  not  I made  departure

(88) “I long very much to go to Vṛndāvana, but because of your insistence I have not gone for two years.

অবশ্য চলিব, দুঁহে করহ সম্মতি ৷  
তোমা-দুঁহা বিনা মোর নাহি অন্য গতি ॥ ৮৯ ॥

avaśya caliba, dum̐he karaha sammati  
certainly  I shall go  both  give  consent  
tomā-dum̐hā vinā mora nāhi anya gati  
you two  without  Mine  there is no  other  refuge

(89) I shall certainly go now; please, both of you, give your consent. I have no refuge but you two.

গৌড়-দেশে হয় মোর ‘দুই সমাশ্রয়’ ৷  
‘জননী’ ‘জাহ্নবী’, — এই দুই দয়াময় ॥ ৯০ ॥

gauḍa-deśe haya mora ‘dui samāśraya’  
in Gauḍa-deśa  are  Mine  two  shelters  
‘jananī’ ‘jāhnavī’, — ei dui dayāmaya  
mother  Jāhnavī  —  these  two  all-merciful

(90) In Gauḍa-deśa I have two shelters, My mother and the Jāhnavī (Gaṅgā); both are all-merciful.

গৌড়-দেশ দিয়া যাব তাঁ-সবা দেখিয়া ৷  
তুমি দুঁহে আজ্ঞা দেহ’ পরসন্ন হঞা ॥ ৯১ ॥

gauḍa-deśa diyā yāba tām̐-sabā dekhiyā  
through Gauḍa-deśa  I shall go  them  seeing  
tumi dum̐he ājñā deha’ parasanna hañā  
you  both  permission  give  pleased  becoming

(91) I shall go by way of Gauḍa-deśa and see them both. Be pleased, both of you, and give Me leave.”

শুনিয়া প্রভুর বাণী মনে বিচারয় ৷  
প্রভু-সনে অতি হঠ কভু ভাল নয় ॥ ৯২ ॥

śuniyā prabhura vāṇī mane vicāraya  
hearing  of the Lord  the words  in the mind  they consider  
prabhu-sane ati haṭha kabhu bhāla naya  
with the Lord  too much  insistence  ever  good  not is

(92) Hearing the Lord’s words, they reflected, “To press the Lord too hard is never good.”

দুঁহে কহে, — এবে বর্ষা, চলিতে নারিবা ৷  
বিজয়া-দশমী আইলে অবশ্য চলিবা ॥ ৯৩ ॥

dum̐he kahe, — ebe varṣā, calite nāribā  
both  say  —  now  the rains  to travel  You will not be able  
vijayā-daśamī āile avaśya calibā  
Vijayā-daśamī  when it comes  certainly  You will go

(93) They both said, “Now it is the rainy season; You cannot travel. When Vijayā-daśamī comes, You will certainly go.”

আনন্দে মহাপ্রভু বর্ষা কৈল সমাধান ৷  
বিজয়া-দশমী-দিনে করিল পয়ান ॥ ৯৪ ॥

ānande mahāprabhu varṣā kaila samādhāna  
in joy  Mahāprabhu  the rainy season  made  completion  
vijayā-daśamī-dine karila payāna  
on the day of Vijayā-daśamī  made  departure

(94) Mahāprabhu happily saw the rainy season through, and on the day of Vijayā-daśamī He set out.

জগন্নাথের প্রসাদ প্রভু যত পাঞাছিল ৷  
কড়ার চন্দন, ডোর, সব সঙ্গে লৈল ॥ ৯৫ ॥

jagannāthera prasāda prabhu yata pāñāchila  
of Jagannātha  the prasāda  the Lord  as much as  had received  
kaḍāra candana, ḍora, saba saṅge laila  
kaḍāra  sandalwood  ropes  all  with  He took

(95) He took with Him all the prasāda of Jagannātha that He had received — *kaḍāra* ointment, sandalwood paste and ropes.

জগন্নাথে আজ্ঞা মাগি’ প্রভাতে চলিলা ৷  
ওড়িয়া ভক্তগণ সঙ্গে পাছে চলি’ আইলা ॥ ৯৬ ॥

jagannāthe ājñā māgi’ prabhāte calilā  
from Jagannātha  permission  begging  at dawn  He set out  
oḍiyā bhakta-gaṇa saṅge pāche cali’ āilā  
the Oḍiyā  devotees  together  behind  going  came

(96) He begged leave of Jagannātha and set out at dawn, and the Oḍiyā devotees came following behind Him.

ওড়িয়া-ভক্তগণে প্রভু যত্নে নিবারিলা ৷  
নিজগণ সঙ্গে প্রভু ‘ভবানীপুর’ আইলা ॥ ৯৭ ॥

oḍiyā-bhakta-gaṇe prabhu yatne nivārilā  
the Oḍiyā devotees  the Lord  with effort  stopped  
nija-gaṇa saṅge prabhu ‘bhavānīpura’ āilā  
His own associates  with  the Lord  to Bhavānīpura  came

(97) With some effort the Lord made the Oḍiyā devotees turn back, and with His own associates He came to Bhavānīpura.

রামানন্দ আইলা পাছে দোলায় চড়িয়া ৷  
বাণীনাথ বহু প্রসাদ দিল পাঠাঞা ॥ ৯৮ ॥

rāmānanda āilā pāche dolāya caḍiyā  
Rāmānanda  came  behind  in a palanquin  riding  
vāṇīnātha bahu prasāda dila pāṭhāñā  
Vāṇīnātha  much  prasāda  gave  sending

(98) Rāmānanda came after Him, riding in a palanquin, and Vāṇīnātha sent a great deal of prasāda.

প্রসাদ ভোজন করি’ তথায় রহিলা ৷  
প্রাতঃকালে চলি’ প্রভু ‘ভুবনেশ্বর’ আইলা ॥ ৯৯ ॥

prasāda bhojana kari’ tathāya rahilā  
prasāda  eating  having done  there  He stayed  
prātaḥ-kāle cali’ prabhu ‘bhuvaneśvara’ āilā  
in the morning  going  the Lord  to Bhuvaneśvara  came

(99) After taking the prasāda He stayed there, and setting out in the morning, the Lord came to Bhuvaneśvara.

‘কটকে’ আসিয়া কৈল ‘গোপাল’ দরশন ৷  
স্বপ্নেশ্বর-বিপ্র কৈল প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১০০ ॥

‘kaṭake’ āsiyā kaila ‘gopāla’ daraśana  
to Kaṭaka  coming  He made  of Gopāla  darśana  
svapneśvara-vipra kaila prabhura nimantraṇa  
the brāhmaṇa Svapneśvara  made  of the Lord  invitation

(100) Coming to Kaṭaka, He had darśana of Gopāla, and the brāhmaṇa Svapneśvara invited the Lord for a meal.

রামানন্দ-রায় সব-গণে নিমন্ত্রিল ৷  
বাহির উদ্যানে আসি, প্রভু বাসা কৈল ॥ ১০১ ॥

rāmānanda-rāya saba-gaṇe nimantrila  
Rāmānanda Rāya  all the associates  invited  
bāhira udyāne āsi, prabhu vāsā kaila  
in an outer  garden  coming  the Lord  residence  made

(101) Rāmānanda Rāya invited all the associates, and the Lord came to a garden outside the town and stayed there.

ভিক্ষা করি’ বকুল-তলে করিলা বিশ্রাম ৷  
প্রতাপরুদ্র-ঠাঞি রায় করিল পয়ান ॥ ১০২ ॥

bhikṣā kari’ bakula-tale karilā viśrāma  
the meal  having taken  under a bakula tree  made  rest  
pratāparudra-ṭhāñi rāya karila payāna  
to Pratāparudra  Rāya  made  departure

(102) After His meal He rested under a *bakula* tree, and Rāmānanda Rāya went to Pratāparudra.

শুনি’ আনন্দিত রাজা অতিশীঘ্র আইলা ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ দণ্ডবৎ ভূমেতে পড়িলা ॥ ১০৩ ॥

śuni’ ānandita rājā ati-śīghra āilā  
hearing  delighted  the king  very quickly  came  
prabhu dekhi’ daṇḍavat bhūmete paḍilā  
the Lord  seeing  like a rod  on the ground  fell

(103) Delighted at the news, the king came at once, and seeing the Lord, he fell flat on the ground like a rod.

পুনঃ উঠে, পুনঃ পড়ে প্রণয়-বিহ্বল ৷  
স্তুতি করে, পুলকাঙ্গ, পড়ে অশ্রুজল ॥ ১০৪ ॥

punaḥ uṭhe, punaḥ paḍe praṇaya-vihvala  
again  he rises  again  he falls  overwhelmed by love  
stuti kare, pulakāṅga, paḍe aśru-jala  
prayers  he offers  with hair standing on end  fall  tears

(104) Overwhelmed by love, he rose and fell again and again; he offered prayers, the hair of his body stood on end, and tears flowed from his eyes.

তাঁর ভক্তি দেখি’ প্রভুর তুষ্ট হৈল মন ৷  
উঠি’ মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

tām̐ra bhakti dekhi’ prabhura tuṣṭa haila mana  
his  devotion  seeing  of the Lord  pleased  became  the mind  
uṭhi’ mahāprabhu tām̐re kailā āliṅgana  
rising  Mahāprabhu  him  made  embrace

(105) Seeing his devotion, the Lord was pleased at heart. Mahāprabhu rose and embraced him.

পুনঃ স্তুতি করি’ রাজা করয়ে প্রণাম ৷  
প্রভু-কৃপা-অশ্রুতে তাঁর দেহ হৈল স্নান ॥ ১০৬ ॥

punaḥ stuti kari’ rājā karaye praṇāma  
again  prayers  offering  the king  makes  obeisance  
prabhu-kṛpā-aśrute tām̐ra deha haila snāna  
in the tears of the Lord’s mercy  his  body  became  bathed

(106) Again the king offered prayers and bowed down, and his body was bathed in tears of the Lord’s mercy.[^16]

সুস্থ করি, রামানন্দ রাজারে বসাইলা ৷  
কায়মনোবাক্যে প্রভু তাঁরে কৃপা কৈলা ॥ ১০৭ ॥

sustha kari, rāmānanda rājāre vasāilā  
calm  making  Rāmānanda  the king  seated  
kāya-mano-vākye prabhu tām̐re kṛpā kailā  
in body, mind and words  the Lord  to him  mercy  showed

(107) Rāmānanda calmed the king and seated him, and the Lord showed him mercy in body, mind and words.

ঐছে তাঁহারে কৃপা কৈল গৌররায় ৷  
‘প্রতাপরুদ্র-সংত্রাতা’ নাম হৈল যায় ॥ ১০৮ ॥

aiche tām̐hāre kṛpā kaila gaurarāya  
such  to him  mercy  showed  Gaurarāya  
‘pratāparudra-saṁtrātā’ nāma haila yāya  
the Saviour of Pratāparudra  the name  became  by which

(108) Gaurarāya showed him such mercy that He came to be called “the Saviour of Pratāparudra.”

রাজ-পাত্রগণ কৈল প্রভুর বন্দন ৷  
রাজারে বিদায় দিলা শচীর নন্দন ॥ ১০৯ ॥

rāja-pātra-gaṇa kaila prabhura vandana  
the royal ministers  made  of the Lord  homage  
rājāre vidāya dilā śacīra nandana  
to the king  leave  gave  of Śacī  the son

(109) The king’s ministers paid homage to the Lord, and Śacī’s son gave the king leave to go.

বাহিরে আসি’ রাজা আজ্ঞা-পত্র লেখাইল ৷  
নিজ-রাজ্যে যত ‘বিষয়ী’, তাহারে পাঠাইল ॥ ১১০ ॥

bāhire āsi’ rājā ājñā-patra lekhāila  
outside  coming  the king  a written order  had written  
nija-rājye yata ‘viṣayī’, tāhāre pāṭhāila  
in his own kingdom  as many  revenue officers  to them  sent

(110) Coming outside, the king had an order drawn up and sent it to all the revenue officers (*viṣayī*) of his kingdom:

‘গ্রামে-গ্রামে’ নূতন আবাস করিবা ৷  
পাঁচ-সাত গৃহ, সব সামগ্র্যে ভরিবা ॥ ১১১ ॥

‘grāme-grāme’ nūtana āvāsa karibā  
in village after village  new  lodgings  you shall make  
pām̐ca-sāta gṛha, saba sāmagrye bharibā  
five or seven  houses  with all  provisions  you shall fill

(111) “In every village build new lodgings; fill five or seven houses with all provisions.

আপনি প্রভুকে লঞা তাঁহা উত্তরিবা ৷  
রাত্রি-দিবা বেত্রহস্তে সেবায় রহিবা ॥ ১১২ ॥

āpani prabhuke lañā tām̐hā uttaribā  
yourselves  the Lord  taking  there  you shall lodge  
rātri-dibā vetra-haste sevāya rahibā  
night and day  cane in hand  in service  you shall remain

(112) Escort the Lord there yourselves and lodge Him, and stay in His service night and day, cane in hand.”

দুই মহাপাত্র, — ‘হরিচন্দন’, ‘মঙ্গরাজ’ ৷  
তাঁরে আজ্ঞা দিল রাজা — করিহ সর্ব্ব কাজ ॥ ১১৩ ॥

dui mahā-pātra, — ‘haricandana’, ‘maṅgarāja’  
two  great ministers  —  Haricandana  Maṅgarāja  
tām̐re ājñā dila rājā — kariha sarva kāja  
to them  order  gave  the king  —  do  every  task

(113) The king gave this order to two of his chief ministers, Haricandana and Maṅgarāja: “See to everything.

এক নব্য-নৌকা আনি’ রাখহ নদী-তীরে ৷  
যাঁহা স্নান করি’ প্রভু যা’ন নদী-পারে ॥ ১১৪ ॥

eka navya-naukā āni’ rākhaha nadī-tīre  
one  new boat  bringing  keep  on the river bank  
yām̐hā snāna kari’ prabhu yā’na nadī-pāre  
where  bath  having taken  the Lord  goes  across the river

(114) Bring a new boat and keep it at the river bank where the Lord will bathe and cross the river.

তাঁহা স্তম্ভ রোপণ কর ‘মহাতীর্থ’ করি’ ৷  
নিত্য স্নান করিহ তাঁহা, তাঁহা যেন মরি ॥ ১১৫ ॥

tām̐hā stambha ropaṇa kara ‘mahā-tīrtha’ kari’  
there  a pillar  erecting  do  a great tīrtha  making  
nitya snāna kariha tām̐hā, tām̐hā yena mari  
daily  bath  take  there  there  may  I die

(115) Erect a pillar there and make it a great *tīrtha*. Bathe there every day — and may I leave my body there.[^17]

চতুর্দ্বারে করহ উত্তম নব্য বাস ৷  
রামানন্দ, যাহ তুমি মহাপ্রভু-পাশ ॥ ১১৬ ॥

caturdvāre karaha uttama navya vāsa  
in Caturdvāra  build  excellent  new  lodgings  
rāmānanda, yāha tumi mahāprabhu-pāśa  
Rāmānanda  go  you  to Mahāprabhu’s side

(116) Build excellent new lodgings in Caturdvāra. And you, Rāmānanda, go to Mahāprabhu.”[^18]

সন্ধ্যাতে চলিবে প্রভু, — নৃপতি শুনিল ৷  
হস্তী-উপর তাম্বুগৃহে স্ত্রীগণে চড়াইল ॥ ১১৭ ॥

sandhyāte calibe prabhu, — nṛpati śunila  
in the evening  will set out  the Lord  —  the king  heard  
hastī-upara tāmbu-gṛhe strī-gaṇe caḍāila  
on elephants  in tents  the women  he seated

(117) The king heard that the Lord would set out in the evening, and he had the royal ladies seated in tents on the backs of elephants.

প্রভুর চলিবার পথে রহে সারি হঞা ৷  
সন্ধ্যাতে চলিলা প্রভু নিজগণ লঞা ॥ ১১৮ ॥

prabhura calibāra pathe rahe sāri hañā  
of the Lord  of the going  on the road  they stay  in a row  forming  
sandhyāte calilā prabhu nija-gaṇa lañā  
in the evening  set out  the Lord  His own associates  taking

(118) They stood in a row along the road the Lord was to take, and in the evening the Lord set out with His associates.

‘চিত্রোৎপলা-নদী’ আসি’ ঘাটে কৈল স্নান ৷  
মহিষীসকল দেখে, করয়ে প্রণাম ॥ ১১৯ ॥

‘citrotpalā-nadī’ āsi’ ghāṭe kaila snāna  
to the river Citrotpalā  coming  at the ghāṭ  made  bath  
mahiṣī-sakala dekhe, karaye praṇāma  
the queens all  see  make  obeisance

(119) Coming to the river Citrotpalā, He bathed at the *ghāṭ*, and all the queens watched Him and bowed down.[^19]

প্রভুর দরশনে সবে হৈল প্রেমময় ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহে, নেত্র অশ্রু বরিষয় ॥ ১২০ ॥

prabhura daraśane sabe haila premamaya  
of the Lord  at the sight  all  became  full of love  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahe, netra aśru variṣaya  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  they say  the eyes  tears  shower

(120) At the sight of the Lord they were all filled with love; they cried “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” and tears streamed from their eyes.

এমন কৃপালু নাহি শুনি ত্রিভুবনে ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেমা হয় যাঁর দূর দরশনে ॥ ১২১ ॥

emana kṛpālu nāhi śuni tribhuvane  
such  merciful  not  I hear  in the three worlds  
kṛṣṇa-premā haya yām̐ra dūra daraśane  
love for Kṛṣṇa  arises  whose  from afar  at the sight

(121) Nowhere in the three worlds have I heard of one so merciful: love for Kṛṣṇa awakens even in those who see Him from afar.

নৌকাতে চড়িয়া প্রভু হৈল নদী পার ৷  
জ্যোৎস্নাবতী রাত্র্যে চলি’ আইলা চতুর্দ্বার ॥ ১২২ ॥

naukāte caḍiyā prabhu haila nadī pāra  
into the boat  climbing  the Lord  became  of the river  across  
jyotsnāvatī rātrye cali’ āilā caturdvāra  
on a moonlit  night  going  came  to Caturdvāra

(122) The Lord boarded the boat and crossed the river, and travelling on through the moonlit night He came to Caturdvāra.

রাত্র্যে তথা রহি’ প্রাতে স্নানকৃত্য কৈল ৷  
হেনকালে জগন্নাথের মহাপ্রসাদ আইল ॥ ১২৩ ॥

rātrye tathā rahi’ prāte snāna-kṛtya kaila  
at night  there  staying  in the morning  bathing  made  
hena-kāle jagannāthera mahā-prasāda āila  
at that time  of Jagannātha  mahā-prasāda  came

(123) He stayed there that night and bathed in the morning. Just then Jagannātha’s mahā-prasāda arrived.

রাজার আজ্ঞায় পড়িছা পাঠায় দিনে দিনে ৷  
বহুত প্রসাদ পাঠায় দিয়া বহু-জনে ॥ ১২৪ ॥

rājāra ājñāya paḍichā pāṭhāya dine dine  
of the king  by the order  the paḍichā  sends  day  by day  
bahuta prasāda pāṭhāya diyā bahu-jane  
abundant  prasāda  sends  by  many people

(124) By the king’s order the *paḍichā* sent prasāda day after day — a great quantity of it, carried by many people.

স্বগণ-সহিতে প্রভু প্রসাদ অঙ্গীকরি’ ৷  
উঠিয়া চলিলা প্রভু বলি’ ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ১২৫ ॥

sva-gaṇa-sahite prabhu prasāda aṅgīkari’  
with His own associates  the Lord  prasāda  accepting  
uṭhiyā calilā prabhu bali’ ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
rising  set out  the Lord  saying  Hari  Hari

(125) The Lord accepted the prasāda with His associates, then rose and set out, calling “Hari! Hari!”

রামানন্দ, মঙ্গরাজ, শ্রীহরিচন্দন ৷  
সঙ্গে সেবা করি’ চলে এই তিন জন ॥ ১২৬ ॥

rāmānanda, maṅgarāja, śrī-haricandana  
Rāmānanda  Maṅgarāja  Śrī Haricandana  
saṅge sevā kari’ cale ei tina jana  
together  service  doing  go  these  three  persons

(126) Rāmānanda, Maṅgarāja and Śrī Haricandana — these three went along with Him, serving Him.

প্রভু-সঙ্গে পুরী-গোসাঞি, স্বরূপ-দামোদর ৷  
জগদানন্দ, মুকুন্দ, গোবিন্দ, কাশীশ্বর ॥ ১২৭ ॥

হরিদাস-ঠাকুর, আর পণ্ডিত-বক্রেশ্বর ৷  
গোপীনাথাচার্য্য, আর পণ্ডিত-দামোদর ॥ ১২৮ ॥

রামাই, নন্দাই, আর বহু ভক্তগণ ৷  
প্রধান কহিলুঁ, সবার কে করে গণন ॥ ১২৯ ॥

prabhu-saṅge purī-gosāñi, svarūpa-dāmodara  
with the Lord  Purī Gosāñi  Svarūpa Dāmodara  
jagadānanda, mukunda, govinda, kāśīśvara  
Jagadānanda  Mukunda  Govinda  Kāśīśvara  
haridāsa-ṭhākura, āra paṇḍita-vakreśvara  
Haridāsa Ṭhākura  and  Paṇḍita Vakreśvara  
gopīnāthācārya, āra paṇḍita-dāmodara  
Gopīnāthācārya  and  Paṇḍita Dāmodara  
rāmāi, nandāi, āra bahu bhakta-gaṇa  
Rāmāi  Nandāi  and  many  devotees  
pradhāna kahilum̐, sabāra ke kare gaṇana  
the chief  I have named  of all  who  makes  a count

(127–129) With the Lord were Purī Gosāñi, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Jagadānanda, Mukunda, Govinda, Kāśīśvara, Haridāsa Ṭhākura, Vakreśvara Paṇḍita, Gopīnāthācārya, Dāmodara Paṇḍita, Rāmāi, Nandāi and many other devotees. I have named only the chief among them — who can count them all?

গদাধর-পণ্ডিত যবে সঙ্গেতে চলিলা ৷  
ক্ষেত্র-সন্ন্যাস না ছাড়িহ — প্রভু নিষেধিলা ॥ ১৩০ ॥

gadādhara-paṇḍita yabe saṅgete calilā  
Gadādhara Paṇḍita  when  along  set out  
kṣetra-sannyāsa nā chāḍiha — prabhu niṣedhilā  
the kṣetra-sannyāsa  not  give up  —  the Lord  forbade

(130) When Gadādhara Paṇḍita set out with them, the Lord forbade him: “Do not give up your *kṣetra-sannyāsa*.”[^20]

পণ্ডিত কহে, — যাঁহা তুমি, সেই নীলাচল ৷  
ক্ষেত্রসন্ন্যাস মোর যাউক রসাতল ॥ ১৩১ ॥

paṇḍita kahe, — yām̐hā tumi, sei nīlācala  
the Paṇḍita  says  —  where  You  that  Nīlācala  
kṣetrasannyāsa mora yāuka rasātala  
kṣetra-sannyāsa  my  let go  to Rasātala

(131) The Paṇḍita said, “Wherever You are, that is Nīlācala. Let my *kṣetra-sannyāsa* go to Rasātala!”

প্রভু কহে, — ইঁহা কর গোপীনাথ সেবন ৷  
পণ্ডিত কহে, — কোটি-সেবা ত্বৎপাদ-দর্শন ॥ ১৩২ ॥

prabhu kahe, — im̐hā kara gopīnātha sevana  
the Lord  says  —  here  do  Gopīnātha’s  service  
paṇḍita kahe, — koṭi-sevā tvat-pāda-darśana  
the Paṇḍita  says  —  ten million services  the sight of Your feet

(132) The Lord said, “Stay here and serve Gopīnātha.” The Paṇḍita said, “Seeing Your feet is worth ten million services.”

প্রভু কহে, — সেবা ছাড়িবে, আমায় লাগে দোষ ৷  
ইঁহা রহি’ সেবা কর, — আমার সন্তোষ ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

prabhu kahe, — sevā chāḍibe, āmāya lāge doṣa  
the Lord  says  —  the service  you will give up  to Me  attaches  the fault  
im̐hā rahi’ sevā kara, — āmāra santoṣa  
here  staying  service  do  —  My  satisfaction

(133) The Lord said, “If you give up the service, the fault will fall on Me. Stay here and serve — that will please Me.”

পণ্ডিত কহে, — সব দোষ আমার উপর ৷  
তোমা-সঙ্গে না যাইব, যাইব একেশ্বর ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

paṇḍita kahe, — saba doṣa āmāra upara  
the Paṇḍita  says  —  all  fault  me  upon  
tomā-saṅge nā yāiba, yāiba ekeśvara  
with You  not  I shall go  I shall go  alone

(134) The Paṇḍita said, “All the fault will fall on me. I shall not go with You; I shall go alone.

‘আই’কে দেখিতে যাইব, না যাইব তোমা লাগি’ ৷  
‘প্রতিজ্ঞা’-‘সেবা’-ত্যাগ-দোষ, — তার আমি ভাগী ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

‘āi’ke dekhite yāiba, nā yāiba tomā lāgi’  
Āi  to see  I shall go  not  I shall go  You  for the sake of  
‘pratijñā’-‘sevā’-tyāga-doṣa, — tāra āmi bhāgī  
of giving up the vow and the service  the fault  —  of that  I  the bearer

(135) I shall go to see Āi (Mother Śacī), not for Your sake. The fault of giving up my vow and my service — that I shall bear myself.”[^21]

এত বলি’ পণ্ডিত-গোসাঞি পৃথক্ চলিলা ৷  
কটক আসি’ প্রভু তাঁরে সঙ্গে আনাইলা ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

eta bali’ paṇḍita-gosāñi pṛthak calilā  
so  saying  Paṇḍita Gosāñi  separately  went  
kaṭaka āsi’ prabhu tām̐re saṅge ānāilā  
to Kaṭaka  coming  the Lord  him  with  had brought

(136) Saying this, Paṇḍita Gosāñi travelled on separately. When the Lord reached Kaṭaka, He had him brought to Him.

পণ্ডিতের গৌরাঙ্গ প্রেম বুঝন না যায় ৷  
‘প্রতিজ্ঞা’, ‘শ্রীকৃষ্ণ-সেবা’ ছাড়িল তৃণপ্রায় ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

paṇḍitera gaurāṅga prema bujhana nā yāya  
of the Paṇḍita  for Gaurāṅga  love  to understand  not  is possible  
‘pratijñā’, ‘śrī-kṛṣṇa-sevā’ chāḍila tṛṇa-prāya  
the vow  the service of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  he gave up  like a straw

(137) The Paṇḍita’s love for Gaurāṅga is beyond understanding: he gave up his vow and the service of Śrī Kṛṣṇa as though they were a blade of grass.

তাঁহার চরিত্রে প্রভু অন্তরে সন্তোষ ৷  
তাঁহার হাতে ধরি’ কহে, করি’ প্রণয়-রোষ ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

tām̐hāra caritre prabhu antare santoṣa  
his  by the conduct  the Lord  inwardly  satisfaction  
tām̐hāra hāte dhari’ kahe, kari’ praṇaya-roṣa  
his  by the hand  taking  He says  making  loving anger

(138) Inwardly the Lord was pleased with his conduct, but He took him by the hand and spoke with loving anger:

‘প্রতিজ্ঞা’, ‘সেবা’ ছাড়িবে, — এ তোমার ‘উদ্দেশ’ ৷  
সে সিদ্ধ হইল — ছাড়ি’ আইলা দূর দেশ ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

‘pratijñā’, ‘sevā’ chāḍibe, — e tomāra ‘uddeśa’  
the vow  the service  to give up  —  this  your  aim  
se siddha haila — chāḍi’ āilā dūra deśa  
that  accomplished  became  —  leaving  you have come  far  country

(139) “You meant to give up your vow and your service, and you have succeeded: you have left them behind and come to a distant land.

আমার সঙ্গে রহিতে চাহ, — বাঞ্ছ নিজ-‘সুখ’ ৷  
তোমার দুই ধর্ম্ম যায়, — আমার হয় ‘দুঃখ’ ॥ ১৪০ ॥

āmāra saṅge rahite cāha, — vāñcha nija-‘sukha’  
Me  with  to stay  you want  —  you desire  your own happiness  
tomāra dui dharma yāya, — āmāra haya ‘duḥkha’  
your  two  duties  go  —  Mine  becomes  sorrow

(140) In wanting to stay with Me, you seek your own happiness. But you are failing in both your duties, and that makes Me sad.

মোর সুখ চাহ যদি, নীলাচলে চল ৷  
আমার শপথ, যদি আর কিছু বল ॥ ১৪১ ॥

mora sukha cāha yadi, nīlācale cala  
My  happiness  you want  if  to Nīlācala  go  
āmāra śapatha, yadi āra kichu bala  
by Me  sworn  if  more  anything  you say

(141) If you want My happiness, go back to Nīlācala. I bind you by My oath — not one word more.”

এত বলি’ মহাপ্রভু নৌকাতে চড়িলা ৷  
মূর্চ্ছিত হঞা পণ্ডিত তথা পড়িলা ॥ ১৪২ ॥

eta bali’ mahāprabhu naukāte caḍilā  
so  saying  Mahāprabhu  into the boat  climbed  
mūrcchita hañā paṇḍita tathā paḍilā  
unconscious  becoming  the Paṇḍita  there  fell

(142) Saying this, Mahāprabhu got into the boat, and the Paṇḍita fell there unconscious.

পণ্ডিতে লঞা যাইতে সার্ব্বভৌমে আজ্ঞা দিলা ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য কহে, — উঠ, ঐছে প্রভুর লীলা ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

paṇḍite lañā yāite sārvabhaume ājñā dilā  
the Paṇḍita  taking  to go  to Sārvabhauma  order  gave  
bhaṭṭācārya kahe, — uṭha, aiche prabhura līlā  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  —  rise  such  of the Lord  the līlā

(143) The Lord ordered Sārvabhauma to take the Paṇḍita back with him. The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Get up — such is the Lord’s *līlā*.

তুমি জান, কৃষ্ণ নিজ প্রতিজ্ঞা ছাড়িলা ৷  
ভক্ত কৃপা-বশে ভীষ্মের প্রতিজ্ঞা রাখিলা ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

tumi jāna, kṛṣṇa nija pratijñā chāḍilā  
you  know  Kṛṣṇa  His own  vow  gave up  
bhakta kṛpā-vaśe bhīṣmera pratijñā rākhilā  
for His devotee  by mercy compelled  Bhīṣma’s  vow  He kept

(144) You know how Kṛṣṇa gave up His own promise: moved by mercy for His devotee, He kept Bhīṣma’s promise.

স্বনিগমমপহায় মৎ প্রতিজ্ঞা-  
মৃতমধিকর্ত্তুমবপ্লুতো রথস্থঃ ৷  
ধৃতরথচরণোঽভ্যয়াচ্চলদ্গু-  
র্হরিরিব হন্তুমিভং গতোত্তরীয়ঃ ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

sva-nigamam apahāya mat-pratijñām  
His own promise  giving up  my promise  
ṛtam adhikartum avapluto ratha-sthaḥ  
true  to make  He jumped down  standing on the chariot  
dhṛta-ratha-caraṇo ’bhyayāc calad-gur  
holding the chariot wheel  He rushed  the earth trembling  
harir iva hantum ibhaṁ gatottarīyaḥ  
a lion  like  to kill  an elephant  His upper cloth fallen

(145) ‘To make my promise true, He gave up His own: He leapt down from the chariot, took up a chariot wheel and rushed at me like a lion about to kill an elephant; the earth trembled beneath Him, and His upper cloth fell away.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.9.37)[^22]

এইমত প্রভু তোমার বিচ্ছেদ সহিয়া ৷  
তোমার প্রতিজ্ঞা রক্ষা কৈল যত্ন করিয়া ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

ei mata prabhu tomāra viccheda sahiyā  
in this  way  the Lord  your  separation  enduring  
tomāra pratijñā rakṣā kaila yatna kariyā  
your  vow  protection  made  effort  making

(146) In the same way the Lord endured separation from you and took great pains to protect your vow.”

এইমত কহি’ তাঁরে প্রবোধ করিলা ৷  
দুইজনে শোকাকুল নীলাচলে আইলা ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

ei mata kahi’ tām̐re prabodha karilā  
in this  way  saying  him  consolation  gave  
dui-jane śokākula nīlācale āilā  
the two  stricken with grief  to Nīlācala  came

(147) With these words the Bhaṭṭācārya consoled the Paṇḍita, and the two of them, stricken with grief, came back to Nīlācala.

প্রভু লাগি’ ধর্ম্ম-কর্ম্ম ছাড়ে ভক্তগণ ৷  
ভক্ত-ধর্ম্ম-হানি প্রভুর না হয় সহন ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

prabhu lāgi’ dharma-karma chāḍe bhakta-gaṇa  
the Lord  for the sake of  religious duties and works  give up  the devotees  
bhakta-dharma-hāni prabhura nā haya sahana  
of the devotees  duty  the loss  for the Lord  not  is  bearable

(148) For the Lord’s sake the devotees give up their religious duties and works, yet the Lord cannot bear to see a devotee’s duty suffer.

‘প্রেমের বিবর্ত্ত’ ইহা শুনে যেই জন ৷  
অচিরে মিলয়ে তাঁরে চৈতন্য-চরণ ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

‘premera vivarta’ ihā śune yei jana  
of love  the strange turns  this  hears  whoever  person  
acire milaye tām̐re caitanya-caraṇa  
soon  are attained  by him  Caitanya’s feet

(149) These are the strange turns of love (*premera vivarta*). Whoever hears of them soon attains Caitanya’s feet.[^23]

দুই রাজপাত্র যেই প্রভু-সঙ্গে যায় ৷  
‘যাজপুর’ আসি’ প্রভু তারে দিলেন বিদায় ॥ ১৫০ ॥

dui rāja-pātra yei prabhu-saṅge yāya  
two  royal officers  who  with the Lord  were going  
‘yājapura’ āsi’ prabhu tāre dilena vidāya  
to Yājapura  coming  the Lord  to them  gave  leave

(150) When the Lord came to Yājapura, He bade farewell to the two royal officers who were travelling with Him.

প্রভু বিদায় দিল, রায় যায় তাঁর সনে ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথা রামানন্দ-সনে রাত্রি দিনে ॥ ১৫১ ॥

prabhu vidāya dila, rāya yāya tām̐ra sane  
the Lord  leave  gave  Rāya  goes  with Him  
kṛṣṇa-kathā rāmānanda-sane rātri dine  
talks of Kṛṣṇa  with Rāmānanda  night  day

(151) The Lord sent them back, but Rāya went on with Him, and night and day He talked of Kṛṣṇa with Rāmānanda.

প্রতিগ্রামে রাজ-আজ্ঞায় রাজভৃত্যগণ ৷  
নব্য-গৃহে নানা-দ্রব্যে করয়ে সেবন ॥ ১৫২ ॥

prati-grāme rāja-ājñāya rāja-bhṛtya-gaṇa  
in every village  by the king’s order  the king’s servants  
navya-gṛhe nānā-dravye karaye sevana  
in a new house  with all kinds of  provisions  do  service

(152) In every village, by royal order, the king’s servants served Him with a new house and all kinds of provisions.

এইমত চলি’ প্রভু ‘রেমুণা’ আইলা ৷  
তথা হৈতে রামানন্দ-রায়ে বিদায় দিলা ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

ei mata cali’ prabhu ‘remuṇā’ āilā  
in this  way  travelling  the Lord  to Remuṇā  came  
tathā haite rāmānanda-rāye vidāya dilā  
from there  to Rāmānanda Rāya  leave  gave

(153) Travelling in this way, the Lord came to Remuṇā, and from there He sent Rāmānanda Rāya back.[^24]

ভূমেতে পড়িলা রায় নাহিক চেতন ৷  
রায়ে কোলে করি’ প্রভু করয়ে ক্রন্দন ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

bhūmete paḍilā rāya nāhika cetana  
to the ground  fell  Rāya  there was no  consciousness  
rāye kole kari’ prabhu karaye krandana  
Rāya  on the lap  taking  the Lord  does  weeping

(154) Rāya fell to the ground unconscious, and the Lord took him on His lap and wept.

রায়ের বিদায়-ভাব না যায় সহন ৷  
কহিতে না পারি এই তাহার বর্ণন ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

rāyera vidāya-bhāva nā yāya sahana  
Rāya’s  feelings at parting  not  can  be borne  
kahite nā pāri ei tāhāra varṇana  
to tell  not  I can  this  its  description

(155) Rāya’s grief at parting was unbearable; I cannot describe it.[^25]

তবে ‘ওঢ়্রদেশ-সীমা’ প্রভু চলি’ আইলা ৷  
তথা রাজ-অধিকারী প্রভুরে মিলিলা ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

tabe ‘oḍhra-deśa-sīmā’ prabhu cali’ āilā  
then  to the border of the land of Oḍhra  the Lord  travelling  came  
tathā rāja-adhikārī prabhure mililā  
there  the royal governor  the Lord  met

(156) Then the Lord came to the border of the land of Oḍhra, and there the king’s governor met Him.

দিন দুই-চারি তেঁহো করিল সেবন ৷  
আগে চলিবারে সেই কহে বিবরণ ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

dina dui-cāri tem̐ho karila sevana  
days  two or four  he  did  service  
āge calibāre sei kahe vivaraṇa  
ahead  for going on  he  tells  the particulars

(157) He served the Lord for a few days and then explained to Him what lay ahead on the road.

মদ্যপ যবন-রাজার আগে অধিকার ৷  
তাঁর ভয়ে পথে কেহ নারে চলিবার ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

madyapa yavana-rājāra āge adhikāra  
drunken  of the yavana king  ahead  the domain  
tām̐ra bhaye pathe keha nāre calibāra  
his  from fear  on the road  anyone  cannot  travel

(158) “Ahead lies the domain of a drunken yavana king. For fear of him no one can travel this road.

পিছলদা পর্য্যন্ত সব তাঁর অধিকার ৷  
তাঁর ভয়ে নদী কেহ হৈতে নারে পার ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

pichaladā paryanta saba tām̐ra adhikāra  
Picchaladā  as far as  all  his  domain  
tām̐ra bhaye nadī keha haite nāre pāra  
his  from fear  the river  anyone  to get  cannot  across

(159) All the land as far as Picchaladā is his, and for fear of him no one can cross the river.

দিন কত রহ, — সন্ধি করি’ তাঁর সনে ৷  
তবে সুখে নৌকাতে করাইব গমনে ॥ ১৬০ ॥

dina kata raha, — sandhi kari’ tām̐ra sane  
days  a few  stay  a truce  making  with him  
tabe sukhe naukāte karāiba gamane  
then  comfortably  by boat  I shall arrange  the journey

(160) Stay here a few days. I will make a truce with him, and then I will send You on comfortably by boat.”

সেই কালে সে যবনের এক অনুচর ৷  
ওড়িয়া-কটকে আইলা করি’ বেশান্তর ॥ ১৬১ ॥

sei kāle se yavanera eka anucara  
at that  time  that  yavana’s  one  follower  
oḍiyā-kaṭake āilā kari’ veśāntara  
to the Oriyā camp  came  making  a change of dress

(161) At that time one of that yavana’s men came to the Oriyā camp in disguise.[^26]

প্রভুর সেই অদ্ভুত চরিত্র দেখিয়া ৷  
হিন্দু-চর কহে, সেই যবন-পাশ গিয়া ॥ ১৬২ ॥

prabhura sei adbhuta caritra dekhiyā  
the Lord’s  that  wonderful  conduct  seeing  
hindu-cara kahe, sei yavana-pāśa giyā  
the Hindu spy  says  to that  yavana  going

(162) Seeing the Lord’s wonderful ways, the spy in his Hindu guise went back to the yavana and said,

এক সন্ন্যাসী আইল জগন্নাথ হৈতে ৷  
অনেক সিদ্ধ-পুরুষ হয় তাঁহার সহিতে ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

eka sannyāsī āila jagannātha haite  
one  sannyāsī  has come  Jagannātha  from  
aneka siddha-puruṣa haya tām̐hāra sahite  
many  perfected men  are  Him  with

(163) “A sannyāsī has come from Jagannātha, and many perfected souls are with Him.

নিরন্তর করে সবে কৃষ্ণ-সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
সবে হাসে, নাচে, গায়, করয়ে ক্রন্দন ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

nirantara kare sabe kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana  
constantly  perform  all  the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa  
sabe hāse, nāce, gāya, karaye krandana  
all  laugh  dance  sing  do  weeping

(164) All of them constantly perform the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa, laughing, dancing, singing and weeping.

লক্ষ লক্ষ লোক আইসে তাহা দেখিবারে ৷  
তাঁরে দেখি’ পুনরপি যাইতে নারে ঘরে ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

lakṣa lakṣa loka āise tāhā dekhibāre  
hundreds of thousands  hundreds of thousands  people  come  it  to see  
tām̐re dekhi’ punarapi yāite nāre ghare  
Him  seeing  again  to go  cannot  home

(165) Hundreds of thousands of people come to see it, and once they have seen Him, they cannot go home again.

সেই সব লোক হয় বাউলের প্রায় ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি’ নাচে, কান্দে, গড়াগড়ি যায় ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

sei saba loka haya bāulera prāya  
those  all  people  become  madmen  like  
‘kṛṣṇa’ kahi’ nāce, kānde, gaḍāgaḍi yāya  
“Kṛṣṇa”  saying  dance  weep  rolling on the ground  go

(166) All those people become like madmen: crying ‘Kṛṣṇa!’, they dance, weep and roll on the ground.

কহিবার কথা নহে, — দেখিলে সে জানি ৷  
তাঁহার প্রভাবে তাঁরে ‘ঈশ্বর’ করি মানি ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

kahibāra kathā nahe, — dekhile se jāni  
for telling  a matter  it is not  if one sees  that  one knows  
tām̐hāra prabhāve tām̐re ‘īśvara’ kari māni  
His  by the power  Him  “God”  as  I accept

(167) It is not something one can describe; one knows it only by seeing it. Such is His power that I accept Him as God (*Īśvara*).”

এত কহি’ সেই চর ‘হরি’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ গায় ৷  
হাসে, কান্দে, নাচে, গায় বাউলের প্রায় ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

eta kahi’ sei cara ‘hari’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ gāya  
this  saying  that  spy  “Hari”  “Kṛṣṇa”  sings  
hāse, kānde, nāce, gāya bāulera prāya  
laughs  weeps  dances  sings  a madman  like

(168) Saying this, the spy himself began to sing “Hari! Kṛṣṇa!”, laughing, weeping, dancing and singing like a madman.

এত শুনি’ যবনের মন ফিরি’ গেল ৷  
আপন ‘বিশ্বাস’ ওড়িয়া স্থানে পাঠাইল ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

eta śuni’ yavanera mana phiri’ gela  
this  hearing  the yavana’s  mind  turning  went  
āpana ‘viśvāsa’ oḍiyā sthāne pāṭhāila  
his own  secretary (*viśvāsa*)  of the Oriyā  to the place  sent

(169) Hearing this, the yavana had a change of heart, and he sent his own secretary (*viśvāsa*) to the Oriyā officer.[^27]

‘বিশ্বাস’ আসিয়া প্রভুর চরণ বন্দিল ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ কহি’ প্রেমে বিহ্বল হইল ॥ ১৭০ ॥

‘viśvāsa’ āsiyā prabhura caraṇa vandila  
the secretary  coming  the Lord’s  feet  worshipped  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ kahi’ preme vihvala haila  
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  in love  overwhelmed  became

(170) The secretary came and bowed at the Lord’s feet, and, crying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”, he was overwhelmed with love.

ধৈর্য্য হঞা ওড়িয়াকে কহে নমস্করি’ ৷  
তোমা-স্থানে পাঠাইলা ম্লেচ্ছ অধিকারী ॥ ১৭১ ॥

dhairya hañā oḍiyāke kahe namaskari’  
composed  becoming  to the Oriyā  he says  bowing  
tomā-sthāne pāṭhāilā mleccha adhikārī  
to you  has sent  the mleccha  governor

(171) Composing himself, he bowed to the Oriyā officer and said, “The *mleccha* governor has sent me to you.

তুমি যদি আজ্ঞা দেহ’ এথাকে আসিয়া ৷  
যবন অধিকারী যায় প্রভুকে মিলিয়া ॥ ১৭২ ॥

tumi yadi ājñā deha’ ethāke āsiyā  
you  if  permission  give  here  coming  
yavana adhikārī yāya prabhuke miliyā  
the yavana  governor  will go  the Lord  having met

(172) If you give permission, the yavana governor will come here, meet the Lord and go back.

বহুত উৎকণ্ঠা তাঁর, কর্য্যাছে বিনয় ৷  
তোমা-সনে এই সন্ধি, নাহি যুদ্ধ ভয় ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

bahuta utkaṇṭhā tām̐ra, karyāche vinaya  
great  eagerness  his  he has shown  humility  
tomā-sane ei sandhi, nāhi yuddha bhaya  
with you  this  truce  there is no  of war  fear

(173) He is very eager and has asked most humbly. This is a truce with you; there need be no fear of war.”

শুনি’ মহাপাত্র কহে হঞা বিস্ময় ৷  
মদ্যপ যবনের চিত্ত ঐছে কে করয় ! ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

śuni’ mahā-pātra kahe hañā vismaya  
hearing  the Mahāpātra  says  becoming  amazed  
madyapa yavanera citta aiche ke karaya!  
of the drunken  yavana  the heart  like this  who  makes

(174) Hearing this, the Mahāpātra was amazed and said, “Who could make the heart of a drunken yavana like this?

আপনে মহাপ্রভু তাঁর মন ফিরাইল ৷  
দর্শন-স্মরণে যাঁর জগৎ তারিল ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

āpane mahāprabhu tām̐ra mana phirāila  
Himself  Mahāprabhu  his  mind  has turned  
darśana-smaraṇe yām̐ra jagat tārila  
by seeing and remembering  whom  the world  is delivered

(175) It is Mahāprabhu Himself who has changed his heart: the whole world is delivered by seeing Him and by remembering Him.”

এত বলি’ বিশ্বাসেরে কহিল বচন ৷  
ভাগ্য তাঁর — আসি’ করুক প্রভু দরশন ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

eta bali’ viśvāsere kahila vacana  
this  saying  to the secretary  spoke  words  
bhāgya tām̐ra — āsi’ karuka prabhu daraśana  
fortune  his  coming  let him have  of the Lord  sight

(176) Saying this, he told the secretary, “It is his good fortune. Let him come and see the Lord.

প্রতীত করিয়ে — যদি নিরস্ত্র হঞা ৷  
আসে তেঁহো পাঁচ-সাত ভৃত্য সঙ্গে লঞা ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

pratīta kariye — yadi nirastra hañā  
trust  I shall place  if  unarmed  being  
āse tem̐ho pām̐ca-sāta bhṛtya saṅge lañā  
comes  he  five or seven  servants  with him  bringing

(177) I will trust him if he comes unarmed, bringing only five or seven servants with him.”

‘বিশ্বাস’ যাঞা তাঁহারে সকল কহিল ৷  
হিন্দুবেশ ধরি’ সেই যবন আইল ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

‘viśvāsa’ yāñā tām̐hāre sakala kahila  
the secretary  going  to him  everything  told  
hindu-veśa dhari’ sei yavana āila  
a Hindu’s dress  putting on  that  yavana  came

(178) The secretary went and told him everything, and the yavana came dressed as a Hindu.

দূর হৈতে প্রভু দেখি’ ভূমেতে পড়িয়া ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ করে অশ্রু-পুলকিত হঞা ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

dūra haite prabhu dekhi’ bhūmete paḍiyā  
from afar  the Lord  seeing  to the ground  falling  
daṇḍavat kare aśru-pulakita hañā  
prostration  makes  with tears and thrilling hair  becoming

(179) Seeing the Lord from afar, he fell to the ground in prostrate obeisance, in tears and with his hair standing on end.

মহাপাত্র আনিল তাঁরে করিয়া সম্মান ৷  
যোড়-হাতে প্রভু-আগে লয় কৃষ্ণনাম ॥ ১৮০ ॥

mahā-pātra ānila tām̐re kariyā sammāna  
the Mahāpātra  brought  him  showing  honour  
yoḍa-hāte prabhu-āge laya kṛṣṇa-nāma  
with joined palms  before the Lord  he takes  the name of Kṛṣṇa

(180) The Mahāpātra brought him forward with honour, and with joined palms he chanted the name of Kṛṣṇa before the Lord:

অধম যবনকুলে কেন জন্ম হৈল ৷  
বিধি মোরে হিন্দুকুলে কেনে না জন্মাইল ॥ ১৮১ ॥

adhama yavana-kule kena janma haila  
in a low  yavana family  why  birth  took place  
vidhi more hindu-kule kene nā janmāila  
Providence  me  in a Hindu family  why  not  caused to be born

(181) “Why was I born in a low yavana family? Why did Providence not have me born in a Hindu family?

‘হিন্দু’ হৈলে পাইতাম তোমার চরণ-সন্নিধান ৷  
ব্যর্থ মোর এই দেহ, যাউক পরাণ ॥ ১৮২ ॥

‘hindu’ haile pāitāma tomāra caraṇa-sannidhāna  
a Hindu  had I been  I would have gained  Your  nearness to the feet  
vyartha mora ei deha, yāuka parāṇa  
useless  my  this  body  let it go  life

(182) Had I been a Hindu, I would have gained a place near Your feet. This body of mine is useless — let my life go!”

এত শুনি’ মহাপাত্র আবিষ্ট হঞা ৷  
প্রভুকে করেন স্তুতি চরণে ধরিয়া ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

eta śuni’ mahā-pātra āviṣṭa hañā  
this  hearing  the Mahāpātra  overwhelmed  becoming  
prabhuke karena stuti caraṇe dhariyā  
to the Lord  offers  praise  the feet  clasping

(183) Hearing this, the Mahāpātra was overwhelmed with feeling; clasping the Lord’s feet, he praised Him:

চণ্ডাল — পবিত্র, যাঁর শ্রীনাম-শ্রবণে ৷  
হেন-তোমার এই জীব পাইল দর্শনে ॥ ১৮৪ ॥

caṇḍāla — pavitra, yām̐ra śrī-nāma-śravaṇe  
an outcaste  is purified  whose  holy name  by hearing  
hena-tomāra ei jīva pāila darśane  
such  You  this  soul  has obtained  sight

(184) “By hearing Your holy name even a *caṇḍāla* becomes pure — and this soul has gained the sight of such a Lord as You!

ইঁহার যে এই গতি, ইথে কি বিস্ময় ?  
তোমার দর্শন-প্রভাব এইমত হয় ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

im̐hāra ye ei gati, ithe ki vismaya?  
his  that  this  state  in this  what  wonder  
tomāra darśana-prabhāva ei mata haya  
Your  sight’s power  this  way  is

(185) What wonder is there in his coming to such a state? Such is the power of seeing You.”

যন্নামধেয়শ্রবণানুকীর্ত্তনাদ্-  
যৎপ্রহ্বণাদ্যৎস্মরণাদপি ক্বচিৎ ৷  
শ্বাদোঽপি সদ্যঃ সবনায় কল্পতে  
কুতঃ পুনস্তে ভগবন্নু দর্শনাৎ ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

yan-nāmadheya-śravaṇānukīrtanād  
whose  name  from hearing and chanting  
yat-prahvaṇād yat-smaraṇād api kvacit  
to whom  from bowing  of whom  from remembering  even  once  
śvādo ‘pi sadyaḥ savanāya kalpate  
a dog-eater  even  at once  for Vedic sacrifice  becomes fit  
kutaḥ punas te bhagavan nu darśanāt  
how much more  then  You  O Lord  indeed  from seeing

(186) “O Lord, if even a dog-eater hears and chants Your name, bows down to You or remembers You but once, he is instantly made fit to perform Vedic sacrifice. How much more, then, one who sees You!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.33.6)[^28]

তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে কৃপা-দৃষ্টি করি’ ৷  
আশ্বাসিয়া কহে, — তুমি কহ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu tām̐re kṛpā-dṛṣṭi kari’  
then  Mahāprabhu  on him  a merciful glance  casting  
āśvāsiyā kahe, — tumi kaha ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’  
reassuring  says  you  say  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Hari”

(187) Then Mahāprabhu cast a merciful glance on him and, reassuring him, said, “Chant ‘Kṛṣṇa’ and ‘Hari’.”

সেই কহে, — মোরে যদি কৈলা অঙ্গীকার ৷  
এক আজ্ঞা দেহ, — সেবা করি যে তোমার ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

sei kahe, — more yadi kailā aṅgīkāra  
he  says  me  if  You have made  acceptance  
eka ājñā deha, — sevā kari ye tomāra  
one  order  give  service  I may do  that  Your

(188) He said, “If You have accepted me, give me one order, so that I may serve You.

গো-ব্রাহ্মণ-বৈষ্ণবে হিংসা কর্যাছি অপার ৷  
সেই পাপ হইতে মোর হউক নিস্তার ॥ ১৮৯ ॥

go-brāhmaṇa-vaiṣṇave hiṁsā karyāchi apāra  
to cows, brāhmaṇas and Vaiṣṇavas  violence  I have done  boundless  
sei pāpa haite mora hauka nistāra  
that  sin  from  my  may there be  deliverance

(189) I have done endless violence to cows, brāhmaṇas and Vaiṣṇavas. May I be delivered from that sin.”

তবে মুকুন্দ দত্ত কহে, — শুন, মহাশয় ৷  
গঙ্গাতীর যাইতে মহাপ্রভুর মন হয় ॥ ১৯০ ॥

tabe mukunda datta kahe, — śuna, mahāśaya  
then  Mukunda  Datta  says  listen  good sir  
gaṅgā-tīra yāite mahāprabhura mana haya  
to the bank of the Gaṅgā  to go  Mahāprabhu’s  mind  is

(190) Then Mukunda Datta said, “Listen, good sir: Mahāprabhu wishes to go to the bank of the Gaṅgā.

তাঁহা যাইতে কর তুমি সহায়-প্রকার ৷  
এই বড় আজ্ঞা, এই বড় উপকার ॥ ১৯১ ॥

tām̐hā yāite kara tumi sahāya-prakāra  
there  to go  give  you  of help  some form  
ei baḍa ājñā, ei baḍa upakāra  
this  the great  order  this  the great  service

(191) Help Him in some way to get there. That is the great order, and that will be a great service.”

তবে সেই মহাপ্রভুর চরণ বন্দিয়া ৷  
সবার চরণ বন্দি’ চলে হৃষ্ট হঞা ॥ ১৯২ ॥

tabe sei mahāprabhura caraṇa vandiyā  
then  he  Mahāprabhu’s  feet  worshipping  
sabāra caraṇa vandi’ cale hṛṣṭa hañā  
everyone’s  feet  worshipping  goes  joyful  becoming

(192) Then he bowed at Mahāprabhu’s feet and at the feet of all, and went away rejoicing.

মহাপাত্র তাঁর সনে কৈল কোলাকুলি ৷  
অনেক সামগ্রী দিয়া করিল মিতালি ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

mahāpātra tām̐ra sane kaila kolākuli  
the Mahāpātra  him  with  exchanged  embraces  
aneka sāmagrī diyā karila mitāli  
many  gifts  giving  made  friendship

(193) The Mahāpātra embraced him and, giving him many gifts, made friends with him.

প্রাতঃকালে সেই বহু নৌকা সাজাঞা ৷  
প্রভুকে আনিতে দিল বিশ্বাস পাঠাঞা ॥ ১৯৪ ॥

prātaḥ-kāle sei bahu naukā sājāñā  
in the morning  he  many  boats  fitting out  
prabhuke ānite dila viśvāsa pāṭhāñā  
the Lord  to bring  he did  the secretary  sending

(194) In the morning the yavana fitted out many boats and sent his secretary to bring the Lord.

মহাপাত্র চলি’ আইলা মহাপ্রভুর সনে ৷  
ম্লেচ্ছ আসি’ কৈল প্রভুর চরণ বন্দনে ॥ ১৯৫ ॥

mahā-pātra cali’ āilā mahāprabhura sane  
the Mahāpātra  travelling  came  Mahāprabhu  with  
mleccha āsi’ kaila prabhura caraṇa vandane  
the mleccha  coming  made  the Lord’s  feet  worship

(195) The Mahāpātra came along with Mahāprabhu, and the *mleccha* came and worshipped the Lord’s feet.

এক নবীন নৌকা, তার মধ্যে ঘর ৷  
স্বগণে চড়াইলা প্রভু তাহার উপর ॥ ১৯৬ ॥

eka navīna naukā, tāra madhye ghara  
one  new  boat  its  in the middle  a cabin  
sva-gaṇe caḍāilā prabhu tāhāra upara  
with His companions  had board  the Lord  it  upon

(196) There was one new boat with a cabin in the middle, and the Lord went on board with His companions.

মহাপাত্রে মহাপ্রভু করিলা বিদায় ৷  
কান্দিতে কান্দিতে সেই তীরে রহি’ চায় ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

mahā-pātre mahāprabhu karilā vidāya  
the Mahāpātra  Mahāprabhu  gave  leave  
kāndite kāndite sei tīre rahi’ cāya  
weeping  weeping  he  on the bank  remaining  gazes

(197) Mahāprabhu bade the Mahāpātra farewell, and he stood weeping on the bank, gazing after Him.

জলদস্যুভয়ে সেই যবন চলিল ৷  
দশ নৌকা ভরি’ বহু সৈন্য সঙ্গে নিল ॥ ১৯৮ ॥

jala-dasyu-bhaye sei yavana calila  
for fear of river pirates  that  yavana  went along  
daśa naukā bhari’ bahu sainya saṅge nila  
ten  boats  filling  many  soldiers  with him  took

(198) For fear of river pirates the yavana went along too, taking many soldiers with him in ten boats.

‘মন্ত্রেশ্বর’-দুষ্টনদে পার করাইল ৷  
‘পিছল্দা’ পর্য্যন্ত যেই যবন আইল ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

‘mantreśvara’-duṣṭa-nade pāra karāila  
Mantreśvara  the dangerous river  across  he brought them  
‘pichaldā’ paryanta yei yavana āila  
Picchaldā  as far as  who  yavana  came

(199) He brought them across the dangerous Mantreśvara river, and the yavana came with them all the way to Picchaldā.[^29]

তাঁরে বিদায় দিল প্রভু সেই গ্রাম হৈতে ৷  
সে-কালে তাঁর প্রেম-চেষ্টা না পারি বর্ণিতে ॥ ২০০ ॥

tām̐re vidāya dila prabhu sei grāma haite  
him  leave  gave  the Lord  that  village  from  
se-kāle tām̐ra prema-ceṣṭā nā pāri varṇite  
at that time  his  signs of love  not  I can  describe

(200) From that village the Lord bade him farewell. I cannot describe the signs of love he showed then.[^30]

অলৌকিক লীলা করে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
যেই ইহা শুনে, তাঁর জন্ম, দেহ ধন্য ॥ ২০১ ॥

alaukika līlā kare śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
beyond this world  pastimes  performs  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
yei ihā śune, tām̐ra janma, deha dhanya  
whoever  this  hears  his  birth  body  are blessed

(201) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya performs pastimes beyond this world. Whoever hears of them is blessed in his birth and in his body.

সেই নৌকা চড়ি’ প্রভু আইলা ‘পানিহাটি’ ৷  
নাবিকেরে পরাইল প্রভু নিজ-কৃপা-সাটী ॥ ২০২ ॥

sei naukā caḍi’ prabhu āilā ‘pānihāṭi’  
that  boat  riding  the Lord  came  to Pāṇihāṭi  
nāvikere parāila prabhu nija-kṛpā-sāṭī  
the boatman  clothed  the Lord  in the cloth of His own mercy

(202) Riding in that boat, the Lord came to Pāṇihāṭi, and He clothed the boatman in the cloth of His own mercy.

প্রভু আইলা বলি’ লোকে হৈল কোলাহল ৷  
মনুষ্য ভরিল সব, কিবা জল, স্থল ॥ ২০৩ ॥

prabhu āilā bali’ loke haila kolāhala  
the Lord  has come  saying  among the people  arose  an uproar  
manuṣya bharila saba, kibā jala, sthala  
with people  filled  everything  whether  water  land

(203) At the news that the Lord had come a great clamour arose, and land and water alike were filled with people.

রাঘব-পণ্ডিত আসি’ প্রভু লঞা গেলা ৷  
পথে যাইতে লোকভিড়ে কষ্টে-সৃষ্ট্যে আইলা ॥ ২০৪ ॥

rāghava-paṇḍita āsi’ prabhu lañā gelā  
Rāghava Paṇḍita  coming  the Lord  taking  went  
pathe yāite loka-bhiḍe kaṣṭe-sṛṣṭye āilā  
on the way  going  in the crowd  with great difficulty  they came

(204) Rāghava Paṇḍita came and took the Lord with him, but because of the crowds on the way they got there only with great difficulty.

একদিন প্রভু তথা করিয়া নিবাস ৷  
প্রাতে কুমারহট্টে আইলা, — যাঁহা শ্রীনিবাস ॥ ২০৫ ॥

ekadina prabhu tathā kariyā nivāsa  
one day  the Lord  there  making  stay  
prāte kumārahaṭṭe āilā, — yām̐hā śrīnivāsa  
in the morning  to Kumārahaṭṭa  He came  where  Śrīnivāsa

(205) The Lord stayed there one day, and in the morning He came to Kumārahaṭṭa, where Śrīnivāsa lived.

তাঁহা হৈতে আগে গেলা শিবানন্দ-ঘর ৷  
বাসুদেব-গৃহে পাছে আইলা ঈশ্বর ॥ ২০৬ ॥

tām̐hā haite āge gelā śivānanda-ghara  
from there  next  He went  to Śivānanda’s house  
vāsudeva-gṛhe pāche āilā īśvara  
to Vāsudeva’s house  afterwards  came  the Lord

(206) From there He went on to Śivānanda’s house, and after that the Lord came to the house of Vāsudeva.

‘বাচস্পতি-গৃহে’ প্রভু যেমতে রহিলা ৷  
লোক-ভিড়-ভয়ে যৈছে ‘কুলিয়া’ আইলা ॥ ২০৭ ॥

‘vācaspati-gṛhe’ prabhu yemate rahilā  
in Vācaspati’s house  the Lord  how  stayed  
loka-bhiḍa-bhaye yaiche ‘kuliyā’ āilā  
for fear of the crowds  how  to Kuliyā  He came

(207) Then the Lord stayed in Vācaspati’s house, and for fear of the crowds He went on to Kuliyā.

মাধবদাস-গৃহে তথা শচীর নন্দন ৷  
লক্ষ-কোটী লোক তথা পাইল দর্শন ॥ ২০৮ ॥

mādhava-dāsa-gṛhe tathā śacīra nandana  
in Mādhava dāsa’s house  there  of Śacī  the son  
lakṣa-koṭī loka tathā pāila darśana  
hundreds of thousands and millions  people  there  obtained  darśana

(208) In Kuliyā the son of Śacī lodged in the house of Mādhava dāsa, and countless millions of people had His darśana.

সাত দিন রহি’ তথা লোক নিস্তারিলা ৷  
সব অপরাধিগণে প্রকারে তারিলা ॥ ২০৯ ॥

sāta dina rahi’ tathā loka nistārilā  
seven  days  staying  there  the people  He delivered  
saba aparādhi-gaṇe prakāre tārilā  
all  the offenders  in His own way  He saved

(209) He stayed there seven days and delivered the people; in His own way He saved even all the offenders.[^31]

‘শান্তিপুরাচার্য্য’-গৃহে ঐছে আইলা ৷  
শচী-মাতা মিলি’ তাঁর দুঃখ খণ্ডাইলা ॥ ২১০ ॥

‘śāntipurācārya’-gṛhe aiche āilā  
to the house of the Ācārya of Śāntipura  in the same way  He came  
śacī-mātā mili’ tām̐ra duḥkha khaṇḍāilā  
Mother Śacī  meeting  her  sorrow  He dispelled

(210) In the same way He came to the house of the Ācārya of Śāntipura, and meeting Mother Śacī, He dispelled her sorrow.

তবে ‘রামকেলি’-গ্রামে প্রভু যৈছে গেলা ৷  
‘নাটশালা’ হৈতে প্রভু পুনঃ ফিরি’ আইলা ॥ ২১১ ॥

tabe ‘rāmakeli’-grāme prabhu yaiche gelā  
then  to the village of Rāmakeli  the Lord  how  went  
‘nāṭaśālā’ haite prabhu punaḥ phiri’ āilā  
Nāṭaśālā  from  the Lord  again  turning back  came

(211) Then the Lord went to the village of Rāmakeli, and from Nāṭaśālā He turned back once again.

শান্তিপুরে পুনঃ কৈল দশ-দিন বাস ৷  
বিস্তারি’ বর্ণিয়াছেন বৃন্দাবন দাস ॥ ২১২ ॥

śāntipure punaḥ kaila daśa-dina vāsa  
in Śāntipura  again  He made  a ten days’  stay  
vistāri’ varṇiyāchena vṛndāvana dāsa  
in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana  dāsa

(212) In Śāntipura He stayed again for ten days. Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail.[^32]

অতএব ইঁহা তার না কৈলুঁ বিস্তার ৷  
পুনরুক্তি হয়, গ্রন্থ বাড়য়ে অপার ॥ ২১৩ ॥

ataeva im̐hā tāra nā kailum̐ vistāra  
therefore  here  of it  not  I have made  elaboration  
punarukti haya, grantha bāḍaye apāra  
repetition  it is  the book  grows  without limit

(213) Therefore I have not described it at length here: it would be a repetition, and the book would grow endlessly.

তার মধ্যে মিলিলা যৈছে রূপ-সনাতন ৷  
নৃসিংহানন্দ কৈল যৈছে পথের সাজন ॥ ২১৪ ॥

tāra madhye mililā yaiche rūpa-sanātana  
that  within  met  how  Rūpa and Sanātana  
nṛsiṁhānanda kaila yaiche pathera sājana  
Nṛsiṁhānanda  made  how  of the road  the decoration

(214) Among these events are how Rūpa and Sanātana met Him, and how Nṛsiṁhānanda decorated the road for Him.

সূত্রমধ্যে সেই লীলা আমি ত’ বর্ণিলুঁ ৷  
অতএব পুনঃ তাহা ইহাঁ না লিখিলুঁ ॥ ২১৫ ॥

sūtra-madhye sei līlā āmi ta’ varṇilum̐  
in the summary  that  pastime  I  indeed  have described  
ataeva punaḥ tāhā ihām̐ nā likhilum̐  
therefore  again  it  here  not  I have written

(215) I have already described these pastimes in the summary, so I have not written of them here again.

পুনরপি প্রভু যদি ‘শান্তিপুর’ আইলা ৷  
রঘুনাথ-দাস আসি’ প্রভুরে মিলিলা ॥ ২১৬ ॥

punarapi prabhu yadi ‘śāntipura’ āilā  
once again  the Lord  when  to Śāntipura  came  
raghunātha-dāsa āsi’ prabhure mililā  
Raghunātha dāsa  coming  the Lord  met

(216) When the Lord came to Śāntipura once again, Raghunātha dāsa came and met Him.

‘হিরণ্য’, ‘গোবর্দ্ধন’, — দুই সহোদর ৷  
সপ্তগ্রামে বারলক্ষ মুদ্রার ঈশ্বর ॥ ২১৭ ॥

‘hiraṇya’, ‘govardhana’, — dui sahodara  
Hiraṇya  Govardhana  two  brothers  
saptagrāme bāra-lakṣa mudrāra īśvara  
in Saptagrāma  twelve lakhs  of coins  the masters

(217) Hiraṇya and Govardhana were two brothers, masters of twelve lakhs of coins in Saptagrāma.[^33]

মহৈশ্বর্য্যযুক্ত দুঁহে — বদান্য, ব্রহ্মণ্য ৷  
সদাচারী, সৎকুলীন, ধার্ম্মিকাগ্রগণ্য ॥ ২১৮ ॥

mahaiśvarya-yukta dum̐he — vadānya, brahmaṇya  
endowed with great wealth  both  generous  devoted to brāhmaṇas  
sadācārī, satkulīna, dhārmikāgra-gaṇya  
of good conduct  of noble family  counted foremost among the righteous

(218) Both were immensely wealthy, generous and devoted to brāhmaṇas; they were men of good conduct and noble family, counted foremost among the righteous.

নদীয়া-বাসী ব্রাহ্মণের উপজীব্য-প্রায় ৷  
অর্থ, ভূমি, গ্রাম দিয়া করেন সহায় ॥ ২১৯ ॥

nadīyā-vāsī brāhmaṇera upajīvya-prāya  
living in Nadīyā  of the brāhmaṇas  the means of livelihood  almost  
artha, bhūmi, grāma diyā karena sahāya  
money  land  villages  giving  they give  help

(219) They were, as it were, the livelihood of the brāhmaṇas of Nadīyā, helping them with gifts of money, land and villages.

নীলাম্বর চক্রবর্তী — আরাধ্য দুঁহার ৷  
চক্রবর্তী করে দুঁহায় ‘ভ্রাতৃ’-ব্যবহার ॥ ২২০ ॥

nīlāmbara cakravartī — ārādhya dum̐hāra  
Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī  worshipful  of both  
cakravartī kare dum̐hāya ‘bhrātṛ’-vyavahāra  
the Cakravartī  shows  to both  a brother’s dealings

(220) Nīlāmbara Cakravartī was revered by both of them, and the Cakravartī treated them as his brothers.

মিশ্র-পুরন্দরের পূর্ব্বে কর্য্যাছেন সেবনে ৷  
অতএব প্রভু ভাল জানে দুইজনে ॥ ২২১ ॥

miśra-purandarera pūrve karyāchena sevane  
to Miśra Purandara  formerly  they had done  service  
ataeva prabhu bhāla jāne dui-jane  
therefore  the Lord  well  knows  the two

(221) In the past they had served Miśra Purandara, and so the Lord knew them both well.

সেই গোবর্দ্ধনের পুত্র — রঘুনাথ দাস ৷  
বাল্যকাল হৈতে তেঁহো বিষয়ে উদাস ॥ ২২২ ॥

sei govardhanera putra — raghunātha dāsa  
that  Govardhana’s  son  Raghunātha  dāsa  
bālya-kāla haite tem̐ho viṣaye udāsa  
childhood  from  he  to worldly things  indifferent

(222) Raghunātha dāsa was the son of that Govardhana. From childhood he was indifferent to worldly things.

সন্ন্যাস করি’ প্রভু যবে শান্তিপুর আইলা ৷  
তবে আসি’ রঘুনাথ প্রভুরে মিলিলা ॥ ২২৩ ॥

sannyāsa kari’ prabhu yabe śāntipura āilā  
sannyāsa  taking  the Lord  when  to Śāntipura  came  
tabe āsi’ raghunātha prabhure mililā  
then  coming  Raghunātha  the Lord  met

(223) When the Lord came to Śāntipura after taking sannyāsa, Raghunātha had come and met Him.

প্রভুর চরণে পড়ে প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ৷  
প্রভু পাদস্পর্শ কৈল করুণা করিয়া ॥ ২২৪ ॥

prabhura caraṇe paḍe premāviṣṭa hañā  
the Lord’s  at the feet  he falls  absorbed in love  becoming  
prabhu pāda-sparśa kaila karuṇā kariyā  
the Lord  the touch of His foot  gave  mercy  showing

(224) Absorbed in love, he fell at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord mercifully touched him with His foot.

তাঁর পিতা সদা করে আচার্য্য-সেবন ৷  
অতএব আচার্য্য তাঁরে হৈলা পরসন্ন ॥ ২২৫ ॥

tām̐ra pitā sadā kare ācārya-sevana  
his  father  always  does  service to the Ācārya  
ataeva ācārya tām̐re hailā parasanna  
therefore  the Ācārya  to him  became  gracious

(225) His father always served the Ācārya, and so the Ācārya was gracious to him.

আচার্য্য-প্রসাদে পাইল প্রভুর উচ্ছিষ্ট-পাত ৷  
প্রভুর চরণ দেখে দিন পাঁচ-সাত ॥ ২২৬ ॥

ācārya-prasāde pāila prabhura ucchiṣṭa-pāta  
by the Ācārya’s grace  he obtained  the Lord’s  plate of remnants  
prabhura caraṇa dekhe dina pām̐ca-sāta  
the Lord’s  feet  he sees  days  five or seven

(226) By the Ācārya’s grace he received the Lord’s plate of remnants, and for five or seven days he saw the Lord’s feet.

প্রভু তাঁরে বিদায় দিয়া গেলা নীলাচল ৷  
তেঁহো ঘরে আসি’ হৈলা প্রেমেতে পাগল ॥ ২২৭ ॥

prabhu tām̐re vidāya diyā gelā nīlācala  
the Lord  him  leave  giving  went  to Nīlācala  
tem̐ho ghare āsi’ hailā premete pāgala  
he  home  coming  became  with love  mad

(227) The Lord bade him farewell and went to Nīlācala, and he came home mad with love.

বার বার পলায় তেঁহো নীলাদ্রি যাইতে ৷  
পিতা তাঁরে বান্ধি’ রাখে, আনি’ পথ হৈতে ॥ ২২৮ ॥

bāra bāra palāya tem̐ho nīlādri yāite  
time  and again  runs away  he  to Nīlādri  to go  
pitā tām̐re bāndhi’ rākhe, āni’ patha haite  
the father  him  binding  keeps  bringing back  the road  from

(228) Again and again he ran away to go to Nīlādri, and his father would bring him back from the road and keep him bound.

পঞ্চ পাইক তাঁরে রাখে রাত্রি-দিনে ৷  
চারি সেবক, দুই ব্রাহ্মণ রহে তাঁর সনে ॥ ২২৯ ॥

pañca pāika tām̐re rākhe rātri-dine  
five  guards  him  keep  night and day  
cāri sevaka, dui brāhmaṇa rahe tām̐ra sane  
four  servants  two  brāhmaṇas  stay  him  with

(229) Five guards watched him night and day, and four servants and two brāhmaṇas stayed with him.

একাদশ জন তাঁরে রাখে নিরন্তর ৷  
নীলাচলে যাইতে না পায়, দুঃখিত অন্তর ॥ ২৩০ ॥

ekādaśa jana tām̐re rākhe nirantara  
eleven  people  him  keep  constantly  
nīlācale yāite nā pāya, duḥkhita antara  
to Nīlācala  to go  not  he is able  sorrowful  heart

(230) Eleven people guarded him constantly. He could not go to Nīlācala, and his heart was full of sorrow.

এবে যদি মহাপ্রভু ‘শান্তিপুর’ আইলা ৷  
শুনিয়া পিতারে রঘুনাথ নিবেদিলা ॥ ২৩১ ॥

ebe yadi mahāprabhu ‘śāntipura’ āilā  
now  when  Mahāprabhu  to Śāntipura  came  
śuniyā pitāre raghunātha nivedilā  
hearing  to his father  Raghunātha  submitted

(231) Now, hearing that Mahāprabhu had come to Śāntipura, Raghunātha humbly said to his father,

আজ্ঞা দেহ’, যাঞা দেখি প্রভুর চরণ ৷  
অন্যথা, না রহে মোর শরীরে জীবন ॥ ২৩২ ॥

ājñā deha’, yāñā dekhi prabhura caraṇa  
permission  give  going  let me see  the Lord’s  feet  
anyathā, nā rahe mora śarīre jīvana  
otherwise  not  remains  my  in the body  life

(232) “Give me leave to go and see the Lord’s feet; otherwise my life will not stay in my body.”

শুনি’ তাঁর পিতা বহু লোক-দ্রব্য দিয়া ৷  
পাঠাইল বলি’ ‘শীঘ্র আসিহ ফিরিয়া’ ॥ ২৩৩ ॥

śuni’ tām̐ra pitā bahu loka-dravya diyā  
hearing  his  father  many  men and goods  giving  
pāṭhāila bali’ ‘śīghra āsiha phiriyā’  
sent him  saying  quickly  come  returning

(233) Hearing this, his father gave him many men and provisions and sent him off, saying, “Come back quickly.”[^34]

সাত দিন শান্তিপুরে প্রভুসঙ্গে রহে ৷  
রাত্রি-দিবসে এই মনঃকথা কহে ॥ ২৩৪ ॥

sāta dina śāntipure prabhusaṅge rahe  
seven  days  in Śāntipura  with the Lord  he stays  
rātri-divase ei manaḥ-kathā kahe  
night and day  this  thought of the heart  he speaks

(234) For seven days he stayed with the Lord in Śāntipura, and night and day he poured out the one thought of his heart:

রক্ষকের হাতে মুঞি কেমনে ছুটিব !  
কেমনে প্রভুর সঙ্গে নীলাচলে যাব ? ॥ ২৩৫ ॥

rakṣakera hāte muñi kemane chuṭiba!  
of the guards  from the hands  I  how  shall escape  
kemane prabhura saṅge nīlācale yāba?  
how  the Lord’s  with  to Nīlācala  shall I go

(235) “How shall I escape from the hands of my guards? How shall I go to Nīlācala with the Lord?”

সর্ব্বজ্ঞ গৌরাঙ্গপ্রভু জানি’ তাঁর মন ৷  
শিক্ষা-রূপে কহে তাঁরে আশ্বাস-বচন ॥ ২৩৬ ॥

sarvajña gaurāṅga-prabhu jāni’ tām̐ra mana  
omniscient  Lord Gaurāṅga  knowing  his  mind  
śikṣā-rūpe kahe tām̐re āśvāsa-vacana  
as instruction  speaks  to him  words of comfort

(236) The omniscient Lord Gaurāṅga knew his mind, and He comforted him with these words of instruction:

স্থির হঞা ঘরে যাও, না হও বাতুল ৷  
ক্রমে ক্রমে পায় লোক ভবসিন্ধুকূল ॥ ২৩৭ ॥

sthira hañā ghare yāo, nā hao vātula  
steady  becoming  home  go  not  be  mad  
krame krame pāya loka bhava-sindhu-kūla  
step  by step  reaches  a person  the shore of the ocean of material existence

(237) “Be steady and go home; do not act like a madman. Step by step a person reaches the shore of the ocean of material existence.

মর্কট-বৈরাগ্য না কর লোক দেখাঞা ৷  
যথাযোগ্য বিষয় ভুঞ্জ’ অনাসক্ত হঞা ॥ ২৩৮ ॥

markaṭa-vairāgya nā kara loka dekhāñā  
monkey renunciation  not  practise  people  showing  
yathā-yogya viṣaya bhuñja’ anāsakta hañā  
as befits  worldly things  accept  unattached  becoming

(238) Do not make a show of monkey renunciation (*markaṭa-vairāgya*) for people to see. Accept worldly things as is fitting, but without attachment.[^35]

অন্তরে নিষ্ঠা কর, বাহ্যে লোক ব্যবহার ৷  
অচিরাৎ কৃষ্ণ তোমায় করিবে উদ্ধার ॥ ২৩৯ ॥

antare niṣṭhā kara, bāhye loka vyavahāra  
within  firm faith  keep  outwardly  worldly  conduct  
acirāt kṛṣṇa tomāya karibe uddhāra  
very soon  Kṛṣṇa  you  will make  deliverance

(239) Keep firm faith within, and outwardly behave as people do in the world. Very soon Kṛṣṇa will deliver you.

বৃন্দাবন দেখি’ যবে আসিব নীলাচলে ৷  
তবে তুমি আমা-পাশ আসিহ কোন ছলে ॥ ২৪০ ॥

vṛndāvana dekhi’ yabe āsiba nīlācale  
Vṛndāvana  having seen  when  I shall come  to Nīlācala  
tabe tumi āmā-pāśa āsiha kona chale  
then  you  to Me  come  some  on pretext

(240) When I have seen Vṛndāvana and come back to Nīlācala, then come to Me on some pretext.

সে ছল সেকালে কৃষ্ণ স্ফুরাবে তোমারে ৷  
কৃষ্ণকৃপা যাঁরে, তাঁরে কে রাখিতে পারে ॥ ২৪১ ॥

se chala se-kāle kṛṣṇa sphurābe tomāre  
that  pretext  at that time  Kṛṣṇa  will reveal  to you  
kṛṣṇa-kṛpā yām̐re, tām̐re ke rākhite pāre  
Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  on whom  him  who  to hold back  can

(241) At that time Kṛṣṇa Himself will show you that pretext. Who can hold back one who has Kṛṣṇa’s mercy?”

এত কহি’ মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে বিদায় দিল ৷  
ঘরে আসি’ মহাপ্রভুর শিক্ষা আচরিল ॥ ২৪২ ॥

eta kahi’ mahāprabhu tām̐re vidāya dila  
this  saying  Mahāprabhu  him  leave  gave  
ghare āsi’ mahāprabhura śikṣā ācarila  
home  coming  Mahāprabhu’s  teaching  he practised

(242) Saying this, Mahāprabhu bade him farewell, and he went home and followed the Lord’s teaching.

বাহ্য বৈরাগ্য বাতুলতা সকল ছাড়িয়া ৷  
যথাযোগ্য কার্য্য করে অনাসক্ত হঞা ॥ ২৪৩ ॥

bāhya vairāgya vātulatā sakala chāḍiyā  
outward  renunciation  madness  all  giving up  
yathā-yogya kārya kare anāsakta hañā  
as befits  duties  he does  unattached  becoming

(243) Giving up all his outward renunciation and madness, he did his duties as was fitting, without attachment.

দেখি’ তাঁর পিতা-মাতা বড় সুখ পাইল ৷  
তাঁহার আবরণ কিছু শিথিল হইল ॥ ২৪৪ ॥

dekhi’ tām̐ra pitā-mātā baḍa sukha pāila  
seeing  his  father and mother  great  happiness  felt  
tām̐hāra āvaraṇa kichu śithila haila  
his  guard  somewhat  slack  became

(244) Seeing this, his father and mother were very happy, and the watch over him was somewhat relaxed.

ইহাঁ প্রভু একত্র করি’ সব ভক্তগণ ৷  
অদ্বৈত-নিত্যানন্দাদি যত ভক্তজন ॥ ২৪৫ ॥

ihām̐ prabhu ekatra kari’ saba bhakta-gaṇa  
here  the Lord  together  gathering  all  the devotees  
advaita-nityānandādi yata bhakta-jana  
Advaita, Nityānanda and others  all  the devotees

(245) Meanwhile the Lord gathered all His devotees together, Advaita, Nityānanda and all the others,

সবা আলিঙ্গন করি’ কহেন গোসাঞি ৷  
সবে আজ্ঞা দেহ’ আমি নীলাচলে যাই ॥ ২৪৬ ॥

sabā āliṅgana kari’ kahena gosāñi  
everyone  embrace  making  says  Gosāñi  
sabe ājñā deha’ āmi nīlācale yāi  
all  permission  give  I  to Nīlācala  may go

(246) and, embracing them all, Gosāñi said, “All of you, give Me leave to go to Nīlācala.

সবার সহিত ইঁহা আমার হইল মিলন ৷  
এ বর্ষ ‘নীলাদ্রি’ কেহ না করিহ গমন ॥ ২৪৭ ॥

sabāra sahita im̐hā āmāra haila milana  
all  with  here  My  has been  meeting  
e varṣa ‘nīlādri’ keha nā kariha gamana  
this  year  to Nīlādri  anyone  not  make  going

(247) I have met all of you here. This year let no one go to Nīlādri.

তাঁহা হৈতে অবশ্য আমি ‘বৃন্দাবন’ যাব ৷  
সবে আজ্ঞা দেহ’, তবে নির্ব্বিঘ্নে আসিব ॥ ২৪৮ ॥

tām̐hā haite avaśya āmi ‘vṛndāvana’ yāba  
from there  certainly  I  to Vṛndāvana  shall go  
sabe ājñā deha’, tabe nirvighne āsiba  
all  permission  give  then  without obstacles  I shall return

(248) From there I shall certainly go to Vṛndāvana. Give Me your leave, all of you, and then I shall come back without any obstacle.”

মাতার চরণে ধরি’ বহু বিনয় করিল ৷  
বৃন্দাবন যাইতে তাঁর আজ্ঞা লইল ॥ ২৪৯ ॥

mātāra caraṇe dhari’ bahu vinaya karila  
of His mother  the feet  clasping  much  humble entreaty  He made  
vṛndāvana yāite tām̐ra ājñā laila  
to Vṛndāvana  to go  her  permission  He took

(249) Clasping His mother’s feet, He entreated her humbly and received her permission to go to Vṛndāvana.

তবে নবদ্বীপে তাঁরে দিল পাঠাঞা ৷  
নীলাদ্রি চলিলা সঙ্গে ভক্তগণ লঞা ॥ ২৫০ ॥

tabe navadvīpe tām̐re dila pāṭhāñā  
then  to Navadvīpa  her  He sent  
nīlādri calilā saṅge bhakta-gaṇa lañā  
to Nīlādri  He set out  with Him  the devotees  taking

(250) Then He sent her back to Navadvīpa and set out for Nīlādri, taking the devotees with Him.

সেই সব লোক পথে করেন সেবন ৷  
সুখে নীলাচল আইলা শচীর নন্দন ॥ ২৫১ ॥

sei saba loka pathe karena sevana  
those  all  people  on the way  do  service  
sukhe nīlācala āilā śacīra nandana  
happily  to Nīlācala  came  Śacī’s  son

(251) All those people served Him along the way, and the son of Śacī came happily to Nīlācala.

প্রভু আসি’ জগন্নাথ দরশন কৈল ৷  
মহাপ্রভু আইলা — গ্রামে কোলাহল হৈল ॥ ২৫২ ॥

prabhu āsi’ jagannātha daraśana kaila  
the Lord  coming  Jagannātha  darśana  had  
mahāprabhu āilā — grāme kolāhala haila  
Mahāprabhu  has come  in the town  an uproar  arose

(252) On arriving, the Lord went to see Jagannātha, and the town was in an uproar: “Mahāprabhu has come!”

আনন্দিত ভক্তগণ আসিয়া মিলিলা ৷  
প্রেম-আলিঙ্গন প্রভু সবারে করিলা ॥ ২৫৩ ॥

ānandita bhakta-gaṇa āsiyā mililā  
delighted  the devotees  coming  met Him  
prema-āliṅgana prabhu sabāre karilā  
loving embrace  the Lord  to all  gave

(253) The delighted devotees came to meet Him, and the Lord embraced them all with love.

কাশীমিশ্র, রামানন্দ, প্রদ্যুম্ন, সার্ব্বভৌম ৷  
বাণীনাথ, শিখি-আদি যত ভক্তগণ ॥ ২৫৪ ॥

kāśī-miśra, rāmānanda, pradyumna, sārvabhauma  
Kāśī Miśra  Rāmānanda  Pradyumna  Sārvabhauma  
vāṇīnātha, śikhi-ādi yata bhakta-gaṇa  
Vāṇīnātha  Śikhi and others  all  the devotees

(254) Kāśī Miśra, Rāmānanda, Pradyumna, Sārvabhauma, Vāṇīnātha, Śikhi and all the other devotees came.

গদাধর-পণ্ডিত আসি’ প্রভুরে মিলিলা ৷  
সবার অগ্রেতে প্রভু কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ২৫৫ ॥

gadādhara-paṇḍita āsi’ prabhure mililā  
Gadādhara Paṇḍita  coming  the Lord  met  
sabāra agrete prabhu kahite lāgilā  
of all  in front  the Lord  to speak  began

(255) Gadādhara Paṇḍita came and met the Lord, and in front of them all He began to speak:

বৃন্দাবন যাব আমি গৌড়দেশ দিয়া ৷  
নিজ-মাতার, গঙ্গার চরণ দেখিয়া ॥ ২৫৬ ॥

vṛndāvana yāba āmi gauḍa-deśa diyā  
to Vṛndāvana  shall go  I  the land of Gauḍa  through  
nija-mātāra, gaṅgāra caraṇa dekhiyā  
My own mother’s  the Gaṅgā’s  feet  seeing

(256) “I thought, ‘I shall go to Vṛndāvana through the land of Gauḍa, seeing on the way the feet of My mother and of the Gaṅgā.’

এত মতে করি’ কৈলুঁ গৌড়েরে গমন ৷  
সহস্রেক সঙ্গে হৈল নিজ-ভক্তগণ ॥ ২৫৭ ॥

eta mate kari’ kailum̐ gauḍere gamana  
this  way  resolving  I made  to Gauḍa  the journey  
sahasreka saṅge haila nija-bhakta-gaṇa  
about a thousand  with Me  were  of My own devotees

(257) With this resolve I set out for Gauḍa, and about a thousand of My own devotees came along with Me.

লক্ষ লক্ষ লোক আইসে কৌতুক দেখিতে ৷  
লোকের সংঘট্টে পথ না পারি চলিতে ॥ ২৫৮ ॥

lakṣa lakṣa loka āise kautuka dekhite  
hundreds of thousands  hundreds of thousands  people  come  the spectacle  to see  
lokera saṅghaṭṭe patha nā pāri calite  
of the people  in the crush  the road  not  I could  travel

(258) Hundreds of thousands of people came to see the spectacle, and in such a throng I could not travel the road.

যথা রহি, তথা ঘর-প্রাচীর হয় চূর্ণ ৷  
যথা নেত্র পড়ে, তথা লোক দেখি পূর্ণ ॥ ২৫৯ ॥

yathā rahi, tathā ghara-prācīra haya cūrṇa  
where  I stay  there  houses and walls  are  crushed  
yathā netra paḍe, tathā loka dekhi pūrṇa  
where  the eye  falls  there  with people  I see  full

(259) Wherever I stayed, houses and walls were crushed; wherever My eyes fell, I saw the place filled with people.

কষ্টে-সৃষ্ট্যে করি’ গেলাঙ রামকেলি-গ্রাম ৷  
আমার ঠাঞি আইলা ‘রূপ’ ‘সনাতন’ নাম ॥ ২৬০ ॥

kaṣṭe-sṛṣṭye kari’ gelāṅa rāmakeli-grāma  
with great difficulty  making  I went  to the village of Rāmakeli  
āmāra ṭhāñi āilā ‘rūpa’ ‘sanātana’ nāma  
to Me  came  Rūpa  Sanātana  named

(260) With great difficulty I reached the village of Rāmakeli. There two men named Rūpa and Sanātana came to Me.

দুই ভাই — ভক্তরাজ, কৃষ্ণকৃপা পাত্র ৷  
ব্যবহারে — রাজমন্ত্রী হয় রাজপাত্র ॥ ২৬১ ॥

dui bhāi — bhakta-rāja, kṛṣṇa-kṛpā pātra  
two  brothers  kings of devotees  of Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  vessels  
vyavahāre — rāja-mantrī haya rāja-pātra  
in worldly affairs  royal ministers  are  royal officers

(261) The two brothers are kings among devotees, vessels of Kṛṣṇa’s mercy; in worldly affairs they are the king’s ministers and high officers.

বিদ্যা-ভক্তি-বুদ্ধি-বলে পরম প্রবীণ ৷  
তবু আপনাকে মানে তৃণ হৈতে হীন ॥ ২৬২ ॥

vidyā-bhakti-buddhi-bale parama pravīṇa  
in learning, devotion, intelligence and strength  supremely  accomplished  
tabu āpanāke māne tṛṇa haite hīna  
still  themselves  they consider  a blade of grass  than  lower

(262) In learning, devotion, intelligence and strength they are supremely accomplished, yet they consider themselves lower than a blade of grass.

তাঁর দৈন্য দেখি’ শুনি’ পাষাণ বিদরে ৷  
আমি তুষ্ট হঞা তবে কহিলুঁ দোঁহারে ॥ ২৬৩ ॥

tām̐ra dainya dekhi’ śuni’ pāṣāṇa vidare  
their  humility  seeing  hearing  a stone  splits  
āmi tuṣṭa hañā tabe kahilum̐ dom̐hāre  
I  pleased  becoming  then  said  to both

(263) At the sight and sound of their humility even a stone would break. Pleased with them, I said to them both,

উত্তম হঞা হীন করি’ মানহ আপনারে ৷  
অচিরে করিবে কৃষ্ণ তোমার উদ্ধারে ॥ ২৬৪ ॥

uttama hañā hīna kari’ mānaha āpanāre  
exalted  being  low  as  you consider  yourselves  
acire karibe kṛṣṇa tomāra uddhāre  
soon  will make  Kṛṣṇa  your  deliverance

(264) ‘Though you are exalted, you consider yourselves low. Kṛṣṇa will soon deliver you.’[^36]

এত কহি’ আমি যবে বিদায় তাঁরে দিল ৷  
গমনকালে সনাতন ‘প্রহেলী’ কহিল ॥ ২৬৫ ॥

eta kahi’ āmi yabe vidāya tām̐re dila  
this  saying  I  when  leave  to them  gave  
gamana-kāle sanātana ‘prahelī’ kahila  
at the time of going  Sanātana  a riddle  spoke

(265) When I had said this and bade them farewell, Sanātana spoke a riddle as I was leaving:

যাঁর সঙ্গে হয় এই লোক লক্ষ, কোটি ৷  
বৃন্দাবন যাইবার এই নহে পরিপাটী ॥ ২৬৬ ॥

yām̐ra saṅge haya ei loka lakṣa, koṭi  
with whom  along  are  these  people  hundreds of thousands  millions  
vṛndāvana yāibāra ei nahe paripāṭī  
to Vṛndāvana  of going  this  is not  the proper way

(266) ‘When hundreds of thousands, even millions of people go along with one, that is not the way to go to Vṛndāvana.’

তবু আমি শুনিলুঁ মাত্র, না কৈলুঁ অবধান ৷  
প্রাতে চলি’ আইলাঙ ‘কানাইর নাটশালা’-গ্রাম ॥ ২৬৭ ॥

tabu āmi śunilum̐ mātra, nā kailum̐ avadhāna  
yet  I  heard  only  not  I paid  attention  
prāte cali’ āilāṅa ‘kānāira nāṭaśālā’-grāma  
in the morning  travelling  I came  to the village of Kānāira Nāṭaśālā

(267) Yet I only heard it and paid it no heed. In the morning I set out and came to the village of Kānāira Nāṭaśālā.

রাত্রিকালে মনে আমি বিচার করিল ৷  
সনাতন মোরে কিবা ‘প্রহেলী’ কহিল ॥ ২৬৮ ॥

rātri-kāle mane āmi vicāra karila  
at night  in My mind  I  consideration  made  
sanātana more kibā ‘prahelī’ kahila  
Sanātana  to Me  what  riddle  spoke

(268) That night I pondered in My mind the riddle that Sanātana had told Me.

ভাল ত’ কহিল, — মোর এত লোক সঙ্গে ৷  
লোক দেখি কহিবে মোরে — ‘এই এক ঢঙ্গে’ ॥ ২৬৯ ॥

bhāla ta’ kahila, — mora eta loka saṅge  
well  indeed  he spoke  My  so many  people  with Me  
loka dekhi kahibe more — ‘ei eka ḍhaṅge’  
people  seeing  will say  of Me  “this  is some  show”

(269) He spoke well. So many people are with Me that whoever sees it will say, ‘This is just a show!’

‘দুর্ল্লভ’ ‘দুর্গম’ সেই ‘নির্জ্জন’ বৃন্দাবন ৷  
একাকী যাইব, কিবা সঙ্গে একজন ॥ ২৭০ ॥

‘durlabha’ ‘durgama’ sei ‘nirjana’ vṛndāvana  
rare  hard to reach  that  solitary  Vṛndāvana  
ekākī yāiba, kibā saṅge eka-jana  
alone  I shall go  or  with  one person

(270) That Vṛndāvana is rare, hard to reach and solitary. I shall go alone, or with just one companion.

মাধবেন্দ্রপুরী তথা গেলা ‘একেশ্বরে’ ৷  
দুগ্ধদানছলে কৃষ্ণ-সাক্ষাৎ দিল তাঁরে ॥ ২৭১ ॥

mādhavendra-purī tathā gelā ‘ekeśvare’  
Mādhavendra Purī  there  went  alone  
dugdhadāna-chale kṛṣṇa-sākṣāt dila tām̐re  
on the pretext of giving milk  Kṛṣṇa  His direct presence  gave  to him

(271) Mādhavendra Purī went there alone, and on the pretext of bringing him milk, Kṛṣṇa appeared to him in person.

বাদিয়ার বাজি পাতি’ চলিলাঙ তথারে ৷  
বহু-সঙ্গে বৃন্দাবন গমন না করে ॥ ২৭২ ॥

bādiyāra bāji pāti’ calilāṅa tathāre  
a juggler’s  show  setting up  I went  there  
bahu-saṅge vṛndāvana gamana nā kare  
with a crowd  to Vṛndāvana  journey  not  one makes

(272) I was travelling there like a juggler with his show. One does not go to Vṛndāvana with a crowd.[^37]

একা যাইব, কিবা সঙ্গে ভৃত্য একজন ৷  
তবে সে শোভয় বৃন্দাবনের গমন ॥ ২৭৩ ॥

ekā yāiba, kibā saṅge bhṛtya eka-jana  
alone  I shall go  or  with  servant  one  
tabe se śobhaya vṛndāvanera gamana  
then  indeed  is graceful  to Vṛndāvana  the going

(273) I shall go alone, or with just one servant; only then is a journey to Vṛndāvana as it should be.

বৃন্দাবন যাব কাঁহা ‘একাকী’ হঞা !  
সৈন্য সঙ্গে চলিয়াছি ঢাক বাজাঞা ! ॥ ২৭৪ ॥

vṛndāvana yāba kām̐hā ‘ekākī’ hañā!  
to Vṛndāvana  I shall go  where  alone  being  
sainya saṅge caliyāchi ḍhāka bājāñā!  
an army  with  I have set out  drums  beating

(274) I was to go to Vṛndāvana alone — and here I have set out with an army, beating drums!

ধিক্ ধিক্ আপনাকে বলি’ হইলাঙ অস্থির ৷  
নিবৃত্ত হঞা পুনঃ আইলাঙ গঙ্গাতীর ॥ ২৭৫ ॥

dhik dhik āpanāke bali’ hailāṅa asthira  
shame  shame  to Myself  saying  I became  restless  
nivṛtta hañā punaḥ āilāṅa gaṅgā-tīra  
turning back  becoming  again  I came  to the bank of the Gaṅgā

(275) Saying ‘Shame, shame on Me!’, I grew restless, and turning back, I came again to the bank of the Gaṅgā.

ভক্তগণে রাখিয়া আইনু স্থানে-স্থানে ৷  
আমা-সঙ্গে আইলা সবে পাঁচ-ছয় জনে ॥ ২৭৬ ॥

bhakta-gaṇe rākhiyā āinu sthāne-sthāne  
the devotees  leaving  I came  in place after place  
āmā-saṅge āilā sabe pām̐ca-chaya jane  
with Me  came  in all  five or six  persons

(276) I left the devotees in their various places and came back; only five or six people came with Me.

নির্ব্বিঘ্নে এবে কৈছে যাইব বৃন্দাবনে ৷  
সবে মেলি’ যুক্তি দেহ’ হঞা পরসন্নে ॥ ২৭৭ ॥

nirvighne ebe kaiche yāiba vṛndāvane  
without obstacles  now  how  shall I go  to Vṛndāvana  
sabe meli’ yukti deha’ hañā parasanne  
all  together  counsel  give  becoming  gracious

(277) Now, how am I to go to Vṛndāvana without obstacles? All of you together, be gracious and give Me your counsel.

গদাধরে ছাড়ি’ গেনু, ইঁহো দুঃখ পাইল ৷  
সেই হেতু বৃন্দাবন যাইতে নারিল ॥ ২৭৮ ॥

gadādhare chāḍi’ genu, im̐ho duḥkha pāila  
Gadādhara  leaving  I went  he  sorrow  felt  
sei hetu vṛndāvana yāite nārila  
that  for reason  to Vṛndāvana  to go  I could not

(278) I went away leaving Gadādhara behind, and he was grieved. For that reason I could not go to Vṛndāvana.”

তবে গদাধর-পণ্ডিত প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ৷  
প্রভু-পদ ধরি’ কহে বিনয় করিয়া ॥ ২৭৯ ॥

tabe gadādhara-paṇḍita premāviṣṭa hañā  
then  Gadādhara Paṇḍita  absorbed in love  becoming  
prabhu-pada dhari’ kahe vinaya kariyā  
the Lord’s feet  clasping  says  humility  showing

(279) Then Gadādhara Paṇḍita, absorbed in love, clasped the Lord’s feet and humbly said,

তুমি যাহাঁ-যাহাঁ রহ, তাহাঁ ‘বৃন্দাবন’ ৷  
তাহাঁ যমুনা, গঙ্গা, সর্ব্বতীর্থগণ ॥ ২৮০ ॥

tumi yāhām̐-yāhām̐ raha, tāhām̐ ‘vṛndāvana’  
You  wherever  stay  there  is Vṛndāvana  
tāhām̐ yamunā, gaṅgā, sarva-tīrtha-gaṇa  
there  the Yamunā  the Gaṅgā  all the holy places

(280) “Wherever You stay, there is Vṛndāvana, and there are the Yamunā, the Gaṅgā and all the holy places.

তবু বৃন্দাবন যাহ’ লোক শিখাইতে ৷  
সেইত করিবে, তোমার যেই লয় চিত্তে ॥ ২৮১ ॥

tabu vṛndāvana yāha’ loka śikhāite  
still  to Vṛndāvana  You go  people  to teach  
seita karibe, tomāra yei laya citte  
that very thing  You will do  Your  which  takes  heart

(281) Still, You go to Vṛndāvana to teach people. You will do just what Your heart decides.[^38]

এই আগে আইলা, প্রভু, বর্ষার চারি মাস ৷  
এই চারি মাস কর নীলাচলে বাস ॥ ২৮২ ॥

ei āge āilā, prabhu, varṣāra cāri māsa  
now  ahead  have come  Lord  of the rains  four  months  
ei cāri māsa kara nīlācale vāsa  
these  four  months  make  in Nīlācala  residence

(282) Lord, the four months of the rains are almost upon us. Spend these four months in Nīlācala.

পাছে সেই আচরিবা, যেই তোমার মন ৷  
আপন-ইচ্ছায় চল, রহ, — কে করে বারণ ॥ ২৮৩ ॥

pāche sei ācaribā, yei tomāra mana  
afterwards  that  You will do  which  Your  mind  
āpana-icchāya cala, raha, — ke kare vāraṇa  
by Your own will  go  stay  who  makes  prohibition

(283) Afterwards do whatever You have in mind. Go or stay as You please — who could stop You?”

শুনি’ সব ভক্ত কহে প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
সবাকার ইচ্ছা পণ্ডিত কৈল নিবেদনে ॥ ২৮৪ ॥

śuni’ saba bhakta kahe prabhura caraṇe  
hearing  all  the devotees  say  the Lord’s  at the feet  
sabākāra icchā paṇḍita kaila nivedane  
of everyone  the desire  the Paṇḍita  has made  submission

(284) Hearing this, all the devotees said at the Lord’s feet, “The Paṇḍita has put forward what we all desire.”

সবার ইচ্ছায় প্রভু চারিমাস রহিলা ৷  
শুনিয়া প্রতাপরুদ্র আনন্দিত হৈলা ॥ ২৮৫ ॥

sabāra icchāya prabhu cārimāsa rahilā  
everyone’s  by the desire  the Lord  four months  stayed  
śuniyā pratāparudra ānandita hailā  
hearing  Pratāparudra  delighted  became

(285) At everyone’s wish the Lord stayed for the four months, and when Pratāparudra heard this, he was delighted.

সেই দিন গদাধর কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
তাঁহা ভিক্ষা কৈল প্রভু লঞা ভক্তগণ ॥ ২৮৬ ॥

sei dina gadādhara kaila nimantraṇa  
that  day  Gadādhara  made  invitation  
tām̐hā bhikṣā kaila prabhu lañā bhakta-gaṇa  
there  meal  took  the Lord  taking  the devotees

(286) That day Gadādhara invited Him, and the Lord took His meal there with the devotees.

ভিক্ষাতে পণ্ডিতের স্নেহ, প্রভুর আস্বাদন ৷  
মনুষ্যের শক্ত্যে দুই না যায় বর্ণন ॥ ২৮৭ ॥

bhikṣāte paṇḍitera sneha, prabhura āsvādana  
at the meal  the Paṇḍita’s  affection  the Lord’s  relishing  
manuṣyera śaktye dui nā yāya varṇana  
by human  power  the two  not  can  be described

(287) No human being could describe the Paṇḍita’s affection at that meal, or how the Lord relished it.

এই মত গৌরলীলা — অনন্ত, অপার ৷  
সংক্ষেপে কহিয়ে, কহা না যায় বিস্তার ॥ ২৮৮ ॥

ei mata gaura-līlā — ananta, apāra  
in this  way  the pastimes of Gaura  endless  boundless  
saṅkṣepe kahiye, kahā nā yāya vistāra  
in brief  I tell;  to tell  not  goes  in full

(288) Such are Gaura’s pastimes, endless and boundless. I tell them in brief, for they cannot be told in full.[^39]

সহস্র-বদনে কহে আপনে ‘অনন্ত’ ৷  
তবু এক লীলার তেঁহো নাহি পায় অন্ত ॥ ২৮৯ ॥

sahasra-vadane kahe āpane ‘ananta’  
with a thousand mouths  tells  Himself  Ananta  
tabu eka līlāra tem̐ho nāhi pāya anta  
still  of one  pastime  He  not  reaches  the end

(289) Ananta Himself tells of them with His thousand mouths, yet He cannot reach the end of even one pastime.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ২৯০ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(290) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.[^40]

[^2]: The same verse as Madhya 4.2, 6.2 and 12.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

[^3]: The *jhāli* are Rāghava Paṇḍita’s alone; they are described in Antya 10.

[^4]: *Tāṅhāra* (“his” or “her”) is taken as referring to the wife: verses 21–25 speak of the devotees’ wives (heading in the edition: “the journey of Candraśekhara’s wife”).

[^5]: The first line is the second line of Madhya 1.96. *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu had told this story to Nityānanda and His other companions at Remuṇā, on His way to Nīlācala (Madhya 4.19–190).

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya-khaṇḍa, chapter 8. Advaita wished the Lord to come without the other sannyāsīs; they had gone to perform their midday rites when the storm broke, and the Lord came alone and ate Advaita’s rice and vegetables.

[^7]: The first line is the same as in Madhya 3.217, 5.140 and 15.13 (and in verse 81 of this chapter).

[^8]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *tarjā* is speech in the *payāra* and other metres that others cannot easily understand.

[^9]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: what Śrī Advaita asked in His *tarjā*, and what Śacī’s son meant by His smile, no one else could understand.

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: body and life are never apart; that the Lord lives in Nīlācala and Nityānanda in Gauḍa is due only to the Lord’s inconceivable power.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: one on whose lips the name of Kṛṣṇa is present “constantly” is the intermediate devotee (*madhyama-bhāgavata*), higher than the beginner (*kaniṣṭha*), who utters the name once (Madhya 15.106). “Constantly” means without any gap — free of other desires, karma, jñāna and slackness.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is the *mahā-bhāgavata* (*uttama-adhikārī*): at the sight of him the name of Kṛṣṇa comes of itself to the lips of the beholder, and by his mercy the intermediate devotee becomes the highest.

[^13]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord’s answers to the residents of Kulīna-grāma over three years (Madhya 15.106 and verses 72, 74) show these three grades; to serve all three is the duty of a Vaiṣṇava householder.

[^14]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī is the first *ṣaṣṭhī* of the cold season, when Jagannātha is dressed in warm clothing; this is *māḍuyā*, the weaver’s unwashed cloth, stiff with starch. Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi found fault with it and showed some disdain for the devotees of Utkala, and so received his due.

[^15]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya-khaṇḍa, chapters 10 and 11.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: *snāna* — “bathed”.

[^17]: In the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition — *kariha* (“bathe”): the king tells his men to bathe there every day, and for himself wishes to leave his body there. Heading in the edition: “The king’s deep love for Gaura.”

[^18]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Caturdvāra is the village one reaches from Kaṭaka after crossing the Mahānadī; it is commonly called “Caudār”.

[^19]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Mahānadī, at the place where one comes out of Kaṭaka, is called the river Citrotpalā.

[^20]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *kṣetra-sannyāsa* — leaving one’s former home and living, alone or with one’s family, in a holy place of Viṣṇu (Puruṣottama-kṣetra, Navadvīpa-dhāma, Mathurā-maṇḍala and the like), taking this as the highest aim; it is the *vānaprastha-dharma* suited to the age of Kali.

[^21]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on receiving the service of Śrī Gopīnātha, Gadādhara Paṇḍita had vowed to devote his whole life to it; by going with the Lord he would incur two faults — breaking the vow and abandoning the service. On the path of *anurāga* great souls take such faults upon themselves.

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is Bhīṣma’s prayer to Śrī Kṛṣṇa before his passing. Kṛṣṇa’s promise was to protect the Pāṇḍavas without taking up arms; Bhīṣma’s, to make Him take them up; the earth trembled and His upper cloth fell because of His fury. Heading in the edition: “The Lord breaks His own promise to keep His devotee’s.” The verse is quoted within Sārvabhauma’s words (143–146), hence the single quotation marks.

[^23]: The second line is the same as in Madhya 6.286; the translation is brought into line with it. Heading in the edition: “For a devotee, to suffer in separation from Bhagavān is natural; but Bhagavān’s suffering in separation from His devotee is *prema-vivarta*, a strange turn of love. Whoever hears of it attains Caitanya.”

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*: another reading has “from Bhadraka”, which agrees with Madhya 1.149 (“Rāmānanda Rāya accompanied Him as far as Bhadraka”); Bhadraka lies south of Bāleśvara, and Remuṇā close beside it. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: many accounts say that the Lord sent Rāmānanda Rāya back from Bhadraka, before He reached Remuṇā.

[^25]: Heading in the edition: “It is impossible to describe how Rāya felt his separation from the Lord.”

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: “in disguise” — being a yavana, he had exchanged a yavana’s dress for a Hindu’s; hence “the Hindu spy” in 162. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Oriyā camp (*kaṭaka*) was the army post on the border of the king of Utkala’s lands.

[^27]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the ruler of Gauḍa had a department called the *viśvāsa-khānā*, staffed only by the most trusted Kāyasthas; these *viśvāsas* were sent wherever the king had important business.

[^28]: Heading in the edition, which confirms the reference: “Hearing, chanting or remembering the Lord’s name, even one born outside the *varṇas* is purified — what then of one who has seen Him face to face?” *Anubhāṣya*: this is Devahūti’s prayer to Śrī Kapiladeva, when, having heard from Him of the yoga of pure bhakti, she was freed from illusion.

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya*: a dangerous river (*duṣṭa-nada*) is a waterway infested by river pirates; it is hard to pass also because the river is very wide and swift.

[^30]: Heading in the edition: “In Picchaladā the Lord sends him back; his wonderful grief.”

[^31]: Heading in the edition: “In Kuliyā the Lord delivered many offenders; therefore it is the ‘seat of Aparādha-bhañjana’, the place where offences are forgiven.”

[^32]: The second line is the same as in verses 56 and 81 of this chapter.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: Hiraṇya and Govardhana Majumdār were Kāyasthas of a noble family who lived in Śrī Kṛṣṇapura near Saptagrāma (Hooghly district); in those days they collected twelve lakhs of coins in revenue every year.

[^34]: The edition (p. 620) puts the father’s words in quotation marks (‘śīghra āsiha phiriyā’); the closing mark, lost in the base text, has been restored.

[^35]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: monkey renunciation (*markaṭa-vairāgya*) is when the heart is full of thoughts of worldly pleasure while outwardly one wears the *kaupīna* and other signs of renunciation. *Anubhāṣya*: monkeys live without house or clothing as if detached, yet never stop gratifying their senses; such renunciation for show is called *markaṭa-vairāgya*.

[^36]: *Anubhāṣya*: Rūpa and Sanātana are Kṛṣṇa’s eternally perfect associates in Vraja; they play the part of conditioned souls only in the eyes of worldly people, and Kṛṣṇa will soon “deliver” them by engaging them in His eternal service.

[^37]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *bādiyā*, or *bede*, are wandering jugglers; wherever they set up their show a crowd gathers, and the Lord was going to Vṛndāvana with just such a crowd, which was not good.

[^38]: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu’s heart is itself Vṛndāvana, the place of Śrī Rādhākānta’s pastimes; still, to teach people, He goes to the earthly Vṛndāvana.

[^39]: The second line is the same as in Ādi 13.53.

[^40]: The verse = Madhya 7.155 (the closing refrain); the translation is the same as there.
