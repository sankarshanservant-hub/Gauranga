“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 24  
**Mercy to Sanātana: The Explanation of the Ātmārāma Verse**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

আত্মারামেতি পদ্যার্কস্যার্থাংশূন্ যঃ প্রকাশয়ন্ ৷  
জগত্তমো জহারাব্যাৎ স চৈতন্যোদয়াচলঃ ॥ ১ ॥

ātmārāmeti padyārkasy-  
the ātmārāma (verse)  of the sun-verse  
ārthāṁśūn yaḥ prakāśayan  
of the meaning  the rays  who  revealing  
jagat-tamo jahārāvyāt  
of the world  the darkness  dispelled  may He protect  
sa caitanyodayācalaḥ  
He  Caitanya  the mountain of sunrise

(1) Śrī Caitanya is the eastern mountain where the sun of the *ātmārāma* verse rose; revealing the rays of its meanings, He dispelled the darkness of the world. May He protect us.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

তবে সনাতন প্রভুর চরণে ধরিয়া ৷  
পুনরপি কহে কিছু বিনয় করিয়া ॥ ৩ ॥

tabe sanātana prabhura caraṇe dhariyā  
then  Sanātana  the Lord’s  feet  clasping  
punarapi kahe kichu vinaya kariyā  
once more  says  something  humility  showing

(3) Then Sanātana clasped the Lord’s feet and once more humbly spoke:

পূর্ব্বে শুনিয়াছোঁ, তুমি সার্ব্বভৌম-স্থানে ৷  
এক শ্লোকের আঠার অর্থ কৈরাছ ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ৪ ॥

pūrve śuniyāchom̐, tumi sārvabhauma-sthāne  
before  I have heard  You  at Sārvabhauma’s place  
eka ślokera āṭhāra artha kairācha vyākhyāne  
one  of a verse  eighteen  meanings  You gave  in explanation

(4) “I have heard that once, at Sārvabhauma’s place, You explained a single verse in eighteen ways.

আত্মারামাশ্চ মুনয়ো নির্গ্রন্থা অপ্যুরুক্রমে ৷  
কুর্ব্বন্ত্যহৈতুকীং ভক্তিমিত্থম্ভূতগুণো হরিঃ ॥ ৫ ॥

ātmārāmāś ca munayo  
delighting in the ātmā  and  the sages  
nirgranthā apy urukrame  
free from knots  even  to Urukrama  
kurvanty ahaitukīṁ bhaktim  
perform  causeless  devotion  
ittham-bhūta-guṇo hariḥ  
of such  qualities  is Hari

(5) “Even sages who delight in the ātmā and are free from the knots of the heart render causeless devotion to Urukrama, for such are the qualities of Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.10)[^3]

আশ্চর্য্য শুনিয়া মোর উৎকণ্ঠিত মন ৷  
কৃপা করি’ কহ যদি, জুড়ায় শ্রবণ ॥ ৬ ॥

āścarya śuniyā mora utkaṇṭhita mana  
the wonder  having heard  my  eager  mind  
kṛpā kari’ kaha yadi, juḍāya śravaṇa  
mercy  showing  You tell  if  are soothed  ears

(6) Having heard of this wonder, I am filled with longing. If You kindly tell me, my ears will be soothed.”

প্রভু কহে, — আমি বাতুল, আমার বচনে ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম বাতুলতা সত্য করি’ মানে ॥ ৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — āmi vātula, āmāra vacane  
the Lord  says  I  am a madman  in My  words  
sārvabhauma vātulatā satya kari’ māne  
Sārvabhauma  madness  as truth  taking  accepts

(7) The Lord said, “I am a madman, and Sārvabhauma takes the madness in My words to be the truth.

কিবা প্রলাপিলাঙ, তার নাহি কিছু মনে ৷  
তোমার সঙ্গ-বলে যদি কিছু হয় মনে ॥ ৮ ॥

kibā pralāpilāṅa, tāra nāhi kichu mane  
what  I babbled  of that  there is not  anything  in My memory  
tomāra saṅga-bale yadi kichu haya mane  
your  by the strength of the company  if  something  comes  to mind

(8) I do not remember anything of what I babbled then. Yet by the strength of your company something may come to My mind.

সহজে আমার কিছু অর্থ নাহি ভাসে ৷  
তোমা-সবার সঙ্গ-বলে যে কিছু প্রকাশে ॥ ৯ ॥

sahaje āmāra kichu artha nāhi bhāse  
of itself  to Me  any  meaning  does not  appear  
tomā-sabāra saṅga-bale ye kichu prakāśe  
of you all  by the strength of the company  whatever  is revealed

(9) No meaning comes to Me of itself; whatever is revealed comes by the strength of the company of all of you.”[^4]

একাদশ পদ এই শ্লোকে সুনির্ম্মল ৷  
পৃথক্ পৃথক্ নানা অর্থ পদে করে ঝলমল ॥ ১০ ॥

ekādaśa pada ei śloke sunirmala  
eleven  words  in this  verse  perfectly pure  
pṛthak pṛthak nānā artha pade kare jhalamala  
separately  separately  various  meanings  in the words  make  glitter

(10) This verse has eleven perfectly pure words, and in each of them, taken separately, many different meanings shine brightly.

‘আত্মা’-শব্দে ব্রহ্ম, দেহ, মন, যত্ন, ধৃতি ৷  
বুদ্ধি, স্বভাব, — এই সাত অর্থ-প্রাপ্তি ॥ ১১ ॥

‘ātmā’-śabde brahma, deha, mana, yatna, dhṛti  
by the word ātmā  Brahman  body  mind  effort  steadfastness  
buddhi, svabhāva, — ei sāta artha-prāpti  
intelligence  nature  these  seven  meanings  are found

(11) The word *ātmā* has seven meanings: Brahman, the body, the mind, effort (*yatna*), steadfastness (*dhṛti*), intelligence (*buddhi*) and nature (*svabhāva*).

আত্মা দেহমনোব্রহ্মস্বভাবধৃতিবুদ্ধিষু প্রযত্নে চ ॥ ১২ ॥

ātmā deha-mano-brahma-svabhāva-  
ātmā  body  mind  Brahman  nature  
dhṛti-buddhiṣu, prayatne ca  
steadfastness  intelligence (in the senses of)  effort  and

(12) “The word *ātmā* is used for the body, the mind, Brahman, nature, steadfastness and intelligence, and also for effort.” (Viśva-prakāśa)

এই সাতে রমে যেই, সেই আত্মারামগণ ৷  
আত্মারামগণের আগে করিয়ে গণন ॥ ১৩ ॥

ei sāte rame yei, sei ātmārāma-gaṇa  
in these  seven  delights  who  they  are the ātmārāmas  
ātmārāma-gaṇera āge kariye gaṇana  
of the ātmārāmas  later  I shall make  the enumeration

(13) Those who delight in these seven are *ātmārāmas*. I shall enumerate the kinds of *ātmārāmas* later.

‘মুনি’-আদি শব্দের অর্থ শুন, সনাতন ৷  
পৃথক্ পৃথক্ অর্থ করি, পাছে করিব মিলন ॥ ১৪ ॥

‘muni’-ādi śabdera artha śuna, sanātana  
of muni  and the other  words  the meanings  hear  Sanātana  
pṛthak pṛthak artha kari, pāche kariba milana  
separately  separately  meanings  I shall give  afterwards  I shall make  the combination

(14) Hear, Sanātana, the meanings of the word *muni* and the others. I shall first give the meaning of each separately and afterwards combine them.

‘মুনি’ শব্দে মননশীল, আর কহে মৌনী ৷  
তপস্বী, ব্রতী, যতি, আর ঋষি, মুনি ॥ ১৫ ॥

‘muni’ śabde manana-śīla, āra kahe maunī  
by the word muni  given to contemplation  also  it means  one who is silent  
tapasvī, vratī, yati, āra ṛṣi, muni  
an ascetic  one who keeps vows  a renunciant  and  a seer  a sage

(15) The word *muni* means one given to contemplation (*manana-śīla*). It also means one who is silent, an ascetic, one who keeps vows, a renunciant (*yati*), a seer (*ṛṣi*) and a sage (*muni*).

‘নির্গ্রন্থ’-শব্দে কহে, অবিদ্যা-গ্রন্থি-হীন ৷  
বিধি-নিষেধ-বেদশাস্ত্র-জ্ঞানাদি-বিহীন ॥ ১৬ ॥

‘nirgrantha’-śabde kahe, avidyā-granthi-hīna  
by the word nirgrantha  is meant  of ignorance  the knot  free from  
vidhi-niṣedha-veda-śāstra-jñānādi-vihīna  
of injunctions  of prohibitions  of the Vedic scriptures  knowledge and so on  devoid of

(16) The word *nirgrantha* means one free from the knot of ignorance, and also one devoid of knowledge of the Vedic scriptures with their injunctions and prohibitions.

মূর্খ, নীচ, ম্লেচ্ছ আদি শাস্ত্ররিক্তগণ ৷  
ধনসঞ্চয়ী — নির্গ্রন্থ আর যে নির্ধন ॥ ১৭ ॥

mūrkha, nīca, mleccha ādi śāstra-rikta-gaṇa  
fools  the low  mlecchas  and others  of scripture  devoid  people  
dhana-sañcayī — nirgrantha āra ye nirdhana  
of wealth  a hoarder  nirgrantha  and  who  is without wealth

(17) Fools, the low-born, *mlecchas* and others who are devoid of scripture; one who hoards wealth; and one who has no wealth — all these are also called *nirgrantha*.

নির্নিশ্চয়ে নিষ্ক্রমার্থে নির্নির্ম্মাণ-নিষেধয়োঃ ৷  
গ্রন্থো ধনেঽথ সন্দর্ভে বর্ণসংগ্রথনেঽপি চ ॥ ১৮ ॥

nir niścaye niṣ kramārthe  
nir  in the sense of certainty  niṣ  in the sense of sequence  
nir nirmāṇa-niṣedhayoḥ  
nir  of construction  of prohibition (in the senses)  
grantho dhane ‘tha sandarbhe  
grantha  in the sense of wealth  then  of a literary work  
varṇa-saṅgrathane ‘pi ca  
of the stringing of letters into words  also  and

(18) “The prefix *nir* is used in the senses of certainty, sequence, construction and prohibition. The word *grantha* means wealth, a literary work, and also the stringing of letters into words.” (Viśva-prakāśa)

‘উরুক্রম’-শব্দে কহে, বড় যাঁর ক্রম ৷  
‘ক্রম’-শব্দে কহে, এই পাদবিক্ষেপণ ॥ ১৯ ॥

‘urukrama’-śabde kahe, baḍa yām̐ra krama  
by the word urukrama  is meant  great  whose  step  
‘krama’-śabde kahe, ei pāda-vikṣepaṇa  
by the word krama  is meant  this  the placing of the foot

(19) The word *urukrama* means He whose step (*krama*) is great. The word *krama* means a step, the placing forward of the foot.

শক্তি কম্পযুক্ত পরিপাট্যে আক্রমণ ৷  
চরণ চালনে কাঁপাইল ত্রিভুবন ॥ ২০ ॥

śakti kampa-yukta paripāṭye ākramaṇa  
power  with trembling  orderly arrangement  stepping forth  
caraṇa cālane kām̐pāila tribhuvana  
of the feet  by the movement  He made tremble  the three worlds

(20) *Krama* also means power, trembling, orderly arrangement and stepping forth (*ākramaṇa*). By the movement of His feet He made the three worlds tremble.

বিষ্ণোর্নু বীর্য্যগণনাং কতমোঽর্হতীহ  
যঃ পার্থিবান্যপি কবির্বিমমে রজাংসি ৷  
চস্কম্ভ যঃ স্বরংহসাস্খলতা ত্রিপৃষ্ঠং  
যস্মাত্রিসাম্যসদনাদুরুকম্পয়ানম্ ॥ ২১ ॥

viṣṇor nu vīrya-gaṇanāṁ katamo ‘rhatīha  
of Viṣṇu  indeed  of the powers  the counting  who  is able  here  
yaḥ pārthivāny api kavir vimame rajāṁsi  
who  earthly  even  a learned man  has counted  the atoms  
caskambha yaḥ sva-raṁhasāskhalatā tri-pṛṣṭhaṁ  
held fast  who  by His own  speed  unchecked  Satyaloka  
yasmāt tri-sāmya-sadanād uru kampayānam  
from which  of the three (guṇas)  of the equilibrium  from the abode  greatly  shaking

(21) “Who in this world could count the mighty deeds of Viṣṇu — even a sage who has counted the atoms of the earth? His unchecked stride shook the universe from *pradhāna*, where the three *guṇas* rest in balance, up to Satyaloka, and it was He who held it fast.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.7.40)[^5]

বিভুরূপে ব্যাপে, শক্ত্যে ধারণ-পোষণ ৷  
মাধুর্য্যশক্ত্যে গোলোক, ঐশ্বর্য্যে পরব্যোম ॥ ২২ ॥

vibhu-rūpe vyāpe, śaktye dhāraṇa-poṣaṇa  
in His all-pervading form  He pervades  by His potency  sustaining  nourishing  
mādhurya-śaktye goloka, aiśvarye paravyoma  
by the potency of sweetness  Goloka  by majesty  Paravyoma

(22) In His all-pervading form He pervades everything, and by His potency He sustains and nourishes it. By His potency of sweetness (*mādhurya-śakti*) He sustains Goloka, and by His majesty (*aiśvarya*) the spiritual sky, Paravyoma.

মায়া-শক্ত্যে ব্রহ্মাণ্ডাদি পরিপাটী-সৃজন ৷  
‘উরুক্রম’-শব্দের এই অর্থ নিরূপণ ॥ ২৩ ॥

māyā-śaktye brahmāṇḍādi paripāṭī-sṛjana  
by the potency of māyā  the universes and so on  orderly  creation  
‘urukrama’-śabdera ei artha nirūpaṇa  
of the word urukrama  this  of the meaning  the determination

(23) By His potency of māyā He creates the universes and all else in orderly succession. This is the meaning of the word *urukrama*.

ক্রমঃ শক্তৌ পরিপাট্যাং ক্রমশ্চালনকম্পয়োঃ ॥ ২৪ ॥

kramaḥ śaktau paripāṭyāṁ  
krama  in the sense of power  of orderly arrangement  
kramaś cālana-kampayoḥ  
krama  of movement  of trembling (in the senses)

(24) “*Krama* means power and orderly arrangement; *krama* also means movement and trembling.” (Viśva-prakāśa)

‘কুর্ব্বন্তি’-পদ এই পরস্মৈপদ হয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণসুখনিমিত্ত ভজনে তাৎপর্য্য কহয় ॥ ২৫ ॥

‘kurvanti’-pada ei parasmaipada haya  
kurvanti  the word  this  in the parasmaipada  is  
kṛṣṇa-sukha-nimitta bhajane tātparya kahaya  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  for the sake of  in worship  the purport  it expresses

(25) The word *kurvanti* is in the *parasmaipada*, the voice used when an action is done for another. It shows that the worship is meant for Kṛṣṇa’s happiness.

স্বরিতঞিতঃ কর্ত্রভিপ্রায়ে ক্রিয়াফলে ॥ ২৬ ॥

svarita-ñitaḥ kartr-  
svarita-accented  marked with ñ (roots)  of the agent  
abhiprāye kriyā-phale  
in the intention  of the action  the fruit (when it is)

(26) “Roots marked with the *svarita* accent or with the indicatory letter *ñ* take the *ātmanepada* endings when the fruit of the action is meant for the agent himself.” (Pāṇini, 1.3.72)

‘হেতু’-শব্দে কহে — ভুক্তি-আদি বাঞ্ছান্তরে ৷  
ভুক্তি, সিদ্ধি, মুক্তি, — মুখ্য এই তিন প্রকারে ॥ ২৭ ॥

‘hetu’-śabde kahe — bhukti-ādi vāñchāntare  
by the word hetu  is meant  enjoyment and so on  other desires  
bhukti, siddhi, mukti, — mukhya ei tina prakāre  
enjoyment  perfection  liberation  chief  these  three  kinds

(27) The word *hetu* (motive) means other desires, beginning with the desire for enjoyment. They are chiefly of three kinds: enjoyment (*bhukti*), mystic perfection (*siddhi*) and liberation (*mukti*).

এক ভুক্তি কহে, ভোগ — অনন্ত প্রকার ৷  
সিদ্ধি — অষ্টাদশ, মুক্তি — পঞ্চবিধাকার ॥ ২৮ ॥

eka bhukti kahe, bhoga — ananta prakāra  
one  bhukti  is called  enjoyment  of endless  kinds  
siddhi — aṣṭādaśa, mukti — pañca-vidhākāra  
siddhis  eighteen  mukti  of five  kinds

(28) *Bhukti*, enjoyment, is of endless kinds; the *siddhis* are eighteen; and *mukti* is of five kinds.

এই যাঁহা নাহি, সেই ভক্তি — ‘অহৈতুকী’ ৷  
যাহা হৈতে বশ হয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণ কৌতুকী ॥ ২৯ ॥

ei yām̐hā nāhi, sei bhakti — ‘ahaitukī’  
these  where  are not  that  bhakti  causeless  
yāhā haite vaśa haya śrī-kṛṣṇa kautukī  
by which  subdued  becomes  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  the playful

(29) Devotion in which these are absent is *ahaitukī*, causeless. By it the playful Śrī Kṛṣṇa comes under control.

‘ভক্তি’-শব্দের অর্থ হয় দশবিধাকার ৷  
এক — ‘সাধন’, ‘প্রেমভক্তি’ — নব প্রকার ॥ ৩০ ॥

‘bhakti’-śabdera artha haya daśa-vidhākāra  
of the word bhakti  the meaning  is  of ten kinds  
eka — ‘sādhana’, ‘prema-bhakti’ — nava prakāra  
one  sādhana  prema-bhakti  nine  kinds

(30) The word *bhakti* has ten meanings: one is *sādhana-bhakti*, and *prema-bhakti* is of nine kinds.

‘রতি’-লক্ষণা, ‘প্রেম’-লক্ষণা, ইত্যাদি প্রচার ৷  
ভাবরূপা, মহাভাব-লক্ষণরূপা আর ॥ ৩১ ॥

‘rati’-lakṣaṇā, ‘prema’-lakṣaṇā, ityādi pracāra  
rati  marked by  prema  marked by  and so on  is proclaimed  
bhāva-rūpā, mahābhāva-lakṣaṇa-rūpā āra  
in the form of bhāva  of mahābhāva  by the marks  in the form of  and

(31) It is proclaimed as marked by *rati*, marked by prema and so on, as having the form of *bhāva*, and as marked by *mahābhāva*.

শান্ত-ভক্তের রতি বাড়ে ‘প্রেম’-পর্য্যন্ত ৷  
দাস্য-ভক্তের রতি হয় ‘রাগ’দশা-অন্ত ॥ ৩২ ॥

śānta-bhaktera rati bāḍe ‘prema’-paryanta  
of the śānta devotee  rati  increases  prema  up to  
dāsya-bhaktera rati haya ‘rāga’-daśā-anta  
of the dāsya devotee  rati  is  of rāga  the stage  up to the end

(32) The *rati* of devotees in *śānta-rasa* grows up to prema; the *rati* of devotees in *dāsya-rasa* reaches the stage of *rāga*.

সখাগণের রতি হয় ‘অনুরাগ’ পর্য্যন্ত ৷  
পিতৃ-মাতৃ-স্নেহ আদি ‘অনুরাগ’-অন্ত ॥ ৩৩ ॥

sakhā-gaṇera rati haya ‘anurāga’ paryanta  
of the friends  rati  is  anurāga  up to  
pitṛ-mātṛ-sneha ādi ‘anurāga’-anta  
of father  of mother  affection  and so on  anurāga  up to the end

(33) The *rati* of His friends reaches up to *anurāga*, and the affection of His father, mother and other elders likewise reaches *anurāga*.

কান্তাগণের রতি পায় ‘মহাভাব’-সীমা ৷  
‘ভক্তি’-শব্দে কহিলুঁ এই অর্থের মহিমা ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kāntā-gaṇera rati pāya ‘mahābhāva’-sīmā  
of the beloveds  rati  reaches  mahābhāva  the limit  
‘bhakti’-śabde kahilum̐ ei arthera mahimā  
by the word bhakti  I have told  of this  meaning  the glory

(34) The *rati* of His beloveds reaches the limit of *mahābhāva*. Thus I have told the glory of the meaning of the word *bhakti*.

‘ইত্থম্ভূতগুণঃ’-শব্দের শুনহ ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
‘ইত্থম্ভূত’-শব্দের ভিন্ন অর্থ, ‘গুণ’-শব্দের আন ॥ ৩৫ ॥

‘ittham-bhūta-guṇaḥ’-śabdera śunaha vyākhyāna  
of the word ittham-bhūta-guṇaḥ  hear  the explanation  
‘ittham-bhūta’-śabdera bhinna artha, ‘guṇa’-śabdera āna  
of the word ittham-bhūta  separate  meaning  of the word guṇa  another

(35) Now hear the explanation of the words *ittham-bhūta-guṇaḥ*. The word *ittham-bhūta* has its own meaning, and the word *guṇa* another.

‘ইত্থম্ভূত’-শব্দের অর্থ — পূর্ণানন্দময় ৷  
যাঁর আগে ব্রহ্মানন্দ তৃণপ্রায় হয় ॥ ৩৬ ॥

‘ittham-bhūta’-śabdera artha — pūrṇānandamaya  
of the word ittham-bhūta  the meaning  full of perfect bliss  
yām̐ra āge brahmānanda tṛṇa-prāya haya  
whom  before  the bliss of Brahman  like a straw  becomes

(36) The word *ittham-bhūta* means He who is full of perfect bliss, before whom the bliss of Brahman is like a blade of grass.

ত্বৎসাক্ষাৎকরণাহ্লাদবিশুদ্ধাব্ধিস্থিতস্য মে ৷  
সুখানি গোষ্পদায়ন্তে ব্রাহ্মাণ্যপি জগদ্গুরো ॥ ৩৭ ॥

tvat-sākṣāt-karaṇāhlāda-  
from seeing You face to face  the joy  
viśuddhābdhi-sthitasya me  
in the pure ocean  situated  for me  
sukhāni goṣ-padāyante  
joys  become like a calf’s hoofprint  
brāhmāṇy api jagad-guro  
of Brahman  even  O Guru of the world

(37) “O Guru of the world, now that I am immersed in the pure ocean of joy that comes from seeing You face to face, all other joys — even the bliss of Brahman — seem to me like the water in a calf’s hoofprint.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 14.36)[^6]

সর্ব্বাকর্ষক, সর্ব্বাহ্লাদক, মহারসায়ন ৷  
আপনার বলে করে সর্ব্ব-বিস্মারণ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

sarvākarṣaka, sarvāhlādaka, mahā-rasāyana  
attracting all  delighting all  the great elixir  
āpanāra bale kare sarva-vismāraṇa  
by His own  power  He brings about  of everything  forgetting

(38) He attracts all, delights all and is the great elixir of rasa. By His own power He makes one forget everything else.

ভুক্তি-মুক্তি-সিদ্ধি-সুখ ছাড়য় যার গন্ধে ৷  
অলৌকিক শক্তি-গুণে কৃষ্ণকৃপায় বান্ধে ॥ ৩৯ ॥

bhukti-mukti-siddhi-sukha chāḍaya yāra gandhe  
of enjoyment  of liberation  of perfection  the happiness  makes one give up  whose  by the scent  
alaukika śakti-guṇe kṛṣṇa-kṛpāya bāndhe  
extraordinary  by the power  of the qualities  by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  He binds

(39) The merest scent of Him makes one give up the happiness of enjoyment, liberation and mystic perfection. By the extraordinary power of His qualities He binds the heart through His mercy.

শাস্ত্রযুক্তি নাহি ইঁহা সিদ্ধান্ত-বিচার ৷  
এই স্বভাব-গুণে, যাতে মাধুর্য্যের সার ॥ ৪০ ॥

śāstra-yukti nāhi im̐hā siddhānta-vicāra  
of scripture  reasoning  there is not  here  of conclusions  deliberation  
ei svabhāva-guṇe, yāte mādhuryera sāra  
this  by the quality of His nature  in which  of sweetness  the essence

(40) Here there is no scriptural reasoning or weighing of conclusions. This happens by the very nature of His qualities, in which lies the essence of sweetness.

‘গুণ’ শব্দের অর্থ — কৃষ্ণের গুণ অনন্ত ৷  
সচ্চিদ্রূপে-গুণে সর্ব্ব পূর্ণানন্দ ॥ ৪১ ॥

‘guṇa’ śabdera artha — kṛṣṇera guṇa ananta  
of the word guṇa  the meaning  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  endless  
sac-cid-rūpe-guṇe sarva pūrṇānanda  
in His form of eternity and consciousness  in His qualities  all  perfect bliss

(41) The word *guṇa* means Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, which are endless. In His form, eternal and conscious, and in His qualities, everything is perfect bliss.

ঐশ্বর্য্য-মাধুর্য্য-কারুণ্যে স্বরূপ-পূর্ণতা ৷  
ভক্তবাৎসল্যে আত্মা-পর্য্যন্ত বদান্যতা ॥ ৪২ ॥

aiśvarya-mādhurya-kāruṇye svarūpa-pūrṇatā  
in majesty  sweetness  compassion  of His own nature  the fullness  
bhakta-vātsalye ātmā-paryanta vadānyatā  
in His affection for His devotees  Himself  up to  generosity

(42) His own nature is full of majesty, sweetness and compassion. In His affection for His devotees His generosity goes so far that He gives even Himself.

অলৌকিক রূপ, রস, সৌরভাদি গুণ ৷  
কারো মন কোন গুণে করে আকর্ষণ ॥ ৪৩ ॥

alaukika rūpa, rasa, saurabhādi guṇa  
extraordinary  form  taste  fragrance and other  qualities  
kāro mana kona guṇe kare ākarṣaṇa  
someone’s  mind  by some  quality  makes  attraction

(43) He has extraordinary qualities — His form, His rasa, His fragrance and others — and by one quality or another He attracts each one’s mind.

সনকাদির মন হরিল সৌরভাদি গুণে ৷  
শুকদেবের মন হরিল লীলা-শ্রবণে ॥ ৪৪ ॥

sanakādira mana harila saurabhādi guṇe  
of Sanaka and the others  the mind  He stole  by fragrance and other  qualities  
śukadevera mana harila līlā-śravaṇe  
of Śukadeva  the mind  He stole  by the hearing of His pastimes

(44) He stole the minds of Sanaka and the other Kumāras by His fragrance and other qualities, and He stole Śukadeva’s mind through the hearing of His pastimes.

তস্যারবিন্দনয়নস্য পদারবিন্দ-  
কিঞ্জল্কমিশ্রতুলসীমকরন্দবায়ুঃ ৷  
অন্তর্গতঃ স্ববিবরেণ চকার তেষাং  
সংক্ষোভমক্ষরজুষামপি চিত্ততন্বোঃ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

tasyāravinda-nayanasya padāravinda-  
of Him  the lotus-eyed  of the lotus feet  
kiñjalka-miśra-tulasī-makaranda-vāyuḥ  
with the filaments  mixed  of tulasī  of the nectar  the breeze  
antar-gataḥ sva-vivareṇa cakāra teṣāṁ  
having entered within  through its own passage  caused  in them  
saṅkṣobham akṣara-juṣām api citta-tanvoḥ  
agitation  devoted to the imperishable Brahman  even  of mind and body

(45) “The breeze that bore the fragrance of tulasī, mingled with the pollen of the lotus-eyed Lord’s lotus feet, entered their nostrils and stirred both their minds and their bodies, though they were devoted to the imperishable Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.15.43)[^7]

পরিনিষ্ঠিতোঽপি নৈর্গুণ্যে উত্তমঃশ্লোকলীলয়া ৷  
গৃহীতচেতা রাজর্ষে আখ্যানং যদধীতবান্ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

pariniṣṭhito ‘pi nairguṇye  
firmly established  though  in transcendence of the guṇas  
uttamaḥ-śloka-līlayā  
of Uttamaśloka  by the pastimes  
gṛhīta-cetā rājarṣe  
captivated  in mind  O royal sage  
ākhyānaṁ yad adhītavān  
the narration  which  I studied

(46) “O royal sage, although I was firmly established in transcendence of the *guṇas*, my mind was captivated by the pastimes of Uttamaśloka, and so I studied this narration.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.1.9)

শ্রীঅঙ্গ-রূপে হরে গোপিকার মন ৷  
রূপ-গুণ শ্রবণে রুক্মিণ্যাদির আকর্ষণ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

śrī-aṅga-rūpe hare gopikāra mana  
of His divine body  by the beauty  He steals  of the gopīs  the minds  
rūpa-guṇa-śravaṇe rukmiṇy-ādira ākarṣaṇa  
of form  of qualities  by hearing  of Rukmiṇī and the others  the attraction

(47) By the beauty of His divine body He steals the minds of the gopīs; Rukmiṇī and the other queens are attracted by hearing of His beauty and qualities.

বীক্ষ্যালকাবৃতমুখং তব কুণ্ডলশ্রী-  
গণ্ডস্থলাধরসুধং হসিতাবলোকম্ ৷  
দত্তাভয়ঞ্চ ভুজদণ্ডযুগং বিলোক্য  
বক্ষঃ শ্রিয়ৈকরমণঞ্চ ভবাম দাস্যঃ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

vīkṣyālakāvṛta-mukhaṁ tava kuṇḍala-śrī-  
having seen  by locks  framed  face  Your  by the beauty of earrings  
gaṇḍa-sthalādhara-sudhaṁ hasitāvalokam  
of the cheeks  of the lips  the nectar  smiling  glance  
dattābhayaṁ ca bhuja-daṇḍa-yugaṁ vilokya  
granting fearlessness  and  of arms  the pair  having beheld  
vakṣaḥ śriyaika-ramaṇaṁ ca bhavāma dāsyaḥ  
the chest  for Śrī  the sole delight  and  we become  maidservants

(48) “Seeing Your face framed by curling locks and graced by the beauty of Your earrings, Your cheeks, the nectar of Your lips and Your smiling glance, and beholding Your two arms that grant fearlessness and Your chest, the sole delight of Śrī, we have become Your maidservants.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.39)

শ্রুত্বা গুণান্ ভুবনসুন্দর শৃণ্বতাং তে  
নির্ব্বিশ্য কর্ণবিবরৈর্হরতোঽঙ্গতাপম্ ৷  
রূপং দৃশাং দৃশিমতামখিলার্থলাভং  
ত্বয্যচ্যুতাবিশতি চিত্তমপত্রপং মে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

śrutvā guṇān bhuvana-sundara śṛṇvatāṁ te  
having heard  qualities  O beauty of the worlds  of those who hear  Your  
nirviśya karṇa-vivarair harato ‘ṅga-tāpam  
having entered  of the ears  through the openings  removing  of the body  the distress  
rūpaṁ dṛśāṁ dṛśimatām akhilārtha-lābhaṁ  
form  for the eyes  of those who see  of all goals  the attainment  
tvayy acyutāviśati cittam apatrapaṁ me  
into You  O Acyuta  enters  heart  shameless  my

(49) “O beauty of the worlds, Your qualities enter the ears of those who hear of them and remove the distress of their bodies, and Your form is, for the eyes of those who see, the attainment of every goal. Having heard of them, O Acyuta, my heart, casting off shame, enters into You.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.52.37)

বংশী-গীতে হরে কৃষ্ণ লক্ষ্ম্যাদির মন ৷  
যোগ্যভাবে জগতের যত যুবতীর গণ ॥ ৫০ ॥

vaṁśī-gīte hare kṛṣṇa lakṣmy-ādira mana  
of the flute  by the song  steals  Kṛṣṇa  of Lakṣmī and the others  the minds  
yogya-bhāve jagatera yata yuvatīra gaṇa  
in a fitting  mood  of the world  all  of young women  the host

(50) By the song of His flute Kṛṣṇa steals the minds of Lakṣmī and others, and of all the young women of the world, each in a fitting mood.

কস্যানুভাবোঽস্য ন দেব বিদ্মহে  
তবাঙ্ঘ্রিরেণুস্পর্শাধিকারঃ ৷  
যদ্বাঞ্ছয়া শ্রীর্ললনাচরত্তপো  
বিহায় কামান্ সুচিরং ধৃতব্রতা ॥ ৫১ ॥

kasyānubhāvo ‘sya na deva vidmahe  
of what  merit  his  not  O Lord  we know  
tavāṅghri-reṇu-sparśādhikāraḥ  
of Your  feet  of the dust  of the touch  the right  
yad-vāñchayā śrīr lalanācarat tapo  
for which  longing  Śrī  the best of women  performed  austerity  
vihāya kāmān su-ciraṁ dhṛta-vratā  
giving up  desires  for a very long time  holding vows

(51) “O Lord, we do not know by what merit this one has gained the right to touch the dust of Your feet. Longing for it, Śrī, the best of women, renounced all desires and practised austerities, keeping her vows for a very long time.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.16.36)[^8]

কাস্ত্র্যঙ্গ তে কলপদামৃতবেণুগীত-  
সম্মোহিতার্য্যচরিতান্ন চলেত্ত্রিলোক্যাম্ ৷  
ত্রৈলোক্যসৌভগমিদঞ্চ নিরীক্ষ্য রূপং  
যদ্গোদ্বিজদ্রুমমৃগাঃ পুলকান্যবিভ্রন্ ॥ ৫২ ॥

kā stry aṅga te kala-padāmṛta-veṇu-gīta-  
what  woman  O dear one  Your  of sweet words  by the nectar  of the flute  by the song  
sammohitārya-caritān na calet tri-lokyām  
enchanted  from the conduct of the noble  not  would stray  in the three worlds  
trailokya-saubhagam idaṁ ca nirīkṣya rūpaṁ  
of the three worlds  the beauty  this  and  having seen  form  
yad go-dvija-druma-mṛgāḥ pulakāny abibhran  
which  cows  birds  trees  deer  thrills of rapture  bore

(52) “O Kṛṣṇa, what woman in the three worlds, enchanted by the song of Your flute with its sweet, nectarean notes, would not stray from the conduct of the noble? Seeing this form of Yours, the beauty of the three worlds, even cows, birds, trees and deer bristle with rapture.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.40)

গুরুতুল্য স্ত্রীগণের বাৎসল্যে আকর্ষণ ৷  
দাস্য-সখ্যাদি-ভাবে পুরুষাদি গণ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

guru-tulya strī-gaṇera vātsalye ākarṣaṇa  
like elders  of the women  in parental love  attraction  
dāsya-sakhyādi-bhāve puruṣādi gaṇa  
in the moods of servitude  friendship and so on  men and others  the host

(53) Women who are like elders He attracts in parental love (*vātsalya*), and men in the moods of servitude, friendship and others.

পক্ষী, মৃগ, বৃক্ষ, লতা, চেতনাচেতন ৷  
প্রেমে মত্ত করি’ আকর্ষয়ে কৃষ্ণগুণ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

pakṣī, mṛga, vṛkṣa, latā, cetanācetana  
birds  beasts  trees  creepers  moving  unmoving  
preme matta kari’ ākarṣaye kṛṣṇa-guṇa  
with love  mad  making  attract  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities

(54) Kṛṣṇa’s qualities attract birds, beasts, trees and creepers — all that moves and all that does not — and make them mad with love (prema).

‘হরিঃ’-শব্দে নানার্থ, দুই — মুখ্যতম ৷  
সর্ব্ব অমঙ্গল হরে, প্রেম দিয়া হরে মন ॥ ৫৫ ॥

‘hariḥ’-śabde nānārtha, dui — mukhyatama  
by the word hariḥ  many meanings  two  foremost  
sarva amaṅgala hare, prema diyā hare mana  
all  inauspiciousness  He takes away  love  giving  He steals  the mind

(55) The word *hariḥ* has many meanings, two of which are foremost: He takes away all that is inauspicious, and, bestowing love (prema), He steals the mind.

যৈছে তৈছে যোহি কোহি করয়ে স্মরণ ৷  
চারিবিধ তাপ তার করে সংহরণ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

yaiche taiche yohi kohi karaye smaraṇa  
somehow  or other  whoever  anyone  does  remembering  
cāri-vidha tāpa tāra kare saṁharaṇa  
of four kinds  distress  his  He makes  removal

(56) He frees from the four kinds of distress anyone who remembers Him, in whatever way.

যথাগ্নিঃ সুসমৃদ্ধার্চ্চিঃ করোত্যেধাংসি ভস্মসাৎ ৷  
তথা মদ্বিষয়া ভক্তিরুদ্ধবৈনাংসি কৃৎস্নশঃ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

yathāgniḥ su-samṛddhārciḥ  
as  fire  brightly blazing  
karoty edhāṁsi bhasma-sāt  
makes  firewood  into ashes  
tathā mad-viṣayā bhaktir  
so  toward Me  directed  devotion  
uddhavaināṁsi kṛtsnaśaḥ  
O Uddhava  sins  entirely

(57) “As a blazing fire turns firewood to ashes, so, O Uddhava, devotion to Me burns up all sins.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.19)

তবে করে ভক্তিবাধক কর্ম্ম, অবিদ্যা নাশ ৷  
শ্রবণাদ্যের ফল ‘প্রেমা’ করয়ে প্রকাশ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

tabe kare bhakti-bādhaka karma, avidyā nāśa  
then  He makes  obstructing devotion  karma  of ignorance  destruction  
śravaṇādyera phala ‘premā’ karaye prakāśa  
of hearing and so on  the fruit  prema  He makes  manifestation

(58) Then He destroys ignorance and the karma that obstructs devotion, and He manifests prema, the fruit of hearing and the other practices.

নিজ-গুণে তবে হরে দেহেন্দ্রিয়মন ৷  
ঐছে কৃপালু কৃষ্ণ, ঐছে তাঁর গুণ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

nija-guṇe tabe hare dehendriya-mana  
by His own  qualities  then  He steals  body  senses  mind  
aiche kṛpālu kṛṣṇa, aiche tām̐ra guṇa  
so  merciful  Kṛṣṇa  such  His  qualities

(59) After that, by His own qualities He steals the body, the senses and the mind. So merciful is Kṛṣṇa, and such are His qualities.

চারি পুরুষার্থ ছাড়ায়, হরে সবার মন ৷  
‘হরি’-শব্দের এই মুখ্য কহিলুঁ লক্ষণ ॥ ৬০ ॥

cāri puruṣārtha chāḍāya, hare sabāra mana  
four  goals of life  makes one give up  He steals  everyone’s  mind  
‘hari’-śabdera ei mukhya kahilum̐ lakṣaṇa  
of the word hari  this  chief  I have told  characteristic

(60) He makes one give up the four goals of human life and steals everyone’s mind. Thus I have told the chief meaning of the word *hari*.

‘চ’, ‘অপি’ — দুই শব্দ তাতে ‘অব্যয়’ হয় ৷  
যেই অর্থ লাগাইয়ে, সেই অর্থ হয় ॥ ৬১ ॥

‘ca’, ‘api’ — dui śabda tāte ‘avyaya’ haya  
ca  api  two  words  among them  indeclinables  are  
yei artha lāgāiye, sei artha haya  
whatever  meaning  one applies  that  meaning  it becomes

(61) The two words *ca* and *api* are indeclinables (*avyaya*): whatever meaning one applies to them, that meaning they take.

তথাপি চ-কারের কহে মুখ্য অর্থ সাত ৷  
অপি-শব্দে মুখ্য অর্থ সাত বিখ্যাত ॥ ৬২ ॥

tathāpi ca-kārera kahe mukhya artha sāta  
still  of the word ca  they give  chief  meanings  seven  
api-śabde mukhya artha sāta vikhyāta  
of the word api  chief  meanings  seven  well known

(62) Still, seven chief meanings of *ca* are given, and the word *api* has seven well-known chief meanings.

চান্বাচয়ে সমাহারেঽন্যোঽন্যার্থে চ সমুচ্চয়ে ৷  
যত্নান্তরে তথা পাদপূরণেঽপ্যবধারণে ॥ ৬৩ ॥

cānvācaye samāhāre  
ca  in the sense of adding a secondary item  of combining into a whole  
‘nyo ‘nyārthe ca samuccaye  
of reciprocity  in the sense  and  of conjunction  
yatnāntare tathā pāda-  
of another effort  likewise  of the foot of a verse  
pūraṇe ‘py avadhāraṇe  
of filling  also  of certainty

(63) “*Ca* is used in the senses of *anvācaya* (adding a secondary item), *samāhāra* (combining into a whole), *anyonya* (reciprocity), *samuccaya* (conjunction), *yatnāntara* (another effort), *pāda-pūraṇa* (filling out the foot of a verse) and *avadhāraṇa* (certainty).” (Viśva-prakāśa)[^9]

অপি সম্ভাবনা-প্রশ্ন-শঙ্কা-গর্হা-সমুচ্চয়ে ৷  
তথা যুক্তপদার্থেষু কামচারক্রিয়াসু চ ॥ ৬৪ ॥

api sambhāvanā-praśna-  
api  of possibility  of question  
śaṅkā-garhā-samuccaye  
of doubt  of censure  of conjunction (in the senses)  
tathā yukta-padārtheṣu  
likewise  in fitting application  
kāma-cāra-kriyāsu ca  
in actions at will  and

(64) “*Api* is used in the senses of *sambhāvanā* (possibility), *praśna* (question), *śaṅkā* (doubt), *garhā* (censure) and *samuccaya* (conjunction), as well as for fitting application (*yukta-padārtha*) and for action at will (*kāmacāra-kriyā*).” (Viśva-prakāśa)

এই ত’ একাদশ পদের অর্থ-নির্ণয় ৷  
এবে শ্লোকার্থ করি, যথা যে লাগয় ॥ ৬৫ ॥

ei ta’ ekādaśa padera artha-nirṇaya  
this  indeed  of the eleven  words  of the meanings  the determination  
ebe ślokārtha kari, yathā ye lāgaya  
now  the meaning of the verse  I shall give  as  whatever  fits

(65) Thus I have determined the meanings of the eleven words. Now I shall give the meaning of the verse, applying each meaning as it fits.

‘ব্রহ্ম’-শব্দের অর্থ — তত্ত্ব সর্ব্ব-বৃহত্তম ৷  
স্বরূপ-ঐশ্বর্য্য করি’ নাহি যাঁর সম ॥ ৬৬ ॥

‘brahma’-śabdera artha — tattva sarva-bṛhattama  
of the word brahma  the meaning  the truth  of all  the greatest  
svarūpa-aiśvarya kari’ nāhi yām̐ra sama  
in nature  in majesty  by  there is no  whose  equal

(66) The word *brahma* means the Truth that is greater than all; in His own nature and majesty no one is His equal.

বৃহত্ত্বাদ্বৃংহণত্বাচ্চ তদ্ব্রহ্ম পরমং বিদুঃ ৷  
তস্মৈ নমস্তে সর্ব্বাত্মন্ যোগিচিন্ত্যাবিকারবৎ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

bṛhattvād bṛṁhaṇatvāc ca  
because of greatness  because of the power to nourish  and  
tad brahma paramaṁ viduḥ  
that  as Brahman  supreme  they know  
tasmai namas te sarvātman  
to Him  obeisance  to You  O Soul of all  
yogi-cintyāvikāravat  
by the yogīs  meditated upon  unchanging

(67) “Because of His greatness (*bṛhattva*) and His power to nourish and expand all (*bṛṁhaṇatva*), the wise know Him as the Supreme Brahman. O Soul of all, unchanging One on whom the yogīs meditate, obeisance unto You.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 1.12.57)

আততত্বাচ্চ মাতৃত্বাদাত্মা হি পরমো হরিঃ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

ātatatvāc ca mātṛtvād  
because of pervading all  and  because of being the progenitor of all  
ātmā hi paramo hariḥ  
Ātmā  indeed  supreme  Hari

(68) “Because He pervades all (*ātatatva*) and is the progenitor of all (*mātṛtva*), Hari is the Supreme Ātmā.” (words of a Tantra quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī in his commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.45)[^10]

সেই ব্রহ্ম-শব্দে কহে স্বয়ংভগবান্ ৷  
অদ্বিতীয়-জ্ঞান, যাঁহা বিনা নাহি আন ॥ ৬৯ ॥

sei brahma-śabde kahe svayaṁbhagavān  
by that  the word brahma  is meant  Svayam Bhagavān  
advitīya-jñāna, yām̐hā vinā nāhi āna  
non-dual knowledge  whom  without  there is not  anything else

(69) That word *brahma* means Svayam Bhagavān, the Lord Himself, the non-dual Knowledge, without whom nothing else exists.

বদন্তি তত্তত্ত্ববিদস্তত্ত্বং যজ্জ্ঞানমদ্বয়ম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মেতি পরমাত্মেতি ভগবানিতি শব্দ্যতে ॥ ৭০ ॥

vadanti tat tattva-vidas  
they call  that  of truth  the knowers  
tattvaṁ yaj jñānam advayam  
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual  
brahmeti paramātmeti  
Brahman  thus  Paramātmā  thus  
bhagavān iti śabdyate  
Bhagavān  thus  is named

(70) “The knowers of the Truth call the non-dual knowledge the Truth; it is named Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.11)[^11]

সেই অদ্বয়-তত্ত্ব কৃষ্ণ — স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তিনকালে সত্য তিঁহো, শাস্ত্র-প্রমাণ ॥ ৭১ ॥

sei advaya-tattva kṛṣṇa — svayaṁ bhagavān  
that  non-dual Truth  Kṛṣṇa  Svayam  Bhagavān  
tina-kāle satya tim̐ho, śāstra-pramāṇa  
in the three times  real  He  of the scriptures  the evidence

(71) That non-dual Truth is Kṛṣṇa, Svayam Bhagavān. He is real in all three phases of time; the scriptures are the evidence.

অহমেবাসমেবাগ্রে নান্যদ্যৎ সদসৎপরম্ ৷  
পশ্চাদহং যদেতচ্চ যোঽবশিষ্যেত সোঽস্ম্যহম্ ॥ ৭২ ॥

aham evāsam evāgre  
I  alone  was  indeed  before (creation)  
nānyad yat sad-asat-param  
nothing else  which  effect  cause  beyond  
paścād ahaṁ yad etac ca  
afterwards  I  what  this (world)  also  
yo ‘vaśiṣyeta so ‘smy aham  
what  remains  that  am  I

(72) “Before creation I alone existed; there was nothing else — neither effect, nor cause, nor That which is beyond them. After creation this whole world is not other than Me, and what remains after its dissolution is also I.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.9.32)[^12]

‘আত্মা’-শব্দে কহে কৃষ্ণ বৃহত্ত্বস্বরূপ ৷  
সর্ব্বব্যাপক, সর্ব্বসাক্ষী, পরমস্বরূপ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

‘ātmā’-śabde kahe kṛṣṇa bṛhattva-svarūpa  
by the word ātmā  is meant  Kṛṣṇa  whose nature is greatness  
sarva-vyāpaka, sarva-sākṣī, parama-svarūpa  
all-pervading  witness of all  the supreme  form

(73) By the word *ātmā* is meant Kṛṣṇa, whose very nature is greatness: He pervades all, witnesses all, and is the supreme form.

আততত্বাচ্চ মাতৃত্বাদাত্মা হি পরমো হরিঃ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

ātatatvāc ca mātṛtvād  
because of pervading all  and  because of being the progenitor of all  
ātmā hi paramo hariḥ  
Ātmā  indeed  supreme  Hari

(74) “Because He pervades all (*ātatatva*) and is the progenitor of all (*mātṛtva*), Hari is the Supreme Ātmā.” (words of a Tantra quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī in his commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.45)[^13]

সেই কৃষ্ণপ্রাপ্তি-হেতু ত্রিবিধ ‘সাধন’ ৷  
জ্ঞান, যোগ, ভক্তি, — তিনের পৃথক্ লক্ষণ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa-prāpti-hetu trividha ‘sādhana’  
of that  Kṛṣṇa  attainment  for the sake of  three kinds of  sādhana  
jñāna, yoga, bhakti, — tinera pṛthak lakṣaṇa  
jñāna  yoga  bhakti  of the three  separate  characteristics

(75) For attaining that Kṛṣṇa there are three kinds of *sādhana* — jñāna, yoga and bhakti — each with its own characteristics.

তিন সাধনে ভগবান্ তিন স্বরূপে ভাসে ৷  
ব্রহ্ম, পরমাত্মা, ভগবত্তা, — ত্রিবিধ প্রকাশে ॥ ৭৬ ॥

tina sādhane bhagavān tina svarūpe bhāse  
in the three  sādhanas  Bhagavān  in three  forms  appears  
brahma, paramātmā, bhagavattā, — trividha prakāśe  
Brahman  Paramātmā  Bhagavān Himself  in three ways  is manifest

(76) Through the three *sādhanas* Bhagavān appears in three forms: He is manifest as Brahman, as Paramātmā and as Bhagavān Himself.

বদন্তি তত্তত্ত্ববিদস্তত্ত্বং যজ্জ্ঞানমদ্বয়ম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মেতি পরমাত্মেতি ভগবানিতি শব্দ্যতে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

vadanti tat tattva-vidas  
they call  that  of truth  the knowers  
tattvaṁ yaj jñānam advayam  
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual  
brahmeti paramātmeti  
Brahman  thus  Paramātmā  thus  
bhagavān iti śabdyate  
Bhagavān  thus  is named

(77) “The knowers of the Truth call the non-dual knowledge the Truth; it is named Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.11)[^14]

‘ব্রহ্ম-আত্মা’-শব্দে যদি কৃষ্ণেরে কহয় ৷  
‘রূঢ়িবৃত্ত্যে’ নির্ব্বিশেষ অন্তর্য্যামী কয় ॥ ৭৮ ॥

‘brahma-ātmā’-śabde yadi kṛṣṇere kahaya  
by the words brahma and ātmā  though  Kṛṣṇa  they denote  
‘rūḍhi-vṛttye’ nirviśeṣa antaryāmī kaya  
by conventional usage  the featureless  the indwelling Lord  they denote

(78) Though the words *brahma* and *ātmā* denote Kṛṣṇa, in their conventional usage (*rūḍhi-vṛtti*) they mean the featureless Brahman and the indwelling Lord (Antaryāmī).

জ্ঞানমার্গে — নির্ব্বিশেষ-ব্রহ্ম প্রকাশে ৷  
যোগমার্গে — অন্তর্য্যামী-স্বরূপেতে ভাসে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

jñāna-mārge — nirviśeṣa-brahma prakāśe  
on the path of knowledge  the featureless  Brahman  is manifest  
yoga-mārge — antaryāmi-svarūpete bhāse  
on the path of yoga  of the indwelling Lord  in the form  He appears

(79) On the path of knowledge He is manifest as the featureless Brahman; on the path of yoga He appears in the form of the Antaryāmī, the indwelling Lord.

রাগভক্তি, বিধিভক্তি হয় দুইরূপ ৷  
‘স্বয়ংভগবান্’, ‘ভগবান্’ — দুই ত’ ‘স্বরূপ’ ॥ ৮০ ॥

rāga-bhakti, vidhi-bhakti haya dui-rūpa  
rāga-bhakti  vidhi-bhakti  are  of two kinds  
‘svayaṁbhagavān’, ‘bhagavān’ — dui ta’ ‘svarūpa’  
Svayam Bhagavān  Bhagavān  two  indeed  forms

(80) Devotion is of two kinds, *rāga-bhakti* and *vidhi-bhakti*; and the Lord’s forms are two: Svayam Bhagavān, the Lord Himself, and Bhagavān.

রাগভক্ত্যে ব্রজে স্বয়ং-ভগবানে পায় ৷  
বিধিভক্ত্যে পার্ষদ-দেহে বৈকুণ্ঠকে যায় ॥ ৮১ ॥

rāga-bhaktye vraje svayaṁ-bhagavāne pāya  
by rāga-bhakti  in Vraja  Svayam Bhagavān  one attains  
vidhi-bhaktye pārṣada-dehe vaikuṇṭhake yāya  
by vidhi-bhakti  of an associate  in the body  to Vaikuṇṭha  one goes

(81) By *rāga-bhakti* one attains Svayam Bhagavān in Vraja; by *vidhi-bhakti* one goes to Vaikuṇṭha in the body of an associate (*pārṣada*).

নায়ং সুখাপো ভগবান্ দেহিনাং গোপিকাসুতঃ ৷  
জ্ঞানিনাঞ্চাত্মভূতানাং যথা ভক্তিমতামিহ ॥ ৮২ ॥

nāyaṁ sukhāpo bhagavān  
not  this  easily attained  Bhagavān  
dehināṁ gopikā-sutaḥ  
for the embodied  of the gopī  the son  
jñānināṁ cātma-bhūtānāṁ  
for the jñānīs  and  for those who realized the self  
yathā bhaktimatām iha  
as  for those who have devotion  here

(82) “This Bhagavān, the son of the gopī, is not so easily attained by the embodied, nor by the jñānīs, nor by those who have realized the self, as He is by those who have devotion.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.9.21)[^15]

যচ্চ ব্রজন্ত্যনিমিষামৃষভানুবৃত্ত্যা  
দূরে-যমা হ্যুপরি নঃ স্পৃহণীয়শীলাঃ ৷  
ভর্ত্তুর্মিথঃ সুযশসঃ কথনানুরাগ-  
বৈক্লব্যবাষ্পকলয়া পুলকীকৃতাঙ্গাঃ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

yac ca vrajanty animiṣām ṛṣabhānuvṛttyā  
to which  and  they go  of the gods  the Lord  by following  
dūre-yamā hy upari naḥ spṛhaṇīya-śīlāḥ  
far away (have left) yama  indeed  above  us  desirable  in nature  
bhartur mithaḥ su-yaśasaḥ kathanānurāga-  
of their Lord  together  the glorious  from speaking  of anurāga  
vaiklavya-bāṣpa-kalayā pulakī-kṛtāṅgāḥ  
of the agitation  of tears  by the drops  thrilling with rapture  in their limbs

(83) “Those whose limbs thrill with rapture and are wet with tears of loving agitation as they speak together of the glories of their Lord; who follow the Lord of the gods and have left *yama* and the other disciplines far behind; whose nature we ourselves long to have — they go to Vaikuṇṭha, which is above us.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.15.25)

সেই উপাসক হয় ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
অকাম, মোক্ষকাম, সর্ব্বকাম আর ॥ ৮৪ ॥

sei upāsaka haya trividha prakāra  
these  worshippers  are  of three  kinds  
akāma, mokṣa-kāma, sarva-kāma āra  
without desire  desiring liberation  with every desire  and

(84) These worshippers are of three kinds: those without desire (*akāma*), those who desire liberation (*mokṣa-kāma*) and those who have every kind of desire (*sarva-kāma*).

অকামঃ সর্ব্বকামো বা মোক্ষকাম উদারধীঃ ৷  
তীব্রেণ ভক্তিযোগেন যজেত পুরুষং পরম্ ॥ ৮৫ ॥

akāmaḥ sarva-kāmo vā  
without desire  full of all desires  or  
mokṣa-kāma udāra-dhīḥ  
desiring liberation  of broad intelligence  
tīvreṇa bhakti-yogena  
with intense  bhakti-yoga  
yajeta puruṣaṁ param  
should worship  the Person  Supreme

(85) “Whether he is without desire, full of every desire, or desirous of liberation, a person of broad intelligence should worship the Supreme Person with intense *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.10)[^16]

বুদ্ধিমান্ অর্থে — যদি ‘বিচারজ্ঞ’ হয় ৷  
নিজ-কাম লাগিহ তবে কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ৮৬ ॥

buddhimān arthe — yadi ‘vicāra-jña’ haya  
intelligent  in the sense  if  discerning  he is  
nija-kāma lāgiha tabe kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
of his own desire  for the sake even  then  Kṛṣṇa  he worships

(86) *Udāra-dhīḥ* means intelligent: if a person can discern, then even for the sake of his own desires he worships Kṛṣṇa.

ভক্তি বিনা কোন সাধন দিতে নারে ফল ৷  
সব ফল দেয় ভক্তি স্বতন্ত্র প্রবল ॥ ৮৭ ॥

bhakti vinā kona sādhana dite nāre phala  
bhakti  without  any  sādhana  to give  cannot  fruit  
saba phala deya bhakti svatantra prabala  
all  fruits  gives  bhakti  independent  powerful

(87) Without bhakti no *sādhana* can give its fruit. Bhakti, independent and powerful, gives every fruit.

অজাগলস্তন-ন্যায় অন্য সাধন ৷  
অতএব হরি ভজে বুদ্ধিমান্ জন ॥ ৮৮ ॥

ajā-gala-stana-nyāya anya sādhana  
of a goat  on the neck  of the teats  like  other  sādhanas  
ataeva hari bhaje buddhimān jana  
therefore  Hari  worships  intelligent  person

(88) Other *sādhanas* are like the teats on a goat’s neck. Therefore an intelligent person worships Hari.

চতুর্ব্বিধা ভজন্তে মাং জনাঃ সুকৃতিনোঽর্জ্জুন ৷  
আর্ত্তো জিজ্ঞাসুরর্থার্থী জ্ঞানী চ ভরতর্ষভ ॥ ৮৯ ॥

catur-vidhā bhajante māṁ  
of four kinds  worship  Me  
janāḥ sukṛtino ‘rjuna  
people  pious  O Arjuna  
ārto jijñāsur arthārthī  
the distressed  the inquisitive  the seeker of wealth  
jñānī ca bharatarṣabha  
the one with knowledge  and  O best of the Bhāratas

(89) “O Arjuna, best of the Bhāratas, four kinds of pious people worship Me: the distressed, the inquisitive, the seeker of wealth, and the one who has knowledge.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.16)

আর্ত্ত, অর্থার্থী, — দুই সকাম-ভিতরে গণি ৷  
জিজ্ঞাসু, জ্ঞানী, — দুই মোক্ষকামী মানি ॥ ৯০ ॥

ārta, arthārthī, — dui sakāma-bhitare gaṇi  
the distressed  the seeker of wealth  two  among those with desires  I count  
jijñāsu, jñānī, — dui mokṣa-kāmī māni  
the inquisitive  the one with knowledge  two  as desiring liberation  I regard

(90) The distressed and the seeker of wealth I count among those with desires (*sakāma*); the inquisitive and the one who has knowledge I regard as desirous of liberation (*mokṣa-kāma*).

এই চারি সুকৃতি হয় মহাভাগ্যবান্ ৷  
তত্তৎকামাদি ছাড়ি, হয় শুদ্ধভক্তিমান্ ॥ ৯১ ॥

ei cāri sukṛti haya mahā-bhāgyavān  
these  four  pious ones  are  greatly fortunate  
tat-tat-kāmādi chāḍi, haya śuddha-bhaktimān  
their respective  desires and so on  giving up  become  possessed of pure devotion

(91) These four pious ones are greatly fortunate. Giving up their respective desires, they come to possess pure devotion.

সাধুসঙ্গ-কৃপা কিম্বা কৃষ্ণের কৃপায় ৷  
কামাদি ‘দুঃসঙ্গ’ ছাড়ি’ শুদ্ধভক্তি পায় ॥ ৯২ ॥

sādhu-saṅga-kṛpā kimvā kṛṣṇera kṛpāya  
of the company of saints  by the mercy  or  Kṛṣṇa’s  by the mercy  
kāmādi ‘duḥsaṅga’ chāḍi’ śuddha-bhakti pāya  
desires and so on  bad company  giving up  pure devotion  they attain

(92) By the mercy of the company of saints, or by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, they give up the “bad company” of desires and the like and attain pure devotion.

সৎসঙ্গান্মুক্ত-দুঃসঙ্গো হাতুং নোৎসহতে বুধঃ ৷  
কীর্ত্ত্যমানং যশো যস্য সকৃদাকর্ণ্য রোচনম্ ॥ ৯৩ ॥

sat-saṅgān mukta-duḥsaṅgo  
through the company of saints  freed from bad company  
hātuṁ notsahate budhaḥ  
to give up  is not able  the wise one  
kīrtyamānaṁ yaśo yasya  
being sung  the glory  of whom  
sakṛd ākarṇya rocanam  
once  having heard  captivating

(93) “A wise person who has been freed from bad company through the company of saints can never give up the captivating glory of the Lord once he has heard it sung.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.10.11)

‘দুঃসঙ্গ’ কহিয়ে — ‘কৈতব’, — ‘আত্মবঞ্চনা’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণভক্তি বিনা অন্য কামনা ॥ ৯৪ ॥

‘duḥsaṅga’ kahiye — ‘kaitava’, — ‘ātma-vañcanā’  
bad company  I call  deceit  self-deception  
kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-bhakti vinā anya kāmanā  
than Kṛṣṇa  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  other  desire

(94) By “bad company” I mean deceit (*kaitava*), self-deception: the desire for anything other than Kṛṣṇa and devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

ধর্ম্মঃ প্রোজ্ঝিত-কৈতবোঽত্র পরমো নির্ম্মৎসরাণাং সতাং  
বেদ্যং বাস্তবমত্র বস্তু শিবদং তাপত্রয়োন্মূলনম্ ৷  
শ্রীমদ্ভাগবতে মহামুনিকৃতে কিংবা পরৈরীশ্বরঃ  
সদ্যো হৃদ্যবরুধ্যতেঽত্র কৃতিভিঃ শুশ্রূষুভিস্তৎক্ষণাৎ ॥ ৯৫ ॥

dharmaḥ projjhita-kaitavo ‘tra paramo nirmatsarāṇāṁ satāṁ  
dharma  wholly-cast-out  deceit  here  supreme  of the non-envious  of the saints  
vedyaṁ vāstavam atra vastu śiva-daṁ tāpa-trayonmūlanam  
to be known  real  here  substance  bestowing good  the threefold miseries  uprooting  
śrīmad-bhāgavate mahā-muni-kṛte kiṁ vā parair īśvaraḥ  
in the beautiful Bhāgavata  by the great sage  composed  what  by others?  the Lord  
sadyo hṛdy avarudhyate ‘tra kṛtibhiḥ śuśrūṣubhis tat-kṣaṇāt  
at once  in the heart  is captured  here  by the pious  who wish to hear  that very moment

(95) “In this beautiful *Bhāgavatam*, composed by the great sage, is taught the supreme dharma of the saintly who are free from envy, from which all deceit has been cast out. Here is known the true Reality, which bestows good and uproots the threefold miseries. What need is there of other scriptures? As soon as the pious wish to hear it, the Lord is at once captured within their hearts.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.2)[^17]

‘প্র’-শব্দে — মোক্ষবাঞ্ছা কৈতবপ্রধান ৷  
এই শ্লোকে শ্রীধরস্বামী করিয়াছেন ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ৯৬ ॥

‘pra’-śabde — mokṣa-vāñchā kaitava-pradhāna  
by the prefix pra  of liberation  the desire  of deceit  the chief  
ei śloke śrīdhara-svāmī kariyāchena vyākhyāna  
this  verse  Śrīdhara Svāmī  has given  the explanation

(96) By the prefix *pra* (in *projjhita*) the desire for liberation is shown to be the chief deceit. So Śrīdhara Svāmī has explained this verse.

সকাম ভক্তে ‘অজ্ঞ’ জানি’ দয়ালু ভগবান্ ৷  
স্ব-চরণ দিয়া করে ইচ্ছার পিধান ॥ ৯৭ ॥

sakāma bhakte ‘ajña’ jāni’ dayālu bhagavān  
having desires  the devotee  ignorant  knowing  merciful  Bhagavān  
sva-caraṇa diyā kare icchāra pidhāna  
His own  feet  giving  makes  of desire  the covering

(97) Knowing a devotee with desires to be ignorant, the merciful Bhagavān gives him His own feet and so covers over his desires.

সত্যং দিশত্যর্থিতমর্থিতো নৃণাং  
নৈবার্থদো যৎ পুনরর্থিতা যতঃ ৷  
স্বয়ং বিধত্তে ভজতামনিচ্ছতা-  
মিচ্ছাপিধানং নিজপাদপল্লবম্ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

satyaṁ diśaty arthitam arthito nṛṇāṁ  
truly  He grants  what is asked  when asked  to men  
naivārtha-do yat punar arthitā yataḥ  
not indeed  giver of that  which  again  asking  from which  
svayaṁ vidhatte bhajatām anicchatām  
Himself  He grants  to those who worship  though not desiring  
icchā-pidhānaṁ nija-pāda-pallavam  
covering all desires  His own  feet like tender shoots

(98) “It is true that when men ask, the Lord grants what they ask for; but He does not give what would make them ask again. To those who worship Him, though they do not desire it, He Himself gives His own feet, tender as new shoots, which put an end to all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.19.26)[^18]

সাধুসঙ্গ, কৃষ্ণকৃপা, ভক্তির স্বভাব ৷  
এ তিনে সব ছাড়ায়, করে কৃষ্ণে ‘ভাব’ ॥ ৯৯ ॥

sādhu-saṅga, kṛṣṇa-kṛpā, bhaktira svabhāva  
the company of saints  Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  of bhakti  the nature  
e tine saba chāḍāya, kare kṛṣṇe ‘bhāva’  
these  three  everything  make one give up  awaken  for Kṛṣṇa  bhāva

(99) The company of saints, Kṛṣṇa’s mercy and the very nature of bhakti — these three make one give up everything else and awaken *bhāva* for Kṛṣṇa.

আগে যত যত অর্থ ব্যাখ্যান করিব ৷  
কৃষ্ণগুণাস্বাদের এই হেতু জানিব ॥ ১০০ ॥

āge yata yata artha vyākhyāna kariba  
further  however many  meanings  explanation  I shall make  
kṛṣṇa-guṇāsvādera ei hetu jāniba  
of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  of the relishing  this  the cause  know

(100) In all the meanings I shall explain from here on, know this to be the cause of relishing Kṛṣṇa’s qualities.

শ্লোকব্যাখ্যা লাগি’ এই করিলুঁ আভাস ৷  
এবে করি শ্লোকের মূলার্থ প্রকাশ ॥ ১০১ ॥

śloka-vyākhyā lāgi’ ei karilum̐ ābhāsa  
of the verse  of the explanation  for the sake  this  I have made  outline  
ebe kari ślokera mūlārtha prakāśa  
now  I make  of the verse  of the root meaning  revelation

(101) I have given this outline for the sake of explaining the verse. Now I shall reveal the verse’s basic meaning.

জ্ঞানমার্গে উপাসক — দুই ত’ প্রকার ৷  
কেবল-ব্রহ্মোপাসক, মোক্ষাকাঙ্ক্ষী আর ॥ ১০২ ॥

jñāna-mārge upāsaka — dui ta’ prakāra  
on the path of knowledge  worshippers  of two  indeed  kinds  
kevala-brahmopāsaka, mokṣākāṅkṣī āra  
worshippers of Brahman alone  those who long for liberation  and

(102) On the path of knowledge the worshippers are of two kinds: those who worship Brahman alone (*kevala-brahmopāsaka*) and those who long for liberation (*mokṣākāṅkṣī*).

কেবল ব্রহ্মোপাসক তিন ভেদ হয় ৷  
সাধক, ব্রহ্মময়, আর প্রাপ্ত-ব্রহ্মলয় ॥ ১০৩ ॥

kevala brahmopāsaka tina bheda haya  
of Brahman alone  worshippers  three  kinds  are  
sādhaka, brahmamaya, āra prāpta-brahma-laya  
the sādhaka  the brahmamaya  and  the prāpta-brahma-laya

(103) Those who worship Brahman alone are of three kinds: the *sādhaka* (beginner), the *brahmamaya* (absorbed in Brahman) and the *prāpta-brahma-laya* (merged in Brahman).

ভক্তি বিনা কেবল জ্ঞানে ‘মুক্তি’ নাহি হয় ৷  
ভক্তি সাধন করে যেই ‘প্রাপ্ত-ব্রহ্মলয়’ ॥ ১০৪ ॥

bhakti vinā kevala jñāne ‘mukti’ nāhi haya  
bhakti  without  by knowledge alone  liberation  not  there is  
bhakti sādhana kare yei ‘prāpta-brahma-laya’  
of bhakti  the sādhana  performs  who  (he is) prāpta-brahma-laya

(104) Without bhakti, by knowledge alone, there is no liberation. It is one who practises the *sādhana* of bhakti who reaches merging in Brahman (*prāpta-brahma-laya*).

ভক্তির স্বভাব, — ব্রহ্ম হৈতে করে আকর্ষণ ৷  
দিব্য দেহ দিয়া করায় কৃষ্ণের ভজন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

bhaktira svabhāva, — brahma haite kare ākarṣaṇa  
of bhakti  the nature  from Brahman  makes  attraction  
divya deha diyā karāya kṛṣṇera bhajana  
divine  body  giving  makes one do  Kṛṣṇa’s  worship

(105) The nature of bhakti is that it draws one away from Brahman; giving one a divine body, it makes one worship Kṛṣṇa.

ভক্তদেহ পাইলে হয় গুণের স্মরণ ৷  
গুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা করে নির্ম্মল ভজন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

bhakta-deha pāile haya guṇera smaraṇa  
the body of a devotee  on obtaining  there is  of the qualities  remembrance  
guṇākṛṣṭa hañā kare nirmala bhajana  
attracted by the qualities  becoming  he does  pure  worship

(106) On obtaining the body of a devotee, one remembers Kṛṣṇa’s qualities; attracted by those qualities, one worships Him with pure devotion.

মুক্তা অপি লীলয়া বিগ্রহং কৃত্বা ভগবন্তং ভজন্তে ॥ ১০৭ ॥

muktā api līlayā vigrahaṁ  
the liberated  even  by līlā  a form  
kṛtvā bhagavantaṁ bhajante  
having assumed  Bhagavān  worship

(107) “Even the liberated, assuming a form (*vigraha*) by the Lord’s *līlā*, worship Bhagavān.” (words of the author of the Sarvajña-bhāṣya, quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī in his commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.21)[^19]

জন্ম হৈতে শুক সনকাদি ‘ব্রহ্মময়’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণগুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ১০৮ ॥

janma haite śuka-sanakādi ‘brahmamaya’  
birth  from  Śuka  Sanaka and the others  absorbed in Brahman  
kṛṣṇa-guṇākṛṣṭa hañā kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities attracted  becoming  Kṛṣṇa  they worship

(108) From birth Śuka, Sanaka and the others were *brahmamaya*, absorbed in Brahman; attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, they worship Kṛṣṇa.

সনকাদ্যের কৃষ্ণকৃপায় সৌরভে হরে মন ৷  
গুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা করে নির্ম্মল ভজন ॥ ১০৯ ॥

sanakādyera kṛṣṇa-kṛpāya saurabhe hare mana  
of Sanaka and the others  by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  by the fragrance  is stolen  the mind  
guṇākṛṣṭa hañā kare nirmala bhajana  
attracted by the qualities  becoming  they do  pure  worship

(109) By Kṛṣṇa’s mercy the minds of Sanaka and the others were stolen by the fragrance of His feet; attracted by His qualities, they worship Him with pure devotion.

তস্যারবিন্দনয়নস্য পদারবিন্দ-  
কিঞ্জল্কমিশ্রতুলসীমকরন্দবায়ুঃ ৷  
অন্তর্গতঃ স্ববিবরেণ চকার তেষাং  
সংক্ষোভমক্ষরজুষামপি চিত্ততন্বোঃ ॥ ১১০ ॥

tasyāravinda-nayanasya padāravinda-  
of Him  the lotus-eyed  of the lotus feet  
kiñjalka-miśra-tulasī-makaranda-vāyuḥ  
with the filaments  mixed  of tulasī  of the nectar  the breeze  
antar-gataḥ sva-vivareṇa cakāra teṣāṁ  
having entered within  through its own passage  caused  in them  
saṅkṣobham akṣara-juṣām api citta-tanvoḥ  
agitation  devoted to the imperishable Brahman  even  of mind and body

(110) “The breeze that bore the fragrance of tulasī, mingled with the pollen of the lotus-eyed Lord’s lotus feet, entered their nostrils and stirred both their minds and their bodies, though they were devoted to the imperishable Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.15.43)[^20]

ব্যাসকৃপায় শুকদেবের লীলাদি-স্মরণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণগুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা করেন ভজন ॥ ১১১ ॥

vyāsa-kṛpāya śukadevera līlādi-smaraṇa  
by Vyāsa’s mercy  of Śukadeva  of the pastimes and so on  remembrance  
kṛṣṇa-guṇākṛṣṭa hañā karena bhajana  
by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities attracted  becoming  he does  worship

(111) By Vyāsa’s mercy Śukadeva came to remember Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes; attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, he worships Him.

হরের্গুণাক্ষিপ্তমতির্ভগবান্ বাদরায়ণিঃ ৷  
অধ্যগান্মহদাখ্যানং নিত্যং বিষ্ণুজনপ্রিয়ঃ ॥ ১১২ ॥

harer guṇākṣipta-matir  
by Hari’s  qualities  captivated  in mind  
bhagavān bādarāyaṇiḥ  
the great  son of Bādarāyaṇa  
adhyagān mahad-ākhyānaṁ  
studied  the great  narration  
nityaṁ viṣṇu-jana-priyaḥ  
ever  to the devotees of Viṣṇu  dear

(112) “His mind captivated by the qualities of Hari, the great Śukadeva, son of Bādarāyaṇa, who is ever dear to the devotees of Viṣṇu, studied this great narration.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.11)

নব-যোগীশ্বর জন্ম হৈতে ‘সাধক’ জ্ঞানী ৷  
বিধি-শিব-নারদ-মুখে কৃষ্ণগুণ শুনি’ ॥ ১১৩ ॥

nava-yogīśvara janma haite ‘sādhaka’ jñānī  
nine  Yogendras  birth  from  sādhakas  jñānīs  
vidhi-śiva-nārada-mukhe kṛṣṇa-guṇa śuni’  
of Brahmā  Śiva  Nārada  from the mouths  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  having heard

(113) The nine Yogendras were *sādhaka* jñānīs from birth. Having heard of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities from the mouths of Brahmā, Śiva and Nārada,

গুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা করে কৃষ্ণের ভজন ৷  
একাদশ-স্কন্ধে তাঁর ভক্তি-বিবরণ ॥ ১১৪ ॥

guṇākṛṣṭa hañā kare kṛṣṇera bhajana  
attracted by the qualities  becoming  they do  Kṛṣṇa’s  worship  
ekādaśa-skandhe tām̐ra bhakti-vivaraṇa  
in the Eleventh Canto  their  devotion  the description

(114) they were attracted by those qualities and worshipped Kṛṣṇa. Their devotion is described in the Eleventh Canto.

অক্লেশাং কমলভুবঃ প্রবিশ্য গোষ্ঠীং  
কুর্ব্বন্তঃ শ্রুতিশিরসাং শ্রুতিং শ্রুতজ্ঞাঃ ৷  
উত্তুঙ্গং যদুপুরসঙ্গমায় রঙ্গং  
যোগীন্দ্রাঃ পুলকভৃতো নবাপ্যবাপুঃ ॥ ১১৫ ॥

akleśāṁ kamala-bhuvaḥ praviśya goṣṭhīṁ  
free from distress  of the lotus-born  having entered  the assembly  
kurvantaḥ śruti-śirasāṁ śrutiṁ śruta-jñāḥ  
performing  of the Upaniṣads  the hearing  knowers of the Śruti  
uttuṅgaṁ yadu-pura-saṅgamāya raṅgaṁ  
lofty  to the city of Yadu  for meeting  the raṅga  
yogīndrāḥ pulaka-bhṛto navāpy avāpuḥ  
the Yogendras  thrilling with rapture  nine  even  reached

(115) “Entering the assembly of the lotus-born Brahmā, which is free from all distress, the nine Yogendras, knowers of the Śruti, heard the Upaniṣads there; yet, their bodies thrilling with rapture, they went to the lofty *raṅga-kṣetra* to meet the Lord in the city of the Yadus.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Śānta-bhakti-laharī, 20)

মোক্ষাকাঙ্ক্ষী জ্ঞানী হয় তিনপ্রকার ৷  
মুমুক্ষু, জীবন্মুক্ত, প্রাপ্তস্বরূপ আর ॥ ১১৬ ॥

mokṣākāṅkṣī jñānī haya tina-prakāra  
who long for liberation  jñānīs  are  of three kinds  
mumukṣu, jīvan-mukta, prāpta-svarūpa āra  
the mumukṣu  the jīvan-mukta  the prāpta-svarūpa  and

(116) Jñānīs who long for liberation are of three kinds: the *mumukṣu* (seeker of liberation), the *jīvan-mukta* (liberated while living) and the *prāpta-svarūpa* (one who has attained his true nature).

‘মুমুক্ষু’ জগতে অনেক সংসারী জন ৷  
‘মুক্তি’ লাগি’ ভক্ত্যে করে কৃষ্ণের ভজন ॥ ১১৭ ॥

‘mumukṣu’ jagate aneka saṁsārī jana  
mumukṣus  in the world  many  attached to worldly life  people  
‘mukti’ lāgi’ bhaktye kare kṛṣṇera bhajana  
liberation  for the sake  with devotion  they do  Kṛṣṇa’s  worship

(117) In the world there are many *mumukṣus*, people attached to worldly life, who worship Kṛṣṇa with devotion for the sake of liberation.

মুমুক্ষবো ঘোররূপান্ হিত্বা ভূতপতীনথ ৷  
নারায়ণ-কলাঃ শান্তা ভজন্তি হ্যনসূয়বঃ ॥ ১১৮ ॥

mumukṣavo ghora-rūpān  
those who seek liberation  terrible in form  
hitvā bhūta-patīn atha  
having given up  the lords of beings  therefore  
nārāyaṇa-kalāḥ śāntā  
of Nārāyaṇa  the partial forms  peaceful  
bhajanti hy anasūyavaḥ  
worship  indeed  free from envy

(118) “Those who seek liberation give up the lords of beings, whose forms are terrible, though they bear them no envy; peaceful, they worship the partial forms (*kalā*) of Nārāyaṇa.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.26)

সেই সবের সাধুসঙ্গে গুণ স্ফুরায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণভজন করায়, ‘মুমুক্ষা’ ছাড়ায় ॥ ১১৯ ॥

sei sabera sādhu-saṅge guṇa sphurāya  
of those  all  in the company of sādhus  the qualities (of Kṛṣṇa)  reveals  
kṛṣṇa-bhajana karāya, ‘mumukṣā’ chāḍāya  
of Kṛṣṇa  worship  makes them do  the desire for liberation  makes them give up

(119) In the company of sādhus, Kṛṣṇa’s qualities are revealed to all of them; that company leads them to worship Kṛṣṇa and makes them give up the desire for liberation.

অহো মহাত্মন্ বহুদোষদুষ্টোঽ-  
প্যেকেন ভাত্যেষ ভবো গুণেন ৷  
সৎসঙ্গমাখ্যেন সুখাবহেন  
কৃতাদ্য নো যেন কৃশা মুমুক্ষা ॥ ১২০ ॥

aho mahātman bahu-doṣa-duṣṭo  
O  great soul  by many faults  corrupted  
‘py ekena bhāty eṣa bhavo guṇena  
though  by one  shines  this  worldly existence  quality  
sat-saṅgamākhyena sukhāvahena  
called the company of saints  bringing happiness  
kṛtādya no yena kṛśā mumukṣā  
made  today  our  by which  weak  desire for liberation

(120) “O great soul, this worldly existence is corrupted by many faults, yet it shines with one quality, called the company of saints, which brings happiness: by it our desire for liberation has today been made weak.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 27)

নারদের সঙ্গে শৌনকাদি মুনিগণ ৷  
মুমুক্ষা ছাড়িয়া কৈলা কৃষ্ণের ভজন ॥ ১২১ ॥

nāradera saṅge śaunakādi muni-gaṇa  
of Nārada  in the company  Śaunaka and the others  the sages  
mumukṣā chāḍiyā kailā kṛṣṇera bhajana  
the desire for liberation  giving up  performed  of Kṛṣṇa  worship

(121) In the company of Nārada, Śaunaka and the other sages gave up the desire for liberation and worshipped Kṛṣṇa.[^21]

কৃষ্ণের দর্শনে, কারো কৃষ্ণের কৃপায় ৷  
মুমুক্ষা ছাড়িয়া গুণে ভজে তাঁর পা’য় ॥ ১২২ ॥

kṛṣṇera darśane, kāro kṛṣṇera kṛpāya  
of Kṛṣṇa  by the sight  some  of Kṛṣṇa  by the mercy  
mumukṣā chāḍiyā guṇe bhaje tām̐ra pā’ya  
the desire for liberation  giving up  (drawn) by His qualities  worship  His  feet

(122) Some on seeing Kṛṣṇa, and some by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, give up the desire for liberation and, drawn by His qualities, worship His feet.

অস্মিন্ সুখঘনমূর্ত্তৌ পরমাত্মনি বৃষ্ণিপত্তনে স্ফুরতি ৷  
আত্মারামতয়া মে বৃথা গতো বত চিরং কালঃ ॥ ১২৩ ॥

asmin sukha-ghana-mūrtau  
this  of bliss  concentrated form  
param-ātmani vṛṣṇi-pattane sphurati  
the Supreme Self  in Vṛṣṇi-pattana (Dvārakā)  when He appeared  
ātmārāmatayā me vṛthā  
by being an ātmārāma  my  in vain  
gato bata ciraṁ kālaḥ  
has passed  alas  long  time

(123) “Now that Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Self, whose form is concentrated bliss, has appeared here in Vṛṣṇi-pattana, I see — alas! — how long my days have passed in vain while I clung to being an *ātmārāma*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Śānta-bhakti-laharī, 34)[^22]

‘জীবন্মুক্ত’ অনেক, সেই দুই ভেদ জানি ৷  
‘ভক্ত্যে জীবন্মুক্ত’, ‘জ্ঞানে জীবন্মুক্ত’ মানি ॥ ১২৪ ॥

‘jīvan-mukta’ aneka, sei dui bheda jāni  
those liberated while living  many  of them  two  kinds  I know  
‘bhaktye jīvan-mukta’, ‘jñāne jīvan-mukta’ māni  
by devotion  liberated while living  by knowledge  liberated while living  I hold

(124) There are many who are liberated while living (*jīvan-mukta*), and I know them to be of two kinds: those liberated while living through devotion and those liberated while living through knowledge.

‘ভক্ত্যে জীবন্মুক্ত’ গুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা ভজে ৷  
শুষ্কজ্ঞানে জীবন্মুক্ত অপরাধে অধো মজে ॥ ১২৫ ॥

‘bhaktye jīvan-mukta’ guṇākṛṣṭa hañā bhaje  
by devotion  liberated while living  attracted by the qualities  becoming  worships  
śuṣka-jñāne jīvan-mukta aparādhe adho maje  
by dry knowledge  liberated while living  through offences  down  sinks

(125) One liberated while living through devotion is attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and worships Him; but one “liberated while living” through dry knowledge sinks down because of his offences.[^23]

যেঽন্যেঽরবিন্দাক্ষ বিমুক্তমানিন-  
স্ত্বয্যস্তভাবাদবিশুদ্ধবুদ্ধয়ঃ ৷  
আরুহ্য কৃচ্ছ্রেণ পরং পদং ততঃ  
পতন্ত্যধোঽনাদৃতযুষ্মদঙ্ঘ্রয়ঃ ॥ ১২৬ ॥

ye ’nye ’ravindākṣa vimukta-māninas  
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated  
tvayy asta-bhāvād aviśuddha-buddhayaḥ  
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence  
āruhya kṛcchreṇa paraṁ padaṁ tataḥ  
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there  
patanty adho ‘nādṛta-yuṣmad-aṅghrayaḥ  
fall  down  having neglected Your feet

(126) “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)[^24]

ব্রহ্মভূতঃ প্রসন্নাত্মা ন শোচতি ন কাঙ্ক্ষতি ৷  
সমঃ সর্ব্বেষু ভূতেষু মদ্ভক্তিং লভতে পরাম্ ॥ ১২৭ ॥

brahma-bhūtaḥ prasannātmā  
one who has realised Brahman  serene in self  
na śocati na kāṅkṣati  
not  grieves  not  desires  
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu  
equal  to all  beings  
mad-bhaktiṁ labhate parām  
for Me  devotion  attains  supreme

(127) “One who has realised Brahman and is serene within neither grieves nor desires. Equal to all beings, he attains supreme devotion to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.54)[^25]

অদ্বৈতবীথীপথিকৈরুপাস্যাঃ  
স্বানন্দসিংহাসনলব্ধদীক্ষাঃ ৷  
শঠেন কেনাপি বয়ং হঠেন  
দাসীকৃতা গোপবধূবিটেন ॥ ১২৮ ॥

advaita-vīthī-pathikair upāsyāḥ  
of nondualism  the path  by travellers  worshipped  
svānanda-siṁhāsana-labdha-dīkṣāḥ  
on the throne of self-bliss  having received initiation  
śaṭhena kenāpi vayaṁ haṭhena  
by a cunning  certain  we  by force  
dāsī-kṛtā gopa-vadhū-viṭena  
maidservants  made  of the cowherd wives  by the lover

(128) “I was worshipped by those who walk the path of non-dualism, and I had received initiation on the throne of the bliss of the self; yet a certain rogue, a lover of the cowherd maidens, has forcibly made me a maidservant.” (Śrī Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta)[^26]

ভক্তিবলে ‘প্রাপ্তস্বরূপ’ দিব্যদেহ পায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণগুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা ভজে কৃষ্ণ-পা’য় ॥ ১২৯ ॥

bhakti-bale ‘prāpta-svarūpa’ divya-deha pāya  
of bhakti  by the strength  one who has attained his true nature  a divine  body  receives  
kṛṣṇa-guṇākṛṣṭa hañā bhaje kṛṣṇa-pā’ya  
by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities attracted  becoming  worships  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(129) By the strength of bhakti, one who has attained his true nature (*prāpta-svarūpa*) receives a divine body. Attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, he worships Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

নিরোধোঽস্যানুশয়নমাত্মনঃ সহ শক্তিভিঃ ৷  
মুক্তির্হিত্বান্যথারূপং স্বরূপেণ ব্যবস্থিতিঃ ॥ ১৩০ ॥

nirodho ‘syānu śayanam  
nirodha  of it  following  lying down to rest  
ātmanaḥ saha śaktibhiḥ  
of the self  together  with its potencies  
muktir hitvānyathā-rūpaṁ  
liberation  having given up  the other form  
svarūpeṇa vyavasthitiḥ  
in one’s own true nature  being established

(130) “*Nirodha* is the lying down to rest of the self, together with its potencies, following (the mystic slumber of the Lord). *Mukti* is to give up the other form and be established in one’s own true nature (*svarūpa*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.10.6)

কৃষ্ণ-বহির্ম্মুখতা-দোষ মায়া হৈতে হয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণোন্মুখী মুক্তি হৈতে মায়া-মুক্তি হয় ॥ ১৩১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bahirmukhatā-doṣa māyā haite haya  
from Kṛṣṇa  of turning away  the fault  from māyā  comes  is  
kṛṣṇonmukhī mukti haite māyā-mukti haya  
turned toward Kṛṣṇa  liberation  from  from māyā  freedom  comes

(131) The fault of turning away from Kṛṣṇa comes from māyā, and freedom from māyā comes from liberation that is turned toward Kṛṣṇa.[^27]

ভয়ং দ্বিতীয়াভিনিবেশতঃ স্যা-  
দীশাদপেতস্য বিপর্য্যয়োঽস্মৃতিঃ ৷  
তন্মায়য়াতো বুধ আভজেত্তং  
ভক্ত্যৈকয়েশং গুরুদেবতাত্মা ॥ ১৩২ ॥

bhayaṁ dvitīyābhiniveśataḥ syād  
fear  from absorption in what is other  arises  
īśād apetasya viparyayo ‘smṛtiḥ  
from the Lord  for one turned away  misconception  forgetfulness  
tan-māyayāto budha ābhajet taṁ  
by His māyā  therefore  the wise one  should worship  Him  
bhaktyaikayeśaṁ guru-devatātmā  
with devotion  exclusive  the Lord  seeing the guru as his deity and his self

(132) “For one who has turned away from the Lord, fear arises from absorption in what is other than Him; by His māyā come forgetfulness of the Lord and a false idea of oneself. Therefore the wise, seeing the guru as his worshipful deity and his very self, should worship the Lord with exclusive devotion.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.37)[^28]

দৈবী হ্যেষা গুণময়ী মম মায়া দুরত্যয়া ৷  
মামেব যে প্রপদ্যন্তে মায়ামেতাং তরন্তি তে ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

daivī hy eṣā guṇa-mayī  
divine  indeed  this  made of the guṇas  
mama māyā duratyayā  
My  māyā  hard to overcome  
mām eva ye prapadyante  
to Me  alone  those who  surrender  
māyām etāṁ taranti te  
māyā  this  cross over  they

(133) “This divine māyā of Mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me alone cross beyond this māyā.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.14)[^29]

ভক্তি বিনা মুক্তি নাহি, ভক্ত্যে মুক্তি হয় ৷  
ভক্ত্যে মুক্তি পাইলে অবশ্য কৃষ্ণ ভজয় ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

bhakti vinā mukti nāhi, bhaktye mukti haya  
bhakti  without  liberation  there is not  by bhakti  liberation  comes  
bhaktye mukti pāile avaśya kṛṣṇa bhajaya  
by bhakti  liberation  having attained  certainly  Kṛṣṇa  worships

(134) Without bhakti there is no liberation; liberation comes through bhakti. And one who has attained liberation through bhakti surely worships Kṛṣṇa.

শ্রেয়ঃসৃতিং ভক্তিমুদস্য তে বিভো  
ক্লিশ্যন্তি যে কেবল-বোধলব্ধয়ে ৷  
তেষামসৌ ক্লেশল এব শিষ্যতে  
নান্যদ্ যথা স্থূলতুষাবঘাতিনাম্ ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

śreyaḥ-sṛtiṁ bhaktim udasya te vibho  
the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord  
kliśyanti ye kevala-bodha-labdhaye  
toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge  
teṣām asau kleśala eva śiṣyate  
for them  this  toil  only  remains  
nānyad yathā sthūla-tuṣāvaghātinām  
nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks

(135) “O almighty Lord, bhakti to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)[^30]

যেঽন্যেঽরবিন্দাক্ষ বিমুক্তমানিন-  
স্ত্বয্যস্তভাবাদবিশুদ্ধবুদ্ধয়ঃ ৷  
আরুহ্য কৃচ্ছ্রেণ পরং পদং ততঃ  
পতন্ত্যধোঽনাদৃতযুষ্মদঙ্ঘ্রয়ঃ ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

ye ‘nye ‘ravindākṣa vimukta-māninas  
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated  
tvayy asta-bhāvād aviśuddha-buddhayaḥ  
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence  
āruhya kṛcchreṇa paraṁ padaṁ tataḥ  
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there  
patanty adho ‘nādṛta-yuṣmad-aṅghrayaḥ  
fall  down  having neglected Your feet

(136) “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)[^31]

য এষাং পুরুষং সাক্ষাদাত্মপ্রভবমীশ্বরম্ ৷  
ন ভজন্ত্যবজানন্তি স্থানাদ্ভ্রষ্টাঃ পতন্ত্যধঃ ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

ya eṣāṁ puruṣaṁ sākṣād  
who  of them  the Puruṣa  directly  
ātma-prabhavam īśvaram  
their own source  the Lord  
na bhajanty avajānanti  
not  worship  disregard  
sthānād bhraṣṭāḥ patanty adhaḥ  
from their position  fallen  they fall  down

(137) “Those among them who do not worship the Puruṣa, their own source and Lord, but disregard Him, fall from their position and sink into the depths.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.3)[^32]

মুক্তা অপি লীলয়া বিগ্রহং কৃত্বা ভগবন্তং ভজন্তে ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

muktā api līlayā vigrahaṁ  
the liberated  even  by līlā  a form  
kṛtvā bhagavantaṁ bhajante  
having assumed  Bhagavān  worship

(138) “Even the liberated, assuming a form (*vigraha*) by the Lord’s *līlā*, worship Bhagavān.” (words of the author of the Sarvajña-bhāṣya, quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī in his commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.21)[^33]

এই ছয় আত্মারাম কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ৷  
পৃথক্ পৃথক্ চ-কারে ইহা ‘অপি’র অর্থ কয় ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

ei chaya ātmārāma kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
these  six  ātmārāmas  Kṛṣṇa  worship  
pṛthak pṛthak ca-kāre ihā ‘api’ra artha kaya  
separately  separately  by the word ca  here  of api  the meaning  expresses

(139) These six kinds of *ātmārāmas* worship Kṛṣṇa. The word *ca*, joined to each of them separately, here expresses the sense of *api* (“also”).

‘আত্মারামাশ্চ অপি’ করে কৃষ্ণে অহৈতুকী ভক্তি ৷  
‘মুনয়ঃ সন্তঃ’ ইতি কৃষ্ণমননে আসক্তি ॥ ১৪০ ॥

‘ātmārāmāś ca api’ kare kṛṣṇe ahaitukī bhakti  
the ātmārāmas  and  also  render  to Kṛṣṇa  causeless  devotion  
‘munayaḥ santaḥ’ iti kṛṣṇa-manane āsakti  
sages  being  thus (means)  in contemplation of Kṛṣṇa  attachment

(140) “*Ātmārāmāś ca api*”: even the *ātmārāmas* render causeless devotion to Kṛṣṇa. “*Munayaḥ santaḥ*” — “being sages” — means that they become attached to contemplating Kṛṣṇa.

‘নির্গ্রন্থাঃ’ — অবিদ্যাহীন, কেহ — বিধিহীন ৷  
যাঁহা যেই যুক্ত, সেই অর্থের অধীন ॥ ১৪১ ॥

‘nirgranthāḥ’ — avidyā-hīna, keha — vidhi-hīna  
nirgranthāḥ  free from ignorance  some  free from rules  
yām̐hā yei yukta, sei arthera adhīna  
where  whichever  fits  of that  meaning  dependent on

(141) “*Nirgranthāḥ*” means free from ignorance (*avidyā*), and for some it means free from rules (*vidhi*). Wherever a meaning fits, the word takes that meaning.

চ-শব্দে করি যদি ‘ইতরেতর’ অর্থ ৷  
আর এক অর্থ কহে পরম সমর্থ ॥ ১৪২ ॥

ca-śabde kari yadi ‘itaretara’ artha  
to the word ca  I give  if  of mutual connection (itaretara)  the meaning  
āra eka artha kahe parama samartha  
another  one  meaning  it expresses  most  cogent

(142) If I give the word *ca* the sense of *itaretara* (mutual connection), it yields yet another most cogent meaning.

‘আত্মারামাশ্চ আত্মারামাশ্চ’ করি’ বার ছয় ৷  
পঞ্চ আত্মারাম, ছয়ে চ-কারে লুপ্ত হয় ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

“ātmārāmāś ca ātmārāmāś ca” kari’ bāra chaya  
ātmārāmas  and  ātmārāmas  and  making  times  six  
pañca ātmārāma chaye ca-kāre lupta haya  
five  ātmārāmas  of the six  with the word ca  dropped  are

(143) When “*ātmārāmāś ca ātmārāmāś ca*” is said six times, five of the six *ātmārāmas* drop out together with their *ca*.

এক ‘আত্মারামঃ’-শব্দ অবশেষ রহে ৷  
এক ‘আত্মারামঃ’-শব্দে ছয়জন কহে ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

eka ‘ātmārāmaḥ’-śabda avaśeṣa rahe  
one  ātmārāmaḥ  word  as the remainder  stays  
eka ‘ātmārāmaḥ’-śabde chaya-jana kahe  
by one  ātmārāmaḥ  word  six persons  it denotes

(144) A single word *ātmārāmaḥ* remains, and that one word denotes all six.

সরূপাণামেকশেষ একবিভক্তৌ উক্তার্থানামপ্রয়োগঃ ৷  
রামশ্চ রামশ্চ রামশ্চ রামা ইতিবৎ ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

sarūpāṇām eka-śeṣa eka-vibhaktau  
of words of the same form  one  remains  in the same case  
uktārthānām aprayogaḥ  
of words whose meaning is already expressed  non-use  
rāmaś ca rāmaś ca rāmaś ca rāmā itivat  
Rāma  and  Rāma  and  Rāma  and  Rāmas  as in

(145) “Of words of the same form in the same case only one remains (*eka-śeṣa*); words whose meaning has already been expressed are not used: thus ‘*rāmaś ca rāmaś ca rāmaś ca*’ becomes ‘*rāmāḥ*’.” (Viśva-prakāśa)[^34]

তবে যে চ-কার, সেই ‘সমুচ্চয়’ কয় ৷  
‘আত্মারামাশ্চ মুনয়শ্চ’ কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

tabe ye ca-kāra, sei ‘samuccaya’ kaya  
then  that  word ca  it  conjunction (samuccaya)  expresses  
“ātmārāmāś ca munayaś ca” kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
ātmārāmas  and  sages  and  Kṛṣṇa  worship

(146) The word *ca* that then remains expresses conjunction (*samuccaya*): “the *ātmārāmas* and the sages (*ātmārāmāś ca munayaś ca*)” worship Kṛṣṇa.

‘নির্গ্রন্থা অপি’র এই ‘অপি’ — সম্ভাবনে ৷  
এই সাত অর্থ প্রথমে করিলুঁ ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

‘nirgranthā api’ra ei ‘api’ — sambhāvane  
of nirgranthā api  this  api  in the sense of possibility  
ei sāta artha prathame karilum̐ vyākhyāne  
these  seven  meanings  first  I have given  in explanation

(147) In “*nirgranthā api*,” this *api* has the sense of possibility (*sambhāvanā*). These are the seven meanings that I have explained first.

অন্তর্য্যামি-উপাসকে ‘আত্মারাম’ কয় ৷  
সেই আত্মারাম যোগীর দুই ভেদ হয় ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

antaryāmi-upāsake ‘ātmārāma’ kaya  
the worshipper of the Antaryāmī  ātmārāma  is called  
sei ātmārāma yogīra dui bheda haya  
of those  ātmārāma  yogīs  two  kinds  there are

(148) One who worships the Antaryāmī, the Lord within the heart, is also called an *ātmārāma*, and these *ātmārāma* yogīs are of two kinds.

সগর্ভ, নিগর্ভ, — এই হয় দুই ভেদ ৷  
এক এক তিন ভেদে ছয় বিভেদ ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

sagarbha, nigarbha, — ei haya dui bheda  
sagarbha  nirgarbha  these  are  two  kinds  
eka eka tina bhede chaya vibheda  
each  one  in three  kinds  six  subdivisions

(149) *Sagarbha* and *nirgarbha* — these are the two kinds. Each of them is of three kinds, and so there are six divisions.[^35]

কেচিৎ স্বদেহান্তর্হৃদয়াবকাশে  
প্রাদেশমাত্রং পুরুষং বসন্তম্ ৷  
চতুর্ভুজং কঞ্জরথাঙ্গশঙ্খ-  
গদাধরং ধারণয়া স্মরন্তি ॥ ১৫০ ॥

kecit sva-dehāntar hṛdayāvakāśe  
some  of their own body  within  in the space of the heart  
prādeśa-mātraṁ puruṣaṁ vasantam  
a span in size  the Person  dwelling  
catur-bhujaṁ kañja-rathāṅga-śaṅkha-  
four-armed  lotus  disc  conch  
gadā-dharaṁ dhāraṇayā smaranti  
mace  holding  by concentration  they remember

(150) “Some, by concentration (*dhāraṇā*), remember the Person who dwells in the space of the heart within their own body: He is a span in size, four-armed, and holds the lotus, the disc, the conch and the mace.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.2.8)

এবং হরৌ ভগবতি প্রতিলব্ধভাবো  
ভক্ত্যা দ্রবদ্ধৃদয় উৎপুলকঃ প্রমোদাৎ ৷  
ঔৎকণ্ঠ্যবাষ্পকলয়া মুহুরর্দ্দ্যমান-  
স্তচ্চাপি চিত্তবড়িশং শনকৈর্বিয়ুঙ্ক্তে ॥ ১৫১ ॥

evaṁ harau bhagavati pratilabdha-bhāvo  
thus  for Hari  Bhagavān  having gained  bhāva  
bhaktyā dravad-dhṛdaya utpulakaḥ pramodāt  
by devotion  his heart melting  his hair standing on end  from joy  
autkaṇṭhya-bāṣpa-kalayā muhur ardyamānas  
of longing  of tears  by the stream  again and again  overwhelmed  
tac cāpi citta-baḍiśaṁ śanakair viyuṅkte  
that  and  also  of the mind  the hook  gradually  withdraws

(151) “Having thus gained *bhāva* for Bhagavān Hari, his heart melting with devotion and the hair of his body standing on end with joy, again and again overwhelmed by streams of tears of longing, the yogī gradually withdraws even the hook of his mind (from the object of meditation).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.28.34)

‘যোগারুরুক্ষু’, ‘যোগারূঢ়’, ‘প্রাপ্তসিদ্ধি’ আর ৷  
এই তিন ভেদে হয় ছয় প্রকার ॥ ১৫২ ॥

‘yogārurukṣu’, ‘yogārūḍha’, ‘prāpta-siddhi’ āra  
wishing to rise to yoga  risen to yoga  having attained perfection  and  
ei tina bhede haya chaya prakāra  
these  three  in divisions  there are  six  kinds

(152) *Yogārurukṣu* (one who wishes to rise to yoga), *yogārūḍha* (one who has risen to yoga) and *prāpta-siddhi* (one who has attained perfection): by these three divisions there are six kinds.

আরুরুক্ষোর্মুনের্যোগং কর্ম্ম কারণমুচ্যতে ৷  
যোগারূঢ়স্য তস্যৈব শমঃ কারণমুচ্যতে ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

ārurukṣor muner yogaṁ  
of the one wishing to ascend  of the sage  to yoga  
karma kāraṇam ucyate  
action  the means  is said to be  
yogārūḍhasya tasyaiva  
of one who has risen to yoga  of him  indeed  
śamaḥ kāraṇam ucyate  
tranquillity  the means  is said to be

(153) “For the sage who wishes to ascend to yoga, action is said to be the means; for that same sage, once he has risen to yoga, tranquillity (*śama*) is said to be the means.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.3)

যদা হি নেন্দ্রিয়ার্থেষু ন কর্ম্মস্বনুষজ্জতে ৷  
সর্ব্বসংকল্পসন্ন্যাসী যোগারূঢ়স্তদোচ্যতে ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

yadā hi nendriyārtheṣu  
when  indeed  not  to the objects of the senses  
na karmasv anuṣajjate  
not  to actions  he is attached  
sarva-saṅkalpa-sannyāsī  
of all  intentions  the renouncer  
yogārūḍhas tadocyate  
risen to yoga  then  he is called

(154) “When one is attached neither to the objects of the senses nor to actions, and has renounced all intentions (*saṅkalpa*), he is then called *yogārūḍha*, one who has risen to yoga.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.4)

এই ছয় যোগী সাধুসঙ্গাদি-হেতু পাঞা ৷  
কৃষ্ণ ভজে কৃষ্ণগুণে আকৃষ্ট হঞা ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

ei chaya yogī sādhu-saṅgādi-hetu pāñā  
these  six  yogīs  the company of sādhus and the like  the cause  obtaining  
kṛṣṇa bhaje kṛṣṇa-guṇe ākṛṣṭa hañā  
Kṛṣṇa  worship  by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  attracted  becoming

(155) Through the company of sādhus and other such causes, these six kinds of yogīs become attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and worship Him.

চ-শব্দে ‘অপি’র অর্থ ইহাও কহয় ৷  
‘মুনি’, ‘নির্গ্রন্থ’-শব্দের পূর্ব্ববৎ অর্থ হয় ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

ca-śabde ‘api’ra artha ihāo kahaya  
the word ca  of api  the meaning  this too  expresses  
‘muni’, ‘nirgrantha’-śabdera pūrvavat artha haya  
muni  nirgrantha  of the words  as before  the meaning  is

(156) Here too the word *ca* expresses the meaning of *api*, and the words *muni* and *nirgrantha* have the same meanings as before.

উরুক্রমে অহৈতুকী কাঁহা কোন অর্থ ৷  
এই তের অর্থ কহিলুঁ পরম সমর্থ ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

urukrame ahaitukī kām̐hā kona artha  
urukrame  ahaitukī  where  which  meaning  
ei tera artha kahilum̐ parama samartha  
these  thirteen  meanings  I have told  most  cogent

(157) The words *urukrame* and *ahaitukī* take whichever meaning fits each case. Thus I have told you thirteen most cogent meanings.[^36]

এই সব শান্ত যবে ভজে ভগবান্ ৷  
‘শান্ত’ ভক্ত করি’ তবে কহি তাঁর নাম ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

ei saba śānta yabe bhaje bhagavān  
these  all  śāntas  when  worship  Bhagavān  
‘śānta’ bhakta kari’ tabe kahi tām̐ra nāma  
śānta  devotees  considering them  then  I call  their  name

(158) When all these peaceful ones (*śānta*) worship Bhagavān, I then call them *śānta* devotees.

‘আত্মা’ শব্দে ‘মন’ কহে, — মনে যেই রমে ৷  
সাধুসঙ্গে সেই ভজে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচরণে ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

‘ātmā’ śabde ‘mana’ kahe, — mane yei rame  
by the word ātmā  the mind  is meant  in the mind  who  delights  
sādhu-saṅge sei bhaje śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇe  
in the company of sādhus  he  worships  Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(159) The word *ātmā* also means the mind (*mana*). One who delights in the mind worships Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s feet in the company of sādhus.

উদরমুপাসতে য ঋষিবর্ত্মসু কূর্পদৃশঃ  
পরিসরপদ্ধতিং হৃদয়মারুণয়ো দহরম্ ৷  
তত উদগাদনন্ত তব ধাম শিরঃ পরমং  
পুনরিহ যৎ সমেত্য ন পতন্তি কৃতান্তমুখে ॥ ১৬০ ॥

udaram upāsate ya ṛṣi-vartmasu kūrpa-dṛśaḥ  
the belly (udara)  worship  those who  on the paths of the ṛṣis  of gross vision  
parisara-paddhatiṁ hṛdayam āruṇayo daharam  
from which the channels branch out  the heart  the Āruṇis  subtle (dahara)  
tata udagād ananta tava dhāma śiraḥ paramaṁ  
from there  rises  O Ananta  Your  abode  the head  supreme  
punar iha yat sametya na patanti kṛtānta-mukhe  
again  here  which  having reached  not  they fall  of death  into the mouth

(160) “Following the paths of the ṛṣis, those of gross vision worship Brahman in the belly (*udara*), while the Āruṇis worship the subtle Brahman (*dahara*) in the heart, from which the channels branch out. From there, O Ananta, the path rises to the head, Your supreme abode; having reached it, they no longer fall into the jaws of death in this world.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.18)

এই কৃষ্ণগুণাকৃষ্ট মহামুনি হঞা ৷  
অহৈতুকী ভক্তি করে নির্গ্রন্থ হঞা ॥ ১৬১ ॥

ei kṛṣṇa-guṇākṛṣṭa mahā-muni hañā  
these  by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities attracted  great sages  becoming  
ahaitukī bhakti kare nirgrantha hañā  
causeless  devotion  render  nirgranthas  becoming

(161) Attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, they become great sages and, becoming *nirgrantha*, render causeless devotion.

‘আত্মা’-শব্দে ‘যত্ন’ কহে — যত্ন করিয়া ৷  
‘মুনয়োঽপি’ কৃষ্ণ ভজে নির্গ্রন্থ হঞা ॥ ১৬২ ॥

‘ātmā’-śabde ‘yatna’ kahe — yatna kariyā  
by the word ātmā  effort  is meant  effort  making  
‘munayo ’pi’ kṛṣṇa bhaje nirgrantha hañā  
sages  also  Kṛṣṇa  worship  nirgranthas  becoming

(162) The word *ātmā* also means effort (*yatna*). Making effort, even sages (*munayo ’pi*) become *nirgrantha* and worship Kṛṣṇa.

তস্যৈব হেতোঃ প্রযতেত কোবিদো  
ন লভ্যতে যদ্ভ্রমতামুপর্য্যধঃ ৷  
তল্লভ্যতে দুঃখবদন্যতঃ সুখং  
কালেন সর্ব্বত্র গভীর-রংহসা ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

tasyaiva hetoḥ prayateta kovido  
of that  indeed  for the sake  should strive  the wise  
na labhyate yad bhramatām upary adhaḥ  
not  is obtained  which  by those who wander  above  below  
tal labhyate duḥkha-vad anyataḥ sukhaṁ  
that  is obtained  like suffering  by other means  happiness  
kālena sarvatra gabhīra-raṁhasā  
by time  everywhere  of irresistible force

(163) “The wise should strive for that alone which is not obtained by those who wander through the worlds above and below. Happiness, like suffering, comes of itself everywhere, brought by time, whose course is irresistible.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.5.18)

সদ্ধর্ম্মস্যাববোধায় যেষাং নির্ব্বন্ধিনী মতিঃ ৷  
অচিরাদেব সর্ব্বার্থঃ সিধ্যত্যেষামভীপ্সিতঃ ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

sad-dharmasyāvabodhāya  
of true religion  for the understanding  
yeṣāṁ nirbandhinī matiḥ  
of whom  persistent  the mind  
acirād eva sarvārthaḥ  
soon  indeed  every aim  
sidhyaty eṣām abhīpsitaḥ  
is fulfilled  of those  desired

(164) “Those whose minds are set with perseverance on understanding true religion soon attain every aim they desire.” (Nārada-purāṇa; Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Pūrva-vibhāga, Sādhana-bhakti-laharī, 103)[^37]

চ শব্দে অপি-অর্থে, ‘অপি’ — অবধারণে ৷  
যত্নাগ্রহ বিনা ভক্তি না জন্মায় প্রেমে ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

ca śabde api-arthe, ‘api’ — avadhāraṇe  
by the word ca  in the sense of api  api  in the sense of certainty  
yatnāgraha vinā bhakti nā janmāya preme  
eager longing  without  bhakti  does not  give birth  to love

(165) The word *ca* has the sense of *api*, and *api* the sense of certainty (*avadhāraṇa*): without eager longing (*yatnāgraha*), bhakti does not give birth to love (prema).

সাধনৌঘৈরনাসঙ্গৈরলভ্যা সুচিরাদপি ৷  
হরিণা চাশ্বদেয়েতি দ্বিধা সা স্যাৎ সুদুর্ল্লভা ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

sādhanaughair anāsaṅgair  
by multitudes of sādhanas  without āsaṅga  
alabhyā su-cirād api  
unattainable  for a very long time  even  
hariṇā cāśv adeyeti  
by Hari  and  quickly  not given  thus  
dvidhā sā syāt su-durlabhā  
in two ways  it  is  very hard to attain

(166) “Bhakti is very hard to attain in two ways: by multitudes of *sādhanas* performed without *āsaṅga*, eager longing, it cannot be attained even in a very long time; and Hari does not give it quickly.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.1.35)

তেষাং সততযুক্তানাং ভজতাং প্রীতিপূর্ব্বকম্ ৷  
দদামি বুদ্ধিযোগং তং যেন মামুপয়ান্তি তে ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

teṣāṁ satata-yuktānāṁ  
to them  ever  longing (for Me)  
bhajatāṁ prīti-pūrvakam  
worshipping  with love  
dadāmi buddhi-yogaṁ taṁ  
I give  understanding  that  
yena mām upayānti te  
by which  to Me  come  they

(167) “To those who are ever devoted to Me and worship Me with love, I give the understanding that brings them to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 10.10)[^38]

‘আত্মা’-শব্দে ‘ধৃতি’ কহে, — ধৈর্য্যে যেই রমে ৷  
ধৈর্য্যবন্ত হঞা তবে করয় ভজনে ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

‘ātmā’-śabde ‘dhṛti’ kahe, — dhairye yei rame  
by the word ātmā  steadfastness  is meant  in steadfastness  who  delights  
dhairyavanta hañā tabe karaya bhajane  
steadfast  becoming  then  he performs  worship

(168) The word *ātmā* also means steadfastness (*dhṛti*). One who delights in steadfastness becomes steadfast and then worships Kṛṣṇa.

‘মুনি’-শব্দে — পক্ষী, ভৃঙ্গ; ‘নির্গ্রন্থে’ — মূর্খজন ৷  
কৃষ্ণকৃপায় সাধুকৃপায় দোঁহার ভজন ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

‘muni’-śabde — pakṣī, bhṛṅga; ‘nirgranthe’ — mūrkha-jana  
by the word muni  a bird  a bumblebee  by nirgrantha  a foolish  person  
kṛṣṇa-kṛpāya sādhu-kṛpāya dom̐hāra bhajana  
by Kṛṣṇa’s  mercy  by the sādhus’  mercy  of both  the worship

(169) The word *muni* also means a bird or a bumblebee, and *nirgrantha* a foolish person. By the mercy of Kṛṣṇa and the mercy of the sādhus, both of them worship Kṛṣṇa.

প্রায়ো বতাম্ব মুনয়ো বিহগা বনেঽস্মিন্  
কৃষ্ণেক্ষিতং তদুদিতং কলবেণুগীতম্ ৷  
আরুহ্য যে দ্রুমভুজান্ রুচিরপ্রবালান্  
শৃণ্বন্তি মীলিতদৃশো বিগতান্যবাচঃ ॥ ১৭০ ॥

prāyo batāmba munayo vihagā vane ‘smin  
almost  indeed  O mother  sages  the birds  in forest  this  
kṛṣṇekṣitaṁ tad-uditaṁ kala-veṇu-gītam  
to see Kṛṣṇa  sung by Him  sweet  of the flute  the song  
āruhya ye druma-bhujān rucira-pravālān  
having risen  who  onto the branches of the trees  with lovely shoots  
śṛṇvanti mīlita-dṛśo vigatānya-vācaḥ  
they hear  with eyes closed  all other sounds silenced

(170) “O mother, the birds of this forest are almost sages: they rise onto the branches of the trees, lovely with fresh shoots, to see Kṛṣṇa, and with eyes closed and all other sounds silenced, they listen to the sweet song of the flute that flows from Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.14)[^39]

এতেঽলিনস্তব যশোঽখিল-লোকতীর্থং  
গায়ন্ত আদিপুরুষানুপথং ভজন্তে ৷  
প্রায়ো অমী মুনিগণা ভবদীয়মুখ্যা  
গূঢ়ং বনেঽপি ন জহত্যনঘাত্মদৈবম্ ॥ ১৭১ ॥

ete ‘linas tava yaśo ‘khila-loka-tīrthaṁ  
these  bees  Your  glory  for all  the worlds  a holy place  
gāyanta ādi-puruṣānupathaṁ bhajante  
singing  O original Person  following Your path  serve  
prāyo amī muni-gaṇā bhavadīya-mukhyā  
probably  these  sages  Your  foremost (devotees)  
gūḍhaṁ vane ‘pi na jahaty anaghātma-daivam  
hidden  in the forest  though  not  they leave  O sinless one  their own deity

(171) (Śrī Kṛṣṇa said to Balarāma:) “O original Person, these bees sing Your glory, which purifies all the worlds, and serve You by following You along Your path. They must be sages, the foremost of Your devotees: though You are hidden in the forest, O sinless one, they do not leave You, the Lord of their hearts.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.15.6)

সরসি সারসহংসবিহঙ্গা-  
শ্চারুগীতহৃতচেতস এত্য ৷  
হরিমুপাসত তে যতচিত্তা  
হন্ত মীলিতদৃশো ধৃতমৌনাঃ ॥ ১৭২ ॥

sarasi sārasa-haṁsa-vihaṅgāś  
in the lake  cranes  swans  birds  
cāru-gīta-hṛta-cetasa etya  
by the lovely  song  stolen  their hearts  approaching  
harim upāsata te yata-cittā  
Hari  worshipped  they  with minds controlled  
hanta mīlita-dṛśo dhṛta-maunāḥ  
ah  with eyes closed  keeping silence

(172) “The cranes, swans and other birds of the lake, their hearts stolen by His lovely song, came near and, with minds controlled — ah! — with eyes closed and keeping silence, worshipped Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.35.11)

কিরাতহূনান্ধ্রপুলিন্দপুক্কশা  
আভীরশুম্ভা যবনাঃ খশাদয়ঃ ৷  
যেঽন্যে চ পাপা যদুপাশ্রয়াশ্রয়াঃ  
শুদ্ধ্যন্তি তস্মৈ প্রভবিষ্ণবে নমঃ ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

kirāta-hūnāndhra-pulinda-pukkaśā  
Kirātas  Hūṇas  Andhras  Pulindas  Pukkaśas  
ābhīra-śumbhā yavanāḥ khaśādayaḥ  
Ābhīras  Śuhmas  Yavanas  Khaśas and others  
ye ‘nye ca pāpā yad-upāśrayāśrayāḥ  
who  others  and  sinful  of those who have taken shelter of Him  taking shelter  
śuddhyanti tasmai prabhaviṣṇave namaḥ  
are purified  to Him  the all-powerful  obeisance

(173) “The Kirātas, Hūṇas, Andhras, Pulindas, Pukkaśas, Ābhīras, Śuhmas, Yavanas, Khaśas and other tribes, and all other sinful people as well, are purified by taking shelter of those who have taken shelter of Him. Obeisance to that all-powerful Lord!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.4.18)

কিম্বা ‘ধৃতি’-শব্দে নিজপূর্ণতাদি-জ্ঞান কয় ৷  
দুঃখাভাবে উত্তমপ্রাপ্ত্যে মহাপূর্ণ হয় ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

kimvā ‘dhṛti’-śabde nija-pūrṇatādi-jñāna kaya  
or  by the word dhṛti  one’s own  fullness and so on  awareness  is meant  
duḥkhābhāve uttama-prāptye mahā-pūrṇa haya  
by the absence of suffering  of the highest  by the attainment  completely full  one becomes

(174) Or the word *dhṛti* means the awareness of one’s own fullness: through the absence of suffering and the attainment of the highest, one becomes completely full.[^40]

ধৃতিঃ স্যাৎ পূর্ণতা-জ্ঞানং দুঃখাভাবোত্তমাপ্তিভিঃ ৷  
অপ্রাপ্তাতীতনষ্টার্থানভিসংশোচনাদিকৃৎ ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

dhṛtiḥ syāt pūrṇatā-jñānaṁ  
dhṛti  is  of fullness  the awareness  
duḥkhābhāvottamāptibhiḥ  
through the absence of suffering  through the attainment of the highest  
aprāptātīta-naṣṭārthā-  
for what is not attained  past  lost  objects  
nabhisaṁśocanādi-kṛt  
absence of lamentation and so on  producing

(175) “*Dhṛti* is the awareness of fullness that comes from the absence of suffering and the attainment of the highest. It frees one from lamenting over objects not attained, past or lost.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.4.144)

কৃষ্ণভক্ত — দুঃখহীন, বাঞ্ছান্তর-হীন ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেম-সেবা-পূর্ণানন্দ-প্রবীণ ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhakta — duḥkha-hīna, vāñchāntara-hīna  
of Kṛṣṇa  the devotee  free from suffering  free from other desires  
kṛṣṇa-prema-sevā-pūrṇānanda-pravīṇa  
to Kṛṣṇa  in love  in service  in full bliss  versed

(176) A devotee of Kṛṣṇa is free from suffering and free from other desires; he is versed in the full bliss of loving service to Kṛṣṇa.

মৎসেবয়া প্রতীতং তে সালোক্যাদিচতুষ্টয়ম্ ৷  
নেচ্ছন্তি সেবয়া পূর্ণাঃ কুতোঽন্যৎ কালবিপ্লুতম্ ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

mat-sevayā pratītaṁ te  
by My service  attained  they  
sālokyādi-catuṣṭayam  
sālokya and the rest  the fourfold  
necchanti sevayā pūrṇāḥ  
do not desire  by service  fulfilled  
kuto ‘nyat kāla-viplutam  
how much less  anything else  destroyed by time

(177) “Fulfilled by serving Me, My devotees do not desire even the four kinds of liberation — *sālokya*, *sārūpya*, *sāmīpya* and *sārṣṭi* — that such service brings them of itself. How then would they seek worldly pleasures, or merging in Brahman? For all that is swept away by time.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.4.67)[^41]

হৃষীকেশে হৃষীকাণি যস্য স্থৈর্য্যগতানি হি ৷  
স এব ধৈর্য্যমাপ্নোতি সংসারে জীবচঞ্চলে ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

hṛṣīkeśe hṛṣīkāṇi  
in Hṛṣīkeśa  the senses  
yasya sthairya-gatāni hi  
whose  steadiness  have attained  indeed  
sa eva dhairyam āpnoti  
he  alone  steadfastness  attains  
saṁsāre jīva-cañcale  
in saṁsāra  where living beings are unsteady

(178) “In this world of birth and death (*saṁsāra*), where all living beings are unsteady, he alone attains steadfastness whose senses have become steady in Hṛṣīkeśa, the Lord of the senses.” (Words of the Gosvāmipāda)[^42]

‘চ’ — অবধারণে, ইহা ‘অপি’ — সমুচ্চয়ে ৷  
ধৃতিমন্ত হঞা ভজে পক্ষী-মূর্খ-চয়ে ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

‘ca’ — avadhāraṇe, ihā ‘api’ — samuccaye  
ca  in the sense of certainty  here  api  in the sense of conjunction  
dhṛtimanta hañā bhaje pakṣī-mūrkha-caye  
steadfast  becoming  worship  of birds  of fools  the multitude

(179) Here *ca* has the sense of certainty (*avadhāraṇa*), and *api* the sense of conjunction (*samuccaya*): birds and foolish people in great numbers become steadfast and worship Kṛṣṇa.

‘আত্মা’-শব্দে ‘বুদ্ধি’ কহে বুদ্ধিবিশেষ ৷  
সামান্যবুদ্ধিযুক্ত যত জীব অশেষ ॥ ১৮০ ॥

‘ātmā’-śabde ‘buddhi’ kahe buddhi-viśeṣa  
by the word ātmā  intelligence  is meant  special intelligence  
sāmānya-buddhi-yukta yata jīva aśeṣa  
with ordinary  intelligence  endowed  all  living beings  countless

(180) The word *ātmā* also means intelligence (*buddhi*), that is, special intelligence; and all the countless living beings are endowed with ordinary intelligence.

বুদ্ধ্যে রমে আত্মারাম — দুই ত’ প্রকার ৷  
‘পণ্ডিত’ মুনিগণ, নির্গ্রন্থ ‘মূর্খ’ আর ॥ ১৮১ ॥

buddhye rame ātmārāma — dui ta’ prakāra  
in intelligence  delights  ātmārāma  of two  indeed  kinds  
‘paṇḍita’ muni-gaṇa, nirgrantha ‘mūrkha’ āra  
learned  the sages  the nirgranthas  foolish  and

(181) The *ātmārāma* who delights in intelligence is of two kinds: the sages, who are learned (*paṇḍita*), and the *nirgranthas*, who are foolish (*mūrkha*).

কৃষ্ণকৃপায় সাধুসঙ্গে রতি-বুদ্ধি পায় ৷  
সব ছাড়ি’ কৃষ্ণভক্তি শুদ্ধবুদ্ধ্যে পায় ॥ ১৮২ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kṛpāya sādhu-saṅge rati-buddhi pāya  
by Kṛṣṇa’s  mercy  in the company of sādhus  attachment (rati)  intelligence  obtains  
saba chāḍi’ kṛṣṇa-bhakti śuddha-buddhye pāya  
everything  giving up  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  with pure intelligence  attains

(182) By Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, in the company of sādhus, one gains attachment (*rati*) and good intelligence. Giving up everything, with pure intelligence one attains devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

অহং সর্ব্বস্য প্রভবো মত্তঃ সর্ব্বং প্রবর্ত্ততে ৷  
ইতি মত্বা ভজন্তে মাং বুধা ভাবসমন্বিতাঃ ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

ahaṁ sarvasya prabhavo  
I  of everything  the source  
mattaḥ sarvaṁ pravartate  
from Me  everything  proceeds  
iti matvā bhajante māṁ  
thus  knowing  worship  Me  
budhā bhāva-samanvitāḥ  
the wise  with bhāva  endowed

(183) “I am the source of everything; everything proceeds from Me. Knowing this, the wise, endowed with *bhāva*, worship Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 10.8)[^43]

তে বৈ বিদন্ত্যতিতরন্তি চ দেবমায়াং  
স্ত্রীশূদ্রহূনশবরা অপি পাপজীবাঃ ৷  
যদ্যদ্ভুতক্রমপরায়ণ-শীল-শিক্ষা-  
স্তির্য্যগ্ জনা অপি কিমু শ্রুতধারণা যে ॥ ১৮৪ ॥

te vai vidanty atitaranti ca deva-māyāṁ  
they  indeed  know  cross beyond  and  the divine  māyā  
strī-śūdra-hūna-śabarā api pāpa-jīvāḥ  
women  śūdras  Hūṇas  Śabaras  even  sinful beings  
yady adbhuta-krama-parāyaṇa-śīla-śikṣās  
if  of Adbhuta-krama (Urukrama)  of those devoted  in the conduct  trained  
tiryag janā api kim u śruta-dhāraṇā ye  
animal  beings  even  what  then  holding to the Śruti  those who

(184) “Women, *śūdras*, Hūṇas, Śabaras and other sinful beings — even animals and birds — come to know (the Lord) and cross beyond the divine māyā if they learn the conduct of those devoted to Adbhuta-krama, the Lord of wondrous deeds. What then to speak of those who hold to the Śruti!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.7.46)

বিচার করিয়া যবে ভজে কৃষ্ণ-পায় ৷  
সেই বুদ্ধি দেন তাঁরে, যাতে কৃষ্ণ পায় ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

vicāra kariyā yabe bhaje kṛṣṇa-pāya  
having reflected  when  one worships  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  
sei buddhi dena tām̐re, yāte kṛṣṇa pāya  
that  intelligence  He gives  to him  by which  Kṛṣṇa  he attains

(185) When, after careful reflection, one worships Kṛṣṇa’s feet, Kṛṣṇa gives him the intelligence by which he attains Him.

তেষাং সততযুক্তানাং ভজতাং প্রীতিপূর্ব্বকম্ ৷  
দদামি বুদ্ধিযোগং তং যেন মামুপয়ান্তি তে ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

teṣāṁ satata-yuktānāṁ  
to them  ever  longing (for Me)  
bhajatāṁ prīti-pūrvakam  
worshipping  with love  
dadāmi buddhi-yogaṁ taṁ  
I give  understanding  that  
yena mām upayānti te  
by which  to Me  come  they

(186) “To those who are ever devoted to Me and worship Me with love, I give the understanding that brings them to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 10.10)[^44]

সৎসঙ্গ, কৃষ্ণসেবা, ভাগবত, নাম ৷  
ব্রজে বাস, — এই পঞ্চসাধন প্রধান ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

sat-saṅga, kṛṣṇa-sevā, bhāgavata, nāma  
the company of saints  to Kṛṣṇa  service  the Bhāgavata  the Name  
vraje vāsa, — ei pañca sādhana pradhāna  
in Vraja  living  these  five  sādhanas  principal

(187) The company of saints, service to Kṛṣṇa, the *Bhāgavata*, the holy Name and living in Vraja — these five are the principal *sādhanas*.

এই পঞ্চ-মধ্যে এক ‘স্বল্প’ যদি হয় ৷  
সুবুদ্ধি জনের হয় কৃষ্ণপ্রেমোদয় ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

ei-pañca-madhye eka ‘svalpa’ yadi haya  
of these five  among  one  slight  if  there is  
subuddhi janera haya kṛṣṇa-premodaya  
of a sensible  person  there comes  for Kṛṣṇa  of love  the awakening

(188) If a sensible person has even the slightest connection with one of these five, love for Kṛṣṇa awakens in him.

দুরূহাদ্ভুতবীর্য্যেঽস্মিন্ শ্রদ্ধা দূরেঽস্তু পঞ্চকে ৷  
যত্র স্বল্পোঽপি সম্বন্ধঃ সদ্ধিয়াং ভাবজন্মনে ॥ ১৮৯ ॥

durūhādbhuta-vīrye ‘smin  
hard to grasp  wondrous  in power  in this  
śraddhā dūre ‘stu pañcake  
faith  far away  let it be  in the group of five  
yatra sv-alpo ‘pi sambandhaḥ  
where  slight  even  connection  
sad-dhiyāṁ bhāva-janmane  
for those of good understanding  for the birth of bhāva

(189) “The power of these five limbs is wondrous and hard to grasp. Let alone faith in them: even the slightest connection with them gives birth to *bhāva* in those of good understanding, who are free from offence.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Pūrva-vibhāga, Sādhana-bhakti-laharī, 238)[^45]

উদার মহতী যাঁর সর্ব্বোত্তমা বুদ্ধি ৷  
নানা কামে ভজে, তবু পায় ভক্তিসিদ্ধি ॥ ১৯০ ॥

udāra mahatī yām̐ra sarvottamā buddhi  
broad  great  whose  best of all  intelligence  
nānā kāme bhaje, tabu pāya bhakti-siddhi  
with various  desires  worships  still  attains  of bhakti  perfection

(190) One whose intelligence is broad, great and best of all may worship with various desires, yet he still attains perfection in bhakti.

অকামঃ সর্ব্বকামো বা মোক্ষকাম উদারধীঃ ৷  
তীব্রেণ ভক্তিযোগেন যজেত পুরুষং পরম্ ॥ ১৯১ ॥

akāmaḥ sarva-kāmo vā  
without desire  full of all desires  or  
mokṣa-kāma udāra-dhīḥ  
desiring liberation  of broad intelligence  
tīvreṇa bhakti-yogena  
with intense  bhakti-yoga  
yajeta puruṣaṁ param  
should worship  the Person  Supreme

(191) “Whether he is without desire, full of every desire, or desirous of liberation, a person of broad intelligence should worship the Supreme Person with intense *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.10)[^46]

ভক্তি-প্রভাব, — সেই কাম ছাড়াঞা ৷  
কৃষ্ণপদে ভক্তি করায় গুণে আকর্ষিয়া ॥ ১৯২ ॥

bhakti-prabhāva, — sei kāma chāḍāñā  
of bhakti  the power  those  desires  making one give up  
kṛṣṇa-pade bhakti karāya guṇe ākarṣiyā  
to Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  devotion  makes one render  by His qualities  attracting

(192) Such is the power of bhakti: it makes one give up those desires and, attracting one by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, makes one render devotion to His feet.

সত্যং দিশত্যর্থিতমর্থিতো নৃণাং  
নৈবার্থদো যৎ পুনরর্থিতা যতঃ ৷  
স্বয়ং বিধত্তে ভজতামনিচ্ছতা-  
মিচ্ছা-পিধানং নিজপাদপল্লবম্ ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

satyaṁ diśaty arthitam arthito nṛṇāṁ  
truly  He grants  what is asked  when asked  to men  
naivārtha-do yat punar arthitā yataḥ  
not indeed  giver of that  which  again  asking  from which  
svayaṁ vidhatte bhajatām anicchatām  
Himself  He grants  to those who worship  though not desiring  
icchā-pidhānaṁ nija-pāda-pallavam  
covering all desires  His own  feet like tender shoots

(193) “It is true that when men ask, the Lord grants what they ask for; but He does not give what would make them ask again. To those who worship Him, though they do not desire it, He Himself gives His own feet, tender as new shoots, which put an end to all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.19.26)[^47]

‘আত্মা’-শব্দে ‘স্বভাব’ কহে, তাতে যেই রমে ৷  
আত্মারাম জীব যত স্থাবর-জঙ্গমে ॥ ১৯৪ ॥

‘ātmā’-śabde ‘svabhāva’ kahe, tāte yei rame  
by the word ātmā  nature  is meant  in it  who  delights  
ātmārāma jīva yata sthāvara-jaṅgame  
ātmārāmas  living beings  all  stationary  moving

(194) The word *ātmā* also means one’s nature (*svabhāva*), and one who delights in it is an *ātmārāma*. Thus all living beings, stationary and moving, are *ātmārāmas*.

জীবের স্বভাব — কৃষ্ণে ‘দাস’-অভিমান ৷  
দেহে আত্ম-জ্ঞানে আচ্ছাদিত সেই ‘জ্ঞান’ ॥ ১৯৫ ॥

jīvera svabhāva — kṛṣṇe ‘dāsa’-abhimāna  
of the jīva  the nature  toward Kṛṣṇa  servant  self-conception  
dehe ātma-jñāne ācchādita sei ‘jñāna’  
in the body  by the notion of self  covered  that  awareness

(195) The nature of the jīva is to know himself as Kṛṣṇa’s servant; that awareness becomes covered when he takes the body to be the self.[^48]

চ-শব্দে ‘এব’, ‘অপি’-শব্দ সমুচ্চয়ে ৷  
‘আত্মারামা এব’ হঞা শ্রীকৃষ্ণ ভজয়ে ॥ ১৯৬ ॥

ca-śabde ‘eva’, ‘api’-śabda samuccaye  
by the word ca  eva  api  the word  in the sense of conjunction  
‘ātmārāmā eva’ hañā śrī-kṛṣṇa bhajaye  
ātmārāmas  indeed  being  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  worship

(196) Here *ca* means *eva* (“indeed”), and *api* has the sense of conjunction (*samuccaya*): it is precisely as *ātmārāmas* (*ātmārāmā eva*) that they worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

এই জীব — সনকাদি সব মুনিজন ৷  
‘নির্গ্রন্থ’ — মূর্খ, নীচ, স্থাবর-জঙ্গম ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

ei jīva — sanakādi saba muni-jana  
these  jīvas  Sanaka and the others  all  sages  
‘nirgrantha’ — mūrkha, nīca, sthāvara-jaṅgama  
the nirgranthas  the foolish  the low  stationary  moving

(197) These jīvas are all the sages (*munis*), such as Sanaka and the others; and the *nirgranthas* are the foolish, the low, and the stationary and moving beings.

ব্যাস-শুক-সনকাদির প্রসিদ্ধ ভজন ৷  
‘নির্গ্রন্থ’ স্থাবরাদির শুন বিবরণ ॥ ১৯৮ ॥

vyāsa-śuka-sanakādira prasiddha bhajana  
of Vyāsa  of Śuka  of Sanaka and the others  well known  the worship  
‘nirgrantha’ sthāvarādira śuna vivaraṇa  
of the nirgranthas  of the stationary and the rest  hear  the account

(198) The worship of Vyāsa, Śuka, Sanaka and the others is well known. Now hear the account of the *nirgranthas* — the stationary beings and the rest.

কৃষ্ণকৃপাদি-হেতু হৈতে স্বভাব উদয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণগুণাকৃষ্ট হঞা তাঁহারে ভজয় ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kṛpādi-hetu haite svabhāva udaya  
Kṛṣṇa’s  mercy and other  causes  from  nature  awakens  
kṛṣṇa-guṇākṛṣṭa hañā tām̐hāre bhajaya  
by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities attracted  becoming  Him  they worship

(199) Through such causes as Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, their true nature (*svabhāva*) awakens, and, attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, they worship Him.

ধন্যেয়মদ্য ধরণী তৃণ-বীরুধস্ত্বৎ-  
পাদস্পৃশো দ্রুমলতাঃ করজাভিমৃষ্টাঃ ৷  
নদ্যোঽদ্রয়ঃ খগমৃগাঃ সদয়াবলোকৈ-  
র্গোপ্যোঽন্তরেণ ভুজয়োরপি যৎস্পৃহা শ্রীঃ ॥ ২০০ ॥

dhanyeyam adya dharaṇī tṛṇa-vīrudhas tvat-  
blessed  this  today  the earth  grasses  shrubs  Your  
pāda-spṛśo druma-latāḥ karajābhimṛṣṭāḥ  
feet  touching  trees  creepers  touched by Your fingers  
nadyo ‘drayaḥ khaga-mṛgāḥ sadayāvalokair  
rivers  mountains  birds  beasts  by Your merciful glances  
gopyo ‘ntareṇa bhujayor api yat-spṛhā śrīḥ  
the gopīs  between  Your arms  even  longed for by whom  Śrī

(200) “Today this earth is blessed; blessed are the grasses and shrubs that touch Your feet, the trees and creepers touched by Your fingers, the rivers, mountains, birds and beasts on whom You cast Your merciful glances, and the gopīs held between Your arms — a place for which even Śrī (Lakṣmī) longs.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.15.8)

গা গোপকৈরনুবনং নয়তোরুদার-  
বেণুস্বনৈঃ কলপদৈস্তনুভৃৎসু সখ্যঃ ৷  
অস্পন্দনং গতিমতাং পুলকস্তরূণাং  
নির্য্যোগপাশকৃতলক্ষণয়োর্বিচিত্রম্ ॥ ২০১ ॥

gā gopakair anu-vanaṁ nayator udāra-  
the cows  with the cowherd boys  from forest to forest  of the two who lead  generous  
veṇu-svanaiḥ kala-padais tanu-bhṛtsu sakhyaḥ  
of the flute  by the sounds  by the sweet notes  among embodied beings  O friends  
aspandanaṁ gatimatāṁ pulakas tarūṇāṁ  
stillness  of moving beings  thrill  of the trees  
niryoga-pāśa-kṛta-lakṣaṇayor vicitram  
marked by ropes and nooses  of the two  wondrous

(201) “O friends, as the two Brothers, marked by the ropes and nooses for tying the cows, lead the cows with the cowherd boys from forest to forest, the generous, sweet notes of Their flutes work wonders among embodied beings: those that move become still, and the trees thrill with ecstasy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.19)

বনলতাস্তরব আত্মনি বিষ্ণুং  
ব্যঞ্জয়ন্ত্য ইব পুষ্পফলাঢ়্যাঃ ৷  
প্রণতভারবিটপা মধুধারাঃ  
প্রেমহৃষ্টতনবো ববৃষুঃ স্ম ॥ ২০২ ॥

vana-latās tarava ātmani viṣṇuṁ  
the forest  creepers  the trees  within themselves  Viṣṇu  
vyañjayantya iva puṣpa-phalāḍhyāḥ  
revealing  as if  with flowers  with fruits  rich  
praṇata-bhāra-viṭapā madhu-dhārāḥ  
bowed  with the weight  their branches  of honey  streams  
prema-hṛṣṭa-tanavo vavṛṣuḥ sma  
with love  thrilled  bodies  showered  indeed

(202) “The forest creepers and trees, rich with flowers and fruits, their branches bowed with the weight and their bodies thrilled with love, poured down streams of honey, as if revealing Viṣṇu within themselves.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.35.9)[^49]

কিরাতহূনান্ধ্র-পুলিন্দ পুক্কশাঃ  
আভীরশুম্ভা যবনাঃ খশাদয়ঃ ৷  
যেঽন্যে চ পাপা যদুপাশ্রয়াশ্রয়াঃ  
শুদ্ধ্যন্তি তস্মৈ প্রভবিষ্ণবে নমঃ ॥ ২০৩ ॥

kirāta-hūnāndhra-pulinda pukkaśāḥ  
Kirātas  Hūṇas  Andhras  Pulindas  Pukkaśas  
ābhīra-śumbhā yavanāḥ khaśādayaḥ  
Ābhīras  Śuhmas  Yavanas  Khaśas and others  
ye ‘nye ca pāpā yad-upāśrayāśrayāḥ  
who  others  and  sinful  of those who have taken shelter of Him  taking shelter  
śuddhyanti tasmai prabhaviṣṇave namaḥ  
are purified  to Him  the all-powerful  obeisance

(203) “The Kirātas, Hūṇas, Andhras, Pulindas, Pukkaśas, Ābhīras, Śuhmas, Yavanas, Khaśas and other tribes, and all other sinful people as well, are purified by taking shelter of those who have taken shelter of Him. Obeisance to that all-powerful Lord!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.4.18)[^50]

আগে ‘তের’ অর্থ করিলুঁ, আর ‘ছয়’ এই ৷  
ঊনবিংশতি অর্থ হইল মিলি’ এই দুই ॥ ২০৪ ॥

āge ‘tera’ artha karilum̐, āra ‘chaya’ ei  
before  thirteen  meanings  I gave  and  six  these  
ūnaviṁśati artha haila mili’ ei dui  
nineteen  meanings  there are  combined  these  two

(204) Earlier I gave thirteen meanings, and now these six: together they make nineteen meanings.[^51]

এই ঊনিশ অর্থ করিলুঁ, আগে শুন আর ৷  
‘আত্মা’-শব্দে ‘দেহ’ কহে, — চারি অর্থ তার ॥ ২০৫ ॥

ei ūniśa artha karilum̐, āge śuna āra  
these  nineteen  meanings  I have given  further  hear  more  
‘ātmā’-śabde ‘deha’ kahe, — cāri artha tāra  
by the word ātmā  the body  is meant  four  meanings  of it

(205) I have given these nineteen meanings; now hear more. The word *ātmā* also means the body (*deha*), and this gives four meanings.

দেহারামী দেহে ভজে ‘দেহোপাধি ব্রহ্ম’ ৷  
সৎসঙ্গে সেহ করে কৃষ্ণের ভজন ॥ ২০৬ ॥

dehārāmī dehe bhaje ‘dehopādhi brahma’  
one who delights in the body  in the body  worships  conditioned by the body  Brahman  
sat-saṅge seha kare kṛṣṇera bhajana  
in the company of saints  he too  performs  of Kṛṣṇa  worship

(206) One who delights in the body (*dehārāmī*) worships in the body a Brahman conditioned by the body; in the company of saints, he too comes to worship Kṛṣṇa.

উদরমুপাসতে য ঋষিবর্ত্মসু কূর্পদৃশঃ  
পরিসরপদ্ধতিং হৃদয়মারুণয়ো দহরম্ ৷  
তত উদগাদনন্ত তব ধাম শিরঃ পরমং  
পুনরিহ যৎ সমেত্য ন পতন্তি কৃতান্তমুখে ॥ ২০৭ ॥

udaram upāsate ya ṛṣi-vartmasu kūrpa-dṛśaḥ  
the belly (udara)  worship  those who  on the paths of the ṛṣis  of gross vision  
parisara-paddhatiṁ hṛdayam āruṇayo daharam  
from which the channels branch out  the heart  the Āruṇis  subtle (dahara)  
tata udagād ananta tava dhāma śiraḥ paramaṁ  
from there  rises  O Ananta  Your  abode  the head  supreme  
punar iha yat sametya na patanti kṛtānta-mukhe  
again  here  which  having reached  not  they fall  of death  into the mouth

(207) “Those of gross vision who follow the paths of the ṛṣis worship (Brahman in) the belly (*udara*); the Āruṇis worship (Brahman, the) subtle (*dahara*), in the heart, from which the channels branch out. From there, O Ananta, (the path) rises to the head, Your supreme abode; having reached it, they do not fall again into the jaws of death in this world.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.18)

দেহারামী কর্ম্মনিষ্ঠ — যাজ্ঞিকাদি জন ৷  
সৎসঙ্গে ‘কর্ম্ম’ ত্যজি’ করয় ভজন ॥ ২০৮ ॥

dehārāmī karma-niṣṭha — yājñikādi jana  
those who delight in the body  devoted to karma  performers of sacrifice and the like  people  
sat-saṅge ‘karma’ tyaji’ karaya bhajana  
in the company of saints  karma  giving up  perform  worship

(208) Those who delight in the body and are devoted to karma — performers of sacrifice and the like — give up karma in the company of saints and worship (Kṛṣṇa).

কর্ম্মণ্যস্মিন্ননাশ্বাসে ধূমধূম্রাত্মনাং ভবান্ ৷  
আপায়য়তি গোবিন্দপাদপদ্মাসবং মধু ॥ ২০৯ ॥

karmaṇy asminn anāśvāse  
in work  this  unreliable  
dhūma-dhūmrātmanāṁ bhavān  
by smoke  blackened in body  you  
āpāyayati govinda-  
give to drink  of Govinda  
pāda-padmāsavaṁ madhu  
of the feet  lotus  the nectar (āsava)  honeyed

(209) “In this sacrificial work, whose fruit is never certain, we have been blackened by the smoke; but you give us to drink the honeyed nectar (*āsava*) of Govinda’s lotus feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.18.12)

‘তপস্বী’ প্রভৃতি যত দেহারামী হয় ৷  
সাধুসঙ্গে তপ ছাড়ি’ শ্রীকৃষ্ণ ভজয় ॥ ২১০ ॥

‘tapasvī’ prabhṛti yata dehārāmī haya  
ascetics  and the like  all  who delight in the body  are  
sādhu-saṅge tapa chāḍi’ śrī-kṛṣṇa bhajaya  
in the company of sādhus  austerity  giving up  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  worship

(210) Ascetics (*tapasvī*) and the like are all *dehārāmīs*, those who delight in the body; in the company of sādhus they give up austerity and worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

যৎপাদসেবাভিরুচিস্তপস্বিনা-  
মশেষজন্মোপচিতং মলং ধিয়ঃ ৷  
সদ্যঃ ক্ষিণোত্যন্বহমেধতী সতী  
যথা পদাঙ্গুষ্ঠবিনিঃসৃতা সরিৎ ॥ ২১১ ॥

yat-pāda-sevābhirucis tapasvinām  
whose  of the feet  for the service  taste  of ascetics  
aśeṣa-janmopacitaṁ malaṁ dhiyaḥ  
in countless  births  accumulated  the impurity  of the mind  
sadyaḥ kṣiṇoty anv-aham edhatī satī  
at once  destroys  day by day  increasing  being  
yathā padāṅguṣṭha-viniḥsṛtā sarit  
as  from the toe of His foot  flowing  the river

(211) “The taste for serving His feet, increasing day by day, at once destroys the impurity that has gathered in the minds of ascetics through countless births, as does the river (Gaṅgā) that flows from the toe of His foot.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 4.21.31)

দেহারামী, সর্ব্বকাম — সব আত্মারাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণকৃপায় কৃষ্ণ ভজে ছাড়ি’ সব কাম ॥ ২১২ ॥

dehārāmī, sarva-kāma — saba ātmārāma  
those who delight in the body  full of desires  all  ātmārāmas  
kṛṣṇa-kṛpāya kṛṣṇa bhaje chāḍi’ saba kāma  
by Kṛṣṇa’s  mercy  Kṛṣṇa  worship  giving up  all  desires

(212) Those who delight in the body and are full of desires are all *ātmārāmas*; by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy they give up all desires and worship Kṛṣṇa.

স্থানাভিলাষী তপসি স্থিতোঽহং  
ত্বাং প্রাপ্তবান্ দেবমুনীন্দ্রগুহ্যম্ ৷  
কাচং বিচিন্বন্নপি দিব্যরত্নং  
স্বামিন্ কৃতার্থোঽস্মি বরং ন যাচে ॥ ২১৩ ॥

sthānābhilāṣī tapasi sthito ‘haṁ  
desiring a position  in austerity  engaged  I  
tvāṁ prāptavān deva-munīndra-guhyam  
You  have attained  hidden from the gods and the best of sages  
kācaṁ vicinvann api divya-ratnaṁ  
glass  searching for  though  a divine jewel  
svāmin kṛtārtho ‘smi varaṁ na yāce  
O Master  fulfilled  I am  a boon  not  I ask

(213) “Desiring a high position, I engaged in austerities, and I have attained You, who are hidden even from the gods and the greatest sages. I was searching for a piece of glass and found a divine jewel. O Master, my purpose is fulfilled; I ask for no boon.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 7.28)

এই চারি অর্থ সহ হইল ‘তেইশ’ অর্থ ৷  
আর তিন অর্থ শুন পরম সমর্থ ॥ ২১৪ ॥

ei cāri artha saha haila ‘teiśa’ artha  
these  four  meanings  together with  there are  twenty-three  meanings  
āra tina artha śuna parama samartha  
further  three  meanings  hear  most  cogent

(214) Together with these four, there are twenty-three meanings. Now hear three more most cogent meanings.

চ-শব্দে ‘সমুচ্চয়ে’, আর অর্থ কয় ৷  
‘আত্মারামাশ্চ মুনয়শ্চ’ কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ২১৫ ॥

ca-śabde ‘samuccaye’, āra artha kaya  
the word ca  in the sense of conjunction  another  meaning  expresses  
‘ātmārāmāś ca munayaś ca’ kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
ātmārāmas  and  sages  and  Kṛṣṇa  worship

(215) The word *ca* in the sense of conjunction (*samuccaya*) gives another meaning: “the *ātmārāmas* and the sages (*ātmārāmāś ca munayaś ca*)” worship Kṛṣṇa.

‘নির্গ্রন্থাঃ’ হঞা ইঁহা ‘অপি’ — নির্দ্ধারণে ৷  
‘রামশ্চ কৃষ্ণশ্চ’ যথা বিহরয়ে বনে ॥ ২১৬ ॥

‘nirgranthāḥ’ hañā im̐hā ‘api’ — nirdhāraṇe  
nirgranthas  becoming  here  api  in the sense of specification  
‘rāmaś ca kṛṣṇaś ca’ yathā viharaye vane  
Rāma  and  Kṛṣṇa  and  as  roam  in the forest

(216) “Becoming *nirgranthas*”: here *api* has the sense of specification (*nirdhāraṇa*), as in “*rāmaś ca kṛṣṇaś ca* roam in the forest.”

চ-শব্দে ‘অন্বাচয়ে’ অর্থ কহে আর ৷  
‘বটো, ভিক্ষামট, গাঞ্চানয়’ যৈছে প্রকার ॥ ২১৭ ॥

ca-śabde ‘anvācaye’ artha kahe āra  
the word ca  in the sense of anvācaya  a meaning  expresses  another  
’baṭo, bhikṣām aṭa, gāṁ cānaya’ yaiche prakāra  
O boy  for alms  go  the cow  and bring  as  in the manner

(217) The word *ca* gives yet another meaning in the sense of *anvācaya* — adding something secondary to what is principal — as in: “*Baṭo, bhikṣām aṭa, gāṁ cānaya*” (“O boy, go for alms, and bring the cow too”).

কৃষ্ণমননে মুনি কৃষ্ণে সর্ব্বদা ভজয় ৷  
‘আত্মারামা অপি’ ভজে, — গৌণ অর্থ কয় ॥ ২১৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa-manane muni kṛṣṇe sarvadā bhajaya  
in contemplation of Kṛṣṇa  the sage  Kṛṣṇa  always  worships  
‘ātmārāmā api’ bhaje, — gauṇa artha kaya  
ātmārāmas  also  worship  secondary  meaning  it expresses

(218) The sage, absorbed in contemplating Kṛṣṇa, always worships Kṛṣṇa; and “the *ātmārāmas* also (*ātmārāmā api*)” worship Him — this expresses the secondary meaning.

‘চ’ এবার্থে ‘মুনয়ঃ এব’ কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ৷  
“আত্মারামা অপি” — ‘অপি’ ‘গর্হা’-অর্থ কয় ॥ ২১৯ ॥

‘ca’ evārthe ‘munayaḥ eva’ kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
ca  in the sense of eva  sages  only  Kṛṣṇa  worship  
“ātmārāmā api” — ‘api’ ‘garhā’-artha kaya  
ātmārāmas  even  api  of censure  the meaning  expresses

(219) Taking *ca* in the sense of *eva*: “only the sages (*munayaḥ eva*)” worship Kṛṣṇa; and in “*ātmārāmā api*” the word *api* expresses censure (*garhā*).

‘নির্গ্রন্থ হঞা’ — এই দুঁহার ‘বিশেষণ’ ৷  
আর অর্থ শুন, যৈছে সাধুর সঙ্গম ॥ ২২০ ॥

‘nirgrantha hañā’ — ei dum̐hāra ‘viśeṣaṇa’  
nirgranthas  becoming  this  of both  a qualifier  
āra artha śuna, yaiche sādhura saṅgama  
another  meaning  hear  how  of the sādhu  the company

(220) “Becoming *nirgranthas*” is a qualifier of both. Now hear another meaning, which shows what the company of sādhus can do.

নির্গ্রন্থ-শব্দে কহে তবে ‘ব্যাধ’, ‘নির্ধন’ ৷  
সাধুসঙ্গে সেহ করে শ্রীকৃষ্ণ ভজন ॥ ২২১ ॥

nirgrantha-śabde kahe tabe ‘vyādha’, ‘nirdhana’  
by the word nirgrantha  is meant  then  a hunter  a pauper  
sādhu-saṅge seha kare śrī-kṛṣṇa bhajana  
in the company of sādhus  he too  performs  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  worship

(221) The word *nirgrantha* then means a hunter (*vyādha*) or a pauper (*nirdhana*); in the company of sādhus, he too worships Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

‘কৃষ্ণারামাশ্চ’ এব কৃষ্ণ-মনন ৷  
ব্যাধ হঞা হয় পূজ্য ভাগবতোত্তম ॥ ২২২ ॥

‘kṛṣṇārāmāś ca’ eva kṛṣṇa-manana  
those who delight in Kṛṣṇa  and  indeed  of Kṛṣṇa  contemplation  
vyādha hañā haya pūjya bhāgavatottama  
a hunter  though being  becomes  worshipful  the best of bhāgavatas

(222) “*Kṛṣṇārāmāś ca*”: those who delight in Kṛṣṇa, that is, in contemplating Kṛṣṇa. Though a hunter, such a person becomes worshipful, the best of the *bhāgavatas*.

এক ভক্ত-ব্যাধের কথা শুন সাবধানে ৷  
যাহা হৈতে হয় সৎসঙ্গ-মহিমার জ্ঞানে ॥ ২২৩ ॥

eka bhakta-vyādhera kathā śuna sāvadhāne  
of one  devotee hunter  the story  hear  attentively  
yāhā haite haya sat-saṅga-mahimāra jñāne  
which  from  comes  of the company of saints  of the glory  knowledge

(223) Hear attentively the story of a hunter who became a devotee; from it one comes to know the glory of the company of saints.

এক দিন শ্রীনারদ দেখি’ নারায়ণ ৷  
ত্রিবেণী স্নানে প্রয়াগ করিলা গমন ॥ ২২৪ ॥

eka dina śrī-nārada dekhi’ nārāyaṇa  
one  day  Śrī Nārada  having seen  Nārāyaṇa  
triveṇī snāne prayāga karilā gamana  
at the Triveṇī  for bathing  to Prayāga  made  his way

(224) One day, after seeing Nārāyaṇa, Śrī Nārada went to Prayāga to bathe at the Triveṇī.

বনপথে দেখে মৃগ আছে ভূমে পড়ি’ ৷  
বাণ-বিদ্ধ ভগ্নপাদ করে ধড়্ফড়ি ॥ ২২৫ ॥

vana-pathe dekhe mṛga āche bhūme paḍi’  
on the forest path  he sees  a deer  is  on the ground  fallen  
bāṇa-viddha bhagna-pāda kare dhaḍ-phaḍi  
pierced by an arrow  with broken legs  makes  writhing

(225) On the forest path he saw a deer lying on the ground, pierced by an arrow, its legs broken, writhing in pain.

আর কতদূরে এক দেখেন শূকর ৷  
তৈছে বিদ্ধ ভগ্নপাদ করে ধড়্ফড় ॥ ২২৬ ॥

āra kata-dūre eka dekhena śūkara  
further  a little way on  a  he sees  boar  
taiche viddha bhagna-pāda kare dhaḍ-phaḍa  
likewise  pierced  with broken legs  makes  writhing

(226) A little farther on he saw a boar, likewise pierced, its legs broken, writhing in pain.

ঐছে এক শশক দেখে আর কতদূরে ৷  
জীবের দুঃখ দেখি’ নারদ ব্যাকুল অন্তরে ॥ ২২৭ ॥

aiche eka śaśaka dekhe āra kata-dūre  
likewise  a  rabbit  he sees  further  a little way on  
jīvera duḥkha dekhi’ nārada vyākula antare  
of the creatures  the suffering  seeing  Nārada  distressed  at heart

(227) A little farther on he saw a rabbit in the same state. Seeing the suffering of these creatures, Nārada was distressed at heart.

কতদূরে দেখে ব্যাধে বৃক্ষে ওঁত হঞা ৷  
মৃগ মারিবারে আছে বাণ যুড়িয়া ॥ ২২৮ ॥

kata-dūre dekhe vyādhe vṛkṣe om̐ta hañā  
a little way on  he sees  the hunter  behind a tree  hidden (õta)  being  
mṛga māribāre āche bāṇa yuḍiyā  
deer  to kill  he is  an arrow  having fitted

(228) A little farther on he saw the hunter hiding behind a tree, an arrow fitted to his bow, waiting to kill deer.

শ্যামবর্ণ রক্তনেত্র মহাভয়ঙ্কর ৷  
ধনুর্বাণ হস্তে, — যেন যম দণ্ডধর ॥ ২২৯ ॥

śyāma-varṇa rakta-netra mahā-bhayaṅkara  
dark in complexion  red-eyed  very  fearsome  
dhanur-bāṇa haste, — yena yama daṇḍa-dhara  
bow and arrows  in his hands  as if  Yama  holding the rod

(229) He was dark in complexion, red-eyed and very fearsome; with bow and arrows in his hands, he looked like Yamarāja holding his rod of punishment.

পথ ছাড়ি’ নারদ তার নিকটে চলিল ৷  
নারদে দেখি’ মৃগ সব পালাঞা গেল ॥ ২৩০ ॥

patha chāḍi’ nārada tāra nikaṭe calila  
the path  leaving  Nārada  to him  near  went  
nārade dekhi’ mṛga saba pālāñā gela  
Nārada  seeing  the deer  all  fleeing  went away

(230) Leaving the path, Nārada went up to him, and on seeing Nārada all the deer ran away.

ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা ব্যাধ তাঁরে গালি দিতে চায় ৷  
নারদ-প্রভাবে মুখে গালি নাহি আয় ॥ ২৩১ ॥

kruddha hañā vyādha tām̐re gāli dite cāya  
angry  becoming  the hunter  him  abuse  to give  wanted  
nārada-prabhāve mukhe gāli nāhi āya  
by Nārada’s  power  to his mouth  abuse  did not  come

(231) The hunter grew angry and wanted to abuse him, but by Nārada’s power no abuse would come to his lips.

গোসাঞি, প্রয়াণ-পথ ছাড়ি’ কেনে আইলা ৷  
তোমা দেখি’ মোর লক্ষ্য মৃগ পলাইলা ॥ ২৩২ ॥

gosāñi, prayāṇa-patha chāḍi’ kene āilā  
O Gosāñi  the common road  leaving  why  have you come  
tomā dekhi’ mora lakṣya mṛga palāilā  
you  seeing  my  target  the deer  has fled

(232) “Gosāñi, why have you left the road (*prayāṇa-patha*) and come here? Seeing you, the deer I was aiming at has fled.”

নারদ কহে, — পথ ভুলি’ আইলাঙ পুছিতে ৷  
মনে এক সংশয় হয়, তাহা খণ্ডাইতে ॥ ২৩৩ ॥

nārada kahe, — patha bhuli’ āilāṅa puchite  
Nārada  said  the way  having lost  I have come  to ask  
mane eka saṁśaya haya, tāhā khaṇḍāite  
in my mind  one  doubt  there is  it  to dispel

(233) Nārada said, “I lost my way and have come to ask you something. There is a doubt in my mind, and I would like to dispel it.

পথে যে শূকর-মৃগ, জানি তোমার হয় ৷  
ব্যাধ কহে, — যেই কহ, সেই ত’ নিশ্চয় ॥ ২৩৪ ॥

pathe ye śūkara-mṛga, jāni tomāra haya  
on the path  which  boar  deer  I know  yours  are  
vyādha kahe, — yei kaha, sei ta’ niścaya  
the hunter  said  what  you say  that  indeed  certain

(234) The boar and the deer on the path — I take it they are yours?” The hunter said, “Yes, what you say is so.”
