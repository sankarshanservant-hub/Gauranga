“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 20  
**Relishing the Meaning of the Verses of Instruction**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the Anubhāṣya, 2026)

প্রেমোদ্ভাবিতহর্ষের্ষ্যোদ্বেগদৈন্যার্ত্তিমিশ্রিতম্ ৷  
লপিতং গৌরচন্দ্রস্য ভাগ্যবদ্ভির্নিষেব্যতে ॥ ১ ॥

premodbhāvita-harṣerṣyod-  
by love  awakened  with joy,  jealousy,  anxiety  
vega-dainyārti-miśritam  
humility  and anguish  mingled  
lapitaṁ gauracandrasya  
the words  of Gauracandra  
bhāgyavadbhir niṣevyate  
by the fortunate  are relished

(1) Only the fortunate can relish the words that Gauracandra spoke in His divine madness, words mingled with the joy, jealousy, anxiety, humility and anguish that love awakens.[^1]

জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya gauracandra jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

এইমত মহাপ্রভু বৈসে নীলাচলে ৷  
রজনী-দিবসে কৃষ্ণবিরহে বিহ্বলে ॥ ৩ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu vaise nīlācale  
thus  Mahāprabhu  stayed  in Nīlācala  
rajanī-divase kṛṣṇa-virahe vihvale  
night and day  in separation from Kṛṣṇa  overwhelmed

(3) Thus Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala, overwhelmed night and day by separation from Kṛṣṇa.

স্বরূপ, রামানন্দ, — এই দুইজন-সনে ৷  
রাত্রি-দিনে রস-গীত-শ্লোক আস্বাদনে ॥ ৪ ॥

svarūpa, rāmānanda, — ei duijana-sane  
Svarūpa  Rāmānanda  these  two  in the company of  
rātri-dine rasa-gīta-śloka āsvādane  
night and day  of rasa  songs  verses  relishing

(4) Night and day, in the company of these two, Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, He relished songs and verses full of rasa.

নানা ভাব উঠে প্রভুর হর্ষ, শোক, রোষ ৷  
দৈন্যোদ্বেগ-আর্ত্তি উৎকণ্ঠা, সন্তোষ ॥ ৫ ॥

nānā bhāva uṭhe prabhura harṣa, śoka, roṣa  
various  emotions  arise  in the Lord  joy,  grief,  anger  
dainyodvega-ārti utkaṇṭhā, santoṣa  
humility,  anxiety,  anguish,  eager longing,  contentment

(5) Many emotions arose in the Lord: joy, grief and anger, humility, anxiety and anguish, eager longing and contentment.

সেই সেই ভাবে নিজ-শ্লোক পড়িয়া ৷  
শ্লোকের অর্থ আস্বাদয়ে দুইবন্ধু লঞা ॥ ৬ ॥

sei sei bhāve nija-śloka paḍiyā  
in that  very  mood  His own  verses  reciting  
ślokera artha āsvādaye dui-bandhu lañā  
of the verses  the meaning  He relishes  two  friends  taking with Him

(6) In each of these moods He would recite His own verses and relish their meaning with His two friends.

কোন দিনে কোন ভাবে শ্লোক-পঠন ৷  
সেই শ্লোক আস্বাদিতে রাত্রি-জাগরণ ॥ ৭ ॥

kona dine kona bhāve śloka-paṭhana  
on some  day  in some  mood  of a verse  recitation  
sei śloka āsvādite rātri-jāgaraṇa  
that  verse  to relish  of the night  keeping awake

(7) Sometimes, in a particular mood, He would recite a verse and stay awake all night relishing it.

হর্ষে প্রভু কহেন, — শুন স্বরূপ-রামরায় ৷  
নামসঙ্কীর্ত্তন — কলৌ পরম উপায় ॥ ৮ ॥

harṣe prabhu kahena, — śuna svarūpa-rāmarāya  
in joy  the Lord  says  listen  Svarūpa and Rāmarāya  
nāma-saṅkīrtana — kalau parama upāya  
of the name  saṅkīrtana  in Kali  the supreme  means

(8) In joy the Lord said, “Listen, Svarūpa and Rāmarāya! In the age of Kali the congregational chanting of the holy name is the supreme means.

সঙ্কীর্ত্তনযজ্ঞে কলৌ কৃষ্ণ-আরাধন ৷  
সেই ত’ সুমেধা পায় কৃষ্ণের চরণ ॥ ৯ ॥

saṅkīrtana-yajñe kalau kṛṣṇa-ārādhana  
by the sacrifice of saṅkīrtana  in Kali  of Kṛṣṇa  worship  
sei ta’ sumedhā pāya kṛṣṇera caraṇa  
he  indeed  intelligent  attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(9) In the age of Kali, Kṛṣṇa is worshipped by the sacrifice of saṅkīrtana. One who worships Him so is truly intelligent and attains Kṛṣṇa’s feet.”

কৃষ্ণবর্ণং ত্বিষাঽকৃষ্ণং সাঙ্গোপাঙ্গাস্ত্রপার্ষদম্ ৷  
যজ্ঞৈঃ সঙ্কীর্ত্তনপ্রায়ৈর্যজন্তি হি সুমেধসঃ ॥ ১০ ॥

kṛṣṇa-varṇaṁ tviṣā’kṛṣṇaṁ  
Kṛṣṇa  describing (or: with the syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’)  by lustre  not dark  
sāṅgopāṅgāstra-pārṣadam  
with limbs,  sub-limbs,  weapons  and associates  
yajñaiḥ saṅkīrtana-prāyair  
by sacrifices  in which saṅkīrtana  predominates  
yajanti hi su-medhasaḥ  
they worship  indeed  the intelligent

(10) “With sacrifices in which congregational chanting predominates, the wise worship the Lord who ever sings of Kṛṣṇa and whose lustre is not dark. He is always surrounded by His intimate companions (*aṅga*), His devotees (*upāṅga*), His weapons and His associates (*pārṣada*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.32)[^3]

নামসঙ্কীর্ত্তনে হয় সর্ব্বানর্থ-নাশ ৷  
সর্ব্ব-শুভোদয়, কৃষ্ণে প্রেমের উল্লাস ॥ ১১ ॥

nāma-saṅkīrtane haya sarvānartha-nāśa  
by the saṅkīrtana of the name  there is  of all anarthas  destruction  
sarva-śubhodaya, kṛṣṇe premera ullāsa  
of all  good  the dawning  for Kṛṣṇa  of love  the joy

(11) “Through the congregational chanting of the holy name all unwanted things (*anartha*) are destroyed, every good fortune dawns, and the joy of love for Kṛṣṇa awakens.”[^4]

চেতোদর্পণমার্জ্জনং ভবমহাদাবাগ্নিনির্ব্বাপণং  
শ্রেয়ঃকৈরবচন্দ্রিকাবিতরণং বিদ্যাবধূজীবনম্ ৷  
আনন্দাম্বুধিবর্দ্ধনং প্রতিপদং পূর্ণামৃতাস্বাদনং  
সর্ব্বাত্মস্নপনং পরং বিজয়তে শ্রীকৃষ্ণসঙ্কীর্ত্তনম্ ॥ ১২ ॥

ceto-darpaṇa-mārjanaṁ bhava-mahā-dāvāgni-nirvāpaṇaṁ  
of the heart  the mirror  cleansing  of material existence  the great  forest fire  extinguishing  
śreyaḥ-kairava-candrikā-vitaraṇaṁ vidyā-vadhū-jīvanam  
of the highest good  for the white lotus  the moonlight  spreading  of knowledge  the bride  the life  
ānandāmbudhi-vardhanaṁ prati-padaṁ pūrṇāmṛtāsvādanaṁ  
of bliss  the ocean  increasing  at every  step  of the full nectar  the taste  
sarvātma-snapanaṁ paraṁ vijayate śrī-kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtanam  
the whole self  bathing  supreme  be victorious  of Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  saṅkīrtana

(12) “The chanting of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name cleanses the mirror of the heart and puts out the great forest fire of material existence. It spreads the moonlight that opens the white lotus of the soul’s highest good; it is the very life of the bride of divine knowledge. It swells the ocean of bliss, gives at every step a taste of the full nectar, and bathes and cools the whole self. May the supreme chanting of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name be ever victorious!” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 1)[^5]

সঙ্কীর্ত্তন হৈতে পাপ-সংসার-নাশন ৷  
চিত্তশুদ্ধি, সর্ব্বভক্তিসাধন-উদ্গম ॥ ১৩ ॥

কৃষ্ণপ্রেমোদ্গম, প্রেমামৃত-আস্বাদন ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রাপ্তি, সেবামৃত-সমুদ্রে মজ্জন ॥ ১৪ ॥

saṅkīrtana haite pāpa-saṁsāra-nāśana  
saṅkīrtana  through  of sin  and of saṁsāra  destruction  
citta-śuddhi, sarva-bhakti-sādhana-udgama  
of the heart  purification,  of all  devotional  practices  the awakening  
kṛṣṇa-premodgama, premāmṛta-āsvādana  
of love for Kṛṣṇa  the awakening,  of the nectar of love  the tasting  
kṛṣṇa-prāpti, sevāmṛta-samudre majjana  
of Kṛṣṇa  the attainment,  in the ocean of the nectar of service  immersion

(13–14) “Through saṅkīrtana sin and material existence are destroyed, the heart is purified, and all the practices of devotion awaken. Love for Kṛṣṇa awakens, and one tastes the nectar of that love. One attains Kṛṣṇa and is immersed in the nectarean ocean of His service.”

উঠিল বিষাদ, দৈন্য, পড়ে আপন-শ্লোক ৷  
যাহার অর্থ শুনি’ সব যায় দুঃখ-শোক ॥ ১৫ ॥

uṭhila viṣāda, dainya, paḍe āpana-śloka  
arose  dejection,  humility,  He recites  His own  verse  
yāhāra artha śuni’ saba yāya duḥkha-śoka  
whose  meaning  hearing  all  go away  sorrow and grief

(15) Then dejection and humility arose in Him, and He recited another verse of His own — a verse whose meaning, once heard, dispels all sorrow and grief.

নাম্নামকারি বহুধা নিজসর্ব্বশক্তি  
স্তত্রার্পিতা নিয়মিতঃ স্মরণে ন কালঃ ৷  
এতাদৃশী তব কৃপা ভগবন্মমাপি  
দুর্দ্দৈবমীদৃশমিহাজনি নানুরাগঃ ॥ ১৬ ॥

nāmnām akāri bahudhā nija-sarva-śaktis  
of names  You have made  many  Your own  all  potencies  
tatrārpitā niyamitaḥ smaraṇe na kālaḥ  
in them  are invested  is fixed  for remembering  not  time  
etādṛśī tava kṛpā bhagavan mamāpi  
such  Your  mercy  O Lord  my  yet  
durdaivam īdṛśam ihājani nānurāgaḥ  
misfortune  such  for them  has arisen  no  attachment

(16) “O Lord, You have revealed many names, and in them You have invested all Your own potencies. You have fixed no time for remembering them. Such is Your mercy, yet such is my misfortune that no attachment for them has arisen in me.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 2)[^6]

অনেক-লোকের বাঞ্ছা — অনেক-প্রকার ৷  
কৃপাতে করিল অনেক নামের প্রচার ॥ ১৭ ॥

aneka-lokera vāñchā — aneka-prakāra  
of many  people  the desires  of many  kinds  
kṛpāte karila aneka nāmera pracāra  
out of mercy  You made  many  names’  propagation

(17) “Different people have desires of many kinds, so in Your mercy You have made known many names.

খাইতে শুইতে যথা তথা নাম লয় ৷  
কাল-দেশ-নিয়ম নাহি, সর্ব্বসিদ্ধি হয় ॥ ১৮ ॥

khāite śuite yathā tathā nāma laya  
while eating  while lying down  wherever  whenever  the name  one takes  
kāla-deśa-niyama nāhi, sarva-siddhi haya  
of time and place  rules  there are none  all perfection  comes

(18) Whether eating or lying down, wherever one may be, one can chant the name. There are no rules of time or place, and through the name all perfection comes.

সর্ব্বশক্তি নামে দিলা করিয়া বিভাগ ৷  
আমার দুর্দ্দৈব, — নামে নাহি অনুরাগ ! ॥ ১৯ ॥

sarva-śakti nāme dilā kariyā vibhāga  
all  potency  in the names  You gave  making  division  
āmāra durdaiva, — nāme nāhi anurāga!  
my  misfortune  for the name  there is no  attachment

(19) In each of the names You have invested all Your potency. But such is my misfortune: I have no attachment for the name!”

যেরূপে লইলে নাম প্রেম উপজয় ৷  
তার লক্ষণ-শ্লোক শুন, স্বরূপ-রামরায় ॥ ২০ ॥

ye-rūpe laile nāma prema upajaya  
in what way  by taking  the name  love  is born  
tāra lakṣaṇa-śloka śuna, svarūpa-rāmarāya  
of that  of the characteristics  the verse  hear  Svarūpa and Rāmarāya

(20) “Svarūpa and Rāmarāya, hear the verse that tells how one should chant the name so that love is born.”

তৃণাদপি সুনীচেন তরোরিব সহিষ্ণুনা ৷  
অমানিনা মানদেন কীর্ত্তনীয়ঃ সদা হরিঃ ॥ ২১ ॥

tṛṇād api su-nīcena  
than grass  even  lower  
taror iva sahiṣṇunā  
a tree  like  tolerant  
amāninā māna-dena  
free from pride  giving honour  
kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ  
to be glorified  always  Hari

(21) “Lower than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, seeking no honour and offering honour to others, one should always sing the glories of Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 3)[^7]

উত্তম হঞা আপনাকে মানে তৃণাধম ৷  
দুইপ্রকারে সহিষ্ণুতা করে বৃক্ষসম ॥ ২২ ॥

uttama hañā āpanāke māne tṛṇādhama  
exalted  being  himself  he considers  lower than grass  
dui-prakāre sahiṣṇutā kare vṛkṣa-sama  
in two  ways  tolerance  he shows  like a tree

(22) “Though he is exalted, a Vaiṣṇava considers himself lower than grass, and like a tree he shows tolerance in two ways.

বৃক্ষ যেন কাটিলেহ কিছু না বোলয় ৷  
শুকাঞা মৈলেহ কারে পানী না মাগয় ॥ ২৩ ॥

vṛkṣa yena kāṭileha kichu nā bolaya  
a tree  as  even when cut  anything  not  says  
śukāñā maileha kāre pānī nā māgaya  
drying up  even dying  from anyone  water  not  asks

(23) When a tree is cut down it says nothing, and even when it is drying up and dying it asks no one for water.

যেই যে মাগয়ে, তারে দেয় আপন-ধন ৷  
ঘর্ম্ম-বৃষ্টি সহে, আনের করয়ে রক্ষণ ॥ ২৪ ॥

yei ye māgaye, tāre deya āpana-dhana  
whoever  whatever  asks  to him  it gives  its own  wealth  
gharma-vṛṣṭi sahe, ānera karaye rakṣaṇa  
heat and rain  it endures  of others  it gives  protection

(24) It gives its wealth to whoever asks anything of it; it endures heat and rain, yet shelters others.

উত্তম হঞা বৈষ্ণব হবে নিরভিমান ৷  
জীবে সম্মান দিবে জানি’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’-অধিষ্ঠান ॥ ২৫ ॥

uttama hañā vaiṣṇava habe nirabhimāna  
exalted  being  a Vaiṣṇava  should be  free from pride  
jīve sammāna dibe jāni’ ‘kṛṣṇa’-adhiṣṭhāna  
to living beings  respect  he should give  knowing  Kṛṣṇa’s dwelling

(25) Though exalted, a Vaiṣṇava should be free from pride. He should honour every living being, knowing it to be the dwelling of Kṛṣṇa.

এইমত হঞা যেই কৃষ্ণনাম লয় ৷  
শ্রীকৃষ্ণচরণে তাঁর প্রেম উপজয় ॥ ২৬ ॥

ei-mata hañā yei kṛṣṇa-nāma laya  
thus  becoming  whoever  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  takes  
śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇe tām̐ra prema upajaya  
for Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  his  love  is born

(26) Whoever chants Kṛṣṇa’s name in this spirit awakens love for the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”

কহিতে কহিতে প্রভুর দৈন্য বাড়িলা ৷  
’শুদ্ধভক্তি’ কৃষ্ণ-ঠাঞি মাগিতে লাগিলা ॥ ২৭ ॥

kahite kahite prabhura dainya bāḍilā  
speaking  speaking  the Lord’s  humility  increased  
’śuddha-bhakti’ kṛṣṇa-ṭhāñi māgite lāgilā  
pure devotion  from Kṛṣṇa  to beg  He began

(27) As He spoke, the Lord’s humility grew, and He began to beg Kṛṣṇa for pure devotion (*śuddha-bhakti*).

প্রেমের স্বভাব, — যাঁহা প্রেমের সম্বন্ধ ৷  
সেই মানে, — কৃষ্ণে মোর নাহি ভক্তিগন্ধ ॥ ২৮ ॥

premera svabhāva, — yām̐hā premera sambandha  
of love  the nature  where  with love  a connection  
sei māne, — kṛṣṇe mora nāhi bhakti-gandha  
he  thinks  for Kṛṣṇa  my  there is not  of devotion  a trace

(28) Such is the nature of love: one who is truly touched by love thinks, “I have not even a trace of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.”

ন ধনং ন জনং ন সুন্দরীং  
কবিতাং বা জগদীশ কাময়ে ৷  
মম জন্মনি জন্মনীশ্বরে  
ভবতাদ্ভক্তিরহৈতুকী ত্বয়ি ॥ ২৯ ॥

na dhanaṁ na janaṁ na sundarīṁ  
not  wealth  not  followers  not  beautiful  
kavitāṁ vā jagad-īśa kāmaye  
poetry  or  O Lord of the universe  I desire  
mama janmani janmanīśvare  
my  in birth  after birth  for the Lord  
bhavatād bhaktir ahaitukī tvayi  
may there be  devotion  causeless  for You

(29) “O Lord of the universe, I desire neither wealth, nor followers, nor beautiful poetry. Birth after birth, may I have causeless devotion to You, my Lord.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 4)[^8][^9]

ধন, জন নাহি মাগোঁ, কবিতা সুন্দরী ৷  
’শুদ্ধভক্তি’ দেহ’ মোরে, কৃষ্ণ, কৃপা করি’ ॥ ৩০ ॥

dhana, jana nāhi māgom̐, kavitā sundarī  
wealth,  followers  not  I beg,  poetry  beautiful  
’śuddha-bhakti’ deha’ more, kṛṣṇa, kṛpā kari’  
pure devotion  give  to me  Kṛṣṇa  mercy  showing

(30) “I do not beg for wealth, for followers or for beautiful poetry. O Kṛṣṇa, be merciful and give me pure devotion!”

অতি দৈন্যে পুনঃ মাগে দাস্যভক্তি-দান ৷  
আপনারে করে সংসারী জীব-অভিমান ॥ ৩১ ॥

ati dainye punaḥ māge dāsya-bhakti-dāna  
in great  humility  again  He begs  of servitude  devotion  the gift  
āpanāre kare saṁsārī jīva-abhimāna  
about Himself  He takes  worldly  a soul’s  self-conception

(31) In great humility He again begged for the gift of devotion in servitude, thinking of Himself as an ordinary soul caught in worldly existence.

অয়ি নন্দতনুজ কিঙ্করং  
পতিতং মাং বিষমে ভবাম্বুধৌ ৷  
কৃপয়া তব পাদপঙ্কজ-  
স্থিত-ধূলীসদৃশং বিচিন্তয় ॥ ৩২ ॥

ayi nanda-tanuja kiṅkaraṁ  
O  of Nanda  son  servant  
patitaṁ māṁ viṣame bhavāmbudhau  
fallen  me  into the terrible  ocean of material existence  
kṛpayā tava pāda-paṅkaja-  
by mercy  Your  of feet  lotus  
sthita-dhūlī-sadṛśaṁ vicintaya  
resting on  a speck of dust  like  consider

(32) “O son of Nanda, I am Your servant, yet I have fallen into the terrible ocean of material existence. Be merciful and consider me a speck of dust at Your lotus feet.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 5)[^10]

তোমার নিত্যদাস মুই, তোমা পাসরিয়া ৷  
পড়িয়াছোঁ ভবার্ণবে মায়াবদ্ধ হঞা ॥ ৩৩ ॥

tomāra nitya-dāsa mui, tomā pāsariyā  
Your  eternal  servant  I  You  forgetting  
paḍiyāchom̐ bhavārṇave māyā-baddha hañā  
I have fallen  into the ocean of material existence  by māyā  bound  becoming

(33) “I am Your eternal servant, but I forgot You and fell into the ocean of material existence, bound by māyā.

কৃপা করি’ কর মোরে পদধূলী-সম ৷  
তোমার সেবক করোঁ তোমার সেবন ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kṛpā kari’ kara more pada-dhūlī-sama  
mercy  showing  make  me  of Your feet  the dust  like  
tomāra sevaka karom̐ tomāra sevana  
Your  servant  I would do  Your  service

(34) Be merciful and make me like a speck of dust at Your feet, so that as Your servant I may serve You.”

পুনঃ অতি উৎকণ্ঠা, দৈন্য হইল উদ্গম ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-ঠাঞি মাগে প্রেম-নামসঙ্কীর্ত্তন ॥ ৩৫ ॥

punaḥ ati utkaṇṭhā, dainya haila udgama  
again  intense  longing  humility  there was  an arising  
kṛṣṇa-ṭhāñi māge prema-nāma-saṅkīrtana  
from Kṛṣṇa  He begs  with love  of the name  the chanting

(35) Again intense longing and humility arose in Him, and He begged Kṛṣṇa that He might chant the holy name with love.

নয়নং গলদশ্রুধারয়া বদনং গদ্গদ-রুদ্ধয়া গিরা ৷  
পুলকৈর্নিচিতং বপুঃ কদা তব নাম-গ্রহণে ভবিষ্যতি ॥ ৩৬ ॥

nayanaṁ galad-aśru-dhārayā  
eyes  of streaming  tears  with a flow  
vadanaṁ gadgada-ruddhayā girā  
mouth  faltering  choked  with a voice  
pulakair nicitaṁ vapuḥ kadā  
with thrills of joy  covered  the body  when  
tava nāma-grahaṇe bhaviṣyati  
Your  name  in taking  will be

(36) “When, as I chant Your name, will my eyes overflow with streaming tears, my voice falter and choke, and my body be covered with thrills of joy?” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 6)[^11]

প্রেমধন বিনা ব্যর্থ দরিদ্র জীবন !  
‘দাস’ করি’ বেতন মোরে দেহ প্রেমধন ! ॥ ৩৭ ॥

prema-dhana vinā vyartha daridra jīvana!  
of love  the wealth  without  futile  poor  life  
‘dāsa’ kari’ vetana more deha prema-dhana!  
Your servant  making  as wages  to me  give  of love  the wealth

(37) “Without the wealth of love, life is poor and futile! Make me Your servant and give me the wealth of love as my wages!”

রসান্তরাবেশে হইল বিয়োগ-স্ফুরণ ৷  
উদ্বেগ, বিষাদ, দৈন্যে করে প্রলাপন ॥ ৩৮ ॥

rasāntarāveśe haila viyoga-sphuraṇa  
by absorption in another rasa  there was  of separation  the manifestation  
udvega, viṣāda, dainye kare pralāpana  
in anxiety,  dejection,  humility  He does  raving

(38) Absorbed in another rasa, He felt the full force of separation, and in anxiety, dejection and humility He began to speak words of divine madness.[^12]

যুগায়িতং নিমেষেণ চক্ষুষা প্রাবৃষায়িতম্ ৷  
শূন্যায়িতং জগৎ সর্ব্বং গোবিন্দ-বিরহেণ মে ॥ ৩৯ ॥

yugāyitaṁ nimeṣeṇa  
seems an age  a moment  
cakṣuṣā prāvṛṣāyitam  
by the eyes  the rainy season is enacted  
śūnyāyitaṁ jagat sarvaṁ  
seems empty  the world  whole  
govinda-viraheṇa me  
in separation from Govinda  for Me

(39) “In separation from Govinda, a moment seems to Me an age, My eyes pour tears like the rains, and the whole world seems empty.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 7)[^13]

উদ্বেগে দিবস না যায়, ‘ক্ষণ’ হৈল ‘যুগ’-সম !  
বর্ষার মেঘপ্রায় অশ্রু বর্ষে নয়ন ! ॥ ৪০ ॥

udvege divasa nā yāya, ‘kṣaṇa’ haila ‘yuga’-sama!  
in anxiety  the day  not  passes  a moment  has become  an age  like  
varṣāra megha-prāya aśru varṣe nayana!  
of the rainy season  a cloud  like  tears  rain  the eyes

(40) “In My anxiety the day will not pass; a moment has become like an age! Like clouds in the rainy season, My eyes rain tears!

গোবিন্দ-বিরহে শূন্য হইল ত্রিভুবন !  
তুষানলে পোড়ে, — যেন না যায় জীবন ॥ ৪১ ॥

govinda-virahe śūnya haila tribhuvana!  
in separation from Govinda  empty  has become  the three worlds  
tuṣānale poḍe, — yena nā yāya jīvana  
in a fire of husks  I burn  as if  not  goes  life

(41) In separation from Govinda the three worlds have become empty! I burn as in a slow fire of husks, and yet life does not leave Me.

কৃষ্ণ উদাসীন হইলা করিতে পরীক্ষণ ৷  
সখী সব কহে, — কৃষ্ণে কর উপেক্ষণ ॥ ৪২ ॥

kṛṣṇa udāsīna hailā karite parīkṣaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  indifferent  has become  to make  a test  
sakhī saba kahe, — kṛṣṇe kara upekṣaṇa  
My friends  all  say  Kṛṣṇa  do  neglect

(42) Kṛṣṇa has become indifferent to test Me, and all My friends say, ‘Ignore Kṛṣṇa.’”

এতেক চিন্তিতে রাধার নির্ম্মল হৃদয় ৷  
স্বাভাবিক প্রেমার স্বভাব করিল উদয় ॥ ৪৩ ॥

eteka cintite rādhāra nirmala hṛdaya  
so much  as He thought  Rādhā’s  pure  heart  
svābhāvika premāra svabhāva karila udaya  
natural  of love  the nature  made  rise

(43) As these thoughts arose, the very nature of spontaneous love dawned in Rādhā’s pure heart.

ঈর্ষ্যা, উৎকণ্ঠা, দৈন্য, প্রৌঢ়ি, বিনয় ৷  
এত ভাব এক-ঠাঞি করিল উদয় ॥ ৪৪ ॥

īrṣyā, utkaṇṭhā, dainya, prauḍhi, vinaya  
jealousy,  eagerness,  humility,  boldness,  entreaty  
eta bhāva eka-ṭhāñi karila udaya  
so many  emotions  in one place  made  rise

(44) Jealousy, eagerness, humility, boldness and entreaty — all these emotions arose at once.[^14]

এত ভাবে রাধার মন অস্থির হইলা ৷  
সখীগণ-আগে প্রৌঢ়ি-শ্লোক যে পড়িলা ॥ ৪৫ ॥

eta bhāve rādhāra mana asthira hailā  
by these  emotions  Rādhā’s  mind  unsteady  became  
sakhī-gaṇa-āge prauḍhi-śloka ye paḍilā  
of the sakhīs  before  a bold verse  which  She spoke

(45) These emotions unsettled Rādhā’s mind, and before Her friends She spoke a bold verse.

সেইভাবে প্রভু সেই শ্লোক উচ্চারিলা ৷  
শ্লোক উচ্চারিতে তদ্রূপ আপনে হইলা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

sei-bhāve prabhu sei śloka uccārilā  
in that mood  the Lord  that  verse  recited  
śloka uccārite tad-rūpa āpane hailā  
the verse  while reciting  of that form  Himself  He became

(46) In that same mood the Lord recited that verse, and as He recited it He Himself became like Her.

আশ্লিষ্য বা পাদরতাং পিনষ্টু মা-  
মদর্শনান্মর্ম্মহতাং করোতু বা ৷  
যথা তথা বা বিদধাতু লম্পটো  
মৎপ্রাণনাথস্তু স এব নাপরঃ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

āśliṣya vā pāda-ratāṁ pinaṣṭu mām  
embracing  or  devoted to His feet  may He make His own (lit. ‘crush’)  Me  
adarśanān marma-hatāṁ karotu vā  
by not appearing  heartbroken  may He make  or  
yathā tathā vā vidadhātu lampaṭo  
as  He likes  or  may He act  the unrestrained lover  
mat-prāṇa-nāthas tu sa eva nāparaḥ  
of My life  the Lord  yet  He  alone  no other

(47) “Let Him embrace Me, this maidservant devoted to His feet, and make Me His own, or break My heart by not appearing before Me. Let that unrestrained lover (*lampaṭa*) do with Me whatever He likes — He alone is the Lord of My life, and no one else.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 8)[^15]

আমি — কৃষ্ণপদ-দাসী, তেঁহো — রসসুখরাশি,  
আলিঙ্গিয়া করে আত্মসাথ ৷  
কিবা না দেয় দরশন, জারেন মোর তনুমন,  
তবু তেঁহো — মোর প্রাণনাথ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

āmi — kṛṣṇa-pada-dāsī, tem̐ho — rasa-sukha-rāśi,  
I  of Kṛṣṇa’s feet  the maidservant  He  of rasa and joy  the abode  
āliṅgiyā kare ātma-sātha  
embracing  He makes  His own  
kibā nā deya daraśana, jārena mora tanu-mana,  
or  not  He gives  sight of Himself  He wears away  My  body and mind  
tabu tem̐ho — mora prāṇa-nātha  
still  He  of My  life  the Lord

(48) “I am the maidservant of Kṛṣṇa’s feet, and He is the very abode of rasa and joy. He may embrace Me and make Me His own, or He may not let Me see Him and so wear away My body and mind — still He is the Lord of My life.

সখি হে, শুন মোর মনের নিশ্চয় ৷  
কিবা অনুরাগ করে, কিবা দুঃখ দিয়া মারে,  
মোর প্রাণেশ্বর কৃষ্ণ, — অন্য নয় ॥ ৪৯ ॥

sakhi he, śuna mora manera niścaya  
O friend  listen  My  of mind  the resolve  
kibā anurāga kare, kibā duḥkha diyā māre,  
whether  love  He shows  or  pain  giving  He kills  
mora prāṇeśvara kṛṣṇa, — anya naya  
My  Lord of life  Kṛṣṇa  another  not

(49) O friend, hear the resolve of My heart. Whether He shows Me love or kills Me with pain, Kṛṣṇa — and no one else — is the Lord of My life.

ছাড়ি’ অন্য নারীগণ, মোর বশ তনুমন,  
মোর সৌভাগ্য প্রকট করিয়া ৷  
তা-সবারে দেয় পীড়া, আমা-সনে করে ক্রীড়া,  
সেই নারীগণে দেখাঞা ॥ ৫০ ॥

chāḍi’ anya nārī-gaṇa, mora vaśa tanu-mana,  
leaving  other  women  under My control  His body and mind  
mora saubhāgya prakaṭa kariyā  
My  good fortune  manifest  making  
tā-sabāre deya pīḍā, āmā-sane kare krīḍā,  
to all of them  He gives  pain  with Me  He makes  play  
sei nārī-gaṇe dekhāñā  
to those  women  showing

(50) Sometimes He leaves the other women, and His body and mind are under My control. Making My good fortune plain, He pains them all by sporting with Me before their eyes.

কিবা তেঁহো লম্পট, শঠ, ধৃষ্ট, সকপট,  
অন্য নারীগণ করি’ সাথ ৷  
মোরে দিতে মনঃপীড়া, মোর আগে করে ক্রীড়া,  
তবু তেঁহো — মোর প্রাণনাথ ॥ ৫১ ॥

kibā tem̐ho lampaṭa, śaṭha, dhṛṣṭa, sakapaṭa,  
or  He  a libertine,  a cheat,  brazen,  deceitful  
anya nārī-gaṇa kari’ sātha  
other  women  making  His companions  
more dite manaḥ-pīḍā, mora āge kare krīḍā,  
to Me  to give  heartache  of Me  before  He makes  play  
tabu tem̐ho — mora prāṇa-nātha  
still  He  of My  life  the Lord

(51) Or else, libertine, cheat, brazen and deceitful as He is, He takes other women with Him and sports with them before My eyes just to pain My heart — still He is the Lord of My life.

না গণি আপন-দুঃখ, সবে বাঞ্ছি তাঁর সুখ,  
তাঁর সুখ — আমার তাৎপর্য্য ৷  
মোরে যদি দিয়া দুঃখ, তাঁর হৈল মহাসুখ,  
সেই দুঃখ — মোর সুখবর্য্য ॥ ৫২ ॥

nā gaṇi āpana-duḥkha, sabe vāñchi tām̐ra sukha,  
not  I count  My own  pain  only  I desire  His  happiness  
tām̐ra sukha — āmāra tātparya  
His  happiness  My  aim  
more yadi diyā duḥkha, tām̐ra haila mahā-sukha,  
to Me  if  giving  pain  His  became  great  happiness  
sei duḥkha — mora sukha-varya  
that  pain  My  of happiness  the best

(52) I do not count My own pain; I desire only His happiness. His happiness is My whole aim. If by giving Me pain He finds great happiness, that pain is My greatest happiness.

যে নারীরে বাঞ্ছে কৃষ্ণ, তার রূপে সতৃষ্ণ,  
তারে না পাঞা হয় দুঃখী ৷  
মুই তার পায়ে পড়ি’, লঞা যাঙ হাতে ধরি’,  
ক্রীড়া করাঞা তাঁরে করোঁ সুখী ॥ ৫৩ ॥

ye nārīre vāñche kṛṣṇa, tāra rūpe satṛṣṇa,  
whichever  woman  desires  Kṛṣṇa  for her  beauty  longing  
tāre nā pāñā haya duḥkhī  
her  not  obtaining  He becomes  unhappy  
mui tāra pāye paḍi’, lañā yāṅa hāte dhari’,  
I  at her  feet  falling  I bring her  by the hand  holding  
krīḍā karāñā tām̐re karom̐ sukhī  
pastimes  arranging  Him  I make  happy

(53) If Kṛṣṇa longs for a certain woman, drawn by her beauty, and grows sad because He cannot meet her, I fall at her feet, take her by the hand and bring her to Him, and by arranging their pastimes I make Him happy.

কান্তা কৃষ্ণে করে রোষ, কৃষ্ণ পায় সন্তোষ,  
সুখ পায় তাড়ন-ভর্ৎসনে ৷  
যথাযোগ্য করে মান, কৃষ্ণ তাতে সুখ পান,  
ছাড়ে মান অল্প-সাধনে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

kāntā kṛṣṇe kare roṣa, kṛṣṇa pāya santoṣa,  
a beloved  with Kṛṣṇa  shows  anger  Kṛṣṇa  gets  satisfaction  
sukha pāya tāḍana-bhartsane  
joy  He gets  from scolding and rebuke  
yathā-yogya kare māna, kṛṣṇa tāte sukha pāna,  
as is fitting  she shows  pique  Kṛṣṇa  in that  joy  gets  
chāḍe māna alpa-sādhane  
she gives up  her pique  with a little  coaxing

(54) When a beloved is angry with Kṛṣṇa, He is pleased and takes joy in her scolding and rebukes. When, as is fitting, she shows loving pique (*māna*), it gives Him delight, and at a little coaxing she lets it go.

সেই নারী জীয়ে কেনে, কৃষ্ণ-মর্ম্ম নাহি জানে,  
তবু কৃষ্ণে করে গাঢ় রোষ ৷  
নিজ-সুখে মানে লাভ, পড়ুক তার শিরে বাজ,  
কৃষ্ণের মাত্র চাহিয়ে সন্তোষ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

sei nārī jīye kene, kṛṣṇa-marma nāhi jāne,  
that  woman  lives  why  Kṛṣṇa’s heart  not  knows  
tabu kṛṣṇe kare gāḍha roṣa  
yet  with Kṛṣṇa  shows  intense  anger  
nija-sukhe māne lābha, paḍuka tāra śire vāja,  
in her own happiness  she sees  gain  may fall  her  on the head  a thunderbolt  
kṛṣṇera mātra cāhiye santoṣa  
Kṛṣṇa’s  only  we desire  satisfaction

(55) Why does a woman live who does not know Kṛṣṇa’s heart and yet is bitterly angry with Him? She counts her own happiness as gain — may a thunderbolt fall on her head! We desire only Kṛṣṇa’s satisfaction.[^16]

যে গোপী মোর করে দ্বেষে, কৃষ্ণের করে সন্তোষে,  
কৃষ্ণ যারে করে অভিলাষ ৷  
মুই তার ঘরে যাঞা, তারে সেবোঁ দাসী হঞা,  
তবে মোর সুখের উল্লাস ॥ ৫৬ ॥

ye gopī mora kare dveṣe, kṛṣṇera kare santoṣe,  
whichever  gopī  towards Me  bears  enmity  Kṛṣṇa’s  makes  satisfaction  
kṛṣṇa yāre kare abhilāṣa  
Kṛṣṇa  whom  makes  desire  
mui tāra ghare yāñā, tāre sevom̐ dāsī hañā,  
I  to her  house  going  her  I serve  a maidservant  becoming  
tabe mora sukhera ullāsa  
then  My  of happiness  the exultation

(56) If a gopī who is hostile to Me pleases Kṛṣṇa, and Kṛṣṇa longs for her company, I will go to her house and serve her as her maidservant. Then My happiness overflows.

কুষ্ঠী-বিপ্রের রমণী, পতিব্রতা-শিরোমণি,  
পতি লাগি’ কৈল বেশ্যার সেবা ৷  
স্তম্ভিল সূর্য্যের গতি, জীয়াইল মৃত পতি,  
তুষ্ট কৈল মুখ্য তিন-দেবা ॥ ৫৭ ॥

kuṣṭhī-viprera ramaṇī, pativratā-śiromaṇi,  
of the leprous  brāhmaṇa  the wife  of chaste wives  the crest jewel  
pati lāgi’ kaila veśyāra sevā  
her husband  for the sake of  she did  to a prostitute  service  
stambhila sūryera gati, jīyāila mṛta pati,  
she stopped  the sun’s  course  she revived  dead  husband  
tuṣṭa kaila mukhya tina-devā  
pleased  made  the chief  three gods

(57) The wife of a leprous brāhmaṇa, the crest jewel of chaste wives, served a prostitute for her husband’s sake. She stopped the course of the sun, brought her dead husband back to life, and pleased the three principal deities.[^17]

কৃষ্ণ — মোর জীবন, কৃষ্ণ — মোর প্রাণধন,  
কৃষ্ণ — মোর প্রাণের পরাণ ৷  
হৃদয়-উপরে ধরোঁ, সেবা করি’ সুখী করোঁ,  
এই মোর সদা রহে ধ্যান ॥ ৫৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa — mora jīvana, kṛṣṇa — mora prāṇa-dhana,  
Kṛṣṇa  My  life  Kṛṣṇa  My  life’s treasure  
kṛṣṇa — mora prāṇera parāṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  of My  life  the life  
hṛdaya-upare dharom̐, sevā kari’ sukhī karom̐,  
upon My heart  I hold Him  service  rendering  happy  I make Him  
ei mora sadā rahe dhyāna  
this  My  always  remains  meditation

(58) Kṛṣṇa is My life; Kṛṣṇa is the treasure of My life; Kṛṣṇa is the very life of My life. I hold Him upon My heart and make Him happy by serving Him — this is My constant meditation.

মোর সুখ — সেবনে, কৃষ্ণের সুখ — সঙ্গমে,  
অতএব দেহ দেঙ দান ৷  
কৃষ্ণ মোরে ‘কান্তা’ করি, কহে মোরে ‘প্রাণেশ্বরী’,  
মোর হয় ‘দাসী’-অভিমান ॥ ৫৯ ॥

mora sukha — sevane, kṛṣṇera sukha — saṅgame,  
My  happiness  in serving  Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  in union  
ataeva deha deṅa dāna  
therefore  My body  I give  as a gift  
kṛṣṇa more ‘kāntā’ kari, kahe more ‘prāṇeśvarī’,  
Kṛṣṇa  Me  His beloved  making  He calls  Me  queen of His life  
mora haya ‘dāsī’-abhimāna  
My  is  of a maidservant  self-conception

(59) My happiness lies in serving Him; Kṛṣṇa’s happiness lies in union with Me. Therefore I offer Him My body. Kṛṣṇa treats Me as His beloved and calls Me the queen of His life, but I think of Myself as His maidservant.

কান্ত-সেবা-সুখপূর, সঙ্গম হৈতে সুমধুর,  
তাতে সাক্ষী — লক্ষ্মী ঠাকুরাণী ৷  
নারায়ণ-হৃদি স্থিতি, তবু পাদসেবায় মতি,  
সেবা করে ‘দাসী’-অভিমানী ॥ ৬০ ॥

kānta-sevā-sukha-pūra, saṅgama haite sumadhura,  
to the beloved  of service  of happiness  the fullness  union  than  sweeter  
tāte sākṣī — lakṣmī ṭhākurāṇī  
of this  the witness  Lakṣmī  Ṭhākurāṇī  
nārāyaṇa-hṛdi sthiti, tabu pāda-sevāya mati,  
in Nārāyaṇa’s heart  her abode  yet  in service to His feet  her inclination  
sevā kare ‘dāsī’-abhimānī  
service  she renders  as a maidservant  thinking of herself

(60) The fullness of joy in serving the beloved is sweeter even than union. The goddess Lakṣmī is witness to this: though she dwells on Nārāyaṇa’s heart, she longs to serve His feet, and she serves Him thinking of herself as His maidservant.”

এই রাধার বচন, বিশুদ্ধপ্রেম-লক্ষণ,  
আস্বাদয়ে শ্রীগৌর-রায় ৷  
ভাবে মন নহে স্থির, সাত্ত্বিকে ব্যাপে শরীর,  
মন-দেহ ধারণ না যায় ॥ ৬১ ॥

ei rādhāra vacana, viśuddha-prema-lakṣaṇa,  
these  Rādhā’s  words  of pure  love  the marks  
āsvādaye śrī-gaura-rāya  
relishes  Śrī  Gaura Rāya  
bhāve mana nahe sthira, sāttvike vyāpe śarīra,  
from emotion  the mind  not  steady  sāttvika symptoms  pervade  the body  
mana-deha dhāraṇa nā yāya  
mind and body  holding still  not  possible

(61) Śrī Gaura Rāya relished these words of Rādhā, which bear the marks of pure love. His mind grew unsteady with emotion, sāttvika symptoms pervaded His body, and He could no longer hold His mind and body still.[^18]

ব্রজেশ্বর-শুদ্ধপ্রেম, — যেন জাম্বুনদ হেম,  
আত্ম-সুখের যাঁহা নাহি গন্ধ ৷  
স্ব-প্রেম জানা’তে লোকে, প্রভু কৈলা এই শ্লোকে,  
পদ কৈলা অর্থের নির্ব্বন্ধ ॥ ৬২ ॥

vrajeśvara-śuddha-prema, — yena jāmbu-nada hema,  
for the Lord of Vraja  pure  love  like  of the Jambū  river  gold  
ātma-sukhera yām̐hā nāhi gandha  
of one’s own happiness  in which  there is no  trace  
sva-prema jānā’te loke, prabhu kailā ei śloke,  
His own love  to make known  to the world  the Lord  made  this  verse  
pada kailā arthera nirbandha  
songs (pada)  He made  of the meaning  as the exposition

(62) In pure love for the Lord of Vraja there is no trace of desire for one’s own happiness: it is like gold from the river Jambū. To make this love known to the world, the Lord composed this verse and set out its meaning at length in songs.[^19][^20]

এইমত মহাপ্রভু ভাবাবিষ্ট হঞা ৷  
প্রলাপ করিলা কিছু শ্লোক পড়িয়া ॥ ৬৩ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu bhāvāviṣṭa hañā  
thus  Mahāprabhu  absorbed in emotion  becoming  
pralāpa karilā kichu śloka paḍiyā  
raving  He did  some  verses  reciting

(63) Thus, absorbed in ecstatic emotion, Mahāprabhu recited some verses and spoke words of divine madness.

পূর্ব্বে অষ্ট-শ্লোক করি’ লোকে শিক্ষা দিলা ৷  
সেই অষ্ট-শ্লোক আপনে আস্বাদিলা ॥ ৬৪ ॥

pūrve aṣṭa-śloka kari’ loke śikṣā dilā  
earlier  eight  verses  composing  to the people  instruction  He gave  
sei aṣṭa-śloka āpane āsvādilā  
those  eight  verses  Himself  He relished

(64) Earlier He had composed these eight verses to instruct the people; now He Himself relished them.

প্রভুর ‘শিক্ষাষ্টক’-শ্লোক যেই পড়ে, শুনে ৷  
কৃষ্ণে প্রেমভক্তি তার বাড়ে দিনে দিনে ॥ ৬৫ ॥

prabhura ‘śikṣāṣṭaka’-śloka yei paḍe, śune  
the Lord’s  Śikṣāṣṭaka  verses  whoever  reads  hears  
kṛṣṇe prema-bhakti tāra bāḍe dine dine  
for Kṛṣṇa  loving devotion  his  grows  day  by day

(65) Whoever reads or hears the Lord’s *Śikṣāṣṭaka* verses will find his loving devotion to Kṛṣṇa growing day by day.

যদ্যপিহ প্রভু — কোটিসমুদ্র-গম্ভীর ৷  
নানা-ভাব-চন্দ্রোদয়ে হয়েন অস্থির ॥ ৬৬ ॥

yadyapiha prabhu — koṭi-samudra-gambhīra  
although  the Lord  millions of oceans  as deep as  
nānā-bhāva-candrodaye hayena asthira  
of various  emotions  at the moonrise  He becomes  restless

(66) Though the Lord is as deep as millions of oceans, He grows restless when the moon of His many emotions rises.[^21]

যেই যেই শ্লোক জয়দেব, ভাগবতে ৷  
রায়ের নাটকে, যেই আর কর্ণামৃতে ॥ ৬৭ ॥

সেই সেই ভাবে শ্লোক করিয়া পঠনে ৷  
সেই সেই ভাবাবেশে করেন আস্বাদনে ॥ ৬৮ ॥

yei yei śloka jayadeva, bhāgavate  
whichever  verses  of Jayadeva  in the Bhāgavata  
rāyera nāṭake, yei āra karṇāmṛte  
in Rāya’s  drama  whichever  and  in the Karṇāmṛta  
sei sei bhāve śloka kariyā paṭhane  
in that very  mood  verses  making  recitation  
sei sei bhāvāveśe karena āsvādane  
in that very  absorption in emotion  He does  relishing

(67–68) Whatever verses of Jayadeva, of the *Bhāgavata*, of Rāmānanda Rāya’s drama and of the *Karṇāmṛta* suited each mood, He would recite in that mood and relish, absorbed in that emotion.

দ্বাদশ বৎসর ঐছে দশা — রাত্রি-দিনে ৷  
কৃষ্ণরস আস্বাদয়ে দুইবন্ধু-সনে ॥ ৬৯ ॥

dvādaśa vatsara aiche daśā — rātri-dine  
twelve  years  such  a state  night and day  
kṛṣṇa-rasa āsvādaye dui-bandhu-sane  
Kṛṣṇa’s  rasa  He relishes  two  friends  with

(69) For twelve years He was in this state night and day, relishing the rasa of Kṛṣṇa with His two friends.

সেই সব লীলা-রস আপনে অনন্ত ৷  
সহস্র-বদনে বর্ণি’ নাহি পা’ন অন্ত ॥ ৭০ ॥

sei saba līlā-rasa āpane ananta  
those  all  of the pastimes  the rasa  Himself  Ananta  
sahasra-vadane varṇi’ nāhi pā’na anta  
with a thousand  mouths  describing  not  He finds  the end

(70) Even Ananta Himself, describing with His thousand mouths the rasa of all these pastimes, cannot reach their end.

জীব ক্ষুদ্রবুদ্ধি কোন্ তাহা পারে বর্ণিতে ?  
তার এক কণা স্পর্শি আপনা শোধিতে ॥ ৭১ ॥

jīva kṣudra-buddhi kon tāhā pāre varṇite?  
a soul  of small intelligence  how  that  can  describe  
tāra eka kaṇā sparśi āpanā śodhite  
of it  one  particle  I touch  myself  to purify

(71) How can a soul of small intelligence describe them? I touch just one particle of them, to purify myself.

যত চেষ্টা, যত প্রলাপ, — নাহি পারাবার ৷  
সে সব বর্ণিতে গ্রন্থ হয় সুবিস্তার ॥ ৭২ ॥

yata ceṣṭā, yata pralāpa, — nāhi pārāvāra  
all  the actions  all  the ravings  there is no  limit  
se saba varṇite grantha haya suvistāra  
that  all  to describe  the book  would be  vast

(72) His actions and His words of divine madness have no limit; to describe them all would make the book enormous.[^22]

বৃন্দাবন-দাস প্রথম যে লীলা বর্ণিল ৷  
সেইসব লীলার আমি সূত্রমাত্র কৈল ॥ ৭৩ ॥

vṛndāvana-dāsa prathama ye līlā varṇila  
Vṛndāvana dāsa  first  which  pastimes  described  
sei-saba līlāra āmi sūtra-mātra kaila  
of all those  pastimes  I  a summary  only  made

(73) Of the pastimes that Vṛndāvana dāsa had described before me, I have given only a summary.[^23]

তাঁর ত্যক্ত ‘অবশেষ’ সংক্ষেপে কহিল ৷  
লীলার বাহুল্যে গ্রন্থ তথাপি বাড়িল ॥ ৭৪ ॥

tām̐ra tyakta ‘avaśeṣa’ saṅkṣepe kahila  
by him  left  the remainder  briefly  I told  
līlāra bāhulye grantha tathāpi bāḍila  
of the pastimes  by the abundance  the book  even so  grew

(74) I have briefly told the “remainder” that he left untold, and even so the book has grown, because the pastimes are so many.

অতএব সেইসব লীলা না পারি বর্ণিবারে ৷  
সমাপ্ত করিলুঁ লীলা করি’ নমস্কারে ॥ ৭৫ ॥

ataeva sei-saba līlā nā pāri varṇibāre  
therefore  all those  pastimes  not  I can  to describe  
samāpta karilum̐ līlā kari’ namaskāre  
I have concluded  the pastimes  making  obeisance

(75) Therefore I cannot describe all those pastimes; offering my obeisances to them, I have brought my account to a close.

যে কিছু কহিলুঁ, এই দিগ্দরশন ৷  
এই অনুসারে হবে তার আস্বাদন ॥ ৭৬ ॥

ye kichu kahilum̐, ei dig-daraśana  
what  little  I have told  this  an indication of direction  
ei anusāre habe tāra āsvādana  
this  following  will be  of them  the relishing

(76) What little I have told is only an indication of the way; by following it one can relish those pastimes.

প্রভুর গম্ভীর-লীলা না পারি বুঝিতে ৷  
বুদ্ধি-প্রবেশ নাহি তাতে, না পারি বর্ণিতে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

prabhura gambhīra-līlā nā pāri bujhite  
the Lord’s  profound pastimes  not  I can  understand  
buddhi-praveśa nāhi tāte, nā pāri varṇite  
of the intellect  entry  there is none  into them  not  I can  describe

(77) I cannot understand the Lord’s profound pastimes; my intellect cannot enter them, and so I cannot describe them.[^24]

সব শ্রোতা-বৈষ্ণবের বন্দিয়া চরণ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিত্র-বর্ণন কৈলুঁ সমাপন ॥ ৭৮ ॥

saba śrotā-vaiṣṇavera vandiyā caraṇa  
of all  the listening  Vaiṣṇavas  honouring  the feet  
caitanya-caritra-varṇana kailum̐ samāpana  
Caitanya’s  deeds  the description  I have  concluded

(78) Bowing at the feet of all the Vaiṣṇavas who hear this, I bring to a close my description of Śrī Caitanya’s deeds.

আকাশ — অনন্ত, তাতে যৈছে পক্ষিগণ ৷  
যার যত শক্তি, তত করে আরোহণ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

ākāśa — ananta, tāte yaiche pakṣi-gaṇa  
the sky  is unlimited  in it  as  the birds  
yāra yata śakti, tata kare ārohaṇa  
whose  as much  strength  that much  makes  ascent

(79) The sky is unlimited, yet each bird in it flies only as high as its strength allows.[^25]

ঐছে মহাপ্রভুর লীলা নাহি ওর-পার ৷  
’জীব’ হঞা কেবা সম্যক্ পারে বর্ণিবার ? ॥ ৮০ ॥

aiche mahāprabhura līlā nāhi ora-pāra  
so  Mahāprabhu’s  pastimes  there is no  near shore or far  
’jīva’ hañā kebā samyak pāre varṇibāra?  
a jīva  being  who  fully  can  describe

(80) So too Mahāprabhu’s pastimes have no shore on either side. Who, being only a *jīva*, can describe them fully?

যাবৎ বুদ্ধির গতি, ততেক বর্ণিলুঁ ৷  
সমুদ্রের মধ্যে যেন এক কণ ছুঁইলুঁ ॥ ৮১ ॥

yāvat buddhira gati, tateka varṇilum̐  
as far as  of the intelligence  the reach  so much  I have described  
samudrera madhye yena eka kaṇa chum̐ilum̐  
of the ocean  in the midst  as if  one  drop  I have touched

(81) I have described as much as my intelligence could reach — as if I had touched a single drop in the midst of the ocean.

নিত্যানন্দ-কৃপাপাত্র — বৃন্দাবন-দাস ৷  
চৈতন্যলীলায় তেঁহো হয়েন ‘আদিব্যাস’ ॥ ৮২ ॥

nityānanda-kṛpā-pātra — vṛndāvana-dāsa  
of Nityānanda  of the mercy  the vessel  —  Vṛndāvana dāsa  
caitanya-līlāya tem̐ho hayena ‘ādi-vyāsa’  
in Caitanya’s pastimes  he  is  ‘the original Vyāsa’

(82) Vṛndāvana dāsa, the vessel of Nityānanda’s mercy, is the original Vyāsa (*ādi-vyāsa*) of Caitanya’s pastimes.[^26]

তাঁর আগে যদ্যপি সব লীলার ভাণ্ডার ৷  
তথাপি অল্প বর্ণিয়া ছাড়িলেন আর ॥ ৮৩ ॥

tām̐ra āge yadyapi saba līlāra bhāṇḍāra  
of him  before  although  of all  the pastimes  the storehouse  
tathāpi alpa varṇiyā chāḍilena āra  
still  a little  having described  he left  the rest

(83) Although the storehouse of all the pastimes lay before him, he described only a little and left the rest.

যে কিছু বর্ণিলুঁ, সেহ সংক্ষেপ করিয়া ৷  
লিখিতে না পারেন, তবু রাখিয়াছেন লিখিয়া ॥ ৮৪ ॥

ye kichu varṇilum̐, seha saṅkṣepa kariyā  
what  little  I have described  that too  brief  making  
likhite nā pārena, tabu rākhiyāchena likhiyā  
to write  not  he could  yet  he has kept  having written

(84) Even the little I have described I have told only in brief — things he could not write out in full, though he did record them.

চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে তেঁহো লিখিয়াছেন স্থানে-স্থানে ৷  
সেই বচন শুন, সেই পরম-প্রমাণে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

caitanya-maṅgale tem̐ho likhiyāchena sthāne-sthāne  
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  he  has written  here and there  
sei vacana śuna, sei parama-pramāṇe  
those  words  hear  that  the supreme evidence

(85) Here and there in the *Caitanya-maṅgala* he has written of them. Hear those words: they are the supreme evidence.[^27]

সংক্ষেপে কহিলুঁ, বিস্তার না যায় কথনে ৷  
বিস্তারিয়া বেদব্যাস করিবেন বর্ণনে ॥ ৮৬ ॥

saṅkṣepe kahilum̐, vistāra nā yāya kathane  
in brief  I have told  in detail  not  is possible  in the telling  
vistāriyā veda-vyāsa karibena varṇane  
in detail  Vedavyāsa  will make  the description

(86) I have told it in brief, for it cannot be told in full. Vedavyāsa will describe it in detail.

চৈতন্যমঙ্গলে ইহা লিখিয়াছে স্থানে-স্থানে ৷  
সত্য কহেন, — আগে ব্যাস করিলা বর্ণনে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

caitanya-maṅgale ihā likhiyāche sthāne-sthāne  
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  this  he has written  here and there  
satya kahena, — āge vyāsa karilā varṇane  
truth  he speaks  —  afterwards  Vyāsa  made  the description

(87) He has written this here and there in the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, and he speaks the truth: afterwards Vyāsa described these pastimes.[^28][^29]

চৈতন্য-লীলামৃত-সিন্ধু — দুগ্ধাব্ধি-সমান ৷  
তৃষ্ণানুরূপ ঝারী ভরি’ তেঁহো কৈলা পান ॥ ৮৮ ॥

caitanya-līlāmṛta-sindhu — dugdhābdhi-samāna  
of Caitanya’s pastimes  of the nectar  the ocean  —  to the ocean of milk  equal  
tṛṣṇānurūpa jhārī bhari’ tem̐ho kailā pāna  
according to his thirst  the pitcher  filling  he  made  drinking

(88) The nectar-ocean of Caitanya’s pastimes is like the Ocean of Milk. He filled his pitcher as his thirst required and drank.

তাঁর ঝারী-শেষামৃত কিছু মোরে দিলা ৷  
ততেকে ভরিল পেট, তৃষ্ণা মোর গেলা ॥ ৮৯ ॥

tām̐ra jhārī-śeṣāmṛta kichu more dilā  
his  of the pitcher  the remnant nectar  a little  to me  gave  
tateke bharila peṭa, tṛṣṇā mora gelā  
by that much  was filled  the stomach  thirst  my  went

(89) He gave me a little of the nectar left in his pitcher, and that little filled me and quenched my thirst.[^30]

আমি — অতিক্ষুদ্র জীব, পক্ষী রাঙ্গাটুনি ৷  
সে যৈছে তৃষ্ণায় পিয়ে সমুদ্রের পানী ॥ ৯০ ॥

তৈছে আমি এক কণ ছুঁইলুঁ লীলার ৷  
এই দৃষ্টান্তে জানিহ প্রভুর লীলার বিস্তার ॥ ৯১ ॥

āmi — ati-kṣudra jīva, pakṣī rāṅgā-ṭuni  
I  —  very tiny  soul  a bird  rāṅgā-ṭuni  
se yaiche tṛṣṇāya piye samudrera pānī  
it  as  in thirst  drinks  of the ocean  the water  
taiche āmi eka kaṇa chum̐ilum̐ līlāra  
so  I  one  drop  have touched  of the pastimes  
ei dṛṣṭānte jāniha prabhura līlāra vistāra  
by this  example  know  the Lord’s  pastimes’  vastness

(90–91) I am a very tiny soul, like the little *rāṅgā-ṭuni* bird. As that bird, when thirsty, drinks the water of the ocean, so I have touched a single drop of the pastimes. By this example know how vast the Lord’s pastimes are.[^31]

’আমি লিখি’, — ইহা মিথ্যা করি অনুমান ৷  
আমার শরীর — কাষ্ঠপুতলী-সমান ॥ ৯২ ॥

’āmi likhi’, — ihā mithyā kari anumāna  
‘I  write’  —  this  false  I consider  a supposition  
āmāra śarīra — kāṣṭha-putalī-samāna  
my  body  —  to a wooden puppet equal

(92) To think “I am writing” is a false notion: my body is like a wooden puppet.[^32]

বৃদ্ধ জরাতুর আমি অন্ধ, বধির ৷  
হস্ত হালে, মনোবুদ্ধি নহে মোর স্থির ॥ ৯৩ ॥

vṛddha jarātura āmi andha, badhira  
old  infirm with age  I  blind  deaf  
hasta hāle, mano-buddhi nahe mora sthira  
the hands  shake  mind and intelligence  are not  my  steady

(93) I am old and infirm, blind and deaf; my hands shake, and my mind and intelligence are not steady.[^33]

নানা-রোগগ্রস্ত, — চলিতে বসিতে না পারি ৷  
পঞ্চরোগ-পীড়া-ব্যাকুল, রাত্রি-দিনে মরি ॥ ৯৪ ॥

nānā-roga-grasta, — calite vasite nā pāri  
by many diseases seized  —  to walk  to sit  not  I can  
pañca-roga-pīḍā-vyākula, rātri-dine mari  
by the pain of five diseases tormented  night and day  I die

(94) Many diseases have seized me; I can neither walk nor sit. Tormented by the pain of five ailments, day and night I am all but dying.

পূর্ব্বে গ্রন্থে ইহা কৈরাছি নিবেদন ৷  
তথাপি লিখিয়ে, শুন ইহার কারণ ॥ ৯৫ ॥

pūrve granthe ihā kairāchi nivedana  
earlier  in the book  this  I have made  submission  
tathāpi likhiye, śuna ihāra kāraṇa  
still  I write  hear  of this  the reason

(95) I have already confessed this earlier in the book. Still I write — hear the reason for it.

শ্রীগোবিন্দ, শ্রীচৈতন্য, শ্রীনিত্যানন্দ ৷  
শ্রীঅদ্বৈত, শ্রীভক্ত, আর শ্রীশ্রোতৃবৃন্দ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

শ্রীস্বরূপ, শ্রীরূপ, শ্রীসনাতন ৷  
শ্রীরঘুনাথ দাস শ্রীগুরু, শ্রীজীব-চরণ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

ইঁহা-সবার চরণ-কৃপায় লেখায় আমারে ৷  
আর এক হয় — তেঁহো অতিকৃপা করে ॥ ৯৮ ॥

śrī govinda, śrī caitanya, śrī nityānanda  
Śrī  Govinda  Śrī  Caitanya  Śrī  Nityānanda  
śrī advaita, śrī bhakta, āra śrī śrotṛ-vṛnda  
Śrī  Advaita  the revered  devotees  and  the revered  listeners  
śrī svarūpa, śrī rūpa, śrī sanātana  
Śrī  Svarūpa  Śrī  Rūpa  Śrī  Sanātana  
śrī raghunātha dāsa śrī guru, śrī jīva-caraṇa  
Śrī  Raghunātha  dāsa  Śrī  guru  Śrī  Jīva’s feet  
im̐hā-sabāra caraṇa-kṛpāya lekhāya āmāre  
of all these  by the mercy of the feet  make write  me  
āra eka haya — tem̐ho ati-kṛpā kare  
and  one  there is  —  He  great mercy  shows

(96–98) Śrī Govinda, Śrī Caitanya, Śrī Nityānanda, Śrī Advaita, the devotees and the revered listeners; Śrī Svarūpa, Śrī Rūpa, Śrī Sanātana; Śrī Raghunātha dāsa, my guru, and the feet of Śrī Jīva — by the mercy of the feet of all these I am made to write. And there is One more: He shows me exceptional mercy.[^34]

শ্রীমদনগোপাল মোরে লেখায় আজ্ঞা করি’ ৷  
কহিতে না যুয়ায়, তবু রহিতে না পারি ॥ ৯৯ ॥

śrī madana-gopāla more lekhāya ājñā kari’  
Śrī  Madana-Gopāla  me  makes write  order  giving  
kahite nā yuyāya, tabu rahite nā pāri  
to say  not  is fitting  yet  to keep (silent)  not  I can

(99) Śrī Madana-Gopāla, by His order, makes me write. It is not fitting to say this, yet I cannot keep silent.

না কহিলে হয় মোর কৃতঘ্নতা-দোষ ৷  
দম্ভ করি বলি’, শ্রোতা, না করিহ রোষ ॥ ১০০ ॥

nā kahile haya mora kṛta-ghnatā-doṣa  
not  if I say  is  my  of ingratitude  the fault  
dambha kari bali’, śrotā, nā kariha roṣa  
pride  making  I speak  O listeners  not  make  anger

(100) If I did not say it, I would be guilty of ingratitude. Though I speak as if out of pride, O listeners, do not be angry.

তোমা-সবার চরণ-ধূলি করিনু বন্দন ৷  
তাতে চৈতন্য লীলা হৈল, যে কিছু লিখন ॥ ১০১ ॥

tomā-sabāra caraṇa-dhūli karinu vandana  
of you all  the dust of the feet  I have made  worship  
tāte caitanya līlā haila, ye kichu likhana  
by that  Caitanya’s  pastimes  came about  what  little  writing

(101) I have bowed down to the dust of the feet of you all, and by this the little I have written of Caitanya’s pastimes came to be.[^35]

এবে অন্ত্যলীলাগণের করি অনুবাদ ৷  
’অনুবাদ’ কৈলে পাই লীলার ‘আস্বাদ’ ॥ ১০২ ॥

ebe antya-līlā-gaṇera kari anuvāda  
now  of the Antya-līlā pastimes  I make  recapitulation  
’anuvāda’ kaile pāi līlāra ‘āsvāda’  
‘recapitulation’  by making  I get  of the pastimes  ‘relish’

(102) Now I shall recapitulate the pastimes of the Antya-līlā: by recapitulating (*anuvāda*) I relish the pastimes again.[^36]

প্রথম পরিচ্ছেদে — রূপের দ্বিতীয়-মিলন ৷  
তার মধ্যে দুইনাটকের বিধান-শ্রবণ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

prathama paricchede — rūpera dvitīya-milana  
in the first  chapter  —  Rūpa’s  second meeting  
tāra madhye dui-nāṭakera vidhāna-śravaṇa  
of that  in the midst  of the two plays  the plan hearing

(103) In the first chapter: Rūpa’s second meeting with the Lord, and how the Lord heard of his plan for the two plays.

তার মধ্যে শিবানন্দ-সঙ্গে কুক্কুর আইলা ৷  
প্রভু তারে কৃষ্ণ কহাঞা মুক্ত করিলা ॥ ১০৪ ॥

tāra madhye śivānanda-saṅge kukkura āilā  
of that  in the midst  with Śivānanda  a dog  came  
prabhu tāre kṛṣṇa kahāñā mukta karilā  
the Lord  it  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  making say  liberated  made

(104) In it a dog came with Śivānanda, and the Lord, making it say “Kṛṣṇa”, liberated it.

দ্বিতীয়ে — ছোট-হরিদাসে করাইলা শিক্ষণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে শিবানন্দের আশ্চর্য্য দর্শন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

dvitīye — choṭa-haridāse karāilā śikṣaṇa  
in the second  —  to Choṭa Haridāsa  caused  instruction  
tāra madhye śivānandera āścarya darśana  
of that  in the midst  Śivānanda’s  wonderful  vision

(105) In the second: the lesson the Lord gave Choṭa Haridāsa, and Śivānanda’s wonderful vision.

তৃতীয়ে — হরিদাসের মহিমা প্রচণ্ড ৷  
দামোদর-পণ্ডিত কৈলা প্রভুরে বাক্যদণ্ড ॥ ১০৬ ॥

tṛtīye — haridāsera mahimā pracaṇḍa  
in the third  —  of Haridāsa  the glory  mighty  
dāmodara-paṇḍita kailā prabhure vākya-daṇḍa  
Dāmodara Paṇḍita  made  to the Lord  rebuke in words

(106) In the third: the mighty glory of Haridāsa, and Dāmodara Paṇḍita’s rebuke to the Lord.

প্রভু ‘নাম’ দিয়া কৈলা ব্রহ্মাণ্ড-মোচন ৷  
হরিদাস করিলা নামের মহিমা-স্থাপন ॥ ১০৭ ॥

prabhu ‘nāma’ diyā kailā brahmāṇḍa-mocana  
the Lord  ‘the Name’  giving  made  the universe’s deliverance  
haridāsa karilā nāmera mahimā-sthāpana  
Haridāsa  made  of the Name  the glory’s establishment

(107) By giving the holy name, the Lord delivered the universe, and Haridāsa established the glory of the name.

চতুর্থে — শ্রীসনাতনের দ্বিতীয়-মিলন ৷  
দেহত্যাগ হৈতে তাঁর করিলা রক্ষণ ॥ ১০৮ ॥

caturthe — śrī-sanātanera dvitīya-milana  
in the fourth  —  of Śrī Sanātana  the second meeting  
deha-tyāga haite tām̐ra karilā rakṣaṇa  
giving up the body  from  his  made  protection

(108) In the fourth: Śrī Sanātana’s second meeting with the Lord, who saved him from giving up his body.

জ্যৈষ্ঠ-মাসের ধূপে প্রভু তাঁরে কৈলা পরীক্ষণ ৷  
শক্তি সঞ্চারিয়া পুনঃ পাঠাইলা বৃন্দাবন ॥ ১০৯ ॥

jyaiṣṭha-māsera dhūpe prabhu tām̐re kailā parīkṣaṇa  
of the month of Jyaiṣṭha  in the blazing sun  the Lord  him  made  test  
śakti sañcāriyā punaḥ pāṭhāilā vṛndāvana  
power  infusing  again  sent  to Vṛndāvana

(109) In the blazing sun of the month of Jyaiṣṭha the Lord tested him; then, investing him with power, He sent him back to Vṛndāvana.

পঞ্চমে — প্রদ্যুম্নমিশ্রে প্রভু কৃপা কৈলা ৷  
রায়-দ্বারা কৃষ্ণকথা তাঁরে শুনাইলা ॥ ১১০ ॥

pañcame — pradyumna-miśre prabhu kṛpā kailā  
in the fifth  —  to Pradyumna Miśra  the Lord  mercy  showed  
rāya-dvārā kṛṣṇa-kathā tām̐re śunāilā  
through Rāya  talks of Kṛṣṇa  to him  made hear

(110) In the fifth: the Lord’s mercy to Pradyumna Miśra, whom He sent to hear talks of Kṛṣṇa from Rāmānanda Rāya.

তার মধ্যে ‘বাঙ্গাল’ কবির নাটক-উপেক্ষণ ৷  
স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি কৈলা বিগ্রহের মহিমা স্থাপন ॥ ১১১ ॥

tāra madhye ‘bāṅgāla’ kavira nāṭaka-upekṣaṇa  
of that  in the midst  ‘Bengali’  poet’s  the play rejection  
svarūpa-gosāñi kailā vigrahera mahimā sthāpana  
Svarūpa Gosāñi  made  of the Deity  glory  establishment

(111) In it: the rejection of a “Bengali” poet’s play, and how Svarūpa Gosāñi established the glory of the Deity (*vigraha*).

ষষ্ঠে — রঘুনাথ-দাস প্রভুরে মিলিলা ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ-আজ্ঞায় চিড়া-মহোৎসব কৈলা ॥ ১১২ ॥

ṣaṣṭhe — raghunātha-dāsa prabhure mililā  
in the sixth  —  Raghunātha dāsa  the Lord  met  
nityānanda-ājñāya ciḍā-mahotsava kailā  
by Nityānanda’s order  the ciḍā festival  held

(112) In the sixth: Raghunātha dāsa met the Lord, and by Nityānanda’s order he held the festival of flat rice (*ciḍā*).

দামোদর-স্বরূপ-ঠাঞি তাঁরে সমর্পিল ৷  
’গোবর্দ্ধন-শিলা’, ‘গুঞ্জামালা’ তাঁরে দিল ॥ ১১৩ ॥

dāmodara-svarūpa-ṭhāñi tām̐re samarpila  
in Dāmodara Svarūpa’s care  him  entrusted  
’govardhana-śilā’, ‘guñjā-mālā’ tām̐re dila  
‘the Govardhana stone’  ‘the guñjā garland’  to him  gave

(113) The Lord entrusted him to Svarūpa Dāmodara and gave him the *śilā* from Govardhana and the *guñjā* garland.

সপ্তম-পরিচ্ছেদে — বল্লভ-ভট্টের মিলন ৷  
নানা মতে কৈলা তাঁর গর্ব্ব খণ্ডন ॥ ১১৪ ॥

saptama-paricchede — vallabha-bhaṭṭera milana  
in the seventh chapter  —  of Vallabha Bhaṭṭa  the meeting  
nānā mate kailā tām̐ra garva khaṇḍana  
various  in ways  made  his  pride  breaking

(114) In the seventh chapter: the meeting with Vallabha Bhaṭṭa, whose pride the Lord broke in various ways.

অষ্টমে — রামচন্দ্র-পুরীর আগমন ৷  
তাঁর ভয়ে কৈলা প্রভু ভিক্ষা সঙ্কোচন ॥ ১১৫ ॥

aṣṭame — rāmacandra-purīra āgamana  
in the eighth  —  of Rāmacandra Purī  the arrival  
tām̐ra bhaye kailā prabhu bhikṣā saṅkocana  
of him  out of fear  made  the Lord  meals  reduction

(115) In the eighth: the arrival of Rāmacandra Purī, for fear of whom the Lord reduced His meals.

নবমে — গোপীনাথ-পট্টনায়ক-মোচন ৷  
ত্রিজগতের লোক প্রভুর পাইল দরশন ॥ ১১৬ ॥

navame — gopīnātha-paṭṭanāyaka-mocana  
in the ninth  —  Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka’s release  
trijagatera loka prabhura pāila daraśana  
of the three worlds  the people  of the Lord  received  sight

(116) In the ninth: the release of Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka; and the people of the three worlds received the Lord’s darśana.

দশমে — কহিলুঁ ভক্তদত্ত-আস্বাদন ৷  
রাঘব-পণ্ডিতের তাঁহা ঝালির সাজন ॥ ১১৭ ॥

daśame — kahilum̐ bhakta-datta-āsvādana  
in the tenth  —  I told  the relishing of what the devotees gave  
rāghava-paṇḍitera tām̐hā jhālira sājana  
of Rāghava Paṇḍita  there  of the bags  the packing

(117) In the tenth I told how the Lord relished what the devotees gave Him, and how Rāghava Paṇḍita packed his bags (*jhāli*).

তার মধ্যে গোবিন্দের কৈলা পরীক্ষণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে পরিমুণ্ডা-নৃত্যের বর্ণন ॥ ১১৮ ॥

tāra madhye govindera kailā parīkṣaṇa  
of that  in the midst  of Govinda  made  testing  
tāra madhye parimuṇḍā-nṛtyera varṇana  
of that  in the midst  of the dance in a circle  the description

(118) There the Lord tested Govinda, and there too is the description of the dance in a circle (*parimuṇḍā-nṛtya*).

একাদশে — হরিদাস-ঠাকুরের নির্য্যাণ ৷  
ভক্ত-বাৎসল্য যাঁহা দেখাইলা গৌরভগবান্ ॥ ১১৯ ॥

ekādaśe — haridāsa-ṭhākurera niryāṇa  
in the eleventh  —  of Haridāsa Ṭhākura  the departure  
bhakta-vātsalya yām̐hā dekhāilā gaura-bhagavān  
affection for devotees  where  showed  Gaura Bhagavān

(119) In the eleventh: the departure of Haridāsa Ṭhākura, in which Gaura Bhagavān showed His affection for His devotees.

দ্বাদশে — জগদানন্দের তৈল-ভঞ্জন ৷  
নিত্যানন্দ কৈলা শিবানন্দেরে তাড়ন ॥ ১২০ ॥

dvādaśe — jagadānandera taila-bhañjana  
in the twelfth  —  Jagadānanda’s  breaking of the oil pot  
nityānanda kailā śivānandere tāḍana  
Nityānanda  made  to Śivānanda  punishment

(120) In the twelfth: Jagadānanda’s breaking of the pitcher of oil, and Nityānanda’s punishment of Śivānanda.

ত্রয়োদশে — জগদানন্দ মথুরা যাই’ আইলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভু-দেবদাসীর গীত শুনিলা ॥ ১২১ ॥

trayodaśe — jagadānanda mathurā yāi’ āilā  
in the thirteenth  —  Jagadānanda  to Mathurā  having gone  came back  
mahāprabhu-deva-dāsīra gīta śunilā  
Mahāprabhu  of the devadāsī  song  heard

(121) In the thirteenth: Jagadānanda went to Mathurā and came back, and Mahāprabhu heard the song of a *devadāsī*.

রঘুনাথ-ভট্টাচার্য্যের তাঁহাই মিলন ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে কৃপা করি’ পাঠাইলা বৃন্দাবন ॥ ১২২ ॥

raghunātha-bhaṭṭācāryera tām̐hāi milana  
of Raghunātha Bhaṭṭācārya  there too  the meeting  
prabhu tām̐re kṛpā kari’ pāṭhāilā vṛndāvana  
the Lord  him  mercy  showing  sent  to Vṛndāvana

(122) There too is the meeting with Raghunātha Bhaṭṭācārya, whom the Lord in His mercy sent to Vṛndāvana.

চতুর্দ্দশে — দিব্যোন্মাদ-আরম্ভ বর্ণন ৷  
’শরীর’ এথা প্রভুর, ‘মন’ গেলা বৃন্দাবন ॥ ১২৩ ॥

caturdaśe — divyonmāda-ārambha varṇana  
in the fourteenth  —  of the divine madness the beginning  the description  
’śarīra’ ethā prabhura, ‘mana’ gelā vṛndāvana  
‘body’  here  the Lord’s  ‘mind’  went  to Vṛndāvana

(123) In the fourteenth: the beginning of the divine madness, when the Lord’s “body” was here and His “mind” had gone to Vṛndāvana.

তার মধ্যে প্রভুর সিংহদ্বারে পতন ৷  
অস্থি-সন্ধি-ত্যাগ, অনুভাবের উদ্গম ॥ ১২৪ ॥

tāra madhye prabhura siṁha-dvāre patana  
of that  in the midst  the Lord’s  at the Siṁha-dvāra  fall  
asthi-sandhi-tyāga, anubhāvera udgama  
of the bones the joints the loosening  of the ecstatic symptoms  the rise

(124) In it: the Lord’s fall at the Siṁha-dvāra, the loosening of His joints, and the rise of ecstatic symptoms (*anubhāva*).

চটক-পর্ব্বত দেখি’ প্রভুর ধাবন ৷  
তার মধ্যে প্রভুর কিছু প্রলাপ বর্ণন ॥ ১২৫ ॥

caṭaka-parvata dekhi’ prabhura dhāvana  
Caṭaka-parvata  seeing  the Lord’s  running  
tāra madhye prabhura kichu pralāpa varṇana  
of that  in the midst  the Lord’s  some  ecstatic raving  the description

(125) Seeing Caṭaka-parvata, the Lord ran towards it; and the chapter describes some of His ecstatic ravings (*pralāpa*).

পঞ্চদশ-পরিচ্ছেদে — উদ্যান-বিলাসে ৷  
বৃন্দাবনভ্রমে যাঁহা করিলা প্রবেশে ॥ ১২৬ ॥

pañcadaśa-paricchede — udyāna-vilāse  
in the fifteenth chapter  —  in the garden  pastimes  
vṛndāvana-bhrame yām̐hā karilā praveśe  
mistaking it for Vṛndāvana  where  made  entry

(126) In the fifteenth chapter: the pastimes in the garden, which the Lord entered, taking it for Vṛndāvana.

তার মধ্যে প্রভুর পঞ্চেন্দ্রিয়-আকর্ষণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে করিলা রাসে কৃষ্ণ-অন্বেষণ ॥ ১২৭ ॥

tāra madhye prabhura pañcendriya-ākarṣaṇa  
of that  in the midst  the Lord’s  of the five senses attraction  
tāra madhye karilā rāse kṛṣṇa-anveṣaṇa  
of that  in the midst  made  in the rāsa  search for Kṛṣṇa

(127) In it: the attraction of the Lord’s five senses, and His search for Kṛṣṇa in the rāsa dance.

ষোড়শে — কালিদাসে প্রভু কৃপা করিলা ৷  
বৈষ্ণবোচ্ছিষ্ট খাইবার ফল দেখাইলা ॥ ১২৮ ॥

ṣoḍaśe — kālidāse prabhu kṛpā karilā  
in the sixteenth  —  to Kālidāsa  the Lord  mercy  showed  
vaiṣṇavocchiṣṭa khāibāra phala dekhāilā  
the Vaiṣṇavas’ remnants  of eating  the fruit  showed

(128) In the sixteenth the Lord showed mercy to Kālidāsa and revealed the fruit of eating the remnants of Vaiṣṇavas.

শিবানন্দের বালকে শ্লোক করাইলা ৷  
সিংহদ্বারের দ্বারী প্রভুরে কৃষ্ণ দেখাইলা ॥ ১২৯ ॥

śivānandera bālake śloka karāilā  
Śivānanda’s  the boy  a verse  caused to compose  
siṁha-dvārera dvārī prabhure kṛṣṇa dekhāilā  
of the Siṁha-dvāra  the doorkeeper  to the Lord  Kṛṣṇa  showed

(129) The Lord had Śivānanda’s young son compose a verse, and the doorkeeper of the Siṁha-dvāra showed Kṛṣṇa to the Lord.

মহাপ্রসাদের তাঁহা মহিমা বর্ণিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণাধরামৃতের ফল-শ্লোক আস্বাদিলা ॥ ১৩০ ॥

mahā-prasādera tām̐hā mahimā varṇilā  
of mahā-prasāda  there  the glory  described  
kṛṣṇādharāmṛtera phala-śloka āsvādilā  
of the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips  the fruit-verse  relished

(130) There He described the glory of mahā-prasāda and relished the verse on the fruit of the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips.

সপ্তদশে — গাভী-মধ্যে প্রভুর পতন ৷  
কূর্ম্মাকার-অনুভাবের তাঁহাই উদ্গম ॥ ১৩১ ॥

saptadaśe — gābhī-madhye prabhura patana  
in the seventeenth  —  among the cows  the Lord’s  fall  
kūrmākāra-anubhāvera tām̐hāi udgama  
of the tortoise-form ecstatic symptom  there  the rise

(131) In the seventeenth: the Lord’s fall among the cows, and the ecstatic symptom (*anubhāva*) in which His body took the form of a tortoise.

কৃষ্ণের শব্দ-গুণে প্রভুর মন আকর্ষিলা ৷  
‘কাস্ত্র্যঙ্গ তে’ শ্লোকের অর্থ আবেশে করিলা ॥ ১৩২ ॥

kṛṣṇera śabda-guṇe prabhura mana ākarṣilā  
Kṛṣṇa’s  by the qualities of sound  the Lord’s  mind  was drawn  
‘kā stry aṅga te’ ślokera artha āveśe karilā  
‘what  woman  O dear  Your’  of the verse  the meaning  in ecstasy  made

(132) The qualities of Kṛṣṇa’s sound drew the Lord’s mind, and in ecstasy He explained the meaning of the verse *kā stry aṅga te*.

ভাব-শাবল্যে পুনঃ কৈলা প্রলাপন ৷  
কর্ণামৃত-শ্লোকের অর্থ কৈলা বিবরণ ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

bhāva-śābalye punaḥ kailā pralāpana  
in the mingling of emotions  again  made  raving  
karṇāmṛta-ślokera artha kailā vivaraṇa  
of the Karṇāmṛta verse  the meaning  made  explanation

(133) In a mingling of emotions (*bhāva-śābalya*) He again spoke in ecstatic raving, and He explained the meaning of a verse of the *Karṇāmṛta*.

অষ্টাদশ পরিচ্ছেদে — সমুদ্রে পতন ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-গোপী-জলকেলি তাঁহা দরশন ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

aṣṭādaśa paricchede — samudre patana  
eighteenth  in the chapter  —  into the ocean  the fall  
kṛṣṇa-gopī-jala-keli tām̐hā daraśana  
of Kṛṣṇa and the gopīs the water-play  there  the vision

(134) In the eighteenth chapter: the Lord’s fall into the ocean, and there His vision of the water-play of Kṛṣṇa and the gopīs.

তাঁহাই দেখিলা কৃষ্ণের বন্য-ভোজন ৷  
জালিয়া উঠাইল, প্রভু আইলা স্ব-ভবন ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

tām̐hāi dekhilā kṛṣṇera vanya-bhojana  
there too  He saw  Kṛṣṇa’s  forest meal  
jāliyā uṭhāila, prabhu āilā sva-bhavana  
a fisherman  lifted out  the Lord  came  to His own residence

(135) There too He saw Kṛṣṇa’s meal in the forest. A fisherman pulled Him out, and the Lord came back to His residence.

ঊনবিংশে — ভিত্ত্যে প্রভুর মুখসংঘর্ষণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণের বিরহ-স্ফূর্ত্তি-প্রলাপ বর্ণন ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

ūnaviṁśe — bhittye prabhura mukha-saṅgharṣaṇa  
in the nineteenth  —  against the wall  the Lord’s  face rubbing  
kṛṣṇera viraha-sphūrti-pralāpa varṇana  
Kṛṣṇa’s  separation-awakened raving  the description

(136) In the nineteenth: the Lord’s rubbing His face against the wall, and His ravings as separation from Kṛṣṇa welled up within Him.

বসন্ত-রজনীতে পুষ্পোদ্যানে বিহরণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণের সৌরভ্য-শ্লোকের অর্থ-বিবরণ ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

vasanta-rajanīte puṣpodyāne viharaṇa  
on a spring night  in the flower garden  wandering  
kṛṣṇera saurabhya-ślokera artha-vivaraṇa  
Kṛṣṇa’s  of the fragrance verse  the meaning-explanation

(137) There too: His wandering in a flower garden on a spring night, and His explanation of the verse on Kṛṣṇa’s fragrance.

বিংশতি-পরিচ্ছেদে — নিজ-‘শিক্ষাষ্টক’ পড়িয়া ৷  
তার অর্থ আস্বাদিলা প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

viṁśati-paricchede — nija-‘śikṣāṣṭaka’ paḍiyā  
in the twentieth chapter  —  His own ‘Śikṣāṣṭaka’  reciting  
tāra artha āsvādilā premāviṣṭa hañā  
its  meaning  relished  absorbed in love  becoming

(138) In the twentieth chapter: reciting His own *Śikṣāṣṭaka*, the Lord, absorbed in love, relished its meaning.

ভক্তে শিখাইতে যেই শিক্ষাষ্টক কহিলা ৷  
সেই শ্লোকাষ্টকের অর্থ পুনঃ আস্বাদিলা ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

bhakte śikhāite yei śikṣāṣṭaka kahilā  
the devotees  to teach  which  Śikṣāṣṭaka  He spoke  
sei ślokāṣṭakera artha punaḥ āsvādilā  
of those  eight verses  the meaning  again  relished

(139) He had spoken the *Śikṣāṣṭaka* to teach the devotees, and now He relished the meaning of those eight verses once more.

মুখ্য-মুখ্য-লীলার অর্থ করিলুঁ কথন ৷  
’অনুবাদ’ হৈতে স্মরে গ্রন্থ-বিবরণ ॥ ১৪০ ॥

mukhya-mukhya-līlāra artha karilum̐ kathana  
of the principal pastimes  the substance  I have made  telling  
’anuvāda’ haite smare grantha-vivaraṇa  
‘recapitulation’  from  is remembered  of the book the contents

(140) I have told the substance of the principal pastimes; by recapitulation (*anuvāda*) the contents of the book are remembered.[^37]

এক এক পরিচ্ছেদের কথা — অনেকপ্রকার ৷  
মুখ্য মুখ্য কহিলুঁ, কথা না যায় বিস্তার ॥ ১৪১ ॥

eka eka paricchedera kathā — aneka-prakāra  
each  each  of the chapter  the topics  —  of many kinds  
mukhya mukhya kahilum̐, kathā nā yāya vistāra  
principal  principal  I have told  the story  not  can  be expanded

(141) Each chapter has topics of many kinds. I have told only the principal ones, for the story cannot be told at length.[^38]

শ্রীরাধা-সহ ‘শ্রীমদনমোহন’ ৷  
শ্রীরাধা-সহ ‘শ্রীগোবিন্দ’-চরণ ॥ ১৪২ ॥

শ্রীরাধা-সহ শ্রীল ‘শ্রীগোপীনাথ’ ৷  
এই তিন ঠাকুর হয় ‘গৌড়িয়ার নাথ’ ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

śrī rādhā-saha ‘śrī madana-mohana’  
Śrī  with Rādhā  ‘Śrī  Madana-mohana’  
śrī rādhā-saha ‘śrī govinda’-caraṇa  
Śrī  with Rādhā  ‘Śrī  Govinda’s’ feet  
śrī rādhā-saha śrīla ‘śrī gopīnātha’  
Śrī  with Rādhā  the beautiful  ‘Śrī  Gopīnātha’  
ei tina ṭhākura haya ‘gauḍiyāra nātha’  
these  three  Deities  are  ‘the Lords of the Gauḍīyas’

(142–143) Śrī Madana-mohana with Śrī Rādhā, the feet of Śrī Govinda with Śrī Rādhā, and beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha with Śrī Rādhā — these three Deities are the Lords of the Gauḍīyas.[^39]

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য, শ্রীযুক্ত নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
শ্রীঅদ্বৈত-আচার্য্য, শ্রীগৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

শ্রীস্বরূপ, শ্রীরূপ, শ্রীসনাতন ৷  
শ্রীগুরু শ্রীরঘুনাথ, শ্রীজীব-চরণ ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

নিজ-শিরে ধরি’ এই সবার চরণ ৷  
যাহা হৈতে হয় সব বাঞ্ছিত-পূরণ ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

śrī kṛṣṇa-caitanya, śrī-yukta nityānanda  
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the glorious  Nityānanda  
śrī advaita-ācārya, śrī gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
Śrī  Advaita Ācārya  Śrī  Gaura’s devotees  
śrī svarūpa, śrī rūpa, śrī sanātana  
Śrī  Svarūpa  Śrī  Rūpa  Śrī  Sanātana  
śrī guru śrī raghunātha, śrī jīva-caraṇa  
Śrī  guru  Śrī  Raghunātha  Śrī  Jīva’s feet  
nija-śire dhari’ ei sabāra caraṇa  
on my own head  holding  of these  all  the feet  
yāhā haite haya saba vāñchita-pūraṇa  
which  from  comes  of all  desires the fulfilment

(144–146) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the glorious Nityānanda, Śrī Advaita Ācārya and the devotees of Śrī Gaura; Śrī Svarūpa, Śrī Rūpa, Śrī Sanātana; Śrī Raghunātha, my guru, and the feet of Śrī Jīva — I hold the feet of all these on my head, for from them comes the fulfilment of every desire.[^40]

সবার চরণ-কৃপা — ‘গুরু উপাধ্যায়ী’ ৷  
তার বাণী — শিষ্যা, তারে বহুত নাচাই ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

sabāra caraṇa-kṛpā — ‘guru upādhyāyī’  
of all  the mercy of the feet  —  ‘the guru  the teacher’  
tāra vāṇī — śiṣyā, tāre bahuta nācāi  
his  speech  —  the pupil  her  much  makes dance

(147) The mercy of the feet of all of them is the teacher (*upādhyāyī*), and the author’s speech is her pupil, whom she makes dance in many ways.[^41][^42]

শিষ্যার শ্রম দেখি’ গুরু নাচান রাখিলা ৷  
’কৃপা’ না নাচায়, ‘বাণী’ বসিয়া রহিলা ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

śiṣyāra śrama dekhi’ guru nācāna rākhilā  
of the pupil  the weariness  seeing  the teacher  dancing  stopped  
’kṛpā’ nā nācāya, ‘vāṇī’ vasiyā rahilā  
‘mercy’  not  makes dance  ‘speech’  sitting  remained

(148) Seeing the pupil’s weariness, the teacher stopped the dance. When “mercy” does not make her dance, “speech” sits still.

অনিপুণা বাণী আপনে নাচিতে না জানে ৷  
যত নাচাইলা, নাচি’ করিলা বিশ্রামে ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

anipuṇā vāṇī āpane nācite nā jāne  
unskilled  speech  by herself  to dance  not  knows  
yata nācāilā, nāci’ karilā viśrāme  
as much as  made dance  having danced  made  rest

(149) Unskilled speech does not know how to dance by herself: she danced as much as she was made to dance, and then she rested.

সব শ্রোতাগণের করি চরণ বন্দন ৷  
যাঁ-সবার চরণ-কৃপা — শুভের কারণ ॥ ১৫০ ॥

saba śrotā-gaṇera kari caraṇa vandana  
of all  the listeners  I make  to the feet  obeisance  
yām̐-sabāra caraṇa-kṛpā — śubhera kāraṇa  
of whom all  the mercy of the feet  —  of good  the cause

(150) I bow down at the feet of all my listeners, the mercy of whose feet is the cause of all good.[^43]

চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত যেই জন শুনে ৷  
তাঁর চরণ ধুঞা করোঁ মুঞি পানে ॥ ১৫১ ॥

caitanya-caritāmṛta yei jana śune  
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  who  person  hears  
tām̐ra caraṇa dhuñā karom̐ muñi pāne  
his  feet  washing  I make  I  drinking

(151) Whoever hears the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* — I wash his feet and drink that water.

শ্রোতার পদরেণু করোঁ মস্তক-ভূষণ ৷  
তোমরা এ-অমৃত পিলে সফল হৈল শ্রম ॥ ১৫২ ॥

śrotāra pada-reṇu karom̐ mastaka-bhūṣaṇa  
of the listener  the foot-dust  I make  the head’s ornament  
tomarā e-amṛta pile saphala haila śrama  
you  this nectar  having drunk  fruitful  became  the labour

(152) I make the dust of my listeners’ feet the ornament of my head. You have drunk this nectar, and my labour has borne fruit.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(153) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.[^44]

চরিতমমৃতমেতচ্ছ্রীলচৈতন্যবিষ্ণোঃ  
শুভদমশুভনাশি শ্রদ্ধয়াস্বাদয়েদ্যঃ ৷  
তদমলপদপদ্মে ভৃঙ্গতামেত্য সোঽয়ং  
রসয়তি রসমুচ্চৈঃ প্রেমমাধ্বীকপূরম্ ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

caritam amṛtam etac chrīla-caitanya-viṣṇoḥ  
the life-story  nectarean  this  of Śrīla Caitanya  Viṣṇu  
śubha-dam aśubha-nāśi śraddhayāsvādayed yaḥ  
bestowing good  destroying evil  with faith would relish  who  
tad-amala-pada-padme bhṛṅgatām etya so ‘yaṁ  
at his (the relisher’s) spotless lotus feet  bee-hood  attaining  he  this one (the author)  
rasayati rasam uccaiḥ prema-mādhvīka-pūram  
relishes  rasa  intensely  full of the honey-wine of prema

(154) This nectarean account of the life of Śrīla Caitanya, who is Viṣṇu Himself, bestows all good and destroys all evil. At the spotless lotus feet of whoever relishes it with faith I become a bee, and drink to the full the rasa that overflows with the intoxicating honey of prema.[^45][^46]

শ্রীমন্মদনগোপাল-গোবিন্দদেব-তুষ্টয়ে ৷  
চৈতন্যার্পিতমস্ত্বেতচ্চৈতন্যচরিতামৃতম্ ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

śrīman-madana-gopāla-  
of Śrīman Madana-Gopāla  
govinda-deva-tuṣṭaye  
and Govinda-deva  for the satisfaction  
caitanyārpitam astv etac  
offered to Caitanya  may be  this  
caitanya-caritāmṛtam  
Caitanya-caritāmṛta

(155) For the satisfaction of Śrīman Madana-Gopāla and Govinda-deva, may this *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* be offered to Śrī Caitanya.[^47]

পরিমলবাসিতভুবনং  
স্বরসোন্মাদিত-রসজ্ঞ-রোলম্বম্ ৷  
গিরিধরচরণাম্ভোজং কঃ  
খলু রসিকঃ সমীহতে হাতুম্ ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

parimala-vāsita-bhuvanaṁ  
by whose fragrance  perfumed  the world  
sva-rasonmādita-rasajña-rolambam  
by its own rasa  intoxicated  the rasa-knowing  bees  
giridhara-caraṇāmbhojaṁ kaḥ  
Giridhara’s  lotus feet  who  
khalu rasikaḥ samīhate hātum  
indeed  rasika  seeks  to abandon

(156) The lotus feet of Giridhara fill the world with their fragrance, and with their own rasa they intoxicate the bees who know rasa. What *rasika* could ever bear to leave them?[^48]

মৎপ্রাণসর্ব্বস্বপদাব্জরেণো-  
র্মদীশ্বরী-শ্রীযুতরাধিকায়াঃ ৷  
প্রাণোরুসর্ব্বস্বপদাব্জরেণুং  
শ্রীশ্রীল-গোবিন্দমহং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

mat-prāṇa-sarvasva-padābja-reṇor-  
of my life  the all-in-all  of the lotus feet  of the dust  
mad-īśvarī-śrī-yuta-rādhikāyāḥ  
of my Mistress  Śrī  Rādhikā  
prāṇoru-sarvasva-padābja-reṇuṁ  
more than life  the all-in-all  lotus feet  whose dust  
śrī-śrīla-govindam ahaṁ prapadye  
Śrī Śrīla Govinda  I  surrender to

(157) The dust of the lotus feet of my Mistress, Śrī Rādhikā, is the whole wealth of my life, and by its power I surrender to Śrī Śrīla Govinda, the dust of whose lotus feet is dearer to Her than life and is Her all in all.[^49]

শাকে সিন্ধ্বগ্নিবাণেন্দৌ জ্যৈষ্ঠে বৃন্দাবনান্তরে ৷  
সূর্য্যাহেঽসিতপঞ্চম্যাং গ্রন্থোঽয়ং পূর্ণতাং গতঃ ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

śāke sindhv-agni-vāṇendau  
in the Śaka year  ocean-fire-arrow-moon (1537)  
jyaiṣṭhe vṛndāvanāntare  
in Jyaiṣṭha  in Vṛndāvana  
sūryāhe ’sita-pañcamyāṁ  
on Sunday  on the fifth day of the dark fortnight  
grantho ’yaṁ pūrṇatāṁ gataḥ  
book  this  completion  reached

(158) In the Śaka year 1537, in the month of Jyaiṣṭha, in Vṛndāvana, on a Sunday, the fifth day of the dark fortnight, this book was completed.[^50][^51]

Heading in the edition: why the practice of the name is so easy — Kṛṣṇa’s great generosity; and why a jīva caught in offences cannot utter the pure name; the verse is cited in the *Padyāvalī* (19).

Heading in the edition: the one desire of the sincere practitioner — eternal and pure devotion; the verse is cited in the *Padyāvalī* (85).

[^10]: Heading in the edition: the practitioner, in his true self, begs mercy of Adhokṣaja, who enjoys spiritual pastimes; the verse is cited in the *Padyāvalī* (13).

[^11]: Heading in the edition: the outward signs of perfection (sādhya-bhakti); the verse is cited in the *Padyāvalī* (84).

[^12]: *Rasāntarāveśa* — absorption in another rasa: from the prayers of a servant (31–37) the Lord passes to the feelings of Śrī Rādhā in separation (39–60). Heading in the edition: the inner signs of perfection — worship rooted in transcendental separation (vipralambha) from Kṛṣṇa.

[^13]: Heading in the edition: the seventh verse of the *Śikṣāṣṭaka*, cited in the *Padyāvalī* (327).

[^14]: Heading in the edition: the Lord, filled with the feeling of Śrī Rādhā, the crown of those wholly in Kṛṣṇa’s power.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pinaṣṭu* — “may He make His own”; *lampaṭa* — absorbed in the joy of pleasing His own senses: “let Him do as He pleases, or sport with other beloveds.” Heading in the edition: the steadfastness (niṣṭhā) of perfection — exclusive dependence on Kṛṣṇa; the verse is cited in the *Padyāvalī* (134).

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: a devotee who thinks he has reached the goal in his own happiness ruins himself — he becomes a sahajiyā who seeks worldly enjoyment. Heading in the edition: reproach of one who longs for enjoyment (sambhoga) with Kṛṣṇa.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: the story is told in the *Āditya*, *Mārkaṇḍeya* (15.29) and *Padma Purāṇas*. The leprous husband of the chaste wife unknowingly disturbed the samādhi of the sage Māṇḍavya and was cursed to die at sunrise; she vowed not to let the sun rise, and Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva, pleased with her fidelity, healed her husband and gave him new life. The point: only such selfless fidelity — the desire for happiness in service alone — befits pure devotees. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: firm fidelity to Kṛṣṇa is the highest dharma of the jīva, born of śṛṅgāra-rasa.

[^18]: Heading in the edition: the Lord, filled with the feeling of Śrī Rādhā, relishes pure love (kevala-prema).

[^19]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *vrajeśvara-śuddha-prema*, “pure love for the Lord of Vraja,” which is translated here; it notes the reading of another manuscript, *vrajera viśuddha prema*, “the pure love of Vraja”; the *Anubhāṣya* also cites the reading *se prema*, “that love.” In the edition — *jāmbunada* (short *u*).

[^20]: Heading in the edition: in the desire to please Kṛṣṇa’s senses there is no desire to please one’s own; the supremely generous Gaura, through the *Śikṣāṣṭaka*, gives the jīvas the essence of the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* — sambandha, abhidheya and prayojana.

[^21]: Heading in the edition: for all His matchless depth, the Lord loses His composure in mahābhāva — the divine madness born of separation — as the ocean heaves at the rising of the full moon.

[^22]: Heading in the edition: fearing to make the book too long, the author stops describing the Lord’s manifestations of love.

[^23]: Heading in the edition: what is described in detail in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* is described briefly in this book, and what is described briefly there is described here in detail.

[^24]: Heading in the edition: though the author is guided by the will of Śrī Caitanya Himself, he speaks in humility.

[^25]: *Anubhāṣya*: compare *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 1.18.23 — as birds fly in the sky as far as their strength allows, so the wise describe the ways of Viṣṇu as far as they are able. Heading in the edition: the ocean of Gaura’s pastimes is beyond this world and beyond the senses (adhokṣaja); a conditioned soul cannot touch it, and the author, thinking himself a jīva, in deep humility only tries to touch a drop of it.

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: the later pure ācāryas who described Gaura’s pastimes after him are, as his non-different parts, also called “Vyāsa” (cf. 86–87). Heading in the edition: the greatness of Ṭhākura Vṛndāvana, and Gaura’s pastimes.

[^27]: *Caitanya-maṅgala* is the original title of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (cf. Ādi 8.33–35). Heading in the edition: the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, the definitive book on Viṣṇu, the Vaiṣṇavas and pure devotion to Viṣṇu, is the highest evidence (*pramāṇa*).

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya*: on the variant reading *āge vyāsa karibena varṇane*, “Vyāsa will describe them in the future”, these and similar words of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* refer to the later pure Vaiṣṇava ācāryas, headed by Kavirāja Gosvāmī, who described Gaura’s pastimes; following the “original Vyāsa” Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura, such ācāryas are also called “Vyāsa” (on 82).

[^33]: Heading in the edition: regarding himself as a mere instrument, the author describes his unfitness and speaks in humility.
