“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 17  
**A Brief Description of the Pastimes of Young Manhood**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে স্বৈরাদ্ভুতেঽহং তং চৈতন্যং যৎপ্রসাদতঃ ৷  
যবনাঃ সুমনায়ন্তে কৃষ্ণনামপ্রজল্পকাঃ ॥ ১ ॥

vande svairādbhutehaṁ taṁ  
I worship  whose acts are free and wondrous  I  Him  
caitanyaṁ yat-prasādataḥ  
Caitanya  by whose  grace  
yavanāḥ sumanāyante  
the yavanas  behave like the good  
kṛṣṇa-nāma-prajalpakāḥ  
the names of Kṛṣṇa  chanting aloud

(1) “I worship Śrī Caitanya, whose deeds are free and wondrous. By His grace even the yavanas, chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa, become saintly.”[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

কৈশোর-লীলার সূত্র করিল গণন ৷  
যৌবনলীলার সূত্র করি অনুক্রম ॥ ৩ ॥

kaiśora-līlāra sūtra karila gaṇana  
of the youthful  pastimes  an outline  I have made  count  
yauvana-līlāra sūtra kari anukrama  
of young manhood  of the pastimes  an outline  I make  in order

(3) I have given an outline of the pastimes of His youth; now I shall outline, in order, the pastimes of His young manhood.

বিদ্যা-সৌন্দর্য্য-সদ্বেশ-সম্ভোগ-নৃত্যকীর্ত্তনৈঃ ৷  
প্রেমনামপ্রদানৈশ্চ গৌরো দীব্যতি যৌবনে ॥ ৪ ॥

vidyā-saundarya-sad-veśa-  
with learning  beauty  fine attire  
sambhoga-nṛtya-kīrtanaiḥ  
enjoyment  dancing and kīrtana  
prema-nāma-pradānaiś ca  
love and the Name  by giving  and  
gauro dīvyati yauvane  
Gaura  plays  in young manhood

(4) “In His young manhood Gaura shone in His play: in learning, beauty and fine attire and their enjoyment, in dancing and kīrtana, and in giving away love and the holy name.”[^2]

যৌবন প্রবেশে অঙ্গের অঙ্গ বিভূষণ ৷  
দিব্য বস্ত্র, দিব্য বেশ, মাল্য-চন্দন ॥ ৫ ॥

yauvana praveśe aṅgera aṅga vibhūṣaṇa  
of young manhood  on the entry  of the body  the limbs  adorned  
divya vastra, divya veśa, mālya-candana  
splendid  garments  splendid  dress  garlands and sandal

(5) As He entered young manhood, ornaments graced every limb of His body, and He wore splendid garments and attire, garlands and sandal paste.

বিদ্যার ঔদ্ধত্যে কাঁহো না করে গণন ৷  
সকল পণ্ডিত জিনি’ করে অধ্যাপন ॥ ৬ ॥

vidyāra auddhatye kām̐ho nā kare gaṇana  
of learning  in the pride  anyone  not  does  regard  
sakala paṇḍita jini’ kare adhyāpana  
all  the paṇḍitas  defeating  does  teaching

(6) In the pride of His learning He took no account of anyone; He defeated all the paṇḍitas and began to teach.

বায়ুব্যাধিচ্ছলে কৈল প্রেম পরকাশ ৷  
ভক্তগণ লঞা কৈল বিবিধ বিলাস ॥ ৭ ॥

vāyu-vyādhicchale kaila prema parakāśa  
of a disorder of vāyu  under the pretext  made  love  manifest  
bhakta-gaṇa lañā kaila vividha vilāsa  
the devotees  taking with Him  made  various  pastimes

(7) Under the pretext of a disorder of the vital airs (*vāyu*), He revealed His love, and with the devotees He enjoyed many pastimes.[^3]

তবে ত’ করিলা প্রভু গয়াতে গমন ৷  
ঈশ্বরপুরীর সঙ্গে তথাই মিলন ॥ ৮ ॥

tabe ta’ karilā prabhu gayāte gamana  
then  indeed  made  the Lord  to Gayā  the journey  
īśvara-purīra saṅge tathāi milana  
Īśvara Purī  with  there  the meeting

(8) Then the Lord went to Gayā, and there He met Īśvara Purī.

দীক্ষা-অনন্তরে হৈল, প্রেমের প্রকাশ ৷  
দেশে আগমন পুনঃ প্রেমের বিলাস ॥ ৯ ॥

dīkṣā-anantare haila, premera prakāśa  
initiation  after  there was  of love  the manifestation  
deśe āgamana punaḥ premera vilāsa  
to His country  return  again  of love  the play

(9) After His initiation His love was revealed, and when He came home again, He gave Himself to the play of love.[^4]

শচীকে প্রেমদান, তবে অদ্বৈত-মিলন ৷  
অদ্বৈত পাইল বিশ্বরূপ-দরশন ॥ ১০ ॥

śacīke prema-dāna, tabe advaita-milana  
to Śacī  the gift of love  then  with Advaita  meeting  
advaita pāila viśvarūpa-daraśana  
Advaita  obtained  of the universal form  the vision

(10) He gave love to Śacī; then He met Advaita, and Advaita beheld His universal form.[^5]

প্রভুর অভিষেক তবে করিল শ্রীবাস ৷  
খাটে বসি’ প্রভু কৈলা ঐশ্বর্য্য প্রকাশ ॥ ১১ ॥

prabhura abhiṣeka tabe karila śrīvāsa  
the Lord’s  abhiṣeka  then  performed  Śrīvāsa  
khāṭe vasi’ prabhu kailā aiśvarya prakāśa  
on the couch  seated  the Lord  made  majesty  manifest

(11) Then Śrīvāsa performed the Lord’s abhiṣeka, and the Lord, seated on the couch, revealed His majesty.[^6]

তবে নিত্যানন্দ-স্বরূপের আগমন ৷  
প্রভুকে মিলিয়া পাইলা ষড়্ভুজ-দর্শন ॥ ১২ ॥

tabe nityānanda-svarūpera āgamana  
then  of Nityānanda Svarūpa  the arrival  
prabhuke miliyā pāilā ṣaḍ-bhuja-darśana  
the Lord  meeting  obtained  of the six-armed form  the vision

(12) Then Nityānanda Svarūpa came, and when He met the Lord, He was granted the vision of the six-armed form.[^7]

প্রথমে ষড়্ভুজ তাঁরে দেখাইল ঈশ্বর ৷  
শঙ্খচক্রগদাপদ্ম-শার্ঙ্গবেণুধর ॥ ১৩ ॥

prathame ṣaḍ-bhuja tām̐re dekhāila īśvara  
first  the six-armed form  to Him  showed  the Lord  
śaṅkha-cakra-gadā-padma-śārṅga-veṇu-dhara  
conch, disc, club, lotus,  Śārṅga bow and flute  holding

(13) First the Lord showed Him the six-armed form, holding conch, disc, club and lotus, the Śārṅga bow and the flute.

পাছে চতুর্ভুজ হৈলা, তিন অঙ্গ বক্র ৷  
দুই হস্তে বেণু বাজায়, দুই হস্তে শঙ্খ-চক্র ॥ ১৪ ॥

pāche catur-bhuja hailā, tina aṅga vakra  
afterwards  four-armed  He became  in three places  body  bent  
dui haste veṇu bājāya, dui haste śaṅkha-cakra  
in two  hands  the flute  He plays  in two  hands  conch and disc

(14) Then He became four-armed, His body bent in three places; with two hands He played the flute, and in the other two He held the conch and disc.

তবে ত’ দ্বিভুজ কেবল বংশীবদন ৷  
শ্যাম-অঙ্গ পীতবস্ত্র ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ১৫ ॥

tabe ta’ dvi-bhuja kevala vaṁśī-vadana  
then  indeed  two-armed  only  the flute at His lips  
śyāma-aṅga pīta-vastra vrajendra-nandana  
dark  body  yellow  garment  the son of Vraja’s king

(15) And then He was simply two-armed, with the flute at His lips: the son of Vraja’s king, dark of body and dressed in yellow.

তবে নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞির ব্যাস-পূজন ৷  
নিত্যানন্দাবেশে কৈল মুষল ধারণ ॥ ১৬ ॥

tabe nityānanda-gosāñira vyāsa-pūjana  
then  of Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Vyāsa-pūjā  
nityānandāveśe kaila muṣala dhāraṇa  
in the mood of Nityānanda  made  the club  holding

(16) Then Nityānanda Gosāñi performed the Vyāsa-pūjā, and the Lord, absorbed in the mood of Nityānanda, took up the club (*muṣala*).[^8]

তবে শচী দেখিল, রামকৃষ্ণ — দুই ভাই ৷  
তবে নিস্তারিল প্রভু জগাই-মাধাই ॥ ১৭ ॥

tabe śacī dekhila, rāma-kṛṣṇa — dui bhāi  
then  Śacī  saw  Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  two  brothers  
tabe nistārila prabhu jagāi-mādhāi  
then  delivered  the Lord  Jagāi and Mādhāi

(17) Then Śacī saw the two brothers as Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, and then the Lord delivered Jagāi and Mādhāi.[^9]

তবে সপ্তপ্রহর ছিলা প্রভু ভাবাবেশে ৷  
যথা তথা ভক্তগণ দেখিল বিশেষে ॥ ১৮ ॥

tabe sapta-prahara chilā prabhu bhāvāveśe  
then  seven  praharas  remained  the Lord  in ecstatic absorption  
yathā tathā bhakta-gaṇa dekhila viśeṣe  
wherever  the devotees  saw  in a special way

(18) Then for seven praharas the Lord remained absorbed in ecstasy, and the devotees, wherever they were, beheld Him in a special way.[^10]

বরাহ-আবেশ হৈলা মুরারি-ভবনে ৷  
তাঁর স্কন্ধে চড়ি’ প্রভু নাচিলা অঙ্গনে ॥ ১৯ ॥

varāha-āveśa hailā murāri-bhavane  
of Varāha  absorption  He had  in Murāri’s  house  
tām̐ra skandhe caḍi’ prabhu nācilā aṅgane  
his  on the shoulders  climbing  the Lord  danced  in the courtyard

(19) In Murāri’s house He became absorbed in the mood of Varāha, and the Lord climbed onto Murāri’s shoulders and danced in the courtyard.[^11]

তবে শুক্লাম্বরের কৈল তণ্ডুল ভক্ষণ ৷  
‘হরের্নাম’ শ্লোকের কৈল অর্থ বিবরণ ॥ ২০ ॥

tabe śuklāmbarera kaila taṇḍula bhakṣaṇa  
then  Śuklāmbara’s  made  rice  eating  
‘harer nāma’ ślokera kaila artha vivaraṇa  
‘harer nāma’  of the verse  made  meaning  explanation

(20) Then He ate the raw rice that Śuklāmbara had begged, and He explained the meaning of the verse *harer nāma*.[^12]

হরের্নাম হরের্নাম হরের্নামৈব কেবলম্ ৷  
কলৌ নাস্ত্যেব নাস্ত্যেব নাস্ত্যেব গতিরন্যথা ॥ ২১ ॥

harer nāma harer nāma  
of Hari  the name  of Hari  the name  
harer nāmaiva kevalam  
of Hari  the name  indeed  only  
kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva  
in Kali  there is not  indeed  there is not  indeed  
nāsty eva gatir anyathā  
there is not  indeed  way  other

(21) “The name of Hari, the name of Hari, the name of Hari alone! In the age of Kali there is no other way, there is no other way, there is no other way.” (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa, 38.126)[^13]

কলিকালে নামরূপে কৃষ্ণ-অবতার ৷  
নাম হৈতে হয় সর্ব্বজগৎ-নিস্তার ॥ ২২ ॥

kali-kāle nāma-rūpe kṛṣṇa-avatāra  
in the age of Kali  in the form of the Name  Kṛṣṇa’s  avatāra  
nāma haite haya sarva-jagat-nistāra  
the Name  from  comes  of the whole world  deliverance

(22) In the age of Kali, Kṛṣṇa descends in the form of the holy name, and through the name the whole world is delivered.

দার্ঢ়্য লাগি’ ‘হরের্নাম’-উক্তি তিনবার ৷  
জড় লোক বুঝাইতে পুনঃ ‘এব’-কার ॥ ২৩ ॥

dārḍhya lāgi’ ‘harer nāma’-ukti tina-vāra  
firmness  for the sake of  ‘harer nāma’  the words  three times  
jaḍa loka bujhāite punaḥ ‘eva’-kāra  
dull  people  to make understand  again  the word ‘eva’

(23) For firmness the words *harer nāma* are said three times, and the word *eva* is added again to make dull people understand.

‘কেবল’ শব্দে পুনরপি নিশ্চয়-করণ ৷  
জ্ঞান-যোগ-তপ আদি কর্ম্ম নিবারণ ॥ ২৪ ॥

‘kevala’ śabde punarapi niścaya-karaṇa  
‘kevala’  by the word  once again  certainty  is made  
jñāna-yoga-tapa ādi karma nivāraṇa  
knowledge, yoga, austerity  and the rest  karma  exclusion

(24) The word *kevala* (“alone”) makes it certain once more, and rules out knowledge, yoga, austerity, karma and all the rest.

অন্যথা যে মানে, তার নাহিক নিস্তার ৷  
নাহি, নাহি, নাহি — তিন উক্ত ‘এব’-কার ॥ ২৫ ॥

anyathā ye māne, tāra nāhika nistāra  
otherwise  who  holds  for him  there is no  deliverance  
nāhi, nāhi, nāhi — tina ukta ‘eva’-kāra  
no, no, no  three times  said  the word ‘eva’

(25) Whoever thinks otherwise has no deliverance: that is why “there is not” is said three times with *eva*.

তৃণ হৈতে নীচ হঞা সদা লবে নাম ৷  
আপনি নিরভিমানী, অন্যে দিবে মান ॥ ২৬ ॥

tṛṇa haite nīca hañā sadā labe nāma  
grass  than  lower  being  always  shall take  the Name  
āpani nirabhimānī, anye dibe māna  
oneself  free from pride  to others  shall give  honour

(26) Thinking oneself lower than the grass, one should always chant the name, seeking no honour for oneself and giving honour to others.

তরুসম সহিষ্ণুতা বৈষ্ণব করিবে ৷  
ভর্ৎসন তাড়নে কাকে কিছু না বলিবে ॥ ২৭ ॥

taru-sama sahiṣṇutā vaiṣṇava karibe  
like a tree  tolerance  the Vaiṣṇava  shall practise  
bhartsana tāḍane kāke kichu nā balibe  
when scolded or beaten  to anyone  anything  not  shall say

(27) A Vaiṣṇava should be as tolerant as a tree; scolded or beaten, he should say nothing to anyone.

কাটিলেহ তরু যেন কিছু না বোলয় ৷  
শুকাইয়া মরে, তবু জল না মাগয় ॥ ২৮ ॥

kāṭileha taru yena kichu nā bolaya  
even when cut  a tree  just as  anything  not  says  
śukāiyā mare, tabu jala nā māgaya  
drying up  it dies  yet  water  not  asks

(28) A tree says nothing even when it is cut down; it may wither and die, yet it never asks for water.

এইমত বৈষ্ণব কারে কিছু না মাগিবে ৷  
অযাচিত-বৃত্তি, কিম্বা শাক-ফল খাবে ॥ ২৯ ॥

ei-mata vaiṣṇava kāre kichu nā māgibe  
in this way  the Vaiṣṇava  of anyone  anything  not  shall ask  
ayācita-vṛtti, kimbā śāka-phala khābe  
living on what comes unasked  or  greens and fruit  shall eat

(29) So a Vaiṣṇava should ask nothing of anyone; he should live on what comes unasked, or eat greens and fruit.

সদা নাম লবে, যথা-লাভেতে সন্তোষ ৷  
এইমত আচার করে ভক্তিধর্ম্ম-পোষ ॥ ৩০ ॥

sadā nāma labe, yathā-lābhete santoṣa  
always  the Name  shall take  with whatever comes  content  
ei mata ācāra kare bhakti-dharma-poṣa  
such  conduct  makes  of bhakti-dharma  nourishment

(30) He should always chant the name and be content with whatever comes. Such conduct nourishes the dharma of devotion.

তৃণাদপি সুনীচেন তরোরিব সহিষ্ণুনা ৷  
অমানিনা মানদেন কীর্ত্তনীয়ঃ সদা হরিঃ ॥ ৩১ ॥

tṛṇād api su-nīcena  
than grass  even  lower  
taror iva sahiṣṇunā  
a tree  like  tolerant  
amāninā māna-dena  
free from pride  giving honour  
kīrtanīyaḥ sadā hariḥ  
to be glorified  always  Hari

(31) “Lower than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, seeking no honour and offering honour to others, one should always sing the glories of Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 3)[^14]

ঊর্দ্ধ্ব বাহু করি’ কহোঁ, শুন, সর্ব্বলোক ৷  
নাম-সূত্রে গাঁথি’ পর কণ্ঠে এই শ্লোক ॥ ৩২ ॥

ūrddhva bāhu kari’ kahom̐, śuna, sarva-loka  
upraised  arms  making  I say  listen  all people  
nāma-sūtre gām̐thi’ para kaṇṭhe ei śloka  
on the thread of the Name  stringing  wear  on the neck  this  verse

(32) With arms upraised I say: listen, everyone! String this verse on the thread of the holy name and wear it around your neck.

প্রভু-আজ্ঞায় কর এই শ্লোক আচরণ ৷  
অবশ্য পাইবে তবে শ্রীকৃষ্ণ-চরণ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

prabhu-ājñāya kara ei śloka ācaraṇa  
by the Lord’s order  do  this  verse  practise  
avaśya pāibe tabe śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇa  
surely  you will attain  then  Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(33) Live by this verse, as the Lord has ordered, and you will surely attain the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.[^15]

তবে প্রভু শ্রীবাসের গৃহে নিরন্তর ৷  
রাত্রে সংকীর্ত্তন কৈল এক সম্বৎসর ॥ ৩৪ ॥

tabe prabhu śrīvāsera gṛhe nirantara  
then  the Lord  in Śrīvāsa’s  house  continually  
rātre saṁkīrtana kaila eka samvatsara  
at night  saṅkīrtana  performed  one  full year

(34) Then for a whole year the Lord held saṅkīrtana every night in Śrīvāsa’s house.

কপাট দিয়া কীর্ত্তন করে পরম আবেশে ৷  
পাষণ্ডী হাসিতে আইসে, না পায় প্রবেশে ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kapāṭa diyā kīrtana kare parama āveśe  
the door  shutting  kīrtana  they do  in deep  absorption  
pāṣaṇḍī hāsite āise, nā pāya praveśe  
the unbelievers  to laugh  come  not  gain  entry

(35) They sang with the doors shut, deeply absorbed; the unbelievers came to mock, but could not get in.

কীর্ত্তন শুনি’ বাহিরে তারা জ্বলি’ পুড়ি’ মরে ৷  
শ্রীবাসেরে দুঃখ দিতে নানা যুক্তি করে ॥ ৩৬ ॥

kīrtana śuni’ bāhire tārā jvali’ puḍi’ mare  
the kīrtana  hearing  outside  they  burning  scorching  die  
śrīvāsere duḥkha dite nānā yukti kare  
to Śrīvāsa  trouble  to give  various  schemes  make

(36) Hearing the kīrtana from outside, they burned with envy and devised all kinds of schemes to trouble Śrīvāsa.

একদিন বিপ্র, নাম — ‘গোপাল চাপাল’ ৷  
পাষণ্ডী-প্রধান সেই দুর্ম্মুখ, বাচাল ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ভবানী-পূজার সব সামগ্রী লইয়া ৷  
রাত্রে শ্রীবাসের দ্বারে স্থান লেপাইয়া ॥ ৩৮ ॥

ekadina vipra, nāma — ‘gopāla cāpāla’  
one day  a brāhmaṇa  by name  Gopāla Cāpāla  
pāṣaṇḍī-pradhāna sei durmukha, vācāla  
of the unbelievers  the chief  that  foul-mouthed  babbler  
bhavānī-pūjāra saba sāmagrī laiyā  
for Bhavānī’s worship  all  the articles  taking  
rātre śrīvāsera dvāre sthāna lepāiyā  
at night  at Śrīvāsa’s  door  the place  smearing

(37–38) There was a brāhmaṇa named Gopāla Cāpāla, a foul-mouthed babbler and the chief of the unbelievers. One night he took all the articles for the worship of Bhavānī and smeared the ground at Śrīvāsa’s door, as if for worship.[^16]

কলার পাত উপরে থুইল ওড়ফুল ৷  
হরিদ্রা, সিন্দূর আর রক্তচন্দন, তণ্ডুল ॥ ৩৯ ॥

kalāra pāta upare thuila oḍa-phula  
of a banana  leaf  on  placed  oḍa flowers  
haridrā, sindūra āra rakta-candana, taṇḍula  
turmeric  vermilion  and  red sandal  rice

(39) On a banana leaf he set out oḍa flowers, turmeric, vermilion, red sandal paste and rice.

মদ্যভাণ্ড-পাশে ধরি’ নিজ ঘরে গেল ৷  
প্রাতঃকালে শ্রীবাস তাহা ত’ দেখিল ॥ ৪০ ॥

madya-bhāṇḍa-pāśe dhari’ nija ghare gela  
a wine pot  beside  placing  his own  home  went  
prātaḥ-kāle śrīvāsa tāhā ta’ dekhila  
in the morning  Śrīvāsa  that  indeed  saw

(40) He set a pot of wine beside them and went home, and in the morning Śrīvāsa saw it all.

বড় বড় লোকেরে আনিল বোলাইয়া ৷  
সবারে কহে শ্রীবাস হাসিয়া হাসিয়া ॥ ৪১ ॥

baḍa baḍa lokere ānila bolāiyā  
great  great  people  brought  calling  
sabāre kahe śrīvāsa hāsiyā hāsiyā  
to all  says  Śrīvāsa  laughing  laughing

(41) He called the respectable people of the town and, laughing heartily, said to them all:

নিত্য রাত্রে করি আমি ভবানী-পূজন ৷  
আমার মহিমা দেখ, ব্রাহ্মণ সজ্জন ॥ ৪২ ॥

nitya rātre kari āmi bhavānī-pūjana  
every  night  do  I  Bhavānī’s worship  
āmāra mahimā dekha, brāhmaṇa sajjana  
my  glory  see  brāhmaṇas  good people

(42) “Every night I worship Bhavānī. See my glory, good brāhmaṇas!”

তবে সব শিষ্টলোক করে হাহাকার ৷  
ঐছে কর্ম্ম হেথা কৈল কোন্ দুরাচার ॥ ৪৩ ॥

tabe saba śiṣṭa-loka kare hāhākāra  
then  all  the respectable people  make  cries of dismay  
aiche karma hethā kaila kon durācāra  
such  a deed  here  did  what  wicked man

(43) Then all those good people cried out in dismay: “What wicked man has done such a thing here?”

হাড়িকে আনিয়া সব দূর করাইল ৷  
জল-গোময় দিয়া সেই স্থান লেপাইল ॥ ৪৪ ॥

hāḍike āniyā saba dūra karāila  
a sweeper  bringing  all  had removed  
jala-gomaya diyā sei sthāna lepāila  
water and cow dung  with  that  place  had smeared

(44) They sent for a sweeper to clear it all away, and had the place cleansed with water and cow dung.

তিন দিন রহি’ সেই গোপাল চাপাল ৷  
সর্ব্বাঙ্গে হইল কুষ্ঠ, বহে রক্তধার ॥ ৪৫ ॥

tina dina rahi’ sei gopāla cāpāla  
three  days  after  that  Gopāla Cāpāla  
sarvāṅge haila kuṣṭha, vahe rakta-dhāra  
on all his limbs  arose  leprosy  flows  stream of blood

(45) Three days later Gopāla Cāpāla was struck with leprosy all over his body, and blood streamed from his sores.

সর্ব্বাঙ্গ বেড়িল কীটে, কাটে নিরন্তর ৷  
অসহ্য বেদনা, দুঃখে জ্বলয়ে অন্তর ॥ ৪৬ ॥

sarvāṅga beḍila kīṭe, kāṭe nirantara  
all his limbs  covered  worms  bite  constantly  
asahya vedanā, duḥkhe jvalaye antara  
unbearable  pain  in agony  burns  his heart

(46) Worms covered his whole body and gnawed at him without rest; the pain was unbearable, and his heart burned with agony.

গঙ্গাঘাটে বৃক্ষতলে রহে ত’ বসিয়া ৷  
একদিন বলে কিছু প্রভুকে দেখিয়া ॥ ৪৭ ॥

gaṅgā-ghāṭe vṛkṣa-tale rahe ta’ vasiyā  
at a Ganges ghāṭa  under a tree  remains  indeed  sitting  
ekadina bale kichu prabhuke dekhiyā  
one day  says  something  the Lord  seeing

(47) He sat under a tree at a ghāṭa on the Ganges, and one day, seeing the Lord, he spoke to Him:

গ্রাম সম্বন্ধে আমি তোমার মাতুল ৷  
ভাগিনা, মুই কুষ্ঠব্যাধিতে হঞাছি ব্যাকুল ॥ ৪৮ ॥

grāma sambandhe āmi tomāra mātula  
by village  relation  I  Your  maternal uncle  
bhāginā, mui kuṣṭha-vyādhite hañāchi vyākula  
nephew  I  by leprosy  have become  tormented

(48) “By village kinship I am Your maternal uncle. Nephew, I am tormented by leprosy.

লোক সব উদ্ধারিতে তোমার অবতার ৷  
মুঞি বড় দুঃখী, মোরে করহ উদ্ধার ॥ ৪৯ ॥

loka saba uddhārite tomāra avatāra  
people  all  to deliver  Your  descent  
muñi baḍa duḥkhī, more karaha uddhāra  
I  very  wretched  me  make  deliverance

(49) You have descended to deliver all people, and I am in great misery. Deliver me!”

এত শুনি’ মহাপ্রভুর হইল ক্রুদ্ধ মন ৷  
ক্রোধাবেশে বলে তারে তর্জ্জন-বচন ॥ ৫০ ॥

eta śuni’ mahāprabhura haila kruddha mana  
this  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  became  angry  the mind  
krodhāveśe bale tāre tarjana-vacana  
in a fit of anger  says  to him  threatening  words

(50) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu grew angry, and in His anger He spoke threatening words to him:

আরে পাপি, ভক্তদ্বেষি, তোরে না উদ্ধারিমু ৷  
কোটিজন্ম এই মতে কীড়ায় খাওয়াইমু ॥ ৫১ ॥

āre pāpi, bhakta-dveṣi, tore nā uddhārimu  
O  sinner  hater of devotees  you  not  I shall deliver  
koṭi-janma ei mate kīḍāya khāoyāimu  
ten million births  in this way  by worms  I shall have you eaten

(51) “You sinner, you hater of devotees! I will not deliver you. For ten million births I will have the worms devour you like this.

শ্রীবাসে করাইলি তুই ভবানী-পূজন ৷  
কোটি জন্ম হবে তোর রৌরবে পতন ॥ ৫২ ॥

śrīvāse karāili tui bhavānī-pūjana  
Śrīvāsa  made to perform  you  Bhavānī’s worship  
koṭi janma habe tora raurave patana  
ten million  births  will be  your  into Raurava  fall

(52) You made it look as though Śrīvāsa worshipped Bhavānī; for ten million births you will fall into the hell of Raurava.[^17]

পাষণ্ডী সংহারিতে মোর এই অবতার ৷  
পাষণ্ডী সংহারি’ ভক্তি করিমু সঞ্চার ॥ ৫৩ ॥

pāṣaṇḍī saṁhārite mora ei avatāra  
the unbelievers  to destroy  My  this  descent  
pāṣaṇḍī saṁhāri’ bhakti karimu sañcāra  
the unbelievers  destroying  bhakti  I shall  spread

(53) I have come to destroy the unbelievers, and when they are gone I shall spread devotion everywhere.”

এত বলি’ গেলা প্রভু করিতে গঙ্গাস্নান ৷  
সেই পাপী দুঃখ ভোগে, না যায় পরাণ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

eta bali’ gelā prabhu karite gaṅgā-snāna  
this  saying  went  the Lord  to take  His bath in the Ganges  
sei pāpī duḥkha bhoge, nā yāya parāṇa  
that  sinner  suffering  endures  not  goes  life

(54) With these words the Lord went to bathe in the Ganges, and the sinner went on suffering, unable to die.

সন্ন্যাস করিয়া যবে প্রভু নীলাচলে গেলা ৷  
তথা হৈতে যবে কুলিয়া গ্রামে আইলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

তবে সেই পাপী প্রভুর লইল শরণ ৷  
হিত উপদেশ কৈল হইয়া করুণ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sannyāsa kariyā yabe prabhu nīlācale gelā  
sannyāsa  taking  when  the Lord  to Nīlācala  went  
tathā haite yabe kuliyā grāme āilā  
there  from  when  to Kuliyā  village  came  
tabe sei pāpī prabhura laila śaraṇa  
then  that  sinner  of the Lord  took  shelter  
hita upadeśa kaila haiyā karuṇa  
beneficial  instruction  gave  becoming  compassionate

(55–56) After the Lord had taken sannyāsa and gone to Nīlācala, He once came from there to the village of Kuliyā. Then the sinner took shelter of Him, and the Lord, moved to compassion, gave him good counsel:[^18]

শ্রীবাস পণ্ডিতের স্থানে আছে অপরাধ ৷  
তথা, যাহ, তেঁহো যদি করেন প্রসাদ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

śrīvāsa paṇḍitera sthāne āche aparādha  
Śrīvāsa  Paṇḍita’s  with regard to  there is  offence  
tathā, yāha, tem̐ho yadi karena prasāda  
there  go  he  if  does  grace

(57) “You have offended Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita. Go to him, and if he shows you his grace,

তবে তোর হবে এই পাপ-বিমোচন ৷  
যদি পুনঃ ঐছে নাহি কর আচরণ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

tabe tora habe ei pāpa-vimocana  
then  your  will be  this  sin  release  
yadi punaḥ aiche nāhi kara ācaraṇa  
if  again  such  not  you do  conduct

(58) you will be freed from this sin, if you never act in such a way again.”

তবে বিপ্র আসি’ লইল শ্রীবাস-শরণ ৷  
তাঁহার কৃপায় হইল পাপ বিমোচন ॥ ৫৯ ॥

tabe vipra āsi’ laila śrīvāsa-śaraṇa  
then  the brāhmaṇa  coming  took  Śrīvāsa’s shelter  
tām̐hāra kṛpāya haila pāpa vimocana  
by his  grace  became  of sin  release

(59) So the brāhmaṇa came and took shelter of Śrīvāsa, and by his grace he was freed from his sin.

আর এক বিপ্র আইল কীর্ত্তন দেখিতে ৷  
দ্বারে কপাট, — না পাইল ভিতরে যাইতে ॥ ৬০ ॥

āra eka vipra āila kīrtana dekhite  
another  one  brāhmaṇa  came  kīrtana  to see  
dvāre kapāṭa, — nā pāila bhitare yāite  
at the door  the bolt  not  could  inside  go

(60) Another brāhmaṇa came to see the kīrtana, but the door was shut and he could not get in.

ফিরি’ গেল বিপ্র ঘরে মনে দুঃখ পাঞা ৷  
আর দিন প্রভুকে কহে গঙ্গায় দেখিয়া ॥ ৬১ ॥

phiri’ gela vipra ghare mane duḥkha pāñā  
returning  went  the brāhmaṇa  home  in his mind  grief  feeling  
āra dina prabhuke kahe gaṅgāya dekhiyā  
another  day  to the Lord  says  at the Ganges  seeing

(61) He went home with a heavy heart, and the next day, seeing the Lord at the Ganges, he said to Him:[^19]

শাপিব তোমারে মুঞি, পাঞাছি মনোদুঃখ ৷  
পৈতা ছিণ্ডিয়া শাপে প্রচণ্ড দুর্ম্মুখ ॥ ৬২ ॥

śāpiba tomāre muñi, pāñāchi mano-duḥkha  
I shall curse  You  I  I have got  grief of heart  
paitā chiṇḍiyā śāpe pracaṇḍa durmukha  
his thread  tearing  curses  fierce  foul-mouthed

(62) “I will curse You, for You have grieved me!” And tearing his sacred thread, the fierce, foul-mouthed man cursed Him:

সংসার-সুখ তোমার হউক বিনাশ ৷  
শাপ শুনি’ মহাপ্রভুর হইল উল্লাস ॥ ৬৩ ॥

saṁsāra-sukha tomāra hauka vināśa  
worldly  happiness  Your  may be  destroyed  
śāpa śuni’ mahāprabhura haila ullāsa  
the curse  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  was  delight

(63) “May Your worldly happiness be destroyed!” Hearing the curse, Mahāprabhu was filled with delight.

প্রভুর শাপ-বার্ত্তা শুনে হঞা শ্রদ্ধাবান্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মশাপ হৈতে তার হয় পরিত্রাণ ॥ ৬৪ ॥

prabhura śāpa-vārtā śune hañā śraddhāvān  
the Lord’s  curse  story  hears  becoming  faithful  
brahma-śāpa haite tāra haya paritrāṇa  
from a brāhmaṇa’s curse  his  becomes  deliverance

(64) Whoever hears with faith the story of the Lord’s curse is delivered from the curse of a brāhmaṇa.[^20]

মুকুন্দ-দত্তেরে কৈল দণ্ড-পরসাদ ৷  
খণ্ডিল তাহার চিত্তের সব অবসাদ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

mukunda-dattere kaila daṇḍa-parasāda  
to Mukunda Datta  gave  punishment as grace  
khaṇḍila tāhāra cittera saba avasāda  
destroyed  his  of heart  all  dejection

(65) To Mukunda Datta the Lord gave His grace in the form of punishment, and so dispelled all the dejection of his heart.[^21]

আচার্য্য-গোসাঞিরে প্রভু করে গুরুভক্তি ৷  
তাহাতে আচার্য্য বড় হয় দুঃখমতি ॥ ৬৬ ॥

ācārya-gosāñire prabhu kare guru-bhakti  
to Ācārya Gosāñi  the Lord  shows  reverence as to a guru  
tāhāte ācārya baḍa haya duḥkha-mati  
at that  the Ācārya  very  becomes  sad at heart

(66) The Lord honoured Ācārya Gosāñi as His guru, and this made the Ācārya very unhappy.

ভঙ্গী করি’ জ্ঞানমার্গ করিল ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
ক্রোধাবেশে প্রভু তারে কৈল অবজ্ঞান ॥ ৬৭ ॥

bhaṅgī kari’ jñāna-mārga karila vyākhyāna  
a pretence  making  the path of knowledge  made  explanation  
krodhāveśe prabhu tāre kaila avajñāna  
in anger  the Lord  to Him  showed  disregard

(67) So, as a ploy, He began to expound the path of knowledge, and the Lord in anger treated Him without respect.

তবে আচার্য্য-গোসাঞির আনন্দ হইল ৷  
লজ্জিত হইয়া প্রভু প্রসাদ করিল ॥ ৬৮ ॥

tabe ācārya-gosāñira ānanda haila  
then  of Ācārya Gosāñi  joy  arose  
lajjita haiyā prabhu prasāda karila  
abashed  becoming  the Lord  grace  showed

(68) Then Ācārya Gosāñi was filled with joy, and the Lord, abashed, showed Him His grace.[^22]

মুরারিগুপ্ত-মুখে শুনি’ রাম-গুণগ্রাম ৷  
ললাটে লিখিল তাঁর ‘রামদাস’ নাম ॥ ৬৯ ॥

murāri-gupta-mukhe śuni’ rāma-guṇa-grāma  
from Murāri Gupta’s  mouth  hearing  Rāma’s  many qualities  
lalāṭe likhila tām̐ra ‘rāmadāsa’ nāma  
on the forehead  wrote  his  ‘Rāmadāsa’  name

(69) Hearing from Murāri Gupta the glories of Rāma, the Lord wrote the name “Rāmadāsa” on his forehead.[^23]

শ্রীধরের লৌহপাত্রে কৈল জলপান ৷  
সমস্ত ভক্তেরে দিল ইষ্ট বরদান ॥ ৭০ ॥

śrīdharera lauha-pātre kaila jala-pāna  
Śrīdhara’s  from the iron pot  made  water drinking  
samasta bhaktere dila iṣṭa vara-dāna  
all  to the devotees  gave  desired  boons

(70) He drank water from Śrīdhara’s iron pot, and to all the devotees He granted the boons they desired.[^24]

হরিদাস ঠাকুরেরে করিল প্রসাদ ৷  
আচার্য্য-স্থানে মাতার খণ্ডাইল অপরাধ ॥ ৭১ ॥

haridāsa ṭhākurere karila prasāda  
Haridāsa Ṭhākura  showed  grace  
ācārya-sthāne mātāra khaṇḍāila aparādha  
at the Ācārya’s place  of His mother  removed  the offence

(71) He showed His grace to Haridāsa Ṭhākura, and freed His mother from her offence against the Ācārya.[^25]

ভক্তগণে প্রভু নাম-মহিমা কহিল ৷  
শুনিয়া পড়ুয়া তাঁহা অর্থবাদ কৈল ॥ ৭২ ॥

bhakta-gaṇe prabhu nāma-mahimā kahila  
to the devotees  the Lord  of the Name  the glory  told  
śuniyā paḍuyā tām̐hā artha-vāda kaila  
hearing  a student  there  explaining it away  made

(72) The Lord told the devotees of the glory of the holy name, and a student who heard it explained it away as mere praise (*arthavāda*).

নামে স্তুতিবাদ শুনি’ প্রভুর হৈল দুঃখ ৷  
সবারে নিষেধিল, — ইহার না দেখিহ মুখ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

nāme stuti-vāda śuni’ prabhura haila duḥkha  
of the Name  exaggerated praise  hearing  of the Lord  was  pain  
sabāre niṣedhila, — ihāra nā dekhiha mukha  
to all  He forbade  his  not  you shall see  face

(73) Hearing the name called an exaggeration, the Lord was pained, and He forbade everyone to look upon that man’s face.[^26]

সগণে সচেলে গিয়া কৈল গঙ্গাস্নান ৷  
ভক্তির মহিমা তাঁহা করিল ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ৭৪ ॥

sagaṇe sacele giyā kaila gaṅgā-snāna  
with His associates  in their clothes  going  made  bath in the Ganges  
bhaktira mahimā tām̐hā karila vyākhyāna  
of bhakti  the glory  there  made  explanation

(74) Then He went with His companions and bathed in the Ganges in His clothes, and there He explained the glory of devotion.

জ্ঞান-কর্ম্ম-যোগ-ধর্ম্মে নহে কৃষ্ণ বশ ৷  
কৃষ্ণবশ-হেতু এক — কৃষ্ণপ্রেমরস ॥ ৭৫ ॥

jñāna-karma-yoga-dharme nahe kṛṣṇa vaśa  
by knowledge, karma, yoga, dharma  not  Kṛṣṇa  controlled  
kṛṣṇa-vaśa-hetu eka — kṛṣṇa-prema-rasa  
of Kṛṣṇa’s control  the cause  one  the rasa of love for Kṛṣṇa

(75) Kṛṣṇa is not brought under control by knowledge, karma, yoga or dharma; the one thing that holds Him is the rasa of love for Him.

ন সাধয়তি মাং যোগো ন সাংখ্যং ধর্ম্ম উদ্ধব ৷  
ন স্বাধ্যায়স্তপস্ত্যাগো যথা ভক্তির্মমোর্জ্জিতা ॥ ৭৬ ॥

na sādhayati māṁ yogo  
not  wins  Me  yoga  
na sāṁkhyaṁ dharma uddhava  
not  sāṅkhya  dharma  O Uddhava  
na svādhyāyas tapas tyāgo  
not  Vedic study  austerity  renunciation  
yathā bhaktir mamorjitā  
as  devotion  to Me  intense

(76) (Śrī Kṛṣṇa said:) “O Uddhava, neither yoga nor sāṅkhya, neither dharma nor study of the Vedas, neither austerity nor renunciation hold sway over Me as intense devotion to Me does.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.20)[^27]

মুরারিকে কহে প্রভু কৃষ্ণবশ কৈলা ৷  
শুনিয়া মুরারি শ্লোক কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৭৭ ॥

murārike kahe prabhu kṛṣṇa-vaśa kailā  
to Murāri  says  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa  under control  you have made  
śuniyā murāri śloka kahite lāgilā  
hearing  Murāri  a verse  to recite  began

(77) The Lord said to Murāri, “You have brought Kṛṣṇa under your control,” and hearing this, Murāri began to recite a verse:[^28]

ক্বাহং দরিদ্রঃ পাপীয়ান্ ক্ব কৃষ্ণঃ শ্রীনিকেতনঃ ৷  
ব্রহ্মবন্ধুরিতিস্মাহং বাহুভ্যাং পরিরম্ভিতঃ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

kvāhaṁ daridraḥ pāpīyān  
where  I  poor  most sinful  
kva kṛṣṇaḥ śrī-niketanaḥ  
where  Kṛṣṇa  the abode of Śrī  
brahma-bandhur iti smāhaṁ  
a brāhmaṇa by birth only  thus  indeed  I  
bāhubhyāṁ parirambhitaḥ  
with both arms  was embraced

(78) “Who am I, a poor and sinful man, and who is Kṛṣṇa, the abode of the goddess of fortune? Yet because I was born a brāhmaṇa, He embraced me in His arms!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.81.16)[^29]

---

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *yavanas* are the mlecchas, those outside Vedic society; devoted to chanting the name, they behave like the good (*sumanasaḥ*). The Lord’s deeds are free (*svaira*) and wondrous — not of this world, and beyond the rules of the smārtas.

[^2]: *Anubhāṣya*: young manhood — once the fifteenth year was past; learning — knowledge of the supreme truth; enjoyment — of garments, garlands, sandal paste and the like; the Lord gave the name of Kṛṣṇa together with love (prema).

[^3]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: having finished His studies and His teaching, Gauracandra, in order to preach pure devotion, for a time feigned this illness; everywhere He explained the name of Kṛṣṇa to His pupils, showed Kṛṣṇa in every rule of grammar, and tried to turn them away from study. *Anubhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 12.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu went to Gayā with His pupils to offer śrāddha for His father. On the way He fell ill with fever and was cured by drinking the water that had washed the feet of brāhmaṇas, showing householders that brāhmaṇas must be honoured. In Gayā He received initiation into the Kṛṣṇa mantra from Śrī Īśvara Purī, and from then on His prema began to show itself; returning to Navadvīpa, He began to preach it.

[^5]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mother Śacī had offended Śrī Advaita Ācārya, and Mahāprabhu said that until Advaita forgave her she would not receive prema. When Advaita came, Śacī took the dust of his feet and was forgiven. At that time Advaita asked to see the universal form once shown to Arjuna, and the Lord granted his prayer.

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the devotees bathed the Lord in Śrīvāsa’s house; Mahāprabhu sat on the couch of Viṣṇu (*viṣṇu-khaṭṭā*) and revealed the majesty of the King of kings; Advaita and others worshipped Him, and He granted each the boon he desired.

[^7]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu was born in the village of Ekacakrā in Bīrbhūm, the son of Hāḍāi Paṇḍita and Padmāvatī. While still a boy He left with a sannyāsī, travelled through many lands and lived long in Mathurā; drawn by Mahāprabhu, He came to Navadvīpa and stayed in the house of Nandana Ācārya, from where the Lord and His devotees brought Him to their own place.

[^8]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the night before the Vyāsa-pūjā, during kīrtana in Śrīvāsa’s house, Mahāprabhu, in the mood of Balarāma, sat on the couch of Viṣṇu and asked Nityānanda for the plough and the club, and the devotees saw them in His hands.

[^9]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śacī saw this in a dream: Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma, the two Deities of her house, were snatching the offerings with Gaurāṅga and Nityānanda. The next day, as Viśvambhara and Nityānanda were eating in her house, she saw before her eyes that they were Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma.

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on that day Mahāprabhu sat on the couch of Viṣṇu in Śrīvāsa’s house; the devotees bathed Him with Ganges water, reciting the *Puruṣa-sūkta*, and offered Him many foods. For seven praharas He showed the moods of all the avatāras, revealed to the devotees the secrets of their past, removed their doubts and granted boons. This is called the *sāta-prahariyā bhāva*, “the mood of seven praharas”, or the *mahā-prakāśa*, “the great revelation”.

[^11]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: crying “Boar! Boar!”, the Lord entered Murāri Gupta’s house in the form of Varāha, lifted a water pot with His teeth, as Varāha lifted the earth, and drank. On another day He climbed onto Murāri’s shoulders and danced for a long time.

[^12]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śuklāmbara Brahmacārī lived in Navadvīpa on the bank of the Ganges. Once, while the Lord was dancing, he came with his bag of begged rice, and Mahāprabhu, out of love for His devotee, took the rice from his bag and ate it with great love.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 7.76.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: lower than grass — lower in every way, free of worldly pride; more tolerant even than a tree; seeking no honour — giving up worldly honour even when one deserves it; offering honour — to everyone, even the unworthy. With these qualities one should always, at every time, sing the name of Hari with lips and tongue.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: the thread is the holy name, and what is strung on it are the four qualities of this verse: humility, tolerance, freedom from pride and honouring others. With worldly pride one cannot chant constantly; free of these four kinds of pride, the pure soul can always chant, and by obeying this command one surely attains the feet of Kṛṣṇa. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: one who chants without this qualification gets only a semblance of the name (*nāmābhāsa*) or commits an offence against it (*nāmāparādha*), and does not attain its fruit, love for Kṛṣṇa.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: the story of Gopāla Cāpāla is not found in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: the worship of Bhavānī is not the act of a Vaiṣṇava. By condemning it the Lord taught that a worldly, mixed (*viddha*) Vaiṣṇava, who at heart holds to many lords and favours the worship of many gods, is bad company.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: Kuliyā is the present town of Navadvīpa on the western bank of the Ganges; in Mahāprabhu’s time Navadvīpa (Antardvīpa, today’s Śrī Māyāpura) lay on the eastern bank. The Kuliyā near Kāñcaḍāpāḍā is not the place where offences were forgiven.

[^19]: In the edition this verse is misnumbered “62”.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: whoever thinks the Lord, the master of māyā, subject to curses, to Yama’s punishment and to the fruits of karma, as a jīva is, invites unbelief; but whoever knows Him as the Supreme Lord, eternally to be served, is freed from the jīva’s beginningless aversion to Kṛṣṇa.

[^21]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the day of the *mahā-prakāśa* Mukunda Datta was left outside the door. The Lord said He would not soon be pleased with him, for among devotees Mukunda spoke of pure devotion, while among Māyāvādīs he accepted the Māyāvāda of the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha*. Hearing this, Mukunda said, “I am fortunate, for some day He will surely show me mercy!” Seeing how firmly Mukunda had given up the company of Māyāvādīs, the Lord at once called him in, punished him for that association, and gave him His grace — the fruit of the company of pure devotees.

[^22]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Advaita Ācārya was a godbrother of Īśvara Purī, Mahāprabhu’s guru, so the Lord honoured Him as a guru, though Advaita was His servant. Grieved by this, Advaita went to Śāntipura and began to explain the path of knowledge to some unfortunate people, in order to be punished. Hearing of it, the Lord came to Śāntipura in anger and gave Him a sound beating. Advaita began to dance: “Today my wish is fulfilled! Mahāprabhu grudgingly honoured me as His guru; today He has treated me as His servant and disciple and saved me from the evil of Māyāvāda.” Seeing this, the Lord was abashed and was pleased with Him. *Anubhāṣya*: this event is not in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*.

[^23]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord asked Murāri Gupta, a worshipper of Rāma, to recite a hymn to Śrī Rāma, and Murāri recited the *Rāmāṣṭaka* with great love. Then the Lord wrote on his forehead: “By My grace, be Rāmadāsa.” *Anubhāṣya*: according to the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, the Lord placed His foot on Murāri’s head and showed him His form as Rāma.

[^24]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the night of the first city kīrtana, after the Kazi had been delivered, Chand Kazi came with the kīrtana as far as Śrīdhara’s courtyard. There, when the kīrtana stopped to rest, Mahāprabhu mercifully drank the water from Śrīdhara’s cracked iron pot, calling it “water offered by a devotee”. The place, to the north-east of Māyāpura, is still called the resting place of the kīrtana.

[^25]: The edition’s heading calls this “the enactment (*abhinaya*) of freeing Śacī from offence”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the day of the *mahā-prakāśa* Mahāprabhu embraced Haridāsa, declared him an incarnation of Prahlāda and granted him a boon. Mother Śacī had blamed Advaita Ācārya for Viśvarūpa’s sannyāsa; Mahāprabhu freed her from this offence against a Vaiṣṇava by having her take the dust of the Ācārya’s feet.

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: to think the glory of the holy name, which is not different from Kṛṣṇa, an exaggeration (*arthavāda*) — something unreal — is sheer unbelief, opposition to the Lord. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this is an offence against the name, and no offence is more terrible; on seeing the face of one who offends the name, one should bathe in one’s clothes.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: yoga — restraint, observances, posture, breath control and the rest; sāṅkhya — the enumeration of the elements of existence taught by Kapila (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: knowledge that identifies the self with Brahman); dharma — varṇāśrama-dharma; renunciation — sannyāsa.

[^28]: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu told Murāri, “By your loving devotion you have brought Kṛṣṇa under your control,” and Murāri replied with the verse of the brāhmaṇa Sudāmā. His humble meaning was: “I have no power to control Kṛṣṇa; it is Kṛṣṇa who, out of love for His devotees, makes an unworthy servant fortunate.”

[^29]: The edition’s heading gives *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 10.81.14 (a different numbering of the chapter’s verses). *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī*): Sudāmā marvels that Kṛṣṇa embraced him only because he was born a brāhmaṇa, not out of friendship, of which he thought himself unworthy; thus the Lord’s reverence for brāhmaṇas is glorified.
