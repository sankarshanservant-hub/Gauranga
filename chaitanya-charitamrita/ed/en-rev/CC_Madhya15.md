“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 15  
**The Feast at Sārvabhauma’s House**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the Anubhāṣya, 2026)

সার্ব্বভৌমগৃহে ভুঞ্জন্ স্বনিন্দকমমোঘকম্ ৷  
অঙ্গীকুর্ব্বন্ স্ফুটাং চক্রে গৌরঃ স্বাং ভক্তবশ্যতাম্ ॥ ১ ॥

sārvabhauma-gṛhe bhuñjan  
in the house of Sārvabhauma  eating  
sva-nindakam amoghakam  
His own reviler  Amogha  
aṅgī-kurvan sphuṭāṁ cakre  
accepting  manifest  made  
gauraḥ svāṁ bhakta-vaśyatām  
Gaura  His own  subjection to His devotees

(1) While taking His meal at Sārvabhauma’s house, Gaura accepted Amogha, who had reviled Him, and so made plain that He is subject to His devotees.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌর ভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

জয় শ্রীচৈতন্যচরিতামৃত-শ্রোতাগণ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত — যাঁর প্রাণধন ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya śrī-caitanya-caritāmṛta-śrotāgaṇa  
glory  to the listeners of the Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta  
caitanya-caritāmṛta — yām̐ra prāṇa-dhana  
the Caitanya-caritāmṛta  —  whose  treasure of life

(3) Glory to the listeners of the *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, for whom it is the treasure of life!

এইমত মহাপ্রভু ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে ৷  
নীলাচলে রহি’ করে নৃত্যগীত রঙ্গে ॥ ৪ ॥

ei mata mahāprabhu bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge  
thus  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees  
nīlācale rahi’ kare nṛtya-gīta raṅge  
in Nīlācala  staying  does  dancing and singing  joyfully

(4) In this way Mahāprabhu stayed in Nīlācala with His devotees, joyfully dancing and singing.

প্রথম-বৎসরে জগন্নাথ দরশন ৷  
নৃত্যগীত করে দণ্ড, পরণাম, স্তবন ॥ ৫ ॥

prathama-vatsare jagannātha daraśana  
in the first year  Jagannātha  seeing  
nṛtya-gīta kare daṇḍa, paraṇāma, stavana  
dancing and singing  does  prostrations  obeisances  prayers

(5) In that first year He went to see Jagannātha; He danced and sang, fell prostrate, bowed down and offered prayers.

‘উপলভোগ’ লাগিলে করে বাহিরে বিজয় ৷  
হরিদাস মিলি’ আইসে আপন-নিলয় ॥ ৬ ॥

‘upala-bhoga’ lāgile kare bāhire vijaya  
the upala-bhoga  when it begins  makes  outside  going forth  
haridāsa mili’ āise āpana-nilaya  
Haridāsa  meeting  comes  to His own dwelling

(6) When the *upala-bhoga* began, He would come out of the temple, meet Haridāsa, and return to His own dwelling.[^3]

ঘরে বসি’ করে প্রভু নাম-সংকীর্ত্তন ৷  
অদ্বৈত আসিয়া করে প্রভুর পূজন ॥ ৭ ॥

ghare vasi’ kare prabhu nāma-saṁkīrtana  
at home  sitting  does  the Lord  the saṅkīrtana of the name  
advaita āsiyā kare prabhura pūjana  
Advaita  coming  does  the Lord’s  worship

(7) Sitting at home, the Lord would chant the holy name, and Advaita would come and worship Him.

সুগন্ধি-সলিলে দেন পাদ্য, আচমন ৷  
সর্ব্বাঙ্গে লেপয়ে প্রভুর সুগন্ধি চন্দন ॥ ৮ ॥

sugandhi-salile dena pādya, ācamana  
with fragrant water  offers  water for the feet  water for sipping  
sarvāṅge lepaye prabhura sugandhi candana  
over all the limbs  smears  of the Lord  fragrant  sandalwood

(8) Advaita offered Him fragrant water to wash His feet and to sip, and smeared fragrant sandalwood paste over all His limbs.

গলে মালা দেন, মাথায় দিল তুলসী-মঞ্জরী ৷  
যোড় হাতে স্তুতি করে পদে নমস্করি’ ॥ ৯ ॥

gale mālā dena, māthāya dila tulasī-mañjarī  
on the neck  a garland  puts  on the head  placed  tulasī buds  
yoḍa hāte stuti kare pade namaskari’  
joined  with hands  prayers  offers  at the feet  bowing

(9) He put a garland around the Lord’s neck and placed tulasī buds on His head; then He bowed at His feet and offered prayers with joined palms.

পূজা-পাত্রে পুষ্প-তুলসী শেষ যে আছিল ৷  
সেই সব লঞা প্রভু আচার্য্যে পূজিল ॥ ১০ ॥

pūjā-pātre puṣpa-tulasī śeṣa ye āchila  
on the worship plate  of flowers and tulasī  remnant  that  was  
sei saba lañā prabhu ācārye pūjila  
that  all  taking  the Lord  the Ācārya  worshipped

(10) Taking whatever flowers and tulasī were left on the worship plate, the Lord worshipped the Ācārya with them.

‘যোঽসি সোঽসি নমোঽস্তু তে’ এই মন্ত্র পড়ে ৷  
মুখবাদ্য করি’ প্রভু হাসায় আচার্য্যেরে ॥ ১১ ॥

‘yo ’si so ’si namo ’stu te’ ei mantra paḍe  
who  You are  that  You are  obeisance  be  to You  this  mantra  recites  
mukha-vādya kari’ prabhu hāsāya ācāryere  
mouth-music  making  the Lord  makes laugh  the Ācārya

(11) He recited the mantra “Whoever You are, that You are — obeisance to You,” and, making funny sounds with His mouth, set the Ācārya laughing.[^4]

এইমত অন্যোন্যে করেন নমস্কার ৷  
প্রভুরে নিমন্ত্রণ করে আচার্য্য বার বার ॥ ১২ ॥

ei mata anyonye karena namaskāra  
thus  to one another  make  obeisance  
prabhure nimantraṇa kare ācārya bāra bāra  
the Lord  invitation  makes  the Ācārya  time  after time

(12) So They paid obeisance to one another, and again and again the Ācārya invited the Lord to a meal.

আচার্য্যের নিমন্ত্রণ — আশ্চর্য্য-কথন ৷  
বিস্তারি’ বর্ণিয়াছেন দাস-বৃন্দাবন ॥ ১৩ ॥

ācāryera nimantraṇa — āścarya-kathana  
the Ācārya’s  invitation  —  a wonderful story  
vistāri’ varṇiyāchena dāsa-vṛndāvana  
in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa

(13) The story of the Ācārya’s invitations is wonderful, and Vṛndāvana dāsa has described it in detail.[^5]

পুনরুক্তি হয় তাহা, না কৈলুঁ বর্ণন ৷  
আর ভক্তগণ করে প্রভুরে নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১৪ ॥

punarukti haya tāhā, nā kailum̐ varṇana  
repetition  would be  it  not  I made  description  
āra bhakta-gaṇa kare prabhure nimantraṇa  
other  devotees  make  to the Lord  invitation

(14) To tell it again would be repetition, so I have not described it. The other devotees also invited the Lord.

এক এক দিন এক এক ভক্তগৃহে মহোৎসব ৷  
প্রভু-সঙ্গে তাঁহা ভোজন করে ভক্ত সব ॥ ১৫ ॥

eka eka dina eka eka bhakta-gṛhe mahotsava  
one  by one  day  in one  after another  devotee’s home  a festival  
prabhu-saṅge tām̐hā bhojana kare bhakta saba  
with the Lord  there  eating  do  devotees  all

(15) Day after day there was a festival in one devotee’s home after another, and all the devotees ate there with the Lord.

চারিমাস রহিলা সবে মহাপ্রভু-সঙ্গে ৷  
জগন্নাথের নানা যাত্রা দেখে মহারঙ্গে ॥ ১৬ ॥

cāri-māsa rahilā sabe mahāprabhu-saṅge  
four months  stayed  all  with Mahāprabhu  
jagannāthera nānā yātrā dekhe mahā-raṅge  
of Jagannātha  various  festivals  see  with great joy

(16) For four months all the devotees stayed with Mahāprabhu and watched Jagannātha’s various festivals with great joy.

কৃষ্ণজন্মযাত্রা-দিনে নন্দ-মহোৎসব ৷  
গোপবেশ হৈলা প্রভু লঞা ভক্তসব ॥ ১৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa-janma-yātrā-dine nanda-mahotsava  
on the day of the festival of Kṛṣṇa’s birth  Nanda’s great festival  
gopa-veśa hailā prabhu lañā bhaktasaba  
in cowherd dress  became  the Lord  taking  all the devotees

(17) On the day of Kṛṣṇa’s birth festival, at Nanda’s great celebration, the Lord and all His devotees dressed as cowherds.[^6]

দধিদুগ্ধ-ভার প্রভু নিজ-স্কন্ধে করি’ ৷  
মহোৎসব-স্থানে আইলা বলি ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ১৮ ॥

dadhi-dugdha-bhāra prabhu nija-skandhe kari’  
a yoke of yogurt and milk  the Lord  on His own shoulder  placing  
mahotsava-sthāne āilā bali ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
to the festival place  came  saying  “Hari”  “Hari”

(18) Carrying a yoke of yogurt and milk on His shoulder, the Lord came to the festival grounds calling out, “Hari! Hari!”

কানাঞি-খুটিয়া আছেন ‘নন্দ’-বেশ ধরি’ ৷  
জগন্নাথ-মাহাতি হঞাছেন ‘ব্রজেশ্বরী’ ॥ ১৯ ॥

kānāñi-khuṭiyā āchena ‘nanda’-veśa dhari’  
Kānāi Khuṭiyā  was  Nanda’s dress  wearing  
jagannātha-māhāti hañāchena ‘vrajeśvarī’  
Jagannātha Māhāti  had become  the Queen of Vraja

(19) Kānāi Khuṭiyā was dressed as Nanda, and Jagannātha Māhāti had become Yaśodā, the Queen of Vraja.

আপনে প্রতাপরুদ্র, আর মিশ্র-কাশী ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম, আর পড়িছা-পাত্র তুলসী ॥ ২০ ॥

āpane pratāparudra, āra miśra-kāśī  
himself  Pratāparudra  and  Kāśī Miśra  
sārvabhauma, āra paḍichā-pātra tulasī  
Sārvabhauma  and  the paḍichā-pātra  Tulasī

(20) King Pratāparudra himself was there, with Kāśī Miśra, Sārvabhauma and Tulasī, the temple *paḍichā*.

ইঁহা-সবা লঞা প্রভু করে নৃত্য-রঙ্গ ৷  
দধি-দুগ্ধ হরিদ্রা-জলে ভরে সবার অঙ্গ ॥ ২১ ॥

im̐hā-sabā lañā prabhu kare nṛtya-raṅga  
them all  taking  the Lord  does  joyful dancing  
dadhi-dugdha haridrā-jale bhare sabāra aṅga  
yogurt and milk  with turmeric water  fills  everyone’s  body

(21) With them all the Lord danced joyfully, and everyone was drenched in yogurt, milk and turmeric water.

অদ্বৈত কহে, — সত্য কহি, না করিহ কোপ ৷  
লগুড় ফিরাইতে পার, তবে জানি গোপ ॥ ২২ ॥

advaita kahe, — satya kahi, nā kariha kopa  
Advaita  says  —  truth  I speak  not  do  anger  
laguḍa phirāite pāra, tabe jāni gopa  
the staff  to twirl  You can  then  I know  a cowherd

(22) Advaita said, “I am telling You the truth — don’t be angry. I will know You are a cowherd only if You can twirl a staff.”

তবে লগুড় লঞা প্রভু ফিরাইতে লাগিলা ৷  
বার বার আকাশে ফেলি’ লুফিয়া ধরিলা ॥ ২৩ ॥

tabe laguḍa lañā prabhu phirāite lāgilā  
then  the staff  taking  the Lord  to twirl  began  
bāra bāra ākāśe pheli’ luphiyā dharilā  
time  after time  into the sky  throwing  snatching  caught

(23) Then the Lord took a staff and began to twirl it, tossing it into the air again and again and catching it.

শিরের উপরে, সম্মুখে, পৃষ্ঠে, দুই-পাশে ৷  
পাদসন্ধ্যে ফিরায় লগুড়, — দেখি’ লোক হাসে ॥ ২৪ ॥

śirera upare, sammukhe, pṛṣṭhe, dui-pāśe  
of the head  above  in front  behind  on both sides  
pāda-sandhye phirāya laguḍa, — dekhi’ loka hāse  
between the feet  twirls  the staff  —  seeing  the people  laugh

(24) He twirled the staff over His head, in front, behind, on both sides and even between His feet, and the people laughed to see it.[^7]

অলাত-চক্রের প্রায় লগুড় ফিরায় ৷  
দেখি’ সর্ব্বলোক-চিত্তে চমৎকার পায় ॥ ২৫ ॥

alāta-cakrera prāya laguḍa phirāya  
a firebrand’s circle  like  the staff  twirls  
dekhi’ sarva-loka-citte camatkāra pāya  
seeing  in the hearts of all people  wonder  arises

(25) He whirled the staff like a circle of fire traced by a firebrand, and all who saw it were struck with wonder.[^8]

এইমত নিত্যানন্দ ফিরায় লগুড় ৷  
কে বুঝিবে তাঁহা, দুঁহার গোপভাব গূঢ় ॥ ২৬ ॥

ei mata nityānanda phirāya laguḍa  
in the same way  Nityānanda  twirls  a staff  
ke bujhibe tām̐hā, dum̐hāra gopa-bhāva gūḍha  
who  will understand  that  of the two  the cowherd mood  hidden

(26) Nityānanda twirled a staff in the same way. Who can understand the hidden cowherd mood of these two?[^9]

প্রতাপরুদ্রের আজ্ঞায় পড়িছা-তুলসী ৷  
জগন্নাথ-প্রসাদ-বস্ত্র এক লঞা আসি’ ॥ ২৭ ॥

pratāparudrera ājñāya paḍichā-tulasī  
by Pratāparudra’s  order  the paḍichā Tulasī  
jagannātha-prasāda-vastra eka lañā āsi’  
a prasāda cloth of Jagannātha  one  taking  coming

(27) At Pratāparudra’s order, Tulasī the *paḍichā* came, bringing a cloth that had been offered to Jagannātha.

বহুমূল্য বস্ত্র প্রভু মস্তকে বান্ধিল ৷  
আচার্য্যাদি প্রভুর গণেরে পরাইল ॥ ২৮ ॥

bahu-mūlya vastra prabhu mastake bāndhila  
costly  cloth  the Lord  on the head  tied  
ācāryādi prabhura gaṇere parāila  
the Ācārya and others  the Lord’s  people  clothed

(28) The Lord tied the costly cloth around His head, and He had the Ācārya and His other companions wear it as well.

কানাঞি-খুটিয়া, জগন্নাথ, — দুইজন ৷  
আবেশে বিলাইল, ঘরে ছিল যত ধন ॥ ২৯ ॥

kānāñi-khuṭiyā, jagannātha, — dui-jana  
Kānāi Khuṭiyā  Jagannātha  —  the two  
āveśe vilāila, ghare chila yata dhana  
in ecstasy  gave away  at home  was  whatever  wealth

(29) Kānāi Khuṭiyā and Jagannātha, in their ecstasy, gave away all the wealth they had at home.

দেখি’ মহাপ্রভু বড় সন্তোষ পাইলা ৷  
মাতাপিতা-জ্ঞানে দুঁহে নমস্কার কৈলা ॥ ৩০ ॥

dekhi’ mahāprabhu baḍa santoṣa pāilā  
seeing  Mahāprabhu  great  satisfaction  felt  
mātā-pitā-jñāne dum̐he namaskāra kailā  
as mother and father  to both  obeisance  made

(30) Seeing this, Mahāprabhu was very pleased and bowed down to the two of them as to His mother and father.

পরম-আবেশে প্রভু আইলা নিজ-ঘর ৷  
এইমত লীলা করে গৌরাঙ্গসুন্দর ॥ ৩১ ॥

parama-āveśe prabhu āilā nija-ghara  
in great ecstasy  the Lord  came  to His own home  
ei mata līlā kare gaurāṅga-sundara  
thus  pastimes  performs  Gaurāṅga-sundara

(31) In deep ecstasy the Lord returned home. Such were the pastimes of Gaurāṅgasundara.

বিজয়া-দশমী — লঙ্কা-বিজয়ের দিনে ৷  
বানর-সৈন্য কৈলা প্রভু লঞা ভক্তগণে ॥ ৩২ ॥

vijayā-daśamī — laṅkā-vijayera dine  
Vijayā-daśamī  —  of the conquest of Laṅkā  on the day  
vānara-sainya kailā prabhu lañā bhakta-gaṇe  
a monkey army  made  the Lord  taking  the devotees

(32) On Vijayā-daśamī, the day of the conquest of Laṅkā, the Lord made the devotees into an army of monkeys.[^10]

হনুমান্-আবেশে প্রভু বৃক্ষশাখা লঞা ৷  
লঙ্কা-গড়ে চড়ি’ ফেলে লঙ্কা ভাঙ্গিয়া ॥ ৩৩ ॥

hanumān-āveśe prabhu vṛkṣa-śākhā lañā  
in the mood of Hanumān  the Lord  a tree branch  taking  
laṅkā-gaḍe caḍi’ phele laṅkā bhāṅgiyā  
on the fort of Laṅkā  climbing  throws down  Laṅkā  breaking

(33) Absorbed in the mood of Hanumān, the Lord took a tree branch, climbed the ramparts of Laṅkā and tore Laṅkā down.

‘কাঁহারে রাব্ণা’ প্রভু কহে ক্রোধাবেশে ৷  
‘জগন্মাতা হরে পাপী, মারিমু সবংশে’ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

‘kām̐hāre rāvṇā’ prabhu kahe krodhāveśe  
where is  the wretch Rāvaṇa  the Lord  says  in a fit of anger  
‘jagan-mātā hare pāpī, mārimu savaṁśe’  
the Mother of the world  stole  the sinner  I will kill  with all his race

(34) In a fit of anger the Lord cried, “Where is that wretch Rāvaṇa? The sinner has carried off the Mother of the world! I will kill him and all his kin!”[^11]

গোসাঞির আবেশ দেখি’ লোকে চমৎকার ৷  
সর্ব্বলোক ‘জয়’ ‘জয়’ করে বার বার ॥ ৩৫ ॥

gosāñira āveśa dekhi’ loke camatkāra  
of the Lord  the ecstasy  seeing  people  amazed  
sarva-loka ‘jaya’ ‘jaya’ kare bāra bāra  
all people  “Jaya”  “Jaya”  make  time  after time

(35) Seeing the Lord’s ecstasy, the people were amazed, and all of them shouted “Jaya! Jaya!” again and again.

এইমত রাসযাত্রা, আর দীপাবলী ৷  
উত্থান-দ্বাদশীযাত্রা দেখিলা সকলি ॥ ৩৬ ॥

ei mata rāsa-yātrā, āra dīpāvalī  
in this way  the Rāsa-yātrā  and  Dīpāvalī  
utthāna-dvādaśī yātrā dekhilā sakali  
the Utthāna-dvādaśī  festival  saw  all

(36) In the same way they all saw the Rāsa-yātrā, Dīpāvalī and the festival of Utthāna-dvādaśī.

একদিন মহাপ্রভু নিত্যানন্দে লঞা ৷  
দুই ভাই যুক্তি কৈল নিভৃতে বসিয়া ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ekadina mahāprabhu nityānande lañā  
one day  Mahāprabhu  Nityānanda  taking  
dui bhāi yukti kaila nibhṛte vasiyā  
the two  brothers  counsel  held  in seclusion  sitting

(37) One day Mahāprabhu took Nityānanda aside, and the two brothers sat together in seclusion and took counsel.

কিবা যুক্তি কৈল দুঁহে, কেহ নাহি জানে ৷  
ফলে অনুমান পাছে কৈল ভক্তগণে ॥ ৩৮ ॥

kibā yukti kaila dum̐he, keha nāhi jāne  
what  counsel  held  the two  anyone  not  knows  
phale anumāna pāche kaila bhakta-gaṇe  
from the result  a guess  afterwards  made  the devotees

(38) What the two decided no one knew; only later could the devotees guess it from what followed.

তবে মহাপ্রভু সব ভক্তে বোলাইল ৷  
গৌড়দেশে যাহ’ সবে বিদায় করিল ॥ ৩৯ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu saba bhakte bolāila  
then  Mahāprabhu  all  devotees  called  
gauḍa-deśe yāha’ sabe vidāya karila  
to Gauḍa  go  all  farewell  made

(39) Then Mahāprabhu called all the devotees and bade them farewell, telling them to go back to Gauḍa.

সবারে কহিল, — প্রতিবৎসর আসিয়া ৷  
গুণ্ডিচা দেখিয়া যাবে আমারে মিলিয়া ॥ ৪০ ॥

sabāre kahila, — prativatsara āsiyā  
to all  said  —  every year  coming  
guṇḍicā dekhiyā yābe āmāre miliyā  
Guṇḍicā  seeing  you will go  Me  meeting

(40) He said to them all, “Come every year: see the Guṇḍicā festival, meet with Me, and then go home.”[^12]

আচার্য্যেরে আজ্ঞা দিল করিয়া সম্মান ৷  
‘আ-চণ্ডাল আদি কৃষ্ণভক্তি দিও দান’ ॥ ৪১ ॥

ācāryere ājñā dila kariyā sammāna  
to the Ācārya  order  gave  showing  respect  
‘ā-caṇḍāla ādi kṛṣṇa-bhakti dio dāna’  
down to the caṇḍālas  and others  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  give  as a gift

(41) With respect He gave this order to the Ācārya: “Give devotion to Kṛṣṇa to everyone, down to the *caṇḍālas*.”

নিত্যানন্দে আজ্ঞা দিল, — ‘যাহ গৌড়দেশে ৷  
অনর্গল প্রেমভক্তি করিহ প্রকাশে ॥ ৪২ ॥

nityānande ājñā dila, — ‘yāha gauḍa-deśe  
to Nityānanda  order  gave  —  go  to Gauḍa  
anargala prema-bhakti kariha prakāśe  
unrestrained  loving devotion  do  manifest

(42) To Nityānanda He gave this order: “Go to Gauḍa and reveal loving devotion freely, without restraint.[^13]

রামদাস, গদাধর আদি কত জনে ৷  
তোমার সহায় লাগি’ দিলু তোমার সনে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

rāma-dāsa, gadādhara ādi kata jane  
Rāmadāsa  Gadādhara  and others  some  persons  
tomāra sahāya lāgi’ dilu tomāra sane  
Your  help  for  I have given  You  with

(43) I am sending Rāmadāsa, Gadādhara and a few others along to help You.[^14]

মধ্যে মধ্যে আমি তোমার নিকটে যাইব ৷  
অলক্ষিতে রহি’ তোমার নৃত্য দেখিব’ ॥ ৪৪ ॥

madhye madhye āmi tomāra nikaṭe yāiba  
from time  to time  I  You  near  will go  
alakṣite rahi’ tomāra nṛtya dekhiba’  
unseen  remaining  Your  dancing  I will see

(44) From time to time I will come to You, and, staying unseen, I will watch You dance.”[^15]

শ্রীবাস-পণ্ডিতে প্রভু করি’ আলিঙ্গন ৷  
কণ্ঠে ধরি’ কহে তাঁরে মধুর বচন ॥ ৪৫ ॥

śrīvāsa-paṇḍite prabhu kari’ āliṅgana  
Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita  the Lord  making  embrace  
kaṇṭhe dhari’ kahe tām̐re madhura vacana  
by the neck  holding  says  to him  sweet  words

(45) The Lord embraced Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and, with His arm around his neck, spoke sweet words to him:

তোমার ঘরে কীর্ত্তনে আমি নিত্য নাচিব ৷  
তুমি দেখা পাবে, আর কেহ না দেখিব ॥ ৪৬ ॥

tomāra ghare kīrtane āmi nitya nāciba  
in your  house  in the kīrtana  I  always  will dance  
tumi dekhā pābe, āra keha nā dekhiba  
you  sight  will get  other  anyone  not  will see

(46) “Every day I will dance in the kīrtana at your house. You will see it, but no one else will.

এই বস্ত্র মাতাকে দিহ’, এই সব প্রসাদ ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ করি’ আমার ক্ষমাইহ অপরাধ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ei vastra mātāke diha’, ei saba prasāda  
this  cloth  to Mother  give  this  all  prasāda  
daṇḍavat kari’ āmāra kṣamāiha aparādha  
prostration  making  My  have forgiven  offence

(47) Give this cloth and all this *prasāda* to My mother. Fall down before her and beg her to forgive My offence.

তাঁর সেবা ছাড়ি’ আমি করিয়াছি সন্ন্যাস ৷  
ধর্ম্ম নহে, করি আমি নিজ-ধর্ম্ম নাশ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

tām̐ra sevā chāḍi’ āmi kariyāchi sannyāsa  
her  service  leaving  I  have taken  sannyāsa  
dharma nahe, kari āmi nija-dharma nāśa  
dharma  it is not  do  I  My own dharma  destruction

(48) I gave up serving her and took sannyāsa. That is not dharma: I am destroying My own dharma.[^16]

তাঁর প্রেম বশ আমি, তাঁর সেবা — ধর্ম্ম ৷  
তাহা ছাড়ি’ করিয়াছি বাতুলের কর্ম্ম ॥ ৪৯ ॥

tām̐ra prema vaśa āmi, tām̐ra sevā — dharma  
her  love  subject to  I am  her  service  —  dharma  
tāhā chāḍi’ kariyāchi vātulera karma  
that  leaving  I have done  of a madman  deed

(49) I am subject to her love, and serving her is My dharma. In giving that up I have acted like a madman.

বাতুল বালকের মাতা নাহি লয় দোষ ৷  
এই জানি’ মাতা মোরে না করয় রোষ ॥ ৫০ ॥

vātula bālakera mātā nāhi laya doṣa  
mad  child’s  mother  not  takes  fault  
ei jāni’ mātā more nā karaya roṣa  
this  knowing  Mother  with Me  not  is  angry

(50) A mother does not hold her mad child at fault. Knowing this, My mother is not angry with Me.

কি কায় সন্ন্যাসে মোর, প্রেম নিজ-ধন ৷  
যে-কালে সন্ন্যাস কৈলুঁ, ছন্ন হৈল মন ॥ ৫১ ॥

ki kāya sannyāse mora, prema nija-dhana  
what  use  in sannyāsa  to Me  love  own wealth  
ye-kāle sannyāsa kailum̐, channa haila mana  
at the time  sannyāsa  I took  deranged  became  mind

(51) What use is *sannyāsa* to Me? Love is My real wealth. When I took sannyāsa, My mind was deranged.

নীলাচলে আছি মুঞি তাঁহার আজ্ঞাতে ৷  
মধ্যে মধ্যে আসিমু তাঁর চরণ দেখিতে ॥ ৫২ ॥

nīlācale āchi muñi tām̐hāra ājñāte  
in Nīlācala  am  I  her  by order  
madhye madhye āsimu tām̐ra caraṇa dekhite  
from time  to time  I will come  her  feet  to see

(52) I stay in Nīlācala at her bidding, and from time to time I will come to see her feet.[^17]

নিত্য যাই’ দেখি মুঞি তাঁহার চরণে ৷  
স্ফূর্ত্তি জ্ঞানে তেঁহো তাহা সত্য নাহি মানে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

nitya yāi’ dekhi muñi tām̐hāra caraṇe  
always  going  see  I  her  feet  
sphūrti jñāne tem̐ho tāhā satya nāhi māne  
a vision  thinking  she  that  true  not  accepts

(53) Every day I go and see her feet, but she takes it for a vision and does not believe it is real.[^18]

একদিন শাল্যন্ন, ব্যঞ্জন পাঁচ-সাত ৷  
শাক, মোচা-ঘণ্ট, ভৃষ্ট পটোল-নিম্বপাত ॥ ৫৪ ॥

লেম্বু-আদাখণ্ড, দধি, দুগ্ধ, খণ্ড-সার ৷  
শালগ্রামে সমর্পিলেন বহু উপহার ॥ ৫৫ ॥

eka-dina śāly-anna, vyañjana pām̐ca-sāta  
one day  śāli rice  vegetable dishes  five or seven  
śāka, mocā-ghaṇṭa, bhṛṣṭa paṭola-nimba-pāta  
greens  banana-flower ghaṇṭa  fried  paṭolas with nīm leaves  
lembu-ādā-khaṇḍa, dadhi, dugdha, khaṇḍa-sāra  
lemon and ginger slices  yogurt  milk  sugar candy  
śālagrāme samarpilena bahu upahāra  
to the śālagrāma  offered  many  gifts

(54–55) One day she offered the *śālagrāma* many gifts: *śāli* rice, five or seven vegetable dishes, greens, *ghaṇṭa* of banana flowers, fried *paṭolas* with *nim* leaves, slices of lemon and ginger, yogurt, milk and sugar candy.

প্রসাদ লঞা কোলে করেন ক্রন্দন ৷  
নিমাইর প্রিয় মোর — এসব ব্যঞ্জন ॥ ৫৬ ॥

prasāda lañā kole karena krandana  
the prasāda  taking  in her lap  does  weeping  
nimāira priya mora — esaba vyañjana  
of Nimāi  favourites  my  —  all these  dishes

(56) She took the *prasāda* on her lap and wept: ‘All these dishes are my Nimāi’s favourites.

নিমাঞি নাহিক এথা, কে করে ভোজন ৷  
মোর ধ্যানে অশ্রুজলে ভরিল নয়ন ॥ ৫৭ ॥

nimāñi nāhika ethā, ke kare bhojana  
Nimāi  not  here  who  does  eating  
mora dhyāne aśru-jale bharila nayana  
on Me  in meditation  with tears  filled  eyes

(57) Nimāi is not here — who will eat them?’ As she thought of Me, her eyes filled with tears.

শীঘ্র যাই’ মুঞি সব করিনু ভক্ষণ ৷  
শূন্যপাত্র দেখি’ অশ্রু করিয়া মার্জ্জন ॥ ৫৮ ॥

śīghra yāi’ muñi saba karinu bhakṣaṇa  
quickly  going  I  all  did  eating  
śūnya-pātra dekhi’ aśru kariyā mārjana  
the empty plate  seeing  tears  making  wiping

(58) I went at once and ate everything. Seeing the plate empty, she wiped away her tears.

‘কে অন্ন-ব্যঞ্জন খাইল, শূন্য কেনে পাত ?  
বালগোপাল কিবা খাইল সব ভাত ? ॥ ৫৯ ॥

‘ke anna-vyañjana khāila, śūnya kene pāta?  
who  rice and vegetables  ate  empty  why  the plate  
bālagopāla kibā khāila saba bhāta?  
Bāla-gopāla  perhaps  ate  all  the rice

(59) ‘Who has eaten the rice and vegetables? Why is the plate empty? Could Bāla-gopāla have eaten all the rice?

কিবা মোর কথায় মনে ভ্রম হঞা গেল !  
কিবা কোন জন্তু আসি’ সকল খাইল ? ॥ ৬০ ॥

kibā mora kathāya mane bhrama hañā gela!  
or  my  in the matter  in the mind  delusion  happened  
kibā kona jantu āsi’ sakala khāila?  
or  some  animal  coming  everything  ate

(60) Or was my mind deluded about it? Or did some animal come and eat it all?

কিবা আমি অন্নপাত্রে ভ্রমে না বাড়িল !’  
এত চিন্তি’ পাক-পাত্র যাঞা দেখিল ॥ ৬১ ॥

kibā āmi anna-pātre bhrame nā bāḍila !’  
or  I  on the rice plate  by mistake  not  served  
eta cinti’ pāka-pātra yāñā dekhila  
so  thinking  the cooking pots  going  looked at

(61) Or did I forget to serve the food on the plate?’ Thinking this, she went and looked into the cooking pots.

অন্নব্যঞ্জন পূর্ণ দেখি’ সকল ভাজনে ৷  
দেখিয়া সংশয় হৈল কিছু চমৎকার মনে ॥ ৬২ ॥

anna-vyañjana pūrṇa dekhi’ sakala bhājane  
with rice and vegetables  full  seeing  all  the vessels  
dekhiyā saṁśaya haila kichu camatkāra mane  
seeing  doubt  arose  some  wonder  in the mind

(62) She saw that all the pots were still full of rice and vegetables, and at the sight she was filled with doubt and wonder.

ঈশানে বোলাঞা পুনঃ স্থান লেপাইল ৷  
পুনরপি গোপালকে অন্ন সমর্পিল ॥ ৬৩ ॥

īśāne bolāñā punaḥ sthāna lepāila  
Īśāna  calling  again  the place  had cleaned  
punarapi gopālake anna samarpila  
once more  to Gopāla  food  offered

(63) She called Īśāna, had the place cleaned and plastered again, and once more offered food to Gopāla.

এইমত যবে করেন উত্তম রন্ধন ৷  
মোরে খাওয়াইতে করে উৎকণ্ঠায় রোদন ॥ ৬৪ ॥

ei mata yabe karena uttama randhana  
thus  whenever  does  excellent  cooking  
more khāoyāite kare utkaṇṭhāya rodana  
Me  to feed  does  in longing  weeping

(64) Whenever she cooks something excellent like this, she weeps with longing to feed Me.

তাঁর প্রেমে আনি’ আমায় করায় ভোজনে ৷  
অন্তরে সুখ মানে তেঁহো, বাহ্যে নাহি মানে ॥ ৬৫ ॥

tām̐ra preme āni’ āmāya karāya bhojane  
her  by love  bringing  Me  makes  eat  
antare sukha māne tem̐ho, bāhye nāhi māne  
within  happiness  feels  she  outwardly  not  accepts

(65) Her love draws Me there and makes Me eat. Within she feels happy, but outwardly she does not accept it.

এই বিজয়া-দশমীতে হৈল এই রীতি ৷  
তাঁহাকে পুছিয়া তাঁর করাইহ প্রতীতি ॥ ৬৬ ॥

ei vijayā-daśamīte haila ei rīti  
on this  Vijayā-daśamī  happened  this  event  
tām̐hāke puchiyā tām̐ra karāiha pratīti  
her  asking  to her  make  conviction

(66) This happened on this very Vijayā-daśamī. Ask her about it and convince her that it was real.”

এতেক কহিতে প্রভু বিহ্বল হইলা ৷  
লোক বিদায় করিতে প্রভু ধৈর্য্য ধরিলা ॥ ৬৭ ॥

eteka kahite prabhu vihvala hailā  
so much  saying  the Lord  overwhelmed  became  
loka vidāya karite prabhu dhairya dharilā  
the people  farewell  to make  the Lord  composure  held

(67) As He said this, the Lord was overwhelmed; but to send the devotees off He kept His composure.

রাঘব-পণ্ডিতে কহেন বচন সরস ৷  
‘তোমার নিষ্ঠা প্রেমে আমি হই তোমার বশ’ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

rāghava-paṇḍite kahena vacana sarasa  
to Rāghava Paṇḍita  speaks  words  tender  
‘tomāra niṣṭhā preme āmi hai tomāra vaśa’  
your  steadfast  by love  I  am  your  subject

(68) To Rāghava Paṇḍita He spoke tender words: “Your steadfast love has brought Me under your control.

ইঁহার কৃষ্ণসেবার কথা শুন, সর্ব্বজন ৷  
পরম-পবিত্র সেবা অতি সর্ব্বোত্তম ॥ ৬৯ ॥

im̐hāra kṛṣṇa-sevāra kathā śuna, sarva-jana  
his  of service to Kṛṣṇa  the account  hear  all people  
parama-pavitra sevā ati sarvottama  
supremely pure  service  most  excellent

(69) Hear, everyone, how he serves Kṛṣṇa. His service is supremely pure, the very best of all.

আর দ্রব্য রহু, শুন নারিকেলের কথা ৷  
পাঁচ গণ্ডা করি’ নারিকেল বিকায় তথা ॥ ৭০ ॥

āra dravya rahu, śuna nārikelera kathā  
other  things  let be  hear  of the coconuts  the story  
pām̐ca gaṇḍā kari’ nārikela vikāya tathā  
five  gaṇḍās  at  a coconut  sells  there

(70) Leaving aside his other offerings, hear about the coconuts. In those parts a coconut sells for five *gaṇḍās*.[^19]

বাটিতে কত শত বৃক্ষে লক্ষ লক্ষ ফল ৷  
তথাপি শুনেন, যথা মিষ্ট নারিকেল ॥ ৭১ ॥

vāṭite kata śata vṛkṣe lakṣa lakṣa phala  
in his garden  how many  hundreds  on trees  lakhs  upon lakhs  fruits  
tathāpi śunena, yathā miṣṭa nārikela  
still  he inquires  where  sweet  coconuts

(71) In his garden hundreds of trees bear fruit by the hundreds of thousands, yet he still asks where the sweetest coconuts are to be had.

এক এক ফলের মূল্য দিয়া চারি চারি পণ ৷  
দশক্রোশ হৈতে আনায় করিয়া যতন ॥ ৭২ ॥

eka eka phalera mūlya diyā cāri cāri paṇa  
for each  one  fruit’s  price  giving  four  four  paṇas  
daśa-krośa haite ānāya kariyā yatana  
ten krośas  from  has brought  taking  pains

(72) He pays four *paṇas* for every single fruit and takes pains to have them brought from ten *krośas* away.[^20]

প্রতিদিন পাঁচ-সাত ফল, ছোলাঞা ৷  
সুশীতল করিয়া রাখে জলে ডুবাঞা ॥ ৭৩ ॥

pratidina pām̐ca-sāta phala, cholāñā  
every day  five or seven  fruits  husking  
suśītala kariyā rākhe jale ḍubāñā  
very cool  making  keeps  in water  immersing

(73) Every day he has five or seven fruits husked and keeps them immersed in water to make them very cool.

ভোগের সময় পুনঃ ছুলি’ সংস্করি’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণে সমর্পণ করে, মুখ ছিদ্র করি’ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

bhogera samaya punaḥ chuli’ saṁskari’  
of the offering  at the time  again  trimming  preparing  
kṛṣṇe samarpaṇa kare, mukha chidra kari’  
to Kṛṣṇa  offering  makes  the top  a hole  making

(74) At the time of the offering he trims and prepares them again, cuts an opening in the top, and offers them to Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণ সেই নারিকেল-জল পান করি’ ৷  
কভু শূন্য ফল রাখেন, কভু জল ভরি’ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa sei nārikela-jala pāna kari’  
Kṛṣṇa  that  coconut water  drinking  doing  
kabhu śūnya phala rākhena, kabhu jala bhari’  
sometimes  empty  the fruit  leaves  sometimes  with water  full

(75) Kṛṣṇa drinks the coconut water: sometimes He leaves the fruit empty, sometimes still full of water.

জলশূন্য ফল দেখি’ পণ্ডিত — হরষিত ৷  
ফল ভাঙ্গি’ শস্যে করে শতপাত্র পূরিত ॥ ৭৬ ॥

jala-śūnya phala dekhi’ paṇḍita — haraṣita  
without water  the fruit  seeing  the Paṇḍita  —  delighted  
phala bhāṅgi’ śasye kare śata-pātra pūrita  
the fruit  breaking  with the pulp  makes  the śata-pātra  filled

(76) When the Paṇḍita sees a fruit emptied of its water, he is delighted. He breaks it open and fills a plate with the pulp.[^21]

শস্য সমর্পণ করি’ বাহিরে ধেয়ান ৷  
শস্য খাঞা কৃষ্ণ করে শূন্য ভাজন ॥ ৭৭ ॥

śasya samarpaṇa kari’ bāhire dheyāna  
the pulp  offering  making  outside  meditation  
śasya khāñā kṛṣṇa kare śūnya bhājana  
the pulp  eating  Kṛṣṇa  makes  empty  the vessel

(77) He offers the pulp and meditates outside, and Kṛṣṇa eats it and leaves the plate empty.

কভু শস্য খাঞা পুনঃ পাত্র ভরে শাঁসে ৷  
শ্রদ্ধা বাড়ে পণ্ডিতের, প্রেমসিন্ধু ভাসে ॥ ৭৮ ॥

kabhu śasya khāñā punaḥ pātra bhare śām̐se  
sometimes  the pulp  eating  again  the vessel  fills  with pulp  
śraddhā bāḍe paṇḍitera, prema-sindhu bhāse  
faith  grows  the Paṇḍita’s  in the ocean of love  floats

(78) Sometimes, having eaten it, Kṛṣṇa fills the plate with pulp again. Then the Paṇḍita’s faith grows, and he floats in an ocean of love.

এক দিন ফলদশ সংস্কার করিয়া ৷  
ভোগ লাগাইতে সেবক আইল লঞা ॥ ৭৯ ॥

eka dina phala-daśa saṁskāra kariyā  
one  day  ten fruits  preparation  making  
bhoga lāgāite sevaka āila lañā  
the offering  to set  a servant  came  bringing

(79) One day a servant prepared ten coconuts and brought them to be offered.

অবসর নাহি হয়, বিলম্ব হইল ৷  
ফল-পাত্র-হাতে সেবক দ্বারে ত’ রহিল ॥ ৮০ ॥

avasara nāhi haya, vilamba haila  
opportunity  not  is  delay  happened  
phala-pātra-hāte sevaka dvāre ta’ rahila  
with the plate of fruits in hand  the servant  at the door  just  stayed

(80) It was not yet time to offer them; there was a delay, so the servant waited at the door with the plate of fruits in his hand.

দ্বারের উপর-ভিতে তেঁহো হাত দিল ৷  
সেই হাতে ফল ছুঁইল, পণ্ডিত দেখিল ॥ ৮১ ॥

dvārera upara-bhite tem̐ho hāta dila  
of the door  on the upper wall  he  hand  put  
sei hāte phala chum̐ila, paṇḍita dekhila  
that  with hand  the fruits  touched  the Paṇḍita  saw

(81) He touched the wall above the door with his hand and then touched the fruits with the same hand. The Paṇḍita saw it.

পণ্ডিত কহে, — দ্বারে লোক করে গতায়াতে ৷  
তার পদধূলি উড়ি’ লাগে উপর ভিতে ॥ ৮২ ॥

paṇḍita kahe, — dvāre loka kare gatāyāte  
the Paṇḍita  says  —  through the door  people  make  coming and going  
tāra pada-dhūli uḍi’ lāge upara bhite  
their  foot-dust  flying up  sticks  on the upper  wall

(82) The Paṇḍita said, “People are always coming and going through this door. The dust from their feet flies up and settles on the wall above it.

সেই ভিতে হাত দিয়া ফল পরশিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-যোগ্য নহে, ফল অপবিত্র হৈলা ॥ ৮৩ ॥

sei bhite hāta diyā phala paraśilā  
that  on the wall  hand  putting  the fruits  you touched  
kṛṣṇa-yogya nahe, phala apavitra hailā  
for Kṛṣṇa fit  not  the fruits  impure  have become

(83) You put your hand on that wall and then touched the fruits. They are no longer fit for Kṛṣṇa — they have become impure.”[^22]

এত বলি’ ফল ফেলে প্রাচীর লঙ্ঘিয়া ৷  
ঐছে পবিত্র প্রেম-সেবা জগৎ জিনিয়া ॥ ৮৪ ॥

eta bali’ phala phele prācīra laṅghiyā  
so  saying  the fruits  throws  the boundary wall  crossing  
aiche pavitra prema-sevā jagat jiniyā  
such  pure  loving service  the world  conquering

(84) With these words he threw the fruits over the boundary wall. Such is his pure, loving service: it conquers the world.

তবে আর নারিকেল সংস্কার করাইল ৷  
পরম পবিত্র করি’ ভোগ লাগাইল ॥ ৮৫ ॥

tabe āra nārikela saṁskāra karāila  
then  other  coconuts  preparation  had made  
parama pavitra kari’ bhoga lāgāila  
supremely  pure  making  offering  set

(85) Then he had other coconuts prepared and offered them with the utmost purity.

এইমত কলা, আম্র, নারঙ্গ, কাঁঠাল ৷  
যাহা যাহা দূর-গ্রামে শুনিয়াছে ভাল ॥ ৮৬ ॥

ei mata kalā, āmra, nāraṅga, kām̐ṭhāla  
in this way  bananas  mangoes  oranges  jackfruits  
yāhā yāhā dūra-grāme śuniyāche bhāla  
whatever  whatever  in a distant village  he has heard  good

(86) In the same way, whenever he hears of good bananas, mangoes, oranges or jackfruits in some distant village,

বহুমূল্য দিয়া আনি’ করিয়া যতন ৷  
পবিত্র সংস্কার করি’ করে নিবেদন ॥ ৮৭ ॥

bahu-mūlya diyā āni’ kariyā yatana  
great price  paying  bringing  taking  pains  
pavitra saṁskāra kari’ kare nivedana  
pure  preparation  making  makes  offering

(87) he pays a great price, takes pains to have them brought, prepares them with purity and offers them.

এই মত ব্যঞ্জনের শাক, মূল, ফল ৷  
এই মত চিড়া, হুড়ুম, সন্দেশ সকল ॥ ৮৮ ॥

ei mata vyañjanera śāka, mūla, phala  
in the same way  for vegetable dishes  greens  roots  fruits  
ei mata ciḍā, huḍuma, sandeśa sakala  
in the same way  flat rice  huḍuma  sweets  all

(88) So too with the greens, roots and vegetables for his dishes, and with flat rice, *huḍuma* and all kinds of sweets;[^23]

এইমত পিঠা-পানা, ক্ষীর-ওদন ৷  
পরম পবিত্র, আর করে সর্ব্বোত্তম ॥ ৮৯ ॥

ei mata piṭhā-pānā, kṣīra-odana  
in the same way  cakes and drinks  sweet rice  
parama pavitra, āra kare sarvottama  
supremely  pure  and  makes  the best of all

(89) so too with cakes, sweet drinks and rice cooked in milk — all supremely pure and the very best.[^24]

কাশমৃদি, আচার, আদি অনেক প্রকার ৷  
গন্ধ, বস্ত্র, অলঙ্কার, সর্ব্বদ্রব্য সার ॥ ৯০ ॥

kāśamṛdi, ācāra, ādi aneka prakāra  
kāśamdi  pickles  and so on  many  kinds  
gandha, vastra, alaṅkāra, sarva-dravya sāra  
scents  cloth  ornaments  of all things  the best

(90) He offers *kāśamdi*, pickles and many other things, and the best of everything: scents, cloth and ornaments.

এইমত প্রেমের সেবা করে অনুপম ৷  
যাহা দেখি’ সর্ব্বলোকের জুড়ায় নয়ন ॥ ৯১ ॥

ei mata premera sevā kare anupama  
in this way  loving  service  does  incomparable  
yāhā dekhi’ sarva-lokera juḍāya nayana  
which  seeing  of all people  are soothed  the eyes

(91) Such is the incomparable service he renders in love; it is a balm to the eyes of all who see it.”

এত বলি’ রাঘবেরে কৈল আলিঙ্গনে ৷  
এইমত সম্মানিল সর্ব্ব ভক্তগণে ॥ ৯২ ॥

eta bali’ rāghavere kaila āliṅgane  
so  saying  Rāghava  made  embrace  
ei mata sammānila sarva bhakta-gaṇe  
in this way  honoured  all  the devotees

(92) With these words He embraced Rāghava, and in the same way He honoured all the devotees.

শিবানন্দ সেনে কহে করিয়া সম্মান ৷  
বাসুদেব-দত্তের তুমি করিহ সমাধান ॥ ৯৩ ॥

śivānanda sene kahe kariyā sammāna  
to Śivānanda Sena  says  showing  respect  
vāsudeva-dattera tumi kariha samādhāna  
of Vāsudeva Datta  you  do  care

(93) Respectfully He said to Śivānanda Sena, “Please look after Vāsudeva Datta.[^25]

পরম উদার ইঁহো, যে দিন যে আইসে ৷  
সেই দিনে ব্যয় করে, নাহি রাখে শেষে ॥ ৯৪ ॥

parama udāra im̐ho, ye dina ye āise  
supremely  generous  he  on whatever  day  whatever  comes  
sei dine vyaya kare, nāhi rākhe śeṣe  
on that  day  spending  does  not  keeps  remainder

(94) He is extremely generous: whatever comes to him on any day, he spends that same day and keeps nothing back.

‘গৃহস্থ’ হয়েন ইঁহো, চাহিয়ে সঞ্চয় ৷  
সঞ্চয় না কৈলে কুটুম্ব-ভরণ নাহি হয় ॥ ৯৫ ॥

‘gṛhastha’ hayena im̐ho, cāhiye sañcaya  
a householder  is  he  is needed  saving  
sañcaya nā kaile kuṭumba-bharaṇa nāhi haya  
saving  not  if made  the family’s upkeep  not  is

(95) He is a householder, and a householder needs to save; without savings he cannot support his family.

ইহার ঘরের আয়-ব্যয়, সব — তোমার স্থানে ৷  
‘সরখেল’ হঞা তুমি করিহ সমাধানে ॥ ৯৬ ॥

ihāra gharera āya-vyaya, saba — tomāra sthāne  
his  household’s  income and expenses  all  —  with you  
‘sarakhela’ hañā tumi kariha samādhāne  
the sarakhela  becoming  you  do  managing

(96) Let all the income and expenses of his household be in your hands. Be his *sarakhela*, his guardian, and manage his affairs.

প্রতিবর্ষে আমার সব ভক্তগণ লঞা ৷  
গুণ্ডিচায় আসিবে সবায় পালন করিয়া ॥ ৯৭ ॥

prati-varṣe āmāra saba bhakta-gaṇa lañā  
every year  My  all  devotees  taking  
guṇḍicāya āsibe sabāya pālana kariyā  
to Guṇḍicā  you will come  all  protection  making

(97) Every year bring all My devotees to the Guṇḍicā festival, and take care of them all.”[^26]

কুলীনগ্রামীরে কহে সম্মান করিয়া ৷  
প্রত্যব্দ আসিবে যাত্রায় পট্টডোরী লঞা ॥ ৯৮ ॥

kulīna-grāmīre kahe sammāna kariyā  
to the residents of Kulīna-grāma  says  respect  showing  
pratyabda āsibe yātrāya paṭṭa-ḍorī lañā  
every year  you will come  to the festival  silk ropes  bringing

(98) To the residents of Kulīna-grāma He said respectfully, “Come every year to the festival, and bring silk ropes.

গুণরাজ-খাঁন কৈল শ্রীকৃষ্ণবিজয় ৷  
তাঁহা একবাক্য তাঁর আছে প্রেমময় ॥ ৯৯ ॥

guṇarāja-khām̐na kaila śrī-kṛṣṇa-vijaya  
Guṇarāja Khān  composed  the Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vijaya  
tām̐hā eka-vākya tām̐ra āche premamaya  
there  one sentence  his  is  full of love

(99) Guṇarāja Khān composed the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vijaya*, and in it there is a sentence of his that is full of love:[^27]

‘নন্দনন্দন কৃষ্ণ — মোর প্রাণনাথ’ ৷  
এই বাক্যে বিকাইনু তাঁর বংশের হাত ॥ ১০০ ॥

‘nandanandana kṛṣṇa — mora prāṇa-nātha’  
Nanda’s son  Kṛṣṇa  —  my  Lord of life  
ei vākye vikāinu tām̐ra vaṁśera hāta  
by this  sentence  I am sold  his  family’s  into the hands

(100) ‘Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda, is the Lord of my life.’ For this one sentence I have sold Myself into the hands of his descendants.[^28]

তোমার কি কথা, তোমার গ্রামের কুক্কুর ৷  
সেহ মোর প্রিয়, অন্যজন রহু দূর ॥ ১০১ ॥

tomāra ki kathā, tomāra grāmera kukkura  
of you  what  talk  of your  village  the dog  
seha mora priya, anya-jana rahu dūra  
even he  to Me  dear  other people  let be  far

(101) What need I say of you? Even a dog of your village is dear to Me, to say nothing of anyone else there.”[^29]

তবে রামানন্দ, আর সত্যরাজ খাঁন ৷  
প্রভুর চরণে কিছু কৈল নিবেদন ॥ ১০২ ॥

tabe rāmānanda, āra satyarāja khām̐na  
then  Rāmānanda  and  Satyarāja Khān  
prabhura caraṇe kichu kaila nivedana  
of the Lord  at the feet  something  made  submission

(102) Then Rāmānanda and Satyarāja Khān humbly put a question at the Lord’s feet:[^30]

গৃহস্থ বিষয়ী আমি, কি মোর সাধনে ৷  
শ্রীমুখে করেন আজ্ঞা, নিবেদি চরণে ॥ ১০৩ ॥

gṛhastha viṣayī āmi, ki mora sādhane  
a householder  worldly  I  what  my  practice  
śrī-mukhe karena ājñā, nivedi caraṇe  
with Your holy mouth  give  the order  I submit  at the feet

(103) “I am a worldly householder. What should my practice be? Please instruct me with Your own lips — I submit this at Your feet.”

প্রভু কহেন, — ‘কৃষ্ণসেবা’, ‘বৈষ্ণব-সেবন’ ৷  
‘নিরন্তর কর কৃষ্ণনাম-সংকীর্ত্তন’ ॥ ১০৪ ॥

prabhu kahena, — ‘kṛṣṇa-sevā’, ‘vaiṣṇava-sevana’  
the Lord  says  —  service to Kṛṣṇa  service to the Vaiṣṇavas  
‘nirantara kara kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṁkīrtana’  
constantly  do  saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name

(104) The Lord said, “Serve Kṛṣṇa, serve the Vaiṣṇavas, and always chant the name of Kṛṣṇa in saṅkīrtana.”

সত্যরাজ বলে, — বৈষ্ণব চিনিব কেমনে ?  
কে বৈষ্ণব, কহ তাঁর সামান্য লক্ষণে ॥ ১০৫ ॥

satyarāja bale, — vaiṣṇava ciniba kemane?  
Satyarāja  says  —  a Vaiṣṇava  shall I recognise  how  
ke vaiṣṇava, kaha tām̐ra sāmānya lakṣaṇe  
who  a Vaiṣṇava  tell  his  general  marks

(105) Satyarāja said, “How am I to recognise a Vaiṣṇava? Tell me who is a Vaiṣṇava — what are his general marks?”

প্রভু কহে, — যাঁর মুখে শুনি একবার ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম, সেই পূজ্য, — শ্রেষ্ঠ সবাকার ॥ ১০৬ ॥

prabhu kahe, — yām̐ra mukhe śuni eka-bāra  
the Lord  says  —  from whose  mouth  I hear  once  
kṛṣṇa-nāma, sei pūjya, — śreṣṭha sabākāra  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  he  worshipable  —  best  of all

(106) The Lord said, “Anyone from whose mouth I hear the name of Kṛṣṇa even once is worthy of worship — he is the best of all.[^31]

এক কৃষ্ণনামে করে সর্ব্ব-পাপ ক্ষয় ৷  
নববিধা ভক্তি পূর্ণ নাম হৈতে হয় ॥ ১০৭ ॥

eka kṛṣṇa-nāme kare sarva-pāpa kṣaya  
one  name of Kṛṣṇa  does  of all sins  destruction  
nava-vidhā bhakti pūrṇa nāma haite haya  
ninefold  devotion  complete  the name  from  becomes

(107) A single name of Kṛṣṇa destroys all sins, and through the holy name the nine kinds of devotion become complete.[^32]

দীক্ষা-পুরশ্চর্য্যা-বিধি অপেক্ষা না করে ৷  
জিহ্বা-স্পর্শে আ-চণ্ডাল সবারে উদ্ধারে ॥ ১০৮ ॥

dīkṣā-puraścaryā-vidhi apekṣā nā kare  
initiation, puraścaryā, rules  dependence  not  does  
jihvā-sparśe ā-caṇḍāla sabāre uddhāre  
by touching the tongue  down to the caṇḍāla  all  delivers

(108) The holy name does not wait upon the rules of initiation or *puraścaryā*. Merely by touching the tongue it delivers everyone, down to the *caṇḍāla*.[^33]

অনুষঙ্গ-ফলে করে সংসারের ক্ষয় ৷  
চিত্ত আকর্ষিয়া করায় কৃষ্ণে প্রেমোদয় ॥ ১০৯ ॥

anuṣaṅga-phale kare saṁsārera kṣaya  
as a by-product  does  of worldly existence  ending  
citta ākarṣiyā karāya kṛṣṇe premodaya  
the heart  attracting  causes  for Kṛṣṇa  the dawning of love

(109) As a mere by-product it ends worldly existence; and by attracting the heart it awakens love for Kṛṣṇa.

আকৃষ্টিঃ কৃতচেতসাং সুমনসামুচ্চাটনং চাংহসা-  
মাচণ্ডালমমূকলোকসুলভো বশ্যশ্চ মুক্তিশ্রিয়ঃ ৷  
নো দীক্ষাং ন চ সৎক্রিয়াং ন চ পুরশ্চর্য্যাং মনাগীক্ষতে  
মন্ত্রোঽয়ং রসনাস্পৃগেব ফলতি শ্রীকৃষ্ণনামাত্মকঃ ॥ ১১০ ॥

ākṛṣṭiḥ kṛta-cetasāṁ su-manasām uccāṭanaṁ cāṁhasām  
attraction  of the liberated  of the wise  uprooting  and  of sins  
ācaṇḍālam amūka-loka-sulabho vaśyaś ca mukti-śriyaḥ  
down to the caṇḍāla  to all people but the mute  easily available  the controller  and  of the wealth of liberation  
no dīkṣāṁ na ca sat-kriyāṁ na ca puraścaryāṁ manāg īkṣate  
not  initiation  nor  and  pious rites  nor  and  puraścaryā  in the least  looks for  
mantro ’yaṁ rasanā-spṛg eva phalati śrī-kṛṣṇa-nāmātmakaḥ  
mantra  this  touching the tongue  merely  bears fruit  consisting of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name

(110) “This mantra, the name of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, draws the liberated and the wise and uproots all sins. Down to the *caṇḍāla*, it is within easy reach of everyone who is not mute, and it holds the wealth of liberation in its power. It does not look in the least for initiation, pious rites or *puraścaryā*: merely by touching the tongue it bears fruit.” (Padyāvalī, 29)[^34]

‘অতএব যাঁর মুখে এক কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
সেই ত’ বৈষ্ণব, করিহ তাঁহার সম্মান’ ॥ ১১১ ॥

‘ataeva yām̐ra mukhe eka kṛṣṇa-nāma  
therefore  in whose  mouth  one  name of Kṛṣṇa  
sei ta’ vaiṣṇava, kariha tām̐hāra sammāna’  
he  indeed  a Vaiṣṇava  do  his  honour

(111) Therefore anyone on whose lips is even one name of Kṛṣṇa is a Vaiṣṇava; show him honour.”[^35]

খণ্ডের মুকুন্দদাস, শ্রীরঘুনন্দন ৷  
শ্রীনরহরি, — এই মুখ্য তিনজন ॥ ১১২ ॥

khaṇḍera mukunda-dāsa, śrī-raghunandana  
of Khaṇḍa  Mukunda dāsa  Śrī Raghunandana  
śrī-narahari, — ei mukhya tina jana  
Śrī Narahari  —  these  chief  three  persons

(112) Of the devotees from Khaṇḍa, three were the chief: Mukunda dāsa, Śrī Raghunandana and Śrī Narahari.

মুকুন্দ-দাসেরে পুছে শচীর নন্দন ৷  
‘তুমি — পিতা, পুত্র তোমার — শ্রীরঘুনন্দন ? ॥ ১১৩ ॥

mukunda-dāsere puche śacīra nandana  
Mukunda dāsa  asks  Śacī’s  son  
‘tumi — pitā, putra tomāra — śrī-raghunandana?  
you  —  the father  son  your  —  Śrī Raghunandana

(113) The son of Śacī asked Mukunda dāsa, “Are you the father, and Śrī Raghunandana your son?

কিবা রঘুনন্দন — পিতা, তুমি — তার তনয় ?  
নিশ্চয় করিয়া কহ, যাউক সংশয়’ ॥ ১১৪ ॥

kibā raghunandana — pitā, tumi — tāra tanaya?  
or  Raghunandana  —  the father  you  —  his  son  
niścaya kariyā kaha, yāuka saṁśaya’  
certainty  making  tell  let go  the doubt

(114) Or is Raghunandana the father, and you his son? Tell Me for certain, so that My doubt may go.”

মুকুন্দ কহে, — রঘুনন্দন আমার ‘পিতা’ হয় ৷  
আমি তার ‘পুত্র’, — এই আমার নিশ্চয় ॥ ১১৫ ॥

mukunda kahe, — raghunandana āmāra ‘pitā’ haya  
Mukunda  says  —  Raghunandana  my  father  is  
āmi tāra ‘putra’, — ei āmāra niścaya  
I  his  son  —  this  my  conviction

(115) Mukunda said, “Raghunandana is my father, and I am his son — that is my conviction.[^36]

আমা সবার কৃষ্ণভক্তি রঘুনন্দন হৈতে ৷  
অতএব পিতা — রঘুনন্দন আমার নিশ্চিতে ॥ ১১৬ ॥

āmā sabāra kṛṣṇa-bhakti raghunandana haite  
of us  all  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  Raghunandana  from  
ataeva pitā — raghunandana āmāra niścite  
therefore  the father  —  Raghunandana  my  certainly

(116) All of us have received devotion to Kṛṣṇa from Raghunandana. So Raghunandana is certainly my father.”

শুনি’ হর্ষে কহে প্রভু — কহিলে নিশ্চয় ৷  
‘যাঁহা হৈতে কৃষ্ণভক্তি সেই গুরু হয়’ ॥ ১১৭ ॥

śuni’ harṣe kahe prabhu — kahile niścaya  
hearing  joyfully  says  the Lord  —  you have said  rightly  
‘yām̐hā haite kṛṣṇa-bhakti sei guru haya’  
from whom  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  he  guru  is

(117) Hearing this, the Lord said joyfully, “You have spoken rightly: he from whom devotion to Kṛṣṇa comes is the guru.”

ভক্তের মহিমা কহিতে প্রভু পায় সুখ ৷  
ভক্তের মহিমা কহিতে হয় পঞ্চমুখ ॥ ১১৮ ॥

bhaktera mahimā kahite prabhu pāya sukha  
of the devotee  the glory  telling  the Lord  gets  happiness  
bhaktera mahimā kahite haya pañca-mukha  
of the devotee  the glory  telling  becomes  five-mouthed

(118) The Lord delights in telling the glories of His devotees; telling them, He becomes as if five-mouthed.

ভক্তগণে কহে, — শুন মুকুন্দের প্রেম ৷  
নির্ম্মল, নিগূঢ় প্রেম, যেন শুদ্ধ হেম ॥ ১১৯ ॥

bhakta-gaṇe kahe, — śuna mukundera prema  
to the devotees  says  —  hear  Mukunda’s  love  
nirmala, nigūḍha prema, yena śuddha hema  
pure  deep  love  like  pure  gold

(119) He said to the devotees, “Hear about Mukunda’s love: it is spotless and deep, like pure gold.

বাহ্যে রাজবৈদ্য ইঁহো, করে রাজ-সেবা ৷  
অন্তরে প্রেম ইঁহার জানিবেক কেবা ॥ ১২০ ॥

bāhye rāja-vaidya im̐ho, kare rāja-sevā  
outwardly  a royal physician  he  does  the king’s service  
antare prema im̐hāra jānibeka kebā  
within  love  his  will know  who

(120) Outwardly he is a royal physician in the king’s service. Who could know the love within him?[^37]

এক দিন ম্লেচ্ছ-রাজার উচ্চ-টুঙ্গিতে ৷  
চিকিৎসার বাত্ কহে ইঁহার অগ্রেতে ॥ ১২১ ॥

eka dina mleccha-rājāra ucca-ṭuṅgite  
one  day  of the Muslim king  on the high platform  
cikitsāra bāt kahe im̐hāra agrete  
of treatment  talk  speaks  him  before

(121) One day, on his high platform, the Muslim king was talking with him about medical treatment.[^38]

হেনকালে এক ময়ূর-পুচ্ছের আড়ানী ৷  
রাজ-শিরোপরি ধরে এক সেবক আনি’ ॥ ১২২ ॥

hena-kāle eka mayūra-pucchera āḍānī  
at that time  a  of peacock feathers  fan  
rāja-śiropari dhare eka sevaka āni’  
over the king’s head  holds  a  servant  bringing

(122) Just then a servant brought a fan of peacock feathers and held it over the king’s head.

শিখিপিচ্ছ দেখি’ মুকুন্দ প্রেমাবিষ্ট হৈলা ৷  
অতি-উচ্চ টুঙ্গি হৈতে ভূমিতে পড়িলা ॥ ১২৩ ॥

śikhi-piccha dekhi’ mukunda premāviṣṭa hailā  
the peacock feathers  seeing  Mukunda  overwhelmed by love  became  
ati-ucca ṭuṅgi haite bhūmite paḍilā  
very high  platform  from  to the ground  fell

(123) At the sight of the peacock feathers Mukunda was overwhelmed with love and fell from the high platform to the ground.

রাজার জ্ঞান, — রাজ-বৈদ্যের হইল মরণ ৷  
আপনে নামিয়া তবে করাইল চেতন ॥ ১২৪ ॥

rājāra jñāna, — rāja-vaidyera haila maraṇa  
the king’s  thought  —  of the royal physician  has happened  death  
āpane nāmiyā tabe karāila cetana  
himself  descending  then  brought about  consciousness

(124) The king thought his royal physician had been killed. He came down himself and brought him back to his senses.

রাজা বলে, ব্যথা তুমি পাইলে কোন্ ঠাঞি ?  
মুকুন্দ কহে, — অতিবড় ব্যথা পাই নাই ॥ ১২৫ ॥

rājā bale, vyathā tumi pāile kon ṭhāñi?  
the king  says  pain  you  got  in which  place  
mukunda kahe, — ati-baḍa vyathā pāi nāi  
Mukunda  says  —  very great  pain  I got  not

(125) The king asked, “Where are you hurt?” Mukunda answered, “I am not badly hurt.”

রাজা কহে, — মুকুন্দ, তুমি পড়িলা কি লাগি’ ?  
মুকুন্দ কহে, — রাজা, মোর ব্যাধি আছে মৃগী ॥ ১২৬ ॥

rājā kahe, — mukunda, tumi paḍilā ki lāgi’?  
the king  says  —  Mukunda  you  fell  why  
mukunda kahe, — rājā, mora vyādhi āche mṛgī  
Mukunda  says  —  O king  my  illness  is  epilepsy

(126) The king asked, “Mukunda, why did you fall?” Mukunda said, “O king, I suffer from epilepsy.”

মহাবিদগ্ধ রাজা, সেই সব জানে ৷  
মুকুন্দেরে হৈল তাঁর ‘মহাসিদ্ধ’-জ্ঞানে ॥ ১২৭ ॥

mahā-vidagdha rājā, sei saba jāne  
very shrewd  the king  that  all  knows  
mukundere haila tām̐ra ‘mahā-siddha’-jñāne  
of Mukunda  became  his  as a great perfected soul  understanding

(127) But the king was very shrewd and understood it all: he came to regard Mukunda as a great perfected soul.[^39]

রঘুনন্দন সেবা করে কৃষ্ণের মন্দিরে ৷  
দ্বারে পুষ্করিণী, তার ঘাটের উপরে ॥ ১২৮ ॥

raghunandana sevā kare kṛṣṇera mandire  
Raghunandana  service  does  in Kṛṣṇa’s  temple  
dvāre puṣkariṇī, tāra ghāṭera upare  
at the door  a pond  its  of the ghāṭa  above

(128) Raghunandana serves in Kṛṣṇa’s temple. At its door there is a pond, and above its bathing steps

কদম্বের এক বৃক্ষে ফুটে বারমাসে ৷  
নিত্য দুই ফুল হয় কৃষ্ণ-অবতংসে ॥ ১২৯ ॥

kadambera eka vṛkṣe phuṭe bāra-māse  
of kadamba  one  on a tree  bloom  twelve months  
nitya dui phula haya kṛṣṇa-avataṁse  
daily  two  flowers  are  for Kṛṣṇa’s ornament

(129) stands a kadamba tree that blossoms all twelve months of the year; every day it gives two flowers to adorn Kṛṣṇa.[^40]

মুকুন্দেরে কহে পুনঃ মধুর বচন ৷  
‘তোমার কার্য্য — ধর্ম্ম-ধন-উপার্জ্জন ॥ ১৩০ ॥

mukundere kahe punaḥ madhura vacana  
to Mukunda  says  again  sweet  words  
‘tomāra kārya — dharma-dhana-upārjana  
your  duty  —  earning dharma and wealth

(130) Again He spoke sweet words to Mukunda: “Your duty is to earn wealth in keeping with dharma.[^41]

রঘুনন্দনের কার্য্য — কৃষ্ণের সেবন ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-সেবা বিনা ইঁহার অন্য নাহি মন ॥ ১৩১ ॥

raghunandanera kārya — kṛṣṇera sevana  
Raghunandana’s  duty  —  of Kṛṣṇa  service  
kṛṣṇa-sevā vinā im̐hāra anya nāhi mana  
service to Kṛṣṇa  without  his  other  not  mind

(131) Raghunandana’s duty is to serve Kṛṣṇa; his mind turns to nothing but serving Kṛṣṇa.

নরহরি রহু আমার ভক্তগণ-সনে ৷  
এই তিন কার্য্য সদা করহ তিন-জনে’ ॥ ১৩২ ॥

narahari rahu āmāra bhakta-gaṇa-sane  
Narahari  let stay  My  with the devotees  
ei tina kārya sadā karaha tina-jane’  
these  three  duties  always  do  you three

(132) And let Narahari stay with My devotees. You three must always carry out these three duties.”

সার্ব্বভৌম, বিদ্যাবাচস্পতি, — দুই ভাই ৷  
দুই জনে কৃপা করি’ কহেন গোসাঞি ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

sārvabhauma, vidyā-vācaspati, — dui bhāi  
Sārvabhauma  Vidyā-vācaspati  —  two  brothers  
dui jane kṛpā kari’ kahena gosāñi  
to the two  persons  mercy  showing  says  the Lord

(133) Showing His mercy to the two brothers, Sārvabhauma and Vidyā-vācaspati, the Lord said:

‘দারু’-‘জল’-রূপে কৃষ্ণ প্রকট সম্প্রতি ৷  
‘দরশন’-‘স্নানে’ করে জীবের মুকতি ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

‘dāru’-‘jala’-rūpe kṛṣṇa prakaṭa samprati  
in the form of wood and of water  Kṛṣṇa  manifest  now  
‘daraśana’-‘snāne’ kare jīvera mukati  
by seeing and by bathing  does  of living beings  liberation

(134) “Kṛṣṇa is now manifest in the form of wood and in the form of water. Through seeing Him and through bathing in Him, He liberates living beings.

‘দারুব্রহ্ম’-রূপে — সাক্ষাৎ শ্রীপুরুষোত্তম ৷  
ভাগীরথী হন সাক্ষাৎ ‘জলব্রহ্ম’-সম ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

‘dāru-brahma’-rūpe — sākṣāt śrī-puruṣottama  
in the form of the Dāru-brahma  —  directly  Śrī Puruṣottama  
bhāgīrathī hana sākṣāt ‘jala-brahma’-sama  
the Bhāgīrathī  is  directly  the Jala-brahma  equal to

(135) As the Dāru-brahma, Brahman in the form of wood, He is Śrī Puruṣottama Himself; and the Bhāgīrathī is the Jala-brahma, Brahman in the form of water.

সার্ব্বভৌম, কর ‘দারুব্রহ্ম’ আরাধন ৷  
বাচস্পতি, কর ‘জলব্রহ্মের’ সেবন ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

sārvabhauma, kara ‘dāru-brahma’ ārādhana  
Sārvabhauma  do  the Dāru-brahma  worship  
vācaspati, kara ‘jala-brahmera’ sevana  
Vācaspati  do  of the Jala-brahma  service

(136) Sārvabhauma, worship the Dāru-brahma. Vācaspati, serve the Jala-brahma.”[^42]

মুরারি-গুপ্তেরে প্রভু করি’ আলিঙ্গন ৷  
তাঁর ভক্তিনিষ্ঠা কহেন, শুন ভক্তগণ ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

murāri-guptere prabhu kari’ āliṅgana  
Murāri Gupta  the Lord  making  embrace  
tām̐ra bhakti-niṣṭhā kahena, śuna bhakta-gaṇa  
his  steadfast devotion  tells  hear  devotees

(137) The Lord embraced Murāri Gupta and spoke of his steadfast devotion: “Listen, devotees![^43]

পূর্ব্বে আমি ইঁহারে লোভাইল বার বার ৷  
পরম মধুর, গুপ্ত, ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

pūrve āmi im̐hāre lobhāila bāra bāra  
before  I  him  tempted  time  after time  
parama madhura, gupta, vrajendra-kumāra  
supremely  sweet  Gupta  the son of the king of Vraja

(138) In the past I tried again and again to win him over: ‘Gupta, the son of the king of Vraja is supremely sweet.

স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ — সর্ব্বাংশী, সর্ব্বাশ্রয় ৷  
বিশুদ্ধ-নির্ম্মল-প্রেম, সর্ব্বরসময় ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa — sarvāṁśī, sarvāśraya  
Himself  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa  —  the source of all portions  the shelter of all  
viśuddha-nirmala-prema, sarva-rasamaya  
pure spotless love  full of all rasas

(139) Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself, the source of all His portions and the shelter of all. He is pure, spotless love, and every rasa is found in Him.

সকল-সদ্গুণ-বৃন্দ-রত্ন-রত্নাকর ৷  
বিদগ্ধ, চতুর, ধীর, রসিক শেখর ॥ ১৪০ ॥

sakala-sadguṇa-vṛnda-ratna-ratnākara  
a jewel-mine of the jewels of all good qualities  
vidagdha, catura, dhīra, rasika śekhara  
clever  skilful  composed  of rasikas  the crest

(140) He is a mine filled with the jewels of every good quality. He is expert, clever and composed — the crown of all who relish rasa.

মধুর-চরিত্র কৃষ্ণের মধুর বিলাস ৷  
চাতুর্য্য, বৈদগ্ধ্য করে যাঁর লীলা-রস ॥ ১৪১ ॥

madhura-caritra kṛṣṇera madhura vilāsa  
of sweet character  Kṛṣṇa’s  sweet  pastimes  
cāturya, vaidagdhya kare yām̐ra līlā-rasa  
cleverness  skill  make  whose  the rasa of pastimes

(141) Kṛṣṇa’s nature is sweet, and His pastimes are sweet; cleverness and skill fill the rasa of His play.

সেই কৃষ্ণ ভজ তুমি, হও কৃষ্ণাশ্রয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণ বিনা অন্য-উপাসনা মনে নাহি লয় ॥ ১৪২ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa bhaja tumi, hao kṛṣṇāśraya  
that  Kṛṣṇa  worship  you  become  sheltered in Kṛṣṇa  
kṛṣṇa vinā anya-upāsanā mane nāhi laya  
Kṛṣṇa  except  other worship  the mind  not  accepts

(142) Worship that Kṛṣṇa and take shelter of Him. Apart from Kṛṣṇa, no other worship can win the mind.’

এইমত বার বার শুনিয়া বচন ৷  
আমার গৌরবে কিছু ফিরি’ গেলা মন ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

ei mata bāra bāra śuniyā vacana  
in this way  time  after time  hearing  words  
āmāra gaurave kichu phiri’ gelā mana  
out of regard for Me  somewhat  turning  went  his mind

(143) Hearing such words from Me again and again, out of regard for Me his mind turned a little.[^44]

আমারে কহেন, — আমি তোমার কিঙ্কর ৷  
তোমার আজ্ঞাকারী আমি নহি স্বতন্তর ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

āmāre kahena, — āmi tomāra kiṅkara  
to Me  says  —  I  Your  servant  
tomāra ājñākārī āmi nahi svatantara  
Your  order-follower  I  am not  independent

(144) He said to Me, ‘I am Your servant. I carry out Your orders; I am not independent.’

এত বলি’ ঘরে গেল, চিন্তি’ রাত্রিকালে ৷  
রঘুনাথ-ত্যাগ-চিন্তায় হইল বিকলে ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

eta bali’ ghare gela, cinti’ rātri-kāle  
so  saying  home  went  thinking  at night  
raghunātha-tyāga-cintāya haila vikale  
at the thought of giving up Raghunātha  became  distraught

(145) With these words he went home. That night, as he thought it over, the idea of giving up Raghunātha left him distraught.

কেমনে ছাড়িব রঘুনাথের চরণ !  
আজি রাত্রে প্রভু মোর করাহ মরণ ! ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

kemane chāḍiba raghunāthera caraṇa!  
how  shall I give up  Raghunātha’s  feet  
āji rātre prabhu mora karāha maraṇa!  
today  at night  O Lord  my  bring about  death

(146) ‘How can I give up the feet of Raghunātha? O Lord, let me die this very night!’

এই মত সর্ব্ব-রাত্রি করেন ক্রন্দন ৷  
মনে সোয়াস্তি নাহি, রাত্রি করেন জাগরণ ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

ei mata sarva-rātri karena krandana  
thus  all night  does  weeping  
mane soyāsti nāhi, rātri karena jāgaraṇa  
in mind  peace  not  at night  does  keeping awake

(147) So he wept all night long; there was no peace in his mind, and he could not sleep.

প্রাতঃকালে আসি’ মোর ধরিল চরণ ৷  
কান্দিতে কান্দিতে কিছু করে নিবেদন ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

prātaḥ-kāle āsi’ mora dharila caraṇa  
in the morning  coming  My  clasped  feet  
kāndite kāndite kichu kare nivedana  
weeping  weeping  something  makes  submission

(148) In the morning he came and clasped My feet, and, weeping bitterly, made this plea to Me:

রঘুনাথের পায় মুঞি বেচিয়াছোঁ মাথা ৷  
কাড়িতে না পারি মাথা, মনে পাই ব্যথা ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

raghunāthera pāya muñi veciyāchom̐ māthā  
at Raghunātha’s  feet  I  have sold  my head  
kāḍite nā pāri māthā, mane pāi vyathā  
to take back  not  I can  the head  in mind  I get  pain

(149) ‘I have sold my head at the feet of Raghunātha. I cannot take it back, and my heart is in anguish.[^45]

শ্রীরঘুনাথ-চরণ ছাড়ান না যায় ৷  
তব আজ্ঞা-ভঙ্গ হয়, কি করি উপায় ! ॥ ১৫০ ॥

śrī-raghunātha-caraṇa chāḍāna nā yāya  
Śrī Raghunātha’s feet  giving up  not  is possible  
tava ājñā-bhaṅga haya, ki kari upāya!  
Your  order-breaking  happens  what  do I  remedy

(150) I cannot give up the feet of Śrī Raghunātha, yet that means breaking Your order. What am I to do?

তাতে মোরে এই কৃপা কর, দয়াময় ৷  
তোমার আগে মৃত্যু হউক, যাউক সংশয় ॥ ১৫১ ॥

tāte more ei kṛpā kara, dayāmaya  
therefore  to me  this  mercy  do  O merciful one  
tomāra āge mṛtyu hauka, yāuka saṁśaya  
of You  before  death  let be  let go  doubt

(151) So, O merciful Lord, show me this mercy: let me die before You, and let my dilemma end.’

এত শুনি’ আমি বড় মনে সুখ পাইলুঁ ৷  
ইঁহারে উঠাঞা তবে আলিঙ্গন কৈলুঁ ॥ ১৫২ ॥

eta śuni’ āmi baḍa mane sukha pāilum̐  
this  hearing  I  great  in the mind  joy  felt  
im̐hāre uṭhāñā tabe āliṅgana kailum̐  
him  raising  then  embrace  I made

(152) “Hearing this, I felt great joy at heart. Then I raised him up and embraced him.

সাধু সাধু, গুপ্ত, তোমার সুদৃঢ় ভজন ৷  
আমার বচনেহ তোমার না টলিল মন ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

sādhu sādhu, gupta, tomāra sudṛḍha bhajana  
excellent  excellent  Gupta  your  firm  worship  
āmāra vacaneha tomāra nā ṭalila mana  
My  even by the words  your  not  wavered  mind

(153) “ ‘Excellent, excellent, Gupta! Your worship is firm indeed: even My words did not shake your mind.

এইমত সেবকের প্রীতি চাহি প্রভু-পায় ৷  
প্রভু ছাড়াইলেহ, পদ ছাড়ান না যায় ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

ei mata sevakera prīti cāhi prabhu-pāya  
such  indeed  of a servant  love  I want  for the Lord’s feet  
prabhu chāḍāileha, pada chāḍāna nā yāya  
the Lord  even if He makes him leave  the feet  to leave  not  is possible

(154) “ ‘This is the love for the Lord’s feet that I want in a servant: even if the Lord tries to make him leave them, he cannot let go of those feet.

এইমত তোমার নিষ্ঠা জানিবার তরে ৷  
তোমারে আগ্রহ আমি কৈলুঁ বারে বারে ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

ei mata tomāra niṣṭhā jānibāra tare  
in this  way  your  steadfastness  to know  for the sake of  
tomāre āgraha āmi kailum̐ bāre bāre  
you  urging  I  did  time  after time

(155) “ ‘It was to learn how steadfast you are that I urged you again and again.

সাক্ষাৎ হনুমান্ তুমি শ্রীরাম-কিঙ্কর ৷  
তুমি কেনে ছাড়িবে তাঁর চরণ-কমল ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

sākṣāt hanumān tumi śrī-rāma-kiṅkara  
in person  Hanumān  you  of Śrī Rāma  the servant  
tumi kene chāḍibe tām̐ra caraṇa-kamala  
you  why  would leave  His  lotus feet

(156) “ ‘You are Hanumān himself, the servant of Śrī Rāma. Why would you ever give up His lotus feet?’

সেই মুরারি-গুপ্ত এই — মোর প্রাণ সম ৷  
ইঁহার দৈন্য শুনি’ মোর ফাটয়ে জীবন ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

sei murāri-gupta ei — mora prāṇa sama  
that  Murāri Gupta  is this one  My  life  equal to  
im̐hāra dainya śuni’ mora phāṭaye jīvana  
his  humility  hearing  My  bursts  life

(157) “This is that same Murāri Gupta, who is as dear to Me as My life. When I hear his humility, My heart breaks.”

তবে বাসুদেবে প্রভু করি’ আলিঙ্গন ৷  
তাঁর গুণ কহে হঞা সহস্র-বদন ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

tabe vāsudeve prabhu kari’ āliṅgana  
then  Vāsudeva  the Lord  making  embrace  
tām̐ra guṇa kahe hañā sahasra-vadana  
his  qualities  speaks  becoming  thousand-mouthed

(158) Then the Lord embraced Vāsudeva and began to describe his qualities as if He had a thousand mouths.

নিজ-গুণ শুনি’ দত্ত মনে লজ্জা পাঞা ৷  
নিবেদন করে প্রভুর চরণে ধরিয়া ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

nija-guṇa śuni’ datta mane lajjā pāñā  
his own qualities  hearing  Datta  in his mind  shame  feeling  
nivedana kare prabhura caraṇe dhariyā  
a petition  makes  of the Lord  the feet  clasping

(159) Hearing his own qualities praised, Vāsudeva Datta felt ashamed. Clasping the Lord’s feet, he made a petition:

জগৎ তারিতে প্রভু তোমার অবতার ৷  
মোর নিবেদন এক করহ অঙ্গীকার ॥ ১৬০ ॥

jagat tārite prabhu tomāra avatāra  
the world  to deliver  O Lord  Your  descent  
mora nivedana eka karaha aṅgīkāra  
my  petition  one  make  acceptance

(160) “O Lord, You have descended to deliver the world. Please grant me one request.

করিতে সমর্থ, তুমি হও, দয়াময় ৷  
তুমি মন কর, তবে অনায়াসে হয় ॥ ১৬১ ॥

karite samartha, tumi hao, dayāmaya  
to do  able  You  are  O merciful one  
tumi mana kara, tabe anāyāse haya  
You  the mind  make  then  without effort  it happens

(161) “O merciful Lord, You are able to do anything. If You only wish it, it happens without effort.

জীবের দুঃখ দেখি’ মোর হৃদয় বিদরে ৷  
সর্ব্বজীবের পাপ প্রভু দেহ’ মোর শিরে ॥ ১৬২ ॥

jīvera duḥkha dekhi’ mora hṛdaya bidare  
of living beings  the suffering  seeing  my  heart  breaks  
sarva-jīvera pāpa prabhu deha’ mora śire  
of all living beings  the sins  O Lord  place  on my  head

(162) “My heart breaks to see the suffering of living beings. O Lord, place the sins of all living beings on my head.

জীবের পাপ লঞা মুঞি করি নরক ভোগ ৷  
সকল জীবের, প্রভু, ঘুচাহ ভবরোগ ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

jīvera pāpa lañā muñi kari naraka bhoga  
of living beings  the sins  taking  I  will undergo  of hell  the suffering  
sakala jīvera, prabhu, ghucāha bhava-roga  
of all  living beings  O Lord  remove  the disease of material existence

(163) “Let me take the sins of the living beings and suffer in hell for them. O Lord, cure all living beings of the disease of material existence.”

এত শুনি’ মহাপ্রভুর চিত্ত দ্রবিলা ৷  
অশ্রু-কম্প-স্বরভঙ্গে কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

eta śuni’ mahāprabhura citta dravilā  
this  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  the heart  melted  
aśru-kampa-svarabhaṅge kahite lāgilā  
with tears, trembling and a faltering voice  to speak  He began

(164) When Mahāprabhu heard this, His heart melted, and with tears, trembling and a faltering voice He began to speak:

তোমার বিচিত্র নহে, তুমি — সাক্ষাৎ প্রহ্লাদ ৷  
তোমার উপরে কৃষ্ণের সম্পূর্ণ প্রসাদ ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

tomāra vicitra nahe, tumi — sākṣāt prahlāda  
in you  wonderful  it is not  you  in person  Prahlāda  
tomāra upare kṛṣṇera sampūrṇa prasāda  
upon  you  Kṛṣṇa’s  complete  grace

(165) “Such words are no wonder coming from you: you are Prahlāda himself. Kṛṣṇa’s grace rests on you in full.

কৃষ্ণ সেই সত্য করে, যেই মাগে ভৃত্য ৷  
ভৃত্য-বাঞ্ছা-পূরণ বিনা নাহি অন্য কৃত্য ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa sei satya kare, yei māge bhṛtya  
Kṛṣṇa  that  true  makes  whatever  asks  the servant  
bhṛtya-vāñchā-pūraṇa vinā nāhi anya kṛtya  
of the servant’s desire  fulfilling  except  there is not  other  task

(166) “Whatever His servant asks for, Kṛṣṇa makes true. He has no task other than fulfilling His servant’s desire.

ব্রহ্মাণ্ড জীবের তুমি বাঞ্ছিলে নিস্তার ৷  
বিনা পাপ-ভোগে হবে সবার উদ্ধার ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

brahmāṇḍa jīvera tumi vāñchile nistāra  
of the universe  of the living beings  you  have desired  deliverance  
vinā pāpa-bhoge habe sabāra uddhāra  
without  suffering for sins  will be  of all  deliverance

(167) “You have desired the deliverance of all beings in the universe, so all will be delivered without suffering for their sins.

অসমর্থ নহে কৃষ্ণ, ধরে সর্ব্ব বল ৷  
তোমাকে বা কেনে ভুঞ্জাইবে পাপ-ফল ? ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

asamartha nahe kṛṣṇa, dhare sarva bala  
incapable  not  Kṛṣṇa  He holds  all  power  
tomāke vā kene bhuñjāibe pāpa-phala?  
you  then  why  would He make suffer  of sins  the fruits

(168) “Kṛṣṇa is not powerless; He holds all power. Why, then, would He make you suffer the fruits of sin?

তুমি যাঁর হিত বাঞ্ছ’, সে হৈল ‘বৈষ্ণব’ ৷  
বৈষ্ণবের পাপ কৃষ্ণ দূর করে সব ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

tumi yām̐ra hita vāñcha’, se haila ‘vaiṣṇava’  
you  whose  welfare  desire  he  has become  a Vaiṣṇava  
vaiṣṇavera pāpa kṛṣṇa dūra kare saba  
of a Vaiṣṇava  the sins  Kṛṣṇa  away  removes  all

(169) “Anyone whose welfare you desire has become a Vaiṣṇava, and Kṛṣṇa removes all the sins of a Vaiṣṇava.

যস্ত্বিন্দ্রগোপমথবেন্দ্রমহো স্বকর্ম্ম-  
বন্ধানুরূপফলভাজনমাতনোতি ৷  
কর্ম্মাণি নির্দহতি কিন্তু চ ভক্তিভাজাং  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ১৭০ ॥

yas tv indra-gopam athavendram aho sva-karma-  
who  but  the indragopa insect  or Indra  oh  of one’s own karma  
bandhānurūpa-phala-bhājanam ātanoti  
to the bondage befitting  of the fruits  the enjoyment  grants  
karmāṇi nirdahati kintu ca bhakti-bhājāṁ  
the karma  He burns up  but  indeed  of those who have devotion  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(170) “I worship Govinda, the original Person. To each, from the tiny *indragopa* insect to Indra himself, He grants the fruits that match the bondage of his own karma; but the karma of those who have devotion He burns away.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.54)[^46]

তোমার ইচ্ছা-মাত্রে হবে ব্রহ্মাণ্ড-মোচন ৷  
সর্ব্ব মুক্ত করিতে কৃষ্ণের নাহি পরিশ্রম ॥ ১৭১ ॥

tomāra icchā-mātre habe brahmāṇḍa-mocana  
your  by the mere wish  will be  the universe’s liberation  
sarva mukta karite kṛṣṇera nāhi pariśrama  
all  liberated  to make  for Kṛṣṇa  there is no  labour

(171) “By your mere wish the universe will be liberated. To liberate everyone is no labour for Kṛṣṇa.

এক উড়ুম্বর-বৃক্ষে লাগে কোটি-ফলে ৷  
কোটি যে ব্রহ্মাণ্ড ভাসে বিরজার জলে ॥ ১৭২ ॥

eka uḍumbara-vṛkṣe lāge koṭi-phale  
on one  uḍumbara tree  grow  millions of fruits  
koṭi ye brahmāṇḍa bhāse virajāra jale  
millions  which  universes  float  of the Virajā  on the waters

(172) “As millions of fruits grow on a single *uḍumbara* tree, so millions of universes float on the waters of the Virajā.

তার এক ফল পড়ি’ যদি নষ্ট হয় ৷  
তথাপি বৃক্ষ নাহি জানে নিজ-অপচয় ॥ ১৭৩ ॥

tāra eka phala paḍi’ yadi naṣṭa haya  
of it  one  fruit  falling  if  lost  becomes  
tathāpi vṛkṣa nāhi jāne nija-apacaya  
still  the tree  not  knows  its own loss

(173) “If one of its fruits falls and is lost, the tree does not even notice its loss.

তৈছে এক ব্রহ্মাণ্ড যদি মুক্ত হয় ৷  
তবু অল্পহানি কৃষ্ণের মনে নাহি লয় ॥ ১৭৪ ॥

taiche eka brahmāṇḍa yadi mukta haya  
so  one  universe  if  liberated  becomes  
tabu alpahāni kṛṣṇera mane nāhi laya  
still  a slight loss  of Kṛṣṇa  in the mind  not  is taken

(174) “So too, if one universe is liberated, Kṛṣṇa does not take it as even a slight loss.

অনন্ত ঐশ্বর্য্য কৃষ্ণের বৈকুণ্ঠাদি-ধাম ৷  
তার গড়খাই — কারণাব্ধি যার নাম ॥ ১৭৫ ॥

ananta aiśvarya kṛṣṇera vaikuṇṭhādi-dhāma  
infinite  opulence  of Kṛṣṇa  Vaikuṇṭha and the other abodes  
tāra gaḍa-khāi — kāraṇābdhi yāra nāma  
their  moat  the Causal Ocean  whose  name

(175) “Kṛṣṇa’s opulence is infinite — Vaikuṇṭha and His other abodes; and the moat around them is known as the Causal Ocean (*Kāraṇābdhi*).

তাতে ভাসে মায়া লঞা অনন্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ড ৷  
গড়খাইতে ভাসে যেন রাই-পূর্ণ ভাণ্ড ॥ ১৭৬ ॥

tāte bhāse māyā lañā ananta brahmāṇḍa  
in it  float  māyā  with  infinite  universes  
gaḍa-khāite bhāse yena rāi-pūrṇa bhāṇḍa  
in the moat  floats  as  with mustard seeds filled  a pot

(176) “On it the infinite universes float together with māyā, like a pot full of mustard seeds floating in a moat.

তার এক রাই-নাশে, হানি নাহি মানি ৷  
ঐছে এক অণ্ড-নাশে কৃষ্ণের নাহি হানি ॥ ১৭৭ ॥

tāra eka rāi-nāśe, hāni nāhi māni  
of it  one  mustard seed  in losing  loss  not  we count  
aiche eka aṇḍa-nāśe kṛṣṇera nāhi hāni  
so  one  universe  in losing  of Kṛṣṇa  there is no  loss

(177) “No one counts it a loss if one of those mustard seeds is lost; likewise, Kṛṣṇa loses nothing if one universe is lost.

সব ব্রহ্মাণ্ড সহ যদি ‘মায়া’র হয় ক্ষয় ৷  
তথাপি না মানে কৃষ্ণ কিছু অপচয় ॥ ১৭৮ ॥

saba brahmāṇḍa saha yadi ‘māyā’ra haya kṣaya  
all  universes  along with  if  of māyā  there is  destruction  
tathāpi nā māne kṛṣṇa kichu apacaya  
still  not  considers  Kṛṣṇa  any  loss

(178) “Even if māyā herself is destroyed along with all the universes, Kṛṣṇa does not consider it any loss.

কোটি-কামধেনু-পতির ছাগী যৈছে মরে ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যপতি কৃষ্ণের মায়া কিবা করে ? ॥ ১৭৯ ॥

koṭi-kāmadhenu-patira chāgī yaiche mare  
of millions of wish-fulfilling cows  of the owner  a she-goat  as  dies  
ṣaḍ-aiśvarya-pati kṛṣṇera māyā kibā kare?  
of the six opulences the Lord  of Kṛṣṇa  māyā  what  does

(179) “It is like the death of a she-goat for the owner of millions of wish-fulfilling cows. What can māyā mean to Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of the six opulences?

জয় জয় জহ্যজামজিত দোষগৃভীতগুণাং  
ত্বমসি যদাত্মনা সমবরুদ্ধসমস্তভগঃ ৷  
অগজগদোকসামখিলশক্ত্যববোধক তে  
ক্বচিদজয়াত্মনা চ চরতোঽনুচরেন্নিগমঃ ॥ ১৮০ ॥

jaya jaya jahy ajām ajita doṣa-gṛbhīta-guṇāṁ  
glory  glory  destroy  the unborn one  O unconquered  who for faults has taken up the qualities  
tvam asi yad ātmanā sam-avaruddha-samasta-bhagaḥ  
You  are  since  by Yourself  holding all excellences in full  
aga-jagad-okasām akhila-śakty-avabodhaka te  
of the unmoving and the moving beings  of all potencies  the awakener  of You  
kvacid ajayātmanā ca carato ’nucaren nigamaḥ  
sometimes  with māyā and with Your own potency  and  acting  describe  the Vedas

(180) “Glory, glory to You, O unconquered one! Destroy the unborn māyā, who has taken up the qualities to bring about faults, for in Yourself You hold all excellences in full. O awakener of all the potencies of moving and unmoving beings, the Vedas describe You sometimes as acting through māyā and sometimes through Your own potency.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.14)[^47]

এই মত সর্ব্বভক্তের কহি’ সব গুণ ৷  
সবারে বিদায় দিল করি’ আলিঙ্গন ॥ ১৮১ ॥

ei mata sarva-bhaktera kahi’ saba guṇa  
in this  way  of all the devotees  telling  all  qualities  
sabāre vidāya dila kari’ āliṅgana  
to all  farewell  He gave  making  embrace

(181) In this way the Lord spoke of the qualities of all the devotees. Then He embraced them all and bade them farewell.

প্রভুর বিচ্ছেদে ভক্ত করেন রোদন ৷  
ভক্তের বিচ্ছেদে প্রভুর বিষণ্ণ হৈল মন ॥ ১৮২ ॥

prabhura vicchede bhakta karena rodana  
of the Lord  in separation  the devotees  do  weeping  
bhaktera vicchede prabhura viṣaṇṇa haila mana  
of the devotees  in separation  of the Lord  sad  became  the mind

(182) The devotees wept at parting from the Lord, and the Lord’s heart grew sad at parting from His devotees.

গদাধর-পণ্ডিত রহিলা প্রভুর পাশে ৷  
যমেশ্বরে প্রভু যাঁরে করাইলা আবাসে ॥ ১৮৩ ॥

gadādhara-paṇḍita rahilā prabhura pāśe  
Gadādhara Paṇḍita  remained  of the Lord  beside  
yameśvare prabhu yām̐re karāilā āvāse  
at Yameśvara  the Lord  whom  had made  to dwell

(183) Gadādhara Paṇḍita, whom the Lord had settled at Yameśvara, remained near Him.[^48]

পুরী-গোসাঞি, জগদানন্দ, স্বরূপ-দামোদর ৷  
দামোদর-পণ্ডিত, আর গোবিন্দ, কাশীশ্বর ॥ ১৮৪ ॥

এইসব সঙ্গে প্রভু বৈসে নীলাচলে ৷  
জগন্নাথ-দরশন নিত্য করে প্রাতঃকালে ॥ ১৮৫ ॥

purī-gosāñi, jagadānanda, svarūpa-dāmodara  
Purī Gosāñi  Jagadānanda  Svarūpa Dāmodara  
dāmodara-paṇḍita, āra govinda, kāśīśvara  
Dāmodara Paṇḍita  and  Govinda  Kāśīśvara  
eisaba saṅge prabhu vaise nīlācale  
these all  with  the Lord  stays  at Nīlācala  
jagannātha-daraśana nitya kare prātaḥ-kāle  
Jagannātha’s sight  daily  takes  in the morning

(184–185) With Purī Gosāñi (Paramānanda Purī), Jagadānanda, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Dāmodara Paṇḍita, Govinda and Kāśīśvara, the Lord stayed at Nīlācala, and every morning He went to see Jagannātha.

প্রভু-পাশ আসি’ সার্ব্বভৌম এক দিন ৷  
যোড়হাত করি’ কিছু কৈল নিবেদন ॥ ১৮৬ ॥

prabhu-pāśa āsi’ sārvabhauma eka dina  
to the Lord  coming  Sārvabhauma  one  day  
yoḍa-hāta kari’ kichu kaila nivedana  
joined palms  making  something  made  petition

(186) One day Sārvabhauma came to the Lord and, with folded hands, made a request.

এবে সব বৈষ্ণব গৌড়দেশে চলি’ গেল ৷  
এবে প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণে অবসর হৈল ॥ ১৮৭ ॥

ebe saba vaiṣṇava gauḍa-deśe cali’ gela  
now  all  the Vaiṣṇavas  to the land of Gauḍa  having gone  departed  
ebe prabhura nimantraṇe avasara haila  
now  the Lord’s  for inviting  opportunity  has come

(187) “Now that all the Vaiṣṇavas have gone back to Gauḍa, there is an opportunity to invite You.

এবে মোর ঘরে ভিক্ষা করহ ‘মাস’ ভরি’ ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — ধর্ম্ম নহে, করিতে না পারি ॥ ১৮৮ ॥

ebe mora ghare bhikṣā karaha ‘māsa’ bhari’  
now  my  in the house  meals  take  a month  full  
prabhu kahe, — dharma nahe, karite nā pāri  
the Lord  says  dharma  it is not  to do  not  I can

(188) “Please take Your meals at my house for a whole month.” The Lord said, “That is not dharma; I cannot do it.”

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে, — ভিক্ষা করহ ‘বিশ’ দিন ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — এ নহে যতিধর্ম্ম-চিহ্ন ॥ ১৮৯ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe, — bhikṣā karaha ‘biśa’ dina  
Sārvabhauma  says  meals  take  twenty  days  
prabhu kahe, — e nahe yati-dharma-cihna  
the Lord  says  this  not  of a renunciant’s dharma  the mark

(189) Sārvabhauma said, “Then take Your meals with me for twenty days.” The Lord said, “That is not in keeping with a renunciant’s dharma.”

সার্ব্বভৌম কহে পুনঃ, — দিন ‘পঞ্চদশ’ ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — তোমার ভিক্ষা ‘এক’ দিবস ॥ ১৯০ ॥

sārvabhauma kahe punaḥ, — dina ‘pañca-daśa’  
Sārvabhauma  says  again  days  fifteen  
prabhu kahe, — tomāra bhikṣā ‘eka’ divasa  
the Lord  says  your  meal  one  day

(190) Sārvabhauma said again, “Then fifteen days.” The Lord said, “I shall take a meal with you for one day.”

তবে সার্ব্বভৌম প্রভুর চরণ ধরিয়া ৷  
‘দশদিন ভিক্ষা কর’ কহে বিনতি করিয়া ॥ ১৯১ ॥

tabe sārvabhauma prabhura caraṇa dhariyā  
then  Sārvabhauma  of the Lord  the feet  clasping  
‘daśadina bhikṣā kara’ kahe vinati kariyā  
“for ten days  meals  take”  says  entreaty  making

(191) Then Sārvabhauma clasped the Lord’s feet and entreated Him: “Take Your meals with me for ten days.”

প্রভু ক্রমে ক্রমে পাঁচ-দিন ঘাটাইল ৷  
পাঁচ-দিন তাঁর ভিক্ষা-নিমন্ত্রণ নিল ॥ ১৯২ ॥

prabhu krame krame pām̐ca-dina ghāṭāila  
the Lord  step  by step  to five days  reduced  
pām̐ca-dina tām̐ra bhikṣā-nimantraṇa nila  
for five days  his  invitation to meals  accepted

(192) Step by step the Lord brought it down to five days, and He accepted his invitation for five days.

তবে সার্ব্বভৌম করে আর নিবেদন ৷  
তোমার সঙ্গে সন্ন্যাসী আছে দশজন ॥ ১৯৩ ॥

tabe sārvabhauma kare āra nivedana  
then  Sārvabhauma  makes  another  petition  
tomāra saṅge sannyāsī āche daśajana  
You  with  sannyāsīs  there are  ten persons

(193) Then Sārvabhauma made another request: “Ten sannyāsīs are with You.

পুরী-গোসাঞির ভিক্ষা পাঁচদিন মোর ঘরে ৷  
পূর্ব্বে আমি কহিয়াছোঁ তোমার গোচরে ॥ ১৯৪ ॥

purī-gosāñira bhikṣā pām̐ca-dina mora ghare  
of Purī Gosāñi  the meals  five days  in my  house  
pūrve āmi kahiyāchom̐ tomāra gocare  
before  I  have told  Your  to notice

(194) “I have already told You that Purī Gosāñi will take his meals at my house for five days.

দামোদর-স্বরূপ, — এই বান্ধব আমার ৷  
কভু তোমার সঙ্গে যাবে কভু একেশ্বর ॥ ১৯৫ ॥

dāmodara-svarūpa, — ei bāndhava āmāra  
Dāmodara Svarūpa  this  friend  mine  
kabhu tomāra saṅge yābe kabhu ekeśvara  
sometimes  You  with  he will come  sometimes  alone

(195) “Svarūpa Dāmodara is my friend: he will come sometimes with You and sometimes alone.

আর অষ্ট সন্ন্যাসীর ভিক্ষা দুই দুই দিবসে ৷  
এক একদিন এক এক জনে পূর্ণ হৈল মাসে ॥ ১৯৬ ॥

āra aṣṭa sannyāsīra bhikṣā dui dui divase  
the other  eight  sannyāsīs’  meals  two  two  days  
eka ekadina eka eka jane pūrṇa haila māse  
one  a day  one  one  person  full  became  the month

(196) “The other eight sannyāsīs will take their meals with me for two days each, one person each day. In this way the month will be filled.

বহুত সন্ন্যাসী যদি আইসে এক ঠাঞি ৷  
সম্মান করিতে নারি, অপরাধ পাই ॥ ১৯৭ ॥

bahuta sannyāsī yadi āise eka ṭhāñi  
many  sannyāsīs  if  come  to one  place  
sammāna karite nāri, aparādha pāi  
honour  to give  I cannot  offence  I incur

(197) “If many sannyāsīs come at once, I will not be able to honour them properly and will commit an offence.

তুমিহ নিজ-ছায়ে আসিবে মোর ঘরে ৷  
কভু সঙ্গে আনিবে স্বরূপ-দামোদরে ॥ ১৯৮ ॥

tumiha nija-chāye āsibe mora ghare  
You Yourself  with Your own shadow  will come  to my  house  
kabhu saṅge ānibe svarūpa-dāmodare  
sometimes  with You  will bring  Svarūpa Dāmodara

(198) “Please come to my house alone, with only Your own shadow, and sometimes bring Svarūpa Dāmodara with You.”[^49]

প্রভুর ইঙ্গিত পাঞা আনন্দিত মন ৷  
সেই দিন মহাপ্রভুর কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১৯৯ ॥

prabhura iṅgita pāñā ānandita mana  
of the Lord  the sign  receiving  joyful  mind  
sei dina mahāprabhura kaila nimantraṇa  
that  day  Mahāprabhu  made  invitation

(199) Receiving the Lord’s sign of consent, Sārvabhauma was delighted, and that same day he invited Mahāprabhu.

‘ষাঠীর মাতা’ নাম, ভট্টাচার্য্যের গৃহিণী ৷  
প্রভুর মহাভক্ত তিঁহো, স্নেহেতে জননী ॥ ২০০ ॥

‘ṣāṭhīra mātā’ nāma, bhaṭṭācāryera gṛhiṇī  
“Ṣāṭhī’s mother”  by name  of the Bhaṭṭācārya  the wife  
prabhura mahā-bhakta tim̐ho, snehete jananī  
of the Lord  a great devotee  she  in affection  a mother

(200) The Bhaṭṭācārya’s wife, known as Ṣāṭhī’s mother, was a great devotee of the Lord and loved Him like a mother.

ঘরে আসি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য তাঁরে আজ্ঞা দিল ৷  
আনন্দে ষাঠীর মাতা পাক চড়াইল ॥ ২০১ ॥

ghare āsi’ bhaṭṭācārya tām̐re ājñā dila  
home  coming  the Bhaṭṭācārya  to her  order  gave  
ānande ṣāṭhīra mātā pāka caḍāila  
in joy  Ṣāṭhī’s  mother  cooking  set going

(201) When the Bhaṭṭācārya came home, he asked her to cook, and Ṣāṭhī’s mother joyfully set about it.

ভট্টাচার্য্যের গৃহে সব দ্রব্য আছে ভরি’ ৷  
যেবা শাকফলাদিক, আনিল আহরি’ ॥ ২০২ ॥

bhaṭṭācāryera gṛhe saba dravya āche bhari’  
of the Bhaṭṭācārya  in the house  all  provisions  are  in plenty  
yebā śāka-phalādika, ānila āhari’  
whatever  greens, fruits and so on  he brought  collecting

(202) His house was well stocked with every provision; whatever greens, fruits and other things were needed, he gathered and brought.

আপনি ভট্টাচার্য্য করে পাকের সব মর্ম্ম ৷  
ষাঠীর মাতা — বিচক্ষণা, জানে পাকের কর্ম্ম ॥ ২০৩ ॥

āpani bhaṭṭācārya kare pākera saba marma  
himself  the Bhaṭṭācārya  does  of the cooking  all  the essentials  
ṣāṭhīra mātā — vicakṣaṇā, jāne pāka karma  
Ṣāṭhī’s  mother  skilled  knows  of cooking  the work

(203) The Bhaṭṭācārya himself saw to all the finer points of the cooking, and Ṣāṭhī’s mother, skilled as she was, knew the whole craft of it.

পাকশালার দক্ষিণে — দুই ভোগালয় ৷  
এক-ঘরে শালগ্রামের ভোগ-সেবা হয় ॥ ২০৪ ॥

pāka-śālāra dakṣiṇe — dui bhogālaya  
of the kitchen  to the south  two  rooms for offering  
eka-ghare śālagrāmera bhoga-sevā haya  
in one room  of the Śālagrāma  the offering of food  is done

(204) To the south of the kitchen were two rooms for offering food. In one of them food was offered to Nārāyaṇa in His form as the Śālagrāma.

আর ঘর মহাপ্রভুর ভিক্ষার লাগিয়া ৷  
নিভৃতে করিয়াছে ভট্ট নূতন করিয়া ॥ ২০৫ ॥

āra ghara mahāprabhura bhikṣāra lāgiyā  
the other  room  of Mahāprabhu  the meal  for the sake of  
nibhṛte kariyāche bhaṭṭa nūtana kariyā  
in a secluded place  had made  Bhaṭṭa  newly  making

(205) The other room was for Mahāprabhu’s meals; the Bhaṭṭa had newly built it in a secluded spot.

বাহ্যে এক দ্বার তার, প্রভু প্রবেশিতে ৷  
পাকশালার এক দ্বার অন্ন পরিবেশিতে ॥ ২০৬ ॥

bāhye eka dvāra tāra, prabhu praveśite  
outside  one  door  of it  for the Lord  to enter  
pāka-śālāra eka dvāra anna pariveśite  
of the kitchen  one  door  food  to serve

(206) It had one door to the outside, for the Lord to enter, and one door from the kitchen, for serving the food.

বত্তিশা-আঠিয়া কলার আঙ্গটিয়া পাতে ৷  
তিন-মান তণ্ডুলের উভারিল ভাতে ॥ ২০৭ ॥

battiśā-āṭhiyā kalāra āṅgaṭiyā pāte  
of the battiśā-āṭhiyā  banana  whole  on leaves  
tina-māna taṇḍulera ubhārila bhāte  
three mānas  of rice  heaped out  cooked rice

(207) On a whole leaf of the *battiśā-āṭhiyā* banana they heaped rice cooked from three *mānas* of grain.[^50]

পীত-সুগন্ধি-ঘৃতে অন্ন সিক্ত কৈল ৷  
চারিদিকে পাতে ঘৃত বহিয়া চলিল ॥ ২০৮ ॥

pīta-sugandhi-ghṛte anna sikta kaila  
with yellow fragrant ghee  the rice  soaked  made  
cāri-dike pāte ghṛta vahiyā calila  
on four sides  on the leaf  the ghee  flowing  went

(208) The rice was soaked with fragrant yellow ghee, which ran over the leaf on all sides.

কেয়াপত্র-কলাখোলা-ডোঙ্গা সারি সারি ৷  
চারিদিকে ধরিয়াছে নানা ব্যঞ্জন ভরি’ ॥ ২০৯ ॥

keyāpatra-kalākholā-ḍoṅgā sāri sāri  
keyā-leaf and banana-bark cups  row  upon row  
cāri-dike dhariyāche nānā vyañjana bhari’  
on four sides  they placed  various  vegetables  filling

(209) All around it, row upon row, cups of *keyā* leaves and banana bark were set out, filled with various vegetable dishes.

দশপ্রকার শাক, নিম্ব-তিক্ত-সুকুত-ঝোল ৷  
মরিচের ঝাল, ছানাবড়া, বড়া, ঘোল ॥ ২১০ ॥

daśaprakāra śāka, nimba-tikta-sukuta-jhola  
ten kinds of  greens  bitter-with-nim sukuta soup  
maricera jhāla, chānā-baḍā, baḍā, ghola  
of pepper  the jhāla  chānā baḍās  baḍās  buttermilk

(210) There were ten kinds of greens, a bitter *sukuta* soup with *nim*, a peppery *jhāla*, *chānā-baḍās*, *baḍās* and buttermilk.

দুগ্ধতুম্বী, দুগ্ধ কুষ্মাণ্ড, বেসর, লাফ্রা ৷  
মোচাঘণ্ট, মোচাভাজা, বিবিধ শাক্রা ॥ ২১১ ॥

dugdha-tumbī, dugdha kuṣmāṇḍa, vesara, lāphrā  
bottle gourd in milk  in milk  pumpkin  vesara  lāphrā  
mocā-ghaṇṭa, mocā-bhājā, vividha śākrā  
banana-flower ghaṇṭa  fried banana flowers  various  śākrā

(211) There was bottle gourd and pumpkin cooked in milk, *vesara* and *lāphrā*, banana flowers made into a *ghaṇṭa* and fried, and various kinds of *śākrā*.[^51]

বৃদ্ধ কুষ্মাণ্ডবড়ীর ব্যঞ্জন অপার ৷  
ফুলবড়ী-ফল-মূল বিবিধ প্রকার ॥ ২১২ ॥

vṛddha kuṣmāṇḍa-baḍīra vyañjana apāra  
of ripe  pumpkin baḍīs  dishes  countless  
phulabaḍī-phala-mūla vividha prakāra  
phula-baḍīs, fruits and roots  various  kinds

(212) There were countless dishes of *baḍīs* made with ripe pumpkin, and *phula-baḍīs*, fruits and roots of many kinds.

নব-নিম্বপত্র-সহ ভৃষ্ট-বার্ত্তাকী ৷  
ফুলবড়ী, পটোল-ভাজা, কুষ্মাণ্ড মান-চাকী ॥ ২১৩ ॥

nava-nimbapatra-saha bhṛṣṭa-vārtākī  
with tender nim leaves  fried eggplant  
phula-baḍī, paṭola-bhājā, kuṣmāṇḍa māna-cākī  
phula-baḍī  fried paṭola  pumpkin  mānacāki

(213) There was eggplant fried with tender *nim* leaves, *phula-baḍī*, fried *paṭola*, and pumpkin and *māna-kacu* cut in rounds and fried.[^52]

ভৃষ্ট-মাষ-মুদ্গ-সূপ অমৃত নিন্দয় ৷  
মধুরাম্ল, বড়াম্লাদি অম্ল পাঁচ ছয় ॥ ২১৪ ॥

bhṛṣṭa-māṣa-mudga-sūpa amṛta nindaya  
of fried urad and mung  soup  nectar  puts to shame  
madhurāmla, baḍāmlādi amla pām̐ca chaya  
sweet-and-sour  baḍāmla and others  sour dishes  five  six

(214) There was a soup of fried urad and mung dāl that put nectar to shame, a sweet-and-sour chutney, *baḍāmla* and other sour dishes — five or six of them.[^53]

মুদ্গবড়া, মাষবড়া, কলাবড়া মিষ্ট ৷  
ক্ষীরপুলি, নারিকেলপুলী, আর যত পিষ্ট ॥ ২১৫ ॥

mudga-baḍā, māṣa-baḍā, kalā-baḍā miṣṭa  
mung baḍās  urad baḍās  banana baḍās  sweet  
kṣīra-puli, nārikela-pulī, āra yata piṣṭa  
kṣīra-puli  coconut pulī  and  all  cakes

(215) There were *baḍās* of mung and of urad, sweet banana *baḍās*, *kṣīra-puli*, coconut *pulī* and every kind of cake.

কাঁজিবড়া, দুগ্ধ-চিড়া, দুগ্ধ-লক্লকী ৷  
আর যত পিঠা কৈল, কহিতে না শকি ॥ ২১৬ ॥

kām̐ji-baḍā, dugdha-ciḍā, dugdha-laklakī  
kā̃ji baḍās  flat rice in milk  milk laklakī  
āra yata piṭhā kaila, kahite nā śaki  
and  as many  cakes  they made  to tell  not  I can

(216) There were *kā̃ji-baḍās*, flat rice in milk and milk *laklakī*. I cannot describe all the cakes that were made.[^54]

ঘৃত সিক্ত পরমান্ন, মৃৎকুণ্ডিকা ভরি’ ৷  
চাঁপাকলা-ঘনদুগ্ধ-আম্র তাহা ধরি ॥ ২১৭ ॥

ghṛta sikta paramānna, mṛt-kuṇḍikā bhari’  
with ghee  soaked  sweet rice  earthen pots  filling  
cām̐pākalā-ghanadugdha-āmra tāhā dhari  
cā̃pā bananas, thick milk and mangoes  in it  placing

(217) Sweet rice soaked with ghee filled earthen pots, with *cā̃pā* bananas, thickened milk and mangoes set in it.

রসালা-মথিত দধি, সন্দেশ অপার ৷  
গৌড়ে উৎকলে যত ভক্ষ্যের প্রকার ॥ ২১৮ ॥

rasālā-mathita dadhi, sandeśa apāra  
rasālā  churned  yogurt  sandeśa  countless  
gauḍe utkale yata bhakṣyera prakāra  
in Gauḍa  in Utkala  as many  of foods  kinds

(218) There was *rasālā*, churned yogurt and countless *sandeśas* — every kind of food known in Gauḍa and Utkala.

শ্রদ্ধা করি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য সব করাইল ৷  
শুভ্র-পীঠোপরি সূক্ষ্ম বসন পাতিল ॥ ২১৯ ॥

śraddhā kari’ bhaṭṭācārya saba karāila  
with faith  making  the Bhaṭṭācārya  all  had made  
śubhra-pīṭhopari sūkṣma vasana pātila  
on a white seat  fine  cloth  spread

(219) The Bhaṭṭācārya had all this prepared with faith. On a white wooden seat he spread a fine cloth.[^55]

দুই-পাশে সুগন্ধি শীতল জল-ঝারী ৷  
অন্ন-ব্যঞ্জনোপরি দিল তুলসী-মঞ্জরী ॥ ২২০ ॥

dui-pāśe sugandhi śītala jala-jhārī  
on two sides  fragrant  cool  water pitchers  
anna-vyañjanopari dila tulasī-mañjarī  
over the rice and vegetables  placed  tulasī buds

(220) On either side stood pitchers of cool, fragrant water, and he placed tulasī buds on the rice and vegetables.

অমৃত-গুটিকা, পিঠা-পানা আনাইল ৷  
জগন্নাথ-প্রসাদ সব পৃথক্ ধরিল ॥ ২২১ ॥

amṛta-guṭikā, piṭhā-pānā ānāila  
amṛta-guṭikā  cakes and sweet drinks  had brought  
jagannātha-prasāda saba pṛthak dharila  
Jagannātha’s prasāda  all  separately  placed

(221) He also had *amṛta-guṭikā*, cakes and sweet drinks brought, and he kept all of Jagannātha’s *prasāda* apart.[^56]

হেনকালে মহাপ্রভু মধ্যাহ্ন করিয়া ৷  
একলে আইল তাঁর হৃদয় জানিয়া ॥ ২২২ ॥

hena-kāle mahāprabhu madhyāhna kariyā  
at that time  Mahāprabhu  the midday duties  finishing  
ekale āila tām̐ra hṛdaya jāniyā  
alone  came  his  heart  knowing

(222) At that time Mahāprabhu, having finished His midday duties, came alone, knowing the Bhaṭṭācārya’s heart.

ভট্টাচার্য্য কৈল তবে পাদ প্রক্ষালন ৷  
ঘরের ভিতরে গেলা করিতে ভোজন ॥ ২২৩ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya kaila tabe pāda prakṣālana  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  did  then  of the feet  washing  
gharera bhitare gelā karite bhojana  
of the room  inside  went  to take  the meal

(223) The Bhaṭṭācārya then washed His feet, and the Lord went into the room to take His meal.

অন্নাদি দেখিয়া প্রভু বিস্মিত হঞা ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যে কহে কিছু ভঙ্গি করিয়া ॥ ২২৪ ॥

annādi dekhiyā prabhu vismita hañā  
the rice and the rest  seeing  the Lord  astonished  being  
bhaṭṭācārye kahe kichu bhaṅgi kariyā  
to the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  something  a gesture  making

(224) Seeing the rice and all the dishes, the Lord was astonished, and with a playful gesture He said to the Bhaṭṭācārya:

অলৌকিক এই সব অন্ন-ব্যঞ্জন ৷  
দুই প্রহর ভিতরে কৈছে হইল রন্ধন ॥ ২২৫ ॥

alaukika ei saba anna-vyañjana  
extraordinary  these  all  rice and vegetables  
dui prahara bhitare kaiche ha-ila randhana  
two  praharas  within  how  was done  the cooking

(225) “All this rice and these dishes are extraordinary! How could they have been cooked within two *praharas*?

শত চুলায় শত জন পাক যদি করে ৷  
তবু শীঘ্র এত দ্রব্য রান্ধিতে না পারে ॥ ২২৬ ॥

śata culāya śata jana pāka yadi kare  
a hundred  on stoves  a hundred  people  cooking  if  do  
tabu śīghra eta dravya rāndhite nā pāre  
still  quickly  so much  food  to cook  not  can

(226) “Even if a hundred people cooked on a hundred stoves, they could not cook so much food so quickly.

কৃষ্ণের ভোগ লাগাঞাছ, — অনুমান করি ৷  
উপরে দেখিয়ে যাতে তুলসী-মঞ্জরী ॥ ২২৭ ॥

kṛṣṇera bhoga lāgāñācha, — anumāna kari  
to Kṛṣṇa  the food  you have offered  a guess  I make  
upare dekhiye yāte tulasī-mañjarī  
on top  I see  since  tulasī buds

(227) “I take it that you have offered this food to Kṛṣṇa, since I see tulasī buds on top of it.

ভাগ্যবান্ তুমি, সফল তোমার উদ্যোগ ৷  
রাধাকৃষ্ণে লাগাঞাছ এতাদৃশ ভোগ ॥ ২২৮ ॥

bhāgyavān tumi, saphala tomāra udyoga  
fortunate  you  successful  your  effort  
rādhā-kṛṣṇe lāgāñācha etādṛśa bhoga  
to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa  you have offered  such  a feast

(228) “You are fortunate, and your effort has borne fruit: you have offered such a feast to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

অন্নের সৌরভ্য, বর্ণ — অতি মনোরম ৷  
রাধাকৃষ্ণ সাক্ষাৎ ইঁহা করিয়াছেন ভোজন ॥ ২২৯ ॥

annera saurabhya, varṇa — ati manorama  
of the rice  the fragrance  the colour  very  charming  
rādhā-kṛṣṇa sākṣāt im̐hā kariyāchena bhojana  
Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa  in person  this  have done  eating

(229) “The fragrance and colour of the rice are most charming. Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa Themselves have eaten of it.

তোমার বহুত ভাগ্য কত প্রশংসিব ৷  
আমি — ভাগ্যবান্, ইহার অবশেষ পাব ॥ ২৩০ ॥

tomāra bahuta bhāgya kata praśaṁsiba  
your  great  fortune  how much  shall I praise  
āmi — bhāgyavān, ihāra avaśeṣa pāba  
I  am fortunate  of it  the remnants  I shall get

(230) “How can I praise your great fortune enough? I too am fortunate, for I shall receive the remnants.

কৃষ্ণের আসন-পীঠ রাখ উঠাঞা ৷  
মোরে প্রসাদ দেহ’ ভিন্ন পাত্র করিয়া ॥ ২৩১ ॥

kṛṣṇera āsana-pīṭha rākha uṭhāñā  
Kṛṣṇa’s  seat  keep  removing  
more prasāda deha’ bhinna pātra kariyā  
to Me  prasāda  give  a separate  plate  making

(231) “Take away Kṛṣṇa’s seat and keep it aside, and give Me *prasāda* on a separate plate.”[^57]

ভট্টাচার্য্য বলে, — প্রভু, না করহ বিস্ময় ৷  
যেই খাবে, তাঁর শক্ত্যে ভোগ সিদ্ধ হয় ॥ ২৩২ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya bale, — prabhu, nā karaha vismaya  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  O Lord  not  make  wonder  
yei khābe, tām̐ra śaktye bhoga siddha haya  
who  will eat  by His  potency  the feast  accomplished  becomes

(232) The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “O Lord, do not be astonished. By the potency of Him who will eat it, the feast was made ready.

উদ্যোগ না ছিল মোর গৃহিণীর রন্ধনে ৷  
যাঁর শক্ত্যে ভোগ সিদ্ধ, সেই তাহা জানে ॥ ২৩৩ ॥

udyoga nā chila mora gṛhiṇīra randhane  
effort  not  there was  my  wife’s  in the cooking  
yām̐ra śaktye bhoga siddha, sei tāhā jāne  
by whose  potency  the feast  accomplished  He  that  knows

(233) “My wife did not have to labour at the cooking. He by whose potency the feast was made ready knows how it was done.

এইত আসনে বসি’ করহ ভোজন ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — পূজ্য এই কৃষ্ণের আসন ॥ ২৩৪ ॥

eita āsane vasi’ karaha bhojana  
on this very  seat  sitting  take  the meal  
prabhu kahe, — pūjya ei kṛṣṇera āsana  
the Lord  says  worshipable  this  Kṛṣṇa’s  seat

(234) “Please sit on this very seat and take Your meal.” The Lord said, “This seat of Kṛṣṇa’s is to be worshipped.”

ভট্ট কহে, — অন্ন, পীঠ, — সমান প্রসাদ ৷  
অন্ন খাবে, পীঠে বসিতে কাঁহা অপরাধ ? ॥ ২৩৫ ॥

bhaṭṭa kahe, — anna, pīṭha, — samāna prasāda  
Bhaṭṭa  says  the food  the seat  equally  prasāda  
anna khābe, pīṭhe vasite kām̐hā aparādha?  
the food  You will eat  on the seat  to sit  where  offence

(235) Bhaṭṭa said, “The food and the seat are equally *prasāda*. If You will eat the food, what offence can there be in sitting on the seat?”

প্রভু কহে, — ভাল কৈলে, শাস্ত্র-আজ্ঞা হয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের সকল শেষ ভৃত্য আস্বাদয় ॥ ২৩৬ ॥

prabhu kahe, — bhāla kaile, śāstra-ājñā haya  
the Lord  says  well  you have done  scripture’s  command  it is  
kṛṣṇera sakala śeṣa bhṛtya āsvādaya  
Kṛṣṇa’s  all  remnants  the servant  relishes

(236) The Lord said, “You are right; this is the command of scripture: the servant relishes all that Kṛṣṇa leaves.

ত্বয়োপযুক্তস্রগ্গন্ধবাসোঽলঙ্কারচর্চ্চিতাঃ ৷  
উচ্ছিষ্টভোজিনো দাসাস্তব মায়াং জয়েম হি ॥ ২৩৭ ॥

tvayopayukta-srag-gandha-  
by You used  garlands  scents  
vāso ’laṅkāra-carcitāḥ  
clothes  with ornaments  adorned  
ucchiṣṭa-bhojino dāsās  
remnants  eating  servants  
tava māyāṁ jayema hi  
Your  māyā  shall conquer  surely

(237) “ ‘Adorned with the garlands, scents, clothes and ornaments You have used, and eating Your remnants, we, Your servants, shall surely conquer Your māyā.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.6.46)[^58]

তথাপি এতেক অন্ন খাওন না যায় ৷  
ভট্ট কহে, — জানি, খাও, যতেক যুয়ায় ॥ ২৩৮ ॥

tathāpi eteka anna khāona nā yāya  
still  so much  food  to eat  not  possible  
bhaṭṭa kahe, — jāni, khāo, yateka yuyāya  
Bhaṭṭa  says  I know  eat  as much as  befits

(238) “Still, no one can eat so much food.” Bhaṭṭa said, “I know how much You eat. Eat as much as befits You.

নীলাচলে ভোজন তুমি কর বায়ান্ন বার ৷  
এক এক ভোগের অন্ন শত শত ভার ॥ ২৩৯ ॥

nīlācale bhojana tumi kara bāyānna bāra  
at Nīlācala  meals  You  take  fifty-two  times  
eka eka bhogera anna śata śata bhāra  
each  each  offering’s  food  hundreds  hundreds  loads

(239) “At Nīlācala You eat fifty-two times, and the food of each offering comes in hundreds upon hundreds of loads.

দ্বারকাতে ষোল-সহস্র মহিষীমন্দিরে ৷  
অষ্টাদশ মাতা, আর যাদবের ঘরে ॥ ২৪০ ॥

dvārakāte ṣola-sahasra mahiṣī-mandire  
in Dvārakā  sixteen thousand  in the queens’ palaces  
aṣṭādaśa mātā, āra yādavera ghare  
eighteen  mothers  and  of the Yādavas  in the houses

(240) “In Dvārakā You eat in the palaces of the sixteen thousand queens, in the homes of Your eighteen mothers and in the houses of the Yādavas.[^59]

ব্রজে জ্যেঠা, খুড়া, মামা, পিসাদি গোপগণ ৷  
সখাবৃন্দ সবার ঘরে দ্বিসন্ধ্যা-ভোজন ॥ ২৪১ ॥

vraje jyeṭhā, khuḍā, māmā, pisādi gopa-gaṇa  
in Vraja  elder uncles  younger uncles  maternal uncles  aunts’ husbands and other  cowherds  
sakhā-vṛnda sabāra ghare dvisandhyā-bhojana  
friends  of all  in the houses  meals at both twilights

(241) “In Vraja You eat twice a day, morning and evening, in the houses of all the cowherds — Your uncles on Your father’s and mother’s side, the husbands of Your aunts and others — and of all Your friends.[^60]

গোবর্দ্ধন-যজ্ঞে অন্ন খাইলা রাশি রাশি ৷  
তার লেখায় এই অন্ন নহে এক গ্রাসী ॥ ২৪২ ॥

govarddhana-yajñe anna khāilā rāśi rāśi  
at the Govardhana sacrifice  food  You ate  heaps  upon heaps  
tāra lekhāya ei anna nahe eka grāsī  
of that  in comparison  this  food  is not  one  mouthful

(242) “At the Govardhana sacrifice You ate heaps upon heaps of food. Compared with that, this food is not even one mouthful.

তুমি ত’ ঈশ্বর, মুঞি — ক্ষুদ্র জীব ছার ৷  
এক-গ্রাস মাধুকরী করহ অঙ্গীকার ॥ ২৪৩ ॥

tumi ta’ īśvara, muñi — kṣudra jīva chāra  
You  indeed  the Lord  I  a tiny  soul  wretched  
eka-grāsa mādhukarī karaha aṅgīkāra  
one mouthful  of mādhukarī  make  acceptance

(243) “You are the Lord Himself, and I am a tiny, wretched soul. Please accept this one mouthful of *mādhukarī*.”[^61]

এত শুনি’ হাসি’ প্রভু বসিলা ভোজনে ৷  
জগন্নাথের প্রসাদ ভট্ট দেন হর্ষ মনে ॥ ২৪৪ ॥

eta śuni’ hāsi’ prabhu vasilā bhojane  
this  hearing  smiling  the Lord  sat  to the meal  
jagannāthera prasāda bhaṭṭa dena harṣa mane  
of Jagannātha  the prasāda  Bhaṭṭa  gives  with joyful  mind

(244) Hearing this, the Lord smiled and sat down to eat, and Bhaṭṭa joyfully served Him Jagannātha’s *prasāda*.

হেনকালে ‘অমোঘ’, — ভট্টাচার্য্যের জামাতা ৷  
কুলীন, নিন্দক তিঁহো ষাঠী-কন্যার ভর্ত্তা ॥ ২৪৫ ॥

hena-kāle ‘amogha’, — bhaṭṭācāryera jāmātā  
at that time  Amogha  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  son-in-law  
kulīna, nindaka tim̐ho ṣāṭhī-kanyāra bhartā  
of a noble family  a blasphemer  he  of Ṣāṭhī the daughter  the husband

(245) At that time Amogha, the Bhaṭṭācārya’s son-in-law and husband of his daughter Ṣāṭhī, happened to be there. He came of a noble (*kulīna*) family, but he was a blasphemer.

ভোজন দেখিতে চাহে, আসিতে না পারে ৷  
লাঠি-হাতে ভট্টাচার্য্য আছেন দুয়ারে ॥ ২৪৬ ॥

bhojana dekhite cāhe, āsite nā pāre  
the meal  to see  he wants  to come in  not  he can  
lāṭhi-hāte bhaṭṭācārya āchena duyāre  
stick in hand  the Bhaṭṭācārya  is  at the door

(246) He wanted to watch the Lord eat but could not come in, for the Bhaṭṭācārya stood at the door, stick in hand.

তিঁহো যদি প্রসাদ দিতে হৈলা আন-মন ৷  
অমোঘ আসি’ অন্ন দেখি’ করয়ে নিন্দন ॥ ২৪৭ ॥

tim̐ho yadi prasāda dite hailā āna-mana  
he  when  prasāda  in serving  became  distracted  
amogha āsi’ anna dekhi’ karaye nindana  
Amogha  coming in  the food  seeing  makes  blasphemy

(247) But when the Bhaṭṭācārya’s attention turned to serving the *prasāda*, Amogha came in, saw the food and began to blaspheme:

এই অন্নে তৃপ্ত হয় দশ বার জন ৷  
একেলা সন্ন্যাসী করে এতেক ভক্ষণ ॥ ২৪৮ ॥

ei anne tṛpta haya daśa bāra jana  
with this  food  satisfied  become  ten  twelve  people  
ekelā sannyāsī kare eteka bhakṣaṇa  
alone  a sannyāsī  does  so much  eating

(248) “This food would satisfy ten or twelve people, and one sannyāsī eats so much all by himself!”

শুনি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য তবে উলটি’ চাহিল ৷  
তাঁর অবধান দেখি’ অমোঘ পলাইল ॥ ২৪৯ ॥

śuni’ bhaṭṭācārya tabe ulaṭi’ cāhila  
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya  then  turning round  looked  
tām̐ra avadhāna dekhi’ amogha palāila  
his  attention  seeing  Amogha  fled

(249) When the Bhaṭṭācārya heard this, he turned round and looked, and Amogha, seeing that he had been noticed, fled.

ভট্টাচার্য্য লাঠি লঞা মারিতে ধাইল ৷  
পলাইল অমোঘ, তার লাগ না পাইল ॥ ২৫০ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya lāṭhi lañā mārite dhāila  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  the stick  taking  to beat  ran  
palāila amogha, tāra lāga nā pāila  
fled  Amogha  him  catch  not  could

(250) The Bhaṭṭācārya took up his stick and ran to beat him, but Amogha got away and he could not catch him.

তবে গালি, শাপ দিতে ভট্টাচার্য্য আইলা ৷  
নিন্দা শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু হাসিতে লাগিলা ॥ ২৫১ ॥

tabe gāli, śāpa dite bhaṭṭācārya āilā  
then  abuse  curses  hurling  the Bhaṭṭācārya  came back  
nindā śuni’ mahāprabhu hāsite lāgilā  
the blasphemy  hearing  Mahāprabhu  to laugh  began

(251) Then the Bhaṭṭācārya came back, cursing and abusing him, and when Mahāprabhu heard all this reviling, He began to laugh.

শুনি’ ষাঠীর মাতা শিরে-বুকে ঘাত মারে ৷  
‘ষাঠী রাণ্ডী হউক’ — ইহা বলে বারে বারে ॥ ২৫২ ॥

śuni’ ṣāṭhīra mātā śire-buke ghāta māre  
hearing  Ṣāṭhī’s  mother  on head and breast  blows  strikes  
‘ṣāṭhī rāṇḍī hauka’ — ihā bale bāre bāre  
“may Ṣāṭhī  a widow  become”  this  she says  again  and again

(252) Hearing of it, Ṣāṭhī’s mother beat her head and breast and cried again and again, “Let Ṣāṭhī become a widow!”

দুঁহার দুঃখ দেখি’ প্রভু দুঁহা প্রবোধিয়া ৷  
দুঁহার ইচ্ছাতে ভোজন কৈল তুষ্ট হঞা ॥ ২৫৩ ॥

dum̐hāra duḥkha dekhi’ prabhu dum̐hā prabodhiyā  
of both  the grief  seeing  the Lord  both  consoling  
dum̐hāra icchāte bhojana kaila tuṣṭa hañā  
of both  by the wish  meal  took  pleased  being

(253) Seeing the grief of them both, the Lord consoled them, and to fulfil their wish He ate, well pleased.

আচমন করাঞা ভট্ট দিল মুখবাস ৷  
তুলসী-মঞ্জরী, লবঙ্গ, এলাচি, রসবাস ॥ ২৫৪ ॥

ācamana karāñā bhaṭṭa dila mukha-vāsa  
ācamana  having Him do  Bhaṭṭa  gave  mouth fresheners  
tulasī-mañjarī, lavaṅga, elāci, rasa-vāsa  
tulasī buds  cloves  cardamom  juicy and fragrant

(254) When the Lord had rinsed His mouth, Bhaṭṭa gave Him mouth fresheners: tulasī buds, cloves and juicy, fragrant cardamom.[^62]

সর্ব্বাঙ্গে লেপিল প্রভুর সুগন্ধি চন্দন ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ হঞা বলে সদৈন্য বচন ॥ ২৫৫ ॥

sarvāṅge lepila prabhura sugandhi candana  
over all the limbs  smeared  of the Lord  fragrant  sandalwood  
daṇḍavat hañā bale sadainya vacana  
prostrate  becoming  says  humble  words

(255) He smeared fragrant sandalwood paste all over the Lord’s body and, falling prostrate, spoke humbly:

নিন্দা করাইতে তোমা আনিনু নিজ-ঘরে ৷  
এই অপরাধ, প্রভু, ক্ষমা কর মোরে ॥ ২৫৬ ॥

nindā karāite tomā āninu nija-ghare  
blasphemy  to have done  You  I brought  to my own house  
ei aparādha, prabhu, kṣamā kara more  
this  offence  O Lord  pardon  make  me

(256) “I brought You to my house only to have You blasphemed. O Lord, forgive me this offence.”

প্রভু কহে, — নিন্দা নহে, ‘সহজ’ কহিল ৷  
ইহাতে তোমার তার কি অপরাধ হৈল ? ॥ ২৫৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — nindā nahe, ‘sahaja’ kahila  
the Lord  says  blasphemy  it is not  the plain truth  he spoke  
ihāte tomāra tāra ki aparādha haila?  
in this  your  his  what  offence  has been

(257) The Lord said, “That was not blasphemy; he only spoke plainly. What offence is there in this, either on your part or on his?”

এত বলি’ মহাপ্রভু চলিলা ভবনে ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্য তাঁর ঘরে গেলা তাঁর সনে ॥ ২৫৮ ॥

eta bali’ mahāprabhu calilā bhavane  
this  saying  Mahāprabhu  went  to His dwelling  
bhaṭṭācārya tām̐ra ghare gelā tām̐ra sane  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  to His  house  went  Him  with

(258) With these words Mahāprabhu went home, and the Bhaṭṭācārya went with Him to His residence.

প্রভু-পদে পড়ি’ বহু আত্মনিন্দা কৈল ৷  
তাঁরে শান্ত করি’ প্রভু ঘরে পাঠাইল ॥ ২৫৯ ॥

prabhu-pade paḍi’ bahu ātma-nindā kaila  
at the Lord’s feet  falling  much  self-reproach  made  
tām̐re śānta kari’ prabhu ghare pāṭhāila  
him  calm  making  the Lord  home  sent

(259) Falling at the Lord’s feet, he reproached himself bitterly. The Lord calmed him and sent him home.

ঘরে আসি’ ভট্টাচার্য্য, ষাঠীর মাতা-সনে ৷  
আপনা নিন্দিয়া কিছু বলেন বচনে ॥ ২৬০ ॥

ghare āsi’ bhaṭṭācārya, ṣāṭhīra mātā-sane  
home  coming  the Bhaṭṭācārya  with Ṣāṭhī’s mother  
āpanā nindiyā kichu balena vacane  
himself  reproaching  some  says  words

(260) Returning home, the Bhaṭṭācārya spoke to Ṣāṭhī’s mother, blaming himself:

চৈতন্য-গোসাঞির নিন্দা শুনি যাহা হৈতে ৷  
তারে বধ কৈলে হয় পাপ-প্রায়শ্চিত্তে ॥ ২৬১ ॥

caitanya-gosāñira nindā śuni yāhā haite  
of Caitanya Gosāñi  blasphemy  I hear  whom  from  
tāre vadha kaile haya pāpa-prāyaścitte  
him  killing  if done  there is  for the sin  atonement

(261) “To kill the man from whom I have heard blasphemy of Caitanya Gosāñi would be the atonement for that sin.[^63]

কিম্বা নিজ-প্রাণ যদি করি বিমোচন ৷  
দুই যোগ্য নহে, দুই — শরীর ব্রাহ্মণ ॥ ২৬২ ॥

kimbā nija-prāṇa yadi kari vimocana  
or  my own life  if  I make  giving up  
dui yogya nahe, dui — śarīra brāhmaṇa  
both  fitting  not  both  bodies  brāhmaṇa

(262) “Or I could give up my own life. But neither is proper, for in body we are both brāhmaṇas.[^64]

পুনঃ সেই নিন্দকের মুখ না দেখিব ৷  
পরিত্যাগ কৈলুঁ, তার নাম না লইব ॥ ২৬৩ ॥

punaḥ sei nindakera mukha nā dekhiba  
again  that  blasphemer’s  face  not  I shall see  
parityāga kailum̐, tāra nāma nā laiba  
renunciation  I have made  his  name  not  I shall take

(263) “I shall never look on that blasphemer’s face again. I have renounced him, and I shall not speak his name.

ষাঠীরে কহ — তারে ছাড়ুক, সে হইল ‘পতিত’ ৷  
‘পতিত’ হইলে ভর্ত্তা, ত্যজিতে উচিত ॥ ২৬৪ ॥

ṣāṭhīre kaha — tāre chāḍuka, se haila ‘patita’  
to Ṣāṭhī  say  him  let her leave  he  has become  fallen  
‘patita’ haile bhartā, tyajite ucita  
fallen  if becomes  a husband  to give up  proper

(264) “Tell Ṣāṭhī to leave him: he has fallen (*patita*). When a husband has fallen, it is proper to give him up.

“পতিঞ্চ পতিতং ত্যজেৎ” ॥ ২৬৫ ॥

“patiṁ ca patitaṁ tyajet”  
a husband  and  fallen  one should give up

(265) “ ‘One should give up a fallen husband.’ ”[^65]

সেই রাত্রে অমোঘ কাঁহা পলাঞা রহিল ৷  
প্রাতঃকালে তার বিসূচিকা-ব্যাধি হৈল ॥ ২৬৬ ॥

sei rātre amogha kām̐hā palāñā rahila  
that  night  Amogha  somewhere  having fled  stayed  
prātaḥ-kāle tāra visūcikā-vyādhi haila  
in the morning  his  cholera  occurred

(266) That night Amogha fled and hid somewhere, and in the morning he was stricken with cholera.

অমোঘ মরেন — শুনি’ কহে ভট্টাচার্য্য ৷  
সহায় হইয়া দৈব, কৈল মোর কার্য্য ॥ ২৬৭ ॥

amogha marena — śuni’ kahe bhaṭṭācārya  
Amogha  is dying  hearing  says  the Bhaṭṭācārya  
sahāya haiyā daiva, kaila mora kārya  
helper  becoming  providence  has done  my  task

(267) Hearing that Amogha was dying, the Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Providence has come to my aid and done my work for me.

ঈশ্বরে ত’ অপরাধ ফলে ততক্ষণ ৷  
এত বলি’ পড়ে দুই শাস্ত্রের বচন ॥ ২৬৮ ॥

īśvare ta’ aparādha phale tata-kṣaṇa  
against the Lord  indeed  an offence  bears fruit  at once  
eta bali’ paḍe dui śāstrera vacana  
this  saying  he recites  two  of scripture  sayings

(268) “An offence against the Lord bears fruit at once.” So saying, he recited two verses of scripture:

মহতা হি প্রয়ত্নেন হস্ত্যশ্বরথপত্তিভিঃ ৷  
অস্মাভির্যদনুষ্ঠেয়ং গন্ধর্ব্বৈস্তদনুষ্ঠিতম্ ॥ ২৬৯ ॥

mahatā hi prayatnena  
by great  indeed  effort  
hasty-aśva-ratha-pattibhiḥ  
with elephants, horses, chariots and foot soldiers  
asmābhir yad anuṣṭheyaṁ  
by us  what  had to be done  
gandharvais tad anuṣṭhitam  
by the Gandharvas  that  has been done

(269) “What we would have had to do with great effort, with elephants, horses, chariots and foot soldiers, the Gandharvas have done.” (Mahābhārata, Vana-parva, 241.15)[^66]

আয়ুঃ শ্রিয়ং যশো ধর্ম্মং লোকানাশিষ এব চ ৷  
হন্তি শ্রেয়াংসি সর্ব্বাণি পুংসো মহদতিক্রমঃ ॥ ২৭০ ॥

āyuḥ śriyaṁ yaśo dharmaṁ  
life  fortune  fame  dharma  
lokān āśiṣa eva ca  
the higher worlds  blessings  also  and  
hanti śreyāṁsi sarvāṇi  
destroys  benefits  all  
puṁso mahad-atikramaḥ  
of a man  offence against the great

(270) “Offence against great souls destroys a man’s life, fortune, fame and dharma, his higher worlds and blessings — all that is good for him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.4.46)[^67]

গোপীনাথাচার্য্য গেলা প্রভু-দরশনে ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে পুছিল ভট্টাচার্য্য-বিবরণে ॥ ২৭১ ॥

gopīnāthācārya gelā prabhu-daraśane  
Gopīnātha Ācārya  went  to see the Lord  
prabhu tām̐re puchila bhaṭṭācārya-vivaraṇe  
the Lord  him  asked  about the Bhaṭṭācārya’s affairs

(271) Gopīnātha Ācārya went to see the Lord, and the Lord asked him about the Bhaṭṭācārya.

আচার্য্য কহে, — উপবাস কৈল দুইজন ৷  
বিসূচিকা-ব্যাধিতে অমোঘ ছাড়িছে জীবন ॥ ২৭২ ॥

ācārya kahe, — upavāsa kaila dui-jana  
the Ācārya  says  fast  have kept  the two  
visūcikā-vyādhite amogha chāḍiche jīvana  
from cholera  Amogha  is leaving  life

(272) The Ācārya said, “The two of them are fasting, and Amogha is dying of cholera.”

শুনি’ কৃপাময় প্রভু আইলা ধাঞা ৷  
অমোঘেরে কহে তার বুকে হস্ত দিয়া ॥ ২৭৩ ॥

śuni’ kṛpāmaya prabhu āilā dhāñā  
hearing  merciful  the Lord  came  running  
amoghere kahe tāra buke hasta diyā  
to Amogha  says  his  on the chest  hand  placing

(273) Hearing this, the merciful Lord came running. Placing His hand on Amogha’s chest, He said:[^68]

সহজে নির্ম্মল এই ‘ব্রাহ্মণ’-হৃদয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের বসিতে এই যোগ্যস্থান হয় ॥ ২৭৪ ॥

sahaje nirmala ei ‘brāhmaṇa’-hṛdaya  
by nature  pure  this  brāhmaṇa’s heart  
kṛṣṇera vasite ei yogya-sthāna haya  
for Kṛṣṇa  to sit  this  a fit place  is

(274) “This heart of a brāhmaṇa is pure by nature; it is a fit place for Kṛṣṇa to sit.

‘মাৎসর্য্য’-চণ্ডাল কেনে ইঁহা বসাইলা ৷  
পরম পবিত্র স্থান অপবিত্র কৈলা ॥ ২৭৫ ॥

‘mātsarya’-caṇḍāla kene im̐hā vasāilā  
the caṇḍāla of envy  why  here  have you seated  
parama pavitra sthāna apavitra kailā  
supremely  pure  place  impure  you have made

(275) “Why have you seated the *caṇḍāla* of envy here? You have made this supremely pure place impure.

সার্ব্বভৌম-সঙ্গে তোমার ‘কলুষ’ হৈল ক্ষয় ৷  
‘কল্মষ’ ঘুচিলে জীব ‘কৃষ্ণনাম’ লয় ॥ ২৭৬ ॥

sārvabhauma-saṅge tomāra ‘kaluṣa’ haila kṣaya  
in Sārvabhauma’s company  your  impurity  has become  destroyed  
‘kalmaṣa’ ghucile jīva ‘kṛṣṇa-nāma’ laya  
the taint  when removed  the soul  Kṛṣṇa’s name  takes

(276) “Through Sārvabhauma’s company your impurity has been destroyed, and when the taint is gone, a soul takes the name of Kṛṣṇa.

উঠহ, অমোঘ, তুমি লও কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
অচিরে তোমারে কৃপা করিবে ভগবান্ ॥ ২৭৭ ॥

uṭhaha, amogha, tumi lao kṛṣṇa-nāma  
get up  Amogha  you  take  Kṛṣṇa’s name  
acire tomāre kṛpā karibe bhagavān  
soon  on you  mercy  will show  Bhagavān

(277) “Get up, Amogha! Take the name of Kṛṣṇa, and Bhagavān will soon show you His mercy.”

শুনি’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ বলি’ অমোঘ উঠিলা ৷  
প্রেমোন্মাদে মত্ত হঞা নাচিতে লাগিলা ॥ ২৭৮ ॥

śuni’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ bali’ amogha uṭhilā  
hearing  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  Amogha  got up  
premonmāde matta hañā nācite lāgilā  
in the madness of love  intoxicated  becoming  to dance  began

(278) Hearing this, Amogha rose up crying, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” and, mad with love, began to dance.

কম্প, অশ্রু, পুলক, স্তম্ভ, স্বেদ, স্বরভঙ্গ ৷  
প্রভু হাসে দেখি’ তার প্রেমের তরঙ্গ ॥ ২৭৯ ॥

kampa, aśru, pulaka, stambha, sveda, svara-bhaṅga  
trembling  tears  hair standing on end  becoming stunned  perspiration  faltering voice  
prabhu hāse dekhi’ tāra premera taraṅga  
the Lord  smiles  seeing  his  of love  the waves

(279) Trembling, tears, hair standing on end, stupor, perspiration and a faltering voice appeared in him, and seeing these waves of love, the Lord smiled.

প্রভুর চরণে ধরি’ করেন বিনয় ৷  
অপরাধ ক্ষম মোরে, প্রভু, দয়াময় ॥ ২৮০ ॥

prabhura caraṇe dhari’ karena vinaya  
of the Lord  the feet  clasping  makes  entreaty  
aparādha kṣama more, prabhu, dayāmaya  
offence  pardon  me  O Lord  merciful

(280) Clasping the Lord’s feet, he humbly entreated Him: “O merciful Lord, forgive my offence.

এই ছার মুখে তোমার করিনু নিন্দনে ৷  
এত বলি’ আপন গালে চড়ায় আপনে ॥ ২৮১ ॥

ei chāra mukhe tomāra karinu nindane  
this  wretched  with mouth  of You  I made  blasphemy  
eta bali’ āpana gāle caḍāya āpane  
this  saying  his own  cheeks  slaps  himself

(281) “With this wretched mouth I blasphemed You!” Saying this, he began to slap his own cheeks.

চড়াইতে চড়াইতে গাল ফুলাইল ৷  
হাতে ধরি’ গোপীনাথাচার্য্য নিষেধিল ॥ ২৮২ ॥

caḍāite caḍāite gāla phulāila  
slapping  slapping  the cheeks  he swelled  
hāte dhari’ gopīnāthācārya niṣedhila  
by the hands  seizing  Gopīnātha Ācārya  stopped him

(282) He slapped himself until his cheeks were swollen. Then Gopīnātha Ācārya seized his hands and stopped him.

প্রভু আশ্বাসন করে স্পর্শি’ তার গাত্র ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম-সম্বন্ধে তুমি মোর স্নেহপাত্র ॥ ২৮৩ ॥

prabhu āśvāsana kare sparśi’ tāra gātra  
the Lord  consolation  gives  touching  his  body  
sārvabhauma-sambandhe tumi mora sneha-pātra  
by relation to Sārvabhauma  you  My  object of affection

(283) The Lord consoled him, touching his body: “Because you are related to Sārvabhauma, you are dear to Me.

সার্ব্বভৌম-গৃহে দাস দাসী, যে কুক্কুর ৷  
সেহ মোর প্রিয়, অন্য জন রহু দূর ॥ ২৮৪ ॥

sārvabhauma-gṛhe dāsa dāsī, ye kukkura  
in Sārvabhauma’s house  servants  maids  even  dogs  
seha mora priya, anya jana rahu dūra  
they too  to Me  dear  other  people  let stay far

(284) “Even the servants, maids and dogs in Sārvabhauma’s house are dear to Me — to say nothing of others.

‘অপরাধ’ নাহি তব, লও কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
এত বলি’ প্রভু আইলা সার্ব্বভৌম-স্থান ॥ ২৮৫ ॥

‘aparādha’ nāhi taba, lao kṛṣṇa-nāma  
offence  there is no  in you  take  Kṛṣṇa’s name  
eta bali’ prabhu āilā sārvabhauma-sthāna  
this  saying  the Lord  came  to Sārvabhauma’s place

(285) “There is no offence left in you. Take the name of Kṛṣṇa.” With these words the Lord went to Sārvabhauma’s house.

প্রভু দেখি’ সার্ব্বভৌম ধরিলা চরণে ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে আলিঙ্গিয়া বসিলা আসনে ॥ ২৮৬ ॥

prabhu dekhi’ sārvabhauma dharilā caraṇe  
the Lord  seeing  Sārvabhauma  clasped  the feet  
prabhu tām̐re āliṅgiyā vasilā āsane  
the Lord  him  embracing  sat  on a seat

(286) Seeing the Lord, Sārvabhauma clasped His feet. The Lord embraced him and sat down.

প্রভু কহে, — অমোঘ শিশু, কিবা তার দোষ ৷  
কেনে উপবাস কর, কেনে কর রোষ ॥ ২৮৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — amogha śiśu, kibā tāra doṣa  
the Lord  says  Amogha  a child  what  his  fault  
kene upavāsa kara, kene kara roṣa  
why  fast  you keep  why  you nurse  anger

(287) The Lord said, “Amogha is a child. What fault is there in him? Why are you fasting, and why are you angry?

উঠ, স্নান কর, দেখ জগন্নাথ-মুখ ৷  
শীঘ্র আসি’ ভোজন কর, তবে মোর সুখ ॥ ২৮৮ ॥

uṭha, snāna kara, dekha jagannātha-mukha  
get up  bath  take  see  Jagannātha’s face  
śīghra āsi’ bhojana kara, tabe mora sukha  
quickly  coming back  meal  take  then  My  happiness

(288) “Get up, bathe and go to see Jagannātha’s face. Then come back quickly and eat; that will make Me happy.

তাবৎ রহিব আমি এথায় বসিয়া ৷  
যাবৎ না পাইবা তুমি প্রসাদ আনিয়া ॥ ২৮৯ ॥

tāvat rahiba āmi ethāya vasiyā  
so long  shall stay  I  here  sitting  
yāvat nā pāibā tumi prasāda āniyā  
as long as  not  honour  you  prasāda  bringing

(289) “I shall sit here until you have brought *prasāda* and honoured it.”

প্রভু পদ ধরি’ ভট্ট কহিতে লাগিলা ৷  
মরিত’ অমোঘ, তারে কেনে জীয়াইলা ॥ ২৯০ ॥

prabhu pada dhari’ bhaṭṭa kahite lāgilā  
of the Lord  the feet  clasping  Bhaṭṭa  to speak  began  
marita’ amogha, tāre kene jīyāilā  
would have died  Amogha  him  why  You revived

(290) Clasping the Lord’s feet, Bhaṭṭa said, “Amogha would have died. Why did You bring him back to life?”

প্রভু কহে, — অমোঘ শিশু, তোমার বালক ৷  
বালক-দোষ না লয় পিতা, তাহাতে পালক ॥ ২৯১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — amogha śiśu, tomāra bālaka  
the Lord  says  Amogha  a child  your  boy  
bālaka-doṣa nā laya pitā, tāhāte pālaka  
the boy’s fault  not  takes  the father  for in that  he is the protector

(291) The Lord said, “Amogha is a child; he is your son. A father does not hold his child’s faults against him, for he is the child’s protector.

এবে ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হৈল, তার গেল ‘অপরাধ’ ৷  
তাহার উপরে এবে করহ প্রসাদ ॥ ২৯২ ॥

ebe ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila, tāra gela ‘aparādha’  
now  a Vaiṣṇava  he has become  his  went  offence  
tāhāra upare ebe karaha prasāda  
him  on  now  show  grace

(292) “He has become a Vaiṣṇava now, and his offence is gone; so show him your favour.”

ভট্ট কহে, — চল, প্রভু, ঈশ্বর-দরশনে ৷  
স্নান করি’ হেথা মুঞি আসিলাঙ এখনে ॥ ২৯৩ ॥

bhaṭṭa kahe, — cala, prabhu, īśvara-daraśane  
Bhaṭṭa  says  go  O Lord  to see Īśvara (Jagannātha)  
snāna kari’ hethā muñi āsilāṅ ekhane  
bath  having taken  here  I  am back  at once

(293) Bhaṭṭa said, “Go, O Lord, to see Jagannātha. I shall bathe and be back here at once.”

প্রভু কহে, — গোপীনাথ, ইহাঞি রহিবা ৷  
ইঁহো প্রসাদ পাইলে, বার্ত্তা আমাকে কহিবা ॥ ২৯৪ ॥

prabhu kahe, — gopīnātha, ihāñi rahibā  
the Lord  says  Gopīnātha  right here  stay  
im̐ho prasāda pāile, vārtā āmāke kahibā  
he  prasāda  having honoured  the news  to Me  tell

(294) The Lord said, “Gopīnātha, stay right here. When he has honoured the *prasāda*, bring Me word.”

এত বলি’ প্রভু গেলা ঈশ্বর-দরশনে ৷  
ভট্ট স্নান দর্শন করি’ করিলা ভোজনে ॥ ২৯৫ ॥

eta bali’ prabhu gelā īśvara-daraśane  
this  saying  the Lord  went  to see Īśvara  
bhaṭṭa snāna darśana kari’ karilā bhojane  
Bhaṭṭa  bath  sight  having made  took  meal

(295) With these words the Lord went to see Jagannātha, and Bhaṭṭa bathed, took darśana and ate.

সেই অমোঘ হৈল প্রভুর ভক্ত ‘একান্ত’ ৷  
প্রেমে নাচে, কৃষ্ণনাম লয় মহাশান্ত ॥ ২৯৬ ॥

sei amogha haila prabhura bhakta ‘ekānta’  
that  Amogha  became  the Lord’s  devotee  exclusive  
preme nāce, kṛṣṇa-nāma laya mahā-śānta  
in love  dances  Kṛṣṇa’s name  takes  deeply peaceful

(296) That same Amogha became the Lord’s exclusive devotee. He would dance in love and chant the name of Kṛṣṇa, deeply at peace.[^69]

ঐছে চিত্র-লীলা করে শচীর নন্দন ৷  
যেই দেখে, শুনে, তাঁর বিস্ময় হয় মন ॥ ২৯৭ ॥

aiche citra-līlā kare śacīra nandana  
such  wonderful pastimes  does  Śacī’s  son  
yei dekhe, śune, tām̐ra vismaya haya mana  
whoever  sees  hears  his  amazed  becomes  mind

(297) Such are the wonderful pastimes of the son of Śacī: whoever sees or hears of them is struck with wonder.

ঐছে ভট্ট-গৃহে করে ভোজন-বিলাস ৷  
তার মধ্যে নানা চিত্র-চরিত্র-প্রকাশ ॥ ২৯৮ ॥

aiche bhaṭṭa-gṛhe kare bhojana-vilāsa  
thus  in Bhaṭṭa’s house  does  the pastime of eating  
tāra madhye nānā citra-caritra-prakāśa  
it  within  various  wonderful deeds  revealing

(298) Thus the Lord enjoyed the pastime of eating at Bhaṭṭa’s house, and in it He revealed many wonderful deeds.

সার্ব্বভৌম-ঘরে এই ভোজন-চরিত ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম-প্রেম যাঁহা হইলা বিদিত ॥ ২৯৯ ॥

sārvabhauma-ghare ei bhojana-carita  
in Sārvabhauma’s house  this  story of the meal  
sārvabhauma-prema yām̐hā hailā vidita  
Sārvabhauma’s love  in which  became  known

(299) This is the story of the Lord’s meal in Sārvabhauma’s house, in which Sārvabhauma’s love became known,

ষাঠীর মাতার প্রেম, আর প্রভুর প্রসাদ ৷  
ভক্ত-সম্বন্ধে যাহা ক্ষমিল অপরাধ ॥ ৩০০ ॥

ṣāṭhīra mātāra prema, āra prabhura prasāda  
of Ṣāṭhī’s mother  the love  and  the Lord’s  grace  
bhakta-sambandhe yāhā kṣamila aparādha  
by relation to a devotee  by which  He pardoned  offence

(300) and the love of Ṣāṭhī’s mother, and the grace of the Lord, by which He forgave an offence because of the offender’s relation to His devotee.[^70]

শ্রদ্ধা করি’ এই লীলা শুনে যেই জন ৷  
অচিরাৎ পায় সেই চৈতন্য-চরণ ॥ ৩০১ ॥

śraddhā kari’ ei līlā śune yei jana  
with faith  making  this  pastime  hears  whoever  person  
acirāt pāya sei caitanya-caraṇa  
soon  attains  he  Caitanya’s feet

(301) Whoever hears this pastime with faith will soon attain the feet of Śrī Caitanya.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ৩০২ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(302) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.[^71]

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: Amogha was the husband of Ṣaṣṭhī, Sārvabhauma’s daughter; “accepted” — counted him among His servants. Heading in the edition: “Gaurasundara makes His own Amogha, who had reviled Him.”

[^2]: The same verse as Madhya 10.2 (= Ādi 4.2); translation as there.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *upala-bhoga* is offered at midday in the *bhoga-vardhana* hall. Before leaving the temple, the Lord would stand behind the Garuḍa pillar, fall prostrate and offer prayers; on the way back He met Haridāsa Ṭhākura at Siddha-bakula and returned to His dwelling, the house of Kāśī Miśra.

[^4]: *Anubhāṣya*: some give the mantra in a fuller form: “O Rādhā, O Kṛṣṇa, O Rāma, O Viṣṇu, O Sītā, O Rāma, O Śiva, O Śiva! Whoever You are, that You are — I always bow to You; whoever You are, that You are — obeisance to You.”

[^5]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see the *Śrī Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya-khaṇḍa, chapter 9.

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya*: the day of Kṛṣṇa’s birth festival here is the day after Janmāṣṭamī, the day of the Nandotsava.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pāda-sandhye* — in the space between the feet.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: a burning brand whirled very fast looks like an unbroken circle of fire; so fast did the Lord whirl the staff that it seemed to be everywhere at once.

[^9]: Heading in the edition: “Nitāi too whirls a staff and shows Himself a cowherd.”

[^10]: Heading in the edition: “On Vijayā-daśamī the Lord dresses the devotees as an army of monkeys and Himself plays Hanumān.”

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Mother of the world is Sītā-devī.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: “The Lord sends off all the devotees of Gauḍa, telling them to come to Him every year for the Guṇḍicā festival.”

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *prākṛta-sahajiyās* claim that Mahāprabhu sent Nityānanda to Gauḍa to continue a family line; such an idea springs only from offence at His feet. Śrī Nityānanda is non-different from the Lord, the original source of all *viṣṇu-tattva*, and no authoritative book records any such order.

[^14]: Heading in the edition: “Nitāi’s companions in preaching — Abhirāma and Gadādhara dāsa.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Gadādhara is Gadādhara dāsa of Eṇḍiyādaha.

[^15]: The edition reads তোমার নিকটে (locative: “near You”); the Bengali text and transliteration are corrected accordingly (scan, p. 582).

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: by taking sannyāsa the Lord failed in the dharma of serving His mother.

[^17]: Heading in the edition: “The Lord promises that even now He will come to Māyāpura from time to time to see Śacī.”

[^18]: Heading in the edition: “The Lord has always visited Śacī, but by the power of His māyā Śacī doubts it.”

[^19]: Money was counted in cowrie shells: a *gaṇḍā* is four cowries, so five *gaṇḍās* are twenty. Heading in the edition: “The wonderful variety of Rāghava’s coconut offerings.”

[^20]: A *paṇa* is twenty *gaṇḍās*, or eighty cowries: four *paṇas* are 320 cowries, sixteen times the usual price (verse 70). A *krośa* is about two miles.

[^21]: The edition reads *śata-pātra* (literally “a hundred vessels”, or a vessel of that name); the commentaries do not explain the word, and it is rendered neutrally as “a plate”.

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: Rāghava Paṇḍita was neither a man of karma afflicted with the worldly “disease of cleanliness” nor a novice devotee who “considers the earth worshipable” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.84.13). He was an eternally perfect servant of Kṛṣṇa, absorbed in transcendental service. Those whose devotion is mixed with karma do not prove their pure devotion by imitating his outward conduct and arguing about worldly purity and impurity (see Antya 4.174, 176; Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.28.4).

[^23]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *huḍuma* is a particular grain; its puffed form (*khai*) is especially common in Utkala.

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*: *kṣīra-odana* is *pāyasa*, rice cooked in milk.

[^25]: Heading in the edition: “Śivānanda is told to look after Vāsudeva Datta, who saves nothing.” *Anubhāṣya*: Śivānanda Sena and Vāsudeva Datta Ṭhākura were living at that time in Kumārahaṭṭa (Hālisahara) and Kāñcaḍāpāḍā.

[^26]: Heading in the edition: “The Lord tells him to bring all the devotees to Purī every year, settling the tolls at the checkpoints.”

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya* (after Bhaktivinoda Ṭhākura’s introduction to the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vijaya*): Guṇarāja Khān was the title given by the king of Gauḍa to Mālādhara Vasu, thirteenth in descent from Daśaratha Vasu; he began the book, a rendering of the Tenth and Eleventh Cantos of the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, in Śaka 1395 and finished it in Śaka 1402. His second son, Lakṣmīnātha Vasu, bore the title Satyarāja Khān, and Lakṣmīnātha’s son was Rāmānanda Vasu (verse 102). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: many consider the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vijaya* the first Bengali poem.

[^28]: *Anubhāṣya*: the full line of the poem reads: “With undivided feeling, with joined palms, glorify Hari: ‘Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda, is the Lord of my life!’”

[^29]: The second line is almost the same as Ādi 10.82.

[^30]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Rāmānanda Vasu and his father Satyarāja Khān were Vaiṣṇava householders of the Vasu family of *kāyasthas*.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: these are the marks of a *kaniṣṭha* (novice) Vaiṣṇava. One who believes that every perfection is attained by the name of Kṛṣṇa alone is a Vaiṣṇava, though his faith is still soft and he does not chant the name constantly. “The best of all” — the best in all of Devī-dhāma: even a novice devotee who worships Viṣṇu stands above the pious *karmī* and the *jñānī*, for he believes that Viṣṇu is the eternal Reality.

[^32]: *Anubhāṣya*: for one who avoids offences against the name and takes shelter of the name of Kṛṣṇa alone, all sins and all worldly desires are destroyed; the nine kinds of devotion (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.5.23) reach completeness only through service to the name: “in Kali-yuga the other kinds of bhakti too are performed together with kīrtana” (Bhakti-sandarbha, 173).

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: initiation awakens divine knowledge and destroys sin; *puraścaryā* is the fivefold worship (worship three times a day, *japa*, *tarpaṇa*, *homa* and feeding *brāhmaṇas*) that brings a mantra to perfection. Śrī Nāma, the *mahā-mantra*, needs none of this: merely uttering it brings every fruit. “Touching the tongue” means a tongue inclined to service: on a tongue that seeks worldly pleasure the name of Kṛṣṇa never appears. Heading in the edition: “Śrī Nāma — the Lord Kṛṣṇa Himself — does not depend on other acts.”

[^34]: Heading in the edition: a verse of Śrīdhara Svāmī quoted in the *Padyāvalī* (29). *Anubhāṣya*: *kṛta-cetasām* — of the liberated; *sumanasām* (another reading, *sumahatām*) — of the wise, who have passed beyond the three *guṇas*; *aṁhasām* — of both piety and sin, the acts born of absorption in the world; *amūka-loka-sulabha* — within reach of everyone who is not mute.

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī (Upadeśāmṛta, 5) says that one on whose lips is the name of Kṛṣṇa should be honoured in the mind. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: a Vaiṣṇava householder need only serve a Vaiṣṇava devoted to the name of Kṛṣṇa alone. One initiated into a Viṣṇu-*mantra* who does not know the truth may be tainted by Māyāvāda — he is “almost a Vaiṣṇava”; but one who has uttered the name of Kṛṣṇa even once without offence, even as a novice, is “a pure Vaiṣṇava”.

[^36]: Heading in the edition: “Mukunda, who seeks no honour himself and honours others, knows Raghunandana to be a devotee of Kṛṣṇa and so sees in him not a son but a guru.”

[^37]: *Anubhāṣya* (on 121): in people’s eyes Mukunda served as a royal physician, but in truth he was a *mahā-bhāgavata*, a *paramahaṁsa* in the guise of a Vaiṣṇava householder, and ordinary people did not know it.

[^38]: *Anubhāṣya*: *ucca-ṭuṅgi* — a small house built on a height.

[^39]: *Anubhāṣya*: *mahā-vidagdha* — especially versed in the science of government; *mahā-siddha* — an extraordinary liberated soul. Heading in the edition: “Though Mukunda dissembles and hides himself, the king regards him as a ‘great soul’.”

[^40]: *Anubhāṣya*: *avataṁsa* — an ornament for the ears or the head.

[^41]: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu knew Mukunda to be a very dear, intimate devotee. Dividing the service among the two brothers and the son, He gave Mukunda dharma and earning a living, Raghunandana the service of the Deity, and Narahari life among the devotees.

[^42]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Sārvabhauma worships Jagannāthadeva, the Dāru-brahma (Brahman in the form of wood), while Vidyā-vācaspati, living in Vidyānagara within Navadvīpa-dhāma, serves the Gaṅgā, the Jala-brahma (Brahman in the form of water).

[^43]: *Anubhāṣya*: for the like devotion to Śrī Rāma of Śrī Anupama (Vallabha), the father of Śrī Jīva, see Antya 4.30–45.

[^44]: Heading in the edition: “Under the Lord’s influence Murāri’s mind changed for a moment.”

[^45]: *Anubhāṣya* quotes the verse: “The Lord of Śrī and the Lord of Jānakī are one and the same Supreme Soul; there is no difference between Them. Still, my all in all is lotus-eyed Rāma.”

[^46]: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to Govinda, the giver of all fruits (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.54).” *Anubhāṣya*: the *indragopa* is a small red insect; for His devotees Govinda destroys both *prārabdha* and *aprārabdha* karma — all the fruits still to be tasted.

[^47]: *Anubhāṣya*: on Janaloka, at the *brahma-satra* sacrifice, Sanandana, one of the four Kumāras, recounts to the sages this prayer of the personified *śrutis*; the repeated *jaya* is a reverent insistence (“show Your supremacy!”); the *Anubhāṣya* takes *aga-jagad-okasām* with *ajām*: the māyā that holds sway over moving and unmoving beings.

[^48]: *Anubhāṣya*: Yameśvara-ṭoṭā is a garden on the sands south-west of the Śrī Mandira; there Mahāprabhu gave Gadādhara Paṇḍita a place to live. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the reading “at Jaleśvara” does not seem right; in Yameśvara-ṭoṭā stands the temple of Śrī Ṭoṭā-Gopīnātha, where Gadādhara Paṇḍita lived, serving Gopīnātha and Mahāprabhu.

[^49]: The edition reads *ānibe* (“will bring”); the earlier text had *āsibena* (“will come”).

[^50]: As in Madhya 3.43, the *battiśā-āṭhiyā* is a banana tree that bears a bunch of thirty-two clusters; *āṅgaṭiyā* — a whole, untorn leaf; the *māna* is a measure of grain. *Anubhāṣya*: with this fine description of the feast the author shows his own matchless skill in cooking and serving; compare Madhya 3.44–55.

[^51]: *Anubhāṣya*: *dugdha-tumbī* — bottle gourd (*lāu*) cooked in milk; *vesara* — vegetables with ground mustard, so called in Utkala; *śākrā* — a sweet vegetable dish.

[^52]: *Anubhāṣya*: *kuṣmāṇḍa māna-cākī* — pumpkin and *māna-kacu* (giant taro) cut into small rounds and fried.

[^53]: *Anubhāṣya*: *madhurāmla* — a chutney, a sweet-and-sour dish; *baḍāmla* — a sour dish with dumplings (*baḍā*) of dāl; the soup is of fried *kalāi* (urad) and fried mung.

[^54]: *Anubhāṣya*: *dugdha-laklakī* — *cuṣī-puli*.

[^55]: *Anubhāṣya*: a piece of fine cloth was spread on a white wooden board (*piṁḍi*), and so a seat was made.

[^56]: *Anubhāṣya*: the food cooked at his home, not yet offered, he did not mix with Jagannātha’s *prasāda*; he took care to keep them apart.

[^57]: The edition reads *pātra* (the earlier text had *pātre*).

[^58]: *Anubhāṣya*: Uddhava, Kṛṣṇa’s dearest servant, offered this prayer when calamities had begun in Dvārakā and he learned that Kṛṣṇa wished to close His manifest pastimes — before the conversation known as the *Uddhava-gītā*.

[^59]: *Anubhāṣya*: the eighteen mothers are Devakī, Rohiṇī and others.

[^60]: *Anubhāṣya* names them from the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*: Kṛṣṇa’s elder paternal uncles Upananda and Abhinanda, the younger ones Sannanda and Nandana, His maternal uncles Yaśodhara, Yaśodeva, Sudeva and others, and the husbands of His paternal aunts, Mahānīla and Sunīla; and it lists His friends — those “like younger brothers”, the “dear friends” (*priya-sakhās*) and the “intimate friends” (*priya-narma-sakhās*).

[^61]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *mādhukarī* — a mouthful of food obtained in the manner of the bee (*madhukara-vṛtti*).

[^62]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *elāci rasa-vāsa* — cardamom, juicy and fragrant.

[^63]: *Anubhāṣya* cites scripture (*Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*, *Bhakti-sandarbha*) on the fruits of blaspheming Vaiṣṇavas: the blasphemer loses his merit and falls into hell; one who hears blasphemy of Bhagavān or His devotee and does not go away also falls (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.74.40); as Satī says (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 4.4.17), one who is powerless should cover his ears and leave, while one who is able should silence the blasphemer’s evil tongue, even at the cost of his own life. Sārvabhauma reasons in the same way (261–263).

[^64]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Amogha is a brāhmaṇa and cannot be killed; Sārvabhauma too is a brāhmaṇa, so he may not take his own life; one course remains — never to see the blasphemer’s face.

[^65]: Heading in the edition: “Words of the *smṛti*.” *Anubhāṣya* cites Yājñavalkya, quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 7.11.28: a husband defiled by a great sin is to be waited for until he is purified.

[^66]: *Anubhāṣya*: when Duryodhana and the Kauravas came to the forest to inspect their cattle stations, the Gandharva king Citrasena took them captive; Duryodhana’s ministers begged the Pāṇḍavas for help, and Bhīmasena, remembering Duryodhana’s past misdeeds, spoke these words.

[^67]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śukadeva spoke this to Parīkṣit when Kaṁsa ordered the Dānavas to persecute the sages and saints; *mahad-atikrama* — disrespect to Viṣṇu and the Vaiṣṇavas in body, mind or word, that is, *vaiṣṇava-aparādha* (compare Antya 3.146, 163).

[^68]: Heading in the edition: “The Lord hurries to the Bhaṭṭa’s house and gives Amogha good counsel.”

[^69]: *Anubhāṣya* quotes the *Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta*: “I worship Amogha Paṇḍita, whom Śrī Gaura made His own: his whole being is full of prema, his voice falters with it, and his body thrills.”

[^70]: *Anubhāṣya*: the fruit of his blasphemy was a mortal illness, and Amogha could no longer make amends; but for the sake of Sārvabhauma and his wife, vessels of His mercy, the Lord forgave the offender, saved his life and gave him devotion to Kṛṣṇa; had He not forgiven Amogha, the punishment would indirectly have fallen on the Bhaṭṭa, his guardian; so the Lord showed His majesty, depth and generosity.

[^71]: The verse = Madhya 7.155 (the closing refrain); the translation is the same as there.
