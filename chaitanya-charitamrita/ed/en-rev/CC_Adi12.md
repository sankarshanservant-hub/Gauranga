“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 12  
**The Description of the Branches of the Advaita Trunk**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

অদ্বৈতাঙ্ঘ্র্যব্জভৃঙ্গাংস্তান্ সারাসারভৃতোঽখিলান্ ৷  
হিত্বাঽসারান্ সারভৃতো নৌমি চৈতন্যজীবনান্ ॥ ১ ॥

advaitāṅghry-abja-bhṛṅgāṁs tān  
of Advaita’s  feet  lotus  the bees  those  
sārāsāra-bhṛto ’khilān  
the essence and the empty  bearing  all  
hitvā ’sārān sāra-bhṛto  
rejecting  the empty  the bearers of the essence  
naumi caitanya-jīvanān  
I bow to  whose life is Caitanya

(1) Like bees, all the followers of Śrī Advaita hover about His lotus feet, yet some of them hold the essence and others are empty. Rejecting the empty, I bow to those who hold the essence, whose very life is Śrī Caitanya.[^1]

জয় জয় মহাপ্রভু শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ জয়াদ্বৈত ধন্য ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya mahāprabhu śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
glory  glory  Mahāprabhu  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
jaya jaya nityānanda jayādvaita dhanya  
glory  glory  Nityānanda  glory to Advaita  the blessed

(2) Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Glory, glory to Nityānanda, and glory to the blessed Advaita!

শ্রীচৈতন্যামরতরোর্দ্বিতীয়স্কন্ধরূপিণঃ ৷  
শ্রীমদদ্বৈতচন্দ্রস্য শাখারূপান্ গণান্নুমঃ ॥ ৩ ॥

śrī-caitanyāmara-taror  
of Śrī  Caitanya  the immortal  tree  
dvitīya-skandha-rūpiṇaḥ  
the second  trunk  who is  
śrīmad-advaita-candrasya  
of the glorious  Advaitacandra  
śākhā-rūpān gaṇān numaḥ  
who are the branches  the followers  we bow to

(3) The glorious Advaitacandra is the second trunk of the immortal tree of Śrī Caitanya, and His followers are the branches of that trunk. To them we bow down.

বৃক্ষের দ্বিতীয় স্কন্ধ — আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি ৷  
তাঁর যত শাখা হইল, তার লেখা নাঞি ॥ ৪ ॥

vṛkṣera dvitīya skandha — ācārya-gosāñi  
of the tree  the second  trunk  —  Ācārya Gosāñi  
tām̐ra yata śākhā haila, tāra lekhā nāñi  
His  as many  branches  grew,  of them  record  there is none

(4) The second trunk of the tree is Ācārya Gosāñi. So many branches grew from Him that there is no writing them all down.

চৈতন্য-মালীর কৃপাজলের সেচনে ৷  
সেই জলে পুষ্ট স্কন্ধ বাড়ে দিনে দিনে ॥ ৫ ॥

caitanya-mālīra kṛpā-jalera secane  
of Caitanya the gardener  of the water of mercy  by the sprinkling  
sei jale puṣṭa skandha bāḍe dine dine  
by that  water  nourished  the trunk  grows  day by day

(5) Caitanya the gardener sprinkles it with the water of His mercy, and, nourished by that water, the trunk grows day by day.[^2]

সেই স্কন্ধে যত প্রেমফল উপজিল ৷  
সেই কৃষ্ণপ্রেমফলে জগৎ ভরিল ॥ ৬ ॥

sei skandhe yata prema-phala upajila  
on that  trunk  as many  fruits of love  grew  
sei kṛṣṇa-prema-phale jagat bharila  
with those  fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa  the world  was filled

(6) So many fruits of love ripened on that trunk that the whole world was filled with the fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa.

সেই জল স্কন্ধে করে শাখাতে সঞ্চার ৷  
ফলে-ফুলে বাড়ে, — শাখা হইল বিস্তার ॥ ৭ ॥

sei jala skandhe kare śākhāte sañcāra  
that  water  through the trunk  makes  into the branches  passage  
phale-phule bāḍe, — śākhā haila vistāra  
with fruit and flower  they grow  —  the branches  became  wide-spreading

(7) Through the trunk that water rises into the branches, and, laden with fruit and flowers, they spread far and wide.

প্রথমে ত’ একমত আচার্য্যের গণ ৷  
পাছে দুইমত হৈল দৈবের কারণ ॥ ৮ ॥

prathame ta’ eka-mata ācāryera gaṇa  
at first  indeed  of one mind  the Ācārya’s  followers  
pāche dui-mata haila daivera kāraṇa  
later  two views  arose  of fate  by reason

(8) At first the Ācārya’s followers were all of one mind; but later, as fate would have it, two views arose among them.

কেহ ত’ আচার্য্যের আজ্ঞায়, কেহ ত’ স্বতন্ত্র ৷  
স্বমত কল্পনা করে দৈব-পরতন্ত্র ॥ ৯ ॥

keha ta’ ācāryera ājñāya, keha ta’ svatantra  
some  indeed  by the Ācārya’s  order,  some  indeed  independent  
sva-mata kalpanā kare daiva-paratantra  
their own views  invent  to fate  subject

(9) Some kept to the Ācārya’s order, while others went their own way: in the grip of fate, they invented views of their own.[^3]

আচার্য্যের মত যেই, সেই মত সার ৷  
তাঁর আজ্ঞা লঙ্ঘি’ চলে, সেই ত’ অসার ॥ ১০ ॥

ācāryera mata yei, sei mata sāra  
the Ācārya’s  teaching  which,  that  teaching  the essence  
tām̐ra ājñā laṅghi’ cale, sei ta’ asāra  
His  order  transgressing  goes,  he  indeed  empty

(10) The Ācārya’s teaching is the essence; whoever goes against His order is empty, mere chaff.[^4]

অসারের নামে ইঁহা নাহি প্রয়োজন ৷  
ভেদ জানিবারে করি একত্র গণন ॥ ১১ ॥

asārera nāme im̐hā nāhi prayojana  
of the empty  in naming  here  there is no  need  
bheda jānibāre kari ekatra gaṇana  
the difference  to know  I make  together  the count

(11) There is no need here to name the empty ones; I count them all together so that the difference may be known.

ধান্যরাশি মাপে যৈছে পাতনা সহিতে ৷  
পশ্চাতে পাতনা উড়াঞা সংস্কার করিতে ॥ ১২ ॥

dhānya-rāśi māpe yaiche pātanā sahite  
a heap of paddy  one measures  just as  chaff  together with  
paścāte pātanā uḍāñā saṁskāra karite  
afterwards  the chaff  blowing away  cleaning  to do

(12) Just so, one first measures a heap of paddy along with its chaff, and afterwards winnows the chaff away to clean the grain.[^5]

অচ্যুতানন্দ — বড় শাখা, আচার্য্য-নন্দন ৷  
আজন্ম সেবিলা তিঁহো চৈতন্যচরণ ॥ ১৩ ॥

acyutānanda — baḍa śākhā, ācārya-nandana  
Acyutānanda  —  a great  branch,  the Ācārya’s  son  
ājanma sevilā tim̐ho caitanya-caraṇa  
from birth  served  he  Caitanya’s  feet

(13) Acyutānanda, the Ācārya’s son, is a great branch. From his very birth he served the feet of Śrī Caitanya.[^6]

চৈতন্য গোসাঞির গুরু — কেশব ভারতী ৷  
এই পিতার বাক্য শুনি’ দুঃখ পাইল অতি ॥ ১৪ ॥

caitanya gosāñira guru — keśava bhāratī  
of Caitanya  Gosāñi  the guru  —  Keśava  Bhāratī  
ei pitāra vākya śuni’ duḥkha pāila ati  
these  of his father  words  hearing,  pain  he felt  great

(14) When he heard his father say that Keśava Bhāratī was the guru of Caitanya Gosāñi, he was deeply pained.

জগদ্গুরুতে তুমি কর ঐছে উপদেশ ৷  
তোমার এই উপদেশে নষ্ট হইল দেশ ॥ ১৫ ॥

jagad-gurute tumi kara aiche upadeśa  
about the guru of the world  You  give  such  instruction  
tomāra ei upadeśe naṣṭa haila deśa  
Your  this  by instruction  ruined  is  the land

(15) “How can You teach such a thing about the guru of the world? This teaching of Yours will bring ruin on the whole land.

চৌদ্দ ভুবনের গুরু — চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ৷  
তাঁর গুরু — অন্য, এই কোন শাস্ত্রে নাই ॥ ১৬ ॥

caudda bhuvanera guru — caitanya-gosāñi  
of the fourteen  worlds  the guru  —  Caitanya Gosāñi  
tām̐ra guru — anya, ei kona śāstre nāi  
His  guru  —  another,  this  in any  scripture  is not

(16) Caitanya Gosāñi is the guru of the fourteen worlds; no scripture anywhere says that anyone else is His guru.”

পঞ্চম বর্ষের বালক কহে সিদ্ধান্তের সার ৷  
শুনিয়া পাইলা আচার্য্য সন্তোষ অপার ॥ ১৭ ॥

pañcama varṣera bālaka kahe siddhāntera sāra  
of five  years  a boy  speaks  of conclusions  the essence  
śuniyā pāilā ācārya santoṣa apāra  
hearing  obtained  the Ācārya  satisfaction  boundless

(17) Hearing a boy of five speak the very essence of the scriptures’ conclusions, the Ācārya was boundlessly pleased.[^7]

কৃষ্ণমিশ্র-নাম আর আচার্য্য-তনয় ৷  
চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি বৈসে যাঁহার হৃদয় ॥ ১৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa-miśra-nāma āra ācārya-tanaya  
Kṛṣṇa  Miśra  by name  another  the Ācārya’s son  
caitanya-gosāñi baise yām̐hāra hṛdaya  
Caitanya Gosāñi  dwells  in whose  heart

(18) Another of the Ācārya’s sons was named Kṛṣṇa Miśra; in his heart Caitanya Gosāñi dwelt.[^8]

শ্রীগোপাল-নামে আর আচার্য্যের সুত ৷  
তাঁহার চরিত্র, শুন, অত্যন্ত অদ্ভুত ॥ ১৯ ॥

śrī-gopāla-nāme āra ācāryera suta  
Śrī  Gopāla  by name  another  the Ācārya’s  son  
tām̐hāra caritra, śuna, atyanta adbhuta  
his  story,  hear,  most  wonderful

(19) Another son of the Ācārya was named Śrī Gopāla. Listen to his story, for it is most wonderful.

গুণ্ডিচা-মন্দিরে মহাপ্রভুর সম্মুখে ৷  
কীর্ত্তনে নর্ত্তন করে বড় প্রেম-সুখে ॥ ২০ ॥

guṇḍicā-mandire mahāprabhura sammukhe  
in the Guṇḍicā  temple  Mahāprabhu’s  before  
kīrtane nartana kare baḍa prema-sukhe  
in the kīrtana  dancing  does  in great  joy of love

(20) When Mahāprabhu was cleansing the Guṇḍicā temple, Gopāla danced before Him in the kīrtana, filled with the great joy of love.[^9]

নানা-ভাবোদ্গম দেহে অদ্ভুত নর্ত্তন ৷  
দুই গোসাঞি ‘হরি’ বলে, আনন্দিত মন ॥ ২১ ॥

nānā-bhāvodgama dehe adbhuta nartana  
many  ecstasies arising  in his body,  wondrous  dancing  
dui gosāñi ‘hari’ bale, ānandita mana  
the two  Gosāñis  ‘Hari’  say,  delighted  at heart

(21) Many ecstasies arose in his body, and his dancing was wondrous; the two Gosāñis, delighted at heart, called out, “Hari!”[^10]

নাচিতে নাচিতে গোপাল হইল মূর্চ্ছিত ৷  
ভূমেতে পড়িল, দেহে নাহিক সম্বিত ॥ ২২ ॥

nācite nācite gopāla haila mūrcchita  
dancing  and dancing  Gopāla  became  unconscious  
bhūmete paḍila, dehe nāhika samvita  
on the ground  he fell,  in his body  there was no  awareness

(22) In the midst of his dancing Gopāla fainted and fell to the ground, with no sign of awareness left in his body.

দুঃখিত হইলা আচার্য্য পুত্র কোলে লঞা ৷  
রক্ষা করে নৃসিংহের মন্ত্র পড়িয়া ॥ ২৩ ॥

duḥkhita hailā ācārya putra kole lañā  
grieved  became  the Ācārya  his son  on His lap  taking  
rakṣā kare nṛsiṁhera mantra paḍiyā  
protection  makes  of Nṛsiṁha  the mantra  reciting

(23) The Ācārya was grieved. Taking His son on His lap, He recited the mantra of Nṛsiṁha to protect him.

নানা মন্ত্র পড়েন আচার্য্য, না হয় চেতন ৷  
আচার্য্যের দুঃখে বৈষ্ণব করেন ক্রন্দন ॥ ২৪ ॥

nānā mantra paḍena ācārya, nā haya cetana  
many  mantras  recites  the Ācārya,  not  comes  consciousness  
ācāryera duḥkhe vaiṣṇava karena krandana  
at the Ācārya’s  grief  the Vaiṣṇavas  do  weeping

(24) The Ācārya recited mantra after mantra, but the boy did not come to, and the Vaiṣṇavas wept to see the Ācārya’s grief.

তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁর হৃদে হস্ত ধরি’ ৷  
‘উঠহ, গোপাল’ — বল বল ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ২৫ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu tām̐ra hṛde hasta dhari’  
then  Mahāprabhu  his  on the chest  hand  placing  
‘uṭhaha, gopāla’ — bala bala ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
‘get up,  Gopāla’  —  say  say  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’

(25) Then Mahāprabhu placed His hand on the boy’s chest and said, “Get up, Gopāla! Say ‘Hari! Hari!’”

উঠিল গোপাল প্রভুর স্পর্শ-ধ্বনি শুনি’ ৷  
আনন্দিত হঞা সবে করে হরিধ্বনি ॥ ২৬ ॥

uṭhila gopāla prabhura sparśa-dhvani śuni’  
rose  Gopāla  the Lord’s  touch and voice  perceiving  
ānandita hañā sabe kare hari-dhvani  
delighted  becoming  all  make  the cry of ‘Hari’

(26) Feeling the Lord’s touch and hearing His voice, Gopāla rose, and everyone, overjoyed, raised the cry of “Hari!”

আচার্য্যের আর পুত্র — শ্রীবলরাম ৷  
আর পুত্র — ‘স্বরূপ’-শাখা ‘জগদীশ’ নাম ॥ ২৭ ॥

ācāryera āra putra — śrī-balarāma  
the Ācārya’s  other  son  —  Śrī  Balarāma  
āra putra — ‘svarūpa’-śākhā ‘jagadīśa’ nāma  
other  son  —  ‘Svarūpa’  a branch,  ‘Jagadīśa’  by name

(27) The Ācārya had other sons as well: Śrī Balarāma, Svarūpa and one named Jagadīśa.[^11]

‘কমলাকান্ত বিশ্বাস’-নাম আচার্য্য-কিঙ্কর ৷  
আচার্য্য-ব্যবহার সব — তাঁহার গোচর ॥ ২৮ ॥

‘kamalākānta viśvāsa’-nāma ācārya-kiṅkara  
‘Kamalākānta  Viśvāsa’  by name  the Ācārya’s  servant  
ācārya-vyavahāra saba — tām̐hāra gocara  
the Ācārya’s  dealings  all  —  to him  known

(28) The Ācārya had a servant named Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, to whom all the Ācārya’s affairs were known.[^12]

নীলাচলে তিঁহো এক পত্রিকা লিখিয়া ৷  
প্রতাপরুদ্রের স্থানে দিল পাঠাইয়া ॥ ২৯ ॥

nīlācale tim̐ho eka patrikā likhiyā  
in Nīlācala  he  a  letter  writing  
pratāparudrera sthāne dila pāṭhāiyā  
to Pratāparudra  gave  sending

(29) In Nīlācala he wrote a letter and sent it to King Pratāparudra.

সেই পত্রীর কথা আচার্য্য নাহি জানে ৷  
কোন পাকে সেই পত্রী আইল প্রভুস্থানে ॥ ৩০ ॥

sei patrīra kathā ācārya nāhi jāne  
of that  letter  the matter  the Ācārya  does not  know  
kona pāke sei patrī āila prabhusthāne  
somehow  that  letter  came  to the Lord

(30) The Ācārya knew nothing about the letter, and somehow it found its way to the Lord.

সে পত্রীতে লেখা আছে, — এই ত’ লিখন ৷  
ঈশ্বরত্বে আচার্য্যেরে ক’রেছে স্থাপন ॥ ৩১ ॥

se patrīte lekhā āche, — ei ta’ likhana  
in that  letter  written  is  —  this  indeed  the writing  
īśvaratve ācāryere ka’reche sthāpana  
as God  the Ācārya  he has  established

(31) This is what the letter said: he had proclaimed the Ācārya to be God Himself.

কিন্তু তাঁর দৈবে কিছু হইয়াছে ঋণ ৷  
ঋণ শোধিবারে চাহি মুদ্রা শত-তিন ॥ ৩২ ॥

kintu tām̐ra daive kichu haiyāche ṛṇa  
but  His  by fate  some  has come about  debt  
ṛṇa śodhibāre cāhi mudrā śata-tina  
the debt  to pay off  I ask  coins  three hundred

(32) “But by fate He has fallen into some debt, and to pay it off I ask for three hundred coins.”

পত্র পড়িয়া প্রভুর মনে হৈল দুঃখ ৷  
বাহিরে হাসিয়া কিছু বলে চাঁদমুখ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

patra paḍiyā prabhura mane haila duḥkha  
the letter  reading  the Lord’s  in mind  arose  sorrow  
bāhire hāsiyā kichu bale cām̐da-mukha  
outwardly  smiling  something  says  the moon-faced

(33) Reading the letter, the Lord was pained at heart, but outwardly the moon-faced Lord smiled and said:

আচার্য্যেরে স্থাপিয়াছে করিয়া ঈশ্বর ৷  
ইথে দোষ নাহি, আচার্য্য — দৈবত ঈশ্বর ॥ ৩৪ ॥

ācāryere sthāpiyāche kariyā īśvara  
the Ācārya  he has established  making  God  
ithe doṣa nāhi, ācārya — daivata īśvara  
in this  fault  there is none,  the Ācārya  —  truly  God

(34) “He has proclaimed the Ācārya to be God, and in that there is no fault: the Ācārya truly is God.

ঈশ্বরের দৈন্য করি’ করিয়াছে ভিক্ষা ৷  
অতএব দণ্ড করি’ করাইব শিক্ষা ॥ ৩৫ ॥

īśvarera dainya kari’ kariyāche bhikṣā  
God’s  poverty  making  he has made  begging  
ataeva daṇḍa kari’ karāiba śikṣā  
therefore  punishment  giving  I shall make  a lesson

(35) But he has made God out to be poor and gone begging for Him. So I shall punish him and teach him a lesson.”[^13]

গোবিন্দেরে আজ্ঞা দিল, — ‘ইহাঁ আজি হৈতে ৷  
বাউলিয়া ‘বিশ্বাসে’ এথা না দিবে আসিতে’ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

govindere ājñā dila, — ‘ihām̐ āji haite  
to Govinda  order  He gave  —  here  today  from  
bāuliyā ‘viśvāse’ ethā nā dibe āsite’  
the madcap  ‘Viśvāsa’  here  not  you shall let  come

(36) He ordered Govinda, “From today on, do not let that madcap Viśvāsa come here.”[^14]

দণ্ড শুনি’ ‘বিশ্বাস’ হইল পরম দুঃখিত ৷  
শুনিয়া প্রভুর দণ্ড আচার্য্য হর্ষিত ॥ ৩৭ ॥

daṇḍa śuni’ ‘viśvāsa’ haila parama duḥkhita  
of the punishment  hearing  ‘Viśvāsa’  became  most  unhappy  
śuniyā prabhura daṇḍa ācārya harṣita  
hearing  of the Lord’s  punishment  the Ācārya  delighted

(37) Hearing of his punishment, Viśvāsa was deeply distressed; but when the Ācārya heard of the Lord’s punishment, He was delighted.

বিশ্বাসেরে কহে, — তুমি বড় ভাগ্যবান্ ৷  
তোমারে করিল দণ্ড প্রভু ভগবান্ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

viśvāsere kahe, — tumi baḍa bhāgyavān  
to Viśvāsa  He says  —  you  very  fortunate  
tomāre karila daṇḍa prabhu bhagavān  
you  has made  punishment  the Lord  Bhagavān

(38) He said to Viśvāsa, “You are most fortunate: the Lord, Bhagavān Himself, has punished you.

পূর্ব্বে মহাপ্রভু মোরে করেন সম্মান ৷  
দুঃখ পাই’ মনে আমি কৈলুঁ অনুমান ॥ ৩৯ ॥

pūrve mahāprabhu more karena sammāna  
before  Mahāprabhu  Me  would show  honour  
duḥkha pāi’ mane āmi kailum̐ anumāna  
pain  feeling  in mind  I  made  a plan

(39) In the past Mahāprabhu used to honour Me. This pained Me, and I thought of a way to change it.

মুক্তি — শ্রেষ্ঠ করি’ কৈনু বাশিষ্ঠ ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা প্রভু মোরে কৈল অপমান ॥ ৪০ ॥

mukti — śreṣṭha kari’ kainu vāśiṣṭha vyākhyāna  
liberation  —  the highest  making  I gave  of the Vāsiṣṭha  an exposition  
kruddha hañā prabhu more kaila apamāna  
angry  becoming  the Lord  Me  did  dishonour

(40) I expounded the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha*, extolling liberation as the highest goal, and the Lord grew angry and treated Me with dishonour.[^15]

দণ্ড পাঞা হৈল মোর পরম আনন্দ ৷  
যে দণ্ড পাইল ভাগ্যবান্ শ্রীমুকুন্দ ॥ ৪১ ॥

daṇḍa pāñā haila mora parama ānanda  
punishment  receiving  came  My  supreme  joy  
ye daṇḍa pāila bhāgyavān śrī-mukunda  
which  punishment  received  fortunate  Śrī Mukunda

(41) Being punished brought Me the greatest joy. Fortunate Śrī Mukunda received the same punishment,

যে দণ্ড পাইল শ্রীশচী ভাগ্যবতী ৷  
সে দণ্ড-প্রসাদ আর লোক পাবে কতি ॥ ৪২ ॥

ye daṇḍa pāila śrī-śacī bhāgyavatī  
which  punishment  received  Śrī  Śacī  the fortunate  
se daṇḍa-prasāda āra loka pābe kati  
that  punishment-mercy  other  people  will get  where

(42) and so did the fortunate Śrī Śacī. Where else could anyone find such mercy in the guise of punishment?”[^16]

এত কহি’ আচার্য্য তাঁরে করিয়া আশ্বাস ৷  
আনন্দিত হইয়া আইল মহাপ্রভু-পাশ ॥ ৪৩ ॥

eta kahi’ ācārya tām̐re kariyā āśvāsa  
so  saying  the Ācārya  him  making  reassurance  
ānandita haiyā āila mahāprabhu-pāśa  
joyful  becoming  came  to Mahāprabhu

(43) With these words the Ācārya comforted him, and then, joyful, He went to Mahāprabhu.

প্রভুকে কহেন — তোমার না বুঝি এ লীলা ৷  
আমা হৈতে প্রসাদপাত্র করিলা কমলা ॥ ৪৪ ॥

prabhuke kahena — tomāra nā bujhi e līlā  
to the Lord  He says  —  Your  not  I understand  this  pastime  
āmā haite prasāda-pātra karilā kamalā  
than Me  of mercy a vessel  You have made  Kamalā

(44) He said to the Lord, “I do not understand this pastime of Yours. You have made Kamalā a greater vessel of Your mercy than Me.

আমারেহ কভু যেই না হয় প্রসাদ ৷  
তোমার চরণে আমি কি কৈনু অপরাধ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

āmāreha kabhu yei nā haya prasāda  
to Me even  ever  such  not  is  mercy  
tomāra caraṇe āmi ki kainu aparādha  
at Your  feet  I  what  did  offence

(45) Such mercy You have never shown even Me. What offence have I committed at Your feet?”

এত শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু হাসিতে লাগিলা ৷  
বোলাইয়া কমলাকান্তে প্রসন্ন হইলা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

eta śuni’ mahāprabhu hāsite lāgilā  
this  hearing  Mahāprabhu  to laugh  began  
bolāiyā kamalākānte prasanna hailā  
having called  Kamalākānta  gracious  became

(46) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu began to laugh; He sent for Kamalākānta and was gracious to him.

আচার্য্য কহে, — ইহাকে কেনে দিলে দরশন ৷  
দুই প্রকারেতে করে মোরে বিড়ম্বন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ācārya kahe, — ihāke kene dile daraśana  
the Ācārya  says  —  to him  why  You gave  audience  
dui prakārete kare more viḍambana  
in two  ways  he makes  of Me  a mockery

(47) The Ācārya said, “Why have You let him see You again? He makes a mockery of Me in two ways.”[^17]

শুনিয়া প্রভুর মন প্রসন্ন হইল ৷  
দুঁহার অন্তর-কথা দুঁহে সে জানিল ॥ ৪৮ ॥

śuniyā prabhura mana prasanna haila  
hearing  the Lord’s  mind  pleased  became  
dum̐hāra antara-kathā dum̐he se jānila  
of the two  the inner words  the two  alone  knew

(48) The Lord was pleased at heart to hear this. Only the two of Them knew what lay within each other’s words.

প্রভু কহে, — বাউলিয়া, ঐছে কেনে কর ৷  
আচার্য্যের লজ্জা-ধর্ম্ম-হানি সে আচর ॥ ৪৯ ॥

prabhu kahe, — bāuliyā, aiche kene kara  
the Lord  says  —  madcap,  thus  why  you do  
ācāryera lajjā-dharma-hāni se ācara  
of the Ācārya  honour  dharma  harm  that  you practise

(49) The Lord said, “Madcap, why do you behave like this? What you do harms the Ācārya’s honour and His dharma.

প্রতিগ্রহ কভু না করিবে রাজধন ৷  
বিষয়ীর অন্ন খাইলে দুষ্ট হয় মন ॥ ৫০ ॥

pratigraha kabhu nā karibe rāja-dhana  
acceptance  ever  not  one should do  of a king’s wealth  
viṣayīra anna khāile duṣṭa haya mana  
of the worldly  food  if one eats  defiled  becomes  the mind

(50) A Vaiṣṇava ācārya must never accept a king’s wealth as a gift. If he eats the food of worldly men, his mind is defiled.[^18]

মন দুষ্ট হইলে নহে কৃষ্ণের স্মরণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণস্মৃতি বিনা হয় নিষ্ফল জীবন ॥ ৫১ ॥

mana duṣṭa haile nahe kṛṣṇera smaraṇa  
the mind  defiled  when it becomes  there is no  of Kṛṣṇa  remembrance  
kṛṣṇa-smṛti vinā haya niṣphala jīvana  
of Kṛṣṇa  remembrance  without  becomes  fruitless  life

(51) When the mind is defiled, one cannot remember Kṛṣṇa, and without remembrance of Kṛṣṇa life bears no fruit.

লোকলজ্জা হয়, ধর্ম্ম-কীর্ত্তি হয় হানি ৷  
ঐছে কর্ম্ম না করিহ কভু ইহা জানি’ ॥ ৫২ ॥

loka-lajjā haya, dharma-kīrti haya hāni  
before people  shame  arises,  dharma  and good name  are  harmed  
aiche karma nā kariha kabhu ihā jāni’  
such  deeds  not  do  ever  this  knowing

(52) It brings shame before the world and ruins one’s dharma and good name. Know this, and never do such a thing.”[^19]

এই শিক্ষা সবাকারে, সবে মনে কৈল ৷  
আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি মনে আনন্দ পাইল ॥ ৫৩ ॥

ei śikṣā sabākāre, sabe mane kaila  
this  teaching  for everyone,  all  in mind  took  
ācārya-gosāñi mane ānanda pāila  
Ācārya Gosāñi  in mind  joy  felt

(53) This teaching was meant for everyone, and everyone took it to heart; and Ācārya Gosāñi rejoiced within.

আচার্য্যের অভিপ্রায় প্রভুমাত্র বুঝে ৷  
প্রভুর গম্ভীর বাক্য আচার্য্য সমুঝে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

ācāryera abhiprāya prabhu-mātra bujhe  
the Ācārya’s  intention  the Lord  alone  understands  
prabhura gambhīra vākya ācārya samujhe  
the Lord’s  deep  words  the Ācārya  understands

(54) Only the Lord understood the Ācārya’s intention, and the Ācārya understood the Lord’s deep words.

এই ত’ প্রস্তাবে আছে বহুল বিচার ৷  
গ্রন্থ-বাহুল্যের ভয়ে নারি লিখিবার ॥ ৫৫ ॥

ei ta’ prastāve āche bahula vicāra  
in this  indeed  episode  there is  much  to consider  
grantha-bāhulyera bhaye nāri likhibāra  
of the book  the lengthening  for fear  I cannot  write

(55) There is much to consider in this episode, but I cannot write of it for fear of making the book too long.

শ্রীযদুনন্দনাচার্য্য — অদ্বৈতের শাখা ৷  
তাঁর শাখা-উপশাখা-গণের নাহি লেখা ॥ ৫৬ ॥

śrī-yadunandanācārya — advaitera śākhā  
Śrī  Yadunandana Ācārya  —  Advaita’s  branch  
tām̐ra śākhā-upaśākhā-gaṇera nāhi lekhā  
his  of branches  and offshoots  the host  there is no  record

(56) Śrī Yadunandana Ācārya is a branch of Advaita. His branches and offshoots are past all writing.

বাসুদেব দত্তের তেঁহো কৃপার ভাজন ৷  
সর্ব্বভাবে আশ্রিয়াছে চৈতন্য-চরণ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

vāsudeva dattera tem̐ho kṛpāra bhājana  
of Vāsudeva  Datta  he  of the mercy  recipient  
sarva-bhāve āśriyāche caitanya-caraṇa  
in every way  has taken shelter of  Caitanya’s  feet

(57) He received the mercy of Vāsudeva Datta and with all his heart took shelter of Śrī Caitanya’s feet.[^20]

ভাগবতাচার্য্য, আর বিষ্ণুদাসাচার্য্য ৷  
চক্রপাণি আচার্য্য, আর অনন্ত আচার্য্য ॥ ৫৮ ॥

bhāgavatācārya, āra viṣṇudāsācārya  
Bhāgavata Ācārya,  and  Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya  
cakrapāṇi ācārya, āra ananta ācārya  
Cakrapāṇi  Ācārya,  and  Ananta  Ācārya

(58) Other branches are Bhāgavata Ācārya, Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya, Cakrapāṇi Ācārya and Ananta Ācārya.[^21]

নন্দিনী, আর কামদেব, চৈতন্যদাস ৷  
দুর্ল্লভ বিশ্বাস, আর বনমালিদাস ॥ ৫৯ ॥

nandinī, āra kāmadeva, caitanya-dāsa  
Nandinī,  and  Kāmadeva,  Caitanya dāsa  
durlabha viśvāsa, āra vanamāli-dāsa  
Durlabha  Viśvāsa,  and  Vanamāli dāsa

(59) Then come Nandinī, Kāmadeva, Caitanya dāsa, Durlabha Viśvāsa and Vanamāli dāsa.

জগন্নাথ কর, আর কর ভবনাথ ৷  
হৃদয়ানন্দ সেন, আর দাস ভোলানাথ ॥ ৬০ ॥

jagannātha kara, āra kara bhavanātha  
Jagannātha  Kara,  and  Kara  Bhavanātha  
hṛdayānanda sena, āra dāsa bholānātha  
Hṛdayānanda  Sena,  and  dāsa  Bholānātha

(60) Jagannātha Kara, Bhavanātha Kara, Hṛdayānanda Sena and Bholānātha dāsa.

[^1]: Heading in the edition: “The servants of Advaita are of two kinds: those who take the essence (*sāra-grāhī*) and the empty.” *Anubhāṣya*: *sāra*, the essence, are the people of Gaurahari who follow Advaita; *asāra*, the empty, are those who merely fancy themselves His followers but have turned away from Gaurahari — Māyāvādīs devoid of pure devotion; those who hold the essence are the *bhāgavatas*, whose life is Gaura. According to the edition’s *Kathāsāra*, the author calls *sāra-grāhī* the Vaiṣṇavas who follow the view of Śrī Acyutānanda (12.73), and all the rest *asāra*.

[^2]: Heading in the edition: “By Gaura’s mercy only those servants of Advaita who take the essence flourish.”

[^3]: Heading in the edition: “Those who take the essence serve Gaura in loyalty to Advaita; the empty, going their own way, oppose Gaura.”

[^4]: Heading in the edition: “Loyalty to the Ācārya is the essence; anything else is empty.” *Sāra* is “essence, kernel”; *asāra*, “empty, without a kernel”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: those who walk the path the Ācārya taught are pure Vaiṣṇavas; those who, subject to fate, left it and invented a teaching of their own are *asāra*.

[^5]: Heading in the edition to 11–12: “Why the non-devotees who fancy themselves servants of Advaita are mentioned, and an example.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: we have no concern with the names of the *asāra*; yet, to keep the Vaiṣṇavas who take the essence apart from the empty, I name them together, as chaff is winnowed from the grain. Chaff (*pātanā*) is the empty paddy that holds no rice.

[^6]: In the edition the branches of Śrī Advaita are listed under numbered headings: (1) Acyutānanda, (2) Kṛṣṇa Miśra, (3) Gopāla, (4) Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, (5) Yadunandana Ācārya, (6)–(38) in 12.58–65; the offshoots of Gadādhara Paṇḍita are numbered (1)–(33), 12.79–87. *Anubhāṣya*: Acyutānanda was Advaita’s eldest son (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 9). Devoted to Mahāprabhu from childhood, he never married; later, in Purī, he took shelter of Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita, and the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* calls him Gadādhara’s disciple (some see in him Kārttikeya, others the gopī Acyutā).

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: in the year Mahāprabhu set His heart on going from Nīlācala to Vṛndāvana by way of Rāmakeli (1433–34 of the Śaka era), Acyutānanda was only five years old (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 4).

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Advaita-carita*): of Advaita’s six sons, three — Acyuta, Kṛṣṇa Miśra and Gopāla — served Śrī Gaurāṅga; they are called the three jewels born from the ocean of Sītā’s womb.

[^9]: The edition’s *Kathāsāra*: this was in Gopāla’s youth, at the cleansing of the Guṇḍicā temple. *Anubhāṣya*: see Madhya 12.143–149.

[^10]: The two Gosāñis are Mahāprabhu and Advaita.

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: Balarāma, Svarūpa and Jagadīśa turned away from Gaura; they were *smārtas* or Māyāvādīs, and therefore not Vaiṣṇavas (compare 12.1 and 12.10: “the empty”).

[^12]: The *Anubhāṣya* distinguishes Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, Advaita’s servant, from the brāhmaṇa Kamalākānta of Navadvīpa, whom Paramānanda Purī brought with him to Nīlācala (Madhya 10.94).

[^13]: Heading in the edition: “To take Nārāyaṇa, full in all six opulences, for a poor man, as though He were a *jīva*, is Māyāvāda, the creed of the *bāuls*.” *Anubhāṣya*: *īśvarera dainya kari’* — “making the Lord out to be poor”.

[^14]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Kamalākānta Viśvāsa was called “the madcap Viśvāsa” because his conclusions were like the ravings of a madman (*bāul*).

[^15]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: while explaining the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha*, Advaita on some pretext declared liberation higher than devotion. *Anubhāṣya*: the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha Rāmāyaṇa* teaches Māyāvāda — liberation as *nirvāṇa*, merging into Brahman — and is opposed to devotion to Viṣṇu; pure devotees therefore do not read it.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: for Advaita’s punishment see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 19; for Mukunda’s, Madhya 10; for Mother Śacī’s, Madhya 22.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: “He calls Me the transcendent Nārāyaṇa, yet in practice he takes Me for a poor worldly man begging for money.”

[^18]: Heading in the edition to 50–52: “The Lord sets out the duty of a Vaiṣṇava ācārya.”

[^19]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* to 49–53: though the Ācārya is Īśvara, His human pastime as the teacher of the world is well known, and for an ācārya to beg money from a king to pay a debt is shameless. Greed must be shunned in every way; a king is by nature a worldly man, and eating his food defiles the heart; with a defiled heart one does not remember Kṛṣṇa, and life passes without fruit. This is forbidden to all, and to ācāryas above all: one who gives instruction in the holy name for money is no teacher of the name but an offender against it.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: Yadunandana Ācārya was the initiating guru of Śrī Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī (Antya 6.160–169). Vāsudeva Datta is Madhuvrata, a singer of Vraja (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 140; see Ādi 10.41).

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: Bhāgavata Ācārya was first among Advaita’s followers and later among Gadādhara’s; he wrote the *Prema-taraṅgiṇī* (*Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta*, 6). Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya went with Acyutānanda to the festival at Kheturi. Ananta Ācārya is Sudevī, one of the eight sakhīs of Vraja; later he too joined the branch of Gadādhara (see 12.80).
