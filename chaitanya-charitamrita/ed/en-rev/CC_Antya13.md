“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 13  
**How Jagadānanda Went to Vṛndāvana**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

কৃষ্ণবিচ্ছেদজাতার্ত্ত্যা ক্ষীণে চাপি মনস্তনূ ৷  
দধাতে ফুল্লতাং ভাবৈর্যস্য তং গৌরমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-viccheda-jātārtyā  
Kṛṣṇa  from separation  born  by the pain  
kṣīṇe cāpi manas-tanū  
wasted  and  though  mind  and body  
dadhāte phullatāṁ bhāvair  
attain  blossoming  through ecstatic emotions  
yasya taṁ gauram āśraye  
whose  that  Gaura  I take shelter of

(1) I take shelter of Gaura, whose mind and body, though wasted by the pain born of separation from Kṛṣṇa, blossomed again through His ecstatic emotions.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^1]

হেনমতে মহাপ্রভু জগদানন্দ-সঙ্গে ৷  
নানামতে আস্বাদয় প্রেমের তরঙ্গে ॥ ৩ ॥

hena-mate mahāprabhu jagadānanda-saṅge  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  with Jagadānanda  in the company  
nānā-mate āsvādaya premera taraṅge  
in many  ways  tastes  of love  in the waves

(3) In this way, in the company of Jagadānanda, Mahāprabhu tasted the waves of love in many ways.

কৃষ্ণবিচ্ছেদে দুঃখে ক্ষীণ মন-কায় ৷  
ভাবাবেশে প্রভু কভু প্রফুল্লিত হয় ॥ ৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-vicchede duḥkhe kṣīṇa mana-kāya  
Kṛṣṇa  in separation  by sorrow  wasted  mind  and body  
bhāvāveśe prabhu kabhu praphullita haya  
in ecstatic absorption  the Lord  sometimes  blossoming  becomes

(4) His mind and body were wasted by the sorrow of separation from Kṛṣṇa, yet at times, absorbed in ecstatic emotion, the Lord would blossom again.

কলার শরলাতে শয়ন, অতি ক্ষীণ কায় ৷  
শরলাতে হাড় লাগে, ব্যথা হয় গায় ॥ ৫ ॥

kalāra śaralāte śayana, ati kṣīṇa kāya  
of the banana  on the bark  lying  very  thin  the body  
śaralāte hāḍa lāge, vyathā haya gāya  
against the bark  the bones  press  pain  is  in the body

(5) He lay on dried banana bark, and His body was very thin. His bones pressed against the bark, and His whole body ached.[^2]

দেখি’ সব ভক্তগণ মহাদুঃখ পায় ৷  
সহিতে নারে জগদানন্দ, সৃজিলা উপায় ॥ ৬ ॥

dekhi’ saba bhakta-gaṇa mahā-duḥkha pāya  
seeing  all  the devotees  great  sorrow  feel  
sahite nāre jagadānanda, sṛjilā upāya  
to bear  could not  Jagadānanda  devised  a means

(6) Seeing this, all the devotees were deeply grieved. Jagadānanda could not bear it, and he devised a remedy.

সূক্ষ্ম বস্ত্র আনি’ গেরি দিয়া রাঙ্গাইলা ৷  
শিমুলীর তুলা দিয়া তাহা পূরাইলা ॥ ৭ ॥

sūkṣma vastra āni’ geri diyā rāṅgāilā  
fine  cloth  bringing  with red ochre  with  dyed  
śimulīra tulā diyā tāhā pūrāilā  
of the śimula tree  cotton  with  it  stuffed

(7) He brought fine cloth, dyed it with red ochre and stuffed it with the cotton of the silk-cotton (*śimula*) tree.

এক তূলি-বালিশ গোবিন্দের হাতে দিলা ৷  
প্রভুরে শোয়াইহ ইহায় — তাহারে কহিলা ॥ ৮ ॥

eka tūli-bāliśa govindera hāte dilā  
one  mattress  pillow  of Govinda  into the hands  gave  
prabhure śoyāiha ihāya — tāhāre kahilā  
the Lord  have lie down  on this  to him  said

(8) He put a mattress and a pillow into Govinda’s hands and told him, “Have the Lord lie down on these.”

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞিকে কহে জগদানন্দ ৷  
আজি আপনে যাঞা প্রভুরে করাইহ শয়ন ॥ ৯ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñike kahe jagadānanda  
to Svarūpa Gosāñi  says  Jagadānanda  
āji āpane yāñā prabhure karāiha śayana  
today  yourself  going  the Lord  have lie down

(9) Jagadānanda said to Svarūpa Gosāñi, “Today please go yourself and have the Lord lie down on them.”

শয়নের কালে স্বরূপ তাঁহাই রহিলা ৷  
তূলি-বালিশ দেখি’ প্রভু ক্রোধাবিষ্ট হইলা ॥ ১০ ॥

śayanera kāle svarūpa tām̐hāi rahilā  
of lying down  at the time  Svarūpa  right there  stayed  
tūli-bāliśa dekhi’ prabhu krodhāviṣṭa hailā  
mattress  pillow  seeing  the Lord  filled with anger  became

(10) At bedtime Svarūpa stayed right there. But when the Lord saw the mattress and pillow, He became angry.

গোবিন্দেরে পুছেন, — ইহা করাইল কোন্ জন ?  
জগদানন্দের নাম শুনি’ সঙ্কোচ হৈল মন ॥ ১১ ॥

govindere puchena, — ihā karāila kon jana?  
Govinda  He asks  this  had made  which  person  
jagadānandera nāma śuni’ saṅkoca haila mana  
of Jagadānanda  the name  hearing  shrinking  became  the mind

(11) He asked Govinda, “Who had this made?” But when He heard Jagadānanda’s name, His heart shrank back in apprehension.

গোবিন্দেরে কহি’ সেই তূলি দূর কৈলা ৷  
কলার শরলা-উপর শয়ন করিলা ॥ ১২ ॥

govindere kahi’ sei tūli dūra kailā  
to Govinda  telling  that  mattress  away  put  
kalāra śaralā-upara śayana karilā  
of the banana  the bark  upon  lying down  did

(12) He had Govinda put the mattress away and lay down on the banana bark.

স্বরূপ কহে, — তোমার ইচ্ছা, কি কহিতে পারি ?  
শয্যা উপেক্ষিলে পণ্ডিত দুঃখ পাবে ভারী ॥ ১৩ ॥

svarūpa kahe, — tomāra icchā, ki kahite pāri?  
Svarūpa  says  Your  will  what  to say  can I  
śayyā upekṣile paṇḍita duḥkha pābe bhārī  
the bed  if You reject  the Paṇḍita  sorrow  will feel  heavy

(13) Svarūpa said, “It is Your will—what can I say? But if You reject the bed, the Paṇḍita will be deeply hurt.”

প্রভু কহেন, — খাট এক আনহ পাড়িতে ৷  
জগদানন্দ চাহে আমায় বিষয় ভুঞ্জাইতে ॥ ১৪ ॥

prabhu kahena, — khāṭa eka ānaha pāḍite  
the Lord  says  a bedstead  one  bring  to set up  
jagadānanda cāhe āmāya viṣaya bhuñjāite  
Jagadānanda  wants  Me  sense objects  to make enjoy

(14) The Lord said, “Then bring a bedstead too and set it up! Jagadānanda wants Me to enjoy worldly pleasures.[^3]

সন্ন্যাসী-মানুষ আমার ভূমিতে শয়ন ৷  
আমারে খাট-তূলি-বালিশ মস্তক মুণ্ডন ॥ ১৫ ॥

sannyāsī-mānuṣa āmāra bhūmite śayana  
a sannyāsī  man  My  on the ground  lying  
āmāre khāṭa-tūli-bāliśa mastaka muṇḍana  
for Me  bedstead  mattress  pillow  of the head  shaving

(15) I am a sannyāsī, and My place is on the ground. A bedstead, a mattress and a pillow would bring Me utter disgrace.”[^4]

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি আসি’ পণ্ডিতে কহিলা ৷  
শুনি’ জগদানন্দ মহাদুঃখ পাইলা ॥ ১৬ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi āsi’ paṇḍite kahilā  
Svarūpa Gosāñi  coming  to the Paṇḍita  told  
śuni’ jagadānanda mahā-duḥkha pāilā  
hearing  Jagadānanda  great  sorrow  felt

(16) Svarūpa Gosāñi came and told the Paṇḍita, and when Jagadānanda heard it, he was deeply grieved.

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি তবে সৃজিলা প্রকার ৷  
কদলীর শুষ্কপত্র আনিলা অপার ॥ ১৭ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi tabe sṛjilā prakāra  
Svarūpa Gosāñi  then  devised  a way  
kadalīra śuṣka-patra ānilā apāra  
of the banana  dry  leaves  brought  countless

(17) Then Svarūpa Gosāñi devised a way. He brought a great quantity of dry banana leaves.

নখে চিরি’ চিরি’ তাহা অতি সূক্ষ্ম কৈলা ৷  
প্রভুর বহির্ব্বাসেতে সে সব ভরিলা ॥ ১৮ ॥

nakhe ciri’ ciri’ tāhā ati sūkṣma kailā  
with his nails  tearing  tearing  them  very  fine  made  
prabhura bahirvāsete se saba bharilā  
of the Lord  into the outer cloths  them  all  filled

(18) Tearing them again and again with his nails, he shredded them very fine and filled the Lord’s outer cloths (*bahirvāsa*) with them.

এইমত দুই কৈলা ওড়ন-পাড়নে ৷  
অঙ্গীকার কৈলা প্রভু অনেক যতনে ॥ ১৯ ॥

ei-mata dui kailā oḍana-pāḍane  
in this way  two  made  covering and bedding  
aṅgīkāra kailā prabhu aneka yatane  
acceptance  made  the Lord  with much  effort

(19) In this way he made two pieces of bedding (*oḍana-pāḍana*), and only after much persuasion did the Lord accept them.[^5]

তাতে শয়ন করেন প্রভু, — দেখি’ সবে সুখী ৷  
জগদানন্দ — ভিতর-বাহিরে মহাদুঃখী ॥ ২০ ॥

tāte śayana karena prabhu, — dekhi’ sabe sukhī  
on them  lying  does  the Lord  seeing  all  happy  
jagadānanda — bhitara-bāhire mahā-duḥkhī  
Jagadānanda  inwardly and outwardly  very  unhappy

(20) The Lord lay on them, and seeing this, everyone was happy. But Jagadānanda remained deeply unhappy, inwardly and outwardly.[^6]

পূর্ব্বে জগদানন্দের ইচ্ছা বৃন্দাবন যাইতে ৷  
প্রভু আজ্ঞা না দেন তাঁরে, না পারে চলিতে ॥ ২১ ॥

pūrve jagadānandera icchā vṛndāvana yāite  
before  of Jagadānanda  the wish  to Vṛndāvana  to go  
prabhu ājñā nā dena tām̐re, nā pāre calite  
the Lord  permission  not  gives  to him  not  could  set out

(21) Jagadānanda had long wished to go to Vṛndāvana, but the Lord would not give him permission, and so he could not set out.

ভিতরের দুঃখ বাহ্যে প্রকাশ না কৈলা ৷  
মথুরা যাইতে প্রভু-স্থানে আজ্ঞা মাগিলা ॥ ২২ ॥

bhitarera duḥkha bāhye prakāśa nā kailā  
inner  sorrow  outwardly  display  not  made  
mathurā yāite prabhu-sthāne ājñā māgilā  
to Mathurā  to go  from the Lord  permission  asked

(22) Without showing his inner sorrow outwardly, he asked the Lord for permission to go to Mathurā.[^7]

প্রভু কহে, — মথুরা যাইবা আমায় ক্রোধ করি’ ৷  
আমায় দোষ লাগাঞা তুমি হইবা ভিখারী ॥ ২৩ ॥

prabhu kahe, — mathurā yāibā āmāya krodha kari’  
the Lord  says  to Mathurā  you will go  with Me  anger  making  
āmāya doṣa lāgāñā tumi haibā bhikhārī  
on Me  blame  laying  you  will become  a beggar

(23) The Lord said, “You will go to Mathurā angry with Me. Laying the blame on Me, you will become a beggar.”[^8]

জগদানন্দ কহে প্রভুর ধরিয়া চরণ ৷  
পূর্ব্ব হৈতে ইচ্ছা মোর যাইতে বৃন্দাবন ॥ ২৪ ॥

jagadānanda kahe prabhura dhariyā caraṇa  
Jagadānanda  says  the Lord’s  clasping  feet  
pūrva haite icchā mora yāite vṛndāvana  
from long  since  the wish  my  to go  to Vṛndāvana

(24) Clasping the Lord’s feet, Jagadānanda said, “For a long time I have wished to go to Vṛndāvana.

প্রভু-আজ্ঞা নাহি, তাতে না পারি যাইতে ৷  
এবে আজ্ঞা দেহ’, অবশ্য যাইমু নিশ্চিতে ॥ ২৫ ॥

prabhu-ājñā nāhi, tāte nā pāri yāite  
the Lord’s  permission  there was not  so  not  could  go  
ebe ājñā deha’, avaśya yāimu niścite  
now  permission  give  surely  I will go  certainly

(25) Without Your permission I could not go. Now please give me permission; I will surely go.”

প্রভু, প্রীতে তাঁর গমন না করেন অঙ্গীকার ৷  
তেঁহো প্রভুর ঠাঞি আজ্ঞা মাগে বার বার ॥ ২৬ ॥

prabhu, prīte tām̐ra gamana nā karena aṅgīkāra  
the Lord  out of love  his  going  not  does  accept  
tem̐ho prabhura ṭhāñi ājñā māge bāra bāra  
he  from the Lord  permission  asks  time  after time

(26) Out of love, the Lord would not agree to his going, but Jagadānanda begged Him for permission again and again.

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞিরে পণ্ডিত কৈলা নিবেদন ৷  
পূর্ব্ব হৈতে বৃন্দাবন যাইতে মোর মন ॥ ২৭ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñire paṇḍita kailā nivedana  
to Svarūpa Gosāñi  the Paṇḍita  made  a request  
pūrva haite vṛndāvana yāite mora mana  
from long  since  to Vṛndāvana  to go  my  heart

(27) The Paṇḍita appealed to Svarūpa Gosāñi: “For a long time my heart has been set on going to Vṛndāvana.

প্রভু-আজ্ঞা বিনা তাঁহা যাইতে না পারি ৷  
এবে আজ্ঞা না দেন মোরে, ক্রোধে যাহ’ বলি ॥ ২৮ ॥

prabhu-ājñā vinā tām̐hā yāite nā pāri  
the Lord’s  permission  without  there  to go  not  can  
ebe ājñā nā dena more, krodhe yāha’ bali  
now  permission  not  He gives  me  in anger  go  saying

(28) Without the Lord’s permission I cannot go there, and now He will not give me permission. He says, ‘You would go in anger.’

সহজেই মোর তাঁহা যাইতে মন হয় ৷  
প্রভু-আজ্ঞা লঞা দেহ’, করিয়ে বিনয় ॥ ২৯ ॥

sahajei mora tām̐hā yāite mana haya  
naturally  my  there  to go  the heart  is  
prabhu-ājñā lañā deha’, kariye vinaya  
the Lord’s  permission  obtaining  give  I make  humble request

(29) My heart is drawn there of its own accord. Please obtain the Lord’s permission for me—I humbly beg you.”

তবে স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি কহে প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
জগদানন্দের ইচ্ছা বড় যাইতে বৃন্দাবনে ॥ ৩০ ॥

tabe svarūpa-gosāñi kahe prabhura caraṇe  
then  Svarūpa Gosāñi  says  of the Lord  at the feet  
jagadānandera icchā baḍa yāite vṛndāvane  
of Jagadānanda  the wish  great  to go  to Vṛndāvana

(30) Then Svarūpa Gosāñi submitted at the Lord’s feet: “Jagadānanda greatly desires to go to Vṛndāvana.

তোমার ঠাঞি আজ্ঞা তেঁহো মাগে বার বার ৷  
আজ্ঞা দেহ’, — মথুরা দেখি’ আইসে একবার ॥ ৩১ ॥

tomāra ṭhāñi ājñā tem̐ho māge bāra bāra  
from You  permission  he  asks  time  after time  
ājñā deha’, — mathurā dekhi’ āise eka-bāra  
permission  give  Mathurā  seeing  let him come back  once

(31) He has asked You for permission again and again. Please let him go: let him see Mathurā once and come back.

আইরে দেখিতে যৈছে গৌড়দেশে যায় ৷  
তৈছে একবার বৃন্দাবন দেখি’ আয় ॥ ৩২ ॥

āire dekhite yaiche gauḍa-deśe yāya  
Mother  to see  just as  to the land of Gauḍa  he goes  
taiche eka-bāra vṛndāvana dekhi’ āya  
so  once  Vṛndāvana  seeing  let him come

(32) Just as he goes to Gauḍa to see Mother Śacī, so let him go once, see Vṛndāvana and return.”

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞির বোলে প্রভু আজ্ঞা দিলা ৷  
জগদানন্দে বোলাঞা তাঁরে শিখাইলা ॥ ৩৩ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñira bole prabhu ājñā dilā  
of Svarūpa Gosāñi  at the word  the Lord  permission  gave  
jagadānande bolāñā tām̐re śikhāilā  
Jagadānanda  calling  him  instructed

(33) At Svarūpa Gosāñi’s word the Lord gave His permission. He called Jagadānanda and instructed him:

বারাণসী পর্য্যন্ত স্বচ্ছন্দে যাইবা পথে ৷  
আগে সাবধানে যাইবা ক্ষত্রিয়াদি-সাথে ॥ ৩৪ ॥

vārāṇasī paryanta svacchande yāibā pathe  
Vārāṇasī  as far as  freely  you may go  on the road  
āge sāvadhāne yāibā kṣatriyādi-sāthe  
beyond  carefully  go  with kṣatriyas and others  together

(34) “As far as Vārāṇasī you may travel the road freely. Beyond that, go carefully, in the company of kṣatriyas and others.

কেবল গৌড়ীয়া পাইলে ‘বাটপাড়’ করি’ বান্ধে ৷  
সব লুটি’ বাঁধি’ রাখে, যাইতে বিরোধে ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kevala gauḍīyā pāile ‘bāṭapāḍa’ kari’ bāndhe  
only  a Gauḍīya  if they find  highway robbery  doing  they bind  
saba luṭi’ bām̐dhi’ rākhe, yāite virodhe  
all  plundering  bound  they keep  to go on  they prevent

(35) If they find a Gauḍīya alone, highwaymen (*bāṭapāḍa*) waylay him and bind him; they plunder everything, keep him bound and do not let him go on.[^9]

মথুরা গেলে সনাতন-সঙ্গে রহিবা ৷  
মথুরার স্বামী-সবের চরণ বন্দিবা ॥ ৩৬ ॥

mathurā gele sanātana-saṅge rahibā  
to Mathurā  having gone  with Sanātana  in the company  stay  
mathurāra svāmī-sabera caraṇa vandibā  
of Mathurā  the lords  all  the feet  honour

(36) When you reach Mathurā, stay with Sanātana, and honour the feet of all the lords of Mathurā.[^10]

দূরে রহি’ ভক্তি করিহ সঙ্গে না রহিবা ৷  
তাঁ-সবার আচার-চেষ্টা লইতে নারিবা ॥ ৩৭ ॥

dūre rahi’ bhakti kariha saṅge nā rahibā  
from afar  staying  devotion  show  in company  not  stay  
tām̐-sabāra ācāra-ceṣṭā laite nāribā  
their  all  conduct  behaviour  to adopt  you cannot

(37) Show them devotion, but from afar, and do not stay in their company: you could never accept their ways and conduct.[^11]

সনাতন-সঙ্গে করিহ বন দরশন ৷  
সনাতনের সঙ্গ না ছাড়িবা একক্ষণ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

sanātana-saṅge kariha vana daraśana  
with Sanātana  make  of the forests  visiting  
sanātanera saṅga nā chāḍibā eka-kṣaṇa  
of Sanātana  the company  not  leave  for one  moment

(38) Visit the forests with Sanātana, and do not leave his side for a moment.

শীঘ্র আসিহ, তাঁহা না রহিহ চিরকাল ৷  
গোবর্দ্ধনে না চড়িহ দেখিতে ‘গোপাল’ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

śīghra āsiha, tām̐hā nā rahiha cira-kāla  
quickly  come back  there  not  stay  for long  
govarddhane nā caḍiha dekhite ‘gopāla’  
Govardhana  not  climb  to see  Gopāla

(39) Come back quickly; do not stay there long. Do not climb Govardhana to see Gopāla.[^12]

আমিহ আসিতেছি, — কহিহ সনাতনে ৷  
আমার তরে একস্থান করে বৃন্দাবনে ॥ ৪০ ॥

āmiha āsitechi, — kahiha sanātane  
I too  am coming  tell  Sanātana  
āmāra tare eka-sthāna kare vṛndāvane  
for Me  a place  let him make  in Vṛndāvana

(40) Tell Sanātana, ‘I too am coming,’ and let him prepare a place for Me in Vṛndāvana.”[^13]

এত বলি’ জগদানন্দে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ৷  
জগদানন্দ চলিলা প্রভুর বন্দিয়া চরণ ॥ ৪১ ॥

eta bali’ jagadānande kailā āliṅgana  
this  saying  Jagadānanda  made  embrace  
jagadānanda calilā prabhura vandiyā caraṇa  
Jagadānanda  set out  the Lord’s  bowing to  feet

(41) Saying this, the Lord embraced Jagadānanda, who bowed at His feet and set out.

সব ভক্তগণ-ঠাঞি আজ্ঞা মাগিলা ৷  
বনপথে চলি’ চলি’ বারাণসী আইলা ॥ ৪২ ॥

saba bhakta-gaṇa-ṭhāñi ājñā māgilā  
all  the devotees  from  permission  asked  
vana-pathe cali’ cali’ vārāṇasī āilā  
by the forest  path  going  going  to Vārāṇasī  came

(42) He took leave of all the devotees, and travelling on and on by the forest path, he came to Vārāṇasī.

তপনমিশ্র, চন্দ্রশেখর, — দোঁহারে মিলিলা ৷  
তাঁর ঠাঞি প্রভুর কথা সকল শুনিলা ॥ ৪৩ ॥

tapana-miśra, candraśekhara, — dom̐hāre mililā  
Tapana Miśra  Candraśekhara  both  met  
tām̐ra ṭhāñi prabhura kathā sakala śunilā  
from him  of the Lord  the news  all  they heard

(43) There he met both Tapana Miśra and Candraśekhara, and they heard from him all the news of the Lord.

মথুরাতে আসি’ মিলিলা সনাতনে ৷  
দুইজনের সঙ্গে দুঁহে আনন্দিত মনে ॥ ৪৪ ॥

mathurāte āsi’ mililā sanātane  
in Mathurā  coming  met  Sanātana  
dui-janera saṅge dum̐he ānandita mane  
of the two  in the company  both  joyful  at heart

(44) Coming to Mathurā, he met Sanātana, and in each other’s company both were joyful at heart.

সনাতন করাইলা তাঁরে দ্বাদশ বন দরশন ৷  
গোকুলে রহিলা দুঁহে দেখি’ মহাবন ॥ ৪৫ ॥

sanātana karāilā tām̐re dvādaśa vana daraśana  
Sanātana  had made  him  twelve  forests  see  
gokule rahilā dum̐he dekhi’ mahāvana  
in Gokula  stayed  both  seeing  Mahāvana

(45) Sanātana showed him the twelve forests, and after seeing Mahāvana the two stayed in Gokula.

সনাতনের গোফাতে দুঁহে রহে একঠাঞি ৷  
পণ্ডিত পাক করেন দেবালয়ে যাই’ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

sanātanera gophāte dum̐he rahe eka-ṭhāñi  
of Sanātana  in the cave  both  stay  in one  place  
paṇḍita pāka karena devālaye yāi’  
the Paṇḍita  cooking  does  to a temple  going

(46) The two lived together in Sanātana’s cave, and the Paṇḍita would go to a temple to cook.[^14]

সনাতন ভিক্ষা করেন যাই’ মহাবনে ৷  
কভু দেবালয়ে, কভু ব্রাহ্মণ-সদনে ॥ ৪৭ ॥

sanātana bhikṣā karena yāi’ mahāvane  
Sanātana  begging  does  going  in Mahāvana  
kabhu devālaye, kabhu brāhmaṇa-sadane  
sometimes  at a temple  sometimes  of brāhmaṇas  at the homes

(47) Sanātana would go begging in Mahāvana—sometimes at a temple, sometimes at the homes of brāhmaṇas.

সনাতন পণ্ডিতের করে সমাধান ৷  
মহাবনে দেন আনি’ মাগি’ অন্ন-পান ॥ ৪৮ ॥

sanātana paṇḍitera kare samādhāna  
Sanātana  of the Paṇḍita  does  service  
mahāvane dena āni’ māgi’ anna-pāna  
in Mahāvana  gives  bringing  begging  food and drink

(48) Sanātana looked after all the Paṇḍita’s needs. In Mahāvana he would beg food and drink, bring it and give it to him.

একদিন সনাতনে পণ্ডিত নিমন্ত্রিলা ৷  
নিত্য-কৃত্য করি’ তেঁহ পাক চড়াইলা ॥ ৪৯ ॥

eka-dina sanātane paṇḍita nimantrilā  
one  day  Sanātana  the Paṇḍita  invited  
nitya-kṛtya kari’ tem̐ha pāka caḍāilā  
daily  duties  doing  he  the cooking  set on the fire

(49) One day the Paṇḍita invited Sanātana, and after finishing his daily duties he began to cook.

‘মুকুন্দ সরস্বতী, নাম সন্ন্যাসী মহাজনে ৷  
এক বহির্ব্বাস তেঁহো দিল সনাতনে ॥ ৫০ ॥

‘mukunda sarasvatī, nāma sannyāsī mahājane  
Mukunda  Sarasvatī  by name  a sannyāsī  a great soul  
eka bahirvāsa tem̐ho dila sanātane  
one  outer cloth  he  gave  to Sanātana

(50) A great sannyāsī named Mukunda Sarasvatī had given Sanātana an outer cloth (*bahirvāsa*).

সনাতন সেই বস্ত্র মস্তকে বান্ধিয়া ৷  
জগদানন্দের বাসা-দ্বারে বসিলা আসিয়া ॥ ৫১ ॥

sanātana sei vastra mastake bāndhiyā  
Sanātana  that  cloth  on his head  tying  
jagadānandera vāsā-dvāre vasilā āsiyā  
of Jagadānanda  at the lodging’s  door  sat  coming

(51) With that cloth tied around his head, Sanātana came and sat at the door of Jagadānanda’s lodging.[^15]

রাতুল বস্ত্র দেখি’ পণ্ডিত প্রেমাবিষ্ট হইলা ৷  
‘মহাপ্রভুর প্রসাদ’ জানি’ তাঁহারে পুছিলা ॥ ৫২ ॥

rātula vastra dekhi’ paṇḍita premāviṣṭa hailā  
red  cloth  seeing  the Paṇḍita  overwhelmed with love  became  
‘mahāprabhura prasāda’ jāni’ tām̐hāre puchilā  
of Mahāprabhu  the gift  thinking  him  asked

(52) Seeing the red cloth, the Paṇḍita was overwhelmed with love, for he took it to be Mahāprabhu’s gift (prasāda), and he asked him:

কাঁহা পাইলা তুমি এই রাতুল বসন ?  
‘মুকুন্দ-সরস্বতী’ দিল, — কহেন সনাতন ॥ ৫৩ ॥

kām̐hā pāilā tumi ei rātula vasana?  
where  did you get  you  this  red  cloth  
‘mukunda-sarasvatī’ dila, — kahena sanātana  
Mukunda Sarasvatī  gave  says  Sanātana

(53) “Where did you get this red cloth?” “Mukunda Sarasvatī gave it,” Sanātana replied.

শুনি’ পণ্ডিতের মনে ক্রোধ উপজিল ৷  
ভাতের হাণ্ডি হাতে হঞা মারিতে আইল ॥ ৫৪ ॥

śuni’ paṇḍitera mane krodha upajila  
hearing  of the Paṇḍita  in the mind  anger  arose  
bhātera hāṇḍi hāte hañā mārite āila  
of rice  the pot  in hand  being  to strike  came

(54) When he heard this, anger arose in the Paṇḍita’s mind, and with the rice pot in his hand he came forward to strike him.

সনাতন তাঁরে জানি’ লজ্জিত হইলা ৷  
বলিতে লাগিলা পণ্ডিত, হাণ্ডি চুলাতে ধরিলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

sanātana tām̐re jāni’ lajjita hailā  
Sanātana  him  understanding  abashed  became  
balite lāgilā paṇḍita, hāṇḍi culāte dharilā  
to speak  began  the Paṇḍita  the pot  on the stove  set

(55) Sanātana, understanding him, was abashed. The Paṇḍita set the pot back on the stove and began to speak:[^16]

তুমি মহাপ্রভুর হও পার্ষদ-প্রধান ৷  
তোমা-সম মহাপ্রভুর প্রিয় নাহি আন ॥ ৫৬ ॥

tumi mahāprabhura hao pārṣada-pradhāna  
you  of Mahāprabhu  are  of the associates  the chief  
tomā-sama mahāprabhura priya nāhi āna  
like you  to Mahāprabhu  dear  there is not  another

(56) “You are one of Mahāprabhu’s chief associates. No one else is as dear to Mahāprabhu as you.

অন্য সন্ন্যাসীর বস্ত্র তুমি ধর শিরে ৷  
কোন্ ঐছে হয়, — ইহা পারে সহিবারে ? ॥ ৫৭ ॥

anya sannyāsīra vastra tumi dhara śire  
another  sannyāsī’s  cloth  you  wear  on the head  
kon aiche haya, — ihā pāre sahibāre?  
who  such  is  this  can  tolerate

(57) Yet you wear another sannyāsī’s cloth on your head! Who could tolerate such a thing?”

সনাতন কহে — সাধু পণ্ডিত-মহাশয় !  
তোমা-সব চৈতন্যের প্রিয় কেহ নয় ॥ ৫৮ ॥

sanātana kahe — sādhu paṇḍita-mahāśaya!  
Sanātana  says  saintly  Paṇḍita  great soul  
tomā-saba caitanyera priya keha naya  
like you  to Caitanya  dear  anyone  is not

(58) Sanātana said, “O saintly Paṇḍita, great soul! No one is as dear to Caitanya as you.

ঐছে চৈতন্যনিষ্ঠা যোগ্য তোমাতে ৷  
তুমি না দেখাইলে ইহা শিখিমু কেমতে ? ॥ ৫৯ ॥

aiche caitanya-niṣṭhā yogya tomāte  
such  firm faith in Caitanya  befits  you  
tumi nā dekhāile ihā śikhimu ke-mate?  
you  not  had shown  this  shall I learn  how

(59) Such steadfast faith in Caitanya befits you. If you had not shown it, how would I ever learn it?[^17]

যাহা দেখিবারে বস্ত্র মস্তকে বান্ধিলুঁ ৷  
সেই অপূর্ব্ব প্রেম এই প্রত্যক্ষ দেখিলুঁ ॥ ৬০ ॥

yāhā dekhibāre vastra mastake bāndhilum̐  
which  in order to see  the cloth  on my head  I tied  
sei apūrva prema ei pratyakṣa dekhilum̐  
that  unprecedented  love  this  directly  I have seen

(60) I tied the cloth on my head in order to see this, and now I have seen that unprecedented love with my own eyes.

রক্তবস্ত্র ‘বৈষ্ণবের’ পরিতে না যুয়ায় ৷  
কোন প্রবাসীরে দিমু, কি কায উহায় ? ॥ ৬১ ॥

rakta-vastra ‘vaiṣṇavera’ parite nā yuyāya  
red cloth  for a Vaiṣṇava  to wear  not  is fitting  
kona pravāsīre dimu, ki kāya uhāya?  
to some  stranger  I shall give  what  use  in it

(61) It is not fitting for a Vaiṣṇava to wear red cloth. I shall give it to some stranger—what use is it to me?”[^18]

পাক করি’ জগদানন্দ চৈতন্যে সমর্পিলা ৷  
দুইজন বসি’ তবে প্রসাদ পাইলা ॥ ৬২ ॥

pāka kari’ jagadānanda caitanye samarpilā  
cooking  having done  Jagadānanda  to Caitanya  offered  
dui-jana vasi’ tabe prasāda pāilā  
the two  sitting  then  prasāda  took

(62) When he had finished cooking, Jagadānanda offered the food to Caitanya, and then the two sat down and honoured the prasāda.

প্রসাদ পাই দুইজনে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ৷  
চৈতন্যবিরহে দুঁহে করিলা ক্রন্দন ॥ ৬৩ ॥

prasāda pāi dui-jane kailā āliṅgana  
prasāda  taking  the two  made  embrace  
caitanya-virahe dum̐he karilā krandana  
from Caitanya  in separation  both  did  weeping

(63) After taking prasāda, the two embraced and wept in separation from Caitanya.

এইমত মাস দুই রহিলা বৃন্দাবনে ৷  
চৈতন্যবিরহ-দুঃখ না যায় সহনে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

ei-mata māsa dui rahilā vṛndāvane  
in this way  months  two  stayed  in Vṛndāvana  
caitanya-viraha-duḥkha nā yāya sahane  
from Caitanya  separation  the sorrow  not  could be  borne

(64) In this way he stayed in Vṛndāvana for two months, but the sorrow of separation from Caitanya was more than he could bear.

মহাপ্রভুর সন্দেশ কহিলা সনাতনে ৷  
আমিহ আসিতেছি, রহিতে করিহ একস্থানে ॥ ৬৫ ॥

mahāprabhura sandeśa kahilā sanātane  
of Mahāprabhu  the message  told  to Sanātana  
āmiha āsitechi, rahite kariha eka-sthāne  
I too  am coming  to stay  prepare  one place

(65) He gave Sanātana Mahāprabhu’s message: “I too am coming. Prepare a place for Me to stay.”

জগদানন্দ-পণ্ডিত তবে আজ্ঞা মাগিলা ৷  
সনাতন প্রভুরে কিছু ভেটবস্তু দিলা ॥ ৬৬ ॥

jagadānanda-paṇḍita tabe ājñā māgilā  
Jagadānanda Paṇḍita  then  leave  asked  
sanātana prabhure kichu bheṭa-vastu dilā  
Sanātana  for the Lord  some  gifts  gave

(66) Then Jagadānanda Paṇḍita asked leave to go, and Sanātana gave him some gifts for the Lord.

রাসস্থলীর বালু আর গোবর্দ্ধনের শিলা ৷  
শুষ্ক পক্ক পীলুফল আর গুঞ্জামালা ॥ ৬৭ ॥

rāsa-sthalīra vālu āra govarddhanera śilā  
of the rāsa  place  the sand  and  of Govardhana  a stone  
śuṣka pakka pīlu-phala āra guñjā-mālā  
dried  ripe  pīlu  fruits  and  guñjā  garland

(67) He sent sand from the site of the rāsa dance, a stone from Govardhana, dried ripe *pīlu* fruits and a garland of *guñjā* seeds.

জগদানন্দ-পণ্ডিত চলিলা সব লঞা ৷  
ব্যাকুল হৈলা সনাতন তাঁরে বিদায় দিয়া ॥ ৬৮ ॥

jagadānanda-paṇḍita calilā saba lañā  
Jagadānanda Paṇḍita  set out  all  taking  
vyākula hailā sanātana tām̐re vidāya diyā  
distraught  became  Sanātana  him  farewell  giving

(68) Jagadānanda Paṇḍita set out with all these things, and Sanātana was distraught as he bade him farewell.

প্রভুর নিমিত্ত একস্থান মনে বিচরিলা ৷  
দ্বাদশাদিত্য-টিলায় এক ‘মঠ’ পাইলা ॥ ৬৯ ॥

prabhura nimitta eka-sthāna mane vicarilā  
of the Lord  for the sake  one  place  in mind  considered  
dvādaśāditya-ṭilāya eka ‘maṭha’ pāilā  
on the Dvādaśāditya mound  one  maṭha  found

(69) Sanātana thought of a place for the Lord and found a temple (*maṭha*) on the Dvādaśāditya mound.[^19]

সেই স্থান রাখিলা গোসাঞি সংস্কার করিয়া ৷  
মঠের আগে রাখিলা এক চালি বান্ধিয়া ॥ ৭০ ॥

sei sthāna rākhilā gosāñi saṁskāra kariyā  
that  place  kept  the Gosāñi  repair  making  
maṭhera āge rākhilā eka cāli bāndhiyā  
of the temple  in front  set up  one  thatched shed  building

(70) The Gosāñi repaired that place and kept it ready, and in front of the temple he built a thatched shed.

শীঘ্র চলি’ নীলাচলে গেলা জগদানন্দ ৷  
ভক্ত সহ গোসাঞি হৈলা পরম আনন্দ ॥ ৭১ ॥

śīghra cali’ nīlācale gelā jagadānanda  
quickly  going  to Nīlācala  went  Jagadānanda  
bhakta saha gosāñi hailā parama ānanda  
the devotees  with  the Lord  became  supremely  joyful

(71) Jagadānanda went quickly back to Nīlācala, and the Lord, together with the devotees, was overjoyed.

প্রভুর চরণ বন্দি’ সবারে মিলিলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে দৃঢ় আলিঙ্গন কৈলা ॥ ৭২ ॥

prabhura caraṇa vandi’ sabāre mililā  
the Lord’s  feet  honouring  with all  he met  
mahāprabhu tām̐re dṛḍha āliṅgana kailā  
Mahāprabhu  him  tight  embrace  gave

(72) He bowed to the Lord’s feet and met everyone, and Mahāprabhu embraced him tightly.

সনাতনের নামে পণ্ডিত দণ্ডবৎ কৈলা ৷  
রাসস্থলীর ধূলি আদি সব ভেট দিলা ॥ ৭৩ ॥

sanātanera nāme paṇḍita daṇḍavat kailā  
Sanātana’s  in the name  the Paṇḍita  obeisance  made  
rāsa-sthalīra dhūli ādi saba bheṭa dilā  
of the place of the rāsa  dust  and so on  all  gifts  gave

(73) In Sanātana’s name the Paṇḍita offered obeisance to the Lord, and he presented all the gifts—dust from the site of the rāsa dance and the rest.

সব দ্রব্য রাখিলেন, পীলু দিলেন বাঁটিয়া ৷  
‘বৃন্দাবনের ফল’ বলি’ খাইলা হৃষ্ট হঞা ॥ ৭৪ ॥

saba dravya rākhilena, pīlu dilena bām̐ṭiyā  
all  the things  He kept,  the pīlu  He gave  distributing  
‘vṛndāvanera phala’ bali’ khāilā hṛṣṭa hañā  
of Vṛndāvana  fruit  saying  they ate  delighted  becoming

(74) The Lord kept all the gifts but distributed the *pīlu* fruits, and everyone ate them with delight, calling them “the fruit of Vṛndāvana.”

যে কেহ জানে, আঁটি চুষিতে লাগিল ৷  
যে না জানে গৌড়ীয়া, পীলু চাবাঞা খাইল ॥ ৭৫ ॥

ye keha jāne, ām̐ṭi cuṣite lāgila  
who  anyone  knew,  the stones  to suck  began  
ye nā jāne gauḍīyā, pīlu cāvāñā khāila  
who  not  knew,  the Gauḍīyās,  the pīlu  chewing  ate

(75) Those who knew the fruit began to suck the stones, but the Gauḍīyās who did not know it chewed the *pīlu* and swallowed it.

মুখে তার ঝাল গেল, জিহ্বা করে জ্বালা ৷  
বৃন্দাবনের ‘পীলু’ খাইতে এই এক লীলা ॥ ৭৬ ॥

mukhe tāra jhāla gela, jihvā kare jvālā  
in the mouth  their  pungency  went,  the tongue  does  burning  
vṛndāvanera ‘pīlu’ khāite ei eka līlā  
of Vṛndāvana  pīlu  in eating  this  one  līlā

(76) Their mouths were stung with pungency, and their tongues burned. Such was the pastime of eating the *pīlu* of Vṛndāvana.

জগদানন্দের আগমনে সবার উল্লাস ৷  
এইমতে নীলাচলে প্রভুর বিলাস ॥ ৭৭ ॥

jagadānandera āgamane sabāra ullāsa  
Jagadānanda’s  at the coming  of all  the joy  
ei-mate nīlācale prabhura vilāsa  
in this way  at Nīlācala  the Lord’s  pastimes

(77) Everyone rejoiced at Jagadānanda’s return. Thus the Lord enjoyed His pastimes at Nīlācala.

একদিন প্রভু যমেশ্বর-টোটা যাইতে ৷  
সেইকালে দেবদাসী লাগিলা গাইতে ॥ ৭৮ ॥

eka-dina prabhu yameśvara-ṭoṭā yāite  
one day  the Lord  to Yameśvara-ṭoṭā  while going  
sei-kāle deva-dāsī lāgilā gāite  
at that  time  a devadāsī  began  to sing

(78) One day, as the Lord was going to Yameśvara-ṭoṭā, a *devadāsī* of the temple began to sing.

গুর্জ্জরীরাগিণী লঞা সুমধুর-স্বরে ৷  
‘গীতগোবিন্দ’-পদ গায় জগমন হরে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

gurjjarī-rāgiṇī lañā sumadhura-svare  
the Gurjarī  rāgiṇī  taking  in a very sweet  voice  
‘gīta-govinda’-pada gāya jaga-mana hare  
of the Gīta-govinda  a song  she sings  of the world  the mind  steals

(79) Taking up the Gurjarī *rāgiṇī*, she was singing a song from the *Gīta-govinda* in a very sweet voice, and it captivated the mind of the whole world.

দূরে গান শুনি’ প্রভুর হইল আবেশ ৷  
স্ত্রী, পুরুষ, কে গায়, — না জানি’ বিশেষ ॥ ৮০ ॥

dūre gāna śuni’ prabhura haila āveśa  
from afar  the song  hearing  the Lord’s  arose  absorption  
strī, puruṣa, ke gāya, — nā jāni’ viśeṣa  
woman,  man,  who  sings  not  knowing  specifically

(80) Hearing the song from afar, the Lord was lost in ecstatic love and could not tell who was singing, a woman or a man.[^20]

তারে মিলিবারে প্রভু আবেশে ধাইলা ৷  
পথে ‘সিজের বাড়ী’ লয়, ফুটিয়া চলিলা ॥ ৮১ ॥

tāre milibāre prabhu āveśe dhāilā  
the singer  to meet  the Lord  in ecstasy  ran  
pathe ‘sijera bāḍī’ laya, phuṭiyā calilā  
on the way  sij  hedge  He takes,  piercing through  He went

(81) In His ecstasy the Lord ran to meet the singer. On the way He went straight into a hedge of *sij* shrubs and forced His way through it.[^21]

অঙ্গে কাঁটা লাগিল, কিছু না জানিলা !  
আস্তে-ব্যস্তে গোবিন্দ তাঁর পাছেতে ধাইলা ॥ ৮২ ॥

aṅge kām̐ṭā lāgila, kichu nā jānilā!  
in the body  thorns  pierced,  anything  not  He knew  
āste-vyaste govinda tām̐ra pāchete dhāilā  
in great haste  Govinda  Him  behind  ran

(82) Thorns pierced His body, but He knew nothing of it! In great haste Govinda ran after Him.

ধাঞা যায়েন প্রভু, স্ত্রী আছে অল্প দূরে ৷  
‘স্ত্রীগান’ বলি’ গোবিন্দ প্রভুরে কৈলা কোলে ॥ ৮৩ ॥

dhāñā yāyena prabhu, strī āche alpa dūre  
running  goes  the Lord,  the woman  is  a little  away  
‘strī-gāna’ bali’ govinda prabhure kailā kole  
a woman’s  song  saying  Govinda  the Lord  took  in his arms

(83) The Lord was running on, and the woman was only a little way off, when Govinda caught the Lord in his arms, crying, “It is a woman singing!”

স্ত্রী-নাম শুনি’ প্রভুর বাহ্য হইলা ৷  
পুনরপি সেই পথে বাহুড়ি’ চলিলা ॥ ৮৪ ॥

strī-nāma śuni’ prabhura bāhya hailā  
woman  the word  hearing  the Lord’s  external awareness  came  
punarapi sei pathe bāhuḍi’ calilā  
again  by that  way  turning back  went

(84) Hearing the word “woman,” the Lord came back to external consciousness and returned the way He had come.[^22]

প্রভু কহে, — গোবিন্দ, আজি রাখিলা জীবন ৷  
স্ত্রী-পরশ হৈলে আমার হইত মরণ ॥ ৮৫ ॥

prabhu kahe, — govinda, āji rākhilā jīvana  
the Lord  says  Govinda,  today  you saved  life  
strī-paraśa haile āmāra haita maraṇa  
woman’s  touch  if it had been  My  would have been  death

(85) The Lord said, “Govinda, today you have saved My life. Had I touched a woman, it would have been My death.[^23]

এ ঋণ শোধিতে আমি নারিমু তোমার ৷  
গোবিন্দ কহে, — জগন্নাথ রাখেন মুই কোন্ ছার ? ॥ ৮৬ ॥

e ṛṇa śodhite āmi nārimu tomāra  
this  debt  to repay  I  shall not be able  to you  
govinda kahe, — jagannātha rākhena mui kon chāra?  
Govinda  says  Jagannātha  protects  I  what  insignificant one

(86) I shall never be able to repay this debt to you.” Govinda replied, “It is Jagannātha who protects. What am I? I am nothing.”

প্রভু কহে, — গোবিন্দ, মোর সঙ্গে রহিবা ৷  
যাঁহা তাঁহা মোর রক্ষায় সাবধান হইবা ॥ ৮৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — govinda, mora saṅge rahibā  
the Lord  says  Govinda,  Me  with  stay  
yām̐hā tām̐hā mora rakṣāya sāvadhāna haibā  
wherever  there  My  in protecting  careful  be

(87) The Lord said, “Govinda, stay with Me. Wherever I go, be watchful and protect Me.”

এত বলি’ লেউটি’ প্রভু গেলা নিজ-স্থানে ৷  
শুনি’ মহা-ভয় পাইলা স্বরূপাদি-মনে ॥ ৮৮ ॥

eta bali’ leuṭi’ prabhu gelā nija-sthāne  
this  saying  turning back  the Lord  went  to His own  place  
śuni’ mahā-bhaya pāilā svarūpādi-mane  
hearing  great  fear  felt  Svarūpa and others  in mind

(88) Saying this, the Lord turned back and went to His residence. Hearing of it, Svarūpa and the others were greatly alarmed at heart.

এথা তপনমিশ্র-পুত্র রঘুনাথ-ভট্টাচার্য্য ৷  
প্রভুরে দেখিতে চলিলা ছাড়ি’ সর্ব্ব কার্য্য ॥ ৮৯ ॥

ethā tapana-miśra-putra raghunātha-bhaṭṭācārya  
meanwhile  Tapana  Miśra’s  son  Raghunātha  Bhaṭṭācārya  
prabhure dekhite calilā chāḍi’ sarva kārya  
the Lord  to see  set out  leaving  all  affairs

(89) Meanwhile Raghunātha Bhaṭṭācārya, the son of Tapana Miśra, left all his affairs and set out to see the Lord.[^24]

কাশী হৈতে চলিলা তেঁহো গৌড় পথ দিয়া ৷  
সঙ্গে সেবক চলে তাঁর ঝালি বহিয়া ॥ ৯০ ॥

kāśī haite calilā tem̐ho gauḍa patha diyā  
Kāśī  from  set out  he  of Gauḍa  the road  by  
saṅge sevaka cale tām̐ra jhāli vahiyā  
with him  a servant  goes  his  bag  carrying

(90) He set out from Kāśī by the road through Gauḍa, and a servant went with him, carrying his bag.[^25]

পথে তারে মিলিলা বিশ্বাস-রামদাস ৷  
বিশ্বাসখানার কায়স্থ তেঁহো রাজার বিশ্বাস ॥ ৯১ ॥

pathe tāre mililā viśvāsa-rāmadāsa  
on the way  him  met  Viśvāsa  Rāmadāsa  
viśvāsa-khānāra kāyastha tem̐ho rājāra viśvāsa  
of the Viśvāsa-khānā  a kāyastha  he  of the king  trusted officer

(91) On the way he was met by Rāmadāsa Viśvāsa, a *kāyastha* of the *Viśvāsa-khānā* and a trusted officer of the king.[^26]

সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে প্রবীণ, কাব্যপ্রকাশ-অধ্যাপক ৷  
পরমবৈষ্ণব, রঘুনাথ-উপাসক ॥ ৯২ ॥

sarva-śāstre pravīṇa, kāvya-prakāśa-adhyāpaka  
in all  scriptures  expert,  of the Kāvya-prakāśa  teacher  
parama-vaiṣṇava, raghunātha-upāsaka  
a great  Vaiṣṇava,  of Raghunātha  worshipper

(92) He was learned in all the scriptures and taught the *Kāvya-prakāśa*; he was a great Vaiṣṇava and a worshipper of Raghunātha (Rāma).[^27]

অষ্টপ্রহর রামনাম জপেন রাত্রি-দিনে ৷  
সর্ব্ব ত্যজি’ চলিলা জগন্নাথ-দরশনে ॥ ৯৩ ॥

aṣṭa-prahara rāma-nāma japena rātri-dine  
eight  praharas  Rāma’s  name  he chants  night  and day  
sarva tyaji’ calilā jagannātha-daraśane  
all  giving up  he set out  Jagannātha  to see

(93) Night and day, through all eight *praharas*, he chanted the name of Rāma. Giving up everything, he had set out to see Jagannātha.

রঘুনাথ-ভট্টের সনে পথেতে মিলিলা ৷  
ভট্টের ঝালি মাথে করি’ বহিয়া চলিলা ॥ ৯৪ ॥

raghunātha-bhaṭṭera sane pathete mililā  
Raghunātha  Bhaṭṭa  with  on the way  he met  
bhaṭṭera jhāli māthe kari’ vahiyā calilā  
the Bhaṭṭa’s  bag  on his head  putting  carrying  went

(94) Meeting Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa on the way, he carried the Bhaṭṭa’s bag on his head as they went.

নানা সেবা করি’ করে পাদ-সম্বাহন ৷  
তাতে রঘুনাথের হয় সঙ্কুচিত মন ॥ ৯৫ ॥

nānā sevā kari’ kare pāda-samvāhana  
various  services  doing  does  feet  massaging  
tāte raghunāthera haya saṅkucita mana  
by that  Raghunātha’s  becomes  embarrassed  mind

(95) He rendered various services and massaged his feet, and this made Raghunātha uneasy at heart.

তুমি বড় লোক, পণ্ডিত, মহাভাগবতে ৷  
সেবা না করিহ, সুখে চল মোর সাথে ॥ ৯৬ ॥

tumi baḍa loka, paṇḍita, mahā-bhāgavate  
you  a great  man,  a paṇḍita,  a great  bhāgavata  
sevā nā kariha, sukhe cala mora sāthe  
service  not  do,  happily  walk  me  with

(96) “You are a great man, a *paṇḍita*, a *mahā-bhāgavata*. Do not serve me; just walk happily with me.”

রামদাস কহে, — আমি শূদ্র অধম !  
‘ব্রাহ্মণের সেবা’, — এই মোর নিজ-ধর্ম্ম ॥ ৯৭ ॥

rāmadāsa kahe, — āmi śūdra adhama!  
Rāmadāsa  says  I  a śūdra  lowly  
‘brāhmaṇera sevā’, — ei mora nija-dharma  
of the brāhmaṇa  service  this  my  own  dharma

(97) Rāmadāsa said, “I am a lowly *śūdra*! To serve a brāhmaṇa is my own *dharma*.

সঙ্কোচ না কর তুমি, আমি — তোমার ‘দাস’ ৷  
তোমার সেবা করিলে হয় হৃদয়ে উল্লাস ॥ ৯৮ ॥

saṅkoca nā kara tumi, āmi — tomāra ‘dāsa’  
hesitation  not  do  you,  I  your  servant  
tomāra sevā karile haya hṛdaye ullāsa  
your  service  doing  there is  in the heart  joy

(98) Do not feel uneasy: I am your servant. When I serve you, my heart is filled with joy.”

এত বলি’ ঝালি বহেন, করেন সেবনে ৷  
রঘুনাথের তারকমন্ত্র জপেন রাত্রি-দিনে ॥ ৯৯ ॥

eta bali’ jhāli vahena, karena sevane  
this  saying  the bag  he carries,  does  service  
raghunāthera tāraka-mantra japena rātri-dine  
Raghunātha’s  delivering  mantra  he chants  night  and day

(99) Saying this, he carried the bag and served him, and night and day he chanted the *tāraka-mantra* of Raghunātha (Rāma), the mantra that delivers.

এইমতে রঘুনাথ আইলা নীলাচলে ৷  
প্রভুর চরণে যাঞা মিলিলা কুতূহলে ॥ ১০০ ॥

ei-mate raghunātha āilā nīlācale  
in this way  Raghunātha  came  to Nīlācala  
prabhura caraṇe yāñā mililā kutūhale  
the Lord’s  at the feet  going  met  with eager joy

(100) In this way Raghunātha came to Nīlācala; he went to the Lord’s feet and met Him with eager joy.

দণ্ডপরণাম করি’ ভট্ট পড়িলা চরণে ৷  
প্রভু ‘রঘুনাথ’ বলি’ কৈলা আলিঙ্গনে ॥ ১০১ ॥

daṇḍa-paraṇāma kari’ bhaṭṭa paḍilā caraṇe  
rod-like  obeisance  making  the Bhaṭṭa  fell  at the feet  
prabhu ‘raghunātha’ bali’ kailā āliṅgane  
the Lord  Raghunātha  saying  did  embrace

(101) The Bhaṭṭa fell flat at His feet in obeisance, and the Lord embraced him, calling him by name: “Raghunātha!”[^28]

মিশ্র আর শেখরের দণ্ডবৎ জানাইলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভু তাঁ-সবার বার্ত্তা পুছিলা ॥ ১০২ ॥

miśra āra śekharera daṇḍavat jānāilā  
of Miśra  and  Śekhara  the obeisances  he conveyed  
mahāprabhu tām̐-sabāra vārtā puchilā  
Mahāprabhu  of them  all  news  asked

(102) He conveyed the obeisances of Miśra and Śekhara, and Mahāprabhu asked for news of them all.

ভাল হইল আইলা দেখ ‘কমললোচন’ ৷  
আজি আমার এথা করিবা প্রসাদ ভোজন ॥ ১০৩ ॥

bhāla haila āilā dekha ‘kamala-locana’  
good  it is  you came,  see  the lotus-eyed (Jagannātha)  
āji āmāra ethā karibā prasāda bhojana  
today  My  here  you will do  prasāda  eating

(103) “It is good that you have come. Go and see the lotus-eyed Lord (Jagannātha), and today you will take prasāda here with Me.”

গোবিন্দেরে কহি’ এক বাসা দেওয়াইলা ৷  
স্বরূপাদি ভক্তগণ-সনে মিলাইলা ॥ ১০৪ ॥

govindere kahi’ eka vāsā deoyāilā  
to Govinda  telling  a  lodging  had given  
svarūpādi bhakta-gaṇa-sane milāilā  
Svarūpa and others  devotees  with  He introduced

(104) Through Govinda the Lord arranged lodging for him and introduced him to Svarūpa and the other devotees.

এইমত প্রভু-সঙ্গে রহিলা অষ্টমাস ৷  
দিনে দিনে প্রভুর কৃপা বাড়য়ে উল্লাস ॥ ১০৫ ॥

ei-mata prabhu-saṅge rahilā aṣṭa-māsa  
in this way  the Lord  with  stayed  eight  months  
dine dine prabhura kṛpā bāḍaye ullāsa  
day  by day  the Lord’s  mercy  increases  joy

(105) In this way he stayed with the Lord for eight months, and day by day the Lord’s mercy and his joy increased.[^29]

[^11]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in their pure parental love for Kṛṣṇa they behave in ways contrary to the views of the *smārtas*, and you, devoted to the mood of majesty, might come to disrespect them; but their bhakti is *rāgātmikā*, and there must be no disrespect for the residents of Vraja, so honour them with devotion from afar.

[^12]: Heading in the edition: the prohibition against climbing Govardhana, who is non-different from Kṛṣṇa. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: if one lives in Vraja for long, one begins to see faults in its residents and one’s faith weakens; those not yet on the path of rāga should visit Vraja and soon return. Govardhana is Bhagavān Himself, so one must not climb it; it is better to have darśana of Gopāla when He goes elsewhere.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: no authoritative book says that the Lord went to Vṛndāvana again; but since in verse 70 Sanātana repairs the place chosen for Mahāprabhu, one may infer that He may later have gone there again.

[^14]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (on 46–47): Sanātana then lived on pieces of flatbread received as *mādhukarī* alms, while Jagadānanda could not do without rice day after day and so went to cook at a temple: the temples of Vraja did not cook rice and dal as prasāda.

[^15]: Heading in the edition: wearing on his head the cloth given by a sannyāsī, Sanātana comes to the Paṇḍita’s lodging.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: *jāni*, “making him understand,” or “knowing that the Paṇḍita was full of love for Gaura.”

[^17]: Heading in the edition (on 58): the humility of Sanātana Gosvāmī, who seeks no honour and honours others, and his praise of the Paṇḍita’s steadfast love for Gaura.

[^18]: Heading in the edition: a paramahaṁsa on the path of rāga is forbidden to wear saffron cloth. *Anubhāṣya*: Vaiṣṇavas are paramahaṁsas and *akiñcanas*, possessing nothing but Kṛṣṇa, and need not put on the saffron dress of sannyāsīs to show their status; moreover, Śrī Gaurahari Himself accepted the dress of an *ekadaṇḍī* sannyāsī, and His servants, mindful of the difference between the Lord and His servant, do not think it fitting to resemble Him in dress; while Vaiṣṇavas who accept sannyāsa under the shelter of a guru who is a Vaiṣṇava paramahaṁsa often wear the saffron dress of the fourth āśrama out of humility, thinking themselves unworthy to be called paramahaṁsas.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: *maṭha*, a temple. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Dvādaśāditya-ṭilā is a high mound on which stands the old, ruined temple of Śrī Madana-mohana; in Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes the twelve Ādityas rose there.

[^20]: Heading in the edition: hearing a song about Kṛṣṇa, the Lord, in half-external consciousness and overwhelmed by love, sees in her singing a transcendental service to Kṛṣṇa and runs towards her.

[^21]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1006) reads *laya*, “He takes (the way through the hedge)”; another reading is *haya*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in Utkala a flower garden is called *phul-bāḍī*; *sij* shrubs grow there—*manasā-sij* and thorny *sij*—and so a hedge of them is called *sijera bāḍī*; *bāḍī* here means “a hedge.”

[^22]: Heading in the edition: the Lord, filled with the mood of the *āśraya* (the abode of love), is the *ācārya*, the teacher of the world; the doctrine of “Gaura-nāgara” is refuted here; the Lord turns back.

[^23]: Heading in the edition: to touch a woman or keep company with her is death for an *ācārya* and a preacher, and so it must be avoided in every way; by thanking Govinda, the Lord teaches this lesson.

[^24]: Heading in the edition: the story of Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī, who from his youth kept the great vow of a lifelong celibate (*naiṣṭhika-brahmacārī*).

[^25]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1007) reads *vahiyā*, “carrying”; another reading is *sājāñā*, “having packed.”

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Kāvya-prakāśa* is a famous treatise on poetics by Mammaṭa Bhaṭṭa. Heading in the edition: Rāmadāsa belonged to the Rāmānandī *sampradāya* (he was a Rāmāyat). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: one who at heart desires liberation is not counted among pure Vaiṣṇavas; as a worshipper of Rāma, Rāmadāsa was “almost a Vaiṣṇava,” but in those days few could distinguish the degrees of pure Vaiṣṇavas, and so he was known in the world as “a great Vaiṣṇava.”
