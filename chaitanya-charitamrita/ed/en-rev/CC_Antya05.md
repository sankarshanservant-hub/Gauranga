“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 5  
**The Story of Pradyumna Miśra**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বৈগুণ্যকীটকলিতঃ পৈশুন্য-ব্রণপীড়িতঃ ৷  
দৈন্যার্ণবে নিমগ্নোঽহং চৈতন্য-বৈদ্যমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ১ ॥

vaiguṇya-kīṭa-kalitaḥ  
by the ripening of karma  by the worm  bitten  
paiśunya-vraṇa-pīḍitaḥ  
of malice  by the sore  afflicted  
dainyārṇave nimagno ’haṁ  
in the ocean of humility  sunk  I  
caitanya-vaidyam āśraye  
Caitanya  the physician  I take shelter of

(1) Bitten by the worm of ripened karma, tormented by the sore of malice, and sunk in an ocean of humility, I take shelter of Caitanya, the physician.[^1]

জয় জয় শচীসুত শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
জয় জয় কৃপাময় নিত্যানন্দ ধন্য ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śacīsuta śrīkṛṣṇacaitanya  
glory  glory  to the son of Śacī  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
jaya jaya kṛpāmaya nityānanda dhanya  
glory  glory  to the all-merciful  Nityānanda  the blessed

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the son of Śacī! Glory, glory to the all-merciful, blessed Nityānanda!

জয়াদ্বৈত কৃপাসিন্ধু জয় ভক্তগণ ৷  
জয় স্বরূপ, গদাধর, রূপ, সনাতন ॥ ৩ ॥

jayādvaita kṛpā-sindhu jaya bhakta-gaṇa  
glory to Advaita  of mercy  the ocean  glory  to the devotees  all  
jaya svarūpa, gadādhara, rūpa, sanātana  
glory  to Svarūpa  Gadādhara  Rūpa  Sanātana

(3) Glory to Advaita, the ocean of mercy! Glory to all the devotees! Glory to Svarūpa, Gadādhara, Rūpa and Sanātana!

একদিন প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ করি’ কিছু করে নিবেদনে ॥ ৪ ॥

ekadina pradyumna-miśra prabhura caraṇe  
one day  Pradyumna Miśra  of the Lord  at the feet  
daṇḍavat kari’ kichu kare nivedane  
obeisance  making  something  he makes  submission

(4) One day Pradyumna Miśra bowed down at the Lord’s feet and humbly made a request.

শুন, প্রভু, মুঞি দীন গৃহস্থ অধম !  
কোন ভাগ্যে পাঞাছোঁ তোমার দুর্ল্লভ চরণ ॥ ৫ ॥

śuna, prabhu, muñi dīna gṛhastha adhama!  
hear  O Lord  I  wretched  householder  lowly  
kona bhāgye pāñāchom̐ tomāra durlabha caraṇa  
by what  fortune  I have obtained  Your  rare  feet

(5) “Hear me, O Lord: I am a wretched, lowly householder. By what fortune have I obtained Your feet, so rarely attained?

কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিবারে মোর ইচ্ছা হয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথা কহ মোরে হঞা সদয় ॥ ৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kathā śunibāre mora icchā haya  
about Kṛṣṇa talks  to hear  my  desire  is  
kṛṣṇa-kathā kaha more hañā sadaya  
about Kṛṣṇa talks  tell  me  becoming  kind

(6) I wish to hear talks about Kṛṣṇa. Be kind to me and tell me about Kṛṣṇa.”

প্রভু কহেন, — কৃষ্ণকথা আমি নাহি জানি ৷  
সবে রামানন্দ জানে, তাঁর মুখে শুনি ॥ ৭ ॥

prabhu kahena, — kṛṣṇa-kathā āmi nāhi jāni  
the Lord  says  about Kṛṣṇa talks  I  not  know  
sabe rāmānanda jāne, tām̐ra mukhe śuni  
only  Rāmānanda  knows  his  from the mouth  I hear

(7) The Lord said, “I know nothing of talks about Kṛṣṇa. Only Rāmānanda knows them, and I hear them from his lips.[^2]

ভাগ্যে তোমার কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিতে হয় মন ৷  
রামানন্দ-পাশ যাই’ করহ শ্রবণ ॥ ৮ ॥

bhāgye tomāra kṛṣṇa-kathā śunite haya mana  
by fortune  your  about Kṛṣṇa talks  to hear  is  the mind  
rāmānanda-pāśa yāi’ karaha śravaṇa  
to Rāmānanda  going  do  hearing

(8) It is your good fortune that your mind longs to hear about Kṛṣṇa. Go to Rāmānanda and hear from him.

কৃষ্ণকথায় রুচি তোমার — বড় ভাগ্যবান্ ৷  
যার কৃষ্ণকথায় রুচি, সেই ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kathāya ruci tomāra — baḍa bhāgyavān  
for talks about Kṛṣṇa  taste  your  very  fortunate  
yāra kṛṣṇa-kathāya ruci, sei bhāgyavān  
whose  for talks about Kṛṣṇa  taste  he  fortunate

(9) You have a taste for talks about Kṛṣṇa — you are very fortunate. One who has a taste for talks about Kṛṣṇa is truly fortunate.”

ধর্ম্মঃ স্বনুষ্ঠিতঃ পুংসাং বিষ্বক্সেনকথাসু যঃ ৷  
নোৎপাদয়েদ্যদি রতিং শ্রম এব হি কেবলম্ ॥ ১০ ॥

dharmaḥ sv-anuṣṭhitaḥ puṁsāṁ  
dharma  well performed  of people  
viṣvaksena-kathāsu yaḥ  
of Viṣvaksena  for the narrations  which  
notpādayed yadi ratiṁ  
not  awakens  if  attraction  
śrama eva hi kevalam  
labor  only  certainly  merely

(10) “Dharma, however well a person performs it, is nothing but wasted labour if it does not awaken attraction (*rati*) to the narrations about the Lord, Viṣvaksena.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.8)[^3]

তবে প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র গেলা রামানন্দের স্থানে ৷  
রায়ের সেবক তাঁরে বসাইল আসনে ॥ ১১ ॥

tabe pradyumna-miśra gelā rāmānandera sthāne  
then  Pradyumna Miśra  went  Rāmānanda’s  to the place  
rāyera sevaka tām̐re vasāila āsane  
Rāya’s  servant  him  seated  on a seat

(11) Then Pradyumna Miśra went to Rāmānanda’s house, and Rāya’s servant offered him a seat.

রায়ের দর্শন না পাঞা সেবকে পুছিল ৷  
রায়ের বৃত্তান্ত সেবক কহিতে লাগিল ॥ ১২ ॥

rāyera darśana nā pāñā sevake puchila  
Rāya’s  sight  not  getting  the servant  he asked  
rāyera vṛttānta sevaka kahite lāgila  
Rāya’s  doings  the servant  to tell  began

(12) Not finding Rāya there, he asked the servant, and the servant told him what Rāya was doing:

দুই দেব-কন্যা হয় পরম-সুন্দরী ৷  
নৃত্য-গীতে সুনিপুণা, বয়সে কিশোরী ॥ ১৩ ॥

dui deva-kanyā haya parama-sundarī  
two  maidens of the Deity  there are  supremely beautiful  
nṛtya-gīte sunipuṇā, vayase kiśorī  
in dance and song  very skilled  in age  in early youth

(13) “There are two maidens of the Deity (*deva-kanyās*), supremely beautiful, very skilled in dance and song, and in the bloom of youth.[^4]

সেই দুঁহে লঞা রায় নিভৃত উদ্যানে ৷  
নিজ-নাটক-গীতের শিখায় নর্ত্তনে ॥ ১৪ ॥

sei dum̐he lañā rāya nibhṛta udyāne  
those  two  taking  Rāya  in a secluded  garden  
nija-nāṭaka-gītera śikhāya nartane  
of his own drama’s songs  he teaches  dancing

(14) Rāya has taken them both to a secluded garden and is teaching them to dance to the songs of his own drama.[^5]

তুমি ইঁহা বসি’ রহ, ক্ষণেকে আসিবেন ৷  
তাঁরে যেই আজ্ঞা দেহ’, সেই করিবেন ॥ ১৫ ॥

tumi im̐hā vasi’ raha, kṣaṇeke āsibena  
you  here  sitting  stay  in a moment  he will come  
tām̐re yei ājñā deha’, sei karibena  
to him  whatever  order  you give  that  he will do

(15) Please sit here and wait; he will come shortly. Whatever you ask of him, he will do.”

তবে প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র তাঁহা রহিল বসিয়া ৷  
রামানন্দ নিভৃতে সেই দুই-জন লঞা ॥ ১৬ ॥

tabe pradyumna-miśra tām̐hā rahila vasiyā  
then  Pradyumna Miśra  there  remained  sitting  
rāmānanda nibhṛte sei dui-jana lañā  
Rāmānanda  in seclusion  those  two  taking

(16) So Pradyumna Miśra stayed there, waiting, while Rāmānanda, in a secluded place, attended to the two maidens.

স্বহস্তে করেন তার অভ্যঙ্গ-মর্দ্দন ৷  
স্বহস্তে করান স্নান, গাত্র সংমার্জ্জন ॥ ১৭ ॥

sva-haste karena tāra abhyaṅga-mardana  
with his own hands  he does  their  anointing with oil  
sva-haste karāna snāna, gātra saṁmārjana  
with his own hands  he gives  a bath  the body  cleansing

(17) With his own hands he anointed them with oil; with his own hands he bathed them and cleansed their bodies.

স্বহস্তে পরান বস্ত্র, সর্ব্বাঙ্গ মণ্ডন ৷  
তবু নির্ব্বিকার রায়-রামানন্দের মন ॥ ১৮ ॥

sva-haste parāna vastra, sarvāṅga maṇḍana  
with his own hands  he puts on  clothes  every limb  adorning  
tabu nirvikāra rāya-rāmānandera mana  
yet  unmoved  of Rāya Rāmānanda  the mind

(18) With his own hands he dressed them and adorned every limb, and yet the mind of Rāya Rāmānanda remained unmoved.

কাষ্ঠ-পাষাণ-স্পর্শে হয় যৈছে ভাব ৷  
তরুণী-স্পর্শে রামানন্দের তৈছে ‘স্বভাব’ ॥ ১৯ ॥

kāṣṭha-pāṣāṇa-sparśe haya yaiche bhāva  
wood and stone  at touching  there is  as  feeling  
taruṇī-sparśe rāmānandera taiche ‘svabhāva’  
a young woman  at touching  Rāmānanda’s  so  nature

(19) What one feels on touching wood or stone, Rāmānanda by nature feels on touching a young woman.

সেব্য-বুদ্ধি আরোপিয়া করেন সেবন ৷  
স্বাভাবিক দাসীভাব করেন আরোপণ ॥ ২০ ॥

sevya-buddhi āropiyā karena sevana  
the sense of the one to be served  attributing  he does  service  
svābhāvika dāsī-bhāva karena āropaṇa  
natural  maidservant’s mood  he does  taking upon himself

(20) Seeing in them the ones to be served (*sevya-buddhi*), he served them, taking upon himself his own natural mood of a maidservant.[^6]

মহাপ্রভুর ভক্তগণের দুর্গম মহিমা ৷  
তাহে রামানন্দের ভাবভক্তি-প্রেম-সীমা ॥ ২১ ॥

mahāprabhura bhakta-gaṇera durgama mahimā  
of Mahāprabhu  of the devotees  unfathomable  glory  
tāhe rāmānandera bhāva-bhakti-prema-sīmā  
among them  Rāmānanda’s  of bhāva, bhakti and prema  the limit

(21) The glory of Mahāprabhu’s devotees is unfathomable, and among them, in Rāmānanda, bhāva, bhakti and prema reach their utmost limit.

তবে সেই দুইজনে নৃত্য শিখাইলা ৷  
গীতের গূঢ় অর্থ অভিনয় করাইলা ॥ ২২ ॥

tabe sei dui-jane nṛtya śikhāilā  
then  those  two  dance  he taught  
gītera gūḍha artha abhinaya karāilā  
of the songs  hidden  meaning  acting  he made them do

(22) Then he taught the two to dance and had them act out the hidden meaning of the songs.[^7]

সঞ্চারী, সাত্ত্বিক, স্থায়ি-ভাবের লক্ষণ ৷  
মুখে-নেত্রে অভিনয় করে প্রকটন ॥ ২৩ ॥

sañcārī, sāttvika, sthāyi-bhāvera lakṣaṇa  
transitory  sāttvika  of the abiding emotions  the signs  
mukhe-netre abhinaya kare prakaṭana  
in face and eyes  acting  they make  manifest

(23) By acting with face and eyes, they brought out the signs of the transitory (*sañcārī*), the *sāttvika* and the abiding (*sthāyi*) feelings.

ভাবপ্রকটন-লাস্য রায় যে শিখায় ৷  
জগন্নাথের আগে দুঁহে প্রকট দেখায় ॥ ২৪ ॥

bhāva-prakaṭana-lāsya rāya ye śikhāya  
bhāva-revealing graceful dance  Rāya  which  teaches  
jagannāthera āge dum̐he prakaṭa dekhāya  
of Jagannātha  before  the two  openly  show

(24) Rāya taught them the graceful feminine dance (*lāsya*) that reveals bhāva, and the two would display it openly before Jagannātha.

তবে সেই দুইজনে প্রসাদ খাওয়াইলা ৷  
নিভৃতে দুঁহারে নিজ-ঘরে পাঠাইলা ॥ ২৫ ॥

tabe sei dui-jane prasāda khāoyāilā  
then  those  two  prasāda  he fed  
nibhṛte dum̐hāre nija-ghare pāṭhāilā  
quietly  both  to their own home  he sent

(25) Then he gave the two prasāda to eat and quietly sent them home.[^8]

প্রতিদিন রায় ঐছে করায় সাধন ৷  
কোন্ জানে ক্ষুদ্র জীব কাঁহা তাঁর মন ? ॥ ২৬ ॥

prati-dina rāya aiche karāya sādhana  
every day  Rāya  thus  makes them do  practice  
kon jāne kṣudra jīva kām̐hā tām̐ra mana?  
who  knows  petty  living being  where  his  mind

(26) Every day Rāya trained them in this way. What petty soul can know where his mind dwells?

মিশ্রের আগমন রায়ে সেবক কহিলা ৷  
শীঘ্র রামানন্দ তবে সভাতে আইলা ॥ ২৭ ॥

miśrera āgamana rāye sevaka kahilā  
Miśra’s  arrival  to Rāya  the servant  told  
śīghra rāmānanda tabe sabhāte āilā  
quickly  Rāmānanda  then  to the hall  came

(27) The servant told Rāya that Miśra had come, and Rāmānanda at once came to the hall.

মিশ্রেরে নমস্কার করে সম্মান করিয়া ৷  
নিবেদন করে কিছু বিনীত হঞা ॥ ২৮ ॥

miśrere namaskāra kare sammāna kariyā  
to Miśra  obeisance  he makes  respect  showing  
nivedana kare kichu vinīta hañā  
submission  he makes  something  humble  becoming

(28) Showing him respect, he bowed to Miśra and humbly said:

বহুক্ষণ আইলা, মোরে কেহ না কহিল ৷  
তোমার চরণে মোর অপরাধ হইল ॥ ২৯ ॥

bahu-kṣaṇa āilā, more keha nā kahila  
long ago  you came  me  anyone  not  told  
tomāra caraṇe mora aparādha haila  
at your  feet  my  offense  has been

(29) “You came long ago, and no one told me. I have committed an offence at your feet.

তোমার আগমনে মোর পবিত্র হৈল ঘর ৷  
আজ্ঞা কর, ক্যা করোঁ তোমার কিঙ্কর ॥ ৩০ ॥

tomāra āgamane mora pavitra haila ghara  
by your  coming  my  pure  became  home  
ājñā kara, kyā karom̐ tomāra kiṅkara  
order  do  what  shall I do  your  servant

(30) Your coming has purified my home. Command me — what shall I, your servant, do?”

মিশ্র কহে, — তোমা দেখিতে হৈল আগমনে ৷  
আপনা পবিত্র কৈলুঁ তোমার দরশনে ॥ ৩১ ॥

miśra kahe, — tomā dekhite haila āgamane  
Miśra  says  you  to see  there was  coming  
āpanā pavitra kailum̐ tomāra daraśane  
myself  pure  I made  by your  sight

(31) Miśra said, “I came to see you, and by seeing you I have purified myself.”

অতিকাল দেখি’ মিশ্র কিছু না কহিল ৷  
বিদায় হইয়া মিশ্র নিজঘর গেল ॥ ৩২ ॥

atikāla dekhi’ miśra kichu nā kahila  
the late hour  seeing  Miśra  anything  not  said  
vidāya haiyā miśra nijaghara gela  
leave  taking  Miśra  to his home  went

(32) Seeing that it was too late, Miśra said nothing more; he took his leave and went home.

আর দিন মিশ্র আইল প্রভু-বিদ্যমানে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিলা রায়স্থানে ? ॥ ৩৩ ॥

āra dina miśra āila prabhu-vidyamāne  
the next  day  Miśra  came  into the Lord’s presence  
prabhu kahe, — kṛṣṇa-kathā śunilā rāyasthāne?  
the Lord  says  about Kṛṣṇa talks  did you hear  from Rāya

(33) The next day Miśra came before the Lord, and the Lord asked, “Did you hear about Kṛṣṇa from Rāya?”

তবে মিশ্র রামানন্দের বৃত্তান্ত কহিলা ৷  
শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু তবে কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৩৪ ॥

tabe miśra rāmānandera vṛttānta kahilā  
then  Miśra  Rāmānanda’s  doings  told  
śuni’ mahāprabhu tabe kahite lāgilā  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  then  to speak  began

(34) Then Miśra told Him what Rāmānanda had been doing, and when Mahāprabhu heard it, He began to speak.

আমি ত’ সন্ন্যাসী, আপনারে বিরক্ত করি’ মানি ৷  
দর্শন রহু দূরে, ‘প্রকৃতি’র নাম যদি শুনি ॥ ৩৫ ॥

তবহিঁ বিকার পায় মোর তনু-মন ৷  
প্রকৃতি-দর্শনে স্থির হয় কোন্ জন ? ॥ ৩৬ ॥

āmi ta’ sannyāsī, āpanāre virakta kari’ māni  
I  indeed  a sannyāsī  Myself  detached  as  I consider  
darśana rahu dūre, ‘prakṛti’ra nāma yadi śuni  
seeing  let it remain  far  of a woman  the name  if  I hear  
tabahim̐ vikāra pāya mora tanu-mana  
even then  agitation  gets  My  body and mind  
prakṛti-darśane sthira haya kon jana?  
a woman  on seeing  steady  is  what  person

(35–36) “I am a sannyāsī and consider Myself detached; yet, to say nothing of seeing a woman, if I merely hear a woman’s name, My body and mind are disturbed. Who can remain steady on seeing a woman?[^9]

রামানন্দ-রায়ের কথা শুন, সর্ব্বজন ৷  
কহিবার নহে, যাহা আশ্চর্য্য-কথন ॥ ৩৭ ॥

rāmānanda-rāyera kathā śuna, sarva-jana  
of Rāmānanda Rāya  the account  hear  all people  
kahibāra nahe, yāhā āścarya-kathana  
to be told  it is not  which  a wondrous account

(37) Hear, all of you, about Rāmānanda Rāya — an account so wondrous that it can hardly be told.

একে দেবদাসী, আর সুন্দরী তরুণী ৷  
তাহাদের সব সেবা করেন আপনি ॥ ৩৮ ॥

eke deva-dāsī, āra sundarī taruṇī  
first  devadāsīs  and  beautiful  young women  
tāhādera saba sevā karena āpani  
for them  all  service  he does  himself

(38) First, they are *devadāsīs*, maidservants of the Deity, and moreover they are beautiful young women; yet he himself performs every service for them.[^10]

স্নানাদি করায়, পরায় বাস-বিভূষণ ৷  
গুহ্য অঙ্গ যত, তার দর্শন-স্পর্শন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

snānādi karāya, parāya vāsa-vibhūṣaṇa  
bathing and so on  he has them do  he puts on  clothes and ornaments  
guhya aṅga yata, tāra darśana-sparśana  
private  parts  all  of them  seeing and touching

(39) He bathes them and dresses them in clothes and ornaments, and he sees and touches all the hidden parts of their bodies.

তবু নির্ব্বিকার রায়-রামানন্দের মন ৷  
নানাভাবোদ্গম তারে করায় শিক্ষণ ॥ ৪০ ॥

tabu nirvikāra rāya-rāmānandera mana  
yet  unmoved  of Rāya Rāmānanda  the mind  
nānābhāvodgama tāre karāya śikṣaṇa  
the rising of various bhāvas  them  he makes  learn

(40) Yet the mind of Rāya Rāmānanda remains unmoved as he teaches them to express the stirrings of many different bhāvas.[^11]

নির্ব্বিকার দেহ-মন — কাষ্ঠ-পাষাণ সম !  
আশ্চর্য্য, — তরুণী-স্পর্শে নির্ব্বিকার মন ! ॥ ৪১ ॥

nirvikāra deha-mana — kāṣṭha-pāṣāṇa sama!  
unmoved  body and mind  wood and stone  like  
āścarya, — taruṇī-sparśe nirvikāra mana!  
wonderful  a young woman  at touching  unmoved  mind

(41) His body and mind are unmoved, like wood or stone. How wonderful — at the touch of a young woman his mind is unmoved!

এক রামানন্দের হয় এই অধিকার ৷  
তাতে জানি অপ্রাকৃত-দেহ তাঁহার ॥ ৪২ ॥

eka rāmānandera haya ei adhikāra  
alone  of Rāmānanda  is  this  qualification  
tāte jāni aprākṛta-deha tām̐hāra  
from that  I know  transcendental  body  his

(42) Rāmānanda alone has this qualification; from it I know that his body is transcendental.

তাঁহার মনের ভাব তেঁহ জানে মাত্র ৷  
তাহা জানিবারে আর দ্বিতীয় নাহি পাত্র ॥ ৪৩ ॥

tām̐hāra manera bhāva tem̐ha jāne mātra  
his  mind’s  state  he  knows  only  
tāhā jānibāre āra dvitīya nāhi pātra  
that  to know  other  second  there is not  fit person

(43) Only he knows the state of his mind; there is no one else fit to know it.

কিন্তু শাস্ত্রদৃষ্ট্যে করি এক অনুমান ৷  
শ্রীভাগবত-শাস্ত্র — তাহাতে প্রমাণ ॥ ৪৪ ॥

kintu śāstra-dṛṣṭye kari eka anumāna  
but  in the light of scripture  I make  one  inference  
śrī-bhāgavata-śāstra — tāhāte pramāṇa  
the Śrī Bhāgavata scripture  of that  the evidence

(44) But in the light of scripture I draw one inference. The *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* bears witness to this.[^12]

ব্রজবধূ-সঙ্গে কৃষ্ণের রাসাদি-বিলাস ৷  
যেই জন কহে, শুনে করিয়া বিশ্বাস ॥ ৪৫ ॥

হৃদ্রোগ-কাম তাঁর তৎকালে হয় ক্ষয় ৷  
তিনগুণ-ক্ষোভ নহে, ‘মহাধীর’ হয় ॥ ৪৬ ॥

vraja-vadhū-saṅge kṛṣṇera rāsādi-vilāsa  
with the young women of Vraja  Kṛṣṇa’s  the rāsa and other pastimes  
yei jana kahe, śune kariyā viśvāsa  
which  person  tells  hears  having  faith  
hṛd-roga-kāma tām̐ra tat-kāle haya kṣaya  
the heart’s disease, lust  his  at once  is  destroyed  
tina-guṇa-kṣobha nahe, ‘mahā-dhīra’  haya  
of the three guṇas  agitation  is not  very steady  he becomes

(45–46) When a person tells of or hears with faith about Kṛṣṇa’s rāsa dance and other pastimes with the young women of Vraja, his lust, the disease of the heart, is destroyed at once. The three guṇas no longer agitate him, and he becomes perfectly steady (*mahā-dhīra*).[^13]

উজ্জ্বল মধুর-রস প্রেমভক্তি পায় ৷  
আনন্দে কৃষ্ণমাধুর্য্যে বিহরে সদায় ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ujjvala madhura-rasa prema-bhakti pāya  
radiant  of madhura-rasa  prema-bhakti  he attains  
ānande kṛṣṇa-mādhurye vihare sadāya  
in bliss  in Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  he sports  always

(47) He attains prema-bhakti in the radiant *madhura-rasa*, and in bliss he ever revels in Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness.

বিক্রীড়িতং ব্রজবধূভিরিদঞ্চ বিষ্ণোঃ  
শ্রদ্ধান্বিতোঽনুশৃণুয়াদথ বর্ণয়েদ্ যঃ ৷  
ভক্তিং পরাং ভগবতি প্রতিলভ্য কামং  
হৃদ্রোগমাশ্বপহিনোত্যচিরেণ ধীরঃ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

vikrīḍitaṁ vraja-vadhūbhir idaṁ ca viṣṇoḥ  
the play  with the young women of Vraja  this  and  of Viṣṇu  
śraddhānvito ’nuśṛṇuyād atha varṇayed yaḥ  
endowed with faith  constantly hears  then  describes  who  
bhaktiṁ parāṁ bhagavati pratilabhya kāmaṁ  
devotion  supreme  for the Lord  having attained  lust  
hṛd-rogam āśv apahinoty acireṇa dhīraḥ  
the heart’s disease  quickly  casts off  soon  steadfast

(48) “The steadfast soul who, endowed with faith, constantly hears of this play of Viṣṇu with the young women of Vraja and then describes it, soon attains supreme devotion to the Lord and quickly casts off lust, the disease of the heart.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.39)[^14]

যে শুনে, যে পড়ে, তাঁর ফল এতাদৃশী ৷  
সেই ভাবাবিষ্ট, যেই সেবে অহর্নিশি ॥ ৪৯ ॥

ye śune, ye paḍe, tām̐ra phala etādṛśī  
who  hears  who  reads  his  fruit  is such  
sei bhāvāviṣṭa, yei seve ahar-niśi  
he  absorbed in bhāva  who  serves  day and night

(49) Such is the fruit for whoever hears or reads of these pastimes. But as for one who, absorbed in bhāva, serves day and night —[^15]

তাঁর ফল কি কহিমু, কহনে না যায় ৷  
নিত্যসিদ্ধ সেই, প্রায়-সিদ্ধ তাঁর কায় ॥ ৫০ ॥

tām̐ra phala ki kahimu, kahane nā yāya  
his  fruit  what  shall I tell  in words  not  it goes  
nitya-siddha sei, prāya-siddha tām̐ra kāya  
eternally perfect  he  almost perfect  his  body

(50) what can I say of his fruit? It cannot be put into words. He is eternally perfect (*nitya-siddha*), and his body is almost perfect (*prāya-siddha*).[^16]

রাগানুগ-মার্গে জানি রায়ের ভজন ৷  
সিদ্ধদেহ-তুল্য, তাতে ‘প্রাকৃত’ নহে মন ॥ ৫১ ॥

rāgānuga-mārge jāni rāyera bhajana  
on the rāgānuga path  I know  Rāya’s  worship  
siddha-deha-tulya, tāte ‘prākṛta’ nahe mana  
to a perfected body  equal  therefore  mundane  is not  the mind

(51) I know that Rāya’s worship follows the path of rāgānuga. His body is like a perfected spiritual body (*siddha-deha*), and so his mind is not mundane.

আমিহ রায়ের স্থানে শুনি কৃষ্ণকথা ৷  
শুনিতে ইচ্ছা হয় যদি, পুনঃ যাহ’ তথা ॥ ৫২ ॥

āmiha rāyera sthāne śuni kṛṣṇa-kathā  
I too  Rāya’s  at the place  hear  talks about Kṛṣṇa  
śunite icchā haya yadi, punaḥ yāha’ tathā  
to hear  desire  is  if  again  go  there

(52) I too hear about Kṛṣṇa from Rāya. If you wish to hear, go there again.

মোর নামে কহিহ, — তেঁহো পাঠাইলা মোরে ৷  
তোমার স্থানে কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিবার তরে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

mora nāme kahiha, — tem̐ho pāṭhāilā more  
in My  name  say  He  sent  me  
tomāra sthāne kṛṣṇa-kathā śunibāra tare  
your  at the place  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  in order

(53) Tell him in My name: ‘He has sent me to hear about Kṛṣṇa from you.’

শীঘ্র যাহ’, যাবৎ তেঁহো আছেন সভাতে ৷  
এত শুনি’ প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র চলিলা ত্বরিতে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

śīghra yāha’, yāvat tem̐ho āchena sabhāte  
quickly  go  while  he  is  in the hall  
eta śuni’ pradyumna-miśra calilā tvarite  
this  hearing  Pradyumna Miśra  went  in haste

(54) Go quickly, while he is still in the hall.” Hearing this, Pradyumna Miśra set off in haste.

রায়-পাশ গেল, রায় প্রণতি করিল ৷  
আজ্ঞা কর, যে লাগি’ আগমন হৈল ॥ ৫৫ ॥

rāya-pāśa gela, rāya praṇati karila  
to Rāya  he went  Rāya  obeisance  made  
ājñā kara, ye lāgi’ āgamana haila  
order  do  what  for  coming  has been

(55) He went to Rāya, and Rāya bowed to him and said, “Command me; tell me why you have come.”

মিশ্র কহে, — মহাপ্রভু পাঠাইলা মোরে ৷  
তোমার স্থানে কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিবার তরে ॥ ৫৬ ॥

miśra kahe, — mahāprabhu pāṭhāilā more  
Miśra  says  Mahāprabhu  sent  me  
tomāra sthāne kṛṣṇa-kathā śunibāra tare  
your  at the place  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  in order

(56) Miśra said, “Mahāprabhu has sent me to hear about Kṛṣṇa from you.”

শুনি’ রামানন্দ মনে হইলা সন্তোষে ৷  
কহিতে লাগিলা কিছু মনের হরিষে ॥ ৫৭ ॥

śuni’ rāmānanda mane hailā santoṣe  
hearing  Rāmānanda  in mind  became  pleased  
kahite lāgilā kichu manera hariṣe  
to speak  began  something  of the mind  in joy

(57) Hearing this, Rāmānanda was pleased, and with joy in his heart he began to speak:

প্রভুর আজ্ঞায় কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিতে আইলা এথা ৷  
ইহা বই মহাভাগ্য আমি পাব কোথা ? ॥ ৫৮ ॥

prabhura ājñāya kṛṣṇa-kathā śunite āilā ethā  
of the Lord  by the order  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  you came  here  
ihā vai mahābhāgya āmi pāba kothā?  
this  besides  great fortune  I  shall find  where

(58) “By the Lord’s order you have come here to hear about Kṛṣṇa. Where else could I find such great fortune?”

এত কহি’ তারে লঞা নিভৃতে বসিলা ৷  
কি কথা শুনিতে চাহ? মিশ্রেরে পুছিলা ॥ ৫৯ ॥

eta kahi’ tāre lañā nibhṛte vasilā  
this  saying  him  taking  in seclusion  he sat  
ki kathā śunite cāha? miśrere puchilā  
what  topic  to hear  do you want  Miśra  he asked

(59) Saying this, he took him to a secluded place, sat down with him and asked Miśra, “What would you like to hear?”

তেঁহো কহে, — যে কহিলা বিদ্যানগরে ৷  
সেই কথা ক্রমে তুমি কহিবা আমারে ॥ ৬০ ॥

tem̐ho kahe, — ye kahilā vidyānagare  
he  says  what  you told  in Vidyānagara  
sei kathā krame tumi kahibā āmāre  
those  topics  in order  you  please tell  me

(60) He said, “Please tell me, in order, all that you spoke of in Vidyānagara.[^17]

আনের কি কথা, তুমি — প্রভুর উপদেষ্টা !  
আমি ত’ ভিক্ষুক বিপ্র, তুমি — মোর পোষ্টা ॥ ৬১ ॥

ānera ki kathā, tumi — prabhura upadeṣṭā!  
of others  what  talk  you  of the Lord  the instructor  
āmi ta’ bhikṣuka vipra, tumi — mora poṣṭā  
I  indeed  a mendicant  brāhmaṇa  you  my  sustainer

(61) Not only of others — you are the instructor of the Lord Himself! I am a poor mendicant brāhmaṇa, and you are my sustainer.

ভাল, মন্দ — কিছু আমি পুছিতে না জানি ৷  
‘দীন’ দেখি’ কৃপা করি’ কহিবা আপনি ॥ ৬২ ॥

bhāla, manda — kichu āmi puchite nā jāni  
good  bad  anything  I  to ask  not  know  
‘dīna’ dekhi’ kṛpā kari’ kahibā āpani  
wretched  seeing  mercy  showing  please tell  yourself

(62) I do not know how to ask anything, good or bad. Seeing how wretched I am, be merciful and tell me of your own accord.”

তবে রামানন্দ ক্রমে কহিতে লাগিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথা-রসামৃত-সিন্ধু উথলিলা ॥ ৬৩ ॥

tabe rāmānanda krame kahite lāgilā  
then  Rāmānanda  in order  to speak  began  
kṛṣṇa-kathā-rasāmṛta-sindhu uthalilā  
the ocean of the nectar of rasa in talks about Kṛṣṇa  surged

(63) Then Rāmānanda began to speak in due order, and the nectar-ocean of rasa in his talk of Kṛṣṇa surged up.

আপনে প্রশ্ন করি’ পাছে করেন সিদ্ধান্ত ৷  
তৃতীয় প্রহর হৈল, নহে কথা-অন্ত ॥ ৬৪ ॥

āpane praśna kari’ pāche karena siddhānta  
himself  question  raising  afterward  he makes  conclusion  
tṛtīya prahara haila, nahe kathā-anta  
the third  prahara  came  there is not  of the talk  end

(64) He himself raised the questions and then gave the conclusive answers (*siddhānta*). The third watch (*prahara*) of the day came, and still the talk did not end.

বক্তা শ্রোতা কহে শুনে দুঁহে প্রেমাবেশে ৷  
আত্মস্মৃতি নাহি, কাঁহা জানে দিন শেষে ॥ ৬৫ ॥

vaktā śrotā kahe śune dum̐he premāveśe  
the speaker  the listener  speaks  hears  both  in the absorption of prema  
ātmasmṛti nāhi, kām̐hā jāne dina śeṣe  
self-awareness  there is not  how  they know  of the day  the end

(65) Speaker and listener, one speaking and the other hearing, were both absorbed in prema. They had no awareness of themselves — how could they know the day had ended?

সেবক কহিল, — ‘দিন হৈল অবসান’ ৷  
তবে রায় কৃষ্ণকথার করিলা বিশ্রাম ॥ ৬৬ ॥

sevaka kahila, — ‘dina haila avasāna’  
the servant  said  the day  has come  to an end  
tabe rāya kṛṣṇakathāra karilā viśrāma  
then  Rāya  to the talk about Kṛṣṇa  made  a pause

(66) When the servant said, “The day is over,” Rāya brought his talk of Kṛṣṇa to a close.

বহুসম্মান করি’ মিশ্রে বিদায় দিলা ৷  
কৃতার্থ হইলাঙ বলি’ মিশ্র নাচিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৬৭ ॥

bahu-sammāna kari’ miśre vidāya dilā  
great respect  showing  to Miśra  leave  he gave  
kṛtārtha hailāṅa bali’ miśra nācite lāgilā  
fulfilled  I have become  saying  Miśra  to dance  began

(67) With great respect he bade Miśra farewell, and Miśra, crying, “My life is fulfilled!” began to dance.

ঘরে গিয়া মিশ্র কৈল স্নান, ভোজন ৷  
সন্ধ্যাকালে দেখিতে আইল প্রভুর চরণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

ghare giyā miśra kaila snāna, bhojana  
home  going  Miśra  did  bathing  eating  
sandhyākāle dekhite āila prabhura caraṇa  
in the evening  to see  he came  of the Lord  the feet

(68) Miśra went home, bathed and took his meal, and in the evening he came to see the Lord’s feet.

প্রভুর চরণ বন্দে উল্লসিত-মনে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — কৃষ্ণকথা হইল শ্রবণে ? ॥ ৬৯ ॥

prabhura  caraṇa  vande  ullasita-mane  
the Lord’s  feet  he worships  with a joyful mind  
prabhu kahe, — kṛṣṇa-kathā haila śravaṇe?  
the Lord  says  talks about Kṛṣṇa  was there  hearing

(69) With a joyful heart he bowed at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord asked, “Did you hear about Kṛṣṇa?”

মিশ্র কহে, — প্রভু, মোরে কৃতার্থ করিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথামৃতার্ণবে মোরে ডুবাইলা ॥ ৭০ ॥

miśra kahe, — prabhu, more kṛtārtha karilā  
Miśra  says  O Lord  me  fulfilled  You have made  
kṛṣṇa-kathāmṛtārṇave more ḍubāilā  
in the ocean of the nectar of talks about Kṛṣṇa  me  You have plunged

(70) Miśra said, “O Lord, You have fulfilled my life! You have plunged me into the nectar-ocean of talks about Kṛṣṇa.

রামানন্দ-রায়-কথা কহিলে না হয় ৷  
‘মনুষ্য’ নহে রায়, কৃষ্ণভক্তিরসময় ॥ ৭১ ॥

rāmānanda-rāya-kathā  kahile  nā haya  
of Rāmānanda Rāya  the words  if told  not  it is possible  
‘manuṣya’ nahe rāya, kṛṣṇa-bhakti-rasa-maya  
a human being  is not  Rāya  made of the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa

(71) The words of Rāmānanda Rāya cannot be retold. Rāya is not a mere human being; he is made of the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.[^18]

আর এক কথা রায় কহিলা আমারে ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণকথা-বক্তা করি’ না জানিহ মোরে ॥ ৭২ ॥

āra  eka  kathā  rāya  kahilā  āmāre  
another  one  thing  Rāya  told  me  
‘kṛṣṇa-kathā-vaktā kari’ nā jāniha more  
of talks about Kṛṣṇa  the speaker  as  not  consider  me

(72) Rāya told me one thing more: ‘Do not think of me as the speaker of these talks about Kṛṣṇa.

মোর মুখে কথা কহেন আপনে গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
যৈছে কহায়, তৈছে কহি, — যেন বীণাযন্ত্র ॥ ৭৩ ॥

mora mukhe kathā kahena āpane gauracandra  
by my  mouth  words  speaks  Himself  Gauracandra  
yaiche kahāya, taiche kahi, — yena vīṇā-yantra  
as  He makes me speak  so  I speak  like  a vīṇā instrument

(73) Gauracandra Himself speaks these words through my mouth. As He makes me speak, so I speak, like a *vīṇā*.

মোর মুখে কথা ইঁহা করে পরচার ৷  
পৃথিবীতে কে জানিবে এ-লীলা তাঁহার ? ॥ ৭৪ ॥

mora mukhe kathā im̐hā kare paracāra  
by my  mouth  the words  He  makes  known  
pṛthivīte ke jānibe e-līlā tām̐hāra?  
in the world  who  will know  this pastime  His

(74) Through my mouth He makes these words known. Who in this world will understand this pastime of His?’

যে-সব শুনিলুঁ, কৃষ্ণ — রসের সাগর ৷  
ব্রহ্মাদি-দেবের এ সব না হয় গোচর ॥ ৭৫ ॥

ye-saba śunilum̐, kṛṣṇa — rasera sāgara  
all that  I heard  Kṛṣṇa  of rasa  the ocean  
brahmādi-devera e saba nā haya gocara  
to Brahmā and the other gods  this  all  not  is  perceptible

(75) All that I heard — that Kṛṣṇa is the ocean of rasa — lies beyond the reach even of Brahmā and the other gods.

হেন ‘রস’ মোরে পান করাইলা তুমি ৷  
জন্মে জন্মে তোমার পায় বিকাইলাঙ আমি ॥ ৭৬ ॥

hena ‘rasa’ more pāna karāilā tumi  
such  rasa  me  to drink  made  You  
janme janme tomāra pāya vikāilāṅa āmi  
birth  after birth  Your  at the feet  sold myself  I

(76) You have made me drink such rasa; birth after birth I have sold myself at Your feet.”

প্রভু কহে, — রামানন্দ বিনয়ের খনি ৷  
আপনার কথা পরমুণ্ডে দেন আনি ॥ ৭৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — rāmānanda vinayera khani  
the Lord  says  Rāmānanda  of humility  a mine  
āpanāra kathā para-muṇḍe dena āni  
his own  words  on another’s head  he places  bringing

(77) The Lord said, “Rāmānanda is a mine of humility. He attributes his own words to another.

মহানুভবের এই সহজ ‘স্বভাব’ হয় ৷  
আপনার গুণ নাহি আপনে কহয় ॥ ৭৮ ॥

mahānubhavera ei sahaja ‘svabhāva’ haya  
of great souls  this  natural  disposition  is  
āpanāra guṇa nāhi āpane kahaya  
their own  qualities  not  themselves  they tell

(78) This is the natural disposition of great souls: they never speak of their own qualities.”

রামানন্দ-রায়ের এই কহিলুঁ গুণ-লেশ ৷  
প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্রেরে যৈছে কৈলা উপদেশ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

rāmānanda-rāyera ei kahilum̐ guṇa-leśa  
of Rāmānanda Rāya  this  I have told  of qualities  a trace  
pradyumna-miśrere yaiche kailā upadeśa  
to Pradyumna Miśra  how  he gave  instruction

(79) Thus I have told a little of Rāmānanda Rāya’s qualities — how he instructed Pradyumna Miśra.

‘গৃহস্থ’ হঞা নহে রায় ষড়্-বর্গের বশে ৷  
‘বিষয়ী’ হঞা সন্ন্যাসীরে উপদেশে ॥ ৮০ ॥

‘gṛhastha’ hañā nahe rāya ṣaḍ-vargera vaśe  
a householder  being  not  Rāya  of the six enemies  under the control  
‘viṣayī’ hañā sannyāsīre upadeśe  
a worldly man  being  sannyāsīs  he instructs

(80) Though a householder, Rāya is not under the control of the six enemies (*ṣaḍ-varga*); though seemingly a worldly man (*viṣayī*), he instructs sannyāsīs.[^19]

এইসব গুণ তাঁর প্রকাশ করিতে ৷  
মিশ্রেরে পাঠাইলা তাঁহা শ্রবণ করিতে ॥ ৮১ ॥

eisaba guṇa tām̐ra prakāśa karite  
all these  qualities  his  manifest  to make  
miśrere pāṭhāilā tām̐hā śravaṇa karite  
Miśra  He sent  there  hearing  to do

(81) To reveal all these qualities of his, the Lord sent Miśra there to hear.

ভক্তগুণ প্রকাশিতে প্রভু ভাল জানে ৷  
নানা-ভঙ্গীতে প্রকাশি’ নিজ লাভ মানে ॥ ৮২ ॥

bhakta-guṇa prakāśite prabhu bhāla jāne  
the devotee’s qualities  to reveal  the Lord  well  knows  
nānā-bhaṅgīte prakāśi’ nija lābha māne  
in various ways  revealing  His own  gain  He considers

(82) The Lord knows well how to reveal the qualities of His devotees; revealing them in various ways, He considers it His own gain.

---

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: *vaiguṇya* — the ripening of the fruits of karma; *paiśunya* — malice. Edition heading: for the jīva bereft of consciousness, stricken with the disease of worldly existence and fallen into the ocean of saṁsāra, the only good lies in taking shelter of Caitanya’s feet.

[^2]: Edition heading: the Lord pretends not to know and sends Miśra, born in a family of brāhmaṇas, to Rāmānanda as an eager disciple — to Rāmānanda, who was not a brāhmaṇa by birth, yet is the guru of all four varṇas and āśramas.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: the dharma of varṇa and āśrama; if it does not awaken attraction (*rati*) to the narrations about the Lord, it is wholly fruitless labour.

[^4]: The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* calls them young *devadāsīs* (now called *māhārīs*), and the *Anubhāṣya* (on 25) “two devadāsīs” — maidservants of the Deity who dance before Śrī Jagannātha.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: his own drama — the *Jagannātha-vallabha*, which Rāmānanda Rāya wrote in Sanskrit.

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the two girls were to play the principal gopīs in the drama, so he regarded them as the ones to be served and took the mood of a maidservant following them; Rāmānanda knew himself to be a maidservant of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī.

[^7]: Edition heading: to stage his drama *Jagannātha-vallabha* before Śrī Jagannātha, Rāya teaches acting in the Jagannātha-vallabha garden.

[^8]: Edition heading: after feeding the two devadāsīs, Rāya sends them home in secret — for the good of ignorant critics who judge only by what the senses show.

[^9]: Edition heading: Bhagavān, the ideal teacher of humility, humbly extols His devotee’s love for Kṛṣṇa as greater than His own.

[^10]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads “তাহাদের সব সেবা”, “every service for them” (*Anubhāṣya*: all kinds of service; cf. 39).

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: the thirty-three kinds of bhāva needed to enact the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa.

[^12]: The second line is the same as Madhya 13.67; translation as there.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is said of one who, with faith and a transcendental heart, hears and describes Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes: the three guṇas cannot overcome him. Let no one think, like the prākṛta-sahajiyās, that without knowledge of sambandha, giving up sādhana-bhakti and taking Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes as a model of worldly enjoyment, one’s lust will vanish by merely hearing; by the word “faith” (*viśvāsa*) Mahāprabhu rejects that view (cf. Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.30).

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pratilabhya* — “having attained”: devotion comes first, and lust is driven out; *anuśṛṇuyāt* — he hears constantly from the lips of the guru, free from worldly obstacles; *varṇayet* — he sings of it step by step, in the way of Śrī Rūpa’s followers; *dhīraḥ* — one who has conquered the six urges.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: one who hears and chants Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes and, following the mood of Śrī Rūpa, serves Kṛṣṇa in the mind at every moment, absorbed in pure rāga, gains a matchless fruit that no worldly language can describe.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: he is an eternally perfect associate; or else his body is almost perfect — though seen by people’s eyes, it is the abode of feelings devoted to the service of Kṛṣṇa, until by Kṛṣṇa’s will he attains *vastu-siddhi*.

[^17]: Edition heading: Miśra asks him to speak again of Kṛṣṇa — of sambandha, abhidheya and prayojana — as Rāya once spoke to the Lord (Madhya 8).

[^18]: Edition heading: it is forbidden to regard as a mortal the guru who sings of Kṛṣṇa.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: worldly people see in Rāmānanda a householder and a *viṣayī*, absorbed in worldly objects, but his mind of pure goodness always worships a single object — the pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa; he is thus a “kṛṣṇa-viṣayī”, able to turn even sannyāsīs to Kṛṣṇa.
