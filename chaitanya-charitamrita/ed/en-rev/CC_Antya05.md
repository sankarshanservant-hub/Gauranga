“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 5  
**The Story of Pradyumna Miśra**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বৈগুণ্যকীটকলিতঃ পৈশুন্য-ব্রণপীড়িতঃ ৷  
দৈন্যার্ণবে নিমগ্নোঽহং চৈতন্য-বৈদ্যমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ১ ॥

vaiguṇya-kīṭa-kalitaḥ  
by the ripening of karma  by the worm  bitten  
paiśunya-vraṇa-pīḍitaḥ  
of malice  by the sore  afflicted  
dainyārṇave nimagno ’haṁ  
in the ocean of humility  sunk  I  
caitanya-vaidyam āśraye  
Caitanya  the physician  I take shelter of

(1) Bitten by the worm of ripened karma, tormented by the sore of malice, and sunk in an ocean of humility, I take shelter of Caitanya, the physician.[^1]

জয় জয় শচীসুত শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
জয় জয় কৃপাময় নিত্যানন্দ ধন্য ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śacīsuta śrīkṛṣṇacaitanya  
glory  glory  to the son of Śacī  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
jaya jaya kṛpāmaya nityānanda dhanya  
glory  glory  to the all-merciful  Nityānanda  the blessed

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the son of Śacī! Glory, glory to the all-merciful, blessed Nityānanda!

জয়াদ্বৈত কৃপাসিন্ধু জয় ভক্তগণ ৷  
জয় স্বরূপ, গদাধর, রূপ, সনাতন ॥ ৩ ॥

jayādvaita kṛpā-sindhu jaya bhakta-gaṇa  
glory to Advaita  of mercy  the ocean  glory  to the devotees  all  
jaya svarūpa, gadādhara, rūpa, sanātana  
glory  to Svarūpa  Gadādhara  Rūpa  Sanātana

(3) Glory to Advaita, the ocean of mercy! Glory to all the devotees! Glory to Svarūpa, Gadādhara, Rūpa and Sanātana!

একদিন প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ করি’ কিছু করে নিবেদনে ॥ ৪ ॥

ekadina pradyumna-miśra prabhura caraṇe  
one day  Pradyumna Miśra  of the Lord  at the feet  
daṇḍavat kari’ kichu kare nivedane  
obeisance  making  something  he makes  submission

(4) One day Pradyumna Miśra bowed down at the Lord’s feet and humbly made a request.

শুন, প্রভু, মুঞি দীন গৃহস্থ অধম !  
কোন ভাগ্যে পাঞাছোঁ তোমার দুর্ল্লভ চরণ ॥ ৫ ॥

śuna, prabhu, muñi dīna gṛhastha adhama!  
hear  O Lord  I  wretched  householder  lowly  
kona bhāgye pāñāchom̐ tomāra durlabha caraṇa  
by what  fortune  I have obtained  Your  rare  feet

(5) “Hear me, O Lord: I am a wretched, lowly householder. By what fortune have I obtained Your feet, so rarely attained?

কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিবারে মোর ইচ্ছা হয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথা কহ মোরে হঞা সদয় ॥ ৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kathā śunibāre mora icchā haya  
about Kṛṣṇa talks  to hear  my  desire  is  
kṛṣṇa-kathā kaha more hañā sadaya  
about Kṛṣṇa talks  tell  me  becoming  kind

(6) I wish to hear talks about Kṛṣṇa. Be kind to me and tell me about Kṛṣṇa.”

প্রভু কহেন, — কৃষ্ণকথা আমি নাহি জানি ৷  
সবে রামানন্দ জানে, তাঁর মুখে শুনি ॥ ৭ ॥

prabhu kahena, — kṛṣṇa-kathā āmi nāhi jāni  
the Lord  says  about Kṛṣṇa talks  I  not  know  
sabe rāmānanda jāne, tām̐ra mukhe śuni  
only  Rāmānanda  knows  his  from the mouth  I hear

(7) The Lord said, “I know nothing of talks about Kṛṣṇa. Only Rāmānanda knows them, and I hear them from his lips.[^2]

ভাগ্যে তোমার কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিতে হয় মন ৷  
রামানন্দ-পাশ যাই’ করহ শ্রবণ ॥ ৮ ॥

bhāgye tomāra kṛṣṇa-kathā śunite haya mana  
by fortune  your  about Kṛṣṇa talks  to hear  is  the mind  
rāmānanda-pāśa yāi’ karaha śravaṇa  
to Rāmānanda  going  do  hearing

(8) It is your good fortune that your mind longs to hear about Kṛṣṇa. Go to Rāmānanda and hear from him.

কৃষ্ণকথায় রুচি তোমার — বড় ভাগ্যবান্ ৷  
যার কৃষ্ণকথায় রুচি, সেই ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kathāya ruci tomāra — baḍa bhāgyavān  
for talks about Kṛṣṇa  taste  your  very  fortunate  
yāra kṛṣṇa-kathāya ruci, sei bhāgyavān  
whose  for talks about Kṛṣṇa  taste  he  fortunate

(9) You have a taste for talks about Kṛṣṇa — you are very fortunate. One who has a taste for talks about Kṛṣṇa is truly fortunate.”

ধর্ম্মঃ স্বনুষ্ঠিতঃ পুংসাং বিষ্বক্সেনকথাসু যঃ ৷  
নোৎপাদয়েদ্যদি রতিং শ্রম এব হি কেবলম্ ॥ ১০ ॥

dharmaḥ sv-anuṣṭhitaḥ puṁsāṁ  
dharma  well performed  of people  
viṣvaksena-kathāsu yaḥ  
of Viṣvaksena  for the narrations  which  
notpādayed yadi ratiṁ  
not  awakens  if  attraction  
śrama eva hi kevalam  
labor  only  certainly  merely

(10) “Dharma, however well a person performs it, is nothing but wasted labour if it does not awaken attraction (*rati*) to the narrations about the Lord, Viṣvaksena.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.8)[^3]

তবে প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র গেলা রামানন্দের স্থানে ৷  
রায়ের সেবক তাঁরে বসাইল আসনে ॥ ১১ ॥

tabe pradyumna-miśra gelā rāmānandera sthāne  
then  Pradyumna Miśra  went  Rāmānanda’s  to the place  
rāyera sevaka tām̐re vasāila āsane  
Rāya’s  servant  him  seated  on a seat

(11) Then Pradyumna Miśra went to Rāmānanda’s house, and Rāya’s servant offered him a seat.

রায়ের দর্শন না পাঞা সেবকে পুছিল ৷  
রায়ের বৃত্তান্ত সেবক কহিতে লাগিল ॥ ১২ ॥

rāyera darśana nā pāñā sevake puchila  
Rāya’s  sight  not  getting  the servant  he asked  
rāyera vṛttānta sevaka kahite lāgila  
Rāya’s  doings  the servant  to tell  began

(12) Not finding Rāya there, he asked the servant, who told him what Rāya was doing:

দুই দেব-কন্যা হয় পরম-সুন্দরী ৷  
নৃত্য-গীতে সুনিপুণা, বয়সে কিশোরী ॥ ১৩ ॥

dui deva-kanyā haya parama-sundarī  
two  maidens of the Deity  there are  supremely beautiful  
nṛtya-gīte sunipuṇā, vayase kiśorī  
in dance and song  very skilled  in age  in early youth

(13) “There are two maidens of the Deity (*deva-kanyās*), supremely beautiful, very skilled in dance and song, and in the bloom of youth.[^4]

সেই দুঁহে লঞা রায় নিভৃত উদ্যানে ৷  
নিজ-নাটক-গীতের শিখায় নর্ত্তনে ॥ ১৪ ॥

sei dum̐he lañā rāya nibhṛta udyāne  
those  two  taking  Rāya  in a secluded  garden  
nija-nāṭaka-gītera śikhāya nartane  
of his own drama’s songs  he teaches  dancing

(14) Rāya has taken them both to a secluded garden and is teaching them to dance to the songs of his own drama.[^5]

তুমি ইঁহা বসি’ রহ, ক্ষণেকে আসিবেন ৷  
তাঁরে যেই আজ্ঞা দেহ’, সেই করিবেন ॥ ১৫ ॥

tumi im̐hā vasi’ raha, kṣaṇeke āsibena  
you  here  sitting  stay  in a moment  he will come  
tām̐re yei ājñā deha’, sei karibena  
to him  whatever  order  you give  that  he will do

(15) Please sit here and wait; he will come shortly. Whatever you ask of him, he will do.”

তবে প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র তাঁহা রহিল বসিয়া ৷  
রামানন্দ নিভৃতে সেই দুই-জন লঞা ॥ ১৬ ॥

tabe pradyumna-miśra tām̐hā rahila vasiyā  
then  Pradyumna Miśra  there  remained  sitting  
rāmānanda nibhṛte sei dui-jana lañā  
Rāmānanda  in seclusion  those  two  taking

(16) So Pradyumna Miśra stayed there, waiting, while Rāmānanda, in a secluded place, attended to the two maidens.

স্বহস্তে করেন তার অভ্যঙ্গ-মর্দ্দন ৷  
স্বহস্তে করান স্নান, গাত্র সংমার্জ্জন ॥ ১৭ ॥

sva-haste karena tāra abhyaṅga-mardana  
with his own hands  he does  their  anointing with oil  
sva-haste karāna snāna, gātra saṁmārjana  
with his own hands  he gives  a bath  the body  cleansing

(17) With his own hands he anointed them with oil; with his own hands he bathed them and cleansed their bodies.

স্বহস্তে পরান বস্ত্র, সর্ব্বাঙ্গ মণ্ডন ৷  
তবু নির্ব্বিকার রায়-রামানন্দের মন ॥ ১৮ ॥

sva-haste parāna vastra, sarvāṅga maṇḍana  
with his own hands  he puts on  clothes  every limb  adorning  
tabu nirvikāra rāya-rāmānandera mana  
yet  unmoved  of Rāya Rāmānanda  the mind

(18) With his own hands he dressed them and adorned every limb, and yet the mind of Rāya Rāmānanda remained unmoved.

কাষ্ঠ-পাষাণ-স্পর্শে হয় যৈছে ভাব ৷  
তরুণী-স্পর্শে রামানন্দের তৈছে ‘স্বভাব’ ॥ ১৯ ॥

kāṣṭha-pāṣāṇa-sparśe haya yaiche bhāva  
wood and stone  at touching  there is  as  feeling  
taruṇī-sparśe rāmānandera taiche ‘svabhāva’  
a young woman  at touching  Rāmānanda’s  so  nature

(19) What one feels on touching wood or stone, Rāmānanda by nature feels on touching a young woman.

সেব্য-বুদ্ধি আরোপিয়া করেন সেবন ৷  
স্বাভাবিক দাসীভাব করেন আরোপণ ॥ ২০ ॥

sevya-buddhi āropiyā karena sevana  
the sense of the one to be served  attributing  he does  service  
svābhāvika dāsī-bhāva karena āropaṇa  
natural  maidservant’s mood  he does  taking upon himself

(20) Seeing in them the ones to be served (*sevya-buddhi*), he served them, taking upon himself his own natural mood of a maidservant.[^6]

মহাপ্রভুর ভক্তগণের দুর্গম মহিমা ৷  
তাহে রামানন্দের ভাবভক্তি-প্রেম-সীমা ॥ ২১ ॥

mahāprabhura bhakta-gaṇera durgama mahimā  
of Mahāprabhu  of the devotees  unfathomable  glory  
tāhe rāmānandera bhāva-bhakti-prema-sīmā  
among them  Rāmānanda’s  of bhāva, bhakti and prema  the limit

(21) The glory of Mahāprabhu’s devotees is unfathomable, and in Rāmānanda bhāva, bhakti and prema reach their utmost limit.

তবে সেই দুইজনে নৃত্য শিখাইলা ৷  
গীতের গূঢ় অর্থ অভিনয় করাইলা ॥ ২২ ॥

tabe sei dui-jane nṛtya śikhāilā  
then  those  two  dance  he taught  
gītera gūḍha artha abhinaya karāilā  
of the songs  hidden  meaning  acting  he made them do

(22) Then he taught the two to dance and had them act out the hidden meaning of the songs.[^7]

সঞ্চারী, সাত্ত্বিক, স্থায়ি-ভাবের লক্ষণ ৷  
মুখে-নেত্রে অভিনয় করে প্রকটন ॥ ২৩ ॥

sañcārī, sāttvika, sthāyi-bhāvera lakṣaṇa  
transitory  sāttvika  of the abiding emotions  the signs  
mukhe-netre abhinaya kare prakaṭana  
in face and eyes  acting  they make  manifest

(23) By acting with face and eyes, they brought out the signs of the transitory (*sañcārī*), the *sāttvika* and the abiding (*sthāyi*) feelings.

ভাবপ্রকটন-লাস্য রায় যে শিখায় ৷  
জগন্নাথের আগে দুঁহে প্রকট দেখায় ॥ ২৪ ॥

bhāva-prakaṭana-lāsya rāya ye śikhāya  
bhāva-revealing graceful dance  Rāya  which  teaches  
jagannāthera āge dum̐he prakaṭa dekhāya  
of Jagannātha  before  the two  openly  show

(24) Rāya taught them the graceful feminine dance (*lāsya*) that reveals bhāva, and the two would display it openly before Jagannātha.

তবে সেই দুইজনে প্রসাদ খাওয়াইলা ৷  
নিভৃতে দুঁহারে নিজ-ঘরে পাঠাইলা ॥ ২৫ ॥

tabe sei dui-jane prasāda khāoyāilā  
then  those  two  prasāda  he fed  
nibhṛte dum̐hāre nija-ghare pāṭhāilā  
quietly  both  to their own home  he sent

(25) Then he gave the two prasāda to eat and quietly sent them home.[^8]

প্রতিদিন রায় ঐছে করায় সাধন ৷  
কোন্ জানে ক্ষুদ্র জীব কাঁহা তাঁর মন ? ॥ ২৬ ॥

prati-dina rāya aiche karāya sādhana  
every day  Rāya  thus  makes them do  practice  
kon jāne kṣudra jīva kām̐hā tām̐ra mana?  
who  knows  petty  living being  where  his  mind

(26) Every day Rāya trained them in this way. What petty soul can know where his mind dwells?

মিশ্রের আগমন রায়ে সেবক কহিলা ৷  
শীঘ্র রামানন্দ তবে সভাতে আইলা ॥ ২৭ ॥

miśrera āgamana rāye sevaka kahilā  
Miśra’s  arrival  to Rāya  the servant  told  
śīghra rāmānanda tabe sabhāte āilā  
quickly  Rāmānanda  then  to the hall  came

(27) The servant told Rāya that Miśra had come, and Rāmānanda at once came to the hall.

মিশ্রেরে নমস্কার করে সম্মান করিয়া ৷  
নিবেদন করে কিছু বিনীত হঞা ॥ ২৮ ॥

miśrere namaskāra kare sammāna kariyā  
to Miśra  obeisance  he makes  respect  showing  
nivedana kare kichu vinīta hañā  
submission  he makes  something  humble  becoming

(28) Showing him respect, he bowed to Miśra and humbly said:

বহুক্ষণ আইলা, মোরে কেহ না কহিল ৷  
তোমার চরণে মোর অপরাধ হইল ॥ ২৯ ॥

bahu-kṣaṇa āilā, more keha nā kahila  
long ago  you came  me  anyone  not  told  
tomāra caraṇe mora aparādha haila  
at your  feet  my  offense  has been

(29) “You came long ago, and no one told me. I have committed an offence at your feet.

তোমার আগমনে মোর পবিত্র হৈল ঘর ৷  
আজ্ঞা কর, ক্যা করোঁ তোমার কিঙ্কর ॥ ৩০ ॥

tomāra āgamane mora pavitra haila ghara  
by your  coming  my  pure  became  home  
ājñā kara, kyā karom̐ tomāra kiṅkara  
order  do  what  shall I do  your  servant

(30) Your coming has purified my home. Command me — what shall I, your servant, do?”

মিশ্র কহে, — তোমা দেখিতে হৈল আগমনে ৷  
আপনা পবিত্র কৈলুঁ তোমার দরশনে ॥ ৩১ ॥

miśra kahe, — tomā dekhite haila āgamane  
Miśra  says  you  to see  there was  coming  
āpanā pavitra kailum̐ tomāra daraśane  
myself  pure  I made  by your  sight

(31) Miśra said, “I came to see you, and by seeing you I have purified myself.”

অতিকাল দেখি’ মিশ্র কিছু না কহিল ৷  
বিদায় হইয়া মিশ্র নিজঘর গেল ॥ ৩২ ॥

atikāla dekhi’ miśra kichu nā kahila  
the late hour  seeing  Miśra  anything  not  said  
vidāya haiyā miśra nijaghara gela  
leave  taking  Miśra  to his home  went

(32) Seeing that it was too late, Miśra said nothing more; he took his leave and went home.

আর দিন মিশ্র আইল প্রভু-বিদ্যমানে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিলা রায়স্থানে ? ॥ ৩৩ ॥

āra dina miśra āila prabhu-vidyamāne  
the next  day  Miśra  came  into the Lord’s presence  
prabhu kahe, — kṛṣṇa-kathā śunilā rāyasthāne?  
the Lord  says  about Kṛṣṇa talks  did you hear  from Rāya

(33) The next day Miśra came before the Lord, and the Lord asked, “Did you hear about Kṛṣṇa from Rāya?”

তবে মিশ্র রামানন্দের বৃত্তান্ত কহিলা ৷  
শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু তবে কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৩৪ ॥

tabe miśra rāmānandera vṛttānta kahilā  
then  Miśra  Rāmānanda’s  doings  told  
śuni’ mahāprabhu tabe kahite lāgilā  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  then  to speak  began

(34) Then Miśra told Him what Rāmānanda had been doing, and when Mahāprabhu heard it, He began to speak.

আমি ত’ সন্ন্যাসী, আপনারে বিরক্ত করি’ মানি ৷  
দর্শন রহু দূরে, ‘প্রকৃতি’র নাম যদি শুনি ॥ ৩৫ ॥

তবহিঁ বিকার পায় মোর তনু-মন ৷  
প্রকৃতি-দর্শনে স্থির হয় কোন্ জন ? ॥ ৩৬ ॥

āmi ta’ sannyāsī, āpanāre virakta kari’ māni  
I  indeed  a sannyāsī  Myself  detached  as  I consider  
darśana rahu dūre, ‘prakṛti’ra nāma yadi śuni  
seeing  let it remain  far  of a woman  the name  if  I hear  
tabahim̐ vikāra pāya mora tanu-mana  
even then  agitation  gets  My  body and mind  
prakṛti-darśane sthira haya kon jana?  
a woman  on seeing  steady  is  what  person

(35–36) “I am a sannyāsī and consider Myself detached; yet, to say nothing of seeing a woman, if I merely hear a woman’s name, My body and mind are disturbed. Who can remain steady on seeing a woman?[^9]

রামানন্দ-রায়ের কথা শুন, সর্ব্বজন ৷  
কহিবার নহে, যাহা আশ্চর্য্য-কথন ॥ ৩৭ ॥

rāmānanda-rāyera kathā śuna, sarva-jana  
of Rāmānanda Rāya  the account  hear  all people  
kahibāra nahe, yāhā āścarya-kathana  
to be told  it is not  which  a wondrous account

(37) Hear, all of you, about Rāmānanda Rāya — an account so wondrous that it can hardly be told.

একে দেবদাসী, আর সুন্দরী তরুণী ৷  
তাহাদের সব সেবা করেন আপনি ॥ ৩৮ ॥

eke deva-dāsī, āra sundarī taruṇī  
first  devadāsīs  and  beautiful  young women  
tāhādera saba sevā karena āpani  
for them  all  service  he does  himself

(38) First, they are *devadāsīs*, maidservants of the Deity, and moreover they are beautiful young women; yet he himself performs every service for them.[^10]

স্নানাদি করায়, পরায় বাস-বিভূষণ ৷  
গুহ্য অঙ্গ যত, তার দর্শন-স্পর্শন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

snānādi karāya, parāya vāsa-vibhūṣaṇa  
bathing and so on  he has them do  he puts on  clothes and ornaments  
guhya aṅga yata, tāra darśana-sparśana  
private  parts  all  of them  seeing and touching

(39) He bathes them and dresses them in clothes and ornaments, and he sees and touches all the hidden parts of their bodies.

তবু নির্ব্বিকার রায়-রামানন্দের মন ৷  
নানাভাবোদ্গম তারে করায় শিক্ষণ ॥ ৪০ ॥

tabu nirvikāra rāya-rāmānandera mana  
yet  unmoved  of Rāya Rāmānanda  the mind  
nānābhāvodgama tāre karāya śikṣaṇa  
the rising of various bhāvas  them  he makes  learn

(40) Yet the mind of Rāya Rāmānanda remains unmoved as he teaches them to express the stirrings of many different bhāvas.[^11]

নির্ব্বিকার দেহ-মন — কাষ্ঠ-পাষাণ সম !  
আশ্চর্য্য, — তরুণী-স্পর্শে নির্ব্বিকার মন ! ॥ ৪১ ॥

nirvikāra deha-mana — kāṣṭha-pāṣāṇa sama!  
unmoved  body and mind  wood and stone  like  
āścarya, — taruṇī-sparśe nirvikāra mana!  
wonderful  a young woman  at touching  unmoved  mind

(41) His body and mind are unmoved, like wood or stone. How wonderful — at the touch of a young woman his mind is unmoved!

এক রামানন্দের হয় এই অধিকার ৷  
তাতে জানি অপ্রাকৃত-দেহ তাঁহার ॥ ৪২ ॥

eka rāmānandera haya ei adhikāra  
alone  of Rāmānanda  is  this  qualification  
tāte jāni aprākṛta-deha tām̐hāra  
from that  I know  transcendental  body  his

(42) Rāmānanda alone has this qualification; from it I know that his body is transcendental.

তাঁহার মনের ভাব তেঁহ জানে মাত্র ৷  
তাহা জানিবারে আর দ্বিতীয় নাহি পাত্র ॥ ৪৩ ॥

tām̐hāra manera bhāva tem̐ha jāne mātra  
his  mind’s  state  he  knows  only  
tāhā jānibāre āra dvitīya nāhi pātra  
that  to know  other  second  there is not  fit person

(43) Only he knows the state of his mind; there is no one else fit to know it.

কিন্তু শাস্ত্রদৃষ্ট্যে করি এক অনুমান ৷  
শ্রীভাগবত-শাস্ত্র — তাহাতে প্রমাণ ॥ ৪৪ ॥

kintu śāstra-dṛṣṭye kari eka anumāna  
but  in the light of scripture  I make  one  inference  
śrī-bhāgavata-śāstra — tāhāte pramāṇa  
the Śrī Bhāgavata scripture  of that  the evidence

(44) But in the light of scripture I draw one inference. The *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* bears witness to this.[^12]

ব্রজবধূ-সঙ্গে কৃষ্ণের রাসাদি-বিলাস ৷  
যেই জন কহে, শুনে করিয়া বিশ্বাস ॥ ৪৫ ॥

হৃদ্রোগ-কাম তাঁর তৎকালে হয় ক্ষয় ৷  
তিনগুণ-ক্ষোভ নহে, ‘মহাধীর’ হয় ॥ ৪৬ ॥

vraja-vadhū-saṅge kṛṣṇera rāsādi-vilāsa  
with the young women of Vraja  Kṛṣṇa’s  the rāsa and other pastimes  
yei jana kahe, śune kariyā viśvāsa  
which  person  tells  hears  having  faith  
hṛd-roga-kāma tām̐ra tat-kāle haya kṣaya  
the heart’s disease, lust  his  at once  is  destroyed  
tina-guṇa-kṣobha nahe, ‘mahā-dhīra’  haya  
of the three guṇas  agitation  is not  very steady  he becomes

(45–46) When a person tells of or hears with faith about Kṛṣṇa’s rāsa dance and other pastimes with the young women of Vraja, his lust, the disease of the heart, is destroyed at once. The three guṇas no longer agitate him, and he becomes perfectly steady (*mahā-dhīra*).[^13]

উজ্জ্বল মধুর-রস প্রেমভক্তি পায় ৷  
আনন্দে কৃষ্ণমাধুর্য্যে বিহরে সদায় ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ujjvala madhura-rasa prema-bhakti pāya  
radiant  of madhura-rasa  prema-bhakti  he attains  
ānande kṛṣṇa-mādhurye vihare sadāya  
in bliss  in Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  he sports  always

(47) He attains prema-bhakti in the radiant *madhura-rasa*, and in bliss he ever revels in Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness.

বিক্রীড়িতং ব্রজবধূভিরিদঞ্চ বিষ্ণোঃ  
শ্রদ্ধান্বিতোঽনুশৃণুয়াদথ বর্ণয়েদ্ যঃ ৷  
ভক্তিং পরাং ভগবতি প্রতিলভ্য কামং  
হৃদ্রোগমাশ্বপহিনোত্যচিরেণ ধীরঃ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

vikrīḍitaṁ vraja-vadhūbhir idaṁ ca viṣṇoḥ  
the play  with the young women of Vraja  this  and  of Viṣṇu  
śraddhānvito ’nuśṛṇuyād atha varṇayed yaḥ  
endowed with faith  constantly hears  then  describes  who  
bhaktiṁ parāṁ bhagavati pratilabhya kāmaṁ  
devotion  supreme  for the Lord  having attained  lust  
hṛd-rogam āśv apahinoty acireṇa dhīraḥ  
the heart’s disease  quickly  casts off  soon  steadfast

(48) “The steadfast soul who, endowed with faith, constantly hears of this play of Viṣṇu with the young women of Vraja and then describes it, soon attains supreme devotion to the Lord and quickly casts off lust, the disease of the heart.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.39)[^14]

যে শুনে, যে পড়ে, তাঁর ফল এতাদৃশী ৷  
সেই ভাবাবিষ্ট, যেই সেবে অহর্নিশি ॥ ৪৯ ॥

ye śune, ye paḍe, tām̐ra phala etādṛśī  
who  hears  who  reads  his  fruit  is such  
sei bhāvāviṣṭa, yei seve ahar-niśi  
he  absorbed in bhāva  who  serves  day and night

(49) Such is the fruit for whoever hears or reads of these pastimes. But as for one who, absorbed in bhāva, serves day and night —[^15]

তাঁর ফল কি কহিমু, কহনে না যায় ৷  
নিত্যসিদ্ধ সেই, প্রায়-সিদ্ধ তাঁর কায় ॥ ৫০ ॥

tām̐ra phala ki kahimu, kahane nā yāya  
his  fruit  what  shall I tell  in words  not  it goes  
nitya-siddha sei, prāya-siddha tām̐ra kāya  
eternally perfect  he  almost perfect  his  body

(50) what can I say of his fruit? It cannot be put into words. He is eternally perfect (*nitya-siddha*), and his body is almost perfect (*prāya-siddha*).[^16]

রাগানুগ-মার্গে জানি রায়ের ভজন ৷  
সিদ্ধদেহ-তুল্য, তাতে ‘প্রাকৃত’ নহে মন ॥ ৫১ ॥

rāgānuga-mārge jāni rāyera bhajana  
on the rāgānuga path  I know  Rāya’s  worship  
siddha-deha-tulya, tāte ‘prākṛta’ nahe mana  
to a perfected body  equal  therefore  mundane  is not  the mind

(51) I know that Rāya’s worship follows the path of rāgānuga. His body is like a perfected spiritual body (*siddha-deha*), and so his mind is not mundane.

আমিহ রায়ের স্থানে শুনি কৃষ্ণকথা ৷  
শুনিতে ইচ্ছা হয় যদি, পুনঃ যাহ’ তথা ॥ ৫২ ॥

āmiha rāyera sthāne śuni kṛṣṇa-kathā  
I too  Rāya’s  at the place  hear  talks about Kṛṣṇa  
śunite icchā haya yadi, punaḥ yāha’ tathā  
to hear  desire  is  if  again  go  there

(52) I too hear about Kṛṣṇa from Rāya. If you wish to hear, go there again.

মোর নামে কহিহ, — তেঁহো পাঠাইলা মোরে ৷  
তোমার স্থানে কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিবার তরে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

mora nāme kahiha, — tem̐ho pāṭhāilā more  
in My  name  say  He  sent  me  
tomāra sthāne kṛṣṇa-kathā śunibāra tare  
your  at the place  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  in order

(53) Tell him in My name: ‘He has sent me to hear about Kṛṣṇa from you.’

শীঘ্র যাহ’, যাবৎ তেঁহো আছেন সভাতে ৷  
এত শুনি’ প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্র চলিলা ত্বরিতে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

śīghra yāha’, yāvat tem̐ho āchena sabhāte  
quickly  go  while  he  is  in the hall  
eta śuni’ pradyumna-miśra calilā tvarite  
this  hearing  Pradyumna Miśra  went  in haste

(54) Go quickly, while he is still in the hall.” Hearing this, Pradyumna Miśra set off in haste.

রায়-পাশ গেল, রায় প্রণতি করিল ৷  
আজ্ঞা কর, যে লাগি’ আগমন হৈল ॥ ৫৫ ॥

rāya-pāśa gela, rāya praṇati karila  
to Rāya  he went  Rāya  obeisance  made  
ājñā kara, ye lāgi’ āgamana haila  
order  do  what  for  coming  has been

(55) He went to Rāya, and Rāya bowed to him and said, “Command me; tell me why you have come.”

মিশ্র কহে, — মহাপ্রভু পাঠাইলা মোরে ৷  
তোমার স্থানে কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিবার তরে ॥ ৫৬ ॥

miśra kahe, — mahāprabhu pāṭhāilā more  
Miśra  says  Mahāprabhu  sent  me  
tomāra sthāne kṛṣṇa-kathā śunibāra tare  
your  at the place  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  in order

(56) Miśra said, “Mahāprabhu has sent me to hear about Kṛṣṇa from you.”

শুনি’ রামানন্দ মনে হইলা সন্তোষে ৷  
কহিতে লাগিলা কিছু মনের হরিষে ॥ ৫৭ ॥

śuni’ rāmānanda mane hailā santoṣe  
hearing  Rāmānanda  in mind  became  pleased  
kahite lāgilā kichu manera hariṣe  
to speak  began  something  of the mind  in joy

(57) Hearing this, Rāmānanda was pleased, and with joy in his heart he began to speak:

প্রভুর আজ্ঞায় কৃষ্ণকথা শুনিতে আইলা এথা ৷  
ইহা বই মহাভাগ্য আমি পাব কোথা ? ॥ ৫৮ ॥

prabhura ājñāya kṛṣṇa-kathā śunite āilā ethā  
of the Lord  by the order  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  you came  here  
ihā vai mahābhāgya āmi pāba kothā?  
this  besides  great fortune  I  shall find  where

(58) “By the Lord’s order you have come here to hear about Kṛṣṇa. Where else could I find such great fortune?”

এত কহি’ তারে লঞা নিভৃতে বসিলা ৷  
কি কথা শুনিতে চাহ? মিশ্রেরে পুছিলা ॥ ৫৯ ॥

eta kahi’ tāre lañā nibhṛte vasilā  
this  saying  him  taking  in seclusion  he sat  
ki kathā śunite cāha? miśrere puchilā  
what  topic  to hear  do you want  Miśra  he asked

(59) Saying this, he took him to a secluded place, sat down with him and asked Miśra, “What would you like to hear?”

তেঁহো কহে, — যে কহিলা বিদ্যানগরে ৷  
সেই কথা ক্রমে তুমি কহিবা আমারে ॥ ৬০ ॥

tem̐ho kahe, — ye kahilā vidyānagare  
he  says  what  you told  in Vidyānagara  
sei kathā krame tumi kahibā āmāre  
those  topics  in order  you  please tell  me

(60) He said, “Please tell me, in order, all that you spoke of in Vidyānagara.[^17]

আনের কি কথা, তুমি — প্রভুর উপদেষ্টা !  
আমি ত’ ভিক্ষুক বিপ্র, তুমি — মোর পোষ্টা ॥ ৬১ ॥

ānera ki kathā, tumi — prabhura upadeṣṭā!  
of others  what  talk  you  of the Lord  the instructor  
āmi ta’ bhikṣuka vipra, tumi — mora poṣṭā  
I  indeed  a mendicant  brāhmaṇa  you  my  sustainer

(61) You are the instructor not only of others but of the Lord Himself! I am a poor mendicant brāhmaṇa, and you are my sustainer.

ভাল, মন্দ — কিছু আমি পুছিতে না জানি ৷  
‘দীন’ দেখি’ কৃপা করি’ কহিবা আপনি ॥ ৬২ ॥

bhāla, manda — kichu āmi puchite nā jāni  
good  bad  anything  I  to ask  not  know  
‘dīna’ dekhi’ kṛpā kari’ kahibā āpani  
wretched  seeing  mercy  showing  please tell  yourself

(62) I do not know how to ask anything, good or bad. Seeing how wretched I am, be merciful and tell me of your own accord.”

তবে রামানন্দ ক্রমে কহিতে লাগিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথা-রসামৃত-সিন্ধু উথলিলা ॥ ৬৩ ॥

tabe rāmānanda krame kahite lāgilā  
then  Rāmānanda  in order  to speak  began  
kṛṣṇa-kathā-rasāmṛta-sindhu uthalilā  
the ocean of the nectar of rasa in talks about Kṛṣṇa  surged

(63) Then Rāmānanda began to speak in due order, and the nectar-ocean of rasa in his talk of Kṛṣṇa surged up.

আপনে প্রশ্ন করি’ পাছে করেন সিদ্ধান্ত ৷  
তৃতীয় প্রহর হৈল, নহে কথা-অন্ত ॥ ৬৪ ॥

āpane praśna kari’ pāche karena siddhānta  
himself  question  raising  afterward  he makes  conclusion  
tṛtīya prahara haila, nahe kathā-anta  
the third  prahara  came  there is not  of the talk  end

(64) He himself raised the questions and then gave the conclusive answers (*siddhānta*). The third watch (*prahara*) of the day came, and still the talk did not end.

বক্তা শ্রোতা কহে শুনে দুঁহে প্রেমাবেশে ৷  
আত্মস্মৃতি নাহি, কাঁহা জানে দিন শেষে ॥ ৬৫ ॥

vaktā śrotā kahe śune dum̐he premāveśe  
the speaker  the listener  speaks  hears  both  in the absorption of prema  
ātmasmṛti nāhi, kām̐hā jāne dina śeṣe  
self-awareness  there is not  how  they know  of the day  the end

(65) Speaker and listener, one speaking and the other hearing, were both absorbed in prema. They had no awareness of themselves — how could they know the day had ended?

সেবক কহিল, — ‘দিন হৈল অবসান’ ৷  
তবে রায় কৃষ্ণকথার করিলা বিশ্রাম ॥ ৬৬ ॥

sevaka kahila, — ‘dina haila avasāna’  
the servant  said  the day  has come  to an end  
tabe rāya kṛṣṇakathāra karilā viśrāma  
then  Rāya  to the talk about Kṛṣṇa  made  a pause

(66) When the servant said, “The day is over,” Rāya brought his talk of Kṛṣṇa to a close.

বহুসম্মান করি’ মিশ্রে বিদায় দিলা ৷  
কৃতার্থ হইলাঙ বলি’ মিশ্র নাচিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৬৭ ॥

bahu-sammāna kari’ miśre vidāya dilā  
great respect  showing  to Miśra  leave  he gave  
kṛtārtha hailāṅa bali’ miśra nācite lāgilā  
fulfilled  I have become  saying  Miśra  to dance  began

(67) With great respect he bade Miśra farewell, and Miśra, crying, “My life is fulfilled!” began to dance.

ঘরে গিয়া মিশ্র কৈল স্নান, ভোজন ৷  
সন্ধ্যাকালে দেখিতে আইল প্রভুর চরণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

ghare giyā miśra kaila snāna, bhojana  
home  going  Miśra  did  bathing  eating  
sandhyākāle dekhite āila prabhura caraṇa  
in the evening  to see  he came  of the Lord  the feet

(68) Miśra went home, bathed and took his meal, and in the evening he came to see the Lord.

প্রভুর চরণ বন্দে উল্লসিত-মনে ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — কৃষ্ণকথা হইল শ্রবণে ? ॥ ৬৯ ॥

prabhura  caraṇa  vande  ullasita-mane  
the Lord’s  feet  he worships  with a joyful mind  
prabhu kahe, — kṛṣṇa-kathā haila śravaṇe?  
the Lord  says  talks about Kṛṣṇa  was there  hearing

(69) With a joyful heart he bowed at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord asked, “Did you hear about Kṛṣṇa?”

মিশ্র কহে, — প্রভু, মোরে কৃতার্থ করিলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণকথামৃতার্ণবে মোরে ডুবাইলা ॥ ৭০ ॥

miśra kahe, — prabhu, more kṛtārtha karilā  
Miśra  says  O Lord  me  fulfilled  You have made  
kṛṣṇa-kathāmṛtārṇave more ḍubāilā  
in the ocean of the nectar of talks about Kṛṣṇa  me  You have plunged

(70) Miśra said, “O Lord, You have fulfilled my life! You have plunged me into the nectar-ocean of talks about Kṛṣṇa.

রামানন্দ-রায়-কথা কহিলে না হয় ৷  
‘মনুষ্য’ নহে রায়, কৃষ্ণভক্তিরসময় ॥ ৭১ ॥

rāmānanda-rāya-kathā  kahile  nā haya  
of Rāmānanda Rāya  the words  if told  not  it is possible  
‘manuṣya’ nahe rāya, kṛṣṇa-bhakti-rasa-maya  
a human being  is not  Rāya  made of the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa

(71) The words of Rāmānanda Rāya cannot be retold. Rāya is not a mere human being; he is made of the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.[^18]

আর এক কথা রায় কহিলা আমারে ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণকথা-বক্তা করি’ না জানিহ মোরে ॥ ৭২ ॥

āra  eka  kathā  rāya  kahilā  āmāre  
another  one  thing  Rāya  told  me  
‘kṛṣṇa-kathā-vaktā kari’ nā jāniha more  
of talks about Kṛṣṇa  the speaker  as  not  consider  me

(72) Rāya told me one thing more: ‘Do not think of me as the speaker of these talks about Kṛṣṇa.

মোর মুখে কথা কহেন আপনে গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
যৈছে কহায়, তৈছে কহি, — যেন বীণাযন্ত্র ॥ ৭৩ ॥

mora mukhe kathā kahena āpane gauracandra  
by my  mouth  words  speaks  Himself  Gauracandra  
yaiche kahāya, taiche kahi, — yena vīṇā-yantra  
as  He makes me speak  so  I speak  like  a vīṇā instrument

(73) Gauracandra Himself speaks these words through my mouth. As He makes me speak, so I speak, like a *vīṇā*.

মোর মুখে কথা ইঁহা করে পরচার ৷  
পৃথিবীতে কে জানিবে এ-লীলা তাঁহার ? ॥ ৭৪ ॥

mora mukhe kathā im̐hā kare paracāra  
by my  mouth  the words  He  makes  known  
pṛthivīte ke jānibe e-līlā tām̐hāra?  
in the world  who  will know  this pastime  His

(74) Through my mouth He makes these words known. Who in this world will understand this pastime of His?’

যে-সব শুনিলুঁ, কৃষ্ণ — রসের সাগর ৷  
ব্রহ্মাদি-দেবের এ সব না হয় গোচর ॥ ৭৫ ॥

ye-saba śunilum̐, kṛṣṇa — rasera sāgara  
all that  I heard  Kṛṣṇa  of rasa  the ocean  
brahmādi-devera e saba nā haya gocara  
to Brahmā and the other gods  this  all  not  is  perceptible

(75) All that I heard — that Kṛṣṇa is the ocean of rasa — lies beyond the reach even of Brahmā and the other gods.

হেন ‘রস’ মোরে পান করাইলা তুমি ৷  
জন্মে জন্মে তোমার পায় বিকাইলাঙ আমি ॥ ৭৬ ॥

hena ‘rasa’ more pāna karāilā tumi  
such  rasa  me  to drink  made  You  
janme janme tomāra pāya vikāilāṅa āmi  
birth  after birth  Your  at the feet  sold myself  I

(76) You have made me drink such rasa; birth after birth I have sold myself at Your feet.”

প্রভু কহে, — রামানন্দ বিনয়ের খনি ৷  
আপনার কথা পরমুণ্ডে দেন আনি ॥ ৭৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — rāmānanda vinayera khani  
the Lord  says  Rāmānanda  of humility  a mine  
āpanāra kathā para-muṇḍe dena āni  
his own  words  on another’s head  he places  bringing

(77) The Lord said, “Rāmānanda is a mine of humility. He attributes his own words to another.

মহানুভবের এই সহজ ‘স্বভাব’ হয় ৷  
আপনার গুণ নাহি আপনে কহয় ॥ ৭৮ ॥

mahānubhavera ei sahaja ‘svabhāva’ haya  
of great souls  this  natural  disposition  is  
āpanāra guṇa nāhi āpane kahaya  
their own  qualities  not  themselves  they tell

(78) This is the natural disposition of great souls: they never speak of their own qualities.”

রামানন্দ-রায়ের এই কহিলুঁ গুণ-লেশ ৷  
প্রদ্যুম্ন-মিশ্রেরে যৈছে কৈলা উপদেশ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

rāmānanda-rāyera ei kahilum̐ guṇa-leśa  
of Rāmānanda Rāya  this  I have told  of qualities  a trace  
pradyumna-miśrere yaiche kailā upadeśa  
to Pradyumna Miśra  how  he gave  instruction

(79) Thus I have told a little of Rāmānanda Rāya’s qualities and of how he instructed Pradyumna Miśra.

‘গৃহস্থ’ হঞা নহে রায় ষড়্-বর্গের বশে ৷  
‘বিষয়ী’ হঞা সন্ন্যাসীরে উপদেশে ॥ ৮০ ॥

‘gṛhastha’ hañā nahe rāya ṣaḍ-vargera vaśe  
a householder  being  not  Rāya  of the six enemies  under the control  
‘viṣayī’ hañā sannyāsīre upadeśe  
a worldly man  being  sannyāsīs  he instructs

(80) Though a householder, Rāya is not under the control of the six enemies (*ṣaḍ-varga*); though seemingly a worldly man (*viṣayī*), he instructs sannyāsīs.[^19]

এইসব গুণ তাঁর প্রকাশ করিতে ৷  
মিশ্রেরে পাঠাইলা তাঁহা শ্রবণ করিতে ॥ ৮১ ॥

eisaba guṇa tām̐ra prakāśa karite  
all these  qualities  his  manifest  to make  
miśrere pāṭhāilā tām̐hā śravaṇa karite  
Miśra  He sent  there  hearing  to do

(81) To reveal all these qualities of his, the Lord sent Miśra there to hear.

ভক্তগুণ প্রকাশিতে প্রভু ভাল জানে ৷  
নানা-ভঙ্গীতে প্রকাশি’ নিজ লাভ মানে ॥ ৮২ ॥

bhakta-guṇa prakāśite prabhu bhāla jāne  
the devotee’s qualities  to reveal  the Lord  well  knows  
nānā-bhaṅgīte prakāśi’ nija lābha māne  
in various ways  revealing  His own  gain  He considers

(82) The Lord knows well how to reveal the qualities of His devotees; revealing them in various ways, He considers it His own gain.

আর এক ‘স্বভাব’ গৌরের শুন, ভক্তগণ ৷  
গূঢ় ঐশ্বর্য্য-স্বভাব করে প্রকটন ॥ ৮৩ ॥

āra eka ‘svabhāva’ gaurera śuna, bhakta-gaṇa  
another  one  ‘trait’  of Gaura  hear  O devotees  
gūḍha aiśvarya-svabhāva kare prakaṭana  
hidden  majestic nature  He makes  manifest

(83) Hear, O devotees, of yet another trait of Gaura: He makes His hidden, majestic nature manifest.

সন্ন্যাসী-পণ্ডিতগণের করিতে গর্ব্ব নাশ ৷  
নীচ-শূদ্র-দ্বারা করেন ধর্ম্মের প্রকাশ ॥ ৮৪ ॥

sannyāsī-paṇḍita-gaṇera karite garva nāśa  
of the sannyāsīs and paṇḍitas  to make  of the pride  destruction  
nīca-śūdra-dvārā karena dharmera prakāśa  
through a lowly śūdra  He makes  of dharma  the manifestation

(84) To destroy the pride of the sannyāsīs and paṇḍitas, He reveals dharma through a lowly śūdra.[^20]

‘ভক্তি’, ‘প্রেম’, ‘তত্ত্ব’ কহে রায়ে করি’ ‘বক্তা’ ৷  
আপনি প্রদ্যুম্নমিশ্র-সহ হয় ‘শ্রোতা’ ॥ ৮৫ ॥

‘bhakti’, ‘prema’, ‘tattva’ kahe rāye kari’ ‘vaktā’  
‘bhakti’  ‘prema’  ‘tattva’  He speaks  Rāya  making  ‘the speaker’  
āpani pradyumna-miśra-saha haya ‘śrotā’  
Himself  with Pradyumna Miśra  becomes  ‘the listener’

(85) He speaks of bhakti, prema and the truth (*tattva*), making Rāya the speaker, while He Himself, together with Pradyumna Miśra, becomes the listener.[^21]

হরিদাস-দ্বারা নাম-মাহাত্ম্য-প্রকাশ ৷  
সনাতন-দ্বারা ভক্তিসিদ্ধান্ত-বিলাস ॥ ৮৬ ॥

haridāsa-dvārā nāma-māhātmya-prakāśa  
through Haridāsa  the glory of the holy name  the manifestation  
sanātana-dvārā bhakti-siddhānta-vilāsa  
through Sanātana  the play of the conclusions of bhakti

(86) Through Haridāsa He revealed the glory of the holy name; through Sanātana, the conclusions of bhakti in all their splendour.[^22]

শ্রীরূপ-দ্বারা ব্রজের রস-প্রেম-লীলা ৷  
কে কহিতে পারে গম্ভীর চৈতন্যের খেলা ? ॥ ৮৭ ॥

śrī-rūpa-dvārā vrajera rasa-prema-līlā  
through Śrī Rūpa  of Vraja  the pastimes of rasa and prema  
ke kahite pāre gambhīra caitanyera khelā?  
who  to tell  can  profound  Caitanya’s  play

(87) Through Śrī Rūpa He revealed Vraja’s pastimes of rasa and prema. Who can describe the profound play of Caitanya?[^23]

শ্রীচৈতন্যলীলা এই — অমৃতের সিন্ধু ৷  
জগৎ ভাসাইতে পারে যার এক বিন্দু ॥ ৮৮ ॥

śrī-caitanya-līlā ei — amṛtera sindhu  
Śrī Caitanya’s pastimes  these  of nectar  an ocean  
jagat  bhāsāite  pāre  yāra  eka  bindu  
the world  to flood  can  whose  one  drop

(88) These pastimes of Śrī Caitanya are an ocean of nectar, a single drop of which can flood the world.

চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত নিত্য কর পান ৷  
যাহা হৈতে ‘প্রেমানন্দ’, ‘ভক্তিতত্ত্ব-জ্ঞান’ ॥ ৮৯ ॥

caitanya-caritāmṛta nitya kara pāna  
the Caitanya-caritāmṛta  always  do  drink  
yāhā haite ‘premānanda’, ‘bhakti-tattva-jñāna’  
from which  ‘the bliss of prema’  ‘knowledge of the truth of bhakti’

(89) Drink the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* constantly, for from it come the bliss of prema and knowledge of the truth of bhakti.

এইমত মহাপ্রভু ভক্তগণ লঞা ৷  
নীলাচলে বিহরয়ে ভক্তি প্রচারিয়া ॥ ৯০ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu bhakta-gaṇa lañā  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  the devotees  taking with Him  
nīlācale  viharaye  bhakti  pracāriyā  
in Nīlācala  sports  bhakti  preaching

(90) In this way Mahāprabhu, together with His devotees, enjoyed His pastimes in Nīlācala, preaching bhakti.

বঙ্গদেশী এক বিপ্র প্রভুর চরিতে ৷  
নাটক করি’ লঞা আইলা শুনাইতে ॥ ৯১ ॥

baṅga-deśī eka vipra prabhura carite  
from Bengal  a  brāhmaṇa  on the Lord’s  life  
nāṭaka kari’ lañā āilā śunāite  
a drama  having made  taking  came  to recite

(91) A brāhmaṇa from Bengal composed a drama on the Lord’s life and brought it with him to recite.[^24]

ভগবান্-আচার্য্য-সনে তার পরিচয় ৷  
তাঁরে মিলি’ তাঁর ঘরে করিল আলয় ॥ ৯২ ॥

bhagavān-ācārya-sane tāra paricaya  
Bhagavān Ācārya  with  his  acquaintance  
tām̐re mili’ tām̐ra ghare karila ālaya  
him  having met  in his  house  made  his lodging

(92) He was acquainted with Bhagavān Ācārya; having met him, he took lodging in his house.

প্রথমে নাটক তেঁহো তাঁরে শুনাইল ৷  
তাঁর সঙ্গে অনেক বৈষ্ণব নাটক শুনিল ॥ ৯৩ ॥

prathame nāṭaka tem̐ho tām̐re śunāila  
first  the drama  he  to him  recited  
tām̐ra saṅge aneka vaiṣṇava nāṭaka śunila  
with him  many  Vaiṣṇavas  the drama  heard

(93) First he recited the drama to him, and many Vaiṣṇavas heard it along with him.

সবেই প্রশংসে নাটক ‘পরম উত্তম’ ৷  
মহাপ্রভুরে শুনাইতে সবার হৈল মন ॥ ৯৪ ॥

sabei praśaṁse nāṭaka ‘parama uttama’  
all  praise  the drama  ‘most excellent’  
mahāprabhure śunāite sabāra haila mana  
to Mahāprabhu  to recite  of all  arose  the wish

(94) Everyone praised the drama as “most excellent”, and all wished to have Mahāprabhu hear it.

গীত, শ্লোক, গ্রন্থ, কবিত্ব — যেই করি’ আনে ৷  
প্রথমে শুনায় সেই স্বরূপের স্থানে ॥ ৯৫ ॥

gīta, śloka, grantha, kavitva — yei kari’ āne  
song  verse  book  poem  —  whatever  having made  one brings  
prathame śunāya sei svarūpera sthāne  
first  has it heard  that  in Svarūpa’s  presence

(95) Whatever song, verse, book or poem anyone composed and brought, he would first recite it before Svarūpa.

স্বরূপ-ঠাঞি উত্তরে যদি, লয় তাঁর মন ৷  
তবে মহাপ্রভু-ঠাঞি করায় শ্রবণ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

svarūpa-ṭhāñi uttare yadi, laya tām̐ra mana  
before Svarūpa  passes  if  accepts  his  mind  
tabe mahāprabhu-ṭhāñi karāya śravaṇa  
then  before Mahāprabhu  causes  the hearing

(96) If it passed Svarūpa’s scrutiny and won his approval, only then would it be recited before Mahāprabhu.[^25]

‘রসাভাস’ হয় যদি ‘সিদ্ধান্তবিরোধ’ ৷  
সহিতে না পারে প্রভু, মনে হয় ক্রোধ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

‘rasābhāsa’ haya yadi ‘siddhānta-virodha’  
‘rasābhāsa’  is  if  ‘opposition to siddhānta’  
sahite nā pāre prabhu, mane haya krodha  
to bear  not  can  the Lord  in the mind  arises  anger

(97) If there was *rasābhāsa*, a mere semblance of rasa, or anything opposed to *siddhānta*, the Lord could not bear it, and anger arose in His mind.[^26]

অতএব প্রভু কিছু আগে নাহি শুনে ৷  
এই মর্য্যাদা প্রভু কৈরাছেন নিয়মে ॥ ৯৮ ॥

ataeva prabhu kichu āge nāhi śune  
therefore  the Lord  anything  first  not  hears  
ei maryādā prabhu kairāchena niyame  
this  rule of propriety  the Lord  has made  as a fixed rule

(98) Therefore the Lord would hear nothing that Svarūpa had not examined first; He had made this a fixed rule of propriety.

স্বরূপের ঠাঞি আচার্য্য কৈলা নিবেদন ৷  
এক কবি প্রভুর নাটক কৈরাছে উত্তম ॥ ৯৯ ॥

svarūpera ṭhāñi ācārya kailā nivedana  
before Svarūpa  the Ācārya  made  a request  
eka kavi prabhura nāṭaka kairāche uttama  
a  poet  on the Lord  a drama  has composed  excellent

(99) Bhagavān Ācārya made a request to Svarūpa: “A poet has composed an excellent drama about the Lord.[^27]

আদৌ তুমি শুন, যদি তোমার মন মানে ৷  
পাছে মহাপ্রভুরে তবে করাইমু শ্রবণে ॥ ১০০ ॥

ādau tumi śuna, yadi tomāra mana māne  
first  you  hear  if  your  mind  accepts  
pāche mahāprabhure tabe karāimu śravaṇe  
afterwards  Mahāprabhu  then  I shall have  hear

(100) Hear it first yourself, and if you approve of it, I shall then have Mahāprabhu hear it.”

স্বরূপ কহে, — তুমি ‘গোপ’ পরম-উদার ৷  
যে-সে শাস্ত্র শুনিতে ইচ্ছা উপজে তোমার ॥ ১০১ ॥

svarūpa kahe, — tumi ‘gopa’ parama-udāra  
Svarūpa  says  —  you  a ‘cowherd’  most generous  
ye-se śāstra śunite icchā upaje tomāra  
any whatever  śāstra  to hear  desire  arises  your

(101) Svarūpa said, “You are a simple ‘cowherd’ (*gopa*), and most generous: you wish to hear any writing whatever.[^28]

‘যদ্বা-তদ্বা’ কবির বাক্যে হয় ‘রসাভাস’ ৷  
সিদ্ধান্তবিরুদ্ধ শুনিতে না হয় উল্লাস ॥ ১০২ ॥

‘yadvā-tadvā’ kavira vākye haya ‘rasābhāsa’  
‘any whatever’  of a poet  in the words  is  ‘rasābhāsa’  
siddhānta-viruddha śunite nā haya ullāsa  
opposed to siddhānta  to hear  not  arises  joy

(102) In the words of just any poet there is *rasābhāsa*, and hearing what is opposed to *siddhānta* brings no joy.[^29]

‘রস’, ‘রসাভাস’ যার নাহিক বিচার ৷  
ভক্তিসিদ্ধান্ত-সিন্ধু নাহি পায় পার ॥ ১০৩ ॥

‘rasa’, ‘rasābhāsa’ yāra nāhika vicāra  
‘rasa’  ‘rasābhāsa’  whose  there is no  discrimination  
bhakti-siddhānta-sindhu nāhi pāya pāra  
of the conclusions of bhakti  the ocean  not  reaches  the shore

(103) One who cannot discriminate between rasa and *rasābhāsa* never reaches the far shore of the ocean of *bhakti-siddhānta*.

‘ব্যাকরণ’ নাহি জানে, না জানে ‘অলঙ্কার’ ৷  
‘নাটকালঙ্কার’-জ্ঞান নাহিক যাহার ॥ ১০৪ ॥

কৃষ্ণলীলা বর্ণিতে না জানে সেই ছার !  
বিশেষে দুর্গম এই চৈতন্য-বিহার ! ॥ ১০৫ ॥

‘vyākaraṇa’ nāhi jāne, nā jāne ‘alaṅkāra’  
‘grammar’  not  knows  not  knows  ‘rhetoric’  
‘nāṭakālaṅkāra’-jñāna nāhika yāhāra  
‘of the ornaments of drama’  knowledge  there is no  whose  
kṛṣṇa-līlā varṇite nā jāne sei chāra!  
Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  to describe  not  knows  that  worthless one  
viśeṣe durgama ei caitanya-vihāra!  
especially  inaccessible  this  play of Caitanya

(104–105) A worthless poet who knows neither grammar nor rhetoric, and has no knowledge of the ornaments of drama, does not know how to describe Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes; and these pastimes of Caitanya are especially hard to enter!

কৃষ্ণলীলা, গৌরলীলা সে করে বর্ণন ৷  
গৌর-পাদপদ্ম যাঁর হয় প্রাণ-ধন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa-līlā, gaura-līlā se kare varṇana  
Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  Gaura’s pastimes  he  does  describe  
gaura-pāda-padma yām̐ra haya prāṇa-dhana  
Gaura’s  lotus feet  whose  are  the wealth of life

(106) Only one for whom the lotus feet of Gaura are the very wealth of life can describe the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa and the pastimes of Gaura.

গ্রাম্য-কবির কবিত্ব শুনিতে হয় ‘দুঃখ’ ৷  
বিদগ্ধ-আত্মীয়-বাক্য শুনিতে হয় ‘সুখ’ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

grāmya-kavira kavitva śunite haya ‘duḥkha’  
of a mundane poet  the poetry  to hear  is  ‘pain’  
vidagdha-ātmīya-vākya śunite haya ‘sukha’  
of one expert and intimate  the words  to hear  is  ‘joy’

(107) To hear the poetry of a mundane poet (*grāmya-kavi*) is painful; to hear the words of one who is expert and intimate (*vidagdha ātmīya*) is a joy.[^30]

রূপ যৈছে দুই নাটক কৈরাছে আরম্ভে ৷  
শুনিতে আনন্দ বাড়ে যার মুখবন্ধে ॥ ১০৮ ॥

rūpa yaiche dui nāṭaka kairāche ārambhe  
Rūpa  as  two  dramas  has composed  at the outset  
śunite ānanda bāḍe yāra mukha-bandhe  
to hear  joy  increases  whose  in the prologue

(108) Take the two dramas Rūpa has composed: merely hearing their prologues makes one’s joy grow.”

ভগবান্-আচার্য্য কহে, — শুন একবার ৷  
তুমি শুনিলে ভাল-মন্দ জানিবে বিচার ॥ ১০৯ ॥

bhagavān-ācārya kahe, — śuna eka-bāra  
Bhagavān Ācārya  says  —  hear  once  
tumi śunile bhāla-manda jānibe vicāra  
you  having heard  good or bad  will know  the judgment

(109) Bhagavān Ācārya said, “Just hear it once. When you have heard it, you will be able to judge whether it is good or bad.”

দুই-তিন-দিন আচার্য্য আগ্রহ করিল ৷  
তাঁর আগ্রহে স্বরূপের শুনিতে ইচ্ছা হৈল ॥ ১১০ ॥

dui-tina-dina ācārya āgraha karila  
two or three days  the Ācārya  insistence  made  
tām̐ra āgrahe svarūpera śunite icchā haila  
by his  insistence  in Svarūpa  to hear  the desire  arose

(110) For two or three days the Ācārya pressed him, and at his insistence Svarūpa became willing to hear it.

সবা লঞা স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি শুনিতে বসিলা ৷  
তবে সেই কবি নান্দী-শ্লোক পড়িলা ॥ ১১১ ॥

sabā lañā svarūpa-gosāñi śunite vasilā  
all  taking  Svarūpa Gosāñi  to hear  sat down  
tabe sei kavi nāndī-śloka paḍilā  
then  that  poet  the nāndī verse  recited

(111) Svarūpa Gosāñi sat down with everyone to listen, and the poet recited the *nāndī* verse, the opening benediction.

বিকচকমলনেত্রে শ্রীজগন্নাথসংজ্ঞে  
কনকরুচিরিহাত্মন্যাত্মতাং যঃ প্রপন্নঃ ৷  
প্রকৃতিজড়মশেষং চেতয়ন্নাবিরাসীৎ  
স দিশতু তব ভব্যং কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যদেবঃ ॥ ১১২ ॥

vikaca-kamala-netre śrī-jagannātha-saṁjñe  
with eyes like blossoming lotuses  named Śrī Jagannātha  
kanaka-rucir ihātmany ātmatāṁ yaḥ prapannaḥ  
golden-hued  here  in the self  selfhood  who  has attained  
prakṛti-jaḍam aśeṣaṁ cetayann āvirāsīt  
inert matter  all  enlivening  appeared  
sa diśatu tava bhavyaṁ kṛṣṇa-caitanya-devaḥ  
He  may grant  your  welfare  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya-deva

(112) “He of golden lustre has here found His self in the One named Śrī Jagannātha, whose eyes are like blossoming lotuses, and has appeared, bringing consciousness to the whole of inert nature. May that Kṛṣṇa Caitanya-deva grant you good fortune.”[^31]

শ্লোক শুনি’ সর্ব্বলোক তাহারে বাখানে ৷  
স্বরূপ কহে, — এই শ্লোক করহ ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ১১৩ ॥

śloka śuni’ sarva-loka tāhāre vākhāne  
the verse  hearing  all the people  him  praised  
svarūpa kahe, — ei śloka karaha vyākhyāne  
Svarūpa  says  —  this  verse  do  explain

(113) Hearing the verse, everyone praised the poet, but Svarūpa said, “Explain this verse.”

কবি কহে, — জগন্নাথ — সুন্দর-শরীর ৷  
চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি — শরীরী মহাধীর ॥ ১১৪ ॥

kavi kahe, — jagannātha — sundara-śarīra  
the poet  says  —  Jagannātha  —  the beautiful body  
caitanya-gosāñi — śarīrī mahā-dhīra  
Caitanya Gosāñi  —  the embodied one  most steady

(114) The poet said, “Jagannātha is the beautiful body, and Caitanya Gosāñi, perfectly steady (*mahā-dhīra*), is the one embodied in it (*śarīrī*).[^32]

সহজে জড়জগতের চেতন করাইতে ৷  
নীলাচলে মহাপ্রভু হৈলা আবির্ভূতে ॥ ১১৫ ॥

sahaje jaḍa-jagatera cetana karāite  
naturally  the inert world  conscious  to make  
nīlācale mahāprabhu hailā āvirbhūte  
in Nīlācala  Mahāprabhu  became  manifest

(115) To bestow consciousness on the inert world, as is His nature, Mahāprabhu has appeared in Nīlācala.”

শুনিয়া সবার হৈল আনন্দিত মন ৷  
দুঃখ পাঞা স্বরূপ কহে সক্রোধ বচন ॥ ১১৬ ॥

śuniyā sabāra haila ānandita mana  
hearing  of all  became  joyful  the mind  
duḥkha pāñā svarūpa kahe sakrodha vacana  
pain  feeling  Svarūpa  speaks  angry  words

(116) Hearing this, everyone was delighted, but Svarūpa, deeply pained, spoke angry words:[^33]

আরে মূর্খ, আপনার কৈলি সর্ব্বনাশ !  
দুই ত’ ঈশ্বরে তোর নাহিক বিশ্বাস ! ॥ ১১৭ ॥

āre mūrkha, āpanāra kaili sarva-nāśa!  
O  fool  your own  you have made  utter ruin  
dui ta’ īśvare tora nāhika viśvāsa!  
in the two  indeed  Lords  your  there is no  faith

(117) “O fool, you have brought about your own utter ruin! You have no faith in either of the two Lords![^34]

পূর্ণানন্দ-চিৎস্বরূপ জগন্নাথ-রায় ৷  
তাঁরে কৈলি জড়-নশ্বর-প্রাকৃত-কায়! ॥ ১১৮ ॥

pūrṇānanda-citsvarūpa jagannātha-rāya  
full of bliss  a form of pure spirit  Lord Jagannātha  
tām̐re kaili jaḍa-naśvara-prākṛta-kāya!  
Him  you have made  an inert, perishable, material  body

(118) Lord Jagannātha is full of bliss, a form of pure spirit, yet you have made Him out to be an inert, perishable, material body![^35]

পূর্ণ-ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্য চৈতন্য — স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তাঁরে কৈলি ক্ষুদ্র-জীব স্ফুলিঙ্গ-সমান ! ॥ ১১৯ ॥

pūrṇa-ṣaḍ-aiśvarya caitanya — svayaṁ bhagavān  
full of the six opulences  Caitanya  —  Svayam Bhagavān  
tām̐re kaili kṣudra-jīva sphuliṅga-samāna!  
Him  you have made  a tiny jīva  a spark  like

(119) Caitanya, full in all six opulences, is Svayam Bhagavān, the Supreme Lord Himself, yet you have made Him out to be a tiny jīva, like a spark![^36]

দুই-ঠাঞি অপরাধে পাইবি দুর্গতি !  
অতত্ত্বজ্ঞ ‘তত্ত্ব’ বর্ণে, তার এই গতি ! ॥ ১২০ ॥

dui-ṭhāñi aparādhe pāibi durgati!  
in two places  by the offence  you will get  a dire fate  
atattva-jña ‘tattva’ varṇe, tāra ei gati!  
one ignorant of truth  ‘truth’  describes  his  this  fate

(120) For this offence against both you will meet a dire fate! When one ignorant of the truth describes the truth, this is the end he comes to!

আর এক কৈরাছ পরম ‘প্রমাদ’ !  
দেহ-দেহি-ভেদ ঈশ্বরে কৈলে ‘অপরাধ’ ! ॥ ১২১ ॥

āra eka kairācha parama ‘pramāda’!  
and  one  you have made  great  ‘blunder’  
deha-dehi-bheda īśvare kaile ‘aparādha’!  
the difference of body and embodied  in the Lord  you made  ‘an offence’

(121) You have made yet another great blunder (*pramāda*): by distinguishing between body and embodied in the Lord, you have committed an offence!

ঈশ্বরের নাহি কভু দেহ-দেহি-ভেদ ৷  
স্বরূপ, দেহ, — চিদানন্দ, নাহিক বিভেদ ॥ ১২২ ॥

īśvarera nāhi kabhu deha-dehi-bheda  
of the Lord  there is not  ever  body–embodied  difference  
svarūpa, deha, — cid-ānanda, nāhika vibheda  
His own form  body  —  spirit and bliss  there is no  difference

(122) In the Lord there is never any difference between body and embodied. His own form (*svarūpa*) and His body are both spirit and bliss; there is no difference between them.

দেহ-দেহি-বিভাগোঽযং নেশ্বরে বিদ্যতে ক্বচিৎ ॥ ১২৩ ॥

deha-dehi-vibhāgo ’yaṁ  
of body and embodied  division  this  
neśvare vidyate kvacit  
not in the Lord  exists  ever

(123) “This division of body and embodied never exists in the Lord.” (Kūrma Purāṇa)[^37]

নাতঃ পরং পরম যদ্ভবতঃ স্বরূপ-  
মানন্দমাত্রমবিকল্পমবিদ্ধবর্চ্চঃ ৷  
পশ্যামি বিশ্বসৃজমেকমবিশ্বমাত্মন্  
ভূতেন্দ্রিয়াত্মকমদস্ত উপাশ্রিতোঽস্মি ॥ ১২৪ ॥

nātaḥ paraṁ parama yad bhavataḥ svarūpam  
not  than this  higher  O Supreme  which  Your  form  
ānanda-mātram avikalpam aviddha-varcaḥ  
pure bliss  free of difference  of unobscured radiance  
paśyāmi viśva-sṛjam ekam aviśvam ātman  
I see  of the universe  the creator  one  distinct from the universe  O Self  
bhūtendriyātmakam adas ta upāśrito ’smi  
the source of the elements and senses  that  Your  sheltered  I am

(124) “O Supreme Lord, I see nothing higher than this form of Yours, which is pure bliss, free of all difference, its radiance unobscured. You are the one creator of the universe, yet You are distinct from it, O Self, and You are the source of the elements and the senses. I have taken shelter of that form of Yours.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.9.3)[^38]

তদ্বা ইদং ভুবনমঙ্গল মঙ্গলায়  
ধ্যানে স্ম নো দর্শিতং ত উপাসকানাম্ ৷  
তস্মৈ নমো ভগবতেঽনুবিধেম তুভ্যং  
যোঽনাদৃতো নরকভাগ্ভিরসৎপ্রসঙ্গৈঃ ॥ ১২৫ ॥

tad vā idaṁ bhuvana-maṅgala maṅgalāya  
that  indeed  this (form)  O good of the worlds  for the good  
dhyāne sma no darśitaṁ ta upāsakānām  
in meditation  indeed  to us  shown  by You  to the worshippers  
tasmai namo bhagavate ’nuvidhema tubhyaṁ  
to Him  obeisance  to Bhagavān  we offer  to You  
yo ’nādṛto naraka-bhāgbhir asat-prasaṅgaiḥ  
who  is not honoured  by those bound for hell  attached to false arguments

(125) “O good of all the worlds, for our good You have shown this very form to us, Your worshippers, in meditation. We bow down to You, Bhagavān, whom those bound for hell, attached to false arguments, do not honour.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.9.4)[^39]

কাঁহা ‘পূর্ণানন্দৈশ্বর্য্য’ কৃষ্ণ ‘মহেশ্বর’ !  
কাঁহা ‘ক্ষুদ্র’ জীব ‘দুঃখী’, ‘মায়ার কিঙ্কর’ ! ॥ ১২৬ ॥

kām̐hā ‘pūrṇānandaiśvarya’ kṛṣṇa ‘maheśvara’!  
where  ‘full of bliss and opulence’  Kṛṣṇa  ‘the Great Lord’  
kām̐hā ‘kṣudra’ jīva ‘duḥkhī’, ‘māyāra kiṅkara’!  
where  ‘tiny’  jīva  ‘miserable’  ‘servant of māyā’

(126) Where is Kṛṣṇa, full of bliss and opulence, the Great Lord (*Maheśvara*)? And where is the tiny, miserable jīva, a servant of māyā?

হ্লাদিন্যা সম্বিদাশ্লিষ্টঃ সচ্চিদানন্দ ঈশ্বরঃ ৷  
স্বাবিদ্যা সংবৃতো জীবঃ সংক্লেশনিকরাকরঃ ॥ ১২৭ ॥

hlādinyā samvidāśliṣṭaḥ  
by hlādinī  embraced by saṁvit  
sac-cid-ānanda īśvaraḥ  
eternal being, consciousness and bliss  the Lord  
svāvidyā saṁvṛto jīvaḥ  
by its own ignorance  covered  the jīva  
saṁkleśa-nikarākaraḥ  
a mine of a multitude of miseries

(127) “The Lord, embraced by the hlādinī and saṁvit potencies, is eternal being, consciousness and bliss; the jīva, covered by its own ignorance, is a mine of countless miseries.” (words of Viṣṇusvāmī quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī in his commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.5–6)[^40]

শুনি’ সভাসদের হৈল মহাচমৎকার ৷  
সত্য কহে গোসাঞি, দুঁহারে কৈরাছে তিরস্কার ॥ ১২৮ ॥

śuni’ sabhāsadera haila mahācamatkāra  
hearing  of the assembly  there was  great astonishment  
satya kahe gosāñi, dum̐hāre kairāche tiraskāra  
truly  speaks  Gosāñi  both  he has insulted

(128) Hearing this, the whole assembly was struck with wonder: “The Gosāñi speaks the truth! The poet has insulted both.”

শুনিয়া কবির হৈল লজ্জা, ভয়, বিস্ময় ৷  
হংস-মধ্যে বক যেন কিছু নাহি কয় ॥ ১২৯ ॥

śuniyā kavira haila lajjā, bhaya, vismaya  
hearing  of the poet  arose  shame  fear  amazement  
haṁsa-madhye baka yena kichu nāhi kaya  
swans  among  a heron  like  anything  not  says

(129) The poet was filled with shame, fear and amazement; like a heron among swans, he could say nothing.

তার দুঃখ দেখি’ স্বরূপ পরম-সদয় ৷  
উপদেশ কৈলা তারে যৈছে ‘হিত’ হয় ॥ ১৩০ ॥

tāra duḥkha dekhi’ svarūpa parama-sadaya  
his  distress  seeing  Svarūpa  most merciful  
upadeśa kailā tāre yaiche ‘hita’ haya  
instruction  gave  to him  so that  ‘good’  may come

(130) Seeing his distress, the most merciful Svarūpa instructed him in a way that would bring him true good:

যাহ’, ভাগবত পড় বৈষ্ণবের স্থানে ৷  
একান্ত আশ্রয় কর চৈতন্য-চরণে ॥ ১৩১ ॥

yāha’, bhāgavata paḍa vaiṣṇavera sthāne  
go  the Bhāgavata  study  from a Vaiṣṇava  
ekānta āśraya kara caitanya-caraṇe  
exclusive  shelter  take  at Caitanya’s feet

(131) “Go and study the *Bhāgavata* under a Vaiṣṇava, and take exclusive shelter at the feet of Caitanya.[^41]

চৈতন্যের ভক্তগণের নিত্য কর ‘সঙ্গ’ ৷  
তবে ত’ জানিবা সিদ্ধান্তসমুদ্র-তরঙ্গ ॥ ১৩২ ॥

caitanyera bhakta-gaṇera nitya kara ‘saṅga’  
of Caitanya’s  devotees  always  keep  ‘the company’  
tabe ta’ jānibā siddhānta-samudra-taraṅga  
then  indeed  you will know  of the ocean of siddhānta  the waves

(132) Always keep the company of Caitanya’s devotees; then you will come to know the waves of the ocean of *siddhānta*.

তবে পাণ্ডিত্য তোমার হইবে সফল ৷  
কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ-লীলা বর্ণিবা নির্ম্মল ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

tabe pāṇḍitya tomāra haibe saphala  
then  learning  your  will become  fruitful  
kṛṣṇera svarūpa-līlā varṇibā nirmala  
Kṛṣṇa’s  true nature and pastimes  you will describe  purely

(133) Then your learning will bear fruit, and you will describe Kṛṣṇa’s true nature and pastimes purely, without fault.

এই শ্লোক কৈরাছ পাঞা সন্তোষ ৷  
তোমার হৃদয়ের অর্থে দুঁহায় লাগে ‘দোষ’ ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

ei śloka kairācha pāñā santoṣa  
this  verse  you have composed  having felt  satisfaction  
tomāra hṛdayera arthe dum̐hāya lāge ‘doṣa’  
your  of the heart  by the meaning  to both  attaches  ‘fault’

(134) You composed this verse with great satisfaction, but the meaning you had in mind lays fault on both Lords.

তুমি যৈছে-তৈছে কহ, না জানিয়া রীতি ৷  
সরস্বতী সেই শব্দে কৈরাছে স্তুতি ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

tumi yaiche-taiche kaha, nā jāniyā rīti  
you  any which way  speak  not  knowing  the proper way  
sarasvatī sei śabde kairāche stuti  
Sarasvatī  with those  words  has made  praise

(135) You spoke at random, not knowing the proper way; yet with those very words Sarasvatī has offered praise.[^42]

যৈছে ইন্দ্র, দৈত্যাদি করে কৃষ্ণের ভর্ৎসন ৷  
সেইশব্দে সরস্বতী করেন স্তবন ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

yaiche indra, daityādi kare kṛṣṇera bhartsana  
as  Indra  the demons and others  do  of Kṛṣṇa  rebuke  
sei-śabde sarasvatī karena stavana  
with those words  Sarasvatī  does  praise

(136) When Indra, the demons and others rebuke Kṛṣṇa, Sarasvatī offers praise with those very words.

বাচালং বালিশং স্তব্ধমজ্ঞং পণ্ডিতমানিনম্ ৷  
কৃষ্ণং মর্ত্ত্যমুপাশ্রিত্য গোপা মে চক্রুরপ্রিয়ম্ ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

vācālaṁ  bāliśaṁ  stabdham  
talkative  childish  stubborn  
ajñaṁ paṇḍita-māninam  
ignorant  thinking Himself learned  
kṛṣṇaṁ martyam upāśritya  
Kṛṣṇa  a mortal  having taken shelter of  
gopā  me  cakrur  apriyam  
the cowherds  to me  have done  an offence

(137) “Taking shelter of Kṛṣṇa — a talkative, childish, stubborn, ignorant mortal who thinks Himself learned — these cowherds have offended me.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.25.5)[^43]

ঐশ্বর্য্য মদে মত্ত ইন্দ্র, — যেন মাতোয়াল ৷  
বুদ্ধিনাশ হৈল, কেবল নাহিক সাম্ভাল ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

aiśvarya made matta indra, — yena mātoyāla  
by the intoxication of opulence  maddened  Indra  —  like  a drunkard  
buddhi-nāśa haila, kevala nāhika sāmbhāla  
loss of intelligence  occurred  at all  there is no  self-control

(138) Indra, maddened by the intoxication of his opulence, was like a drunkard: his intelligence was lost, and he had no self-control at all.

ইন্দ্র বলে, — মুঞি কৃষ্ণের কৈরাছি নিন্দন ৷  
তারই মুখে সরস্বতী করেন স্তবন ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

indra bale, — muñi kṛṣṇera kairāchi nindana  
Indra  says  —  I  of Kṛṣṇa  have made  reviling  
tārai mukhe sarasvatī karena stavana  
from his very  mouth  Sarasvatī  does  praise

(139) Indra says, “I have reviled Kṛṣṇa,” yet from his very mouth Sarasvatī offers praise.

‘বাচাল’ কহিয়ে — ‘বেদপ্রবর্ত্তক’ ধন্য ৷  
‘বালিশ’ — তথাপি ‘শিশু-প্রায়’ গর্ব্বশূন্য ॥ ১৪০ ॥

‘vācāla’ kahiye — ‘veda-pravartaka’ dhanya  
‘talkative’  we call  —  ‘the promulgator of the Vedas’  glorious  
‘bāliśa’ — tathāpi ‘śiśu-prāya’ garva-śūnya  
‘childish’  —  yet  ‘like a child’  free of pride

(140) ‘*Vācāla*’ (talkative) means the glorious One who set the Vedas in motion; and ‘*bāliśa*’ (childish) means that, great as He is, He is like a child, free of pride.

বন্দ্যাভাবে ‘অনম্র’ — ‘স্তব্ধ’-শব্দে কয় ৷  
যাহা হৈতে অন্য ‘বিজ্ঞ’ নাহি — সে ‘অজ্ঞ’ হয় ॥ ১৪১ ॥

vandyābhāve ‘anamra’ — ‘stabdha’-śabde kaya  
for want of one to honour  ‘not bowing’  —  by the word ‘stabdha’  is said  
yāhā haite anya ‘vijña’ nāhi — se ‘ajña’ haya  
than whom  another  ‘knower’  there is not  —  He  ‘ajña’  is

(141) Because there is no one for Him to honour, He bows to none: this is what the word ‘*stabdha*’ (stubborn) says. And He beyond whom there is no other knower is ‘*ajña*’ (ignorant) — there is nothing for Him to learn.

পণ্ডিতের মান্য-পাত্র — হয় ‘পণ্ডিতমানী’ ৷  
তথাপি ভক্তবাৎসল্যে ‘মনুষ্য’-অভিমানী ॥ ১৪২ ॥

paṇḍitera mānya-pātra — haya ‘paṇḍita-mānī’  
of the learned  the object of honour  —  is  ‘paṇḍita-mānī’  
tathāpi bhakta-vātsalye ‘manuṣya’-abhimānī  
still  out of affection for devotees  ‘human’  considers Himself

(142) The one honoured by the learned is ‘*paṇḍita-mānī*’; yet out of affection for His devotees He regards Himself as a human being, a ‘mortal’.

জরাসন্ধ কহে, — কৃষ্ণ — ‘পুরুষ-অধম’ ৷  
তোর সঙ্গে না যুঝিমু, ‘যাহি বন্ধুহন্’ ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

jarāsandha kahe, — kṛṣṇa — ‘puruṣa-adhama’  
Jarāsandha  says  —  Kṛṣṇa  —  ‘the lowest of men’  
tora saṅge nā yujhimu, ‘yāhi bandhu-han’  
with You  not  I shall fight  ‘go away, slayer of kinsmen’

(143) Jarāsandha said, “Kṛṣṇa is the lowest of men! I will not fight with You — go away, slayer of Your kinsmen!”

যাহা হৈতে অন্য পুরুষসকল — ‘অধম’ ৷  
সেই হয় ‘পুরুষাধম’ — সরস্বতীর মন ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

yāhā haite anya puruṣa-sakala — ‘adhama’  
than whom  other  persons all  —  ‘lower’  
sei haya ‘puruṣādhama’ — sarasvatīra mana  
He  is  ‘puruṣādhama’  —  Sarasvatī’s  meaning

(144) He in comparison with whom all other persons are lower is ‘*puruṣādhama*’ — that is Sarasvatī’s meaning.

‘বান্ধে সবারে’ — তাতে অবিদ্যা ‘বন্ধু’ হয় ৷  
‘অবিদ্যা-নাশক’ — ‘বন্ধুহন্’-শব্দে কয় ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

‘bāndhe sabāre’ — tāte avidyā ‘bandhu’ haya  
‘binds  all’  —  therefore  ignorance  ‘bandhu’  is  
‘avidyā-nāśaka’ — ‘bandhu-han’-śabde kaya  
‘destroyer of ignorance’  —  by the word ‘bandhu-han’  is said

(145) Because it binds everyone, ignorance (*avidyā*) is the ‘*bandhu*’; so the word ‘*bandhu-han*’ means the destroyer of ignorance.

এইমত শিশুপাল করিল নিন্দন ৷  
সেইবাক্যে সরস্বতী করেন স্তবন ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

ei-mata śiśupāla karila nindana  
in this way  Śiśupāla  did  reviling  
sei-vākye sarasvatī karena stavana  
with those words  Sarasvatī  does  praise

(146) In the same way Śiśupāla reviled Kṛṣṇa, and with those very words Sarasvatī offered praise.

তৈছে এই শ্লোকে তোমার অর্থে ‘নিন্দা’ আইসে ৷  
সরস্বতীর অর্থ শুন, যাতে ‘স্তুতি’ ভাসে ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

taiche ei śloke tomāra arthe ‘nindā’ āise  
so  in this  verse  by your  meaning  ‘blame’  comes  
sarasvatīra  artha  śuna,  yāte  ‘stuti’  bhāse  
Sarasvatī’s  meaning  hear  in which  ‘praise’  shines

(147) So too with this verse: in your meaning it speaks blame. Now hear Sarasvatī’s meaning, in which praise shines forth.

জগন্নাথ হয় কৃষ্ণের ‘আত্মস্বরূপ’ ৷  
কিন্তু ইঁহা দারুব্রহ্ম — ‘স্থাবর-স্বরূপ’ ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

jagannātha haya kṛṣṇera ‘ātma-svarūpa’  
Jagannātha  is  Kṛṣṇa’s  ‘very self’  
kintu im̐hā dāru-brahma — ‘sthāvara-svarūpa’  
but  here  the Dāru-brahma  —  ‘an immobile form’

(148) Jagannātha is Kṛṣṇa’s very self (*ātma-svarūpa*), but here He is the Dāru-brahma, in an immobile form.[^44]

তাঁহা-সহ আত্মতা একরূপ হঞা ৷  
কৃষ্ণ একতত্ত্বরূপ — দুইরূপ হঞা ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

tām̐hā-saha  ātmatā  eka-rūpa  hañā  
with Him  selfhood  one form  being  
kṛṣṇa eka-tattva-rūpa — dui-rūpa hañā  
Kṛṣṇa  the one truth  —  two forms  having become

(149) Being one in selfhood with Him, Kṛṣṇa, the one truth, has appeared in two forms.[^45]

সংসারতারণ-হেতু যেই ইচ্ছা-শক্তি ৷  
তাহার মিলন করি’ একতা ঐছে প্রাপ্তি ॥ ১৫০ ॥

saṁsāra-tāraṇa-hetu yei icchā-śakti  
for deliverance from saṁsāra  that  will-potency  
tāhāra milana kari’ ekatā aiche prāpti  
of that  union  making  oneness  thus  is attained

(150) Uniting in that will (*icchā-śakti*) whose aim is to deliver souls from *saṁsāra*, They attain such oneness.

সকল সংসারী-লোকের করিতে উদ্ধার ৷  
গৌর-জঙ্গম-রূপে কৈলা অবতার ॥ ১৫১ ॥

sakala saṁsārī-lokera karite uddhāra  
all  worldly  people  to deliver  
gaura-jaṅgama-rūpe kailā avatāra  
of Gaura  in the moving  form  made  descent

(151) To deliver all the people of the world, He descended in the moving form of Gaura.

জগন্নাথের দর্শনে খণ্ডায় সংসার ৷  
সব-দেশের সব-লোক নারে আসিবার ॥ ১৫২ ॥

jagannāthera darśane khaṇḍāya saṁsāra  
by the sight of Jagannātha  is cut  saṁsāra  
saba-deśera saba-loka nāre āsibāra  
of all  lands  all  people  cannot  come

(152) By seeing Jagannātha one is freed from *saṁsāra*, but not all the people of every land can come to Him.

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যপ্রভু দেশে দেশে যাঞা ৷  
সব-লোকে নিস্তারিলা জঙ্গম-ব্রহ্ম হঞা ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-prabhu deśe deśe yāñā  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu  land to land  going  
saba-loke nistārilā jaṅgama-brahma hañā  
all people  delivered  the moving Brahman  becoming

(153) So Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu, going from land to land as the moving Brahman (*jaṅgama-brahma*), delivered everyone.

সরস্বতীর অর্থ এই কহিলুঁ বিবরণ ৷  
এহো ভাগ্য তোমার — ঐছে করিলা বর্ণন ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

sarasvatīra artha ei kahilum̐ vivaraṇa  
Sarasvatī’s  meaning  this  I have told  the explanation  
eho bhāgya tomāra — aiche karilā varṇana  
this too  fortune  yours  —  thus  you made  the description

(154) This is the explanation I have given of Sarasvatī’s meaning. It is your good fortune, too, that you described Them in this way.

কৃষ্ণে গালি দিতে করে নাম উচ্চারণ ৷  
সেই নাম হয় তার ‘মুক্তি’র কারণ ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

kṛṣṇe gāli dite kare nāma uccāraṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  to abuse  makes  the name  utterance  
sei nāma haya tāra ‘mukti’ra kāraṇa  
that  name  becomes  his  of ‘liberation’  the cause

(155) One may utter Kṛṣṇa’s name only to abuse Him, yet that very name becomes the cause of his liberation.”[^46]

তবে সেই কবি সবার চরণে পড়িয়া ৷  
সবা শরণ লৈল দন্তে তৃণ লঞা ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

tabe sei kavi sabāra caraṇe paḍiyā  
then  that  poet  at the feet of all  falling  
sabā śaraṇa laila dante tṛṇa lañā  
of all  shelter  took  in the teeth  straw  holding

(156) Then the poet fell at everyone’s feet and, holding straw between his teeth, took shelter of them all.

তবে সব ভক্ত তারে অঙ্গীকার কৈলা ৷  
তার গুণ কহি’ মহাপ্রভুরে মিলাইলা ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

tabe saba bhakta tāre aṅgīkāra kailā  
then  all  devotees  him  accepted  
tāra guṇa kahi’ mahāprabhure milāilā  
his  qualities  telling  with Mahāprabhu  introduced

(157) All the devotees then accepted him and, speaking of his good qualities, introduced him to Mahāprabhu.[^47]

সেই কবি সর্ব্ব ত্যজি’ রহিলা নীলাচলে ৷  
গৌরভক্তগণের কৃপা কে কহিতে পারে ? ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

sei kavi sarva tyaji’ rahilā nīlācale  
that  poet  everything  giving up  stayed  in Nīlācala  
gaura-bhakta-gaṇera kṛpā ke kahite pāre?  
of Gaura’s devotees  the mercy  who  to describe  can

(158) The poet gave up everything and remained in Nīlācala. Who can describe the mercy of Gaura’s devotees?[^48]

এই ত’ কহিলুঁ প্রদ্যুম্নমিশ্র-বিবরণ ৷  
প্রভুর আজ্ঞায় কৈল কৃষ্ণকথার শ্রবণ ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

ei ta’ kahilum̐ pradyumna-miśra-vivaraṇa  
thus  indeed  I have told  of Pradyumna Miśra  the account  
prabhura ājñāya kaila kṛṣṇa-kathāra śravaṇa  
by the Lord’s order  he did  of Kṛṣṇa-kathā  the hearing

(159) Thus I have told the account of Pradyumna Miśra, who by the Lord’s order heard talks about Kṛṣṇa.

তার মধ্যে কহিলুঁ রামানন্দের মহিমা ৷  
আপনে শ্রীমুখে প্রভু বর্ণে যাঁর সীমা ॥ ১৬০ ॥

tāra madhye kahilum̐ rāmānandera mahimā  
within that  I have told  Rāmānanda’s  glory  
āpane śrī-mukhe prabhu varṇe yām̐ra sīmā  
Himself  with His holy mouth  the Lord  describes  whose  limit

(160) Within it I have spoken of the glory of Rāmānanda, the full extent of which the Lord Himself described with His own holy lips.

প্রস্তাবে কহিলুঁ কবির নাটক-বিবরণ ৷  
অজ্ঞ হঞা শ্রদ্ধায় পাইল প্রভুর চরণ ॥ ১৬১ ॥

prastāve kahilum̐ kavira nāṭaka-vivaraṇa  
in passing  I have told  the poet’s  drama  account  
ajña hañā śraddhāya pāila prabhura caraṇa  
ignorant  being  by faith  obtained  the Lord’s  feet

(161) In passing I have also related the story of the poet’s drama: ignorant though he was, by faith he attained the Lord’s feet.[^49]

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-লীলা — অমৃতের সার ৷  
একলীলা-প্রবাহে বহে শত-শত ধার ॥ ১৬২ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-līlā — amṛtera sāra  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s pastimes  —  of nectar  the essence  
ekalīlā-pravāhe vahe śata-śata dhāra  
of a single pastime  in the stream  flow  hundreds upon hundreds  of currents

(162) The pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya are the essence of nectar: from the stream of a single pastime flow hundreds upon hundreds of currents.

শ্রদ্ধা করি’ এই লীলা যেই পড়ে, শুনে ৷  
গৌরলীলা, ভক্তি, ভক্ত, রস-তত্ত্ব জানে ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

śraddhā kari’ ei līlā yei paḍe, śune  
faith  having  this  pastime  whoever  reads  hears  
gaura-līlā, bhakti, bhakta, rasa-tattva jāne  
Gaura’s pastimes  bhakti  the devotees  the truth of rasa  knows

(163) Whoever reads or hears this pastime with faith comes to know the pastimes of Gaura, bhakti, the devotees and the truth of rasa.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(164) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: *vaiguṇya* — the ripening of the fruits of karma; *paiśunya* — malice. Edition heading: for the jīva bereft of consciousness, stricken with the disease of worldly existence and fallen into the ocean of saṁsāra, the only good lies in taking shelter of Caitanya’s feet.

[^2]: Edition heading: the Lord pretends not to know and sends Miśra, born in a family of brāhmaṇas, to Rāmānanda as an eager disciple — to Rāmānanda, who was not a brāhmaṇa by birth, yet is the guru of all four varṇas and āśramas.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: the dharma of varṇa and āśrama; if it does not awaken attraction (*rati*) to the narrations about the Lord, it is wholly fruitless labour.

[^4]: The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* calls them young *devadāsīs* (now called *māhārīs*), and the *Anubhāṣya* (on 25) “two devadāsīs” — maidservants of the Deity who dance before Śrī Jagannātha.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: his own drama — the *Jagannātha-vallabha*, which Rāmānanda Rāya wrote in Sanskrit.

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the two girls were to play the principal gopīs in the drama, so he regarded them as the ones to be served and took the mood of a maidservant following them; Rāmānanda knew himself to be a maidservant of Śrīmatī Rādhārāṇī.

[^7]: Edition heading: to stage his drama *Jagannātha-vallabha* before Śrī Jagannātha, Rāya teaches acting in the Jagannātha-vallabha garden.

[^8]: Edition heading: after feeding the two devadāsīs, Rāya sends them home in secret — for the good of ignorant critics who judge only by what the senses show.

[^9]: Edition heading: Bhagavān, the ideal teacher of humility, humbly extols His devotee’s love for Kṛṣṇa as greater than His own.

[^10]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads “তাহাদের সব সেবা”, “every service for them” (*Anubhāṣya*: all kinds of service; cf. 39).

[^11]: *Anubhāṣya*: the thirty-three kinds of bhāva needed to enact the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa.

[^12]: The second line is the same as Madhya 13.67; translation as there.

[^13]: *Anubhāṣya*: this is said of one who, with faith and a transcendental heart, hears and describes Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes: the three guṇas cannot overcome him. Let no one think, like the prākṛta-sahajiyās, that without knowledge of sambandha, giving up sādhana-bhakti and taking Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes as a model of worldly enjoyment, one’s lust will vanish by merely hearing; by the word “faith” (*viśvāsa*) Mahāprabhu rejects that view (cf. Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.30).

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: *pratilabhya* — “having attained”: devotion comes first, and lust is driven out; *anuśṛṇuyāt* — he hears constantly from the lips of the guru, free from worldly obstacles; *varṇayet* — he sings of it step by step, in the way of Śrī Rūpa’s followers; *dhīraḥ* — one who has conquered the six urges.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: one who hears and chants Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes and, following the mood of Śrī Rūpa, serves Kṛṣṇa in the mind at every moment, absorbed in pure rāga, gains a matchless fruit that no worldly language can describe.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: he is an eternally perfect associate; or else his body is almost perfect — though seen by people’s eyes, it is the abode of feelings devoted to the service of Kṛṣṇa, until by Kṛṣṇa’s will he attains *vastu-siddhi*.

[^17]: Edition heading: Miśra asks him to speak again of Kṛṣṇa — of sambandha, abhidheya and prayojana — as Rāya once spoke to the Lord (Madhya 8).

[^18]: Edition heading: it is forbidden to regard as a mortal the guru who sings of Kṛṣṇa.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: worldly people see in Rāmānanda a householder and a *viṣayī*, absorbed in worldly objects, but his mind of pure goodness always worships a single object — the pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa; he is thus a “kṛṣṇa-viṣayī”, able to turn even sannyāsīs to Kṛṣṇa.

[^20]: Edition heading: worldly varṇāśrama, learning and the like are no sign of one able to speak of true dharma. *Anubhāṣya*: the worldly eye knows Rāmānanda Rāya as a śūdra, the lowest of the varṇas, and as a householder, the lowest of the āśramas; through him the Lord instructed Pradyumna Miśra, a brāhmaṇa by birth, and He Himself, having accepted sannyāsa, accepted the dharma Rāmānanda preached. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: whoever, knowing the difference between mundane and transcendental truth, has learned transcendental devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the teacher of all souls, whatever his birth and āśrama.

[^21]: Edition heading: the Lord Himself in the dress of a sannyāsī and Miśra, a brāhmaṇa by birth, both become disciples eager to hear and choose as their guru Śrī Rāya — dressed as a householder, born outside the brāhmaṇa families, a singer of Kṛṣṇa — and so teach the world.

[^22]: Edition heading: to Ṭhākura Haridāsa, born among the yavanas, the Lord gives the rank of guru of the world and ācārya of the holy name; Śrī Sanātana, though he had lived among mlecchas, is the ācārya of the conclusions of devotion to Kṛṣṇa, that is, of knowledge of *sambandha*.

[^23]: Edition heading: Śrī Rūpa is the ācārya of the rasa of loving devotion of Vraja, that is, of *abhidheya*.

[^24]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads “লঞা আইলা শুনাইতে”, without “প্রভুকে” (“to the Lord”). Edition heading: the story of a worldly poet from East Bengal in the guise of a brāhmaṇa.

[^25]: Edition heading (95–96): the rule — Svarūpa Dāmodara examines everything first; only a work that has passed his test receives the Lord’s grace.

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: *rasābhāsa* is what seems to be rasa but is incomplete in its marks; Śrī Viśvanātha Cakravartī says that it arises when two rasas hostile to each other are joined; *siddhānta-virodha* is contradiction of the conclusions of the path of bhakti. Edition heading: the one cause of the magnanimous Lord’s anger is contradiction of the word of bhakti-siddhānta.

[^27]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads “এক কবি”, “a poet” (the text Vṛndāvana Candra dāsa followed has “এক বিপ্র”, “a brāhmaṇa”). Edition heading: Bhagavān Ācārya’s request to Svarūpa; praise of the worldly poet’s work.

[^28]: Edition heading: Svarūpa, best of the knowers of Kṛṣṇa-tattva, who knows the heart, rebukes Bhagavān Ācārya.

[^29]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *yadvā tadvā kavi* — just any poet, that is, those who compose verse without properly knowing either rasa-tattva or the Vaiṣṇava *siddhānta*.

[^30]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *grāmya-kavi* — poets who write verse about worldly men and women; *vidagdha ātmīya-vākya* — the work of one close at heart, skilled in the knowledge of tattva and belonging to the community of pure devotees. Edition heading: a mundane poet, who does not seek Kṛṣṇa’s happiness, is an outsider (*bahiraṅga*); a transcendental poet, devoted to Kṛṣṇa’s happiness, is one’s own (*antaraṅga*).

[^31]: Edition heading: the *nāndī* verse, composed by the Bengali brāhmaṇa. The words *ātmani ātmatāṁ prapannaḥ* bear both the poet’s sense (114) and Sarasvatī’s (148–149). *Anubhāṣya*: in the poet’s sense, Gaura entered Jagannātha’s body as its owner, and the “inert nature” is the Deity of Jagannātha, seen as wood; in Sarasvatī’s sense, Śrī Kṛṣṇa in Gaura’s golden form attained complete non-difference from Śrī Jagannātha — the Lord of māyā, the Dāru-brahma, the Supreme Self full in six opulences.

[^32]: Edition heading: the foolish poet’s error — Māyāvāda, opposed to the conclusions of pure bhakti. *Anubhāṣya*: *śarīrī* — he whose body it is, the embodied one (*dehī*).

[^33]: Edition heading: all rejoiced, but Śrī Svarūpa, the Vaiṣṇava ācārya and teacher of the world, grows angry and so shows his mercy: with the sword of scriptural siddhānta he cuts down the false doctrine.

[^34]: Edition heading: the cause of his anger: (1) to regard Viṣṇu as a jīva leads to hell.

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: to see in the Deity of Jagannātha only a wooden image, perishable and material, is an offence; the Deity is non-different from Śrī Kṛṣṇa, and the devotee, with eyes of bhakti, sees Him directly as the full form of eternity, knowledge and bliss.

[^36]: *Anubhāṣya*: by the śruti “As sparks fly from a fire…” the jīva is like a spark of the fire that is the great Viṣṇu, a particle of spirit; but Śrī Caitanya-deva, the Lord of māyā, taking a human form does not make Him a tiny jīva subject to matter.

[^37]: Edition heading: words of the *Kūrma Purāṇa* quoted in the *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta* (Pūrva-khaṇḍa, 128).

[^38]: = Madhya 25.36; translation as there.

[^39]: = Madhya 25.37; translation as there. *Anubhāṣya* (on Madhya 25.37): *asat-prasaṅgaiḥ* — those attached to false arguments, absorbed in speculation on the impersonal Brahman contrary to the path of the śrutis.

[^40]: = Madhya 18.114; translation as there. Edition heading: the *Sarvajña-sūkta* as quoted in the *Bhagavat-sandarbha*, or the words of Śrī Viṣṇusvāmī quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī in his commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.7.6.

[^41]: *Anubhāṣya*: from a Māyāvādī, from a grammarian clever with words but without bhakti, or from a pleasure-seeker greedy for money, the *Bhāgavata* will not bring love for Kṛṣṇa — only the taste for the mundane will grow; it must be studied only with a Vaiṣṇava paramahaṁsa who has renounced the world, and its meaning, revealed by Śrī Caitanya, is attained by taking exclusive shelter at His feet.

[^42]: Edition heading: even through the words of a fool or an enemy reviling Kṛṣṇa, pure Sarasvatī — Kṛṣṇa’s maidservant, the very word of bhakti-siddhānta — serves Gaura-Kṛṣṇa. *Anubhāṣya*: out of ignorance the poet did not see the difference between Māyāvāda and the path of bhakti, and expressed himself at random; but with those very ill-chosen words Sarasvatī glorified her worshipful Gaura-Kṛṣṇa.

[^43]: *Anubhāṣya*: in Indra’s sense — wordy, a child or a fool, ill-mannered, blind to consequences, a mortal who thinks himself learned; in Sarasvatī’s sense (140–142) — the source of the Vedas, free of pride, bowing to none, all-knowing, honoured by the learned, appearing as a man out of love for His devotees.

[^44]: *Dāru-brahma* — the Brahman manifest in a form of sacred wood (*dāru*); to see in the Deity only a wooden image is an offence (*Anubhāṣya*, see 118). Edition heading (147–148): Svarūpa explains the worldly poet’s words as praise of Kṛṣṇa and establishes the non-difference of the Dāru-brahma, Jagannātha, from the moving Brahman (*jaṅgama-brahma*), Gaurahari.

[^45]: Edition heading: to deliver the world, the one Deity is manifest in two forms by the power of His will (*icchā-śakti*).

[^46]: Edition heading: even when Kṛṣṇa is reviled, the name uttered as *stobha* — *nāmābhāsa* — grants liberation. *Stobha* is a kind of *nāmābhāsa*: the name uttered not for its own sake.

[^47]: Edition heading: first the devotees’ mercy, and through it the mercy of Mahāprabhu.

[^48]: Edition heading: the poet accepts the dharma of sannyāsa and lives in Purī.

[^49]: Edition heading: through faith in the Vaiṣṇavas even an ignorant poet obtains the Lord’s mercy.
