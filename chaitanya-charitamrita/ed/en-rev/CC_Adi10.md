“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 10  
**The Description of the Branches of the Main Trunk**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

শ্রীচৈতন্যপদাম্ভোজ-মধুপেভ্যো নমো নমঃ ৷  
কথঞ্চিদাশ্রয়াদ্ যেষাং শ্বাপি তদ্গন্ধভাগ্ভবেৎ ॥ ১ ॥

śrī-caitanya-padāmbhoja-  
of Śrī Caitanya’s  lotus feet  
madhupebhyo namo namaḥ  
to the bees  obeisance  obeisance  
kathañcid āśrayād yeṣāṁ  
in any way  by taking shelter  of whom  
śvāpi tad-gandha-bhāg bhavet  
even a dog  of that fragrance  a partaker  may become

(1) Again and again I bow to the bees that drink the honey of Śrī Caitanya’s lotus feet. Whoever takes shelter of them in any way at all, even one as low as a dog, comes to share in the fragrance of those feet.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaitacandra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এই মালীর — এই বৃক্ষের অকথ্য কথন ৷  
এবে শুন মুখ্যশাখার নাম-বিবরণ ॥ ৩ ॥

ei mālīra — ei vṛkṣera akathya kathana  
of this gardener — of this tree  inexpressible  the account  
ebe śuna mukhya-śākhāra nāma-vivaraṇa  
now  hear  of the main branches  of the names  the account

(3) This gardener, this tree, is beyond all telling. Now hear the names of its principal branches.

চৈতন্য-গোসাঞির যত পারিষদচয় ৷  
লঘু-গুরু-ভাব তাঁর না হয় নিশ্চয় ॥ ৪ ॥

caitanya-gosāñira yata pāriṣada-caya  
of Caitanya Gosāñi  as many  associates  the host  
laghu-guru-bhāva tām̐ra nā haya niścaya  
lesser or greater  status  their  not  is  determinable

(4) Of all the host of Caitanya Gosāñi’s associates, none can determine who is greater and who is lesser.[^2]

যত যত মহান্ত কৈলা তাঁ-সবার গণন ৷  
কেহ করিবারে নারে জ্যেষ্ঠ-লঘু-ক্রম ॥ ৫ ॥

yata yata mahānta kailā tāṅ-sabāra gaṇana  
as many  as many  great souls  made  of them all  an enumeration  
keha karibāre nāre jyeṣṭha-laghu-krama  
anyone  to make  is not able  of senior and junior  the order

(5) Many great souls have listed them all, yet none has been able to rank them as senior and junior.

অতএব তাঁ-সবারে করি’ নমস্কার ৷  
নাম-মাত্র করি, দোষ না লবে আমার ॥ ৬ ॥

ataeva tām̐-sabāre kari’ namaskāra  
therefore  to them all  having made  obeisance  
nāma-mātra kari, doṣa nā labe āmāra  
names  only  I give  fault  do not take  my

(6) Therefore, bowing down to them all, I shall simply name them. Please do not count this as a fault in me.

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-প্রেমামরতরোঃ প্রিয়ান্ ৷  
শাখারূপান্ ভক্তগণান্ কৃষ্ণপ্রেমফলপ্রদান্ ॥ ৭ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
I worship  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya,  
premāmara-taroḥ priyān  
the immortal tree of love,  the dear ones  
śākhā-rūpān bhakta-gaṇān  
in the form of branches  the devotees  
kṛṣṇa-prema-phala-pradān  
of love for Kṛṣṇa  the fruit  who give

(7) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the immortal tree of love. I worship His dear devotees, who are the branches of that tree and give everyone the fruit of love for Kṛṣṇa.[^3]

শ্রীবাস পণ্ডিত, আর শ্রীরাম পণ্ডিত ৷  
দুই ভাই — দুই শাখা, জগতে বিদিত ॥ ৮ ॥

śrīvāsa paṇḍita, āra śrī-rāma paṇḍita  
Śrīvāsa  Paṇḍita  and  Śrī  Rāma  Paṇḍita  
dui bhāi — dui śākhā, jagate vidita  
two  brothers —  two  branches  in the world  renowned

(8) Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and Śrī Rāma Paṇḍita — two brothers, two branches — are renowned throughout the world.[^4]

শ্রীপতি, শ্রীনিধি — তাঁর দুই সহোদর ৷  
চারি ভাইর দাস-দাসী, গৃহ-পরিকর ॥ ৯ ॥

śrīpati, śrīnidhi — tām̐ra dui sahodara  
Śrīpati,  Śrīnidhi —  their  two  brothers  
cāri bhāira dāsa-dāsī, gṛha-parikara  
of the four  brothers  menservants and maidservants,  household

(9) Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi are their two brothers, and the four brothers had menservants, maidservants and a whole household.

দুই শাখার উপশাখায় তাঁ-সবার গণন ৷  
যাঁর গৃহে মহাপ্রভুর সদা সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ॥ ১০ ॥

dui śākhāra upaśākhāya tāṅ-sabāra gaṇana  
of the two  branches  among the offshoots  of them all  the counting  
yām̐ra gṛhe mahāprabhura sadā saṅkīrtana  
in whose  house  Mahāprabhu’s  constant  saṅkīrtana

(10) All of them are counted among the offshoots of these two branches. In their house Mahāprabhu held saṅkīrtana constantly.[^5]

সবংশে করেন যাঁরা চৈতন্যের সেবা ৷  
গৌরচন্দ্র বিনা নাহি জানে দেবী-দেবা ॥ ১১ ॥

savaṁśe karena yām̐rā caitanyera sevā  
with their family  render  who  Caitanya  service  
gauracandra vinā nāhi jāne devī-devā  
Gauracandra  besides  not  know  goddess or god

(11) With their whole family they served Śrī Caitanya, and apart from Gauracandra they knew no god or goddess.

‘আচার্য্যরত্ন’ নাম ধরে বড় এক শাখা ৷  
তাঁর পরিকর, তাঁর শাখা-উপশাখা ॥ ১২ ॥

‘ācāryaratna’ nāma dhare baḍa eka śākhā  
‘Ācāryaratna’  the name  bears  big  one  branch  
tām̐ra parikara, tām̐ra śākhā-upaśākhā  
his  associates  his  branches and offshoots

(12) Another great branch bears the name Ācāryaratna; his associates are its branches and offshoots.

আচার্য্যরত্নের নাম ‘শ্রীচন্দ্রশেখর’ ৷  
যাঁর ঘরে দেবী-ভাবে নাচিলা ঈশ্বর ॥ ১৩ ॥

ācāryaratnera nāma ‘śrī-candraśekhara’  
of Ācāryaratna  the name  ‘Śrī Candraśekhara’  
yām̐ra ghare devī-bhāve nācilā īśvara  
in whose  house  in the mood of the Goddess  danced  the Lord

(13) The name of Ācāryaratna is Śrī Candraśekhara. In his house the Lord danced in the form of the Goddess, the Ādi-śakti.[^6]

পুণ্ডরীক বিদ্যানিধি — বড়শাখা জানি ৷  
যাঁর নাম লঞা প্রভু কান্দিলা আপনি ॥ ১৪ ॥

puṇḍarīka vidyānidhi — baḍa-śākhā jāni  
Puṇḍarīka  Vidyānidhi —  a great  branch  I know  
yām̐ra nāma lañā prabhu kāndilā āpani  
whose  name  uttering  the Lord  wept  Himself

(14) Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi is a great branch; the Lord Himself would weep as He spoke his name.[^7]

বড় শাখা, — গদাধর পণ্ডিত-গোসাঞি ৷  
তেঁহো লক্ষ্মীরূপা, তাঁর সম কেহ নাই ॥ ১৫ ॥

baḍa śākhā, — gadādhara paṇḍita-gosāñi  
a great  branch —  Gadādhara  Paṇḍita  Gosāñi  
tem̐ho lakṣmī-rūpā, tām̐ra sama keha nāi  
he  Lakṣmī in form,  to him  equal  anyone  there is not

(15) Another great branch is Gadādhara Paṇḍita Gosāñi. He is Lakṣmī Herself in form, and none is his equal.[^8]

তাঁর শিষ্য-উপশিষ্য, — তাঁর উপশাখা ৷  
এইমত সব শাখা-উপশাখার লেখা ॥ ১৬ ॥

tām̐ra śiṣya-upaśiṣya, — tām̐ra upaśākhā  
his  disciples and their disciples —  his  offshoots  
eimata saba śākhā-upaśākhāra lekhā  
in this way  of all  the branches and offshoots  the account

(16) His disciples and their disciples are the offshoots of this branch, and so it is with all the other branches and offshoots.

বক্রেশ্বর পণ্ডিত — প্রভুর বড় প্রিয়ভৃত্য ৷  
এক-ভাবে চব্বিশ প্রহর যাঁর নৃত্য ॥ ১৭ ॥

vakreśvara paṇḍita — prabhura baḍa priya bhṛtya  
Vakreśvara  Paṇḍita —  of the Lord  a very  dear  servant  
eka-bhāve cabbiśa prahara yām̐ra nṛtya  
in one ecstasy  twenty-four  praharas  whose  dance

(17) Vakreśvara Paṇḍita was a servant very dear to the Lord. In a single unbroken ecstasy he could dance for twenty-four praharas — three days and nights.

আপনে মহাপ্রভু গাহেন যাঁর নৃত্যকালে ৷  
প্রভুর চরণ ধরি’ বক্রেশ্বর বলে ॥ ১৮ ॥

āpane mahāprabhu gāhena yām̐ra nṛtya-kāle  
Himself  Mahāprabhu  sings  whose  at the time of dancing  
prabhura caraṇa dhari’ vakreśvara bale  
the Lord’s  feet  clasping  Vakreśvara  says

(18) While he danced, Mahāprabhu Himself would sing, and once Vakreśvara clasped the Lord’s feet and said:

দশসহস্র গন্ধর্ব্ব মোরে দেহ’, চন্দ্রমুখ ৷  
তারা গায়, মুঞি নাচি, তবে মোর সুখ ॥ ১৯ ॥

daśa-sahasra gandharva more deha’, candramukha  
ten  thousand  Gandharvas  to me  give,  O moon-faced One  
tārā gāya, muñi nāci — tabe mora sukha  
they  sing,  I  dance —  then  my  happiness

(19) “O moon-faced Lord, give me ten thousand Gandharvas! Let them sing while I dance — that would be my joy.”

প্রভু বলেন, — তুমি মোর পক্ষ এক শাখা ৷  
আকাশে উড়িয়া যাঙ, পাঙ আর পাখা ॥ ২০ ॥

prabhu balena, — tumi mora pakṣa eka śākhā  
the Lord  says —  you  My  wing  one  side  
ākāśe uḍiyā yāṅa, pāṅa āra pākhā  
in the sky  flying  I would go,  if I got  another  wing

(20) The Lord replied, “You are one of My wings. If I had another like you, I would fly up into the sky.”

পণ্ডিত জগদানন্দ প্রভুর প্রাণরূপ ৷  
লোকে খ্যাত যেঁহো সত্যভামার স্বরূপ ॥ ২১ ॥

paṇḍita jagadānanda prabhura prāṇa-rūpa  
Paṇḍita  Jagadānanda  of the Lord  the very life  
loke khyāta yem̐ho satyabhāmāra svarūpa  
among people  famed  who  of Satyabhāmā  the manifestation

(21) Paṇḍita Jagadānanda is the Lord’s very life. He is renowned as a manifestation of Satyabhāmā.[^9]

প্রীত্যে করিতে চাহে প্রভুকে লালন-পালন ৷  
বৈরাগ্য-লোক-ভয়ে প্রভু না মানে কখন ॥ ২২ ॥

prītye karite cāhe prabhuke lālana-pālana  
out of love  to do  wants  for the Lord  care and nurture  
vairāgya-loka-bhaye prabhu nā māne kakhana  
for renunciation  people  fearing  the Lord  not  accepts  ever

(22) Out of love he longed to surround the Lord with care and comfort, but the Lord, guarding His renunciation and wary of what people would say, never accepted it.

দুইজনে খট্মটি লাগায় কোন্দল ৷  
তাঁর প্রীত্যের কথা আগে কহিব সকল ॥ ২৩ ॥

dui-jane khaṭmaṭi lāgāya kondala  
the two  little squabbles  start  quarrels  
tām̐ra prītyera kathā āge kahiba sakala  
of his  love  the account  later  I shall tell  all

(23) So the two of them would fall into little quarrels. I shall tell the whole story of his love later on.

রাঘব-পণ্ডিত — প্রভুর আদ্য-অনুচর ৷  
তাঁর শাখা মুখ্য এক, — মকরধ্বজ কর ॥ ২৪ ॥

rāghava-paṇḍita — prabhura ādya-anucara  
Rāghava  Paṇḍita —  of the Lord  an early  follower  
tām̐ra śākhā mukhya eka, — makaradhvaja kara  
his  branch  main  one —  Makaradhvaja  Kara

(24) Rāghava Paṇḍita was one of the Lord’s first followers, and his chief branch is Makaradhvaja Kara.[^10]

তাঁহার ভগিনী দময়ন্তী প্রভুর প্রিয় দাসী ৷  
প্রভুর ভোগসামগ্রী যে করে বারমাসি ॥ ২৫ ॥

tām̐hāra bhaginī damayantī prabhura priya dāsī  
his  sister  Damayantī  of the Lord  dear  maidservant  
prabhura bhoga-sāmagrī ye kare vāra-māsi  
of the Lord  the provisions  who  makes  all year round

(25) His sister Damayantī, a dear maidservant of the Lord, prepared provisions for Him all year round.

সে সব সামগ্রী যত ঝালিতে ভরিয়া ৷  
রাঘব লইয়া যা’ন গুপত করিয়া ॥ ২৬ ॥

se saba sāmagrī yata jhālite bhariyā  
those  all  provisions  as many  in bags  filling  
rāghava laiyā yā’na gupata kariyā  
Rāghava  taking  goes  secretly  keeping

(26) Filling bags with all those provisions, Rāghava would carry them secretly to the Lord.

বারমাস তাহা প্রভু করেন অঙ্গীকার ৷  
‘রাঘবের ঝালি’ বলি’ প্রসিদ্ধি যাহার ॥ ২৭ ॥

vāra-māsa tāhā prabhu karena aṅgīkāra  
all the year  them  the Lord  accepts  
‘rāghavera jhāli’ bali’ prasiddhi yāhāra  
‘Rāghava’s bags’  calling  the fame  of which

(27) All year round the Lord accepted them, and they became famous as “Rāghava’s bags.”[^11]

সে সব সামগ্রী আগে করিব বিস্তার ৷  
যাহার শ্রবণে ভক্তের বহে অশ্রুধার ॥ ২৮ ॥

se saba sāmagrī āge kariba vistāra  
those  all  provisions  later  I shall make  in detail  
yāhāra śravaṇe bhaktera vahe aśrudhāra  
of which  in the hearing  of devotees  flow  streams of tears

(28) I shall describe those provisions in detail later. Hearing of them, the devotees shed streams of tears.

প্রভুর অত্যন্ত প্রিয় — পণ্ডিত গঙ্গাদাস ৷  
যাঁহার স্মরণে হয় সর্ব্ববন্ধ-নাশ ॥ ২৯ ॥

prabhura atyanta priya — paṇḍita gaṅgādāsa  
of the Lord  extremely  dear —  Paṇḍita  Gaṅgādāsa  
yām̐hāra smaraṇe haya sarva-bandha-nāśa  
by whose  remembrance  there is  of all  bonds  destruction

(29) Paṇḍita Gaṅgādāsa was extremely dear to the Lord. By remembering him all bondage is destroyed.

চৈতন্য-পার্ষদ — শ্রীআচার্য্য পুরন্দর ৷  
পিতা করি’ যাঁরে বলে গৌরাঙ্গসুন্দর ॥ ৩০ ॥

caitanya-pārṣada — śrī-ācārya purandara  
Caitanya’s  associate —  Śrī  Ācārya  Purandara  
pitā kari’ yām̐re bale gaurāṅga-sundara  
father  calling  whom  addresses  Gaurāṅgasundara

(30) Śrī Purandara Ācārya was an associate of Śrī Caitanya; Gaurāṅgasundara called him Father.[^12]

দামোদরপণ্ডিত শাখা প্রেমেতে প্রচণ্ড ৷  
প্রভুর উপরে যেঁহো কৈল বাক্যদণ্ড ॥ ৩১ ॥

dāmodara-paṇḍita śākhā premete pracaṇḍa  
Dāmodara  Paṇḍita  the branch  in love  fierce  
prabhura upare yem̐ho kaila vākya-daṇḍa  
of the Lord  toward  who  made  a chastisement in words

(31) The branch Dāmodara Paṇḍita was fierce in his love; he even rebuked the Lord in words.

দণ্ড-কথা কহিব আগে বিস্তার করিয়া ৷  
দণ্ডে তুষ্ট প্রভু তাঁরে পাঠাইলা নদীয়া ॥ ৩২ ॥

daṇḍa-kathā kahiba āge vistāra kariyā  
of the rebuke  the story  I shall tell  later  in detail  making  
daṇḍe tuṣṭa prabhu tām̐re pāṭhāilā nadīyā  
with the rebuke  pleased  the Lord  him  sent  to Nadīyā

(32) I shall tell the story of that rebuke in detail later. Pleased with it, the Lord sent him to Nadīyā.[^13]

তাঁহার অনুজ শাখা — শঙ্করপণ্ডিত ৷  
‘প্রভু-পাদোপাধান’ যাঁর নাম বিদিত ॥ ৩৩ ॥

tām̐hāra anuja śākhā — śaṅkara-paṇḍita  
his  younger brother  a branch —  Śaṅkara  Paṇḍita  
‘prabhu-pādopādhāna’ yām̐ra nāma vidita  
‘the Lord’s footrest’  whose  name  is known

(33) His younger brother, Śaṅkara Paṇḍita, is a branch known by the name “the Lord’s footrest.”

সদাশিবপণ্ডিত যাঁর প্রভুপদে আশ ৷  
প্রথমেই নিত্যানন্দের যাঁর ঘরে বাস ॥ ৩৪ ॥

sadāśiva-paṇḍita yām̐ra prabhu-pade āśa  
Sadāśiva  Paṇḍita  whose  at the Lord’s feet  hope  
prathamei nityānandera yām̐ra ghare vāsa  
at the very first  Nityānanda’s  in whose  house  dwelling

(34) Sadāśiva Paṇḍita set all his hope on the Lord’s feet, and at first Nityānanda lived in his house.

শ্রীনৃসিংহ-উপাসক — প্রদ্যুম্ন ব্রহ্মচারী ৷  
প্রভু তাঁর নাম কৈলা ‘নৃসিংহানন্দ’ করি’ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

śrī-nṛsiṁha-upāsaka — pradyumna brahmacārī  
of Śrī Nṛsiṁha  a worshipper —  Pradyumna  Brahmacārī  
prabhu tām̐ra nāma kailā ‘nṛsiṁhānanda’ kari’  
the Lord  his  name  made  ‘Nṛsiṁhānanda’  making

(35) Pradyumna Brahmacārī was a worshipper of Śrī Nṛsiṁha, and the Lord gave him the name Nṛsiṁhānanda.

নারায়ণ-পণ্ডিত এক বড়ই উদার ৷  
চৈতন্য-চরণ বিনু নাহি জানে আর ॥ ৩৬ ॥

nārāyaṇa-paṇḍita eka baḍai udāra  
Nārāyaṇa  Paṇḍita  one  most  generous  
caitanya-caraṇa vinu nāhi jāne āra  
of Caitanya  the feet  besides  not  knows  anything else

(36) Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍita was a most generous soul who knew nothing but the feet of Śrī Caitanya.

শ্রীমান্পণ্ডিত শাখা — প্রভুর নিজ ভৃত্য ৷  
দেউটি ধরেন, যবে প্রভু করেন নৃত্য ॥ ৩৭ ॥

śrīmān-paṇḍita śākhā — prabhura nija bhṛtya  
Śrīmān  Paṇḍita  a branch —  of the Lord  own  servant  
deuṭi dharena, yabe prabhu karena nṛtya  
the torch  holds  when  the Lord  dances

(37) The branch Śrīmān Paṇḍita was the Lord’s personal servant; whenever the Lord danced, he held the torch.

শুক্লাম্বর-ব্রহ্মচারী বড় ভাগ্যবান্ ৷  
যাঁর অন্ন মাগি’ কাড়ি’ খাইলা ভগবান্ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

śuklāmbara-brahmacārī baḍa bhāgyavān  
Śuklāmbara  Brahmacārī  very  fortunate  
yām̐ra anna māgi’ kāḍi’ khāilā bhagavān  
whose  rice  begging  snatching  ate  the Lord

(38) Śuklāmbara Brahmacārī was most fortunate: the Lord Himself would beg his rice, or snatch it from him, and eat it.[^14]

নন্দন-আচার্য্য-শাখা জগতে বিদিত ৷  
লুকাইয়া দুই প্রভুর যাঁর ঘরে স্থিত ॥ ৩৯ ॥

nandana-ācārya-śākhā jagate vidita  
Nandana  Ācārya  a branch  in the world  famed  
lukāiyā dui prabhura yām̐ra ghare sthita  
hiding  the two  Lords  in whose  house  stayed

(39) The branch Nandana Ācārya is famed in the world, for the two Lords hid in his house.

শ্রীমুকুন্দ-দত্ত-শাখা — প্রভুর সমাধ্যায়ী ৷  
যাঁহার কীর্ত্তনে নাচে চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ॥ ৪০ ॥

śrī-mukunda-datta-śākhā — prabhura samādhyāyī  
Śrī  Mukunda  Datta  a branch —  of the Lord  a fellow student  
yām̐hāra kīrtane nāce caitanya-gosāñi  
in whose  kīrtana  dances  Caitanya Gosāñi

(40) The branch Śrī Mukunda Datta was the Lord’s fellow student; when he sang kīrtana, Caitanya Gosāñi danced.

বাসুদেব দত্ত — প্রভুর ভৃত্য মহাশয় ৷  
সহস্র-মুখে যাঁর গুণ কহিলে না হয় ॥ ৪১ ॥

vāsudeva datta — prabhura bhṛtya mahāśaya  
Vāsudeva  Datta —  of the Lord  a servant  great-souled  
sahasra-mukhe yām̐ra guṇa kahile nā haya  
with a thousand mouths  whose  qualities  telling  cannot be done

(41) Vāsudeva Datta was the Lord’s great-souled servant; even with a thousand mouths one could not tell his virtues.

জগতে যতেক জীব, তার পাপ লঞা ৷  
নরক ভুঞ্জিতে চাহে জীব ছাড়াইয়া ॥ ৪২ ॥

jagate yateka jīva, tāra pāpa lañā  
in the world  as many  beings,  their  sins  taking  
naraka bhuñjite cāhe jīva chāḍāiyā  
hell  to suffer  wants  the beings  setting free

(42) He wished to take upon himself the sins of every living being in the world and suffer in hell himself, if only they could be set free.[^15]

হরিদাসঠাকুর-শাখার অদ্ভুত চরিত ৷  
তিন লক্ষ নাম তেঁহো লয়েন অপতিত ॥ ৪৩ ॥

haridāsa-ṭhākura-śākhāra adbhuta carita  
of the branch Haridāsa Ṭhākura  wonderful  the character  
tina lakṣa nāma tem̐ho layena apatita  
three  hundred thousand  names  he  takes  without fail

(43) Wonderful is the life of the branch Haridāsa Ṭhākura: without fail he chanted three hundred thousand names every day.

তাঁহার অনন্ত গুণ — কহি দিঙ্মাত্র ৷  
আচার্য্য-গোসাঞি যাঁরে ভুঞ্জায় শ্রাদ্ধপাত্র ॥ ৪৪ ॥

tām̐hāra ananta guṇa — kahi diṅmātra  
his  endless  virtues —  I tell  only a glimpse  
ācārya-gosāñi yām̐re bhuñjāya śrāddha-pātra  
Ācārya Gosāñi  whom  fed  the śrāddha plate

(44) His virtues are endless; I can give only a glimpse of them. Advaita Ācārya fed him the plate of the śrāddha offering.[^16]

প্রহ্লাদ-সমান তাঁর গুণের তরঙ্গ ৷  
যবন-তাড়নেও যাঁর নাহিক ভ্রূভঙ্গ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

prahlāda-samāna tām̐ra guṇera taraṅga  
like Prahlāda  his  of virtues  the waves  
yavana-tāḍaneo yām̐ra nāhika bhrū-bhaṅga  
even when beaten by the Yavanas  whose  not  a frown

(45) The waves of his virtues were like Prahlāda’s: even when the Yavanas beat him, he did not so much as frown.

তেঁহো সিদ্ধি পাইলে তাঁর দেহ লঞা কোলে ৷  
নাচিল চৈতন্যপ্রভু মহাকুতুহলে ॥ ৪৬ ॥

tem̐ho siddhi pāile tām̐ra deha lañā kole  
he  perfection  attained when  his  body  taking  on His lap  
nācila caitanya-prabhu mahā-kutuhale  
danced  Caitanya Prabhu  in great  joy

(46) When he attained perfection, Caitanya Prabhu took his body in His arms and danced in great joy.

তাঁর লীলা বর্ণিয়াছেন বৃন্দাবন দাস ৷  
যেবা অবশিষ্ট, আগে করিব প্রকাশ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

tām̐ra līlā varṇiyāchena vṛndāvana dāsa  
his  pastimes  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa  
yebā avaśiṣṭa, āge kariba prakāśa  
whatever  remains,  later  I shall make  known

(47) Vṛndāvana dāsa has described his pastimes; what remains I shall tell later.

তাঁর উপশাখা, — যত কুলীনগ্রামী জন ৷  
সত্যরাজ-আদি — তাঁর কৃপার ভাজন ॥ ৪৮ ॥

tām̐ra upaśākhā, — yata kulīna-grāmī jana  
his  offshoots —  all the  of Kulīna-grāma  people  
satyarāja-ādi — tām̐ra kṛpāra bhājana  
Satyarāja and others —  of his  mercy  the recipients

(48) All the people of Kulīna-grāma are his offshoots; Satyarāja and others received his mercy.[^17]

শ্রীমুরারি গুপ্ত-শাখা — প্রেমের ভাণ্ডার ৷  
প্রভুর হৃদয় দ্রবে শুনি’ দৈন্য যাঁর ॥ ৪৯ ॥

śrī-murāri gupta-śākhā — premera bhāṇḍāra  
Śrī  Murāri  Gupta  a branch —  of love  a storehouse  
prabhura hṛdaya drave śuni’ dainya yām̐ra  
of the Lord  the heart  melts  hearing  the humility  whose

(49) The branch Śrī Murāri Gupta is a storehouse of love; hearing his humility, the Lord’s heart would melt.[^18]

প্রতিগ্রহ নাহি করে, না লয় কার ধন ৷  
আত্মবৃত্তি করি’ করে কুটুম্ব ভরণ ॥ ৫০ ॥

pratigraha nāhi kare, nā laya kāra dhana  
gifts  not  accepts,  not  takes  anyone’s  wealth  
ātma-vṛtti kari’ kare kuṭumba bharaṇa  
his own calling  following  makes  of the family  support

(50) He accepted no gifts and took no one’s money, but supported his family by his own calling.[^19]

চিকিৎসা করেন যারে হইয়া সদয় ৷  
দেহরোগ, ভবরোগ, — দুই তার ক্ষয় ॥ ৫১ ॥

cikitsā karena yāre haiyā sadaya  
treatment  gives  to whom  being  merciful  
deha-roga, bhava-roga, — dui tāra kṣaya  
of the body  the disease,  of worldly existence  the disease —  both  his  are destroyed

(51) Whomever he treated with kindness was freed from both diseases — the disease of the body and the disease of worldly existence.

শ্রীমান্ সেন প্রভুর সেবক প্রধান ৷  
চৈতন্য-চরণ বিনু নাহি জানে আন ॥ ৫২ ॥

śrīmān sena prabhura sevaka pradhāna  
Śrīmān  Sena  of the Lord  a servant  foremost  
caitanya-caraṇa vinu nāhi jāne āna  
of Caitanya  the feet  besides  not  knows  anything else

(52) Śrīmān Sena was a foremost servant of the Lord and knew nothing but the feet of Śrī Caitanya.

শ্রীগদাধর দাস-শাখা সর্ব্বোপরি ৷  
কাজীগণের মুখে যেঁহ বোলাইল হরি ॥ ৫৩ ॥

śrī-gadādhara dāsa-śākhā sarvopari  
Śrī  Gadādhara  dāsa  a branch  above all  
kājī-gaṇera mukhe yem̐ha bolāila hari  
of the Kazis  in the mouths  who  made utter  ‘Hari’

(53) The branch Śrī Gadādhara dāsa stands above all; he made even the Kazis utter the name of Hari.[^20]

শিবানন্দ সেন — প্রভুর ভৃত্য অন্তরঙ্গ ৷  
প্রভু-স্থানে যাইতে সবে লয়েন যাঁর সঙ্গ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

śivānanda sena — prabhura bhṛtya antaraṅga  
Śivānanda  Sena —  of the Lord  a servant  intimate  
prabhu-sthāne yāite sabe layena yām̐ra saṅga  
to the Lord’s place  going  all  take  whose  company

(54) Śivānanda Sena was an intimate servant of the Lord; all who went to see the Lord went in his company.

প্রতিবর্ষে প্রভুগণ সঙ্গেতে লইয়া ৷  
নীলাচলে চলেন পথে পালন করিয়া ॥ ৫৫ ॥

prativarṣe prabhu-gaṇa saṅgete laiyā  
every year  the Lord’s devotees  with him  taking  
nīlācale calena pathe pālana kariyā  
to Nīlācala  goes  on the way  care  taking

(55) Every year he took the Lord’s devotees with him to Nīlācala, caring for them all along the way.[^21]

ভক্তে কৃপা করেন প্রভু এ-তিন স্বরূপে ৷  
‘সাক্ষাৎ’, ‘আবেশ’ আর ‘আবির্ভাব’ রূপে ॥ ৫৬ ॥

bhakte kṛpā karena prabhu e-tina svarūpe  
to the devotees  mercy  shows  the Lord  in these three  forms  
‘sākṣāt’, ‘āveśa’ āra ‘āvirbhāva’ rūpe  
‘sākṣāt’,  ‘āveśa’  and  ‘āvirbhāva’  in the form

(56) The Lord shows His mercy to the devotees in three ways: by His direct presence (*sākṣāt*), by entering into someone with His power (*āveśa*), and by manifesting Himself where He is not present in person (*āvirbhāva*).

‘সাক্ষাতে’ সকল ভক্ত দেখে নির্ব্বিশেষ ৷  
নকুল ব্রহ্মচারী-দেহে প্রভুর ‘আবেশ’ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

‘sākṣāte’ sakala bhakta dekhe nirviśeṣa  
in ‘sākṣāt’  all  the devotees  see  without distinction  
nakula brahmacārī-dehe prabhura ‘āveśa’  
Nakula  Brahmacārī’s  in the body  the Lord’s  ‘āveśa’

(57) In His direct presence all the devotees see Him alike; in the body of Nakula Brahmacārī there was the Lord’s *āveśa*.[^22]

‘প্রদ্যুম্ন ব্রহ্মচারী’ তাঁর আগে নাম ছিল ৷  
‘নৃসিংহানন্দ’ নাম প্রভু পাছে ত’ রাখিল ॥ ৫৮ ॥

‘pradyumna brahmacārī’ tām̐ra āge nāma chila  
‘Pradyumna  Brahmacārī’  his  earlier  name  was  
‘nṛsiṁhānanda’ nāma prabhu pāche ta’ rākhila  
‘Nṛsiṁhānanda’  the name  the Lord  afterwards  indeed  gave

(58) His earlier name was Pradyumna Brahmacārī; afterwards the Lord gave him the name Nṛsiṁhānanda.

তাঁহাতে হইল চৈতন্যের ‘আবির্ভাব’ ৷  
অলৌকিক ঐছে প্রভুর অনেক স্বভাব ॥ ৫৯ ॥

tām̐hāte haila caitanyera ‘āvirbhāva’  
in him  there was  Caitanya’s  ‘āvirbhāva’  
alaukika aiche prabhura aneka svabhāva  
extraordinary  such  of the Lord  many  ways

(59) In him Śrī Caitanya manifested Himself (*āvirbhāva*). The Lord has many such wonderful, unearthly ways.

আস্বাদিল এ সব রস সেন শিবানন্দ ৷  
বিস্তারি’ কহিব আগে এসব আনন্দ ॥ ৬০ ॥

āsvādila e saba rasa sena śivānanda  
tasted  these  all  rasas  Sena  Śivānanda  
vistāri’ kahiba āge esaba ānanda  
in detail  I shall tell  later  all this  bliss

(60) Śivānanda Sena tasted all these rasas; I shall describe this bliss in detail later.[^23]

শিবানন্দের উপশাখা — তাঁর পরিকর ৷  
পুত্র-ভৃত্য-আদি করি’ চৈতন্য-কিঙ্কর ॥ ৬১ ॥

śivānandera upaśākhā — tām̐ra parikara  
Śivānanda’s  offshoots —  his  associates  
putra-bhṛty-ādi kari’ caitanya-kiṅkara  
sons  servants and others  taking together  Caitanya’s  servants

(61) The offshoots of Śivānanda are his own people — his sons, his servants and others, all of them servants of Śrī Caitanya.

চৈতন্যদাস, রামদাস, আর কর্ণপূর ৷  
তিন পুত্র শিবানন্দের প্রভুর ভক্তশূর ॥ ৬২ ॥

caitanya-dāsa, rāmadāsa, āra karṇapūra  
Caitanya dāsa,  Rāmadāsa,  and  Karṇapūra  
tina putra śivānandera prabhura bhakta-śūra  
three  sons  of Śivānanda,  of the Lord  heroic devotees

(62) Caitanya dāsa, Rāmadāsa and Karṇapūra, the three sons of Śivānanda, were heroic devotees of the Lord.[^24]

শ্রীবল্লভ সেন, আর সেন শ্রীকান্ত ৷  
শিবানন্দ-সম্বন্ধে প্রভুর ভক্ত একান্ত ॥ ৬৩ ॥

śrī-vallabha sena, āra sena śrīkānta  
Śrī Vallabha  Sena,  and  Sena  Śrīkānta  
śivānanda-sambandhe prabhura bhakta ekānta  
through relation with Śivānanda  of the Lord  devotees  exclusive

(63) Śrī Vallabha Sena and Śrīkānta Sena, related to Śivānanda, were wholly devoted to the Lord.[^25]

প্রভুপ্রিয় গোবিন্দানন্দ — মহাভাগবত ৷  
প্রভুর কীর্ত্তনীয়া আদি — শ্রীগোবিন্দ দত্ত ॥ ৬৪ ॥

prabhu-priya govindānanda — mahābhāgavata  
dear to the Lord  Govindānanda —  a great devotee  
prabhura kīrtanīyā ādi — śrī-govinda datta  
of the Lord  the kīrtana singer  first —  Śrī  Govinda  Datta

(64) Govindānanda, dear to the Lord, was a great devotee, and Śrī Govinda Datta was the leading singer in the Lord’s kīrtana.[^26]

শ্রীবিজয়দাস-নাম প্রভুর আখরিয়া ৷  
প্রভুরে অনেক গ্রন্থ দিয়াছে লিখিয়া ॥ ৬৫ ॥

śrī-vijaya-dāsa-nāma prabhura ākhariyā  
Śrī  Vijaya  dāsa  by name  of the Lord  the copyist  
prabhure aneka grantha diyāche likhiyā  
to the Lord  many  books  gave  having written out

(65) Śrī Vijaya dāsa was the Lord’s copyist; he wrote out many books for Him.[^27]

‘রত্নবাহু’ বলি’ প্রভু থুইল তাঁর নাম ৷  
অকিঞ্চন প্রভুর প্রিয় কৃষ্ণদাস-নাম ॥ ৬৬ ॥

‘ratnabāhu’ bali’ prabhu thuila tām̐ra nāma  
‘Ratnabāhu’  calling  the Lord  kept  his  name  
akiñcana prabhura priya kṛṣṇadāsa-nāma  
the renunciant  of the Lord  dear  Kṛṣṇadāsa  by name

(66) The Lord named him Ratnabāhu, “Jewel-armed.” Akiñcana Kṛṣṇadāsa, too, was dear to the Lord.

খোলা-বেচা শ্রীধর প্রভুর প্রিয়দাস ৷  
যাঁহা-সনে প্রভু করে নিত্য পরিহাস ॥ ৬৭ ॥

kholā-vecā śrīdhara prabhura priya-dāsa  
the seller of banana-leaf cups  Śrīdhara  of the Lord  a dear servant  
yām̐hā-sane prabhu kare nitya parihāsa  
with whom  the Lord  makes  constantly  jokes

(67) Śrīdhara, who sold cups of banana bark, was a dear servant of the Lord, and the Lord was always joking with him.[^28]

প্রভু যাঁর নিত্য লয় থোড়-মোচা-ফল ৷  
যাঁর ফুটা-লৌহপাত্রে প্রভু পিলা জল ॥ ৬৮ ॥

prabhu yām̐ra nitya laya thoḍa-mocā-phala  
the Lord  from whom  daily  takes  banana pith  banana flowers  fruit  
yām̐ra phuṭā-lauhapātre prabhu pilā jala  
whose  broken  iron pot  from  the Lord  drank  water

(68) Every day the Lord took banana pith, banana flowers and fruit from him, and once He drank water from his broken iron pot.

প্রভুর অতিপ্রিয় দাস ভগবান্ পণ্ডিত ৷  
যাঁর দেহে কৃষ্ণ পূর্ব্বে হৈল অধিষ্ঠিত ॥ ৬৯ ॥

prabhura atipriya dāsa bhagavān paṇḍita  
of the Lord  very dear  servant  Bhagavān  Paṇḍita  
yām̐ra dehe kṛṣṇa pūrve haila adhiṣṭhita  
in whose  body  Kṛṣṇa  formerly  was  seated

(69) Bhagavān Paṇḍita was a servant very dear to the Lord; once Kṛṣṇa had dwelt within his body.

জগদীশ পণ্ডিত, আর হিরণ্য মহাশয় ৷  
যারে কৃপা কৈল বাল্যে প্রভু দয়াময় ॥ ৭০ ॥

jagadīśa paṇḍita, āra hiraṇya mahāśaya  
Jagadīśa  Paṇḍita,  and  Hiraṇya  Mahāśaya  
yāre kṛpā kaila bālye prabhu dayāmaya  
to whom  mercy  showed  in childhood  the Lord  merciful

(70) Jagadīśa Paṇḍita and Hiraṇya Mahāśaya were shown mercy by the merciful Lord when He was still a child.

এই দুই-ঘরে প্রভু একাদশী দিনে ৷  
বিষ্ণুর নৈবেদ্য মাগি’ খাইল আপনে ॥ ৭১ ॥

ei dui-ghare prabhu ekādaśī dine  
in these  two houses  the Lord  on Ekādaśī  day  
viṣṇura naivedya māgi’ khāila āpane  
Viṣṇu’s  offering  begging  ate  Himself

(71) In these two houses, on the day of Ekādaśī, the Lord begged for the food offered to Viṣṇu and ate it Himself.[^29]

প্রভুর পড়ুয়া দুই, — পুরুষোত্তম, সঞ্জয় ৷  
ব্যাকরণে দুই শিষ্য — দুই মহাশয় ॥ ৭২ ॥

prabhura paḍuyā dui, — puruṣottama, sañjaya  
of the Lord  pupils  two —  Puruṣottama,  Sañjaya  
vyākaraṇe dui śiṣya — dui mahāśaya  
in grammar  two  students —  two  great souls

(72) Two of the Lord’s pupils were Puruṣottama and Sañjaya, students of His in grammar and two great souls.[^30]

বনমালী পণ্ডিত-শাখা বিখ্যাত জগতে ৷  
সোণার মুষল হল যে দেখিল প্রভুর হাতে ॥ ৭৩ ॥

vanamālī paṇḍita-śākhā vikhyāta jagate  
Vanamālī  Paṇḍita  a branch  famous  in the world  
soṇāra muṣala hala ye dekhila prabhura hāte  
golden  club  plough  who  saw  in the Lord’s  hand

(73) The branch Vanamālī Paṇḍita is famous in the world; he saw a golden club and plough in the Lord’s hands.

শ্রীচৈতন্যের অতি প্রিয় বুদ্ধিমন্ত খান্ ৷  
আজন্ম আজ্ঞাকারী তেঁহো সেবক-প্রধান ॥ ৭৪ ॥

śrī-caitanyera ati priya buddhimanta khān  
of Śrī Caitanya  very  dear  Buddhimanta  Khān  
ājanma ājñākārī tem̐ho sevaka-pradhāna  
from birth  obedient  he  a servant  chief

(74) Buddhimanta Khān was very dear to Śrī Caitanya; obedient to Him all his life, he was among His chief servants.[^31]

গরুড় পণ্ডিত লয় শ্রীনাম-মঙ্গল ৷  
নাম-বলে বিষ যাঁরে না করিল বল ॥ ৭৫ ॥

garuḍa paṇḍita laya śrīnāma-maṅgala  
Garuḍa  Paṇḍita  takes  the auspicious holy name  
nāma-bale viṣa yām̐re nā karila bala  
by the power of the name  poison  whom  not  overpowered

(75) Garuḍa Paṇḍita constantly chanted the auspicious holy name, and by the power of the name even a snake’s poison could not harm him.

গোপীনাথ সিংহ — এক চৈতন্যের দাস ৷  
অক্রূর বলি’ প্রভু যাঁরে কৈলা পরিহাস ॥ ৭৬ ॥

gopīnātha siṁha — eka caitanyera dāsa  
Gopīnātha  Siṁha —  one  of Caitanya  servant  
akrūra bali’ prabhu yām̐re kailā parihāsa  
Akrūra  calling  the Lord  with whom  joked

(76) Gopīnātha Siṁha was a servant of Śrī Caitanya, whom the Lord would jokingly call Akrūra.

ভাগবতী দেবানন্দ বক্রেশ্বর-কৃপাতে ৷  
ভাগবতের ভক্তি-অর্থ পাইল প্রভু হৈতে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

bhāgavatī devānanda vakreśvara-kṛpāte  
the Bhāgavata reciter  Devānanda  by Vakreśvara’s mercy  
bhāgavatera bhakti-artha pāila prabhu haite  
of the Bhāgavata  the devotional meaning  received  the Lord  from

(77) Devānanda, the reciter of the *Bhāgavata*, is an offshoot of Vakreśvara’s branch: by Vakreśvara’s mercy he received from the Lord the devotional meaning of the *Bhāgavata*.[^32]

খণ্ডবাসী মুকুন্দদাস, শ্রীরঘুনন্দন ৷  
নরহরিদাস, চিরঞ্জীব, সুলোচন ॥ ৭৮ ॥

এই সব মহাশাখা — চৈতন্য-কৃপাধাম ৷  
প্রেম-ফল-ফুল করে যাহাঁ তাহাঁ দান ॥ ৭৯ ॥

khaṇḍavāsī mukunda-dāsa, śrī-raghunandana  
of Khaṇḍa  the residents  Mukunda dāsa,  Śrī  Raghunandana  
narahari-dāsa, cirañjīva, sulocana  
Narahari dāsa,  Ciranjīva,  Sulocana  
ei saba mahāśākhā — caitanya-kṛpādhāma  
these  all  great branches —  of Caitanya’s  mercy  abodes  
prema-phala-phula kare yāhām̐ tāhām̐ dāna  
of love  fruits  flowers  make  everywhere  gift

(78–79) Mukunda dāsa, Śrī Raghunandana, Narahari dāsa, Ciranjīva and Sulocana, all of Śrīkhaṇḍa, are great branches, abodes of Śrī Caitanya’s mercy. Everywhere they give away the fruits and flowers of love.[^33]

কুলীনগ্রামবাসী সত্যরাজ, রামানন্দ ৷  
যদুনাথ, পুরুষোত্তম, শঙ্কর, বিদ্যানন্দ ॥ ৮০ ॥

kulīnagrāma-vāsī satyarāja, rāmānanda  
of Kulīna-grāma  the residents  Satyarāja,  Rāmānanda  
yadunātha, puruṣottama, śaṅkara, vidyānanda  
Yadunātha,  Puruṣottama,  Śaṅkara,  Vidyānanda

(80) From Kulīna-grāma came Satyarāja, Rāmānanda, Yadunātha, Puruṣottama, Śaṅkara and Vidyānanda.[^34]

বাণীনাথ বসু আদি যত গ্রামী জন ৷  
সবেই চৈতন্যভৃত্য, — চৈতন্য-প্রাণধন ॥ ৮১ ॥

vāṇīnātha vasu ādi yata grāmī jana  
Vāṇīnātha  Vasu  and others  all  of the village  people  
sabei caitanya-bhṛtya, — caitanya-prāṇadhana  
all  Caitanya’s servants —  Caitanya  the treasure of their life

(81) Vāṇīnātha Vasu and all the other villagers were servants of Śrī Caitanya, and He was the treasure of their lives.

প্রভু কহে, কুলীনগ্রামের যে হয় কুক্কুর ৷  
সেই মোর প্রিয়, অন্যজন রহু দূর ॥ ৮২ ॥

prabhu kahe, kulīnagrāmera ye haya kukkura  
the Lord  says,  of Kulīna-grāma  who  is  a dog  
sei mora priya, anya jana rahu dūra  
he  to Me  dear,  other  people  let alone

(82) The Lord said, “Even a dog of Kulīna-grāma is dear to Me — what to speak of its people!

কুলীনগ্রামীর ভাগ্য কহনে না যায় ৷  
শূকর চরায় ডোম, সেহ কৃষ্ণ গায় ॥ ৮৩ ॥

kulīnagrāmīra bhāgya kahane nā yāya  
of the people of Kulīna-grāma  the fortune  to describe  is not possible  
śūkara carāya ḍoma, seha kṛṣṇa gāya  
pigs  herds  the Ḍoma,  he too  of Kṛṣṇa  sings

(83) “The good fortune of the people of Kulīna-grāma is beyond words: even the Ḍoma who herds the pigs there sings of Kṛṣṇa.”

অনুপম-বল্লভ, শ্রীরূপ-সনাতন ৷  
এই তিন শাখা বৃক্ষের পশ্চিমে গণন ॥ ৮৪ ॥

anupama-vallabha, śrī-rūpa-sanātana  
Anupama  Vallabha,  Śrī  Rūpa  Sanātana  
ei tina śākhā vṛkṣera paścime gaṇana  
these  three  branches  of the tree  on the west  are counted

(84) Anupama Vallabha, Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana are counted as the three branches on the western side of the tree.[^35]

তাঁর মধ্যে রূপ-সনাতন — বড় শাখা ৷  
অনুপম, জীব, রাজেন্দ্রাদি — উপশাখা ॥ ৮৫ ॥

tām̐ra madhye rūpa-sanātana — baḍa śākhā  
of them  among  Rūpa  Sanātana —  the great branch  
anupama, jīva, rājendrādi — upaśākhā  
Anupama,  Jīva,  Rājendra and others —  offshoots

(85) Among them Rūpa and Sanātana are the great branch, and Anupama, Jīva, Rājendra and others are its offshoots.[^36]

মালীর ইচ্ছায় শাখা বহুত বাড়িল ৷  
বাড়িয়া পশ্চিম দেশ সব আচ্ছাদিল ॥ ৮৬ ॥

mālīra icchāya śākhā bahuta bāḍila  
of the gardener  by the wish  the branches  greatly  grew  
bāḍiyā paścima deśa saba ācchādila  
growing  the western  lands  all  covered

(86) By the gardener’s wish these branches grew so vast that they covered all the western lands.

আ-সিন্ধুনদী-তীর আর হিমালয় ৷  
বৃন্দাবন-মথুরাদি যত তীর্থ হয় ॥ ৮৭ ॥

ā-sindhunadī-tīra āra himālaya  
up to the banks of the river Sindhu  and  the Himālaya  
vṛndāvana-mathurādi yata tīrtha haya  
Vṛndāvana  Mathurā and others  as many  holy places  there are

(87) They spread as far as the banks of the Sindhu and the Himālaya, over Vṛndāvana, Mathurā and every holy place.[^37]

দুই শাখার প্রেমফলে সকল ভাসিল ৷  
প্রেমফলাস্বাদে লোক উন্মত্ত হইল ॥ ৮৮ ॥

dui śākhāra prema-phale sakala bhāsila  
of the two  branches  with the fruit of love  everything  was flooded  
prema-phalāsvāde loka unmatta haila  
the fruit of love  tasting  the people  maddened  became

(88) The fruits of love from these two branches flooded everything, and tasting them the people grew mad with love.

পশ্চিমের লোক সব মূঢ় অনাচার ৷  
তাহাঁ প্রচারিল দুঁহে ভক্তি-সদাচার ॥ ৮৯ ॥

paścimera loka saba mūḍha anācāra  
of the west  the people  all  foolish  of bad conduct  
tāhām̐ pracārila dum̐he bhakti-sadācāra  
there  preached  the two  devotional  good conduct

(89) The people of the west were all foolish and of degraded conduct; there the two brothers taught the good conduct of bhakti.[^38]

শাস্ত্রদৃষ্ট্যে কৈল লুপ্ততীর্থের উদ্ধার ৷  
বৃন্দাবনে কৈল শ্রীমূর্ত্তি-পূজার প্রচার ॥ ৯০ ॥

śāstra-dṛṣṭye kaila lupta-tīrthera uddhāra  
by the scriptures’ sight  made  of the lost holy places  recovery  
vṛndāvane kaila śrīmūrti-pūjāra pracāra  
in Vṛndāvana  made  of Deity worship  the spreading

(90) Guided by the scriptures they recovered the lost holy places, and in Vṛndāvana they spread the worship of the Deities.[^39]

মহাপ্রভুর প্রিয় ভৃত্য — রঘুনাথদাস ৷  
সর্ব্বত্যজি’ কৈল প্রভুর পদতলে বাস ॥ ৯১ ॥

mahāprabhura priya bhṛtya — raghunātha-dāsa  
Mahāprabhu’s  dear  servant —  Raghunātha dāsa  
sarva tyaji’ kaila prabhura pada-tale vāsa  
everything  giving up  made  at the Lord’s  soles of the feet  dwelling

(91) Raghunātha dāsa was a dear servant of Mahāprabhu; giving up everything, he made his home at the Lord’s feet.[^40]

প্রভুসমর্পিল তাঁরে স্বরূপের হাতে ৷  
প্রভুর গুপ্তসেবা কৈল স্বরূপের সাথে ॥ ৯২ ॥

prabhu samarpila tām̐re svarūpera hāte  
the Lord  entrusted  him  of Svarūpa  into the hands  
prabhura gupta-sevā kaila svarūpera sāthe  
the Lord’s  confidential service  did  Svarūpa  with

(92) The Lord placed him in the hands of Svarūpa, and together with Svarūpa he rendered the Lord confidential service.[^41]

ষোড়শ বৎসর কৈল অন্তরঙ্গ-সেবন ৷  
স্বরূপের অন্তর্ধানে আইলা বৃন্দাবন ॥ ৯৩ ॥

ṣoḍaśa vatsara kaila antaraṅga-sevana  
sixteen  years  did  intimate  service  
svarūpera antardhāne āilā vṛndāvana  
of Svarūpa  at the disappearance  came  to Vṛndāvana

(93) For sixteen years he rendered intimate service, and when Svarūpa passed away, he came to Vṛndāvana.[^42]

বৃন্দাবনে দুই ভাইর চরণ দেখিয়া ৷  
গোবর্ধনে ত্যজিব দেহ ভৃগুপাত করিয়া ॥ ৯৪ ॥

vṛndāvane dui bhāira caraṇa dekhiyā  
in Vṛndāvana  of the two  brothers  the feet  having seen  
govardhane tyajiba deha bhṛgupāta kariyā  
at Govardhana  I shall give up  the body  a leap from the cliff  making

(94) He thought, “In Vṛndāvana I shall see the feet of the two brothers, and then at Govardhana I shall give up my body by throwing myself from the hill.”

এই ত’ নিশ্চয় করি’ আইল বৃন্দাবনে ৷  
আসি’ রূপ-সনাতনের বন্দিল চরণে ॥ ৯৫ ॥

ei ta’ niścaya kari’ āila vṛndāvane  
this  indeed  resolution  making  came  to Vṛndāvana  
āsi’ rūpa-sanātanera vandila caraṇe  
having come  of Rūpa and Sanātana  worshipped  the feet

(95) With this resolve he came to Vṛndāvana and bowed down at the feet of Rūpa and Sanātana.

তবে দুই ভাই তাঁরে মরিতে না দিল ৷  
নিজ তৃতীয় ভাই করি’ নিকটে রাখিল ॥ ৯৬ ॥

tabe dui bhāi tām̐re marite nā dila  
then  the two  brothers  him  to die  not  allowed  
nija tṛtīya bhāi kari’ nikaṭe rākhila  
their own  third  brother  making  near  kept

(96) But the two brothers would not let him die; they kept him close by as their own third brother.

মহাপ্রভুর লীলা যত বাহির-অন্তর ৷  
দুই ভাই তাঁর মুখে শুনে নিরন্তর ॥ ৯৭ ॥

mahāprabhura līlā yata bāhira-antara  
of Mahāprabhu  the pastimes  all  outer and inner  
dui bhāi tām̐ra mukhe śune nirantara  
the two  brothers  from his  mouth  hear  constantly

(97) From his lips the two brothers constantly heard all the pastimes of Mahāprabhu, both outer and inner.

অন্ন-জল ত্যাগ কৈল অন্য-কথন ৷  
পল দুই-তিন মাঠা করেন ভক্ষণ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

anna-jala tyāga kaila anya-kathana  
food and water  giving up  made  what to speak of other things  
pala dui-tina māṭhā karena bhakṣaṇa  
palas  two or three  of buttermilk  makes  eating

(98) He gave up food and water, to say nothing of anything else, taking only two or three palas of buttermilk.

সহস্র দণ্ডবৎ করে, লয় লক্ষ নাম ৷  
দুই সহস্র বৈষ্ণবেরে নিত্য পরণাম ॥ ৯৯ ॥

sahasra daṇḍavat kare, laya lakṣa nāma  
a thousand  prostrations  makes,  takes  a hundred thousand  names  
dui sahasra vaiṣṇavere nitya paraṇāma  
two  thousand  to Vaiṣṇavas  daily  obeisances

(99) Every day he offered a thousand prostrations, chanted a hundred thousand names and bowed down to two thousand Vaiṣṇavas.

রাত্রি-দিনে রাধাকৃষ্ণের মানস সেবন ৷  
প্রহরেক মহাপ্রভুর চরিত্র-কথন ॥ ১০০ ॥

rātri-dine rādhā-kṛṣṇera mānasa sevana  
night and day  to Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa  in the mind  service  
prahareka mahāprabhura caritra-kathana  
for a prahara  Mahāprabhu’s  of the character  narration

(100) Night and day he served Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa within his mind, and for one prahara he spoke of the life of Mahāprabhu.[^43]

তিন সন্ধ্যা রাধা-কুণ্ডে অপতিত স্নান ৷  
ব্রজবাসী বৈষ্ণবেরে আলিঙ্গন দান ॥ ১০১ ॥

tina sandhyā rādhā-kuṇḍe apatita snāna  
at the three  junctures of the day  in Rādhā-kuṇḍa  unfailing  bath  
vraja-vāsī vaiṣṇavere āliṅgana dāna  
to the residents of Vraja  Vaiṣṇavas  embrace  gives

(101) At dawn, noon and evening he bathed in Rādhā-kuṇḍa without fail, and he embraced the Vaiṣṇavas of Vraja.

সার্ধ সপ্তপ্রহর করে ভক্তির সাধনে ৷  
চারি দণ্ড নিদ্রা, সেহ নহে কোনদিনে ॥ ১০২ ॥

sārdha sapta-prahara kare bhaktira sādhane  
seven and a half  praharas  makes  of bhakti  in the practice  
cāri daṇḍa nidrā, seha nahe kona-dine  
four  daṇḍas  sleep,  that  is not  on some days

(102) Seven and a half praharas he spent in the practice of bhakti; he slept four daṇḍas, and on some days not even that.

তাঁহার সাধনরীতি শুনিতে চমৎকার ৷  
সেই রূপ-রঘুনাথ প্রভু যে আমার ॥ ১০৩ ॥

tām̐hāra sādhana-rīti śunite camatkāra  
his  way of practice  to hear  wonderful  
sei rūpa-raghunātha prabhu ye āmāra  
those  Rūpa and Raghunātha  masters  who  my

(103) Wonderful it is to hear of his way of practice. These Rūpa and Raghunātha are my masters.[^44]

ইঁহা-সবার যৈছে হৈল প্রভুর মিলন ৷  
আগে বিস্তারিয়া তাহা করিব বর্ণন ॥ ১০৪ ॥

im̐hā-sabāra yaiche haila prabhura milana  
of them  all  how  was  with the Lord  the meeting  
āge vistāriyā tāhā kariba varṇana  
later  in detail  that  I shall make  the description

(104) How each of them met the Lord I shall describe in detail later.[^45]

শ্রীগোপাল ভট্ট — এক শাখা সর্ব্বোত্তম ৷  
রূপ-সনাতন-সঙ্গে যাঁর প্রেম-আলাপন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

śrī-gopāla bhaṭṭa — eka śākhā sarvottama  
Śrī  Gopāla  Bhaṭṭa —  one  branch  the very best  
rūpa-sanātana-saṅge yām̐ra prema-ālāpana  
Rūpa and Sanātana  with  whose  loving conversation

(105) Śrī Gopāla Bhaṭṭa is a branch of the very highest, whose talks of love were shared with Rūpa and Sanātana.[^46]

শঙ্করারণ্য — আচার্য্য-বৃক্ষের এক শাখা ৷  
মুকুন্দ, কাশীনাথ, রুদ্র, — উপশাখা লেখা ॥ ১০৬ ॥

śaṅkarāraṇya — ācārya-vṛkṣera eka śākhā  
Śaṅkarāraṇya —  of the Ācārya’s  tree  one  branch  
mukunda, kāśīnātha, rudra, — upaśākhā lekhā  
Mukunda,  Kāśīnātha,  Rudra —  as offshoots  are written

(106) Śaṅkarāraṇya is one branch of the Ācārya’s tree, and Mukunda, Kāśīnātha and Rudra are recorded as its offshoots.[^47]

শ্রীনাথ পণ্ডিত — প্রভুর কৃপার ভাজন ৷  
যাঁর কৃষ্ণসেবা দেখি’ বশ ত্রিভুবন ॥ ১০৭ ॥

śrīnātha paṇḍita — prabhura kṛpāra bhājana  
Śrīnātha  Paṇḍita —  of the Lord’s  mercy  the recipient  
yām̐ra kṛṣṇa-sevā dekhi’ vaśa tri-bhuvana  
whose  service to Kṛṣṇa  seeing  captivated  the three worlds

(107) Śrīnātha Paṇḍita received the Lord’s mercy; seeing his service to Kṛṣṇa, the three worlds were captivated.[^48]

জগন্নাথ আচার্য্য প্রভুর প্রিয় দাস ৷  
প্রভুর আজ্ঞাতে তেঁহো কৈল গঙ্গাবাস ॥ ১০৮ ॥

jagannātha ācārya prabhura priya dāsa  
Jagannātha  Ācārya  of the Lord  dear  servant  
prabhura ājñāte tem̐ho kaila gaṅgā-vāsa  
by the Lord’s  order  he  made  a dwelling by the Gaṅgā

(108) Jagannātha Ācārya was a dear servant of the Lord; at the Lord’s command he made his home by the Gaṅgā.[^49]

কৃষ্ণদাস বৈদ্য, আর পণ্ডিত-শেখর ৷  
কবিচন্দ্র, আর কীর্ত্তনীয়া ষষ্ঠীবর ॥ ১০৯ ॥

kṛṣṇadāsa vaidya, āra paṇḍita-śekhara  
Kṛṣṇadāsa  Vaidya,  and  Paṇḍita  Śekhara  
kavicandra, āra kīrtanīyā ṣaṣṭhīvara  
Kavicandra,  and  the kīrtana singer  Ṣaṣṭhīvara

(109) Other branches are Kṛṣṇadāsa Vaidya, Paṇḍita Śekhara, Kavicandra and the kīrtana singer Ṣaṣṭhīvara;

শ্রীনাথ মিশ্র, শুভানন্দ, শ্রীরাম, ঈশান ৷  
শ্রীনিধি, শ্রীগোপীকান্ত, মিশ্র ভগবান্ ॥ ১১০ ॥

śrīnātha miśra, śubhānanda, śrīrāma, īśāna  
Śrīnātha  Miśra,  Śubhānanda,  Śrīrāma,  Īśāna  
śrīnidhi, śrīgopīkānta, miśra bhagavān  
Śrīnidhi,  Śrī Gopīkānta,  Miśra  Bhagavān

(110) Śrīnātha Miśra, Śubhānanda, Śrīrāma, Īśāna, Śrīnidhi, Śrī Gopīkānta, Bhagavān Miśra;

সুবুদ্ধি মিশ্র, হৃদয়ানন্দ, কমলনয়ন ৷  
মহেশ পণ্ডিত, শ্রীকর, শ্রীমধুসূদন ॥ ১১১ ॥

subuddhi miśra, hṛdayānanda, kamala-nayana  
Subuddhi  Miśra,  Hṛdayānanda,  Kamala-nayana  
maheśa paṇḍita, śrīkara, śrī-madhusūdana  
Maheśa  Paṇḍita,  Śrīkara,  Śrī  Madhusūdana

(111) Subuddhi Miśra, Hṛdayānanda, Kamala-nayana, Maheśa Paṇḍita, Śrīkara, Śrī Madhusūdana;

পুরুষোত্তম, শ্রীগালীম, জগন্নাথদাস ৷  
শ্রীচন্দ্রশেখর বৈদ্য, দ্বিজ হরিদাস ॥ ১১২ ॥

puruṣottama, śrī-gālīma, jagannātha-dāsa  
Puruṣottama,  Śrī  Gālīma,  Jagannātha dāsa  
śrī-candraśekhara vaidya, dvija haridāsa  
Śrī  Candraśekhara  Vaidya,  Dvija  Haridāsa

(112) Puruṣottama, Śrī Gālīma, Jagannātha dāsa, Śrī Candraśekhara Vaidya, Dvija Haridāsa;[^50]

রামদাস, কবিদত্ত, শ্রীগোপালদাস ৷  
ভাগবতাচার্য্য, ঠাকুর সারঙ্গদাস ॥ ১১৩ ॥

rāmadāsa, kavidatta, śrī-gopāladāsa  
Rāmadāsa,  Kavidatta,  Śrī  Gopāla dāsa  
bhāgavatācārya, ṭhākura sāraṅgadāsa  
Bhāgavatācārya,  Ṭhākura  Sāraṅga dāsa

(113) Rāmadāsa, Kavidatta, Śrī Gopāla dāsa, Bhāgavatācārya, Ṭhākura Sāraṅga dāsa;[^51]

জগন্নাথ তীর্থ, বিপ্র শ্রীজানকীনাথ ৷  
গোপাল আচার্য্য, আর বিপ্র বাণীনাথ ॥ ১১৪ ॥

jagannātha tīrtha, vipra śrī-jānakīnātha  
Jagannātha  Tīrtha,  the brāhmaṇa  Śrī  Jānakīnātha  
gopāla ācārya, āra vipra vāṇīnātha  
Gopāla  Ācārya,  and  the brāhmaṇa  Vāṇīnātha

(114) Jagannātha Tīrtha, the brāhmaṇa Śrī Jānakīnātha, Gopāla Ācārya and the brāhmaṇa Vāṇīnātha.

গোবিন্দ, মাধব, বাসুদেব, — তিন ভাই ৷  
যাঁ-সবার কীর্ত্তনে নাচে চৈতন্য-নিতাই ॥ ১১৫ ॥

govinda, mādhava, vāsudeva, — tina bhāi  
Govinda,  Mādhava,  Vāsudeva —  three  brothers  
yām̐-sabāra kīrtane nāce caitanya-nitāi  
of them all  in the kīrtana  dance  Caitanya and Nitāi

(115) Govinda, Mādhava and Vāsudeva were three brothers, and when they sang kīrtana, Caitanya and Nitāi danced.[^52]

রামদাস অভিরাম — সখ্য-প্রেমরাশি ৷  
ষোলসাঙ্গের কাষ্ঠ তুলি’ যে করিল বাঁশী ॥ ১১৬ ॥

rāmadāsa abhirāma — sakhya-premarāśi  
Rāmadāsa  Abhirāma —  of fraternal  love  a heap  
ṣolasāṅgera kāṣṭha tuli’ ye karila vām̐śī  
of sixteen bearers  the log  lifting  who  made  a flute

(116) Rāmadāsa Abhirāma was a mountain of fraternal love; he lifted a log that took sixteen men to carry and made it his flute.[^53]

প্রভুর আজ্ঞায় নিত্যানন্দ গৌড়ে চলিলা ৷  
তাঁর সঙ্গে তিনজন প্রভু-আজ্ঞায় আইলা ॥ ১১৭ ॥

prabhura ājñāya nityānanda gauḍe calilā  
by the Lord’s  order  Nityānanda  to Gauḍa  went  
tām̐ra saṅge tina-jana prabhu-ājñāya āilā  
with Him  three persons  by the Lord’s order  came

(117) At the Lord’s command Nityānanda went to Gauḍa, and by the Lord’s command three devotees went with Him:

শ্রীরামদাস, মাধব, আর বাসুদেব ঘোষ ৷  
প্রভু-সঙ্গে রহে গোবিন্দ পাইয়া সন্তোষ ॥ ১১৮ ॥

śrī rāmadāsa, mādhava, āra vāsudeva ghoṣa  
Śrī  Rāmadāsa,  Mādhava,  and  Vāsudeva  Ghoṣa  
prabhu-saṅge rahe govinda pāiyā santoṣa  
the Lord  with  stayed  Govinda  getting  joy

(118) Śrī Rāmadāsa, Mādhava and Vāsudeva Ghoṣa. Govinda stayed with the Lord, and was overjoyed to do so.[^54]

ভাগবতাচার্য্য, চিরঞ্জীব শ্রীরঘুনন্দন ৷  
শ্রীমাধবাচার্য্য, কমলাকান্ত, শ্রীযদুনন্দন ॥ ১১৯ ॥

bhāgavatācārya, cirañjīva śrī-raghunandana  
Bhāgavatācārya,  Ciranjīva  Śrī  Raghunandana  
śrī-mādhavācārya, kamalākānta, śrī-yadunandana  
Śrī Mādhavācārya,  Kamalākānta,  Śrī  Yadunandana

(119) Other branches are Bhāgavatācārya, Ciranjīva, Śrī Raghunandana, Śrī Mādhavācārya, Kamalākānta and Śrī Yadunandana.[^55]

মহা-কৃপাপাত্র প্রভুর জগাই, মাধাই ৷  
‘পতিতপাবন’ নামের সাক্ষী দুই ভাই ॥ ১২০ ॥

mahā-kṛpā-pātra prabhura jagāi, mādhāi  
of great  mercy  recipients  of the Lord  Jagāi,  Mādhāi  
‘patita-pāvana’ nāmera sākṣī dui bhāi  
‘deliverer of the fallen’  of the name  witnesses  the two  brothers

(120) Jagāi and Mādhāi received the Lord’s great mercy; these two brothers bear witness to His name “Deliverer of the Fallen.”[^56]

গৌড়দেশ-ভক্তের কৈল সংক্ষেপ কথন ৷  
অনন্ত চৈতন্যভক্ত না যায় গণন ॥ ১২১ ॥

gauḍa-deśa-bhaktera kaila saṅkṣepa kathana  
of the devotees of Gauḍa  made  brief  account  
ananta caitanya-bhakta nā yāya gaṇana  
endless  Caitanya’s devotees  cannot  be  counted

(121) I have given a brief account of the devotees of Gauḍa; Śrī Caitanya’s devotees are endless and cannot be counted.

নীলাচলে এই সব ভক্ত প্রভুসঙ্গে ৷  
দুই স্থানে প্রভু-সেবা কৈল নানা-রঙ্গে ॥ ১২২ ॥

nīlācale ei saba bhakta prabhu-saṅge  
in Nīlācala  these  all  devotees  with the Lord  
dui sthāne prabhu-sevā kaila nānā-raṅge  
in two  places  service to the Lord  did  in various ways

(122) All these devotees were also with the Lord in Nīlācala, and in both places they served Him in many ways.[^57]

কেবল নীলাচলে প্রভুর যে যে ভক্তগণ ৷  
সংক্ষেপে করিয়ে কিছু সে সব কথন ॥ ১২৩ ॥

kevala nīlācale prabhura ye ye bhakta-gaṇa  
only  in Nīlācala  of the Lord  who  who  devotees  
saṅkṣepe kariye kichu se saba kathana  
briefly  I make  something  about them  all  an account

(123) Now I shall speak briefly of the Lord’s devotees who were with Him only in Nīlācala.

[^5]: Heading in the edition: “Their undivided devotion to Gaura.”

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya*: in Candraśekhara’s house a sacred drama was performed in which Mahāprabhu danced as the Goddess (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 18); the place is now known as Vraja-pattana. Candraśekhara was with Mahāprabhu in Kāṭoyā at His sannyāsa and, returning to Navadvīpa, told everyone of it. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: according to some books, Candraśekhara was the husband of the sister of Mahāprabhu’s mother.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*): Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi was Vṛṣabhānu, the father of Śrī Rādhā; Mahāprabhu called him “Father” and gave him the name Premanidhi. A disciple of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, he was the guru of Gadādhara Paṇḍita and the friend of Svarūpa Dāmodara; his home was in Cāṭigrām.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*): Gadādhara Paṇḍita is Śrī Rādhā, the queen of Vṛndāvana; Svarūpa Dāmodara saw in him the Lakṣmī of Vraja. His branch is described at the end of Ādi 12.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: in Navadvīpa he joined the Lord’s kīrtanas at Śrīvāsa’s house and in the home of Candraśekhara; after the Lord’s sannyāsa, on the way to Orissa, he carried His staff and begged alms for Him.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: Rāghava Paṇḍita’s home was in Pānihāṭi on the bank of the Gaṅgā; his samādhi is there, and the Deity Śrī Madana-mohana is still served nearby. Makaradhvaja Kara also lived in Pānihāṭi.

[^11]: The bags are described in Antya 10.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya* (from the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5): when the Lord came to Pānihāṭi, Purandara Ācārya hurried to Him; the Lord, calling him Father, embraced him in ecstatic love, and seeing the Lord, he wept uncontrollably.

[^13]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the rebuke is described in Antya 3. *Anubhāṣya*: at the Lord’s wish Dāmodara Paṇḍita went to Gauḍa to be with Mother Śacī, and for the Ratha-yātrā he would come to Purī again with the devotees (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 9).

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: in Navadvīpa Mahāprabhu would joyfully snatch and eat the rice that Śuklāmbara had gathered by begging (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 16 and 25).

[^15]: His prayer is described in Madhya 15.159–180 (*Anubhāṣya*).

[^16]: The *śrāddha-pātra* is offered to the most worthy brāhmaṇa; Advaita Ācārya offered it to Haridāsa, who was born in a Muslim family (Antya 3).

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: Satyarāja Khān was the son of Guṇarāja Khān of Kulīna-grāma and the father of Rāmānanda Vasu. Haridāsa Ṭhākura spent a cāturmāsya in Kulīna-grāma and blessed the whole Vasu family. Mahāprabhu asked Satyarāja to bring silk ropes for Jagannātha every year and explained to him who is a Vaiṣṇava, a better Vaiṣṇava and the best of Vaiṣṇavas (Madhya 15–16).

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: Murāri Gupta, the author of the *Śrī Caitanya-carita*, was born of a physician family of Śrīhaṭṭa and later lived in Navadvīpa; he was older than Mahāprabhu. In his house the Lord showed His form as Varāha, and to him He revealed Himself as Śrī Rāma.

[^19]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *ātma-vṛtti* — the calling proper to one’s own class: Murāri Gupta was a physician (kavirāja).

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: Gadādhara dāsa lived at Eṛiyādaha on the Gaṅgā and was one of Nityānanda’s chief helpers in preaching in Gauḍa. The local Kazi opposed the kīrtana; one night Gadādhara came to him singing and asked him to say the name of Hari. The Kazi replied, “I will say ‘Hari’ tomorrow,” and Gadādhara, full of love, cried out, “Why tomorrow? You have just said ‘Hari’!” He is the radiance of Śrī Rādhā and is counted among the associates of both Gaura and Nityānanda.

[^21]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śivānanda Sena lived in Kumārahaṭṭa (Hālisahar); every year he guided the devotees of Gauḍa to Nīlācala and bore all the costs of the journey (Madhya 16.19–26).

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: according to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (74) it is the other way round: the Lord’s *āvirbhāva* was in Nakula Brahmacārī and His *āveśa* in Pradyumna Miśra. The Lord always manifests Himself (*āvirbhāva*) in four places: in Śacī’s house, in Nityānanda’s dancing, in Śrīvāsa’s kīrtana and in Rāghava’s house (Antya 2.34–35).

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śivānanda Sena experienced and tasted all three kinds of the Lord’s mercy — *sākṣāt*, *āveśa* and *āvirbhāva* (Antya 2).

[^24]: *Anubhāṣya*: Caitanya dāsa, the eldest, wrote a commentary on the *Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta*. Karṇapūra, also called Paramānanda dāsa or Purī dāsa, a disciple of Śrīnātha Paṇḍita, wrote the *Ānanda-vṛndāvana-campū*, the *Alaṅkāra-kaustubha*, the drama *Śrī Caitanya-candrodaya*, the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* and other works.

[^25]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrīvallabha and Śrīkānta were the sons of Śivānanda’s sister. When on the way to Purī Nityānanda scolded Śivānanda and pushed him with His foot, Śrīkānta took offence, left the party and reached the Lord first; the all-knowing Lord consoled him and told Govinda to give him His remnants while he stayed in Nīlācala (Antya 12). At the Ratha-yātrā both brothers sang in Mukunda’s party (Madhya 13).

[^26]: *Anubhāṣya*: in the kīrtanas of Navadvīpa Govinda Datta was the lead singer (*mūla-gāyaka*); his home was at Sukhacara near Khaḍadaha.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: Vijaya dāsa, a scribe of Navadvīpa, was one of the *nava-nidhi* (nine treasures); in Śuklāmbara’s house the Lord showed him special mercy (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 25).

[^28]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrīdhara was a poor brāhmaṇa of Navadvīpa who lived by his banana grove; the Lord bargained and quarrelled with him playfully over his goods, showed him mercy during the *sāta-prahariyā* manifestation, drank water from his old leaking iron pot at the time of the Kazi’s subjugation, and on the night before His sannyāsa ate the gourd Śrīdhara had brought. According to Karṇapūra he is Kusumāsava, one of the twelve cowherd boys.

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya*: the story is told in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 4. According to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, in Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes they were the wives of the sacrificing brāhmaṇas (*yajña-patnīs*), and now the Lord begged for their food and ate it on Ekādaśī.

[^30]: *Anubhāṣya*: from the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* one might take “Puruṣottama Sañjaya” for one person, the son of Mukunda Sañjaya, in whose house Nimāi taught His pupils; by calling Puruṣottama and Sañjaya two students and two great souls, Kavirāja Gosvāmī corrects that understanding.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: Buddhimanta Khān, a wealthy devotee of Navadvīpa, bore all the expenses of the Lord’s second marriage, to Viṣṇupriyā, the daughter of Sanātana Miśra, and gathered the clothes and ornaments for the drama in Candraśekhara’s house.

[^32]: In the edition the heading is “(5a) the branch of Devānanda Paṇḍita,” that is, an offshoot of Vakreśvara’s branch (5). *Anubhāṣya*: Devānanda, who sought liberation, used to recite the *Bhāgavata*; once his students drove out Śrīvāsa, who was weeping at the reading, and Mahāprabhu sternly rebuked Devānanda for his disrespect to a Vaiṣṇava. Later Vakreśvara Paṇḍita stayed in his house and sang of Kṛṣṇa; by his mercy Devānanda came to understand the Lord’s glory, and the Lord told him to explain the *Bhāgavata* in the light of bhakti (Madhya 1: the mercy at Kuliyā). In Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes he is Bhāguri Muni, the paṇḍita of Nanda’s court.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: Mukunda dāsa was the elder brother of Narahari Sarakāra Ṭhākura, and Raghunandana was Mukunda’s son; Raghunandana’s descendants still live in Śrīkhaṇḍa, four miles west of Kāṭoyā. Narahari’s disciple was Locana dāsa Ṭhākura, the author of the *Caitanya-maṅgala*. The sons of Ciranjīva were Rāmacandra Kavirāja, a disciple of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya, and the poet Govinda dāsa Kavirāja. Mukunda’s wonderful love for Kṛṣṇa is described in Madhya 15.113–131.

[^34]: In the edition they are offshoots of Haridāsa’s branch (20a–20e), like all the people of Kulīna-grāma (10.48). *Anubhāṣya*: all of them belonged to the Vasu family, devotees of Kṛṣṇa skilled in enacting His pastimes and followers of Haridāsa Ṭhākura; in the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* Rāmānanda Vasu and Satyarāja are Kalakaṇṭhī and Sukaṇṭhī.

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: Anupama was the younger brother of Śrī Sanātana and Śrī Rūpa and the father of Śrī Jīva; his earlier name was Vallabha, and Mahāprabhu gave him the name Anupama. He worshipped Śrī Rāma and saw Mahāprabhu as Śrī Rāmacandra Himself. In Śaka 1436, on the way to Nīlācala, he left his body on the bank of the Gaṅgā.

[^36]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Jīva, the son of Śrī Vallabha, studied all the scriptures in Kāśī under Madhusūdana Vācaspati and then took shelter of Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana in Vṛndāvana; after their departure he was the foremost ācārya of the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas and sent Śrīnivāsa, Narottama and Śyāmānanda to Gauḍa with the books of the Gosvāmīs.

[^37]: Heading in the edition: “The deliverance of all Bhārata.”

[^38]: Headings in the edition to 10.89–90: “(1) establishing the conduct of bhakti,” “(2) recovering the lost holy places and (3) preaching the worship of the Deities.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: through contact with the Muslims the people of the western regions had lost proper conduct; by the mercy of Rūpa and Sanātana they turned to righteous ways.

[^39]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the lost holy places are Rādhā-kuṇḍa and others; the Deities are Śrī Madana-mohana, Śrī Govinda, Śrī Gopīnātha and others — seven in all.

[^40]: *Anubhāṣya*: Raghunātha dāsa was born about Śaka 1416 in the village of Kṛṣṇapura near Saptagrāma, in the house of Govardhana, the younger brother of Hiraṇya Majumadāra, in a Kāyastha family. According to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* he is Rasa-mañjarī (others say Rati-mañjarī or Bhānumatī).

[^41]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: confidential service is that to which outsiders have no access — all that the Lord needs at the time of His bath, His meals, His rest and His kīrtana; in 10.93 it is called intimate service (*antaraṅga-sevā*).

[^42]: Heading in the edition: “After the departure of Śrī Gaura and Svarūpa — to Vṛndāvana.”

[^43]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: service in the mind — meditation on the service of the eight periods of the day (*aṣṭa-kālīya*), together with chanting the holy name.

[^44]: Heading in the edition: “The author regards himself as the eternal servant of Rūpa and Raghunātha.” *Anubhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇadāsa Kavirāja Gosvāmī called Śrī Raghunātha dāsa and Śrī Rūpa “my master” (*prabhu*), and that is why at the end of every chapter he writes, “Hoping for the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha…”; the opinion that Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa is meant here and was Kavirāja’s guru has no evidence.

[^45]: Raghunātha’s meeting with the Lord is told in Antya 6 (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*).

[^46]: *Anubhāṣya*: Gopāla Bhaṭṭa, a brāhmaṇa from the south, was a friend of Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana; in his name Sanātana compiled the *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*; he made the first draft of the *Ṣaṭ-sandarbha*, which Śrī Jīva later put in order, and he installed the Deity of Śrī Rādhā-ramaṇa. His disciples were Śrīnivāsa Ācārya and Gopīnātha Pūjārī. He forbade Kavirāja Gosvāmī to write about him in this book, and so only his name is given here.

[^47]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śaṅkarāraṇya is Viśvarūpa, the elder brother of Mahāprabhu (according to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, Saṅkarṣaṇa); he took sannyāsa and left this world in Śaka 1432 at Pāṇḍarapura (Madhya 9.299–300). Mukunda is Mukunda Sañjaya, in whose house Viśvambhara taught; Kāśīnātha is the brāhmaṇa who arranged the Lord’s marriage with Viṣṇupriyā; Rudra is Rudra Paṇḍita.

[^48]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrīnātha Paṇḍita lived in Kāñcaḍāpāḍā and installed the Deity Śrī Kṛṣṇa-rāya there; he was the guru of Kavi Karṇapūra.

[^49]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the bank of the Alakanandā within Navadvīpa he founded a village called Gaṅgā-vāsa.

[^50]: *Anubhāṣya*: Candraśekhara Vaidya, a scribe by profession, is the one in whose house the Lord stayed in Kāśī (10.152–154).

[^51]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Bhāgavatācārya lived in Varāhanagara, where his hermitage is still called “Bhāgavatācārya’s seat”; Ṭhākura Sāraṅga dāsa was from Māmagāchi. The heading in the edition has “Śārṅga Ṭhākura.” *Anubhāṣya*: Bhāgavatācārya’s name was Raghunātha; the Lord gave him that title on hearing him recite the *Bhāgavatam* (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5).

[^52]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Ghoṣa brothers — Govinda, Mādhava and Vāsudeva — came of a Kāyastha family of northern Rāḍha; Mādhava Ghoṣa was a matchless kīrtana singer, very dear to Nityānanda. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Govinda installed the Deity of Gopīnātha at Agradvīpa.

[^53]: In the edition the heading is “(84) Ṭhākura Abhirāma.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Abhirāma lived at Khānākula-Kṛṣṇanagara.

[^54]: Heading in the edition to 10.117: “Abhirāma, Mādhava and Vāsu Ghoṣa preach the holy name in Gauḍa with Nitāi.”

[^55]: In the edition’s heading Bhāgavatācārya, Ciranjīva and Raghunandana are marked with their earlier numbers (75, 41, 39a) — they are the same persons named above; the new branches are (85) Mādhavācārya, (86) Kamalākānta and (87) Yadunandana. *Anubhāṣya*: Kamalākānta is Kamalākānta Viśvāsa of Advaita’s party; Yadunandana Ācārya belongs to Advaita’s branch (Antya 6.160–169).

[^56]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*): Jagāi and Mādhāi are Jaya and Vijaya, the gatekeepers of Vaikuṇṭha, born of their own will in Navadvīpa. Brāhmaṇas by birth, they lived as robbers and were steeped in every sin, but by the mercy of Mahāprabhu and Nityānanda they received the holy name and became great devotees.

[^57]: Heading in the edition: “Their service to Gaura both in Gauḍa and in Oḍra.”
