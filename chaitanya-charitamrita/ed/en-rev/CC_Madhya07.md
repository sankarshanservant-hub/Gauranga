“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 7  
**The Journey to the South: The Deliverance of Vāsudeva**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

ধন্যং তং নৌমি চৈতন্যং বাসুদেবং দয়ার্দ্দ্রধীঃ ৷  
নষ্টকুষ্ঠং রূপপুষ্টং ভক্তিতুষ্টং চকার যঃ ॥ ১ ॥

dhanyaṁ taṁ naumi caitanyaṁ  
blessed  that  I praise  Caitanya  
vāsudevaṁ dayārdra-dhīḥ  
Vāsudeva  His mind melting with compassion  
naṣṭa-kuṣṭhaṁ rūpa-puṣṭaṁ  
cured of leprosy  filled with beauty  
bhakti-tuṣṭaṁ cakāra yaḥ  
satisfied in devotion  made  who

(1) I praise that blessed Caitanya who, His heart melting with compassion, cured Vāsudeva of leprosy, filled him with beauty and made him content in devotion.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

এইমতে সার্ব্বভৌমের নিস্তার করিল ৷  
দক্ষিণ-গমনে প্রভুর ইচ্ছা উপজিল ॥ ৩ ॥

ei-mate sārvabhaumera nistāra karila  
in this way  of Sārvabhauma  the deliverance  He accomplished  
dakṣiṇa-gamane prabhura icchā upajila  
for going south  of the Lord  the desire  arose

(3) Having thus delivered Sārvabhauma, the Lord felt the desire to go to the south.

মাঘ-শুক্লপক্ষে প্রভু করিল সন্ন্যাস ৷  
ফাল্গুনে আসিয়া কৈল নীলাচলে বাস ॥ ৪ ॥

māgha-śukla-pakṣe prabhu karila sannyāsa  
in the bright fortnight of Māgha  the Lord  took  sannyāsa  
phālgune āsiyā kaila nīlācale vāsa  
in Phālguna  coming  made  in Nīlācala  His residence

(4) The Lord accepted sannyāsa in the bright fortnight of Māgha, and in Phālguna He came to live in Nīlācala.

ফাল্গুনের শেষে দোলযাত্রা সে দেখিল ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে বহুবিধ নৃত্যগীত কৈল ॥ ৫ ॥

phālgunera śeṣe dola-yātrā se dekhila  
of Phālguna  at the end  the Dola-yātrā  He  saw  
premāveśe bahu-vidha nṛtya-gīta kaila  
absorbed in love  many kinds of  dancing and singing  He did

(5) At the end of Phālguna He saw the Dola-yātrā festival and, absorbed in love, danced and sang in many ways.

চৈত্রে রহি’ কৈল সার্ব্বভৌম-বিমোচন ৷  
বৈশাখের প্রথমে দক্ষিণ যাইতে হৈল মন ॥ ৬ ॥

caitre rahi’ kaila sārvabhauma-vimocana  
in Caitra  staying  He made  Sārvabhauma’s deliverance  
vaiśākhera prathame dakṣiṇa yāite haila mana  
of Vaiśākha  at the beginning  south  to go  arose  the wish

(6) Staying on through Caitra, He delivered Sārvabhauma; and at the beginning of Vaiśākha He resolved to go south.

নিজগণ আনি’ কহে বিনয় করিয়া ৷  
আলিঙ্গন করি’ সবায় শ্রীহস্তে ধরিয়া ॥ ৭ ॥

nija-gaṇa āni’ kahe vinaya kariyā  
His own associates  bringing  He speaks  humility  showing  
āliṅgana kari’ sabāya śrī-haste dhariyā  
embrace  making  all  with His divine hands  holding

(7) He called His associates together, embraced them all, took them by the hand and humbly spoke to them.

তোমা-সবা জানি আমি প্রাণাধিক করি’ ৷  
প্রাণ ছাড়া যায়, তোমা-সবা ছাড়িতে না পারি ॥ ৮ ॥

tomā-sabā jāni āmi prāṇādhika kari’  
you all  know  I  dearer than life  as  
prāṇa chāḍā yāya, tomā-sabā chāḍite nā pāri  
life  leaving  may go  you all  to leave  not  I can

(8) “I know all of you to be dearer to Me than life itself. My life may leave Me, but I cannot leave you.

তুমি-সব বন্ধু মোর বন্ধুকৃত্য কৈলে ৷  
ইঁহা আনি’ মোরে জগন্নাথ দেখাইলে ॥ ৯ ॥

tumi-saba bandhu mora bandhu-kṛtya kaile  
you all  friends  My  a friend’s duty  you have done  
im̐hā āni’ more jagannātha dekhāile  
here  bringing  to Me  Jagannātha  you have shown

(9) You are My friends, and you have done a friend’s duty: you brought Me here and showed Me Jagannātha.

এবে সবা-স্থানে মুঞি মাগোঁ এক দানে ৷  
সবে মেলি’ আজ্ঞা দেহ, যাইব দক্ষিণে ॥ ১০ ॥

ebe sabā-sthāne muñi māgom̐ eka dāne  
now  from all  I  beg  one  gift  
sabe meli’ ājñā deha, yāiba dakṣiṇe  
all  together  permission  give  I shall go  south

(10) Now I beg one gift from all of you: give Me your permission together, and I shall go south.

বিশ্বরূপ-উদ্দেশে অবশ্য আমি যাব ৷  
একাকী যাইব, কাহো সঙ্গে না লইব ॥ ১১ ॥

viśvarūpa-uddeśe avaśya āmi yāba  
in search of Viśvarūpa  certainly  I  shall go  
ekākī yāiba, kāho saṅge nā laiba  
alone  I shall go  anyone  with (Me)  not  I shall take

(11) I must certainly go in search of Viśvarūpa. I shall go alone and take no one with Me.[^3]

সেতুবন্ধ হৈতে আমি না আসি যাবৎ ৷  
নীলাচলে তুমি-সব রহিবে তাবৎ ॥ ১২ ॥

setubandha haite āmi nā āsi yāvat  
Setubandha  from  I  not  return  until  
nīlācale tumi-saba rahibe tāvat  
in Nīlācala  you all  will stay  so long

(12) Until I return from Setubandha, all of you should stay here in Nīlācala.”

বিশ্বরূপ-সিদ্ধি-প্রাপ্তি জানেন সকল ৷  
দক্ষিণ-দেশ উদ্ধারিতে করেন এই ছল ॥ ১৩ ॥

viśvarūpa-siddhi-prāpti jānena sakala  
Viśvarūpa’s  perfection  attainment  He knows  all  
dakṣiṇa-deśa uddhārite karena ei chala  
the southern  country  to deliver  He makes  this  pretext

(13) The all-knowing Lord knew that Viśvarūpa had already attained perfection; He made this pretext only to deliver the southern country.[^4]

শুনিয়া সবার মনে হৈল মহাদুঃখ ৷  
নিঃশব্দ হইলা সবে, শুকাইল মুখ ॥ ১৪ ॥

śuniyā sabāra mane haila mahā-duḥkha  
hearing  of all  in the minds  arose  great sorrow  
niḥśabda hailā sabe, śukāila mukha  
silent  became  all  withered  the faces

(14) Hearing this, everyone was stricken with great sorrow. All fell silent, and their faces withered.

নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু কহে, — ঐছে কৈছে হয় ৷  
একাকী যাইবে তুমি, কে ইহা সহয় ॥ ১৫ ॥

nityānanda-prabhu kahe, — aiche kaiche haya  
Nityānanda Prabhu  says  so  how  can it be  
ekākī yāibe tumi, ke ihā sahaya  
alone  will go  You  who  this  can bear

(15) Nityānanda Prabhu said, “How can this be? You will go alone — who could bear it?

দুই-এক সঙ্গে চলুক্, না পড় হঠ-রঙ্গে ৷  
যারে কহ, সেই-দুই চলুক্ তোমার সঙ্গে ॥ ১৬ ॥

dui-eka saṅge caluk, nā paḍa haṭha-raṅge  
one or two  with (You)  let go  not  fall  into the hands of rogues  
yāre kaha, sei-dui caluk tomāra saṅge  
whom  You say  those two  let go  You  with

(16) Let one or two go with You, so that You do not fall into the hands of rogues — any two You choose.[^5]

দক্ষিণের তীর্থপথ আমি সব জানি ৷  
আমি সঙ্গে যাই, প্রভু, আজ্ঞা দেহ তুমি ॥ ১৭ ॥

dakṣiṇera tīrtha-patha āmi saba jāni  
of the south  the routes to the holy places  I  all  know  
āmi saṅge yāi, prabhu, ājñā deha tumi  
I  with (You)  go  Prabhu  permission  give  You

(17) I know all the routes to the holy places of the south. Let Me go with You, Prabhu — give Me Your permission.”

প্রভু কহে, আমি — নর্ত্তক, তুমি — সূত্রধার ৷  
তুমি যৈছে নাচাও, তৈছে নর্ত্তন আমার ॥ ১৮ ॥

prabhu kahe, āmi — nartaka, tumi — sūtra-dhāra  
the Lord  says  I  a dancer  You  the puppeteer  
tumi yaiche nācāo, taiche nartana āmāra  
You  as  make dance  so  the dancing  My

(18) The Lord replied, “I am a dancer and You are the puppeteer. As You make Me dance, so I dance.[^6]

সন্ন্যাস করিয়া আমি চলিলাঙ বৃন্দাবন ৷  
তুমি আমা লঞা আইলে অদ্বৈত-ভবন ॥ ১৯ ॥

sannyāsa kariyā āmi calilāṅa vṛndāvana  
sannyāsa  having taken  I  set out  for Vṛndāvana  
tumi āmā lañā āile advaita-bhavana  
You  Me  taking  brought  to Advaita’s house

(19) After taking sannyāsa I set out for Vṛndāvana, but You led Me instead to Advaita’s house.

নীলাচল আসিতে পথে ভাঙ্গিলা মোর দণ্ড ৷  
তোমা-সবার গাঢ়-স্নেহে আমার কার্য্য-ভঙ্গ ॥ ২০ ॥

nīlācala āsite pathe bhāṅgilā mora daṇḍa  
to Nīlācala  coming  on the way  You broke  My  daṇḍa  
tomā-sabāra gāḍha-snehe āmāra kārya-bhaṅga  
of you all  by the deep affection  My  work  is hindered

(20) On the way to Nīlācala You broke My daṇḍa. The deep affection all of you bear for Me hinders My work.

জগদানন্দ চাহে আমা বিষয় ভুঞ্জাইতে ৷  
যেই কহে, সেই ভয়ে চাহিয়ে করিতে ॥ ২১ ॥

jagadānanda cāhe āmā viṣaya bhuñjāite  
Jagadānanda  wants  Me  sense objects  to make enjoy  
yei kahe, sei bhaye cāhiye karite  
whatever  he says  that  out of fear  I try  to do

(21) Jagadānanda wants Me to enjoy worldly comforts, and whatever he says, I try to do out of fear.

কভু যদি ইঁহার বাক্য করিয়ে অন্যথা ৷  
ক্রোধে তিন দিন মোরে নাহি কহে কথা ॥ ২২ ॥

kabhu yadi im̐hāra vākya kariye anyathā  
ever  if  his  word  I make  otherwise  
krodhe tina dina more nāhi kahe kathā  
in anger  three  days  to Me  not  he speaks  a word

(22) If ever I go against his word, he is angry with Me and does not speak to Me for three days.

মুকুন্দ হয়েন দুঃখী দেখি’ সন্ন্যাস-ধর্ম্ম ৷  
তিনবারে শীতে স্নান, ভূমিতে শয়ন ॥ ২৩ ॥

mukunda hayena duḥkhī dekhi’ sannyāsa-dharma  
Mukunda  becomes  unhappy  seeing  the duties of sannyāsa  
tinabāre śīte snāna, bhūmite śayana  
three times  in the cold  bathing  on the ground  lying

(23) Mukunda is unhappy to see the duties of My sannyāsa: bathing three times a day even in the cold, and sleeping on the bare ground.

অন্তরে দুঃখী মুকুন্দ, নাহি কহে মুখে ৷  
ইহার দুঃখ দেখি’ মোর দ্বিগুণ হয়ে দুঃখে ॥ ২৪ ॥

antare duḥkhī mukunda, nāhi kahe mukhe  
within  unhappy  Mukunda  not  he says  with his mouth  
ihāra duḥkha dekhi’ mora dvi-guṇa haye duḥkhe  
his  unhappiness  seeing  My  twofold  becomes  unhappiness

(24) Mukunda grieves within but says nothing; and seeing his grief, I grieve twice as much.

আমি ত’ — সন্ন্যাসী, দামোদর — ব্রহ্মচারী ৷  
সদা রহে আমার উপর শিক্ষা-দণ্ড ধরি’ ॥ ২৫ ॥

āmi ta’ — sannyāsī, dāmodara — brahmacārī  
I  indeed  a sannyāsī  Dāmodara  a brahmacārī  
sadā rahe āmāra upara śikṣā-daṇḍa dhari’  
always  he stays  Me  over  the rod of instruction  holding

(25) I am a sannyāsī and Dāmodara a brahmacārī, yet he always stands over Me with the rod of instruction.[^7]

ইঁহার আগে আমি না জানি ব্যবহার ৷  
ইঁহারে না ভায় স্বতন্ত্র চরিত্র আমার ॥ ২৬ ॥

im̐hāra āge āmi nā jāni vyavahāra  
him  before  I  not  know  proper conduct  
im̐hāre nā bhāya svatantra caritra āmāra  
to him  not  is pleasing  independent  nature  My

(26) In his eyes I know nothing of proper conduct, and My independent ways do not please him.

লোকাপেক্ষা নাহি ইঁহার কৃষ্ণকৃপা হৈতে ৷  
আমি লোকাপেক্ষা কভু না পারি ছাড়িতে ॥ ২৭ ॥

lokāpekṣā nāhi im̐hāra kṛṣṇa-kṛpā haite  
regard for people  not  his  Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  by  
āmi lokāpekṣā kabhu nā pāri chāḍite  
I  regard for people  ever  not  can  give up

(27) By Kṛṣṇa’s mercy he cares nothing for what people think; but I can never give up regard for what people think.

অতএব তুমি-সব রহ নীলাচলে ৷  
দিনকত আমি তীর্থ ভ্রমিব একলে ॥ ২৮ ॥

ataeva tumi-saba raha nīlācale  
therefore  you all  stay  in Nīlācala  
dinakata āmi tīrtha bhramiba ekale  
days  some  I  holy places  shall wander  alone

(28) Therefore all of you stay in Nīlācala, while for some time I wander alone to the holy places.”

ইঁহা-সবার বশ প্রভু হয়ে যে যে-গুণে ৷  
দোষারোপ-ছলে করে গুণ আস্বাদনে ॥ ২৯ ॥

im̐hā-sabāra vaśa prabhu haye ye ye-guṇe  
of these all  controlled  the Lord  becomes  by which  qualities  
doṣāropa-chale kare guṇa āsvādane  
of fault-finding  under the pretext  He does  of qualities  relishing

(29) The Lord is controlled by the very qualities of these devotees, and under the pretext of finding fault with them He relished their virtues.[^8]

চৈতন্যের ভক্ত-বাৎসল্য — অকথ্য-কথন ৷  
আপনে বৈরাগ্য-দুঃখ করেন সহন ॥ ৩০ ॥

caitanyera bhakta-vātsalya — akathya-kathana  
Caitanya’s  affection for His devotees  indescribable  
āpane vairāgya-duḥkha karena sahana  
Himself  the hardships of renunciation  He does  endure

(30) Caitanya’s affection for His devotees is beyond words. He Himself endures the hardships of renunciation.

সেই দুঃখ দেখি’ যেই ভক্ত দুঃখ পায় ৷  
সেই দুঃখ তাঁর শক্ত্যে সহন না যায় ॥ ৩১ ॥

sei duḥkha dekhi’ yei bhakta duḥkha pāya  
that  hardship  seeing  which  devotee  pain  feels  
sei duḥkha tām̐ra śaktye sahana nā yāya  
that  pain  His  by power  to bear  not  is possible

(31) But when a devotee is pained to see those hardships, that pain He has no power to bear.

গুণ দোষোদগার ছলে সবা নিষেধিয়া ৷  
একাকী ভ্রমিবেন তীর্থ বৈরাগ্য করিয়া ॥ ৩২ ॥

guṇa doṣodagāra chale sabā niṣedhiyā  
virtues  speaking of faults  under the pretext  all  forbidding  
ekākī bhramibena tīrtha vairāgya kariyā  
alone  He will wander  holy places  renunciation  practising

(32) So, under the pretext of declaring their virtues to be faults, He forbade them all, intending to wander alone to the holy places in strict renunciation.

তবে চারিজন বহু মিনতি করিল ৷  
স্বতন্ত্র ঈশ্বর প্রভু কভু না মানিল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

tabe cārijana bahu minati karila  
then  the four persons  much  pleading  made  
svatantra īśvara prabhu kabhu nā mānila  
independent  Lord  the Lord  at all  not  accepted

(33) Then the four devotees pleaded with Him at length, but the Lord, independent and supreme, would not consent.

তবে নিত্যানন্দ কহে, — যে আজ্ঞা তোমার ৷  
দুঃখ সুখ যে হউক্ কর্ত্তব্য আমার ॥ ৩৪ ॥

tabe nityānanda kahe, — ye ājñā tomāra  
then  Nityānanda  says  whatever  order  Your  
duḥkha sukha ye hauk kartavya āmāra  
sorrow  happiness  whatever  may be  duty  My

(34) Then Nityānanda said, “Whatever You order is My duty, whether it brings sorrow or happiness.

কিন্তু এক নিবেদন করোঁ আর বার ৷  
বিচার করিয়া তাহা কর অঙ্গীকার ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kintu eka nivedana karom̐ āra bāra  
but  one  request  I make  once  more  
vicāra kariyā tāhā kara aṅgīkāra  
consideration  making  it  make  acceptance

(35) But I make one more request: consider it, and then accept it.

কৌপীন, বহির্ব্বাস আর জলপাত্র ৷  
আর কিছু নাহি যাবে, সবে এই মাত্র ॥ ৩৬ ॥

kaupīna, bahir-vāsa āra jala-pātra  
kaupīna  outer cloth  and  water pot  
āra kichu nāhi yābe, sabe ei mātra  
other  anything  not  will go  all  this  only

(36) A kaupīna, an outer cloth and a water pot — nothing else will go with You; that is all.

তোমার দুই হস্ত বদ্ধ নাম-গণনে ৷  
জলপাত্র বহির্ব্বাস বহিবে কেমনে ॥ ৩৭ ॥

tomāra dui hasta baddha nāma-gaṇane  
Your  two  hands  bound  in counting the names  
jala-pātra bahirvāsa vahibe kemane  
water pot  outer cloth  You will carry  how

(37) Both Your hands are occupied in counting the holy names. How will You carry the water pot and the outer cloth?

প্রেমাবেশে পথে তুমি হবে অচেতন ৷  
এ-সব সামগ্রী তোমার কে করে রক্ষণ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

premāveśe pathe tumi habe acetana  
absorbed in love  on the way  You  will be  unconscious  
e-saba sāmagrī tomāra ke kare rakṣaṇa  
these all  things  Your  who  will make  safekeeping

(38) On the way You will fall unconscious in the ecstasy of love. Who will look after these things of Yours then?

‘কৃষ্ণদাস’-নামে এই সরল ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
ইঁহো সঙ্গে করি’ লহ, ধর নিবেদন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

‘kṛṣṇadāsa’-nāme ei sarala brāhmaṇa  
‘Kṛṣṇadāsa’  by name  this  simple  brāhmaṇa  
im̐ho saṅge kari’ laha, dhara nivedana  
him  with (You)  making  take  accept  the request

(39) Here is a simple brāhmaṇa named Kṛṣṇadāsa. Please take him with You — grant My request.[^9]

জলপাত্র-বস্ত্র বহি’ তোমা-সঙ্গে যাবে ৷  
যে তোমার ইচ্ছা, কর, কিছু না বলিবে ॥ ৪০ ॥

jala-pātra-vastra vahi’ tomā-saṅge yābe  
the water pot and cloth  carrying  with You  he will go  
ye tomāra icchā, kara, kichu nā balibe  
whatever  Your  wish  do  anything  not  he will say

(40) He will go with You and carry the water pot and the cloth. Do whatever You wish; he will say nothing.”

তবে তাঁর বাক্য প্রভু করি’ অঙ্গীকারে ৷  
তাহা-সবা লঞা গেলা সার্ব্বভৌম-ঘরে ॥ ৪১ ॥

tabe tām̐ra vākya prabhu kari’ aṅgīkāre  
then  His  words  the Lord  making  acceptance  
tāhā-sabā lañā gelā sārvabhauma-ghare  
them all  taking  went  to Sārvabhauma’s house

(41) The Lord then accepted His words and, taking them all along, went to Sārvabhauma’s house.

নমস্করি’ সার্ব্বভৌম আসন নিবেদিল ৷  
সবাকারে মিলি’ তবে আসনে বসিল ॥ ৪২ ॥

namaskari’ sārvabhauma āsana nivedila  
bowing  Sārvabhauma  seats  offered  
sabākāre mili’ tabe āsane vasila  
all  meeting  then  on a seat  sat

(42) Sārvabhauma bowed down and offered them seats; then, after greeting everyone, he sat down.

নানা কৃষ্ণবার্ত্তা প্রভু কহিল তাঁহারে ৷  
তোমার ঠাঞি আইলাঙ আজ্ঞা মাগিবারে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

nānā kṛṣṇa-vārtā prabhu kahila tām̐hāre  
various  talks of Kṛṣṇa  the Lord  spoke  to him  
tomāra ṭhāñi āilāṅa ājñā māgibāre  
to you  I have come  permission  to ask

(43) The Lord spoke with him of Kṛṣṇa in many ways and then said, “I have come to you to ask your permission.

সন্ন্যাস করি’ বিশ্বরূপ গিয়াছে দক্ষিণে ৷  
অবশ্য করিব আমি তাঁর অন্বেষণে ॥ ৪৪ ॥

sannyāsa kari’ viśvarūpa giyāche dakṣiṇe  
sannyāsa  having taken  Viśvarūpa  has gone  south  
avaśya kariba āmi tām̐ra anveṣaṇe  
certainly  I shall make  I  his  search

(44) Viśvarūpa took sannyāsa and went to the south, and I must go in search of him.

আজ্ঞা দেহ, অবশ্য আমি দক্ষিণে চলিব ৷  
তোমার আজ্ঞাতে শুভে লেউটি’ আসিব ॥ ৪৫ ॥

ājñā deha, avaśya āmi dakṣiṇe caliba  
permission  give  certainly  I  south  shall go  
tomāra ājñāte śubhe leuṭi’ āsiba  
by your  permission  safely  turning back  I shall come

(45) Give Me permission, for I must go to the south. With your permission I shall come back safely.”[^10]

শুনি’ সার্ব্বভৌম হৈলা অত্যন্ত কাতর ৷  
চরণে ধরিয়া কহে বিষাদ-উত্তর ॥ ৪৬ ॥

śuni’ sārvabhauma hailā atyanta kātara  
hearing  Sārvabhauma  became  extremely  distressed  
caraṇe dhariyā kahe viṣāda-uttara  
the feet  clasping  he speaks  a sorrowful reply

(46) Hearing this, Sārvabhauma was deeply distressed. Clasping the Lord’s feet, he replied sorrowfully,

বহুজন্মের পুণ্যফলে পাইনু তোমার সঙ্গ ৷  
হেন-সঙ্গ বিধি মোর করিলেক ভঙ্গ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

bahu-janmera puṇya-phale pāinu tomāra saṅga  
of many births  by the fruit of piety  I obtained  Your  company  
hena-saṅga vidhi mora karileka bhaṅga  
such company  Providence  my  has made  broken

(47) “By the piety of many births I obtained Your company, and now Providence is breaking it off.

শিরে বজ্র পড়ে যদি, পুত্র মরি’ যায় ৷  
তাহা সহি, তোমার বিচ্ছেদ সহন না যায় ॥ ৪৮ ॥

śire vajra paḍe yadi, putra mari’ yāya  
on the head  a thunderbolt  falls  if  a son  dying  goes  
tāhā sahi, tomāra viccheda sahana nā yāya  
that  I bear  Your  separation  to bear  not  is possible

(48) If a thunderbolt struck my head or my son died, I could bear it; but separation from You I cannot bear.

স্বতন্ত্র-ঈশ্বর তুমি করিবে গমন ৷  
দিন-কথো রহ, দেখি তোমার চরণ ॥ ৪৯ ॥

svatantra-īśvara tumi karibe gamana  
the independent Lord  You  will make  departure  
dina-katho raha, dekhi tomāra caraṇa  
a few days  stay  let me see  Your  feet

(49) You are the independent Lord, and You will go. But stay a few days, so that I may see Your feet.”

তাহার বিনয়ে প্রভুর শিথিল হৈল মন ৷  
রহিল দিবস-কথো, না কৈল গমন ॥ ৫০ ॥

tāhāra vinaye prabhura śithila haila mana  
his  by humility  of the Lord  softened  became  the heart  
rahila divasa-katho, nā kaila gamana  
He stayed  a few days  not  made  departure

(50) His humble plea softened the Lord’s heart, and He stayed on a few days instead of leaving.

ভট্টাচার্য্য আগ্রহ করি’ করেন নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
গৃহে পাক করি’ প্রভুকে করা’ন ভোজন ॥ ৫১ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya āgraha kari’ karena nimantraṇa  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  insistence  making  makes  invitation  
gṛhe pāka kari’ prabhuke karā’na bhojana  
at home  cooking  doing  the Lord  makes take  the meal

(51) The Bhaṭṭācārya insistently invited Him, had food cooked at home and fed the Lord.

তাঁহার ব্রাহ্মণী, তাঁর নাম — ‘ষাঠীর মাতা’ ৷  
রান্ধি’ ভিক্ষা দেন তিঁহো, আশ্চর্য্য তাঁর কথা ॥ ৫২ ॥

tām̐hāra brāhmaṇī, tām̐ra nāma — ‘ṣāṭhīra mātā’  
his  brāhmaṇa wife  her  name  ‘Ṣāṭhī’s mother’  
rāndhi’ bhikṣā dena tim̐ho, āścarya tām̐ra kathā  
having cooked  alms  gives  she  wonderful  her  story

(52) His wife, known as “Ṣāṭhī’s mother”, cooked and served the Lord His meals. Her story is a wonderful one.[^11]

আগে ত’ কহিব তাহা করিয়া বিস্তার ৷  
এবে কহি প্রভুর দক্ষিণ-যাত্রা সমাচার ॥ ৫৩ ॥

āge ta’ kahiba tāhā kariyā vistāra  
later  indeed  I shall tell  that  making  in detail  
ebe kahi prabhura dakṣiṇa-yātrā samācāra  
now  I tell  the Lord’s  southern journey  the account

(53) I shall tell it in detail later on; now I relate the account of the Lord’s journey to the south.

দিন পাঁচ রহি’ প্রভু ভট্টাচার্য্য-স্থানে ৷  
চলিবার লাগি’ আজ্ঞা মাগিলা আপনে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

dina pām̐ca rahi’ prabhu bhaṭṭācārya-sthāne  
days  five  staying  the Lord  at the Bhaṭṭācārya’s place  
calibāra lāgi’ ājñā māgilā āpane  
for going  permission  asked  Himself

(54) After staying five days with the Bhaṭṭācārya, the Lord Himself asked permission to set out.

প্রভুর আগ্রহে ভট্ট সম্মত হইলা ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে লঞা জগন্নাথ-মন্দিরে গেলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

prabhura āgrahe bhaṭṭa sammata hailā  
by the Lord’s  insistence  the Bhaṭṭa  consenting  became  
prabhu tām̐re lañā jagannātha-mandire gelā  
the Lord  him  taking  to the temple of Jagannātha  went

(55) At the Lord’s insistence the Bhaṭṭācārya consented, and the Lord took him to the temple of Jagannātha.

দর্শন করি’ ঠাকুর-পাশ আজ্ঞা মাগিলা ৷  
পূজারী প্রভুরে মালা-প্রসাদ আনি’ দিলা ॥ ৫৬ ॥

darśana kari’ ṭhākura-pāśa ājñā māgilā  
darśana  having had  from the Deity  permission  He asked  
pūjārī prabhure mālā-prasāda āni’ dilā  
the priest  to the Lord  the garland-prasāda  bringing  gave

(56) After seeing Jagannātha, He asked the Deity for permission, and the priest brought the Lord a garland from the Deity as prasāda.

আজ্ঞা-মালা-পাঞা হর্ষে নমস্কার করি’ ৷  
আনন্দে দক্ষিণদেশে চলে গৌরহরি ॥ ৫৭ ॥

ājñā-mālā-pāñā harṣe namaskāra kari’  
permission and garland receiving  joyfully  obeisance  making  
ānande dakṣiṇadeśe cale gaurahari  
in bliss  to the southern land  goes  Gaurahari

(57) Receiving the permission and the garland, Gaurahari joyfully bowed down and set out in bliss for the south.

ভট্টাচার্য্য সঙ্গে আর যত নিজ জন ৷  
জগন্নাথ প্রদক্ষিণ করি, করিলা গমন ॥ ৫৮ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya saṅge āra yata nija jana  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  with  and  all  His own  people  
jagannātha pradakṣiṇa kari, karilā gamana  
Jagannātha  circumambulation  making  He made  departure

(58) With the Bhaṭṭācārya and all His own people He walked around Jagannātha’s temple and then departed.

সমুদ্র-তীরে-তীরে আলালনাথ-পথে ৷  
সার্ব্বভৌম কহিলেন আচার্য্য-গোপীনাথে ॥ ৫৯ ॥

samudra-tīre-tīre ālālanātha-pathe  
along the seashore  on the road to Ālālanātha  
sārvabhauma kahilena ācārya-gopīnāthe  
Sārvabhauma  said  to Gopīnātha Ācārya

(59) On the road to Ālālanātha along the seashore, Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya:[^12]

চারি কৌপীন-বহির্ব্বাস রাখিয়াছি ঘরে ৷  
তাহা, প্রসাদান্ন, লঞা আইস বিপ্রদ্বারে ॥ ৬০ ॥

cāri kaupīna-bahirvāsa rākhiyāchi ghare  
four  kaupīnas and outer cloths  I have kept  at home  
tāhā, prasādānna, lañā āisa vipra-dvāre  
them  prasāda  taking  come  by a brāhmaṇa

(60) “I have kept four sets of kaupīnas and outer cloths at home. Have them brought by a brāhmaṇa, together with prasāda.”

তবে সার্ব্বভৌম কহে প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
অবশ্য পালিবে, প্রভু, মোর নিবেদনে ॥ ৬১ ॥

tabe sārvabhauma kahe prabhura caraṇe  
then  Sārvabhauma  says  at the Lord’s  feet  
avaśya pālibe, prabhu, mora nivedane  
certainly  You will fulfil  Prabhu  my  request

(61) Then Sārvabhauma, bowing at the Lord’s feet, said, “Prabhu, be sure to grant my request.

‘রামানন্দ রায়’ আছে গোদাবরী-তীরে ৷  
অধিকারী হয়েন তিঁহো বিদ্যানগরে ॥ ৬২ ॥

‘rāmānanda rāya’ āche godāvarī-tīre  
Rāmānanda Rāya  is  on the bank of the Godāvarī  
adhikārī hayena tim̐ho vidyānagare  
governor  is  he  in Vidyānagara

(62) Rāmānanda Rāya lives on the bank of the Godāvarī; he is the governor of Vidyānagara.[^13]

শূদ্র বিষয়ী-জ্ঞানে উপেক্ষা না করিবে ৷  
আমার বচনে তাঁরে অবশ্য মিলিবে ॥ ৬৩ ॥

śūdra viṣayī-jñāne upekṣā nā karibe  
as a śūdra and a worldly man  regarding  neglect  not  You will do  
āmāra vacane tām̐re avaśya milibe  
by my  word  him  certainly  You will meet

(63) Do not neglect him, thinking him a śūdra and a worldly man (*viṣayī*). At my request, be sure to meet him.[^14]

তোমার সঙ্গের যোগ্য তিঁহো একজন ৷  
পৃথিবীতে রসিক ভক্ত নাহি তাঁর সম ॥ ৬৪ ॥

tomāra saṅgera yogya tim̐ho eka jana  
of Your  company  worthy  he  the one  person  
pṛthivīte rasika bhakta nāhi tām̐ra sama  
on earth  rasika  devotee  there is no  his  equal

(64) He is the one man worthy of Your company; in all the world there is no devotee who relishes rasa (*rasika*) as he does.

পাণ্ডিত্য আর ভক্তিরস, — দুঁহের তিঁহো সীমা ৷  
সম্ভাষিলে জানিবে তুমি তাঁহার মহিমা ॥ ৬৫ ॥

pāṇḍitya āra bhakti-rasa, — dum̐hera tim̐ho sīmā  
learning  and  the rasa of bhakti  of both  he  the limit  
sambhāṣile jānibe tumi tām̐hāra mahimā  
speaking with him  will know  You  his  greatness

(65) In learning and in the rasa of devotion he has reached the limit of both. When You speak with him, You will know his greatness.

অলৌকিক বাক্য চেষ্টা তাঁর না বুঝিয়া ৷  
পরিহাস করিয়াছি তাঁরে ‘বৈষ্ণব’ জানিয়া ॥ ৬৬ ॥

alaukika vākya ceṣṭā tām̐ra nā bujhiyā  
extraordinary  words  actions  his  not  understanding  
parihāsa kariyāchi tām̐re ‘vaiṣṇava’ jāniyā  
mockery  I have made  him  a ‘Vaiṣṇava’  knowing

(66) Not understanding his extraordinary words and actions, I used to mock him, knowing him to be a Vaiṣṇava.[^15]

তোমার প্রসাদে এবে জানিনু তাঁর তত্ত্ব ৷  
সম্ভাষিলে জানিবে তাঁর যেমন মহত্ত্ব ॥ ৬৭ ॥

tomāra prasāde ebe jāninu tām̐ra tattva  
by Your  grace  now  I know  his  truth  
sambhāṣile jānibe tām̐ra yemana mahattva  
speaking with him  You will know  his  how  great

(67) By Your grace I now understand who he truly is. Speak with him, and You will see how great he is.”[^16]

অঙ্গীকার করি’ প্রভু তাঁহার বচন ৷  
তাঁরে বিদায় দিতে তাঁরে কৈল আলিঙ্গন ॥ ৬৮ ॥

aṅgīkāra kari’ prabhu tām̐hāra vacana  
acceptance  making  the Lord  his  words  
tām̐re vidāya dite tām̐re kaila āliṅgana  
him  farewell  to give  him  made  an embrace

(68) The Lord accepted his words and, bidding him farewell, embraced him.

ঘরে কৃষ্ণ ভজি’ মোরে করিহ আশীর্ব্বাদে ৷  
নীলাচলে আসি’ যেন তোমার প্রসাদে ॥ ৬৯ ॥

ghare kṛṣṇa bhaji’ more kariha āśīrvāde  
at home  Kṛṣṇa  worshipping  Me  make  blessings  
nīlācale āsi’ yena tomāra prasāde  
to Nīlācala  I may come  so that  by your  grace

(69) “Worship Kṛṣṇa at home and bless Me, so that by your grace I may return to Nīlācala.”[^17]

এত বলি’ মহাপ্রভু করিলা গমন ৷  
মূর্চ্ছিত হঞা তাঁহা পড়িলা সার্ব্বভৌম ॥ ৭০ ॥

eta bali’ mahāprabhu karilā gamana  
so  saying  Mahāprabhu  made  departure  
mūrcchita hañā tām̐hā paḍilā sārvabhauma  
unconscious  becoming  there  fell  Sārvabhauma

(70) With these words Mahāprabhu departed, and Sārvabhauma fell unconscious on the spot.

তাঁরে উপেক্ষিয়া কৈল শীঘ্র গমন ৷  
কে বুঝিতে পারে মহাপ্রভুর চিত্ত-মন ॥ ৭১ ॥

tām̐re upekṣiyā kaila śīghra gamana  
him  disregarding  He made  swift  departure  
ke bujhite pāre mahāprabhura citta-mana  
who  to understand  can  of Mahāprabhu  the heart and mind

(71) Paying no heed to him, the Lord went quickly on His way. Who can understand the heart and mind of Mahāprabhu?

মহানুভাবের চিত্তের স্বভাব এই হয় ৷  
পুষ্প-সম কোমল, কঠিন বজ্রময় ॥ ৭২ ॥

mahānubhāvera cittera svabhāva ei haya  
of great souls  of the heart  the nature  this  is  
puṣpa-sama komala, kaṭhina vajra-maya  
like a flower  soft  hard  like a thunderbolt

(72) Such is the nature of the hearts of great souls: they are soft as a flower, and hard as a thunderbolt.

বজ্রাদপি কঠোরাণি মৃদূনি কুসুমাদপি ৷  
লোকোত্তরাণাং চেতাংসি কো নু বিজ্ঞাতুমীশ্বরঃ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

vajrād api kaṭhorāṇi  
than a thunderbolt  even  harder  
mṛdūni kusumād api  
softer  than a flower  even  
lokottarāṇāṁ cetāṁsi  
of those beyond this world  the hearts  
ko nu vijñātum īśvaraḥ  
who  indeed  to understand  is able

(73) “Harder than a thunderbolt, softer than a flower — such are the hearts of extraordinary souls. Who can ever understand them?” (Uttara-rāma-carita, 3.27)[^18]

নিত্যানন্দপ্রভু ভট্টাচার্য্যে উঠাইল ৷  
তাঁর লোকসঙ্গে তাঁরে ঘরে পাঠাইল ॥ ৭৪ ॥

nityānandaprabhu bhaṭṭācārye uṭhāila  
Nityānanda Prabhu  the Bhaṭṭācārya  raised  
tām̐ra loka-saṅge tām̐re ghare pāṭhāila  
his  with people  him  home  sent

(74) Nityānanda Prabhu lifted the Bhaṭṭācārya up and sent him home with his men.

ভক্তগণ শীঘ্র আসি’ লৈল প্রভুর সাথ ৷  
বস্ত্র-প্রসাদ লঞা তবে আইলা গোপীনাথ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

bhakta-gaṇa śīghra āsi’ laila prabhura sātha  
the devotees  quickly  coming  took  the Lord’s  company  
vastra-prasāda lañā tabe āilā gopīnātha  
cloth and prasāda  bringing  then  came  Gopīnātha

(75) The devotees quickly came and joined the Lord, and then Gopīnātha arrived with the cloth and the prasāda.

সবা-সঙ্গে প্রভু তবে আলালনাথ আইলা ৷  
নমস্কার করি’ তারে বহুস্তুতি কৈলা ॥ ৭৬ ॥

sabā-saṅge prabhu tabe ālālanātha āilā  
with all  the Lord  then  to Ālālanātha  came  
namaskāra kari’ tāre bahu-stuti kailā  
obeisance  making  to Him  many prayers  offered

(76) Then the Lord came with them all to Ālālanātha, bowed down to the Deity and offered Him many prayers.

প্রেমাবেশে নৃত্যগীত কৈল কতক্ষণ ৷  
দেখিতে আইলা তাঁহা বৈসে যত জন ॥ ৭৭ ॥

premāveśe nṛtya-gīta kaila kata-kṣaṇa  
absorbed in love  dancing and singing  He did  for some time  
dekhite āilā tām̐hā vaise yata jana  
to see  came  there  dwell  as many as  people

(77) For some time He danced and sang, absorbed in love, and all the people who lived there came to see Him.

চৌদিকেতে সব লোক বলে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে মধ্যে নৃত্য করে গৌরহরি ॥ ৭৮ ॥

caudikete saba loka bale ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
on all sides  all  the people  say  Hari  Hari  
premāveśe madhye nṛtya kare gaurahari  
absorbed in love  in the middle  dance  does  Gaurahari

(78) All around Him the people called out, “Hari! Hari!” and in their midst Gaurahari danced, absorbed in love.

কাঞ্চন-সদৃশ দেহ, অরুণ বসন ৷  
পুলকাশ্রু-কম্প-স্বেদ তাহাতে ভূষণ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

kāñcana-sadṛśa deha, aruṇa vasana  
like gold  body  reddish  garment  
pulakāśru-kampa-sveda tāhāte bhūṣaṇa  
horripilation, tears, trembling and perspiration  on it  the ornaments

(79) His body was like gold, His garment the colour of dawn, and horripilation, tears, trembling and perspiration adorned Him.

দেখিয়া লোকের মনে হৈল চমৎকার ৷  
যত লোক আইসে, কেহ নাহি যায় ঘর ॥ ৮০ ॥

dekhiyā lokera mane haila camatkāra  
seeing  of the people  in the minds  arose  wonder  
yata loka āise, keha nāhi yāya ghara  
as many  people  come  anyone  not  goes  home

(80) The sight filled the people with wonder. Of all who came, no one went home.

কেহ নাচে, কেহ গায় ‘শ্রীকৃষ্ণ’ ‘গোপাল’ ৷  
প্রেমেতে ভাসিল লোক, — স্ত্রী-বৃদ্ধ-আবাল ॥ ৮১ ॥

keha nāce, keha gāya ‘śrī-kṛṣṇa’ ‘gopāla’  
some  dance  some  sing  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  Gopāla  
premete bhāsila loka, — strī-vṛddha-ābāla  
in love  floated  the people  women, the old and children

(81) Some danced, and some sang, “Śrī Kṛṣṇa! Gopāla!” Everyone — women, the old and children alike — was carried away on the tide of love.

দেখি’ নিত্যানন্দ প্রভু কহে ভক্তগণে ৷  
এইরূপে আগে নৃত্য হবে গ্রামে-গ্রামে ॥ ৮২ ॥

dekhi’ nityānanda prabhu kahe bhakta-gaṇe  
seeing  Nityānanda  Prabhu  says  to the devotees  
ei-rūpe āge nṛtya habe grāme-grāme  
in this way  ahead  dancing  there will be  in village after village

(82) Seeing this, Nityānanda Prabhu said to the devotees, “Further on there will be dancing like this in village after village.”

অতিকাল হৈল, লোক ছাড়িয়া না যায় ৷  
তবে নিত্যানন্দ-গোসাঞি সৃজিলা উপায় ॥ ৮৩ ॥

atikāla haila, loka chāḍiyā nā yāya  
much time  passed  the people  leaving  not  go  
tabe nityānanda-gosāñi sṛjilā upāya  
then  Nityānanda Gosāñi  devised  a means

(83) Much time had passed, yet the people would not leave. Then Nityānanda Gosāñi devised a plan.

মধ্যাহ্ন করিতে গেলা প্রভুকে লঞা ৷  
তাহা দেখি’ লোক আইসে চৌদিকে ধাঞা ॥ ৮৪ ॥

madhyāhna karite gelā prabhuke lañā  
midday duties  to perform  He went  the Lord  taking  
tāhā dekhi’ loka āise caudike dhāñā  
that  seeing  people  come  from all sides  running

(84) He took the Lord away to perform His midday duties, and at this the people came running from all sides.

মধ্যাহ্ন করিতে আইলা দেবতা-মন্দিরে ৷  
নিজগণ প্রবেশি’ কপাট দিল বহির্দ্বারে ॥ ৮৫ ॥

madhyāhna karite āilā devatā-mandire  
midday duties  to perform  They came  to the temple of the Deity  
nija-gaṇa praveśi’ kapāṭa dila bahir-dvāre  
Their own people  letting in  the doors  shut  at the outer gate

(85) To perform Their midday duties They came to the temple of the Deity, let Their own people in and shut the outer gate.

তবে দুইপ্রভুরে গোপীনাথ ভিক্ষা করাইল ৷  
প্রভুর শেষ প্রসাদান্ন সবে বাঁটি’ খাইল ॥ ৮৬ ॥

tabe dui-prabhure gopīnātha bhikṣā karāila  
then  the two Lords  Gopīnātha  meal  made take  
prabhura śeṣa prasādānna sabe bām̐ṭi’ khāila  
the Lord’s  remnants  prasāda  all  sharing  ate

(86) Then Gopīnātha served the two Lords Their meal, and everyone shared and ate the Lord’s remnants.

শুনি’ শুনি’ লোক-সব আসি’ বহির্দ্বারে ৷  
‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ বলি’ লোক কলরব করে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

śuni’ śuni’ loka-saba āsi’ bahir-dvāre  
hearing  hearing  all the people  coming  to the outer gate  
‘hari’ ‘hari’ bali’ loka kala-rava kare  
Hari  Hari  saying  the people  clamour  make

(87) Hearing where He was, all the people came to the outer gate and raised a clamour, crying, “Hari! Hari!”

তবে মহাপ্রভু দ্বার করাইল মোচন ৷  
আনন্দে আসিয়া লোক পাইল দরশন ॥ ৮৮ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu dvāra karāila mocana  
then  Mahāprabhu  the door  had  opened  
ānande āsiyā loka pāila daraśana  
joyfully  coming  the people  obtained  darśana

(88) Then Mahāprabhu had the door opened, and the people came in joyfully and saw Him.

এইমত সন্ধ্যা পর্য্যন্ত লোক আসে, যায় ৷  
‘বৈষ্ণব’ হইল লোক, সবে নাচে, গায় ॥ ৮৯ ॥

ei-mata sandhyā paryanta loka āse, yāya  
in this way  evening  until  people  come  go  
‘vaiṣṇava’ haila loka, sabe nāce, gāya  
Vaiṣṇavas  became  the people  all  dance  sing

(89) In this way people came and went until evening. They all became Vaiṣṇavas, and they all danced and sang.

এইরূপে সেই ঠাঞি ভক্তগণ-সঙ্গে ৷  
সেই রাত্রি গোঙাইলা কৃষ্ণকথা-রঙ্গে ॥ ৯০ ॥

ei-rūpe sei ṭhāñi bhakta-gaṇa-saṅge  
in this way  at that  place  with the devotees  
sei rātri goṅāilā kṛṣṇa-kathā-raṅge  
that  night  He spent  in the joy of talks about Kṛṣṇa

(90) So He spent that night there with the devotees, rejoicing in talks about Kṛṣṇa.

প্রাতঃকালে স্নান করি’ করিলা গমন ৷  
ভক্তগণে বিদায় দিলা করি’ আলিঙ্গন ॥ ৯১ ॥

prātaḥ-kāle snāna kari’ karilā gamana  
in the morning  bath  having taken  He made  departure  
bhakta-gaṇe vidāya dilā kari’ āliṅgana  
to the devotees  farewell  He gave  making  embraces

(91) In the morning He bathed and set out, bidding the devotees farewell with embraces.

মূর্চ্ছিত হঞা সবে ভূমিতে পড়িলা ৷  
তাঁহা-সবা পানে প্রভু ফিরি’ না চাহিলা ॥ ৯২ ॥

mūrcchita hañā sabe bhūmite paḍilā  
unconscious  becoming  all  on the ground  fell  
tām̐hā-sabā pāne prabhu phiri’ nā cāhilā  
them all  towards  the Lord  turning  not  looked

(92) They all fell to the ground unconscious, but the Lord did not turn back to look at them.

বিচ্ছেদে ব্যাকুল প্রভু চলিলা দুঃখী হঞা ৷  
পাছে কৃষ্ণদাস যায় জলপাত্র লঞা ॥ ৯৩ ॥

vicchede vyākula prabhu calilā duḥkhī hañā  
in separation  agitated  the Lord  went on  sorrowful  becoming  
pāche kṛṣṇadāsa yāya jala-pātra lañā  
behind  Kṛṣṇadāsa  goes  the water pot  carrying

(93) Agitated by the separation, the Lord went on in sorrow, and behind Him came Kṛṣṇadāsa carrying the water pot.

ভক্তগণ উপবাসী তাঁহাই রহিলা ৷  
আর দিনে দুঃখী হঞা নীলাচলে আইলা ॥ ৯৪ ॥

bhakta-gaṇa upavāsī tām̐hāi rahilā  
the devotees  fasting  right there  remained  
āra dine duḥkhī hañā nīlācale āilā  
on the next  day  sorrowful  becoming  to Nīlācala  came

(94) The devotees stayed there fasting, and the next day they returned to Nīlācala in sorrow.

মত্তসিংহ-প্রায় প্রভু করিলা গমন ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে যায় করি’ নাম-সংকীর্ত্তন ॥ ৯৫ ॥

matta-siṁha-prāya prabhu karilā gamana  
like a maddened lion  the Lord  made  departure  
premāveśe yāya kari’ nāma-saṁkīrtana  
absorbed in love  He goes  making  saṅkīrtana of the name

(95) The Lord went on like a maddened lion, absorbed in love, singing the saṅkīrtana of the holy name:

কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! হে ৷  
কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! হে ॥  
কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! রক্ষ মাম্ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! কৃষ্ণ! পাহি মাম্ ॥  
রাম! রাঘব! রাম! রাঘব! রাম! রাঘব! রক্ষ মাম্ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ! কেশব! কৃষ্ণ! কেশব! কৃষ্ণ! কেশব! পাহি মাম্ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! he  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  O  
kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! he  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  O  
kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! rakṣa mām  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  protect  Me  
kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! kṛṣṇa! pāhi mām  
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  save  Me  
rāma! rāghava! rāma! rāghava! rāma! rāghava! rakṣa mām  
Rāma  descendant of Raghu  Rāma  descendant of Raghu  Rāma  descendant of Raghu  protect  Me  
kṛṣṇa! keśava! kṛṣṇa! keśava! kṛṣṇa! keśava! pāhi mām  
Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  save  Me

(96) “O Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! O Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Protect Me! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa! Save Me! Rāma! Rāghava! Rāma! Rāghava! Rāma! Rāghava! Protect Me! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Save Me!”[^19]

এই শ্লোক পড়ি’ পথে চলিলা গৌরহরি ৷  
লোক দেখি’ পথে কহে, — বল ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

ei śloka paḍi’ pathe calilā gaurahari  
this  verse  reciting  on the road  went  Gaurahari  
loka dekhi’ pathe kahe, — bala ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
people  seeing  on the road  He says  say  Hari  Hari

(97) Reciting this verse, Gaurahari went along the road, and whenever He saw people on the way, He told them, “Say ‘Hari! Hari!’”

সেই লোক প্রেমমত্ত হঞা বলে ‘হরি’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ৷  
প্রভুর পাছে সঙ্গে যায় দর্শন-সতৃষ্ণ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

sei loka premamatta hañā bale ‘hari’ ‘kṛṣṇa’  
those  people  maddened with love  becoming  say  Hari  Kṛṣṇa  
prabhura pāche saṅge yāya darśana-satṛṣṇa  
the Lord’s  behind  along  go  thirsting for His sight

(98) Those people, maddened with love, would call out, “Hari! Kṛṣṇa!” and follow behind the Lord, thirsting to see Him.

কতক্ষণে রহি’ প্রভু তারে আলিঙ্গিয়া ৷  
বিদায় করিল তারে শক্তি সঞ্চারিয়া ॥ ৯৯ ॥

kata-kṣaṇe rahi’ prabhu tāre āliṅgiyā  
after some time  stopping  the Lord  them  embracing  
vidāya karila tāre śakti sañcāriyā  
farewell  made  them  power  transmitting

(99) After a while the Lord would stop, embrace them, invest them with His power and send them back.[^20]

সেইজন নিজ-গ্রামে করিয়া গমন ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ বলি’ হাসে, কান্দে, নাচে অনুক্ষণ ॥ ১০০ ॥

sei-jana nija-grāme kariyā gamana  
that person  to his own village  making  return  
‘kṛṣṇa’ bali’ hāse, kānde, nāce anukṣaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  saying  laughs  weeps  dances  constantly

(100) Each of them returned to his own village and constantly laughed, wept and danced, calling, “Kṛṣṇa!”

যারে দেখে, তারে কহে, — কহ কৃষ্ণনাম ৷  
এইমত ‘বৈষ্ণব’ কৈল সব নিজ-গ্রাম ॥ ১০১ ॥

yāre dekhe, tāre kahe, — kaha kṛṣṇa-nāma  
whom  he sees  to him  says  say  Kṛṣṇa’s name  
ei-mata ‘vaiṣṇava’ kaila saba nija-grāma  
in this way  Vaiṣṇava  made  all  his own village

(101) Whomever he saw, he told, “Say the name of Kṛṣṇa!” In this way he made his whole village Vaiṣṇava.

গ্রামান্তর হৈতে দেখিতে আইল যত জন ৷  
তাঁর দর্শন-কৃপায় হয় তাঁর সম ॥ ১০২ ॥

grāmāntara haite dekhite āila yata jana  
from other villages  to see  came  as many as  people  
tām̐ra darśana-kṛpāya haya tām̐ra sama  
his  by the mercy of darśana  become  his  equal

(102) All who came from other villages to see him became like him by the mercy of seeing him.

সেই যাই’ গ্রামের লোক বৈষ্ণব করয় ৷  
অন্যগ্রামী আসি’ তাঁরে দেখি’ বৈষ্ণব হয় ॥ ১০৩ ॥

sei yāi’ grāmera loka vaiṣṇava karaya  
he  going  of the village  the people  Vaiṣṇava  makes  
anya-grāmī āsi’ tām̐re dekhi’ vaiṣṇava haya  
people of other villages  coming  him  seeing  Vaiṣṇava  become

(103) Each of these went home and made the people of his own village Vaiṣṇavas, and people from yet other villages came, saw him and became Vaiṣṇavas.

সেই যাই’ অন্য গ্রামে করে উপদেশ ৷  
এইমত ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হৈল সব দক্ষিণ-দেশ ॥ ১০৪ ॥

sei yāi’ anya grāme kare upadeśa  
he  going  in another  village  makes  instruction  
ei-mata ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila saba dakṣiṇa-deśa  
in this way  Vaiṣṇava  became  all  the southern country

(104) They in turn went and taught in other villages, and in this way the whole of the south became Vaiṣṇava.

এইমত পথে যাইতে শত শত জন ৷  
‘বৈষ্ণব’ করেন তাঁরে করি’ আলিঙ্গন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

ei-mata pathe yāite śata śata jana  
in this way  on the road  going  hundreds  hundreds  people  
‘vaiṣṇava’ karena tām̐re kari’ āliṅgana  
Vaiṣṇava  He makes  them  making  embrace

(105) In this way, as He travelled, the Lord made Vaiṣṇavas of hundreds upon hundreds of people by embracing them.

যেই গ্রামে রহি’ ভিক্ষা করেন যাঁর ঘরে ৷  
সেই গ্রামের যত লোক আইসে দেখিবারে ॥ ১০৬ ॥

yei grāme rahi’ bhikṣā karena yām̐ra ghare  
in which  village  staying  alms  He takes  in whose  house  
sei grāmera yata loka āise dekhibāre  
of that  village  all  people  come  to see

(106) In whichever village He stayed and took His meal in someone’s house, all the people of that village came to see Him.

প্রভুর কৃপায় হয় মহাভাগবত ৷  
সেইসব আচার্য্য হঞা তারিল জগৎ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

prabhura kṛpāya haya mahābhāgavata  
by the Lord’s  mercy  they become  mahā-bhāgavatas  
seisaba ācārya hañā tārila jagat  
all of them  ācāryas  becoming  delivered  the world

(107) By the Lord’s mercy they became mahā-bhāgavatas, and as ācāryas they all delivered the world.

এইমত কৈলা যাবৎ গেলা সেতুবন্ধে ৷  
সর্ব্বদেশ ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হৈল প্রভুর সম্বন্ধে ॥ ১০৮ ॥

ei-mata kailā yāvat gelā setubandhe  
in this way  He did  until  He went  to Setubandha  
sarva-deśa ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila prabhura sambandhe  
all lands  Vaiṣṇava  became  through the Lord’s  connection

(108) He did this all the way to Setubandha, and through their connection with the Lord all the lands became Vaiṣṇava.

নবদ্বীপে যেই শক্তি না কৈলা প্রকাশে ৷  
সেই শক্তি প্রকাশি’ নিস্তারিল দক্ষিণদেশে ॥ ১০৯ ॥

navadvīpe yei śakti nā kailā prakāśe  
in Navadvīpa  which  power  not  He made  manifest  
sei śakti prakāśi’ nistārila dakṣiṇa-deśe  
that  power  manifesting  He delivered  the southern country

(109) The power He had not manifested in Navadvīpa He now manifested to deliver the southern country.[^21]

প্রভুকে যে ভজে, তারে তাঁর কৃপা হয় ৷  
সেই সে এ-সব লীলা সত্য করি’ লয় ॥ ১১০ ॥

prabhuke ye bhaje, tāre tām̐ra kṛpā haya  
the Lord  who  worships  to him  His  mercy  comes  
sei se e-saba līlā satya kari’ laya  
he  alone  these  all  pastimes  true  making  accepts

(110) Whoever worships the Lord receives His mercy, and only such a person accepts all these pastimes as true.

অলৌকিক-লীলায় যার না হয় বিশ্বাস ৷  
ইহলোক, পরলোক তার হয় নাশ ॥ ১১১ ॥

alaukika-līlāya yāra nā haya viśvāsa  
in the extraordinary pastimes  whose  not  is  faith  
iha-loka, para-loka tāra haya nāśa  
this world  the next world  his  is  ruin

(111) Whoever has no faith in these extraordinary pastimes is ruined both in this world and in the next.

প্রথমে কহিল প্রভুর যেরূপে গমন ৷  
এইমত জানিহ যাবৎ দক্ষিণ-ভ্রমণ ॥ ১১২ ॥

prathame kahila prabhura ye-rūpe gamana  
first  I told  the Lord’s  in what manner  journey  
ei-mata jāniha yāvat dakṣiṇa-bhramaṇa  
in this way  know  as long as  the southern tour

(112) I have told first how the Lord travelled; know that it was the same throughout His tour of the south.

এইমত যাইতে যাইতে গেলা কূর্ম্মস্থানে ৷  
কূর্ম্ম দেখি’ কৈল তারে স্তবন-প্রণামে ॥ ১১৩ ॥

ei-mata yāite yāite gelā kūrma-sthāne  
in this way  going  going  He went  to Kūrma-sthāna  
kūrma dekhi’ kaila tāre stavana-praṇāme  
Kūrma  seeing  He made  to Him  prayers and obeisances

(113) Travelling on in this way, He came to Kūrma-sthāna. Seeing the Deity of Kūrma, He offered Him prayers and obeisances.

প্রেমাবেশে হাসি’ কান্দি’ নৃত্য-গীত কৈল ৷  
দেখি’ সর্ব্ব লোকের চিত্তে চমৎকার হৈল ॥ ১১৪ ॥

premāveśe hāsi’ kāndi’ nṛtya-gīta kaila  
absorbed in love  laughing  weeping  dancing and singing  He did  
dekhi’ sarva lokera citte camatkāra haila  
seeing  of all  the people  in the hearts  wonder  arose

(114) Absorbed in love, He laughed and wept, danced and sang, and all who saw it were filled with wonder.

আশ্চর্য্য শুনিয়া লোক আইল দেখিবারে ৷  
প্রভুর রূপ-প্রেম দেখি’ হৈলা চমৎকারে ॥ ১১৫ ॥

āścarya śuniyā loka āila dekhibāre  
the wonder  hearing  people  came  to see  
prabhura rūpa-prema dekhi’ hailā camatkāre  
the Lord’s  beauty and love  seeing  became  amazed

(115) Hearing of this wonder, people came to see, and they were amazed at the Lord’s beauty and love.

দর্শনে ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হৈল, বলে ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে নাচে লোক ঊর্দ্ধ বাহু করি’ ॥ ১১৬ ॥

darśane ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila, bale ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘hari’  
by the sight  Vaiṣṇavas  became  say  Kṛṣṇa  Hari  
premāveśe nāce loka ūrddha bāhu kari’  
absorbed in love  dance  the people  raised  arms  making

(116) Just by seeing Him they became Vaiṣṇavas and called out, “Kṛṣṇa! Hari!” Absorbed in love, the people danced with their arms raised.

কৃষ্ণনাম লোকমুখে শুনি’ অবিরাম ৷  
সেই লোক ‘বৈষ্ণব’ কৈল অন্য সব গ্রাম ॥ ১১৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nāma loka-mukhe śuni’ avirāma  
Kṛṣṇa’s name  from the mouths of people  hearing  ceaselessly  
sei loka ‘vaiṣṇava’ kaila anya saba grāma  
those  people  Vaiṣṇava  made  other  all  villages

(117) Having heard the name of Kṛṣṇa ceaselessly from others’ lips, these people in turn made all the other villages Vaiṣṇava.

এইমত পরম্পরায় দেশ ‘বৈষ্ণব’ হৈল ৷  
কৃষ্ণনামামৃত-বন্যায় দেশ ভাসাইল ॥ ১১৮ ॥

ei-mata paramparāya deśa ‘vaiṣṇava’ haila  
in this way  by succession  the land  Vaiṣṇava  became  
kṛṣṇa-nāmāmṛta-vanyāya deśa bhāsāila  
by the flood of the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the land  He flooded

(118) In this way, one from another, the whole land became Vaiṣṇava: He flooded the country with the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s name.

কতক্ষণে প্রভু যদি বাহ্য প্রকাশিলা ৷  
কূর্ম্মের সেবক বহু সম্মান করিলা ॥ ১১৯ ॥

kata-kṣaṇe prabhu yadi bāhya prakāśilā  
after a while  the Lord  when  outward consciousness  showed  
kūrmera sevaka bahu sammāna karilā  
of Kūrma  the servant  much  honour  showed

(119) After some time, when the Lord returned to outward consciousness, the servant of the Deity of Kūrma honoured Him greatly.

যেই গ্রামে যায় তাঁহা এই ব্যবহার ৷  
এক ঠাঞি কহিল, না কহিব আর বার ॥ ১২০ ॥

yei grāme yāya tām̐hā ei vyavahāra  
which  village  He goes  there  this  behaviour  
eka ṭhāñi kahila, nā kahiba āra bāra  
in one  place  I have told  not  I shall tell  again

(120) In every village He went to, it was the same. I have described it here in one place and shall not describe it again.

‘কূর্ম্ম’-নামে সেই গ্রামে বৈদিক ব্রাহ্মণ ৷  
বহু শ্রদ্ধা-ভক্ত্যে কৈল প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১২১ ॥

‘kūrma’-nāme sei grāme vaidika brāhmaṇa  
‘Kūrma’  by name  in that  village  a Vedic  brāhmaṇa  
bahu śraddhā-bhaktye kaila prabhura nimantraṇa  
great  with faith and devotion  made  the Lord’s  invitation

(121) In that village a Vedic brāhmaṇa named Kūrma invited the Lord with great faith and devotion.

ঘরে আনি’ প্রভুর কৈল পাদ প্রক্ষালন ৷  
সেই জল বংশ-সহিত করিল ভক্ষণ ॥ ১২২ ॥

ghare āni’ prabhura kaila pāda prakṣālana  
home  bringing  the Lord’s  made  feet  washing  
sei jala vaṁśa-sahita karila bhakṣaṇa  
that  water  with his family  made  drinking

(122) He brought the Lord home and washed His feet, and he and his whole family drank that water.

অনেকপ্রকার স্নেহে ভিক্ষা করাইল ৷  
গোসাঞির প্রসাদান্ন সবংশে খাইল ॥ ১২৩ ॥

aneka-prakāra snehe bhikṣā karāila  
of many kinds  with affection  meal  made take  
gosāñira prasādānna sa-vaṁśe khāila  
Gosāñi’s  prasāda  with his family  ate

(123) With great affection he fed the Lord many kinds of food, and he and his family ate the Gosāñi’s remnants.

‘যেই পাদপদ্ম তোমার ব্রহ্মা ধ্যান করে ৷  
সেই পাদপদ্ম সাক্ষাৎ আইল মোর ঘরে ॥ ১২৪ ॥

‘yei pāda-padma tomāra brahmā dhyāna kare  
which  lotus feet  Your  Brahmā  meditation  makes  
sei pāda-padma sākṣāt āila mora ghare  
those  lotus feet  in person  have come  to my  house

(124) “Your lotus feet, on which Brahmā meditates — those same lotus feet have come in person to my house!

মোর ভাগ্যের সীমা না যায় কহন ৷  
আজি মোর শ্লাঘ্য হৈল জন্ম-কুল-ধন ॥ ১২৫ ॥

mora bhāgyera sīmā nā yāya kahana  
my  of good fortune  the limit  not  can be  told  
āji mora ślāghya haila janma-kula-dhana  
today  my  glorious  became  birth, family and wealth

(125) There is no telling the limit of my good fortune. Today my birth, my family and my wealth have become glorious.

কৃপা কর, প্রভু, মোরে, যাঙ তোমা-সঙ্গে ৷  
সহিতে নারিমু তোমার বিরহ-তরঙ্গে ॥ ১২৬ ॥

kṛpā kara, prabhu, more, yāṅa tomā-saṅge  
mercy  show  Prabhu  to me  let me go  with You  
sahite nārimu tomāra viraha-taraṅge  
to bear  I shall not be able  Your  waves of separation

(126) Be merciful to me, Prabhu — let me go with You. I shall not be able to bear the waves of separation from You.”

প্রভু কহে, — ঐছে বাত্ কভু না কহিবা ৷  
গৃহে রহি’ কৃষ্ণ-নাম নিরন্তর লৈবা ॥ ১২৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — aiche bāt kabhu nā kahibā  
the Lord  says  such  talk  ever  not  you should say  
gṛhe rahi’ kṛṣṇa-nāma nirantara laibā  
at home  staying  Kṛṣṇa’s name  constantly  you should take

(127) The Lord said, “Never speak like that. Stay at home and chant the name of Kṛṣṇa constantly.

যারে দেখ, তারে কহ ‘কৃষ্ণ’-উপদেশ ৷  
আমার আজ্ঞায় গুরু হঞা তার’ এই দেশ ॥ ১২৮ ॥

yāre dekha, tāre kaha ‘kṛṣṇa’-upadeśa  
whom  you see  to him  tell  the teaching about Kṛṣṇa  
āmāra ājñāya guru hañā tāra’ ei deśa  
by My  order  guru  becoming  deliver  this  land

(128) Whomever you see, teach him about Kṛṣṇa. By My order become a guru and deliver this land.

কভু না বাধিবে তোমার বিষয়-তরঙ্গ ৷  
পুনরপি এই ঠাঞি পাবে মোর সঙ্গ ॥ ১২৯ ॥

kabhu nā bādhibe tomāra viṣaya-taraṅga  
ever  not  will obstruct  you  the waves of worldly life  
punarapi ei ṭhāñi pābe mora saṅga  
again  in this  place  you will get  My  company

(129) Then the waves of worldly life will never obstruct you, and here, in this very place, you will have My company again.”

এইমত যাঁর ঘরে করে প্রভু ভিক্ষা ৷  
সেই ঐছে কহে, তাঁরে করায় এই শিক্ষা ॥ ১৩০ ॥

ei mata yām̐ra ghare kare prabhu bhikṣā  
in this way  in whose  house  takes  the Lord  alms  
sei aiche kahe, tām̐re karāya ei śikṣā  
he  likewise  speaks  to him  He gives  this  teaching

(130) In whatever house the Lord took His meal, the host spoke in the same way, and the Lord gave him this same teaching.

পথে যাইতে দেবালয়ে রহে যেই গ্রামে ৷  
যাঁর ঘরে ভিক্ষা করে, সেই মহাজনে ॥ ১৩১ ॥

কূর্ম্মে যৈছে রীতি, তৈছে কৈল সর্ব্বঠাঞি ৷  
নীলাচলে পুনঃ যাবৎ না আইলা গোসাঞি ॥ ১৩২ ॥

pathe yāite devālaye rahe yei grāme  
on the road  going  at a temple  He stays  in which  village  
yām̐ra ghare bhikṣā kare, sei mahā-jane  
in whose  house  alms  He takes  to that  great soul  
kūrme yaiche rīti, taiche kaila sarva-ṭhāñi  
with Kūrma  as  the custom  so  He did  everywhere  
nīlācale punaḥ yāvat nā āilā gosāñi  
to Nīlācala  again  until  not  came  Gosāñi

(131–132) On His way, in whatever village He stayed at a temple, and with whatever great soul in whose house He took His meal, He did as He had done with Kūrma. So He did everywhere, until Gosāñi came back to Nīlācala.

অতএব ইঁহা কহিলাঙ করিয়া বিস্তার ৷  
এইমত জানিবে প্রভুর সর্ব্বত্র ব্যবহার ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

ataeva im̐hā kahilāṅa kariyā vistāra  
therefore  here  I have told  making  in detail  
ei-mata jānibe prabhura sarvatra vyavahāra  
in this way  you should know  the Lord’s  everywhere  conduct

(133) That is why I have described it here in detail; know that this is how the Lord acted everywhere.

এইমত সেই রাত্রি তাঁহাই রহিলা ৷  
প্রাতঃকালে প্রভু স্নান করিয়া চলিলা ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

ei-mata sei rātri tām̐hāi rahilā  
in this way  that  night  right there  He stayed  
prātaḥ-kāle prabhu snāna kariyā calilā  
in the morning  the Lord  bath  having taken  set out

(134) He stayed there that night, and in the morning, after bathing, the Lord set out.

প্রভুর অনুব্রজি’ কূর্ম্ম বহু দূর আইলা ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে যত্ন করি’ ঘরে পাঠাইলা ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

prabhura anuvraji’ kūrma bahu dūra āilā  
the Lord  following  Kūrma  a long  way  came  
prabhu tām̐re yatna kari’ ghare pāṭhāilā  
the Lord  him  with care  home  sent

(135) Kūrma followed the Lord a long way, until the Lord gently persuaded him to go home.

‘বাসুদেব’-নাম এক দ্বিজ মহাশয় ৷  
সর্ব্বাঙ্গে গলিত কুষ্ঠ, তাতে কীড়াময় ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

‘vāsudeva’-nāma eka dvija mahāśaya  
‘Vāsudeva’  by name  a  brāhmaṇa  noble  
sarvāṅge galita kuṣṭha, tāte kīḍā-maya  
all over the body  rotting  leprosy  in it  full of worms

(136) There was a noble brāhmaṇa named Vāsudeva. His whole body was rotting with leprosy and swarming with worms.

অঙ্গ হৈতে যেই কীড়া খসিয়া পড়য় ৷  
উঠাঞা সেই কীড়া রাখে সেই ঠাঞি ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

aṅga haite yei kīḍā khasiyā paḍaya  
from the body  whatever  worm  slipping  falls  
uṭhāñā sei kīḍā rākhe sei ṭhāñi  
picking up  that  worm  he puts  in that  place

(137) Whenever a worm slipped and fell from his body, he would pick it up and put it back in the same place.

রাত্রিতে শুনিলা তিঁহো গোসাঞির আগমন ৷  
দেখিবারে আইলা প্রভাতে কূর্ম্মের ভবন ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

rātrite śunilā tim̐ho gosāñira āgamana  
at night  heard  he  the Gosāñi’s  arrival  
dekhibāre āilā prabhāte kūrmera bhavana  
to see  he came  in the morning  to Kūrma’s  house

(138) At night he heard of the Gosāñi’s arrival, and in the morning he came to Kūrma’s house to see Him.

প্রভুর গমন কূর্ম্ম-মুখেতে শুনিঞা ৷  
ভূমিতে পড়িলা দুঃখে মূর্চ্ছিত হঞা ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

prabhura gamana kūrma-mukhete śuniñā  
the Lord’s  departure  from Kūrma’s mouth  hearing  
bhūmite paḍilā duḥkhe mūrcchita hañā  
on the ground  he fell  in grief  unconscious  becoming

(139) When he heard from Kūrma that the Lord had gone, he fell to the ground unconscious with grief.

অনেক প্রকার বিলাপ করিতে লাগিলা ৷  
সেইক্ষণে আসি’ প্রভু তাঁরে আলিঙ্গিলা ॥ ১৪০ ॥

aneka prakāra vilāpa karite lāgilā  
many  kinds  lamentation  to make  he began  
sei-kṣaṇe āsi’ prabhu tām̐re āliṅgilā  
at that very moment  coming  the Lord  him  embraced

(140) He began to lament in many ways, and at that very moment the Lord came and embraced him.

প্রভু-স্পর্শে দুঃখ-সঙ্গে কুষ্ঠ দূরে গেল ৷  
আনন্দ সহিতে অঙ্গ সুন্দর হইল ॥ ১৪১ ॥

prabhu-sparśe duḥkha-saṅge kuṣṭha dūre gela  
by the Lord’s touch  together with the grief  the leprosy  far  went  
ānanda sahite aṅga sundara haila  
bliss  together with  the body  beautiful  became

(141) At the Lord’s touch his leprosy vanished together with his grief, and his body became beautiful and full of bliss.

প্রভুর কৃপা দেখি’ তাঁর বিস্ময় হৈল মন ৷  
শ্লোক পড়ি’ পায়ে ধরি’ করয়ে স্তবন ॥ ১৪২ ॥

prabhura kṛpā dekhi’ tām̐ra vismaya haila mana  
the Lord’s  mercy  seeing  his  amazed  became  mind  
śloka paḍi’ pāye dhari’ karaye stavana  
a verse  reciting  the feet  holding  he makes  praise

(142) Seeing the Lord’s mercy, he was amazed. Holding the Lord’s feet, he recited a verse and praised Him:

ক্বাহং দরিদ্রঃ পাপীয়ান্ ক্ব কৃষ্ণঃ শ্রীনিকেতনঃ ৷  
ব্রহ্মবন্ধুরিতি স্মাহং বাহুভ্যাং পরিরম্ভিতঃ ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

kvāhaṁ daridraḥ pāpīyān  
where  I  poor  most sinful  
kva kṛṣṇaḥ śrī-niketanaḥ  
where  Kṛṣṇa  the abode of Śrī  
brahma-bandhur iti smāhaṁ  
a brāhmaṇa by birth only  thus  indeed  I  
bāhubhyāṁ parirambhitaḥ  
with both arms  was embraced

(143) “Who am I, a poor and sinful man, and who is Kṛṣṇa, the abode of the goddess of fortune? Yet because I was born a brāhmaṇa, He embraced me in His arms!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.81.16)

বহু স্তুতি করি’ কহে, — শুন, দয়াময় ৷  
জীবে এই গুণ নাহি, তোমাতে এই হয় ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

bahu stuti kari’ kahe, — śuna, dayā-maya  
many  prayers  making  he says  hear  O Merciful One  
jīve ei guṇa nāhi, tomāte ei haya  
in a jīva  this  quality  is not  in You  this  is

(144) After offering many prayers he said, “Hear me, O Merciful One: no jīva has this quality; it is found in You alone.

মোরে দেখি’ মোর গন্ধে পলায় পামর ৷  
হেন-মোরে স্পর্শ’ তুমি, — স্বতন্ত্র-ঈশ্বর ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

more dekhi’ mora gandhe palāya pāmara  
me  seeing  at my  smell  flee  the wretched  
hena-more sparśa’ tumi, — svatantra-īśvara  
such a me  touch  You  the independent Lord

(145) Seeing me and smelling my stench, even the wretched flee from me; yet You touch someone like me. You are the independent Lord!

কিন্তু আছিলাঙ ভাল অধম হঞা ৷  
এবে অহঙ্কার মোর জন্মিবে আসিয়া ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

kintu āchilāṅa bhāla adhama hañā  
but  I was  well off  lowly  being  
ebe ahaṅkāra mora janmibe āsiyā  
now  pride  my  will be born  coming

(146) But I was better off being lowly; now pride will arise in me.”

প্রভু কহে, — কভু তোমার না হবে অভিমান ৷  
নিরন্তর কহ তুমি ‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ নাম ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — kabhu tomāra nā habe abhimāna  
the Lord  says  ever  your  not  will be  pride  
nirantara kaha tumi ‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ nāma  
constantly  say  you  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  the name

(147) The Lord said, “You will never become proud. Chant the name ‘Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!’ constantly.

কৃষ্ণ উপদেশি’ কর জীবের নিস্তার ৷  
অচিরাতে কৃষ্ণ তোমা করিবেন অঙ্গীকার ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa upadeśi’ kara jīvera nistāra  
about Kṛṣṇa  teaching  make  of the jīvas  deliverance  
acirāte kṛṣṇa tomā karibena aṅgīkāra  
soon  Kṛṣṇa  you  will make  acceptance

(148) Teach about Kṛṣṇa and deliver the jīvas, and very soon Kṛṣṇa will accept you as His own.”

এতেক কহিয়া প্রভু কৈল অন্তর্দ্ধানে ৷  
দুইবিপ্র গলাগলি কান্দে প্রভুর গুণে ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

eteka kahiyā prabhu kaila antarddhāne  
so much  having said  the Lord  made  disappearance  
dui vipra galāgali kānde prabhura guṇe  
two  brāhmaṇas  embracing  weep  for the Lord’s  qualities

(149) Having said this, the Lord disappeared, and the two brāhmaṇas wept in each other’s arms, remembering the Lord’s qualities.

‘বাসুদেবোদ্ধার’ এই কহিল আখ্যান ৷  
‘বাসুদেবামৃতপ্রদ’ হৈল প্রভুর নাম ॥ ১৫০ ॥

‘vāsudevoddhāra’ ei kahila ākhyāna  
‘Vāsudeva’s deliverance’  this  I have told  the story  
‘vāsudevāmṛta-prada’ haila prabhura nāma  
‘the Giver of nectar to Vāsudeva’  became  the Lord’s  name

(150) Thus I have told the story of Vāsudeva’s deliverance, through which the Lord received the name Vāsudevāmṛta-prada, “the Giver of nectar to Vāsudeva”.

এই ত’ কহিল প্রভুর প্রথম গমন ৷  
কূর্ম্ম-দরশন, বাসুদেব-বিমোচন ॥ ১৫১ ॥

ei ta’ kahila prabhura prathama gamana  
this  indeed  I have told  the Lord’s  first  journey  
kūrma-daraśana, vāsudeva-vimocana  
the sight of Kūrma  Vāsudeva’s deliverance

(151) Thus I have told of the beginning of the Lord’s journey: His visit to Kūrma and the deliverance of Vāsudeva.

শ্রদ্ধা করি’ এই লীলা যে করে শ্রবণ ৷  
অচিরাতে মিলয়ে তারে চৈতন্য-চরণ ॥ ১৫২ ॥

śraddhā kari’ ei līlā ye kare śravaṇa  
faith  having  this  pastime  who  makes  hearing  
acirāte milaye tāre caitanya-caraṇa  
soon  obtains  he  Caitanya’s feet

(152) Whoever hears this pastime with faith soon attains the feet of Caitanya.

চৈতন্যলীলার আদি-অন্ত নাহি জানি ৷  
সেই লিখি, যেই মহান্তের মুখে শুনি ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

caitanya-līlāra ādi-anta nāhi jāni  
of Caitanya’s pastimes  beginning and end  not  I know  
sei likhi, yei mahāntera mukhe śuni  
that  I write  what  of the great souls  from the mouths  I hear

(153) I do not know the beginning or the end of Caitanya’s pastimes; I write only what I have heard from the lips of great souls.

ইথে অপরাধ মোর না লইও, ভক্তগণ ৷  
তোমা-সবার চরণ — মোর একান্ত শরণ ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

ithe aparādha mora nā laio, bhakta-gaṇa  
in this  offence  my  not  take  O devotees  
tomā-sabāra caraṇa — mora ekānta śaraṇa  
of you all  the feet  my  sole  refuge

(154) O devotees, do not hold my offences in this against me: your feet are my only refuge.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(155) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^2]: The same verse as Ādi 4.2; translation as there.

[^3]: Heading in the edition: the Lord wishes to go alone, so that under the pretext of searching for His elder brother Viśvarūpa He may deliver the south.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu knew well that Viśvarūpa had attained perfection (*siddhi*) long before; He only pretended to go in search of him in order to deliver the south. *Anubhāṣya*: see Madhya 9.299–300.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: *haṭha-raṅge* — “into the clutches of a cheat or swindler”.

[^6]: Heading in the edition: under the guise of feigned reproach the Lord glorifies the qualities of Nityānanda, Jagadānanda, Dāmodara and others.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: a sannyāsī is the guru of a brahmacārī, so it is not fitting for a brahmacārī to instruct a sannyāsī.

[^8]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *doṣāropa-chale*. *Anubhāṣya*: the very qualities by which the Lord was controlled He deliberately called “faults”, and so made known the greatness of His devotees.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: this brāhmaṇa, Kālā Kṛṣṇadāsa, is not the Kālā Kṛṣṇadāsa of Ādi 11.37 (an eternal friend from Vraja, one of the twelve gopālas); this brāhmaṇa later went to Gauḍa (Madhya 10.62–74).

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: *leuṭi* — the western (Hindi) word *lauṭ*, “returning”.

[^11]: Her story is told in Madhya 15 (Ṣāṭhī, Sārvabhauma’s daughter, and her husband Amogha).

[^12]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the road south runs along the seashore; six krośas from Purī lies the village of Ālālanātha, where the Deity of four-armed Vāsudeva, Ālālanātha, resides.

[^13]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *adhikārī* — the chief royal official; Vidyānagara is now called Porabandar.

[^14]: *Anubhāṣya*: the karaṇa caste, into which Rāmānanda was born, was known in Utkala society as “śaukra-śūdras” (śūdras by birth); yet in truth he was a brāhmaṇa — indeed a Vaiṣṇava paramahaṁsa, the guru of brāhmaṇas. And though to the world he was a royal servant, a *viṣayī*, in truth he was a sannyāsī of the highest kind.

[^15]: Heading in the edition: formerly the Bhaṭṭācārya mocked the Vaiṣṇava, thinking him lower than a smārta. *Anubhāṣya*: so the jñānīs and karmīs who turn away from Caitanya mock a Vaiṣṇava: for them sense perception and inference are everything, while the Vaiṣṇava relies on revealed testimony (*śabda-pramāṇa*) and follows the path of *śrauta*.

[^16]: Heading in the edition: later, by Caitanya’s mercy, the Bhaṭṭācārya realised the greatness of the spiritual Vaiṣṇava.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: here the Lord honours a Vaiṣṇava householder: a pure Vaiṣṇava gṛhastha who has taken shelter at Śrī Caitanya’s feet is worthy of worship even from a sannyāsī and is his guru; this the Lord showed by asking Sārvabhauma for his blessing.

[^18]: Heading in the edition: as contradictory attributes meet in Bhagavān, so His devotee is both soft and hard; the verse is from Bhavabhūti’s *Uttara-rāma-carita* (Act 3, verse 27).

[^19]: Rāghava — the descendant of Raghu; Keśava — the slayer of Keśī. The vocative *he* that ends the first two lines is rendered by “O” at their beginning. Heading in the edition: the verse sung by the Lord Himself. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *rakṣa mām* — “protect me”; *pāhi mām* — “keep me”.

[^20]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the essence of the *hlādinī* potency and the essence of the *saṁvit* potency together form *bhakti-śakti*, the power of devotion; whoever receives it by the mercy of Kṛṣṇa or His devotee becomes a supreme devotee. On whomever Mahāprabhu bestowed His mercy, He invested this power and entrusted him with preaching the Vaiṣṇava dharma.

[^21]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: although Navadvīpa is a *dhāma*, the *nyāya* and *smṛti* śāstras were then especially strong there, and among their teachers many had turned away from the Lord; to deliver them the Lord showed no special power.
