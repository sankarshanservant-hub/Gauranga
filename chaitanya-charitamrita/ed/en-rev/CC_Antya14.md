“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 14  
**A Description of the Divine Madness: the Lord Hastens to Caṭaka Hill**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

কৃষ্ণবিচ্ছেদবিভ্রান্ত্যা মনসা বপুষা ধিয়া ৷  
যদ্যদ্ব্যধত্ত গৌরাঙ্গস্তল্লেশঃ কথ্যতেঽধুনা ॥ ১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-viccheda-vibhrāntyā  
Kṛṣṇa  from separation  through the bewilderment  
manasā vapuṣā dhiyā  
with the mind  with the body  with the intelligence  
yad yad vyadhatta gaurāṅgas  
whatever  whatever  did  Gaurāṅga  
tal-leśaḥ kathyate ’dhunā  
of that  a particle  is told  now

(1) Now I shall tell a small part of all that Gaurāṅga did with His mind, body and intelligence in the bewilderment born of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
জয় জয় গৌরচন্দ্র ভক্তগণ-প্রাণ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya svayaṁ bhagavān  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  Himself  the Supreme Lord  
jaya jaya gauracandra bhakta-gaṇa-prāṇa  
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  of the devotees  the life

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya, the Supreme Lord Himself! Glory, glory to Gauracandra, the life of His devotees!

জয় জয় নিত্যানন্দ চৈতন্য-জীবন ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতাচার্য্য জয় গৌরপ্রিয়তম ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya nityānanda caitanya-jīvana  
glory  glory  to Nityānanda  Caitanya’s  life  
jayādvaitācārya jaya gaura-priyatama  
glory to Advaitācārya  glory  to Gaura  most dear

(3) Glory, glory to Nityānanda, the life of Caitanya! Glory to Advaitācārya, most dear to Gaura!

জয় স্বরূপ, শ্রীবাসাদি প্রভুভক্তগণ ৷  
শক্তি দেহ’, — করি যেন চৈতন্য বর্ণন ॥ ৪ ॥

jaya svarūpa, śrīvāsādi prabhu-bhakta-gaṇa  
glory  to Svarūpa  to Śrīvāsa and the others  the Lord’s  devotees  
śakti deha’, — kari yena caitanya varṇana  
strength  give  I may do  so that  of Caitanya  the description

(4) Glory to Svarūpa, to Śrīvāsa and to all the Lord’s devotees! Grant me the strength to describe Śrī Caitanya.[^1]

প্রভুর বিরহোন্মাদ ভাব গম্ভীর ৷  
বুঝিতে না পারে কেহ, যদ্যপি হয় ‘ধীর’ ॥ ৫ ॥

prabhura virahonmāda bhāva gambhīra  
the Lord’s  madness of separation  mood  profound  
bujhite nā pāre keha, yadyapi haya ‘dhīra’  
to understand  not  can  anyone  even if  he is  wise

(5) The Lord’s madness in separation is a mood so profound that no one can understand it, however wise he may be.[^2]

বুঝিতে না পারি যাহা, বর্ণিতে কে পারে ?  
সেই বুঝে, বর্ণে, চৈতন্য শক্তি দেন যাঁরে ॥ ৬ ॥

bujhite nā pāri yāhā, varṇite ke pāre?  
to understand  not  we can  what  to describe  who  can  
sei bujhe, varṇe, caitanya śakti dena yām̐re  
he  understands  describes  Caitanya  power  gives  to whom

(6) Who can describe what we cannot even understand? Only one to whom Caitanya gives the power can understand it and describe it.

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি আর রঘুনাথ-দাস ৷  
এই দুইর কড়চাতে এ-লীলা প্রকাশ ॥ ৭ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi āra raghunātha-dāsa  
Svarūpa Gosāñi  and  Raghunātha dāsa  
ei duira kaḍacāte e-līlā prakāśa  
of these  two  in the diaries  this pastime  is revealed

(7) These pastimes are revealed in the diaries (*kaḍacā*) of these two, Svarūpa Gosāñi and Raghunātha dāsa.

সেকালে এ দুই রহেন মহাপ্রভুর পাশে ৷  
আর সব কড়চা-কর্ত্তা রহেন দূরদেশে ॥ ৮ ॥

se-kāle e dui rahena mahāprabhura pāśe  
at that time  these  two  stayed  Mahāprabhu’s  at the side  
āra saba kaḍacā-kartā rahena dūra-deśe  
other  all  diary-writers  stayed  in distant lands

(8) At that time these two lived at Mahāprabhu’s side, while all the other diarists lived in distant places.

ক্ষণে ক্ষণে অনুভবি’ এই দুইজন ৷  
সংক্ষেপে বাহুল্যে করেন কড়চা-গ্রন্থন ॥ ৯ ॥

kṣaṇe kṣaṇe anubhavi’ ei duijana  
moment  by moment  experiencing  these  two  
saṅkṣepe bāhulye karena kaḍacā-granthana  
briefly  at length  make  diary  composition

(9) Experiencing His pastimes moment by moment, these two recorded them in their diaries, sometimes briefly and sometimes at length.[^3]

স্বরূপ — ‘সূত্রকর্ত্তা’, রঘুনাথ — ‘বৃত্তিকার’ ৷  
তার বাহুল্য বর্ণি পাঁজি-টীকা-ব্যবহার ॥ ১০ ॥

svarūpa — ‘sūtra-kartā’, raghunātha — ‘vṛttikāra’  
Svarūpa  the author of the sūtras  Raghunātha  the author of the explanation  
tāra bāhulya varṇi pām̐ji-ṭīkā-vyavahāra  
their  expansion  I describe  of a pā̃ji-ṭīkā  in the manner

(10) Svarūpa is the author of the *sūtras*, and Raghunātha the author of their explanation (*vṛtti*). I describe them more fully, in the manner of a *pā̃ji-ṭīkā* (an introductory commentary).[^4]

তাতে বিশ্বাস করি’ শুন ভাবের বর্ণন ৷  
হইবে ভাবের জ্ঞান, পাইবা প্রেমধন ॥ ১১ ॥

tāte viśvāsa kari’ śuna bhāvera varṇana  
in that  faith  placing  hear  of the emotions  the description  
haibe bhāvera jñāna, pāibā prema-dhana  
will arise  of the emotions  understanding  you will gain  of love  the treasure

(11) Hear with faith this description of His feelings (*bhāva*): you will come to understand them and gain the treasure of prema.

কৃষ্ণ মথুরায় গেলে, গোপীর যে দশা হৈল ৷  
কৃষ্ণবিচ্ছেদে প্রভুর সে দশা উপজিল ॥ ১২ ॥

kṛṣṇa mathurāya gele, gopīra ye daśā haila  
Kṛṣṇa  to Mathurā  when He went  of the gopīs  which  state  arose  
kṛṣṇa-vicchede prabhura se daśā upajila  
Kṛṣṇa  in separation from  in the Lord  that  state  arose

(12) The state that came upon the gopīs when Kṛṣṇa went to Mathurā arose in the Lord in His separation from Kṛṣṇa.

উদ্ধব-দর্শনে যৈছে রাধার বিলাপ ৷  
ক্রমে ক্রমে হৈল প্রভুর উন্মাদ-বিলাপ ॥ ১৩ ॥

uddhava-darśane yaiche rādhāra vilāpa  
Uddhava  on seeing  as  Rādhā’s  lament  
krame krame haila prabhura unmāda-vilāpa  
gradually  arose  the Lord’s  lament of madness

(13) As Rādhā lamented when she saw Uddhava, so little by little the Lord too began to lament in His madness.[^5]

রাধিকার ভাবে প্রভুর সদা ‘অভিমান’ ৷  
সেই ভাবে আপনাকে হয় ‘রাধা’-জ্ঞান ॥ ১৪ ॥

rādhikāra bhāve prabhura sadā ‘abhimāna’  
of Rādhikā  in the mood  the Lord’s  always  self-conception  
sei bhāve āpanāke haya ‘rādhā’-jñāna  
in that  mood  of Himself  there is  the sense of being Rādhā

(14) In the mood of Rādhikā the Lord always had her self-conception (*abhimāna*); in that mood He thought of Himself as Rādhā.[^6]

দিব্যোন্মাদে ঐছে হয়, কি ইহা বিস্ময় ?  
অধিরূঢ়-ভাবে দিব্যোন্মাদ-প্রলাপ হয় ॥ ১৫ ॥

divyonmāde aiche haya, ki ihā vismaya?  
in divine madness  thus  it is  what  in this  wonder  
adhirūḍha-bhāve divyonmāda-pralāpa haya  
in adhirūḍha-bhāva  of divine madness  incoherent talk  occurs

(15) Such is divine madness (*divyonmāda*) — why wonder at it? In *adhirūḍha-bhāva*, the highest stage of love, divine madness pours itself out in incoherent words.[^7]

এতস্য মোহনাখ্যস্য গতিং কামপ্যুপেয়ুষঃ ৷  
ভ্রমাভা কাপি বৈচিত্রী দিব্যোন্মাদ ইতীর্য্যতে ৷  
উদ্ঘূর্ণা-চিত্রজল্পাদ্যাস্তদ্ভেদা বহবো মতাঃ ॥ ১৬ ॥

etasya mohanākhyasya  
of this  called mohana  
gatiṁ kām apy upeyuṣaḥ  
a state  some  indescribable  having reached  
bhramābhā kāpi vaicitrī  
resembling bewilderment  some  wondrous display  
divyonmāda itīryate  
divine madness  thus  is called  
udghūrṇā-citra-jalpādyās  
udghūrṇā  citra-jalpa  and others  
tad-bhedā bahavo matāḥ  
its varieties  many  are held

(16) “When the *bhāva* called *mohana* reaches a certain indescribable state, there appears a wondrous display resembling bewilderment; it is called divine madness (*divyonmāda*). It is held to have many varieties, such as *udghūrṇā* and *citra-jalpa*.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi, 14.190)

একদিন মহাপ্রভু কৈরাছেন শয়ন ৷  
কৃষ্ণ রাসলীলা করে, — দেখিলা স্বপন ॥ ১৭ ॥

ekadina mahāprabhu kairāchena śayana  
one day  Mahāprabhu  had lain down  to rest  
kṛṣṇa rāsa-līlā kare, — dekhilā svapana  
Kṛṣṇa  the rāsa-līlā  performs  He saw  a dream

(17) One day, as Mahāprabhu lay asleep, He saw Kṛṣṇa’s rāsa-līlā in a dream.

ত্রিভঙ্গ-সুন্দর-দেহ, মুরলীবদন ৷  
পীতাম্বর, বনমালা, মদনমোহন ॥ ১৮ ॥

tribhaṅga-sundara-deha, muralīvadana  
bent in three places  beautiful  body  with the flute at His lips  
pītāmbara, vana-mālā, madana-mohana  
yellow garment  forest garland  of Madana  the enchanter

(18) His beautiful form was bent in three places, the flute was at His lips, and He wore yellow cloth and a garland of forest flowers — the enchanter of Madana, the god of love.

মণ্ডলীবন্ধে গোপীগণ করেন নর্ত্তন ৷  
মধ্যে রাধা-সহ নাচে ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ১৯ ॥

maṇḍalī-bandhe gopī-gaṇa karena nartana  
in a circle  the gopīs  were  dancing  
madhye rādhā-saha nāce vrajendra-nandana  
in the middle  with Rādhā  dances  of the king of Vraja  the son

(19) The gopīs danced in a circle, and in the middle the son of the king of Vraja danced with Rādhā.

দেখি’ প্রভু সেই রসে আবিষ্ট হৈলা ৷  
‘বৃন্দাবনে কৃষ্ণ পাইনু’ — এই জ্ঞান কৈলা ॥ ২০ ॥

dekhi’ prabhu sei rase āviṣṭa hailā  
seeing  the Lord  in that  rasa  absorbed  became  
‘vṛndāvane kṛṣṇa pāinu’ — ei jñāna kailā  
in Vṛndāvana  Kṛṣṇa  I have found  this  thought  He had

(20) Seeing this, the Lord became absorbed in that rasa and thought, “I have found Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana!”

প্রভুর বিলম্ব দেখি’ গোবিন্দ জাগাইলা ৷  
জাগিলে ‘স্বপ্ন’-জ্ঞান হৈল, প্রভু দুঃখী হৈলা ॥ ২১ ॥

prabhura vilamba dekhi’ govinda jāgāilā  
the Lord’s  lateness  seeing  Govinda  woke Him  
jāgile ‘svapna’-jñāna haila, prabhu duḥkhī hailā  
on waking  that it was a dream  He understood  the Lord  sorrowful  became

(21) When Govinda saw that the Lord was late in rising, he woke Him. On waking, the Lord understood that it had been a dream, and He was filled with sorrow.

দেহাভ্যাসে নিত্যকৃত্য করি’ সমাপন ৷  
কালে যাই’ কৈলা জগন্নাথ দরশন ॥ ২২ ॥

dehābhyāse nitya-kṛtya kari’ samāpana  
by bodily habit  the daily duties  having  completed  
kāle yāi’ kailā jagannātha daraśana  
at the proper time  going  He made  Jagannātha’s  darśana

(22) Out of bodily habit He completed His daily duties, and at the proper time He went to see Jagannātha.

যাবৎ কাল দর্শন করেন গরুড়ের পাছে ৷  
প্রভুর আগে দর্শন করে লোক লাখে লাখে ॥ ২৩ ॥

yāvat kāla darśana karena garuḍera pāche  
for as long as  darśana  He takes  of Garuḍa  behind  
prabhura āge darśana kare loka lākhe lākhe  
the Lord’s  in front  darśana  take  people  by hundreds of thousands

(23) He would behold Jagannātha from behind the Garuḍa pillar, while in front of Him people in their hundreds of thousands took darśana.

ওড়িয়া এক স্ত্রী ভীড়ে দর্শন না পাঞা ৷  
গরুড়ে চড়ি’ দেখে প্রভুর স্কন্ধে পদ দিয়া ॥ ২৪ ॥

oḍiyā eka strī bhīḍe darśana nā pāñā  
an Oriyā  one  woman  in the crowd  sight  not  getting  
garuḍe caḍi’ dekhe prabhura skandhe pada diyā  
onto Garuḍa  climbing  looks  on the Lord’s  shoulder  foot  placing

(24) An Oriyā woman who could not see through the crowd climbed onto the Garuḍa pillar and, with her foot on the Lord’s shoulder, gazed at Jagannātha.

দেখিয়া গোবিন্দ ব্যস্তে সেই স্ত্রীরে বর্জ্জিলা ৷  
তারে নামাইতে প্রভু গোবিন্দে নিষেধিলা ॥ ২৫ ॥

dekhiyā govinda vyaste sei strīre varjilā  
seeing (this)  Govinda  hastily  that  woman  put off  
tāre nāmāite prabhu govinde niṣedhilā  
her  to bring down  the Lord  Govinda  forbade

(25) Seeing this, Govinda hastily tried to pull her down, but the Lord forbade him.[^8]

‘আদিবস্যা’ এই স্ত্রীরে না কর বর্জ্জন ৷  
করুক যথেষ্ট জগন্নাথ দরশন ॥ ২৬ ॥

‘ādi-vasyā’ ei strīre nā kara varjana  
ādivasyā  this  woman  do not  make  removal  
karuka yatheṣṭa jagannātha daraśana  
let her make  as she wishes  Jagannātha’s  darśana

(26) “Foolish fellow (*ādivasyā*)! Do not remove this woman. Let her look at Jagannātha as much as she wishes.”[^9]

আস্তে-ব্যস্তে সেই নারী ভূমেতে নামিলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভুরে দেখি’ তাঁর চরণ বন্দিলা ॥ ২৭ ॥

āste-vyaste sei nārī bhūmete nāmilā  
hurriedly  that  woman  to the ground  came down  
mahāprabhure dekhi’ tām̐ra caraṇa vandilā  
Mahāprabhu  seeing  His  feet  she honored

(27) The woman hurriedly climbed down, and when she saw Mahāprabhu she bowed at His feet.

তার আর্ত্তি দেখি’ প্রভু কহিতে লাগিলা ৷  
এত আর্ত্তি জগন্নাথ মোরে নাহি দিলা ! ॥ ২৮ ॥

tāra ārti dekhi’ prabhu kahite lāgilā  
her  eagerness  seeing  the Lord  to speak  began  
eta ārti jagannātha more nāhi dilā!  
such  eagerness  Jagannātha  to Me  not  has given

(28) Seeing her eagerness, the Lord began to speak: “Jagannātha has not given Me such eagerness![^10]

জগন্নাথে আবিষ্ট ইহার তনু-মন-প্রাণে ৷  
মোর স্কন্ধে পদ দিয়াছে, তাহা নাহি জানে ॥ ২৯ ॥

jagannāthe āviṣṭa ihāra tanu-mana-prāṇe  
in Jagannātha  absorbed  her  body  mind  and life  
mora skandhe pada diyāche, tāhā nāhi jāne  
on My  shoulder  foot  she has placed  that  not  she knows

(29) Her body, mind and life are absorbed in Jagannātha; she does not even know that she has put her foot on My shoulder.[^11]

অহো ভাগ্যবতী এই, বন্দি ইহার পায় ৷  
ইহার প্রসাদে ঐছে আর্ত্তি আমার বা হয় ॥ ৩০ ॥

aho bhāgyavatī ei, vandi ihāra pāya  
oh  fortunate  is she  I honor  her  feet  
ihāra prasāde aiche ārti āmāra vā haya  
her  by the grace  such  eagerness  in Me  may  arise

(30) Oh, how fortunate she is! I bow to her feet. By her grace may such eagerness arise in Me too.

পূর্ব্বে আমি যবে কৈলুঁ জগন্নাথ দরশন ৷  
জগন্নাথে দেখি — সাক্ষাৎ ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ৩১ ॥

pūrve āmi yabe kailum̐ jagannātha daraśana  
before  I  when  made  Jagannātha’s  darśana  
jagannāthe dekhi — sākṣāt vrajendra-nandana  
in Jagannātha  I saw  directly  of the king of Vraja  the son

(31) Before, whenever I beheld Jagannātha, I saw in Him the son of the king of Vraja Himself.

স্বপ্নের দর্শনাবেশে তদ্রূপ হৈল মন ৷  
যাঁহা তাঁহা দেখি সর্ব্বত্র মুরলী-বদন ॥ ৩২ ॥

svapnera darśanāveśe tad-rūpa haila mana  
of the dream  in absorption in the vision  like that  became  the mind  
yām̐hā tām̐hā dekhi sarvatra muralī-vadana  
wherever  I look  everywhere  the One with the flute at His lips

(32) Absorbed in the vision of My dream, My mind has stayed in that state, and wherever I look I see the One with the flute at His lips.”[^12]

এবে যদি স্ত্রীরে দেখি’ প্রভুর বাহ্য হৈল ৷  
জগন্নাথ-সুভদ্রা-বলরামের স্বরূপ দেখিল ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ebe yadi strīre dekhi’ prabhura bāhya haila  
now  when  the woman  seeing  the Lord’s  external consciousness  came  
jagannātha-subhadrā-balarāmera svarūpa dekhila  
of Jagannātha  Subhadrā  and Balarāma  the forms  He saw

(33) But now, at the sight of the woman, the Lord came back to outward consciousness and saw the forms of Jagannātha, Subhadrā and Balarāma.

কুরুক্ষেত্রে দেখি’ কৃষ্ণে ঐছে হৈল মন ৷  
কাঁহা কুরুক্ষেত্রে আইলাঙ, কাঁহা বৃন্দাবন ? ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kurukṣetre dekhi’ kṛṣṇe aiche haila mana  
at Kurukṣetra  seeing  Kṛṣṇa  so  became  His mind  
kām̐hā kurukṣetre āilāṅa, kām̐hā vṛndāvana?  
where  to Kurukṣetra  I have come  where  Vṛndāvana

(34) His mind became like that of one who sees Kṛṣṇa at Kurukṣetra: “How have I come to Kurukṣetra? And where is Vṛndāvana?”[^13]

প্রাপ্তরত্ন হারাঞা ঐছে ব্যগ্র হইলা ৷  
বিষণ্ণ হঞা প্রভু নিজ-বাসা আইলা ॥ ৩৫ ॥

prāpta-ratna hārāñā aiche vyagra hailā  
a found jewel  losing  so  agitated  He became  
viṣaṇṇa hañā prabhu nija-vāsā āilā  
dejected  becoming  the Lord  to His own  residence  came

(35) Like one who has lost a jewel he had just found, He was in turmoil, and He returned, dejected, to His residence.

ভূমির উপর বসি’ নিজ-নখে ভূমি লিখে ৷  
অশ্রু-গঙ্গা নেত্রে বহে, কিছুই না দেখে ॥ ৩৬ ॥

bhūmira upara vasi’ nija-nakhe bhūmi likhe  
the ground  upon  sitting  with His own nails  the ground  He scratches  
aśru-gaṅgā netre vahe, kichui nā dekhe  
a Ganges of tears  from the eyes  flows  anything  not  He sees

(36) Sitting on the ground, He scratched the ground with His nails; a Ganges of tears flowed from His eyes, and He saw nothing at all.

পাইনু বৃন্দাবননাথ, পুনঃ হারাইনু ৷  
কে মোর নিলেক কৃষ্ণ? কাঁহা মুই আইনু ? ॥ ৩৭ ॥

pāinu vṛndāvana-nātha, punaḥ hārāinu  
I found  of Vṛndāvana  the Lord  again  I lost  
ke mora nileka kṛṣṇa? kām̐hā mui āinu?  
who  My  has taken  Kṛṣṇa  where  I  have come

(37) “I found the Lord of Vṛndāvana, and I have lost Him again! Who has taken My Kṛṣṇa? Where am I now?”

স্বপ্নাবেশে প্রেমে প্রভুর গর গর মন ৷  
বাহ্য হৈলে হয়, — যেন হারাইল ধন ॥ ৩৮ ॥

svapnāveśe preme prabhura gara gara mana  
in absorption in the dream  with love  the Lord’s  overflowing  mind  
bāhya haile haya, — yena hārāila dhana  
external consciousness  when it comes  it is  as if  He lost  a treasure

(38) Absorbed in the dream, the Lord’s mind overflowed with love; when external consciousness returned, it was as if He had lost a treasure.

উন্মত্তের প্রায় প্রভু করেন গান-নৃত্য ৷  
দেহের স্বভাবে করেন স্নান-ভোজন-কৃত্য ॥ ৩৯ ॥

unmattera prāya prabhu karena gāna-nṛtya  
a madman  like  the Lord  does  singing  dancing  
dehera svabhāve karena snāna-bhojana-kṛtya  
of the body  by the nature  He does  bathing  eating  duties

(39) Like a madman the Lord sang and danced; He bathed and ate merely out of bodily habit.

রাত্রি হৈলে স্বরূপ-রামানন্দে লঞা ৷  
আপন মনের ভাব কহে উঘাড়িয়া ॥ ৪০ ॥

rātri haile svarūpa-rāmānande lañā  
night  when it came  Svarūpa  and Rāmānanda  taking  
āpana manera bhāva kahe ughāḍiyā  
His own  heart’s  feelings  He tells  opening

(40) When night came, He would sit with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda and open to them the feelings of His heart.

প্রাপ্তপ্রণষ্টাচ্যুতবিত্ত আত্মা  
যয়ৌ বিষাদোজ্ঝিত-দেহগেহঃ ৷  
গৃহীতকাপালিকধর্ম্মকো মে  
বৃন্দাবনং সেন্দ্রিয়শিষ্যবৃন্দঃ ॥ ৪১ ॥

prāpta-praṇaṣṭācyuta-vitta ātmā  
having found  having lost  Acyuta  the wealth  My mind  
yayau viṣādojjhita-deha-gehaḥ  
went  out of grief  abandoning  the body  the house  
gṛhīta-kāpālika-dharmako me  
having taken up  a kāpālika’s  ways  My  
vṛndāvanaṁ sendriya-śiṣya-vṛndaḥ  
to Vṛndāvana  with the senses  the disciples  the band

(41) “My mind found the wealth of Acyuta and lost it again. In its grief it abandoned its house, the body, took up the ways of a *kāpālika* yogī, and went off to Vṛndāvana with its band of disciples, the senses.”[^14]

প্রাপ্তরত্ন হারাঞা,  তার গুণ সঙরিয়া,  
মহাপ্রভু সন্তাপে বিহ্বল ৷  
রায়-স্বরূপের কণ্ঠ ধরি’,  কহে হাহা ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’,  
ধৈর্য্য গেল, হইলা চপল ॥ ৪২ ॥

prāptaratna hārāñā,  
a found jewel  having lost  
tāra guṇa saṅariyā,  
its  qualities  remembering  
mahāprabhu santāpe vihvala  
Mahāprabhu  with anguish  overwhelmed  
rāya-svarūpera kaṇṭha dhari’,  
of Rāya and Svarūpa  the necks  clasping  
kahe hāhā ‘hari’ ‘hari’,  
He says  alas  Hari  Hari  
dhairya gela, hailā capala  
composure  went  He became  restless

(42) Having lost the jewel He had found and remembering its qualities, Mahāprabhu was overwhelmed with anguish. Clasping Rāya and Svarūpa by the neck, He cried, “Alas! Hari! Hari!” His composure gone, He became restless.

শুন, বান্ধব, কৃষ্ণের মাধুরী ৷  
যার লোভে মোর মন, ছাড়ি’ লোক-বেদধর্ম্ম,  
যোগী হঞা হইল ভিখারী ॥ ৪৩ ॥

śuna, bāndhava, kṛṣṇera mādhurī  
hear  friends  of Kṛṣṇa  the sweetness  
yāra lobhe mora mana,  
for which  in greed  My  mind  
chāḍi’ loka-veda-dharma,  
giving up  of society  of the Vedas  the duties  
yogī hañā haila bhikhārī  
a yogī  becoming  became  a beggar

(43) “Hear, My friends, of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness! Craving it, My mind has given up the duties of society and of the Vedas, become a yogī and turned beggar.

কৃষ্ণলীলা-মণ্ডল, শুদ্ধ শঙ্খকুণ্ডল,  
গড়িয়াছে শুক কারিকর ৷  
সেই কুণ্ডল কাণে পরি’, তৃষ্ণা-লাউ-থালী ধরি’,  
আশা-ঝুলি কান্ধের উপর ॥ ৪৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-līlā-maṇḍala,  
of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  the circle  
śuddha śaṅkha-kuṇḍala,  
pure  conch-shell  earrings  
gaḍiyāche śuka kārikara  
has fashioned  Śuka  the craftsman  
sei kuṇḍala kāṇe pari’,  
those  earrings  in the ears  wearing  
tṛṣṇā-lāu-thālī dhari’,  
of thirst  the gourd  bowl  holding  
āśā-jhuli kāndhera upara  
of hope  the bag  the shoulder  upon

(44) My mind’s earrings of pure conch-shell are the circle of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, fashioned by Śuka the craftsman. Wearing those earrings, it holds the gourd-bowl of thirst, and the bag of hope hangs from its shoulder.[^15]

চিন্তা-কান্থা উড়ি’ গায়, ধূলি-বিভূতি-মলিন-কায়,  
‘হাহা কৃষ্ণ’ প্রলাপ-উত্তর ৷  
উদ্বেগ দ্বাদশ হাতে, লোভের ঝুলি নিল মাথে,  
ভিক্ষাভাবে ক্ষীণ কলেবর ॥ ৪৫ ॥

cintā-kānthā uḍi’ gāya,  
of anxiety  the patched quilt  wrapping  about the body  
dhūli-vibhūti-malina-kāya,  
with dust  ash  soiled  body  
‘hāhā kṛṣṇa’ pralāpa-uttara  
alas  Kṛṣṇa  raving  the answer  
udvega dvādaśa hāte,  
agitation  twelve (bangles)  on the arm  
lobhera jhuli nila māthe,  
of greed  the bag  it took  on the head  
bhikṣābhāve kṣīṇa kalevara  
for lack of alms  thin  the body

(45) The patched quilt of anxiety is wrapped about it, its body is grey with dust and ash, and to every word it answers with the raving cry, “Alas, Kṛṣṇa!” On its arm are the twelve bangles of agitation, on its head the bag of longing, and for want of alms its body has wasted away.[^16]

ব্যাস, শুকাদি যোগিগণ, কৃষ্ণ আত্মা নিরঞ্জন,  
ব্রজে তাঁর যত লীলাগণ ৷  
ভাগবতাদি শাস্ত্রগণে, করিয়াছে বর্ণনে,  
সেই তর্জ্জা পড়ে অনুক্ষণ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

vyāsa, śukādi yogi-gaṇa,  
Vyāsa  Śuka and the other  yogīs  
kṛṣṇa ātmā nirañjana,  
Kṛṣṇa  the Self  stainless  
vraje tām̐ra yata līlā-gaṇa  
in Vraja  His  all  the pastimes  
bhāgavatādi śāstra-gaṇe,  
in the Bhāgavata and other  scriptures  
kariyāche varṇane,  
have made  description  
sei tarjā paḍe anukṣaṇa  
those  songs  it recites  constantly

(46) The yogīs Vyāsa, Śuka and others have described in the *Bhāgavata* and other scriptures all the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa, the stainless Self, in Vraja; those songs (*tarjā*) it recites without cease.

দশেন্দ্রিয়ে শিষ্য করি’, ‘মহা-বাউল’ নাম ধরি’,  
শিষ্য লঞা করিল গমন ৷  
মোর দেহ স্ব-সদন, বিষয়-ভোগ মহাধন,  
সব ছাড়ি’ গেলা বৃন্দাবন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

daśendriye śiṣya kari’,  
the ten senses  disciples  making  
‘mahā-bāula’ nāma dhari’,  
Mahā-bāul  the name  taking  
śiṣya lañā karila gamana  
the disciples  taking  it made  departure  
mora deha sva-sadana,  
My  body  its own  home  
viṣaya-bhoga mahādhana,  
sense enjoyment  the great wealth  
saba chāḍi’ gelā vṛndāvana  
all  leaving  it went  to Vṛndāvana

(47) Making the ten senses its disciples and taking the name “Mahā-bāul”, it set off with them. Leaving everything — its own home, My body, and the great wealth of sense enjoyment — it went to Vṛndāvana.[^17]

বৃন্দাবনে প্রজাগণ, যত স্থাবর-জঙ্গম,  
বৃক্ষ-লতা গৃহস্থ-আশ্রমে ৷  
তার ঘরে ভিক্ষাটন, ফল-মূল-পত্রাশন,  
এই বৃত্তি করে শিষ্যসনে ॥ ৪৮ ॥

vṛndāvane prajā-gaṇa,  
in Vṛndāvana  the subjects  
yata sthāvara-jaṅgama,  
all  the stationary  and moving  
vṛkṣa-latā gṛhastha-āśrame  
trees  creepers  in the householder  order  
tāra ghare bhikṣāṭana,  
in their  homes  begging  
phala-mūla-patrāśana,  
fruits  roots  leaves  eating  
ei vṛtti kare śiṣya-sane  
this  livelihood  it follows  with the disciples

(48) The subjects of Vṛndāvana — all beings, stationary and moving, the trees and creepers — live as householders. It begs in their homes and eats fruits, roots and leaves; this is how it lives with its disciples.

কৃষ্ণ-গুণ, রূপ, রস, গন্ধ, শব্দ, পরশ,  
সে সুধা আস্বাদে গোপীগণ ৷  
তা-সবার গ্রাস-শেষে, আনি’ পঞ্চেন্দ্রিয় শিষ্যে,  
সে ভিক্ষায় রাখেন জীবন ॥ ৪৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa-guṇa, rūpa, rasa,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  form  taste  
gandha, śabda, paraśa,  
fragrance  sound  touch  
se sudhā āsvāde gopī-gaṇa  
that  nectar  relish  the gopīs  
tā-sabāra grāsa-śeṣe,  
of them all  of the mouthfuls  in the remnants  
āni’ pañcendriya śiṣye,  
bringing  the five senses  the disciples  
se bhikṣāya rākhena jīvana  
by those  alms  they sustain  life

(49) The gopīs relish the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, form, taste, fragrance, sound and touch. The disciples, the five senses, bring the remnants of their meal, and on those alms they sustain their lives.

শূন্যকুঞ্জমণ্ডপ-কোণে, যোগাভ্যাস কৃষ্ণধ্যানে,  
তাঁহা রহে লঞা শিষ্যগণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ আত্মা নিরঞ্জন, সাক্ষাৎ দেখিতে মন,  
ধ্যানে রাত্রি করে জাগরণ ॥ ৫০ ॥

śūnya-kuñja-maṇḍapa-koṇe,  
empty  bower  pavilion  in a corner  
yogābhyāsa kṛṣṇa-dhyāne,  
yoga practice  on Kṛṣṇa  in meditation  
tām̐hā rahe lañā śiṣya-gaṇa  
there  it stays  with  the disciples  
kṛṣṇa ātmā nirañjana,  
Kṛṣṇa  the Self  stainless  
sākṣāt dekhite mana,  
directly  to see  the mind (longs)  
dhyāne rātri kare jāgaraṇa  
in meditation  the nights  it spends  awake

(50) In the corner of an empty bower-pavilion it stays with its disciples, practising yoga in meditation on Kṛṣṇa. Longing to see Kṛṣṇa, the stainless Self, face to face, it keeps vigil all night in meditation.[^18]

মন কৃষ্ণবিয়োগী, দুঃখে মন হৈল যোগী,  
সে বিয়োগে দশ দশা হয় ৷  
সে দশায় ব্যাকুল হঞা, মন গেল পলাঞা,  
শূন্য মোর শরীর আলয় ॥ ৫১ ॥

mana kṛṣṇa-viyogī,  
the mind  from Kṛṣṇa  separated  
duḥkhe mana haila yogī,  
in sorrow  the mind  became  a yogī  
se viyoge daśa daśā haya  
in that  separation  ten  states  arise  
se daśāya vyākula hañā,  
by those  states  distraught  becoming  
mana gela palāñā,  
the mind  went  fleeing  
śūnya mora śarīra ālaya  
empty  My  body  the dwelling

(51) Separated from Kṛṣṇa, My mind became a yogī in its sorrow, and in that separation the ten states came upon it. Overwhelmed by them, it fled, and its dwelling, My body, stands empty.”[^19]

কৃষ্ণের বিয়োগে গোপীর দশ দশা হয় ৷  
সেই দশ দশা হয় প্রভুর উদয় ॥ ৫২ ॥

kṛṣṇera viyoge gopīra daśa daśā haya  
Kṛṣṇa  in separation from  of the gopīs  ten  states  arise  
sei daśa daśā haya prabhura udaya  
those  ten  states  are  in the Lord  arisen

(52) In separation from Kṛṣṇa ten states arise in the gopīs; those same ten states arose in the Lord.

চিন্তাত্র জাগরোদ্বেগৌ তানবং মলিনাঙ্গতা ৷  
প্রলাপো ব্যাধিরুন্মাদো মোহো মৃত্যুর্দ্দশা দশ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

cintātra jāgarodvegau  
anxious thought  here  sleeplessness  agitation  
tānavaṁ malināṅgatā  
emaciation  uncleanliness of body  
pralāpo vyādhir unmādo  
incoherent talk  disease  madness  
moho mṛtyur daśā daśa  
fainting  death  states  ten

(53) “In this separation there are ten states: anxious thought, sleeplessness, agitation, emaciation, uncleanliness of body, incoherent talk, disease, madness, fainting and death.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi, 15.167)[^20]

এই দশ-দশায় প্রভু ব্যাকুল রাত্রিদিনে ৷  
কভু কোন দশা উঠে, স্থির নহে মনে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

ei daśa-daśāya prabhu vyākula rātridine  
by these  ten states  the Lord  distraught  night and day  
kabhu kona daśā uṭhe, sthira nahe mane  
sometimes  some  state  arises  steady  not  in mind

(54) Night and day the Lord was distraught by these ten states: now one state would arise, now another, and His mind found no rest.

এত কহি’ মহাপ্রভু মৌন হইলা ৷  
রামানন্দ-রায় শ্লোক পড়িতে লাগিলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

eta kahi’ mahāprabhu mauna hailā  
this  having said  Mahāprabhu  silent  became  
rāmānanda-rāya śloka paḍite lāgilā  
Rāmānanda Rāya  verses  to recite  began

(55) Having said this, Mahāprabhu fell silent, and Rāmānanda Rāya began to recite verses.[^21]

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি করে কৃষ্ণলীলা গান ৷  
দুই জনে কিছু কৈলা প্রভুর বাহ্য জ্ঞান ॥ ৫৬ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi kare kṛṣṇa-līlā gāna  
Svarūpa Gosāñi  does  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  singing  
dui jane kichu kailā prabhura bāhya jñāna  
the two  persons  somewhat  made  the Lord’s  external  awareness

(56) Svarūpa Gosāñi sang of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, and together the two brought the Lord somewhat back to external awareness.

এইমত অর্দ্ধরাত্রি কৈলা নির্যাপণ ৷  
ভিতর-প্রকোষ্ঠে প্রভুরে করাইলা শয়ন ॥ ৫৭ ॥

ei-mata ardha-rātri kailā niryāpaṇa  
in this way  half the night  they made  pass  
bhitara-prakoṣṭhe prabhure karāilā śayana  
in the inner  room  the Lord  they made  lie down

(57) In this way they passed half the night, and then they had the Lord lie down in the inner room.

রামানন্দ-রায় তবে গেলা নিজ ঘরে ৷  
স্বরূপ-গোবিন্দ দুঁহে শুইলেন দ্বারে ॥ ৫৮ ॥

rāmānanda-rāya tabe gelā nija ghare  
Rāmānanda Rāya  then  went  to his own  house  
svarūpa-govinda dum̐he śuilena dvāre  
Svarūpa  Govinda  both  lay down  at the door

(58) Rāmānanda Rāya then went home, and Svarūpa and Govinda both lay down at the door.

সব রাত্রি মহাপ্রভু করে জাগরণ ৷  
উচ্চ করি’ কহে কৃষ্ণনামসঙ্কীর্ত্তন ॥ ৫৯ ॥

saba rātri mahāprabhu kare jāgaraṇa  
all  night  Mahāprabhu  does  keeping awake  
ucca kari’ kahe kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṅkīrtana  
loudly  making  He utters  of Kṛṣṇa’s  names  saṅkīrtana

(59) Mahāprabhu stayed awake all night, loudly chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa.

শব্দ না পাঞা স্বরূপ কপাট কৈলা দূরে ৷  
তিনদ্বার দেওয়া আছে, প্রভু নাহি ঘরে ॥ ৬০ ॥

śabda nā pāñā svarūpa kapāṭa kailā dūre  
sound  not  getting  Svarūpa  the door  moved  away  
tinadvāra deoyā āche, prabhu nāhi ghare  
the three doors  shut  are  the Lord  not  in the room

(60) Hearing no sound, Svarūpa opened the door: the three doors were still shut, yet the Lord was not in the room.

চিন্তিত হইল সবে প্রভুরে না দেখিয়া ৷  
প্রভু চাহি’ বুলে সবে ব্যাকুল হঞা ॥ ৬১ ॥

cintita haila sabe prabhure nā dekhiyā  
anxious  became  all  the Lord  not  seeing  
prabhu cāhi’ bule sabe vyākula hañā  
the Lord  looking for  wander  all  distraught  becoming

(61) Not seeing the Lord, everyone grew anxious, and in great distress they all went about looking for Him.

সিংহদ্বারের উত্তর-দিশায় আছে এক ঠাঞি ৷  
তার মধ্যে পড়ি’ আছেন চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ॥ ৬২ ॥

siṁha-dvārera uttara-diśāya āche eka ṭhāñi  
of the Siṁha-dvāra  on the north side  there is  one  place  
tāra madhye paḍi’ āchena caitanya-gosāñi  
of it  in the midst  fallen  is  Caitanya  Gosāñi

(62) On the north side of the Siṁha-dvāra (the Lion Gate) there is a certain spot, and there Caitanya Gosāñi lay fallen.

দেখি’ স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি-আদি আনন্দিত হৈলা ৷  
প্রভুর দশা দেখি’ পুনঃ চিন্তিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৬৩ ॥

dekhi’ svarūpa-gosāñi-ādi ānandita hailā  
seeing  Svarūpa Gosāñi and the others  joyful  became  
prabhura daśā dekhi’ punaḥ cintite lāgilā  
of the Lord  the condition  seeing  again  to worry  began

(63) Finding Him, Svarūpa Gosāñi and the others were overjoyed; but when they saw the Lord’s condition, they grew anxious again.

প্রভু পড়ি’ আছেন দীর্ঘ হাত পাঁচ-ছয় ৷  
অচেতন দেহ, নাসায় শ্বাস নাহি বয় ॥ ৬৪ ॥

prabhu paḍi’ āchena dīrgha hāta pām̐ca-chaya  
the Lord  fallen  is  long  cubits  five or six  
acetana deha, nāsāya śvāsa nāhi vaya  
unconscious  the body  in the nostrils  breath  not  flows

(64) The Lord lay there, five or six cubits long; His body was senseless, and no breath stirred in His nostrils.

এক এক হস্ত-পাদ — দীর্ঘ তিন-হাত ৷  
অস্থিগ্রন্থি ভিন্ন, চর্ম্ম আছে মাত্র তাত ॥ ৬৫ ॥

হস্ত, পাদ, গ্রীবা, কটি, অস্থি, সন্ধি যত ৷  
এক এক বিতস্তি ভিন্ন হঞাছে তত ॥ ৬৬ ॥

eka eka hasta-pāda — dīrgha tina-hāta  
each  each  arm and leg  —  long  three cubits  
asthi-granthi bhinna, carma āche mātra tāta  
of the bones  the joints  separated  the skin  remains  only  warmth  
hasta, pāda, grīvā, kaṭi, asthi, sandhi yata  
hands  feet  neck  waist  bones  joints  as many as  
eka eka vitasti bhinna hañāche tata  
each  each  a span  separated  had become  so many

(65–66) Each of His arms and legs had grown three cubits long. The joints of His bones had come apart: there remained only the skin, and the warmth that showed He was alive. His hands and feet, neck and waist, His bones and all His joints had each come apart by a span.[^22]

চর্ম্মমাত্র উপরে, সন্ধি আছে দীর্ঘ হঞা ৷  
দুঃখিত হইলা সবে প্রভুরে দেখিয়া ॥ ৬৭ ॥

carma-mātra upare, sandhi āche dīrgha hañā  
skin  only  above  the joints  are  long  being  
duḥkhita hailā sabe prabhure dekhiyā  
grieved  became  all  the Lord  seeing

(67) Only skin covered the joints, which lay drawn out. Seeing the Lord in this state, everyone was stricken with grief.

মুখে লালা-ফেন প্রভুর উত্তান-নয়ন ৷  
দেখিয়া সকল ভক্তের দেহ ছাড়ে প্রাণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

mukhe lālā-phena prabhura uttāna-nayana  
at the mouth  saliva and foam  the Lord’s  upturned  eyes  
dekhiyā sakala bhaktera deha chāḍe prāṇa  
seeing  all  the devotees’  body  leaves  life

(68) Saliva and foam were at the Lord’s mouth, and His eyes were turned upward. Seeing this, all the devotees felt as if life were leaving their bodies.

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি তবে উচ্চ করিয়া ৷  
প্রভুর কাণে কৃষ্ণনাম কহে ভক্তগণ লঞা ॥ ৬৯ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi tabe ucca kariyā  
Svarūpa Gosāñi  then  loud  making  
prabhura kāṇe kṛṣṇa-nāma kahe bhakta-gaṇa lañā  
of the Lord  in the ear  Kṛṣṇa’s name  says  the devotees  taking along

(69) Then Svarūpa Gosāñi, together with the devotees, loudly chanted the name of Kṛṣṇa into the Lord’s ear.

বহুক্ষণে কৃষ্ণনাম হৃদয়ে পশিলা ৷  
‘হরিবোল’ বলি’ প্রভু গর্জ্জিয়া উঠিলা ॥ ৭০ ॥

bahu-kṣaṇe kṛṣṇa-nāma hṛdaye paśilā  
after a long time  Kṛṣṇa’s name  into the heart  entered  
‘hari-bola’ bali’ prabhu garjiyā uṭhilā  
Haribol  saying  the Lord  roaring  rose

(70) After a long while the name of Kṛṣṇa entered His heart. Crying “Haribol!”, the Lord rose up with a roar.

চেতন পাইতে অস্থি-সন্ধি লাগিল ৷  
পূর্ব্বপ্রায় যথাবৎ শরীর হইল ॥ ৭১ ॥

cetana pāite asthi-sandhi lāgila  
consciousness  on regaining  the bone-joints  joined  
pūrva-prāya yathāvat śarīra haila  
as before  just as it was  the body  became

(71) As He regained consciousness, the joints of His bones came together, and His body became just as it had been before.

এই লীলা মহাপ্রভুর রঘুনাথদাস ৷  
‘চৈতন্যস্তবকল্পবৃক্ষে’ কৈরাছেন প্রকাশ ॥ ৭২ ॥

ei līlā mahāprabhura raghunāthadāsa  
this  pastime  of Mahāprabhu  Raghunātha dāsa  
‘caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣe’ kairāchena prakāśa  
in the Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa  has  described

(72) Raghunātha dāsa has described this pastime of Mahāprabhu in his *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*:

ক্বচিন্মিশ্রাবাসে ব্রজপতিসুতস্যোরুবিরহাৎ  
শ্লথচ্ছ্রীসন্ধিত্বাদ্দধদধিকদৈর্ঘ্যং ভুজপদোঃ ৷  
লুঠন্ ভূমৌ কাক্বা বিকলবিকলং গদ্গদবচা  
রুদন্ শ্রীগৌরাঙ্গো হৃদয় উদয়ন্মাং মদয়তি ॥ ৭৩ ॥

kvacin miśrāvāse vraja-  
once  in Miśra’s house  of Vraja’s  
pati-sutasyoru-virahāt  
lord’s  son’s  from intense separation  
ślathac chrī-sandhitvād dadhad-  
loosening  beauty and joints  through  bearing  
adhika-dairghyaṁ bhuja-padoḥ  
extra  length  of arms and legs  
luṭhan bhūmau kākvā vikala-  
rolling  on the ground  piteously  in utter  
vikalaṁ gadgada-vacā  
distress  with choked voice  
rudan śrī-gaurāṅgo hṛdaya  
weeping  Śrī Gaurāṅga  in the heart  
udayan māṁ madayati  
rising  me  maddens

(73) “Once, in the house of Miśra, in intense separation from the son of the lord of Vraja, the beauty and the joints of His limbs gave way, and His arms and legs grew unnaturally long. Rolling on the ground, in utter distress, He wept piteously in a voice choked with feeling. That Śrī Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, 4)[^23]

সিংহদ্বারে দেখি’ প্রভুর বিস্ময় হইলা ৷  
ক্যা কর, কিবা — এই স্বরূপে পুছিলা ॥ ৭৪ ॥

siṁha-dvāre dekhi’ prabhura vismaya hailā  
at the Siṁha-dvāra  seeing  the Lord’s  astonishment  was  
kyā kara, kibā — ei svarūpe puchilā  
what  do you do  why  —  this  of Svarūpa  He asked

(74) Finding Himself at the Siṁha-dvāra, the Lord was astonished, and He asked Svarūpa, “What are you doing? Why?”[^24]

স্বরূপ কহে, — উঠ, প্রভু, চল নিজ-ঘরে ৷  
তথাই তোমারে সব করিমু গোচরে ॥ ৭৫ ॥

svarūpa kahe, — uṭha, prabhu, cala nija-ghare  
Svarūpa  says  —  get up  Prabhu  come  to Your own room  
tathāi tomāre saba karimu gocare  
there  to You  all  I shall make  known

(75) Svarūpa said, “Get up, Prabhu, and come to Your own room. There I shall tell You everything.”

এত বলি’ প্রভুরে ধরি’ ঘরে লঞা গেলা ৷  
তাঁহার অবস্থা সব কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৭৬ ॥

eta bali’ prabhure dhari’ ghare lañā gelā  
so  saying  the Lord  holding  to the room  taking  went  
tām̐hāra avasthā saba kahite lāgilā  
His  condition  all  to tell  began

(76) Saying this, he held the Lord and took Him to His room, and there began to tell Him all about the state He had been in.

শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু বড় হৈলা চমৎকার ৷  
প্রভু কহে, — কিছু স্মৃতি নাহিক আমার! ॥ ৭৭ ॥

śuni’ mahāprabhu baḍa hailā camatkāra  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  greatly  became  astonished  
prabhu kahe, — kichu smṛti nāhika āmāra!  
the Lord  says  —  any  memory  is not  Mine

(77) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu was greatly astonished. The Lord said, “I remember nothing of it!

সবে দেখি — হয় মোর কৃষ্ণ বিদ্যমান ৷  
বিদ্যুৎপ্রায় দেখা দিয়া হয় অন্তর্দ্ধান ॥ ৭৮ ॥

sabe dekhi — haya mora kṛṣṇa vidyamāna  
only  I see  —  is  My  Kṛṣṇa  present  
vidyut-prāya dekhā diyā haya antardhāna  
lightning  like  appearance  giving  is  vanishing

(78) I only see that My Kṛṣṇa is present: He appears like a flash of lightning and vanishes.”

হেনকালে জগন্নাথের পানি-শঙ্খ বাজিলা ৷  
স্নান করি’ মহাপ্রভু দরশনে গেলা ॥ ৭৯ ॥

hena-kāle jagannāthera pāni-śaṅkha bājilā  
at that time  of Jagannātha  the conch (pāni-śaṅkha)  sounded  
snāna kari’ mahāprabhu daraśane gelā  
bath  taking  Mahāprabhu  for darśana  went

(79) Just then the conch (*pāni-śaṅkha*) of Jagannātha sounded, and Mahāprabhu bathed and went for darśana.[^25]

এই ত’ কহিলুঁ প্রভুর অদ্ভুত বিকার ৷  
যাহার শ্রবণে লোকে লাগে চমৎকার ॥ ৮০ ॥

ei ta’ kahilum̐ prabhura adbhuta vikāra  
thus  indeed  I have told  of the Lord  wonderful  transformations  
yāhāra śravaṇe loke lāge camatkāra  
of which  in the hearing  in people  arises  wonder

(80) Thus I have described the Lord’s wonderful transformations; whoever hears of them is struck with wonder.

লোকে নাহি দেখে ঐছে, শাস্ত্রে নাহি শুনি ৷  
হেন ভাব ব্যক্ত করে ন্যাসি-চূড়ামণি ॥ ৮১ ॥

loke nāhi dekhe aiche, śāstre nāhi śuni  
people  not  see  such  in the scriptures  not  we hear  
hena bhāva vyakta kare nyāsi-cūḍāmaṇi  
such  ecstasy  manifest  makes  of sannyāsīs  the crest-jewel

(81) People have never seen such things, nor do we hear of them in the scriptures; yet the crest-jewel of sannyāsīs reveals such feelings.

শাস্ত্রলোকাতীত যেই যেই ভাব হয় ৷  
ইতর-লোকের তাতে না হয় নিশ্চয় ॥ ৮২ ॥

śāstra-lokātīta yei yei bhāva haya  
scriptures  world  beyond  whatever  whatever  emotion  is  
itara-lokera tāte nā haya niścaya  
of ordinary  people  in that  not  is  conviction

(82) Ordinary people cannot believe in feelings (*bhāva*) that lie beyond the scriptures and beyond this world.[^26]

রঘুনাথ-দাসের সদা প্রভুসঙ্গে স্থিতি ৷  
তাঁর মুখে শুনি’ লিখি করিয়া প্রতীতি ॥ ৮৩ ॥

raghunātha-dāsera sadā prabhu-saṅge sthiti  
Raghunātha dāsa’s  always  with the Lord  stay  
tām̐ra mukhe śuni’ likhi kariyā pratīti  
his  from the mouth  hearing  I write  making  conviction

(83) Raghunātha dāsa always stayed with the Lord. I write what I heard from his own lips, with full faith in it.

একদিন মহাপ্রভু সমুদ্রে যাইতে ৷  
‘চটক’-পর্ব্বত দেখিলেন আচম্বিতে ॥ ৮৪ ॥

ekadina mahāprabhu samudre yāite  
one day  Mahāprabhu  to the sea  going  
‘caṭaka’-parvata dekhilena ācambite  
Caṭaka  hill  He saw  suddenly

(84) One day, as Mahāprabhu was going to the sea, He suddenly caught sight of the Caṭaka hill.[^27]

গোবর্দ্ধন-শৈল-জ্ঞানে আবিষ্ট হইলা ৷  
পর্ব্বত-দিশাতে প্রভু ধাঞা চলিলা ॥ ৮৫ ॥

govardhana-śaila-jñāne āviṣṭa hailā  
Govardhana  hill  in the thought  absorbed  became  
parvata-diśāte prabhu dhāñā calilā  
hill  toward  the Lord  running  went

(85) Taking it for Govardhana Hill, the Lord was overcome with feeling and ran toward it.

হন্তায়মদ্রিরবলা হরিদাসবর্য্যো  
যদ্রামকৃষ্ণচরণ-স্পর্শ-প্রমোদঃ ৷  
মানং তনোতি সহ-গোগণয়োস্তয়োর্যৎ  
পানীয় সূযবস কন্দর কন্দমূলৈঃ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

hantāyam adrir abalā hari-dāsa-varyo  
ah  this  hill  O friends  best of Hari’s servants  
yad rāma-kṛṣṇa-caraṇa-sparśa-pramodaḥ  
since  of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  the feet  by the touch  rejoicing  
mānaṁ tanoti saha-go-gaṇayos tayor yat  
honour  offers  with Their herds of cows  to Them  because  
pānīya sūyavasa kandara kanda-mūlaiḥ  
with water  fine grass  caves  bulbs  roots

(86) “Ah, friends, this hill is the best of Hari’s servants! Thrilled by the touch of the feet of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, it honours Them, together with Their cows and companions, with water to drink, fine grass, caves, and bulbs and roots.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.18)[^28]

এই শ্লোক পড়ি’ প্রভু চলেন বায়ুবেগে ৷  
গোবিন্দ ধাইল পাছে, নাহি পায় লাগে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

ei śloka paḍi’ prabhu calena vāyu-vege  
this  verse  reciting  the Lord  goes  with the speed of the wind  
govinda dhāila pāche, nāhi pāya lāge  
Govinda  ran  behind  not  could  reach

(87) Reciting this verse, the Lord rushed on with the speed of the wind. Govinda ran after Him but could not catch up.

ফুকার পড়িল, মহা-কোলাহল হইল ৷  
যেই যাঁহা ছিল সেই উঠিয়া ধাইল ॥ ৮৮ ॥

phukāra paḍila, mahā-kolāhala haila  
a cry  arose  great  uproar  there was  
yei yām̐hā chila sei uṭhiyā dhāila  
whoever  where  was  he  getting up  ran

(88) A cry went up, and there was a great uproar. Everyone, wherever he was, got up and ran.

স্বরূপ, জগদানন্দ, পণ্ডিত-গদাধর ৷  
রামাই, নন্দাই, আর পণ্ডিত-শঙ্কর ॥ ৮৯ ॥

svarūpa, jagadānanda, paṇḍita-gadādhara  
Svarūpa  Jagadānanda  Paṇḍita Gadādhara  
rāmāi, nandāi, āra paṇḍita-śaṅkara  
Rāmāi  Nandāi  and  Paṇḍita Śaṅkara

(89) Among them were Svarūpa, Jagadānanda, Gadādhara Paṇḍita, Rāmāi, Nandāi and Śaṅkara Paṇḍita.

পুরী-ভারতী-গোসাঞি আইলা সিন্ধুতীরে ৷  
ভগবান্-আচার্য্য খঞ্জ চলিলা ধীরে ধীরে ॥ ৯০ ॥

purī-bhāratī-gosāñi āilā sindhu-tīre  
Purī  Bhāratī  Gosāñi  came  to the seashore  
bhagavān-ācārya khañja calilā dhīre dhīre  
Bhagavān Ācārya  lame  went  slowly  slowly

(90) Purī Gosāñi and Bhāratī Gosāñi came to the seashore, and the lame Bhagavān Ācārya followed very slowly.

প্রথমে চলিলা প্রভু, — যেন বায়ুগতি ৷  
স্তম্ভভাব পথে হৈল, চলিতে নাহি শক্তি ॥ ৯১ ॥

prathame calilā prabhu, — yena vāyu-gati  
at first  went  the Lord  —  like  the wind’s  speed  
stambha-bhāva pathe haila, calite nāhi śakti  
stupor  ecstasy  on the way  arose  to move  no  strength

(91) At first the Lord went like the wind; but on the way He was seized by stupor (*stambha*) and had no strength to move.

প্রতি-রোমকূপে মাংস — ব্রণের আকার ৷  
তার উপরে রোমোদ্গম — কদম্বপ্রকার ॥ ৯২ ॥

prati-roma-kūpe māṁsa — vraṇera ākāra  
at every  hair  pore  the flesh  —  of a boil  the shape  
tāra upare romodgama — kadamba-prakāra  
it  upon  the hair standing on end  —  kadamba  like

(92) At every pore the flesh swelled up like a boil, and on it the hairs stood on end, like the filaments of a kadamba flower.

প্রতি-রোমে প্রস্বেদ পড়ে রুধিরের ধার ৷  
কণ্ঠে ঘর্ঘর, নাহি বর্ণের উচ্চার ॥ ৯৩ ॥

prati-rome prasveda paḍe rudhirera dhāra  
at every  hair  perspiration  falls  of blood  a stream  
kaṇṭhe gharghara, nāhi varṇera uccāra  
in the throat  gurgling  no  of syllables  utterance

(93) From every pore sweat poured out with streams of blood. His throat gurgled, and He could not utter a single syllable.

দুই নেত্রে ভরি’ অশ্রু বহয়ে অপার ৷  
সমুদ্রে মিলিলা যেন গঙ্গা-যমুনা-ধার ॥ ৯৪ ॥

dui netre bhari’ aśru vahaye apāra  
two  eyes  filling  tears  flow  boundless  
samudre mililā yena gaṅgā-yamunā-dhāra  
in the sea  met  as if  Gaṅgā  Yamunā  streams

(94) His two eyes filled, and tears flowed endlessly, as if the streams of the Gaṅgā and the Yamunā had met the sea.

বৈবর্ণ্য শঙ্খপ্রায়, শ্বেত হৈল অঙ্গ ৷  
তবে কম্প উঠে, — যেন সমুদ্রে তরঙ্গ ॥ ৯৫ ॥

vaivarṇya śaṅkha-prāya, śveta haila aṅga  
pallor  conch  like  white  became  the body  
tabe kampa uṭhe, — yena samudre taraṅga  
then  trembling  rises  —  like  in the sea  waves

(95) His pallor was like that of a conch, and His body turned white. Then a trembling arose in Him, like waves on the sea.

কাঁপিতে কাঁপিতে প্রভু ভূমেতে পড়িলা ৷  
তবে ত’ গোবিন্দ প্রভুর নিকটে আইলা ॥ ৯৬ ॥

kām̐pite kām̐pite prabhu bhūmete paḍilā  
trembling  trembling  the Lord  on the ground  fell  
tabe ta’ govinda prabhura nikaṭe āilā  
then  indeed  Govinda  of the Lord  near  came

(96) Trembling all over, the Lord fell to the ground. Then Govinda came up to Him.

করঙ্গের জলে করে সর্ব্বাঙ্গ সিঞ্চন ৷  
বহির্ব্বাস লঞা করে অঙ্গ সংবীজন ॥ ৯৭ ॥

karaṅgera jale kare sarvāṅga siñcana  
of the waterpot  with water  makes  all limbs  sprinkling  
bahirvāsa lañā kare aṅga saṁvījana  
the outer cloth  taking  makes  of the body  fanning

(97) With water from the waterpot (*karaṅga*) he sprinkled the Lord’s whole body, and with the outer cloth he fanned Him.

স্বরূপাদিগণ তাঁহা আসিয়া মিলিলা ৷  
প্রভুর অবস্থা দেখি’ কান্দিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৯৮ ॥

svarūpādi-gaṇa tām̐hā āsiyā mililā  
Svarūpa and others  there  coming  met  
prabhura avasthā dekhi’ kāndite lāgilā  
of the Lord  the condition  seeing  to weep  began

(98) Svarūpa and the others came and joined them there; seeing the Lord’s condition, they began to weep.

প্রভুর অঙ্গে দেখে অষ্টসাত্ত্বিক বিকার ৷  
আশ্চর্য্য সাত্ত্বিক দেখি’ হৈলা চমৎকার ॥ ৯৯ ॥

prabhura aṅge dekhe aṣṭa-sāttvika vikāra  
of the Lord  in the body  they see  eight  sāttvika  transformations  
āścarya sāttvika dekhi’ hailā camatkāra  
wonderful  sāttvika  seeing  became  amazed

(99) They saw the eight sāttvika transformations in the Lord’s body and were amazed at these wondrous signs.[^29]

উচ্চ সঙ্কীর্ত্তন করে প্রভুর শ্রবণে ৷  
সুশীতল জলে করে প্রভুর অঙ্গ সম্মার্জ্জনে ॥ ১০০ ॥

ucca saṅkīrtana kare prabhura śravaṇe  
loud  saṅkīrtana  they make  at the Lord’s  ear  
suśītala jale kare prabhura aṅga sammārjane  
very cool  with water  they make  the Lord’s  body  washing

(100) They sang loud saṅkīrtana right at the Lord’s ear and bathed His body with very cool water.

এইমত বহুবার কীর্ত্তন করিতে ৷  
‘হরিবোল’ বলি’ প্রভু উঠে আচম্বিতে ॥ ১০১ ॥

ei-mata bahu-bāra kīrtana karite  
in this way  many  times  kīrtana  doing  
‘hari-bola’ bali’ prabhu uṭhe ācambite  
Haribol  saying  the Lord  rises  suddenly

(101) When they had sung kīrtana in this way many times, the Lord suddenly rose, crying “Haribol!”

সানন্দে সকল বৈষ্ণব বলে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
উঠিল মঙ্গলধ্বনি চতুর্দ্দিক্ ভরি’ ॥ ১০২ ॥

sānande sakala vaiṣṇava bale ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
joyfully  all  Vaiṣṇavas  say  Hari  Hari  
uṭhila maṅgala-dhvani catur-dik bhari’  
arose  auspicious  sound  the four  directions  filling

(102) Joyfully all the Vaiṣṇavas cried, “Hari! Hari!”, and an auspicious sound arose, filling the four directions.

উঠি’ মহাপ্রভু বিস্মিত, ইতি উতি চায় ৷  
যে দেখিতে চায়, তাহা দেখিতে না পায় ॥ ১০৩ ॥

uṭhi’ mahāprabhu vismita, iti uti cāya  
having risen  Mahāprabhu  astonished  here  there  looks  
ye dekhite cāya, tāhā dekhite nā pāya  
what  to see  He wants  that  to see  not  can

(103) Rising, Mahāprabhu looked here and there in astonishment, but what He longed to see, He could not see.

‘বৈষ্ণব’ দেখিয়া প্রভুর অর্দ্ধবাহ্য হইল ৷  
স্বরূপ-গোসাঞিরে কিছু কহিতে লাগিল ॥ ১০৪ ॥

‘vaiṣṇava’ dekhiyā prabhura ardha-bāhya haila  
the Vaiṣṇavas  seeing  of the Lord  half-external consciousness  became  
svarūpa-gosāñire kichu kahite lāgila  
to Svarūpa Gosāñi  something  to say  began

(104) Seeing the Vaiṣṇavas, the Lord came half into external consciousness and began to say something to Svarūpa Gosāñi:

গোবর্দ্ধন হৈতে মোরে কে ইঁহা আনিল ?  
পাঞা কৃষ্ণের লীলা দেখিতে না পাইল ॥ ১০৫ ॥

govardhana haite more ke im̐hā ānila?  
Govardhana  from  Me  who  here  brought  
pāñā kṛṣṇera līlā dekhite nā pāila  
having found  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes  to see  not  I could

(105) “Who has brought Me here from Govardhana? I had found Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, but I could not go on seeing them.

ইঁহা হৈতে আজি মুই গেনু গোবর্দ্ধনে ৷  
দেখোঁ, — যদি কৃষ্ণ করেন গোধন-চারণে ॥ ১০৬ ॥

im̐hā haite āji mui genu govardhane  
from here  today  I  went  to Govardhana  
dekhom̐, — yadi kṛṣṇa karena godhana-cāraṇe  
I would see  whether  Kṛṣṇa  does  of cows  grazing

(106) Today I went from here to Govardhana to see whether Kṛṣṇa was grazing the cows.

গোবর্দ্ধনে চড়ি’ কৃষ্ণ বাজাইলা বেণু ৷  
গোবর্দ্ধনের চৌদিকে চরে সব ধেনু ॥ ১০৭ ॥

govardhane caḍi’ kṛṣṇa bājāilā veṇu  
on Govardhana  climbing  Kṛṣṇa  played  the flute  
govardhanera caudike care saba dhenu  
of Govardhana  all around  graze  all  the cows

(107) Kṛṣṇa climbed Govardhana and played His flute, and all around Govardhana the cows were grazing.

বেণুনাদ শুনি’ আইলা রাধা-ঠাকুরাণী ৷  
সব সখীগণ-সঙ্গে করিয়া সাজনি ॥ ১০৮ ॥

veṇu-nāda śuni’ āilā rādhā-ṭhākurāṇī  
of the flute  the sound  hearing  came  Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī  
saba sakhī-gaṇa-saṅge kariyā sājani  
all  the sakhīs  with  making  adornment

(108) Hearing the sound of the flute, Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī came, beautifully dressed and adorned, with all Her sakhīs.

রাধা লঞা কৃষ্ণ প্রবেশিলা কন্দরাতে ৷  
সখীগণ চাহে কেহ ফুল উঠাইতে ॥ ১০৯ ॥

rādhā lañā kṛṣṇa praveśilā kandarāte  
Rādhā  taking  Kṛṣṇa  entered  into a cave  
sakhī-gaṇa cāhe keha phula uṭhāite  
the sakhīs  want  some  flowers  to gather

(109) Kṛṣṇa took Rādhā with Him and entered a cave, and some of the sakhīs wanted to gather flowers.[^30]

হেনকালে তুমি-সব কোলাহল কৈলা ৷  
তাঁহা হৈতে ধরি’ মোরে ইঁহা লঞা আইলা ॥ ১১০ ॥

hena-kāle tumi-saba kolāhala kailā  
at that time  you all  uproar  made  
tām̐hā haite dhari’ more im̐hā lañā āilā  
there  from  seizing  Me  here  taking  came

(110) Just then all of you raised an uproar, seized Me and carried Me back here.

কেনে বা আনিলা মোরে বৃথা দুঃখ দিতে ৷  
পাঞা কৃষ্ণের লীলা, না পাইনু দেখিতে ॥ ১১১ ॥

kene vā ānilā more vṛthā duḥkha dite  
why  then  brought  Me  in vain  pain  to give  
pāñā kṛṣṇera līlā, nā pāinu dekhite  
having found  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes  not  I could  see

(111) Why did you bring Me here, only to give Me pain for nothing? I had found Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, but I could not go on seeing them.”

এত বলি’ মহাপ্রভু করেন ক্রন্দন ৷  
তাঁর দশা দেখি’ বৈষ্ণব করেন রোদন ॥ ১১২ ॥

eta bali’ mahāprabhu karena krandana  
so  saying  Mahāprabhu  does  weeping  
tām̐ra daśā dekhi’ vaiṣṇava karena rodana  
His  condition  seeing  the Vaiṣṇavas  do  crying

(112) Saying this, Mahāprabhu wept, and seeing His condition, the Vaiṣṇavas wept too.

হেনকালে আইলা পুরী, ভারতী, — দুইজন ৷  
দুঁহে দেখি’ মহাপ্রভুর হইল সম্ভ্রম ॥ ১১৩ ॥

hena-kāle āilā purī, bhāratī, — dui-jana  
at that time  came  Purī  Bhāratī  —  the two  
dum̐he dekhi’ mahāprabhura haila sambhrama  
both  seeing  Mahāprabhu’s  was  reverence

(113) Meanwhile the two, Purī and Bhāratī, arrived. Seeing them both, Mahāprabhu was filled with reverence.

নিপট্ট-বাহ্য হইলে প্রভু দুঁহারে বন্দিলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভুরে দুইজন প্রেমালিঙ্গন কৈলা ॥ ১১৪ ॥

nipaṭṭa-bāhya haile prabhu dum̐hāre vandilā  
full  external consciousness  when it came  the Lord  to both  offered respects  
mahāprabhure dui-jana premāliṅgana kailā  
Mahāprabhu  the two  loving embrace  made

(114) When He came fully into external consciousness, the Lord offered His respects to them both, and the two embraced Mahāprabhu with love.

প্রভু কহে, — দুঁহে কেনে আইলা এত দূরে ?  
পুরীগোসাঞি কহে, — তোমার নৃত্য দেখিবারে ॥ ১১৫ ॥

prabhu kahe, — dum̐he kene āilā eta dūre?  
the Lord  says  —  you two  why  came  so  far  
purīgosāñi kahe, — tomāra nṛtya dekhibāre  
Purī Gosāñi  says  —  Your  dance  to see

(115) The Lord said, “Why have you two come so far?” Purī Gosāñi said, “To see Your dancing.”

লজ্জিত হইলা প্রভু পুরীর বচনে ৷  
সমুদ্রঘাট আইলা সব বৈষ্ণব-সনে ॥ ১১৬ ॥

lajjita hailā prabhu purīra vacane  
embarrassed  became  the Lord  of Purī  at the words  
samudra-ghāṭa āilā saba vaiṣṇava-sane  
to the sea’s  bathing place  came  all  Vaiṣṇavas  with

(116) At Purī’s words the Lord was embarrassed, and He went with all the Vaiṣṇavas to the bathing place at the sea.

স্নান করি’ মহাপ্রভু ঘরেতে আইলা ৷  
সবা লঞা মহাপ্রসাদ ভোজন করিলা ॥ ১১৭ ॥

snāna kari’ mahāprabhu gharete āilā  
bath  taking  Mahāprabhu  home  came  
sabā lañā mahā-prasāda bhojana karilā  
all  taking along  mahā-prasāda  eating  did

(117) After bathing, Mahāprabhu returned home and, together with everyone, partook of mahā-prasāda.

এই ত’ কহিলুঁ প্রভুর দিব্যোন্মাদ-ভাব ৷  
ব্রহ্মাও কহিতে নারে যাহার প্রভাব ॥ ১১৮ ॥

ei ta’ kahilum̐ prabhura divyonmāda-bhāva  
thus  indeed  I have told  of the Lord  divine madness  mood  
brahmāo kahite nāre yāhāra prabhāva  
even Brahmā  to tell  cannot  whose  power

(118) Thus I have described the Lord’s mood of divine madness (*divyonmāda*), whose power even Brahmā cannot describe.

‘চটক’-গিরি-গমন-লীলা রঘুনাথদাস ৷  
‘চৈতন্যস্তবকল্পবৃক্ষে’ কৈরাছেন প্রকাশ ॥ ১১৯ ॥

‘caṭaka’-giri-gamana-līlā raghunāthadāsa  
Caṭaka  hill  going  pastime  Raghunātha dāsa  
‘caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣe’ kairāchena prakāśa  
in the Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa  has  described

(119) Raghunātha dāsa has described the pastime of the Lord’s running to the Caṭaka hill in his *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*:

সমীপে নীলাদ্রেশ্চটকগিরিরাজস্য কলনা  
দয়ে গোষ্ঠে গোবর্দ্ধনগিরিপতিং লোকিতুমিতঃ ৷  
ব্রজন্নস্মীত্যুক্ত্বা প্রমদ ইব ধাবন্নবধৃতো  
গণৈঃ স্বৈর্গৌরাঙ্গো হৃদয় উদয়ন্মাং মদয়তি ॥ ১২০ ॥

samīpe nīlādreś caṭaka-giri-  
near  of Nīlādri  Caṭaka  hill  
rājasya kalanād  
the king  from seeing  
aye goṣṭhe govardhana-giri-  
oh  in Vraja  Govardhana  hills’  
patiṁ lokitum itaḥ  
king  to see  from here  
vrajann asmīty uktvā pramada  
going  I am  thus  saying  mad  
iva dhāvann avadhṛto  
as if  running  followed  
gaṇaiḥ svair gaurāṅgo hṛdaya  
by companions  His own  Gaurāṅga  in the heart  
udayan māṁ madayati  
rising  me  maddens

(120) “Near Nīlādri He saw the Caṭaka hill, a king among hills, and cried, ‘Ah! From here I am going to Vraja, to see Govardhana, the lord of hills!’ Saying this, He ran like one mad, and His own companions followed after Him. That Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, 8)[^31]

এবে প্রভু যত কৈলা অলৌকিক লীলা ৷  
কে বুঝিতে পারে সেই মহাপ্রভুর খেলা ॥ ১২১ ॥

ebe prabhu yata kailā alaukika līlā  
now  the Lord  all that  did  extraordinary  pastimes  
ke bujhite pāre sei mahāprabhura khelā  
who  to understand  can  that  of Mahāprabhu  play

(121) Who can understand these extraordinary pastimes of the Lord, this play of Mahāprabhu?[^32]

সংক্ষেপে করিয়া করি দিক্ দরশন ৷  
যেই ইহা শুনে, পায় কৃষ্ণের চরণ ॥ ১২২ ॥

saṅkṣepe kariyā kari dik daraśana  
in brief  making  I do  of direction  showing  
yei ihā śune, pāya kṛṣṇera caraṇa  
whoever  this  hears  attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(122) Telling it briefly, I have only pointed the way. Whoever hears this attains the feet of Kṛṣṇa.[^33]

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১২৩ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(123) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

[^1]: The second line is the same as Madhya 12.3; translated as there.

[^2]: Heading in the edition: without Gaura’s mercy even a great scholar cannot understand the Lord’s transcendental divine madness.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya*: these two constantly experienced Mahāprabhu’s pastimes and wrote them down, now briefly, now at length, in the form of diaries rather than formal books.

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the author sets out both accounts, enlarging them a little, after the manner of a *pā̃ji-ṭīkā*, which “fluffs out” the thoughts of the original source as one fluffs out cotton.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: for Śrī Rādhā’s lament see the *Bhramara-gītā*, Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.47.12–21.

[^6]: Heading in the edition: the Lord is filled only with the mood of Śrī Rādhā in separation from Kṛṣṇa, and so in Him there is no desire of Kṛṣṇa for the enjoyment of union (*sambhoga*). *Anubhāṣya*: though Śrī Gaurasundara is Kṛṣṇa Himself, His body is joined with Śrīmatī Rādhikā, and so He was always immersed in her mood.

[^7]: *Divyonmāda* is the highest manifestation of *mahābhāva*, not worldly madness.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: to climb the Garuḍa pillar is an offence to a Vaiṣṇava, and to set one’s foot on the Lord’s shoulder is an offence to the feet of Bhagavān; fearing this, Govinda hurried to bring the woman down.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya* refers to Antya 10.116, where *ādivasyā* is explained as “unfortunate”, “unwise”, “foolish”, “fickle”, “impatient”.

[^10]: Heading in the edition: seeing her ardent love, the Lord speaks humbly of Himself and glorifies her as His guru. *Anubhāṣya*: *ārti* is a burning desire to see; longing to see Jagannātha, she forgot what is proper and improper and, without knowing it, set her foot on the shoulder of the all-worshipful Mahāprabhu.

[^11]: Heading in the edition: a lesson — not to look upon a servant of Kṛṣṇa with sense-born knowledge, by outward marks such as “woman” or “man”.

[^12]: Verses 28–32 are the Lord’s words, as in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (the quotation closes after verse 32). Some editions read verse 31 as *pūrve āsi’ yabe … jagannāthe dekhe*, which makes verses 31–32 the narrator’s account.

[^13]: Heading in the edition: seeing Vāsudeva at Kurukṣetra, the Lord is filled with the mood of a gopī separated from Śyāma.

[^14]: Heading in the edition: a verse spoken by Gosvāmipāda. *Anubhāṣya* (on verse 51): the *kāpālikas* are a kind of yogī who wander with a human skull; they do not follow the Vedas and are not Vaiṣṇavas — only the comparison with their ways is taken here.

[^15]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: these are the earrings of the *Bhāgavatam*. An ordinary yogī carries a gourd water-pot and a begging bowl; the yogī that is the Lord’s mind has made its bowl from the gourd of thirst for Kṛṣṇa and hung on its shoulder the bag of hope, “I shall attain Kṛṣṇa.”

[^16]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the twelve bangles are the eight *sāttvika-vikāras*, the surge of the mind, trembling, sighing, restlessness and anxious thought. *Anubhāṣya*: a variant reads *lobhera jhulnī māthe*; *jhulnī* is a cloth that may cover the head.

[^17]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Bāul yogīs take ten disciples each.

[^18]: *Śūnya*, “empty”: according to the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*, a bower where Kṛṣṇa is not. Heading in the edition: the state of sleeplessness (*jāgara*).

[^19]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the empty dwelling of the body is an image of fainting (*pralaya*), with which the ten states end. Heading in the edition: the states of disease (*vyādhi*), fainting (*moha*) and death (*mṛtyu* — *pralaya*); *citra-jalpa*.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: these are the ten states in the separation called *pravāsa*, when the beloved has gone to a distant land; *moha* is bewilderment of mind (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: fainting).

[^21]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1017) reads *mauna hailā*, “fell silent”. Heading in the edition: Rāmānanda Rāya recites verses suited to the Lord’s mood of separation and to the hour.

[^22]: A span (*vitasti*) is half a cubit (*hasta*). *Anubhāṣya*: *tāta* is the warmth which shows that life still remains in the body.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: *śrī* is beauty: both the beauty and the joints of His arms and legs gave way, leaving their places; *madayati* — “gladdens, fills with bliss”.

[^24]: *Kyā kara, kibā* is Hindustani (*keyā karo, keṁo*): “What are you doing? Why?” (*Anubhāṣya*). In half-external consciousness, the Lord asks why people have gathered around Him.

[^25]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *pāni-śaṅkha*. The *Anubhāṣya* explains *pāṇi-śaṅkha* as a conch held in the hand and blown (or the clapping of hands when the temple doors are opened), and, by another reading, *pānī-śaṅkha*, a conch [holding water] for *ācamana*.

[^26]: Heading in the edition: the transcendental signs of feelings directed to Adhokṣaja are beyond the grasp of those whose knowledge is born of the senses.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: Caṭaka-parvata is a high sand hill that looks like a mountain — a dune.

[^28]: The same verse as Madhya 18.34; translated as there. According to the *Anubhāṣya*, the gopīs sing this verse: wandering in longing for Kṛṣṇa, they see before them Govardhana, the king of mountains.

[^29]: *Anubhāṣya*: the eight sāttvika transformations are stupor, perspiration, hair standing on end, faltering of the voice, trembling, change of colour, tears and loss of consciousness.

[^30]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *sakhī-gaṇa cāhe keha phula uṭhāite*, “some of the sakhīs wanted to gather flowers”; other editions read *sakhī-gaṇa kahe more phula uṭhāite*, “the sakhīs told Me to gather flowers”.

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: *caṭaka-giri-rāja* is a mountain that appears as a sand hill; *avadhṛtaḥ* — “followed from behind” (by Svarūpa and the others); *madayati* — “fills with bliss”.

[^32]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *ke bujhite pāre*, “who can understand”; other editions read *ke varṇite pāre*, “who can describe”.

[^33]: Heading in the edition: only by following the Lord in His separation from Kṛṣṇa does the soul attain Kṛṣṇa’s feet.
