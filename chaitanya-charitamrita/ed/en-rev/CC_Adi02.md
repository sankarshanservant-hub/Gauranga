“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 2  
**The Auspicious Invocation Indicating the Object: Determining the Truth of Caitanya**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

শ্রীচৈতন্যপ্রভুং বন্দে বালোঽপি যদনুগ্রহাৎ ৷  
তরেন্নানামতগ্রাহব্যাপ্তং সিদ্ধান্তসাগরম্ ॥ ১ ॥

śrī-caitanya-prabhuṁ vande  
Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship  
bālo ’pi yad-anugrahāt  
a child  even  by whose  mercy  
taren nānā-mata-grāha-  
may cross  of various  doctrines  crocodiles  
vyāptaṁ siddhānta-sāgaram  
filled with  of conclusions  the ocean

(1) I worship Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, by whose mercy even an ignorant child can cross the ocean of philosophical conclusions, which teems with the crocodiles of various doctrines.[^1]

কৃষ্ণোৎকীর্ত্তনগাননর্ত্তনকলাপাথোজনি-ভ্রাজিতা  
সদ্ভক্তাবলিহংসচক্রমধুপশ্রেণীবিহারাস্পদম্ ৷  
কর্ণানন্দিকলধ্বনির্বহতু মে জিহ্বামরুপ্রাঙ্গণে  
শ্রীচৈতন্যদয়ানিধে তব লসল্লীলাসুধাস্বর্ধুনী ॥ ২ ॥

kṛṣṇotkīrtana-gāna-nartana-kalā-pāthojani-bhrājitā  
of Kṛṣṇa  loud glorification  singing  dancing  arts  lotuses  adorned  
sad-bhaktāvali-haṁsa-cakra-madhupa-śreṇī-vihārāspadam  
of true  devotees  rows  swans  cakravāka birds  bees  swarms  of pleasure  abode  
karṇānandi-kala-dhvanir vahatu me jihvā-maru-prāṅgaṇe  
delighting the ears  sweet  sound  may it flow  my  of the tongue  desert  over the expanse  
śrī-caitanya dayā-nidhe tava lasal-līlā-sudhā-svardhunī  
O Śrī Caitanya  of mercy  ocean  Your  shining  pastimes  nectar  celestial Gaṅgā

(2) O Śrī Caitanya, ocean of mercy! May the celestial Gaṅgā of the nectar of Your shining pastimes flow over the desert of my tongue. She is adorned with the lotuses of the loud glorification of Kṛṣṇa, of singing and of dancing; she is the place of delight for the rows of true devotees, who are like swans, cakravāka birds and bees; and her sweet murmur gladdens the ear.[^2]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(3) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

তৃতীয় শ্লোকের অর্থ করি বিবরণ ৷  
বস্তু-নির্দ্দেশরূপ মঙ্গলাচরণ ॥ ৪ ॥

tṛtīya ślokera artha kari vivaraṇa  
of the third  verse  the meaning  I make  explanation  
vastu-nirdeśa-rūpa maṅgalācaraṇa  
the Object  indicating  in the form of  auspicious invocation

(4) Now I shall explain in detail the meaning of the third verse — the auspicious invocation that indicates the Object, the Supreme Truth (*vastu-nirdeśa*).[^3]

যদদ্বৈতং ব্রহ্মোপনিষদি তদপ্যস্য তনুভা  
য আত্মান্তর্যামী পুরুষ ইতি সোঽস্যাংশবিভবঃ ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যৈঃ পূর্ণো য ইহ ভগবান্ স স্বয়ময়ং  
ন চৈতন্যাৎ কৃষ্ণাজ্জগতি পরতত্ত্বং পরমিহ ॥ ৫ ॥

yad advaitaṁ brahmopaniṣadi tad apy asya tanu-bhā  
which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  also  His  bodily  effulgence  
ya ātmāntar-yāmī puruṣa iti so ’syāṁśa-vibhavaḥ  
who  the Supersoul  the Indweller  the Puruṣa  thus  He  His  portion  manifestation  
ṣaḍ-aiśvaryaiḥ pūrṇo ya iha bhagavān sa svayam ayaṁ  
with six  opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one  
na caitanyāt kṛṣṇāj jagati para-tattvaṁ param iha  
not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  supreme  truth  higher  here

(5) “There is no Truth in the world higher than Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. That which the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is the effulgence of His body; the Puruṣa, the Supersoul who dwells within every heart, is His partial manifestation; and Bhagavān, full in all six opulences, is He Himself.”[^4]

ব্রহ্ম, আত্মা, ভগবান্ — অনুবাদ তিন ৷  
অঙ্গপ্রভা, অংশ, স্বরূপ — তিন বিধেয় চিহ্ন ॥ ৬ ॥

brahma, ātmā, bhagavān — anuvāda tina  
Brahman,  Paramātmā,  Bhagavān —  subjects  three  
aṅga-prabhā, aṁśa, svarūpa — tina vidheya cihna  
of the body  effulgence,  portion,  own form —  three  predicates  marks

(6) Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān are the three *anuvādas* — that which is spoken of; the effulgence of the body, the partial manifestation and His own form are the three *vidheyas* — that which is stated about them.[^5]

অনুবাদ আগে, পাছে বিধেয় স্থাপন ৷  
সেই অর্থ কহি, শুন শাস্ত্রবিবরণ ॥ ৭ ॥

anuvāda āge, pāche vidheya sthāpana  
the subject  first,  afterwards  the predicate  placing  
sei artha kahi, śuna śāstra-vivaraṇa  
that  meaning  I tell,  hear  of scripture  the explanation

(7) First the *anuvāda* is stated, and afterwards the *vidheya* is set down. In this way I shall tell the meaning of the verse; hear now the explanation according to scripture.

স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ, বিষ্ণু-পরতত্ত্ব ৷  
পূর্ণজ্ঞান পূর্ণানন্দ পরম মহত্ত্ব ॥ ৮ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa, viṣṇu-paratattva  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa,  of Viṣṇu  the supreme truth  
pūrṇa-jñāna pūrṇānanda parama mahattva  
full  knowledge  full  bliss  supreme  greatness

(8) Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself, the supreme truth among all forms of Viṣṇu (*viṣṇu-tattva*). He is fullness of knowledge, fullness of bliss and the highest greatness.[^6]

‘নন্দসুত’ বলি’ যাঁরে ভাগবতে গাই ৷  
সেই কৃষ্ণ অবতীর্ণ চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ॥ ৯ ॥

‘nanda-suta’ bali’ yām̐re bhāgavate gāi  
‘Nanda’s son’  calling  whom  in the Bhāgavata  they sing  
sei kṛṣṇa avatīrṇa caitanya-gosāñi  
that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  (as) Caitanya  the Lord

(9) He whom the «Bhāgavatam» glorifies as the son of Nanda — that same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi.

প্রকাশবিশেষে তেঁহ ধরে তিন নাম ৷  
ব্রহ্ম, পরমাত্মা আর স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ১০ ॥

prakāśa-viśeṣe tem̐ha dhare tina nāma  
in particular manifestations  He  bears  three  names  
brahma, paramātmā āra svayaṁ bhagavān  
Brahman,  Paramātmā  and  Himself  Bhagavān

(10) In His particular manifestations He bears three names: Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān Himself.

বদন্তি তত্তত্ত্ববিদস্তত্ত্বং যজ্জ্ঞানমদ্বয়ম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মেতি পরমাত্মেতি ভগবানিতি শব্দ্যতে ॥ ১১ ॥

vadanti tat tattva-vidas  
they call  that  of truth  the knowers  
tattvaṁ yaj jñānam advayam  
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual  
brahmeti paramātmeti  
Brahman  thus  Paramātmā  thus  
bhagavān iti śabdyate  
Bhagavān  thus  is named

(11) “The knowers of the Truth call the non-dual knowledge the Truth; it is named Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.11)[^7]

তাঁহার অঙ্গের শুদ্ধ কিরণ-মণ্ডল ৷  
উপনিষদ্ কহে তাঁরে ব্রহ্ম সুনির্ম্মল ॥ ১২ ॥

tām̐hāra aṅgera śuddha kiraṇa-maṇḍala  
His  of the body  pure  of rays  the orb  
upaniṣad kahe tām̐re brahma sunirmala  
the Upaniṣads  call  that  Brahman  most pure

(12) That which the Upaniṣads call the most pure Brahman is the pure radiance of the rays of His body.

চর্ম্মচক্ষে দেখে যৈছে সূর্য্য নির্ব্বিশেষ ৷  
জ্ঞানমার্গে লইতে নারে তাঁহার বিশেষ ॥ ১৩ ॥

carma-cakṣe dekhe yaiche sūrya nirviśeṣa  
with fleshly  eyes  one sees  as  the sun  without features  
jñāna-mārge laite nāre tām̐hāra viśeṣa  
on the path of knowledge  to perceive  cannot  His  particular features

(13) As the sun appears to fleshly eyes as mere brilliance without features, so on the path of knowledge one cannot perceive His particular features.

যস্য প্রভা প্রভবতো জগদণ্ডকোটি-  
কোটিষ্বশেষবসুধাদিবিভূতিভিন্নম্ ৷  
তদ্ব্রহ্মনিষ্কলমনন্তমশেষভূতং  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ১৪ ॥

yasya prabhā prabhavato jagad-aṇḍa-koṭi-  
whose  effulgence  of the all-mighty  of universes  of millions  
koṭiṣv aśeṣa-vasudhādi-vibhūti-bhinnam  
in millions  unlimited  earths and the like  opulences  distinct from  
tad brahma niṣkalam anantam aśeṣa-bhūtaṁ  
that  Brahman  undivided  infinite  all-complete  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(14) “I worship Govinda, the original Person. The effulgence of Him, the all-mighty, is Brahman — undivided, infinite and complete, distinct from the opulences of countless universes, their earths and all else.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.40)[^8]

কোটী কোটী ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে যে ব্রহ্মের বিভূতি ৷  
সেই ব্রহ্ম গোবিন্দের হয় অঙ্গকান্তি ॥ ১৫ ॥

koṭī koṭī brahmāṇḍe ye brahmera vibhūti  
millions  upon millions  in the universes  which  of Brahman  the glory  
sei brahma govindera haya aṅga-kānti  
that  Brahman  of Govinda  is  of the body  the radiance

(15) That Brahman whose glory is spread through millions upon millions of universes is the radiance of Govinda’s body.

সেই গোবিন্দ ভজি আমি, তেঁহো মোর পতি ৷  
তাঁহার প্রসাদে মোর হয় সৃষ্টিশক্তি ॥ ১৬ ॥

sei govinda bhaji āmi, tem̐ho mora pati  
that  Govinda  I worship  I,  He  my  Lord  
tām̐hāra prasāde mora haya sṛṣṭi-śakti  
His  by the grace  to me  is  of creation  the power

(16) I worship that Govinda; He is my Lord. By His grace I (Brahmā) have the power to create.

মুনয়ো বাতবসনাঃ শ্রমণা ঊর্দ্ধমন্থিনঃ ৷  
ব্রহ্মাখ্যং ধাম তে যান্তি শান্তাঃ সন্ন্যাসিনোঽমলাঃ ॥ ১৭ ॥

munayo vāta-vasanāḥ  
the sages  the wind  as clothing  
śramaṇā ūrdhva-manthinaḥ  
ascetics  upward  (who keep their seed)  
brahmākhyaṁ dhāma te yānti  
called Brahman  the abode  they  go to  
śāntāḥ sannyāsino ’malāḥ  
peaceful  renunciants  pure

(17) “Sages clothed by the wind, ascetics who keep their seed drawn upward, peaceful, pure renunciants — they go to the abode called Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.6.47)[^9]

আত্মান্তর্য্যামী যাঁরে যোগশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
সেহ গোবিন্দের অংশ বিভূতি যে হয় ॥ ১৮ ॥

ātmāntaryāmī yām̐re yoga-śāstre kaya  
the Supersoul  the Indweller  whom  in the yoga  scriptures  they speak of  
seha govindera aṁśa vibhūti ye haya  
He  of Govinda  portion  manifestation  indeed  is

(18) The Supersoul, the Indweller, of whom the scriptures of yoga speak, is a partial manifestation of Govinda.[^10]

অনন্ত স্ফটিকে যৈছে এক সূর্য্য ভাসে ৷  
তৈছে জীবে গোবিন্দের অংশ প্রকাশে ॥ ১৯ ॥

ananta sphaṭike yaiche eka sūrya bhāse  
in countless  crystals  as  one  sun  is reflected  
taiche jīve govindera aṁśa prakāśe  
so  in living beings  of Govinda  the portion  is manifest

(19) As one sun is reflected in countless crystals, so Govinda’s portion, the Supersoul, is manifest in every living being.[^11]

অথবা বহুনৈতেন কিং জ্ঞাতেন তবার্জ্জুন ৷  
বিষ্টভ্যাহমিদং কৃৎস্নমেকাংশেন স্থিতো জগৎ ॥ ২০ ॥

atha vā bahunaitena  
but  or  with much  this  
kiṁ jñātena tavārjuna  
what (use)  by knowing  for you  Arjuna  
viṣṭabhyāham idaṁ kṛtsnam  
pervading  I  this  entire  
ekāṁśena sthito jagat  
by one portion  I stand  the universe

(20) “But what need have you, Arjuna, of all this detailed knowledge? By one portion of Myself I pervade and support this entire universe.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 10.42)[^12]

তমিমমহমজং শরীরভাজাং  
হৃদি হৃদি ধিষ্ঠিতমাত্মকল্পিতানাম্ ৷  
প্রতিদৃশমিব নৈকধার্কমেকং  
সমধিগতোঽস্মি বিধূতভেদমোহঃ ॥ ২১ ॥

tam imam aham ajaṁ śarīra-bhājāṁ  
Him  this  I  the unborn  of the embodied  
hṛdi hṛdi dhiṣṭhitam ātma-kalpitānām  
in heart  in heart  abiding  created by Himself  
prati-dṛśam iva naikadhārkam ekaṁ  
to each  eye  as if  in many ways  the sun  one  
samadhigato ’smi vidhūta-bheda-mohaḥ  
have attained  I  having cast off  of difference  the illusion

(21) (Grandsire Bhīṣma said:) “O Śrī Kṛṣṇa! Having cast off the illusion of ‘difference’ between all Your forms, I have attained You, the Unborn, who abide in the heart of each of the embodied beings You have created — like the one sun, which each eye sees in its own way.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.9.42)[^13]

সেই ত’ গোবিন্দ সাক্ষাচ্চৈতন্য গোসাঞি ৷  
জীব নিস্তারিতে ঐছে দয়ালু আর নাই ॥ ২২ ॥

sei ta’ govinda sākṣāc caitanya gosāñi  
that  indeed  Govinda  directly  Caitanya  the Lord  
jīva nistārite aiche dayālu āra nāi  
the souls  in delivering  so  merciful  another  there is not

(22) That very Govinda is Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi Himself. There is none so merciful in delivering the souls.[^14]

পরব্যোমেতে বৈসে নারায়ণ নাম ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যপূর্ণ লক্ষ্মীকান্ত ভগবান্ ॥ ২৩ ॥

para-vyomete vaise nārāyaṇa nāma  
in the spiritual  sky  dwells  Nārāyaṇa  named  
ṣaḍ-aiśvarya-pūrṇa lakṣmī-kānta bhagavān  
with six  opulences  full  of Lakṣmī  the beloved  Bhagavān

(23) In the spiritual sky dwells the Lord called Nārāyaṇa — Bhagavān, full in the six opulences, the beloved of Lakṣmī.

বেদ, ভাগবত, উপনিষদ্, আগম ৷  
‘পূর্ণতত্ত্ব’ যাঁরে কহে, নাহি যাঁর সম ॥ ২৪ ॥

veda, bhāgavata, upaniṣad, āgama  
the Vedas,  the Bhāgavata,  the Upaniṣads,  the Āgamas  
‘pūrṇa-tattva’ yām̐re kahe, nāhi yām̐ra sama  
‘the complete  Truth’  whom  they call,  there is not  to whom  an equal

(24) He whom the Vedas, the «Bhāgavatam», the Upaniṣads and the Āgamas call “the complete Truth”, and who has no equal —

ভক্তিযোগে ভক্ত পায় যাঁহার দর্শন ৷  
সূর্য্য যেন সবিগ্রহ দেখে দেবগণ ॥ ২৫ ॥

bhakti-yoge bhakta pāya yām̐hāra darśana  
through bhakti-yoga  the devotees  attain  whose  sight  
sūrya yena savigraha dekhe deva-gaṇa  
the sun  as  with its form  see  the gods

(25) Him the devotees behold through bhakti-yoga, as the gods behold the sun in its own form.[^15]

জ্ঞান-যোগ-মার্গে তাঁরে ভজে যেই সব ৷  
ব্রহ্ম-আত্মরূপে তাঁরে করে অনুভব ॥ ২৬ ॥

jñāna-yoga-mārge tām̐re bhaje yei saba  
on the path of knowledge and yoga  Him  worship  who  all  
brahma-ātma-rūpe tām̐re kare anubhava  
as Brahman and the Self  Him  they  realize

(26) Those who worship Him on the paths of knowledge and yoga realize Him as Brahman and as the Supersoul.

উপাসনা-ভেদে জানি ঈশ্বর-মহিমা ৷  
অতএব সূর্য্য তাঁর দিয়ে ত’ উপমা ॥ ২৭ ॥

upāsanā-bhede jāni īśvara-mahimā  
by the difference of worship  I know  of the Lord  the greatness  
ataeva sūrya tām̐ra diye ta’ upamā  
therefore  the sun  for Him  is given  indeed  as a simile

(27) By the differences in worship we know the greatness of the Lord; that is why He is compared to the sun.

সেই নারায়ণ কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ অভেদ ৷  
একই বিগ্রহ, কিন্তু আকার-বিভেদ ॥ ২৮ ॥

sei nārāyaṇa kṛṣṇera svarūpa abheda  
that  Nārāyaṇa  from Kṛṣṇa’s  own form  not different  
ekai vigraha, kintu ākāra-vibheda  
one and the same  form,  but  of shape  difference

(28) That Nārāyaṇa is not different from Kṛṣṇa’s own form: They are one and the same Person, differing only in shape.

ইঁহো ত’ দ্বিভুজ, তিঁহো ধরে চারি হাথ ৷  
ইঁহো বেণু ধরে, তিঁহো চক্রাদিক সাথ ॥ ২৯ ॥

im̐ho ta’ dvi-bhuja, tim̐ho dhare cāri hātha  
this One  indeed  two-armed,  that One  holds  four  hands  
im̐ho veṇu dhare, tim̐ho cakrādika sātha  
this One  the flute  holds,  that One  the disc and the rest  with

(29) This One has two arms, while that One has four; this One holds the flute, that One the disc and the other emblems.

নারায়ণস্ত্বং ন হি সর্ব্বদেহিনা-  
মাত্মাস্যধীশাখিললোকসাক্ষী ৷  
নারায়ণোঽঙ্গং নরভূ-জলায়না-  
ত্তচ্চাপি সত্যং ন তবৈব মায়া ॥ ৩০ ॥

nārāyaṇas tvaṁ na hi sarva-dehinām  
Nārāyaṇa  You  not  indeed  of all  the embodied  
ātmāsy adhīśākhila-loka-sākṣī  
the Self  You are  O Lord  of all  worlds  the witness  
nārāyaṇo ’ṅgaṁ nara-bhū-jalāyanāt  
Nārāyaṇa  (Your) limb  from Nara  born  waters  abode  
tac cāpi satyaṁ na tavaiva māyā  
that  also  true  not  Your  indeed  māyā

(30) “Are You not Nārāyaṇa? For You are the Self of all embodied beings, O Lord, and the witness of all the worlds. Nārāyaṇa is Your portion — so called because His abode is the waters born of Nara; and that too is true, not Your māyā.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.14)[^16]

শিশু বৎস হরি’ ব্রহ্মা করি’ অপরাধ ৷  
অপরাধ ক্ষমাইতে মাগেন প্রসাদ ॥ ৩১ ॥

śiśu vatsa hari’ brahmā kari’ aparādha  
the boys  calves  having stolen  Brahmā  having committed  offence  
aparādha kṣamāite māgena prasāda  
the offence  to have forgiven  begs  for mercy

(31) Having stolen the boys and the calves, Brahmā had committed an offence; to have it forgiven, he begged for mercy:

তোমার নাভিপদ্ম হৈতে আমার জন্মোদয় ৷  
তুমি পিতা-মাতা, আমি তোমার তনয় ॥ ৩২ ॥

tomāra nābhi-padma haite āmāra janmodaya  
Your  of the navel  from the lotus  my  birth  
tumi pitā-mātā, āmi tomāra tanaya  
You  father and mother,  I  Your  son

(32) “From the lotus of Your navel I was born; You are my father and mother, and I am Your son.

পিতা মাতা বালকের না লয় অপরাধ ৷  
অপরাধ ক্ষম, মোরে করহ প্রসাদ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

pitā mātā bālakera nā laya aparādha  
father  mother  of a child  do not  take  the offences  
aparādha kṣama, more karaha prasāda  
the offence  forgive,  to me  show  mercy

(33) A father and mother do not hold a child’s offences against him. Forgive my offence and be merciful to me.”

কৃষ্ণ কহেন — ব্রহ্মা, তোমার পিতা নারায়ণ ৷  
আমি গোপ, তুমি কৈছে আমার নন্দন ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahena — brahmā, tomāra pitā nārāyaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  says —  Brahmā,  your  father  Nārāyaṇa  
āmi gopa, tumi kaiche āmāra nandana  
I  a cowherd,  you  how  My  son

(34) Kṛṣṇa said: “Brahmā, your father is Nārāyaṇa. I am a cowherd boy — how can you be My son?”

ব্রহ্মা বলেন, তুমি কি না হও নারায়ণ ৷  
তুমি নারায়ণ — শুন তাহার কারণ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

brahmā balena, tumi ki nā hao nārāyaṇa  
Brahmā  says,  You  whether  not  are  Nārāyaṇa  
tumi nārāyaṇa — śuna tāhāra kāraṇa  
You  Nārāyaṇa —  hear  of that  the reason

(35) Brahmā said: “Are You not Nārāyaṇa? You are Nārāyaṇa — hear the reason.

প্রাকৃতাপ্রাকৃত-সৃষ্ট্যে যত জীবরূপ ৷  
তাহার যে আত্মা তুমি মূল-স্বরূপ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

prākṛtāprākṛta-sṛṣṭye yata jīva-rūpa  
in the material and spiritual  creation  all  living  forms  
tāhāra ye ātmā tumi mūla-svarūpa  
their  who  the Self  You  the original  form

(36) You are the Self of all living beings, as many as there are in the material and spiritual creation; You are their original source.[^17]

পৃথ্বী যৈছে ঘটকুলের কারণ আশ্রয় ৷  
জীবের নিদান তুমি, তুমি সর্ব্বাশ্রয় ॥ ৩৭ ॥

pṛthvī yaiche ghaṭa-kulera kāraṇa āśraya  
the earth  as  of the pots  of the multitude  the cause  the support  
jīvera nidāna tumi, tumi sarvāśraya  
of the souls  the cause  You,  You  of all  the shelter

(37) As the earth is the cause and the support of all earthen pots, so You are the cause of all souls, and You are the shelter of all.

‘নার’ শব্দে কহে সর্ব্বজীবের নিচয় ৷  
‘অয়ন’ শব্দেতে কহে তাহার আশ্রয় ॥ ৩৮ ॥

‘nāra’ śabde kahe sarva-jīvera nicaya  
‘nāra’  by the word  is meant  of all souls  the aggregate  
‘ayana’ śabdete kahe tāhāra āśraya  
‘ayana’  by the word  is meant  their  shelter

(38) The word *nāra* means the aggregate of all souls, and the word *ayana* means their shelter.

অতএব তুমি হও মূল নারায়ণ ৷  
এই এক হেতু, শুন দ্বিতীয় কারণ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

ataeva tumi hao mūla nārāyaṇa  
therefore  You  are  the original  Nārāyaṇa  
ei eka hetu, śuna dvitīya kāraṇa  
this  one  reason,  hear  the second  reason

(39) Therefore You are the original Nārāyaṇa. That is one reason; hear now the second.

জীবের ঈশ্বর — পুরুষাদি অবতার ৷  
তাঁহা সবা হৈতে তোমার ঐশ্বর্য্য অপার ॥ ৪০ ॥

jīvera īśvara — puruṣādi avatāra  
of the souls  the lords —  the Puruṣas and other  avatāras  
tām̐hā sabā haite tomāra aiśvarya apāra  
Them  all  than  Your  majesty  unlimited

(40) The Puruṣas and the other avatāras are the lords of the souls, but Your majesty is unlimited, greater than that of Them all.

অতএব অধীশ্বর তুমি সর্ব্বপিতা ৷  
তোমার শক্তিতে তাঁরা জগৎ-রক্ষিতা ॥ ৪১ ॥

ataeva adhīśvara tumi sarva-pitā  
therefore  the supreme Lord  You  of all  the father  
tomāra śaktite tām̐rā jagat-rakṣitā  
by Your  power  They  of the world  the protectors

(41) Therefore You are the supreme Lord, the father of all; it is by Your power that They protect the world.

নারের অয়ন যাতে করহ পালন ৷  
অতএব হও তুমি মূল নারায়ণ ॥ ৪২ ॥

nārera ayana yāte karaha pālana  
of the nāras  the shelters  since  You do  protection  
ataeva hao tumi mūla nārāyaṇa  
therefore  are  You  the original  Nārāyaṇa

(42) Since You protect those who are the shelter of the nāras, You are the original Nārāyaṇa.

তৃতীয় কারণ শুন শ্রীভগবান্ ৷  
অনন্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ড বহু বৈকুণ্ঠাদি ধাম ॥ ৪৩ ॥

tṛtīya kāraṇa śuna śrī-bhagavān  
the third  reason  hear  O Śrī Bhagavān  
ananta brahmāṇḍa bahu vaikuṇṭhādi dhāma  
unlimited  universes  many  Vaikuṇṭhas and other  abodes

(43) Hear the third reason, O Śrī Bhagavān. There are unlimited universes and many abodes — the Vaikuṇṭhas and others.

ইথে যত জীব, তার ত্রৈকালিক কর্ম্ম ৷  
তাহা দেখ, সাক্ষী তুমি, জান সব মর্ম্ম ॥ ৪৪ ॥

ithe yata jīva, tāra trai-kālika karma  
in them  all  beings,  their  of the three times  deeds  
tāhā dekha, sākṣī tumi, jāna saba marma  
that  You see,  the witness  You,  You know  of all  the essence

(44) All the beings in them, and their deeds in past, present and future — You see it all; You are the witness, You know the heart of everything.

তোমার দর্শনে সর্ব্ব জগতের স্থিতি ৷  
তুমি না দেখিলে কার নাহি স্থিতি গতি ॥ ৪৫ ॥

tomāra darśane sarva jagatera sthiti  
by Your  sight  of all  worlds  the existence  
tumi nā dekhile kāra nāhi sthiti gati  
You  not  if You see —  of anyone  is not  rest  motion

(45) By Your glance all the worlds exist; if You did not look on them, nothing could either stand or move.

নারের অয়ন যাতে কর দরশন ৷  
তাহাতেও হও তুমি মূল নারায়ণ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

nārera ayana yāte kara daraśana  
of the nāras  the ways  since  You do  seeing  
tāhāteo hao tumi mūla nārāyaṇa  
by that also  are  You  the original  Nārāyaṇa

(46) Since You see the ways of all the nāras, by that too You are the original Nārāyaṇa.”

কৃষ্ণ কহেন — ব্রহ্মা, তোমার না বুঝি বচন ৷  
জীব-হৃদি, জলে বৈসে সেই নারায়ণ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahena — brahmā, tomāra nā bujhi vacana  
Kṛṣṇa  says —  Brahmā,  your  not  I understand  words  
jīva-hṛdi, jale vaise sei nārāyaṇa  
in the hearts of beings,  in the waters  dwells  that  Nārāyaṇa

(47) Kṛṣṇa said: “Brahmā, I do not understand your words. Nārāyaṇa is He who dwells in the hearts of living beings and in the waters.”

ব্রহ্মা কহে — জলে জীবে যেই নারায়ণ ৷  
সে সব তোমার অংশ, — এ সত্য বচন ॥ ৪৮ ॥

brahmā kahe — jale jīve yei nārāyaṇa  
Brahmā  says —  in the waters  in beings  who  Nārāyaṇa  
se saba tomāra aṁśa, — e satya vacana  
They  all  Your  portions, —  this  true  word

(48) Brahmā said: “The Nārāyaṇa who lies in the waters and dwells in the beings — in all these forms He is Your portion. What I say is true.

কারণাব্ধি-গর্ভোদক-ক্ষীরোদকশায়ী ৷  
মায়াদ্বারা সৃষ্টি করে, তাতে সব মায়ী ॥ ৪৯ ॥

kāraṇābdhi-garbhodaka-kṣīrodaka-śāyī  
of the Causal ocean  Garbhodaka  Kṣīrodaka  who lie  
māyā-dvārā sṛṣṭi kare, tāte saba māyī  
through māyā  creation  They do,  therefore  all  masters of māyā

(49) Kāraṇodakaśāyī, Garbhodakaśāyī and Kṣīrodakaśāyī Viṣṇu create through māyā; therefore all of Them are *māyī*, masters of māyā.[^18]

সেই তিন জলশায়ী সর্ব্ব-অন্তর্য্যামী ৷  
ব্রহ্মাণ্ডবৃন্দের আত্মা যে পুরুষ-নামী ॥ ৫০ ॥

sei tina jala-śāyī sarva-antaryāmī  
those  three  who lie on the waters  of all  the Indwellers  
brahmāṇḍa-vṛndera ātmā ye puruṣa-nāmī  
of the universes  of the multitude  the Self  who  Puruṣa  named

(50) These three, who lie on the waters, dwell within all as the Supersoul: They who are called Puruṣas are the Self of all the universes.[^19]

হিরণ্যগর্ভের আত্মা গর্ভোদকশায়ী ৷  
ব্যষ্টিজীব-অন্তর্য্যামী ক্ষীরোদকশায়ী ॥ ৫১ ॥

hiraṇya-garbhera ātmā garbhodaka-śāyī  
of Hiraṇyagarbha  the Self  Garbhodakaśāyī  
vyaṣṭi-jīva-antaryāmī kṣīrodaka-śāyī  
of the individual being  the Indweller  Kṣīrodakaśāyī

(51) Garbhodakaśāyī is the Self of Hiraṇyagarbha, and Kṣīrodakaśāyī is the Indweller of each individual being.[^20]

এ সভার দর্শনে ত’ আছে মায়াগন্ধ ৷  
তুরীয় কৃষ্ণের নাহি মায়ার সম্বন্ধ ॥ ৫২ ॥

e sabhāra darśane ta’ āche māyā-gandha  
of Them  all  in the glance  indeed  there is  of māyā  a trace  
turīya kṛṣṇera nāhi māyāra sambandha  
of the fourth  Kṛṣṇa  there is not  with māyā  connection

(52) In Their glancing upon māyā there is, as it were, a trace of māyā; but Kṛṣṇa, who abides in the fourth (*turīya*), has no connection with māyā at all.[^21]

বিরাট্ হিরণ্যগর্ভশ্চ কারণং চেত্যুপাধয়ঃ ৷  
ঈশস্য যৎ ত্রিভির্হীনং তুরীয়ং তৎ প্রচক্ষতে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

virāṭ hiraṇya-garbhaś ca  
Virāṭ  Hiraṇyagarbha  and  
kāraṇaṁ cety upādhayaḥ  
Kāraṇa  thus  designations  
īśasya yat tribhir hīnaṁ  
of the Lord  that which  of the three  devoid  
turīyaṁ tat pracakṣate  
the fourth  that  they call

(53) “Virāṭ, Hiraṇyagarbha and Kāraṇa are designations of the Lord; that which is free from these three is called the fourth (*turīya*).” (Śrīdhara Svāmī’s commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.15.16)[^22]

যদ্যপি তিনের মায়া লইয়া ব্যবহার ৷  
তথাপি তৎস্পর্শ নাহি, সভে মায়া-পার ॥ ৫৪ ॥

yadyapi tinera māyā laiyā vyavahāra  
although  of the three  with māyā  having to do  dealings  
tathāpi tat-sparśa nāhi, sabhe māyā-pāra  
yet  its  touch  is not,  all  beyond māyā

(54) Although the three have dealings with māyā, she does not touch Them: all of Them are beyond māyā.[^23]

এতদীশনমীশস্য প্রকৃতিস্থোঽপি তদ্গুণৈঃ ৷  
ন যুজ্যতে সদাত্মস্থৈর্যথা বুদ্ধিস্তদাশ্রয়া ॥ ৫৫ ॥

etad īśanam īśasya  
this  the lordship  of the Lord  
prakṛti-stho ’pi tad-guṇaiḥ  
in material nature  abiding  though  by its qualities  
na yujyate sadātma-sthair  
not  is affected  ever  abiding in Him  
yathā buddhis tad-āśrayā  
as  the intelligence  sheltered in Him

(55) “This is the lordship of the Lord: though He abides within material nature, He is never affected by its qualities — just as the intelligence of those who have taken shelter of Him is not affected.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.11.38)

সেই তিন জনের তুমি পরম আশ্রয় ৷  
তুমি মূলনারায়ণ — ইথে কি সংশয় ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sei tina janera tumi parama āśraya  
those  three  Persons’  You  supreme  shelter  
tumi mūla nārāyaṇa — ithe ki saṁśaya  
You  the original  Nārāyaṇa —  in this  what  doubt

(56) You are the supreme shelter of those three; You are the original Nārāyaṇa — what doubt can there be?

সেই তিনের অংশী পরব্যোম-নারায়ণ ৷  
তেঁহ তোমার প্রকাশ, তুমি মূল-নারায়ণ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

sei tinera aṁśī paravyoma-nārāyaṇa  
of those  three  the source  of the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  
tem̐ha tomāra prakāśa, tumi mūla-nārāyaṇa  
He  Your  manifestation,  You  the original  Nārāyaṇa

(57) The source of those three is the Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky. He is Your manifestation (*prakāśa*), and You are the original Nārāyaṇa.”[^24]

অতএব ব্রহ্মবাক্যে পরব্যোম-নারায়ণ ৷  
তেঁহ কৃষ্ণের প্রকাশ — এই তত্ত্ব-বিবরণ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

ataeva brahma-vākye paravyoma-nārāyaṇa  
therefore  of Brahmā  in the words  of the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  
tem̐ha kṛṣṇera prakāśa — ei tattva-vivaraṇa  
He  Kṛṣṇa’s  manifestation —  this  of the truth  explanation

(58) Thus, by Brahmā’s words, the Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky is a manifestation of Kṛṣṇa. This is the explanation of the truth.[^25]

এই শ্লোক তত্ত্ব-লক্ষণ ভাগবত-সার ৷  
পরিভাষা-রূপে ইহার সর্ব্বত্রাধিকার ॥ ৫৯ ॥

ei śloka tattva-lakṣaṇa bhāgavata-sāra  
this  verse  of the truth  characteristic  of the Bhāgavata  the essence  
paribhāṣā-rūpe ihāra sarvatrādhikāra  
of a paribhāṣā  in the form  its  everywhere  application

(59) This verse (2.30), which reveals the characteristics of the Truth, is the essence of the «Bhāgavatam»; as a rule of interpretation (*paribhāṣā*) it applies everywhere.[^26]

ব্রহ্ম, আত্মা, ভগবান্ — কৃষ্ণের বিহার ৷  
এ অর্থ না জানি’ মূর্খ অর্থ করে আর ॥ ৬০ ॥

brahma, ātmā, bhagavān — kṛṣṇera vihāra  
Brahman,  Paramātmā,  Bhagavān —  of Kṛṣṇa  the manifestations  
e artha nā jāni’ mūrkha artha kare āra  
this  meaning  not  knowing  the foolish  meaning  make  other

(60) Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān are manifestations of Kṛṣṇa. Not knowing this meaning, fools interpret otherwise.[^27]

অবতারী নারায়ণ, কৃষ্ণ অবতার ৷  
তেঁহ চতুর্ভুজ, ইঁহ মনুষ্য-আকার ॥ ৬১ ॥

avatārī nārāyaṇa, kṛṣṇa avatāra  
the source of avatāras  Nārāyaṇa,  Kṛṣṇa  an avatāra  
tem̐ha catur-bhuja, im̐ha manuṣya-ākāra  
That One  four-armed,  this One  of human  shape

(61) They say: “Nārāyaṇa is the source of the avatāras, and Kṛṣṇa is His avatāra; for That One has four arms, while this One has a human form.”

এইমতে নানারূপ করে পূর্ব্বপক্ষ ৷  
তাহারে নির্জ্জিতে ভাগবত-পদ্য দক্ষ ॥ ৬২ ॥

ei-mate nānā-rūpa kare pūrva-pakṣa  
in this way  various  kinds  make  objections  
tāhāre nirjite bhāgavata-padya dakṣa  
them  to defeat  of the Bhāgavata  a verse  is expert

(62) In this way they raise various objections, but a verse of the «Bhāgavatam» is expert in defeating them.

বদন্তি তত্তত্ত্ববিদস্তত্ত্বং যজ্জ্ঞানমদ্বয়ম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মেতি পরমাত্মেতি ভগবানিতি শব্দ্যতে ॥ ৬৩ ॥

vadanti tat tattva-vidas  
they call  that  of truth  the knowers  
tattvaṁ yaj jñānam advayam  
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual  
brahmeti paramātmeti  
Brahman  thus  Paramātmā  thus  
bhagavān iti śabdyate  
Bhagavān  thus  is named

(63) “The knowers of the Truth call the non-dual knowledge the Truth; it is named Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.11)[^28]

শুন ভাই, এ শ্লোকার্থ করহ বিচার ৷  
এক মুখ্যতত্ত্ব, তিন তাহার প্রচার ॥ ৬৪ ॥

śuna bhāi, e ślokārtha karaha vicāra  
hear  brothers,  of this  verse’s meaning  make  consideration  
eka mukhya-tattva, tina tāhāra pracāra  
one  principal  Truth,  three  its  manifestations

(64) Hear, brothers, and consider the meaning of this verse: the principal Truth is one, and It is manifest in three ways.

অদ্বয়জ্ঞান তত্ত্ববস্তু কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ ৷  
ব্রহ্ম, আত্মা, ভগবান্, — তিন তাঁর রূপ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

advaya-jñāna tattva-vastu kṛṣṇera svarūpa  
non-dual  knowledge  the real  Truth  Kṛṣṇa’s  own form  
brahma, ātmā, bhagavān, — tina tām̐ra rūpa  
Brahman,  Paramātmā,  Bhagavān, —  three  His  forms

(65) The non-dual knowledge, the Truth itself, is Kṛṣṇa’s own form; Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān are His three forms.[^29]

এই শ্লোকের অর্থে তুমি হৈলা নির্ব্বচন ৷  
আর এক শুন ভাগবতের বচন ॥ ৬৬ ॥

ei ślokera arthe tumi hailā nirvacana  
of this  verse  by the meaning  you  became  speechless  
āra eka śuna bhāgavatera vacana  
another  one  hear  of the Bhāgavata  statement

(66) The meaning of this verse has left you speechless. Now hear another statement of the «Bhāgavatam».

এতে চাংশকলাঃ পুংসঃ কৃষ্ণস্তু ভগবান্ স্বয়ম্ ৷  
ইন্দ্রারিব্যাকুলং লোকং মৃড়য়ন্তি যুগে যুগে ॥ ৬৭ ॥

ete cāṁśa-kalāḥ puṁsaḥ  
these  and  portions  and parts  of the Puruṣa  
kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam  
Kṛṣṇa  but  Bhagavān  Himself  
indrāri-vyākulaṁ lokaṁ  
by the enemies of Indra  troubled  the world  
mṛḍayanti yuge yuge  
make happy  in age  after age

(67) “All these (avatāras) are portions (*aṁśa*) and parts of portions (*kalā*) of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself. Age after age They bring happiness to the world when it is troubled by the enemies of Indra.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.28)[^30]

সব অবতারের করি সামান্য-লক্ষণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে কৃষ্ণচন্দ্রের করিল গণন ॥ ৬৮ ॥

saba avatārera kari sāmānya-lakṣaṇa  
all  of the avatāras  having made  general  characteristics  
tāra madhye kṛṣṇa-candrera karila gaṇana  
their  among  Kṛṣṇacandra  made  counting

(68) Describing the general characteristics of all the avatāras, he counted Kṛṣṇacandra among them.

তবে সূত গোসাঞি মনে পাঞা বড় ভয় ৷  
যাঁর যে লক্ষণ তাহা করিল নিশ্চয় ॥ ৬৯ ॥

tabe sūta gosāñi mane pāñā baḍa bhaya  
then  Sūta  Gosāñi  in mind  feeling  great  fear  
yām̐ra ye lakṣaṇa tāhā karila niścaya  
of whom  which  characteristic  that  made  determination

(69) Then Sūta Gosāñi, feeling great apprehension in his mind, determined the distinguishing characteristic of each.

অবতার সব — পুরুষের কলা-অংশ ৷  
স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ সর্ব্ব-অবতংস ॥ ৭০ ॥

avatāra saba — puruṣera kalā-aṁśa  
the avatāras  all —  of the Puruṣa  parts  portions  
svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa sarva-avataṁsa  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of all  the crown

(70) All the avatāras are portions and parts of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself, the crown of all.

পূর্ব্বপক্ষ কহে — তোমার ভাল ত’ ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
পরব্যোমে-নারায়ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ৭১ ॥

pūrva-pakṣa kahe — tomāra bhāla ta’ vyākhyāna  
the opponent  says —  your  good  indeed  explanation  
paravyome-nārāyaṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
of the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  Himself  Bhagavān

(71) An opponent says: “Your explanation is fine, but the Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky is Bhagavān Himself.

তেঁহ আসি’ কৃষ্ণরূপে করেন অবতার ৷  
এই অর্থ শ্লোকে দেখি — কি আর বিচার ॥ ৭২ ॥

tem̐ha āsi’ kṛṣṇa-rūpe karena avatāra  
He  coming  in the form of Kṛṣṇa  makes  descent  
ei artha śloke dekhi — ki āra vicāra  
this  meaning  in the verse  I see —  what  further  consideration

(72) It is He who comes and descends in the form of Kṛṣṇa. This is the meaning I see in the verse — what more is there to consider?”

তারে কহে — কেনে কর কুতর্কানুমান ৷  
শাস্ত্রবিরুদ্ধার্থ কভু না হয় প্রমাণ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

tāre kahe — kene kara kutarkānumāna  
to him  I say —  why  do you  of false logic  inferences  
śāstra-viruddhārtha kabhu nā haya pramāṇa  
to scripture  contrary  meaning  ever  not  is  evidence

(73) To him I say: “Why do you draw inferences by false logic? A meaning contrary to scripture can never be evidence.

অনুবাদমনুক্ত্বা তু ন বিধেয়মুদীরয়েৎ ৷  
ন হ্যলব্ধাস্পদং কিঞ্চিৎ কুত্রচিৎ প্রতিতিষ্ঠতি ॥ ৭৪ ॥

anuvādam anuktvā tu  
the subject  not having stated  but  
na vidheyam udīrayet  
not  the predicate  one should utter  
na hy alabdhāspadaṁ kiñcit  
not  indeed  without a footing  anything  
kutracit pratitiṣṭhati  
anywhere  stands

(74) ‘One should not state the predicate without first stating the subject; for nothing that has not found its footing can stand anywhere.’ (Ekādaśī-tattva, 13)[^31]

অনুবাদ না কহিয়া না কহি বিধেয় ৷  
আগে অনুবাদ কহি, পশ্চাদ্বিধেয় ॥ ৭৫ ॥

anuvāda nā kahiyā nā kahi vidheya  
the subject  not  having stated  not  one states  the predicate  
āge anuvāda kahi, paścād vidheya  
first  the subject  one states,  afterwards  the predicate

(75) Without stating the subject one does not state the predicate: first the subject is stated, and afterwards the predicate.

[^1]: «Anubhāṣya»: *bālaḥ* — “an inexperienced child”; the crocodiles are the mutually contending doctrines of Aulūkya, Jina, Buddha, Jaimini, Patañjali, Gautama, Kaṇāda, Kapila, Śaṅkara, Dattātreya and others; by the Lord’s mercy even the inexperienced one sets them aside as straw and comes to know the pure feet of Kṛṣṇa.

[^2]: «Anubhāṣya»: the swans, cakravākas and bees are pure devotees in different moods (*bhāva*), for whom these pastimes are a place of supreme joy; the murmur of the river is the sweet, indistinct sound of the stream of Śrī Hari’s pastimes, sung by His devotees.

[^3]: Indicating the Object (*vastu-nirdeśa*) is one of the three kinds of auspicious invocation (Ādi 1.22). In the edition the verse is headed: “explanation of the third of the first fourteen verses — indicating the Object”.

[^4]: The same verse as Ādi 1.3. «Anubhāṣya»: Brahman is the radiance of His transcendental body, and the Paramātmā a particular manifestation of His majesty; both are only two aspects of His form, perceived according to the capacity of the seer — not the fullness of the Lord, as Bhagavān is. «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “He whom they call Bhagavān, the shelter and source of Brahman and Paramātmā, is my Lord, Bhagavān Himself.”

[^5]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: by the rules of rhetoric one first names the *anuvāda*, the known, and then the *vidheya*, the new. That Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān exist is known from the Vedas; that Brahman is the effulgence of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s body, Paramātmā His portion and Bhagavān He Himself, is not yet known — and this is what is stated.

[^6]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “The conclusion of the scriptures is this: the supreme truth of *viṣṇu-tattva* is Bhagavān Himself, Kṛṣṇacandra.”

[^7]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: the first realization of that non-dual knowledge is Brahman, the second Paramātmā, the third Bhagavān.

[^8]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “… the Brahman distinct from the unlimited opulences — earths and the rest — in millions upon millions of universes, undivided, infinite and complete, has arisen from the effulgence of that original Person, Govinda.”

[^9]: «Anubhāṣya»: with this verse it is shown that attaining Kṛṣṇa’s feet is easy, whereas renunciants who labour hard attain, as the fruit of their practice, only the realm of Brahman; *vāta-vasanāḥ* — naked; *śramaṇāḥ* — mendicants who emaciate the body; *brahmākhyam* — featureless.

[^10]: «Anubhāṣya»: the Puruṣa who dwells within is the Paramātmā, “only a manifestation of a portion (*aṁśa-vibhūti*) of Govinda”; the three Puruṣa-avatāras govern the material world, while Bhagavān Himself, abiding in the fourth, *turīya*, is attached to none of its affairs.

[^11]: «Anubhāṣya»: the one Śrī Govinda, ever manifest in Goloka-Vṛndāvana, appears in the hearts of countless souls as the Paramātmā, the Person whom the soul is to serve.

[^12]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “by one portion, as the Paramātmā, I have entered and abide in the whole world.”

[^13]: «Anubhāṣya»: *bheda-mohaḥ* — the illusion that the Lord’s forms (*prakāśa*, *vilāsa*) differ in name, form, qualities and pastimes; *ātma-kalpitānām* — “created by Himself”. Reference to the «Bhāgavatam» as in the edition.

[^14]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: by the word *sākṣāt* (“directly, Himself”) the author establishes that Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is Govinda Himself, not His *prakāśa* or *vilāsa*.

[^15]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: the eternal form of the Lord cannot be seen by material senses or by the efforts of knowledge; through bhakti, through love, only the devotees become fit to see it — as the gods, whose divine eyes pierce the net of the sun’s rays, see the sun’s own form.

[^16]: «Anubhāṣya»: the verse gives three meanings of the name Nārāyaṇa, corresponding to the three Puruṣa-avatāras: “the Self of all the embodied” (*nāra* — the aggregate of souls, *ayana* — their shelter) is Kṣīrodakaśāyī; “the witness of all the worlds” (*ayase* — “You know”) is Garbhodakaśāyī; “He whose abode is the waters born of Nara” is Kāraṇodakaśāyī. All of Them are Your portions, and You are the original Nārāyaṇa. “Not māyā” — not transient like what māyā produces: māyā cannot touch Your spiritual body, even in the avatāras.

[^17]: This is the first reason why Kṛṣṇa is the original Nārāyaṇa: *nāra* — all souls, *ayana* — their shelter (2.38). «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: the material creation lies within māyā (Gītā, 7.4–5).

[^18]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “Because They create through māyā, these three Puruṣas are *māyī*, that is, lords in relation to māyā.” Māyā does not touch Them (2.54).

[^19]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: *ye puruṣa-nāmī* — “They whose name is Puruṣa”. The Self of the universes in this verse is all three of Them: Kāraṇodakaśāyī of all the universes together, Garbhodakaśāyī of the aggregate of beings in a universe, Kṣīrodakaśāyī of each individual being (2.51).

[^20]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: Hiraṇyagarbha is the aggregate of beings (*samaṣṭi*); its Indweller is Garbhodakaśāyī; the Indweller of each separate being (*vyaṣṭi*) is Kṣīrodakaśāyī.

[^21]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: beyond the three Puruṣas is the *turīya*, “the fourth”: Nārāyaṇa, Lord of the spiritual sky, the *vilāsa* of Kṛṣṇacandra, utterly free from any trace of māyā; and the Puruṣas, as it says on 2.54, “only cast Their glance upon māyā, but do not touch it”.

[^22]: «Anubhāṣya»: Virāṭ is the gross, Hiraṇyagarbha the subtle, Kāraṇa — ignorance or *prakṛti*; these are particular manifestations (*upādhi*) of the Puruṣa-avatāra, the creator of the *mahat*; the *turīya*, free from them, is Vaikuṇṭha, beyond the three Puruṣas. Śrīdhara Svāmī cites this verse to explain the word *turīya*.

[^23]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: Hiraṇyagarbha and the other beings, collective and individual, are under māyā; the three Puruṣas, though dealing with her, are beyond her — They are lords of māyā; They glance at her, but do not touch her.

[^24]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «তেঁহ তোমার প্রকাশ» — “He is Your manifestation”. «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: the Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky is the source of the Puruṣa-avatāras; He is “Your secondary manifestation (*gauṇa-prakāśa*) in the form of *vilāsa*”. «Anubhāṣya»: His *vilāsa* is the fourfold form — Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha — from whom the three Puruṣas appear.

[^25]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «তেঁহ কৃষ্ণের প্রকাশ» (another reading: «তেঁহো কৃষ্ণের বিলাস»). In meaning this manifestation is a *vilāsa*: on 2.57 the «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya» calls Nārāyaṇa “a secondary manifestation in the form of *vilāsa*”.

[^26]: «Anubhāṣya»: “this verse” is the one quoted above (2.30), “Are You not Nārāyaṇa…”. «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: *paribhāṣā* — a governing rule: “this characteristic you will find everywhere in the «Bhāgavatam»”.

[^27]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: *vihāra* — a manifestation (*prakāśa*) for His pastimes.

[^28]: The same verse as 2.11 (so the «Anubhāṣya» also notes).

[^29]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “non-dual knowledge” in this verse is the original Reality, Kṛṣṇa’s own form.

[^30]: «Anubhāṣya»: *ete* — “the avatāras named before”; *aṁśāḥ* — portions of the Puruṣa-avatāra, *kalāḥ* — portions of portions; *mṛḍayanti*, “make happy”, refers to these avatāras. «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “Rāma, Nṛsiṁha and others are portions or parts of the Puruṣa; They protect the world oppressed by demons.”

[^31]: The reference is as in the edition: “a rule of rhetoric, «Ekādaśī-tattva», 13”.
