“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 2  
**The Auspicious Invocation Indicating the Object: Determining the Truth of Caitanya**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

শ্রীচৈতন্যপ্রভুং বন্দে বালোঽপি যদনুগ্রহাৎ ৷  
তরেন্নানামতগ্রাহব্যাপ্তং সিদ্ধান্তসাগরম্ ॥ ১ ॥

śrī-caitanya-prabhuṁ vande  
Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship  
bālo ’pi yad-anugrahāt  
a child  even  by whose  mercy  
taren nānā-mata-grāha-  
may cross  of various  doctrines  crocodiles  
vyāptaṁ siddhānta-sāgaram  
filled with  of conclusions  the ocean

(1) I worship Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, by whose mercy even an ignorant child can cross the ocean of philosophical conclusions, which teems with the crocodiles of various doctrines.[^1]

কৃষ্ণোৎকীর্ত্তনগাননর্ত্তনকলাপাথোজনি-ভ্রাজিতা  
সদ্ভক্তাবলিহংসচক্রমধুপশ্রেণীবিহারাস্পদম্ ৷  
কর্ণানন্দিকলধ্বনির্বহতু মে জিহ্বামরুপ্রাঙ্গণে  
শ্রীচৈতন্যদয়ানিধে তব লসল্লীলাসুধাস্বর্ধুনী ॥ ২ ॥

kṛṣṇotkīrtana-gāna-nartana-kalā-pāthojani-bhrājitā  
of Kṛṣṇa  loud glorification  singing  dancing  arts  lotuses  adorned  
sad-bhaktāvali-haṁsa-cakra-madhupa-śreṇī-vihārāspadam  
of true  devotees  rows  swans  cakravāka birds  bees  swarms  of pleasure  abode  
karṇānandi-kala-dhvanir vahatu me jihvā-maru-prāṅgaṇe  
delighting the ears  sweet  sound  may it flow  my  of the tongue  desert  over the expanse  
śrī-caitanya dayā-nidhe tava lasal-līlā-sudhā-svardhunī  
O Śrī Caitanya  of mercy  ocean  Your  shining  pastimes  nectar  celestial Gaṅgā

(2) O Śrī Caitanya, ocean of mercy! May the celestial Gaṅgā of the nectar of Your shining pastimes flow over the desert of my tongue. She is adorned with the lotuses of the loud glorification of Kṛṣṇa, of singing and of dancing; she is the place of delight for the rows of true devotees, who are like swans, cakravāka birds and bees; and her sweet murmur gladdens the ear.[^2]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(3) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

তৃতীয় শ্লোকের অর্থ করি বিবরণ ৷  
বস্তু-নির্দ্দেশরূপ মঙ্গলাচরণ ॥ ৪ ॥

tṛtīya ślokera artha kari vivaraṇa  
of the third  verse  the meaning  I make  explanation  
vastu-nirdeśa-rūpa maṅgalācaraṇa  
the Object  indicating  in the form of  auspicious invocation

(4) Now I shall explain in detail the meaning of the third verse — the auspicious invocation that indicates the Object, the Supreme Truth (*vastu-nirdeśa*).[^3]

যদদ্বৈতং ব্রহ্মোপনিষদি তদপ্যস্য তনুভা  
য আত্মান্তর্যামী পুরুষ ইতি সোঽস্যাংশবিভবঃ ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যৈঃ পূর্ণো য ইহ ভগবান্ স স্বয়ময়ং  
ন চৈতন্যাৎ কৃষ্ণাজ্জগতি পরতত্ত্বং পরমিহ ॥ ৫ ॥

yad advaitaṁ brahmopaniṣadi tad apy asya tanu-bhā  
which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  also  His  bodily  effulgence  
ya ātmāntar-yāmī puruṣa iti so ’syāṁśa-vibhavaḥ  
who  the Supersoul  the Indweller  the Puruṣa  thus  He  His  portion  manifestation  
ṣaḍ-aiśvaryaiḥ pūrṇo ya iha bhagavān sa svayam ayaṁ  
with six  opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one  
na caitanyāt kṛṣṇāj jagati para-tattvaṁ param iha  
not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  supreme  truth  higher  here

(5) “There is no Truth in the world higher than Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. That which the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is the effulgence of His body; the Puruṣa, the Supersoul who dwells within every heart, is His partial manifestation; and Bhagavān, full in all six opulences, is He Himself.”[^4]

ব্রহ্ম, আত্মা, ভগবান্ — অনুবাদ তিন ৷  
অঙ্গপ্রভা, অংশ, স্বরূপ — তিন বিধেয় চিহ্ন ॥ ৬ ॥

brahma, ātmā, bhagavān — anuvāda tina  
Brahman,  Paramātmā,  Bhagavān —  subjects  three  
aṅga-prabhā, aṁśa, svarūpa — tina vidheya cihna  
of the body  effulgence,  portion,  own form —  three  predicates  marks

(6) Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān are the three *anuvādas* — that which is spoken of; the effulgence of the body, the partial manifestation and His own form are the three *vidheyas* — that which is stated about them.[^5]

অনুবাদ আগে, পাছে বিধেয় স্থাপন ৷  
সেই অর্থ কহি, শুন শাস্ত্রবিবরণ ॥ ৭ ॥

anuvāda āge, pāche vidheya sthāpana  
the subject  first,  afterwards  the predicate  placing  
sei artha kahi, śuna śāstra-vivaraṇa  
that  meaning  I tell,  hear  of scripture  the explanation

(7) First the *anuvāda* is stated, and afterwards the *vidheya* is set down. In this way I shall tell the meaning of the verse; hear now the explanation according to scripture.

স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ, বিষ্ণু-পরতত্ত্ব ৷  
পূর্ণজ্ঞান পূর্ণানন্দ পরম মহত্ত্ব ॥ ৮ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa, viṣṇu-paratattva  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa,  of Viṣṇu  the supreme truth  
pūrṇa-jñāna pūrṇānanda parama mahattva  
full  knowledge  full  bliss  supreme  greatness

(8) Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself, the supreme truth among all forms of Viṣṇu (*viṣṇu-tattva*). He is fullness of knowledge, fullness of bliss and the highest greatness.[^6]

‘নন্দসুত’ বলি’ যাঁরে ভাগবতে গাই ৷  
সেই কৃষ্ণ অবতীর্ণ চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ॥ ৯ ॥

‘nanda-suta’ bali’ yām̐re bhāgavate gāi  
‘Nanda’s son’  calling  whom  in the Bhāgavata  they sing  
sei kṛṣṇa avatīrṇa caitanya-gosāñi  
that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  (as) Caitanya  the Lord

(9) He whom the «Bhāgavatam» glorifies as the son of Nanda — that same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi.

প্রকাশবিশেষে তেঁহ ধরে তিন নাম ৷  
ব্রহ্ম, পরমাত্মা আর স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ১০ ॥

prakāśa-viśeṣe tem̐ha dhare tina nāma  
in particular manifestations  He  bears  three  names  
brahma, paramātmā āra svayaṁ bhagavān  
Brahman,  Paramātmā  and  Himself  Bhagavān

(10) In His particular manifestations He bears three names: Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān Himself.

বদন্তি তত্তত্ত্ববিদস্তত্ত্বং যজ্জ্ঞানমদ্বয়ম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মেতি পরমাত্মেতি ভগবানিতি শব্দ্যতে ॥ ১১ ॥

vadanti tat tattva-vidas  
they call  that  of truth  the knowers  
tattvaṁ yaj jñānam advayam  
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual  
brahmeti paramātmeti  
Brahman  thus  Paramātmā  thus  
bhagavān iti śabdyate  
Bhagavān  thus  is named

(11) “The knowers of the Truth call the non-dual knowledge the Truth; it is named Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.11)[^7]

তাঁহার অঙ্গের শুদ্ধ কিরণ-মণ্ডল ৷  
উপনিষদ্ কহে তাঁরে ব্রহ্ম সুনির্ম্মল ॥ ১২ ॥

tām̐hāra aṅgera śuddha kiraṇa-maṇḍala  
His  of the body  pure  of rays  the orb  
upaniṣad kahe tām̐re brahma sunirmala  
the Upaniṣads  call  that  Brahman  most pure

(12) That which the Upaniṣads call the most pure Brahman is the pure radiance of the rays of His body.

চর্ম্মচক্ষে দেখে যৈছে সূর্য্য নির্ব্বিশেষ ৷  
জ্ঞানমার্গে লইতে নারে তাঁহার বিশেষ ॥ ১৩ ॥

carma-cakṣe dekhe yaiche sūrya nirviśeṣa  
with fleshly  eyes  one sees  as  the sun  without features  
jñāna-mārge laite nāre tām̐hāra viśeṣa  
on the path of knowledge  to perceive  cannot  His  particular features

(13) As the sun appears to fleshly eyes as mere brilliance without features, so on the path of knowledge one cannot perceive His particular features.

যস্য প্রভা প্রভবতো জগদণ্ডকোটি-  
কোটিষ্বশেষবসুধাদিবিভূতিভিন্নম্ ৷  
তদ্ব্রহ্মনিষ্কলমনন্তমশেষভূতং  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ১৪ ॥

yasya prabhā prabhavato jagad-aṇḍa-koṭi-  
whose  effulgence  of the all-mighty  of universes  of millions  
koṭiṣv aśeṣa-vasudhādi-vibhūti-bhinnam  
in millions  unlimited  earths and the like  opulences  distinct from  
tad brahma niṣkalam anantam aśeṣa-bhūtaṁ  
that  Brahman  undivided  infinite  all-complete  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(14) “I worship Govinda, the original Person. The effulgence of Him, the all-mighty, is Brahman — undivided, infinite and complete, distinct from the opulences of countless universes, their earths and all else.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.40)[^8]

কোটী কোটী ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে যে ব্রহ্মের বিভূতি ৷  
সেই ব্রহ্ম গোবিন্দের হয় অঙ্গকান্তি ॥ ১৫ ॥

koṭī koṭī brahmāṇḍe ye brahmera vibhūti  
millions  upon millions  in the universes  which  of Brahman  the glory  
sei brahma govindera haya aṅga-kānti  
that  Brahman  of Govinda  is  of the body  the radiance

(15) That Brahman whose glory is spread through millions upon millions of universes is the radiance of Govinda’s body.

সেই গোবিন্দ ভজি আমি, তেঁহো মোর পতি ৷  
তাঁহার প্রসাদে মোর হয় সৃষ্টিশক্তি ॥ ১৬ ॥

sei govinda bhaji āmi, tem̐ho mora pati  
that  Govinda  I worship  I,  He  my  Lord  
tām̐hāra prasāde mora haya sṛṣṭi-śakti  
His  by the grace  to me  is  of creation  the power

(16) I worship that Govinda; He is my Lord. By His grace I (Brahmā) have the power to create.

মুনয়ো বাতবসনাঃ শ্রমণা ঊর্দ্ধমন্থিনঃ ৷  
ব্রহ্মাখ্যং ধাম তে যান্তি শান্তাঃ সন্ন্যাসিনোঽমলাঃ ॥ ১৭ ॥

munayo vāta-vasanāḥ  
the sages  the wind  as clothing  
śramaṇā ūrdhva-manthinaḥ  
ascetics  upward  (who keep their seed)  
brahmākhyaṁ dhāma te yānti  
called Brahman  the abode  they  go to  
śāntāḥ sannyāsino ’malāḥ  
peaceful  renunciants  pure

(17) “Sages clothed by the wind, ascetics who keep their seed drawn upward, peaceful, pure renunciants — they go to the abode called Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.6.47)[^9]

আত্মান্তর্য্যামী যাঁরে যোগশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
সেহ গোবিন্দের অংশ বিভূতি যে হয় ॥ ১৮ ॥

ātmāntaryāmī yām̐re yoga-śāstre kaya  
the Supersoul  the Indweller  whom  in the yoga  scriptures  they speak of  
seha govindera aṁśa vibhūti ye haya  
He  of Govinda  portion  manifestation  indeed  is

(18) The Supersoul, the Indweller, of whom the scriptures of yoga speak, is a partial manifestation of Govinda.[^10]

অনন্ত স্ফটিকে যৈছে এক সূর্য্য ভাসে ৷  
তৈছে জীবে গোবিন্দের অংশ প্রকাশে ॥ ১৯ ॥

ananta sphaṭike yaiche eka sūrya bhāse  
in countless  crystals  as  one  sun  is reflected  
taiche jīve govindera aṁśa prakāśe  
so  in living beings  of Govinda  the portion  is manifest

(19) As one sun is reflected in countless crystals, so Govinda’s portion, the Supersoul, is manifest in every living being.[^11]

অথবা বহুনৈতেন কিং জ্ঞাতেন তবার্জ্জুন ৷  
বিষ্টভ্যাহমিদং কৃৎস্নমেকাংশেন স্থিতো জগৎ ॥ ২০ ॥

atha vā bahunaitena  
but  or  with much  this  
kiṁ jñātena tavārjuna  
what (use)  by knowing  for you  Arjuna  
viṣṭabhyāham idaṁ kṛtsnam  
pervading  I  this  entire  
ekāṁśena sthito jagat  
by one portion  I stand  the universe

(20) “But what need have you, Arjuna, of all this detailed knowledge? By one portion of Myself I pervade and support this entire universe.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 10.42)[^12]

তমিমমহমজং শরীরভাজাং  
হৃদি হৃদি ধিষ্ঠিতমাত্মকল্পিতানাম্ ৷  
প্রতিদৃশমিব নৈকধার্কমেকং  
সমধিগতোঽস্মি বিধূতভেদমোহঃ ॥ ২১ ॥

tam imam aham ajaṁ śarīra-bhājāṁ  
Him  this  I  the unborn  of the embodied  
hṛdi hṛdi dhiṣṭhitam ātma-kalpitānām  
in heart  in heart  abiding  created by Himself  
prati-dṛśam iva naikadhārkam ekaṁ  
to each  eye  as if  in many ways  the sun  one  
samadhigato ’smi vidhūta-bheda-mohaḥ  
have attained  I  having cast off  of difference  the illusion

(21) (Grandsire Bhīṣma said:) “O Śrī Kṛṣṇa! Having cast off the illusion of ‘difference’ between all Your forms, I have attained You, the Unborn, who abide in the heart of each of the embodied beings You have created — like the one sun, which each eye sees in its own way.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.9.42)[^13]

সেই ত’ গোবিন্দ সাক্ষাচ্চৈতন্য গোসাঞি ৷  
জীব নিস্তারিতে ঐছে দয়ালু আর নাই ॥ ২২ ॥

sei ta’ govinda sākṣāc caitanya gosāñi  
that  indeed  Govinda  directly  Caitanya  the Lord  
jīva nistārite aiche dayālu āra nāi  
the souls  in delivering  so  merciful  another  there is not

(22) That very Govinda is Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi Himself. There is none so merciful in delivering the souls.[^14]

পরব্যোমেতে বৈসে নারায়ণ নাম ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যপূর্ণ লক্ষ্মীকান্ত ভগবান্ ॥ ২৩ ॥

para-vyomete vaise nārāyaṇa nāma  
in the spiritual  sky  dwells  Nārāyaṇa  named  
ṣaḍ-aiśvarya-pūrṇa lakṣmī-kānta bhagavān  
with six  opulences  full  of Lakṣmī  the beloved  Bhagavān

(23) In the spiritual sky dwells the Lord called Nārāyaṇa — Bhagavān, full in the six opulences, the beloved of Lakṣmī.

বেদ, ভাগবত, উপনিষদ্, আগম ৷  
‘পূর্ণতত্ত্ব’ যাঁরে কহে, নাহি যাঁর সম ॥ ২৪ ॥

veda, bhāgavata, upaniṣad, āgama  
the Vedas,  the Bhāgavata,  the Upaniṣads,  the Āgamas  
‘pūrṇa-tattva’ yām̐re kahe, nāhi yām̐ra sama  
‘the complete  Truth’  whom  they call,  there is not  to whom  an equal

(24) He whom the Vedas, the «Bhāgavatam», the Upaniṣads and the Āgamas call “the complete Truth”, and who has no equal —

ভক্তিযোগে ভক্ত পায় যাঁহার দর্শন ৷  
সূর্য্য যেন সবিগ্রহ দেখে দেবগণ ॥ ২৫ ॥

bhakti-yoge bhakta pāya yām̐hāra darśana  
through bhakti-yoga  the devotees  attain  whose  sight  
sūrya yena savigraha dekhe deva-gaṇa  
the sun  as  with its form  see  the gods

(25) Him the devotees behold through bhakti-yoga, as the gods behold the sun in its own form.[^15]

---

[^1]: «Anubhāṣya»: *bālaḥ* — “an inexperienced child”; the crocodiles are the mutually contending doctrines of Aulūkya, Jina, Buddha, Jaimini, Patañjali, Gautama, Kaṇāda, Kapila, Śaṅkara, Dattātreya and others; by the Lord’s mercy even the inexperienced one sets them aside as straw and comes to know the pure feet of Kṛṣṇa.

[^2]: «Anubhāṣya»: the swans, cakravākas and bees are pure devotees in different moods (*bhāva*), for whom these pastimes are a place of supreme joy; the murmur of the river is the sweet, indistinct sound of the stream of Śrī Hari’s pastimes, sung by His devotees.

[^3]: Indicating the Object (*vastu-nirdeśa*) is one of the three kinds of auspicious invocation (Ādi 1.22). In the edition the verse is headed: “explanation of the third of the first fourteen verses — indicating the Object”.

[^4]: The same verse as Ādi 1.3. «Anubhāṣya»: Brahman is the radiance of His transcendental body, and the Paramātmā a particular manifestation of His majesty; both are only two aspects of His form, perceived according to the capacity of the seer — not the fullness of the Lord, as Bhagavān is. «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “He whom they call Bhagavān, the shelter and source of Brahman and Paramātmā, is my Lord, Bhagavān Himself.”

[^5]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: by the rules of rhetoric one first names the *anuvāda*, the known, and then the *vidheya*, the new. That Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān exist is known from the Vedas; that Brahman is the effulgence of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s body, Paramātmā His portion and Bhagavān He Himself, is not yet known — and this is what is stated.

[^6]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “The conclusion of the scriptures is this: the supreme truth of *viṣṇu-tattva* is Bhagavān Himself, Kṛṣṇacandra.”

[^7]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: the first realization of that non-dual knowledge is Brahman, the second Paramātmā, the third Bhagavān.

[^8]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “… the Brahman distinct from the unlimited opulences — earths and the rest — in millions upon millions of universes, undivided, infinite and complete, has arisen from the effulgence of that original Person, Govinda.”

[^9]: «Anubhāṣya»: with this verse it is shown that attaining Kṛṣṇa’s feet is easy, whereas renunciants who labour hard attain, as the fruit of their practice, only the realm of Brahman; *vāta-vasanāḥ* — naked; *śramaṇāḥ* — mendicants who emaciate the body; *brahmākhyam* — featureless.

[^10]: «Anubhāṣya»: the Puruṣa who dwells within is the Paramātmā, “only a manifestation of a portion (*aṁśa-vibhūti*) of Govinda”; the three Puruṣa-avatāras govern the material world, while Bhagavān Himself, abiding in the fourth, *turīya*, is attached to none of its affairs.

[^11]: «Anubhāṣya»: the one Śrī Govinda, ever manifest in Goloka-Vṛndāvana, appears in the hearts of countless souls as the Paramātmā, the Person whom the soul is to serve.

[^12]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: “by one portion, as the Paramātmā, I have entered and abide in the whole world.”

[^13]: «Anubhāṣya»: *bheda-mohaḥ* — the illusion that the Lord’s forms (*prakāśa*, *vilāsa*) differ in name, form, qualities and pastimes; *ātma-kalpitānām* — “created by Himself”. Reference to the «Bhāgavatam» as in the edition.

[^14]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: by the word *sākṣāt* (“directly, Himself”) the author establishes that Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is Govinda Himself, not His *prakāśa* or *vilāsa*.

[^15]: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya»: the eternal form of the Lord cannot be seen by material senses or by the efforts of knowledge; through bhakti, through love, only the devotees become fit to see it — as the gods, whose divine eyes pierce the net of the sun’s rays, see the sun’s own form.
