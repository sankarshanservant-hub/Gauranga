“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 7  
**The Meeting with Vallabha Bhaṭṭa**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

চৈতন্যচরণাম্ভোজমকরন্দলিহো ভজে ৷  
যেষাং প্রসাদমাত্রেণ পামরোঽপ্যমরো ভবেৎ ॥ ১ ॥

caitanya-caraṇāmbhoja-  
of Caitanya  of the lotus feet  
makaranda-liho bhaje  
the honey  who lick  I worship  
yeṣāṁ prasāda-mātreṇa  
whose  by the mercy alone  
pāmaro ’py amaro bhavet  
a wretch  even  immortal  becomes

(1) I worship those who sip the honey of Śrī Caitanya’s lotus feet: by their mercy alone even a wretched soul becomes immortal.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

বর্ষান্তরে যত গৌড়ের ভক্তগণ আইলা ৷  
পূর্ব্ববৎ মহাপ্রভু সবারে মিলিলা ॥ ৩ ॥

varṣāntare yata gauḍera bhakta-gaṇa āilā  
in the next year  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees  came  
pūrvavat mahāprabhu sabāre mililā  
as before  Mahāprabhu  all  met

(3) The next year all the devotees of Gauḍa came, and Mahāprabhu met them all as before.

এইমত বিলাস প্রভুর ভক্তগণ লঞা ৷  
হেনকালে বল্লভ-ভট্ট মিলিল আসিয়া ॥ ৪ ॥

ei-mata vilāsa prabhura bhakta-gaṇa lañā  
in this way  pastimes  of the Lord  the devotees  with  
hena-kāle vallabha-bhaṭṭa milila āsiyā  
at that time  Vallabha Bhaṭṭa  met (Him)  coming

(4) While the Lord was thus enjoying His pastimes with the devotees, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa came to meet Him.

আসিয়া বন্দিল ভট্ট প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
প্রভু ‘ভাগবতবুদ্ধ্যে’ কৈলা আলিঙ্গনে ॥ ৫ ॥

āsiyā vandila bhaṭṭa prabhura caraṇe  
coming  bowed  the Bhaṭṭa  of the Lord  at the feet  
prabhu ‘bhāgavata-buddhye’ kailā āliṅgane  
the Lord  ‘regarding him as a bhāgavata’  gave  an embrace

(5) Coming before the Lord, the Bhaṭṭa bowed at His feet, and the Lord embraced him, regarding him as a *bhāgavata*, a devotee.

মান্য করি’ প্রভু তারে নিকটে বসাইলা ৷  
বিনয় করিয়া ভট্ট কহিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৬ ॥

mānya kari’ prabhu tāre nikaṭe vasāilā  
honour  showing  the Lord  him  near  seated  
vinaya kariyā bhaṭṭa kahite lāgilā  
humility  showing  the Bhaṭṭa  to speak  began

(6) Showing him honour, the Lord seated him near Himself, and the Bhaṭṭa humbly began to speak.

বহুদিন মনোরথ তোমা দেখিবারে ৷  
জগন্নাথ পূর্ণ কৈলা, দেখিলুঁ তোমারে ॥ ৭ ॥

bahu-dina manoratha tomā dekhibāre  
for many days  the desire  You  to see  
jagannātha pūrṇa kailā, dekhilum̐ tomāre  
Jagannātha  fulfilled  I have seen  You

(7) “For many days I have longed to see You. Jagannātha has fulfilled my desire: I have seen You.

তোমার দর্শন যে পায়, সেই ভাগ্যবান্ ৷  
তোমাকে দেখিয়ে, — যেন সাক্ষাৎ ভগবান্ ॥ ৮ ॥

tomāra darśana ye pāya, sei bhāgyavān  
Your  sight  who  obtains  he  fortunate  
tomāke dekhiye, — yena sākṣāt bhagavān  
You  I see  as if  directly  Bhagavān

(8) Whoever obtains Your sight is fortunate. When I see You, it is as if I see Bhagavān Himself.

তোমারে যে স্মরণ করে, সে হয় পবিত্র ৷  
দর্শনে পবিত্র হবে, — ইথে কি বিচিত্র ? ॥ ৯ ॥

tomāre ye smaraṇa kare, se haya pavitra  
You  who  remembers  he  becomes  pure  
darśane pavitra habe, — ithe ki vicitra?  
by the sight  pure  will become  in this  what  wonder

(9) Whoever remembers You becomes pure. What wonder, then, that one becomes pure by seeing You?

যেষাং সংস্মরণাৎ পুংসাং সদ্যঃ শুদ্ধ্যন্তি বৈ গৃহাঃ ৷  
কিং পুনর্দ্দর্শনস্পর্শপাদশৌচাসনাদিভিঃ ॥ ১০ ॥

yeṣāṁ saṁsmaraṇāt puṁsāṁ  
of whom  by the remembrance  of men  
sadyaḥ śuddhyanti vai gṛhāḥ  
at once  become pure  indeed  the homes  
kiṁ punar darśana-sparśa-  
how much more  by seeing  touching  
pāda-śaucāsanādibhiḥ  
washing the feet  offering a seat and so on

(10) ‘When people merely remember such saints, their very homes become pure at once. How much more, then, when they see them and touch them, wash their feet and offer them a seat!’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.19.33)[^3]

কলিকালের ধর্ম্ম — কৃষ্ণনাম-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-শক্তি বিনা নহে তার প্রবর্ত্তন ॥ ১১ ॥

kali-kālera dharma — kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṅkīrtana  
of the age of Kali  the dharma  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the saṅkīrtana  
kṛṣṇa-śakti vinā nahe tāra pravartana  
Kṛṣṇa’s power  without  is not  its  propagation

(11) The dharma of the age of Kali is the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name. Without Kṛṣṇa’s power no one can propagate it.[^4]

তাহা প্রবর্ত্তাইলা তুমি, — এই ত ‘প্রমাণ’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণশক্তি ধর তুমি, — ইথে নাহি আন ॥ ১২ ॥

tāhā pravartāilā tumi, — ei ta ‘pramāṇa’  
that  have propagated  You  this  indeed  ‘the proof’  
kṛṣṇa-śakti dhara tumi, — ithe nāhi āna  
Kṛṣṇa’s power  bear  You  in this  there is not  otherwise

(12) You have propagated it — that is the proof. You bear Kṛṣṇa’s power; of this there is no doubt.

জগতে করিলা তুমি কৃষ্ণনাম প্রকাশে ৷  
যেই তোমা দেখে, সেই কৃষ্ণপ্রেমে ভাসে ॥ ১৩ ॥

jagate karilā tumi kṛṣṇa-nāma prakāśe  
in the world  have made  You  Kṛṣṇa’s name  manifest  
yei tomā dekhe, sei kṛṣṇa-preme bhāse  
whoever  You  sees  he  in love for Kṛṣṇa  floats

(13) You have made Kṛṣṇa’s name manifest in the world. Whoever sees You floats in love for Kṛṣṇa.

প্রেম-পরকাশ নহে কৃষ্ণশক্তি বিনে ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ — এক প্রেমদাতা, শাস্ত্র-প্রমাণে ॥ ১৪ ॥

prema-parakāśa nahe kṛṣṇa-śakti vine  
of prema  the manifestation  is not  Kṛṣṇa’s power  without  
‘kṛṣṇa’ — eka prema-dātā, śāstra-pramāṇe  
‘Kṛṣṇa’  the one  giver of prema  by the evidence of scripture

(14) Without Kṛṣṇa’s power prema is not manifested. Kṛṣṇa alone is the giver of prema — so the scriptures testify.

সন্ত্ববতারা বহবঃ পঙ্কজনাভস্য সর্ব্বতোভদ্রাঃ ৷  
কৃষ্ণাদন্যঃ কো বা লতাস্বপি প্রেমদো ভবতি ॥ ১৫ ॥

santv avatārā bahavaḥ  
let there be  avatāras  many  
paṅkaja-nābhasya sarvatobhadrāḥ  
of the lotus-naveled One  in every way  auspicious  
kṛṣṇād anyaḥ ko vā latāsv  
than Kṛṣṇa  other  who  indeed  to the creepers  
api prema-do bhavati  
even  love  the giver  becomes

(15) ‘Let there be many all-auspicious avatāras of the Lord whose navel is a lotus; but who other than Kṛṣṇa gives prema even to the creepers?’” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.5.37)[^5]

মহাপ্রভু কহে — শুন, ভট্ট মহামতি ৷  
মায়াবাদী সন্ন্যাসী আমি, না জানি কৃষ্ণভক্তি ॥ ১৬ ॥

mahāprabhu kahe — śuna, bhaṭṭa mahā-mati  
Mahāprabhu  says  listen  Bhaṭṭa  great-minded  
māyāvādī sannyāsī āmi, nā jāni kṛṣṇa-bhakti  
a Māyāvādī  sannyāsī  I  not  know  devotion to Kṛṣṇa

(16) Mahāprabhu said, “Listen, wise Bhaṭṭa. I am a Māyāvādī sannyāsī; I do not know devotion to Kṛṣṇa.[^6]

অদ্বৈতাচার্য্য-গোসাঞি — ‘সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর’ ৷  
তাঁর সঙ্গে আমার মন হইল নির্ম্মল ॥ ১৭ ॥

advaitācārya-gosāñi — ‘sākṣāt īśvara’  
Advaitācārya Gosāñi  ‘Īśvara Himself’  
tām̐ra saṅge āmāra mana haila nirmala  
His  in the company  My  mind  became  pure

(17) Advaitācārya Gosāñi is Īśvara Himself, and in His company My mind became pure.

সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কৃষ্ণভক্ত্যে নাহি যাঁর সম ৷  
অতএব ‘অদ্বৈত-আচার্য্য’ তাঁর নাম ॥ ১৮ ॥

sarva-śāstre kṛṣṇa-bhaktye nāhi yām̐ra sama  
in all the scriptures  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  there is not  whose  equal  
ataeva ‘advaita-ācārya’ tām̐ra nāma  
therefore  ‘Advaita Ācārya’  His  name

(18) In all the scriptures and in devotion to Kṛṣṇa He has no equal; therefore His name is Advaita Ācārya, the peerless teacher.

যাঁহার কৃপাতে ম্লেচ্ছের হয় কৃষ্ণভক্তি ৷  
কে কহিতে পারে তাঁর বৈষ্ণবতা-শক্তি ? ॥ ১৯ ॥

yām̐hāra kṛpāte mlecchera haya kṛṣṇa-bhakti  
by whose  mercy  of mlecchas  arises  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  
ke kahite pāre tām̐ra vaiṣṇavatā-śakti?  
who  to tell  can  His  of Vaiṣṇava nature the power

(19) By His mercy even mlecchas gain devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Who can describe the power of His Vaiṣṇava nature?

নিত্যানন্দ-অবধূত — ‘সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর’ ৷  
ভাবোন্মাদে মত্ত কৃষ্ণপ্রেমের সাগর ॥ ২০ ॥

nityānanda-avadhūta — ‘sākṣāt īśvara’  
Nityānanda Avadhūta  ‘Īśvara Himself’  
bhāvonmāde matta kṛṣṇa-premera sāgara  
by the madness of bhāva  intoxicated  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the ocean

(20) Nityānanda Avadhūta is Īśvara Himself. Intoxicated with the madness of ecstatic emotion (*bhāva*), He is an ocean of love for Kṛṣṇa.

ষড়্দর্শন-বেত্তা ভট্টাচার্য্য-সার্ব্বভৌম ৷  
ষড়্দর্শনে জগদ্গুরু ভাগবতোত্তম ॥ ২১ ॥

ṣaḍdarśana-vettā bhaṭṭācārya-sārvabhauma  
of the six darśanas  the knower  Bhaṭṭācārya Sārvabhauma  
ṣaḍdarśane jagadguru bhāgavatottama  
in the six darśanas  the world’s guru  the best of bhāgavatas

(21) Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya knows the six systems of philosophy (*darśanas*). In these six systems he is the guru of the world, and he is the best of bhāgavatas.

তেঁহ দেখাইলা মোরে ভক্তিযোগ-পার ৷  
তাঁর প্রসাদে জানিলুঁ ‘কৃষ্ণভক্তিযোগ’ সার ॥ ২২ ॥

tem̐ha dekhāilā more bhakti-yoga-pāra  
he  showed  Me  of bhakti-yoga  the farthest limit  
tām̐ra prasāde jānilum̐ ‘kṛṣṇa-bhakti-yoga’ sāra  
by his  grace  I came to know  ‘Kṛṣṇa-bhakti-yoga’  the essence

(22) He showed Me the farthest limit of bhakti-yoga; by his grace I came to know that the yoga of devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the essence.

রামানন্দ রায় — কৃষ্ণ-রসের ‘নিধান’ ৷  
তেঁহ জানাইলা, কৃষ্ণ — স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ২৩ ॥

rāmānanda rāya — kṛṣṇa-rasera ‘nidhāna’  
Rāmānanda Rāya  of the rasa of Kṛṣṇa  ‘the treasury’  
tem̐ha jānāilā, kṛṣṇa — svayaṁ bhagavān  
he  made known  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān Himself

(23) Rāmānanda Rāya is a treasury of the rasa of Kṛṣṇa. He made Me understand that Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself.

তাতে প্রেমভক্তি — ‘পুরুষার্থ-শিরোমণি’ ৷  
রাগমার্গে প্রেমভক্তি ‘সর্ব্বাধিক’ জানি ॥ ২৪ ॥

tāte prema-bhakti — ‘puruṣārtha-śiromaṇi’  
through that  prema-bhakti  ‘the crown jewel of the goals of life’  
rāga-mārge prema-bhakti ‘sarvādhika’ jāni  
on the path of rāga  prema-bhakti  ‘the highest of all’  I know

(24) Through him I came to know that prema-bhakti is the crown jewel of the goals of life (*puruṣārthas*), and that prema-bhakti on the path of spontaneous attraction (*rāga*) is the highest of all.[^7]

দাস্য, সখ্য, বাৎসল্য, আর যে শৃঙ্গার ৷  
দাস, সখা, গুরু, কান্তা, — ‘আশ্রয়’ যাহার ॥ ২৫ ॥

dāsya, sakhya, vātsalya, āra ye śṛṅgāra  
servitude,  friendship,  parental love,  and  which  the love of the beloved  
dāsa, sakhā, guru, kāntā, — ‘āśraya’ yāhāra  
servant,  friend,  elder,  beloved  ‘the shelter’  of which

(25) Servitude, friendship, parental love and the love of the beloved (*śṛṅgāra*) — of these the servant, the friend, the elder and the beloved are the shelter (*āśraya*).

‘ঐশ্বর্য্যজ্ঞানযুক্ত’, ‘কেবল’-ভাব আর ৷  
ঐশ্বর্য্য-জ্ঞানে না পাই ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ॥ ২৬ ॥

‘aiśvarya-jñāna-yukta’, ‘kevala’-bhāva āra  
‘joined with knowledge of majesty’  ‘pure’ bhāva  and  
aiśvarya-jñāne nā pāi vrajendra-kumāra  
by knowledge of majesty  not  one attains  the son of the king of Vraja

(26) There is bhāva joined with the knowledge of majesty, and there is pure (*kevala*) bhāva. By the knowledge of majesty one does not attain the son of the king of Vraja.[^8]

নায়ং সুখাপো ভগবান্ দেহিনাং গোপিকাসুতঃ ৷  
জ্ঞানিনাঞ্চাত্মভূতানাং যথা ভক্তিমতামিহ ॥ ২৭ ॥

nāyaṁ sukhāpo bhagavān  
not  this  easily attained  Bhagavān  
dehināṁ gopikā-sutaḥ  
for the embodied  of the gopī  the son  
jñānināṁ cātma-bhūtānāṁ  
for the jñānīs  and  for those who realized the self  
yathā bhaktimatām iha  
as  for those who have devotion  here

(27) ‘This Bhagavān, the son of the gopī, is not so easily attained by the embodied, nor by the jñānīs, nor by those who have realized the self, as He is by those who have devotion.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.9.21)[^9]

‘আত্মভূত’-শব্দে কহে ‘পারিষদগণ’ ৷  
ঐশ্বর্য্য-জ্ঞানে লক্ষ্মী না পাইলা ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ২৮ ॥

‘ātma-bhūta’-śabde kahe ‘pāriṣada-gaṇa’  
‘ātma-bhūta’  by the word  means  ‘the associates’  
aiśvarya-jñāne lakṣmī nā pāilā vrajendra-nandana  
in knowledge of majesty  Lakṣmī  not  attained  the son of the king of Vraja

(28) The word *ātma-bhūta* means His associates. Because of her knowledge of His majesty, Lakṣmī did not attain the son of the king of Vraja.[^10]

নায়ং শ্রিয়োঽঙ্গ উ নিতান্তরতেঃ প্রসাদঃ  
স্বর্যোষিতাং নলিনগন্ধরুচাং কুতোঽন্যাঃ ৷  
রাসোৎসবেঽস্য ভুজদণ্ডগৃহীতকণ্ঠ-  
লব্ধাশিষাং য উদগাদ্ব্রজসুন্দরীণাম্ ॥ ২৯ ॥

nāyaṁ śriyo ’ṅga u nitānta-rateḥ prasādaḥ  
not  this  of Śrī  on the chest  indeed  wholly attached  favour  
svar-yoṣitāṁ nalina-gandha-rucāṁ kuto ’nyāḥ  
of the celestial women  of lotuses  the fragrance  having the lustre  how then  others  
rāsotsave ’sya bhuja-daṇḍa-gṛhīta-kaṇṭha-  
at the festival of rāsa  His  by the arms  embraced  around the neck  
labdhāśiṣāṁ ya udagād vraja-sundarīṇām  
who obtained their desire  which  arose  of Vraja  the beautiful women

(29) ‘At the festival of the rāsa dance He embraced the beautiful women of Vraja around their necks with His arms, and they attained their heart’s desire. Such favour was not granted even to Śrī, who is wholly devoted to Him, nor to the celestial women, fragrant and radiant like lotuses — what then of others?’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.47.60)[^11]

শুদ্ধভাবে সখা করে স্কন্ধে আরোহণ ৷  
শুদ্ধভাবে ব্রজেশ্বরী করেন বন্ধন ॥ ৩০ ॥

śuddha-bhāve sakhā kare skandhe ārohaṇa  
in pure bhāva  the friend  does  on the shoulders  climbing  
śuddha-bhāve vrajeśvarī karena bandhana  
in pure bhāva  the queen of Vraja  does  binding

(30) In pure bhāva His friend climbs on His shoulders, and in pure bhāva the queen of Vraja binds Him.[^12]

‘মোর সখা’, ‘মোর পুত্র’, — এই ‘শুদ্ধ’ মন ৷  
অতএব শুক-ব্যাস করে প্রশংসন ॥ ৩১ ॥

‘mora sakhā’, ‘mora putra’, — ei ‘śuddha’ mana  
‘my  friend’,  ‘my  son’  this  ‘pure’  mind  
ataeva śuka-vyāsa kare praśaṁsana  
therefore  Śuka and Vyāsa  do  praise

(31) ‘My friend,’ ‘my son’ — such is the pure mind. Therefore Śuka and Vyāsa praise it.

ইত্থং সতাং ব্রহ্মসুখানুভূত্যা  
দাস্যং গতানাং পরদৈবতেন ৷  
মায়াশ্রিতানাং নরদারকেণ  
সার্দ্ধং বিজহ্রুঃ কৃতপুণ্যপুঞ্জাঃ ॥ ৩২ ॥

itthaṁ satāṁ brahma-sukhānubhūtyā  
thus  for the sages  the experience of the bliss of Brahman  
dāsyaṁ gatānāṁ para-daivatena  
servitude  for those who have attained  the Supreme Deity  
māyāśritānāṁ nara-dārakeṇa  
for those under māyā  a human boy  
sārdhaṁ vijahruḥ kṛta-puṇya-puñjāḥ  
with (Him)  played  who had amassed  of pious deeds  heaps

(32) ‘Thus the cowherd boys, who had amassed heaps of pious deeds, played with Him who is to the sages the experience of Brahman’s bliss, to those who have taken up His service the Supreme Deity, and to those under the spell of *māyā* a human child.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.12.11)[^13]

ত্রয়্যা চোপনিষদ্ভিশ্চ সাংখ্যযোগৈশ্চ সাত্বতৈঃ ৷  
উপগীয়মানমাহাত্ম্যং হরিং সাঽমন্যতাত্মজম্ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

trayyā copaniṣadbhiś ca  
by the three Vedas  and by the Upaniṣads  and  
sāṁkhya-yogaiś ca sātvataiḥ  
by Sāṅkhya and yoga  and  by the Sātvatas  
upagīyamāna-māhātmyaṁ  
whose glory is sung  
hariṁ sā ’manyatātmajam  
Hari  she  considered  her son

(33) ‘She looked upon Hari, whose glory is sung by the three Vedas, the Upaniṣads, the works on Sāṅkhya and yoga and the Sātvata scriptures, as her own son.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.45)[^14]

নন্দঃ কিমকরোদ্ব্রহ্মন্ শ্রেয় এবং মহোদয়ম্ ৷  
যশোদা বা মহাভাগা পপৌ যস্যাঃ স্তনং হরিঃ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

nandaḥ kim akarod brahman  
Nanda  what  did  O brāhmaṇa  
śreya evaṁ mahodayam  
good deed  such  great fruit (bearing)  
yaśodā vā mahā-bhāgā  
Yaśodā  or  most fortunate  
papau yasyāḥ stanaṁ hariḥ  
drank  whose  breast  Hari

(34) ‘O brāhmaṇa, what good deed did Nanda perform to win such great fortune? And what did the most fortunate Yaśodā do, that Hari Himself drank from her breast?’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.46)[^15]

ঐশ্বর্য্য দেখিলেহ ‘শুদ্ধের’ ঐশ্বর্য্য নহে জ্ঞান ৷  
অতএব ঐশ্বর্য্য হইতে ‘কেবল’-ভাব প্রধান ॥ ৩৫ ॥

aiśvarya dekhileha ‘śuddhera’ aiśvarya nahe jñāna  
majesty  even on seeing  ‘the pure one’s’  of majesty  is not  awareness  
ataeva aiśvarya haite ‘kevala’-bhāva pradhāna  
therefore  than majesty  ‘pure’ bhāva  superior

(35) Even when he sees Kṛṣṇa’s majesty, one whose bhāva is pure has no awareness of it. Therefore pure bhāva is superior to the mood of majesty.

এ সব শিখাইলা মোরে রায়-রামানন্দ ৷  
সে-সব শুনিতে হয় পরম আনন্দ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

e saba śikhāilā more rāya-rāmānanda  
these  all  taught  Me  Rāya Rāmānanda  
se-saba śunite haya parama ānanda  
those all  to hear  is  supreme  bliss

(36) All this Rāya Rāmānanda taught Me, and to hear it is supreme bliss.[^16]

কহন না যায় রামানন্দের প্রভাব ৷  
রায়-প্রসাদে জানিলুঁ ব্রজের ‘শুদ্ধ’ ভাব ॥ ৩৭ ॥

kahana nā yāya rāmānandera prabhāva  
to tell  not  is possible  Rāmānanda’s  power  
rāya-prasāde jānilum̐ vrajera ‘śuddha’ bhāva  
by Rāya’s grace  I came to know  of Vraja  the ‘pure’  bhāva

(37) Rāmānanda’s power cannot be described. By Rāya’s grace I came to know the pure bhāva of Vraja.

দামোদর-স্বরূপ — ‘প্রেমরস’ মূর্ত্তিমান্ ৷  
য়াঁর সঙ্গে হৈল ব্রজ-মধুর-রস-জ্ঞান ॥ ৩৮ ॥

dāmodara-svarūpa — ‘prema-rasa’ mūrtimān  
Dāmodara Svarūpa  ‘the rasa of prema’  embodied  
yām̐ra saṅge haila vraja-madhura-rasa-jñāna  
whose  in the company  arose  of the madhura-rasa of Vraja  the knowledge

(38) Dāmodara Svarūpa is the rasa of prema embodied. In his company I came to know the sweet rasa (*madhura-rasa*) of Vraja.

‘শুদ্ধপ্রেম’ ব্রজদেবীর — কামগন্ধহীন ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণসুখতাৎপর্য্য’, — এই তার চিহ্ন ॥ ৩৯ ॥

‘śuddha-prema’ vraja-devīra — kāma-gandha-hīna  
‘pure prema’  of the goddesses of Vraja  of lust the scent  without  
‘kṛṣṇa-sukha-tātparya’, — ei tāra cihna  
‘Kṛṣṇa’s happiness as the aim’  this  its  sign

(39) The pure prema of the goddesses of Vraja is without even a trace of lust (*kāma*). Its sign is this: its only aim is Kṛṣṇa’s happiness.

যত্তে সুজাতচরণাম্বুরুহং স্তনেষু  
ভীতাঃ শনৈঃ প্রিয় দধীমহি কর্কশেষু ৷  
তেনাটবীমটসি তদ্ব্যথতে ন কিং স্বিৎ  
কূর্পাদিভির্ভ্রমতি ধীর্ভবদায়ুষাং নঃ ॥ ৪০ ॥

yat te sujāta-caraṇāmburuhaṁ staneṣu  
which  Your  tender  lotus feet  on the breasts  
bhītāḥ śanaiḥ priya dadhīmahi karkaśeṣu  
fearful  gently  O dear  we place  hard  
tenāṭavīm aṭasi tad vyathate na kiṁ svit  
with them  the forest  You wander  they  are pained  not  whether  
kūrpādibhir bhramati dhīr bhavad-āyuṣāṁ naḥ  
by fine stones and such  whirls  the mind  of whom You are the life  our

(40) ‘O dear one, Your tender lotus feet we place gently on our hard breasts, afraid to hurt them — and with those feet You wander through the forest! Are they not hurt by the sharp pebbles? You are our very life, and our minds are reeling.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.19)[^17]

পতিসুতান্বয়ভ্রাতৃবান্ধবা-  
নতিবিলঙ্ঘ্য তেঽন্ত্যচ্যুতাগতাঃ ৷  
গতিবিদস্তবোদ্গীতমোহিতাঃ  
কিতব যোষিতঃ কস্ত্যজেন্নিশি ॥ ৪১ ॥

pati-sutānvaya-bhrātṛ-bāndhavān  
husbands, sons, kin, brothers and relatives  
ativilaṅghya te ’nty acyutāgatāḥ  
wholly disregarding  Your  to the side  O Acyuta  come  
gati-vidas tavodgīta-mohitāḥ  
knowing our purpose  by Your song  enchanted  
kitava yoṣitaḥ kas tyajen niśi  
O cheat  women  who  would abandon  at night

(41) ‘Disregarding husbands, sons, kin, brothers and relatives, we have come to Your side, O Acyuta, enchanted by Your song. You know why we have come. O cheat, who would abandon women at night?’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.16)[^18]

সর্ব্বোত্তম ভজন এই সর্ব্বভক্তি জিনি’ ৷  
অতএব কৃষ্ণ কহে, — ‘আমি তোমার ঋণী’ ॥ ৪২ ॥

sarvottama bhajana ei sarva-bhakti jini’  
the highest  worship  this  every form of bhakti  surpassing  
ataeva kṛṣṇa kahe, — ‘āmi tomāra ṛṇī’  
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  says  ‘I  your  debtor’

(42) This worship is the highest of all, surpassing every form of bhakti. Therefore Kṛṣṇa says, ‘I am your debtor.’

ন পারয়েঽহং নিরবদ্যসংয়ুজাং  
স্বসাধুকৃত্যং বিবুধায়ুষাপি বঃ ৷  
যা মাঽভজন্ দুর্জ্জয়গেহশৃঙ্খলাঃ  
সংবৃশ্চ্য তদ্বঃ প্রতিয়াতু সাধুনা ॥ ৪৩ ॥

na pāraye ’haṁ niravadya-saṁyujāṁ  
not  am able  I  of the faultlessly united  
sva-sādhu-kṛtyaṁ vibudhāyuṣāpi vaḥ  
own good deed  in a god’s lifetime  even  to you  
yā mā ’bhajan durjaya-geha-śṛṅkhalāḥ  
who  Me  worshipped  hard to conquer  of home  the fetters  
saṁvṛścya tad vaḥ pratiyātu sādhunā  
completely cutting  that  your  let it be requited  by the good deed

(43) ‘O gopīs, your union with Me is faultless. Even in a lifetime of the gods I could not repay you for what you have done: you cut the fetters of home, so hard to break, and came to Me. So let your own good deed be your reward.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.32.22)[^19]

ঐশ্বর্য্য-জ্ঞান হৈতে কেবলা-ভাব — প্রধান ৷  
পৃথিবীতে ভক্ত নাহি উদ্ধব-সমান ॥ ৪৪ ॥

aiśvarya-jñāna haite kevalā-bhāva — pradhāna  
of majesty  the knowledge  than  pure bhāva  superior  
pṛthivīte bhakta nāhi uddhava-samāna  
on earth  devotee  there is not  Uddhava’s  equal

(44) Pure bhāva is superior to the knowledge of majesty. On earth there is no devotee equal to Uddhava,

তেঁহ যাঁর পদধূলি করেন প্রার্থন ৷  
স্বরূপের সঙ্গে পাইলুঁ এ সব শিক্ষণ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

tem̐ha yām̐ra padadhūli karena prārthana  
he  whose  the dust of the feet  does  pray for  
svarūpera saṅge pāilum̐ e saba śikṣaṇa  
Svarūpa’s  in the company  I received  these  all  teachings

(45) yet he prays for the dust of their feet. All this I learned in the company of Svarūpa.[^20]

হরিদাস-ঠাকুর — মহাভাগবত-প্রধান ৷  
প্রতি দিন লয় তেঁহ তিনলক্ষ নাম ॥ ৪৬ ॥

haridāsa-ṭhākura — mahā-bhāgavata-pradhāna  
Haridāsa Ṭhākura  of the great bhāgavatas  the foremost  
prati dina laya tem̐ha tina-lakṣa nāma  
every  day  takes  he  three lakhs  names

(46) Haridāsa Ṭhākura is the foremost of the great bhāgavatas. Every day he chants three lakhs (three hundred thousand) of names.

নামের মহিমা আমি তাঁর ঠাঞি শিখিলুঁ ৷  
তাঁর প্রসাদে নামের মহিমা জানিলুঁ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

nāmera mahimā āmi tām̐ra ṭhāñi śikhilum̐  
of the name  the glory  I  from him  learned  
tām̐ra prasāde nāmera mahimā jānilum̐  
by his  grace  of the name  the glory  I came to know

(47) From him I learned the glory of the holy name; by his grace that glory was revealed to Me.

আচার্য্যরত্ন, আচার্য্যনিধি, পণ্ডিত-গদাধর ৷  
জগদানন্দ, দামোদর, শঙ্কর, বক্রেশ্বর ॥ ৪৮ ॥

ācāryaratna, ācāryanidhi, paṇḍita-gadādhara  
Ācāryaratna,  Ācāryanidhi,  Paṇḍita Gadādhara  
jagadānanda, dāmodara, śaṅkara, vakreśvara  
Jagadānanda,  Dāmodara,  Śaṅkara,  Vakreśvara

(48) Ācāryaratna, Ācāryanidhi, Gadādhara Paṇḍita, Jagadānanda, Dāmodara, Śaṅkara, Vakreśvara,

কাশীশ্বর, মুকুন্দ, বাসুদেব, মুরারি ৷  
আর যত ভক্তগণ গৌড়ে অবতরি’ ॥ ৪৯ ॥

kāśīśvara, mukunda, vāsudeva, murāri  
Kāśīśvara,  Mukunda,  Vāsudeva,  Murāri  
āra yata bhakta-gaṇa gauḍe avatari’  
and  all the  devotees  in Gauḍa  having descended

(49) Kāśīśvara, Mukunda, Vāsudeva, Murāri and all the other devotees who descended in Gauḍa

কৃষ্ণ-নাম-প্রেম কৈলা জগতে প্রচার ৷  
ইঁহা সবার সঙ্গে কৃষ্ণভক্তি যে আমার ॥ ৫০ ॥

kṛṣṇa-nāma-prema kailā jagate pracāra  
of Kṛṣṇa’s name and prema  made  in the world  preaching  
im̐hā sabāra saṅge kṛṣṇa-bhakti ye āmāra  
of these  all  in the company  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  which  My

(50) have preached the name of Kṛṣṇa and prema throughout the world, and whatever devotion to Kṛṣṇa I have, I have gained in their company.”[^21]

ভট্টের হৃদয়ে দৃঢ় অভিমান জানি’ ৷  
ভঙ্গী করি’ মহাপ্রভু কহে এত বাণী ॥ ৫১ ॥

bhaṭṭera hṛdaye dṛḍha abhimāna jāni’  
in the Bhaṭṭa’s  heart  firm  pride  knowing  
bhaṅgī kari’ mahāprabhu kahe eta vāṇī  
a hint  making  Mahāprabhu  spoke  these  words

(51) Mahāprabhu knew how firmly pride was rooted in the Bhaṭṭa’s heart, and so He spoke all this by way of a hint.[^22]

আমি সে ‘বৈষ্ণব’, — ভক্তিসিদ্ধান্ত সব জানি ৷  
আমি সে ভাগবত-অর্থ উত্তম বাখানি ॥ ৫২ ॥

āmi se ‘vaiṣṇava’, — bhakti-siddhānta saba jāni  
I  indeed  ‘a Vaiṣṇava’  of bhakti the conclusions  all  I know  
āmi se bhāgavata-artha uttama vākhāni  
I  indeed  of the Bhāgavata the meaning  excellently  explain

(52) ‘I am a Vaiṣṇava,’ the Bhaṭṭa thought. ‘I know all the conclusions of bhakti, and no one explains the meaning of the Bhāgavata better than I.’

ভট্টের মনেতে এই ছিল দীর্ঘ গর্ব্ব ৷  
প্রভুর বচন শুনি’ সে হইল খর্ব্ব ॥ ৫৩ ॥

bhaṭṭera manete ei chila dīrgha garva  
the Bhaṭṭa’s  in the mind  this  was  long-standing  pride  
prabhura vacana śuni’ se haila kharva  
the Lord’s  words  hearing  that  became  humbled

(53) Such was the pride that had long lived in his mind, but the Lord’s words humbled it.[^23]

প্রভুর মুখে বৈষ্ণবতা শুনিয়া সবার ৷  
ভট্টের ইচ্ছা হৈল তাঁ-সবারে দেখিবার ॥ ৫৪ ॥

prabhura mukhe vaiṣṇavatā śuniyā sabāra  
the Lord’s  from the mouth  the Vaiṣṇava qualities  hearing  of all  
bhaṭṭera icchā haila tām̐-sabāre dekhibāra  
the Bhaṭṭa’s  desire  arose  them all  to see

(54) Hearing from the Lord’s own mouth about the Vaiṣṇava qualities of all these devotees, the Bhaṭṭa wished to see them all.

ভট্ট কহে, — এ সব বৈষ্ণব রহে কোন্ স্থানে ?  
কোন্প্রকারে পাইমু ইঁহা-সবার দর্শনে ? ॥ ৫৫ ॥

bhaṭṭa kahe, — e saba vaiṣṇava rahe kon sthāne?  
the Bhaṭṭa  says  these  all  Vaiṣṇavas  stay  in what  place  
kon prakāre pāimu im̐hā-sabāra darśane?  
in what  way  shall I obtain  of these all  the sight

(55) The Bhaṭṭa said, “Where do all these Vaiṣṇavas live? How can I see them?”

প্রভু কহে, — কেহ গৌড়ে, কেহ দেশান্তরে ৷  
সব আসিয়াছে রথযাত্রা দেখিবারে ॥ ৫৬ ॥

prabhu kahe, — keha gauḍe, keha deśāntare  
the Lord  says  some  in Gauḍa  some  in other lands  
saba āsiyāche ratha-yātrā dekhibāre  
all  have come  the Ratha-yātrā  to see

(56) The Lord said, “Some live in Gauḍa, some in other lands, but all have come to see the Ratha-yātrā.

ইঁহাই রহেন সবে, বাসা — নানা-স্থানে ৷  
ইঁহাই পাইবা তুমি সবার দর্শনে ॥ ৫৭ ॥

im̐hāi rahena sabe, vāsā — nānā-sthāne  
here  stay  all  lodgings  in various places  
im̐hāi pāibā tumi sabāra darśane  
here  will obtain  you  of all  the sight

(57) They are all staying here, in lodgings in various places. Here you will be able to see them all.”

তবে ভট্ট কহে বহু বিনয় বচন ৷  
বহু যত্ন করি’ প্রভুরে কৈল নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

tabe bhaṭṭa kahe bahu vinaya vacana  
then  the Bhaṭṭa  spoke  many  humble  words  
bahu yatna kari’ prabhure kaila nimantraṇa  
great  effort  making  the Lord  made  an invitation

(58) Then the Bhaṭṭa spoke many humble words and earnestly invited the Lord to a meal.[^24]

আর দিন সব বৈষ্ণব প্রভু-স্থানে আইলা ৷  
সবা-সনে মহাপ্রভু ভট্টে মিলাইলা ॥ ৫৯ ॥

āra dina saba vaiṣṇava prabhu-sthāne āilā  
the next  day  all  Vaiṣṇavas  to the Lord’s place  came  
sabā-sane mahāprabhu bhaṭṭe milāilā  
with all of them  Mahāprabhu  the Bhaṭṭa  made meet

(59) The next day all the Vaiṣṇavas came to the Lord, and Mahāprabhu introduced the Bhaṭṭa to them all.

‘বৈষ্ণবে’র তেজ দেখি’ ভট্টের চমৎকার ৷  
তাঁ-সবার আগে ভট্ট — খদ্যোত-আকার ॥ ৬০ ॥

‘vaiṣṇave’ra teja dekhi’ bhaṭṭera camatkāra  
of the Vaiṣṇavas  the radiance  seeing  the Bhaṭṭa’s  amazement  
tām̐-sabāra āge bhaṭṭa — khadyota-ākāra  
them all  before  the Bhaṭṭa  a firefly’s  form

(60) Seeing the radiance of the Vaiṣṇavas, the Bhaṭṭa was amazed: before them all he was like a firefly.

তবে ভট্ট বহু মহাপ্রসাদ আনাইল ৷  
গণ-সহ মহাপ্রভুরে ভোজন করাইল ॥ ৬১ ॥

tabe bhaṭṭa bahu mahā-prasāda ānāila  
then  the Bhaṭṭa  much  mahā-prasāda  had brought  
gaṇa-saha mahāprabhure bhojana karāila  
with His associates  Mahāprabhu  a meal  gave

(61) Then the Bhaṭṭa had a great quantity of mahā-prasāda brought and fed Mahāprabhu and His associates.

পরমানন্দ-পুরী-সঙ্গে সন্ন্যাসীর গণ ৷  
একদিকে বৈসে সব করিতে ভোজন ॥ ৬২ ॥

paramānanda-purī-saṅge sannyāsīra gaṇa  
with Paramānanda Purī  of sannyāsīs  the group  
eka-dike vaise saba karite bhojana  
on one side  sit  all  to take  the meal

(62) The sannyāsīs, with Paramānanda Purī, all sat on one side to eat.

অদ্বৈত, নিত্যানন্দ-রায় — পার্শ্বে দুইজন ৷  
মধ্যে মহাপ্রভু বসিলা, আগে-পাছে ভক্তগণ ॥ ৬৩ ॥

advaita, nityānanda-rāya — pārśve dui-jana  
Advaita,  Nityānanda Rāya  at the sides  the two  
madhye mahāprabhu vasilā, āge-pāche bhakta-gaṇa  
in the middle  Mahāprabhu  sat  in front and behind  the devotees

(63) Mahāprabhu sat in the middle, with Advaita and Nityānanda Rāya on either side, and the devotees in front and behind.

গৌড়ের ভক্ত যত কহিতে না পারি ৷  
অঙ্গনে বসিলা সব হঞা সারি-সারি ॥ ৬৪ ॥

gauḍera bhakta yata kahite nā pāri  
of Gauḍa  the devotees  how many  to tell  not  I can  
aṅgane vasilā saba hañā sāri-sāri  
in the courtyard  sat  all  being  in rows

(64) I cannot tell how many devotees from Gauḍa there were; they all sat in rows in the courtyard.

প্রভুর ভক্তগণ দেখি’ ভট্টের চমৎকার ৷  
প্রত্যক্ষে সবার পদে কৈল নমস্কার ॥ ৬৫ ॥

prabhura bhakta-gaṇa dekhi’ bhaṭṭera camatkāra  
the Lord’s  devotees  seeing  the Bhaṭṭa’s  amazement  
pratyakṣe sabāra pade kaila namaskāra  
in (His) presence  of all  at the feet  made  obeisance

(65) Seeing the Lord’s devotees, the Bhaṭṭa was amazed, and in the Lord’s presence he bowed at the feet of them all.[^25]

স্বরূপ, জগদানন্দ, কাশীশ্বর, শঙ্কর ৷  
পরিবেশন করে, আর রাঘব, দামোদর ॥ ৬৬ ॥

svarūpa, jagadānanda, kāśīśvara, śaṅkara  
Svarūpa,  Jagadānanda,  Kāśīśvara,  Śaṅkara  
pariveśana kare, āra rāghava, dāmodara  
serving  do  and  Rāghava,  Dāmodara

(66) Svarūpa, Jagadānanda, Kāśīśvara and Śaṅkara served the food, and so did Rāghava and Dāmodara.

মহাপ্রসাদ বল্লভ-ভট্ট বহু আনাইল ৷  
প্রভু-সহ সন্ন্যাসিগণ ভোজনে বসিল ॥ ৬৭ ॥

mahāprasāda vallabha-bhaṭṭa bahu ānāila  
mahā-prasāda  Vallabha Bhaṭṭa  much  had brought  
prabhu-saha sannyāsi-gaṇa bhojane vasila  
with the Lord  the sannyāsīs  to the meal  sat

(67) Vallabha Bhaṭṭa had brought a great deal of mahā-prasāda, and the sannyāsīs sat down with the Lord to eat.

প্রসাদ পায় বৈষ্ণবগণ, বলে ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ৷  
হরিধ্বনি উঠিল সব ব্রহ্মাণ্ড ভরি’ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

prasāda pāya vaiṣṇava-gaṇa, bale ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
prasāda  take  the Vaiṣṇavas  say  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’  
hari-dhvani uṭhila saba brahmāṇḍa bhari’  
the sound of Hari  rose  the whole  universe  filling

(68) The Vaiṣṇavas honoured the prasāda, saying “Hari! Hari!”, and the sound of “Hari” rose and filled the whole universe.

মালা, চন্দন, গুবাক, পান অনেক আনিল ৷  
সবা’ পূজা করি’ ভট্ট আনন্দিত হৈল ॥ ৬৯ ॥

mālā, candana, guvāka, pāna aneka ānila  
garlands,  sandal paste,  areca nuts,  betel leaves  plenty  brought  
sabā’ pūjā kari’ bhaṭṭa ānandita haila  
all  honouring  the Bhaṭṭa  joyful  became

(69) He brought plenty of garlands, sandal paste, areca nuts and betel leaves. Honouring everyone with them, the Bhaṭṭa was filled with joy.

রথযাত্রা-দিনে প্রভু কীর্ত্তন আরম্ভিলা ৷  
পূর্ব্ববৎ সাত-সম্প্রদায় পৃথক্ করিলা ॥ ৭০ ॥

ratha-yātrā-dine prabhu kīrtana ārambhilā  
on the day of the Ratha-yātrā  the Lord  kīrtana  began  
pūrvavat sāta-sampradāya pṛthak karilā  
as before  seven groups  separate  made

(70) On the day of the Ratha-yātrā the Lord began the kīrtana. As before, He formed seven separate groups.

অদ্বৈত, নিত্যানন্দ, হরিদাস, বক্রেশ্বর ৷  
শ্রীবাস, রাঘব, পণ্ডিত-গদাধর ॥ ৭১ ॥

advaita, nityānanda, haridāsa, vakreśvara  
Advaita,  Nityānanda,  Haridāsa,  Vakreśvara  
śrīvāsa, rāghava, paṇḍita-gadādhara  
Śrīvāsa,  Rāghava,  Paṇḍita Gadādhara

(71) Advaita, Nityānanda, Haridāsa, Vakreśvara, Śrīvāsa, Rāghava and Gadādhara Paṇḍita —

সাত জন সাত-ঠাঞি করেন কীর্ত্তন ৷  
‘হরিবোল’ বলি’ প্রভু করেন ভ্রমণ ॥ ৭২ ॥

sāta jana sāta-ṭhāñi karena kīrtana  
seven  persons  in seven places  do  kīrtana  
‘hari-bola’ bali’ prabhu karena bhramaṇa  
‘Hari bol’  saying  the Lord  does  wandering

(72) these seven led the kīrtana in seven places, and the Lord moved from group to group, calling “Hari bol!”[^26]

চৌদ্দ মাদল বাজে উচ্চ সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
এক এক নর্ত্তকের প্রেমে ভাসিল ভুবন ॥ ৭৩ ॥

caudda mādala bāje ucca saṅkīrtana  
fourteen  mādalas  sound  loud  saṅkīrtana  
eka eka nartakera preme bhāsila bhuvana  
of each  each  dancer  in the prema  floated  the world

(73) Fourteen *mādalas* (drums) sounded in the loud saṅkīrtana, and the world floated in the prema of each of the dancers.[^27]

দেখি’ বল্লভ-ভট্টের হৈল চমৎকার ৷  
আনন্দে বিহ্বল নাহি আপন-সাম্ভাল ॥ ৭৪ ॥

dekhi’ vallabha-bhaṭṭera haila camatkāra  
seeing  Vallabha Bhaṭṭa’s  was  amazement  
ānande vihvala nāhi āpana-sāmbhāla  
in bliss  overwhelmed  not  his own  composure

(74) Seeing this, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa was amazed. Overwhelmed with bliss, he lost all composure.

তবে মহাপ্রভু সবার নৃত্য রাখিল ৷  
প্রভুর চরিত্রে ভট্টের চমৎকার হৈল ॥ ৭৫ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu sabāra nṛtya rākhila  
then  Mahāprabhu  of all  the dancing  stopped  
prabhura caritre bhaṭṭera camatkāra haila  
the Lord’s  at the conduct  the Bhaṭṭa’s  amazement  was

(75) Then Mahāprabhu stopped everyone’s dancing, and the Bhaṭṭa was amazed at the Lord’s conduct.

যাত্রান্তরে ভট্ট যাই’ মহাপ্রভু-স্থানে ৷  
প্রভুর চরণে কিছু কৈল নিবেদনে ॥ ৭৬ ॥

yātrāntare bhaṭṭa yāi’ mahāprabhu-sthāne  
at another festival  the Bhaṭṭa  going  to Mahāprabhu’s place  
prabhura caraṇe kichu kaila nivedane  
the Lord’s  at the feet  something  made  submission

(76) At another festival the Bhaṭṭa went to Mahāprabhu and submitted a request at His feet:[^28]

ভাগবতের টীকা কিছু কৈরাছি লিখন ৷  
আপনে মহাপ্রভু যদি করেন শ্রবণ ॥ ৭৭ ॥

bhāgavatera ṭīkā kichu kairāchi likhana  
on the Bhāgavata  a commentary  some  I have done  writing  
āpane mahāprabhu yadi karena śravaṇa  
Yourself  Mahāprabhu  if  do  hearing

(77) “I have written something of a commentary on the Bhāgavata. Would You, Mahāprabhu, hear it Yourself?”

প্রভু কহে, — ভাগবতার্থ বুঝিতে না পারি ৷  
ভাগবতার্থ শুনিতে আমি নহি অধিকারী ॥ ৭৮ ॥

prabhu kahe, — bhāgavatārtha bujhite nā pāri  
the Lord  says  the meaning of the Bhāgavata  to understand  not  I can  
bhāgavatārtha śunite āmi nahi adhikārī  
the meaning of the Bhāgavata  to hear  I  am not  qualified

(78) The Lord said, “I cannot understand the meaning of the Bhāgavata. I am not qualified even to hear its meaning.[^29]

বসি’ কৃষ্ণনাম মাত্র করিয়ে গ্রহণে ৷  
সংখ্যা-নাম পূর্ণ মোর নহে রাত্রি-দিনে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

vasi’ kṛṣṇa-nāma mātra kariye grahaṇe  
sitting  Kṛṣṇa’s name  only  I do  taking  
saṁkhyā-nāma pūrṇa mora nahe rātri-dine  
the fixed number of names  complete  My  is not  night and day

(79) I simply sit and chant Kṛṣṇa’s name, and even so, night and day, I cannot complete My fixed number of names.”

ভট্ট কহে, — কৃষ্ণনামের অর্থ-ব্যাখ্যানে ৷  
বিস্তার কৈরাছি, তাহা করহ শ্রবণে ॥ ৮০ ॥

bhaṭṭa kahe, — kṛṣṇa-nāmera artha-vyākhyāne  
the Bhaṭṭa  says  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  in explaining the meaning  
vistāra kairāchi, tāhā karaha śravaṇe  
expansion  I have made  that  do  hearing

(80) The Bhaṭṭa said, “I have explained the meaning of Kṛṣṇa’s name at length. Please hear it.”

প্রভু কহে, — কৃষ্ণনামের বহু অর্থ না মানি ৷  
‘শ্যামসুন্দর’ ‘যশোদানন্দন’, — এইমাত্র জানি ॥ ৮১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — kṛṣṇa-nāmera bahu artha nā māni  
the Lord  says  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  many  meanings  not  I accept  
‘śyāma-sundara’ ‘yaśodā-nandana’, — ei-mātra jāni  
‘the dark beauty’  ‘the son of Yaśodā’  this only  I know

(81) The Lord said, “I do not accept many meanings of Kṛṣṇa’s name. I know only this: Śyāmasundara, the dark and beautiful One, and Yaśodā-nandana, the son of Yaśodā.

তমালশ্যামলত্বিষি শ্রীযশোদাস্তনন্ধয়ে ৷  
কৃষ্ণনাম্নো রূঢ়িরিতি সর্ব্বশাস্ত্র-বিনির্ণয়ঃ ॥ ৮২ ॥

tamāla-śyāmala-tviṣi  
in Him whose luster is dark like the tamāla tree  
śrī-yaśodā-stanan-dhaye  
in the One who drinks at Śrī Yaśodā’s breast  
kṛṣṇa-nāmno rūḍhir iti  
of the name ‘Kṛṣṇa’  the conventional meaning  thus  
sarva-śāstra-vinirṇayaḥ  
of all scriptures  the conclusion

(82) ‘The conventional meaning (*rūḍhi*) of the name “Kṛṣṇa” is He whose lustre is dark like a *tamāla* tree and who drinks at the breast of Śrī Yaśodā: this is the conclusion of all the scriptures.’ (Nāma-kaumudī)[^30]

এই অর্থ আমি মাত্র জানিয়ে নির্দ্ধার ৷  
আর সর্ব্ব-অর্থে মোর নাহি অধিকার ॥ ৮৩ ॥

ei artha āmi mātra jāniye nirdhāra  
this  meaning  I  only  know  as settled  
āra sarva-arthe mora nāhi adhikāra  
other  in all meanings  My  is not  qualification

(83) This meaning alone I know as settled; I have no qualification for any other meanings.”

ফল্গুপ্রায় ভট্টের নামাদি সব-ব্যাখ্যা ৷  
সর্ব্বজ্ঞ প্রভু জানি’ তারে করেন উপেক্ষা ॥ ৮৪ ॥

phalguprāya bhaṭṭera nāmādi saba-vyākhyā  
nearly worthless  the Bhaṭṭa’s  of the name and so on  all the explanations  
sarvajña prabhu jāni’ tāre karena upekṣā  
all-knowing  the Lord  knowing  them  does  disregard

(84) The Bhaṭṭa’s explanations of the name and the rest were all nearly worthless (*phalgu*). Knowing this, the all-knowing Lord disregarded them.

বিমনা হঞা ভট্ট গেলা নিজ-ঘর ৷  
প্রভু-বিষয়ে ভক্তি কিছু হইল অন্তর ॥ ৮৫ ॥

vimanā hañā bhaṭṭa gelā nija-ghara  
dejected  becoming  the Bhaṭṭa  went  to his own  home  
prabhu-viṣaye bhakti kichu haila antara  
toward the Lord  devotion  somewhat  became  removed

(85) Dejected, the Bhaṭṭa went home, and his devotion to the Lord partly ebbed away.[^31]

তবে ভট্ট গেলা পণ্ডিত-গোসাঞির ঠাঞি ৷  
নানা মতে প্রীতি করি’ করে আসা-যাই ॥ ৮৬ ॥

tabe bhaṭṭa gelā paṇḍita-gosāñira ṭhāñi  
then  the Bhaṭṭa  went  to Paṇḍita Gosāñi’s  place  
nānā mate prīti kari’ kare āsā-yāi  
in various  ways  friendship  showing  makes  coming and going

(86) Then the Bhaṭṭa went to Paṇḍita Gosāñi. Showing friendship in many ways, he kept coming and going.[^32]

প্রভুর উপেক্ষায় সব নীলাচলের জন ৷  
ভট্টের ব্যাখ্যান কিছু না করে শ্রবণ ॥ ৮৭ ॥

prabhura upekṣāya saba nīlācalera jana  
because of the Lord’s  disregard  all  of Nīlācala  the people  
bhaṭṭera vyākhyāna kichu nā kare śravaṇa  
the Bhaṭṭa’s  explanations  any  not  do  hear

(87) Because the Lord disregarded him, none of the people of Nīlācala would listen to the Bhaṭṭa’s explanations.

লজ্জিত হৈল ভট্ট, হৈল অপমানে ৷  
দুঃখিত হঞা গেল পণ্ডিতের স্থানে ॥ ৮৮ ॥

lajjita haila bhaṭṭa, haila apamāne  
ashamed  became  the Bhaṭṭa,  was  in dishonour  
duḥkhita hañā gela paṇḍitera sthāne  
grieved  being  he went  to the Paṇḍita’s  place

(88) The Bhaṭṭa felt ashamed and dishonoured, and in his grief he went to the Paṇḍita.

দৈন্য করি’ কহে, — নিলু তোমার শরণ ৷  
তুমি কৃপা করি’ রাখ আমার জীবন ॥ ৮৯ ॥

dainya kari’ kahe, — nilu tomāra śaraṇa  
humility  showing  he says  I have taken  your  shelter  
tumi kṛpā kari’ rākha āmāra jīvana  
you  mercy  showing  save  my  life

(89) Humbly he said, “I have taken shelter of you. Be merciful and save my life.

কৃষ্ণনাম-ব্যাখ্যা যদি করহ শ্রবণ ৷  
তবে মোর লজ্জা-পঙ্ক হয় প্রক্ষালন ॥ ৯০ ॥

kṛṣṇanāma-vyākhyā yadi karaha śravaṇa  
of the name of Kṛṣṇa  the explanation  if  you do  hear  
tabe mora lajjā-paṅka haya prakṣālana  
then  my  of shame  the mire  is  washed away

(90) If you will hear my explanation of Kṛṣṇa’s name, the mire of my shame will be washed away.”

সঙ্কটে পড়িলা পণ্ডিত, করয়ে সংশয় ৷  
কি করিবেন, — ইহা করিতে নারেন নিশ্চয় ॥ ৯১ ॥

saṅkaṭe paḍilā paṇḍita, karaye saṁśaya  
into a dilemma  fell  the Paṇḍita,  feels  doubt  
ki karibena, — ihā karite nārena niścaya  
what  he should do  this  to  cannot  decide

(91) The Paṇḍita was caught in a dilemma and full of doubt: he could not decide what he should do.[^33]

যদ্যপি পণ্ডিত আর না কৈলা অঙ্গীকার ৷  
ভট্ট যাই’ তবু পড়ে করি’ বলাৎকার ॥ ৯২ ॥

yadyapi paṇḍita āra nā kailā aṅgīkāra  
although  the Paṇḍita  further  not  gave  consent  
bhaṭṭa yāi’ tabu paḍe kari’ balātkāra  
the Bhaṭṭa  going  still  reads  making  force

(92) Although the Paṇḍita did not consent, the Bhaṭṭa came and read to him anyway, forcing it on him.

আভিজাত্যে পণ্ডিত করিতে নারে নিষেধন ৷  
এ সঙ্কটে, কৃষ্ণ, রাখ, লইলাঙ শরণ ॥ ৯৩ ॥

ābhijātye paṇḍita karite nāre niṣedhana  
because of nobility  the Paṇḍita  to make  cannot  a refusal  
e saṅkaṭe, kṛṣṇa, rākha, lailāṅa śaraṇa  
in this  dilemma,  Kṛṣṇa,  protect me,  I have taken  shelter

(93) The Bhaṭṭa was a man of noble standing (*ābhijātya*), and so the Paṇḍita could not stop him. “Kṛṣṇa, protect me in this dilemma,” he prayed. “I have taken shelter of You.[^34]

অন্তর্য্যামী প্রভু জানিবেন মোর মন ৷  
তাঁরে ভয় নাহি কিছু, ‘বিষম’ তাঁর গণ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

antaryāmī prabhu jānibena mora mana  
the Indweller  the Lord  will know  my  mind  
tām̐re bhaya nāhi kichu, ‘viṣama’ tām̐ra gaṇa  
of Him  fear  not  any,  ‘severe’  His  associates

(94) The Lord dwells in the heart; He will know my mind, and I have no fear of Him. But His associates are ‘severe.’”[^35]

যদ্যপি বিচারে পণ্ডিতের নাহি দোষ ৷  
তথাপি প্রভুর গণ করে প্রণয়-রোষ ॥ ৯৫ ॥

yadyapi vicāre paṇḍitera nāhi doṣa  
although  on consideration  of the Paṇḍita  is not  fault  
tathāpi prabhura gaṇa kare praṇaya-roṣa  
still  the Lord’s  associates  show  loving  anger

(95) Although, rightly judged, the Paṇḍita was not at fault, the Lord’s associates still showed him an anger born of love (*praṇaya-roṣa*).

প্রত্যহ বল্লভ-ভট্ট আইসে প্রভু-স্থানে ৷  
‘উদ্গ্রাহাদি’ প্রায় করে আচার্য্যাদি-সনে ॥ ৯৬ ॥

pratyaha vallabha-bhaṭṭa āise prabhu-sthāne  
every day  Vallabha  Bhaṭṭa  comes  to the Lord’s  place  
‘udgrāhādi’ prāya kare ācāryādi-sane  
‘disputes and the like’  as it were  makes  with the Ācārya and others

(96) Every day Vallabha Bhaṭṭa came to the Lord and picked arguments with the Ācārya and others.

যেই কিছু করে ভট্ট ‘সিদ্ধান্ত’ স্থাপন ৷  
শুনিতেই আচার্য্য তাহা করেন খণ্ডন ॥ ৯৭ ॥

yei kichu kare bhaṭṭa ‘siddhānta’ sthāpana  
whatever  any  makes  the Bhaṭṭa  ‘conclusion’  establishing  
śunitei ācārya tāhā karena khaṇḍana  
on hearing it  the Ācārya  that  does  refute

(97) Whatever ‘conclusion’ (*siddhānta*) the Bhaṭṭa tried to establish, the Ācārya refuted the moment he heard it.

আচার্য্যাদি-আগে ভট্ট যবে যবে যায় ৷  
রাজহংস-মধ্যে যেন রহে বকপ্রায় ॥ ৯৮ ॥

ācāryādi-āge bhaṭṭa yabe yabe yāya  
of the Ācārya and others  before  the Bhaṭṭa  whenever  whenever  goes  
rājahaṁsa-madhye yena rahe baka-prāya  
royal swans  among  as  remains  a heron  like

(98) Whenever the Bhaṭṭa came before the Ācārya and the others, he was like a heron among royal swans.

একদিন ভট্ট পুছিল আচার্য্যেরে ৷  
জীব-‘প্রকৃতি’ ‘পতি’ করি’ মানয়ে কৃষ্ণেরে ॥ ৯৯ ॥

ekadina bhaṭṭa puchila ācāryere  
one day  the Bhaṭṭa  asked  the Ācārya  
jīva-‘prakṛti’ ‘pati’ kari’ mānaye kṛṣṇere  
the jīva  ‘prakṛti’  ‘husband’  as  accepts  Kṛṣṇa

(99) One day the Bhaṭṭa asked the Ācārya: “The jīva is *prakṛti*, the female, and accepts Kṛṣṇa as her husband (*pati*).

পতিব্রতা হঞা পতির নাম নাহি লয় ৷  
তোমরা কৃষ্ণনাম লহ, — কোন্ ধর্ম্ম হয় ? ॥ ১০০ ॥

pati-vratā hañā patira nāma nāhi laya  
devoted to her husband  being  of the husband  the name  not  takes  
tomarā kṛṣṇa-nāma laha, — kon dharma haya?  
you  of Kṛṣṇa  the name  take  what  religion  is

(100) A wife devoted to her husband does not utter her husband’s name. Yet you utter Kṛṣṇa’s name — what sort of dharma is that?”

আচার্য্য কহে, — আগে তোমার ‘ধর্ম্ম’ মূর্ত্তিমান্ ৷  
ইঁহারে পুছহ, ইঁহ করিবেন সমাধান ॥ ১০১ ॥

ācārya kahe, — āge tomāra ‘dharma’ mūrtimān  
the Ācārya  says  before  you  ‘dharma’  embodied  
im̐hāre puchaha, im̐ha karibena samādhāna  
Him  ask,  He  will give  the resolution

(101) The Ācārya said, “Before you stands dharma embodied. Ask Him; He will resolve the question.”

প্রভু কহেন, — তুমি না জানহ ‘ধর্ম্মাধর্ম্ম’ ৷  
স্বামী-আজ্ঞা পালে, — এই পতিব্রতা-ধর্ম্ম ॥ ১০২ ॥

prabhu kahena, — tumi nā jānaha dharmādharma  
the Lord  says  you  not  know  dharma and adharma  
svāmī-ājñā pāle, — ei pati-vratā-dharma  
of the husband  the order  she follows  this  of the faithful wife  the dharma

(102) The Lord said, “You do not know what is dharma and what is adharma. A faithful wife’s dharma is to follow her husband’s order.[^36]

পতির আজ্ঞা, — নিরন্তর তাঁর নাম লইতে ৷  
পতির আজ্ঞা পতিব্রতা না পারে লঙ্ঘিতে ॥ ১০৩ ॥

patira ājñā, — nirantara tām̐ra nāma laite  
the husband’s  order  ceaselessly  his  name  to take  
patira ājñā pati-vratā nā pāre laṅghite  
the husband’s  order  the faithful wife  not  can  transgress

(103) The husband’s order is to utter his name ceaselessly, and a faithful wife cannot transgress her husband’s order.

অতএব নাম লয়, নামের ‘ফল’ পায় ৷  
নামের ফলে কৃষ্ণপদে ‘প্রেম’ উপজায় ॥ ১০৪ ॥

ataeva nāma laya, nāmera ‘phala’ pāya  
therefore  the name  she takes,  of the name  the fruit  she gets  
nāmera phale kṛṣṇa-pade ‘prema’ upajāya  
by the name’s  fruit  for Kṛṣṇa’s feet  love  arises

(104) Therefore she utters the name and gains the fruit of the name: by that fruit love (prema) for Kṛṣṇa’s feet awakens.”

শুনিয়া বল্লভ-ভট্ট হৈল নির্ব্বচন ৷  
ঘরে যাই’ মনে দুঃখে করেন চিন্তন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

śuniyā vallabha-bhaṭṭa haila nirvacana  
hearing  Vallabha  Bhaṭṭa  became  speechless  
ghare yāi’ mane duḥkhe karena cintana  
home  going  in his mind  in sorrow  does  brooding

(105) Hearing this, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa fell silent. He went home and brooded sorrowfully:

নিত্য আমার এই সভায় হয় কক্ষাপাত ৷  
একদিন উপরে যদি হয় মোর বাত্ ॥ ১০৬ ॥

nitya āmāra ei sabhāya haya kakṣā-pāta  
daily  my  in this  assembly  is  defeat  
ekadina upare yadi haya mora bāt  
one day  on top  if  is  my  word

(106) “Every day I am defeated in this assembly. If only my word would prevail just once,

তবে সুখ হয়, আর সব লজ্জা যায় ৷  
স্ব-বচন স্থাপিতে আমি কি করি উপায় ? ॥ ১০৭ ॥

tabe sukha haya, āra saba lajjā yāya  
then  happiness  is,  and  all  shame  goes  
sva-vacana sthāpite āmi ki kari upāya?  
my own  statement  to establish  I  what  do  means

(107) I would be happy, and all my shame would be gone. What means can I find to establish my own view?”

আর দিন আসি’ বসিলা প্রভুরে নমস্করি’ ৷  
সভাতে কহেন কিছু মনে গর্ব্ব করি’ ॥ ১০৮ ॥

āra dina āsi’ vasilā prabhure namaskari’  
next  day  coming  he sat  to the Lord  bowing  
sabhāte kahena kichu mane garva kari’  
in the assembly  says  something  in the mind  pride  holding

(108) The next day he came, bowed to the Lord, and sat down. Then, proud at heart, he spoke in the assembly:

ভাগবতে স্বামীর ব্যাখ্যান কৈরাছি খণ্ডন ৷  
লইতে না পারি তাঁর ব্যাখ্যান-বচন ॥ ১০৯ ॥

bhāgavate svāmīra vyākhyāna kairāchi khaṇḍana  
in the Bhāgavata  Svāmī’s  explanation  I have  refuted  
laite nā pāri tām̐ra vyākhyāna-vacana  
to accept  not  can  his  explanation’s  words

(109) “In the *Bhāgavata* I have refuted Śrīdhara Svāmī’s explanations. I cannot accept the words of his commentary.

সেই ব্যাখ্যা করেন যাঁহা যেই পড়ে আনি’ ৷  
একবাক্যতা নাহি, তাতে ‘স্বামী’ নাহি মানি ॥ ১১০ ॥

sei vyākhyā karena yām̐hā yei paḍe āni’  
that  explanation  he gives  wherever  whatever  he reads  taking up  
eka-vākyatā nāhi, tāte ‘svāmī’ nāhi māni  
consistency  is not,  therefore  ‘Svāmī’  not  I accept

(110) He explains whatever passage he takes up just as it stands there; there is no consistency in it. Therefore I do not accept Svāmī.”[^37]

প্রভু হাসি’ কহে, — স্বামী না মানে যেই জন ৷  
বেশ্যার ভিতরে তারে করিয়ে গণন ॥ ১১১ ॥

prabhu hāsi’ kahe, — svāmī nā māne yei jana  
the Lord  smiling  says  svāmī  not  accepts  who  person  
veśyāra bhitare tāre kariye gaṇana  
of prostitutes  among  him  I make  counting

(111) The Lord smiled and said, “Whoever does not accept the *svāmī* (the husband), I count among prostitutes.”[^38]

এত কহি’ মহাপ্রভু মৌন ধরিলা ৷  
শুনিয়া সবার মনে সন্তোষ হইলা ॥ ১১২ ॥

eta kahi’ mahāprabhu mauna dharilā  
this  saying  Mahāprabhu  silence  held  
śuniyā sabāra mane santoṣa hailā  
hearing  of all  in the minds  satisfaction  arose

(112) Having said this, Mahāprabhu fell silent. Hearing it, everyone was satisfied at heart.

জগতের হিত লাগি’ গৌর-অবতার ৷  
অন্তরের অভিমান জানেন তাহার ॥ ১১৩ ॥

jagatera hita lāgi’ gaura-avatāra  
of the world  the good  for the sake  Gaura’s  descent  
antarera abhimāna jānena tāhāra  
inner  pride  He knows  his

(113) Gaura descended for the good of the world, and He knew the pride hidden in the Bhaṭṭa’s heart.

নানা অবজ্ঞানে ভট্টে শোধেন ভগবান্ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ যৈছে খণ্ডিলেন ইন্দ্রের অভিমান ॥ ১১৪ ॥

nānā avajñāne bhaṭṭe śodhena bhagavān  
by various  disregard  the Bhaṭṭa  purifies  Bhagavān  
kṛṣṇa yaiche khaṇḍilena indrera abhimāna  
Kṛṣṇa  as  broke  Indra’s  pride

(114) By various kinds of disregard Bhagavān purified the Bhaṭṭa, just as Kṛṣṇa broke the pride of Indra.

অজ্ঞ জীব নিজ-‘হিতে’ ‘অহিত’ করি’ মানে ৷  
গর্ব্ব চূর্ণ হৈলে, পাছে উঘাড়ে নয়নে ॥ ১১৫ ॥

ajña jīva nija-‘hite’ ‘ahita’ kari’ māne  
ignorant  jīva  his own  ‘good’  ‘harm’  as  regards  
garva cūrṇa haile, pāche ughāḍe nayane  
pride  crushed  when,  afterwards  open  the eyes

(115) An ignorant jīva takes his own good for harm. When his pride has been crushed, his eyes open at last.

ঘরে আসি’ রাত্র্যে ভট্ট চিন্তিতে লাগিল ৷  
পূর্ব্বে প্রয়াগে মোরে মহা-কৃপা কৈল ॥ ১১৬ ॥

ghare āsi’ rātrye bhaṭṭa cintite lāgila  
home  coming  at night  the Bhaṭṭa  to think  began  
pūrve prayāge more mahā-kṛpā kaila  
earlier  in Prayāga  to me  great  mercy  He showed

(116) Back at home that night, the Bhaṭṭa began to think: “Earlier, in Prayāga, the Lord showed me great mercy.

স্বগণ-সহিতে মোর মানিলা নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
এবে কেনে প্রভুর মোতে ফিরি’ গেল মন ? ॥ ১১৭ ॥

svagaṇa-sahite mora mānilā nimantraṇa  
His associates  together with  my  accepted  invitation  
ebe kene prabhura mote phiri’ gela mana?  
now  why  the Lord’s  toward me  turned  went  mind

(117) With His associates He accepted my invitation. Why has the Lord’s mind now turned against me?

‘আমি জিতি’, — এই গর্ব্ব-শূন্য হউক ইঁহার চিত ৷  
ঈশ্বর-স্বভাব, — করেন সবাকার হিত ॥ ১১৮ ॥

‘āmi jiti’, — ei garva-śūnya hauka im̐hāra cita  
‘I  win’  this  pride  free of  may become  his  mind  
īśvara-svabhāva, — karena sabākāra hita  
of the Lord  the nature  does  of all  the good

(118) ‘May his mind become free of this pride — “I shall win”’: such is His intention. It is the Lord’s nature to do good to all.

আপনা জানাইতে আমি করি অভিমান ৷  
সে গর্ব্ব খণ্ডাইতে মোর করেন অপমান ॥ ১১৯ ॥

āpanā jānāite āmi kari abhimāna  
myself  to make known  I  do  pride  
se garva khaṇḍāite mora karena apamāna  
that  pride  to break  me  does  dishonour

(119) To make a name for myself, I give way to pride; to break that pride, He dishonours me.[^39]

আমার ‘হিত’ করেন, — ইহো আমি মানি ‘দুঃখ’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণের উপরে কৈল যেন ইন্দ্র মহামূর্খ ॥ ১২০ ॥

āmāra ‘hita’ karena, — iho āmi māni ‘duḥkha’  
my  ‘good’  He does,  this  I  regard  ‘sorrow’  
kṛṣṇera upare kaila yena indra mahāmūrkha  
Kṛṣṇa  toward  did  as  Indra  great fool

(120) He is doing me good, yet I take it as sorrow — just as Indra, that great fool, behaved toward Kṛṣṇa.”[^40]

এত চিন্তি’ প্রাতে আসি’ প্রভুর চরণে ৷  
দৈন্য করি’ স্তুতি করি’ লইল শরণে ॥ ১২১ ॥

eta cinti’ prāte āsi’ prabhura caraṇe  
thus  thinking  in the morning  coming  at the Lord’s  feet  
dainya kari’ stuti kari’ laila śaraṇe  
humility  showing  praise  offering  took  shelter

(121) Thinking this, he came to the Lord in the morning and, humbly offering praise, took shelter at His feet.

আমি অজ্ঞ জীব, — অজ্ঞোচিত কর্ম্ম কৈলুঁ ৷  
তোমার আগে মূর্খ আমি পাণ্ডিত্য প্রকাশিলুঁ ॥ ১২২ ॥

āmi ajña jīva, — ajñocita karma kailum̐  
I  ignorant  jīva  fit for the ignorant  deeds  I did  
tomāra āge mūrkha āmi pāṇḍitya prakāśilum̐  
You  before  fool  I  learning  displayed

(122) “I am an ignorant jīva, and I acted as the ignorant act. Fool that I am, I displayed my learning before You.

তুমি — ঈশ্বর, নিজোচিত কৃপা কৈলা ৷  
অপমান করি’ সর্ব্ব গর্ব্ব খণ্ডাইলা ॥ ১২৩ ॥

tumi — īśvara, nijocita kṛpā kailā  
You  the Lord,  befitting Yourself  mercy  showed  
apamāna kari’ sarva garva khaṇḍāilā  
dishonour  doing  all  pride  You broke

(123) You are the Lord, and You showed mercy befitting Yourself: by dishonouring me, You broke all my pride.

আমি — অজ্ঞ, ‘হিত’-স্থানে মানি ‘অপমানে’ ৷  
ইন্দ্র যেন কৃষ্ণের নিন্দা করিল অজ্ঞানে ॥ ১২৪ ॥

āmi — ajña, ‘hita’-sthāne māni ‘apamāne’  
I  ignorant,  ‘good’  in place of  I regard  ‘dishonour’  
indra yena kṛṣṇera nindā karila ajñāne  
Indra  as  of Kṛṣṇa  blasphemy  did  in ignorance

(124) I am ignorant: in place of good I saw dishonour, just as Indra blasphemed Kṛṣṇa in ignorance.

তোমার কৃপা-অঞ্জনে গর্ব্ব-আন্ধ্য গেল ৷  
তুমি এত কৃপা কৈলা, — এবে ‘জ্ঞান’ হৈল ॥ ১২৫ ॥

tomāra kṛpā-añjane garva-āndhya gela  
Your  mercy  by the salve  pride’s  blindness  went  
tumi eta kṛpā kailā, — ebe ‘jñāna’ haila  
You  so much  mercy  showed  now  ‘knowledge’  has come

(125) By the salve of Your mercy the blindness of my pride is gone. You have shown me such mercy that now true knowledge has come.

অপরাধ কৈনু, ক্ষম, লইনু শরণ ৷  
কৃপা করি’ মোর মাথে ধরহ চরণ ॥ ১২৬ ॥

aparādha kainu, kṣama, lainu śaraṇa  
offences  I committed,  forgive,  I have taken  shelter  
kṛpā kari’ mora māthe dharaha caraṇa  
mercy  showing  on my  head  place  Your feet

(126) I have committed offences; forgive me. I have taken shelter of You. Be merciful and place Your feet on my head.”

প্রভু কহে — তুমি ‘পণ্ডিত’ ‘মহাভাগবত’ ৷  
দুইগুণ যাঁহা, তাঁহা নাহি গর্ব্ব-পর্ব্বত ॥ ১২৭ ॥

prabhu kahe — tumi ‘paṇḍita’ ‘mahā-bhāgavata’  
the Lord  says  you  ‘a paṇḍita’  ‘a great devotee’  
dui-guṇa yām̐hā, tām̐hā nāhi garva-parvata  
two  qualities  where,  there  is not  of pride  a mountain

(127) The Lord said, “You are a paṇḍita and a great devotee (*mahā-bhāgavata*). Where these two qualities are found, there can be no mountain of pride.

শ্রীধরস্বামী নিন্দি’ নিজ-টীকা কর !  
শ্রীধরস্বামী নাহি মান’, — এত ‘গর্ব্ব’ ধর! ॥ ১২৮ ॥

śrīdhara-svāmī nindi’ nija-ṭīkā kara!  
Śrīdhara  Svāmī  blaspheming  your own  commentary  you make  
śrīdhara-svāmī nāhi māna’, — eta ‘garva’ dhara!  
Śrīdhara  Svāmī  not  you accept  such  ‘pride’  you hold

(128) Yet you blaspheme Śrīdhara Svāmī and write your own commentary! You do not accept Śrīdhara Svāmī — such is the pride you hold!

শ্রীধরস্বামী প্রসাদে ‘ভাগবত’ জানি ৷  
জগদ্গুরু শ্রীধরস্বামী ‘গুরু’ করি’ মানি ॥ ১২৯ ॥

śrīdhara-svāmī prasāde ‘bhāgavata’ jāni  
of Śrīdhara  Svāmī  by the grace  the ‘Bhāgavata’  I know  
jagad-guru śrīdhara-svāmī ‘guru’ kari’ māni  
the world’s guru  Śrīdhara  Svāmī  ‘guru’  as  I accept

(129) By the grace of Śrīdhara Svāmī I understand the *Bhāgavata*. I accept Śrīdhara Svāmī, the guru of the world, as My guru.

শ্রীধর-উপরে গর্ব্বে যে কিছু লিখিবে ৷  
‘অর্থব্যস্ত’ লিখন সেই, লোকে না মানিবে ॥ ১৩০ ॥

śrīdhara-upare garve ye kichu likhibe  
Śrīdhara  above  out of pride  whatever  anything  one will write  
‘artha-vyasta’ likhana sei, loke nā mānibe  
‘distorted in meaning’  writing  that,  people  not  will accept

(130) Whatever anyone writes out of pride, setting himself above Śrīdhara, will be distorted in meaning (*artha-vyasta*), and people will not accept it.

শ্রীধরের অনুগত যে করে লিখন ৷  
সব লোক মান্য করি’ করিবে গ্রহণ ॥ ১৩১ ॥

śrīdharera anugata ye kare likhana  
Śrīdhara’s  following  who  does  writing  
saba loka mānya kari’ karibe grahaṇa  
all  people  respect  showing  will make  acceptance

(131) But what is written in the footsteps of Śrīdhara all people will honour and accept.

শ্রীধরানুগত কর ভাগবত-ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
অভিমান ছাড়ি’ ভজ কৃষ্ণ ভগবান্ ॥ ১৩২ ॥

śrīdharānugata kara bhāgavata-vyākhyāna  
following Śrīdhara  make  the Bhāgavata’s  explanation  
abhimāna chāḍi’ bhaja kṛṣṇa bhagavān  
pride  giving up  worship  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān

(132) Explain the *Bhāgavata* in the footsteps of Śrīdhara. Give up your pride and worship Kṛṣṇa, Bhagavān.

অপরাধ ছাড়ি’ কর কৃষ্ণসঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
অচিরাৎ পাবে তবে কৃষ্ণের চরণ ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

aparādha chāḍi’ kara kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana  
offences  giving up  do  Kṛṣṇa  saṅkīrtana  
acirāt pābe tabe kṛṣṇera caraṇa  
very soon  you will attain  then  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(133) Give up offences and perform the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa. Then you will very soon attain the feet of Kṛṣṇa.”[^41]

ভট্ট কহে, — যদি মোরে হইলা প্রসন্ন ৷  
একদিন পুনঃ মোর মান’ নিমন্ত্রণ ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

bhaṭṭa kahe, — yadi more hailā prasanna  
the Bhaṭṭa  says  if  with me  You have become  pleased  
ekadina punaḥ mora māna’ nimantraṇa  
one day  again  my  accept  invitation

(134) The Bhaṭṭa said, “If You are now pleased with me, accept my invitation once again some day.”

প্রভু অবতীর্ণ হৈলা জগৎ তারিতে ৷  
মানিলেন নিমন্ত্রণ, তারে সুখ দিতে ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

prabhu avatīrṇa hailā jagat tārite  
the Lord  descended  has  the world  to deliver  
mānilena nimantraṇa, tāre sukha dite  
He accepted  the invitation,  to him  joy  to give

(135) The Lord descended to deliver the world; He accepted the invitation to give the Bhaṭṭa joy.

জগতের ‘হিত’ হউক — এই প্রভুর মন ৷  
দণ্ড করি’ করে তার হৃদয় শোধন ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

jagatera ‘hita’ hauka — ei prabhura mana  
of the world  ‘good’  may be  this  the Lord’s  mind  
daṇḍa kari’ kare tāra hṛdaya śodhana  
punishment  doing  He does  his  heart’s  purification

(136) “May the world be blessed” — such is the Lord’s mind. Even when He punishes, He does so to purify a person’s heart.

স্বগণ-সহিত প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ কৈলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভু তারে তবে প্রসন্ন হইলা ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

svagaṇa-sahita prabhura nimantraṇa kailā  
His associates  together with  the Lord’s  invitation  he made  
mahāprabhu tāre tabe prasanna hailā  
Mahāprabhu  with him  then  pleased  became

(137) The Bhaṭṭa invited the Lord together with His associates, and Mahāprabhu was then pleased with him.

জগদানন্দ-পণ্ডিতের শুদ্ধ গাঢ় ভাব ৷  
সত্যভামা-প্রায় প্রেম ‘বাম্য-স্বভাব’ ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

jagadānanda-paṇḍitera śuddha gāḍha bhāva  
of Jagadānanda  Paṇḍita  pure  deep  bhāva  
satyabhāmā-prāya prema ‘vāmya-svabhāva’  
Satyabhāmā  like  love  ‘contrary nature’

(138) Jagadānanda Paṇḍita’s bhāva was pure and deep. Like Satyabhāmā’s, his love had a contrary nature (*vāmya*).[^42]

বার-বার প্রণয় কলহ করে প্রভু-সনে ৷  
অন্যোঽন্যে খট্মটি চলে দুইজনে ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

bāra-bāra praṇaya kalaha kare prabhu-sane  
again and again  loving  quarrels  makes  with the Lord  
anyo-’nye khaṭmaṭi cale dui-jane  
mutually  squabbling  goes on  between the two

(139) Again and again he had loving quarrels with the Lord, and the two of them kept squabbling with each other.[^43]

গদাধর-পণ্ডিতের শুদ্ধ গাঢ় ভাব ৷  
রুক্মিণী-দেবীর যৈছে ‘দক্ষিণ-স্বভাব’ ॥ ১৪০ ॥

gadādhara-paṇḍitera śuddha gāḍha bhāva  
of Gadādhara  Paṇḍita  pure  deep  bhāva  
rukmiṇī-devīra yaiche ‘dakṣiṇa-svabhāva’  
of Rukmiṇī  Devī  as  ‘compliant nature’

(140) Gadādhara Paṇḍita’s bhāva was pure and deep, with a compliant nature (*dakṣiṇa*) like that of Rukmiṇī-devī.[^44]

তাঁর প্রণয়-রোষ দেখিতে প্রভুর ইচ্ছা হয় ৷  
ঐশ্বর্য্য-জ্ঞানে তাঁর রোষ নাহি উপজয় ॥ ১৪১ ॥

tām̐ra praṇaya-roṣa dekhite prabhura icchā haya  
his  loving  anger  to see  the Lord’s  desire  is  
aiśvarya-jñāne tām̐ra roṣa nāhi upajaya  
by the knowledge of majesty  his  anger  not  arises

(141) The Lord wanted to see his loving anger (*praṇaya-roṣa*), but because Gadādhara was conscious of the Lord’s majesty, anger never arose in him.

এই লক্ষ্য পাঞা প্রভু কৈলা রোষাভাস ৷  
শুনি’ পণ্ডিতের চিত্তে উপজিল ত্রাস ॥ ১৪২ ॥

ei lakṣya pāñā prabhu kailā roṣābhāsa  
this  pretext  getting  the Lord  made  a semblance of anger  
śuni’ paṇḍitera citte upajila trāsa  
hearing  in the Paṇḍita’s  heart  arose  fear

(142) Taking this occasion, the Lord feigned anger, and when the Paṇḍita heard of it, fear arose in his heart.[^45]

পূর্ব্বে যেন কৃষ্ণ যদি উপহাস কৈল ৷  
শুনি’ রুক্মিণীর মনে ত্রাস উপজিল ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

pūrve yena kṛṣṇa yadi upahāsa kaila  
long ago  as  Kṛṣṇa  when  mockery  made  
śuni’ rukmiṇīra mane trāsa upajila  
hearing  in Rukmiṇī’s  mind  fear  arose

(143) So it was long ago: when Kṛṣṇa teased Rukmiṇī mockingly, fear arose in her mind as she heard Him.

বল্লভ-ভট্টের হয় বাৎসল্য-উপাসন ৷  
বালগোপাল-মন্ত্রে তেঁহো করেন সেবন ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

vallabha-bhaṭṭera haya vātsalya-upāsana  
of Vallabha  Bhaṭṭa  is  parental-love  worship  
bāla-gopāla-mantre tem̐ho karena sevana  
with the Bāla-gopāla  mantra  he  does  service

(144) Vallabha Bhaṭṭa worshipped in the mood of parental love (*vātsalya*); he served with the Bāla-gopāla mantra.

পণ্ডিতের সনে তার মন ফিরি’ গেল ৷  
কিশোরগোপাল-উপাসনায় মন দিল ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

paṇḍitera sane tāra mana phiri’ gela  
the Paṇḍita’s  in company  his  mind  turned  went  
kiśora-gopāla-upāsanāya mana dila  
to the Kiśora-gopāla  worship  mind  he gave

(145) In the company of the Paṇḍita his heart changed, and he gave his mind to the worship of Kiśora-gopāla.[^46]

পণ্ডিতের ঠাঞি চাহে মন্ত্রাদি শিখিতে ৷  
পণ্ডিত কহে, — এই কর্ম্ম নহে আমা হৈতে ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

paṇḍitera ṭhāñi cāhe mantrādi śikhite  
from the Paṇḍita  he wants  mantra and the rest  to learn  
paṇḍita kahe, — ei karma nahe āmā haite  
the Paṇḍita  says  this  act  is not  from me

(146) He wanted to receive the mantra and so on from the Paṇḍita, but the Paṇḍita said, “This is not for me to do.

আমি — পরতন্ত্র, আমার প্রভু — গৌরচন্দ্র ৷  
তাঁর আজ্ঞা বিনা আমি না হই ‘স্বতন্ত্র’ ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

āmi — paratantra, āmāra prabhu — gauracandra  
I  dependent,  my  Lord  Gauracandra  
tām̐ra ājñā vinā āmi nā hai ‘svatantra’  
His  order  without  I  not  am  ‘independent’

(147) I am dependent; my Lord is Gauracandra. Without His order I am not free to act on my own.

তুমি যে আমার ঠাঞি কর আগমন ৷  
তাহাতেই প্রভু মোরে দেন ওলাহন ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

tumi ye āmāra ṭhāñi kara āgamana  
you  that  my  to the place  make  coming  
tāhātei prabhu more dena olāhana  
for that alone  the Lord  me  gives  reproach

(148) Just because you come to me, the Lord reproaches me.”

এইমত ভট্টের কথেক দিন গেল ৷  
শেষে যদি প্রভু তারে সুপ্রসন্ন হৈল ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

ei-mata bhaṭṭera katheka dina gela  
in this way  of the Bhaṭṭa  some  days  passed  
śeṣe yadi prabhu tāre suprasanna haila  
at last  when  the Lord  with him  well pleased  became

(149) In this way some days passed for the Bhaṭṭa. At last the Lord became fully pleased with him.

নিমন্ত্রণের দিনে পণ্ডিতে বোলাইলা ৷  
স্বরূপ, জগদানন্দ, গোবিন্দে পাঠাইলা ॥ ১৫০ ॥

nimantraṇera dine paṇḍite bolāilā  
of the invitation  on the day  the Paṇḍita  He had called  
svarūpa, jagadānanda, govinde pāṭhāilā  
Svarūpa,  Jagadānanda,  Govinda  He sent

(150) On the day of the invitation the Lord called for the Paṇḍita, sending Svarūpa, Jagadānanda and Govinda to bring him.

পথে পণ্ডিতেরে স্বরূপ কহেন বচন ৷  
পরীক্ষিতে প্রভু তোমারে কৈলা উপেক্ষণ ॥ ১৫১ ॥

pathe paṇḍitere svarūpa kahena vacana  
on the way  to the Paṇḍita  Svarūpa  says  words  
parīkṣite prabhu tomāre kailā upekṣaṇa  
to test  the Lord  you  made  disregard

(151) On the way Svarūpa said to the Paṇḍita, “The Lord disregarded you to test you.

তুমি কেনে আসি’ তাঁরে না দিলা ওলাহন ?  
ভীতপ্রায় হঞা কেনে করিলা সহন ? ॥ ১৫২ ॥

tumi kene āsi’ tām̐re nā dilā olāhana?  
you  why  coming  to Him  not  gave  reproach  
bhīta-prāya hañā kene karilā sahana?  
frightened  as if  being  why  did you  endure

(152) Why didn’t you come and reproach Him? Why did you put up with it, as if you were afraid?”

পণ্ডিত কহেন, — প্রভু সর্ব্বজ্ঞ শিরোমণি ৷  
তাঁর সনে ‘হঠ’ করি, — ভাল নাহি মানি ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

paṇḍita kahena, — prabhu sarvajña śiromaṇi  
the Paṇḍita  says  the Lord  of the all-knowing  the crown jewel  
tām̐ra sane ‘haṭha’ kari, — bhāla nāhi māni  
Him  with  ‘obstinacy’  I make  good  not  I consider

(153) The Paṇḍita said, “The Lord is the crown jewel of the all-knowing. I do not think it right to be obstinate with Him.

যেই কহে, সেই সহি নিজ-শিরে ধরি’ ৷  
আপনে করিবেন কৃপা গুণ-দোষ বিচারি’ ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

yei kahe, sei sahi nija-śire dhari’  
whatever  He says  that  I bear  on my own  head  holding  
āpane karibena kṛpā guṇa-doṣa vicāri’  
Himself  He will show  mercy  merits  faults  considering

(154) Whatever He says, I bear it, holding it on my head. He Himself will weigh my merits and faults and show me mercy.”

এত বলি’ পণ্ডিত প্রভুর স্থানে আইলা ৷  
রোদন করিয়া প্রভুর চরণে পড়িলা ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

eta bali’ paṇḍita prabhura sthāne āilā  
thus  saying  the Paṇḍita  to the Lord’s  place  came  
rodana kariyā prabhura caraṇe paḍilā  
weeping  doing  at the Lord’s  feet  fell

(155) Saying this, the Paṇḍita came to the Lord and, weeping, fell at His feet.

ঈষৎ হাসিয়া প্রভু কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ৷  
সবারে শুনাঞা কহেন মধুর বচন ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

īṣat hāsiyā prabhu kailā āliṅgana  
slightly  smiling  the Lord  made  an embrace  
sabāre śunāñā kahena madhura vacana  
to all  making hear  He speaks  sweet  words

(156) Smiling slightly, the Lord embraced him and spoke sweet words for all to hear:

আমি চালাইলুঁ তোমা, তুমি না চলিলা ৷  
ক্রোধে কিছু না কহিলা, সকল সহিলা ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

āmi cālāilum̐ tomā, tumi nā calilā  
I  showed anger  to you,  you  not  were moved  
krodhe kichu nā kahilā, sakala sahilā  
in anger  anything  not  you said,  all  you bore

(157) “I showed you anger, but you were not moved. You said nothing in anger; you bore it all.

আমার ভঙ্গীতে তোমার মন না চলিলা ৷  
সুদৃঢ় সরলভাবে আমারে কিনিলা ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

āmāra bhaṅgīte tomāra mana nā calilā  
My  by the gestures  your  mind  not  was moved  
sudṛḍha sarala-bhāve āmāre kinilā  
very firm  by simplicity of heart  Me  you have bought

(158) My gestures did not move your mind. By your firm, simple-hearted love you have bought Me.”

পণ্ডিতের ভাব-মুদ্রা কহন না যায় ৷  
‘গদাধর-প্রাণনাথ’ নাম হৈল যায় ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

paṇḍitera bhāva-mudrā kahana nā yāya  
of the Paṇḍita  of the bhāva  the marks  description  not  goes  
‘gadādhara-prāṇa-nātha’ nāma haila yāya  
‘Gadādhara’s  life’s  Lord’  name  became  by which

(159) The marks of the Paṇḍita’s bhāva cannot be described; by them the Lord came to be named ‘Gadādhara-prāṇanātha’ (Lord of Gadādhara’s life).

পণ্ডিতে প্রভুর প্রসাদ কহন না যায় ৷  
‘গদাই-গৌরাঙ্গ’ বলি’ যাঁরে লোকে গায় ॥ ১৬০ ॥

paṇḍite prabhura prasāda kahana nā yāya  
to the Paṇḍita  the Lord’s  favour  description  not  goes  
‘gadāi-gaurāṅga’ bali’ yām̐re loke gāya  
‘Gadāi-Gaurāṅga’  calling  whom  people  sing

(160) The Lord’s favour to the Paṇḍita cannot be described. People sing of Him as ‘Gadāi-Gaurāṅga.’

চৈতন্যপ্রভুর লীলা কে বুঝিতে পারে ?  
একলীলায় বহে গঙ্গার শত শত ধারে ॥ ১৬১ ॥

caitanya-prabhura līlā ke bujhite pāre?  
of Caitanya Prabhu  the pastimes  who  to understand  can  
eka-līlāya vahe gaṅgāra śata śata dhāre  
in one pastime  flows  of the Gaṅgā  hundreds  hundreds  in streams

(161) Who can understand the pastimes of Caitanya Prabhu? In one pastime the Gaṅgā flows in hundreds and hundreds of streams.

পণ্ডিতের সৌজন্য, ব্রহ্মণ্যতা-গুণ ৷  
দৃঢ় প্রেমমুদ্রা লোকে করিলা খ্যাপন ॥ ১৬২ ॥

paṇḍitera saujanya, brahmaṇyatā-guṇa  
of the Paṇḍita  the gentleness,  brahminical  virtue  
dṛḍha prema-mudrā loke karilā khyāpana  
firm  of love  the mark  to the world  He made  proclamation

(162) The Lord made known to the world the Paṇḍita’s gentleness, his brahminical virtue, and the firm mark of his love.

অভিমান-পঙ্ক ধুঞা ভট্টেরে শোধিলা ৷  
সেইদ্বারা আর সব লোকে শিখাইলা ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

abhimāna-paṅka dhuñā bhaṭṭere śodhilā  
of pride  the mire  washing  the Bhaṭṭa  He purified  
sei-dvārā āra saba loke śikhāilā  
by that  others  all  people  He taught

(163) Washing away the mire of pride, He purified the Bhaṭṭa, and by this He taught everyone else as well.

অন্তরে ‘অনুগ্রহ’, বাহ্যে ‘উপেক্ষার প্রায়’ ৷  
বাহ্যার্থ যেই লয়, সেই নাশ যায় ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

antare ‘anugraha’, bāhye ‘upekṣāra prāya’  
within  ‘grace’,  outwardly  ‘like disregard’  
bāhyārtha yei laya, sei nāśa yāya  
the outer meaning  whoever  takes,  he  ruin  goes to

(164) Within is grace; outwardly it looks like disregard. Whoever takes only the outer meaning is ruined.[^47]

নিগূঢ় চৈতন্যলীলা বুঝিতে কার শক্তি ?  
সেই বুঝে, গৌরচন্দ্রে যাঁর দৃঢ় ভক্তি ॥ ১৬৫ ॥

nigūḍha caitanya-līlā bujhite kāra śakti?  
hidden  of Caitanya  the pastimes  to understand  whose  power  
sei bujhe, gauracandre yām̐ra dṛḍha bhakti  
he  understands  in Gauracandra  whose  firm  devotion

(165) Who has the power to understand the hidden pastimes of Caitanya? Only one whose devotion to Gauracandra is firm understands them.

দিনান্তরে পণ্ডিত কৈল প্রভুর নিমন্ত্রণ ৷  
প্রভু তাঁহা ভিক্ষা কৈল লঞা ভক্তগণ ॥ ১৬৬ ॥

dināntare paṇḍita kaila prabhura nimantraṇa  
on another day  the Paṇḍita  made  the Lord’s  invitation  
prabhu tām̐hā bhikṣā kaila lañā bhakta-gaṇa  
the Lord  there  alms-meal  took  bringing  the devotees

(166) On another day the Paṇḍita invited the Lord, and the Lord took His meal there together with the devotees.

তাঁহাই বল্লভ-ভট্ট প্রভুর আজ্ঞা লৈল ৷  
পণ্ডিত-ঠাঞি পূর্ব্বপ্রার্থিত সব সিদ্ধি হৈল ॥ ১৬৭ ॥

tām̐hāi vallabha-bhaṭṭa prabhura ājñā laila  
right there  Vallabha  Bhaṭṭa  the Lord’s  permission  took  
paṇḍita-ṭhāñi pūrva-prārthita saba siddhi haila  
from the Paṇḍita  long  prayed for  all  fulfilment  came

(167) There Vallabha Bhaṭṭa received the Lord’s permission, and from the Paṇḍita he obtained all he had long prayed for.[^48]

এই ত’ কহিলুঁ বল্লভ-ভট্টের মিলন ৷  
যাহার শ্রবণে পায় গৌরপ্রেমধন ॥ ১৬৮ ॥

ei ta’ kahilum̐ vallabha-bhaṭṭera milana  
this  indeed  I have told  with Vallabha  Bhaṭṭa  the meeting  
yāhāra śravaṇe pāya gaura-prema-dhana  
of which  by hearing  one gains  for Gaura  love’s  treasure

(168) Thus I have told of the meeting with Vallabha Bhaṭṭa. By hearing of it, one gains the treasure of love for Gaura.[^49]

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১৬৯ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(169) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.[^50]

[^6]: Heading in the edition: the humility of Bhagavān, and His pretence.

[^7]: *Anubhāṣya*: prema-bhakti is higher than the four goals of life (dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa) — it is the crown jewel of the puruṣārthas; service on the path of rāgānugā is higher than the worship of Kṛṣṇa on the path of rules (*vidhi*).

In the edition the reference is “canto 10, chapter 9, verse 31”; in the standard numbering, 10.9.21.

[^10]: *Anubhāṣya*: Lakṣmīdevī and the associates called *ātma-bhūta* are potencies non-different from Kṛṣṇa; even so, Lakṣmī is full of the mood of majesty and therefore did not gain the right to serve Vrajendra-nandana in the madhura-rasa. Heading in the edition: the meaning of a word of the verse; Lakṣmī has no right to take part in the rāsa dance.

[^11]: The same verse as Madhya 8.80 (= 8.231, 9.121); translation as there.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: “in pure bhāva” — not enchanted or bound by the knowledge of majesty, but ruled by pure, unmixed (*kevala*) *rati*. Heading in the edition: the friendship (*sakhya*) and parental love (*vātsalya*) of the people of Vraja are *kevala*, that is, pure *rāgātmikā* bhakti.

[^13]: The same verse as Madhya 8.75; translation as there. In the edition the reference is “canto 10, chapter 12, verse 21”; in the standard numbering, 10.12.11.

[^14]: The same verse as Madhya 19.204; translation as there.

[^15]: The same verse as Madhya 8.77; translation as there.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: some manuscripts read here *anargala rasa-vettā prema-sukhānanda* — “a boundless knower of rasa, full of the bliss of prema”. Heading in the edition: the Lord declares Rāya His instructing guru (*śikṣā-guru*).

[^17]: The same verse as Ādi 4.173 (= Madhya 8.218, 18.65); translation as there.

[^18]: The same verse as Madhya 19.210; translation as there. *Anubhāṣya*: another reading has here the couplet “The pure love of the gopīs is free of the knowledge of majesty; in love they scold Him — this is its sign.”

[^19]: The same verse as Ādi 4.180 (= Madhya 8.92); translation as there.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: another reading cites here Uddhava’s prayer (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.47.61): “Ah, if only I could be some bush, creeper or herb in Vṛndāvana that receives the dust of these gopīs’ feet!” — Uddhava, having accepted as his ideal the gopīs’ love, free of the knowledge of majesty, prays for eternal service to them.

[^21]: Heading in the edition: only in the company of a sādhu who himself follows pure bhakti and preaches it does the jīva attain devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

[^22]: Heading in the edition: to rid Vallabha of his pride, the Lord describes the qualities of devotees far worthier than he.

[^23]: Heading in the edition: by the Lord’s mercy Vallabha’s pride is crushed. *Anubhāṣya*: “long-standing pride” — a well-nourished, towering self-conceit.

[^24]: The meal took place at the Lord’s residence: the Bhaṭṭa had mahā-prasāda brought there and fed the Lord and His associates (vv. 59–67).

[^25]: Some editions read *pratyeke*, “to each one”; the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition has *pratyakṣe*, “in (the Lord’s) presence”.

[^26]: Some editions read *karena nartana*, “they dance”; the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition has *karena kīrtana*, “they lead the kīrtana”. Heading in the edition: like a whirling firebrand (*alāta-cakra*), the Lord moves from kīrtana to kīrtana.

[^27]: Fourteen *mādalas* for all seven groups — two in each (compare Madhya 13.48).

[^28]: Some editions read *yātrānantare*, “after the festival”; the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition has *yātrāntare*, “at another festival”, that is, on another day (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*).

[^29]: Heading in the edition: considering Himself unqualified, the Lord speaks humbly and in pretence; without the aim of pleasing Kṛṣṇa and His own, the meaning of the Bhāgavata cannot be grasped by worldly learning.

[^30]: Heading in the edition: the *rūḍhi* meaning of the name “Kṛṣṇa” — a verse from Śrī Lakṣmīdhara’s *Nāma-kaumudī*, cited in the *Kṛṣṇa-sandarbha*. *Anubhāṣya*: *rūḍhi* is the power of a word to convey the meaning of the whole word without depending on the meanings of root and suffix.

[^31]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the devotion he had for the Lord partly ebbed away. Heading in the edition: the Bhaṭṭa leaves with a heavy heart; his pride is humbled, and therefore his devotion to the Lord weakens.

[^32]: *Paṇḍita Gosāñi* is Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita Gosvāmī (*Anubhāṣya*). Heading in the edition: the Bhaṭṭa begins to court Śrī Gadādhara’s favour.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu had disregarded Vallabha Bhaṭṭa; yet if the Paṇḍita listened to his explanation of the name, it would pain the Lord. Which side was he to protect? So the Paṇḍita was caught between two fires.

[^34]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *ābhijātye* — because of noble standing, that is, because Vallabha Bhaṭṭa was honoured among scholars. The *Anubhāṣya* gives three reasons: out of embarrassment; because Vallabha’s learning was not wholly opposed to bhakti; for the sake of the respect customary in society. Heading in the edition: Gadādhara, who honours everyone and wishes to trouble no one, is caught between two fires and begs Kṛṣṇa for mercy.

[^35]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord knew the position the Paṇḍita was in, so he had no fear of His displeasure. What he feared was that some Vaiṣṇava might call him “Vallabha’s companion” and speak against him.

[^36]: Heading in the edition: to show Vallabha mercy and point out his eternal good, the Lord answers sharply and severely, yet truly: the jīva, by nature *prakṛti*, is enjoined by Kṛṣṇa Himself, her husband, always to utter His name.

[^37]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śrīdhara Svāmī explains each statement just as it stands where it occurs, and so, Vallabha says, there is no unity of meaning in him. The word ‘Svāmī’ is taken up by the Lord in the next verse.

[^38]: A pun: *svāmī* means both Śrīdhara Svāmī and “husband, master”; the Lord answers with Vallabha’s own words (110) and continues the image of the faithful wife (99–103). Heading in the edition: the Lord upholds the commentary of Śrīdhara Svāmī, “the sole guardian of bhakti”; only Śrīdhara accepts Viṣṇu, the one Spirit, as Lord (*svāmī*), while his opponent, who mixes spirit and matter, is the one who is wayward.

[^39]: Heading in the edition: by disregard, dishonour and other dealings unpleasant to the senses, the Lord, partial to no one, frees the scholar who has turned from Him of arrogance and envy.

[^40]: Heading in the edition: wise is one who sees the Lord’s mercy in the fruits of karma that are painful at first but beneficial in the end.

[^41]: The second line is the same as Antya 3.136; translation as there. Heading in the edition: the kīrtana of Kṛṣṇa free of the ten offences to the holy name leads to Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

[^42]: Heading in the edition: Jagadānanda is the avatāra of Satyabhāmā; his contrary nature (*vāmya*) and pure, deep love for Gaura. Here the *Anubhāṣya* cites from the *Bṛhad-bhāgavatāmṛta* (1.7.83) the account of how Satyabhāmā, offended, withdrew to the chamber of sulking and Kṛṣṇa ordered her to be brought.

[^43]: Heading in the edition: Jagadānanda’s quarrels of love (*praṇaya-kalaha*) with the Lord.

[^44]: Heading in the edition: Gadādhara is the avatāra of Rukmiṇī; his compliant nature (*dakṣiṇa*) and pure, deep love for Gaura. *Anubhāṣya* (on 140–141): once in Dvārakā Kṛṣṇa jokingly advised Rukmiṇī to choose another husband; she was frightened and, by her compliant nature, fell at His feet. So too in Gaura’s pastimes: Jagadānanda, like Satyabhāmā, is contrary and quarrels out of love, while Gadādhara, like Rukmiṇī, is timid and always follows the Lord in everything.

[^45]: Heading in the edition: on the pretext of goodwill toward Vallabha, the Lord outwardly shows feigned anger and so tests Gadādhara’s love; Gadādhara is frightened.

[^46]: Heading in the edition: association with Gadādhara inclines Vallabha to worship in *madhura-rasa*.

[^47]: *Anubhāṣya*: wishing Vallabha Bhaṭṭa well, the Lord outwardly disregarded him and so cleansed him of the conceit of a scholar and a Vaiṣṇava; and since Gadādhara Paṇḍita had not rejected Vallabha altogether, for some days the Lord showed disregard to him as well. Yet Mahāprabhu was never displeased with Gadādhara, the embodiment of His own *svarūpa-śakti*, nor can He be; whoever fails to grasp the hidden meaning of this pastime and loses faith in Śrī Gadādhara’s feet will surely fall.

[^48]: Heading in the edition: there Vallabha receives from Gadādhara initiation into the Kiśora-gopāla mantra in *madhura-rasa*.

[^49]: Heading in the edition: the meeting of Gadādhara and Vallabha grants love for Gaura.
