“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 19  
**The Mercy Shown to Śrī Rūpa**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বৃন্দাবনীয়াং রসকেলিবার্ত্তাং  
কালেন লুপ্তাং নিজশক্তিমুৎকঃ ৷  
সঞ্চার্য্য রূপে ব্যতনোৎ পুনঃ স  
প্রভুর্বিধৌ প্রাগিব লোকসৃষ্টিম্ ॥ ১ ॥

vṛndāvanīyāṁ rasa-keli-vārtāṁ  
of Vṛndāvana  of the playful pastimes full of rasa  the account  
kālena luptāṁ nija-śaktim utkaḥ  
by time  lost  His own power  eager  
sañcārya rūpe vyatanot punaḥ sa  
having infused  into Rūpa  He spread  again  He  
prabhur vidhau prāg iva loka-sṛṣṭim  
the Lord  into Brahmā  before  as  the creation of the world

(1) Eager to restore the account of Vṛndāvana’s pastimes full of rasa, which had been lost over time, the Lord infused His own power into Rūpa and spread it anew, just as long ago He had infused His power into Brahmā and so brought forth the creation of the world.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^1]

শ্রীরূপ-সনাতন রহে রামকেলি-গ্রামে ৷  
প্রভুরে মিলিয়া, গেলা আপন-ভবনে ॥ ৩ ॥

śrī-rūpa-sanātana rahe rāmakeli-grāme  
Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana  stayed  in Rāmakeli  village  
prabhure miliyā, gelā āpana-bhavane  
with the Lord  having met  went  to their own  homes

(3) Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana were staying in the village of Rāmakeli. Having met the Lord there, they returned to their own homes.

দুইভাই বিষয়-ত্যাগের উপায় সৃজিল ৷  
বহুধন দিয়া দুই ব্রাহ্মণে বরিল ॥ ৪ ॥

duibhāi viṣaya-tyāgera upāya sṛjila  
the two brothers  of worldly affairs  of giving up  a means  devised  
bahu-dhana diyā dui brāhmaṇe varila  
much  wealth  giving  two  brāhmaṇas  they engaged

(4) The two brothers devised a way to give up their worldly affairs: they engaged two brāhmaṇas and gave them much wealth.

কৃষ্ণমন্ত্রে করাইল দুই পুরশ্চরণ ৷  
অচিরাৎ পাইবারে চৈতন্য-চরণ ॥ ৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-mantre karāila dui puraścaraṇa  
with the mantra of Kṛṣṇa  had performed  two  puraścaraṇas  
acirāt pāibāre caitanya-caraṇa  
soon  to attain  Caitanya’s  feet

(5) These brāhmaṇas performed two puraścaraṇas for them with the Kṛṣṇa mantra, so that the brothers might soon attain the feet of Caitanya.

শ্রীরূপ গোসাঞি তবে নৌকাতে ভরিয়া ৷  
আপনার ঘরে আইলা বহুধন লঞা ॥ ৬ ॥

śrī-rūpa gosāñi tabe naukāte bhariyā  
Śrī Rūpa  Gosvāmī  then  a boat  loading  
āpanāra ghare āilā bahu-dhana lañā  
to his own  home  came  much  wealth  taking

(6) Then Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī loaded a boat and came to his own home, bringing great wealth with him.[^2]

ব্রাহ্মণ-বৈষ্ণবে দিলা তার অর্দ্ধ-ধনে ৷  
এক চৌঠি ধন দিলা কুটুম্ব-ভরণে ॥ ৭ ॥

brāhmaṇa-vaiṣṇave dilā tāra ardha-dhane  
to brāhmaṇas and Vaiṣṇavas  he gave  of it  half the wealth  
eka cauṭhi dhana dilā kuṭumba-bharaṇe  
one  quarter  of the wealth  he gave  for the support of his relatives

(7) He gave half of that wealth to brāhmaṇas and Vaiṣṇavas, and a quarter for the support of his relatives.

দণ্ডবন্ধ লাগি’ চৌঠি সঞ্চয় করিলা ৷  
ভাল-ভাল বিপ্র-স্থানে স্থাপ্য রাখিলা ॥ ৮ ॥

daṇḍa-bandha lāgi’ cauṭhi sañcaya karilā  
punishment and imprisonment  against  a quarter  savings  he made  
bhāla-bhāla vipra-sthāne sthāpya rākhilā  
with trustworthy  brāhmaṇas  in deposit  he kept

(8) The remaining quarter he saved against punishment and imprisonment, leaving it on deposit with trustworthy brāhmaṇas.[^3]

গৌড়ে রাখিল মুদ্রা দশ-হাজারে ৷  
সনাতন ব্যয় করে, রাখে মুদি-ঘরে ॥ ৯ ॥

gauḍe rākhila mudrā daśa-hājāre  
in Gauḍa  he left  coins  ten thousand  
sanātana vyaya kare, rākhe mudi-ghare  
Sanātana  spending  makes  keeps  in a grocer’s house

(9) In Gauḍa he left ten thousand coins in the keeping of a grocer, for Sanātana to spend.

শ্রীরূপ শুনিল প্রভুর নীলাদ্রি-গমন ৷  
বনপথে যাবেন প্রভু শ্রীবৃন্দাবন ॥ ১০ ॥

śrī-rūpa śunila prabhura nīlādri-gamana  
Śrī Rūpa  heard  the Lord’s  to Nīlādri  going  
vana-pathe yābena prabhu śrī-vṛndāvana  
by the forest path  will go  the Lord  to Śrī Vṛndāvana

(10) Śrī Rūpa heard that the Lord had gone to Nīlādri and would go to Śrī Vṛndāvana by the forest path.

রূপ-গোসাঞি নীলাচলে পাঠাইল দুইজন ৷  
প্রভু যবে বৃন্দাবন করেন গমন ॥ ১১ ॥

rūpa-gosāñi nīlācale pāṭhāila duijana  
Rūpa Gosvāmī  to Nīlācala  sent  two men  
prabhu yabe vṛndāvana karena gamana  
the Lord  when  to Vṛndāvana  makes  departure

(11) Rūpa Gosvāmī sent two men to Nīlācala to learn when the Lord would set out for Vṛndāvana.

শীঘ্র আসি’ মোরে তাঁর দিবা সমাচার ৷  
শুনিয়া তদনুরূপ করিব ব্যবহার ॥ ১২ ॥

śīghra āsi’ more tām̐ra dibā samācāra  
quickly  coming  to me  of Him  you will give  news  
śuniyā tad-anurūpa kariba vyavahāra  
hearing  in accordance with that  I will make  arrangements

(12) “Come back quickly and bring me news of Him. When I hear it, I shall act accordingly.”

এথা সনাতন-গোসাঞি ভাবে মনে মন ৷  
রাজা মোরে প্রীতি করে, সে — মোর বন্ধন ॥ ১৩ ॥

ethā sanātana-gosāñi bhāve mane mana  
here  Sanātana Gosvāmī  thinks  in his mind  to himself  
rājā more prīti kare, se — mora bandhana  
the king  me  affection  shows  that  my  bondage

(13) Meanwhile Sanātana Gosvāmī thought to himself: “The king is fond of me, and that is my bondage.

কোন মতে রাজা যদি মোরে ক্রুদ্ধ হয় ৷  
তবে অব্যাহতি হয়, করিলুঁ নিশ্চয় ॥ ১৪ ॥

kona mate rājā yadi more kruddha haya  
by some  means  the king  if  with me  angry  becomes  
tabe avyāhati haya, karilum̐ niścaya  
then  release  will be  I have made  decision

(14) If somehow the king becomes angry with me, I shall be released. So I have decided.”

অস্বাস্থ্যের ছদ্ম করি’ রহে নিজ-ঘরে ৷  
রাজকার্য্য ছাড়িলা, না যায় রাজদ্বারে ॥ ১৫ ॥

asvāsthyera chadma kari’ rahe nija-ghare  
of illness  pretext  making  stays  in his own house  
rājakārya chāḍilā, nā yāya rāja-dvāre  
the king’s business  he gave up  not  goes  to the king’s court

(15) Pretending to be ill, he stayed at home. He gave up the king’s business and no longer went to the court.

লোভী কায়স্থগণ রাজকার্য্য করে ৷  
আপনে স্বগৃহে করে শাস্ত্রের বিচারে ॥ ১৬ ॥

lobhī kāyastha-gaṇa rāja-kārya kare  
greedy  kāyastha officials  the king’s business  carry on  
āpane svagṛhe kare śāstrera vicāre  
he himself  in his own home  makes  of the scriptures  study

(16) Greedy kāyastha officials carried on the king’s business, while he himself studied the scriptures at home.[^4]

ভট্টাচার্য্য পণ্ডিত বিশ ত্রিশ লঞা ৷  
ভাগবত বিচার করেন সভাতে বসিয়া ॥ ১৭ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya paṇḍita biśa triśa lañā  
bhaṭṭācāryas  paṇḍitas  twenty  thirty  gathering  
bhāgavata vicāra karena sabhāte vasiyā  
the Bhāgavata  study  makes  in the assembly  sitting

(17) Gathering twenty or thirty learned bhaṭṭācāryas and paṇḍitas, he would sit in assembly with them and study the Bhāgavata.

আর দিন গৌড়েশ্বর, সঙ্গে একজন ৷  
আচম্বিতে গোসাঞি-সভাতে কৈল আগমন ॥ ১৮ ॥

āra dina gauḍeśvara, saṅge eka-jana  
another  day  the ruler of Gauḍa  with him  one person  
ācambite gosāñi-sabhāte kaila āgamana  
suddenly  into the Gosvāmī’s assembly  made  arrival

(18) One day the ruler of Gauḍa, with one companion, suddenly came into the Gosvāmī’s assembly.[^5]

পাৎসাহ দেখিয়া সবে সম্ভ্রমে উঠিলা ৷  
সম্ভ্রমে আসন দিয়া রাজারে বসাইলা ॥ ১৯ ॥

pātsāha dekhiyā sabe sambhrame uṭhilā  
the emperor  seeing  all  respectfully  rose  
sambhrame āsana diyā rājāre vasāilā  
respectfully  a seat  giving  the king  they seated

(19) Seeing the emperor, everyone rose in awe, and with due respect they offered the king a seat.

রাজা কহে, — তোমার স্থানে বৈদ্য পাঠাইলুঁ ৷  
বৈদ্য কহে, — ব্যাধি নাহি, সুস্থ যে দেখিলুঁ ॥ ২০ ॥

rājā kahe, — tomāra sthāne vaidya pāṭhāilum̐  
the king  says  to you  a physician  I sent  
vaidya kahe, — vyādhi nāhi, sustha ye dekhilum̐  
the physician  says  illness  there is none  healthy  that  I saw

(20) The king said: “I sent my physician to you, and he says, ‘There is no illness; I found him healthy.’

আমার যে কিছু কার্য্য, সব তোমা লঞা ৷  
কার্য্য ছাড়ি’ রহিলা তুমি ঘরেতে বসিয়া ॥ ২১ ॥

āmāra ye kichu kārya, saba tomā lañā  
my  whatever  business  all  rests with you  
kārya chāḍi’ rahilā tumi gharete vasiyā  
business  leaving  have stayed  you  at home  sitting

(21) Whatever business I have rests entirely on you, yet you have left your work and sit at home.

মোর যত কার্য্য কাম, সব কৈলা নাশ ৷  
কি তোমার হৃদয়ে আছে, কহ মোর পাশ ॥ ২২ ॥

mora yata kārya kāma, saba kailā nāśa  
my  all  affairs  undertakings  all  you have made  ruin  
ki tomāra hṛdaye āche, kaha mora pāśa  
what  in your  heart  is  tell  to me

(22) You have ruined all my affairs and undertakings. What is in your heart? Tell me.”

সনাতন কহে, — নহে আমা হৈতে কাম ৷  
আর একজন দিয়া কর সমাধান ॥ ২৩ ॥

sanātana kahe, — nahe āmā haite kāma  
Sanātana  says  will not be  from me  the work  
āra eka-jana diyā kara samādhāna  
another  one person  through  make  arrangement

(23) Sanātana said: “The work can no longer be done by me. Have someone else see to it.”

তবে ক্রুদ্ধ হঞা রাজা কহে আরবার ৷  
তোমার ‘বড় ভাই’ করে দস্যুব্যবহার ॥ ২৪ ॥

tabe kruddha hañā rājā kahe āra-bāra  
then  angry  becoming  the king  says  again  
tomāra ‘baḍa bhāi’ kare dasyu-vyavahāra  
your  ‘elder brother’  does  the conduct of a robber

(24) Then the king grew angry and spoke again: “Your ‘elder brother’ behaves like a robber.[^6]

জীব-পশু মারি’ কৈল চাক্লা সব নাশ ৷  
এথা তুমি কৈলা মোর সর্ব্ব কার্য্য নাশ ॥ ২৫ ॥

jīva-paśu māri’ kaila cāklā saba nāśa  
living beings and animals  killing  he has made  the districts  all  ruin  
ethā tumi kailā mora sarva kārya nāśa  
here  you  have made  my  all  affairs  ruin

(25) Killing living beings and animals, he has laid waste all the districts, and here you have ruined all my affairs.”

সনাতন কহে, — তুমি স্বতন্ত্র গৌড়েশ্বর ৷  
যে যেই দোষ করে, দেহ’ তার ফল ॥ ২৬ ॥

sanātana kahe, — tumi svatantra gauḍeśvara  
Sanātana  says  you  independent  ruler of Gauḍa  
ye yei doṣa kare, deha’ tāra phala  
whoever  whatever  fault  commits  give  him  its fruit

(26) Sanātana said: “You are the independent ruler of Gauḍa. Whatever fault anyone commits, give him its fruit.”[^7]

এত শুনি’ গৌড়েশ্বর উঠি’ ঘরে গেলা ৷  
পলাইব বলি’ সনাতনেরে বান্ধিলা ॥ ২৭ ॥

eta śuni’ gauḍeśvara uṭhi’ ghare gelā  
this  hearing  the ruler of Gauḍa  rising  home  went  
palāiba bali’ sanātanere bāndhilā  
he will flee  thinking  Sanātana  he put in confinement

(27) Hearing this, the ruler of Gauḍa rose and went home. Fearing that he would flee, he had Sanātana put in confinement.

হেনকালে গেল রাজা ওড়িয়া মারিতে ৷  
সনাতনে কহে, — তুমি চল মোর সাথে ॥ ২৮ ॥

hena-kāle gela rājā oḍiyā mārite  
at that time  went  the king  the Oriyās  to attack  
sanātane kahe, — tumi cala mora sāthe  
to Sanātana  says  you  come  with me

(28) At that time the king set out to attack the Oriyās, and he said to Sanātana: “Come with me.”

তেঁহো কহে, — যাবে তুমি দেবতায় দুঃখ দিতে ৷  
মোর শক্তি নাহি, তোমার সঙ্গে যাইতে ॥ ২৯ ॥

tem̐ho kahe, — yābe tumi devatāya duḥkha dite  
he  says  you will go  to the Deity  pain  to give  
mora śakti nāhi, tomāra saṅge yāite  
my  strength  there is not  with you  to go

(29) He replied: “You are going to bring pain to the Deity. I have no strength to go with you.”

তবে তাঁরে বান্ধি’ রাখি’ করিলা গমন ৷  
এথা নীলাচল হৈতে প্রভু চলিলা বৃন্দাবন ॥ ৩০ ॥

tabe tām̐re bāndhi’ rākhi’ karilā gamana  
then  him  in confinement  keeping  he made  departure  
ethā nīlācala haite prabhu calilā vṛndāvana  
meanwhile  Nīlācala  from  the Lord  set out  for Vṛndāvana

(30) Then the king departed, leaving him in confinement. Meanwhile the Lord set out from Nīlācala for Vṛndāvana.

তবে সেই দুই চর রূপ-ঠাঞি আইল ৷  
বৃন্দাবন চলিলা প্রভু — আসিয়া কহিল ॥ ৩১ ॥

tabe sei dui cara rūpa-ṭhāñi āila  
then  those  two  messengers  to Rūpa  came  
vṛndāvana calilā prabhu — āsiyā kahila  
to Vṛndāvana  has set out  the Lord  coming  they said

(31) Then the two messengers came back to Rūpa and told him: “The Lord has set out for Vṛndāvana.”

শুনিয়া শ্রীরূপ লিখিল সনাতন-ঠাঁঞি ৷  
বৃন্দাবন চলিলা শ্রীচৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ॥ ৩২ ॥

śuniyā śrī-rūpa likhila sanātana-ṭhām̐ñi  
hearing  Śrī Rūpa  wrote  to Sanātana  
vṛndāvana calilā śrī-caitanya-gosāñi  
to Vṛndāvana  has set out  Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi

(32) Hearing this, Śrī Rūpa wrote to Sanātana: “Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi has set out for Vṛndāvana.

আমি-দুইভাই চলিলাঙ তাঁহারে মিলিতে ৷  
তুমি যৈছে তৈছে ছুটি’ আইস তাঁহা হৈতে ॥ ৩৩ ॥

āmi-dui-bhāi calilāṅa tām̐hāre milite  
we two brothers  have set out  Him  to meet  
tumi yaiche taiche chuṭi’ āisa tām̐hā haite  
you  in whatever  way  getting free  come  from there

(33) We two brothers are setting out to meet Him. Get free in whatever way you can and come away from there.

দশসহস্র মুদ্রা তথা আছে মুদি-স্থানে ৷  
তাহা দিয়া কর শীঘ্র আত্ম-বিমোচনে ॥ ৩৪ ॥

daśa-sahasra mudrā tathā āche mudi-sthāne  
ten thousand  coins  there  are  with the grocer  
tāhā diyā kara śīghra ātma-vimocane  
with them  paying  make  quickly  your own release

(34) Ten thousand coins are there with the grocer. Use them to free yourself quickly.

যৈছে তৈছে ছুটি’ তুমি আইস বৃন্দাবন ৷  
এত লিখি’ দুই-ভাই করিলা গমন ॥ ৩৫ ॥

yaiche taiche chuṭi’ tumi āisa vṛndāvana  
in whatever  way  getting free  you  come  to Vṛndāvana  
eta likhi’ dui-bhāi karilā gamana  
this  having written  the two brothers  made  departure

(35) Get free in whatever way you can and come to Vṛndāvana.” Having written this, the two brothers set out.

অনুপম মল্লিক, তাঁর নাম — ‘শ্রীবল্লভ’ ৷  
রূপ-গোসাঞির ছোটভাই — পরম-বৈষ্ণব ॥ ৩৬ ॥

anupama mallika, tām̐ra nāma — ‘śrī-vallabha’  
Anupama  Mallika  his  name  ‘Śrī Vallabha’  
rūpa-gosāñira choṭa-bhāi — parama-vaiṣṇava  
Rūpa Gosvāmī’s  younger brother  a great Vaiṣṇava

(36) Anupama Mallika, whose name was Śrī Vallabha, was Rūpa Gosvāmī’s younger brother and a great Vaiṣṇava.[^8]

তাঁহারে লঞা রূপ-গোসাঞি প্রয়াগে আইলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভু তাঁহা, শুনি’ আনন্দিত হৈলা ॥ ৩৭ ॥

tām̐hāre lañā rūpa-gosāñi prayāge āilā  
him  taking  Rūpa Gosvāmī  to Prayāga  came  
mahāprabhu tām̐hā, śuni’ ānandita hailā  
Mahāprabhu  there  hearing  he became joyful

(37) Taking him along, Rūpa Gosvāmī came to Prayāga, and he rejoiced to hear that Mahāprabhu was there.

প্রভু চলিয়াছেন বিন্দুমাধব-দরশনে ৷  
লক্ষ লক্ষ লোক আইসে প্রভুর মিলনে ॥ ৩৮ ॥

prabhu caliyāchena bindu-mādhava-daraśane  
the Lord  had gone  for darśana of Bindu-mādhava  
lakṣa lakṣa loka āise prabhura milane  
hundreds of thousands  of people  come  the Lord  to meet

(38) The Lord had gone for darśana of Bindu-mādhava, and hundreds of thousands of people came to meet Him.

কেহ কান্দে, কেহ হাসে, কেহ নাচে, গায় ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ’ ‘কৃষ্ণ’ বলি’ কেহ পড়াগড়ি যায় ॥ ৩৯ ॥

keha kānde, keha hāse, keha nāce, gāya  
some  weep  some  laugh  some  dance  sing  
‘kṛṣṇa’ ‘kṛṣṇa’ bali’ keha paḍāgaḍi yāya  
‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  crying  some  on the ground  roll

(39) Some wept, some laughed, some danced and sang, and some rolled on the ground, crying, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”

গঙ্গা-যমুনা প্রয়াগ নারিল ডুবাইতে ৷  
প্রভু ডুবাইল কৃষ্ণপ্রেমের বন্যাতে ॥ ৪০ ॥

gaṅgā-yamunā prayāga nārila ḍubāite  
the Gaṅgā and the Yamunā  Prayāga  could not  flood  
prabhu ḍubāila kṛṣṇa-premera vanyāte  
the Lord  flooded  of love for Kṛṣṇa  with the deluge

(40) The Gaṅgā and the Yamunā could not flood Prayāga, but the Lord flooded it with the deluge of love for Kṛṣṇa.

ভিড় দেখি’ দুইভাই রহিলা নির্জ্জনে ৷  
প্রভুর আবেশ হৈল মাধব-দরশনে ॥ ৪১ ॥

bhiḍa dekhi’ duibhāi rahilā nirjane  
the crowd  seeing  the two brothers  stayed  in a secluded place  
prabhura āveśa haila mādhava-daraśane  
the Lord’s  ecstasy  arose  of Mādhava  at the sight

(41) Seeing the crowd, the two brothers stayed apart in a secluded place. At the sight of Mādhava the Lord was overwhelmed with ecstasy.

প্রেমাবেশে নাচে প্রভু হরিধ্বনি করি’ ৷  
ঊর্দ্ধ্ববাহু করি’ বলে — বল ‘হরি’ ‘হরি’ ॥ ৪২ ॥

premāveśe nāce prabhu hari-dhvani kari’  
in loving ecstasy  dances  the Lord  the sound of Hari’s name  making  
ūrddhva-bāhu kari’ bale — bala ‘hari’ ‘hari’  
His arms raised  making  He says  say  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’

(42) Absorbed in love, the Lord danced, calling out the name of Hari. Raising His arms, He said: “Say ‘Hari! Hari!’”

প্রভুর মহিমা দেখি’ লোকে চমৎকার ৷  
প্রয়াগে প্রভুর লীলা নারি বর্ণিবার ॥ ৪৩ ॥

prabhura mahimā dekhi’ loke camatkāra  
the Lord’s  greatness  seeing  the people  were amazed  
prayāge prabhura līlā nāri varṇibāra  
in Prayāga  the Lord’s  pastimes  I cannot  describe

(43) Seeing the Lord’s greatness, the people were amazed. I cannot describe the Lord’s pastimes in Prayāga.

দাক্ষিণাত্য-বিপ্র-সনে আছে পরিচয় ৷  
সেই বিপ্র নিমন্ত্রিয়া নিল নিজালয় ॥ ৪৪ ॥

dākṣiṇātya-vipra-sane āche paricaya  
with a brāhmaṇa from the South  there was  acquaintance  
sei vipra nimantriyā nila nijālaya  
that  brāhmaṇa  inviting  took  to his home

(44) The Lord was acquainted with a brāhmaṇa from the South, and that brāhmaṇa invited Him and took Him to his home.

বিপ্র-গৃহে আসি’ প্রভু নিভৃতে বসিলা ৷  
শ্রীরূপ-বল্লভ দুঁহে আসিয়া মিলিলা ॥ ৪৫ ॥

vipra-gṛhe āsi’ prabhu nibhṛte vasilā  
to the brāhmaṇa’s house  coming  the Lord  in seclusion  sat  
śrī-rūpa-vallabha dum̐he āsiyā mililā  
Śrī Rūpa and Vallabha  both  coming  met

(45) The Lord came to the brāhmaṇa’s house and sat in seclusion, and there Śrī Rūpa and Vallabha both came to meet Him.

দুইগুচ্ছ তৃণ দুঁহে দশনে ধরিয়া ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ দূরে পড়ে দণ্ডবৎ হঞা ॥ ৪৬ ॥

dui-guccha tṛṇa dum̐he daśane dhariyā  
two  bunches  of straw  both  in their teeth  holding  
prabhu dekhi’ dūre paḍe daṇḍavat hañā  
the Lord  seeing  from a distance  they fall  like rods  becoming

(46) Each holding a bunch of straw between his teeth, the two of them saw the Lord from afar and fell flat on the ground like rods.

নানা শ্লোক পড়ি’ উঠে, পড়ে বার বার ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ প্রেমাবেশ হইল দুঁহার ॥ ৪৭ ॥

nānā śloka paḍi’ uṭhe, paḍe bāra bāra  
various  verses  reciting  they rise  fall  again  and again  
prabhu dekhi’ premāveśa haila dum̐hāra  
the Lord  seeing  loving ecstasy  arose  in both

(47) Reciting various verses, they rose and fell again and again. At the sight of the Lord both were overwhelmed with love.

শ্রীরূপে দেখিয়া প্রভুর প্রসন্ন হৈল মন ৷  
‘উঠ, উঠ, রূপ, আইস’, বলিলা বচন ॥ ৪৮ ॥

śrī-rūpe dekhiyā prabhura prasanna haila mana  
Śrī Rūpa  seeing  the Lord’s  pleased  became  mind  
uṭha, uṭha, rūpa, āisa, balilā vacana  
get up  get up  Rūpa  come  He spoke  the words

(48) Seeing Śrī Rūpa, the Lord was pleased at heart. “Get up, get up, Rūpa! Come here!” He said.

কৃষ্ণের করুণা কিছু না যায় বর্ণনে ৷  
বিষয়কূপ হৈতে তোমা কাড়িল দুইজনে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

kṛṣṇera karuṇā kichu nā yāya varṇane  
Kṛṣṇa’s  mercy  at all  not  can be  described  
viṣaya-kūpa haite tomā kāḍila dui-jane  
from the well of worldly life  out of  you  He has pulled  both

(49) “Kṛṣṇa’s mercy cannot be described at all. He has pulled you both out of the well of worldly life.”

ন মেঽভক্তশ্চতুর্ব্বেদী মদ্ভক্তঃ শ্বপচঃ প্রিয়ঃ ৷  
তস্মৈ দেয়ং ততো গ্রাহ্যং স চ পূজ্যো যথা হ্যহম্ ॥ ৫০ ॥

na me ’bhaktaś catur-vedī  
not  to Me  a nondevotee  a knower of the four Vedas  
mad-bhaktaḥ śva-pacaḥ priyaḥ  
My devotee  a dog-eater  dear  
tasmai deyaṁ tato grāhyaṁ  
to him  should be given  from him  should be accepted  
sa ca pūjyo yathā hy aham  
he  and  is to be worshiped  as  indeed  I

(50) “Even a knower of the four Vedas is not dear to Me if he is not devoted to Me, but My devotee is dear to Me even if he was born a dog-eater. One should give to him and accept from him; he is to be worshipped just as I am.” (Itihāsa-samuccaya, quoted in Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 10.91)[^9]

এই শ্লোক পড়ি’ দুঁহারে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ৷  
কৃপাতে দুঁহার মাথায় ধরিলা চরণ ॥ ৫১ ॥

ei śloka paḍi’ dum̐hāre kailā āliṅgana  
this  verse  reciting  both  He embraced  
kṛpāte dum̐hāra māthāya dharilā caraṇa  
in mercy  of both  on the heads  He placed  His feet

(51) Reciting this verse, He embraced them both, and in His mercy He placed His feet on their heads.

প্রভু-কৃপা পাঞা দুঁহে দুইহাত যুড়ি’ ৷  
দীন হঞা স্তুতি করে বিনয়-আচরি’ ॥ ৫২ ॥

prabhu-kṛpā pāñā dum̐he dui hāta yuḍi’  
the Lord’s mercy  receiving  both  two  hands  joining  
dīna hañā stuti kare vinaya-ācari’  
humble  becoming  prayers  offer  meekness  showing

(52) Having received the Lord’s mercy, the two of them joined their palms and, humble and meek, offered prayers:

নমো মহাবদান্যায় কৃষ্ণপ্রেমপ্রদায় তে ৷  
কৃষ্ণায় কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যনাম্নে গৌরত্বিষে নমঃ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

namo mahā-vadānyāya  
obeisances  to the most munificent  
kṛṣṇa-prema-pradāya te  
love for Kṛṣṇa  to the giver  to You  
kṛṣṇāya kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
to Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
nāmne gaura-tviṣe namaḥ  
named  of golden complexion  obeisances

(53) “Obeisances to You, the most munificent, the giver of love for Kṛṣṇa! Obeisances to Kṛṣṇa Himself, who bears the name Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and shines with a golden complexion.”

যোঽজ্ঞানমত্তং ভুবনং দয়ালু-  
রুল্লাঘয়ন্নপ্যকরোৎ প্রমত্তম্ ৷  
স্বপ্রেমসম্পৎসুধয়াদ্ভুতেঽহং  
শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যমমুং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৫৪ ॥

yo ’jñāna-mattaṁ bhuvanaṁ dayālur  
who  by ignorance maddened  the world  merciful  
ullāghayann apy akarot pramattam  
curing  yet  made  maddened  
sva-prema-sampat-sudhayādbhute ’haṁ  
with the nectar of the treasure of His own love  the doer of wondrous deeds  I  
śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanyam amuṁ prapadye  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Him  take refuge

(54) “I take refuge in Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the doer of wondrous deeds, who in His mercy cured the world maddened by ignorance and then made it mad with the nectar of His own treasure of love.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 1.2)

তবে মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে নিকটে বসাইলা ৷  
‘সনাতনের বার্ত্তা কহ’ — তাঁহারে পুছিলা ॥ ৫৫ ॥

tabe mahāprabhu tām̐re nikaṭe vasāilā  
then  Mahāprabhu  him  near  seated  
sanātanera vārtā kaha — tām̐hāre puchilā  
of Sanātana  the news  tell  him  He asked

(55) Then Mahāprabhu seated him close by and asked him: “Tell Me the news of Sanātana.”

রূপ কহেন, — তেঁহো বন্দী হয় রাজ-ঘরে ৷  
তুমি যদি উদ্ধার’, তবে হইবে উদ্ধারে ॥ ৫৬ ॥

rūpa kahena, — tem̐ho bandī haya rāja-ghare  
Rūpa  says  he  a prisoner  is  in the king’s house  
tumi yadi uddhāra’, tabe haibe uddhāre  
You  if  deliver  then  he will be  delivered

(56) Rūpa said: “He is a prisoner in the king’s house. If You deliver him, then he will be delivered.”

প্রভু কহে, — সনাতনের হঞাছে মোচন ৷  
অচিরাৎ আমা-সহ হইবে মিলন ॥ ৫৭ ॥

prabhu kahe, — sanātanera hañāche mocana  
the Lord  says  of Sanātana  has taken place  the release  
acirāt āmā-saha haibe milana  
soon  with Me  will be  the meeting

(57) The Lord said: “Sanātana has already been released, and he will soon meet Me.”

মধ্যাহ্ন করিতে বিপ্র প্রভুরে কহিলা ৷  
রূপ-গোসাঞি সে-দিবস তথাঞি রহিলা ॥ ৫৮ ॥

madhyāhna karite vipra prabhure kahilā  
the midday meal  to take  the brāhmaṇa  to the Lord  said  
rūpa-gosāñi se-divasa tathāñi rahilā  
Rūpa Gosvāmī  that day  there  stayed

(58) The brāhmaṇa asked the Lord to take His midday meal, and Rūpa Gosvāmī stayed there that day.

ভট্টাচার্য্য দুই ভাইয়ে নিমন্ত্রণ কৈল ৷  
প্রভুর শেষপ্রসাদ-পাত্র দুইভাই পাইল ॥ ৫৯ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya dui bhāiye nimantraṇa kaila  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  the two  brothers  invitation  made  
prabhura śeṣa prasāda-pātra duibhāi pāila  
of the Lord’s  remnants  of prasāda the plate  the two brothers  received

(59) The Bhaṭṭācārya invited the two brothers as well, and they received the plate with the remnants of the Lord’s prasāda.[^10]

ত্রিবেণী-উপর প্রভুর বাসা-ঘর স্থান ৷  
দুইভাই বাসা কৈল প্রভু-সন্নিধান ॥ ৬০ ॥

triveṇī-upara prabhura vāsā-ghara sthāna  
at Triveṇī  the Lord’s  of the lodging  the place  
duibhāi vāsā kaila prabhu-sannidhāna  
the two brothers  lodging  made  near the Lord

(60) The Lord’s lodging was at Triveṇī, and the two brothers took lodging near the Lord.

সে-কালে বল্লভ-ভট্ট রহে আড়াইল-গ্রামে ৷  
‘মহাপ্রভু আইলা’ শুনি’ আইল তাঁর স্থানে ॥ ৬১ ॥

se-kāle vallabha-bhaṭṭa rahe āḍāila-grāme  
at that time  Vallabha Bhaṭṭa  lived  in the village of Āḍāila  
‘mahāprabhu āilā’ śuni’ āila tām̐ra sthāne  
‘Mahāprabhu  has come’  hearing  came  to Him

(61) At that time Vallabha Bhaṭṭa was living in the village of Āḍāila. Hearing that Mahāprabhu had come, he came to Him.[^11]

তেঁহো দণ্ডবৎ কৈল, প্রভু কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ৷  
দুইজনে কৃষ্ণকথা হৈল কতক্ষণ ॥ ৬২ ॥

tem̐ho daṇḍavat kaila, prabhu kailā āliṅgana  
he  prostrated obeisances  made  the Lord  embraced  
dui jane kṛṣṇa-kathā haila kata-kṣaṇa  
between the two  talk of Kṛṣṇa  there was  for some time

(62) He fell before the Lord in obeisance, and the Lord embraced him. Then the two of them spoke about Kṛṣṇa for some time.

কৃষ্ণকথায় প্রভুর মহাপ্রেম উথলিল ৷  
ভট্টের সংকোচে প্রভু সম্বরণ কৈল ॥ ৬৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa-kathāya prabhura mahā-prema uthalila  
in the talk of Kṛṣṇa  the Lord’s  great love  surged up  
bhaṭṭera saṅkoce prabhu samvaraṇa kaila  
before Bhaṭṭa  out of reserve  the Lord  restraint  made

(63) As they spoke of Kṛṣṇa, great love surged up in the Lord, but out of reserve before Bhaṭṭa He held it back.[^12]

অন্তরে গর-গর প্রেম, নহে সম্বরণ ৷  
দেখি’ চমৎকার হৈল বল্লভ-ভট্টের মন ॥ ৬৪ ॥

antare gara-gara prema, nahe samvaraṇa  
within  surging  love  not  restrained  
dekhi’ camatkāra haila vallabha-bhaṭṭera mana  
seeing  amazed  became  Vallabha Bhaṭṭa’s  mind

(64) Yet within Him love was surging and could not be held back, and seeing this, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa was amazed.

তবে ভট্ট মহাপ্রভুরে নিমন্ত্রণ কৈলা ৷  
মহাপ্রভু দুইভাই তাঁহারে মিলাইলা ॥ ৬৫ ॥

tabe bhaṭṭa mahāprabhure nimantraṇa kailā  
then  Bhaṭṭa  Mahāprabhu  invitation  made  
mahāprabhu duibhāi tām̐hāre milāilā  
Mahāprabhu  the two brothers  with him  introduced

(65) Then Bhaṭṭa invited Mahāprabhu for a meal, and Mahāprabhu introduced the two brothers to him.

দুইভাই দূর হৈতে ভূমিতে পড়িয়া ৷  
ভট্টে দণ্ডবৎ কৈলা অতি দীন হঞা ॥ ৬৬ ॥

duibhāi dūra haite bhūmite paḍiyā  
the two brothers  from a distance  on the ground  falling  
bhaṭṭe daṇḍavat kailā ati dīna hañā  
to Bhaṭṭa  obeisances  made  very  humble  becoming

(66) From a distance the two brothers fell to the ground and, in great humility, offered obeisances to Bhaṭṭa.

ভট্ট মিলিবারে যায়, দুঁহে পলায় দূরে ৷  
‘অস্পৃশ্য পামর মুঞি, না ছুঁইহ মোরে’ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

bhaṭṭa milibāre yāya, dum̐he palāya dūre  
Bhaṭṭa  to meet  goes  both  draw back  far away  
aspṛśya pāmara muñi, nā chum̐iha more  
untouchable  sinful  I  not  touch  me

(67) When Bhaṭṭa went to embrace them, they both drew far back: “I am untouchable and sinful. Do not touch me!”

ভট্টের বিস্ময় হৈল, প্রভুর হর্ষ মন ৷  
ভট্টেরে কহিলা প্রভু তাঁর বিবরণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

bhaṭṭera vismaya haila, prabhura harṣa mana  
Bhaṭṭa’s  astonishment  arose  the Lord’s  joyful  mind  
bhaṭṭere kahilā prabhu tām̐ra vivaraṇa  
to Bhaṭṭa  told  the Lord  about him  an account

(68) Bhaṭṭa was astonished, and the Lord was pleased at heart. Then the Lord told Bhaṭṭa about Rūpa:[^13]

‘ইঁহো না স্পর্শিহ, ইঁহো জাতি অতি-হীন !  
বৈদিক, যাজ্ঞিক তুমি কুলীন প্রবীণ !’ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

im̐ho nā sparśiha, im̐ho jāti ati-hīna!  
him  not  touch  he  by birth  very low  
vaidika, yājñika tumi kulīna pravīṇa!  
a Vedic scholar  a performer of sacrifices  you  of noble family  learned

(69) “Do not touch him; he is of very low birth! You are a Vedic scholar and a performer of sacrifices, of noble family and learned!”

ইঁহার মুখে নিরন্তর কৃষ্ণনাম শুনি’ ৷  
ভট্ট কহে, প্রভুর কিছু ইঙ্গিত-ভঙ্গী জানি’ ॥ ৭০ ॥

im̐hāra mukhe nirantara kṛṣṇa-nāma śuni’  
from his  mouth  constantly  the name of Kṛṣṇa  hearing  
bhaṭṭa kahe, prabhura kichu iṅgita-bhaṅgī jāni’  
Bhaṭṭa  says  the Lord’s  something  of the hints  understanding

(70) Hearing the name of Kṛṣṇa constantly from Rūpa’s mouth, and grasping something of the Lord’s hint, Bhaṭṭa said:

‘দুঁহার মুখে কৃষ্ণনাম করিছে নর্ত্তন ৷  
এই দুই ‘অধম’ নহে, হয় ‘সর্ব্বোত্তম’ ॥ ৭১ ॥

dum̐hāra mukhe kṛṣṇa-nāma kariche nartana  
of both  in the mouths  the name of Kṛṣṇa  is doing  dance  
ei dui ‘adhama’ nahe, haya ‘sarvottama’  
these  two  ‘lowly’  are not  are  ‘the best of all’

(71) “The name of Kṛṣṇa is dancing in the mouths of both. These two are not ‘lowly’ — they are ‘the best of all.’”

অহো বত শ্বপচোঽতো গরীয়ান্  
যজ্জিহ্বাগ্রে বর্ত্ততে নাম তুভ্যম্ ৷  
তেপুস্তপস্তে জুহুবুঃ সস্নুরার্য্যা  
ব্রহ্মানূচুর্নাম গৃণন্তি যে তে ॥ ৭২ ॥

aho bata śva-paco ’to garīyān  
oh  how wonderful  a dog-eater  therefore  most worthy  
yaj-jihvāgre vartate nāma tubhyam  
on the tip of whose tongue  is present  the name  Your  
tepus tapas te juhuvuḥ sasnur āryā  
have performed  austerities  they  have offered sacrifice  have bathed (in all holy waters)  noble ones  
brahmānūcur nāma gṛṇanti ye te  
have recited the Vedas  the name  utter  who  Your

(72) “Oh, how wonderful! Even a dog-eater is worthy of the highest honour if Your name is on the tip of his tongue. Those who utter Your name have already performed austerities and offered sacrifices; they have bathed in all the holy waters, they are truly noble, and they have studied the Vedas.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.33.7)[^14]

শুনি’ মহাপ্রভু তাঁরে বহু প্রশংসিলা ৷  
প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা শ্লোক পড়িতে লাগিলা ॥ ৭৩ ॥

śuni’ mahāprabhu tām̐re bahu praśaṁsilā  
hearing  Mahāprabhu  him  much  praised  
premāviṣṭa hañā śloka paḍite lāgilā  
overwhelmed with love  becoming  verses  to recite  began

(73) Hearing this, Mahāprabhu praised him highly and, overwhelmed with love, began to recite verses:

শুচিঃ সদ্ভক্তিদীপ্তাগ্নিদগ্ধদুর্জাতিকল্মষঃ ৷  
শ্বপাকোঽপি বুধৈঃ শ্লাঘ্যো ন বেদজ্ঞোঽপি নাস্তিকঃ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

śuciḥ sad-bhakti-dīptāgni-  
pure  by the blazing fire of true devotion  
dagdha-durjāti-kalmaṣaḥ  
burned is the taint of low birth  
śva-pāko ’pi budhaiḥ ślāghyo  
a dog-cooker  even  by the wise  is praised  
na veda-jño ’pi nāstikaḥ  
not  a knower of the Vedas  even  an atheist

(74) “One in whom the blazing fire of true devotion has burned away the taint of low birth is pure; even if born a dog-cooker, he is praised by the wise. But an unbeliever is not praised, even if he knows the Vedas.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 3.11)[^15]

ভগবদ্ভক্তিহীনস্য জাতিঃ শাস্ত্রং জপস্তপঃ ৷  
অপ্রাণস্যৈব দেহস্য মণ্ডনং লোকরঞ্জনম্ ॥ ৭৫ ॥

bhagavad-bhakti-hīnasya  
of one devoid of devotion to the Lord  
jātiḥ śāstraṁ japas tapaḥ  
birth  scripture  japa  austerity  
aprāṇasyaiva dehasya  
of a lifeless  just  body  
maṇḍanaṁ loka-rañjanam  
decoration  pleasing the people

(75) “For one devoid of devotion to the Lord, high birth, learning in scripture, japa and austerity are like ornaments on a lifeless body: they only please the people.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 3.12)

প্রভুর প্রেমাবেশ, আর প্রভাব ভক্তিসার ৷  
সৌন্দর্য্যাদি দেখি’ ভট্টের হৈল চমৎকার ॥ ৭৬ ॥

prabhura premāveśa, āra prabhāva bhakti-sāra  
the Lord’s  absorption in love  and  power  the essence of devotion  
saundaryādi dekhi’ bhaṭṭera haila camatkāra  
beauty and the rest  seeing  Bhaṭṭa’s  there was  amazement

(76) Seeing the Lord’s absorption in love, His power — the very essence of devotion — His beauty and His other qualities, Bhaṭṭa was amazed.

সগণে প্রভুরে ভট্ট নৌকাতে চড়াঞা ৷  
ভিক্ষা দিতে নিজ-ঘরে চলিলা লঞা ॥ ৭৭ ॥

sagaṇe prabhure bhaṭṭa naukāte caḍāñā  
with His companions  the Lord  Bhaṭṭa  on a boat  seating  
bhikṣā dite nija-ghare calilā lañā  
a meal  to offer  to his own house  set out  taking

(77) Bhaṭṭa seated the Lord and His companions in a boat and set out with them for his own home to offer Him a meal.

যমুনার জল দেখি’ চিক্কণ শ্যামল ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে মহাপ্রভু হইলা বিহ্বল ॥ ৭৮ ॥

yamunāra jala dekhi’ cikkaṇa śyāmala  
of the Yamunā  the water  seeing  glossy  dark  
premāveśe mahāprabhu hailā vihvala  
in loving ecstasy  Mahāprabhu  became  overwhelmed

(78) Seeing the glossy dark water of the Yamunā, Mahāprabhu was overwhelmed with love.

হুঙ্কার করি’ যমুনার জলে দিলা ঝাঁপ ৷  
প্রভু দেখি’ সবার মনে হৈল ভয়-কাঁপ ॥ ৭৯ ॥

huṅkāra kari’ yamunāra jale dilā jhām̐pa  
a roar  making  into the Yamunā’s  water  He made  a leap  
prabhu dekhi’ sabāra mane haila bhaya-kām̐pa  
the Lord  seeing  in everyone’s  mind  arose  trembling fear

(79) With a loud roar He leaped into the water of the Yamunā. Seeing this, everyone trembled with fear.

আস্তে-ব্যস্তে সবে ধরি’ প্রভুরে উঠাইল ৷  
নৌকার উপরে প্রভু নাচিতে লাগিল ॥ ৮০ ॥

āste-vyaste sabe dhari’ prabhure uṭhāila  
in great haste  all  catching hold  the Lord  lifted out  
naukāra upare prabhu nācite lāgila  
on the boat  the Lord  to dance  began

(80) In great haste they all caught hold of the Lord and lifted Him out, and on the boat the Lord began to dance.

মহাপ্রভুর ভরে নৌকা করে টলমল ৷  
ডুবিতে লাগিল নৌকা, ঝলকে ভরে জল ॥ ৮১ ॥

mahāprabhura bhare naukā kare ṭalamala  
Mahāprabhu’s  under the weight  the boat  makes  rocking  
ḍubite lāgila naukā, jhalake bhare jala  
to sink  began  the boat  in gushes  fills  water

(81) Under Mahāprabhu’s weight the boat rocked to and fro; it began to sink, filling with water in gushes.

যদ্যপি ভট্টের আগে প্রভুর ধৈর্য্য হৈল মন ৷  
দুর্ব্বার উদ্ভট প্রেম নহে সম্বরণ ॥ ৮২ ॥

yadyapi bhaṭṭera āge prabhura dhairya haila mana  
although  Bhaṭṭa’s  before  the Lord’s  steady  became  mind  
durvāra udbhaṭa prema nahe samvaraṇa  
irresistible  mighty  love  is not  restrained

(82) Although in Bhaṭṭa’s presence the Lord had steadied His mind, His irresistible, mighty love could not be held back.

দেশ-পাত্র দেখি’ মহাপ্রভু ধৈর্য্য হইল ৷  
আড়াইলের ঘাটে নৌকা আসি’ উত্তরিল ॥ ৮৩ ॥

deśa-pātra dekhi’ mahāprabhu dhairya haila  
the place and the people  seeing  Mahāprabhu  composed  became  
āḍāilera ghāṭe naukā āsi’ uttarila  
at Āḍāila’s  ghāṭa  the boat  coming  landed

(83) Considering the place and the people, Mahāprabhu became composed, and the boat came to land at the ghāṭa of Āḍāila.[^16]

ভয়ে ভট্ট সঙ্গে রহে, মধ্যাহ্ন করাঞা ৷  
নিজ-গৃহে আনিলা প্রভুরে সঙ্গেতে লঞা ॥ ৮৪ ॥

bhaye bhaṭṭa saṅge rahe, madhyāhna karāñā  
out of fear  Bhaṭṭa  near  stays  the midday bath  having Him take  
nija-gṛhe ānilā prabhure saṅgete lañā  
to his own house  brought  the Lord  with him  taking

(84) Out of fear Bhaṭṭa stayed close by the Lord. After seeing to His midday bath, he took the Lord with him to his own house.

আনন্দিত হঞা ভট্ট দিল দিব্যাসন ৷  
আপনে করিল প্রভুর পাদপ্রক্ষালন ॥ ৮৫ ॥

ānandita hañā bhaṭṭa dila divyāsana  
joyful  becoming  Bhaṭṭa  gave  a fine seat  
āpane karila prabhura pāda-prakṣālana  
himself  performed  the Lord’s  washing of the feet

(85) Joyfully Bhaṭṭa offered Him a fine seat and himself washed the Lord’s feet.

সবংশে সেই জল মস্তকে ধরিল ৷  
নূতন কৌপীন-বহির্ব্বাস পরাইল ॥ ৮৬ ॥

savaṁśe sei jala mastake dharila  
with his whole family  that  water  on the head  placed  
nūtana kaupīna-bahirvāsa parāila  
new  kaupīna and outer cloth  dressed Him in

(86) With his whole family he placed that water on his head, and he dressed the Lord in a new kaupīna and outer cloth.

গন্ধ-পুষ্প-ধূপ-দীপে মহাপূজা কৈল ৷  
ভট্টাচার্য্যে মান্য করি’ পাক করাইল ॥ ৮৭ ॥

gandha-puṣpa-dhūpa-dīpe mahā-pūjā kaila  
with sandal paste, flowers, incense and lamps  great worship  made  
bhaṭṭācārye mānya kari’ pāka karāila  
to the Bhaṭṭācārya  respect  showing  cooking  had done

(87) He worshipped the Lord grandly with sandal paste, flowers, incense and lamps, and with due respect he had Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya do the cooking.[^17]

ভিক্ষা করাইল প্রভুরে সস্নেহ যতনে ৷  
রূপগোসাঞি-দুইভাইয়ে করাইল ভোজনে ॥ ৮৮ ॥

bhikṣā karāila prabhure sasneha yatane  
the meal  had taken  the Lord  with affection  with care  
rūpagosāñi-duibhāiye karāila bhojane  
Rūpa Gosvāmī and his brother  had  eat

(88) With affection and care he fed the Lord, and he also fed the two brothers, Rūpa Gosvāmī and Anupama.

ভট্টাচার্য্য শ্রীরূপে দেওয়াইল ‘অবশেষ’ ৷  
তবে সেই প্রসাদ কৃষ্ণদাস পাইল শেষ ॥ ৮৯ ॥

bhaṭṭācārya śrī-rūpe deoyāila ‘avaśeṣa’  
the Bhaṭṭācārya  to Śrī Rūpa  had given  the remnants  
tabe sei prasāda kṛṣṇadāsa pāila śeṣa  
then  that  prasāda  Kṛṣṇadāsa  received  the rest

(89) The Bhaṭṭācārya had the Lord’s remnants given to Śrī Rūpa, and Kṛṣṇadāsa then received what remained of that prasāda.

মুখবাস দিয়া প্রভুরে করাইল শয়ন ৷  
আপনে ভট্ট করেন প্রভুর পাদ-সম্বাহন ॥ ৯০ ॥

mukha-vāsa diyā prabhure karāila śayana  
mouth-freshening spices  giving  the Lord  had  lie down  
āpane bhaṭṭa karena prabhura pāda-samvāhana  
himself  Bhaṭṭa  does  the Lord’s  massaging of the feet

(90) He gave the Lord spices to freshen His mouth and had Him lie down to rest, and Bhaṭṭa himself massaged the Lord’s feet.

প্রভু পাঠাইল তাঁরে করিতে ভোজনে ৷  
ভোজন করি’ আইলা তেঁহো প্রভুর চরণে ॥ ৯১ ॥

prabhu pāṭhāila tām̐re karite bhojane  
the Lord  sent  him  to take  his meal  
bhojana kari’ āilā tem̐ho prabhura caraṇe  
the meal  having taken  came  he  to the Lord’s  feet

(91) The Lord sent him to take his meal, and after eating he came back to the Lord’s feet.

হেনকালে আইলা রঘুপতি উপাধ্যায় ৷  
তিরুহিতা পণ্ডিত, বড় বৈষ্ণব, মহাশয় ॥ ৯২ ॥

hena-kāle āilā raghupati upādhyāya  
at that time  came  Raghupati  Upādhyāya  
tiruhitā paṇḍita, baḍa vaiṣṇava, mahāśaya  
of Tirhut  a paṇḍita  a great  Vaiṣṇava  a noble soul

(92) At that time Raghupati Upādhyāya arrived, a paṇḍita from Tirhut, a great Vaiṣṇava and a noble soul.[^18]

আসি’ তেঁহো কৈল প্রভুর চরণ বন্দন ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণে মতি রহু’ বলি’ প্রভুর বচন ॥ ৯৩ ॥

āsi’ tem̐ho kaila prabhura caraṇa vandana  
coming  he  made  the Lord’s  feet  obeisance  
‘kṛṣṇe mati rahu’ bali’ prabhura vacana  
‘in Kṛṣṇa  the mind  may it rest’  saying  the Lord’s  words

(93) He came and bowed at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord said to him: “May your mind rest in Kṛṣṇa.”

শুনি’ আনন্দিত হৈল উপাধ্যায়ের মন ৷  
প্রভু তাঁরে কহিল, — ‘কহ কৃষ্ণের বর্ণন’ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

śuni’ ānandita haila upādhyāyera mana  
hearing  joyful  became  Upādhyāya’s  mind  
prabhu tām̐re kahila, — kaha kṛṣṇera varṇana  
the Lord  to him  said  tell  of Kṛṣṇa  a description

(94) Hearing this, Upādhyāya was delighted at heart. The Lord said to him: “Describe Kṛṣṇa to Me.”

নিজ-কৃত কৃষ্ণলীলা-শ্লোক-পড়িল ৷  
শুনি’ মহাপ্রভুর মহা-প্রেমাবেশ হৈল ॥ ৯৫ ॥

nija-kṛta kṛṣṇa-līlā-śloka-paḍila  
of his own composition  about Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  a verse  he recited  
śuni’ mahāprabhura mahā-premāveśa haila  
hearing  Mahāprabhu’s  great  absorption in love  came

(95) He recited a verse of his own about Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, and hearing it, Mahāprabhu was overwhelmed with great love.

শ্রুতিমপরে স্মৃতিমিতরে ভারতমন্যে ভজন্তু ভব-ভীতাঃ ৷  
অহমিহ নন্দং বন্দে যস্যালিন্দে পরং ব্রহ্ম ॥ ৯৬ ॥

śrutim apare smṛtim itare bhāratam anye bhajantu bhava-bhītāḥ  
the śruti  some  the smṛti  others  the Mahābhārata  still others  let worship  afraid of worldly existence  
aham iha nandaṁ vande yasyālinde paraṁ brahma  
I  here  Nanda  worship  in whose courtyard  the Supreme  Brahman

(96) “Let those who fear worldly existence worship — some the śruti, others the smṛti, still others the Mahābhārata. But here I bow to Nanda, in whose courtyard the Supreme Brahman plays.” (Padyāvalī, 126)

‘আগে কহ’ — প্রভু-বাক্যে উপাধ্যায় কহিল ৷  
রঘুপতি উপাধ্যায় নমস্কার কৈল ॥ ৯৭ ॥

‘āge kaha’ — prabhu-vākye upādhyāya kahila  
‘further  speak’  at the Lord’s word  Upādhyāya  spoke  
raghupati upādhyāya namaskāra kaila  
Raghupati  Upādhyāya  obeisance  made

(97) When the Lord said, “Speak on,” Upādhyāya spoke again; Raghupati Upādhyāya offered his obeisances.[^19]

কম্প্রতি কথয়িতুমীশে সম্প্রতি কো বা প্রতীতিমায়াতু ৷  
গোপতি-তনয়াকুঞ্জে গোপবধূটী-বিটং ব্রহ্ম ॥ ৯৮ ॥

kaṁ prati kathayitum īśe samprati ko vā pratītim āyātu  
to whom  addressing  to tell  am I able  now  who  or  belief  would come to  
go-pati-tanayā-kuñje gopa-vadhūṭī-viṭaṁ brahma  
in the grove of the daughter of the Sun  the lover of the young cowherd wives  Brahman

(98) “To whom can I tell it, and who would believe it now, that in the groves of the daughter of the Sun, the Yamunā, Brahman is the lover of the young cowherd girls?” (Padyāvalī, 98)

প্রভু কহেন, — কহ, তেঁহো পড়ে কৃষ্ণলীলা ৷  
প্রেমাবেশে প্রভুর দেহ-মন আলুয়াইলা ॥ ৯৯ ॥

prabhu kahena, — kaha, tem̐ho paḍe kṛṣṇa-līlā  
the Lord  says  speak  he  recites  Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  
premāveśe prabhura deha-mana āluyāilā  
in loving ecstasy  the Lord’s  body and mind  were thrown into turmoil

(99) The Lord said, “Go on,” and he recited verses on Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes. In the ecstasy of love the Lord’s body and mind were thrown into turmoil.

প্রেম দেখি’ উপাধ্যায়ের হৈল চমৎকার ৷  
‘মনুষ্য নহে, ইঁহো’ — ‘কৃষ্ণ’ করিল নির্দ্ধার ॥ ১০০ ॥

prema dekhi’ upādhyāyera haila camatkāra  
the love  seeing  Upādhyāya’s  there was  amazement  
‘manuṣya nahe, im̐ho’ — ‘kṛṣṇa’ karila nirddhāra  
‘a human being  is not  He’  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  he made  conclusion

(100) Seeing such love, Upādhyāya was amazed. “He is not a human being,” he concluded. “He is Kṛṣṇa.”

প্রভু কহে, — উপাধ্যায়, শ্রেষ্ঠ মান’ কায় ?  
‘শ্যামমেব পরং রূপং’ — কহে উপাধ্যায় ॥ ১০১ ॥

prabhu kahe, — upādhyāya, śreṣṭha māna’ kāya?  
the Lord  says  Upādhyāya  the best  consider  whom  
‘śyāmam eva paraṁ rūpaṁ’ — kahe upādhyāya  
‘the dark  indeed  supreme  form’  says  Upādhyāya

(101) The Lord said: “Upādhyāya, whom do you consider the best?” “*Śyāmam eva paraṁ rūpam* — the dark form is the supreme form,” said Upādhyāya.

শ্যাম-রূপের বাসস্থান শ্রেষ্ঠ মান’ কায় ?  
‘পুরী মধুপুরী বরা’ — কহে উপাধ্যায় ॥ ১০২ ॥

śyāma-rūpera vāsa-sthāna śreṣṭha māna’ kāya?  
of the dark form  the abode  the best  consider  which  
‘purī madhu-purī varā’ — kahe upādhyāya  
‘city  Madhupurī  the best’  says  Upādhyāya

(102) “Which abode of that dark form do you consider the best?” “*Purī madhu-purī varā* — Madhupurī is the best of cities,” said Upādhyāya.

বাল্য, পৌগণ্ড, কৈশোরে, শ্রেষ্ঠ মান’ কায় ?  
‘বয়ঃ কৈশোরকং ধ্যেয়ং’ — কহে উপাধ্যায় ॥ ১০৩ ॥

bālya, paugaṇḍa, kaiśore, śreṣṭha māna’ kāya?  
childhood  boyhood  youth (of these)  the best  consider  which  
‘vayaḥ kaiśorakaṁ dhyeyaṁ’ — kahe upādhyāya  
‘the age  of youth  to be meditated on’  says  Upādhyāya

(103) “Of childhood, boyhood and youth, which do you consider the best?” “*Vayaḥ kaiśorakaṁ dhyeyam* — youth is the age to meditate on,” said Upādhyāya.

রসগণ-মধ্যে তুমি শ্রেষ্ঠ মান’ কায় ?  
‘আদ্য এব পরো রসঃ’ — কহে উপাধ্যায় ॥ ১০৪ ॥

rasa-gaṇa-madhye tumi śreṣṭha māna’ kāya?  
of the rasas  among  you  the best  consider  which  
‘ādya eva paro rasaḥ’ — kahe upādhyāya  
‘the original  indeed  supreme  rasa’  says  Upādhyāya

(104) “Among all the rasas, which do you consider the best?” “*Ādya eva paro rasaḥ* — the original rasa is the highest,” said Upādhyāya.[^20]

প্রভু কহে, — ভাল তত্ত্ব শিখাইলা মোরে ৷  
এত বলি’ শ্লোক পড়ে গদগদ-স্বরে ॥ ১০৫ ॥

prabhu kahe, — bhāla tattva śikhāilā more  
the Lord  says  an excellent  truth  you have taught  Me  
eta bali’ śloka paḍe gadagada-svare  
so  saying  a verse  He recites  in a choked voice

(105) The Lord said: “You have taught Me an excellent truth.” Saying this, He recited the verse in a voice choked with emotion:

শ্যামমেব পরং রূপং পুরী মধুপুরী বরা ৷  
বয়ঃ কৈশোরকং ধ্যেয়মাদ্য এব পরো রসঃ ॥ ১০৬ ॥

śyāmam eva paraṁ rūpaṁ  
the dark  indeed  supreme  form  
purī madhu-purī varā  
city  Madhupurī  the best  
vayaḥ kaiśorakaṁ dhyeyam  
the age  of youth  to be meditated on  
ādya eva paro rasaḥ  
the original  indeed  supreme  rasa

(106) “The dark form is the supreme form; Madhupurī is the best of cities; youth (kaiśora) is the age to meditate on; and the original rasa, śṛṅgāra, is the highest rasa.” (Padyāvalī, 82)[^21]

প্রেমাবেশে প্রভু তাঁরে কৈলা আলিঙ্গন ৷  
প্রেমে মত্ত হঞা তেঁহো করেন নর্ত্তন ॥ ১০৭ ॥

premāveśe prabhu tām̐re kailā āliṅgana  
in loving ecstasy  the Lord  him  embraced  
preme matta hañā tem̐ho karena nartana  
with love  intoxicated  becoming  he  does  dancing

(107) In the ecstasy of love the Lord embraced him, and he, intoxicated with love, began to dance.

দেখি’ বল্লভ-ভট্ট মনে চমৎকার হৈল ৷  
দুই পুত্র আনি’ প্রভুর চরণে পাড়িল ॥ ১০৮ ॥

dekhi’ vallabha-bhaṭṭa mane camatkāra haila  
seeing  Vallabha Bhaṭṭa  in mind  amazement  there was  
dui putra āni’ prabhura caraṇe pāḍila  
two  sons  bringing  at the Lord’s  feet  he made fall

(108) Seeing this, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa was amazed. He brought his two sons and had them fall at the Lord’s feet.[^22]

প্রভু দেখিবারে গ্রামের সব-লোক আইল ৷  
প্রভু-দরশনে সব-লোক ‘কৃষ্ণভক্ত’ হইল ॥ ১০৯ ॥

prabhu dekhibāre grāmera saba-loka āila  
the Lord  to see  of the village  all the people  came  
prabhu-daraśane saba-loka ‘kṛṣṇa-bhakta’ haila  
on seeing the Lord  all the people  ‘devotees of Kṛṣṇa’  became

(109) All the people of the village came to see the Lord, and on seeing Him they all became devotees of Kṛṣṇa.

ব্রাহ্মণসকল করেন প্রভুর ‘নিমন্ত্রণ’ ৷  
বল্লভ-ভট্ট তাঁ সবারে করেন নিবারণ ॥ ১১০ ॥

brāhmaṇa-sakala karena prabhura ‘nimantraṇa’  
the brāhmaṇas  all  make  the Lord’s  ‘invitation’  
vallabha-bhaṭṭa tām̐ sabāre karena nivāraṇa  
Vallabha Bhaṭṭa  them  all  makes  restraint

(110) All the brāhmaṇas wanted to invite the Lord, but Vallabha Bhaṭṭa held them all back:

‘প্রেমোন্মাদে পড়ে গোসাঞি মধ্য-যমুনাতে ৷  
প্রয়াগে চালাইব, ইঁহা না দিব রহিতে ॥ ১১১ ॥

premonmāde paḍe gosāñi madhya-yamunāte  
in the madness of love  throws Himself  Gosāñi  into the middle of the Yamunā  
prayāge cālāiba, im̐hā nā diba rahite  
to Prayāga  I will take  here  not  I will let  stay

(111) “In the madness of love Gosāñi throws Himself into the middle of the Yamunā. I will take Him to Prayāga; I will not let Him stay here.

যাঁর ইচ্ছা, প্রয়াগে যাঞা করিবে নিমন্ত্রণ’ ৷  
এত বলি’ প্রভু লঞা করিল গমন ॥ ১১২ ॥

yām̐ra icchā, prayāge yāñā karibe nimantraṇa  
whose  wish  to Prayāga  going  will make  invitation  
eta bali’ prabhu lañā karila gamana  
so  saying  the Lord  taking  he made  departure

(112) Whoever wishes may go to Prayāga and invite Him there.” Saying this, he took the Lord and set out.

গঙ্গা-পথে মহাপ্রভুরে নৌকাতে বসাঞা ৷  
প্রয়াগে আইলা ভট্ট গোসাঞিরে লঞা ॥ ১১৩ ॥

gaṅgā-pathe mahāprabhure naukāte vasāñā  
by the Gaṅgā route  Mahāprabhu  in a boat  seating  
prayāge āilā bhaṭṭa gosāñire lañā  
to Prayāga  came  Bhaṭṭa  Gosāñi  taking

(113) Seating Mahāprabhu in a boat, Bhaṭṭa brought Gosāñi to Prayāga by way of the Gaṅgā.

লোক-ভিড়-ভয়ে প্রভু ‘দশাশ্বমেধে’ যাঞা ৷  
রূপ-গোসাঞিরে শিক্ষা করা’ন শক্তি সঞ্চারিয়া ॥ ১১৪ ॥

loka-bhiḍa-bhaye prabhu ‘daśāśvamedhe’ yāñā  
of the crowds of people  out of fear  the Lord  to ‘Daśāśvamedha’  going  
rūpa-gosāñire śikṣā karā’na śakti sañcāriyā  
Rūpa Gosvāmī  teaching  gives  power  infusing

(114) Fearing the crowds, the Lord went to Daśāśvamedha, and there He taught Rūpa Gosvāmī, infusing him with His power.

কৃষ্ণতত্ত্ব-ভক্তিতত্ত্ব, রসতত্ত্ব-প্রান্ত ৷  
সব শিখাইল প্রভু ভাগবত-সিদ্ধান্ত ॥ ১১৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-tattva-bhakti-tattva, rasa-tattva-prānta  
the truth of Kṛṣṇa  the truth of devotion  of the truth of rasa  the furthest limit  
saba śikhāila prabhu bhāgavata-siddhānta  
all  taught  the Lord  the conclusions of the Bhāgavata

(115) The Lord taught him everything: the truth of Kṛṣṇa, the truth of devotion and the truth of rasa to its furthest limit — the conclusions of the Bhāgavata.

রামানন্দ-পাশে যত সিদ্ধান্ত শুনিলা ৷  
রূপে কৃপা করি’ তাহা সব সঞ্চারিলা ॥ ১১৬ ॥

rāmānanda-pāśe yata siddhānta śunilā  
from Rāmānanda  all  conclusions  He had heard  
rūpe kṛpā kari’ tāhā saba sañcārilā  
into Rūpa  mercy  showing  them  all  He infused

(116) All the conclusions He had heard from Rāmānanda He mercifully infused into Rūpa.

শ্রীরূপ-হৃদয়ে প্রভু শক্তি সঞ্চারিলা ৷  
সর্ব্বতত্ত্ব নিরূপিয়া ‘প্রবীণ’ করিলা ॥ ১১৭ ॥

śrī-rūpa-hṛdaye prabhu śakti sañcārilā  
into Śrī Rūpa’s heart  the Lord  power  infused  
sarva-tattva nirūpiyā ‘pravīṇa’ karilā  
all truths  revealing  ‘expert’  He made

(117) The Lord infused His power into Śrī Rūpa’s heart and, revealing every truth to him, made him an expert.

শিবানন্দ-সেনের পুত্র ‘কবিকর্ণপূর’ ৷  
‘রূপের মিলন’ স্ব-গ্রন্থে লিখিয়াছেন প্রচুর ॥ ১১৮ ॥

śivānanda-senera putra ‘kavi-karṇapūra’  
of Śivānanda Sena  the son  ‘Kavi-karṇapūra’  
‘rūpera milana’ sva-granthe likhiyāchena pracura  
‘the meeting with Rūpa’  in his own book  has written  at length

(118) Kavi-karṇapūra, the son of Śivānanda Sena, has written at length in his book about the Lord’s meeting with Rūpa.

কালেন বৃন্দাবনকেলিবার্ত্তা  
লুপ্তেতি তাং খ্যাপয়িতুং বিশিষ্য ৷  
কৃপামৃতেনাভিষিষেচ দেব-  
স্তত্রৈব রূপঞ্চ সনাতনঞ্চ ॥ ১১৯ ॥

kālena vṛndāvana-keli-vārtā  
by time  of the pastimes of Vṛndāvana  the account  
lupteti tāṁ khyāpayituṁ viśiṣya  
lost  therefore  it  to make known  in a special way  
kṛpāmṛtenābhiṣiṣeca devas  
with the nectar of mercy  sprinkled  the Lord  
tatraiva rūpaṁ ca sanātanaṁ ca  
there indeed  Rūpa  and  Sanātana  and

(119) “Since over time the account of the pastimes of Vṛndāvana had been lost, the Lord, to make it known in a special way, sprinkled Rūpa and Sanātana there with the nectar of His mercy.” (Caitanya-candrodaya, 9.38)

যঃ প্রাগেব প্রিয়গুণগণৈর্গাঢ়বদ্ধোঽপি মুক্তো  
গেহাধ্যাসাদ্রস ইব পরো মূর্ত্ত এবাপ্যমূর্ত্তঃ ৷  
প্রেমালাপৈর্দৃঢ়তরপরিষ্বঙ্গরঙ্গৈঃ প্রয়াগে  
তং শ্রীরূপং সমমনুপমেনানুজগ্রাহ দেবঃ ॥ ১২০ ॥

yaḥ prāg eva priya-guṇa-gaṇair gāḍha-baddho ’pi mukto  
who  before  already  by the multitude of the Beloved’s qualities  tightly bound  though  freed  
gehādhyāsād rasa iva paro mūrta evāpy amūrtaḥ  
from attachment to home  rasa  like  supreme  embodied  indeed though  intangible  
premālāpair dṛḍhatara-pariṣvaṅga-raṅgaiḥ prayāge  
with loving talks  with the joys of the tightest embraces  in Prayāga  
taṁ śrī-rūpaṁ samam anupamenānujagrāha devaḥ  
him  Śrī Rūpa  together  with Anupama favored  the Lord

(120) “Though Śrī Rūpa had long been bound fast by the many qualities of his Beloved, he was free from attachment to home. The Lord is like the supreme rasa, which, though intangible, has taken on a visible form. In Prayāga He favoured Rūpa and his younger brother Anupama with loving talks and the joy of the tightest embraces.” (Caitanya-candrodaya, 9.29)[^23]

প্রিয়স্বরূপে দয়িতস্বরূপে  
প্রেমস্বরূপে সহজাভিরূপে ৷  
নিজানুরূপে প্রভুরেকরূপে  
ততান রূপে স্ববিলাসরূপে ॥ ১২১ ॥

priya-svarūpe dayita-svarūpe  
dear by nature  beloved by nature  
prema-svarūpe sahajābhirūpe  
the embodiment of love  naturally beautiful  
nijānurūpe prabhur eka-rūpe  
like Himself  the Lord  the principal form  
tatāna rūpe sva-vilāsa-rūpe  
spread  through Rūpa  the form of His own pastimes

(121) “Rūpa is dear to the Lord by his very nature and beloved by Him; he is love embodied, and his form is naturally beautiful. He is like the Lord Himself, His principal form and the form of His own pastimes. Through such a Rūpa the Lord spread the scriptures of bhakti-rasa.” (Caitanya-candrodaya, 9.30)[^24]

এইমত কর্ণপূর লিখে স্থানে-স্থানে ৷  
প্রভু কৃপা কৈলা যৈছে রূপ-সনাতনে ॥ ১২২ ॥

ei-mata karṇapūra likhe sthāne-sthāne  
in this way  Karṇapūra  writes  in various places  
prabhu kṛpā kailā yaiche rūpa-sanātane  
the Lord  mercy  showed  how  to Rūpa and Sanātana

(122) In this way Karṇapūra writes in various places how the Lord showed His mercy to Rūpa and Sanātana.

মহাপ্রভুর যত বড় বড় ভক্ত মাত্র ৷  
রূপ-সনাতন — সবার কৃপা-গৌরব-পাত্র ॥ ১২৩ ॥

mahāprabhura yata baḍa baḍa bhakta mātra  
Mahāprabhu’s  as many  great  great  devotees  all  
rūpa-sanātana — sabāra kṛpā-gaurava-pātra  
Rūpa and Sanātana  of all  of affection and esteem  the objects

(123) Rūpa and Sanātana were the objects of the affection and esteem of all Mahāprabhu’s great devotees.

কেহ যদি দেশে যায় দেখি’ বৃন্দাবন ৷  
তাঁরে প্রশ্ন করেন প্রভুর পারিষদগণ ॥ ১২৪ ॥

keha yadi deśe yāya dekhi’ vṛndāvana  
anyone  if  home  goes  having seen  Vṛndāvana  
tām̐re praśna karena prabhura pāriṣada-gaṇa  
to him  questions  put  the Lord’s  associates

(124) Whenever anyone returned home after seeing Vṛndāvana, the Lord’s associates would ask him:

‘‘কহ, — তাঁহা কৈছে রহে রূপ-সনাতন ?  
কৈছে রহে, কৈছে বৈরাগ্য, কৈছে ভোজন ? ॥ ১২৫ ॥

kaha, — tām̐hā kaiche rahe rūpa-sanātana?  
tell  there  how  live  Rūpa and Sanātana  
kaiche rahe, kaiche vairāgya, kaiche bhojana?  
how  they live  how  renunciation  how  eating

(125) “Tell us, how do Rūpa and Sanātana live there? How do they live, what is their renunciation like, what do they eat?

কৈছে অষ্টপ্রহর করেন শ্রীকৃষ্ণ-ভজন ?’’  
তবে প্রশংসিয়া কহে সেই ভক্তগণ ॥ ১২৬ ॥

kaiche aṣṭa-prahara karena śrī-kṛṣṇa-bhajana?  
how  all day and night  they do  worship of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  
tabe praśaṁsiyā kahe sei bhakta-gaṇa  
then  praising  say  those  devotees

(126) How do they worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa all day and night?” Then those devotees would answer with praise:

‘‘অনিকেত দুঁহে, বনে যত বৃক্ষগণ ৷  
এক এক বৃক্ষের তলে এক এক রাত্রি শয়ন ॥ ১২৭ ॥

aniketa dum̐he, vane yata vṛkṣa-gaṇa  
without dwelling  both  in the forest  as many  trees  
eka eka vṛkṣera tale eka eka rātri śayana  
one  one  tree  under  one  one  night  sleeping

(127) “They have no dwelling. Of all the trees in the forest, they sleep under each tree for one night only.

‘বিপ্রগৃহে’ স্থূলভিক্ষা, কাঁহা মাধুকরী ৷  
শুষ্ক রুটী-চানা চিবায় ভোগ পরিহরি’ ॥ ১২৮ ॥

‘vipra-gṛhe’ sthūla-bhikṣā, kām̐hā mādhu-karī  
‘in the homes of brāhmaṇas’  a full meal as alms  somewhere  mādhukarī  
śuṣka ruṭī-cānā civāya bhoga parihari’  
dry  bread and chickpeas  they chew  enjoyment  rejecting

(128) In the homes of brāhmaṇas they take a full meal as alms; elsewhere they gather mādhukarī. Rejecting all enjoyment, they chew dry bread and parched chickpeas.[^25]

[^4]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the kāyasthas were officials who served under Sanātana. Tradition says that after Sanātana his post went to his subordinate, Purandara Khān.

[^6]: According to the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*, the “elder brother” (in quotation marks in the edition) is the emperor himself: Husain Shāh regarded Sanātana as his “younger brother,” and in loving anger he says of himself that he only marches against other lands and, being a *yavana* by birth, hunts and kills many living beings and animals in the districts of Gauḍa.

[^7]: According to the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*, these words carry a hidden meaning: the king, who himself behaves like a robber, will reap the fruit of that, and the minister who has neglected his duties deserves the fruit of dismissal.

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: on him see Ādi 10.84.

[^9]: Reference as in the edition’s heading: *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa* 10.91, words of the *Itihāsa-samuccaya*.

[^10]: The Bhaṭṭācārya here is evidently Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya, the Lord’s companion on the way to Vṛndāvana, who is so named in verse 87 (edition heading: “Balabhadra cooks the meal”); the host is the brāhmaṇa from the South (44).

[^11]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Vallabha Bhaṭṭa was a Vaiṣṇava paṇḍita who later became an ācārya of the Viṣṇusvāmī sampradāya; he is known as Vallabhācārya. Āḍāila is the village of Aḍeli on the far bank of the Yamunā, about a mile from the confluence.

[^12]: In this passage *bhaṭṭa* is Vallabha Bhaṭṭa; the *Bhaṭṭācārya* of verses 87 and 89 is Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya, the Lord’s companion (*Anubhāṣya* on verse 87).

[^13]: Edition heading: “Seeing Vallabha, proud of his noble family and learning, as an outsider, the Lord gives him worldly honour — that is, He misleads him.”

[^14]: The same verse as Madhya 11.192; translation as there.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: the unbeliever (*nāstika*) is one who has turned away from the service of Kṛṣṇa; such a brāhmaṇa, even if he has mastered the Vedas, is to be avoided by one who seeks the highest goal.

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: “the place and the people” (*deśa-pātra*) — on a boat that was nearly sinking there was no room for dancing; moreover, beside a paṇḍita poor in prema such as Vallabha Dīkṣita, the sāttvika-bhāvas do not blossom. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: that place was almost devoid of prema, and beside Him was Vallabha Bhaṭṭa, much given to argument; seeing this, Mahāprabhu restrained Himself.

[^17]: The name of Balabhadra follows the edition’s heading: “Worship of the Lord; Balabhadra cooks the meal.”

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: *tiruhitā* means a native of Tirhut (today the districts of Saran, Champaran, Muzaffarpur and Darbhanga). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Tirhut is the land of Mithilā; several verses by Raghupati Upādhyāya are included in the *Padyāvalī*.

[^19]: The lines of this verse follow the order of the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 669): first “*‘āge kaha’ — prabhu-vākye upādhyāya kahila*,” then “*raghupati upādhyāya namaskāra kaila*.” Other editions print them in the reverse order.

[^20]: Edition heading: “The transcendental *śṛṅgāra-rasa* is the highest of the rasas and the best object of worship.”

[^21]: Edition heading: a verse of Mādhavendra Purī quoted in the *Padyāvalī* (82).

[^22]: *Anubhāṣya*: Vallabha’s two sons were Gopīnātha and Viṭṭhaleśvara; but the Lord came to Prayāga in 1434 or 1435 of the Śaka era, when Viṭṭhala had not yet been born. In the edition “two” (*dui*) is followed by an editorial “(?)”, and the heading speaks of one son.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Beloved is Gaura; Śrī Rūpa’s attachment to home was only enacted for the sake of the pastime.

[^24]: Edition heading: “*Śrī Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka*, Act 9, words of Sārvabhauma” (the verse number is not given).

[^25]: *Anubhāṣya*: *sthūla-bhikṣā* is alms after which one need beg for no more food; *mādhukarī* — as the bee gathers honey from many flowers, so the renunciant collects a little food in many places. Renouncing everything that excites the senses, they sustained the body for bhajana on dry bread and parched chickpeas.
