“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Antya-līlā  
Chapter 17  
**The Tortoise Form in Ecstasy, and the Ravings of Madness**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

লিখ্যতে শ্রীল-গৌরেন্দোরত্যদ্ভুতমলৌকিকম্ ৷  
যৈর্দৃষ্টং তন্মুখাচ্ছ্রুত্বা দিব্যোন্মাদ-বিচেষ্টিতম্ ॥ ১ ॥

likhyate śrīla-gaurendor  
is described  of Śrīla  Gauracandra  
aty-adbhutam alaukikam  
most  wonderful  unearthly  
yair dṛṣṭaṁ tan-mukhāc chrutvā  
by whom  seen  from their mouths  having heard  
divyonmāda-viceṣṭitam  
of divine madness  the conduct

(1) From the lips of those who saw them with their own eyes I have heard of the most wonderful, unearthly deeds of Śrīla Gauracandra in His divine madness, and now I describe them.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এইমত মহাপ্রভু রাত্রি-দিবসে ৷  
উন্মাদের চেষ্টা, প্রলাপ করে প্রেমাবেশে ॥ ৩ ॥

ei-mata mahāprabhu rātri-divase  
in this way  Mahāprabhu  night  and day  
unmādera ceṣṭā, pralāpa kare premāveśe  
of madness  the behaviour  ravings  makes  absorbed in love

(3) In this way, night and day, Mahāprabhu, absorbed in love, behaved like one mad and spoke in ravings.

একদিন প্রভু স্বরূপ-রামানন্দ-সঙ্গে ৷  
অর্দ্ধরাত্রি গোঙাইলা কৃষ্ণকথা-রঙ্গে ॥ ৪ ॥

eka-dina prabhu svarūpa-rāmānanda-saṅge  
one  day  the Lord  Svarūpa  Rāmānanda  with  
ardha-rātri goṅāilā kṛṣṇa-kathā-raṅge  
half  the night  passed  of Kṛṣṇa  talks  in the delight

(4) One day the Lord passed half the night with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, delighting in talks about Kṛṣṇa.

যবে যেই ভাব প্রভু করয়ে উদয় ৷  
ভাবানুরূপ গীত গায় স্বরূপ-মহাশয় ॥ ৫ ॥

yabe yei bhāva prabhu karaye udaya  
when  whatever  feeling  the Lord  makes  arise  
bhāvānurūpa gīta gāya svarūpa-mahāśaya  
to the feeling  suited  a song  sings  Svarūpa  Mahāśaya

(5) Whatever feeling arose in the Lord, Svarūpa Mahāśaya would sing a song to suit it.

বিদ্যাপতি, চণ্ডীদাস, শ্রীগীতগোবিন্দ ৷  
ভাবানুরূপ শ্লোক পড়েন রায়-রামানন্দ ॥ ৬ ॥

vidyāpati, caṇḍīdāsa, śrī-gīta-govinda  
Vidyāpati  Caṇḍīdāsa  the Śrī Gīta-govinda  
bhāvānurūpa śloka paḍena rāya-rāmānanda  
to the feeling  suited  verses  recites  Rāya  Rāmānanda

(6) And Rāya Rāmānanda would recite verses suited to His feelings from Vidyāpati, Caṇḍīdāsa and the *Śrī Gīta-govinda*.

মধ্যে মধ্যে আপনে প্রভু শ্লোক পড়িয়া ৷  
শ্লোকের অর্থ করেন প্রভু বিলাপ করিয়া ॥ ৭ ॥

madhye madhye āpane prabhu śloka paḍiyā  
now and  then  Himself  the Lord  a verse  reciting  
ślokera artha karena prabhu vilāpa kariyā  
of the verse  the meaning  makes  the Lord  lament  making

(7) Now and then the Lord would Himself recite a verse and, lamenting, explain its meaning.

এইমতে নানাভাবে অর্দ্ধরাত্রি হৈল ৷  
গোসাঞিরে শয়ন করাই’ দুঁহে ঘরে গেল ॥ ৮ ॥

ei-mate nānā-bhāve ardha-rātri haila  
in this way  in various  feelings  half  the night  passed  
gosāñire śayana karāi’ dum̐he ghare gela  
the Lord  to bed  having put  the two  home  went

(8) So, amid many feelings, half the night went by. Then the two put the Lord to bed and went home.

গম্ভীরার দ্বারে গোবিন্দ করিলা শয়ন ৷  
অর্দ্ধরাত্রিতে প্রভু করেন উচ্চসঙ্কীর্ত্তন ॥ ৯ ॥

gambhīrāra dvāre govinda karilā śayana  
of the Gambhīrā  at the door  Govinda  made  his bed  
ardha-rātrite prabhu karena ucca-saṅkīrtana  
at midnight  the Lord  makes  loud  saṅkīrtana

(9) Govinda lay down to sleep at the door of the Gambhīrā, and at midnight the Lord began to chant the holy names aloud.

আচম্বিতে শুনেন প্রভু কৃষ্ণবেণু-গান ৷  
ভাবাবেশে প্রভু তাহাঁ করিলা প্রয়াণ ॥ ১০ ॥

ācambite śunena prabhu kṛṣṇa-veṇu-gāna  
suddenly  hears  the Lord  of Kṛṣṇa’s  flute  the song  
bhāvāveśe prabhu tāhām̐ karilā prayāṇa  
absorbed in feeling  the Lord  there  made  departure

(10) Suddenly the Lord heard the song of Kṛṣṇa’s flute and, absorbed in feeling, set off towards it.

তিনদ্বারে কপাট ঐছে আছে ত’ লাগিয়া ৷  
ভাবাবেশে প্রভু গেলা বাহির হঞা ॥ ১১ ॥

tina-dvāre kapāṭa aiche āche ta’ lāgiyā  
in the three  doors  the panels  so  are  indeed  fastened  
bhāvāveśe prabhu gelā bāhira hañā  
absorbed in feeling  the Lord  went  outside  becoming

(11) The three doors stayed shut as they were, yet the Lord, lost in that feeling, passed outside.

সিংহদ্বার-দক্ষিণে আছে তৈলঙ্গী-গাভীগণ ৷  
তাঁহা যাই’ পড়িলা প্রভু হঞা অচেতন ॥ ১২ ॥

siṁha-dvāra-dakṣiṇe āche tailaṅgī-gābhī-gaṇa  
of the Siṁha-dvāra  to the south  are  Tailaṅga  cows  the herd  
tām̐hā yāi’ paḍilā prabhu hañā acetana  
there  going  fell  the Lord  becoming  unconscious

(12) To the south of the Siṁha-dvāra there was a herd of cows from Tailaṅga. The Lord went there and fell down unconscious.

এথা গোবিন্দ মহাপ্রভুর শব্দ না পাঞা ৷  
স্বরূপেরে বোলাইল কপাট খুলিয়া ॥ ১৩ ॥

ethā govinda mahāprabhura śabda nā pāñā  
here  Govinda  of Mahāprabhu  the sound  not  getting  
svarūpere bolāila kapāṭa khuliyā  
Svarūpa  called  the doors  opening

(13) Meanwhile Govinda, no longer hearing any sound from Mahāprabhu, opened the doors and called Svarūpa.

তবে স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি সঙ্গে লঞা ভক্তগণ ৷  
দেউটি জ্বালিয়া করেন প্রভুর অন্বেষণ ॥ ১৪ ॥

tabe svarūpa-gosāñi saṅge lañā bhakta-gaṇa  
then  Svarūpa  Gosāñi  with  taking  the devotees  
deuṭi jvāliyā karena prabhura anveṣaṇa  
torches  lighting  make  for the Lord  a search

(14) Then Svarūpa Gosāñi took the devotees with him, and they lit torches and searched for the Lord.

ইতি-উতি অন্বেষিয়া সিংহদ্বারে গেলা ৷  
গাভীগণ-মধ্যে যাই’ প্রভুরে পাইলা ॥ ১৫ ॥

iti-uti anveṣiyā siṁha-dvāre gelā  
here and  there  searching  to the Siṁha-dvāra  went  
gābhī-gaṇa-madhye yāi’ prabhure pāilā  
of the cows  among  going  the Lord  found

(15) After searching here and there, they went to the Siṁha-dvāra and found the Lord among the cows.

পেটের ভিতর হস্ত-পদ — কূর্ম্মের আকার ৷  
মুখে ফেন, পুলকাঙ্গ, নেত্রে অশ্রুধার ॥ ১৬ ॥

peṭera bhitara hasta-pada — kūrmera ākāra  
of the belly  inside  hands  and feet  of a tortoise  the shape  
mukhe phena, pulakāṅga, netre aśru-dhāra  
in the mouth  foam  thrilled limbs  in the eyes  streams of tears

(16) His hands and feet had drawn inside His body, and He had the shape of a tortoise. There was foam at His mouth, the hair of His body stood on end, and tears streamed from His eyes.

অচেতন পড়িয়াছেন, — যেন কুষ্মাণ্ড-ফল ৷  
বাহিরে জড়িমা, অন্তরে আনন্দ-বিহ্বল ॥ ১৭ ॥

acetana paḍiyāchena, — yena kuṣmāṇḍa-phala  
unconscious  is lying  like  a pumpkin  fruit  
bāhire jaḍimā, antare ānanda-vihvala  
outside  stupor  within  with bliss  overwhelmed

(17) He lay unconscious, like a great pumpkin: outwardly inert, but within overwhelmed with bliss.

গাভী সব চৌদিকে শুঁকে প্রভুর শ্রীঅঙ্গ ৷  
দূর কৈলে নাহি ছাড়ে প্রভুর শ্রীঅঙ্গ-সঙ্গ ॥ ১৮ ॥

gābhī saba caudike śum̐ke prabhura śrī-aṅga  
cows  all  on every side  sniff  the Lord’s  holy  body  
dūra kaile nāhi chāḍe prabhura śrī-aṅga-saṅga  
away  driven  not  leave  the Lord’s  holy  body’s  company

(18) On every side the cows were sniffing the Lord’s holy body; even when driven away, they would not leave His side.

অনেক করিলা যত্ন, না হয় চেতন ৷  
প্রভুরে উঠাঞা ঘরে আনিলা ভক্তগণ ॥ ১৯ ॥

aneka karilā yatna, nā haya cetana  
much  made  effort  not  becomes  conscious  
prabhure uṭhāñā ghare ānilā bhakta-gaṇa  
the Lord  lifting  home  brought  the devotees

(19) The devotees tried hard to revive Him, but He did not regain consciousness. Then they lifted the Lord and carried Him home.

উচ্চ করি’ শ্রবণে করে নামসঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
অনেকক্ষণে মহাপ্রভু পাইলা চেতন ॥ ২০ ॥

ucca kari’ śravaṇe kare nāma-saṅkīrtana  
loud  making  in the ears  make  of the name  saṅkīrtana  
aneka-kṣaṇe mahāprabhu pāilā cetana  
after a long  while  Mahāprabhu  regained  consciousness

(20) They sang the holy name loudly into His ears, and after a long while Mahāprabhu regained consciousness.[^2]

চেতন হইলে হস্ত-পাদ বাহিরে আইল ৷  
পূর্ব্ববৎ যথাযোগ্য শরীর হইল ॥ ২১ ॥

cetana haile hasta-pāda bāhire āila  
conscious  when He became  hands  and feet  outside  came  
pūrvavat yathā-yogya śarīra haila  
as before  in proper proportion  the body  became

(21) As He came to Himself, His hands and feet emerged again, and His body returned to its proper form, as before.

উঠিয়া বসিলেন প্রভু, চাহেন ইতি-উতি ৷  
স্বরূপে কহেন, — তুমি আমা আনিলা কতি ? ॥ ২২ ॥

uṭhiyā vasilena prabhu, cāhena iti-uti  
rising  sat  the Lord  looks  here and there  
svarūpe kahena, — tumi āmā ānilā kati?  
to Svarūpa  says  you  Me  have brought  where

(22) The Lord sat up and looked about Him. He said to Svarūpa, “Where have you brought Me?

বেণু-শব্দ শুনি’ আমি গেলাঙ বৃন্দাবন ৷  
দেখি, — গোষ্ঠে বেণু বাজায় ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ২৩ ॥

veṇu-śabda śuni’ āmi gelāṅa vṛndāvana  
the flute’s  sound  hearing  I  went  to Vṛndāvana  
dekhi, — goṣṭhe veṇu bājāya vrajendra-nandana  
I see  in the pasture  the flute  plays  of the king of Vraja  the son

(23) Hearing the sound of the flute, I went to Vṛndāvana, and I saw the son of the king of Vraja playing His flute in the pasture.

সঙ্কেত-বেণু-নাদে রাধা গেলা কুঞ্জঘরে ৷  
কুঞ্জেরে চলিলা কৃষ্ণ ক্রীড়া করিবারে ॥ ২৪ ॥

saṅketa-veṇu-nāde rādhā gelā kuñja-ghare  
of the signal  of the flute  at the call  Rādhā  went  to the bower  house  
kuñjere calilā kṛṣṇa krīḍā karibāre  
to the bower  went  Kṛṣṇa  pastimes  to make

(24) At the signal call of His flute Rādhā went to the bower, and Kṛṣṇa too went there for His pastimes.

তাঁর পাছে পাছে আমি করিনু গমন ৷  
তাঁর ভূষণ-ধ্বনিতে আমার হরিল শ্রবণ ॥ ২৫ ॥

tām̐ra pāche pāche āmi karinu gamana  
Him  behind  behind  I  made  going  
tām̐ra bhūṣaṇa-dhvanite āmāra harila śravaṇa  
His  ornaments’  by the jingling  My  was stolen  hearing

(25) I followed close behind Him, and the jingling of His ornaments captivated My ears.

গোপীগণ-সহ বিহার, হাস, পরিহাস ৷  
কণ্ঠধ্বনি-উক্তি শুনি’ মোর কর্ণোল্লাস ॥ ২৬ ॥

gopī-gaṇa-saha vihāra, hāsa, parihāsa  
the gopīs  with  pastimes  laughter  jesting  
kaṇṭha-dhvani-ukti śuni’ mora karṇollāsa  
voice’s  sound  words  hearing  My  ears’ joy

(26) Kṛṣṇa was enjoying His pastimes with the *gopīs*, laughing and jesting, and My ears rejoiced to hear His voice and His words.

হেনকালে তুমি সব কোলাহল করি’ ৷  
আমা ইঁহা লঞা আইলা বলাৎকার করি’ ॥ ২৭ ॥

hena-kāle tumi saba kolāhala kari’  
at that time  you  all  an uproar  making  
āmā im̐hā lañā āilā balātkāra kari’  
Me  here  taking  brought  force  using

(27) Just then all of you raised an uproar and brought Me here by force.

শুনিতে না পাইনু সেই অমৃতসম বাণী ৷  
শুনিতে না পাইনু ভূষণ-মুরলীর ধ্বনি ॥ ২৮ ॥

śunite nā pāinu sei amṛta-sama vāṇī  
to hear  not  did I get  those  nectar  like  words  
śunite nā pāinu bhūṣaṇa-muralīra dhvani  
to hear  not  did I get  of the ornaments  of the flute  the sound

(28) I could no longer hear those words, sweet as nectar; I could no longer hear the jingling of His ornaments and the sound of His flute.”

ভাবাবেশে স্বরূপে কহেন গদ্গদ-বাণী ৷  
‘কর্ণ তৃষ্ণায় মরি, পড় ‘রসামৃত’ শুনি’ ॥ ২৯ ॥

bhāvāveśe svarūpe kahena gadgada-vāṇī  
absorbed in feeling  to Svarūpa  says  choked  words  
‘karṇa tṛṣṇāya mari, paḍa ‘rasāmṛta’ śuni’  
My ears  with thirst  I die  recite  nectar of rasa  I shall hear

(29) Absorbed in feeling, the Lord said to Svarūpa in a choked voice, “My ears are dying of thirst. Recite something full of the nectar of *rasa* — let Me hear it.”

স্বরূপ-গোসাঞি প্রভুর ভাব জানিয়া ৷  
ভাগবতের শ্লোক পড়ে মধুর করিয়া ॥ ৩০ ॥

svarūpa-gosāñi prabhura bhāva jāniyā  
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  of the Lord  the mood  knowing  
bhāgavatera śloka paḍe madhura kariyā  
of the Bhāgavata  a verse  recites  sweet  making

(30) Svarūpa Gosāñi, knowing the Lord’s mood, sweetly recited a verse from the *Bhāgavata*:

কাস্ত্র্যঙ্গ তে কলপদামৃতবেণুগীত-  
সম্মোহিতার্য্যচরিতান্ন চলেৎ ত্রিলোক্যাম্ ৷  
ত্রৈলোক্য-সৌভগমিদঞ্চ নিরীক্ষ্য রূপং  
যদ্গোদ্বিজদ্রুমমৃগাঃ পুলকান্যবিভ্রন্ ॥ ৩১ ॥

kā stry aṅga te kala-padāmṛta-veṇu-gīta-  
what  woman  O dear one  Your  of sweet words  by the nectar  of the flute  by the song  
sammohitārya-caritān na calet tri-lokyām  
enchanted  from the conduct of the noble  not  would stray  in the three worlds  
trailokya-saubhagam idaṁ ca nirīkṣya rūpaṁ  
of the three worlds  the beauty  this  and  having seen  form  
yad go-dvija-druma-mṛgāḥ pulakāny abibhran  
which  cows  birds  trees  deer  thrills of rapture  bore

(31) “O Kṛṣṇa, what woman in the three worlds, enchanted by the song of Your flute with its sweet, nectarean notes, would not stray from the conduct of the noble? Seeing this form of Yours, the beauty of the three worlds, even cows, birds, trees and deer bristle with rapture.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.40)[^3]

শুনি’ প্রভু গোপীভাবে আবিষ্ট হইলা ৷  
ভাগবতের শ্লোকার্থ করিতে লাগিলা ॥ ৩২ ॥

śuni’ prabhu gopī-bhāve āviṣṭa hailā  
hearing  the Lord  in the mood of the gopīs  absorbed  became  
bhāgavatera ślokārtha karite lāgilā  
of the Bhāgavata  the verse’s meaning  to make  began

(32) Hearing this, the Lord became absorbed in the mood of the *gopīs* and began to explain the meaning of the verse:

হৈল গোপী-ভাবাবেশ, কৈল রাসে পরবেশ,  
কৃষ্ণের শুনি’ উপেক্ষা-বচন ৷  
কৃষ্ণের মুখ-হাস্য-বাণী, ত্যাগে তাহা সত্য মানি’,  
রোষে কৃষ্ণে দেন ওলাহন ॥ ৩৩ ॥

haila gopī-bhāvāveśa,  
there was  in the gopīs’  mood  absorption  
kaila rāse paraveśa,  
made  into the rāsa  entry  
kṛṣṇera śuni’ upekṣā-vacana  
Kṛṣṇa’s  hearing  of disregard  words  
kṛṣṇera mukha-hāsya-vāṇī,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  mouth’s  smiling  words  
tyāge tāhā satya māni’,  
as rejection  them  true  taking  
roṣe kṛṣṇe dena olāhana  
in anger  to Kṛṣṇa  gives  reproach

(33) Absorbed in the mood of the *gopīs*, He entered the *rāsa* dance. There He heard Kṛṣṇa’s words of indifference, took those words, spoken with a smile, as a true rejection, and reproached Kṛṣṇa in anger:[^4]

নাগর, কহ, তুমি করিয়া নিশ্চয় ৷  
এই ত্রিজগৎ ভরি’, আছে যত যোগ্যা নারী,  
তোমার বেণু কাঁহা না আকর্ষয়? ॥ ৩৪ ॥ধ্রু॥

nāgara, kaha, tumi kariyā niścaya  
O lover  say  You  making  certain  
ei trijagat bhari’,  
these  three worlds  filling  
āche yata yogyā nārī,  
there are  as many  worthy  women  
tomāra veṇu kām̐hā nā ākarṣaya?  
Your  flute  whom  not  attracts

(34) “O Beloved, tell us truly: of all the worthy women who fill the three worlds, which is not drawn by Your flute?

কৈলা জগতে বেণুধ্বনি, সিদ্ধমন্ত্রা যোগিনী,  
দূতী হঞা মোহে নারী-মন ৷  
মহোৎকণ্ঠা বাড়াঞা, আর্য্যপথ ছাড়াঞা,  
আনি’ তোমায় করে সমর্পণ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

kailā jagate veṇu-dhvani,  
You made  in the world  the flute’s  sound  
siddha-mantrā yoginī,  
of perfected mantras  a yoginī  
dūtī hañā mohe nārī-mana  
a go-between  becoming  enchants  women’s  minds  
mahotkaṇṭhā bāḍāñā,  
great  longing  increasing  
ārya-patha chāḍāñā,  
the noble  path  making them leave  
āni’ tomāya kare samarpaṇa  
bringing  to You  makes  offering

(35) You sent the sound of Your flute into the world, and like a *yoginī* perfect in her mantras, it becomes a go-between and enchants the minds of women. It swells their longing, makes them forsake the path of the noble, and delivers them to You.

ধর্ম্ম ছাড়ায় বেণুদ্বারে, হানে কটাক্ষ-কামশরে,  
লজ্জা, ভয়, সকল ছাড়ায় ৷  
এবে আমায় করি’ রোষ, কহি’ পতি ত্যাগে ‘দোষ’,  
ধার্ম্মিক হঞা ধর্ম্ম শিখাও ॥ ৩৬ ॥

dharma chāḍāya veṇu-dvāre,  
dharma  You make them leave  by the flute  
hāne kaṭākṣa-kāma-śare,  
You strike  of glances  of Kāma  with the arrows  
lajjā, bhaya, sakala chāḍāya  
shame  fear  all  You make them leave  
ebe āmāya kari’ roṣa,  
now  at us  making  anger  
kahi’ pati tyāge ‘doṣa’,  
saying  husbands  in leaving  a fault  
dhārmika hañā dharma śikhāo  
righteous  becoming  dharma  You teach

(36) With Your flute You make us abandon *dharma*; You strike us with the arrows of Kāma, Your sidelong glances, and make us cast off shame and fear and everything else. And now You are angry with us, You say it is wrong to leave our husbands, and, like a man of virtue, You teach us *dharma*![^5]

অন্যকথা, অন্যমন, বাহিরে অন্য আচরণ,  
এই সব শঠ-পরিপাটী ৷  
তুমি জান পরিহাস, হয় নারীর সর্ব্বনাশ,  
ছাড় এই সব কুটীনাটী ॥ ৩৭ ॥

anya-kathā, anya-mana,  
other  words  other  mind  
bāhire anya ācaraṇa,  
outwardly  other  conduct  
ei saba śaṭha-paripāṭī  
this  all  of a cheat  the artifices  
tumi jāna parihāsa,  
You  know  jest  
haya nārīra sarva-nāśa,  
is  women’s  utter ruin  
chāḍa ei saba kuṭīnāṭī  
give up  these  all  tricks

(37) Your words say one thing, Your mind another, and outwardly You act in yet another way — all these are the artifices of a cheat. For You it is a jest, but for women it is utter ruin. Give up all these tricks!

বেণুনাদ অমৃত-ঘোলে, অমৃত-সমান মিঠা বোলে,  
অমৃত-সমান ভূষণ-শিঞ্জিত ৷  
তিন অমৃতে হরে কাণ, হরে মন, হরে প্রাণ,  
কেমনে নারী ধরিবেক চিত ? ॥ ৩৮ ॥

veṇu-nāda amṛta-ghole,  
the flute’s  sound  nectar  outdoes  
amṛta-samāna miṭhā bole,  
nectar  equal  sweet  words  
amṛta-samāna bhūṣaṇa-śiñjita  
nectar  equal  the ornaments’  jingling  
tina amṛte hare kāṇa,  
by the three  nectars  steals  the ears  
hare mana, hare prāṇa,  
steals  the mind  steals  the life  
kemane nārī dharibeka cita?  
how  a woman  will hold  her heart

(38) The sound of Your flute outdoes nectar; Your sweet words are like nectar, and the jingling of Your ornaments is like nectar too. With these three nectars You steal our ears, You steal our minds, You steal our very life. How can a woman keep hold of her heart?”[^6]

[^1]: *Anubhāṣya*: those who saw it were the most fortunate intimate devotees headed by Dāmodara and Raghunātha; “wonderful” — such as had never been heard of, “unearthly” — such as had never been seen. Heading in the edition: the transcendental pastimes of Gaura, heard from the lips of the guru by the path of *śrauta* (hearing).

[^2]: Heading in the edition: by the loud *saṅkīrtana* the Lord regains consciousness — a half-outward consciousness (*ardha-bāhya*).

[^3]: The same verse as Madhya 24.52; translation as there. The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Madhya 24.52. Heading in the edition: the sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s flute enchants and draws all His servants, whatever they may be.

[^4]: Heading in the edition: a gopī describes her feelings for Kṛṣṇa (*citra-jalpa*). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in the *rāsa-līlā* the gopīs heard Kṛṣṇa’s indifferent words and took it as true that He had rejected them.

[^5]: Heading in the edition: “The transcendental young Madana, or Kāmadeva Anaṅga.”

[^6]: *Anubhāṣya*: *ghole* — from the colloquial *ghola khāoyāya*, “makes [it] drink buttermilk”, that is, outdoes, overpowers nectar. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* understands it otherwise: the sound of the flute is nectarean buttermilk. In other editions (variant readings noted in the *Anubhāṣya*) — *role*, “with the sound”, and *ugāre*, “pours forth”.
