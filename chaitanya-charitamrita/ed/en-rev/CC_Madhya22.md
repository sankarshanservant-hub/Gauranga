“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 22  
**The Discourse on the Truth of Bhakti as the Abhidheya**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যদেবং তং করুণার্ণবম্ ৷  
কলাবপ্যতিগূঢ়েয়ং ভক্তির্যেন প্রকাশিতা ॥ ১ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
devaṁ taṁ karuṇārṇavam  
the Lord  Him  the ocean of mercy  
kalāv apy ati-gūḍheyaṁ  
in Kali  even  this most hidden  
bhaktir yena prakāśitā  
bhakti  by whom  was revealed

(1) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, the ocean of mercy, by whom this most hidden bhakti was revealed even in the age of Kali.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

এই ত’ কহিলুঁ সম্বন্ধ-তত্ত্বের বিচার ৷  
বেদশাস্ত্রে উপদেশে, কৃষ্ণ — এক সার ॥ ৩ ॥

ei ta’ kahilum̐ sambandha-tattvera vicāra  
thus  indeed  I have told  of sambandha-tattva  the analysis  
veda-śāstre upadeśe, kṛṣṇa — eka sāra  
of the Vedas and śāstras  in the teaching  Kṛṣṇa  the one  essence

(3) “Thus I have explained to you the truth of *sambandha*. In the teaching of the Vedas and the śāstras, Kṛṣṇa is the one essence.[^3]

এবে কহি, শুন, অভিধেয়-লক্ষণ ৷  
যাহা হৈতে পাই — কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণপ্রেমধন ॥ ৪ ॥

ebe kahi, śuna, abhidheya-lakṣaṇa  
now  I speak  listen  of abhidheya  the characteristics  
yāhā haite pāi — kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana  
which  through  one attains  Kṛṣṇa  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa

(4) Now listen as I describe *abhidheya*, the means by which one attains Kṛṣṇa and the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণভক্তি — অভিধেয়, সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
অতএব মুনিগণ করিয়াছে নিশ্চয় ॥ ৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhakti — abhidheya, sarva-śāstre kaya  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is the abhidheya  all the scriptures  declare  
ataeva muni-gaṇa kariyāche niścaya  
therefore  the sages  have made  the conclusion

(5) All the scriptures declare that devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the *abhidheya*, and so the sages have concluded.

শ্রুতির্মাতা পৃষ্টা দিশতি ভবদারাধনবিধিং  
যথা মাতুর্ব্বাণী স্মৃতিরপি তথা বক্তি ভগিনী ৷  
পুরাণাদ্যা যে বা সহজনিবহাস্তে তদনুগা  
অতঃ সত্যং জ্ঞাতং মুরহর ভবানেব শরণম্ ॥ ৬ ॥

śrutir mātā pṛṣṭā diśati bhavad-ārādhana-vidhiṁ  
śruti  the mother  when asked  points out  of Your worship  the method  
yathā mātur vāṇī smṛtir api tathā vakti bhaginī  
as  of the mother  the words  smṛti  too  so  speaks  the sister  
purāṇādyā ye vā sahaja-nivahās te tad-anugā  
the Purāṇas and the rest  which  and  the brothers  they  following her  
ataḥ satyaṁ jñātaṁ murahara bhavān eva śaraṇam  
therefore  truly  it is known  O slayer of Mura  You  alone  the shelter

(6) “When Mother Śruti is asked, she points out the way to worship You. Smṛti, her sister, speaks just as the mother does, and the Purāṇas and the rest, like brothers, follow her. Therefore, O slayer of Mura, I have truly understood that You alone are my shelter.” (Words of a sage)[^4]

অদ্বয়জ্ঞান-তত্ত্ব কৃষ্ণ — স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
‘স্বরূপ-শক্তি’রূপে তাঁর হয় অবস্থান ॥ ৭ ॥

advaya-jñāna-tattva kṛṣṇa — svayaṁ bhagavān  
the Truth of nondual knowledge  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān  
‘svarūpa-śakti’-rūpe tām̐ra haya avasthāna  
of svarūpa-śakti  in the form  His  is  abiding

(7) Kṛṣṇa, the nondual Truth (*advaya-jñāna-tattva*), is Bhagavān Himself. He abides in the form of His own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*).[^5]

স্বাংশ-বিভিন্নাংশ-রূপে হঞা বিস্তার ৷  
অনন্ত বৈকুণ্ঠ-ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে করেন বিহার ॥ ৮ ॥

svāṁśa-vibhinnāṁśa-rūpe hañā vistāra  
as His own parts and separated parts  in the form  having become  expanded  
ananta vaikuṇṭha-brahmāṇḍe karena vihāra  
in endless  Vaikuṇṭhas and universes  He performs  pastimes

(8) Expanding Himself as His own parts (*svāṁśa*) and separated parts (*vibhinnāṁśa*), He sports in endless Vaikuṇṭhas and universes.[^6]

স্বাংশ-বিস্তার — চতুর্ব্ব্যূহ, অবতারগণ ৷  
বিভিন্নাংশ জীব — তাঁর শক্তিতে গণন ॥ ৯ ॥

svāṁśa-vistāra — catur-vyūha, avatāra-gaṇa  
the expansion of His own parts  the catur-vyūha  the host of avatāras  
vibhinnāṁśa jīva — tām̐ra śaktite gaṇana  
the separated part  the jīva  His  among the energies  is counted

(9) His own parts are the *catur-vyūha* and the host of avatāras. The jīva is His separated part and is counted among His energies.

সেই বিভিন্নাংশ জীব — দুই ত’ প্রকার ৷  
এক — ‘নিত্যমুক্ত’, এক — ‘নিত্য-সংসার’ ॥ ১০ ॥

sei vibhinnāṁśa jīva — dui ta’ prakāra  
those  separated parts  the jīvas  two  indeed  kinds  
eka — ‘nitya-mukta’, eka — ‘nitya-saṁsāra’  
one  eternally liberated  one  eternally in worldly existence

(10) Those jīvas, His separated parts, are of two kinds: some are eternally liberated (*nitya-mukta*), and some are eternally in worldly existence (*nitya-saṁsāra*).

‘নিত্যমুক্ত’ — নিত্য কৃষ্ণচরণে উন্মুখ ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ-পারিষদ’ নাম, ভুঞ্জে সেবা-সুখ ॥ ১১ ॥

‘nitya-mukta’ — nitya kṛṣṇa-caraṇe unmukha  
the eternally liberated  ever  toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet  turned  
‘kṛṣṇa-pāriṣada’ nāma, bhuñje sevā-sukha  
Kṛṣṇa’s associates  named  they enjoy  the joy of service

(11) The eternally liberated are ever turned toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet. They are called Kṛṣṇa’s associates (*pārṣadas*), and they enjoy the happiness of serving Him.

‘নিত্যবদ্ধ’ — কৃষ্ণ হৈতে নিত্য-বহির্ম্মুখ ৷  
‘নিত্যসংসার’, ভুঞ্জে নরকাদি-দুঃখ ॥ ১২ ॥

‘nitya-baddha’ — kṛṣṇa haite nitya-bahirmukha  
the eternally bound  from Kṛṣṇa  eternally turned away  
‘nitya-saṁsāra’, bhuñje narakādi-duḥkha  
eternally in worldly existence  they suffer  the miseries of hell and the like

(12) The eternally bound (*nitya-baddha*) jīva is forever turned away from Kṛṣṇa. Eternally caught in worldly existence, he suffers the miseries of hell and the like.

সেই দোষে মায়া-পিশাচী দণ্ড করে তারে ৷  
আধ্যাত্মিকাদি তাপত্রয় তারে জারি’ মারে ॥ ১৩ ॥

sei doṣe māyā-piśācī daṇḍa kare tāre  
for that  fault  the witch Māyā  punishment  inflicts  on him  
ādhyātmikādi tāpa-traya tāre jāri’ māre  
beginning with those of body and mind  the threefold miseries  him  wearing down  torment

(13) For this fault the witch Māyā punishes him: the threefold miseries, beginning with those of body and mind (*ādhyātmika*), wear him down and torment him.

কাম-ক্রোধের দাস হঞা তার লাথি খায় ৷  
ভ্রমিতে ভ্রমিতে যদি সাধু-বৈদ্য পায় ॥ ১৪ ॥

kāma-krodhera dāsa hañā tāra lāthi khāya  
of lust and anger  the servant  having become  her  kicks  he suffers  
bhramite bhramite yadi sādhu-vaidya pāya  
wandering  wandering  if  a sādhu-physician  he finds

(14) Having become the servant of lust and anger, he suffers her kicks. But if, wandering on and on, he meets a sādhu, a physician of the soul,

তাঁর উপদেশ-মন্ত্রে পিশাচী পলায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তি পায়, তবে কৃষ্ণ-নিকট যায় ॥ ১৫ ॥

tām̐ra upadeśa-mantre piśācī palāya  
his  by the mantra of instruction  the witch  flees  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti pāya, tabe kṛṣṇa-nikaṭa yāya  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  he obtains  then  near Kṛṣṇa  he goes

(15) by the mantra of that sādhu’s instruction the witch flees. The soul obtains devotion to Kṛṣṇa and then draws near to Kṛṣṇa.

কামাদীনাং কতি ন কতিধা পালিতা দুর্নিদেশা-  
স্তেষাং জাতা ময়ি ন করুণা ন ত্রপা নোপশান্তিঃ ৷  
উৎসৃজ্যৈতানথ যদুপতে সাম্প্রতং লব্ধবুদ্ধি-  
স্ত্বামায়াতঃ শরণমভয়ং মাং নিযুঙ্ক্ষ্বাত্মদাস্যে ॥ ১৬ ॥

kāmādīnāṁ kati na katidhā pālitā durnideśās  
of lust and the rest  how many  not  in how many ways  were obeyed  the evil commands  
teṣāṁ jātā mayi na karuṇā na trapā nopaśāntiḥ  
of them  arose  toward me  no  pity  no  shame  nor peace  
utsṛjyaitān atha yadu-pate sāmprataṁ labdha-buddhis  
having abandoned them  now  O Lord of the Yadus  at present  having gained good sense  
tvām āyātaḥ śaraṇam abhayaṁ māṁ niyuṅkṣvātma-dāsye  
to You  I have come  for shelter  fearless  me  engage in Your service

(16) “How many evil commands of lust and the rest have I not obeyed, and in how many ways! Yet they never took pity on me, nor did I feel shame or find peace. Now, O Lord of the Yadus, I have cast them off; good sense has dawned in me, and I have come to You, the fearless shelter. Engage me in Your service.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 6)

কৃষ্ণভক্তি হয় অভিধেয়-প্রধান ৷  
ভক্তিমুখ-নিরীক্ষক কর্ম্ম-যোগ-জ্ঞান ॥ ১৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhakti haya abhidheya-pradhāna  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is  the chief abhidheya  
bhakti-mukha-nirīkṣaka karma-yoga-jñāna  
on the face of bhakti  looking  karma  yoga  jñāna

(17) Devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the chief *abhidheya*. Karma, yoga and jñāna all look to the face of bhakti for their results.[^7]

এই সব সাধনের অতি তুচ্ছ বল ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তি বিনা তাহা দিতে নারে ফল ॥ ১৮ ॥

ei saba sādhanera ati tuccha bala  
of these  all  practices  very  feeble  the strength  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti vinā tāhā dite nāre phala  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without  they  to give  cannot  fruit

(18) The strength of all these practices is very feeble: without devotion to Kṛṣṇa they cannot give any fruit.

নৈষ্কর্ম্ম্যমপ্যচ্যুতভাব-বর্জ্জিতং  
ন শোভতে জ্ঞানমলং নিরঞ্জনম্ ৷  
কুতঃ পুনঃ শশ্বদভদ্রমীশ্বরে  
ন চার্পিতং কর্ম্ম যদপ্যকারণম্ ॥ ১৯ ॥

naiṣkarmyam apy acyuta-bhāva-varjitaṁ  
freedom from action  even  love for Acyuta  devoid of  
na śobhate jñānam alaṁ nirañjanam  
not  shines  knowledge  at all  stainless  
kutaḥ punaḥ śaśvad abhadram īśvare  
how then  again  ever  inauspicious  to the Lord  
na cārpitaṁ karma yad apy akāraṇam  
not  offered  action  which  even  free of motive

(19) “Even stainless knowledge, free from all action (*naiṣkarmya*), does not shine if it is devoid of love for Acyuta. How then can action shine, ever inauspicious as it is, if it is not offered to the Lord, even when it is free of selfish motive?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.5.12)[^8]

তপস্বিনো দানপরা যশস্বিনো  
মনস্বিনো মন্ত্রবিদঃ সুমঙ্গলাঃ ৷  
ক্ষেমং ন বিন্দন্তি বিনা যদর্পণং  
তস্মৈ সুভদ্রশ্রবসে নমো নমঃ ॥ ২০ ॥

tapasvino dāna-parā yaśasvino  
ascetics  devoted to charity  the renowned  
manasvino mantra-vidaḥ su-maṅgalāḥ  
the thoughtful  knowers of mantras  of auspicious conduct  
kṣemaṁ na vindanti vinā yad-arpaṇaṁ  
welfare  not  attain  without  offering to Him  
tasmai subhadra-śravase namo namaḥ  
to Him  of auspicious fame  obeisance  obeisance

(20) “Ascetics, the generous, the renowned, the thoughtful, the knowers of mantras and those of auspicious conduct — none of them attains true welfare without offering everything to Him. Again and again I bow to Him, whose fame is auspicious.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.4.17)

কেবল জ্ঞান ‘মুক্তি’ দিতে নারে ভক্তি বিনে ৷  
কৃষ্ণোন্মুখে সেই মুক্তি হয় বিনা-জ্ঞানে ॥ ২১ ॥

kevala jñāna ‘mukti’ dite nāre bhakti vine  
mere  knowledge  liberation  to give  cannot  bhakti  without  
kṛṣṇonmukhe sei mukti haya vinā-jñāne  
to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa  that  liberation  comes  without knowledge

(21) Knowledge alone cannot give liberation without bhakti; but to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa that liberation comes even without knowledge.

শ্রেয়ঃসৃতিং ভক্তিমুদস্য তে বিভো  
ক্লিশ্যন্তি যে কেবলবোধলব্ধয়ে ৷  
তেষামসৌ ক্লেশল এব শিষ্যতে  
নান্যদ্ যথা স্থূলতুষাবঘাতিনাম্ ॥ ২২ ॥

śreyaḥ-sṛtiṁ bhaktim udasya te vibho  
the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord  
kliśyanti ye kevala-bodha-labdhaye  
toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge  
teṣām asau kleśala eva śiṣyate  
for them  this  toil  only  remains  
nānyad yathā sthūla-tuṣāvaghātinām  
nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks

(22) “O almighty Lord, bhakti to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)

দৈবী হ্যেষা গুণময়ী মম মায়া দুরত্যয়া ৷  
মামেব যে প্রপদ্যন্তে মায়ামেতাং তরন্তি তে ॥ ২৩ ॥

daivī hy eṣā guṇa-mayī  
divine  indeed  this  made of the guṇas  
mama māyā duratyayā  
My  māyā  hard to overcome  
mām eva ye prapadyante  
to Me  alone  those who  surrender  
māyām etāṁ taranti te  
māyā  this  cross over  they

(23) “This divine māyā of Mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me alone cross beyond this māyā.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.14)[^9]

‘কৃষ্ণ-নিত্যদাস’ — জীব তাহা ভুলি’ গেল ৷  
এই দোষে মায়া তার গলায় বান্ধিল ॥ ২৪ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa-nitya-dāsa’ — jīva tāhā bhuli’ gela  
Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant  the jīva  that  having forgotten  went  
ei doṣe māyā tāra galāya bāndhila  
for that  fault  Māyā  his  around the neck  bound

(24) The jīva is Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant, but he has forgotten it. For that fault Māyā has bound him by the neck.[^10]

তাতে কৃষ্ণ ভজে, করে গুরুর সেবন ৷  
মায়াজাল ছুটে, পায় কৃষ্ণের চরণ ॥ ২৫ ॥

tāte kṛṣṇa bhaje, kare gurura sevana  
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he renders  the guru’s  service  
māyā-jāla chuṭe, pāya kṛṣṇera caraṇa  
the net of Māyā  falls away  he attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(25) If he then worships Kṛṣṇa and serves his guru, the net of Māyā falls away and he attains Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

চারি বর্ণাশ্রমী যদি কৃষ্ণ নাহি ভজে ৷  
স্বকর্ম্ম করিলেও সে রৌরবে পড়ি’ মজে ॥ ২৬ ॥

cāri varṇāśramī yadi kṛṣṇa nāhi bhaje  
of the four  followers of varṇa and āśrama  if  Kṛṣṇa  do not  worship  
svakarma karileo se raurave paḍi’ maje  
their own duties  even performing  they  in the hell Raurava  having fallen  sink

(26) If the followers of the four *varṇas* and *āśramas* do not worship Kṛṣṇa, then even while performing their own duties they fall into the hell Raurava and sink there.[^11]

মুখবাহূরুপাদেভ্যঃ পুরুষস্যাশ্রমৈঃ সহ ৷  
চত্বারো জজ্ঞিরে বর্ণা গুণৈর্ব্বিপ্রাদয়ঃ পৃথক্ ॥ ২৭ ॥

mukha-bāhūru-pādebhyaḥ  
from the mouth, arms, thighs and feet  
puruṣasyāśramaiḥ saha  
of the Puruṣa  with the āśramas  together  
catvāro jajñire varṇā  
four  were born  the varṇas  
guṇair viprādayaḥ pṛthak  
by their qualities  brāhmaṇas and the rest  distinct

(27) “From the mouth, arms, thighs and feet of the Puruṣa the four *varṇas*, beginning with the brāhmaṇas, were born together with the *āśramas*, each distinct by its qualities.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.2)[^12]

য এষাং পুরুষং সাক্ষাদাত্মপ্রভবমীশ্বরম্ ৷  
ন ভজন্ত্যবজানন্তি স্থানাদ্ভ্রষ্টাঃ পতন্ত্যধঃ ॥ ২৮ ॥

ya eṣāṁ puruṣaṁ sākṣād  
who  of them  the Puruṣa  directly  
ātma-prabhavam īśvaram  
their own source  the Lord  
na bhajanty avajānanti  
not  worship  disregard  
sthānād bhraṣṭāḥ patanty adhaḥ  
from their position  fallen  they fall  down

(28) “Those among them who do not worship the Puruṣa, their own source and Lord, but disregard Him, fall from their position and go down.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.3)

জ্ঞানী জীবন্মুক্তদশা পাইনু করি’ মানে ৷  
বস্তুতঃ বুদ্ধি ‘শুদ্ধ’ নহে কৃষ্ণভক্তি বিনে ॥ ২৯ ॥

jñānī jīvan-mukta-daśā pāinu kari’ māne  
the jñānī  of liberation in this life  the state  “I have attained”  thus  thinks  
vastutaḥ buddhi ‘śuddha’ nahe kṛṣṇa-bhakti vine  
in truth  the intelligence  pure  is not  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without

(29) The jñānī thinks, ‘I have attained the state of liberation in this life (*jīvan-mukti*).’ But in truth the intelligence does not become pure without devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

যেঽন্যেঽরবিন্দাক্ষ বিমুক্তমানিন-  
স্ত্বয়্যস্তভাবাদবিশুদ্ধবুদ্ধয়ঃ ৷  
আরুহ্য কৃচ্ছ্রেণ পরং পদং ততঃ  
পতন্ত্যধোঽনাদৃতযুষ্মদঙ্ঘ্রয়ঃ ॥ ৩০ ॥

ye ‘nye ‘ravindākṣa vimukta-māninas  
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated  
tvayy asta-bhāvād aviśuddha-buddhayaḥ  
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence  
āruhya kṛcchreṇa paraṁ padaṁ tataḥ  
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there  
patanty adho ‘nādṛta-yuṣmad-aṅghrayaḥ  
fall  down  having neglected Your feet

(30) “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)[^13]

কৃষ্ণ — সূর্য্যসম; মায়া হয় অন্ধকার ৷  
যাঁহা কৃষ্ণ, তাঁহা নাহি মায়ার অধিকার ॥ ৩১ ॥

kṛṣṇa — sūrya-sama; māyā haya andhakāra  
Kṛṣṇa  like the sun  Māyā  is  darkness  
yām̐hā kṛṣṇa, tām̐hā nāhi māyāra adhikāra  
where  Kṛṣṇa  there  is no  of Māyā  dominion

(31) Kṛṣṇa is like the sun, and Māyā is darkness. Where Kṛṣṇa is, Māyā has no power.[^14]

বিলজ্জমানয়া যস্য স্থাতুমীক্ষাপথেঽমুয়া ৷  
বিমোহিতা বিকত্থন্তে মমাহমিতি দুর্ধিয়ঃ ॥ ৩২ ॥

vilajjamānayā yasya  
ashamed  whose  
sthātum īkṣā-pathe ‘muyā  
to stand  in the path of the sight  by her  
vimohitā vikatthante  
bewildered  boast  
mamāham iti durdhiyaḥ  
“mine” and “I”  thus  the foolish

(32) “Māyā is ashamed to stand in the path of His sight; yet the foolish, bewildered by her, boast of ‘I’ and ‘mine’.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.5.13)

‘কৃষ্ণ, তোমার হঙ’ যদি বলে একবার ৷  
মায়াবন্ধ হৈতে কৃষ্ণ তারে করে পার ॥ ৩৩ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa, tomāra haṅa’ yadi bale eka-bāra  
“Kṛṣṇa  Yours  I am”  if  he says  once  
māyā-bandha haite kṛṣṇa tāre kare pāra  
from the bondage of Māyā  Kṛṣṇa  him  makes  carried across

(33) If someone says even once, ‘Kṛṣṇa, I am Yours’, Kṛṣṇa carries him across, beyond the bondage of Māyā.[^15]

সকৃদেব প্রপন্নো যস্তবাস্মীতি চ যাচতে ৷  
অভয়ং সর্ব্বদা তস্মৈ দদাম্যেতদ্ব্রতং মম ॥ ৩৪ ॥

sakṛd eva prapanno yas  
once  only  surrendered  who  
tavāsmīti ca yācate  
“I am Yours”  thus  and  begs  
abhayaṁ sarvadā tasmai  
fearlessness  always  to him  
dadāmy etad vrataṁ mama  
I give  this  vow  My

(34) “To one who surrenders to Me even once and prays, ‘I am Yours’, I always grant fearlessness. This is My vow.” (Rāmāyaṇa, Yuddha-kāṇḍa, 18.33)[^16]

মুক্তি-ভুক্তি-সিদ্ধিকামী ‘সুবুদ্ধি’ যদি হয় ৷  
গাঢ়-ভক্তিযোগে তবে কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ৩৫ ॥

mukti-bhukti-siddhi-kāmī ‘subuddhi’ yadi haya  
desiring liberation, enjoyment and perfections  truly intelligent  if  he becomes  
gāḍha-bhakti-yoge tabe kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
by deep bhakti-yoga  then  Kṛṣṇa  he worships

(35) If one who desires liberation, worldly enjoyment or mystic perfections becomes truly intelligent, he then worships Kṛṣṇa with deep *bhakti-yoga*.

অকামঃ সর্ব্বকামো বা মোক্ষকাম উদারধীঃ ৷  
তীব্রেণ ভক্তিযোগেন যজেত পুরুষং পরম্ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

akāmaḥ sarva-kāmo vā  
without desire  full of all desires  or  
mokṣa-kāma udāra-dhīḥ  
desiring liberation  of broad intelligence  
tīvreṇa bhakti-yogena  
with intense  bhakti-yoga  
yajeta puruṣaṁ param  
should worship  the Person  Supreme

(36) “Whether he is without desire, full of every desire, or desirous of liberation, a person of broad intelligence should worship the Supreme Person with intense *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.10)

অন্যকামী যদি করে কৃষ্ণের ভজন ৷  
না মাগিলেহ কৃষ্ণ তারে দেন স্ব-চরণ ॥ ৩৭ ॥

anya-kāmī yadi kare kṛṣṇera bhajana  
one with other desires  if  performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  worship  
nā māgileha kṛṣṇa tāre dena sva-caraṇa  
not  even having asked  Kṛṣṇa  to him  gives  His own feet

(37) If one who has other desires worships Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa gives him His own feet even though he has not asked for them.

কৃষ্ণ কহে, — ‘আমা ভজে, মাগে বিষয় সুখ ৷  
অমৃত ছাড়ি’ বিষ মাগে, — এই বড় মূর্খ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahe, — ‘āmā bhaje, māge viṣaya sukha  
Kṛṣṇa  says  Me  he worships  he asks for  sense  pleasure  
amṛta chāḍi’ viṣa māge, — ei baḍa mūrkha  
nectar  giving up  poison  he asks for  this one  a great  fool

(38) Kṛṣṇa says, ‘He worships Me, yet asks for sense pleasure. Giving up nectar, he asks for poison — he is a great fool.

আমি — বিজ্ঞ, এই মূর্খে ‘বিষয়’ কেনে দিব ?  
স্ব-চরণামৃত দিয়া ‘বিষয়’ ভুলাইব’ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

āmi — vijña, ei mūrkhe ‘viṣaya’ kene diba?  
I  am wise  to this  fool  sense objects  why  should I give  
sva-caraṇāmṛta diyā ‘viṣaya’ bhulāiba’  
the nectar of My own feet  giving  sense objects  I shall make him forget

(39) But I am wise: why should I give sense objects to this fool? I will give him the nectar of My own feet and make him forget sense objects.’

সত্যং দিশত্যর্থিতমর্থিতো নৃণাং  
নৈবার্থদো যৎ পুনরর্থিতা যতঃ ৷  
স্বয়ং বিধত্তে ভজতামনিচ্ছতা-  
মিচ্ছাপিধানং নিজপাদপল্লবম্ ॥ ৪০ ॥

satyaṁ diśaty arthitam arthito nṛṇāṁ  
truly  He grants  what is asked  when asked  to men  
naivārtha-do yat punar arthitā yataḥ  
not indeed  giver of that  which  again  asking  from which  
svayaṁ vidhatte bhajatām anicchatām  
Himself  He grants  to those who worship  though not desiring  
icchā-pidhānaṁ nija-pāda-pallavam  
covering all desires  His own  feet like tender shoots

(40) “It is true that when men ask, the Lord grants what they ask for; but He does not give what would make them ask again. To those who worship Him, though they do not desire it, He Himself gives His own feet, tender as new shoots, which put an end to all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.19.26)[^17]

কাম লাগি’ কৃষ্ণে ভজে, পায় কৃষ্ণ-রসে ৷  
কাম ছাড়ি’ ‘দাস’ হৈতে হয় অভিলাষে ॥ ৪১ ॥

kāma lāgi’ kṛṣṇe bhaje, pāya kṛṣṇa-rase  
desire  for the sake of  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he attains  the taste of Kṛṣṇa  
kāma chāḍi’ ‘dāsa’ haite haya abhilāṣe  
desire  giving up  a servant  to become  there arises  the longing

(41) One who worships Kṛṣṇa for the sake of some desire comes to taste Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness (*kṛṣṇa-rasa*). Then, giving up that desire, he longs to become His servant.

স্থানাভিলাষী তপসি স্থিতোঽহং  
ত্বাং প্রাপ্তবান্ দেবমুনীন্দ্রগুহ্যম্ ৷  
কাচং বিচিন্বন্নপি দিব্যরত্নং  
স্বামিন্ কৃতার্থোঽস্মি বরং ন যাচে ॥ ৪২ ॥

sthānābhilāṣī tapasi sthito ‘haṁ  
desiring a position  in austerity  engaged  I  
tvāṁ prāptavān deva-munīndra-guhyam  
You  have attained  hidden from the gods and the best of sages  
kācaṁ vicinvann api divya-ratnaṁ  
glass  searching for  though  a divine jewel  
svāmin kṛtārtho ‘smi varaṁ na yāce  
O Master  fulfilled  I am  a boon  not  I ask

(42) “Desiring a high position, I engaged in austerities, and I have attained You, who are hidden even from the gods and the greatest sages. I was searching for a piece of glass and found a divine jewel. O Master, my purpose is fulfilled; I ask for no boon.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 7.28)

সংসার ভ্রমিতে কোন ভাগ্যে কেহ তরে ৷  
নদীর প্রবাহে যেন কাষ্ঠ লাগে তীরে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

saṁsāra bhramite kona bhāgye keha tare  
in worldly existence  while wandering  by some  fortune  someone  is delivered  
nadīra pravāhe yena kāṣṭha lāge tīre  
of a river  in the current  as  a log  touches  the bank

(43) While wandering in worldly existence, someone by some good fortune is delivered, as a log carried by a river’s current comes to rest on the bank.

মৈবং মমাধমস্যাপি স্যাদেবাচ্যুতদর্শনম্ ৷  
হ্রিয়মাণঃ কালনদ্যা ক্বচিত্তরতি কশ্চন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

maivaṁ mamādhamasyāpi  
not so  of me  lowly  even  
syād evācyuta-darśanam  
there will be  surely  the sight of Acyuta  
hriyamāṇaḥ kāla-nadyā  
being carried away  by the river of time  
kvacit tarati kaścana  
at times  crosses over  someone

(44) “It is not so that I, lowly as I am, cannot see Acyuta: I will surely see Him. For even among those carried away by the river of time, someone at times reaches the shore.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.38.5)[^18]

কোন ভাগ্যে কারো সংসার ক্ষয়োন্মুখ হয় ৷  
সাধুসঙ্গে তরে, কৃষ্ণে রতি উপজয় ॥ ৪৫ ॥

kona bhāgye kāro saṁsāra kṣayonmukha haya  
by some  fortune  someone’s  worldly existence  near its end  becomes  
sādhu-saṅge tare, kṛṣṇe rati upajaya  
in the company of sādhus  he is delivered  for Kṛṣṇa  attraction  arises

(45) When by some good fortune someone’s worldly existence nears its end, he is delivered through the company of sādhus, and attraction (*rati*) for Kṛṣṇa awakens in him.

ভবাপবর্গো ভ্রমতো যদা ভবে-  
জ্জনস্য তর্হ্যচ্যুত সৎসমাগমঃ ৷  
সৎসঙ্গমো যর্হি তদৈব সদ্গতৌ  
পরাবরেশে ত্বয়ি জায়তে রতিঃ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

bhavāpavargo bhramato yadā bhavej  
the end of worldly existence  of one wandering  when  comes  
janasya tarhy acyuta sat-samāgamaḥ  
of a person  then  O Acyuta  meeting with the saints  
sat-saṅgamo yarhi tadaiva sad-gatau  
association with the saints  when  at that very time  for the goal of the saints  
parāvareśe tvayi jāyate ratiḥ  
for the Lord of the high and the low  for You  is born  attraction

(46) “O Acyuta, when a wandering soul’s worldly existence comes to its end, he meets the saints. And when he associates with the saints, attraction (*rati*) is born in him for You, the goal of the saints and the Lord of the high and the low.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.51.53)[^19]

কৃষ্ণ যদি কৃপা করে কোন ভাগ্যবানে ৷  
গুরু-অন্তর্য্যামি-রূপে শিখায় আপনে ॥ ৪৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa yadi kṛpā kare kona bhāgyavāne  
Kṛṣṇa  if  mercy  shows  to some  fortunate soul  
guru-antaryāmi-rūpe śikhāya āpane  
in the forms of guru and the Indweller  teaches  Himself

(47) If Kṛṣṇa shows mercy to some fortunate soul, He Himself teaches him in the forms of the guru and the Indweller (*antaryāmī*).

নৈবোপয়ন্ত্যপচিতিং কবয়স্তবেশ  
ব্রহ্মায়ুষাপি কৃতমৃদ্ধমুদঃ স্মরন্তঃ ৷  
যোঽন্তর্বহিস্তনুভৃতামশুভং বিধুন্ব-  
ন্নাচার্য্যচৈত্ত্যবপুষা স্বগতিং ব্যনক্তি ॥ ৪৮ ॥

naivopayanty apacitiṁ kavayas taveśa  
not  attain  repayment  the wise  Your,  O Lord  
brahmāyuṣāpi kṛtam ṛddha-mudaḥ smarantaḥ  
with Brahmā’s lifespan  even  Your deeds  with growing joy  remembering  
yo ’ntar bahis tanu-bhṛtām aśubhaṁ vidhunvann  
who  within  without  of the embodied  the inauspicious  dispelling  
ācārya-caitya-vapuṣā sva-gatiṁ vyanakti  
as ācārya  and Supersoul  in forms  Your own path  reveal

(48) “O Lord! Even if the wise lived as long as Brahmā and remembered with ever-growing joy all that You have done for them, they could not repay their debt to You. For, dispelling all that is inauspicious in embodied souls, You come to them from without as the ācārya and from within as the Supersoul, and reveal to them the way to Yourself.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.29.6)[^20]

সাধুসঙ্গে কৃষ্ণভক্ত্যে শ্রদ্ধা যদি হয় ৷  
ভক্তিফল ‘প্রেম’ হয়, সংসার যায় ক্ষয় ॥ ৪৯ ॥

sādhu-saṅge kṛṣṇa-bhaktye śraddhā yadi haya  
in the company of sādhus  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  faith  if  arises  
bhakti-phala ‘prema’ haya, saṁsāra yāya kṣaya  
the fruit of bhakti  prema  comes  worldly existence  goes  to its end

(49) If, in the company of sādhus, faith in devotion to Kṛṣṇa arises, then prema, the fruit of bhakti, appears, and worldly existence comes to an end.

যদৃচ্ছয়া মৎকথাদৌ জাতশ্রদ্ধস্তু যঃ পুমান্ ৷  
ন নির্বিণ্ণো নাতিসক্তো ভক্তিযোগোঽস্য সিদ্ধিদঃ ॥ ৫০ ॥

yadṛcchayā mat-kathādau  
by good fortune  in narrations about Me and the rest  
jāta-śraddhas tu yaḥ pumān  
in whom faith is born  but  who  the person  
na nirviṇṇo nāti-sakto  
not  disgusted  not overly attached  
bhakti-yogo ‘sya siddhi-daḥ  
bhakti-yoga  for him  gives perfection

(50) “For one who by good fortune has come to have faith in narrations about Me and the like, and who is neither disgusted with the world nor overly attached to it, *bhakti-yoga* grants perfection.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.8)[^21]

মহৎ-কৃপা বিনা কোন কর্ম্মে ‘ভক্তি’ নয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তি দূরে রহু, সংসার নহে ক্ষয় ॥ ৫১ ॥

mahat-kṛpā vinā kona karme ‘bhakti’ naya  
of the great souls  the mercy  without  by any  activity  bhakti  is not  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti dūre rahu, saṁsāra nahe kṣaya  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  aside  let it be  worldly existence  does not  end

(51) Without the mercy of great souls no activity yields bhakti. Without it, worldly existence does not even come to an end, let alone devotion to Kṛṣṇa arise.

রহূগণৈতৎ তপসা ন যাতি  
ন চেজ্যয়া নির্ব্বপণাদ্গৃহাদ্বা ৷  
ন চ্ছন্দসা নৈব জলাগ্নিসূর্য্যৈ-  
র্ব্বিনা মহৎপাদরজোঽভিষেকম্ ॥ ৫২ ॥

rahūgaṇaitat tapasā na yāti  
O Rahūgaṇa  this  by austerity  not  is attained  
na cejyayā nirvapaṇād gṛhād vā  
not  and by worship  by renunciation  by household life  or  
na cchandasā naiva jalāgni-sūryair  
not  by the Vedas  nor  by water, fire and sun  
vinā mahat-pāda-rajo-‘bhiṣekam  
without  bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls

(52) “O Rahūgaṇa, this is not attained by austerity, by worship, by renunciation or by household duties, nor by the Vedas, nor by worship of water, fire and the sun, without bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.12.12)[^22]

নৈষাং মতিস্তাবদুরুক্রমাঙ্ঘ্রিং  
স্পৃশত্যনর্থাপগমো যদর্থঃ ৷  
মহীয়সাং পাদরজোঽভিষেকং  
নিষ্কিঞ্চনানাং ন বৃণীত যাবৎ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

naiṣāṁ matis tāvad urukramāṅghriṁ  
not  their  mind  so long  Urukrama’s feet  
spṛśaty anarthāpagamo yad-arthaḥ  
touches  the removal of anarthas  whose purpose  
mahīyasāṁ pāda-rajo-‘bhiṣekaṁ  
of the great  bathing in the dust of the feet  
niṣkiñcanānāṁ na vṛṇīta yāvat  
of those who own nothing  not  accepts  as long as

(53) “So long as people do not accept a bath in the dust of the feet of the great, who own nothing (*niṣkiñcana*), their minds cannot touch the feet of Urukrama, whose purpose is to remove all *anarthas*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.5.32)[^23]

‘সাধুসঙ্গ’, ‘সাধুসঙ্গ’ — সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
লবমাত্র সাধুসঙ্গে সর্ব্বসিদ্ধি হয় ॥ ৫৪ ॥

‘sādhu-saṅga’, ‘sādhu-saṅga’ — sarva-śāstre kaya  
sādhu-saṅga  sādhu-saṅga  all the scriptures  declare  
lava-mātra sādhu-saṅge sarva-siddhi haya  
a mere moment of  sādhu-saṅga  all perfection  comes

(54) ‘The company of sādhus, the company of sādhus!’ — so declare all the scriptures. Even a moment of *sādhu-saṅga* brings every perfection.

তুলয়াম লবেনাপি ন স্বর্গং নাপুনর্ভবম্ ৷  
ভগবৎসঙ্গিসঙ্গস্য মর্ত্ত্যানাং কিমুতাশিষঃ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

tulayāma lavenāpi  
we compare  with a moment  even  
na svargaṁ nāpunar-bhavam  
not  heaven  not liberation  
bhagavat-saṅgi-saṅgasya  
of association with those who associate with the Lord  
martyānāṁ kim utāśiṣaḥ  
of mortals  what  then  the blessings

(55) “We cannot compare even heaven or liberation with a moment of association with those who associate with the Lord — what then of the blessings mortals seek?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.18.13)[^24]

কৃষ্ণ কৃপালু অর্জ্জুনেরে লক্ষ্য করিয়া ৷  
জগতেরে রাখিয়াছেন উপদেশ দিয়া ॥ ৫৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa kṛpālu arjunere lakṣya kariyā  
Kṛṣṇa  merciful  Arjuna  the aim  making  
jagatere rākhiyāchena upadeśa diyā  
the world  has protected  instruction  giving

(56) Merciful Kṛṣṇa, speaking to Arjuna, has protected the whole world by giving this instruction:

সর্ব্বগুহ্যতমং ভূয়ঃ শৃণু মে পরমং বচঃ ৷  
ইষ্টোঽসি মে দৃঢ়মিতি ততো বক্ষ্যামি তে হিতম্ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

sarva-guhyatamaṁ bhūyaḥ  
the most secret of all  again  
śṛṇu me paramaṁ vacaḥ  
hear  My  supreme  word  
iṣṭo ‘si me dṛḍham iti  
dear  you are  to Me  very  thus  
tato vakṣyāmi te hitam  
therefore  I shall speak  to you  your good

(57) “Hear again My supreme word, the most secret of all. You are very dear to Me, and therefore I shall tell you what is for your good.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.64)

মন্মনা ভব মদ্ভক্তো মদ্যাজী মাং নমস্কুরু ৷  
মামেবৈষ্যসি সত্যং তে প্রতিজানে প্রিয়োঽসি মে ॥ ৫৮ ॥

man-manā bhava mad-bhakto  
with mind fixed on Me  be  My devotee  
mad-yājī māṁ namaskuru  
worshipping Me  to Me  bow down  
mām evaiṣyasi satyaṁ te  
to Me  surely you will come  truly  to you  
pratijāne priyo ‘si me  
I promise  dear  you are  to Me

(58) “Fix your mind on Me, be My devotee, worship Me and bow down to Me. You will surely come to Me — truly I promise you this, for you are dear to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.65)

পূর্ব্ব আজ্ঞা, — বেদ-ধর্ম্ম, কর্ম্ম, যোগ, জ্ঞান ৷  
সব সাধি’ অবশেষ-আজ্ঞা — বলবান্ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

pūrva ājñā, — veda-dharma, karma, yoga, jñāna  
the earlier  commands  Vedic dharma  karma  yoga  jñāna  
saba sādhi’ avaśeṣa-ājñā — balavān  
all  having established  the final command  is strongest

(59) His earlier commands concerned Vedic *dharma*, karma, yoga and jñāna. Having established all of these, He gave His final command, and it is the strongest.

এই আজ্ঞাবলে ভক্তের ‘শ্রদ্ধা’ যদি হয় ৷  
সর্ব্বকর্ম্ম ত্যাগ করি’ সে কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ৬০ ॥

ei ājñā-bale bhaktera ‘śraddhā’ yadi haya  
of this  by the force of the command  in the devotee  faith  if  arises  
sarva-karma tyāga kari’ se kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
all activities  giving up  having made  he  Kṛṣṇa  worships

(60) If by the force of this command faith arises in the devotee, he gives up all other activities and worships Kṛṣṇa.

তাবৎ কর্ম্মাণি কুর্ব্বীত ন নির্ব্বিদ্যেত যাবতা ৷  
মৎকথাশ্রবণাদৌ বা শ্রদ্ধা যাবন্ন জায়তে ॥ ৬১ ॥

tāvat karmāṇi kurvīta  
so long  duties  one should perform  
na nirvidyeta yāvatā  
not  one becomes weary  as long as  
mat-kathā-śravaṇādau vā  
in hearing My narrations and the rest  or  
śraddhā yāvan na jāyate  
faith  as long as  not  is born

(61) “One should go on performing one’s prescribed duties only so long as one has not grown weary of them, or so long as faith has not been born in hearing about Me and the like.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.9)[^25]

‘শ্রদ্ধা’-শব্দে — বিশ্বাস কহে সুদৃঢ় নিশ্চয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণে ভক্তি কৈলে সর্ব্বকর্ম্ম কৃত হয় ॥ ৬২ ॥

‘śraddhā’-śabde — viśvāsa kahe sudṛḍha niścaya  
by the word “śraddhā”  trust  is meant  very firm  conviction  
kṛṣṇe bhakti kaile sarva-karma kṛta haya  
to Kṛṣṇa  devotion  when one renders  all duties  done  become

(62) The word *śraddhā* means trust: a very firm conviction that by rendering devotion to Kṛṣṇa one has fulfilled all one’s duties.[^26]

যথা তরোর্ম্মূলনিষেচনেন  
তৃপ্যন্তি তৎস্কন্ধভুজোপশাখাঃ ৷  
প্রাণোপহারাচ্চ যথেন্দ্রিয়াণাং  
তথৈব সর্ব্বার্হণমচ্যুতেজ্যা ॥ ৬৩ ॥

yathā taror mūla-niṣecanena  
as  of a tree  by watering the root  
tṛpyanti tat-skandha-bhujopaśākhāḥ  
are satisfied  its trunk, branches and twigs  
prāṇopahārāc ca yathendriyāṇāṁ  
by feeding the life-breath  and  as  of the senses  
tathaiva sarvārhaṇam acyutejyā  
just so  the worship of all  is the worship of Acyuta

(63) “As by watering the root of a tree its trunk, branches and twigs are all nourished, and as by feeding the life-breath (*prāṇa*) all the senses are satisfied, so the worship of Acyuta is the worship of all.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 4.31.14)

শ্রদ্ধাবান্ জন হয় ভক্তি-অধিকারী ৷  
‘উত্তম’, ‘মধ্যম’, ‘কনিষ্ঠ’ — শ্রদ্ধা-অনুসারী ॥ ৬৪ ॥

śraddhāvān jana haya bhakti-adhikārī  
having faith  a person  is  qualified for bhakti  
‘uttama’, ‘madhyama’, ‘kaniṣṭha’ — śraddhā-anusārī  
highest  middle  lowest  according to faith

(64) A person who has faith is qualified for bhakti. According to his faith he is of the highest, the middle or the lowest level (*uttama*, *madhyama*, *kaniṣṭha*).

শাস্ত্রযুক্ত্যে সুনিপুণ, দৃঢ়শ্রদ্ধা যাঁর ৷  
‘উত্তম অধিকারী’ সেই তারয় সংসার ॥ ৬৫ ॥

śāstra-yuktye sunipuṇa, dṛḍha-śraddhā yām̐ra  
in scripture and reasoning  very expert  firm faith  whose  
‘uttama adhikārī’ sei tāraya saṁsāra  
of the highest  qualification  he  delivers  the world

(65) One who is expert in scripture and reasoning and whose faith is firm is an *uttama adhikārī*, of the highest qualification; he delivers the world.

শাস্ত্র-যুক্তি নাহি জানে দৃঢ়, শ্রদ্ধাবান্ ৷  
‘মধ্যম-অধিকারী’ সেই মহা-ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ৬৬ ॥

śāstra-yukti nāhi jāne dṛḍha, śraddhāvān  
the reasoning of scripture  not  knows  firmly  having faith  
‘madhyama-adhikārī’ sei mahā-bhāgyavān  
of middle qualification  he  greatly fortunate

(66) One who does not firmly know the reasoning of scripture but has faith is a *madhyama adhikārī*, of the middle qualification; he is most fortunate.[^27]

যাহার কোমল শ্রদ্ধা, সে — ‘কনিষ্ঠ’ জন ৷  
ক্রমে ক্রমে তেঁহো ভক্ত হইবে ‘উত্তম’ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

yāhāra komala śraddhā, se — ‘kaniṣṭha’ jana  
whose  tender  faith  he  a kaniṣṭha  person  
krame krame tem̐ho bhakta haibe ‘uttama’  
step by step  step by step  he  a devotee  will become  of the highest

(67) One whose faith is still tender is a *kaniṣṭha*, a beginner. Step by step he too will become a devotee of the highest level.

রতি-প্রেম-তারতম্যে ভক্ত — তর-তম ৷  
একাদশ স্কন্ধে তার করিয়াছে লক্ষণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

rati-prema-tāratamye bhakta — tara-tama  
by the degree of rati and prema  devotees  higher and highest  
ekādaśa skandhe tāra kariyāche lakṣaṇa  
in the eleventh  canto  their  has been given  description

(68) According to the degree of their *rati* and prema, devotees are higher and highest. The Eleventh Canto has described their characteristics.

সর্ব্বভূতেষু যঃ পশ্যেদ্ভগবদ্ভাবমাত্মনঃ ৷  
ভূতানি ভগবত্যাত্মন্যেষ ভাগবতোত্তমঃ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

sarva-bhūteṣu yaḥ paśyed  
in all beings  who  sees  
bhagavad-bhāvam ātmanaḥ  
of the Lord  the presence  of the Self  
bhūtāni bhagavaty ātmany  
the beings  in the Lord  in the Self  
eṣa bhāgavatottamaḥ  
he  the best of devotees

(69) “One who sees the Lord, the Soul of all, present in all beings, and sees all beings in the Lord, is the best of devotees (*bhāgavatottama*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.45)[^28]

ঈশ্বরে তদধীনেষু বালিশেষু দ্বিষৎসু চ ৷  
প্রেম-মৈত্রী-কৃপোপেক্ষা যঃ করোতি স মধ্যমঃ ॥ ৭০ ॥

īśvare tad-adhīneṣu  
to the Lord  to those dependent on Him  
bāliśeṣu dviṣatsu ca  
to the ignorant  to the hostile  and  
prema-maitrī-kṛpopekṣā  
love, friendship, mercy and indifference  
yaḥ karoti sa madhyamaḥ  
who  shows  he  is of the middle level

(70) “One who shows love for the Lord, friendship for those who depend on Him, mercy to the ignorant and indifference to the hostile is a devotee of the middle level (*madhyama*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.46)

অর্চ্চায়ামেব হরয়ে পূজাং যঃ শ্রদ্ধয়েহতে ৷  
ন তদ্ভক্তেষু চান্যেষু স ভক্তঃ প্রাকৃতঃ স্মৃতঃ ॥ ৭১ ॥

arcāyām eva haraye  
in the Deity  only  to Hari  
pūjāṁ yaḥ śraddhayehate  
worship  who  with faith performs  
na tad-bhakteṣu cānyeṣu  
not  to His devotees  and to others  
sa bhaktaḥ prākṛtaḥ smṛtaḥ  
he  a devotee  on the worldly level  is considered

(71) “One who with faith worships Hari only in the Deity, but does not honour His devotees or others, is known as a *prākṛta* devotee, one still on the worldly level.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.47)

সর্ব্ব মহা-গুণগণ বৈষ্ণব-শরীরে ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তে কৃষ্ণের গুণ, সকলি সঞ্চারে ॥ ৭২ ॥

sarva mahā-guṇa-gaṇa vaiṣṇava-śarīre  
all  the great qualities  in the person of a Vaiṣṇava  
kṛṣṇa-bhakte kṛṣṇera guṇa, sakali sañcāre  
into Kṛṣṇa’s devotee  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  all  pass

(72) All great qualities dwell in a Vaiṣṇava; all of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities pass into Kṛṣṇa’s devotee.

যস্যাস্তি ভক্তির্ভগবত্যকিঞ্চনা  
সর্ব্বৈর্গুণৈস্তত্র সমাসতে সুরাঃ ৷  
হরাবভক্তস্য কুতো মহদ্গুণা  
মনোরথেনাসতি ধাবতো বহিঃ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

yasyāsti bhaktir bhagavaty akiñcanā  
in whom  there is  devotion  to the Lord  free of desire  
sarvair guṇais tatra samāsate surāḥ  
with all  qualities  there  dwell  the gods  
harāv abhaktasya kuto mahad-guṇā  
to Hari  of one not devoted  whence  great qualities  
mano-rathenāsati dhāvato bahiḥ  
by the chariot of the mind  toward the unreal  running  outward

(73) “In one whose devotion to the Lord is free of desire all the gods dwell, and with them every virtue. But how can great qualities be found in one who is not devoted to Hari? Driven by his desires, his mind is forever running outward after what is passing.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.18.12)[^29]

সেই সব গুণ হয় বৈষ্ণব-লক্ষণ ৷  
সব কহা না যায়, করি দিগ্দরশন ॥ ৭৪ ॥

sei saba guṇa haya vaiṣṇava-lakṣaṇa  
those  all  qualities  are  the marks of a Vaiṣṇava  
saba kahā nā yāya, kari dig-daraśana  
all  to tell  not  possible  I give  an indication

(74) All these qualities are the marks of a Vaiṣṇava. They cannot all be told, so I shall only point to some of them.

কৃপালু, অকৃতদ্রোহ, সত্যসার, সম ৷  
নির্দ্দোষ, বদান্য, মৃদু, শুচি, অকিঞ্চন ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kṛpālu, akṛta-droha, satya-sāra, sama  
merciful  harming no one  devoted to truth  equal to all  
nirdoṣa, vadānya, mṛdu, śuci, akiñcana  
faultless  magnanimous  gentle  pure  owning nothing

(75) A Vaiṣṇava is merciful, harms no one, is devoted to truth, equal to all, faultless, magnanimous, gentle, pure and without possessions (*akiñcana*);[^30]

সর্ব্বোপকারক, শান্ত, কৃষ্ণৈকশরণ ৷  
অকাম, নিরীহ, স্থির, বিজিত-ষড়্গুণ ॥ ৭৬ ॥

sarvopakāraka, śānta, kṛṣṇaika-śaraṇa  
benefactor of all  peaceful  having Kṛṣṇa as the only shelter  
akāma, nirīha, sthira, vijita-ṣaḍ-guṇa  
without desire  free from worldly striving  steady  having conquered the six

(76) he is a benefactor of all, peaceful, with Kṛṣṇa as his only shelter, free from desire, free from worldly striving, steady, the conqueror of the six (*ṣaḍ-guṇa*);

মিতভুক্ অপ্রমত্ত, মানদ, অমানী ৷  
গম্ভীর, করুণ, মৈত্র, কবি, দক্ষ, মৌনী ॥ ৭৭ ॥

mita-bhuk apramatta, mānada, amānī  
moderate in eating  sober  giving honor to others  seeking no honor  
gambhīra, karuṇa, maitra, kavi, dakṣa, maunī  
grave  compassionate  friendly  a poet  expert  silent

(77) he is moderate in eating, sober, giving honour to others while seeking none for himself, grave, compassionate, friendly, a poet, expert and sparing in speech.

তিতিক্ষবঃ কারুণিকাঃ সুহৃদঃ সর্ব্বদেহিনাম্ ৷  
অজাতশত্রবঃ শান্তাঃ সাধবঃ সাধুভূষণাঃ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

titikṣavaḥ kāruṇikāḥ  
tolerant  compassionate  
suhṛdaḥ sarva-dehinām  
well-wishers  of all embodied beings  
ajāta-śatravaḥ śāntāḥ  
with no enemies  peaceful  
sādhavaḥ sādhu-bhūṣaṇāḥ  
following the scriptures  adorned with saintliness

(78) “The sādhus are tolerant and compassionate, well-wishers of all embodied beings. They have no enemies and are peaceful; they follow the scriptures, and saintly conduct is their ornament.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.21)

মহৎসেবাং দ্বারমাহুর্ব্বিমুক্তে-  
স্তমোদ্বারং যোষিতাং সঙ্গিসঙ্গম্ ৷  
মহান্তস্তে সমচিত্তাঃ প্রশান্তাঃ  
বিমন্যবঃ সুহৃদঃ সাধবো যে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

mahat-sevāṁ dvāram āhur vimuktes  
service to the great  the door  they call  of liberation  
tamo-dvāraṁ yoṣitāṁ saṅgi-saṅgam  
the door to darkness  of those attached to women  the company  
mahāntas te sama-cittāḥ praśāntāḥ  
the great  they  equal-minded  peaceful  
vimanyavaḥ suhṛdaḥ sādhavo ye  
free from anger  well-wishers  saintly  who

(79) “Service to the great is called the door to liberation, and the company of those attached to women the door to darkness. The great are those who are equal-minded, peaceful, free from anger, well-wishers of all and saintly.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.5.2)

কৃষ্ণভক্তি-জন্মমূল হয় ‘সাধুসঙ্গ’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেম জন্মে, তিঁহো পুনঃ মুখ্য অঙ্গ ॥ ৮০ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhakti-janma-mūla haya ‘sādhu-saṅga’  
of the birth of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the root  is  sādhu-saṅga  
kṛṣṇa-prema janme, tim̐ho punaḥ mukhya aṅga  
love for Kṛṣṇa  is born  it  again  the principal  limb

(80) The root from which devotion to Kṛṣṇa is born is *sādhu-saṅga*. And even when love for Kṛṣṇa is born, *sādhu-saṅga* remains its principal limb.

ভবাপবর্গো ভ্রমতো যদা ভবে-  
জ্জনস্য তর্হ্যচ্যুত সৎসমাগমঃ ৷  
সৎসঙ্গমো যর্হি তদৈব সদ্গতৌ  
পরাবরেশে ত্বয়ি জায়তে রতিঃ ॥ ৮১ ॥

bhavāpavargo bhramato yadā bhavej  
the end of worldly existence  of one wandering  when  comes  
janasya tarhy acyuta sat-samāgamaḥ  
of a person  then  O Acyuta  meeting with the saints  
sat-saṅgamo yarhi tadaiva sad-gatau  
association with the saints  when  at that very time  for the goal of the saints  
parāvareśe tvayi jāyate ratiḥ  
for the Lord of the high and the low  for You  is born  attraction

(81) “O Acyuta, when a wandering soul’s worldly existence comes to its end, he meets the saints. And when he associates with the saints, attraction (*rati*) is born in him for You, the goal of the saints and the Lord of the high and the low.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.51.53)[^31]

অত আত্যন্তিকং ক্ষেমং পৃচ্ছামো ভবতোঽনঘাঃ ৷  
সংসারেঽস্মিন্ ক্ষণার্দ্ধোঽপি সৎসঙ্গঃ সেবধির্নৃণাম্ ॥ ৮২ ॥

ata ātyantikaṁ kṣemaṁ  
therefore  the ultimate  good  
pṛcchāmo bhavato ‘naghāḥ  
we ask  you  O sinless ones  
saṁsāre ‘smin kṣaṇārdho ‘pi  
in worldly existence  this  half a moment  even  
sat-saṅgaḥ sevadhir nṛṇām  
association with saints  a treasure  for men

(82) “Therefore, O sinless ones, we ask you about the ultimate good. In this world even half a moment of association with saints is a treasure for humankind.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.30)

সতাং প্রসঙ্গান্মম বীর্য্যসম্বিদো  
ভবন্তি হৃৎকর্ণরসায়নাঃ কথাঃ ৷  
তজ্জোষণাদাশ্বপবর্গবর্ত্মনি  
শ্রদ্ধা রতির্ভক্তিরনুক্রমিষ্যতি ॥ ৮৩ ॥

satāṁ prasaṅgān mama vīrya-samvido  
of the saints  in the company  My  prowess  revealing  
bhavanti hṛt-karṇa-rasāyanāḥ kathāḥ  
become  to heart and ear  delightful  talks  
taj-joṣaṇād āśv apavarga-vartmani  
by relishing them  quickly  on the path of liberation  
śraddhā ratir bhaktir anukramiṣyati  
faith,  attraction,  love  will follow in turn

(83) (Lord Kapila to His mother Devahūti:) “In the company of the saints there are talks revealing My power, delightful to the heart and to the ear. By relishing them one quickly comes to faith in Me, the path of liberation, and after it to attraction (*rati*) and love (*prema-bhakti*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.25)[^32]

অসৎসঙ্গত্যাগ, — এই বৈষ্ণব-আচার ৷  
‘স্ত্রীসঙ্গী’ — এক অসাধু, ‘কৃষ্ণাভক্ত’ আর ॥ ৮৪ ॥

asat-saṅga-tyāga, — ei vaiṣṇava-ācāra  
of bad association  the rejection  this  Vaiṣṇava  conduct  
‘strī-saṅgī’ — eka asādhu, ‘kṛṣṇābhakta’ āra  
one attached to women  one kind  of the unholy  one who is not Kṛṣṇa’s devotee  another

(84) To give up bad association — this is the conduct of a Vaiṣṇava. One who is attached to women is one kind of unholy person (*asādhu*); one who is not a devotee of Kṛṣṇa is the other.

সত্যং শৌচং দয়া মৌনং বুদ্ধির্হ্রীঃ শ্রীর্যশঃ ক্ষমা ৷  
শমো দমো ভগশ্চেতি যৎসঙ্গাদ্যাতি সংক্ষয়ম্ ॥ ৮৫ ॥

satyaṁ śaucaṁ dayā maunaṁ  
truthfulness  purity  compassion  silence  
buddhir hrīḥ śrīr yaśaḥ kṣamā  
intelligence  modesty  beauty  fame  forbearance  
śamo damo bhagaś ceti  
control of the mind  control of the senses  fortune  and  thus  
yat-saṅgād yāti saṅkṣayam  
by the company of whom  goes  to ruin

(85) “Truthfulness, purity, compassion, silence, intelligence, modesty, beauty, fame, forbearance, control of the mind and of the senses, and fortune — all go to ruin through the company of such people.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.31.33)

তেষ্বশান্তেষু মূঢ়েষু খণ্ডিতাত্মস্বসাধুষু ৷  
সঙ্গং ন কুর্য্যাচ্ছোচ্যেষু যোষিৎক্রীড়ামৃগেষু চ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

teṣv aśānteṣu mūḍheṣu  
with those  restless  foolish  
khaṇḍitātmasv asādhuṣu  
who have lost sight of the self  unholy  
saṅgaṁ na kuryāc chocyeṣu  
company  not  one should keep  pitiable  
yoṣit-krīḍā-mṛgeṣu ca  
of women  play-  pets  and

(86) “One should never keep company with the restless and the foolish, with the unholy who have lost sight of the self, with those pitiable men who are pets for women to play with.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.31.34)

ন তথাস্য ভবেন্মোহো বন্ধশ্চান্যপ্রসঙ্গতঃ ৷  
যোষিৎসঙ্গাদ্যথা পুংসো যথা তৎসঙ্গিসঙ্গতঃ ॥ ৮৭ ॥

na tathāsya bhaven moho  
not  so  for him  arises  delusion  
bandhaś cānya-prasaṅgataḥ  
bondage  and  from other  attachment  
yoṣit-saṅgād yathā puṁso  
from the company of women  as  for a man  
yathā tat-saṅgi-saṅgataḥ  
as  of those attached to them  from the company

(87) “From no other attachment does a man fall into such delusion and bondage as from the company of women and of those attached to women.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.31.35)

বরং হুতবহজ্বালা-পঞ্জরান্তর্ব্যবস্থিতিঃ ৷  
ন শৌরিচিন্তাবিমুখ-জনসম্বাসবৈশসম্ ॥ ৮৮ ॥

varaṁ huta-vaha-jvālā-  
better  of fire  of the flames  
pañjarāntar-vyavasthitiḥ  
inside a cage  to dwell  
na śauri-cintā-vimukha-  
not  from thought of Kṛṣṇa (Śauri)  turned away  
jana-samvāsa-vaiśasam  
of people  living with  the torment

(88) “Better to dwell in a cage amid blazing flames than to suffer the torment of living with people who have turned away from the thought of Kṛṣṇa.” (Kātyāyana-saṁhitā)

মা দ্রাক্ষীঃ ক্ষীণপুণ্যান্ ক্বচিদপি  
ভগবদ্ভক্তিহীনান্ মনুষ্যান্ ॥ ৮৯ ॥

mā drākṣīḥ kṣīṇa-puṇyān kvacid api  
do not  look at  whose piety is spent  ever  even  
bhagavad-bhakti-hīnān manuṣyān  
for the Lord  devotion  devoid of  people

(89) “Never even look upon people who are devoid of devotion to the Lord, whose piety is spent.” (Words of the Gosvāmipāda)[^33]

এত সব ছাড়ি’ আর বর্ণাশ্রম-ধর্ম্ম ৷  
অকিঞ্চন হঞা লয় কৃষ্ণৈক-শরণ ॥ ৯০ ॥

eta saba chāḍi’ āra varṇāśrama-dharma  
these  all  giving up  and  of varṇa and āśrama  the dharma  
akiñcana hañā laya kṛṣṇaika-śaraṇa  
owning nothing  becoming  he takes  of Kṛṣṇa alone  shelter

(90) Giving up all this, and the *dharma* of *varṇa* and *āśrama* as well, he becomes *akiñcana*, one who owns nothing, and takes shelter of Kṛṣṇa alone.[^34]

সর্ব্বধর্ম্মান্ পরিত্যজ্য মামেকং শরণং ব্রজ ৷  
অহং ত্বাং সর্ব্বপাপেভ্যো মোক্ষয়িষ্যামি মা শুচঃ ॥ ৯১ ॥

sarva-dharmān parityajya  
all  dharmas  giving up  
mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja  
to Me  alone  for shelter  come  
ahaṁ tvāṁ sarva-pāpebhyo  
I  you  from all  sins  
mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ  
shall free  do not  grieve

(91) “Give up all *dharmas* and take shelter of Me alone. I shall free you from all sins; do not grieve.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.66)[^35]

ভক্তবৎসল, কৃতজ্ঞ, সমর্থ, বদান্য ৷  
হেন কৃষ্ণ ছাড়ি’ পণ্ডিত নাহি ভজে অন্য ॥ ৯২ ॥

bhakta-vatsala, kṛtajña, samartha, vadānya  
affectionate to His devotees  grateful  all-capable  generous  
hena kṛṣṇa chāḍi’ paṇḍita nāhi bhaje anya  
such  Kṛṣṇa  leaving  a wise man  does not  worship  another

(92) Kṛṣṇa is affectionate to His devotees, grateful, all-capable and generous. No wise man would leave such a Kṛṣṇa to worship another.

কঃ পণ্ডিতস্ত্বদপরং শরণং সমীয়া-  
ভক্তপ্রিয়াদৃতগিরঃ সুহৃদঃ কৃতজ্ঞাৎ ৷  
সর্ব্বান্ দদাতি সুহৃদো ভজতোঽভিকামা-  
নাত্মানমপ্যুপচয়াপচয়ৌ ন যস্য ॥ ৯৩ ॥

kaḥ paṇḍitas tvad-aparaṁ śaraṇaṁ samīyād  
what  wise man  other than You  shelter  would approach  
bhakta-priyād ṛta-giraḥ suhṛdaḥ kṛtajñāt  
than the One who loves His devotees  true to His word  the friend  grateful  
sarvān dadāti suhṛdo bhajato ‘bhikāmān  
all  He gives  to His friends  who worship Him  desires  
ātmānam apy upacayāpacayau na yasya  
Himself  even  increase and decrease  not  of whom

(93) “What wise man would approach any shelter but You? You love Your devotees, You are true to Your word, You are their friend and are grateful. To the friends who worship You, You give all they desire, and even Your own self — and You neither increase nor diminish.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.48.26)

বিজ্ঞ-জনের হয় যদি কৃষ্ণগুণ-জ্ঞান ৷  
অন্য ত্যজি’ ভজে, তাতে উদ্ধব — প্রমাণ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

vijña-janera haya yadi kṛṣṇa-guṇa-jñāna  
of a wise  person  arises  if  of Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  knowledge  
anya tyaji’ bhaje, tāte uddhava — pramāṇa  
others  giving up  he worships (Him)  of that  Uddhava  the proof

(94) When a wise person comes to know Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, he gives up all else and worships Him. Of this Uddhava is the proof:

অহো বকী যং স্তনকালকূটং  
জিঘাংসয়াপায়য়দপ্যসাধ্বী ৷  
লেভে গতিং ধাত্র্যুচিতাং ততোঽন্যং  
কং বা দয়ালুং শরণং ব্রজেম ॥ ৯৫ ॥

aho bakī yaṁ stana-kāla-kūṭaṁ  
ah  Pūtanā (sister of Baka)  whom  from her breast  deadly poison  
jighāṁsayāpāyayad apy asādhvī  
wishing to kill  made drink  even though  wicked  
lebhe gatiṁ dhātry-ucitāṁ tato ‘nyaṁ  
attained  the destination  befitting a nurse  than Him  other  
kaṁ vā dayāluṁ śaraṇaṁ vrajema  
whom  indeed  merciful  for shelter  should we approach

(95) “Ah! Wishing to kill Him, the wicked Pūtanā made Him drink deadly poison from her breast — and still she attained the destination befitting His nurse. To whom else so merciful should we go for shelter?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.2.23)

শরণাগতের, অকিঞ্চনের — একই লক্ষণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে প্রবেশয়ে ‘আত্মসমর্পণ’ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

śaraṇāgatera, akiñcanera — ekai lakṣaṇa  
of the surrendered  of the one who owns nothing  the very same  characteristics  
tāra madhye praveśaye ‘ātma-samarpaṇa’  
of them  within  enters  self-offering

(96) The surrendered soul (*śaraṇāgata*) and the one who owns nothing (*akiñcana*) have the same characteristics; and within surrender there is also self-offering (*ātma-samarpaṇa*).

আনুকূল্যস্য সঙ্কল্পঃ প্রাতিকূল্যস্যবর্জ্জনম্ ৷  
রক্ষিষ্যতীতি বিশ্বাসো গোপ্তৃত্বে বরণং তথা ৷  
আত্মনিক্ষেপ-কার্পণ্যে ষড়্বিধা শরণাগতিঃ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

ānukūlyasya saṅkalpaḥ  
of what is favourable  acceptance  
prātikūlyasya varjanam  
of what is unfavourable  rejection  
rakṣiṣyatīti viśvāso goptṛtve varaṇaṁ tathā  
He will protect  thus  faith  as protector  acceptance  likewise  
ātma-nikṣepa-kārpaṇye ṣaḍ-vidhā śaraṇāgatiḥ  
offering of the self  humility  six kinds of  surrender

(97) “Surrender (*śaraṇāgati*) is of six kinds: the resolve to accept what is favourable to devotion; the rejection of what is unfavourable; faith that He will protect; acceptance of Him as one’s guardian; the offering of oneself; and humility.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 11.417)[^36]

তবাস্মীতি বদন্ বাচা তথৈব মনসা বিদন্ ৷  
তৎস্থানমাশ্রিতস্তন্বা মোদতে শরণাগতঃ ॥ ৯৮ ॥

tavāsmīti vadan vācā  
‘Yours  I am’  thus  saying  with speech  
tathaiva manasā vidan  
just so  with the mind  knowing  
tat-sthānam āśritas tanvā  
of Him  the abode  taking shelter of  with the body  
modate śaraṇāgataḥ  
rejoices  the surrendered soul

(98) “Saying with his words, ‘I am Yours’, knowing it so in his mind, and with his body taking shelter of the Lord’s abode, the surrendered soul rejoices.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 11.418)[^37]

শরণ লঞা করে কৃষ্ণে আত্মসমর্পণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ তাঁরে করে তৎকালে আত্মসম ॥ ৯৯ ॥

śaraṇa lañā kare kṛṣṇe ātma-samarpaṇa  
shelter  taking  he makes  to Kṛṣṇa  the offering of himself  
kṛṣṇa tām̐re kare tat-kāle ātma-sama  
Kṛṣṇa  him  makes  at that moment  like Himself

(99) Taking shelter, he offers himself to Kṛṣṇa, and at that very moment Kṛṣṇa makes him like Himself.[^38]

মর্ত্ত্যো যদা ত্যক্তসমস্তকর্ম্মা  
নিবেদিতাত্মা বিচিকীর্ষিতো মে ৷  
তদামৃতত্বং প্রতিপদ্যমানো  
ময়াত্মভূয়ায় চ কল্পতে বৈ ॥ ১০০ ॥

martyo yadā tyakta-samasta-karmā  
a mortal  when  having given up  all  works  
niveditātmā vicikīrṣito me  
having offered himself  whom I wish to exalt  by Me  
tadāmṛtatvaṁ pratipadyamāno  
then  immortality  attaining  
mayātma-bhūyāya ca kalpate vai  
with Me  for a nature like Mine  and  becomes fit  indeed

(100) “When a mortal gives up all works and offers himself to Me, I wish to exalt him. Then he attains immortality and becomes fit to share with Me a nature like My own.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.23.34)

এবে সাধনভক্তি-লক্ষণ শুন, সনাতন ৷  
যাহা হৈতে পাই কৃষ্ণপ্রেম-মহাধন ॥ ১০১ ॥

ebe sādhana-bhakti-lakṣaṇa śuna, sanātana  
now  of sādhana-bhakti  the characteristics  hear  Sanātana  
yāhā haite pāi kṛṣṇa-prema-mahā-dhana  
from which  one obtains  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the great treasure

(101) Now hear, Sanātana, the characteristics of *sādhana-bhakti*, devotion in practice, by which one obtains the great treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

কৃতিসাধ্যা ভবেৎ সাধ্যভাবা সা সাধনাভিধা ৷  
নিত্যসিদ্ধস্য ভাবস্য প্রাকট্যং হৃদি সাধ্যতা ॥ ১০২ ॥

kṛti-sādhyā bhavet sādhya-  
by action  to be accomplished  becomes  whose goal  
bhāvā sā sādhanābhidhā  
is bhāva  it  is called sādhana  
nitya-siddhasya bhāvasya  
of the eternally  perfect  bhāva  
prākaṭyaṁ hṛdi sādhyatā  
the manifestation  in the heart  is the attainment

(102) “Devotion carried out by the senses, whose goal is *bhāva*, is called *sādhana-bhakti*. *Bhāva* is eternally perfect; to ‘attain’ it is to make it manifest in the heart.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.2)

শ্রবণাদি-ক্রিয়া — তার ‘স্বরূপ’-লক্ষণ ৷  
‘তটস্থ’-লক্ষণে উপজায় প্রেমধন ॥ ১০৩ ॥

śravaṇādi-kriyā — tāra ‘svarūpa’-lakṣaṇa  
hearing and the rest  the activities  its  essential  characteristic  
‘taṭastha’-lakṣaṇe upajāya prema-dhana  
by the marginal  characteristic  arises  the treasure of love

(103) Hearing and the other activities are its essential characteristic (*svarūpa-lakṣaṇa*). Its marginal characteristic (*taṭastha-lakṣaṇa*) is that the treasure of love arises from it.

নিত্যসিদ্ধ কৃষ্ণপ্রেম ‘সাধ্য’ কভু নয় ৷  
শ্রবণাদি শুদ্ধচিত্তে করয়ে উদয় ॥ ১০৪ ॥

nitya-siddha kṛṣṇa-prema ‘sādhya’ kabhu naya  
eternally perfect  for Kṛṣṇa  love  something to be produced  ever  is not  
śravaṇādi śuddha-citte karaye udaya  
hearing and the rest  in the purified  heart  cause  its rising

(104) Love for Kṛṣṇa is eternally perfect; it is never something produced by effort. Hearing and the other practices make it rise in the purified heart.[^39]

এই ত’ সাধনভক্তি — দুই ত’ প্রকার ৷  
এক ‘বৈধী ভক্তি’, ‘রাগানুগা-ভক্তি’ আর ॥ ১০৫ ॥

ei ta’ sādhana-bhakti — dui ta’ prakāra  
this  indeed  sādhana-bhakti  two  indeed  kinds  
eka ‘vaidhī bhakti’, ‘rāgānugā-bhakti’ āra  
one  vaidhī-bhakti  rāgānugā-bhakti  the other

(105) This *sādhana-bhakti* is of two kinds: one is *vaidhī-bhakti*, devotion according to rules, and the other is *rāgānugā-bhakti*, devotion that follows spontaneous love.[^40]

রাগহীন জন ভজে শাস্ত্রের আজ্ঞায় ৷  
‘বৈধী-ভক্তি’ বলি’ তারে সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে গায় ॥ ১০৬ ॥

rāga-hīna jana bhaje śāstrera ājñāya  
of rāga  devoid  a person  worships  of scripture  by the command  
‘vaidhī-bhakti’ bali’ tāre sarva-śāstre gāya  
vaidhī-bhakti  calling  it  in all scriptures  they sing

(106) When a person in whom *rāga*, spontaneous love, has not awakened worships by the command of scripture, all the scriptures call it *vaidhī-bhakti*.

তস্মাদ্ভারত সর্ব্বাত্মা ভগবান্ হরিরীশ্বরঃ ৷  
শ্রোতব্যঃ কীর্ত্তিতব্যশ্চ স্মর্ত্তব্যশ্চেচ্ছতাভয়ম্ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

tasmād bhārata sarvātmā  
therefore  O descendant of Bharata  the Soul of all  
bhagavān harir īśvaraḥ  
the Lord  Hari  the Controller  
śrotavyaḥ kīrtitavyaś ca  
is to be heard of  is to be glorified  and  
smartavyaś cecchatābhayam  
is to be remembered  and  by one who desires  fearlessness

(107) “Therefore, O descendant of Bharata, one who desires fearlessness must hear about, glorify and remember the Lord Hari, the Controller and the Soul of all.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.1.5)

মুখবাহূরুপাদেভ্যঃ পুরুষস্যাশ্রমৈঃ সহ ৷  
চত্বারো জজ্ঞিরে বর্ণা গুণৈর্ব্বিপ্রাদয়ঃ পৃথক্ ॥ ১০৮ ॥

mukha-bāhūru-pādebhyaḥ  
from the mouth  arms  thighs  feet  
puruṣasyāśramaiḥ saha  
of the Puruṣa  the āśramas  together with  
catvāro jajñire varṇā  
four  were born  varṇas  
guṇair viprādayaḥ pṛthak  
by their guṇas  the brāhmaṇas and the rest  separately

(108) “From the mouth, arms, thighs and feet of the Puruṣa the four *varṇas*, beginning with the brāhmaṇas, were born together with the *āśramas*, each distinct by its qualities.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.2)[^41]

স্মর্ত্তব্যঃ সততং বিষ্ণুর্ব্বিস্মর্ত্তব্যো ন জাতুচিৎ ৷  
সর্ব্বে বিধিনিষেধাঃ স্যুরেতয়োরেব কিঙ্করাঃ ॥ ১০৯ ॥

smartavyaḥ satataṁ viṣṇur  
is to be remembered  always  Viṣṇu  
vismartavyo na jātucit  
is to be forgotten  not  ever  
sarve vidhi-niṣedhāḥ syur  
all  injunctions and prohibitions  should be  
etayor eva kiṅkarāḥ  
of these two  only  the servants

(109) “Viṣṇu is always to be remembered and never to be forgotten. All injunctions and prohibitions are servants of these two rules alone.” (Padma-purāṇa)

বিবিধাঙ্গ সাধনভক্তির বহু ত’ বিস্তার ৷  
সংক্ষেপে কহিয়ে কিছু সাধনাঙ্গ-সার ॥ ১১০ ॥

vividhāṅga sādhana-bhaktira bahu ta’ vistāra  
of many limbs  of sādhana-bhakti  great  indeed  the extent  
saṅkṣepe kahiye kichu sādhanāṅga-sāra  
in brief  I shall tell  something  of the limbs of practice  the essence

(110) *Sādhana-bhakti*, with its many limbs, is very vast. In brief I shall tell you something of the essence of its limbs.

গুরুপাদাশ্রয়, দীক্ষা, গুরুর সেবন ৷  
সদ্ধর্ম্মশিক্ষা-পৃচ্ছা, সাধুমার্গানুগমন ॥ ১১১ ॥

guru-pādāśraya, dīkṣā, gurura sevana  
shelter at the guru’s feet  initiation  of the guru  service  
sad-dharma-śikṣā-pṛcchā, sādhu-mārgānugamana  
of true dharma  learning  inquiry  of the saints  the path  following

(111) Taking shelter at the feet of the guru; initiation (*dīkṣā*); serving the guru; learning true *dharma* and inquiring about it; following the path of the saints;

কৃষ্ণপ্রীত্যে ভোগত্যাগ, কৃষ্ণতীর্থে বাস ৷  
যাবৎ নির্ব্বাহ-প্রতিগ্রহ, একাদশ্যুপবাস ॥ ১১২ ॥

kṛṣṇa-prītye bhoga-tyāga, kṛṣṇa-tīrthe vāsa  
for Kṛṣṇa’s pleasure  of enjoyment  giving up  in Kṛṣṇa’s holy place  living  
yāvat nirvāha-pratigraha, ekādaśy-upavāsa  
as much as  for maintenance  accepting  on Ekādaśī  fasting

(112) giving up one’s own enjoyment for Kṛṣṇa’s pleasure; living in a holy place of Kṛṣṇa; accepting only what is needed to maintain life; fasting on Ekādaśī;

ধাত্র্যশ্বত্থগোবিপ্র-বৈষ্ণব-পূজন ৷  
সেবা-নামাপরাধাদি দূরে বিসর্জ্জন ॥ ১১৩ ॥

dhātry-aśvattha-go-vipra-vaiṣṇava-pūjana  
the dhātrī  aśvattha  cows  brāhmaṇas  Vaiṣṇavas  honouring  
sevā-nāmāparādhādi dūre visarjana  
in service  to the Name  offences and the like  far away  casting off

(113) honouring the *dhātrī* (*āmalakī*) and *aśvattha* trees, the cows, the brāhmaṇas and the Vaiṣṇavas; casting far away offences in service, offences to the Holy Name and the like;

অবৈষ্ণব-সঙ্গত্যাগ, বহুশিষ্য না করিব ৷  
বহুগ্রন্থ-কলাভ্যাস-ব্যাখ্যান বর্জ্জিব ॥ ১১৪ ॥

avaiṣṇava-saṅga-tyāga, bahu-śiṣya nā kariba  
of non-Vaiṣṇavas  company  giving up  many  disciples  not  I shall make  
bahu-grantha-kalābhyāsa-vyākhyāna varjiba  
of many  books  partial study  expounding  I shall avoid

(114) giving up the company of non-Vaiṣṇavas; not taking many disciples; not studying bits of many books, and not expounding them for argument;

হানি লাভে সম, শোকাদির বশ না হইব ৷  
অন্যদেব, অন্যশাস্ত্র নিন্দা না করিব ॥ ১১৫ ॥

hāni lābhe sama, śokādira vaśa nā haiba  
in loss  in gain  even-minded  of grief and the like  under the sway  not  I shall be  
anya-deva, anya-śāstra nindā nā kariba  
other  gods  other  scriptures  blasphemy  not  I shall do

(115) remaining even-minded in loss and gain; not falling under the sway of grief and the like; not blaspheming other gods or other scriptures;

বিষ্ণু-বৈষ্ণব-নিন্দা, গ্রাম্যবার্ত্তা না শুনিব ৷  
প্রাণীমাত্রে মনোবাক্যে উদ্বেগ না দিব ॥ ১১৬ ॥

viṣṇu-vaiṣṇava-nindā, grāmya-vārtā nā śuniba  
of Viṣṇu  of Vaiṣṇavas  blasphemy  worldly  talk  not  I shall hear  
prāṇī-mātre mano-vākye udvega nā diba  
to any living being  in mind  in speech  distress  not  I shall give

(116) not listening to blasphemy of Viṣṇu or the Vaiṣṇavas, nor to worldly talk; not causing distress to any living being in mind or in speech;

শ্রবণ, কীর্ত্তন, স্মরণ, পূজন, বন্দন ৷  
পরিচর্য্যা, দাস্য, সখ্য, আত্মনিবেদন ॥ ১১৭ ॥

śravaṇa, kīrtana, smaraṇa, pūjana, vandana  
hearing  glorifying  remembering  worship  offering prayers  
paricaryā, dāsya, sakhya, ātma-nivedana  
attending  servitude  friendship  offering of the self

(117) hearing, glorifying, remembering, worship, offering prayers (*vandana*), attending on the Lord (*paricaryā*), servitude (*dāsya*), friendship (*sakhya*) and the offering of oneself (*ātma-nivedana*);

অগ্রে নৃত্য, গীত, বিজ্ঞপ্তি, দণ্ডবন্নতি ৷  
অভ্যুত্থান, অনুব্রজ্যা, তীর্থগৃহে গতি ॥ ১১৮ ॥

agre nṛtya, gīta, vijñapti, daṇḍavan-nati  
before (the Deity)  dancing  singing  opening the heart  prostrate obeisance  
abhyutthāna, anuvrajyā, tīrtha-gṛhe gati  
rising to greet  following behind  to holy places  to temples  going

(118) dancing and singing before the Deity; opening one’s heart to Him (*vijñapti*); bowing down flat; rising to greet Him; following behind Him; going to holy places and temples;

পরিক্রমা, স্তবপাঠ, জপ, সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
ধূপ-মাল্য-গন্ধ-মহাপ্রসাদ-ভোজন ॥ ১১৯ ॥

parikramā, stava-pāṭha, japa, saṅkīrtana  
circumambulation  of prayers  reciting  japa  congregational chanting  
dhūpa-mālya-gandha-mahāprasāda-bhojana  
of incense  garlands  fragrance  mahāprasāda  eating

(119) circumambulation; reciting prayers; *japa*; *saṅkīrtana*; smelling the incense, garlands and fragrances offered to the Lord; honouring *mahāprasāda*;

আরাত্রিক-মহোৎসব-শ্রীমূর্ত্তি-দর্শন ৷  
নিজপ্রিয়-দান, ধ্যান, তদীয়-সেবন ॥ ১২০ ॥

ārātrika-mahotsava-śrīmūrti-darśana  
of the ārati  festivals  of the Deity  seeing  
nija-priya-dāna, dhyāna, tadīya-sevana  
of what is dear to oneself  offering  meditation  of tadīya  service

(120) seeing the *ārati*, the festivals and the Deity (*śrī-mūrti*); offering what is dear to oneself; meditation; serving *tadīya*, what is His own.[^42]

‘তদীয়’ — তুলসী-বৈষ্ণব-মথুরা-ভাগবত ৷  
এই চারির সেবা হয় কৃষ্ণের অভিমত ॥ ১২১ ॥

‘tadīya’ — tulasī-vaiṣṇava-mathurā-bhāgavata  
tadīya  Tulasī  Vaiṣṇavas  Mathurā  the Bhāgavatam  
ei cārira sevā haya kṛṣṇera abhimata  
of these four  service  is  of Kṛṣṇa  the desire

(121) *Tadīya*, “His own,” means Tulasī, the Vaiṣṇavas, Mathurā and the *Bhāgavatam*. Kṛṣṇa desires service to these four.

কৃষ্ণার্থে অখিল চেষ্টা, তৎকৃপাবলোকন ৷  
জন্ম-দিনাদি-মহোৎসব লঞা ভক্তগণ ॥ ১২২ ॥

kṛṣṇārthe akhila ceṣṭā, tat-kṛpāvalokana  
for Kṛṣṇa  all  endeavours  of His  mercy  looking for  
janma-dinādi-mahotsava lañā bhakta-gaṇa  
of His birthday and the like  festivals  together with  the devotees

(122) Doing all one’s endeavours for Kṛṣṇa; looking for His mercy; celebrating His birthday and other festivals with the devotees;

সর্ব্বথা শরণাপত্তি, কার্ত্তিকাদি-ব্রত ৷  
‘চতুঃষষ্টি অঙ্গ’ এই পরম মহত্ত্ব ॥ ১২৩ ॥

sarvathā śaraṇāpatti, kārtikādi-vrata  
in every way  surrender  Kārttika and other  vows  
‘catuḥ-ṣaṣṭi aṅga’ ei parama mahattva  
sixty-four  limbs  these  supreme  greatness

(123) surrendering to Him in every way; observing Kārttika and other vows — these are the sixty-four limbs (*aṅgas*) of devotion, and supreme is their greatness.

সাধুসঙ্গ, নামকীর্ত্তন, ভাগবতশ্রবণ ৷  
মথুরাবাস, শ্রীমূর্ত্তির শ্রদ্ধায় সেবন ॥ ১২৪ ॥

sādhu-saṅga, nāma-kīrtana, bhāgavata-śravaṇa  
of the saints  the company  of the Name  chanting  the Bhāgavatam  hearing  
mathurā-vāsa, śrī-mūrtira śraddhāya sevana  
in Mathurā  living  of the Deity  with faith  service

(124) The company of saints; chanting the Holy Name; hearing the *Bhāgavatam*; living in Mathurā; serving the Deity with faith —

সকলসাধন-শ্রেষ্ঠ এই পঞ্চ অঙ্গ ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেম জন্মায় এই পাঁচের অল্প সঙ্গ ॥ ১২৫ ॥

sakala-sādhana-śreṣṭha ei pañca aṅga  
of all  practices  the best  these  five  limbs  
kṛṣṇa-prema janmāya ei pām̐cera alpa saṅga  
for Kṛṣṇa  love  gives birth to  of these  five  slight  contact

(125) these five limbs are the best of all practices. Even slight contact with these five gives birth to love for Kṛṣṇa.[^43]

সজাতীয়াশয়ে স্নিগ্ধে সাধৌ সঙ্গঃ স্বতো বরে ৷  
শ্রীমদ্ভাগবতার্থানামাস্বাদো রসিকৈঃ সহ ॥ ১২৬ ॥

sajātīyāśaye snigdhe sādhau saṅgaḥ svato vare  
of like aspiration  affectionate  with a saint  association  than oneself  superior  
śrīmad bhāgavatārthānām āsvādo rasikaiḥ saha  
of Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam  of the meanings  the relishing  with rasikas  together

(126) “One should associate with a saint who shares one’s aspiration, is affectionate and is superior to oneself, and one should relish the meaning of the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* together with *rasikas*, those who know its taste.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Pūrva-vibhāga, Sādhana-bhakti-laharī, 40)[^44]

শ্রদ্ধা বিশেষতঃ প্রীতিঃ শ্রীমূর্ত্তেরঙ্ঘ্রিসেবনে ৷  
নামসংকীর্ত্তনং শ্রীমন্মথুরামণ্ডলে স্থিতিঃ ॥ ১২৭ ॥

śraddhā viśeṣataḥ prītiḥ  
faith  special  love  
śrī-mūrter aṅghri-sevane  
of the Deity  of the feet  in the service  
nāma-saṅkīrtanaṁ śrīman-  
of the Name  congregational chanting  in the beautiful  
mathurā-maṇḍale sthitiḥ  
of Mathurā  region  residence

(127) “With faith and with special love one should serve the feet of the Deity, chant the Holy Name in *saṅkīrtana*, and live in the beautiful region of Mathurā.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Pūrva-vibhāga, Sādhana-bhakti-laharī, 41)[^45]

দুরূহাদ্ভুতবীর্য্যেঽস্মিন্ শ্রদ্ধা দূরেঽস্তু পঞ্চকে ৷  
যত্র স্বল্পোঽপি সম্বন্ধঃ সদ্ধিয়াং ভাবজন্মনে ॥ ১২৮ ॥

durūhādbhuta-vīrye ‘smin  
hard to grasp  wondrous  in power  in this  
śraddhā dūre ‘stu pañcake  
faith  far away  let it be  in the group of five  
yatra svalpo ‘pi sambandhaḥ  
where  slight  even  connection  
sad-dhiyāṁ bhāva-janmane  
for those of good understanding  for the birth of bhāva

(128) “The power of these five limbs is wondrous and hard to grasp. Let alone faith in them: even the slightest connection with them gives birth to *bhāva* in those of good understanding, who are free from offence.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Pūrva-vibhāga, Sādhana-bhakti-laharī, 87)[^46]

‘এক’ অঙ্গ সাধে, কেহ সাধে ‘বহু’ অঙ্গ ৷  
‘নিষ্ঠা’ হৈতে উপজয় প্রেমের তরঙ্গ ॥ ১২৯ ॥

‘eka’ aṅga sādhe, keha sādhe ‘bahu’ aṅga  
one  limb  practises (one)  someone  practises  many  limbs  
‘niṣṭhā’ haite upajaya premera taraṅga  
steadiness  from  arise  of love  the waves

(129) Some practise one limb, some practise many; from steadiness (*niṣṭhā*) arise the waves of love.

‘এক’ অঙ্গে সিদ্ধি পাইল বহু ভক্তগণ ৷  
অম্বরীষাদি ভক্তের ‘বহু’ অঙ্গ-সাধন ॥ ১৩০ ॥

‘eka’ aṅge siddhi pāila bahu bhakta-gaṇa  
by one  limb  perfection  attained  many  devotees  
ambarīṣādi bhaktera ‘bahu’ aṅga-sādhana  
Ambarīṣa and other  devotees’  of many  limbs  practice

(130) Many devotees have attained perfection through a single limb; devotees such as Ambarīṣa practised many limbs.

শ্রীবিষ্ণোঃ শ্রবণে পরীক্ষিদভবদ্বৈয়াসকিঃ কীর্ত্তনে  
প্রহ্লাদঃ স্মরণে তদঙ্ঘ্রিভজনে লক্ষ্মীঃ পৃথুঃ পূজনে ৷  
অক্রূরস্ত্বভিবন্দনে কপিপতির্দাস্যেঽথ সখ্যেঽর্জ্জুনঃ  
সর্ব্বস্বাত্মনিবেদনে বলিরভূৎ কৃষ্ণাপ্তিরেষাং পরম্ ॥ ১৩১ ॥

śrī-viṣṇoḥ śravaṇe parīkṣid abhavad vaiyāsakiḥ kīrtane  
of Śrī Viṣṇu  in hearing  Parīkṣit  was  the son of Vyāsa  in glorifying  
prahlādaḥ smaraṇe tad-aṅghri-bhajane lakṣmīḥ pṛthuḥ pūjane  
Prahlāda  in remembering  His  feet  in serving  Lakṣmī  Pṛthu  in worship  
akrūras tv abhivandane kapi-patir dāsye ‘tha sakhye ‘rjunaḥ  
Akrūra  and  in offering prayers  the lord of the monkeys  in servitude  and  in friendship  Arjuna  
sarva-svātma-nivedane balir abhūt kṛṣṇāptir eṣāṁ param  
of all he had and himself  in the offering  Bali  was  the attainment of Kṛṣṇa  of these  the highest

(131) “Parīkṣit attained perfection by hearing about Śrī Viṣṇu; Śukadeva, the son of Vyāsa, by glorifying Him; Prahlāda by remembering Him; Lakṣmī by serving His feet; Pṛthu by worship; Akrūra by offering prayers; Hanumān, lord of the monkeys, by servitude; Arjuna by friendship; and Bali by offering all he had and himself. All of them attained Kṛṣṇa in the highest way.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.265)[^47]

স বৈ মনঃ কৃষ্ণপদারবিন্দয়ো-  
র্ব্বচাংসি বৈকুণ্ঠগুণানুবর্ণনে ৷  
করৌ হরের্মন্দিরমার্জ্জনাদিষু  
শ্রুতিঞ্চকারাচ্যুতসৎকথোদয়ে ॥ ১৩২ ॥

sa vai manaḥ kṛṣṇa-padāravindayor  
he  indeed  the mind  of Kṛṣṇa  on the lotus feet  
vacāṁsi vaikuṇṭha-guṇānuvarṇane  
words  of the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha  of the qualities  in describing  
karau harer mandira-mārjanādiṣu  
the hands  of Hari  of the temple  in cleaning and the like  
śrutiṁ cakārācyuta-sat-kathodaye  
the ears  he engaged  about Acyuta  holy  narrations  in the telling

(132) “King Ambarīṣa engaged his mind on Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, his words in describing the qualities of the Lord of Vaikuṇṭha, his hands in cleaning Hari’s temple and similar service, and his ears in hearing the holy narrations of Acyuta.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.4.18)[^48]

মুকুন্দলিঙ্গালয়দর্শনে দৃশৌ  
তদ্ভৃত্যগাত্রস্পর্শেঽঙ্গসঙ্গমম্ ৷  
ঘ্রাণঞ্চ তৎপাদসরোজসৌরভে  
শ্রীমত্তুলস্যা রসনাং তদর্পিতে ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

mukunda-liṅgālaya-darśane dṛśau  
of Mukunda  of the images  of the temples  in seeing  the eyes  
tad-bhṛtya-gātra-sparśe ‘ṅga-saṅgamam  
of His  servants  of the bodies  in touching  the touch of his limbs  
ghrāṇaṁ ca tat-pāda-saroja-saurabhe  
the sense of smell  and  of His  feet  lotus  in the fragrance  
śrīmat-tulasyā rasanāṁ tad-arpite  
of the beautiful  Tulasī  the tongue  offered to Him  in what was

(133) “He engaged his eyes in seeing Mukunda’s Deities and temples, his body in touching the bodies of the Lord’s servants, his sense of smell in the fragrance of the beautiful Tulasī at the Lord’s lotus feet, and his tongue in what had been offered to Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.4.19)

পাদৌ হরেঃ ক্ষেত্রপদানুসর্পণে  
শিরো হৃষীকেশপদাভিবন্দনে ৷  
কামঞ্চ দাস্যে ন তু কামকাম্যয়া  
যথোত্তমঃশ্লোকজনাশ্রয়া রতিঃ ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

pādau hareḥ kṣetra-padānusarpaṇe  
the feet  of Hari  holy places  along the paths  in walking  
śiro hṛṣīkeśa-padābhivandane  
the head  of Hṛṣīkeśa  to the feet  in bowing  
kāmaṁ ca dāsye na tu kāma-kāmyayā  
desire  and  in servitude  not  but  for the sake of enjoyment  
yathottamaḥ śloka-janāśrayā ratiḥ  
so that  of Uttamaśloka  in the devotees  sheltered  attraction

(134) “He engaged his feet in walking to the Lord’s holy places, his head in bowing to the feet of Hṛṣīkeśa, and his desire in servitude, not in seeking enjoyment — so that there arose in him the attraction (*rati*) that takes shelter of the devotees of Uttamaśloka.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.4.20)[^49]

কাম ত্যজি’ কৃষ্ণ ভজে শাস্ত্র-আজ্ঞা মানি’ ৷  
দেব-ঋষি-পিতৃদিগের কভু নহে ঋণী ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

kāma tyaji’ kṛṣṇa bhaje śāstra-ājñā māni’  
desires  giving up  Kṛṣṇa  worships  of scripture  the command  honouring  
deva-ṛṣi-pitṛdigera kabhu nahe ṛṇī  
to the gods  the sages  the forefathers  ever  is not  a debtor

(135) One who gives up desires and worships Kṛṣṇa, honouring the command of scripture, is never in debt to the gods, the sages or the forefathers.

দেবর্ষিভূতাপ্তনৃণাং পিতৄণাং  
ন কিঙ্করো নায়মৃণী চ রাজন্ ৷  
সর্ব্বাত্মনা যঃ শরণং শরণ্যং  
গতো মুকুন্দং পরিহৃত্য কর্ত্তম্ ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

devarṣi-bhūtāpta-nṛṇāṁ pitṝṇāṁ  
of the gods  sages  beings  kinsmen  men  of the forefathers  
na kiṅkaro nāyam ṛṇī ca rājan  
not  a servant  nor  he  a debtor  and  O king  
sarvātmanā yaḥ śaraṇaṁ śaraṇyaṁ  
with his whole being  who  for shelter  to the giver of shelter  
gato mukundaṁ parihṛtya kartam  
has gone  to Mukunda  giving up  his duties

(136) “O king, one who has given up his duties and with his whole being has gone for shelter to Mukunda, the giver of shelter, is no longer a servant or a debtor to the gods, the sages, other beings, his kinsmen, mankind or the forefathers.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.41)

বিধি-ধর্ম্ম ছাড়ি’ ভজে কৃষ্ণের চরণ ৷  
নিষিদ্ধ পাপাচারে তার কভু নহে মন ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

vidhi-dharma chāḍi’ bhaje kṛṣṇera caraṇa  
of rules  the dharma  giving up  worships  of Kṛṣṇa  the feet  
niṣiddha pāpācāre tāra kabhu nahe mana  
to forbidden  sinful conduct  his  ever  does not go  mind

(137) One who gives up the *dharma* of rules and worships Kṛṣṇa’s feet never turns his mind to forbidden, sinful conduct.[^50]

অজ্ঞানে বা হয় যদি ‘পাপ’ উপস্থিত ৷  
কৃষ্ণ তাঁরে শুদ্ধ করে, না করায় প্রায়শ্চিত্ত ॥ ১৩৮ ॥

ajñāne vā haya yadi ‘pāpa’ upasthita  
through ignorance  or  happens  if  sin  occurs  
kṛṣṇa tām̐re śuddha kare, nā karāya prāyaścitta  
Kṛṣṇa  him  pure  makes  not  makes him perform  atonement

(138) And if through ignorance he should fall into ‘sin’, Kṛṣṇa purifies him; He does not make him perform atonement.

স্বপাদমূলং ভজতঃ প্রিয়স্য  
ত্যক্তান্যভাবস্য হরিঃ পরেশঃ ৷  
বিকর্ম্ম যচ্চোৎপতিতং কথঞ্চিৎ  
ধুনোতি সর্ব্বং হৃদি সন্নিবিষ্টঃ ॥ ১৩৯ ॥

sva-pāda-mūlaṁ bhajataḥ priyasya  
His own  of the feet  the root  of one who worships  the dear one  
tyaktānya-bhāvasya hariḥ pareśaḥ  
who has given up  other  feelings  Hari  the Supreme Lord  
vikarma yac cotpatitaṁ kathañcit  
sinful act  which  and  has occurred  somehow  
dhunoti sarvaṁ hṛdi sanniviṣṭaḥ  
removes  all  in the heart  residing

(139) “Should a dear devotee who worships the root of His feet, having given up every other feeling, somehow commit a sinful act, Hari, the Supreme Lord, seated in his heart, removes it all.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.42)

জ্ঞান-বৈরাগ্যাদি — ভক্তির কভু নহে ‘অঙ্গ’ ৷  
অহিংসা-যম-নিয়মাদি বুলে কৃষ্ণভক্ত-সঙ্গ ॥ ১৪০ ॥

jñāna-vairāgyādi — bhaktira kabhu nahe ‘aṅga’  
knowledge  renunciation and the like  of bhakti  ever  are not  limbs  
ahiṁsā-yama-niyamādi bule kṛṣṇa-bhakta-saṅga  
non-violence  yama  niyama and the like  accompany  of Kṛṣṇa’s devotees  the company

(140) Knowledge (jñāna), renunciation (*vairāgya*) and the like are never limbs of devotion. Non-violence, restraint (*yama*), regulation (*niyama*) and the like follow along in the company of Kṛṣṇa’s devotees.[^51]

তস্মান্মদ্ভক্তিযুক্তস্য যোগিনো বৈ মদাত্মনঃ ৷  
ন জ্ঞানং ন চ বৈরাগ্যং প্রায়ঃ শ্রেয়ো ভবেদিহ ॥ ১৪১ ॥

tasmān mad-bhakti-yuktasya  
therefore  in devotion to Me  engaged  
yogino vai mad-ātmanaḥ  
of a yogī  indeed  whose mind is in Me  
na jñānaṁ na ca vairāgyaṁ  
not  knowledge  nor  and  renunciation  
prāyaḥ śreyo bhaved iha  
as a rule  the highest good  is  here

(141) “Therefore, for the *yogī* engaged in devotion to Me, whose mind is in Me, neither knowledge nor renunciation is, as a rule, the means to the highest good in this world.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.31)[^52]

এতে ন হ্যদ্ভুতা ব্যাধ তবাহিংসাদয়ো গুণাঃ ৷  
হরিভক্তৌ প্রবৃত্তা যে ন তে স্যুঃ পরতাপিনঃ ॥ ১৪২ ॥

ete na hy adbhutā vyādha  
these  not  indeed  wonderful  O hunter  
tavāhiṁsādayo guṇāḥ  
your  non-violence and the other  virtues  
hari-bhaktau pravṛttā ye  
in devotion to Hari  engaged  who  
na te syuḥ para-tāpinaḥ  
not  they  are  causing pain to others

(142) “O hunter, it is no wonder that you have non-violence and these other virtues: those who are engaged in devotion to Hari never cause pain to others.” (Skanda-purāṇa)

বৈধীভক্তি-সাধনের কহিলুঁ বিবরণ ৷  
রাগানুগা-ভক্তির লক্ষণ শুন, সনাতন ॥ ১৪৩ ॥

vaidhī-bhakti-sādhanera kahilum̐ vivaraṇa  
of vaidhī-bhakti  of the practice  I have told  the description  
rāgānugā-bhaktira lakṣaṇa śuna, sanātana  
of rāgānugā-bhakti  the characteristics  hear  Sanātana

(143) I have described to you the practice of *vaidhī-bhakti*. Now, Sanātana, hear the characteristics of *rāgānugā-bhakti*.

রাগাত্মিকা-ভক্তি — ‘মুখ্যা’ ব্রজবাসি-জনে ৷  
তার অনুগত ভক্তির ‘রাগানুগা’-নামে ॥ ১৪৪ ॥

rāgātmikā-bhakti — ‘mukhyā’ vraja-vāsi-jane  
rāgātmikā-bhakti  pre-eminent  in the people of Vraja  
tāra anugata bhaktira ‘rāgānugā’-nāme  
it  following  of the devotion  rāgānugā  by name

(144) *Rāgātmikā-bhakti*, devotion whose very nature is *rāga*, is found pre-eminently in the people of Vraja. Devotion that follows it is called *rāgānugā*.[^53]

ইষ্টে স্বারসিকী রাগঃ পরমাবিষ্টতা ভবেৎ ৷  
তন্ময়ী যা ভবেদ্ভক্তিঃ সাত্র রাগাত্মিকোদিতা ॥ ১৪৫ ॥

iṣṭe svārasikī rāgaḥ  
for the beloved  natural  rāga  
paramāviṣṭatā bhavet  
supreme absorption  is  
tan-mayī yā bhaved bhaktiḥ  
with it  filled  which  is  devotion  
sātra rāgātmikoditā  
it  here  rāgātmikā  is called

(145) “Natural, intense absorption in one’s beloved is *rāga*. Devotion filled with such *rāga* is here called *rāgātmikā*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.272)[^54]

ইষ্টে ‘গাঢ়-তৃষ্ণা’ — রাগের স্বরূপ-লক্ষণ ৷  
ইষ্টে ‘আবিষ্টতা’ — তটস্থ-লক্ষণ কথন ॥ ১৪৬ ॥

iṣṭe ‘gāḍha-tṛṣṇā’ — rāgera svarūpa-lakṣaṇa  
for the beloved  intense  thirst  of rāga  the essential  characteristic  
iṣṭe ‘āviṣṭatā’ — taṭastha-lakṣaṇa kathana  
in the beloved  absorption  the marginal  characteristic  is said to be

(146) Intense thirst for the beloved is the essential characteristic of *rāga*; absorption in the beloved is said to be its marginal characteristic.

রাগময়ী-ভক্তির হয় ‘রাগাত্মিকা’-নাম ৷  
তাহা শুনি’ লুব্ধ হয় কোন ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ১৪৭ ॥

rāgamayī-bhaktira haya ‘rāgātmikā’-nāma  
filled with rāga  of devotion  is  rāgātmikā  the name  
tāhā śuni’ lubdha haya kona bhāgyavān  
of it  hearing  filled with longing  becomes  some  fortunate one

(147) Devotion full of *rāga* is named *rāgātmikā*. Hearing of it, some fortunate soul is filled with longing (*lobha*) for it.

লোভে ব্রজবাসীর ভাবে করে অনুগতি ৷  
শাস্ত্রযুক্তি নাহি মানে রাগানুগার প্রকৃতি ॥ ১৪৮ ॥

lobhe vraja-vāsīra bhāve kare anugati  
out of longing  of the people of Vraja  the mood  he makes  following  
śāstra-yukti nāhi māne rāgānugāra prakṛti  
of scripture  the arguments  does not  heed  of the rāgānugā  the nature

(148) Out of that longing he follows the mood of the people of Vraja. It is the nature of the *rāgānugā* devotee not to depend on the arguments of scripture.[^55]

বিরাজন্তীমভিব্যক্তাং ব্রজবাসিজনাদিষু ৷  
রাগাত্মিকামনুসৃতা যা সা রাগানুগোচ্যতে ॥ ১৪৯ ॥

virājantīm abhivyaktāṁ  
shining  clearly manifest  
vraja-vāsi-janādiṣu  
in the people of Vraja and others  
rāgātmikām anusṛtā  
rāgātmikā  following  
yā sā rāgānugocyate  
which  it  rāgānugā  is called

(149) “Devotion that follows the *rāgātmikā* devotion shining, clearly manifest, in the people of Vraja is called *rāgānugā*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.270)

তত্তদ্ভাবাদিমাধুর্য্যে শ্রুতে ধীর্যদপেক্ষতে ৷  
নাত্র শাস্ত্রং ন যুক্তিঞ্চ তল্লোভোৎপত্তিলক্ষণম্ ॥ ১৫০ ॥

tat-tad-bhāvādi-mādhurye  
of their  respective  moods and the like  in the sweetness  
śrute dhīr yad apekṣate  
when it is heard  the intellect  that  depends on  
nātra śāstraṁ na yuktiṁ ca  
not  here  scripture  nor  reasoning  and  
tal lobhotpatti-lakṣaṇam  
that  of longing  of the arising  the sign

(150) “When one hears of the sweetness of their moods and the rest, and the intellect is drawn to it without depending on scripture or reasoning — that is the sign that longing has arisen.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.292)[^56]

বাহ্য, অভ্যন্তর, — ইহার দুই ত’ সাধন ৷  
‘বাহ্যে’ সাধক-দেহে করে শ্রবণ-কীর্ত্তন ॥ ১৫১ ॥

bāhya, abhyantara, — ihāra dui ta’ sādhana  
external  internal  of it  two  indeed  practices  
‘bāhye’ sādhaka-dehe kare śravaṇa-kīrtana  
externally  in the body of a sādhaka  he does  hearing and chanting

(151) Its practice is of two kinds, external and internal. Externally, in the body of a practitioner (*sādhaka*), he hears and chants;[^57]

‘মনে’ নিজ-সিদ্ধদেহ করিয়া ভাবন ৷  
রাত্রি-দিনে করে ব্রজে কৃষ্ণের সেবন ॥ ১৫২ ॥

‘mane’ nija-siddha-deha kariyā bhāvana  
in the mind  his own perfected body  making  contemplation  
rātri-dine kare vraje kṛṣṇera sevana  
night and day  he does  in Vraja  to Kṛṣṇa  service

(152) and in his mind, contemplating his own perfected spiritual body (*siddha-deha*), he serves Kṛṣṇa in Vraja night and day.

সেবা সাধকরূপেণ সিদ্ধরূপেণ চাত্র হি ৷  
তদ্ভাবলিপ্সুনা কার্য্যা ব্রজলোকানুসারতঃ ॥ ১৫৩ ॥

sevā sādhaka-rūpeṇa  
service  in the form of a sādhaka  
siddha-rūpeṇa cātra hi  
in the perfected form  and  here  indeed  
tad-bhāva-lipsunā kāryā  
for their bhāva  by one who longs  is to be done  
vraja-lokānusārataḥ  
the people of Vraja  following

(153) “One who longs for the *bhāva* of the people of Vraja should serve, following them, both in the form of a *sādhaka* and in the perfected form.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.295)

নিজাভীষ্ট কৃষ্ণপ্রেষ্ঠ পাছে ত’ লাগিয়া ৷  
নিরন্তর সেবা করে অন্তর্ম্মনা হঞা ॥ ১৫৪ ॥

nijābhīṣṭa kṛṣṇa-preṣṭha pāche ta’ lāgiyā  
his own chosen  of Kṛṣṇa  dear one  behind  indeed  keeping close  
nirantara sevā kare antarmanā hañā  
constantly  service  he does  with mind turned within  becoming

(154) Keeping close behind the dear one of Kṛṣṇa whom he has chosen as his own ideal, he serves without cease, his mind turned within.[^58]

কৃষ্ণং স্মরন্ জনঞ্চাস্য প্রেষ্ঠং নিজসমীহিতম্ ৷  
তত্তৎকথা-রতশ্চাসৌ কুর্য্যাদ্বাসং ব্রজে সদা ॥ ১৫৫ ॥

kṛṣṇaṁ smaran janaṁ cāsya  
Kṛṣṇa  remembering  the person  and  His  
preṣṭhaṁ nija-samīhitam  
dearest  his own chosen  
tat-tat-kathā-rataś cāsau  
in their  respective  narrations  absorbed  and  he  
kuryād vāsaṁ vraje sadā  
should make  residence  in Vraja  always

(155) “Remembering Kṛṣṇa and that dearest devotee of His whom he has chosen as his own, and absorbed in narrations about them, he should always live in Vraja.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.294)

দাস-সখা-পিত্রাদি-প্রেয়সীর গণ ৷  
রাগমার্গে নিজ-নিজ-ভাবের গণন ॥ ১৫৬ ॥

dāsa-sakhā-pitrādi-preyasīra gaṇa  
of servants  friends  parents and others  beloveds  the groups  
rāga-mārge nija-nija-bhāvera gaṇana  
on the path of rāga  of each one’s own  bhāva  the reckoning

(156) Servants, friends, parents and the others, and the beloveds: on the path of *rāga* each devotee is reckoned among them according to his own *bhāva*.[^59]

ন কর্হিচিন্মৎপরাঃ শান্তরূপে  
নঙ্ক্ষ্যন্তি নো মেঽনিমিষো লেঢ়ি হেতিঃ ৷  
যেষামহং প্রিয় আত্মা সুতশ্চ  
সখা গুরুঃ সুহৃদো দৈবমিষ্টম্ ॥ ১৫৭ ॥

na karhicin mat-parāḥ śānta-rūpe  
never  ever  devoted to Me  in the abode of peace  
naṅkṣyanti no me ‘nimiṣo leḍhi hetiḥ  
will perish  not  My  time  devours  weapon  
yeṣām ahaṁ priya ātmā sutaś ca  
for whom  I  the beloved  the Self  son  and  
sakhā guruḥ suhṛdo daivam iṣṭam  
friend  guru  well-wisher  deity  the desired

(157) (Lord Kapila to Devahūti:) “Those devoted to Me, for whom I am the beloved, the Self, son, friend, guru, well-wisher, deity and all they desire, are never lost in My abode of peace, and My weapon, time, does not devour them.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.38)[^60]

পতিপুত্রসুহৃদ্ভ্রাতৃপিতৃবন্মিত্রবদ্ধরিম্ ৷  
যে ধ্যায়ন্তি সদোদ্যুক্তাস্তেভ্যোঽপীহ নমো নমঃ ॥ ১৫৮ ॥

pati-putra-suhṛd-bhrātṛ-  
husband  son  well-wisher  brother  
pitṛvan mitravad dharim  
as father  as friend  on Hari  
ye dhyāyanti sadodyuktās  
who  meditate  always  zealous  
tebhyo ‘pīha namo namaḥ  
to them  even  here  obeisance  obeisance

(158) “Again and again I bow to those in this world who always zealously meditate on Hari as their husband, son, well-wisher, brother, father or friend.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.308)[^61]

এইমত করে যেবা রাগানুগা-ভক্তি ৷  
কৃষ্ণের চরণে তাঁর উপজয় ‘প্রীতি’ ॥ ১৫৯ ॥

ei mata kare yebā rāgānugā-bhakti  
in this  way  practises  whoever  rāgānugā-bhakti  
kṛṣṇera caraṇe tām̐ra upajaya ‘prīti’  
of Kṛṣṇa  for the feet  in him  arises  love

(159) In whoever practises *rāgānugā-bhakti* in this way, love (*prīti*) for Kṛṣṇa’s feet arises.

প্রীত্যঙ্কুরে ‘রতি’, ‘ভাব’ — হয় দুই নাম ৷  
যাহা হৈতে বশ হন শ্রীভগবান্ ॥ ১৬০ ॥

prīty-aṅkure ‘rati’, ‘bhāva’ — haya dui nāma  
of love  in the sprout  rati  bhāva  are  two  names  
yāhā haite vaśa hana śrī-bhagavān  
by which  subdued  becomes  Śrī Bhagavān

(160) When love is still a sprout, it has two names, *rati* and *bhāva*; by it Śrī Bhagavān Himself is won over.[^62]

যাহা হৈতে পাই কৃষ্ণের প্রেম-সেবন ৷  
এত ত’ কহিলুঁ ‘অভিধেয়’-বিবরণ ॥ ১৬১ ॥

yāhā haite pāi kṛṣṇera prema-sevana  
by which  one obtains  to Kṛṣṇa  in love  service  
eta ta’ kahilum̐ ‘abhidheya’-vivaraṇa  
thus  indeed  I have told  of the abhidheya  the description

(161) That by which one obtains loving service to Kṛṣṇa (*prema-sevā*) — thus I have described the *abhidheya*, the means.[^63]

অভিধেয়, সাধন-ভক্তি এবে কহিলুঁ সনাতন ৷  
সংক্ষেপে কহিলুঁ, বিস্তার না যায় বর্ণন ॥ ১৬২ ॥

abhidheya, sādhana-bhakti ebe kahilum̐ sanātana  
the abhidheya  sādhana-bhakti  now  I have told  Sanātana  
saṅkṣepe kahilum̐, vistāra nā yāya varṇana  
in brief  I have told it  in full  not  can be  described

(162) Now, Sanātana, I have told you of the *abhidheya*, *sādhana-bhakti*. I have told it in brief; it cannot be described in full.”[^64]

অভিধেয় সাধনভক্তি শুনে যেই জন ৷  
অচিরাৎ পায় সেই কৃষ্ণপ্রেমধন ॥ ১৬৩ ॥

abhidheya sādhana-bhakti śune yei jana  
the abhidheya  sādhana-bhakti  hears  whatever  person  
acirāt pāya sei kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana  
very soon  obtains  that one  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the treasure

(163) Whoever hears of *sādhana-bhakti*, the *abhidheya*, very soon obtains the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১৬৪ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(164) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
