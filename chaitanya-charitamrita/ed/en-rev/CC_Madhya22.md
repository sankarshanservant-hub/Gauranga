“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 22  
**The Discourse on the Truth of Bhakti as the Abhidheya**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যদেবং তং করুণার্ণবম্ ৷  
কলাবপ্যতিগূঢ়েয়ং ভক্তির্যেন প্রকাশিতা ॥ ১ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
devaṁ taṁ karuṇārṇavam  
the Lord  Him  the ocean of mercy  
kalāv apy ati-gūḍheyaṁ  
in Kali  even  this most hidden  
bhaktir yena prakāśitā  
bhakti  by whom  was revealed

(1) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, the ocean of mercy, by whom this most hidden bhakti was revealed even in the age of Kali.[^1]

জয় জয় শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

এই ত’ কহিলুঁ সম্বন্ধ-তত্ত্বের বিচার ৷  
বেদশাস্ত্রে উপদেশে, কৃষ্ণ — এক সার ॥ ৩ ॥

ei ta’ kahilum̐ sambandha-tattvera vicāra  
thus  indeed  I have told  of sambandha-tattva  the analysis  
veda-śāstre upadeśe, kṛṣṇa — eka sāra  
of the Vedas and śāstras  in the teaching  Kṛṣṇa  the one  essence

(3) “Thus I have explained to you the truth of *sambandha*. In the teaching of the Vedas and the śāstras, Kṛṣṇa is the one essence.[^3]

এবে কহি, শুন, অভিধেয়-লক্ষণ ৷  
যাহা হৈতে পাই — কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণপ্রেমধন ॥ ৪ ॥

ebe kahi, śuna, abhidheya-lakṣaṇa  
now  I speak  listen  of abhidheya  the characteristics  
yāhā haite pāi — kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-prema-dhana  
which  through  one attains  Kṛṣṇa  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa

(4) Now listen as I describe *abhidheya*, the means by which one attains Kṛṣṇa and the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণভক্তি — অভিধেয়, সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
অতএব মুনিগণ করিয়াছে নিশ্চয় ॥ ৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhakti — abhidheya, sarva-śāstre kaya  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is the abhidheya  all the scriptures  declare  
ataeva muni-gaṇa kariyāche niścaya  
therefore  the sages  have made  the conclusion

(5) All the scriptures declare that devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the *abhidheya*, and so the sages have concluded.

শ্রুতির্মাতা পৃষ্টা দিশতি ভবদারাধনবিধিং  
যথা মাতুর্ব্বাণী স্মৃতিরপি তথা বক্তি ভগিনী ৷  
পুরাণাদ্যা যে বা সহজনিবহাস্তে তদনুগা  
অতঃ সত্যং জ্ঞাতং মুরহর ভবানেব শরণম্ ॥ ৬ ॥

śrutir mātā pṛṣṭā diśati bhavad-ārādhana-vidhiṁ  
śruti  the mother  when asked  points out  of Your worship  the method  
yathā mātur vāṇī smṛtir api tathā vakti bhaginī  
as  of the mother  the words  smṛti  too  so  speaks  the sister  
purāṇādyā ye vā sahaja-nivahās te tad-anugā  
the Purāṇas and the rest  which  and  the brothers  they  following her  
ataḥ satyaṁ jñātaṁ murahara bhavān eva śaraṇam  
therefore  truly  it is known  O slayer of Mura  You  alone  the shelter

(6) “When Mother Śruti is asked, she points out the way to worship You. Smṛti, her sister, speaks just as the mother does, and the Purāṇas and the rest, like brothers, follow her. Therefore, O slayer of Mura, I have truly understood that You alone are my shelter.” (Words of a sage)[^4]

অদ্বয়জ্ঞান-তত্ত্ব কৃষ্ণ — স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
‘স্বরূপ-শক্তি’রূপে তাঁর হয় অবস্থান ॥ ৭ ॥

advaya-jñāna-tattva kṛṣṇa — svayaṁ bhagavān  
the Truth of nondual knowledge  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān  
‘svarūpa-śakti’-rūpe tām̐ra haya avasthāna  
of svarūpa-śakti  in the form  His  is  abiding

(7) Kṛṣṇa, the nondual Truth (*advaya-jñāna-tattva*), is Bhagavān Himself. He abides in the form of His own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*).[^5]

স্বাংশ-বিভিন্নাংশ-রূপে হঞা বিস্তার ৷  
অনন্ত বৈকুণ্ঠ-ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে করেন বিহার ॥ ৮ ॥

svāṁśa-vibhinnāṁśa-rūpe hañā vistāra  
as His own parts and separated parts  in the form  having become  expanded  
ananta vaikuṇṭha-brahmāṇḍe karena vihāra  
in endless  Vaikuṇṭhas and universes  He performs  pastimes

(8) Expanding Himself as His own parts (*svāṁśa*) and separated parts (*vibhinnāṁśa*), He sports in endless Vaikuṇṭhas and universes.[^6]

স্বাংশ-বিস্তার — চতুর্ব্ব্যূহ, অবতারগণ ৷  
বিভিন্নাংশ জীব — তাঁর শক্তিতে গণন ॥ ৯ ॥

svāṁśa-vistāra — catur-vyūha, avatāra-gaṇa  
the expansion of His own parts  the catur-vyūha  the host of avatāras  
vibhinnāṁśa jīva — tām̐ra śaktite gaṇana  
the separated part  the jīva  His  among the energies  is counted

(9) His own parts are the *catur-vyūha* and the host of avatāras. The jīva is His separated part and is counted among His energies.

সেই বিভিন্নাংশ জীব — দুই ত’ প্রকার ৷  
এক — ‘নিত্যমুক্ত’, এক — ‘নিত্য-সংসার’ ॥ ১০ ॥

sei vibhinnāṁśa jīva — dui ta’ prakāra  
those  separated parts  the jīvas  two  indeed  kinds  
eka — ‘nitya-mukta’, eka — ‘nitya-saṁsāra’  
one  eternally liberated  one  eternally in worldly existence

(10) Those jīvas, His separated parts, are of two kinds: some are eternally liberated (*nitya-mukta*), and some are eternally in worldly existence (*nitya-saṁsāra*).

‘নিত্যমুক্ত’ — নিত্য কৃষ্ণচরণে উন্মুখ ৷  
‘কৃষ্ণ-পারিষদ’ নাম, ভুঞ্জে সেবা-সুখ ॥ ১১ ॥

‘nitya-mukta’ — nitya kṛṣṇa-caraṇe unmukha  
the eternally liberated  ever  toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet  turned  
‘kṛṣṇa-pāriṣada’ nāma, bhuñje sevā-sukha  
Kṛṣṇa’s associates  named  they enjoy  the joy of service

(11) The eternally liberated are ever turned toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet. They are called Kṛṣṇa’s associates (*pārṣadas*), and they enjoy the happiness of serving Him.

‘নিত্যবদ্ধ’ — কৃষ্ণ হৈতে নিত্য-বহির্ম্মুখ ৷  
‘নিত্যসংসার’, ভুঞ্জে নরকাদি-দুঃখ ॥ ১২ ॥

‘nitya-baddha’ — kṛṣṇa haite nitya-bahirmukha  
the eternally bound  from Kṛṣṇa  eternally turned away  
‘nitya-saṁsāra’, bhuñje narakādi-duḥkha  
eternally in worldly existence  they suffer  the miseries of hell and the like

(12) The eternally bound (*nitya-baddha*) jīva is forever turned away from Kṛṣṇa. Eternally caught in worldly existence, he suffers the miseries of hell and the like.

সেই দোষে মায়া-পিশাচী দণ্ড করে তারে ৷  
আধ্যাত্মিকাদি তাপত্রয় তারে জারি’ মারে ॥ ১৩ ॥

sei doṣe māyā-piśācī daṇḍa kare tāre  
for that  fault  the witch Māyā  punishment  inflicts  on him  
ādhyātmikādi tāpa-traya tāre jāri’ māre  
beginning with those of body and mind  the threefold miseries  him  wearing down  torment

(13) For this fault the witch Māyā punishes him: the threefold miseries, beginning with those of body and mind (*ādhyātmika*), wear him down and torment him.

কাম-ক্রোধের দাস হঞা তার লাথি খায় ৷  
ভ্রমিতে ভ্রমিতে যদি সাধু-বৈদ্য পায় ॥ ১৪ ॥

kāma-krodhera dāsa hañā tāra lāthi khāya  
of lust and anger  the servant  having become  her  kicks  he suffers  
bhramite bhramite yadi sādhu-vaidya pāya  
wandering  wandering  if  a sādhu-physician  he finds

(14) Having become the servant of lust and anger, he suffers her kicks. But if, wandering on and on, he meets a sādhu, a physician of the soul,

তাঁর উপদেশ-মন্ত্রে পিশাচী পলায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তি পায়, তবে কৃষ্ণ-নিকট যায় ॥ ১৫ ॥

tām̐ra upadeśa-mantre piśācī palāya  
his  by the mantra of instruction  the witch  flees  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti pāya, tabe kṛṣṇa-nikaṭa yāya  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  he obtains  then  near Kṛṣṇa  he goes

(15) by the mantra of that sādhu’s instruction the witch flees. The soul obtains devotion to Kṛṣṇa and then draws near to Kṛṣṇa.

কামাদীনাং কতি ন কতিধা পালিতা দুর্নিদেশা-  
স্তেষাং জাতা ময়ি ন করুণা ন ত্রপা নোপশান্তিঃ ৷  
উৎসৃজ্যৈতানথ যদুপতে সাম্প্রতং লব্ধবুদ্ধি-  
স্ত্বামায়াতঃ শরণমভয়ং মাং নিযুঙ্ক্ষ্বাত্মদাস্যে ॥ ১৬ ॥

kāmādīnāṁ kati na katidhā pālitā durnideśās  
of lust and the rest  how many  not  in how many ways  were obeyed  the evil commands  
teṣāṁ jātā mayi na karuṇā na trapā nopaśāntiḥ  
of them  arose  toward me  no  pity  no  shame  nor peace  
utsṛjyaitān atha yadu-pate sāmprataṁ labdha-buddhis  
having abandoned them  now  O Lord of the Yadus  at present  having gained good sense  
tvām āyātaḥ śaraṇam abhayaṁ māṁ niyuṅkṣvātma-dāsye  
to You  I have come  for shelter  fearless  me  engage in Your service

(16) “How many evil commands of lust and the rest have I not obeyed, and in how many ways! Yet they never took pity on me, nor did I feel shame or find peace. Now, O Lord of the Yadus, I have cast them off; good sense has dawned in me, and I have come to You, the fearless shelter. Engage me in Your service.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 6)

কৃষ্ণভক্তি হয় অভিধেয়-প্রধান ৷  
ভক্তিমুখ-নিরীক্ষক কর্ম্ম-যোগ-জ্ঞান ॥ ১৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhakti haya abhidheya-pradhāna  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is  the chief abhidheya  
bhakti-mukha-nirīkṣaka karma-yoga-jñāna  
on the face of bhakti  looking  karma  yoga  jñāna

(17) Devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the chief *abhidheya*. Karma, yoga and jñāna all look to the face of bhakti for their results.[^7]

এই সব সাধনের অতি তুচ্ছ বল ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তি বিনা তাহা দিতে নারে ফল ॥ ১৮ ॥

ei saba sādhanera ati tuccha bala  
of these  all  practices  very  feeble  the strength  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti vinā tāhā dite nāre phala  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without  they  to give  cannot  fruit

(18) The strength of all these practices is very feeble: without devotion to Kṛṣṇa they cannot give any fruit.

নৈষ্কর্ম্ম্যমপ্যচ্যুতভাব-বর্জ্জিতং  
ন শোভতে জ্ঞানমলং নিরঞ্জনম্ ৷  
কুতঃ পুনঃ শশ্বদভদ্রমীশ্বরে  
ন চার্পিতং কর্ম্ম যদপ্যকারণম্ ॥ ১৯ ॥

naiṣkarmyam apy acyuta-bhāva-varjitaṁ  
freedom from action  even  love for Acyuta  devoid of  
na śobhate jñānam alaṁ nirañjanam  
not  shines  knowledge  at all  stainless  
kutaḥ punaḥ śaśvad abhadram īśvare  
how then  again  ever  inauspicious  to the Lord  
na cārpitaṁ karma yad apy akāraṇam  
not  offered  action  which  even  free of motive

(19) “Even stainless knowledge, free from all action (*naiṣkarmya*), does not shine if it is devoid of love for Acyuta. How then can action shine, ever inauspicious as it is, if it is not offered to the Lord, even when it is free of selfish motive?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.5.12)[^8]

তপস্বিনো দানপরা যশস্বিনো  
মনস্বিনো মন্ত্রবিদঃ সুমঙ্গলাঃ ৷  
ক্ষেমং ন বিন্দন্তি বিনা যদর্পণং  
তস্মৈ সুভদ্রশ্রবসে নমো নমঃ ॥ ২০ ॥

tapasvino dāna-parā yaśasvino  
ascetics  devoted to charity  the renowned  
manasvino mantra-vidaḥ su-maṅgalāḥ  
the thoughtful  knowers of mantras  of auspicious conduct  
kṣemaṁ na vindanti vinā yad-arpaṇaṁ  
welfare  not  attain  without  offering to Him  
tasmai subhadra-śravase namo namaḥ  
to Him  of auspicious fame  obeisance  obeisance

(20) “Ascetics, the generous, the renowned, the thoughtful, the knowers of mantras and those of auspicious conduct — none of them attains true welfare without offering everything to Him. Again and again I bow to Him, whose fame is auspicious.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.4.17)

কেবল জ্ঞান ‘মুক্তি’ দিতে নারে ভক্তি বিনে ৷  
কৃষ্ণোন্মুখে সেই মুক্তি হয় বিনা-জ্ঞানে ॥ ২১ ॥

kevala jñāna ‘mukti’ dite nāre bhakti vine  
mere  knowledge  liberation  to give  cannot  bhakti  without  
kṛṣṇonmukhe sei mukti haya vinā-jñāne  
to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa  that  liberation  comes  without knowledge

(21) Knowledge alone cannot give liberation without bhakti; but to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa that liberation comes even without knowledge.

শ্রেয়ঃসৃতিং ভক্তিমুদস্য তে বিভো  
ক্লিশ্যন্তি যে কেবলবোধলব্ধয়ে ৷  
তেষামসৌ ক্লেশল এব শিষ্যতে  
নান্যদ্ যথা স্থূলতুষাবঘাতিনাম্ ॥ ২২ ॥

śreyaḥ-sṛtiṁ bhaktim udasya te vibho  
the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord  
kliśyanti ye kevala-bodha-labdhaye  
toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge  
teṣām asau kleśala eva śiṣyate  
for them  this  toil  only  remains  
nānyad yathā sthūla-tuṣāvaghātinām  
nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks

(22) “O almighty Lord, bhakti to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)

দৈবী হ্যেষা গুণময়ী মম মায়া দুরত্যয়া ৷  
মামেব যে প্রপদ্যন্তে মায়ামেতাং তরন্তি তে ॥ ২৩ ॥

daivī hy eṣā guṇa-mayī  
divine  indeed  this  made of the guṇas  
mama māyā duratyayā  
My  māyā  hard to overcome  
mām eva ye prapadyante  
to Me  alone  those who  surrender  
māyām etāṁ taranti te  
māyā  this  cross over  they

(23) “This divine māyā of Mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me alone cross beyond this māyā.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.14)[^9]

‘কৃষ্ণ-নিত্যদাস’ — জীব তাহা ভুলি’ গেল ৷  
এই দোষে মায়া তার গলায় বান্ধিল ॥ ২৪ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa-nitya-dāsa’ — jīva tāhā bhuli’ gela  
Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant  the jīva  that  having forgotten  went  
ei doṣe māyā tāra galāya bāndhila  
for that  fault  Māyā  his  around the neck  bound

(24) The jīva is Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant, but he has forgotten it. For that fault Māyā has bound him by the neck.[^10]

তাতে কৃষ্ণ ভজে, করে গুরুর সেবন ৷  
মায়াজাল ছুটে, পায় কৃষ্ণের চরণ ॥ ২৫ ॥

tāte kṛṣṇa bhaje, kare gurura sevana  
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he renders  the guru’s  service  
māyā-jāla chuṭe, pāya kṛṣṇera caraṇa  
the net of Māyā  falls away  he attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet

(25) If he then worships Kṛṣṇa and serves his guru, the net of Māyā falls away and he attains Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

চারি বর্ণাশ্রমী যদি কৃষ্ণ নাহি ভজে ৷  
স্বকর্ম্ম করিলেও সে রৌরবে পড়ি’ মজে ॥ ২৬ ॥

cāri varṇāśramī yadi kṛṣṇa nāhi bhaje  
of the four  followers of varṇa and āśrama  if  Kṛṣṇa  do not  worship  
svakarma karileo se raurave paḍi’ maje  
their own duties  even performing  they  in the hell Raurava  having fallen  sink

(26) If the followers of the four *varṇas* and *āśramas* do not worship Kṛṣṇa, then even while performing their own duties they fall into the hell Raurava and sink there.[^11]

মুখবাহূরুপাদেভ্যঃ পুরুষস্যাশ্রমৈঃ সহ ৷  
চত্বারো জজ্ঞিরে বর্ণা গুণৈর্ব্বিপ্রাদয়ঃ পৃথক্ ॥ ২৭ ॥

mukha-bāhūru-pādebhyaḥ  
from the mouth, arms, thighs and feet  
puruṣasyāśramaiḥ saha  
of the Puruṣa  with the āśramas  together  
catvāro jajñire varṇā  
four  were born  the varṇas  
guṇair viprādayaḥ pṛthak  
by their qualities  brāhmaṇas and the rest  distinct

(27) “From the mouth, arms, thighs and feet of the Puruṣa the four *varṇas*, beginning with the brāhmaṇas, were born together with the *āśramas*, each distinct by its qualities.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.2)[^12]

য এষাং পুরুষং সাক্ষাদাত্মপ্রভবমীশ্বরম্ ৷  
ন ভজন্ত্যবজানন্তি স্থানাদ্ভ্রষ্টাঃ পতন্ত্যধঃ ॥ ২৮ ॥

ya eṣāṁ puruṣaṁ sākṣād  
who  of them  the Puruṣa  directly  
ātma-prabhavam īśvaram  
their own source  the Lord  
na bhajanty avajānanti  
not  worship  disregard  
sthānād bhraṣṭāḥ patanty adhaḥ  
from their position  fallen  they fall  down

(28) “Those among them who do not worship the Puruṣa, their own source and Lord, but disregard Him, fall from their position and go down.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.3)

জ্ঞানী জীবন্মুক্তদশা পাইনু করি’ মানে ৷  
বস্তুতঃ বুদ্ধি ‘শুদ্ধ’ নহে কৃষ্ণভক্তি বিনে ॥ ২৯ ॥

jñānī jīvan-mukta-daśā pāinu kari’ māne  
the jñānī  of liberation in this life  the state  “I have attained”  thus  thinks  
vastutaḥ buddhi ‘śuddha’ nahe kṛṣṇa-bhakti vine  
in truth  the intelligence  pure  is not  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without

(29) The jñānī thinks, ‘I have attained the state of liberation in this life (*jīvan-mukti*).’ But in truth the intelligence does not become pure without devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

যেঽন্যেঽরবিন্দাক্ষ বিমুক্তমানিন-  
স্ত্বয়্যস্তভাবাদবিশুদ্ধবুদ্ধয়ঃ ৷  
আরুহ্য কৃচ্ছ্রেণ পরং পদং ততঃ  
পতন্ত্যধোঽনাদৃতযুষ্মদঙ্ঘ্রয়ঃ ॥ ৩০ ॥

ye ‘nye ‘ravindākṣa vimukta-māninas  
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated  
tvayy asta-bhāvād aviśuddha-buddhayaḥ  
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence  
āruhya kṛcchreṇa paraṁ padaṁ tataḥ  
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there  
patanty adho ‘nādṛta-yuṣmad-aṅghrayaḥ  
fall  down  having neglected Your feet

(30) “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)[^13]

কৃষ্ণ — সূর্য্যসম; মায়া হয় অন্ধকার ৷  
যাঁহা কৃষ্ণ, তাঁহা নাহি মায়ার অধিকার ॥ ৩১ ॥

kṛṣṇa — sūrya-sama; māyā haya andhakāra  
Kṛṣṇa  like the sun  Māyā  is  darkness  
yām̐hā kṛṣṇa, tām̐hā nāhi māyāra adhikāra  
where  Kṛṣṇa  there  is no  of Māyā  dominion

(31) Kṛṣṇa is like the sun, and Māyā is darkness. Where Kṛṣṇa is, Māyā has no power.[^14]

বিলজ্জমানয়া যস্য স্থাতুমীক্ষাপথেঽমুয়া ৷  
বিমোহিতা বিকত্থন্তে মমাহমিতি দুর্ধিয়ঃ ॥ ৩২ ॥

vilajjamānayā yasya  
ashamed  whose  
sthātum īkṣā-pathe ‘muyā  
to stand  in the path of the sight  by her  
vimohitā vikatthante  
bewildered  boast  
mamāham iti durdhiyaḥ  
“mine” and “I”  thus  the foolish

(32) “Māyā is ashamed to stand in the path of His sight; yet the foolish, bewildered by her, boast of ‘I’ and ‘mine’.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.5.13)

‘কৃষ্ণ, তোমার হঙ’ যদি বলে একবার ৷  
মায়াবন্ধ হৈতে কৃষ্ণ তারে করে পার ॥ ৩৩ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa, tomāra haṅa’ yadi bale eka-bāra  
“Kṛṣṇa  Yours  I am”  if  he says  once  
māyā-bandha haite kṛṣṇa tāre kare pāra  
from the bondage of Māyā  Kṛṣṇa  him  makes  carried across

(33) If someone says even once, ‘Kṛṣṇa, I am Yours’, Kṛṣṇa carries him across, beyond the bondage of Māyā.[^15]

সকৃদেব প্রপন্নো যস্তবাস্মীতি চ যাচতে ৷  
অভয়ং সর্ব্বদা তস্মৈ দদাম্যেতদ্ব্রতং মম ॥ ৩৪ ॥

sakṛd eva prapanno yas  
once  only  surrendered  who  
tavāsmīti ca yācate  
“I am Yours”  thus  and  begs  
abhayaṁ sarvadā tasmai  
fearlessness  always  to him  
dadāmy etad vrataṁ mama  
I give  this  vow  My

(34) “To one who surrenders to Me even once and prays, ‘I am Yours’, I always grant fearlessness. This is My vow.” (Rāmāyaṇa, Yuddha-kāṇḍa, 18.33)[^16]

মুক্তি-ভুক্তি-সিদ্ধিকামী ‘সুবুদ্ধি’ যদি হয় ৷  
গাঢ়-ভক্তিযোগে তবে কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ৩৫ ॥

mukti-bhukti-siddhi-kāmī ‘subuddhi’ yadi haya  
desiring liberation, enjoyment and perfections  truly intelligent  if  he becomes  
gāḍha-bhakti-yoge tabe kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
by deep bhakti-yoga  then  Kṛṣṇa  he worships

(35) If one who desires liberation, worldly enjoyment or mystic perfections becomes truly intelligent, he then worships Kṛṣṇa with deep *bhakti-yoga*.

অকামঃ সর্ব্বকামো বা মোক্ষকাম উদারধীঃ ৷  
তীব্রেণ ভক্তিযোগেন যজেত পুরুষং পরম্ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

akāmaḥ sarva-kāmo vā  
without desire  full of all desires  or  
mokṣa-kāma udāra-dhīḥ  
desiring liberation  of broad intelligence  
tīvreṇa bhakti-yogena  
with intense  bhakti-yoga  
yajeta puruṣaṁ param  
should worship  the Person  Supreme

(36) “Whether he is without desire, full of every desire, or desirous of liberation, a person of broad intelligence should worship the Supreme Person with intense *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.10)

অন্যকামী যদি করে কৃষ্ণের ভজন ৷  
না মাগিলেহ কৃষ্ণ তারে দেন স্ব-চরণ ॥ ৩৭ ॥

anya-kāmī yadi kare kṛṣṇera bhajana  
one with other desires  if  performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  worship  
nā māgileha kṛṣṇa tāre dena sva-caraṇa  
not  even having asked  Kṛṣṇa  to him  gives  His own feet

(37) If one who has other desires worships Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa gives him His own feet even though he has not asked for them.

কৃষ্ণ কহে, — ‘আমা ভজে, মাগে বিষয় সুখ ৷  
অমৃত ছাড়ি’ বিষ মাগে, — এই বড় মূর্খ ॥ ৩৮ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahe, — ‘āmā bhaje, māge viṣaya sukha  
Kṛṣṇa  says  Me  he worships  he asks for  sense  pleasure  
amṛta chāḍi’ viṣa māge, — ei baḍa mūrkha  
nectar  giving up  poison  he asks for  this one  a great  fool

(38) Kṛṣṇa says, ‘He worships Me, yet asks for sense pleasure. Giving up nectar, he asks for poison — he is a great fool.

আমি — বিজ্ঞ, এই মূর্খে ‘বিষয়’ কেনে দিব ?  
স্ব-চরণামৃত দিয়া ‘বিষয়’ ভুলাইব’ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

āmi — vijña, ei mūrkhe ‘viṣaya’ kene diba?  
I  am wise  to this  fool  sense objects  why  should I give  
sva-caraṇāmṛta diyā ‘viṣaya’ bhulāiba’  
the nectar of My own feet  giving  sense objects  I shall make him forget

(39) But I am wise: why should I give sense objects to this fool? I will give him the nectar of My own feet and make him forget sense objects.’

সত্যং দিশত্যর্থিতমর্থিতো নৃণাং  
নৈবার্থদো যৎ পুনরর্থিতা যতঃ ৷  
স্বয়ং বিধত্তে ভজতামনিচ্ছতা-  
মিচ্ছাপিধানং নিজপাদপল্লবম্ ॥ ৪০ ॥

satyaṁ diśaty arthitam arthito nṛṇāṁ  
truly  He grants  what is asked  when asked  to men  
naivārtha-do yat punar arthitā yataḥ  
not indeed  giver of that  which  again  asking  from which  
svayaṁ vidhatte bhajatām anicchatām  
Himself  He grants  to those who worship  though not desiring  
icchā-pidhānaṁ nija-pāda-pallavam  
covering all desires  His own  feet like tender shoots

(40) “It is true that when men ask, the Lord grants what they ask for; but He does not give what would make them ask again. To those who worship Him, though they do not desire it, He Himself gives His own feet, tender as new shoots, which put an end to all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.19.26)[^17]

কাম লাগি’ কৃষ্ণে ভজে, পায় কৃষ্ণ-রসে ৷  
কাম ছাড়ি’ ‘দাস’ হৈতে হয় অভিলাষে ॥ ৪১ ॥

kāma lāgi’ kṛṣṇe bhaje, pāya kṛṣṇa-rase  
desire  for the sake of  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he attains  the taste of Kṛṣṇa  
kāma chāḍi’ ‘dāsa’ haite haya abhilāṣe  
desire  giving up  a servant  to become  there arises  the longing

(41) One who worships Kṛṣṇa for the sake of some desire comes to taste Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness (*kṛṣṇa-rasa*). Then, giving up that desire, he longs to become His servant.

স্থানাভিলাষী তপসি স্থিতোঽহং  
ত্বাং প্রাপ্তবান্ দেবমুনীন্দ্রগুহ্যম্ ৷  
কাচং বিচিন্বন্নপি দিব্যরত্নং  
স্বামিন্ কৃতার্থোঽস্মি বরং ন যাচে ॥ ৪২ ॥

sthānābhilāṣī tapasi sthito ‘haṁ  
desiring a position  in austerity  engaged  I  
tvāṁ prāptavān deva-munīndra-guhyam  
You  have attained  hidden from the gods and the best of sages  
kācaṁ vicinvann api divya-ratnaṁ  
glass  searching for  though  a divine jewel  
svāmin kṛtārtho ‘smi varaṁ na yāce  
O Master  fulfilled  I am  a boon  not  I ask

(42) “Desiring a high position, I engaged in austerities, and I have attained You, who are hidden even from the gods and the greatest sages. I was searching for a piece of glass and found a divine jewel. O Master, my purpose is fulfilled; I ask for no boon.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 7.28)

সংসার ভ্রমিতে কোন ভাগ্যে কেহ তরে ৷  
নদীর প্রবাহে যেন কাষ্ঠ লাগে তীরে ॥ ৪৩ ॥

saṁsāra bhramite kona bhāgye keha tare  
in worldly existence  while wandering  by some  fortune  someone  is delivered  
nadīra pravāhe yena kāṣṭha lāge tīre  
of a river  in the current  as  a log  touches  the bank

(43) While wandering in worldly existence, someone by some good fortune is delivered, as a log carried by a river’s current comes to rest on the bank.

মৈবং মমাধমস্যাপি স্যাদেবাচ্যুতদর্শনম্ ৷  
হ্রিয়মাণঃ কালনদ্যা ক্বচিত্তরতি কশ্চন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

maivaṁ mamādhamasyāpi  
not so  of me  lowly  even  
syād evācyuta-darśanam  
there will be  surely  the sight of Acyuta  
hriyamāṇaḥ kāla-nadyā  
being carried away  by the river of time  
kvacit tarati kaścana  
at times  crosses over  someone

(44) “It is not so that I, lowly as I am, cannot see Acyuta: I will surely see Him. For even among those carried away by the river of time, someone at times reaches the shore.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.38.5)[^18]

কোন ভাগ্যে কারো সংসার ক্ষয়োন্মুখ হয় ৷  
সাধুসঙ্গে তরে, কৃষ্ণে রতি উপজয় ॥ ৪৫ ॥

kona bhāgye kāro saṁsāra kṣayonmukha haya  
by some  fortune  someone’s  worldly existence  near its end  becomes  
sādhu-saṅge tare, kṛṣṇe rati upajaya  
in the company of sādhus  he is delivered  for Kṛṣṇa  attraction  arises

(45) When by some good fortune someone’s worldly existence nears its end, he is delivered through the company of sādhus, and attraction (*rati*) for Kṛṣṇa awakens in him.

ভবাপবর্গো ভ্রমতো যদা ভবে-  
জ্জনস্য তর্হ্যচ্যুত সৎসমাগমঃ ৷  
সৎসঙ্গমো যর্হি তদৈব সদ্গতৌ  
পরাবরেশে ত্বয়ি জায়তে রতিঃ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

bhavāpavargo bhramato yadā bhavej  
the end of worldly existence  of one wandering  when  comes  
janasya tarhy acyuta sat-samāgamaḥ  
of a person  then  O Acyuta  meeting with the saints  
sat-saṅgamo yarhi tadaiva sad-gatau  
association with the saints  when  at that very time  for the goal of the saints  
parāvareśe tvayi jāyate ratiḥ  
for the Lord of the high and the low  for You  is born  attraction

(46) “O Acyuta, when a wandering soul’s worldly existence comes to its end, he meets the saints. And when he associates with the saints, attraction (*rati*) is born in him for You, the goal of the saints and the Lord of the high and the low.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.51.53)[^19]

কৃষ্ণ যদি কৃপা করে কোন ভাগ্যবানে ৷  
গুরু-অন্তর্য্যামি-রূপে শিখায় আপনে ॥ ৪৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa yadi kṛpā kare kona bhāgyavāne  
Kṛṣṇa  if  mercy  shows  to some  fortunate soul  
guru-antaryāmi-rūpe śikhāya āpane  
in the forms of guru and the Indweller  teaches  Himself

(47) If Kṛṣṇa shows mercy to some fortunate soul, He Himself teaches him in the forms of the guru and the Indweller (*antaryāmī*).

নৈবোপয়ন্ত্যপচিতিং কবয়স্তবেশ  
ব্রহ্মায়ুষাপি কৃতমৃদ্ধমুদঃ স্মরন্তঃ ৷  
যোঽন্তর্বহিস্তনুভৃতামশুভং বিধুন্ব-  
ন্নাচার্য্যচৈত্ত্যবপুষা স্বগতিং ব্যনক্তি ॥ ৪৮ ॥

naivopayanty apacitiṁ kavayas taveśa  
not  attain  repayment  the wise  Your,  O Lord  
brahmāyuṣāpi kṛtam ṛddha-mudaḥ smarantaḥ  
with Brahmā’s lifespan  even  Your deeds  with growing joy  remembering  
yo ’ntar bahis tanu-bhṛtām aśubhaṁ vidhunvann  
who  within  without  of the embodied  the inauspicious  dispelling  
ācārya-caitya-vapuṣā sva-gatiṁ vyanakti  
as ācārya  and Supersoul  in forms  Your own path  reveal

(48) “O Lord! Even if the wise lived as long as Brahmā and remembered with ever-growing joy all that You have done for them, they could not repay their debt to You. For, dispelling all that is inauspicious in embodied souls, You come to them from without as the ācārya and from within as the Supersoul, and reveal to them the way to Yourself.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.29.6)[^20]

সাধুসঙ্গে কৃষ্ণভক্ত্যে শ্রদ্ধা যদি হয় ৷  
ভক্তিফল ‘প্রেম’ হয়, সংসার যায় ক্ষয় ॥ ৪৯ ॥

sādhu-saṅge kṛṣṇa-bhaktye śraddhā yadi haya  
in the company of sādhus  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  faith  if  arises  
bhakti-phala ‘prema’ haya, saṁsāra yāya kṣaya  
the fruit of bhakti  prema  comes  worldly existence  goes  to its end

(49) If, in the company of sādhus, faith in devotion to Kṛṣṇa arises, then prema, the fruit of bhakti, appears, and worldly existence comes to an end.

যদৃচ্ছয়া মৎকথাদৌ জাতশ্রদ্ধস্তু যঃ পুমান্ ৷  
ন নির্বিণ্ণো নাতিসক্তো ভক্তিযোগোঽস্য সিদ্ধিদঃ ॥ ৫০ ॥

yadṛcchayā mat-kathādau  
by good fortune  in narrations about Me and the rest  
jāta-śraddhas tu yaḥ pumān  
in whom faith is born  but  who  the person  
na nirviṇṇo nāti-sakto  
not  disgusted  not overly attached  
bhakti-yogo ‘sya siddhi-daḥ  
bhakti-yoga  for him  gives perfection

(50) “For one who by good fortune has come to have faith in narrations about Me and the like, and who is neither disgusted with the world nor overly attached to it, *bhakti-yoga* grants perfection.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.8)[^21]

মহৎ-কৃপা বিনা কোন কর্ম্মে ‘ভক্তি’ নয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তি দূরে রহু, সংসার নহে ক্ষয় ॥ ৫১ ॥

mahat-kṛpā vinā kona karme ‘bhakti’ naya  
of the great souls  the mercy  without  by any  activity  bhakti  is not  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti dūre rahu, saṁsāra nahe kṣaya  
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  aside  let it be  worldly existence  does not  end

(51) Without the mercy of great souls no activity yields bhakti. Without it, worldly existence does not even come to an end, let alone devotion to Kṛṣṇa arise.

রহূগণৈতৎ তপসা ন যাতি  
ন চেজ্যয়া নির্ব্বপণাদ্গৃহাদ্বা ৷  
ন চ্ছন্দসা নৈব জলাগ্নিসূর্য্যৈ-  
র্ব্বিনা মহৎপাদরজোঽভিষেকম্ ॥ ৫২ ॥

rahūgaṇaitat tapasā na yāti  
O Rahūgaṇa  this  by austerity  not  is attained  
na cejyayā nirvapaṇād gṛhād vā  
not  and by worship  by renunciation  by household life  or  
na cchandasā naiva jalāgni-sūryair  
not  by the Vedas  nor  by water, fire and sun  
vinā mahat-pāda-rajo-‘bhiṣekam  
without  bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls

(52) “O Rahūgaṇa, this is not attained by austerity, by worship, by renunciation or by household duties, nor by the Vedas, nor by worship of water, fire and the sun, without bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.12.12)[^22]

নৈষাং মতিস্তাবদুরুক্রমাঙ্ঘ্রিং  
স্পৃশত্যনর্থাপগমো যদর্থঃ ৷  
মহীয়সাং পাদরজোঽভিষেকং  
নিষ্কিঞ্চনানাং ন বৃণীত যাবৎ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

naiṣāṁ matis tāvad urukramāṅghriṁ  
not  their  mind  so long  Urukrama’s feet  
spṛśaty anarthāpagamo yad-arthaḥ  
touches  the removal of anarthas  whose purpose  
mahīyasāṁ pāda-rajo-‘bhiṣekaṁ  
of the great  bathing in the dust of the feet  
niṣkiñcanānāṁ na vṛṇīta yāvat  
of those who own nothing  not  accepts  as long as

(53) “So long as people do not accept a bath in the dust of the feet of the great, who own nothing (*niṣkiñcana*), their minds cannot touch the feet of Urukrama, whose purpose is to remove all *anarthas*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.5.32)[^23]

‘সাধুসঙ্গ’, ‘সাধুসঙ্গ’ — সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
লবমাত্র সাধুসঙ্গে সর্ব্বসিদ্ধি হয় ॥ ৫৪ ॥

‘sādhu-saṅga’, ‘sādhu-saṅga’ — sarva-śāstre kaya  
sādhu-saṅga  sādhu-saṅga  all the scriptures  declare  
lava-mātra sādhu-saṅge sarva-siddhi haya  
a mere moment of  sādhu-saṅga  all perfection  comes

(54) ‘The company of sādhus, the company of sādhus!’ — so declare all the scriptures. Even a moment of *sādhu-saṅga* brings every perfection.

তুলয়াম লবেনাপি ন স্বর্গং নাপুনর্ভবম্ ৷  
ভগবৎসঙ্গিসঙ্গস্য মর্ত্ত্যানাং কিমুতাশিষঃ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

tulayāma lavenāpi  
we compare  with a moment  even  
na svargaṁ nāpunar-bhavam  
not  heaven  not liberation  
bhagavat-saṅgi-saṅgasya  
of association with those who associate with the Lord  
martyānāṁ kim utāśiṣaḥ  
of mortals  what  then  the blessings

(55) “We cannot compare even heaven or liberation with a moment of association with those who associate with the Lord — what then of the blessings mortals seek?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.18.13)[^24]

কৃষ্ণ কৃপালু অর্জ্জুনেরে লক্ষ্য করিয়া ৷  
জগতেরে রাখিয়াছেন উপদেশ দিয়া ॥ ৫৬ ॥

kṛṣṇa kṛpālu arjunere lakṣya kariyā  
Kṛṣṇa  merciful  Arjuna  the aim  making  
jagatere rākhiyāchena upadeśa diyā  
the world  has protected  instruction  giving

(56) Merciful Kṛṣṇa, speaking to Arjuna, has protected the whole world by giving this instruction:

সর্ব্বগুহ্যতমং ভূয়ঃ শৃণু মে পরমং বচঃ ৷  
ইষ্টোঽসি মে দৃঢ়মিতি ততো বক্ষ্যামি তে হিতম্ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

sarva-guhyatamaṁ bhūyaḥ  
the most secret of all  again  
śṛṇu me paramaṁ vacaḥ  
hear  My  supreme  word  
iṣṭo ‘si me dṛḍham iti  
dear  you are  to Me  very  thus  
tato vakṣyāmi te hitam  
therefore  I shall speak  to you  your good

(57) “Hear again My supreme word, the most secret of all. You are very dear to Me, and therefore I shall tell you what is for your good.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.64)

মন্মনা ভব মদ্ভক্তো মদ্যাজী মাং নমস্কুরু ৷  
মামেবৈষ্যসি সত্যং তে প্রতিজানে প্রিয়োঽসি মে ॥ ৫৮ ॥

man-manā bhava mad-bhakto  
with mind fixed on Me  be  My devotee  
mad-yājī māṁ namaskuru  
worshipping Me  to Me  bow down  
mām evaiṣyasi satyaṁ te  
to Me  surely you will come  truly  to you  
pratijāne priyo ‘si me  
I promise  dear  you are  to Me

(58) “Fix your mind on Me, be My devotee, worship Me and bow down to Me. You will surely come to Me — truly I promise you this, for you are dear to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.65)

পূর্ব্ব আজ্ঞা, — বেদ-ধর্ম্ম, কর্ম্ম, যোগ, জ্ঞান ৷  
সব সাধি’ অবশেষ-আজ্ঞা — বলবান্ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

pūrva ājñā, — veda-dharma, karma, yoga, jñāna  
the earlier  commands  Vedic dharma  karma  yoga  jñāna  
saba sādhi’ avaśeṣa-ājñā — balavān  
all  having established  the final command  is strongest

(59) His earlier commands concerned Vedic *dharma*, karma, yoga and jñāna. Having established all of these, He gave His final command, and it is the strongest.

এই আজ্ঞাবলে ভক্তের ‘শ্রদ্ধা’ যদি হয় ৷  
সর্ব্বকর্ম্ম ত্যাগ করি’ সে কৃষ্ণেরে ভজয় ॥ ৬০ ॥

ei ājñā-bale bhaktera ‘śraddhā’ yadi haya  
of this  by the force of the command  in the devotee  faith  if  arises  
sarva-karma tyāga kari’ se kṛṣṇere bhajaya  
all activities  giving up  having made  he  Kṛṣṇa  worships

(60) If by the force of this command faith arises in the devotee, he gives up all other activities and worships Kṛṣṇa.

তাবৎ কর্ম্মাণি কুর্ব্বীত ন নির্ব্বিদ্যেত যাবতা ৷  
মৎকথাশ্রবণাদৌ বা শ্রদ্ধা যাবন্ন জায়তে ॥ ৬১ ॥

tāvat karmāṇi kurvīta  
so long  duties  one should perform  
na nirvidyeta yāvatā  
not  one becomes weary  as long as  
mat-kathā-śravaṇādau vā  
in hearing My narrations and the rest  or  
śraddhā yāvan na jāyate  
faith  as long as  not  is born

(61) “One should go on performing one’s prescribed duties only so long as one has not grown weary of them, or so long as faith has not been born in hearing about Me and the like.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.9)[^25]

‘শ্রদ্ধা’-শব্দে — বিশ্বাস কহে সুদৃঢ় নিশ্চয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণে ভক্তি কৈলে সর্ব্বকর্ম্ম কৃত হয় ॥ ৬২ ॥

‘śraddhā’-śabde — viśvāsa kahe sudṛḍha niścaya  
by the word “śraddhā”  trust  is meant  very firm  conviction  
kṛṣṇe bhakti kaile sarva-karma kṛta haya  
to Kṛṣṇa  devotion  when one renders  all duties  done  become

(62) The word *śraddhā* means trust: a very firm conviction that by rendering devotion to Kṛṣṇa one has fulfilled all one’s duties.[^26]

যথা তরোর্ম্মূলনিষেচনেন  
তৃপ্যন্তি তৎস্কন্ধভুজোপশাখাঃ ৷  
প্রাণোপহারাচ্চ যথেন্দ্রিয়াণাং  
তথৈব সর্ব্বার্হণমচ্যুতেজ্যা ॥ ৬৩ ॥

yathā taror mūla-niṣecanena  
as  of a tree  by watering the root  
tṛpyanti tat-skandha-bhujopaśākhāḥ  
are satisfied  its trunk, branches and twigs  
prāṇopahārāc ca yathendriyāṇāṁ  
by feeding the life-breath  and  as  of the senses  
tathaiva sarvārhaṇam acyutejyā  
just so  the worship of all  is the worship of Acyuta

(63) “As by watering the root of a tree its trunk, branches and twigs are all nourished, and as by feeding the life-breath (*prāṇa*) all the senses are satisfied, so the worship of Acyuta is the worship of all.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 4.31.14)

শ্রদ্ধাবান্ জন হয় ভক্তি-অধিকারী ৷  
‘উত্তম’, ‘মধ্যম’, ‘কনিষ্ঠ’ — শ্রদ্ধা-অনুসারী ॥ ৬৪ ॥

śraddhāvān jana haya bhakti-adhikārī  
having faith  a person  is  qualified for bhakti  
‘uttama’, ‘madhyama’, ‘kaniṣṭha’ — śraddhā-anusārī  
highest  middle  lowest  according to faith

(64) A person who has faith is qualified for bhakti. According to his faith he is of the highest, the middle or the lowest level (*uttama*, *madhyama*, *kaniṣṭha*).

শাস্ত্রযুক্ত্যে সুনিপুণ, দৃঢ়শ্রদ্ধা যাঁর ৷  
‘উত্তম অধিকারী’ সেই তারয় সংসার ॥ ৬৫ ॥

śāstra-yuktye sunipuṇa, dṛḍha-śraddhā yām̐ra  
in scripture and reasoning  very expert  firm faith  whose  
‘uttama adhikārī’ sei tāraya saṁsāra  
of the highest  qualification  he  delivers  the world

(65) One who is expert in scripture and reasoning and whose faith is firm is an *uttama adhikārī*, of the highest qualification; he delivers the world.

শাস্ত্র-যুক্তি নাহি জানে দৃঢ়, শ্রদ্ধাবান্ ৷  
‘মধ্যম-অধিকারী’ সেই মহা-ভাগ্যবান্ ॥ ৬৬ ॥

śāstra-yukti nāhi jāne dṛḍha, śraddhāvān  
the reasoning of scripture  not  knows  firmly  having faith  
‘madhyama-adhikārī’ sei mahā-bhāgyavān  
of middle qualification  he  greatly fortunate

(66) One who does not firmly know the reasoning of scripture but has faith is a *madhyama adhikārī*, of the middle qualification; he is most fortunate.[^27]

যাহার কোমল শ্রদ্ধা, সে — ‘কনিষ্ঠ’ জন ৷  
ক্রমে ক্রমে তেঁহো ভক্ত হইবে ‘উত্তম’ ॥ ৬৭ ॥

yāhāra komala śraddhā, se — ‘kaniṣṭha’ jana  
whose  tender  faith  he  a kaniṣṭha  person  
krame krame tem̐ho bhakta haibe ‘uttama’  
step by step  step by step  he  a devotee  will become  of the highest

(67) One whose faith is still tender is a *kaniṣṭha*, a beginner. Step by step he too will become a devotee of the highest level.

রতি-প্রেম-তারতম্যে ভক্ত — তর-তম ৷  
একাদশ স্কন্ধে তার করিয়াছে লক্ষণ ॥ ৬৮ ॥

rati-prema-tāratamye bhakta — tara-tama  
by the degree of rati and prema  devotees  higher and highest  
ekādaśa skandhe tāra kariyāche lakṣaṇa  
in the eleventh  canto  their  has been given  description

(68) According to the degree of their *rati* and prema, devotees are higher and highest. The Eleventh Canto has described their characteristics.

সর্ব্বভূতেষু যঃ পশ্যেদ্ভগবদ্ভাবমাত্মনঃ ৷  
ভূতানি ভগবত্যাত্মন্যেষ ভাগবতোত্তমঃ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

sarva-bhūteṣu yaḥ paśyed  
in all beings  who  sees  
bhagavad-bhāvam ātmanaḥ  
of the Lord  the presence  of the Self  
bhūtāni bhagavaty ātmany  
the beings  in the Lord  in the Self  
eṣa bhāgavatottamaḥ  
he  the best of devotees

(69) “One who sees the Lord, the Soul of all, present in all beings, and sees all beings in the Lord, is the best of devotees (*bhāgavatottama*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.45)[^28]

ঈশ্বরে তদধীনেষু বালিশেষু দ্বিষৎসু চ ৷  
প্রেম-মৈত্রী-কৃপোপেক্ষা যঃ করোতি স মধ্যমঃ ॥ ৭০ ॥

īśvare tad-adhīneṣu  
to the Lord  to those dependent on Him  
bāliśeṣu dviṣatsu ca  
to the ignorant  to the hostile  and  
prema-maitrī-kṛpopekṣā  
love, friendship, mercy and indifference  
yaḥ karoti sa madhyamaḥ  
who  shows  he  is of the middle level

(70) “One who shows love for the Lord, friendship for those who depend on Him, mercy to the ignorant and indifference to the hostile is a devotee of the middle level (*madhyama*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.46)

অর্চ্চায়ামেব হরয়ে পূজাং যঃ শ্রদ্ধয়েহতে ৷  
ন তদ্ভক্তেষু চান্যেষু স ভক্তঃ প্রাকৃতঃ স্মৃতঃ ॥ ৭১ ॥

arcāyām eva haraye  
in the Deity  only  to Hari  
pūjāṁ yaḥ śraddhayehate  
worship  who  with faith performs  
na tad-bhakteṣu cānyeṣu  
not  to His devotees  and to others  
sa bhaktaḥ prākṛtaḥ smṛtaḥ  
he  a devotee  on the worldly level  is considered

(71) “One who with faith worships Hari only in the Deity, but does not honour His devotees or others, is known as a *prākṛta* devotee, one still on the worldly level.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.47)

সর্ব্ব মহা-গুণগণ বৈষ্ণব-শরীরে ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তে কৃষ্ণের গুণ, সকলি সঞ্চারে ॥ ৭২ ॥

sarva mahā-guṇa-gaṇa vaiṣṇava-śarīre  
all  the great qualities  in the person of a Vaiṣṇava  
kṛṣṇa-bhakte kṛṣṇera guṇa, sakali sañcāre  
into Kṛṣṇa’s devotee  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  all  pass

(72) All great qualities dwell in a Vaiṣṇava; all of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities pass into Kṛṣṇa’s devotee.

যস্যাস্তি ভক্তির্ভগবত্যকিঞ্চনা  
সর্ব্বৈর্গুণৈস্তত্র সমাসতে সুরাঃ ৷  
হরাবভক্তস্য কুতো মহদ্গুণা  
মনোরথেনাসতি ধাবতো বহিঃ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

yasyāsti bhaktir bhagavaty akiñcanā  
in whom  there is  devotion  to the Lord  free of desire  
sarvair guṇais tatra samāsate surāḥ  
with all  qualities  there  dwell  the gods  
harāv abhaktasya kuto mahad-guṇā  
to Hari  of one not devoted  whence  great qualities  
mano-rathenāsati dhāvato bahiḥ  
by the chariot of the mind  toward the unreal  running  outward

(73) “In one whose devotion to the Lord is free of desire all the gods dwell, and with them every virtue. But how can great qualities be found in one who is not devoted to Hari? Driven by his desires, his mind is forever running outward after what is passing.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.18.12)[^29]

সেই সব গুণ হয় বৈষ্ণব-লক্ষণ ৷  
সব কহা না যায়, করি দিগ্দরশন ॥ ৭৪ ॥

sei saba guṇa haya vaiṣṇava-lakṣaṇa  
those  all  qualities  are  the marks of a Vaiṣṇava  
saba kahā nā yāya, kari dig-daraśana  
all  to tell  not  possible  I give  an indication

(74) All these qualities are the marks of a Vaiṣṇava. They cannot all be told, so I shall only point to some of them.

কৃপালু, অকৃতদ্রোহ, সত্যসার, সম ৷  
নির্দ্দোষ, বদান্য, মৃদু, শুচি, অকিঞ্চন ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kṛpālu, akṛta-droha, satya-sāra, sama  
merciful  harming no one  devoted to truth  equal to all  
nirdoṣa, vadānya, mṛdu, śuci, akiñcana  
faultless  magnanimous  gentle  pure  owning nothing

(75) A Vaiṣṇava is merciful, harms no one, is devoted to truth, equal to all, faultless, magnanimous, gentle, pure and without possessions (*akiñcana*);[^30]

সর্ব্বোপকারক, শান্ত, কৃষ্ণৈকশরণ ৷  
অকাম, নিরীহ, স্থির, বিজিত-ষড়্গুণ ॥ ৭৬ ॥

sarvopakāraka, śānta, kṛṣṇaika-śaraṇa  
benefactor of all  peaceful  having Kṛṣṇa as the only shelter  
akāma, nirīha, sthira, vijita-ṣaḍ-guṇa  
without desire  free from worldly striving  steady  having conquered the six

(76) he is a benefactor of all, peaceful, with Kṛṣṇa as his only shelter, free from desire, free from worldly striving, steady, the conqueror of the six (*ṣaḍ-guṇa*);

মিতভুক্ অপ্রমত্ত, মানদ, অমানী ৷  
গম্ভীর, করুণ, মৈত্র, কবি, দক্ষ, মৌনী ॥ ৭৭ ॥

mita-bhuk apramatta, mānada, amānī  
moderate in eating  sober  giving honor to others  seeking no honor  
gambhīra, karuṇa, maitra, kavi, dakṣa, maunī  
grave  compassionate  friendly  a poet  expert  silent

(77) he is moderate in eating, sober, giving honour to others while seeking none for himself, grave, compassionate, friendly, a poet, expert and sparing in speech.

তিতিক্ষবঃ কারুণিকাঃ সুহৃদঃ সর্ব্বদেহিনাম্ ৷  
অজাতশত্রবঃ শান্তাঃ সাধবঃ সাধুভূষণাঃ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

titikṣavaḥ kāruṇikāḥ  
tolerant  compassionate  
suhṛdaḥ sarva-dehinām  
well-wishers  of all embodied beings  
ajāta-śatravaḥ śāntāḥ  
with no enemies  peaceful  
sādhavaḥ sādhu-bhūṣaṇāḥ  
following the scriptures  adorned with saintliness

(78) “The sādhus are tolerant and compassionate, well-wishers of all embodied beings. They have no enemies and are peaceful; they follow the scriptures, and saintly conduct is their ornament.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.21)

মহৎসেবাং দ্বারমাহুর্ব্বিমুক্তে-  
স্তমোদ্বারং যোষিতাং সঙ্গিসঙ্গম্ ৷  
মহান্তস্তে সমচিত্তাঃ প্রশান্তাঃ  
বিমন্যবঃ সুহৃদঃ সাধবো যে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

mahat-sevāṁ dvāram āhur vimuktes  
service to the great  the door  they call  of liberation  
tamo-dvāraṁ yoṣitāṁ saṅgi-saṅgam  
the door to darkness  of those attached to women  the company  
mahāntas te sama-cittāḥ praśāntāḥ  
the great  they  equal-minded  peaceful  
vimanyavaḥ suhṛdaḥ sādhavo ye  
free from anger  well-wishers  saintly  who

(79) “Service to the great is called the door to liberation, and the company of those attached to women the door to darkness. The great are those who are equal-minded, peaceful, free from anger, well-wishers of all and saintly.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.5.2)

কৃষ্ণভক্তি-জন্মমূল হয় ‘সাধুসঙ্গ’ ৷  
কৃষ্ণপ্রেম জন্মে, তিঁহো পুনঃ মুখ্য অঙ্গ ॥ ৮০ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhakti-janma-mūla haya ‘sādhu-saṅga’  
of the birth of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the root  is  sādhu-saṅga  
kṛṣṇa-prema janme, tim̐ho punaḥ mukhya aṅga  
love for Kṛṣṇa  is born  it  again  the principal  limb

(80) The root from which devotion to Kṛṣṇa is born is *sādhu-saṅga*. And even when love for Kṛṣṇa is born, *sādhu-saṅga* remains its principal limb.

ভবাপবর্গো ভ্রমতো যদা ভবে-  
জ্জনস্য তর্হ্যচ্যুত সৎসমাগমঃ ৷  
সৎসঙ্গমো যর্হি তদৈব সদ্গতৌ  
পরাবরেশে ত্বয়ি জায়তে রতিঃ ॥ ৮১ ॥

bhavāpavargo bhramato yadā bhavej  
the end of worldly existence  of one wandering  when  comes  
janasya tarhy acyuta sat-samāgamaḥ  
of a person  then  O Acyuta  meeting with the saints  
sat-saṅgamo yarhi tadaiva sad-gatau  
association with the saints  when  at that very time  for the goal of the saints  
parāvareśe tvayi jāyate ratiḥ  
for the Lord of the high and the low  for You  is born  attraction

(81) “O Acyuta, when a wandering soul’s worldly existence comes to its end, he meets the saints. And when he associates with the saints, attraction (*rati*) is born in him for You, the goal of the saints and the Lord of the high and the low.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.51.53)[^31]

অত আত্যন্তিকং ক্ষেমং পৃচ্ছামো ভবতোঽনঘাঃ ৷  
সংসারেঽস্মিন্ ক্ষণার্দ্ধোঽপি সৎসঙ্গঃ সেবধির্নৃণাম্ ॥ ৮২ ॥

ata ātyantikaṁ kṣemaṁ  
therefore  the ultimate  good  
pṛcchāmo bhavato ‘naghāḥ  
we ask  you  O sinless ones  
saṁsāre ‘smin kṣaṇārdho ‘pi  
in worldly existence  this  half a moment  even  
sat-saṅgaḥ sevadhir nṛṇām  
association with saints  a treasure  for men

(82) “Therefore, O sinless ones, we ask you about the ultimate good. In this world even half a moment of association with saints is a treasure for humankind.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.30)
