“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 3  
**The Auspicious Invocation of Blessing: The General Cause of Caitanya’s Descent**  
(English translation from the Bengali, following the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition and the «Anubhāṣya», 2026 — draft)

শ্রীচৈতন্যপ্রভুং বন্দে যৎপাদাশ্রয়বীর্য্যতঃ ৷  
সংগৃহ্ণাত্যাকরব্রাতাদজ্ঞঃ সিদ্ধান্তসন্মণীন্ ॥ ১ ॥

śrī-caitanya-prabhuṁ vande  
Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship  
yat-pādāśraya-vīryataḥ  
by whose  feet  of the shelter  by the power  
saṅgṛhṇāty ākara-vrātād  
gathers  from the mines  from the multitude  
ajñaḥ siddhānta-san-maṇīn  
the ignorant one  of conclusive truth  precious jewels

(1) I bow to the Lord Śrī Caitanya. Through the power of taking shelter at His feet, even an ignorant man gathers the precious jewels of conclusive truth (*siddhānta*) from the many mines of scripture.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

তৃতীয় শ্লোকের অর্থ কৈল বিবরণ ৷  
চতুর্থ শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন ভক্তগণ ॥ ৩ ॥

tṛtīya ślokera artha kaila vivaraṇa  
of the third  verse  of the meaning  I have made  the explanation  
caturtha ślokera artha śuna bhakta-gaṇa  
of the fourth  verse  the meaning  hear  O devotees

(3) I have explained the meaning of the third verse. Now, O devotees, hear the meaning of the fourth.

অনর্পিতচরীং চিরাৎ করুণয়াবতীর্ণঃ কলৌ  
সমর্পয়িতুমুন্নতোজ্জ্বলরসাং স্বভক্তিশ্রিয়ম্ ৷  
হরিঃ পুরটসুন্দরদ্যুতিকদম্বসন্দীপিতঃ  
সদা হৃদয়কন্দরে স্ফুরতু বঃ শচীনন্দনঃ ॥ ৪ ॥

anarpita-carīṁ cirāt karuṇayāvatīrṇaḥ kalau  
not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in Kali  
samarpayitum unnatojjvala-rasāṁ sva-bhakti-śriyam  
to bestow  of the most exalted radiant rasa  devotion to Himself  the treasure  
hariḥ puraṭa-sundara-dyuti-kadamba-sandīpitaḥ  
Hari  golden  beautiful  lustre  with a mass  illumined  
sadā hṛdaya-kandare sphuratu vaḥ śacī-nandanaḥ  
always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  Śacī’s  son

(4) “May the Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, illumined by a mass of lustre more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the inmost cave of your hearts. Out of His mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a very long time — the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted, radiant *ujjvala-rasa*.” (Vidagdha-mādhava, 1.2)[^1]

পূর্ণ ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ৷  
গোলোকে ব্রজের সহ নিত্য বিহার ॥ ৫ ॥

pūrṇa bhagavān kṛṣṇa vrajendra-kumāra  
complete  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of Vraja’s king  the son  
goloke vrajera saha nitya vihāra  
in Goloka  Vraja  together with  eternal  pastimes

(5) Kṛṣṇa, the son of the king of Vraja, is Bhagavān in all His fullness. Together with all Vraja He enjoys His pastimes eternally in Goloka.

ব্রহ্মার একদিনে তিঁহো একবার ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হঞা করেন প্রকট বিহার ॥ ৬ ॥

brahmāra eka dine tim̐ho eka-bāra  
of Brahmā  in one  day  He  once  
avatīrṇa hañā karena prakaṭa vihāra  
descended  having become  performs  manifest  pastimes

(6) Once in a day of Brahmā He descends and displays those pastimes in this world.[^2]

সত্য, ত্রেতা, দ্বাপর, কলি, চারিযুগ জানি ৷  
সেই চারিযুগে দিব্য একযুগ মানি ॥ ৭ ॥

satya, tretā, dvāpara, kali, cāri-yuga jāni  
Satya,  Tretā,  Dvāpara,  Kali —  four  ages  we know  
sei cāri-yuge divya eka-yuga māni  
those  four  ages  divine  one  age  we count

(7) The four ages are Satya, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali, and together they make up one divine age (*divya-yuga*).

একাত্তর চতুর্যুগে এক মন্বন্তর ৷  
চৌদ্দ মন্বন্তর ব্রহ্মার দিবস ভিতর ॥ ৮ ॥

ekāttara catur-yuge eka manv-antara  
in seventy-one  cycles of four ages  one  manvantara  
caudda manv-antara brahmāra divasa bhitara  
fourteen  manvantaras  of Brahmā  of the day  within

(8) Seventy-one such cycles make one manvantara, the reign of a Manu, and fourteen manvantaras make up a day of Brahmā.[^3]

‘বৈবস্বত’ নাম এই সপ্তম মন্বন্তর ৷  
সাতাইশ চতুর্যুগ গেলে তাহার অন্তর ॥ ৯ ॥

‘vaivasvata’ nāma ei saptama manv-antara  
‘Vaivasvata’  by name  this  seventh  manvantara  
sātāiśa catur-yuga gele tāhāra antara  
twenty-seven  cycles of four ages  when gone  of it  within

(9) The present, seventh manvantara is called Vaivasvata, and twenty-seven of its cycles have already passed.

অষ্টাবিংশ চতুর্যুগে দ্বাপরের শেষে ৷  
ব্রজের সহিত হয় কৃষ্ণের প্রকাশে ॥ ১০ ॥

aṣṭāviṁśa catur-yuge dvāparera śeṣe  
in the twenty-eighth  cycle of four ages  of Dvāpara  at the end  
vrajera sahita haya kṛṣṇera prakāśe  
Vraja  together with  takes place  of Kṛṣṇa  the manifestation

(10) At the end of the Dvāpara of the twenty-eighth cycle, Kṛṣṇa appears in this world together with His Vraja.[^4]

দাস্য, সখ্য, বাৎসল্য, শৃঙ্গার — চারি রস ৷  
চারি ভাবের ভক্ত যত কৃষ্ণ তার বশ ॥ ১১ ॥

dāsya, sakhya, vātsalya, śṛṅgāra — cāri rasa  
servitude,  friendship,  parental love,  the love of the beloved —  four  rasas  
cāri bhāvera bhakta yata kṛṣṇa tāra vaśa  
of the four  moods  devotees  all  Kṛṣṇa  of them  under the sway

(11) Servitude, friendship, parental affection and the love of the beloved (*śṛṅgāra*) are the four rasas, and Kṛṣṇa is under the sway of all the devotees who have these four moods.[^5]

দাস-সখা-পিতামাতা-প্রেয়সীগণ লঞা ৷  
ব্রজে ক্রীড়া করে কৃষ্ণ প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ॥ ১২ ॥

dāsa-sakhā-pitā-mātā-preyasī-gaṇa lañā  
servants  friends  father  mother  beloveds  the company  taking  
vraje krīḍā kare kṛṣṇa premāviṣṭa hañā  
in Vraja  play  makes  Kṛṣṇa  in love absorbed  becoming

(12) With His servants and friends, His father and mother and His beloved ones, Kṛṣṇa sports in Vraja, absorbed in love.

যথেষ্ট বিহরি’ কৃষ্ণ করে অন্তর্ধান ৷  
অন্তর্ধান করি’ মনে করে অনুমান ॥ ১৩ ॥

yatheṣṭa vihari’ kṛṣṇa kare antardhāna  
as He wished  having sported  Kṛṣṇa  makes  disappearance  
antardhāna kari’ mane kare anumāna  
disappearance  having made  in the mind  makes  reflection

(13) Having sported as He wished, Kṛṣṇa disappears, and afterwards He reflects:

চিরকাল নাহি করি প্রেমভক্তি দান ৷  
ভক্তি বিনা জগতের নাহি অবস্থান ॥ ১৪ ॥

cira-kāla nāhi kari prema-bhakti dāna  
for a long time  not  I make  of loving devotion  the gift  
bhakti vinā jagatera nāhi avasthāna  
devotion  without  of the world  is not  standing

(14) “For a long time I have not given the gift of loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*), and without devotion the world has no ground to stand on.

সকল জগতে মোরে করে বিধি-ভক্তি ৷  
বিধিভক্ত্যে ব্রজভাব পাইতে নাহি শক্তি ॥ ১৫ ॥

sakala jagate more kare vidhi-bhakti  
in all  the world  to Me  they do  devotion by rule  
vidhi-bhaktye vraja-bhāva pāite nāhi śakti  
by devotion by rule  of Vraja  the mood  to attain  there is not  the power

(15) All over the world they worship Me by the rules of scripture (*vidhi-bhakti*), but devotion by rule has no power to reach the love of Vraja.

ঐশ্বর্য্যজ্ঞানেতে সব জগৎ মিশ্রিত ৷  
ঐশ্বর্য্য-শিথিল-প্রেমে নাহি মোর প্রীত ॥ ১৬ ॥

aiśvarya-jñānete saba jagat miśrita  
of majesty  with the awareness  the whole  world  is mixed  
aiśvarya-śithila-preme nāhi mora prīta  
by majesty  slackened  in love  is not  My  pleasure

(16) The whole world is permeated by the awareness of My majesty, and love slackened by awe gives Me no joy.[^6]

ঐশ্বর্য্যজ্ঞানে বিধি ভজন করিয়া ৷  
বৈকুণ্ঠকে যায় চতুর্ব্বিধ মুক্তি পাঞা ॥ ১৭ ॥

aiśvarya-jñāne vidhi bhajana kariyā  
of majesty  in the awareness  by rule  worship  performing  
vaikuṇṭhake yāya catur-vidha mukti pāñā  
to Vaikuṇṭha  they go  of four  kinds  liberation  attaining

(17) Worshipping by rule in the awareness of My majesty, they go to Vaikuṇṭha and attain the four kinds of liberation.

সার্ষ্টি, সারূপ্য, আর সামীপ্য, সালোক্য ৷  
সাযুজ্য না লয় ভক্ত যাতে ব্রহ্ম-ঐক্য ॥ ১৮ ॥

sārṣṭi, sārūpya, āra sāmīpya, sālokya  
equal opulence,  likeness of form,  and  nearness,  residence in the same world  
sāyujya nā laya bhakta yāte brahma-aikya  
merging  not  accepts  the devotee  in which  with Brahman  oneness

(18) These are *sārṣṭi* (opulence equal to Mine), *sārūpya* (a form like Mine), *sāmīpya* (nearness to Me) and *sālokya* (residence in My world). But *sāyujya*, in which one becomes one with Brahman, the devotee never accepts.[^7]

যুগধর্ম্ম প্রবর্ত্তামু নাম-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
চারি ভাব-ভক্তি দিয়া নাচামু ভুবন ॥ ১৯ ॥

yuga-dharma pravartāmu nāma-saṅkīrtana  
of the age  the religion  I shall set going  of the name  congregational chanting  
cāri bhāva-bhakti diyā nācāmu bhuvana  
four  moods  devotion  giving  I shall make dance  the world

(19) I shall inaugurate the religion of the age, the congregational chanting of the holy name (*nāma-saṅkīrtana*), and, giving devotion in the four moods, I shall make the whole world dance.

আপনি করিমু ভক্তভাব অঙ্গীকারে ৷  
আপনি আচরি’ ভক্তি শিখামু সবারে ॥ ২০ ॥

āpani karimu bhakta-bhāva aṅgīkāre  
Myself  I shall make  of a devotee  the mood  acceptance  
āpani ācari’ bhakti śikhāmu sabāre  
Myself  practising  devotion  I shall teach  to all

(20) I shall take on the mood of a devotee, and by practising devotion Myself I shall teach it to all.

আপনে না কৈলে ধর্ম্ম শিখান না যায় ৷  
এই ত’ সিদ্ধান্ত গীতা-ভাগবতে গায় ॥ ২১ ॥

āpane nā kaile dharma śikhāna nā yāya  
oneself  not  having done  dharma  to teach  not  is possible  
ei ta’ siddhānta gītā-bhāgavate gāya  
this  indeed  the conclusion  in the Gītā and the Bhāgavata  they sing

(21) Unless one practises dharma oneself, one cannot teach it. This is the conclusion sung in the *Gītā* and the *Bhāgavatam*.

যদা যদা হি ধর্ম্মস্য গ্লানির্ভবতি ভারত ৷  
অভ্যুত্থানমধর্ম্মস্য তদাত্মানং সৃজাম্যহম্ ॥ ২২ ॥

yadā yadā hi dharmasya  
when  when  indeed  of dharma  
glānir bhavati bhārata  
a decline  there is  O descendant of Bharata  
abhyutthānam adharmasya  
a rise  of irreligion  
tadātmānaṁ sṛjāmy aham  
then  Myself  I send forth  I

(22) “Whenever, O descendant of Bharata, dharma declines and irreligion rises, then I manifest Myself.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 4.7)

পরিত্রাণায় সাধূনাং বিনাশায় চ দুষ্কৃতাম্ ৷  
ধর্ম্মসংস্থাপনার্থায় সম্ভবামি যুগে যুগে ॥ ২৩ ॥

paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṁ  
for the deliverance  of the saints  
vināśāya ca duṣkṛtām  
for the destruction  and  of the wicked  
dharma-saṁsthāpanārthāya  
of dharma  the establishing  for the sake of  
sambhavāmi yuge yuge  
I appear  in age  after age

(23) “To deliver the saints, to destroy the wicked and to establish dharma, I appear in age after age.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 4.8)[^8]

উৎসীদেয়ুরিমে লোকা ন কুর্য্যাং কর্ম্ম চেদহম্ ৷  
সঙ্করস্য চ কর্ত্তা স্যামুপহন্যামিমাঃ প্রজাঃ ॥ ২৪ ॥

utsīdeyur ime lokā  
would perish  these  worlds  
na kuryāṁ karma ced aham  
not  I should do  action  if  I  
saṅkarasya ca kartā syām  
of confusion  and  the maker  I would be  
upahanyām imāḥ prajāḥ  
I would ruin  these  beings

(24) “If I did not act, these worlds would perish; I would be the cause of confusion among the classes of men and would ruin all these beings.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 3.24)[^9]

যদ্যদাচরতি শ্রেষ্ঠস্তত্তদেবেতরো জনঃ ৷  
স যৎ প্রমাণং কুরুতে লোকস্তদনুবর্ত্ততে ॥ ২৫ ॥

yad yad ācarati śreṣṭhas  
whatever  whatever  does  the best  
tat tad evetaro janaḥ  
that  that  indeed  other  people  
sa yat pramāṇaṁ kurute  
he  whatever  standard  sets  
lokas tad anuvartate  
the world  that  follows

(25) “Whatever the best of men does, others do the same; whatever standard he sets, the world follows.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 3.21)

যুগধর্ম্ম-প্রবর্ত্তন হয় অংশ হৈতে ৷  
আমা বিনা অন্যে নারে ব্রজপ্রেম দিতে ॥ ২৬ ॥

yuga-dharma-pravartana haya aṁśa haite  
of the age  of the religion  the establishing  happens  a portion  from  
āmā vinā anye nāre vraja-prema dite  
Me  without  others  cannot  of Vraja  love  to give

(26) The religion of the age can be established by My portion (*aṁśa*), but none but Me can give the love of Vraja.[^10]

সন্ত্ববতারা বহবঃ পঙ্কজনাভস্য সর্ব্বতো-ভদ্রাঃ ৷  
কৃষ্ণাদন্যঃ কো বা লতাস্বপি প্রেমদো ভবতি ॥ ২৭ ॥

santv avatārā bahavaḥ  
let there be  avatāras  many  
paṅkaja-nābhasya sarvato-bhadrāḥ  
of the lotus-naveled One  in every way  auspicious  
kṛṣṇād anyaḥ ko vā latāsv  
than Kṛṣṇa  other  who  indeed  to the creepers  
api prema-do bhavati  
even  love  the giver  becomes

(27) “Let there be many all-auspicious avatāras of the Lord whose navel is a lotus; but who other than Kṛṣṇa gives prema even to the creepers?” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.5.37)[^11]

তাহাতে আপন ভক্তগণ করি’ সঙ্গে ৷  
পৃথিবীতে অবতরি’ করিমু নানা রঙ্গে ॥ ২৮ ॥

tāhāte āpana bhakta-gaṇa kari’ saṅge  
therefore  My own  devotees  having made  companions  
pṛthivīte avatari’ karimu nānā raṅge  
on earth  having descended  I shall make  various  sports

(28) Therefore, taking My own devotees with Me, I shall descend to earth and enjoy many kinds of pastimes.”

এত ভাবি’ কলিকালে প্রথম সন্ধ্যায় ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হৈলা কৃষ্ণ আপনি নদীয়ায় ॥ ২৯ ॥

eta bhāvi’ kali-kāle prathama sandhyāya  
thus  having thought  of the age of Kali  in the first  junction  
avatīrṇa hailā kṛṣṇa āpani nadīyāya  
descended  became  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  in Nadīyā

(29) Thinking thus, Kṛṣṇa Himself descended in Nadia in the first junction (*sandhyā*) of the age of Kali.[^12]

চৈতন্যসিংহের নবদ্বীপে অবতার ৷  
সিংহগ্রীব, সিংহবীর্য্য, সিংহের হুঙ্কার ॥ ৩০ ॥

caitanya-siṁhera navadvīpe avatāra  
of the lion Caitanya  in Navadvīpa  the descent  
siṁha-grīva, siṁha-vīrya, siṁhera huṅkāra  
lion’s  neck,  lion’s  strength,  of a lion  the roar

(30) So the lion Caitanya appeared in Navadvīpa — with the neck of a lion, the strength of a lion and the roar of a lion.

সেই সিংহ বসুক্ জীবের হৃদয়-কন্দরে ৷  
কল্মষ-দ্বিরদ নাশে যাঁহার হুঙ্কারে ॥ ৩১ ॥

sei siṁha vasuk jīvera hṛdaya-kandare  
that  lion  may He sit  of the living being  of the heart  in the cave  
kalmaṣa-dvirada nāśe yām̐hāra huṅkāre  
of sin  the elephant  perishes  at whose  roar

(31) May that lion take His seat in the cave of every soul’s heart — the lion at whose roar the elephant of sin perishes.

প্রথম লীলায় তাঁর ‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ নাম ৷  
ভক্তিরসে ভরিল, ধরিল ভূতগ্রাম ॥ ৩২ ॥

prathama līlāya tām̐ra ‘viśvambhara’ nāma  
in the first  pastimes  His  ‘Viśvambhara’  the name  
bhakti-rase bharila, dharila bhūta-grāma  
with the rasa of devotion  He filled,  He upheld  of beings  the multitude

(32) In His early pastimes His name was Viśvambhara. He filled the world with the nectar of devotion and upheld all living beings.

ডুভৃঞ্ ধাতুর অর্থ — পোষণ, ধারণ ৷  
পুষিল, ধরিল প্রেম দিয়া ত্রিভুবন ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ḍu-bhṛñ-dhātura artha — poṣaṇa, dhāraṇa  
‘ḍubhṛñ’  of the root  the meaning —  nourishing,  sustaining  
puṣila, dharila prema diyā tri-bhuvana  
He nourished,  He sustained  love  giving  the three worlds

(33) The root *ḍubhṛñ* means “to nourish and to sustain”: giving love, He nourished and sustained the three worlds.

শেষলীলায় নাম ধরে ‘শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য’ ৷  
শ্রীকৃষ্ণ জানায়ে সব বিশ্ব কৈল ধন্য ॥ ৩৪ ॥

śeṣa-līlāya nāma dhare ‘śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya’  
in the last pastimes  the name  He bears  ‘Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’  
śrī-kṛṣṇa jānāye saba viśva kaila dhanya  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa  making known  the whole  universe  He made  blessed

(34) In His later pastimes He bears the name Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. Making Śrī Kṛṣṇa known to all, He blessed the whole world.[^13]

তাঁর যুগাবতার জানি’ গর্গ মহাশয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের নামকরণে করিয়াছে নির্ণয় ॥ ৩৫ ॥

tām̐ra yugāvatāra jāni’ garga mahāśaya  
His  as the avatāra of the age  knowing  Garga  the great soul  
kṛṣṇera nāma-karaṇe kariyāche nirṇaya  
of Kṛṣṇa  at the name-giving  has made  the determination

(35) Knowing Him to be the avatāra of the age, the great Garga declared it at Kṛṣṇa’s name-giving ceremony.

আসন্ বর্ণাস্ত্রয়ো হ্যস্য গৃহ্ণতোঽনুযুগং তনূঃ ৷  
শুক্লো রক্তস্তথা পীত ইদানীং কৃষ্ণতাং গতঃ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

āsan varṇās trayo hy asya  
there were  colours  three  indeed  of Him  
gṛhṇato ’nu-yugaṁ tanūḥ  
accepting  in each age  bodies  
śuklo raktas tathā pīta  
white  red  likewise  yellow  
idānīṁ kṛṣṇatāṁ gataḥ  
now  blackness  has attained

(36) “Your son, who accepts a body in every age, has appeared in three colours — white, red and yellow; now He has become dark (*kṛṣṇa*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.13)[^14]

শুক্ল, রক্ত, পীতবর্ণ — এই তিন দ্যুতি ৷  
সত্য-ত্রেতা-কলিকালে ধরেন শ্রীপতি ॥ ৩৭ ॥

śukla, rakta, pīta-varṇa — ei tina dyuti  
white,  red,  yellow  colour —  these  three  lustres  
satya-tretā-kali-kāle dharena śrī-pati  
in the Satya,  Tretā,  Kali  ages  bears  the Lord of Śrī

(37) White, red and yellow — these three lustres the Lord of Śrī assumes in the Satya, Tretā and Kali ages.

ইদানীং দ্বাপরে তিঁহো হৈলা কৃষ্ণবর্ণ ৷  
এই সব শাস্ত্রাগমপুরাণের মর্ম্ম ॥ ৩৮ ॥

idānīṁ dvāpare tim̐ho hailā kṛṣṇa-varṇa  
now  in Dvāpara  He  became  dark  in colour  
ei saba śāstrāgama-purāṇera marma  
this  all  of the scriptures, Āgamas and Purāṇas  the essence

(38) Now, in Dvāpara, He has become dark. This is the essence of all the scriptures — the Āgamas and the Purāṇas.

দ্বাপরে ভগবান্ শ্যামঃ পীতবাসা নিজায়ুধঃ ৷  
শ্রীবৎসাদিভিরঙ্কৈশ্চ লক্ষণৈরুপলক্ষিতঃ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

dvāpare bhagavān śyāmaḥ  
in Dvāpara  the Lord  dark  
pīta-vāsā nijāyudhaḥ  
in yellow garments  with His own weapons  
śrī-vatsādibhir aṅkaiś ca  
by Śrīvatsa and the rest  by marks  and  
lakṣaṇair upalakṣitaḥ  
by characteristics  distinguished

(39) “In Dvāpara the Lord is dark, clad in yellow and bearing His own weapons, and He is known by the Śrīvatsa and other marks and characteristics.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.27)

কলিযুগে যুগধর্ম্ম — নামের প্রচার ৷  
তথি লাগি’ পীতবর্ণ চৈতন্যাবতার ॥ ৪০ ॥

kali-yuge yuga-dharma — nāmera pracāra  
in Kali-yuga  of the age  the religion —  of the name  the spreading  
tathi lāgi’ pīta-varṇa caitanyāvatāra  
for that  sake  yellow  in colour  the Caitanya avatāra

(40) In the age of Kali the religion of the age is the spreading of the holy name. For this the golden-hued avatāra, Caitanya, has come.[^15]

তপ্তহেম-সম কান্তি, প্রকাণ্ড শরীর ৷  
নবমেঘ জিনি কণ্ঠধ্বনি যে গম্ভীর ॥ ৪১ ॥

tapta-hema-sama kānti, prakāṇḍa śarīra  
molten  gold  like  lustre,  mighty  body  
nava-megha jini kaṇṭha-dhvani ye gambhīra  
new  clouds  surpassing  of the throat  the sound  which  deep

(41) His lustre is like molten gold, His body tall and mighty, and His deep voice surpasses the thunder of fresh rain-clouds.

দৈর্ঘ্য-বিস্তারে যেই আপনার হাত ৷  
চারি হস্ত হয় ‘মহাপুরুষ’ বিখ্যাত ॥ ৪২ ॥

dairghya-vistāre yei āpanāra hāta  
in length  and in breadth  who  of one’s own  hands (cubits)  
cāri hasta haya ‘mahā-puruṣa’ vikhyāta  
four  cubits  is  ‘great person’  famed

(42) One whose height and arm-span both measure four of his own cubits is famed as a *mahāpuruṣa*, a great person.

‘ন্যগ্রোধপরিমণ্ডল’ হয় তাঁর নাম ৷  
ন্যগ্রোধপরিমণ্ডল-তনু চৈতন্য গুণধাম ॥ ৪৩ ॥

‘nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala’ haya tām̐ra nāma  
‘nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala’  is  his  the name  
nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala-tanu caitanya guṇa-dhāma  
of nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala  the body  Caitanya  of virtues  the abode

(43) Such a person is called *nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala*, and Caitanya, the abode of all virtues, has such a body.[^16]

আজানুলম্বিত ভুজ কমললোচন ৷  
তিলফুল-জিনি-নাসা, সুধাংশু-বদন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

ājānulambita bhuja kamala-locana  
to the knees  hanging  arms,  lotus  eyes  
tilaphula-jini-nāsā, sudhāṁśu-vadana  
the sesame flower  surpassing  nose,  the moon  face

(44) His arms reach down to His knees, His eyes are like lotuses, His nose is finer than a sesame flower, and His face is like the moon.

শান্ত, দান্ত, কৃষ্ণভক্তি-নিষ্ঠাপরায়ণ ৷  
ভক্তবৎসল, সুশীল, সর্ব্বভূতে সম ॥ ৪৫ ॥

śānta, dānta, kṛṣṇa-bhakti-niṣṭhā-parāyaṇa  
peaceful,  self-controlled,  to devotion to Kṛṣṇa  steadfastly  dedicated  
bhakta-vatsala, suśīla, sarva-bhūte sama  
to devotees  affectionate,  gentle,  to all  beings  equal

(45) He is peaceful and self-controlled, wholly given to devotion to Kṛṣṇa. He loves His devotees, is gentle by nature and looks on all beings with an equal eye.

চন্দনের অঙ্গদ-বালা, চন্দন-ভূষণ ৷  
নৃত্যকালে পরি’ করেন কৃষ্ণসংকীর্ত্তন ॥ ৪৬ ॥

candanera aṅgada-bālā, candana-bhūṣaṇa  
of sandalwood  armlets and bracelets,  sandal  ornaments  
nṛtya-kāle pari’ karena kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana  
at the time of dancing  having put on  performs  Kṛṣṇa  saṅkīrtana

(46) Adorned with sandal paste and wearing armlets and bracelets of sandalwood, He dances in the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s names.

এই সব গুণ লঞা মুনি বৈশম্পায়ন ৷  
সহস্রনামে কৈল তাঁর নাম গণন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ei saba guṇa lañā muni vaiśampāyana  
these  all  qualities  taking  the sage  Vaiśampāyana  
sahasra-nāme kaila tām̐ra nāma gaṇana  
in the Thousand Names  made  His  of names  the enumeration

(47) Drawing on all these qualities, the sage Vaiśampāyana included His names in the *Thousand Names* of Viṣṇu.[^17]

দুই লীলা চৈতন্যের — আদি আর শেষ ৷  
দুই লীলায় চারি চারি নাম বিশেষ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

dui līlā caitanyera — ādi āra śeṣa  
two  pastimes  of Caitanya —  early  and  last  
dui līlāya cāri cāri nāma viśeṣa  
in the two  pastimes  four  four  names  special

(48) Caitanya’s pastimes are of two parts, the early and the later, and in each of them He has four special names.[^18]

সুবর্ণবর্ণো হেমাঙ্গো বরাঙ্গশ্চন্দনাঙ্গদী ৷  
সন্ন্যাসকৃচ্ছমঃ শান্তো নিষ্ঠাশান্তিপরায়ণঃ ॥ ৪৯ ॥

suvarṇa-varṇo hemāṅgo  
of golden  colour  with limbs of gold  
varāṅgaś candanāṅgadī  
with beautiful limbs  wearing sandal armlets  
sannyāsa-kṛc chamaḥ śānto  
sannyāsa  taking  self-restrained  peaceful  
niṣṭhā-śānti-parāyaṇaḥ  
to steadfastness and peace  devoted

(49) “He is of golden colour, His limbs are like gold, His form is beautiful, and He wears armlets of sandal. He has taken sannyāsa, He is self-restrained and tranquil, and His highest refuge is steadfastness and peace.” (Mahābhārata, Dāna-dharma, 149; Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma, 92 and 95)[^19]

ব্যক্ত করি’ ভাগবতে কহে বার বার ৷  
কলিযুগে — কৃষ্ণনামসঙ্কীর্ত্তন সার ॥ ৫০ ॥

vyakta kari’ bhāgavate kahe bāra bāra  
clearly  making  in the Bhāgavata  it says  time  after time  
kali-yuge — kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṅkīrtana sāra  
in Kali-yuga —  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  congregational chanting  the essence

(50) The *Bhāgavatam* makes it clear again and again: in the age of Kali the congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa’s name is the essence of all.

কৃষ্ণবর্ণং ত্বিষাঽকৃষ্ণং সাঙ্গোপাঙ্গাস্ত্রপার্ষদম্ ৷  
যজ্ঞৈঃ সঙ্কীর্ত্তনপ্রায়ৈর্যজন্তি হি সুমেধসঃ ॥ ৫১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-varṇaṁ tviṣā’kṛṣṇaṁ  
Kṛṣṇa  describing (or: with the syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’)  by lustre  not dark  
sāṅgopāṅgāstra-pārṣadam  
with limbs,  sub-limbs,  weapons  and associates  
yajñaiḥ saṅkīrtana-prāyair  
by sacrifices  in which saṅkīrtana  predominates  
yajanti hi su-medhasaḥ  
they worship  indeed  the intelligent

(51) “With sacrifices in which congregational chanting predominates, the wise worship the Lord who ever sings of Kṛṣṇa and whose lustre is not dark. He is always surrounded by His intimate companions (*aṅga*), His devotees (*upāṅga*), His weapons and His associates (*pārṣada*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.32)[^20]

শুন, ভাই, এই সব চৈতন্য-মহিমা ৷  
এই শ্লোকে কহে তাঁর মহিমার সীমা ॥ ৫২ ॥

śuna, bhāi, ei saba caitanya-mahimā  
hear,  brothers,  this  all  of Caitanya  the glory  
ei śloke kahe tām̐ra mahimāra sīmā  
in this  verse  it tells  His  of glory  the limit

(52) Hear, brothers, of all this glory of Caitanya: this verse speaks of the very height of His greatness.

‘কৃষ্ণ’ এই দুই বর্ণ সদা যাঁর মুখে ৷  
অথবা, কৃষ্ণকে তিঁহো বর্ণে নিজ সুখে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa’ ei dui varṇa sadā yām̐ra mukhe  
‘Kṛṣṇa’  these  two  syllables  always  in whose  mouth  
athavā, kṛṣṇake tim̐ho varṇe nija sukhe  
or,  Kṛṣṇa  He  describes  in His own  joy

(53) The two syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’ are always in His mouth; or else, in His own joy He describes Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণবর্ণ-শব্দের অর্থ দুই ত’ প্রমাণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ বিনু তাঁর মুখে নাহি আইসে আন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-varṇa-śabdera artha dui ta’ pramāṇa  
of the word ‘kṛṣṇa-varṇa’  the meaning  two  thus  are proved  
kṛṣṇa vinu tām̐ra mukhe nāhi āise āna  
Kṛṣṇa  except  His  to the mouth  not  comes  anything else

(54) Thus the two meanings of the word *kṛṣṇa-varṇa* are established: nothing but Kṛṣṇa ever comes to His lips.

কেহ তাঁরে বলে যদি কৃষ্ণ-বরণ ৷  
আর বিশেষণে তারে করে নিবারণ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

keha tām̐re bale yadi kṛṣṇa-varaṇa  
anyone  Him  calls  if  dark in colour  
āra viśeṣaṇe tāre kare nivāraṇa  
the next  by the adjective  that  makes  refutation

(55) And if anyone should say that He is dark in colour, the next adjective rules this out.

দেহকান্ত্যে হয় তেঁহো অকৃষ্ণ-বরণ ৷  
অকৃষ্ণবরণে তাঁর কহে পীতবরণ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

deha-kāntye haya tem̐ho akṛṣṇa-varaṇa  
by the body’s lustre  is  He  not dark  in colour  
akṛṣṇa-varaṇe tām̐ra kahe pīta-varaṇa  
by ‘not dark in colour’  His  it tells  yellow  colour

(56) By the lustre of His body He is not dark; and by the words “not dark” the verse tells of His yellow colour.[^21]

কলৌ যং বিদ্বাংসঃ স্ফুটমভিযজন্তে দ্যুতিভরা-  
দকৃষ্ণাঙ্গং কৃষ্ণং মখবিধিভিরুৎকীর্ত্তনময়ৈঃ ৷  
উপাস্যঞ্চ প্রাহুর্যমখিলচতুর্থাশ্রমজুষাং  
স দেবশ্চৈতন্যাকৃতিরতিতরাং নঃ কৃপয়তু ॥ ৫৭ ॥

kalau yaṁ vidvāṁsaḥ sphuṭam abhiyajante dyuti-bharād  
in Kali  whom  the learned  clearly  worship  from the abundance of lustre  
akṛṣṇāṅgaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ makha-vidhibhir utkīrtana-mayaiḥ  
not dark in body  Kṛṣṇa  with rites of sacrifice  consisting of loud chanting  
upāsyaṁ ca prāhur yam akhila-caturthāśrama-juṣāṁ  
the worshipable  and  they declare  whom  of all  those in the fourth āśrama  
sa devaś caitanyākṛtir atitarāṁ naḥ kṛpayatu  
that  Lord  in the form of Caitanya  exceedingly  to us  may He be merciful

(57) “May that Lord who has appeared in the form of Caitanya be ever merciful to us! In the age of Kali the learned openly worship Him with sacrifices of loud congregational chanting. He is Kṛṣṇa, yet from the abundance of His lustre His body is not dark, and they declare Him the object of worship for all who have entered the fourth āśrama.” (Stava-mālā, Second Caitanyāṣṭaka, 1)[^22]

প্রত্যক্ষ তাঁহার তপ্তকাঞ্চনের দ্যুতি ৷  
যাঁহার ছটায় নাশে অজ্ঞান-তমস্ততি ॥ ৫৮ ॥

pratyakṣa tām̐hāra tapta-kāñcanera dyuti  
visible  His  of molten  gold  the lustre  
yām̐hāra chaṭāya nāśe ajñāna-tamastati  
by whose  radiance  is destroyed  of ignorance  of the darkness  the expanse

(58) His lustre of molten gold is plain to see, and its radiance dispels all the darkness of ignorance.

জীবের কল্মষ-তমো নাশ করিবারে ৷  
অঙ্গ-উপাঙ্গ-নাম নানা অস্ত্র ধরে ॥ ৫৯ ॥

jīvera kalmaṣa-tamo nāśa karibāre  
of the living beings  of sin  the darkness  destruction  in order to make  
aṅga-upāṅga-nāma nānā astra dhare  
aṅga,  upāṅga,  the name —  various  weapons  He holds

(59) To destroy the darkness of the living beings’ sins, He bears many weapons — His intimate companions (*aṅga*), His devotees (*upāṅga*) and the holy name.

ভক্তির বিরোধী কর্ম্ম, ধর্ম্ম বা অধর্ম্ম ৷  
তাহার ‘কল্মষ’ নাম, সেই মহাতমঃ ॥ ৬০ ॥

bhaktira virodhī karma, dharma vā adharma  
to devotion  opposed  action,  dharma  or  adharma  
tāhāra ‘kalmaṣa’ nāma, sei mahā-tamaḥ  
its  ‘sin’  the name,  that  the great darkness

(60) Any action opposed to devotion, be it righteous or unrighteous, is called ‘sin’ (*kalmaṣa*). That is the great darkness.

বাহু তুলি’ হরি বলি’ প্রেমদৃষ্ট্যে চায় ৷  
করিয়া কল্মষ নাশ প্রেমেতে ভাসায় ॥ ৬১ ॥

bāhu tuli’ hari bali’ prema-dṛṣṭye cāya  
arms  raising,  ‘Hari’  saying,  with a loving glance  He looks  
kariyā kalmaṣa nāśa premete bhāsāya  
making  of sin  destruction  in love  He floods

(61) Raising His arms and calling “Hari!”, He casts His loving glance on all; He destroys their sins and floods them with love.

স্মিতালোকঃ শোকং হরতি জগতাং যস্য পরিতো  
গিরান্তু প্রারম্ভঃ কুশলপটলীং পল্লবয়তি ৷  
পদালম্ভঃ কং বা প্রণয়তি ন হি প্রেমনিবহং  
স দেবশ্চৈতন্যাকৃতিরতিতরাং নঃ কৃপয়তু ॥ ৬২ ॥

smitālokaḥ śokaṁ harati jagatāṁ yasya parito  
the smiling glance  sorrow  removes  of the worlds  whose  entirely  
girāṁ tu prārambhaḥ kuśala-paṭalīṁ pallavayati  
of words  but  the beginning  of blessings  the row  makes burst into leaf  
padālambhaḥ kaṁ vā praṇayati na hi prema-nivahaṁ  
of the feet  the touch  what  indeed  brings  not  indeed  of love  the flood  
sa devaś caitanyākṛtir atitarāṁ naḥ kṛpayatu  
that  Lord  in the form of Caitanya  exceedingly  to us  may He be merciful

(62) “His smiling glance wholly takes away the sorrow of the worlds; His very first words make a whole row of blessings burst into leaf; and what flood of love does the touch of His feet not bring? May that Lord who has appeared in the form of Caitanya be ever merciful to us!” (Stava-mālā, Second Caitanyāṣṭaka, 8)

শ্রীঅঙ্গ, শ্রীমুখ যেই করে দরশন ৷  
তার পাপক্ষয় হয়, পায় প্রেমধন ॥ ৬৩ ॥

śrī-aṅga, śrī-mukha yei kare daraśana  
beautiful  body,  beautiful  face  whoever  makes  the seeing  
tāra pāpa-kṣaya haya, pāya prema-dhana  
his  of sins  destruction  happens,  he attains  of love  the treasure

(63) Whoever sees His beautiful form and face is freed from sin and attains the treasure of love.

অন্য অবতারে সব সৈন্য-শস্ত্র সঙ্গে ৷  
চৈতন্য-কৃষ্ণের সৈন্য অঙ্গ-উপাঙ্গে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

anya avatāre saba sainya-śastra saṅge  
in other  avatāras  all  armies  weapons  with  
caitanya-kṛṣṇera sainya aṅga-upāṅge  
of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the army —  aṅgas  and upāṅgas

(64) In other avatāras the Lord came with armies and weapons, but the army of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa is His intimate companions and devotees (*aṅga* and *upāṅga*).

সদোপাস্যঃ শ্রীমান্ ধৃতমনুজকায়ৈঃ প্রণয়িতাং  
বহদ্ভির্গীর্বাণৈর্গিরিশপরমেষ্ঠিপ্রভৃতিভিঃ ৷  
স্বভক্তেভ্যঃ শুদ্ধাং নিজভজনমুদ্রামুপদিশন্  
স চৈতন্যঃ কিং মে পুনরপি দৃশোর্যাস্যতি পদম্ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

sadopāsyaḥ śrīmān dhṛta-manuja-kāyaiḥ praṇayitāṁ  
always  worshipped  the beautiful one  by those who have taken  human  bodies  love  
vahadbhir gīr-vāṇair giriśa-parameṣṭhi-prabhṛtibhiḥ  
bearing  by the gods  Śiva  Brahmā  headed by  
sva-bhaktebhyaḥ śuddhāṁ nija-bhajana-mudrām upadiśan  
to His own devotees  pure  His own  worship  the way  teaching  
sa caitanyaḥ kiṁ me punar api dṛśor yāsyati padam  
that  Caitanya  will  my  again  even  of the eyes  come  to the path

(65) “He is ever worshipped by the gods headed by Śiva and Brahmā, who, full of love for Him, have taken human bodies; and He teaches His own devotees the pure way of worshipping Him. Will that beautiful Caitanya come once again before my eyes?” (Stava-mālā, First Caitanyāṣṭaka, 1)[^23]

আঙ্গোপাঙ্গ অস্ত্র করে স্বকার্য্যসাধন ৷  
‘অঙ্গ’ শব্দের অর্থ আর শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ৬৬ ॥

āṅgopāṅga astra kare sva-kārya-sādhana  
aṅgas and upāṅgas —  weapons —  make  of His own  work  the accomplishment  
‘aṅga’ śabdera artha āra śuna diyā mana  
‘aṅga’  of the word  the meaning  another  hear  giving  the mind

(66) These weapons, His intimate companions and devotees, accomplish His work. Now hear with attention another meaning of the word *aṅga*.

‘অঙ্গ’ শব্দে অংশ কহে শাস্ত্র-পরমাণ ৷  
অঙ্গের অবয়ব ‘উপাঙ্গ’ ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ৬৭ ॥

‘aṅga’ śabde aṁśa kahe śāstra-paramāṇa  
‘aṅga’  by the word  a portion  says  of scripture  the evidence  
aṅgera avayava ‘upāṅga’ vyākhyāna  
of the aṅga  a part  ‘upāṅga’  is explained

(67) The evidence of scripture says that the word *aṅga* means a portion (*aṁśa*), and a part of that portion is called *upāṅga*.

নারায়ণস্ত্বং ন হি সর্ব্বদেহিনা-  
মাত্মাস্যধীশাখিললোকসাক্ষী ৷  
নারায়ণোঽঙ্গং নরভূ-জলায়না-  
ত্তচ্চাপি সত্যং ন তবৈব মায়া ॥ ৬৮ ॥

nārāyaṇas tvaṁ na hi sarva-dehinām  
Nārāyaṇa  You  not  indeed  of all  the embodied  
ātmāsy adhīśākhila-loka-sākṣī  
the Self  You are  O Lord  of all  worlds  the witness  
nārāyaṇo ’ṅgaṁ nara-bhū-jalāyanāt  
Nārāyaṇa  (Your) limb  from Nara  born  waters  abode  
tac cāpi satyaṁ na tavaiva māyā  
that  also  true  not  Your  indeed  māyā

(68) “Are You not Nārāyaṇa? For You are the Self of all embodied beings, O Lord, and the witness of all the worlds. Nārāyaṇa is Your portion — so called because His abode is the waters born of Nara; and that too is true, not Your māyā.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.14)[^24]

জলশায়ী অন্তর্য্যামী যেই নারায়ণ ৷  
সেহো তোমার অংশ, তুমি মূল নারায়ণ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

jala-śāyī antar-yāmī yei nārāyaṇa  
on the waters  lying  the Indweller  who  Nārāyaṇa  
seho tomāra aṁśa, tumi mūla nārāyaṇa  
He  Your  portion,  You  the original  Nārāyaṇa

(69) The Nārāyaṇa who lies on the waters and dwells within every heart is Your portion (*aṁśa*); You are the original Nārāyaṇa.[^25]

‘অঙ্গ’-শব্দে অংশ কহে, সেহো সত্য হয় ৷  
মায়াকার্য্য নহে — সব চিদানন্দময় ॥ ৭০ ॥

‘aṅga’-śabde aṁśa kahe, seho satya haya  
‘aṅga’  by the word  a portion  it says,  that  real  is  
māyā-kārya nahe — saba cid-ānanda-maya  
of māyā  a product  not —  all  of consciousness and bliss  full

(70) By the word *aṅga* the portions (*aṁśa*) are meant, and They are real. They are not a product of māyā: all of Them are full of consciousness and bliss.[^26]

অদ্বৈত, নিত্যানন্দ — চৈতন্যের দুই অঙ্গ ৷  
অঙ্গের অবয়বগণ কহিয়ে উপাঙ্গ ॥ ৭১ ॥

advaita, nityānanda — caitanyera dui aṅga  
Advaita,  Nityānanda —  of Caitanya  two  aṅgas  
aṅgera avayava-gaṇa kahiye upāṅga  
of the aṅgas  the parts  all  I call  upāṅgas

(71) Advaita and Nityānanda are the two *aṅgas* of Caitanya, and the parts of these *aṅgas* I call *upāṅgas*.

অঙ্গোপাঙ্গ তীক্ষ্ণ অস্ত্র প্রভুর সহিতে ৷  
সেই সব অস্ত্র হয় পাষণ্ড দলিতে ॥ ৭২ ॥

aṅgopāṅga tīkṣṇa astra prabhura sahite  
aṅgas and upāṅgas  sharp  weapons  the Lord  with  
sei saba astra haya pāṣaṇḍa dalite  
those  all  weapons  are  the unbelievers  to crush

(72) These *aṅgas* and *upāṅgas*, ever with the Lord, are His sharp weapons for crushing the unbelievers.[^27]

নিত্যানন্দ গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ হলধর ৷  
অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর ॥ ৭৩ ॥

nityānanda gosāñi sākṣāt haladhara  
Nityānanda  Gosāñi  directly  the Bearer of the plough  
advaita ācārya gosāñi sākṣāt īśvara  
Advaita  Ācārya  Gosāñi  directly  the Lord

(73) Nityānanda Gosāñi is Haladhara Himself, the bearer of the plough, and Advaita Ācārya Gosāñi is the Lord (*Īśvara*) Himself.[^28]

শ্রীবাসাদি পারিষদ সৈন্য সঙ্গে লঞা ৷  
দুই সেনাপতি বুলেন কীর্ত্তন করিয়া ॥ ৭৪ ॥

śrīvāsādi pāriṣada sainya saṅge lañā  
Śrīvāsa and others  of the associates  the army  with  taking  
dui senā-pati bulena kīrtana kariyā  
two  generals  wander  kīrtana  performing

(74) Taking with them the army of associates headed by Śrīvāsa, the two generals go everywhere performing kīrtana.

পাষণ্ডদলনবানা নিত্যানন্দ রায় ৷  
আচার্য্য-হুঙ্কারে পাপ-পাষণ্ডী পলায় ॥ ৭৫ ॥

pāṣaṇḍa-dalana-vānā nityānanda rāya  
of the unbelievers  of the crushing  the banner (bearing)  Nityānanda  Rāya  
ācārya-huṅkāre pāpa-pāṣaṇḍī palāya  
at the Ācārya’s  roar  sins  unbelievers  flee

(75) Nityānanda Rāya bears the banner of the crusher of unbelievers, and at the roar of the Ācārya sins and unbelievers flee.[^29]

সঙ্কীর্ত্তন-প্রবর্ত্তক শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
সঙ্কীর্ত্তন-যজ্ঞে তাঁরে ভজে, সেই ধন্য ॥ ৭৬ ॥

saṅkīrtana-pravartaka śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
of saṅkīrtana  the founder  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
saṅkīrtana-yajñe tām̐re bhaje, sei dhanya  
by the sacrifice of saṅkīrtana  Him  worships,  he  blessed

(76) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the founder of saṅkīrtana, and blessed is he who worships Him by the sacrifice of saṅkīrtana.

সেই ত’ সুমেধা, আর কুবুদ্ধি সংসার ৷  
সর্ব্ব-যজ্ঞ হৈতে কৃষ্ণনামযজ্ঞ সার ॥ ৭৭ ॥

sei ta’ sumedhā, āra kubuddhi saṁsāra  
he  indeed  intelligent,  but  foolish  the world  
sarva-yajña haite kṛṣṇa-nāma-yajña sāra  
all  sacrifices  than  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the sacrifice  the essence

(77) He alone is truly intelligent; the rest of the world is foolish. Of all sacrifices the sacrifice of Kṛṣṇa’s name is the essence.

কোটি অশ্বমেধ এক কৃষ্ণ নাম সম ৷  
যেই কহে, সে পাষণ্ডী, দণ্ডে তারে যম ॥ ৭৮ ॥

koṭi aśvamedha eka kṛṣṇa nāma sama  
ten million  aśvamedhas  to one  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  equal  
yei kahe, se pāṣaṇḍī, daṇḍe tāre yama  
whoever  says,  he  an unbeliever,  punishes  him  Yama

(78) Whoever says that ten million horse sacrifices (*aśvamedha*) equal one name of Kṛṣṇa is an unbeliever, and Yama punishes him.[^30]

‘ভাগবতসন্দর্ভ’-গ্রন্থের মঙ্গলাচরণে ৷  
এ-শ্লোক জীবগোসাঞি করিয়াছেন ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

‘bhāgavata-sandarbha’-granthera maṅgalācaraṇe  
‘Bhāgavata-sandarbha’  of the book  in the auspicious invocation  
e-śloka jīva-gosāñi kariyāchena vyākhyāne  
this  verse  Jīva  Gosāñi  has made  in the explanation

(79) Śrī Jīva Gosāñi has explained this verse of the *Bhāgavatam* (3.51) in the auspicious invocation of his book *Bhāgavata-sandarbha*.[^31]

অন্তঃকৃষ্ণং বহির্গৌরং দর্শিতাঙ্গাদিবৈভবম্ ৷  
কলৌ সঙ্কীর্ত্তনাদ্যৈঃ স্ম কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যমাশ্রিতাঃ ॥ ৮০ ॥

antaḥ kṛṣṇaṁ bahir gauraṁ  
inwardly  Kṛṣṇa  outwardly  golden  
darśitāṅgādi-vaibhavam  
who has displayed  of the aṅgas and the rest  the splendour  
kalau saṅkīrtanādyaiḥ sma  
in Kali  by saṅkīrtana and the like  indeed  
kṛṣṇa-caitanyam āśritāḥ  
of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  having taken shelter (we)

(80) “Inwardly He is Kṛṣṇa, outwardly He is golden, and He has displayed the splendour of His *aṅgas* and other associates. In this age of Kali, through saṅkīrtana and the other limbs of devotion, we have taken shelter of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.” (Tattva-sandarbha, 2)[^32]

উপপুরাণেহ শুনি শ্রীকৃষ্ণবচন ৷  
কৃপা করি’ ব্যাস প্রতি করিয়াছেন কথন ॥ ৮১ ॥

upa-purāṇeha śuni śrī-kṛṣṇa-vacana  
in the Upapurāṇas  also  we hear  Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  words  
kṛpā kari’ vyāsa prati kariyāchena kathana  
mercy  showing  Vyāsa  to  He has made  the speech

(81) In the Upapurāṇas too we hear the words of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, which He mercifully spoke to Vyāsa:

অহমেব ক্বচিদ্ব্রহ্মন্ সন্ন্যাসাশ্রমমাশ্রিতঃ ৷  
হরিভক্তিং গ্রাহয়ামি কলৌ পাপহতান্নরান্ ॥ ৮২ ॥

aham eva kvacid brahman  
I  alone  at some time  O brāhmaṇa  
sannyāsāśramam āśritaḥ  
of sannyāsa  the order  having taken  
hari-bhaktiṁ grāhayāmi  
to Hari  devotion  I make accept  
kalau pāpa-hatān narān  
in Kali  by sin  struck down  people

(82) “O brāhmaṇa, at some time in the age of Kali I Myself shall take the order of sannyāsa and lead people stricken by sin to devotion to Hari.” (Upapurāṇa)[^33]

ভাগবত, ভারতশাস্ত্র, আগম, পুরাণ ৷  
চৈতন্য-কৃষ্ণ-অবতার-প্রকট প্রমাণ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

bhāgavata, bhārata-śāstra, āgama, purāṇa  
the Bhāgavata,  the Bhārata scripture,  the Āgamas,  the Purāṇas  
caitanya-kṛṣṇa-avatāra-prakaṭa pramāṇa  
of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa’s  descent  manifest  proof

(83) The *Bhāgavatam*, the *Mahābhārata*, the Āgamas and the Purāṇas give clear proof of the descent of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa.[^34]

প্রত্যক্ষে দেখহ নানা প্রকট প্রভাব ৷  
অলৌকিক কর্ম্ম, অলৌকিক অনুভাব ॥ ৮৪ ॥

pratyakṣe dekhaha nānā prakaṭa prabhāva  
directly  see  various  manifest  powers  
alaukika karma, alaukika anubhāva  
extraordinary  deeds,  extraordinary  signs of ecstasy

(84) See for yourselves His many manifest powers — His wondrous deeds and the extraordinary signs of His love.

দেখিয়া না দেখে যত অভক্তের গণ ৷  
উলূকে না দেখে যেন সূর্য্যের কিরণ ॥ ৮৫ ॥

dekhiyā nā dekhe yata abhaktera gaṇa  
seeing  not  see  all  of the non-devotees  the crowd  
ulūke nā dekhe yena sūryera kiraṇa  
the owl  not  sees  as  of the sun  the rays

(85) Yet the non-devotees see without seeing, as the owl does not see the rays of the sun.[^35]

ত্বাং শীলরূপচরিতৈঃ পরমপ্রকৃষ্টৈঃ  
সত্ত্বেন সাত্ত্বিকতয়া প্রবলৈশ্চ শাস্ত্রৈঃ ৷  
প্রখ্যাতদৈবপরমার্থবিদাং মতৈশ্চ  
নৈবাসুরপ্রকৃতয়ঃ প্রভবন্তি বোদ্ধুম্ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

tvāṁ śīla-rūpa-caritaiḥ parama-prakṛṣṭaiḥ  
You  by nature, forms and deeds  supremely  excellent  
sattvena sāttvikatayā prabalaiś ca śāstraiḥ  
by power  by purity  mighty  and  by scriptures  
prakhyāta-daiva-paramārtha-vidāṁ mataiś ca  
of the renowned  knowers of the divine supreme truth  by the opinions  and  
naivāsura-prakṛtayaḥ prabhavanti boddhum  
never  those of demonic nature  are able  to understand

(86) “Though You are made known by Your supremely excellent nature, forms and deeds, by Your wondrous power and purity, by the mighty scriptures and by the teachings of the renowned knowers of the divine Truth, those of demonic nature can never understand You.” (Stotra-ratna, 15)[^36]

আপনা লুকাইতে কৃষ্ণ নানা যত্ন করে ৷  
তথাপি তাঁহার ভক্ত জানয়ে তাঁহারে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

āpanā lukāite kṛṣṇa nānā yatna kare  
Himself  to hide  Kṛṣṇa  various  efforts  makes  
tathāpi tām̐hāra bhakta jānaye tām̐hāre  
still  His  devotee  knows  Him

(87) Kṛṣṇa tries in many ways to hide Himself, yet His devotee knows Him.

উল্লঙ্ঘিতত্রিবিধসীমসমাতিশায়ি-  
সম্ভাবনং তব পরিব্রঢ়িমস্বভাবম্ ৷  
মায়াবলেন ভবতাপি নিগুহ্যমানং  
পশ্যন্তি কেচিদনিশং ত্বদনন্যভাবাঃ ॥ ৮৮ ॥

ullaṅghita-trividha-sīma-samātiśāyi-  
transgressing  three kinds of  limits,  equal and superior  
sambhāvanaṁ tava parivraḍhima-svabhāvam  
the possibility (excluding)  Your  of supremacy  the nature  
māyā-balena bhavatāpi niguhyamānaṁ  
of māyā  by the power  by You  even  being hidden  
paśyanti kecid aniśaṁ tvad-ananya-bhāvāḥ  
see  some  constantly  to You  exclusively devoted

(88) “Your sovereign nature transcends the three kinds of limits and admits of no equal or superior. Though You hide it by the power of Your māyā, some who are devoted to You alone see it constantly.” (Stotra-ratna, 18)[^37]

অসুরস্বভাবে কৃষ্ণে কভু নাহি জানে ৷  
লুকাইতে নারে কৃষ্ণ ভক্তজন-স্থানে ॥ ৮৯ ॥

asura-svabhāve kṛṣṇe kabhu nāhi jāne  
of demonic  nature  Kṛṣṇa  ever  not  know  
lukāite nāre kṛṣṇa bhakta-jana-sthāne  
to hide  cannot  Kṛṣṇa  of the devotees  of the people  from the place

(89) Those of demonic nature never know Kṛṣṇa, but from His devotees He cannot hide.

দ্বৌ ভূতসর্গৌ লোকেঽস্মিন্ দৈব আসুর এব চ ৷  
বিষ্ণুভক্তঃ স্মৃতো দৈব আসুরস্তদ্বিপর্য্যয়ঃ ॥ ৯০ ॥

dvau bhūta-sargau loke ’smin  
two  of beings  creations  in world  this  
daiva āsura eva ca  
divine  demonic  indeed  and  
viṣṇu-bhaktaḥ smṛto daiva  
of Viṣṇu  the devotee  is called  divine  
āsuras tad-viparyayaḥ  
demonic  of that  the opposite

(90) “In this world there are two kinds of created beings, the divine and the demonic. The devotee of Viṣṇu is called divine, and his opposite demonic.” (Padma Purāṇa)[^38]

আচার্য্য গোসাঞি প্রভুর ভক্ত-অবতার ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-অবতার-হেতু যাঁহার হুঙ্কার ॥ ৯১ ॥

ācārya gosāñi prabhura bhakta-avatāra  
the Ācārya  Gosāñi —  of the Lord  as devotee  avatāra  
kṛṣṇa-avatāra-hetu yām̐hāra huṅkāra  
of Kṛṣṇa’s  descent  the cause  whose  roar

(91) Ācārya Gosāñi is the Lord’s avatāra as a devotee, and His roar was the cause of Kṛṣṇa’s descent.

কৃষ্ণ যদি পৃথিবীতে করেন অবতার ৷  
প্রথমে করেন গুরুবর্গের সঞ্চার ॥ ৯২ ॥

kṛṣṇa yadi pṛthivīte karena avatāra  
Kṛṣṇa  when  on earth  makes  descent  
prathame karena guru-vargera sañcāra  
first  makes  of the elders  the appearance

(92) When Kṛṣṇa descends to earth, He first causes His elders to appear.

পিতা মাতা গুরু আদি যত মান্যগণ ৷  
প্রথমে করেন সবার পৃথিবীতে জনম ॥ ৯৩ ॥

pitā mātā guru ādi yata mānya-gaṇa  
father  mother  guru  and others  all  honoured ones  
prathame karena sabāra pṛthivīte janama  
first  makes  of all  on earth  the birth

(93) Father, mother, guru and all who are to be honoured — He first has them all take birth on earth.

মাধব-ঈশ্বর-পুরী, শচী, জগন্নাথ ৷  
অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য প্রকট হৈলা সেই সাথ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

mādhava-īśvara-purī, śacī, jagannātha  
Mādhava(endra)  Īśvara  Purī,  Śacī,  Jagannātha  
advaita ācārya prakaṭa hailā sei sātha  
Advaita  Ācārya  manifest  became  together with them

(94) Mādhavendra Purī, Īśvara Purī, Śacī and Jagannātha appeared, and Advaita Ācārya appeared along with them.

প্রকটিয়া দেখে আচার্য্য সকল সংসার ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তিগন্ধহীন বিষয়-ব্যবহার ॥ ৯৫ ॥

prakaṭiyā dekhe ācārya sakala saṁsāra  
having appeared  sees  the Ācārya  the whole  world  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti gandha-hīna viṣaya-vyavahāra  
of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the scent  devoid of  worldly  dealings

(95) Having appeared, the Ācārya saw the whole world busy with worldly affairs, without even a trace of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

কেহ পাপে, কেহ পুণ্যে করে বিষয়-ভোগ ৷  
ভক্তিগন্ধ নাহি, যাতে যায় ভবরোগ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

keha pāpe, keha puṇye kare viṣaya-bhoga  
some  by sin,  some  by piety  make  of sense objects  enjoyment  
bhakti-gandha nāhi, yāte yāya bhava-roga  
of devotion  the scent  is not,  by which  goes  of worldly existence  the disease

(96) Some through sin, some through piety, all were enjoying the objects of the senses; there was not even a scent of the devotion that cures the disease of worldly existence.

লোকগতি দেখি’ আচার্য্য করুণ-হৃদয় ৷  
বিচার করেন, লোকের কৈছে হিত হয় ॥ ৯৭ ॥

loka-gati dekhi’ ācārya karuṇa-hṛdaya  
of the people  the condition  seeing  the Ācārya  compassionate  in heart  
vicāra karena, lokera kaiche hita haya  
reflection  makes,  of the people  how  good  may be

(97) Seeing the state of the people, the compassionate Ācārya pondered how they might be helped.

আপনি শ্রীকৃষ্ণ যদি করেন অবতার ৷  
আপনে আচরি’ ভক্তি করেন প্রচার ॥ ৯৮ ॥

āpani śrī-kṛṣṇa yadi karena avatāra  
Himself  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  if  makes  descent  
āpane ācari’ bhakti karena pracāra  
Himself  practising  devotion  makes  the preaching

(98) He thought: “If Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself were to descend, He would practise devotion Himself and so spread it.

নাম বিনু কলিকালে ধর্ম্ম নাহি আর ৷  
কলিকালে কৈছে হবে কৃষ্ণ অবতার ॥ ৯৯ ॥

nāma vinu kali-kāle dharma nāhi āra  
the name  besides  in the age of Kali  religion  is not  other  
kali-kāle kaiche habe kṛṣṇa avatāra  
in the age of Kali  how  will be  Kṛṣṇa’s  descent

(99) In the age of Kali there is no religion but the holy name. Yet how will Kṛṣṇa descend in the age of Kali?

শুদ্ধভাবে করিব কৃষ্ণের আরাধন ৷  
নিরন্তর সদৈন্যে করিব নিবেদন ॥ ১০০ ॥

śuddha-bhāve kariba kṛṣṇera ārādhana  
in a pure  mood  I shall do  of Kṛṣṇa  worship  
nirantara sadainye kariba nivedana  
unceasingly  with humility  I shall make  petition

(100) With a pure heart I shall worship Kṛṣṇa, and with humility I shall pray to Him unceasingly.

আনিয়া কৃষ্ণেরে করোঁ কীর্ত্তন সঞ্চার ৷  
তবে সে ‘অদ্বৈত’ নাম সফল আমার ॥ ১০১ ॥

āniyā kṛṣṇere karom̐ kīrtana sañcāra  
having brought  Kṛṣṇa  I make  of kīrtana  the spreading  
tabe se ‘advaita’ nāma saphala āmāra  
then  only  ‘Advaita’  the name  fruitful  My

(101) Only when I have brought Kṛṣṇa here and spread His kīrtana will My name ‘Advaita’ bear fruit.”[^39]

কৃষ্ণ বশ করিবেন কোন্ আরাধনে ৷  
বিচারিতে এক শ্লোক আইল তাঁর মনে ॥ ১০২ ॥

kṛṣṇa vaśa karibena kon ārādhane  
Kṛṣṇa  subdued  will make  by what  worship  
vicārite eka śloka āila tām̐ra mane  
while reflecting  one  verse  came  His  to mind

(102) As He pondered by what worship Kṛṣṇa could be won over, a verse came to His mind.

তুলসীদলমাত্রেণ জলস্য চুলুকেন বা ৷  
বিক্রীণীতে স্বমাত্মানং ভক্তেভ্যো ভক্তবৎসলঃ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

tulasī-dala-mātreṇa  
of tulasī  by a leaf  merely  
jalasya culukena vā  
of water  by a palmful  or  
vikrīṇīte svam ātmānaṁ  
sells  His own  self  
bhaktebhyo bhakta-vatsalaḥ  
to the devotees  affectionate to His devotees

(103) “For merely a leaf of tulasī or a palmful of water, the Lord who loves His devotees sells Himself to them.” (Viṣṇu-dharma; Gautamīya-tantra)[^40]

এই শ্লোকার্থ আচার্য্য করেন বিচারণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণকে তুলসীজল দেয় যেই জন ॥ ১০৪ ॥

তার ঋণ শোধিতে কৃষ্ণ করেন চিন্তন ৷  
‘জল-তুলসীর সম কিছু ঘরে নাহি ধন’ ॥ ১০৫ ॥

ei ślokārtha ācārya karena vicāraṇa  
this  of the verse the meaning  the Ācārya  makes  consideration  
kṛṣṇake tulasī-jala deya yei jana  
to Kṛṣṇa  tulasī and water  gives  who  the person  
tāra ṛṇa śodhite kṛṣṇa karena cintana  
his  debt  to repay  Kṛṣṇa  makes  reflection  
‘jala-tulasīra sama kichu ghare nāhi dhana’  
‘water and tulasī  equal to  anything  in the house  is not  wealth’

(104–105) The Ācārya pondered the meaning of this verse: “Kṛṣṇa is indebted to the person who offers Him tulasī and water. He considers how to repay him, and finds: ‘In all My house there is no wealth equal to water and tulasī.’”[^41]

তবে আত্মা বেচি’ করে ঋণের শোধন ৷  
এত ভাবি’ আচার্য্য করেন আরাধন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

tabe ātmā veci’ kare ṛṇera śodhana  
then  Himself  having sold  makes  of the debt  the repayment  
eta bhāvi’ ācārya karena ārādhana  
thus  having thought  the Ācārya  makes  worship

(106) So He repays the debt by selling Himself. Thinking thus, the Ācārya began His worship.

গঙ্গাজলে তুলসীমঞ্জরী অনুক্ষণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণপাদপদ্ম ভাবি’ করে সমর্পণ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

gaṅgā-jale tulasī-mañjarī anukṣaṇa  
with Gaṅgā water  tulasī  buds  constantly  
kṛṣṇa-pāda-padma bhāvi’ kare samarpaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  lotuses  meditating on  makes  offering

(107) Meditating on Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, He constantly offered Him tulasī buds (*mañjarī*) with the water of the Gaṅgā.

কৃষ্ণের আহ্বান করে করিয়া হুঙ্কার ৷  
এমতে কৃষ্ণের করাইল অবতার ॥ ১০৮ ॥

kṛṣṇera āhvāna kare kariyā huṅkāra  
of Kṛṣṇa  the invocation  makes  making  a roar  
e-mate kṛṣṇera karāila avatāra  
in this way  of Kṛṣṇa  He caused  the descent

(108) With a mighty roar He called out to Kṛṣṇa, and in this way He brought about Kṛṣṇa’s descent.[^42]

চৈতন্যের অবতারে এই মুখ্য হেতু ৷  
ভক্তের ইচ্ছায় অবতরে ধর্ম্মসেতু ॥ ১০৯ ॥

caitanyera avatāre ei mukhya hetu  
of Caitanya  in the descent  this  the chief  cause  
bhaktera icchāya avatare dharma-setu  
of the devotee  by the desire  descends  of dharma  the bridge (protector)

(109) This is the chief cause of Caitanya’s descent: the Lord, the protector of dharma, descends by the desire of His devotee.[^43]

ত্বং ভক্তিযোগপরিভাবিত-হৃৎসরোজ  
আস্সে শ্রুতেক্ষিতপথো ননু নাথ পুংসাম্ ৷  
যদ্যদ্ধিয়া ত উরুগায় বিভাবয়ন্তি  
তত্তদ্বপুঃ প্রণয়সে সদনুগ্রহায় ॥ ১১০ ॥

tvaṁ bhakti-yoga-paribhāvita-hṛt-saroja  
You  by devotional service  made fit  of the heart  in the lotus  
āsse śrutekṣita-patho nanu nātha puṁsām  
You dwell  by hearing  seen  whose path  surely  O Lord  of persons  
yad yad dhiyā ta urugāya vibhāvayanti  
whatever  whatever  with the mind  Your  O widely sung One  they contemplate  
tat tad vapuḥ praṇayase sad-anugrahāya  
that  that  form  You reveal  to the devotees  out of mercy

(110) “O Lord, the path to You is seen through hearing, and You dwell in the lotus of the heart that devotion has purified. O Urugāya, widely sung One, whatever form of Yours Your devotees contemplate, that very form You reveal to them out of mercy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.9.11)[^44]

এই শ্লোকের অর্থ কহি সংক্ষেপের সার ৷  
ভক্তের ইচ্ছায় কৃষ্ণের সর্ব্ব অবতার ॥ ১১১ ॥

ei ślokera artha kahi saṅkṣepera sāra  
this  of the verse  the meaning  I tell  of the summary  the essence  
bhaktera icchāya kṛṣṇera sarva avatāra  
of the devotee  by the desire  of Kṛṣṇa  all  descents

(111) The meaning of this verse, in brief, is this: all Kṛṣṇa’s descents take place by the desire of His devotees.

চতুর্থ শ্লোকের অর্থ কৈল সুনিশ্চিতে ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হৈলা গৌর প্রেম প্রকাশিতে ॥ ১১২ ॥

caturtha ślokera artha kaila suniścite  
of the fourth  verse  the meaning  I have made  well ascertained  
avatīrṇa hailā gaura prema prakāśite  
descended  became  Gaura  love  to reveal

(112) Thus I have made the meaning of the fourth verse clear: Gaura descended to reveal divine love.[^45]

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১১৩ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(113) Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

---

[^1]: The same verse as Ādi 1.4. *Anubhāṣya*: with this verse Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī, blessing the world, opens the drama *Vidagdha-mādhava*, and the author, his follower, follows the guru he holds dear. *Unnatojjvala-rasa* is “the exalted *ujjvala-rasa*, that is, *śṛṅgāra-rasa*”; *sva-bhakti-śrī* is “the beauty of prema for Him”; *sphuratu* — “may He reveal Himself”.

[^2]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in Goloka, the splendour (*vaibhava*) of Gokula, Kṛṣṇa dwells eternally with everything that makes up the rasa of Vraja; these are His unmanifest (*aprakaṭa*) pastimes. Once in every kalpa, that is, in each day of Brahmā, He descends into the world and displays them as manifest (*prakaṭa*) pastimes.

[^3]: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Sūrya-siddhānta*): Kali-yuga lasts 432,000 solar years, Dvāpara twice as long, Tretā three times and Satya four times; together they make 4,320,000 years — a *mahā-yuga*, or *divya-yuga*. Seventy-one *mahā-yugas* make a manvantara; fourteen manvantaras, with fifteen junctions (*sandhi*) each as long as a Satya-yuga, make a thousand *yugas* — a day of Brahmā, or kalpa.

[^4]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «সহিত» (another reading: «সহিতে»). *Anubhāṣya*: when twenty-seven *mahā-yugas* of the Vaivasvata manvantara have passed, in the twenty-eighth, after Satya and Tretā, at the end of Dvāpara, comes the time of Kṛṣṇa’s appearance.

[^5]: *Anubhāṣya*: *śānta-rasa* is not named here because, though in the material world it is held highest, in the spiritual world it stands lowest: in it there is no exchange of feeling between Kṛṣṇa and His devotee. In servitude, friendship, parental love and *madhura* the love for Kṛṣṇa rises step by step. *Śṛṅgāra* is the love of the beloved ones of Vraja, not a conjugal relationship (see Ādi 1.4).

[^6]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in *vidhi-bhakti* the awareness of majesty (*aiśvarya*) prevails; in that mood prema slackens and loses its depth, and such love does not please the Lord.

[^7]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *sārṣṭi* — opulence equal to Viṣṇu’s; *sārūpya* — a form like Viṣṇu’s, with four arms and so on; *sāmīpya* — dwelling near Him; *sālokya* — residence in His world. The *Anubhāṣya* refers to the *Bhāgavatam* (3.29.13): “Even when offered *sālokya*, *sārṣṭi*, *sārūpya*, *sāmīpya* and oneness, My people do not accept them without My service.”

[^8]: *Anubhāṣya*: the saints (*sādhu*) are those devoted to serving the Lord, and He delivers them from the obstacles to their service; the wicked (*duṣkṛt*) are the enemies of the devotees whom none but He can slay — Rāvaṇa, Kaṁsa, Keśī and others; dharma is non-envious service to Bhagavān, whose chief mark is saṅkīrtana.

[^9]: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord, free from any desire for worldly enjoyment, acts to set an example: if He did not act, people taking Him as their model would fall; *upahanyām* — “I would ruin”, that is, “make them impure”.

[^10]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord wishes to appear in order to bring the world two things — the religion of the age, *nāma-saṅkīrtana*, and the love of Vraja. The religion of the age can be established even by a partial avatāra, but the love of Vraja none can give except the complete Bhagavān, Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

[^11]: In the edition — “the words of Bilvamaṅgala” (*Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, Pūrva-khaṇḍa, 5.37). *Anubhāṣya* and *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the “creepers” (*latā*) are those who have taken shelter of Him.

[^12]: *Anubhāṣya*: the first *sandhyā* is the opening period of an age, a twelfth part of it; the first *sandhyā* of Kali-yuga lasts 36,000 solar years. Śrī Gaurasundara appeared when 4,586 years of Kali had passed — that is, in its first *sandhyā* — at Śrī Māyāpur in Navadvīpa.

[^13]: In the edition verses 3.32 and 3.34 are headed: the name Viśvambhara is the presiding Deity of *abhidheya* (the means), the name Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya the presiding Deity of *sambandha* (knowledge of the relationship). *Anubhāṣya*: the later pastimes are the twenty-four years after sannyāsa. Mahāprabhu received the name “Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya” when He took sannyāsa from Keśava Bhāratī; in Śaṅkara’s line this is a *brahmacārī* name, and He never called Himself by the sannyāsa title “Bhāratī”: in that line the sannyāsa name carries the claim to be God, whereas the *brahmacārī* name breathes service to the guru and is not opposed to bhakti.

[^14]: Garga Muni’s words to Nanda Mahārāja. *Anubhāṣya*: *tathā* (“likewise”) points to the future: He will be yellow in the first *sandhyā* of the Kali-yuga of the twenty-eighth *mahā-yuga* of the Vaivasvata manvantara. Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī (*Krama-sandarbha*, quoted in the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* on 3.51) adds that Śrī Kṛṣṇa is the complete form, in whom all the avatāras are contained.

[^15]: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Madhvācārya, in his commentary on the *Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad*, quotes the *Nārāyaṇa-saṁhitā*: “In Dvāpara people worship Viṣṇu by the Pañcarātra, but in Kali the Lord Hari is worshipped by the name alone.” The *Kali-santaraṇa Upaniṣad*: “Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare / Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare — these sixteen names destroy the contamination of Kali; no higher means is seen in all the Vedas.”

[^16]: *Anubhāṣya*: *nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala* is one whose height and girth are four of his own cubits — a *mahāpuruṣa*; so too is Viṣṇu called, complete in all four of His forms (*catur-vyūha*), who, surpassing all beings, holds them in check by His *māyā*.

[^17]: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Thousand Names* is the *Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma* of the *Mahābhārata*, Dāna-dharma, chapter 149; commentaries on it were written by Śaṅkarācārya, Śrī Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa and other Vaiṣṇava ācāryas.

[^18]: *Anubhāṣya*: the early pastimes are His life as a householder, the first twenty-four years; the later are His sannyāsa, the last twenty-four years (cf. 3.32–34). The eight names are given in the next verse.

[^19]: *Anubhāṣya*: the first four names belong to the early pastimes, the last four to the later. According to the *Anubhāṣya* and the commentary *Nāmārtha-sudhā* of Śrī Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa which it quotes: *varāṅga* — a body bearing the marks of a *mahāpuruṣa*; *candanāṅgadī* — wearing sandal armlets that gladden the hearts of the devotees; *śama* — free from worldly attraction (Baladeva: contemplating the secrets of Hari); *śānta* — whose mind is fixed on Kṛṣṇa alone; *niṣṭhā* — steadfastness, one-pointedness of mind (Baladeva: in Him the sacrifice of Hari-kīrtana is established); *śānti* — peace (Baladeva: He subdues the opponents of bhakti, the *kevalādvaitins*); *parāyaṇa* — the highest shelter (Baladeva: of all the moods up to *mahābhāva*). The source is given as in the edition.

[^20]: *Anubhāṣya*: *kṛṣṇa-varṇam* — “who sings of Kṛṣṇa”, or “in whom are the two syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’” (3.53); *tviṣākṛṣṇam* — not dark by lustre, that is, golden: of the white, red and yellow (3.36) the third remains; the *aṅgas* are Nityānanda and Advaita, the *upāṅgas* Śrīvāsa and the other devotees, the weapons the holy name and the like, the associates Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara and others; *saṅkīrtana* is many people singing together the names of Hari and His glories. That He is Kṛṣṇa Himself the author shows below (3.79–80).

[^21]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «অকৃষ্ণবরণে তাঁর কহে» (in other copies «তাঁর» is missing).

[^22]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: through the overwhelming lustre of Śrī Rādhikā’s heart, Śrī Kṛṣṇa has become not dark but golden; He alone is worshipped by the *paramahaṁsas* — those who have entered the fourth āśrama, sannyāsa.

[^23]: *Anubhāṣya*: the gods headed by Śiva and Brahmā took human bodies to nourish their love for Him; “His own devotees” are Svarūpa, Rāmānanda and His other intimates; the “pure way of worship” is free from other desires and uncovered by karma and jñāna.

[^24]: The same verse as Ādi 2.30 (see the note there on the three meanings of the name Nārāyaṇa). Here the author cites it for the word *aṅgam*, “part”: Nārāyaṇa is Your *aṅga*, that is, Your *aṁśa* (3.67, 3.69).

[^25]: In the edition the verse is introduced by the words «অস্যার্থঃ» — “its meaning (of verse 3.68) is this”.

[^26]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *aṅga* here means the portions (*aṁśa*), the three Puruṣas headed by Kāraṇodakaśāyī; They are full of consciousness and bliss, the true Īśvaras, not anything made by māyā; and so Advaita and Nityānanda are the Lord’s two *aṅgas*. *Anubhāṣya*: in the realm of māyā a thing divided by māyā becomes a part; but Viṣṇu-tattva is not subject to māyā, and Viṣṇu, though a portion, is not divided in substance — like a lamp lit from a lamp. All the Viṣṇu forms are masters of māyā, and she has no power over Them; it is otherwise with separated parts such as Brahmā and Śiva, who are capable of transformation, as milk is of turning into curd.

[^27]: *Anubhāṣya*: the unbelievers (*pāṣaṇḍa*) are those who imagine Viṣṇu, the master of māyā, to be equal to Śiva and others who are subject to māyā, and who, not realising the eternity of the Lord’s pastimes, take eternal devotion to be mere transient activity; to dispel their folly Viṣṇu and His own people labour.

[^28]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: that is, Advaita is directly the avatāra of Mahā-Viṣṇu.

[^29]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *vānā* — a sign, a banner: an instrument like trumpets and drums by which the crushing of the unbelievers is proclaimed.

[^30]: *Anubhāṣya*: to consider the name equal to pious rites — vows, renunciation, fire sacrifices — is the eighth offence against the holy name, to be avoided in every way. “The gift of ten million cows at an eclipse, a whole kalpa’s dwelling at Prayāga on the Gaṅgā, ten thousand sacrifices, a gift of gold as great as Mount Meru — none of these equals a hundredth part of the glorification of Govinda.”

[^31]: *Anubhāṣya*: the verse “*antaḥ kṛṣṇam…*” (3.80) is the second verse of the invocation of the *Ṣaṭ-sandarbha* (*Bhāgavata-sandarbha*) and is simply an explanation of Karabhājana’s verse (3.51); its meaning is unfolded at the beginning of the *Sarva-saṁvādinī*.

[^32]: *Anubhāṣya*: *antaḥ kṛṣṇam* — within, in His heart, He is Kṛṣṇa, but the mood of His heart, the heart of the Nāgara, is covered by the mood of Rādhā’s heart; *bahir gauram* — outwardly, by the radiance of His body, He is golden; *darśitāṅgādi-vaibhavam* — who has displayed the splendour of His *aṅgas*, *upāṅgas*, weapons and associates. The source is given as in the edition: *Tattva-sandarbha*, the first of the six *Sandarbhas*, verse 2.

[^33]: *Anubhāṣya*: “at some time” (*kvacit*) — in the first *sandhyā* of the Kali-yuga of the twenty-eighth *catur-yuga* of the Vaivasvata manvantara; sannyāsa is the fourth āśrama.

[^34]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in the *Bhāgavatam* — “*kṛṣṇa-varṇaṁ tviṣākṛṣṇam*”, “*āsan varṇās trayaḥ*”, “*channaḥ kalau*” (“hidden in Kali”); in the *Mahābhārata* — “*sambhavāmi yuge yuge*”, “*sannyāsa-kṛc chamaḥ śāntaḥ*”; in the Vedas — “*mahān prabhur vai puruṣaḥ*”, “*yadā paśyaḥ paśyate rukma-varṇam*”; in the tantras that follow the Āgamas — “*māyāpure bhaviṣyāmi śacī-sutaḥ*”; in the Upapurāṇa — “*aham eva…*”: thus it is proved that Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa is the direct descent of the Lord.

[^35]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *ulūka* — the owl, blind by day; not seeing the sun’s rays, it cannot admit that the sun exists.

[^36]: The source is given as in the edition: Śrī Yāmunācārya’s (Ālavandār’s) *Stotra-ratna*, verse 15. *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Yāmunācārya is the guru and grand-guru of Śrī Rāmānujācārya; *sattvena* — by extraordinary power, *sāttvikatayā* — by the predominance of pure goodness; those of demonic nature are the ill-natured, hostile to the devotees. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Vyāsa and other devotees who know the supreme truth come to know You through the powerful scriptures of goodness.

[^37]: The source is given as in the edition: verse 18. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: everything is bound by three limits — place, time and thought — but Your hidden nature, which has no equal or superior, surpasses them; You cover it by the power of māyā, yet Your exclusive devotees always see You. *Anubhāṣya*: “by the power of māyā” — by the power of His own *yogamāyā*.

[^38]: *Anubhāṣya*: the demonic is one absorbed in the enjoyment of māyā (*māyā-bhoga-nirataḥ*), a man of material nature.

[^39]: In the edition the verse is headed: “Viṣṇu’s descent is brought about through Viṣṇu — hence His name Advaita”: Advaita, Viṣṇu Himself, is non-different from the Lord whom He calls.

[^40]: The source is given as in the edition («বিষ্ণুধর্ম-বচন ও গৌতমীয়-তন্ত্র-বাক্য»). *Anubhāṣya*: “merely a leaf of tulasī” — without sandal paste, mantras and the rest; *vikrīṇīte* (“sells”) — He places Himself under His devotees’ control. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: some copies have here two more verses on the tulasī buds: “As Rādhā is dear to Viṣṇu, so is the *mañjarī* dear to Hari; therefore one should offer it with care, mixed with sandal.”

[^41]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «এই শ্লোকার্থ» (another reading: «এ শ্লোকার্থ»). In the edition 3.104 and 3.105 are numbered separately.

[^42]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «কৃষ্ণের» (another reading: «কৃষ্ণেরে»); the sense is the same.

[^43]: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇa, the protector (literally “the bridge”, *setu*) of dharma, descends by the desire of His devotee; Caitanya appeared at the prayer of the great devotee Advaita Ācārya.

[^44]: Brahmā’s prayer. *Anubhāṣya*: *śrutekṣita-pathaḥ* — “He whose path is seen through hearing the conclusions of scripture”; *paribhāvita* — the heart made worthy by devotion, by love; *urugāya* — “widely sung”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: You ever move along the paths of Your devotees’ hearing and sight.

[^45]: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «কৈল» (“I have made”; another reading: «হৈল», “has become”).
