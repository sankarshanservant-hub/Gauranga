## 9

> Subheading: Devotion to Gaura without devotion to Kṛṣṇa, and devotion to Kṛṣṇa without devotion to Gaura, are non-devotion (*abhakti*) —

Jarāsandha and others abandoned the worship of Śrī Kṛṣṇa — the Supreme Truth, Viṣṇu, the original form (*svayaṁ-rūpa*) — out of hatred or indifference toward Him. They worshipped Viṣṇu with Vedic mantras, but even that worship ended in nothing but demoniac *dharma*. In the same way, when the *jīva* forgets his nature as a minute particle of spirit (*aṇu-cid-dharma*), that is, his servitude to Caitanya, his attempt to worship Viṣṇu is also nothing but a calamitous, demoniac *dharma* — that is, non-Vaiṣṇavism.

## 10

> Subheading: The reason for the Lord's pastime of *sannyāsa* —

## 11

Śrī Caitanyacandra is a Reality nondifferent from the truth of Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa. Out of ignorance some people equated the pastimes of Śrī Gaurasundara with their own material attachments, full of the desire for enjoyment, and so they could not understand Him. Considering their predicament, Śrī Gaurahari Himself, before the eyes of the world, accepted the *dharma* of a renunciant (*yati-dharma*) and thus showed mercy to foolish people.

## 12

> Subheading: To lack devotion to the supremely magnanimous Gaura is a demoniac disposition —

“O *jīva*, worship Kṛṣṇa alone!” — such is the mercy of Śrī Caitanyacandra. Whoever cannot even perceive it is certainly an *asura*, a non-Vaiṣṇava devoid of devotion to Viṣṇu, however much worldly people may honor him as the best in the world. And if one abandons the worship of Kṛṣṇa and worships Caitanya, Śrī Caitanya's mercy is not upon him: such worship is imaginary, born of Kali. The same holds for one who follows the godless *smārtas* or the society of *pañcopāsakas* (worshippers of five deities) and strives to worship Viṣṇu for some petty, perishable self-interest. He may reject one of the six *tattvas* whose very self is Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and show faith or worship to another. Or he may consider Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu merely the best of mortal beings, separate from Kṛṣṇa, and so have no faith in Gaura's name, Gaura's mantra and Gaura's *śakti*. This too is demoniac *dharma* — imagination born of Kali and opposed to the truth.

> Subheading: Gaura's mercy is more wonderful than any other mercy —

## 14–15

By the power of their own narrow, pleasure-seeking intelligence, people imagine one ideal of mercy or another. But the mercy of Śrī Caitanyacandra is not of that kind.

After studying the science of logic (*tarka-śāstra*), a person comes to think that no other faculty equals reasoning (*tarka*) in the power to determine the nature of a thing and to uncover the truth. So, falling into the hands of reasoning, the *jīva* gives it the place of his sole patron and protector. But if an intelligent *jīva* carefully examines the foundation on which reasoning stands, he can understand how weak, how incapable and how deficient his worldly senses of knowledge are when it comes to grasping the subject of Bhagavān. Often reasoning merely settles on untruth as a truth established by argument; and so, as the fruit of false argument (*kutarka*), one obtains birth as a jackal.

Even so, there are people ready to determine the true nature of things relying chiefly on direct perception (*pratyakṣa*) and inference (*anumāna*). Kavirāja Gosvāmī addresses them too. He says: let those who have sight and the power of judgment, who have experienced every picture of every kind of mercy or have gained the ability and the opportunity to see them, make a list of all kinds of mercy and compare them with the mercy of Śrī Gaurahari. Then they will know that a mercy like that of Śrī Gaurahari is found in no created thing, nor in the creator, nor in the series of avatāras, nor even in the source of the avatāras — in Kṛṣṇa. The mercy of Gaurahari, the embodiment of magnanimity (*udāra-vigraha*), certainly brings more wonder and amazement than any other.

## 16

> Subheading: As long as offenses remain, hearing and chanting about Kṛṣṇa countless times is in vain —

If someone takes up the devotion called hearing and chanting without taking shelter of Śrī Caitanya's feet, he has no prospect of attaining love for Kṛṣṇa even in many births. According to the teaching of Śrī Caitanyacandra, devotees are lower than a blade of grass and more tolerant than a tree; free from pride themselves, they honor others and are not occupied with any worldly conceit. Such persons become free from the grip of the ten offenses, are able to take Kṛṣṇa's name constantly, and attain *prema*.

The meaning is this. The *jīva* who has turned away from the service of Kṛṣṇa seeks to gratify the material senses or to achieve some perishable self-interest. He imagines a difference between Kṛṣṇa's name and Kṛṣṇa, the possessor of the name — between the word that designates (*vācaka*) and the One designated (*vācya*) — as if it were the label of some object perceptible to the material senses, or ordinary material letters. He does not know himself to be the eternal servant of Śrī Nāma-Prabhu. Such a *jīva* may utter the name and so forth with offenses countless times, yet he will not be able to attain *kṛṣṇa-prema*, which is attained through pure *sādhana-bhakti*. The words of the *Padma Purāṇa* quoted in the *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa* (11.289): “If even one name [of the Lord] is on a person's tongue, or has entered the path of his memory, or has reached his ears — whether pronounced correctly or incorrectly, so long as it is not broken — it certainly delivers him. But if, O brāhmaṇa, it is cast amid [concern for] body, wealth, followers, greed and godlessness, it does not quickly bear fruit here.” And further: “Therefore the name of Śrī Kṛṣṇa and all that is His cannot be grasped by the senses. Only when the tongue and the other senses are turned toward service does it reveal itself upon them.” (*Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, *Pūrva-vibhāga* 2.234)

## 20

> Subheading: But in the pastime of Gaurasundara, the embodiment of magnanimity, *prema-bhakti* is given to all, down to the most fallen —

Even sinful and wicked people like Jagāi and Mādhāi, once they receive Gaura's mercy, will give up sin and wickedness and one day attain *prema* for Śrī Kṛṣṇa.
