# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Madhya-līlā 11

## 0

**Summary.** Sārvabhauma tried to arrange a meeting between Pratāparudra and Mahāprabhu, but Mahāprabhu refused. Rāmānanda Rāya came to Puruṣottama, met Mahāprabhu and described the king's many Vaiṣṇava qualities, and the Lord's heart changed. The king revealed his humility and his vow to Sārvabhauma. Sārvabhauma told the king of a way to see Mahāprabhu's feet. When the time of *anavasara* came, Mahāprabhu, distraught at being unable to see the Lord, went to Ālālanātha. Some time later He heard that the devotees were coming from Gauḍa and returned to Puruṣottama. As Śrī Advaita and the other devotees approached, Svarūpa and Govinda took garlands given by the Lord and went to bring them. The king watched the Vaiṣṇavas arrive from the roof of his palace. At Sārvabhauma's wish, Śrī Gopīnātha Ācārya identified each of these Vaiṣṇavas. The king and Sārvabhauma then discussed at length Śrī Caitanya's being Kṛṣṇa, and the newly arrived Vaiṣṇavas' honoring *prasāda* without first shaving their heads and fasting. After this the king arranged lodgings and *prasāda* for the Vaiṣṇavas. Mahāprabhu had many joyful conversations with Vāsudeva Datta and the other Vaiṣṇavas. Seeing Haridāsa's humility, He gave him a secluded place in the garden (the Ṭoṭā) and spoke of Haridāsa's glory. Then, in the temple of Jagannātha, He divided the devotees into four groups, and a great *saṅkīrtana* took place. (After this) the Vaiṣṇavas went to their own lodgings at the Lord's order.

## 1

Śrī Gauracandra, whose body was adorned with many kinds of *bhāvas*, performed an exceedingly wild dance with the devotees in the house of Śrī Jagannātha, and by His own sweetness drowned this world in a flood of love.

## 8

Śrī Caitanyadeva said sorrowfully: “Alas! There are *niṣkiñcanas*, people free of worldly attachment, who have turned to the Lord's service and wish to cross the ocean of worldly existence completely. For them, seeing worldly people and women is worse than taking poison.”

## 9–10

Sārvabhauma said, “Prabhu, what You say is true. But King Pratāparudradeva is a servant of Jagannātha and the best of devotees.” The Lord replied: “He may be Jagannātha's servant and the best of devotees, but a ‘king’ is like a deadly serpent. See: even touching a woman carved of wood can cause some agitation. In the same way, merely seeing a king, even the best of devotees, can give rise to an *anartha*, something unwanted, in one who is renounced.”

## 11

Just as the sight of a snake, and even of something shaped like one, agitates the mind, so even the outward form of a woman or a worldly man inspires fear.

## 15

***Gajapati*** — certain kings bore titles such as *chatrapati* (lord of the parasol), *narapati* (lord of men), *aśvapati* (lord of horses) and so on. In the same way *gajapati*, lord of elephants, was the title of the emperors of Orissa.

## 22

You received a salary, payment for your labor, in the post of governor of South Kaliṅga. Now you have been relieved of your duties, yet you will receive that same salary.

## 25

Rāmānanda said, “Prabhu, I saw in the king such love-pain for You that not even a particle of it is in me.”

## 28

O Pārtha, those who are devotees of Me alone are not truly My devotees; but those who are devotees of My devotees I know to be My “best devotees.”

## 29–30

Regard for serving Me, bowing down with the whole body, special worship of My devotees, seeing all beings in relation to Me, bodily effort for My sake, glorifying My qualities with words, offering the mind to Me, and giving up all desires — all these are the marks of a devotee.

## 31

O goddess, the worship of Viṣṇu is higher than the worship of other gods, and the worship of His devotee is higher than the worship of Viṣṇu.

## 32

There are servants of Kṛṣṇa who walk the path to Vaikuṇṭha, who ever sing of Janārdana, the God of gods. Service to them is out of reach for one of little austerity.

## 33–34

***Purī*** — Paramānanda Purī. ***Bhāratī*** — Brahmānanda Bhāratī. ***Svarūpa*** — the famous Dāmodara-svarūpa. ***Nityānanda*** — Nityānanda Prabhu. Rāmānanda bowed at the feet of these four Gosāñis.

## 39

Having seen Jagannātha, go straight home and meet your family.

## 47

“He gives His *darśana* to those not fit to be looked at, the low-born, yet He will not give it to me. Has He descended having resolved to show mercy to all *jīvas* but me?”

## 56

Reciting the verses of the five chapters on Śrī Kṛṣṇa's *rāsa* (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, Canto 10, Chapters 29–33), you will go alone and clasp Mahāprabhu's feet.

## 63

During the *anavasara* the Lord could not see Jagannātha, and, beside Himself in separation from Kṛṣṇa, He would go to Ālālanātha and stay there.

## 68

***Narendra*** — the pond called “Narendra,” where the Candana-yātrā festival is held. Even today Gauḍīya devotees, on entering Puruṣottama, wash their hands and feet in the water of Narendra pond and then go to the temple.

## 72

Sārvabhauma said, “I do not know any of them, (but) I wish to know.”

## 83

***The Ācārya said*** — Gopīnātha Ācārya said.

## 88

***Govinda Ghoṣa*** appeared in a family of Uttara-rāḍhī Kāyasthas; he is the one called “Ghoṣa Ṭhākura.” To this day (near Kāṭoyā) the fair of Ghoṣa Ṭhākura is held at Agradvīpa.

***Vāsu Ghoṣa*** composed many songs about Mahāprabhu; among the songs of the *mahājanas* they hold the foremost place.

## 99

In the age of Kali, whoever worships Kṛṣṇa Caitanya with the sacrifice of *saṅkīrtana* is truly intelligent. Those who do not worship Him are *kali-hata*: their intelligence has been struck down by Kali.

## 103

One who lacks His mercy, even if he is a *paṇḍita*, cannot accept Kṛṣṇa Caitanya as the Lord (*Īśvara*), though he sees all His divine majesty and hears of it: he is without His mercy.

## 111–113

The king said: “The scriptures prescribe that on entering a holy place one must fast that day and have one's head shaved there. Why do these Vaiṣṇavas not do so, and for what reason do they partake of food and water?” The Bhaṭṭācārya replied: “What you describe is *vaidhī-dharma*, the path of rules. But the *dharma* of the path of *rāga* holds another subtle secret. The Lord ordered shaving and fasting indirectly, through the sages, in the scriptures; but the order to eat *prasāda* He proclaimed Himself.”

## 118

When the Lord, contemplated within one's heart, shows a person mercy by inspiring his heart, that person gives up his settled attachment to worldly convention and to the Vedas.

## 119

***Paḍichā*** — the word *paḍichā* comes from the word *parīkṣā* (inspection); therefore the *paḍichā's* duty is supervision.

## 139–140

***The Mukunda of whom Vāsu speaks*** — Mukunda Datta, the younger brother of Vāsudeva Datta. Mukunda had been with Mahāprabhu (since childhood).

Vāsudeva said: “Mukunda took shelter of Your feet before me, and I did so later. So Mukunda's spiritual birth came before mine, and therefore I have become the younger.”

## 146

Dāmodara Paṇḍita was the elder brother, and Śaṅkara Paṇḍita the younger. The Lord said: “Dāmodara, My love for you is mixed with reverence: it is love with respect. But for Śaṅkara I have only pure love. From now on keep Śaṅkara with you.” Dāmodara replied, “Prabhu, Śaṅkara has received more of Your affection, so although he is my younger brother, he has become the elder.”

## 151

O Ananta, I had long been sinking in the ocean of worldly existence, and now at last I have found You, like the shore. O Lord, You too, having found me, have obtained the best recipient of Your mercy. This verse belongs to the *stotra* of Ālavandāru Yāmunācārya.

## 166

***In the Ṭoṭā*** — in the garden.

## 178

Kindly order whatever You wish: we two are Your servants, who carry out Your orders.

## 183

***Cūḍā*** — the spire of the Jagannātha temple.

## 192

O Lord, those on whose lips is Your name are the best, even if they are *śva-pacas*, dog-eaters. Those who chant Your name have already performed every kind of austerity and every sacrifice, bathed in all the holy places and studied all the Vedas with their auxiliaries; therefore they are counted among the Āryans.

## 199

***In proper order*** — seating each where he ought to sit, one after another.

## 214

***A variant reading:*** “Seeing the evening offering of incense (*dhūpa*), He began *saṅkīrtana*. The *paḍichā* brought garlands and sandal paste. On four sides four groups performed *saṅkīrtana*, and in the middle the Lord, the son of Śacī, danced.”

## 223

***All the people bathe*** — the people all around bathe in streams of tears.

## 224

***Beḍā-nṛtya*** — dancing around the temple.

## 233

When Śrī Kṛṣṇa held a picnic on the riverbank (*pulina-bhojana*) in Vraja, the cowherd boys sat all around Him, and each of them saw Kṛṣṇa eating with His face turned toward him. In the same way, when Mahāprabhu danced, each of the devotees around Him saw His face directly before him. This too is a display of the Lord's majesty (*aiśvarya*). ***Nehāne*** — sees.

## 238

***Puṣpāñjali*** — the offering of flowers (*puṣpāñjali*) to Jagannāthadeva.

*Thus ends the eleventh chapter of the “Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya.”*
