## 110

*Cāturmāsya* is the four months from the twelfth day of the bright half of the month of Āṣāḍha to the twelfth day of the bright half of the month of Kārttika.

## 112

*Rāmajapī* is the brāhmaṇa who was chanting the name of Rāma.

## 122

*Anavasara*. After the Snāna-yātrā, for several days, until the day before the darśana called “Navayauvana,” there is no darśana of Jagannātha. That period is called *anavasara*.
