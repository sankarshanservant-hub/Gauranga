# Anubhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 12

## 1

> Subheading: Advaita's followers are of two kinds: those who accept the essence and those who accept what is worthless —

**sārāsāra-bhṛtaḥ** (the essence: Advaita's followers who belong to Gaurahari; the worthless: those who merely fancy themselves His followers but have turned away from Gaurahari; those who carry the one or the other) **akhilān** (all) **advaitāṅghry-abja-bhṛṅgān** (Advaita's feet are lotuses, and the bees at them are Advaita's servants) (considering them so), **asārān** (the Māyāvādīs who only seem to be His followers and lack pure devotion) **hitvā** (leaving aside), **caitanya-jīvanān** (those whose life is Caitanya, for whom Gaura is life itself) **sāra-bhṛtaḥ** (the *bhāgavatas* who accept the essence) **tān** (those devotees of Advaita) **naumi** (I praise).

## 3

> Subheading: Obeisances to Advaita's servants, devotees of Gaura who accept the essence —

**śrī-caitanyāmara-taroḥ** (of the wish-fulfilling tree, Gaura) **dvitīya-skandha-rūpiṇaḥ** **śrīmad-advaita-candrasya** **śākhā-rūpān** (like the branches of a tree) **gaṇān** (the people who have taken shelter of Kṛṣṇa) (we) **numaḥ** (bow down).
