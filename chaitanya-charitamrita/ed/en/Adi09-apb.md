# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 9

## 0

**Summary.** In the ninth chapter the author describes devotion as a tree and so reveals a mystery. He makes Viśvambhara Gaurāṅga the main tree and describes Him as the gardener of the tree of devotion and as the giver and enjoyer of its fruit. The planting of this fruit tree began in Śrī Navadvīpa-dhāma; later such gardens of the fruit of *prema* were extended to Puruṣottama, Vṛndāvana and other places. Śrī Mādhavendra Purī is the first sprout of this tree, and his disciple Śrī Īśvara Purī nourished that sprout. Mahāprabhu Caitanyadeva, though He is the gardener, by His inconceivable power also became the trunk of this tree. The nine *sannyāsīs* headed by Paramānanda Purī are its roots. Above the main trunk two more trunks arose, Śrī Advaita and Śrī Nityānanda. From these two trunks came forth branches and sub-branches of every kind, which encircled the world. The fruit of *prema* from this tree was given everywhere to everyone without distinction. Having planted the tree of devotion in this way, the Lord let the world taste its fruit and made the whole world mad. This description is an allegory (*rūpaka*).

## 1

I bow down to Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, the guru of the world, by whose mercy even a dog can swim across the great ocean.

## 5

“*Āpana-śodhana*”: for one's own purification.

## 6

Śrī Caitanya Himself is the divine tree of *prema*, and He Himself is its gardener. I take shelter of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who gives and enjoys the fruits of that tree.

## 10

“Śrī Mādhava Purī” is Mādhavendra Purī. He was a famous *sannyāsī* in the *sampradāya* of Śrī Madhvācārya, and Śrī Caitanyadeva was the disciple of his disciple. Before him there was no sign of *prema-bhakti* in the Śrī Madhva-sampradāya. In his verse “*ayi dīna-dayārdra nātha*…” the teaching that Mahāprabhu later gave was present in seed form.

## 11

“Īśvara Purī”: Īśvara Purī, the *mantra-guru* of Mahāprabhu, was born in a brāhmaṇa family in Kumārahaṭṭa, that is, in the village of Hālisahara. He was a disciple of Mādhavendra Purī.

## 14

All the *sannyāsīs* named “Purī” are related to Śrī Īśvara Purī, and all those named “Bhāratī” are related to Keśava Bhāratī, the guru who gave Mahāprabhu *sannyāsa*.

## 42

The fulfillment of birth for an embodied *jīva* is always to do good to others with his life, wealth, intelligence and speech.

## 43

An intelligent person does by deed, mind and word whatever benefits living beings in this life and the next.
