“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 2  
**The Truth of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu**  
(translated from the Bengali for this edition)

শ্রীচৈতন্যপ্রভুং বন্দে বালোঽপি যদনুগ্রহাৎ ৷  
তরেন্নানামতগ্রাহব্যাপ্তং সিদ্ধান্তসাগরম্ ॥ ১ ॥

śrī-caitanya-prabhuṁ vande  
Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship  
bālo ’pi yad-anugrahāt  
a child  even  by whose  mercy  
taren nānā-mata-grāha-  
may cross  of various  doctrines  the crocodiles  
vyāptaṁ siddhānta-sāgaram  
infested with  of conclusive truths  the ocean

(1) I worship Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. By His mercy even a child can cross the ocean of conclusive truths, which is infested with the crocodiles of various doctrines.

কৃষ্ণোৎকীর্ত্তনগাননর্ত্তনকলাপাথোজনি-ভ্রাজিতা  
সদ্ভক্তাবলিহংসচক্রমধুপশ্রেণীবিহারাস্পদম্ ৷  
কর্ণানন্দিকলধ্বনির্বহতু মে জিহ্বামরুপ্রাঙ্গণে  
শ্রীচৈতন্যদয়ানিধে তব লসল্লীলাসুধাস্বর্ধুনী ॥ ২ ॥

kṛṣṇotkīrtana-gāna-nartana-kalā-pāthojani-bhrājitā  
of Kṛṣṇa's loud glorification  singing  dancing  the arts  with lotuses  adorned  
sad-bhaktāvali-haṁsa-cakra-madhupa-śreṇī-vihārāspadam  
of true  devotees  rows  swans  cakravāka birds  bees  swarms  the place of sport  
karṇānandi-kala-dhvanir vahatu me jihvā-maru-prāṅgaṇe  
delighting the ears  sweet  murmur  may flow  my  of the tongue  desert  in the courtyard  
śrī-caitanya dayā-nidhe tava lasal-līlā-sudhā-svardhunī  
O Śrī Caitanya  of mercy  O ocean  Your  resplendent  of pastimes  of nectar  the heavenly Gaṅgā

(2) O Śrī Caitanya, ocean of mercy! May the heavenly Gaṅgā of the nectar of Your resplendent pastimes flow through the desert courtyard of my tongue. Its lotuses are the arts of glorifying Kṛṣṇa aloud, of singing and of dancing. Rows of true devotees sport in it like swans, cakravāka birds and swarms of bees, and its sweet murmur delights the ears.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ৩ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host

(3) All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

তৃতীয় শ্লোকের অর্থ করি বিবরণ ৷  
বস্তু-নির্দ্দেশরূপ মঙ্গলাচরণ ॥ ৪ ॥

tṛtīya ślokera artha kari vivaraṇa  
of the third  verse  the meaning  I make  explanation  
vastu-nirdeśa-rūpa maṅgalācaraṇa  
the subject  defining  in the form of  invocation

(4) Now I shall explain the meaning of the third verse, the invocation (*maṅgalācaraṇa*) that defines the subject.

যদদ্বৈতং ব্রহ্মোপনিষদি তদপ্যস্য তনুভা  
য আত্মান্তর্যামী পুরুষ ইতি সোঽস্যাংশবিভবঃ ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যৈঃ পূর্ণো য ইহ ভগবান্ স স্বয়ময়ং  
ন চৈতন্যাৎ কৃষ্ণাজ্জগতি পরতত্ত্বং পরমিহ ॥ ৫ ॥

yad advaitaṁ brahmopaniṣadi tad apy asya tanu-bhā  
that which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  too  His  the radiance of the body  
ya ātmāntar-yāmī puruṣa iti so ’syāṁśa-vibhavaḥ  
who  the Self dwelling within  the Puruṣa  thus (called)  he  His  plenary expansion  
ṣaḍ-aiśvaryaiḥ pūrṇo ya iha bhagavān sa svayam ayaṁ  
with the six opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one  
na caitanyāt kṛṣṇāj jagati para-tattvaṁ param iha  
not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  Supreme Truth  higher  here

(5) “What the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is only the radiance of His body. The one called the Puruṣa, the Supersoul dwelling within all, is His plenary expansion. And Bhagavān, full of the six opulences, is He Himself. In this world there is no Supreme Truth higher than Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa.”

ব্রহ্ম, আত্মা, ভগবান্ — অনুবাদ তিন ৷  
অঙ্গপ্রভা, অংশ, স্বরূপ — তিন বিধেয় চিহ্ন ॥ ৬ ॥

brahma, ātmā, bhagavān — anuvāda tina  
Brahman  the Supersoul  Bhagavān  subjects  three  
aṅga-prabhā, aṁśa, svarūpa — tina vidheya cihna  
the radiance of the body  the portion  His own form  three  predicate  marks

(6) Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān are the three subjects (*anuvāda*). The radiance of His body, His portion and His own form (*svarūpa*) are the three predicates (*vidheya*) that define them.

অনুবাদ আগে, পাছে বিধেয় স্থাপন ৷  
সেই অর্থ কহি, শুন শাস্ত্রবিবরণ ॥ ৭ ॥

anuvāda āge, pāche vidheya sthāpana  
the subject  first  after  the predicate  is placed  
sei artha kahi, śuna śāstra-vivaraṇa  
that  meaning  I tell  hear  of scripture  the explanation

(7) The subject comes first, and the predicate is placed after it. I shall tell you the meaning of the verse in this way; now hear it explained according to scripture.

স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ, বিষ্ণু-পরতত্ত্ব ৷  
পূর্ণজ্ঞান পূর্ণানন্দ পরম মহত্ত্ব ॥ ৮ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa, viṣṇu-paratattva  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of Viṣṇu  the Supreme Truth  
pūrṇa-jñāna pūrṇānanda parama mahattva  
full  knowledge  full bliss  supreme  greatness

(8) Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*), the Supreme Truth of all Viṣṇu forms. He is full knowledge and full bliss, and His greatness is supreme.

‘নন্দসুত’ বলি’ যাঁরে ভাগবতে গাই ৷  
সেই কৃষ্ণ অবতীর্ণ চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি ॥ ৯ ॥

‘nanda-suta’ bali’ yāṅre bhāgavate gāi  
the son of Nanda  calling  whom  in the Bhāgavata  they sing  
sei kṛṣṇa avatīrṇa caitanya-gosāñi  
that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  Caitanya  Gosāñi

(9) That Kṛṣṇa, whom the Bhāgavatam glorifies as the son of Nanda, has descended as Lord Caitanya.

প্রকাশবিশেষে তেঁহ ধরে তিন নাম ৷  
ব্রহ্ম, পরমাত্মা আর স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ১০ ॥

prakāśa-viśeṣe teṅha dhare tina nāma  
manifestation  in particular  He  bears  three  names  
brahma, paramātmā āra svayaṁ bhagavān  
Brahman  the Supersoul  and  Himself  Bhagavān

(10) According to His particular manifestations He bears three names: Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān, the Supreme Lord Himself.

বদন্তি তত্তত্ত্ববিদস্তত্ত্বং যজ্জ্ঞানমদ্বয়ম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মেতি পরমাত্মেতি ভগবানিতি শব্দ্যতে ॥ ১১ ॥

vadanti tat tattva-vidas  
declare  that  the knowers of the Truth  
tattvaṁ yaj jñānam advayam  
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual  
brahmeti paramātmeti  
Brahman as  the Supersoul as  
bhagavān iti śabdyate  
Bhagavān  as  is named

(11) “The knowers of the Truth declare that the Truth is non-dual knowledge. It is named Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.2.11)

তাঁহার অঙ্গের শুদ্ধ কিরণ-মণ্ডল ৷  
উপনিষদ্ কহে তাঁরে ব্রহ্ম সুনির্ম্মল ॥ ১২ ॥

tāṅhāra aṅgera śuddha kiraṇa-maṇḍala  
His  of the body  pure  of rays  the halo  
upaniṣad kahe tāṅre brahma sunirmala  
the Upaniṣads  call  it  Brahman  utterly pure

(12) The pure halo of rays from His body is what the Upaniṣads call the utterly pure Brahman.

চর্ম্মচক্ষে দেখে যৈছে সূর্য্য নির্ব্বিশেষ ৷  
জ্ঞানমার্গে লইতে নারে তাঁহার বিশেষ ॥ ১৩ ॥

carma-cakṣe dekhe yaiche sūrya nirviśeṣa  
with fleshly eyes  one sees  as  the sun  featureless  
jñāna-mārge laite nāre tāṅhāra viśeṣa  
on the path of knowledge  to grasp  cannot  His  distinctive features

(13) To the physical eye the sun appears featureless. In the same way, on the path of knowledge one cannot grasp His distinctive features.

যস্য প্রভা প্রভবতো জগদণ্ডকোটি-  
কোটিষ্বশেষবসুধাদিবিভূতিভিন্নম্ ৷  
তদ্ব্রহ্মনিষ্কলমনন্তমশেষভূতং  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ১৪ ॥

yasya prabhā prabhavato jagad-aṇḍa-koṭi-  
whose  effulgence  of the mighty one  of universes  of millions  
koṭiṣv aśeṣa-vasudhādi-vibhūti-bhinnam  
in millions  countless  earths and other  by opulences  differentiated  
tad brahma niṣkalam anantam aśeṣa-bhūtaṁ  
that  Brahman  undivided  infinite  complete  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original Person  Him  I  worship

(14) “I worship Govinda, the original Person. In the millions upon millions of universes of that mighty Lord, His effulgence appears differentiated by countless opulences, such as earths. That effulgence is Brahman — undivided, infinite and complete.” (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.40)

কোটী কোটী ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে যে ব্রহ্মের বিভূতি ৷  
সেই ব্রহ্ম গোবিন্দের হয় অঙ্গকান্তি ॥ ১৫ ॥

koṭī koṭī brahmāṇḍe ye brahmera vibhūti  
millions  of millions  in universes  which  of Brahman  the opulence  
sei brahma govindera haya aṅga-kānti  
that  Brahman  of Govinda  is  the luster of the body

(15) Brahman, whose opulence fills millions upon millions of universes, is the luster of Govinda’s body.

সেই গোবিন্দ ভজি আমি, তেঁহো মোর পতি ৷  
তাঁহার প্রসাদে মোর হয় সৃষ্টিশক্তি ॥ ১৬ ॥

sei govinda bhaji āmi, teṅho mora pati  
that  Govinda  worship  I  He  my  master  
tāṅhāra prasāde mora haya sṛṣṭi-śakti  
His  by the grace  my  is  the power of creation

(16) I worship that Govinda; He is my master. By His grace I have the power to create.

মুনয়ো বাতবসনাঃ শ্রমণা ঊর্দ্ধমন্থিনঃ ৷  
ব্রহ্মাখ্যং ধাম তে যান্তি শান্তাঃ সন্ন্যাসিনোঽমলাঃ ॥ ১৭ ॥

munayo vāta-vasanāḥ  
sages  wind  clad in  
śramaṇā ūrdhva-manthinaḥ  
ascetics  upward  drawing (their seed)  
brahmākhyaṁ dhāma te yānti  
called Brahman  the abode  they  reach  
śāntāḥ sannyāsino ’malāḥ  
peaceful  renunciants  pure

(17) “Sages clad only in the wind, ascetics who draw their seed upward, peaceful and pure renunciants — they reach the abode called Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.6.47)

আত্মান্তর্য্যামী যাঁরে যোগশাস্ত্রে কয় ৷  
সেহ গোবিন্দের অংশ বিভূতি যে হয় ॥ ১৮ ॥

ātmāntaryāmī yāṅre yoga-śāstre kaya  
the Self, the indwelling Lord  whom  in the scriptures of yoga  they call  
seha govindera aṁśa vibhūti ye haya  
He  of Govinda  portion  manifestation of opulence  indeed  is

(18) He whom the scriptures of yoga call the Self, the indwelling Lord (*antaryāmī*), is a portion of Govinda and a manifestation of His opulence.

অনন্ত স্ফটিকে যৈছে এক সূর্য্য ভাসে ৷  
তৈছে জীবে গোবিন্দের অংশ প্রকাশে ॥ ১৯ ॥

ananta sphaṭike yaiche eka sūrya bhāse  
in countless  crystals  as  one  sun  is reflected  
taiche jīve govindera aṁśa prakāśe  
so  in the living beings  of Govinda  the portion  is manifest

(19) Just as one sun is reflected in countless crystals, so Govinda’s portion is manifest in all living beings.

অথবা বহুনৈতেন কিং জ্ঞাতেন তবার্জ্জুন ৷  
বিষ্টভ্যাহমিদং কৃৎস্নমেকাংশেন স্থিতো জগৎ ॥ ২০ ॥

atha vā bahunaitena  
or else  by much  this  
kiṁ jñātena tavārjuna  
what  by knowing  your  O Arjuna  
viṣṭabhyāham idaṁ kṛtsnam  
pervading and supporting  I  this  entire  
ekāṁśena sthito jagat  
with one portion  abide  the universe

(20) “But what need is there, Arjuna, for you to know all this in detail? I pervade and support this entire universe with a single portion of Myself.” (Bhagavad-gītā 10.42)

তমিমমহমজং শরীরভাজাং  
হৃদি হৃদি ধিষ্ঠিতমাত্মকল্পিতানাম্ ৷  
প্রতিদৃশমিব নৈকধার্কমেকং  
সমধিগতোঽস্মি বিধূতভেদমোহঃ ॥ ২১ ॥

tam imam aham ajaṁ śarīra-bhājāṁ  
Him  this  I  the unborn  of the embodied  
hṛdi hṛdi dhiṣṭhitam ātma-kalpitānām  
in heart  in heart  abiding  created by Himself  
prati-dṛśam iva naikadhārkam ekaṁ  
to each  eye  like  in many ways  the sun  one  
samadhigato ’smi vidhūta-bheda-mohaḥ  
have realized  I am  dispelled  of difference  the delusion

(21) “My delusion of difference is gone, and I have realized Him, this unborn Lord. He abides in the heart of every embodied being He has created, just as the one sun appears in many ways to every eye that sees it.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.9.42)

সেই ত’ গোবিন্দ সাক্ষাচ্চৈতন্য গোসাঞি ৷  
জীব নিস্তারিতে ঐছে দয়ালু আর নাই ॥ ২২ ॥

sei ta’ govinda sākṣāc caitanya gosāñi  
that  indeed  Govinda  directly  Caitanya  Gosāñi  
jīva nistārite aiche dayālu āra nāi  
souls  in delivering  so  merciful  another  there is not

(22) That same Govinda is directly Lord Caitanya. No one else is so merciful in delivering souls.

পরব্যোমেতে বৈসে নারায়ণ নাম ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যপূর্ণ লক্ষ্মীকান্ত ভগবান্ ॥ ২৩ ॥

para-vyomete vaise nārāyaṇa nāma  
in the spiritual sky  resides  Nārāyaṇa  named  
ṣaḍ-aiśvarya-pūrṇa lakṣmī-kānta bhagavān  
with the six opulences  full  of Lakṣmī  the beloved  the Lord

(23) In the spiritual sky resides the Lord named Nārāyaṇa. He is full of the six opulences and is the beloved of Lakṣmī.

বেদ, ভাগবত, উপনিষদ্, আগম ৷  
‘পূর্ণতত্ত্ব’ যাঁরে কহে, নাহি যাঁর সম ॥ ২৪ ॥

veda, bhāgavata, upaniṣad, āgama  
the Vedas  the Bhāgavata  the Upaniṣads  the Āgamas  
‘pūrṇa-tattva’ yāṅre kahe, nāhi yāṅra sama  
the complete Truth  whom  call  there is not  whose  equal

(24) The Vedas, the Bhāgavatam, the Upaniṣads and the Āgamas call Him “the complete Truth”; no one is equal to Him.

ভক্তিযোগে ভক্ত পায় যাঁহার দর্শন ৷  
সূর্য্য যেন সবিগ্রহ দেখে দেবগণ ॥ ২৫ ॥

bhakti-yoge bhakta pāya yāṅhāra darśana  
through bhakti-yoga  the devotee  obtains  whose  sight  
sūrya yena savigraha dekhe deva-gaṇa  
the sun  as  in his form  see  the gods

(25) Through *bhakti-yoga*, the path of devotion, the devotees obtain the sight of Him, just as the gods see the sun in his personal form.

জ্ঞান-যোগ-মার্গে তাঁরে ভজে যেই সব ৷  
ব্রহ্ম-আত্মরূপে তাঁরে করে অনুভব ॥ ২৬ ॥

jñāna-yoga-mārge tāṅre bhaje yei saba  
of knowledge  of yoga  on the path  Him  worship  who  all  
brahma-ātma-rūpe tāṅre kare anubhava  
of Brahman  of the Supersoul  in the form  Him  they  realize

(26) Those who worship Him on the paths of knowledge and yoga realize Him as Brahman and as the Supersoul.

উপাসনা-ভেদে জানি ঈশ্বর-মহিমা ৷  
অতএব সূর্য্য তাঁর দিয়ে ত’ উপমা ॥ ২৭ ॥

upāsanā-bhede jāni īśvara-mahimā  
of worship  by the differences  one knows  of the Lord  the greatness  
ataeva sūrya tāṅra diye ta’ upamā  
therefore  of the sun  to Him  I give  indeed  the comparison

(27) One comes to know the greatness of the Lord according to the different kinds of worship. That is why I compare Him to the sun.

সেই নারায়ণ কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ অভেদ ৷  
একই বিগ্রহ, কিন্তু আকার-বিভেদ ॥ ২৮ ॥

sei nārāyaṇa kṛṣṇera svarūpa abheda  
that  Nārāyaṇa  of Kṛṣṇa  His own form  non-different  
ekai vigraha, kintu ākāra-vibheda  
one and the same  form  but  in shape  difference

(28) That Nārāyaṇa is non-different from Kṛṣṇa’s own form. They are one and the same divine form (*vigraha*), but Their appearance differs.

ইঁহো ত’ দ্বিভুজ, তিঁহো ধরে চারি হাথ ৷  
ইঁহো বেণু ধরে, তিঁহো চক্রাদিক সাথ ॥ ২৯ ॥

iṅho ta’ dvi-bhuja, tiṅho dhare cāri hātha  
this one  indeed  two-armed  that one  has  four  arms  
iṅho veṇu dhare, tiṅho cakrādika sātha  
this one  the flute  holds  that one  the disc and others  with

(29) Kṛṣṇa has two arms, and Nārāyaṇa has four. Kṛṣṇa holds the flute, and Nārāyaṇa holds the disc and His other emblems.

নারায়ণস্ত্বং ন হি সর্ব্বদেহিনা-  
মাত্মাস্যধীশাখিললোকসাক্ষী ৷  
নারায়ণোঽঙ্গং নরভূ-জলায়না-  
ত্তচ্চাপি সত্যং ন তবৈব মায়া ॥ ৩০ ॥

nārāyaṇas tvaṁ na hi sarva-dehinām  
Nārāyaṇa  You  not  indeed  of all  embodied beings  
ātmāsy adhīśākhila-loka-sākṣī  
the Self  You are  O Lord  of all  worlds  the witness  
nārāyaṇo ’ṅgaṁ nara-bhū-jalāyanāt  
Nārāyaṇa  Your portion  of Nara  born  the water  as resting place  
tac cāpi satyaṁ na tavaiva māyā  
that  and also  true  not  Your  indeed  māyā

(30) “O Lord, are You not Nārāyaṇa? You are the Self of all embodied beings and the witness of all the worlds. The one called Nārāyaṇa because His resting place is the water born of Nara is Your portion. And that portion too is real; it is not Your māyā.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.14)

শিশু বৎস হরি’ ব্রহ্মা করি’ অপরাধ ৷  
অপরাধ ক্ষমাইতে মাগেন প্রসাদ ॥ ৩১ ॥

śiśu vatsa hari’ brahmā kari’ aparādha  
the boys  the calves  having stolen  Brahmā  having committed  offense  
aparādha kṣamāite māgena prasāda  
the offense  to have forgiven  begs  mercy

(31) Brahmā had offended Kṛṣṇa by stealing the cowherd boys and the calves. To have his offense forgiven, he begged for mercy:

তোমার নাভিপদ্ম হৈতে আমার জন্মোদয় ৷  
তুমি পিতা-মাতা, আমি তোমার তনয় ॥ ৩২ ॥

tomāra nābhi-padma haite āmāra janmodaya  
Your  navel  lotus  from  my  birth  
tumi pitā-mātā, āmi tomāra tanaya  
You  father  mother  I  Your  son

(32) “I was born from the lotus of Your navel. You are my father and mother, and I am Your son.

পিতা মাতা বালকের না লয় অপরাধ ৷  
অপরাধ ক্ষম, মোরে করহ প্রসাদ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

pitā mātā bālakera nā laya aparādha  
father  mother  of a child  not  take  offense  
aparādha kṣama, more karaha prasāda  
the offense  forgive  to me  show  mercy

(33) A father and mother do not take offense at what their child does. Forgive my offense and be merciful to me.”

কৃষ্ণ কহেন — ব্রহ্মা, তোমার পিতা নারায়ণ ৷  
আমি গোপ, তুমি কৈছে আমার নন্দন ॥ ৩৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahena — brahmā, tomāra pitā nārāyaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  says  Brahmā  your  father  Nārāyaṇa  
āmi gopa, tumi kaiche āmāra nandana  
I  a cowherd  you  how  My  son

(34) Kṛṣṇa said, “Brahmā, your father is Nārāyaṇa. I am a cowherd boy. How can you be My son?”

ব্রহ্মা বলেন, তুমি কি না হও নারায়ণ ৷  
তুমি নারায়ণ — শুন তাহার কারণ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

brahmā balena, tumi ki nā hao nārāyaṇa  
Brahmā  says  You  (question)  not  are  Nārāyaṇa  
tumi nārāyaṇa — śuna tāhāra kāraṇa  
You  Nārāyaṇa  hear  of that  the reason

(35) Brahmā said, “Are You not Nārāyaṇa? You are Nārāyaṇa — hear the reason why.

প্রাকৃতাপ্রাকৃত-সৃষ্ট্যে যত জীবরূপ ৷  
তাহার যে আত্মা তুমি মূল-স্বরূপ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

prākṛtāprākṛta-sṛṣṭye yata jīva-rūpa  
in the material and spiritual  creation  as many as  living beings'  forms  
tāhāra ye ātmā tumi mūla-svarūpa  
their  that  Self  You  the original  being

(36) You are the Self of all the living beings in the material and the spiritual creation. You are their original source.

পৃথ্বী যৈছে ঘটকুলের কারণ আশ্রয় ৷  
জীবের নিদান তুমি, তুমি সর্ব্বাশ্রয় ॥ ৩৭ ॥

pṛthvī yaiche ghaṭa-kulera kāraṇa āśraya  
the earth  as  of pots  of the multitude  the cause  the shelter  
jīvera nidāna tumi, tumi sarvāśraya  
of the souls  the origin  You  You  the shelter of all

(37) Just as the earth is the cause and the shelter of all pots, so You are the origin of all souls. You are the shelter of all.

‘নার’ শব্দে কহে সর্ব্বজীবের নিচয় ৷  
‘অয়ন’ শব্দেতে কহে তাহার আশ্রয় ॥ ৩৮ ॥

‘nāra’ śabde kahe sarva-jīvera nicaya  
nāra  by the word  is meant  of all  living beings  the aggregate  
‘ayana’ śabdete kahe tāhāra āśraya  
ayana  by the word  is meant  their  shelter

(38) The word *nāra* means the aggregate of all living beings, and the word *ayana* means their shelter.

অতএব তুমি হও মূল নারায়ণ ৷  
এই এক হেতু, শুন দ্বিতীয় কারণ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

ataeva tumi hao mūla nārāyaṇa  
therefore  You  are  the original  Nārāyaṇa  
ei eka hetu, śuna dvitīya kāraṇa  
this  one  reason  hear  the second  reason

(39) Therefore You are the original Nārāyaṇa. That is the first reason; now hear the second.

জীবের ঈশ্বর — পুরুষাদি অবতার ৷  
তাঁহা সবা হৈতে তোমার ঐশ্বর্য্য অপার ॥ ৪০ ॥

jīvera īśvara — puruṣādi avatāra  
of the living beings  the lords  the Puruṣa and other  avatāras  
tāṅhā sabā haite tomāra aiśvarya apāra  
Them  all  than  Your  opulence  boundless

(40) The lords of the living beings are the Puruṣa and the other avatāras. Your opulence is boundlessly greater than Theirs.

অতএব অধীশ্বর তুমি সর্ব্বপিতা ৷  
তোমার শক্তিতে তাঁরা জগৎ-রক্ষিতা ॥ ৪১ ॥

ataeva adhīśvara tumi sarva-pitā  
therefore  the supreme Lord  You  the father of all  
tomāra śaktite tāṅrā jagat-rakṣitā  
by Your  potency  They  of the world  protectors

(41) Therefore You are the supreme Lord, the father of all. It is by Your potency that They protect the world.

নারের অয়ন যাতে করহ পালন ৷  
অতএব হও তুমি মূল নারায়ণ ॥ ৪২ ॥

nārera ayana yāte karaha pālana  
of the nāras  the shelters  since  You do  protection  
ataeva hao tumi mūla nārāyaṇa  
therefore  are  You  the original  Nārāyaṇa

(42) Since You protect Those who are the shelter of the living beings, You are the original Nārāyaṇa.

তৃতীয় কারণ শুন শ্রীভগবান্ ৷  
অনন্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ড বহু বৈকুণ্ঠাদি ধাম ॥ ৪৩ ॥

tṛtīya kāraṇa śuna śrī-bhagavān  
the third  reason  hear  O Lord  
ananta brahmāṇḍa bahu vaikuṇṭhādi dhāma  
countless  universes  many  Vaikuṇṭha and other  abodes

(43) O Lord, hear the third reason. There are countless universes and many abodes such as Vaikuṇṭha.

ইথে যত জীব, তার ত্রৈকালিক কর্ম্ম ৷  
তাহা দেখ, সাক্ষী তুমি, জান সব মর্ম্ম ॥ ৪৪ ॥

ithe yata jīva, tāra trai-kālika karma  
in them  as many as  beings  their  of the three times  deeds  
tāhā dekha, sākṣī tumi, jāna saba marma  
that  You see  the witness  You  You know  of all  the essence

(44) You see the deeds of all the beings in them, past, present and future. You are the witness, and You know the inner meaning of everything.

তোমার দর্শনে সর্ব্ব জগতের স্থিতি ৷  
তুমি না দেখিলে কার নাহি স্থিতি গতি ॥ ৪৫ ॥

tomāra darśane sarva jagatera sthiti  
by Your  glance  of all  the worlds  the existence  
tumi nā dekhile kāra nāhi sthiti gati  
You  not  if You look  of anyone  there is not  rest  movement

(45) All the worlds exist by Your glance. If You did not look upon them, no one could either stay or move.

নারের অয়ন যাতে কর দরশন ৷  
তাহাতেও হও তুমি মূল নারায়ণ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

nārera ayana yāte kara daraśana  
of the nāras  the course  since  You do  seeing  
tāhāteo hao tumi mūla nārāyaṇa  
by that too  are  You  the original  Nārāyaṇa

(46) Since You see the course of all living beings, for this reason too You are the original Nārāyaṇa.”

কৃষ্ণ কহেন — ব্রহ্মা, তোমার না বুঝি বচন ৷  
জীব-হৃদি, জলে বৈসে সেই নারায়ণ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahena — brahmā, tomāra nā bujhi vacana  
Kṛṣṇa  says  Brahmā  your  not  I understand  words  
jīva-hṛdi, jale vaise sei nārāyaṇa  
in the heart of beings  on the water  resides  that  Nārāyaṇa

(47) Kṛṣṇa said, “Brahmā, I do not understand your words. Nārāyaṇa is He who resides in the hearts of the living beings and on the waters.”

ব্রহ্মা কহে — জলে জীবে যেই নারায়ণ ৷  
সে সব তোমার অংশ, — এ সত্য বচন ॥ ৪৮ ॥

brahmā kahe — jale jīve yei nārāyaṇa  
Brahmā  says  on the water  in the beings  who  Nārāyaṇa  
se saba tomāra aṁśa, — e satya vacana  
They  all  Your  portions  this  true  word

(48) Brahmā said, “The Nārāyaṇa who lies on the waters and dwells in the living beings — all these are Your portions. This is the truth.”

কারণাব্ধি-গর্ভোদক-ক্ষীরোদকশায়ী ৷  
মায়াদ্বারা সৃষ্টি করে, তাতে সব মায়ী ॥ ৪৯ ॥

kāraṇābdhi-garbhodaka-kṣīrodaka-śāyī  
in the Causal Ocean  the Garbha Ocean  the Ocean of Milk  lying  
māyā-dvārā sṛṣṭi kare, tāte saba māyī  
through māyā  creation  do  therefore  all  wielders of māyā

(49) Kāraṇodakaśāyī, Garbhodakaśāyī and Kṣīrodakaśāyī create through māyā; therefore They are all called wielders of māyā (*māyī*).

সেই তিন জলশায়ী সর্ব্ব-অন্তর্য্যামী ৷  
ব্রহ্মাণ্ডবৃন্দের আত্মা যে পুরুষ-নামী ॥ ৫০ ॥

sei tina jala-śāyī sarva-antaryāmī  
those  three  lying on the waters  dwelling within all  
brahmāṇḍa-vṛndera ātmā ye puruṣa-nāmī  
of the universes  multitude  the Self  who  named Puruṣa

(50) These three Lords who lie on the waters dwell within all. The one named the Puruṣa is the Self of all the universes.

হিরণ্যগর্ভের আত্মা গর্ভোদকশায়ী ৷  
ব্যষ্টিজীব-অন্তর্য্যামী ক্ষীরোদকশায়ী ॥ ৫১ ॥

hiraṇya-garbhera ātmā garbhodaka-śāyī  
of Hiraṇyagarbha  the Self  Garbhodakaśāyī  
vyaṣṭi-jīva-antaryāmī kṣīrodaka-śāyī  
of the individual  being  the indwelling Lord  Kṣīrodakaśāyī

(51) Garbhodakaśāyī is the Self of Hiraṇyagarbha, and Kṣīrodakaśāyī is the indwelling Lord of each individual being.

এ সভার দর্শনে ত’ আছে মায়াগন্ধ ৷  
তুরীয় কৃষ্ণের নাহি মায়ার সম্বন্ধ ॥ ৫২ ॥

e sabhāra darśane ta’ āche māyā-gandha  
of these  all  in the glance  indeed  there is  of māyā  a trace  
turīya kṛṣṇera nāhi māyāra sambandha  
the fourth  Kṛṣṇa's  there is not  with māyā  connection

(52) Because all these Lords cast Their glance upon māyā, a trace of māyā is associated with Them. But Kṛṣṇa, the fourth (*turīya*), has no connection with māyā.

বিরাট্ হিরণ্যগর্ভশ্চ কারণং চেত্যুপাধয়ঃ ৷  
ঈশস্য যৎ ত্রিভির্হীনং তুরীয়ং তৎ প্রচক্ষতে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

virāṭ hiraṇya-garbhaś ca  
Virāṭ  Hiraṇyagarbha  and  
kāraṇaṁ cety upādhayaḥ  
the Cause  and thus  designations  
īśasya yat tribhir hīnaṁ  
of the Lord  what  of the three  free  
turīyaṁ tat pracakṣate  
the fourth  that  they call

(53) “Virāṭ, Hiraṇyagarbha and the Cause are designations of the Lord. That which is free of these three is called the fourth (*turīya*).” (Śrīdhara Svāmī, commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.15.16)

যদ্যপি তিনের মায়া লইয়া ব্যবহার ৷  
তথাপি তৎস্পর্শ নাহি, সভে মায়া-পার ॥ ৫৪ ॥

yadyapi tinera māyā laiyā vyavahāra  
although  of the three  māyā  with  dealings  
tathāpi tat-sparśa nāhi, sabhe māyā-pāra  
still  its  touch  there is not  all  beyond māyā

(54) Although these three deal with māyā, māyā does not touch Them. All of Them are beyond māyā.

এতদীশনমীশস্য প্রকৃতিস্থোঽপি তদ্গুণৈঃ ৷  
ন যুজ্যতে সদাত্মস্থৈর্যথা বুদ্ধিস্তদাশ্রয়া ॥ ৫৫ ॥

etad īśanam īśasya  
this  the divinity  of the Lord  
prakṛti-stho ’pi tad-guṇaiḥ  
in material nature  situated  though  by its  qualities  
na yujyate sadātma-sthair  
not  is touched  always  abiding in Him  
yathā buddhis tad-āśrayā  
as  the intelligence  taking shelter of Him

(55) “This is the divinity of the Lord: although He is within material nature, He is never touched by its qualities, though they always rest in Him. In the same way the intelligence of those who take shelter of Him remains untouched by them.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.11.38)

সেই তিন জনের তুমি পরম আশ্রয় ৷  
তুমি মূলনারায়ণ — ইথে কি সংশয় ॥ ৫৬ ॥

sei tina janera tumi parama āśraya  
of those  three  persons  You  the supreme  shelter  
tumi mūla nārāyaṇa — ithe ki saṁśaya  
You  the original  Nārāyaṇa  in this  what  doubt

(56) “You are the supreme shelter of these three. You are the original Nārāyaṇa — what doubt can there be?

সেই তিনের অংশী পরব্যোম-নারায়ণ ৷  
তেঁহ তোমার প্রকাশ, তুমি মূল-নারায়ণ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

sei tinera aṁśī paravyoma-nārāyaṇa  
of those  three  the source  of the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  
teṅha tomāra prakāśa, tumi mūla-nārāyaṇa  
He  Your  manifestation  You  the original Nārāyaṇa

(57) The source of these three is Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky. He is Your manifestation, and You are the original Nārāyaṇa.”[^57-1]

অতএব ব্রহ্মবাক্যে পরব্যোম-নারায়ণ ৷  
তেঁহো কৃষ্ণের বিলাস — এই তত্ত্ব-বিবরণ ॥ ৫৮ ॥

ataeva brahma-vākye paravyoma-nārāyaṇa  
therefore  in Brahmā's  words  of the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  
teṅho kṛṣṇera vilāsa — ei tattva-vivaraṇa  
He  of Kṛṣṇa  the pastime form  this  of the truth  the explanation

(58) Thus, according to Brahmā’s words, Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky is a pastime form (*vilāsa*) of Kṛṣṇa. This is the explanation of the truth.

এই শ্লোক তত্ত্ব-লক্ষণ ভাগবত-সার ৷  
পরিভাষা-রূপে ইহার সর্ব্বত্রাধিকার ॥ ৫৯ ॥

ei śloka tattva-lakṣaṇa bhāgavata-sāra  
this  verse  of the truth  the definition  of the Bhāgavata  the essence  
paribhāṣā-rūpe ihāra sarvatrādhikāra  
of a governing rule  in the form  its  everywhere  authority

(59) This verse defines the truth and is the essence of the Bhāgavatam. As a governing rule (*paribhāṣā*), it holds authority everywhere.

ব্রহ্ম, আত্মা, ভগবান্ — কৃষ্ণের বিহার ৷  
এ অর্থ না জানি’ মূর্খ অর্থ করে আর ॥ ৬০ ॥

brahma, ātmā, bhagavān — kṛṣṇera vihāra  
Brahman  the Supersoul  Bhagavān  of Kṛṣṇa  manifestations  
e artha nā jāni’ mūrkha artha kare āra  
this  meaning  not  knowing  fools  meaning  make  another

(60) Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān are manifestations of Kṛṣṇa. Not knowing this meaning, fools interpret the verse otherwise.

অবতারী নারায়ণ, কৃষ্ণ অবতার ৷  
তেঁহ চতুর্ভুজ, ইঁহ মনুষ্য-আকার ॥ ৬১ ॥

avatārī nārāyaṇa, kṛṣṇa avatāra  
the source of avatāras  Nārāyaṇa  Kṛṣṇa  an avatāra  
teṅha catur-bhuja, iṅha manuṣya-ākāra  
He  four-armed  this one  of human  form

(61) They say that Nārāyaṇa is the source of the avatāras and Kṛṣṇa is an avatāra, since Nārāyaṇa has four arms and Kṛṣṇa has a human form.

এইমতে নানারূপ করে পূর্ব্বপক্ষ ৷  
তাহারে নির্জ্জিতে ভাগবত-পদ্য দক্ষ ॥ ৬২ ॥

ei-mate nānā-rūpa kare pūrva-pakṣa  
in this  way  of various  kinds  make  objections  
tāhāre nirjite bhāgavata-padya dakṣa  
them  to defeat  of the Bhāgavata  a verse  is able

(62) In this way they raise objections of many kinds. A verse of the Bhāgavatam is able to defeat them:

বদন্তি তত্তত্ত্ববিদস্তত্ত্বং যজ্জ্ঞানমদ্বয়ম্ ৷  
ব্রহ্মেতি পরমাত্মেতি ভগবানিতি শব্দ্যতে ॥ ৬৩ ॥

vadanti tat tattva-vidas  
declare  that  the knowers of the Truth  
tattvaṁ yaj jñānam advayam  
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual  
brahmeti paramātmeti  
Brahman as  the Supersoul as  
bhagavān iti śabdyate  
Bhagavān  as  is named

(63) “The knowers of the Truth declare that the Truth is non-dual knowledge. It is named Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.2.11)

শুন ভাই, এ শ্লোকার্থ করহ বিচার ৷  
এক মুখ্যতত্ত্ব, তিন তাহার প্রচার ॥ ৬৪ ॥

śuna bhāi, e ślokārtha karaha vicāra  
hear  brothers  of this  verse  the meaning  make  consideration  
eka mukhya-tattva, tina tāhāra pracāra  
one  principal  Truth  three  its  manifestations

(64) Listen, brothers, and consider the meaning of this verse. There is one principal Truth, and It has three manifestations.

অদ্বয়জ্ঞান তত্ত্ববস্তু কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ ৷  
ব্রহ্ম, আত্মা, ভগবান্, — তিন তাঁর রূপ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

advaya-jñāna tattva-vastu kṛṣṇera svarūpa  
non-dual  knowledge  the Absolute Reality  of Kṛṣṇa  the own form  
brahma, ātmā, bhagavān, — tina tāṅra rūpa  
Brahman  the Supersoul  Bhagavān  three  His  forms

(65) The non-dual knowledge, the Absolute Reality, is Kṛṣṇa’s own form. Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān are His three forms.

এই শ্লোকের অর্থে তুমি হৈলা নির্ব্বচন ৷  
আর এক শুন ভাগবতের বচন ॥ ৬৬ ॥

ei ślokera arthe tumi hailā nirvacana  
of this  verse  by the meaning  you  became  speechless  
āra eka śuna bhāgavatera vacana  
another  one  hear  of the Bhāgavata  statement

(66) The meaning of this verse has left you speechless. Now hear another statement of the Bhāgavatam.

এতে চাংশকলাঃ পুংসঃ কৃষ্ণস্তু ভগবান্ স্বয়ম্ ৷  
ইন্দ্রারিব্যাকুলং লোকং মৃড়য়ন্তি যুগে যুগে ॥ ৬৭ ॥

ete cāṁśa-kalāḥ puṁsaḥ  
these  and  portions  parts  of the Puruṣa  
kṛṣṇas tu bhagavān svayam  
Kṛṣṇa  but  Bhagavān  Himself  
indrāri-vyākulaṁ lokaṁ  
by the enemies of Indra  troubled  the world  
mṛḍayanti yuge yuge  
make happy  in age  after age

(67) “All these are portions and parts of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself. Age after age They bring relief to the world when it is troubled by the enemies of Indra.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.3.28)

সব অবতারের করি সামান্য-লক্ষণ ৷  
তার মধ্যে কৃষ্ণচন্দ্রের করিল গণন ॥ ৬৮ ॥

saba avatārera kari sāmānya-lakṣaṇa  
of all  avatāras  having made  general  description  
tāra madhye kṛṣṇa-candrera karila gaṇana  
them  among  Kṛṣṇacandra  made  counting

(68) After describing the general features of all the avatāras, Sūta counted Kṛṣṇacandra among Them.

তবে সূত গোসাঞি মনে পাঞা বড় ভয় ৷  
যাঁর যে লক্ষণ তাহা করিল নিশ্চয় ॥ ৬৯ ॥

tabe sūta gosāñi mane pāñā baḍa bhaya  
then  Sūta  Gosāñi  in the mind  feeling  great  fear  
yāṅra ye lakṣaṇa tāhā karila niścaya  
of whom  which  characteristic  that  made  determination

(69) Then Sūta Gosvāmī grew very apprehensive and defined the distinctive characteristic of each.

অবতার সব — পুরুষের কলা-অংশ ৷  
স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ সর্ব্ব-অবতংস ॥ ৭০ ॥

avatāra saba — puruṣera kalā-aṁśa  
the avatāras  all  of the Puruṣa  parts  portions  
svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa sarva-avataṁsa  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of all  the crown

(70) All the avatāras are parts and portions of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord Himself, the crown of all.

পূর্ব্বপক্ষ কহে — তোমার ভাল ত’ ব্যাখ্যান ৷  
পরব্যোমে-নারায়ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ৭১ ॥

pūrva-pakṣa kahe — tomāra bhāla ta’ vyākhyāna  
the opponent  says  your  fine  indeed  explanation  
paravyome-nārāyaṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
of the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  Himself  Bhagavān

(71) An opponent says, “A fine explanation you give! But Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky is the Supreme Lord Himself.

তেঁহ আসি’ কৃষ্ণরূপে করেন অবতার ৷  
এই অর্থ শ্লোকে দেখি — কি আর বিচার ॥ ৭২ ॥

teṅha āsi’ kṛṣṇa-rūpe karena avatāra  
He  coming  in the form of Kṛṣṇa  makes  descent  
ei artha śloke dekhi — ki āra vicāra  
this  meaning  in the verse  I see  what  more  consideration

(72) He comes and descends in the form of Kṛṣṇa. That is the meaning I see in the verse. What more is there to consider?”

তারে কহে — কেনে কর কুতর্কানুমান ৷  
শাস্ত্রবিরুদ্ধার্থ কভু না হয় প্রমাণ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

tāre kahe — kene kara kutarkānumāna  
to him  one says  why  do you make  of false logic  inference  
śāstra-viruddhārtha kabhu nā haya pramāṇa  
contrary to scripture  meaning  ever  not  is  proof

(73) The answer to him is this: “Why do you draw inferences by false logic? A meaning that contradicts scripture can never be proof.”

অনুবাদমনুক্ত্বা তু ন বিধেয়মুদীরয়েৎ ৷  
ন হ্যলব্ধাস্পদং কিঞ্চিৎ কুত্রচিৎ প্রতিতিষ্ঠতি ॥ ৭৪ ॥

anuvādam anuktvā tu  
the subject  without stating  but  
na vidheyam udīrayet  
not  the predicate  one should utter  
na hy alabdhāspadaṁ kiñcit  
not  for  without a footing  anything  
kutracit pratitiṣṭhati  
anywhere  stands firm

(74) “One should not state the predicate without first stating the subject, for nothing without a footing can stand firm anywhere.” (Ekādaśī-tattva 13)

অনুবাদ না কহিয়া না কহি বিধেয় ৷  
আগে অনুবাদ কহি, পশ্চাদ্বিধেয় ॥ ৭৫ ॥

anuvāda nā kahiyā nā kahi vidheya  
the subject  not  having stated  not  one states  the predicate  
āge anuvāda kahi, paścād vidheya  
first  the subject  one states  after  the predicate

(75) One does not state the predicate without first stating the subject. First one states the subject, and then the predicate.

‘বিধেয়’ কহিয়ে তারে, যে বস্তু অজ্ঞাত ৷  
‘অনুবাদ’ কহি তারে, যেই হয় জ্ঞাত ॥ ৭৬ ॥

‘vidheya’ kahiye tāre, ye vastu ajñāta  
predicate  one calls  that  which  thing  unknown  
‘anuvāda’ kahi tāre, yei haya jñāta  
subject  one calls  that  which  is  known

(76) The predicate is what is not yet known, and the subject is what is already known.

যৈছে কহি, — এই বিপ্র পরম পণ্ডিত ৷  
বিপ্র — অনুবাদ, ইহার বিধেয় — পাণ্ডিত্য ॥ ৭৭ ॥

yaiche kahi, — ei vipra parama paṇḍita  
as  we say  this  brāhmaṇa  great  scholar  
vipra — anuvāda, ihāra vidheya — pāṇḍitya  
brāhmaṇa  the subject  his  predicate  learning

(77) For example, we say, “This brāhmaṇa is a great scholar.” Here *brāhmaṇa* is the subject, and his learning is the predicate.

বিপ্র বলি’ জানি, তার পাণ্ডিত্য অজ্ঞাত ৷  
অতএব বিপ্র আগে, পাণ্ডিত্য পশ্চাৎ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

vipra bali’ jāni, tāra pāṇḍitya ajñāta  
a brāhmaṇa  as  we know  his  learning  unknown  
ataeva vipra āge, pāṇḍitya paścāt  
therefore  brāhmaṇa  first  learning  after

(78) We know that he is a brāhmaṇa, but his learning is not known. Therefore *brāhmaṇa* comes first and his learning after.

তৈছে ইঁহ অবতার, সব তাঁর জ্ঞাত ৷  
কার অবতার — এই বস্তু অবিজ্ঞাত ॥ ৭৯ ॥

taiche iṅha avatāra, saba tāṅra jñāta  
so  here  avatāras  all  They  known  
kāra avatāra — ei vastu avijñāta  
whose  avatāras  this  thing  unknown

(79) In the same way, the avatāras here are all known, but whose avatāras They are is not known.

‘এতে’ শব্দে অবতারের আগে অনুবাদ ৷  
‘পুরুষের অংশ’ পাছে বিধেয় সংবাদ ॥ ৮০ ॥

‘ete’ śabde avatārera āge anuvāda  
“these”  by the word  of the avatāras  first  the subject  
‘puruṣera aṁśa’ pāche vidheya saṁvāda  
“portions of the Puruṣa”  after  of the predicate  the statement

(80) First the word *ete*, “these,” states the subject, the avatāras. Then the words “portions of the Puruṣa” state the predicate.

তৈছে কৃষ্ণ অবতার-ভিতরে হৈল জ্ঞাত ৷  
তাঁহার বিশেষ-জ্ঞান সেই অবিজ্ঞাত ॥ ৮১ ॥

taiche kṛṣṇa avatāra-bhitare haila jñāta  
so  Kṛṣṇa  the avatāras  among  became  known  
tāṅhāra viśeṣa-jñāna sei avijñāta  
His  distinctive  knowledge  that  unknown

(81) In the same way, Kṛṣṇa was known as one among the avatāras, but His distinctive nature was not known.

অতএব ‘কৃষ্ণ’ শব্দ আগে অনুবাদ ৷  
‘স্বয়ং ভগবত্তা’ পিছে বিধেয় সংবাদ ॥ ৮২ ॥

ataeva ‘kṛṣṇa’ śabda āge anuvāda  
therefore  “Kṛṣṇa”  the word  first  the subject  
‘svayaṁ bhagavattā’ piche vidheya saṁvāda  
“being the Lord Himself”  after  of the predicate  the statement

(82) Therefore the word *Kṛṣṇa* comes first as the subject, and after it the predicate states that He is the Supreme Lord Himself.

কৃষ্ণের স্বয়ং-ভগবত্তা — ইহা হৈল সাধ্য ৷  
স্বয়ং-ভগবানের কৃষ্ণত্ব হৈল বাধ্য ॥ ৮৩ ॥

kṛṣṇera svayaṁ-bhagavattā — ihā haila sādhya  
Kṛṣṇa's  being the Lord Himself  this  became  the thing to be established  
svayaṁ-bhagavānera kṛṣṇatva haila bādhya  
of the Lord Himself  being Kṛṣṇa  became  binding

(83) Kṛṣṇa's being the Supreme Lord Himself is what must be established (*sādhya*); that the Supreme Lord Himself is Kṛṣṇa follows as a binding conclusion (*bādhya*).

কৃষ্ণ যদি অংশ হৈত, অংশী নারায়ণ ৷  
তবে বিপরীত হৈত সূতের বচন ॥ ৮৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa yadi aṁśa haita, aṁśī nārāyaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  if  a portion  were  the source  Nārāyaṇa  
tabe viparīta haita sūtera vacana  
then  reversed  would be  of Sūta  the statement

(84) If Kṛṣṇa were a portion and Nārāyaṇa the source, Sūta’s statement would have been the reverse.

নারায়ণ অংশী যেই স্বয়ং-ভগবান্ ৷  
তেঁহ শ্রীকৃষ্ণ — ঐছে করি তা ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ৮৫ ॥

nārāyaṇa aṁśī yei svayaṁ-bhagavān  
Nārāyaṇa  the source  who  the Lord Himself  
teṅha śrī-kṛṣṇa — aiche kari tā vyākhyāna  
He  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  thus  would make  that  explanation

(85) He would have put it this way: “Nārāyaṇa, the source of the portions, the Supreme Lord Himself — He is Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”

ভ্রম, প্রমাদ, বিপ্রলিপ্সা, করণাপাটব ৷  
আর্ষ-বিজ্ঞবাক্যে নাহি দোষ এই সব ॥ ৮৬ ॥

bhrama, pramāda, vipralipsā, karaṇāpāṭava  
error  inattention  the wish to cheat  imperfection of the senses  
ārṣa-vijña-vākye nāhi doṣa ei saba  
of the sages  wise  in the words  there are not  faults  these  all

(86) Error, inattention, the wish to cheat and imperfect senses — none of these faults are found in the words of the wise sages.

বিরুদ্ধার্থ কহ তুমি, কহিতে কর রোষ ৷  
তোমার অর্থে অবিমৃষ্টবিধেয়াংশ-দোষ ॥ ৮৭ ॥

viruddhārtha kaha tumi, kahite kara roṣa  
a contradictory meaning  speak  you  when told  you show  anger  
tomāra arthe avimṛṣṭa-vidheyāṁśa-doṣa  
in your  meaning  of the ill-considered predicate  the fault

(87) You give a contradictory meaning, and when this is pointed out you become angry. Your interpretation has the fault called *avimṛṣṭa-vidheyāṁśa*, an ill-placed predicate.

যাঁর ভগবত্তা হৈতে অন্যের ভগবত্তা ৷  
‘স্বয়ং ভগবান্’ শব্দের তাহাতেই সত্তা ॥ ৮৮ ॥

yāṅra bhagavattā haite anyera bhagavattā  
whose  divinity  from  of others  divinity  
‘svayaṁ bhagavān’ śabdera tāhātei sattā  
“the Lord Himself”  of the words  in Him alone  application

(88) The words “the Supreme Lord Himself” apply only to Him from whose divinity the others derive Theirs.

দীপ হৈতে যৈছে বহু দীপের জ্বলন ৷  
মূল এক দীপ তাহা করিয়ে গণন ॥ ৮৯ ॥

dīpa haite yaiche bahu dīpera jvalana  
a lamp  from  as  many  lamps'  lighting  
mūla eka dīpa tāhā kariye gaṇana  
the original  one  lamp  that  I count

(89) When many lamps are lit from one lamp, I count that one as the original lamp.

তৈছে সব অবতারের কৃষ্ণ সে কারণ ৷  
আর এক শ্লোক শুন, কুব্যাখ্যা-খণ্ডন ॥ ৯০ ॥

taiche saba avatārera kṛṣṇa se kāraṇa  
so  of all  the avatāras  Kṛṣṇa  He  the cause  
āra eka śloka śuna, kuvyākhyā-khaṇḍana  
another  one  verse  hear  of false interpretations  the refutation

(90) In the same way, Kṛṣṇa is the cause of all the avatāras. Now hear another verse, which refutes false interpretations.

অত্র সর্গো বিসর্গশ্চ স্থানং পোষণমূতয়ঃ ৷  
মন্বন্তরেশানুকথা নিরোধো মুক্তিরাশ্রয়ঃ ॥ ৯১ ॥

দশমস্য বিশুদ্ধ্যর্থং নবানামিহ লক্ষণম্ ৷  
বর্ণয়ন্তি মহাত্মানঃ শ্রুতেনার্থেন চাঞ্জসা ॥ ৯২ ॥

atra sargo visargaś ca  
here  primary creation  secondary creation  and  
sthānaṁ poṣaṇam ūtayaḥ  
maintenance  protection  the impetus for action  
manvantareśānukathā  
the reigns of the Manus  of the Lord  narrations  
nirodho muktir āśrayaḥ  
dissolution  liberation  the shelter  
daśamasya viśuddhy-arthaṁ  
of the tenth  for clear knowledge  
navānām iha lakṣaṇam  
of the nine  here  the description  
varṇayanti mahātmānaḥ  
describe  the great souls  
śrutenārthena cāñjasā  
by direct statement  by implied meaning  and  plainly

(91–92) “Here are described primary creation, secondary creation, maintenance, protection, the impetus for action, the reigns of the Manus, the narrations of the Lord, dissolution, liberation and the shelter. To make the tenth clearly known, the great souls describe the other nine here, plainly, by direct statement and by implied meaning.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.10.1–2)

আশ্রয় জানিতে কহি এ নব পদার্থ ৷  
এ নবের উৎপত্তি-হেতু সেই আশ্রয়ার্থ ॥ ৯৩ ॥

āśraya jānite kahi e nava padārtha  
the shelter  to know  I describe  these  nine  categories  
e navera utpatti-hetu sei āśrayārtha  
of these  nine  of origin  the cause  that  the meaning of shelter

(93) I describe these nine categories so that the shelter may be known. The cause from which these nine arise — that is what *shelter* means.

কৃষ্ণ এক সর্ব্বাশ্রয়, কৃষ্ণ সর্ব্বধাম ৷  
কৃষ্ণের শরীরে সর্ব্ব-বিশ্বের বিশ্রাম ॥ ৯৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa eka sarvāśraya, kṛṣṇa sarva-dhāma  
Kṛṣṇa  alone  the shelter of all  Kṛṣṇa  of all  the abode  
kṛṣṇera śarīre sarva-viśvera viśrāma  
Kṛṣṇa's  in the body  of all  the universes  the repose

(94) Kṛṣṇa alone is the shelter of all; Kṛṣṇa is the abode of all. All the universes rest in His body.

দশমে দশমং লক্ষ্যমাশ্রিতাশ্রয়বিগ্রহম্ ৷  
শ্রীকৃষ্ণাখ্যং পরং ধাম জগদ্ধাম নমামি তৎ ॥ ৯৫ ॥

daśame daśamaṁ lakṣyam  
in the Tenth  the tenth  subject  
āśritāśraya-vigraham  
of the sheltered  of the shelter  the embodiment  
śrī-kṛṣṇākhyaṁ paraṁ dhāma  
called Śrī Kṛṣṇa  the supreme  abode  
jagad-dhāma namāmi tat  
of the world  the abode  I bow to  that

(95) “The Tenth Canto reveals the tenth subject, the embodiment of the shelter of all who take shelter. I bow to that supreme abode called Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the abode of the universe.” (Śrīdhara Svāmī, Bhāvārtha-dīpikā on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.1.1)

কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ, আর শক্তিত্রয়-জ্ঞান ৷  
যাঁর হয়, তাঁর নাহি কৃষ্ণেতে অজ্ঞান ॥ ৯৬ ॥

kṛṣṇera svarūpa, āra śakti-traya-jñāna  
of Kṛṣṇa  the own form  and  of the three potencies  knowledge  
yāṅra haya, tāṅra nāhi kṛṣṇete ajñāna  
whose  is  his  there is not  about Kṛṣṇa  ignorance

(96) One who knows Kṛṣṇa’s own form and His three potencies is never ignorant about Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপের হয় ষড়্বিধ বিলাস ৷  
প্রাভব-বৈভব-রূপে দ্বিবিধ প্রকাশ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

kṛṣṇera svarūpera haya ṣaḍ-vidha vilāsa  
of Kṛṣṇa  of the own form  are  six kinds of  pastime forms  
prābhava-vaibhava-rūpe dvi-vidha prakāśa  
prābhava  vaibhava  in the forms  two kinds of  manifestation

(97) Kṛṣṇa’s own form has six kinds of pastime forms (*vilāsa*). His manifestations are of two kinds, *prābhava* and *vaibhava*.

অংশ-শক্ত্যাবেশরূপে দ্বিবিধাবতার ৷  
বাল্য-পৌগণ্ড-ধর্ম্ম দুই ত’ প্রকার ॥ ৯৮ ॥

aṁśa-śaktyāveśa-rūpe dvi-vidhāvatāra  
partial  empowered  in the forms  two kinds of  avatāras  
bālya-paugaṇḍa-dharma dui ta’ prakāra  
of childhood  of boyhood  the characteristics  two  indeed  kinds

(98) His avatāras are of two kinds: partial avatāras and empowered avatāras (*śaktyāveśa*). There are two further kinds: His forms of childhood and of boyhood (*paugaṇḍa*).

কিশোরস্বরূপ কৃষ্ণ স্বয়ং অবতারী ৷  
ক্রীড়া করে এই ছয়-রূপে বিশ্ব ভরি’ ॥ ৯৯ ॥

kiśora-svarūpa kṛṣṇa svayaṁ avatārī  
youthful  own form  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  the source of avatāras  
krīḍā kare ei chaya-rūpe viśva bhari’  
pastimes  performs  in these  six  forms  the universe  filling

(99) Kṛṣṇa in His own youthful form (*kiśora*) is Himself the source of all avatāras. He sports in these six forms and fills the universe.

এই ছয়-রূপে হয় অনন্ত বিভেদ ৷  
অনন্তরূপে একরূপ, নাহি কিছু ভেদ ॥ ১০০ ॥

ei chaya-rūpe haya ananta vibheda  
in these  six  forms  are  endless  varieties  
ananta-rūpe eka-rūpa, nāhi kichu bheda  
in endless  forms  one  form  there is not  any  difference

(100) These six forms have endless varieties. Yet in all these endless forms He is one form; there is no difference at all.

চিচ্ছক্তি, স্বরূপশক্তি, অন্তরঙ্গা নাম ৷  
তাহার বৈভব অনন্ত বৈকুণ্ঠাদি ধাম ॥ ১০১ ॥

cic-chakti, svarūpa-śakti, antaraṅgā nāma  
the spiritual potency  His own potency  internal  named  
tāhāra vaibhava ananta vaikuṇṭhādi dhāma  
its  opulence  countless  Vaikuṇṭha and other  abodes

(101) The spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*) is called His very own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*) and the internal potency. Its opulence is the countless abodes such as Vaikuṇṭha.

মায়াশক্তি, বহিরঙ্গা, জগৎকারণ ৷  
তাহার বৈভব অনন্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের গণ ॥ ১০২ ॥

māyā-śakti, bahiraṅgā, jagat-kāraṇa  
the potency māyā  external  the cause of the world  
tāhāra vaibhava ananta brahmāṇḍera gaṇa  
its  opulence  countless  of universes  multitudes

(102) The potency called māyā is external and is the cause of the material world. Its opulence is the countless multitudes of universes.

জীবশক্তি তটস্থাখ্য, নাহি যার অন্ত ৷  
মুখ্য তিন শক্তি, তার বিভেদ অনন্ত ॥ ১০৩ ॥

jīva-śakti taṭasthākhya, nāhi yāra anta  
the potency of the jīvas  called marginal  there is not  whose  end  
mukhya tina śakti, tāra vibheda ananta  
principal  three  potencies  their  varieties  endless

(103) The potency of the living beings is called marginal (*taṭastha*), and it has no end. These are the three principal potencies, and their varieties are endless.

এই ত’ স্বরূপগণ, আর তিন শক্তি ৷  
সবার আশ্রয় কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণে সবার স্থিতি ॥ ১০৪ ॥

ei ta’ svarūpa-gaṇa, āra tina śakti  
these  indeed  of His own forms  the multitude  and  three  potencies  
sabāra āśraya kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇe sabāra sthiti  
of all  the shelter  Kṛṣṇa  in Kṛṣṇa  of all  the existence

(104) These are His own forms and the three potencies. Kṛṣṇa is the shelter of them all, and all of them exist in Kṛṣṇa.

যদ্যপি ব্রহ্মাণ্ডগণের পুরুষ আশ্রয় ৷  
সেহ পুরুষাদি সবার কৃষ্ণ মূলাশ্রয় ॥ ১০৫ ॥

yadyapi brahmāṇḍa-gaṇera puruṣa āśraya  
although  of the universes  multitude  the Puruṣa  the shelter  
seha puruṣādi sabāra kṛṣṇa mūlāśraya  
of those  the Puruṣa and others  all  Kṛṣṇa  the original shelter

(105) Although the Puruṣa is the shelter of all the universes, Kṛṣṇa is the original shelter of the Puruṣa and of all the others.

স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণ সর্ব্বাশ্রয় ৷  
পরম ঈশ্বর কৃষ্ণ সর্ব্বশাস্ত্রে কয় ॥ ১০৬ ॥

svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa sarvāśraya  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  the shelter of all  
parama īśvara kṛṣṇa sarva-śāstre kaya  
supreme  Lord  Kṛṣṇa  in all scriptures  is said

(106) Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord Himself; Kṛṣṇa is the shelter of all. All the scriptures declare that Kṛṣṇa is the supreme Lord.

ঈশ্বরঃ পরমঃ কৃষ্ণঃ সচ্চিদানন্দবিগ্রহঃ ৷  
অনাদিরাদির্গোবিন্দঃ সর্ব্বকারণকারণম্ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ  
the Lord  supreme  Kṛṣṇa  
sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ  
of eternity  knowledge  bliss  whose form  
anādir ādir govindaḥ  
without beginning  the beginning  Govinda  
sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam  
of all  causes  the cause

(107) “Kṛṣṇa, who is Govinda, is the supreme Lord. His form is eternity, knowledge and bliss. He has no beginning, yet He is the beginning of all, the cause of all causes.” (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.1)

এ সব সিদ্ধান্ত তুমি জান ভালমতে ৷  
তবু পূর্ব্বপক্ষ কর আমা চালাইতে ॥ ১০৮ ॥

e saba siddhānta tumi jāna bhāla-mate  
these  all  conclusions  you  know  well  
tabu pūrva-pakṣa kara āmā cālāite  
still  objections  you make  me  to unsettle

(108) You know all these conclusions well, yet you raise objections to unsettle me.

সেই কৃষ্ণ অবতারী ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ৷  
আপনে চৈতন্যরূপে কৈল অবতার ॥ ১০৯ ॥

sei kṛṣṇa avatārī vrajendra-kumāra  
that  Kṛṣṇa  the source of avatāras  the son of the king of Vraja  
āpane caitanya-rūpe kaila avatāra  
Himself  in the form of Caitanya  made  descent

(109) That Kṛṣṇa, the son of the king of Vraja, the source of the avatāras, has Himself descended in the form of Caitanya.

অতএব চৈতন্য-গোসাঞি পরতত্ত্ব-সীমা ৷  
তাঁরে ক্ষীরোদশায়ী কহি, কি তাঁর মহিমা ॥ ১১০ ॥

ataeva caitanya-gosāñi paratattva-sīmā  
therefore  Caitanya  Gosāñi  of the Supreme Truth  the utmost limit  
tāṅre kṣīroda-śāyī kahi, ki tāṅra mahimā  
Him  Kṣīrodaśāyī  calling  what  His  glory

(110) Therefore Lord Caitanya is the utmost limit of the Supreme Truth. What glory is there in calling Him Kṣīrodaśāyī?

সেই ত’ ভক্তের বাক্য নহে ব্যভিচারী ৷  
সকল সম্ভবে তাঁতে, যাতে অবতারী ॥ ১১১ ॥

sei ta’ bhaktera vākya nahe vyabhicārī  
those  indeed  of a devotee  words  are not  wrong  
sakala sambhave tāṅte, yāte avatārī  
all  is possible  in Him  since  the source of avatāras

(111) Yet such words of a devotee are not wrong. Everything is possible in Him, since He is the source of the avatāras.

অবতারীর দেহে সব অবতারের স্থিতি ৷  
কেহো কোনমতে কহে, যেমন যার মতি ॥ ১১২ ॥

avatārīra dehe saba avatārera sthiti  
of the source of avatāras  in the body  all  avatāras'  existence  
keho kona-mate kahe, yemana yāra mati  
someone  in some  way  speaks  as  whose  understanding

(112) All the avatāras exist within the body of Him who is their source. So people speak of Him in different ways, each according to his understanding.

কৃষ্ণকে কহয়ে কেহ — নরনারায়ণ ৷  
কেহো কহে, কৃষ্ণ হয় সাক্ষাৎ বামন ॥ ১১৩ ॥

kṛṣṇake kahaye keha — nara-nārāyaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa  calls  someone  Nara-Nārāyaṇa  
keho kahe, kṛṣṇa haya sākṣāt vāmana  
someone  says  Kṛṣṇa  is  directly  Vāmana

(113) Some call Kṛṣṇa Nara-Nārāyaṇa, and some say that Kṛṣṇa is Vāmana Himself.

কেহো কহে, কৃষ্ণ ক্ষীরোদশায়ী অবতার ৷  
অসম্ভব নহে, সত্য বচন সবার ॥ ১১৪ ॥

keho kahe, kṛṣṇa kṣīroda-śāyī avatāra  
someone  says  Kṛṣṇa  of Kṣīrodaśāyī  an avatāra  
asambhava nahe, satya vacana sabāra  
impossible  is not  true  the words  of all

(114) Some say that Kṛṣṇa is an avatāra of Kṣīrodaśāyī. That is not impossible; the words of all of them are true.

কেহো কহে, পরব্যোমে নারায়ণ-হরি ৷  
সকল সম্ভবে কৃষ্ণে, যাতে অবতারী ॥ ১১৫ ॥

keho kahe, para-vyome nārāyaṇa-hari  
someone  says  in the spiritual  sky  Nārāyaṇa  Hari  
sakala sambhave kṛṣṇe, yāte avatārī  
all  is possible  in Kṛṣṇa  since  the source of avatāras

(115) Some call Him Hari, the Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky. Everything is possible in Kṛṣṇa, since He is the source of the avatāras.

সব শ্রোতাগণের করি চরণ বন্দন ৷  
এ সব সিদ্ধান্ত শুন, করি’ এক মন ॥ ১১৬ ॥

saba śrotā-gaṇera kari caraṇa vandana  
of all  the listeners  I do  to the feet  obeisance  
e saba siddhānta śuna, kari’ eka mana  
these  all  conclusions  hear  making  one  mind

(116) I bow at the feet of all my listeners. Hear all these conclusions with undivided attention.

সিদ্ধান্ত বলিয়া চিত্তে না কর অলস ৷  
ইহা হৈতে কৃষ্ণে লাগে সুদৃঢ় মানস ॥ ১১৭ ॥

siddhānta baliyā citte nā kara alasa  
conclusions  thinking (them)  in the heart  not  be  lazy  
ihā haite kṛṣṇe lāge sudṛḍha mānasa  
this  from  to Kṛṣṇa  becomes attached  firmly  the mind

(117) Do not let your heart grow lazy, thinking these are mere philosophical conclusions. Through them the mind becomes firmly attached to Kṛṣṇa.

চৈতন্য-মহিমা জানি এ সব সিদ্ধান্তে ৷  
চিত্ত দৃঢ় হঞা লাগে মহিমা-জ্ঞান হৈতে ॥ ১১৮ ॥

caitanya-mahimā jāni e saba siddhānte  
of Caitanya  the greatness  one knows  through these  all  conclusions  
citta dṛḍha hañā lāge mahimā-jñāna haite  
the heart  firm  becoming  becomes attached  of the greatness  from knowing

(118) Through all these conclusions one comes to know the greatness of Caitanya. Knowing His greatness, the heart grows firm and becomes attached to Him.

চৈতন্যপ্রভুর মহিমা কহিবার তরে ৷  
কৃষ্ণের মহিমা কহি করিয়া বিস্তারে ॥ ১১৯ ॥

caitanya-prabhura mahimā kahibāra tare  
of Caitanya Mahāprabhu  the greatness  to tell  in order to  
kṛṣṇera mahimā kahi kariyā vistāre  
of Kṛṣṇa  the greatness  I tell  doing  in detail

(119) To tell the greatness of Caitanya Mahāprabhu, I have described the greatness of Kṛṣṇa in detail.

চৈতন্য-গোসাঞির এই তত্ত্ব-নিরূপণ ৷  
স্বয়ং ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন ॥ ১২০ ॥

caitanya-gosāñira ei tattva-nirūpaṇa  
of Caitanya  Gosāñi  this  of the truth  the determination  
svayaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇa vrajendra-nandana  
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  the son of the king of Vraja

(120) This is the established truth about Lord Caitanya: He is Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Lord Himself, the son of the king of Vraja.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১২১ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(121) Ever hoping for the mercy of the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
