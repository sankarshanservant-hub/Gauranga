## 159

Pichaladā is a village named Pichaladā on the bank of the Rūpanārāyaṇa river, near Tamluk (?).

## 161

The Oṛiyā *kaṭaka* is the “army camp” (*sainya-kaṭaka*), that is, the encampment on the frontier of the Utkala king's realm; it is what the verse calls the “Oṛiyā *kaṭaka*.”

## 169

*Viśvāsa*. The Yavana ruler of the land of Gauḍa had a department (*daptar*) called the *viśvāsa-khānā*. Only the most trustworthy Kāyasthas were given posts in it. Whenever the king had important business somewhere, Kāyastha *viśvāsas* were sent there.

## 186

O Bhagavān, even one born in a family of *caṇḍālas* or Yavanas at once becomes fit for the *savana* sacrifice as soon as he hears Your name, repeats it after hearing it, bows to You and remembers You. Such a Lord You are — what does the sight of You not bestow?

## 199

Mantreśvara is the name of a large river near Diamond Harbour. Along this river the boat came to the village of Pichaladā on the bank of the Rūpanārāyaṇa; on one side the village of Pichaladā borders the Mantreśvara (?).[^199-1]

## 202

Pāṇihāṭī is the village of Pāṇihāṭī on the bank of the Gaṅgā, not far from the *śrīpāṭa* of Khaḍadaha.

---

[^199-1]: Our verse translation reads differently: there everyone goes ashore at Mantreśvara, a dangerous place. According to the commentary, the Yavana took the Lord across the dangerous river Mantreśvara.
