# Anubhāṣya on Madhya-līlā 20

## 1

**yat** (*yasya*) **prasādāt** (by whose mercy) **nīcaḥ** (one absorbed in worldly things) **api** **bhakti-śāstra-pravartakaḥ** (an author of the scriptures of devotion) **syāt**, **taṁ** **anantādbhutaiśvaryaṁ** (full of unlimited opulence never seen before) **śrī-caitanya-mahāprabhuṁ** **ahaṁ** **vande**.

> Subheading: The request for a favour in return —

## 8

> Subheading: For the sake of pure service to Hari, Sanātana turns even an act condemned by worldly morality to favourable use — this is the true *dharma* —

## 10

## 15

> Subheading: Sanātana is released from prison —

## 16

## 17

> Subheading: Meeting with the chief of the robbers —

## 18

## 20

> Subheading: The robber receives Sanātana warmly; Sanātana bathes and eats —

## 22

*Dui upavāse* — having fasted for two days.

## 23

> Subheading: Sanātana's doubt and alarm; from Īśāna he learns of the money —

## 24

## 26

> Subheading: Sanātana gives the robber the money and asks for help —

## 29

> Subheading: The robber gives up his plan to kill, refuses the money and agrees to help —

## 33

> Subheading: The robber helps Sanātana cross the hills —

## 34

> Subheading: Sanātana asks Īśāna what he still has, and sends him home —
