# Anubhāṣya on Antya-līlā 1

## 1

> Subheading: Homage to the all-merciful Supreme Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu —

**yat-kṛpā** (*yat*, whose — that is, Caitanyacandra's — mercy) **paṅguṁ** (a person without the strength to take a step) **śailaṁ** (to the peak of the highest mountain) **laṅghayate** (carries up), **mūkam** (a person without the power of speech) **śrutiṁ** (the Veda) **āvartayet** (makes recite), **tam īśvaram** (that Supreme Lord, Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Mahāprabhu) **ahaṁ vande** (I worship).

## 2

> Subheading: A prayer for the mercy of the saints —

**santaḥ** (the saints) **sva-kṛpā-yaṣṭi-dānena** (by giving their own mercy as a support) **durgame** (impassable) **pathi** (on the path of worldly existence) **muhuḥ** (again and again) **skhalat-pāda-gateḥ** (of one whose feet stumble, who has strayed from the path) **andhasya** (eyeless) **me** (my) **avalambanam** (shelter) **santu** (may become).
