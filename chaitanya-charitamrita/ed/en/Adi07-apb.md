# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 7

## 0

**Summary.** This seventh chapter describes the glory of the Pañca-tattva. Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who comprises the five *tattvas*, descended and gave the world the holy name and *prema*, and a great flood of *prema* arose. Several kinds of sophists (*kutārkikas*) — the Māyāvādīs, the blasphemers and others — fled from that flood. Wishing to deliver them, Mahāprabhu accepted *sannyāsa* and, by preaching pure devotion, drew all those people to His feet. He also wished to deliver the Māyāvādī *sannyāsīs* living in Kāśī. At the entreaty of the devotees He met all those *sannyāsīs* together in the house of a certain brāhmaṇa in Vārāṇasī-dhāma. First He showed them the majesty of His own true nature (*svarūpa*) and so won their faith. Then, answering their questions, He showed that the doctrine of Māyāvāda has no foundation and pointed out every kind of fault in the views of Śrī Śaṅkarācārya. By the strength of the *sukṛti* they gained by seeing Bhagavān, He brought them onto the path of *bhakti* and bestowed His mercy on them.

## 1

I bow down to Śrī Caitanya, the refuge of those who have no other refuge — that is, of the *akiñcanas* — who grants the greatest goal to people bereft of the highest goal (*paramārtha*). And I describe the magnanimity with which He gives *prema-bhakti*.

## 3

In the first chapter I described *guru-tattva*, dividing it into the *dīkṣā-guru* and the *śikṣā-guru*. The six *tattvas* are those named in the verse “*vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān*….” Now, in this verse, setting *guru-tattva* aside, I examine the remaining five *tattvas*.

## 6

I bow down to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who comprises the five *tattvas*: Kṛṣṇa's *bhakta-rūpa*, *bhakta-svarūpa*, *bhakta-avatāra*, *bhakta* and *bhakta-śakti*.

## 20–21

Śrī Kṛṣṇa's own pastimes are the storehouse of *prema*. That storehouse had indeed come into the world, but its doors were locked and sealed. In the avatāra of Śrī Caitanya the five *tattvas* together broke that seal, opened the doors and, plundering *prema*, tasted it.

## 25–27

When the storehouse of *prema* was thrown open, the flood of the *rasa* of *prema* drowned the whole world with tremendous force. In it perished the seed from which the bondage of ignorance (*avidyā*) grows in the conditioned *jīvas* — their forgetfulness that they are Kṛṣṇa's servants.

## 29

The Māyāvādīs are Prakāśānanda and the other *sannyāsīs*. In every matter that is real they raise disputes about “*māyā*”! They call Brahman “beyond *māyā*,” yet they make Īśvara “an associate of *māyā*” and call the bodies of all Īśvara's avatāras “made of *māyā*.” They say that *māyā* takes part in the very constitution of the *jīva*, that is, that every notion of “I” in the *jīva* is made by *māyā*. From this they conclude that once the *jīva* is liberated, no state that could be called “the pure *jīva*” remains. In other words, they teach that the liberated *jīva* becomes nondifferent from Brahman.

Those devoted to karma (*karma-niṣṭha*) are Devānanda and other *karmīs* devoid of *bhakti*, and the *smārtas* dulled by karma — that is, those who declare karma and its fruits to be the chief aim of the *jīva*.

The sophists are Sārvabhauma and other logicians who deny Īśvara.

The blasphemers are the man whom the Lord chased with a stick, and Gopāla Cāpāla and others who blasphemed the Lord and His devotees.

The *pāṣaṇḍīs* are those who explain other gods as equal to Bhagavān.

The lowly students are those who take learning to be a cause for argument and do not know that learning is the means to attain Īśvara.

## 39

As soon as the Lord accepted *sannyāsa*, the sophists, those devoted to karma, the blasphemers, the *pāṣaṇḍīs* and the lowly students one after another took shelter at His feet, and even many *mlecchas* accepted His guidance. Only the Māyāvādīs of Vārāṇasī-dhāma, having fled from the flood of *prema*, remained apart!

## 45–46

The Vaidya Candraśekhara was a *śūdra* by *varṇa*. It is not proper for *sannyāsīs* to spend the night in a *śūdra*'s house, but Mahāprabhu, out of mercy to him, stayed in his house: for He is the independent Īśvara, and before His mercy all are equal, brāhmaṇa and *śūdra* alike. He accepted *bhikṣā*, that is, His meals, in the house of Tapana Miśra. And nowhere did He accept invitations together with other *sannyāsīs*.

## 57

Even so, the Lord, wishing to bestow mercy on the *sannyāsīs*, inspired the brāhmaṇa from within his heart, and that is why he made such an insistent request.

## 67

“A *sannyāsī* of the *sampradāya*”: according to the teaching of Śrī Śaṅkarācārya, the brāhmaṇas who accept *sannyāsa* in the order of the ten names are the world-honored “Vedic *sannyāsīs*,” that is, genuine *sannyāsīs* sanctioned by the *śāstras*.

## 76

In Kali there is no way other than the name of Hari; the name of Hari is the only way.

## 84

“*Dharma*,” “*artha*,” “*kāma*” and “*mokṣa*” are the four goals of human life (*puruṣārthas*). Love for Kṛṣṇa is the fifth goal. The bliss of Brahman and the like — the first stage of *mokṣa* — cannot be compared even with a single drop of it. *Dharma*, *artha*, *kāma* and *mokṣa* are not the “fruit” of Kṛṣṇa's name. All the *śāstras* agree that love for Kṛṣṇa is the one and only fruit of Kṛṣṇa's name.
