# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 7

## 0

**Summary.** This seventh chapter describes the glory of the Pañca-tattva. Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who comprises the five *tattvas*, descended and gave the world the holy name and *prema*, and a great flood of *prema* arose. Several kinds of sophists (*kutārkikas*) — the Māyāvādīs, the blasphemers and others — fled from that flood. Wishing to deliver them, Mahāprabhu accepted *sannyāsa* and, by preaching pure devotion, drew all those people to His feet. He also wished to deliver the Māyāvādī *sannyāsīs* living in Kāśī. At the entreaty of the devotees He met all those *sannyāsīs* together in the house of a certain brāhmaṇa in Vārāṇasī-dhāma. First He showed them the majesty of His own true nature (*svarūpa*) and so won their faith. Then, answering their questions, He showed that the doctrine of Māyāvāda has no foundation and pointed out every kind of fault in the views of Śrī Śaṅkarācārya. By the strength of the *sukṛti* they gained by seeing Bhagavān, He brought them onto the path of *bhakti* and bestowed His mercy on them.

## 1

I bow down to Śrī Caitanya, the refuge of those who have no other refuge — that is, of the *akiñcanas* — who grants the greatest goal to people bereft of the highest goal (*paramārtha*). And I describe the magnanimity with which He gives *prema-bhakti*.

## 3

In the first chapter I described *guru-tattva*, dividing it into the *dīkṣā-guru* and the *śikṣā-guru*. The six *tattvas* are those named in the verse “*vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān*….” Now, in this verse, setting *guru-tattva* aside, I examine the remaining five *tattvas*.

## 6

I bow down to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, who comprises the five *tattvas*: Kṛṣṇa's *bhakta-rūpa*, *bhakta-svarūpa*, *bhakta-avatāra*, *bhakta* and *bhakta-śakti*.
