# Anubhāṣya on Antya-līlā 5

## 1

> Subheading: For the *jīva* bereft of consciousness (*a-caitanya*), stricken with the disease of worldly existence and fallen into the ocean of *saṁsāra*, the only good lies in taking shelter of Caitanya's feet —

**vaiguṇya-kīṭa-kalitaḥ** (*vaiguṇya* is the ripening of karma; bitten by the worm that it is)[^1-1] **paiśunya-vraṇa-pīḍitaḥ** (*paiśunya* is malice; suffering from the sore, that is, the wound, that it is) **dainyārṇave** (in the ocean of humility) **nimagnaḥ** **aham** **caitanya-vaidyam** (the physician, Mahāprabhu) **āśraye** (I have taken shelter).

## 4

> Subheading: The Lord's conversation with Pradyumna Miśra; Miśra humbly asks the Lord to let him hear about Kṛṣṇa —

## 7

> Subheading: Pretending to know nothing, the Lord tells Miśra, born in a family of *brāhmaṇas* by seed (*śaukra*), to go as a disciple eager to hear to Rāmānanda, the guru of all four *varṇas* and *āśramas*, who was not born in a family of *brāhmaṇas* by seed —

## 8

> Subheading: Praise of the good fortune of one who wishes to hear about Kṛṣṇa —

## 9

> Subheading: The definition of one who is truly fortunate —

## 10

> Subheading: Without *rati* for Kṛṣṇa — the *bhakti* that is the goal (*sādhya-bhakti*) — the practice of prescribed *dharma* is fruitless — (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 1.2.8)

At the beginning of their hearing the *Bhāgavatam* from Śrī Sūta, the disciple of Śrī Śukadeva, Śaunaka and the other sages asked him six questions. The first was: “What is the absolute (*aikāntika*) highest good of man?” Answering it, Sūta explains that man's duty is to worship Adhokṣaja, and along the way he tells how prescribed *dharma* gains its purpose:

**puṁsām** (of people) **yaḥ** **sv-anuṣṭhitaḥ** (the *dharma* well performed — the observance of divine *varṇāśrama* and the like — even when it takes the form of *sādhana-bhakti*) **yadi** **viṣvaksena-kathāsu** (for the narrations about Viṣvaksena, Bhagavān, and about the *bhāgavatas*, that is, for the *kīrtana* of His name, form, qualities, pastimes and so on) **ratim** (taste) **na utpādayet** (does not awaken, then that *svadharma*) **kevalam** (entirely) **hi** (certainly) **śramaḥ eva** (wasted labor, fruitless: that *dharma* has not the slightest result; it is certainly useless — this is the meaning), for it is said: “If a man's work in this world does not lead to *dharma*, his *dharma* to detachment, and his detachment to the service of the feet of Him whose feet are holy places, then though he lives, he is dead.” (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 3.23.56)

## 11

> Subheading: Pradyumna Miśra goes to the house of Rāmānanda, who is absorbed in direct service to Śrī Rādhā-Govinda; Rāya's servant receives him —

## 12

> Subheading: At Miśra's question the servant tells what Rāya — a *mahā-bhāgavata*, a *paramahaṁsa*, an *ātmārāma* — is doing —

## 14

**Nija-nāṭaka** (his own play): the play *Jagannātha-vallabha*, which Śrī Rāmānanda Rāya wrote in Sanskrit.

## 15

> Subheading: Miśra is asked to sit for a while —

## 16

> Subheading: Miśra waits —

## 17

> Subheading: The *ātmārāma* Rāmānanda serves Śrī Rādhā directly; that service is spiritual —

**Abhyaṅga-mardana**: anointing with oil.

## 18

**Maṇḍana**: adorning with ornaments and the like. **Nirvikāra**: Rāmānanda Prabhu sees Śrī Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa, who are beyond the senses (*adhokṣaja*), everywhere (see Madhya 8.273, 274 and 276); he is the crest-jewel of the *paramahaṁsas* and the guru even of the *vidvat-sannyāsīs*. He did not behave like worldly men who think themselves male enjoyers: on seeing women and the like, he did not turn to material enjoyment for the gratification of his own senses, nor did he fall prey to agitation of body or mind.

## 19

> Subheading: The nature of Śrī Rāmānanda Gosvāmī, the crest-jewel of the *vidvat-sannyāsīs*, detached from material enjoyment, who has conquered the six urges (*vega*) —

## 20

> Subheading: In his transcendental perfected body (*siddha-deha*), full of spiritual bliss, the *mahā-bhāgavata* Rāya, following *rāgātmikā-bhakti* in the mood of a gopī, serves his mistress, Śrī Rādhā, in her transcendental spiritual pastimes —

## 21

> Subheading: The glory of Gaura's devotees is inconceivable; among them, in Śrī Rāya, *bhāva*, *prema* and *bhakti* reach their limit —

## 22

> Subheading: To stage his play *Jagannātha-vallabha* before Śrī Jagannātha, Rāya teaches acting in the Jagannātha-vallabha garden —

He taught how, through the actress (*naṭī*), to reveal well before the connoisseur of the transcendental *rasa* of Vraja the deep feelings contained in the songs of the play.

## 24

**Bhāva-prakaṭana-lāsya**: a woman's dance that reveals *bhāva*.

## 25

> Subheading: After feeding the two *devadāsīs*, Rāya sends them home secretly, for the good of ignorant critics who see only with the senses —

## 26

> Subheading: The *mahā-bhāgavata* Rāya's service to his mistress in his perfected body is beyond the sense-born knowledge of living beings who follow the path of logic —

Every day he teaches the *devadāsīs* such transcendental acting. Petty people — worldly, absorbed in sense objects, busy gratifying the senses — cannot, with a mind that craves to enjoy insentient matter (*acit*), grasp the unearthly, transcendental realm of Prabhu Rāmānanda's mind: that mind is made of pure goodness (*śuddha-sattva*) and is engaged in serving Kṛṣṇa.

## 27

> Subheading: Hearing from his servant that Miśra has come, Rāya, who gives honor to others (*mānada*), comes to the assembly hall —

## 28

> Subheading: Rāya, free of pride (*amānī*) and giving honor to others, greets Miśra fittingly and expresses his humility —

## 31

> Subheading: Miśra answers humbly —

## 32

> Subheading: Seeing that the time is unsuitable, Miśra returns home that day —

**Atikāla**: the time for conversation had passed; that is, if they began to talk at the wrong time, it would be inconvenient for both.

## 33

> Subheading: The next day the Lord asks Miśra whether he has heard about Kṛṣṇa from Rāya —

## 34

> Subheading: Miśra tells the Lord about Śrī Rāya —

## 35

> Subheading: Bhagavān, the model teacher of the *dharma* of freedom from pride, humbly glorifies His devotee's love for Kṛṣṇa as greater than His own —

**Prakṛti**: a *yoṣit*, that is, a woman, an object of enjoyment for a man.

## 37

> Subheading: The character of Śrī Rāmānanda Gosvāmī — free from craving for sense pleasure and from any inclination to look at women, the detached crest-jewel of the *vidvat-sannyāsīs*, who sees Adhokṣaja, a *mahā-bhāgavata*, an *ātmārāma* —

## 38

**Saba sevā**: every kind of service (see verse 39).
