## 70

The humble Vidura was the pillow for the feet of Kṛṣṇa, the Thousand-headed Puruṣa. When he spoke thus, the sage Maitreya, his hairs standing on end with the bliss of speaking about Bhagavān, began to speak.

## 72

**ughāḍa-aṅge** — with the body uncovered.

## 76

In separation from Vraja, which was as dear to Him as countless lives, the incoherent words of madness arose in Him, and this state kept growing stronger. Therefore Gauracandra, His mind agitated, rubbed His moonlike face against the wall day after day and bore the blood that flowed from the wounds. This Gaurāṅgadeva, rising in my heart, is driving me mad.
