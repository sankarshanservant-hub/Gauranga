## 15

> Subheading: The well-to-do Śivānanda pays the road toll and serves the devotees by guiding them —

*Ghāṭī-samādhāna* (settling matters at the toll post). When pilgrims or travellers passed through a *zamindar's* estate (*mahāl*), a toll was levied on them. In former times road taxes and the like were not collected, so this toll went to the owners of the roads and landings. At each post Śivānanda Sena paid the toll collectors (*ghāṭoyālas*) the road toll owed by the pilgrims to Jagannātha.

## 16

*Oḍiyā-pathera* — of the road that leads to Orissa.

## 17

The toll collectors behaved outrageously, extorting excess duties from the pilgrims, and to take more than their due they would hold the pilgrims at the toll post. Śivānanda himself stood surety (*jāmin*) for all the pilgrims and had them released.

## 60

> Subheading: In his simple affection Parameśvara lacks outward courtesy, that is, any sense of outer propriety; yet the Lord, who accepts the heart's feeling, is pleased with his guilelessness —

Variant reading: *praśraya pāgala śuddha-vaidagdhī nā jāne*. *Praśraya* means affection, affectionate respect, humility, trust, fond insistence (*ābdār*). *Prāgalbhya* means boldness, arrogance, spirit. *Vaidagdhī* means cleverness, refined taste, grace, skill, learning, artfulness, a cultivated manner.
