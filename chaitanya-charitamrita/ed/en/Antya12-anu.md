## 15

> Subheading: The well-to-do Śivānanda pays the road toll and serves the devotees by guiding them —

*Ghāṭī-samādhāna* (settling matters at the toll post). When pilgrims or travellers passed through a *zamindar's* estate (*mahāl*), a toll was levied on them. In former times road taxes and the like were not collected, so this toll went to the owners of the roads and landings. At each post Śivānanda Sena paid the toll collectors (*ghāṭoyālas*) the road toll owed by the pilgrims to Jagannātha.

## 16

> Subheading: Śivānanda knows the road to Orissa —

*Oḍiyā-pathera* — of the road that leads to Orissa.

## 17

## 108

> Subheading: The Lord — the guru of the world and teacher of mankind in the form of an ācārya — shows exemplary conduct —

“At the morning bath, during a vow, at a *śrāddha*, on Dvādaśī and during an eclipse, oil is like smearing oneself with wine; therefore one should avoid oil.” Some explain the word “vow” (*vrata*) here as “the vow of a renunciant” (*yati-vrata*). The *smārta* Raghunandana Bhaṭṭācārya writes in the *Tithi-tattva*: “Ghee, mustard oil, oil scented with flowers and cooked oil are not faulty for anointing at any time.” That is, for a “householder” it is no fault to anoint oneself with ghee, mustard oil, flower oil or cooked oil.

> Subheading: Addressing the Paṇḍita, the Lord, the guru of the world, teaches the *sādhaka* his duty: to serve with the finest things the Lord alone, the only enjoyer, and to serve out of the heart's natural inclination (*svārasikī*); only in this is the *jīva's* labor of service fulfilled —

## 112

> Subheading: The Lord, the guru of the world and teacher of mankind, teaches the *sādhaka* or ācārya how improper it is to strive for enjoyment to please one's own senses —

A mendicant (*bhikṣu*), that is, a *sannyāsī*, has no helpers and must not accept help from others. Here the Lord says how pleasant it would be to hire, as pleasure-loving enjoyers do, someone like a servant to rub Him with fragrant oil, the companion of luxury. This is sarcasm (*śleṣokti*).

## 114

> Subheading: Censure of one who wears a renunciant's dress yet loves enjoyments that gratify his senses —

*Dārī sannyāsī* — one who enjoys women: a fallen tantric renunciant (*yati*) whose conduct is hypocritical.
