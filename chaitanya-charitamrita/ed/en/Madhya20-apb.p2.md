## 145

Let all those Purāṇas and Āgamas declare, until the end of the *kalpa*, that the deity each of them speaks of is “the chief,” and so bewilder the moving and the unmoving. Yet when all those Āgamas and other scriptures are examined well, in their conclusions they establish Viṣṇu alone as Bhagavān.

## 147–148

What all the statements of the Vedas enjoin, what they establish, what they have in view when they weigh alternatives (*vikalpa*) — no one but Me knows this purport of the Vedas. I say: the statements of the Vedas directly enjoin and name Me, and when they weigh alternatives, they speak of Me as well. I alone am the purport of all the Vedas. The Vedas examine *māyā* alone, at last reject it completely, and come to rest (cease from reasoning).

## 150

The potency of His own nature (*svarūpa-śakti*) and the world, which is the work of all His potencies, have one shelter only — Kṛṣṇa.

## 155

The *para* name is the highest, that is, the principal name: “Kṛṣṇa,” “Govinda” and the like are the principal names of Bhagavān.
