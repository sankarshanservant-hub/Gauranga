# Anubhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 13

## 1

> Subheading: By Gaura's grace even a fallen soul becomes able to describe His pastimes —

**yasya** (of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva) **prasādataḥ** (by the compassion) **ayam** (one like me) **adhamaḥ** **api** **tal-līlā-varṇane** **sadyaḥ** **yogyaḥ** **syāt**, **sa caitanya-devaḥ** **prasīdatu**.

## 5

> Subheading: With the rays of their service to Hari, the moonlike devotees destroy the darkness of the *jīvas'* ignorance —

## 6

> Subheading: The beginning of the description of Gaura's pastimes —

## 7

> Subheading: The promise to describe first in brief, as *sūtras*, and later in detail —

## 8

> Subheading: The 48 years of Mahāprabhu's manifest pastimes —

## 10

> Subheading: The first 24 years, His household pastimes in Navadvīpa; the last 24 years, His enactment of *sannyāsa* in Nīlācala —

## 12

> Subheading: Six of the last 24 years: searching for Kṛṣṇa and preaching in North and South India —

## 14

> Subheading: The household pastimes are the Ādi-līlā, and the *sannyāsa* pastimes the Madhya- and Antya-līlā —

## 15

> Subheading: Murāri compiled the Ādi-līlā in the *Caitanya-carita*, and Svarūpa the last pastimes in the *Kaḍacā* —

## 17

> Subheading: The *sūtras* of these two are the source of every description of the Lord's pastimes —

## 18

> Subheading: The four parts of the Ādi-līlā —

## 19

> Subheading: Homage to the auspicious full moon of Phālguna —

**yasyāṁ** (on which, on the full moon of Phālguna) **kṛṣṇa-nāmabhiḥ** (together with the names of Kṛṣṇa) **śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanyaḥ** (the form not different from Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, the original source of the avatāras, the Lord of Goloka — from His own realm, from the chamber of Gaura, into the manifest world, into the earthly Navadvīpa) **'vatīrṇaḥ** (descended), **tāṁ** **sarva-sad-guṇa-pūrṇāṁ** **phālguna-pūrṇimām** (the goddess in the form of a lunar day [*tithi*], descended into worldly time, beyond this world, devoted to service) (I) **vande** (worship).

## 20

> Subheading: Using the lunar eclipse as a pretext, the Lord moves the *jīvas* to chant Hari's name —

## 22

> Subheading: Throughout the Ādi-līlā the Lord moves everyone to chant Hari's name —

## 23

> Subheading: To make everyone chant the name He cries, and the name alone stops Him —

## 24

> Subheading: Everyone who comes to see Him utters the name —

## 25

> Subheading: The first appearance of the name “Gaurahari” —

## 26

> Subheading: As He grows, He moves the *jīvas* to chant the name at all times —

## 28

> Subheading: In His boyhood, studying and teaching, He explains Kṛṣṇa's name in every subject and moves others to chant it —
