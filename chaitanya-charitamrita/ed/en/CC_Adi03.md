“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 3  
**The Reasons for Śrī Caitanya's Advent**  
(translated from the Bengali for this edition)

শ্রীচৈতন্যপ্রভুং বন্দে যৎপাদাশ্রয়বীর্য্যতঃ ৷  
সংগৃহ্ণাত্যাকরব্রাতাদজ্ঞঃ সিদ্ধান্তসন্মণীন্ ॥ ১ ॥

śrī-caitanya-prabhuṁ vande  
Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship  
yat-pādāśraya-vīryataḥ  
by the power  of taking shelter  at whose feet  
saṅgṛhṇāty ākara-vrātād  
gathers  from the mines  the multitude  
ajñaḥ siddhānta-san-maṇīn  
an ignorant man  of conclusive truth  the precious  jewels

(1) I worship Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. By the power of taking shelter at His feet, even an ignorant person gathers the precious jewels of conclusive truth from a multitude of mines.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host

(2) All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

তৃতীয় শ্লোকের অর্থ কৈল বিবরণ ৷  
চতুর্থ শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন ভক্তগণ ॥ ৩ ॥

tṛtīya ślokera artha kaila vivaraṇa  
of the third  verse  of the meaning  I have made  the explanation  
caturtha ślokera artha śuna bhakta-gaṇa  
of the fourth  verse  the meaning  hear  O devotees

(3) I have explained the meaning of the third verse. Now, O devotees, hear the meaning of the fourth.

অনর্পিতচরীং চিরাৎ করুণয়াবতীর্ণঃ কলৌ  
সমর্পয়িতুমুন্নতোজ্জ্বলরসাং স্বভক্তিশ্রিয়ম্ ৷  
হরিঃ পুরটসুন্দরদ্যুতিকদম্বসন্দীপিতঃ  
সদা হৃদয়কন্দরে স্ফুরতু বঃ শচীনন্দনঃ ॥ ৪ ॥

anarpita-carīṁ cirāt karuṇayāvatīrṇaḥ kalau  
not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in the age of Kali  
samarpayitum unnatojjvala-rasāṁ sva-bhakti-śriyam  
to bestow  most exalted radiant rasa  the treasure of devotion to Himself  
hariḥ puraṭa-sundara-dyuti-kadamba-sandīpitaḥ  
Hari  than gold  more beautiful  of radiance  by a mass  illumined  
sadā hṛdaya-kandare sphuratu vaḥ śacī-nandanaḥ  
always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  the son of Śacī

(4) “Out of mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a long time: the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted and radiant *rasa* of divine love. May that Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, resplendent with a mass of radiance more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the cave of your heart.” (Vidagdha-mādhava 1.2)

পূর্ণ ভগবান্ কৃষ্ণ ব্রজেন্দ্রকুমার ৷  
গোলোকে ব্রজের সহ নিত্য বিহার ॥ ৫ ॥

pūrṇa bhagavān kṛṣṇa vrajendra-kumāra  
complete  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  the son of the king of Vraja  
goloke vrajera saha nitya vihāra  
in Goloka  Vraja  together with  eternal  pastimes

(5) Kṛṣṇa, the son of the king of Vraja, is the Supreme Lord in His fullness. Together with His Vraja He enjoys eternal pastimes in Goloka.

ব্রহ্মার একদিনে তিঁহো একবার ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হঞা করেন প্রকট বিহার ॥ ৬ ॥

brahmāra eka dine tiṅho eka-bāra  
of Brahmā  in one  day  He  once  
avatīrṇa hañā karena prakaṭa vihāra  
having descended  performs  manifest  pastimes

(6) Once in a day of Brahmā He descends and displays these pastimes openly in this world.

সত্য, ত্রেতা, দ্বাপর, কলি, চারিযুগ জানি ৷  
সেই চারিযুগে দিব্য একযুগ মানি ॥ ৭ ॥

satya, tretā, dvāpara, kali, cāri-yuga jāni  
Satya  Tretā  Dvāpara  Kali  four ages  we know  
sei cāri-yuge divya eka-yuga māni  
those  four ages  divine  one age  we count

(7) There are four ages: Satya, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali. Together these four are counted as one divine age (*divya-yuga*).

একাত্তর চতুর্যুগে এক মন্বন্তর ৷  
চৌদ্দ মন্বন্তর ব্রহ্মার দিবস ভিতর ॥ ৮ ॥

ekāttara catur-yuge eka manv-antara  
in seventy-one  cycles of four ages  one  manvantara  
caudda manv-antara brahmāra divasa bhitara  
fourteen  manvantaras  of Brahmā  of the day  within

(8) Seventy-one such cycles make one *manvantara*, the reign of a Manu, and a day of Brahmā holds fourteen manvantaras.

‘বৈবস্বত’ নাম এই সপ্তম মন্বন্তর ৷  
সাতাইশ চতুর্যুগ গেলে তাহার অন্তর ॥ ৯ ॥

‘vaivasvata’ nāma ei saptama manv-antara  
Vaivasvata  by name  this  seventh  manvantara  
sātāiśa catur-yuga gele tāhāra antara  
twenty-seven  cycles of four ages  having passed  of it  within

(9) The present manvantara, the seventh, is called Vaivasvata. When twenty-seven of its cycles of four ages have passed,

অষ্টাবিংশ চতুর্যুগে দ্বাপরের শেষে ৷  
ব্রজের সহিতে হয় কৃষ্ণের প্রকাশে ॥ ১০ ॥

aṣṭāviṁśa catur-yuge dvāparera śeṣe  
in the twenty-eighth  cycle of four ages  of Dvāpara  at the end  
vrajera sahite haya kṛṣṇera prakāśe  
Vraja  together with  takes place  of Kṛṣṇa  the manifestation

(10) at the end of the Dvāpara age of the twenty-eighth cycle, Kṛṣṇa appears in this world together with His Vraja.

দাস্য, সখ্য, বাৎসল্য, শৃঙ্গার — চারি রস ৷  
চারি ভাবের ভক্ত যত কৃষ্ণ তার বশ ॥ ১১ ॥

dāsya, sakhya, vātsalya, śṛṅgāra — cāri rasa  
servitude  friendship  parental love  amorous love  four  rasas  
cāri bhāvera bhakta yata kṛṣṇa tāra vaśa  
of the four  moods  devotees  all  Kṛṣṇa  their  under the sway

(11) Servitude, friendship, parental love and amorous love are the four *rasas*. Kṛṣṇa is under the sway of all the devotees who love Him in these four moods.

দাস-সখা-পিতামাতা-প্রেয়সীগণ লঞা ৷  
ব্রজে ক্রীড়া করে কৃষ্ণ প্রেমাবিষ্ট হঞা ॥ ১২ ॥

dāsa-sakhā-pitā-mātā-preyasī-gaṇa lañā  
servants  friends  father  mother  beloveds  the host  taking along  
vraje krīḍā kare kṛṣṇa premāviṣṭa hañā  
in Vraja  play  makes  Kṛṣṇa  absorbed in love  becoming

(12) With His servants, His friends, His father and mother and His beloveds, Kṛṣṇa sports in Vraja, absorbed in love.

যথেষ্ট বিহরি’ কৃষ্ণ করে অন্তর্ধান ৷  
অন্তর্ধান করি’ মনে করে অনুমান ॥ ১৩ ॥

yatheṣṭa vihari’ kṛṣṇa kare antardhāna  
as He wished  having sported  Kṛṣṇa  makes  disappearance  
antardhāna kari’ mane kare anumāna  
having disappeared  in the mind  makes  reflection

(13) When He has sported to His heart's content, Kṛṣṇa disappears. Then He reflects within Himself:

চিরকাল নাহি করি প্রেমভক্তি দান ৷  
ভক্তি বিনা জগতের নাহি অবস্থান ॥ ১৪ ॥

cira-kāla nāhi kari prema-bhakti dāna  
for a long time  not  I have made  of loving devotion  the gift  
bhakti vinā jagatera nāhi avasthāna  
devotion  without  of the world  no  standing

(14) “For a long time I have not given the gift of loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*). Without devotion the world cannot truly stand.

সকল জগতে মোরে করে বিধি-ভক্তি ৷  
বিধিভক্ত্যে ব্রজভাব পাইতে নাহি শক্তি ॥ ১৫ ॥

sakala jagate more kare vidhi-bhakti  
in the whole  world  Me  they make  devotion by rules  
vidhi-bhaktye vraja-bhāva pāite nāhi śakti  
by devotion by rules  the mood of Vraja  to attain  not  the power

(15) Everywhere in the world people worship Me by the rules of scripture (*vidhi-bhakti*). But devotion by rules has no power to attain the mood of Vraja.

ঐশ্বর্য্যজ্ঞানেতে সব জগৎ মিশ্রিত ৷  
ঐশ্বর্য্য-শিথিল-প্রেমে নাহি মোর প্রীত ॥ ১৬ ॥

aiśvarya-jñānete saba jagat miśrita  
of majesty  with awareness  the whole  world  is mixed  
aiśvarya-śithila-preme nāhi mora prīta  
by majesty  weakened  in love  not  My  pleasure

(16) The whole world is tinged with awareness of My majesty, and love weakened by awe of My majesty does not please Me.

ঐশ্বর্য্যজ্ঞানে বিধি ভজন করিয়া ৷  
বৈকুণ্ঠকে যায় চতুর্ব্বিধ মুক্তি পাঞা ॥ ১৭ ॥

aiśvarya-jñāne vidhi bhajana kariyā  
in awareness of majesty  by rules  worship  doing  
vaikuṇṭhake yāya catur-vidha mukti pāñā  
to Vaikuṇṭha  they go  four  kinds of  liberation  attaining

(17) Those who worship by the rules, aware of My majesty, attain the four kinds of liberation and go to Vaikuṇṭha:

সার্ষ্টি, সারূপ্য, আর সামীপ্য, সালোক্য ৷  
সাযুজ্য না লয় ভক্ত যাতে ব্রহ্ম-ঐক্য ॥ ১৮ ॥

sārṣṭi, sārūpya, āra sāmīpya, sālokya  
equal opulence  the same form  and  nearness  the same world  
sāyujya nā laya bhakta yāte brahma-aikya  
merging  not  accepts  the devotee  in which  oneness with Brahman

(18) *sārṣṭi*, opulence equal to Mine; *sārūpya*, a form like Mine; *sāmīpya*, nearness to Me; and *sālokya*, residence in My world. The devotee never accepts *sāyujya*, merging, in which one becomes one with Brahman.

যুগধর্ম্ম প্রবর্ত্তামু নাম-সঙ্কীর্ত্তন ৷  
চারি ভাব-ভক্তি দিয়া নাচামু ভুবন ॥ ১৯ ॥

yuga-dharma pravartāmu nāma-saṅkīrtana  
the religion of the age  I shall establish  of the Name  congregational chanting  
cāri bhāva-bhakti diyā nācāmu bhuvana  
of the four  moods  devotion  giving  I shall make dance  the world

(19) I shall establish the religion of the age, the congregational chanting of the Holy Name (*nāma-saṅkīrtana*). Giving devotion in the four moods, I shall make the whole world dance.

আপনি করিমু ভক্তভাব অঙ্গীকারে ৷  
আপনি আচরি’ ভক্তি শিখামু সবারে ॥ ২০ ॥

āpani karimu bhakta-bhāva aṅgīkāre  
Myself  I shall make  of a devotee  the mood  acceptance  
āpani ācari’ bhakti śikhāmu sabāre  
Myself  practising  devotion  I shall teach  to everyone

(20) I Myself shall take the mood of a devotee, and by practising devotion Myself I shall teach it to everyone.

আপনে না কৈলে ধর্ম্ম শিখান না যায় ৷  
এই ত’ সিদ্ধান্ত গীতা-ভাগবতে গায় ॥ ২১ ॥

āpane nā kaile dharma śikhāna nā yāya  
oneself  not  having done  religion  to teach  not  is possible  
ei ta’ siddhānta gītā-bhāgavate gāya  
this  indeed  conclusion  in the Gītā and the Bhāgavatam  they sing

(21) Unless one practises religion oneself, one cannot teach it. This conclusion is sung in the Gītā and the Bhāgavatam:

যদা যদা হি ধর্ম্মস্য গ্লানির্ভবতি ভারত ৷  
অভ্যুত্থানমধর্ম্মস্য তদাত্মানং সৃজাম্যহম্ ॥ ২২ ॥

yadā yadā hi dharmasya  
whenever  whenever  indeed  of religion  
glānir bhavati bhārata  
a decline  occurs  O descendant of Bharata  
abhyutthānam adharmasya  
the rise  of irreligion  
tadātmānaṁ sṛjāmy aham  
then  Myself  manifest  I

(22) “Whenever religion declines and irreligion rises, O descendant of Bharata, I manifest Myself.” (Bhagavad-gītā 4.7)

পরিত্রাণায় সাধূনাং বিনাশায় চ দুষ্কৃতাম্ ৷  
ধর্ম্মসংস্থাপনার্থায় সম্ভবামি যুগে যুগে ॥ ২৩ ॥

paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṁ  
for the deliverance  of the saintly  
vināśāya ca duṣkṛtām  
for the destruction  and  of the wicked  
dharma-saṁsthāpanārthāya  
of religion  for the sake of establishing  
sambhavāmi yuge yuge  
I appear  in age  after age

(23) “To deliver the saintly, to destroy the wicked and to establish religion, I appear age after age.” (Bhagavad-gītā 4.8)

উৎসীদেয়ুরিমে লোকা ন কুর্য্যাং কর্ম্ম চেদহম্ ৷  
সঙ্করস্য চ কর্ত্তা স্যামুপহন্যামিমাঃ প্রজাঃ ॥ ২৪ ॥

utsīdeyur ime lokā  
would perish  these  worlds  
na kuryāṁ karma ced aham  
not  would perform  action  if  I  
saṅkarasya ca kartā syām  
of confusion of the social orders  and  the cause  I would be  
upahanyām imāḥ prajāḥ  
I would ruin  these  people

(24) “If I did not perform action, these worlds would perish. I would cause confusion among the social orders and ruin all these people.” (Bhagavad-gītā 3.24)

যদ্যদাচরতি শ্রেষ্ঠস্তত্তদেবেতরো জনঃ ৷  
স যৎ প্রমাণং কুরুতে লোকস্তদনুবর্ত্ততে ॥ ২৫ ॥

yad yad ācarati śreṣṭhas  
whatever  whatever  does  a great man  
tat tad evetaro janaḥ  
that  that  indeed  other  people  
sa yat pramāṇaṁ kurute  
he  whatever  the standard  makes  
lokas tad anuvartate  
the world  that  follows

(25) “Whatever a great man does, others do the same. Whatever standard he sets, the world follows.” (Bhagavad-gītā 3.21)

যুগধর্ম্ম-প্রবর্ত্তন হয় অংশ হৈতে ৷  
আমা বিনা অন্যে নারে ব্রজপ্রেম দিতে ॥ ২৬ ॥

yuga-dharma-pravartana haya aṁśa haite  
of the religion of the age  the establishment  takes place  a portion  from  
āmā vinā anye nāre vraja-prema dite  
Me  except  others  cannot  the love of Vraja  to give

(26) The religion of each age is established by My partial manifestations, but no one except Me can give the love of Vraja.

সন্ত্ববতারা বহবঃ পঙ্কজনাভস্য সর্ব্বতো-ভদ্রাঃ ৷  
কৃষ্ণাদন্যঃ কো বা লতাস্বপি প্রেমদো ভবতি ॥ ২৭ ॥

santv avatārā bahavaḥ  
let there be  avatāras  many  
paṅkaja-nābhasya sarvato-bhadrāḥ  
of the lotus-naveled Lord  all-auspicious  
kṛṣṇād anyaḥ ko vā latāsv  
than Kṛṣṇa  other  who  indeed  to the creepers  
api prema-do bhavati  
even  love  the giver  is

(27) “Let the lotus-naveled Lord have many avatāras, all of them auspicious in every way. But who other than Kṛṣṇa gives love even to the creepers?” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta 1.5.37)

তাহাতে আপন ভক্তগণ করি’ সঙ্গে ৷  
পৃথিবীতে অবতরি’ করিমু নানা রঙ্গে ॥ ২৮ ॥

tāhāte āpana bhakta-gaṇa kari’ saṅge  
therefore  My own  devotees  making  companions  
pṛthivīte avatari’ karimu nānā raṅge  
on earth  descending  I shall do  various  pastimes

(28) Therefore I shall descend to earth with My own devotees as My companions and enjoy many kinds of pastimes.”

এত ভাবি’ কলিকালে প্রথম সন্ধ্যায় ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হৈলা কৃষ্ণ আপনি নদীয়ায় ॥ ২৯ ॥

eta bhāvi’ kali-kāle prathama sandhyāya  
thus  thinking  in the age of Kali  in the first  junction  
avatīrṇa hailā kṛṣṇa āpani nadīyāya  
descended  became  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  in Nadia

(29) Thinking in this way, Kṛṣṇa Himself descended in Nadia in the first period (*sandhyā*) of the age of Kali.

চৈতন্যসিংহের নবদ্বীপে অবতার ৷  
সিংহগ্রীব, সিংহবীর্য্য, সিংহের হুঙ্কার ॥ ৩০ ॥

caitanya-siṁhera navadvīpe avatāra  
of the lion Caitanya  in Navadvīpa  the descent  
siṁha-grīva, siṁha-vīrya, siṁhera huṅkāra  
a lion's neck  a lion's strength  of a lion  the roar

(30) Thus the lion Caitanya descended in Navadvīpa. He had the neck of a lion, the strength of a lion and the roar of a lion.

সেই সিংহ বসুক্ জীবের হৃদয়-কন্দরে ৷  
কল্মষ-দ্বিরদ নাশে যাঁহার হুঙ্কারে ॥ ৩১ ॥

sei siṁha vasuk jīvera hṛdaya-kandare  
that  lion  may He sit  of the soul  of the heart  in the cave  
kalmaṣa-dvirada nāśe yāṅhāra huṅkāre  
of sin  the elephant  is destroyed  whose  by the roar

(31) May that lion take His seat in the cave of the heart of every soul. His roar destroys the elephant of sin.

প্রথম লীলায় তাঁর ‘বিশ্বম্ভর’ নাম ৷  
ভক্তিরসে ভরিল, ধরিল ভূতগ্রাম ॥ ৩২ ॥

prathama līlāya tāṅra ‘viśvambhara’ nāma  
in the first  pastimes  His  Viśvambhara  the name  
bhakti-rase bharila, dharila bhūta-grāma  
with the rasa of devotion  He filled  He sustained  the host of beings

(32) In His early pastimes His name was Viśvambhara, for He filled the world with the *rasa* of devotion and sustained all living beings.

ডুভৃঞ্ ধাতুর অর্থ — পোষণ, ধারণ ৷  
পুষিল, ধরিল প্রেম দিয়া ত্রিভুবন ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ḍu-bhṛñ-dhātura artha — poṣaṇa, dhāraṇa  
ḍubhṛñ  of the verbal root  the meaning  nourishing  sustaining  
puṣila, dharila prema diyā tri-bhuvana  
He nourished  He sustained  love  giving  the three worlds

(33) The verbal root *ḍubhṛñ* means “to nourish” and “to sustain.” By giving love He nourished and sustained the three worlds.

শেষলীলায় নাম ধরে ‘শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য’ ৷  
শ্রীকৃষ্ণ জানায়ে সব বিশ্ব কৈল ধন্য ॥ ৩৪ ॥

śeṣa-līlāya nāma dhare ‘śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya’  
in the later  pastimes  the name  He bears  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
śrī-kṛṣṇa jānāye saba viśva kaila dhanya  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa  making known  the whole  universe  He made  blessed

(34) In His later pastimes He bears the name Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. By making Śrī Kṛṣṇa known, He blessed the whole universe.

তাঁর যুগাবতার জানি’ গর্গ মহাশয় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের নামকরণে করিয়াছে নির্ণয় ॥ ৩৫ ॥

tāṅra yugāvatāra jāni’ garga mahāśaya  
His  as the avatāra for the age  knowing  Garga  the great soul  
kṛṣṇera nāma-karaṇe kariyāche nirṇaya  
of Kṛṣṇa  at the name-giving ceremony  has made  a determination

(35) Knowing Him to be the avatāra for the age, the great sage Garga declared it at Kṛṣṇa's name-giving ceremony:

আসন্ বর্ণাস্ত্রয়ো হ্যস্য গৃহ্ণতোঽনুযুগং তনূঃ ৷  
শুক্লো রক্তস্তথা পীত ইদানীং কৃষ্ণতাং গতঃ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

āsan varṇās trayo hy asya  
there were  colors  three  indeed  of Him  
gṛhṇato ’nu-yugaṁ tanūḥ  
who accepts  in each age  bodies  
śuklo raktas tathā pīta  
white  red  and also  yellow  
idānīṁ kṛṣṇatāṁ gataḥ  
now  darkness  has attained

(36) “Your son appears in every age, accepting a body. He has had three colors: white, red and yellow. Now He has come dark.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.8.13)

শুক্ল, রক্ত, পীতবর্ণ — এই তিন দ্যুতি ৷  
সত্য-ত্রেতা-কলিকালে ধরেন শ্রীপতি ॥ ৩৭ ॥

śukla, rakta, pīta-varṇa — ei tina dyuti  
white  red  yellow color  these  three  lusters  
satya-tretā-kali-kāle dharena śrī-pati  
in the Satya, Tretā and Kali ages  assumes  the husband of Śrī

(37) The husband of Śrī assumes these three lusters, white, red and yellow, in the Satya, Tretā and Kali ages.

ইদানীং দ্বাপরে তিঁহো হৈলা কৃষ্ণবর্ণ ৷  
এই সব শাস্ত্রাগমপুরাণের মর্ম্ম ॥ ৩৮ ॥

idānīṁ dvāpare tiṅho hailā kṛṣṇa-varṇa  
now  in Dvāpara  He  became  dark in color  
ei saba śāstrāgama-purāṇera marma  
this  all  of the scriptures, Āgamas and Purāṇas  the essence

(38) Now, in the Dvāpara age, He has become dark. This is the essence of the scriptures, the Āgamas and the Purāṇas.

দ্বাপরে ভগবান্ শ্যামঃ পীতবাসা নিজায়ুধঃ ৷  
শ্রীবৎসাদিভিরঙ্কৈশ্চ লক্ষণৈরুপলক্ষিতঃ ॥ ৩৯ ॥

dvāpare bhagavān śyāmaḥ  
in Dvāpara  the Lord  dark  
pīta-vāsā nijāyudhaḥ  
in yellow garments  with His own weapons  
śrī-vatsādibhir aṅkaiś ca  
with the Śrīvatsa and other  marks  and  
lakṣaṇair upalakṣitaḥ  
with signs  distinguished

(39) “In the Dvāpara age the Lord appears dark, clad in yellow, bearing His own weapons and marked with the Śrīvatsa and other signs.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.5.27)

কলিযুগে যুগধর্ম্ম — নামের প্রচার ৷  
তথি লাগি’ পীতবর্ণ চৈতন্যাবতার ॥ ৪০ ॥

kali-yuge yuga-dharma — nāmera pracāra  
in the age of Kali  the religion of the age  of the Name  the spreading  
tathi lāgi’ pīta-varṇa caitanyāvatāra  
for that  purpose  yellow in color  the avatāra Caitanya

(40) In the age of Kali the religion of the age is spreading the Holy Name. For this purpose the Lord has descended as Caitanya, yellow in color.

তপ্তহেম-সম কান্তি, প্রকাণ্ড শরীর ৷  
নবমেঘ জিনি কণ্ঠধ্বনি যে গম্ভীর ॥ ৪১ ॥

tapta-hema-sama kānti, prakāṇḍa śarīra  
of molten gold  like  the luster  mighty  body  
nava-megha jini kaṇṭha-dhvani ye gambhīra  
new clouds  surpassing  the sound of the voice  that  deep

(41) His luster is like molten gold and His body is tall and mighty. His deep voice surpasses the thunder of fresh rain clouds.

দৈর্ঘ্য-বিস্তারে যেই আপনার হাত ৷  
চারি হস্ত হয় ‘মহাপুরুষ’ বিখ্যাত ॥ ৪২ ॥

dairghya-vistāre yei āpanāra hāta  
in height  and in breadth  who  his own  cubits  
cāri hasta haya ‘mahā-puruṣa’ vikhyāta  
four  cubits  is  a mahā-puruṣa  is known as

(42) One whose height and the span of whose arms both measure four of his own cubits is known as a *mahā-puruṣa*, a great personality.

‘ন্যগ্রোধপরিমণ্ডল’ হয় তাঁর নাম ৷  
ন্যগ্রোধপরিমণ্ডল-তনু চৈতন্য গুণধাম ॥ ৪৩ ॥

‘nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala’ haya tāṅra nāma  
nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala  is  his  name  
nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala-tanu caitanya guṇa-dhāma  
of nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala  the body  Caitanya  of virtues  the abode

(43) Such a person is called *nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala*, “proportioned like a banyan tree.” Caitanya, the abode of all virtues, has such a body.

আজানুলম্বিত ভুজ কমললোচন ৷  
তিলফুল-জিনি-নাসা, সুধাংশু-বদন ॥ ৪৪ ॥

ājānulambita bhuja kamala-locana  
to the knees  reaching  arms  lotus  eyes  
tilaphula-jini-nāsā, sudhāṁśu-vadana  
than the sesame flower  surpassing  nose  the moon  face

(44) His arms reach down to His knees and His eyes are like lotuses. His nose is finer than the sesame flower, and His face is like the moon.

শান্ত, দান্ত, কৃষ্ণভক্তি-নিষ্ঠাপরায়ণ ৷  
ভক্তবৎসল, সুশীল, সর্ব্বভূতে সম ॥ ৪৫ ॥

śānta, dānta, kṛṣṇa-bhakti-niṣṭhā-parāyaṇa  
peaceful  self-controlled  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  steadfastly  wholly given  
bhakta-vatsala, suśīla, sarva-bhūte sama  
affectionate to devotees  gentle  to all  beings  equal

(45) He is peaceful and self-controlled, wholly and steadfastly given to devotion to Kṛṣṇa. He is affectionate to His devotees, gentle by nature and equal to all living beings.

চন্দনের অঙ্গদ-বালা, চন্দন-ভূষণ ৷  
নৃত্যকালে পরি’ করেন কৃষ্ণসংকীর্ত্তন ॥ ৪৬ ॥

candanera aṅgada-bālā, candana-bhūṣaṇa  
of sandalwood  armlets  bracelets  sandal  ornaments  
nṛtya-kāle pari’ karena kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana  
at the time of dancing  wearing  He performs  Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana

(46) When He dances in the congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's names, He wears armlets and bracelets of sandalwood and is adorned with sandal paste.

এই সব গুণ লঞা মুনি বৈশম্পায়ন ৷  
সহস্রনামে কৈল তাঁর নাম গণন ॥ ৪৭ ॥

ei saba guṇa lañā muni vaiśampāyana  
these  all  qualities  taking  the sage  Vaiśampāyana  
sahasra-nāme kaila tāṅra nāma gaṇana  
in the Thousand Names  made  His  of names  enumeration

(47) Taking all these qualities into account, the sage Vaiśampāyana listed His names in the Thousand Names of Viṣṇu (*Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma*).

দুই লীলা চৈতন্যের — আদি আর শেষ ৷  
দুই লীলায় চারি চারি নাম বিশেষ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

dui līlā caitanyera — ādi āra śeṣa  
two  pastimes  of Caitanya  the first  and  the last  
dui līlāya cāri cāri nāma viśeṣa  
in the two  pastimes  four  four  names  special

(48) Caitanya's pastimes have two parts, the early and the later, and in each He bears four special names.

সুবর্ণবর্ণো হেমাঙ্গো বরাঙ্গশ্চন্দনাঙ্গদী ৷  
সন্ন্যাসকৃচ্ছমঃ শান্তো নিষ্ঠাশান্তিপরায়ণঃ ॥ ৪৯ ॥

suvarṇa-varṇo hemāṅgo  
of golden  color  with a body of gold  
varāṅgaś candanāṅgadī  
with beautiful limbs  wearing sandalwood armlets  
sannyāsa-kṛc chamaḥ śānto  
accepting sannyāsa  equipoised  peaceful  
niṣṭhā-śānti-parāyaṇaḥ  
of steadfastness and peace  the supreme abode

(49) “His complexion is golden and His body is like gold. His limbs are beautiful, and He wears armlets of sandalwood. He accepts the renounced order (*sannyāsa*). He is equipoised and peaceful, the supreme abode of steadfastness and peace.” (Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma, Mahābhārata)

ব্যক্ত করি’ ভাগবতে কহে বার বার ৷  
কলিযুগে — কৃষ্ণনামসঙ্কীর্ত্তন সার ॥ ৫০ ॥

vyakta kari’ bhāgavate kahe bāra bāra  
clearly  making  in the Bhāgavatam  it says  again  and again  
kali-yuge — kṛṣṇa-nāma-saṅkīrtana sāra  
in the age of Kali  of Kṛṣṇa's name  congregational chanting  the essence

(50) The Bhāgavatam says clearly, again and again, that in the age of Kali the congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's name is the essence of all religion.

কৃষ্ণবর্ণং ত্বিষাঽকৃষ্ণং সাঙ্গোপাঙ্গাস্ত্রপার্ষদম্ ৷  
যজ্ঞৈঃ সঙ্কীর্ত্তনপ্রায়ৈর্যজন্তি হি সুমেধসঃ ॥ ৫১ ॥

kṛṣṇa-varṇaṁ tviṣā’kṛṣṇaṁ  
uttering Kṛṣṇa  by luster  not dark  
sāṅgopāṅgāstra-pārṣadam  
with limbs, sub-limbs, weapons and associates  
yajñaiḥ saṅkīrtana-prāyair  
by sacrifices  consisting chiefly of saṅkīrtana  
yajanti hi su-medhasaḥ  
worship  indeed  the intelligent

(51) “Intelligent people worship, by sacrifices consisting chiefly of congregational chanting, the Lord who always utters the name ‘Kṛṣṇa’ (*kṛṣṇa-varṇam*) and whose luster is not dark. He is accompanied by His limbs, His sub-limbs, His weapons and His associates.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.5.32)

শুন, ভাই, এই সব চৈতন্য-মহিমা ৷  
এই শ্লোকে কহে তাঁর মহিমার সীমা ॥ ৫২ ॥

śuna, bhāi, ei saba caitanya-mahimā  
hear  brothers  this  all  of Caitanya  the glory  
ei śloke kahe tāṅra mahimāra sīmā  
in this  verse  it tells  His  of glory  the limit

(52) Hear, brothers, all this glory of Caitanya. This verse tells the very limit of His glory.

‘কৃষ্ণ’ এই দুই বর্ণ সদা যাঁর মুখে ৷  
অথবা, কৃষ্ণকে তিঁহো বর্ণে নিজ সুখে ॥ ৫৩ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa’ ei dui varṇa sadā yāṅra mukhe  
Kṛṣṇa  these  two  syllables  always  in whose  mouth  
athavā, kṛṣṇake tiṅho varṇe nija sukhe  
or  Kṛṣṇa  He  describes  in His own  joy

(53) The two syllables “Kṛṣ-ṇa” are always in His mouth; or else, He describes Kṛṣṇa in His own joy.

কৃষ্ণবর্ণ-শব্দের অর্থ দুই ত’ প্রমাণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণ বিনু তাঁর মুখে নাহি আইসে আন ॥ ৫৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-varṇa-śabdera artha dui ta’ pramāṇa  
of the word kṛṣṇa-varṇa  meanings  two  indeed  established  
kṛṣṇa vinu tāṅra mukhe nāhi āise āna  
Kṛṣṇa  except  His  into the mouth  not  comes  anything else

(54) These are the two established meanings of the word *kṛṣṇa-varṇa*. Nothing but Kṛṣṇa ever comes to His lips.

কেহ তাঁরে বলে যদি কৃষ্ণ-বরণ ৷  
আর বিশেষণে তারে করে নিবারণ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

keha tāṅre bale yadi kṛṣṇa-varaṇa  
anyone  Him  calls  if  dark in color  
āra viśeṣaṇe tāre kare nivāraṇa  
the next  epithet  that  makes  prevention

(55) If anyone should call Him dark in color, the next epithet rules this out.

দেহকান্ত্যে হয় তেঁহো অকৃষ্ণ-বরণ ৷  
অকৃষ্ণবরণে কহে পীতবরণ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

deha-kāntye haya teṅho akṛṣṇa-varaṇa  
by the luster of the body  is  He  not dark in color  
akṛṣṇa-varaṇe kahe pīta-varaṇa  
by not dark in color  it means  yellow in color

(56) By the luster of His body He is not dark, and “not dark” means yellow.

কলৌ যং বিদ্বাংসঃ স্ফুটমভিযজন্তে দ্যুতিভরা-  
দকৃষ্ণাঙ্গং কৃষ্ণং মখবিধিভিরুৎকীর্ত্তনময়ৈঃ ৷  
উপাস্যঞ্চ প্রাহুর্যমখিলচতুর্থাশ্রমজুষাং  
স দেবশ্চৈতন্যাকৃতিরতিতরাং নঃ কৃপয়তু ॥ ৫৭ ॥

kalau yaṁ vidvāṁsaḥ sphuṭam abhiyajante dyuti-bharād  
in Kali  whom  the wise  openly  worship  from the abundance of luster  
akṛṣṇāṅgaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ makha-vidhibhir utkīrtana-mayaiḥ  
not dark in body  Kṛṣṇa  with sacrificial rites  consisting of loud chanting  
upāsyaṁ ca prāhur yam akhila-caturthāśrama-juṣāṁ  
the object of worship  and  they call  whom  of all  who live in the fourth order  
sa devaś caitanyākṛtir atitarāṁ naḥ kṛpayatu  
that  Lord  in the form of Caitanya  exceedingly  to us  may He show mercy

(57) “In the age of Kali the wise openly worship Kṛṣṇa, whose body is not dark because of its abundant luster, with sacrificial rites that consist of loud chanting of His names. They declare Him the object of worship of all who live in the renounced order. May that Lord, who has appeared in the form of Caitanya, be abundantly merciful to us.” (Stava-mālā, Second Caitanyāṣṭaka 1)

প্রত্যক্ষ তাঁহার তপ্তকাঞ্চনের দ্যুতি ৷  
যাঁহার ছটায় নাশে অজ্ঞান-তমস্ততি ॥ ৫৮ ॥

pratyakṣa tāṅhāra tapta-kāñcanera dyuti  
visibly  His  of molten gold  the luster  
yāṅhāra chaṭāya nāśe ajñāna-tamastati  
by whose  radiance  is destroyed  of ignorance  the darkness

(58) His luster of molten gold is plain to see, and its radiance dispels the darkness of ignorance.

জীবের কল্মষ-তমো নাশ করিবারে ৷  
অঙ্গ-উপাঙ্গ-নাম নানা অস্ত্র ধরে ॥ ৫৯ ॥

jīvera kalmaṣa-tamo nāśa karibāre  
of the souls  of sin  the darkness  destruction  to make  
aṅga-upāṅga-nāma nānā astra dhare  
limbs  sub-limbs  the Name  various  weapons  He holds

(59) To destroy the darkness of the souls' sins, He bears various weapons: His limbs (*aṅga*), His sub-limbs (*upāṅga*) and the Holy Name.

ভক্তির বিরোধী কর্ম্ম, ধর্ম্ম বা অধর্ম্ম ৷  
তাহার ‘কল্মষ’ নাম, সেই মহাতমঃ ॥ ৬০ ॥

bhaktira virodhī karma, dharma vā adharma  
to devotion  opposed  action  piety  or  impiety  
tāhāra ‘kalmaṣa’ nāma, sei mahā-tamaḥ  
its  kalmaṣa  the name  that  great darkness

(60) Any action opposed to devotion, whether pious or impious, is called *kalmaṣa*, sin. That is the great darkness.

বাহু তুলি’ হরি বলি’ প্রেমদৃষ্ট্যে চায় ৷  
করিয়া কল্মষ নাশ প্রেমেতে ভাসায় ॥ ৬১ ॥

bāhu tuli’ hari bali’ prema-dṛṣṭye cāya  
arms  raising  Hari  saying  with a glance of love  He looks  
kariyā kalmaṣa nāśa premete bhāsāya  
making  of sin  destruction  in love  He floods

(61) Raising His arms and calling “Hari!”, He looks on all with eyes full of love. He destroys their sins and floods them with love.

স্মিতালোকঃ শোকং হরতি জগতাং যস্য পরিতো  
গিরান্তু প্রারম্ভঃ কুশলপটলীং পল্লবয়তি ৷  
পদালম্ভঃ কং বা প্রণয়তি ন হি প্রেমনিবহং  
স দেবশ্চৈতন্যাকৃতিরতিতরাং নঃ কৃপয়তু ॥ ৬২ ॥

smitālokaḥ śokaṁ harati jagatāṁ yasya parito  
the smiling glance  sorrow  takes away  of the world  whose  entirely  
girāṁ tu prārambhaḥ kuśala-paṭalīṁ pallavayati  
of words  and  the beginning  of good fortunes  the multitude  makes blossom  
padālambhaḥ kaṁ vā praṇayati na hi prema-nivahaṁ  
the touch of the feet  what  indeed  brings forth  not  surely  of love  a flood  
sa devaś caitanyākṛtir atitarāṁ naḥ kṛpayatu  
that  Lord  in the form of Caitanya  exceedingly  to us  may He show mercy

(62) “His smiling glance takes away all the sorrow of the world, and the mere beginning of His words makes good fortune blossom in abundance. What flood of love does the touch of His feet not bring forth? May that Lord, who has appeared in the form of Caitanya, be abundantly merciful to us.” (Stava-mālā, Second Caitanyāṣṭaka 8)

শ্রীঅঙ্গ, শ্রীমুখ যেই করে দরশন ৷  
তার পাপক্ষয় হয়, পায় প্রেমধন ॥ ৬৩ ॥

śrī-aṅga, śrī-mukha yei kare daraśana  
the beautiful body  the beautiful face  whoever  makes  seeing  
tāra pāpa-kṣaya haya, pāya prema-dhana  
his  of sins  destruction  occurs  he obtains  the treasure of love

(63) Whoever sees His beautiful body and His beautiful face is freed from sin and receives the treasure of love.

অন্য অবতারে সব সৈন্য-শস্ত্র সঙ্গে ৷  
চৈতন্য-কৃষ্ণের সৈন্য অঙ্গ-উপাঙ্গে ॥ ৬৪ ॥

anya avatāre saba sainya-śastra saṅge  
in other  avatāras  all  soldiers and weapons  along  
caitanya-kṛṣṇera sainya aṅga-upāṅge  
of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the army  limbs and sub-limbs

(64) In His other avatāras the Lord came with soldiers and weapons, but the army of Kṛṣṇa as Caitanya is His limbs and sub-limbs.

সদোপাস্যঃ শ্রীমান্ ধৃতমনুজকায়ৈঃ প্রণয়িতাং  
বহদ্ভির্গীর্বাণৈর্গিরিশপরমেষ্ঠিপ্রভৃতিভিঃ ৷  
স্বভক্তেভ্যঃ শুদ্ধাং নিজভজনমুদ্রামুপদিশন্  
স চৈতন্যঃ কিং মে পুনরপি দৃশোর্যাস্যতি পদম্ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

sadopāsyaḥ śrīmān dhṛta-manuja-kāyaiḥ praṇayitāṁ  
ever worshipable  the beautiful one  who had taken human bodies  love  
vahadbhir gīr-vāṇair giriśa-parameṣṭhi-prabhṛtibhiḥ  
bearing  by the gods  Śiva and Brahmā  headed by  
sva-bhaktebhyaḥ śuddhāṁ nija-bhajana-mudrām upadiśan  
to His own devotees  pure  the way of worshipping Him  teaching  
sa caitanyaḥ kiṁ me punar api dṛśor yāsyati padam  
that  Caitanya  will  my  again  also  of the eyes  come  within the range

(65) “The gods headed by Śiva and Brahmā, who took human bodies and bore love for Him, always worship Him. To His own devotees He teaches the pure way of worshipping Him. Will that beautiful Caitanya ever again come within the range of my eyes?” (Stava-mālā, First Caitanyāṣṭaka 1)

আঙ্গোপাঙ্গ অস্ত্র করে স্বকার্য্যসাধন ৷  
‘অঙ্গ’ শব্দের অর্থ আর শুন দিয়া মন ॥ ৬৬ ॥

āṅgopāṅga astra kare sva-kārya-sādhana  
limbs and sub-limbs  weapons  make  of their own task  accomplishment  
‘aṅga’ śabdera artha āra śuna diyā mana  
aṅga  of the word  the meaning  another  hear  giving  the mind

(66) His limbs and sub-limbs are the weapons that accomplish His work. Now hear attentively another meaning of the word *aṅga*.

‘অঙ্গ’ শব্দে অংশ কহে শাস্ত্র-পরমাণ ৷  
অঙ্গের অবয়ব ‘উপাঙ্গ’ ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ৬৭ ॥

‘aṅga’ śabde aṁśa kahe śāstra-paramāṇa  
by the word aṅga  a portion  says  scriptural  evidence  
aṅgera avayava ‘upāṅga’ vyākhyāna  
of the aṅga  a part  upāṅga  is explained

(67) According to the evidence of scripture, the word *aṅga* means a plenary portion (*aṁśa*), and *upāṅga* is explained as a part of that portion.

নারায়ণস্ত্বং ন হি সর্ব্বদেহিনা-  
মাত্মাস্যধীশাখিললোকসাক্ষী ৷  
নারায়ণোঽঙ্গং নরভূ-জলায়না-  
ত্তচ্চাপি সত্যং ন তবৈব মায়া ॥ ৬৮ ॥

nārāyaṇas tvaṁ na hi sarva-dehinām  
Nārāyaṇa  You  not  indeed  of all  embodied beings  
ātmāsy adhīśākhila-loka-sākṣī  
the Self  You are  O Lord  of all  worlds  the witness  
nārāyaṇo ’ṅgaṁ nara-bhū-jalāyanāt  
Nārāyaṇa  Your portion  of Nara  born  the water  as resting place  
tac cāpi satyaṁ na tavaiva māyā  
that  and also  true  not  Your  indeed  māyā

(68) “O Lord, are You not Nārāyaṇa? You are the Self of all embodied beings and the witness of all the worlds. The one called Nārāyaṇa because His resting place is the water born of Nara is Your portion. And that portion too is real; it is not Your māyā.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.14)

জলশায়ী অন্তর্য্যামী যেই নারায়ণ ৷  
সেহো তোমার অংশ, তুমি মূল নারায়ণ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

jala-śāyī antar-yāmī yei nārāyaṇa  
lying on the water  the indwelling Lord  who  Nārāyaṇa  
seho tomāra aṁśa, tumi mūla nārāyaṇa  
He too  Your  portion  You  the original  Nārāyaṇa

(69) The Nārāyaṇa who lies on the water and dwells within all is Your portion. You are the original Nārāyaṇa.

‘অঙ্গ’-শব্দে অংশ কহে, সেহো সত্য হয় ৷  
মায়াকার্য্য নহে — সব চিদনন্দময় ॥ ৭০ ॥

‘aṅga’-śabde aṁśa kahe, seho satya haya  
by the word aṅga  a portion  it says  that too  true  is  
māyā-kārya nahe — saba cid-ānanda-maya  
of māyā  a product  not  all  full of spirit and bliss

(70) Here the word *aṅga* means a portion, and that portion too is real. It is not a product of māyā; it is wholly spiritual and full of bliss.

অদ্বৈত, নিত্যানন্দ — চৈতন্যের দুই অঙ্গ ৷  
অঙ্গের অবয়বগণ কহিয়ে উপাঙ্গ ॥ ৭১ ॥

advaita, nityānanda — caitanyera dui aṅga  
Advaita  Nityānanda  of Caitanya  two  limbs  
aṅgera avayava-gaṇa kahiye upāṅga  
of the limbs  the parts  all  I call  sub-limbs

(71) Advaita and Nityānanda are the two limbs (*aṅga*) of Caitanya, and I call the parts of these limbs His sub-limbs (*upāṅga*).

অঙ্গোপাঙ্গ তীক্ষ্ণ অস্ত্র প্রভুর সহিতে ৷  
সেই সব অস্ত্র হয় পাষণ্ড দলিতে ॥ ৭২ ॥

aṅgopāṅga tīkṣṇa astra prabhura sahite  
limbs and sub-limbs  sharp  weapons  the Lord  with  
sei saba astra haya pāṣaṇḍa dalite  
those  all  weapons  are  the unbelievers  to crush

(72) His limbs and sub-limbs are sharp weapons that are always with the Lord, and all these weapons serve to crush the unbelievers (*pāṣaṇḍa*).

নিত্যানন্দ গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ হলধর ৷  
অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য গোসাঞি সাক্ষাৎ ঈশ্বর ॥ ৭৩ ॥

nityānanda gosāñi sākṣāt haladhara  
Nityānanda  Gosāñi  directly  the holder of the plow  
advaita ācārya gosāñi sākṣāt īśvara  
Advaita  Ācārya  Gosāñi  directly  the Lord

(73) Lord Nityānanda is Haladhara Himself, the bearer of the plow, and Advaita Ācārya is the Lord Himself.

শ্রীবাসাদি পারিষদ সৈন্য সঙ্গে লঞা ৷  
দুই সেনাপতি বুলেন কীর্ত্তন করিয়া ॥ ৭৪ ॥

śrīvāsādi pāriṣada sainya saṅge lañā  
Śrīvāsa and others  associates  army  along  taking  
dui senā-pati bulena kīrtana kariyā  
two  generals  go about  kīrtana  performing

(74) Taking with them the army of associates headed by Śrīvāsa, these two generals go about performing *kīrtana*.

পাষণ্ডদলনবানা নিত্যানন্দ রায় ৷  
আচার্য্য-হুঙ্কারে পাপ-পাষণ্ডী পলায় ॥ ৭৫ ॥

pāṣaṇḍa-dalana-vānā nityānanda rāya  
of the unbelievers  crushing  whose vow  Nityānanda  Rāya  
ācārya-huṅkāre pāpa-pāṣaṇḍī palāya  
at the Ācārya's roar  sin and the unbelievers  flee

(75) Nityānanda Rāya is vowed to crush the unbelievers, and at the roar of Advaita Ācārya sin and the unbelievers flee.

সঙ্কীর্ত্তন-প্রবর্ত্তক শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য ৷  
সঙ্কীর্ত্তন-যজ্ঞে তাঁরে ভজে, সেই ধন্য ॥ ৭৬ ॥

saṅkīrtana-pravartaka śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya  
of saṅkīrtana  the founder  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
saṅkīrtana-yajñe tāṅre bhaje, sei dhanya  
by the sacrifice of saṅkīrtana  Him  worships  that one  fortunate

(76) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the founder of saṅkīrtana. Whoever worships Him by the sacrifice of saṅkīrtana is truly fortunate.

সেই ত’ সুমেধা, আর কুবুদ্ধি সংসার ৷  
সর্ব্ব-যজ্ঞ হৈতে কৃষ্ণনামযজ্ঞ সার ॥ ৭৭ ॥

sei ta’ sumedhā, āra kubuddhi saṁsāra  
he  indeed  intelligent  and  foolish  the world  
sarva-yajña haite kṛṣṇa-nāma-yajña sāra  
of all  sacrifices  than  the sacrifice of Kṛṣṇa's name  the essence

(77) Such a person is truly intelligent, while the rest of the world lacks good sense. Of all sacrifices, the sacrifice of chanting Kṛṣṇa's name is the essence.

কোটি অশ্বমেধ এক কৃষ্ণ নাম সম ৷  
যেই কহে, সে পাষণ্ডী, দণ্ডে তারে যম ॥ ৭৮ ॥

koṭi aśvamedha eka kṛṣṇa nāma sama  
ten million  horse sacrifices  one  of Kṛṣṇa  name  equal  
yei kahe, se pāṣaṇḍī, daṇḍe tāre yama  
whoever  says  he  an unbeliever  punishes  him  Yama

(78) Whoever says that ten million horse sacrifices equal a single name of Kṛṣṇa is an unbeliever, and Yama punishes him.

‘ভাগবতসন্দর্ভ’-গ্রন্থের মঙ্গলাচরণে ৷  
এ-শ্লোক জীবগোসাঞি করিয়াছেন ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ৭৯ ॥

‘bhāgavata-sandarbha’-granthera maṅgalācaraṇe  
Bhāgavata-sandarbha  of the book  in the invocation  
e-śloka jīva-gosāñi kariyāchena vyākhyāne  
this verse  Jīva Gosāñi  has made  in explanation

(79) In the invocation of his book *Bhāgavata-sandarbha*, Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī has explained this verse:

অন্তঃকৃষ্ণং বহির্গৌরং দর্শিতাঙ্গাদিবৈভবম্ ৷  
কলৌ সঙ্কীর্ত্তনাদ্যৈঃ স্ম কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যমাশ্রিতাঃ ॥ ৮০ ॥

antaḥ kṛṣṇaṁ bahir gauraṁ  
inwardly  Kṛṣṇa  outwardly  golden  
darśitāṅgādi-vaibhavam  
who has displayed  His limbs and so on  the splendor  
kalau saṅkīrtanādyaiḥ sma  
in Kali  by saṅkīrtana and other means  indeed  
kṛṣṇa-caitanyam āśritāḥ  
Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  have taken shelter

(80) “In the age of Kali, through saṅkīrtana and other practices, we have taken shelter of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa within and golden without, and who has displayed the splendor of His limbs and sub-limbs.” (Bhāgavata-sandarbha 1.2)

উপপুরাণেহ শুনি শ্রীকৃষ্ণবচন ৷  
কৃপা করি’ ব্যাস প্রতি করিয়াছেন কথন ॥ ৮১ ॥

upa-purāṇeha śuni śrī-kṛṣṇa-vacana  
in the Upapurāṇas too  we hear  Śrī Kṛṣṇa's  words  
kṛpā kari’ vyāsa prati kariyāchena kathana  
mercy  showing  Vyāsa  to  He has made  a statement

(81) In the Upapurāṇas too we hear the words that Śrī Kṛṣṇa mercifully spoke to Vyāsa:

অহমেব ক্বচিদ্ব্রহ্মন্ সন্ন্যাসাশ্রমমাশ্রিতঃ ৷  
হরিভক্তিং গ্রাহয়ামি কলৌ পাপহতান্নরান্ ॥ ৮২ ॥

aham eva kvacid brahman  
I  Myself  sometimes  O brāhmaṇa  
sannyāsāśramam āśritaḥ  
the order of sannyāsa  having accepted  
hari-bhaktiṁ grāhayāmi  
devotion to Hari  I cause to accept  
kalau pāpa-hatān narān  
in Kali  by sin  stricken  people

(82) “O brāhmaṇa, sometimes I Myself accept the renounced order (*sannyāsa*) and lead the people of Kali, stricken by sin, to accept devotion to Hari.”

ভাগবত, ভারতশাস্ত্র, আগম, পুরাণ ৷  
চৈতন্য-কৃষ্ণ-অবতার-প্রকট প্রমাণ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

bhāgavata, bhārata-śāstra, āgama, purāṇa  
the Bhāgavatam  the Mahābhārata  the Āgamas  the Purāṇas  
caitanya-kṛṣṇa-avatāra-prakaṭa pramāṇa  
of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  of the descent  manifest  evidence

(83) The Bhāgavatam, the Mahābhārata, the Āgamas and the Purāṇas give clear evidence of Kṛṣṇa's descent as Caitanya.

প্রত্যক্ষে দেখহ নানা প্রকট প্রভাব ৷  
অলৌকিক কর্ম্ম, অলৌকিক অনুভাব ॥ ৮৪ ॥

pratyakṣe dekhaha nānā prakaṭa prabhāva  
directly  see  various  manifest  powers  
alaukika karma, alaukika anubhāva  
extraordinary  deeds  extraordinary  signs of ecstasy

(84) And you can see with your own eyes His many manifest powers: His extraordinary deeds and His extraordinary signs of ecstasy (*anubhāva*).

দেখিয়া না দেখে যত অভক্তের গণ ৷  
উলূকে না দেখে যেন সূর্য্যের কিরণ ॥ ৮৫ ॥

dekhiyā nā dekhe yata abhaktera gaṇa  
seeing  not  see  all  of the nondevotees  the crowd  
ulūke nā dekhe yena sūryera kiraṇa  
the owl  not  sees  as  of the sun  the rays

(85) Yet the nondevotees see all this and do not see it, just as the owl does not see the rays of the sun.

ত্বাং শীলরূপচরিতৈঃ পরমপ্রকৃষ্টৈঃ  
সত্ত্বেন সাত্ত্বিকতয়া প্রবলৈশ্চ শাস্ত্রৈঃ ৷  
প্রখ্যাতদৈবপরমার্থবিদাং মতৈশ্চ  
নৈবাসুরপ্রকৃতয়ঃ প্রভবন্তি বোদ্ধুম্ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

tvāṁ śīla-rūpa-caritaiḥ parama-prakṛṣṭaiḥ  
You  by character, form and deeds  supremely  excellent  
sattvena sāttvikatayā prabalaiś ca śāstraiḥ  
by strength  by pure goodness  mighty  and  by the scriptures  
prakhyāta-daiva-paramārtha-vidāṁ mataiś ca  
of the renowned  godly  knowers of the highest truth  by the views  and  
naivāsura-prakṛtayaḥ prabhavanti boddhum  
not at all  those of demonic nature  are able  to understand

(86) “Your character, form and deeds are supremely excellent, and Your strength and pure goodness, the mighty scriptures and the views of renowned godly knowers of the highest truth all make You known. Yet those of demonic nature can never understand You.” (Stotra-ratna 12)

আপনা লুকাইতে কৃষ্ণ নানা যত্ন করে ৷  
তথাপি তাঁহার ভক্ত জানয়ে তাঁহারে ॥ ৮৭ ॥

āpanā lukāite kṛṣṇa nānā yatna kare  
Himself  to hide  Kṛṣṇa  various  efforts  makes  
tathāpi tāṅhāra bhakta jānaye tāṅhāre  
nevertheless  His  devotee  knows  Him

(87) Kṛṣṇa makes many efforts to hide Himself, yet His devotee knows Him.

উল্লঙ্ঘিতত্রিবিধসীমসমাতিশায়ি-  
সম্ভাবনং তব পরিব্রঢ়িমস্বভাবম্ ৷  
মায়াবলেন ভবতাপি নিগুহ্যমানং  
পশ্যন্তি কেচিদনিশং ত্বদনন্যভাবাঃ ॥ ৮৮ ॥

ullaṅghita-trividha-sīma-samātiśāyi-  
which transcends  the threefold  limits  equal or superior  
sambhāvanaṁ tava parivraḍhima-svabhāvam  
the possibility of  Your  of supremacy  the nature  
māyā-balena bhavatāpi niguhyamānaṁ  
by the power of māyā  by You  even  being hidden  
paśyanti kecid aniśaṁ tvad-ananya-bhāvāḥ  
see  some  constantly  devoted to You alone

(88) “Your supreme nature transcends the three kinds of limits, and nothing can be equal to it or greater. Though You hide it by the power of Your own māyā, some, devoted to You alone, see it at all times.” (Stotra-ratna 13)

অসুরস্বভাবে কৃষ্ণে কভু নাহি জানে ৷  
লুকাইতে নারে কৃষ্ণ ভক্তজন-স্থানে ॥ ৮৯ ॥

asura-svabhāve kṛṣṇe kabhu nāhi jāne  
by demonic  nature  Kṛṣṇa  ever  not  know  
lukāite nāre kṛṣṇa bhakta-jana-sthāne  
to hide  cannot  Kṛṣṇa  from His devotees  the people

(89) Those of demonic nature never know Kṛṣṇa, but Kṛṣṇa cannot hide from His devotees.

দ্বৌ ভূতসর্গৌ লোকেঽস্মিন্ দৈব আসুর এব চ ৷  
বিষ্ণুভক্তঃ স্মৃতো দৈব আসুরস্তদ্বিপর্য্যয়ঃ ॥ ৯০ ॥

dvau bhūta-sargau loke ’smin  
two  of beings  creations  in the world  this  
daiva āsura eva ca  
divine  demonic  indeed  and  
viṣṇu-bhaktaḥ smṛto daiva  
a devotee of Viṣṇu  is known  as divine  
āsuras tad-viparyayaḥ  
demonic  his  opposite

(90) “In this world there are two kinds of created beings, the divine and the demonic. A devotee of Viṣṇu is called divine, and the demonic is his opposite.” (Padma Purāṇa)

আচার্য্য গোসাঞি প্রভুর ভক্ত-অবতার ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-অবতার-হেতু যাঁহার হুঙ্কার ॥ ৯১ ॥

ācārya gosāñi prabhura bhakta-avatāra  
the Ācārya  Gosāñi  the Lord's  devotee-avatāra  
kṛṣṇa-avatāra-hetu yāṅhāra huṅkāra  
of Kṛṣṇa's descent  the cause  whose  roar

(91) Advaita Ācārya is the Lord's avatāra as a devotee, and His roar caused Kṛṣṇa to descend.

কৃষ্ণ যদি পৃথিবীতে করেন অবতার ৷  
প্রথমে করেন গুরুবর্গের সঞ্চার ॥ ৯২ ॥

kṛṣṇa yadi pṛthivīte karena avatāra  
Kṛṣṇa  when  on earth  makes  descent  
prathame karena guru-vargera sañcāra  
first  He makes  of His elders  the appearance

(92) Whenever Kṛṣṇa descends on earth, He first sends His elders.

পিতা মাতা গুরু আদি যত মান্যগণ ৷  
প্রথমে করেন সবার পৃথিবীতে জনম ॥ ৯৩ ॥

pitā mātā guru ādi yata mānya-gaṇa  
father  mother  guru  and so on  all  the respected ones  
prathame karena sabāra pṛthivīte janama  
first  He makes  of all  on earth  the birth

(93) First He has all those He honors, His father, mother, guru and others, take birth on earth.

মাধব-ঈশ্বর-পুরী, শচী, জগন্নাথ ৷  
অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য প্রকট হৈলা সেই সাথ ॥ ৯৪ ॥

mādhava-īśvara-purī, śacī, jagannātha  
Mādhava  Īśvara  Purī  Śacī  Jagannātha  
advaita ācārya prakaṭa hailā sei sātha  
Advaita  Ācārya  manifest  became  with them

(94) Mādhavendra Purī, Īśvara Purī, Śacī and Jagannātha Miśra appeared, and Advaita Ācārya appeared along with them.

প্রকটিয়া দেখে আচার্য্য সকল সংসার ৷  
কৃষ্ণভক্তিগন্ধহীন বিষয়-ব্যবহার ॥ ৯৫ ॥

prakaṭiyā dekhe ācārya sakala saṁsāra  
having appeared  sees  the Ācārya  the whole  world  
kṛṣṇa-bhakti gandha-hīna viṣaya-vyavahāra  
of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without a trace  worldly  dealings

(95) When Advaita Ācārya appeared, He saw the whole world absorbed in worldly affairs, without a trace of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

কেহ পাপে, কেহ পুণ্যে করে বিষয়-ভোগ ৷  
ভক্তিগন্ধ নাহি, যাতে যায় ভবরোগ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

keha pāpe, keha puṇye kare viṣaya-bhoga  
some  in sin  some  in piety  make  sense enjoyment  
bhakti-gandha nāhi, yāte yāya bhava-roga  
of devotion  a trace  none  by which  goes away  the disease of worldly existence

(96) Some enjoyed the world through sin and some through piety, but no one had even a trace of devotion, which cures the disease of worldly existence.

লোকগতি দেখি’ আচার্য্য করুণ-হৃদয় ৷  
বিচার করেন, লোকের কৈছে হিত হয় ॥ ৯৭ ॥

loka-gati dekhi’ ācārya karuṇa-hṛdaya  
the state of the people  seeing  the Ācārya  compassionate at heart  
vicāra karena, lokera kaiche hita haya  
consideration  makes  of the people  how  welfare  will be

(97) Seeing the condition of the people, the compassionate Ācārya considered how they could be truly benefited.

আপনি শ্রীকৃষ্ণ যদি করেন অবতার ৷  
আপনে আচরি’ ভক্তি করেন প্রচার ॥ ৯৮ ॥

āpani śrī-kṛṣṇa yadi karena avatāra  
Himself  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  if  makes  descent  
āpane ācari’ bhakti karena pracāra  
Himself  practising  devotion  makes  preaching

(98) “If Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself descends, He will practise devotion Himself and preach it.

নাম বিনু কলিকালে ধর্ম্ম নাহি আর ৷  
কলিকালে কৈছে হবে কৃষ্ণ অবতার ॥ ৯৯ ॥

nāma vinu kali-kāle dharma nāhi āra  
the Name  except  in the age of Kali  religion  none  other  
kali-kāle kaiche habe kṛṣṇa avatāra  
in the age of Kali  how  will be  Kṛṣṇa's  descent

(99) In the age of Kali there is no religion but the Holy Name. Yet how will Kṛṣṇa descend in the age of Kali?

শুদ্ধভাবে করিব কৃষ্ণের আরাধন ৷  
নিরন্তর সদৈন্যে করিব নিবেদন ॥ ১০০ ॥

śuddha-bhāve kariba kṛṣṇera ārādhana  
with a pure  heart  I shall do  Kṛṣṇa's  worship  
nirantara sadainye kariba nivedana  
constantly  with humility  I shall make  entreaty

(100) I shall worship Kṛṣṇa with a pure heart and entreat Him constantly with humility.

আনিয়া কৃষ্ণেরে করোঁ কীর্ত্তন সঞ্চার ৷  
তবে সে ‘অদ্বৈত’ নাম সফল আমার ॥ ১০১ ॥

āniyā kṛṣṇere karoṅ kīrtana sañcāra  
bringing  Kṛṣṇa  I make  of kīrtana  the spreading  
tabe se ‘advaita’ nāma saphala āmāra  
then  indeed  Advaita  name  fruitful  My

(101) If I bring Kṛṣṇa and spread *kīrtana*, only then will My name Advaita be fulfilled.”

কৃষ্ণ বশ করিবেন কোন্ আরাধনে ৷  
বিচারিতে এক শ্লোক আইল তাঁর মনে ॥ ১০২ ॥

kṛṣṇa vaśa karibena kon ārādhane  
Kṛṣṇa  subdued  to make  by what  worship  
vicārite eka śloka āila tāṅra mane  
while considering  one  verse  came  to His  mind

(102) As He considered by what worship He could win Kṛṣṇa over, a verse came to His mind:

তুলসীদলমাত্রেণ জলস্য চুলুকেন বা ৷  
বিক্রীণীতে স্বমাত্মানং ভক্তেভ্যো ভক্তবৎসলঃ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

tulasī-dala-mātreṇa  
of tulasī  a leaf  merely by  
jalasya culukena vā  
of water  by a palmful  or  
vikrīṇīte svam ātmānaṁ  
sells  His own  self  
bhaktebhyo bhakta-vatsalaḥ  
to the devotees  affectionate to His devotees

(103) “Kṛṣṇa, who is affectionate to His devotees, sells Himself to them for merely a leaf of tulasī or a palmful of water.” (Gautamīya-tantra)

এ শ্লোকার্থ আচার্য্য করেন বিচারণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণকে তুলসীজল দেয় যেই জন ॥ ১০৪ ॥

তার ঋণ শোধিতে কৃষ্ণ করেন চিন্তন ৷  
‘জল-তুলসীর সম কিছু ঘরে নাহি ধন’ ॥ ১০৫ ॥

e ślokārtha ācārya karena vicāraṇa  
this  the meaning of the verse  the Ācārya  makes  consideration  
kṛṣṇake tulasī-jala deya yei jana  
to Kṛṣṇa  tulasī and water  gives  who  the person  
tāra ṛṇa śodhite kṛṣṇa karena cintana  
his  debt  to repay  Kṛṣṇa  makes  thought  
‘jala-tulasīra sama kichu ghare nāhi dhana’  
to water and tulasī  equal  any  in the house  not  wealth

(104–105) The Ācārya pondered the meaning of this verse: “When someone offers Kṛṣṇa tulasī and water, Kṛṣṇa thinks how to repay His debt to that person: ‘In My house there is no wealth equal to water and tulasī.’

তবে আত্মা বেচি’ করে ঋণের শোধন ৷  
এত ভাবি’ আচার্য্য করেন আরাধন ॥ ১০৬ ॥

tabe ātmā veci’ kare ṛṇera śodhana  
then  Himself  selling  makes  of the debt  repayment  
eta bhāvi’ ācārya karena ārādhana  
thus  thinking  the Ācārya  makes  worship

(106) So He repays the debt by selling Himself.” Thinking in this way, the Ācārya began His worship.

গঙ্গাজলে তুলসীমঞ্জরী অনুক্ষণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণপাদপদ্ম ভাবি’ করে সমর্পণ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

gaṅgā-jale tulasī-mañjarī anukṣaṇa  
with Gaṅgā water  tulasī buds  constantly  
kṛṣṇa-pāda-padma bhāvi’ kare samarpaṇa  
Kṛṣṇa's  feet  lotus  meditating on  makes  offering

(107) Meditating on Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet, He constantly offered them tulasī buds (*mañjarī*) with Gaṅgā water.

কৃষ্ণের আহ্বান করে করিয়া হুঙ্কার ৷  
এমতে কৃষ্ণেরে করাইল অবতার ॥ ১০৮ ॥

kṛṣṇera āhvāna kare kariyā huṅkāra  
of Kṛṣṇa  the invocation  makes  making  a roar  
e-mate kṛṣṇere karāila avatāra  
in this way  Kṛṣṇa  caused  descent

(108) With mighty roars He called out to Kṛṣṇa, and in this way He caused Kṛṣṇa to descend.

চৈতন্যের অবতারে এই মুখ্য হেতু ৷  
ভক্তের ইচ্ছায় অবতরে ধর্ম্মসেতু ॥ ১০৯ ॥

caitanyera avatāre ei mukhya hetu  
of Caitanya  in the descent  this  main  cause  
bhaktera icchāya avatare dharma-setu  
of the devotee  by the desire  descends  the upholder of religion

(109) This is the main cause of Caitanya's descent: the Lord, the upholder of religion, descends by the desire of His devotee.

ত্বং ভক্তিযোগপরিভাবিত-হৃৎসরোজ  
আস্সে শ্রুতেক্ষিতপথো ননু নাথ পুংসাম্ ৷  
যদ্যদ্ধিয়া ত উরুগায় বিভাবয়ন্তি  
তত্তদ্বপুঃ প্রণয়সে সদনুগ্রহায় ॥ ১১০ ॥

tvaṁ bhakti-yoga-paribhāvita-hṛt-saroja  
You  by bhakti-yoga  purified  of the heart  in the lotus  
āsse śrutekṣita-patho nanu nātha puṁsām  
reside  whose path is seen by hearing  indeed  O Lord  of persons  
yad yad dhiyā ta urugāya vibhāvayanti  
whatever  whatever  with the mind  Your  O widely sung Lord  they contemplate  
tat tad vapuḥ praṇayase sad-anugrahāya  
that  that  form  You manifest  to favor the devoted

(110) “O Lord, You are seen by the path of hearing, and You dwell in the lotus of the heart purified by *bhakti-yoga*. O widely glorified Lord, whatever form of Yours Your devotees contemplate, You manifest that very form to show them Your favor.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 3.9.11)

এই শ্লোকের অর্থ কহি সংক্ষেপের সার ৷  
ভক্তের ইচ্ছায় কৃষ্ণের সর্ব্ব অবতার ॥ ১১১ ॥

ei ślokera artha kahi saṅkṣepera sāra  
of this  verse  the meaning  I tell  in brief  the essence  
bhaktera icchāya kṛṣṇera sarva avatāra  
of the devotee  by the desire  of Kṛṣṇa  all  descents

(111) I tell the essence of this verse in brief: all of Kṛṣṇa's descents take place by the desire of His devotees.

চতুর্থ শ্লোকের অর্থ হৈল সুনিশ্চিতে ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হৈলা গৌর প্রেম প্রকাশিতে ॥ ১১২ ॥

caturtha ślokera artha haila suniścite  
of the fourth  verse  the meaning  became  well established  
avatīrṇa hailā gaura prema prakāśite  
descended  became  Gaura  love  to reveal

(112) Thus the meaning of the fourth verse has been firmly established: Gaura descended to reveal love (*prema*).

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১১৩ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(113) Ever hoping for the mercy of the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
