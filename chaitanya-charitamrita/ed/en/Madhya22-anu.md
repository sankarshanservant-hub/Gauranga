> Subheading: Obeisance to the Lord, the *avatāra* who purifies the age of Kali, the giver of *prema* —

**kalau** (in the age devoid of *dharma*, the time of disputes based on logic) **yuge** **api** **yena** (by Mahāprabhu) **ati-gūḍhā** (unknown even to the knowers of true *dharma* in the Satya, Tretā and Dvāpara ages, when *dharma* abounded) **iyaṁ** **bhaktiḥ** (unmotivated service to Kṛṣṇa) **prakāśitā** (was proclaimed to all), **taṁ** **karuṇārṇavam** (the ocean of compassion for the *jīvas*) **śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-devam** **ahaṁ** **vande**.

> Subheading: *Śruti*, *smṛti*, the Purāṇas and the Pañcarātra enjoin devotion to Kṛṣṇa alone as the *abhidheya* — (the words of a sage) —

**mātā** (the mother — like a mother, wishing their welfare and protecting the *jīvas*) **śrutiḥ** **pṛṣṭā** (when asked) **bhavad-ārādhana-vidhim** (service to Kṛṣṇa) **diśati** (commands); **yathā** **mātuḥ** (of *śruti*) **vāṇī** (the word), **tathā** **bhaginī** (cherished by mother *śruti*) **smṛtiḥ** **api** **vakti** (reveals — speaks of devotion to Kṛṣṇa); **purāṇādyāḥ** (the Purāṇas, *Āgamas* and others) **ye** **vā** **sahaja-nivahāḥ** (the brothers), **te** (they too) **tad-anugāḥ** (following the mother and the sister, teach devotion to Kṛṣṇa); **ataḥ** **he murahara** (O Murāri), **bhavān** **eva** (my) **śaraṇam** **iti** (by me) **satyaṁ** **jñātam**.

## 7

> Subheading: Kṛṣṇa and His *svarūpa-śakti*, though one, are joined to each other in embrace for the sake of pastimes (*vilāsa*) —

Kṛṣṇa is the truth of nondual knowledge (*advaya-jñāna-tattva*). The potency and the possessor of potency are the truth of nondifference (*abheda-tattva*). Let no one mistakenly take the word “potency” to mean the *māyā-śakti*, which covers the *jīva*'s true nature. The potency that is engaged solely in the service of Kṛṣṇa Himself, the *svarūpa-śakti*, is distinct from the *māyā-śakti*. The *svarūpa-śakti* and Kṛṣṇa, the possessor of the *svarūpa-śakti*, abide inseparably.

## 16

> Subheading: The prayer of one who has surrendered — (*Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, Paścima-vibhāga, “Prīti-bhakti-laharī,” sixth verse) —

**kāmādīnām** (of lust, anger, greed, delusion, intoxication, envy and the rest) **durnideśāḥ** (wicked orders) **katidhā** (of how many kinds) **mayā** **kati** **na** **pālitāḥ** (have I not obeyed? — indeed I have); **teṣāṁ** (on the part of those enemies, lust and the others) **mayi** **karuṇā** (compassion), **na** **trapā** (nor shame on my part), **na** **upaśāntiḥ** (nor even a wish on my part to give them up) **na** **ca** **jātā** (has arisen). **atha** (after that) **he yadupate**, **sāmpratam** (now) **tān** (lust and the others) **utsṛjya** (casting off the rule of the enemies) **labdha-buddhiḥ** (having gained understanding) **abhayam** (who grants complete fearlessness) **śaraṇaṁ** **tvām** **āyātaḥ** (I have come); **mām** **ātma-dāsye** (in service to You) **niyuṅkṣva** (engage).
