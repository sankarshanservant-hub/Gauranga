> Subheading: Singing *kīrtana*, He goes around the town of Navadvīpa —

The *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya, Chapter 23: “May my mind cling to Your feet, (O Śārṅgadhara,) may my mind cling to Your feet! This is Caitanyacandra's first *saṅkīrtana*: the devotees sing, and Śrī Śacīnandana dances. In Nadīyā a road runs along the bank of the Gaṅgā, and along that road, ahead of all, Gaurarāya goes dancing. First Gaurahari danced a long while at His own *ghāṭa*, and then He went on to Mādhāi's *ghāṭa*. ‘Viśvambhara, the Lord of all, dances along the banks of the Bhāgīrathī’ (refrain). At the edge of Nadīyā lies the settlement of Simuliyā, and there the Lord arrived, dancing all the way. Then the Ṭhākura took the road to the Kazi's house, and dancing He came to the Kazi's quarter.”
