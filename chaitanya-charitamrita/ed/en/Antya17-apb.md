# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Antya-līlā 17

## 0

**Summary.** Mahāprabhu was overcome by the madness of love (*premonmāda*) in many different forms. This chapter describes how one night, without opening the doors, He passed over three walls and fell among the cows from Tailaṅga, having taken the shape of a tortoise.

## 1

I write of the most wonderful, unearthly acts of Śrī Gauracandra in His divine madness only after hearing of them from the mouths of those who saw them with their own eyes.

## 29

**karṇa-tṛṣṇāya** — from thirst to hear of Kṛṣṇa's qualities.

## 33–38

Absorbed in their feeling, the *gopīs* entered the *rāsa-līlā*. There they heard Kṛṣṇa's words of disregard (*upekṣā-vacana*), that is, His indifferent words, and took it as true that Kṛṣṇa had rejected them. So they speak to Kṛṣṇa in anger: “O lover (*nāgara*), tell us: is there any worthy woman in the three worlds whom Your flute does not attract? When You play Your flute in this world, its sound, like a *yoginī* who has mastered mantras and the like, becomes a go-between (*dūtī*) and enchants (allures) the minds of women. It greatly increases their longing, makes them abandon the path enjoined by the Vedas (serving their husbands, elders, and others), and delivers them to You (in the mood of *parakīyā* beloveds). With Your flute and with the arrows of Kāma — Your glances — You pierced us, made us give up the path of *dharma*, shame, and fear, and brought us to You. You Yourself made us leave our husbands and so on, and now You point out to us that this is a sin and, like a righteous man, teach us *dharma*! In Your mind is one thing, in Your words another, and in Your conduct a third. All this is the skilled trickery of a cheat (mere cunning). You know only how to jest, but for a woman it is utter ruin. So give up all this deceit. First, the sound of the flute is churned nectar; on top of that, sweet words — the nectar of speech; and on top of that, a nectarean sound [of ornaments]. These three nectars together steal our ears, mind, and life.”

## 38

**śiñjita** — sound.
