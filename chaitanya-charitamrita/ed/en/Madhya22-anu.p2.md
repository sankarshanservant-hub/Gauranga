## 92

> Subheading: Kṛṣṇa is the only one to be worshiped —

Kṛṣṇa loves His devotees, and He is grateful, generous and all-powerful. No wise person leaves Him to worship trivial things other than Kṛṣṇa. One who gives up the worship of Kṛṣṇa and becomes infatuated with material objects is a fool who destroys himself, and one as foolish as he is very rare.

## 93

> Subheading: Kṛṣṇa, the Self of all, gives His very Self —

After fulfilling Kubjā's cherished desire, Bhagavān Śrī Kṛṣṇa came with Rāma to Akrūra's house, wishing to send him to Hastināpura. Akrūra, bowing to Them, offers this prayer:

(Since You) **bhajataḥ** (to those who worship You) **sarvān** **suhṛdaḥ** (to friends) **abhikāmān** (what they desire, in full), **yasya** (and He for whom) **upacayāpacayau** (decrease and increase) **na** (there are not), (such) **ātmānam** (Your own form) **api** **dadāti**, (therefore) **bhakta-priyāt** (who love Your devotees) **ṛta-giraḥ** (truthful in speech) **suhṛdaḥ** (the friend) **kṛtajñāt** (who repay the devotees' love) **tvattaḥ** (apart from You) **aparaṁ** **śaraṇam** (shelter) **kaḥ** **paṇḍitaḥ** **samīyāt** (would go to)?

## 94

> Subheading: Uddhava is the proof that one should worship Kṛṣṇa alone —

As soon as an experienced person comes to know Kṛṣṇa's qualities, he gives up other worship and worships Kṛṣṇa alone. Uddhava is the proof of this.

## 95

> Subheading: Kṛṣṇa is supremely merciful, an ocean of mercy —

The *mahā-bhāgavata* Śrīla Uddhava, stricken with grief in separation from Śrī Kṛṣṇa, describes to Vidura Śrī Kṛṣṇa's childhood pastimes and more:

**aho** (how wonderful) **bakī** (Pūtanā) **jighāṁsayā** (even with the wish to kill) **stana-kāla-kūṭam** (the *kālakūṭa* poison smeared on her breasts) **yam** (Kṛṣṇa) **apāyayat**, **asādhvī** (though a wicked demoness hostile to Kṛṣṇa) **dhātry-ucitām** (befitting a nurse who gives the breast and protects) **gatim** (the highest destination) **lebhe**, **tataḥ** (than Śrī Kṛṣṇa) **anyam** (other) **kaṁ** **vā** **dayālum** (generous one) **śaraṇaṁ** **vrajema** (that is, whom shall we worship)?

## 97

> Subheading: The six kinds of *śaraṇāgati* —

**ānukūlyasya** (of what helps the worship of Kṛṣṇa) **saṅkalpaḥ** (firm resolve, or acceptance), **prātikūlyasya varjanam** (giving up association with whatever opposes the worship of Kṛṣṇa), (**mām**) **rakṣiṣyati iti viśvāsaḥ** (firm faith, of this kind: “That Bhagavān, Lord of the three worlds, will arrange our welfare” and so on), **goptṛtve** (as master, as maintainer, or as husband) **varaṇam** (praying, or accepting, of the kind described in the *Nṛsiṁha Purāṇa*: “‘I take shelter of You, the God of gods, Janārdana’ — whoever has come to Me for shelter in this way, I deliver from suffering”), **ātma-nikṣepa-kārpaṇye** (offering the self, of the kind described in the *Gautamīya-tantra*: “As I am directed by some God who dwells in the heart, so I act”; and humility, which reveals one's own lowliness, of this kind: “No one is more supremely merciful than You, and no one is more supremely pitiable than I,” and so on — that is, plaintive prayer) — **iti ṣaḍ-vidhā śaraṇāgatiḥ** (taking shelter).

In the *Bhakti-sandarbha* (section 236) Śrī Jīva Prabhu writes: “It is sixfold when divided into limbs and the whole (*aṅga* and *aṅgī*). Of these, only ‘accepting Him as protector’ is the whole, since it has the same meaning as the word *śaraṇāgati*. The others are limbs, because they accompany it. \* \* \* Thus, for one whose shelter is complete with all its limbs, it bears its full fruit at once; for others, it does so according to what they possess and in due order. This shelter is praised (in the verse *Bhāgavatam* 11.19.9), where it is said that He removes all the sufferings of those who have taken shelter of Him and showers His own sweetness on them from every side.”
