## 74–75

> Subheading: The description of five more, higher qualities; in the foremost *jīvas*, such as Rudra, these fifty-five qualities are eternally present in part, and in Viṣṇu in full — (*Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, *Dakṣiṇa-vibhāga*, *Vibhāva-laharī*, 37–44)

**atha** **giriśādiṣu** (in Śiva and others) **ye** **pañca-guṇāḥ** **aṁśena** (not in full) **syuḥ** (are present — they are named) — **sadā-svarūpa-samprāptaḥ** (possessed of a consciousness that *māyā* cannot overpower) **sarva-jñaḥ** (knowing all, that is, knowing the three times — past, future and present) **nitya-nūtanaḥ** (by His sweetness ever new, as if never tasted before) **sac-cid-ānanda-sāndrāṅgaḥ** (whose form is a concentrated embodiment of *sac-cid-ānanda*) **sarva-siddhi-niṣevitaḥ** (whose feet are worshiped by all attainable fruits) **sva-vaśākhila-siddhiḥ syāt** (in a variant reading the last two quarters, beginning with “sva-vaśa…,” are absent).
