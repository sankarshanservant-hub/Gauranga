# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 6

## 0

**Summary.** Here the true nature and the glory of Śrīmad Advaita Ācārya Prabhu are established by examining two verses. *Māyā* has two functions (*vṛttis*): *nimitta* and *upādāna*. The *puruṣa-avatāra* seen in *prakṛti* as the efficient cause is called “Mahā-Viṣṇu.” And Mahā-Viṣṇu's second form, present in *pradhāna*, the material cause, is “Advaita.” This Advaita took on a special role as agent in the creation of the world and so on; He also accepted the mood of a devotee and taught *bhakti* to the world. To say that He is Caitanya's servant only adds to His glory, for in no *rasa* can one taste Kṛṣṇa's sweetness without the mood of a servant (*dāsya-bhāva*) contained within each of them.

## 1

I bow down to Śrīmad Advaitācārya, whose deeds are wondrous; by His mercy even an ignorant person can describe His true nature.

## 4–5

Mahā-Viṣṇu, who creates this world by *māyā*, is the Creator of the world; the Lord Advaitācārya is His avatāra. He is called “Advaita” because He is a *tattva* nondifferent from Hari, and “Ācārya” because He teaches *bhakti*. I take shelter of this Lord Advaitācārya, the avatāra in the form of a devotee.

## 10

One and the same *māyā* is called “*pradhāna*” in its material portion (*upādāna*) and “*māyā*” in its efficient portion (*nimitta*). In keeping with these two functions of *māyā*, Mahā-Viṣṇu abides in two forms. In one form Mahā-Viṣṇu abides in *prakṛti* as the efficient cause of the world — this is the form of “Viṣṇu.” In His second form He abides in *pradhāna* and, in the form of Rudra, is called “Advaita.” Advaita is therefore not in the least different from the Puruṣa; only Their bodies differ.

## 15

*Prakṛti* has two divisions, “*nimitta*” and “*upādāna*.” In the same way the Puruṣa creates the universe by taking two forms: as the *nimitta*, the form of “Mahā-Viṣṇu,” and as the *upādāna*, the form of “Advaita.”

## 22

See Ādi 2.30.

## 39

Advaita Prabhu was a disciple of Mādhavendra Purī, and His godbrother Īśvara Purī was Mahāprabhu's guru. Because of this relationship Mahāprabhu regards Ācārya Gosāñi as His “guru.” In truth Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi is the Lord of all, and Advaita Ācārya Prabhu is His servant. In this relationship Advaita Prabhu regarded Himself as a “servant.”

## 43

*Brahma-sukha* (the happiness of Brahman) is the happiness that comes with the thought of nondifference: “I am Brahman.”[^43-1]

## 47

*Āgala* means foremost, the first.

## 52

*Guru*: the elders, who abide in the *rasa* of parental love (*vātsalya*); *sama*: the equals (who abide in the *rasa* of friendship, *sakhya*); *laghu*: the younger ones. Love for Kṛṣṇa gives the mood of a servant to all three. Therefore all of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya's elders, equals and juniors are His servants.

## 58

O Uddhava, even if Kṛṣṇa is regarded as Īśvara, let my mind still rest in that Kṛṣṇa.

## 59–60

Nanda said: “O Uddhava, may all the workings of our minds take shelter of Śrī Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet, may our words sing His names, and may our bodies bow down to Him. Whatever our condition may be by the will of Īśvara, according to the fruits of our karma, may charity and other auspicious deeds increase our love (*rati*) for Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Person.”

## 61

*Sakhya* is of two kinds: “with awareness of majesty” and *kevala*, that is, “unmixed,” *sakhya*. Śrīdāmā and the other friends in Vraja have *kevala sakhya*: they do not know Kṛṣṇa's majesty.

## 63

When Kṛṣṇa lay down, one friend began to massage His feet, and another, in a mood of pure friendship, to fan Him with a fan made of leaves.

## 66

O You who destroy the sorrows of Vraja! O supreme hero among women! O You whose gentle smile dispels the pride of Your own people![^66-1] O friend, we are Your maidservants: show us Your lotus face.

## 67

How sad that our *ārya-putra* now lives in the city of Mathurā! O Uddhava, does He remember the home of His father Nanda and His cowherd friends? Does He ever speak of us, His maidservants? Ah, will He ever again place on our heads His hand, fragrant like *aguru*?

## 70

O Lord! O lover! O dearest! O mighty-armed one! I am Your most wretched maidservant: bring me near to You!

## 72

I was performing austerities, longing to touch Śrī Kṛṣṇa's feet. In His mercy Kṛṣṇa came with His friend and took my hand in marriage. Since then I have been His maidservant who sweeps His house.

## 73

Through many austerities, giving up all attachment, we have attained the position of maidservants in the house of this Person, who delights in Himself (*ātmārāma*).

## 76

The ten bodies are the umbrella, sandals, bed, pillow, garments, ornaments, pleasure garden, dwelling, sacred thread and throne.

## 80

Whatever mood [toward Kṛṣṇa] one may take up, one must certainly acknowledge the mood of a servant (*dāsya*) contained within every mood.

## 100

O Uddhava! Neither Brahmā, nor Saṅkarṣaṇa, nor Lakṣmī, nor even I Myself am as dear to Me as you, My devotee, are dear to Me.

## 101

One who regards himself as Kṛṣṇa's equal does not taste His sweetness.

*Thus ends the sixth chapter of the “Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya.”*

[^43-1]: The verse translation reads differently: “the happiness of abiding in the effulgence of Brahman.”
[^66-1]: The edition reads *nija-jana-sandoha (garva)* (“of the multitude of His own people (pride)”); the verse has *smaya* (pride). Translated according to the verse.
