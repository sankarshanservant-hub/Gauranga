## 107

Śrī Gaurahari, through the conduct of a *siddha*, displays the highest pinnacle of *vipralambha-rasa*, the *rasa* of separation. Those who hold to knowledge born of the senses therefore take His direct experiences for raving and for actions full of delusion. Foolish people devoted to the senses are always bound by the troubles of the external world; so the Object of service, in His spiritual form, is not drawn toward them. They think that just as the visible world makes them the center of enjoyment, so the transcendental acts of Śrī Gaurasundara must also belong to that world! Under the pretense of following, the doctrine called “Nadīyā-nāgarī,” a perverted and wicked view that seeks to gratify the senses, is not the path shown by Śrī Gaurasundara and His followers. The adherents of this doctrine are materialists in search of enjoyment (*jaḍa-bhoga-vādīs*), and therefore enemies of Viṣṇu.

## 108

Śrī Kṛṣṇa sent His friend Uddhava to Gokula, wishing to ease somewhat the longing of the *gopīs* and of His father and mother. On seeing Uddhava, Kṛṣṇa's friend, Śrīmatī Rādhikā expressed in many ways a hidden anger, full of many emotions and revealing Her intense, ultimate longing (*utkaṇṭhā*). Fully absorbed in Śrī Rādhā, Śrī Gaurasundara stayed intoxicated day and night with this *māthura-bhāva*, the mood of separation while Kṛṣṇa is in Mathurā. This is *citra-jalpa-bhāva*, the state of “varied talk.” The *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi* says: “When, on seeing the beloved's friend, [the heroine] utters talk full of many emotions, swelling with hidden anger and ending in intense longing, that is *jalpa*.” For one who has taken shelter at the feet of Śrī Gaura, worship of Kṛṣṇa is precisely this long separation (*vipralambha*). Abundant separation is the cause of union (*sambhoga*). Not understanding this, many take union to be the very essence and fail to grasp the one thing needed: in the life of the *sādhaka* and of the *siddha* alike, the *rasa* of separation must be kindled.

## 165

> Subheading: The definition of *kāma* and *prema* —

“When the bond of feeling between a young couple is in no way destroyed, even when there is cause for its destruction, it is called *prema*.” When a cause for separation arises and yet the firm bond of feeling between two lovers is destroyed by nothing — that is called *prema*. The *gopīs*, the *āśraya* of love, are bound to Kṛṣṇa by a firm bond of feeling, exclusively and with their whole being. They are the model of renouncing one's own happiness, that is, *kāma*, and are intent only on the service that delights Kṛṣṇa. Thus one sees how, sacrificing their own happiness for Kṛṣṇa, they feel immense joy, and how firm the bond of their feeling is.

## 217

Śrī Rādhikā is Śrī Kṛṣṇa's all in all. The other *gopīs* are only what serves the *rasa*: they increase the relish of Śrī Kṛṣṇa's pastimes with Rādhā.

“The lovely-browed girls whose playful movements attract Mādhava from every side are the friends of the queen of Vṛndāvana; they are held to be of five kinds: *sakhīs*, *nitya-sakhīs*, *prāṇa-sakhīs*, *priya-sakhīs* and the celebrated *parama-preṣṭha-sakhīs*… Though their love for the Two reaches the highest limit, sometimes it seems greater for the One, sometimes for the Other… A *sakhī* fully unfolds the loving pastimes [of the Divine Couple].”

The lovely *gopīs*, able to attract Śrī Kṛṣṇa completely by actions born of eager loving desire, are the friends of Śrī Rādhikā, the queen of Vṛndāvana, and are of five kinds: *sakhī*, *nitya-sakhī*, *prāṇa-sakhī*, *priya-sakhī* and *parama-preṣṭha-sakhī*. Out of supreme love the eight *parama-preṣṭha-sakhīs* sometimes take Śrī Kṛṣṇa's side during a lovers' quarrel (*māna*), and sometimes, when Rādhā is *khaṇḍitā* (offended), they take Śrī Rādhā's side. Showing affection to one and opposition to the other, they increase the *rasa*.
