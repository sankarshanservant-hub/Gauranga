# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Madhya-līlā 18

## 0

**Summary.** At Āriṭ-grāma Mahāprabhu discovered Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Śyāma-kuṇḍa, and then at Govardhana He had *darśana* of Harideva. The Lord would not climb Govardhana to see Gopāla, and so Gopāla, on the pretext (*chala*) of fear of the *mlecchas*, came down from Annakūṭa-grāma to the village of Gāṭholī. Mahāprabhu went there and had His *darśana*. Long afterwards, to mercifully give *darśana* to the best of devotees, Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī, Gopāla came to Mathurā and stayed “for a month” in the temple of Viṭhṭhaleśvara; Kavirāja Gosvāmī tells of this here, in passing. Mahāprabhu saw Nandīśvara, Pāvana-sarovara, Śeṣaśāyī, Khelā-tīrtha, Bhāṇḍīravana, Bhadravana, Lohavana, Mahāvana and other places, and after seeing Gokula He returned to Mathurā. He took up lodging at Akrūra-ghāṭa and every day went to Vṛndāvana, visiting Kālīya-hrada, Dvādaśāditya-ghāṭa, Keśī-ghāṭa, Rāsa-sthalī, Cīra-ghāṭa, Āmli-talā and other places. At night a fisherman used to catch fish in Kālīya-hrada, and many people took him for “Kṛṣṇa” and came looking for him. But when they saw Mahāprabhu, their mistaken notion (*vivarta-buddhi*) was dispelled, and Kṛṣṇa was revealed to them all. Then the Lord established that a *sannyāsī*, that is, a *jīva*, is a particle of spirit (*cit-kaṇa*). At Akrūra-ghāṭa the Lord would stay submerged in the water for a long time, and so Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya decided to take Mahāprabhu from Vraja-maṇḍala to Prayāga. Resolving, “We shall bathe in the Gaṅgā at Soro-kṣetra and then go to Prayāga,” they set out. On the way, in a certain village, Bijulī Khān, who was riding with Paṭhān horsemen, saw the Lord lying unconscious in ecstatic love. He said, “His companions have drugged Him with *dhuturā*, killed Him, and are taking His wealth,” and he had the Lord's companions bound. When the Lord's ecstasy subsided, He conversed with a Muslim teacher (*mleccha-ācārya*) of Bijulī Khān's band and examined the scriptures with him; in this way the Lord established “devotion to Kṛṣṇa” from the Koran itself. Bijulī Khān and the horsemen under him took shelter at Mahāprabhu's feet and became “devotees of Kṛṣṇa.” At that place there still stands a village known as “the village of the Paṭhān Vaiṣṇavas.” After bathing in the Gaṅgā at Soro, the Lord reached Triveṇī.

## 1

In Vṛndāvana, Gaurāṅgacandra gave His *darśana* to all beings, moving and unmoving, and so delighted them; and seeing them, He Himself felt joy. In this way He wandered everywhere.

## 3–5

Āriṭ-grāma is the place where the demon Ariṣṭa was killed. Arriving there, the Lord asked, “Where is Rādhā-kuṇḍa?” But no one could tell Him, and the brāhmaṇa who accompanied Him did not know either. Then the omniscient Bhagavān understood that this *tīrtha* had been “lost” (*lupta*), and He bathed in the little water standing in two rice fields nearby. This indicated that those rice fields were Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Śyāma-kuṇḍa.

## 12

By its own qualities this lake, Rādhā-kuṇḍa, is as supremely dear to Kṛṣṇa as Śrī Rādhā. In this *kuṇḍa* Śrī Kṛṣṇacandra always sports with Śrī Rādhā with great love. Whoever bathes in this *kuṇḍa* even once attains love for Kṛṣṇa like that of Śrī Rādhikā. Who in this world, then, can describe the glory and sweetness of Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa?

## 15

*Sumanaḥ-sarovara* is Kusuma-sarovara.

## 21

*Pāka* means cooking food (*anna-pāka*).

## 25

Gauracandra had vowed, “I will not climb the hill of Govardhana,” and He thought of Himself as a devotee of Kṛṣṇa. So Gopāla Himself came down from Govardhana and gave Him *darśana*.

## 27

*Turuka*: a particular kind of Muslim troops (Turks or Paṭhāns).

## 34

This hill, Govardhana, is the foremost of Vaiṣṇavas, because, thrilled by the bliss of the touch of Rāma's and Kṛṣṇa's feet, he regales Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa[^34-1] together with the cows and cowherd boys: he gives water for the cowherds to drink and food — grass, bulbs, roots and the like.

## 38

May you be protected by the left arm of lotus-eyed Śrī Kṛṣṇa, with which He lifted Govardhana, the king of hills, and played with it like a ball.

---

[^34-1]: So in the edition (checked against the scan); in the verse, and earlier in this same sentence, it is Rāma and Kṛṣṇa.
