# Anubhāṣya on Antya-līlā 11

## 1

> Subheading: Homage to Gaura, who danced holding Haridāsa's body in His arms —

**yaḥ** (Caitanyadeva) **yan-mūrtiṁ** (whose body, that is, Haridāsa's body) **saṁsthitām** (which had entered *samādhi*) **api** **svāṅke** (in His own arms) **kṛtvā** **nanarta**, **taṁ** **haridāsaṁ** **tat-prabhuṁ** **taṁ** **caitanyaṁ** **ca** **namāmi**.

## 5

*Gaura-deha* — He whose body shines with a golden hue.[^5-1]

## 7

*Caitanyera ārya* — the one whom Mahāprabhu honors.

## 10

> Subheading: The author's humble words; to purify himself he describes Caitanya's qualities and pastimes —

## 11

> Subheading: The Lord enjoys *kīrtana* with His devotees in Nīlācala —

## 12

> Subheading: By day, *nāma-saṅkīrtana* and *darśana* of Jagannātha; by night, together with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, the Lord relishes the *rasa* of Śrī Rādhā's love for Kṛṣṇa —

## 13

> Subheading: In separation from Kṛṣṇa the *sāttvika-bhāvas* arise in the Lord's body —

## 15

> Subheading: The Lord's two constant companions in His transcendental pastimes of separation (*vipralambha*) —

## 16

> Subheading: The story of Haridāsa; Govinda goes to bring *prasāda* to Haridāsa —

## 17

> Subheading: Haridāsa Ṭhākura's condition at the time of his departure —

## 18

> Subheading: Govinda urges him to accept *prasāda*; Haridāsa wishes to fast —

## 19

> Subheading: Haridāsa shows how a *sādhaka* who has taken shelter of the holy name should rightly honor *prasāda* —

## 21

> Subheading: One day the Lord comes to Haridāsa and asks whether all is well with him —

## 22

> Subheading: Haridāsa's humble words —

## 23

> Subheading: Answering the Lord's question, Haridāsa tells of his sorrow: he cannot chant the name by count (*saṅkhyā-nāma*) —

Here too we learn that every *sādhaka* who has taken shelter of the holy name has only one rule to keep: following Ṭhākura Haridāsa, to chant aloud the “Hare Kṛṣṇa” *mahā-mantra* (sixteen names, thirty-two syllables), taking up a fixed number and holding firmly to the vow. See Antya 3.99, 113–115, 120, 123–124, 129, 175, 223, 227, 238–242 and other verses.

## 24

> Subheading: The Lord tells Haridāsa, whose body is transcendental and perfect, to reduce the practice he merely enacts (*sādhanābhinaya*) —

## 25

> Subheading: The Lord's own words: “Haridāsa descended as the ācārya and preacher of the holy name” —

“Your descent” (*tomāra avatāra*): by the Lord's will His devotees and associates descend to earth to serve Him.

## 27

> Subheading: Haridāsa's humble words, which would melt a stone, and his glorification of the Lord's greatness —

## 30

*Śrāddha-pātra* — the plate of the *śrāddha* feast. The *Viṣṇu-smṛti* says: “These are called the lowest of brāhmaṇas (*brāhmaṇāpasada*), defilers of the row (*paṅkti-dūṣaka*). A wise man should carefully avoid them at the *śrāddha* rite.” Even if one is born a brāhmaṇa by seed (*śaukra-brāhmaṇa*), the *śrāddha* plate is not to be given to a brāhmaṇa whom the *smṛti* calls an *apasada*, a defiler of the row. Here, however, the *śrāddha* plate due to a pure brāhmaṇa was given to Haridāsa, a brāhmaṇa by initiation (*daikṣa-vipra*). Though he was born in a *mleccha* family, he has the right to it, for he is a “servant of Hari” (*hari-jana*).

## 31

> Subheading: Haridāsa makes his wish known to the Lord —

## 32

> Subheading: Haridāsa wishes to conclude his pastimes even before the Lord's departure —

## 33

> Subheading: Haridāsa reveals his wish to depart, together with his longing to find happiness in serving Gaura-Kṛṣṇa with body, mind and speech —

## 37

> Subheading: The Lord fulfills Haridāsa's desire —

## 38

> Subheading: Remembering the separation from an associate of His pastimes, the Lord speaks words full of sorrow that touch the heart deeply —

## 39

> Subheading: Haridāsa begs the Lord for mercy without guile —

## 40

> Subheading: Humble words again —

## 42

> Subheading: Before the Lord, who loves His devotees, Haridāsa calls himself a mere semblance of His servant and firmly hopes that his desire will be fulfilled —

## 43

> Subheading: The Lord leaves, assuring him that He will come the next day —

## 45

> Subheading: The next morning, after seeing Jagannātha, the Lord comes with His devotees to see Haridāsa —

---

[^5-1]: In the verse translation, otherwise: “Glory to the spiritual body of Śrī Caitanya. He is Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Lord Himself.” The *Anubhāṣya* takes *gaura-deha* as an epithet of Kṛṣṇa: Kṛṣṇa whose body shines with a golden hue.
