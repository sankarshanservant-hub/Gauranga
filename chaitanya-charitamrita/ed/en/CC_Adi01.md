“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Ādi-līlā  
Chapter 1  
**The Supreme Principle of the Guru**  
(translated from the Bengali for this edition)

বন্দে গুরূনীশভক্তানীশমীশাবতারকান্ ৷  
তৎপ্রকাশাংশ্চ তচ্চক্তীঃ কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যসংজ্ঞকম্ ॥ ১ ॥

vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān  
I worship  the gurus  the Lord's devotees  
īśam īśāvatārakān  
the Lord  the Lord's avatāras  
tat-prakāśāṁś ca tac-caktīḥ  
His manifestations  and  His potencies  
kṛṣṇa-caitanya-saṁjñakam  
Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  known as

(1) I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, the Lord’s avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies, and the Lord Himself, who is known as Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যনিত্যানন্দৌ সহোদিতৌ ৷  
গৌড়োদয়ে পুষ্পবন্তৌ চিত্রৌ শন্দৌ তমোনুদৌ ॥ ২ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
nityānandau sahoditau  
(and) Nityānanda  risen together  
gauḍodaye puṣpavantau  
on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and the moon  
citrau śan-dau tamo-nudau  
wondrous  bestowing auspiciousness  dispelling darkness

(2) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda, who have risen together like the sun and the moon on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa. Wondrous and bestowing auspiciousness, They dispel the darkness.

যদদ্বৈতং ব্রহ্মোপনিষদি তদপ্যস্য তনুভা  
য আত্মান্তর্য্যামী পুরুষ ইতি সোঽস্যাংশবিভবঃ ৷  
ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যৈঃ পূর্ণো য ইহ ভগবান্ স স্বয়ময়ং  
ন চৈতন্যাৎ কৃষ্ণাজ্জগতি পরতত্ত্বং পরমিহ ॥ ৩ ॥

yad advaitaṁ brahmopaniṣadi tad apy asya tanu-bhā  
that which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  too  His  the radiance of the body  
ya ātmāntaryāmī puruṣa iti so ’syāṁśa-vibhavaḥ  
who  the Self dwelling within  the Puruṣa  thus (called)  he  His  plenary expansion  
ṣaḍ-aiśvaryaiḥ pūrṇo ya iha bhagavān sa svayam ayaṁ  
with the six opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one  
na caitanyāt kṛṣṇāj jagati para-tattvaṁ param iha  
not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  Supreme Truth  higher  here

(3) What the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is only the radiance of His body. The one called the Puruṣa, the Supersoul dwelling within all, is His plenary expansion. And Bhagavān, full of the six opulences, is He Himself. In this world there is no Supreme Truth higher than Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa.

অনর্পিতচরীং চিরাৎ করুণয়াবতীর্ণঃ কলৌ  
সমর্পয়িতুমুন্নতোজ্জ্বলরসাং স্বভক্তিশ্রিয়ম্ ৷  
হরিঃ পুরটসুন্দরদ্যুতিকদম্বসন্দীপিতঃ  
সদা হৃদয়কন্দরে স্ফুরতু বঃ শচীনন্দনঃ ॥ ৪ ॥

anarpita-carīṁ cirāt karuṇayāvatīrṇaḥ kalau  
not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in the age of Kali  
samarpayitum unnatojjvala-rasāṁ sva-bhakti-śriyam  
to bestow  most exalted radiant rasa  the treasure of devotion to Himself  
hariḥ puraṭa-sundara-dyuti-kadamba-sandīpitaḥ  
Hari  than gold  more beautiful  of radiance  by a mass  illumined  
sadā hṛdaya-kandare sphuratu vaḥ śacī-nandanaḥ  
always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  the son of Śacī

(4) “Out of mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a long time: the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted and radiant *rasa* of divine love. May that Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, resplendent with a mass of radiance more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the cave of your heart.” (Vidagdha-mādhava 1.2)

রাধা কৃষ্ণপ্রণয়বিকৃতির্হ্লাদিনীশক্তিরস্মা-  
দেকাত্মানাবপি ভুবি পুরা দেহভেদং গতৌ তৌ ৷  
চৈতন্যাখ্যং প্রকটমধুনা তদ্দ্বয়ং চৈক্যমাপ্তং  
রাধাভাবদ্যুতিসুবলিতং নৌমি কৃষ্ণস্বরূপম্ ॥ ৫ ॥

rādhā kṛṣṇa-praṇaya-vikṛtir hlādinī śaktir asmād  
Rādhā  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the transformation  pleasure-giving  potency  therefore  
ekātmānāv api bhuvi purā deha-bhedaṁ gatau tau  
one in soul  although  on earth  long ago  separate bodies  assumed  They two  
caitanyākhyaṁ prakaṭam adhunā tad-dvayaṁ caikyam āptaṁ  
called Caitanya  manifest  now  those two  and  oneness  attained  
rādhā-bhāva-dyuti-suvalitaṁ naumi kṛṣṇa-svarūpam  
with Rādhā's emotion and radiance  adorned  I bow to  Kṛṣṇa Himself

(5) “Rādhā is the transformation of love for Kṛṣṇa; She is His pleasure-giving potency (*hlādinī śakti*). Therefore, although Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in soul, long ago They assumed separate forms on earth. Now the two have become one and appeared as Caitanya. I bow to Him, who is Kṛṣṇa Himself, adorned with Rādhā’s emotion and radiance.” (Svarūpa Dāmodara’s diary)

শ্রীরাধায়াঃ প্রণয়মহিমা কীদৃশো বানয়ৈবা-  
স্বাদ্যো যেনাদ্ভুতমধুরিমা কীদৃশো বা মদীয়ঃ ৷  
সৌখ্যঞ্চাস্যা মদনুভবতঃ কীদৃশং বেতি লোভা-  
ত্তদ্ভাবাঢ়্যঃ সমজনি শচীগর্ভসিন্ধৌ হরীন্দুঃ ॥ ৬ ॥

śrī-rādhāyāḥ praṇaya-mahimā kīdṛśo vānayaivā-  
of Śrī Rādhā  of love  the greatness  of what kind  or  by Her alone  
svādyo yenādbhuta-madhurimā kīdṛśo vā madīyaḥ  
relished  by which (love)  wondrous sweetness  of what kind  or  Mine  
saukhyaṁ cāsyā mad-anubhavataḥ kīdṛśaṁ veti lobhāt  
the happiness  and  Her  from realizing Me  of what kind  or  thus  out of longing  
tad-bhāvāḍhyaḥ samajani śacī-garbha-sindhau harīnduḥ  
rich with Her emotion  was born  in the ocean of Śacī's womb  the moon Hari

(6) “How great is Śrī Rādhā’s love? What is My own wondrous sweetness, which She alone relishes through that love? And what happiness does She feel when She realizes Me?” Longing to know this, the moon Hari, rich with Her emotion, was born from the ocean of Śacī’s womb.

সঙ্কর্ষণঃ কারণতোয়শায়ী  
গর্ভোদশায়ী চ পয়োঽব্ধিশায়ী ৷  
শেষশ্চ যস্যাংশকলাঃ স নিত্যা-  
নন্দাখ্যরামঃ শরণং মমাস্তু ॥ ৭ ॥

saṅkarṣaṇaḥ kāraṇa-toya-śāyī  
Saṅkarṣaṇa  lying in the Causal Ocean  
garbhoda-śāyī ca payo’bdhi-śāyī  
lying in the Garbha Ocean  and  lying in the Ocean of Milk  
śeṣaś ca yasyāṁśa-kalāḥ sa nityā-  
Śeṣa  and  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-  
nandākhya-rāmaḥ śaraṇaṁ mamāstu  
-nanda called  Rāma  shelter  my  may be

(7) Saṅkarṣaṇa, Kāraṇodakaśāyī, Garbhodakaśāyī, Kṣīrodakaśāyī and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions. May that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my shelter.

মায়াতীতে ব্যাপিবৈকুণ্ঠলোকে  
পূর্ণৈশ্বর্য্যে শ্রীচতুর্ব্যূহমধ্যে ৷  
রূপং যস্যোদ্ভাতি সঙ্কর্ষণাখ্যং  
তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৮ ॥

māyātīte vyāpi-vaikuṇṭha-loke  
beyond māyā  in the all-pervading realm of Vaikuṇṭha  
pūrṇaiśvarye śrī-catur-vyūha-madhye  
full of opulence  among the divine four vyūhas  
rūpaṁ yasyodbhāti saṅkarṣaṇākhyaṁ  
the form  whose  shines forth  called Saṅkarṣaṇa  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(8) Beyond māyā lies the all-pervading realm of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence. There, among the four divine vyūhas, His form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines forth. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.

মায়াভর্ত্তাজাণ্ডসঙ্ঘাশ্রয়াঙ্গঃ  
শেতে সাক্ষাৎ কারণাম্ভোধিমধ্যে ৷  
যস্যৈকাংশঃ শ্রীপুমানদিদেব-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ৯ ॥

māyā-bhartājāṇḍa-saṅghāśrayāṅgaḥ  
the master of māyā  whose body shelters the multitude of universes  
śete sākṣāt kāraṇāmbhodhi-madhye  
lies  in person  in the midst of the Causal Ocean  
yasyaikāṁśaḥ śrī-pumān ādi-devas  
whose  one portion  the blessed Puruṣa  the original Lord  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(9) The blessed Puruṣa, the original Lord and the master of māyā, whose body shelters countless universes, lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean. He is one portion of Nityānanda. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.

যস্যাংশাংশঃ শ্রীল-গর্ভোদশায়ী  
যন্নাভ্যব্জং লোকসঙ্ঘাতনালম্ ৷  
লোকস্রষ্টুঃ সূতিকাধামধাতু-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১০ ॥

yasyāṁśāṁśaḥ śrīla-garbhoda-śāyī  
whose  portion of a portion  the blessed Garbhodakaśāyī  
yan-nābhy-abjaṁ loka-saṅghāta-nālam  
from whose navel the lotus  of the multitude of worlds  the stem  
loka-sraṣṭuḥ sūtikā-dhāma dhātus  
of the creator of the worlds  the birthplace  of Brahmā  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(10) “The blessed Garbhodakaśāyī is a portion of His portion. The lotus from His navel has a stem that holds the multitude of worlds, and it is the birthplace of Brahmā, the creator of the worlds. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.”

যস্যাংশাংশাংশঃ পরাত্মাখিলানাং  
পোষ্টা বিষ্ণুর্ভাতি দুগ্ধাব্ধিশায়ী ৷  
ক্ষৌণীভর্ত্তা যৎকলা সোঽপ্যনন্ত-  
স্তং শ্রীনিত্যানন্দরামং প্রপদ্যে ॥ ১১ ॥

yasyāṁśāṁśāṁśaḥ parātmākhilānāṁ  
whose  portion of a portion of a portion  the Supersoul  of all beings  
poṣṭā viṣṇur bhāti dugdhābdhi-śāyī  
the maintainer  Viṣṇu  shines  lying in the Ocean of Milk  
kṣauṇī-bhartā yat-kalā so ’py anantas  
the upholder of the earth  whose part  he  also  Ananta  
taṁ śrī-nityānanda-rāmaṁ prapadye  
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender

(11) “Viṣṇu who lies in the Ocean of Milk, the Supersoul and maintainer of all beings, is a portion of a portion of His portion. Ananta, who upholds the earth, is also His part. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.”

মহাবিষ্ণুর্জগৎকর্ত্তা মায়য়া যঃ সৃজত্যদঃ ৷  
তস্যাবতার এবায়মদ্বৈতাচার্য্য ঈশ্বরঃ ॥ ১২ ॥

mahā-viṣṇur jagat-kartā  
Mahā-Viṣṇu  the creator of the world  
māyayā yaḥ sṛjaty adaḥ  
through māyā  who  creates  this (world)  
tasyāvatāra evāyam  
His  avatāra  indeed  this  
advaitācārya īśvaraḥ  
Advaita Ācārya  the Lord

(12) Mahā-Viṣṇu, the creator of the world, creates this universe through His energy, māyā. The Lord Advaita Ācārya is His very avatāra.

অদ্বৈতং হরিণাদ্বৈতাদচার্য্যং ভক্তিশংসনাৎ ৷  
ভক্তাবতারমীশং তমদ্বৈতাচার্য্যমাশ্রয়ে ॥ ১৩ ॥

advaitaṁ hariṇādvaitād  
(called) Advaita  being non-different from Hari  
ācāryaṁ bhakti-śaṁsanāt  
(called) Ācārya  for proclaiming bhakti  
bhaktāvatāram īśaṁ tam  
the avatāra as a devotee  the Lord  Him  
advaitācāryam āśraye  
Advaita Ācārya  I take shelter of

(13) He is called Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, and Ācārya because He proclaims devotion (*bhakti*). I take shelter of that Lord, Advaita Ācārya, who descended as a devotee.

পঞ্চতত্ত্বাত্মকং কৃষ্ণং ভক্তরূপস্বরূপকম্ ৷  
ভক্তাবতারং ভক্তাখ্যং নমামি ভক্তশক্তিকম্ ॥ ১৪ ॥

pañca-tattvātmakaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ  
comprising the five truths  Kṛṣṇa  
bhakta-rūpa-svarūpakam  
in the form of a devotee  in the identity of a devotee  
bhaktāvatāraṁ bhaktākhyaṁ  
the avatāra as a devotee  the one called a devotee  
namāmi bhakta-śaktikam  
I bow to  the devotional potency

(14) “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who comprises the five truths (Pañca-tattva). He appears in the form of a devotee, in the identity of a devotee, as the avatāra of a devotee, as the one called a devotee, and as the devotional potency.”

জয়তাং সুরতৌ পঙ্গোর্মম মন্দমতের্গতী ৷  
মৎসর্ব্বস্বপদাম্ভোজৌ রাধামদনমোহনৌ ॥ ১৫ ॥

jayatāṁ suratau paṅgor  
all glory  to the most merciful  of the lame  
mama manda-mater gatī  
my  of little intelligence  the refuge  
mat-sarvasva-padāmbhojau  
whose lotus feet are my all in all  
rādhā-madana-mohanau  
Rādhā  and Madana-mohana

(15) All glory to the most merciful Rādhā and Madana-mohana! They are the refuge of me, who am lame and of little intelligence, and Their lotus feet are my all in all.

দীব্যদ্বৃন্দারণ্যকল্পদ্রুমাধঃ-  
শ্রীমদ্রত্নাগারসিংহাসনস্থৌ ৷  
শ্রীশ্রীরাধা-শ্রীলগোবিন্দদেবৌ  
প্রেষ্ঠালীভিঃ সেব্যমানৌ স্মরামি ॥ ১৬ ॥

dīvyad-vṛndāraṇya-kalpa-drumādhaḥ-  
resplendent  in the forest of Vṛndāvana  a wish-fulfilling tree  beneath  
śrīmad-ratnāgāra-siṁhāsana-sthau  
in a splendid  jewelled temple  on a throne  seated  
śrī śrī rādhā-śrīla-govinda-devau  
Śrī Śrī  Rādhā  Śrīla Govinda-deva  
preṣṭhālībhiḥ sevyamānau smarāmi  
by Their dearest companions  being served  I remember

(16) In resplendent Vṛndāvana, beneath a wish-fulfilling tree, Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Śrīla Govinda-deva sit on a throne in a splendid jewelled temple, and Their dearest companions serve Them. I remember Them.

শ্রীমান্ রাসরসারম্ভী বংশীবটতটস্থিতঃ ৷  
কর্ষন্ বেণুস্বনৈর্গোপীর্গোপীনাথঃ শ্রিয়েঽস্তু নঃ ॥ ১৭ ॥

śrīmān rāsa-rasārambhī  
the beautiful  who began the rasa of the rāsa dance  
vaṁśīvaṭa-taṭa-sthitaḥ  
at Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing  
karṣan veṇu-svanair gopīr  
attracting  with the sounds of His flute  the gopīs  
gopī-nāthaḥ śriye ’stu naḥ  
Gopīnātha  for the welfare  may He be  our

(17) Beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha began the sweet *rasa* of the rāsa dance. He stands on the riverbank at Vaṁśīvaṭa and draws the gopīs with the sound of His flute. May He bestow His blessings upon us.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ১৮ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host

(18) All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

এই তিন ঠাকুর গৌড়ীয়াকে করিয়াছেন আত্মসাৎ ৷  
এ তিনের চরণ বন্দোঁ, তিনে মোর নাথ ॥ ১৯ ॥

ei tina ṭhākura gauḍīyāke kariyāchena ātmasāt  
these  three  Deities  the Gauḍīyas  have made  Their own  
e tinera caraṇa vandoṅ, tine mora nātha  
these three's  feet  I worship  the three  my  masters

(19) These three Deities have made the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas Their own. I worship the feet of these three, for these three are my masters.

গ্রন্থের আরম্ভে করি মঙ্গলাচরণ ৷  
গুরু, বৈষ্ণব, ভগবান্ — তিনের স্মরণ ॥ ২০ ॥

granthera ārambhe kari maṅgalācaraṇa  
of the book  at the beginning  I make  the invocation  
guru, vaiṣṇava, bhagavān — tinera smaraṇa  
guru  Vaiṣṇava  Bhagavān  of the three  remembrance

(20) At the beginning of this book I offer the auspicious invocation (*maṅgalācaraṇa*) by remembering three: the guru, the Vaiṣṇavas and the Lord.

তিনের স্মরণে হয় বিঘ্নবিনাশন ৷  
অনায়াসে হয় নিজ বাঞ্চিতপূরণ ॥ ২১ ॥

tinera smaraṇe haya vighna-vināśana  
of the three  in remembering  there is  destruction of obstacles  
anāyāse haya nija vāñcita-pūraṇa  
easily  there is  one's own  fulfilment of desires

(21) Remembering these three destroys every obstacle, and one’s own desires are easily fulfilled.

সে মঙ্গলাচরণ হয় ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
বস্তুনির্দ্দেশ, আশীর্ব্বাদ, নমস্কার ॥ ২২ ॥

se maṅgalācaraṇa haya tri-vidha prakāra  
that  invocation  is  threefold  in kind  
vastu-nirdeśa, āśīrvāda, namaskāra  
defining the subject  blessing  obeisance

(22) That invocation is of three kinds: defining the subject, giving a blessing and offering obeisances.

প্রথম দুই শ্লোকে ইষ্টদেব-নমস্কার ৷  
সামান্য-বিশেষ-রূপে দুই ত’ প্রকার ॥ ২৩ ॥

prathama dui śloke iṣṭa-deva-namaskāra  
in the first  two  verses  obeisance to the worshipful Lord  
sāmānya-viśeṣa-rūpe dui ta’ prakāra  
general and particular  in form  two  indeed  kinds

(23) In the first two verses I offer obeisances to the worshipful Lord. These are of two kinds: general and particular.

তৃতীয় শ্লোকেতে করি বস্তুর নির্দ্দেশ ৷  
যাহা হৈতে জানি পরতত্ত্বের উদ্দেশ ॥ ২৪ ॥

tṛtīya ślokete kari vastura nirdeśa  
in the third  verse  I make  of the subject  the definition  
yāhā haite jāni para-tattvera uddeśa  
which  from  one knows  of the Supreme Truth  the identity

(24) In the third verse I define the subject, and from it one can know the identity of the Supreme Truth.

চতুর্থ শ্লোকেতে করি জগতে আশীর্ব্বাদ ৷  
সর্ব্বত্র মাগিয়ে কৃষ্ণচৈতন্য-প্রসাদ ॥ ২৫ ॥

caturtha ślokete kari jagate āśīrvāda  
in the fourth  verse  I make  to the world  blessing  
sarvatra māgiye kṛṣṇa-caitanya-prasāda  
everywhere  I pray for  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya's  mercy

(25) In the fourth verse I bless the world, praying that the mercy of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya may reach everywhere.

সেই শ্লোকে কহি বাহ্যাবতার-কারণ ৷  
পঞ্চ ষষ্ঠ শ্লোকে কহি মূল-প্রয়োজন ॥ ২৬ ॥

sei śloke kahi bāhyāvatāra-kāraṇa  
in that  verse  I tell  the external reason for the descent  
pañca ṣaṣṭha śloke kahi mūla-prayojana  
in the fifth  sixth  verses  I tell  the root purpose

(26) In that verse I also state the external reason for His descent. In the fifth and sixth verses I state its root purpose.

এই ছয় শ্লোকে কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যের তত্ত্ব ৷  
আর পঞ্চ শ্লোকে নিত্যানন্দের মহত্ত্ব ॥ ২৭ ॥

ei chaya śloke kṛṣṇa caitanyera tattva  
in these  six  verses  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya's  truth  
āra pañca śloke nityānandera mahattva  
then  in five  verses  Nityānanda's  greatness

(27) These six verses describe the truth of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. The next five verses describe the greatness of Nityānanda.

আর দুই শ্লোকে অদ্বৈত-তত্ত্বাখ্যান ৷  
আর এক শ্লোকে পঞ্চতত্ত্বের ব্যাখ্যান ॥ ২৮ ॥

āra dui śloke advaita-tattvākhyāna  
then  in two  verses  an account of Advaita's truth  
āra eka śloke pañca-tattvera vyākhyāna  
then  in one  verse  of the Pañca-tattva  the explanation

(28) The next two verses give an account of the truth of Advaita, and one more verse explains the Pañca-tattva.

এই চৌদ্দশ্লোকে করি মঙ্গলাচরণ ৷  
তহিঁ মধ্যে কহি সব বস্তুনিরূপণ ॥ ২৯ ॥

ei caudda śloke kari maṅgalācaraṇa  
in these  fourteen  verses  I make  the invocation  
tahiṅ madhye kahi saba vastu-nirūpaṇa  
that  within  I tell  all  the description of the subject

(29) With these fourteen verses I offer the invocation, and within them I describe the whole subject.

সব শ্রোতা-বৈষ্ণবেরে করি’ নমস্কার ৷  
এই সব শ্লোকের করি অর্থ-বিচার ॥ ৩০ ॥

saba śrotā-vaiṣṇavere kari’ namaskāra  
to all  the listening Vaiṣṇavas  having offered  obeisances  
ei saba ślokera kari artha-vicāra  
of all these  verses  I make  examination of the meaning

(30) Offering obeisances to all the Vaiṣṇavas who are listening, I shall now examine the meaning of all these verses.

সকল বৈষ্ণব, শুন করি’ একমন ৷  
চৈতন্য-কৃষ্ণের শাস্ত্র য়েমত নিরূপণ ॥ ৩১ ॥

sakala vaiṣṇava, śuna kari’ eka-mana  
all  Vaiṣṇavas  listen  making  one-pointed mind  
caitanya-kṛṣṇera śāstra yemata-nirūpaṇa  
of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the scriptures  as  establish

(31) O Vaiṣṇavas, all of you, listen with undivided attention to how the scriptures establish the truth of Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণ, গুরুদ্বয়, ভক্ত, অবতার, প্রকাশ ৷  
শক্তি, — এই ছয় রূপে করেন বিলাস ॥ ৩২ ॥

kṛṣṇa, gurudvaya, bhakta, avatāra, prakāśa  
Kṛṣṇa  the two kinds of guru  devotees  avatāras  manifestations  
śakti, — ei chaya rūpe karena vilāsa  
potencies  in these  six  forms  He enjoys  pastimes

(32) The Lord enjoys His pastimes in six forms: as Kṛṣṇa Himself, as the two kinds of guru, as the devotees, as the avatāras, as His manifestations and as His potencies.

এই ছয় তত্ত্বের করি চরণ বন্দন ৷  
প্রথমে সামান্যে করি মঙ্গলাচরণ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

ei chaya tattvera kari caraṇa vandana  
of these  six  truths  I make  to the feet  worship  
prathame sāmānye kari maṅgalācaraṇa  
first  in general  I make  the invocation

(33) I worship the feet of these six truths. First I offer the invocation in a general way.

বন্দে গুরূনীশভক্তানীশমীশাবতারকান্ ৷  
তৎপ্রকাশাংশ্চ তচ্চক্তীঃ কৃষ্ণচৈতন্যসংজ্ঞকম্ ॥ ৩৪ ॥

vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān  
I worship  the gurus  the Lord's devotees  
īśam īśāvatārakān  
the Lord  the Lord's avatāras  
tat-prakāśāṁś ca tac-caktīḥ  
His manifestations  and  His potencies  
kṛṣṇa-caitanya-saṁjñakam  
Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  known as

(34) I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, the Lord’s avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies, and the Lord Himself, who is known as Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.

মন্ত্রগুরু আর যত শিক্ষাগুরুগণ ৷  
তাঁহার চরণ আগে করিয়ে বন্দন ॥ ৩৫ ॥

mantra-guru āra yata śikṣā-guru-gaṇa  
the initiating guru  and  all  the instructing gurus  
tāṅhāra caraṇa āge kariye vandana  
their  feet  first  I  worship

(35) First I worship the feet of my initiating guru and of all my instructing gurus.

শ্রীরূপ, সনাতন, ভট্ট রঘুনাথ ৷  
শ্রীজীব, গোপালভট্ট, দাস রঘুনাথ ॥ ৩৬ ॥

śrī-rūpa, sanātana, bhaṭṭa raghunātha  
Śrī Rūpa  Sanātana  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha  
śrī-jīva, gopāla-bhaṭṭa, dāsa raghunātha  
Śrī Jīva  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa  Dāsa  Raghunātha

(36) Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha Dāsa —

এই ছয় গুরু — শিক্ষাগুরু যে আমার ৷  
তাঁ’ সবার পাদপদ্মে কোটি নমস্কার ॥ ৩৭ ॥

ei chaya guru — śikṣā-guru ye āmāra  
these  six  gurus  instructing gurus  who  mine  
tāṅ’ sabāra pāda-padme koṭi namaskāra  
their  all  at the lotus feet  ten million  obeisances

(37) these six are my instructing gurus. I offer ten million obeisances at the lotus feet of them all.

ভগবানের ভক্ত যত শ্রীবাস প্রধান ৷  
তাঁহার চরণপদ্মে সহস্র প্রণাম ॥ ৩৮ ॥

bhagavānera bhakta yata śrīvāsa pradhāna  
of the Lord  devotees  all  Śrīvāsa  the chief  
tāṅhāra caraṇa-padme sahasra praṇāma  
their  at the lotus feet  thousands of  obeisances

(38) Śrīvāsa is the chief of all the devotees of the Lord. I offer thousands of obeisances at their lotus feet.

অদ্বৈত আচার্য্য — প্রভুর অংশ-অবতার ৷  
তাঁর পাদপদ্মে কোটি প্রণতি আমার ॥ ৩৯ ॥

advaita ācārya — prabhura aṁśa-avatāra  
Advaita  Ācārya  Lord Caitanya's  partial avatāra  
tāṅra pāda-padme koṭi praṇati āmāra  
His  at the lotus feet  ten million  obeisances  my

(39) Advaita Ācārya is a partial avatāra of the Lord. I offer ten million obeisances at His lotus feet.

নিত্যানন্দরায় — প্রভুর স্বরূপপ্রকাশ ৷  
তাঁর পাদপদ্ম বন্দো য়াঁর মুঞি দাস ॥ ৪০ ॥

nityānanda-rāya — prabhura svarūpa-prakāśa  
Nityānanda  Rāya  Lord Caitanya's  manifestation of His own form  
tāṅra pāda-padma vando yāṅra muñi dāsa  
His  lotus feet  I worship  whose  I  servant

(40) Nityānanda Rāya is the manifestation of the Lord’s own form (*svarūpa-prakāśa*). I worship the lotus feet of Him whose servant I am.

গদাধর পণ্ডিতাদি — প্রভুর নিজশক্তি ৷  
তাঁ-সবার চরণে মোর সহস্র প্রণতি ॥ ৪১ ॥

gadādhara paṇḍitādi — prabhura nija-śakti  
Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others  Lord Caitanya's  own potencies  
tāṅ-sabāra caraṇe mora sahasra praṇati  
their  all  at the feet  my  thousands of  obeisances

(41) Gadādhara Paṇḍita and the others are the Lord’s own potencies. I offer thousands of obeisances at the feet of them all.

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য প্রভু স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তাঁহার পদারবিন্দে অনন্ত প্রণাম ॥ ৪২ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya prabhu svayaṁ bhagavān  
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  the Lord  Himself  Bhagavān  
tāṅhāra padāravinde ananta praṇāma  
His  at the lotus feet  endless  obeisances

(42) Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu is the Supreme Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*). I offer endless obeisances at His lotus feet.

সাবরণে প্রভুরে করিয়া নমস্কার ৷  
এই ছয় তেঁহো যৈছে — করিয়ে বিচার ॥ ৪৩ ॥

sāvaraṇe prabhure kariyā namaskāra  
with His associates  to the Lord  having made  obeisance  
ei chaya teṅho yaiche — kariye vicāra  
these  six  He  how (is)  I make  consideration

(43) Having offered obeisances to the Lord together with His associates, I shall now consider how He is present in these six.

যদ্যপি আমার গুরু — চৈতন্যের দাস ৷  
তথাপি জানিয়ে আমি তাঁহার প্রকাশ ॥ ৪৪ ॥

yadyapi āmāra guru — caitanyera dāsa  
although  my  guru  Caitanya's  servant  
tathāpi jāniye āmi tāṅhāra prakāśa  
still  know  I  His  manifestation

(44) Although my guru is a servant of Caitanya, I know him to be His manifestation.

গুরু কৃষ্ণরূপ হন শাস্ত্রের প্রমাণে ৷  
গুরুরূপে কৃষ্ণ কৃপা করেন ভক্তগণে ॥ ৪৫ ॥

guru kṛṣṇa-rūpa hana śāstrera pramāṇe  
the guru  Kṛṣṇa's form  is  of the scriptures  by the evidence  
guru-rūpe kṛṣṇa kṛpā karena bhakta-gaṇe  
in the form of the guru  Kṛṣṇa  mercy  shows  to the devotees

(45) The scriptures testify that the guru is a form of Kṛṣṇa. In the form of the guru, Kṛṣṇa shows mercy to His devotees.

আচার্য্যং মাং বিজানীয়ান্নাবমন্যেত কর্হিচিৎ ৷  
ন মর্ত্ত্যবুদ্ধ্যাসূয়েত সর্ব্বদেবময়ো গুরুঃ ॥ ৪৬ ॥

ācāryaṁ māṁ vijānīyān  
the ācārya  as Me  one should know  
nāvamanyeta karhicit  
should not disrespect  ever  
na martya-buddhyāsūyeta  
not  thinking him mortal  should envy  
sarva-deva-mayo guruḥ  
comprising all the gods  the guru

(46) “One should know the ācārya as Me and never disrespect him. One should not envy him, thinking him an ordinary mortal, for the guru embodies all the gods.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.17.27)

শিক্ষাগুরুকে ত’ জানি কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ ৷  
অন্তর্য্যামী, ভক্তশ্রেষ্ঠ — এই দুই রূপ ॥ ৪৭ ॥

śikṣā-guruke ta’ jāni kṛṣṇera svarūpa  
the instructing guru  indeed  I know  Kṛṣṇa's  own form  
antaryāmī, bhakta-śreṣṭha — ei dui rūpa  
the indwelling Lord  the best of devotees  these  two  forms

(47) I know the instructing guru to be Kṛṣṇa’s own form. He appears in two forms: as the indwelling Supersoul and as the best of devotees.

নৈবোপয়ন্ত্যপচিতিং কবয়স্তবেশ  
ব্রহ্মায়ুষাপি কৃতমৃদ্ধমুদঃ স্মরন্তঃ ৷  
যোঽন্তর্বহিস্তনুভৃতামশুভং বিধুন্ব-  
ন্নাচার্য্য-চৈত্ত্যবপুষা স্বগতিং ব্যনক্তি ॥ ৪৮ ॥

naivopayanty apacitiṁ kavayas taveśa  
not at all  attain  repayment  the wise  Your  O Lord  
brahmāyuṣāpi kṛtam ṛddha-mudaḥ smarantaḥ  
even with Brahmā's lifespan  (Your) deeds  with growing joy  remembering  
yo ’ntar bahis tanu-bhṛtām aśubhaṁ vidhunvann  
who  within  without  of embodied beings  misfortune  dispelling  
ācārya-caitya-vapuṣā sva-gatiṁ vyanakti  
in the forms of the ācārya and the Supersoul  the way to Yourself  reveals

(48) “O Lord, even with a lifespan as long as Brahmā’s the wise cannot repay their debt to You, though they remember Your kindness with ever-growing joy. For You dispel the misfortune of embodied beings from within and from without, and in the forms of the ācārya and the Supersoul You reveal the way to Yourself.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.29.6)

তেষাং সততযুক্তানাং ভজতাং প্রীতিপূর্ব্বকম্ ৷  
দদামি বুদ্ধিযোগং তং যেন মামুপয়ান্তি তে ॥ ৪৯ ॥

teṣāṁ satata-yuktānāṁ  
to them  constantly  united (with Me)  
bhajatāṁ prīti-pūrvakam  
who worship (Me)  with love  
dadāmi buddhi-yogaṁ taṁ  
I give  the yoga of understanding  that  
yena mām upayānti te  
by which  to Me  come  they

(49) “To those who are always united with Me and worship Me with love, I give the understanding by which they come to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā 10.10)

যথা ভগবান্ ব্রহ্মণে স্বয়মুপদিশ্যানুভাবিতবান্ ॥ ৫০ ॥

yathā bhagavān brahmaṇe  
as  the Lord  to Brahmā  
svayam upadiśyānubhāvitavān  
Himself  having instructed  made (him) realize

(50) “Just as the Lord Himself instructed Brahmā and made him realize the truth.”

জ্ঞানং পরমগুহ্যং মে যদ্বিজ্ঞান-সমন্বিতম্ ৷  
সরহস্যং তদঙ্গঞ্চ গৃহাণ গদিতং ময়া ॥ ৫১ ॥

jñānaṁ parama-guhyaṁ me  
knowledge  supremely confidential  of Me  
yad vijñāna-samanvitam  
which  with realization  endowed  
sa-rahasyaṁ tad-aṅgaṁ ca  
with its mystery  its auxiliary part  and  
gṛhāṇa gaditaṁ mayā  
accept  spoken  by Me

(51) “Accept from Me the most confidential knowledge of Myself, endowed with realization, together with its mystery and its auxiliary part, as I now speak it.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.9.31)

যাবানহং যথাভাবো যদ্রূপগুণকর্ম্মকঃ ৷  
তথৈব তত্ত্ববিজ্ঞানমস্তু তে মদনুগ্রহাৎ ॥ ৫২ ॥

yāvān ahaṁ yathā-bhāvo  
as great as  I (am)  of whatever nature  
yad-rūpa-guṇa-karmakaḥ  
of whatever form, qualities and deeds  
tathaiva tattva-vijñānam  
exactly so  realization of the truth  
astu te mad-anugrahāt  
may there be  your  by My grace

(52) “By My grace, may you realize in truth exactly how great I am, what My nature is, and what My form, qualities and deeds are.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.9.32)

অহমেবাসমেবাগ্রে নান্যৎ যৎ সদসৎপরম্ ৷  
পশ্চাদহং যদেতচ্চ যোঽবশিষ্যেত সোঽস্ম্যহম্ ॥ ৫৩ ॥

aham evāsam evāgre  
I  alone  was  indeed  in the beginning  
nānyat yat sad-asat-param  
not  anything else  which  effect, cause, or beyond them  
paścād ahaṁ yad etac ca  
afterwards  I  what  this (creation)  and  
yo ‘vaśiṣyeta so ‘smy aham  
what  remains  that  am  I

(53) “Before creation I alone existed; there was nothing else, neither effect nor cause, nor what lies beyond them. After creation I exist, and all this creation too is I. And what remains at the end, that am I.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.9.33)

ঋতেঽর্থং যৎ প্রতীয়েত ন প্রতীয়েত চাত্মনি ৷  
তদ্বিদ্যাদত্মনো মায়াং যথাভাসো যথা তমঃ ॥ ৫৪ ॥

ṛte ’rthaṁ yat pratīyeta  
apart from the reality (Me)  what  may appear  
na pratīyeta cātmani  
does not appear  and  in the Self (Me)  
tad vidyād ātmano māyāṁ  
that  one should know  My  māyā  
yathābhāso yathā tamaḥ  
like a reflection  like  darkness

(54) “Whatever appears apart from Me, the Reality, and whatever does not appear in Me, know that to be My māyā; it is like a reflection and like darkness.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.9.34)

যথা মহান্তি ভূতানি ভূতেষূচ্চাবচেষ্বনু ৷  
প্রবিষ্টান্যপ্রবিষ্টানি তথা তেষু ন তেষ্বহম্ ॥ ৫৫ ॥

yathā mahānti bhūtāni  
as  the great  elements  
bhūteṣūccāvaceṣv anu  
in beings  great and small  after (creation)  
praviṣṭāny apraviṣṭāni  
have entered  have not entered  
tathā teṣu na teṣv aham  
so  in them  not  in them  I

(55) “As the great elements have entered all beings, great and small, and yet have not entered them, so I am within them and yet not within them.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.9.35)

এতাবদেব জিজ্ঞাস্যং তত্ত্বজিজ্ঞাসুনাত্মনঃ ৷  
অন্বয়-ব্যতিরেকাভ্যাং যৎ স্যাৎ সর্ব্বত্র সর্ব্বদা ॥ ৫৬ ॥

etāvad eva jijñāsyaṁ  
this much  only  to be inquired into  
tattva-jijñāsunātmanaḥ  
by one who seeks the truth  of the Supreme Self  
anvaya-vyatirekābhyāṁ  
by direct and indirect means  
yat syāt sarvatra sarvadā  
which  is  everywhere  always

(56) “One who seeks the truth of the Supreme Self should inquire into this alone, by direct and indirect means: that which exists everywhere and always.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.9.36)

চিন্তামণির্জয়তি সোমগিরির্গুরুর্মে  
শিক্ষাগুরুশ্চ ভগবান্ শিখিপিঞ্ছমৌলিঃ ৷  
যৎপাদকল্পতরুপল্লবশেখরেষু  
লীলাস্বয়ম্বররসং লভতে জয়শ্রীঃ ॥ ৫৭ ॥

cintāmaṇir jayati somagirir gurur me  
Cintāmaṇi  glory to  Somagiri  guru  my  
śikṣā-guruś ca bhagavān śikhi-piñcha-mauliḥ  
instructing guru  and  the Lord  whose crown is a peacock feather  
yat-pāda-kalpataru-pallava-śekhareṣu  
whose  feet  of the wish-fulfilling tree  like the new leaves  at the tips  
līlā-svayaṁvara-rasaṁ labhate jayaśrīḥ  
of play  of choosing her beloved  the rasa  obtains  Jayaśrī

(57) “Glory to Cintāmaṇi, and glory to Somagiri, my guru! Glory to my instructing guru, the Lord whose crown bears a peacock feather. At the tips of His feet, which are like the tender leaves of a wish-fulfilling tree, Jayaśrī (Śrī Rādhā) relishes the *rasa* of choosing Him as Her beloved in play.” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta)

জীবে সাক্ষাৎ নাহি তাতে গুরু চৈত্যরূপে ৷  
শিক্ষাগুরু হয় কৃষ্ণ মহান্তস্বরূপে ॥ ৫৮ ॥

jīve sākṣāt nāhi tāte guru caitya-rūpe  
to the soul  directly (visible)  not  therefore  the guru  in the form of the Supersoul  
śikṣā-guru haya kṛṣṇa mahānta-svarūpe  
instructing guru  is  Kṛṣṇa  in the form of a great devotee

(58) As the guru within (*caitya-guru*), the Lord is not directly visible to the soul. Therefore Kṛṣṇa becomes the instructing guru in the form of a great devotee.

ততো দুঃসঙ্গমুৎসৃজ্য সৎসু সজ্জেত বুদ্ধিমান্ ৷  
সন্ত এবাস্য ছিন্দন্তি মনোব্যাসঙ্গমুক্তিভিঃ ॥ ৫৯ ॥

tato duḥsaṅgam utsṛjya  
therefore  bad company  giving up  
satsu sajjeta buddhi-mān  
with the saints  should associate  the intelligent  
santa evāsya chindanti  
the saints  indeed  his  cut away  
mano-vyāsaṅgam uktibhiḥ  
the mind's attachment  with their words

(59) “Therefore an intelligent person should give up bad company and associate with saints. With their words the saints cut away the attachments of his mind.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.26.26)

সতাং প্রসঙ্গান্মম বীর্য্যসংবিদো  
ভবন্তি হৃৎকর্ণরসায়নাঃ কথাঃ ৷  
তজ্জোষণাদাশ্বপবর্গবর্ত্মনি  
শ্রদ্ধা রতির্ভক্তিরনুক্রমিষ্যতি ॥ ৬০ ॥

satāṁ prasaṅgān mama vīrya-saṁvido  
of the saints  through association  My  power  discussions  
bhavanti hṛt-karṇa-rasāyanāḥ kathāḥ  
become  to heart and ear  a delight  the narrations  
taj-joṣaṇād āśv apavarga-vartmani  
by relishing them  quickly  on the path of liberation  
śraddhā ratir bhaktir anukramiṣyati  
faith  rati  devotion  will follow in order

(60) “In the association of saints, discussions of My power become narrations that delight the heart and the ear. By relishing them one quickly advances on the path of liberation: faith, *rati* and devotion follow one after another.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 3.25.25)

ঈশ্বরস্বরূপ ভক্ত তাঁর অধিষ্ঠান ৷  
ভক্তের হৃদয়ে কৃষ্ণের সতত বিশ্রাম ॥ ৬১ ॥

īśvara-svarūpa bhakta tāṅra adhiṣṭhāna  
the Lord's  own form  the devotee  His  abode  
bhaktera hṛdaye kṛṣṇera satata viśrāma  
of the devotee  in the heart  Kṛṣṇa's  constant  resting place

(61) The devotee is the very form of the Lord, for he is the Lord’s abode. Kṛṣṇa always rests in the heart of His devotee.

সাধবো হৃদয়ং মহ্যং সাধূনাং হৃদয়ম্ত্বহম্ ৷  
মদন্যত্তে ন জানন্তি নাহং তেভ্যো মনাগপি ॥ ৬২ ॥

sādhavo hṛdayaṁ mahyaṁ  
the saints  the heart  My  
sādhūnāṁ hṛdayaṁ tv aham  
of the saints  the heart  indeed  I  
mad-anyat te na jānanti  
other than Me  they  do not  know  
nāhaṁ tebhyo manāg api  
not  I  besides them  even slightly

(62) “The saints are My heart, and I am the heart of the saints. They know nothing but Me, and I know nothing but them, not even a little.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 9.4.68)

ভবদ্বিধা ভাগবতাস্তীর্থভূতাঃ স্বয়ং প্রভো ৷  
তীর্থীকুর্ব্বন্তি তীর্থানি স্বান্তঃস্থেন গদাভৃতা ॥ ৬৩ ॥

bhavad-vidhā bhāgavatās  
like you  devotees  
tīrtha-bhūtāḥ svayaṁ prabho  
holy places  yourselves  O lord  
tīrthī-kurvanti tīrthāni  
make holy  the holy places  
svāntaḥ-sthena gadā-bhṛtā  
dwelling in their hearts  by the bearer of the club

(63) “My lord, devotees like you are themselves holy places. Through the Lord who holds the club and dwells in their hearts, they make even the holy places holy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.13.10)

সেই ভক্তগণ হয় দ্বিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
পারিষদ্গণ এক, সাধকগণ আর ॥ ৬৪ ॥

sei bhakta-gaṇa haya dvi-vidha prakāra  
these  devotees  are  two  kinds  
pāriṣad-gaṇa eka, sādhaka-gaṇa āra  
eternal associates  one  practitioners  the other

(64) These devotees are of two kinds: the Lord’s eternal associates and the practitioners (*sādhakas*).

ঈশ্বরের অবতার এ-তিন প্রকার ৷  
অংশ-অবতার, আর গুণ-অবতার ॥ ৬৫ ॥

শক্ত্যাবেশ-অবতার — তৃতীয় এমত ৷  
অংশ-অবতার — পুরুষ-মৎস্যাদিক যত ॥ ৬৬ ॥

īśvarera avatāra e-tina prakāra  
the Lord's  avatāras  these three  kinds  
aṁśa-avatāra, āra guṇa-avatāra  
partial avatāras  and  guṇa-avatāras  
śaktyāveśa-avatāra — tṛtīya e-mata  
empowered avatāras  the third  thus  
aṁśa-avatāra — puruṣa-matsyādika yata  
partial avatāras  the Puruṣas, Matsya and the rest  all

(65–66) The avatāras of the Lord are of three kinds: partial avatāras, guṇa-avatāras, who preside over the modes of nature, and, third, empowered avatāras (*śaktyāveśa*). The partial avatāras are the Puruṣas, Matsya and all the others.

ব্রহ্মা বিষ্ণু শিব — তিন গুণাবতারে গণি ৷  
শক্ত্যবেশ — সনকাদি, পৃথু, ব্যাসমুনি ॥ ৬৭ ॥

brahmā viṣṇu śiva — tina guṇāvatāre gaṇi  
Brahmā  Viṣṇu  Śiva  three  as guṇa-avatāras  I count  
śakty-āveśa — sanakādi, pṛthu, vyāsa-muni  
empowered  Sanaka and the others  Pṛthu  the sage Vyāsa

(67) I count Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva as the three guṇa-avatāras. The empowered avatāras are Sanaka and his brothers, Pṛthu and the sage Vyāsa.

দুইরূপে হয় ভগবানের প্রকাশ ৷  
একে ত’ প্রকাশ হয়, আরে ত’ বিলাস ॥ ৬৮ ॥

dui-rūpe haya bhagavānera prakāśa  
in two forms  is  the Lord's  manifestation  
eke ta’ prakāśa haya, āre ta’ vilāsa  
one  indeed  prakāśa  is  the other  indeed  vilāsa

(68) The Lord manifests Himself in two ways: one is called *prakāśa* (manifestation), the other *vilāsa* (pastime form).

একই বিগ্রহ যদি হয় বহুরূপ ৷  
আকারে ত’ ভেদ নাহি, একই স্বরূপ ॥ ৬৯ ॥

মহিষী-বিবাহে, যৈছে যৈছে কৈল রাস ৷  
ইহাকে কহিয়ে কৃষ্ণের মুখ্য ‘প্রকাশ’ ॥ ৭০ ॥

eka-i vigraha yadi haya bahu-rūpa  
one and the same  form  if  becomes  many forms  
ākāre ta’ bheda nāhi, eka-i svarūpa  
in appearance  indeed  difference  none  one and the same  identity  
mahiṣī-vivāhe, yaiche yaiche kaila rāsa  
in marrying the queens  as  as  He performed  the rāsa dance  
ihāke kahiye kṛṣṇera mukhya ‘prakāśa’  
this  is called  Kṛṣṇa's  principal  prakāśa

(69–70) When one and the same form appears as many, without any difference in appearance and with a single identity, this is called Kṛṣṇa’s principal prakāśa. So He appeared when He married the queens and when He performed the rāsa dance.

চিত্রং বতৈতদেকেন বপুষা যুগপৎ পৃথক্ ৷  
গৃহেষু দ্ব্যষ্টসাহস্রং স্ত্রিয় এক উদাবহৎ ॥ ৭১ ॥

citraṁ bataitad ekena  
wonderful  indeed  this  with one  
vapuṣā yugapat pṛthak  
body  simultaneously  separately  
gṛheṣu dvy-aṣṭa-sāhasraṁ  
in the palaces  twice eight thousand  
striya eka udāvahat  
women  He alone  married

(71) “How wonderful it is that with one body the Lord alone married sixteen thousand women at the same time, each separately in her own palace!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.69.2)

রাসোৎসবঃ সংপ্রবৃত্তো গোপীমণ্ডলমণ্ডিতঃ ৷  
যোগেশ্বরেণ কৃষ্ণেন তাসাং মধ্যে দ্বয়োর্দ্বয়োঃ ॥ ৭২ ॥

rāsotsavaḥ sampravṛtto  
the festival of the rāsa  began  
gopī-maṇḍala-maṇḍitaḥ  
by the circle of gopīs  adorned  
yogeśvareṇa kṛṣṇena  
by the master of yoga  Kṛṣṇa  
tāsāṁ madhye dvayor dvayoḥ  
of them  between  each two  (of two)

(72) “The festival of the rāsa dance began, adorned by the circle of gopīs. Kṛṣṇa, the master of yoga, appeared between each two of them.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.33.3)

প্রবিষ্টেন গৃহীতানাং কণ্ঠে স্বনিকটং স্ত্রিয়ঃ ৷  
যং মন্যেরন্নভস্তাবদ্বিমানশতসঙ্কুলম্ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

দিবৌকসাং সদারাণামৌৎসুক্যাপহৃতাত্মনাম্ ৷  
ততো দুন্দুভয়ো নেদুর্নিপেতুঃ পুষ্পবৃষ্টয়ঃ ॥ ৭৪ ॥

praviṣṭena gṛhītānāṁ  
by (Him) who had entered  of those embraced  
kaṇṭhe sva-nikaṭaṁ striyaḥ  
around the neck  beside herself  the women  
yaṁ manyeran nabhas tāvad  
whom  might think  the sky  then  
vimāna-śata-saṅkulam  
with hundreds of airships  crowded  
divaukasāṁ sa-dārāṇām  
of the gods  with their wives  
autsukya-apahṛtātmanām  
by eagerness  whose minds were carried away  
tato dundubhayo nedur  
then  kettledrums  sounded  
nipetuḥ puṣpa-vṛṣṭayaḥ  
fell  showers of flowers

(73–74) “He entered between them and held each by the neck, so that every gopī thought He was beside her alone. Then the sky was crowded with hundreds of airships of the gods and their wives, whose minds were carried away by eagerness. Kettledrums sounded, and showers of flowers fell.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.33.3–4)

অনেকত্র প্রকটতা রূপস্যৈকস্য যৈকদা ৷  
সর্ব্বথা তৎস্বরূপৈব স প্রকাশ ইতীর্য্যতে ॥ ৭৫ ॥

anekatra prakaṭatā  
in many places  manifestation  
rūpasyaikasya yaikadā  
of a form  of one  which  at one time  
sarvathā tat-svarūpaiva  
in every respect  its  very identity  
sa prakāśa itīryate  
that  prakāśa  is called

(75) “When one form appears in many places at one time and is identical in every respect, it is called a prakāśa.” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta 1.21)

একই বিগ্রহ যদি আকারে হয় আন ৷  
অনেক প্রকাশ হয়, ‘বিলাস’ তার নাম ॥ ৭৬ ॥

eka-i vigraha yadi ākāre haya āna  
one and the same  form  but  in appearance  is  different  
aneka prakāśa haya, ‘vilāsa’ tāra nāma  
many  manifestations  are  vilāsa  its  name

(76) But when one and the same form appears as many manifestations that differ in appearance, this is called vilāsa.

স্বরূপমন্যাকারং যত্তস্য ভাতি বিলাসতঃ ৷  
প্রায়েণাত্মসমং শক্ত্যা স বিলাসো নিগদ্যতে ॥ ৭৭ ॥

svarūpam anyākāraṁ yat  
a form  of different appearance  which  
tasya bhāti vilāsataḥ  
His  shines forth  for pastimes  
prāyeṇātma-samaṁ śaktyā  
almost  equal to Himself  in power  
sa vilāso nigadyate  
that  vilāsa  is called

(77) “When the Lord, for His pastimes, manifests a form of different appearance, almost equal to Himself in power, that form is called a vilāsa.” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta 1.15)

যৈছে বলদেব, পরব্যোমে নারায়ণ ৷  
যৈছে বাসুদেব প্রদ্যুম্নাদি সঙ্কর্ষণ ॥ ৭৮ ॥

yaiche baladeva, paravyome nārāyaṇa  
such as  Baladeva  in the spiritual sky  Nārāyaṇa  
yaiche vāsudeva pradyumnādi saṅkarṣaṇa  
such as  Vāsudeva  Pradyumna and the others  Saṅkarṣaṇa

(78) Examples are Baladeva, Nārāyaṇa in the spiritual sky, and Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and the others.

ঈশ্বরের শক্তি হয় ত্রিবিধ প্রকার ৷  
এক লক্ষ্মীগণ, পুরে মহিষীগণ আর ॥ ৭৯ ॥

ব্রজে গোপীগণ আর সবাতে প্রধান ৷  
ব্রজেন্দ্রনন্দন যা’তে স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ৮০ ॥

īśvarera śakti haya trividha prakāra  
the Lord's  potencies  are  three  kinds  
eka lakṣmī-gaṇa, pure mahiṣī-gaṇa āra  
one  the Lakṣmīs  in the city (Dvārakā)  the queens  another  
vraje gopī-gaṇa āra sabāte pradhāna  
in Vraja  the gopīs  and  among all  the foremost  
vrajendra-nandana yā’te svayaṁ bhagavān  
the son of the king of Vraja  since  Himself  Bhagavān

(79–80) The Lord’s potencies are of three kinds: the goddesses of fortune (Lakṣmīs), the queens in Dvārakā, and the gopīs in Vraja. The gopīs are the foremost of all, for the son of the king of Vraja is the Supreme Lord Himself.

স্বয়ংরূপ কৃষ্ণের কায়ব্যূহ — তাঁর সম ৷  
ভক্ত সহিতে হয় তাঁহার আবরণ ॥ ৮১ ॥

svayaṁ-rūpa kṛṣṇera kāya-vyūha — tāṅra sama  
of the original form  Kṛṣṇa's  bodily expansions  to Him  equal  
bhakta sahite haya tāṅhāra āvaraṇa  
devotees  together with  are  His  entourage

(81) These potencies are bodily expansions (*kāya-vyūha*) of Kṛṣṇa, the original form of the Lord, and they are equal to Him. Together with the devotees they form His entourage.

ভক্ত আদি ক্রমে কৈল সবার বন্দন ৷  
এ-সবার বন্দন সর্ব্ব শুভের কারণ ॥ ৮২ ॥

bhakta ādi krame kaila sabāra vandana  
devotees  and the others  in order  I made  of all  worship  
e-sabāra vandana sarva śubhera kāraṇa  
of all these  worship  of all  good  the cause

(82) Thus I have worshipped them all in order, beginning with the devotees. Worship of them all is the cause of every good fortune.

প্রথম শ্লোকে সামান্য মঙ্গলাচরণ ৷  
দ্বিতীয় শ্লোকেতে করি বিশেষ বন্দন ॥ ৮৩ ॥

prathama śloke sāmānya maṅgalācaraṇa  
in the first  verse  general  invocation  
dvitīya ślokete kari viśeṣa vandana  
in the second  verse  I make  particular  worship

(83) The first verse is a general invocation. In the second verse I offer worship in particular.

বন্দে শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্যনিত্যানন্দৌ সহোদিতৌ ৷  
গৌড়োদয়ে পুষ্পবন্তৌ চিত্রৌ শন্দৌ তমোনুদৌ ॥ ৮৪ ॥

vande śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-  
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  
nityānandau sahoditau  
(and) Nityānanda  risen together  
gauḍodaye puṣpavantau  
on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and the moon  
citrau śan-dau tamo-nudau  
wondrous  bestowing auspiciousness  dispelling darkness

(84) I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda, who have risen together like the sun and the moon on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa. Wondrous and bestowing auspiciousness, They dispel the darkness.

ব্রজে যে বিহরে পূর্ব্বে কৃষ্ণ-বলরাম ৷  
কোটীসূর্য্যচন্দ্র জিনি’ দোঁহার নিজধাম ॥ ৮৫ ॥

সেই দুই জগতেরে হইয়ে সদয় ৷  
গৌড়দেশে পূর্ব্বশৈলে করিল উদয় ॥ ৮৬ ॥

vraje ye vihare pūrve kṛṣṇa-balarāma  
in Vraja  who  sported  formerly  Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma  
koṭī-sūrya-candra jini’ doṅhāra nija-dhāma  
ten million suns and moons  surpassing  of both  own  effulgence  
sei dui jagatere haiye sadaya  
those  two  to the world  becoming  merciful  
gauḍadeśe pūrva-śaile karila udaya  
in the land of Gauḍa  on the eastern mountain  made  rising

(85–86) Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma formerly sported in Vraja, and Their effulgence surpasses ten million suns and moons. Becoming merciful to the world, these two have risen on the eastern horizon of the land of Gauḍa.

শ্রীকৃষ্ণচৈতন্য আর প্রভু নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
যাঁহার প্রকাশে সর্ব্ব জগৎ আনন্দ ॥ ৮৭ ॥

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya āra prabhu nityānanda  
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  and  Lord  Nityānanda  
yāṅhāra prakāśe sarva jagat ānanda  
whose  by the appearance  whole  world  joyful

(87) They are Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda Prabhu, whose appearance has filled the whole world with joy.

সূর্য্যচন্দ্র হরে যৈছে সব অন্ধকার ৷  
বস্তু প্রকাশিয়া করে ধর্ম্মের প্রচার ॥ ৮৮ ॥

এইমত দুই ভাই জীবের অজ্ঞান-  
তমোনাশ করি’ কৈল তত্ত্ববস্তু-দান ॥ ৮৯ ॥

sūrya-candra hare yaiche saba andhakāra  
the sun and moon  remove  as  all  darkness  
vastu prakāśiyā kare dharmera pracāra  
things  revealing  make  of dharma  the spreading  
ei mata dui bhāi jīvera ajñāna-  
in this way  two  brothers  of the souls  of ignorance  
tamo-nāśa kari’ kaila tattva-vastu-dāna  
of the darkness  destruction  having made  made  the gift of the Absolute Reality

(88–89) The sun and moon remove all darkness, reveal things, and so allow dharma to spread. In the same way the two brothers have destroyed the darkness of the souls’ ignorance and given them the Absolute Reality.

অজ্ঞান-তমের নাম কহিয়ে ‘কৈতব’ ৷  
ধর্ম্ম-অর্থ-কাম-বাঞ্ছা এই সব ॥ ৯০ ॥

ajñāna-tamera nāma kahiye ‘kaitava’  
of ignorance  of the darkness  the name  I call  cheating  
dharma-artha-kāma-vāñchā ei saba  
the desire for dharma, wealth and pleasure  this  all

(90) I call this darkness of ignorance “cheating” (*kaitava*). It is the whole range of desires for religious merit (*dharma*), wealth and pleasure.

ধর্ম্মঃ প্রোজ্ঝিতকৈতবোঽত্র পরমো নির্ম্মৎসরাণাং সতাং  
বেদ্যং বাস্তবমত্র বস্তু শিবদং তাপত্রয়োন্মূলনম্ ৷  
শ্রীমদ্ভাগবতে মহামুনিকৃতে কিংবাপরৈরীশ্বরঃ  
সদ্যো হৃদ্যবরুধ্যতেঽত্র কৃতিভিঃ শুশ্রূষুভিস্তৎক্ষণাৎ ॥ ৯১ ॥

dharmaḥ projjhita-kaitavo ‘tra paramo nirmatsarāṇāṁ satāṁ  
dharma  from which cheating is cast out  here  supreme  of the non-envious  saints  
vedyaṁ vāstavam atra vastu śiva-daṁ tāpa-trayonmūlanam  
to be known  real  here  the Reality  bestowing good  uprooting the threefold miseries  
śrīmad-bhāgavate mahā-muni-kṛte kiṁ vā parair īśvaraḥ  
in the beautiful Bhāgavatam  composed by the great sage  what  then  with others  the Lord  
sadyo hṛdy avarudhyate ‘tra kṛtibhiḥ śuśrūṣubhis tat-kṣaṇāt  
at once  in the heart  is captured  here  by the fortunate  who wish to hear  instantly

(91) “In this beautiful *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, composed by the great sage, the supreme dharma is set forth for saints free from envy, and every kind of cheating is cast out of it. Here the true Reality is to be known, which bestows the highest good and uproots the threefold miseries. What need is there of other scriptures? As soon as the fortunate wish to hear it, the Lord is captured in their hearts.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.1.2)

তার মধ্যে মোক্ষবাঞ্ছা কৈতব-প্রধান ৷  
যাহা হৈতে কৃষ্ণভক্তি হয় অন্তর্দ্ধান ॥ ৯২ ॥

tāra madhye mokṣa-vāñchā kaitava-pradhāna  
them  among  the desire for liberation  the chief cheating  
yāhā haite kṛṣṇa-bhakti haya antardhāna  
which  from  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  becomes  vanishing

(92) Among these the desire for liberation is the chief cheating, for it makes devotion to Kṛṣṇa vanish.

“প্র-শব্দেন মোক্ষাভিসন্ধিরপি নিরস্তঃ” ইতি ॥ ৯৩ ॥

“pra-śabdena mokṣābhisandhir api nirastaḥ” iti  
by the prefix pra-  the intention of liberation  even  is rejected  thus

(93) “By the prefix *pra-* even the intention to attain liberation is rejected.” (Śrīdhara Svāmī, Bhāvārtha-dīpikā 1.1.2)

কৃষ্ণভক্তির বাধক — যত শুভাশুভ কর্ম্ম ৷  
সেই এক জীবের অজ্ঞানতমো-ধর্ম্ম ॥ ৯৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa-bhaktira bādhaka — yata śubhāśubha karma  
of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the obstacle  all  auspicious and inauspicious  action  
sei eka jīvera ajñāna-tamo-dharma  
that  one  of the soul  of the darkness of ignorance  the nature

(94) All action, auspicious and inauspicious, is an obstacle to devotion to Kṛṣṇa. It too belongs to the soul’s darkness of ignorance.

যাঁহার প্রসাদে এই তমো হয় নাশ ৷  
তমো নাশ করি’ করে তত্ত্বের প্রকাশ ॥ ৯৫ ॥

yāṅhāra prasāde ei tamo haya nāśa  
whose  by the mercy  this  darkness  is  destroyed  
tamo nāśa kari’ kare tattvera prakāśa  
darkness  destruction  having made  make  of the truth  revelation

(95) By the mercy of these two brothers this darkness is destroyed. Having destroyed the darkness, They reveal the truth.

তত্ত্ববস্তু — কৃষ্ণ, কৃষ্ণভক্তি, প্রেমরূপ ৷  
নামসংকীর্ত্তন — সর্ব্ব আনন্দস্বরূপ ॥ ৯৬ ॥

tattva-vastu — kṛṣṇa, kṛṣṇa-bhakti, prema-rūpa  
the Absolute Reality  Kṛṣṇa  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  in the form of love  
nāma-saṅkīrtana — sarva ānanda-svarūpa  
the congregational chanting of the Name  all  bliss itself

(96) The Absolute Reality is Kṛṣṇa, devotion to Kṛṣṇa in the form of love (*prema*), and the congregational chanting of the Holy Name. All of it is bliss itself.

সূর্য্য চন্দ্র বাহিরের তমঃ সে বিনাশে ৷  
বহির্ব্বস্তু ঘট পট আদি সে প্রকাশে ॥ ৯৭ ॥

sūrya candra bāhirera tamaḥ se vināśe  
sun  moon  external  darkness  they  destroy  
bahir-vastu ghaṭa paṭa ādi se prakāśe  
external objects  pots  cloth  and so on  they  reveal

(97) The sun and moon destroy external darkness and reveal external objects such as pots and cloth.

দুই ভাই হৃদয়ের ক্ষালি’ অন্ধকার ৷  
দুই ভাগবত-সঙ্গে করান সাক্ষাৎকার ॥ ৯৮ ॥

dui bhāi hṛdayera kṣāli’ andhakāra  
two  brothers  of the heart  washing away  the darkness  
dui bhāgavata-saṅge karāna sākṣātkāra  
two  bhāgavatas  with  cause  meeting

(98) But the two brothers wash away the darkness of the heart and bring one to meet the two bhāgavatas.

এক ভাগবত বড় — ভাগবতশাস্ত্র ৷  
আর ভাগবত — ভক্ত ভক্তি-রস-পাত্র ॥ ৯৯ ॥

eka bhāgavata baḍa — bhāgavata-śāstra  
one  bhāgavata  great  the Bhāgavata scripture  
āra bhāgavata — bhakta bhakti-rasa-pātra  
the other  bhāgavata  the devotee  vessel of the rasa of devotion

(99) One bhāgavata is the great scripture, the *Bhāgavatam*. The other bhāgavata is the devotee, a vessel of the *rasa* of devotion.

দুই ভাগবত দ্বারা দিয়া ভক্তিরস ৷  
তাঁহার হৃদয়ে তাঁর প্রেমে হয় বশ ॥ ১০০ ॥

dui bhāgavata dvārā diyā bhakti-rasa  
two  bhāgavatas  through  giving  the rasa of devotion  
tāṅhāra hṛdaye tāṅra preme haya vaśa  
his  in the heart  by his  love  is  subdued

(100) Through the two bhāgavatas They give the rasa of devotion. Then the Lord, dwelling in the devotee’s heart, is subdued by his love.

এক অদ্ভুত — সমকালে দোঁহার প্রকাশ ৷  
আর অদ্ভুত — চিত্তগুহার তমঃ করে নাশ ॥ ১০১ ॥

eka adbhuta — sama-kāle doṅhāra prakāśa  
one  wonder  at the same time  of both  the appearance  
āra adbhuta — citta-guhāra tamaḥ kare nāśa  
another  wonder  of the cave of the heart  darkness  make  destruction

(101) One wonder is that both appear at the same time. Another wonder is that They destroy the darkness in the cave of the heart.

এই চন্দ্র সূর্য্য দুই পরম সদয় ৷  
জগতের ভাগ্যে গৌড়ে করিল উদয় ॥ ১০২ ॥

ei candra sūrya dui parama sadaya  
this  moon  sun  two  supremely  merciful  
jagatera bhāgye gauḍe karila udaya  
of the world  by the good fortune  in Gauḍa  made  rising

(102) This moon and this sun are supremely merciful. By the good fortune of the world They have risen in Gauḍa.

সেই দুই প্রভুর করি চরণ বন্দন ৷  
যাঁহা হইতে বিঘ্ননাশ অভীষ্টপূরণ ॥ ১০৩ ॥

sei dui prabhura kari caraṇa vandana  
those  two  Lords'  I make  feet  worship  
yāṅhā haite vighna-nāśa abhīṣṭa-pūraṇa  
which  from  destruction of obstacles  fulfilment of desires

(103) I worship the feet of these two Lords. This destroys every obstacle and fulfils one’s cherished desires.

এই দুই শ্লোকে কৈল মঙ্গল বন্দন ৷  
তৃতীয় শ্লোকের অর্থ শুন সর্ব্বজন ॥ ১০৪ ॥

ei dui śloke kaila maṅgala vandana  
in these  two  verses  I made  auspicious  worship  
tṛtīya ślokera artha śuna sarva-jana  
of the third  verse  the meaning  listen  all people

(104) In these two verses I have offered auspicious worship. Now listen, everyone, to the meaning of the third verse.

বক্তব্য-বাহুল্য, গ্রন্থ-বিস্তারের ডরে ৷  
বিস্তারি’ না বর্ণি সারার্থ কহি অল্পাক্ষরে ॥ ১০৫ ॥

vaktavya-bāhulya, grantha-vistārera ḍare  
abundance of what is to be said  of the book's  expansion  for fear  
vistāri’ nā varṇi sārārtha kahi alpākṣare  
in detail  not  I describe  the essential meaning  I tell  in few words

(105) There is much to be said, but I fear the book would grow too long. So I do not describe it in detail; I tell the essential meaning in few words.

‘মিতঞ্চ সারঞ্চ বচো হি বাগ্মিতা’ ইতি ॥ ১০৬ ॥

‘mitañ ca sārañ ca vaco hi vāgmitā’ iti  
measured  and  essential  and  speech  indeed  eloquence  thus

(106) “Speech that is brief and essential is true eloquence.”

শুনিলে খণ্ডিবে চিত্তের অজ্ঞানাদি দোষ ৷  
কৃষ্ণে গাঢ় প্রেম হবে, পাইবে সন্তোষ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

śunile khaṇḍibe cittera ajñānādi doṣa  
on hearing  will remove  of the heart  ignorance and other  faults  
kṛṣṇe gāḍha prema habe, pāibe santoṣa  
for Kṛṣṇa  deep  love  will be  will find  satisfaction

(107) Hearing this removes ignorance and the other faults of the heart. One will gain deep love for Kṛṣṇa and find true satisfaction.

শ্রীচৈতন্য-নিত্যানন্দ-অদ্বৈত-মহত্ত্ব ৷  
তাঁর ভক্ত-ভক্তি-নাম-প্রেমরসতত্ত্ব ॥ ১০৮ ॥

ভিন্ন ভিন্ন লিখিয়াছি করিয়া বিচার ৷  
শুনিলে জানিবে সব বস্তুতত্ত্বসার ॥ ১০৯ ॥

śrī-caitanya-nityānanda-advaita-mahattva  
of Śrī Caitanya, Nityānanda and Advaita  the greatness  
tāṅra bhakta-bhakti-nāma-prema-rasa-tattva  
Their  devotees, devotion, the Name, love, rasa  the truth  
bhinna bhinna likhiyāchi kariyā vicāra  
separately  separately  I have written  having made  consideration  
śunile jānibe saba vastu-tattva-sāra  
on hearing  will know  all  the essence of the truth of the Reality

(108–109) After careful consideration I have written separately of the greatness of Śrī Caitanya, Nityānanda and Advaita, and of the truth of Their devotees, of devotion, of the Holy Name, of love and of rasa. By hearing this, one will know the whole essence of the truth of the Absolute Reality.

শ্রীরূপ-রঘুনাথ-পদে যার আশ ৷  
চৈতন্যচরিতামৃত কহে কৃষ্ণদাস ॥ ১১০ ॥

śrī-rūpa-raghunātha-pade yāra āśa  
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  at the feet  whose  hope  
caitanya-caritāmṛta kahe kṛṣṇadāsa  
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa

(110) Ever hoping for the mercy of the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
