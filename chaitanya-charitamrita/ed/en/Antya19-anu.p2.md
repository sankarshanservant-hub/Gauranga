## 70

> Subheading: Maitreya speaks to Vidura, who served as a pillow for Kṛṣṇa's feet — (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 3.13.5)

The great-souled Vidura asked the *mahā-bhāgavata* sage Maitreya about the deeds of Svāyambhuva Manu, the best of Hari's devotees, and of Śatarūpā. In reply Maitreya spoke about Hari, and Śrī Śukadeva is relating that account to Parīkṣit:

(**śrī-śuka uvāca** —) **bhagavat-kathāyāṁ** (in describing the qualities of Śrī Hari) **praṇīyamānaḥ** (urged on by Vidura) **prahṛṣṭa-romā** (he whose hairs stood on end) **muniḥ** (Maitreya) **iti** **bruvāṇaṁ** (to the one asking) **sahasra-śīrṣṇaḥ** (of the Thousand-headed One, Śrī Kṛṣṇa) **caraṇopadhānaṁ** (the one on whom the feet are placed, that is, the one on whose lap Śrī Kṛṣṇa lovingly stretches out His feet) **vinītaṁ** (humble) **viduraṁ** **abhyacaṣṭa** (spoke).

It is said that Bhagavān Śrī Kṛṣṇa came to Vidura's house, rested both His feet on Vidura's lap and fell asleep (see the *Sārārtha-darśinī* commentary).
