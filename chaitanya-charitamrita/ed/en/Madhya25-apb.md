# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Madhya 25

## 0

**Summary.** A brāhmaṇa from Mahārāṣṭra was a servant of Mahāprabhu. Whenever he heard the Lord's glory, he was filled with joy. One day he invited the *sannyāsīs* and Mahāprabhu, brought them together, and made the *sannyāsīs* fit to receive Mahāprabhu's mercy; this is described in the seventh chapter of the Ādi-līlā. From that day the Lord's greatness became known throughout the city of Vārāṇasī, and many of its residents became His followers. One of Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī's disciples also became a follower of the Lord. When this disciple censured Māyāvāda and described the greatness of the teaching of pure devotion that Mahāprabhu had given, Prakāśānanda Svāmī defended his own position with many arguments. One day, after bathing at Pañcanada, Mahāprabhu began *kīrtana* with His devotees in the temple of Bindu-mādhava, and Prakāśānanda came there with his disciples. Prakāśānanda fell down and clasped Mahāprabhu's feet, condemned his own past conduct, and asked about the truth of *bhakti* in harmony with Vedānta. Mahāprabhu taught him the unprecedented doctrine of devotion established in the Brahma-sampradāya, showed that the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* is the commentary on the *Brahma-sūtra*, and, explaining the *Catuḥ-ślokī*, set forth the whole truth. From that day the *sannyāsīs* became “devotees.” Mahāprabhu instructed Sanātana, told him to go to Vṛndāvana, and set out for Puruṣottama. After this Kavirāja Gosvāmī described something of the history of Śrī Rūpa, Śrī Sanātana and Subuddhi Rāya. Mahāprabhu set out with Balabhadra through Jhārikhaṇḍa and arrived in Śrī Puruṣottama. In the last part of this chapter Śrī Kavirāja Gosvāmī listed the subjects of every chapter of the Madhya-līlā and instructed all *jīvas* to read this *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

## 1

Having made the *sannyāsīs* and the other residents of Kāśī “Vaiṣṇavas,” and having performed the purificatory rite (*saṁskāra*) for Sanātana in the best way, the Lord came to Nīlādri.

“I have described this before in detail” — see Ādi 7.

## 26

*Ācārya* means Śaṅkarācārya.

## 36

“O Supreme! This form of Yours is bliss alone, free of all differentiation (*avikalpa*), radiant and beyond *māyā*. There is no form higher than the one I now behold. O Soul (*ātmā*)! You create the universe and yet are distinct from it. This form of Yours that I behold is the source of the elements and the senses; in it I take shelter (*prapatti*).”

## 37

“O auspiciousness of the universe! For our good You have revealed in meditation this form of Yours, worthy of our worship. To this form of Bhagavān we bow down and render service. People defiled by bad company and destined for hell have no regard for this spiritual form.[^37-1]”
