# Anubhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 17

## 1

> Subheading: By Gaura's grace even the impure become pure —

**yat** (of Caitanyadeva) **prasādataḥ** (by the compassion) **yavanāḥ** (the *mlecchas*) **kṛṣṇa-nāma-prajalpakāḥ** (being firmly devoted to chanting the name) **sumanāyante** (behave like people of good mind, *sumanas*), **taṁ** **svairādbhutehaṁ** (Him whose deeds are free, independent, and wonderful, beyond this world — Him who has the power to overstep the injunctions of the *smārtas*) **caitanyam** **ahaṁ** **vande**.

## 4

> Subheading: Various pastimes in youth —

**gauraḥ** **yauvane** (when His fifteenth year had passed and youth appeared) **vidyā-saundarya-sad-veśa-sambhoga-nṛtya-kīrtanaiḥ** (with knowledge of the supreme goal, with beauty, with the dress of a sādhu, with the enjoyment of garments, garlands, sandalwood paste and the like, with dancing, *kīrtana* and so on) **prema-nāma-pradānaiḥ** (by distributing Kṛṣṇa's name together with *prema*) **dīvyati** (sports).

## 12

> Subheading: Meeting Śrī Nityānanda —

The meeting with Nityānanda: see the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya, Chapter 3. How, at the Vyāsa-pūjā in Śrīvāsa's house, Nityānanda saw Mahāprabhu six-armed (holding conch, disc, club, lotus, plough and pestle, *muṣala*): ibid., Madhya, Chapter 5.
