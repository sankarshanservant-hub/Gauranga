## 26

> Subheading: Śivānanda takes care of everything —

The *ghāṭiyāls* were inspectors on the road. They unjustly and unlawfully exacted extra money from travelers. Śivānanda would give them what was rightfully due to them and ask them to give up their excessive demands.

## 32–34

“From Mahāprabhu's lips.” Mahāprabhu had earlier heard this from the lips of Śrīpāda Īśvara Purī (Madhya 4.18). After staying a few days in Śāntipura, in Śrī Advaita's house, He set out for Nīlācala with Nityānanda, Jagadānanda, Dāmodara Paṇḍita and Mukunda. On reaching Remuṇā He told them the story of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, of Giridhārī Gopāla of Vṛndāvana and of Kṣīra-corā Gopīnātha of Remuṇā (see Madhya 4.19–190).
