# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Antya-līlā 7

## 0

**Summary.** This chapter describes the arrival of Vallabha Bhaṭṭa: how the Lord joked with him in many ways and corrected his conclusions (*siddhāntas*), how He accepted his invitation, and how, seeing the Bhaṭṭa's special devotion to Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita, He feigned indifference toward the Paṇḍita. When the Bhaṭṭa had become wholly submissive, the Lord accepted his invitation, told him to learn the meaning of the mantra and other things from Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita, and showed His affection for the Paṇḍita.

## 1

I worship the devotees who are eager for the honey of Caitanya's lotus feet: by their mercy alone even a fallen soul becomes immortal.

## 10

The mere remembrance of them purifies people's homes. How much is gained by seeing them, touching them, washing their feet, offering them a seat and so on cannot be put into words.

## 74

*Sāmbhāla*: self-control.

## 76

*Yātrāntare*: at another festival, on another day[^76-1].

## 82

To be dark like a *tamāla* tree and to suck at Yaśodā's breast — this is the *rūḍhi*, the primary meaning, present in the name “Kṛṣṇa,” as all the scriptures conclude.

## 84

*Phalgu-prāya*: nearly worthless.

## 85

The devotion he had for the Lord partly went away[^85-1].

## 93

*Ābhijātye* — because of his noble standing (*kaulīnya*), that is, because Vallabha Bhaṭṭa was held in honor among scholars.

## 96

*Udgrāhādi* — disputes (*vitarka*) and the like.

## 106

*Kakṣā-pāta* — defeat.

## 110

Whatever statement comes up in a given place, Śrīdhara Svāmī accepts it as it stands and explains it so. Therefore his explanations do not everywhere have a single, consistent sense (*eka-vākyatā*) or harmony (*sāmañjasya*); and so I do not accept Śrīdhara Svāmī.

## 115

*Ughāḍe nayane* — the eyes open (sight dawns).

## 130

*Artha-vyasta* — contrary in meaning.

## 148

*Olāhana* — punishment by words, a reproach.

## 162

*Loke karilā khyāpana* — the Lord made it known to everyone.[^162-1]

*Thus ends the seventh chapter of the “Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya.”*

[^76-1]: The verse translation reads differently: “After the festival.”

[^162-1]: In the verse translation, otherwise: “Gadādhara Paṇḍita is famous throughout the world for his brahminical qualities and gentleness, and also for the steadfastness of his pure love for Śrī Caitanya.”
