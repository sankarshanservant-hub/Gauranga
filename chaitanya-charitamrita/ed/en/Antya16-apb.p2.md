## 80

***Dalai*** — a doorkeeper.

## 87

“O friend, doorkeeper, where is Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of My life? Show Him to Me here, quickly!” Saying this to the doorkeeper like a madman, [the Lord] took him by the hand and hurried to see Kṛṣṇa.[^87-1] May such a Gaurāṅga rise in my heart and madden me.

## 88

*Vallabha-bhoga* is what in this region is called *bāla-bhoga*.

## 100

A pious act that gives rise to Kṛṣṇa's mercy is called *sukṛti* (*sukṛti* turned toward *bhakti*).

---

[^87-1]: The verse translation reads differently: when the doorkeeper answered, “Come quickly, You will see Your Beloved!”, the Lord seized his hand.
