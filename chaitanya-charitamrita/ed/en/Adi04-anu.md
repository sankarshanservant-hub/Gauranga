# Anubhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 4

## 1

> Subheading: The nature of Śrī Kṛṣṇa is ascertained by the mercy of Śrī Gaura —

**bālaḥ** (a child) **api** **śrī-caitanya-prasādena** (by the mercy of Gaura) **śāstram** **dṛṣṭvā** **vraja-vilāsinaḥ** (of the son of the king of Vraja) **tad-rūpasya** (of the form of Gaura, nondifferent from Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa) **vinirṇayam** (a statement of the truth about Him) **kurute**.

## 5

> Subheading: The meaning of the fourth of the fourteen opening verses: preaching the name and *prema* is the external cause of the Gaura-avatāra —

## 7

> Subheading: Approaching the hidden cause of the Gaura-avatāra, the author first describes how the pastimes of Kṛṣṇa and Viṣṇu differ —

## 8

> Subheading: The work of Viṣṇu is to protect the *sādhus* and destroy the wicked; this is not the work of Kṛṣṇa Himself —

## 9

> Subheading: When Kṛṣṇa, the source of the avatāras, descends, the avatāra Viṣṇu joins Him —

## 13

> Subheading: Through Viṣṇu, His part present within His body, He removes the world's burden and performs the pastime of maintaining it —

## 15

> Subheading: Viṣṇu descends to preach *vidhi-bhakti*, devotion by rules; Kṛṣṇa descends as Gaura to preach *rāga-bhakti*, devotion of spontaneous love —

## 17

For this same verse see Ādi 6.16.

## 20

> Subheading: (*Śrīmad Bhagavad-gītā*, Chapter 4, verse 11)

The teaching on *yoga* that Bhagavān once gave to Sūrya came down through the succession and in time became distorted. Then He gave the same teaching again to Arjuna. Bhagavān speaks this verse while explaining the reason for His manifest pastimes:

**[he] pārtha** (O Arjuna), **ye** (the devotees) **yathā** (with whatever feeling) **mām** (to Me, Kṛṣṇa) **prapadyante** (surrender), **ahaṁ tathaiva tān bhajāmi** (in that very way I show them mercy). **manuṣyāḥ sarvaśaḥ** (people, in every way) **mama vartma** (My path — the path to perfection) **anuvartante** (follow).
