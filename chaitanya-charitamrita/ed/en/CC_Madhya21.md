“Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  
Madhya-līlā  
Chapter 21  
**The Sweetness of Śrī Kṛṣṇa and His Boundless Glories**  
(translated from the Bengali for this edition)

অগত্যেকগতিং নত্বা হীনার্থাধিকসাধকম্ ৷  
শ্রীচৈতন্যং লিখাম্যস্য মাধুর্য্যৈশ্বর্য্যশীকরম্ ॥ ১ ॥

agaty-eka-gatiṁ natvā  
of the shelterless  the only shelter  having bowed  
hīnārthādhika-sādhakam  
to the deprived  the highest  who grants  
śrī-caitanyaṁ likhāmy asya  
to Śrī Caitanya  I write  His  
mādhuryaiśvarya-śīkaram  
of sweetness and majesty  a drop

(1) Having bowed to Śrī Caitanya, the only refuge of the shelterless, who grants the highest gift to the deprived, I write of a drop of His sweetness and majesty.

জয় জয় শ্রীচৈতন্য জয় নিত্যানন্দ ৷  
জয়াদ্বৈতচন্দ্র জয় গৌরভক্তবৃন্দ ॥ ২ ॥

jaya jaya śrī-caitanya jaya nityānanda  
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda  
jayādvaita-candra jaya gaura-bhakta-vṛnda  
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude

(2) Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

সর্ব্বস্বরূপের ধাম — পরব্যোম-ধামে ৷  
পৃথক্ পৃথক্ বৈকুণ্ঠ, নাহিক গণনে ॥ ৩ ॥

sarva svarūpera dhāma — paravyoma-dhāme  
of all  the forms  the abode  of Paravyoma  in the realm  
pṛthak pṛthak vaikuṇṭha, nāhika gaṇane  
separate  separate  Vaikuṇṭhas  there is no  counting

(3) The abode of all the Lord’s forms is the realm of Paravyoma. There each form has its own Vaikuṇṭha, and they are beyond counting.

শত, সহস্র, অযুত, লক্ষ, কোটি-যোজন ৷  
এক এক বৈকুণ্ঠের বিস্তার বর্ণন ॥ ৪ ॥

śata, sahasra, ayuta, lakṣa, koṭi-yojana  
a hundred  a thousand  ten thousand  a hundred thousand  ten million  yojanas  
eka eka vaikuṇṭhera vistāra varṇana  
each  single  Vaikuṇṭha’s  extent  is described

(4) The extent of each Vaikuṇṭha is described in hundreds, thousands, tens of thousands, hundreds of thousands and tens of millions of *yojanas*.

সব বৈকুণ্ঠ — ব্যাপক, আনন্দ-চিন্ময় ৷  
পারিষদ-ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্য-পূর্ণ সব হয় ॥ ৫ ॥

saba vaikuṇṭha — vyāpaka, ānanda-cinmaya  
all  Vaikuṇṭhas  all-pervading  blissful  spiritual  
pāriṣada-ṣaḍaiśvarya-pūrṇa saba haya  
with associates  with the six opulences  full  all  are

(5) All the Vaikuṇṭhas are all-pervading, made of bliss and spirit, and all are full of associates and of the six opulences.

অনন্ত বৈকুণ্ঠ এক এক দেশে যার ৷  
সেই পরব্যোমের কেবা গণয়ে বিস্তার ॥ ৬ ॥

ananta vaikuṇṭha eka eka deśe yāra  
endless  Vaikuṇṭhas  in each  single  part  of which  
sei paravyomera kebā gaṇaye vistāra  
of that  Paravyoma  who  can count  the expanse

(6) Who can measure the expanse of that Paravyoma, in each part of which lie endless Vaikuṇṭhas?

অনন্ত বৈকুণ্ঠ-পরব্যোম যার দলশ্রেণী ৷  
সর্ব্বোপরি কৃষ্ণলোক ‘কর্ণিকার’ গণি ॥ ৭ ॥

ananta vaikuṇṭha-paravyoma yāra dala-śreṇī  
endless  Vaikuṇṭhas  of Paravyoma  whose  petals  rows  
sarvopari kṛṣṇaloka ‘karṇikāra’ gaṇi  
above all  Kṛṣṇaloka  the whorl  I count

(7) It is a lotus whose rows of petals are the endless Vaikuṇṭhas of Paravyoma; above them all, Kṛṣṇaloka is its whorl (*karṇikā*).

এইমত ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্য, স্থান, অবতার ৷  
ব্রহ্মা, শিব অন্ত না পায় — জীব কোন্ ছার ॥ ৮ ॥

ei mata ṣaḍ-aiśvarya, sthāna, avatāra  
in this  way  the six opulences  the abodes  the avatāras  
brahmā, śiva anta nā pāya — jīva kon chāra  
Brahmā  Śiva  the end  do not  find  the jīva  what  a trifle

(8) Such are His six opulences, His abodes and His *avatāras*. Even Brahmā and Śiva cannot find their end — what then of the insignificant *jīva*?

কো বেত্তি ভূমন্ ভগবন্ পরাত্মন্  
যোগেশ্বরোতীর্ভবতস্ত্রিলোক্যাম্ ৷  
ক্ব বা কথং বা কতি বা কদেতি ৷  
বিস্তারয়ন্ ক্রীড়সি যোগমায়াম্ ॥ ৯ ॥

ko vetti bhūman bhagavan parātman  
who  knows  O Bhūman  O Bhagavān  O Supreme Soul  
yogeśvarotīr bhavatas tri-lokyām  
O Lord of yoga  the pastimes  Your  in the three worlds  
kva vā kathaṁ vā kati vā kadeti  
where  or  how  or  how many  or  when  
vistārayan krīḍasi yoga-māyām  
expanding  You play  Yogamāyā

(9) “O Bhūman, O Bhagavān, O Supreme Soul, O Lord of yoga! Who in the three worlds can know where, how, how many and when are Your pastimes, as You play, expanding Your Yogamāyā?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.21)

এইমত কৃষ্ণের দিব্য সদ্গুণ অনন্ত ৷  
ব্রহ্মা-শিব-সনকাদি না পায় যাঁর অন্ত ॥ ১০ ॥

ei mata kṛṣṇera divya sad-guṇa ananta  
in this  way  Kṛṣṇa’s  divine  virtues  are endless  
brahmā-śiva-sanakādi nā pāya yāṅra anta  
Brahmā  Śiva  Sanaka and others  do not  find  whose  end

(10) So endless are Kṛṣṇa’s divine virtues that even Brahmā, Śiva, Sanaka and the other sages cannot find their end.

গুণাত্মনস্তেঽপি গুণান্ বিমাতুং  
হিতাবতীর্ণস্য ক ঈশিরেঽস্য ৷  
কালেন যৈর্বা বিমিতাঃ সুকল্পৈ  
র্ভূ-পাংশবঃ খে মিহিকা দ্যুভাসঃ ॥ ১১ ॥

guṇātmanas te ’pi guṇān vimātuṁ  
of You whose self is the qualities  Your  even  the qualities  to count  
hitāvatīrṇasya ka īśire ’sya  
who descended for the good  who  have been able  of this (world)  
kālena yair vā vimitāḥ su-kalpair  
in time  by whom  or  have been counted  the most skilled  
bhū-pāṁśavaḥ khe mihikā dyu-bhāsaḥ  
of the earth  the particles  in the sky  the snowflakes  of the luminaries  the rays

(11) “In the course of time the most skilled may have counted the particles of the earth, the snowflakes in the sky and the rays of the luminaries. But who among them could count the qualities of You, the Lord of all qualities, who have descended for the good of the world?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.7)

ব্রহ্মাদি রহু — সহস্রবদনে ‘অনন্ত’ ৷  
নিরন্তর গায় মুখে, না পায় গুণের অন্ত ॥ ১২ ॥

brahmādi rahu — sahasra-vadane ‘ananta’  
Brahmā and the others  leave aside  with a thousand  mouths  Ananta  
nirantara gāya mukhe, nā pāya guṇera anta  
ceaselessly  sings  with His mouths  does not  find  of the qualities  the end

(12) Leave aside Brahmā and the others: Ananta sings ceaselessly with His thousand mouths, yet He finds no end to Kṛṣṇa’s qualities.

নান্তং বিদাম্যহমমী মুনয়োঽগ্রজাস্তে  
মায়াবলস্য পুরুষস্য কুতোঽপরে যে ৷  
গায়ন্ গুণান্ দশশতানন আদিদেবঃ  
শেষোঽধুনাপি সমবস্যতি নাস্য পারম্ ॥ ১৩ ॥

nāntaṁ vidāmy aham amī munayo ’grajās te  
not  the end  know  I  these  sages  elder  your  
māyā-balasya puruṣasya kuto ’pare ye  
of the Master of māyā’s power  of the Person  how much less  others  who  
gāyan guṇān daśa-śatānana ādi-devaḥ  
singing  the qualities  with ten  hundred  mouths  the original Lord  
śeṣo ’dhunāpi samavasyati nāsya pāram  
Śeṣa  even now  reaches  not  His  far shore

(13) “Neither I nor these sages, your elder brothers, know the end of the Supreme Person, the Master of the power of māyā — how then could others? Even Śeṣa, the original Lord, who sings His qualities with a thousand mouths, has not reached their far shore to this day.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.7.41)

তেঁহো রহু — সর্ব্বজ্ঞ-শিরোমণি শ্রীকৃষ্ণ ৷  
নিজ-গুণের অন্ত না পাঞা হয়েন সতৃষ্ণ ॥ ১৪ ॥

teṅho rahu — sarvajña-śiromaṇi śrī-kṛṣṇa  
He  leave aside  of the omniscient  the crest-jewel  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  
nija-guṇera anta nā pāñā hayena satṛṣṇa  
of His own  qualities  the end  not  finding  becomes  full of longing

(14) Leave Him aside: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself, the crest-jewel of the omniscient, cannot find the end of His own qualities, and so He is filled with longing.

দ্যুপতয় এব তে ন যযুরন্তমনন্ততয়া  
ত্বমপি যদন্তরাণ্ডনিচয়া ননু সাবরণাঃ ৷  
খ ইব রজাংসি বান্তি বয়সা সহ যচ্ছ্রুতয়-  
স্ত্বয়ি হি ফলন্ত্যতন্নিরসনেন ভবন্নিধনাঃ ॥ ১৫ ॥

dyu-pataya eva te na yayur antam anantatayā  
the lords of heaven  even  Your  not  reached  the end  because of endlessness  
tvam api yad-antarāṇḍa-nicayā nanu sāvaraṇāḥ  
You  even  since  within whom  of universes  the multitudes  indeed  with their coverings  
kha iva rajāṁsi vānti vayasā saha yac chrutayas  
in the sky  like  specks of dust  revolve  with time  together  since  the śrutis  
tvayi hi phalanty atan-nirasanena bhavan-nidhanāḥ  
in You  indeed  bear fruit  by rejecting what is not You  having their end in You

(15) “Because You are endless, even the lords of heaven have not reached Your end — nor do You Yourself, for within You multitudes of universes, each in its coverings, revolve together with time like specks of dust in the sky. Therefore the *śrutis*, rejecting all that is not You, bear their fruit in You and find their conclusion in You.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.41)

সেহ রহু — ব্রজে যবে কৃষ্ণ অবতার ৷  
তাঁর চরিত্র বিচারিতে মন না পায় পার ॥ ১৬ ॥

seha rahu — vraje yabe kṛṣṇa avatāra  
that too  leave aside  in Vraja  when  Kṛṣṇa  descended  
tāṅra caritra vicārite mana nā pāya pāra  
His  deeds  in pondering  the mind  does not  find  the shore

(16) Leave that aside as well. When Kṛṣṇa descended in Vraja, the mind could find no end in pondering His deeds.

প্রাকৃতাপ্রাকৃত সৃষ্টি কৈলা একক্ষণে ৷  
অশেষ-বৈকুণ্ঠজাণ্ড স্বস্বনাথ-সনে ॥ ১৭ ॥

prākṛtāprākṛta sṛṣṭi kailā eka-kṣaṇe  
material and transcendental  creation  He made  in a single moment  
aśeṣa-vaikuṇṭhajāṇḍa sva-sva-nātha-sane  
countless  Vaikuṇṭhas  and universes  with their own  lords

(17) In a single moment He made both the material and the transcendental creation — countless Vaikuṇṭhas and universes, each with its own lord.

এমত অন্যত্র নাহি শুনিয়ে অদ্ভুত ৷  
যাহার শ্রবণে চিত্ত হয় অবধূত ॥ ১৮ ॥

e-mata anyatra nāhi śuniye adbhuta  
such  elsewhere  not  one hears  a wonder  
yāhāra śravaṇe citta haya avadhūta  
of which  on hearing  the heart  becomes  overwhelmed

(18) Nowhere else does one hear of such a wonder. Simply by hearing of it, the heart is overwhelmed.

‘কৃষ্ণবৎসৈরসংখ্যাতৈঃ’ — শুকদেব-বাণী ৷  
কৃষ্ণ-সঙ্গে কত গোপ — সংখ্যা নাহি জানি ॥ ১৯ ॥

‘kṛṣṇa-vatsair asaṁkhyātaiḥ’ — śukadeva-vāṇī  
with Kṛṣṇa’s  calves  countless  Śukadeva’s  words  
kṛṣṇa-saṅge kata gopa — saṁkhyā nāhi jāni  
with Kṛṣṇa  how many  cowherd boys  their number  not  I know

(19) “With Kṛṣṇa’s countless calves” — so says Śukadeva. How many cowherd boys were with Kṛṣṇa I do not know: their number is not known.

এক এক গোপ করে যে বৎস চারণ ৷  
কোটি, অর্ব্বুদ, শঙ্খ, পদ্ম, তাহার গণন ॥ ২০ ॥

eka eka gopa kare ye vatsa cāraṇa  
each  single  cowherd boy  does  which  calves  herding  
koṭi, arbuda, śaṅkha, padma, tāhāra gaṇana  
ten million  a hundred million  a trillion  ten trillion  of them  the count

(20) The calves that each cowherd boy herded were counted in *koṭis*, *arbudas*, *śaṅkhas* and *padmas* — tens of millions, hundreds of millions, trillions, tens of trillions.

বেত্র, বেণু, দল, শৃঙ্গ, বস্ত্র, অলঙ্কার ৷  
গোপগণের যত তায় নাহি লেখা-পার ॥ ২১ ॥

vetra, veṇu, dala, śṛṅga, vastra, alaṅkāra  
canes  flutes  leaves  horns  garments  ornaments  
gopa-gaṇera yata tāya nāhi lekhā-pāra  
of the cowherd boys  as many as  of that  there is no  of writing  end

(21) Canes, flutes, leaves, horns, garments and ornaments — however many the cowherd boys had, there is no end to writing of them.

সবে হৈলা চতুর্ভুজ বৈকুণ্ঠের পতি ৷  
পৃথক্ পৃথক্ ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের ব্রহ্মা করে স্তুতি ॥ ২২ ॥

sabe hailā caturbhuja vaikuṇṭhera pati  
all  became  four-armed  of Vaikuṇṭha  lords  
pṛthak pṛthak brahmāṇḍera brahmā kare stuti  
of separate  separate  universes  the Brahmās  offer  praise

(22) All of them became four-armed lords of Vaikuṇṭha, and the Brahmās of separate universes offered them praise.

এক কৃষ্ণদেহ হৈতে সবার প্রকাশে ৷  
ক্ষণেকে সবাই সেই সেই শরীরে প্রবেশে ॥ ২৩ ॥

eka kṛṣṇa-deha haite sabāra prakāśe  
from one  Kṛṣṇa’s  body  of all  the manifestation  
kṣaṇeke sabāi sei sei śarīre praveśe  
in a moment  all  into that  same  body  enter

(23) All of them were manifested from Kṛṣṇa’s one body, and in a moment they all entered that same body again.

ইহা দেখি’ ব্রহ্মা হৈলা মোহিত, বিস্মিত ৷  
স্তুতি করি’ সেই পাছে করিলা নিশ্চিত ॥ ২৪ ॥

ihā dekhi’ brahmā hailā mohita, vismita  
this  seeing  Brahmā  became  bewildered  astonished  
stuti kari’ sei pāche karilā niścita  
praise  having offered  he  afterwards  made  a conclusion

(24) Seeing this, Brahmā was bewildered and astonished. After offering praise, he came to this conclusion:

যে কহে, — কৃষ্ণের বৈভব মুঞি সব জানোঁ ৷  
সে জানুক, — কায়মনে মুঞি এই মানোঁ ॥ ২৫ ॥

ye kahe, — kṛṣṇera vaibhava muñi saba jānoṅ  
who  says  Kṛṣṇa’s  glory  I  all  know  
se jānuka, — kāya-mane muñi ei mānoṅ  
he  let him know  with body and mind  I  this  accept

(25) “If someone says, ‘I know all of Kṛṣṇa’s glory,’ let him know it. As for me, with body and mind I accept this:

এই যে তোমার অনন্ত বৈভবামৃতসিন্ধু ৷  
মোর বাঙ্মানসের গম্য নহে এক বিন্দু ॥ ২৬ ॥

ei ye tomāra ananta vaibhavāmṛta-sindhu  
this  very  Your  endless  of glory  of nectar  ocean  
mora vāṅ-mānasera gamya nahe eka bindu  
my  of speech and mind  within reach  is not  one  drop

(26) of this endless nectar ocean of Your glory, not a single drop lies within reach of my speech and mind.

জানন্ত এব জানন্তু কিং বহূক্ত্যা ন মে প্রভো ৷  
মনসো বপুষো বাচো বৈভবং তব গোচরঃ ॥ ২৭ ॥

jānanta eva jānantu  
the knowers  indeed  let them know  
kiṁ bahūktyā na me prabho  
what  by much speaking  not  to me  O Lord  
manaso vapuṣo vāco  
of mind  of body  of speech  
vaibhavaṁ tava gocaraḥ  
glory  Your  within reach

(27) ‘Let those who know go on knowing. What use is much talk? O Lord, Your glory is beyond the reach of my mind, body and speech.’” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.38)

কৃষ্ণের মহিমা রহু — কেবা তার জ্ঞাতা ৷  
বৃন্দাবন-স্থানের দেখ — আশ্চর্য্য বিভুতা ॥ ২৮ ॥

kṛṣṇera mahimā rahu — kebā tāra jñātā  
Kṛṣṇa’s  glory  leave aside  who  of it  the knower  
vṛndāvana-sthānera dekha — āścarya vibhutā  
of Vṛndāvana  of the place  behold  wondrous  majesty

(28) Leave aside Kṛṣṇa’s own glory — who can know it? Behold instead the wondrous majesty of the land of Vṛndāvana.

ষোলক্রোশ বৃন্দাবন — শাস্ত্রের প্রকাশে ৷  
তার একদেশে বৈকুণ্ঠজাণ্ডগণ ভাসে ॥ ২৯ ॥

ṣola-krośa vṛndāvana — śāstrera prakāśe  
sixteen  krośas  Vṛndāvana  of the scriptures  by the revelation  
tāra eka-deśe vaikuṇṭhajāṇḍa-gaṇa bhāse  
its  in one part  Vaikuṇṭhas and universes  multitudes  float

(29) Vṛndāvana measures sixteen *krośas*, as the scriptures reveal; yet in a single part of it multitudes of Vaikuṇṭhas and universes float.

অপার ঐশ্বর্য্য কৃষ্ণের — নাহিক গণন ৷  
শাখা-চন্দ্র-ন্যায়ে করি দিগ্দরশন ॥ ৩০ ॥

apāra aiśvarya kṛṣṇera — nāhika gaṇana  
boundless  opulence  Kṛṣṇa’s  there is no  counting  
śākhā-candra-nyāye kari dig-daraśana  
of the branch  and the moon  by the maxim  I give  a glimpse

(30) Kṛṣṇa’s opulence is boundless, beyond counting. I have only pointed toward it, as one shows the moon by pointing to a branch (*śākhā-candra-nyāya*).

ঐশ্বর্য্য কহিতে স্ফুরিল ঐশ্বর্য্য সাগর ৷  
মনেন্দ্রিয় ডুবিলা, প্রভু হইলা ফাঁপর ॥ ৩১ ॥

aiśvarya kahite sphurila aiśvarya sāgara  
opulence  while describing  surged up  of opulence  the ocean  
manendriya ḍubilā, prabhu hailā phāṅpara  
mind and senses  sank  the Lord  became  overwhelmed

(31) As He described this opulence, an ocean of opulence surged up within Him. His mind and senses sank in it, and the Lord was overwhelmed.

ভাগবতের এই শ্লোক পড়িলা আপনে ৷  
অর্থ আস্বাদিতে সুখে করেন ব্যাখ্যানে ॥ ৩২ ॥

bhāgavatera ei śloka paḍilā āpane  
of the Bhāgavata  this  verse  recited  Himself  
artha āsvādite sukhe karena vyākhyāne  
the meaning  to relish  joyfully  He gives  an explanation

(32) He Himself recited this verse of the *Bhāgavatam*, and to relish its meaning He joyfully explained it.

স্বয়ন্ত্বসাম্যাতিশয়স্ত্র্যধীশঃ  
স্বারাজ্যলক্ষ্ম্যাপ্তসমস্তকামঃ ৷  
বলিং হরদ্ভিশ্চিরলোকপালৈঃ  
কিরীটকোটীড়িতপাদপীঠঃ ॥ ৩৩ ॥

svayaṁ tv asāmyātiśayas try-adhīśaḥ  
Himself  indeed  with none equal or superior  of the three  the Lord  
svārājya-lakṣmy-āpta-samasta-kāmaḥ  
by Svārājya  Lakṣmī  attained  all  desires  
baliṁ haradbhiś cira-loka-pālaiḥ  
tribute  by those bringing  by the age-old  of the worlds  guardians  
kirīṭa-koṭīḍita-pāda-pīṭhaḥ  
of crowns  by the tips  praised  of the feet  the footstool

(33) “He Himself has none equal to Him and none above Him; He is the Lord of the three. Through Svārājya-lakṣmī, the wealth of His own realm, all His desires are fulfilled. The age-old guardians of the worlds bring Him tribute and praise His footstool with the tips of their crowns.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.2.21)

পরম ঈশ্বর কৃষ্ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ৷  
তাতে বড়, তাঁর সম কেহ নাহি আন ॥ ৩৪ ॥

parama īśvara kṛṣṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
supreme  Lord  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān  
tāte baḍa, tāṅra sama keha nāhi āna  
than Him  greater  to Him  equal  anyone  there is not  other

(34) Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord, Bhagavān Himself. There is no one else greater than Him or equal to Him.

ঈশ্বরঃ পরমঃ কৃষ্ণঃ সচ্চিদানন্দবিগ্রহঃ ৷  
অনাদিরাদির্গোবিন্দঃ সর্ব্বকারণকারণম্ ॥ ৩৫ ॥

īśvaraḥ paramaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ  
the Lord  Supreme  Kṛṣṇa  
sac-cid-ānanda-vigrahaḥ  
of being  knowledge  bliss  whose form  
anādir ādir govindaḥ  
without beginning  the beginning  Govinda  
sarva-kāraṇa-kāraṇam  
of all  causes  the cause

(35) “Kṛṣṇa, Govinda, is the Supreme Lord; His form is eternal being, knowledge and bliss. He has no beginning, yet He is the beginning of all, the cause of all causes.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.1)

ব্রহ্মা, বিষ্ণু, হর, — এই সৃষ্ট্যাদি-ঈশ্বর ৷  
তিনে আজ্ঞাকারী কৃষ্ণের, কৃষ্ণ — অধীশ্বর ॥ ৩৬ ॥

brahmā, viṣṇu, hara, — ei sṛṣṭyādi-īśvara  
Brahmā  Viṣṇu  Hara  these  of creation and the rest  the lords  
tine ājñākārī kṛṣṇera, kṛṣṇa — adhīśvara  
the three  carrying out the orders  of Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  the Supreme Lord

(36) Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Hara (Śiva) are the lords of creation, maintenance and destruction. All three carry out Kṛṣṇa’s orders, and Kṛṣṇa is their Supreme Lord.

সৃজামি তন্নিযুক্তোঽহং হরো হরতি তদ্বশঃ ৷  
বিশ্বং পুরুষরূপেণ পরিপাতি ত্রিশক্তিধৃক্ ॥ ৩৭ ॥

sṛjāmi tan-niyukto ’haṁ  
I create  by Him  appointed  I  
haro harati tad-vaśaḥ  
Hara  destroys  under His control  
viśvaṁ puruṣa-rūpeṇa  
the universe  in the form of the Puruṣa  
paripāti tri-śakti-dhṛk  
maintains  three  potencies  the bearer of

(37) “Appointed by Him, I create; under His control Hara (Śiva) destroys; and He, the bearer of the three potencies, maintains the universe in the form of the Puruṣa.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.6.32)

এ সামান্য, ত্র্যধীশ্বরের শুন অর্থ আর ৷  
জগৎকারণ তিন পুরুষাবতার ॥ ৩৮ ॥

e sāmānya, tryadhīśvarera śuna artha āra  
this  is general  of tryadhīśvara  hear  the meaning  another  
jagat-kāraṇa tina puruṣāvatāra  
of the world  the causes  three  Puruṣa-avatāras

(38) That is the general meaning. Now hear another meaning of *tryadhīśvara*, “the Lord of the three”: the three Puruṣa-avatāras, the causes of the world.

মহাবিষ্ণু, পদ্মনাভ, ক্ষীরোদকস্বামী ৷  
এই তিন — স্থূল-সূক্ষ্ম-সর্ব্ব-অন্তর্য্যামী ॥ ৩৯ ॥

mahā-viṣṇu, padmanābha, kṣīrodaka-svāmī  
Mahā-Viṣṇu  Padmanābha  of the Milk Ocean  the Lord  
ei tina — sthūla-sūkṣma-sarva-antaryāmī  
these  three  of the gross  of the subtle  of all  the indwelling Lords

(39) Mahā-Viṣṇu, Padmanābha and the Lord of the Milk Ocean — these three are the indwelling Lords of the gross, of the subtle and of all.

এই তিন — সর্ব্বাশ্রয়, জগৎ-ঈশ্বর ৷  
ইঁহো — কলা-অংশ, যাঁর কৃষ্ণ — অধীশ্বর ॥ ৪০ ॥

ei tina — sarvāśraya, jagat-īśvara  
these  three  the shelter of all  of the world  lords  
iṅho — kalā-aṁśa, yāṅra kṛṣṇa — adhīśvara  
They  kalā  aṁśa  whose  Kṛṣṇa  the Supreme Lord

(40) These three are the shelter of all and the lords of the world. Yet They are only His parts (*kalā* and *aṁśa*), and Kṛṣṇa is Their Supreme Lord.

যস্যৈকনিশ্বসিতকালমথাবলম্ব্য  
জীবন্তি লোমবিলজা জগদণ্ডনাথাঃ ৷  
বিষ্ণুর্মহান্ স ইহ যস্য কলাবিশেষো  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ৪১ ॥

yasyaika-niśvasita-kālam athāvalambya  
whose  one  exhalation’s  span  then  depending on  
jīvanti loma-vila-jā jagad-aṇḍa-nāthāḥ  
live  from the pores  born  of the universes  the lords  
viṣṇur mahān sa iha yasya kalā-viśeṣo  
Viṣṇu  the Great  He  here  whose  kalā  particular  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(41) “The lords of the universes, born from His pores, abide for the span of one of His breaths. That Mahā-Viṣṇu is a particular kalā of Govinda, the original Person, whom I worship.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.48)

এই অর্থ — ‘বাহ্য’, শুন ‘গূঢ়’ অর্থ আর ৷  
তিন আবাস-স্থান কৃষ্ণের শাস্ত্রে খ্যাতি যার ॥ ৪২ ॥

ei artha — ‘bāhya’, śuna ‘gūḍha’ artha āra  
this  meaning  external  hear  hidden  meaning  another  
tina āvāsa-sthāna kṛṣṇera śāstre khyāti yāra  
three  abodes  places  Kṛṣṇa’s  in the scriptures  renown  whose

(42) That meaning is external; now hear the hidden one. Kṛṣṇa has three abodes, renowned in the scriptures.

‘অন্তঃপুর’ — গোলোক-শ্রীবৃন্দাবন ৷  
যাঁহা নিত্যস্থিতি মাতাপিতা-বন্ধুগণ ॥ ৪৩ ॥

‘antaḥpura’ — goloka-śrī-vṛndāvana  
the inner abode  Goloka  Śrī  Vṛndāvana  
yāṅhā nitya-sthiti mātā-pitā-bandhu-gaṇa  
where  eternal  dwelling  mother  father  friends  the host

(43) His inner abode (*antaḥpura*) is Goloka, Śrī Vṛndāvana, where His mother, father and friends dwell eternally.

মধুরৈশ্বর্য্য-মাধুর্য্য-কৃপাদি-ভাণ্ডার ৷  
যোগমায়া দাসী যাঁহা রাসাদি লীলা-সার ॥ ৪৪ ॥

madhuraiśvarya-mādhurya-kṛpādi-bhāṇḍāra  
of sweet majesty  of sweetness  of mercy and the rest  the storehouse  
yogamāyā dāsī yāṅhā rāsādi līlā-sāra  
Yogamāyā  maidservant  where  the rāsa and the rest  of the pastimes  the essence

(44) It is the storehouse of sweet majesty, sweetness, mercy and every other treasure. There Yogamāyā is the maidservant, and there are the *rāsa* and the other pastimes, the essence of all *līlā*.

করুণানিকুরম্বকোমলে মধুরৈশ্বর্য্যবিশেষশালিনি ৷  
জয়তি ব্রজরাজনন্দনে ন হি চিন্তাকণিকাভ্যুদেতি নঃ ॥ ৪৫ ॥

karuṇā-nikuramba-komale  
with multitudes of mercy  tender  
madhuraiśvarya-viśeṣa-śālini  
with sweet majesty  special  graced  
jayati vraja-rāja-nandane  
triumphs (when)  of Vraja  of the king  the son  
na hi cintā-kaṇikābhyudeti naḥ  
not  indeed  of anxiety  a particle  arises  in us

(45) “When the son of the king of Vraja triumphs — He who is tender with abundant mercy and graced with a special sweet majesty — not even a particle of anxiety arises in us.”

তার তলে পরব্যোম — ‘বিষ্ণুলোক’-নাম ৷  
নারায়ণ-আদি অনন্ত স্বরূপের ধাম ॥ ৪৬ ॥

tāra tale paravyoma — ‘viṣṇuloka’-nāma  
its  below  Paravyoma  Viṣṇuloka  by name  
nārāyaṇa-ādi ananta svarūpera dhāma  
of Nārāyaṇa  and the others  endless  forms  the abode

(46) Below it lies Paravyoma, called Viṣṇuloka, the abode of Nārāyaṇa and His other endless forms.

‘মধ্যম-আবাস’ কৃষ্ণের — ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্য-ভাণ্ডার ৷  
অনন্ত স্বরূপে যাঁহা করেন বিহার ॥ ৪৭ ॥

‘madhyama-āvāsa’ kṛṣṇera — ṣaḍ-aiśvarya-bhāṇḍāra  
the middle  abode  Kṛṣṇa’s  of the six opulences  the treasury  
ananta svarūpe yāṅhā karena vihāra  
in endless  forms  where  He performs  pastimes

(47) This is Kṛṣṇa’s middle abode (*madhyama-āvāsa*), the treasury of the six opulences, where He sports in endless forms.

অনন্ত বৈকুণ্ঠ যাঁহা — ভাণ্ডার-কোঠরি ৷  
পারিষদগণে ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্যে আছে ভরি’ ॥ ৪৮ ॥

ananta vaikuṇṭha yāṅhā — bhāṇḍāra-koṭhari  
endless  Vaikuṇṭhas  where  of the treasury  rooms  
pāriṣada-gaṇe ṣaḍ-aiśvarye āche bhari’  
with associates  with the six opulences  are  filled

(48) There the endless Vaikuṇṭhas are the rooms of the treasury, filled with associates and with the six opulences.

গোলোকনাম্নি নিজধাম্নি তলে চ তস্য  
দেবী-মহেশ হরিধামসু তেষু তেষু ৷  
তে তে প্রভাবনিচয়া বিহিতাশ্চ যেন  
গোবিন্দমাদিপুরুষং তমহং ভজামি ॥ ৪৯ ॥

goloka-nāmni nija-dhāmni tale ca tasya  
Goloka  called  own  abode  below  and  of that  
devī-maheśa-hari-dhāmasu teṣu teṣu  
of Devī  of Maheśa  of Hari  in the abodes  in those  in those  
te te prabhāva-nicayā vihitāś ca yena  
those  those  powers  multitudes  were established  and  by whom  
govindam ādi-puruṣaṁ tam ahaṁ bhajāmi  
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship

(49) “Below His own abode, called Goloka, lie the abodes of Devī, Maheśa and Hari, and in each of them He established its own powers. I worship that Govinda, the original Person.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.43)

প্রধান-পরমব্যোম্নোরন্তরে বিরজা নদী ৷  
বেদাঙ্গস্বেদজনিতৈস্তোয়ৈঃ প্রস্রাবিতা শুভা ॥ ৫০ ॥

pradhāna-parama-vyomnor  
of pradhāna  of the supreme  sky  
antare virajā nadī  
between  the Virajā  river  
vedāṅga-sveda-janitais  
of Him whose limbs are the Vedas  by the sweat  produced  
toyaiḥ prasrāvitā śubhā  
by the waters  flowing  auspicious

(50) “Between *pradhāna* and the supreme spiritual sky flows the auspicious river Virajā, streaming with waters born of the sweat of the Lord, whose limbs are the Vedas.” (Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 255.57)

তস্যাঃ পারে পরব্যোম ত্রিপাদ্ভূতং সনাতনম্ ৷  
অমৃতং শাশ্বতং নিত্যমনন্তং পরমং পদম্ ॥ ৫১ ॥

tasyāḥ pāre paravyoma  
of it  on the far shore  Paravyoma  
tripād-bhūtaṁ sanātanam  
three quarters  consisting of  eternal  
amṛtaṁ śāśvataṁ nityam  
immortal  everlasting  eternal  
anantaṁ paramaṁ padam  
endless  supreme  abode

(51) “Beyond it lies Paravyoma, the eternal realm of three quarters (*tripād-bhūta*) — immortal, everlasting, endless, the supreme abode.” (Padma Purāṇa)

তার তলে ‘বাহ্যাবাস’ বিরজার পার ৷  
অনন্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ড যাঁহা কোঠরি অপার ॥ ৫২ ॥

tāra tale ‘bāhyāvāsa’ virajāra pāra  
its  below  the outer abode  of the Virajā  beyond  
ananta brahmāṇḍa yāṅhā koṭhari apāra  
endless  universes  where  rooms  countless

(52) Below it, beyond the Virajā, lies the outer abode (*bāhyāvāsa*), whose countless rooms are the endless universes.

‘দেবীধাম’ নাম তার, জীব যার বাসী ৷  
জগল্লক্ষ্মী রাখে, যাঁহা রহে মায়া-দাসী ॥ ৫৩ ॥

‘devī-dhāma’ nāma tāra, jīva yāra vāsī  
Devī-dhāma  the name  its  the jīvas  whose  residents  
jagal-lakṣmī rākhe, yāṅhā rahe māyā-dāsī  
Jagal-lakṣmī  maintains  where  remains  Māyā  maidservant

(53) Its name is Devī-dhāma, and its residents are the *jīvas*. Jagal-lakṣmī maintains it, and there Māyā remains as a maidservant.

এই তিন ধামের হয় কৃষ্ণ অধীশ্বর ৷  
গোলোক-পরব্যোম — প্রকৃতির পর ॥ ৫৪ ॥

ei tina dhāmera haya kṛṣṇa adhīśvara  
of these  three  abodes  is  Kṛṣṇa  the Supreme Lord  
goloka-paravyoma — prakṛtira para  
Goloka  Paravyoma  of material nature  beyond

(54) Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord of these three abodes. Goloka and Paravyoma lie beyond material nature.

চিচ্ছক্তিবিভূতি-ধাম — ত্রিপাদৈশ্বর্য্য-নাম ৷  
মায়িক বিভূতি — একপাদ অভিধান ॥ ৫৫ ॥

cic-chakti-vibhūti-dhāma — tripād-aiśvarya-nāma  
of the spiritual potency  of the opulence  the abode  of three quarters  opulence  the name  
māyika vibhūti — eka-pāda abhidhāna  
of māyā  the opulence  one  quarter  is called

(55) The abode of the opulence of His spiritual potency is called *tripād-aiśvarya*, the opulence of three quarters; the opulence of māyā is called *ekapāda*, one quarter.

ত্রিপাদ্বিভূতের্ধামত্বাৎ ত্রিপাদ্ভূতং হি তৎ পদম্ ৷  
বিভূতির্মায়িকী সর্ব্বা প্রোক্তা পাদাত্মিকা যতঃ ॥ ৫৬ ॥

tri-pād-vibhūter dhāmatvāt  
of three  quarters  of the opulence  being the abode  
tri-pād-bhūtaṁ hi tat padam  
of three  quarters  consisting  indeed  that  abode  
vibhūtir māyikī sarvā  
opulence  of māyā  all  
proktā pādātmikā yataḥ  
is declared  as one quarter  because

(56) “That realm is called *tripād-bhūta*, ‘consisting of three quarters’, because it is the abode of the opulence of three quarters; all the opulence of māyā is declared to be one quarter.” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.5.286)

ত্রিপাদবিভূতি কৃষ্ণের — বাক্য-অগোচর ৷  
একপাদ বিভূতির শুনহ বিস্তার ॥ ৫৭ ॥

tripāda-vibhūti kṛṣṇera — vākya-agocara  
three quarters  opulence  Kṛṣṇa’s  to words  inaccessible  
eka-pāda vibhūtira śunaha vistāra  
of one  quarter  of the opulence  hear  the description

(57) Kṛṣṇa’s opulence of three quarters lies beyond words. Hear now a description of the opulence of one quarter.

অনন্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের যত ব্রহ্মা-রুদ্রগণ ৷  
চিরলোকপাল-শব্দে তাঁহার গণন ॥ ৫৮ ॥

ananta brahmāṇḍera yata brahmā-rudra-gaṇa  
of endless  universes  all  of the Brahmās  of the Rudras  the hosts  
cira-loka-pāla-śabde tāṅhāra gaṇana  
of the age-old  of the worlds  guardians  by the word  their  reckoning

(58) All the Brahmās and Rudras of the endless universes are meant by the words “the age-old guardians of the worlds” (*cira-loka-pāla*).

একদিন দ্বারকাতে কৃষ্ণ দেখিবারে ৷  
ব্রহ্মা আইলা, — দ্বারপাল জানাইল কৃষ্ণেরে ॥ ৫৯ ॥

eka-dina dvārakāte kṛṣṇa dekhibāre  
one day  in Dvārakā  Kṛṣṇa  to see  
brahmā āilā, — dvāra-pāla jānāila kṛṣṇere  
Brahmā  came  the doorkeeper  informed  Kṛṣṇa

(59) One day Brahmā came to Dvārakā to see Kṛṣṇa, and the doorkeeper informed Kṛṣṇa.

কৃষ্ণ কহেন, — ‘কোন্ ব্রহ্মা, কি নাম তাহার ?’  
দ্বারী আসি’ ব্রহ্মারে পুছে আর বার ॥ ৬০ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahena, — kon brahmā, ki nāma tāhāra?  
Kṛṣṇa  says  which  Brahmā  what  name  his  
dvārī āsi’ brahmāre puche āra bāra  
the doorkeeper  coming  Brahmā  asks  once  again

(60) Kṛṣṇa said, “Which Brahmā? What is his name?” The doorkeeper went back and asked Brahmā once more.

বিস্মিত হঞা ব্রহ্মা দ্বারীকে কহিলা ৷  
‘কহ গিয়া সনক-পিতা চতুর্ম্মুখ আইলা’ ॥ ৬১ ॥

vismita hañā brahmā dvārīke kahilā  
astonished  becoming  Brahmā  to the doorkeeper  said  
kaha giyā sanaka-pitā caturmukha āilā  
tell  going  of Sanaka  the father  the four-headed  has come

(61) Astonished, Brahmā told the doorkeeper, “Go and tell Him that the four-headed Brahmā, the father of Sanaka, has come.”

কৃষ্ণে জানাঞা দ্বারী ব্রহ্মারে লঞা গেলা ৷  
কৃষ্ণের চরণে ব্রহ্মা দণ্ডবৎ কৈলা ॥ ৬২ ॥

kṛṣṇe jānāñā dvārī brahmāre lañā gelā  
to Kṛṣṇa  having informed  the doorkeeper  Brahmā  taking  went  
kṛṣṇera caraṇe brahmā daṇḍavat kailā  
Kṛṣṇa’s  at the feet  Brahmā  obeisance  made

(62) The doorkeeper informed Kṛṣṇa and brought Brahmā in, and Brahmā fell prostrate at Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

কৃষ্ণ মান্য-পূজা করি’ তাঁরে প্রশ্ন কৈল ৷  
‘কি লাগি’ তোমার ইঁহা আগমন হৈল ?’ ॥ ৬৩ ॥

kṛṣṇa mānya-pūjā kari’ tāṅre praśna kaila  
Kṛṣṇa  respect  honour  showing  to him  a question  put  
ki lāgi’ tomāra iṅhā āgamana haila?  
what  for  your  here  coming  has been

(63) Kṛṣṇa honoured him with due respect and asked, “For what purpose have you come here?”

ব্রহ্মা কহে, — ‘তাহা পাছে করিব নিবেদন ৷  
এক সংশয় মনে হয়, করহ ছেদন ॥ ৬৪ ॥

brahmā kahe, — tāhā pāche kariba nivedana  
Brahmā  says  that  later  I shall make  submission  
eka saṁśaya mane haya, karaha chedana  
one  doubt  in my mind  is  make  cutting

(64) Brahmā said, “I will tell You that later. First, please cut away a doubt that has arisen in my mind.

‘কোন্ ব্রহ্মা ? পুছিলে তুমি কোন্ অভিপ্রায়ে ?  
আমা বই জগতে আর কোন্ ব্রহ্মা হয়ে ?’ ॥ ৬৫ ॥

kon brahmā? puchile tumi kon abhiprāye?  
which  Brahmā  You asked  You  with what  intention  
āmā bai jagate āra kon brahmā haye?  
me  besides  in the universe  another  which  Brahmā  is there

(65) With what intention did You ask, ‘Which Brahmā?’ Besides me, what other Brahmā is there in the universe?”

শুনি’ হাসি’ কৃষ্ণ তবে করিলেন ধ্যানে ৷  
অসংখ্য ব্রহ্মার গণ আইলা ততক্ষণে ॥ ৬৬ ॥

śuni’ hāsi’ kṛṣṇa tabe karilena dhyāne  
hearing  smiling  Kṛṣṇa  then  entered  meditation  
asaṁkhya brahmāra gaṇa āilā tata-kṣaṇe  
countless  of Brahmās  a host  came  at that very moment

(66) Hearing this, Kṛṣṇa smiled and became absorbed in meditation, and at that very moment countless Brahmās arrived.

দশ-বিশ-শত-সহস্র-অযুত লক্ষ-বদন ৷  
কোট্যর্ব্বুদ মুখ কারো, না যায় গণন ॥ ৬৭ ॥

daśa-biśa-śata-sahasra-ayuta lakṣa-vadana  
with ten  twenty  a hundred  a thousand  ten thousand  a hundred thousand  faces  
koṭy-arbuda mukha kāro, nā yāya gaṇana  
ten million  a hundred million  faces  of some  not  can be  counted

(67) Some had ten faces, some twenty, a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand, a hundred thousand; some had tens and hundreds of millions of faces — they could not be counted.

রুদ্রগণ আইলা লক্ষ-কোটি-বদন ৷  
ইন্দ্রগণ আইলা লক্ষ কোটি-নয়ন ॥ ৬৮ ॥

rudra-gaṇa āilā lakṣa-koṭi-vadana  
the Rudras  host  came  with hundreds of thousands  millions of  faces  
indra-gaṇa āilā lakṣa koṭi-nayana  
the Indras  host  came  with hundreds of thousands  millions of  eyes

(68) Rudras came with hundreds of thousands and millions of faces, and Indras came with hundreds of thousands and millions of eyes.

দেখি’ চতুর্ম্মুখ ব্রহ্মা ফাঁপর হইলা ৷  
হস্তিগণ-মধ্যে যেন শশক রহিলা ॥ ৬৯ ॥

dekhi’ caturmukha brahmā phāṅpara hailā  
seeing  four-headed  Brahmā  bewildered  became  
hasti-gaṇa-madhye yena śaśaka rahilā  
of elephants  a herd  amid  as if  a rabbit  remained

(69) Seeing this, four-headed Brahmā was bewildered, like a rabbit standing amid a herd of elephants.

আসি’ সব ব্রহ্মা কৃষ্ণ-পাদপীঠ-আগে ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ করিতে মুকুট পাদপীঠে লাগে ॥ ৭০ ॥

āsi’ saba brahmā kṛṣṇa-pāda-pīṭha-āge  
coming  all  Brahmās  Kṛṣṇa’s  of the feet  the footstool  before  
daṇḍavat karite mukuṭa pāda-pīṭhe lāge  
obeisance  while offering  crowns  the footstool  touch

(70) All the Brahmās came before Kṛṣṇa’s footstool, and as they offered obeisance their crowns touched it.

কৃষ্ণের অচিন্ত্য-শক্তি লিখিতে কেহ নারে ৷  
যত ব্রহ্মা, তত মূর্ত্তি একই শরীরে ॥ ৭১ ॥

kṛṣṇera acintya-śakti likhite keha nāre  
Kṛṣṇa’s  inconceivable  power  to describe  anyone  cannot  
yata brahmā, tata mūrti ekai śarīre  
as many  Brahmās  so many  forms  in one and the same  body

(71) No one can describe Kṛṣṇa’s inconceivable power: as many Brahmās as there were, so many forms were in His one body.

পাদপীঠ-মুকুটাগ্র-সংঘট্টে উঠে ধ্বনি ৷  
পাদপীঠে স্তুতি করে মুকুট হেন জানি ॥ ৭২ ॥

pāda-pīṭha-mukuṭāgra-saṁghaṭṭe uṭhe dhvani  
of the footstool  of the crowns’  tips  from the striking  arises  a sound  
pāda-pīṭhe stuti kare mukuṭa hena jāni  
to the footstool  praise  offer  the crowns  so  I think

(72) As the tips of the crowns struck the footstool, a sound arose; it seemed to me that the crowns were offering praise to the footstool.

যোড়-হাতে ব্রহ্মা-রুদ্রাদি করয়ে স্তবন ৷  
“বড় কৃপা করিলা প্রভু, দেখাইলা চরণ ॥ ৭৩ ॥

yoḍa-hāte brahmā-rudrādi karaye stavana  
with joined  palms  Brahmās  Rudras and others  offer  praise  
baḍa kṛpā karilā prabhu, dekhāilā caraṇa  
great  mercy  You have shown  O Lord  You showed  Your feet

(73) With joined palms the Brahmās, the Rudras and the others offered praise: “O Lord, You have shown great mercy in revealing Your feet.

ভাগ্য, মোরে বোলাইলা ‘দাস’ অঙ্গীকরি’ ৷  
কোন্ আজ্ঞা হয়, তাহা করি শিরে ধরি’” ॥ ৭৪ ॥

bhāgya, more bolāilā ‘dāsa’ aṅgīkari’  
fortune  me  You called  as servant  accepting  
kon ājñā haya, tāhā kari śire dhari’  
what  order  there is  that  I shall do  on my head  bearing

(74) It is my good fortune that You have called me, accepting me as Your servant. Whatever Your order may be, I will carry it out, placing it on my head.”

কৃষ্ণ কহে, — “তোমা-সবা দেখিতে চিত্ত হৈল ৷  
তাহা লাগি’ এক ঠাঞি সবা বোলাইল ॥ ৭৫ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahe, — tomā-sabā dekhite citta haila  
Kṛṣṇa  says  you  all  to see  the desire  arose  
tāhā lāgi’ eka ṭhāñi sabā bolāila  
that  for the sake of  in one  place  all  I called

(75) Kṛṣṇa said, “I wished to see you all, and so I have called you all together in one place.

সুখী হও সবে, কিছু নাহি দৈত্য-ভয় ?”  
তারা কহে, — ‘তোমার প্রসাদে সর্ব্বত্রই জয় ॥ ৭৬ ॥

sukhī hao sabe, kichu nāhi daitya-bhaya?  
happy  are (you)  all  any  is there not  of the demons  fear  
tārā kahe, — tomāra prasāde sarvatra-i jaya  
they  say  by Your  grace  everywhere  victory

(76) Are you all happy? Have you no fear of the demons?” They replied, “By Your grace we are victorious everywhere.

সম্প্রতি পৃথিবীতে যেবা হৈয়াছিল ভার ৷  
অবতীর্ণ হঞা তাহা করিলা সংহার’ ॥ ৭৭ ॥

samprati pṛthivīte yebā haiyāchila bhāra  
now  on the earth  whatever  had become  burden  
avatīrṇa hañā tāhā karilā saṁhāra  
descended  having become  that  You made  destruction

(77) Whatever burden had lately come upon the earth, You have descended and destroyed it.”

দ্বারকাদি — বিভুতির এই ত’ প্রমাণ ৷  
‘আমারই ব্রহ্মাণ্ডে কৃষ্ণ’ সবার হৈল জ্ঞান ॥ ৭৮ ॥

dvārakādi — vibhutira ei ta’ pramāṇa  
Dvārakā and the rest  of the opulence  this  indeed  the proof  
‘āmārai brahmāṇḍe kṛṣṇa’ sabāra haila jñāna  
in my own  universe  Kṛṣṇa  of all  was  the understanding

(78) This is the proof of the opulence (*vibhūti*) of Dvārakā and Kṛṣṇa’s other abodes: each of them understood, “Kṛṣṇa is in my own universe.”

কৃষ্ণ-সহ দ্বারকা-বৈভব অনুভব হৈল ৷  
একত্র মিলনে কেহ কাহো না দেখিল ॥ ৭৯ ॥

kṛṣṇa-saha dvārakā-vaibhava anubhava haila  
with Kṛṣṇa  of Dvārakā  the splendour  experience  there was  
ekatra milane keha kāho nā dekhila  
in one place  in the meeting  anyone  anyone  not  saw

(79) Each experienced the splendour of Dvārakā together with Kṛṣṇa; yet, though all met in one place, none of them saw any other.

তবে কৃষ্ণ সর্ব্ব-ব্রহ্মাগণে বিদায় দিলা ৷  
দণ্ডবৎ হঞা সবে নিজ-ঘরে গেলা ॥ ৮০ ॥

tabe kṛṣṇa sarva-brahmā-gaṇe vidāya dilā  
then  Kṛṣṇa  to all the Brahmās  leave  gave  
daṇḍavat hañā sabe nija-ghare gelā  
prostrate  becoming  all  to their own homes  went

(80) Then Kṛṣṇa gave all the Brahmās leave to go, and they all bowed down flat and returned to their own homes.

দেখি’ চতুর্ম্মুখ ব্রহ্মার হৈল চমৎকার ৷  
কৃষ্ণের চরণে আসি’ কৈলা নমস্কার ॥ ৮১ ॥

dekhi’ caturmukha brahmāra haila camatkāra  
seeing  of the four-faced  Brahmā  there was  amazement  
kṛṣṇera caraṇe āsi’ kailā namaskāra  
of Kṛṣṇa  at the feet  coming  made  obeisance

(81) Seeing this, the four-faced Brahmā was amazed. He came to Kṛṣṇa’s feet and offered obeisance.

ব্রহ্মা বলে, — ‘পূর্ব্বে আমি যে নিশ্চয় করিলুঁ ৷  
তার উদাহরণ আমি আজি ত’ দেখিলুঁ’ ॥ ৮২ ॥

brahmā bale, — pūrve āmi ye niścaya kariluṅ  
Brahmā  says  before  I  what  conclusion  made  
tāra udāharaṇa āmi āji ta’ dekhiluṅ  
of that  the example  I  today  indeed  saw

(82) Brahmā said, “What I concluded before, today I have seen demonstrated before my eyes.

জানন্ত এব জানন্তু কিং বহূক্ত্যা ন মে প্রভো ৷  
মনসো বপুষো বাচো বৈভবং তব গোচরঃ ॥ ৮৩ ॥

jānanta eva jānantu  
the knowing  indeed  let them know  
kiṁ bahūktyā na me prabho  
what  with many words  not  my  O Lord  
manaso vapuṣo vāco  
of mind  of body  of speech  
vaibhavaṁ tava gocaraḥ  
glory  Your  within the range

(83) “Let those who know You know. What use are many words? O Lord, Your glory lies beyond the reach of my mind, my body and my speech.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.38)

কৃষ্ণ কহে, “এই ব্রহ্মাণ্ড পঞ্চাশৎ-কোটি যোজন ৷  
অতি ক্ষুদ্র, তাতে তোমার চারি বদন ॥ ৮৪ ॥

kṛṣṇa kahe, “ei brahmāṇḍa pañcāśat-koṭi yojana  
Kṛṣṇa  says  this  universe  fifty crore  yojanas  
ati kṣudra, tāte tomāra cāri vadana  
very  small  therefore  your  four  faces

(84) Kṛṣṇa said, “This universe measures fifty crore *yojanas*. It is very small, and so you have only four faces.

কোন ব্রহ্মাণ্ড শতকোটি, কোন লক্ষকোটি ৷  
কোন নিযুতকোটি, কোন কোটি-কোটি ॥ ৮৫ ॥

kona brahmāṇḍa śata-koṭi, kona lakṣa-koṭi  
some  universe  a hundred crore  some  a hundred thousand crore  
kona niyuta-koṭi, kona koṭi-koṭi  
some  a million crore  some  crore upon crore

(85) Some universes measure a hundred crore *yojanas*, some a hundred thousand crore, some a million crore, some crore upon crore.

ব্রহ্মাণ্ডানুরূপ ব্রহ্মার শরীর-বদন ৷  
এইরূপে পালি আমি ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের গণ ॥ ৮৬ ॥

brahmāṇḍānurūpa brahmāra śarīra-vadana  
according to the universe  of Brahmā  body and faces  
ei-rūpe pāli āmi brahmāṇḍera gaṇa  
in this way  maintain  I  of the universes  the multitude

(86) A Brahmā’s body and faces correspond to the size of his universe. In this way I maintain the countless universes.

‘একপাদ বিভূতি’, ইহার নাহি পরিমাণ ৷  
‘ত্রিপাদ বিভূতির’ কেবা করে পরিমাণ” ॥ ৮৭ ॥

‘eka-pāda vibhūti’, ihāra nāhi parimāṇa  
the one-quarter  opulence  of it  there is not  measure  
‘tri-pāda vibhūtira’ kebā kare parimāṇa  
of the three-quarter  opulence  who  makes  measurement

(87) The *eka-pāda vibhūti*, the one quarter of My opulence, has no measure. Who, then, can measure the *tri-pāda vibhūti*, the other three quarters?”

তস্যাঃ পারে পরব্যোম ত্রিপাদ্ভূতং সনাতনম্ ৷  
অমৃতং শাশ্বতং নিত্যমনন্তং পরমং পদম্ ॥ ৮৮ ॥

tasyāḥ pāre paravyoma  
of it  on the far shore  Paravyoma  
tripād-bhūtaṁ sanātanam  
three-quarters  consisting of  eternal  
amṛtaṁ śāśvataṁ nityam  
immortal  everlasting  constant  
anantaṁ paramaṁ padam  
infinite  supreme  abode

(88) “On the far shore of the Virajā lies Paravyoma, the eternal three quarters of His opulence: immortal, everlasting and constant, infinite, the supreme abode.” (Padma Purāṇa)

তবে কৃষ্ণ ব্রহ্মারে দিলেন বিদায় ৷  
কৃষ্ণের বিভূতি-স্বরূপ জানান না যায় ॥ ৮৯ ॥

tabe kṛṣṇa brahmāre dilena vidāya  
then  Kṛṣṇa  to Brahmā  gave  leave  
kṛṣṇera vibhūti-svarūpa jānāna nā yāya  
of Kṛṣṇa  of the opulence  the nature  to know  not  is possible

(89) Then Kṛṣṇa gave Brahmā leave to go. The nature of Kṛṣṇa’s opulence cannot be known.

’ত্র্যধীশ্বর’-শব্দের অর্থ ‘গূঢ়’ আর হয় ৷  
’ত্রি’-শব্দে কৃষ্ণের তিন লোক কয় ॥ ৯০ ॥

’tryadhīśvara’-śabdera artha ‘gūḍha’ āra haya  
tryadhīśvara  of the word  the meaning  a hidden  another  there is  
’tri’-śabde kṛṣṇera tina loka kaya  
by the word tri  Kṛṣṇa’s  three  worlds  are meant

(90) The word *tryadhīśvara* (“Lord of the three”) has yet another, hidden meaning: by the word *tri* (“three”) Kṛṣṇa’s three abodes are meant.

গোলোকাখ্য গোকুল, মথুরা, দ্বারাবতী ৷  
এই তিন লোকে কৃষ্ণের সহজে নিত্য-স্থিতি ॥ ৯১ ॥

golokākhya gokula, mathurā, dvārāvatī  
called Goloka  Gokula  Mathurā  Dvārāvatī  
ei tina loke kṛṣṇera sahaje nitya-sthiti  
in these  three  worlds  Kṛṣṇa’s  natural  eternal residence

(91) Gokula, which is called Goloka, Mathurā and Dvārāvatī (Dvārakā): in these three abodes Kṛṣṇa naturally and eternally resides.

অন্তরঙ্গ-পূর্ণৈশ্বর্য্যপূর্ণ তিন ধাম ৷  
তিনের অধীশ্বর — কৃষ্ণ স্বয়ং ভগবান্ ॥ ৯২ ॥

antaraṅga-pūrṇaiśvarya-pūrṇa tina dhāma  
internal  with full majesty  filled  three  abodes  
tinera adhīśvara — kṛṣṇa svayaṁ bhagavān  
of the three  the supreme master  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān Himself

(92) These three abodes are internal and filled with full majesty, and their supreme master is Kṛṣṇa, Bhagavān Himself.

পূর্ব্ব-উক্ত ব্রহ্মাণ্ডের যত দিক্পাল ৷  
অনন্ত বৈকুণ্ঠাবরণ, চিরলোকপাল ॥ ৯৩ ॥

তাঁ-সবার মুকুট কৃষ্ণপাদপীঠ-আগে ৷  
দণ্ডবৎকালে তার মণি পীঠে লাগে ॥ ৯৪ ॥

pūrva-ukta brahmāṇḍera yata dik-pāla  
previously  mentioned  of the universes  all  guardians of the quarters  
ananta vaikuṇṭhāvaraṇa, cira-loka-pāla  
of the infinite  Vaikuṇṭhas and coverings  the eternal  rulers of the worlds  
tāṅ-sabāra mukuṭa kṛṣṇa-pāda-pīṭha-āge  
their all  crowns  of Kṛṣṇa  of the feet  the footstool  before  
daṇḍavat-kāle tāra maṇi pīṭhe lāge  
of prostration  at the time  their  jewels  on the footstool  touch

(93–94) All the guardians of the quarters in the universes I spoke of before, and the eternal rulers of the infinite Vaikuṇṭhas and of the coverings of the universes, bow down before Kṛṣṇa’s footstool, and as they prostrate themselves the jewels of their crowns touch it.

মণি পীঠে ঠেকাঠেকি, উঠে ঝন্ঝনি ৷  
পীঠের স্তুতি করে মুকুট — হেন জানি ॥ ৯৫ ॥

maṇi pīṭhe ṭhekāṭheki, uṭhe jhanjhani  
jewels  on the footstool  knocking together  arises  a jingling  
pīṭhera stuti kare mukuṭa — hena jāni  
of the footstool  praise  make  the crowns  so  I know

(95) The jewels knock together on the footstool and a jingling arises: it is as if, I think, the crowns were singing the footstool’s praises.

নিজ-চিচ্ছক্ত্যে কৃষ্ণ নিত্য বিরাজমান ৷  
চিচ্ছক্তি-সম্পত্তির ‘ষড়ৈশ্বর্য্য’ নাম ॥ ৯৬ ॥

nija-cic-chaktye kṛṣṇa nitya virājamāna  
with His own spiritual potency  Kṛṣṇa  eternally  is present  
cic-chakti-sampattira ‘ṣaḍaiśvarya’ nāma  
of the spiritual potency  the wealth  the six opulences  the name

(96) Kṛṣṇa is eternally present with His own spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*), and the wealth of that potency is called the “six opulences” (*ṣaḍaiśvarya*).

সেই স্বারাজ্যলক্ষ্মী করে নিত্য পূর্ণকাম ৷  
অতএব বেদে কহে ‘স্বয়ং ভগবান্’ ॥ ৯৭ ॥

sei svārājya-lakṣmī kare nitya pūrṇa kāma  
that  royal fortune  makes  eternally  fulfilled in desire  
ataeva vede kahe ‘svayaṁ bhagavān’  
therefore  in the Vedas  they call (Him)  Bhagavān Himself

(97) That royal fortune (*svārājya-lakṣmī*) keeps Him eternally fulfilled in every desire, and therefore the Vedas call Him “Bhagavān Himself.”

কৃষ্ণের ঐশ্বর্য্য — অপার অমৃতের সিন্ধু ৷  
অবগাহিতে নারি’ তার ছুইলুঁ এক বিন্দু ॥ ৯৮ ॥

kṛṣṇera aiśvarya — apāra amṛtera sindhu  
of Kṛṣṇa  the majesty  a boundless  of nectar  ocean  
avagāhite nāri’ tāra chuiluṅ eka bindu  
to plunge in  unable  of it  I have touched  one  drop

(98) Kṛṣṇa’s majesty is a boundless ocean of nectar. Unable to plunge into it, I have touched but a single drop.”

ঐশ্বর্য্য কহিতে প্রভুর কৃষ্ণস্ফূর্ত্তি হৈল ৷  
মাধুর্য্যে মজিল মন, এক শ্লোক পড়িল ॥ ৯৯ ॥

aiśvarya kahite prabhura kṛṣṇa-sphūrti haila  
majesty  while speaking of  in the Lord  the manifestation of Kṛṣṇa  there was  
mādhurye majila mana, eka śloka paḍila  
in sweetness  sank  the mind  one  verse  He recited

(99) As the Lord spoke of majesty, Kṛṣṇa was revealed within His heart. His mind sank into sweetness, and He recited a verse.

যন্মর্ত্ত্যলীলৌপয়িকং স্বযোগ-  
মায়াবলং দর্শয়তা গৃহীতম্ ৷  
বিস্মাপনং স্বস্য চ সৌভগর্দ্ধেঃ  
পরং পদং ভূষণভূষণাঙ্গম্ ॥ ১০০ ॥

yan martya-līlaupayikaṁ sva-yoga-  
which  suited to pastimes among mortals  His own yoga-  
māyā-balaṁ darśayatā gṛhītam  
-māyā’s  power  by Him displaying  was assumed  
vismāpanaṁ svasya ca saubhagardheḥ  
astonishing  to Himself  even  of the wealth of fortune  
paraṁ padaṁ bhūṣaṇa-bhūṣaṇāṅgam  
the supreme  abode  of ornaments  the ornament  whose limbs

(100) “To display the power of His own Yogamāyā, He assumed a form suited to His pastimes among mortals — a form that astonished even Himself, the supreme abode of all good fortune, whose limbs are the ornament of ornaments.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.2.12)

কৃষ্ণের যতেক খেলা, সর্ব্বোত্তম নরলীলা,  
নরবপু তাহার স্বরূপ ৷  
গোপবেশ, বেণুকর, নবকিশোর, নটবর,  
নরলীলার হয় অনুরূপ ॥ ১০১ ॥

kṛṣṇera yateka khelā, sarvottama nara-līlā,  
Kṛṣṇa’s  all  pastimes  the highest  the human-like pastime  
nara-vapu tāhāra svarūpa  
a human-like body  His  own form  
gopa-veśa, veṇu-kara, nava-kiśora, naṭa-vara,  
dressed as a cowherd  flute in hand  freshly youthful  the best of dancers  
nara-līlāra haya anurūpa  
for the human-like pastime  is  fitting

(101) Of all Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes the highest is His human-like pastime (*nara-līlā*), and a human-like form is His own true form. Dressed as a cowherd boy, flute in hand, ever freshly youthful, the finest of dancers — this form perfectly suits His human-like pastimes.

কৃষ্ণের মধুর রূপ শুন, সনাতন ৷  
যে রূপের এক কণ, ডুবায় যে ত্রিভুবন,  
সর্ব্ব প্রাণী করে আকর্ষণ ॥ ১০২ ॥

kṛṣṇera madhura rūpa śuna, sanātana  
Kṛṣṇa’s  sweet  form  hear  O Sanātana  
ye rūpera eka kaṇa, ḍubāya ye tribhuvana,  
of which  form  one  particle  floods  that  the three worlds  
sarva prāṇī kare ākarṣaṇa  
all  living beings  makes  attraction

(102) Hear, Sanātana, of Kṛṣṇa’s sweet form, a single particle of which floods the three worlds and draws all living beings to itself.

যোগমায়া চিচ্ছক্তি, বিশুদ্ধসত্ত্ব-পরিণতি,  
তার শক্তি লোকে দেখাইতে ৷  
এই রূপ-রতন, ভক্তগণের গূঢ়ধন,  
প্রকট কৈলা নিত্যলীলা হৈতে ॥ ১০৩ ॥

yogamāyā cic-chakti, viśuddha-sattva-pariṇati,  
Yogamāyā  the spiritual potency  of pure sattva  the transformation  
tāra śakti loke dekhāite  
her  power  to the world  to show  
ei rūpa-ratana, bhakta-gaṇera gūḍha-dhana,  
this  form  jewel  of the devotees  the hidden treasure  
prakaṭa kailā nitya-līlā haite  
manifest  He made  the eternal pastimes  from

(103) Yogamāyā is the spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*), a transformation of pure *sattva* (*viśuddha-sattva*). To show her power to the world, He brought this jewel of a form, the hidden treasure of the devotees, into the manifest world from His eternal pastimes.

রূপ দেখি’ আপনার, কৃষ্ণের হৈল চমৎকার,  
আস্বাদিতে মনে উঠে কাম ৷  
‘স্বসৌভাগ্য’ যাঁর নাম, সৌন্দর্য্যাদি গুণগ্রাম,  
এইরূপ নিত্য তাঁর ধাম ॥ ১০৪ ॥

rūpa dekhi’ āpanāra, kṛṣṇera haila camatkāra,  
form  seeing  His own  in Kṛṣṇa  there was  amazement  
āsvādite mane uṭhe kāma  
to taste  in the mind  arises  desire  
‘sva-saubhāgya’ yāṅra nāma, saundaryādi guṇa-grāma,  
His own good fortune  whose  name  beauty and the rest  of qualities  the host  
ei-rūpa nitya tāṅra dhāma  
this form  eternal  their  abode

(104) Seeing His own form, Kṛṣṇa is amazed, and a desire to taste it rises in His mind. His own good fortune (*sva-saubhāgya*), the host of qualities beginning with beauty, has this form as its eternal abode.

ভূষণের ভূষণ অঙ্গ, তাঁহে ললিত ত্রিভঙ্গ,  
তাহার উপর ভ্রূধনু-নর্ত্তন ৷  
তেরছে নেত্রান্ত বাণ, তার দৃঢ় সন্ধান,  
বিন্ধে রাধা-গোপীগণ-মন ॥ ১০৫ ॥

bhūṣaṇera bhūṣaṇa aṅga, tāṅhe lalita tri-bhaṅga,  
of ornaments  the ornament  the body  in it  graceful  threefold bending  
tāhāra upara bhrūdhanu-nartana  
of that  above  of the bow of the brows  the dance  
terache netrānta bāṇa, tāra dṛḍha sandhāna,  
askance  of the eye-corners  the arrows  their  firm  aim  
vindhe rādhā-gopī-gaṇa-mana  
pierce  of Rādhā  of the gopīs  of the host  the hearts

(105) His body is the ornament of ornaments, and in it is the graceful threefold bending (*tribhaṅga*). Above it dances the bow of His brows; the arrows of His sidelong glances, firmly aimed, pierce the hearts of Rādhā and the gopīs.

ব্রহ্মাণ্ডোপরি পরব্যোম, তাঁহা যে স্বরূপগণ,  
তাঁ-সবার বলে হরে মন ৷  
পতিব্রতা শিরোমণি, যাঁরে কহে বেদবাণী,  
আকর্ষয়ে সেই লক্ষ্মীগণ ॥ ১০৬ ॥

brahmāṇḍopari paravyoma, tāṅhā ye svarūpa-gaṇa,  
above the universes  Paravyoma  there  which  divine forms  
tāṅ-sabāra bale hare mana  
of them all  by force  steals  the minds  
pati-vratā śiromaṇi, yāṅre kahe veda-vāṇī,  
of chaste wives  the crest-jewel  whom  calls  the word of the Vedas  
ākarṣaye sei lakṣmī-gaṇa  
attracts  those  Lakṣmīs

(106) Above the universes lies Paravyoma; this form forcibly steals the minds of all the divine forms (*svarūpas*) there. It attracts even the Lakṣmīs, whom the word of the Vedas calls the crest-jewels of chaste wives.

চড়ি’ গোপী-মনোরথে, মন্মথের মন মথে,  
নাম ধরে ‘মদনমোহন’ ৷  
জিনি’ পঞ্চশর-দর্প, স্বয়ং নবকন্দর্প,  
রাস করে লঞা গোপীগণ ॥ ১০৭ ॥

caḍi’ gopī-manorathe, manmathera mana mathe,  
mounted  on the gopīs’  chariot of desire  of Cupid  the mind  churns  
nāma dhare ‘madana-mohana’  
name  He bears  Madana-mohana  
jini’ pañcaśara-darpa, svayaṁ nava-kandarpa,  
conquering  of the five-arrowed one  the pride  Himself  a new Cupid  
rāsa kare lañā gopī-gaṇa  
the rāsa  performs  taking  the gopīs

(107) Mounted on the chariot of the gopīs’ heartfelt desire, He churns the mind of Cupid himself, and so He bears the name Madana-mohana, the enchanter of Cupid. Conquering the pride of the five-arrowed god, He, Himself a new Cupid (*nava-kandarpa*), performs the *rāsa* dance with the gopīs.

নিজ-সম সখা-সঙ্গে, গোগণ-চারণ-রঙ্গে,  
বৃন্দাবনে স্বচ্ছন্দ বিহার ৷  
যাঁর বেণু-ধ্বনি শুনি’ স্থাবর-জঙ্গম প্রাণী,  
পুলক, কম্প, অশ্রু বহে ধার ॥ ১০৮ ॥

nija-sama sakhā-saṅge, go-gaṇa-cāraṇa-raṅge,  
equal to Himself  with friends  of the cows  of the herds  of grazing  in the joy  
vṛndāvane svacchanda vihāra  
in Vṛndāvana  free  sport  
yāṅra veṇu-dhvani śuni’ sthāvara-jaṅgama prāṇī,  
whose  flute-sound  hearing  unmoving and moving  beings  
pulaka, kampa, aśru vahe dhāra  
thrill  trembling  of tears  flow  streams

(108) With friends equal to Himself, in the joy of grazing the cows, He sports freely in Vṛndāvana. Hearing the sound of His flute, all beings, unmoving and moving, thrill and tremble, and tears flow from them in streams.

মুক্তাহার — বকপাঁতি, ইন্দ্রধনু-পিঞ্ছ তথি,  
পীতাম্বর — বিজলী সঞ্চার ৷  
কৃষ্ণ নব-জলধর, জগৎ-শস্য-উপর,  
বরিষয়ে লীলামৃত-ধার ॥ ১০৯ ॥

muktā-hāra — baka-pāṅti, indra-dhanu-piñcha tathi,  
the pearl necklace  of herons  a line  the rainbow  the peacock feather  there  
pītāmbara — vijalī sañcāra  
the yellow garment  of lightning  the flash  
kṛṣṇa nava-jaladhara, jagat-śasya-upara,  
Kṛṣṇa  a fresh rain cloud  of the world  the crops  upon  
variṣaye līlāmṛta-dhāra  
pours  of the nectar of pastimes  showers

(109) His pearl necklace is a line of herons, His peacock feather the rainbow, His yellow garment a flash of lightning. Kṛṣṇa is a fresh rain cloud pouring showers of the nectar of His pastimes upon the crops of the world.

মাধুর্য্য ভগবত্তা-সার, ব্রজে কৈল পরচার,  
তাহা শুক — ব্যাসের নন্দন ৷  
স্থানে স্থানে ভাগবতে, বর্ণিয়াছে জানাইতে,  
তাহা শুনি’ নাচে ভক্তগণ ॥ ১১০ ॥

mādhurya bhagavattā-sāra, vraje kaila paracāra,  
sweetness  of Godhood  the essence  in Vraja  He made  known  
tāhā śuka — vyāsera nandana  
that  Śuka  of Vyāsa  the son  
sthāne sthāne bhāgavate, varṇiyāche jānāite,  
in place  after place  in the Bhāgavata  has described  to make known  
tāhā śuni’ nāce bhakta-gaṇa  
that  hearing  dance  the devotees

(110) Sweetness, the essence of Godhood (*bhagavattā*), He made known in Vraja. Śuka, the son of Vyāsa, has described it in place after place in the *Bhāgavata* to make it known, and hearing it the devotees dance.

কহিতে কৃষ্ণের রসে, শ্লোক পড়ে প্রেমাবেশে,  
প্রেমে সনাতন-হাত ধরি’ ৷  
গোপী-ভাগ্য, কৃষ্ণ-গুণ, যে করিল বরণন,  
ভাবাবেশে মথুরা-নাগরী ॥ ১১১ ॥

kahite kṛṣṇera rase, śloka paḍe premāveśe,  
speaking  of Kṛṣṇa’s  rasa  a verse  recites  in the rapture of love  
preme sanātana-hāta dhari’  
in love  Sanātana’s hand  holding  
gopī-bhāgya, kṛṣṇa-guṇa, ye karila varaṇana,  
the gopīs’ fortune  Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  which  made  description  
bhāvāveśe mathurā-nāgarī  
in ecstatic absorption  of Mathurā  the city women

(111) Speaking of the *rasa* of Kṛṣṇa, the Lord, in the rapture of love, lovingly took Sanātana’s hand and recited the verse in which the women of Mathurā, absorbed in ecstasy, described the gopīs’ fortune and Kṛṣṇa’s qualities.

গোপ্যস্তপঃ কিমচরন্ যদমুষ্য রূপং  
লাবণ্যসারমসমোর্দ্ধ্বমনন্যসিদ্ধম্ ৷  
দৃগ্ভিঃ পিবন্ত্যনুসবাভিনবং দুরাপ-  
মেকান্তধাম যশসঃ শ্রিয় ঐশ্বরস্য ॥ ১১২ ॥

gopyas tapaḥ kim acaran yad amuṣya rūpaṁ  
the gopīs  austerity  what  performed  since  His  form  
lāvaṇya-sāram asamordhvam ananya-siddham  
of loveliness  the essence  unequalled and unsurpassed  not perfected by anything else  
dṛgbhiḥ pibanty anusavābhinavaṁ durāpam  
with the eyes  drink  at every moment new  hard to attain  
ekānta-dhāma yaśasaḥ śriya aiśvarasya  
the sole abode  of fame  of beauty  of majesty

(112) “What austerities did the gopīs perform, that with their eyes they drink His form — the essence of loveliness, unequalled and unsurpassed, perfect in itself, new at every moment, hard to attain, the sole abode of fame, beauty and majesty?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.44.14)

তারুণ্যামৃত — পারাবার, তরঙ্গ — লাবণ্যসার,  
তাতে সে আবর্ত্ত ভাবোদ্গম ৷  
বংশীধ্বনি — চক্রবাত, নারীর মান — তৃণপাত,  
তাহা ডুবায়, না হয় উদ্গম ॥ ১১৩ ॥

tāruṇyāmṛta — pārāvāra, taraṅga — lāvaṇya-sāra,  
of youth’s nectar  an ocean  the waves  the essence of loveliness  
tāte se āvarta bhāvodgama  
in it  that  whirlpool  the rising of emotions  
vaṁśī-dhvani — cakravāta, nārīra māna — tṛṇa-pāta,  
the flute’s sound  a whirlwind  of women  pride  grass and leaves  
tāhā ḍubāya, nā haya udgama  
that  it sinks  not  is  rising again

(113) His youth is a nectar ocean; its waves are the essence of loveliness, its whirlpools the surging of emotions. The sound of His flute is a whirlwind, and women’s pride is but grass and leaves: the whirlwind sinks it, and it never rises again.

সখি হে, কোন্ তপ কৈল গোপীগণ ৷  
কৃষ্ণরূপ-সুমাধুরী, পিবি’ পিবি’ নেত্র ভরি’,  
শ্লাঘ্য করে জন্ম-তনু-মন ॥ ১১৪ ॥

sakhi he, kon tapa kaila gopī-gaṇa  
O friend  what  austerity  performed  the gopīs  
kṛṣṇa-rūpa-sumādhurī, pibi’ pibi’ netra bhari’,  
Kṛṣṇa’s form’s  exquisite sweetness  drinking  drinking  the eyes  filling  
ślāghya kare janma-tanu-mana  
praiseworthy  they make  birth  body  mind

(114) O friend, what austerity did the gopīs perform? Drinking and drinking the exquisite sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s form until their eyes are full, they make their birth, body and mind worthy of praise.

যে মাধুরীর ঊর্দ্ধ্ব আন, নাহি যার সমান,  
পরব্যোমে স্বরূপের গণে ৷  
যিঁহো সর্ব্ব-অবতারী, পরব্যোম-অধিকারী,  
এ মাধুর্য্য নাহি নারায়ণে ॥ ১১৫ ॥

ye mādhurīra ūrdhva āna, nāhi yāra samāna,  
which  of sweetness  higher  other  there is not  of which  equal  
paravyome svarūpera gaṇe  
in Paravyoma  of the divine forms  in the host  
yiṅho sarva-avatārī, paravyoma-adhikārī,  
who  the source of all avatāras  of Paravyoma  the ruler  
e mādhurya nāhi nārāyaṇe  
this  sweetness  there is not  in Nārāyaṇa

(115) Among all the divine forms in Paravyoma there is nothing higher than this sweetness, nor anything equal to it. Even Nārāyaṇa, the source of all the *avatāras* and the ruler of Paravyoma, does not possess this sweetness.

তাতে সাক্ষী সেই রমা, নারায়ণের প্রিয়তমা,  
পতিব্রতাগণের উপাস্যা ৷  
তিঁহো যে মাধুর্য্যলোভে, ছাড়ি’ সব কামভোগে,  
ব্রত করি’ করিলা তপস্যা ॥ ১১৬ ॥

tāte sākṣī sei ramā, nārāyaṇera priyatamā,  
in that  the witness  that  Ramā  of Nārāyaṇa  the most beloved  
pativratā-gaṇera upāsyā  
of the chaste wives  the object of worship  
tiṅho ye mādhurya-lobhe, chāḍi’ saba kāma-bhoge,  
she  who  for sweetness  in greed  giving up  all  enjoyments of desire  
vrata kari’ karilā tapasyā  
a vow  taking  performed  austerity

(116) The witness to this is Ramā (Lakṣmī), Nārāyaṇa’s most beloved, whom all chaste wives worship. Longing for that sweetness, she gave up every enjoyment she might desire, took a vow and performed austerities.

সেই ত’ মাধুর্য্য-সার, অন্য-সিদ্ধি নাহি তার,  
তিঁহো — মাধুর্য্যাদি-গুণখনি ৷  
আর সব প্রকাশে, তাঁর দত্ত গুণ ভাসে,  
যাঁহা যত প্রকাশে কার্য্য জানি ॥ ১১৭ ॥

sei ta’ mādhurya-sāra, anya-siddhi nāhi tāra,  
that  indeed  of sweetness  the essence  from another  perfection  there is not  of it  
tiṅho — mādhuryādi-guṇa-khani  
He  of sweetness and other  qualities  the mine  
āra saba prakāśe, tāṅra datta guṇa bhāse,  
in the other  all  manifestations  by Him  given  qualities  shine  
yāṅhā yata prakāśe kārya jāni  
where  as much  in a manifestation  the work  I know

(117) That is the very essence of sweetness, owing its perfection to nothing else; He is the mine of sweetness and of all other qualities. In all His other manifestations shine the qualities He grants them, as much in each as its work requires — this I know.

গোপীভাব-দরপণ, নব নব ক্ষণে ক্ষণ,  
তার আগে কৃষ্ণের মাধুর্য্য ৷  
দোঁহে করে হুড়াহুড়ি, বাড়ে মুখ নাহি মুড়ি,  
নব নব দোঁহার প্রাচুর্য্য ॥ ১১৮ ॥

gopī-bhāva-darapaṇa, nava nava kṣaṇe kṣaṇa,  
of the gopīs’ love  the mirror  new  new  moment  by moment  
tāra āge kṛṣṇera mādhurya  
of it  before  Kṛṣṇa’s  sweetness  
doṅhe kare huḍāhuḍi, bāḍe mukha nāhi muḍi,  
the two  make  a contest  grow  face  not  turning  
nava nava doṅhāra prācurya  
new  new  of the two  the abundance

(118) The gopīs’ love is a mirror, new at every moment, and before it stands Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness. The two vie with each other and keep growing, never turning back, and the abundance of both is ever new.

কর্ম্ম, তপ, যোগ, জ্ঞান, বিধি-ভক্তি, জপ, ধ্যান,  
ইহা হৈতে মাধুর্য্য দুর্ল্লভ ৷  
কেবল যে রাগমার্গে, ভজে কৃষ্ণে অনুরাগে,  
তাঁরে কৃষ্ণমাধুর্য্য সুলভ ॥ ১১৯ ॥

karma, tapa, yoga, jñāna, vidhi-bhakti, japa, dhyāna,  
karma  austerity  yoga  knowledge  devotion by rules  japa  meditation  
ihā haite mādhurya durlabha  
these  through  sweetness  hard to attain  
kevala ye rāga-mārge, bhaje kṛṣṇe anurāge,  
only  who  on the path of spontaneous love  worships  Kṛṣṇa  with deep attachment  
tāṅre kṛṣṇa-mādhurya sulabha  
for him  Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  easy to attain

(119) Through *karma*, austerity, yoga, knowledge, devotion according to rules (*vidhi-bhakti*), *japa* or meditation this sweetness is hard to attain. Only one who worships Kṛṣṇa with deep attachment (*anurāga*) on the path of spontaneous love (*rāga-mārga*) attains Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness easily.

সেইরূপ ব্রজাশ্রয়, ঐশ্বর্য্য-মাধুর্য্যময়,  
দিব্যগুণগণ-রত্নালয় ৷  
আনের বৈভব-সত্তা, কৃষ্ণদত্ত ভগবত্তা,  
কৃষ্ণ — সর্ব্ব-অংশী, সর্ব্বাশ্রয় ॥ ১২০ ॥

sei-rūpa vrajāśraya, aiśvarya-mādhuryamaya,  
that form  whose home is Vraja  full of majesty and sweetness  
divya-guṇa-gaṇa-ratnālaya  
of divine  qualities  of the host  the jewel-mine  
ānera vaibhava-sattā, kṛṣṇa-datta bhagavattā,  
of others  of majesty  the existence  given by Kṛṣṇa  Godhood  
kṛṣṇa — sarva-aṁśī, sarvāśraya  
Kṛṣṇa  the whole of all parts  the shelter of all

(120) That form, whose home is Vraja, is full of majesty and sweetness, a jewel-mine of divine qualities. The majestic existence of others, their Godhood (*bhagavattā*), is given by Kṛṣṇa: Kṛṣṇa is the whole of whom all are parts (*sarva-aṁśī*), the shelter of all.

শ্রী, লজ্জা, দয়া, কীর্ত্তি, ধৈর্য্য, বৈশারদী মতি,  
এই সব কৃষ্ণে প্রতিষ্ঠিত ৷  
সুশীল, মৃদু, বদান্য, কৃষ্ণ বিনা নাহি অন্য,  
কৃষ্ণ করে জগতের হিত ॥ ১২১ ॥

śrī, lajjā, dayā, kīrti, dhairya, vaiśāradī mati,  
beauty  modesty  mercy  fame  steadfastness  skilful  intelligence  
ei saba kṛṣṇe pratiṣṭhita  
these  all  in Kṛṣṇa  are established  
suśīla, mṛdu, vadānya, kṛṣṇa vinā nāhi anya,  
good-natured  gentle  generous  Kṛṣṇa  except  there is not  another  
kṛṣṇa kare jagatera hita  
Kṛṣṇa  does  of the world  the good

(121) Beauty, modesty, mercy, fame, steadfastness and skilful intelligence — all these are established in Kṛṣṇa. Good-natured, gentle and generous — there is no one so but Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa does good to the world.

কৃষ্ণ দেখি’ যত জন, কৈল নিমিষে নিন্দন,  
ব্রজে বিধি নিন্দে গোপীগণ ৷  
সেই সব শ্লোক পড়ি’, মহাপ্রভু অর্থ করি’,  
সুখে মাধুর্য্য করে আস্বাদন ॥ ১২২ ॥

kṛṣṇa dekhi’ yata jana, kaila nimiṣe nindana,  
Kṛṣṇa  seeing  all  people  made  of the blinking  reproach  
vraje vidhi ninde gopī-gaṇa  
in Vraja  the Creator  reproach  the gopīs  
sei saba śloka paḍi’, mahāprabhu artha kari’,  
those  all  verses  reciting  Mahāprabhu  the meaning  explaining  
sukhe mādhurya kare āsvādana  
in joy  the sweetness  makes  tasting

(122) All who saw Kṛṣṇa reproached the blinking of their eyes, and in Vraja the gopīs reproached the Creator. Reciting those verses and explaining their meaning, Mahāprabhu joyfully tasted their sweetness.

যস্যাননং মকরকুণ্ডলচারুকর্ণ-  
ভ্রাজৎকপোলসুভগং সবিলাসহাসম্ ৷  
নিত্যোৎসবং ন ততৃপুর্দৃশিভিঃ পিবন্ত্যো  
নার্য্যো নরাশ্চ মুদিতাঃ কুপিতা নিমেশ্চ ॥ ১২৩ ॥

yasyānanaṁ makara-kuṇḍala-cāru-karṇa-  
whose  face  with makara earrings  lovely  ears  
bhrājat-kapola-subhagaṁ sa-vilāsa-hāsam  
shining  cheeks  graceful  with playful  smile  
nityotsavaṁ na tatṛpur dṛśibhiḥ pibantyo  
an eternal festival  not  were sated  with the eyes  drinking  
nāryo narāś ca muditāḥ kupitā nimeś ca  
women  men  and  delighted  angry  with the maker of blinking  and

(123) “Women and men drank with their eyes His face — lovely with ears graced by *makara* earrings and with shining cheeks, smiling playfully, an eternal festival — and were never sated. Delighted, they were angry with the maker of the blinking eyelids.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.24.65)

অটতি যদ্ভবানহ্নি কাননং  
ত্রুটির্যুগায়তে ত্বামপশ্যতাম্ ৷  
কুটিলকুন্তলং শ্রীমুখঞ্চ তে  
জড় উদীক্ষতাং পক্ষ্মকৃদ্দৃশাম্ ॥ ১২৪ ॥

aṭati yad bhavān ahni kānanaṁ  
wander  when  You  by day  the forest  
truṭir yugāyate tvām apaśyatām  
a moment  becomes a yuga  You  for those not seeing  
kuṭila-kuntalaṁ śrī-mukhaṁ ca te  
with curling  locks  beautiful face  and  Your  
jaḍa udīkṣatāṁ pakṣma-kṛd dṛśām  
a fool  for those gazing  the maker of eyelids  of the eyes

(124) “When by day You wander in the forest, a moment without You becomes an age for us. And as we gaze upon Your beautiful face framed in curling locks, we see what a fool was the maker of our eyelids.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.15)

কামগায়ত্রী-মন্ত্ররূপ, হয় কৃষ্ণের স্বরূপ,  
সার্দ্ধ-চব্বিশ অক্ষর তার হয় ৷  
সে অক্ষর ‘চন্দ্র’ হয়, কৃষ্ণ করি’ উদয়,  
ত্রিজগৎ কৈলা কামময় ॥ ১২৫ ॥

kāma-gāyatrī-mantra-rūpa, haya kṛṣṇera svarūpa,  
the Kāma-gāyatrī  mantra’s  form  is  Kṛṣṇa’s  own form  
sārdha-cabbiśa akṣara tāra haya  
twenty-four and a half  syllables  its  are  
se akṣara ‘candra’ haya, kṛṣṇa kari’ udaya,  
those  syllables  moons  are  Kṛṣṇa  making  rise  
trijagat kailā kāmamaya  
the three worlds  made  full of love’s desire

(125) The form of the Kāma-gāyatrī mantra is Kṛṣṇa’s own form. It has twenty-four and a half syllables; those syllables are moons, and, making Kṛṣṇa rise, they have filled the three worlds with love’s desire (*kāma*).

সখি হে, কৃষ্ণমুখ — দ্বিজরাজ-রাজ ৷  
কৃষ্ণবপু সিংহাসনে, বসি’ রাজ্য শাসনে,  
করে সঙ্গে চন্দ্রের সমাজ ॥ ১২৬ ॥

sakhi he, kṛṣṇa-mukha — dvija-rāja-rāja  
O friend  Kṛṣṇa’s  face  of the moons  the king  
kṛṣṇa-vapu siṁhāsane, vasi’ rājya śāsane,  
of Kṛṣṇa’s body  on the throne  seated  the kingdom  ruling  
kare saṅge candrera samāja  
holds  together  of moons  a court

(126) O friend, Kṛṣṇa’s face is the emperor of moons. Seated on the throne of Kṛṣṇa’s body, it rules its kingdom, holding court with an assembly of moons.

দুই গণ্ড সুচিক্কণ, জিনি’ মণি-সুদর্পণ,  
সেই দুই পূর্ণচন্দ্র জানি ৷  
ললাটে অষ্টমী-ইন্দু, তাহাতে চন্দন-বিন্দু,  
সেহ এক পূর্ণচন্দ্র মানি ॥ ১২৭ ॥

dui gaṇḍa sucikkaṇa, jini’ maṇi-sudarpaṇa,  
two  cheeks  glossy  surpassing  fine jewelled mirrors  
sei dui pūrṇa-candra jāni  
those  two  full moons  I know  
lalāṭe aṣṭamī-indu, tāhāte candana-bindu,  
on the forehead  a half-moon  on it  sandal  dot  
seha eka pūrṇa-candra māni  
that too  one  full moon  I count

(127) His two glossy cheeks, surpassing fine jewelled mirrors, I know to be two full moons. On His forehead is a half-moon, and on it a dot of sandal paste: that too I count as a full moon.

করনখ চান্দের ঠাট, বংশী-উপর করে নাট,  
তার গীত মুরলীর তান ৷  
পদনখ-চন্দ্রগণ, তলে করে নর্ত্তন,  
নূপুরের ধ্বনি যার গান ॥ ১২৮ ॥

kara-nakha cāndera ṭhāṭa, vaṁśī-upara kare nāṭa,  
the fingernails  of moons  a company  on the flute  make  dance  
tāra gīta muralīra tāna  
their  song  of the flute  the melody  
pada-nakha-candra-gaṇa, tale kare nartana,  
the toenail moons  the host  below  make  dancing  
nūpurera dhvani yāra gāna  
of the anklets  the sound  whose  song

(128) The nails of His fingers, a company of moons, dance upon the flute, and their song is the flute’s melody. Below, the moons of His toenails dance, and the jingling of His anklets is their song.

নাচে মকর-কুণ্ডল, নেত্র — লীলা-কমল,  
বিলাসী রাজা সতত নাচায় ৷  
ভ্রূ — ধনু, নেত্র — বাণ, ধনুর্গুণ — দুই কাণ,  
নারীমন-লক্ষ্য বিন্ধে তায় ॥ ১২৯ ॥

nāce makara-kuṇḍala, netra — līlā-kamala,  
dance  makara earrings  the eyes  playful lotuses  
vilāsī rājā satata nācāya  
the pleasure-loving  king  constantly  makes dance  
bhrū — dhanu, netra — bāṇa, dhanur-guṇa — dui kāṇa,  
the brows  a bow  the eyes  arrows  the bowstring  the two  ears  
nārī-mana-lakṣya vindhe tāya  
of women’s hearts  the target  pierces  with them

(129) The *makara* earrings dance, and the eyes, playful lotuses: the pleasure-loving king makes them dance without cease. His brows are a bow, His eyes arrows, His two ears the bowstring; with them He pierces the target, the hearts of women.

এই চান্দের বড় নাট, পসারি’ চান্দের হাট,  
বিনি-মূলে বিলায় নিজামৃত ৷  
কাঁহো স্মিত-জ্যোৎস্নামৃতে, কাঁহারে অধরামৃতে,  
সব লোক করে আপ্যায়িত ॥ ১৩০ ॥

ei cāndera baḍa nāṭa, pasāri’ cāndera hāṭa,  
of this  moon  the grand  dance  spreading out  of moons  a market  
vini-mūle vilāya nijāmṛta  
without price  distributes  its own nectar  
kāṅho smita-jyotsnāmṛte, kāṅhāre adharāmṛte,  
some  with the nectar of the moonlight of His smile  some  with the nectar of His lips  
saba loka kare āpyāyita  
all  people  makes  satisfied

(130) Grand is this moon’s dance: spreading out a market of moons, it gives away its own nectar without price. Some it satisfies with the moonlit nectar of His smile, some with the nectar of His lips — it satisfies everyone.

বিপুলায়তারুণ, মদন-মদ-ঘূর্ণন,  
মন্ত্রী যার এ দুই নয়ন ৷  
লাবণ্য কেলি-সদন, জন-নেত্র-রসায়ন,  
সুখময় গোবিন্দ-বদন ॥ ১৩১ ॥

vipulāyatāruṇa, madana-mada-ghūrṇana,  
broad, long and reddish  Madana’s  intoxication  making reel  
mantrī yāra e dui nayana  
ministers  whose  these  two  eyes  
lāvaṇya keli-sadana, jana-netra-rasāyana,  
of loveliness  of play  the home  of people’s  eyes  the elixir  
sukhamaya govinda-vadana  
full of joy  Govinda’s  face

(131) Its ministers are His two eyes, broad, long and reddish, which make even Madana (Cupid) reel in his intoxication. Govinda’s face, full of joy, is the pleasure-house of loveliness and an elixir for the eyes of all.

যাঁর পুণ্যপুঞ্জফলে, সে-মুখ-দর্শন মিলে,  
দুই আঁখি কি করিবে পানে ?  
দ্বিগুণ বাড়ে তৃষ্ণা-লোভ, পিতে নারে — মনঃক্ষোভ,  
দুঃখে করে বিধির নিন্দনে ॥ ১৩২ ॥

yāṅra puṇya-puñja-phale, se-mukha-darśana mile,  
whose  of piety  of heaps  by the fruit  of that face  the sight  is obtained  
dui āṅkhi ki karibe pāne?  
two  eyes  what  will do  in drinking  
dviguṇa bāḍe tṛṣṇā-lobha, pite nāre — manaḥ-kṣobha,  
twofold  grows  thirst and greed  to drink  is unable  agitation of mind  
duḥkhe kare vidhira nindane  
in sorrow  makes  of the Creator  reproach

(132) One who obtains the sight of that face as the fruit of heaps of piety — what can his two eyes drink of it? His thirst and greed grow twofold; unable to drink, his mind is agitated, and in sorrow he reproaches the Creator:

না দিলেক লক্ষ-কোটি, সবে দিলা আঁখি দুটি,  
তাতে দিলা নিমিষ-আচ্ছাদন ৷  
বিধি — জড় তপোধন, রসশূন্য তার মন,  
নাহি জানে যোগ্য সৃজন ॥ ১৩৩ ॥

nā dileka lakṣa-koṭi, sabe dilā āṅkhi duṭi,  
not  he gave  hundreds of thousands and millions  only  he gave  eyes  two  
tāte dilā nimiṣa-ācchādana  
to them  he gave  blinking  coverings  
vidhi — jaḍa tapodhana, rasa-śūnya tāra mana,  
the Creator  a dull  ascetic  void of taste  his  mind  
nāhi jāne yogya sṛjana  
not  he knows  proper  creation

(133) “He did not give hundreds of thousands, millions of eyes; he gave only two, and on them he put blinking lids. The Creator is a dull ascetic; his mind is void of taste (*rasa*), and he does not know how to create properly.

যে দেখিবে কৃষ্ণানন, তার করে দ্বি-নয়ন,  
বিধি হঞা হেন অবিচার ৷  
মোর যদি বোল ধরে, কোটী আঁখি তার করে,  
তবে জানি যোগ্য সৃষ্টি তার ॥ ১৩৪ ॥

ye dekhibe kṛṣṇānana, tāra kare dvi-nayana,  
who  will see  Kṛṣṇa’s face  for him  makes  two eyes  
vidhi hañā hena avicāra  
the Creator  being  such  injustice  
mora yadi bola dhare, koṭī āṅkhi tāra kare,  
my  if  word  he takes  millions  of eyes  for him  he makes  
tabe jāni yogya sṛṣṭi tāra  
then  I know  proper  creation  his

(134) For one who is to see Kṛṣṇa’s face he makes only two eyes — such injustice from the Creator! If he would take my advice and make millions of eyes for such a one, then I would know his creation to be proper.”

কৃষ্ণাঙ্গ-মাধুর্য্য — সিন্ধু, সুমধুর মুখ — ইন্দু,  
অতি-মধু স্মিত — সুকিরণে ৷  
এ-তিনে লাগিল মন, লোভে করে আস্বাদন,  
শ্লোক পড়ে, স্বহস্ত চালনে ॥ ১৩৫ ॥

kṛṣṇāṅga-mādhurya — sindhu, sumadhura mukha — indu,  
of Kṛṣṇa’s body  the sweetness  an ocean  the very sweet  face  the moon  
ati-madhu smita — sukiraṇe  
the exceedingly sweet  smile  its fine rays  
e-tine lāgila mana, lobhe kare āsvādana,  
to these three  clung  the mind  in greed  makes  tasting  
śloka paḍe, svahasta cālane  
a verse  recites  His own hand  moving

(135) The sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s body is an ocean, His very sweet face the moon, and His exceedingly sweet smile its fine rays. The Lord’s mind clung to these three; greedily He tasted them and, waving His hand, recited a verse.

মধুরং মধুরং বপুরস্য বিভোর্মধুরং মধুরং বদনং মধুরম্ ৷  
মধুগন্ধি মৃদুস্মিতমেতদহো মধুরং মধুরং মধুরং মধুরম্ ॥ ১৩৬ ॥

দুর্দ্দৈব-বৈদ্য না দেয় এক বিন্দু ! ॥ ১৩৭ ॥

তাতে যেই মুখ সুধাকর ৷
