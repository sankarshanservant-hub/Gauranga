# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 8

## 0

**Summary.** In the eighth chapter the greatness of Śrī Caitanya and Nityānanda is described in this way: even if a person chants the name of Kṛṣṇa birth after birth, he does not obtain the treasure of *prema* as long as offenses against the holy name remain in him. From this one must understand that the *sāttvika-vikāras* (spiritual transformations) and other signs shown by one who offends the name are mere pretense. But whoever takes the names of Caitanya and Nityānanda without duplicity and expresses joy — the two Lords directly free his heart from offenses, and then, when he chants Kṛṣṇa's name, love awakens in him. Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura had noted the final pastimes of Mahāprabhu only briefly in his *Śrī Caitanya-bhāgavata*, and they remained to be described. Therefore, at the order of the Vaiṣṇavas living in Śrī Vṛndāvana, and after receiving the garland of command from Śrīla Madana-mohana, Kavirāja Gosvāmī composed this book.

## 1

I bow down to Bhagavān Caitanyadeva. By His will I, a fool like a painted puppet, have suddenly begun the dance of writing this book.

## 7

“All this”: one who serves Kṛṣṇa without accepting this Pañca-tattva does not receive Kṛṣṇa's mercy.

## 16

A person burdened with the ten kinds of offenses against the holy name may hear and chant for many births, yet he never obtains the treasure of *prema* at Kṛṣṇa's feet.

## 17

By the endeavor of knowledge one easily attains liberation, and by the merit of sacrifices and the like one easily gains the enjoyments of heaven; but even by thousands of practices one does not easily attain *hari-bhakti*. The meaning is this: besides practice there is something more — service to the pure devotee and knowledge of one's relationship with the Supreme Truth (*sambandha-jñāna*). By taking shelter of this one attains *hari-bhakti*.

## 18

If the devotees hope for worldly enjoyment (*bhukti*) and liberation (*mukti*), Kṛṣṇa keeps the truth of pure devotion hidden from them; He gives them *bhukti* and *mukti* and so is free of them.

“*Chuṭe*”: leaves, goes away.

## 19

Nārada said: “O child Yudhiṣṭhira! For you and for the Yadus, Bhagavān Kṛṣṇacandra is sometimes master, guru, worshipable Deity, dear friend, head of the family, and at times even a servant.” Here one should understand this: to those who worship Him, Mukunda easily grants “liberation”; but when He sees that a devotee has some firm skill in worship (*niṣṭhā-cāturya*), He gives that devotee “*bhakti-yoga*.”[^19-1]

## 21–22

There is one wonderful feature of the avatāra of Śrī Caitanya. To anyone who comes near Him He gives the hidden storehouse of *prema*, without considering whether that person is worthy or not, for He is the independent Lord. Consider also that Caitanyacandra has descended as the guru of the whole world. Whether one is an offender or free from offense, if he surrenders and calls out to Him, “O Gaurāṅga! O Kṛṣṇa Caitanya!” he is overwhelmed by the thrill and the tears of love for Kṛṣṇa.


---

[^19-1]: The verse translation reads differently: the verse is given there as the words of Śukadeva Gosvāmī to Mahārāja Parīkṣit (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 5.6.18; so also in the *Anubhāṣya*), and it says that the Lord gives *bhakti-yoga* to no one.
