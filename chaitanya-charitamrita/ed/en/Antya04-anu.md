# Anubhāṣya on Antya-līlā 4

## 1

> Subheading: Gaurasundara saves Sanātana from his resolve to give up his body —

**śrī-gauraḥ** (Mahāprabhu) **vṛndāvanāt** **punaḥ** **prāptaṁ** (who, after their meeting in Kāśī, had come to Kṣetra) **śrī-sanātanam** (Prabhu) **snehāt** **deha-pātāt** (from the destruction of his body) **avan** (protecting) **parīkṣayā** **śuddhaṁ** **cakre**.

## 3

> Subheading: Rūpa goes from Purī to Gauḍa, and Sanātana comes from Vṛndāvana to Purī —

## 4

> Subheading: Having endured great hardship on the path through Jhārikhaṇḍa, he arrives in Purī —

## 5

> Subheading: To external vision sores are seen all over Sanātana's body —

## 6

> Subheading: On the way Sanātana is seized by detachment and speaks of his own lowliness —

***Nirveda***: detachment (*virakti*); ***asāra***: unfit for serving Kṛṣṇa.

## 9

> Subheading: Regarding himself as a worldly, impure *jīva*, he fears transgressing due reverence (*maryādā*) —

## 10

> Subheading: The resolve to give up his body before Jagannātha's chariot while the Lord is dancing —

## 13

> Subheading: Arrival at Ṭhākura Haridāsa's place —

## 14

> Subheading: Obeisance to Haridāsa, and Haridāsa's embrace —

## 15

> Subheading: Sanātana longs to see the Lord's feet —

## 16

> Subheading: The Lord arrives —

## 17

> Subheading: Both bow to the Lord; the Lord embraces Haridāsa —

## 18

> Subheading: Sanātana's arrival astonishes and delights the Lord —

## 19

> Subheading: Bhagavān steps forward to embrace His dearest, best of devotees; Sanātana draws back and speaks humbly —

## 21

> Subheading: Bhagavān forcibly embraces His dearest, best of devotees —

## 22

> Subheading: Sanātana meets the devotees —

## 23

> Subheading: Out of humility Haridāsa and Sanātana sit below the devotees —
