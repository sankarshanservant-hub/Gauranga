# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Madhya 23

## 0

**Summary.** Next the Lord described the characteristics of *bhāva*, the characteristics of *prema* and of its appearance, and the characteristic behavior of those in whom *bhāva* has arisen. He explained how *prema* gradually becomes *mahābhāva*. Explaining the five kinds of *rati*, He explained the corresponding *rasas*, spoke of the position (*sthiti*) of the *rasas*, established the supremacy of *śṛṅgāra-rasa* over all the others, and described its two kinds, *svakīya* and *parakīya*. He explained Kṛṣṇa's sixty-four qualities and Rādhikā's twenty-five qualities, and described the nature of one qualified for *kṛṣṇa-bhakti-rasa* and its eight characteristics (*aṣṭāṅga-lakṣaṇa*). The Lord taught Sanātana the confidential conclusions of the *Bhāgavatam*, the eternal pastimes of Goloka described in the *Harivaṁśa*, and the wrong and the true explanation of the *keśa-avatāra* (the “hair incarnation”). Having taught him all this, He placed His hand on Sanātana's head and invested him with His power.

## 1

The nectar of His own *prema* and name is His hidden treasure, never before given to anyone. Gaura-Kṛṣṇa, supremely magnanimous by nature, distributed it to everyone, down to the very lowest. I surrender to Him.

## 5

That principle (*tattva*) which by nature is *viśuddha-sattva*, like a ray of the sun of *prema*, and which softens the heart with taste (*ruci*), is called *bhāva*.

## 6

That *bhāva* is by nature *śuddha-sattva* is its essential characteristic (*svarūpa-lakṣaṇa*). That it softens the heart with taste is its accompanying characteristic (*taṭastha-lakṣaṇa*).

## 7

When that *bhāva* softens the heart completely, is marked by an intense sense of “He is mine” (*mamatā*), and itself becomes dense in nature, the wise call it *prema*.

## 8

“Exclusive *mamatā* toward Viṣṇu” means that Viṣṇu alone is the object of the sense “He is mine,” and no one else. Bhīṣma, Prahlāda, Uddhava, Nārada and other Vaiṣṇavas call such *mamatā*, joined with *prema*, (*prema-*)*bhakti*.

## 9–13

If by some *sukṛti* that turns one toward *bhakti* a *jīva* comes to have faith (*śraddhā*) in exclusive devotion (*ananya-bhakti*), that *jīva* seeks the company of sādhus, pure devotees. From that company of sādhus arise hearing and chanting (*śravaṇa-kīrtana*). To the extent that hearing and chanting go on, to that extent *sādhana-bhakti* removes all *anarthas*. From the time faith arises, hearing and chanting remove the gross *anarthas*, and then faith itself rises as *niṣṭhā*, steadiness in exclusive devotion. *Niṣṭhā* gradually becomes *ruci*. Then from that taste *āsakti* is born. When *āsakti* becomes pure, *bhāva*, or *rati*, appears — the sprout of love for Kṛṣṇa (*kṛṣṇa-prīti*). When that *rati* deepens, it receives the name *prema*. That *prema* is the *prayojana-tattva*, the abode of all bliss.

## 14–15

First faith; from it, the company of sādhus; from that, *bhajana-kriyā* (the practice of worship); from that, freedom from *anarthas*; then *niṣṭhā*; from that, *ruci* and *āsakti*. Up to this point it is *sādhana-bhakti*. From it *bhāva* gradually arises, and at last *prema*. Know that this is how *prema* arises in *sādhakas*.

## 18–19

*Kṣānti* means forbearance (*kṣamā*); *avyartha-kālatva*, such effort that no time passes in vain; *virakti*, detachment from everything not related to Kṛṣṇa; *māna-śūnyatā*, freedom from pride even when there is reason for pride; *āśā-bandha* (firm hope); *samutkaṇṭhā* (eager longing); a constant taste for singing Kṛṣṇa's name; attachment to describing His qualities; love for the places where He lived. Such *anubhāvas* are seen in a person's nature when the sprout of *bhāva* has been born in him.

## 21

“You *brāhmaṇas*, and Gaṅgā-devī, know that I have surrendered and that my heart is fixed on (given to) Kṛṣṇa. Now let the magical creature sent by the *brāhmaṇa*, or Takṣaka himself, bite me as he likes; and you, go on singing about Kṛṣṇa (*kṛṣṇa-kathā*).”
