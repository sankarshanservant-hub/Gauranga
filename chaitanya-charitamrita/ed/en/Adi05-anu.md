# Anubhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 5

## 1

> Subheading: By the mercy of Nityānanda one comes to know the true nature of Nityānanda —

**yasya** (Nityānanda's) **icchayā** (by the mercy) **ajñena** (by one unversed in the scriptures) **api** (even [by me]) **tat-svarūpam** (the truth of Nityānanda) **nirūpyate** (is described), **tam** **anantādbhutaiśvaryam** (Him whose majesty is endless and wondrous, that is, who possesses a majesty beyond place, time and circumstance) **īśvaram** (the God of gods) **śrī-nityānandam** **ahaṁ** **vande**.

## 3

> Subheading: In six verses the truth of Gaura, in five verses the truth of Nityānanda —

## 4

> Subheading: The truth of Baladeva —

## 6

> Subheading: Kṛṣṇa is Gaura; Balarāma is Nitāi —

## 7

> Subheading: Explanation of the seventh of the fourteen opening verses: Nityānanda, or Baladeva, is the whole whose parts are Saṅkarṣaṇa, the Lords who lie in the waters of the Causal, Garbha and Milk Oceans, and Śeṣa — (a verse from the diary of Śrī Svarūpa Gosvāmī)

**saṅkarṣaṇaḥ** (Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa in Paravyoma), **kāraṇa-toya-śāyī** (the original *puruṣa-avatāra*), **garbhoda-śāyī** (the second *puruṣa-avatāra*, Hiraṇyagarbha, the aggregate Viṣṇu), **payo 'bdhi-śāyī** (the third *puruṣa-avatāra*, who lies in the Ocean of Milk, the individual Viṣṇu), **śeṣaḥ** (Anantadeva) — **yasya aṁśa-kalāḥ**, **saḥ nityānandākhya-rāmaḥ** (Baladeva) **mama śaraṇam astu**.

## 8

> Subheading: The *mūla-Saṅkarṣaṇa* Baladeva serves Kṛṣṇa in five forms —

## 10

> Subheading: In four forms — for creating the spiritual and the material, for maintenance, for entering into creation and so on — and as Śeṣa, in ten bodies, He serves —

## 8–10

The five forms of Śrī Balarāma: 1) Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa, 2) Kāraṇodaśāyī, 3) Garbhodaśāyī, 4) Kṣīrodaśāyī and 5) Śeṣa.

## 12

> Subheading: Describing the truth of Nityānanda, the author explains the seventh verse in four verses —

## 13

> Subheading: (a verse from the diary of Śrī Svarūpa Gosvāmī)

**māyātīte** (beyond the region made of the *guṇas*) **vyāpi-vaikuṇṭha-loke** (in Vaikuṇṭha, the undivided support, free from *māyā* and unlimited), **pūrṇaiśvarye** (full of the complete range of potencies), **śrī-catur-vyūha-madhye** (among the four Viṣṇus — Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha) **yasya** (Nityānanda-Rāma's) **saṅkarṣaṇākhyaṁ rūpam** **udbhāti** (shines), **tam** **nityānanda-rāmam** **ahaṁ** **prapadye**.

## 14

> Subheading: “Paravyoma,” endowed with six transcendental opulences —

## 15

> Subheading: The world of Brahman and the worlds above it, the abode of Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras —

## 16

> Subheading: Above Paravyoma, Kṛṣṇaloka in three forms —

## 17

> Subheading: On the very highest level, Vraja, Goloka and Śvetadvīpa —

## 18

> Subheading: Goloka-Vṛndāvana, an abode wholly nondifferent from Kṛṣṇa —
