# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Antya-līlā 11

## 0

**Summary.** In this chapter, when Brahma Haridāsa Ṭhākura gave up his body with Mahāprabhu's permission, Mahāprabhu carried him with special devotion and great ceremony to the seashore and laid him in *samādhi* there. With His own hands He covered him with sand and raised a *cautārā* (platform) over him. Then He bathed in the ocean, Himself begged alms and held the festival of Haridāsa's departure (*vijaya-mahotsava*).

## 1

I bow to Haridāsa, and I bow to his Lord, that Caitanyadeva who danced holding in His arms the body Haridāsa had left.

## 20

*Rañca* — a particle.

## 32

*Sei līlā* (“that pastime”) — the pastime of Your departure (*antardhāna-līlā*).
