## 87

*Thus ends the twentieth chapter of the “Anubhāṣya.”*

In the four hundred twenty-ninth year of the Caitanya era,
on the thirty-first day of Jyaiṣṭha, in the month of Trivikrama,[^158-1]
living at Śrī Vraja-pattana and calling out, “Gaurahari!” —
I, Dayita-dāsiyā,[^158-2] the lowest of men, (1)

in Navadvīpa, at Māyāpura, not far from the Lord's house,
have completed the “Anubhāṣya” —
the servant of Śrī Gaurakiśora dāsa, who now lives at Kuliyā,
this unworthy one. (2)

Today, on this happy day, to whom but Bhaktivinoda
shall I tell the happy news?
Hearing the “Anubhāṣya,” he was supremely delighted
and bestowed great mercy on me. (3)

His merciful words, his way of serving Mādhava,
have no equal in the three worlds.
No one else, having taken this human body,
gave away the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa as he did. (4)

Having received that master's power, I now sing the “Anubhāṣya”;
nothing in it is mine.
As long as life lasts, I shall remember him in this world;
eternally I long for those feet. (5)

Gadādhara's dearest friend, Śrī Svarūpa Dāmodara,
always worships Gaura-Kṛṣṇa.
Seeing the suffering of the world, he took the dress of a beggar
and day and night serves the name of Kṛṣṇa. (6)

These two [appeared] by the will of Śrī Gaura — how can I, insignificant, tell their glory? —
this is an account of transcendental associates.
Having appeared in this world, they serve the Lord in whom Kṛṣṇa and Gaura are one —
such a secret is not spoken of just anywhere. (7)

O intimate devotees of Śrī Gaurāṅga, followers of Bhaktivinoda,
who abide in transcendental feeling!
Read the “Anubhāṣya” with care in the company of devotees,
and attain love for the Divine Couple. (8)

*The “Anubhāṣya” on the book “Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta” is complete.*
