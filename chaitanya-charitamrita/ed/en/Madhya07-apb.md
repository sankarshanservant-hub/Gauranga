# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Madhya-līlā 7

## 0

**Summary.** In the bright fortnight of the month of Māgha Mahāprabhu took *sannyāsa*, and in the month of Phālguna He settled in Nīlācala. In Phālguna He saw the Dola-yātrā, in Caitra He delivered Sārvabhauma, and in Vaiśākha He set out on His journey to the south. He proposed to tour the south alone, and so Nityānanda Prabhu gave Him a *brāhmaṇa* named “Kṛṣṇadāsa” as His companion. When the Lord was leaving, Sārvabhauma gave Him four sets of *kaupīnas* and outer cloths (*bahirvāsa*) and asked Him to meet Rāmānanda Rāya on the bank of the Godāvarī. Nityānanda Prabhu and several other devotees went with the Lord as far as Ālālanātha. Leaving them, and taking only Kṛṣṇadāsa with Him, Mahāprabhu walked on, saying, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” In whatever village He spent the night, He infused power (*śakti-sañcāra*) into those who surrendered to Him and ordered them to make the whole land Vaiṣṇava. They in turn taught *bhakti* to others, and the number of devotees grew in other villages. In this way the Lord reached Kūrma-sthāna. There He showed mercy to a *brāhmaṇa* named “Kūrma” and delivered a *brāhmaṇa* (*vipra*) named “Vāsudeva” from rotting leprosy. Because He delivered Vāsudeva, the Lord received one of His names, “Vāsudevāmṛta-prada” (He who gave nectar to Vāsudeva).

## 1

With a mind softened by compassion, He freed the devotee named “Vāsudeva” from leprosy, endowed him with a beautiful form and made him content in *bhakti*. To that glorious Caitanyadeva I bow.

## 13

Mahāprabhu is all-knowing. He knew full well that Viśvarūpa had already attained perfection (*siddhi*) before that. But to deliver the southern land, He brought forward a pretext: that He would go in search of Viśvarūpa.

## 25–26

[The Lord says:] “Dāmodara (the *brahmacārī*) always disciplines Me with the rod of instruction so that it seems that in his presence I am someone with no idea how to behave.”

## 27

[The Lord says:] “Dāmodara Paṇḍita and the others have more of Kṛṣṇa's mercy. Therefore they do not care what people think, and they want Me to enjoy all sorts of worldly things. But I am a humble *sannyāsī*. I cannot ignore what people think, and so I behave as *dharma* requires.”

## 59

The road south runs along the seashore. Six *krośas* from Purī lies the village of “Ālālanātha.” There resides “Ālālanātha,” the Deity of four-armed Vāsudeva. His temple stands in a small village in the middle of a forest, and an excellent *paramānna* is offered to Him there. The *pāṇḍās* still show the marks of the hot *paramānna* on the Deity.

## 62

***Adhikārī*** — the king's chief officer. Vidyānagara is nowadays called “Porabandara.”

## 73

The hearts of extraordinary persons are harder than a thunderbolt and at the same time softer than a flower; others are not able to understand this.[^73-1]

## 96

***Rakṣa mām*** — protect me; ***pāhi mām*** — sustain me.

## 99

***Śakti sañcāriyā*** (“infusing power”). The essence of the *hlādinī* potency and the essence of the *saṁvit* potency together constitute “*bhakti-śakti*,” the power of devotion. Anyone into whom Kṛṣṇa or a devotee, out of mercy, infuses this power becomes a “*parama-bhakta*,” a supreme devotee. Whomever Mahāprabhu favored, He infused with such power and entrusted with preaching Vaiṣṇava *dharma*.

## 108

***Setubandha*** — Setubandha-Rāmeśvara stands on the seashore, on the far side of “Rāmnad,” at the southern edge of Bhāratavarṣa.

## 109

Although Navadvīpa is a *dhāma*, at that time *nyāya* and the *smṛti-śāstras* held special sway there, and among the teachers of those *śāstras* there were many people averse to the Lord. The Lord did not manifest any special power to deliver them. That is why the author says this.

## 113

***Kūrma-sthāna*** — a *tīrtha*; there is a temple of Kūrmadeva there. The *Prapannāmṛta* tells how Śrī Jagannāthadeva carried Śrī Rāmānuja Svāmī away from Śrī Puruṣottama by night and left him at Kūrma-tīrtha.

## 150

This name is found among the “hundred names of Śrī Caitanya” composed by Śrī Sārvabhauma.

*Thus ends the seventh chapter of the “Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya.”*
