# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Madhya-līlā 15

## 0

**Summary.** When the Ratha-yātrā was over, Śrī Advaita Prabhu worshiped Mahāprabhu with flowers and *tulasī*, and Mahāprabhu in turn worshiped Advaitācārya with the flowers and *tulasī* left on the tray of worship, reciting the *mantra* “Whoever You are, that You are.” After that Advaitācārya invited Mahāprabhu and fed Him. On the day of the Nandotsava the Lord and His associates dressed as cowherds and held a festival of joy. On Vijayā-daśamī, at the festival of the conquest of Laṅkā, He dressed His devotees as an army of monkeys, and He Himself, absorbed in the mood of Hanumān, displayed great joy. Then, after the assembled devotees had seen the other festivals, He told them to return to Gauḍa-deśa. Mahāprabhu sent Nityānanda Prabhu to Gauḍa-deśa as well, together with Rāmadāsa, Gadādhara dāsa and a few other Vaiṣṇavas. Afterwards, with many humble words, He sent *prasāda*, cloth and other things for His mother (through Śrīvāsa). He gave leave to Rāghava Paṇḍita, Vāsudeva Datta, the devotees of Kulīnagrāma and all the other Vaiṣṇavas, describing their many virtues at length. Answering the question of Rāmānanda and Satyarāja, Mahāprabhu instructed householder Vaiṣṇavas to serve Vaiṣṇavas devoted to the pure name. He described the glory of the Vaiṣṇavas of Khaṇḍa (and assigned them their service), (ordered Sārvabhauma and Vidyā-vācaspati to serve the Dāru-brahma and the Jala-brahma), explained Murāri Gupta's devotion to the feet of Śrī Rāma, and, in response to Vāsudeva's prayer, wholly worthy of a Vaiṣṇava, considered Kṛṣṇa's power to deliver the whole world (without effort). Then, while the Lord was accepting a meal at Sārvabhauma's house, some evil thought arose in Amogha, and the next morning he was struck by cholera. The Lord mercifully cured him and gave him a taste for the name of Kṛṣṇa.

## 102–106

Rāmānanda Vasu and his father Satyarāja Khān were householder Vaiṣṇavas of the Kāyastha Vasu line, the glory of Bengal. They asked the Lord, “What is the duty, the *sādhana*, of a householder Vaiṣṇava?” The Lord answered, “To serve Kṛṣṇa, to serve the Vaiṣṇavas and to chant Kṛṣṇa's name without cease — this is the only duty of a householder Vaiṣṇava.” Then Satyarāja asked, “Serving Kṛṣṇa and chanting Kṛṣṇa's name are easy to understand. But if one cannot recognize a Vaiṣṇava, serving Vaiṣṇavas becomes very difficult. So, O Lord, who is a Vaiṣṇava, and what are his general marks?” The Lord answered, “One from whose lips Kṛṣṇa's name is heard even once is the best of all, a Vaiṣṇava worthy of honor.”
