## 5–6

Mahāprabhu delivered people by giving “direct” *darśana*, by “entering” (*āviṣṭa*) the body of Nakula Brahmacārī, and by “appearing” (*āvirbhūta*) before Pradyumna, or Nṛsiṁhānanda Brahmacārī. In four places Mahāprabhu constantly displayed His “appearance” (*āvirbhāva*): (1) in Śrī Śacī's household temple, (2) where Śrī Nityānanda danced, (3) at the *kīrtana* in Śrīvāsa's courtyard, and (4) in Śrī Rāghava's house. (See verse 34.)

## 10

***Sapta-dvīpa*** (the seven *dvīpas*): see the *Anubhāṣya* on Madhya 20.218 and *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, Canto 5, Chapters 16 and 20.

***Nava-khaṇḍa*** (the nine *khaṇḍas*). The *Siddhānta-śiromaṇi*, in the *Golādhyāya*, in the section *Bhuvana-kośa*, says: “In Bhārata-varṣa [there are nine *khaṇḍas*]: Aindra, Kaśeru, Tāmraparṇa, then Gabhastimat, next the one called Kumārikā, Nāga, Saumya, Vāruṇa, and the last *khaṇḍa*, named Gāndharva.” These are (1) Aindra, (2) Kaśeru, (3) Tāmraparṇa, (4) Gabhastimat, (5) Kumārikā, (6) Nāga, (7) Saumya, (8) Vāruṇa and (9) Gāndharva.
