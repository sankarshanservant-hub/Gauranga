## 153

> Subheading: At Remuṇā (?) the Lord lets Rāya go —

“From there”: another reading has “from Bhadraka.” If “from there” is taken to mean “from Remuṇā,” it disagrees with the reading of Madhya 1.149: “Rāmānanda Rāya accompanied Him as far as Bhadraka.” Some hold that Remuṇā then belonged to the Bhadraka district, but there is no evidence for this. Others hold that in the verse just mentioned “Remuṇā” is the right reading in place of “Bhadraka.” It seems more likely, however, that Rāya turned back from Bhadraka. Bhadraka lies four *yojanas* south of Bāleśvara, and Remuṇā about half a *yojana* (5 miles) to its west.

## 186

> Subheading: Hearing, chanting or remembering the Lord's name purifies even an *antyaja* — what then of seeing Him face to face — (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 3.33.6) —

When mother Devahūti heard from Śrī Kapiladeva about the yoga of pure *bhakti*, the covering of delusion fell away from her, and she offers prayers to Bhagavān Śrī Kapiladeva, the founder of pure *sāṅkhya* knowledge:

**bhagavan** (O Bhagavān), **kvacit** **yan-nāmadheya-śravaṇānukīrtanāt** (by first hearing the name of You, Bhagavān, and then — *anu* — chanting it), **yat-prahvaṇāt** (by bowing one's head to You), **yat-smaraṇāt** (by remembering You, Bhagavān) **ca** **śvādaḥ** (one born in a family of dog-eaters, the lowest of all) **api** **sadyaḥ** (at once) **savanāya** (for the *soma* sacrifice) **kalpate** (becomes fit); **te** (Your) **darśanāt** **punaḥ** **kutaḥ** **nu** (what need is there to say; the sense is, “I have attained the goal”).
