# Verse 155 (= Antya 2.172) — word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev; verse 85, line 1 (= Antya 4.85) —
# word-by-word after ed/en-rev. The quotation of Advaita and Nityānanda runs over 78–79.

@78
Advaita  Avadhūta  something  say  at the Lord’s feet
naturally  by Your  qualities  the world  is sold
= Then Advaita and the Avadhūta (Nityānanda) said something at the Lord’s feet: “The whole world is naturally sold to Your qualities.

@79
again  moreover  You bind  with such  of merciful words  a rope
You  leaving  who  where  to go  can
= And now You bind us again with such a rope of merciful words! Who, leaving You, could go anywhere?”

@80
then  the Lord  all  consoling
to all  farewell  gave  composed  becoming
= Then the Lord consoled them all and, composing Himself, bade everyone farewell.

@81
to Nityānanda  He said  You  not  come  again  and again
right there  My  company  will be  Yours
= To Nityānanda He said, “Do not come again and again. There itself You will have My company.”

@82
go  all  the devotees  weeping  doing
Mahāprabhu  remained  at home  sorrowful  becoming
= All the devotees set off weeping, and Mahāprabhu remained at home, sorrowful.

@83
by His own  mercy  quality  the Lord  bound  all
of Mahāprabhu  the mercy-debt  who  to repay  can
= By the quality of His own mercy the Lord bound everyone. Who can repay the debt of Mahāprabhu’s mercy?

@84
whom  as  makes dance  the Lord  independent  Supreme Lord
therefore  Him  leaving  people  go  to other lands
= The Lord, the independent Supreme Lord, makes each one dance as He wills; therefore people leave Him and go to other lands.

@85
of wood  a puppet  as  the conjurer  makes dance
the Supreme Lord’s  conduct  at all  understanding  not  is possible
= As a conjurer makes a wooden puppet dance, so the Lord acts; His conduct cannot be understood at all.

@86
in the previous year  Jagadānanda  Āi (Śacī)  to see
the Lord’s order  taking  came  to Nadīyā  town
= The previous year Jagadānanda, taking the Lord’s order, had gone to the town of Nadīyā to see Āi (mother Śacī).

@87
Āi’s  feet  going  did  worship
of Jagannātha  the cloth  prasāda  offered  presentation
= Going to Āi, he worshipped her feet and presented her with cloth and *prasāda* of Jagannātha.

@88
the Lord’s  in the name  to the mother  obeisance  he offered
the Lord’s  humble  praises  to the mother  told
= In the Lord’s name he offered obeisance to the mother, and he conveyed to her the Lord’s humble words of praise.

@89
Jagadānanda  getting  the mother  joyful  at heart
he  of the Lord  talks  tells  she hears  night and day
= Having Jagadānanda with her, the mother was joyful at heart. He spoke of the Lord, and she listened night and day.

@90
Jagadānanda  says  mother  on certain  certain  days
your  here  coming  the Lord  does  eating
= Jagadānanda said, “Mother, on certain days the Lord comes here to you and eats.

@91
eating  having done  He says  joyful  becoming
mother  today  fed  to the throat  filling
= After eating, He says joyfully, ‘Today Mother fed Me till I was full to the throat.

@92
I  going  eating  do  mother  not  knows
directly  eat  I  she  a dream  as  considers
= I go there and eat, and Mother does not know it. I eat in person, but she takes it for a dream.’”

@93
the mother  says  how many  I cook  excellent  dishes
Nimāi  here  may eat  desire  is  my  mind
= The mother said, “How many excellent dishes I cook! ‘May Nimāi eat them here’—such is the desire of my heart.

@94
Nimāi  has eaten  such  is  my  mind
afterwards  awareness  comes  I  saw  a dream
= It seems to me that Nimāi has eaten, but afterwards I think: ‘I saw a dream.’”

@95
in this way  Jagadānanda  with mother Śacī
of Caitanya  happy  talks  tells  night and day
= In this way Jagadānanda spoke with mother Śacī, night and day, of happy things about Caitanya.

@96
of Nadīyā  the devotees  with all  he met
Jagadānanda  getting  all  joyful  became
= He met all the devotees of Nadīyā, and they all rejoiced to have Jagadānanda with them.

@97
the Ācārya  to meet  then  went  Jagadānanda
Jagadānanda  getting  became  the Ācārya  joyful
= Then Jagadānanda went to meet the Ācārya (Advaita), and the Ācārya was overjoyed to see him.

@98
Vāsudeva,  Murāri  Gupta  Jagadānanda  getting
joyfully  kept  at home  not  let  go
= Vāsudeva and Murāri Gupta, having Jagadānanda with them, joyfully kept him in their homes and would not let him go.

@99
of Caitanya  intimate  talks  they hear  from his  mouth
themselves  forget  all  in the joy of talks about Caitanya
= From his mouth they heard intimate talks about Caitanya, and in the joy of those talks all of them forgot themselves.

@100
Jagadānanda  to meet  goes  whichever  devotee’s house
that  very  devotee  in joy  himself  forgets
= Whichever devotee’s house Jagadānanda went to, that devotee forgot himself in joy.

@101
of Caitanya  the object of love  Jagadānanda  blessed
whom  he meets  he  thinks  I have found  Caitanya
= Blessed is Jagadānanda, the object of Caitanya’s love! Whoever met him felt, “I have found Caitanya.”

@102
in Śivānanda Sena’s  house  going  he stayed
candanādi  oil  there  one mātrā  made
= He went and stayed in Śivānanda Sena’s house, and there he prepared one *mātrā* (sixteen *sers*) of *candanādi* oil.

@103
fragrant  making  the oil  a gāgarī  filling
to Nīlācala  taking  came  care  taking
= He made the oil fragrant, filled a *gāgarī* (a large pitcher) with it, and carefully brought it to Nīlācala.

@104
with Govinda  the oil  placing  he kept
on the Lord’s body  apply  the oil  to Govinda  he said
= He left the oil with Govinda and told him, “Apply this oil to the Lord’s body.”

@105
then  to the Lord  Govinda  made  a submission
Jagadānanda  candanādi oil  has brought
= Then Govinda submitted to the Lord: “Jagadānanda has brought *candanādi* oil.

@106
his  wish  the Lord  a little  on the head  applies
of bile  air  disorders  the aggravation  calm  becoming  goes
= His wish is that You apply a little to Your head, so that the aggravation of the disorders of bile and air (*pitta* and *vāyu*) may subside.

@107
one pitcher  fragrant  oil  in Gauḍa  making
here  he has brought  much  care  taking
= He prepared a pitcher of fragrant oil in Gauḍa and brought it here with great care.”

@108
the Lord  says  of a sannyāsī  in oil  is not  the right
moreover  fragrant  oil  utter  disgrace
= The Lord said, “A *sannyāsī* has no right to oil—and fragrant oil at that is an utter disgrace!

@109
to Jagannātha  give  the oil  lamp  so that  burns
his  labour  will be  supremely  fruitful
= Give the oil to Jagannātha, so that it may burn in the lamps; then his labour will bear the highest fruit.”

@110
this  message  Govinda  to Jagadānanda  told
silent  becoming  remained  the Paṇḍita  anything  not  said
= Govinda told Jagadānanda this message. The Paṇḍita remained silent and said nothing.

@111
days  ten  passing  Govinda  informed  once  more
the Paṇḍita’s  wish  the oil  may He make  acceptance
= When ten days had passed, Govinda informed the Lord once more: “The Paṇḍita wishes that You accept the oil.”

@112
hearing  the Lord  speaks  some  angry  words
a masseur  one  keep  to do  massage
= Hearing this, the Lord spoke some angry words: “Then keep a masseur to give Me massages!

@113
this  pleasure  for  I  took  sannyāsa
My  utter ruin  your  all  amusement
= Was it for this pleasure that I took *sannyāsa*? For Me it would be utter ruin, and for all of you a joke!

@114
on the road  going  of oil  the scent  My  whoever  will get
dārī-sannyāsī  as  Me  will call
= Whoever passes Me on the road and catches the scent of oil on Me will call Me a *dārī-sannyāsī* (a *sannyāsī* who keeps women).”

@115
hearing  the Lord’s  words  Govinda  silence  kept
in the morning  Jagadānanda  to the Lord’s place  came
= Hearing the Lord’s words, Govinda fell silent. In the morning Jagadānanda came to the Lord.

@116
the Lord  says  Paṇḍita  oil  you brought  Gauḍa  from
I  indeed  a sannyāsī  oil  not  can  accept
= The Lord said, “Paṇḍita, you have brought oil from Gauḍa, but I am a *sannyāsī* and cannot accept oil.

@117
to Jagannātha  give  taking  lamp  so that  burns
your  all  labour  will become  fruitful
= Take it and give it to Jagannātha, so that it may burn in the lamps; then all your labour will bear fruit.”

@118
the Paṇḍita  says  who  to You  tells  false words
I  Gauḍa  from  oil  ever  not  brought
= The Paṇḍita said, “Who tells You these lies? I never brought any oil from Gauḍa.”

@119
so  saying  the room  from  the oil pitcher  bringing
the Lord’s  before  in the courtyard  threw  breaking
= So saying, he brought the pitcher of oil out of the room and smashed it in the courtyard before the Lord.

@120
the oil  breaking  by that  road  his own house  going
lying down  remained  in the room  the door  bolting
= Having broken the oil pitcher, he went straight to his own house, bolted the door and lay down inside.

@121
on the third  day  the Lord  his  to the door  going
get up  Paṇḍita  saying  speaks  calling
= On the third day the Lord went to his door and called out, “Get up, Paṇḍita!

@122
today  alms (lunch)  you will give  Me  doing  cooking
at midday  I shall come  now  I go  to the darśana
= Today you will cook and give Me My *bhikṣā* (meal). I shall come at midday; now I am going for *darśana* (of Jagannātha).”

@123
so  saying  the Lord  went  the Paṇḍita  got up
bath  taking  various  dishes  cooking  did
= So saying, the Lord left, and the Paṇḍita got up, bathed and cooked various dishes.

@124
midday duties  having done  the Lord  came  to eat
feet  washing  doing  sat  on the seat
= Having finished His midday duties, the Lord came to eat. He washed His feet and sat down on His seat.

@125
with ghee  fine rice  on a banana leaf  a heap  made
of banana  cups  filling  dishes  all around  placed
= On a banana leaf the Paṇḍita heaped fine *śāli* rice with ghee, and all around it he set banana-leaf cups filled with dishes.

@126
on the rice and dishes  tulasī  blossoms
of Jagannātha  cakes and sweet drinks  before  brings  holding
= On the rice and the dishes lay *tulasī* blossoms, and he brought Jagannātha’s cakes and sweet drinks and set them before the Lord.

@127
the Lord  says  on a second leaf  serve  rice and dishes
you  Me  today  together  shall do  eating
= The Lord said, “Serve rice and dishes on a second leaf. Today you and I shall eat together.”

@128
hand  raising  remains  the Lord  not  does  eating
then  the Paṇḍita  speaks  some  loving  words
= The Lord sat with His hand raised and did not eat. Then the Paṇḍita spoke some loving words:

@129
Yourself  prasāda  take  afterwards  I  shall take
Your  insistence  I  how  shall break
= “You take the *prasāda* first; I shall take it afterwards. How could I disregard Your insistence?”

@130
then  Mahāprabhu  happily  to eat  sat
of the dishes  the taste  getting  to speak  began
= Then Mahāprabhu happily sat down to eat. Tasting the dishes, He began to speak:

@131
of one possessed by anger  of the cooking  is  such  taste
this  indeed  I know  on you  Kṛṣṇa’s  grace
= “Cooking done in a fit of anger has such a taste! From this I know that Kṛṣṇa’s grace is upon you.

@132
Himself  will eat  Kṛṣṇa  that  for the sake of
your  by the hand  cooking  makes do  excellent  making
= Because Kṛṣṇa Himself will eat it, He has the cooking done so excellently by your hands.

@133
such  nectar  food  to Kṛṣṇa  you  offer
your  good fortune’s  limit  who  does  describe
= You offer such nectarean food to Kṛṣṇa—who can describe the limit of your good fortune?”

@134
the Paṇḍita  says  who  will eat  He  the cook
I and the like  only  merely  ingredients  collector
= The Paṇḍita said, “The One who will eat is Himself the cook. People like me are merely collectors of the ingredients.”

@135
again  and again  the Paṇḍita  various  dishes  serves
out of fear  anything  not  says  the Lord  eats  with delight
= Again and again the Paṇḍita served various dishes. Out of fear the Lord said nothing and ate with delight.

@136
insistence  making  the Paṇḍita  made eat
other  days  than  the eating  was  tenfold
= The Paṇḍita insisted and fed Him, and He ate ten times more than on other days.

@137
again  and again  the Lord  to rise  makes  up His mind
at that moment  the Paṇḍita  serves  dishes
= Again and again the Lord made up His mind to rise, and at that very moment the Paṇḍita would serve more dishes.

@138
anything  to say  cannot  the Lord  eats  in fear
not  if He eats  Jagadānanda  will make  a fast
= The Lord could not say anything; He ate out of fear: “If I do not eat, Jagadānanda will fast.”

@139
then  the Lord  speaks  making  humble  respect
tenfold  you have fed Me  now  make  an end
= Then the Lord spoke with humble respect: “You have fed Me tenfold; now bring it to an end.”

@140
then  Mahāprabhu  rising  did  ācamana
the Paṇḍita  brought  mouth-fresheners  garland  sandalwood
= Then Mahāprabhu rose and rinsed His mouth and hands (*ācamana*), and the Paṇḍita brought spices to freshen His mouth, a garland and sandalwood paste.

@141
sandalwood and the rest  accepting  the Lord  sat  in that  place
Me  before  today  you  do  eating
= Accepting the sandalwood paste and the rest, the Lord sat down right there: “Today you must eat in front of Me.”

@142
the Paṇḍita  says  the Lord  going  may take  rest
I  now  prasāda  shall take  making  arrangements
= The Paṇḍita said, “Let the Lord go and rest. I shall take *prasāda* now, once I have finished arranging things.

@143
of the kitchen  the work  have done  Rāmāi,  Raghunātha
to these  all  to give  I want  some  dishes and rice
= Rāmāi and Raghunātha have done the work in the kitchen; I want to give all of them some rice and dishes.”

@144
the Lord  says  Govinda  you  right here  will stay
the Paṇḍita  eating  having done  to Me  will tell
= The Lord said, “Govinda, you stay right here. When the Paṇḍita has eaten, tell Me.”

@145
so  saying  Mahāprabhu  made  departure
to Govinda  the Paṇḍita  some  speaks  words
= So saying, Mahāprabhu departed, and the Paṇḍita said some words to Govinda:

@146
you  quickly  go  to do  foot-massage
tell Him  the Paṇḍita  now  has sat  to eat
= “Go quickly and massage the Lord’s feet. Tell Him: ‘The Paṇḍita has now sat down to eat.’

@147
for you  of the Lord  the remnants  I shall keep  holding
the Lord  to sleep  when He goes  you  eat  coming
= I shall keep the Lord’s remnants (*śeṣa*) for you. When the Lord falls asleep, come and eat.”

@148
Rāmāi,  Nandāi,  and  Govinda,  Raghunātha
to all  dividing  gave  the Lord’s  dishes and rice
= To Rāmāi, Nandāi, Govinda and Raghunātha he gave shares of the Lord’s rice and dishes.

@149
himself  the Lord’s  remnants  did  eating
then  Govinda  the Lord  sent  again
= He himself ate the Lord’s remnants. Then the Lord sent Govinda again:

@150
see  Jagadānanda  prasāda  takes  or  not  takes
quickly  coming  the news  you will tell  to Me
= “See whether Jagadānanda is taking *prasāda* or not, and come quickly and tell Me the news.”

@151
Govinda  coming  seeing  told  of the Paṇḍita  the eating
then  Mahāprabhu  did  at ease  lying down
= Govinda came, saw, and reported that the Paṇḍita was eating. Then Mahāprabhu lay down at ease.

@152
between Jagadānanda and the Lord  love  goes on  in this way
between Satyabhāmā and Kṛṣṇa  as  we hear  in the Bhāgavata
= In this way the love between Jagadānanda and the Lord goes on, as we hear in the *Bhāgavata* of the love between Satyabhāmā and Kṛṣṇa.

@153
of Jagadānanda  of the good fortune  who  will tell  the limit
of Jagadānanda  of the good fortune  he  alone  the comparison
= Who can tell the limit of Jagadānanda’s good fortune? Of Jagadānanda’s good fortune he alone is the comparison.

@154
Jagadānanda’s  Prema-vivarta  hears  whatever  person
of love  the true nature  knows  gets  the wealth of love
= Whoever hears Jagadānanda’s *Prema-vivarta* knows the true nature of love and attains the wealth of love.[^154-1]

@155
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

@notes
[^154-1]: *Prema-vivarta*: the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* gives two meanings — the *vivarta* of love, that is, the behaviour in which, in the dealings of love, anger seems to appear; and the book *Prema-vivarta*, in which Jagadānanda wrote about Mahāprabhu’s life.
