@136
therefore  the Bhāgavata  —  of the sūtras  of the meaning  the embodiment
by himself composed  of the sūtras  his own  commentary  in nature
= Therefore the *Bhāgavatam* is the meaning of the *sūtras* in embodied form: it is by nature the author’s own commentary on the *sūtras* he himself composed.

@137
the meaning  this  of the Brahma-sūtras
of the meaning of the Mahābhārata  the determination
of the Gāyatrī  of a commentary  in the form  it
by the meaning of the Vedas  enriched
a book  of eighteen thousand (verses)
Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam  named
= “This book named *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, of eighteen thousand verses, is the meaning of the *Brahma-sūtras*; it settles the meaning of the *Mahābhārata*; it has the form of a commentary on the Gāyatrī; and it is enriched by the meaning of the Vedas.” (Garuḍa Purāṇa)

@138
of all  the Vedas and Itihāsas
the essence  the essence  extracted
= “… the very essence of all the Vedas and Itihāsas, extracted.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.42)

@139
of all Vedānta  the essence  indeed
Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam  is held to be
by its  nectar of rasa  of one satisfied
not  elsewhere  can be  attraction  ever
= “*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* is held to be the essence of all Vedānta. One who is satisfied with the nectar of its *rasa* is never attracted to anything else.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 12.13.15)

@140
of the Gāyatrī  with the meaning  this  book’s  beginning
satyaṁ  paraṁ (the Supreme Truth)  —  sambandha  dhīmahi (let us meditate)  —  in sādhana (abhidheya)  prayojana
= This book begins with the meaning of the Gāyatrī: “*satyaṁ param*” (the Supreme Truth) is the *sambandha*, and “*dhīmahi*” (let us meditate) is both the practice (*sādhana*) and the goal (*prayojana*).

@141
birth and so on  of this  from whom  directly  indirectly  and  in all purposes  fully aware  self-luminous
revealed  the Veda  through the heart  who  to the first poet  are bewildered  about whom  the wise
of fire  water  earth  as  the interchange  in whom  the threefold creation  real
by power  His own  always  free of all deception  the truth  supreme  let us meditate on
= “Let us meditate on the Supreme Truth, from whom, directly and indirectly, this universe arises, endures and dissolves; who is fully aware of all purposes and shines by Himself (*svarāṭ*); who revealed the Veda through the heart to the first poet, Brahmā; about whom even the wise are bewildered; in whom, as fire, water and earth pass into one another, the threefold creation is real; and who by His own power is ever free of all deception.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.1)

@142
dharma  wholly-cast-out  deceit  here  supreme  of the non-envious  of the saints
to be known  real  here  substance  bestowing good  the threefold miseries  uprooting
in the beautiful Bhāgavata  by the great sage  composed  what  by others?  the Lord
at once  in the heart  is captured  here  by the pious  who wish to hear  that very moment
= “In this beautiful *Bhāgavatam*, composed by the great sage, is taught the supreme dharma of the saintly who are free from envy, from which all deceit has been cast out. Here is known the true Reality, which bestows good and uproots the threefold miseries. What need is there of other scriptures? As soon as the pious wish to hear it, the Lord is at once captured within their hearts.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.2)

@143
of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  of rasa  the very form  Śrī Bhāgavata
therefore  the Vedic  scriptures  than  supreme  greatness
= The *Śrī Bhāgavatam* is the very form of the *rasa* of devotion to Kṛṣṇa; therefore its greatness surpasses that of the Vedic scriptures.

@144
of the Vedas  wish-fulfilling  of the tree  fallen  the fruit
from Śuka’s  mouth  with nectar  juice  filled
drink  the Bhāgavatam  rasa  up to (the bliss of) liberation
again and again  O  rasikas  on earth  bhāvukas
= “O *rasikas*, O *bhāvukas* on earth! Again and again drink the *Bhāgavatam*, the ripe fruit fallen from the wish-fulfilling tree of the Vedas, filled with nectarean juice from the mouth of Śuka: drink its *rasa*, which embraces even the bliss of liberation.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.3)

@145
we  but  not  are satiated
by the exploits  of Uttamaśloka
which  for those who hear  rasa  who know
sweet  sweet  at every  step
= “We are never satiated with the exploits of Uttamaśloka, for to those who know *rasa* and hear of them they grow sweeter and sweeter at every step.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.19)

@146
therefore  the Bhāgavata  do  study
this  from  you will gain  of the sūtras  of the śruti  of the meaning  the essence
= Therefore study the *Bhāgavatam* carefully: from it you will gain the essential meaning of the *sūtras* and the *śruti*.

@147
constantly  do  saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name
easily  liberation  you will attain  you will attain  the treasure of love
= Always chant the name of Kṛṣṇa in *saṅkīrtana*: you will easily attain liberation, and you will attain the treasure of love.

@148
one who has realised Brahman  serene in self
not  grieves  not  desires
equal  to all  beings
for Me  devotion  attains  supreme
= “One who has realised Brahman and is serene within neither grieves nor desires. Equal to all beings, he attains supreme devotion to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.54)

@149
the liberated  even  by līlā  a form
having assumed  Bhagavān  worship
= “Even the liberated, assuming a form (*vigraha*) by the Lord’s *līlā*, worship Bhagavān.” (words of the author of the Sarvajña-bhāṣya, quoted by Śrīdhara Svāmī in his commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.21)

@150
firmly established  though  in transcendence of the guṇas
of Uttamaśloka  by the pastimes
captivated  in mind  O royal sage
the narration  which  I studied
= “O royal sage, although I was firmly established in transcendence of the *guṇas*, my mind was captivated by the pastimes of Uttamaśloka, and so I studied this narration.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.1.9)

@151
of Him  the lotus-eyed  of the lotus feet
with the filaments  mixed  of tulasī  of the nectar  the breeze
having entered within  through its own passage  caused  in them
agitation  devoted to the imperishable Brahman  even  of mind and body
= “The breeze that bore the fragrance of tulasī, mingled with the pollen of the lotus-eyed Lord’s lotus feet, entered their nostrils and stirred both their minds and their bodies, though they were devoted to the imperishable Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.15.43)

@152
delighting in the ātmā  and  the sages
free from knots  even  to Urukrama
perform  causeless  devotion
of such  qualities  is Hari
= “Even sages who delight in the ātmā and are free from the knots of the heart render causeless devotion to Urukrama, for such are the qualities of Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.10)

@153
at that time  that  Mahārāṣṭrian  brāhmaṇa
in the assembly  told  of that  verse  the explanation
= Just then the brāhmaṇa from Mahārāṣṭra told the assembly how that verse had been explained.

@154
of this  verse  the meaning  the Lord  sixty-one  ways
has given  which  hearing  people  are amazed
= “The Lord has explained the meaning of this verse in sixty-one ways; all who hear it are amazed.”

@155
then  all  the people  to hear  insistence  showed
sixty-one  meanings  the Lord  unfolding  told
= Then everyone insisted on hearing them, and the Lord unfolded the sixty-one meanings in detail.

@156
hearing  of the people  great  amazement  arose
Caitanya Gosāñi  —  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  they concluded
= Hearing them, the people were struck with wonder and concluded that Caitanya Gosāñi is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself.

@157
thus  having spoken  rising  went  Gaurahari
obeisances  offer  the people  the sound of ‘Hari’  making
= Having said this, Gaurahari rose and left, and the people bowed down to Him, calling out the name of Hari.

@158
all  of Kāśī  the residents  perform  saṅkīrtana of the Name
in love  laugh  weep  sing  perform  dance
= All the residents of Kāśī took up the *saṅkīrtana* of the Name; in love they laughed, wept, sang and danced.

@159
the sannyāsīs  the paṇḍitas  do  of the Bhāgavata  study
Vārāṇasī  the city  the Lord  made  deliverance
= The *sannyāsīs* and *paṇḍitas* began to study the *Bhāgavatam*. Thus the Lord delivered the city of Vārāṇasī.

@160
His own  people  taking  the Lord  came  to His lodging
Vārāṇasī  became  a second  Nadīyā  town
= Taking His own people with Him, the Lord came back to His lodging. Vārāṇasī had become a second Nadīyā.

@161
His own  associates  with  the Lord  says  a smile  making
to Kāśī  I  came  to sell  the wares of bhāva
= Surrounded by His own associates, the Lord said with a smile, “I came to Kāśī to sell My wares of ecstatic feeling (*bhāva*).

@162
in Kāśī  a buyer  there is not  the goods  not  sell
again  to My country  carrying  to take  not  is possible
= In Kāśī there were no buyers, and the goods would not sell; yet they could not be carried back home again.

@163
I  the load  would carry  of you  all  sorrow  arose
of you  all  by the wish  without  price  I distributed
= I would have carried the load back, but you were all grieved; so, by your wish, I have given it away free of charge.”

@164
all  say  —  the people  to deliver  Your  descent
the east  the south  the west  You have made  deliverance
= Everyone said, “You have descended to deliver the people. You have delivered the east, the south and the west.

@165
only one  Vārāṇasī  was  from You  turned away
it  having delivered  You made  of us  all  happiness
= Vārāṇasī alone was turned away from You; by delivering it, You have made us all happy.”

@166
in Vārāṇasī  town  when  an uproar  arose
hearing  villagers  country  people  to come  began
= When the uproar arose in the town of Vārāṇasī, people from the villages and the countryside heard of it and began to come.

@167
lakhs  crores  of people  come  there is no  counting
in the cramped  place  of the Lord  not  get  sight
= People came in hundreds of thousands, in millions, beyond counting; in that cramped place they could not get a sight of the Lord.

@168
the Lord  when  to bathe  goes  to see Viśveśvara
on two  sides  the people  make  of the Lord  beholding
= Whenever the Lord went to bathe or to see Viśveśvara, people lined both sides of the way to behold Him.

@169
arms  raising  the Lord  says  —  say  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Hari’
prostrated obeisances  offer  the people  the sound of ‘Hari’  making
= Raising His arms, the Lord would say, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa!’ Say ‘Hari!’” and the people fell down before Him, calling out the name of Hari.

@170
in this way  days  five  the people  delivering
the next  day  departed  the Lord  uneasy  becoming
= In this way the Lord delivered the people for five days; the next day He set out, His heart uneasy.

@171
at night  rising  the Lord  when  made  departure
behind  following  took  then  devotees  five  persons
= When the Lord rose at night and set off, five devotees followed close behind Him:

@172
Tapana Miśra  Raghunātha  the Mahārāṣṭrian  brāhmaṇa
Candraśekhara  Paramānanda the kīrtana singer  —  five  persons
= Tapana Miśra, Raghunātha, the brāhmaṇa from Mahārāṣṭra, Candraśekhara and Paramānanda the *kīrtana* singer — these were the five.

@173
all  wish  with the Lord  to Nīlācala  to go
to all  leave  gave  the Lord  with care
= All of them wished to go to Nīlācala with the Lord, but He took leave of them all with loving care:

@174
whose  wish  later  come  Me  to see
now  I  alone  shall go  of Jhārikhaṇḍa  by the path
= “Whoever wishes may come later to see Me; now I shall go alone by the path through Jhārikhaṇḍa.”

@175
to Sanātana  He said  —  you  go  to Vṛndāvana
your  two  brothers  there  have made  their way
= To Sanātana He said, “Go to Vṛndāvana. Your two brothers have already gone there.

@176
with a patched quilt and a water pot  My  penniless  devotees
to Vṛndāvana  when they come  them  do  take care of
= My penniless devotees, who own only a patched quilt and a water pot — when they come to Vṛndāvana, take care of them.”

@177
thus  saying  went  the Lord  all  embracing
all  fell  there  unconscious  becoming
= Saying this, the Lord embraced them all and departed, and they all fell to the ground unconscious.

@178
after a while  rising  all  in sorrow  home  came
Sanātana Gosāñi  for Vṛndāvana  set out
= After a while they got up and returned home in sorrow, and Sanātana Gosāñi set out for Vṛndāvana.

@179
meanwhile  Rūpa Gosāñi  when  to Mathurā  came
at Dhruva-ghāṭa  him  Subuddhi Rāya  met
= Meanwhile, when Rūpa Gosāñi came to Mathurā, Subuddhi Rāya met him at Dhruva-ghāṭa.

@180
formerly  when  Subuddhi Rāya  was  in Gauḍa  a governor
Husain Khān  the Saiyad  did  his  service
= Formerly, when Subuddhi Rāya was a governor (*adhikārī*) in Gauḍa, Saiyad Husain Khān was in his service.

@181
a large pond  to dig  him  munsif  made
a fault  finding  Rāya  him  with a whip  struck
= Rāya put him in charge (*munsīph*) of digging a large pond; finding a fault in his work, Rāya struck him with a whip.

@182
later  when  Husain Khān  in Gauḍa  king  became
Subuddhi Rāya  he  greatly  advanced
= Later, when Husain Khān became king of Gauḍa, he greatly advanced Subuddhi Rāya.

@183
his  wife  on his  body  sees  of the beating  the marks
Subuddhi Rāya  to kill  says  to the king
= His wife saw the marks of the whipping on his body and urged the king to have Subuddhi Rāya killed.

@184
the king  says  —  my  patron  Rāya  is  a father
him  shall kill  I  —  good  is not  the word
= The king said, “Rāya has been my patron; he is a father to me. That I should kill him — such talk is not right.”

@185
the wife  says  —  his caste  take  if  of life  not  you will deprive
the king  says  —  caste  if taken  he  not  will live
= His wife said, “If you will not take his life, then take his caste.” The king said, “If his caste is taken, he will not survive.”

@186
the wife  to die  wishes  the king  into a dilemma  fell
from a karoyā  water  his  into the mouth  had put
= His wife threatened to die, and the king was caught in a dilemma. At last he had water from a Muslim’s water pot (*karoyā*) put into Subuddhi Rāya’s mouth.

@187
then  Subuddhi Rāya  that  pretext  obtaining
to Vārāṇasī  came  all  worldly affairs  giving up
= Taking this as his pretext (*chadma*), Subuddhi Rāya gave up all his worldly affairs and came to Vārāṇasī.

@188
atonement  asked  he  of the paṇḍitas  the group
they  say  —  boiling  ghee  drinking  give up  your life
= He asked the *paṇḍitas* what atonement he should perform. They said, “Drink boiling ghee and give up your life.”

@189
someone  says  —  this  is not (so)  slight  the fault  is
hearing  remained  Rāya  having  doubt
= Another said, “Not so: the fault is slight.” Hearing this, Rāya was left in doubt.

@190
then  when  Mahāprabhu  to Vārāṇasī  came
Him  meeting  Rāya  his own  story  told
= Then, when Mahāprabhu came to Vārāṇasī, Rāya met Him and told Him his story.

@191
the Lord  says  —  from here  go  to Vṛndāvana
constantly  do  saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name
= The Lord said, “Go from here to Vṛndāvana and always chant the name of Kṛṣṇa in *saṅkīrtana*.

@192
by one  semblance of the Name (nāmābhāsa)  your  sins and faults  will go
and  the Name  taking  Kṛṣṇa’s feet  you will attain
= By the mere semblance of the Name (*nāmābhāsa*) your sins and faults will go; and by chanting the Name itself you will attain Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

@193
and  Kṛṣṇa’s name  taking  in Kṛṣṇa’s abode  residence
of the greatest sins  is  this  the atonement
= Chanting Kṛṣṇa’s name further, you will dwell in Kṛṣṇa’s abode. This is the atonement even for the greatest sins.”

@194
receiving  the order  Rāya  for Vṛndāvana  set out
Prayāga  Ayodhyā  by way of  to Naimiṣāraṇya  came
= Receiving this order, Rāya set out for Vṛndāvana and, by way of Prayāga and Ayodhyā, came to Naimiṣāraṇya.

@195
some  days  Rāya  in Naimiṣāraṇya  stayed
the Lord  Vṛndāvana  from  to Prayāga  went
= Rāya stayed some days in Naimiṣāraṇya; meanwhile the Lord went from Vṛndāvana to Prayāga.

@196
to Mathurā  coming  Rāya  of the Lord  news  received
of the Lord  meeting  not  getting  in his mind  great  sorrow  arose
= Coming to Mathurā, Rāya received news of the Lord; having missed meeting Him, he was deeply grieved.

@197
dry  wood  bringing  Rāya  sells  in Mathurā
five  six  paisā  there are  for one  each  bundle
= Rāya would gather dry wood and sell it in Mathurā, earning five or six *paisā* a bundle.

@198
himself  lives  of one  paisā  chickpeas  chewing
the other  paisā  with a merchant  keeps  saving
= He himself lived on one *paisā*’s worth of chickpeas and kept the rest of his money with a merchant.

@199
a poor  Vaiṣṇava  seeing  him  makes  eat
Gauḍīyas  when they come  yogurt  rice  oil  massage
= Whenever he saw a poor Vaiṣṇava, he fed him; and when Gauḍīyas came, he gave them yogurt and rice and had them massaged with oil.

@200
Rūpa Gosāñi  having come  to him  much  affection  showed
with himself  taking  the twelve  forests  showed
= When Rūpa Gosāñi came, Rāya showed him great affection, took him along and showed him the twelve forests.
