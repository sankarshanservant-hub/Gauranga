# English names and terms — CC Madhya 12 (verses 1–111; stage A, part 1; additions to `glossary-Adi01.md` … `glossary-Madhya11*.md`)

|---|---|---|
| Śrī Guṇḍicā-mandira | the temple of Śrī Guṇḍicā; the Guṇḍicā temple | 12.1, 12.73, 12.81 (chapter title: *The Guṇḍicā Temple*, after the Russian title) |
| kāṇe mudrā | the yogī’s rings in the ears | 12.20 (no material named in the text; *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: of conch, worn by the *kān-phaṭā* yogīs) |

| dui kula-nāśa | both his worlds are lost | 12.48 (Gauḍīya Maṭha reading *kula*; vedabase *loka*) |
| ātmā vai jāyate putraḥ | ‘One’s self is born as one’s son’ | 12.56 (scriptural maxim, quoted without source) |
| uddīpana | an *uddīpana* (one who awakens the remembrance of Kṛṣṇa) | 12.59 |
| kṛtārtha | I have attained My goal | 12.62 |
| paḍichā; paḍichā-pātra | the *paḍichā*; the *paḍichā-pātra* | 12.72–78 (temple superintendent; as in Madhya 11) |
| Jagamohana | the Śrī Jagamohana | 12.83 (hall before the sanctum; as in Madhya 4) |
| bhoga-mandira | the *bhoga-mandira* | 12.87 (hall where offerings are placed) |
| jhikura; kā̃kara | grit; pebbles | 12.88, 12.93 |
| khāparā | clay bowls | 12.98 |
| siṁhāsana | the throne | 12.82, 12.97–104 |
| praṇālikā | the drain | 12.103 |
| ghāṭa | the bathing *ghāṭa* | 12.107 |
| svarūpa, bhāratī, purī | Svarūpa (Dāmodara), (Brahmānanda) Bhāratī, (Paramānanda) Purī | 12.109 — names kept as in the text |
