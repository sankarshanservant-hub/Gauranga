# English names and terms — CC Antya 5, verses 1–82 (stage A, part 1; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 5»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` are used as given there: Rāmānanda Rāya; Pradyumna Miśra; Svarūpa (Dāmodara);
Vidyānagara; *devadāsī*; *deva-kanyā*; *sevya-buddhi*; *ṣaḍ-varga*, the six enemies; *viṣayī*; *siddha-deha*; *nitya-siddha*;
*prāya-siddha*, almost perfect; *vaiguṇya*, the ripening of karma (Anubhāṣya).
