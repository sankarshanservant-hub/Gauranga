@title The Return of Śrī Caitanya

@1

= I worship that raincloud, Gaura, who by the nectar of His own sight brought back to life the crops of His devotees, withered by the drought of separation.

@2

glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3

= Earlier, when Mahāprabhu had gone to the south, King Pratāparudra summoned Sārvabhauma.

@4

= Bowing to him, the king offered him a seat and then asked him for news of Mahāprabhu.

@5

= “I have heard that a great soul has come from Gauḍa and is staying in your house, and that He is supremely merciful.

@6

= Everyone says that He has shown you great mercy. Be kind enough to let me see Him.”

@7

= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “All that you have heard is true. But it is not possible for you to see Him.

@8

= He is a renounced sannyāsī who lives in solitude; not even in a dream would He meet a king.

@9

= Still, I would have found some way to arrange for you to see Him; but just now He has gone to the south.”

@10

the Bhaṭṭa  says  of a great soul  this  one  pastime
= The king asked, “Why did He leave Jagannātha and go away?” The Bhaṭṭācārya replied, “That is a pastime of great souls.

@11

= To purify the holy places, He travels on pilgrimage, and on that pretext He delivers worldly people.

@12

= “Devotees such as you are themselves holy places, my lord. By the Lord who dwells within your hearts, you make even the holy places holy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.13.10)

@13

= That is the unchanging nature of a Vaiṣṇava. But He is no jīva: He is the independent Lord Himself.”

@14

at His feet  falling  effort  making  why  not  did you keep (Him)
= The king said, “Why did you let Him go? Why did you not fall at His feet and do all you could to keep Him here?”

@15

directly  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  He  is not  dependent on others
= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “He is the Lord Himself, fully independent — Śrī Kṛṣṇa in person. He depends on no one.

@16
still  to keep  Him  great effort  I made
of the Lord  independent  the will  to keep (Him)  I could not
= Even so, I tried very hard to keep Him; but the Lord’s will is His own, and I could not hold Him back.”

@17

you  Him  Kṛṣṇa  call  therefore  as truth  I accept
= The king said, “Bhaṭṭa, you are the crest-jewel of the learned. Since you call Him Kṛṣṇa, I accept it as the truth.

@18
again  here  His  when there is  coming
once  seeing  I will make  fruitful  my eyes
= When He comes here again, let me see Him just once and make my eyes fruitful.”

@19

= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “He will come back soon. I would like a secluded place for Him to stay.

@20

= It should be near the Lord Jagannātha and also quiet. Please choose and give such a place.”

@21

= The king said, “Kāśī Miśra’s house is just such a place. It is near the Lord and very secluded.”

= Having said this, the king waited eagerly, and the Bhaṭṭācārya went and told Kāśī Miśra.

@23

= Kāśī Miśra said, “I am most fortunate: Prabhupāda will stay in my house!”

@24

= In this way all the people of Puruṣottama were eager to meet the Lord.

@25

= When everyone’s longing had grown intense, Mahāprabhu came back quickly from the south.

@26

= Hearing of it, everyone rejoiced. They all came to Sārvabhauma and pleaded with him:

@27

= “Arrange for us all to see the Lord. By your grace may we reach His feet.”

= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Tomorrow the Lord will go to Kāśī Miśra’s house; there I will bring you all to meet Him.”

= The next day Mahāprabhu went with the Bhaṭṭācārya and joyfully saw Jagannātha.

= There the servants of the Deity met Him and gave Him *mahā-prasāda*, and Mahāprabhu embraced them all.

= After seeing the Lord, Mahāprabhu came out, and the Bhaṭṭācārya brought Him to Kāśī Miśra’s house.

= Sārvabhauma said, “Lord, this residence is fit for You. Please accept it — that is Kāśī Miśra’s hope.”

= The Lord said, “This body belongs to all of you. Whatever you say, that I must do.”

@38

@39
