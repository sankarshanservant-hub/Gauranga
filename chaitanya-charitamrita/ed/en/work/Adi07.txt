= Bowing down to Śrī Caitanya, the only refuge of the helpless, who grants the highest goal even to those whose aims are lowly, I now describe His magnanimity in giving loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*).

@2

= All glory, all glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever has taken shelter of His feet is truly blessed.

@3

= Earlier I offered obeisance to the six truths, beginning with the guru, and I have described the truth of the guru (*guru-tattva*). Now I shall consider the other five.

@4

the five truths  taking  He enjoys  in the joy of saṅkīrtana
= The five truths (Pañca-tattva) descended together with Śrī Caitanya, and with the Pañca-tattva He delights in the joy of congregational chanting (*saṅkīrtana*).

@5

rasa  to relish  the truth  various  distinctions
= The Pañca-tattva is one substance; there is no difference among Them. Yet to relish *rasa*, this one truth appears in various distinct forms.

@6

having the form of a devotee and the nature of a devotee
the avatāra as a devotee  the one called a devotee
I bow to  having the energy of devotion
= “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who comprises the five truths: He appears in the form of a devotee and as the very nature of a devotee, as the avatāra of a devotee and as one known as a devotee, and as the energy of devotion.”

@7

without a second  the son of Nanda  the crown of rasikas
= Kṛṣṇa is the original Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*), the one Supreme Lord. He has no equal; He is the son of Nanda and the crown jewel of all who relish *rasa*.

@8

= He enjoys the *rāsa* dance and His other pastimes, and He is the lover of the young women of Vraja. All the others you see are His associates.

@9

= That same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and with Him have come those associates, every one of them blessed.

@10

= Caitanya, the Lord, alone is the truth of the Supreme Lord (*īśvara-tattva*). His pure body is filled with the mood of a devotee.

@11

Himself  to relish  Kṛṣṇa  takes  a devotee's mood
= Kṛṣṇa's sweetness has one wonderful property: to relish Himself, Kṛṣṇa takes on the mood of a devotee.

@12

‘the nature of a devotee’  His  Nityānanda  brother
= Therefore Caitanya Gosāñi assumes the mood of a devotee, and His brother Nityānanda is the very nature of a devotee (*bhakta-svarūpa*).

@13

= Ācārya Gosāñi (Advaita) is His avatāra as a devotee (*bhakta-avatāra*). We all sing of these three truths as the Lord (*prabhu*).

@14

= One is Mahāprabhu, and the other two are Prabhus. The two Prabhus serve the feet of Mahāprabhu.

= I regard these three truths as worshipable by all (*sarvārādhya*). The fourth, the truth of the devotee (*bhakta-tattva*), I know as the worshiper (*ārādhaka*).

@16

= Śrīvāsa and the countless millions of other devotees are counted within the truth of the pure devotee (*śuddha-bhakta*).

= Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others are avatāras of the Lord's energy (*śakti*); they are counted as His intimate devotees (*antaraṅga-bhaktas*).

@18-19

@22

= The good and the wicked, the lame, the dull-witted and the blind — all the people of the world were drowned in the flood of love.

= The more the five poured down the rain of love, the higher the waters rose, until they covered the three worlds.

= Seeing this, Mahāprabhu thought: “I tried to drown the whole world, but some have escaped, and My promise has been broken. To drown them too, I shall devise some playful stratagem.”

= The Lord is the avatāra of mercy, come to deliver everyone, and to deliver everyone He uses unlimited ingenuity.
