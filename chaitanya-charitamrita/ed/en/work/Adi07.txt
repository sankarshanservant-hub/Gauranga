@title Śrī Pañca-tattva

@1
of the helpless  the only refuge  having bowed to
to the lowly  the higher goal  who grants
Śrī Caitanya  is described  His
of loving devotion  the magnanimity
= Bowing down to Śrī Caitanya, the only refuge of the helpless, who grants the highest goal even to those whose aims are lowly, I now describe His magnanimity in giving loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*).

@2
glory  glory  to Mahāprabhu  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
His  who has taken shelter of the feet  he  greatly  blessed
= All glory, all glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever has taken shelter of His feet is truly blessed.

@3
earlier  to the guru and the rest  six  truths  I offered  obeisance
the truth of the guru  I have described  now  of the five  the consideration
= Earlier I offered obeisance to the six truths, beginning with the guru, and I have described the truth of the guru (*guru-tattva*). Now I shall consider the other five.

@4
the five truths  descended  Caitanya  together with
the five truths  taking  He enjoys  in the joy of saṅkīrtana
= The five truths (Pañca-tattva) descended together with Śrī Caitanya, and with the Pañca-tattva He delights in the joy of congregational chanting (*saṅkīrtana*).

@5
the five truths  one substance  there is not  any  difference
rasa  to relish  the truth  various  distinctions
= The Pañca-tattva is one substance; there is no difference among Them. Yet to relish *rasa*, this one truth appears in various distinct forms.

@6
comprising the five truths  Kṛṣṇa
having the form of a devotee and the nature of a devotee
the avatāra as a devotee  the one called a devotee
I bow to  having the energy of devotion
= “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who comprises the five truths: He appears in the form of a devotee and as the very nature of a devotee, as the avatāra of a devotee and as one known as a devotee, and as the energy of devotion.”

@7
original  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa  the one  Supreme Lord
without a second  the son of Nanda  the crown of rasikas
= Kṛṣṇa is the original Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*), the one Supreme Lord. He has no equal; He is the son of Nanda and the crown jewel of all who relish *rasa*.

@8
of the rāsa and other pastimes  the enjoyer  of the damsels of Vraja  the lover
others  as many  all  you see  His  associates
= He enjoys the *rāsa* dance and His other pastimes, and He is the lover of the young women of Vraja. All the others you see are His associates.

@9
that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
those  associates  with Him  all  blessed
= That same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and with Him have come those associates, every one of them blessed.

@10
alone  the truth of the Lord  Caitanya  the Lord
full of a devotee's mood  His  pure  body
= Caitanya, the Lord, alone is the truth of the Supreme Lord (*īśvara-tattva*). His pure body is filled with the mood of a devotee.

@11
of Kṛṣṇa's sweetness  one  wonderful  property
Himself  to relish  Kṛṣṇa  takes  a devotee's mood
= Kṛṣṇa's sweetness has one wonderful property: to relish Himself, Kṛṣṇa takes on the mood of a devotee.

@12
therefore  a devotee's mood  accepts  Caitanya  Gosāñi
‘the nature of a devotee’  His  Nityānanda  brother
= Therefore Caitanya Gosāñi assumes the mood of a devotee, and His brother Nityānanda is the very nature of a devotee (*bhakta-svarūpa*).

@13
‘the avatāra as a devotee’  His  Ācārya  Gosāñi
these  three  truths  we all  as Lord  regarding  sing of
= Ācārya Gosāñi (Advaita) is His avatāra as a devotee (*bhakta-avatāra*). We all sing of these three truths as the Lord (*prabhu*).

@14
one  Mahāprabhu  and  Prabhus  two
the two  Prabhus  serve  of Mahāprabhu  the feet
= One is Mahāprabhu, and the other two are Prabhus. The two Prabhus serve the feet of Mahāprabhu.

@15
these  three  truths  ‘worshipable by all’  as  I regard
fourth  which  the truth of the devotee  ‘the worshiper’  as  I know
= I regard these three truths as worshipable by all (*sarvārādhya*). The fourth, the truth of the devotee (*bhakta-tattva*), I know as the worshiper (*ārādhaka*).

@16
Śrīvāsa and the rest  as many  millions upon millions  devotees
of ‘pure devotees’  within the truth  of them all  the counting
= Śrīvāsa and the countless millions of other devotees are counted within the truth of the pure devotee (*śuddha-bhakta*).

@17
Gadādhara Paṇḍita and the rest  of the Lord  ‘energy’-avatāras
‘intimate devotees’  as  the counting  whose
= Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others are avatāras of the Lord's energy (*śakti*); they are counted as His intimate devotees (*antaraṅga-bhaktas*).

@18-19
whom all  taking  of the Lord  eternal  pastimes
whom all  taking  of the Lord  of kīrtana  the spreading
whom all  taking  He does  of prema  the relishing
whom all  taking  giving  He does  the treasure of prema
= With them the Lord enjoys His eternal pastimes, and with them He spreads the *kīrtana*. With them He relishes love (*prema*), and with them He gives away the treasure of love.

@20-21
those  five truths  joining together  to the earth  coming
of the ancient storehouse of prema  the seal  breaking open
the five  together  plunder  prema  do  relishing
as much  as much  they drink  thirst  grows  every moment
= Coming together to the earth, the five truths broke the seal of the ancient storehouse of love. Together the five plundered that love and relished it, and the more they drank, the more their thirst grew at every moment.

@22
again  again  making drink  become  greatly maddened
dance  weep  laugh  sing  as if  intoxicated
= Making others drink it again and again, They became wild with joy. They danced, wept, laughed and sang as if intoxicated.

@23
of the worthy and unworthy  discrimination  there is not  there is not  of proper and improper places
whoever  wherever  they find  there  they do  the gift of prema
= They made no distinction between the worthy and the unworthy, nor between proper and improper places. Whomever they found, wherever they found him, they gave him love.

@24
plundering  eating  giving away  the storehouse  they empty
wonderful  the storehouse  prema  a hundredfold  increases
= Plundering, tasting and giving away, they emptied the storehouse. Yet this storehouse is wonderful: the love in it increases a hundredfold.

@25
surged up  the flood of prema  on all sides  spreads
women  the old  children  the young  all  it drowns
= The flood of love surged up and spread in all directions, drowning women, the old, children and the young — everyone.

@26
the good  the wicked  the lame  the dull  the blind
in the flood of prema  drowned  of the world  the people
= The good and the wicked, the lame, the dull-witted and the blind — all the people of the world were drowned in the flood of love.

@27
the world  drowned  of the souls  became  the seed  destroyed
that  seeing  of the five  persons  supreme  joy
= The world was drowned, and the seed (of material existence) in the souls was destroyed. Seeing this, the five felt supreme joy.

@28
as much  as much  the rain of prema  make  the five persons
so much  so much  rises  the water  pervades  the three worlds
= The more the five poured down the rain of love, the higher the waters rose, until they covered the three worlds.

@29-30
Māyāvādīs  those devoted to karma  false logicians
blasphemers  unbelievers  all  scholars  the lowest
they  all  very clever  running  fled
that  flood  them all  to touch  could not
= But the Māyāvādīs, those devoted to fruitive work (*karma*), the false logicians, the blasphemers, the unbelievers (*pāṣaṇḍīs*) and all the degraded scholars — every one of them, being very clever, ran away, and the flood could not touch them.

@31-32
that  seeing  Mahāprabhu  does  thinking
the world  to drown  I  made  effort
some  some  escaped  the promise  became  broken
them all  to drown  I shall set  some  stratagem
= Seeing this, Mahāprabhu thought: “I tried to drown the whole world, but some have escaped, and My promise has been broken. To drown them too, I shall devise some playful stratagem.”

@33
thus  saying  in the mind  something  having made  consideration
the sannyāsa order  the Lord  did  accept
= Saying this and pondering it in His mind, the Lord accepted the renounced order (*sannyāsa*).

@34
twenty-four  years  He remained  in the householder order
in the twenty-fifth  year  He did  (took up) the dharma of a renunciant
= For twenty-four years He remained in the householder order, and in His twenty-fifth year He took up the life of a renunciant (*yati*).

@35
sannyāsa  having accepted  the Lord  did  attraction
all who  had fled  the logicians and the rest
= Having accepted *sannyāsa*, the Lord attracted all who had fled — the logicians and the rest.

@36
the scholars  the unbelievers  the karmīs  the blasphemers and the rest  all
they  coming  at the Lord's feet  become  bowed down
= The scholars, the unbelievers, the fruitive workers (*karmīs*), the blasphemers and all the others came and bowed down at the Lord's feet.

@37
offenses  He forgave  they drowned  in the waters of prema
who  will escape  of the Lord  the great net of prema
= He forgave their offenses, and they drowned in the waters of love. Who can escape the Lord's great net of love?

@38
all  to deliver  the Lord  the avatāra of mercy
all  to deliver  does  ingenuity  unlimited
= The Lord is the avatāra of mercy, come to deliver everyone, and to deliver everyone He uses unlimited ingenuity.

@39
then  His own  devotees  made  all  mlecchas  and others
all  escaped  only  of Kāśī  the Māyāvādīs
= Then He made His devotees of everyone, even the *mlecchas* (those outside Vedic culture). Only the Māyāvādīs of Kāśī escaped.

@40
to Vṛndāvana  going  the Lord  stayed  in Kāśī
the Māyāvādīs  Him  began  to criticize
= On His way to Vṛndāvana the Lord stayed in Kāśī (Vārāṇasī), and the Māyāvādīs began to criticize Him:

@41
a sannyāsī  having become  He does  singing  dancing
not  does  of Vedānta  hearing  does  saṅkīrtana
= “Though He is a *sannyāsī*, He sings and dances. He does not hear Vedānta; He performs *saṅkīrtana*.

@42
a fool  sannyāsī  His own dharma  not  knows
a sentimentalist  having become  wanders  sentimentalists  with
= This foolish *sannyāsī* does not know His own duty (*dharma*). He has become a sentimentalist (*bhāvuka*) and wanders about with sentimentalists.”

@43
this  all  hearing  the Lord  smiles  within His mind
ignoring  with anyone  not  did  conversation
= Hearing all this, the Lord smiled within Himself. He ignored them and spoke to none of them.

@44
ignoring  He did  to Mathurā  going
Mathurā  having seen  again  He did  return
= Ignoring them, He went on to Mathurā, and after seeing Mathurā He came back again.

@45
in Kāśī  a scribe  the śūdra Śrī Candraśekhara
his  in the house  stayed  the Lord  the independent  Lord
= In Kāśī lived a scribe named Śrī Candraśekhara, a śūdra. The Lord, the independent Supreme Lord, stayed in his house.

@46
of Tapana Miśra  in the house  taking His meals
of the sannyāsīs  in the company  not  accepts  invitations
= He took His meals (*bhikṣā*) regularly at the house of Tapana Miśra, and He did not accept invitations to dine with the *sannyāsīs*.

@47
Sanātana  Gosāñi  coming  there  met
his  instruction  for the sake of  the Lord  two months  stayed
= Sanātana Gosāñi came and met Him there, and the Lord stayed for two months to instruct him.

@48
him  He taught  all  of the Vaiṣṇavas  the dharma
of the Śrī Bhāgavata and other  scriptures  all  hidden  meanings
= He taught him the whole dharma of the Vaiṣṇavas and all the hidden meanings of the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* and the other scriptures.

@49
meanwhile  Candraśekhara  Tapana Miśra
distressed  being  at the Lord's feet  made  an appeal
= Meanwhile Candraśekhara and Tapana Miśra, deeply distressed, made an appeal at the Lord's feet:

@50
how much  shall we hear  O Lord  of You  the blasphemy
not  we can  bear  now  we shall give up  life
= “O Lord, how long must we hear You blasphemed? We cannot bear it; now we shall give up our lives.

@51
You  blaspheme  all  the sannyāsīs
to hear  not  we can  breaks  heart and ears
= All the *sannyāsīs* blaspheme You. We cannot bear to hear it; our hearts and ears are breaking.”

@52
this  hearing  remains  the Lord  slightly  smiling
at that  time  one  brāhmaṇa  met  coming
= Hearing this, the Lord only smiled slightly. At that time a brāhmaṇa came to meet Him.

@53
coming  a request  makes  the feet  clasping
one  thing  I beg  please give  pleased  being
= He came, clasped the Lord's feet and made a request: “I beg one thing of You. Be pleased with me and grant it.

@54
all  the sannyāsīs  I  have made  invitation
You  if  come  fulfilled  becomes  my  mind
= I have invited all the *sannyāsīs*. If You come too, my heart's desire will be fulfilled.

@55
not  You go  to gatherings of sannyāsīs  this  I  know
on me  mercy  show  the invitation  accepting
= I know that You do not go to gatherings of *sannyāsīs*, but please show me mercy and accept my invitation.”

@56
the Lord  smiling  the invitation  did  accept
to the sannyāsīs  mercy  for the sake of  this  stratagem  His
= The Lord smiled and accepted the invitation. He used this stratagem to show mercy to the *sannyāsīs*.

@57
that  brāhmaṇa  knows  the Lord  not  goes  anyone's  house
His  by the inspiration  Him  great insistence  makes
= The brāhmaṇa knew that the Lord does not go to anyone's house, yet, inspired by the Lord Himself, he pressed Him earnestly.

@58
on the next  day  went  the Lord  that  brāhmaṇa's house
He saw  were seated  the sannyāsīs  together
= The next day the Lord went to the brāhmaṇa's house and saw the *sannyāsīs* seated together.

@59
to all  offering obeisance  went  to wash His feet
feet  having washed  sat  in that  place
= He offered obeisance to them all and went to wash His feet. Having washed His feet, He sat down right there, in that place.

@60
sitting  He made  some  majesty  manifestation
greatly effulgent  body  of millions of suns  the radiance
= Sitting there, He revealed a little of His divine majesty (*aiśvarya*). His body blazed with great effulgence, shining like millions of suns.
