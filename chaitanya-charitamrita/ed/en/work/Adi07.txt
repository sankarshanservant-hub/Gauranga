= Bowing down to Śrī Caitanya, the only refuge of the helpless, who grants the highest goal even to those whose aims are lowly, I now describe His magnanimity in giving loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*).

@2

= All glory, all glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever has taken shelter of His feet is truly blessed.

@3

= Earlier I offered obeisance to the six truths, beginning with the guru, and I have described the truth of the guru (*guru-tattva*). Now I shall consider the other five.

@4

the five truths  taking  He enjoys  in the joy of saṅkīrtana
= The five truths (Pañca-tattva) descended together with Śrī Caitanya, and with the Pañca-tattva He delights in the joy of congregational chanting (*saṅkīrtana*).

@5

rasa  to relish  the truth  various  distinctions
= The Pañca-tattva is one substance; there is no difference among Them. Yet to relish *rasa*, this one truth appears in various distinct forms.

@6

having the form of a devotee and the nature of a devotee
the avatāra as a devotee  the one called a devotee
I bow to  having the energy of devotion
= “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who comprises the five truths: He appears in the form of a devotee and as the very nature of a devotee, as the avatāra of a devotee and as one known as a devotee, and as the energy of devotion.”

@7

without a second  the son of Nanda  the crown of rasikas
= Kṛṣṇa is the original Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*), the one Supreme Lord. He has no equal; He is the son of Nanda and the crown jewel of all who relish *rasa*.

@8

= He enjoys the *rāsa* dance and His other pastimes, and He is the lover of the young women of Vraja. All the others you see are His associates.

@9

= That same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and with Him have come those associates, every one of them blessed.

@10

= Caitanya, the Lord, alone is the truth of the Supreme Lord (*īśvara-tattva*). His pure body is filled with the mood of a devotee.

@11

Himself  to relish  Kṛṣṇa  takes  a devotee's mood
= Kṛṣṇa's sweetness has one wonderful property: to relish Himself, Kṛṣṇa takes on the mood of a devotee.

@12

‘the nature of a devotee’  His  Nityānanda  brother
= Therefore Caitanya Gosāñi assumes the mood of a devotee, and His brother Nityānanda is the very nature of a devotee (*bhakta-svarūpa*).

@13

= Ācārya Gosāñi (Advaita) is His avatāra as a devotee (*bhakta-avatāra*). We all sing of these three truths as the Lord (*prabhu*).

@14

= One is Mahāprabhu, and the other two are Prabhus. The two Prabhus serve the feet of Mahāprabhu.

= I regard these three truths as worshipable by all (*sarvārādhya*). The fourth, the truth of the devotee (*bhakta-tattva*), I know as the worshiper (*ārādhaka*).

@16

= Śrīvāsa and the countless millions of other devotees are counted within the truth of the pure devotee (*śuddha-bhakta*).

= Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others are avatāras of the Lord's energy (*śakti*); they are counted as His intimate devotees (*antaraṅga-bhaktas*).

@18-19

@22

= The good and the wicked, the lame, the dull-witted and the blind — all the people of the world were drowned in the flood of love.

= The more the five poured down the rain of love, the higher the waters rose, until they covered the three worlds.

= Seeing this, Mahāprabhu thought: “I tried to drown the whole world, but some have escaped, and My promise has been broken. To drown them too, I shall devise some playful stratagem.”

= The Lord is the avatāra of mercy, come to deliver everyone, and to deliver everyone He uses unlimited ingenuity.

= Hearing this, the Lord smiled and said, “If you will not take offense, I shall explain.”

@103

= Hearing this, all the *sannyāsīs* said, “We see You as Nārāyaṇa Himself.

@104

= Hearing Your words soothes our ears, and seeing Your sweetness soothes our eyes.

@105

= Your presence fills everyone's mind with joy. Your words can never be improper.”

@106
the Lord  says  the Vedānta-sūtra  the Lord's words

= The Lord said, “The *Vedānta-sūtra* is the word of the Supreme Lord, spoken by Śrī Nārāyaṇa in the form of Vyāsa.

@107
mistakes  illusion  the tendency to cheat  imperfect senses

= Mistakes, illusion, the tendency to cheat and imperfect senses — none of these faults exist in the words of the Lord.

@108

= The truth that the *sūtras* teach together with the Upaniṣads is supremely great when taken in its primary, direct sense (*mukhya-vṛtti*).

@109

= But the commentary that the Ācārya (Śaṅkara) wrote by the secondary, indirect sense (*gauṇa-vṛtti*) ruins everything for those who hear it.

@110

the secondary meaning  he made  the primary  meaning  covering
= He is not at fault: by the order of the Lord he covered the primary meaning and put forward a secondary one.

@111

= In its primary sense the word ‘Brahman’ means Bhagavān, the Lord, who is full of spiritual majesty and has none above or equal to Him.

@112

= His powers and His body are all spiritual in form. Covering these spiritual powers, he calls Him ‘formless’ (*nirākāra*).

@113
spiritual bliss  body  His  abode  entourage
them  he calls  of material goodness  a transformation
= His body, His abode and His entourage are spiritual bliss, yet he calls them a transformation of material goodness (*sattva*).

@114
his  fault  there is not  he  carrying out an order  a servant

= He is not at fault, for he is a servant carrying out an order. But whoever listens to him is utterly ruined.

@115
material  considering  regards  Viṣṇu's body
blasphemy of Viṣṇu  other  there is not  this  above
= He regards the body of Viṣṇu as material, and there is no blasphemy of Viṣṇu greater than this.

@116

of the soul  the nature  like  of a spark  a particle
= The Lord is like a blazing fire, and the soul by nature is like a particle of a spark.

@117

the Gītā, the Viṣṇu Purāṇa and the rest  in that  the evidence
= The soul is energy (*śakti*); Kṛṣṇa is the possessor of energy (*śaktimān*). The *Gītā*, the *Viṣṇu Purāṇa* and other scriptures are the evidence for this.

@118
inferior  this  than this  but  another
energy  know  My  superior

by which  this  is sustained  world
= “This material nature is inferior. But know, O mighty-armed one, that beyond it I have another, superior energy, which consists of the souls (*jīvas*) and by which this world is sustained.” (Bhagavad-gītā 7.5)

@119
Viṣṇu's energy  superior  is declared
called the knower of the field  likewise  superior
called ignorance and karma  another

= “The energy of Viṣṇu is declared to be superior; the energy called the knower of the field (*kṣetrajña*) is likewise superior. The other, called ignorance and karma, is considered the third energy.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 6.7.61)

@120
such  the truth of the soul  taking  writing  the Supreme Truth
covered  he made  the supreme  greatness of the Lord
= Taking the truth of the soul in this way and calling it the Supreme Truth, he covered the supreme greatness of the Lord.

@121
of Vyāsa  in the sūtras  speaks  the doctrine of ‘transformation’
Vyāsa  mistaken  saying  he  raised  dispute
= Vyāsa's *sūtras* teach the doctrine of transformation (*pariṇāma-vāda*). But he (Śaṅkara) declared that Vyāsa was mistaken and raised a dispute.

@122
by the doctrine of transformation  the Lord  becomes  subject to change
thus  saying  the doctrine of ‘illusion’  establishment  which  he makes
= He argued that by the doctrine of transformation the Lord would become subject to change, and on that ground he established the doctrine of illusion (*vivarta-vāda*).

@123
in truth  the doctrine of transformation  that  alone  is the evidence
in the body  the idea of self  is  of illusion  the place
= In truth, the doctrine of transformation alone is the evidence. The proper place of illusion (*vivarta*) is the idea that the body is the self.

@124
with inconceivable energy endowed  Śrī Bhagavān
by His will  in the form of the world  undergoes  transformation
= Śrī Bhagavān, who possesses inconceivable energy, by His will undergoes transformation in the form of the world.

@125

material  touchstone  in that  as an example  we take
= Yet by that inconceivable energy He remains unchanged. As an example we take the material touchstone (*cintāmaṇi*).

@126
various  heaps of jewels  come  the touchstone  from

= Heaps of various jewels come from the touchstone, yet the gem itself remains unchanged in its nature.

@127
in a material object  if  inconceivable power  there is
of the Lord  inconceivable energy  in this  what  wonder
= If a material object has inconceivable power, what wonder is there in the inconceivable energy of the Lord?

@128
‘praṇava’  that  the great statement  of the Vedas  the source
the Lord's own form  praṇava  of all the worlds  the abode
= The *praṇava* (*oṁ*) is the great statement (*mahā-vākya*), the root of the Vedas. The *praṇava* is the very form of the Lord, the abode of all the worlds.

@129
of the shelter of all  of the Lord  makes  praṇava  the indication
‘tat tvam asi’  the statement  is  of the Vedas  one  part
= The *praṇava* points to the Lord, the shelter of all, whereas the statement ‘*tat tvam asi*’ (“you are that”) is only one part of the Vedas.

@130
‘praṇava’  the great statement  that  making  covering

= Covering the *praṇava*, the true great statement, he set up ‘*tat tvam asi*’ as the great statement.

@131
in all the Vedas and sūtras  makes  Kṛṣṇa's  naming
the primary meaning  giving up  made  by indirect sense  explanation
= In all the Vedas and *sūtras* it is Kṛṣṇa who is named. But he gave up the primary meaning and explained them by indirect interpretation (*lakṣaṇā*).

@132
self-evident  the Vedas  of evidence  the crown jewel
indirect interpretation  if one makes  of self-evidence  the loss
= The Vedas are self-evident (*svataḥ-pramāṇa*), the crown jewel of all evidence. Interpreting them indirectly destroys their self-evidence.

@133
in this way  in each sūtra  the natural meaning  giving up
a secondary meaning  explanation  he makes  all  imagination  making
= In this way, giving up the natural meaning of every *sūtra*, he explained a secondary meaning, inventing it all from his imagination.”

@134

= In this way the Lord pointed out the faults in the explanation of every *sūtra*, and the *sannyāsīs* were amazed to hear it.

@135

@136

for the sake of the sampradāya  as truth  this  we accept
= We all know that these meanings are the Ācārya's own imagination. Only out of loyalty to our *sampradāya* do we accept them as the truth.

@137

= Explain the primary meaning, and let us see Your power.” Then the Lord began to explain all the *sūtras* by their primary meaning:

@138

= “The greatest reality, which we call ‘Brahman’, is Śrī Bhagavān. He is full of six kinds of majesty (*aiśvarya*) and is the abode of the Supreme Truth.

@139

= In His own nature and majesty there is not a trace of māyā. Bhagavān is the object of relationship (*sambandha*) in all the Vedas.

= If we call Him impersonal (*nirviśeṣa*) and do not accept His spiritual energy, we accept only half of His nature, and His completeness is lost.

@141

= The means we take up to attain Bhagavān is devotion (*bhakti*) that begins with hearing; it is what brings one to Kṛṣṇa.

@142

@143

= By love Kṛṣṇa comes under the control of His devotee, and by love one attains the joyful *rasa* of serving Kṛṣṇa.
