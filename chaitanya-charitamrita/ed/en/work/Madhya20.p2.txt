# CC Madhya 20, stage A, part 2 (verses 136–270); read by build_en.py together with Madhya20.txt

@136
thus  the scriptures  say  karma  jñāna  yoga  giving up
by devotion  Kṛṣṇa  subdued  becomes  by devotion  Him  worship
= “In the same way the scriptures say: give up *karma*, *jñāna* and *yoga*. By devotion Kṛṣṇa is brought under control; worship Him with devotion.

@137
not  wins  Me  yoga
not  sāṅkhya  dharma  O Uddhava
not  Vedic study  austerity  renunciation
as  devotion  to Me  intense
= (Śrī Kṛṣṇa said:) “O Uddhava, neither yoga nor sāṅkhya, neither dharma nor study of the Vedas, neither austerity nor renunciation can win Me as intense devotion to Me does.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.20)

@138
by devotion  I  by exclusive  am attained
by faith  the Self  dear  of the saintly
devotion  purifies  fixed on Me
dog-eaters  even  from (the stain of) birth
= “I, the dear Self of the saintly, am attained only by exclusive devotion born of faith. Devotion fixed on Me purifies even dog-eaters from the stain of their birth.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.21)

@139
therefore  devotion  of Kṛṣṇa  of attaining  the means
abhidheya  calling  it  in all the scriptures  they sing
= “Therefore devotion is the means of attaining Kṛṣṇa. All the scriptures sing of it as *abhidheya*, the means.

@140
wealth  on obtaining  as  of happiness  enjoyment  the fruit  one gets
from happiness  enjoyment  sorrow  by itself  flees
= “When one obtains wealth, one gets its fruit, the enjoyment of happiness; and before the enjoyment of happiness sorrow flees by itself.

@141
so  of devotion  as the fruit  for Kṛṣṇa  love  arises
in love  the tasting of Kṛṣṇa  when there is  worldly existence  destruction  attains
= “So too, as the fruit of devotion, love (*prema*) for Kṛṣṇa arises; and when Kṛṣṇa is tasted in love, worldly existence comes to an end.

@142
of poverty  the destruction  of worldly existence  the end  of love  the fruit  is not
of love  the happiness  the enjoyment  the chief  goal  is
= “The end of poverty and the end of worldly existence are not the ‘fruit’ of love. To enjoy the happiness of love is the chief goal (*prayojana*).

@143
in the Vedic  scriptures  are told  sambandha  abhidheya  prayojana
Kṛṣṇa  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  love  three  great treasures
= “The Vedic scriptures speak of *sambandha*, *abhidheya* and *prayojana* — Kṛṣṇa, devotion to Kṛṣṇa and love: these are the three great treasures.

@144
in the Vedas and the rest  all  scriptures  Kṛṣṇa  the chief  sambandha
of Him  by knowledge  as a matter of course  goes  of māyā  the bondage
= “In the Vedas and all the scriptures, Kṛṣṇa is the chief object of relationship (*sambandha*). By knowing Him, the bondage of *māyā* falls away as a matter of course.

@145
to bewilder  the moving and unmoving  world  those  those  Purāṇas and Āgamas
that  that  very  indeed  deity  supreme  let them prattle  till the end of the kalpa
in conclusion  however  one  alone  Bhagavān  Viṣṇu  of all the scriptures
the subjects  of analysis  to the comparison  when brought  is ascertained
= “To bewilder the world of moving and unmoving beings, let the various Purāṇas and Āgamas prattle until the end of the *kalpa* that this or that deity is supreme. But when the subjects of all the scriptures are carefully analyzed and compared, the conclusion establishes that Viṣṇu alone is Bhagavān.” (Padma Purāṇa)

@146
by the primary  secondary  sense  or  by affirmation and negation
of the Vedas  the declaration  only  speaks of  Kṛṣṇa
= “Whether by the primary or the secondary sense of words, or by affirmation and negation, the declaration of the Vedas speaks only of Kṛṣṇa.

@147
what  it enjoins  what  it declares
what  naming  it considers as an option
thus  of this (Veda)  the heart  in the world
not  another  than Me  knows  anyone
= “What does it enjoin? What does it declare? What does it name, only to weigh it as an option? No one in the world but Me knows the heart of the Veda.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.21.42)

@148
Me  it enjoins  it declares  Me
having been considered  is set aside  indeed  I
this much  of all the Vedas  the meaning
the Veda  resting on  Me  the differences
as mere māyā  having named  at the end
having negated  comes to rest
= “It enjoins Me, it declares Me; and what it considers and then sets aside is also Me. This is the meaning of all the Vedas: resting on Me, the Veda names the differences of this world as mere *māyā*, negates them, and in the end comes to rest.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.21.43)

@149
Kṛṣṇa’s  own nature  is infinite  splendor  is boundless
the spiritual potency  the potency of māyā  the potency of the jīvas  and
= “Kṛṣṇa’s own nature (*svarūpa*) is infinite, and His splendor is boundless: the spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*), the potency of *māyā* (*māyā-śakti*) and the potency of the *jīvas* (*jīva-śakti*).

@150
Vaikuṇṭha  the multitude of universes  of the potencies  the works  are
of the potency of His own nature  of the works of the potencies  Kṛṣṇa  the shelter
= “Vaikuṇṭha and the countless universes are the works of these potencies. Of His own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*) and of all the works of His potencies, Kṛṣṇa is the shelter.

@151
in the Tenth (Canto)  the tenth  the subject
of the sheltered  the shelter  the embodiment
Śrī Kṛṣṇa  named  the supreme  abode
of the world  the abode  I bow  to That
= “In the Tenth Canto the tenth subject is revealed: the embodiment of the shelter of all who take shelter, the supreme abode named Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the abode of the world. To That I bow down.” (Śrīdhara Svāmī, *Bhāvārtha-dīpikā* on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.1.1)

@152
of Kṛṣṇa  on the own nature  the deliberation  hear  Sanātana
of non-dual  knowledge  the Truth  in Vraja  the son of Vraja’s king
= “Hear, Sanātana, an inquiry into Kṛṣṇa’s own nature. He is the Truth of non-dual knowledge; in Vraja He is the son of the king of Vraja.

@153
of all  the origin  of all  the source of the parts  of youths  the crown
of spirit and bliss  whose body  the shelter of all  the Lord of all
= “He is the origin of all and the source of all parts (*aṁśī*), the crown of youths. His body is spirit and bliss; He is the shelter of all and the Lord of all.

@154
the Lord  Supreme  Kṛṣṇa
of being  knowledge  bliss  whose form
without beginning  the beginning  Govinda
of all  causes  the cause
= “Kṛṣṇa, Govinda, is the Supreme Lord; His form is eternal being, knowledge and bliss. He has no beginning, yet He is the beginning of all, the cause of all causes.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.1)

@155
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  Govinda  the supreme  name
with all opulence  full  whose  Goloka  the eternal abode
= “Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself; ‘Govinda’ is His supreme name. He is full of all opulence, and Goloka is His eternal abode.

@156
these  and  portions  and parts  of the Puruṣa
Kṛṣṇa  but  Bhagavān  Himself
by the enemies of Indra  troubled  the world
make happy  in age  after age
= “All these (avatāras) are portions (*aṁśa*) and parts of portions (*kalā*) of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself. Age after age They bring happiness to the world when it is troubled by the enemies of Indra.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.3.28)

@157
knowledge  yoga  devotion  of the three  practices  by the power
Brahman  Paramātmā  Bhagavān  threefold  He is manifest
= “According to the three practices — knowledge, *yoga* and devotion — He is manifest in three ways: as Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.

@158
they call  that  of truth  the knowers
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual
Brahman  thus  Paramātmā  thus
Bhagavān  thus  is named
= “The knowers of the Truth call the non-dual knowledge the Truth; it is named Brahman, Paramātmā and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.11)

@159
Brahman  of the body  the effulgence  His  as the featureless  manifestation
the sun  as  to the fleshly  eye  as mere light  appears
= “Brahman is the effulgence of His body, His featureless manifestation — just as the sun appears to the fleshly eye as nothing but light.

@160
whose  effulgence  of the all-mighty  of universes  of millions
in millions  unlimited  earths and the like  opulences  distinct from
that  Brahman  undivided  infinite  all-complete
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “I worship Govinda, the original Person. Brahman — undivided, infinite and complete, distinct from all the opulences of countless universes, their earths and all else — is the effulgence of Him, the Almighty.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.40)

@161
the Paramātmā  He who  He  of Kṛṣṇa  one  part
of the soul  the Soul  is  Kṛṣṇa  of all  the crown
= “He who is the Paramātmā is a part of Kṛṣṇa; Kṛṣṇa, the crown of all, is the Soul of the soul.

@162
Kṛṣṇa  this  know  you
the Soul  of all souls
for the good of the world  He  also  here
as if embodied  appears  by His own potency
= “Know this Kṛṣṇa to be the Soul of all souls. For the good of the world He appears here, by His own potency, as if He were an embodied being.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.55)

@163
but  or  with much  this
what (use)  by knowing  for you  Arjuna
pervading  I  this  entire
by one portion  I stand  the universe
= “But what need have you, Arjuna, of all this detailed knowledge? By one portion of Myself I pervade and support this entire universe.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 10.42)

@164
by devotion  of Bhagavān  the realization  in His full form
in one and the same  form  His  infinite  forms
= “Through devotion Bhagavān is realized in His full form: in His one form are His infinite forms.

@165
svayaṁ-rūpa  tad-ekātma-rūpa  āveśa  by name
first of all  in three forms  abides  Bhagavān
= “First of all, Bhagavān abides in three forms, named *svayaṁ-rūpa*, *tad-ekātma-rūpa* and *āveśa*.
