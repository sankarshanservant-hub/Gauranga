# 105 as Madhya 23.36 (here navaḥ premā, “this new prema”).

@title The Divine Madness of Śrī Caitanya

@1
I bow down  to Him  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya
of those devoted to their mothers  the crown jewel
raving  His face  rubbing
in the spring garden  sported  who
= I bow down to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the crown jewel of all who are devoted to their mothers. Raving in divine madness, He rubbed His face against the walls; and He sported in the spring garden.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  Mahāprabhu  for Kṛṣṇa  absorbed in love
mad  ravings  makes  night  and day
= In this way Mahāprabhu, absorbed in love for Kṛṣṇa, spoke the ravings of divine madness night and day.

@4
to the Lord  very  dear  Paṇḍita  Jagadānanda
in whose  conduct  the Lord  finds  joy
= Jagadānanda Paṇḍita was very dear to the Lord, and the Lord took joy in his conduct.

@5
every  year  the Lord  him  sends  to Nadia
by separation  grieved  knowing  His mother  to console
= Every year the Lord sent him to Nadia to console His mother, for He knew how she grieved in separation from Him.

@6
to Nadia  go  to Mother  tell  My obeisances
in My  name  the lotus feet  touch  her
= “Go to Nadia and convey My obeisances to Mother. Touch her lotus feet on My behalf.

@7
tell  her  you  please remember
every day  coming  your  I worship  feet
= Tell her: ‘Please remember — every day I come and bow at your feet.

@8
on whatever  day  your  wish  to feed (Me)
on that  day  surely  I  do  eat
= On any day you wish to feed Me, I certainly come and eat.

@9
your  service  giving up  I  took  sannyāsa
a madman  becoming  I  made  of duty  ruin
= I gave up serving you and took *sannyāsa*. I became a madman and abandoned My duty.

@10
this  offence  you  not  take  My
on you  dependent  I  son  indeed  your
= Do not hold this offence against Me. I am dependent on you — I am your son.

@11
in Nīlācala  stay  I  your  by order
as long as  I live  so long  I  cannot  leave
= I stay in Nīlācala by your order, and as long as I live I cannot leave it.’ ”

@12
in the cowherd  pastime  received  which  prasāda  cloth
to Mother  sends  that  of Purī  at the word
= The cloth *prasāda* that the Lord received at the festival of Jagannātha’s cowherd pastime He sent to His mother at the request of Paramānanda Purī.

@13
Jagannātha’s  best  prasāda  bringing  with care
to Mother  separately  sends  and  to the devotees
= He also took care to obtain the best of Jagannātha’s *prasāda*, and He sent it separately to His mother and to the devotees.

@14
of those devoted to their mothers  the Lord  is  the crown jewel
sannyāsa  having taken  always  serves  His mother
= The Lord is the crown jewel of all who are devoted to their mothers. Even after taking *sannyāsa*, He always served His mother.

@15
Jagadānanda  to Nadia  going  with Mother  met
the Lord’s  as many  messages  all  told
= Jagadānanda went to Nadia, met the Lord’s mother and told her all the Lord’s messages.

@16
the Ācārya and other  devotees  met  prasāda  giving
from Mother  leave  took  a month  staying
= He met Advaita Ācārya and the other devotees and gave them *prasāda*. After staying a month, he took leave of the Lord’s mother.

@17
of the Ācārya  to  going  leave  asked
Ācārya  Gosāñi  for the Lord  a message  gave
= He went to the Ācārya to take leave of him, and Ācārya Gosāñi gave him a message for the Lord.

@18
a tarjā  riddle  the Ācārya  speaks  in hints
the Lord  only  understands  anyone  understand  not  can
= The Ācārya spoke a riddle in the form of a *tarjā* (*tarjā-prahelī*), full of hints, which only the Lord could understand; no one else could.

@19
to the Lord  tell  my  ten million  obeisances
this  submission  His  at the feet  my
= “Convey my countless obeisances to the Lord, and lay this submission of mine at His feet:

@20
to the madman  tell  people  have become  mad
to the madman  tell  in the market  not  sells  rice
= Tell the madman (*bāul*) that the people have gone mad. Tell the madman that rice no longer sells in the market.

@21
to the madman  tell  in work  is not  the āul
to the madman  tell  this  has said  a madman
= Tell the madman that the *āul* — the one mad with love — is no longer engaged in worldly affairs. Tell the madman that a madman has said this.”

@22
this  hearing  Jagadānanda  to laugh  began
to Nīlācala  coming  then  to the Lord  told
= Hearing this, Jagadānanda began to laugh. Then he returned to Nīlācala and told it to the Lord.

@23
the tarjā  hearing  Mahāprabhu  slightly  smiled
his  whatever  order  saying  silence  kept
= Hearing the *tarjā*, Mahāprabhu smiled slightly and said, “That is his order.” Then He fell silent.

@24
learning (of this)  Svarūpa  Gosāñi  the Lord  asked
this  of the tarjā  the meaning  to understand  I could not
= Learning of this, Svarūpa Gosāñi asked the Lord, “I could not understand the meaning of this *tarjā*.”[^24-1]

@25
the Lord  says  the Ācārya  is  a worshipper  great
of the Āgama  scriptures  in the rules  and regulations  expert
= The Lord said, “The Ācārya is a great worshipper, expert in the rules and regulations of the Āgama scriptures.

@26
for worship  the Deity  makes  invocation
for worship  some  time  makes  keeping (Him) present
= For worship he invokes the Deity, and for the worship he keeps the Deity present for some time.

@27
the worship  completion  when  afterwards  makes  sending away
of the tarjā  not  I know  the meaning  what  his  mind
= When the worship is completed, he then sends the Deity away. I do not know the meaning of the *tarjā*, or what is in his mind.

@28
a great  master of mystic power  the Ācārya  in tarjās  skilled
even I  to understand  cannot  of the tarjā  the meaning
= The Ācārya is a great master of mystic power, skilled in *tarjās*. Even I cannot understand the meaning of his *tarjā*.”

@29
hearing  astonished  became  all  the devotees
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  somewhat  became  downcast
= Hearing this, all the devotees were astonished, and Svarūpa Gosāñi became somewhat downcast.

@30
that  day  from  of the Lord  another  state  arose
of Kṛṣṇa  separation’s  state  doubly  increased
= From that day the Lord’s condition changed: His feeling of separation from Kṛṣṇa grew twice as strong.

@31
of madness  ravings  behaviour  makes  night  and day
in Rādhā’s  feeling  absorbed  separation  grows  every moment
= Night and day He behaved and raved like one mad. Absorbed in the feelings of Rādhā, He felt the separation grow every moment.

@32
suddenly  appears  Kṛṣṇa’s  to Mathurā  departure
of udghūrṇā  the state  arose  of madness  a symptom
= Suddenly Kṛṣṇa’s departure for Mathurā appeared before Him, and the state of *udghūrṇā*, a symptom of divine madness, arose in Him.

@33
Rāmānanda’s  neck  holding  makes  ravings
Svarūpa  asks  taking (him) as  His own  friends
= Holding Rāmānanda by the neck, He raved, and He questioned Svarūpa, taking him for one of His own friends (*sakhīs*).

@34
long ago  as  Viśākhā  Rādhikā  asked
that  verse  reciting  ravings  to make  began
= Reciting the verse with which Rādhikā once questioned Viśākhā, He began to rave:

@35
where  of Nanda’s  dynasty  the moon  where  with peacock feathers  adorned
where  deep  of the flute  the sound  where  indeed  of the sapphire  the luster
where  in the rāsa  with rapture  dancing  where  O friend  of life  the saving medicine (-)
treasure  My  dearest friend  where  alas  alas  ah  fie on  the Creator
= “O friend, where is Kṛṣṇa, the moon of Nanda’s dynasty, adorned with peacock feathers? Where is He whose flute sounds so deep, whose luster is like the sapphire? Where is He who dances in the rapture of the *rāsa*? Where is He — My treasure, the medicine that saves My life, My dearest friend? Alas! Alas! Fie on the Creator!” (Lalita-mādhava, 3.25)

@36
of the king of Vraja  the dynasty  the ocean of milk
Kṛṣṇa  in it  the full  moon
being born  made  the world  illumined
of luster  the nectar  who  drink
constantly  drinking  live
of Vraja’s  people  the eyes  cakora birds
= “The dynasty of the king of Vraja is an ocean of milk, and Kṛṣṇa, born in it like the full moon, has lit up the world. The eyes of the people of Vraja are *cakora* birds: they drink the nectar of His luster constantly, and only by drinking it do they live.

@37
O friend  O  where  Kṛṣṇa  let (Me) see
for a moment  whose  face
not  seeing  breaks  the heart
quickly  show  not  remains  life
= O friend, where is Kṛṣṇa? Let Me see Him! If I do not see His face even for a moment, My heart breaks. Show Him to Me quickly, or My life will not remain.

@38
these  of Vraja  the women
by the sun of desire  scorched  night lotuses
own  of the rays (hands)  the nectar  giving  as a gift
blossoming  makes  who
where  My  moon  that
show  O friend  save  My  life
= The women of Vraja are night-blooming lotuses scorched by the sun of loving desire (*kāma*). Where is that moon of Mine who makes them blossom with the gift of the nectar of His rays — His hands? O friend, show Him to Me and save My life!

@39
where  that  of the crown  the beauty
of the peacock feathers  the waving
in a new cloud  like  a rainbow
the yellow garment  lightning’s  flash
the pearl  necklace  a line of herons
a new cloud  surpassing  the dark body
= Where is the beauty of His crown, with its waving peacock feathers, like a rainbow in a fresh cloud? His yellow garment is a flash of lightning, His pearl necklace a line of herons, and His dark body surpasses a fresh rain cloud.

@40
once  whose  in the eyes  catches
always  his  in the heart  stays awake
Kṛṣṇa’s  body  like  mango  sap
of women  into the mind  entering  goes
with effort  not  comes out
a body  is not  of the seyākul  a thorn
= Once Kṛṣṇa’s body catches someone’s eye, it stays awake in that heart forever, like the sap of the mango tree. It enters the minds of women and will not come out, however hard they try. It is not a body — it is a thorn of the *seyākul* bush!
