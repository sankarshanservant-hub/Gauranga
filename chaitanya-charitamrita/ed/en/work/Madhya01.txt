@title The Pastimes of Śrī Caitanya

@1
whose  by the grace  an ignorant person  even
at once  omniscience  attains
that  Śrī Caitanyadeva  to me
the Lord  may He be fully gracious
= May the Lord Śrī Caitanyadeva be fully gracious to me — He by whose grace even an ignorant person at once becomes all-knowing.

@2
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
(and) Nityānanda  risen together
on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and the moon
wondrous  bestowing auspiciousness  dispelling darkness
= I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda, who have risen together like the sun and the moon on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa. Wondrous and bestowing auspiciousness, They dispel the darkness.

@3
all glory  to the most merciful  of the lame
my  of little intelligence  the refuge
whose lotus feet are my all in all
Rādhā  and Madana-mohana
= All glory to the most merciful Rādhā and Madana-mohana! They are the refuge of me, who am lame and of little intelligence, and Their lotus feet are my all in all.

@4
resplendent  in the forest of Vṛndāvana  a wish-fulfilling tree  beneath
in a splendid  jewelled temple  on a throne  seated
Śrī Śrī  Rādhā  Śrīla Govinda-deva
by Their dearest companions  being served  I remember
= In resplendent Vṛndāvana, beneath a wish-fulfilling tree, Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Śrīla Govinda-deva sit on a throne in a splendid jewelled temple, and Their dearest companions serve Them. I remember Them.

@5
the beautiful  who began the rasa of the rāsa dance
at Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing
attracting  with the sounds of His flute  the gopīs
Gopīnātha  for the welfare  may He be  our
= Beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha began the sweet *rasa* of the rāsa dance. He stands on the riverbank at Vaṁśīvaṭa and draws the gopīs with the sound of His flute. May He bestow His blessings upon us.

@6
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to the ocean of mercy
glory  glory  to Śacī’s son  glory  to the friend of the fallen
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to the ocean of mercy! Glory, glory to the son of Śacī! Glory to the friend of the fallen!

@7
glory  glory  to Nityānanda  glory to Advaitacandra
glory  glory  headed by Śrīvāsa  of Gaura  the devotees  to the host
= Glory, glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory, glory to all the devotees of Gaura, headed by Śrīvāsa!

@8
earlier  I told  of the Ādi-līlā  the sūtras
which  has described in full  Vṛndāvana dāsa
= Earlier I told the *sūtras* (brief threads) of the Ādi-līlā, which Vṛndāvana dāsa has described in full.

@9
therefore  of it  I  only the sūtras  made
whatever  something  particular  within the sūtras themselves  I told
= Therefore I gave only the sūtras of it; whatever particulars there were, I told within the sūtras themselves.

@10
now  I tell  of the final  pastimes  the main  sūtras
of the Lord  endless  pastimes  not  can be  described
= Now I shall tell the main sūtras of the final pastimes (Śeṣa-līlā). The Lord’s pastimes are endless; they cannot be fully described.

@11
among them  which  portion  Vṛndāvana dāsa
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  in detail  made  a description
= Of these, the portion that Vṛndāvana dāsa described in detail in his *Caitanya-maṅgala* —

@12
of that  portion  here  the sūtras  only  I shall write
there  whatever  particular  something (left unsaid)  here  I shall expand
= — of that portion I shall write here only the sūtras; whatever particulars remain unsaid there, I shall expand here.

@13
of Caitanya’s pastimes  the Vyāsa  is Vṛndāvana dāsa
by his  order  I do  his  remnants  chewing
= Vṛndāvana dāsa is the Vyāsa of Caitanya’s pastimes. By his order I am only chewing his remnants.

@14
with devotion  on my head  holding  his  feet
of the final pastimes  the sūtras  now  I make  description
= With devotion I hold his feet upon my head, and now I describe the sūtras of the final pastimes.

@15
twenty-four  years  the Lord’s  at home  stay
there  which  He performed  pastimes  Ādi-līlā  the name
= For twenty-four years the Lord lived at home; the pastimes He performed there are called the Ādi-līlā.

@16
of the twenty-fourth  year  at the end  which  the month of Māgha
in its  bright fortnight  the Lord  took  sannyāsa
= At the close of His twenty-fourth year, in the bright fortnight of the month of Māgha, the Lord accepted *sannyāsa*.

@17
sannyāsa  having taken  twenty-four  years  stay
there  which  pastimes  their  Śeṣa-līlā  name
= After taking sannyāsa He remained for twenty-four years; the pastimes of that time are called the Śeṣa-līlā.

@18
of the Śeṣa-līlā  ‘Madhya’  ‘Anta’  two  names  there are
by the difference of pastimes  Vaiṣṇavas  all  difference of names  speak
= The Śeṣa-līlā has two names, ‘Madhya’ (middle) and ‘Anta’ (final): all Vaiṣṇavas name its parts differently according to the difference in the pastimes.

@19
of these  six  years  going and coming
Nīlācala  Gauḍa  Setubandha  Vṛndāvana
= Of these, six years were spent going and coming between Nīlācala, Gauḍa, Setubandha and Vṛndāvana.

@20
there  which  pastimes  their  Madhya-līlā  name
after that  the pastimes  Antya-līlā  the designation
= The pastimes of that period are called the Madhya-līlā; the pastimes after it are named the Antya-līlā.

@21
Ādi-līlā  Madhya-līlā  Antya-līlā  and
now  the Madhya-līlā  somewhat  I make  expansion
= Thus there are the Ādi-līlā, the Madhya-līlā and the Antya-līlā. Now I shall expand somewhat on the Madhya-līlā.

@22
eighteen  years  only  at Nīlācala  residence
Himself  practising  the living beings  He taught  devotion
= For eighteen years He stayed only at Nīlācala. Practising devotion Himself, He taught it to the living beings.

@23
of these  six  years  devotees  with
loving devotion  He established  of dancing  and singing  in the joy
= Of these, for six years, in the company of His devotees, He established loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*) in the joy of dancing and singing.

@24
Nityānanda Gosāñi  He sent  to the land of Gauḍa
He  the land of Gauḍa  flooded  with the nectar of love
= He sent Nityānanda Gosāñi to the land of Gauḍa, and Nityānanda flooded Gauḍa with the nectar of love.

@25
by nature  Nityānanda  in love for Kṛṣṇa  unrestrained
by the Lord’s order  He made  here  and there  the gift of love
= Nityānanda is by nature unrestrained in love for Kṛṣṇa, and by the Lord’s order He gave that love away everywhere.

@26
at His  feet  my  millions of  obeisances
to Caitanya  what is dear  He who  made accept  the world
= Millions of obeisances at His feet! He made the whole world accept what is dear to Caitanya.

@27
Caitanya Gosāñi  whom  calls  elder  brother
He  says  my  Lord  is Caitanya Gosāñi
= He whom Caitanya Gosāñi calls His ‘elder brother’ says, ‘My Lord is Caitanya Gosāñi.’

@28
although  Himself  is  the Lord  Balarāma
still  of Caitanya  He holds  of a servant  the self-conception
= Although He Himself is Lord Balarāma, He still regards Himself as Caitanya’s servant.

@29
Caitanya  serve  Caitanya  sing of  take  Caitanya’s name
to Caitanya  who  devotion  shows  he  My  life
= “Serve Caitanya! Sing of Caitanya! Take Caitanya’s name! Whoever is devoted to Caitanya is My very life.”

@30
thus  people  devotion to Caitanya  He made take up
the wretched  the lowly  the blasphemers  all  He delivered
= In this way He made people take up devotion to Caitanya and delivered everyone — the wretched, the lowly and even blasphemers.

@31
then  the Lord  to Vraja  sent  Rūpa and Sanātana
by the Lord’s order  the two  brothers  came  to Vṛndāvana
= Then the Lord sent Rūpa and Sanātana to Vraja, and by His order the two brothers came to Vṛndāvana.

@32
devotion  preaching  all the holy places  they revealed
of Madana-gopāla  and Govinda  the service  they spread
= Preaching devotion, they revealed all the holy places and spread the service of Madana-gopāla and Govinda.

@33
various  scriptures  gathering  they made  books of devotion  the essence
foolish  fallen people  they  made  deliverance
= Gathering many scriptures, they composed books of devotion that are their essence, and so delivered foolish and fallen people.

@34
the Lord’s  by order  they made  of all  the scriptures  study
of Vraja  the confidential  devotion  they made  preaching
= By the Lord’s order they examined all the scriptures and preached the confidential devotion of Vraja.

@35
Hari-bhakti-vilāsa  and  Bhāgavatāmṛta
Daśama-ṭippanī  and  Daśama-carita
= *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*, *Bhāgavatāmṛta*, *Daśama-ṭippanī* and *Daśama-carita* —

@36
these  all  books  composed  Gosāñi  Sanātana
Rūpa Gosāñi  composed  as many as  who  can make  a count
= all these books Sanātana Gosāñi composed. And who can count all that Rūpa Gosāñi composed?

@37
principal  principal  some  I make  enumeration
in a hundred thousand  verses  he made  of Vraja’s pastimes  description
= I shall name some of the principal ones. In a hundred thousand verses he described the pastimes of Vraja.

@38
Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu  and  Vidagdha-mādhava
Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi  and  Lalita-mādhava
= *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, *Vidagdha-mādhava*, *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi* and *Lalita-mādhava*;

@39
Dāna-keli-kaumudī  and  many  hymns (Stavāvalī)
eighteen  Līlā-cchandas  and  Padyāvalī
= *Dāna-keli-kaumudī*, many hymns (*Stavāvalī*), the eighteen *Līlā-cchandas* and *Padyāvalī*;

@40
Govinda-virudāvalī  its  lakṣaṇa (definitions)
Mathurā-māhātmya  and  Nāṭaka-varṇana
= *Govinda-virudāvalī* with its *lakṣaṇa* (definitions), *Mathurā-māhātmya* and *Nāṭaka-varṇana*.

@41
Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta and others  who  can make  a count
everywhere  he made  of Vraja’s pastimes  description
= Who can count *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta* and the rest? Everywhere in them he described the pastimes of Vraja.

@42
his  brother’s son  named  Śrī Jīva Gosāñi
as many  books of devotion  he composed  of them  end  there is none
= His nephew is named Śrī Jīva Gosāñi. There is no end to the books of devotion he composed.

@43
Śrī Bhāgavata-sandarbha  named  an extensive book
of the conclusions of devotion  in it  he has written  the essence
= In his extensive book named *Śrī Bhāgavata-sandarbha* he has written the essence of the conclusions of devotion.

@44
Gopāla-campū  named  a book  most mighty
of the eternal pastimes  the establishment  in which  with the rasa of Vraja  full
= The mighty book named *Gopāla-campū* establishes the eternal pastimes and is full of the *rasa* of Vraja.

@45
in this way  various  books  having made  manifest
their community  together with  they made  in Vṛndāvana  residence
= In this way they brought out many books and lived in Vṛndāvana together with their community.

@46
in the first  year  headed by Advaita  the devotees
the Lord  to see  made  to Nīlādri  journey
= In the first year the devotees headed by Advaita went to Nīlādri to see the Lord.

@47
the Ratha-yātrā  having seen  there  they stayed  four months
with the Lord  dancing  singing  supreme  joy
= Having seen the Ratha-yātrā, they stayed there four months, dancing and singing with the Lord in supreme joy.

@48
of farewell  at the time  the Lord  said  to all
every year  come  all  the Guṇḍicā  to see
= When they took leave, the Lord told them all, “Come every year, all of you, to see the Guṇḍicā festival.”

@49
by the Lord’s order  the devotees  every year  coming
the Guṇḍicā  having seen  they go back  the Lord  having met
= By the Lord’s order the devotees came every year, saw the Guṇḍicā festival, met the Lord and went back.

@50
twelve  years  thus  they made  coming and going
mutually  of both  each other  without  there is no  existence
= For twelve years they came and went like this; neither the Lord nor His devotees could live without each other.

@51
of this  at the end  which  remain  twelve  years
of Kṛṣṇa  pastimes of separation  the Lord’s  within the heart
= In the twelve years that remained at the end, the Lord’s heart was filled with the pastimes of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@52
constantly  night and day  of separation  in the madness
He laughs  weeps  dances  sings  in great  sorrow
= Night and day, in the divine madness of separation, He laughed, wept, danced and sang in great sorrow.

@53
at whatever  time  He takes  of Jagannātha  darśana
in the mind  He thinks  at Kurukṣetra  I have found  union
= Whenever He took *darśana* of Jagannātha, He thought, “I have found Him again at Kurukṣetra.”

@54
of the Ratha-yātrā  in front  when  He performs  dance
there  this  song  only  He makes  singing
= When He danced in front of the Ratha-yātrā, He sang only this song:

@55
that very  Lord of My life  I have found
whom  for  in the fire of Madana  pining  I was wasting away
= “I have found that very Lord of My life for whom I was wasting away, burning in the fire of Madana (love).”

@56
to this  refrain song  He dances  the second  prahara
Kṛṣṇa  taking  to Vraja  I go  this feeling  within
= To this refrain He danced through the second *prahara* (watch) of the day, feeling within, “Taking Kṛṣṇa, I go back to Vraja.”

@57
in this  mood  of the dance  in the midst  He recites  a  verse
of that  verse  the meaning  anyone  not  understands  of the people
= In this mood, in the midst of the dance, He recited a verse whose meaning none of the people understood:

@58
who  stole my heart in my girlhood  He  the same  indeed  my beloved  those  same  nights of Caitra
those same  and  fragrant with blossoming mālatī  full  breezes from the kadambas
she  the same  am I  yet  there  for the pastimes of love
of the Revā  on the bank  of the vetasī  tree  beneath  My heart  yearns
= “He who stole My heart in My girlhood is the same beloved; these are the same moonlit nights of Caitra, the same full breezes from the kadamba trees, fragrant with blossoming *mālatī*; and I am the same girl. Yet My heart yearns for those pastimes of love there, beneath the *vetasī* tree on the bank of the Revā.” (Padyāvalī 386)

@59
of this  verse  the meaning  knows  alone  Svarūpa
by providence  that  year  there  had come  Rūpa
= Svarūpa alone knew the meaning of this verse. By providence, that year Rūpa had come there.

@60
from the Lord’s mouth  the verse  hearing  Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi
of that  verse  of the meaning  a verse  composed  right there
= Hearing the verse from the Lord’s mouth, Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi then and there composed a verse giving its meaning.
