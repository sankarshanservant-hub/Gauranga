@title The Fault-finding of Rāmacandra Purī

@1
Him  I worship  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya
of Rāmacandra Purī  out of fear
than the ordinary food  His own  who
alms-food  reduced
= I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who, out of fear of Rāmacandra Purī, reduced His own meals to less than the ordinary measure of food.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī  Caitanya  of mercy  the ocean  the descent
Brahmā,  Śiva and the rest  worship  the feet  whose
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya, the descent of the ocean of mercy, whose feet Brahmā, Śiva and the other gods worship!

@3
glory  glory  to Śrīvāsa and the rest  all  the devotees
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  the Lord  whose  of life  the treasure
= Glory, glory to all the devotees headed by Śrīvāsa, for whom the Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, is the treasure of their lives!

@4
in this way  Gauracandra  His own  devotees  with
in Nīlācala  plays  makes  of love for Kṛṣṇa  in the waves
= In this way Gauracandra enjoyed His pastimes in Nīlācala with His own devotees, in the waves of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@5
at this  time  Rāmacandra  Purī  Gosāñi  came
Paramānanda  Purī  and  the Lord  met
= At that time Rāmacandra Purī Gosāñi came and met Paramānanda Purī and the Lord.

@6
Paramānanda  Purī  did  at the feet  homage
Purī  Gosāñi  gave  him  a firm  embrace
= Paramānanda Purī paid homage at his feet, and Purī Gosāñi embraced him firmly.

@7
Mahāprabhu  made  to him  prostrate  obeisance
embrace  giving  he  made  of Kṛṣṇa  remembrance
= Mahāprabhu offered him prostrate obeisance; he embraced the Lord and remembered Kṛṣṇa.

@8
the three  persons  about the Lord  conversation  held  for some  time
Jagadānanda  Paṇḍita  him  invited
= For some time the three of them spoke together about the Lord. Then Jagadānanda Paṇḍita invited him for a meal.

@9
Jagannātha’s  prasāda  he brought  of the meal  for the sake
as much as he liked  meal  ate  he  of fault-finding  for the sake
= Jagadānanda brought Jagannātha’s prasāda for the meal, and Rāmacandra Purī ate as much as he liked, only to find fault afterwards.

@10
meal  having taken  says  Purī  listen  Jagadānanda
the remnants  of prasāda  you  do  eat
= When he had eaten, the Purī said, “Listen, Jagadānanda: you eat the prasāda that remains.”

@11
insistence  showing  him  having seated  fed
himself  insistence  showing  serving  did
= Insisting, he seated Jagadānanda and fed him, and he himself insistently served the food.

@12
insistence  showing  again  and again  fed
rinsing of the mouth  when he did  fault-finding  to do  began
= Insisting again and again, he fed him; and when Jagadānanda had rinsed his mouth, he began to find fault:

@13
I heard  Caitanya’s  followers  make  much  eating
‘true’  that  saying  with my own eyes  I have seen  now
= “I had heard that Caitanya’s followers eat a great deal. That report is true: now I have seen it with my own eyes.

@14
a sannyāsī  so much  feeding  they make  of dharma  ruin
renunciants  being  so much  they eat  of renunciation  there is not  ‘a semblance’
= Feeding a sannyāsī so much, they ruin his dharma; and though they are renunciants, they eat so much that they have not even a semblance of renunciation!”

@15
this  indeed  the nature  his  insistence  showing
afterwards  fault-finding  makes  first  much  having fed
= This was his nature: first he would insist on feeding someone a great deal, and afterwards he would find fault with him.

@16
formerly  when  Mādhavendra  Purī  made  departure from the world
Rāmacandra  Purī  then  came  his  to the place
= Formerly, when Mādhavendra Purī was departing from this world, Rāmacandra Purī came to him.

@17
Purī  Gosāñi  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s  name  chanting
Mathurā  not  I have attained  saying  makes  weeping
= Purī Gosāñi was chanting the name of Kṛṣṇa, and weeping, “I have not attained Mathurā!”

@18
Rāmacandra  Purī  then  instructs  him
disciple  being  to the guru  he speaks  fear  not  does
= Then Rāmacandra Purī instructed him. Though a disciple, he spoke to his guru without fear:

@19
you  full  the bliss of Brahman  do  remember
knower of Brahman  being  why  do  weeping
= “You are full; remember the bliss of Brahman. You are a knower of Brahman — why do you weep?”

@20
hearing  in Mādhavendra’s mind  anger  arose
away  away  most sinful one  saying  rebuke  made
= Hearing this, Mādhavendra Purī grew angry. “Away, away, you sinner!” he cried, and rebuked him.

@21
‘Kṛṣṇa’s mercy’  not  I have attained  not  I have attained  ‘Mathurā’
in my own  grief  I am dying  this one  to give  came  burning pain
= “I have not attained Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, I have not attained Mathurā. I am leaving this world in my own grief, and this one has come to add burning pain!

@22
to me  face  not  you shall show  you  go  wherever
you  seeing  if I die  my  will be  an evil  end
= Never show me your face again; go wherever you like! If I leave this world while looking at you, I shall come to an evil end.

@23
Kṛṣṇa  not  I have attained  I am dying  in my own  grief
to me  ‘Brahman’  teaches  this  wretched  fool
= I am leaving this world in my own grief, not having attained Kṛṣṇa, and this wretched fool teaches me about Brahman!”

@24
this  that  Śrī  Mādhavendrapāda  disregard  made
by that  offense  his  ‘worldly desires’  were born
= Śrī Mādhavendrapāda thus disregarded him, and through that offense worldly desires (*vāsanā*) were born in Rāmacandra Purī.

@25
dry  Brahman  knower  there is not  with Kṛṣṇa  ‘relationship’
of all  people  fault-finding  makes  in fault-finding  stubbornly fixed
= He became a dry knower of Brahman, without any relationship with Kṛṣṇa. He found fault with everyone and clung stubbornly to fault-finding.

@26
Īśvara  Purī  does  to Śrīpāda  service
with his own hands  does  stool, urine and the rest  cleaning
= Īśvara Purī, however, served Śrīpāda Mādhavendra Purī, cleaning away his stool and urine with his own hands.

@27
constantly  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  makes him  remember
Kṛṣṇa’s  name,  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes  makes him hear  every moment
= He constantly helped him remember the name of Kṛṣṇa, and at every moment let him hear Kṛṣṇa’s name and Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.

@28
pleased  being  Purī  him  gave  an embrace
blessing  gave  for Kṛṣṇa  your  may there be  of love  the treasure
= Pleased with him, Purī Gosāñi embraced him and gave him a blessing: “May you have the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa!”

@29
that  from  Īśvara  Purī  ‘of love  an ocean’
Rāmacandra  Purī  became  of everyone  a fault-finder
= From then on Īśvara Purī was an ocean of love, while Rāmacandra Purī became a fault-finder of everyone.

@30
of a great soul’s  mercy  punishment  ‘witnesses’  the two  persons
these  two  through  taught  the world’s  people
= The two of them are witnesses of a great soul’s mercy and punishment; through these two he taught the people of the world.

@31
of the world  guru  Mādhavendra  making  of love  a gift
this  verse  reciting  he  made  departure from the world
= Having given the gift of love, Mādhavendra, the guru of the world, recited this verse and departed from this world:

@32
O  melting with compassion for the wretched  Lord  O
of Mathurā  Lord  when shall You be seen
heart  at not seeing You  stricken
O Beloved  wanders  what  do  I
= “O Lord, melting with compassion for the wretched! O Lord of Mathurā, when shall I see You? My heart, stricken at not seeing You, has lost all rest. O Beloved, what am I to do?”

@33
in this  verse  love for Kṛṣṇa  makes  teaching
of Kṛṣṇa  in separation  of the devotee  bhāva  special
= This verse teaches love for Kṛṣṇa: the special feeling (*bhāva-viśeṣa*) of a devotee in separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@34
on the earth  planting  making  went  of love  the sprout
that  of love  of the sprout  the tree  Caitanya  Ṭhākura
= He planted the sprout of love on the earth and departed. Śrī Caitanya Ṭhākura is the tree that grew from that sprout of love.

@35
in this context  I have told  Purī  Gosāñi’s  departure
whoever  this  hears  he  very  fortunate
= In this context I have told of Purī Gosāñi’s departure from this world. Whoever hears of it is greatly fortunate.

@36
Rāmacandra  Purī  thus  stayed  in Nīlācala
renounced  by nature  sometimes  stays  in some  place
= So Rāmacandra Purī stayed on in Nīlācala. Being a renunciant by nature, he stayed now in one place, now in another.

@37
uninvited  meal  takes  there is no  fixed rule
of others’  meals  the arrangement  takes  account
= He took his meals uninvited, with no fixed rule, and he kept account of how others took their meals.

@38
the Lord’s  for the invitation  is needed  cowries  four  paṇas
sometimes  Kāśīśvara  Govinda  eat  three  persons
= To invite the Lord for a meal cost four paṇas of cowries, for sometimes Kāśīśvara and Govinda ate as well — three persons.

@39
daily  the Lord’s  meal  here and there  is
someone  if  the price  brings  four  paṇas  fixed
= Every day the Lord took His meal at one place or another. If someone brought money for it, the amount was fixed at four paṇas.

@40
the Lord’s  stay  ways  meals  sleep  going out
Rāmacandra  Purī  makes  all  inquiry
= Rāmacandra Purī inquired into everything about the Lord: where He stayed, His ways, His meals, His sleep and His going out.

@41
the Lord’s  all  qualities  to touch  could not
faults  seeking  he goes about  anywhere  fault  not  found
= He could not touch any of the Lord’s virtues. He went about seeking faults, but found no fault anywhere.

@42
a sannyāsī  being  makes  of sweets  eating
by this  enjoyment  is  how  of the senses  control
= “He is a sannyāsī, yet He eats sweets! With such enjoyment, how can there be control of the senses?”

@43
this  fault-finding  making  he speaks  of all  people  in the presence
the Lord  to see  still  he comes  every  day
= Finding this fault, he spoke of it before everyone; and yet he came every day to see the Lord.

@44
the Lord  as a guru  regarding him  makes  reverence  honor
he  faults  seeking  goes about  this  his  occupation
= Regarding him as a guru, the Lord showed him reverence and honor, but he went about looking for faults — that was his occupation.

@45
whatever  fault-finding  he makes  that  the Lord  all  knows
still  respect  makes  very great  with reverence
= The Lord knew all the fault-finding he did, yet He still treated him with great respect and reverence.

@46
one day  in the morning  came  of the Lord  to the house
ants  seeing  something  says  in reply
= One morning he came to the Lord’s house. Seeing ants there, he made a remark:

@47
at night  here  sugarcane molasses  was
therefore  ants  are moving about  alas
of renounced  sannyāsīs  this  for the senses
craving  so  saying  getting up  he went
= “Last night there was sugarcane molasses here; that is why the ants are crawling about. Alas! Such is the craving of the senses in renounced sannyāsīs!” Saying this, he got up and left.

@48
the Lord  through others  fault-finding  had  heard
now  directly  He heard  ‘invented’  blame
= The Lord had heard of his fault-finding through others; now He heard the invented blame with His own ears.

@49
naturally  ants  everywhere  wander
in that  argument  raising  a fault  he attaches
= Ants naturally wander about everywhere, yet he raised an argument from it and attached a fault to the Lord.

@50
hearing  that  of the Lord  embarrassment  fear  in the mind
Govinda  calling  some  says  words
= Hearing this, the Lord felt embarrassment and fear in His mind. He called Govinda and spoke to him:

@51
today  from  meal  My  this  indeed  rule
of the piṇḍā-bhoga  one  quarter  of five  gaṇḍās’ worth  vegetables
= “From today this is the rule for My meals: a quarter-pot of the rice offered on Jagannātha’s altar (*piṇḍā-bhoga*), and vegetables worth five gaṇḍās.

@52
this  except  more  other  anything  not  you shall take
more  if you bring  Me  here  not  you shall see
= Take nothing more than this. If you bring more, you will not see Me here again.”

@53
to all  the Vaiṣṇavas  Govinda  tells  this  news
hearing  of everyone  on the head  as if  there was  a thunderbolt’s stroke
= Govinda told all the Vaiṣṇavas this news. Hearing it, they felt as if a thunderbolt had struck their heads.

@54
Rāmacandra  Purī  everyone  gives  rebuke
this  great sinner  coming  life  has taken  of all
= Everyone reproached Rāmacandra Purī: “This sinner has come here and taken the life of us all!”

@55-56
that  day  a  brāhmaṇa  made  invitation
one  quarter-pot  rice  of five  gaṇḍās’ worth  vegetables
this  only  Govinda  made  acceptance
on his head  blows  strikes  the brāhmaṇa  makes  lamentation
= That day a brāhmaṇa invited the Lord. Govinda accepted only a quarter-pot of rice and vegetables worth five gaṇḍās, and the brāhmaṇa struck his head and cried out in grief.

@57
that  rice  vegetables  the Lord  half  ate
whatever  remained  that  Govinda  received
= The Lord ate half of that rice and vegetables, and whatever remained Govinda received.

@58
half a meal  takes  the Lord  Govinda  half a meal
all  the devotees  then  gave up  eating
= The Lord ate half a meal and Govinda half a meal, and then all the devotees gave up eating.

@59
to Govinda  Kāśīśvara  the Lord’s  was made  order
you two  elsewhere  begging  do  of the belly  filling
= The Lord ordered Govinda and Kāśīśvara, “You two beg elsewhere and fill your stomachs.”

@60
in this way  in great  grief  days  some  passed
hearing  Rāmacandra  Purī  the Lord  near  came
= Some days passed in this great grief. Hearing of it, Rāmacandra Purī came to the Lord.

@61
obeisance  making  the Lord  did  at the feet  homage
to the Lord  says  some  smiling  words
= The Lord bowed down and paid homage at his feet, and the Purī, smiling, spoke to the Lord:

@62
a sannyāsī’s  dharma  is not  ‘of the senses  gratification’
somehow or other  makes  only  of the belly  filling
= “A sannyāsī’s dharma is not to gratify the senses. He just fills his stomach somehow or other.

@63
You  thin  I see  I hear  You make  half-eating
this  ‘dry  renunciation’  is not  a sannyāsī’s  ‘dharma’
= I see that You have grown thin, and I hear You eat only half Your meals. This dry renunciation is not a sannyāsī’s dharma.

@64
as is fitting  the belly  fills  not  makes  ‘sense objects’  enjoyment
of a sannyāsī  then  perfected  becomes  of knowledge  yoga
= He fills his stomach as much as is fitting and does not enjoy sense objects. Then the sannyāsī’s *jñāna-yoga* is perfected.

@65
not  of one who eats too much  even  yoga  is
not  and  entirely  of one who does not eat
not  and  too much  sleep  of one given to
of one who keeps awake  not  indeed  and  O Arjuna
= ‘O Arjuna, there is no yoga for one who eats too much, nor for one who does not eat at all, nor for one given to too much sleep, nor for one who stays awake too long.’ (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.16)

@66
of one regulated in eating and recreation
regulated  in effort  in actions
regulated in sleep and waking
yoga  becomes  of sorrows  the destroyer
= ‘For one who is regulated in eating and recreation, regulated in his efforts at work, and regulated in sleep and waking, yoga becomes the destroyer of all sorrow.’” (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.17)

@67
the Lord  says  ignorant  boy  I  ‘disciple’  your
to Me  instruction  you give  this  fortune  My
= The Lord said, “I am an ignorant boy, your disciple. That you instruct Me is My good fortune.”

@68
this  hearing  Rāmacandra  Purī  getting up  went
the devotees  half-eating  make  the Gosāñi  heard
= Hearing this, Rāmacandra Purī got up and left. The Gosāñi heard that the devotees were eating only half their meals.

@69
another  day  the devotees  with  Paramānanda  Purī
to the Lord  near  submitted  humility  courtesy  making
= On another day Paramānanda Purī, with the devotees, humbly and courteously submitted to the Lord:

@70
Rāmacandra  Purī  is  of a fault-finder  the nature
his  at the word  food  giving up  what  will be  gain
= “Rāmacandra Purī has the nature of a fault-finder. What will be gained by giving up food at his word?

@71
of the Purī  the nature  as much as one likes  food  having made eat
who  not  eats  him  he feeds  effort  making
= It is the Purī’s nature to make people eat as much as they like; whoever does not eat, he feeds with great effort.

@72
having fed  then  him  makes  fault-finding
so much  food  you eat  your  how much  is there  wealth
= Having fed him, he then finds fault with him: ‘You eat so much food — how much wealth do you have?

@73
a sannyāsī  so much  feeding  you make  of dharma  ruin
therefore  I know  your  any  there is not  ‘semblance’
= By feeding a sannyāsī so much, you ruin his dharma! So I know that you have not the slightest semblance of it.’

@74
who  how  in behavior  who  how  eats
this  inquiry  he  makes  always
= How each person behaves and how each one eats — he is always inquiring into this.

@75
in the scriptures  which  two  duties  have made  rejection
that  activity  constantly  his  doing
= The two things that the scriptures forbid are what he does constantly.

@76
of others  the nature  and actions
not  one should praise  not  one should blame
the universe  of one nature  seeing
with prakṛti  with the puruṣa  and
= ‘One should neither praise nor blame the nature and actions of others, seeing the universe as one in nature, together with prakṛti and the puruṣa.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.28.1)

@77
of them  among  the earlier injunction  ‘praise’  giving up
the later injunction  ‘blame’  he does  ‘stronger’  knowing it
= Of the two, he gives up the earlier injunction — praise — and practices the later one — blame — knowing that the later one is ‘stronger’.

@78
of the earlier  and later  between  the later injunction  stronger
= ‘Between an earlier and a later injunction, the later injunction is stronger.’ (a maxim of Nyāya)

@79
where  virtues  a hundred  are  those  not  makes  acceptance
virtues  among  by deceit  makes  of faults  imputation
= Where there are a hundred virtues, he does not accept them; among the virtues he deceitfully imputes faults.

@80
his  nature  this  to tell  not  is fitting
still  I tell  something  heart-felt  pain  receiving
= It is not fitting even to speak of his nature. Still I tell You something of it, for it pains us to the heart.

@81
his  at the word  why  food  renunciation  You make
as before  invitations  accept  of all  the request  heed
= Why do You give up food at his word? Accept invitations as before and heed the request of us all.”

@82
the Lord  says  all of you  why  with the Purī  make  anger
‘natural’  dharma  speaks  he  his  what  fault
= The Lord said, “Why are you all angry with the Purī? He speaks the natural dharma of a sannyāsī. What fault is there in him?

@83
a renunciant  being  of the tongue  greed  very  improper
a renunciant’s  dharma  life  to maintain  food  only  he eats
= For a renunciant to be greedy of the tongue is very improper. A renunciant’s dharma is to eat only as much food as keeps him alive.”

@84
then  all  together  the Lord  much  entreaty  made
of all  at the insistence  the Lord  half  kept
= Then all of them together entreated the Lord earnestly, and at everyone’s insistence the Lord agreed to take half of His former measure.

@85
two paṇas  of cowries  is needed  the Lord’s  for the invitation
sometimes  two  persons  eaters  sometimes  three  persons
= To invite the Lord now cost two paṇas of cowries. Sometimes two persons ate, sometimes three.

@86
whose cooked food may not be eaten  a brāhmaṇa  if  makes  invitation
prasāda  the price  to take  is needed  cowries  two paṇas
= If a brāhmaṇa whose cooked food could not be accepted invited the Lord, two paṇas of cowries were needed to buy prasāda.

@87
whose cooked food may be eaten  a brāhmaṇa  if  invitation  makes
some  ‘prasāda’  brings  some  cooking  makes  at home
= If a brāhmaṇa whose cooked food could be accepted invited Him, he would bring some prasāda and cook some at home.

@88
Paṇḍita  Gosāñi  Bhagavān  Ācārya  Sārvabhauma
of invitation  on the day  if  make  invitation
= When Paṇḍita Gosāñi, Bhagavān Ācārya or Sārvabhauma invited Him on the day of their invitation,

@89
of them all  by the wish  the Lord  takes  meal
there  the Lord’s  independence  is not  as  their  mind
= the Lord ate according to their wishes. There the Lord had no independence: it was as they wished.

@90
to the devotees  happiness  to give  the Lord’s  ‘descent’
where  how  fitting  there  He makes  conduct
= The Lord descended to give happiness to His devotees, and He acted as was fitting in each place and situation.

@91
sometimes  worldly  ways  as if  ‘ordinary’  person
sometimes  independent  makes  ‘divine majesty’  manifestation
= Sometimes He followed worldly ways, like an ordinary person; sometimes, being independent, He displayed His divine majesty (*aiśvarya*).

@92
sometimes  of Rāmacandra  Purī  is  a servant  almost
sometimes  him  not  regards  sees  a straw  like
= Sometimes He was almost like a servant of Rāmacandra Purī; sometimes He paid him no regard and looked on him as a straw.

@93
divine  conduct  of the Lord  of the intellect  beyond the reach
when  whatever  He does  that  all  enchanting
= The Lord’s divine conduct is beyond the reach of the intellect. Whatever He does at any time is all enchanting.

@94
in this way  Rāmacandra  Purī  in Nīlācala
days  some  staying  went  ‘pilgrimage’  to make
= In this way Rāmacandra Purī stayed some days in Nīlācala and then left on pilgrimage.

@95
he  when gone  the Lord’s  associates  became  delighted
from the head  a stone  as if  fell  suddenly
= When he had gone, the Lord’s associates were delighted, as if a stone had suddenly fallen from their heads.

@96
freely  invitations  the Lord’s  kīrtana  dancing
freely  make  all  prasāda  eating
= Freely the Lord again accepted invitations and freely He sang kīrtana and danced; freely everyone again ate prasāda.

@97
the guru  disregard  if he makes  such  result  is
gradually  the Lord  even up to  in offenses  one becomes caught
= When the guru disregards someone, such is the result: gradually he comes to offend even the Lord.

@98
although  as a guru  regarding him  the Lord  his  fault  not  took
of it  the fruit  by means of  the people  teaching  gave
= Although the Lord, regarding him as a guru, did not take offense at his faults, through their fruit He taught the people.

@99
Caitanya’s  conduct  like  of nectar  a flood
in hearing  to the ears  to the mind  feels  sweet
= Śrī Caitanya’s conduct is like a flood of nectar; when heard, it is sweet to the ears and to the mind.

@100
Caitanya’s  conduct  I write  hear  with one mind
easily  you will attain  love  of Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  at the feet
= I write of Śrī Caitanya’s conduct: hear it with undivided attention, and you will easily attain love for the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

@101
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
