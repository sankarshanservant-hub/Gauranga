# English names and terms — CC Madhya 25, verses 136–276 (stage A, part 2; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Мадхья 25»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (Madhya 25, commentary parts 1–2) are used as given there: *rasika* / *bhāvuka*; *ā-layam* (up to and including the bliss of liberation); Uttamaśloka; *nāmābhāsa* (the semblance of the Name); *munsīph*; *karoyā*; *chadma* (pretext); *vyavahāra-sneha*; *paḍichā* (Tulasī the *paḍichā*); Āṭhāranālā; Narendra (lake); Jhārikhaṇḍa; *amṛta-pūra* (a filling of nectar), *sukarpūra* (fine camphor); Śrīman Madana-Gopāla, Govinda-deva; *samudaya-loka* (the mass of people; Anubhāṣya: all the wicked, unqualified people); *sahṛdaya-sumanas*.
