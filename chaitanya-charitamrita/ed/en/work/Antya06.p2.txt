# CC Antya 6, verses 111–222 (stage A, part 2): our English translation from the Bengali and English word-by-word.
# Verse 137 (= Madhya 23.24) — word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev.

@111
of Rāghava Ṭhākura  the prasāda  of nectar  the essence
Mahāprabhu  which  to eat  comes  again and again
= The *prasāda* of Rāghava Ṭhākura is the very essence of nectar; Mahāprabhu comes again and again to eat it.

@112
cooking  having done  Rāghava  when  the offering  places
of Mahāprabhu  for the sake  an offering  separate  he serves out
= When Rāghava has cooked and places the offering before the Deity, he serves out a separate offering for Mahāprabhu.

@113
every day  Mahāprabhu  partakes  of the meal
now and then  the Lord  to him  gives  His sight
= Mahāprabhu eats there every day, and now and then the Lord lets Rāghava see Him.

@114
to the two brothers  Rāghava  bringing  serves
with care  feeds Them  not  remains  any left over
= Rāghava brought the meal and served the two brothers; he fed Them with care, and nothing was left over.

@115
how many  offerings  he brings  such  not  I know
in Rāghava's  house  cooks  Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī
= I cannot say how many dishes he brought. In Rāghava's house Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī Herself cooks.

@116
from Durvāsā  She  has received  a boon
than nectar  the cooking  Hers  more  sweet
= She received a boon from Durvāsā: Her cooking is sweeter than nectar.

@117
fragrant  beautiful  prasāda  of sweetness  the essence
the two  brothers  it  eating  satisfaction  boundless
= The fragrant, beautiful *prasāda* was the very essence of sweetness, and the two brothers ate it with boundless satisfaction.

@118
to the meal  to sit  to Raghunātha  say  all the people
the Paṇḍita  says  he  later  will take  the meal
= Everyone asked Raghunātha to sit down to the meal, but the Paṇḍita said, “He will eat later.”

@119
the devotees  to the throat  filling  took  the meal
‘Hari’  the sound  making  rising  did  ācamana
= The devotees ate their fill, up to the throat; then, calling out “Hari!”, they rose and rinsed their mouths and hands.

@120
the meal  having taken  the two  brothers  did  ācamana
Rāghava  bringing  put on Them  garlands and sandal
= When the two brothers had eaten, They rinsed Their mouths and hands, and Rāghava brought garlands and sandal paste and adorned Them.

@121
biḍā  he fed Them  did  to the feet  homage
to the devotees  he gave  biḍā  garlands and sandal
= He gave Them *biḍā* (prepared betel) to chew and bowed at Their feet; then he gave the devotees *biḍā*, garlands and sandal paste.

@122
Rāghava's  mercy  Raghunātha  upon
of the two brothers  remnant  the plates  he gave  to him
= Rāghava's mercy rested on Raghunātha: he gave him the plates with the remnants of the two brothers.

@123
he said  Caitanya Gosāñi  has taken  the meal
His  remnants  if you get  your  will be cut  bondage
= He said, “Caitanya Gosāñi has eaten from these. If you receive His remnants, your bondage will be cut.”

@124
in the devotee's heart  in the devotee's home  always  is His abode
sometimes  hidden  sometimes  manifest  independent  Bhagavān
= The independent Bhagavān always abides in the heart and in the home of His devotee — sometimes hidden, sometimes manifest.

@125
everywhere  ‘all-pervading’  of the Lord  always  everywhere  the dwelling
in this  doubt  whose  he  goes  to ruin
= The Lord is all-pervading; He dwells everywhere, always. Whoever doubts this goes to ruin.

@126
in the morning  Nityānanda  Prabhu  bath in the Gaṅgā  having taken
that  at the foot of the tree  sat  His own associates  taking
= In the morning Nityānanda Prabhu bathed in the Gaṅgā and sat with His associates at the foot of that same tree.

@127
Raghunātha  coming  did  to the feet  homage
through Rāghava Paṇḍita  made  his submission
= Raghunātha came and bowed at His feet, and through Rāghava Paṇḍita he humbly made his request:

@128
low  wretched  I  base  the lowest of souls
my  desire  is  that I may attain  Caitanya's feet
= “I am low and wretched, base, the lowest of souls. Yet my desire is to attain the feet of Caitanya.

@129
a dwarf  being  as if  the moon  to catch  wants
many  efforts  I made  in that  ever  success  not
= It is like a dwarf wanting to catch the moon. I have tried many times, but never succeeded.

@130
as many times  I flee  I  home and all  leaving
father  mother  the two  me  keep  bound
= Every time I run away, leaving home and all, my father and mother both keep me bound.

@131
Your  mercy  without  anyone  ‘Caitanya’  not  attains
You  mercy  if You show  Him  even the lowest  attains
= Without Your mercy no one attains Caitanya; if You show mercy, even the lowest attains Him.

@132
unworthy  I  a request  to make  I feel  fear
to me  ‘Caitanya’  give  O Gosāñi  being  kind
= Unworthy as I am, I am afraid even to ask. O Gosāñi, be kind and grant me Caitanya.

@133
my  on the head  Your foot  placing  bestow  grace
without obstacles  Caitanya  that I may attain  give  blessing
= Place Your foot on my head and grant me this grace; bless me, that I may attain Caitanya without obstacles.”

@134
hearing  smiling  says  the Lord  to all  the devotees
his  worldly happiness  to Indra's happiness  equal
= Hearing this, the Lord smiled and said to all the devotees, “His worldly happiness equals the happiness of Indra.

@135
by Caitanya's mercy  even that  not  pleases  his mind
all of you  blessing  give  may he attain  Caitanya's feet
= Yet by Caitanya's mercy even that holds no charm for his mind. All of you, bless him, that he may attain the feet of Caitanya.

@136
of Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet  the fragrance  whoever  person  obtains
Brahmaloka  and the rest  the happiness  to him  not  appeals
= One who has caught the fragrance of Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet finds no charm in the happiness of Brahmaloka and the other worlds.”

@137
who  hard to give up  wife and sons
friends and kingdom  dear to the heart
gave up  while young  even  like filth
for Uttamaśloka  longing
= “Longing for Uttamaśloka, Bharata, while still young, cast off like so much filth his wife and sons, his friends and kingdom — all so dear to his heart and so hard to leave.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.14.43)

@138
then  Raghunātha  the Lord  near  called
on his  head  His foot  placing  to speak  began
= Then the Lord called Raghunātha near, placed His foot on his head and began to speak:

@139
you  that  arranged  this  picnic on the sandy bank
to you  mercy  showing  Gaura  made  His coming
= “To this picnic on the riverbank that you arranged, Gaura came to show you mercy.

@140
mercy  showing  He did  ciḍā and milk  eating
the dancing  seeing  at night  He did  prasāda  partaking
= In His mercy He ate *ciḍā* with milk, and at night, after watching the dancing, He partook of the *prasāda*.
