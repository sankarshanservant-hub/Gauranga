@title The Branches of Śrī Advaita’s Trunk

@1
of Advaita  of the feet  lotus  the bees  those
the essence and the chaff  bearing  all
rejecting  the worthless  the bearers of the essence
I praise  whose life is Caitanya
= Among all the bees at Advaita’s lotus feet, some hold to the essence and some to the chaff. Rejecting those who hold the chaff, I praise those who hold the essence, whose very life is Caitanya.

@2
glory  glory  to Mahāprabhu  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
glory  glory  to Nityānanda  glory to Advaita  the blessed
= Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Glory, glory to Nityānanda! Glory to the blessed Advaita!

@3
of the immortal tree Śrī Caitanya
the second trunk  whose form is
of Śrīmad Advaitacandra
as branches  the followers  we praise
= We bow to the followers of Śrīmad Advaitacandra, the second trunk of the immortal tree that is Śrī Caitanya; they are its branches.

@4
of the tree  the second  trunk  Ācārya Gosāñi
His  as many  branches  grew  their  account  is not
= The second trunk of the tree is Ācārya Gosāñi. The branches that grew from Him are beyond counting.

@5
of Caitanya the gardener  of mercy’s water  by the sprinkling
by that  water  nourished  the trunk  grows  day by day
= Sprinkled with the water of mercy by Caitanya the gardener, the trunk is nourished by that water and grows day by day.

@6
on that  trunk  as many  fruits of love  grew
with those  fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa  the world  filled
= The fruits of love that grew on that trunk—fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa—filled the world.

@7
that  water  the trunk  makes  into the branches  flow
with fruits and flowers  grows  the branches  became  spread
= The trunk passes that water on to the branches; laden with fruits and flowers, they grow and spread far and wide.

@8
at first  indeed  of one mind  the Ācārya’s  followers
later  of two minds  became  of fate  by reason
= At first the Ācārya’s followers were of one mind; later, by the turn of fate, they split into two views.

@9
some  indeed  by the Ācārya’s  order  some  indeed  independent
their own view  invent  to fate  subject
= Some kept to the Ācārya’s order; others became independent and, under the sway of fate (*daiva*), invented views of their own.

@10
of the Ācārya  the view  which  that  view  is the essence
His  order  transgressing  acts  he  indeed  is worthless
= The Ācārya’s view is the essence. Whoever transgresses His order is worthless.

@11
of the worthless  of the names  here  no  need
the difference  to know  I make  together  a count
= There is no need here to name the worthless. I count them all together only so that the difference may be known.

@12
of paddy  a heap  one measures  as  the husks  with
afterwards  the husks  winnowing  cleaning  to do
= It is like measuring a heap of paddy together with its husks and afterwards winnowing the husks away to clean it.

@13
Acyutānanda  a great  branch  the Ācārya’s  son
from birth  served  he  Caitanya’s  feet
= Acyutānanda, the Ācārya’s son, is a great branch. From birth he served the feet of Caitanya.

@14
of Caitanya Gosāñi  the guru  Keśava  Bhāratī
this  of his father  statement  hearing  pain  felt  great
= Hearing his father say that Keśava Bhāratī was the guru of Caitanya Gosāñi, he was deeply pained.

@15
about the guru of the world  You  make  such  a teaching
Your  by this  teaching  ruined  became  the land
= “You give such teaching about the guru of the world! By this teaching of Yours the land is ruined.

@16
of the fourteen worlds  the guru  Caitanya Gosāñi
His  guru  another  this  in any  scripture  is not
= Caitanya Gosāñi is the guru of the fourteen worlds. No scripture says that anyone else is His guru.”

@17
in his fifth  year  a boy  speaks  of the conclusions  the essence
hearing  obtained  the Ācārya  satisfaction  boundless
= Hearing a boy in his fifth year speak the essence of the scriptural conclusions, the Ācārya was boundlessly pleased.

@18
Kṛṣṇa Miśra  named  another  the Ācārya’s  son
Caitanya Gosāñi  dwells  in whose  heart
= Another son of the Ācārya is named Kṛṣṇa Miśra; Caitanya Gosāñi dwells in his heart.

@19
Śrī Gopāla  named  another  the Ācārya’s  son
his  conduct  hear  most  wonderful
= Another son of the Ācārya is named Śrī Gopāla. Hear of his conduct—it is most wonderful.

@20
in the Guṇḍicā  temple  of Mahāprabhu  before
in the kīrtana  dancing  does  great  in love’s joy
= In the Guṇḍicā temple, before Mahāprabhu, he danced in the kīrtana in the great joy of love.

@21
many  ecstasies arising  in the body  wonderful  dancing
the two  Gosāñis  “Hari”  say  joyful  at heart
= Many ecstasies arose in his body, and his dancing was wonderful; the two Gosāñis, joyful at heart, called out “Hari!”

@22
dancing  dancing  Gopāla  became  unconscious
on the ground  fell  in the body  was not  awareness
= As he danced on and on, Gopāla fainted and fell to the ground; no awareness was left in his body.

@23
grieved  became  the Ācārya  his son  on the lap  taking
protection  makes  of Nṛsiṁha  the mantra  reciting
= Grieved, the Ācārya took His son on His lap and sought to protect him by reciting the mantra of Nṛsiṁha.

@24
many  mantras  recites  the Ācārya  not  comes  consciousness
at the Ācārya’s  grief  the Vaiṣṇavas  make  weeping
= The Ācārya recited many mantras, but Gopāla did not come to. Seeing the Ācārya’s grief, the Vaiṣṇavas wept.

@25
then  Mahāprabhu  his  on the chest  hand  placing
“get up  Gopāla”  say  say  “Hari”  “Hari”
= Then Mahāprabhu placed His hand on Gopāla’s chest and said, “Get up, Gopāla! Say ‘Hari’! Say ‘Hari’!”

@26
rose  Gopāla  the Lord’s  touch and voice  hearing
joyful  becoming  all  make  the sound of Hari
= At the Lord’s touch and voice Gopāla rose, and everyone, overjoyed, called out the name of Hari.

@27
of the Ācārya  another  son  Śrī Balarāma
another  son  “Svarūpa”  the branch  “Jagadīśa”  named
= Another son of the Ācārya is Śrī Balarāma; another son is the branch Svarūpa; another is named Jagadīśa.

@28
“Kamalākānta Viśvāsa”  named  the Ācārya’s servant
the Ācārya’s  dealings  all  to him  known
= The Ācārya had a servant named Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, who knew all the Ācārya’s affairs.

@29
in Nīlācala  he  a  letter  writing
of Pratāparudra  to the place  gave  sending
= In Nīlācala he wrote a letter and sent it to Pratāparudra.

@30
of that letter  the matter  the Ācārya  not  knows
by some  turn  that  letter  came  to the Lord
= The Ācārya knew nothing of that letter, but by some turn of events it reached the Lord.

@31
in that letter  written  is  this  indeed  the writing
in the position of the Lord  the Ācārya  he had  established
= What was written in the letter was this: it declared the Ācārya to be the Lord Himself,

@32
but  His  by fate  some  has become  debt
the debt  to clear  I ask  rupees  three hundred
= “but by fate He has fallen into some debt; to clear it, I ask for three hundred rupees.”

@33
the letter  reading  in the Lord’s  mind  arose  sorrow
outwardly  smiling  something  says  the moon-faced
= Reading the letter, the Lord was pained at heart, but outwardly the moon-faced Lord smiled and said:

@34
the Ācārya  he has established  making  the Lord
in this  fault  is none  the Ācārya  divine  the Lord
= “He has declared the Ācārya to be the Lord. There is no fault in that: the Ācārya is indeed the divine Lord.

@35
of the Lord  poverty  making  he has made  begging
therefore  punishment  making  I will make  a lesson
= But he has made the Lord out to be poor and has begged on His behalf. So I will punish him and teach him a lesson.”

@36
to Govinda  the order  gave  “here  today  from
the madcap  Viśvāsa  here  not  let  to come”
= He ordered Govinda, “From today, do not let this madcap (*bāuliyā*) Viśvāsa come here.”

@37
the punishment  hearing  Viśvāsa  became  very  sorrowful
hearing  of the Lord  the punishment  the Ācārya  delighted
= Hearing of the punishment, Viśvāsa was deeply grieved; but the Ācārya, hearing of the Lord’s punishment, was delighted.

@38
to Viśvāsa  He says  you  very  fortunate
you  has made  punishment  the Lord  Bhagavān
= He said to Viśvāsa, “You are most fortunate: the Lord, Bhagavān Himself, has punished you.

@39
formerly  Mahāprabhu  Me  showed  respect
pain  feeling  in mind  I  made  a plan
= Formerly Mahāprabhu treated Me with respect. That pained Me, and I thought of a plan.

@40
liberation  the best  making  I gave  of the Vāsiṣṭha  an exposition
angry  becoming  the Lord  to Me  did  dishonor
= I expounded the *Vāsiṣṭha*, declaring liberation the highest, and the Lord grew angry and dishonored Me.

@41
punishment  receiving  became  my  supreme  joy
which  punishment  received  fortunate  Śrī Mukunda
= Receiving that punishment, I felt supreme joy—the same punishment that the fortunate Śrī Mukunda received.

@42
which  punishment  received  Śrī Śacī  the fortunate
that  grace of punishment  other  people  will get  where
= The punishment that the fortunate Śrī Śacī received—where can anyone else find such grace in punishment?”

@43
thus  saying  the Ācārya  him  comforting
joyful  becoming  came  to Mahāprabhu
= Having comforted him with these words, the Ācārya joyfully went to Mahāprabhu.

@44
to the Lord  He says  Your  not  I understand  this  pastime
than Me  of grace  the object  You made  Kamalā
= He said to the Lord, “I do not understand this pastime of Yours. You have made Kamalā a greater object of Your grace than Me.

@45
to Me  even  ever  such  not  is  grace
at Your  feet  I  what  did  offense
= Such grace You have never shown even to Me. What offense have I committed at Your feet?”

@46
this  hearing  Mahāprabhu  to laugh  began
calling  Kamalākānta  pleased  became
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu began to laugh. He called Kamalākānta and was pleased with him.

@47
the Ācārya  says  to him  why  gave  audience
in two  ways  he does  to Me  mockery
= The Ācārya said, “Why have You let him see You? He has made a mockery of Me in two ways.”

@48
hearing  the Lord’s  mind  pleased  became
of both  the inner  meaning  both  they  knew
= Hearing this, the Lord was pleased at heart. Each of Them knew the other’s inner meaning.

@49
the Lord  says  madcap  such  why  do you
of the Ācārya  disgrace  dharma  harm  that  you practice
= The Lord said, “*Bāuliyā*, why do you act like this? What you do brings the Ācārya disgrace and harms His dharma.

@50
acceptance  ever  not  should make  of the king  wealth
of the worldly man’s  food  eating  defiled  becomes  the mind
= He must never accept a king’s wealth. When one eats the food of worldly people, the mind becomes defiled.

@51
the mind  defiled  being  is not  of Kṛṣṇa  remembrance
remembrance of Kṛṣṇa  without  becomes  fruitless  life
= When the mind is defiled, one cannot remember Kṛṣṇa, and without remembrance of Kṛṣṇa, life is fruitless.

@52
before people  shame  arises  dharma  good name  becomes  loss
such  act  not  do  ever  this  knowing
= One is shamed before people, and one’s dharma and good name are lost. Knowing this, never do such a thing.”

@53
this  teaching  for all  all  in mind  took
Ācārya Gosāñi  in mind  joy  felt
= This teaching was meant for everyone, and everyone took it to heart. Ācārya Gosāñi felt joy within.

@54
of the Ācārya  the intention  the Lord  alone  understands
of the Lord  deep  words  the Ācārya  comprehends
= Only the Lord understands the Ācārya’s intention, and only the Ācārya grasps the Lord’s deep words.

@55
in this  indeed  matter  is  much  to consider
of the book’s  enlarging  for fear  I cannot  write
= There is much to consider in this matter, but I fear making the book too long, so I cannot write it out.

@56
Śrī Yadunandanācārya  of Advaita  a branch
his  of branches  sub-branches  of the host  is not  account
= Śrī Yadunandanācārya is a branch of Advaita. His branches and sub-branches are beyond counting.

@57
of Vāsudeva  Datta  he  of mercy  the recipient
in every way  has taken shelter  Caitanya’s feet
= He is the recipient of Vāsudeva Datta’s mercy and has taken shelter at Caitanya’s feet in every way.

@58
Bhāgavatācārya  and  Viṣṇudāsācārya
Cakrapāṇi  Ācārya  and  Ananta  Ācārya
= Bhāgavatācārya, Viṣṇudāsācārya, Cakrapāṇi Ācārya, Ananta Ācārya,

@59
Nandinī  and  Kāmadeva  Caitanya dāsa
Durlabha  Viśvāsa  and  Vanamāli dāsa
= Nandinī, Kāmadeva, Caitanya dāsa, Durlabha Viśvāsa, Vanamāli dāsa,

@60
Jagannātha  Kara  and  Kara  Bhavanātha
Hṛdayānanda  Sena  and  dāsa  Bholānātha
= Jagannātha Kara, Bhavanātha Kara, Hṛdayānanda Sena, Bholānātha dāsa,

@61
Yādava dāsa  Vijaya dāsa  dāsa  Janārdana
Ananta dāsa  Kānu Paṇḍita  dāsa  Nārāyaṇa
= Yādava dāsa, Vijaya dāsa, Janārdana dāsa, Ananta dāsa, Kānu Paṇḍita, Nārāyaṇa dāsa,

@62
Śrīvatsa  Paṇḍita  Brahmacārī  Haridāsa
Puruṣottama  Brahmacārī  and  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Śrīvatsa Paṇḍita, Haridāsa Brahmacārī, Puruṣottama Brahmacārī, Kṛṣṇadāsa,

@63
Puruṣottama  Paṇḍita  and  Raghunātha
Vanamālī  Kavicandra  and  Vaidyanātha
= Puruṣottama Paṇḍita, Raghunātha, Vanamālī Kavicandra, Vaidyanātha,

@64
Lokanātha  Paṇḍita  and  Murāri  Paṇḍita
Śrī Haricaraṇa  and  Mādhava  Paṇḍita
= Lokanātha Paṇḍita, Murāri Paṇḍita, Śrī Haricaraṇa, Mādhava Paṇḍita,

@65
Vijaya  Paṇḍita  and  Paṇḍita  Śrīrāma
countless  Advaita’s branches  how many  will I take  names
= Vijaya Paṇḍita and Paṇḍita Śrīrāma. Advaita’s branches are countless—how many names can I give?

@66
by the gardener  given  water  the Advaita trunk  supplies
by that  water  live  the branches  flowers and fruits  bear
= The Advaita trunk supplies the water that the gardener gives; by that water the branches live and bear flowers and fruits.

@67
of these  among  after the gardener  some  branches
not  accept  Caitanya the gardener  misfortune  because of
= Among them, after the gardener’s departure, some branches, through misfortune, did not accept Caitanya the gardener.

@68
He made them grow  He kept them alive  Him  not  they accepted
ungrateful  they became  at them  the trunk  angry  became
= He had made them grow and kept them alive, yet they did not accept Him. They became ungrateful, and the trunk grew angry with them.

@69
angry  becoming  the trunk  to them  water  not  passes
for lack of water  withered  the branch  drying up  dies
= In its anger the trunk passes them no water, and for lack of water the branch withers, dries up and dies.

@70
without Caitanya  the body  dry wood  like
while living  dead  he  when dead  punishes  Yama
= A body without Caitanya—without consciousness—is like dry wood. Such a person is dead while still alive, and when he dies, Yama punishes him.

@71
only  this  group  toward  is not  this  punishment
from Caitanya  averse  whoever  he  indeed  an unbeliever
= This punishment is not for this group alone: whoever turns away from Caitanya is a *pāṣaṇḍa*, an unbeliever.

@72
whether  scholar  whether  ascetic  or  householder  renunciant
from Caitanya  averse  whoever  his  this  end
= Be he a scholar, an ascetic, a householder or a renunciant—whoever turns away from Caitanya meets this end.

@73
whoever  took  of Śrī Acyutānanda  the view
those  the Ācārya’s  followers  great devotees
= Those of the Ācārya’s followers who took Śrī Acyutānanda’s view are great devotees.

@74
each one  of the Ācārya’s  mercy  the recipient
without effort  attained  he  Caitanya’s feet
= Each of them received the Ācārya’s mercy and easily attained the feet of Caitanya.

@75
to those  the Ācārya’s followers  my  millions of  obeisances
Acyutānanda  like  Caitanya  the life  whose
= To those followers of the Ācārya I offer millions of obeisances; like Acyutānanda, they have Caitanya as their life.

@76
thus  indeed  I have told  of Ācārya Gosāñi  the followers
of the three  trunks  I made  of the branches  a brief  count
= Thus I have told of Ācārya Gosāñi’s followers; I have given a brief count of the branches of the three trunks.

@77
of the branches  sub-branches  of them  is no  counting
something only  telling  I make  an indication
= The sub-branches of these branches are beyond counting; telling only a little, I give an indication.

@78
Śrī Gadādhara  Paṇḍita’s sub-branches  most excellent
his  sub-branches  some  I make  indeed  a count
= The sub-branches of Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita are most excellent; I shall count some of them.

@79
best of the branches  Dhruvānanda  Śrīdhara  Brahmacārī
Bhāgavatācārya  Haridāsa  Brahmacārī
= Foremost among the branches are Dhruvānanda, Śrīdhara Brahmacārī, Bhāgavatācārya, Haridāsa Brahmacārī,

@80
Ananta  Ācārya  Kavidatta  Miśra Nayana
Gaṅgāmantrī  Māmu  Ṭhākura  Kaṇṭhābharaṇa
= Ananta Ācārya, Kavidatta, Nayana Miśra, Gaṅgāmantrī, Māmu Ṭhākura, Kaṇṭhābharaṇa,

@81
Bhūgarbha  Gosāñi  and  Bhāgavata  dāsa
who  both  coming  made  in Vṛndāvana  residence
= Bhūgarbha Gosāñi and Bhāgavata dāsa, who both came to live in Vṛndāvana,

@82
Vāṇīnātha  Brahmacārī  great  soul
Vallabha Caitanya dāsa  full of love for Kṛṣṇa
= the great soul Vāṇīnātha Brahmacārī, Vallabha Caitanya dāsa, full of love for Kṛṣṇa,

@83
Śrīnātha  Cakravartī  and  Uddhava  dāsa
Jitāmitra  the woodcutter  Jagannātha dāsa
= Śrīnātha Cakravartī, Uddhava dāsa, Jitāmitra, Jagannātha dāsa the woodcutter (*kāṣṭhakāṭā*),

@84
Śrī Hari  Ācārya  dāsa  Puriyā-gopāla
Kṛṣṇadāsa  Brahmacārī  Puṣpagopāla
= Śrī Hari Ācārya, Puriyā-gopāla dāsa, Kṛṣṇadāsa Brahmacārī, Puṣpagopāla,

@85
Śrīharṣa  Raghu Miśra  Paṇḍita  Lakṣmīnātha
Baṅgavāṭī Caitanya dāsa  Śrī Raghunātha
= Śrīharṣa, Raghu Miśra, Paṇḍita Lakṣmīnātha, Caitanya dāsa of Baṅgavāṭī, Śrī Raghunātha,

@86
Amogha  Paṇḍita  Hastigopāla  Caitanya-vallabha
Yadu  Gāṅguli  and  Maṅgala  Vaiṣṇava
= Amogha Paṇḍita, Hastigopāla, Caitanya-vallabha, Yadu Gāṅguli and Maṅgala Vaiṣṇava.

@87
Cakravartī  Śivānanda  among the branches  exalted
Madana-gopāla’s  at the feet  whose  rest
= Śivānanda Cakravartī stands high among the branches; his resting place is the feet of Madana-gopāla.

@88
thus  indeed  in brief  I have told  of the Paṇḍita  the followers
in the same way  other  of branches and sub-branches  the count
= Thus I have briefly told of the Paṇḍita’s followers; the other branches and sub-branches are to be counted in the same way.

@89
of the Paṇḍita  the followers  all  devotees  blessed
the beloved of life  of all  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
= All the Paṇḍita’s followers are blessed devotees, and Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the beloved of their lives.

@90
of these  three  trunks  I made  of the branches  the count
of whom all  by remembrance  of worldly bondage  release
= I have counted the branches of these three trunks; by remembering them all, one is released from the bondage of worldly existence.

@91
of whom all  by remembrance  we attain  Caitanya’s feet
of whom all  by remembrance  is  of desires  fulfillment
= By remembering them all we attain Caitanya’s feet; by remembering them all our desires are fulfilled.

@92
therefore  of them all  I worship  the feet
of Caitanya the gardener  I tell  of the pastimes  the sequence
= Therefore I worship the feet of them all, and now I tell in order the pastimes of Caitanya the gardener.

@93
of Gaura’s pastimes  the nectar  ocean  shoreless  fathomless
who  to make  can  in it  of plunging  the wish
= The nectar ocean of Gaura’s pastimes is shoreless and fathomless. Who could even wish to plunge into it?

@94
of its  sweetness  by the fragrance  enticed  becomes  the mind
therefore  on the shore  staying  I taste  one  drop
= Yet my mind is enticed by the fragrance of its sweetness, so, standing on the shore, I taste a single drop.

@95
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  at the feet  whose  hope
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, tell the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
