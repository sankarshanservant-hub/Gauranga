@title The Order of the Vaiṣṇavas

@1
I offer obeisance  to Caitanya-deva  to Him
to the Supreme Lord  by whose will
forcibly  is made to dance  wonderfully
on the stage of writing  a fool  even  this
= I offer obeisance to Śrī Caitanya-deva, the Supreme Lord, by whose will even this fool is forcibly made to dance so wonderfully on the stage of writing.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  Gauracandra
glory  glory  to the supremely blissful  glory  to Nityānanda
= All glory, all glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! All glory to Him who is supreme bliss, glory to Nityānanda!

@3
glory  glory to Advaita  Ācārya  all-merciful
glory  glory  to Gadādhara  Paṇḍita  the noble-hearted
= Glory, glory to the all-merciful Advaita Ācārya! Glory, glory to the noble-hearted Gadādhara Paṇḍita!

@4
glory  glory  Śrīvāsa and the others  all  devotees
bowed down  being  I worship  of them all  the feet
= Glory, glory to all the devotees headed by Śrīvāsa! Bowing low, I worship the feet of them all.

@5
a mute  poetry  composes  of whom all  by the remembrance
a lame man  mountains  crosses  a blind man  sees  the stars
= By remembering Them, the mute compose poetry, the lame cross mountains and the blind see the stars.

@6
all this  not  accept  who  scholars  all
of them all  study of learning  the croaking of frogs
= Those scholars who do not accept all this — their study of learning is only the croaking of frogs.

@7
this  all  not  accepts  whoever  practices  devotion to Kṛṣṇa
Kṛṣṇa's mercy  is not  upon him  is not  his  goal
= Whoever does not accept all this, even though he practices devotion to Kṛṣṇa, does not receive Kṛṣṇa's mercy and reaches no goal.

@8
formerly  as  Jarāsandha and the other  kings
Vedic religion  following  perform  of Viṣṇu  worship
= Formerly kings such as Jarāsandha followed the Vedic religion and worshiped Viṣṇu,

@9
Kṛṣṇa  not  accepts  therefore  demon  as  we regard
Caitanya  not  if one accepts  likewise  a demon  him  we know
= but they did not accept Kṛṣṇa, and so we regard them as demons (*daityas*). In the same way, we know that one who does not accept Śrī Caitanya is a demon.

@10
Me  not  if they accept  all  people  will meet  ruin
for this  reason  melting with mercy  the Lord  accepted  sannyāsa
= “If people do not accept Me, they will all be ruined.” For this reason the Lord, His heart melting with mercy, accepted *sannyāsa*.

@11
thinking (Me) a sannyāsī  to Me  they will offer  obeisance
even so  will be cut off  suffering  they will attain  deliverance
= “Thinking Me a *sannyāsī*, they will offer Me obeisance; even so, their suffering will be cut off and they will attain deliverance.”

@12
such  merciful  Caitanya  not  worships  who  person
the best of all  even if he is  him  among the demons  counting
= A person who does not worship so merciful a Caitanya is counted among the demons (*asuras*), even if he is the best of men.

@13
therefore  again  I say  raised  arms  with
Caitanya and Nityānanda  worship  false argument  giving up
= Therefore, with arms raised, I say again: give up false argument and worship Caitanya and Nityānanda!

@14
if  or  a logician  says  logic  that  is the proof
in the science of logic  established  whatever  that  is worthy of worship
= Or if a logician says, “Logic is the proof; whatever is established by the science of logic, that alone is worthy of worship,”

@15
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya's mercy  do  consider
consideration  if you make  in the heart  you will find  wonder
= then consider the mercy of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. If you consider it, your heart will be struck with wonder.

@16
many  births  performs  if  hearing  chanting
still  indeed  not  obtains  at Kṛṣṇa's feet  the treasure of love
= One may hear and chant for many births and still not attain the treasure of love (*prema*) for Kṛṣṇa's feet.

@17
by knowledge  easily attained  liberation
enjoyment  by sacrifice and other pious acts
that  this  by thousands of spiritual practices
devotion to Hari  very rarely attained
= “Liberation is easily attained through knowledge, and enjoyment through sacrifices and other pious acts; but devotion to Hari is extremely rare, even after thousands of spiritual practices.” (Tantra; also Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 1.1.36)

@18
Kṛṣṇa  if  slips away  to a devotee  enjoyment  liberation  giving
ever  devotion  not  gives  keeps  hiding
= Kṛṣṇa may slip away by giving a devotee enjoyment or liberation, but devotion He does not readily give; He keeps it hidden.

@19
O king  master  guru  indeed  of you  of the Yadus
worshipable Deity  beloved friend  head of the family  sometimes  and  servant  of you
let it be  so  my dear  the Lord  to those who worship  Mukunda
liberation  gives  sometimes  indeed  not  bhakti-yoga
= “O king, the Lord Mukunda is the master, guru, worshipable Deity, beloved friend and head of the family for you and the Yadus, and sometimes He is even your servant. Be that as it may, my dear king, to those who worship Him the Lord sometimes grants liberation, but not *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 5.6.18)

@20
such  love  Śrī Caitanya  gave  here  and there
Jagāi  Mādhāi  even to  of others  what  talk
= Such love Śrī Caitanya gave everywhere, even to Jagāi and Mādhāi — what to speak of others!

@21
the independent Lord  love  the hidden treasure-house
distributed  to whomever  to anyone  not  made  discrimination
= The independent Lord distributed love, that hidden treasure, to anyone and everyone without discrimination.

@22
even today  see  Caitanya's name  whoever  takes
in love for Kṛṣṇa  with thrills and tears overwhelmed  he  becomes
= See how even today whoever takes Caitanya's name is overwhelmed with love for Kṛṣṇa, his hair standing on end and tears flowing.

@23
Nityānanda  on saying  there is  the rise of love for Kṛṣṇa
are shaken  all  limbs  a Gaṅgā of tears  flows
= When one says “Nityānanda,” love for Kṛṣṇa awakens; all one's limbs are shaken, and a Gaṅgā of tears flows.

@24
Kṛṣṇa's name  makes  of offenses  consideration
Kṛṣṇa  if one says  of an offender  not  is  transformation
= The name of Kṛṣṇa takes offenses into account: when an offender says “Kṛṣṇa,” no transformation takes place in him.

@25
that  hard as stone  heart  alas  this
which  being taken  by the names of Hari
not  is transformed  and  when  transformation
in the eyes  tears  in the hairs of the body  thrill
= “Alas, that heart is as hard as stone which does not melt when it takes up the names of Hari; for when it does melt, tears fill the eyes and the hairs of the body stand on end with joy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.3.24)

@26
one  by Kṛṣṇa's name  makes  of all sins  destruction
of love  the cause  devotion  makes  manifest
= A single name of Kṛṣṇa destroys all sins and manifests devotion, the cause of love.

@27
of love  on the rise  there are  love's  transformations
perspiration, trembling, hair standing on end and the rest  choked voice and streams of tears
= When love arises, love's transformations appear: perspiration, trembling, hair standing on end, a choked voice and streams of tears.

@28
effortlessly  the end of worldly existence  Kṛṣṇa's  service
of one  Kṛṣṇa's name  as the fruit  one obtains  such  wealth
= Worldly existence ends without effort, and one gains service to Kṛṣṇa: such wealth one obtains as the fruit of a single name of Kṛṣṇa.

@29-30
such  Kṛṣṇa's name  if  one takes  many times
still  if  love  is not  is not  a stream of tears
then  I know  offenses  in him  abundant
the seed of Kṛṣṇa's name  in him  not  makes  sprout
= If one chants such a name of Kṛṣṇa many times and still love does not appear and tears do not flow, then I know that his offenses are many, and the seed of Kṛṣṇa's name does not sprout in him.

@31
with Caitanya and Nityānanda  there are not  these  considerations
the name  on taking  love  They give  flows  a stream of tears
= With Caitanya and Nityānanda there are no such considerations. When one takes Their names, They give love, and tears flow.

@32
independent  Lord  the Master  extremely  magnanimous
Him  not  if one worships  ever  not  is  deliverance
= The Lord is the independent Supreme Lord and supremely magnanimous. Without worshiping Him one is never delivered.

@33
O  foolish  people  hear  the Caitanya-maṅgala
Caitanya's glory  in which  you will know  all
= O foolish people, hear the *Caitanya-maṅgala* (the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*), in which you will come to know all of Śrī Caitanya's glory.

@34
Kṛṣṇa's pastimes  in the Bhāgavatam  tells  Veda-vyāsa
of Caitanya's pastimes  the Vyāsa  Vṛndāvana dāsa
= Veda-vyāsa tells Kṛṣṇa's pastimes in the *Bhāgavatam*; the Vyāsa of Śrī Caitanya's pastimes is Vṛndāvana dāsa.

@35
Vṛndāvana dāsa  composed  the Caitanya-maṅgala
of which  by the hearing  is destroyed  all  that is inauspicious
= Vṛndāvana dāsa composed the *Caitanya-maṅgala*; by hearing it, all that is inauspicious is destroyed.

@36
of Caitanya and Nitāi  in which  we know  the glory
in which  we know  of the conclusions of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the limit
= From it we learn the glory of Caitanya and Nitāi, and from it we learn the farthest reach of the conclusions of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

@37
in the Bhāgavatam  all  of the conclusions of devotion  the essence
he has written  here  knowing  making  extraction
= All the essence of the conclusions of devotion in the *Bhāgavatam* he has written down in it, drawing it out with full understanding.

@38
the Caitanya-maṅgala  hears  if  an unbeliever  a yavana
he too  a great Vaiṣṇava  becomes  at that moment
= If even an unbeliever (*pāṣaṇḍī*) or a *yavana* hears the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, he at once becomes a great Vaiṣṇava.

@39
a human being  to compose  cannot  such  book  blessed
through the mouth of Vṛndāvana dāsa  the speaker  Śrī Caitanya
= No human being could compose such a blessed book: the speaker, through the mouth of Vṛndāvana dāsa, is Śrī Caitanya Himself.

@40
at Vṛndāvana dāsa's feet  millions of  obeisances
such  book  composing  he  delivered  the world
= Millions of obeisances at the feet of Vṛndāvana dāsa! By writing such a book he has delivered the world.

@41
Nārāyaṇī  of Caitanya  of the remnants  the recipient
her  in the womb  was born  Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa
= Nārāyaṇī received the remnants of Śrī Caitanya's food, and from her womb Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa was born.

@42
his  how  wonderful  of Caitanya's life  description
of which  by the hearing  pure  made  the three worlds
= How wonderful is his description of Śrī Caitanya's life! Hearing it has purified the three worlds.

@43
therefore  worship  O people  Caitanya and Nityānanda
will be cut off  the sufferings of worldly existence  you will attain  the bliss of love
= Therefore, O people, worship Caitanya and Nityānanda! The sufferings of worldly existence will be cut off, and you will attain the bliss of love.

@44
Vṛndāvana dāsa  composed  the Caitanya-maṅgala
in it  Caitanya's pastimes  he described  all
= Vṛndāvana dāsa composed the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, and in it he described all of Śrī Caitanya's pastimes.

@45
in brief  making  all  pastimes  he arranged
afterwards  expanding  of them  made  description
= First he arranged all the pastimes in brief, as threads (*sūtra*); afterwards he described them in detail.

@46
of Caitanya-candra  the pastimes  endless  boundless
describing  describing  the book  became  long
= The pastimes of Caitanya-candra are endless and boundless; as he went on describing them, the book grew long.

@47
the length  seeing  some  hesitation  felt  the mind
in the summary  included  some  pastimes  not  made  description
= Seeing its length, he felt some hesitation, and some pastimes included in the summary he did not describe.

@48
in describing Nityānanda's pastimes  there was  absorption
Caitanya's  final pastimes  remained  left over
= He became absorbed in describing Nityānanda's pastimes, and Śrī Caitanya's final pastimes were left untold.

@49
those  all  of pastimes  to hear  the description
living in Vṛndāvana  of the devotees  longing  the mind
= The devotees living in Vṛndāvana longed to hear a description of all those pastimes.

@50
in Vṛndāvana  beneath a wish-fulfilling tree  a golden  abode
the great Yogapīṭha  there  a jeweled throne
= In Vṛndāvana, beneath a wish-fulfilling tree, stands a golden temple. There is the great Yogapīṭha, with a jeweled throne.

@51
on it  seated  is  always  the son of the king of Vraja
Śrī Govinda-deva  by name  in person  the transcendental Cupid (Madana)
= On it is always seated the son of the king of Vraja, named Śrī Govinda-deva, the transcendental god of love (*Madana*) in person.

@52
royal service  there is  there  wonderful  of kinds
divine  offerings  divine  clothes and ornaments
= There He receives royal service of many wonderful kinds, with divine offerings and divine clothes and ornaments.

@53
thousands of  servants  service  render  at every moment
with a thousand mouths  the service  not  can be  described
= Thousands of servants serve Him at every moment; even with a thousand mouths that service could not be described.

@54
of the service  the superintendent  Śrī Paṇḍita Haridāsa
his  fame and qualities  in all the world  are manifest
= The superintendent of that service is Śrī Haridāsa Paṇḍita, whose fame and virtues are known throughout the world.

@55
well-mannered  tolerant  peaceful  generous  grave
sweet in speech  sweet in conduct  deeply steady
= He is well-mannered, tolerant, peaceful, generous and grave; his words are sweet, his conduct is sweet, and he is deeply steady.

@56
of all  one who shows honor  he does  of all  the welfare
duplicity, envy and malice  devoid of  his  heart
= He honors everyone and works for everyone's welfare. His heart is free of duplicity, envy and malice.

@57
Kṛṣṇa's  which  general  good qualities  fifty
those  all  of qualities  his  in the body  the abode
= All the fifty general good qualities of Kṛṣṇa reside in his person.

@58
of whom  there is  devotion  for the Lord  free of material desire
with all  qualities  there  reside  the gods
for Hari  of one without devotion  where  great qualities
by the chariot of the mind  toward the unreal  running  outward
= “In one who has unalloyed devotion (*akiñcanā bhakti*) for the Lord, all the gods reside with all their good qualities. But how can great qualities exist in one who has no devotion to Hari? Riding the chariot of his mind, he runs outward toward what is unreal.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 5.18.12)

@59
of Paṇḍita Gosāñi  the disciple  Ananta Ācārya
whose body is full of love for Kṛṣṇa  magnanimous  noble in every way
= Ananta Ācārya was a disciple of Paṇḍita Gosāñi (Gadādhara Paṇḍita). His body was filled with love for Kṛṣṇa; he was magnanimous and noble in every way.

@60
his  unlimited  qualities  who  can  make manifest
his  dear  disciple  this  Paṇḍita Haridāsa
= Who can describe his unlimited qualities? His dear disciple is this Haridāsa Paṇḍita.

@61
in Caitanya and Nityānanda  his  supreme  faith
in Caitanya's life  his  supreme  delight
= He has supreme faith in Caitanya and Nityānanda and takes supreme delight in Śrī Caitanya's life.

@62
of Vaiṣṇavas  the qualities  appreciating  not  he sees  faults
by body, mind and words  gives  to the Vaiṣṇavas  satisfaction
= He appreciates the good qualities of Vaiṣṇavas and never sees their faults. With body, mind and words he pleases the Vaiṣṇavas.

@63
constantly  hears  he  the Caitanya-maṅgala
his  by the grace  hear  the Vaiṣṇavas  all
= He constantly hears the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, and by his grace all the Vaiṣṇavas hear it too.

@64
by his words  the assembly  bright  makes  like  the full moon
by the nectar of his own qualities  increases  the Vaiṣṇavas' bliss
= With his words he lights up the assembly like the full moon, and with the nectar of his qualities he increases the joy of the Vaiṣṇavas.

@65
he  great  mercy  showing  order  gave  to me
Gaurāṅga's  final pastimes  to describe  for the sake of
= Out of great kindness he ordered me to describe Gaurāṅga's final pastimes.

@66
of Kāśīśvara Gosāñi  the disciple  Govinda Gosāñi
of Govinda  dear servant  his  equal  there is none
= Govinda Gosāñi, a disciple of Kāśīśvara Gosāñi, is a dear servant of Śrī Govinda; there is no one equal to him.

@67
Yādavācārya Gosāñi  of Śrī Rūpa  the companion
in Caitanya's life  he  very  great  delighting
= Yādavācārya Gosāñi, a companion of Śrī Rūpa, takes great delight in Śrī Caitanya's life.

@68
of Paṇḍita Gosāñi  the disciple  Bhūgarbha Gosāñi
talk of Gaura  except  his  in the mouth  other  there is none
= Bhūgarbha Gosāñi, a disciple of Paṇḍita Gosāñi, speaks of nothing but Gaura.

@69
his  disciples  Govinda's  worshiper  Caitanya dāsa
Mukundānanda Cakravartī  loving  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= His disciples are Caitanya dāsa, the worshiper of Govinda, Mukundānanda Cakravartī and Kṛṣṇadāsa, who is full of love.

@70
of Ācārya Gosāñi  the disciple  Cakravartī Śivānanda
always  his  in the heart  Śrī Caitanya and Nityānanda
= Śivānanda Cakravartī, a disciple of Ācārya Gosāñi, always holds Śrī Caitanya and Nityānanda in his heart.

@71
and  as many as  in Vṛndāvana  live  devotees
the final pastimes  to hear  of all  there was  the wish
= All the other devotees living in Vṛndāvana also wished to hear the final pastimes.

@72
to me  order  gave  all  compassion  showing
of them all  at the word  I write  shameless  becoming
= Out of compassion they all gave me the order, and at their word I write, casting off shame.

@73
the Vaiṣṇavas'  order  having received  with an anxious heart
to Madana-gopāla  I went  permission  to ask
= Having received the order of the Vaiṣṇavas, but anxious at heart, I went to Madana-gopāla to ask His permission.

@74
audience  having had  I made  to the feet  obeisance
Gosāñi dāsa  the priest  does  service to the feet
= After seeing Him, I worshiped His feet. The priest Gosāñi dāsa was serving His feet.

@75
of the Lord  at the feet  when  permission  I asked
from the Lord's neck  a garland  slipping  fell
= When I asked for permission at the Lord's feet, a garland slipped from the Lord's neck and fell.

@76
all  Vaiṣṇavas  the sound of “Hari”  made
Gosāñi dāsa  bringing  the garland  my  on the neck  placed
= All the Vaiṣṇavas called out “Hari!”, and Gosāñi dāsa brought the garland and placed it around my neck.

@77
the garland of permission  receiving  my  there was  joy
right there  I made  of this  book  the beginning
= Receiving the garland as His permission, I was filled with joy, and right there I began this book.

@78
this  book  makes write  me  Madana-mohana
my  writing  like  a parrot's  recitation
= It is Madana-mohana who makes me write this book; my writing is like a parrot's recitation.

@79
that  I write  Madana-gopāla  me  what  makes write
of wood  a puppet  as  a magician  makes dance
= I write whatever Madana-gopāla makes me write, as a magician makes a wooden puppet dance.

@80
family Deity  my  Madana-mohana
whose  servants  Raghunātha  Rūpa  Sanātana
= My family Deity is Madana-mohana, whose servants are Raghunātha, Rūpa and Sanātana.

@81
Vṛndāvana dāsa's  lotus feet  making  meditation
his  permission  taking  I write  in which  is welfare
= Meditating on the lotus feet of Vṛndāvana dāsa and taking his permission, I write what brings auspiciousness.

@82
in Caitanya's pastimes  the Vyāsa  Vṛndāvana dāsa
his  mercy  without  to others  not  is  revealed
= In Śrī Caitanya's pastimes, Vṛndāvana dāsa is the Vyāsa. Without his mercy they are not revealed to anyone else.

@83
foolish  low  insignificant  I  greedy for sense objects
by the strength of the Vaiṣṇavas' order  I make  such  daring
= I am foolish, low, insignificant and greedy for sense objects; only by the strength of the Vaiṣṇavas' order do I dare so much.

@84
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  of the feet  this  power
of which  by the remembrance  fulfilled  are  desires  all
= This is the power of the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha: by remembering them, all one's desires are fulfilled.

@85
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  at the feet  whose  hope
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, tell the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
