= By the mercy of Śrī Caitanya, even a child who has studied the scriptures can ascertain the true form of Him who enjoys pastimes in Vraja.

= All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

= I have explained the meaning of the fourth verse. Now hear attentively the meaning of the fifth.

@4

= Before disclosing the meaning of the original verse and applying it, I shall first give a hint of it.

@5

= This I have shown to be the essence of the fourth verse: the Lord descended in this form to spread love for Kṛṣṇa and His Holy Name.

@6
true  this  reason  but  it  external
another  one  reason  hear  there is  internal
= This reason is true, but it is external. Hear: there is another reason, an internal one.

@7

Kṛṣṇa  descended  became  in the scriptures  they proclaim
= The scriptures proclaim that in the past Kṛṣṇa descended to remove the burden of the earth.

@8
of the Supreme Lord Himself  the work  is not  of the burden  removal
of maintenance  the agent  Viṣṇu  does  of the world  protection
= Yet removing that burden is not the work of the Supreme Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*). Viṣṇu, the maintainer, protects the world.

@9
but  of Kṛṣṇa  which  is  of descent  the time
of the burden  of removal  the time  with it  became  coincident
= But the time for removing the burden coincided with the time of Kṛṣṇa's descent.

@10
the complete  Bhagavān  descends  at which  time
other  all  avatāras  in Him  coming  join
= When the complete Bhagavān descends, all the other avatāras come and join in Him.

@11-12
Nārāyaṇa  the four vyūhas  Matsya  and other  avatāras
of the yugas  of the manvantaras  avatāras  as many as  there are  others
all  coming  of Kṛṣṇa  in the body  become  descended
thus  descends  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān  complete
= Nārāyaṇa, the four *vyūhas*, Matsya and the other avatāras, the avatāras of the yugas and of the manvantaras, and all others there are — all come and descend within the body of Kṛṣṇa. In this way Kṛṣṇa, the complete Bhagavān, descends.

@13
therefore  Viṣṇu  then  of Kṛṣṇa  in the body
of Viṣṇu  by means  Kṛṣṇa  does  of the demons  the slaying
= Therefore Viṣṇu was then present within Kṛṣṇa's body, and it was through Viṣṇu that Kṛṣṇa slew the demons.

@14
secondary  work  this  of the demons  killing
for which  the descent  I tell  that  root  cause
= This killing of demons is a secondary task. Now I shall tell the root cause for which He descended.

@15-16
of love  of the rasa  the essence  to make  tasting
of spontaneous love  of the path  devotion  in the world  to make  propagation
of the relishers of rasa  the crown  Kṛṣṇa  supremely  merciful
these  two  reasons  from  of the desire  the arising
= Kṛṣṇa, the crown of all relishers of *rasa*, is supremely merciful. His desire to descend arose from two reasons: to taste the very essence of the *rasa* of love, and to spread in the world devotion on the path of spontaneous love (*rāga-mārga*).

@17
by awareness of majesty  whole  world  mixed
by majesty  weakened  in love  not  My  pleasure
= “The whole world is mixed with awareness of My majesty, and love weakened by that majesty gives Me no pleasure.

@18
Me  as the Lord  regards  himself  lowly
by his  love  controlled  I  not  become  subservient
= If someone regards Me as the Lord and himself as lowly, I am not controlled by his love and do not become subservient to him.

@19
to Me  indeed  whichever  whichever  devotee  worships  in whichever  mood
him  in that  that  mood  I reciprocate  this  My  nature
= In whatever mood any devotee worships Me, in that very mood I reciprocate with him. Such is My nature.

@20
those who  as  to Me  surrender
them  so indeed  I reciprocate with  I
My  path  follow
people  O son of Pṛthā  in every way
= “As people surrender to Me, so I reciprocate with them. O son of Pṛthā, people follow My path in every way.” (Bhagavad-gītā 4.11)

@21-22
my  son  my  friend  my  of my life  the lord
in such a mood  whoever  to Me  renders  pure devotion
himself  great  regards  Me  equal  or lower
in that  mood  become  I  his  subservient
= When someone serves Me with pure devotion thinking, ‘He is my son,’ ‘He is my friend,’ ‘He is the lord of my life,’ and regards himself as great and Me as his equal or even lower, then in that mood I become subservient to him.

@23
to Me  devotion  indeed  of living beings
for immortality  is fit
fortunately  that  has arisen  for Me  affection
of you  leading to Me
= “Devotion to Me brings living beings to immortality. How fortunate you are that you have come to love Me, for that love brings you to Me!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.82.44)

@24
mother  Me  in the mood of a son  does  binding
as utterly helpless  thinking  does  fondling  caring
= My mother binds Me, seeing Me as her son. Thinking Me utterly helpless, she fondles Me and cares for Me.

@25
friend  in pure friendship  does  on My shoulders  climbing
You  what  big  man  You  I  equal
= My friend, in pure friendship, climbs onto My shoulders and says, ‘What sort of big man are You? You and I are equal!’

@26
beloved  if  jealous anger  showing  does  rebuke
the Vedas  hymns  than  steals  that  My  heart
= And when My beloved, in a mood of jealous anger (*māna*), rebukes Me, that captivates My heart more than the hymns of the Vedas.

@27-28
this  pure devotion  taking  I shall make  descent
I shall perform  many kinds of  wonderful  pastimes
in Vaikuṇṭha and elsewhere  not  which  which  of the pastimes  manifestation
those  those  pastimes  I shall perform  in which  My  wonder
= Bringing this pure devotion with Me, I shall descend and perform wonderful pastimes of many kinds. I shall perform pastimes never manifest in Vaikuṇṭha or anywhere else — pastimes that fill even Me with wonder.

@29
toward Me  of the gopīs  in the mood of a paramour
Yogamāyā  will create  by her own power
= By her own power Yogamāyā will make the gopīs regard Me as their paramour (*upapati*).

@30
even I  not  know  that  not  know  the gopīs
of the two  by the beauty and qualities  of the two  always  steals  the mind
= Neither I nor the gopīs will know this, for the beauty and qualities of each will always captivate the mind of the other.

@31
religious norms  leaving aside  out of loving attachment  the two  make  union
sometimes  they meet  sometimes  not  they meet  of destiny  the arrangement
= Setting aside religious norms, the two will unite out of loving attachment (*rāga*). Sometimes they will meet and sometimes not, as destiny arranges.

@32
these  all  of rasa  the essence  I shall make  tasting
by this  means  I shall show  to all  devotees  mercy
= I shall taste the very essence of all these *rasas*, and in this way I shall bestow mercy on all devotees.

@33
of Vraja  the pure  love  hearing  the devotees
on the path of rāga  worship  so that  giving up  religious duties and rites
= Hearing of the pure love of Vraja, the devotees will worship Me on the path of spontaneous love, giving up religious duties and rites.”

@34
for the sake of mercy  to the devotees
a human  body  having assumed
performs  such  pastimes
which  having heard  devoted to Him  one must become
= “To show mercy to His devotees, He assumes a humanlike form and performs such pastimes that whoever hears of them must become wholly devoted to Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.33.36)

@35
bhavet  the verb  in the injunctive mood  that  here  says
a duty  certainly  this  otherwise  a fault
= The verb *bhavet*, “must become,” is in the injunctive mood (*vidhi-liṅ*). It declares this a binding duty; to neglect it is a fault.

@36-37
this  desire  as  of Kṛṣṇa's appearance  the cause
of the demons  slaying  a secondary  purpose
in the same  way  Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the complete  Bhagavān
of the religion of the age  establishing  is not  His  desire
= Just as this desire was the cause of Kṛṣṇa's appearance, while slaying the demons was a secondary purpose, so too Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa is the complete Bhagavān, and establishing the religion of the age is not what He Himself desires.

@38
for some  reason  when  arose  to descend  the intention
of the religion of the age  the time  became  at that  time  coincident
= When, for His own reason, He resolved to descend, the time for the religion of the age coincided with it.

@39
for the two  reasons  having descended  taking  the devotees
Himself  relishes  love and  of the Name  the congregational chanting
= For these two reasons He descended with His devotees, and Himself relished love and the congregational chanting of the Name.

@40
by that  means  even to the caṇḍālas  kīrtana  He spreads
of the Name  of love  a garland  weaving  He placed on  the world
= In this way He spread *kīrtana* even to the outcastes (*caṇḍālas*). Weaving a garland of the Name and of love, He placed it around the neck of the whole world.

@41
in this way  of a devotee  the mood  making  acceptance
Himself  practising  devotion  He made  preaching
= Thus, accepting the mood of a devotee, He preached devotion by practising it Himself.

@42
servitude  friendship  parental love  and  which  amorous love
four  loves  of four kinds  devotees  the vessels
= Servitude, friendship, parental love and amorous love are the four kinds of love, and the four kinds of devotees are their vessels.

@43
own  own  mood  all  the best  making  consider
in one's own mood  make  with Kṛṣṇa  of happiness  the tasting
= Each of them considers his own mood the best, and in that mood relishes happiness with Kṛṣṇa.

@44
impartial  becoming  in the heart  consideration  if  we make
all  rasas  than  in amorous love  greater  sweetness
= Yet if we consider the matter impartially in our hearts, amorous love holds greater sweetness than all the other *rasas*.

@45
in each higher one  this  of taste
with special  delight  full  although
love  by one's inclination  sweet
appears  some one  to someone
= “Although this love (*rati*) holds ever greater delight of taste in each higher *rasa*, any one of them appears sweetest to a particular devotee according to his own inclination.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 2.5.38)

@46
therefore  madhura  rasa  I call  its  name
svakīyā  parakīyā  in the form of  two kinds  arrangement
= Therefore I call it *madhura-rasa*, the sweet *rasa*. It takes two forms: *svakīyā*, love as one's own wife, and *parakīyā*, love as another's.

@47
in the parakīyā mood  greatest  of rasa  exultation
Vraja  except  its  elsewhere  not  abode
= In the *parakīyā* mood *rasa* rises to its greatest exultation, and it dwells nowhere but in Vraja.

@48
of the young women of Vraja  this  mood  boundless
among  them  of Śrī Rādhā  of the mood  the pinnacle
= In the young women of Vraja this love is boundless, and among them Śrī Rādhā's love is its very pinnacle.

@49
mature  pure  mood  love  the highest of all
of Kṛṣṇa  of sweetness  of the rasa  of the tasting  the cause
= Her mature and pure love is the highest of all; through it Kṛṣṇa's sweetness is tasted.

@50
therefore  that  mood  acceptance  making
fulfilled  His own  desire  Gaurāṅga  Śrī Hari
= Therefore Śrī Hari, appearing as Gaurāṅga, accepted that mood and fulfilled His own desire.

@51
of the lords of the gods  the fortress  the goal  supremely  of the Upaniṣads
of the sages  the all-in-all  of those who bow  of the hosts  the sweetness
the essence  of love  of all  the cowherd maidens  lotus-eyed

= “He is the fortress of the lords of the gods, the supreme goal of the Upaniṣads, the all-in-all of the sages, the sweetness of the hosts who bow to Him, and the very essence of the love of all the lotus-eyed cowherd maidens. Will that Caitanya ever again come within the range of my eyes?” (Stava-mālā, First Caitanyāṣṭaka 2)

@52

of rasa  the wealth  taking away  sweet  to enjoy  indescribable  who
complexion  His own  covered  radiance  here  Hers  manifesting
that  Lord  in the form of Caitanya  abundantly  to us  may be merciful
= “Playful by nature, He wished to take for Himself and enjoy the boundless, indescribably sweet wealth of *rasa* belonging to one among the host of His loving ones. So He covered His own complexion and revealed here Her radiance. May that Lord, who has appeared in the form of Caitanya, be abundantly merciful to us.” (Stava-mālā, Second Caitanyāṣṭaka 3)

@53

= He established religion for the sake of accepting that mood. Now hear, everyone, as I tell the chief reason for this.

@55

Therefore, although Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in soul, long ago They assumed separate forms on earth. Now the two have become one and appeared as Caitanya. I bow to Him, who is Kṛṣṇa Himself, adorned with Rādhā’s emotion and radiance.” (Svarūpa Dāmodara’s diary)

Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa  one  soul  two  bodies  assuming

= Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one soul. Assuming two bodies, They sport with each other and relish *rasa*.

@57

the emotion  to relish  both  became  in one place
= Now those two are one as Lord Caitanya: to relish that love, both have come together in one form.

@58

= For this reason I shall first describe this truth, for from it the glory of Gaura will be told.

@59

His very own potency  hlādinī  the name  whose
= Rādhikā is the transformation of love for Kṛṣṇa. She is His very own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*), whose name is *hlādinī*.

@60

= *Hlādinī* makes Kṛṣṇa taste bliss, and through *hlādinī* He nourishes His devotees.

@61

= Kṛṣṇa's own form is eternity, knowledge and bliss, and it is complete. His one spiritual potency takes three forms.

@62

in the aspect of consciousness  saṁvit  which  as knowledge  making  we regard
= In its aspect of bliss it is *hlādinī*, in its aspect of existence *sandhinī*, and in its aspect of consciousness *saṁvit*, which we know as knowledge.

@63

= “*Hlādinī*, *sandhinī* and *saṁvit* exist in You as one, O basis of all. But the mixed potency that brings both joy and pain is not in You, for You are free from the material qualities (*guṇas*).” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 1.12.69)

@64
of sandhinī  the essential  portion  śuddha-sattva  the name
of Bhagavān  the existence  has  in which  its rest
= The essence of *sandhinī* is called *śuddha-sattva*, pure being. In it the existence of Bhagavān rests.

@65

= Kṛṣṇa's mother and father, His abode, His house, His bed, His seat and all the rest are transformations of His *śuddha-sattva*.

@66

= “Pure being (*viśuddha-sattva*) is called *vasudeva*, because in it the Supreme Person is revealed without any covering. In that pure being I worship with my mind Bhagavān Vāsudeva, who is beyond the reach of the senses (*adhokṣaja*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 4.3.23)

@67

= Knowledge of Kṛṣṇa as Bhagavān is the essence of *saṁvit*. Knowledge of Brahman and all other knowledge form its retinue.

= The essence of *hlādinī* is love (*prema*); the essence of *prema* is *bhāva*; and the highest limit of *bhāva* is called *mahābhāva*.

= Śrī Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī is *mahābhāva* embodied. She is the mine of all virtues and the crown jewel of Kṛṣṇa's beloveds.
