= When the complete Bhagavān descends, all the other avatāras come and join in Him.

@13

= Therefore Viṣṇu was then present within Kṛṣṇa's body, and it was through Viṣṇu that Kṛṣṇa slew the demons.

@14

@15-16

of the relishers of rasa  the crown  Kṛṣṇa  supremely  merciful

= Kṛṣṇa, the crown of all relishers of *rasa*, is supremely merciful. His desire to descend arose from two reasons: to taste the very essence of the *rasa* of love, and to spread in the world devotion on the path of spontaneous love (*rāga-mārga*).

@17

= “The whole world is mixed with awareness of My majesty, and love weakened by that majesty gives Me no pleasure.

@18

= If someone regards Me as the Lord and himself as lowly, I am not controlled by his love and do not become subservient to him.

@19

= In whatever mood any devotee worships Me, in that very mood I reciprocate with him. Such is My nature.

= “As people surrender to Me, so I reciprocate with them. O son of Pṛthā, people follow My path in every way.” (Bhagavad-gītā 4.11)

@21-22

= When someone serves Me with pure devotion thinking, ‘He is my son,’ ‘He is my friend,’ ‘He is the lord of my life,’ and regards himself as great and Me as his equal or even lower, then in that mood I become subservient to him.

@23

of you  leading to Me
= “Devotion to Me brings living beings to immortality. How fortunate you are that you have come to love Me, for that love brings you to Me!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.82.44)

@24

= My mother binds Me, seeing Me as her son. Thinking Me utterly helpless, she fondles Me and cares for Me.

@25

= My friend, in pure friendship, climbs onto My shoulders and says, ‘What sort of big man are You? You and I are equal!’

= And when My beloved, in a mood of jealous anger (*māna*), rebukes Me, that captivates My heart more than the hymns of the Vedas.

= Neither I nor the gopīs will know this, for the beauty and qualities of each will always captivate the mind of the other.

= Hearing of the pure love of Vraja, the devotees will worship Me on the path of spontaneous love, giving up religious duties and rites.”

= “To show mercy to His devotees, He assumes a humanlike form and performs such pastimes that whoever hears of them must become wholly devoted to Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.33.36)

= The verb *bhavet*, “must become,” is in the injunctive mood (*vidhi-liṅ*). It declares this a binding duty; to neglect it is a fault.
