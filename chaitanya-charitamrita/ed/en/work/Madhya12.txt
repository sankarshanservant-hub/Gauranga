@title The Guṇḍicā Temple

@1
of Śrī Guṇḍicā  the temple  with His own  people
sweeping  by washing  He  Gaura
like His own heart  cool  bright  and
for Kṛṣṇa’s sitting  fit  made
= Together with His own devotees, Gaura swept and washed the temple of Śrī Guṇḍicā. He made it cool and radiant like His own heart, a fit place for Kṛṣṇa to be seated.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

@3
glory  glory  to Śrīvāsa and the others  Gaura’s devotees
strength  give  I may do  so that  of Caitanya  the description
= Glory, glory to Śrīvāsa and all the devotees of Gaura! Give me strength, that I may describe Śrī Caitanya.

@4
earlier  the South  from  the Lord  when  came
Him  to meet  the Gajapati  eager  became
= Earlier, when the Lord returned from the South, the Gajapati king grew eager to meet Him.

@5
Kaṭaka  from  a letter  gave  to Sārvabhauma
of the Lord  permission  is  if  to see (Him)  I shall go
= From Kaṭaka he sent a letter to Sārvabhauma: “If the Lord permits, I shall come to see Him.”

@6
the Bhaṭṭācārya  wrote  of the Lord  permission  not  was
again  the king  to him  a letter  sent
= The Bhaṭṭācārya wrote back that the Lord had not given permission. Then the king sent him another letter:

@7
of the Lord  near  are  all the  devotees
me  for the sake of  to all of them  make  a petition
= “Please petition, on my behalf, all the devotees who are with the Lord.

@8
those  all  merciful ones  to me  becoming  kind
me  for the sake of  at the Lord’s feet  will make  humble entreaty
= May all those merciful souls be kind to me and humbly plead for me at the Lord’s feet.

@9
of all of them  by the grace  one attains  Śrī  of the Lord  the feet
the Lord’s mercy  without  my  kingdom  not  pleases
= By their grace one reaches the feet of the Lord. Without the Lord’s mercy, my kingdom gives me no joy.

@10
if  to me  mercy  not  will show  Gaurahari
the kingdom  leaving  a yogī  becoming  I shall become  a beggar
= If Gaurahari will not be merciful to me, I shall give up my kingdom, become a yogī and live as a beggar.”

@11
the Bhaṭṭācārya  the letter  seeing  anxious  becoming
to the devotees  went  that  letter  taking
= Seeing the letter, the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious, and he took it to the devotees.

@12
all  meeting  he told  the king’s  account
afterwards  that  letter  to all  caused  to see
= Meeting them all, he told them about the king, and then he showed everyone the letter.

@13
the letter  seeing  of all  in the mind  arose  wonder
to the Lord’s feet  of the Gajapati  so much  devotion  there is
= Seeing the letter, everyone was amazed: “What devotion the Gajapati has for the Lord’s feet!”

@14
all  say  the Lord  him  ever  not  will meet
we all  speak  if  pain  He  will feel
= They all said, “The Lord will never meet him. And if we ask Him, He will be pained.”

@15
Sārvabhauma  says  all  go  once
to meet  not  we shall say  we shall tell  the king’s conduct
= Sārvabhauma said, “Let us all go to Him once. We will not ask Him to meet the king; we will only tell Him how the king is behaving.”

@16
so  saying  all  went  of Mahāprabhu  to the place
to speak  eager  all  not  say  in words
= Saying this, they all went to Mahāprabhu. Everyone was eager to speak, yet no one said a word.

@17
the Lord  says  what  to say  of all  the coming
I see  to say  you want  not  you speak  what  reason
= The Lord said, “What have you all come to tell Me? I see that you want to say something, yet you do not speak. Why?”

@18
Nityānanda  says  to You  we want  to submit
not  having spoken  to remain  we cannot  to speak  fear  in the heart
= Nityānanda said, “We want to put something before You. We cannot keep silent, yet our hearts are afraid to speak.

@19
proper or improper  to You  all  we want  to submit
You  not  if he meets  the king  wants  a yogī  to become
= Proper or improper, we want to tell You everything: if he cannot meet You, the king wants to become a yogī.

@20
in the ears  the earrings  taking  I  shall become  a beggar
royal enjoyment  is not  in the heart  without  Gaurahari
= He says, ‘I will put the yogī’s rings in my ears and become a beggar. Without Gaurahari, my heart finds no joy in royal pleasures.

@21
I shall see  that  moonlike face  the eyes  filling
I shall hold  those  lotus feet  to the heart  lifting
= I shall gaze on that moonlike face to my eyes’ fill, and I shall lift those lotus feet and hold them to my heart.’”

@22
although  hearing  of the Lord  soft  became  the heart
still  outwardly  He speaks  harsh  words
= Though the Lord’s heart softened when He heard this, outwardly He spoke harsh words:

@23
of you all  the desire  this  Me  taking
the king  make meet  now  to Kaṭaka  going
= “You all want to take Me to Kaṭaka and have Me meet the king.

@24
spiritual good  let it be  people  will make  blame
people  let them be  Dāmodara  will make  rebuke
= Never mind spiritual welfare — people will condemn Me. And never mind the people — Dāmodara will scold Me.

@25
of you all  at the bidding  I  not  meet  the king
Dāmodara  says  when  I meet  then  him
= I will not meet the king at the bidding of all of you. When Dāmodara tells Me to, then I shall meet him.”

@26
Dāmodara  says  You  independent  the Lord
what should and should not be done  all  to You  known
= Dāmodara said, “You are the independent Lord. You know everything — what should be done and what should not.

@27
I  what  insignificant soul  to You  rule  shall give
Yourself  You will meet  him  that also  I shall see
= What tiny soul am I, to give You rules? You will meet him of Your own accord, and that too I shall see.

@28
the king  You  affection  makes  You  controlled by affection
his  by the affection  will cause  him  Your  touch
= The king holds You in affection, and You are ruled by affection. His affection will win him Your touch.

@29
although  the Lord  You  supremely independent
still  by nature  You are  subject to love
= Though You are the Lord and supremely independent, by Your very nature You are subject to love.”

@30
Nityānanda  says  such  is  what  person
who  to You  says  ‘make  the meeting with the king’
= Nityānanda said, “Who is there who would tell You, ‘Go and meet the king’?

@31
but  of a loving  person  the nature  one  is
the beloved  not  if he gets  his own life  he  gives up
= But one who loves has this nature: if he cannot reach the one he longs for, he gives up his life.

@32
of the sacrificing brāhmaṇas the wives  all  of that  the proof
Kṛṣṇa  for the sake of  before the husband  gave up  life
= The wives of the brāhmaṇas who performed sacrifice are proof of this: for Kṛṣṇa’s sake, one of them gave up her life before her husband’s eyes.

@33
one  means  there is  if  You give  attention
You  not  even if You meet  him  remains  his  life
= There is one way, if You will hear it, by which his life may be saved even though You do not meet him.

@34
one  outer cloth  if  You give  mercy  showing
it  getting  life  he will keep  of You  hope  holding
= If You mercifully give him one of Your outer cloths, he will receive it and keep himself alive, holding on to the hope of seeing You.”

@35
the Lord  says  you all  very  learned
whatever  good  is  that  make  the arrangement
= The Lord said, “You are all very learned. Arrange whatever is best.”

@36
then  Nityānanda Gosāñi  of Govinda  from
begging  took  of the Lord  one  outer cloth
= Then Nityānanda Gosāñi asked Govinda for one of the Lord’s outer cloths and took it.

@37
that  outer cloth  to Sārvabhauma  gave
Sārvabhauma  that  cloth  to the king  sent
= He gave the cloth to Sārvabhauma, and Sārvabhauma sent it to the king.

@38
the cloth  getting  of the king  became  joyful  the mind
as the Lord’s form  regarding  does  of the cloth  worship
= Receiving the cloth, the king rejoiced, and he worshipped it as the Lord Himself.

@39
Rāmānanda  Rāya  when  the South  from  came
with the Lord  to stay  the king  he petitioned
= When Rāmānanda Rāya returned from the South, he asked the king to let him stay with the Lord.

@40
then  the king  gladly  to him  permission  gave
himself  meeting  for the sake of  to speak  began
= The king gladly gave him leave, and himself began to speak of his wish to meet the Lord:

@notes
[^2]: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.
