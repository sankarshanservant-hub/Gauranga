@title The Loving Service of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī

@1
to whom  to give  stealing  of sweet rice  the pot
Gopīnātha  Kṣīra-corā  named  became
Śrī Gopāla  appeared  conquered  being
by whose love  to that  Mādhavendra  bowed down  am I
= I bow down to Mādhavendra Purī, for whose sake Gopīnātha stole a pot of sweet rice and so became known as Kṣīra-corā, “the thief of sweet rice”, and conquered by whose love Śrī Gopāla revealed Himself.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3-4
to Nīlādri  the coming  of Jagannātha  the sight
of Sārvabhauma  Bhaṭṭācārya and the Lord  the meeting
these  all  pastimes  Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa
in detail  has described  in excellent  description
= The Lord’s coming to Nīlādri, His seeing Jagannātha, His meeting with Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya—all these pastimes Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa has described in detail, and his description is excellent.

@5
by nature  wonderful  sweet  Caitanya’s  pastimes
Vṛndāvana dāsa’s  from the mouth  of nectar  a stream
= The pastimes of Caitanya are by their very nature wonderful and sweet, and from the lips of Vṛndāvana dāsa they flow as a stream of nectar.

@6
therefore  them  if I describe  there will be  repetition
pride  showing  I describe  if  there is not  such  power
= Therefore, if I describe them again, it will only be repetition; and were I to describe them out of pride, I have no such power.

@7
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  what  he made  description
in the form of a summary  those  pastimes  I make  mention
= The pastimes he has described in the *Caitanya-maṅgala* I shall only touch upon, in brief summary.

@8
in his  summary  they are  he  not  made  description
as  best I can  making  of those  pastimes  the telling
= Pastimes he mentioned in his summary but did not describe in detail, I shall tell as best I can.

@9
therefore  at his  feet  I make  obeisance
at his  feet  offence  not  may there be  my
= Therefore I bow down at his feet, praying that I may commit no offence at his feet.

@10
in this way  Mahāprabhu  went  to Nīlācala
four  devotees  with  of Kṛṣṇa  kīrtana  joyfully
= In this way Mahāprabhu went on towards Nīlācala with four devotees, joyfully singing the kīrtana of Kṛṣṇa.

@11
alms  for  one day  to a  village  going
Himself  much  food  brought  having begged
= One day He went to a village for alms and Himself begged and brought back a great deal of food.

@12
on the way  great  great  toll-keepers  hindrance  not  make
on them  all  mercy  bestowing  He came  to Remuṇā
= On the way the many powerful toll-keepers at the crossings did not hinder Him. Bestowing His mercy on them all, He came to Remuṇā.

@13
in Remuṇā  Gopīnātha  supremely  enchanting
devotion  with  took  the Lord  His  darśana
= In Remuṇā is Gopīnātha, supremely enchanting, and with devotion the Lord took His darśana.

@14
His  lotus feet  near  obeisance  while making
His  flower crown  fell  the Lord’s  on the head
= As the Lord bowed down near His lotus feet, Gopīnātha’s crown of flowers fell onto His head.

@15
the crown  receiving  Mahāprabhu’s  delighted  mind
much  dancing and singing  did  taking along  the devotees
= Receiving the crown, Mahāprabhu was filled with joy, and with His devotees He danced and sang at length.

@16
the Lord’s  majesty  seeing  love  beauty and qualities
astonished  became  of Gopīnātha  the servants
= Seeing the Lord’s majesty, His love, His beauty and His qualities, the servants of Gopīnātha were struck with wonder.

@17
in many ways  with love  did  the Lord’s  service
that  night  there  the Lord  spent
= Lovingly they served the Lord in many ways, and that night the Lord stayed there.

@18
for the mahā-prasāda sweet rice  out of eagerness  stayed  the Lord  there
before  Īśvara Purī  to Him  had told  the story
= The Lord stayed there out of eagerness for the mahā-prasāda of sweet rice, for Īśvara Purī had once told Him its story.

@19
Kṣīra-corā  Gopīnātha  famous  His  name
to the devotees  tells  the Lord  that  very  story
= His famous name is “Kṣīra-corā Gopīnātha”, and the Lord told the devotees the story behind it.

@20
formerly  Mādhava Purī  for the sake of  sweet rice  did  theft
therefore  the name  became  Kṣīra-corā Hari
= Long ago He stole sweet rice for Mādhava Purī, and so He came to be called “Kṣīra-corā Hari”, Hari the thief of sweet rice.

@21
formerly  Śrī Mādhava Purī  came  to Vṛndāvana
wandering  wandering  went  where  Govardhana
= Once Śrī Mādhava Purī came to Vṛndāvana, and wandering on and on, he reached Govardhana.

@22
with love  maddened  not  his  of night and day  awareness
now  rises  now  falls  no  sense of place
= Mad with love, he could not tell night from day. Now he would rise, now he would fall, with no thought of where he was.

@23
the hill  circumambulation  making  to Govinda-kuṇḍa  coming
bath  taking  beneath a tree  was  in the evening  seated
= After walking around the hill, he came to Govinda-kuṇḍa and bathed there, and in the evening he sat beneath a tree.

@24
a cowherd boy  one  milk  pot  carrying
coming  before him  holding it  something  said  smiling
= A cowherd boy came carrying a pot of milk. Holding it out before him, He smiled and said:

@25
Purī  this  milk  taking  do  you  drinking
having begged  why  not  you eat  what  do you  meditate on
= “Purī, take this milk and drink it. Why do you not beg for food and eat? What are you meditating on?”

@26
the boy’s  by the beauty  Purī’s  became  delight
His  by the sweet words  went away  hunger and thirst
= Purī was delighted by the boy’s beauty, and at His sweet words his hunger and thirst vanished.

@27
Purī  said  who  You  where  Your  dwelling
how  did You know  I  keep  a fast
= Purī said, “Who are You? Where do You live? How did You know that I was fasting?”

@28
the boy  said  a cowherd  I  in this  village  live
My  in the village  anyone  not  remains  fasting
= The boy said, “I am a cowherd and live in this village. In My village no one goes hungry.

@29
some  food  begging  eat  some  live on milk
to one who does not beg  I  give  indeed  food
= Some beg for food and eat, and some live on milk; but to one who does not beg I Myself give food.

@30
water  to fetch  the women  you  seeing  went
the women  milk  giving  Me  sent
= The women who came to fetch water saw you, and they have given Me milk and sent Me to you.

@31
cow-milking  to do  I want  quickly  I  shall go
again  coming  I  this  pot  shall take
= I must go now to milk the cows, but I will come back and take this pot.”

@32
this  saying  went  the boy  not  was seen  any more
Mādhava Purī’s  in the heart  arose  wonder
= Saying this, the boy went away and was seen no more, and Mādhava Purī’s heart was filled with wonder.

@33
milk  drinking  having done  the pot  washing  set aside
the path  he watches  that  boy  again  not  came
= He drank the milk, washed the pot and set it aside. He kept watching the path, but the boy did not come back.

@34
sitting  the Name  takes  Purī  no  sleep  comes
at the end  of the night  a trance  came  of the outward senses  the stilling
= Purī sat chanting the Name, and no sleep came to him. At the end of the night a trance came over him, and his outward senses were stilled.

@35
in a dream  he sees  that  boy  before him  coming
to a  grove  taking  went  by the hand  holding
= In a dream he saw that same boy come before him, take him by the hand and lead him into a grove.

@36
the grove  showing  He says  I  in this  grove  stay
from cold, rain, wind and heat  great suffering  I get
= Showing him the grove, He said, “I stay in this grove, and I suffer greatly from cold and rain, wind and heat.

@37
of the village  the people  bringing  Me  take out  the grove  from
on top of the hill  taking  keep  properly
= Bring the people of the village and take Me out of the grove. Carry Me up the hill and keep Me there properly.

@38
a  temple  making  there  do  installation
with much  cool  water  do  of the holy body  bathing
= Build a temple there and install Me in it, and bathe My body with plenty of cool water.

@39
for many days  your  path  I do  watching
when  coming  Mādhava  Me  will do  service
= For many days I have been watching the path for you, wondering when Mādhava would come and serve Me.

@40
your  conquered by love  making  of service  acceptance
darśana  giving  I shall deliver  all  the world
= Conquered by your love, I shall accept your service, and by giving My darśana I shall deliver the whole world.

@41
Śrī Gopāla  name  My  the Holder of Govardhana
by Vajra  installed  I  here  the master
= My name is Śrī Gopāla; I am the Holder of Govardhana. I was installed by Vajra, and I am the master here.

@42
the top of the hill  from  Me  in the grove  hiding
of the mlecchas  out of fear  servant  My  went  fleeing
= In fear of the *mlecchas* My servant brought Me down from the top of the hill, hid Me in this grove and fled.

@43
that  since  stay  I  in this  grove
good  have come  you  Me  take out  carefully
= Since then I have stayed in this grove. It is good that you have come; take Me out carefully.”

@44
this  saying  that  boy  disappeared
waking  Mādhava Purī  reflection  made
= Saying this, the boy disappeared. Mādhava Purī woke up and began to reflect.

@45
Śrī Kṛṣṇa  saw  I  could not  recognise
this  saying  in the rapture of love  fell  to the ground
= “I saw Śrī Kṛṣṇa, but I could not recognise Him!” Saying this, he fell to the ground, overwhelmed with love.

@46
for a while  weeping  doing  mind  made  steady
the order’s fulfilment  for the sake of  became  composed
= He wept for a while, then steadied his mind and became composed in order to carry out the command.

@47
morning bath  taking  Purī  into the village  went
all  people  together  gathering  to speak  began
= After his morning bath Purī went into the village, gathered all the people together and said:

@48
of the village  the Lord  your  the Holder of Govardhana
in the grove  He is  come  Him  out  then  let us bring
= “The Lord of your village, the Holder of Govardhana, is in the grove. Come, let us bring Him out.

@49
axes  spades  take  a way  to make
very  dense  the grove  we cannot  enter
= Take axes and spades to clear a way, for the grove is so dense that we cannot get in.”

@50
hearing  the people  him  with  went  joyfully
the grove  cutting  a way  making  made  entry
= Hearing this, the people joyfully went with him. Cutting through the thicket, they made a way and entered.

@51
the Deity  they saw  with earth and grass  covered
seeing  all  people  became  with joy  amazed
= They found the Deity covered with earth and grass, and at the sight all the people were amazed and overjoyed.

@52
the covering  away  putting  made  visible
very heavy  the Deity  no one  could  move
= They cleared away the covering and the Deity came into view. He was immensely heavy, and no one could move Him.

@53
very very strong  men  together  gathering
to the top of the hill  went  Purī  the Deity  taking
= Gathering the very strongest men, Purī took the Deity up to the top of the hill.

@54
of stone  on a throne  the Deity  they seated
big  one  stone  behind  as support  they gave
= They seated the Deity on a throne of stone, and set a large stone behind Him for support.

@55
of the village  the brāhmaṇas  all  new  pots  taking
of Govinda-kuṇḍa  water  brought  straining
= All the brāhmaṇas of the village took new pots and brought water from Govinda-kuṇḍa, straining it.

@56
nine  hundred pots  of water  made  brought
various  instruments and drums  sound  the women  sing  songs
= Nine hundred pots of water were brought. Instruments and drums of every kind resounded, and the women sang.

@57
some  sing  some  dance  a great festival  there was
yogurt  milk  ghee  came  in the village  as much as  there was
= Some sang and some danced; it became a great festival. All the yogurt, milk and ghee in the village was brought.

@58
foodstuffs for the offering  came  sandeśa and the rest  all
various  gifts  them  to describe  can I  how many
= Foodstuffs for the offering came, *sandeśa* and every other sweet, and gifts of so many kinds that I cannot describe them all.

@59
tulasī  and so on  flowers  cloth  came  in abundance
himself  Mādhava Purī  performed  the abhiṣeka
= Tulasī, flowers and cloth came in abundance, and Mādhava Purī himself performed the bathing ceremony (*abhiṣeka*).

@60
the dirt of the body  away  removing  had Him  bathed
much  oil  applying  made  the holy body  glossy
= Removing the dirt from His body, he bathed Him, and with plenty of oil he made the holy body glisten.
