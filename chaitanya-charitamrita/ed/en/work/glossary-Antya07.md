# English names and terms — CC Antya 7, verses 1–85 (stage A, part 1; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 7»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 7») are used as given there: Vallabha Bhaṭṭa, the Bhaṭṭa;
Dāmodara Svarūpa (order as in the edition, 7.38); *bhāva* joined with the knowledge of majesty / *kevala*, pure *bhāva*;
the crown jewel of the *puruṣārthas*; *ātma-bhūta*; long-standing pride (*dīrgha garva*), humbled (*kharva*); *rūḍhi*;
*phalgu* (worthless); *Nāma-kaumudī*. Verses 2, 15, 27, 29, 32–34, 40–41, 43 — word for word as in the revised
Ādi 1.18, 3.27, 4.173, 4.180, Madhya 8.75, 8.77, 8.80, 8.226, 19.204, 19.210 (`ed/en-rev/`).
