# Verse 139 (= Antya 2.172) — word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev.

@71
quickly  going  to Nīlācala  went  Jagadānanda
the devotees  with  the Lord  became  supremely  joyful
= Jagadānanda went quickly back to Nīlācala, and the Lord, together with the devotees, was overjoyed.

@72
the Lord’s  feet  honouring  with all  he met
Mahāprabhu  him  tight  embrace  gave
= He bowed to the Lord’s feet and met everyone, and Mahāprabhu embraced him tightly.

@73
Sanātana’s  in the name  the Paṇḍita  obeisance  made
of the place of the rāsa  dust  and so on  all  gifts  gave
= In Sanātana’s name the Paṇḍita offered obeisance to the Lord, and he presented all the gifts—dust from the place of the *rāsa* dance and the rest.

@74
all  the things  He kept,  the pīlu  He gave  distributing
of Vṛndāvana  fruit  saying  they ate  delighted  becoming
= The Lord kept all the gifts but distributed the *pīlu* fruits, and everyone ate them with delight, calling them “the fruit of Vṛndāvana.”

@75
who  anyone  knew,  the stones  to suck  began
who  not  knew,  the Gauḍīyās,  the pīlu  chewing  ate
= Those who knew the fruit began to suck the stones, but the Gauḍīyās who did not know it chewed the *pīlu* and swallowed it.

@76
in the mouth  their  pungency  went,  the tongue  does  burning
of Vṛndāvana  pīlu  in eating  this  one  līlā
= Their mouths were stung with pungency, and their tongues burned. Such was one *līlā* in the eating of the *pīlu* of Vṛndāvana.

@77
Jagadānanda’s  at the coming  of all  the joy
in this way  at Nīlācala  the Lord’s  pastimes
= Everyone rejoiced at Jagadānanda’s return. Thus the Lord enjoyed His pastimes at Nīlācala.

@78
one day  the Lord  to Yameśvara-ṭoṭā  while going
at that  time  a devadāsī  began  to sing
= One day, as the Lord was going to Yameśvara-ṭoṭā, a *devadāsī* began to sing.

@79
the Gurjarī  rāgiṇī  taking  in a very sweet  voice
of the Gīta-govinda  a song  she sings  of the world  the mind  steals
= In a very sweet voice she was singing a song of the *Gīta-govinda* in the Gurjarī *rāgiṇī*, a song that steals the mind of the world.

@80
from afar  the song  hearing  the Lord’s  arose  absorption
woman,  man,  who  sings  not  knowing  specifically
= Hearing the song from afar, the Lord became absorbed in ecstasy, not knowing at all who was singing, a woman or a man.

@81
the singer  to meet  the Lord  in ecstasy  ran
on the way  sij  hedge  He takes,  piercing through  He went
= In His ecstasy the Lord ran to meet the singer. On the way He went straight into a hedge of *sij* shrubs and forced His way through it.

@82
in the body  thorns  pierced,  anything  not  He knew
in great haste  Govinda  Him  behind  ran
= Thorns pierced His body, but He knew nothing of it! In great haste Govinda ran after Him.

@83
running  goes  the Lord,  the woman  is  a little  away
a woman’s  song  saying  Govinda  the Lord  took  in his arms
= The Lord was running on, and the woman was only a little way off, when Govinda caught the Lord in his arms, crying, “It is a woman singing!”

@84
woman  the word  hearing  the Lord’s  external awareness  came
again  by that  way  turning back  went
= Hearing the word “woman,” the Lord returned to external consciousness, and He turned back and went the same way.

@85
the Lord  says  Govinda,  today  you saved  life
woman’s  touch  if it had been  My  would have been  death
= The Lord said, “Govinda, today you have saved My life. Had I touched a woman, it would have been My death.

@86
this  debt  to repay  I  shall not be able  to you
Govinda  says  Jagannātha  protects  I  what  insignificant one
= I shall never be able to repay this debt to you.” Govinda said, “It is Jagannātha who protects. What am I, insignificant as I am?”

@87
the Lord  says  Govinda,  Me  with  stay
wherever  there  My  in protecting  careful  be
= The Lord said, “Govinda, stay with Me. Wherever I am, be careful to protect Me.”

@88
this  saying  turning back  the Lord  went  to His own  place
hearing  great  fear  felt  Svarūpa and others  in mind
= Saying this, the Lord turned back and went to His residence. Hearing of it, Svarūpa and the others were greatly alarmed at heart.

@89
meanwhile  Tapana  Miśra’s  son  Raghunātha  Bhaṭṭācārya
the Lord  to see  set out  leaving  all  affairs
= Meanwhile Raghunātha Bhaṭṭācārya, the son of Tapana Miśra, left all his affairs and set out to see the Lord.

@90
Kāśī  from  set out  he  of Gauḍa  the road  by
with him  a servant  goes  his  bag  carrying
= He set out from Kāśī by the road through Gauḍa, and a servant went with him, carrying his bag.

@91
on the way  him  met  Viśvāsa  Rāmadāsa
of the Viśvāsa-khānā  a kāyastha  he  of the king  trusted officer
= On the way he was met by Rāmadāsa Viśvāsa, a *kāyastha* of the *Viśvāsa-khānā* and a trusted officer of the king.

@92
in all  scriptures  expert,  of the Kāvya-prakāśa  teacher
a great  Vaiṣṇava,  of Raghunātha  worshipper
= He was expert in all the scriptures and a teacher of the *Kāvya-prakāśa*, a great Vaiṣṇava, a worshipper of Raghunātha (Rāma).

@93
eight  praharas  Rāma’s  name  he chants  night  and day
all  giving up  he set out  Jagannātha  to see
= Night and day, through all eight *praharas*, he chanted the name of Rāma. Giving up everything, he had set out to see Jagannātha.

@94
Raghunātha  Bhaṭṭa  with  on the way  he met
the Bhaṭṭa’s  bag  on his head  putting  carrying  went
= He met Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa on the way, and he went along carrying the Bhaṭṭa’s bag on his head.

@95
various  services  doing  does  feet  massaging
by that  Raghunātha’s  becomes  embarrassed  mind
= He rendered various services and massaged his feet, and this made Raghunātha uneasy at heart.

@96
you  a great  man,  a paṇḍita,  a great  bhāgavata
service  not  do,  happily  walk  me  with
= “You are a great man, a *paṇḍita*, a *mahā-bhāgavata*. Do not serve me; just walk happily with me.”

@97
Rāmadāsa  says  I  a śūdra  lowly
of the brāhmaṇa  service  this  my  own  dharma
= Rāmadāsa said, “I am a lowly *śūdra*! To serve a *brāhmaṇa* is my own *dharma*.

@98
hesitation  not  do  you,  I  your  servant
your  service  doing  there is  in the heart  joy
= Do not feel uneasy: I am your servant. When I serve you, my heart is filled with joy.”

@99
this  saying  the bag  he carries,  does  service
Raghunātha’s  delivering  mantra  he chants  night  and day
= Saying this, he carried the bag and served him, and night and day he chanted the *tāraka-mantra* of Raghunātha (Rāma), the mantra that delivers.

@100
in this way  Raghunātha  came  to Nīlācala
the Lord’s  at the feet  going  met  with eager joy
= In this way Raghunātha came to Nīlācala; he went to the Lord’s feet and met Him with eager joy.

@101
rod-like  obeisance  making  the Bhaṭṭa  fell  at the feet
the Lord  Raghunātha  saying  did  embrace
= Offering obeisance like a rod, the Bhaṭṭa fell at His feet, and the Lord, calling “Raghunātha!”, embraced him.

@102
of Miśra  and  Śekhara  the obeisances  he conveyed
Mahāprabhu  of them  all  news  asked
= He conveyed the obeisances of Miśra and Śekhara, and Mahāprabhu asked for news of them all.

@103
good  it is  you came,  see  the lotus-eyed (Jagannātha)
today  My  here  you will do  prasāda  eating
= “It is good that you have come. Go and see the lotus-eyed Lord (Jagannātha), and today you will take *prasāda* here with Me.”

@104
to Govinda  telling  a  lodging  had given
Svarūpa and others  devotees  with  He introduced
= Through Govinda the Lord had a lodging given to him, and He introduced him to Svarūpa and the other devotees.

@105
in this way  the Lord  with  stayed  eight  months
day  by day  the Lord’s  by the mercy  increases  joy
= In this way he stayed with the Lord for eight months, and by the Lord’s mercy his joy increased day by day.

@106
from time  to time  Mahāprabhu’s  he makes  invitation
home-cooked  rice  he makes,  and  various  vegetable dishes
= From time to time he invited Mahāprabhu, cooking rice at home and various vegetable dishes.

@107
Raghunātha  Bhaṭṭa  in cooking  very  expert
whatever  he cooks,  that  becomes  nectar  like
= Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa was highly expert in cooking; whatever he cooked became like nectar.

@108
with great  satisfaction  the Lord  does  eating
the Lord’s  remnants  plate  the Bhaṭṭa’s  eating
= With great satisfaction the Lord would eat, and the Bhaṭṭa would eat the remnants on the Lord’s plate.

@109
Rāmadāsa  when  first  the Lord  met
Mahāprabhu  much  to him  mercy  not  showed
= When Rāmadāsa first met the Lord, Mahāprabhu did not show him much mercy.

@110
at heart  desiring liberation  he,  of learning  proud
all  hearts  knower  the Lord  omniscient  Bhagavān
= At heart he desired liberation, and he was proud of his learning. The Lord knows every heart: He is Bhagavān, who knows all.

@111
Rāmadāsa  made  then  at Nīlācala  residence
to the Paṭṭanāyaka  family  teaches  Kāvya-prakāśa
= Rāmadāsa then settled at Nīlācala and taught the *Kāvya-prakāśa* to the Paṭṭanāyaka family.

@112
eight  months  staying  the Lord  to the Bhaṭṭa  farewell  gave
marriage  not  make  saying  forbade
= When he had stayed eight months, the Lord bade the Bhaṭṭa farewell and forbade him, saying, “Do not marry.

@113
aged  mother  father’s  going  do  service
a Vaiṣṇava  from  the Bhāgavata  do  study
= Go and serve your aged mother and father, and study the *Bhāgavata* under a Vaiṣṇava.

@114
again  once more  come  to Nīlācala
this  saying  neck  garland  gave  his  on the neck
= Come to Nīlācala once more.” Saying this, the Lord placed the garland from His own neck around his neck.

@115
embrace  making  the Lord  farewell  to him  gave
with love  overflowing  the Bhaṭṭa  to weep  began
= Embracing him, the Lord bade him farewell, and the Bhaṭṭa, overflowing with love, began to weep.

@116
Svarūpa  and others  devotees  from  leave  asking
to Vārāṇasī  came  the Bhaṭṭa  the Lord’s  permission  receiving
= Asking leave of Svarūpa and the other devotees, and having received the Lord’s permission, the Bhaṭṭa came to Vārāṇasī.

@117
four  years  at home  father  mother’s  service  did
a Vaiṣṇava  paṇḍita  from  the Bhāgavata  studied
= For four years he served his father and mother at home, and he studied the *Bhāgavata* under a Vaiṣṇava *paṇḍita*.

@118
father  mother  Kāśī  when they attained  detached  becoming
again  the Lord’s  to  came  home and all  leaving
= When his father and mother departed this world at Kāśī, he became detached, left home and all, and came to the Lord again.

@119
as before  eight  months  the Lord  near  he stayed
eight  months  staying  again  the Lord  order  gave
= As before, he stayed with the Lord for eight months, and when the eight months had passed, the Lord again gave him His order:

@120
My  by order,  Raghunātha,  go  to Vṛndāvana
there  going  stay  Rūpa  Sanātana  with
= “Raghunātha, by My order go to Vṛndāvana. Go there and stay with Rūpa and Sanātana.

@121
the Bhāgavata  read,  always  take  Kṛṣṇa’s  name
soon  will show  mercy  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān
= Read the *Bhāgavata* and always take the name of Kṛṣṇa. Very soon Kṛṣṇa, Bhagavān, will bestow His mercy on you.”

@122
this  saying  the Lord  him  embrace  gave
the Lord’s  by the mercy  for Kṛṣṇa  with love  intoxicated  became
= Saying this, the Lord embraced him, and by the Lord’s mercy he became intoxicated with love for Kṛṣṇa.

@123
fourteen  cubits  Jagannātha’s  of tulasī  mālā
plain  betel  roll  at a festival  had received
= At a festival the Lord had received Jagannātha’s *tulasī-mālā*, fourteen cubits long, and a plain roll of betel (*chuṭā-pāna-viḍā*).

@124
that  mālā,  plain  betel  the Lord  to him  gave
worshipful  Deity  considering  the mālā  holding  kept
= The Lord gave him that *mālā* and the plain betel, and he kept the *mālā*, regarding it as his worshipful Deity (*iṣṭa-deva*).

@125
the Lord  from  permission  taking  went  to Vṛndāvana
shelter  took  coming  in Rūpa  Sanātana
= Taking the Lord’s permission, he went to Vṛndāvana, and on arriving there he took shelter of Rūpa and Sanātana.

@126
Rūpa  Gosvāmī’s  in the assembly  does  Bhāgavata  reading
the Bhāgavata  while reading  with love  is overwhelmed  his  mind
= In Rūpa Gosvāmī’s assembly he would recite the *Bhāgavata*, and as he read, his mind would lose its hold, overwhelmed with love.

@127
tears,  trembling,  faltering voice  the Lord’s  by the mercy
the eyes  blocking  does  tears,  not  he is able  to read
= By the Lord’s mercy, tears, trembling and a faltering voice would come; tears would veil his eyes, and he could not go on reading.

@128
cuckoo’s  voice  throat,  in it  of rāgas  variety
one  verse  while reading  he changes  three  four  rāgas
= His voice was like the cuckoo’s, and in it lay many *rāgas*: reading a single verse, he would pass through three or four *rāgas*.

@129
Kṛṣṇa’s  beauty  sweetness  when  he reads,  hears
with love  overwhelmed  then,  anything  not  he knows
= When he read or heard of Kṛṣṇa’s beauty and sweetness, he was overwhelmed with love and knew nothing else.

@130
Govinda’s  at the feet  made  self  surrender
Govinda’s  feet  lotus  whose  life’s  treasure
= He surrendered himself at the feet of Govinda: Govinda’s lotus feet were the treasure of his life.

@131
his own  disciple  telling  Govinda’s  temple  had built
flute,  makara  earrings and so on  as ornaments  making  gave
= Through his own disciple he had a temple built for Govinda, and he gave the Deity a flute, *makara*-shaped earrings and other ornaments.

@132
village  talk  not  he hears,  not  speaks  with the tongue
of Kṛṣṇa  in talks  worship and so on  eight  praharas  pass
= He would not listen to worldly talk, nor speak it with his tongue. All the eight *praharas* of his day passed in talks of Kṛṣṇa, worship and the like.

@133
of Vaiṣṇavas  blameworthy  deeds  not  he lets fall  into the ears
all  Kṛṣṇa  worship  do  this  only  he knows
= He would not let the blameworthy deeds of a Vaiṣṇava reach his ears. “Everyone serves Kṛṣṇa”—this alone he knew.

@134
by Mahāprabhu  given  the mālā  of remembrance  at the time
prasāda  kaḍāra  together with  tying  puts  on the neck
= At the time of remembrance (*smaraṇa*) he would tie around his neck the *mālā* given by Mahāprabhu, together with the *prasāda*.

@135
Mahāprabhu’s  by the mercy  for Kṛṣṇa  love  unrestrained
this  indeed  I have told  in it  Caitanya’s  mercy  fruit
= By Mahāprabhu’s mercy his love for Kṛṣṇa flowed unrestrained. This I have told; in it is the fruit of Caitanya’s mercy.

@136-137
Jagadānanda’s  I have told  to Vṛndāvana  going
that  within  the devadāsī’s  song  hearing
Mahāprabhu’s  on Raghunātha  mercy  great  fruit
in one  chapter  three  topics  I have told  all
= I have told of Jagadānanda’s journey to Vṛndāvana, and in the midst of it, of the Lord’s hearing the *devadāsī*’s song, and of the great fruit of Mahāprabhu’s mercy upon Raghunātha. Thus in one chapter I have told all three topics.

@138
who  these  all  narrations  hears  faith  with
to him  Kṛṣṇa  love  wealth  gives  Gaurahari
= Whoever hears all these narrations with faith, Gaurahari gives him the wealth of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@139
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
