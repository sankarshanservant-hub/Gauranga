# English names and terms — CC Antya 19 (stage A; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 19»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` and in the revised Antya 14–17 are used as given there: *pralāpa* (raving, ravings;
RU «бессвязные речи»); divine madness (RU «божественное безумие»); Svarūpa / Svarūpa Gosāñi (as in the text; RU «Сварупа Дамодара»);
Rāmānanda Rāya; Gambhīrā; Madana-mohana (RU им. п. «Мадана-мохан»); *udghūrṇā*; *madayati* in the refrain of the
*Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa* — “makes me mad with joy” (RU «сводит меня с ума от счастья»).
Names and terms as in the commentaries on this chapter (`ed/en/Antya19-apb.md`, `-anu.md`; glossary «Антья 19», parts 1–2):
*tarjā* (*tarjā-prahelī*), *bāul* / *āul*, invoking / keeping present / sending away the Deity (26–27), the garden of Jagannātha-vallabha,
*seyākul*, *kumudinī* (night-blooming lotus), *kāma* of the *gopīs* — “loving desire” (not “lust”), *dattāpahāra*, the *Bhramara-gītā*.
Verbatim from revised chapters (`ed/en-rev/`): 2 (Ādi 1.18), 75 (Antya 14.72), 112 (Ādi 1.110); 105 as Madhya 23.36; line 1 of 77 (Antya 15.4, 17.3).
