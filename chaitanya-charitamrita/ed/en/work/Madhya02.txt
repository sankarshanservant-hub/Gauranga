@title The Divine Madness of Śrī Caitanya

@1
in chapter  this  of the Lord  final
pastimes  sūtras  in describing
of Gaura  from Kṛṣṇa  in separation
raving and the rest  is described
= In this chapter, while describing the *sūtras* of the Lord’s final pastimes, I describe Gaura’s raving (*pralāpa*) and other states in separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
the last  which  remained  of the Lord  twelve  years
of Kṛṣṇa  separation  the feeling  was  constant
= In the last twelve years that remained to the Lord, He felt separation from Kṛṣṇa constantly.

@4
of Śrī Rādhikā  the behaviour  as  on seeing Uddhava
such  a state  of the Lord  was  night and day
= As Śrī Rādhikā behaved when She saw Uddhava, so was the Lord’s state night and day.

@5
constant  was  of the Lord  separation  madness
bewildered  actions  always  raving  speech
= The Lord was constantly in the divine madness of separation: His actions were always bewildered, and His speech was raving.

@6
from the pores  blood oozing  teeth  all  loosened
at one moment  the body  thin  becomes  at another  the body  swells
= Blood oozed from His pores, and all His teeth shook. At one moment His body grew thin, at another it swelled.

@7
within the Gambhīrā  at night  not  of sleep  a particle
against the wall  face and head  rubs  wounds  become  all over
= Inside the Gambhīrā He did not sleep a moment at night. He rubbed His face and head against the wall, and they were covered with wounds.

@8
on three  doors  bolts  the Lord  goes  outside
sometimes  at the Lion Gate  falls  sometimes  in the waters of the sea
= Though the three doors were bolted, the Lord would go outside. Sometimes He fell at the Lion Gate, sometimes into the waters of the sea.

@9
Caṭaka  hill  seeing  for Govardhana  mistaking
running  goes  cries of anguish  making  weeping
= Seeing the Caṭaka hill, He mistook it for Govardhana, and ran towards it, weeping and crying out in anguish.

@10
groves and gardens  seeing  as Vṛndāvana  conceiving
there  going  dances  sings  at times  unconscious  falls
= Seeing the groves and gardens, He thought they were Vṛndāvana. He would go there to dance and sing, and at times He fell unconscious.

@11
nowhere  not  heard  which  of ecstasy  transformations
those  ecstasies  became  in the Lord’s  body  manifest
= Transformations of ecstasy never heard of anywhere became manifest in the Lord’s body.

@12
of hands and feet  the joints  all  a span  in length
joints  leaving  separate  became  the skin  remained  in place
= All the joints of His arms and legs came apart by a span; they left their sockets, and only the skin held them in place.

@13
hands  feet  head  all  the body  within
withdrawn  became  a tortoise’s form  was seen  in the Lord
= His hands, feet and head all withdrew into His body, and the Lord was seen in the form of a tortoise.

@14
in this way  wondrous  ecstasies  in the body  manifest
in the mind  emptiness  in words  alas  despair
= Thus wondrous ecstasies appeared in His body. His mind was empty, and His words were full of lament and despair:

@15
where  My  of life  Lord  with the flute at His lips
what  shall I do  where  shall I find  the son of the king of Vraja
= “Where is the Lord of My life, with the flute at His lips? What shall I do? Where shall I find the son of the king of Vraja?

@16
to whom  shall I tell  who  knows  My  sorrow
the son of the king of Vraja  without  bursts  My  heart
= To whom can I speak? Who knows My sorrow? Without the son of the king of Vraja My heart is breaking.”

@17
thus  lamentation  makes  bewildered  at heart
of Rāya  from the drama  verses  recites  constantly
= Thus He lamented, bewildered at heart, and constantly recited verses from Rāya’s drama:

@18
love’s  breaking  the pains  knows  Hari  not  this  not  and  love  either
proper and improper place  knows  nor even  Madana  knows  us  weak
another  knows  not  and  another’s  sorrow  all  our  life  is  not obedient
two or three  only  days  youth  this  alas  alas  of the Creator  what  is the way
= [Śrī Rādhā laments:] “This Hari does not know the pains we suffer when love is broken; nor does love know where it should and should not strike; nor does even Madana know how weak we are. No one knows another’s sorrow in full; our very life is not in our control, and this youth lasts only two or three days. Alas, alas, O Creator, what is to become of us?” (Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka 3.9)

@19
sprouted  of love the sprout  broke (it)  that  of sorrow  flood
Kṛṣṇa  it  not  does  drink
outwardly  king of lovers  inwardly  of a cheat  the work
others’ women  in killing  intent
= “In My heart the sprout of love came up, but a flood of sorrow broke it, and Kṛṣṇa does not drink from it. Outwardly He is the king of lovers, but within He does the work of a cheat, intent on killing other men’s wives.

@20
O friend  not  I understand  the Creator’s  ordinance
happiness  for the sake of  I loved  came out  contrary  the course
now  goes  not  stays  life
= O friend, I do not understand the Creator’s ordinance. I loved for the sake of happiness, but everything turned out the opposite. Now life is leaving Me; it will not stay.

@21
crooked  love  senseless  not  knows  proper and improper place
good and bad  cannot  consider
of the cruel  cheat  with the rope of qualities  hands and neck  binding  Me
keeps  unable  to get free
= Love is crooked and senseless: it does not know where it should strike and where not, nor can it consider what is good and bad. With the rope of the qualities of that cruel cheat it has bound My hands and neck, and I cannot get free.

@22
that  Madana  bodiless  in harming others  expert
five  arrows  fixes  constantly
of a helpless woman  into the body  piercing  has made  riddled
pain  gives  not  takes  life
= That bodiless Madana, so skilled in harming others, constantly fixes his five arrows to the bow. He has pierced the body of a helpless woman through and through: he gives her pain but does not take her life.

@23
of another  which  sorrow  in the heart  another  that  not  knows
true  this  of scripture  the verdict
of other  people  why  write  not  knows  the friend dear as life
since  says  patience  to hold
= What sorrow lies in another’s heart, no one else knows: this verdict of scripture is true. Why speak of others? Even My friend, dear to Me as life, does not know it, for she only tells Me to be patient.

@24
Kṛṣṇa  of mercy  the ocean  some day  will accept
friend  your  these  useless  words
of the living being  the life  unsteady  like  on a lotus leaf  water
so many  days  will live  which  person
= ‘Kṛṣṇa is an ocean of mercy; some day He will accept You’ — friend, these words of yours are useless. A living being’s life is as unsteady as water on a lotus leaf: who will live so many days?

@25
a hundred  years  up to  of the living being  life’s  end
this  statement  you make  not  considering
of a woman  youth’s  wealth  on which  Kṛṣṇa  sets  His mind
that  youth  days  two or four
= You say without thinking that a living being’s life lasts a hundred years. But the wealth of a woman’s youth, on which Kṛṣṇa sets His mind, lasts only two or four days.

@26
fire  as  its own  light  showing  charming
the moth  attracting  kills
Kṛṣṇa  so  His own  qualities  showing  steals  the mind
afterwards  of sorrow  into the ocean  casts
= As fire shows its charming light, draws the moth and kills it, so Kṛṣṇa shows His qualities and steals the heart, and then casts it into an ocean of sorrow.”

@27
so much  lament  making  in dejection  Śrī Gaurahari
opening  of sorrow  the door
of ecstasy  of the waves  by the force  in many ways  the mind  moves
another  one  verse  made  recitation
= Lamenting so much in dejection, Śrī Gaurahari opened the door of His sorrow. Driven by the force of the waves of ecstasy, His mind moved in many ways, and He recited another verse:

@28
of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  form and the rest  service  without
useless  My  days  all senses  utterly
of stone  dry wood  burdens  alas
I bear  how  them  shameless
= “Without serving Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s form and the rest, all My days and all My senses are utterly useless. Alas, how do I, shameless, go on bearing these senses, burdens of stone and dry wood?

@29
of the flute song’s nectar  abode  of beauty’s nectar  birthplace
who  not  sees  that moon-like  face
of those  eyes  what  use  let fall  his  on the head  a thunderbolt
those  eyes  remain  for what  reason
= That moon-like face is the abode of the nectar of His flute song and the birthplace of the nectar of beauty. What use are the eyes of one who does not see it? Let a thunderbolt fall on his head! Why do such eyes remain?

@30
O friend  hear  My  struck  destiny’s power
My  body heart mind  all  senses
Kṛṣṇa  without  all  fruitless
= O friend, hear Me: My fortune is struck down. My body, heart and mind, and all My senses — without Kṛṣṇa all are fruitless.

@31
Kṛṣṇa’s  sweet  words  of nectar  a river
their  entry  is not  into which  ears
of a holed cowrie shell  a hole  like  know  those  ears
their  birth  was  in vain
= Kṛṣṇa’s sweet words are a river of nectar. Know that the ears they do not enter are like the hole in a broken cowrie shell: their birth was in vain.

@32
of Kṛṣṇa  the nectar of the lips  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  and deeds
of the essence of nectar  the taste  surpassing
their  taste  who  not  knows  being born  not  died  why
that  tongue  a frog’s tongue  like
= The nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips, and Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and deeds, surpass in taste the very essence of nectar. Why did one who does not know their taste not die at birth? That tongue is like a frog’s tongue.

@33
of musk  and the blue lotus  in mingling  which  fragrance
that  steals  its  pride
such  Kṛṣṇa’s  body’s  fragrance  whose  not  that  connection
that  nose  a bellows  like
= The fragrance of Kṛṣṇa’s body steals the pride even of the scent born from mingling musk and the blue lotus. The nose that has no connection with that fragrance is like a bellows.

@34
Kṛṣṇa’s  hands  and feet  the palms and soles  than millions of moons  cooler
their  touch  like  a touchstone
their  touch  not  whose  he  let go  to ruin
that  body  like iron  I know
= The palms of Kṛṣṇa’s hands and the soles of His feet are cooler than millions of moons, and their touch is like a touchstone. Let one who has not known that touch go to ruin: I know his body is like iron.”

@35
making  so much  lament  the Lord  Śrī Śacīnandana
opening  of the heart  the grief
in humility  despondency  dejection  of the heart  in exhaustion
again  recites  one  verse
= Lamenting so much, the Lord, Śrī Śacīnandana, laid open the grief of His heart. In humility, despondency and dejection, His heart exhausted, He recited another verse:

@36
when  came  by fortune  the enemy of Madhu  this  of the eyes  on the path
then  our  mind  by wretched Madana  stolen  was
again  in which  He  a moment  even  of the eyes  comes  on the path
we shall make  in it  all the ghaṭikās  with jewels  studded
= “When by good fortune Madhu’s enemy came within our sight, our mind was stolen by that wretched Madana. When He comes again into our sight even for a moment, we shall stud all the *ghaṭikās* of that time with jewels.” (Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka 3.11)

@37
at the time  or  as in a dream  I saw  the one with the flute at His lips
at that  time  came  two  enemies
bliss  and  Madana  stealing  took  My  mind
to see  not  I could  eyes  filling
= “At the very time I saw Him with the flute at His lips, as if in a dream, two enemies came — Bliss and Madana. They stole My mind, and I could not see Him to My eyes’ content.

@38
again  if  some  moment  grants  Kṛṣṇa’s  sight
then  those  ghaṭīs  kṣaṇas  and palas
giving  garlands  and sandal  various  jewelled  ornaments
adorned  I shall make  all
= If some moment ever again grants Me the sight of Kṛṣṇa, then I shall adorn all those *ghaṭīs*, *kṣaṇas* and *palas* with garlands and sandal paste and many jewelled ornaments.”

@39
in a moment  external  became  the mind  before  sees  two  persons
to them  asks  I  not  Caitanya (conscious)
like a dream  what  I saw  what  I  raved
you  anything  heard  of humble words
= In a moment His mind came to external consciousness, and seeing two persons before Him, He asked them: “Am I not Caitanya? What did I see, as though in a dream? What did I say in My raving? Did you hear any words of misery?

@40
hear  My  of life  friend
not  for Kṛṣṇa  love’s wealth  poor  My  life
body and senses  useless  My  all
= Hear Me, friend of My life: I have no wealth of love for Kṛṣṇa. My life is poor, and My body and senses are all useless.”

@41
again  says  alas  alas  hear  Svarūpa and Rāmarāya
this  My  heart’s  conviction
having heard  consider  is  is not  tell  the truth
so  saying  a verse  utters
= Again He said: “Alas, alas! Hear, Svarūpa and Rāmarāya, what My heart is sure of. Having heard it, consider and tell Me truly whether it is so or not.” Saying this, He uttered a verse:

@42
free of deceit  love  not  indeed  is  in the human  world
if  it is  whose  separation  in separation  occurring  who  lives
= “Love free of deceit does not exist in the human world. If it exists, how can anyone be separated? And if separation comes, who can live?”

@43
without deceit  love for Kṛṣṇa  like  of the Jāmbū river  gold
that  love  in the human world  not  exists
if  is  with it  union  not  is  then  separation
separation  occurring  anyone  not  lives
= “Love for Kṛṣṇa without deceit is like the gold of the Jāmbū river: such love does not exist in the human world. If one is joined with it, there is no separation; and if separation comes, no one lives.”

@44
so  saying  the son of Śacī  a verse  recites  wondrous
hear  both  of one  mind  becoming
of My own  heart’s  affairs  to tell  I feel  shame
still  I tell  shame’s seed  eating
= Having said this, the son of Śacī recited a wondrous verse, and the two listened with one mind: “I feel ashamed to tell what is in My heart, yet I speak, having swallowed the seed of shame.

@45
not  of love a trace  is  even slightly  in Me  for Hari
I weep  of good fortune  the greatness  to display
of Him who plays the flute  of the face  without seeing
I sustain  that  the insects of My life  in vain
= “There is not even a slight trace of love for Hari in Me. I weep only to display how great My good fortune is. Without seeing the face of Him who plays the flute, I sustain in vain this insect-like life of Mine.”

@46
far  of pure love  the scent  of feigned  love  the bond
even that  of Mine  is not  at Kṛṣṇa’s feet
yet  that  I make  weeping  my good fortune’s  display
I make  this  know  for certain
= Far from Me is even the scent of pure love; I do not have even a bond of feigned love at Kṛṣṇa’s feet. If I weep, I do it to display My good fortune — know this for certain.

@47
in which  the flute sound’s  joy  not  seeing  that  moon-like  face
although  there is not  support (ālambana)
My own body  I make  love  only  of kāma  the way
the insect of life  I make  maintenance
= Without seeing that moon-like face, in which lies the joy of the flute’s sound, I have no support (*ālambana*) for My life; still I love My own body. This is simply the way of *kāma*, desire for oneself: I maintain this insect of a life.

@48
love for Kṛṣṇa  spotless  like  pure  Ganges  water
that  love  of nectar  an ocean
spotless  in that  attachment (anurāga)  not  hides  another  stain
on white cloth  like  an ink  drop
= Love for Kṛṣṇa is spotless, like the pure water of the Ganges; that love is an ocean of nectar. In that spotless attachment (*anurāga*) no other stain can hide, just as a drop of ink cannot hide on white cloth.

@49
pure love’s  happiness  ocean  if I get  its  one  drop
that  drop  the world  drowns
to tell  fit  not  yet  the madman  speaks
when told  even  who  believes
= Pure love is an ocean of joy; if one gets a single drop of it, that drop drowns the world. It is not fit to be told, yet the madman speaks — and when it is told, who believes it?”

@50
in this way  day after day  Svarūpa and Rāmānanda  with
His own  feelings  makes  known
outwardly  poison’s burning  is  within  blissful
love for Kṛṣṇa  wondrous  nature
= In this way, day after day, He revealed His feelings to Svarūpa and Rāmānanda. Outwardly there was the burning of poison, but within was bliss: such is the wondrous nature of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@51
this  love’s  tasting  hot  sugarcane  chewing
the mouth  burns  not  can be  given up
that  love  in whose  heart  its  power  he alone  knows
of poison and nectar  in one  meeting
= Tasting this love is like chewing hot sugarcane: the mouth burns, yet one cannot give it up. Only he in whose heart that love lives knows its power — poison and nectar meeting in one.

@52
by its pains  of fresh  kālakūṭa poison  pungency’s  pride  banishing
by the outpouring  of joys  of nectar  sweetness’s  conceit  humbling
love  O beautiful one  for Nanda’s son  directed  awakens  in whose  heart
are known  clearly  its  crooked and sweet  by him  alone  powers
= “O beautiful one, the love for Nanda’s son that awakens in someone’s heart banishes by its pains the pride of the fresh *kālakūṭa* poison in its pungency, and by its outpouring of joys humbles the conceit of nectar in its sweetness. Its crooked and sweet powers are clearly known by that person alone.” (Vidagdha-mādhava 2.18)

@53
when  sees  Jagannātha  with Śrī Rāma and Subhadrā
then  thinks  I have come  to Kurukṣetra
fruitful  has become  life  I have seen  the lotus-eyed One
are soothed  body  mind  eyes
= When He saw Jagannātha with Śrī Balarāma and Subhadrā, He thought: “I have come to Kurukṣetra! My life has borne fruit: I have seen the lotus-eyed One, and My body, mind and eyes are soothed.”

@54
of Garuḍa  in the presence  staying  takes  darśana
that  bliss  what  shall I tell  in words
of the Garuḍa pillar  at the base  is  a  low  channel
that  channel  filled  with tears
= Standing near Garuḍa, He took darśana of the Lord. What can I say of that bliss? At the base of the Garuḍa pillar there was a low channel in the floor, and it filled with His tears.

@55
from there  home  coming  the ground  on  sitting
with His nails  makes  on the earth  writing
alas  where  is Vṛndāvana  where  the son of the king of cowherds
where  that  one with the flute at His lips
= Returning home from there, He would sit on the ground and write on the earth with His nails: “Alas, where is Vṛndāvana? Where is the son of the king of the cowherds? Where is He with the flute at His lips?

@56
where  that  threefold-bending  stance  where  that  flute  song
where  that  Yamunā’s  bank
where  that  rāsa  play  where  dancing  singing  laughter
where  the Lord  Madana-mohana
= Where is that stance, bending in three places? Where is that song of the flute? Where is that bank of the Yamunā? Where is that play of the rāsa? Where are the dancing, the singing and the laughter? Where is My Lord, Madana-mohana?”

@57
arose  various  ecstasies’ agitation  in the mind  was  anxiety
for a moment  only  cannot  pass time
in the strong  fire of separation  composure  became  shaken
various  verses  began  to recite
= Many surging ecstasies arose, and His mind grew anxious; He could not pass even a moment. In the strong fire of separation His composure was shaken, and He began to recite various verses:

@58
these  unfortunate  days and nights
O Hari  Your sight  without
O friend of the helpless  of compassion the sole ocean
alas  alas  alas  alas  how  shall I pass
= “O Hari, O friend of the helpless, O sole ocean of compassion! Alas, alas, how shall I pass these unfortunate days and nights without seeing You?” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta 41)

@59
Your  sight  without  unfortunate  these  nights and days
this  time  not  can  pass
You  of the helpless  the friend  boundless  of compassion ocean
mercy  showing  give  sight
= “Without seeing You, these nights and days are unfortunate; this time will not pass. You are the friend of the helpless, a boundless ocean of compassion: be merciful and let Me see You.”

@60
arose  ecstasy’s  restlessness  the mind  became  unsteady
of ecstasy  the course  to understand  not  is possible
without seeing  burns  the mind  how  shall I get  sight
of Kṛṣṇa  He asks  the means
= Restless ecstasy arose and His mind grew unsteady; the course of ecstasy cannot be understood. His mind burned from not seeing Kṛṣṇa, and He asked Kṛṣṇa Himself the means of seeing Him:

@61
Your boyhood  of the three worlds a wonder  so  know
My restlessness  and  to You  or  to Me  alone  known
so  what  shall I do  in solitude  O flute-player
charming  lotus face  to behold  with My two eyes
= “O You who play the flute, know that Your boyhood is the wonder of the three worlds, and My restlessness is known only to You and Me. What then shall I do to behold Your charming lotus face with My two eyes in solitude?” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta 32)

@62
Your  sweetness’s  power  by it  My  restlessness
these  two  You (and)  I  know
what  shall I do  where  shall I go  where  going  You  shall I find
that  to Me  tell  indeed  Yourself
= “The power of Your sweetness, and My restlessness because of it — these two only You and I know. What shall I do? Where shall I go? Where can I go to find You? Tell Me this Yourself!”

@63
of various  ecstasies  the force  arose  junction and mingling
ecstasy with ecstasy  there was  a great battle
eagerness  restlessness  humility  anger and indignation  and other  soldiers
the madness of love  of all  the cause
= Under the force of many ecstasies, they met and mingled (*sandhi* and *śābalya*), and ecstasy fought ecstasy in a great battle. Eagerness, restlessness, humility, anger, indignation and others were the soldiers, and the madness of love was the cause of it all.

@64
maddened elephants  the ecstasies  the Lord’s  body  a sugarcane field
in the elephants’ fight  of the field  the trampling
to the Lord  came  divine madness  of body and mind  exhaustion
in ecstatic absorption  makes  address
= The ecstasies were maddened elephants and the Lord’s body a field of sugarcane: in the elephants’ battle the field was trampled. Divine madness came upon the Lord, His body and mind were exhausted, and absorbed in ecstasy He cried out:

@65
O God  O Beloved  O sole Friend of the world
O Kṛṣṇa  O Fickle One  O sole Ocean of compassion
O Lord  O Lover  O Delight of the eyes
alas  alas  when  indeed  will You be  on the path  of the eyes  of Mine
= “O God, O Beloved, O sole Friend of the world! O Kṛṣṇa, O Fickle One, O sole Ocean of compassion! O Lord, O Lover, O Delight of the eyes! Alas, alas, when will You again come before My eyes?” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta 40)

@66
of madness  the signs  cause  Kṛṣṇa’s  appearance
in ecstatic absorption  arises  loving  pique
of mocking  words  the manner  pique  pride  veiled praise
sometimes  censure  sometimes  even  honour
= The signs of madness made Kṛṣṇa appear before Him, and in the absorption of ecstasy loving pique (*praṇaya-māna*) arose: mocking speech, pique, pride and veiled praise — now censure, now honour.

@67
You  God  in play  absorbed  of the world  women  all
with them  You make  the play You desire
You  My  Beloved  therefore  sit  in My  heart
by My  good fortune  You have made  Your coming
= “You are God (*deva*), absorbed in play: with all the women of the world You play as You like. You are My Beloved (*dayita*): so sit within My heart, since by My good fortune You have come.

@68
of the world  the women  all  You make  attraction
there  You make  for all  satisfaction
You  Kṛṣṇa  hearts  You steal  such  what  rogue
You  indeed  who  makes  honour
= You attract all the women of the world and satisfy them all. You are Kṛṣṇa, the stealer of hearts — what a rogue! Who would ever honour You?

@69
Your  fickle  mind  in one place  not  is  staying
in that  Yours  is not  any  fault
You  indeed  compassion’s ocean  My  life’s  friend
at You  is not  My  ever  anger
