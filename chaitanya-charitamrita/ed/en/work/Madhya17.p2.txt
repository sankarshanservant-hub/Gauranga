# CC Madhya 17, stage A, part 2 (verses 118–234); read by build_en.py together with Madhya17.txt

@118
whoever  Him  sees  he  God  taking Him for  says
such  the art of enchanting  whoever  sees  he  is bewildered
= “Whoever sees Him declares Him to be God. Such is His art of enchantment: whoever looks at Him falls under its spell.

@119
Sārvabhauma  Bhaṭṭācārya  a scholar  formidable
I have heard  Caitanya’s  in the company  became  mad
= “Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya is a formidable scholar, yet I hear that in Caitanya’s company he has gone mad.

@120
a sannyāsī  in name only  a great conjurer
in Kāśī  not  will sell  His  wares of emotion
= “He is a sannyāsī in name only; in fact He is a great conjurer. But here in Kāśī His wares of emotion will not sell.

@121
Vedānta  hearing  do  do not  go  His  to the side
of unruly people  in the company  of both worlds  ruin
= “Keep hearing Vedānta and do not go near Him. The company of unruly people ruins both this world and the next.”

@122
this  hearing  that  brāhmaṇa  great pain  felt
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  saying  there  from  rising  went
= Hearing this, the brāhmaṇa was deeply pained. Saying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” he rose and left that place.

@123
of the Lord  by the sight  pure  had become  his  mind
before the Lord  sorrowful  being  tells  the account
= His mind had been purified by seeing the Lord. Sorrowfully he came before the Lord and told Him everything.

@124
hearing  Mahāprabhu  then  slightly  smiled
again  that  brāhmaṇa  the Lord  asked
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu smiled slightly, and the brāhmaṇa asked the Lord again:

@125
him  before  when  I  Your  name  took
he  Your  name  knows  himself  said
= “When I spoke Your name before him, he himself said that he knew Your name.

@126
Your  faults  in telling  makes  of the name  utterance
Caitanya  Caitanya  thus  says  three times
= “While speaking of Your faults he uttered Your name, saying ‘Caitanya, Caitanya’ three times.

@127
in three times  the name of Kṛṣṇa  not  came  his  to the mouth
with contempt  the name  he takes  hearing  I feel  pain
= “But in all three times the name of Kṛṣṇa never came to his lips. He takes Your name with contempt, and hearing it pains me.

@128
of this  the reason  to me  tell  mercy  showing
You  seeing  mouth  my  says  Kṛṣṇa  Hari
= “Please be kind and tell me the reason for this. When I see You, my mouth says ‘Kṛṣṇa!’ and ‘Hari!’ ”

@129
the Lord  says  Māyāvādīs  to Kṛṣṇa  are offenders
Brahman  ātmā  caitanya  they say  incessantly
= The Lord said, “The Māyāvādīs are offenders against Kṛṣṇa. They incessantly say ‘Brahman,’ ‘ātmā,’ ‘caitanya’ (consciousness).

@130
therefore  their  to the mouth  not  comes  the name of Kṛṣṇa
the name of Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa’s own form  both are  equal
= “Therefore the name of Kṛṣṇa does not come to their lips. The name of Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa’s own form are one and the same.

@131
the name  the Deity form  the svarūpa  the three  one in form
among the three  difference  there is not  the three  are forms of consciousness and bliss
= “The name, the form (*vigraha*) and the essential nature (*svarūpa*) are one. There is no difference among the three: all three are forms of consciousness and bliss.

@132
of body and embodied  of name and named  in Kṛṣṇa  there is not  difference
of the jīva  the nature  in name  body  and self  difference
= “In Kṛṣṇa there is no difference between the body and its possessor, or between the name and the one named. It is the jīva’s nature that his name, body and true self are different.

@133
the name  a wish-fulfilling gem  is Kṛṣṇa
of consciousness  of rasa  the embodiment
complete  pure  eternally  liberated
because of non-difference  of the name  and the named
= “The name is a wish-fulfilling gem; it is Kṛṣṇa Himself, the embodiment of consciousness and rasa — complete, pure and eternally liberated, for the name and the named are not different.” (Padma Purāṇa)

@134
therefore  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  body  pastimes
by material senses  graspable  are not  they are  self-manifest
= “Therefore Kṛṣṇa’s name, His body and His pastimes cannot be grasped by the material senses; they manifest themselves.

@135
Kṛṣṇa’s name  Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  all
Kṛṣṇa’s  svarūpa  equal to  all  consciousness and bliss
= “Kṛṣṇa’s name, His qualities and all His pastimes are equal to Kṛṣṇa’s own form: all of them are consciousness and bliss.

@136
therefore  Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name and the rest
not  can be  grasped  by the senses
when turned toward service  indeed  on the tongue and the rest
of itself  alone  manifests  this
= “Therefore Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name, form, qualities and pastimes cannot be grasped by the material senses. When one is turned toward service, they reveal themselves of their own accord, beginning on the tongue.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 1.2.234)

@137
than the bliss of Brahman  more than  full bliss  of the pastimes  the rasa
the knower of Brahman  attracting  makes  subject to itself
= “The rasa of His pastimes is fuller bliss than the bliss of Brahman. It attracts even the knower of Brahman and makes him its own.

@138
in his own happiness  secluded  whose mind  therefore  having cast off other  feelings
yet  by the Unconquered One’s  charming  pastimes  attracted  whose heart  about Him
composed  out of mercy  who  of the truth  the lamp  the Purāṇa
to him  of all  sins  the destroyer  to the son of Vyāsa  bowed am I
= “His mind was absorbed in the happiness of the self, and so he had cast off every other feeling; yet his heart was drawn by the charming pastimes of the Unconquered Lord, and out of mercy he composed the Purāṇa about Him, the lamp of the truth. To him, the son of Vyāsa, the destroyer of all sins, I bow down.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 12.12.69)

@139
than the bliss of Brahman  more than  full bliss  are Kṛṣṇa’s qualities
therefore  they attract  of the ātmārāma  the mind
= “Kṛṣṇa’s qualities are fuller bliss than the bliss of Brahman. Therefore they attract the mind even of one who delights in the self.

@140
delighting in the ātmā  and  the sages
free from knots  even  to Urukrama
perform  causeless  devotion
of such  qualities  is Hari
= “Even sages who delight in the ātmā and are free from the knots of the heart render causeless devotion to Urukrama, for such are the qualities of Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.10)

@141
these  all  let them be  with Kṛṣṇa’s feet  in connection
of the ātmārāma  the mind  steals  of tulasī  the fragrance
= “Let all this be. The fragrance of tulasī that has touched Kṛṣṇa’s feet steals the mind of one who delights in the self.

@142
of Him  the lotus-eyed  of the lotus feet
with the filaments  mixed  of tulasī  of the nectar  the breeze
having entered within  through its own passage  caused  in them
agitation  devoted to the imperishable Brahman  even  of mind and body
= “The breeze, carrying the fragrance of the tulasī mixed with the filaments of the lotus feet of the lotus-eyed Lord, entered within them through their nostrils and stirred both mind and body, though they were devoted to the imperishable Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 3.15.43)

@143
therefore  the name of Kṛṣṇa  not  comes  their  to the mouth
the Māyāvādīs  since  are utterly averse
= “Therefore the name of Kṛṣṇa does not come to the lips of the Māyāvādīs, for they are utterly averse to the Lord.

@144
wares of emotion  to sell  I  came  to Kāśī
buyer  there is none  not  it sells  taking it  I shall go  home
= “I came to Kāśī to sell My wares of emotion, but there is no buyer; they do not sell, so I shall have to take them back home.

@145
heavy  load  bringing  I came  how  taking it  shall I go
a small  a trifling  price  if I get  right here  I shall sell
= “I came with a heavy load — how can I carry it back? If I get even a small price, I will sell it right here.”

@146
thus  saying  that  brāhmaṇa  His own  making
in the morning  rising  to Mathurā  set out  Gaurahari
= Saying this, Gaurahari made that brāhmaṇa His own, and rising early the next morning He set out for Mathurā.

@147
those  three  with Him  go  the Lord  forbade
from a distance  from  the three  home  He sent
= Those three followed Him, but the Lord forbade them, and from some way along the road He sent all three back home.
