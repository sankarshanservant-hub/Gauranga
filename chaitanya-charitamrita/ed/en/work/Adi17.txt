@title The Pastimes of Śrī Caitanya in His Youth

@1
I bow down  whose ways are free and wondrous  I  that
to Caitanya  by whose  grace
the yavanas  become like the gods
Kṛṣṇa’s  names  loudly chanting
= I bow down to Śrī Caitanya, whose ways are free and wondrous. By His grace even *yavanas* (those outside Vedic culture), loudly chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa, become like the gods.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees all
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of the kaiśora pastimes  the summary  I have made  an enumeration
of the pastimes of youth  the summary  I make  in sequence
= I have listed the pastimes of His *kaiśora* age in summary. Now I give in sequence the summary of the pastimes of His youth (*yauvana*).

@4
with learning  beauty  fine dress
enjoyment  dancing  kīrtana
love  the Name  by giving  and
Gaura  sports  in youth
= In His youth Gaura sports with learning, beauty, fine dress, enjoyment, dancing and *kīrtana*, and by giving away love of God and the holy name.

@5
youth  on entering  of the body  the limbs  adorned
divine  cloth  divine  dress  garlands and sandalwood
= As He entered youth, His limbs were adorned with divine cloth and divine dress, with garlands and sandalwood paste.

@6
of learning  in the pride  anyone  not  He does  regard
all  scholars  defeating  He does  teaching
= In the pride of His learning He took no account of anyone; defeating all the scholars, He taught.

@7
of a disorder of vāyu  on the pretext  He made  love  manifest
the devotees  taking  He made  various  pastimes
= On the pretext of a disorder of *vāyu* (the vital air), He manifested love of God, and with His devotees He enjoyed various pastimes.

@8
then  indeed  made  the Lord  to Gayā  a journey
Īśvara Purī  with  there  a meeting
= Then the Lord journeyed to Gayā, and there He met Īśvara Purī.

@9
initiation  after  there was  of love  the manifestation
home  the return  again  of love  the play
= After His initiation, love of God became manifest in Him. He returned home, and again love played in Him.

@10
to Śacī  love  the gift  then  with Advaita  the meeting
Advaita  obtained  of the universal form  the sight
= He gave love of God to Śacī; then came the meeting with Advaita, who was granted the sight of the universal form.

@11
of the Lord  the ceremonial bathing  then  performed  Śrīvāsa
on the cot  sitting  the Lord  made  majesty  manifest
= Then Śrīvāsa performed the Lord’s *abhiṣeka* (ceremonial bathing), and sitting on a cot, the Lord manifested His divine majesty.

@12
then  of Nityānanda Svarūpa  the arrival
the Lord  meeting  He obtained  of the six-armed form  the sight
= Then Nityānanda Svarūpa came. Meeting the Lord, He was granted the sight of the six-armed form.

@13
first  the six-armed (form)  to Him  showed  the Lord
conch  disc  club  lotus  the bow Śārṅga  flute  holding
= First the Lord showed Him the six-armed form, holding the conch, disc, club, lotus, the bow Śārṅga and the flute.

@14
then  four-armed  He became  in three  the body  bent
in two  hands  the flute  He plays  in two  hands  conch and disc
= Then He became four-armed, His body bent in three places; with two hands He played the flute, and in the other two He held the conch and disc.

@15
then  indeed  two-armed  only  a flute  at the lips
dark  body  yellow  cloth  of the king of Vraja  the son
= Then He was simply two-armed, the flute at His lips — the son of the king of Vraja, dark of body and dressed in yellow.

@16
then  of Nityānanda Gosāñi  the vyāsa-pūjā
in Nityānanda’s mood  He made  the club  the holding
= Then came Nityānanda Gosāñi’s *vyāsa-pūjā*; and in the mood of Nityānanda, the Lord held the club (*muṣala*).

@17
then  Śacī  saw  Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  two  brothers
then  delivered  the Lord  Jagāi and Mādhāi
= Then Śacī saw the two brothers as Rāma and Kṛṣṇa. Then the Lord delivered Jagāi and Mādhāi.

@18
then  seven praharas  remained  the Lord  in ecstatic absorption
here and there  the devotees  saw (Him)  in a special way
= Then for seven *praharas* (twenty-one hours) the Lord remained absorbed in ecstasy, and wherever they were, the devotees saw Him in His special glory.

@19
in Varāha’s mood  He was  in Murāri’s house
his  on the shoulders  climbing  the Lord  danced  in the courtyard
= In Murāri’s house the Lord was absorbed in the mood of Varāha, and climbing onto Murāri’s shoulders, He danced in the courtyard.

@20
then  of Śuklāmbara  He made  raw rice  the eating
‘harer nāma’  of the verse  He made  the meaning  the explanation
= Then He ate Śuklāmbara’s raw rice, and He explained the meaning of the verse “*harer nāma*.”

@21
of Hari  the name  of Hari  the name
of Hari  the name  indeed  only
in Kali  there is no  indeed  there is no  indeed
there is no  indeed  way  other
= “The name of Hari, the name of Hari, only the name of Hari! In Kali there is no other way, no other way, no other way at all.” (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa 38.126)

@22
in the age of Kali  in the form of the Name  Kṛṣṇa’s  descent
the Name  from  comes  of the whole world  the deliverance
= In the age of Kali, Kṛṣṇa descends in the form of the Name; through the Name the whole world is delivered.

@23
for firmness  ‘harer nāma’  the phrase  three times
dull  people  to make understand  again  the word ‘eva’
= For firmness the phrase “*harer nāma*” is spoken three times; and to make dull people understand, the word *eva* (“indeed”) is added again.

@24
‘kevala’  by the word  once more  the confirmation
knowledge  yoga  austerity  and other  practices  the exclusion
= The word *kevala* (“only”) confirms it once more, excluding knowledge (*jñāna*), *yoga*, austerity and all other practices.

@25
otherwise  who  thinks  for him  there is no  deliverance
no  no  no  thrice  spoken  the word ‘eva’
= Whoever thinks otherwise has no deliverance: “there is no, there is no, there is no” — thrice the word *eva* is spoken.

@26
grass  than  lower  being  always  one should take  the Name
oneself  without pride  to others  one should give  honor
= Being lower than a blade of grass, one should always take the Name; free of pride oneself, one should give honor to others.

@27
like a tree  forbearance  a Vaiṣṇava  will practice
scolded  beaten  to anyone  anything  not  he will say
= A Vaiṣṇava should practice forbearance like a tree; though scolded or beaten, he should say nothing to anyone.

@28
even when cut  a tree  as  anything  not  says
withering  it dies  yet  water  not  asks
= Even when cut down, a tree says nothing; though it withers and dies, it does not ask for water.

@29
in this way  a Vaiṣṇava  from anyone  anything  not  will ask
living on what comes unasked  or  vegetables and fruits  he will eat
= In the same way a Vaiṣṇava will ask no one for anything. He will live on what comes unasked, or eat vegetables and fruits.

@30
always  the Name  he will take  with whatever comes  content
in this way  conduct  makes  of the path of bhakti  the nourishment
= He will always take the Name and be content with whatever comes. Such conduct nourishes the path of devotion (*bhakti-dharma*).

@31
than grass  even  more humble
a tree  like  forbearing
seeking no honor  giving honor
to be glorified  always  Hari
= “More humble than a blade of grass, as forbearing as a tree, seeking no honor and giving honor to others — so one should always glorify Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka 3)

@32
upraised  arms  making  I say  listen  all  people
of the Name  on the thread  stringing  wear  on the neck  this  verse
= With arms upraised I say: Listen, everyone! String this verse on the thread of the Name and wear it on your neck.

@33
by the Lord’s order  do  of this  verse  the practice
surely  you will attain  then  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  the feet
= By the Lord’s order, practice what this verse teaches; then you will surely attain the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

@34
then  the Lord  of Śrīvāsa  in the house  constantly
at night  saṅkīrtana  performed  one  year
= Then for one whole year the Lord performed *saṅkīrtana* every night in Śrīvāsa’s house.

@35
the doors  closing  kīrtana  they do  in deepest  absorption
the unbelievers  to mock  come  not  get  entrance
= With the doors closed they performed *kīrtana* in deepest absorption. Unbelievers came to mock but could not get in.

@36
the kīrtana  hearing  outside  they  burning  scorching  die
to Śrīvāsa  trouble  to give  various  schemes  they make
= Hearing the *kīrtana* from outside, they burned with envy, and they devised various schemes to trouble Śrīvāsa.

@37-38
one day  a brāhmaṇa  by name  Gopāla Cāpāla
among unbelievers chief  he  foul-mouthed  a chatterer
of Bhavānī’s worship  all  the articles  taking
at night  of Śrīvāsa  at the door  a spot  having smeared
= One day a brāhmaṇa named Gopāla Cāpāla — a leader among the unbelievers, foul-mouthed and a chatterer — took all the articles for the worship of Bhavānī (Durgā). At night he smeared a spot at Śrīvāsa’s door,

@39
of a banana  a leaf  upon  he placed  oḍa flowers
turmeric  vermilion  and  red sandalwood  rice
= and on a banana leaf he placed *oḍa* (hibiscus) flowers, turmeric, vermilion and red sandalwood, and rice.

@40
a pot of wine  beside  setting  his own  home  he went
in the morning  Śrīvāsa  that  indeed  saw
= Setting a pot of wine beside them, he went home. In the morning Śrīvāsa saw it all.

@41
the leading  people  he brought  having called
to all  says  Śrīvāsa  smiling  smiling
= He called the leading men of the town and, smiling broadly, said to them all:

@42
every  night  do  I  Bhavānī’s worship
my  glory  see  brāhmaṇas  gentlemen
= “Every night I worship Bhavānī. See my glory, O respectable brāhmaṇas!”

@43
then  all  the gentlemen  make  cries of dismay
such  a deed  here  did  what  wicked man
= Then all the gentlemen cried out in dismay: “What wicked man has done such a thing here?”

@44
a sweeper  bringing  all  away  they had removed
water and cow dung  with  that  spot  they had smeared
= They called a sweeper and had everything removed, and had the spot purified with water and cow dung.

@45
three  days  after  that  Gopāla Cāpāla
all over his body  was  leprosy  flows  blood in streams
= Three days later that Gopāla Cāpāla was stricken with leprosy all over his body, and blood flowed in streams.

@46
his whole body  covered  worms  bite  incessantly
unbearable  pain  in suffering  burns  his heart
= Worms covered his whole body and gnawed at him incessantly. The pain was unbearable, and his heart burned with suffering.

@47
at a Gaṅgā ghāṭa  under a tree  he stays  sitting
one day  says  something  the Lord  seeing
= He stayed sitting under a tree by a bathing place on the Gaṅgā. One day, seeing the Lord, he said:

@48
by village  relation  I  Your  maternal uncle
nephew  I  by the disease of leprosy  have become  tormented
= “By village relationship I am Your maternal uncle. Nephew, I am tormented by leprosy.

@49
people  all  to deliver  Your  descent
I  very  miserable  me  make  deliverance
= You have descended to deliver all people. I am in great misery — deliver me!”

@50
this  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  became  angry  the mind
in anger  says  to him  words of rebuke
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu grew angry, and in His anger He spoke words of rebuke to him:

@51
O  sinner  hater of devotees  you  not  I will deliver
ten million births  in this way  by worms  I will have eaten
= “O sinner, hater of devotees, I will not deliver you! For ten million births I will have you eaten by worms like this.

@52
Śrīvāsa  made to do  you  Bhavānī’s worship
ten million  births  will be  your  into Raurava  fall
= You made Śrīvāsa out to be a worshiper of Bhavānī. For ten million births you will fall into the hell Raurava.

@53
unbelievers  to destroy  My  this  descent
unbelievers  destroying  bhakti  I will make  spread
= I have descended to destroy the unbelievers. Destroying the unbelievers, I shall spread devotion.”

@54
so  saying  went  the Lord  to do  bathing in the Gaṅgā
that  sinner  suffering  endures  not  goes  his life
= Saying this, the Lord went to bathe in the Gaṅgā. The sinner went on suffering, yet his life did not leave him.

@55-56
sannyāsa  having taken  when  the Lord  to Nīlācala  went
there  from  when  to Kuliyā  village  He came
then  that  sinner  of the Lord  took  shelter
beneficial  instruction  He gave  becoming  compassionate
= After taking *sannyāsa*, the Lord went to Nīlācala; and when He came from there to the village of Kuliyā, that sinner took shelter of Him. Moved by compassion, the Lord gave him beneficial instruction:

@57
Śrīvāsa  Paṇḍita’s  in the place  there is  an offense
there  go  he  if  does  mercy
= “You have offended Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita. Go to him; if he shows you mercy,

@58
then  your  will be  this  sin’s release
if  again  such  not  you do  act
= then you will be freed from this sin — if you never act like this again.”

@59
then  the brāhmaṇa  coming  took  Śrīvāsa’s shelter
his  by the mercy  there was  from sin  release
= Then the brāhmaṇa came and took shelter of Śrīvāsa, and by his mercy he was freed from the sin.

@60
another  one  brāhmaṇa  came  the kīrtana  to see
at the door  the panels (shut)  not  he could  inside  to go
= Another brāhmaṇa came to see the *kīrtana*, but the doors were shut, and he could not get in.

@61
returning  went  the brāhmaṇa  home  in mind  grief  getting
next  day  to the Lord  says  at the Gaṅgā  seeing (Him)
= The brāhmaṇa went home aggrieved. The next day, seeing the Lord at the Gaṅgā, he said to Him:

@62
I will curse  You  I  I have got  grief of mind
the sacred thread  tearing  curses  the fierce  foul-mouthed man
= “I will curse You — I have been hurt!” And tearing his sacred thread, that fierce, foul-mouthed man cursed Him:

@63
worldly  happiness  Your  let there be  destruction
the curse  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  was  delight
= “May Your worldly happiness be destroyed!” Hearing the curse, Mahāprabhu was delighted.

@64
of the Lord  of the curse  the account  hears  being  faithful
of brāhmaṇas  a curse  from  his  is  protection
= One who hears with faith the account of the Lord’s curse is protected from the curse of brāhmaṇas.

@65
to Mukunda Datta  He made  the mercy of punishment
He removed  his  of the heart  all  dejection
= He gave Mukunda Datta the mercy of punishment and removed all the dejection from his heart.

@66
to Ācārya Gosāñi  the Lord  shows  devotion as to a guru
by that  the Ācārya  greatly  becomes  grieved
= The Lord showed Advaita Ācārya Gosāñi the devotion due to a guru, and this grieved the Ācārya greatly.

@67
a ruse  making  the path of knowledge  he made  an exposition
in anger  the Lord  to him  showed  disrespect
= So, by a ruse, he expounded the path of knowledge, and the Lord, in anger, treated him with disrespect.

@68
then  of Ācārya Gosāñi  joy  there was
abashed  becoming  the Lord  mercy  showed
= Then Ācārya Gosāñi was filled with joy, and the Lord, abashed, showed him mercy.

@69
from Murāri Gupta’s mouth  hearing  of Rāma  the glories  the many
on the forehead  He wrote  his  ‘Rāmadāsa’  the name
= Hearing Rāma’s many glories from the mouth of Murāri Gupta, the Lord wrote on his forehead the name “Rāmadāsa” (servant of Rāma).

@70
of Śrīdhara  from the iron pot  He made  water-drinking
to all  the devotees  He gave  desired  boons
= He drank water from Śrīdhara’s iron pot, and He granted all the devotees the boons they desired.

@71
to Haridāsa Ṭhākura  He showed  mercy
toward the Ācārya  His mother’s  He removed  offense
= He showed mercy to Haridāsa Ṭhākura, and He freed His mother from her offense against Advaita Ācārya.

@72
to the devotees  the Lord  the Name’s glory  spoke
hearing  a student  there  exaggeration  called (it)
= The Lord spoke to the devotees about the glory of the Name. Hearing it, a student there called it *arthavāda* (exaggerated praise).

@73
about the Name  ‘mere praise’  hearing  of the Lord  there was  pain
all  He forbade  his  not  see  face
= Hearing the Name’s glories called mere praise, the Lord was pained, and He told everyone: “Do not look at this man’s face!”

@74
with His associates  in His clothes  going  He made  bathing in the Gaṅgā
of bhakti  the glory  there  He made  explanation
= With His associates He went and bathed in the Gaṅgā with His clothes on, and there He explained the glory of devotion.

@75
by knowledge  karma  yoga  religious duty  not  Kṛṣṇa  controlled
of controlling Kṛṣṇa  the cause  one  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the rasa
= Kṛṣṇa is not brought under control by knowledge, *karma*, *yoga* or religious duty. Only one thing brings Kṛṣṇa under control: the *rasa* of love for Him.

@76
not  controls  Me  yoga
not  sāṅkhya  religious duty  O Uddhava
not  Vedic study  austerity  renunciation
as  devotion  to Me  strong
= “O Uddhava, neither *yoga*, nor *sāṅkhya*, nor religious duty, nor Vedic study, austerity or renunciation brings Me under control as does strong devotion to Me.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.14.20)

@77
to Murāri  says  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa  controlled  (you) made
hearing  Murāri  a verse  to recite  began
= The Lord said to Murāri, “You have brought Kṛṣṇa under your control.” Hearing this, Murāri began to recite a verse:

@78
who am I  poor  most sinful
who  Kṛṣṇa  the abode of Śrī
a brāhmaṇa in name only  thus  indeed  I
with His arms  embraced
= “Who am I — poor and most sinful — and who is Kṛṣṇa, the abode of the goddess of fortune? Yet I, a brāhmaṇa in name only, was embraced in His arms.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.81.16)

@79
one day  the Lord  all  the devotees  taking
saṅkīrtana  having performed  sat down  tired  being
= One day the Lord performed *saṅkīrtana* with all His devotees and then sat down, tired.

@80
one  mango seed  the Lord  in the courtyard  planted
that moment  sprouting  a tree  to grow  began
= The Lord planted a mango seed in the courtyard. At once it sprouted, and a tree began to grow.

@81
before their eyes  the tree  began  to bear fruit
ripened  many  fruits  all  amazed
= Before their eyes the tree began to bear fruit; many fruits ripened, and everyone was amazed.

@82
some two hundred  fruits  the Lord  quickly  had picked
washing  having done  to Kṛṣṇa  as an offering  He offered
= The Lord quickly had some two hundred fruits picked, washed them and offered them to Kṛṣṇa.

@83
red and yellow  in color  no  stone or skin
of one man  the stomach  fills  on eating  one  fruit
= They were red and yellow, without stone or skin, and one fruit would fill a man’s stomach.

@84
seeing  pleased  became  of Śacī  the son
all  He fed  first  having tasted
= Seeing them, the son of Śacī was pleased. He tasted them first and then fed everyone.

@85
stone and skin  none  full of nectarean juice
one  fruit  on eating  with juice  the stomach  fills
= Without stone or skin, they were full of nectarean juice; eating one fruit filled the stomach with its juice.

@86
in this way  every day  it fruited  twelve  months
the Vaiṣṇavas  eat  the fruit  the Lord’s  delight
= In this way the tree bore fruit every day for twelve months, and the Vaiṣṇavas ate it, to the Lord’s delight.

@87
these  all  pastimes  performs  of Śacī  the son
other  people  not  know  except  the devotees
= The son of Śacī performed all these pastimes, but no one knew of them except His devotees.

@88
in this way  twelve months  of kīrtana  at the end
a mango festival  the Lord  holds  day after day
= Thus for twelve months, at the end of each *kīrtana*, the Lord held a festival of mangoes day after day.

@89
kīrtana  while performing  the Lord  came  clouds
by His own  will  He made  of the clouds  the dispersal
= Once, while the Lord was performing *kīrtana*, clouds gathered, and by His own will He dispersed them.

@90
one day  the Lord  to Śrīvāsa  an order  gave
‘the great  Thousand  Names’  read  to hear  My mind  wishes
= One day the Lord said to Śrīvāsa: “Read the great *Sahasra-nāma* (the Thousand Names); I wish to hear it.”

@91
reading  he came  in the hymn  to Nṛsiṁha’s  name
hearing  overwhelmed  became  the Lord  the abode of virtues
= As he read, he came in the hymn to the name of Nṛsiṁha; hearing it, the Lord, the abode of all virtues, was overwhelmed.

@92
in Nṛsiṁha’s mood  the Lord  in His hand  a club  taking
unbelievers  to strike  goes  into the town  running
= In the mood of Nṛsiṁha the Lord took a club in His hand and ran into the town to strike down the unbelievers.

@93
in Nṛsiṁha’s mood  seeing  greatly  blazing
the road  leaving  flee  the people  getting  great  fear
= Seeing Him absorbed in Nṛsiṁha’s mood and blazing with power, people left the road and fled in great fear.

@94
the people’s fear  seeing  of the Lord  outer awareness  came
to Śrīvāsa’s house  going  the club  He threw down
= Seeing the people’s fear, the Lord returned to outer awareness; He went to Śrīvāsa’s house and threw down the club.

@95
to Śrīvāsa  says  the Lord  becoming  dejected
the people  fear  got  My  is  offense
= Dejected, the Lord said to Śrīvāsa: “The people were frightened — that is My offense.”

@96
Śrīvāsa  says  whoever  Your  name  takes
his  millions of  offenses  all  are  destroyed
= Śrīvāsa said: “Whoever takes Your name — all his millions of offenses are destroyed.

@97
offense  none  You made  of the people  deliverance
whoever  You  saw  his  was released  worldly bondage
= There was no offense; You delivered the people. Whoever saw You was released from worldly bondage.”

@98
so  saying  Śrīvāsa  rendered  service
pleased  becoming  the Lord  came  to His own  home
= Saying this, Śrīvāsa served Him, and the Lord, pleased, returned to His own home.

@99
another  day  a devotee of Śiva  Śiva’s glories  sings
in the Lord’s  courtyard  dances  a ḍamaru  plays
= Another day a devotee of Śiva came singing Śiva’s glories; he danced in the Lord’s courtyard, playing a *ḍamaru* drum.

@100
in Maheśa’s mood  became  of Śacī  the son
his  on the shoulders  climbing  dancing  He did  a long time
= The son of Śacī became absorbed in the mood of Maheśa (Śiva); He climbed onto the man’s shoulders and danced for a long time.

@101
another  day  a  beggar  came  to beg
the Lord’s  dancing  seeing  dancing  began  to do
= Another day a beggar came for alms. Seeing the Lord dance, he began to dance too.

@102
with the Lord  dancing  he does  in supreme  joy
the Lord  to him  love  gave  in love’s rasa  he floats
= He danced with the Lord in supreme joy; the Lord gave him love of God, and he floated in its *rasa*.

@103
another  day  an astrologer  one  omniscient  came
to him  honor  showing  the Lord  a question  asked
= Another day an omniscient astrologer came. The Lord honored him and asked:

@104
who  was  in My previous birth  I  tell  calculating
to calculate  began  the omniscient one  the Lord’s words  hearing
= “Who was I in My previous birth? Calculate and tell Me.” Hearing the Lord’s words, the omniscient man began to calculate.

@105
calculating  in meditation  sees  the omniscient one  a great effulgence
of the endless  Vaikuṇṭhas and universes  of all  the shelter
= Having calculated, he saw in meditation a form of great effulgence — the shelter of all, of the endless Vaikuṇṭhas and universes,

@106
the Supreme Truth  the Supreme Brahman  the Supreme Lord
seeing  the Lord’s  form  the omniscient one  was  bewildered
= the Supreme Truth, the Supreme Brahman, the Supreme Lord. Seeing the Lord’s form, the omniscient man was bewildered.

@107
to say  not  could  anything  silent  he became
the Lord  again  question  asked  to speak  he began
= He could say nothing and fell silent. The Lord asked again, and he began to speak:

@108
in the previous  birth  were  You  the supreme shelter
the complete  Bhagavān  full of all majesty
= “In Your previous birth You were the supreme shelter, the complete Bhagavān, full of all majesty.

@109
before  as  were  You  now also  that form
hard to understand  eternal bliss  Your  true nature
= As You were before, so You are now. Your true nature is eternal bliss, hard to understand.”

@110
the Lord  laughing  said  you  anything  not  have understood
before  I  was  by caste  a cowherd
= The Lord laughed and said: “You have understood nothing. Before, I was a cowherd by caste.

@111
in a cowherd’s house  birth  was  of the cows  a herdsman
by that  merit  have become  I  a brāhmaṇa’s son
= I was born in a cowherd’s house and tended the cows. By that merit I have now become a brāhmaṇa’s son.”

@112
the omniscient one  says  I  that  in meditation  saw
in it  majesty  seeing  bewildered  I became
= The omniscient man said: “That is what I saw in meditation, and seeing majesty in it, I was bewildered.

@113
that form  this form  I see  as one
sometimes  a difference  I see  that  by māyā  Your
= I see that form and this form as one. If ever I see a difference, that is by Your *māyā*.

@114
whoever  You are  that  You are  to You  obeisance
the Lord  to him  love  giving  made  reward
= Whoever You may be, I bow down to You.” The Lord rewarded him with love of God.

@115
one  day  the Lord  in the Viṣṇu pavilion  sitting
‘honey  bring’  ‘honey  bring’  says  calling out
= One day, sitting in the Viṣṇu pavilion, the Lord called out: “Bring honey! Bring honey!”

@116
Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Lord’s  mood  understood
a vessel of Gaṅgā water  bringing  before (Him)  placed
= Nityānanda Gosāñi understood the Lord’s mood; He brought a vessel of Gaṅgā water and set it before Him.

@117
water  drinking  He dances  becoming  overwhelmed
the pastime of drawing the Yamunā  see  all
= The Lord drank the water and danced, overwhelmed, and everyone saw the pastime of drawing the Yamunā.

@118
intoxicated  gait  Baladeva’s  imitation
Ācārya  Śekhara  Him  sees  in Rāma’s form
= He moved with an intoxicated gait, like Baladeva, and Ācārya Śekhara (Candraśekhara) saw Him in the form of Rāma.

@119
Vanamālī  Ācārya  sees  a golden  plough
all  together  dancing  do  in bliss  overwhelmed
= Vanamālī Ācārya saw a golden plough, and all danced together, overwhelmed with bliss.

@120
in this way  dancing  went on  four  praharas
in the evening  bathing in the Gaṅgā  having done  all  went  home
= So the dancing went on for four *praharas* (twelve hours); in the evening everyone bathed in the Gaṅgā and went home.

@121
to the townspeople  the Lord  when  an order  gave
in house after house  saṅkīrtana  to perform  they began
= When the Lord gave the townspeople His order, they began to perform *saṅkīrtana* in every house:

@122
O Hari  to Hari  obeisance  O Kṛṣṇa  to Yādava  obeisance
Gopāla  Govinda  Rāma  Śrī Madhusūdana
= “*Hari haraye namaḥ, kṛṣṇa yādavāya namaḥ; gopāla govinda rāma śrī-madhusūdana!*”

@123
mṛdaṅgas and karatālas  the great sound of saṅkīrtana
‘Hari’  ‘Hari’  the sound  except  other  not  heard
= With *mṛdaṅgas* and *karatālas* the sound of *saṅkīrtana* was tremendous; no sound was heard but “Hari! Hari!”

@124
hearing  that  angry  became  all  the Muslims
to the Kazi  coming  all  made  a complaint
= Hearing it, all the Muslims grew angry; they went to the Kazi and lodged a complaint.

@125
in anger  in the evening  the Kazi  to one  house  came
a mṛdaṅga  breaking  to the people  to speak  he began
= One evening the Kazi angrily came to a house, broke a *mṛdaṅga* and said to the people:

@126
till now  openly  anyone  not  practiced  Hindu rites
now  this  effort  you push  on whose  strength  I would know
= “Till now no one practiced Hindu rites openly. Now you press on with this effort — on whose strength, I would like to know?

@127
anyone  kīrtana  not  do  in the whole  town
today  I  forgiving  am going  home
= No one in the whole town is to perform *kīrtana*. Today I forgive you and go home.

@128
again  if  kīrtana  doing  I catch  (you)
all property  fining  his  caste  indeed  I will take
= If I catch anyone performing *kīrtana* again, I will confiscate all he owns and take away his caste.”

@129
so  saying  the Kazi  left  the townspeople
to the Lord  reported  getting  great  grief
= Saying this, the Kazi left, and the townspeople, deeply grieved, reported it to the Lord.

@130
the Lord  an order  gave  go  do  kīrtana
I  will destroy  today  all  the Muslims
= The Lord ordered: “Go and perform *kīrtana*! Today I shall destroy all the Muslims.”

@131
home  going  all  people  perform  kīrtana
for fear of the Kazi  at ease  not  startled  their minds
= Everyone went home and performed *kīrtana*, but out of fear of the Kazi they were not at ease; their minds were alarmed.

@132
in all their  hearts  fear  the Lord  in His mind  knowing
to speak  began  to the people  quickly  calling  bringing
= Knowing the fear in their hearts, the Lord quickly called the people together and said:

@133
through every part of the town  today  I shall do  kīrtana
in the evening  make  all of you  of the town  decoration
= “Today I shall perform *kīrtana* through every part of the town. In the evening, all of you, decorate the town.

@134
in the evening  lamps  all  light  in every house
see  what  Kazi  coming  Me  stops
= In the evening light lamps in every house. We shall see what Kazi comes to stop Me!”

@135
so  saying  in the evening  set out  Gaurarāya
of kīrtana  formed  the Lord  three  groups
= Saying this, in the evening Gaurarāya set out. The Lord formed three groups of *kīrtana*.

@136
in the front  group  dancing  does  Haridāsa
in the middle  dances  Ācārya Gosāñi  in supreme  joy
= In the front group Haridāsa danced; in the middle Ācārya Gosāñi danced in supreme joy.

@137
in the last  group  dancing  does  Gauracandra
with Him  dancing  moves  Prabhu  Nityānanda
= In the last group Gauracandra danced, and Nityānanda Prabhu moved along dancing with Him.

@138
Vṛndāvana dāsa  this  in the Caitanya-maṅgala
in detail  has described  by the power of Caitanya’s mercy
= Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail in the *Caitanya-maṅgala* by the power of Caitanya’s mercy.
