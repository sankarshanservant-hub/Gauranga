@title The Pastimes of Śrī Caitanya in His Youth

@1
I bow down  whose ways are free and wondrous  I  that
to Caitanya  by whose  grace
the yavanas  become like the gods
Kṛṣṇa’s  names  loudly chanting
= I bow down to Śrī Caitanya, whose ways are free and wondrous. By His grace even *yavanas* (those outside Vedic culture), loudly chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa, become like the gods.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees all
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of the kaiśora pastimes  the summary  I have made  an enumeration
of the pastimes of youth  the summary  I make  in sequence
= I have listed the pastimes of His *kaiśora* age in summary. Now I give in sequence the summary of the pastimes of His youth (*yauvana*).

@4
with learning  beauty  fine dress
enjoyment  dancing  kīrtana
love  the Name  by giving  and
Gaura  sports  in youth
= In His youth Gaura sports with learning, beauty, fine dress, enjoyment, dancing and *kīrtana*, and by giving away love of God and the holy name.

@5
youth  on entering  of the body  the limbs  adorned
divine  cloth  divine  dress  garlands and sandalwood
= As He entered youth, His limbs were adorned with divine cloth and divine dress, with garlands and sandalwood paste.

@6
of learning  in the pride  anyone  not  He does  regard
all  scholars  defeating  He does  teaching
= In the pride of His learning He took no account of anyone; defeating all the scholars, He taught.

@7
of a disorder of vāyu  on the pretext  He made  love  manifest
the devotees  taking  He made  various  pastimes
= On the pretext of a disorder of *vāyu* (the vital air), He manifested love of God, and with His devotees He enjoyed various pastimes.

@8
then  indeed  made  the Lord  to Gayā  a journey
Īśvara Purī  with  there  a meeting
= Then the Lord journeyed to Gayā, and there He met Īśvara Purī.

@9
initiation  after  there was  of love  the manifestation
home  the return  again  of love  the play
= After His initiation, love of God became manifest in Him. He returned home, and again love played in Him.

@10
to Śacī  love  the gift  then  with Advaita  the meeting
Advaita  obtained  of the universal form  the sight
= He gave love of God to Śacī; then came the meeting with Advaita, who was granted the sight of the universal form.

@11
of the Lord  the ceremonial bathing  then  performed  Śrīvāsa
on the cot  sitting  the Lord  made  majesty  manifest
= Then Śrīvāsa performed the Lord’s *abhiṣeka* (ceremonial bathing), and sitting on a cot, the Lord manifested His divine majesty.

@12
then  of Nityānanda Svarūpa  the arrival
the Lord  meeting  He obtained  of the six-armed form  the sight
= Then Nityānanda Svarūpa came. Meeting the Lord, He was granted the sight of the six-armed form.

@13
first  the six-armed (form)  to Him  showed  the Lord
conch  disc  club  lotus  the bow Śārṅga  flute  holding
= First the Lord showed Him the six-armed form, holding the conch, disc, club, lotus, the bow Śārṅga and the flute.

@14
then  four-armed  He became  in three  the body  bent
in two  hands  the flute  He plays  in two  hands  conch and disc
= Then He became four-armed, His body bent in three places; with two hands He played the flute, and in the other two He held the conch and disc.

@15
then  indeed  two-armed  only  a flute  at the lips
dark  body  yellow  cloth  of the king of Vraja  the son
= Then He was simply two-armed, the flute at His lips — the son of the king of Vraja, dark of body and dressed in yellow.

@16
then  of Nityānanda Gosāñi  the vyāsa-pūjā
in Nityānanda’s mood  He made  the club  the holding
= Then came Nityānanda Gosāñi’s *vyāsa-pūjā*; and in the mood of Nityānanda, the Lord held the club (*muṣala*).

@17
then  Śacī  saw  Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  two  brothers
then  delivered  the Lord  Jagāi and Mādhāi
= Then Śacī saw the two brothers as Rāma and Kṛṣṇa. Then the Lord delivered Jagāi and Mādhāi.

@18
then  seven praharas  remained  the Lord  in ecstatic absorption
here and there  the devotees  saw (Him)  in a special way
= Then for seven *praharas* (twenty-one hours) the Lord remained absorbed in ecstasy, and wherever they were, the devotees saw Him in His special glory.

@19
in Varāha’s mood  He was  in Murāri’s house
his  on the shoulders  climbing  the Lord  danced  in the courtyard
= In Murāri’s house the Lord was absorbed in the mood of Varāha, and climbing onto Murāri’s shoulders, He danced in the courtyard.

@20
then  of Śuklāmbara  He made  raw rice  the eating
‘harer nāma’  of the verse  He made  the meaning  the explanation
= Then He ate Śuklāmbara’s raw rice, and He explained the meaning of the verse “*harer nāma*.”

@21
of Hari  the name  of Hari  the name
of Hari  the name  indeed  only
in Kali  there is no  indeed  there is no  indeed
there is no  indeed  way  other
= “The name of Hari, the name of Hari, only the name of Hari! In Kali there is no other way, no other way, no other way at all.” (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa 38.126)

@22
in the age of Kali  in the form of the Name  Kṛṣṇa’s  descent
the Name  from  comes  of the whole world  the deliverance
= In the age of Kali, Kṛṣṇa descends in the form of the Name; through the Name the whole world is delivered.

@23
for firmness  ‘harer nāma’  the phrase  three times
dull  people  to make understand  again  the word ‘eva’
= For firmness the phrase “*harer nāma*” is spoken three times; and to make dull people understand, the word *eva* (“indeed”) is added again.

@24
‘kevala’  by the word  once more  the confirmation
knowledge  yoga  austerity  and other  practices  the exclusion
= The word *kevala* (“only”) confirms it once more, excluding knowledge (*jñāna*), *yoga*, austerity and all other practices.

@25
otherwise  who  thinks  for him  there is no  deliverance
no  no  no  thrice  spoken  the word ‘eva’
= Whoever thinks otherwise has no deliverance: “there is no, there is no, there is no” — thrice the word *eva* is spoken.

@26
grass  than  lower  being  always  one should take  the Name
oneself  without pride  to others  one should give  honor
= Being lower than a blade of grass, one should always take the Name; free of pride oneself, one should give honor to others.

@27
like a tree  forbearance  a Vaiṣṇava  will practice
scolded  beaten  to anyone  anything  not  he will say
= A Vaiṣṇava should practice forbearance like a tree; though scolded or beaten, he should say nothing to anyone.

@28
even when cut  a tree  as  anything  not  says
withering  it dies  yet  water  not  asks
= Even when cut down, a tree says nothing; though it withers and dies, it does not ask for water.

@29
in this way  a Vaiṣṇava  from anyone  anything  not  will ask
living on what comes unasked  or  vegetables and fruits  he will eat
= In the same way a Vaiṣṇava will ask no one for anything. He will live on what comes unasked, or eat vegetables and fruits.

@30
always  the Name  he will take  with whatever comes  content
in this way  conduct  makes  of the path of bhakti  the nourishment
= He will always take the Name and be content with whatever comes. Such conduct nourishes the path of devotion (*bhakti-dharma*).

@31
than grass  even  more humble
a tree  like  forbearing
seeking no honor  giving honor
to be glorified  always  Hari
= “More humble than a blade of grass, as forbearing as a tree, seeking no honor and giving honor to others — so one should always glorify Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka 3)

@32
upraised  arms  making  I say  listen  all  people
of the Name  on the thread  stringing  wear  on the neck  this  verse
= With arms upraised I say: Listen, everyone! String this verse on the thread of the Name and wear it on your neck.

@33
by the Lord’s order  do  of this  verse  the practice
surely  you will attain  then  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  the feet
= By the Lord’s order, practice what this verse teaches; then you will surely attain the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

@34
then  the Lord  of Śrīvāsa  in the house  constantly
at night  saṅkīrtana  performed  one  year
= Then for one whole year the Lord performed *saṅkīrtana* every night in Śrīvāsa’s house.

@35
the doors  closing  kīrtana  they do  in deepest  absorption
the unbelievers  to mock  come  not  get  entrance
= With the doors closed they performed *kīrtana* in deepest absorption. Unbelievers came to mock but could not get in.

@36
the kīrtana  hearing  outside  they  burning  scorching  die
to Śrīvāsa  trouble  to give  various  schemes  they make
= Hearing the *kīrtana* from outside, they burned with envy, and they devised various schemes to trouble Śrīvāsa.

@37-38
one day  a brāhmaṇa  by name  Gopāla Cāpāla
among unbelievers chief  he  foul-mouthed  a chatterer
of Bhavānī’s worship  all  the articles  taking
at night  of Śrīvāsa  at the door  a spot  having smeared
= One day a brāhmaṇa named Gopāla Cāpāla — a leader among the unbelievers, foul-mouthed and a chatterer — took all the articles for the worship of Bhavānī (Durgā). At night he smeared a spot at Śrīvāsa’s door,

@39
of a banana  a leaf  upon  he placed  oḍa flowers
turmeric  vermilion  and  red sandalwood  rice
= and on a banana leaf he placed *oḍa* (hibiscus) flowers, turmeric, vermilion and red sandalwood, and rice.

@40
a pot of wine  beside  setting  his own  home  he went
in the morning  Śrīvāsa  that  indeed  saw
= Setting a pot of wine beside them, he went home. In the morning Śrīvāsa saw it all.

@41
the leading  people  he brought  having called
to all  says  Śrīvāsa  smiling  smiling
= He called the leading men of the town and, smiling broadly, said to them all:

@42
every  night  do  I  Bhavānī’s worship
my  glory  see  brāhmaṇas  gentlemen
= “Every night I worship Bhavānī. See my glory, O respectable brāhmaṇas!”

@43
then  all  the gentlemen  make  cries of dismay
such  a deed  here  did  what  wicked man
= Then all the gentlemen cried out in dismay: “What wicked man has done such a thing here?”

@44
a sweeper  bringing  all  away  they had removed
water and cow dung  with  that  spot  they had smeared
= They called a sweeper and had everything removed, and had the spot purified with water and cow dung.

@45
three  days  after  that  Gopāla Cāpāla
all over his body  was  leprosy  flows  blood in streams
= Three days later that Gopāla Cāpāla was stricken with leprosy all over his body, and blood flowed in streams.

@46
his whole body  covered  worms  bite  incessantly
unbearable  pain  in suffering  burns  his heart
= Worms covered his whole body and gnawed at him incessantly. The pain was unbearable, and his heart burned with suffering.

@47
at a Gaṅgā ghāṭa  under a tree  he stays  sitting
one day  says  something  the Lord  seeing
= He stayed sitting under a tree by a bathing place on the Gaṅgā. One day, seeing the Lord, he said:

@48
by village  relation  I  Your  maternal uncle
nephew  I  by the disease of leprosy  have become  tormented
= “By village relationship I am Your maternal uncle. Nephew, I am tormented by leprosy.

@49
people  all  to deliver  Your  descent
I  very  miserable  me  make  deliverance
= You have descended to deliver all people. I am in great misery — deliver me!”

@50
this  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  became  angry  the mind
in anger  says  to him  words of rebuke
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu grew angry, and in His anger He spoke words of rebuke to him:

@51
O  sinner  hater of devotees  you  not  I will deliver
ten million births  in this way  by worms  I will have eaten
= “O sinner, hater of devotees, I will not deliver you! For ten million births I will have you eaten by worms like this.

@52
Śrīvāsa  made to do  you  Bhavānī’s worship
ten million  births  will be  your  into Raurava  fall
= You made Śrīvāsa out to be a worshiper of Bhavānī. For ten million births you will fall into the hell Raurava.

@53
unbelievers  to destroy  My  this  descent
unbelievers  destroying  bhakti  I will make  spread
= I have descended to destroy the unbelievers. Destroying the unbelievers, I shall spread devotion.”

@54
so  saying  went  the Lord  to do  bathing in the Gaṅgā
that  sinner  suffering  endures  not  goes  his life
= Saying this, the Lord went to bathe in the Gaṅgā. The sinner went on suffering, yet his life did not leave him.

@55-56
sannyāsa  having taken  when  the Lord  to Nīlācala  went
there  from  when  to Kuliyā  village  He came
then  that  sinner  of the Lord  took  shelter
beneficial  instruction  He gave  becoming  compassionate
= After taking *sannyāsa*, the Lord went to Nīlācala; and when He came from there to the village of Kuliyā, that sinner took shelter of Him. Moved by compassion, the Lord gave him beneficial instruction:

@57
Śrīvāsa  Paṇḍita’s  in the place  there is  an offense
there  go  he  if  does  mercy
= “You have offended Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita. Go to him; if he shows you mercy,

@58
then  your  will be  this  sin’s release
if  again  such  not  you do  act
= then you will be freed from this sin — if you never act like this again.”

@59
then  the brāhmaṇa  coming  took  Śrīvāsa’s shelter
his  by the mercy  there was  from sin  release
= Then the brāhmaṇa came and took shelter of Śrīvāsa, and by his mercy he was freed from the sin.

@60
another  one  brāhmaṇa  came  the kīrtana  to see
at the door  the panels (shut)  not  he could  inside  to go
= Another brāhmaṇa came to see the *kīrtana*, but the doors were shut, and he could not get in.

@61
returning  went  the brāhmaṇa  home  in mind  grief  getting
next  day  to the Lord  says  at the Gaṅgā  seeing (Him)
= The brāhmaṇa went home aggrieved. The next day, seeing the Lord at the Gaṅgā, he said to Him:

@62
I will curse  You  I  I have got  grief of mind
the sacred thread  tearing  curses  the fierce  foul-mouthed man
= “I will curse You — I have been hurt!” And tearing his sacred thread, that fierce, foul-mouthed man cursed Him:

@63
worldly  happiness  Your  let there be  destruction
the curse  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  was  delight
= “May Your worldly happiness be destroyed!” Hearing the curse, Mahāprabhu was delighted.

@64
of the Lord  of the curse  the account  hears  being  faithful
of brāhmaṇas  a curse  from  his  is  protection
= One who hears with faith the account of the Lord’s curse is protected from the curse of brāhmaṇas.
