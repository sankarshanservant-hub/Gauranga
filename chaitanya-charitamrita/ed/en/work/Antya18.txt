# 25 bhinna-setuḥ: of the elephant (“having broken through the dikes”) and of Kṛṣṇa (“beyond the bounds of worldly convention”), as in the Anubhāṣya and the Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya.

@title Śrī Caitanya in the Waters of the Ocean

@1
of the autumn  moonlit  ocean  by the sight  arisen  the Yamunā
from the mistaking  running  who  into this  from Hari  separation’s  burning  ocean  as if
plunged  unconscious  in the water  remaining  the night  whole
at dawn  found  by His own  may protect  He  Śacī’s  son  here  us
= Seeing the ocean in the light of the autumn moon, He took it for the Yamunā and ran into it. He plunged into it as if into an ocean of the burning pain of separation from Hari, lay unconscious in its waters the whole night, and at dawn was found by His own devotees. May that son of Śacī protect us here.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  Mahāprabhu  in Nīlācala  stays
night  and day  of Kṛṣṇa  separation  in the ocean  floats
= In this way Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala, floating night and day in the ocean of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@4
autumn  season’s  night  all  with moonlight  bright
the Lord  His own  companions  taking  wanders  night  whole
= It was an autumn night, bright everywhere with moonlight, and the Lord wandered the whole night with His companions.

@5
in garden  after garden  wanders  wonders  to see
of the rāsa  pastime  songs  verses  reciting  hearing
= He wandered from garden to garden, watching wonders and reciting and hearing songs and verses about the *rāsa* dance.

@6
the Lord  absorbed in love  does  singing  dancing
sometimes  absorbed in love  of the rāsa  pastime  imitation
= Absorbed in love, the Lord sang and danced, and at times, absorbed in love, He enacted the *rāsa-līlā*.

@7
sometimes  in the madness of feeling  the Lord  here and there  runs
on the ground  falling  sometimes  swoons  sometimes  rolling  goes
= Sometimes, in the madness of ecstatic feeling, the Lord ran here and there; sometimes He fell to the ground and swooned, and sometimes He rolled about.

@8
of the rāsa  pastime  one  verse  when  recites  hears
as before  then  the meaning  gives  Himself
= Whenever He recited or heard a verse about the *rāsa-līlā*, He Himself would explain its meaning, as before.

@9
in this way  in the rāsa-līlā  are  as many  verses
of all  the meaning  gives  feels  sometimes  joy  sorrow
= In this way He explained the meaning of every verse about the *rāsa-līlā*, feeling now joy, now sorrow.

@10
of those  all  verses  the meaning  those  all  transformations
those  all  to describe  the book  becomes  very  long
= To describe the meaning of all those verses and all those ecstatic transformations would make the book far too long.

@11
in twelve  years  whatever  pastimes  moment  by moment
of excessive  length  for fear  in the book  not  did I  write
= For fear of making the book too long, I have not written down all the pastimes that took place moment by moment during those twelve years.

@12
before  as  I have shown  the direction  indicating
so  know  of the transformations  of the ravings  the description
= As I have only pointed out the direction before, so take this description of His ecstatic transformations and ravings.

@13
with a thousand  mouths  if  speaks  Ananta
of one  day’s  pastimes  still  not  finds  the end
= Even if Ananta were to describe them with His thousand mouths, He could not reach the end of the pastimes of a single day.

@14
ten million  ages  for  if  were to write  Gaṇeśa
of one  day’s  pastimes  still  not  finds  the end
= Even if Gaṇeśa were to write for ten million ages, he could not come to the end of the pastimes of a single day.

@15
of the devotee  love’s  transformations  seeing  Kṛṣṇa’s  wonder
Kṛṣṇa  whose  not  finds  end  who  insignificant  else
= Seeing the transformations of love in His devotee, even Kṛṣṇa is struck with wonder! When Kṛṣṇa Himself cannot find their end, what of anyone else, so insignificant?

@16-17
of the devotee’s  love  which  states  which  courses  kinds
as much  sorrow  as much  happiness  as many  transformations
Kṛṣṇa  that  fully  not  can  know
a devotee’s  mood  accepts  that  to taste
= Kṛṣṇa cannot fully know all the states of His devotee’s love, its courses and its kinds, all its sorrow, all its happiness and all its transformations. So, to taste them, He accepts the mood of a devotee.

@18
Kṛṣṇa  makes dance  love  the devotee  makes dance
itself  dances  the three  dance  in one place
= Love makes Kṛṣṇa dance and makes the devotee dance, and it dances itself: all three dance together in one place.

@19
of love  the transformations  to describe  wants  whoever  person
the moon  to catch  wants  as if  being  a dwarf
= Whoever wants to describe the transformations of love is like a dwarf who wants to catch the moon.

@20
the wind  as  of the ocean’s  water  carries off  one  drop
of Kṛṣṇa  love  a drop  so  of the living being  the touch
= As the wind carries off a single drop of the ocean’s water, so a living being can touch only a drop of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@21
moment  by moment  rise  of love  waves  endless
a living being  insignificant  how  their  will find  end
= Moment by moment endless waves of love rise up. How could an insignificant living being ever find their end?

@22
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  what  does  tasting
only  alone  know  that  Svarūpa  and the others  the circle
= What Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya tasted only Svarūpa and the others of His inner circle know.

@23
a living being  being  makes  whoever  of it  description
himself  to purify  of it  touches  one  drop
= If anyone, being an ordinary living being, describes it, he only touches a single drop of it, to purify himself.

@24
in this way  of the rāsa  verses  all  recited
at the end  of the water  pastimes  a verse  to recite  began
= In this way the Lord recited all the verses about the *rāsa* dance, and at the end He began to recite a verse about the water pastimes:

@25
with them  accompanied  fatigue  to remove  of bodies  by the contact
crushed  garland’s  He  from the breasts  with kuṅkuma  reddened
by bees like the lords of the Gandharvas  followed  entered  the water
weary  with she-elephants  the king of elephants  like  having broken the dikes
= “Weary, Kṛṣṇa entered the water with the gopīs to relieve His fatigue, as a tired king of elephants, having broken through the dikes, enters the water with his she-elephants: for He too had passed beyond the bounds of worldly convention. Bees followed Him, singing like the lords of the Gandharvas, drawn by His garland, crushed by the contact of their bodies and reddened with the *kuṅkuma* from their breasts.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.22)

@26
in this way  Mahāprabhu  wandering  wandering
Āiṭoṭā  from  the ocean  sees  suddenly
= Wandering about in this way, Mahāprabhu suddenly saw the ocean from Āiṭoṭā.

@27
in the moon’s  radiance  surging  waves  bright
glittering  make  as if  the Yamunā’s  water
= The surging waves, bright in the moonlight, glittered like the waters of the Yamunā.

@28
for the Yamunā  mistaking  the Lord  running  went
unnoticed  going  into the ocean’s  water  a leap  gave
= Taking it for the Yamunā, the Lord ran towards it and, unnoticed by anyone, leapt into the ocean.

@29
on falling  there was  a swoon  nothing  not  knows
sometimes  sink  sometimes  float  of waves  the host
= As soon as He fell in, He swooned and knew nothing. The waves now sank Him beneath the water, now raised Him to the surface.

@30
by the waves  carried  drifts  like  a dry  log
who  to understand  can  this  Caitanya’s  drama
= Carried by the waves, He drifted about like a dry log. Who can understand this drama of Caitanya?

@31
of Koṇārka  towards  the Lord  the waves  taking  go
sometimes  sinking  keep  sometimes  floating  taking  go
= The waves carried the Lord towards Koṇārka, sometimes keeping Him under the water, sometimes bearing Him along on the surface.

@32
in the Yamunā  water  pastimes  the gopīs  with
Kṛṣṇa  performs  Mahāprabhu  absorbed  in that  play
= Kṛṣṇa was enjoying water pastimes with the gopīs in the Yamunā, and Mahāprabhu was absorbed in that play.

@33
here  Svarūpa  and the others  the Lord  not  seeing
where  has gone  the Lord  say  startled  being
= Here, not seeing the Lord, Svarūpa and the others were startled and said, “Where has the Lord gone?”

@34
with the speed of the mind  went  the Lord  to see  could not
the Lord  not  seeing  doubts  to make  began
= The Lord had gone with the speed of the mind, and they could not see Him go. Not seeing Him, they began to wonder:

@35
Jagannātha  to see  perhaps  to the temple  went
in another  garden  or  in madness  fell
= “Has He gone to the temple to see Jagannātha? Or has He fallen down somewhere in another garden, in His madness?

@36
to the Guṇḍicā  temple  went  or  to Narendra
to Caṭaka  hill  went  or  to Koṇārka
= Has He gone to the Guṇḍicā temple, or to Narendra Lake? To Caṭaka-parvata, or to Koṇārka?”

@37
this  saying  all  wander  the Lord  looking for
of the ocean  to the shore  came  some  people  taking
= Saying this, they all went about looking for the Lord, and then came to the seashore, taking a few others with them.

@38
searching  wandering  thus  of the night  the end  came
departure  has made  the Lord  conclusion  made
= As they wandered about searching like this, the night came to an end, and they concluded, “The Lord has departed.”

@39
the Lord’s  in separation  whose  in the body  not  life
of harm  fear  except  whose  in the mind  not  other
= In separation from the Lord they all felt as if life had left their bodies, and no one could think of anything but the fear that some harm had come to Him.

@40
harm  fearing  of loved ones  hearts  are  indeed
= “The hearts of those who love always fear some harm.” (Abhijñāna-śakuntala, 4)
