# English names and terms — CC Antya 11, verses 1–108 (stage A; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 11»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 11») are used as given there: *rañca* (a particle);
*gaura-deha* (He whose body shines with a golden hue — here an attribute of Kṛṣṇa, verse 5); *Caitanyera ārya* (the one
whom Mahāprabhu honors, verse 7); *śrāddha-pātra* (the plate of the *śrāddha* feast, verse 30); *mahā-tīrtha* (a great holy
place, verse 64); *ḍora*, *kaḍāra* (verse 66); *cāṅgaḍā*, *pichāḍā*, *puñjā* (verses 75–78); *ācamana* (verse 89);
*vijaya* (departure, verses 91, 101); *parama-vidvān* (verse 105); Bhīṣma’s departure (verse 57).
Verbatim from revised chapters (`ed/en-rev/`): verse 108 (= Antya 2.172); line 1 of 10 (Ādi 9.5), line 2 of 12 (Antya 9.6),
line 2 of 44 (Antya 1.102), line 1 of 67 (Madhya 12.137), line 1 of 84 (Antya 7.66), line 1 of 85 (Madhya 11.201),
line 1 of 106 (Madhya 18.228).
