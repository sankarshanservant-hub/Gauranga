@title The Wish-fulfilling Tree of Devotion

@1
that  glorious  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya-
Lord  I worship  the guru of the world
by whose mercy  even a dog
the great ocean  can cross  easily
= I worship that glorious Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the guru of the world, by whose mercy even a dog can easily cross the great ocean.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  Gauracandra
glory  glory to Advaita  glory  glory  to Nityānanda
= All glory, all glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! Glory, glory to Advaita! Glory, glory to Nityānanda!

@3
glory  glory  Śrīvāsa and the others  Gaura's devotees
of all desires  of the fulfillment  the cause  of whom  the remembrance
= Glory, glory to Gaura's devotees headed by Śrīvāsa, the remembrance of whom fulfills all desires!

@4
Śrī Rūpa  Sanātana  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha
Śrī Jīva  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa  Dāsa  Raghunātha
= Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha dāsa —

@5
by the grace of all these  I write  of Caitanya's pastimes and qualities
I know  or  not  know  I do  my own purification
= by the grace of all these I write of Śrī Caitanya's pastimes and qualities. Whether I understand them or not, I write for my own purification.

@6
the gardener  Himself  of Kṛṣṇa-
love the immortal tree  Himself
the giver  the enjoyer  of its fruits
who  that  Caitanya  I take shelter of
= I take shelter of Śrī Caitanya, who is Himself the gardener and Himself the immortal tree of love for Kṛṣṇa, and who both gives and enjoys its fruits.

@7
the Lord  says  I  Viśvambhara  the name  bear
the name  meaningful  becomes  if  with love  the universe  I fill
= The Lord said, “I bear the name Viśvambhara, ‘He who fills and sustains the universe.’ The name will be fulfilled only if I fill the universe with love.”

@8
thus  thinking  took up  the Lord  the gardener's role
in Navadvīpa  He began  the work of an orchard
= Thinking this, the Lord took up the gardener's role and in Navadvīpa began the work of an orchard.

@9
Śrī Caitanya  the gardener  to the earth  bringing
the wish-fulfilling tree of devotion  planted  watering  with the water of His will
= Śrī Caitanya, the gardener, brought the wish-fulfilling tree of devotion (*bhakti-kalpataru*) to the earth and planted it, watering it with His own will.

@10
glory  Śrī  Mādhava Purī  brimming with love for Kṛṣṇa
of the wish-fulfilling tree of devotion  he  the first  sprout
= Glory to Śrī Mādhava Purī, brimming with love for Kṛṣṇa! He is the first sprout of the wish-fulfilling tree of devotion.

@11
in the form of Śrī Īśvara Purī  the sprout  strong  became
Himself  Caitanya the gardener  the trunk  appeared as
= In the form of Śrī Īśvara Purī the sprout grew strong, and Caitanya the gardener Himself appeared as the trunk.

@12
by His own inconceivable energy  the gardener  being  the trunk  becomes
of all  branches  that  trunk  the root and support
= By His own inconceivable energy the gardener also became the trunk, and that trunk is the root and support of all the branches.

@13-15
Paramānanda  Purī  and  Keśava  Bhāratī
Brahmānanda  Purī  and  Brahmānanda  Bhāratī
Viṣṇu Purī  Keśava Purī  Purī  Kṛṣṇānanda
Śrī Nṛsiṁha Tīrtha  and  Purī  Sukhānanda
these  nine  roots  sprang forth  at the base of the tree
on these  nine  roots  the tree  made  steady
= Paramānanda Purī and Keśava Bhāratī, Brahmānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī, Viṣṇu Purī, Keśava Purī, Kṛṣṇānanda Purī, Śrī Nṛsiṁha Tīrtha and Sukhānanda Purī — these nine roots sprang forth at the base of the tree, and on these nine roots the tree stood steady.

@16
the central root  Paramānanda Purī  deeply steady
on these  nine  roots  the tree  made  firm
= The central root is Paramānanda Purī, steady and wise. On these nine roots the tree stood firm.

@17
the trunk's  above  many  branches  sprang forth
higher  higher  branches  countless  became
= Above the trunk many branches sprang forth, and higher and higher they grew beyond counting.

@18
twenty  twenty  branches  forming  each  each  cluster
mighty  mighty branches  covered  the universe  all
= With twenty branches forming each cluster, the mighty branches covered the whole universe.

@19
on each  branch  sub-branches  hundreds  hundreds
as many as  grew  branches  who  will count  how many
= On each branch grew hundreds and hundreds of sub-branches. Who can count all the branches that grew?

@20
principal  principal  of the branches  the listing of names
later  indeed  I shall make  hear  of the tree  the description
= I shall list the names of the principal branches later; now hear the description of the tree.

@21
of the branches  above  became  the tree  two  trunks
one  Advaita  by name  the other  Nityānanda
= Above the branching, the tree became two trunks: one named Advaita, the other Nityānanda.

@22
on those  two trunks  branches  as many as  grew
their  sub-branches  the world  covered
= From those two trunks grew many branches, and their sub-branches covered the world.

@23
great  branches  sub-branches  their  sub-branches
the world  pervaded  of them  who  will make  a record
= Great branches, sub-branches and their sub-branches pervaded the world. Who could write them all down?

@24
disciples  disciples of disciples  and  followers
the world  pervaded  of them  there is no  counting
= Disciples, disciples of disciples and their followers have pervaded the world; there is no counting them.

@25
the udumbara tree  as  bears fruit  on all  parts
in this  way  on the tree of devotion  everywhere  fruit  appears
= As the udumbara fig bears fruit on every part, so fruit appears everywhere on the tree of devotion.

@26
of the main trunk  on the branches and sub-branches
grew  which  the fruit of love  nectar  surpasses
= The fruit of love that grew on the branches and sub-branches of the main trunk surpasses nectar.

@27
ripened  which  the fruit of love  nectar-sweet
distributes  Caitanya the gardener  not  takes  price
= When the fruits of love ripened, sweet as nectar, Caitanya the gardener distributed them without taking any price.

@28
in the three worlds  as much as  there is  wealth and jewels
of one fruit  the price  as  that  not  I count
= All the wealth and jewels in the three worlds I do not reckon as the price of a single fruit.

@29
asks  or  not  asks  anyone  worthy  or  unworthy
of this  consideration  not  He knows  He gives  only
= Whether one asks or not, whether one is worthy or unworthy, He knows no such consideration; He simply gives.

@30
handful  handful  filling  He throws  in the four directions
the poor  gathering  eat  the gardener  smiles
= Filling His cupped hands again and again, He throws the fruits in all four directions. The poor gather them and eat, and the gardener smiles.

@31
the gardener  says  hear  family of the tree
main branches and sub-branches  of every  kind
= The gardener said, “Listen, family of the tree, all you main branches and sub-branches of every kind!

@32
extraordinary  the tree  performs  the work of all the senses
stationary  being  holds  of moving beings  the nature
= This extraordinary tree performs the work of all the senses: though stationary, it has the nature of moving beings.

@33
this  tree's  parts  are  all  conscious
growing  have pervaded  all  all  the worlds
= All the parts of this tree are conscious, and as they grow, they spread through all the worlds.

@34
alone  the gardener  I  where  where  shall go
alone  or  how many  fruits  picking  shall distribute
= I am the only gardener. How many places can I go? How many fruits can I pick and distribute alone?

@35
alone  picking up  to give  there is  hard labor
some  receive  some  not  receive  remains  in the mind  uncertainty
= It is hard work to pick them up and give them out alone, and I am left uncertain: some may receive them, and some may not.

@36
therefore  I  order  have given  to all
anywhere  everywhere  the fruit of love  give  to whomever  to anyone
= Therefore I give all of you this order: give the fruit of love anywhere and everywhere, to anyone and everyone.

@37
alone  the gardener  I  how many  fruits  shall eat
not  giving  or  this  fruit  else  what  shall I do
= I am the only gardener. How many fruits can I eat? And if I do not give this fruit away, what else would I do with it?

@38
with the nectar of My own will  the tree  I water  constantly
thereby  countless  fruits  of the tree  upon
= I water the tree constantly with the nectar of My own will, and so countless fruits grow upon it.

@39
therefore  all  fruits  give  to whomever  to anyone
having eaten  let become  people  free from old age  free from death
= Therefore give all the fruits to anyone and everyone. Let people eat them and become free from old age and death.

@40
the world  pervading  My  will be  holy  fame
happy  becoming  people  My  will sing  glory
= Then My holy fame will pervade the world, and people, made happy, will sing My glory.

@41
in the land of Bhārata  has come  human birth  whose
the birth  fruitful  making  do  welfare of others
= One who has taken human birth in the land of Bhārata should make his birth fruitful by working for the welfare of others.

@42
this much  the success of birth
of the embodied  here  among the embodied
with life  with wealth  with intelligence  with words
the true good  the practice  always
= “This is the fulfillment of birth for embodied beings in this world: always to act for the true good of others with one's life, wealth, intelligence and words.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.22.35)

@43
of living beings  for the welfare
which  alone  here  in the next world  and
by deed  by mind  by word
that  alone  the intelligent  should do
= “By deed, mind and word, an intelligent person should do only what serves the welfare of living beings in this world and the next.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 3.12.45)

@44
a gardener  a man  Mine  is not  kingdom or wealth
fruits and flowers  giving  I make  merit  earning
= I am only a gardener; I have no kingdom or wealth. By giving fruits and flowers I earn merit (*puṇya*).

@45
the gardener  being  the tree  I became  this  indeed  by the wish
of all living beings  welfare  is  the tree  from
= Though I am the gardener, I have also become the tree, with this very wish: that all living beings may benefit from the tree.

@46
oh  of these  excellent  birth
sustaining all living beings
of a good man  like  from whom  indeed
turned away  go  not  those who ask
= “Oh, how excellent is the birth of these trees, which sustain all living beings! Like a good-hearted man, they never send away disappointed anyone who comes to them in need.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.22.33)”

@47
this  order  gave  when  Caitanya the gardener
supreme  joy  attained  the family of the tree
= When Caitanya the gardener gave this order, the family of the tree felt supreme joy.

@48
who  where  there  gift  makes  the fruit of love
by tasting the fruit  intoxicated  people  became  all
= Each of them gave away the fruit of love here and there, and by tasting the fruit all people became intoxicated.

@49
greatly intoxicating  the fruit of love  the belly  filling  eat
became maddened  all  people  laugh  dance  sing
= People ate their fill of the highly intoxicating fruit of love, and everyone became maddened: they laugh, dance and sing.

@50
some  rolling about  go  some  indeed  roaring
seeing  delighted  becoming  smiles  the gardener
= Some roll on the ground, some roar; seeing this, the gardener smiles with delight.

@51
this  gardener  eats  this  fruit of love
always  intoxicated  remains  helpless and overwhelmed
= This gardener Himself eats this fruit of love and remains always intoxicated, helpless and overwhelmed.

@52
all people  intoxicated  He made  equal to Himself
with love  intoxicated  people  except  not  I see  others
= He made everyone as intoxicated as Himself; I see no one but people maddened with love.

@53
who  who  earlier  criticism  made  calling  a drunkard
those  the fruit  eat  dance  say  good  good
= Those who earlier had criticized Him, calling Him a drunkard, now eat the fruit, dance and say, “Wonderful, wonderful!”

@54
this  indeed  I have told  the distribution of the fruit of love
now  hear  the givers of the fruit  which  which  branches
= Thus I have told of the distribution of the fruit of love. Now hear which branches give the fruit.

@55
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  at the feet  whose  hope
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, tell the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
