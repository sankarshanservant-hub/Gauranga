@title Instructions to Sanātana Gosvāmī

@1
I bow down  to Him whose opulence is unlimited and wonderful
to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu
a lowly one  even  by whose mercy  may become
of the scriptures of devotion  a promulgator
= I bow down to Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu, whose opulence is unlimited and wonderful. By His mercy even a lowly person may become a promulgator of the scriptures of devotion.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
here  in Gauḍa  Sanātana  was  in prison
of Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī  a letter  came  at that time
= Meanwhile, in Gauḍa, Sanātana was still in prison when a letter from Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī reached him.

@4
the letter  receiving  Sanātana  delighted  became
to the yavana jailer  to speak  he began
= Sanātana was delighted to receive the letter, and he began to speak to the yavana who guarded him:

@5
you  are a  living saint (jindā-pīr)  greatly fortunate
in the books and the Koran  the scriptures  is  your  knowledge
= “You are a *jindā-pīr*, a living saint, and greatly fortunate. You are learned in the Koran and the other scriptures.

@6
one  prisoner  releases  if  one’s own religion  regarding
from worldly existence  him  liberated  makes  the Lord
= If someone, true to his own religion, releases a prisoner, the Lord frees him from worldly existence.

@7
before  I  for you  have done  favors
you  me  releasing  do  a favor in return
= I have done you favors in the past. Now release me and return the favor.

@8
five  thousand  coins  you  make  acceptance
merit  wealth  two  gains  will be  yours
= Please accept five thousand coins. You will gain twice over — merit and wealth.”

@9
then  that  yavana  says  listen  sir (mahāśaya)
you  I would release  but  I have  fear of the king
= The yavana replied, “Listen, sir. I would release you, but I fear the king.”

@10-11
Sanātana  says  you  do not  have  fear of the king
to the south  he has gone  if  coming back  he returns
to him  say  he  to relieve himself  went
of the Ganges  near  the Ganges  seeing  a leap  he made
= Sanātana said, “Do not fear the king. He has gone south. If he comes back, tell him that I went out to relieve myself and, coming near the Ganges and seeing it, jumped in.

@12
much  you looked  his  trace  not  you found
with the fetters  drowning  somewhere  carried away  he went
= Tell him that you searched long but found no trace of me — that I drowned with my fetters on and was carried away somewhere.

@13
any  fear  there is not  I  in this land  will not  stay
a dervish  becoming  I  to Mecca  will go
= There is nothing to fear: I shall not stay in this land. I shall become a dervish and go to Mecca.”

@14
still  of the yavana  the mind  pleased  not  he saw
seven thousand  coins  before him  in a heap  he made
= Still Sanātana saw that the yavana was not satisfied, so he heaped seven thousand coins before him.

@15
greed  arose  of the yavana  the coins  seeing
at night  the crossing of the Ganges  he made  the fetters  cutting
= At the sight of the coins the yavana grew greedy. That night he cut off Sanātana’s fetters and took him across the Ganges.

@16
the road by the fort gate  he gave up  he could not  there  go
night and day  walking  he came  to the Pātaḍā hills
= Sanātana avoided the road through the fort gate, for he could not go that way. Walking night and day, he came to the Pātaḍā hills.

@17
there  a  landholder (bhaumika)  was  to him  he went
the hills  across  take  me  a request  he made
= There lived a landholder (*bhaumika*), a chief of robbers. Sanātana went to him and humbly asked, “Please take me across the hills.”

@18
of that landholder  with him  was  a palmist
in the landholder’s  ear  says  he  knowing  this  matter
= With that landholder was a palmist. Perceiving the matter, he whispered into the landholder’s ear,

@19
with this man  of gold  eight  mohurs  there are
hearing  delighted  the landholder  to Sanātana  says
= “This man has eight gold mohurs.” Hearing this, the landholder was delighted and said to Sanātana,

@20
tonight  the hills  crossing  I will make  my own men  giving
a meal  take  you  cooking  having done
= “Tonight I will have my own men take you across the hills. Meanwhile, cook and have your meal.”

@21
so  saying  grain  he gave  showing  respect
Sanātana  going  then  made  a bath in the river
= With these words he respectfully gave him food grains, and Sanātana went and bathed in the river.

@22
after a fast of two days  he did  cooking and eating
the royal minister  Sanātana  reflected  in his mind
= Having fasted for two days, he cooked and ate. But Sanātana, once a royal minister, reflected in his mind:

@23
this  landholder  why  to me  respect  has shown
thus  thinking  Sanātana  Īśāna  asked
= “Why has this landholder shown me such respect?” Thinking this, Sanātana asked Īśāna,

@24
with you  I know  some  valuables  there are
Īśāna  says  with me  seven  mohurs  there are
= “I know you have some valuables with you.” Īśāna replied, “I have seven mohurs.”

@25
hearing  Sanātana  him  made  rebuke
with you  why  have you brought  this  death (kāla-yama)
= Hearing this, Sanātana rebuked him: “Why have you brought this death along with you?”

@26
then  those  seven  mohurs  in his hand  taking
the landholder  near  going  says  the mohurs  holding out
= Then he took the seven mohurs in his hand, went to the landholder and, holding out the coins, said,

@27
these  seven  gold  mohurs  were  mine
this  taking  religion  regarding  the hills  make  crossing
= “I had these seven gold mohurs. Take them, and as a matter of religious duty take me across the hills.

@28
a royal prisoner  I am  by the fort gate  to go  not  I can
merit  there will be  the hills  me  give  crossing having made
= I am the king’s prisoner and cannot go by the fort gate. You will earn merit — take me across the hills.”

@29
the landholder  smiling  says  I  had known  before
eight  mohurs  there are  of your  servant  in the hem of the cloth
= The landholder smiled and said, “I knew it already: your servant has eight mohurs tied in the hem of his cloth.

@30
you  killing  the mohurs  I would have taken  of today  night
good  it was  told  you  I was freed  from sin
= Tonight I would have killed you and taken the mohurs. It is good that you told me — I have been spared that sin.

@31
satisfied  have become  I  the mohurs  not  I will take
of merit  for the sake  the hills  you  across taking  I will give
= I am pleased. I will not take the mohurs. For the sake of merit alone I will take you across the hills.”

@32
the Gosvāmī  says  someone  the valuables  will take  me  killing
my  life  protection  do  the valuables  accepting
= Sanātana Gosvāmī said, “Someone else will kill me for these valuables. Accept them and save my life.”

@33
then  the landholder  with the Gosvāmī  four  armed men  gave
night after night  by forest paths  the hills  across  took
= Then the landholder sent four armed men with him, and night after night they led him across the hills along forest paths.

@34
across  having come  the Gosvāmī  then  asked  Īśāna
I know  remaining  valuables  some  are  with you
= Having crossed, Sanātana Gosvāmī asked Īśāna, “I know you still have something left with you.”

@35
Īśāna  says  one  mohur  is  remaining
the Gosvāmī  says  the mohur  taking  go  you  home
= Īśāna replied, “One mohur is left.” Sanātana Gosvāmī said, “Take the mohur and go home.”

@36
him  leave  giving  the Gosvāmī  went on  alone
in his hand  a water pot  a torn  quilt  free from fear  he became
= Sending him away, Sanātana Gosvāmī went on alone. With nothing but a water pot in his hand and a torn quilt, he was now free from fear.

@37
walking  walking  the Gosvāmī  then  came  to Hājipura
in the evening  he sat down  a  garden  within
= Walking on and on, Sanātana Gosvāmī came to Hājipura, and in the evening he sat down in a garden.

@38
in that  Hājipura  lived  Śrīkānta  his  name
of the Gosvāmī  the sister’s husband  he does  the king’s work
= In that Hājipura lived the Gosvāmī’s brother-in-law, his sister’s husband Śrīkānta, who was in the king’s service.

@39
three  lakhs  of coins  the king  had given  to him
horses  the price  taking  he sends  to the emperor
= The king had given him three hundred thousand coins; with this money he bought horses and sent them to the emperor.

@40
a tower  on  sitting  he  the Gosvāmī  saw
at night  with one man  to the Gosvāmī  he came
= Sitting on a tower, Śrīkānta saw Sanātana Gosvāmī, and at night he came to him with one attendant.

@41
the two  meeting  there  an intimate talk  had
of the imprisonment and release  the story  the Gosvāmī  all  told
= The two met there and talked intimately, and Sanātana Gosvāmī told him the whole story of his imprisonment and release.

@42
he  says  a couple of days  stay  in this  place
become presentable  give up  these  dirty  clothes
= Śrīkānta said, “Stay here a couple of days. Make yourself presentable and give up these dirty clothes.”

@43
the Gosvāmī  says  even a moment  here  not  I will stay
the Ganges  across  take me  at once  I will go
= Sanātana Gosvāmī said, “I will not stay here even a moment. Take me across the Ganges; I must go at once.”

@44
with care  he  a  woollen Bhoṭa blanket  gave
the Ganges  across  he took  the Gosvāmī  went on
= Śrīkānta lovingly gave him a woollen Bhoṭa blanket and took him across the Ganges, and Sanātana Gosvāmī went on his way.

@45
then  to Vārāṇasī  the Gosvāmī  came  in some days
hearing  delighted  he became  of the Lord’s  arrival
= After some days Sanātana Gosvāmī reached Vārāṇasī, and he was overjoyed to hear that the Lord had arrived there.

@46
to Candraśekhara’s  house  coming  at the door  he sat
Mahāprabhu  knowing  to Candraśekhara  said
= He came to Candraśekhara’s house and sat down at the door. Mahāprabhu knew this and said to Candraśekhara,

@47
at the door  a  Vaiṣṇava  there is  call  him
Candraśekhara  sees  a Vaiṣṇava  there is not  at the door
= “There is a Vaiṣṇava at the door. Call him in.” Candraśekhara looked, but saw no Vaiṣṇava at the door.

@48
at the door  a Vaiṣṇava  there is not  to the Lord  he said
anyone  is there  saying  the Lord  him  asked
= He told the Lord, “There is no Vaiṣṇava at the door.” The Lord asked him, “Is anyone there at all?”

@49
he  says  a  dervish  is  at the door
him  bring  at the Lord’s  word  he said  coming  to him
= He replied, “There is a dervish at the door.” “Bring him in,” said the Lord, and at His word Candraśekhara came out and said to him,

@50
the Lord  you  is calling  come  dervish
hearing  in joy  Sanātana  made  entrance
= “The Lord is calling you. Come in, dervish!” Hearing this, Sanātana joyfully went in.

@51
him  in the courtyard  seeing  the Lord  running  came
him  an embrace  making  overwhelmed with love  He became
= Seeing him in the courtyard, the Lord came running, embraced him and was overwhelmed with love.

@52
at the Lord’s touch  overwhelmed with love  became  Sanātana
me  do not  touch  he says  in a choked  voice
= At the Lord’s touch Sanātana too was overwhelmed with love. In a voice choked with emotion he said, “Do not touch me!”

@53
the two  embracing each other  weeping  boundless
seeing  of Candraśekhara  there was  amazement
= Embracing each other, the two wept without end, and Candraśekhara was amazed to see it.

@54
then  the Lord  his  hand  taking  led him in
a raised platform  upon  at His own side  He seated him
= Then the Lord took him by the hand, led him in and seated him beside Himself on a raised platform.

@55
with His holy hand  He does  his  body  cleansing
he  says  me  O Lord  do not  make  touching
= With His own hand the Lord wiped Sanātana’s body clean. Sanātana said, “O Lord, do not touch me!”

@56
the Lord  says  you  I touch  Myself  to purify
by the power of devotion  can  you  the universe  purify
= The Lord said, “I touch you to purify Myself, for by the power of your devotion you can purify the entire universe.

@57
like you  devotees
holy places  being  yourselves,  O lord
make holy  the holy places
by Him within  who bears the club
= “Devotees such as you are themselves holy places, my lord. By the Lord who dwells within your hearts, you make even the holy places holy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.13.10)

@58
not  to Me  a nondevotee  a knower of the four Vedas
My devotee  a dog-eater  dear
to him  should be given  from him  should be accepted
he  and  is to be worshipped  as  indeed  I
= “Even a knower of the four Vedas is not dear to Me if he is not devoted to Me, but My devotee is dear to Me even if he was born a dog-eater. One should give to him and accept from him; he is to be worshipped just as I am.” (Itihāsa-samuccaya, quoted in Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 10.91)

@59
than a brāhmaṇa  twelve  qualities  endowed with  of the lotus-naveled Lord
from the lotus feet  averse  a dog-eater  better
I consider  to Him offered  mind  words  deeds  wealth
and life  purifies  he  his family  not  but  one full of pride
= “I consider a dog-eater who has offered his mind, words, deeds, wealth and life to the lotus-naveled Lord better than a brāhmaṇa endowed with the twelve qualities who is averse to the Lord’s lotus feet. Such a devotee purifies his whole family, but the brāhmaṇa full of pride does not.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.10)

@60
you  I see  you  I touch  I sing  your  qualities
of all the senses  the fruit  this  of the scriptures  the conclusion
= To see you, to touch you and to sing your qualities is the fruit of all the senses — this is the conclusion of the scriptures.

@61
of the eyes  the fruit  of one like you  the sight  indeed
of the body  the fruit  of one like you  bodily contact
of the tongue  the fruit  of one like you  the glorification  indeed
very rare  devotees of the Lord  indeed  in the world
= “The fruit of the eyes is to see one like you; the fruit of the body is to touch the body of one like you; the fruit of the tongue is to glorify one like you. Pure devotees of the Lord are very rare in this world.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 13.2)

@62
so  saying  says  the Lord  listen  Sanātana
Kṛṣṇa  very  merciful  of the fallen  the purifier
= Having said this, the Lord continued, “Listen, Sanātana. Kṛṣṇa is most merciful; He is the purifier of the fallen.

@63
from the hell Mahā-raurava  you  He made  deliverance
of mercy  the ocean  Kṛṣṇa  deep  boundless
= He has delivered you from the hell called Mahā-raurava. Kṛṣṇa is an ocean of mercy, deep and boundless.”

@64
Sanātana  says  Kṛṣṇa  I  do not  know
of my  deliverance  the cause  Your  mercy  I consider
= Sanātana replied, “I do not know Kṛṣṇa. I consider Your mercy alone the cause of my deliverance.”

@65
how  you escaped  saying  the Lord  a question  asked
from beginning to end  the whole  story  he  told
= The Lord asked, “How did you escape?” and Sanātana told Him the whole story from beginning to end.

@66
the Lord  says  your  two  brothers  in Prayāga  met Me
Rūpa  Anupama  both  to Vṛndāvana  have gone
= The Lord said, “I met your two brothers, Rūpa and Anupama, in Prayāga. Both have gone on to Vṛndāvana.”

@67
Tapana Miśra  and  Candraśekhara
at the Lord’s order  Sanātana  met  both
= At the Lord’s bidding Sanātana met both Tapana Miśra and Candraśekhara.

@68
Tapana Miśra  then  him  made  invitation
the Lord  says  a shave  have  go  Sanātana
= Tapana Miśra then invited him for his meals, and the Lord said, “Go and have a shave, Sanātana.”

@69
Candraśekhara  the Lord  says  calling
this  garb  away  put  go  him  taking
= Calling Candraśekhara, the Lord said, “Take him with you and remove this garb.”

@70
shaved  having made  him  a bath in the Ganges  had him take
Śekhara  bringing  him  new  cloth  gave
= Candraśekhara had him shaved and bathed in the Ganges, and then brought him new cloth.

@71
that  cloth  Sanātana  did not  make  acceptance
hearing  of the Lord  in the mind  joy  boundless
= Sanātana did not accept that cloth, and when the Lord heard this, His joy knew no bounds.

@72
the midday duties  having done  the Lord  went  a meal  to take
Sanātana  taking  He went  of Tapana Miśra  to the house
= Having finished His midday duties, the Lord went to take His meal, and He took Sanātana with Him to Tapana Miśra’s house.

@73
the washing of the feet  having done  for the meal  He sat
to Sanātana  food  give  to Miśra  He said
= After washing His feet He sat down for the meal and said to Miśra, “Serve Sanātana too.”

@74
Miśra  says  of Sanātana  some  duty  there is
You  the meal  take  prasāda  to him  I will give  afterwards
= Miśra said, “Sanātana has something to attend to. Please take Your meal; I will give him prasāda afterwards.”

@75
the meal  having taken  Mahāprabhu  rest  took
Miśra  of the Lord  the plate of remnants  to Sanātana  gave
= After the meal Mahāprabhu lay down to rest, and Miśra gave Sanātana the plate with the Lord’s remnants.

@76
Miśra  to Sanātana  gave  new  cloth
the cloth  not  he took  he  makes  a request
= Miśra offered Sanātana new cloth, but he would not take it. Instead he made a request:

@77
to me  cloth  to give  if  your  is  wish
of your own wear  one  give  old
= “If you wish to give me cloth, give me one of your own that is old.”

@78
then  Miśra  old  one  dhoti  gave
he  two  outer cloths and loincloths  made
= Then Miśra gave him an old dhoti, and Sanātana made it into two sets of outer cloth (*bahirvāsa*) and loincloth (*kaupīna*).

@79
to a Mahārāṣṭrian  brāhmaṇa  the Lord  introduced  Sanātana
that  brāhmaṇa  him  made  a standing invitation
= The Lord introduced Sanātana to a brāhmaṇa from Mahārāṣṭra, and that brāhmaṇa gave him a standing invitation:

@80
Sanātana  you  as long as  in Kāśī  you will stay
so long  in my  house  your meals  do  take
= “Sanātana, as long as you stay in Kāśī, please take your meals at my house.”

@81
Sanātana  says  I  mādhukarī  will do
of a brāhmaṇa  in the house  why  in one place  food  should I take
= Sanātana said, “I shall live by *mādhukarī*, begging a little from house to house. Why should I take all my meals at one brāhmaṇa’s house?”

@82
at Sanātana’s  renunciation  of the Lord  joy  boundless
the Bhoṭa blanket  toward  the Lord  looks  again  and again
= The Lord was boundlessly pleased by Sanātana’s renunciation, yet again and again He glanced at the woollen Bhoṭa blanket.

@83
Sanātana  understood  this  to the Lord  is not  pleasing
the blanket  giving up  to do  he thought of  a means
= Sanātana understood that the Lord did not like it, and he thought of a way to give the blanket up.

@84
so  thinking  he went  to the Ganges  the midday bath  to take
a  Bengali  quilt  having washed  had put  to dry
= With this in mind he went to the Ganges for his midday bath. There a Bengali had washed his quilt and spread it out to dry.

@85
to him  he says  O  brother  do  a favor
this  blanket  taking  this  quilt  give  me
= Sanātana said to him, “Brother, do me a favor: take this blanket and give me this quilt.”

@86
he  says  a joke  you make  a respectable man  being
a costly  blanket  you will give  why  a quilt  taking
= The man said, “Are you, a respectable gentleman, joking with me? Why would you give a costly blanket in exchange for a quilt?”

@87
he  says  a joke  it is not  I speak  the truth
the blanket  take  you  give  me  this quilt
= Sanātana said, “I am not joking; I am telling the truth. Take the blanket and give me your quilt.”

@88
so  saying  the quilt  he took  the blanket  to him  giving
to the Lord  he came  the quilt  over his shoulders  putting
= With these words he gave the man the blanket and took the quilt, and, throwing the quilt over his shoulders, he came back to the Lord.

@89
the Lord  says  your  Bhoṭa blanket  where  has gone
at the Lord’s feet  the whole  story  the Gosvāmī  told
= The Lord asked, “Where has your Bhoṭa blanket gone?” and at the Lord’s feet Sanātana Gosvāmī told Him the whole story.

@90-91
the Lord  says  this  I  have done  consideration
the disease of worldliness  destroyed  Kṛṣṇa  since  your
He  why  would keep  your  last  worldly enjoyment
the disease  destroying  a good physician  does not  keep  a remnant of  the disease
= The Lord said, “I have thought about this. Kṛṣṇa has destroyed your disease of worldliness — why would He leave you this last bit of worldly enjoyment? Once a good physician has cured a disease, he leaves no trace of it behind.

@92
of three  coins  a blanket  on the body  by mādhukarī  one’s food
to religion  harm  there is  people  make  ridicule
= To wear a blanket worth three gold coins while living on *mādhukarī* is a breach of religious principle, and people laugh at it.”

@93
the Gosvāmī  says  He who  destroyed  sinful worldly enjoyment
by His  will  has gone  my  last  worldly  disease
= Sanātana Gosvāmī said, “By the will of Him who destroyed my sinful worldly enjoyment, the last of my worldly disease is gone.”

@94
pleased  becoming  the Lord  on him  mercy  bestowed
by His  mercy  questions  to ask  his  power  arose
= The Lord was pleased and bestowed His mercy on him, and by that mercy Sanātana gained the power to ask questions.

@95
before  as  of Rāya  the Lord  questions  asked
by His  power  Rāmānanda  to them  answers  gave
= Just as earlier the Lord had put questions to Rāya Rāmānanda, and Rāmānanda had answered them by the Lord’s own power,

@96
here  by the Lord’s  power  questions  asks  Sanātana
Himself  Mahāprabhu  makes  of the truth  exposition
= so now Sanātana asked questions by the Lord’s power, and Mahāprabhu Himself explained the truth.

@97
of Kṛṣṇa  of the true nature  of the sweetness  of the majesty
and of the rasa of devotion  the shelter
the truth  to Sanātana  the Lord
mercifully  taught  He
= Out of His mercy the Lord taught Sanātana the truth that is the shelter of Kṛṣṇa’s own nature, His sweetness, His majesty and the *rasa* of devotion.

@98
then  Sanātana  the Lord’s  feet  clasping
humble  entreaty  makes  in his teeth  straw  taking
= Then Sanātana clasped the Lord’s feet and, holding a straw between his teeth, humbly entreated Him:

@99
low by birth  in low company  fallen  most wretched
into the well of sinful worldliness  having fallen  I have wasted  my life
= “I am low-born, I keep low company, I am fallen and wretched. Fallen into the well of sinful worldliness, I have wasted my life.

@100
my own  good and harm  anything  not  I know
in worldly dealings  learned  that  as truth  I accept
= I know nothing of what is good or harmful for me. I am learned only in worldly dealings, and I take that for the truth.

@101
mercy  showing  since  me  You have made  deliverance
by Your own mercy  tell  the duty  my
= Since You have mercifully delivered me, then by Your own mercy tell me what my duty is.

@102
who  am I  why  me  burn  the threefold miseries
this  not  I know  how  good  will come
= Who am I? Why do the threefold miseries burn me? I do not know this, nor how I may attain my true good.

@103
of the goal and the means  the truth  to ask  not  I know
mercy  showing  all  the truths  tell  please  Yourself
= I do not even know how to ask about the truth of the goal (*sādhya*) and the means (*sādhana*). Be merciful and explain all these truths Yourself.”

@104
the Lord  says  Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  in you  full  is
all  the truths  you know  of you  there are not  the threefold miseries
= The Lord said, “Kṛṣṇa’s mercy rests on you in full. You know all these truths, and the threefold miseries do not touch you.
