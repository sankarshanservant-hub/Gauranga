# CC Madhya 10, stage A, part 2 (verses 96–190); read by build_en.py together with Madhya10.txt

@96
in ecstatic love  He made  his  feet  worship
he  in ecstatic love  made  to the Lord  an embrace
= Overwhelmed with love, the Lord bowed at his feet, and Paramānanda Purī, overwhelmed with love, embraced the Lord.

@97
the Lord  says  in your company  to live  desire  there is
to Me  mercy  showing  make  Nīlādri  your shelter
= The Lord said, “I long to live in your company. Be merciful to Me and make Nīlādri your home.”

@98
Purī  says  in Your company  to live  desire  I have
from Gauḍa  setting out  I have come  to the city of Nīlācala
= Purī replied, “I too long to live in Your company. That is why I set out from Gauḍa and came to the city of Nīlācala.

@99
from the south  hearing of  Your  return
Śacī  delighted  and  all  the devotees
= When they heard that You had come back from the south, Śacī was overjoyed, and so were all the devotees.

@100
all  are coming  You  to see
their  delay  seeing  I have come  quickly
= They are all coming to see You, but seeing that they were delayed, I came on ahead in haste.”

@101
of Kāśī Miśra  in the house  secluded  one  room
the Lord  to him  gave  and  for service  a servant
= The Lord gave him a secluded room in Kāśī Miśra’s house, and a servant to attend him.

@102
on the next  day  came  Svarūpa  Dāmodara
of the Lord  most  intimate  of rasa  an ocean
= The next day Svarūpa Dāmodara arrived — the Lord’s most intimate companion, an ocean of *rasa*.

@103
“Puruṣottama Ācārya”  his  name  in his former āśrama
in Navadvīpa  lived  he  of the Lord  at the feet
= In his former *āśrama* his name was Puruṣottama Ācārya, and he lived in Navadvīpa at the Lord’s feet.

@104
of the Lord  the sannyāsa  seeing  maddened  becoming
sannyāsa  acceptance  he made  to Vārāṇasī  going
= When he saw the Lord accept *sannyāsa*, he became as if mad; he went to Vārāṇasī and took *sannyāsa* himself.

@105
“Caitanyānanda”  guru  his  order  gave  to him
Vedānta  having studied  teach  to all  people
= His guru, Caitanyānanda, ordered him, “Study Vedānta and teach it to everyone.”

@106
supremely  renounced  he  supremely  learned
with body and mind  he has taken shelter  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes
= He was supremely renounced and supremely learned, and with body and mind he had taken shelter of Śrī Kṛṣṇa and His pastimes.

@107
“free of cares  Kṛṣṇa  I shall worship”  for this  very  reason
in madness  made  he  sannyāsa  acceptance
= “I shall worship Kṛṣṇa free of every care” — for this very reason he accepted *sannyāsa* in his madness.

@108
sannyāsa  he took  of the śikhā  and the sacred thread  giving up  in the form
the yogapaṭṭa  not  was given  the name  became  “Svarūpa”
= He accepted *sannyāsa* in the form of giving up his *śikhā* and sacred thread, but he was not given the *yogapaṭṭa*, and his name became “Svarūpa.”

@109
from his guru  permission  asking  he came  to Nīlācala
night and day  of love for Kṛṣṇa  in the bliss  overwhelmed
= With his guru’s leave he came to Nīlācala, and there, night and day, he was overwhelmed with the bliss of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@110
of learning  the limit  speech  there is not  with anyone
in solitude  he lives  people  all  do not  know (him)
= He was the very limit of learning, yet he spoke with no one; he lived in solitude, and people did not know him.

@111
of Kṛṣṇa-rasa  of the truth  the knower  his body  of love  the form
directly  of Mahāprabhu  a second  self
= He knew the truth of Kṛṣṇa’s *rasa*, and his very body was love embodied; he was Mahāprabhu’s second self.

@112
a book  a verse  a song  anyone  to the Lord  brings
Svarūpa  examination  when he has made  afterwards  the Lord  hears
= If anyone brought the Lord a book, a verse or a song, the Lord would hear it only after Svarūpa had examined it.

@113
contrary to the conclusions of bhakti  and  rasābhāsa
on hearing  there is not  of the Lord  of the heart  delight
= Anything contrary to the conclusions of *bhakti*, or any *rasābhāsa* (a distorted *rasa*), brought the Lord’s heart no delight when He heard it.

@114
therefore  Svarūpa Gosāñi  makes  examination
pure  it is  if  to the Lord  he has it  heard
= Therefore Svarūpa Gosāñi examined everything, and only if it was pure did he let the Lord hear it.

@115
Vidyāpati  Caṇḍīdāsa  the Śrī Gīta-govinda
with these  three  songs  he gives  to the Lord  joy
= With the songs of Vidyāpati and Caṇḍīdāsa and the *Śrī Gīta-govinda* — these three — he delighted the Lord.

@116
in music  like a Gandharva  in scripture  Bṛhaspati
like Dāmodara  another  there is not  great-minded one
= In music he was like a Gandharva, in scripture a Bṛhaspati; there was no one else so great-minded as Dāmodara.

@117
of Advaita and Nityānanda  the most  beloved
of Śrīvāsa and the other  devotees  he is  dear as life
= He was most dear to Advaita and Nityānanda, and to Śrīvāsa and the other devotees he was as dear as life itself.

@118
that  Dāmodara  coming  prostrate  fell
the feet  clasping  a verse  to recite  began
= That Dāmodara came and fell prostrate before the Lord, and, clasping His feet, he began to recite a verse:

@119
easily  shaking off the dust  of distress  making pure  unfolding  fragrant joy
stilling  of the scriptures  the disputes  rasa  bestowing  offering the heart  divine madness
ever  with devotion  delighting  full of rapture  of sweetness  the very limit
O Śrī Caitanya  of mercy  ocean  Your  mercy  may it be  rising in fullness
= “O Śrī Caitanya, ocean of mercy! May Your mercy rise in all its fullness — the mercy that effortlessly shakes off the dust of distress and makes the heart pure, that unfolds a fragrant joy, that stills the quarrels over scripture, that bestows *rasa* and fills the heart with divine madness, that ever delights in devotion, that is full of rapture and is the very limit of sweetness.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 8.14)

@120
raising him  Mahāprabhu  made  an embrace
both  in ecstatic love  became  unconscious
= Mahāprabhu raised him up and embraced him, and both of them, overwhelmed with love, lost consciousness.

@121
after a while  both  steady  when  became
then  Mahāprabhu  to him  to speak  began
= After a while, when they had both grown calm, Mahāprabhu began to speak to him:

@122
you  that  would come  today  in a dream  I saw
well  it has been  a blind man  as if  two  eyes  obtained
= “Today I saw in a dream that you would come. How good this is — as if a blind man had gained his two eyes!”

@123
Svarūpa  says  my Lord  my  forgive  offence
You  leaving  elsewhere  I went  I committed  a blunder
= Svarūpa said, “My Lord, forgive my offence. I left You and went elsewhere — I have made a grave mistake.

@124
for Your  feet  my  there is not  of love  a trace
You  leaving  sinful  I  went  to another land
= I have not a trace of love for Your feet; sinner that I am, I left You and went off to another land.

@125
I  You  left  You  me  not  left
of mercy  the rope  around the neck  binding  to Your feet  You brought
= I left You, but You did not leave me: You bound me by the neck with the rope of Your mercy and brought me back to Your feet.”

@126
then  Svarūpa  made  of Nitāi  the feet  worship
Nityānanda Prabhu  made  a loving embrace
= Then Svarūpa bowed at the feet of Nitāi, and Nityānanda Prabhu embraced him with love.

@127
Jagadānanda  Mukunda  Śaṅkara  Sārvabhauma
with all  as befitting  he made  meeting
= He met Jagadānanda, Mukunda, Śaṅkara and Sārvabhauma, greeting each as was fitting.

@128
of Paramānanda  Purī  he made  the feet  worship
Purī Gosāñi  him  made  loving  embrace
= He bowed at the feet of Paramānanda Purī, and Purī Gosāñi embraced him with love.

@129
Mahāprabhu  gave  to him  secluded  a dwelling
for water and other  services  He gave  one  servant
= Mahāprabhu gave him a secluded dwelling and a servant to bring him water and see to his other needs.

@130
the next  day  with Sārvabhauma  and other  devotees
sitting  is  Mahāprabhu  in the joy of talks about Kṛṣṇa
= The next day Mahāprabhu was sitting with Sārvabhauma and other devotees, joyfully speaking of Kṛṣṇa.

@131
at that time  of Govinda  there was  the arrival
obeisance  making  he speaks  humble words
= At that moment Govinda arrived; bowing down, he humbly said:

@132
of Īśvara Purī  the servant  “Govinda”  my  name
of Purī Gosāñi  by the order  I have come  to Your  place
= “I am the servant of Īśvara Purī, and my name is Govinda. On Purī Gosāñi’s order I have come to You.

@133
at the time of attaining perfection  Gosāñi  order  gave  to me
to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  going  serve  Him
= When he was attaining perfection, Gosāñi ordered me, ‘Go to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and serve Him.’

@134
Kāśīśvara  will come  all  holy places  having seen
of my master  by the order  I  have come  to Your feet  hastening
= Kāśīśvara will come after visiting all the holy places, but on my master’s order I have hurried to Your feet.”

@135
the Lord  said  Purīśvara  parental affection  shows  to Me
mercy  showing  to Me  he has sent  you
= The Lord said, “Purīśvara has always had a father’s affection for Me; out of mercy he has sent you to Me.”

@136
this  hearing  Sārvabhauma  the Lord  asked
Purī Gosāñi  a śūdra servant  why  then  kept
= Hearing this, Sārvabhauma asked the Lord, “Why did Purī Gosāñi keep a servant of *śūdra* birth?”

@137
the Lord  says  Īśvara  is  supremely  independent
of Īśvara  the mercy  is not  subject to the Vedas
= The Lord said, “Īśvara — the Lord, and so too Īśvara Purī — is supremely independent; the mercy of Īśvara is not subject to the rules of the Vedas.

@138
of Īśvara  the mercy  caste and family  does not  regard
in Vidura’s  house  Kṛṣṇa  took  a meal
= The mercy of Īśvara takes no account of caste or family: Kṛṣṇa ate in the house of Vidura.

@139
loving  service  waits for  only  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  the mercy
by love  controlled  becoming  He does  independent  conduct
= Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s mercy waits only for loving service; controlled by love, He acts free of all rules.

@140
than reverence  millions of times  happiness  in loving  dealings
supreme bliss  there is  whose  name  on hearing
= For Him whose very name brings supreme bliss to those who hear it, loving dealings give millions of times more joy than reverential formality.”

@141
this  saying  Govinda  He made  an embrace
Govinda  made  of the Lord  the feet  worship
= Saying this, the Lord embraced Govinda, and Govinda bowed at the Lord’s feet.

@142
the Lord  says  Bhaṭṭācārya  make  consideration
of the guru  the servant  is  worthy of honour  for Me
= The Lord said, “Bhaṭṭācārya, consider this: My guru’s servant is worthy of My respect.

@143
him  service to Myself  to have him do  not  is proper
the guru  the order  has given  what  can I do  remedy
= It is not proper for Me to have him serve Me, yet My guru has ordered it. What am I to do?”

@144
Bhaṭṭa  says  of the guru  the order  is  powerful
the guru’s order  not  one transgresses  scripture  the evidence
= The Bhaṭṭa said, “The guru’s order is powerful, and one does not transgress it — scripture is the proof:

@145
he  having heard  on his mother  by Bhārgava
of the father  by the order  a blow struck  as on an enemy
accepted  of the elder brother  the command  that
the order  of elders  indeed  not to be deliberated
= “Having heard that Bhārgava (Paraśurāma), on his father’s order, struck down his own mother as though she were an enemy, he (Lakṣmaṇa) accepted that command of his elder brother. The orders of one’s elders are not to be questioned.” (Raghu-vaṁśa, 14.46)

@146
without deliberation  of the guru  the order
by me  must be carried out  of the great soul
the good  indeed  thus  for you  and
for me  and also  especially
= “The order of my guru, that great soul, must be carried out by me without deliberation; in this lies your good, and especially mine.” (Rāmāyaṇa, Ayodhyā-kāṇḍa, 22.9)

@147
then  Mahāprabhu  him  made  acceptance
of His own  sacred  body  for the service  He gave  the right
= Then Mahāprabhu accepted him and gave him the right to serve His own sacred person.

@148
of the Lord  dear  servant  as  all  give  honour
all  of the Vaiṣṇavas  Govinda  does  care
= Everyone honoured Govinda as the Lord’s dear servant, and Govinda looked after all the Vaiṣṇavas.

@149
junior  senior kīrtana singer  two  Haridāsas
Rāmāi  Nandāi  stay  of Govinda  by the side
= The two Haridāsas, the junior and the senior, both *kīrtana* singers, and Rāmāi and Nandāi stayed with Govinda.

@150
with Govinda  together  they do  of the Lord  service
of Govinda  of the fortune  the limit  not  can be  described
= Together with Govinda they served the Lord. The limit of Govinda’s good fortune cannot be described.

@151
the next  day  Mukunda Datta  says  to the Lord  at His place
Brahmānanda Bhāratī  has come  for Your  sight
= The next day Mukunda Datta came to the Lord and said, “Brahmānanda Bhāratī has come to see You.

@152
permission  give  if  him  shall I bring  here
the Lord  says  guru  he  I shall go  his  to the presence
= If You permit, shall I bring him here?” The Lord said, “He is My guru; I shall go to him Myself.”

@153
this  saying  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees
setting out  came  of Brahmānanda Bhāratī  before
= Saying this, Mahāprabhu set out with the devotees and came before Brahmānanda Bhāratī.

@154
Brahmānanda  had put on  of deerskin  a garment
that  seeing  the Lord  pain  felt  at heart
= Brahmānanda was wearing a deerskin, and when the Lord saw it He was pained at heart.

@155
seeing  indeed  pretence  He made  as if  He sees  not
Mukunda  He asks  where  Bhāratī Gosāñi
= Though He saw him, He pretended not to, and asked Mukunda, “Where is Bhāratī Gosāñi?”

@156
Mukunda  says  here  in front  see  present
the Lord  says  he  is not  you  are ignorant
= Mukunda said, “Here he is, right before You.” The Lord said, “That is not he. You are mistaken.

@157
one  as another  you call  there is not  your  knowledge
Bhāratī Gosāñi  why  would wear  a hide
= You are taking someone else for him — you do not know. Why would Bhāratī Gosāñi wear a hide?”

@158
hearing  Brahmānanda  makes  in his heart  reflection
my  skin garment  this  not  pleases  Him
= Hearing this, Brahmānanda reflected in his heart: “He does not like this skin garment of mine.

@159
rightly  He speaks  the skin garment  pride  for the sake of  I wear
by wearing a skin garment  saṁsāra  not  I cross
= He is right: I wear this skin only out of pride. Wearing a skin will not carry me across *saṁsāra*.

@160
today  from  not  I shall wear  this  skin garment
the Lord  the outer cloth  had brought  knowing  his heart
= From today I shall not wear this skin.” Knowing his heart, the Lord had a *sannyāsī*’s outer cloth brought for him.
