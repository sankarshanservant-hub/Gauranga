# CC Madhya 10, stage A, part 2 (verses 96–190); read by build_en.py together with Madhya10.txt

@96
in ecstatic love  He made  his  feet  worship
he  in ecstatic love  made  to the Lord  an embrace
= Overwhelmed with love, the Lord bowed at his feet, and Paramānanda Purī, overwhelmed with love, embraced the Lord.

@97
the Lord  says  in your company  to live  desire  there is
to Me  mercy  showing  make  Nīlādri  your shelter
= The Lord said, “I long to live in your company. Be merciful to Me and make Nīlādri your home.”

@98
Purī  says  in Your company  to live  desire  I have
from Gauḍa  setting out  I have come  to the city of Nīlācala
= Purī replied, “I too long to live in Your company. That is why I set out from Gauḍa and came to the city of Nīlācala.

@99
from the south  hearing of  Your  return
Śacī  delighted  and  all  the devotees
= When they heard that You had come back from the south, Śacī was overjoyed, and so were all the devotees.

@100
all  are coming  You  to see
their  delay  seeing  I have come  quickly
= They are all coming to see You, but seeing that they were delayed, I came on ahead in haste.”

@101
of Kāśī Miśra  in the house  secluded  one  room
the Lord  to him  gave  and  for service  a servant
= The Lord gave him a secluded room in Kāśī Miśra’s house, and a servant to attend him.

@102
on the next  day  came  Svarūpa  Dāmodara
of the Lord  most  intimate  of rasa  an ocean
= The next day Svarūpa Dāmodara arrived — the Lord’s most intimate companion, an ocean of *rasa*.

@103
“Puruṣottama Ācārya”  his  name  in his former āśrama
in Navadvīpa  lived  he  of the Lord  at the feet
= In his former *āśrama* his name was Puruṣottama Ācārya, and he lived in Navadvīpa at the Lord’s feet.

@104
of the Lord  the sannyāsa  seeing  maddened  becoming
sannyāsa  acceptance  he made  to Vārāṇasī  going
= When he saw the Lord accept *sannyāsa*, he became as if mad; he went to Vārāṇasī and took *sannyāsa* himself.

@105
“Caitanyānanda”  guru  his  order  gave  to him
Vedānta  having studied  teach  to all  people
= His guru, Caitanyānanda, ordered him, “Study Vedānta and teach it to everyone.”

@106
supremely  renounced  he  supremely  learned
with body and mind  he has taken shelter  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes
= He was supremely renounced and supremely learned, and with body and mind he had taken shelter of Śrī Kṛṣṇa and His pastimes.

@107
“free of cares  Kṛṣṇa  I shall worship”  for this  very  reason
in madness  made  he  sannyāsa  acceptance
= “I shall worship Kṛṣṇa free of every care” — for this very reason he accepted *sannyāsa* in his madness.

@108
sannyāsa  he took  of the śikhā  and the sacred thread  giving up  in the form
the yogapaṭṭa  not  was given  the name  became  “Svarūpa”
= He accepted *sannyāsa* in the form of giving up his *śikhā* and sacred thread, but he was not given the *yogapaṭṭa*, and his name became “Svarūpa.”

@109
from his guru  permission  asking  he came  to Nīlācala
night and day  of love for Kṛṣṇa  in the bliss  overwhelmed
= With his guru’s leave he came to Nīlācala, and there, night and day, he was overwhelmed with the bliss of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@110
of learning  the limit  speech  there is not  with anyone
in solitude  he lives  people  all  do not  know (him)
= He was the very limit of learning, yet he spoke with no one; he lived in solitude, and people did not know him.

@111
of Kṛṣṇa-rasa  of the truth  the knower  his body  of love  the form
directly  of Mahāprabhu  a second  self
= He knew the truth of Kṛṣṇa’s *rasa*, and his very body was love embodied; he was Mahāprabhu’s second self.

@112
a book  a verse  a song  anyone  to the Lord  brings
Svarūpa  examination  when he has made  afterwards  the Lord  hears
= If anyone brought the Lord a book, a verse or a song, the Lord would hear it only after Svarūpa had examined it.

@113
contrary to the conclusions of bhakti  and  rasābhāsa
on hearing  there is not  of the Lord  of the heart  delight
= Anything contrary to the conclusions of *bhakti*, or any *rasābhāsa* (a distorted *rasa*), brought the Lord’s heart no delight when He heard it.

@114
therefore  Svarūpa Gosāñi  makes  examination
pure  it is  if  to the Lord  he has it  heard
= Therefore Svarūpa Gosāñi examined everything, and only if it was pure did he let the Lord hear it.

@115
Vidyāpati  Caṇḍīdāsa  the Śrī Gīta-govinda
with these  three  songs  he gives  to the Lord  joy
= With the songs of Vidyāpati and Caṇḍīdāsa and the *Śrī Gīta-govinda* — these three — he delighted the Lord.

@116
in music  like a Gandharva  in scripture  Bṛhaspati
like Dāmodara  another  there is not  great-minded one
= In music he was like a Gandharva, in scripture a Bṛhaspati; there was no one else so great-minded as Dāmodara.

@117
of Advaita and Nityānanda  the most  beloved
of Śrīvāsa and the other  devotees  he is  dear as life
= He was most dear to Advaita and Nityānanda, and to Śrīvāsa and the other devotees he was as dear as life itself.

@118
that  Dāmodara  coming  prostrate  fell
the feet  clasping  a verse  to recite  began
= That Dāmodara came and fell prostrate before the Lord, and, clasping His feet, he began to recite a verse:

@119
easily  shaking off the dust  of distress  making pure  unfolding  fragrant joy
stilling  of the scriptures  the disputes  rasa  bestowing  offering the heart  divine madness
ever  with devotion  delighting  full of rapture  of sweetness  the very limit
O Śrī Caitanya  of mercy  ocean  Your  mercy  may it be  rising in fullness
= “O Śrī Caitanya, ocean of mercy! May Your mercy rise in all its fullness — the mercy that effortlessly shakes off the dust of distress and makes the heart pure, that unfolds a fragrant joy, that stills the quarrels over scripture, that bestows *rasa* and fills the heart with divine madness, that ever delights in devotion, that is full of rapture and is the very limit of sweetness.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 8.10)

@120
raising him  Mahāprabhu  made  an embrace
both  in ecstatic love  became  unconscious
= Mahāprabhu raised him up and embraced him, and both of them, overwhelmed with love, lost consciousness.

@121
after a while  both  steady  when  became
then  Mahāprabhu  to him  to speak  began
= After a while, when they had both grown calm, Mahāprabhu began to speak to him:

@122
you  that  would come  today  in a dream  I saw
well  it has been  a blind man  as if  two  eyes  obtained
= “Today I saw in a dream that you would come. How good this is — as if a blind man had gained his two eyes!”

@123
Svarūpa  says  my Lord  my  forgive  offence
You  leaving  elsewhere  I went  I committed  a blunder
= Svarūpa said, “My Lord, forgive my offence. I left You and went elsewhere — I have made a grave mistake.

@124
for Your  feet  my  there is not  of love  a trace
You  leaving  sinful  I  went  to another land
= I have not a trace of love for Your feet; sinner that I am, I left You and went off to another land.

@125
I  You  left  You  me  not  left
of mercy  the rope  around the neck  binding  to Your feet  You brought
= I left You, but You did not leave me: You bound me by the neck with the rope of Your mercy and brought me back to Your feet.”
