@title The Reasons for Śrī Caitanya's Advent

@1
Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship
by the power  of taking shelter  at whose feet
gathers  from the mines  the multitude
an ignorant man  of conclusive truth  the precious  jewels
= I worship Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. By the power of taking shelter at His feet, even an ignorant person gathers the precious jewels of conclusive truth from a multitude of mines.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host
= All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of the third  verse  of the meaning  I have made  the explanation
of the fourth  verse  the meaning  hear  O devotees
= I have explained the meaning of the third verse. Now, O devotees, hear the meaning of the fourth.

@4
not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in the age of Kali
to bestow  most exalted radiant rasa  the treasure of devotion to Himself
Hari  than gold  more beautiful  of radiance  by a mass  illumined
always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  the son of Śacī
= “Out of mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a long time: the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted and radiant *rasa* of divine love. May that Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, resplendent with a mass of radiance more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the cave of your heart.” (Vidagdha-mādhava 1.2)

@5
complete  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  the son of the king of Vraja
in Goloka  Vraja  together with  eternal  pastimes
= Kṛṣṇa, the son of the king of Vraja, is the Supreme Lord in His fullness. Together with His Vraja He enjoys eternal pastimes in Goloka.

@6
of Brahmā  in one  day  He  once
having descended  performs  manifest  pastimes
= Once in a day of Brahmā He descends and displays these pastimes openly in this world.

@7
Satya  Tretā  Dvāpara  Kali  four ages  we know
those  four ages  divine  one age  we count
= There are four ages: Satya, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali. Together these four are counted as one divine age (*divya-yuga*).

@8
in seventy-one  cycles of four ages  one  manvantara
fourteen  manvantaras  of Brahmā  of the day  within
= Seventy-one such cycles make one *manvantara*, the reign of a Manu, and a day of Brahmā holds fourteen manvantaras.

@9
Vaivasvata  by name  this  seventh  manvantara
twenty-seven  cycles of four ages  having passed  of it  within
= The present manvantara, the seventh, is called Vaivasvata. When twenty-seven of its cycles of four ages have passed,

@10
in the twenty-eighth  cycle of four ages  of Dvāpara  at the end
Vraja  together with  takes place  of Kṛṣṇa  the manifestation
= at the end of the Dvāpara age of the twenty-eighth cycle, Kṛṣṇa appears in this world together with His Vraja.

@11
servitude  friendship  parental love  amorous love  four  rasas
of the four  moods  devotees  all  Kṛṣṇa  their  under the sway
= Servitude, friendship, parental love and amorous love are the four *rasas*. Kṛṣṇa is under the sway of all the devotees who love Him in these four moods.

@12
servants  friends  father  mother  beloveds  the host  taking along
in Vraja  play  makes  Kṛṣṇa  absorbed in love  becoming
= With His servants, His friends, His father and mother and His beloveds, Kṛṣṇa sports in Vraja, absorbed in love.

@13
as He wished  having sported  Kṛṣṇa  makes  disappearance
having disappeared  in the mind  makes  reflection
= When He has sported to His heart's content, Kṛṣṇa disappears. Then He reflects within Himself:

@14
for a long time  not  I have made  of loving devotion  the gift
devotion  without  of the world  no  true existence
= “For a long time I have not given the gift of loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*). Without devotion the world has no true existence.

@15
in the whole  world  Me  they make  devotion by rules
by devotion by rules  the mood of Vraja  to attain  not  the power
= Everywhere in the world people worship Me by the rules of scripture (*vidhi-bhakti*). But devotion by rules has no power to attain the mood of Vraja.

@16
of majesty  with awareness  the whole  world  is mixed
by majesty  weakened  in love  not  My  pleasure
= The whole world is tinged with awareness of My majesty, and love weakened by awe of My majesty does not please Me.

@17
in awareness of majesty  by rules  worship  doing
to Vaikuṇṭha  they go  four  kinds of  liberation  attaining
= Those who worship by the rules, aware of My majesty, attain the four kinds of liberation and go to Vaikuṇṭha:

@18
equal opulence  the same form  and  nearness  the same world
merging  not  accepts  the devotee  in which  oneness with Brahman
= *sārṣṭi*, opulence equal to Mine; *sārūpya*, a form like Mine; *sāmīpya*, nearness to Me; and *sālokya*, residence in My world. The devotee never accepts *sāyujya*, merging, in which one becomes one with Brahman.

@19
the religion of the age  I shall establish  of the Name  congregational chanting
of the four  moods  devotion  giving  I shall make dance  the world
= I shall establish the religion of the age, the congregational chanting of the Holy Name (*nāma-saṅkīrtana*). Giving devotion in the four moods, I shall make the whole world dance.

@20
Myself  I shall make  of a devotee  the mood  acceptance
Myself  practising  devotion  I shall teach  to everyone
= I Myself shall take the mood of a devotee, and by practising devotion Myself I shall teach it to everyone.

@21
oneself  not  having done  religion  to teach  not  is possible
this  indeed  conclusion  in the Gītā and the Bhāgavatam  they sing
= Unless one practises religion oneself, one cannot teach it. This conclusion is sung in the Gītā and the Bhāgavatam:

@22
whenever  whenever  indeed  of religion
a decline  occurs  O descendant of Bharata
the rise  of irreligion
then  Myself  manifest  I
= “Whenever religion declines and irreligion rises, O descendant of Bharata, I manifest Myself.” (Bhagavad-gītā 4.7)

@23
for the deliverance  of the saintly
for the destruction  and  of the wicked
of religion  for the sake of establishing
I appear  in age  after age
= “To deliver the saintly, to destroy the wicked and to establish religion, I appear age after age.” (Bhagavad-gītā 4.8)

@24
would perish  these  worlds
not  would perform  action  if  I
of confusion of the social orders  and  the cause  I would be
I would ruin  these  peoples
= “If I did not perform action, these worlds would perish. I would cause confusion among the social orders and ruin all these peoples.” (Bhagavad-gītā 3.24)

@25
whatever  whatever  does  a great man
that  that  indeed  other  people
he  whatever  the standard  makes
the world  that  follows
= “Whatever a great man does, others do the same. Whatever standard he sets, the world follows.” (Bhagavad-gītā 3.21)

@26
of the religion of the age  the establishment  takes place  a portion  from
Me  except  others  cannot  the love of Vraja  to give
= The religion of each age is established by My partial manifestations, but no one except Me can give the love of Vraja.

@27
let there be  avatāras  many
of the lotus-naveled Lord  all-auspicious
than Kṛṣṇa  other  who  indeed  to the creepers
even  love  the giver  is
= “Let the lotus-naveled Lord have many avatāras, all of them auspicious in every way. But who other than Kṛṣṇa gives love even to the creepers?” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta 1.5.37)

@28
therefore  My own  devotees  making  companions
on earth  descending  I shall do  various  pastimes
= Therefore I shall descend to earth with My own devotees as My companions and enjoy many kinds of pastimes.”

@29
thus  thinking  in the age of Kali  in the first  junction
descended  became  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  in Nadia
= Thinking in this way, Kṛṣṇa Himself descended in Nadia in the first period (*sandhyā*) of the age of Kali.

@30
of the lion Caitanya  in Navadvīpa  the descent
a lion's neck  a lion's strength  of a lion  the roar
= Thus the lion Caitanya descended in Navadvīpa. He had the neck of a lion, the strength of a lion and the roar of a lion.

@31
that  lion  may He sit  of the soul  of the heart  in the cave
of sin  the elephant  is destroyed  whose  by the roar
= May that lion take His seat in the cave of the heart of every soul. His roar destroys the elephant of sin.

@32
in the first  pastimes  His  Viśvambhara  the name
with the rasa of devotion  He filled  He sustained  the host of beings
= In His early pastimes His name was Viśvambhara, for He filled the world with the *rasa* of devotion and sustained all living beings.

@33
ḍubhṛñ  of the verbal root  the meaning  nourishing  sustaining
He nourished  He sustained  love  giving  the three worlds
= The verbal root *ḍubhṛñ* means “to nourish” and “to sustain.” By giving love He nourished and sustained the three worlds.

@34
in the later  pastimes  the name  He bears  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
Śrī Kṛṣṇa  making known  the whole  universe  He made  blessed
= In His later pastimes He bears the name Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. By making Śrī Kṛṣṇa known, He blessed the whole universe.

@35
His  as the avatāra for the age  knowing  Garga  the great soul
of Kṛṣṇa  at the name-giving ceremony  has made  a determination
= Knowing Him to be the avatāra for the age, the great sage Garga declared it at Kṛṣṇa's name-giving ceremony:

@36
there were  colors  three  indeed  of Him
who accepts  in each age  bodies
white  red  and also  yellow
now  darkness  has attained
= “Your son appears in every age, accepting a body. He has had three colors: white, red and yellow. Now He has come dark.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.8.13)

@37
white  red  yellow color  these  three  lusters
in the Satya, Tretā and Kali ages  assumes  the husband of Śrī
= The husband of Śrī assumes these three lusters, white, red and yellow, in the Satya, Tretā and Kali ages.

@38
now  in Dvāpara  He  became  dark in color
this  all  of the scriptures, Āgamas and Purāṇas  the essence
= Now, in the Dvāpara age, He has become dark. This is the essence of the scriptures, the Āgamas and the Purāṇas.

@39
in Dvāpara  the Lord  dark
in yellow garments  with His own weapons
with the Śrīvatsa and other  marks  and
with signs  distinguished
= “In the Dvāpara age the Lord appears dark, clad in yellow, bearing His own weapons and marked with the Śrīvatsa and other signs.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.5.27)

@40
in the age of Kali  the religion of the age  of the Name  the spreading
for that  purpose  yellow in color  the avatāra Caitanya
= In the age of Kali the religion of the age is spreading the Holy Name. For this purpose the Lord has descended as Caitanya, yellow in color.

@41
of molten gold  like  the luster  mighty  body
new clouds  surpassing  the sound of the voice  that  deep
= His luster is like molten gold and His body is tall and mighty. His deep voice surpasses the thunder of fresh rain clouds.

@42
in height  and in breadth  who  his own  cubits
four  cubits  is  a mahā-puruṣa  is known as
= One whose height and the span of whose arms both measure four of his own cubits is known as a *mahā-puruṣa*, a great personality.

@43
nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala  is  his  name
of nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala  the body  Caitanya  of virtues  the abode
= Such a person is called *nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala*, “proportioned like a banyan tree.” Caitanya, the abode of all virtues, has such a body.

@44
to the knees  reaching  arms  lotus  eyes
than the sesame flower  surpassing  nose  the moon  face
= His arms reach down to His knees and His eyes are like lotuses. His nose is finer than the sesame flower, and His face is like the moon.

@45
peaceful  self-controlled  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  steadfastly  wholly given
affectionate to devotees  gentle  to all  beings  equal
= He is peaceful and self-controlled, wholly and steadfastly given to devotion to Kṛṣṇa. He is affectionate to His devotees, gentle by nature and equal to all living beings.

@46
of sandalwood  armlets  bracelets  sandal  ornaments
at the time of dancing  wearing  He performs  Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana
= When He dances in the congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's names, He wears armlets and bracelets of sandalwood and is adorned with sandal paste.

@47
these  all  qualities  taking  the sage  Vaiśampāyana
in the Thousand Names  made  His  of names  enumeration
= Taking all these qualities into account, the sage Vaiśampāyana listed His names in the Thousand Names of Viṣṇu (*Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma*).

@48
two  pastimes  of Caitanya  the first  and  the last
in the two  pastimes  four  four  names  special
= Caitanya's pastimes have two parts, the early and the later, and in each He bears four special names.

@49
of golden  color  with a body of gold
with beautiful limbs  wearing sandalwood armlets
accepting sannyāsa  equipoised  peaceful
of steadfastness and peace  the supreme abode
= “His complexion is golden and His body is like gold. His limbs are beautiful, and He wears armlets of sandalwood. He accepts the renounced order (*sannyāsa*). He is equipoised and peaceful, the supreme abode of steadfastness and peace.” (Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma, Mahābhārata)

@50
clearly  making  in the Bhāgavatam  it says  again  and again
in the age of Kali  of Kṛṣṇa's name  congregational chanting  the essence
= The Bhāgavatam says clearly, again and again, that in the age of Kali the congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa's name is the essence of all religion.

@51
uttering Kṛṣṇa  by luster  not dark
with limbs, sub-limbs, weapons and associates
by sacrifices  consisting chiefly of saṅkīrtana
worship  indeed  the intelligent
= “Intelligent people worship, by sacrifices consisting chiefly of congregational chanting, the Lord who always utters the name ‘Kṛṣṇa’ (*kṛṣṇa-varṇam*) and whose luster is not dark. He is accompanied by His limbs, His sub-limbs, His weapons and His associates.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.5.32)

@52
hear  brothers  this  all  of Caitanya  the glory
in this  verse  it tells  His  of glory  the limit
= Hear, brothers, all this glory of Caitanya. This verse tells the very limit of His glory.

@53
Kṛṣṇa  these  two  syllables  always  in whose  mouth
or  Kṛṣṇa  He  describes  in His own  joy
= The two syllables “Kṛṣ-ṇa” are always in His mouth; or else, He describes Kṛṣṇa in His own joy.

@54
of the word kṛṣṇa-varṇa  meanings  two  indeed  established
Kṛṣṇa  except  His  into the mouth  not  comes  anything else
= These are the two established meanings of the word *kṛṣṇa-varṇa*. Nothing but Kṛṣṇa ever comes to His lips.

@55
anyone  Him  calls  if  dark in color
the next  epithet  that  makes  prevention
= If anyone should call Him dark in color, the next epithet rules this out.

@56
by the luster of the body  is  He  not dark in color
by not dark in color  it means  yellow in color
= By the luster of His body He is not dark, and “not dark” means yellow.

@57
in Kali  whom  the wise  openly  worship  from the abundance of luster
not dark in body  Kṛṣṇa  with sacrificial rites  consisting of loud chanting
the object of worship  and  they call  whom  of all  who live in the fourth order
that  Lord  in the form of Caitanya  exceedingly  to us  may He show mercy
= “In the age of Kali the wise openly worship Kṛṣṇa, whose body is not dark because of its abundant luster, with sacrificial rites that consist of loud chanting of His names. They declare Him the object of worship of all who live in the renounced order. May that Lord, who has appeared in the form of Caitanya, be abundantly merciful to us.” (Stava-mālā, Second Caitanyāṣṭaka 1)

@58
visibly  His  of molten gold  the luster
by whose  radiance  is destroyed  of ignorance  the darkness
= His luster of molten gold is plain to see, and its radiance dispels the darkness of ignorance.

@59
of the souls  of sin  the darkness  destruction  to make
limbs  sub-limbs  the Name  various  weapons  He holds
= To destroy the darkness of the souls' sins, He bears various weapons: His limbs (*aṅga*), His sub-limbs (*upāṅga*) and the Holy Name.

@60
to devotion  opposed  action  piety  or  impiety
its  kalmaṣa  the name  that  great darkness
= Any action opposed to devotion, whether pious or impious, is called *kalmaṣa*, sin. That is the great darkness.
