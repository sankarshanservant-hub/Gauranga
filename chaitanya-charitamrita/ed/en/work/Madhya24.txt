@title Explanation of the Ātmārāma Verse

@1
the ātmārāma (verse)  of the sun-verse
of the meaning  the rays  who  revealing
of the world  the darkness  dispelled  may He protect
He  Caitanya  the mountain of sunrise
= May Śrī Caitanya, the eastern mountain from which the sun rises, protect us. Revealing the rays of meaning of the sun-like *ātmārāma* verse, He dispelled the darkness of the world.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
then  Sanātana  the Lord’s  feet  clasping
once more  says  something  humility  showing
= Then Sanātana clasped the Lord’s feet and once more humbly spoke:

@4
before  I have heard  You  at Sārvabhauma’s place
one  in a verse  eighteen  meanings  You gave  in explanation
= “I have heard that once, at Sārvabhauma’s place, You explained a single verse in eighteen ways.

@5
delighting in the ātmā  and  the sages
free from knots  even  to Urukrama
perform  causeless  devotion
of such  qualities  is Hari
= “Even sages who delight in the ātmā and are free from the knots of the heart render causeless devotion to Urukrama, for such are the qualities of Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.7.10)

@6
the wonder  having heard  my  eager  mind
mercy  showing  You tell  if  are soothed  ears
= Hearing of this wonder, my mind is filled with longing. If You kindly tell me, my ears will be soothed.”

@7
the Lord  says  I  am a madman  in My  words
Sārvabhauma  madness  as truth  taking  accepts
= The Lord said, “I am a madman, and Sārvabhauma takes the madness in My words to be the truth.

@8
what  I babbled  of that  there is not  anything  in My memory
your  by the strength of the company  if  something  comes  to mind
= I do not remember anything of what I babbled then. Yet by the strength of your company something may come to My mind.

@9
of itself  to Me  any  meaning  does not  appear
of you all  by the strength of the company  whatever  is revealed
= No meaning comes to Me of itself; whatever is revealed comes by the strength of the company of all of you.”

@10
eleven  words  in this  verse  perfectly pure
separately  various  meanings  in the words  make  glitter
= This verse has eleven perfectly pure words, and in each of them, taken separately, many different meanings shine brightly.

@11
by the word ātmā  Brahman  body  mind  effort  steadfastness
intelligence  nature  these  seven  meanings  are found
= The word *ātmā* has seven meanings: Brahman, the body, the mind, effort (*yatna*), steadfastness (*dhṛti*), intelligence (*buddhi*) and nature (*svabhāva*).

@12
ātmā  body  mind  Brahman  nature
steadfastness  intelligence (in the senses of)  effort  and
= “The word *ātmā* is used for the body, the mind, Brahman, nature, steadfastness and intelligence, and also for effort.” (Viśva-prakāśa)

@13
in these  seven  delights  who  they  are the ātmārāmas
of the ātmārāmas  later  I shall make  the enumeration
= Those who delight in these seven are *ātmārāmas*. I shall enumerate the kinds of *ātmārāmas* later.

@14
of muni  and the other  words  the meanings  hear  Sanātana
separately  separately  meanings  I shall give  afterwards  I shall make  the combination
= Hear, Sanātana, the meanings of the word *muni* and the others. I shall first give the meaning of each separately and afterwards combine them.

@15
by the word muni  given to contemplation  also  it means  one who is silent
an ascetic  one who keeps vows  a renunciant  and  a seer  a sage
= The word *muni* means one given to contemplation (*manana-śīla*). It also means one who is silent, an ascetic, one who keeps vows, a renunciant (*yati*), a seer (*ṛṣi*) and a sage (*muni*).

@16
by the word nirgrantha  is meant  of ignorance  the knot  free from
of injunctions  of prohibitions  of the Vedic scriptures  knowledge and so on  devoid of
= The word *nirgrantha* means one free from the knot of ignorance, and also one devoid of knowledge of the Vedic scriptures with their injunctions and prohibitions.

@17
fools  the low  mlecchas  and others  of scripture  devoid  people
of wealth  a hoarder  nirgrantha  and  who  is without wealth
= Fools, the low-born, *mlecchas* and others who are devoid of scripture; one who hoards wealth; and one who has no wealth — all these are also called *nirgrantha*.

@18
nir  in the sense of certainty  niṣ  in the sense of sequence
nir  of construction  of prohibition (in the senses)
grantha  in the sense of wealth  then  of a literary work
of the stringing of letters into words  also  and
= “The prefix *nir* is used in the senses of certainty, sequence, construction and prohibition. The word *grantha* means wealth, a literary work, and also the stringing of letters into words.” (Viśva-prakāśa)

@19
by the word urukrama  is meant  great  whose  step
by the word krama  is meant  this  the placing of the foot
= The word *urukrama* means He whose step (*krama*) is great. The word *krama* means a step, the placing forward of the foot.

@20
power  with trembling  orderly arrangement  stepping forth
of the feet  by the movement  He made tremble  the three worlds
= *Krama* also means power, trembling, orderly arrangement and stepping forth (*ākramaṇa*). By the movement of His feet He made the three worlds tremble.

@21
of Viṣṇu  indeed  of the powers  the counting  who  is able  here
who  earthly  even  a learned man  has counted  the atoms
held fast  who  by His own  speed  unchecked  Satyaloka
from which  of the three (guṇas)  of the equilibrium  from the abode  greatly  shaking
= “Even a learned man who could count the atoms of the earth — who among such could count the powers of Viṣṇu? When His unchecked stride shook the universe from *pradhāna*, the abode of the equilibrium of the three *guṇas*, up to Satyaloka, it was He who held Satyaloka fast.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.7.40)

@22
in His all-pervading form  He pervades  by His potency  sustaining  nourishing
by the potency of sweetness  Goloka  by majesty  Paravyoma
= In His all-pervading form He pervades everything, and by His potency He sustains and nourishes it. By His potency of sweetness (*mādhurya-śakti*) He sustains Goloka, and by His majesty (*aiśvarya*) the spiritual sky, Paravyoma.

@23
by the potency of māyā  the universes and so on  orderly  creation
of the word urukrama  this  of the meaning  the determination
= By His potency of *māyā* He creates the universes and all else in orderly succession. Thus is the meaning of the word *urukrama* determined.

@24
krama  in the sense of power  of orderly arrangement
krama  of movement  of trembling (in the senses)
= “*Krama* means power and orderly arrangement; *krama* also means movement and trembling.” (Viśva-prakāśa)

@25
kurvanti  the word  this  in the parasmaipada  is
of Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  for the sake of  in worship  the purport  it expresses
= The word *kurvanti* is in the *parasmaipada*, the voice used when an action is done for another. It shows that the worship is meant for Kṛṣṇa’s happiness.

@26
svarita-accented  marked with ñ (roots)  of the agent
in the intention  of the action  the fruit (when it is)
= “Roots marked with the *svarita* accent or with the indicatory letter *ñ* take the *ātmanepada* endings when the fruit of the action is meant for the agent himself.” (Pāṇini, 1.3.72)

@27
by the word hetu  is meant  enjoyment and so on  other desires
enjoyment  perfection  liberation  chief  these  three  kinds
= The word *hetu* (motive) means other desires, beginning with the desire for enjoyment. They are chiefly of three kinds: enjoyment (*bhukti*), mystic perfection (*siddhi*) and liberation (*mukti*).

@28
one  bhukti  is called  enjoyment  of endless  kinds
siddhis  eighteen  mukti  of five  kinds
= *Bhukti*, enjoyment, is of endless kinds; the *siddhis* are eighteen; and *mukti* is of five kinds.

@29
these  where  are not  that  bhakti  causeless
by which  subdued  becomes  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  the playful
= Devotion in which these are absent is *ahaitukī*, causeless. By it the playful Śrī Kṛṣṇa comes under control.

@30
of the word bhakti  the meaning  is  of ten kinds
one  sādhana  prema-bhakti  nine  kinds
= The word *bhakti* has ten meanings: one is *sādhana-bhakti*, and *prema-bhakti* is of nine kinds.

@31
rati  marked by  prema  marked by  and so on  is proclaimed
in the form of bhāva  of mahābhāva  by the marks  in the form of  and
= It is proclaimed as marked by *rati*, marked by *prema* and so on, as having the form of *bhāva*, and as marked by *mahābhāva*.

@32
of the śānta devotee  rati  increases  prema  up to
of the dāsya devotee  rati  is  of rāga  the stage  up to the end
= The *rati* of devotees in *śānta-rasa* grows up to *prema*; the *rati* of devotees in *dāsya-rasa* reaches the stage of *rāga*.

@33
of the friends  rati  is  anurāga  up to
of father  of mother  affection  and so on  anurāga  up to the end
= The *rati* of His friends reaches up to *anurāga*, and the affection of His father, mother and other elders likewise reaches *anurāga*.

@34
of the beloveds  rati  reaches  mahābhāva  the limit
by the word bhakti  I have told  of this  meaning  the glory
= The *rati* of His beloveds reaches the limit of *mahābhāva*. Thus I have told the glory of the meaning of the word *bhakti*.

@35
of the word ittham-bhūta-guṇaḥ  hear  the explanation
of the word ittham-bhūta  separate  meaning  of the word guṇa  another
= Now hear the explanation of the words *ittham-bhūta-guṇaḥ*. The word *ittham-bhūta* has its own meaning, and the word *guṇa* another.

@36
of the word ittham-bhūta  the meaning  full of perfect bliss
whom  before  the bliss of Brahman  like a straw  becomes
= The word *ittham-bhūta* means He who is full of perfect bliss, before whom the bliss of Brahman is like a blade of grass.

@37
from seeing You face to face  the joy
in the pure ocean  situated  for me
joys  become like a calf’s hoofprint
of Brahman  even  O Guru of the world
= “O Guru of the world, now that I am immersed in the pure ocean of joy that comes from seeing You face to face, all other joys — even the bliss of Brahman — seem to me like the water in a calf’s hoofprint.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 14.36)

@38
attracting all  delighting all  the great elixir
by His own  power  He brings about  of everything  forgetting
= He attracts all, delights all and is the great elixir of *rasa*. By His own power He makes one forget everything else.

@39
of enjoyment  of liberation  of perfection  the happiness  makes one give up  whose  by the scent
extraordinary  by the power  of the qualities  by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  He binds
= Their mere scent makes one give up the happiness of enjoyment, liberation and mystic perfection. By the extraordinary power of His qualities He binds the heart through His mercy.

@40
of scripture  reasoning  there is not  here  of conclusions  deliberation
this  by the quality of His nature  in which  of sweetness  the essence
= Here there is no scriptural reasoning or weighing of conclusions. This happens by the very nature of His qualities, in which lies the essence of sweetness.

@41
of the word guṇa  the meaning  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  endless
in His form of eternity and consciousness  in His qualities  all  perfect bliss
= The word *guṇa* means Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, which are endless. In His form, eternal and conscious, and in His qualities, everything is perfect bliss.

@42
in majesty  sweetness  compassion  of His own nature  the fullness
in His affection for His devotees  Himself  up to  generosity
= His own nature is full of majesty, sweetness and compassion. In His affection for His devotees His generosity goes so far that He gives even Himself.

@43
extraordinary  form  taste  fragrance and other  qualities
someone’s  mind  by some  quality  makes  attraction
= He has extraordinary qualities — His form, His *rasa*, His fragrance and others — and by one quality or another He attracts each one’s mind.

@44
of Sanaka and the others  the mind  He stole  by fragrance and other  qualities
of Śukadeva  the mind  He stole  by the hearing of His pastimes
= He stole the minds of Sanaka and the other Kumāras by His fragrance and other qualities, and He stole Śukadeva’s mind through the hearing of His pastimes.

@45
of Him  the lotus-eyed  of the lotus feet
with the filaments  mixed  of tulasī  of the nectar  the breeze
having entered within  through its own passage  caused  in them
agitation  devoted to the imperishable Brahman  even  of mind and body
= “The breeze that bore the fragrance of tulasī, mingled with the pollen of the lotus-eyed Lord’s lotus feet, entered their nostrils and stirred both their minds and their bodies, though they were devoted to the imperishable Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.15.43)

@46
firmly established  though  in transcendence of the guṇas
of Uttamaśloka  by the pastimes
captivated  in mind  O royal sage
the narration  which  I studied
= “O royal sage, although I was firmly established in transcendence of the *guṇas*, my mind was captivated by the pastimes of Uttamaśloka, and so I studied this narration.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.1.9)

@47
of His divine body  by the beauty  He steals  of the gopīs  the minds
of form  of qualities  by hearing  of Rukmiṇī and the others  the attraction
= By the beauty of His divine body He steals the minds of the *gopīs*; Rukmiṇī and the other queens are attracted by hearing of His beauty and qualities.

@48
having seen  by locks  framed  face  Your  by the beauty of earrings
of the cheeks  of the lips  the nectar  smiling  glance
granting fearlessness  and  of arms  the pair  having beheld
the chest  for Śrī  the sole delight  and  we become  maidservants
= “Seeing Your face framed by curling locks and graced by the beauty of Your earrings, Your cheeks, the nectar of Your lips and Your smiling glance, and beholding Your two arms that grant fearlessness and Your chest, the sole delight of Śrī, we have become Your maidservants.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.39)

@49
having heard  qualities  O beauty of the worlds  of those who hear  Your
having entered  of the ears  through the openings  removing  of the body  the distress
form  for the eyes  of those who see  of all goals  the attainment
into You  O Acyuta  enters  heart  shameless  my
= “O beauty of the worlds, Your qualities enter the ears of those who hear of them and remove the distress of their bodies, and Your form is, for the eyes of those who see, the attainment of every goal. Having heard of them, O Acyuta, my heart, casting off shame, enters into You.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.52.37)

@50
of the flute  by the song  steals  Kṛṣṇa  of Lakṣmī and the others  the minds
in a fitting  mood  of the world  all  of young women  the host
= By the song of His flute Kṛṣṇa steals the minds of Lakṣmī and others, and of all the young women of the world, each in a fitting mood.

@51
of what  merit  his  not  O Lord  we know
of Your  feet  of the dust  of the touch  the right
for which  longing  Śrī  the best of women  performed  austerity
giving up  desires  for a very long time  holding vows
= “O Lord, we do not know by what merit this one has gained the right to touch the dust of Your feet. Longing for it, Śrī, the best of women, renounced all desires and practised austerities, keeping her vows for a very long time.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.16.36)

@52
what  woman  O dear one  Your  of sweet words  by the nectar  of the flute  by the song
enchanted  from the conduct of the noble  not  would stray  in the three worlds
of the three worlds  the beauty  this  and  having seen  form
which  cows  birds  trees  deer  thrills of rapture  bore
= “O Kṛṣṇa, what woman in the three worlds, enchanted by the song of Your flute with its sweet, nectarean notes, would not stray from the conduct of the noble? Seeing this form of Yours, the beauty of the three worlds, even cows, birds, trees and deer bristle with rapture.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.40)

@53
like elders  of the women  in parental love  attraction
in the moods of servitude  friendship and so on  men and others  the host
= Women who are like elders He attracts in parental love (*vātsalya*), and men in the moods of servitude, friendship and others.

@54
birds  beasts  trees  creepers  moving  unmoving
with love  mad  making  attract  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities
= Birds, beasts, trees and creepers — all that moves and does not move — Kṛṣṇa’s qualities attract, making them mad with love (*prema*).

@55
by the word hariḥ  many meanings  two  foremost
all  inauspiciousness  He takes away  love  giving  He steals  the mind
= The word *hariḥ* has many meanings, two of which are foremost: He takes away all that is inauspicious, and, bestowing love (*prema*), He steals the mind.

@56
somehow  or other  whoever  anyone  does  remembering
of four kinds  distress  his  He makes  removal
= Whoever remembers Him, in whatever way, He frees from the four kinds of distress.

@57
as  fire  brightly blazing
makes  firewood  into ashes
so  toward Me  directed  devotion
O Uddhava  sins  entirely
= “As a blazing fire turns firewood to ashes, so, O Uddhava, devotion to Me burns up all sins.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.19)

@58
then  He makes  obstructing devotion  karma  of ignorance  destruction
of hearing and so on  the fruit  prema  He makes  manifestation
= Then He destroys ignorance and the *karma* that obstructs devotion, and He manifests *prema*, the fruit of hearing and the other practices.

@59
by His own  qualities  then  He steals  body  senses  mind
so  merciful  Kṛṣṇa  such  His  qualities
= Then by His own qualities He steals the body, senses and mind. So merciful is Kṛṣṇa, and such are His qualities.

@60
four  goals of life  makes one give up  He steals  everyone’s  mind
of the word hari  this  chief  I have told  characteristic
= He makes one give up the four goals of human life and steals everyone’s mind. Thus I have told the chief meaning of the word *hari*.
