# CC Antya 7, verses 86–169 (stage A, part 2): our English translation from the Bengali and English word-by-word.
# Verse 169 (= Antya 2.172) — word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev; verse 133, line 2 (= Antya 3.136) — from ed/en-rev.

@86
then  the Bhaṭṭa  went  to Paṇḍita Gosāñi’s  place
in various  ways  friendship  showing  makes  coming and going
= Then the Bhaṭṭa went to Paṇḍita Gosāñi. Showing friendship in many ways, he kept coming and going.

@87
because of the Lord’s  disregard  all  of Nīlācala  the people
the Bhaṭṭa’s  explanations  any  not  do  hear
= Because the Lord disregarded him, none of the people of Nīlācala would listen to the Bhaṭṭa’s explanations.

@88
ashamed  became  the Bhaṭṭa,  was  in dishonor
grieved  being  he went  to the Paṇḍita’s  place
= The Bhaṭṭa felt ashamed and dishonored, and in his grief he went to the Paṇḍita.

@89
humility  showing  he says  I have taken  your  shelter
you  mercy  showing  save  my  life
= Humbly he said, “I have taken shelter of you. Be merciful and save my life.

@90
of the name of Kṛṣṇa  the explanation  if  you do  hear
then  my  of shame  the mire  is  washed away
= If you will hear my explanation of Kṛṣṇa’s name, the mire of my shame will be washed away.”

@91
into a dilemma  fell  the Paṇḍita,  feels  doubt
what  he should do  this  to  cannot  decide
= The Paṇḍita was caught in a dilemma and full of doubt: he could not decide what he should do.

@92
although  the Paṇḍita  further  not  gave  consent
the Bhaṭṭa  going  still  reads  making  force
= Although the Paṇḍita did not consent, the Bhaṭṭa came and read to him anyway, forcing it on him.

@93
out of noble courtesy  the Paṇḍita  to make  cannot  a refusal
in this  dilemma,  Kṛṣṇa,  protect me,  I have taken  shelter
= Out of noble courtesy (*ābhijātya*) the Paṇḍita could not forbid him. “Kṛṣṇa, protect me in this dilemma,” he prayed. “I have taken shelter of You.

@94
the Indweller  the Lord  will know  my  mind
of Him  fear  not  any,  ‘severe’  His  associates
= The Lord dwells in the heart; He will know my mind, and I have no fear of Him. But His associates are ‘severe.’”

@95
although  on consideration  of the Paṇḍita  is not  fault
still  the Lord’s  associates  show  loving  anger
= Although on consideration the Paṇḍita was not at fault, the Lord’s associates still showed him an anger born of love (*praṇaya-roṣa*).

@96
every day  Vallabha  Bhaṭṭa  comes  to the Lord’s  place
‘disputes and the like’  as it were  makes  with the Ācārya and others
= Every day Vallabha Bhaṭṭa came to the Lord and started disputes, as it were, with the Ācārya and others.

@97
whatever  any  makes  the Bhaṭṭa  ‘conclusion’  establishing
on hearing it  the Ācārya  that  does  refute
= Whatever ‘conclusion’ (*siddhānta*) the Bhaṭṭa tried to establish, the Ācārya refuted the moment he heard it.

@98
of the Ācārya and others  before  the Bhaṭṭa  whenever  whenever  goes
royal swans  among  as  remains  a heron  like
= Whenever the Bhaṭṭa came before the Ācārya and the others, he was like a heron among royal swans.

@99
one day  the Bhaṭṭa  asked  the Ācārya
the jīva  ‘prakṛti’  ‘husband’  as  accepts  Kṛṣṇa
= One day the Bhaṭṭa asked the Ācārya: “The *jīva* is *prakṛti*, the female, and accepts Kṛṣṇa as her husband (*pati*).

@100
devoted to her husband  being  of the husband  the name  not  takes
you  of Kṛṣṇa  the name  take  what  religion  is
= A wife devoted to her husband does not utter her husband’s name. Yet you utter Kṛṣṇa’s name — what sort of *dharma* is that?”

@101
the Ācārya  says  before  you  ‘dharma’  embodied
Him  ask,  He  will give  the resolution
= The Ācārya said, “Before you stands *dharma* embodied. Ask Him; He will resolve the question.”

@102
the Lord  says  you  not  know  dharma and adharma
of the husband  the order  she follows  this  of the faithful wife  the dharma
= The Lord said, “You do not know what is *dharma* and what is *adharma*. A faithful wife’s *dharma* is to follow her husband’s order.

@103
the husband’s  order  ceaselessly  his  name  to take
the husband’s  order  the faithful wife  not  can  transgress
= The husband’s order is to utter his name ceaselessly, and a faithful wife cannot transgress her husband’s order.

@104
therefore  the name  she takes,  of the name  the fruit  she gets
by the name’s  fruit  for Kṛṣṇa’s feet  love  arises
= Therefore she utters the name and gains the fruit of the name: by that fruit love (*prema*) for Kṛṣṇa’s feet awakens.”

@105
hearing  Vallabha  Bhaṭṭa  became  speechless
home  going  in his mind  in sorrow  does  brooding
= Hearing this, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa fell silent. He went home and brooded sorrowfully:

@106
daily  my  in this  assembly  is  defeat
one day  on top  if  is  my  word
= “Every day I am defeated in this assembly. If only my word would prevail just once,

@107
then  happiness  is,  and  all  shame  goes
my own  statement  to establish  I  what  do  means
= I would be happy, and all my shame would be gone. What means can I find to establish my own view?”

@108
next  day  coming  he sat  to the Lord  bowing
in the assembly  says  something  in the mind  pride  holding
= The next day he came, bowed to the Lord, and sat down. Then, proud at heart, he spoke in the assembly:

@109
in the Bhāgavata  Svāmī’s  explanation  I have  refuted
to accept  not  can  his  explanation’s  words
= “In the Bhāgavata I have refuted Śrīdhara Svāmī’s explanations. I cannot accept the words of his commentary.

@110
that  explanation  he gives  wherever  whatever  he reads  taking up
consistency  is not,  therefore  ‘Svāmī’  not  I accept
= He explains whatever passage he takes up just as it stands there; there is no consistency in it. Therefore I do not accept Svāmī.”

@111
the Lord  smiling  says  svāmī  not  accepts  who  person
of prostitutes  among  him  I make  counting
= The Lord smiled and said, “Whoever does not accept the *svāmī* (the husband), I count among prostitutes.”

@112
this  saying  Mahāprabhu  silence  held
hearing  of all  in the minds  satisfaction  arose
= Having said this, Mahāprabhu fell silent. Hearing it, everyone was satisfied at heart.

@113
of the world  the good  for the sake  Gaura’s  descent
inner  pride  He knows  his
= Gaura descended for the good of the world, and He knew the pride hidden in the Bhaṭṭa’s heart.

@114
by various  disregard  the Bhaṭṭa  purifies  Bhagavān
Kṛṣṇa  as  broke  Indra’s  pride
= By various kinds of disregard Bhagavān purified the Bhaṭṭa, just as Kṛṣṇa broke the pride of Indra.

@115
ignorant  jīva  his own  ‘good’  ‘harm’  as  regards
pride  crushed  when,  afterwards  open  the eyes
= An ignorant *jīva* takes his own good for harm. When his pride has been crushed, his eyes open at last.

@116
home  coming  at night  the Bhaṭṭa  to think  began
earlier  in Prayāga  to me  great  mercy  He showed
= Back at home that night, the Bhaṭṭa began to think: “Earlier, in Prayāga, the Lord showed me great mercy.

@117
His associates  together with  my  accepted  invitation
now  why  the Lord’s  toward me  turned  went  mind
= With His associates He accepted my invitation. Why has the Lord’s mind now turned against me?

@118
‘I  win’  this  pride  free of  may become  his  mind
of the Lord  the nature  does  of all  the good
= ‘May his mind become free of this pride — “I shall win”’: such is His intention. It is the Lord’s nature to do good to all.

@119
myself  to make known  I  do  pride
that  pride  to break  me  does  dishonor
= To make a name for myself, I give way to pride; to break that pride, He dishonors me.

@120
my  ‘good’  He does,  this  I  regard  ‘sorrow’
Kṛṣṇa  toward  did  as  Indra  great fool
= He is doing me good, yet I take it as sorrow — just as Indra, that great fool, behaved toward Kṛṣṇa.”

@121
thus  thinking  in the morning  coming  at the Lord’s  feet
humility  showing  praise  offering  took  shelter
= Thinking this, he came to the Lord in the morning and, humbly offering praise, took shelter at His feet.

@122
I  ignorant  jīva  fit for the ignorant  deeds  I did
You  before  fool  I  learning  displayed
= “I am an ignorant *jīva*, and I acted as the ignorant act. Fool that I am, I displayed my learning before You.

@123
You  the Lord,  befitting Yourself  mercy  showed
dishonor  doing  all  pride  You broke
= You are the Lord, and You showed mercy befitting Yourself: by dishonoring me, You broke all my pride.

@124
I  ignorant,  ‘good’  in place of  I regard  ‘dishonor’
Indra  as  of Kṛṣṇa  blasphemy  did  in ignorance
= I am ignorant: in place of good I saw dishonor, just as Indra blasphemed Kṛṣṇa in ignorance.

@125
Your  mercy  by the salve  pride’s  blindness  went
You  so much  mercy  showed  now  ‘knowledge’  has come
= By the salve of Your mercy the blindness of my pride is gone. You have shown me such mercy that now true knowledge has come.
