# CC Madhya 15, stage A, part 2 (verses 152–302); read by build_en.py together with Madhya15.txt

@152
this  hearing  I  great  in the mind  joy  felt
him  raising  then  embrace  I made
= “Hearing this, I felt great joy at heart. Then I raised him up and embraced him.

@153
excellent  excellent  Gupta  your  firm  worship
My  even by the words  your  not  wavered  mind
= “ ‘Excellent, excellent, Gupta! Your worship is firm indeed: even My words did not shake your mind.

@154
such  indeed  of a servant  love  I want  for the Lord’s feet
the Lord  even if He makes him leave  the feet  to leave  not  is possible
= “ ‘This is the love for the Lord’s feet that I want in a servant: even if the Lord tries to make him leave them, he cannot let go of those feet.

@155
in this  way  your  steadfastness  to know  for the sake of
you  urging  I  did  time  after time
= “ ‘It was to learn how steadfast you are that I urged you again and again.

@156
in person  Hanumān  you  of Śrī Rāma  the servant
you  why  would leave  His  lotus feet
= “ ‘You are Hanumān himself, the servant of Śrī Rāma. Why would you ever give up His lotus feet?’

@157
that  Murāri Gupta  is this one  My  life  equal to
his  humility  hearing  My  bursts  life
= “This is that same Murāri Gupta, who is as dear to Me as My life. When I hear his humility, My heart breaks.”

@158
then  Vāsudeva  the Lord  making  embrace
his  qualities  speaks  becoming  thousand-mouthed
= Then the Lord embraced Vāsudeva and began to describe his qualities as if He had a thousand mouths.

@159
his own qualities  hearing  Datta  in his mind  shame  feeling
a petition  makes  of the Lord  the feet  clasping
= Hearing his own qualities praised, Vāsudeva Datta felt ashamed. Clasping the Lord’s feet, he made a petition:

@160
the world  to deliver  O Lord  Your  descent
my  petition  one  make  acceptance
= “O Lord, You have descended to deliver the world. Please grant me one request.

@161
to do  able  You  are  O merciful one
You  the mind  make  then  without effort  it happens
= “O merciful Lord, You are able to do anything. If You only wish it, it happens without effort.

@162
of living beings  the suffering  seeing  my  heart  breaks
of all living beings  the sins  O Lord  place  on my  head
= “My heart breaks to see the suffering of living beings. O Lord, place the sins of all living beings on my head.

@163
of living beings  the sins  taking  I  will undergo  of hell  the suffering
of all  living beings  O Lord  remove  the disease of material existence
= “Let me take the sins of the living beings and suffer in hell for them. O Lord, cure all living beings of the disease of material existence.”

@164
this  hearing  of Mahāprabhu  the heart  melted
with tears, trembling and a faltering voice  to speak  He began
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu’s heart melted. With tears, trembling and a faltering voice, He began to speak.

@165
in you  wonderful  it is not  you  in person  Prahlāda
upon  you  Kṛṣṇa’s  complete  grace
= “This is no wonder in you: you are Prahlāda himself. Kṛṣṇa’s grace rests on you in full.

@166
Kṛṣṇa  that  true  makes  whatever  asks  the servant
of the servant’s desire  fulfilling  except  there is not  other  task
= “Whatever His servant asks for, Kṛṣṇa makes true. He has no task other than fulfilling His servant’s desire.

@167
of the universe  of the living beings  you  have desired  deliverance
without  suffering for sins  will be  of all  deliverance
= “You have desired the deliverance of all beings in the universe, so all will be delivered without suffering for their sins.

@168
incapable  not  Kṛṣṇa  He holds  all  power
you  then  why  would He make suffer  of sins  the fruits
= “Kṛṣṇa is not powerless; He holds all power. Why, then, would He make you suffer the fruits of sin?

@169
you  whose  welfare  desire  he  has become  a Vaiṣṇava
of a Vaiṣṇava  the sins  Kṛṣṇa  away  removes  all
= “Anyone whose welfare you desire has become a Vaiṣṇava, and Kṛṣṇa removes all the sins of a Vaiṣṇava.

@170
who  but  the indragopa insect  or Indra  oh  of one’s own karma
to the bondage befitting  of the fruits  the enjoyment  grants
the karma  He burns up  but  indeed  of those who have devotion
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “I worship Govinda, the original Person. To each, from the tiny *indragopa* insect to Indra himself, He grants the fruits that match the bondage of his own karma; but the karma of those who have devotion He burns away.” (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.54)

@171
your  by the mere wish  will be  the universe’s liberation
all  liberated  to make  for Kṛṣṇa  there is no  labour
= “By your mere wish the universe will be liberated. To liberate everyone is no labour for Kṛṣṇa.

@172
on one  uḍumbara tree  grow  millions of fruits
millions  which  universes  float  of the Virajā  on the waters
= “As millions of fruits grow on a single *uḍumbara* tree, so millions of universes float on the waters of the Virajā.

@173
of it  one  fruit  falling  if  lost  becomes
still  the tree  not  knows  its own loss
= “If one of its fruits falls and is lost, the tree does not even notice its loss.

@174
so  one  universe  if  liberated  becomes
still  a slight loss  of Kṛṣṇa  in the mind  not  is taken
= “So too, if one universe is liberated, Kṛṣṇa does not take it as even a slight loss.

@175
infinite  opulence  of Kṛṣṇa  Vaikuṇṭha and the other abodes
their  moat  the Causal Ocean  whose  name
= “Kṛṣṇa’s opulence is infinite: Vaikuṇṭha and His other abodes. Their moat is the ocean called the Causal Ocean (*Kāraṇābdhi*).

@176
in it  float  māyā  with  infinite  universes
in the moat  floats  as  with mustard seeds filled  a pot
= “On it the infinite universes float together with *māyā*, like a pot full of mustard seeds floating in a moat.

@177
of it  one  mustard seed  in losing  loss  not  we count
so  one  universe  in losing  of Kṛṣṇa  there is no  loss
= “No one counts it a loss if one of those mustard seeds is lost; likewise, Kṛṣṇa loses nothing if one universe is lost.

@178
all  universes  along with  if  of māyā  there is  destruction
still  not  considers  Kṛṣṇa  any  loss
= “Even if *māyā* herself is destroyed along with all the universes, Kṛṣṇa does not consider it any loss.

@179
of millions of wish-fulfilling cows  of the owner  a she-goat  as  dies
of the six opulences the Lord  of Kṛṣṇa  māyā  what  does
= “It is like the death of a she-goat for the owner of millions of wish-fulfilling cows. What can *māyā* mean to Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of the six opulences?

@180
glory  glory  destroy  the unborn one  O unconquered  who for faults has taken up the qualities
You  are  since  by Yourself  holding all excellences in full
of the unmoving and the moving beings  of all potencies  the awakener  of You
sometimes  with māyā and with Your own potency  and  acting  describe  the Vedas
= “Glory, glory to You, O unconquered one! Destroy the unborn *māyā*, who has taken up the qualities to bring about faults, for in Yourself You hold all excellences in full. O awakener of all the potencies of moving and unmoving beings, the Vedas describe You sometimes as acting through *māyā* and sometimes through Your own potency.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.87.14)

@181
in this  way  of all the devotees  telling  all  qualities
to all  farewell  He gave  making  embrace
= In this way the Lord spoke of the qualities of all the devotees. Then He embraced them all and bade them farewell.
