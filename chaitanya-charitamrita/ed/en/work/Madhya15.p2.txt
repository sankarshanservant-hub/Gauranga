then  abuse  curses  hurling  the Bhaṭṭācārya  came back
the blasphemy  hearing  Mahāprabhu  to laugh  began
= Then the Bhaṭṭācārya came back, cursing and abusing him. Hearing all this reviling, Mahāprabhu began to laugh.

@252
hearing  Ṣāṭhī’s  mother  on head and breast  blows  strikes
“may Ṣāṭhī  a widow  become”  this  she says  again  and again
= Hearing of it, Ṣāṭhī’s mother beat her head and breast and cried again and again, “Let Ṣāṭhī become a widow!”

@253
of both  the grief  seeing  the Lord  both  consoling
of both  by the wish  meal  took  pleased  being
= Seeing the grief of them both, the Lord consoled them, and to fulfil their wish He ate, well pleased.

@254
ācamana  having Him do  Bhaṭṭa  gave  mouth fresheners
tulasī buds  cloves  cardamom  juicy and fragrant
= When the Lord had rinsed His mouth, Bhaṭṭa gave Him mouth fresheners: tulasī buds, cloves and juicy, fragrant cardamom.

@255
over all the limbs  smeared  of the Lord  fragrant  sandalwood
prostrate  becoming  says  humble  words
= He smeared fragrant sandalwood paste all over the Lord’s body and, falling prostrate, spoke humbly:

@256
blasphemy  to have done  You  I brought  to my own house
this  offence  O Lord  pardon  make  me
= “I brought You to my house only to have You blasphemed. O Lord, forgive me this offence.”

@257
the Lord  says  blasphemy  it is not  the plain truth  he spoke
in this  your  his  what  offence  has been
= The Lord said, “That was not blasphemy; he only spoke plainly. What offence is there in this, either on your part or on his?”

@258
this  saying  Mahāprabhu  went  to His dwelling
the Bhaṭṭācārya  to His  house  went  Him  with
= With these words Mahāprabhu went home, and the Bhaṭṭācārya went with Him to His residence.

@259
at the Lord’s feet  falling  much  self-reproach  made
him  calm  making  the Lord  home  sent
= Falling at the Lord’s feet, he reproached himself bitterly. The Lord calmed him and sent him home.

@260
home  coming  the Bhaṭṭācārya  with Ṣāṭhī’s mother
himself  reproaching  some  says  words
= Returning home, the Bhaṭṭācārya reproached himself and said to Ṣāṭhī’s mother:

@261
of Caitanya Gosāñi  blasphemy  I hear  whom  from
him  killing  if done  there is  for the sin  atonement
= “To kill the man from whom I have heard blasphemy of Caitanya Gosāñi would be the atonement for that sin.

@262
or  my own life  if  I make  giving up
both  fitting  not  both  bodies  brāhmaṇa
= “Or I could give up my own life. But neither is proper, for both bodies are those of brāhmaṇas.

@263
again  that  blasphemer’s  face  not  I shall see
renunciation  I have made  his  name  not  I shall take
= “I shall never look on that blasphemer’s face again. I have renounced him, and I shall not speak his name.

@264
to Ṣāṭhī  say  him  let her leave  he  has become  fallen
fallen  if becomes  a husband  to give up  proper
= “Tell Ṣāṭhī to leave him: he has fallen (*patita*). When a husband has fallen, it is proper to give him up.

@265
a husband  and  fallen  one should give up
= “ ‘One should give up a fallen husband.’ ”

@266
that  night  Amogha  somewhere  having fled  stayed
in the morning  his  cholera  occurred
= That night Amogha fled and hid somewhere, and in the morning he was stricken with cholera.

@267
Amogha  is dying  hearing  says  the Bhaṭṭācārya
helper  becoming  providence  has done  my  task
= Hearing that Amogha was dying, the Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Providence has come to my aid and done my work for me.

@268
against the Lord  indeed  an offence  bears fruit  at once
this  saying  he recites  two  of scripture  sayings
= “An offence against the Lord bears fruit at once.” So saying, he recited two verses of scripture:

@269
by great  indeed  effort
with elephants, horses, chariots and foot soldiers
by us  what  had to be done
by the Gandharvas  that  has been done
= “What we would have had to do with great effort, with elephants, horses, chariots and foot soldiers, the Gandharvas have done.” (Mahābhārata, Vana-parva 241.15)

@270
life  fortune  fame  dharma
the higher worlds  blessings  also  and
destroys  benefits  all
of a man  offence against the great
= “Offence against great souls destroys a man’s life, fortune, fame and dharma, his higher worlds and blessings — all that is good for him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.4.46)

@271
Gopīnātha Ācārya  went  to see the Lord
the Lord  him  asked  about the Bhaṭṭācārya’s affairs
= Gopīnātha Ācārya went to see the Lord, and the Lord asked him about the Bhaṭṭācārya.

@272
the Ācārya  says  fast  have kept  the two
from cholera  Amogha  is leaving  life
= The Ācārya said, “The two of them are fasting, and Amogha is dying of cholera.”

@273
hearing  merciful  the Lord  came  running
to Amogha  says  his  on the chest  hand  placing
= Hearing this, the merciful Lord came running. Placing His hand on Amogha’s chest, He said:

@274
by nature  pure  this  brāhmaṇa’s heart
for Kṛṣṇa  to sit  this  a fit place  is
= “This heart of a brāhmaṇa is pure by nature; it is a fit place for Kṛṣṇa to sit.

@275
the caṇḍāla of envy  why  here  have you seated
supremely  pure  place  impure  you have made
= “Why have you seated the *caṇḍāla* of envy here? You have made this supremely pure place impure.

@276
in Sārvabhauma’s company  your  impurity  has become  destroyed
the taint  when removed  the soul  Kṛṣṇa’s name  takes
= “By Sārvabhauma’s company your impurity has been destroyed, and when the taint is gone, a soul takes the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@277
get up  Amogha  you  take  Kṛṣṇa’s name
soon  on you  mercy  will show  Bhagavān
= “Get up, Amogha! Take the name of Kṛṣṇa, and Bhagavān will soon show you His mercy.”

@278
hearing  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  Amogha  got up
in the madness of love  intoxicated  becoming  to dance  began
= Hearing this, Amogha rose up crying, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” and, mad with love, began to dance.

@279
trembling  tears  hair standing on end  becoming stunned  perspiration  faltering voice
the Lord  smiles  seeing  his  of love  the waves
= Trembling, tears, hair standing on end, stupor, perspiration and a faltering voice appeared in him, and seeing these waves of love, the Lord smiled.

@280
of the Lord  the feet  clasping  makes  entreaty
offence  pardon  me  O Lord  merciful
= Clasping the Lord’s feet, he humbly entreated Him: “O merciful Lord, forgive my offence.

@281
this  wretched  with mouth  of You  I made  blasphemy
this  saying  his own  cheeks  slaps  himself
= “With this wretched mouth I blasphemed You!” Saying this, he began to slap his own cheeks.

@282
slapping  slapping  the cheeks  he swelled
by the hands  seizing  Gopīnātha Ācārya  stopped him
= He slapped himself until his cheeks were swollen. Then Gopīnātha Ācārya seized his hands and stopped him.

@283
the Lord  consolation  gives  touching  his  body
by relation to Sārvabhauma  you  My  object of affection
= The Lord consoled him, touching his body: “Because you are related to Sārvabhauma, you are dear to Me.

@284
in Sārvabhauma’s house  servants  maids  even  dogs
they too  to Me  dear  other  people  let stay far
= “Even the servants, maids and dogs in Sārvabhauma’s house are dear to Me — what to speak of others.

@285
offence  there is no  in you  take  Kṛṣṇa’s name
this  saying  the Lord  came  to Sārvabhauma’s place
= “There is no offence left in you. Take the name of Kṛṣṇa.” With these words the Lord went to Sārvabhauma’s house.

@286
the Lord  seeing  Sārvabhauma  clasped  the feet
the Lord  him  embracing  sat  on a seat
= Seeing the Lord, Sārvabhauma clasped His feet. The Lord embraced him and sat down.

@287
the Lord  says  Amogha  a child  what  his  fault
why  fast  you keep  why  you nurse  anger
= The Lord said, “Amogha is a child. What fault is there in him? Why are you fasting, and why are you angry?

@288
get up  bath  take  see  Jagannātha’s face
quickly  coming back  meal  take  then  My  happiness
= “Get up, bathe and go to see Jagannātha’s face. Then come back quickly and eat; that will make Me happy.

@289
so long  shall stay  I  here  sitting
as long as  not  honour  you  prasāda  bringing
= “I shall sit here until you have brought *prasāda* and honoured it.”

@290
of the Lord  the feet  clasping  Bhaṭṭa  to speak  began
would have died  Amogha  him  why  You revived
= Clasping the Lord’s feet, Bhaṭṭa said, “Amogha would have died. Why did You bring him back to life?”

@291
the Lord  says  Amogha  a child  your  boy
the boy’s fault  not  takes  the father  for in that  he is the protector
= The Lord said, “Amogha is a child; he is your son. A father does not hold his child’s faults against him, for he is his protector.

@292
now  a Vaiṣṇava  he has become  his  went  offence
him  on  now  show  grace
= “Now he has become a Vaiṣṇava, and his offence is gone. Now show him your favour.”

@293
Bhaṭṭa  says  go  O Lord  to see Īśvara (Jagannātha)
bath  having taken  here  I  am back  at once
= Bhaṭṭa said, “Go, O Lord, to see Jagannātha. I shall bathe and be back here at once.”

@294
the Lord  says  Gopīnātha  right here  stay
he  prasāda  having honoured  the news  to Me  tell
= The Lord said, “Gopīnātha, stay right here. When he has honoured the *prasāda*, bring Me word.”

@295
this  saying  the Lord  went  to see Īśvara
Bhaṭṭa  bath  sight  having made  took  meal
= With these words the Lord went to see Jagannātha, and Bhaṭṭa bathed, took *darśana* and ate.

@296
that  Amogha  became  the Lord’s  devotee  exclusive
in love  dances  Kṛṣṇa’s name  takes  deeply peaceful
= That same Amogha became the Lord’s exclusive devotee. He would dance in love and chant the name of Kṛṣṇa, deeply at peace.

@297
such  wonderful pastimes  does  Śacī’s  son
whoever  sees  hears  his  amazed  becomes  mind
= Such are the wonderful pastimes of the son of Śacī: whoever sees or hears of them is struck with wonder.

@298
thus  in Bhaṭṭa’s house  does  the pastime of eating
it  within  various  wonderful deeds  revealing
= Thus the Lord enjoyed the pastime of eating at Bhaṭṭa’s house, and in it He revealed many wonderful deeds.

@299
in Sārvabhauma’s house  this  story of the meal
Sārvabhauma’s love  in which  became  known
= This is the story of the Lord’s meal in Sārvabhauma’s house, in which Sārvabhauma’s love became known,

@300
of Ṣāṭhī’s mother  the love  and  the Lord’s  grace
by relation to a devotee  by which  He pardoned  offence
= and the love of Ṣāṭhī’s mother, and the grace of the Lord, by which He forgave an offence because of the offender’s relation to His devotee.

@301
with faith  making  this  pastime  hears  whoever  person
soon  attains  he  Caitanya’s feet
= Whoever hears this pastime with faith will soon attain the feet of Śrī Caitanya.

@302
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
