@title The Lord’s Youthful Pastimes

@1
of mercy  of nectar  the river  whose
the world  flooding  even though
flowing downward  always  shines
Him  Lord Caitanya  I worship
= I worship Lord Caitanya. Though the river of His nectarean mercy floods the whole world, it always shines as a river flowing downward — toward the lowly.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s devotees  all
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
may He be glorious  youthful  Caitanya
by her who took form  through the household order
by Lakṣmī worshiped  then  by the goddess of speech
of the directions  of the conqueror  of the conquest  on the pretext
= May the youthful Caitanya be glorious! When He entered household life, Lakṣmī in person worshiped Him; and then, on the pretext of His victory over the conqueror of all directions, the goddess of speech worshiped Him too.

@4
this  indeed  of the kaiśora pastimes  the sūtra  outline
students  to teach  made  a beginning
= This is the outline, in *sūtras*, of His *kaiśora* (youthful) pastimes: He began to teach students.

@5
hundreds  hundreds  students  with  always  teaching
explanations  hearing  of all people  astonished  the minds
= He was always teaching, with hundreds upon hundreds of students around Him, and everyone who heard His explanations was astonished.

@6
in all scriptures  all  scholars  obtain  defeat
by His humble manner  anyone’s  pain  not  is
= In every scripture every scholar met defeat at His hands, yet His manner was so courteous that no one was hurt.

@7
various  pranks  does  students  with
in the Jāhnavī  water sports  does  in many  playful ways
= With His students He played all kinds of high-spirited pranks, and He sported in the waters of the Jāhnavī (the Gaṅgā) in many playful ways.

@8
after some  days  made  the Lord  to Vaṅga  going
wherever  He goes  there  makes them take up  the congregational chanting of the name
= Some days later the Lord went to Vaṅga (East Bengal). Wherever He went, He had people take up the congregational chanting of the holy name.

@9
of learning  the power  seeing  wonder  in the heart
hundreds  hundreds  students  coming  began  to study
= Seeing the power of His learning, people marveled at heart, and hundreds upon hundreds of students came to study with Him.

@10
in that  land  a brāhmaṇa  name  Miśra Tapana
certainty  to make  could not  the goal and the means
= In that land lived a brāhmaṇa named Tapana Miśra. He could not settle what the goal of life (*sādhya*) is, nor the means of attaining it (*sādhana*).

@11
by many scriptures  by many statements  in the mind  confusion  arises
of goal and means  the best  not  becomes  certain
= Many scriptures and many opinions bewildered his mind, and he could not be sure which goal and which means are the highest.

@12
in a dream  a  brāhmaṇa  says  listen  Tapana
to Nimāi Paṇḍita’s place  make  going
= In a dream a brāhmaṇa told him, “Listen, Tapana: go to Nimāi Paṇḍita.

@13
He  your  goal and means  will make  certain
directly  the Lord  He  there is no  doubt
= He will settle for you the goal and the means. He is the Lord Himself — there is no doubt about it.”

@14
the dream  having seen  Miśra  coming  to the Lord’s  feet
of the dream  the account  all  made  submission
= After this dream Miśra came to the Lord’s feet and told Him the whole dream.

@15
the Lord  pleased  becoming  the goal and the means  told
the congregational chanting of the name  do  instruction  gave
= Pleased, the Lord told him the goal and the means. He instructed him: “Perform the congregational chanting of the holy name.”

@16
his  wish  with the Lord  in Navadvīpa  to live
the Lord  order  gave  you  go  to Vārāṇasī
= Tapana Miśra wished to live with the Lord in Navadvīpa, but the Lord ordered him, “Go to Vārāṇasī.

@17
there  with Me  your  will be  meeting
the order  receiving  Miśra  made  to Kāśī  going
= There you will meet Me.” Receiving this order, Miśra went to Kāśī.

@18
the Lord’s  endless  pastimes  to understand  not  I can
His own company  making him give up  why  sends  to the city of Kāśī
= I cannot understand the Lord’s endless pastimes: why does He deprive Miśra of His company and send him to the city of Kāśī?

@19
in this  way  of Vaṅga  of the people  did  of all  good
the name  giving  devotees  made  by teaching  scholars
= In this way He did good to all the people of Vaṅga: by giving them the holy name He made them devotees, and by teaching them He made them scholars.

@20
in this  way  in Vaṅga  the Lord  does  various  pastimes
here  in Navadvīpa  Lakṣmī  in separation  sorrowful  became
= While the Lord performed various pastimes in Vaṅga, here in Navadvīpa Lakṣmī grew sorrowful in separation from Him.

@21
the Lord’s  separation’s  serpent  Lakṣmī  bit
of the serpent of separation  by the venom  her  the other world  became
= The serpent of separation from the Lord bit Lakṣmī, and by the venom of that serpent she departed for the other world.

@22
within  knew  the Lord  since  the knower of hearts
to His homeland  came  the Lord  Śacī’s grief  knowing
= The Lord knew of it within, for He knows every heart. Knowing Śacī’s grief, He returned to His homeland.

@23
home  came  the Lord  much  bringing  wealth and people
the truth  speaking  made  Śacī’s  grief  dispelling
= The Lord came home bringing much wealth and many followers, and by speaking the truth He dispelled Śacī’s grief.

@24
students  taking  again  of learning  the play
by the strength of learning  all  conquering  pride  displaying
= Again He delighted in learning with His students; conquering everyone by the strength of His learning, He displayed a scholar’s pride.

@25
then  with Viṣṇupriyā  the goddess  the marriage
then  indeed  made  the Lord  of the digvijayī  conquest
= Then came His marriage with Viṣṇupriyā Ṭhākurāṇī, and after that the Lord conquered the *digvijayī* (the conqueror of all directions).

@26
Vṛndāvana dāsa  this  has made  in detail
clearly  not  does  of faults and merits  the analysis
= Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail, but he did not set out clearly the analysis of the faults and merits.

@27
that  part  I tell  to him  making  obeisance
which  hearing  the digvijayī  made  himself  reproach
= Bowing to him, I shall tell that part — hearing it, the *digvijayī* reproached himself.

@28
moonlit  night  the Lord  students  with
was sitting  on the bank of the Gaṅgā  of learning  in discussion
= One moonlit night the Lord was sitting with His students on the bank of the Gaṅgā, discussing points of learning.

@29
at that time  the digvijayī  right there  came
to the Gaṅgā  worship  making  the Lord  met
= Just then the *digvijayī* arrived there. After offering prayers to the Gaṅgā, he met the Lord.

@30
seated  him  the Lord  respect  showing
the digvijayī  says  in his mind  contempt  holding
= The Lord respectfully gave him a seat, and the *digvijayī*, inwardly contemptuous, said:

@31
grammar  You teach  Nimāi  Paṇḍita  Your  name
in the children’s science  people  Your  speak of  many virtues
= “You teach grammar, and Your name is Nimāi Paṇḍita. People speak of Your many virtues in the children’s science (*bālya-śāstra*, grammar).

@32
grammars  among  I know  You teach  the Kalāpa
I have heard  in tricky puzzles  Your  students’  conversation
= Among the grammars, I know, You teach the *Kalāpa*. I have heard Your students talking in tricky verbal puzzles (*phāṅki*).”

@33
the Lord  says  grammar  I teach  conceit  I hold
the students  not  understand  I  explain  cannot
= The Lord said, “I teach grammar — so I fancy. But My students do not understand, and I cannot make them understand.

@34
where  you  in all scriptures  in poetry  expert
where  I  merely  a child  a student  new
= What comparison is there between you, expert in all the scriptures and in poetry, and Me — a mere child, a novice student?

@35
your  poetry  some  to hear  is  My mind
mercy  showing  make  if  of the Gaṅgā  description
= I would like to hear some of your poetry, if you would kindly describe the Gaṅgā.”

@36
hearing  the brāhmaṇa  with pride  to describe  began
in a ghaṭī  one  a hundred  verses  the Gaṅgā  described
= Hearing this, the brāhmaṇa proudly began, and within a single *ghaṭī* (twenty-four minutes) he described the Gaṅgā in a hundred verses.

@37
hearing  made  the Lord  much  honor
you  like  on earth  poet  is not  another
= When He had heard them, the Lord honored him greatly: “On earth there is no other poet like you.

@38
your  poem’s  verses  to understand  whose  power
you  well  know  the meaning  or  Sarasvatī
= Who has the power to understand the verses of your poem? Only you know their meaning well — or Sarasvatī.

@39
of one  verse  the meaning  if  you make  with your own mouth
hearing  all  people  then  obtain  great happiness
= If you yourself explain the meaning of one verse, everyone will hear it with great delight.”

@40
then  the digvijayī  for explanation  the verse  asked
of the hundred verses  one  verse  the Lord  then  recited
= Then the *digvijayī* asked which verse to explain, and the Lord recited one of the hundred verses.
