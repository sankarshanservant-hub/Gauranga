@title The Branches of Śrī Nityānanda’s Trunk

@1
of Nityānanda  of the feet  lotus
the bees  with love’s  honey  maddened
having bowed  to all  among them  the chief
are written  a few  by me
= Bowing to all the bees at Nityānanda’s lotus feet, maddened by the honey of love, I write here of a few of the foremost among them.

@2
glory  glory  to Mahāprabhu  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
at His  feet  sheltered  whoever  he  is blessed
= Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever takes shelter at His feet is blessed.

@3
glory  glory  to Śrī Advaita  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  glory  of Mahāprabhu  all the devotees  the host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Advaita! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory, glory to all the devotees of Mahāprabhu!

@4
of that  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
of pure love  immortal  of the tree
the upper  trunk  of the Avadhūta-moon
as branches  the followers  we praise
= Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the immortal tree of pure love, and the moonlike Avadhūta is its upper trunk. We bow to His followers, who are the branches.

@5
of the Śrī Nityānanda tree  the trunk  very mighty
on it  grew  branches  and sub-branches  in abundance
= The trunk of the Śrī Nityānanda tree is very mighty, and on it grew branches and sub-branches in abundance.

@6
of the gardener  by the water of will  grow  the branches
with love’s  flowers  and fruits  filling  covered  the world
= Watered by the gardener’s will, the branches grow; laden with the flowers and fruits of love, they have covered the world.

@7
countless  endless  followers  who  can  count
to purify  myself  I name  the chief  the chief  persons
= Who can count these countless, endless followers? To purify myself, I name only the chief among them.

@8
Śrī Vīrabhadra  Gosāñi  of the trunk  the great branch
His  sub-branches  as many  countless  their  account
= Śrī Vīrabhadra Gosāñi is the great branch of the trunk. His sub-branches are countless; no one could write of them all.

@9
the Lord  being  calls Himself  a great devotee
beyond Vedic duty  being  in Vedic duty  engaged
= Though He is the Lord, He calls Himself a great devotee; though He is beyond the duties of the Vedas, He keeps to them.

@10
within  the Lord’s  activity  outwardly  free of pride
of devotion to Caitanya  in the hall  He  the main  pillar
= Within, He acts as the Lord; outwardly, He is free of pride. He is the main pillar in the hall of devotion to Caitanya.

@11
even today  whose  of mercy  the glory  through
Caitanya and Nityānanda  sing  all  in the world
= Even today, through the glory of His mercy, the whole world sings of Caitanya and Nityānanda.

@12
of that  Vīrabhadra Gosāñi  the feet  the shelter
by whose  grace  is  of the cherished desire  fulfillment
= The feet of that Vīrabhadra Gosāñi are my shelter; by His grace one’s cherished desires are fulfilled.

@13
Śrī Rāmadāsa  and  Gadādhara dāsa
of Caitanya Gosāñi  devotees  stayed  his  at the side
= Śrī Rāmadāsa and Gadādhara dāsa, devotees of Caitanya Gosāñi, stayed at Nityānanda’s side.

@14
to Nityānanda  the order  gave  when  to Gauḍa  to go
Mahāprabhu  these  two  gave  Him  with
= When Mahāprabhu ordered Nityānanda to go to Gauḍa, He sent these two with Him.

@15
therefore  in both groups  of both  the counting
of Mādhava and Vāsudeva  Ghoṣa  this  the explanation
= That is why both are counted in both groups. The same holds for Mādhava and Vāsudeva Ghoṣa.

@16
Rāmadāsa  a chief branch  of friendship’s  love  a treasury
of sixteen bearers  the log  who  lifting  made  a flute
= Rāmadāsa is a chief branch, a treasury of love in friendship. He lifted a log that took sixteen men to carry and made it into a flute.

@17
Gadādhara dāsa  in the gopīs’ mood  full of bliss
in whose  house  the toll-pastime  performed  Nityānanda
= Gadādhara dāsa is full of bliss in the mood of the gopīs. In his house Nityānanda performed the toll-pastime (*dāna-keli*).

@18
Śrī Mādhava  Ghoṣa  chief  among the kīrtana singers
Nityānanda Prabhu  dance  does  to whose  singing
= Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa is chief among the kīrtana singers; Nityānanda Prabhu dances to his singing.

@19
Vāsudeva  in song  makes  of the Lord  description
wood  and stone  melt  which  on hearing
= Vāsudeva describes the Lord in song; hearing it, even wood and stone melt.

@20
of Murāri Caitanya dāsa  extraordinary  pastimes
of a tiger  on the cheek  a slap  gives  with snakes  play
= The pastimes of Murāri Caitanya dāsa are extraordinary: he slaps tigers on the cheek and plays with snakes.

@21
of Nityānanda  the followers  as many  all  friends of Vraja
horn  stick  cowherd dress  on the head  peacock feather
= All of Nityānanda’s followers are cowherd friends of Vraja, with horns, sticks and cowherd dress, and peacock feathers on their heads.

@22
Raghunātha  Vaidya  Upādhyāya  great soul
at whose  sight  for Kṛṣṇa  loving devotion  arises
= Raghunātha Vaidya Upādhyāya is a great soul: at the sight of him, loving devotion to Kṛṣṇa awakens.

@23
Sundarānanda  of Nityānanda  a branch  servant  intimate
with whom  Nityānanda  enjoys  the playful jests of Vraja
= Sundarānanda is a branch of Nityānanda and His intimate servant; with him Nityānanda enjoys the playful jests of Vraja.

@24
Kamalākara  Pippalāi  extraordinary  ways
extraordinary  love  his  in the world  known
= The ways of Kamalākara Pippalāi are extraordinary, and his extraordinary love is known throughout the world.

@25
Sūryadāsa  Sarakhela  his  brother  Kṛṣṇadāsa
in Nityānanda  firm  faith  of love  the abode
= Sūryadāsa Sarakhela and his brother Kṛṣṇadāsa have firm faith in Nityānanda; they are abodes of love.

@26
in Śrī Gaurīdāsa  Paṇḍita  devotion overflowing with love
love for Kṛṣṇa  to give  to take  holds  great power
= Śrī Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita has devotion overflowing with love. He has great power both to give and to receive love for Kṛṣṇa.

@27
to Nityānanda  offered  caste  family  community
Śrī Caitanya and Nityānanda  making  lords of his life
= He offered his caste, family and community to Nityānanda, making Śrī Caitanya and Nityānanda the lords of his life.

@28
of Nityānanda  Prabhu  dear  Paṇḍita  Purandara
of the ocean of love  in the midst  moves  like  Mandara
= Paṇḍita Purandara, dear to Nityānanda Prabhu, moves through the ocean of love like Mount Mandara.

@29
Parameśvara dāsa  Nityānanda his only shelter
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  attains  him  who  remembers
= Parameśvara dāsa takes shelter in Nityānanda alone. Whoever remembers him attains devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

@30
Śrī  Jagadīśa  Paṇḍita  is  of the world  the purifier
of love for Kṛṣṇa  the nectar  rains  like  rain  heavy
= Śrī Jagadīśa Paṇḍita is the purifier of the world. He showers the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa like heavy monsoon rain.

@31
Nityānanda’s  dear  servant  Paṇḍita  Dhanañjaya
utterly  detached  always  full of love for Kṛṣṇa
= Paṇḍita Dhanañjaya, Nityānanda’s dear servant, is utterly detached and always full of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@32
Maheśa  Paṇḍita  of Vraja  generous  cowherd boy
to the drum’s beat  dance  does  with love  intoxicated
= Maheśa Paṇḍita is a generous cowherd boy of Vraja. Intoxicated with love, he dances to the beat of the kettledrum.

@33
in Navadvīpa  Puruṣottama  Paṇḍita  great soul
at Nityānanda’s name  whose  great madness  arises
= In Navadvīpa lives the great soul Puruṣottama Paṇḍita, who goes wild with ecstasy at the name of Nityānanda.

@34
Balarāma dāsa  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the taste  relishing
at Nityānanda’s name  becomes  utterly  mad
= Balarāma dāsa relishes the taste of love for Kṛṣṇa; at the name of Nityānanda he becomes utterly mad with ecstasy.

@35
great devotee  Yadunātha  Kavicandra
in whose  heart  dance  does  Nityānanda
= Yadunātha Kavicandra is a great devotee, in whose heart Nityānanda dances.

@36
in Rāḍha  whose  birth  Kṛṣṇadāsa  best of brāhmaṇas
of Śrī Nityānanda  he  the supreme  servant
= Kṛṣṇadāsa, the best of brāhmaṇas, who was born in Rāḍha, is a supreme servant of Śrī Nityānanda.

@37
Kālā  Kṛṣṇadāsa  great  foremost Vaiṣṇava
the Nityānanda-moon  except  not  knows  anything else
= Kālā Kṛṣṇadāsa is a great and foremost Vaiṣṇava; he knows nothing but the moonlike Nityānanda.

@38
Śrī Sadāśiva  Kavirāja  great  soul
Śrī Puruṣottama dāsa  his  son
= Śrī Sadāśiva Kavirāja is a great soul, and Śrī Puruṣottama dāsa is his son.

@39
from birth  absorbed  in Nityānanda’s  feet
constantly  childhood play  does  with Kṛṣṇa
= From birth he was absorbed in Nityānanda’s feet, and he constantly played the games of childhood with Kṛṣṇa.

@40
his  son  great soul  Śrī Kānu  Ṭhākura
in whose  body  dwells  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the nectar  a flood
= His son is the great soul Śrī Kānu Ṭhākura, in whose body dwells a flood of the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@41
of great devotees  the best  Datta  Uddhāraṇa
in every way  serves  Nityānanda’s  feet
= Uddhāraṇa Datta, the best of great devotees, serves the feet of Nityānanda in every way.

@42
Ācārya  Vaiṣṇavānanda  in devotion  qualified
formerly  name  was  whose  Raghunātha Purī
= Ācārya Vaiṣṇavānanda is fully qualified in devotion; formerly his name was Raghunātha Purī.

@43
Viṣṇudāsa  Nandana  Gaṅgādāsa  three  brothers
formerly  in whose  house  stayed  Ṭhākura  Nitāi
= Viṣṇudāsa, Nandana and Gaṅgādāsa are three brothers, in whose house Ṭhākura Nitāi once stayed.

@44
Nityānanda’s servant  Paramānanda  Upādhyāya
Śrī Jīva  Paṇḍita  Nityānanda’s  qualities  sings
= Paramānanda Upādhyāya is Nityānanda’s servant, and Śrī Jīva Paṇḍita sings of Nityānanda’s qualities.

@45
Paramānanda  Gupta  devotee of Kṛṣṇa  great-minded
formerly  in whose  house  Nityānanda’s  dwelling
= Paramānanda Gupta is a great-minded devotee of Kṛṣṇa, in whose house Nityānanda once lived.

@46
Nārāyaṇa  Kṛṣṇadāsa  and  Manohara
Devānanda  four  brothers  Nitāi’s  servants
= Nārāyaṇa, Kṛṣṇadāsa, Manohara and Devānanda — these four brothers are servants of Nitāi.

@47
Hoḍa  Kṛṣṇadāsa  Nityānanda Prabhu his life
Śrī Nityānanda’s  feet  except  not  knows  anything else
= For Hoḍa Kṛṣṇadāsa, Nityānanda Prabhu is life itself; he knows nothing but the feet of Śrī Nityānanda.

@48
Nakaḍi  Mukunda  Sūrya  Mādhava  Śrīdhara
Rāmānanda  Vasu  Jagannātha  Mahīdhara
= Nakaḍi, Mukunda, Sūrya, Mādhava, Śrīdhara, Rāmānanda Vasu, Jagannātha, Mahīdhara,

@49
Śrīmanta  Gokula dāsa  Hariharānanda
Śivāi  Nandāi  Avadhūta  Paramānanda
= Śrīmanta, Gokula dāsa, Hariharānanda, Śivāi, Nandāi, Avadhūta Paramānanda,

@50
Vasanta  Navanī  Hoḍa  Gopāla  Sanātana
Viṣṇāi  Hājarā  Kṛṣṇānanda  Sulocana
= Vasanta, Navanī Hoḍa, Gopāla, Sanātana, Viṣṇāi Hājarā, Kṛṣṇānanda, Sulocana,

@51
Kaṁsāri  Sena  Rāmasena  Rāmacandra  Kavirāja
Govinda  Śrīraṅga  Mukunda  three  physicians
= Kaṁsāri Sena, Rāmasena, Rāmacandra Kavirāja, the three physicians Govinda, Śrīraṅga and Mukunda,

@52
Pītāmbara  Mādhavācārya  dāsa  Dāmodara
Śaṅkara  Mukunda  Jñānadāsa  Manohara
= Pītāmbara, Mādhavācārya, Dāmodara dāsa, Śaṅkara, Mukunda, Jñānadāsa, Manohara,

@53
the dancer  Gopāla  Rāmabhadra  Gaurāṅga dāsa
Nṛsiṁha Caitanya  Mīnaketana  Rāmadāsa
= the dancer Gopāla, Rāmabhadra, Gaurāṅga dāsa, Nṛsiṁha Caitanya and Mīnaketana Rāmadāsa.

@54
Vṛndāvana dāsa  Nārāyaṇī’s  son
the Caitanya-maṅgala  who  made  composition
= Vṛndāvana dāsa is the son of Nārāyaṇī; he composed the *Caitanya-maṅgala* (the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*).

@55
in the Bhāgavata  Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  described  Vedavyāsa
in Caitanya’s pastimes  the Vyāsa  Vṛndāvana dāsa
= Vedavyāsa described Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes in the *Bhāgavata*; Vṛndāvana dāsa is the Vyāsa of Caitanya’s pastimes.

@56
of all branches  the best  Vīrabhadra  Gosāñi
His  sub-branches  as many  of them  end  is not
= Vīrabhadra Gosāñi is the best of all the branches, and His sub-branches are endless.

@57
endless  Nityānanda’s followers  who  can  count
for self-purification  I have written  a few  persons
= Nityānanda’s followers are endless — who can count them? To purify myself, I have written of a few.

@58
these  all branches  full  with ripe fruits of love
whom  they see  to him  giving  flooded  everyone
= All these branches are laden with the ripe fruits of love. Giving them to whomever they met, they flooded everyone with love.

@59
unrestrained  love  of all  activity  unrestrained
love  to give  Kṛṣṇa  to give  all  hold  great power
= Their love knows no bounds, and neither do their deeds. All of them have great power to give love and to give Kṛṣṇa.

@60
in brief  I have told  these  Nityānanda’s followers
whose  limit  not  finds  the thousand-mouthed
= Thus I have briefly told of Nityānanda’s followers, whose limit even the thousand-mouthed one cannot reach.

@61
of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  at the feet  whose  hope
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, tell the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
