= His father, Śatānanda Khān, was a great man of worldly affairs, but the Ācārya had turned away from worldly things: renunciation was foremost in him.

@95

= Even a *mahā-bhāgavata*, whose treasure of life is Kṛṣṇa, will surely have his heart turned by hearing Māyāvāda.”

@97

= The Ācārya said, “Our hearts are fixed on Kṛṣṇa; a commentary cannot turn our minds.”

= Ashamed and afraid, the Ācārya fell silent, and the next day he sent Gopāla back to his native place.

= One day the Ācārya invited the Lord. At home he cooked rice and made various vegetable dishes.
