# CC Madhya 14, stage A, part 2 (verses 129–257); read by build_en.py together with Madhya14.txt

@129
umbrellas, cāmaras, standards  of flags  multitudes
ahead of many instruments  dance  the temple dancers
= There were multitudes of umbrellas, *cāmaras*, standards and flags, and ahead of the many musical instruments danced the temple dancers (*deva-dāsīs*).

@130
betel boxes  water jugs  fans  cāmaras
with (her)  maidservants  a hundred  necklaces  divine  ornaments and garments
= Hundreds of maidservants accompanied her with betel boxes, water jugs, fans and *cāmaras*, wearing necklaces and divine ornaments and garments.

@131
great  splendor  with  a large retinue
angry  becoming  Lakṣmī-devī  came (to)  the Lion Gate
= With great splendor and a large retinue, Lakṣmī-devī came in anger to the Lion Gate.

@132
Jagannātha’s  chief  chief  all  servants
Lakṣmī-devī’s  maidservants  do  binding
= Lakṣmī-devī’s maidservants seized all of Jagannātha’s chief servants and bound them.

@133
binding  bringing  throw down  Lakṣmī’s  at the feet
on thieves  punishment  they inflict  as if  they take  various riches
= Having bound them, they brought them and threw them down at Lakṣmī’s feet. They punished them like thieves, as if to exact all kinds of riches from them.

@134
as if senseless  them  they do  beating
of many kinds  abuse  they give  with mocking words
= They beat them until they were nearly senseless and heaped all kinds of abuse and mockery on them.

@135
with Lakṣmī  of the maidservants  the boldness  seeing
laugh  Mahāprabhu’s  associates  to their mouths  hands  putting
= Seeing the boldness of Lakṣmī’s maidservants, Mahāprabhu’s associates laughed, covering their mouths with their hands.

@136
Dāmodara  says  such  of māna  a kind
in the three worlds  where  do I see  I have heard  not  ever
= Svarūpa Dāmodara said, “Where in the three worlds does one see *māna*, loving pique, of this kind? I have never heard of such a thing.

@137
a woman in māna  dejected  gives up  her ornaments
on the ground  sitting  with her nails  draws lines  with a darkened face
= A woman in *māna* dejectedly lays aside her ornaments; she sits on the ground, her face clouded, and draws lines on it with her nails.

@138
before  of Satyabhāmā  I hear  of this kind  māna
in Vraja  of the gopīs  the māna  of rasa  a treasure-house
= I have heard before of such *māna* in Satyabhāmā; but in Vraja the *māna* of the gopīs is a treasure-house of *rasa*.

@139
she  her own wealth  all  manifest  making
against her beloved  goes  an army  arraying
= This goddess, however, displays all her wealth and marches against her beloved with an army in battle array!”

@140
the Lord  says  tell  of Vraja  of the māna  the kinds
Svarūpa  says  the river of the gopīs’ māna  with a hundred streams
= The Lord said, “Tell Me about the kinds of *māna* in Vraja.” Svarūpa replied, “The river of the gopīs’ *māna* flows in a hundred streams.

@141
of the heroines  the natures  in the workings of love  many  differences
by those  differences  of many kinds  of māna  the appearance
= The natures of the heroines differ greatly, and so do the ways their love behaves; according to these differences, *māna* appears in many forms.

@142
completely  of the gopīs  the māna  not  can be  told
by one or two  varieties  I make  an indication
= The gopīs’ *māna* cannot be described in full; I shall only point the way with one or two of its varieties.

@143
in māna  some  are  dhīrā  some  indeed  adhīrā
in these  three divisions  some  are  dhīrādhīrā
= In *māna* some are *dhīrā*, composed; some are *adhīrā*, impetuous; and some, making the third division, are *dhīrādhīrā*, both composed and impetuous.

@144
the dhīrā  her lover  from afar  seeing  rises to greet him
near  when he comes  she makes  of a seat  an offering
= Seeing her lover from afar, the composed heroine rises to greet him, and when he comes near she offers him a seat.

@145
in her heart  anger  with her lips  speaks  sweet  words
the beloved  when he embraces  him  she does  embracing
= Anger lies in her heart, but her lips speak sweet words; when her beloved embraces her, she embraces him in return.

@146
straightforward  behavior  she does  of māna  nourishing
or  with ironic words  she does  rebuffing the beloved
= Her behavior is straightforward, yet she nurses her *māna*; or else she rebuffs her beloved with ironic words.

@147
the adhīrā  with harsh words  does  scolding
with the lotus on her ear  she strikes  she does  with a garland  binding
= The impetuous heroine scolds her lover with harsh words; she strikes him with the lotus from her ear or binds him with her garland.

@148
the dhīrādhīrā  with crooked words  makes  mockery
sometimes  praise  sometimes  reproach  sometimes  or  indifference
= The heroine who is both composed and impetuous mocks him with crooked words: at times she praises him, at times she reproaches him, and at times she is indifferent.

@149
mugdhā  madhyā  pragalbhā  three  of heroines  kinds
the mugdhā  does not  know  of māna  the artful varieties
= There are three kinds of heroine: *mugdhā* (artless), *madhyā* (intermediate) and *pragalbhā* (bold). The artless heroine does not know the artful ways of *māna*.

@150
her face  covering  she does  only  weeping
of her lover  the tender words  hearing  becomes  pacified
= She only covers her face and weeps, and when she hears her lover’s tender words, she is pacified.

@151
the madhyā  the pragalbhā  take on  dhīrā and the other  divisions
of them  among  of all  by nature  three kinds
= The intermediate and the bold heroines take on the divisions of *dhīrā* and the rest, and among all of them there are three kinds by nature.

@152
some  prakharā  some  mṛdu  some  are  samā
by their own natures  Kṛṣṇa’s  increase  the bounds of love
= Some are *prakharā*, sharp; some are *mṛdu*, gentle; and some are *samā*, balanced. Each, by her own nature, expands the bounds of Kṛṣṇa’s love.

@153
sharpness  gentleness  a balanced nature  faultless
by each  such  nature  Kṛṣṇa  they cause  delight
= Sharpness, gentleness and a balanced nature are all faultless, and by each of these natures they delight Kṛṣṇa.”

@154
this  talk  hearing  the Lord’s  bliss  boundless
tell  tell  Dāmodara  He says  time  after time
= Hearing this, the Lord felt boundless bliss. “Go on, go on, Dāmodara!” He said again and again.

@155
Dāmodara  says  Kṛṣṇa  the crown of relishers
of rasa  the taster  whose body is made of rasa
= Dāmodara said, “Kṛṣṇa is the crown of all who relish *rasa*. He tastes *rasa*, and His very body is made of *rasa*.

@156
whose form is made of love  Kṛṣṇa  subject to His devotees’ love
in pure love  in the qualities of rasa  the gopīs  are expert
= Kṛṣṇa’s form is made of love, and He is subject to the love of His devotees. In pure love and in the qualities of *rasa* the gopīs are supremely expert.

@157
of the gopīs  in the love  there is no  fault of rasābhāsa
therefore  Kṛṣṇa’s  it causes  supreme  satisfaction
= In the gopīs’ love there is no fault of *rasābhāsa*, a mere semblance of *rasa*; therefore it gives Kṛṣṇa supreme satisfaction.”

@158
thus  by the moon’s rays  resplendent  nights
He  whose desires are true  surrounded by devoted maidens
enjoyed  within Himself  keeping His amorous feelings
all  of autumn  of poetic narrations  the abodes of rasa
= “Thus Kṛṣṇa, whose desires are ever true, surrounded by the maidens devoted to Him and keeping His amorous feelings within Himself, enjoyed all those nights resplendent with moonlight — the autumn nights that are the abode of the *rasa* told in poetry.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.33.25)

@159
vāmā  one  group of gopīs  dakṣiṇā  another  group
in various moods  they make  Kṛṣṇa  rasa  taste
= “One group of gopīs is *vāmā*, contrary, and another is *dakṣiṇā*, compliant. Through their various moods they let Kṛṣṇa taste *rasa*.

@160
the gopīs  among  the foremost  Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī
of the pure, radiant rasa  of love  of jewels  a mine
= Among the gopīs the foremost is Śrī Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī, a mine of the jewels of love in the pure and radiant *rasa* (*ujjvala-rasa*).

@161
in age  madhyamā  She  by nature  samā
in deep  love  She  always  vāmā
= In age She is *madhyā*, in the bloom of youth; by nature She is *samā*, balanced; yet in the depth of Her love She is always *vāmā*, contrary.

@162
from a contrary nature  māna  rises  constantly
of that  in the midst  rises  Kṛṣṇa’s  ocean of bliss
= From Her contrary nature *māna* constantly arises, and in the midst of it the ocean of Kṛṣṇa’s bliss rises.”

@163
of a snake  like  the movement  of love
by nature  winding  is
therefore  with cause  without cause  and
of the young pair  pique  arises
= “Like the movement of a snake, the course of love is winding by nature. Therefore pique (*māna*) arises between the young lovers, with cause and without cause.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi, Śṛṅgāra-bheda-kathana, 102)[^m14-163]

@164
this  hearing  swells  the Lord’s  of bliss  the ocean
tell  tell  says  the Lord  speaks  Dāmodara
= Hearing this, the ocean of the Lord’s bliss swelled. “Go on, go on,” the Lord said, and Dāmodara continued:

@165
adhirūḍha  mahābhāva  Rādhikā’s  love
utterly pure  spotless  like  fire-refined  gold
= “Rādhikā’s love is *adhirūḍha mahābhāva*, the highest summit of *mahābhāva*. It is utterly pure and spotless, like gold refined in fire.

@166
of Kṛṣṇa  the sight  if  She obtains  suddenly
with ornaments of many bhāvas  She becomes  adorned
= Whenever She suddenly catches sight of Kṛṣṇa, She becomes adorned with the ornaments of many *bhāvas*, ecstatic feelings.

@167
eight  sāttvika  joy and the rest  vyabhicārī  whose
natural  love  twenty  bhāva ornaments
= Hers are the eight *sāttvika* transformations, the *vyabhicārī-bhāvas* beginning with joy, Her natural love and the twenty ornaments of *bhāva*:

@168
kila-kiñcita  kuṭṭamita  vilāsa  lalita
vivvoka  moṭṭāyita  and  maugdhya  cakita
= *kila-kiñcita*, *kuṭṭamita*, *vilāsa*, *lalita*, *vivvoka*, *moṭṭāyita*, *maugdhya*, *cakita* and others.

@169
with so many  bhāva ornaments  adorned  Śrī  Rādhā’s  form
on seeing  surge  the waves of Kṛṣṇa’s ocean of happiness
= When Kṛṣṇa sees Śrī Rādhā’s form adorned with so many ornaments of *bhāva*, the waves of the ocean of His happiness surge high.

@170
of kila-kiñcita and the other bhāvas  hear  the description
by which  bhāva ornaments  Rādhā  steals  Kṛṣṇa’s mind
= Now hear a description of *kila-kiñcita* and the other *bhāvas*, the ornaments of feeling by which Rādhā steals Kṛṣṇa’s mind.

@171
Rādhā  seeing  Kṛṣṇa  if  to touch  makes up His mind
on the path to the toll crossing  when  He bars  Her going
= When Kṛṣṇa sees Rādhā and wants to touch Her, when He bars Her way on the path to the toll crossing (*dāna-ghāṭi*),

@172
when  coming  He forbids  flowers  to pick
before Her friends  He wants  if  on Her body  His hand  to place
= when He comes and forbids Her to pick flowers, or when He tries to touch Her in front of Her friends —

@173
in all these  situations  of kila-kiñcita  the rise
first  joy  the sañcārī  the root cause
= in all these situations *kila-kiñcita* arises. Its root cause is the *sañcārī-bhāva* of joy (*harṣa*), which comes first.

@174
of pride, longing, weeping
smiling, envy, fear and anger
the mingling  from joy
is called  kila-kiñcita
= “The mingling of pride, longing, weeping, smiling, envy, fear and anger, born of joy, is called *kila-kiñcita*.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi, Vibhāva-kathana, 71)

@175
other  seven  bhāvas  coming  naturally  combine
by the union of eight bhāvas  mahābhāva  arises
= The other seven *bhāvas* come and naturally combine with joy, and from the union of these eight *bhāvas* arises *mahābhāva*.

@176
pride  longing  fear  dry weeping
anger  envy  there are  and  a faint smile
= The seven are pride, longing, fear, tearless weeping, anger, envy and a faint smile.

@177
of various flavors  eight bhāvas  in one  the meeting
whose  in the tasting  satisfied  becomes  Kṛṣṇa’s mind
= These eight *bhāvas*, each of a different flavor, meet in one, and in tasting that blend Kṛṣṇa’s mind is fully satisfied.

@178
yogurt  sugar  ghee  honey  black pepper  camphor
by mixing with cardamom  as  rasālā  is sweet
= It is like *rasālā*, which is so sweet because yogurt, sugar, ghee, honey, black pepper, camphor and cardamom are blended in it.

@179
with these bhāvas  filled  seeing  Rādhā’s face and eyes
than union  happiness  He obtains  ten million times
= Seeing Rādhā’s face and eyes filled with these *bhāvas*, Kṛṣṇa feels ten million times more happiness than in union with Her.

@180
within  with a smile  shining  with drops of tears  strewn  its lashes
slightly  reddened at the corners  overflowing with playfulness  in front  narrowing
of Her who was stopped  on the path  by Mādhava  with sweetly curving pupils raised
Rādhā’s  a bouquet of kila-kiñcita  glance  good fortune  on you  may it bestow
= “When Mādhava stopped Rādhā on the path, Her glance shone with a smile held within; its lashes were strewn with tears and its corners slightly reddened; brimming with playful delight, it narrowed, its pupils sweetly curving upward. May that glance of Rādhā’s, a bouquet of *kila-kiñcita*, bestow good fortune on you.” (Dāna-keli-kaumudī 1)

@181
with eyes restless, reddened at the corners and clouded with tears  delighted by rasa
with lips trembling in playful joy  with arched brows  with a dawning smile
of Rādhā  adorned with kila-kiñcita  He  seeing  the face  than from union
bliss  that  obtained  ten million times greater  which  was  not  within the range of words
= “Seeing Rādhā’s face adorned with *kila-kiñcita* — Her restless eyes clouded with tears and reddened at the corners, Her face alight with *rasa*, Her lips trembling with playful delight, Her brows arched and a smile dawning — Kṛṣṇa attained a bliss ten million times greater than that of union with Her, a bliss beyond the reach of words.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta 9.18)

@182
this  hearing  the Lord  became  joyful  in mind
absorbed in happiness  becoming  Svarūpa  He did  embracing
= Hearing this, the Lord rejoiced at heart, and, absorbed in happiness, He embraced Svarūpa.

@183
of vilāsa and the other bhāva ornaments  tell  indeed  the characteristics
by which  bhāva  Rādhā  steals  Govinda’s  mind
= “Now tell Me the characteristics of *vilāsa* and the other ornaments of *bhāva* by which Rādhā steals Govinda’s mind.”

@184
then  indeed  Svarūpa Gosāñi  to speak  began
hearing  the Lord’s  devotees  great happiness  obtained
= Then Svarūpa Gosāñi began to speak, and the Lord’s devotees were overjoyed to hear him.

Rādhā  is sitting  or  into Vṛndāvana  is going
there  suddenly  of Kṛṣṇa  the sight  She obtains
= “Rādhā may be sitting, or going into the forest of Vṛndāvana, when suddenly She catches sight of Kṛṣṇa.
