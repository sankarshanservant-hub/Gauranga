# CC Madhya 19, stage A, part 2 (verses 129–257); read by build_en.py together with Madhya19.txt

@129
a water pot  only  in the hand  a patched quilt  torn  outer cloth
Kṛṣṇa  talks  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  of dancing  jubilation
= “They carry nothing in their hands but a water pot; they have a patched quilt and a torn outer cloth. Talks of Kṛṣṇa, the name of Kṛṣṇa and jubilant dancing are their life.

@130
eight praharas  Kṛṣṇa  worship  four  daṇḍas  in sleep
of the name  saṅkīrtana  in love  even that  is not  on some  days
= “Throughout the eight *praharas* of day and night they worship Kṛṣṇa, sleeping only four *daṇḍas* — and on some days, in their love for the congregational chanting of the name, not even that.

@131
sometimes  on bhakti-rasa  scriptures  they do  writing
about Caitanya  talks  they hear  they do  on Caitanya  meditation
= “Sometimes they write scriptures on the *rasa* of devotion; they hear talks about Caitanya and meditate on Caitanya.”

@132
these words  hearing  of the great souls  great happiness  arises
Caitanya’s  mercy  on whom  in him  what  wonder
= Hearing this, the great souls felt great happiness. What wonder is there in one on whom Caitanya’s mercy rests?

@133
Caitanya’s  mercy  Rūpa  has written  himself
Rasāmṛta-sindhu  of the book  in the auspicious invocation
= Rūpa himself has written of Caitanya’s mercy in the auspicious invocation (*maṅgalācaraṇa*) of his book *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*:

@134
in the heart  whose  by the prompting  set to work  I  wretched  by nature  though
of that  Hari  the lotus feet  I worship  of Caitanya-deva
= “Though I am wretched, I have been set to this work by His prompting within my heart. I worship the lotus feet of that Hari, Śrī Caitanya-deva.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.1.2)

@135
in this way  ten days  at Prayāga  He stayed
to Śrī Rūpa  instruction  He gave  power  infusing
= In this way the Lord stayed at Prayāga for ten days and taught Śrī Rūpa, infusing him with His power.

@136
the Lord  says  hear  Rūpa  of bhakti-rasa  the characteristics
in the form of sūtras  I speak  in full  not  can be  described
= The Lord said: “Listen, Rūpa, to the characteristics of *bhakti-rasa*. I shall speak of it briefly, in the form of *sūtras*; it cannot be described in full.

@137
without near or far shore  deep  of bhakti-rasa  the ocean
you  to give a taste  of it  I tell  one  drop
= “The ocean of *bhakti-rasa* is deep and has no shore on either side. To give you a taste of it, I shall tell you of a single drop.

@138
thus  the universe  filling  countless  jīvas
of eighty-four lakhs  in species  they do  wandering
= “Countless *jīvas* fill the universe, wandering through the 8,400,000 species of life.

@139
of the tip of a hair  a hundredth  part  again  into a hundred parts  making
to that  equal  subtle  of the jīva  the own nature  I consider
= “Divide the tip of a hair into a hundred parts, and one of these again into a hundred: as subtle as that, I consider, is the *jīva*’s own nature (*svarūpa*).

@140
of the tip of a hair  of a hundredth  part
to a hundredth part  similar  in nature
the jīva  of subtle nature  this
beyond counting  indeed  a particle of spirit
= “This *jīva*, subtle by nature, is like a hundredth part of a hundredth part of the tip of a hair. These particles of spirit are indeed beyond counting.”

@141
of the tip of a hair  of a hundredth  part
a hundredfold  divided  and
a part  the jīva  he  is to be known
thus  and says  the supreme  śruti
= “The *jīva* should be known as a part of the tip of a hair divided into a hundred, and that hundredth divided again a hundredfold — so says the supreme *śruti*.” (Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad, 5.9)

@142
of the subtle  also  I  the jīva
= “Of subtle things, I am the *jīva*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.16.11)

@143
countless  eternal  the embodied  if  all-pervading
then  not  subjection  thus  the law  O Eternal  not  otherwise
was born  and  from which  that  not leaving  the controller  is
as equal  of those who regard  which  is not accepted  because of the fault of the view
= (The personified Vedas pray:) “O Eternal One! If the countless, eternal embodied beings were all-pervading, there would be no law that they are subject to You; yet it cannot be otherwise. That from which they are born, never leaving them, is their controller. The view of those who regard them as equal to You is not accepted, because the view itself is faulty.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.30)

@144
of them  among  stationary  moving  two  divisions
among the moving  birds  water-  and land-dwellers  the subdivision
= “Among them there are two divisions — the stationary and the moving; and the moving are divided into birds, water-dwellers and land-dwellers.

@145
of them  among  the human species  very  few
of them  among  mlecchas  Pulindas  Buddhists  Śabaras
= “Among these, human beings are very few; and among humans there are *mlecchas*, Pulindas, Buddhists and Śabaras.

@146
of the faithful to the Vedas  among  half  the Vedas  with the mouth  accept
forbidden by the Vedas  sins  they commit  dharma  not  regard
= “Of those faithful to the Vedas, half accept the Vedas only with their lips: they commit sins the Vedas forbid and have no regard for *dharma*.

@147
of those who practice dharma  among  many  devoted to karma
of millions  devoted to karma  among  one  jñānī  the best
= “Among those who practice *dharma*, many are devoted to *karma*; among millions devoted to *karma*, one *jñānī* is best.

@148
of millions  jñānīs  among  is  one person  liberated
of millions  liberated  among  rare  one  Kṛṣṇa’s devotee
= “Among millions of *jñānīs*, one is liberated (*mukta*); and among millions of the liberated, even one devotee of Kṛṣṇa is rare.

@149
Kṛṣṇa’s devotee  free from desire  therefore  peaceful
enjoyment  liberation  perfection  desiring  all  restless
= “A devotee of Kṛṣṇa is free from desire, and therefore peaceful (*śānta*). Those who desire enjoyment, liberation or mystic perfections are all restless.

@150
of the liberated  even  of the perfected
Nārāyaṇa  devoted to
very rare  of peaceful mind
among millions  even  O great sage
= “O great sage, even among millions of the liberated and the perfected, one devoted to Nārāyaṇa, whose mind is at peace, is very rare.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 6.14.5)
