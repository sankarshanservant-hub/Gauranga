@title Śrī Caitanya’s Dealings with Jagadānanda Paṇḍita

@1
hear  hear  constantly
sing  sing  with joy
meditate  meditate  O devotees
the nectar of Caitanya’s deeds
= O devotees, hear, hear constantly the nectar of Śrī Caitanya’s deeds; sing, sing it with joy; meditate, meditate on it.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to the merciful one
glory  glory  to Nityānanda  to the ocean of mercy  glory
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to the merciful Lord! Glory, glory to Nityānanda! Glory to the ocean of mercy!

@3
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the ocean of compassion
glory  of Gaura  to the devotees  whose hearts are full of mercy
= Glory to Advaitacandra, the ocean of compassion! Glory to the devotees of Gaura, whose hearts are full of mercy!

@4
after this  of Mahāprabhu  dejected  the heart
of separation from Kṛṣṇa  the state  manifests  constantly
= After this Mahāprabhu’s heart was dejected, and His state of separation from Kṛṣṇa manifested in Him constantly.

@5
alas  alas  Kṛṣṇa  Lord of My life  son of the king of Vraja
where  shall I go  where  shall I find (You)  O You whose face bears the flute
= “Alas, alas, Kṛṣṇa, Lord of My life, son of the king of Vraja! Where shall I go? Where shall I find You, O You whose lips hold the flute?”

@6
night and day  this  state  peace  there is not  in the mind
with difficulty  the night  He passes  with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda
= Night and day He was in this state, and there was no peace in His mind. With difficulty He passed the nights in the company of Svarūpa and Rāmānanda.

@7
meanwhile  in the land of Gauḍa  of the Lord  all  the devotees
the Lord  to see  all  set out
= Meanwhile in the land of Gauḍa all the Lord’s devotees set out to see Him.

@8
Śivānanda Sena  and  Ācārya Gosāñi
in Navadvīpa  all  the devotees  gathered  in one  place
= Śivānanda Sena, Advaita Ācārya and all the devotees gathered in one place, in Navadvīpa.

@9
the residents of Kulīnagrāma  and  all  the residents of Khaṇḍa
together  met  all  to Navadvīpa  coming
= The residents of Kulīnagrāma and all the residents of Khaṇḍa came to Navadvīpa, and all met there together.

@10
to Nityānanda Prabhu  although  permission  there was not
still  to see  sets out  Caitanya Gosāñi
= Although Nityānanda Prabhu did not have the Lord’s permission, He still set out to see Caitanya Gosāñi.

@11
Śrīvāsa and the others  the four  brothers  along with  Mālinī
of Ācāryaratna  with  his  wife
= Śrīvāsa and his brothers, four in all, went with Mālinī, and Ācāryaratna’s wife went with him.

@12
Śivānanda’s wife  goes  three sons  taking
Rāghava Paṇḍita  goes  his bags  having packed
= Śivānanda’s wife went, taking her three sons, and Rāghava Paṇḍita went with his bags (*jhāli*) packed.

@13
Datta  Gupta  Vidyānidhi  and  all  the others
two or three  hundred  devotees  set out
= Vāsudeva Datta, Murāri Gupta, Vidyānidhi and all the others — two or three hundred devotees set out.

@14
Śacīmātā  having seen  all  her  permission  taking
joyfully  went  chanting of Kṛṣṇa  doing
= They all went to see Śacīmātā and, taking her permission, set out joyfully, chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa.

@15
Śivānanda Sena  does  the settling at the toll posts
all  care  taking  happily  leading  goes
= Śivānanda Sena settled matters at the toll posts (*ghāṭī*); taking care of everyone, he led them happily along.

@16
of all  all  the affairs  he does  gives  lodging places
Śivānanda  knows  of the Oḍiyā roads  the details
= He attended to all their affairs and found them places to stay, for Śivānanda knew the roads through Orissa well.

@17
one  day  all  the people  at the toll post  they detained
all  having got released  Śivānanda  alone  remained
= One day all the people were detained at a toll post. To get them all released, Śivānanda stayed behind alone.

@18
all  having gone  stayed  within the village  under a tree
Śivānanda  without  a lodging place  not  is found
= The others went on and waited under a tree in a village, for without Śivānanda no lodging could be found.

@19
Nityānanda Prabhu  from hunger  distressed  becoming
Śivānanda  abuse  heaps on  lodging  not  having found
= Nityānanda Prabhu, distressed by hunger and finding no lodging, began to abuse Śivānanda:[^19-1]

@20
three  sons  may they die  of Śivā  now (still)  not  he has come
from hunger  dying  I am  for Me  lodging  not  he arranged
= “May Śivānanda’s three sons die! He has still not come. I am dying of hunger, and he has not arranged any lodging for Me!”

@21
having heard  Śivānanda’s  wife  to weep  began
at that time  Śivānanda  the toll post  from  came
= Hearing this, Śivānanda’s wife began to weep. Just then Śivānanda came back from the toll post.

@22
Śivānanda’s  wife  to him  says  weeping
on the sons  a curse  has laid  Gosāñi  lodging  not  having found
= Weeping, Śivānanda’s wife said to him, “Finding no lodging, Gosāñi has laid a curse on our sons.”

@23
he  says  crazy woman  why  are you dying  weeping
may they die  my  three  sons  His  misfortunes  taking (upon themselves)
= He said, “Crazy woman, why are you killing yourself with weeping? Let my three sons die, taking His misfortunes upon themselves!”[^23-1]

@24
thus  having said  to the Lord’s side  went  Śivānanda
rising  him  a kick  gave  Prabhu Nityānanda
= Saying this, Śivānanda went to the Lord. Nityānanda Prabhu rose and kicked him.

@25
delighted  became  Śivāi  the blow of the foot  having received
quickly  a lodging house  he arranged  to a cowherd’s house  having gone
= Receiving the blow of His foot, Śivānanda was delighted. He quickly went to the house of a cowherd (*goyālā*) and arranged lodging there.

@26
at the feet  holding  the Lord  to the lodging  taking  went
lodging  having given  delighted  becoming  to speak  began
= Holding the Lord’s feet, he took Him to the lodging. Having given Him a place to stay, he spoke in delight:

@27
today  me  as a servant  making  acceptance  You have made
as  the offense  of the servant  fitting  result  You have given
= “Today You have accepted me as Your servant. You have given the fruit that the servant’s offense deserved.

@28
punishment  in the guise of  mercy  You show  this  Your  compassion
in the three worlds  Your  character  understands  which  person
= In the guise of punishment You show mercy — such is Your compassion. Who in the three worlds can understand Your character?

@29
for Brahmā  rare  Your  the dust of the holy feet
such  the touch of the feet  has obtained  my  lowly  body
= The dust of Your holy feet is rare even for Brahmā, yet my lowly body has received the touch of such feet.

@30
today  my  successful  became  birth  family  deeds
today  I have obtained  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  wealth  pleasure  dharma
= Today my birth, my family and my deeds have become successful. Today I have obtained devotion to Kṛṣṇa, as well as wealth, pleasure and *dharma*.”

@31
having heard  of Nityānanda Prabhu  delighted  the mind
rising  Śivānanda  He made  an embrace of love
= Hearing this, Nityānanda Prabhu was delighted at heart. He rose and embraced Śivānanda with love.

@32
delighted  Śivānanda  makes  the arrangements
to the Ācārya and the other Vaiṣṇavas  gave  lodging places
= Delighted, Śivānanda made the arrangements and gave lodgings to Advaita Ācārya and the other Vaiṣṇavas.

@33
of Nityānanda Prabhu  all  the conduct  contrary
angry  becoming  a kick  giving  He does  his  good
= All of Nityānanda Prabhu’s ways are paradoxical: when He becomes angry and kicks someone, He does that person good.[^33-1]

@34
Śivānanda’s  nephew  Śrīkānta Sena  by name
of his maternal uncle  out of sight  says  taking offense
= Śivānanda had a nephew named Śrīkānta Sena. Hurt and indignant, he spoke when his uncle was out of hearing:

@35
of Caitanya  an associate  my  maternal uncle’s  fame
lordliness  shows  Gosāñi  him  He kicks
= “My uncle is known as an associate of Caitanya, yet Gosāñi lords it over him and kicks him.”

@36
thus  having said  Śrīkānta  the boy  ahead  going  goes
the company  leaving  ahead  went  to Mahāprabhu’s  place
= Saying this, the boy Śrīkānta went on ahead. Leaving the party, he went in advance to where Mahāprabhu was.

@37
with a shirt on the body  he makes  prostrate  obeisance
Govinda  says  Śrīkānta  first  the shirt  take off
= Still wearing his shirt (*peṭāṅgi*), he offered prostrate obeisances. Govinda said, “Śrīkānta, first take off your shirt.”

@38
the Lord  says  Śrīkānta  has come  having got  sorrow of mind
anything  not  say  let him do  whatever  his  happiness
= The Lord said, “Śrīkānta has come here with a sorrowful heart. Say nothing to him; let him do as he pleases.”

@39
of the Vaiṣṇavas  the news  Gosāñi  asked
one by one  of all  the names  Śrīkānta  made known
= The Lord asked for news of the Vaiṣṇavas, and Śrīkānta told Him about each of them by name.

@40
sorrow  having got  he has come  this  the Lord’s  statement  hearing
he knew  omniscient  the Lord  thus  inferring
= Hearing the Lord say, “He has come here sorrowful,” Śrīkānta concluded that the Lord is omniscient.

@41
Śivānanda  He kicked  this  not  he told
here  all  the Vaiṣṇavas  coming  met (Him)
= He did not tell the Lord that Nityānanda had kicked Śivānanda. Meanwhile all the Vaiṣṇavas arrived and met the Lord.

@42
as before  the Lord  made  with all  the meeting
the women  from afar  made  of the Lord  the sight (darśana)
= As before, the Lord met with everyone, and the women had His *darśana* from a distance.

@43
lodging houses  as before  to all  He had given
to the eating of mahā-prasāda  all  He called
= As before, He had lodgings given to everyone, and He called them all to take *mahā-prasāda*.

@44
Śivānanda  the three sons  to Gosāñi  introduced
on account of the relation with Śivānanda  to all  great mercy  He showed
= Śivānanda introduced his three sons to the Lord, and for Śivānanda’s sake the Lord showed great mercy to them all.

@45
the youngest son  seeing  the Lord  the name  asked
Paramānanda dāsa  the name  Sena  made known
= Seeing the youngest son, the Lord asked his name, and Śivānanda Sena told Him it was Paramānanda dāsa.

@46-47
before  when  Śivānanda  to the Lord’s place  came
then  Mahāprabhu  to him  to speak  began
this time  your  that  which will be  son
Purīdāsa  as  the name  give  of him
= Earlier, when Śivānanda had come to the Lord, Mahāprabhu had said to him, “The son who will be born to you this time — name him Purīdāsa.”

@48
then  of the mother  in the womb  is  that  indeed  son
Śivānanda  home  went  birth  took place  his
= That son was then in his mother’s womb. Śivānanda went home, and the child was born.

@49
by the Lord’s order  they gave  the name  Paramānanda dāsa
Purīdāsa  calling (him)  the Lord  makes  jest
= By the Lord’s order they named him Paramānanda dāsa, and the Lord jokingly called him Purīdāsa.

@50
Śivānanda  when  that  child  introduced
Mahāprabhu  His big toe  his  in the mouth  placed
= When Śivānanda presented that child, Mahāprabhu placed His big toe in the boy’s mouth.

@51
of Śivānanda  the ocean of good fortune  who  will reach  the shore
whose  all  family  the Lord  calls  His own
= Who can reach the shore of the ocean of Śivānanda’s good fortune? The Lord calls his whole family His own.

@52
then  all  the devotees  taking  He took  the meal
to Govinda  an order  He gave  having rinsed (His mouth)
= Then the Lord took His meal with all the devotees. After rinsing His mouth and hands, He gave Govinda an order:

@53
Śivānanda’s  wife  sons  as long as  here
My  plate of remnants  they  should  receive
= “As long as Śivānanda’s wife and sons are here, they should receive the plate of My remnants.”

@54
a resident of Nadīyā  a confectioner  his  name  Parameśvara
sweets  he sells  of the Lord’s  house  near  his  home
= There was a confectioner (*modaka*), a resident of Nadīyā, named Parameśvara. He sold sweets, and his home was near the Lord’s house.

@55
in childhood  the Lord  his  to the house  again and again  goes
milk  sugar  sweets  he gives  the Lord  that  eats
= In His childhood the Lord would go to his house again and again, and Parameśvara would give Him milk, sugar and sweets, which He ate.

@56
for the Lord  affection  his  from childhood
that  year  he  came  the Lord  to see
= He had had affection for the Lord since the Lord’s childhood. That year he came to see the Lord.

@57
Parameśvarā  I  saying  prostrated himself  he did
him  seeing  the Lord  with affection  him  asked
= Saying, “I am Parameśvarā,” he offered prostrate obeisances. Seeing him, the Lord spoke to him with affection:[^57-1]

@58
Parameśvara  well  be  good  it is  you have come
Mukunda’s  mother  has come  to the Lord  he said
= “Parameśvara, may you be well! It is good that you have come.” Then he told the Lord, “Mukunda’s mother has come.”

@59
of Mukunda’s mother  the name  hearing  the Lord  embarrassed  became
yet  his  out of affection  anything  not  He said
= Hearing the name of Mukunda’s mother, the Lord felt embarrassed, yet out of affection for him He said nothing.[^59-1]

@60
the boldness of familiarity  pure courtesy  not  knows
within  pleased  became  the Lord  his  that  quality
= The boldness of loving familiarity (*praśraya-prāgalbhya*) knows nothing of pure courtesy (*śuddha-vaidagdhī*). Within His heart the Lord was pleased with that quality of his.[^60-1]

@61
as before  all  taking  the cleansing of Guṇḍicā
before the chariot  as before  He danced
= As before, the Lord cleansed the Guṇḍicā temple with everyone, and as before He danced in front of the chariot.

@62
of the Cāturmāsya  all  the festivals  they had  the sight
Mālinī  and others  the Lord  invited
= During the four months of *Cāturmāsya* they saw all the festivals, and Mālinī and the other ladies invited the Lord for meals.

@63
to the Lord  dear  various  foodstuffs  they had brought  the homeland  from
those  vegetable dishes  making  offering  give  with home-cooked rice
= They had brought from home various foodstuffs dear to the Lord. Making vegetable dishes with them, they offered Him meals of home-cooked rice.

@64
by day  various  pastimes  He does  taking  the devotees
at night  in separation from Kṛṣṇa  the Lord  does  weeping
= By day the Lord enjoyed various pastimes with the devotees; at night He wept in separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@65
in this way  in various pastimes  the Cāturmāsya  passed
to the land of Gauḍa  to go  then  to the devotees  an order  He gave
= In this way the four months of *Cāturmāsya* passed in various pastimes. Then the Lord ordered the devotees to return to the land of Gauḍa.

@66
all  the devotees  make  of Mahāprabhu  invitation
to all the devotees  speaks  the Lord  sweet  words
= All the devotees invited Mahāprabhu for meals, and the Lord spoke sweet words to all of them:

@67
every year  you come  all  Me  to see
in coming  in going  hardship  you suffer  in many ways
= “Every year you all come to see Me, and in coming and going you suffer hardships of many kinds.

@68
of you all  the hardship  knowing  I want  to forbid
of you all  in the happiness of company  greed  grows  in the heart
= Knowing your hardships, I want to forbid you to come. But in My heart the greed for the happiness of your company grows.

@69
to Nityānanda  an order  I gave  in Gauḍa  to remain
the order  transgressing  He came  what  can I  say
= I ordered Nityānanda to remain in Gauḍa, yet He has broken My order and come. What can I say?

@70
has come  Ācārya Gosāñi  to Me  mercy  showing
by the debt of love  bound  I  to repay  not  can
= Ācārya Gosāñi has come here, showing Me mercy. I am bound by a debt of love that I can never repay.

@71
Me  for the sake of  wife  sons  home and so on  leaving
many  difficult  roads  crossing  they come  running
= For My sake they leave wife, sons and home, and come running over many difficult roads.

@72
I  this  in Nīlācala  stay  indeed  sitting
labor  there is not  My  of all  for the sake of
= And I simply stay here, sitting in Nīlācala; I take no trouble for any of you.

@73
a sannyāsī  person  I  there is not  any  wealth
what  giving  your  debt  shall I  repay
= I am a *sannyāsī*; I have no wealth at all. With what shall I repay My debt to you?

@74
the body  alone  wealth  to you  I have  offered
there  I am sold  where  to sell  your  mind (wish)
= My body is My only wealth, and I have offered it to you. Wherever you wish to sell Me, there I am sold.”

@75
by the Lord’s  words  of all  pleased  became  the minds
streaming  with eyes  all  do  weeping
= All were pleased at heart by the Lord’s words, and all wept with tears streaming from their eyes.

@76
the Lord  of all  the necks  holding  does  weeping
weeping  weeping  all  He embraced
= Holding each of them by the neck, the Lord wept, and weeping and weeping He embraced them all.

@77
all  remained  anyone  to go  could not
more  days  five  seven  in this way  passed
= Everyone stayed on; no one could leave. Five or seven more days passed in this way.

@notes
[^19-1]: The *Anubhāṣya* heads this verse: Nityānanda, in His transcendental form as a cowherd boy of Vraja, has not satisfied His hunger and shows Śivānanda a feigned semblance of anger.
[^23-1]: *Tāṅra bālāi laña*, “taking His misfortunes upon themselves”: an expression of loving devotion, wishing that every ill that threatens the beloved may fall upon oneself.
[^33-1]: The *Anubhāṣya* heads this verse: the semblance of anger in Nityānanda, the *guru*, is a hidden, supreme mercy and the sign of eternal good.
[^57-1]: *Parameśvarā* (*parameśvaryā*): the homely form in which the simple confectioner speaks his own name.
[^59-1]: The *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord, the *guru* of the world and teacher of people, who enacted the pastime of *sannyāsa*, felt embarrassed on hearing a woman’s name, even though she was elderly and like a mother to Him.
[^60-1]: To say to a *sannyāsī*, “Mukunda’s mother has come,” is the boldness born of long familiarity (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*). The *Anubhāṣya* records the variant reading *praśraya-pāgala*, “simple in his affection,” and explains: *praśraya* — affection, affectionate respect, humility, trust, loving liberty; *prāgalbhya* — boldness, forwardness; *vaidagdhī* — refinement, polish, skill, elegance of manners. The Lord, who accepts the feeling of the heart, was pleased with his guilelessness.
