@title The Lord’s Paugaṇḍa Pastimes

@1
the evil-minded  good-mindedness  indeed
attains  whose  at the lotus feet
of a flower  by the offering  merely
Him  Lord Caitanya  I worship
= “I worship Lord Caitanya: by merely offering a flower (*sumanas*) at His lotus feet, even an evil-minded person becomes good-minded.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 7.1)

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s devotees  all
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of the paugaṇḍa pastimes  the sūtras  I make  enumeration
in the paugaṇḍa age  the Lord’s  chief  study
= I shall now list the *sūtras* of the *paugaṇḍa* pastimes. In His *paugaṇḍa* years the Lord’s chief occupation was study.

@4
the paugaṇḍa pastimes  of Caitanya-
Kṛṣṇa  very extensive
the beginning of learning  as the start  hand-
taking  as the end  enchanting
= The *paugaṇḍa* pastimes of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa are very extensive and enchanting: they begin with the start of His learning and end with His taking a bride’s hand.

@5
of Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita  at the place  studies  grammar
on hearing  only  by heart  made  sūtras and glosses
= He studied grammar with Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita and learned the *sūtras* and their glosses by heart on hearing them only once.

@6
in a short time  became  in the pañjī-ṭīkā  expert
of long standing  students  defeats  being  new
= In a short time He became expert in the *Pañjī-ṭīkā*; though a newcomer, He outdid students of long standing.

@7
the pastimes of study  the Lord’s  Vṛndāvana dāsa
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  made  detailed  description
= Vṛndāvana dāsa has described the Lord’s pastimes of study in detail in the *Caitanya-maṅgala*.

@8
one  day  at the mother’s  feet  making  obeisance
the Lord  says  Mother  to Me  give  one  gift
= One day, bowing at His mother’s feet, the Lord said, “Mother, give Me one gift.”

@9
the mother  says  that  I will give  what  You  will ask
the Lord  says  on Ekādaśī  grains  not  you shall eat
= His mother said, “I will give whatever You ask.” The Lord said, “Do not eat grains on Ekādaśī.”

@10
Śacī  says  not  I will eat  well indeed  You said
from that  since  Ekādaśī  to observe  began
= Śacī said, “I will not eat them; You have spoken well.” From then on she began to observe Ekādaśī.

@11
then  Miśra  Viśvarūpa’s  seeing  youth
a girl  seeking  marriage  to give  made  mind
= Then Miśra, seeing that Viśvarūpa had reached youth, resolved to find a bride and arrange his marriage.

@12
Viśvarūpa  hearing  home  leaving  fled
sannyāsa  taking  pilgrimage  to make  went
= Hearing of it, Viśvarūpa left home and fled; he took *sannyāsa* and went on pilgrimage.

@13
hearing  of Śacī and Miśra  sorrowful  became  the minds
then  the Lord  mother and father  made  consolation
= Hearing this, Śacī and Miśra were grief-stricken, and the Lord consoled His mother and father:

@14
good  it has become  Viśvarūpa  sannyāsa  has taken
the father’s family  the mother’s family  both  has delivered
= “It is good that Viśvarūpa has taken *sannyāsa*: he has delivered both his father’s family and his mother’s.

@15
I  indeed  will do  you  both’s  service
hearing  satisfied  became  of father and mother  the minds
= I will serve you both.” Hearing this, His father and mother were comforted.

@16
one day  offered  betel  eating
on the ground  fell  the Lord  unconscious  becoming
= One day, after chewing betel that had been offered to the Deity, the Lord fell to the ground unconscious.

@17
hastily  father and mother  on the face  gave  water
well  becoming  says  the Lord  extraordinary  story
= His father and mother hastily sprinkled water on His face, and when He came to, the Lord told them an extraordinary story:

@18
here  from  Viśvarūpa  Me  taking  went
sannyāsa  take  You  to Me  said
= “Viśvarūpa took Me away from here and told Me, ‘Take *sannyāsa*.’

@19
I  said  My  helpless  father and mother
I  am a boy  of sannyāsa  what  know  I  the matter
= I said, ‘My father and mother are helpless, and I am only a boy—what do I know of *sannyāsa*?

@20
a householder  becoming  I shall do  to father and mother  service
by this  pleased  will be  Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa
= As a householder I shall serve My father and mother; that will please Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa.’

@21
then  Viśvarūpa  here  sent  Me
to the mother  told  millions and millions of  obeisances
= Then Viśvarūpa sent Me back here and asked Me to convey millions upon millions of obeisances to Mother.”

@22
in this way  various  pastimes  does  Gaurahari
for what  reason  the pastimes  this  to understand  not  I can
= In this way Gaurahari performed various pastimes; why He performed them, I cannot understand.

@23
some days  remaining  Miśra  went  to the other world
mother and son  of both  increased  in the heart  grief
= After some days Miśra departed for the other world, and grief welled up in the hearts of both mother and son.

@24
friends and relatives  coming  the two  consoled
the rites for the father  according to the rules  the Lord  performed
= Friends and relatives came and consoled them both, and the Lord performed the rites for His father according to scriptural rule.

@25
after some  days  the Lord  in the mind  made  reflection
a householder  I have become  now  I want  household dharma
= Some days later the Lord reflected, “I have remained a householder; now I should follow the duties of household life.

@26
a housewife  without  household dharma  not  is  beautiful
so  thinking  marriage  to make  became  mind
= Without a wife, household life is not graceful.” Thinking this, He resolved to marry.

@27
not  the house  a home  so  they say
the housewife  the home  is called
with her  indeed  together  all
the goals of life  one attains
= “It is not the house that is called the home; the wife is called the home. Together with her one attains all the goals of life.”

@28
by providence  one  day  the Lord  having studied  coming back
Vallabhācārya’s  daughter  sees  on the path to the Gaṅgā
= One day, by providence, as the Lord was returning from His studies, He saw Vallabhācārya’s daughter on the path to the Gaṅgā.

@29
eternally perfect  feeling  of the two  arising  made
by providence  Vanamālī  the matchmaker  to Śacī’s place  came
= Their eternally perfect love awakened in them both, and by providence the matchmaker Vanamālī came to Śacī.

@30
by Śacī’s  hint  the match  made  arranged
Lakṣmī  married  Śacī’s  son
= At a hint from Śacī he arranged the match, and the son of Śacī married Lakṣmī.

@31
in detail  has described  that  Vṛndāvana dāsa
this  indeed  of the paugaṇḍa pastimes  the sūtra  manifestation
= Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail. Here only the *sūtra* of the *paugaṇḍa* pastimes is set forth.

@32
in the paugaṇḍa pastimes  pastimes  many  indeed  kinds
Vṛndāvana dāsa  this  has made  in detail
= There are many kinds of pastimes in the *paugaṇḍa* period, and Vṛndāvana dāsa has described them in detail.

@33
therefore  a mere indication  here  I have shown
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  among all people  fame  has arisen
= Therefore I have given only a glimpse of them here; through the *Caitanya-maṅgala* they have become famous among all people.

@34
at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  whose  hope
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Hoping for the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, Kṛṣṇadāsa tells the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
