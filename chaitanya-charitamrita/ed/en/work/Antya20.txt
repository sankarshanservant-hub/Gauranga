@title The Śikṣāṣṭaka Prayers

@1
by love  awakened  with joy,  jealousy,  anxiety
humility  and anguish  mingled
the words  of Gauracandra
by the fortunate  are relished
= Only the fortunate can relish the words that Gauracandra spoke in His divine madness, words mingled with the joy, jealousy, anxiety, humility and anguish that love awakens.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
thus  Mahāprabhu  stayed  in Nīlācala
night and day  in separation from Kṛṣṇa  overwhelmed
= Thus Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala, overwhelmed night and day by separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@4
Svarūpa  Rāmānanda  these  two  in the company of
night and day  of rasa  songs  verses  relishing
= Night and day, in the company of these two, Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, He relished songs and verses full of *rasa*.

@5
various  emotions  arise  in the Lord  joy,  grief,  anger
humility,  anxiety,  anguish,  eager longing,  contentment
= Many emotions arose in the Lord: joy, grief and anger, humility, anxiety and anguish, eager longing and contentment.

@6
in that  very  mood  His own  verses  reciting
of the verses  the meaning  He relishes  two  friends  taking with Him
= In each of these moods He would recite His own verses and relish their meaning with His two friends.

@7
on some  day  in some  mood  of a verse  recitation
that  verse  to relish  of the night  keeping awake
= On some day, in some particular mood, He would recite a verse and stay awake the whole night relishing it.

@8
in joy  the Lord  says  listen  Svarūpa and Rāma Rāya
of the name  saṅkīrtana  in Kali  the supreme  means
= In joy the Lord said, “Listen, Svarūpa and Rāma Rāya! In the age of Kali the congregational chanting of the holy name is the supreme means.

@9
by the sacrifice of saṅkīrtana  in Kali  of Kṛṣṇa  worship
he  indeed  intelligent  attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
= In the age of Kali, Kṛṣṇa is worshiped by the sacrifice of *saṅkīrtana*. One who worships Him so is truly intelligent and attains Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

@10
Kṛṣṇa  describing (or: with the syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’)  by lustre  not dark
with limbs,  sub-limbs,  weapons  and associates
by sacrifices  in which saṅkīrtana  predominates
they worship  indeed  the intelligent
= “With sacrifices in which congregational chanting predominates, the wise worship the Lord who ever sings of Kṛṣṇa and whose lustre is not dark. He is always surrounded by His intimate companions (*aṅga*), His devotees (*upāṅga*), His weapons and His associates (*pārṣada*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.32)

@11
by the saṅkīrtana of the name  there is  of all anarthas  destruction
of all  good  the dawning  for Kṛṣṇa  of love  the joy
= Through the congregational chanting of the holy name all unwanted things (*anartha*) are destroyed, every good fortune dawns, and the joy of love for Kṛṣṇa awakens.

@12
of the heart  the mirror  cleansing  of material existence  the great  forest fire  extinguishing
of the highest good  for the white lotus  the moonlight  spreading  of knowledge  the bride  the life
of bliss  the ocean  increasing  at every  step  of the full nectar  the taste
the whole self  bathing  supreme  be victorious  of Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  saṅkīrtana
= “The chanting of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name cleanses the mirror of the heart and puts out the great forest fire of material existence. It spreads the moonlight that opens the white lotus of the soul’s highest good; it is the very life of the bride of divine knowledge. It swells the ocean of bliss, gives at every step a taste of the full nectar, and bathes and cools the whole self. May the supreme chanting of Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s name be ever victorious!” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 1)

@13-14
saṅkīrtana  through  of sin  and of saṁsāra  destruction
of the heart  purification,  of all  devotional  practices  the awakening
of love for Kṛṣṇa  the awakening,  of the nectar of love  the tasting
of Kṛṣṇa  the attainment,  in the ocean of the nectar of service  immersion
= Through *saṅkīrtana* sin and material existence are destroyed, the heart is purified, and all the practices of devotion awaken. Love for Kṛṣṇa awakens, and one tastes the nectar of that love. One attains Kṛṣṇa and is immersed in the nectarean ocean of His service.”

@15
arose  dejection,  humility,  He recites  His own  verse
whose  meaning  hearing  all  go away  sorrow and grief
= Then dejection and humility arose in Him, and He recited another of His own verses; by hearing its meaning all sorrow and grief go away.

@16
of names  You have made  many  Your own  all  potencies
in them  are invested  is fixed  for remembering  not  time
such  Your  mercy  O Lord  my  yet
misfortune  such  for them  has arisen  no  attachment
= “O Lord, You have revealed many names, and in them You have invested all Your own potencies. You have fixed no time for remembering them. Such is Your mercy, yet such is my misfortune that no attachment for them has arisen in me.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 2)

@17
of many  people  the desires  of many  kinds
out of mercy  You made  many  names’  propagation
= “Different people have desires of many kinds, so out of mercy You have spread many names.

@18
while eating  while lying down  wherever  whenever  the name  one takes
of time and place  rules  there are none  all perfection  comes
= Whether eating or lying down, wherever one may be, one can chant the name. There are no rules of time or place, and through the name all perfection comes.

@19
all  potency  in the names  You gave  making  division
my  misfortune  for the name  there is no  attachment
= You divided all Your potency among the names and gave it to them. But such is my misfortune: I have no attachment for the name!”

@20
in what way  by taking  the name  love  is born
of that  of the characteristics  the verse  hear  Svarūpa and Rāma Rāya
= “Svarūpa and Rāma Rāya, hear the verse that tells how one should chant the name so that love is born.

@21
than grass  even  lower
a tree  like  tolerant
free from pride  giving honour
to be glorified  always  Hari
= “Lower than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, seeking no honour and offering honour to others, one should always sing the glories of Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 3)

@22
exalted  being  himself  he considers  lower than grass
in two  ways  tolerance  he shows  like a tree
= “Though he is exalted, a Vaiṣṇava considers himself lower than grass, and like a tree he shows tolerance in two ways.

@23
a tree  as  even when cut  anything  not  says
drying up  even dying  from anyone  water  not  asks
= When a tree is cut down it says nothing, and even when it is drying up and dying it asks no one for water.

@24
whoever  whatever  asks  to him  it gives  its own  wealth
heat and rain  it endures  of others  it gives  protection
= It gives its wealth to whoever asks anything of it; it endures heat and rain, yet shelters others.

@25
exalted  being  a Vaiṣṇava  should be  free from pride
to living beings  respect  he should give  knowing  Kṛṣṇa’s dwelling
= Though exalted, a Vaiṣṇava should be free from pride. He should honor every living being, knowing it to be the dwelling of Kṛṣṇa.

@26
thus  becoming  whoever  Kṛṣṇa’s  name  takes
for Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  his  love  is born
= Whoever chants Kṛṣṇa’s name in this spirit awakens love for the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”

@27
speaking  speaking  the Lord’s  humility  increased
pure devotion  from Kṛṣṇa  to beg  He began
= As He spoke, the Lord’s humility grew, and He began to beg Kṛṣṇa for pure devotion (*śuddha-bhakti*).

@28
of love  the nature  where  with love  a connection
he  thinks  for Kṛṣṇa  my  there is not  of devotion  a trace
= Such is the nature of love: one who is truly touched by love thinks, “I have not even a trace of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.”

@29
not  wealth  not  followers  not  beautiful
poetry  or  O Lord of the universe  I desire
my  in birth  after birth  for the Lord
may there be  devotion  causeless  for You
= “O Lord of the universe, I desire neither wealth, nor followers, nor beautiful poetry. Birth after birth, may I have causeless devotion to You, my Lord.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 4)[^29-1]

@30
wealth,  followers  not  I beg,  poetry  beautiful
pure devotion  give  to me  Kṛṣṇa  mercy  showing
= “I do not beg for wealth, for followers or for beautiful poetry. O Kṛṣṇa, be merciful and give me pure devotion!”

@31
in great  humility  again  He begs  of servitude  devotion  the gift
about Himself  He takes  worldly  a soul’s  self-conception
= In great humility He again begged for the gift of devotion in servitude, thinking of Himself as an ordinary soul caught in worldly existence.

@32
O  of Nanda  son  servant
fallen  me  into the terrible  ocean of material existence
by mercy  Your  of feet  lotus
resting on  a speck of dust  like  consider
= “O son of Nanda, I am Your servant, yet I have fallen into the terrible ocean of material existence. Be merciful and consider me a speck of dust at Your lotus feet.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 5)

@33
Your  eternal  servant  I  You  forgetting
I have fallen  into the ocean of material existence  by māyā  bound  becoming
= “I am Your eternal servant, but I forgot You and fell into the ocean of material existence, bound by *māyā*.

@34
mercy  showing  make  me  of Your feet  the dust  like
Your  servant  I would do  Your  service
= Be merciful and make me like a speck of dust at Your feet, so that as Your servant I may serve You.”

@35
again  intense  longing  humility  there was  an arising
from Kṛṣṇa  He begs  with love  of the name  the chanting
= Again intense longing and humility arose in Him, and He begged Kṛṣṇa for the chanting of the holy name with love.

@36
eyes  of streaming  tears  with a flow
mouth  faltering  choked  with a voice
with thrills of joy  covered  the body  when
Your  name  in taking  will be
= “When, as I chant Your name, will my eyes overflow with streaming tears, my voice falter and choke, and my body be covered with thrills of joy?” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 6)

@37
of love  the wealth  without  futile  poor  life
Your servant  making  as wages  to me  give  of love  the wealth
= “Without the wealth of love, life is poor and futile! Make me Your servant and give me the wealth of love as my wages!”

@38
by absorption in another rasa  there was  of separation  the manifestation
in anxiety,  dejection,  humility  He does  raving
= Absorbed in another *rasa*, He felt the full force of separation, and in anxiety, dejection and humility He began to speak words of divine madness.

@39
seems an age  a moment
by the eyes  the rainy season is enacted
seems empty  the world  whole
in separation from Govinda  for Me
= “In separation from Govinda, a moment seems to Me an age, My eyes pour tears like the rains, and the whole world seems empty.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 7)

@40
in anxiety  the day  not  passes  a moment  has become  an age  like
of the rainy season  a cloud  like  tears  rain  the eyes
= “In My anxiety the day will not pass; a moment has become like an age! Like clouds in the rainy season, My eyes rain tears!

@41
in separation from Govinda  empty  has become  the three worlds
in a fire of husks  I burn  as if  not  goes  life
= In separation from Govinda the three worlds have become empty! I burn as in a slow fire of husks, and yet life does not leave Me.

@42
Kṛṣṇa  indifferent  has become  to make  a test
My friends  all  say  Kṛṣṇa  do  neglect
= Kṛṣṇa has become indifferent to test Me, and all My friends say, ‘Ignore Kṛṣṇa.’”

@43
so much  as He thought  Rādhā’s  pure  heart
natural  of love  the nature  made  rise
= As these thoughts arose, the very nature of spontaneous love dawned in Rādhā’s pure heart.

@44
jealousy,  eagerness,  humility,  boldness,  entreaty
so many  emotions  in one place  made  rise
= Jealousy, eagerness, humility, boldness and entreaty — all these emotions arose at once.

@45
by these  emotions  Rādhā’s  mind  unsteady  became
of the sakhīs  before  a bold verse  which  She spoke
= These emotions unsettled Rādhā’s mind, and before Her friends She spoke a bold verse.

@46
in that mood  the Lord  that  verse  recited
the verse  while reciting  of that form  Himself  He became
= In that same mood the Lord recited that verse, and as He recited it He Himself became like Her.

@47
embracing  or  devoted to His feet  may He crush  Me
by not appearing  heartbroken  may He make  or
as  He likes  or  may He act  the unrestrained lover
of My life  the Lord  yet  He  alone  no other
= “Let Him embrace and crush in His arms this maidservant devoted to His feet, or break My heart by not appearing before Me. Let that unrestrained lover (*lampaṭa*) do with Me whatever He likes — He alone is the Lord of My life, and no one else.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 8)

@48
I  of Kṛṣṇa’s feet  the maidservant  He  of rasa and joy  the abode
embracing  He makes  His own
or  not  He gives  sight of Himself  He wears away  My  body and mind
still  He  of My  life  the Lord
= “I am the maidservant of Kṛṣṇa’s feet, and He is the very abode of *rasa* and joy. He may embrace Me and make Me His own, or He may not let Me see Him and so wear away My body and mind — still He is the Lord of My life.

@49
O friend  listen  My  of mind  the resolve
whether  love  He shows  or  pain  giving  He kills
My  Lord of life  Kṛṣṇa  another  not
= O friend, hear the resolve of My heart. Whether He shows Me love or kills Me with pain, Kṛṣṇa — and no one else — is the Lord of My life.

@50
leaving  other  women  under My control  His body and mind
My  good fortune  manifest  making
to all of them  He gives  pain  with Me  He makes  play
to those  women  showing
= Sometimes He leaves the other women, and His body and mind are under My control. Making My good fortune plain, He pains them all by sporting with Me before their eyes.

@51
or  He  a libertine,  a cheat,  brazen,  deceitful
other  women  making  His companions
to Me  to give  heartache  of Me  before  He makes  play
still  He  of My  life  the Lord
= Or else, libertine, cheat, brazen and deceitful as He is, He takes other women with Him and sports with them before My eyes just to pain My heart — still He is the Lord of My life.

@52
not  I count  My own  pain  only  I desire  His  happiness
His  happiness  My  aim
to Me  if  giving  pain  His  became  great  happiness
that  pain  My  of happiness  the best
= I do not count My own pain; I desire only His happiness. His happiness is My whole aim. If by giving Me pain He finds great happiness, that pain is My greatest happiness.

@53
whichever  woman  desires  Kṛṣṇa  for her  beauty  longing
her  not  obtaining  He becomes  unhappy
I  at her  feet  falling  I bring her  by the hand  holding
pastimes  arranging  Him  I make  happy
= If Kṛṣṇa desires some woman, longing for her beauty, and is unhappy because He cannot have her, I fall at her feet, take her by the hand and bring her to Him, and by arranging their pastimes I make Him happy.

@54
a beloved  with Kṛṣṇa  shows  anger  Kṛṣṇa  gets  satisfaction
joy  He gets  from scolding and rebuke
as is fitting  she shows  pique  Kṛṣṇa  in that  joy  gets
she gives up  her pique  with a little  coaxing
= When a beloved is angry with Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa is pleased; He delights in her scolding and rebukes. When she shows loving pique (*māna*) as is fitting, Kṛṣṇa delights in it, and she gives it up at a little coaxing.

@55
that  woman  lives  why  Kṛṣṇa’s heart  not  knows
yet  with Kṛṣṇa  shows  intense  anger
in her own happiness  she sees  gain  may fall  her  on the head  a thunderbolt
Kṛṣṇa’s  only  we desire  satisfaction
= Why does a woman live who does not know Kṛṣṇa’s heart and yet is bitterly angry with Him? She counts her own happiness as gain — may a thunderbolt fall on her head! We desire only Kṛṣṇa’s satisfaction.

@56
whichever  gopī  toward Me  bears  enmity  Kṛṣṇa’s  makes  satisfaction
Kṛṣṇa  whom  makes  desire
I  to her  house  going  her  I serve  a maidservant  becoming
then  My  of happiness  the exultation
= If a *gopī* who is hostile to Me pleases Kṛṣṇa, and Kṛṣṇa desires her, I will go to her house and serve her as her maidservant. Then My happiness overflows.

@57
of the leprous  brāhmaṇa  the wife  of chaste wives  the crest jewel
her husband  for the sake of  she did  to a prostitute  service
she stopped  the sun’s  course  she revived  dead  husband
pleased  made  the chief  three gods
= The wife of a leprous *brāhmaṇa*, the crest jewel of chaste wives, served a prostitute for her husband’s sake. She stopped the course of the sun, brought her dead husband back to life, and pleased the three principal deities.

@58
Kṛṣṇa  My  life  Kṛṣṇa  My  life’s treasure
Kṛṣṇa  of My  life  the life
upon My heart  I hold Him  service  rendering  happy  I make Him
this  My  always  remains  meditation
= Kṛṣṇa is My life; Kṛṣṇa is the treasure of My life; Kṛṣṇa is the very life of My life. I hold Him upon My heart and make Him happy by serving Him — this is My constant meditation.

@59
My  happiness  in serving  Kṛṣṇa’s  happiness  in union
therefore  My body  I give  as a gift
Kṛṣṇa  Me  His beloved  making  He calls  Me  queen of His life
My  is  of a maidservant  self-conception
= My happiness lies in serving Him; Kṛṣṇa’s happiness lies in union with Me. Therefore I offer Him My body. Kṛṣṇa treats Me as His beloved and calls Me the queen of His life, but I think of Myself as His maidservant.

@60
to the beloved  of service  of happiness  the fullness  union  than  sweeter
of this  the witness  Lakṣmī  Ṭhākurāṇī
in Nārāyaṇa’s heart  her abode  yet  in service to His feet  her inclination
service  she renders  as a maidservant  thinking of herself
= The fullness of joy in serving the beloved is sweeter even than union. The goddess Lakṣmī is witness to this: though she dwells on Nārāyaṇa’s heart, she longs to serve His feet, and she serves Him thinking of herself as His maidservant.”

@61
these  Rādhā’s  words  of pure  love  the marks
relishes  Śrī  Gaura Rāya
from emotion  the mind  not  steady  sāttvika symptoms  pervade  the body
mind and body  holding still  not  possible
= Śrī Gaura Rāya relished these words of Rādhā, which bear the marks of pure love. His mind grew unsteady with emotion, *sāttvika* symptoms pervaded His body, and He could no longer hold His mind and body still.

@62
for the Lord of Vraja  pure  love  like  of the Jāmbū  river  gold
of one’s own happiness  in which  there is no  trace
His own love  to make known  to the world  the Lord  made  this  verse
songs (pada)  He made  of the meaning  as the exposition
= Pure love for the Lord of Vraja is like gold from the river Jāmbū: in it there is no trace of desire for one’s own happiness. To make this love of His known to the world, the Lord composed this verse, and He composed songs that set out its meaning.[^62-1]

@63
thus  Mahāprabhu  absorbed in emotion  becoming
raving  He did  some  verses  reciting
= Thus, absorbed in ecstatic emotion, Mahāprabhu recited some verses and spoke words of divine madness.

@64
earlier  eight  verses  composing  to the people  instruction  He gave
those  eight  verses  Himself  He relished
= Earlier He had composed these eight verses to instruct the people; now He Himself relished them.

@65
the Lord’s  Śikṣāṣṭaka  verses  whoever  reads  hears
for Kṛṣṇa  loving devotion  his  grows  day  by day
= Whoever reads or hears the Lord’s *Śikṣāṣṭaka* verses will find his loving devotion to Kṛṣṇa growing day by day.

@66
although  the Lord  millions of oceans  as deep as
of various  emotions  at the moonrise  He becomes  restless
= Though the Lord is as deep as millions of oceans, He grows restless when the moon of His many emotions rises.

@67-68
whichever  verses  of Jayadeva  in the Bhāgavata
in Rāya’s  drama  whichever  and  in the Karṇāmṛta
in that very  mood  verses  making  recitation
in that very  absorption in emotion  He does  relishing
= Whatever verses of Jayadeva, of the *Bhāgavata*, of Rāmānanda Rāya’s drama and of the *Karṇāmṛta* suited each mood, He would recite in that mood and relish, absorbed in that emotion.

@69
twelve  years  such  a state  night and day
Kṛṣṇa’s  rasa  He relishes  two  friends  with
= For twelve years He was in this state night and day, relishing the *rasa* of Kṛṣṇa with His two friends.

@70
those  all  of the pastimes  the rasa  Himself  Ananta
with a thousand  mouths  describing  not  He finds  the end
= Even Ananta Himself, describing with His thousand mouths the *rasa* of all these pastimes, cannot reach their end.

@71
a soul  of small intelligence  how  that  can  describe
of it  one  particle  I touch  myself  to purify
= How can a soul of small intelligence describe them? I touch one particle of them only to purify myself.

@72
all  the actions  all  the ravings  there is no  limit
that  all  to describe  the book  would be  vast
= His actions and His words of divine madness have no limit; to describe them all would make the book enormous.

@73
Vṛndāvana dāsa  first  which  pastimes  described
of all those  pastimes  I  a summary  only  made
= Of the pastimes that Vṛndāvana dāsa had described before me, I have given only a summary.

@74
by him  left  the remainder  briefly  I told
of the pastimes  by the abundance  the book  even so  grew
= I have briefly told the “remainder” that he left untold, and even so the book has grown, because the pastimes are so many.

@75
therefore  all those  pastimes  not  I can  to describe
I have concluded  the pastimes  making  obeisance
= Therefore I cannot describe all those pastimes; offering my obeisances to them, I have brought my account to a close.

@76
what  little  I have told  this  an indication of direction
this  following  will be  of them  the relishing
= What little I have told is only an indication of the way; by following it one can relish those pastimes.

@77
the Lord’s  profound pastimes  not  I can  understand
of the intellect  entry  there is none  into them  not  I can  describe
= I cannot understand the Lord’s profound pastimes; my intellect cannot enter them, and so I cannot describe them.

@78
of all  the listening  Vaiṣṇavas  honoring  the feet
Caitanya’s  deeds  the description  I have  concluded
= Bowing at the feet of all the Vaiṣṇavas who hear this, I bring to a close my description of Śrī Caitanya’s deeds.

@79
the sky  is unlimited  in it  as  the birds
whose  as much  strength  that much  makes  ascent
= The sky is unlimited, yet each bird in it flies only as high as its strength allows.

@80
so  Mahāprabhu’s  pastimes  there is no  near shore or far
a jīva  being  who  fully  can  describe
= So too Mahāprabhu’s pastimes have no shore on either side. Who, being only a *jīva*, can describe them fully?

@notes
[^29-1]: *Sundarīṁ kavitām*, “beautiful poetry”: so the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* and the *Anubhāṣya* take the words (in the *Anubhāṣya*, eloquence and learning in the service of religiosity, wealth and pleasure); other translations take *sundarī* as “a beautiful woman.”
[^62-1]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *vrajeśvara-śuddha-prema*, “pure love for the Lord of Vraja,” which is translated here; it notes the reading of another manuscript, *vrajera viśuddha prema*, “the pure love of Vraja.”
