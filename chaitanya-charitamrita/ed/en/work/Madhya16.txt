@title Śrī Caitanya’s Longing to Go to Vṛndāvana

@1

= Sprinkling the garden of Gauḍa with the nectar of His glances, the cloud that is Gaura revived the people — creepers scorched by the fire of worldly existence.

@2

glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3

= The Lord conceived the desire to go to Vṛndāvana, and when Pratāparudra heard of it, he was downcast.

@4

= The king summoned the two of them, Sārvabhauma and Rāmānanda, and spoke to them humbly:

@5

= “The Lord has a mind to leave Nīlādri and go elsewhere. Please make every effort to keep Him here.

@6

= Without Him this kingdom gives me no pleasure. Use every means to keep the Gosāñi here.”

@7

= Then the Lord took counsel with the two of them, Rāmānanda and Sārvabhauma: “I will go to Vṛndāvana.”

@8

= Both of them said, “See the Ratha-yātrā first; when Kārtika comes, then You may go.”

@9

= When Kārtika came, they said, “Now it is bitterly cold. See the Dola-yātrā and then go — that is the proper way.”

@10

to going  consent  not  give  of separation  out of fear
= Saying “today, tomorrow,” they raised one pretext after another; afraid of separation, they would not consent to His going.

@11

= Although the Lord is independent and no one can stop Him, He would not leave without the consent of His devotees.

@12

= In the third year all the devotees of Gauḍa resolved to go to Nīlācala.

@13

= They all gathered and went to Advaita Ācārya, and the Ācārya joyfully set out to see the Lord.

= Seeing his devotion, the Lord was pleased at heart. Mahāprabhu rose and embraced him.

@106
again  prayers  offering  the king  makes  obeisance
in the tears of the Lord’s mercy  his  body  became  bathed
= Again the king offered prayers and bowed down, and his body was bathed in tears of the Lord’s mercy.

@107
calm  making  Rāmānanda  the king  seated
in body, mind and words  the Lord  to him  mercy  showed
= Rāmānanda calmed the king and seated him, and the Lord showed him mercy in body, mind and words.

@108
such  to him  mercy  showed  Gaurarāya
the Saviour of Pratāparudra  the name  became  by which
= Gaurarāya showed him such mercy that He came to be called “the Saviour of Pratāparudra.”

@109
the royal ministers  made  of the Lord  homage
to the king  leave  gave  of Śacī  the son
= The king’s ministers paid homage to the Lord, and Śacī’s son gave the king leave to go.

@110
outside  coming  the king  a written order  had written
in his own kingdom  as many  revenue officers  to them  sent
= Coming outside, the king had an order drawn up and sent it to all the revenue officers (*viṣayī*) of his kingdom:

@111
in village after village  new  lodgings  you shall make
five or seven  houses  with all  provisions  you shall fill
= “In every village build new lodgings; fill five or seven houses with all provisions.

@112
yourselves  the Lord  taking  there  you shall lodge
night and day  cane in hand  in service  you shall remain
= Bring the Lord there yourselves and lodge Him there, and stay in His service night and day, cane in hand.”

@113
two  great ministers  —  Haricandana  Maṅgarāja
to them  order  gave  the king  —  do  every  task
= The king gave this order to two of his chief ministers, Haricandana and Maṅgarāja: “See to everything.

@114
one  new boat  bringing  keep  on the river bank
where  bath  having taken  the Lord  goes  across the river
= Bring a new boat and keep it at the river bank where the Lord will bathe and cross the river.

@115
there  a pillar  erecting  do  a great tīrtha  making
daily  bath  take  there  there  may  I die
= There erect a pillar, making it a great *tīrtha*. Bathe there every day — and may I leave my body there.

@116
in Caturdvāra  build  excellent  new  lodgings
Rāmānanda  go  you  to Mahāprabhu’s side
= Build excellent new lodgings in Caturdvāra. And you, Rāmānanda, go to Mahāprabhu.”

@117
in the evening  will set out  the Lord  —  the king  heard
on elephants  in tents  the women  he seated
= The king heard that the Lord would set out in the evening, and he had the women of the palace seated in tents on the backs of elephants.

@118
of the Lord  of the going  on the road  they stay  in a row  forming
in the evening  set out  the Lord  His own associates  taking
= They stood in a row along the road the Lord was to take, and in the evening the Lord set out with His associates.

@119
to the river Citrotpalā  coming  at the ghāṭ  made  bath
the queens all  see  make  obeisance
= Coming to the river Citrotpalā, He bathed at the *ghāṭ*, and all the queens watched Him and bowed down.

@120
of the Lord  at the sight  all  became  full of love
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  they say  the eyes  tears  shower
= At the sight of the Lord they were all filled with love; they cried “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” and tears streamed from their eyes.

@121
such  merciful  not  I hear  in the three worlds
love for Kṛṣṇa  arises  whose  from afar  at the sight
= Nowhere in the three worlds have I heard of one so merciful: love for Kṛṣṇa awakens even in those who see Him from afar.

@122
into the boat  climbing  the Lord  became  of the river  across
on a moonlit  night  going  came  to Caturdvāra
= The Lord boarded the boat and crossed the river, and travelling on through the moonlit night He came to Caturdvāra.

@123
at night  there  staying  in the morning  bathing  made
at that time  of Jagannātha  mahā-prasāda  came
= He stayed there that night and bathed in the morning. Just then Jagannātha’s *mahā-prasāda* arrived.

@124
of the king  by the order  the paḍichā  sends  day  by day
abundant  prasāda  sends  by  many people
= By the king’s order the *paḍichā* sent *prasāda* day after day — a great quantity of it, carried by many people.

@125
with His own associates  the Lord  prasāda  accepting
rising  set out  the Lord  saying  Hari  Hari
= The Lord accepted the *prasāda* with His associates, then rose and set out, calling “Hari! Hari!”

@126
Rāmānanda  Maṅgarāja  Śrī Haricandana
together  service  doing  go  these  three  persons
= Rāmānanda, Maṅgarāja and Śrī Haricandana — these three went along with Him, serving Him.

@127-129
with the Lord  Purī Gosāñi  Svarūpa Dāmodara
Jagadānanda  Mukunda  Govinda  Kāśīśvara
Haridāsa Ṭhākura  and  Paṇḍita Vakreśvara
Gopīnāthācārya  and  Paṇḍita Dāmodara
Rāmāi  Nandāi  and  many  devotees
the chief  I have named  of all  who  makes  a count
= With the Lord were Purī Gosāñi, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Jagadānanda, Mukunda, Govinda, Kāśīśvara, Haridāsa Ṭhākura, Vakreśvara Paṇḍita, Gopīnāthācārya, Dāmodara Paṇḍita, Rāmāi, Nandāi and many other devotees. I have named only the chief among them — who can count them all?

@130
Gadādhara Paṇḍita  when  along  set out
the kṣetra-sannyāsa  not  give up  —  the Lord  forbade
= When Gadādhara Paṇḍita set out with them, the Lord forbade him: “Do not give up your *kṣetra-sannyāsa*.”

@131
the Paṇḍita  says  —  where  You  that  Nīlācala
kṣetra-sannyāsa  my  let go  to Rasātala
= The Paṇḍita said, “Wherever You are, that is Nīlācala. Let my *kṣetra-sannyāsa* go to Rasātala!”

@132
the Lord  says  —  here  do  Gopīnātha’s  service
the Paṇḍita  says  —  ten million services  the sight of Your feet
= The Lord said, “Stay here and serve Gopīnātha.” The Paṇḍita said, “Seeing Your feet is worth ten million services.”

@133
the Lord  says  —  the service  you will give up  to Me  attaches  the fault
here  staying  service  do  —  My  satisfaction
= The Lord said, “If you give up the service, the fault will fall on Me. Stay here and serve — that will please Me.”

@134
the Paṇḍita  says  —  all  fault  me  upon
with You  not  I shall go  I shall go  alone
= The Paṇḍita said, “All the fault will be upon me. I shall not go with You; I shall go alone.

@135
Āi  to see  I shall go  not  I shall go  You  for the sake of
of giving up the vow and the service  the fault  —  of that  I  the bearer
= I shall go to see Āi (Mother Śacī), not for Your sake. The fault of giving up my vow and my service — that I shall bear myself.”

@136
so  saying  Paṇḍita Gosāñi  separately  went
to Kaṭaka  coming  the Lord  him  with  had brought
= Saying this, Paṇḍita Gosāñi travelled on separately. When the Lord reached Kaṭaka, He had him brought to Him.

@137
of the Paṇḍita  for Gaurāṅga  love  to understand  not  is possible
the vow  the service of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  he gave up  like a straw
= The Paṇḍita’s love for Gaurāṅga is beyond understanding: he gave up his vow and the service of Śrī Kṛṣṇa as though they were a blade of grass.

@138
his  by the conduct  the Lord  inwardly  satisfaction
his  by the hand  taking  He says  making  loving anger
= Inwardly the Lord was pleased with his conduct, but He took him by the hand and spoke with loving anger:

@139
the vow  the service  to give up  —  this  your  aim
that  accomplished  became  —  leaving  you have come  far  country
= “Giving up your vow and your service — that was your aim, and it has been accomplished: you have left them and come to a distant land.

@140
Me  with  to stay  you want  —  you desire  your own happiness
your  two  duties  go  —  Mine  becomes  sorrow
= You want to stay with Me — you are seeking your own happiness. But you are losing both your duties, and that makes Me sad.

@141
My  happiness  you want  if  to Nīlācala  go
by Me  sworn  if  more  anything  you say
= If you want My happiness, go back to Nīlācala. I adjure you by Myself — do not say another word.”

@142
so  saying  Mahāprabhu  into the boat  climbed
unconscious  becoming  the Paṇḍita  there  fell
= Saying this, Mahāprabhu got into the boat, and the Paṇḍita fell there unconscious.

@143
the Paṇḍita  taking  to go  to Sārvabhauma  order  gave
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  —  rise  such  of the Lord  the līlā
= The Lord ordered Sārvabhauma to take the Paṇḍita back with him. The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Get up — such is the Lord’s *līlā*.

@144
you  know  Kṛṣṇa  His own  vow  gave up
for His devotee  by mercy compelled  Bhīṣma’s  vow  He kept
= You know that Kṛṣṇa gave up His own vow; compelled by mercy for His devotee, He kept Bhīṣma’s vow.

@145
His own promise  giving up  my promise
true  to make  He jumped down  standing on the chariot
holding the chariot wheel  He rushed  the earth trembling
a lion  like  to kill  an elephant  His upper cloth fallen
= “To make my promise true, He gave up His own: He leapt down from the chariot, took up a chariot wheel and rushed at me like a lion going to kill an elephant, so that the earth trembled, and His upper cloth fell away” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.9.37).
