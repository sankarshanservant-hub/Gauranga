@title Śrī Caitanya’s Longing to Go to Vṛndāvana

@1
the garden of Gauḍa  the Gaura-cloud
sprinkling  with the nectar of His glances
by the fire of worldly existence  scorched  of the people
the creepers  revived
= Sprinkling the garden of Gauḍa with the nectar of His glances, the cloud that is Gaura revived the people — creepers scorched by the fire of worldly existence.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of the Lord  arose  the desire  to go  to Vṛndāvana
hearing  Pratāparudra  became  downcast
= The Lord conceived the desire to go to Vṛndāvana, and when Pratāparudra heard of it, he was downcast.

@4
Sārvabhauma  Rāmānanda  summoning  the two  persons
to both  speaks  the king  humble  words
= The king summoned the two of them, Sārvabhauma and Rāmānanda, and spoke to them humbly:

@5
Nīlādri  leaving  of the Lord  the mind  elsewhere  to go
you  make  effort  Him  to keep
= “The Lord has a mind to leave Nīlādri and go elsewhere. Please make every effort to keep Him here.

@6
Him  without  this  kingdom  to me  not  is pleasing
the Gosāñi  to keep  employ  various  means
= Without Him this kingdom gives me no pleasure. Use every means to keep the Gosāñi here.”

@7
Rāmānanda  Sārvabhauma  with the two
then  counsel  takes  the Lord  —  “I will go  to Vṛndāvana”
= Then the Lord took counsel with the two of them, Rāmānanda and Sārvabhauma: “I will go to Vṛndāvana.”

@8
the two  say  —  the Ratha-yātrā  do  seeing
Kārtika  when it comes  then  make  departure
= Both of them said, “See the Ratha-yātrā first; when Kārtika comes, then You may go.”

@9
Kārtika  when it came  they say  —  now  great cold
the Dola-yātrā  having seen  go  —  this  good  way
= When Kārtika came, they said, “Now it is bitterly cold. See the Dola-yātrā and then go — that is the proper way.”

@10
today–tomorrow  saying  raise  various  pretexts
to going  consent  not  give  of separation  out of fear
= Saying “today, tomorrow,” they raised one pretext after another; afraid of separation, they would not consent to His going.

@11
although  independent  the Lord  not  is to be stopped
the devotees’ wish  without  the Lord  not  makes  departure
= Although the Lord is independent and no one can stop Him, He would not leave without the consent of His devotees.

@12
in the third  year  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees
to Nīlācala  to go  of all  arose  the wish
= In the third year all the devotees of Gauḍa resolved to go to Nīlācala.

@13
all  gathering  went  Advaita Ācārya’s  to the side
the Lord  to see  the Ācārya  set out  joyfully
= They all gathered and went to Advaita Ācārya, and the Ācārya joyfully set out to see the Lord.

@14-15
although  of the Lord  the order  in Gauḍa  to stay
to Nityānanda Prabhu  prema-bhakti  to make manifest
nevertheless  set out  Mahāprabhu  to see
Nityānanda’s  loving dealings  who  can  understand
= Although the Lord had ordered Nityānanda Prabhu to stay in Gauḍa and reveal *prema-bhakti*, He nevertheless set out to see Mahāprabhu. Who can understand the ways of Nityānanda’s love?

@16-17
Ācāryaratna  Vidyānidhi  Śrīvāsa  Rāmāi
Vāsudeva  Murāri  Govinda and the others  the three brothers
Rāghava Paṇḍita  his own bags  having prepared
the residents of Kulīna-grāma  go  silk ropes  taking
= Ācāryaratna, Vidyānidhi, Śrīvāsa, Rāmāi, Vāsudeva, Murāri, the three brothers Govinda and the others, and Rāghava Paṇḍita, who had packed his bags (*jhāli*) — all set out; the residents of Kulīna-grāma came bringing silk ropes.

@18
the resident of Khaṇḍa  Narahari  Śrī Raghunandana
all the devotees  go  of them  who  makes  a count
= Narahari of Khaṇḍa and Śrī Raghunandana came too. All the devotees set out — who can count them?

@19
Śivānanda Sena  does  at the toll stations  the settling
all  care  taking  happily  leading  goes
= Śivānanda Sena settled the tolls at the crossings; taking care of everyone, he led them happily along.

@20
of all  all the affairs  he does  gives  lodging places
Śivānanda  knows  of the Oḍiyā roads  the details
= He attended to everyone’s needs and found them places to stay, for Śivānanda knew the roads through Orissa well.

@21
that  year  the Lord  to see  all  the ladies
went  with the Ācārya  Acyuta’s  mother
= That year all the ladies went to see the Lord. Acyuta’s mother went with the Ācārya.

@22
with Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita  went  Mālinī
with Śivānanda  goes  his  wife
= Mālinī went with Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, and Śivānanda’s wife went with him.

@23
Śivānanda’s  son  name  —  Caitanya dāsa
he  set out  the Lord  to see  joyfully
= Śivānanda’s son, named Caitanya dāsa, also set out joyfully to see the Lord.

@24
with Ācāryaratna  goes  his  wife
of that one  of the love  the account  to tell  not  I know how
= Ācāryaratna’s wife went with him. I do not know how to describe that love.

@25
all  the ladies  to Mahāprabhu  a meal  to give
of the Lord  various  favourite  foods  took  from home
= To offer Mahāprabhu a meal, all the ladies brought from home many of the Lord’s favourite foods.

@26
Śivānanda Sena  does  all  the arranging
the toll collectors  pacifying  gives  to all  lodging places
= Śivānanda Sena arranged everything. He pacified the toll collectors (*ghāṭiyāla*) and found lodgings for everyone.

@27
food  giving  does  of all  everywhere  the care
in supreme  bliss  goes  of the Lord  to the sight
= Everywhere he looked after them all and gave them food, and he went on in supreme bliss to see the Lord.

@28
to Remuṇā  coming  they made  of Gopīnātha  darśana
the Ācārya  did  there  kīrtana  dancing
= Coming to Remuṇā, they had *darśana* of Gopīnātha, and there the Ācārya sang and danced.

@29
Nityānanda’s  acquaintance  with all the people
great  honour  coming  showed  the servants
= Nityānanda was known to all the people there, and the temple servants came and showed Him great honour.

@30
that  night  all  the great souls  right there  stayed
twelve  pots of kṣīra  bringing  before  the servant  placed
= All those great souls stayed there that night, and a servant brought twelve pots of *kṣīra* (sweet rice) and set them before them.

@31
the kṣīra  dividing  to all  gave  Nityānanda Prabhu
the kṣīra prasāda  receiving  of all  increased  the bliss
= Nityānanda Prabhu divided the *kṣīra* and gave it to everyone, and receiving that *kṣīra-prasāda*, they were all filled with joy.

@32
of Mādhava Purī  the story  of Gopāla  the installation
from him  Gopāla  how  asked for  sandalwood
= The story of Mādhava Purī, the installation of Gopāla, how Gopāla asked him for sandalwood,

@33
him  for the sake of  Gopīnātha  the kṣīra  theft  made
of Mahāprabhu  from the mouth  before  this  story  they had heard
= and how Gopīnātha stole *kṣīra* for his sake — they had heard this story earlier from Mahāprabhu’s own mouth.

@34
that  story  all  among  tells  Nityānanda
hearing  in the hearts of the Vaiṣṇavas  increased  the bliss
= Now Nityānanda told that story among them all, and the Vaiṣṇavas’ hearts swelled with joy as they listened.

@35
in this way  going on  going on  to Kaṭaka  they came
Sākṣi-gopāla  seeing  all  that  day  stayed
= Travelling on in this way, they came to Kaṭaka. There they saw Sākṣi-gopāla and stayed that day.

@36
of Sākṣi-gopāla  the story  tells  Nityānanda
hearing  in the hearts of the Vaiṣṇavas  increased  the bliss
= Nityānanda told the story of Sākṣi-gopāla, and the Vaiṣṇavas’ hearts swelled with joy as they listened.

@37
the Lord  to meet  of all  eagerness  in the heart
quickly  making  came  all  to Śrī Nīlācala
= Their hearts were all eager to meet the Lord, so they hurried on to Śrī Nīlācala.

@38
at Āṭhāranālā  they have come  the Gosāñi  hearing
two garlands  sent  Govinda’s  in the hand  placing
= Hearing that they had reached Āṭhāranālā, the Gosāñi placed two garlands in Govinda’s hands and sent him to them.

@39
the two  garlands  Govinda  on the two  persons  put
Advaita  the Avadhūta Gosāñi  great  happiness  felt
= Govinda put the two garlands on the two of them, and Advaita and the Avadhūta Gosāñi (Nityānanda) were overjoyed.

@40
right there  beginning  made  of Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana
dancing  dancing  going  came  the two
= Right there they began Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana, and the two of them came on, dancing all the way.

@41
again  garlands  giving  to Svarūpa and the others  to His own men
ahead  sending forward  sent  of Śacī  by the son
= Then Śacī’s son gave garlands to Svarūpa and His other associates and sent them ahead.

@42
to Narendra  coming  there  with all  they met
by Mahāprabhu  given  the garlands  on all  they put
= Coming to Narendra, they met everyone there and put on them all the garlands Mahāprabhu had given.

@43
near the Siṁha-dvāra  they have come  hearing  Gaurarāya
Himself  coming  the Lord  met  with all
= When Gaurarāya heard that they had reached the Siṁha-dvāra, the Lord came Himself and met them all.

@44
all  taking  He made  Jagannātha-darśana
all  taking  came  again  to His own residence
= Taking them all with Him, He had *darśana* of Jagannātha, and then He brought them all back to His own residence.

@45
Vāṇīnātha  Kāśī Miśra  prasāda  brought
with His own hand  all  the Lord  prasāda  fed
= Vāṇīnātha and Kāśī Miśra brought *prasāda*, and the Lord fed everyone *prasāda* with His own hand.

@46
in the previous year  whose  whatever  lodging place
there  all  sending  made rest
= He sent each of them to the lodging they had had the year before and let them rest.

@47
in this way  the devotees  stayed  four  months
the Lord  with  do  the pastimes of kīrtana
= In this way the devotees stayed four months, enjoying the pastimes of *kīrtana* with the Lord.

@48
as before  the time of the Ratha-yātrā  when  came
all  taking  the Guṇḍicā temple  He washed
= When the time of the Ratha-yātrā came, He took them all, as before, and washed the Guṇḍicā temple.

@49
the residents of Kulīna-grāma  silk ropes  to Jagannātha  gave
as before  in front of the chariot  dancing  He did
= The residents of Kulīna-grāma gave silk ropes to Jagannātha, and as before He danced in front of the chariot.

@50
much  dancing  having done  then  went  to the garden
on the bank of the pond  there  going  made  rest
= After dancing a great deal He went to the garden, and there He rested on the bank of the pond.

@51
from Rāḍha  one  brāhmaṇa  he  —  a servant of Nityānanda
most fortunate  he  name  —  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= There was a brāhmaṇa from Rāḍha, a servant of Nityānanda, most fortunate, named Kṛṣṇadāsa.

@52
a pitcher  filling  of the Lord  he  bathing  did
his  by the bathing  the Lord  greatly pleased  became
= He filled a pitcher and bathed the Lord, and the Lord was greatly pleased with his bathing Him.

@53
of the Balagaṇḍi offering  much  prasāda  came
all  with  Mahāprabhu  prasāda  ate
= A great deal of *prasāda* came from the Balagaṇḍi offering, and Mahāprabhu honoured the *prasāda* with them all.

@54
as before  the Ratha-yātrā  He made  seeing
the Herā-pañcamī festival  sees  taking  the devotees
= As before, He watched the Ratha-yātrā, and with His devotees He saw the festival of Herā-pañcamī.

@55
Ācārya Gosāñi  of the Lord  made  invitation
that  in the midst of  made  how  a storm of rain
= Ācārya Gosāñi invited the Lord, and in the middle of it he brought on a storm of rain —

@56
in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa
Śrīvāsa  the Lord  then  made  invitation
= all this Vṛndāvana dāsa has described in detail. Then Śrīvāsa invited the Lord.

@57
the Lord’s  favourite dishes  all  cooks  Mālinī
“in devotion  a maidservant” — such self-conception  “in affection  a mother”
= Mālinī cooked all the Lord’s favourite dishes. In devotion she thought of herself as His maidservant; in affection she was His mother.

@58
Ācāryaratna and the others  as many  chief  devotees
from time  to time  the Lord  make  invitation
= Ācāryaratna and the other leading devotees invited the Lord from time to time.

@59
at the end of Cāturmāsya  again  Nityānanda  taking
some kind of  counsel  holds  daily  in private  sitting
= At the end of Cāturmāsya the Lord would again take Nityānanda aside every day and sit with Him in private, holding some counsel.

@60
Ācārya Gosāñi  to the Lord  speaks  in hints
the Ācārya  riddles  recites  anyone  to understand  not  is able
= Ācārya Gosāñi spoke to the Lord in hints; the Ācārya recited riddles (*tarjā*) that no one could understand.

@61
his  face  seeing  smiles  of Śacī  the son
acceptance  knowing  the Ācārya  does  dancing
= Looking at his face, Śacī’s son smiled; taking this as His assent, the Ācārya began to dance.

@62
whether  a prayer  whether  an order  —  anyone  not  understood
embrace  making  the Lord  him  leave  gave
= Whether it was a prayer or an instruction, no one understood. Then the Lord embraced him and bade him farewell.

@63
to Nityānanda  says  the Lord  —  listen  Śrīpāda
this  I  beg  You  grant  the favour
= The Lord said to Nityānanda, “Listen, Śrīpāda: I beg this of You — grant Me this favour.

@64
every year  to Nīlācala  You  not  should come
in Gauḍa  staying  My  desire  fulfilled  should make
= Do not come to Nīlācala every year. Stay in Gauḍa and fulfil My desire.

@65
that  fulfilment  does  —  such  another  not  I see
My  difficult  work  You  from  is accomplished
= I see no one else who could accomplish it. My difficult work can be done only by You.”

@66
Nityānanda  says  —  I  the body  You  the life
the body  the life  different  not are  —  this  indeed  the proof
= Nityānanda said, “I am the body and You are the life. Body and life are not separate — this is the proof.

@67
by inconceivable potency  bring about  You  of that  the accomplishment
whatever  You make Me do  that  I do  there is no  rule
= Yet by Your inconceivable potency You bring this about. Whatever You make Me do, that I do; there is no rule here.”

@68
Him  leave  gave  the Lord  making  an embrace
in this way  leave  gave  all  the devotees
= The Lord embraced Him and bade Him farewell, and in the same way He bade farewell to all the devotees.

@69
the resident of Kulīna-grāma  as before  made  a submission
Lord  an order  give  —  my  duty  practice
= As before, a resident of Kulīna-grāma submitted, “Lord, please instruct me: what is my duty, my practice?”

@70

two  do  quickly  you will obtain  the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa
= The Lord said, “Serve the Vaiṣṇavas and chant the Holy Name in *saṅkīrtana*. Do these two, and you will quickly attain the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”

@71

= He asked, “Who is a Vaiṣṇava? What are his marks?” Then the Lord, knowing his mind, smiled and said,

@72

= “One in whose mouth the Name of Kṛṣṇa is always present is the best of Vaiṣṇavas. Worship his feet.”

@73
after a year  again  they  such  a question  asked

= A year later they asked the same question again, and the Lord taught them the gradation of Vaiṣṇavas:

@74

= “One whose very sight brings the Name of Kṛṣṇa to the mouth — know him to be the foremost Vaiṣṇava.”

@76

@77

= He had a loving friendship with Svarūpa, and the two of them stayed together, absorbed in talks about Kṛṣṇa.

= He gave the *mantra* to Gadādhara Paṇḍita a second time, and he saw the festival on the day of Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī.

@79

@80

= That night Jagannātha and Balāi came to him, and the two brothers slapped him, laughing all the while.

@81
the cheeks  swelled  the Ācārya’s  in the heart  joy

= His cheeks swelled, but the Ācārya rejoiced at heart. Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail.
