@title The Supreme Principle of the Guru

@1
I worship  the gurus  the Lord's devotees
the Lord  the Lord's avatāras
His manifestations  and  His potencies
Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  known as
= I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, the Lord’s avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies, and the Lord Himself, who is known as Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.

@2
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
(and) Nityānanda  risen together
on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and the moon
wondrous  bestowing auspiciousness  dispelling darkness
= I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda, who have risen together like the sun and the moon on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa. Wondrous and bestowing auspiciousness, They dispel the darkness.

@3
that which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  too  His  the radiance of the body
who  the Self dwelling within  the Puruṣa  thus (called)  he  His  plenary expansion
with the six opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one
not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  Supreme Truth  higher  here
= What the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is only the radiance of His body. The one called the Puruṣa, the Supersoul dwelling within all, is His plenary expansion. And Bhagavān, full of the six opulences, is He Himself. In this world there is no Supreme Truth higher than Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa.

@4
not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in the age of Kali
to bestow  most exalted radiant rasa  the treasure of devotion to Himself
Hari  than gold  more beautiful  of radiance  by a mass  illumined
always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  the son of Śacī
= “Out of mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a long time: the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted and radiant *rasa* of divine love. May that Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, resplendent with a mass of radiance more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the cave of your heart.” (Vidagdha-mādhava 1.2)

@5
Rādhā  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the transformation  pleasure-giving  potency  therefore
one in soul  although  on earth  long ago  separate bodies  assumed  They two
called Caitanya  manifest  now  those two  and  oneness  attained
with Rādhā's emotion and radiance  adorned  I bow to  Kṛṣṇa Himself
= Rādhā is the transformation of love for Kṛṣṇa; She is His pleasure-giving potency (*hlādinī śakti*). Therefore, although Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in soul, long ago They assumed separate forms on earth. Now the two have become one and appeared as Caitanya. I bow to Him, who is Kṛṣṇa Himself, adorned with Rādhā’s emotion and radiance.

@6
of Śrī Rādhā  of love  the greatness  of what kind  or  by Her alone
relished  by which (love)  wondrous sweetness  of what kind  or  Mine
the happiness  and  Her  from realizing Me  of what kind  or  thus  out of longing
rich with Her emotion  was born  in the ocean of Śacī's womb  the moon Hari
= “How great is Śrī Rādhā’s love? What is My own wondrous sweetness, which She alone relishes through that love? And what happiness does She feel when She realizes Me?” Longing to know this, the moon Hari, rich with Her emotion, was born from the ocean of Śacī’s womb.

@7
Saṅkarṣaṇa  lying in the Causal Ocean
lying in the Garbha Ocean  and  lying in the Ocean of Milk
Śeṣa  and  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-
-nanda called  Rāma  shelter  my  may be
= Saṅkarṣaṇa, Kāraṇodakaśāyī, Garbhodakaśāyī, Kṣīrodakaśāyī and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions. May that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my shelter.

@8
beyond māyā  in the all-pervading realm of Vaikuṇṭha
full of opulence  among the divine four vyūhas
the form  whose  shines forth  called Saṅkarṣaṇa
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
= Beyond māyā lies the all-pervading realm of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence. There, among the four divine vyūhas, His form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines forth. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.

@9
the master of māyā  whose body shelters the multitude of universes
lies  in person  in the midst of the Causal Ocean
whose  one portion  the blessed Puruṣa  the original Lord
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
= The blessed Puruṣa, the original Lord and the master of māyā, whose body shelters countless universes, lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean. He is one portion of Nityānanda. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.

@10
whose  portion of a portion  the blessed Garbhodakaśāyī
from whose navel the lotus  of the multitude of worlds  the stem
of the creator of the worlds  the birthplace  of Brahmā
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
= “The blessed Garbhodakaśāyī is a portion of His portion. The lotus from His navel has a stem that holds the multitude of worlds, and it is the birthplace of Brahmā, the creator of the worlds. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.” (Svarūpa Dāmodara’s diary)

@11
whose  portion of a portion of a portion  the Supersoul  of all beings
the maintainer  Viṣṇu  shines  lying in the Ocean of Milk
the upholder of the earth  whose part  he  also  Ananta
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
= “Viṣṇu who lies in the Ocean of Milk, the Supersoul and maintainer of all beings, is a portion of a portion of His portion. Ananta, who upholds the earth, is also His part. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.” (Svarūpa Dāmodara’s diary)

@12
Mahā-Viṣṇu  the creator of the world
through māyā  who  creates  this (world)
His  avatāra  indeed  this
Advaita Ācārya  the Lord
= Mahā-Viṣṇu, the creator of the world, creates this universe through His energy, māyā. The Lord Advaita Ācārya is His very avatāra.

@13
(called) Advaita  being non-different from Hari
(called) Ācārya  for proclaiming bhakti
the avatāra as a devotee  the Lord  Him
Advaita Ācārya  I take shelter of
= He is called Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, and Ācārya because He proclaims devotion (*bhakti*). I take shelter of that Lord, Advaita Ācārya, who descended as a devotee.

@14
comprising the five truths  Kṛṣṇa
in the form of a devotee  in the identity of a devotee
the avatāra as a devotee  the one called a devotee
I bow to  the devotional potency
= “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who comprises the five truths (Pañca-tattva). He appears in the form of a devotee, in the identity of a devotee, as the avatāra of a devotee, as the one called a devotee, and as the devotional potency.” (Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī)

@15
all glory  to the most merciful  of the lame
my  of little intelligence  the refuge
whose lotus feet are my all in all
Rādhā  and Madana-mohana
= All glory to the most merciful Rādhā and Madana-mohana! They are the refuge of me, who am lame and of little intelligence, and Their lotus feet are my all in all.

@16
resplendent  in the forest of Vṛndāvana  a wish-fulfilling tree  beneath
in a splendid  jewelled temple  on a throne  seated
Śrī Śrī  Rādhā  Śrīla Govinda-deva
by Their dearest companions  being served  I remember
= In resplendent Vṛndāvana, beneath a wish-fulfilling tree, Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Śrīla Govinda-deva sit on a throne in a splendid jewelled temple, and Their dearest companions serve Them. I remember Them.

@17
the beautiful  who began the rasa of the rāsa dance
at Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing
attracting  with the sounds of His flute  the gopīs
Gopīnātha  for the welfare  may He be  our
= Beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha began the sweet *rasa* of the rāsa dance. He stands on the riverbank at Vaṁśīvaṭa and draws the gopīs with the sound of His flute. May He bestow His blessings upon us.

@18
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host
= All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@19
these  three  Deities  the Gauḍīyas  have made  Their own
these three's  feet  I worship  the three  my  masters
= These three Deities have made the Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas Their own. I worship the feet of these three, for these three are my masters.

@20
of the book  at the beginning  I make  the invocation
guru  Vaiṣṇava  Bhagavān  of the three  remembrance
= At the beginning of this book I offer the auspicious invocation (*maṅgalācaraṇa*) by remembering three: the guru, the Vaiṣṇavas and the Lord.

@21
of the three  in remembering  there is  destruction of obstacles
easily  there is  one's own  fulfilment of desires
= Remembering these three destroys every obstacle, and one’s own desires are easily fulfilled.

@22
that  invocation  is  threefold  in kind
defining the subject  blessing  obeisance
= That invocation is of three kinds: defining the subject, giving a blessing and offering obeisances.

@23
in the first  two  verses  obeisance to the worshipful Lord
general and particular  in form  two  indeed  kinds
= In the first two verses I offer obeisances to the worshipful Lord. These are of two kinds: general and particular.

@24
in the third  verse  I make  of the subject  the definition
which  from  one knows  of the Supreme Truth  the identity
= In the third verse I define the subject, and from it one can know the identity of the Supreme Truth.

@25
in the fourth  verse  I make  to the world  blessing
everywhere  I pray for  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya's  mercy
= In the fourth verse I bless the world, praying that the mercy of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya may reach everywhere.

@26
in that  verse  I tell  the external reason for the descent
in the fifth  sixth  verses  I tell  the root purpose
= In that verse I also state the external reason for His descent. In the fifth and sixth verses I state its root purpose.

@27
in these  six  verses  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya's  truth
then  in five  verses  Nityānanda's  greatness
= These six verses describe the truth of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. The next five verses describe the greatness of Nityānanda.

@28
then  in two  verses  an account of Advaita's truth
then  in one  verse  of the Pañca-tattva  the explanation
= The next two verses give an account of the truth of Advaita, and one more verse explains the Pañca-tattva.

@29
in these  fourteen  verses  I make  the invocation
that  within  I tell  all  the description of the subject
= With these fourteen verses I offer the invocation, and within them I describe the whole subject.

@30
to all  the listening Vaiṣṇavas  having offered  obeisances
of all these  verses  I make  examination of the meaning
= Offering obeisances to all the Vaiṣṇavas who are listening, I shall now examine the meaning of all these verses.

@31
all  Vaiṣṇavas  listen  making  one-pointed mind
of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the scriptures  as  establish
= O Vaiṣṇavas, all of you, listen with undivided attention to how the scriptures establish the truth of Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa.

@32
Kṛṣṇa  the two kinds of guru  devotees  avatāras  manifestations
potencies  in these  six  forms  He enjoys  pastimes
= The Lord enjoys His pastimes in six forms: as Kṛṣṇa Himself, as the two kinds of guru, as the devotees, as the avatāras, as His manifestations and as His potencies.

@33
of these  six  truths  I make  to the feet  worship
first  in general  I make  the invocation
= I worship the feet of these six truths. First I offer the invocation in a general way.

@34
I worship  the gurus  the Lord's devotees
the Lord  the Lord's avatāras
His manifestations  and  His potencies
Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  known as
= I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, the Lord’s avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies, and the Lord Himself, who is known as Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.

@35
the initiating guru  and  all  the instructing gurus
their  feet  first  I  worship
= First I worship the feet of my initiating guru and of all my instructing gurus.

@36
Śrī Rūpa  Sanātana  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha
Śrī Jīva  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa  Dāsa  Raghunātha
= Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha Dāsa —

@37
these  six  gurus  instructing gurus  who  mine
their  all  at the lotus feet  ten million  obeisances
= these six are my instructing gurus. I offer ten million obeisances at the lotus feet of them all.

@38
of the Lord  devotees  all  Śrīvāsa  the chief
their  at the lotus feet  thousands of  obeisances
= Śrīvāsa is the chief of all the devotees of the Lord. I offer thousands of obeisances at their lotus feet.

@39
Advaita  Ācārya  Lord Caitanya's  partial avatāra
His  at the lotus feet  ten million  obeisances  my
= Advaita Ācārya is a partial avatāra of the Lord. I offer ten million obeisances at His lotus feet.

@40
Nityānanda  Rāya  Lord Caitanya's  manifestation of His own form
His  lotus feet  I worship  whose  I  servant
= Nityānanda Rāya is the manifestation of the Lord’s own form (*svarūpa-prakāśa*). I worship the lotus feet of Him whose servant I am.

@41
Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others  Lord Caitanya's  own potencies
their  all  at the feet  my  thousands of  obeisances
= Gadādhara Paṇḍita and the others are the Lord’s own potencies. I offer thousands of obeisances at the feet of them all.

@42
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  the Lord  Himself  Bhagavān
His  at the lotus feet  endless  obeisances
= Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu is the Supreme Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*). I offer endless obeisances at His lotus feet.

@43
with His associates  to the Lord  having made  obeisance
these  six  He  how (is)  I make  consideration
= Having offered obeisances to the Lord together with His associates, I shall now consider how He is present in these six.

@44
although  my  guru  Caitanya's  servant
still  know  I  His  manifestation
= Although my guru is a servant of Caitanya, I know him to be His manifestation.

@45
the guru  Kṛṣṇa's form  is  of the scriptures  by the evidence
in the form of the guru  Kṛṣṇa  mercy  shows  to the devotees
= The scriptures testify that the guru is a form of Kṛṣṇa. In the form of the guru, Kṛṣṇa shows mercy to His devotees.

@46
the ācārya  as Me  one should know
should not disrespect  ever
not  thinking him mortal  should envy
comprising all the gods  the guru
= “One should know the ācārya as Me and never disrespect him. One should not envy him, thinking him an ordinary mortal, for the guru embodies all the gods.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.17.27)

@47
the instructing guru  indeed  I know  Kṛṣṇa's  own form
the indwelling Lord  the best of devotees  these  two  forms
= I know the instructing guru to be Kṛṣṇa’s own form. He appears in two forms: as the indwelling Supersoul and as the best of devotees.
