@title Rāmānanda Rāya Instructs Pradyumna Miśra

@1
by the ripening of karma  by the worm  bitten
of malice  by the sore  afflicted
in the ocean of humility  sunk  I
Caitanya  the physician  I take shelter of
= Bitten by the worm of ripening karma and afflicted by the sore of malice, I, sunk in an ocean of humility, take shelter of Caitanya, the physician.

@2
glory  glory  to the son of Śacī  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
glory  glory  to the all-merciful  Nityānanda  the blessed
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the son of Śacī! Glory, glory to the all-merciful, blessed Nityānanda!

@3
glory to Advaita  of mercy  the ocean  glory  to the devotees  all
glory  to Svarūpa  Gadādhara  Rūpa  Sanātana
= Glory to Advaita, the ocean of mercy! Glory to all the devotees! Glory to Svarūpa, Gadādhara, Rūpa and Sanātana!

@4
one day  Pradyumna Miśra  of the Lord  at the feet
obeisance  making  something  he makes  submission
= One day Pradyumna Miśra bowed down at the Lord’s feet and humbly made a request.

@5
hear  O Lord  I  wretched  householder  lowly
by what  fortune  I have obtained  Your  rare  feet
= “Hear me, O Lord: I am a wretched, lowly householder. By what fortune have I obtained Your feet, so rarely attained?

@6
about Kṛṣṇa talks  to hear  my  desire  is
about Kṛṣṇa talks  tell  me  becoming  kind
= I wish to hear talks about Kṛṣṇa. Be kind to me and tell me about Kṛṣṇa.”

@7
the Lord  says  about Kṛṣṇa talks  I  not  know
only  Rāmānanda  knows  his  from the mouth  I hear
= The Lord said, “I do not know talks about Kṛṣṇa. Only Rāmānanda knows them; I hear them from his lips.

@8
by fortune  your  about Kṛṣṇa talks  to hear  is  the mind
to Rāmānanda  going  do  hearing
= It is your good fortune that your mind longs to hear about Kṛṣṇa. Go to Rāmānanda and hear from him.

@9
for talks about Kṛṣṇa  taste  your  very  fortunate
whose  for talks about Kṛṣṇa  taste  he  fortunate
= You have a taste for talks about Kṛṣṇa — you are very fortunate. One who has a taste for talks about Kṛṣṇa is truly fortunate.”

@10
dharma  well performed  of people
of Viṣvaksena  for the narrations  which
not  awakens  if  attraction
labor  only  certainly  merely
= “The *dharma* a person performs, however well, is certainly nothing but wasted labor if it does not awaken attraction (*rati*) for the narrations about Viṣvaksena, the Lord.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.8)

@11
then  Pradyumna Miśra  went  Rāmānanda’s  to the place
Rāya’s  servant  him  seated  on a seat
= Then Pradyumna Miśra went to Rāmānanda’s house, and Rāya’s servant offered him a seat.

@12
Rāya’s  sight  not  getting  the servant  he asked
Rāya’s  doings  the servant  to tell  began
= Not finding Rāya there, he asked the servant, and the servant told him what Rāya was doing:

@13
two  maidens of the Deity  there are  supremely beautiful
in dance and song  very skilled  in age  in early youth
= “There are two *deva-kanyā*s, maidens dedicated to the service of the Deity, supremely beautiful, very skilled in dance and song, and in their early youth.

@14
those  two  taking  Rāya  in a secluded  garden
of his own drama’s songs  he teaches  dancing
= Rāya has taken them both to a secluded garden and is teaching them to dance to the songs of his own drama.

@15
you  here  sitting  stay  in a moment  he will come
to him  whatever  order  you give  that  he will do
= Please sit here and wait; he will come shortly. Whatever you ask of him, he will do.”

@16
then  Pradyumna Miśra  there  remained  sitting
Rāmānanda  in seclusion  those  two  taking
= So Pradyumna Miśra stayed there, waiting, while Rāmānanda was in a secluded place with the two maidens.

@17
with his own hands  he does  their  anointing with oil
with his own hands  he gives  a bath  the body  cleansing
= With his own hands he anointed them with oil; with his own hands he bathed them and cleansed their bodies.

@18
with his own hands  he puts on  clothes  every limb  adorning
yet  unmoved  of Rāya Rāmānanda  the mind
= With his own hands he dressed them and adorned every limb, and yet the mind of Rāya Rāmānanda remained unmoved.

@19
wood and stone  at touching  there is  as  feeling
a young woman  at touching  Rāmānanda’s  so  nature
= As one feels when touching wood or stone, so is Rāmānanda’s nature when he touches a young woman.

@20
the sense of the one to be served  attributing  he does  service
natural  maidservant’s mood  he does  taking upon himself
= Regarding them as the ones to be served (*sevya-buddhi*), he serves them, and he takes upon himself his own natural mood of a maidservant.

@21
of Mahāprabhu  of the devotees  unfathomable  glory
among them  Rāmānanda’s  of bhāva, bhakti and prema  the limit
= The glory of Mahāprabhu’s devotees is unfathomable, and among them Rāmānanda is the utmost limit of *bhāva*, *bhakti* and *prema*.

@22
then  those  two  dance  he taught
of the songs  hidden  meaning  acting  he made them do
= Then he taught the two to dance and had them act out the hidden meaning of the songs.

@23
transitory  sāttvika  of the abiding emotions  the signs
in face and eyes  acting  they make  manifest
= Through acting with the face and eyes they made manifest the signs of the transitory (*sañcārī*), the *sāttvika* and the abiding (*sthāyi*) emotions.

@24
bhāva-revealing graceful dance  Rāya  which  teaches
of Jagannātha  before  the two  openly  show
= The graceful dance that reveals *bhāva*, which Rāya teaches them, the two display openly before Jagannātha.

@25
then  those  two  prasāda  he fed
quietly  both  to their own home  he sent
= Then he fed the two *prasāda* and quietly sent them home.

@26
every day  Rāya  thus  makes them do  practice
who  knows  petty  living being  where  his  mind
= Every day Rāya trained them in this way. What petty soul can know where his mind dwells?

@27
Miśra’s  arrival  to Rāya  the servant  told
quickly  Rāmānanda  then  to the hall  came
= The servant told Rāya that Miśra had come, and Rāmānanda at once came to the hall.

@28
to Miśra  obeisance  he makes  respect  showing
submission  he makes  something  humble  becoming
= Showing him respect, he bowed to Miśra and humbly said:

@29
long ago  you came  me  anyone  not  told
at your  feet  my  offense  has been
= “You came long ago, and no one told me. I have committed an offense at your feet.

@30
by your  coming  my  pure  became  home
order  do  what  shall I do  your  servant
= Your coming has purified my home. Command me — what shall I, your servant, do?”

@31
Miśra  says  you  to see  there was  coming
myself  pure  I made  by your  sight
= Miśra said, “I came to see you, and by seeing you I have purified myself.”

@32
the late hour  seeing  Miśra  anything  not  said
leave  taking  Miśra  to his home  went
= Seeing that it was too late, Miśra said nothing more; he took his leave and went home.

@33
the next  day  Miśra  came  into the Lord’s presence
the Lord  says  about Kṛṣṇa talks  did you hear  from Rāya
= The next day Miśra came before the Lord, and the Lord asked, “Did you hear about Kṛṣṇa from Rāya?”

@34
then  Miśra  Rāmānanda’s  doings  told
hearing  Mahāprabhu  then  to speak  began
= Then Miśra told Him what Rāmānanda had been doing, and when Mahāprabhu heard it, He began to speak.

@35-36
I  indeed  a sannyāsī  Myself  detached  as  I consider
seeing  let it remain  far  of a woman  the name  if  I hear
even then  agitation  gets  My  body and mind
a woman  on seeing  steady  is  what  person
= “I am a *sannyāsī* and consider Myself detached; yet, to say nothing of seeing a woman, if I merely hear a woman’s name, My body and mind are disturbed. Who can remain steady on seeing a woman?

@37
of Rāmānanda Rāya  the account  hear  all people
to be told  it is not  which  a wondrous account
= Hear, all of you, about Rāmānanda Rāya — an account so wondrous that it can hardly be told.

@38
first  devadāsīs  and  beautiful  young women
for them  all  service  he does  himself
= First, they are *devadāsī*s, maidservants of the Deity, and moreover they are beautiful young women; yet he himself performs every service for them.

@39
bathing and so on  he has them do  he puts on  clothes and ornaments
private  parts  all  of them  seeing and touching
= He bathes them, dresses them in clothes and ornaments, and sees and touches all the private parts of their bodies.

@40
yet  unmoved  of Rāya Rāmānanda  the mind
the rising of various bhāvas  them  he makes  learn
= Yet the mind of Rāya Rāmānanda remains unmoved as he teaches them to express the rising of many different *bhāva*s.

@41
unmoved  body and mind  wood and stone  like
wonderful  a young woman  at touching  unmoved  mind
= His body and mind are unmoved, like wood or stone. How wonderful — at the touch of a young woman his mind is unmoved!

@42
alone  of Rāmānanda  is  this  qualification
from that  I know  transcendental  body  his
= Rāmānanda alone has this qualification; from it I know that his body is transcendental.

@43
his  mind’s  state  he  knows  only
that  to know  other  second  there is not  fit person
= Only he knows the state of his mind; there is no one else fit to know it.

@44
but  of scripture  by the view  I make  one  inference
the Śrī Bhāgavata scripture  of that  the evidence
= But in the light of scripture I draw one inference, and the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* bears witness to it.

@45-46
with the young women of Vraja  Kṛṣṇa’s  the rāsa and other pastimes
which  person  tells  hears  having  faith
the heart’s disease, lust  his  at once  is  destroyed
of the three guṇas  agitation  is not  very sober  he becomes
= Whoever, with faith, tells of or hears about Kṛṣṇa’s *rāsa* dance and other pastimes with the young women of Vraja — his lust, the disease of the heart, is destroyed at once. The three *guṇas* no longer agitate him, and he becomes ‘deeply sober’ (*mahā-dhīra*).

@47
radiant  of madhura-rasa  prema-bhakti  he attains
in bliss  in Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  he sports  always
= He attains *prema-bhakti* in the radiant *madhura-rasa* and always sports in bliss in Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness.

@48
the play  with the young women of Vraja  this  and  of Viṣṇu
endowed with faith  constantly hears  then  describes  who
devotion  supreme  for the Lord  having attained  lust
the heart’s disease  quickly  casts off  soon  sober
= “One who, endowed with faith, constantly hears about this play of Viṣṇu with the young women of Vraja and then describes it, soon attains supreme devotion to the Lord and, becoming sober, quickly casts off lust, the disease of the heart.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.39)

@49
who  hears  who  reads  his  fruit  is such
he  absorbed in bhāva  who  serves  day and night
= Whoever hears it or reads it gains such a fruit. And one who serves day and night is absorbed in *bhāva*;

@50
his  fruit  what  shall I tell  in words  not  it goes
eternally perfect  he  almost perfect  his  body
= what can I say of his fruit? It cannot be put into words. He is eternally perfect (*nitya-siddha*), and his body is almost perfect (*prāya-siddha*).

@51
on the rāgānuga path  I know  Rāya’s  worship
to a perfected body  equal  therefore  mundane  is not  the mind
= I know that Rāya worships on the path of *rāgānuga*. His body is like a perfected body (*siddha-deha*), and so his mind is not mundane.

@52
I too  Rāya’s  at the place  hear  talks about Kṛṣṇa
to hear  desire  is  if  again  go  there
= I too hear about Kṛṣṇa from Rāya. If you wish to hear, go there again.

@53
in My  name  say  He  sent  me
your  at the place  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  in order
= Tell him in My name: ‘He has sent me to hear about Kṛṣṇa from you.’

@54
quickly  go  while  he  is  in the hall
this  hearing  Pradyumna Miśra  went  in haste
= Go quickly, while he is still in the hall.” Hearing this, Pradyumna Miśra set off in haste.

@55
to Rāya  he went  Rāya  obeisance  made
order  do  what  for  coming  has been
= He went to Rāya, and Rāya bowed to him and said, “Command me; tell me why you have come.”

@56
Miśra  says  Mahāprabhu  sent  me
your  at the place  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  in order
= Miśra said, “Mahāprabhu has sent me to hear about Kṛṣṇa from you.”

@57
hearing  Rāmānanda  in mind  became  pleased
to speak  began  something  of the mind  in joy
= Hearing this, Rāmānanda was pleased at heart, and with joy in his heart he said:

@58
of the Lord  by the order  talks about Kṛṣṇa  to hear  you came  here
this  besides  great fortune  I  shall find  where
= “By the Lord’s order you have come here to hear about Kṛṣṇa. Where else could I find such great fortune?”

@59
this  saying  him  taking  in seclusion  he sat
what  topic  to hear  do you want  Miśra  he asked
= Saying this, he took him to a secluded place, sat down with him and asked Miśra, “What would you like to hear?”

@60
he  says  what  you told  in Vidyānagara
those  topics  in order  you  please tell  me
= He said, “What you told in Vidyānagara — please tell me those things, in their order.

@61
of others  what  talk  you  of the Lord  the instructor
I  indeed  a mendicant  brāhmaṇa  you  my  sustainer
= What to speak of others — you are the instructor of the Lord! I am only a mendicant *brāhmaṇa*, and you are my sustainer.

@62
good  bad  anything  I  to ask  not  know
wretched  seeing  mercy  showing  please tell  yourself
= Good or bad, I do not know how to ask anything. Seeing how wretched I am, be merciful and tell me yourself.”

@63
then  Rāmānanda  in order  to speak  began
the ocean of the nectar of rasa in talks about Kṛṣṇa  surged
= Then Rāmānanda began to speak, step by step, and the ocean of the nectar of *rasa* in talks about Kṛṣṇa surged up.

@64
himself  question  raising  afterward  he makes  conclusion
the third  prahara  came  there is not  of the talk  end
= He himself raised the questions and then gave the conclusions. The third watch (*prahara*) of the day came, and still the talk did not end.

@65
the speaker  the listener  speaks  hears  both  in the absorption of prema
self-awareness  there is not  how  they know  of the day  the end
= Speaker and listener, one speaking and the other hearing, were both absorbed in *prema*. They had no awareness of themselves — how could they know the day had ended?

@66
the servant  said  the day  has come  to an end
then  Rāya  to the talk about Kṛṣṇa  made  a pause
= When the servant said, “The day is over,” Rāya brought the talk about Kṛṣṇa to a pause.

@67
great respect  showing  to Miśra  leave  he gave
fulfilled  I have become  saying  Miśra  to dance  began
= With great respect he bade Miśra farewell, and Miśra, crying, “My life is fulfilled!” began to dance.

@68
home  going  Miśra  did  bathing  eating
in the evening  to see  he came  of the Lord  the feet
= Miśra went home, bathed and took his meal, and in the evening he came to see the Lord’s feet.

@69
the Lord’s  feet  he worships  with a joyful mind
the Lord  says  talks about Kṛṣṇa  was there  hearing
= With a joyful heart he bowed at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord asked, “Did you hear about Kṛṣṇa?”

@70
Miśra  says  O Lord  me  fulfilled  You have made
in the ocean of the nectar of talks about Kṛṣṇa  me  You have plunged
= Miśra said, “O Lord, You have fulfilled my life! You have plunged me into the ocean of the nectar of talks about Kṛṣṇa.

@71
of Rāmānanda Rāya  the words  if told  not  it is possible
a human being  is not  Rāya  made of the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa
= The words of Rāmānanda Rāya cannot be retold. Rāya is not a mere human being; he is made of the *rasa* of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

@72
another  one  thing  Rāya  told  me
of talks about Kṛṣṇa  the speaker  as  not  consider  me
= Rāya told me one thing more: ‘Do not think of me as the speaker of these talks about Kṛṣṇa.

@73
by my  mouth  words  speaks  Himself  Gauracandra
as  He makes me speak  so  I speak  like  a vīṇā instrument
= Gauracandra Himself speaks these words through my mouth. As He makes me speak, so I speak, like a *vīṇā*.

@74
by my  mouth  the words  He  makes  known
in the world  who  will know  this pastime  His
= Through my mouth He makes these words known. Who in this world will understand this pastime of His?’

@75
all that  I heard  Kṛṣṇa  of rasa  the ocean
to Brahmā and the other gods  this  all  not  is  perceptible
= All that I heard — that Kṛṣṇa is the ocean of *rasa* — lies beyond the reach even of Brahmā and the other gods.

@76
such  rasa  me  to drink  made  You
birth  after birth  Your  at the feet  sold myself  I
= You have made me drink such *rasa*; birth after birth I have sold myself at Your feet.”

@77
the Lord  says  Rāmānanda  of humility  a mine
his own  words  on another’s head  he places  bringing
= The Lord said, “Rāmānanda is a mine of humility. He takes his own words and places them on another’s head.

@78
of great souls  this  natural  disposition  is
their own  qualities  not  themselves  they tell
= This is the natural disposition of great souls: they never speak of their own qualities.”

@79
of Rāmānanda Rāya  this  I have told  of qualities  a trace
to Pradyumna Miśra  how  he gave  instruction
= Thus I have told a trace of Rāmānanda Rāya’s qualities: how he instructed Pradyumna Miśra.

@80
a householder  being  not  Rāya  of the six enemies  under the control
a worldly man  being  sannyāsīs  he instructs
= Though a householder, Rāya is not under the control of the six enemies (*ṣaḍ-varga*); though seemingly a worldly man (*viṣayī*), he instructs *sannyāsīs*.

@81
all these  qualities  his  manifest  to make
Miśra  He sent  there  hearing  to do
= To reveal all these qualities of his, the Lord sent Miśra there to hear.

@82
the devotee’s qualities  to reveal  the Lord  well  knows
in various ways  revealing  His own  gain  He considers
= The Lord knows well how to reveal the qualities of His devotees; revealing them in various ways, He considers it His own gain.
