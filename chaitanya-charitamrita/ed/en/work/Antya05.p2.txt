@83

@84

= To destroy the pride of the *sannyāsīs* and *paṇḍitas*, He makes *dharma* manifest through a lowly *śūdra*.

Himself  with Pradyumna Miśra  becomes  ‘the listener’
= He speaks of *bhakti*, *prema* and *tattva*, making Rāya the speaker, while He Himself, together with Pradyumna Miśra, becomes the listener.

@86

= Through Haridāsa He revealed the glory of the holy name; through Sanātana, the play of the conclusions of *bhakti*.

@87

= Through Śrī Rūpa He revealed Vraja’s pastimes of *rasa* and *prema*. Who can describe the profound play of Caitanya?

@88

the world  to flood  can  whose  one  drop
= These pastimes of Śrī Caitanya are an ocean of nectar, a single drop of which can flood the world.

@89

from which  ‘the bliss of prema’  ‘knowledge of the truth of bhakti’
= Drink the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* constantly, for from it come the bliss of *prema* and knowledge of the truth of *bhakti*.

@90

= In this way Mahāprabhu, together with His devotees, enjoyed His pastimes in Nīlācala, preaching *bhakti*.

@91

@92

= He was acquainted with Bhagavān Ācārya; having met him, he took lodging in his house.

@93

= First he recited the drama to him, and many Vaiṣṇavas heard it along with him.

@94

to Mahāprabhu  to recite  of all  arose  the wish
= Everyone praised the drama as “most excellent,” and all wished to have Mahāprabhu hear it.

@95

= Whatever song, verse, book or poem anyone composed and brought, he would first recite it before Svarūpa.

@96

= If it passed before Svarūpa and his mind accepted it, only then would it be recited for Mahāprabhu to hear.

@97

= If there was *rasābhāsa*, a mere semblance of *rasa*, or anything opposed to *siddhānta*, the Lord could not bear it, and anger arose in His mind.

@98

this  rule of propriety  the Lord  has made  as a fixed rule
= Therefore the Lord would not hear anything first; He had made this rule of propriety a fixed principle.

@99

= Bhagavān Ācārya made a request to Svarūpa: “A brāhmaṇa has composed an excellent drama about the Lord.

@100

@101

= Svarūpa said, “You are a ‘cowherd’ (*gopa*), most generous: a desire arises in you to hear any scripture whatever.

@102

= In the words of just any poet there is *rasābhāsa*, and hearing what is opposed to *siddhānta* brings no joy.

@103

of the conclusions of bhakti  the ocean  not  reaches  the shore
= One who cannot discriminate between *rasa* and *rasābhāsa* never reaches the far shore of the ocean of *bhakti-siddhānta*.

@104-105

= One who knows neither grammar nor rhetoric and has no knowledge of the ornaments of drama — such a worthless one does not know how to describe Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, and this play of Caitanya is especially hard to approach.

@106

@108

to hear  joy  increases  whose  in the prologue
= Take the two dramas Rūpa has composed: on hearing their very opening, the prologue, one’s joy increases.”

@109

= Bhagavān Ācārya said, “Just hear it once. When you have heard it, you will be able to judge whether it is good or bad.”

@110

@112
