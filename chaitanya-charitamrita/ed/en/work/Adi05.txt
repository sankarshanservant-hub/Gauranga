@title The Divine Majesty of Śrī Nityānanda

@1
I worship  unlimited  wonderful  possessing opulence
Śrī Nityānanda  the Lord
by whose will  His  true nature
by an ignorant person  even  is ascertained
= I worship Śrī Nityānanda, the Lord, whose opulence is unlimited and wonderful. By His will even an ignorant person can come to understand His true nature.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host
= All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in these  six verses  I have told  of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the glory
in five verses  I tell  of Nityānanda  of the truth  the full extent
= In these six verses I have told the glory of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. In five verses I now tell the truth about Nityānanda in its full extent.

@4
the source of all avatāras  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān
His  second  body  Śrī Balarāma
= Kṛṣṇa, the source of all avatāras, is the Supreme Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*). Śrī Balarāma is His second body.

@5
one and the same  nature  of both  different only  the bodies
the first  expansion of the body  of Kṛṣṇa's pastimes  the helper
= Both are one and the same in nature; only Their bodies differ. Balarāma is the first expansion of Kṛṣṇa's body (*kāya-vyūha*) and His helper in His pastimes.

@6
that  Kṛṣṇa  in Navadvīpa  Śrī Caitanya-candra
that  Balarāma  with Him  Śrī Nityānanda
= That Kṛṣṇa is Śrī Caitanya-candra in Navadvīpa, and that Balarāma is Śrī Nityānanda, who is always with Him.

@7
Saṅkarṣaṇa  lying in the Causal Ocean
lying in the Garbha Ocean  and  lying in the Ocean of Milk
Śeṣa  and  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-
-nanda called  Rāma  shelter  my  may be
= “Saṅkarṣaṇa, Kāraṇodakaśāyī, Garbhodakaśāyī, Kṣīrodakaśāyī and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions. May that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my shelter.” (Svarūpa Dāmodara’s diary)

@8
Śrī Balarāma  Gosāñi  the original  Saṅkarṣaṇa
five  forms  assuming  He renders  to Kṛṣṇa  service
= Śrī Balarāma Gosāñi is the original Saṅkarṣaṇa. Assuming five forms, He serves Kṛṣṇa.

@9
personally  He renders  to Kṛṣṇa's pastimes  help
of the pastime of creation  the work  He does  assuming  four  bodies
= In His own person He assists Kṛṣṇa in His pastimes, and assuming four other forms, He carries out the work of the pastime of creation.

@10
creation and the rest  service  His  of the order  the carrying out
of Śeṣa  in the form  He renders  to Kṛṣṇa  various  service
= Creation and the rest are His service: in them He carries out Kṛṣṇa's order. And in the form of Śeṣa He serves Kṛṣṇa in many ways.

@11
in all forms  He relishes  of serving Kṛṣṇa  the bliss
that  Balarāma  with Gaura  Nityānanda
= In all these forms He relishes the bliss of serving Kṛṣṇa. That same Balarāma is Nityānanda, the companion of Gaura.

@12
of the seventh  verse  the meaning  I have made  four verses
by which  of Nityānanda  the truth  knows  the whole world
= I have explained the meaning of the seventh verse in four verses, so that the whole world may know the truth about Nityānanda.

@13
beyond māyā  in the all-pervading  Vaikuṇṭha  realm
full of opulence  divine  four vyūhas  among
the form  whose  shines forth  called Saṅkarṣaṇa
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
= “Beyond māyā lies the all-pervading realm of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence. There, among the four divine *vyūhas*, His form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines forth. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.” (Svarūpa Dāmodara’s diary)

@14
of material nature  beyond  Paravyoma  named  an abode
Kṛṣṇa's  form  like  with opulence  and other  qualities  endowed
= Beyond material nature lies the abode called Paravyoma, the spiritual sky. Like Kṛṣṇa's own form, it is endowed with opulence and every other excellence.

@15
all-pervading  unlimited  the supreme  Vaikuṇṭha  and other  abodes
of Kṛṣṇa  of Kṛṣṇa's avatāras  there  the resting place
= The supreme Vaikuṇṭha and the other abodes there are all-pervading and unlimited. In them Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras reside.

@16
its  in the upper  part  Kṛṣṇaloka  is renowned
Dvārakā  Mathurā  Gokula  in three divisions  exists
= In its uppermost region is the realm renowned as Kṛṣṇaloka. It exists in three divisions: Dvārakā, Mathurā and Gokula.

@17
above all  Śrī Gokula  of Vraja  the abode
Śrī Goloka  Śvetadvīpa  Vṛndāvana  named
= Highest of all is Śrī Gokula, the abode of Vraja. It is also called Śrī Goloka, Śvetadvīpa and Vṛndāvana.

@18
all-pervading  unlimited  vast  to Kṛṣṇa's body  equal
above and below  it extends  there is not  its  limit
= Like Kṛṣṇa's own body, it is all-pervading, unlimited and vast. It extends above and below, and it has no limit.

@19
in the universe  the manifestation  its  by Kṛṣṇa's  will
one and the same  nature  its  there are not  two  bodies
= By Kṛṣṇa's will it is manifested within the material universe as well. Yet its nature is one and the same; it does not have two bodies.

@20
of touchstone  the ground  of wish-fulfilling trees  full  forests
with fleshly  eyes  one sees  it  to the material world  similar
= Its ground is touchstone (*cintāmaṇi*), and its forests are full of wish-fulfilling trees. But fleshly eyes see it as no different from the material world.

@21
with eyes of love  one sees  its  true form  the manifestation
with the cowherd boys and girls  together  where  Kṛṣṇa's  pastimes
= Only with eyes of love does one see it as it truly is — the place where Kṛṣṇa enjoys His pastimes with the cowherd boys and girls.

@22
of touchstones  heaps  in abodes  wish-fulfilling trees
by millions surrounded  the surabhi cows  tending
by hundreds of thousands of Lakṣmīs  with reverence  served
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “I worship Govinda, the original Person, who tends the *surabhi* cows in abodes built of countless touchstones and surrounded by millions of wish-fulfilling trees. Hundreds of thousands of goddesses of fortune serve Him with loving reverence.” (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.29)

@23
in Mathurā and Dvārakā  His own  form  manifesting
in many  forms  He enjoys pastimes  the catur-vyūha  becoming
= In Mathurā and Dvārakā He manifests His own form. Becoming the four *vyūhas* (*catur-vyūha*), He enjoys pastimes there in many forms.

@24
Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha
of all catur-vyūhas  the source  transcendent  pure
= Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha are the source of all other *catur-vyūhas*. They are transcendent (*turīya*) and pure.

@25
in these  three  abodes  Kṛṣṇa  solely  full of pastimes
His own  associates  taking  He plays  endless  time
= In these three abodes Kṛṣṇa is wholly absorbed in pastimes. With His own associates He plays there eternally.

@26
of the spiritual sky  within  making  of His form  manifestation
of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  He performs  various  pastimes
= In the spiritual sky He manifests His own form as Nārāyaṇa and enjoys pastimes of many kinds.

@27
the original form  of Kṛṣṇa  only  two-armed
of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  that  body  four-armed
= Kṛṣṇa's original form has only two arms; in the form of Nārāyaṇa that same body has four.

@28
conch  disc  club  lotus  full of great opulence
Śrī  Bhū  Nīlā  potencies  whose  feet  serve
= He holds the conch, disc, club and lotus, and He is full of supreme opulence. His potencies Śrī, Bhū and Nīlā serve His feet.

@29
although  only  His  pastime  only  nature
still  the souls  out of mercy  He does  one  work
= Although His nature is to engage in pastimes alone, out of mercy He does one thing for the souls:

@30
sālokya  sāmīpya  sārṣṭi  sārūpya  kinds
four  liberations  giving  He does  the souls  deliverance
= He delivers them by granting four kinds of liberation: *sālokya* (living in His abode), *sāmīpya* (nearness to Him), *sārṣṭi* (opulence like His) and *sārūpya* (a form like His).

@31
of those liberated by merging into Brahman  there  there is not  access
outside Vaikuṇṭha  is  of all of them  the residence
= Those who attain liberation by merging into Brahman (*sāyujya*) have no access there; they remain outside Vaikuṇṭha.

@32
outside Vaikuṇṭha  one  effulgent  region
of Kṛṣṇa's  body  the radiance  supremely  brilliant
= Outside Vaikuṇṭha is a region of light. It is the radiance of Kṛṣṇa's body, supremely brilliant.

@33
Siddhaloka  the name  its  of material nature  beyond
spiritual in nature  there  there are not  of the spiritual potency  transformations
= It is called Siddhaloka, and it lies beyond material nature. It is spiritual in nature, but the spiritual potency displays no variety there.

@34
the sun's  disc  as  outside  without variety
within  of the sun  the chariot  and so on  with variety
= It is like the disc of the sun: from outside the sun appears featureless, but within it there is variety — the sun-god's chariot and all the rest.

@35
from lust  from hatred  from fear  from affection
as  through devotion  on the Lord  the mind
having fixed  that  sin  having given up
many  that  goal  have attained
= “As one reaches Him by fixing the mind on the Lord with devotion, so many have fixed their minds on Him through lust, hatred, fear or affection, cast off their sin and reached that same goal.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 7.1.30)

@36
that which  of the enemies  of the beloved  and
to be attained  one  as if  is said
that  of Brahman and Kṛṣṇa  because of the oneness
the rays and the sun  resembling
= “It is said that the goal of the Lord's enemies and of His beloved devotees is as if one and the same. This is because Brahman and Kṛṣṇa are one, as the rays and the sun are one.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 1.2.278)

@37
likewise  in the spiritual sky  various  of the spiritual potency  pastimes
without variety  of light  the sphere  outside  is manifest
= In the same way, within the spiritual sky the spiritual potency displays pastimes of every variety, while outside it a featureless sphere of light is manifest.

@38
featureless  Brahman  that  only  made of light
of sāyujya  those qualified  there  attain  absorption
= That featureless Brahman is nothing but light. Those who are fit for *sāyujya* attain absorption in it.

@39
Siddhaloka  but  of darkness
beyond  where  reside  indeed
the perfected ones  in the bliss of Brahman  immersed
the demons  and  by Hari  slain
= “Beyond darkness lies Siddhaloka. There reside the perfected beings (*siddhas*), immersed in the bliss of Brahman, and the demons slain by Hari.” (Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa)

@40
in that  spiritual sky  of Nārāyaṇa  on four  sides
of Dvārakā  the catur-vyūha  in a second  manifestation
= In that spiritual sky, on Nārāyaṇa's four sides, the *catur-vyūha* of Dvārakā is present in a second manifestation.

@41
Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha
the second catur-vyūha  this  transcendent  pure
= Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha — this is the second *catur-vyūha*. They too are transcendent and pure.

@42
there  which  of Rāma  the form  Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa
of the spiritual potency  the shelter  He  of causes  the cause
= Rāma's form there is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa. He is the shelter of the spiritual potency and the cause of all causes.

@43
of the spiritual potency  a display  one  śuddha-sattva  named
of śuddha-sattva  made  all  Vaikuṇṭha and the other  abodes
= One display of the spiritual potency is called *śuddha-sattva*, pure being. All the abodes, Vaikuṇṭha and the rest, are made of *śuddha-sattva*.

@44
six kinds of  opulence  there  all  spiritual
of Saṅkarṣaṇa  the glory  all  know  for certain
= The six kinds of opulence there are all spiritual. Know for certain that all of them are the glory of Saṅkarṣaṇa.

@45
jīva  named  called marginal  one  potency  there is
Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  of all  souls  the shelter
= There is one potency called marginal (*taṭasthā*); it is named *jīva*. Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of all the souls.

@46
from whom  the origin of the universe  in whom  dissolution
of that  Puruṣa  Saṅkarṣaṇa  the shelter
= Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of that Puruṣa from whom the universe arises and into whom it dissolves.

@47
the shelter of all  wonderful in every way  opulence  boundless
Ananta  to describe  cannot  the glory  whose
= He is the shelter of all and wonderful in every way, and His opulence is boundless. Even Ananta cannot describe His glory.

@48
transcendent  of pure being  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named
He  whose  portion  that  Nityānanda-Rāma
= That transcendent One, made of pure being and named Saṅkarṣaṇa, is a portion of Nityānanda-Rāma.

@49
of the eighth  verse  I have made  brief  explanation
of the ninth  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  your mind
= I have briefly explained the eighth verse. Now hear attentively the meaning of the ninth.

@50
the master of māyā  whose body shelters the multitude of universes
lies  in person  in the midst of the Causal Ocean
whose  one portion  the blessed Puruṣa  the original Lord
to Him  Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
= “The blessed Puruṣa, the original Lord and the master of māyā, whose body shelters the multitude of universes, lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean. He is but one portion of Nityānanda. I surrender to that Śrī Nityānanda-Rāma.” (Svarūpa Dāmodara’s diary)

@51
outside Vaikuṇṭha  which  effulgent  abode
of it  outside  Kāraṇārṇava  named
= Outside Vaikuṇṭha lies the abode of light, and beyond it lies what is called the Kāraṇārṇava, the Causal Ocean.

@52
Vaikuṇṭha  surrounding  one  there is  ocean
endless  shoreless  its  there is not  limit
= Surrounding Vaikuṇṭha is an ocean, endless and shoreless, without any limit.

@53
of Vaikuṇṭha  earth and the rest  all  spiritual
of material  elements  there  birth  not  is
= In Vaikuṇṭha the earth and all the other elements are spiritual. No material element ever arises there.

@54
spiritual  water  that  the supreme  cause
of which  one  drop  the Gaṅgā  of the fallen  the purifier
= That spiritual water is the supreme cause. The Gaṅgā, purifier of the fallen, is a single drop of it.

@55
in that  very  Causal Ocean  that  Saṅkarṣaṇa
His own  in one  portion  does  lying down
= In that Causal Ocean, the same Saṅkarṣaṇa lies in one of His portions.

@56
of the mahat  the creator  the Puruṣa  He  of the world  the cause
the first  avatāra  does  of māyā  seeing
= He is the Puruṣa, the creator of the *mahat-tattva* and the cause of the world. This first avatāra casts His glance upon māyā.

@57
the potency māyā  remains  of the Causal Ocean  outside
the Causal  Ocean  māyā  to touch  cannot
= The potency called māyā remains outside the Causal Ocean; māyā cannot touch its waters.

@58
of that  very  māyā  two kinds of  states
of the world  the ingredient  pradhāna  prakṛti
= That māyā exists in two aspects. As the material ingredient of the world, she is called *pradhāna* or *prakṛti*.

@59
of the world  the cause  is not  prakṛti  inert by nature
potency  infusing  to her  Kṛṣṇa  shows  mercy
= Prakṛti, inert by nature, cannot be the cause of the world. Kṛṣṇa shows her mercy by infusing her with His potency.

@60
by Kṛṣṇa's  potency  prakṛti  becomes  secondary  cause
by the power of fire  iron  as  does  burning
= By Kṛṣṇa's potency prakṛti becomes a secondary cause, just as iron burns by the power of fire.

@61
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  the original  of the world  cause
prakṛti  a cause  like  of a goat  on the neck  the nipples
= Therefore Kṛṣṇa is the original cause of the world. Prakṛti is a cause only in the way the nipples on a goat's neck are nipples.

@62
in the aspect of māyā  I call  her  the efficient  cause
that too  is not  because  the agent  cause  Nārāyaṇa
= In her aspect as māyā she is called the efficient cause. But she is not that either, for the agent who acts as the cause is Nārāyaṇa.

@63
of the pot  the efficient  cause  as  the potter
so  of the world  the maker  the Puruṣa-avatāra
= As the potter is the efficient cause of a pot, so the Puruṣa-avatāra is the maker of the world.

@64
Kṛṣṇa  the maker  māyā  to Him  renders  help
of the pot  the cause  wheel  rod and so on  instruments
= Kṛṣṇa is the maker, and māyā merely assists Him, as the wheel, the rod and the other instruments assist in making a pot.

@65
from afar  the Puruṣa  does  upon māyā  glancing
of souls  in the form  seed  into her  He does  placing
= From afar the Puruṣa glances at māyā and places in her the seed in the form of the souls.

@66
with one  reflection of His body  He does  with māyā  contact
from māyā  are born  then  of universes  multitudes
= Only a reflection of His body comes into contact with māyā, and from māyā multitudes of universes are then born.

@67
countless  unlimited  as many as  of universes  clusters
in as many  forms  the Puruṣa  does  into all  entering
= However many universes there are, countless and unlimited, in so many forms the Puruṣa enters them all.

@68
from the Puruṣa's nostrils  when  comes out  the breath
the exhalation  with  there is  of the universes  manifestation
= When the breath goes out from the Puruṣa's nostrils, the universes become manifest with His exhalation.

@69
again  the breath  when  enters  within
with the breath  the universes  enter  into the Puruṣa's body
= And when the breath enters Him again, the universes enter the Puruṣa's body with it.

@70
of a window  through the openings  as  specks of dust  move
of the Puruṣa  in the pores of the skin  of universes  swarms
= As specks of dust move through the openings of a lattice window, so swarms of universes move through the pores of the Puruṣa's skin.

@71
whose  one  exhalation  the duration  then  depending on
live  born from the pores of the skin  of the universes  the lords
Viṣṇu  great  He  here  whose  part  a particular
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “The lords of the universes, born from the pores of His skin, live only for the duration of one of His exhalations. He is Mahā-Viṣṇu, and He is a particular part of Govinda, the original Person, whom I worship.” (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.48)

@72
where  I  by darkness  the mahat  false ego  ether  air  fire  water  earth
enclosed  of a universe  in the pot  seven  spans  having a body
where  such  countless  universes  like specks of dust  the movement
like air-holes of a window  the pores of whose skin  and  Your  greatness
= “What am I, with a body seven spans tall, inside the pot of a universe enclosed by darkness, the *mahat*, false ego, ether, air, fire, water and earth? And what is Your greatness? Through the pores of Your skin countless such universes move like specks of dust through the openings of a window.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.11)

@73
of a portion  the portion  which  kalā  its  name
of Govinda  the counterpart  Śrī Balarāma
= A portion of a portion is called a *kalā*. Śrī Balarāma is Govinda's counterpart.

@74
His  one  form  Śrī Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa
His  portion  the Puruṣa  is  among the kalās  counted
= One of His forms is Śrī Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa, and His portion, the Puruṣa, is counted as a *kalā*.

@75
whom  indeed  kalā  I call  He  Mahā-Viṣṇu
the great Puruṣa  source of avatāras  He  all-conquering
= The one I call a *kalā* is Mahā-Viṣṇu. He is the great Puruṣa, the source of avatāras, victorious over all.

@76
lying in the Garbha and Milk Oceans  both  Puruṣa  named
those  two  whose  portions  Viṣṇu  the abode of the universe
= Garbhodaśāyī and Kṣīrodaśāyī are both called Puruṣas. Those two are portions of Him — Viṣṇu, the abode of the universes.

@77
of Viṣṇu  indeed  three  forms
called Puruṣa  now  they know
one  indeed  of the mahat  the creator
the second  and  within the universe  situated
the third  in all beings  situated
them  knowing  one is liberated
= “Viṣṇu has three forms, known as the Puruṣas. The first creates the *mahat-tattva*, the second lies within the universe, and the third dwells in all beings. One who knows them is liberated.” (Sātvata-tantra; Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, Pūrva-khaṇḍa 2.9)

@78
although  I call  Him  of Kṛṣṇa  a kalā  making
of Matsya, Kūrma and other  avatāras  He  the source
= Although I call Him a *kalā* of Kṛṣṇa, He is the source of Matsya, Kūrma and the other avatāras.

@79
these  and  portions and parts  of the Puruṣa
Kṛṣṇa  but  Bhagavān  Himself
by the enemies of Indra  troubled  the world
make happy  in age  after age
= “All these are portions and parts of the Puruṣa, but Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself. Age after age They bring relief to the world when it is troubled by the enemies of Indra.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.3.28)

@80
that  Puruṣa  of creation, maintenance and dissolution  the doer
various  avatāras  He makes  of the world  the sustainer
= That Puruṣa creates, maintains and dissolves the universe. He descends as many avatāras, for He is the sustainer of the world.

@81
of creation and the rest  for the sake  which  of a portion  the descent
that  indeed  portion  I call  avatāra  by name
= The portion that descends for creation and the rest is called an avatāra.

@82
the first avatāra  the great Puruṣa  Bhagavān
of all avatāras  the seed  the shelter of all  the abode
= The great Puruṣa is the first avatāra and Bhagavān. He is the seed of all avatāras, the abode where all find shelter.

@83
the first  avatāra  the Puruṣa  of the Supreme
time  nature  cause and effect  the mind  and
the elements  false ego  the modes  the senses
the universal form  the independent one  the unmoving  the moving  of the Great One
= “The Puruṣa is the first avatāra of the Supreme. Time, nature, cause and effect, the mind, the elements, false ego, the modes, the senses, the universal form (*virāṭ*), the independent one (*svarāṭ*), and all beings, unmoving and moving, are manifestations of the Great One.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.6.42)

@84
He accepted  of the Puruṣa  the form
Bhagavān  with the mahat and the rest
produced  of sixteen  parts
in the beginning  the worlds  with the desire to create
= “In the beginning, desiring to create the worlds, Bhagavān manifested the form of the Puruṣa, together with the *mahat-tattva* and the other elements — sixteen parts in all.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.3.1)

@85
although  the shelter of all  He  in Him  the world
of the indwelling Self  in the form  He  of the world  the support
= He is the shelter of all, and the material world rests in Him. As the indwelling Self (*antarātmā*) He is also the support of the world.

@86
with prakṛti  His  both kinds of  relation
still  with prakṛti  there is not  of touch  a trace
= Thus He is related to prakṛti in both ways, yet He has not the faintest trace of contact with her.
