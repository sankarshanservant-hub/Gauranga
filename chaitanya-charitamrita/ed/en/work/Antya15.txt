@title The Divine Madness of Śrī Caitanya in Separation from Kṛṣṇa (continued)

@1
in the unfathomable  of Kṛṣṇa  in the ocean of feelings
whose heart sank and rose
by Gaura  Hari  of prema
the greatness  abundantly  was shown
= In the unfathomable ocean of feelings for Kṛṣṇa, Gaurahari’s heart now sank and now rose; and so He revealed the greatness of *prema* in all its fullness.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the Supreme Lord
glory  to Nityānanda  full of bliss  whose body
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the Supreme Lord! Glory to Nityānanda, whose body is full of bliss!

@3
glory to Advaitācārya  to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  most dear
glory  to Śrīvāsa  and the others  the Lord’s  devotees
= Glory to Advaitācārya, most dear to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Glory to Śrīvāsa and all the Lord’s devotees!

@4
in this way  Mahāprabhu  night  and day
of Himself  awareness  none  absorbed in love for Kṛṣṇa
= In this way, night and day, Mahāprabhu was so absorbed in love for Kṛṣṇa that He had no awareness of Himself.

@5
sometimes  in feeling  immersed  sometimes  half  external  awareness
sometimes  external  awareness  in three  ways  the Lord’s  state
= Sometimes He was immersed in feeling, sometimes half aware of the outer world, sometimes fully aware of it: the Lord passed through these three states.

@6
bathing  seeing the Lord  eating  by the body’s  nature  happen
of the potter  the wheel  as  constantly  turns
= Bathing, going to see Lord Jagannātha and eating went on by the body’s own nature, as a potter’s wheel keeps turning by itself.

@7
one day  makes  the Lord  of Jagannātha  the seeing
in Jagannātha  He sees  directly  the son of the king of Vraja
= One day, when the Lord went to see Jagannātha, in Jagannātha He saw the son of the king of Vraja Himself.

@8
at once  appear  to the Lord  of Kṛṣṇa  five  qualities
the five  qualities  make  of the five senses  attraction
= At once the five qualities of Kṛṣṇa appeared to the Lord, and these five qualities drew His five senses.

@9
one mind  in five  directions  five  qualities  pull
by the tugging  the Lord’s  mind  became  unconscious
= The five qualities pulled His one mind in five directions, and in this tug-of-war the Lord’s mind fell unconscious.

@10
at that time  of the Lord  the upala-bhoga  ended
the devotees  Mahāprabhu  home  taking  came
= At that time Lord Jagannātha’s *upala-bhoga* offering came to an end, and the devotees brought Mahāprabhu home.

@11
Svarūpa  Rāmānanda  these  two  persons  with
lament  He makes  of both  the necks  holding
= With these two, Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, He lamented, His arms around their necks.

@12
of Kṛṣṇa  in separation  Rādhā’s  anxious  mind
to Viśākhā  She tells  Her own  of the longing  the cause
= In separation from Kṛṣṇa, Rādhā’s mind was restless with longing, and She told Viśākhā the cause of it.

@13
that  verse  reciting  Himself  makes  heartache
of the verse  the meaning  makes hear  to the two  making  lament
= Reciting that verse Himself, the Lord poured out the anguish of His heart, and lamenting, He explained its meaning to the two of them.

@14
of beauty  of the nectar  of the ocean  by the waves
of women  of the hearts  the hills  who floods
delighting the ears  jesting  charming
whose words  than millions of moons  cooler  whose body
of fragrance  of nectar  by the flood  covered
the world  than nectar  lovelier  whose lips
Śrī  of the king of the cowherds  the son  He  draws
by force  five senses  O friend  My
= “O friend, with the waves of the nectar ocean of His beauty He floods the hills of women’s hearts; His playful, charming words delight the ear; His body is cooler than millions of moons; with a flood of nectarean fragrance He covers the world; His lips are lovelier than nectar — that son of the king of the cowherds draws all five of My senses by force.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 8.3)

@15
Kṛṣṇa’s  form  sound  touch  fragrance  of the lips  taste
whose  sweetness  to describe  not  is possible
seeing  in greed  the five
one  horse  My  mind
mounting  the five  in five directions  run
= “Kṛṣṇa’s form, His voice, His touch, His fragrance and the taste of His lips — their sweetness cannot be described. Seeing them, the five, in their greed, all mount one horse — My mind — and the five run off in five directions.

@16
O friend  ah  hear  of My  sorrow  the cause
My  five senses
great  greedy ones  robbers
all  say  steal  another’s wealth
= O friend, hear the cause of My sorrow. My five senses are utterly greedy robbers: they all say, ‘Steal another’s wealth!’

@17
one  horse  at one moment
the five  in five  directions  pull
one  mind  which  way  shall run
at one time  all  pull
goes  of the horse  the life
this  pain  to bear  not  is possible
= One horse, and at one moment the five pull it in five directions — which way is the one mind to run? When all pull at once, the horse’s life departs. I cannot bear this pain.

@18
the senses  not  I make  anger
of these all  where  the fault
of Kṛṣṇa’s  form and the rest  great  attraction
form and the rest  the five  the five  pull
goes  of the horse  the life
in My  body  not  remains  life
= I am not angry with My senses — what fault is theirs? Kṛṣṇa’s form and the rest attract with great force. Form and the rest, the five, pull the five senses; the horse’s life departs, and life will not stay in My body.

@19
of Kṛṣṇa’s  beauty  of nectar  the ocean
of it  of a wave  a drop
one  drop  the world  drowns
in the three worlds  as many as  women
their  hearts  high  mountains
those  drowning  further  rising  rushes
= The beauty of Kṛṣṇa is an ocean of nectar; a single drop of its waves drowns the world. The hearts of all the women in the three worlds are high mountains: it drowns them and surges on beyond.

@20
Kṛṣṇa’s  of the words  the sweetness
of various  rasas  jests  bearing
its  injustice  to describe  not  is possible
of the world’s  women  into the ears
sweetness  with the rope (quality)  binding  pulls
by the tugging  of the ears  the life  departs
= The sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s words, full of playful jests of every *rasa* — its injustice cannot be told. Entering the ears of the world’s women, it binds them with the rope of its sweetness and pulls; and in that tugging the ears’ very life departs.

@21
Kṛṣṇa’s  body  very cool
what  shall I say  its  power
by its luster  conquers  millions of moons  sandal
mountain-like  of women  the breasts
those  in drawing  skilled
draws  of the women  the minds
= Kṛṣṇa’s body is so cool — what can I say of its power? Its luster surpasses millions of moons and sandal. It is skilled at drawing the mountain-like breasts of women, and it draws their minds.

@22
Kṛṣṇa’s body  of fragrance  full
musk  charming
of the blue lotus  steals  of pride  the wealth
of the world’s  women  the nostrils
of them  inside  makes  a dwelling
the women  makes  attraction
= Kṛṣṇa’s body is full of fragrance; it humbles the pride of charming musk and robs the blue lotus of its wealth of pride. It makes its home in the nostrils of the women of the world and draws them to itself.

@23
Kṛṣṇa’s  the nectar of the lips
with it  camphor  gentle  smile
by its own  sweetness  steals  of women  the mind
elsewhere  takes away  the longing
not  having obtained  in the mind  agitation
of Vraja’s  women  the principal wealth
= The nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips, with the camphor of His gentle smile, steals women’s minds by its own sweetness. It takes away all longing for anything else; not obtaining it, the mind is in turmoil. It is the chief wealth of the women of Vraja.”

@24
so  saying  Gaurahari
of the two  the necks  holding
says  hear  Svarūpa  Rāmarāya
what  shall I do  where  shall I go
where  going  Kṛṣṇa  shall I find
you two  to Me  tell  that  means
= Saying this, Gaurahari clasped the two by their necks and said, “Listen, Svarūpa and Rāmarāya: what shall I do, where shall I go? Where can I go to find Kṛṣṇa? Both of you, tell Me how!”

@25
in this way  Gaura  the Lord  every  day after day
lament  makes  with Svarūpa  and Rāmānanda
= In this way, day after day, the Lord Gaura lamented with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda.

@26
those  two  persons  the Lord  make  consolation
Svarūpa  sings  Rāya  makes  of verses  recitation
= Those two consoled the Lord: Svarūpa sang songs, and Rāya recited verses.

@27
Karṇāmṛta  Vidyāpati  Śrī Gīta-govinda
of these  in verses and songs  to the Lord  give  joy
= With the verses and songs of the *Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta*, of Vidyāpati and of the *Śrī Gīta-govinda* they gave the Lord joy.

@28
one day  Mahāprabhu  to the sea  going
of flowers  a garden  there  sees  suddenly
= One day, on His way to the sea, Mahāprabhu suddenly saw a flower garden.

@29
Vṛndāvana  mistaking it for  there  entered  running
absorbed in prema  wanders  there  Kṛṣṇa  searching for
= Taking it for Vṛndāvana, He ran into it, and absorbed in *prema* He wandered there searching for Kṛṣṇa,

@30
in the rāsa  Rādhā  taking  Kṛṣṇa  disappearance  made
afterwards  the gopīs  as  searching  wandered
= just as, when Kṛṣṇa disappeared from the *rāsa* dance taking Rādhā with Him, the *gopīs* afterwards wandered about searching for Him.

@31
in that  mood absorbed  the Lord  each  tree  creeper
verses  reciting  reciting  searching  wanders  here  there
= Absorbed in that mood, the Lord wandered here and there searching, reciting verse after verse to each tree and creeper:

@32
cūta  priyāla  panasa  āsana  kovidāra
jambu  arka  bilva  bakula  mango  kadamba  nīpa
who  other  for others’ good  born  on the Yamunā’s banks
tell  of Kṛṣṇa  the path  who have lost our selves  to us
= “O *cūta*, *priyāla*, *panasa*, *āsana* and *kovidāra*, *jambu*, *arka*, *bilva*, *bakula*, mango, *kadamba* and *nīpa*, and all you other trees on the banks of the Yamunā, whose life is for the good of others — tell us, who have lost our very selves, which way Kṛṣṇa went!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.9)

@33
perhaps  O tulasī  O auspicious one
to Govinda’s  feet  dear
with  you  by swarms of bees  bearing
seen  your  very dear  Acyuta
= “O auspicious *tulasī*, dear to Govinda’s feet! Have you seen your most beloved Acyuta pass by, wearing you, with swarms of bees around Him?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.7)

@34
O mālatī  was seen  by you  perhaps
O mallikā  O jāti  O yūthikā
joy  your  producing  gone
by the touch of His hand  Mādhava
= “O *mālatī*, O *mallikā*, O *jāti*, O *yūthikā*! Have you seen Mādhava pass by, giving you joy with the touch of His hand?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.8)

@35
O mango  panasa  piyāla  jambu  kovidāra
of a holy place  dwellers  all  do  to others  good
= “O mango, *panasa*, *piyāla*, *jambu* and *kovidāra*! You all dwell in a holy place — do good to others.

@36
Kṛṣṇa  to you  here  came  did you have  sight of Him
of Kṛṣṇa  the whereabouts  telling  save  our lives
= Did Kṛṣṇa come here to you? Did you see Him? Tell us where Kṛṣṇa is and save our lives!”

@37
an answer  not  getting  again  they make  a surmise
all these  male  in kind  of Kṛṣṇa  friends  like
= Receiving no answer, they reasoned again: “These are all male, like Kṛṣṇa’s friends.

@38
these  why  will tell  of Kṛṣṇa  the whereabouts  to us
these  female  in kind  creepers  our  friends  almost
= Why would they tell us where Kṛṣṇa is? But these creepers are female, almost our friends.

@39
surely  they will tell  they have had  of Kṛṣṇa  the sight
thus  reasoning  they ask  tulasī and the others
= They will surely tell us — they have seen Kṛṣṇa.” Reasoning thus, they questioned the *tulasī* and the other plants:

@40
O tulasī  O mālatī  O yūthī  O mādhavī  O mallikā
your  beloved  Kṛṣṇa  came  your  near
= “O *tulasī*, *mālatī*, *yūthī*, *mādhavī* and *mallikā*! Did your beloved Kṛṣṇa come near you?

@41
you all  are  our  friends  like
Kṛṣṇa’s whereabouts  telling  all of you  save  our lives
= You are all like our friends. Tell us where Kṛṣṇa is, all of you, and save our lives!”

@42
an answer  not  getting  again  they think  within
these  Kṛṣṇa’s  maidservants  out of fear  not  tell  us
= Receiving no answer, they thought within themselves: “These are Kṛṣṇa’s maidservants; out of fear they will not tell us.”

@43
ahead  does  seeing  of Kṛṣṇa’s body  the fragrance  catching
their  faces  looking at  they ask  certainty  making
= Ahead they saw some does, and catching the fragrance of Kṛṣṇa’s body, they looked at their faces and, now certain, questioned them:

@44
perhaps  O doe  came  with His beloved  here  with His limbs
expanding  of the eyes  O friend  the great joy  Acyuta  your
of the beloved’s  body  from contact  of the breasts  with kuṅkuma  colored
of the kunda garland  of our Lord  here  blows  the fragrance
= “O friend, O doe, did Acyuta come this way with His beloved, delighting your eyes with the beauty of His limbs? For here blows the fragrance of our Lord’s *kunda* garland, colored with the *kuṅkuma* from His beloved’s breasts where it touched Her body.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.11)

@45
tell  O doe  with Rādhā  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  in every way
to you  joy  to give  did He come  not  say  otherwise
= “Tell us, O doe: did Śrī Kṛṣṇa come here with Rādhā to give you every joy? Do not tell us otherwise.

@46
Rādhā’s  dear  friends  we  are not  outsiders
from far  away  we know  His  what kind  body’s fragrance
= We are Rādhā’s dear friends, not outsiders; even from afar we know the fragrance of His body.

@47
with Rādhā’s  body  by contact  with the breasts’  kuṅkuma  adorned
Kṛṣṇa’s  kunda  garland  with the fragrance  the air  is scented
= The air is scented with the fragrance of Kṛṣṇa’s *kunda* garland, adorned with *kuṅkuma* from Rādhā’s breasts where it touched Her body.

@48
Kṛṣṇa  here  leaving  went  this one  pining in separation
what  answer  will give  she  not  hears  our words
= Kṛṣṇa has left this place, and she is pining in separation. What answer can she give? She does not even hear our words.”

@49
ahead  trees  they see  of flowers  and fruits  under the weight
branches  all  have fallen  the earth  upon
= Ahead they saw trees whose branches had all bent down to the earth under the weight of flowers and fruits.

@50
Kṛṣṇa  seeing  these  all  make  obeisance
Kṛṣṇa’s  going  they ask  them  certainty  making
= Concluding that all these trees had bowed down on seeing Kṛṣṇa, they asked them, to be sure, which way He had gone:

@51
the arm  on His beloved’s shoulder  placing  holding a lotus
Rāma’s younger brother  by the tulasī bees  in swarms  blind with intoxication
followed  here  your  O trees  obeisance
whether  welcomes  passing  with loving glances
= “O trees, did Rāma’s younger brother pass this way, His arm on His beloved’s shoulder and a lotus in His hand, followed by swarms of bees blind with the intoxication of His *tulasī*? And as He passed, did He welcome your obeisances with loving glances?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.30.12)

@52
of the beloved  on the face  bees  fall  them  to drive away
the pastime lotus  in waving  became  of distracted mind
= As bees settled on His beloved’s face, He waved His pastime lotus to drive them away, and His mind was distracted.

@53
your  obeisance  whether  did He pay  attention
or  not  did He  tell  word  as proof
= Did He pay any attention to your obeisances, or did He not? Tell us, give us your word as proof.

@54
of Kṛṣṇa  in separation  these  servants  grieving
what  answer  will they give  their  is not  consciousness
= These servants are grieving in separation from Kṛṣṇa. What answer can they give? They are not even conscious.”

@55
so  saying  further  they go  of the Yamunā  to the bank
they see  there  Kṛṣṇa  is  of a kadamba  beneath
= Saying this, they went on to the bank of the Yamunā, and there they saw Kṛṣṇa beneath a *kadamba* tree.

@56
millions  of gods of love  enchanting  with the flute at His lips
boundless  by beauty  steals  the world’s  eyes and mind
= He enchants millions of gods of love; the flute is at His lips; with His boundless beauty He steals the eyes and minds of the whole world.

@57
the beauty  seeing  to the ground  falls  a swoon  getting
at that time  Svarūpa and the others  met  arriving
= Seeing that beauty, the Lord fell to the ground in a swoon. Just then Svarūpa and the others arrived and found Him.

@58
as before  in all His limbs  sāttvika  bhāvas  all
within  of bliss  the tasting  outwardly  overwhelmed
= As before, all the *sāttvika-bhāvas* appeared in all His limbs. Within He was tasting bliss; outwardly He was overwhelmed.

@59
as before  all  together  made  consciousness
rising  on all sides  the Lord  makes  looking
= As before, they all together brought Him back to consciousness. Rising, the Lord looked all around.

@60
where  went  Kṛṣṇa  now  I got  sight
whose  beauty  My  stole  eyes and mind
= “Where has Kṛṣṇa gone? I saw Him just now — Him whose beauty has stolen My eyes and mind!

@61
again  why  not  I see  with the flute at His lips
His  sight  in greed  wander  My eyes
= Why do I not see Him again, with the flute at His lips? My eyes wander about, greedy to see Him.”

@62
to Viśākhā  Rādhā  as  verse  spoke
that  verse  Mahāprabhu  to recite  began
= Then Mahāprabhu began to recite the verse that Rādhā had spoken to Viśākhā:

@63
than a new cloud  shining brighter  whose luster  than new

with a beautiful  flute  gleaming  autumn

= “O friend, His luster shines brighter than a new cloud; His garment is more charming than new lightning; His face, with the gleaming, beautiful flute, is like the full autumn moon; He is adorned with peacock feathers and radiant with a lovely necklace shining like stars — that enchanter of Madana increases the longing of My eyes.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 8.4)

@64
new  cloud  glossy  complexion
ground  collyrium  smooth
the blue lotus  putting to shame  very soft
conquering  of comparisons  the host
steals  of all  eyes and mind
Kṛṣṇa’s  luster  supremely  powerful
= “Kṛṣṇa’s complexion is glossy as a new cloud and smooth as ground collyrium; it puts the blue lotus to shame with its softness. Surpassing every comparison, Kṛṣṇa’s luster is supremely powerful: it steals the eyes and minds of all.

@65

Kṛṣṇa  wondrous  cloud
My  eyes  cātaka birds
not  seeing  of thirst  dying  go
= Tell Me, O friend, what shall I do? Kṛṣṇa is a wondrous cloud, and the *cātaka* birds of My eyes, not seeing Him, are dying of thirst.

@66
lightning  the yellow cloth  steady  is not  ever
pearl  necklace  of cranes  a row  lovely
Indra’s  bow  peacock  feather
above  has  appeared
another  bow  the Vaijayantī  garland
= His yellow cloth is the lightning, never steady; His pearl necklace is a lovely row of cranes; His peacock feather is a rainbow appearing above, and His Vaijayantī garland is another rainbow.

@67
of the flute  soft sweet  sound
sweet  thunder  hearing
in Vṛndāvana  dance  peacocks  in flocks
spotless  full in all digits
of beauty  moonlight  sparkling
a wondrous moon  in it  rising
= Hearing the soft, sweet sound of His flute — the cloud’s sweet thunder — the peacocks of Vṛndāvana dance. And in that cloud a wondrous moon is rising, spotless and full in all its digits, its moonlight of beauty sparkling.

@68
of pastimes  of nectar  by the shower
it sprinkles  fourteen  worlds
such  a cloud  when  appeared
of misfortune  of the storm  by the wind
the cloud  was taken  to another  place
dies  the cātaka  to drink  not  could
= With a shower of the nectar of His pastimes it drenches all fourteen worlds — but when such a cloud appeared, the storm wind of My misfortune carried it away to another place, and the *cātaka* is dying, for it could not drink.”

@69
again  He says  alas  alas
recite  recite  Rāmarāya
says  the Lord  choked  in speech
Rāmānanda  recites  a verse
hearing  the Lord’s  joy  and sorrow
Himself  the Lord  makes  explanation
= Again He said, “Alas, alas! Recite, recite, Rāmarāya!” — so the Lord spoke, His voice choked with feeling. Rāmānanda recited a verse; hearing it, the Lord felt joy and sorrow, and He Himself explained it.

@70
having seen  by locks  framed  face  Your  by the beauty of earrings
of the cheeks  of the lips  the nectar  smiling  glance
granting fearlessness  and  of arms  the pair  having beheld
the chest  for Śrī  the sole delight  and  we become  maidservants
= “Seeing Your face framed by curling locks and graced by the beauty of Your earrings, Your cheeks, the nectar of Your lips and Your smiling glance, and beholding Your two arms that grant fearlessness and Your chest, the sole delight of Śrī, we have become Your maidservants.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.39)

@71

in it  of the lips  sweet  smile  the bait
Vraja’s  women  coming  coming
into the snare  falling  become  maidservants

= “Having conquered the lotus and the moon, Kṛṣṇa has set the snare of His face, baited with the sweet smile of His lips. The women of Vraja come, fall into the snare and become His maidservants, leaving shame, husbands, home and hearth.

@72

not  regards  right and wrong
steals  of the women-does  the hearts
makes  various  means  for that
= Our friend Kṛṣṇa behaves like a hunter. He cares nothing for right or wrong; He steals the hearts of the women-does and devises many means to do it.

@73
the cheeks  sparkling
dance  makara  earrings
by which  dance  steal  women  all
smiling  of sidelong glances  with arrows
their  all  in the hearts  He strikes
of women  in killing  not  any  fear
= On His sparkling cheeks His *makara* earrings dance, and by that dance they steal the hearts of all women. With the arrows of His smiling sidelong glances He strikes their hearts, and He has no fear at all of killing women.

@74
very  high  broad
Lakṣmī  Śrīvatsa  ornaments
Kṛṣṇa’s  that  robber  chest
of Vraja  goddesses  hundreds of  thousands

of Hari  maidservants  in making  skilled
