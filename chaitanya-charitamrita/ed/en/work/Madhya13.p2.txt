# CC Madhya 13, stage A, part 2 (verses 105–209); read by build_en.py together with Madhya13.txt

@105
of a fountain  the jets  as  flow  tears
around  people  as many as  were drenched  all
= Tears streamed from His eyes like jets from a fountain, and everyone around Him was drenched.

@106
of His body  the luster  golden in color  appears  rosy
sometimes  the luster  we see  as if  a mallikā flower  resembling
= The golden luster of His body looked rosy, and at times it looked white, like a *mallikā* flower.

@107
sometimes  stunned  sometimes  the Lord  on the ground  rolls
dry wood  like  feet and hands  not  move
= Sometimes He stood stunned, and sometimes He rolled on the ground; His hands and feet, stiff as dry wood, would not move.

@108
sometimes  on the ground  He falls  sometimes  breath  becomes  absent
which  seeing  of the devotees  the life  becomes  faint
= Sometimes He fell to the ground, and sometimes His breathing stopped. Seeing this, the devotees felt their own life ebbing away.

@109
sometimes  from eyes and nose  water  from the mouth  falls  foam
of nectar  streams  on the moon’s disc  flow  as if
= Sometimes water flowed from His eyes and nose and foam fell from His mouth, as if streams of nectar were flowing over the disc of the moon.

@110
that  foam  taking  Śubhānanda  made  drinking
of love for Kṛṣṇa  a relisher of the rasa  he  most fortunate
= Śubhānanda took that foam and drank it. He was most fortunate, a true relisher of the *rasa* of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@111
in this way  the tāṇḍava dance  He did  for some time
into a special feeling  the Lord’s  entered  mind
= Thus the Lord danced the vigorous *tāṇḍava* dance for some time, and then His mind entered a special mood.

@112
the tāṇḍava dance  stopping  to Svarūpa  an order  He gave
His heart  knowing  Svarūpa  to sing  began
= Leaving the *tāṇḍava* dance, He told Svarūpa to sing, and Svarūpa, who knew His heart, began to sing:

@113
that  very  Lord of My life  I have found
whom  for  in the fire of Madana  pining  I was wasting away
= “I have found that very Lord of My life for whom I was wasting away, burning in the fire of Madana (love).”[^m13-113]

@114
this  refrain  in a loud voice  sings  Dāmodara
in bliss  sweet  dance  does  the Lord
= Svarūpa Dāmodara sang this refrain in a loud voice, and the Lord, in bliss, danced a sweet dance.

@115
slowly  slowly  Jagannātha  makes  moving
ahead  dance  doing  goes  of Śacī  the son
= Jagannātha moved forward slowly, slowly, and the son of Śacī went ahead of Him, dancing.

@116
on Jagannātha  the eyes  fixing  all  dance  sing
the kīrtana singers  with  the Lord  behind  behind  goes
= With their eyes fixed on Jagannātha, everyone danced and sang, and the Lord, with the kīrtana singers, followed behind.

@117
in Jagannātha  absorbed  the Lord’s  eyes and heart
with His holy  two hands  He makes  of the song  the acting out
= The Lord’s eyes and heart were absorbed in Jagannātha, and with His two holy hands He acted out the song.

@118
Gaura  if  behind  goes  Śyāma  becomes  still
Gaura  ahead  goes  Śyāma  moves  slowly, slowly
= When Gaura fell behind, Śyāma stood still; when Gaura went ahead, Śyāma moved on, slowly, slowly.

@119
in this way  Gaura and Śyāma  the two  push against each other
on His own chariot  Śyāma  keeps  Gaura  the mighty
= Thus Gaura and Śyāma vied with each other, and mighty Gaura kept Śyāma waiting on His chariot.

@120
dancing  dancing  in the Lord  arose  a change of mood
the hands  raising  a verse  He recites  making  a loud voice
= As He danced, the Lord’s mood changed. Raising His arms, He recited a verse in a loud voice:

@121
who  stole my heart in my girlhood  He  the same  indeed  my beloved  those  same  nights of Caitra
those same  and  fragrant with blossoming mālatī  full  breezes from the kadambas
she  the same  am I  yet  there  for the pastimes of love
of the Revā  on the bank  of the vetasī  tree  beneath  My heart  yearns
= “He who stole My heart in My girlhood is now My lord. These are the same moonlit nights of Caitra, and the same full breezes blow from the kadamba groves, fragrant with blossoming *mālatī*. I too am the same. Yet My heart yearns for Our play of love there, beneath the *vetasī* trees on the bank of the Revā.” (Padyāvalī, 386)[^m13-121]

@122
this  verse  Mahāprabhu  recites  time  after time
Svarūpa  except  the meaning  anyone  not  knows  of it
= Mahāprabhu recited this verse again and again, and no one but Svarūpa knew its meaning.

@123
this verse’s meaning  earlier  I have made  an explanation
of the verse  the inner meaning  I make  briefly  a telling
= I have explained the meaning of this verse before; now I will briefly tell its inner sense.

@124
once  as  at Kurukṣetra  all  the gopīs
Kṛṣṇa’s  sight  obtaining  joyful  in mind
= Once, at Kurukṣetra, all the gopīs rejoiced at heart to see Kṛṣṇa.

@125
Jagannātha  seeing  in the Lord  that  mood  arose
in that mood absorbed  becoming  the refrain  He had sung
= When the Lord saw Jagannātha, that same mood arose in Him, and, absorbed in it, He had the refrain sung.

@126
at last  Rādhā  to Kṛṣṇa  makes  an appeal
the same  You  the same  I  the same  fresh  union
= At last Rādhā made Her appeal to Kṛṣṇa: “You are the same, I am the same, and Our union is as fresh as before.

@127
still  My  mind  steals  Vṛndāvana
in Vṛndāvana  rising  make  Your own feet
= Yet Vṛndāvana steals My heart. Make Your feet appear in Vṛndāvana once again.

@128
here  a forest of people  elephants  horses  the rumble of chariots
there  a forest of flowers  of bees and cuckoos  the sound  I hear
= Here there is a forest of people, elephants and horses and the rumble of chariots; there was a forest of flowers, where one heard the humming of bees and the call of the cuckoos.

@129
here  royal dress  with You  all  the kṣatriyas
there  cowherd’s dress  with You  the playing of the flute
= Here You wear royal dress and are surrounded by kṣatriya warriors; there You wore the dress of a cowherd boy, and with You was only the song of Your flute.

@130
in Vraja  You  with  which  happiness  tasting
of that  ocean of happiness  here  not is  one  drop
= Of that ocean of happiness I tasted with You in Vraja, not one drop is here.

@131
Me  taking  again  pastimes  do  in Vṛndāvana
then  My  heart’s desire  becomes  surely  fulfilled
= Take Me with You and play with Me again in Vṛndāvana. Only then will My heart’s desire be fulfilled.”

@132
in the Bhāgavatam  there are  as  Rādhikā’s words
before  that  in the summary  I have made  a description
= Rādhikā’s words as they stand in the *Bhāgavatam* I have already described before, in the summary of the book.

@133
in that mood absorbed  the Lord  recites  another  verse
of those  all  verses  the meaning  not  understand  people
= Absorbed in that mood, the Lord recited another verse, but no one understood the meaning of such verses.

@134
Svarūpa Gosāñi  knows  not  tells  the meaning  of it
Śrī  Rūpa Gosāñi  made  that  meaning’s  proclamation
= Svarūpa Gosāñi knew it but did not tell its meaning; Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi made that meaning known.

@135
Svarūpa  with  whose  meaning  He makes  relishing
in the midst of the dance  that  verse  He makes  reciting
= In the midst of the dance He recited the verse whose meaning He relished with Svarūpa:

@136
they said  and  Your  O lotus-naveled Lord  lotus feet
by the masters of yoga  in the heart  meditated upon  of fathomless wisdom
of worldly life  into the well  of the fallen  for rescue  the support
to home  attached  even  in the mind  may they rise  always  our
= (The gopīs said:) “O Lord whose navel is like a lotus, the masters of yoga, whose wisdom is fathomless, meditate within their hearts on Your lotus feet, the support by which souls fallen into the well of worldly life are lifted out. May those feet always rise in our minds too, though we are busy with our homes.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.82.48)[^m13-136]
