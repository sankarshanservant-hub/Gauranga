= On the left was the brāhmaṇa settlement (*vipra-śāsana*) with its coconut grove; on the right was a flower garden like Vṛndāvana.

@195
in front  dance  does  Gaura  taking  the devotees
the chariot  stopping  Jagannātha  makes  seeing
= In front, Gaura danced with His devotees, and Jagannātha, having stopped His chariot, watched Him.

@196
at that  place  the food offering  is offered  there is  a custom
millions of  offerings  Jagannātha  makes  tasting
= By custom, food is offered to Jagannātha at that place, and He tastes millions of offerings.

@197
of Jagannātha  humble and great  all  the devotees
each his own  the best food  make  offering
= All of Jagannātha’s devotees, humble and great, offered Him the best food they had.

@198
the king  the host of queens  ministers  friends
of Nīlācala  the residents  all  humble and great  people
= The king, the queens, the ministers and friends, all the residents of Nīlācala, humble and great,

@199
of various lands  natives  all  pilgrim  people
each his own food  there  make  offering
= and all the pilgrims from many lands offered their own food there.

@200
in front and behind  on both  sides  of the gardens  in the groves
whoever  whatever (place)  finds  offers  there is no  rule
= In front and behind, on both sides, in the groves of the gardens, each offered his food wherever he found a place; there was no fixed rule.

@201
of the offering  at the time  of people  a great  crowd  formed
the dance  leaving  Mahāprabhu  into a garden  went
= At the time of the offering a great crowd gathered, so Mahāprabhu stopped dancing and went into a garden.

@202
absorbed in love  Mahāprabhu  the garden  reaching
in the flower garden  on a house’s raised platform  remained  lying
= Absorbed in love, Mahāprabhu entered the flower garden and lay down there on the raised platform (*piṇḍā*) of a house.

@203
from the exertion of dancing  the Lord’s  on the body  heavy  sweat
fragrant  cool breeze  He makes  enjoying
= After the exertion of the dance His body was covered with sweat, and He enjoyed the cool, fragrant breeze.

@204
all  the devotees  kīrtana singers  coming  to the garden
under each tree  all  make  rest
= All the devotees and kīrtana singers came into the garden and rested under the trees.

@205
thus  I have told  of the Lord  the great saṅkīrtana
before Jagannātha  how  He did  dancing
= Thus I have told of the Lord’s great *saṅkīrtana* and how He danced before Jagannātha.

@206
in front of the chariot  the Lord  how  did  dancing
in the Śrī Caitanyāṣṭaka  Rūpa Gosāñi  has made  a description
= How the Lord danced in front of the chariot, Rūpa Gosāñi has described in his *Śrī Caitanyāṣṭaka*:

@207
of the One seated on the chariot  near  on the main road  of Nīlācala  of the Lord
by abundant waves of love  throbbing  by the joy of dancing  overwhelmed
joyfully  by those singing  surrounded in body  by the Vaiṣṇavas
that  Caitanya  will  my  again  even  of the eyes  come  into the path
= “Before the Lord of Nīlācala, seated on His chariot on the great road, He danced, overwhelmed by the joy of a dance that throbbed with abundant waves of love, surrounded by Vaiṣṇavas singing in delight. Will that Caitanya ever again come before my eyes?” (Stava-mālā, First Caitanyāṣṭaka, 7)

@208
this  who  hears  he  Śrī  Caitanya  attains
firm  faith  with  loving devotion  arises
= Whoever hears this attains Śrī Caitanya, and loving devotion with firm faith awakens in him.

@209
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.[^m13-209]
