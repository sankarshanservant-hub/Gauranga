# CC Madhya 13, stage A, part 2 (verses 105–209); read by build_en.py together with Madhya13.txt

@105
of a fountain  the jets  as  flow  tears
around  people  as many as  were drenched  all
= Tears streamed from His eyes like jets from a fountain, and everyone around Him was drenched.

@106
of His body  the luster  golden in color  appears  rosy
sometimes  the luster  we see  as if  a mallikā flower  resembling
= The golden luster of His body looked rosy, and at times it looked white, like a *mallikā* flower.

@107
sometimes  stunned  sometimes  the Lord  on the ground  rolls
dry wood  like  feet and hands  not  move
= Sometimes He stood stunned, and sometimes He rolled on the ground; His hands and feet, stiff as dry wood, would not move.

@108
sometimes  on the ground  He falls  sometimes  breath  becomes  absent
which  seeing  of the devotees  the life  becomes  faint
= Sometimes He fell to the ground, and sometimes His breathing stopped. Seeing this, the devotees felt their own life ebbing away.

@109
sometimes  from eyes and nose  water  from the mouth  falls  foam
of nectar  streams  on the moon’s disc  flow  as if
= Sometimes water flowed from His eyes and nose and foam fell from His mouth, as if streams of nectar were flowing over the disc of the moon.

@110
that  foam  taking  Śubhānanda  made  drinking
of love for Kṛṣṇa  a relisher of the rasa  he  most fortunate
= Śubhānanda took that foam and drank it. He was most fortunate, a true relisher of the *rasa* of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@111
in this way  the tāṇḍava dance  He did  for some time
into a special feeling  the Lord’s  entered  mind
= Thus the Lord danced the vigorous *tāṇḍava* dance for some time, and then His mind entered a special mood.

@112
the tāṇḍava dance  stopping  to Svarūpa  an order  He gave
His heart  knowing  Svarūpa  to sing  began
= Leaving the *tāṇḍava* dance, He told Svarūpa to sing, and Svarūpa, who knew His heart, began to sing:

@113
that  very  Lord of My life  I have found
whom  for  in the fire of Madana  pining  I was wasting away
= “I have found that very Lord of My life for whom I was wasting away, burning in the fire of Madana (love).”[^m13-113]

@114
this  refrain  in a loud voice  sings  Dāmodara
in bliss  sweet  dance  does  the Lord
= Svarūpa Dāmodara sang this refrain in a loud voice, and the Lord, in bliss, danced a sweet dance.

@115
slowly  slowly  Jagannātha  makes  moving
ahead  dance  doing  goes  of Śacī  the son
= Jagannātha moved forward slowly, slowly, and the son of Śacī went ahead of Him, dancing.

@116
on Jagannātha  the eyes  fixing  all  dance  sing
the kīrtana singers  with  the Lord  behind  behind  goes
= With their eyes fixed on Jagannātha, everyone danced and sang, and the Lord, with the kīrtana singers, followed behind.

@117
in Jagannātha  absorbed  the Lord’s  eyes and heart
with His holy  two hands  He makes  of the song  the acting out
= The Lord’s eyes and heart were absorbed in Jagannātha, and with His two holy hands He acted out the song.

@118
Gaura  if  behind  goes  Śyāma  becomes  still
Gaura  ahead  goes  Śyāma  moves  slowly, slowly
= When Gaura fell behind, Śyāma stood still; when Gaura went ahead, Śyāma moved on, slowly, slowly.

@119
in this way  Gaura and Śyāma  the two  push against each other
on His own chariot  Śyāma  keeps  Gaura  the mighty
= Thus Gaura and Śyāma vied with each other, and mighty Gaura kept Śyāma waiting on His chariot.

@120
dancing  dancing  in the Lord  arose  a change of mood
the hands  raising  a verse  He recites  making  a loud voice
= As He danced, the Lord’s mood changed. Raising His arms, He recited a verse in a loud voice:

@121
who  stole my heart in my girlhood  He  the same  indeed  my beloved  those  same  nights of Caitra
those same  and  fragrant with blossoming mālatī  full  breezes from the kadambas
she  the same  am I  yet  there  for the pastimes of love
of the Revā  on the bank  of the vetasī  tree  beneath  My heart  yearns
= “He who stole My heart in My girlhood is now My lord. These are the same moonlit nights of Caitra, and the same full breezes blow from the kadamba groves, fragrant with blossoming *mālatī*. I too am the same. Yet My heart yearns for Our play of love there, beneath the *vetasī* trees on the bank of the Revā.” (Padyāvalī, 386)[^m13-121]

@122
this  verse  Mahāprabhu  recites  time  after time
Svarūpa  except  the meaning  anyone  not  knows  of it
= Mahāprabhu recited this verse again and again, and no one but Svarūpa knew its meaning.

@123
this verse’s meaning  earlier  I have made  an explanation
of the verse  the inner meaning  I make  briefly  a telling
= I have explained the meaning of this verse before; now I will briefly tell its inner sense.

@124
once  as  at Kurukṣetra  all  the gopīs
Kṛṣṇa’s  sight  obtaining  joyful  in mind
= Once, at Kurukṣetra, all the gopīs rejoiced at heart to see Kṛṣṇa.

@125
Jagannātha  seeing  in the Lord  that  mood  arose
in that mood absorbed  becoming  the refrain  He had sung
= When the Lord saw Jagannātha, that same mood arose in Him, and, absorbed in it, He had the refrain sung.

@126
at last  Rādhā  to Kṛṣṇa  makes  an appeal
the same  You  the same  I  the same  fresh  union
= At last Rādhā made Her appeal to Kṛṣṇa: “You are the same, I am the same, and Our union is as fresh as before.

@127
still  My  mind  steals  Vṛndāvana
in Vṛndāvana  rising  make  Your own feet
= Yet Vṛndāvana steals My heart. Make Your feet appear in Vṛndāvana once again.

@128
here  a forest of people  elephants  horses  the rumble of chariots
there  a forest of flowers  of bees and cuckoos  the sound  I hear
= Here there is a forest of people, elephants and horses and the rumble of chariots; there was a forest of flowers, where one heard the humming of bees and the call of the cuckoos.

@129
here  royal dress  with You  all  the kṣatriyas
there  cowherd’s dress  with You  the playing of the flute
= Here You wear royal dress and are surrounded by kṣatriya warriors; there You wore the dress of a cowherd boy, and with You was only the song of Your flute.

@130
in Vraja  You  with  which  happiness  tasting
of that  ocean of happiness  here  not is  one  drop
= Of that ocean of happiness I tasted with You in Vraja, not one drop is here.

@131
Me  taking  again  pastimes  do  in Vṛndāvana
then  My  heart’s desire  becomes  surely  fulfilled
= Take Me with You and play with Me again in Vṛndāvana. Only then will My heart’s desire be fulfilled.”

@132
in the Bhāgavatam  there are  as  Rādhikā’s words
before  that  in the summary  I have made  a description
= Rādhikā’s words as they stand in the *Bhāgavatam* I have already described before, in the summary of the book.

@133
in that mood absorbed  the Lord  recites  another  verse
of those  all  verses  the meaning  not  understand  people
= Absorbed in that mood, the Lord recited another verse, but no one understood the meaning of such verses.

@134
Svarūpa Gosāñi  knows  not  tells  the meaning  of it
Śrī  Rūpa Gosāñi  made  that  meaning’s  proclamation
= Svarūpa Gosāñi knew it but did not tell its meaning; Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi made that meaning known.

@135
Svarūpa  with  whose  meaning  He makes  relishing
in the midst of the dance  that  verse  He makes  reciting
= In the midst of the dance He recited the verse whose meaning He relished with Svarūpa:

@136
they said  and  Your  O lotus-naveled Lord  lotus feet
by the masters of yoga  in the heart  meditated upon  of fathomless wisdom
of worldly life  into the well  of the fallen  for rescue  the support
to home  attached  even  in the mind  may they rise  always  our
= (The gopīs said:) “O Lord whose navel is like a lotus, the masters of yoga, whose wisdom is fathomless, meditate within their hearts on Your lotus feet, the support by which souls fallen into the well of worldly life are lifted out. May those feet always rise in our minds too, though we are busy with our homes.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.82.48)[^m13-136]

@137
for others  the heart —  the mind  My  mind —  Vṛndāvana
‘mind’  ‘forest’  as one  making  I know
there  Your  two feet  You make  if  rising
then  Your  full  mercy  I consider
= “For others the heart is the mind, but My mind is Vṛndāvana: I take *mana* (mind) and *vana* (forest) as one. If You make Your two feet appear there, I will count it Your full mercy.

@138
O Lord of My life  hear  My  true  appeal
Vraja —  My  home  there  with You  union
not  obtaining  not  remains  life
= O Lord of My life, hear My true appeal. Vraja is My home, and there I long to be with You; if I cannot have that, My life will not remain.

@139
before  through Uddhava  now  directly  to Me
in yoga and knowledge  You told  the means
You —  expert  merciful  You know  My  heart
to Me  in this way  to speak  not  is fitting
= Before through Uddhava, and now to My face, You have told Me that yoga and knowledge are the way. You are clever and merciful, and You know My heart: it is not fitting to speak to Me like this.

@140
the mind  drawing away  from You  on worldly things  I want  to fix
effort  making  I cannot  draw it away
her  meditation  teaching  You give  people  making laugh  You kill
the fitting and the unfitting  not  You make  consideration
= I want to draw My mind away from You and fix it on worldly things, but however hard I try, I cannot draw it away. And You teach such a one to meditate! You kill her by making her a laughingstock; You do not consider what is fitting and what is not.

@141
not are  the gopīs  masters of yoga  the lotus feet  Your
meditation  doing  they will get  satisfaction
Your  polish of words  of it  within  duplicity
hearing  of the gopīs  still more  grows  anger
= The gopīs are not masters of yoga, to find satisfaction in meditating on Your lotus feet. Your polished words hide duplicity (*kuṭināṭī*), and hearing them, the gopīs grow only angrier.

@142
of the body  memory  not is  whose  of worldly life  the well  where  for them
from that  not  they want  deliverance
of separation  of the ocean  in the waters  kāma’s timiṅgila  swallows
the gopīs  take  of it  across
= Those who have no memory of their bodies — where is the well of worldly life for them? They do not want deliverance from it. In the waters of the ocean of separation the *timiṅgila* of *kāma*, their longing for You, is swallowing them: carry the gopīs across that ocean!

@143
Vṛndāvana  Govardhana  the Yamunā’s bank  the forest
in those  groves  the rāsa and other  pastimes
of that  Vraja  the people  mother  father  friends
very  strange  how  You forgot
= Vṛndāvana, Govardhana, the bank of the Yamunā and the forest; the *rāsa* dance and the other pastimes in those groves; the people of Vraja — Your mother, father and friends: how could You forget them? It is very strange!

@144
expert  gentle  virtuous  well-mannered  affectionate  compassionate
You  in You  not is  a semblance of fault
yet  that  Your  mind  not  remembers  the people of Vraja
that —  of My  ill fortune  the play
= You are clever, gentle, virtuous, well-mannered, affectionate and compassionate; in You there is not even a shadow of fault. That Your mind no longer remembers the people of Vraja is only the play of My ill fortune.

@145
not  I count  My own sorrow  seeing  of Vraja’s queen  the face
of the people of Vraja  the heart  breaks
either  You kill  the residents of Vraja  or  revive  to Vraja  coming
why  You keep alive  sorrow  to make them endure
= I do not count My own sorrow; but seeing the face of the queen of Vraja, the people of Vraja are heartbroken. Either kill the residents of Vraja, or come to Vraja and bring them back to life. Why do You keep them alive only to suffer?

@146
Your  that  other  dress  other  company  other  land
to the people of Vraja  ever  not  pleases
the land of Vraja  to leave  not  they can  You  not  seeing  they die
of the people of Vraja  what  will be  the way
= Your other dress, Your other company and Your other land never please the people of Vraja. They cannot leave the land of Vraja, yet without seeing You they die. What will become of them?

@147
You —  of Vraja  the life  of the king of Vraja  the treasure of life
You —  of all  Vraja  the wealth
moist with mercy  Your  mind  coming  revive  the people of Vraja
in Vraja  rising  make  Your own feet
= You are the life of Vraja and the treasure of the life of Vraja’s king; You are the wealth of all Vraja. Your heart is soft with mercy: come and revive the people of Vraja, and make Your feet appear in Vraja once again.”

@148
having heard  Rādhikā’s words  the love of Vraja  to the mind  bringing
with feeling  agitated  body and mind
of the people of Vraja  the love  hearing  Himself  ‘a debtor’  considering
makes  Kṛṣṇa  to Her  consolation
= Hearing Rādhikā’s words and remembering the love of Vraja, Kṛṣṇa was shaken in body and mind. Hearing of the love of the people of Vraja, He considered Himself their debtor and began to console Her:

@149
O beloved, dear as life  hear  My  these  true words
of all of you  in remembering  pine in tears  I  night and day
My  sorrow  not  knows  any  person
= “My beloved, dear to Me as My life, hear these true words of Mine. Remembering all of you, I weep night and day, and no one knows My sorrow.

@150
of Vraja the residents  all  people  mother  father  friends
all  are  to Me  equal to life
of them  among  the gopīs  directly  My  life
You  of My  life  the life
= All the people of Vraja — My mother, My father and My friends — are as dear to Me as life. Among them the gopīs are My very life, and You are the life of My life.

@151
of all of you  by the rasa of love  Me  made  subject
I  yours  dependent  only
from all of you  tearing away  Me  to a distant land  taking
has kept  ill fortune  powerful
= The *rasa* of your love has made Me subject to you all; I am yours alone. It is a powerful ill fortune that has torn Me away from you, taken Me to a distant land and kept Me there.

@152
the beloved  without the lover’s company  the lover  of the beloved’s company  without
not  lives  this  true  proof
My  state  hears  when  her  this  state  will be
in this  fear  both  keep  life
= A beloved without her lover’s company, or a lover without his beloved’s, cannot live — this is proven truth. Yet each fears: “When she hears of my state, she will come to the same state”; and for this fear both keep themselves alive.

@153
that  faithful wife  loving  loving  that  husband
in separation  who  wish  the beloved’s  good
not  count  their own sorrow  wish  the beloved’s  happiness
those  two  meet  soon
= A loving, faithful wife and a loving husband who in separation wish only for each other’s good, who do not count their own sorrow and want only the beloved’s happiness — such a pair soon meet again.

@154
to keep  Your  life  serve  I  Nārāyaṇa
by His  power  I come  day after day
with You  play  having made  again  I go  to Yadupurī
that  You  consider  My  sphūrti
= To keep You alive I worship Nārāyaṇa, and by His power I come to You every day. After playing with You I go back to Yadupurī, and You take this to be only a vision of Me (*sphūrti*).

@155
My  fortune  for Me  Your  that  love  is
that  love —  supremely  powerful
secretly  Me  it brings  company  it gives  with You
openly too  it will bring  soon
= My good fortune is the love You have for Me. That love is supremely powerful: it brings Me to You secretly and unites Me with You, and soon it will bring Me openly too.

@156
of the Yādavas  the enemies  all  wicked  Kaṁsa’s party
them  I  made  all  destruction
remain  two or four  people  them  killing  to Vṛndāvana
come  I  know  for certain
= I have destroyed all the wicked enemies of the Yādavas, the whole party of Kaṁsa. Only two or four remain; when I have killed them, I will come to Vṛndāvana — know this for certain.

@157
those  enemies  from  the people of Vraja  to protect
I stay  in the kingdom  indifferent  being
whatever  with wives, sons, wealth  I make  of the kingdom  the covering
of the Yadus  satisfaction  for the sake of
= To protect the people of Vraja from those enemies I remain in the kingdom, but indifferent to it. And if I fill the kingdom with wives, sons and wealth, it is only to please the Yadus.

@158
Your  that  quality of love  makes  Me  attraction
will bring  Me  in days  ten  twenty
again  coming  to Vṛndāvana  the maidens of Vraja  with You
I will sport  night and day
= The quality of Your love draws Me, and it will bring Me back within ten or twenty days. Then, coming again to Vṛndāvana, I will sport night and day with You, the maidens of Vraja.”

@159
so much  to Her  having said  Kṛṣṇa  to Vraja  to go  eager
one  verse  reciting  He made her hear
that  verse  hearing  Rādhā  were dispelled  all  obstacles
in attaining Kṛṣṇa  confidence  arose
= Having said all this to Her, Kṛṣṇa, eager to go to Vraja, recited a verse for Her to hear. When Rādhā heard it, all Her doubts were dispelled, and She felt sure that She would attain Kṛṣṇa.

@160
to Me  devotion  indeed  of beings
for immortality  is fit
by good fortune  which  arose  for Me  the love
of you  bringing Me
= “Devotion to Me brings living beings to immortality. And how fortunate you are to have the love that brings you to Me!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.82.44)[^m13-160]

@161
these  all  meanings  the Lord  Svarūpa  with
night and day  in the room  sitting  He makes  relishing
= Night and day the Lord would sit in His room with Svarūpa and relish all these meanings.

@162
at the time of the dance  in that  mood  absorbed  becoming
the verse  reciting  He dances  at Jagannātha’s face  looking
= At the time of the dance, absorbed in that mood, He recited the verse and danced, gazing at Jagannātha’s face.

@163
of Svarūpa Gosāñi  the fortune  not  can  be described
in the Lord  absorbed  whose  body  words  mind
= The good fortune of Svarūpa Gosāñi cannot be described, for his body, words and mind were absorbed in the Lord.

@164
in Svarūpa’s senses  the Lord’s  own senses
entering  becoming  make  of the song  relishing
= The Lord’s own senses entered Svarūpa’s senses, and through them He relished the song.

@165
of feeling  in the absorption  sometimes  on the ground  sitting
with the forefinger  on the ground  He writes  face downward  becoming
= Sometimes, overwhelmed by feeling, He would sit on the ground with His face lowered and write on the ground with His forefinger.

@166
on the finger  injury  will be  knowing  Dāmodara
in fear  with his own hand  stops  the Lord’s hand
= Knowing that the finger would be injured, Dāmodara anxiously stopped the Lord’s hand with his own.

@167
of the Lord  suited to the mood  Svarūpa’s  singing
whenever  whatever  rasa  that  he makes  embodied
= Svarūpa’s singing always matched the Lord’s mood: whatever *rasa* the Lord felt at the moment, his song brought it to life.

@168-169
of Śrī Jagannātha  He sees  the beautiful  lotus face
of it  upon  beautiful  pair of eyes
of the sun  in the rays  the face  makes  glittering
garlands  clothes  divine  ornaments  fragrance
= The Lord gazed at the lotus face of Śrī Jagannātha with its two beautiful eyes, His face glittering in the rays of the sun, and at His garlands, His clothes, His divine ornaments and His fragrance.

@170
of the Lord  in the heart  of bliss  the ocean  surged
madness  a storm wind  at that moment  arose
= An ocean of bliss surged in the Lord’s heart, and at once madness rose in it like a storm wind.
