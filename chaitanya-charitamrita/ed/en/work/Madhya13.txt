@title The Ratha-yātrā

@1
he  may he be victorious  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
before the holy chariot  danced  who
by whom there was  of the worlds  wonder
Jagannātha  even  astonished
= May Kṛṣṇa Caitanya be victorious, who danced before the holy chariot! His dance filled the world with wonder, and even Jagannātha was astonished.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

@3
glory  to the listeners  hear  making  one  mind
at the Ratha-yātrā  the dance  of the Lord  supremely  enchanting
= Glory to you, my listeners! Hear with undivided attention of the Lord’s supremely enchanting dance at the Ratha-yātrā.

@4
the next  day  Mahāprabhu  becoming  watchful
at night  rising  with His people  performed  the morning bath
= The next day Mahāprabhu was on the watch: He rose while it was still night and bathed with His companions before dawn.

@5
the Pāṇḍu-vijaya  to see  made  going
Jagannātha  the journey  made  leaving  the throne
= They went to see the *Pāṇḍu-vijaya*, when Jagannātha leaves His throne and sets out on His journey.

@6
himself  Pratāparudra  taking  his officers
to Mahāprabhu’s  people  causes  the sight of the procession
= King Pratāparudra himself, with his officers, arranged for Mahāprabhu’s companions to see the procession.

@7
Advaita  Nitāi  and others  with Him  the devotees
in joy  Mahāprabhu  watches  the Lord’s going
= With Advaita, Nitāi and the other devotees, Mahāprabhu joyfully watched the Lord set out.

@8
powerful  the dayitās  like  maddened  elephants
Jagannātha  the procession  cause to make  doing  hand to hand
= The powerful *dayitās*, like maddened elephants, carried Jagannātha forward, passing Him from hand to hand.

@9
some  dayitās  make  of the shoulders  support
some  dayitās  hold  the holy lotus feet
= Some *dayitās* supported His shoulders, and others held His holy lotus feet.

@10
at the waist  tied  strong  thick  silk rope
on two  sides  the dayitās  lift  it  holding
= A strong, thick silk rope was tied around His waist, and holding it from both sides the *dayitās* lifted Him.

@11
high  firm  cotton cushions  all  spreading  at places  at places
one  cushion  from  quickly  to another  cushion  they bring
= Thick, firm cushions of cotton were laid down one after another, and they quickly moved Him from one cushion to the next.

@12
by the strike of the Lord’s feet  the cushions  become  pieces  pieces
the cotton  all  flying  goes  a sound  is  tremendous
= Under the strike of the Lord’s feet the cushions burst to pieces, and the cotton flew everywhere with a tremendous sound.

@13
the sustainer of the universe  Jagannātha  who  to move  can
by His own  will  He moves  to enjoy  His pastimes
= Who can move Jagannātha, the sustainer of the universe? He moves by His own will, to enjoy His pastimes.

@14
Mahāprabhu  “Maṇimā”  “Maṇimā”  makes  the call
in the din of many instruments  nothing  not  is heard
= Mahāprabhu called out, “*Maṇimā*! *Maṇimā*!” — but in the din of so many instruments nothing could be heard.

@15
then  Pratāparudra  does  himself  service
a golden broom  taking  does  of the road  sweeping
= Then Pratāparudra served in person: taking a golden broom, he swept the road.

@16
with sandalwood water  does  of the road  sprinkling
menial  service  does  though seated  on the royal throne
= He sprinkled the road with sandalwood water. He sat on the royal throne, yet he did this menial service.

@17
exalted  being  the king  does  menial  service
therefore  of Jagannātha  of the mercy  the recipient
= Exalted as he was, the king did menial service; therefore he became a recipient of Jagannātha’s mercy.

@18
Mahāprabhu  happiness  felt  that service  seeing
Mahāprabhu’s  mercy  arose  that service  from
= Mahāprabhu was happy to see this service, and through it the king won Mahāprabhu’s mercy.

@19
of the chariot  the decoration  seeing  the people  amazed
new  golden  the chariot  Sumeru  in shape
= The people marvelled at the decoration of the chariot. It was new and golden, towering like Mount Sumeru.

@20-21
hundreds  hundreds  with fine cāmaras and mirrors  shining
above  a flag  shines  a canopy  spotless
little bells  tinkling bells  sound  of the bells  the ringing
with many  painted silk cloths  the chariot  adorned
= Hundreds upon hundreds of fine *cāmaras* and mirrors made it shine. Above, a flag flew and a spotless canopy gleamed. Little bells jingled and tinkled, and the large bells rang. The chariot was adorned with many painted silk cloths.

@22
in play  mounted  the Lord  the chariot  upon
on the other  two  chariots  mount  Subhadrā  Haladhara
= In His play the Lord mounted the chariot, and Subhadrā and Haladhara mounted the other two.

@23
fifteen  days  the Lord  Mahā-Lakṣmī  with
with her  sport  enjoyed  in seclusion  staying
= For fifteen days the Lord had stayed in seclusion with Mahā-Lakṣmī, enjoying His pastimes with her.

@24
her  consent  taking  to the devotees  joy  to give
on the chariot  mounting  went out  pastimes  to enjoy
= Now, with her consent, He mounted the chariot and went out to enjoy His pastimes and give joy to His devotees.

@25
fine  white sand  on the road  a riverbank  like
on two  sides  gardens  all  like  Vṛndāvana
= The road was covered with fine white sand like the bank of a river, and the gardens on both sides were like Vṛndāvana.

@26
on the chariot  mounted  Jagannātha  made  His going
on both sides  looking  goes  joyful at heart
= Riding on His chariot, Jagannātha went along, looking at both sides with a joyful heart.

@27
the gauḍas  all  the chariot  pull  feeling  joy
at times  fast  goes  the chariot  at times  goes  slowly
= The *gauḍas* pulled the chariot joyfully. At times it moved fast, at times slowly.

@28
at times  still  becoming  it stays  even when pulled  not  moves
by the Lord’s  will  it moves  not  moves  by anyone’s  strength
= At times it stood still and would not move, however they pulled. It moved by the Lord’s will, not by anyone’s strength.

@29
then  Mahāprabhu  all  taking  the devotees
with His own hand  put on  all  garlands and sandal
= Then Mahāprabhu gathered all the devotees and with His own hand adorned each of them with garlands and sandalwood paste.

@30
Paramānanda  Purī  and  Bhāratī  Brahmānanda
from the holy hand  sandal  receiving  increased  joy
= Paramānanda Purī and Brahmānanda Bhāratī received sandalwood paste from His holy hand, and their joy grew.

@31
Advaita Ācārya  and  Prabhu Nityānanda
by the touch of the holy hand  of both  was  joy
= Advaita Ācārya and Nityānanda Prabhu also rejoiced at the touch of His holy hand.

@32
to the kīrtana singers  gave  garlands and sandal
Svarūpa  Śrīvāsa  among whom  the chief  two
= He gave garlands and sandalwood paste to the *kīrtana* singers, the chief of whom were Svarūpa and Śrīvāsa.

@33
in four  groups  there were  twenty-four  singers
two  two  mṛdaṅgas  counting  there were  eight  men
= There were twenty-four singers in four groups, and with two *mṛdaṅga* players for each group, eight more men.

@34
then  Mahāprabhu  in His mind  consideration  making
four  groups  gave  the singers  dividing
= Then, after thinking it over, Mahāprabhu divided the singers into four groups.

@notes
[^2]: The same verse as Ādi 10.2; translation as there.
