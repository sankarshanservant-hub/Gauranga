@title Śrī Caitanya Tastes the Nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s Lips

@1
I bow down  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
of Kṛṣṇa  of love  the nectar  indeed  who
having tasted  making taste  the devotees
of prema  the initiation  taught
= I bow down to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who Himself tasted the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa and made His devotees taste it; so He taught them initiation into *prema*.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  Mahāprabhu  stays  in Nīlācala
the devotees  with  always  by love  overwhelmed
= In this way Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala with His devotees, always overwhelmed by love.

@4
the next year  came  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees
as before  coming  made  with the Lord  meeting
= The next year all the devotees of Gauḍa came again and, as before, met the Lord.

@5
them all  with  came  Kālidāsa  by name
Kṛṣṇa’s  name  except  he  not  knew  anything else
= With them came a devotee named Kālidāsa, who knew nothing but the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@6
a great  devotee  he  simple  generous
of Kṛṣṇa’s  name  by the signs  conducted  his dealings
= He was a great devotee, simple and generous, and he conducted all his dealings with the name of Kṛṣṇa as his signal.

@7
in fun  he  if  at dice  played
Hare Kṛṣṇa  Hare Kṛṣṇa  saying  the dice  threw
= If he played at dice for fun, he would throw the dice saying, “Hare Kṛṣṇa! Hare Kṛṣṇa!”

@8
of Raghunātha  dāsa  he  was  a kinsman  uncle
of Vaiṣṇavas  the remnants  eating  he  became  old
= He was an uncle of Raghunātha dāsa by kinship, and he grew old eating the remnants of Vaiṣṇavas.

@9
in the land of Gauḍa  are  as many  of Vaiṣṇavas  as
of all  the remnants  he  did  eat
= He ate the remnants of all the Vaiṣṇavas there were in Gauḍa.

@10
brāhmaṇa  Vaiṣṇavas  as many as  small  great  are
the best  things  as gifts  taking  to them  goes
= To all the Vaiṣṇavas of brāhmaṇa birth, small and great, he would go with the finest things as gifts.

@11
from them  the plates of remnants  takes  begging
wherever  not  gets  then  stays  hiding
= From them he would beg the plates with their remnants; where he did not get them, he would stay hidden nearby.

@12
eating  having done  the plate  throwing away  goes
secretly  that  plate  bringing  licking  eats
= When a Vaiṣṇava had eaten and thrown away his plate, he would secretly bring that plate and lick it clean.

@13
of śūdra  Vaiṣṇavas  to the homes  goes  gifts  taking
in this way  their  remnants  eats  secretly
= He went with gifts to the homes of Vaiṣṇavas of śūdra birth too, and in the same way secretly ate their remnants.

@14
bhūṅimāli  by caste  a Vaiṣṇava  Jhaḍu  his  name
mangoes  taking  he  went  to his  place
= There was a Vaiṣṇava named Jhaḍu, of the *bhūṅimāli* caste. Kālidāsa went to his home, taking mangoes.

@15
the mangoes  as a gift  giving  his  feet  he honored
his  wife  then  obeisance  made
= He offered him the mangoes, bowed at his feet, and then offered obeisance to his wife.

@16
his wife  with  he  was  seated
much  honor  showed  Kālidāsa  seeing
= Jhaḍu was sitting with his wife; seeing Kālidāsa, he received him with great respect.

@17
friendly talk  for some time  having made  with him
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  says  to him  in sweet  words
= After talking with him a while, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura said to him sweetly:

@18
I  low  born  you  guest  the most excellent
in what  way  shall I do  you  service
= “I am of low birth, and you are a most honored guest. How shall I serve you?

@19
permission  give  a brāhmaṇa’s  to the house  provisions  taking  I will give
there  you  prasāda  take  then  I  shall live
= Give me leave to take provisions to a brāhmaṇa’s house. There you can take *prasāda*, and then I shall live.”

@20
Kālidāsa  says  Ṭhākura  mercy  show  to me
your  for the sight  have come  I  fallen  sinner
= Kālidāsa said, “Ṭhākura, be merciful to me! I, a fallen sinner, have come to see you.

@21
pure  have become  I  have gained  the sight
fulfilled  I have become  my  successful  life
= By seeing you I have become pure. My purpose is fulfilled, and my life has borne its fruit.

@22
one  desire  there is  if  mercy  showing  you do
of the feet  the dust  give  your foot  my  on the head  place
= I have one desire, if you will be merciful: give me the dust of your feet, and place your foot on my head.”

@23
the Ṭhākura  says  such  words  to speak  not  is fitting
I  low  born  you  a noble  gentleman
= The Ṭhākura said, “It is not fitting to say such things. I am of low birth, and you are a noble gentleman.”

@24
then  Kālidāsa  verses  reciting  made him hear
hearing  Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura’s  great  joy  was
= Then Kālidāsa recited verses for him, and hearing them, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura was greatly pleased.

@25
not  to Me  a nondevotee  a knower of the four Vedas
My devotee  a dog-eater  dear
to him  should be given  from him  should be accepted
he  and  is to be worshipped  as  indeed  I
= “Even a knower of the four Vedas is not dear to Me if he is not devoted to Me, but My devotee is dear to Me even if he was born a dog-eater. One should give to him and accept from him; he is to be worshipped just as I am.” (Itihāsa-samuccaya, quoted in Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 10.91)

@26
than a brāhmaṇa  twelve  qualities  endowed with  of the lotus-naveled Lord
from the lotus feet  averse  a dog-eater  better
I consider  to Him offered  mind  words  deeds  wealth
and life  purifies  he  his family  not  but  one full of pride
= “I consider a dog-eater whose mind, words, deeds, wealth and very life are offered to the Lord of the lotus navel better than a brāhmaṇa endowed with the twelve qualities who has turned away from His lotus feet. Such a devotee purifies his whole family; the brāhmaṇa swollen with pride cannot.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.10)

@27
oh  how wonderful  a dog-eater  therefore  most worthy
on the tip of whose tongue  is present  the name  Your
have performed  austerities  they  have offered sacrifice  have bathed (in all holy waters)  noble ones
have recited the Vedas  the name  utter  who  Your
= “Oh, how wonderful! Even a dog-eater is worthy of the highest honour if Your name is on the tip of his tongue. Those who utter Your name have already performed austerities and offered sacrifices; they have bathed in all the holy waters, they are truly noble, and they have studied the Vedas.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.33.7)

@28
hearing  the Ṭhākura  says  scripture  this  true  is
he  low  is not  in whom  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is spoken of
= Hearing this, the Ṭhākura said, “What scripture says is true: one in whom there is devotion to Kṛṣṇa is not low.[^28-1]

@29
I  low  born  my  is no  devotion to Kṛṣṇa
others  such  are  in me  is no  such  power
= But I am of low birth, and I have no devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Others may be such, but I have no such power.”

@30
to him  bowing  Kālidāsa  leave  asked
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  then  him  following  went
= Bowing to him, Kālidāsa asked leave to go, and Jhaḍu Ṭhākura walked after him to see him off.

@31
him  leave  giving  the Ṭhākura  when  home  came
his  feet’s  marks  where  fell
= When the Ṭhākura had seen him off and returned home, Kālidāsa went to the place where his footprints lay.

@32
that  dust  taking  Kālidāsa  all over his body  smeared
him  near  in one place  hiding  stayed
= Kālidāsa took that dust and smeared it all over his body, and then he hid near Jhaḍu’s house.

@33
Jhaḍu  Ṭhākura  home  going  seeing  the mangoes
in his mind alone  to Kṛṣṇacandra  offered  all
= On reaching home Jhaḍu Ṭhākura saw the mangoes and offered them all to Kṛṣṇacandra within his mind.

@34
banana  leaves  bark  from  the mangoes  taking out
his  wife  to him  gives  he eats  sucking
= His wife took the mangoes out of the banana leaves and bark in which they were wrapped and handed them to him, and he sucked them.

@35
sucking  sucking  the sucked  stones  threw  into the wrappings
him  having fed  his  wife  eats  afterwards
= He sucked them and threw the sucked stones into the wrappings. When she had fed him, his wife ate afterwards.

@36
stones  sucked  those  wrappings  filling

= She filled those wrappings with the sucked stones, took them out, and threw them into the pit for leftovers.

@37
those  wrappings  stones  skins  sucks  Kālidāsa
sucking  sucking  comes  in love  jubilation
= Kālidāsa sucked those wrappings, stones and skins, and as he sucked them he was jubilant with love.

@38
in this way  as many  Vaiṣṇavas  live  in the land of Gauḍa
Kālidāsa  thus  of all  took  the remnants
= In this way Kālidāsa took the remnants of all the Vaiṣṇavas who lived in the land of Gauḍa.

@39

Mahāprabhu  on him  great  mercy  showed
= When this Kālidāsa came to Nīlācala, Mahāprabhu showed him great mercy.

@40

= Every day, when the Lord went to see Lord Jagannātha, Govinda went with Him carrying His waterpot.

@41

= On the north side of the Siṁha-dvāra, behind the door panel, at the foot of the twenty-two steps, there was a low pit.

@42

= In that pit the Lord would wash His feet, and then He would go to see the Lord.

@43

= Mahāprabhu had given Govinda a rule: “See that no one takes the water that washes My feet.”

@44

intimate  devotees  take  making  some  pretext
= No living being could take that water; only intimate devotees took it, on one pretext or another.

@45
one  day  the Lord  there  feet  to wash
Kālidāsa  coming  there  held out  his hands
= One day, as the Lord was washing His feet there, Kālidāsa came and held out his cupped hands.

@46
one  palmful  two  palmfuls  three  palmfuls  he drank
then  Mahāprabhu  him  prohibition  made
= He drank one palmful, two palmfuls, three palmfuls, and then Mahāprabhu stopped him:

@47

thus far  of your desire  the fulfilment  I have made  your
= “From now on, do not do this again. Thus far I have fulfilled your desire.”

@48
of the omniscient  the crest jewel  Caitanya  the Lord
in Vaiṣṇavas  his  faith  knows  the heart
= Caitanya, the Lord, the crest jewel of the omniscient, knew Kālidāsa’s heart and his faith in Vaiṣṇavas.

@49
that  quality  on account of  the Lord  with him  pleased  became
to others  rare  mercy  to him  showed
= Pleased with him for that quality, the Lord showed him a mercy rarely granted to others.

@50
twenty-two  steps  behind  above  on the south  side
one  Nṛsiṁha  Deity  is present  going up  on the left side
= Behind the twenty-two steps, above them on the south side, to the left as one goes up, is a Deity of Lord Nṛsiṁha.

@51
every  day  to Him  the Lord  makes  obeisance

= Every day the Lord bowed down to Him and, bowing, recited this verse again and again:

@52

of Hiraṇyakaśipu  the chest
the stone  chisel  whose nails
= “Obeisance to You, Lord Narasiṁha, who give joy to Prahlāda, and whose nails are a chisel on the stone of Hiraṇyakaśipu’s chest.” (Nṛsiṁha-purāṇa)

@53

in Nṛsiṁha  the original  shelter  I take
= “Nṛsiṁha is here, Nṛsiṁha is there; wherever I go, there is Nṛsiṁha. Nṛsiṁha is outside, Nṛsiṁha is in the heart. I take shelter of Nṛsiṁha, the original Lord.” (Nṛsiṁha-purāṇa)

@54

home  coming  made  the midday  meal
= Then the Lord took *darśana* of Jagannātha, and coming home, He took His midday meal.

@55

= Kālidāsa was waiting hopefully at the outer door. Knowing this, the Lord gave Govinda a sign.

@56

to Kālidāsa  gave  the Lord’s  plate of remnants  as a gift
= Govinda understood all the Lord’s signs, and he gave Kālidāsa the Lord’s plate of remnants.

@57
of Vaiṣṇavas  remnants  of eating  such  greatness
to Kālidāsa  made obtain  the Lord’s  mercy  the limit
= Such is the greatness of eating the remnants of Vaiṣṇavas: it brought Kālidāsa the utmost limit of the Lord’s mercy.

@58

which  from  you will obtain  desired  all  aims
= Therefore, giving up disgust and shame, eat the leftovers (*jhuṭā*) of Vaiṣṇavas; through them you will obtain all you desire.

@59

devotee’s  remnants  if they become  mahā-mahā-prasāda  called
= The remnants of Kṛṣṇa are called *mahā-prasāda*; when they become the remnants of a devotee, they are called *mahā-mahā-prasāda*.

@60

by the devotee  eaten  remnants  these  three  of practice  the strength
= The dust of a devotee’s feet, the water that has washed his feet, and the remnants of what he has eaten — these three are the strength of spiritual practice.

@61

again  again  in all  scriptures  loudly  they declare
= By serving these three one attains love for Kṛṣṇa; all the scriptures declare this loudly again and again.

@62

faith  having  do  these three  service
= Therefore I say again and again: listen, devotees! Serve these three with faith.

@63

Kṛṣṇa’s  grace  of that  witness  Kālidāsa
= From these three come the jubilation of Kṛṣṇa’s name and of love for Him, and Kṛṣṇa’s grace. Kālidāsa is the witness to this.

@64
in Nīlācala  Mahāprabhu  stays  in this way
to Kālidāsa  great  mercy  showed  unnoticed
= In this way Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala, and without anyone noticing He showed Kālidāsa great mercy.

@65

= That year Śivānanda came with his wife and brought with him his youngest son, Purī dāsa.

= Taking his son, he came to the Lord and had the boy bow at the Lord’s feet.

= Śivānanda tried hard with the boy, but still he did not say the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@69

= The Lord said, “I have made the whole world take the name; I have made even the unmoving creatures say the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@70

= Yet this boy I could not make say the name of Kṛṣṇa!” Hearing this, Svarūpa Gosāñi said:

@71

= “You have instructed him in the mantra of Kṛṣṇa’s name. Having received the mantra, he will not disclose it before anyone.

@72

= He chants it within his mind but does not utter it aloud. This, I guess, is what is in his mind.”

@73

= Another day the Lord said, “Recite, Purī dāsa!” and the boy composed and recited this verse:

@74

= He was a child of seven, who had not studied, yet he composed such a verse! Everyone was amazed.

= Such is the greatness of Caitanya Prabhu’s mercy; even Brahmā and the other gods cannot find its limit.

= The devotees stayed with the Lord for four months; then the Lord gave them leave, and they all went back to Gauḍa.

= In their company the Lord kept His external awareness, but when they had gone, His divine madness again prevailed.

= Night and day Kṛṣṇa’s form, fragrance and taste appeared to Him; He experienced them directly, as if Kṛṣṇa were touching Him.

= The Lord said to him, “Where is Kṛṣṇa, the Lord of My life? Show Me Kṛṣṇa!” — and He took his hand.

= Raghunātha dāsa has described this pastime in his own book, the *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*:

@87

= “‘Where is My beloved Kṛṣṇa? That Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, 7)

@94
