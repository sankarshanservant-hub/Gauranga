# CC Madhya 6, stage A, part 2 (verses 144–287); read by build_en.py together with Madhya06.txt

@144
“apādāna” (source)  “karaṇa” (instrument)  “adhikaraṇa” (locus) — cases  three
of Bhagavān  of possessing attributes  these  three  signs
= These three cases — the ablative (*apādāna*), the instrumental (*karaṇa*) and the locative (*adhikaraṇa*) — are the three signs that Bhagavān possesses attributes.

@145
the Lord  many  to become  when  He made  His mind
upon the material energy  then  He cast  a glance
= When Bhagavān resolved to become many, He cast His glance upon the material energy.

@146
at that  time  not  are born  material  mind and eyes
therefore  non-material  of Brahman  the eyes and mind
= At that time no material mind or eyes had yet come into being; therefore the eyes and mind of Brahman are not material.

@147
by the word “Brahman”  is meant  the complete  Svayaṁ  Bhagavān
Svayaṁ  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of scripture  the evidence
= The word “Brahman” denotes the complete Svayaṁ Bhagavān, the Lord Himself, and the Lord Himself is Kṛṣṇa — such is the evidence of scripture.

@148
of the Vedas  the hidden  meaning  understanding  not  is
by the words of the Purāṇas  that  meaning  is made  certain
= The hidden meaning of the Vedas is not easily understood, and the words of the Purāṇas make that meaning certain.

@149
oh  the fortune  oh  the fortune
of Nanda  the cowherds  the residents of Vraja
whose friend  the supreme bliss
the complete  Brahman  eternal
= “Oh, the fortune, oh, the fortune of Nanda, the cowherds and all the residents of Vraja, whose friend is the supreme bliss, the complete and eternal Brahman!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.32)

@150
the “apāṇi-pāda” śruti  rejects  material  hands and feet
again  it says  swiftly  He moves  He makes  of everything  acceptance
= The śruti text “apāṇi-pāda” (“without hands and feet”) denies Him material hands and feet; yet it says again that He moves swiftly and accepts all things.

@151
therefore  the śruti  says  Brahman  possessed of attributes
the primary sense  abandoning  by a secondary sense  they take  as featureless
= Therefore the śruti declares that Brahman possesses attributes; yet, abandoning the primary sense for a secondary one, they hold Him to be featureless.

@152
full of the six opulences, of bliss  the form  whose
such a Bhagavān  you  call  formless
= His form is bliss itself, full of the six opulences — and such a Bhagavān you call formless!

@153
natural  three  potencies  which  in Brahman  are
“without potency”  making  Him  you make  your conclusion
= Brahman has three natural potencies, yet you conclude that He has no potency at all.

@154
Viṣṇu’s potency  superior  is declared
called the knower of the field  likewise superior
called ignorance and karma  another
a third  potency  is held to be
= “The potency of Viṣṇu is declared to be superior; the potency called the knower of the field (*kṣetrajña*) is likewise superior; and the other, called ignorance and karma, is held to be a third potency.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 6.7.61)

@155
by which  the knower of the field  potency  that
enveloped  O king  going everywhere
of worldly existence  the miseries  all
obtains  here  continual
= “Enveloped by that, O king, the potency called the knower of the field, though it can go everywhere, suffers here all the miseries of worldly existence without end.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 6.7.62)

@156
by it  because of being covered  and
the potency  the knower of the field  called
in all beings  O king
in varying degrees  exists
= “And because it is covered by that, O king, the potency called the knower of the field exists in all beings in varying degrees.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa 6.7.63)

@157
hlādinī  sandhinī  samvit
in You  one  of all  the shelter
of joy  of pain  causing  mixed
in You  not  of the guṇas  devoid
= “O Lord, You are the basis of all, and in You Hlādinī, Sandhinī and Samvit are one potency. But that which causes joy, pain and a mixture of the two is not in You, for You are beyond the guṇas.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 1.12.69)

@158
made of eternity, knowledge and bliss  is  the Lord’s  own nature
in three  aspects  the spiritual potency  is  three  forms
= The Lord’s own nature is made of eternity, knowledge and bliss, and in these three aspects His spiritual potency takes three forms.

@159
in the aspect of bliss  hlādinī  in the aspect of being  sandhinī
in the aspect of knowledge  samvit  which  knowledge of Kṛṣṇa  we hold
= In the aspect of bliss it is *hlādinī*, in the aspect of being *sandhinī*, and in the aspect of knowledge *samvit*, which we regard as knowledge of Kṛṣṇa.

@160
internal  the spiritual potency  marginal  the potency of the jīvas
external  māyā  all three  render  loving devotion
= The spiritual potency is internal, the potency of the jīvas is marginal, and māyā is external — and all three render loving service.

@161
of six  kinds  opulence  the Lord’s  spiritual potency’s  play
such  potency  not  you accept  utmost  audacity
= The six kinds of opulence are the play of the Lord’s spiritual potency. That you do not accept such a potency is the height of audacity.

@162
“the master of māyā”  “subject to māyā”  between the Lord  and the jīva  the difference
such a jīva  with the Lord  you call  indeed  non-different
= The Lord is the master of māyā, and the jīva is subject to māyā — this is the difference between them. Yet you call such a jīva one with the Lord.

@163
in the Gītā  the jīva  as  a “potency”  is accepted
such  a jīva  “non-different”  you call  from the Lord  with
= In the Gītā the jīva is accepted as a potency, yet you call such a jīva non-different from the Lord.

@164
earth  water  fire  air
ether  mind  intelligence  indeed  and
false ego  thus this  My
separated  nature  eightfold
= “Earth, water, fire, air, ether, mind, intelligence and false ego — these eight make up My separated nature.” (Bhagavad-gītā 7.4)

@165
inferior  this  beyond this  but  another
nature  know  My  superior
consisting of jīvas  O mighty-armed
by which  this  is taken up  the world
= “This nature is inferior. But know, O mighty-armed, that I have another, superior nature, consisting of the jīvas, who take up this world for their own enjoyment.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.5)

@166
the Lord’s  sacred  form  of eternity, knowledge and bliss in shape
that form  you call  of the mode of goodness  a transformation
= The Lord’s sacred form is made of eternity, knowledge and bliss, yet you call that form a transformation of the mode of goodness.

@167
the sacred  form  who  not  accepts  he  indeed  godless
untouchable  not to be looked at  he  becomes  punishable by Yama
= Whoever does not accept the Lord’s sacred form is godless (*pāṣaṇḍī*). He is not to be touched or even looked at, and Yama will punish him.

@168
the Vedas  not  accepting  the Buddhist  is  indeed  an unbeliever
sheltered in the Vedas  atheistic doctrine  than the Buddhist  worse
= The Buddhist, who does not accept the Vedas, is an unbeliever (*nāstika*); but an atheistic doctrine that takes shelter in the Vedas is worse than Buddhism.

@169
of the jīvas  the deliverance  for  the sūtras  composed  Vyāsa
the Māyāvādī commentary  if one hears  there is  utter ruin
= Vyāsa composed the sūtras for the deliverance of the jīvas, but whoever hears the Māyāvādī commentary on them is utterly ruined.

@170
the doctrine of transformation  by Vyāsa’s sūtras  is upheld
of inconceivable potency  the Lord  in the form of the world  is transformed
= The doctrine of transformation (*pariṇāma-vāda*) is the view of Vyāsa’s sūtras: the Lord, whose potency is inconceivable, transforms into the world.

@171
a gem  as  unchanged  brings forth  loads of gold
the form of the world  becomes  the Lord  still  unchanged
= As a gem, itself unchanged, brings forth loads of gold, so the Lord becomes the world and yet remains unchanged.

@172
Vyāsa  is mistaken  saying  to those  sūtras  fault  ascribing
the doctrine of illusion  he has established  fancy  making
= Saying that Vyāsa was mistaken and finding fault with his sūtras, he has set up the doctrine of illusion (*vivarta-vāda*) by his own invention.

@173
of the jīva  in the body  the notion of self  that  false  is
the world  but  false  is not  perishable  only  is
= What is false is the jīva’s sense of self in the body. The world is not false — it is only perishable.

@174
praṇava  which  the great statement  of the Lord  the form
from praṇava  all the Vedas  to the world  origin
= The *praṇava* (Oṁ), which is the great statement (*mahā-vākya*), is the form of the Lord. From the praṇava come all the Vedas and the world.

@175
“tat tvam asi”  for the jīva’s sake  partial  statement
the praṇava  not  accepting  it  he calls  the great statement
= “Tat tvam asi” is a partial statement, meant for the jīva; yet, not accepting the praṇava, he calls that the great statement.”

@176
thus  in the fanciful  commentary  hundreds of  faults  He showed
the Bhaṭṭācārya  objections  without end  made
= In this way the Lord pointed out hundreds of faults in that fanciful commentary, and the Bhaṭṭācārya raised countless objections.

@177
captious wrangling  quibbles  points of defeat and the like  many  he raised
all  refuting  the Lord  His own view  that  established
= He brought up much captious wrangling (*vitaṇḍā*), quibbling (*chala*), points of defeat (*nigraha*) and the like, but the Lord refuted them all and established His own view.

@178
Bhagavān  sambandha  bhakti  abhidheya  is
love  prayojana  in the Vedas  three  realities  are stated
= “Bhagavān is the relationship (*sambandha*), bhakti is the means (*abhidheya*), and love is the goal (*prayojana*) — these three truths the Vedas declare.

@179
else  whatever  anything  is said  all of it  fancy
self-evident  in the words of the Veda  not  I apply  a secondary meaning
= Whatever else anyone says is all fancy. In the self-evident words of the Veda I do not resort to a secondary meaning.

@180
of the Ācārya  fault  there is not  the Lord’s order  there was
therefore  fancy  making  an atheistic scripture  he made
= The Ācārya (Śaṅkara) is not at fault: it was the Lord’s order. That is why he invented an atheistic scripture.”

@181
by your own scriptures  imagined  you  and
people  averse to Me  make
Me  and  hide  so that  may be
creation  this  ever increasing
= “By scriptures of your own imagining, turn people away from Me, and hide Me, so that this creation may go on increasing more and more.” (Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa 62.31)

@182
Māyāvāda  a false scripture
disguised  Buddhism  is called
by me  indeed  set forth  O Devī
in Kali  in the form of a brāhmaṇa
= “Māyāvāda is a false scripture; it is called Buddhism in disguise. It was I, O Devī, who set it forth in the age of Kali in the form of a brāhmaṇa.” (Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa 25.7)

@183
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya  became  utterly  astonished
from his mouth  not  comes out  a word  he became  stunned
= Hearing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was utterly astonished. Not a word left his lips; he sat stunned.

@184
the Lord  says  Bhaṭṭācārya  do not  make  wonder
to Bhagavān  devotion  the highest goal of life  is
= The Lord said, “Do not be amazed, Bhaṭṭācārya. Devotion to Bhagavān is the highest goal of human life.

@185
the ātmārāmas  even  do  of the Lord  worship
such  inconceivable  Bhagavān’s  qualities
= Even those who delight in the self (*ātmārāma*) worship the Lord — so inconceivable are the qualities of Bhagavān.”

@186
delighting in the self  and  the sages
free from all knots  even  to Urukrama
perform  causeless  devotion
of such  qualities  is Hari
= “Even sages who delight in the self and are free from all knots render causeless devotion to Urukrama, for such are the qualities of Hari.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.7.10)

@187
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  listen  sir
of this  verse  the meaning  to hear  desire  there is
= Hearing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Listen, my dear sir (*mahāśaya*): I wish to hear the meaning of this verse.”

@188
the Lord  says  you  what  meaning  make  that  first  I will hear
afterwards  I  will make  meaning  whatever  little  I know
= The Lord said, “First let Me hear what meaning you give it. Afterwards I shall explain it, as far as I know.”

@189
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya  of the verse  made  an explanation
according to logic  raises  various  propositions
= Hearing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya explained the verse, raising various propositions in the manner of the science of logic.

@190
of nine kinds  meanings  he gave  scriptural views  taking
hearing  the Lord  says  something  slightly  smiling
= He gave nine different meanings on the authority of scripture. Hearing them, the Lord smiled slightly and said:

@191
Bhaṭṭācārya  I know  you  the very  Bṛhaspati
of scripture  explanation  to make  like this  no one’s  there is not  power
= “Bhaṭṭācārya, I know that you are Bṛhaspati himself. No one else has the power to explain scripture like this.

@192
but  you  meaning  made  by the brilliance of scholarship
this  besides  of the verse  there is  further  intent
= But you have explained it by the brilliance of your scholarship; beyond this, the verse has another intent.”

@193
the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  at the request  the Lord  explanation  made
his  nine  meanings  among  one  not  He touched
= At the Bhaṭṭācārya’s request the Lord explained the verse, without touching even one of his nine meanings.

@194
in the “ātmārāmāś ca” verse  eleven  words  there are
separately  separately  He made  of the words  the meaning  certain
= The verse “ātmārāmāś ca” has eleven words, and He established the meaning of each word separately.

@195
to each word  giving prominence  with “ātmārāma”  combining
eighteen  meanings  He made  the intent  taking
= Giving prominence to each word in turn and combining it with “ātmārāma”, He brought out eighteen meanings according to the verse’s intent.

@196
Bhagavān  His  potency  His  qualities
inconceivable  power  of the three  not  can be  described
= “Bhagavān, His potency and His qualities — the inconceivable power of these three cannot be described.

@197
other  all  goals and means  making  eclipse
these  three  steal  of the perfected and the practitioners  the minds
= Eclipsing every other goal and means, these three steal the hearts of the perfected and of those who still practise.”

@198
Sanaka and the others, Śukadeva  of this  the proof
in this way  various  meanings  He makes  explanation
= Sanaka and the other Kumāras, and Śukadeva, are proof of this. In this way the Lord explained many meanings.

@199
hearing  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  in the mind  there was  wonder
the Lord  as Kṛṣṇa  knowing  he does  himself  reproach
= Hearing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was struck with wonder. Knowing the Lord to be Kṛṣṇa, he began to reproach himself:

@200
He  indeed  directly  Kṛṣṇa  I  not  knowing
a great offence  I committed  proud  being
= “He is Kṛṣṇa Himself, and I, not knowing it, have committed a great offence in my pride.”

@201
self-reproach  making  he took  of the Lord  shelter
mercy  to show  then  of the Lord  arose  the mind
= Reproaching himself, he took shelter of the Lord, and the Lord then resolved to show him mercy.

@202
His own form  the Lord  to him  caused  to see
four-armed in form  the Lord  became  then
= The Lord showed him His own form: He became four-armed.

@203
He showed  to him  first  the four-armed form
then  dark  with the flute at the lips  His very own  form
= First He showed him the four-armed form, and then His very own form — dark, with the flute at His lips.

@204
seeing  Sārvabhauma  obeisance  making  falling down
again  rising  prayers  he offers  two  hands  joining
= Seeing this, Sārvabhauma fell down flat in obeisance; then he rose and, with folded hands, offered prayers.

@205
by the Lord’s  mercy  to him  were revealed  all  truths
of the holy name  of love  the giving  and so on  he describes  the greatness
= By the Lord’s mercy all truths were revealed to him, and he described the greatness of the holy name, of the gift of love and the rest.

@206
a hundred  verses  he composed  one  daṇḍa  not  passing
Bṛhaspati  such  verses  not  can  compose
= Within a single *daṇḍa* (twenty-four minutes) he composed a hundred verses — verses such as even Bṛhaspati could not compose.

@207
hearing  with joy  the Lord  him  made  an embrace
the Bhaṭṭācārya  in the rapture of love  became  unconscious
= Hearing them, the Lord joyfully embraced him, and the Bhaṭṭācārya, overwhelmed by love, lost consciousness.

@208
tears  stupor  thrill  sweat  trembling  violent
he dances  sings  weeps  falls  the Lord’s feet  clasping
= Tears, stupor, thrill, sweat and violent trembling came over him. He danced, sang and wept, and fell down clasping the Lord’s feet.

@209
seeing  Gopīnāthācārya  delighted at heart
the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  dance  seeing  laugh  the Lord’s  companions
= Seeing this, Gopīnāthācārya was delighted at heart, and the Lord’s companions laughed to see the Bhaṭṭācārya dance.

@210
Gopīnāthācārya  says  Mahāprabhu  to
that very  Bhaṭṭācārya’s  You  have made  this  state
= Gopīnāthācārya said to Mahāprabhu, “It is that very Bhaṭṭācārya whom You have brought to such a state!”

@211
the Lord  says  you  a devotee  your  association  from
Jagannātha  to him  mercy  showed  fully
= The Lord said, “You are a devotee, and through association with you Jagannātha has shown him full mercy.”

@212
then  the Bhaṭṭācārya  the Lord  calm  made
calm  becoming  the Bhaṭṭācārya  many  prayers  made
= Then the Lord calmed the Bhaṭṭācārya, and the Bhaṭṭācārya, once composed, offered many prayers.

@213
the world  have delivered  You  that  a small deed
me  have delivered  You  this  power  wonderful
= “That You have delivered the world is a small deed; that You have delivered me — that is a wondrous power!

@214
by logic  dulled  I  like  a lump of iron
me  have melted  You  power  mighty
= Logic had made me as hard and dull as a lump of iron, yet You have melted me — mighty is Your power!”

@215
the prayers  hearing  Mahāprabhu  to His own lodging  came
the Bhaṭṭācārya  through the Ācārya  a meal  arranged
= Having heard these prayers, Mahāprabhu returned to His lodging, and the Bhaṭṭācārya had a meal served to Him through Gopīnāthācārya.
