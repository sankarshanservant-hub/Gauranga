# CC Madhya 9, stage A, part 2 (verses 183–365); read by build_en.py together with Madhya09.txt

@183
the brāhmaṇa  said  O Lord  my  in the forest  dwelling
for cooking  provisions  in the forest  not  are found  at present
= The brāhmaṇa said, “O Lord, I live in the forest, and at present the things needed for cooking are not to be had here.

@184
wild  greens, fruits and roots  will bring  Lakṣmaṇa
then  Sītā  will do  what is needed for cooking
= Lakṣmaṇa will bring wild greens, fruits and roots, and then Sītā will do the cooking.”

@185
his  worship  hearing  the Lord  pleased  became
in great haste  that  brāhmaṇa  cooking  did
= The Lord was pleased to hear of his way of worship, and the brāhmaṇa cooked in great haste.

@186
the Lord  His meal  took  of the day  in the third watch
dejected  that  brāhmaṇa  a fast  keeps
= The Lord took His meal in the third watch of the day, but the brāhmaṇa, dejected, went on fasting.

@187
the Lord  said  brāhmaṇa  why  do you keep  a fast
why  so much  sorrow  why  do you give way  to despair
= The Lord said, “Brāhmaṇa, why are you fasting? Why such sorrow, why such despair?”

@188
the brāhmaṇa  said  my  for life  there is no  need
into fire or water  entering  I shall give up  my life
= The brāhmaṇa said, “I have no more use for my life. I shall enter fire or water and give it up.

@189
the mother of the world  Mahā-Lakṣmī  Sītā the Queen
a rākṣasa  touched  her  this  with my ears  I hear
= Sītā, our Queen, is Mahā-Lakṣmī, the mother of the world — and a rākṣasa touched her! This I hear with my own ears.

@190
this  body  to keep  ever  not  is fitting
with this  grief  burns  the body  life  not  departs
= It is not fitting for me to keep this body any longer. My body burns with this grief, yet life does not leave it.”

@191
the Lord  said  this  thought  not  do entertain  any more
a paṇḍita  being  in your mind  not  do you make  consideration
= The Lord said, “Do not think this way any more. You are a paṇḍita — why do you not consider the matter?

@192
of the Lord  the beloved  Sītā  of spirit  of bliss  the form
of material  senses  her  to see  there is no  power
= Sītā, the beloved of the Lord, is a form of spirit and bliss; material senses have no power even to see her.

@193
of touching  the matter  let it be  not  they obtain  sight
of Sītā  of the form  an illusory likeness  carried off  Rāvaṇa
= Touching her is out of the question — they cannot even see her. What Rāvaṇa carried off was only an illusory likeness of Sītā.

@194
Rāvaṇa  as soon as he came  Sītā  disappearance  made
of Rāvaṇa  before  a māyā-Sītā  she sent
= The moment Rāvaṇa came, Sītā disappeared and sent a *māyā-Sītā* before him.

@195
transcendental  reality  is not  within the range of the material
in the Vedas and Purāṇas  this  is said  constantly
= A transcendental reality is never within the range of the material: the Vedas and Purāṇas declare this again and again.

@196
faith  have  you  in My  words
again  wrong thoughts  not  do keep  in your mind
= Have faith in My words, and never again let such wrong thoughts enter your mind.”

@197
the Lord’s  in the words  of the brāhmaṇa  arose  faith
a meal  he ate  arose  for life  hope
= The brāhmaṇa came to believe the Lord’s words. He ate, and his hope of life returned.

@198
him  having comforted  the Lord  made  departure
in the Kṛtamālā  a bath  taking  He came  to Durvaśana
= Having comforted him, the Lord went on. After bathing in the Kṛtamālā, He came to Durvaśana.

@199
in Durvaśana  of Raghunātha  He had  darśana
on the Mahendra hill  to Paraśurāma  He offered  homage
= In Durvaśana He had *darśana* of Raghunātha, and on the Mahendra hill He offered homage to Paraśurāma.

@200
to Setubandha  coming  He took  at Dhanus-tīrtha  a bath
Rāmeśvara  seeing  there  He took  rest
= Coming to Setubandha, He bathed at Dhanus-tīrtha; then He saw Rāmeśvara and rested there.

@201
in an assembly of brāhmaṇas  He hears  there  the Kūrma Purāṇa
of it  in the midst  came  of a chaste wife  the story
= There, in an assembly of brāhmaṇas, He heard the *Kūrma Purāṇa*, and in it came the story of the chaste wife.

@202
of chaste wives  the crown jewel  of Janaka  the daughter
of the world  the mother  Sītā  of Rāma  the wife
= The daughter of Janaka, the crown jewel of chaste wives, is Sītā, the mother of the world and the wife of Rāma.

@203
Rāvaṇa  seeing  Sītā  took  of Agni  shelter
from Rāvaṇa  Agni  made  of Sītā  a covering
= When Sītā saw Rāvaṇa, she took shelter of Agni, and Agni hid Sītā from Rāvaṇa.

@204
a māyā-Sītā  Rāvaṇa  took  He heard  in the story
hearing  Mahāprabhu  became  joyful  at heart
= He heard in the story that Rāvaṇa had taken a “*māyā-Sītā*,” and hearing it, Mahāprabhu rejoiced at heart.

@205
Sītā  taking  he kept her  with Pārvatī
a māyā-Sītā  giving  Agni  deceived  Rāvaṇa
= Agni took Sītā and kept her with Pārvatī, and deceived Rāvaṇa by giving him a “*māyā-Sītā*.”

@206
Raghunātha  coming  when  Rāvaṇa  slew
the ordeal by fire  to undergo  Sītā  He brought
= When Raghunātha came and slew Rāvaṇa, He had Sītā brought to undergo the ordeal by fire.

@207
then  the māyā-Sītā  Agni  made  disappear
the real Sītā  bringing  he gave  in Rāma’s presence
= Then Agni made the *māyā-Sītā* vanish, brought the real Sītā and gave her back in Rāma’s presence.

@208
these  conclusions  hearing  the Lord’s  joy  arose
from the brāhmaṇas  asking  that  leaf  He took
= Hearing these conclusions, the Lord was overjoyed. He asked the brāhmaṇas for that leaf and took it.

@209
a new  leaf  having had written  into the book  He had it put
for credence  for the sake  the old  leaf  asking  He took
= He had a new leaf written and put into the book, and, for the sake of credence, asked for the old leaf and took it.

@210
the leaf  taking  again  to Southern Mathurā  He came
to Rāmadāsa  the brāhmaṇa  that  leaf  bringing  He gave
= Taking the leaf, He came back to Southern Mathurā and gave it to the brāhmaṇa Rāmadāsa.

@211
by Sītā  propitiated  Agni
a shadow Sītā  produced
her  carried off  the ten-headed one
Sītā  to the abode of Agni  went
= “Propitiated by Sītā, Agni produced a shadow Sītā. The ten-headed Rāvaṇa carried her off, while Sītā herself went to the abode of Agni.” (Kūrma Purāṇa)

@212
at the time of the ordeal  into the fire
the shadow Sītā  entered  she
Agni  Sītā  bringing
before Him  delivered
= “At the time of the ordeal the shadow Sītā entered the fire, and Agni brought Sītā and delivered her before Rāma.” (Kūrma Purāṇa)

@213
the leaf  receiving  of the brāhmaṇa  became  joyful  the mind
the Lord’s  feet  clasping  he makes  weeping
= Receiving the leaf, the brāhmaṇa was overjoyed. He clasped the Lord’s feet and wept.

@214
the brāhmaṇa  said  You  directly  Śrī Raghunandana
of a sannyāsī  in the garb  to me  You gave  darśana
= The brāhmaṇa said, “You are Śrī Raghunandana Himself, and You have given me *darśana* in the garb of a sannyāsī.

@215
from great sorrow  me  You made  deliverance
today  in my  house  a meal  please make  acceptance
= You have delivered me from great sorrow. Please accept a meal in my house today.

@216
through grief of mind  a good  meal  not  I gave  on that  day
by my  good fortune  once again  I have obtained  darśana
= That day, in my grief, I did not give You a proper meal; by my good fortune I have seen You once again.”

@217
so  saying  that  brāhmaṇa  happily  cooking  did
in an excellent  manner  the Lord  a meal  he caused to take
= Saying this, the brāhmaṇa happily cooked and served the Lord a splendid meal.

@218
that  night  there  staying  to him  mercy  showing
in the Pāṇḍya country  to the Tāmraparṇī  went  Gaurahari
= Gaurahari stayed there that night, showing him mercy, and then went to the Tāmraparṇī in the Pāṇḍya country.

@219
in the Tāmraparṇī  a bath  taking  on the bank of the Tāmraparṇī
the nine  Tripatis  seeing  He roams  joyfully
= He bathed in the Tāmraparṇī and, on its bank, saw Naya Tripati, the nine Tripatis, wandering about joyfully.

@220
at Ciyaḍatalā  holy place  seeing  Śrī Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa
to Tilakāñcī  coming  He had  of Śiva  darśana
= At the holy place of Ciyaḍatalā He saw Śrī Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, and coming to Tilakāñcī, He had *darśana* of Śiva.

@221
at Gajendra-mokṣaṇa-tīrtha  seeing  the Deity of Viṣṇu
to Pānāgaḍi-tīrtha  coming  He saw  the Lord of Sītā
= At Gajendra-mokṣaṇa-tīrtha He saw the Deity of Viṣṇu, and coming to Pānāgaḍi-tīrtha, He saw the Lord of Sītā.

@222
to Cāmtāpura  coming  seeing  Śrī Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa
to Śrī Vaikuṇṭha  coming  He had  of Viṣṇu  darśana
= Coming to Cāmtāpura, He saw Śrī Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa; coming to Śrī Vaikuṇṭha, He had *darśana* of Viṣṇu.

@223
on the Malaya hill  He offered  homage to Agastya
Kanyākumārī  there  He had  darśana
= On the Malaya hill He offered homage to Agastya, and there He had *darśana* of Kanyākumārī.

@224
in Āmlitalā  seeing  Śrī Rāma  Gaurahari
to the Mallāra country  came  where  the Bhaṭṭathāris
= Having seen Śrī Rāma at Āmlitalā, Gaurahari came to the Mallāra country, where the Bhaṭṭathāris live.

@225
Tamāla-kārttika  seeing  He came  to Vetāpani
Raghunātha  seeing  there  He spent  the night
= After seeing Tamāla-kārttika He came to Vetāpani; there He saw Raghunātha and spent the night.

@226
of the Gosāñi  in the company  stays  Kṛṣṇadāsa  the brāhmaṇa
with the Bhaṭṭathāris  there  took place  a meeting
= The brāhmaṇa Kṛṣṇadāsa, who was travelling with the Lord, met the Bhaṭṭathāris there.

@227
women and wealth  showing  his  greed  they aroused
noble  simple  of the brāhmaṇa  loss of reason  they caused
= By showing him women and wealth they aroused his greed and robbed that good and simple brāhmaṇa of his reason.

@228
in the morning  rising  came  the brāhmaṇa  to the Bhaṭṭathāris’ dwelling
him  in search of  the Lord  came  swiftly
= Rising in the morning, the brāhmaṇa went to the Bhaṭṭathāris’ dwelling, and the Lord quickly came there in search of him.

@229
coming  He says  to all  the Bhaṭṭathāris
My  brāhmaṇa  you  keep  for what  reason
= Coming there, He said to all the Bhaṭṭathāris, “Why are you keeping My brāhmaṇa?

@230
I too  a sannyāsī  see  you too  sannyāsīs
to Me  pain  you give  your  ‘justice’  not  I accept
= See, I am a sannyāsī, and you too are sannyāsīs. Yet you give Me pain — I cannot call this justice on your part.”

@231
hearing  all  the Bhaṭṭathāris  rise up  weapons  taking
to kill  came  all  from all sides  running
= Hearing this, all the Bhaṭṭathāris rose up with weapons and came running at Him from all sides to kill Him.

@232
their  weapons  on their  bodies  fall  from their hands
into pieces  cut  the Bhaṭṭathāris  flee  in four  directions
= Their weapons fell from their hands upon their own bodies. Cut to pieces, the Bhaṭṭathāris fled in all directions.

@233
in the Bhaṭṭathāris’ dwelling  a great  arose  wailing
by the hair  seizing  the brāhmaṇa  taking  He made  departure
= A great wailing arose in the Bhaṭṭathāris’ dwelling, and the Lord seized the brāhmaṇa by the hair and led him away.

@234
that  day  going  He came  to the bank of the Payasvinī
a bath  taking  He went  to the temple of Ādi-keśava
= That same day He came to the bank of the Payasvinī, bathed, and went to the temple of Ādi-keśava.

@235
Keśava  seeing  with love  overwhelmed  He became
obeisances  prayers  dancing  singing  much  He did
= Seeing Keśava, He was overwhelmed with love and offered many obeisances and prayers, dancing and singing.

@236
the love  seeing  the people  felt  great amazement
all the people  showed  the Lord  the highest  honour
= Seeing His love, the people were utterly amazed, and everyone honoured the Lord in the highest way.

@237
with great devotees  together  there  discussion  He held
of the ‘Brahma-saṁhitā chapter’  the manuscript  there  He found
= There He held discussions with great devotees, and there He found the manuscript of the chapter of the *Brahma-saṁhitā*.

@238
the manuscript  finding  the Lord’s  arose  joy  boundless
trembling, tears, perspiration, stupor, horripilation  transformations
= Finding the manuscript, the Lord felt boundless joy, and the transformations of ecstasy appeared in Him — trembling, tears, perspiration, stupor and horripilation.

@239
a scripture of conclusions  there is none  to the ‘Brahma-saṁhitā’  equal
of Govinda’s  glory  of the knowledge  the supreme  source
= No scripture of conclusive truth equals the *Brahma-saṁhitā*: it is the supreme source of the knowledge of Govinda’s glory.

@240
in few syllables  it states  conclusions  boundless
of all  Vaiṣṇava  scriptures  among  the very  essence
= In few words it sets forth boundless conclusions; among all the Vaiṣṇava scriptures it is the very essence.

@241
with great  care  that  manuscript  He took  having it copied
to ‘Ananta-padmanābha’  He came  joyful  becoming
= With great care He had the manuscript copied and took it with Him, and joyfully came to Ananta-padmanābha.

@242
for two days  of Padmanābha  He had  darśana
in bliss  to see  He came  Śrī Janārdana
= For two days He had *darśana* of Padmanābha, and then joyfully came to see Śrī Janārdana.

@243
for two days  there  performing  kīrtana and dancing
to the Payasvinī  coming  He sees  Śaṅkara  Nārāyaṇa
= For two days He sang and danced there in *kīrtana*; then, coming to the Payasvinī, He saw Śaṅkara-nārāyaṇa.

@244
to the Śṛṅgeri Maṭha  He came  the seat of Śaṅkarācārya
Matsya-tīrtha  seeing  He took  in the Tuṅgabhadrā  a bath
= He came to the Śṛṅgeri Maṭha, the seat of Śaṅkarācārya; then He saw Matsya-tīrtha and bathed in the Tuṅgabhadrā.

@245
to the seat of Madhvācārya  He came  where  the ‘Tattvavādīs’
in Uḍupī  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  seeing  there  He became  mad with love
= He came to the seat of Madhvācārya, where the Tattvavādīs live, and seeing “Kṛṣṇa” in Uḍupī, He became mad with love.
