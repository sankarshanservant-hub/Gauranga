# CC Madhya 9, stage A, part 2 (verses 183–365); read by build_en.py together with Madhya09.txt

@183
the brāhmaṇa  said  O Lord  my  in the forest  dwelling
for cooking  provisions  in the forest  not  are found  at present
= The brāhmaṇa said, “O Lord, I live in the forest, and at present the things needed for cooking are not to be had here.

@184
wild  greens, fruits and roots  will bring  Lakṣmaṇa
then  Sītā  will do  what is needed for cooking
= Lakṣmaṇa will bring wild greens, fruits and roots, and then Sītā will do the cooking.”

@185
his  worship  hearing  the Lord  pleased  became
in great haste  that  brāhmaṇa  cooking  did
= The Lord was pleased to hear of his way of worship, and the brāhmaṇa cooked in great haste.

@186
the Lord  His meal  took  of the day  in the third watch
dejected  that  brāhmaṇa  a fast  keeps
= The Lord took His meal in the third watch of the day, but the brāhmaṇa, dejected, went on fasting.

@187
the Lord  said  brāhmaṇa  why  do you keep  a fast
why  so much  sorrow  why  do you give way  to despair
= The Lord said, “Brāhmaṇa, why are you fasting? Why such sorrow, why such despair?”

@188
the brāhmaṇa  said  my  for life  there is no  need
into fire or water  entering  I shall give up  my life
= The brāhmaṇa said, “I have no more use for my life. I shall enter fire or water and give it up.

@189
the mother of the world  Mahā-Lakṣmī  Sītā the Queen
a rākṣasa  touched  her  this  with my ears  I hear
= Sītā, our Queen, is Mahā-Lakṣmī, the mother of the world — and a rākṣasa touched her! This I hear with my own ears.

@190
this  body  to keep  ever  not  is fitting
with this  grief  burns  the body  life  not  departs
= It is not fitting for me to keep this body any longer. My body burns with this grief, yet life does not leave it.”

@191
the Lord  said  this  thought  not  do entertain  any more
a paṇḍita  being  in your mind  not  do you make  consideration
= The Lord said, “Do not think this way any more. You are a paṇḍita — why do you not consider the matter?

@192
of the Lord  the beloved  Sītā  of spirit  of bliss  the form
of material  senses  her  to see  there is no  power
= Sītā, the beloved of the Lord, is a form of spirit and bliss; material senses have no power even to see her.

@193
of touching  the matter  let it be  not  they obtain  sight
of Sītā  of the form  an illusory likeness  carried off  Rāvaṇa
= Touching her is out of the question — they cannot even see her. What Rāvaṇa carried off was only an illusory likeness of Sītā.

@194
Rāvaṇa  as soon as he came  Sītā  disappearance  made
of Rāvaṇa  before  a māyā-Sītā  she sent
= The moment Rāvaṇa came, Sītā disappeared and sent a *māyā-Sītā* before him.

@195
transcendental  reality  is not  within the range of the material
in the Vedas and Purāṇas  this  is said  constantly
= A transcendental reality is never within the range of the material: the Vedas and Purāṇas declare this again and again.

@196
faith  have  you  in My  words
again  wrong thoughts  not  do keep  in your mind
= Have faith in My words, and never again let such wrong thoughts enter your mind.”

@197
the Lord’s  in the words  of the brāhmaṇa  arose  faith
a meal  he ate  arose  for life  hope
= The brāhmaṇa came to believe the Lord’s words. He ate, and his hope of life returned.

@198
him  having comforted  the Lord  made  departure
in the Kṛtamālā  a bath  taking  He came  to Durvaśana
= Having comforted him, the Lord went on. After bathing in the Kṛtamālā, He came to Durvaśana.

@199
in Durvaśana  of Raghunātha  He had  darśana
on the Mahendra hill  to Paraśurāma  He offered  homage
= In Durvaśana He had *darśana* of Raghunātha, and on the Mahendra hill He offered homage to Paraśurāma.

@200
to Setubandha  coming  He took  at Dhanus-tīrtha  a bath
Rāmeśvara  seeing  there  He took  rest
= Coming to Setubandha, He bathed at Dhanus-tīrtha; then He saw Rāmeśvara and rested there.

@201
in an assembly of brāhmaṇas  He hears  there  the Kūrma Purāṇa
of it  in the midst  came  of a chaste wife  the story
= There, in an assembly of brāhmaṇas, He heard the *Kūrma Purāṇa*, and in it came the story of the chaste wife.

@202
of chaste wives  the crown jewel  of Janaka  the daughter
of the world  the mother  Sītā  of Rāma  the wife
= The daughter of Janaka, the crown jewel of chaste wives, is Sītā, the mother of the world and the wife of Rāma.

@203
Rāvaṇa  seeing  Sītā  took  of Agni  shelter
from Rāvaṇa  Agni  made  of Sītā  a covering
= When Sītā saw Rāvaṇa, she took shelter of Agni, and Agni hid Sītā from Rāvaṇa.

@204
a māyā-Sītā  Rāvaṇa  took  He heard  in the story
hearing  Mahāprabhu  became  joyful  at heart
= He heard in the story that Rāvaṇa had taken a “*māyā-Sītā*,” and hearing it, Mahāprabhu rejoiced at heart.

@205
Sītā  taking  he kept her  with Pārvatī
a māyā-Sītā  giving  Agni  deceived  Rāvaṇa
= Agni took Sītā and kept her with Pārvatī, and deceived Rāvaṇa by giving him a “*māyā-Sītā*.”

@206
Raghunātha  coming  when  Rāvaṇa  slew
the ordeal by fire  to undergo  Sītā  He brought
= When Raghunātha came and slew Rāvaṇa, He had Sītā brought to undergo the ordeal by fire.

@207
then  the māyā-Sītā  Agni  made  disappear
the real Sītā  bringing  he gave  in Rāma’s presence
= Then Agni made the *māyā-Sītā* vanish, brought the real Sītā and gave her back in Rāma’s presence.

@208
these  conclusions  hearing  the Lord’s  joy  arose
from the brāhmaṇas  asking  that  leaf  He took
= Hearing these conclusions, the Lord was overjoyed. He asked the brāhmaṇas for that leaf and took it.

@209
a new  leaf  having had written  into the book  He had it put
for credence  for the sake  the old  leaf  asking  He took
= He had a new leaf written and put into the book, and, for the sake of credence, asked for the old leaf and took it.

@210
the leaf  taking  again  to Southern Mathurā  He came
to Rāmadāsa  the brāhmaṇa  that  leaf  bringing  He gave
= Taking the leaf, He came back to Southern Mathurā and gave it to the brāhmaṇa Rāmadāsa.

@211
by Sītā  propitiated  Agni
a shadow Sītā  produced
her  carried off  the ten-headed one
Sītā  to the abode of Agni  went
= “Propitiated by Sītā, Agni produced a shadow Sītā. The ten-headed Rāvaṇa carried her off, while Sītā herself went to the abode of Agni.” (Kūrma Purāṇa)

@212
at the time of the ordeal  into the fire
the shadow Sītā  entered  she
Agni  Sītā  bringing
before Him  delivered
= “At the time of the ordeal the shadow Sītā entered the fire, and Agni brought Sītā and delivered her before Rāma.” (Kūrma Purāṇa)
