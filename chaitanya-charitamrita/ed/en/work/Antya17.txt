= “Ah! Without opening the three doors, He passed high over the three tall walls and fell among the cows of Kaliṅga. Through His great separation from Kṛṣṇa His body contracted, and He appeared like a tortoise. That Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa, 5)
