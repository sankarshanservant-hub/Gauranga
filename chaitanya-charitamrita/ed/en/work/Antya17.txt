@title Śrī Caitanya in the Form of a Tortoise

@1
is described  of Śrīla  Gauracandra
most  wonderful  unearthly
by whom  seen  from their mouths  having heard
of divine madness  the conduct
= Having heard it from the mouths of those who saw it with their own eyes, I describe the most wonderful, unearthly conduct of Śrīla Gauracandra in His divine madness.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  Mahāprabhu  night  and day
of madness  the behaviour  ravings  makes  absorbed in love
= In this way, night and day, Mahāprabhu, absorbed in love, behaved like one mad and spoke in ravings.

@4
one  day  the Lord  Svarūpa  Rāmānanda  with
half  the night  passed  of Kṛṣṇa  talks  in the delight
= One day the Lord passed half the night with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, delighting in talks about Kṛṣṇa.

@5
when  whatever  feeling  the Lord  makes  arise
to the feeling  suited  a song  sings  Svarūpa  Mahāśaya
= Whenever some feeling arose in the Lord, Svarūpa Mahāśaya would sing a song suited to that feeling.

@6
Vidyāpati  Caṇḍīdāsa  the Śrī Gīta-govinda
to the feeling  suited  verses  recites  Rāya  Rāmānanda
= And Rāya Rāmānanda would recite verses from Vidyāpati, Caṇḍīdāsa and the *Śrī Gīta-govinda* suited to His feelings.

@7
now and  then  Himself  the Lord  a verse  reciting
of the verse  the meaning  makes  the Lord  lament  making
= Now and then the Lord would Himself recite a verse and explain its meaning, lamenting.

@8
in this way  in various  feelings  half  the night  passed
the Lord  to bed  having put  the two  home  went
= In this way, in various feelings, half the night passed. Then, after putting the Lord to bed, the two of them went home.

@9
of the Gambhīrā  at the door  Govinda  made  his bed
at midnight  the Lord  makes  loud  saṅkīrtana
= Govinda lay down to sleep at the door of the Gambhīrā, and at midnight the Lord began loud *saṅkīrtana*.

@10
suddenly  hears  the Lord  of Kṛṣṇa’s  flute  the song
absorbed in feeling  the Lord  there  made  departure
= Suddenly the Lord heard the song of Kṛṣṇa’s flute and, absorbed in feeling, set off towards it.

@11
in the three  doors  the panels  so  are  indeed  fastened
absorbed in feeling  the Lord  went  outside  becoming
= The panels of the three doors remained fastened as they were, yet, absorbed in feeling, the Lord went out.

@12
of the Siṁha-dvāra  to the south  are  Tailaṅga  cows  the herd
there  going  fell  the Lord  becoming  unconscious
= To the south of the Siṁha-dvāra there was a herd of cows from Tailaṅga. Going there, the Lord fell unconscious.

@13
here  Govinda  of Mahāprabhu  the sound  not  getting
Svarūpa  called  the doors  opening
= Meanwhile Govinda, no longer hearing any sound from Mahāprabhu, opened the doors and called Svarūpa.

@14
then  Svarūpa  Gosāñi  with  taking  the devotees
torches  lighting  make  for the Lord  a search
= Then Svarūpa Gosāñi took the devotees with him, and they lit torches and searched for the Lord.

@15
here and  there  searching  to the Siṁha-dvāra  went
of the cows  among  going  the Lord  found
= After searching here and there, they went to the Siṁha-dvāra and found the Lord among the cows.

@16
of the belly  inside  hands  and feet  of a tortoise  the shape
in the mouth  foam  thrilled limbs  in the eyes  streams of tears
= His hands and feet had drawn inside His body, and He had the shape of a tortoise. There was foam at His mouth, the hair of His body stood on end, and tears streamed from His eyes.

@17
unconscious  is lying  like  a pumpkin  fruit
outside  stupor  within  with bliss  overwhelmed
= He lay unconscious, like a great pumpkin: outwardly inert, but within overwhelmed with bliss.

@18
cows  all  on every side  sniff  the Lord’s  holy  body
away  driven  not  leave  the Lord’s  holy  body’s  company
= On every side the cows were sniffing the Lord’s holy body; even when driven away, they would not leave His side.

@19
much  made  effort  not  becomes  conscious
the Lord  lifting  home  brought  the devotees
= The devotees tried hard to revive Him, but He did not regain consciousness. Then they lifted the Lord and carried Him home.

@20
loud  making  in the ears  make  of the name  saṅkīrtana
after a long  while  Mahāprabhu  regained  consciousness
= They sang the holy name loudly into His ears, and after a long while Mahāprabhu regained consciousness.

@21
conscious  when He became  hands  and feet  outside  came
as before  in proper proportion  the body  became
= When He came to Himself, His hands and feet came out again, and His body became as it had been, in its proper proportions.

@22
rising  sat  the Lord  looks  here and there
to Svarūpa  says  you  Me  have brought  where
= The Lord got up, sat, and looked about Him. He said to Svarūpa, “Where have you brought Me?

@23
the flute’s  sound  hearing  I  went  to Vṛndāvana
I see  in the pasture  the flute  plays  of the king of Vraja  the son
= Hearing the sound of the flute, I went to Vṛndāvana, and I saw the son of the king of Vraja playing His flute in the pasture.

@24
of the signal  of the flute  at the call  Rādhā  went  to the bower  house
to the bower  went  Kṛṣṇa  pastimes  to make
= At the signal call of His flute, Rādhā went to the bower, and Kṛṣṇa too went to the bower to enjoy His pastimes.

@25
Him  behind  behind  I  made  going
His  ornaments’  by the jingling  My  was stolen  hearing
= I followed close behind Him, and the jingling of His ornaments captivated My ears.

@26
the gopīs  with  pastimes  laughter  jesting
voice’s  sound  words  hearing  My  ears’ joy
= He sported with the *gopīs*, laughing and jesting, and My ears rejoiced to hear the sound of His voice and His words.

@27
at that time  you  all  an uproar  making
Me  here  taking  brought  force  using
= Just then all of you raised an uproar and brought Me here by force.

@28
to hear  not  did I get  those  nectar  like  words
to hear  not  did I get  of the ornaments  of the flute  the sound
= I could not hear those words like nectar; I could not hear the sound of His ornaments and His flute.”

@29
absorbed in feeling  to Svarūpa  says  choked  words
My ears  with thirst  I die  recite  nectar of rasa  I shall hear
= Absorbed in feeling, the Lord said to Svarūpa in a choked voice, “My ears are dying of thirst. Recite something full of the nectar of *rasa* — let Me hear it.”

@30
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  of the Lord  the mood  knowing
of the Bhāgavata  a verse  recites  sweet  making
= Svarūpa Gosāñi, knowing the Lord’s mood, sweetly recited a verse from the *Bhāgavata*:

@31
what  woman  O dear one  Your  of sweet words  by the nectar  of the flute  by the song
enchanted  from the conduct of the noble  not  would stray  in the three worlds
of the three worlds  the beauty  this  and  having seen  form
which  cows  birds  trees  deer  thrills of rapture  bore
= “O Kṛṣṇa, what woman in the three worlds, enchanted by the song of Your flute with its sweet, nectarean notes, would not stray from the conduct of the noble? Seeing this form of Yours, the beauty of the three worlds, even cows, birds, trees and deer bristle with rapture.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.40)

@32
hearing  the Lord  in the mood of the gopīs  absorbed  became
of the Bhāgavata  the verse’s meaning  to make  began
= Hearing this, the Lord became absorbed in the mood of the *gopīs* and began to explain the meaning of the verse:

@33
there was  in the gopīs’  mood  absorption
made  into the rāsa  entry
Kṛṣṇa’s  hearing  of disregard  words
Kṛṣṇa’s  mouth’s  smiling  words
as rejection  them  true  taking
in anger  to Kṛṣṇa  gives  reproach
= Absorbed in the mood of the *gopīs*, He entered the *rāsa* dance. Hearing Kṛṣṇa’s words of disregard and taking His smiling, playful words as a true rejection, He reproached Kṛṣṇa in anger:

@34
O lover  say  You  making  certain
these  three worlds  filling
there are  as many  worthy  women
Your  flute  whom  not  attracts
= “O lover, tell us truly: of all the worthy women who fill the three worlds, which is not drawn by Your flute?

@35
You made  in the world  the flute’s  sound
of perfected mantras  a yoginī
a go-between  becoming  enchants  women’s  minds
great  longing  increasing
the noble  path  making them leave
bringing  to You  makes  offering
= You sent the sound of Your flute into the world, and like a *yoginī* perfect in her mantras, it becomes a go-between and enchants the minds of women. It swells their longing, makes them leave the path of the noble, and brings them and gives them up to You.

@36
dharma  You make them leave  by the flute
You strike  of glances  of Kāma  with the arrows
shame  fear  all  You make them leave
now  at us  making  anger
saying  husbands  in leaving  a fault
righteous  becoming  dharma  You teach
= With Your flute You make us abandon *dharma*; You strike us with the arrows of Kāma, Your sidelong glances, and make us cast off shame and fear and everything else. And now You are angry with us, You say it is a fault to leave our husbands, and, turned righteous, You teach us *dharma*!

@37
other  words  other  mind
outwardly  other  conduct
this  all  of a cheat  the artifices
You  know  jest
is  women’s  utter ruin
give up  these  all  tricks
= Your words say one thing, Your mind another, and outwardly You act in yet another way — all these are the artifices of a cheat. For You it is only a jest, but for women it is utter ruin. Give up all these tricks!

@38
the flute’s  sound  nectar  outdoes
nectar  equal  sweet  words
nectar  equal  the ornaments’  jingling
by the three  nectars  steals  the ears
steals  the mind  steals  the life
how  a woman  will hold  her heart
= The sound of Your flute outdoes nectar; Your sweet words are like nectar, and the jingling of Your ornaments is like nectar too. With these three nectars You steal our ears, You steal our minds, You steal our very life. How can a woman keep hold of her heart?”[^38-1]
