# English names and terms — CC Antya 6, verses 223–329 (stage A, part 3; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 6»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 6») are used as given there: the *chatra*; the
*guñjā* garland; the *govardhana-śilā*; *antaraṅga-sevā* (intimate service); *pratiṣṭhā* (prestige); *rājasic* invitation;
*akṛṣṭa-pacyā* (ripening without plowing); eight *paṇas* of cowries; the Siṁha-dvāra; *puṣpa-añjali*; Yadunandana Ācārya,
Vāsudeva Datta. Verse 329 (= Ādi 1.110) — verbatim from `ed/en-rev`; 239 (~ Ādi 17.31, here তরোরপি “even than a tree”),
243 (~ Madhya 1.133), 328 (~ Antya 1.222) — matching lines from `ed/en-rev`.
