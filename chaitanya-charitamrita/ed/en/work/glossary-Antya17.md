# English names and terms — CC Antya 17 (stage A; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 17»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` and in the revised Antya 14–16 are used as given there: *pralāpa* (raving, ravings;
RU «бессвязные речи»); divine madness (*divyonmāda*; RU «божественное безумие»); Svarūpa / Svarūpa Gosāñi (as in the text;
RU «Сварупа Дамодара»); Rāmānanda Rāya; Siṁha-dvāra; Gambhīrā; Madana-mohana (RU им. п. «Мадана-мохан»); *madayati* in the
refrain of the *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa* — “makes me mad with joy” (RU «сводит меня с ума от счастья»).
Names as in the commentaries on this chapter (`ed/en/Antya17-apb.md`, `-anu.md`): Tailaṅga, *caṭaka* bird, Līlāśuka, Piṅgalā.
Verbatim from revised chapters (`ed/en-rev/`): 2 (Ādi 1.18), 31 (Madhya 24.52), 73 (Ādi 1.110); 71 (Antya 16.86).
