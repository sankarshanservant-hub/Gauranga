@title The Story of Sākṣi-gopāla

@1
on His feet  walking  who  in the form of the Deity
the Lord of the brāhmaṇas  indeed  a hundred days’ journey away
to the land  went  for a brāhmaṇa’s sake  of wondrous  deeds
to that  Sākṣi-gopāla  I  bowed down  am
= I bow down to Sākṣi-gopāla, the Lord of the brāhmaṇas, whose deeds are wondrous: in His form as the Deity He walked on His own feet, for a brāhmaṇa’s sake, to a land a hundred days’ journey away.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
walking  walking  came  Yājapura  village
the Deity Varāha  seeing  made  obeisance
= Walking on and on, the Lord came to the village of Yājapura, and there He saw the Deity of Varāha and bowed down to Him.

@4
dancing and singing  did  in love  many  prayers
in Yājapura  that  night  staying  made  departure
= Full of love, He danced and sang and offered many prayers. He stayed that night in Yājapura and then went on His way.

@5
to Kaṭaka  came  Sākṣi-gopāla  to see
Gopāla’s beauty  seeing  became  joyful
= He came to Kaṭaka to see Sākṣi-gopāla, and the sight of Gopāla’s beauty filled Him with joy.

@6
absorbed in love  dancing and singing  did  for some time
absorbed  being  made  to Gopāla  prayers
= Absorbed in love, He danced and sang for some time, and in that rapture He offered prayers to Gopāla.

@7
that  night  there  staying  with the devotees
of Gopāla  the old story  hears  the Lord  with delight
= He stayed there that night with His devotees, and with delight the Lord heard the old story of Gopāla.

@8
Nityānanda Gosāñi  when  holy places  travelled
Sākṣi-gopāla  to see  to Kaṭaka  came
= When Nityānanda Gosāñi had travelled through the holy places, He had come to Kaṭaka to see Sākṣi-gopāla.

@9
of Sākṣi-gopāla  the story  hearing  from people’s lips
that  story  He tells  the Lord  hears  with great happiness
= He had heard the story of Sākṣi-gopāla from the people there. Now He told it, and the Lord listened with great happiness.

@10
long ago  of Vidyānagara  two  indeed  brāhmaṇas
pilgrimage  to make  both  made  departure
= Long ago two brāhmaṇas of Vidyānagara set out together on pilgrimage.

@11
Gayā  Vārāṇasī  Prayāga  all  visiting
to Mathurā  came  both  joyful  being
= They visited Gayā, Vārāṇasī, Prayāga and all the holy places, and then the two of them came joyfully to Mathurā.

@12
on the forest pilgrimage  the forests  seeing  they see  Govardhana
the twelve forests  seeing  at last  went  to Vṛndāvana
= On the pilgrimage through the forests of Vraja they saw the forests and saw Govardhana; and after seeing all twelve forests, at last they came to Vṛndāvana.

@13
in Vṛndāvana  at Govinda’s place  a great temple
in that  temple  Gopāla’s  great service  takes place
= In Vṛndāvana, at the place of Govinda, there stood a great temple, and in that temple Gopāla was served with great splendour.

@14
at Keśī-tīrtha  at Kāliya-hrada and others  did  bathing
Śrī Gopāla  seeing  there  made  rest
= They bathed at Keśī-tīrtha, Kāliya-hrada and the other holy places, and after seeing Śrī Gopāla they rested there.

@15
Gopāla’s beauty  of both  the hearts  took  stealing
happiness  finding  they stay  there  days  two or four
= Gopāla’s beauty stole the hearts of both of them, and they stayed there happily for a few days.

@16
the two brāhmaṇas  among  one  brāhmaṇa  nearly old
the other  brāhmaṇa  young  him  gives  help
= Of the two brāhmaṇas, one was elderly and the other young, and the young one looked after him.

@17
the younger brāhmaṇa  does  always  his  service
by his  service  the brāhmaṇa’s  pleased  became  heart
= The younger brāhmaṇa served him constantly, and the elder was pleased at heart with his service.

@18
the brāhmaṇa  says  you  me  much  service  did
helper  being  me  pilgrimage  made to do
= The elder brāhmaṇa said, “You have served me in so many ways. With your help I have been able to make this pilgrimage.

@19
even a son  father’s  such  not  does  service
by your  kindness  I  not  felt  fatigue
= Even a son does not serve his father like this. By your kindness I have felt no fatigue.

@20
ingratitude  it is  you  not  if I do  honour
therefore  to you  I  shall give  the gift of a daughter
= It would be ingratitude not to honour you. So I shall give you my daughter in marriage.”

@21
the younger brāhmaṇa  says  listen  respected brāhmaṇa
the impossible  you speak  why  which  not  happens
= The younger brāhmaṇa said, “Listen, respected sir! Why do you speak of something impossible, something that can never be?

@22
of very high family  you  in learning, wealth and all else  rich
I  of no family  and  of wealth and learning  devoid
= You are of a very noble family and rich in learning, wealth and every other gift, while I am of no family and have neither wealth nor learning.

@23
for the gift of a daughter  a fit recipient  I  not  am  your
for Kṛṣṇa’s pleasure  I do  your  service
= I am not fit to receive your daughter. I serve you only to please Kṛṣṇa.

@24
by service to brāhmaṇas  Kṛṣṇa’s  pleasure  great  is
by His  satisfaction  devotion’s wealth  increases
= Kṛṣṇa is greatly pleased by service to brāhmaṇas, and when He is pleased, the wealth of devotion grows.”

@25
the elder brāhmaṇa  says  you  not  do  doubt
to you  daughter  shall give  I  in this  what  wonder
= The elder brāhmaṇa said, “Have no doubt. I shall give you my daughter—what is so surprising in that?”

@26
the younger brāhmaṇa  says  your  wife and sons  all
many  kinsmen and relatives  your  many  friends
= The younger brāhmaṇa said, “You have a wife and sons, many kinsmen and relatives, and a great many friends.

@27
of them all  consent  without  is not  the gift of a daughter
Rukmiṇī’s  father  Bhīṣmaka  of that  proof
= Without the consent of them all, a daughter cannot be given in marriage. Bhīṣmaka, the father of Rukmiṇī, is proof of this.

@28
Bhīṣmaka’s  wish  to Kṛṣṇa  daughter  to offer
of the son  by the opposition  daughter  could not  give
= Bhīṣmaka wished to give his daughter to Kṛṣṇa, but his son opposed it, and he could not give her.”

@29
the elder brāhmaṇa  says  daughter  my  own wealth
own wealth  to give  will forbid  what  person
= The elder brāhmaṇa said, “My daughter is my own wealth. Who can forbid me to give away what is my own?

@30
to you  daughter  I shall give  all  making  disregard
doubt  not  do  you  do  acceptance
= I shall give you my daughter, and I shall pay no heed to any of them. Have no doubt—only accept her.”

@31
the younger brāhmaṇa  says  if  daughter  to give  your mind
Gopāla  before  say  this  true word
= The younger brāhmaṇa said, “If you have truly made up your mind to give me your daughter, then say these true words before Gopāla.”

@32
Gopāla  before  the brāhmaṇa  to speak  began
You  know  own daughter  to him  I  gave
= Before Gopāla the elder brāhmaṇa began to speak: “You know it, Lord: I have given my own daughter to him.”

@33
the younger brāhmaṇa  says  Lord  You  my  witness
You  as witness  I will call  if  otherwise  I see
= The younger brāhmaṇa said, “Lord, You are my witness. If I see him go back on his word, I will call on You to bear witness.”

@34
thus  saying  the two  went  homeward
regarding him as guru  the younger brāhmaṇa  much  service  does
= With these words the two set out for home, and all along the way the younger brāhmaṇa served the elder devotedly, regarding him as his guru.

@35
home  coming  the two  went  to their own houses
after some  days  the elder brāhmaṇa  anxious  at heart
= When they reached their own country, each went to his own house. Some days later the elder brāhmaṇa grew anxious at heart:

@36
at the holy place  to the brāhmaṇa  word  I gave  how  true  becomes
wife  son  kinsmen  friends  will know  surely
= “At a holy place I gave my word to a brāhmaṇa—how can I make it true? My wife, my son, my kinsmen and friends will surely come to know of it.”

@37
one day  own people  together  gathered
of them all  before  whole  story  told
= One day he gathered his family together and told them the whole story.

@38
hearing  all  relatives  his  make  lamentation
such  words  to your mouth  you  not  will bring  again
= Hearing it, all his relatives cried out in dismay: “Never let such words pass your lips again!

@39
to a lowly man  daughter  if you give  family  will go  to ruin
hearing  all  people  will make  mockery
= If you give your daughter to a lowly man, our family will be ruined, and everyone who hears of it will laugh at us.”

@40
the brāhmaṇa  says  word given at a holy place  how  I make  otherwise
what  may be  that  may be  I  shall give  the gift of a daughter
= The brāhmaṇa said, “How can I break a promise made at a holy place? Come what may, I shall give her in marriage.”

@41
kinsmen  people  say  we  you  will abandon
wife and son  say  poison  eating  will die
= His kinsmen said, “Then we shall disown you,” and his wife and son said, “We shall take poison and die.”

@42
the brāhmaṇa  says  the witness  calling  will make  judgement
winning  daughter  will take  my  vain  dharma  becomes
= The brāhmaṇa said, “He will call his witness and have the matter judged. He will win and take my daughter, and my dharma will come to nothing.”

@43
the son  says  an image  the witness  and it  in a far  land
who  your  witness  will give  worry  you make  why
= His son said, “The witness is only an image, and it is in a distant land. Who will bear witness against you? Why worry?

@44
not  I said  not  say  this  lie
only  say  my  any  not  remembrance
= You need not say, ‘I never said it’—don’t tell that lie. Just say, ‘I remember nothing.’

@45
you  if  say  I  anything  not  know
then  I  argument  making  the brāhmaṇa  will defeat
= If you say, ‘I know nothing about it,’ I will argue the case and defeat the brāhmaṇa.”

@46
thus  hearing  the brāhmaṇa’s  troubled  became  mind
single-mindedly  meditates  the brāhmaṇa  on Gopāla’s feet
= Hearing this, the brāhmaṇa grew troubled, and with all his heart he meditated on Gopāla’s feet:

@47
my  dharma  protection  gets  not  die  own people
both  protection  do  Gopāla  I have taken  shelter
= “Let my dharma be kept, and let my family not die. O Gopāla, protect them both—I have taken shelter of You.”

@48
in this way  the brāhmaṇa  in his heart  to ponder  began
next  day  the younger brāhmaṇa  his  to house  came
= So the brāhmaṇa pondered within himself. The next day the younger brāhmaṇa came to his house.

@49
coming  with great devotion  obeisance  making
humility  showing  says  hands  both  joining
= He came in, bowed down with deep devotion, and with folded hands humbly said:

@50
you  to me  daughter  to give  have made  promise
now  anything  not  you say  what  your  conduct
= “You promised to give me your daughter. Now you say nothing at all. What kind of conduct is this?”

@51
thus  hearing  that  brāhmaṇa  remains  silence  keeping
his  son  to beat  came  in his hand  a stick  taking
= Hearing this, the brāhmaṇa kept silent, and his son came at the young man with a stick in his hand to beat him.

@52
you  wretch  my  sister  you want  to marry
a dwarf  being  the moon  as if  you want  indeed  to catch
= “You wretch! You want to marry my sister? You are like a dwarf who wants to catch the moon!”

@53
the stick  seeing  that  brāhmaṇa  fleeing  went
next  day  of the village  the people  together  gathered
= Seeing the stick, the young brāhmaṇa ran away. The next day he gathered the people of the village.

@54
all  the people  the elder brāhmaṇa  calling  brought
then  that  younger brāhmaṇa  to speak  began
= The people sent for the elder brāhmaṇa and brought him there, and then the younger brāhmaṇa began to speak:

@55
he  to me  daughter  to give  has made  promise
now  that  not  he gives  ask  his  conduct
= “This man promised to give me his daughter. Now he will not give her—ask him why he behaves like this.”

@56
then  that  brāhmaṇa  asked  all the people
daughter  why  not  you give  if  you have given  word
= Then everyone asked the brāhmaṇa, “If you gave your word, why do you not give him your daughter?”

@57
the brāhmaṇa  says  hear  people  my  submission
when  what  I have said  my  there is not  remembrance
= The brāhmaṇa said, “Listen, good people, to what I have to say: I do not remember ever saying such a thing.”

@58
thus  hearing  his  son  a quibble in the words  finding
brazen  becoming  speaks  in front  coming
= Hearing this, his son found in his father’s words a pretext for a quibble. He came forward and spoke out brazenly:

@59
on the pilgrimage  father  with  was  much  money
money  seeing  this  scoundrel’s  to take  became  desire
= “On the pilgrimage my father carried a great deal of money. When this scoundrel saw it, he wanted to take it.

@60
other  anyone  with  not  this one  with  alone
dhuturā  feeding  father  made  mad
= There was no one else with my father—only this man. He fed him *dhuturā* and drove him out of his mind.

@61
all  money  taking  says  a thief  took  the money
daughter  to give  he wanted  raised  word
= He took all the money and said, ‘A thief took the money.’ And now he puts about the story that my father wanted to give him his daughter.

@62
you  all  people  make  judgement
my  father’s  daughter  to give  fit  is  to him
= All of you, good people, judge for yourselves: is this man fit to receive my father’s daughter?”

@63
thus  hearing  people’s  in minds  arose  doubt
possible  from greed for money  people  give up  fear of dharma
= Hearing this, the people began to have doubts: “It may well be so—greed for money makes people lose their fear of dharma.”

@64
then  the younger brāhmaṇa  says  listen  honourable people
argument  to win  he speaks  false words
= Then the younger brāhmaṇa said, “Listen, honourable people! He is telling lies to win the argument.

@65
this  brāhmaṇa  my  with service  pleased  when  became
to you  I  daughter  shall give  himself  said
= When this brāhmaṇa was pleased with my service, he himself said, ‘I shall give you my daughter.’

@66
then  I  forbade  listen  best of the twice-born
your  daughter’s  fit  not am  I  bridegroom
= But I tried to dissuade him: ‘Listen, best of the twice-born: I am not a fit husband for your daughter.

@67
where  you  learned  rich  most noble
where  I  poor  ignorant  lowly  without family
= You are learned, rich and of the noblest family; I am poor, ignorant and lowly, a man of no family. How can we compare?’

@68
still  this  brāhmaṇa  to me  says  time  after time
to you  daughter  I shall give  you  do  acceptance
= Still this brāhmaṇa told me again and again, ‘I shall give you my daughter; you must accept her.’

@69
then  I  said  listen  wise one
your  of wife, son and kinsmen  not  will be  consent
= Then I said, ‘Listen, wise sir: your wife, your son and your kinsmen will never consent.

@70
daughter  to give  you will not be able  will be  false word
again  says  the brāhmaṇa  making  effort
= You will not be able to give her, and your word will turn out false.’ But the brāhmaṇa insisted once more:

@71
daughter  to you  I shall give  hesitation  not  make  in heart
own daughter  I shall give  who  can  forbid
= ‘I shall give my daughter to you; have no doubt in your heart. It is my own daughter I am giving—who can forbid me?’

@72
then  I  said  firm  making  mind
Gopāla’s  before  say  these true  words
= Then, with my mind made firm, I said, ‘Say these true words before Gopāla.’

@73
then  he  Gopāla’s  before  said
You  know  to this  brāhmaṇa  daughter  I  gave
= So he declared before Gopāla, ‘You know it: I have given my daughter to this brāhmaṇa.’

@74
then  I  Gopāla  witness  making
said  at His  feet  bowed  being
= Then I made Gopāla my witness, bowed down at His feet and said:

@75
if  this  brāhmaṇa  to me  not  will give  the gift of a daughter
as witness  I will call  You  be  ready
= ‘If this brāhmaṇa does not give me his daughter, I shall call on You as my witness. Be ready for that.’

@76
in this  word  witness  my  is  a great personage
whose  word  true  taking  accept  the three worlds
= So in this matter my witness is a great personage, whose word the three worlds accept as truth.”

@77-78
then  the elder brāhmaṇa  says  this  true  word
Gopāla  if  witness  gives  Himself  coming  here
then  daughter  will give  I  know  for certain
his  son  says  this  good  word  is
= Then the elder brāhmaṇa said, “Very well. If Gopāla comes here Himself and bears witness, then I will give my daughter—know this for certain.” His son said, “That is a fair arrangement.”

@79
in the elder brāhmaṇa’s  mind  Kṛṣṇa  very  merciful
surely  my  word  He  will make  proven
= The elder brāhmaṇa thought, “Kṛṣṇa is most merciful. He will surely prove my words true.”

@80
in the son’s  mind  an image  not  will come  witness  to give
with this  thought  the two  being  in agreement
= The son thought, “An image will never come to bear witness.” Each with his own thought, the two of them agreed.
