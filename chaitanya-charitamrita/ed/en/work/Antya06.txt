@title Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī Meets Śrī Caitanya

@1
by the ropes of mercy  who  from the blind well of a wretched household
having lifted out  by a clever device  Raghunātha dāsa
entrusting  to Svarūpa  made  His intimate one
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Him  take refuge
= I take refuge in Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who by the ropes of His mercy cleverly lifted Raghunātha dāsa out of the blind well of a wretched household life, entrusted him to Svarūpa, and made him His intimate associate.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  Gauracandra  with the devotees
in Nīlācala  various  pastimes  performs  in various joyful ways
= In this way Gauracandra, together with His devotees, performed various pastimes in Nīlācala in many joyful ways.

@4
although  within  separation from Kṛṣṇa  afflicts (Him)
outwardly  not  He shows (it)  for fear of the devotees’ sorrow
= Although separation from Kṛṣṇa afflicted Him within, He did not show it outwardly, for fear that His devotees would grieve.

@5
intense  the pain of separation  when  comes out
then  what  agitation  of the Lord  description  not  is possible
= But when the intense pain of separation broke out, the Lord’s agitation was beyond description.

@6
Rāmānanda’s  talks about Kṛṣṇa  Svarūpa’s  singing
in the pain of separation  the Lord’s  preserve  life
= Rāmānanda’s talks about Kṛṣṇa and Svarūpa’s singing kept the Lord alive in the pain of separation.

@7
by day  the Lord  in various company  becomes  other  the mind
at night  increases  the Lord’s  pain of separation
= By day, in the company of various people, the Lord’s mind was diverted; but at night His pain of separation grew.

@8
His  for the happiness  with (Him)  stay  two  persons
with verses and songs about the rasa of Kṛṣṇa  they give  consolation
= For His happiness two persons stayed with Him and consoled Him with verses and songs about the sweet *rasa* of Kṛṣṇa.

@9
Subala  as  formerly  of Kṛṣṇa’s happiness  the helper
for giving happiness to Gaura  so  Rāmānanda Rāya
= As Subala formerly helped to make Kṛṣṇa happy, so Rāmānanda Rāya gave happiness to Gaura.

@10
formerly  as  Rādhā’s  Lalitā  the chief helper
so  Svarūpa Gosāñi  preserves  the Lord’s  life
= As Lalitā was formerly Rādhā’s chief helper, so Svarūpa Gosāñi kept the Lord alive.

@11
of the two  persons  the good fortune  telling  not  is possible
the Lord’s  ‘intimate associates’  as  whom  people  praise
= The good fortune of these two cannot be described: people praise them as the Lord’s intimate associates.

@12
in this way  sports  Gaura  taking  the devotees
the meeting with Raghunātha  now  hear  O devotees
= In this way Gaura enjoyed His pastimes with His devotees. Now, O devotees, hear of His meeting with Raghunātha.

@13
formerly  to Śāntipura  Raghunātha  when  came
Mahāprabhu  mercy  showing  him  instructed
= Earlier, when Raghunātha had come to Śāntipura, Mahāprabhu mercifully instructed him.

@14
the Lord’s  by the instruction  he  to his own home  goes
monkey renunciation  giving up  became  ‘like a worldly man’
= Following the Lord’s instruction, he went home and, giving up *markaṭa-vairāgya* (monkey renunciation), lived outwardly like a worldly man.

@15
within  detachment  outwardly  does  all  work
seeing (this)  indeed  of mother and father  gladdened  the minds
= Within he was detached, but outwardly he did all his duties; seeing this, his mother and father were glad at heart.

@16
from Mathurā  the Lord  has come  the news  when  he received
to the Lord  to go  preparations  he made
= When he received news that the Lord had returned from Mathurā, he prepared to go to Him.

@17
at this time  of the province  a  Muslim (mleccha)  official
of the province of Saptagrāma  he  was  the caudhurī
= At that time there was a Muslim official of the province: he was the *caudhurī*, the revenue collector, of the Saptagrāma province.

@18
Hiraṇya dāsa  the province  took  a permanent lease  making
his  authority  went  is mortified  he  seeing (this)
= Hiraṇya dāsa had taken the province on a permanent revenue lease (*makrari*). The official lost his authority, and seeing this he burned with resentment.

@19
twelve  lakhs  gives  to the king  collects  twenty  lakhs
that  Turk  anything  not  getting  became  an enemy
= Hiraṇya collected twenty lakhs and paid the king twelve. Getting nothing, that “Turk” became his enemy.

@20
at the royal court  a report  submitting  the vizier  he brought
Hiraṇya dāsa  fled  Raghunātha  he bound
= He submitted a report (*kaiphiyat*) at the royal court and brought the vizier. Hiraṇya dāsa fled, and Raghunātha was arrested.

@21
every day  Raghunātha  he does  rebuking
your father and uncle  bring  otherwise  you will get  torment
= Every day he rebuked Raghunātha: “Bring your father and uncle, or you will suffer!”

@22
to beat (him)  coming  when  he sees  Raghunātha
the mind  turning  goes  then  not  he can  beat (him)
= Whenever he came to beat him, the sight of Raghunātha changed his heart, and he could not strike him.

@23
especially  thinking (him) a Kāyastha  inwardly  he feels  fear
with his mouth  threatens  roars  to beat  fearful  the heart
= Above all, knowing him to be a Kāyastha, he was afraid at heart: aloud he threatened and roared, but inwardly he feared to strike him.

@24
then  Raghunātha  some  thought of  means
humbly  he speaks  at that Muslim’s feet
= Then Raghunātha thought of a plan and humbly spoke at that Muslim’s feet:

@25
my  father  (and) uncle  are  your  two  brothers
brother with brother  you  quarrel  do  always
= “My father and uncle are your two brothers. As brothers do, you are always quarreling.

@26
sometimes  quarrel  sometimes  affection  of this  certainty  there is none
tomorrow  again  three  brothers  will be  in one place
= Sometimes you quarrel, sometimes you are friends — nothing about it is fixed. Tomorrow the three brothers will be together again.

@27
I  as  my father’s  so  your  child
I  your  ward  you  my  protector
= As I am my father’s child, so I am yours. I am your ward, and you are my protector.

@28
a protector  being  the ward  to punish  not  befits
you  all  scriptures  know  like a living saint (jindā pīr)
= It does not befit a protector to punish his ward. You know all the scriptures; you are like a *jindā pīr*, a living saint.”

@29
this  hearing  that  Muslim’s  heart  melted
down his beard  flowing  tears  fall  to weep  he began
= Hearing this, the Muslim’s heart melted; tears flowed down his beard, and he began to weep.

@30
the Muslim  says  today  from  you  my  son
today  I will release  you  making  a  pretext
= The Muslim said, “From today you are my son. Today I will get you released on some pretext.”

@31
to the vizier  speaking  Raghunātha  he had released
with affection  to Raghunātha  to speak  he began
= He spoke to the vizier and had Raghunātha released. Then he began to speak to Raghunātha with affection:

@32
your  uncle  foolish  eight lakhs  eats
I  a sharer  to me  something  to give  is fitting
= “Your uncle is a fool: he enjoys eight lakhs himself. I am a sharer in it; he ought to give me something.

@33
go  you  your  uncle  bring to meet  me
in whatever way  good  is  let him do  the charge  I have given  to him
= Go and bring your uncle to meet me. Let him do whatever is best — I leave it to him.”

@34
Raghunātha  coming  then  the uncle  brought to meet (him)
the Muslim  with  winning over  he did  all  calm  became
= Then Raghunātha came and brought his uncle to meet him. He won the Muslim over, and everything was settled.

@35
in this way  Raghunātha’s  a year  passed
in the second  year  to flee  mind  he made
= So a year of Raghunātha’s life passed. In the second year he resolved to run away.

@36
at night  rising  alone  he went  fleeing
from far away  his father  him  brought back  catching
= Rising at night, he ran away alone, but his father caught him far away and brought him back.

@37
in this way  again and again  he flees  catching  they bring back
then  his  mother  says  with his  father
= Again and again he fled, and each time they caught him and brought him back. Then his mother said to his father,

@38
the son  mad  has become  keep (him)  tied up
his  father  says  to her  dejected  being
= “Our son has gone mad. Keep him tied up.” His father, dejected, replied to her:

@39
like Indra’s  wealth  a wife  like an apsarā
all these  to bind  could not  whose  mind
= “Wealth like Indra’s and a wife like an *apsarā* — all this could not bind his mind.

@40
of rope  by the binding  him  will you keep  how
birth-giving  father  cannot  prārabdha  annul
= How will you keep him with ropes? A father who gives birth cannot annul *prārabdha*, the fruit of past deeds that has begun to ripen.
