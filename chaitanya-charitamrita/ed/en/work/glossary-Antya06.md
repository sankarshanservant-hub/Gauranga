# English names and terms — CC Antya 6, verses 1–110 (stage A, part 1; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 6»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 6») are used as given there: *ciḍā* (flat rice), *dadhi*
(yogurt); the festival of *ciḍā* and *dadhi* (*ciḍā-dadhi-mahotsava*); *markaṭa-vairāgya* (monkey renunciation); *caudhurī*;
the vizier (*ujir*); *makrari* (a permanent lease of revenue); *kaiphiyat* (a written report); lakh; Kāyastha; *prārabdha*;
*sūtra* (a pretext); *nirviṇṇa*; *lāg*; *cabutarā* (platform); *piṇḍā*; *holnā*; *āroyā-ciḍā*; *ni-sakḍi*; *pasāri* (vendor);
*pulina-bhojana* (the picnic on the sandy bank); Hiraṇya dāsa; Rāghava Paṇḍita; Rāmadāsa (Abhirāma Ṭhākura); Uddhāraṇa Datta;
Kṛṣṇadāsa Hoḍa; Saptagrāma; Pāṇihāṭī.
