@76
happy  are (you)  all  any  is there not  of the demons  fear
they  say  by Your  grace  everywhere  victory
= Are you all happy? Have you no fear of the demons?” They replied, “By Your grace we are victorious everywhere.

@77
now  on the earth  whatever  had become  burden
descended  having become  that  You made  destruction
= Whatever burden had lately come upon the earth, You have descended and destroyed it.”

@78
Dvārakā and the rest  of the opulence  this  indeed  the proof

= This is the proof of the opulence (*vibhūti*) of Dvārakā and Kṛṣṇa’s other abodes: each of them understood, “Kṛṣṇa is in my own universe.”

@79
with Kṛṣṇa  of Dvārakā  the splendour  experience  there was
in one place  in the meeting  anyone  anyone  not  saw
= Each experienced the splendour of Dvārakā together with Kṛṣṇa; yet, though all met in one place, none of them saw any other.

@80

prostrate  becoming  all  to their own homes  went
= Then Kṛṣṇa gave all the Brahmās leave to go, and they all bowed down flat and returned to their own homes.

@81

= Seeing this, the four-faced Brahmā was amazed. He came to Kṛṣṇa’s feet and offered obeisance.

@82
Brahmā  says  before  I  what  conclusion  made
of that  the example  I  today  indeed  saw
= Brahmā said, “What I concluded before, today I have seen demonstrated before my eyes.

@83

of mind  of body  of speech
glory  Your  within the range
= “Let those who know You know. What use are many words? O Lord, Your glory lies beyond the reach of my mind, my body and my speech.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.38)

@84
Kṛṣṇa  says  this  universe  fifty crore  yojanas
very  small  therefore  your  four  faces
= Kṛṣṇa said, “This universe measures fifty crore *yojanas*. It is very small, and so you have only four faces.

@85
some  universe  a hundred crore  some  a hundred thousand crore
some  a million crore  some  crore upon crore
= Some universes measure a hundred crore *yojanas*, some a hundred thousand crore, some a million crore, some crore upon crore.

@86
according to the universe  of Brahmā  body and faces
in this way  maintain  I  of the universes  the multitude
= A Brahmā’s body and faces correspond to the size of his universe. In this way I maintain the countless universes.

@87
the one-quarter  opulence  of it  there is not  measure
of the three-quarter  opulence  who  makes  measurement
= The *eka-pāda vibhūti*, the one quarter of My opulence, has no measure. Who, then, can measure the *tri-pāda vibhūti*, the other three quarters?”

@88
of it  on the far shore  Paravyoma
three-quarters  consisting of  eternal
immortal  everlasting  constant
infinite  supreme  abode
= “On the far shore of the Virajā lies Paravyoma, the eternal three quarters of His opulence: immortal, everlasting and constant, infinite, the supreme abode.” (Padma Purāṇa)

@89
then  Kṛṣṇa  to Brahmā  gave  leave
of Kṛṣṇa  of the opulence  the nature  to know  not  is possible
= Then Kṛṣṇa gave Brahmā leave to go. The nature of Kṛṣṇa’s opulence cannot be known.

@90
tryadhīśvara  of the word  the meaning  a hidden  another  there is
by the word tri  Kṛṣṇa’s  three  worlds  are meant
= The word *tryadhīśvara* (“Lord of the three”) has yet another, hidden meaning: by the word *tri* (“three”) Kṛṣṇa’s three abodes are meant.

@91
called Goloka  Gokula  Mathurā  Dvārāvatī
in these  three  worlds  Kṛṣṇa’s  natural  eternal residence
= Gokula, which is called Goloka, Mathurā and Dvārāvatī (Dvārakā): in these three abodes Kṛṣṇa naturally and eternally resides.

@92
internal  with full majesty  filled  three  abodes
of the three  the supreme master  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān Himself
= These three abodes are internal and filled with full majesty, and their supreme master is Kṛṣṇa, Bhagavān Himself.

@93-94
previously  mentioned  of the universes  all  guardians of the quarters
of the infinite  Vaikuṇṭhas and coverings  the eternal  rulers of the worlds
their all  crowns  of Kṛṣṇa  of the feet  the footstool  before

= All the guardians of the quarters in the universes I spoke of before, and the eternal rulers of the infinite Vaikuṇṭhas and of the coverings of the universes, bow down before Kṛṣṇa’s footstool, and as they prostrate themselves the jewels of their crowns touch it.

@95
jewels  on the footstool  knocking together  arises  a jingling
of the footstool  praise  make  the crowns  so  I know
= The jewels knock together on the footstool and a jingling arises: it is as if, I think, the crowns were singing the footstool’s praises.

@96
with His own spiritual potency  Kṛṣṇa  eternally  is present
of the spiritual potency  the wealth  the six opulences  the name
= Kṛṣṇa is eternally present with His own spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*), and the wealth of that potency is called the “six opulences” (*ṣaḍaiśvarya*).

@97
that  royal fortune  makes  eternally  fulfilled in desire
therefore  in the Vedas  they call (Him)  Bhagavān Himself
= That royal fortune (*svārājya-lakṣmī*) keeps Him eternally fulfilled in every desire, and therefore the Vedas call Him “Bhagavān Himself.”

@98
of Kṛṣṇa  the majesty  a boundless  of nectar  ocean
to plunge in  unable  of it  I have touched  one  drop
= Kṛṣṇa’s majesty is a boundless ocean of nectar. Unable to plunge into it, I have touched but a single drop.”

@99
majesty  while speaking of  in the Lord  the manifestation of Kṛṣṇa  there was
in sweetness  sank  the mind  one  verse  He recited
= As the Lord spoke of majesty, Kṛṣṇa was revealed within His heart. His mind sank into sweetness, and He recited a verse.

@100
which  suited to pastimes among mortals  His own yoga-
-māyā’s  power  by Him displaying  was assumed
astonishing  to Himself  even  of the wealth of fortune
the supreme  abode  of ornaments  the ornament  whose limbs
= “To display the power of His own Yogamāyā, He assumed a form suited to His pastimes among mortals — a form that astonished even Himself, the supreme abode of all good fortune, whose limbs are the ornament of ornaments.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.2.12)

@101
Kṛṣṇa’s  all  pastimes  the highest  the human-like pastime
a human-like body  His  own form
dressed as a cowherd  flute in hand  freshly youthful  the best of dancers
for the human-like pastime  is  fitting
= Of all Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes the highest is His human-like pastime (*nara-līlā*), and a human-like form is His own true form. Dressed as a cowherd boy, flute in hand, ever freshly youthful, the finest of dancers — this form perfectly suits His human-like pastimes.

@102
Kṛṣṇa’s  sweet  form  hear  O Sanātana
of which  form  one  particle  floods  that  the three worlds
all  living beings  makes  attraction
= Hear, Sanātana, of Kṛṣṇa’s sweet form, a single particle of which floods the three worlds and draws all living beings to itself.

@103
Yogamāyā  the spiritual potency  of pure sattva  the transformation
her  power  to the world  to show
this  form  jewel  of the devotees  the hidden treasure
manifest  He made  the eternal pastimes  from
= Yogamāyā is the spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*), a transformation of pure *sattva* (*viśuddha-sattva*). To show her power to the world, He brought this jewel of a form, the hidden treasure of the devotees, into the manifest world from His eternal pastimes.

@104
form  seeing  His own  in Kṛṣṇa  there was  amazement
to taste  in the mind  arises  desire
His own good fortune  whose  name  beauty and the rest  of qualities  the host
this form  eternal  their  abode
= Seeing His own form, Kṛṣṇa is amazed, and a desire to taste it rises in His mind. His own good fortune (*sva-saubhāgya*), the host of qualities beginning with beauty, has this form as its eternal abode.

@105
of ornaments  the ornament  the body  in it  graceful  threefold bending
of that  above  of the bow of the brows  the dance
askance  of the eye-corners  the arrows  their  firm  aim
pierce  of Rādhā  of the gopīs  of the host  the hearts
= His body is the ornament of ornaments, and in it is the graceful threefold bending (*tribhaṅga*). Above it dances the bow of His brows; the arrows of His sidelong glances, firmly aimed, pierce the hearts of Rādhā and the gopīs.

@106
above the universes  Paravyoma  there  which  divine forms
of them all  by force  steals  the minds
of chaste wives  the crest-jewel  whom  calls  the word of the Vedas
attracts  those  Lakṣmīs
= Above the universes lies Paravyoma; this form forcibly steals the minds of all the divine forms (*svarūpas*) there. It attracts even the Lakṣmīs, whom the word of the Vedas calls the crest-jewels of chaste wives.

@107
mounted  on the gopīs’  chariot of desire  of Cupid  the mind  churns
name  He bears  Madana-mohana
conquering  of the five-arrowed one  the pride  Himself  a new Cupid
the rāsa  performs  taking  the gopīs
= Mounted on the chariot of the gopīs’ heartfelt desire, He churns the mind of Cupid himself, and so He bears the name Madana-mohana, the enchanter of Cupid. Conquering the pride of the five-arrowed god, He, Himself a new Cupid (*nava-kandarpa*), performs the *rāsa* dance with the gopīs.

@108
equal to Himself  with friends  of the cows  of the herds  of grazing  in the joy
in Vṛndāvana  free  sport
whose  flute-sound  hearing  unmoving and moving  beings
thrill  trembling  of tears  flow  streams
= With friends equal to Himself, in the joy of grazing the cows, He sports freely in Vṛndāvana. Hearing the sound of His flute, all beings, unmoving and moving, thrill and tremble, and tears flow from them in streams.

@109
the pearl necklace  of herons  a line  the rainbow  the peacock feather  there
the yellow garment  of lightning  the flash
Kṛṣṇa  a fresh rain cloud  of the world  the crops  upon
pours  of the nectar of pastimes  showers
= His pearl necklace is a line of herons, His peacock feather the rainbow, His yellow garment a flash of lightning. Kṛṣṇa is a fresh rain cloud pouring showers of the nectar of His pastimes upon the crops of the world.

@110

that  Śuka  of Vyāsa  the son
in place  after place  in the Bhāgavata  has described  to make known
that  hearing  dance  the devotees
= Sweetness, the essence of Godhood (*bhagavattā*), He made known in Vraja. Śuka, the son of Vyāsa, has described it in place after place in the *Bhāgavata* to make it known, and hearing it the devotees dance.

@111
speaking  of Kṛṣṇa’s  rasa  a verse  recites  in the rapture of love
in love  Sanātana’s hand  holding
the gopīs’ fortune  Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  which  made  description
in ecstatic absorption  of Mathurā  the city women
= Speaking of the *rasa* of Kṛṣṇa, the Lord, in the rapture of love, lovingly took Sanātana’s hand and recited the verse in which the women of Mathurā, absorbed in ecstasy, described the gopīs’ fortune and Kṛṣṇa’s qualities.

@112
the gopīs  austerity  what  performed  since  His  form
of loveliness  the essence  unequalled and unsurpassed  not perfected by anything else
with the eyes  drink  at every moment new  hard to attain
the sole abode  of fame  of beauty  of majesty
= “What austerities did the gopīs perform, that with their eyes they drink His form — the essence of loveliness, unequalled and unsurpassed, perfect in itself, new at every moment, hard to attain, the sole abode of fame, beauty and majesty?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.44.14)

@113
of youth’s nectar  an ocean  the waves  the essence of loveliness
in it  that  whirlpool  the rising of emotions
the flute’s sound  a whirlwind  of women  pride  grass and leaves

= His youth is a nectar ocean; its waves are the essence of loveliness, its whirlpools the surging of emotions. The sound of His flute is a whirlwind, and women’s pride is but grass and leaves: the whirlwind sinks it, and it never rises again.

@114

Kṛṣṇa’s form’s  exquisite sweetness  drinking  drinking  the eyes  filling
praiseworthy  they make  birth  body  mind
= O friend, what austerity did the gopīs perform? Drinking and drinking the exquisite sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s form until their eyes are full, they make their birth, body and mind worthy of praise.

@115

in Paravyoma  of the divine forms  in the host

= Among all the divine forms in Paravyoma there is nothing higher than this sweetness, nor anything equal to it. Even Nārāyaṇa, the source of all the *avatāras* and the ruler of Paravyoma, does not possess this sweetness.

@116

of the chaste wives  the object of worship
she  who  for sweetness  in greed  giving up  all  enjoyments of desire
a vow  taking  performed  austerity
= The witness to this is Ramā (Lakṣmī), Nārāyaṇa’s most beloved, whom all chaste wives worship. Longing for that sweetness, she gave up every enjoyment she might desire, took a vow and performed austerities.

@117

He  of sweetness and other  qualities  the mine
in the other  all  manifestations  by Him  given  qualities  shine

= That is the very essence of sweetness, owing its perfection to nothing else; He is the mine of sweetness and of all other qualities. In all His other manifestations shine the qualities He grants them, as much in each as its work requires — this I know.

@118

of it  before  Kṛṣṇa’s  sweetness
the two  make  a contest  grow  face  not  turning
new  new  of the two  the abundance
= The gopīs’ love is a mirror, new at every moment, and before it stands Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness. The two vie with each other and keep growing, never turning back, and the abundance of both is ever new.

@119

only  who  on the path of spontaneous love  worships  Kṛṣṇa  with deep attachment
for him  Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  easy to attain
= Through *karma*, austerity, yoga, knowledge, devotion according to rules (*vidhi-bhakti*), *japa* or meditation this sweetness is hard to attain. Only one who worships Kṛṣṇa with deep attachment (*anurāga*) on the path of spontaneous love (*rāga-mārga*) attains Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness easily.

@120

of divine  qualities  of the host  the jewel-mine
of others  of majesty  the existence  given by Kṛṣṇa  Godhood
Kṛṣṇa  the whole of all parts  the shelter of all
= That form, whose home is Vraja, is full of majesty and sweetness, a jewel-mine of divine qualities. The majestic existence of others, their Godhood (*bhagavattā*), is given by Kṛṣṇa: Kṛṣṇa is the whole of whom all are parts (*sarva-aṁśī*), the shelter of all.

@121

good-natured  gentle  generous  Kṛṣṇa  except  there is not  another
Kṛṣṇa  does  of the world  the good
= Beauty, modesty, mercy, fame, steadfastness and skilful intelligence — all these are established in Kṛṣṇa. Good-natured, gentle and generous — there is no one so but Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa does good to the world.

@122

in Vraja  the Creator  reproach  the gopīs

= All who saw Kṛṣṇa reproached the blinking of their eyes, and in Vraja the gopīs reproached the Creator. Reciting those verses and explaining their meaning, Mahāprabhu joyfully tasted their sweetness.

@123

= “Women and men drank with their eyes His face — lovely with ears graced by *makara* earrings and with shining cheeks, smiling playfully, an eternal festival — and were never sated. Delighted, they were angry with the maker of the blinking eyelids.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.24.65)

@124

= “When by day You wander in the forest, a moment without You becomes an age for us. And as we gaze upon Your beautiful face framed in curling locks, we see what a fool was the maker of our eyelids.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.15)

@125

= The form of the Kāma-gāyatrī mantra is Kṛṣṇa’s own form. It has twenty-four and a half syllables; those syllables are moons, and, making Kṛṣṇa rise, they have filled the three worlds with love’s desire (*kāma*).

@126

= O friend, Kṛṣṇa’s face is the emperor of moons. Seated on the throne of Kṛṣṇa’s body, it rules its kingdom, holding court with an assembly of moons.

@127

@128

= The nails of His fingers, a company of moons, dance upon the flute, and their song is the flute’s melody. Below, the moons of His toenails dance, and the jingling of His anklets is their song.

@129

= The *makara* earrings dance, and the eyes, playful lotuses: the pleasure-loving king makes them dance without cease. His brows are a bow, His eyes arrows, His two ears the bowstring; with them He pierces the target, the hearts of women.

@130

= Grand is this moon’s dance: spreading out a market of moons, it gives away its own nectar without price. Some it satisfies with the moonlit nectar of His smile, some with the nectar of His lips — it satisfies everyone.

@131

= Its ministers are His two eyes, broad, long and reddish, which make even Madana (Cupid) reel in his intoxication. Govinda’s face, full of joy, is the pleasure-house of loveliness and an elixir for the eyes of all.

@132

= One who obtains the sight of that face as the fruit of heaps of piety — what can his two eyes drink of it? His thirst and greed grow twofold; unable to drink, his mind is agitated, and in sorrow he reproaches the Creator:

@133

= “He did not give hundreds of thousands, millions of eyes; he gave only two, and on them he put blinking lids. The Creator is a dull ascetic; his mind is void of taste (*rasa*), and he does not know how to create properly.

@134

= For one who is to see Kṛṣṇa’s face he makes only two eyes — such injustice from the Creator! If he would take my advice and make millions of eyes for such a one, then I would know his creation to be proper.”

@135

= The sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s body is an ocean, His very sweet face the moon, and His exceedingly sweet smile its fine rays. The Lord’s mind clung to these three; greedily He tasted them and, waving His hand, recited a verse.
