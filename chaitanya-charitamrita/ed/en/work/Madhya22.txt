@title The Path of Bhakti

@1
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
the Lord  Him  the ocean of mercy
in Kali  even  this most hidden
bhakti  by whom  was revealed
= I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, the ocean of mercy, by whom this most hidden *bhakti* was revealed even in the age of Kali.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
thus  indeed  I have told  of sambandha-tattva  the analysis
of the Vedas and śāstras  in the teaching  Kṛṣṇa  the one  essence
= Thus I have explained the truth of *sambandha*: in the teaching of the Vedas and the scriptures, Kṛṣṇa is the one essence.

@4
now  I speak  listen  of abhidheya  the characteristics
which  through  one attains  Kṛṣṇa  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa
= Now listen as I describe *abhidheya*, the means by which one attains Kṛṣṇa and the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@5
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is the abhidheya  all the scriptures  declare
therefore  the sages  have made  the conclusion
= All the scriptures declare that devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the *abhidheya*, and so the sages have concluded.

@6
śruti  the mother  when asked  points out  of Your worship  the method
as  of the mother  the words  smṛti  too  so  speaks  the sister
the Purāṇas and the rest  which  and  the brothers  they  following her
therefore  truly  it is known  O slayer of Mura  You  alone  the shelter
= “When Mother Śruti is asked, she points out the method of worshipping You. Smṛti, her sister, speaks just as the mother does, and the Purāṇas and the rest, like brothers, follow her. Therefore, O slayer of Mura, I have truly understood that You alone are the shelter.”

@7
the Truth of nondual knowledge  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān
of svarūpa-śakti  in the form  His  is  abiding
= Kṛṣṇa, the nondual Truth (*advaya-jñāna-tattva*), is Bhagavān Himself. He abides in the form of His own internal energy (*svarūpa-śakti*).

@8
as His own parts and separated parts  in the form  having become  expanded
in endless  Vaikuṇṭhas and universes  He performs  pastimes
= Expanding Himself as His own parts (*svāṁśa*) and separated parts (*vibhinnāṁśa*), He sports in endless Vaikuṇṭhas and universes.

@9
the expansion of His own parts  the catur-vyūha  the host of avatāras
the separated part  the jīva  His  among the energies  is counted
= His own parts are the *catur-vyūha* and the host of *avatāras*. The *jīva* is His separated part and is counted among His energies.

@10
those  separated parts  the jīvas  two  indeed  kinds
one  eternally liberated  one  eternally in worldly existence
= Those *jīvas*, His separated parts, are of two kinds: some are eternally liberated (*nitya-mukta*), and some are eternally in worldly existence (*nitya-saṁsāra*).

@11
the eternally liberated  ever  toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet  turned
Kṛṣṇa’s associates  named  they enjoy  the joy of service
= The eternally liberated are ever turned toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet. They are called Kṛṣṇa’s associates (*pārṣadas*), and they enjoy the happiness of serving Him.

@12
the eternally bound  from Kṛṣṇa  eternally turned away
eternally in worldly existence  they suffer  the miseries of hell and the like
= The eternally bound (*nitya-bandha*) are forever turned away from Kṛṣṇa. Eternally caught in worldly existence, they suffer the miseries of hell and the like.

@13
for that  fault  the witch Māyā  punishment  inflicts  on him
beginning with those of body and mind  the threefold miseries  him  wearing down  torment
= For this fault the witch Māyā punishes him: the threefold miseries, beginning with those of body and mind (*ādhyātmika*), wear him down and torment him.

@14
of lust and anger  the servant  having become  her  kicks  he suffers
wandering  wandering  if  a sādhu-physician  he finds
= Become the servant of lust and anger, he suffers her kicks. But if, wandering on and on, he meets a *sādhu*, a physician of the soul,

@15
his  by the mantra of instruction  the witch  flees
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  he obtains  then  near Kṛṣṇa  he goes
= by the mantra of that *sādhu*’s instruction the witch flees. The soul obtains devotion to Kṛṣṇa and then comes near to Kṛṣṇa.

@16
of lust and the rest  how many  not  in how many ways  were obeyed  the evil commands
of them  arose  toward me  no  pity  no  shame  nor peace
having abandoned them  now  O Lord of the Yadus  at present  having gained good sense
to You  I have come  for shelter  fearless  me  engage in Your service
= “How many evil commands of lust and the rest have I not obeyed, and in how many ways! Yet they never took pity on me, nor did I feel shame or find peace. Now, O Lord of the Yadus, I have cast them off; good sense has dawned in me, and I have come to You, the fearless shelter. Engage me in Your service.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 6)

@17
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is  the chief abhidheya
on the face of bhakti  looking  karma  yoga  jñāna
= Devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the chief *abhidheya*. *Karma*, *yoga* and *jñāna* all look to the face of *bhakti* for their results.

@18
of these  all  practices  very  feeble  the strength
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without  they  to give  cannot  fruit
= The strength of all these practices is very feeble: without devotion to Kṛṣṇa they cannot give any fruit.

@19
freedom from action  even  love for Acyuta  devoid of
not  shines  knowledge  at all  stainless
how then  again  ever  inauspicious  to the Lord
not  offered  action  which  even  free of motive
= “Even stainless knowledge, free from all action (*naiṣkarmya*), does not shine if it is devoid of love for Acyuta. How then can action shine — by nature ever inauspicious — if it is not offered to the Lord, even when it is free of selfish motive?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.5.12)

@20
ascetics  devoted to charity  the renowned
the thoughtful  knowers of mantras  of auspicious conduct
welfare  not  attain  without  offering to Him
to Him  of auspicious fame  obeisance  obeisance
= “Ascetics, the generous, the renowned, the thoughtful, the knowers of mantras and those of auspicious conduct — none of them attains true welfare without offering everything to Him. Again and again I bow to Him, whose fame is auspicious.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.4.17)

@21
mere  knowledge  liberation  to give  cannot  bhakti  without
to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa  that  liberation  comes  without knowledge
= Knowledge alone cannot give liberation without *bhakti*; but to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa that liberation comes even without knowledge.

@22
the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord
toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge
for them  this  toil  only  remains
nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks
= “O almighty Lord, *bhakti* to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)

@23
divine  indeed  this  made of the guṇas
My  māyā  hard to overcome
to Me  alone  those who  surrender
māyā  this  cross over  they
= “This divine māyā of Mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me alone cross beyond this māyā.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.14)

@24
Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant  the jīva  that  having forgotten  went
for that  fault  Māyā  his  around the neck  bound
= The *jīva* is Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant, but he has forgotten it. For that fault Māyā has bound him by the neck.

@25
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he renders  the guru’s  service
the net of Māyā  falls away  he attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
= If he then worships Kṛṣṇa and serves his *guru*, the net of Māyā falls away and he attains Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

@26
of the four  followers of varṇa and āśrama  if  Kṛṣṇa  do not  worship
their own duties  even performing  they  in the hell Raurava  having fallen  sink
= If the followers of the four *varṇas* and *āśramas* do not worship Kṛṣṇa, then even while performing their own duties they fall into the hell Raurava and sink there.

@27
from the mouth, arms, thighs and feet
of the Puruṣa  with the āśramas  together
four  were born  the varṇas
by their qualities  brāhmaṇas and the rest  distinct
= “From the mouth, arms, thighs and feet of the Puruṣa the four *varṇas*, beginning with the *brāhmaṇas*, were born together with the *āśramas*, each distinct by its qualities.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.2)

@28
who  of them  the Puruṣa  directly
their own source  the Lord
not  worship  disregard
from their position  fallen  they fall  down
= “Those among them who do not worship the Puruṣa, their own source and Lord, but disregard Him, fall from their position and go down.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.3)

@29
the jñānī  of liberation in this life  the state  “I have attained”  thus  thinks
in truth  the intelligence  pure  is not  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without
= The *jñānī* thinks, “I have attained the state of liberation in this life (*jīvan-mukti*).” But in truth the intelligence does not become pure without devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

@30
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there
fall  down  having neglected Your feet
= “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)
