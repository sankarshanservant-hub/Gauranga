@title The Path of Bhakti

@1
I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
the Lord  Him  the ocean of mercy
in Kali  even  this most hidden
bhakti  by whom  was revealed
= I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, the ocean of mercy, by whom this most hidden *bhakti* was revealed even in the age of Kali.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
thus  indeed  I have told  of sambandha-tattva  the analysis
of the Vedas and śāstras  in the teaching  Kṛṣṇa  the one  essence
= Thus I have explained the truth of *sambandha*: in the teaching of the Vedas and the scriptures, Kṛṣṇa is the one essence.

@4
now  I speak  listen  of abhidheya  the characteristics
which  through  one attains  Kṛṣṇa  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa
= Now listen as I describe *abhidheya*, the means by which one attains Kṛṣṇa and the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@5
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is the abhidheya  all the scriptures  declare
therefore  the sages  have made  the conclusion
= All the scriptures declare that devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the *abhidheya*, and so the sages have concluded.

@6
śruti  the mother  when asked  points out  of Your worship  the method
as  of the mother  the words  smṛti  too  so  speaks  the sister
the Purāṇas and the rest  which  and  the brothers  they  following her
therefore  truly  it is known  O slayer of Mura  You  alone  the shelter
= “When Mother Śruti is asked, she points out the method of worshipping You. Smṛti, her sister, speaks just as the mother does, and the Purāṇas and the rest, like brothers, follow her. Therefore, O slayer of Mura, I have truly understood that You alone are the shelter.”

@7
the Truth of nondual knowledge  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān
of svarūpa-śakti  in the form  His  is  abiding
= Kṛṣṇa, the nondual Truth (*advaya-jñāna-tattva*), is Bhagavān Himself. He abides in the form of His own internal energy (*svarūpa-śakti*).

@8
as His own parts and separated parts  in the form  having become  expanded
in endless  Vaikuṇṭhas and universes  He performs  pastimes
= Expanding Himself as His own parts (*svāṁśa*) and separated parts (*vibhinnāṁśa*), He sports in endless Vaikuṇṭhas and universes.

@9
the expansion of His own parts  the catur-vyūha  the host of avatāras
the separated part  the jīva  His  among the energies  is counted
= His own parts are the *catur-vyūha* and the host of *avatāras*. The *jīva* is His separated part and is counted among His energies.

@10
those  separated parts  the jīvas  two  indeed  kinds
one  eternally liberated  one  eternally in worldly existence
= Those *jīvas*, His separated parts, are of two kinds: some are eternally liberated (*nitya-mukta*), and some are eternally in worldly existence (*nitya-saṁsāra*).

@11
the eternally liberated  ever  toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet  turned
Kṛṣṇa’s associates  named  they enjoy  the joy of service
= The eternally liberated are ever turned toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet. They are called Kṛṣṇa’s associates (*pārṣadas*), and they enjoy the happiness of serving Him.

@12
the eternally bound  from Kṛṣṇa  eternally turned away
eternally in worldly existence  they suffer  the miseries of hell and the like
= The eternally bound (*nitya-bandha*) are forever turned away from Kṛṣṇa. Eternally caught in worldly existence, they suffer the miseries of hell and the like.

@13
for that  fault  the witch Māyā  punishment  inflicts  on him
beginning with those of body and mind  the threefold miseries  him  wearing down  torment
= For this fault the witch Māyā punishes him: the threefold miseries, beginning with those of body and mind (*ādhyātmika*), wear him down and torment him.

@14
of lust and anger  the servant  having become  her  kicks  he suffers
wandering  wandering  if  a sādhu-physician  he finds
= Become the servant of lust and anger, he suffers her kicks. But if, wandering on and on, he meets a *sādhu*, a physician of the soul,

@15
his  by the mantra of instruction  the witch  flees
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  he obtains  then  near Kṛṣṇa  he goes
= by the mantra of that *sādhu*’s instruction the witch flees. The soul obtains devotion to Kṛṣṇa and then comes near to Kṛṣṇa.

@16
of lust and the rest  how many  not  in how many ways  were obeyed  the evil commands
of them  arose  toward me  no  pity  no  shame  nor peace
having abandoned them  now  O Lord of the Yadus  at present  having gained good sense
to You  I have come  for shelter  fearless  me  engage in Your service
= “How many evil commands of lust and the rest have I not obeyed, and in how many ways! Yet they never took pity on me, nor did I feel shame or find peace. Now, O Lord of the Yadus, I have cast them off; good sense has dawned in me, and I have come to You, the fearless shelter. Engage me in Your service.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 6)

@17
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is  the chief abhidheya
on the face of bhakti  looking  karma  yoga  jñāna
= Devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the chief *abhidheya*. *Karma*, *yoga* and *jñāna* all look to the face of *bhakti* for their results.

@18
of these  all  practices  very  feeble  the strength
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without  they  to give  cannot  fruit
= The strength of all these practices is very feeble: without devotion to Kṛṣṇa they cannot give any fruit.

@19
freedom from action  even  love for Acyuta  devoid of
not  shines  knowledge  at all  stainless
how then  again  ever  inauspicious  to the Lord
not  offered  action  which  even  free of motive
= “Even stainless knowledge, free from all action (*naiṣkarmya*), does not shine if it is devoid of love for Acyuta. How then can action shine — by nature ever inauspicious — if it is not offered to the Lord, even when it is free of selfish motive?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.5.12)

@20
ascetics  devoted to charity  the renowned
the thoughtful  knowers of mantras  of auspicious conduct
welfare  not  attain  without  offering to Him
to Him  of auspicious fame  obeisance  obeisance
= “Ascetics, the generous, the renowned, the thoughtful, the knowers of mantras and those of auspicious conduct — none of them attains true welfare without offering everything to Him. Again and again I bow to Him, whose fame is auspicious.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.4.17)

@21
mere  knowledge  liberation  to give  cannot  bhakti  without
to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa  that  liberation  comes  without knowledge
= Knowledge alone cannot give liberation without *bhakti*; but to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa that liberation comes even without knowledge.

@22
the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord
toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge
for them  this  toil  only  remains
nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks
= “O almighty Lord, *bhakti* to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)

@23
divine  indeed  this  made of the guṇas
My  māyā  hard to overcome
to Me  alone  those who  surrender
māyā  this  cross over  they
= “This divine māyā of Mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me alone cross beyond this māyā.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.14)

@24
Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant  the jīva  that  having forgotten  went
for that  fault  Māyā  his  around the neck  bound
= The *jīva* is Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant, but he has forgotten it. For that fault Māyā has bound him by the neck.

@25
therefore  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he renders  the guru’s  service
the net of Māyā  falls away  he attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
= If he then worships Kṛṣṇa and serves his *guru*, the net of Māyā falls away and he attains Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

@26
of the four  followers of varṇa and āśrama  if  Kṛṣṇa  do not  worship
their own duties  even performing  they  in the hell Raurava  having fallen  sink
= If the followers of the four *varṇas* and *āśramas* do not worship Kṛṣṇa, then even while performing their own duties they fall into the hell Raurava and sink there.

@27
from the mouth, arms, thighs and feet
of the Puruṣa  with the āśramas  together
four  were born  the varṇas
by their qualities  brāhmaṇas and the rest  distinct
= “From the mouth, arms, thighs and feet of the Puruṣa the four *varṇas*, beginning with the *brāhmaṇas*, were born together with the *āśramas*, each distinct by its qualities.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.2)

@28
who  of them  the Puruṣa  directly
their own source  the Lord
not  worship  disregard
from their position  fallen  they fall  down
= “Those among them who do not worship the Puruṣa, their own source and Lord, but disregard Him, fall from their position and go down.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.3)

@29
the jñānī  of liberation in this life  the state  “I have attained”  thus  thinks
in truth  the intelligence  pure  is not  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without
= The *jñānī* thinks, “I have attained the state of liberation in this life (*jīvan-mukti*).” But in truth the intelligence does not become pure without devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

@30
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there
fall  down  having neglected Your feet
= “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)

@31
Kṛṣṇa  like the sun  Māyā  is  darkness
where  Kṛṣṇa  there  is no  of Māyā  dominion
= Kṛṣṇa is like the sun, and Māyā is darkness. Where Kṛṣṇa is, Māyā has no power.

@32
ashamed  whose
to stand  in the path of the sight  by her
bewildered  boast
“mine” and “I”  thus  the foolish
= “Māyā is ashamed to stand in the path of His sight; yet the foolish, bewildered by her, boast of ‘I’ and ‘mine.’” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.5.13)

@33
“Kṛṣṇa  Yours  I am”  if  he says  once
from the bondage of Māyā  Kṛṣṇa  him  makes  carried across
= If someone says even once, “Kṛṣṇa, I am Yours,” Kṛṣṇa carries him across, beyond the bondage of Māyā.

@34
once  only  surrendered  who
“I am Yours”  thus  and  begs
fearlessness  always  to him
I give  this  vow  My
= “To one who surrenders to Me even once and prays, ‘I am Yours,’ I always grant fearlessness. This is My vow.” (Rāmāyaṇa, Yuddha-kāṇḍa, 18.33)

@35
desiring liberation, enjoyment and perfections  truly intelligent  if  he becomes
by deep bhakti-yoga  then  Kṛṣṇa  he worships
= If one who desires liberation, worldly enjoyment or mystic perfections becomes truly intelligent, he then worships Kṛṣṇa with deep *bhakti-yoga*.

@36
without desire  full of all desires  or
desiring liberation  of broad intelligence
with intense  bhakti-yoga
should worship  the Person  Supreme
= “Whether he is without desire, full of every desire, or desirous of liberation, a person of broad intelligence should worship the Supreme Person with intense *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.10)

@37
one with other desires  if  performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  worship
not  even having asked  Kṛṣṇa  to him  gives  His own feet
= If one who has other desires worships Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa gives him His own feet even though he has not asked for them.

@38
Kṛṣṇa  says  Me  he worships  he asks for  sense  pleasure
nectar  giving up  poison  he asks for  this one  a great  fool
= Kṛṣṇa says, “He worships Me, yet asks for sense pleasure. Giving up nectar, he asks for poison — he is a great fool.

@39
I  am wise  to this  fool  sense objects  why  should I give
the nectar of My own feet  giving  sense objects  I shall make him forget
= “But I am wise: why should I give sense objects to this fool? I will give him the nectar of My own feet and make him forget sense objects.”

@40
truly  He grants  what is asked  when asked  to men
not indeed  giver of that  which  again  asking  from which
Himself  He grants  to those who worship  though not desiring
covering all desires  His own  feet like tender shoots
= “It is true that when men ask, Kṛṣṇa grants what they ask for; but He does not give what would make them ask again. To those who worship Him, though they do not desire it, He Himself gives His own feet, tender as new shoots, which cover over all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.19.27)

@41
desire  for the sake of  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he attains  the taste of Kṛṣṇa
desire  giving up  a servant  to become  there arises  the longing
= One who worships Kṛṣṇa for the sake of some desire comes to taste Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness (*kṛṣṇa-rasa*). Then, giving up that desire, he longs to become His servant.

@42
desiring a position  in austerity  engaged  I
You  have attained  hidden from the gods and the best of sages
glass  searching for  though  a divine jewel
O Master  fulfilled  I am  a boon  not  I ask
= “Desiring a high position, I engaged in austerities, and I have attained You, who are hidden even from the gods and the greatest sages. I was searching for a piece of glass and found a divine jewel. O Master, my purpose is fulfilled; I ask for no boon.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 7.28)

@43
in worldly existence  while wandering  by some  fortune  someone  is delivered
of a river  in the current  as  a log  touches  the bank
= While wandering in worldly existence, someone by some good fortune is delivered, as a log carried by a river’s current comes to rest on the bank.

@44
not so  of me  lowly  even
there will be  surely  the sight of Acyuta
being carried away  by the river of time
at times  crosses over  someone
= “It is not so that I, lowly as I am, cannot see Acyuta: I will surely see Him. For even among those carried away by the river of time, someone at times reaches the shore.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.38.5)

@45
by some  fortune  someone’s  worldly existence  near its end  becomes
in the company of sādhus  he is delivered  for Kṛṣṇa  attraction  arises
= When by some good fortune someone’s worldly existence nears its end, he is delivered through the company of *sādhus*, and attraction (*rati*) for Kṛṣṇa awakens in him.

@46
the end of worldly existence  of one wandering  when  comes
of a person  then  O Acyuta  meeting with the saints
association with the saints  when  at that very time  for the goal of the saints
for the Lord of the high and the low  for You  is born  attraction
= “O Acyuta, when a wandering soul’s worldly existence comes to its end, he meets the saints. And when he associates with the saints, attraction (*rati*) is born in him for You, the goal of the saints and the Lord of the high and the low.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.51.53)

@47
Kṛṣṇa  if  mercy  shows  to some  fortunate soul
in the forms of guru and the Indweller  teaches  Himself
= If Kṛṣṇa shows mercy to some fortunate soul, He Himself teaches him in the forms of the *guru* and the Indweller (*antaryāmī*).

@48
not  attain  repayment  the wise  Your,  O Lord
with Brahmā’s lifespan  even  Your deeds  with growing joy  remembering
who  within  without  of the embodied  the inauspicious  dispelling
as ācārya  and Supersoul  in forms  Your own path  reveal
= “O Lord! Even if the wise lived as long as Brahmā and remembered with ever-growing joy all that You have done for them, they could not repay their debt to You. For, dispelling all that is inauspicious in embodied souls, You come to them from without as the ācārya and from within as the Supersoul, and reveal to them the way to Yourself.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.29.6)

@49
in the company of sādhus  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  faith  if  arises
the fruit of bhakti  prema  comes  worldly existence  goes  to its end
= If, in the company of *sādhus*, faith in devotion to Kṛṣṇa arises, then *prema*, the fruit of *bhakti*, appears, and worldly existence comes to an end.

@50
by good fortune  in narrations about Me and the rest
in whom faith is born  but  who  the person
not  disgusted  not overly attached
bhakti-yoga  for him  gives perfection
= “For one who by good fortune has come to have faith in narrations about Me and the like, and who is neither disgusted with the world nor overly attached to it, *bhakti-yoga* grants perfection.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.8)

@51
of the great souls  the mercy  without  by any  activity  bhakti  is not
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  aside  let it be  worldly existence  does not  end
= Without the mercy of great souls no activity yields *bhakti*. Devotion to Kṛṣṇa aside, without it even worldly existence does not come to an end.

@52
O Rahūgaṇa  this  by austerity  not  is attained
not  and by worship  by renunciation  by household life  or
not  by the Vedas  nor  by water, fire and sun
without  bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls
= “O Rahūgaṇa, this is not attained by austerity, by worship, by renunciation or by household duties, nor by the Vedas, nor by worship of water, fire and the sun, without bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.12.12)

@53
not  their  mind  so long  Urukrama’s feet
touches  the removal of anarthas  whose purpose
of the great  bathing in the dust of the feet
of those who own nothing  not  accepts  as long as
= “So long as people do not accept a bath in the dust of the feet of the great, who own nothing (*niṣkiñcana*), their minds cannot touch the feet of Urukrama, whose purpose is to remove all *anarthas*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.5.32)

@54
sādhu-saṅga  sādhu-saṅga  all the scriptures  declare
a mere moment of  sādhu-saṅga  all perfection  comes
= “The company of *sādhus*, the company of *sādhus*!” — so declare all the scriptures. Even a moment of *sādhu-saṅga* brings every perfection.

@55
we compare  with a moment  even
not  heaven  not liberation
of association with those who associate with the Lord
of mortals  what  then  the blessings
= “We cannot compare even heaven or liberation with a moment of association with those who associate with the Lord — what then of the blessings mortals seek?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.18.13)

@56
Kṛṣṇa  merciful  Arjuna  the aim  making
the world  has protected  instruction  giving
= Merciful Kṛṣṇa, speaking to Arjuna, has protected the whole world by giving this instruction:

@57
the most secret of all  again
hear  My  supreme  word
dear  you are  to Me  very  thus
therefore  I shall speak  to you  your good
= “Hear again My supreme word, the most secret of all. You are very dear to Me, and therefore I shall tell you what is for your good.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.64)

@58
with mind fixed on Me  be  My devotee
worshipping Me  to Me  bow down
to Me  surely you will come  truly  to you
I promise  dear  you are  to Me
= “Fix your mind on Me, be My devotee, worship Me and bow down to Me. You will surely come to Me — truly I promise you this, for you are dear to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.65)

@59
the earlier  commands  Vedic dharma  karma  yoga  jñāna
all  having established  the final command  is strongest
= His earlier commands concerned Vedic *dharma*, *karma*, *yoga* and *jñāna*. Having established all of these, He gave His final command, and it is the strongest.

@60
of this  by the force of the command  in the devotee  faith  if  arises
all activities  giving up  having made  he  Kṛṣṇa  worships
= If by the force of this command faith arises in the devotee, he gives up all other activities and worships Kṛṣṇa.
