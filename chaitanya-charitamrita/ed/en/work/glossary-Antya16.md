# English names and terms — CC Antya 16 (stage A; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 16»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` are used as given there: *mahā-prasāda*, *mahā-mahā-prasāda*;
remnants (*ucchiṣṭa*), *jhuṭā*; pious merit (*sukṛti*); *upala-bhoga*; *jagamohana*; the Garuḍa pillar;
Madana-mohana; *pralāpa* (raving); divine madness (*unmāda*). Names as in the commentaries on this chapter
(`ed/en/Antya16-apb.md`, `-anu.md`): Kālidāsa, Jhaḍu Ṭhākura, Purī dāsa, Śivānanda, Siṁha-dvāra.
Verbatim from revised chapters (`ed/en-rev/`): 2 (Ādi 1.18), 26 (Antya 4.69), 27 (Madhya 11.192); 25 (Madhya 19.50);
116 line 2 (Antya 14.55); verse 87 ends “makes me mad with joy”, as in the other verses of the *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*.
