@title Sanātana Gosvāmī Comes to Śrī Caitanya

@1
from Vṛndāvana  again  who had come
Śrī Gaura  Śrī Sanātana
from the fall of the body  protecting  out of affection
pure  made  by a test
= When Śrī Sanātana came again from Vṛndāvana, Śrī Gaura, out of affection, saved him from giving up his body and, by a test, made him pure.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
Nīlācala  from  Rūpa  to Gauḍa  when  went
Mathurā  from  Sanātana  to Nīlācala  came
= When Rūpa went from Nīlācala to Gauḍa, Sanātana came from Mathurā to Nīlācala.

@4
by the Jhārikhaṇḍa forest path  he came  alone  walking
sometimes  fasting  sometimes  chewing  doing
= He came alone, on foot, by the forest path through Jhārikhaṇḍa, sometimes fasting, sometimes eating only a little dry food to chew.

@5
of Jhārikhaṇḍa  of the water  through the fault  fasting  from
on the body  sores  appeared  fluid  oozes  on scratching
= Because of the bad water of Jhārikhaṇḍa and from fasting, sores broke out on his body, and when he scratched them, fluid oozed from them.

@6
detachment  arose  on the way  he makes  reflection
low  birth  body  my  utterly  worthless
= On the way a mood of detachment (*nirveda*) arose in him, and he reflected: “I am of low birth, and my body is utterly worthless.

@7
to Jagannātha  if I go  His  sight  not  I shall obtain
of the Lord  sight  always  to have  I shall not be able
= If I go to Jagannātha, I shall not obtain His *darśana*, nor shall I be able to see the Lord at all times.

@8
the temple  near  I hear  His  residence  situated
the temple  near  to go  my  there is not  power
= I hear that His residence is near the temple, but I have no power to go near the temple.

@9
of Jagannātha  the servants  move about  of their duties  at the demand
their  touch  if there is  my  there will be  offense
= The servants of Jagannātha move about there on their duties; if I touch them, I shall be guilty of an offense.

@10
therefore  if  this  body  in a good place  I give
of sorrow  relief  there is  and  a good destination  I attain
= So if I give this body up in a good place, my sorrow will be eased and I shall attain a good destination.

@11
Jagannātha  at the Ratha-yātrā  will be  outside
His  under the chariot’s wheel  I shall give up  this  body
= At the Ratha-yātrā Jagannātha will come out; under the wheel of His chariot I shall give up this body.

@12
of Mahāprabhu  before  and  seeing  Jagannātha
at the chariot  the body  I shall give up  this  the supreme goal of life
= Before Mahāprabhu, and seeing Jagannātha, I shall give up my body at the chariot: this is the highest goal of life.”

@13
this  indeed  resolve  making  to Nīlācala  he came
people  asking  at Haridāsa’s place  he arrived
= Having made this resolve, he came to Nīlācala and, asking people the way, arrived at the dwelling of Haridāsa.

@14
of Haridāsa  made  he  of the feet  worship
knowing (him)  Haridāsa  him  made  embrace
= He bowed down at the feet of Haridāsa, and Haridāsa, recognizing him, embraced him.

@15
Mahāprabhu  to see  his  eager  mind
Haridāsa  says  the Lord  will come  now
= His heart was eager to see Mahāprabhu. Haridāsa said, “The Lord will come now.”

@16
at that time  the Lord  the ‘upala-bhoga’  having seen
Haridāsa  to meet  came  the devotees  taking
= Just then the Lord, after seeing the *upala-bhoga* offering, came with His devotees to meet Haridāsa.

@17
the Lord  seeing  both  fall  flat like rods  becoming
the Lord  embraced  Haridāsa  raising
= Seeing the Lord, both of them fell flat like rods. The Lord raised Haridāsa and embraced him.

@18
Haridāsa  says  Sanātana  makes  obeisance
Sanātana  seeing  the Lord  became  astonished
= Haridāsa said, “Sanātana is offering You obeisance.” Seeing Sanātana, the Lord was astonished.

@19
Sanātana  to embrace  the Lord  forward  came
back  draws  Sanātana  to speak  began
= The Lord came forward to embrace Sanātana, but Sanātana drew back and began to say:

@20
me  do not  touch  O Lord  I fall  at Your  feet
first  of low birth  fallen  and  oozing sores  on the body
= “Do not touch me, O Lord, I fall at Your feet! I am low-born and fallen, and besides, my body is covered with oozing sores.”

@21
by force  the Lord  him  embrace  made
the moisture of the sores  Mahāprabhu’s  the holy body  touched
= But the Lord embraced him by force, and the moisture of his sores touched Mahāprabhu’s holy body.

@22
all  with the devotees  the Lord  made meet  Sanātana
Sanātana  made  of all  of the feet  worship
= The Lord introduced Sanātana to all the devotees, and Sanātana bowed down at the feet of each of them.

@23
the Lord  with  sat  the raised platform  upon  the devotees
of the platform  below  sat  Haridāsa  Sanātana
= The devotees sat with the Lord on the raised platform, while Haridāsa and Sanātana sat below the platform.

@24
of welfare  news  Mahāprabhu  asks  Sanātana
he  says  supreme  good fortune  I have seen  the feet
= Mahāprabhu asked Sanātana how he was. He replied, “All is supremely auspicious, for I have seen Your feet.”

@25
of Mathurā  of all the Vaiṣṇavas  the welfare  He asked
of all  the welfare  Sanātana  made known
= He asked after the welfare of all the Vaiṣṇavas of Mathurā, and Sanātana told Him that all were well.

@26
the Lord  says  here  Rūpa  was  ten  months
here  from  to Gauḍa  he went  it has been  days  ten
= The Lord said, “Rūpa was here for ten months. He left here for Gauḍa ten days ago.

@27
your  brother  of Anupama  took place  attainment of the Gaṅgā
good  he was  in Raghunātha  firm  his  devotion
= Your brother Anupama has departed from this world on the bank of the Gaṅgā. He was a good soul; his devotion to Raghunātha (Śrī Rāma) was firm.”

@28
Sanātana  says  in a low  family  my  birth
irreligion  wrongdoing  whatever  my  family’s way
= Sanātana said, “I was born in a low family; every kind of irreligion and wrongdoing was the custom of my family.

@29
such  a family  aversion  giving up  You made  acceptance
by Your  mercy  in the family  auspiciousness  my
= Yet, setting aside all aversion, You accepted such a family; by Your mercy, all is auspicious in my family.

@30
that  Anupama  brother  of childhood  from the time
Raghunātha  worship  does  with a firm mind
= From his childhood my brother Anupama worshipped Raghunātha with a firm mind.

@31
night  and day  of Raghunātha  the ‘Name’  and  ‘meditation’
the Rāmāyaṇa  incessantly  he hears  makes  singing
= Night and day he chanted the Name of Raghunātha and meditated on Him; incessantly he heard the *Rāmāyaṇa* and sang it.

@32
I  and  Rūpa  his  elder  brothers
us  two  with  he  stays  constantly
= Rūpa and I are his elder brothers, and he always stayed with the two of us.

@33
us  all  with  talks of Kṛṣṇa  the Bhāgavata  he hears
his  test  made  we  two
= Together with us he would hear talks about Kṛṣṇa and the *Bhāgavata*. The two of us put him to a test.

@34
hear  Vallabha  Kṛṣṇa  supremely  sweet
beauty  sweetness  love’s  pastimes  abundant
= ‘Listen, Vallabha: Kṛṣṇa is supremely sweet. His beauty, His sweetness and the pastimes of His love are boundless.

@35
the worship of Kṛṣṇa  do  you  us  two  with
three  brothers  together  we shall live  in the joy of talks about Kṛṣṇa
= Worship Kṛṣṇa together with the two of us. We three brothers will live together, rejoicing in talks about Kṛṣṇa.’

@36
in this way  again and again  speak  two  persons
of us  two  out of respect  somewhat  turned  his mind
= In this way the two of us spoke to him again and again, and out of respect for us his mind turned somewhat.

@37
of you  two  the order  I  how  shall disobey
initiation-mantra  give  the worship of Kṛṣṇa  I shall do
= ‘How can I disobey the order of you two? Give me the initiation mantra, and I shall worship Kṛṣṇa.’

@38
so  saying  at night  he makes  reflection
how  shall I give up  of Raghunātha  the feet
= Having said this, he reflected through the night: ‘How shall I give up the feet of Raghunātha?’

@39
the whole  night  weeping  doing  he made  keeping awake
in the morning  to us  two  he made  submission
= He stayed awake the whole night, weeping, and in the morning he submitted to the two of us:

@40
of Raghunātha  at the lotus feet  I have sold  my head
to take back  not  I can  my head  I feel  great  pain
= ‘I have sold my head at the lotus feet of Raghunātha. I cannot take my head back; it gives me great pain.

@41
mercy  doing  to me  permission  give  you two
birth  after birth  may I serve  of Raghunātha  the feet
= Be merciful, both of you, and give me leave to serve the feet of Raghunātha birth after birth.

@42
of Raghunātha  the lotus feet  to give up  not  is possible
of giving up  the thought  when it comes  my life  bursting  goes
= I cannot give up the lotus feet of Raghunātha; at the very thought of giving them up, my heart breaks.’

@43
then  we two  him  embrace  made
excellent  firm devotion  your  saying  we praised
= Then the two of us embraced him and praised him, saying, ‘Excellent! Your devotion is firm.’

@44
which  family  upon  Your  there is  a trace of mercy
all  auspiciousness  in it  are cut away  all  troubles
= The family upon which there is even a trace of Your mercy has every blessing, and all its troubles are cut away.”

@45
the Lord  says  in this way  Murāri Gupta
before  I  tested  his  this  way
= The Lord said, “In the same way I once tested Murāri Gupta; this was his way too.

@46
that  devotee  blessed  who  not  leaves  of the master  the feet
that  master  blessed  who  not  leaves  his own people
= Blessed is the devotee who does not leave the feet of his master, and blessed is the master who does not abandon his own.

@47
by misfortune  a servant  if  goes  to another place
that  master  blessed  him  by the hair  seizing  brings back
= If by misfortune a servant goes elsewhere, blessed is the master who seizes him by the hair and brings him back.

@48
good  it was  your  here  took place  coming
in this  house  stay  here  Haridāsa  with
= It is good that you have come here. Stay here in this house with Haridāsa.

@49
in the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  you both  supremely  foremost
Kṛṣṇa’s rasa  relishing  do  take  Kṛṣṇa’s Name
= You are both supremely eminent in the *rasa* of devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Relish the *rasa* of Kṛṣṇa and chant the Name of Kṛṣṇa.”

@50
so  saying  Mahāprabhu  rising  departed
through Govinda  to both  prasāda  He sent
= Having said this, Mahāprabhu rose and left, and He sent *prasāda* to both of them through Govinda.

@51
in this way  Sanātana  stays  near the Lord
of Jagannātha  the wheel  seeing  makes  obeisance
= Thus Sanātana stayed near the Lord. Seeing the wheel (the Nīla-cakra) atop Jagannātha’s temple, he would bow down.

@52
the Lord  coming  every day  meets  the two
intimate discussion  talks of Kṛṣṇa  speaks  for some time
= Every day the Lord came and met them both, and for a while He spoke intimately with them about Kṛṣṇa.

@53
divine  prasāda  receiving  daily  in Jagannātha’s temple
that  bringing  daily  without fail  gives  to both
= Every day He received the divine *prasāda* in the temple of Jagannātha, and every day without fail He brought it and gave it to them both.

@54
one day  coming  the Lord  the two  met
to Sanātana  suddenly  to speak  began
= One day the Lord came and met them both, and suddenly began to speak to Sanātana:

@55
Sanātana  by giving up the body  Kṛṣṇa  if  one obtains
millions of bodies  in a moment  then  to give up  one could
= “Sanātana, if Kṛṣṇa could be attained by giving up the body, I would give up millions of bodies in a moment.

@56
by giving up the body  Kṛṣṇa  not  one obtains  one obtains  by worship
of attaining Kṛṣṇa  means  any  there is not  ‘devotion’  without
= One does not attain Kṛṣṇa by giving up the body; one attains Him by worship. There is no means of attaining Kṛṣṇa except *bhakti*.

@57
giving up the body and the like  whatever  all  the nature of darkness
by the ways of darkness and passion  of Kṛṣṇa  not  one obtains  the essence
= Giving up the body and all such acts are of the nature of darkness (*tamo-dharma*); through the ways of darkness and passion one cannot grasp the essence of Kṛṣṇa.

@58
‘devotion’  without  for Kṛṣṇa  ever  not  ‘love’s awakening’
love  without  attainment of Kṛṣṇa  anything else  from  is not
= Without *bhakti*, love for Kṛṣṇa never awakens; and without love, Kṛṣṇa is not attained by any other means.

@59
not  wins  Me  yoga
not  sāṅkhya  dharma  O Uddhava
not  Vedic study  austerity  renunciation
as  devotion  to Me  intense
= (Śrī Kṛṣṇa said:) “O Uddhava, neither yoga nor sāṅkhya, neither dharma nor study of the Vedas, neither austerity nor renunciation can win Me as intense devotion to Me does.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.20)

@60
giving up the body and the like  the nature of darkness  the cause of sin
the practitioner  not  obtains  by that  of Kṛṣṇa  the feet
= Giving up the body and the like are of the nature of darkness and a cause of sin; by them the *sādhaka* does not attain the feet of Kṛṣṇa.

@61
a loving  devotee  in separation  wishes  the body  to give up
by love  Kṛṣṇa  is met  he  not  can  die
= A loving devotee in separation wishes to give up his body; but by that very love he meets Kṛṣṇa, and he cannot die.

@62
of deep attachment  the separation  not  can be  borne
therefore  the one full of attachment  desires  his own  death
= Separation in deep *anurāga* cannot be borne; therefore one who is full of *anurāga* longs for his own death.

@63
whose  feet  lotus  in the dust  bathing  the great souls
desire  Umā’s husband (Śiva)  like  their own  darkness  for dispelling
if  O lotus-eyed one  not  I obtain  Your  grace
I shall give up  my life  by vows  emaciated  through a hundred births  it may be
= (Rukmiṇī wrote to Kṛṣṇa:) “O lotus-eyed one, great souls like Umā’s lord (Śiva) long to bathe in the dust of Your lotus feet to dispel their own darkness. If I do not obtain Your grace, I shall give up my life, emaciated by vows, so that, even after a hundred births, it may be mine.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.52.43)

@64
sprinkle  O dear one  our  Your  lips’  nectar  with the flood
smiling glance  sweet  song  born of  love’s desire  the fire
not  if  we  by the fire born of separation  consumed  bodies
by meditation  we shall go  of the feet  to the place  O friend  Your
= (The *gopīs* said:) “O dear one, with the flood of nectar from Your lips quench the fire of love’s desire that Your smiling glances and sweet song have kindled in our hearts. Otherwise, O friend, our bodies consumed by the fire of separation, we shall reach the place of Your feet by meditation.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.35)

@65
wicked intelligence  giving up  do  hearing and chanting
soon  you will obtain  then  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa
= Give up this wicked intelligence (*kubuddhi*) and engage in hearing and chanting; then you will soon obtain the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@66
one of low birth  is not  for the worship of Kṛṣṇa  unfit
a brāhmaṇa of good family  is not  for worship  fit
= One of low birth is not unfit for the worship of Kṛṣṇa, nor is a *brāhmaṇa* of good family fit for it by that alone.

@67
whoever  worships  he  is great  the non-devotee  low  contemptible
in the worship of Kṛṣṇa  there is not  caste family and the like  consideration
= Whoever worships is great; the non-devotee is low and contemptible. In the worship of Kṛṣṇa there is no consideration of caste, family and the like.

@68
to the lowly  greater  kindness  shows  Bhagavān
the high-born  the learned  the rich  great  pride
= Bhagavān shows greater kindness to the lowly; the high-born, the learned and the rich have great pride.

@69
than a brāhmaṇa  twelve  qualities  endowed with  of the lotus-naveled Lord
from the lotus feet  averse  a dog-eater  better
I consider  to Him offered  mind  words  deeds  wealth
and life  purifies  he  his family  not  but  one full of pride
= “I consider a dog-eater whose mind, words, deeds, wealth and very life are offered to the Lord of the lotus navel better than a brāhmaṇa endowed with the twelve qualities who has turned away from His lotus feet. Such a devotee purifies his whole family; the brāhmaṇa swollen with pride cannot.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.10)

@70
of the forms of worship  among  the best  ninefold  devotion
‘love for Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  to give  holds  great power
= Among the forms of worship the best is the ninefold *bhakti*: it has great power to bestow love for Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa Himself.

@71
of them  among  the best of all  the chanting of the Name
without offense  the Name  if one takes  obtains  the treasure of love
= Of these the best of all is *nāma-saṅkīrtana*. One who chants the Name without offense obtains the treasure of love.”

@72
this  hearing  of Sanātana  there was  astonishment
to the Lord  not  seems fit  my  resolve to die
= Hearing this, Sanātana was astonished: “My resolve to give up my body does not please the Lord.

@73
the omniscient  Mahāprabhu  has forbidden  me
of the Lord  the feet  clasping  he says  to Him
= The all-knowing Mahāprabhu has forbidden me.” Clasping the Lord’s feet, he said to Him:

@74
omniscient  merciful  You  the Lord  independent
as  You make dance  so  I dance  like  a wooden puppet
= “You are all-knowing and merciful — the independent Lord. As You make me dance, so I dance, like a wooden puppet.

@75
low  fallen  wretched  I  wretched by nature
me  if You keep alive  Your  what  will be  gain
= I am low, fallen and wretched, wretched by nature. What will You gain by keeping me alive?”

@76
the Lord  says  your  body  My  own property
you  to Me  have made  self-surrender
= The Lord said, “Your body is My own property, for you have surrendered yourself to Me.

@77
another’s  property  you  why  wish  to destroy
right and wrong  discrimination  is it that  not  you can  make
= Why do you wish to destroy another’s property? Can you not discern right from wrong?

@78
your  body  My  chief  ‘instrument’
by this  body  shall accomplish  I  many  purposes
= Your body is My chief instrument (*sādhana*); through this body I shall accomplish many purposes:

@79
of the devotee  devotion  love for Kṛṣṇa  of the truths  the establishing
of the Vaiṣṇava  the duties  and  Vaiṣṇava conduct
= establishing the truths about the devotee, devotion and love for Kṛṣṇa; the duties of a Vaiṣṇava, and Vaiṣṇava conduct;

@80
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  of service in love for Kṛṣṇa  the introduction
of lost holy places  the recovery  and  of renunciation  teaching
= introducing devotion to Kṛṣṇa and service in love for Kṛṣṇa; recovering the lost holy places, and teaching renunciation.
