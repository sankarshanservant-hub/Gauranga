@title Talks with Rāmānanda Rāya

@1

of His own  devotion  of the conclusions  of the hosts  the nectars
the ocean Gaura  by these  by him  poured out
of knowing them  the abode of jewels  the state  attains
= The ocean Gaura infused into the cloud — His devotee named Rāma — the nectars of the many conclusions of His own devotion; and when that cloud poured them out, the ocean itself became a treasury of jewels: the knowledge of those conclusions.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
as before  the Lord  onward  journey  made
at the holy place of Jiyaḍa-Nṛsiṁha  after some days  He arrived
= As before, the Lord journeyed on, and after some days He came to the holy place of Jiyaḍa-Nṛsiṁha.

@4
Nṛsiṁha  seeing  He made  prostrate  obeisance
absorbed in love  He offered  much  dancing  singing  praise
= Seeing Nṛsiṁha, He fell prostrate before Him, and, absorbed in love, He danced, sang and offered many prayers.

@5
Śrī Nṛsiṁha  glory  to Nṛsiṁha  glory  glory  to Nṛsiṁha
to the Lord of Prahlāda  glory  of Padmā  of the face  of the lotus  to the bee
= “Śrī Nṛsiṁha! Glory to Nṛsiṁha! Glory, glory to Nṛsiṁha! Glory to the Lord of Prahlāda, the bee at the lotus of Padmā’s face!

@6
fierce  though  not fierce  indeed  this
to His own  devotees  the man-lion
like a lion  to its own  cubs
to others  fierce  in might
= “Fierce though He is, this Man-lion is not fierce to His own devotees — like a lion, gentle to its own cubs and fierce in its might to others.” (Śrīdhara Svāmī’s commentary on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.1)

@7
in this way  various  verses  reciting  praise  He offered
of Nṛsiṁha  the servant  garland  prasāda  bringing  gave
= Reciting many such verses, He offered His praise. Then a servant of Nṛsiṁha brought Him the Deity’s garland and prasāda.

@8
as before  some  brāhmaṇa  made  an invitation
that  night  there  staying  He made  departure
= As before, a certain brāhmaṇa invited Him. He stayed there that night and then went on.

@9
in the morning  rising  the Lord  went on  absorbed in love
of directions  of the intermediate directions  no  awareness  night and day
= Rising in the morning, the Lord walked on, absorbed in love, unaware, night and day, of any direction.

@10
as before  Vaiṣṇavas  making  all  the people
on the bank of the Godāvarī  the Lord  arrived  after some days
= As before, He made Vaiṣṇavas of all the people, and after some days the Lord came to the bank of the Godāvarī.

@11
the Godāvarī  seeing  arose  of the Yamunā  remembrance
on the bank  the forest  seeing  remembrance  arose  of Vṛndāvana
= Seeing the Godāvarī, He remembered the Yamunā; seeing the forest on its bank, He remembered Vṛndāvana.

@12
in that  forest  for some time  doing  dancing and singing
the Godāvarī  crossing  there  He took  bath
= He danced and sang in that forest for some time; then He crossed the Godāvarī and bathed there.

@13
the ghāṭa  leaving  some distance away  near the water
sitting  the Lord  does  of Kṛṣṇa’s  names  saṅkīrtana
= Leaving the bathing-place, He sat by the water some distance away and chanted the names of Kṛṣṇa.

@14
at that time  in a palanquin  riding  Rāmānanda  Rāya
bath  to take  came  music  playing
= Just then Rāmānanda Rāya arrived in a palanquin to bathe, with music playing.

@15
with him  together  many  came  Vedic  brāhmaṇas
by the rules  performed  he  bathing and the rest  tarpaṇa
= Many Vedic brāhmaṇas came with him. He bathed and offered tarpaṇa and the other rites according to the rules.

@16
the Lord  him  seeing  knew  this is  Rāmānanda Rāya
him  to meet  of the Lord  the mind  rising  runs
= Seeing him, the Lord knew: “This is Rāmānanda Rāya,” and His heart leapt up and ran out to meet him.

@17
still  composure  keeping  the Lord  remained  seated
Rāmānanda  came  a wonderful  sannyāsī  seeing
= Still, the Lord kept His composure and remained seated. Seeing a wonderful sannyāsī, Rāmānanda came to Him.

@18
to a hundred suns  equal  radiance  dawn-coloured  garment
well-formed  tall  body  lotus  eyes
= His radiance was like that of a hundred suns, His garment the colour of dawn; His body was tall and beautifully formed, His eyes like lotuses.

@19
seeing  in his  mind  arose  wonder
coming near  he made  prostrate  obeisance
= Seeing Him, Rāmānanda was struck with wonder. He came forward and fell prostrate before Him.

@20
rising  the Lord  says  rise  say  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa
him  to embrace  of the Lord  the heart  longing
= The Lord rose and said, “Get up — say ‘Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!’” His heart longed to embrace him.

@21
still  He asked  you  Rāya  Rāmānanda
he  said  yes  I  servant  a śūdra  lowly
= Still He asked, “Are you Rāya Rāmānanda?” He replied, “Yes, I am Your servant, a lowly śūdra.”

@22
then  him  made  the Lord  firm  embrace
absorbed in love  Master and servant  both  unconscious
= Then the Lord held him in a firm embrace, and, absorbed in love, Master and servant both lost consciousness.

@23
natural  love  of both  awakening  made
each other  embracing  both  on the ground  fell
= The love natural to both of them awoke; embracing each other, they fell to the ground.

@24
stupor  sweat  tears  trembling  hair standing on end  pallor
of both  in the mouths  one heard  faltering  Kṛṣṇa  the syllables
= Stupor, sweat, tears, trembling, hair standing on end, change of colour — and from the lips of both came the name “Kṛṣṇa” in a faltering voice.

@25
seeing  of the brāhmaṇas  arose  wonder
Vedic  brāhmaṇas  all  make  reflection
= Seeing this, the brāhmaṇas were amazed, and all those Vedic brāhmaṇas began to reason among themselves:

@26
this  indeed  of the sannyāsī  splendour  we see  to Brahman  equal
a śūdra  embracing  why  does He  weeping
= “We see that this sannyāsī’s splendour is like that of Brahman. Why does He weep, embracing a śūdra?

@27
this  mahārāja  a great scholar  grave
of the sannyāsī  by the touch  maddened  became  unsteady
= And this mahārāja is a great scholar and a grave man, yet at the touch of this sannyāsī he has become maddened and lost all composure.”

@28
in this way  the brāhmaṇas  think  in the mind  to themselves
outsiders  people  seeing  the Lord  made  restraint
= So the brāhmaṇas reflected within themselves. Seeing people who were strangers to such feelings, the Lord restrained Himself.

@29
composed  becoming  both  in that  place  sat down
then  smiling  Mahāprabhu  to speak  began
= Growing calm, the two sat down on that spot, and Mahāprabhu, smiling, began to speak.

@30
Sārvabhauma  Bhaṭṭācārya  told  of your  virtues
you  to meet  Me  made (urge)  earnestly
= “Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya told Me of your virtues and earnestly urged Me to meet you.

@31
you  to meet  My  here  coming
good  it was  without effort  I obtained  sight (of you)
= It was to meet you that I came here, and how good it is that I have found you without any effort!”

@32
Rāya  said  Sārvabhauma  holds  as a servant
even out of sight  for my  welfare  is  careful
= Rāya said, “Sārvabhauma regards me as his servant; even in my absence he is careful of my welfare.

@33
by his  mercy  I obtained  Your  sight
today  successful  became  my  human birth
= By his mercy I have obtained Your sight; today my human birth has borne its fruit.

@34
to Sārvabhauma  Your  mercy  of it  this  sign
an untouchable  You touched  being  of his  love subject to
= Your mercy to Sārvabhauma is plain from this: yielding to his love, You have touched an untouchable.

@35
where  You  directly  the Lord  Nārāyaṇa
where  I  a king’s servant  worldly  lowest of śūdras
= Where are You — the Lord Nārāyaṇa Himself — and where am I — a servant of the king, a worldly man, the lowest of śūdras?

@36
my  touch  not  You showed  disgust  fear of the Vedas
me  to see  You  the Vedas  forbid
= You did not shrink from touching me, nor did You fear the Vedas, though the Vedas forbid You even to see me.

@37
Your  mercy  You  makes do  a blameworthy deed
directly  the Lord  You  who  knows  Your  heart
= Your mercy makes You do what is condemned. You are the Lord Himself — who can know Your heart?

@38
me  to deliver  Your  here  coming
supremely compassionate  You  of the fallen  the purifier
= You have come here to deliver me. You are supremely compassionate, the purifier of the fallen.

@39
of great souls  the nature  this  to deliver  the wretched
own business  not  yet  they go  to their  houses
= This is the nature of great souls: to deliver the wretched. Though they have no business of their own, they still go to their houses.

@40
of the great  the coming  of people
householders  poor in mind
for the highest good  O Lord
not  otherwise  is meant  ever
= “O lord, when great souls come to people — to householders of poor understanding — it is for nothing else than their highest good.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.4)

@41
with me  together  brāhmaṇas and others  about a thousand  people
at Your  sight  of all  melted  the hearts
= About a thousand people have come with me, brāhmaṇas and others, and at the sight of You all their hearts have melted.

@42
Kṛṣṇa  Hari  names  I hear  of all  in the mouths
of all  the limbs  thrilling  tears  in the eyes
= I hear the names “Kṛṣṇa” and “Hari” on everyone’s lips; the hair of all stands on end, and their eyes are filled with tears.

@43
in form  in nature  Your  of the Lord  the signs
in a jīva  not  possible  these  transcendental  qualities
= In form and in nature You bear the marks of the Lord. Such transcendental qualities are not possible in a jīva.”

@44
the Lord  said  you  the best of great devotees
at your  sight  of all  melted  the hearts
= The Lord said, “You are the best of the great devotees (*mahā-bhāgavata*); it is at the sight of you that all their hearts have melted.

@45
of others  what  to speak  I  a Māyāvādī  sannyāsī
even I  at your  touch  in love for Kṛṣṇa  float
= What to speak of others — I am a Māyāvādī sannyāsī, yet even I float in love for Kṛṣṇa at your touch.

@46
this  knowing  hard  My  heart  to purify
Sārvabhauma  told (Me)  you  to meet
= Knowing this, Sārvabhauma told Me to meet you, so that My hard heart might be purified.”

@47

= So the two praised each other’s virtues, their hearts rejoicing at the sight of each other.

@48

= Just then a Vedic brāhmaṇa, a Vaiṣṇava, bowed down to the Lord and invited Him for a meal.

@49

to Rāmānanda  says  the Lord  slightly  smiling
= Knowing him to be a Vaiṣṇava, the Lord accepted his invitation; then, smiling slightly, He said to Rāmānanda:

@50

= “I long to hear talks about Kṛṣṇa from your lips. May I see you again!”

@51

= Rāya said, “If You have come to purify a wretch like me, my wicked heart will not become pure merely by seeing You.

@52

= Stay five or seven days and cleanse it; then this wicked mind of mine will become pure.”

@53

= Though neither could bear the separation, Rāmānanda Rāya bowed down and took his leave.

@54

= The Lord went to that brāhmaṇa’s house and took His meal there; and while both were eagerly waiting, evening came.

@55

= The Lord had bathed and performed His evening duties and was sitting, when Rāya came with a single servant to meet Him.

@56

the two  about Kṛṣṇa  talks  speak  in that  place
= Rāya bowed down, the Lord embraced him, and there the two began to speak about Kṛṣṇa.

@57

= The Lord said, “Recite a verse that determines the goal (*sādhya*).” Rāya said, “By following one’s own *dharma*, devotion to Viṣṇu arises.

= “The Supreme Person, Viṣṇu, is worshipped by a person who keeps the conduct of *varṇa* and *āśrama*. There is no other path to please Him.” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 3.8.9)

Rāya  said  to Kṛṣṇa  of actions  offering  of all goals  the essence
= The Lord said, “This is external. Go further.” Rāya said, “The essence of all goals is to offer one’s actions to Kṛṣṇa.

= “Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer in sacrifice, whatever you give away and whatever austerity you perform, O son of Kuntī, do it as an offering to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 9.27)

= The Lord said, “This is external. Go further.” Rāya said, “Giving up one’s own *dharma* — this is the essence of the goal.

= “Knowing in this way their merits and faults, he who gives up all his duties, even those I Myself have enjoined, and worships Me — he is the best of men.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.11.32)

= “Give up all *dharmas* and take shelter of Me alone. I shall free you from all sins; do not grieve.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.66)

= “One who has realized Brahman and is serene within neither grieves nor desires. Equal to all beings, he attains supreme devotion to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.54)

@66

= The Lord said, “This is external. Go further.” Rāya said, “Devotion free from (the endeavour for) knowledge is the essence of the goal.

@67

= “Those who give up the endeavour for knowledge and simply bow down, living by the accounts of You spoken by the saintly — who, remaining where they are, take these accounts into their ears and honour them with body, speech and mind — by them You, O Unconquerable One, are in fact conquered within the three worlds.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.3)

@68

= The Lord said, “This is so, but go further.” Rāya said, “Loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*) is the essence of all goals.

@69

= “The Friend of the afflicted may be worshipped with many offerings, but only love makes the devotee’s heart melt with joy — just as food and drink give pleasure only so long as there is hunger in the stomach and burning thirst.” (Padyāvalī, 13)

@70

for it  eagerness  alone  the price  the only

= “A mind imbued with the *rasa* of devotion to Kṛṣṇa — buy it, if it can be found anywhere! Its only price is eagerness (*laulya*); it cannot be obtained by the pious deeds of ten million births.” (Padyāvalī, 14)

@71
the Lord  said  this  is so  further  say  more
Rāya  said  love in servitude  of all goals  the essence
= The Lord said, “This is so, but go further.” Rāya said, “Love in servitude (*dāsya-prema*) is the essence of all goals.

@72

= “A person becomes pure merely by hearing His name. What, then, remains to be attained by the servants of Him whose feet are holy places?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 9.5.16)

= “When, O Lord, serving You alone without cease, with every other desire completely stilled, shall I live as Your wholly devoted eternal servant and fill my life with joy?” (Stotra-ratna, 43)

@74

= The Lord said, “This is so; go a little further.” Rāya said, “Love in friendship (*sakhya-prema*) is the essence of all goals.

@75

for those under māyā  a human boy
with (Him)  played  who had amassed  of pious deeds  heaps
= “Thus with Him — to the sages the experience of the bliss of Brahman, to those who have taken up His service the Supreme Deity, and to those under the spell of *māyā* a human boy — the cowherd boys played, for they had amassed heaps of pious deeds.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.12.11)

@76

Rāya  said  parental love  of all goals  the essence
= The Lord said, “This is excellent, but go further.” Rāya said, “Parental love (*vātsalya-prema*) is the essence of all goals.

Yaśodā  or  most fortunate

= “O brāhmaṇa, what good deed did Nanda perform to win such great fortune? And what did the most fortunate Yaśodā do, that Hari Himself drank from her breast?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.46)

= “Neither Brahmā, nor Śiva, nor even Śrī, who rests on His breast, obtained the mercy that the cowherd woman (Yaśodā) received from the Giver of liberation.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.9.20)

@79

= The Lord said, “This is excellent, but go further.” Rāya said, “The love of the beloved for her Lover (*kānta-bhāva*) is the essence of the goal that is *prema*.

@80

= “The grace that came to the beauties of Vraja in the festival of the *rāsa*, when they received the blessing of His mighty arms about their necks, was never given even to Śrī, who rests on His breast in utmost devotion, nor to the women of heaven, fragrant and lustrous as lotuses — what then to speak of others?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.47.60)

@81

yellow garments  wearing  garlanded

= “Among them appeared Śauri (Kṛṣṇa), His lotus face smiling, wearing yellow garments and a garland — the very enchanter of Cupid.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.32.2)

@82

= There are many means of attaining Kṛṣṇa, and many degrees in attaining Him.

@83

= But whatever *rasa* a devotee has is for him the best. Yet when one considers impartially, there are higher and higher degrees.

@84

love  by inclination  sweet

= “Although love (*rati*), rising from one kind to the next, is ever richer in delight, by the inclination of the heart one particular kind seems sweetest to a particular devotee.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.5.38)

@85

= The qualities of each preceding *rasa* are present in the one that follows; counting one, two and so on, they increase up to five.

@86

= As the qualities increase, the taste increases in each *rasa*; and the qualities of *śānta*, *dāsya*, *sakhya* and *vātsalya* all dwell in *mādhurya*.

@87

= It is as with the qualities of ether and the other elements: in each following element they increase — two, three — until earth has all five.

= Kṛṣṇa is attained completely through this *prema*. Kṛṣṇa is under the control of this *prema* — so the Bhāgavata says:

@89

= “Devotion to Me brings living beings to immortality. And how fortunate you are to have the love that brings you to Me!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.82.44)

= Kṛṣṇa has a firm promise that holds for all time: as one worships Him, so does He reciprocate.

@91

= But He cannot reciprocate this *prema* in equal measure, and so He becomes a debtor — so the Bhāgavata says:

@92
