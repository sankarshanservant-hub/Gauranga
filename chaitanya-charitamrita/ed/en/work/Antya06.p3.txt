# CC Antya 6, verses 223–329 (stage A, part 3): our English translation from the Bengali and English word-by-word.
# Verse 329 (= Ādi 1.110) — verbatim from ed/en-rev; 239 (~ Ādi 17.31), 243 (~ Madhya 1.133), 328 (~ Antya 1.222) — matching lines from ed/en-rev.

@223
a renunciant  will do  always  of the name  saṅkīrtana
by begging  eating  he does  of life  the maintenance
= A renunciant should always chant the holy name in *saṅkīrtana*, and he should keep himself alive by eating what he begs.

@224
a renunciant  having become  whoever  does  dependence on others
of the goal  attainment  is not  Kṛṣṇa  shows  neglect
= If one who has become a renunciant depends on others, he does not reach the goal, and Kṛṣṇa neglects him.

@225
a renunciant  having become  does  of the tongue  craving
the highest goal  goes  and  becomes  of tastes  under the sway
= If one who has become a renunciant indulges the craving of the tongue, he loses the highest goal and falls under the sway of tastes.

@226
of a renunciant  the duty  always  of the name  saṅkīrtana
with greens  leaves  fruits  roots  of the belly  the filling
= The duty of a renunciant is to chant the holy name in *saṅkīrtana* always and to fill his belly with greens, leaves, fruits and roots.

@227
of the tongue  out of craving  who  here and there  runs
to genitals and belly  devoted  Kṛṣṇa  not  attains
= One who runs here and there out of the tongue’s craving, devoted to the genitals and the belly, does not attain Kṛṣṇa.”

@228
the next  day  Raghunātha  at Svarūpa’s  feet
his own  duty  about  made  submission
= The next day Raghunātha came to Svarūpa’s feet and inquired about his own duty:

@229
for what  reason  He made me leave  home  not  I know  the purpose
what  my  duty  the Lord  may He  instruct
= “Why did He make me leave home? I do not know the purpose. What is my duty? May the Lord instruct me.”

@230
the Lord  before  a word  even  not  speaks  Raghunātha
Svarūpa  Govinda  through  has told  his own  matters
= Before the Lord Raghunātha would not utter a single word; he had his own concerns told through Svarūpa and Govinda.

@231
the Lord  before  Svarūpa  submitted  on the next  day
Raghunātha  submits  at the Lord’s  feet
= The next day Svarūpa made this submission before the Lord: “Raghunātha submits at the Lord’s feet:

@232
what  my  duty  I  not  know  the purpose
Yourself  with Your holy mouth  to me  may You  instruct
= ‘What is my duty? I do not know the purpose. May You Yourself instruct me with Your own holy mouth.’ ”

@233
smiling  Mahāprabhu  to Raghunātha  said
your  instructor  as  Svarūpa  I have given
= Smiling, Mahāprabhu said to Raghunātha, “I have given you Svarūpa as your instructor.

@234
the goal  the means  the truth  learn  his  from
I  so much  not  know  he  as much as  knows
= Learn from him the truth of the goal (*sādhya*) and the means (*sādhana*). I do not know as much as he knows.

@235
still  in My  order  faith  if  there is
My  in these  words  you  make  firm conviction
= Still, if you have faith in My instruction, then hold firmly to these words of Mine.

@236
worldly talk  not  you shall hear  worldly news  not  you shall speak
fine things  not  you shall eat  and  fine things  not  you shall wear
= Do not listen to worldly talk, and do not speak of worldly matters. Do not eat fine food, and do not wear fine clothes.

@237
seeking no honour  giving honour  being  Kṛṣṇa’s name  always  you shall take
in Vraja  to Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa  service  in the mind  you shall do
= Seeking no honour yourself and giving honour to others, always chant the name of Kṛṣṇa, and within your mind serve Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vraja.

@238
this  indeed  in brief  I  have given  instruction
of Svarūpa  from  of this  you will get  the full detail
= Thus I have given you instruction in brief; you will learn it in full detail from Svarūpa.

@239
than grass  even  lower
than a tree  even  tolerant
free from pride  giving honour
to be glorified  always  Hari
= “Lower than a blade of grass, more tolerant even than a tree, seeking no honour and offering honour to others, one should always sing the glories of Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 3)

@240
this  hearing  Raghunātha  bowed to  the feet
Mahāprabhu  gave  him  the embrace of mercy
= Hearing this, Raghunātha bowed down at the Lord’s feet, and Mahāprabhu mercifully embraced him.

@241
again  entrusted  him  Svarūpa’s  into the care
intimate service  he does  Svarūpa  with
= Once more the Lord entrusted him to Svarūpa’s care, and together with Svarūpa he rendered intimate service (*antaraṅga-sevā*).

@242
at that  time  came  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees
as before  the Lord  with all  made  meeting
= At that time all the devotees from Gauḍa arrived, and the Lord met them all as before.

@243
all  taking  made  the Lord  of the Guṇḍicā  cleansing
all  taking  made  the Lord  the picnic in the garden
= Taking them all, the Lord cleansed the Guṇḍicā temple, and with them all He held a picnic in the garden.

@244
at the Ratha-yātrā  all  taking  He did  dancing
seeing  Raghunātha’s  amazed  became  mind
= At the Ratha-yātrā He danced together with them all, and seeing this Raghunātha was struck with wonder.

@245
Raghunātha  dāsa  when  all  met
Advaita Ācārya  to him  much  mercy  showed
= When Raghunātha dāsa met them all, Advaita Ācārya showed him great mercy.

@246
Śivānanda Sena  to him  tells  the account
you  to fetch  your  father  sent  ten  men
= Śivānanda Sena told him what had happened: “Your father sent ten men to fetch you.

@247
you  to send back  a letter  he sent  to me
Jhā̃karā  from  you  not  finding  they went  home
= He sent me a letter asking me to send you back. Not finding you, they turned back home from Jhā̃karā.”

@248
four  months  staying  the devotees  to Gauḍa  went
hearing  Raghunātha’s  father  a man  sent
= After staying four months, the devotees returned to Gauḍa. When Raghunātha’s father heard of it, he sent a man.

@249
that  man  of Śivānanda Sena  asked
Mahāprabhu’s  place  at  a  Vaiṣṇava  did you see
= That man asked Śivānanda Sena, “At Mahāprabhu’s place did you see a Vaiṣṇava?

@250
of Govardhana  the son  he  the name  Raghunātha
in Nīlācala  acquaintance  is there  you  with
= He is Govardhana’s son, and his name is Raghunātha. Did you come to know him in Nīlācala?”

@251
Śivānanda  says  he  is  the Lord’s  place  at
greatly  famous  he  who  not  knows
= Śivānanda said, “He is with the Lord. He is very famous — who does not know him?

@252
Svarūpa’s  into the care  him  He has  entrusted
the Lord’s  devotees’  he  is  as dear as life
= The Lord has entrusted him to Svarūpa’s care, and to the Lord’s devotees he is as dear as life.

@253
night  and day  does  he  of the name  saṅkīrtana
a moment  even  not  leaves  the Lord’s  feet
= Day and night he chants the holy name in *saṅkīrtana*, and not for a moment does he leave the Lord’s feet.

@254
utmost  renunciation  his  no  food  clothing
somehow  or other  food  taking  keeps  life
= His renunciation is complete: he has no concern for food or clothing. Eating whatever comes somehow or other, he keeps himself alive.

@255
ten daṇḍas  of the night  having passed  the puṣpa-añjali  having seen
at the Siṁha-dvāra  standing  he is  food  for the sake of
= When ten *daṇḍas* of the night have passed, after seeing the *puṣpa-añjali*, he stands at the Siṁha-dvāra for food.

@256
someone  if  gives  then  he does  eating
sometimes  fasting  sometimes  he does  chewing
= If someone gives him something, he eats; sometimes he fasts, and sometimes he chews parched grain.”

@257
this  hearing  that  man  to Govardhana
told  going  all  Raghunātha’s  account
= Hearing this, the man went to Govardhana and told him all about Raghunātha.

@258
hearing  his  mother  father  grieved  became
the son  to  goods and men  sent
= Hearing it, his mother and father were grieved, and they sent goods and men to their son.

@259
four  hundred  coins  two  servants  one  brāhmaṇa
of Śivānanda  to  they sent  at once
= At once they sent four hundred coins, two servants and a *brāhmaṇa* to Śivānanda.

@260
Śivānanda  says  you  all  to go  will not be able
I  go  when  my  with  you will go
= Śivānanda said, “You will not be able to go there by yourselves. When I go, you will go with me.
