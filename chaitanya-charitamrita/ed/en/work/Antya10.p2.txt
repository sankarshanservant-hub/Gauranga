# CC Antya 10, verses 83–162 (stage A, part 2): our English translation from the Bengali and English word-by-word.
# Text of the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (ed/fixes/Antya10.txt): 93 prasāda laite, 98 prabhura nidrāya, 132 karāya upayoga, etc.
# Verse 162 (= Antya 2.172) — word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev; verse 133, line 1 (= Madhya 12.69,
# 15.4) — word-by-word after ed/en-rev. Govinda’s list (118–123) is one quotation.

@83-84
always  there is  this  firm  rule
the Lord  when  prasāda  having taken  does  lying down
Govinda  coming  does  of the feet  massage
then  going  the Lord’s  remnants  does  eating
= It had always been a firm rule: when the Lord lay down after taking prasāda, Govinda would come and massage His feet, and only then go and eat the Lord’s remnants.

@85
the whole  doorway  filling  the Lord  has  lain down
inside  to go  he could not;  Govinda  makes  a request
= That day the Lord lay down filling the whole doorway. Unable to go inside, Govinda made a request:

@86
to one side  move,  me  let  inside  go
the Lord  said  strength  there is not  the body  to move
= “Please move to one side and let me go in.” The Lord said, “I have no strength to move My body.”

@87
again and  again  Govinda  says  to one side  to move
the Lord  said  the body  I  cannot  move
= Again and again Govinda asked Him to move aside, but the Lord said, “I cannot move My body.”

@88
Govinda  said  to do  I want  of the feet  massage
the Lord  said  do  or  not  do,  whatever  your  mind
= Govinda said, “I want to massage Your feet.” The Lord said, “Do it or not, as you like.”

@89
then  Govinda  outer cloth  Him  over  placing
inner  into the room  went  Govinda  the Lord  stepping over
= Then Govinda spread his outer cloth (*bahirvāsa*) over Him, stepped over the Lord and went into the inner room.[^89-1]

@90
of the feet  massage  he did,  waist and back  he pressed
by the gentle  rubbing  the Lord’s  weariness  went
= He massaged His feet and pressed His waist and back, and by that gentle rubbing the Lord’s weariness went away.

@91
happily  sleep  came  to the Lord,  Govinda  presses  the body
two daṇḍas  after  the Lord’s  was  waking
= The Lord fell happily asleep while Govinda pressed His body, and after some two *daṇḍas* (about forty-eight minutes) the Lord woke.

@92
Govinda  seeing  the Lord  says  angry  becoming
today  why  so long  you are  sitting
= Seeing Govinda, the Lord spoke angrily: “Why have you been sitting here so long today?

@93
My  sleep  when it came  why  not  you went  prasāda  to take
Govinda  said  in the doorway  You lay,  to go  there is not  way
= When I fell asleep, why didn’t you go to take prasāda?” Govinda said, “You lay in the doorway; there was no way to go.”

@94
the Lord  said  inside  then  you came  how
in the same way  why  prasāda  to take  not  did you  go
= The Lord said, “Then how did you come inside? Why didn’t you go out the same way to take prasāda?”

@95
Govinda  said (to himself)  my  service  that  rule
offense  let there be,  or  to hell  going
= Govinda said within himself, “Service is my rule. Let there be offense, or even going to hell.

@96
service  for the sake of  ten million  offenses  not  I count
for my own sake  of the shadow of offense  fear  I feel
= For the sake of service I do not count even ten million offenses, but for my own sake I fear even the shadow of an offense.”

@97
this  all  in mind  thinking  Govinda  remained (silent)
the Lord  what  asked,  of that  answer  not  he gave
= Thinking all this, Govinda remained silent and gave no answer to what the Lord had asked.

@98
every day  in the Lord’s  sleep  he goes  prasāda  to take
of that  day  the weariness  seeing  he began  to press
= Every day, when the Lord slept, he would go to take prasāda; but that day, seeing the Lord so weary, he began to press His body.

@99
even to go  way  there is not;  he would go  how
great  offense  is  of the Lord  in stepping over
= And even to go there was no way. How could he go? To step over the Lord is a great offense.

@100
these  all  are  of the scriptures of bhakti  subtle  secret
Caitanya’s  by the mercy  knows  these  all  principles
= All these are the subtle secrets of the scriptures of bhakti. Only by Śrī Caitanya’s mercy does one know all these principles.

@101
of the devotees  virtues  to reveal  the Lord  very  playful
these  all  to reveal  He made  such  a play
= The Lord delights in revealing the virtues of His devotees; to reveal all this He played such a scene.

@102
briefly  I have told  this  in a circle  dance
even today  sing  which  Caitanya’s  servants
= Thus I have briefly told of this dance in a circle (*parimuṇḍā-nṛtya*), which Śrī Caitanya’s servants sing of even today.

@103
in this way  Mahāprabhu  taking  His own associates
of the Guṇḍicā temple  He did  washing,  cleaning
= In this way Mahāprabhu, with His own associates, washed and cleaned the Guṇḍicā temple.

@104
as before  did  the Lord  kīrtana,  dancing
as before  in the garden  did  forest meal
= As before, the Lord sang kīrtana and danced, and as before He took a meal in the garden as in a forest (*vanya-bhojana*).

@105
as before  the chariot  in front of  He did  dancing
the Herā-pañcamī  festival  He did  seeing
= As before, He danced in front of the chariot and saw the Herā-pañcamī festival.

@106
four  months  of the rains  stayed  all  the devotees
Janmāṣṭamī  and other  festivals  they did  seeing
= All the devotees stayed for the four months of the rains and saw Janmāṣṭamī and the other festivals.

@107
earlier  when  Gauḍa  from  the devotees  came
the Lord  something  to feed  of all  the desire  was
= Earlier, when the devotees had come from Gauḍa, every one of them wished to feed the Lord something.

@108
someone  some  prasāda  bringing  gives  to Govinda
this  so that  without fail  eating  does  the Gosāñi
= Each would bring some prasāda and give it to Govinda, saying, “See that the Gosāñi eats this without fail.”

@109
some  peḍā,  some  nāḍu,  some  cakes  drinks
of great  value  fine  prasāda  kinds  whose  various
= Some brought *peḍā*, some *nāḍu*, some cakes and sweet drinks — costly, fine prasāda of many kinds.[^109-1]

@110
so-and-so  this  has given  Govinda  makes  report
keep it  aside,  saying  the Lord  not  does  eating
= Govinda would tell Him, “So-and-so has given this,” but the Lord would say, “Put it aside,” and would not eat.

@111
keeping  and keeping  of the room  filled  one  corner
of a hundred  people  food  as much  was  stored up
= As he kept putting things aside, one corner of the room filled up: food enough for a hundred people was stored there.

@112
to Govinda  all  ask  with  eagerness
by me  given  prasāda  the Lord  whether  you made  eat
= Everyone eagerly asked Govinda, “Did you have the Lord eat the prasāda I gave?”

@113
to some  something  saying  Govinda  does  putting off
another  day  to the Lord  says  words of despair
= Govinda put them off, telling each one something. Then one day he spoke to the Lord in despair:

@114
the Ācārya  and other  honored men  with  care
You  to feed  things  give  my  in the place
= “Advaita Ācārya and the other honored devotees carefully give me things to feed You.

@115
You  them  not  eat;  they  ask  time and  again
how much  putting off  shall I do,  how  my  deliverance
= You do not eat them, and they ask again and again. How long can I put them off? How shall I be delivered?”

@116
the Lord  said  the old residents,  sorrow  why  feel
who  what  has given,  that  bring  here
= The Lord said, “Those who have long lived with Me — why should they feel sorrow? Bring here whatever anyone has given.”[^116-1]

@117
thus  saying  Mahāprabhu  sat  to eat
names  calling  Govinda  makes  offering
= Saying this, Mahāprabhu sat down to eat, and Govinda offered Him everything, naming each giver:

@118
the Ācārya’s  this  paiḍa (coconut),  various  juicy  cakes
this  amṛta-guṭikā,  maṇḍā,  camphor  vessel
= “This is the Ācārya’s *paiḍa*, and various cakes full of juice; this is *amṛta-guṭikā*, *maṇḍā*, and a jar of camphor.

@119
Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita’s  these  many  kinds
cakes,  drinks,  amṛta-maṇḍā  sugar lotuses  and
= These many kinds are from Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita: cakes, sweet drinks, *amṛta-maṇḍā* and sugar lotuses (*padma-cini*).

@120
Ācāryaratna’s  these  all  gifts
Ācāryanidhi’s  these  many  kinds
= All these gifts are Ācāryaratna’s, and these many kinds are Ācāryanidhi’s.

@121
Vāsudeva Datta’s,  Murāri Gupta’s  and
Buddhimanta Khān’s  these  various  kinds
= These are from Vāsudeva Datta and Murāri Gupta, and these various kinds from Buddhimanta Khān.

@122
Śrīmān Sena,  Śrīmān Paṇḍita,  Nandana Ācārya
by them all  given  this  do  eat
= Śrīmān Sena, Śrīmān Paṇḍita, Nandana Ācārya — please eat what they have given.

@123
Kulīnagrāma’s  these  first  see  all
of the Khaṇḍa-dwelling  people  these  see  as many
= Look first at all these from Kulīnagrāma, and look at these, just as many, from the people of Khaṇḍa.”

@124
thus  of all  the names  saying  the Lord’s  before  he places
satisfied  becoming  the Lord  all  eating  does
= So, naming every one, Govinda set the things before the Lord, and the Lord, well pleased, ate them all.

@125-126
although  a month  stale  with the top cut open  coconuts
amṛta-guṭikā and others,  drinks and others  all
yet  almost fresh  all  of the things  the taste
stale  tasteless  not  that  the Lord’s  grace
= Though the coconuts with their tops cut open, the *amṛta-guṭikās*, the drinks and all the rest were a month old, everything tasted almost fresh. Nothing was stale or tasteless — such was the Lord’s grace.[^125-1]

@127
of a hundred  people  food  the Lord  in one daṇḍa  ate
more  anything  is there?  saying  Govinda  He asked
= In a single *daṇḍa* the Lord ate food enough for a hundred people, and asked Govinda, “Is there anything more?”

@128
Govinda  says  Rāghava’s  jhālis  only  are left
the Lord  said  today  let them stay,  them  I will see  later
= Govinda said, “Only Rāghava’s *jhālis* are left.” The Lord said, “Let them stay today; I will look at them later.”

@129
another  day  the Lord  when  in seclusion  eating  did
Rāghava’s  jhālis  opening  everything  He saw
= Another day, when the Lord ate in seclusion, He opened Rāghava’s *jhālis* and looked at everything.

@130
all  of the things  a little  of each  partaking  He did
tasty,  fragrant  finding  much  He praised
= He tasted a little of every item and, finding them delicious and fragrant, praised them highly.

@131
a year  for  the rest  He kept  storing
at meal  time  Svarūpa’s  serving  taking out
= The rest He kept in store for a whole year, and at mealtimes Svarūpa would take out a little and serve it.

@132
sometimes  at night  something  he makes  partake
of the devotee’s  faith  things  surely  He does  enjoy
= Sometimes, at night too, Svarūpa would have Him partake of a little; for the Lord surely enjoys what a devotee gives with faith (*śraddhā*).

@133
thus  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees
Cāturmāsya  He passed  in the joy of talks about Kṛṣṇa
= Thus Mahāprabhu passed the Cāturmāsya with His devotees in the joy of talks about Kṛṣṇa.

@134
from time to time  the Ācārya and others  make  invitation
at home  rice  they cook  and  various  vegetable dishes
= From time to time Advaita Ācārya and others would invite Him, cooking rice and various vegetable dishes at home:

@135
of pepper  hot dishes,  and  sweet-and-sour  and
ginger,  salt,  lime,  milk,  yogurt,  sugar candy
= hot peppery dishes and sweet-and-sour ones, ginger, salt, lime, milk, yogurt and sugar candy (*khaṇḍa-sāra*);

@136
greens  two or four,  and  of śuktā  broth
nimba  eggplant,  and  fried  paṭola
= two or four kinds of greens, a broth of *śuktā*, eggplant with *nimba*, and fried *paṭola*;

@137
fried  phula-baḍī,  and  mung  dal  soup
various  dishes  they cook  the Lord’s  to suit
= fried *phula-baḍī* and mung dal soup — they cooked various dishes to suit the Lord’s taste.

@138
Jagannātha’s  prasāda  they bring  to make  mixed
sometimes  alone  He goes,  sometimes  associates  with
= They would bring Jagannātha’s prasāda to mix with these. Sometimes the Lord went alone, sometimes with His associates.

@139
Ācāryaratna,  Ācāryanidhi,  Nandana,  Rāghava
Śrīvāsa and others  as many  devotees,  brāhmaṇas  all
= Ācāryaratna, Ācāryanidhi, Nandana, Rāghava, Śrīvāsa and all the other devotees who were brāhmaṇas —

@140-141
in this way  invitation  they make  care  taking
Vāsudeva,  Gadādhara,  Gupta  Murāri
of Kulīnagrāma,  of Khaṇḍa,  and  as many  people
Jagannātha’s  prasāda  bringing  make  invitation
= these would invite Him in this way with great care. Vāsudeva, Gadādhara, Murāri Gupta, the people of Kulīnagrāma and of Khaṇḍa, and all the others would invite Him by bringing Jagannātha’s prasāda.

@142

Śivānanda’s  eldest  son’s  ‘Caitanya  dāsa’  name
= Now hear the story of Śivānanda Sena’s invitation. Śivānanda’s eldest son was named Caitanya dāsa.

@143
the Lord  to introduce  him  along  he brought
when introduced,  the Lord  his  name  then  asked
= He brought him along to introduce him to the Lord, and when he had introduced him, the Lord asked his name.

@144

what  name  you have given,  understanding  not  goes
= Hearing the name Caitanya dāsa, Gaurarāya said, “What name have you given him? I cannot make it out.”

@145
Sena  said  what  I knew,  that  name  I gave
thus  saying  Mahāprabhu  invitation  he made
= Sena said, “I gave him the name I knew.” Saying this, he invited Mahāprabhu.

@146

the devotees  with  the Lord  to eat  sat
= He had costly prasāda of Jagannātha brought, and the Lord sat down to eat with the devotees.

@147
Śivānanda’s  out of regard  the Lord  did  eating
too heavy  by the meal  pleased  not  was the mind
= Out of regard for Śivānanda the Lord ate, but His mind was not pleased by so heavy a meal.

@148
another  day  Caitanya dāsa  made  invitation
the Lord’s  desire  understanding  he brought  dishes
= Another day Caitanya dāsa invited Him, and, understanding the Lord’s desire, brought dishes to suit it:

@149
yogurt,  lime,  ginger,  and  phula-baḍā,  salt
the things  seeing  the Lord’s  pleased  became  mind
= yogurt, lime, ginger, *phula-baḍā* and salt. Seeing these things, the Lord was pleased at heart.

@150

satisfied  have become  I  by his  invitation
= The Lord said, “This boy knows My mind. I am satisfied with his invitation.”

@151

= Saying this, He ate rice with yogurt, and made Caitanya dāsa the recipient of His remnants.

@152
four  months  in this way  in invitations  pass
some  Vaiṣṇavas  a day  not  get
= In this way four months passed in invitations, and some Vaiṣṇavas could not get a day.

@153
Gadādhara  Paṇḍita,  Bhaṭṭācārya  Sārvabhauma
of them all  there is  for bhikṣā  fixed days
= Gadādhara Paṇḍita and Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya had fixed days for feeding Him (*bhikṣā*).

@154-155
Gopīnātha Ācārya,  Jagadānanda,  Kāśīśvara

= Gopīnātha Ācārya, Jagadānanda, Kāśīśvara, Bhagavān, Rāmabhadra Ācārya, Śaṅkara and Vakreśvara would from time to time invite Him to rice cooked at home. When others invited Him, the prasāda cost two *paṇas* of cowries.

@156

Rāmacandra Purī  out of fear  they reduced  invitation
= At first the fixed sum had been four *paṇas* of cowries, but out of fear of Rāmacandra Purī they reduced the invitation.

@157

of Nīlācala  companion  devotees  with Him  remained
= After they had stayed four months, He bade farewell to the devotees of Gauḍa, and His companions of Nīlācala remained with Him.

@158

= Thus I have told of the invitations to the Lord’s meals and how He relished the things His devotees gave.

@159

of that  in the midst  in a circle  dance  telling
= Within it is the account of Rāghava’s *jhālis*, and within it too the telling of the dance in a circle (*parimuṇḍā-nṛtya*).

@160

= Whoever hears the narrations of Śrī Caitanya with faith will surely attain love for Śrī Caitanya’s feet.

@161

= To hear them is like nectar; they soothe the ears and the mind. Fortunate is he who relishes them.

@162

“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

@notes
[^89-1]: *Bahirvāsa*, the outer cloth: the *Anubhāṣya* (heading to this verse) says that Govinda places his own *bahirvāsa* on the Lord’s body and steps over Him.
[^109-1]: *Peḍā*, a sweet of thickened milk; the *Anubhāṣya* records the variant *paiḍa* (coconut, as in verse 118).
[^116-1]: *Ādivasyā*, “old residents”: those who have long lived with the Lord (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: they have no cause for sorrow, for the delicacies were brought by those who have just come from Gauḍa). The *Anubhāṣya* heads the verse “The Lord asks why the devotees are sorrowful” and records other senses given to the word: “unfortunate,” “foolish,” “fickle-minded,” “overeager” (*ā-dekhlā*, *kāṅlā*).
[^125-1]: *Mukutā nārikela*, coconuts with the top cut open (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*).
