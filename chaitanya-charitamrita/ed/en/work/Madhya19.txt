@title Śrī Caitanya Instructs Rūpa Gosvāmī

@1
of Vṛndāvana  of the playful pastimes full of rasa  the account
by time  lost  His own power  eager
having infused  into Rūpa  He spread  again  He
the Lord  into Brahmā  before  as  the creation of the world
= Eager to restore it, the Lord infused His own power into Rūpa and spread anew the account of Vṛndāvana’s pastimes full of rasa, which had been lost over time, just as long ago He had infused His power into Brahmā and so brought forth the creation of the world.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

@3
Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana  stayed  in Rāmakeli  village
with the Lord  having met  went  to their own  homes
= Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana were staying in the village of Rāmakeli. Having met the Lord there, they returned to their own homes.

@4
the two brothers  of worldly affairs  of giving up  a means  devised
much  wealth  giving  two  brāhmaṇas  they engaged
= The two brothers devised a way to give up their worldly affairs. Giving them much wealth, they engaged two brāhmaṇas

@5
with the mantra of Kṛṣṇa  had performed  two  puraścaraṇas
soon  to attain  Caitanya’s  feet
= and had them perform two puraścaraṇas with the Kṛṣṇa mantra, so that they might soon attain the feet of Caitanya.

@6
Śrī Rūpa  Gosvāmī  then  a boat  loading
to his own  home  came  much  wealth  taking
= Then Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī loaded a boat and came to his own home, bringing great wealth with him.

@7
to brāhmaṇas and Vaiṣṇavas  he gave  of it  half the wealth
one  quarter  of the wealth  he gave  for the support of his relatives
= He gave half of that wealth to brāhmaṇas and Vaiṣṇavas, and a quarter for the support of his relatives.

@8
punishment and imprisonment  against  a quarter  savings  he made
with trustworthy  brāhmaṇas  in deposit  he kept
= The remaining quarter he saved against punishment and imprisonment, leaving it on deposit with trustworthy brāhmaṇas.

@9
in Gauḍa  he left  coins  ten thousand
Sanātana  spending  makes  keeps  in a grocer’s house
= In Gauḍa he left ten thousand coins in the keeping of a grocer, for Sanātana to spend.

@10
Śrī Rūpa  heard  the Lord’s  to Nīlādri  going
by the forest path  will go  the Lord  to Śrī Vṛndāvana
= Śrī Rūpa heard that the Lord had gone to Nīlādri and would go to Śrī Vṛndāvana by the forest path.

@11
Rūpa Gosvāmī  to Nīlācala  sent  two men
the Lord  when  to Vṛndāvana  makes  departure
= Rūpa Gosvāmī sent two men to Nīlācala to learn when the Lord would set out for Vṛndāvana.

@12
quickly  coming  to me  of Him  you will give  news
hearing  in accordance with that  I will make  arrangements
= “Come back quickly and bring me news of Him. When I hear it, I shall act accordingly.”

@13
here  Sanātana Gosvāmī  thinks  in his mind  to himself
the king  me  affection  shows  that  my  bondage
= Meanwhile Sanātana Gosvāmī thought to himself: “The king is fond of me, and that is my bondage.

@14
by some  means  the king  if  with me  angry  becomes
then  release  will be  I have made  decision
= If somehow the king becomes angry with me, I shall be released. So I have decided.”

@15
of illness  pretext  making  stays  in his own house
the king’s business  he gave up  not  goes  to the king’s court
= Pretending to be ill, he stayed at home. He gave up the king’s business and no longer went to the court.

@16
greedy  kāyastha officials  the king’s business  carry on
he himself  in his own home  makes  of the scriptures  study
= Greedy kāyastha officials carried on the king’s business, while he himself studied the scriptures at home.

@17
bhaṭṭācāryas  paṇḍitas  twenty  thirty  gathering
the Bhāgavata  study  makes  in the assembly  sitting
= Gathering twenty or thirty learned bhaṭṭācāryas and paṇḍitas, he would sit in assembly with them and study the Bhāgavata.

@18
another  day  the ruler of Gauḍa  with him  one person
suddenly  into the Gosvāmī’s assembly  made  arrival
= One day the ruler of Gauḍa, with one companion, suddenly came into the Gosvāmī’s assembly.

@19
the emperor  seeing  all  respectfully  rose
respectfully  a seat  giving  the king  they seated
= Seeing the emperor, everyone rose respectfully, offered him a seat and seated the king with due respect.

@20
the king  says  to you  a physician  I sent
the physician  says  illness  there is none  healthy  that  I saw
= The king said: “I sent my physician to you, and the physician says, ‘There is no illness; I found him healthy.’

@21
my  whatever  business  all  rests with you
business  leaving  have stayed  you  at home  sitting
= Whatever business I have rests entirely on you, yet you have left your work and sit at home.

@22
my  all  affairs  undertakings  all  you have made  ruin
what  in your  heart  is  tell  to me
= You have ruined all my affairs and undertakings. What is in your heart? Tell me.”

@23
Sanātana  says  will not be  from me  the work
another  one person  through  make  arrangement
= Sanātana said: “The work can no longer be done by me. Arrange it through someone else.”

@24
then  angry  becoming  the king  says  again
your  ‘elder brother’  does  the conduct of a robber
= Then the king grew angry and spoke again: “Your ‘elder brother’ behaves like a robber.[^24]

@25
living beings and animals  killing  he has made  the districts  all  ruin
here  you  have made  my  all  affairs  ruin
= Killing living beings and animals, he has laid waste all the districts, and here you have ruined all my affairs.”

@26
Sanātana  says  you  independent  ruler of Gauḍa
whoever  whatever  fault  commits  give  him  its fruit
= Sanātana said: “You are the independent ruler of Gauḍa. Whoever commits whatever fault, give him its fruit.”[^26]

@27
this  hearing  the ruler of Gauḍa  rising  home  went
he will flee  thinking  Sanātana  he put in confinement
= Hearing this, the ruler of Gauḍa rose and went home. Fearing that he would flee, he had Sanātana put in confinement.

@28
at that time  went  the king  the Oriyās  to attack
to Sanātana  says  you  come  with me
= At that time the king set out to attack the Oriyās, and he said to Sanātana: “Come with me.”

@29
he  says  you will go  to the Deity  pain  to give
my  strength  there is not  with you  to go
= He replied: “You are going to bring pain to the Deity. I have no strength to go with you.”

@30
then  him  in confinement  keeping  he made  departure
meanwhile  Nīlācala  from  the Lord  set out  for Vṛndāvana
= Then the king departed, leaving him in confinement. Meanwhile the Lord set out from Nīlācala for Vṛndāvana.

@notes
[^2]: The same verse as Ādi 4.2; translation as there.
[^24]: According to the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*, the “elder brother” (in quotation marks in the edition) is the emperor himself: Husain Shāh regarded Sanātana as his “younger brother,” and in loving anger he says of himself that he only marches against other lands and, being a *yavana* by birth, hunts and kills many living beings and animals in the districts of Gauḍa.
[^26]: According to the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*, these words carry a hidden meaning: the king, who himself behaves like a robber, will reap the fruit of that, and the minister who has neglected his duties deserves the fruit of dismissal.

@31
then  those  two  messengers  to Rūpa  came
to Vṛndāvana  has set out  the Lord  coming  they said
= Then the two messengers came back to Rūpa and told him: “The Lord has set out for Vṛndāvana.”

@32
hearing  Śrī Rūpa  wrote  to Sanātana
to Vṛndāvana  has set out  Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi
= Hearing this, Śrī Rūpa wrote to Sanātana: “Śrī Caitanya Gosāñi has set out for Vṛndāvana.

@33
we two brothers  have set out  Him  to meet
you  in whatever  way  getting free  come  from there
= We two brothers are setting out to meet Him. Get free in whatever way you can and come away from there.

@34
ten thousand  coins  there  are  with the grocer
with them  paying  make  quickly  your own release
= Ten thousand coins are there with the grocer. Use them to free yourself quickly.

@35
in whatever  way  getting free  you  come  to Vṛndāvana
this  having written  the two brothers  made  departure
= Get free in whatever way you can and come to Vṛndāvana.” Having written this, the two brothers set out.

@36
Anupama  Mallika  his  name  ‘Śrī Vallabha’
Rūpa Gosvāmī’s  younger brother  a great Vaiṣṇava
= Anupama Mallika, whose name was Śrī Vallabha, was Rūpa Gosvāmī’s younger brother and a great Vaiṣṇava.

@37
him  taking  Rūpa Gosvāmī  to Prayāga  came
Mahāprabhu  there  hearing  he became joyful
= Taking him along, Rūpa Gosvāmī came to Prayāga, and he rejoiced to hear that Mahāprabhu was there.

@38
the Lord  had gone  for darśana of Bindu-mādhava
hundreds of thousands  of people  come  the Lord  to meet
= The Lord had gone for darśana of Bindu-mādhava, and hundreds of thousands of people came to meet Him.

@39
some  weep  some  laugh  some  dance  sing
‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  crying  some  on the ground  roll
= Some wept, some laughed, some danced and sang, and some rolled on the ground, crying, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”

@40
the Gaṅgā and the Yamunā  Prayāga  could not  flood
the Lord  flooded  of love for Kṛṣṇa  with the deluge
= The Gaṅgā and the Yamunā could not flood Prayāga, but the Lord flooded it with the deluge of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@41
the crowd  seeing  the two brothers  stayed  in a secluded place
the Lord’s  ecstasy  arose  of Mādhava  at the sight
= Seeing the crowd, the two brothers stayed apart in a secluded place. At the sight of Mādhava the Lord was overwhelmed with ecstasy.

@42
in loving ecstasy  dances  the Lord  the sound of Hari’s name  making
His arms raised  making  He says  say  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’
= Absorbed in love, the Lord danced, calling out the name of Hari. Raising His arms, He said: “Say ‘Hari! Hari!’”

@43
the Lord’s  greatness  seeing  the people  were amazed
in Prayāga  the Lord’s  pastimes  I cannot  describe
= Seeing the Lord’s greatness, the people were amazed. I cannot describe the Lord’s pastimes in Prayāga.

@44
with a brāhmaṇa from the South  there was  acquaintance
that  brāhmaṇa  inviting  took  to his home
= The Lord was acquainted with a brāhmaṇa from the South, and that brāhmaṇa invited Him and took Him to his home.

@45
to the brāhmaṇa’s house  coming  the Lord  in seclusion  sat
Śrī Rūpa and Vallabha  both  coming  met
= The Lord came to the brāhmaṇa’s house and sat in seclusion, and there Śrī Rūpa and Vallabha both came to meet Him.

@46
two  bunches  of straw  both  in their teeth  holding
the Lord  seeing  from a distance  they fall  like rods  becoming
= Each holding a bunch of straw between his teeth, the two of them saw the Lord and fell flat on the ground like rods from a distance.

@47
various  verses  reciting  they rise  fall  again  and again
the Lord  seeing  loving ecstasy  arose  in both
= Reciting various verses, they rose and fell again and again. At the sight of the Lord both were overwhelmed with love.

@48
Śrī Rūpa  seeing  the Lord’s  pleased  became  mind
get up  get up  Rūpa  come  He spoke  the words
= Seeing Śrī Rūpa, the Lord was pleased at heart. “Get up, get up, Rūpa! Come here!” He said.

@49
Kṛṣṇa’s  mercy  at all  not  can be  described
from the well of worldly life  out of  you  He has pulled  both
= “Kṛṣṇa’s mercy cannot be described at all. He has pulled you both out of the well of worldly life.

@50
not  to Me  a nondevotee  a knower of the four Vedas
My devotee  a dog-eater  dear
to him  should be given  from him  should be accepted
he  and  is to be worshiped  as  indeed  I
= “A knower of the four Vedas who is not devoted to Me is not dear to Me, but My devotee, even if born a dog-eater, is dear to Me. To him gifts should be given, and from him gifts should be accepted; he is to be worshiped just as I am.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 10.91, from the Itihāsa-samuccaya)

@51
this  verse  reciting  both  He embraced
in mercy  of both  on the heads  He placed  His feet
= Reciting this verse, He embraced them both, and in His mercy He placed His feet on their heads.

@52
the Lord’s mercy  receiving  both  two  hands  joining
humble  becoming  prayers  offer  meekness  showing
= Having received the Lord’s mercy, the two of them joined their palms and, humble and meek, offered prayers:

@53
obeisances  to the most munificent
love for Kṛṣṇa  to the giver  to You
to Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
named  of golden complexion  obeisances
= “Obeisances to You, the most munificent, the giver of love for Kṛṣṇa! Obeisances to Kṛṣṇa Himself, who bears the name Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and shines with a golden complexion.”

@54
who  by ignorance maddened  the world  merciful
curing  yet  made  maddened
with the nectar of the treasure of His own love  the doer of wondrous deeds  I
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Him  take refuge
= “I take refuge in Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the doer of wondrous deeds, who in His mercy cured the world maddened by ignorance and then made it mad with the nectar of the treasure of His own love.” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 1.2)

@55
then  Mahāprabhu  him  near  seated
of Sanātana  the news  tell  him  He asked
= Then Mahāprabhu seated him close by and asked him: “Tell Me the news of Sanātana.”

@56
Rūpa  says  he  a prisoner  is  in the king’s house
You  if  deliver  then  he will be  delivered
= Rūpa said: “He is a prisoner in the king’s house. If You deliver him, then he will be delivered.”

@57
the Lord  says  of Sanātana  has taken place  the release
soon  with Me  will be  the meeting
= The Lord said: “Sanātana has already been released, and he will soon meet Me.”

@58
the midday meal  to take  the brāhmaṇa  to the Lord  said
Rūpa Gosvāmī  that day  there  stayed
= The brāhmaṇa asked the Lord to take His midday meal, and Rūpa Gosvāmī stayed there that day.

@59
the bhaṭṭācārya  the two  brothers  invitation  made
of the Lord’s  remnants  of prasāda the plate  the two brothers  received
= The bhaṭṭācārya invited the two brothers as well, and they received the plate with the remnants of the Lord’s prasāda.

@60
at Triveṇī  the Lord’s  of the lodging  the place
the two brothers  lodging  made  near the Lord
= The Lord’s lodging was at Triveṇī, and the two brothers took lodging near the Lord.
