@title The Divine Madness of Śrī Caitanya in Separation from Kṛṣṇa

@1
Kṛṣṇa  from separation  through the bewilderment
with the mind  with the body  with the intelligence
whatever  whatever  did  Gaurāṅga
of that  a particle  is told  now
= Now I shall tell a small part of all that Gaurāṅga did with His mind, body and intelligence in the bewilderment born of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  Himself  the Supreme Lord
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  of the devotees  the life
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya, the Supreme Lord Himself! Glory, glory to Gauracandra, the life of His devotees!

@3
glory  glory  to Nityānanda  Caitanya’s  life
glory to Advaitācārya  glory  to Gaura  most dear
= Glory, glory to Nityānanda, the life of Caitanya! Glory to Advaitācārya, most dear to Gaura!

@4
glory  to Svarūpa  to Śrīvāsa and the others  the Lord’s  devotees
strength  give  I may do  so that  of Caitanya  the description
= Glory to Svarūpa, to Śrīvāsa and to all the Lord’s devotees! Grant me the strength to describe Śrī Caitanya.

@5
the Lord’s  madness of separation  mood  profound
to understand  not  can  anyone  even if  he is  wise
= The Lord’s mood of madness in separation is profound: no one can understand it, even if he is wise.

@6
to understand  not  we can  what  to describe  who  can
he  understands  describes  Caitanya  power  gives  to whom
= What we cannot understand — who can describe it? Only he understands and describes it to whom Caitanya gives the power.

@7
Svarūpa Gosāñi  and  Raghunātha dāsa
of these  two  in the diaries  this pastime  is revealed
= These pastimes are revealed in the diaries (*kaḍacā*) of these two, Svarūpa Gosāñi and Raghunātha dāsa.

@8
at that time  these  two  stayed  Mahāprabhu’s  at the side
other  all  diary-writers  stayed  in distant lands
= At that time these two lived at Mahāprabhu’s side, while all the other diarists lived in distant places.

@9
moment  by moment  experiencing  these  two
briefly  at length  make  diary  composition
= Experiencing His pastimes moment by moment, these two recorded them in their diaries, sometimes briefly and sometimes at length.

@10
Svarūpa  the author of the sūtras  Raghunātha  the author of the explanation
their  expansion  I describe  of a pā̃ji-ṭīkā  in the manner
= Svarūpa is the author of the *sūtras*, and Raghunātha the author of their explanation (*vṛtti*). I describe them more fully, in the manner of a *pā̃ji-ṭīkā* (an introductory commentary).

@11
in that  faith  placing  hear  of the emotions  the description
will arise  of the emotions  understanding  you will gain  of love  the treasure
= Place your faith in this and hear the description of these emotions. You will come to understand them and gain the treasure of love.

@12
Kṛṣṇa  to Mathurā  when He went  of the gopīs  which  state  arose
Kṛṣṇa  in separation from  in the Lord  that  state  arose
= The state that came upon the *gopīs* when Kṛṣṇa went to Mathurā arose in the Lord in His separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@13
Uddhava  on seeing  as  Rādhā’s  lament
gradually  arose  the Lord’s  lament of madness
= Just as Rādhā lamented on seeing Uddhava, so, little by little, the Lord broke into the lament of madness.

@14
of Rādhikā  in the mood  the Lord’s  always  self-conception
in that  mood  of Himself  there is  the sense of being Rādhā
= In the mood of Rādhikā the Lord always had her self-conception (*abhimāna*); in that mood He thought of Himself as Rādhā.

@15
in divine madness  thus  it is  what  in this  wonder
in adhirūḍha-bhāva  of divine madness  incoherent talk  occurs
= In divine madness (*divyonmāda*) this is how it is — what is surprising in that? In *adhirūḍha-bhāva* the incoherent talk of divine madness arises.

@16
of this  called mohana
a state  some  indescribable  having reached
resembling bewilderment  some  wondrous display
divine madness  thus  is called
udghūrṇā  citra-jalpa  and others
its varieties  many  are held
= “When the *bhāva* called *mohana* reaches a certain indescribable state, there appears a wondrous display resembling bewilderment; it is called divine madness (*divyonmāda*). It is held to have many varieties, such as *udghūrṇā* and *citra-jalpa*.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.190)

@17
one day  Mahāprabhu  had lain down  to rest
Kṛṣṇa  the rāsa-līlā  performs  He saw  a dream
= One day Mahāprabhu had lain down to rest, and He dreamed that Kṛṣṇa was performing the *rāsa-līlā*.

@18
bent in three places  beautiful  body  with the flute at His lips
yellow garment  forest garland  of Madana  the enchanter
= His beautiful body was bent in three places, the flute was at His lips; He wore yellow cloth and a garland of forest flowers — He was the enchanter of Madana, the god of love.

@19
in a circle  the gopīs  were  dancing
in the middle  with Rādhā  dances  of the king of Vraja  the son
= The *gopīs* danced in a circle, and in the middle the son of the king of Vraja danced with Rādhā.

@20
seeing  the Lord  in that  rasa  absorbed  became
in Vṛndāvana  Kṛṣṇa  I have found  this  thought  He had
= Seeing this, the Lord became absorbed in that *rasa* and thought, “I have found Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana!”

@21
the Lord’s  lateness  seeing  Govinda  woke Him
on waking  that it was a dream  He understood  the Lord  sorrowful  became
= Seeing that the Lord was late in rising, Govinda woke Him. On waking, the Lord understood that it had been a dream, and He became sorrowful.

@22
by bodily habit  the daily duties  having  completed
at the proper time  going  He made  Jagannātha’s  darśana
= Out of bodily habit He completed His daily duties, and at the proper time He went to see Jagannātha.

@23
for as long as  darśana  He takes  of Garuḍa  behind
the Lord’s  in front  darśana  take  people  by hundreds of thousands
= He would look at Jagannātha from behind the Garuḍa pillar, and in front of the Lord hundreds of thousands of people were looking too.

@24
an Oriyā  one  woman  in the crowd  sight  not  getting
onto Garuḍa  climbing  looks  on the Lord’s  shoulder  foot  placing
= An Oriyā woman who could not see through the crowd climbed onto the Garuḍa pillar and looked, placing her foot on the Lord’s shoulder.

@25
seeing (this)  Govinda  hastily  that  woman  put off
her  to bring down  the Lord  Govinda  forbade
= Seeing this, Govinda hastily tried to put her off, but the Lord forbade Govinda to bring her down.

@26
ādivasyā  this  woman  do not  make  removal
let her make  as she wishes  Jagannātha’s  darśana
= “Foolish fellow (*ādivasyā*)! Do not remove this woman. Let her look at Jagannātha as much as she wishes.”

@27
hurriedly  that  woman  to the ground  came down
Mahāprabhu  seeing  His  feet  she honored
= That woman hurriedly came down to the ground, and, seeing Mahāprabhu, she bowed at His feet.

@28
her  eagerness  seeing  the Lord  to speak  began
such  eagerness  Jagannātha  to Me  not  has given
= Seeing her eagerness, the Lord began to speak: “Jagannātha has not given Me such eagerness!

@29
in Jagannātha  absorbed  her  body  mind  and life
on My  shoulder  foot  she has placed  that  not  she knows
= Her body, mind and life are absorbed in Jagannātha; she does not even know that she has put her foot on My shoulder.

@30
oh  fortunate  is she  I honor  her  feet
her  by the grace  such  eagerness  in Me  may  arise
= Oh, how fortunate she is! I bow to her feet. By her grace may such eagerness arise in Me too.

@31
before  I  when  made  Jagannātha’s  darśana
in Jagannātha  I saw  directly  of the king of Vraja  the son
= Before, when I looked at Jagannātha, I saw in Jagannātha the son of the king of Vraja Himself.

@32
of the dream  in absorption in the vision  like that  became  the mind
wherever  I looked  everywhere  the One with the flute at His lips
= Absorbed in what I saw in the dream, My mind stayed in that state, and wherever I looked I saw everywhere the One with the flute at His lips.”[^32-1]

@33
now  when  the woman  seeing  the Lord’s  external consciousness  came
of Jagannātha  Subhadrā  and Balarāma  the forms  He saw
= Now, when on seeing the woman the Lord came to external consciousness, He saw the forms of Jagannātha, Subhadrā and Balarāma.

@34
at Kurukṣetra  seeing  Kṛṣṇa  so  became  His mind
where  to Kurukṣetra  I have come  where  Vṛndāvana
= His mind became like that of one who sees Kṛṣṇa at Kurukṣetra: “Where have I come — to Kurukṣetra! And where is Vṛndāvana?”

@35
a found jewel  losing  so  agitated  He became
dejected  becoming  the Lord  to His own  residence  came
= Like one who has lost a jewel he had found, He became agitated, and the Lord came back, dejected, to His residence.

@36
the ground  upon  sitting  with His own nails  the ground  He scratches
a Ganges of tears  from the eyes  flows  anything  not  He sees
= Sitting on the ground, He scratched the ground with His nails; a Ganges of tears flowed from His eyes, and He saw nothing at all.

@37
I found  of Vṛndāvana  the Lord  again  I lost
who  My  has taken  Kṛṣṇa  where  I  have come
= “I found the Lord of Vṛndāvana, and I have lost Him again! Who has taken My Kṛṣṇa? Where have I come?”

@38
in absorption in the dream  with love  the Lord’s  overflowing  mind
external consciousness  when it comes  it is  as if  He lost  a treasure
= Absorbed in the dream, the Lord’s mind overflowed with love; when external consciousness returned, it was as if He had lost a treasure.

@39
a madman  like  the Lord  does  singing  dancing
of the body  by the nature  He does  bathing  eating  duties
= Like a madman the Lord sang and danced; He bathed and ate only by the habit of the body.

@40
night  when it came  Svarūpa  and Rāmānanda  taking
His own  heart’s  feelings  He tells  opening
= When night came, He would sit with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda and open to them the feelings of His heart.

@41
having found  having lost  Acyuta  the wealth  My mind
went  out of grief  abandoning  the body  the house
having taken up  a kāpālika’s  ways  My
to Vṛndāvana  with the senses  the disciples  the band
= “My mind found the wealth of Acyuta and lost it again. In its grief it abandoned its house, the body, took up the ways of a *kāpālika* yogī, and went off to Vṛndāvana with its band of disciples, the senses.”

@42
a found jewel  having lost
its  qualities  remembering
Mahāprabhu  with anguish  overwhelmed
of Rāya and Svarūpa  the necks  clasping
He says  alas  Hari  Hari
composure  went  He became  restless
= Having lost the jewel He had found and remembering its qualities, Mahāprabhu was overwhelmed with anguish. Clasping Rāya and Svarūpa by the neck, He cried, “Alas! Hari! Hari!” His composure gone, He became restless.

@43
hear  friends  of Kṛṣṇa  the sweetness
for which  in greed  My  mind
giving up  of society  of the Vedas  the duties
a yogī  becoming  became  a beggar
= “Hear, My friends, of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness! Craving it, My mind has given up the duties of society and of the Vedas, become a yogī and turned beggar.

@44
of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  the circle
pure  conch-shell  earrings
has fashioned  Śuka  the craftsman
those  earrings  in the ears  wearing
of thirst  the gourd  bowl  holding
of hope  the bag  the shoulder  upon
= The circle of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes is its pure conch-shell earrings, fashioned by the craftsman Śuka. Wearing those earrings, holding the gourd-bowl of thirst, with the bag of hope over its shoulder,

@45
of anxiety  the patched quilt  wrapping  about the body
with dust  ash  soiled  body
alas  Kṛṣṇa  raving  the answer
agitation  twelve (bangles)  on the arm
of greed  the bag  it took  on the head
for lack of alms  thin  the body
= wrapped in the patched quilt of anxiety, its body soiled with dust and ash, it answers everything with the raving cry, “Alas, Kṛṣṇa!” The twelve bangles of agitation are on its arm, the bag of longing is on its head, and for lack of alms its body has grown thin.

@46
Vyāsa  Śuka and the other  yogīs
Kṛṣṇa  the Self  stainless
in Vraja  His  all  the pastimes
in the Bhāgavata and other  scriptures
have made  description
those  songs  it recites  constantly
= The yogīs Vyāsa, Śuka and others have described in the Bhāgavata and the other scriptures all the pastimes in Vraja of Kṛṣṇa, the stainless Self; those songs (*tarjā*) it recites constantly.

@47
the ten senses  disciples  making
Mahā-bāul  the name  taking
the disciples  taking  it made  departure
My  body  its own  home
sense enjoyment  the great wealth
all  leaving  it went  to Vṛndāvana
= Making the ten senses its disciples and taking the name “Mahā-bāul”, it set off with its disciples. Leaving everything — its own home, My body, and the great wealth of sense enjoyment — it went to Vṛndāvana.

@48
in Vṛndāvana  the subjects
all  the stationary  and moving
trees  creepers  in the householder  order
in their  homes  begging
fruits  roots  leaves  eating
this  livelihood  it follows  with the disciples
= The subjects of Vṛndāvana — all beings, stationary and moving, the trees and creepers — live as householders. It begs in their homes and eats fruits, roots and leaves; this is how it lives with its disciples.

@49
Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  form  taste
fragrance  sound  touch
that  nectar  relish  the gopīs
of them all  of the mouthfuls  in the remnants
bringing  the five senses  the disciples
by those  alms  they sustain  life
= The *gopīs* relish the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, form, taste, fragrance, sound and touch. The disciples, the five senses, bring the remnants of their meal, and on those alms they sustain their lives.

@50
empty  bower  pavilion  in a corner
yoga practice  on Kṛṣṇa  in meditation
there  it stays  with  the disciples
Kṛṣṇa  the Self  stainless
directly  to see  the mind (longs)
in meditation  the nights  it spends  awake
= In the corner of an empty bower-pavilion it stays with its disciples, practising yoga in meditation on Kṛṣṇa. Longing to see Kṛṣṇa, the stainless Self, face to face, it stays awake all night in meditation.

@51
the mind  from Kṛṣṇa  separated
in sorrow  the mind  became  a yogī
in that  separation  ten  states  arise
by those  states  distraught  becoming
the mind  went  fleeing
empty  My  body  the dwelling
= Separated from Kṛṣṇa, My mind became a yogī in its sorrow, and in that separation the ten states arose. Distraught in those states, the mind fled away, and My body, its dwelling, stands empty.”

@52
Kṛṣṇa  in separation from  of the gopīs  ten  states  arise
those  ten  states  are  in the Lord  arisen
= In separation from Kṛṣṇa ten states arise in the *gopīs*; those same ten states arose in the Lord.

@53
anxious thought  here  sleeplessness  agitation
emaciation  uncleanliness of body
incoherent talk  disease  madness
fainting  death  states  ten
= “Here the ten states are anxious thought, sleeplessness, agitation, emaciation, uncleanliness of body, incoherent talk, disease, madness, fainting and death.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 15.167)

@54
by these  ten states  the Lord  distraught  night and day
sometimes  some  state  arises  steady  not  in mind
= Night and day the Lord was distraught by these ten states: now one state would arise, now another, and His mind found no rest.

@55
this  having said  Mahāprabhu  silence  kept
Rāmānanda Rāya  verses  to recite  began
= Having said this, Mahāprabhu fell silent, and Rāmānanda Rāya began to recite verses.

@56
Svarūpa Gosāñi  does  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  singing
the two  persons  somewhat  made  the Lord’s  external  awareness
= Svarūpa Gosāñi sang of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, and together the two brought the Lord somewhat back to external awareness.

@57
in this way  half the night  they made  pass
in the inner  room  the Lord  they made  lie down
= In this way they passed half the night, and then they had the Lord lie down in the inner room.

@58
Rāmānanda Rāya  then  went  to his own  house
Svarūpa  Govinda  both  lay down  at the door
= Rāmānanda Rāya then went home, and Svarūpa and Govinda both lay down at the door.

@59
all  night  Mahāprabhu  does  keeping awake
loudly  making  He utters  of Kṛṣṇa’s  names  saṅkīrtana
= Mahāprabhu stayed awake all night, loudly chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa.

@60
sound  not  getting  Svarūpa  the door  moved  away
the three doors  shut  are  the Lord  not  in the room
= Hearing no sound, Svarūpa opened the door: the three doors were still shut, yet the Lord was not in the room.

@notes
[^32-1]: Verses 28–32 are the Lord’s words, as in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (the quotation closes after verse 32). Some editions read verse 31 as *pūrve āsi’ yabe … jagannāthe dekhe*, which makes verses 31–32 the narrator’s account.
