@title The Divine Madness of Śrī Caitanya in Separation from Kṛṣṇa

@1
Kṛṣṇa  from separation  through the bewilderment
with the mind  with the body  with the intelligence
whatever  whatever  did  Gaurāṅga
of that  a particle  is told  now
= Now I shall tell a small part of all that Gaurāṅga did with His mind, body and intelligence in the bewilderment born of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  Himself  the Supreme Lord
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  of the devotees  the life
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya, the Supreme Lord Himself! Glory, glory to Gauracandra, the life of His devotees!

@3
glory  glory  to Nityānanda  Caitanya’s  life
glory to Advaitācārya  glory  to Gaura  most dear
= Glory, glory to Nityānanda, the life of Caitanya! Glory to Advaitācārya, most dear to Gaura!

@4
glory  to Svarūpa  to Śrīvāsa and the others  the Lord’s  devotees
strength  give  I may do  so that  of Caitanya  the description
= Glory to Svarūpa, to Śrīvāsa and to all the Lord’s devotees! Grant me the strength to describe Śrī Caitanya.

@5
the Lord’s  madness of separation  mood  profound
to understand  not  can  anyone  even if  he is  wise
= The Lord’s mood of madness in separation is profound: no one can understand it, even if he is wise.

@6
to understand  not  we can  what  to describe  who  can
he  understands  describes  Caitanya  power  gives  to whom
= What we cannot understand — who can describe it? Only he understands and describes it to whom Caitanya gives the power.

@7
Svarūpa Gosāñi  and  Raghunātha dāsa
of these  two  in the diaries  this pastime  is revealed
= These pastimes are revealed in the diaries (*kaḍacā*) of these two, Svarūpa Gosāñi and Raghunātha dāsa.

@8
at that time  these  two  stayed  Mahāprabhu’s  at the side
other  all  diary-writers  stayed  in distant lands
= At that time these two lived at Mahāprabhu’s side, while all the other diarists lived in distant places.

@9
moment  by moment  experiencing  these  two
briefly  at length  make  diary  composition
= Experiencing His pastimes moment by moment, these two recorded them in their diaries, sometimes briefly and sometimes at length.

@10
Svarūpa  the author of the sūtras  Raghunātha  the author of the explanation
their  expansion  I describe  of a pā̃ji-ṭīkā  in the manner
= Svarūpa is the author of the *sūtras*, and Raghunātha the author of their explanation (*vṛtti*). I describe them more fully, in the manner of a *pā̃ji-ṭīkā* (an introductory commentary).

@11
in that  faith  placing  hear  of the emotions  the description
will arise  of the emotions  understanding  you will gain  of love  the treasure
= Place your faith in this and hear the description of these emotions. You will come to understand them and gain the treasure of love.

@12
Kṛṣṇa  to Mathurā  when He went  of the gopīs  which  state  arose
Kṛṣṇa  in separation from  in the Lord  that  state  arose
= The state that came upon the *gopīs* when Kṛṣṇa went to Mathurā arose in the Lord in His separation from Kṛṣṇa.

@13
Uddhava  on seeing  as  Rādhā’s  lament
gradually  arose  the Lord’s  lament of madness
= Just as Rādhā lamented on seeing Uddhava, so, little by little, the Lord broke into the lament of madness.

@14
of Rādhikā  in the mood  the Lord’s  always  self-conception
in that  mood  of Himself  there is  the sense of being Rādhā
= In the mood of Rādhikā the Lord always had her self-conception (*abhimāna*); in that mood He thought of Himself as Rādhā.

@15
in divine madness  thus  it is  what  in this  wonder
in adhirūḍha-bhāva  of divine madness  incoherent talk  occurs
= In divine madness (*divyonmāda*) this is how it is — what is surprising in that? In *adhirūḍha-bhāva* the incoherent talk of divine madness arises.

@16
of this  called mohana
a state  some  indescribable  having reached
resembling bewilderment  some  wondrous display
divine madness  thus  is called
udghūrṇā  citra-jalpa  and others
its varieties  many  are held
= “When the *bhāva* called *mohana* reaches a certain indescribable state, there appears a wondrous display resembling bewilderment; it is called divine madness (*divyonmāda*). It is held to have many varieties, such as *udghūrṇā* and *citra-jalpa*.” (Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi 14.190)

@17
one day  Mahāprabhu  had lain down  to rest
Kṛṣṇa  the rāsa-līlā  performs  He saw  a dream
= One day Mahāprabhu had lain down to rest, and He dreamed that Kṛṣṇa was performing the *rāsa-līlā*.

@18
bent in three places  beautiful  body  with the flute at His lips
yellow garment  forest garland  of Madana  the enchanter
= His beautiful body was bent in three places, the flute was at His lips; He wore yellow cloth and a garland of forest flowers — He was the enchanter of Madana, the god of love.

@19
in a circle  the gopīs  were  dancing
in the middle  with Rādhā  dances  of the king of Vraja  the son
= The *gopīs* danced in a circle, and in the middle the son of the king of Vraja danced with Rādhā.

@20
seeing  the Lord  in that  rasa  absorbed  became
in Vṛndāvana  Kṛṣṇa  I have found  this  thought  He had
= Seeing this, the Lord became absorbed in that *rasa* and thought, “I have found Kṛṣṇa in Vṛndāvana!”

@21
the Lord’s  lateness  seeing  Govinda  woke Him
on waking  that it was a dream  He understood  the Lord  sorrowful  became
= Seeing that the Lord was late in rising, Govinda woke Him. On waking, the Lord understood that it had been a dream, and He became sorrowful.

@22
by bodily habit  the daily duties  having  completed
at the proper time  going  He made  Jagannātha’s  darśana
= Out of bodily habit He completed His daily duties, and at the proper time He went to see Jagannātha.

@23
for as long as  darśana  He takes  of Garuḍa  behind
the Lord’s  in front  darśana  take  people  by hundreds of thousands
= He would look at Jagannātha from behind the Garuḍa pillar, and in front of the Lord hundreds of thousands of people were looking too.

@24
an Oriyā  one  woman  in the crowd  sight  not  getting
onto Garuḍa  climbing  looks  on the Lord’s  shoulder  foot  placing
= An Oriyā woman who could not see through the crowd climbed onto the Garuḍa pillar and looked, placing her foot on the Lord’s shoulder.

@25
seeing (this)  Govinda  hastily  that  woman  put off
her  to bring down  the Lord  Govinda  forbade
= Seeing this, Govinda hastily tried to put her off, but the Lord forbade Govinda to bring her down.

@26
ādivasyā  this  woman  do not  make  removal
let her make  as she wishes  Jagannātha’s  darśana
= “Foolish fellow (*ādivasyā*)! Do not remove this woman. Let her look at Jagannātha as much as she wishes.”

@27
hurriedly  that  woman  to the ground  came down
Mahāprabhu  seeing  His  feet  she honored
= That woman hurriedly came down to the ground, and, seeing Mahāprabhu, she bowed at His feet.

@28
her  eagerness  seeing  the Lord  to speak  began
such  eagerness  Jagannātha  to Me  not  has given
= Seeing her eagerness, the Lord began to speak: “Jagannātha has not given Me such eagerness!

@29
in Jagannātha  absorbed  her  body  mind  and life
on My  shoulder  foot  she has placed  that  not  she knows
= Her body, mind and life are absorbed in Jagannātha; she does not even know that she has put her foot on My shoulder.

@30
oh  fortunate  is she  I honor  her  feet
her  by the grace  such  eagerness  in Me  may  arise
= Oh, how fortunate she is! I bow to her feet. By her grace may such eagerness arise in Me too.
