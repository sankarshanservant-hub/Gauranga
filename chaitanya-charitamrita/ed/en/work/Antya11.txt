@title The Departure of Haridāsa Ṭhākura

@1
I bow  to Haridāsa  to him
to Caitanya  to Him  and  his Lord
departed  though  whose  body
on His own lap  placing  danced  who
= I bow to Haridāsa and to his Lord, Caitanya, who took Haridāsa’s body on His lap after he had departed, and danced.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī  Caitanya  glory  to the all-merciful
glory to Him dear to Advaita  dear to Nityānanda  glory
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to the all-merciful Lord! Glory to Him who is dear to Advaita and dear to Nityānanda!

@3
glory  to the Lord of Śrīnivāsa  the master of Haridāsa
glory  dear to Gadādhara  the Lord of Svarūpa’s life
= Glory to the Lord of Śrīnivāsa, the master of Haridāsa! Glory to Him who is dear to Gadādhara, the Lord of Svarūpa’s life!

@4
glory  dear to Kāśīśvara  the Lord of Jagadānanda’s life
glory  the Lord of Rūpa, Sanātana and Raghunātha
= Glory to Him who is dear to Kāśīśvara, the Lord of Jagadānanda’s life! Glory to the Lord of Rūpa, Sanātana and Raghunātha!

@5
glory  golden  of body  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  the Supreme Lord
mercy  showing  give  O Lord  of Your own feet  the gift
= Glory to Kṛṣṇa of golden form, the Supreme Lord Himself! O Lord, be merciful and grant me the shelter of Your feet.

@6
glory  Nityānanda the moon  Caitanya’s  life
Your  at the lotus feet  devotion  give  as a gift
= Glory to Nityānandacandra, the life of Caitanya! Grant me devotion to Your lotus feet.

@7
glory  glory to Advaitacandra  by Caitanya  honored
at Your own feet  devotion  give  glory to Advaitācārya
= Glory to Advaitacandra, whom Caitanya honors! Glory to Advaitācārya! Grant me devotion to Your feet.

@8
glory  Gaura’s  devotees  all  Gaura  whose  life
all  devotees  together  to me  devotion  give  as a gift
= Glory to all the devotees of Gaura, whose very life is Gaura! All you devotees, together grant me devotion.

@9
glory  to Rūpa  Sanātana  Jīva  Raghunātha
Raghunātha  Gopāla  six  my  lords of life
= Glory to Rūpa, Sanātana, Jīva, Raghunātha, Raghunātha and Gopāla! These six are the lords of my life.

@10
of all these  by the mercy  I write  Caitanya’s  pastimes  qualities
somehow  or other  I write  I make  myself  pure
= By the mercy of all of them I write of Caitanya’s pastimes and qualities. However I may write, I do it to purify myself.

@11
thus  Mahāprabhu’s  in Nīlācala  residence
with Him  the devotees  taking  the pastimes of kīrtana
= Thus Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala, enjoying the pastimes of kīrtana with His devotees.

@12
by day  dancing and kīrtana  seeing the Lord (Jagannātha)
at night  Rāya and Svarūpa  with  the relishing of rasa
= By day He danced and sang kīrtana and beheld Lord Jagannātha; by night He relished rasa with Rāya and Svarūpa.

@13
thus  Mahāprabhu’s  in happiness  time  passes
Kṛṣṇa’s  of separation  transformations  in His body  many  arise
= So Mahāprabhu’s days passed happily, and in His body many transformations of separation from Kṛṣṇa appeared.

@14
day  by day  grow  the transformations  at night  intensely
anxiety  agitation  delirious talk and the rest  as many as  the scriptures  describe
= Day by day these transformations grew, and at night they became intense: anxiety, agitation, delirious talk and all the others the scriptures describe.

@15
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  and  Rāmānanda Rāya
night and day  give  the two  the Lord  help
= Svarūpa Gosāñi and Rāmānanda Rāya, the two of them, attended the Lord day and night.

@16
one day  Govinda  mahā-prasāda  taking
to Haridāsa  to give  went  joyful  being
= One day Govinda took mahā-prasāda and joyfully went to give it to Haridāsa.

@17
he sees  Haridāsa  Ṭhākura  has  lain down
slowly  slowly  is doing  the counted  saṅkīrtana
= He saw that Haridāsa Ṭhākura was lying down, very slowly chanting his fixed number of names.

@18
Govinda  says  get up  coming  take  the meal
Haridāsa  says  today  I shall observe  a fast
= Govinda said, “Get up, come and eat.” Haridāsa replied, “Today I shall fast.

@19
the counted  chanting  is complete  not  how  shall I eat
mahā-prasāda  you have brought  how  shall I disregard (it)
= My counted chanting is not complete; how can I eat? Yet you have brought mahā-prasāda; how can I disregard it?”

@20
so  saying  to the mahā-prasāda  he made  obeisance
one  particle  taking  of it  he made  eating
= Saying this, he bowed down to the mahā-prasāda, took a single particle (*rañca*) of it and ate it.

@21
the next  day  Mahāprabhu  his  place  came to
well  are you  Haridāsa  saying  him  He asked
= The next day Mahāprabhu came to him and asked, “Haridāsa, are you well?”

@22
obeisance  making  he  made  submission
the body  well  is  my  unwell  intelligence and mind
= He bowed down and submitted, “My body is well, but my mind and intelligence are not.”

@23
the Lord  says  what  illness  tell  indeed  precisely
he  says  the counted  chanting  not  is completed
= The Lord said, “What is the illness? Tell Me exactly.” He said, “I cannot complete my counted chanting.”

@24
the Lord  says  old  you have become  the number  small  make
perfect  in body  you are  for sādhana  eagerness  why  do you show
= The Lord said, “You have grown old; reduce your number. You are perfect in body; why are you so intent on sādhana?

@25
the people  to deliver  this  your  descent
of the name  the glory  in the world  you have made  preaching
= You have descended to deliver the people, and you have preached the glory of the holy name in the world.

@26
now  small  the number  making  do  saṅkīrtana
Haridāsa  says  hear  my  submission
= Now reduce your number and perform saṅkīrtana.” Haridāsa said, “Please hear my submission.

@27
in a low caste  birth  my  contemptible  body
in low deeds  engaged  I  the lowest  wretch
= I was born in a low caste, and my body is contemptible. I am given to low deeds — I am the lowest of wretches.

@28
unfit to be seen  unfit to be touched  me  acceptance  You made
Raurava  from  snatching  me  to Vaikuṇṭha  You raised
= I am unfit to be seen or touched, yet You accepted me. You snatched me out of the hell of Raurava and raised me up to Vaikuṇṭha.

@29
independent  Lord  You  are  acting at will
the world  You make dance  whom  as  wish  there is
= You are the independent Lord and act as You will. You make the world dance — each one as You please.

@30
much  You made dance  me  grace  bestowing
of brāhmaṇas  the śrāddha plate  I ate  a mleccha  being
= By Your grace You have made me dance in many ways: though a *mleccha*, I ate the *śrāddha* plate meant for brāhmaṇas.

@31
one  desire  there is  my  many  days  since
the pastimes  will wind up  You  feels  my  heart
= For many days I have had one desire. My heart tells me that You will soon wind up Your pastimes.

@32
that  pastime  O Lord  to me  ever  not  show
Yourself  before  my  body  let it fall
= Lord, never let me see that pastime. Let my body fall before You.

@33
on my heart  I shall hold  Your  lotus  feet
with my eyes  I shall see  Your  moonlike  face
= I shall hold Your lotus feet to my heart and gaze with my eyes upon Your moonlike face.

@34
with my tongue  I shall utter  Your  ‘Kṛṣṇa-Caitanya’  name
in this way  my  desire  I shall leave  my life
= With my tongue I shall utter Your name, “Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.” This is my desire — to give up my life in this way.

@35
my  this  desire  if  by Your  grace  comes true
this  request  my  grant  O merciful One
= If by Your grace this desire of mine may come true, then grant this request of mine, O merciful Lord.

@36
this  low  body  my  may it fall  You  before
this  fulfillment of desire  my  on You alone  depends
= Let this low body of mine fall before You. The fulfillment of this desire depends on You alone.”

@37
the Lord  says  Haridāsa  whatever  you  will ask
Kṛṣṇa  merciful  that  surely  will do
= The Lord said, “Haridāsa, whatever you ask, merciful Kṛṣṇa will surely grant.

@38
but  My  whatever  happiness  all  with you  having
for you  fitting  it is not  you will go  Me  leaving
= But whatever happiness I have, I have it all with you. It is not fitting for you to go away and leave Me.”

@39
the feet  clasping  says  Haridāsa  not  do  māyā
surely  to me, the fallen  O Lord  show  this  mercy
= Clasping His feet, Haridāsa said, “Do not show me *māyā*! Lord, surely show this fallen one this mercy (*dayā*).

@40
my  crest jewels  how many  how many  great souls
Your  of the pastimes  helpers  millions of devotees  there are
= There are so many great souls, crest jewels far above me; millions of devotees assist in Your pastimes.

@41
like me  if  one  insect  dying  goes
one  ant  if it dies  to the earth  what  loss  has there been
= If one insect like me dies and is gone, what is lost? When an ant dies, what loss does the earth suffer?

@42
affectionate to devotees  You  I  a semblance of a devotee
surely  You will fulfil  O Lord  my  this  hope
= You are affectionate to Your devotees (*bhakata-vatsala*), and I am a mere semblance of a devotee. Surely, Lord, You will fulfil this hope of mine.”

@43
the midday duties  to perform  the Lord  went  Himself
the Lord (Jagannātha)  after seeing  tomorrow  He will give  darśana
= The Lord then went to perform His midday duties, promising that the next day, after seeing Jagannātha, He would come to give Haridāsa His darśana.

@44
then  Mahāprabhu  him  making  embrace
the midday duties  to perform  to the sea  He made  His way
= Mahāprabhu embraced him and went to the sea to perform His midday duties.

@45
in the morning  the Lord (Jagannātha)  seeing  all  the devotees  taking
Haridāsa  to see  He came  haste  making
= In the morning, after seeing Jagannātha, He hurried with all the devotees to see Haridāsa.

@46
Haridāsa’s  before  coming  He gave  darśana
Haridāsa  bowed to  the Lord’s  and  the Vaiṣṇavas’  feet
= Coming before Haridāsa, the Lord gave him darśana, and Haridāsa bowed to the feet of the Lord and of the Vaiṣṇavas.

@47
the Lord  says  Haridāsa  tell  the news
Haridāsa  says  O Lord  whatever  order  Your
= The Lord said, “Haridāsa, what news?” Haridāsa said, “Lord, as You command.”

@48
in the courtyard  began  the Lord  a great saṅkīrtana
Vakreśvara Paṇḍita  where  does  dancing
= In the courtyard the Lord began a great saṅkīrtana, in which Vakreśvara Paṇḍita danced.

@49
Svarūpa Gosāñi  and others  all  the Lord’s  associates
Haridāsa  surrounding  do  saṅkīrtana of the name
= Svarūpa Gosāñi and all the Lord’s other associates surrounded Haridāsa and sang the holy name.

@50
Rāmānanda  Sārvabhauma  of all  in front
Haridāsa’s  qualities  the Lord  began  to tell
= Before Rāmānanda, Sārvabhauma and all the others, the Lord began to speak of Haridāsa’s qualities.

@51
Haridāsa’s  qualities  telling  the Lord  became  five-mouthed
telling  telling  the Lord’s  grows  great joy
= As He told of Haridāsa’s qualities, the Lord became as if five-mouthed, and the more He spoke, the greater His joy grew.

@52
by Haridāsa’s  qualities  everyone’s  amazed  become  minds
all the devotees  worship  Haridāsa’s  feet
= Haridāsa’s qualities filled everyone’s mind with wonder, and all the devotees bowed to his feet.

@53
Haridāsa  in front of himself  the Lord  seated
his own eyes  two  bees  on the lotus face  placed
= Haridāsa seated the Lord in front of himself and fixed his eyes, like two bees, on the lotus of His face.

@54
to his own heart  bringing  holding  the Lord’s  feet
all  devotees’  of the feet  the dust  of his head  the ornament
= He brought the Lord’s feet to his heart and held them there; the dust of the feet of all the devotees adorned his head.

@55
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa-Caitanya  Prabhu  he says  again  and again
of the Lord’s face  the sweetness  he drinks  in his eyes  streams of tears
= Again and again he said, “Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu!” He drank in the sweetness of the Lord’s face, and streams of tears flowed from his eyes.

@56
Śrī  Kṛṣṇa-Caitanya  the word  while doing  utterance
the name  together with  his life  he made  go out
= While uttering the words “Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya,” he sent his life-air out of the body together with the name.

@57
a great master of yoga  like  at his own will  departure
Bhīṣma’s  departure  for everyone  there was  the remembrance
= Like a great master of yoga, he departed at his own will, and everyone remembered the departure of Bhīṣma.

@58
‘Hari’  with the sounds ‘Kṛṣṇa’  all  make  a tumult
in the bliss of love  Mahāprabhu  became  overwhelmed
= Everyone raised a tumult with the sounds “Hari!” and “Kṛṣṇa!”, and Mahāprabhu was overwhelmed with the bliss of love.

@59
Haridāsa’s  body  the Lord  onto His lap  lifting
in the courtyard  dances  the Lord  absorbed in love  being
= Lifting Haridāsa’s body onto His lap, the Lord danced in the courtyard, absorbed in love.

@60
by the Lord’s  ecstasy  helpless  all the devotees
absorbed in love  all  dance  do  kīrtana
= The Lord’s ecstasy overpowered all the devotees; absorbed in love, they all danced and sang kīrtana.
