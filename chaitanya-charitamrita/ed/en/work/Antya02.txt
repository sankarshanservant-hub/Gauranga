@title The Punishment of Choṭa Haridāsa

@1
I bow down  of my śrī guru  the beautiful lotus feet  to the śrī gurus  to the Vaiṣṇavas  and
to Śrī Rūpa  with his elder brother  with Raghunātha and his companions  to him  with Jīva
with Advaita  with the Avadhūta  with His associates  together  to Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva
of Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa  to the feet  with Their companions  with Lalitā  and Śrī Viśākhā  and
= I bow down to the beautiful lotus feet of my śrī guru, to the śrī gurus and to the Vaiṣṇavas; to Śrī Rūpa, together with his elder brother (Sanātana), with Raghunātha and his companions and with Jīva; to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, together with Advaita, with the Avadhūta (Nityānanda) and with His associates; and to the feet of Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, together with Their companions, Lalitā and Śrī Viśākhā.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
all  the worlds  to deliver  Gaura’s  descent
of deliverance  the means  His  three  kinds
= Gaura descended to deliver all the worlds, and the means by which He delivered them were of three kinds.

@4
direct  sight  and  in a worthy  devotee-soul
‘entering’  He makes  somewhere  somewhere  by ‘appearing’
= He delivered by being seen in person; and in some places He made His ‘āveśa’, entering a worthy devotee, and in others He delivered by His ‘āvirbhāva’, appearing Himself.

@5
by direct  sight  almost  all  He delivered
of Nakula  Brahmacārī  in the body  ‘entered’  He
= By being seen in person He delivered almost everyone; and He ‘entered’ the body of Nakula Brahmacārī.

@6
of Pradyumna Nṛsiṁhānanda  before  He made  ‘appearance’
‘the people  I shall deliver’  —  this  of the Lord  the nature
= Before Pradyumna Nṛsiṁhānanda He made His ‘āvirbhāva’ (appeared). “I shall deliver the people” — such is the nature of the Lord.

@7
by direct  sight  the whole  world  He delivered
once  who  saw  he  fulfilled  became
= By being seen in person He delivered the whole world: whoever saw Him even once attained the goal of life.

@8
of Gauḍa  the devotees  every year  coming
again  to Gauḍa  go  with the Lord  having met
= Every year the devotees of Gauḍa would come, meet the Lord and go back again to Gauḍa.

@9
and  of many  lands  the people  coming  to Jagannātha
Caitanya’s  feet  seeing  became  fulfilled
= And people from many lands came to Jagannātha; seeing Caitanya’s feet, they attained the goal of life.

@10
of the seven dvīpas  the people  and  of the nine khaṇḍas  the dwellers
devas  gandharvas  kinnaras  in human  guise  coming
= People of the seven dvīpas (islands) and dwellers of the nine khaṇḍas (regions) — devas, gandharvas and kinnaras — came in human guise.

@11
the Lord  seeing  they go  Vaiṣṇavas  becoming
‘Kṛṣṇa’  saying  dance  all  by prema  overwhelmed
= Seeing the Lord, they went away as Vaiṣṇavas; crying “Kṛṣṇa!”, they all danced, overwhelmed by prema.

@12
in this way  by sight  the three worlds  delivering
those who  any  to come  could not  many  worldly people
= In this way, by being seen, He delivered the three worlds. But there were many worldly people who could not come.

@13
them  all  to deliver  the Lord  in those  all  lands
in the bodies of worthy devotee-souls  makes  ‘entering’
= To deliver them, in all those lands the Lord made His ‘āveśa’, entering the bodies of worthy devotees.

@14
in that  soul  His own power  makes  manifest
his  by the sight  Vaiṣṇavas  become  in all lands
= In that soul He manifests His own power, and by seeing him people in every land become Vaiṣṇavas.

@15
in this way  by entering  He delivered  the three worlds
in Gauḍa  how  the entering  I make  a brief indication
= Thus, by His āveśa, He delivered the three worlds. I shall show in brief, as an example, how His āveśa took place in Gauḍa.

@16
in the Āmbuyā  district  there is  Nakula Brahmacārī
a supreme  Vaiṣṇava  he  great  in standing
= In the district of Āmbuyā lived Nakula Brahmacārī, a supreme Vaiṣṇava of very high standing.

@17
of Gauḍa  the people  to deliver  the wish  arose
in Nakula’s  heart  the Lord  ‘entering’  made
= Wishing to deliver the people of Gauḍa, the Lord made His ‘āveśa’ in Nakula’s heart.

@18
as if possessed by a spirit  Nakula  by prema  overwhelmed  being
laughs  weeps  dances  sings  mad  being
= Overwhelmed by prema, Nakula became as if possessed by a spirit: like a madman he laughed, wept, danced and sang.

@19
tears  trembling  stupor  perspiration  the sāttvika  transformations
constant  in prema  dancing  frequent  roars
= Tears, trembling, stupor and perspiration — the sāttvika transformations — appeared in him; in prema he danced constantly and roared again and again.

@20
the same  golden lustre  the same  always  absorption in prema
that  to see  comes  all  Gauḍa
= He had the same golden lustre as the Lord and the same constant absorption in prema, and all Gauḍa came to see it.

@21
whom  he sees  to him  says  say  Kṛṣṇa’s name
his  by the sight  the people  become  wild with prema
= To everyone he saw he said, “Say the name of Kṛṣṇa!” And by seeing him the people became wild with prema.

@22
of Caitanya  the entering  is  in Nakula’s  body
hearing  Śivānanda  came  having  doubt
= Hearing that Caitanya had entered Nakula’s body, Śivānanda came, doubting it.

@23
a test  to make  his  when  the wish  arose
outside  staying  then  reflection  he made
= Wishing to test him, he stayed outside and reflected:

@24-25
himself  he calls  me  this  if  I know
my  iṣṭa-mantra  knowing  he tells  himself
then  I know  in him  is  Caitanya’s entering
so  thinking  Śivānanda  stayed  at a distance
= “If he calls me himself — if I see that — and, knowing my iṣṭa-mantra, tells it to me himself, then I shall know that Caitanya has entered him.” Thinking thus, Śivānanda stayed at a distance.

@26
countless  of people  the throng  —  some  come  go
of people  in the press  some  sight  not  get
= There was a vast throng of people, some coming, some going; in the press of the crowd some could not even see him.

@27
the brahmacārī  says  —  Śivānanda  is  far off
people  two  four  go  call  him
= The brahmacārī said, “Śivānanda is standing far off. Two or four of you go and call him.”

@28
in four directions  run  the people  ‘Śivānanda’  crying
‘Śivānanda’  who is  you  calls  the brahmacārī
= The people ran in every direction, crying, “Śivānanda! Who is Śivānanda? The brahmacārī is calling you!”

@29
hearing  Śivānanda  Sena  there  quickly  came
obeisance  having made  him  near  sat
= Hearing this, Śivānanda Sena quickly came there, offered his obeisances and sat down near him.

@30
the brahmacārī  says  —  you  made  doubt
with one mind  being  that  hear  with certainty
= The brahmacārī said, “You have doubted. Now listen with undivided attention, and be certain:

@31
the ‘Gaura-Gopāla mantra’  your  of four syllables
disbelief  give up  which  you have made  within
= your ‘Gaura-Gopāla mantra’ has four syllables. Give up the disbelief you have kept in your heart.”

@32
then  in Śivānanda’s  mind  conviction  arose
much  respect  showing  great  devotion  he showed
= Then conviction arose in Śivānanda’s mind, and he showed him great respect and much devotion.

@33
such  Mahāprabhu’s  inconceivable  power
now  hear  the Lord’s  how  takes place  ‘appearance’
= Such is the inconceivable power of Mahāprabhu. Now hear how the Lord’s ‘āvirbhāva’ (appearance) takes place.

@34-35
in Śacī’s  house  and  at Nityānanda’s  dancing
at Śrīvāsa’s  kīrtana  and  in Rāghava’s  home
in these  four  places  the Lord’s  always  ‘appearance’
by prema drawn  He is  —  the Lord’s  innate  nature
= In Śacī’s house, at Nityānanda’s dancing, at Śrīvāsa’s kīrtana and in Rāghava’s home — in these four places the Lord always makes His ‘āvirbhāva’. He is drawn by prema: that is the Lord’s innate nature.

@36
of Nṛsiṁhānanda  before  appearing
a meal  He took  that  hear  attentively
= Appearing before Nṛsiṁhānanda, He took a meal. Hear of it attentively.

@37
Śivānanda’s  nephew  Śrīkānta Sena  by name
by the Lord’s  mercy  he  very  fortunate
= Śivānanda’s nephew was named Śrīkānta Sena; by the Lord’s mercy he was most fortunate.

@38
one  year  he  for the first time  alone
the Lord  to see  came  with longing  in his heart
= One year he came for the first time, alone, to see the Lord, his heart full of longing.

@39
Mahāprabhu  him  seeing  great  mercy  showed
about two months  he  of the Lord  near  stayed
= When Mahāprabhu saw him, He showed him great mercy, and he stayed near the Lord for about two months.

@40
then  the Lord  him  order  gave  to Gauḍa  to go
the devotees  He forbade  here  to come
= Then the Lord ordered him to go to Gauḍa, and He forbade the devotees to come here:

@41
this year  there  I  shall go  Myself
there  I shall meet  all  with Advaita and the others
= “This year I shall go there Myself, and there I shall meet everyone — Advaita and all the others.

@42
to Śivānanda  say  —  I  this  in Pauṣa month
unexpectedly  certainly  I  shall come  his  to the side
= Tell Śivānanda that this month of Pauṣa I shall certainly come to him unexpectedly.

@43
Jagadānanda  is  there  he  alms  will give
to all  say  —  this  year  anyone  not  shall come
= Jagadānanda is there; he will give Me bhikṣā (feed Me). And tell everyone that no one is to come this year.”

@44
Śrīkānta  coming  to Gauḍa  the message  told
hearing  in the devotees’ minds  joy  arose
= Śrīkānta came to Gauḍa and delivered the message, and when the devotees heard it, their hearts were filled with joy.

@45
had been setting out  the Ācārya  remained  still  being
Śivānanda  Jagadānanda  remain  expectation  making
= Advaita Ācārya, who had been setting out, stayed where He was, and Śivānanda and Jagadānanda remained, waiting in expectation.

@46
Pauṣa  month  came  the two  provisions  having made
until evening  remain  waiting
= When the month of Pauṣa came, the two of them made all the preparations and waited until evening.

@47
so  the month  passed  the Gosāñi  not  came
Jagadānanda  Śivānanda  sorrowful  became
= In this way the month passed, and the Lord did not come. Jagadānanda and Śivānanda became sorrowful.

@48
unexpectedly  Nṛsiṁhānanda  there  came
the two  him  meeting  then  in a place  seated
= Unexpectedly Nṛsiṁhānanda arrived there. The two of them received him and gave him a seat.

@49
the two  unhappy  seeing  to them  says  Nṛsiṁhānanda
you  both  why  I see  joyless
= Seeing them both unhappy, Nṛsiṁhānanda said to them, “Why do I see you both so joyless?”

@50
then  Śivānanda  to him  everything  told
‘I shall come’  word  having given  the Lord  why  not  came
= Then Śivānanda told him everything: “The Lord gave His word that He would come. Why has He not come?”

@51
hearing  the brahmacārī  says  —  be  content
I  indeed  shall bring  Him  on the third  day
= Hearing this, the brahmacārī said, “Be content. I shall bring Him on the third day.”

@52
his  power  prema  know  both of them
he will bring  the Lord  now  certainty  they made  in mind
= Both of them knew his power and his prema, and they were certain in their minds that he would now bring the Lord.

@53
‘Pradyumna Brahmacārī’  —  his  own name
‘Nṛsiṁhānanda’  the name  his  made  Gaura-dhāma
= His own name was Pradyumna Brahmacārī; it was Gaura, the abode of all, who named him Nṛsiṁhānanda.

@54
two days  meditation  having made  to Śivānanda  he said
to Pāṇihāṭi  village  I  the Lord  have brought
= After meditating for two days, he said to Śivānanda, “I have brought the Lord to the village of Pāṇihāṭi.

@55
tomorrow  at noon  He  will come  your  to the house
cooking  provisions  bring  I  bhikṣā  shall give  to Him
= Tomorrow at noon He will come to your house. Bring the provisions for cooking; I shall give Him bhikṣā.

@56
then  Him  here  I  shall bring  quickly
with certainty  I have said  any  doubt  not  make
= Then I shall quickly bring Him here. I tell you this with certainty — have no doubt at all.

@57
what  I require  that  do  being  prompt
very  quickly  I shall do  cooking  hear  further
= Whatever I require, provide it promptly. I shall cook very quickly. Listen further:

@58
cooking  provisions  bring  I  what  want
what  he asked  Śivānanda  bringing  gave  that
= bring the provisions for cooking, whatever I want.” Whatever he asked for, Śivānanda brought and gave him.

@59
morning  from  cooking  he did  without limit
many  soups  vegetable dishes  cakes  sweet-rice  offerings
= From early morning he cooked without limit: many kinds of soup and vegetable dishes, cakes, and offerings of sweet rice.

@60
of Jagannātha  distinct  offering  separately  he served out
Caitanya  Prabhu  for  another  offering  he made
= He served out a distinct offering separately for Jagannātha, and he made another offering for Caitanya Prabhu.

@61
for his worshipful Deity  Nṛsiṁha  for  separately  he served out
to the three  having offered  outside  meditation  he did
= He served out a separate offering for his worshipful Deity, Nṛsiṁha. Having offered the food to all three, he sat outside and meditated.

@62
he sees  quickly  coming  sat down  Caitanya Gosāñi
the three  offerings  He ate  anything  left over  not
= He saw Caitanya Gosāñi quickly come and sit down. He ate all three offerings, and nothing was left.

@63
with bliss  overwhelmed  Pradyumna  fall  streams of tears
alas, alas  what  You do  saying  cries out
= Overwhelmed with bliss, Pradyumna shed streams of tears, and he cried out, “Alas, alas! What are You doing?

@64
Jagannātha and You  are one  You eat  His  offering
Nṛsiṁha’s  offering  why  do You  consume
= You and Jagannātha are one, so You may eat His offering. But why do You consume Nṛsiṁha’s offering?

@65
for Nṛsiṁha  there was  I know  today  a fast
the master  fasting  remains  live  how  the servant
= I see that today Nṛsiṁha has had to fast. If the master goes without food, how can his servant live?”

@66
the meal  seeing  although  his  in the heart  joy
Nṛsiṁha  for the sake of  outwardly  some  makes  show of sorrow
= Although his heart rejoiced at seeing the Lord eat, outwardly, for Nṛsiṁha’s sake, he made some show of sorrow.

@67
Svayaṁ  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gosāñi
with Jagannātha and Nṛsiṁha  any  difference  is not
= Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gosāñi is Svayaṁ Bhagavān, the Supreme Lord Himself: between Him and Jagannātha and Nṛsiṁha there is no difference at all.

@68
this  to know  of Pradyumna  secret  was  the wish
that  showed  the Lord  doing  eating
= Pradyumna had a secret wish to know this, and by eating the Lord showed it to him.

@69
the meal  having taken  the Lord  went  to Pāṇihāṭi
pleasure  He felt  seeing  of the dishes  the excellence
= Having eaten, the Lord went to Pāṇihāṭi, pleased by the excellence of the dishes.

@70
Śivānanda  says  —  why  do you  cry out
the brahmacārī  says  —  see  of the Lord  the conduct
= Śivānanda said, “Why are you crying out?” The brahmacārī said, “Look at the Lord’s conduct!

@71
of three  persons  the offerings  He  alone  ate
Jagannātha and Nṛsiṁha  fasting  became
= He alone has eaten the offerings meant for all three, and Jagannātha and Nṛsiṁha have been left fasting.”

@72
hearing  in Śivānanda’s  mind  arose  doubt
whether  in the ecstasy of prema  he speaks  or  true  it is
= Hearing this, doubt arose in Śivānanda’s mind: was he speaking in the ecstasy of prema, or was it true?

@73
then  to Śivānanda  something  says  the brahmacārī
the provisions  bring  for Nṛsiṁha  again  cooking  I will do
= Then the brahmacārī said to Śivānanda, “Bring the provisions; I shall cook for Nṛsiṁha once more.”

@74
then  Śivānanda  for the offering  provisions  brought
cooking  having done  to Nṛsiṁha  the offering  he presented
= Śivānanda then brought the provisions for the offering, and the brahmacārī cooked and offered the food to Nṛsiṁha.

@75
in the following year  Śivānanda  taking  the devotees
in Nīlācala  sees  going  the Lord’s  feet
= The following year Śivānanda took the devotees to Nīlācala and saw the Lord’s feet.

@76
one day  in the assembly  the Lord  the subject  brought up
Nṛsiṁhānanda’s  virtues  to tell  He began
= One day, in the assembly, the Lord brought up the subject and began to speak of Nṛsiṁhānanda’s virtues:

@77
last year  in Pauṣa  Me  he made  eat
ever  not  I ate  such  sweets  vegetable dishes
= “Last year, in Pauṣa, he fed Me. Never have I eaten such sweets and vegetable dishes.”

@78
hearing  the devotees  in mind  wonder  felt
in Śivānanda’s  mind  then  conviction  was born
= Hearing this, the devotees were filled with wonder, and then conviction was born in Śivānanda’s mind.

@79
in this way  in Śacī’s house  constantly  He eats
in Śrīvāsa’s  house  He does  the seeing of kīrtana
= In the same way He constantly takes His meals in Śacī’s house, and in Śrīvāsa’s house He watches the kīrtana.

@80
Nityānanda’s  dancing  He watches  coming  again and again
‘constant  appearance’  in Rāghava’s  house
= Again and again He comes to watch Nityānanda’s dancing, and in Rāghava’s house His ‘āvirbhāva’ is constant.

@81
by prema controlled  Gaura Prabhu  where  the highest prema
by prema  controlled  being  there  He gives  His sight
= Gaura Prabhu is controlled by prema: wherever there is the highest prema, there, controlled by it, He gives His darśana.

@82
Śivānanda’s  the extent of prema  who  to tell  can
by whose  prema  controlled  the Lord  comes  again and again
= Who can tell the full extent of Śivānanda’s prema? Controlled by his prema, the Lord comes to him again and again.

@83
thus  indeed  I have told  of Gaura  the ‘appearance’
this  whoever  hears  knows  Caitanya’s  power
= Thus I have told of Gaura’s ‘āvirbhāva’. Whoever hears of it comes to know Caitanya’s power.

@84
in Puruṣottama  near the Lord  Bhagavān Ācārya
a supreme  Vaiṣṇava  he  very learned  noble
= In Puruṣottama, near the Lord, lived Bhagavān Ācārya, a supreme Vaiṣṇava, very learned and noble.

@85
by the mood of friendship  overcome  his heart  of a cowherd boy  an incarnation
with Svarūpa Gosāñi  friendly  dealings
= His heart was filled with the mood of friendship (sakhya); he was an incarnation of a cowherd boy, and with Svarūpa Gosāñi he dealt as a friend.

@86
with single-minded devotion  has taken shelter  of Caitanya’s feet
from time to time  the Lord  he  makes  an invitation
= He had taken shelter at Caitanya’s feet with single-minded devotion, and from time to time he would invite the Lord for a meal.
