@title The Deliverance of Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya

@1
I bow down  to that  Gauracandra  who
by false arguments  whose heart was hardened
Sārvabhauma  the Lord of all
of devotion  the great abode  made
= I bow down to Gauracandra, the Lord of all, who made Sārvabhauma, whose heart had grown hard with false arguments, into a great abode of devotion.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in ecstasy  went  the Lord  to Jagannātha’s temple
Jagannātha  seeing  in love  He became  restless
= Absorbed in ecstasy, the Lord went to the temple of Jagannātha, and when He saw Jagannātha He was overwhelmed with love.

@4
Jagannātha  to embrace  He went  running
in the temple  He fell  with love  overwhelmed  being
= He ran forward to embrace Jagannātha, but, overwhelmed with love, He fell down in the temple.

@5
by providence  Sārvabhauma  Him  sees
the temple guard  from striking  he  made  prevention
= By providence Sārvabhauma saw Him there, and he stopped the temple guard from striking Him.

@6
the Lord’s  beauty  and  of love  the transformations
seeing  Sārvabhauma  became  amazed  boundlessly
= Seeing the Lord’s beauty and the transformations of love in His body, Sārvabhauma was amazed beyond measure.

@7
for a long time  consciousness  is not  of the offering  the time  came
Sārvabhauma  in his mind  then  a means  considered
= For a long time the Lord did not regain consciousness, and the time came to offer food to Jagannātha. Then Sārvabhauma considered what to do.

@8
by disciples  and temple guards  had (Him)  carried
to his house  bringing  in a pure  place  he kept (Him)  lying
= With the help of his disciples and the temple guards he had Him carried to his house and laid Him down in a pure place.

@9
breathing in and out  there is not  of the abdomen  movement
seeing  anxious  became  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind
= There was no breath and no movement of the abdomen; seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious.

@10
fine  cotton  bringing  at the tip of the nostrils  he held
slightly  moves  the cotton  seeing  composure  came
= He brought a fine wisp of cotton and held it before the Lord’s nostrils, and when he saw the cotton stir slightly, he grew calmer.

@11
sitting  the Bhaṭṭācārya  in his mind  makes  reflection
this  of great love for Kṛṣṇa  the sāttvika  transformation
= Sitting there, the Bhaṭṭācārya reflected: “This is a *sāttvika* transformation of great love for Kṛṣṇa.

@12
sūddīpta  sāttvika  this  the name  which  pralaya
in eternally  perfect  devotees  that  sūddīpta  bhāva  occurs
= This swoon, called *pralaya*, is a *sūddīpta sāttvika*, the ecstatic symptom at its most blazing; such *sūddīpta bhāva* appears only in eternally perfect devotees.

@13
adhirūḍha-mahābhāva  in whom  his  this  transformation
of a human being  in the body  I see  very  astonishing
= This transformation belongs to one who has *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva*. That I should see it in a human body is astonishing indeed!”

@14
thus  thinking  the Bhaṭṭācārya  remains  sitting
Nityānanda and the others  at the Lion Gate  arrived  going
= While the Bhaṭṭācārya sat there thinking this, Nityānanda and the others arrived at the Lion Gate.

@15
there  hearing  people  speak  among themselves  the talk
such a  sannyāsī  coming  seeing  Jagannātha
= There they heard people talking among themselves: “Such a sannyāsī came here and saw Jagannātha.

@16
unconscious  He became  consciousness  not  is  in the body
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house
= He fell unconscious, and no consciousness returned to His body. Sārvabhauma has taken Him to his own house.”

@17
hearing  all  understood  this  Mahāprabhu’s  doing
at that time  came  there  Gopīnātha Ācārya
= Hearing this, they all understood that it was Mahāprabhu’s doing. Just then Gopīnātha Ācārya came there.

@18
of Nadīyā  a resident  of Viśārada  the son-in-law
Mahāprabhu’s  devotee  he  of the Lord  the truth  knowing
= He was a resident of Nadīyā and the son-in-law of Viśārada, a devotee of Mahāprabhu who knew the truth about the Lord.

@19
with Mukunda  previously  there is  acquaintance
Mukunda  seeing  his  became  surprise
= He had known Mukunda before, and he was surprised to see him there.

@20
Mukunda  him  seeing  made  obeisance
he  embracing (him)  asks  of the Lord  the news
= On seeing him, Mukunda bowed down, and Gopīnātha embraced him and asked for news of the Lord.

@21
Mukunda  says  of the Lord  here  has been  the coming
we all  have come  Mahāprabhu  with
= Mukunda said, “The Lord has come here, and we have all come with Mahāprabhu.”

@22
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Ācārya  made  obeisance
all  meeting  he asks  of the Lord  news  again  and again
= The Ācārya bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi and, meeting them all, asked again and again for news of the Lord.

@23
Mukunda  says  Mahāprabhu  sannyāsa  having taken
to Nīlācala  came  with (Him)  us all  taking
= Mukunda said, “After taking sannyāsa, Mahāprabhu came to Nīlācala and brought us all with Him.

@24
us all  leaving  ahead  He went  for darśana
we all  behind  came  of Him  in search
= He left us and went on ahead for darśana, and we have followed in search of Him.

@25
among themselves  of the people  from the mouths  what  talk  we heard
in Sārvabhauma’s house  the Lord  inference  we made
= From what we heard people saying among themselves, we have gathered that the Lord is in Sārvabhauma’s house.

@26
of the Lord  at the sight  the Lord  in love  unconscious
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house
= At the sight of Jagannātha the Lord fell unconscious in love, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.

@27
with you  for meeting  when  my  was  mind
by providence  at that  moment  I got  your  sight
= Just as I was wishing to meet you, by providence I saw you at that very moment.

@28
come  all  let us go  Sārvabhauma’s  to the house
the Lord  seeing  afterwards  we shall have  of the Lord  darśana
= Come, let us all go to Sārvabhauma’s house. After seeing the Lord, we shall have darśana of Jagannātha.”

@29
this  hearing  Gopīnātha  all  taking
to Sārvabhauma’s house  went  delighted  being
= Hearing this, Gopīnātha joyfully took them all to Sārvabhauma’s house.

@30
to Sārvabhauma’s place  going  the Lord  he saw
the Lord  seeing  of the Ācārya  sorrow and joy  arose
= When he came to Sārvabhauma’s house and saw the Lord, the Ācārya felt both sorrow and joy.

@31
Sārvabhauma  informing  all  he took  inside
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  he  made  obeisance
= He informed Sārvabhauma and brought them all inside, and Sārvabhauma bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi.

@32
all  with  as befitting  he made  meeting
the Lord  seeing  of all  became  joyful  the minds
= He greeted each of them as was fitting, and all rejoiced at the sight of the Lord.

@33
Sārvabhauma  sent  all  darśana  to have
Candaneśvara  his own son  he gave  of all  along with
= Sārvabhauma sent them all for darśana and gave them his own son, Candaneśvara, to go with them.

@34
Jagannātha  seeing  of all  there was  joy
in ecstasy  absorbed  became  Prabhu  Nityānanda
= Seeing Jagannātha, everyone was filled with joy, and Nityānanda Prabhu was overcome with ecstasy.

@35
all  together  holding  Him  steady  made
of the Lord  the servant  garland-prasāda  bringing  gave
= Together they held Him and steadied Him, and a servant of Jagannātha brought garlands and prasāda and gave them to them.

@36
prasāda  receiving  all  became  joyful  in mind
once again  came  all  Mahāprabhu’s  to the place
= Receiving the prasāda, they all rejoiced, and they came back to Mahāprabhu.

@37
loudly  making  perform  all  of the name  saṁkīrtana
in the third  watch  came  of the Lord  consciousness
= They all chanted the holy name loudly together, and in the third watch of the day the Lord regained consciousness.

@38
a roar  making  He rises  Hari  Hari  saying
in joy  Sārvabhauma  His  took  the dust of the feet
= With a roar He rose up, crying, “Hari! Hari!”, and Sārvabhauma joyfully took the dust of His feet.

@39
Sārvabhauma  says  quickly  perform  the midday duties
I  alms  shall give  today  mahā-prasāda
= Sārvabhauma said, “Quickly finish Your midday duties. Today I shall offer You mahā-prasāda as alms.”

@40
in the sea  bath  having taken  the Lord  quickly  came
the feet  washing  the Lord  on a seat  sat
= The Lord bathed in the sea and quickly returned. He washed His feet and sat down on His seat.

@41
much  prasāda  Sārvabhauma  had brought
then  Mahāprabhu  happily  eating  did
= Sārvabhauma had a great quantity of prasāda brought, and Mahāprabhu happily took His meal.

@42
on golden plates  rice  excellent  vegetables
with the devotees  the Lord  does  eating
= Rice and excellent vegetable dishes were served on golden plates, and the Lord ate together with His devotees.

@43
Sārvabhauma  serving  does  himself
the Lord  says  to Me  give  lāphrā vegetables
= Sārvabhauma served them himself. The Lord said, “Give Me the *lāphrā*, the plain mixed vegetables,

@44
cakes and sweet drinks  give  you  to all these
to all  the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  joining  two  hands
= and give the cakes and sweet drinks to all of them.” With folded hands the Bhaṭṭācārya said to them all:

@45
Jagannātha  as  has done  eating
today  all  mahā-prasāda  do  tasting
= “Today all of you must taste all the mahā-prasāda, just as Jagannātha has eaten it.”

@46
so  saying  cakes and sweet drinks  all  he fed
the meal  having given  rinsing of the mouth  he had them do
= Saying this, he fed them all the cakes and sweet drinks, and when they had eaten he gave them water to rinse their mouths.

@47
permission  asking  Gopīnātha  Ācārya  taking
of the Lord  near  came  eating  having done
= Then, taking leave, he went with Gopīnātha Ācārya to eat, and after eating he came back to the Lord.

@48
namo nārāyaṇāya  saying  obeisance  he made
on Kṛṣṇa  the mind  may be  saying  Gosāñi  spoke
= Saying, “*Namo nārāyaṇāya*”, he bowed down to the Lord, and Gosāñi replied, “*Kṛṣṇe matir astu*—may your mind be fixed on Kṛṣṇa.”

@49
hearing  Sārvabhauma  in his mind  consideration  made
a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī  He  by His words  he knew
= Hearing this, Sārvabhauma reflected, and from His words he understood that He was a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī.

@50
to Gopīnātha  Ācārya  says  Sārvabhauma
of Gosāñi  to know  I want  what  the former āśrama
= Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya, “I would like to know about Gosāñi’s life before He took sannyāsa.”

@51
Gopīnātha Ācārya  says  in Navadvīpa  home
Jagannātha  name  title  Miśra Purandara
= Gopīnātha Ācārya said, “His home is in Navadvīpa. There lived a man named Jagannātha, whose title was Miśra Purandara.

@52
Viśvambhara  name  His  his  He  son
of Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī  He is  the daughter’s son
= This is his son, named Viśvambhara. He is also the grandson of Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, his daughter’s son.”

@53
Sārvabhauma  says  Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī
of Viśārada  a fellow student  this  his  fame
= Sārvabhauma said, “Nīlāmbara Cakravartī is known as a fellow student of Viśārada.

@54
Miśra Purandara  by him  respected  this  I know
of my father  through the connection  both  worthy of honour  making  I regard
= Miśra Purandara, I know, was respected by him. Because of their bond with my father, I honour them both.”

@55
with Nadīyā  through the connection  Sārvabhauma  glad  became
pleased  being  to Gosāñi  to speak  began
= Sārvabhauma was glad to find this connection with Nadīyā, and in his pleasure he began to speak to Gosāñi.

@56
naturally  worthy of worship  You  moreover  indeed  sannyāsa
therefore  am  Your  I  own servant
= “You are worthy of honour by birth, and on top of that You are a sannyāsī. Therefore I am Your own servant.”

@57
hearing  Mahāprabhu  made  of Śrī Viṣṇu  remembrance
to the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  some  humble words
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu remembered Śrī Viṣṇu and spoke some humble words to the Bhaṭṭācārya.

@58
you  the teacher of the world  of all people  the benefactor
Vedānta  you teach  of sannyāsīs  the helper
= “You are the teacher of the whole world and the benefactor of all people. You teach Vedānta, and you are a helper of sannyāsīs.

@59
I  a boy sannyāsī  good and bad  not  know
your  shelter  I have taken  guru  making  I regard
= I am a young sannyāsī who does not know good from bad. I have taken shelter of you, and I regard you as My guru.

@60
your  association  for the sake of  My  here  coming
in every  way  you will  Me  protect
= I have come here for your company alone. Please take care of Me in every way.

@61
today  which  happened  My  great  danger
from that  made  you  My  rescue
= Today I fell into great danger, and you rescued Me from it.”

@62
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  alone  You  not  go  for darśana
me  with  You will go  or  my  men  with
= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Do not go alone for darśana. Go with me or with my men.”

@63
the Lord  says  the temple  inside  not  I shall go
of Garuḍa  beside  staying  darśana  I shall have
= The Lord said, “I shall not go inside the temple. I shall stand beside the Garuḍa pillar and take darśana from there.”

@64
to Gopīnātha Ācārya  says  Sārvabhauma
you  Gosāñi  taking  have Him take  darśana
= Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya, “Take Gosāñi for darśana.

@65
my  mother’s sister’s house  a secluded  place
there  lodging  give  make  all  arrangements
= My maternal aunt’s house is a secluded place. Give Him lodging there and see to everything He needs.”

@66
Gopīnātha  the Lord  taking  there  lodging  gave
water pot  and so on  all  arrangements  made
= Gopīnātha took the Lord there and gave Him lodging, and he arranged everything for Him—a water pot and all the rest.

@67
the next  day  Gopīnātha  of the Lord  to the place  going
of the rising from bed  darśana  had Him take  taking (Him)
= The next day Gopīnātha went to the Lord and took Him to see Jagannātha rise from His bed.

@68
Mukunda Datta  taking  came  Sārvabhauma’s  to the place
Sārvabhauma  something  to him  said  in words
= Then he came with Mukunda Datta to Sārvabhauma, and Sārvabhauma said to him:

@69
by nature  humble  the sannyāsī  to see  beautiful
my  great love  increases  Him  for
= “This sannyāsī is humble by nature and beautiful to see, and my affection for Him keeps growing.

@70
in which  sampradāya  sannyāsa  has He made  acceptance
what  name  His  to hear  there is  desire
= In which sampradāya has He taken sannyāsa? And what is His name? I would like to hear.”

@71
Gopīnātha  says  His  name  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa-caitanya
guru  His  Keśava Bhāratī  greatly  blessed
= Gopīnātha said, “His name is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and His guru is the greatly blessed Keśava Bhāratī.”

@72
Sārvabhauma  says  His  name  most excellent
the Bhāratī sampradāya  this  is  middling
= Sārvabhauma said, “His name is excellent, but the Bhāratī sampradāya is only of middling rank.”

@73
Gopīnātha  says  His  there is not  regard for externals
therefore  of a great  sampradāya  there is not  need
= Gopīnātha said, “He has no regard for externals, so He has no need of a higher sampradāya.”

@74
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  His  full  youth
how  of sannyāsa  the duty  will be  kept
= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “He is in the full bloom of youth. How will He keep the vows of sannyāsa?

@75
constantly  to Him  Vedānta  I shall teach
of renunciation  of non-duality  on the path  entering  I shall cause
= I shall teach Him Vedānta constantly and lead Him onto the path of renunciation and non-dualism.

@76
He says  if  again  the sannyāsa cloth  giving
initiation  I shall perform  into the higher  sampradāya  bringing
= If He agrees, I shall give Him the sannyāsa cloth (*yoga-paṭṭa*) once again, perform the rites, and bring Him into a higher sampradāya.”

@77
hearing  Gopīnātha  Mukunda  both  sorrowful  became
Gopīnātha Ācārya  something  to say  began
= Hearing this, Gopīnātha and Mukunda were both saddened, and Gopīnātha Ācārya began to speak.

@78
Bhaṭṭācārya  you  His  not  know  greatness
of Godhood  of the signs  in Him alone  the limit
= “Bhaṭṭācārya, you do not know His greatness. In Him the signs of Godhood reach their utmost limit.

@79
therefore  renowned  He  the Supreme Lord
of the ignorant  in the presence  anything  is not  of the wise  perceptible
= That is why He is renowned as the Supreme Lord. But what the wise perceive lies beyond the reach of the ignorant.”

@80
the disciples  say  the Lord  you say  by what  proof
the Ācārya  says  the opinion of the wise  of the Lord  on the signs
= The disciples said, “By what proof do you call Him the Lord?” The Ācārya said, “By the verdict of the wise on the signs of the Lord.”

@81
a disciple  says  of the Lord  the truth  we establish  by inference
the Ācārya  says  by inference  is not  knowledge of the Lord
= A disciple said, “We establish the truth of the Lord by inference.” The Ācārya replied, “The Lord is not known by inference.

@82
inference  proof  is not  of the Lord  of the truth  in knowing
mercy  without  the Lord  anyone  not  knows
= Inference is no proof in knowing the truth of the Lord. Without His mercy no one can know the Lord.

@83
of the Lord  of mercy  a particle  there is  indeed  on whom
he  indeed  of the Lord  the truth  to know  is able
= Only one who has received even a particle of the Lord’s mercy can know the truth of the Lord.

@84
yet  Your  O Lord  of lotus feet  of the pair
of the grace  by a trace  favoured  only  indeed
knows  the truth  of Bhagavān’s  greatness
not  and  another  one  even  long  speculating
= “O Lord, only one who has been favoured with even a trace of the grace of Your two lotus feet can know the truth of Your greatness, O Bhagavān. No one else can know it, though he search for a long time.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.29)

@85-86
although  the teacher of the world  you  of scripture  possessing knowledge
on earth  there is not  a scholar  you  equal to
of the Lord  of mercy  a particle  there is not  on you
therefore  of the Lord  the truth  not  you are able  to know
= Although you are the teacher of the world, learned in the scriptures, and there is no scholar on earth equal to you, not a particle of the Lord’s mercy has come to you. That is why you cannot know the truth of the Lord.

@87
your  there is not  fault  in scripture  this  it says
by scholarship and the like  of the Lord  of the truth  knowledge  ever  is not
= It is not your fault. The scriptures say this: the truth of the Lord can never be known by scholarship and the like.”

@88
Sārvabhauma  says  Ācārya  speak  carefully
on you  the Lord’s mercy  for this  what  proof
= Sārvabhauma said, “Ācārya, speak with care. What proof is there that the Lord’s mercy is upon you?”

@89
the Ācārya  says  in regard to the Reality  there is  knowledge of the Reality
of the Reality  knowledge of the truth  comes  by mercy  the proof
= The Ācārya said, “Knowledge of the Reality concerns the Reality Itself, and the truth of the Reality is known only by Its mercy—that is the proof.

@90
His  in the body  all  of the Lord  the signs
great  absorption in love  you  have got  sight
= All the signs of the Lord are present in His body, and you yourself have seen His great absorption in love.

@91
still  indeed  knowledge of the Lord  not  is  yours
of the Lord  māyā  this  saying  usage
= Even so, you do not know Him as the Lord. This is what is called the Lord’s māyā.

@92
seeing  not  sees  Him  the averse  person
hearing  smiling  Sārvabhauma  spoke  words
= A person turned away from God sees Him and yet does not see Him.” Hearing this, Sārvabhauma smiled and said:

@93
friendly  discussion  consideration  we make  not  do  anger
by the vision of scripture  I speak  at all  not  take  offence
= “We are only having a friendly discussion; do not be angry. I speak from the standpoint of scripture, so take no offence.

@94
a great  devotee  is  Caitanya Gosāñi
in this  age of Kali  Viṣṇu’s  avatāra  there is not
= Caitanya Gosāñi is a great devotee, but in this age of Kali there is no avatāra of Viṣṇu.

@95
therefore  Triyuga  making  they call  Viṣṇu’s name
in the Kali-yuga  avatāra  there is not  the knowledge of scripture
= That is why Viṣṇu is called Triyuga, ‘He who appears in three ages’. The scriptures teach that there is no avatāra in the Kali-yuga.”

@96
hearing  the Ācārya  says  sorrowful  being  in mind
learned in scripture  being  you  make  pride
= Hearing this, the Ācārya grew sad at heart and said, “You are learned in the scriptures, and you are proud of it.

@97
the Bhāgavata  the Bhārata  the two  of the scriptures  the foremost
of those  two books  to the words  there is not  attention
= The *Bhāgavata* and the *Mahābhārata* are the foremost of all scriptures, yet you pay no attention to the words of these two books.

@98
those  two  say  in Kali  a direct avatāra
you  say  in Kali  there is not  of Viṣṇu  appearance
= Both of them speak of a direct avatāra in the age of Kali, and yet you say that Viṣṇu does not appear in Kali.

@99
in the age of Kali  a līlā-avatāra  not  makes  Bhagavān
therefore  Triyuga  making  they call  His  name
= It is because in the age of Kali Bhagavān does not appear as a *līlā-avatāra* that He is called Triyuga.

@100
in every age  makes  Kṛṣṇa  a yuga-avatāra
devoted to logic  heart  your  there is not  discernment
= But in every age Kṛṣṇa appears as the *yuga-avatāra*. Your heart is given over to logic, and so you have no discernment.

@101
there were  colours  three  indeed  of Him
accepting  in each age  bodies
white  red  likewise  yellow
now  blackness  has attained
= “Your son, who accepts a body in every age, has appeared in three colours — white, red and yellow; now He has become dark (*kṛṣṇa*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.13)

@102
thus  in Dvāpara  O lord of the earth
they praise  the Lord of the universe
of various  tantras  by the rules
in Kali  also  how  hear
= “Thus, O king, do people praise the Lord of the universe in the Dvāpara age. Now hear how they worship Him in the age of Kali as well, by the rules of various scriptures (*tantras*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.31)

@103
Kṛṣṇa  describing (or: with the syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’)  by lustre  not dark
with limbs,  sub-limbs,  weapons  and associates
