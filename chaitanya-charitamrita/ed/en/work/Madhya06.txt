@title The Deliverance of Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya

@1
I bow down  to that  Gauracandra  who
by false arguments  whose heart was hardened
Sārvabhauma  the Lord of all
of devotion  the great abode  made
= I bow down to Gauracandra, the Lord of all, who made Sārvabhauma, whose heart had grown hard with false arguments, into a great abode of devotion.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in ecstasy  went  the Lord  to Jagannātha’s temple
Jagannātha  seeing  in love  He became  restless
= Absorbed in ecstasy, the Lord went to the temple of Jagannātha, and when He saw Jagannātha He was overwhelmed with love.

@4
Jagannātha  to embrace  He went  running
in the temple  He fell  with love  overwhelmed  being
= He ran forward to embrace Jagannātha, but, overwhelmed with love, He fell down in the temple.

@5
by providence  Sārvabhauma  Him  sees
the temple guard  from striking  he  made  prevention
= By providence Sārvabhauma saw Him there, and he stopped the temple guard from striking Him.

@6
the Lord’s  beauty  and  of love  the transformations
seeing  Sārvabhauma  became  amazed  boundlessly
= Seeing the Lord’s beauty and the transformations of love in His body, Sārvabhauma was amazed beyond measure.

@7
for a long time  consciousness  is not  of the offering  the time  came
Sārvabhauma  in his mind  then  a means  considered
= For a long time the Lord did not regain consciousness, and the time came to offer food to Jagannātha. Then Sārvabhauma considered what to do.

@8
by disciples  and temple guards  had (Him)  carried
to his house  bringing  in a pure  place  he kept (Him)  lying
= With the help of his disciples and the temple guards he had Him carried to his house and laid Him down in a pure place.

@9
breathing in and out  there is not  of the abdomen  movement
seeing  anxious  became  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind
= There was no breath and no movement of the abdomen; seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious.

@10
fine  cotton  bringing  at the tip of the nostrils  he held
slightly  moves  the cotton  seeing  composure  came
= He brought a fine wisp of cotton and held it before the Lord’s nostrils, and when he saw the cotton stir slightly, he grew calmer.

@11
sitting  the Bhaṭṭācārya  in his mind  makes  reflection
this  of great love for Kṛṣṇa  the sāttvika  transformation
= Sitting there, the Bhaṭṭācārya reflected: “This is a *sāttvika* transformation of great love for Kṛṣṇa.

@12
sūddīpta  sāttvika  this  the name  which  pralaya
in eternally  perfect  devotees  that  sūddīpta  bhāva  occurs
= This swoon, called *pralaya*, is a *sūddīpta sāttvika*, the ecstatic symptom at its most blazing; such *sūddīpta bhāva* appears only in eternally perfect devotees.

@13
adhirūḍha-mahābhāva  in whom  his  this  transformation
of a human being  in the body  I see  very  astonishing
= This transformation belongs to one who has *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva*. That I should see it in a human body is astonishing indeed!”

@14
thus  thinking  the Bhaṭṭācārya  remains  sitting
Nityānanda and the others  at the Lion Gate  arrived  going
= While the Bhaṭṭācārya sat there thinking this, Nityānanda and the others arrived at the Lion Gate.

@15
there  hearing  people  speak  among themselves  the talk
such a  sannyāsī  coming  seeing  Jagannātha
= There they heard people talking among themselves: “Such a sannyāsī came here and saw Jagannātha.

@16
unconscious  He became  consciousness  not  is  in the body
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house
= He fell unconscious, and no consciousness returned to His body. Sārvabhauma has taken Him to his own house.”

@17
hearing  all  understood  this  Mahāprabhu’s  doing
at that time  came  there  Gopīnātha Ācārya
= Hearing this, they all understood that it was Mahāprabhu’s doing. Just then Gopīnātha Ācārya came there.

@18
of Nadīyā  a resident  of Viśārada  the son-in-law
Mahāprabhu’s  devotee  he  of the Lord  the truth  knowing
= He was a resident of Nadīyā and the son-in-law of Viśārada, a devotee of Mahāprabhu who knew the truth about the Lord.

@19
with Mukunda  previously  there is  acquaintance
Mukunda  seeing  his  became  surprise
= He had known Mukunda before, and he was surprised to see him there.

@20
Mukunda  him  seeing  made  obeisance
he  embracing (him)  asks  of the Lord  the news
= On seeing him, Mukunda bowed down, and Gopīnātha embraced him and asked for news of the Lord.

@21
Mukunda  says  of the Lord  here  has been  the coming
we all  have come  Mahāprabhu  with
= Mukunda said, “The Lord has come here, and we have all come with Mahāprabhu.”

@22
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Ācārya  made  obeisance
all  meeting  he asks  of the Lord  news  again  and again
= The Ācārya bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi and, meeting them all, asked again and again for news of the Lord.

@23
Mukunda  says  Mahāprabhu  sannyāsa  having taken
to Nīlācala  came  with (Him)  us all  taking
= Mukunda said, “After taking sannyāsa, Mahāprabhu came to Nīlācala and brought us all with Him.

@24
us all  leaving  ahead  He went  for darśana
we all  behind  came  of Him  in search
= He left us and went on ahead for darśana, and we have followed in search of Him.

@25
among themselves  of the people  from the mouths  what  talk  we heard
in Sārvabhauma’s house  the Lord  inference  we made
= From what we heard people saying among themselves, we have gathered that the Lord is in Sārvabhauma’s house.

@26
of the Lord  at the sight  the Lord  in love  unconscious
Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house
= At the sight of Jagannātha the Lord fell unconscious in love, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.

@27
with you  for meeting  when  my  was  mind
by providence  at that  moment  I got  your  sight
= Just as I was wishing to meet you, by providence I saw you at that very moment.

@28
come  all  let us go  Sārvabhauma’s  to the house
the Lord  seeing  afterwards  we shall have  of the Lord  darśana
= Come, let us all go to Sārvabhauma’s house. After seeing the Lord, we shall have darśana of Jagannātha.”

@29
this  hearing  Gopīnātha  all  taking
to Sārvabhauma’s house  went  delighted  being
= Hearing this, Gopīnātha joyfully took them all to Sārvabhauma’s house.

@30
to Sārvabhauma’s place  going  the Lord  he saw
the Lord  seeing  of the Ācārya  sorrow and joy  arose
= When he came to Sārvabhauma’s house and saw the Lord, the Ācārya felt both sorrow and joy.

@31
Sārvabhauma  informing  all  he took  inside
to Nityānanda Gosāñi  he  made  obeisance
= He informed Sārvabhauma and brought them all inside, and Sārvabhauma bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi.

@32
all  with  as befitting  he made  meeting
the Lord  seeing  of all  became  joyful  the minds
= He greeted each of them as was fitting, and all rejoiced at the sight of the Lord.

@33
Sārvabhauma  sent  all  darśana  to have
Candaneśvara  his own son  he gave  of all  along with
= Sārvabhauma sent them all for darśana and gave them his own son, Candaneśvara, to go with them.

@34
Jagannātha  seeing  of all  there was  joy
in ecstasy  absorbed  became  Prabhu  Nityānanda
= Seeing Jagannātha, everyone was filled with joy, and Nityānanda Prabhu was overcome with ecstasy.

@35
all  together  holding  Him  steady  made
of the Lord  the servant  garland-prasāda  bringing  gave
= Together they held Him and steadied Him, and a servant of Jagannātha brought garlands and prasāda and gave them to them.

@36
prasāda  receiving  all  became  joyful  in mind
once again  came  all  Mahāprabhu’s  to the place
= Receiving the prasāda, they all rejoiced, and they came back to Mahāprabhu.

@37
loudly  making  perform  all  of the name  saṁkīrtana
in the third  watch  came  of the Lord  consciousness
= They all chanted the holy name loudly together, and in the third watch of the day the Lord regained consciousness.

@38
a roar  making  He rises  Hari  Hari  saying
in joy  Sārvabhauma  His  took  the dust of the feet
= With a roar He rose up, crying, “Hari! Hari!”, and Sārvabhauma joyfully took the dust of His feet.

@39
Sārvabhauma  says  quickly  perform  the midday duties
I  alms  shall give  today  mahā-prasāda
= Sārvabhauma said, “Quickly finish Your midday duties. Today I shall offer You mahā-prasāda as alms.”

@40
in the sea  bath  having taken  the Lord  quickly  came
the feet  washing  the Lord  on a seat  sat
= The Lord bathed in the sea and quickly returned. He washed His feet and sat down on His seat.
