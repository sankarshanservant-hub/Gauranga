@title Śrī Caitanya’s Pilgrimage to the Holy Places

@1
of various  doctrines  by the crocodiles  seized
of the South  the people  elephants
with the disc of mercy  freeing  them
Gaura  made  He  Vaiṣṇavas
= The people of the South were like elephants seized by the crocodiles of various doctrines. Gaura freed them with the disc of His mercy and made them Vaiṣṇavas.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
to the south  the journey  of the Lord  most  extraordinary
thousands  upon thousands  of holy places  He made  visit
= The Lord’s journey to the south was most extraordinary: He visited thousands upon thousands of holy places.

@4
those  all  holy places  touching  great holy places  He made
under that  pretext  of that  land  the people  He delivered
= By touching all those holy places He made them great holy places, and under that pretext He delivered the people of that land.

@5
of those  holy places  the order  to arrange  not  I am able
right and left  to the holy places  the journey  was  back and forth
= I cannot set those holy places in order, for His journey to them went right and left, back and forth.

@6
therefore  the names  only  I make  enumeration
to describe  not  I am able  their  proper  sequence
= Therefore I simply list their names; I cannot describe them in their proper sequence.

@7-8
as before  on the road  going  whoever  received  sight
to whichever  village  He went  of that  village  all  the people
all  Vaiṣṇavas  become  say  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Hari”
other  villages  deliver  them  Vaiṣṇavas  making
= As before, whoever saw Him on the road and all the people of every village He came to became Vaiṣṇavas and chanted “Kṛṣṇa” and “Hari.” They in turn delivered other villages, making their people Vaiṣṇavas.

@9
of the south  of the land  the people  of many  kinds
some  jñānīs  some  karmīs  pāṣaṇḍīs  countless
= The people of the South were of many kinds: some were jñānīs, some were karmīs, and there were countless pāṣaṇḍīs.

@10
those  all  people  of the Lord  of the sight  by the power
their  own  doctrines  giving up  became  Vaiṣṇavas
= By the power of seeing the Lord, all those people gave up their own doctrines and became Vaiṣṇavas.

@11
the Vaiṣṇavas  among  of Rāma  worshipers  all
some  Tattvavādīs  some  were  Śrī Vaiṣṇavas
= Among the Vaiṣṇavas, all were worshipers of Rāma: some were Tattvavādīs, and some were Śrī Vaiṣṇavas.

@12
those  all  Vaiṣṇavas  of Mahāprabhu  upon seeing
of Kṛṣṇa  worshipers  became  take  the name of Kṛṣṇa
= Upon seeing Mahāprabhu, all those Vaiṣṇavas became worshipers of Kṛṣṇa and took up the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@13
Rāma  descendant of Raghu  Rāma  descendant of Raghu  Rāma  descendant of Raghu  save  me
Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  Kṛṣṇa  Keśava  protect  me
= “Rāma! Rāghava! Rāma! Rāghava! Rāma! Rāghava! Save me! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Kṛṣṇa! Keśava! Protect me!”

@14
this  verse  on the way  reciting  He made  the journey
at Gautamī-gaṅgā  arriving  He took  a bath in the Gaṅgā
= Reciting this verse along the way, the Lord journeyed on. He came to Gautamī-gaṅgā and bathed in the Gaṅgā there.

@15
to Mallikārjuna-tīrtha  going  Maheśa  He saw
there  all  the people  the name of Kṛṣṇa  He made take
= He went to Mallikārjuna-tīrtha and saw Maheśa (Śiva). There He made all the people take the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@16
the servant of Rāma  Mahādeva  He made  sight
to Ahobala-nṛsiṁha  He made  going
= He had darśana of Mahādeva, the servant of Rāma, and then went to Ahobala-nṛsiṁha.

@17
Nṛsiṁha  seeing  to Him  He made  obeisances and prayers
to Siddhavaṭa  He went  where  the Deity  of Sītā’s Lord
= Seeing Nṛsiṁha, He offered Him obeisances and prayers. Then He went to Siddhavaṭa, where the Deity of Sītā’s Lord is.

@18
Raghunātha  seeing  He made  obeisances  prayers
there  a  brāhmaṇa  the Lord  made  invitation
= Seeing Raghunātha, He offered obeisances and prayers. There a brāhmaṇa invited the Lord to take His meal.

@19
that  brāhmaṇa  the name of Rāma  constantly  takes
“Rāma”  “Rāma”  except  other  words  not  he speaks
= That brāhmaṇa chanted the name of Rāma constantly. Apart from “Rāma, Rāma,” he spoke no other word.

@20
that  day  in his  house  staying  alms  accepting
to him  mercy  showing  onward  went  Gaurahari
= Gaurahari stayed in his house that day and accepted His meal there. Then, after blessing him, He went on.

@21
at Skanda-kṣetra  He made  of Skanda  sight
to Trimaṭha  He came  there  seeing  Trivikrama
= At Skanda-kṣetra He had darśana of Skanda. Then He came to Trimaṭha and saw Trivikrama there.

@22
again  to Siddhavaṭa  He came  of that  brāhmaṇa  to the house
that  brāhmaṇa  the name of Kṛṣṇa  takes  constantly
= He came back to Siddhavaṭa, to the house of that brāhmaṇa, and now the brāhmaṇa was constantly chanting the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@23
meal  taking  Mahāprabhu  to him  question  put
tell  brāhmaṇa  this  your  what  state  has come
= After taking His meal, Mahāprabhu asked him, “Tell Me, brāhmaṇa, what state has come over you?

@24
before  you  constantly  used to take  the name of Rāma
now  why  constantly  you take  the name of Kṛṣṇa
= Before, you chanted the name of Rāma constantly. Why do you now constantly chant the name of Kṛṣṇa?”

@25
the brāhmaṇa  says  this  of You  of the sight  by the power
You  seeing  went away  my  from birth  nature
= The brāhmaṇa said, “This is by the power of seeing You. When I saw You, the nature I had from birth left me.

@26
from childhood  of the name of Rāma  the taking  mine
You  seeing  the name of Kṛṣṇa  came  once
= From childhood I have chanted the name of Rāma. When I saw You, the name of Kṛṣṇa came to me once.

@27
from that  time  the name of Kṛṣṇa  on my tongue  sat
the name of Kṛṣṇa  appears  the name of Rāma  far  went away
= From that time the name of Kṛṣṇa has sat on my tongue. The name of Kṛṣṇa now manifests there, and the name of Rāma has gone far away.

@28
from childhood  time  my  nature  one  is
of the name  of the glory  of the scriptures  I make  collecting
= From childhood I have had one habit: I collect what the scriptures say about the glory of the holy name.

@29
take delight  the yogīs  in the Infinite
in true bliss  in the spiritual self
thus  by the word “Rāma”  this
the Supreme  Brahman  is called
= “The yogīs take delight in the Infinite, in true bliss, in the spiritual Self. Therefore this Supreme Brahman is called by the word ‘Rāma.’ ” (Padma Purāṇa, Śata-nāma-stotra)

@30
“kṛṣ”  being  denoting  the sound
“ṇa”  and  bliss  denoting
of the two  the union  the Supreme  Brahman
Kṛṣṇa  thus  is called
= “The sound ‘kṛṣ’ denotes being, and ‘ṇa’ denotes bliss. Their union, the Supreme Brahman, is called ‘Kṛṣṇa.’ ” (Mahābhārata, Udyoga-parva, 71.4)

@31
of the Supreme  Brahman  the two names  equal  turned out
again  further  in the scriptures  some  distinction  I found
= Both names belong to the Supreme Brahman and so are equal. But in the scriptures I found a further distinction.

@32
“Rāma  Rāma  Rāma” (chanting)
I take delight  in Rāma  enchanting
to the thousand names  equal
the name of Rāma  O fair-faced one
= “O fair-faced one, chanting ‘Rāma, Rāma, Rāma,’ I take delight in the enchanting Rāma. The name of Rāma is equal to the thousand names (of Viṣṇu).” (Padma Purāṇa, Bṛhad-viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma-stotra, 72.335)

@33
of the thousand names  holy
by three repetitions  indeed  what  result
by one repetition  indeed  of Kṛṣṇa
the name alone  that  gives
= “The result obtained by reciting the thousand holy names (of Viṣṇu) three times is given by a single utterance of one name of Kṛṣṇa.” (Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa)

@34
in this  statement  of the name of Kṛṣṇa  the glory  boundless
still  to take  I could not  hear  the reason  for that
= This statement shows the boundless glory of the name of Kṛṣṇa. Still I could not chant it; hear the reason.

@35
my chosen Deity  Rāma  His  in the name  happiness  I find
happiness  finding  the name of Rāma  night and day  I sing
= Rāma is my chosen Deity, and I find happiness in His name. Finding that happiness, I sang the name of Rāma night and day.

@36
of You  at the sight  when  the name of Kṛṣṇa  came
its  glory  then  in my heart  took hold
= When the name of Kṛṣṇa came to me at the sight of You, its glory took hold of my heart.

@37
that  Kṛṣṇa  You  this  directly  I have ascertained
so  saying  the brāhmaṇa  of the Lord  at the feet  fell
= I have clearly understood that You are that very Kṛṣṇa.” Saying this, the brāhmaṇa fell at the Lord’s feet.

@38
to him  mercy  showing  the Lord  departed  the next  day
to Vṛddhakāśī  coming  He made  of Śiva  sight
= After showing him mercy, the Lord set out the next day. He came to Vṛddhakāśī and had darśana of Śiva.

@39
from there  setting out  further  He went  to a  village
of brāhmaṇas  a community  there  He made  rest
= Going on from there, He came to a village where there was a community of brāhmaṇas, and He rested there.

@40
of the Lord  by the influence  people  came  for sight
hundreds of thousands and millions  of people  come  not  can be  counted
= Drawn by the Lord’s influence, people came to see Him. Hundreds of thousands and millions came; they could not be counted.

@41
of the Gosāñi  the beauty  seeing  in it  absorption in love
all  “Kṛṣṇa”  say  Vaiṣṇava  became  the whole  land
= Seeing the Lord’s beauty and His absorption in love, everyone said “Kṛṣṇa,” and the whole land became Vaiṣṇava.

@42
logicians  Mīmāṁsakas  all  Māyāvādīs
Sāṅkhya  Pātañjala  smṛti  Purāṇa  Āgama
= There were logicians and Mīmāṁsakas, all the Māyāvādīs, and followers of Sāṅkhya and Pātañjala (yoga), of the smṛtis, the Purāṇas and the Āgamas.

@43
of their own  scriptures  in insisting  all  fierce
all  doctrines  finding fault  the Lord  makes  to pieces  to pieces
= All of them were fierce in insisting on their own scriptures, but the Lord exposed the faults of every doctrine and broke them to pieces.

@44
everywhere  establishes  the Lord  the Vaiṣṇava conclusion
of the Lord  the conclusion  anyone  not  is able  to refute
= Everywhere the Lord established the Vaiṣṇava siddhānta, and no one could refute His conclusions.

@45
defeated  defeated  into the Lord’s doctrine  they make  entry
in this way  Vaiṣṇava  He made  the southern  land
= Defeated again and again, they entered into the Lord’s doctrine. In this way He made the South Vaiṣṇava.

@46
the pāṣaṇḍīs  came  all  of His learning  hearing
pride  showing  they came  with them  disciples  bringing
= Hearing of His learning, all the pāṣaṇḍīs came. Full of pride, they came bringing their disciples with them.

@47
a Buddhist ācārya  great  scholar  in his own  nine  principles
of the Lord  before  insistence  showing  began  to speak
= A Buddhist ācārya, a great scholar in his own nine principles, began to argue them insistently before the Lord.

@48
although  not to be spoken with  the Buddhists  improper  to look at
still  spoke  the Lord  the pride  to break
= Although the Buddhists are not to be spoken with, and it is improper even to look at them, the Lord spoke in order to break their pride.

@49
on logic  based  Buddhist  scriptures  “nine  principles”
by logic itself  refuted  the Lord  not  they can  establish
= The Buddhist scriptures, with their “nine principles,” rest on logic, so the Lord refuted them by logic alone, and they could not uphold them.

@50
the Buddhist ācārya  “nine  propositions”  all  raised
with firm  reasoning and logic  the Lord  to pieces  to pieces  broke
= The Buddhist ācārya put forward all nine propositions, and the Lord broke them to pieces with firm reasoning and logic.

@51
the philosophers  scholars  all  suffered  defeat
people  laughter  raise  the Buddhists  felt  shame and fear
= All the scholar-philosophers were defeated. People laughed, and the Buddhists felt shame and fear.

@52
the Lord  a Vaiṣṇava  knowing  the Buddhists  home  went
all  Buddhists  gathering  then  an evil plot  made
= Knowing the Lord to be a Vaiṣṇava, the Buddhists went home. Then all of them gathered and made an evil plot.

@53
impure  food  a  on a plate  filling
of the Lord  before  they brought  “mahā-prasāda”  calling it
= They filled a plate with impure food and brought it before the Lord, calling it “mahā-prasāda.”

@54
at that time  huge  a  bird  came
in its beak  taking  the plate together with  the food  carrying off  went
= Just then a huge bird came, took the plate with the food in its beak and carried it off.

@55
of the Buddhists  upon  the food  falls  impure  being
of the Buddhist ācārya  on the head  the plate  fell  with a clang
= The impure food fell upon the Buddhists, and the plate fell with a clang on the head of the Buddhist ācārya.

@56
edgewise  fell  the plate  the head  it cut
unconscious  becoming  the ācārya  to the ground  fell
= The plate fell edgewise and cut his head, and the ācārya fell to the ground unconscious.

@57
cries of lamentation  raising  weep  all  the disciples
all  coming  at the Lord’s feet  took  shelter
= All the disciples cried out in lamentation and wept. They came and took shelter at the Lord’s feet:

@58
You  indeed  the Lord  Himself  forgive  the offense
bring back to life  our  guru  show  mercy
= “You are the Lord Himself. Forgive our offense. Bring our guru back to life — be merciful!”

@59
the Lord  says  all  say  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Hari”
of the guru  into the ear  say  the name of Kṛṣṇa  loudly  doing
= The Lord said, “All of you say ‘Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, Hari!’ Say the name of Kṛṣṇa loudly into your guru’s ear.

@60
your  guru  then  will regain  consciousness
all  the Buddhists  together  perform  the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa
= Then your guru will regain consciousness.” All the Buddhists together performed the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name.

@61
of the guru  into the ear  say  all  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Rāma”  “Hari”
consciousness  regaining  the ācārya  says  “Hari”  “Hari”
= All of them said “Kṛṣṇa! Rāma! Hari!” into their guru’s ear. Regaining consciousness, the ācārya said, “Hari! Hari!”

@62
“Kṛṣṇa”  saying  the ācārya  to the Lord  shows  humility
seeing (this)  all  the people  were  amazed
= Saying “Kṛṣṇa,” the ācārya humbly prayed to the Lord. Seeing this, all the people were amazed.

@63
in this way  sport  making  of Śacī  the son
disappearance  made  no one  not  gets  sight (of Him)
= Having played in this way, the son of Śacī disappeared, and no one could see Him.

@64
Mahāprabhu  setting out  came  to Tripati-Trimalla
four-armed  Deity  seeing  to Veṅkaṭādri  goes
= Mahāprabhu went on and came to Tripati and Trimalla. Seeing the four-armed Deity, He went on to Veṅkaṭādri.

@65
to Tripati  coming  He made  of Śrī Rāma  sight
Raghunātha  before  He made  obeisances  prayers
= Coming to Tripati, He had darśana of Śrī Rāma, and before Raghunātha He offered obeisances and prayers.

@66
by His own influence  all the people  making  amazed
to Pānā-nṛsiṁha  came  the Lord  all-merciful
= Amazing all the people by His influence, the all-merciful Lord came to Pānā-nṛsiṁha.

@67
to Nṛsiṁha  obeisances and prayers  absorbed in love  He made
of the Lord  by the influence  the people  amazed  became
= Absorbed in love, He offered obeisances and prayers to Nṛsiṁha, and the people were astonished at the Lord’s influence.

@68
to Śiva-kāñcī  coming  He made  of Śiva  sight
by His influence  Vaiṣṇavas  He made  all  the Śaivas
= Coming to Śiva-kāñcī, He had darśana of Śiva, and by His influence He made all the Śaivas Vaiṣṇavas.

@69
to Viṣṇu-kāñcī  coming  He saw  Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa
obeisance  making  He made  many  prayers
= Coming to Viṣṇu-kāñcī, He saw Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa, bowed down and offered many prayers.

@70
absorbed in love  dancing and singing  much  He did
two days  staying  the people  devotees of Kṛṣṇa  He made
= Absorbed in love, He danced and sang a great deal. He stayed there two days and made the people devotees of Kṛṣṇa.

@71
Trimalaya  seeing  He went  to Trikālahastī  the place
Mahādeva  seeing  to him  He made  obeisances
= After seeing Trimalaya, He went to Trikālahastī. There He saw Mahādeva and bowed down to him.

@72
Pakṣī-tīrtha  seeing  He made  of Śiva  sight
to Vṛddhakola-tīrtha  then  He made  going
= At Pakṣī-tīrtha He had darśana of Śiva, and then He went to Vṛddhakola-tīrtha.

@73
Śveta-varāha  seeing  to Him  bowing down
to Pītāmbara Śiva’s  place  went  Gaurahari
= There He saw Śveta-varāha and bowed down to Him. Then Gaurahari went to the place of Śiva Pītāmbara.

@74
Śiyālī  Bhairavī  of the goddess  making  sight
of the Kāverī  to the bank  came  of Śacī  the son
= After seeing the goddess Śiyālī-bhairavī, the son of Śacī came to the bank of the Kāverī.

@75
at Go-samāja  Śiva  seeing  He came  to Vedāvana
Mahādeva  seeing  to him  He made  homage
= At Go-samāja He saw Śiva, and then He came to Vedāvana. There He saw Mahādeva and paid him homage.

@76
Amṛta-liṅga Śiva  seeing  homage  He made
in all  the temples of Śiva  the Śaivas  Vaiṣṇavas  became
= Seeing Śiva Amṛta-liṅga, He paid him homage. In all the temples of Śiva, the Śaivas became Vaiṣṇavas.

@77
to Devasthāna  coming  He made  of Viṣṇu  sight
the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas  with  there  discussions  constantly
= Coming to Devasthāna, He had darśana of Viṣṇu, and there He was constantly in discussion with the Śrī Vaiṣṇavas.

@78
at Kumbhakarṇa-kapāla  seeing  the lake
at Śiva-kṣetra  Śiva  sees  Gaurāṅga-sundara
= At Kumbhakarṇa-kapāla the beautiful Gaurāṅga saw the lake, and at Śiva-kṣetra He saw Śiva.

@79
at Pāpanāśana  of Viṣṇu  He made  sight
to Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra  then  He made  going
= At Pāpanāśana He had darśana of Viṣṇu, and then He went to Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra.

@80
in the Kāverī  bath  taking  seeing  Raṅganātha
prayers and obeisances  making  considered (Himself)  fulfilled
= He bathed in the Kāverī and saw Raṅganātha. Offering prayers and obeisances, He felt His purpose fulfilled.

@81
absorbed in love  He did  much  singing  dancing
seeing (this)  amazed  became  of all  the people  the minds
= Absorbed in love, He sang and danced a great deal, and all the people who saw it were amazed.

@82
a Śrī Vaiṣṇava  one  “Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa”  by name
the Lord  invitation  made  showing  respect
= A Śrī Vaiṣṇava named Veṅkaṭa Bhaṭṭa respectfully invited the Lord.

@83
to his own house  bringing  he made  washing of the feet
that  water  taking  he made  with his whole family  drinking
= He brought the Lord to his house and washed His feet, and he and his whole family drank that water.

@84
meal  having given  something  he made  request
Cāturmāsya  coming  O Lord  has become  near
= After serving Him a meal, he made a request: “O Lord, Cāturmāsya has come and is at hand.

@85
during Cāturmāsya  mercy  showing  stay  in my  house
about Kṛṣṇa  talks  speaking  mercifully  deliver  me
= Be merciful and stay in my house for Cāturmāsya. Speak about Kṛṣṇa and mercifully deliver me.”

@86
in his  house  stayed  the Lord  in talks about Kṛṣṇa  with relish
with the Bhaṭṭa  spent  happily  four  months
= The Lord stayed in his house, relishing talks about Kṛṣṇa, and happily spent the four months with the Bhaṭṭa.

@87
in the Kāverī  bath  taking  of Śrī Raṅga  sight
every day  absorbed in love  He performs  dancing
= Every day He bathed in the Kāverī, had darśana of Śrī Raṅga and danced, absorbed in love.

@88
beauty and the rest  absorption in love  seeing  all the people
to see  they come  they see  is destroyed  sorrow and grief
= Everyone who saw His beauty and the rest and His absorption in love came to see Him; they saw Him, and their sorrow and grief were destroyed.

@89
hundreds  of thousands  of people  came  from various  lands
all  the name of Kṛṣṇa  say  the Lord  on seeing
= Hundreds of thousands of people came from various lands, and on seeing the Lord all of them chanted the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@90
the name of Kṛṣṇa  except  no one  not  says  anything else
all  devotees of Kṛṣṇa  became  the people  amazed
= No one spoke anything but the name of Kṛṣṇa. Everyone became a devotee of Kṛṣṇa, and the people were amazed.

@91
in Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra  dwell  all  the Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇas
one  day each  all  made  invitation
= All the Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇas living in Śrī Raṅga-kṣetra invited Him, each for one day.

@92
one  day each  the Cāturmāsya  complete  became
some  brāhmaṇas  meal  to give  not  got (the chance)
= One day each, and the Cāturmāsya came to an end; some brāhmaṇas never got the chance to serve Him a meal.

@93
in that  holy place  lives  a  Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇa
to the temple  coming  he makes  of the Gītā  recitation
= In that holy place lived a Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇa who would come to the temple and recite the Gītā.

@94
the eighteen chapters  he reads  by bliss  overwhelmed
incorrectly  he reads  people  make  mockery
= Overwhelmed with bliss, he read all eighteen chapters; he read them incorrectly, and people mocked him.

@95
