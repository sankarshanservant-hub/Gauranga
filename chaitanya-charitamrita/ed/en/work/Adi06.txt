@title The Divine Majesty of Śrī Advaita Ācārya

@1
I worship  Him  Śrī Advaita-
-ācārya  wonderful  whose deeds
by whose  mercy  an ignorant person  even
His  true nature  may describe
= I worship Śrī Advaita Ācārya, whose deeds are wonderful. By His mercy even an ignorant person can describe His true nature.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host
= All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in five  verses  I have told  of Śrī Nityānanda  the truth
in two verses  I tell  of Advaita Ācārya  the greatness
= In five verses I have told the truth about Śrī Nityānanda. Now in two verses I tell the greatness of Advaita Ācārya.

@4
Mahā-Viṣṇu  the creator of the world
through māyā  who  creates  this (world)
His  avatāra  indeed  this
Advaita Ācārya  the Lord
= Mahā-Viṣṇu, the creator of the world, creates this universe through His energy, māyā. The Lord Advaita Ācārya is His very avatāra.

@5
(called) Advaita  being non-different from Hari
(called) Ācārya  for proclaiming bhakti
the avatāra as a devotee  the Lord  Him
Advaita Ācārya  I take shelter of
= He is called Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, and Ācārya because He proclaims devotion (*bhakti*). I take shelter of that Lord, Advaita Ācārya, who descended as a devotee.

@6
Advaita  Ācārya  Gosāñi  directly  the Lord
whose  greatness  not is  of the jīva  within the range
= Advaita Ācārya is the Lord Himself. His greatness lies beyond the grasp of the living being (*jīva*).

@7
Mahā-Viṣṇu  creation  performs  of the world  and other  works
His  avatāra  direct  Advaita  Ācārya
= Mahā-Viṣṇu carries out the creation of the world and all the other works of the cosmos. Advaita Ācārya is His direct avatāra.

@8
which  Puruṣa  creation  maintenance  performs  through māyā
unlimited  universes  creation  performs  in play
= That Puruṣa creates and maintains the world through māyā. He creates unlimited universes in play (*līlā*).

@9
by His will  unlimited  forms  He makes  manifest
in one  each  form  He makes  into a universe  entrance
= By His will He manifests unlimited forms, and in a separate form He enters each universe.

@10
of that  Puruṣa  a portion  Advaita  there is not  any  difference
a body  particular  His  there is not  separation
= Advaita is a portion of that Puruṣa, and there is no difference at all between Them. He is a particular body of the Puruṣa, never separate from Him.

@11
help  He gives  Him  taking up  the pradhāna
millions of  universes  He makes  by His will  the fashioning
= He assists the Puruṣa by taking up the *pradhāna*, and by His will He fashions millions of universes.

@12
of the world  the good  Advaita  of auspicious  qualities  the abode
auspicious  conduct  always  Auspicious  whose  name
= Advaita is the good of the whole world, the abode of all auspicious qualities. His conduct is always auspicious, and auspiciousness itself is His name.

@13
millions of  portions  millions of  energies  millions of  avatāras
so many  taking  creates  the Puruṣa  the whole  world
= Taking millions of portions, millions of energies and millions of avatāras, the Puruṣa creates the whole world.

@14
māyā  just as  two  aspects  efficient (cause)  material (cause)
māyā  the efficient  cause  the material (cause)  the pradhāna
= Māyā has two aspects, the efficient cause (*nimitta*) and the material cause (*upādāna*). Māyā is the efficient cause, and the *pradhāna* is the material cause.

@15
the Puruṣa  the Lord  in the same way  two forms  assuming
the universe  creation  performs  the efficient  the material (causes)  taking
= In the same way the Lord, the Puruṣa, assumes two forms and creates the universe through the efficient and the material cause.

@16
Himself  the Puruṣa  of the world  the efficient  cause
of Advaita  in the form  the material (cause)  becomes  Nārāyaṇa
= The Puruṣa Himself is the efficient cause of the world, and in the form of Advaita, Nārāyaṇa becomes the material cause.

@17
as the efficient aspect  casts  He  on māyā  a glance
as the material (cause)  Advaita  performs  of the universes  creation
= As the efficient aspect, He glances at māyā; as the material cause, Advaita creates the universes.

@18
although  the Sāṅkhya  holds  the pradhāna  the cause
from inert matter  ever  not is  of the world  creation
= Although the Sāṅkhya philosophy holds that the *pradhāna* is the cause, the world can never come into being from inert matter.

@19
His own  creative power  the Lord  infuses  into the pradhāna
of the Lord  by the power  then  there is  indeed  creation
= The Lord infuses His own creative power into the *pradhāna*, and only then, by the Lord's power, does creation take place.

@20
Advaita  Ācārya  of millions of universes  the creator
and  in one  each  form  of a universe  the maintainer
= Advaita Ācārya is the creator of millions of universes, and in a separate form He maintains each universe.

@21
of that  Nārāyaṇa  the principal  limb  Advaita
aṅga  by the word  portion (aṁśa)  meaning  says  the Bhāgavata
= Advaita is the principal limb (*aṅga*) of that Nārāyaṇa. The *Bhāgavatam* uses the word *aṅga* in the sense of *aṁśa*, a portion.

@22
Nārāyaṇa  You  not  indeed  of all  embodied beings
the Self  You are  O Lord  of all  worlds  the witness
Nārāyaṇa  Your portion  of Nara  born  the water  as resting place
that  and also  true  not  Your  indeed  māyā
= “O Lord, are You not Nārāyaṇa? You are the Self of all embodied beings and the witness of all the worlds. The one called Nārāyaṇa because His resting place is the water born of Nara is Your portion. And that portion too is real; it is not Your māyā.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.14)

@23
of the Lord  limb  portion  full of consciousness and bliss
with māyā  connection  there is not  this  verse  says
= This verse says that the Lord's limbs and portions are full of consciousness and bliss and have no connection with māyā.

@24
aṁśa  not  saying  why  call (you)  Him  aṅga
than aṁśa  aṅga (is better)  since  it is  more intimate
= Why call Him an *aṅga*, a limb, rather than an *aṁśa*, a portion? Because the word *aṅga* expresses greater intimacy than *aṁśa*.

@25
of Mahā-Viṣṇu  a portion  Advaita  of good qualities  the abode
from the Lord  non-different  therefore  Advaita  the full  name
= Advaita, the abode of all good qualities, is a portion of Mahā-Viṣṇu. He is non-different from the Lord, and so His full name is Advaita, “non-dual.”

@26
before  just as  He did  of all  the universe  creation
having descended  He did  now  of bhakti  the establishing
= Just as He once created the entire universe, so now He has descended and established the path of devotion.

@27
the souls  He delivered  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  making  a gift
in the Gītā and the Bhāgavata  He made  of bhakti  the explanation
= He delivered the souls by giving them devotion to Kṛṣṇa, and He explained the *Bhagavad-gītā* and the *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* in the light of bhakti.

@28
bhakti  instruction  except  His  there is not  work
therefore  the name  became  Advaita  Ācārya
= He has no work other than teaching bhakti; therefore His name became Advaita Ācārya.

@29
of the Vaiṣṇavas  the guru  He  of the world  the most honored
of the two names  by the union  became  Advaita  Ācārya
= He is the guru of the Vaiṣṇavas and the most honored person in the world. The union of the two names became “Advaita Ācārya.”

@30
of the Lotus-eyed  He  since  limb  portion
Kamalākṣa  calling (Him)  He bears  the name  as a crest jewel
= Because He is a limb and portion of the lotus-eyed Lord, He is called Kamalākṣa, “the lotus-eyed,” and He wears that name like a crest jewel.

@31
of the Lord  likeness of form  attain  the associates
four-armed  in yellow garments  like  Nārāyaṇa
= The Lord's associates attain a form like His own (*sārūpya*): like Nārāyaṇa, they are four-armed and dressed in yellow.

@32
Advaita  Ācārya  of the Lord  the foremost portion
His  truth  name  qualities  all  wonderful
= Advaita Ācārya is the foremost portion of the Lord. His truth, His name and His qualities are all wonderful.

@33
by whose  tulasī and water  by whose  roaring call
His own associates  together with  Caitanya's  descent
= By His offerings of tulasī and water and by His roaring calls, Śrī Caitanya descended together with His associates.

@34
through whom  made  the Lord  of kīrtana  the spreading
through whom  made  the Lord  of the world  the deliverance
= Through Him the Lord spread the kīrtana, and through Him the Lord delivered the world.

@35
of the Ācārya  Gosāñi  of the qualities  the greatness  boundless
the jīva  a worm  where  will find  of it  the limit
= The qualities and greatness of Advaita Ācārya are boundless. How could a soul, a mere worm, ever find their limit?

@36
the Ācārya  Gosāñi  of Caitanya  the principal  limb
another  one  limb  His  the Lord  Nityānanda
= Advaita Ācārya is the principal limb of Śrī Caitanya, and Lord Nityānanda is His other limb.

@37
of the Lord  the sub-limbs  Śrīvāsa and other  devotees
hands  face  eyes  limbs  the disc and other  weapons  like
= The Lord's sub-limbs (*upāṅgas*) are Śrīvāsa and the other devotees. They are like His hands, face, eyes and other limbs, and like His disc and other weapons.

@38
with all these  taking  of Caitanya Prabhu  the pastimes
with all these  taking  He makes  of what He desired  the spreading
= With all of them Śrī Caitanya performs His pastimes, and with all of them He spreads what He desires to give.

@39
of Mādhavendra Purī  He  a disciple  in this  awareness
the Ācārya Gosāñi  the Lord  as guru  making  regards
= Knowing that Advaita is a disciple of Mādhavendra Purī, the Lord regards Him as His guru.

@40
in His humanlike  pastimes  of dharma  the bounds  keeping
with praise  with devotion  He does  His  feet  worship
= In His humanlike pastimes He keeps the bounds of dharma, and with praise and devotion He worships Advaita's feet.

@41
Caitanya Gosāñi  the Ācārya  holds  as Master  in awareness
Himself  He holds  His  servant  in self-conception
= Advaita Ācārya, however, regards Śrī Caitanya as His Master (*prabhu*) and thinks of Himself as His servant (*dāsa*).

@42
in that  self-conception's  joy  Himself  He forgets
Kṛṣṇa's servants  become  to the souls  instruction  He gives
= In the joy of that self-conception He forgets Himself and teaches all souls: “Become servants of Kṛṣṇa!”

@43
Kṛṣṇa's  servant  in the self-conception  which  of bliss  the ocean
ten million  of Brahman  the happiness  not is  of it  one  drop
= Such is the ocean of bliss in thinking oneself Kṛṣṇa's servant that the happiness of Brahman, multiplied ten million times, does not equal one drop of it.

@44
I  indeed  Caitanya's servant  and  Nityānanda
of a servant's mood  equal to  there is not  elsewhere  bliss
= [Advaita says:] “I am a servant of Caitanya, and so is Nityānanda.” Nowhere is there bliss equal to the mood of a servant.

@45
supreme  beloved  Lakṣmī  on (His) chest  dwells
she  of service  the happiness  begs  making  entreaty
= Lakṣmī, the Lord's most beloved, dwells on His chest, yet she too humbly begs for the happiness of serving Him.

@46
in the mood of service  blissful  the associates
Brahmā  Śiva  Nārada and  Śuka  Sanātana
= All the Lord's associates are blissful in the mood of service: Brahmā, Śiva, Nārada, Śuka, Sanātana Kumāra and others.

@47
Nityānanda  the Avadhūta  among all  foremost
in Caitanya's  love of service  became  mad
= Nityānanda Avadhūta, foremost among them all, became mad with love in the service of Śrī Caitanya.

@48-49
Śrīvāsa  Haridāsa  Rāmadāsa  Gadādhara
Murāri  Mukunda  Candraśekhara  Vakreśvara
all these  learned  men  of the highest  greatness
Caitanya's  service  all  makes  mad
= Śrīvāsa, Haridāsa, Rāmadāsa, Gadādhara, Murāri, Mukunda, Candraśekhara and Vakreśvara are learned men of the highest greatness, yet service to Śrī Caitanya makes them all mad with love.

@50
thus  they sing  dance  make  loud laughter
the people  they teach  become  Caitanya's  servants
= So they sing, dance and laugh aloud, and they teach everyone: “Become servants of Śrī Caitanya!”

@51
Caitanya Gosāñi  Me  holds  as guru
even so  My  there is  servant  self-conception
= [Advaita thinks:] “Śrī Caitanya regards Me as His guru, yet I still feel Myself to be His servant.”

@52
of love for Kṛṣṇa  this  one  unprecedented  power
elders  equals  juniors  it makes have  the servant's  mood
= This is one unprecedented power of love for Kṛṣṇa: it gives the mood of a servant to elders, equals and juniors alike.

@53
of this  the proof  hear  of the scriptures  the explanation
of the great  the experience  in which  firm  proof
= Hear the proof of this in the explanation of the scriptures, where the experience of great souls stands as firm proof.

@54-55
of others  what  talk  in Vraja  Nanda  the great soul
his  equal  elder  of Kṛṣṇa  other  anyone  is not
in pure  parental love  of God  awareness  not  his
him  even  love  makes  service  imitate
= What to speak of others? In Vraja no one is so much Kṛṣṇa's elder (*guru*) as the noble Nanda. In his pure parental love (*vātsalya*) he has no awareness that Kṛṣṇa is God, yet love moves even him to take on the mood of a servant.

@56
he  love  devotion  begs  of Kṛṣṇa  at the feet
his  holy  mouth's  words  of that  the proof
= He too prays for love and devotion at Kṛṣṇa's feet, and the words from his own lips prove it.

@57-58
hear  Uddhava  truly  Kṛṣṇa  my  son
He  God  so  if  your  mind  takes it
still  on Him  may remain  my  mind's  movements
on your  God  Kṛṣṇa  may be  my  devotion
= “Listen, Uddhava: truly, Kṛṣṇa is my son. If you think He is God, so be it. Still, may every movement of my mind rest on Him, and may my heart be fixed on Kṛṣṇa, your God.”

@59
of the mind  the movements  our  may be
Kṛṣṇa's  lotus feet  taking shelter of
the words  uttering  of the names
the body  in bowing to Him and so on
= “May our minds take shelter of Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet, may our words speak His names, and may our bodies bow down to Him and serve Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.47.66)

@60
by karma  being made to wander
wherever  by the Lord's will
by auspicious deeds  by gifts
love  our  for Kṛṣṇa  the Lord (may be)
= “Wherever we may wander by the Lord's will, driven by our karma, may our good deeds and our gifts bring us love for Kṛṣṇa, the Lord.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.47.67)
