@61
O kurarī  you lament  you  bereft of sleep  not  you lie down
sleeps  in the world  at night  the Lord  His awareness hidden
we  like  O friend  can it be  deeply  pierced  in heart
of the lotus-eyed one  by the smile  generous  by the playful glance
= (The queens of Dvārakā:) “O kurarī bird, you lament without sleep and do not even lie down, while at night the Lord sleeps in this world, His awareness hidden. Can it be, O friend, that like us you have had your heart deeply pierced by the generous, playful glance and smile of the lotus-eyed one?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.90.15)

@89-91
by devotion  washed away  whose faults

= “Their faults have been washed away by devotion, and their hearts are clear and bright. They are attached to the Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam and delight in the company of *rasikas*. The wealth of happiness in devotion to Govinda’s feet has become their very life, and they perform only those practices that are intimate to *prema*. In the hearts of such devotees *rati* shines — bliss by its very nature, illumined by two kinds of impressions (*saṁskāra*), past and present — and it is brought to the state of *rasa*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.7–9)

@111-112
