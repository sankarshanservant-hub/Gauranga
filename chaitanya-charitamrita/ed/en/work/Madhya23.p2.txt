# Verses 64 (= Ādi 4.83) and 121 (= Madhya 7.155): word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev.

@61
O kurarī  you lament  you  bereft of sleep  not  you lie down
sleeps  in the world  at night  the Lord  His awareness hidden
we  like  O friend  can it be  deeply  pierced  in heart
of the lotus-eyed one  by the smile  generous  by the playful glance
= (The queens of Dvārakā:) “O kurarī bird, you lament without sleep and do not even lie down, while at night the Lord sleeps in this world, His awareness hidden. Can it be, O friend, that like us you have had your heart deeply pierced by the generous, playful glance and smile of the lotus-eyed one?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.90.15)

@62
of the king of Vraja  the son  Kṛṣṇa  of heroes  the crest-jewel
of heroines  the crest-jewel  Rādhā  Ṭhākurāṇī
= Kṛṣṇa, the son of the king of Vraja, is the crest-jewel of heroes (*nāyaka*), and Śrī Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī is the crest-jewel of heroines (*nāyikā*).

@63
among heroes  the crown jewel
Kṛṣṇa  indeed  Bhagavān  Himself
in whom  eternally  all
shine  great  qualities
= “Kṛṣṇa, Bhagavān Himself, is the crown jewel of all heroes, for in Him all great qualities shine eternally.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.17)

@64
the goddess  full of Kṛṣṇa  is declared
Rādhikā  the supreme  deity
all  Lakṣmīs  containing  all
splendour  the enchantress  supreme
= “The goddess (*devī*) Rādhikā is declared to be full of Kṛṣṇa (*kṛṣṇa-mayī*). She is the supreme deity; all the Lakṣmīs are within Her; She is the whole of splendour (*sarva-kānti*) and the supreme enchantress.” (Bṛhad-gautamīya-tantra)

@65
endless  of Kṛṣṇa  qualities  sixty-four  principal
each  one  quality  hearing  are soothed  of the devotees  the ears
= Kṛṣṇa’s qualities are endless, and sixty-four of them are principal. The devotees’ ears are soothed on hearing of each one of them.

@66
this  hero  with beautiful limbs
with all  auspicious  marks  endowed
delightful to see  with splendour  endowed
strong  with youth  endowed
= “This hero (1) has beautiful limbs, (2) bears all auspicious marks, and is (3) delightful to see, (4) radiant, (5) strong and (6) ever youthful.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.23)

@67
of many  wondrous  languages  the knower
truthful in speech  speaking pleasantly
eloquent  deeply learned
intelligent  quick-witted
= “He (7) knows many wondrous languages, (8) is truthful in speech and (9) speaks pleasantly; He is (10) eloquent, (11) deeply learned, (12) intelligent and (13) brilliantly quick-witted.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.24)

@68
artful  clever  expert
grateful  firm in His vows
place  time  the right person  knowing
with scripture as His eye  pure  self-controlled
= “He is (14) artful, (15) clever, (16) expert, (17) grateful and (18) firm in His vows; (19) He knows what befits place, time and person; (20) He sees through the eye of scripture; He is (21) pure and (22) self-controlled.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.25)

@69
steadfast  restrained  forgiving
grave  composed  equal to all
generous  righteous  heroic
compassionate  to the worthy  giving honour
= “He is (23) steadfast, (24) restrained, (25) forgiving, (26) grave, (27) composed, (28) equal to all, (29) generous, (30) righteous, (31) heroic and (32) compassionate, and (33) He honours those worthy of honour.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.26)

@70
courteous  humble  bashful
of the surrendered  protector
happy  of His devotees  the friend  by love
subdued  to all  good  doing
= “He is (34) courteous, (35) humble and (36) bashful; (37) He protects the surrendered; He is (38) ever happy, (39) the friend of His devotees and (40) subdued by love; and (41) He does good to all.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.27)

@71
mighty  famous  loved
by the people  of the saintly  the shelter
of women  the hearts  captivating
worshipped by all  opulent
= “He is (42) mighty, (43) famous, (44) loved by the people, (45) the shelter of the saintly, (46) the captivator of women’s hearts, (47) worshipped by all and (48) supremely opulent.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.28)

@72
the greatest  the Lord  and  thus
qualities  His  are proclaimed
oceans  like  fifty
unfathomable  of Hari  these
= “He is (49) the greatest of all and (50) the Lord. These fifty qualities of Hari have been proclaimed; like oceans, they are unfathomable.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.29)

@73
in living beings  these  dwelling  although
in tiny drops  at times
in complete fullness  shine
there  only  in the Supreme Person
= “Although these qualities are at times found in living beings, there they are only tiny drops; in complete fullness they shine only in the Supreme Person (*Puruṣottama*).” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.30)

@74
now  five qualities  which  exist
partially  in Śiva and others
always  in His own nature  established
all-knowing  ever  new
= “Now there are five qualities that exist in part in Śiva and others: (51) He is always established in His own nature (*svarūpa*), (52) He knows all, and (53) He is ever new.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.37)

@75
of being, consciousness and bliss  whose limbs are concentrated
of consciousness and bliss  a dense form
under His control  all  perfections  are
by all  perfections  served
= “(54) His limbs are concentrated being, consciousness and bliss — His form is dense consciousness and bliss; and (55) all perfections are under His control and serve Him.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.38)

@76
now  are described  qualities  five
which  in the Lord of Lakṣmī and others  are present
inconceivable  great  power
millions of  universes  in His body
= “Now five qualities are described that are present in the Lord of Lakṣmī (Nārāyaṇa) and His other forms: (56) He has inconceivable great power, and (57) His body holds millions of universes.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.39)

@77
of all the avatāras  the seed
to slain enemies  liberation  granting
the self-satisfied  attracting  thus
these  in Kṛṣṇa  truly  wondrous
= “(58) He is the seed of all the *avatāras*; (59) He grants liberation to the enemies He slays; (60) He attracts even the self-satisfied (*ātmārāma*). In Kṛṣṇa these qualities are truly wondrous.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.40)

@78
all  wonders  astonishing
of pastimes  of waves  the ocean
incomparable  sweet  with love
adorned  of His beloved ones  the circle
= “(61) He is an ocean whose waves are pastimes that astonish even all wonders; (62) the circle of His beloved ones is adorned with incomparable, sweet love.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.41)

@79
of the three worlds  the minds  attracting
of the flute  with the sweet  call
unequalled and unsurpassed  of form  by the beauty
astonishing  the moving and the unmoving
= “(63) The sweet call of His flute attracts the minds of the three worlds; (64) the beauty of His form, which none can equal or surpass, astonishes all beings, moving and unmoving.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.42)

@80
pastimes  by love  the abundance of beloveds
the sweetness  of flute and of form
thus  unique  is declared  of Govinda  the set of four
thus  qualities  of four  kinds  sixty-four  are described
= “His pastimes, the abundance of beloved ones bound to Him by love, and the sweetness of His flute and of His form — these four are declared to be Govinda’s unique qualities. Thus sixty-four qualities of four kinds have been described.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.43–44)
