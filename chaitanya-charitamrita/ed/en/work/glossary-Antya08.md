# English names and terms — CC Antya 8 (stage A; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 8»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 8») are used as given there: Rāmacandra Purī (Gosāñi);
Mādhavendra Purī (Purīpāda, Purī Gosāñi); Īśvara Purī; Paramānanda Purī; Govinda, Kāśīśvara; dry renunciation
(*śuṣka-vairāgya*); earlier / later injunction; *abhojyānna vipra*; *ek-cauṭhi* (quarter-pot); cane molasses; mercy / punishment
(*anugraha* / *nigraha*); departure from the world (*niryāṇa*, *antarddhāna* — never “death”); stubborn attachment (*nirbandha*);
special feeling (*bhāva-viśeṣa*). Verses 3 (line 1), 32 and 101 — word for word as in the revised Ādi 8.4, Madhya 4.197,
Antya 2.172 (`ed/en-rev/`).
