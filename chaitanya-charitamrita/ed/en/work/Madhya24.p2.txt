# Verses already revised elsewhere — word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev: 127 (= Madhya 8.65), 128 (= Madhya 10.178), 132 (= Madhya 20.119), 133 (= Madhya 20.121), 135 (= Madhya 22.22), 137 (= Madhya 22.28), 164 (= Madhya 20.106), 167 and 186 (= Ādi 1.49), 177 (= Ādi 4.208), 189 (= Madhya 22.128), 191 (= Madhya 22.36), 193 (= Madhya 22.40), 202 (= Madhya 8.275), 213 (= Madhya 22.42); 126 and 136 follow Madhya 22.30.

@118
those who seek liberation  terrible in form
having given up  the lords of beings  therefore
of Nārāyaṇa  the partial forms  peaceful
worship  indeed  free from envy
= “Those who seek liberation give up the lords of beings, whose forms are terrible, though they bear them no envy; peaceful, they worship the partial forms (*kalā*) of Nārāyaṇa.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.26)

@119
of those  all  in the company of sādhus  the qualities (of Kṛṣṇa)  reveals
of Kṛṣṇa  worship  makes them do  the desire for liberation  makes them give up
= In the company of sādhus, Kṛṣṇa’s qualities are revealed to all of them; that company leads them to worship Kṛṣṇa and makes them give up the desire for liberation.

@120
O  great soul  by many faults  corrupted
though  by one  shines  this  worldly existence  quality
called the company of saints  bringing happiness
made  today  our  by which  weak  desire for liberation
= “O great soul, this worldly existence is corrupted by many faults, yet it shines with one quality, called the company of saints, which brings happiness: by it our desire for liberation has today been made weak.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 27)

@121
of Nārada  in the company  Śaunaka and the others  the sages
the desire for liberation  giving up  performed  of Kṛṣṇa  worship
= In the company of Nārada, Śaunaka and the other sages gave up the desire for liberation and worshipped Kṛṣṇa.

@122
of Kṛṣṇa  by the sight  some  of Kṛṣṇa  by the mercy
the desire for liberation  giving up  (drawn) by His qualities  worship  His  feet
= Some on seeing Kṛṣṇa, and some by Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, give up the desire for liberation and, drawn by His qualities, worship His feet.

@123
this  of bliss  concentrated form
the Supreme Self  in Vṛṣṇi-pattana (Dvārakā)  when He appeared
by being an ātmārāma  my  in vain
has passed  alas  long  time
= “Now that Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Self, whose form is concentrated bliss, has appeared here in Vṛṣṇi-pattana, I see — alas! — that for a long time my days passed in vain in being an *ātmārāma*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Śānta-bhakti-laharī, 34)

@124
those liberated while living  many  of them  two  kinds  I know
by devotion  liberated while living  by knowledge  liberated while living  I hold
= There are many who are liberated while living (*jīvan-mukta*), and I know them to be of two kinds: those liberated while living through devotion and those liberated while living through knowledge.

@125
by devotion  liberated while living  attracted by the qualities  becoming  Kṛṣṇa  worships
by dry knowledge  liberated while living  through offences  down  sinks
= One liberated while living through devotion is attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and worships Him; but one “liberated while living” through dry knowledge sinks down because of his offences.

@126
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there
fall  down  having neglected Your feet
= “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)

@127
one who has realised Brahman  serene in self
not  grieves  not  desires
equal  to all  beings
for Me  devotion  attains  supreme
= “One who has realised Brahman and is serene within neither grieves nor desires. Equal to all beings, he attains supreme devotion to Me.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.54)

@128
of nondualism  the path  by travellers  worshipped
on the throne of self-bliss  having received initiation
by a cunning  certain  we  by force
maidservants  made  of the cowherd wives  by the lover
= “I was worshipped by those who walk the path of non-dualism, and I had received initiation on the throne of the bliss of the self; yet a certain rogue, a lover of the cowherd maidens, has forcibly made me a maidservant.” (Śrī Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta)

@129
of bhakti  by the strength  one who has attained his true nature  a divine  body  receives
by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities attracted  becoming  worships  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
= By the strength of bhakti, one who has attained his true nature (*prāpta-svarūpa*) receives a divine body. Attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, he worships Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

@130
nirodha  of it  following  lying down to rest
of the self  together  with its potencies
liberation  having given up  the other form
in one’s own true nature  being established
= “*Nirodha* is the lying down to rest of the self, together with its potencies, following (the mystic slumber of the Lord). *Mukti* is to give up the other form and be established in one’s own true nature (*svarūpa*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.10.6)

@131
from Kṛṣṇa  of turning away  the fault  from māyā  comes  is
turned toward Kṛṣṇa  liberation  from  from māyā  freedom  comes
= The fault of turning away from Kṛṣṇa comes from māyā, and freedom from māyā comes from liberation that is turned toward Kṛṣṇa.[^131]

@132
fear  from absorption in what is other  arises
from the Lord  for one turned away  misconception  forgetfulness
by His māyā  therefore  the wise one  should worship  Him
with devotion  exclusive  the Lord  seeing the guru as his deity and his self
= “For one who has turned away from the Lord, fear arises from absorption in what is other than Him; by His māyā come forgetfulness of the Lord and a false idea of oneself. Therefore the wise, seeing the guru as his worshipful deity and his very self, should worship the Lord with exclusive devotion.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.37)

@133
divine  indeed  this  made of the guṇas
My  māyā  hard to overcome
to Me  alone  those who  surrender
māyā  this  cross over  they
= “This divine māyā of Mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me alone cross beyond this māyā.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.14)

@134
bhakti  without  liberation  there is not  by bhakti  liberation  comes
by bhakti  liberation  having attained  certainly  Kṛṣṇa  worships
= Without bhakti there is no liberation; liberation comes through bhakti. And one who has attained liberation through bhakti surely worships Kṛṣṇa.

@135
the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord
toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge
for them  this  toil  only  remains
nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks
= “O almighty Lord, bhakti to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)

@136
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there
fall  down  having neglected Your feet
= “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)

@137
who  of them  the Puruṣa  directly
their own source  the Lord
not  worship  disregard
from their position  fallen  they fall  down
= “Those among them who do not worship the Puruṣa, their own source and Lord, but disregard Him, fall from their position and sink into the depths.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.3)

@138
the liberated  even  in pastime  a form
having assumed  Bhagavān  worship
= “Even the liberated, assuming a form in the Lord’s pastimes (*līlā*), worship Bhagavān.” (the author of the *Sarvajña-bhāṣya*)

@139
these  six  ātmārāmas  Kṛṣṇa  worship
separately  separately  by the word ca  here  of api  the meaning  expresses
= These six kinds of *ātmārāmas* worship Kṛṣṇa. The word *ca*, joined to each of them separately, here expresses the sense of *api* (“also”).

@140
the ātmārāmas  and  also  render  to Kṛṣṇa  causeless  devotion
sages  being  thus (means)  in contemplation of Kṛṣṇa  attachment
= “*Ātmārāmāś ca api*”: even the *ātmārāmas* render causeless devotion to Kṛṣṇa. “*Munayaḥ santaḥ*” — “being sages” — means that they become attached to contemplating Kṛṣṇa.

@141
nirgranthāḥ  free from ignorance  some  free from rules
where  whichever  fits  of that  meaning  dependent on
= “*Nirgranthāḥ*” means free from ignorance (*avidyā*), and for some it means free from rules (*vidhi*). Wherever a meaning fits, the word takes that meaning.

@142
to the word ca  I give  if  of mutual connection (itaretara)  the meaning
another  one  meaning  it expresses  most  cogent
= If I give the word *ca* the sense of *itaretara* (mutual connection), it yields yet another most cogent meaning.

@143
ātmārāmas  and  ātmārāmas  and  making  times  six
five  ātmārāmas  of the six  with the word ca  dropped  are
= Repeating “*ātmārāmāś ca ātmārāmāś ca*” six times, five of the six *ātmārāmas* are dropped together with their *ca*.

@144
one  ātmārāmaḥ  word  as the remainder  stays
by one  ātmārāmaḥ  word  six persons  it denotes
= A single word *ātmārāmaḥ* remains, and that one word denotes all six.

@145
of words of the same form  one  remains  in the same case
of words whose meaning is already expressed  non-use
Rāma  and  Rāma  and  Rāma  and  Rāmas  as in
= “Of words of the same form in the same case only one remains (*eka-śeṣa*); words whose meaning has already been expressed are not used: thus ‘*rāmaś ca rāmaś ca rāmaś ca*’ becomes ‘*rāmāḥ*’.” (Viśva-prakāśa)

@146
then  that  word ca  it  conjunction (samuccaya)  expresses
ātmārāmas  and  sages  and  Kṛṣṇa  worship
= The word *ca* that then remains expresses conjunction (*samuccaya*): “the *ātmārāmas* and the sages (*ātmārāmāś ca munayaś ca*)” worship Kṛṣṇa.

@147
of nirgranthā api  this  api  in the sense of possibility
these  seven  meanings  first  I have given  in explanation
= In “*nirgranthā api*,” this *api* has the sense of possibility (*sambhāvanā*). These are the seven meanings that I have explained first.

@148
the worshipper of the Antaryāmī  ātmārāma  is called
of those  ātmārāma  yogīs  two  kinds  there are
= One who worships the Antaryāmī, the Lord within the heart, is also called an *ātmārāma*, and these *ātmārāma* yogīs are of two kinds.

@149
sagarbha  nirgarbha  these  are  two  kinds
each  one  in three  kinds  six  subdivisions
= *Sagarbha* and *nirgarbha* — these are the two kinds. Each of them is of three kinds, and so there are six divisions.

@150
some  of their own body  within  in the space of the heart
a span in size  the Person  dwelling
four-armed  lotus  disc  conch
mace  holding  by concentration  they remember
= “Some, by concentration (*dhāraṇā*), remember the Person who dwells in the space of the heart within their own body: He is a span in size, four-armed, and holds the lotus, the disc, the conch and the mace.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.2.8)

@151
thus  for Hari  Bhagavān  having gained  bhāva
by devotion  his heart melting  his hair standing on end  from joy
of longing  of tears  by the stream  again and again  overwhelmed
that  and  also  of the mind  the hook  gradually  withdraws
= “Having thus gained *bhāva* for Bhagavān Hari, his heart melting with devotion and the hair of his body standing on end with joy, again and again overwhelmed by streams of tears of longing, the yogī gradually withdraws even the hook of his mind (from the object of meditation).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.28.34)

@152
wishing to rise to yoga  risen to yoga  having attained perfection  and
these  three  in divisions  there are  six  kinds
= *Yogārurukṣu* (one who wishes to rise to yoga), *yogārūḍha* (one who has risen to yoga) and *prāpta-siddhi* (one who has attained perfection): by these three divisions there are six kinds.

@153
of the one wishing to ascend  of the sage  to yoga
action  the means  is said to be
of one who has risen to yoga  of him  indeed
tranquillity  the means  is said to be
= “For the sage who wishes to ascend to yoga, action is said to be the means; for that same sage, once he has risen to yoga, tranquillity (*śama*) is said to be the means.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.3)

@154
when  indeed  not  to the objects of the senses
not  to actions  he is attached
of all  intentions  the renouncer
risen to yoga  then  he is called
= “When one is attached neither to the objects of the senses nor to actions, and has renounced all intentions (*saṅkalpa*), he is then called *yogārūḍha*, one who has risen to yoga.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.4)

@155
these  six  yogīs  the company of sādhus and the like  the cause  obtaining
Kṛṣṇa  worship  by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  attracted  becoming
= These six kinds of yogīs, obtaining such causes as the company of sādhus, become attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and worship Kṛṣṇa.

@156
the word ca  of api  the meaning  this too  expresses
muni  nirgrantha  of the words  as before  the meaning  is
= Here too the word *ca* expresses the meaning of *api*, and the words *muni* and *nirgrantha* have the same meanings as before.

@157
urukrame  ahaitukī  where  which  meaning
these  thirteen  meanings  I have told  most  cogent
= The words *urukrame* and *ahaitukī* take whichever meaning fits each case. Thus I have told you thirteen most cogent meanings.

@158
these  all  śāntas  when  worship  Bhagavān
śānta  devotees  considering them  then  I call  their  name
= When all these peaceful ones (*śānta*) worship Bhagavān, I then call them *śānta* devotees.

@159
by the word ātmā  the mind  is meant  in the mind  who  delights
in the company of sādhus  he  worships  Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
= The word *ātmā* also means the mind (*mana*). One who delights in the mind worships Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s feet in the company of sādhus.

@160
the belly (udara)  worship  those who  on the paths of the ṛṣis  of gross vision
from which the channels branch out  the heart  the Āruṇis  subtle (dahara)
from there  rises  O Ananta  Your  abode  the head  supreme
again  here  which  having reached  not  they fall  of death  into the mouth
= “Those of gross vision who follow the paths of the ṛṣis worship (Brahman in) the belly (*udara*); the Āruṇis worship (Brahman, the) subtle (*dahara*), in the heart, from which the channels branch out. From there, O Ananta, (the path) rises to the head, Your supreme abode; having reached it, they do not fall again into the jaws of death in this world.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.18)

@161
these  by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities attracted  great sages  becoming
causeless  devotion  render  nirgranthas  becoming
= Attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, they become great sages and, becoming *nirgrantha*, render causeless devotion.

@162
by the word ātmā  effort  is meant  effort  making
sages  also  Kṛṣṇa  worship  nirgranthas  becoming
= The word *ātmā* also means effort (*yatna*). Making effort, even sages (*munayo ’pi*) become *nirgrantha* and worship Kṛṣṇa.

@163
of that  indeed  for the sake  should strive  the wise
not  is obtained  which  by those who wander  above  below
that  is obtained  like suffering  by other means  happiness
by time  everywhere  of irresistible force
= “The wise should strive for that alone which is not obtained by those who wander through the worlds above and below. Happiness, like suffering, comes of itself everywhere, brought by time, whose course is irresistible.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.5.18)

@164
soon  indeed  every aim
is fulfilled  of those  desired
of true religion  for the understanding
of whom  persistent  the mind
= “Those whose minds are set with perseverance on understanding true religion soon attain every aim they desire.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Pūrva-vibhāga, Sādhana-bhakti-laharī, 47)

@165
by the word ca  in the sense of api  api  in the sense of certainty
eager longing  without  bhakti  does not  give birth  to love
= The word *ca* has the sense of *api*, and *api* the sense of certainty (*avadhāraṇa*): without eager longing (*yatnāgraha*), bhakti does not give birth to love (*prema*).

@notes
[^131]: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *kṛṣṇonmukhī mukti*, “liberation turned toward Kṛṣṇa”; another reading has *kṛṣṇonmukha bhakti … māyā-mukta haya*, “through devotion turned toward Kṛṣṇa one is freed from māyā.” According to the *Anubhāṣya*, freedom from māyā comes through serving Kṛṣṇa, that is, through turning toward Him.
