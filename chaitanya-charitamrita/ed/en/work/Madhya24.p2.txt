@118

worship  indeed  free from envy
= “Those who seek liberation give up the lords of beings, whose forms are terrible, though they bear them no envy; peaceful, they worship the partial forms (*kalā*) of Nārāyaṇa.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.2.26)

@119

of Kṛṣṇa  worship  makes them do  the desire for liberation  makes them give up
= In the company of sādhus, Kṛṣṇa’s qualities are revealed to all of them; that company leads them to worship Kṛṣṇa and makes them give up the desire for liberation.

= “O great soul, this worldly existence is corrupted by many faults, yet it shines with one quality, called the company of saints, which brings happiness: by it our desire for liberation has today been made weak.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 27)

@122

@123
this  of bliss  concentrated form

by being an ātmārāma  my  in vain

= “Now that Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Self, whose form is concentrated bliss, has appeared here in Vṛṣṇi-pattana, I see — alas! — that for a long time my days passed in vain in being an *ātmārāma*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Śānta-bhakti-laharī, 34)

@124
those liberated while living  many  of them  two  kinds  I know
by devotion  liberated while living  by knowledge  liberated while living  I hold
= There are many who are liberated while living (*jīvan-mukta*), and I know them to be of two kinds: those liberated while living through devotion and those liberated while living through knowledge.

@125

= One liberated while living through devotion is attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and worships Him; but one “liberated while living” through dry knowledge sinks down because of his offences.

@131

= The fault of turning away from Kṛṣṇa comes from māyā, and freedom from māyā comes from liberation that is turned toward Kṛṣṇa.[^131]

@132

by His māyā  therefore  the wise one  should worship  Him
with devotion  exclusive  the Lord  seeing the guru as his deity and his self
= “For one who has turned away from the Lord, fear arises from absorption in what is other than Him; by His māyā come forgetfulness of the Lord and a false idea of oneself. Therefore the wise, seeing the guru as his worshipful deity and his very self, should worship the Lord with exclusive devotion.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.37)

@139

@140

sages  being  thus (means)  in contemplation of Kṛṣṇa  attachment
= “*Ātmārāmāś ca api*”: even the *ātmārāmas* render causeless devotion to Kṛṣṇa. “*Munayaḥ santaḥ*” — “being sages” — means that they become attached to contemplating Kṛṣṇa.

@141

= “*Nirgranthāḥ*” means free from ignorance (*avidyā*), and for some it means free from rules (*vidhi*). Wherever a meaning fits, the word takes that meaning.

@151

= “For the sage who wishes to ascend to yoga, action is said to be the means; for that same sage, once he has risen to yoga, tranquillity (*śama*) is said to be the means.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 6.3)

@160

From there, O Ananta, (the path) rises to the head, Your supreme abode; having reached it, they do not fall again into the jaws of death in this world.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.87.18)

@177

@184

= The company of saints, service to Kṛṣṇa, the *Bhāgavata*, the holy Name and living in Vraja — these five are the principal *sādhanas*.

@188

= If a sensible person has even a slight (connection with) one of these five, love for Kṛṣṇa awakens in him.

= “The power of these five limbs is wondrous and hard to grasp. Let alone faith in them: even the slightest connection with them gives birth to *bhāva* in those of good understanding, who are free from offence.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Pūrva-vibhāga, Sādhana-bhakti-laharī, 87)

@190
broad  great  whose  best of all  intelligence
with various  desires  worships  still  attains  of bhakti  perfection
= One whose intelligence is broad, great and best of all may worship with various desires, yet he still attains perfection in bhakti.

@191
without desire  full of all desires  or

with intense  bhakti-yoga

= “Whether he is without desire, full of every desire, or desirous of liberation, a person of broad intelligence should worship the Supreme Person with intense *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.10)

@192
of bhakti  the power  those  desires  making one give up
to Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  devotion  makes one render  by His qualities  attracting
= Such is the power of bhakti: it makes one give up those desires and, attracting one by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, makes one render devotion to His feet.

@193

not indeed  giver of that  which  again  asking  from which
Himself  He grants  to those who worship  though not desiring
covering all desires  His own  feet like tender shoots
= “It is true that when men ask, the Lord grants what they ask for; but He does not give what would make them ask again. To those who worship Him, though they do not desire it, He Himself gives His own feet, tender as new shoots, which put an end to all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.19.26)

@194
by the word ātmā  nature  is meant  in it  who  delights
ātmārāmas  living beings  all  stationary  moving
= The word *ātmā* also means one’s nature (*svabhāva*), and one who delights in it is an *ātmārāma*. Thus all living beings, stationary and moving, are *ātmārāmas*.

@195
of the jīva  the nature  toward Kṛṣṇa  servant  self-conception
in the body  by the notion of self  covered  that  awareness
= The nature of the *jīva* is to know himself as Kṛṣṇa’s servant; that awareness becomes covered when he takes the body to be the self.

@196
by the word ca  eva  api  the word  in the sense of conjunction
ātmārāmas  indeed  being  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  worship
= The word *ca* means *eva* (“indeed”), and the word *api* has the sense of conjunction: being *ātmārāmas* indeed (*ātmārāmā eva*), (all beings) worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

@197
these  jīvas  Sanaka and the others  all  sages
the nirgranthas  the foolish  the low  stationary  moving
= These *jīvas* are all the sages (*munis*), such as Sanaka and the others; and the *nirgranthas* are the foolish, the low, and the stationary and moving beings.

@198
of Vyāsa  of Śuka  of Sanaka and the others  well known  the worship
of the nirgranthas  of the stationary and the rest  hear  the account
= The worship of Vyāsa, Śuka, Sanaka and the others is well known. Now hear the account of the *nirgranthas* — the stationary beings and the rest.

@199
Kṛṣṇa’s  mercy and other  causes  from  nature  awakens
by Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities attracted  becoming  Him  they worship
= Through such causes as Kṛṣṇa’s mercy, their true nature (*svabhāva*) awakens, and, attracted by Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, they worship Him.

@200

= “Today this earth is blessed; blessed are the grasses and shrubs that touch Your feet, the trees and creepers touched by Your fingers, the rivers, mountains, birds and beasts on whom You cast Your merciful glances, and the gopīs held between Your arms — a place for which even Śrī (Lakṣmī) longs.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.15.8)

@201

stillness  of moving beings  thrill  of the trees

= “O friends, as the two Brothers, marked by the ropes and nooses for tying the cows, lead the cows with the cowherd boys from forest to forest, the generous, sweet notes of Their flutes work wonders among embodied beings: those that move become still, and the trees thrill with ecstasy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.19)

@202

= “The forest creepers and trees, rich with flowers and fruits, their branches bowed with the weight and their bodies thrilled with love, poured down streams of honey, as if revealing Viṣṇu within themselves.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.35.9)

@206

@207
