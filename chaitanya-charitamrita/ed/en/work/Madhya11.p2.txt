# CC Madhya 11, stage A, part 2 (verses 122–243); read by build_en.py together with Madhya11.txt

@122
the Lord’s  orders  carry out  you two  careful  being
an order  there is not  still  do (it)  His hints  understanding
= “Both of you must carefully carry out the Lord’s orders. And even where there is no order, understand His wishes from His hints and act on them.”

@123
so  saying  leave  gave  those  two
Sārvabhauma  to see  came  the meeting with the Vaiṣṇavas
= With these words the king dismissed the two of them. Sārvabhauma came to see the meeting with the Vaiṣṇavas.

@124
Gopīnātha Ācārya  Bhaṭṭācārya  Sārvabhauma
both  watch  from afar  the Lord’s meeting with the Vaiṣṇavas
= Gopīnātha Ācārya and Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya both watched from a distance as the Lord met the Vaiṣṇavas.

@125
the Lion Gate  on the right  leaving  all  the Vaiṣṇavas
by the road to Kāśī Miśra’s house  made  their way
= Leaving the Lion Gate on their right, all the Vaiṣṇavas made their way along the road to Kāśī Miśra’s house.

@126
at that time  Mahāprabhu  with His own companions
the Vaiṣṇavas  met  coming  on the road  with much  delight
= At that moment Mahāprabhu came with His own companions and met the Vaiṣṇavas on the road with great delight.

@127
Advaita  made  the Lord’s  feet  worship
the Ācārya  gave  the Lord  a loving embrace
= Advaita bowed at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord embraced the Ācārya with love.

@128
in the bliss of love  became  both  utterly unsteady
the occasion  seeing  the Lord  became  somewhat  composed
= Both were utterly overcome by the bliss of love; then, seeing the occasion, the Lord composed Himself a little.

@129
Śrīvāsa and the others  made  the Lord’s  feet  worship
each one  gave  the Lord  a loving embrace
= Śrīvāsa and the others bowed at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord embraced each of them with love.

@130
one by one  all the devotees  He made  greeting
all  taking  inside  He made  His way
= He greeted all the devotees one by one and took them all inside.

@131
Miśra’s  residence  that  is  a small  place
countless  Vaiṣṇavas  there  were  contained
= Miśra’s residence was a small place, yet the countless Vaiṣṇavas were all accommodated there.

@132
close to Himself  the Lord  all  seated
Himself  with His own hand  to all  garlands and scent  gave
= The Lord seated them all close to Himself, and with His own hand He gave everyone garlands and sandal paste.

@133
the Bhaṭṭācārya  came  then  to Mahāprabhu’s  place
as befitting  he met  all  with
= Then the Bhaṭṭācārya came to where Mahāprabhu was and greeted everyone as befitted each.

@134
to Advaita  says  the Lord  in sweet words
today  I  complete  have become  by Your  coming
= The Lord spoke sweetly to Advaita: “Today, with Your coming, I am complete.”

@135-136
Advaita  says  of the Lord  this  nature  is
although  in Himself  complete  full of all majesty
still  in the company of devotees  there is  joyful delight
with the devotees  He enjoys  eternally  various  pastimes
= Advaita said, “This is the Lord’s nature: although He is complete in Himself and full of all majesty, still He finds joyful delight in the company of His devotees, and with His devotees He eternally enjoys pastimes of every kind.”

@137
Vāsudeva  seeing  the Lord  delighted  being
to him  something  says  on his  body  hand  placing
= The Lord was delighted to see Vāsudeva, and, placing His hand on his body, He said to him:

@138
although  Mukunda  with Me  from childhood
than him  greater  joy  you  to see
= “Though Mukunda has been with Me since childhood, I feel greater joy in seeing you.”

@139
Vāsu  says  Mukunda  obtained  Your  company
Your  feet  he obtained  that is  his rebirth
= Vāsu said, “Mukunda obtained Your company; he obtained Your feet — that was his second birth.

@140
younger  being  Mukunda  now  has become  my  elder
Your  mercy’s recipient  therefore  in all qualities  the best
= Though he is younger, Mukunda has now become my elder. He is the recipient of Your mercy, and so he excels in every quality.”

@141
again  the Lord  says  I  for you
two  books  have brought  the South  from
= The Lord spoke again: “I have brought two books from the South for you.

@142
Svarūpa’s  with  they are  take  them  copying
Vāsudeva  delighted  the books  receiving
= Svarūpa has them; copy them and take them.” Vāsudeva was delighted to receive the books.

@143
each  Vaiṣṇava  all  copying  took
gradually  gradually  the two  books  everywhere  spread
= Every one of the Vaiṣṇavas made a copy for himself, and so, little by little, the two books spread everywhere.

@144
to Śrīvāsa and the others  says  the Lord  showing  great affection
by you  four brothers  I  have been  bought
= With great affection the Lord said to Śrīvāsa and his brothers, “I have been bought by you four brothers.”

@145
Śrīvāsa  says  why  You say  the opposite
at the price of mercy  four  brothers  we are  by You  bought
= Śrīvāsa replied, “Why do You say the opposite? With the price of Your mercy You have bought us four brothers.”

@146
Śaṅkara  seeing  the Lord  says  to Dāmodara
reverential love  My  you  for
= Seeing Śaṅkara, the Lord said to Dāmodara, “My love for you is mixed with reverence,

@147
pure  exclusive love  Śaṅkara  for
therefore  with you  keep  Śaṅkara
= but for Śaṅkara I have pure, unmixed love. So keep Śaṅkara with you.”

@148
Dāmodara  says  Śaṅkara  younger  me  than
now  my  elder  brother  by Your  mercy
= Dāmodara said, “Śaṅkara is younger than I, but now, by Your mercy, he has become my elder brother.”

@149
to Śivānanda  says  the Lord  your  for Me
deep  attachment  there is  I know  before  from
= The Lord said to Śivānanda, “I have known from long before of your deep attachment to Me.”

@150
hearing  Śivānanda Sena  overwhelmed by love  being
like a rod  being  falls  a verse  reciting
= Hearing this, Śivānanda Sena was overwhelmed with love; he fell flat like a rod before the Lord and recited this verse:

@151
sinking  O Infinite One  in the ocean of worldly existence  within
for long  by me  the shore  like  You are  found
by You  too  is found  O Lord  now
the best  vessel  this  of mercy
= “O Infinite One, for so long I was sinking in the ocean of worldly existence, and now I have found You, like the shore. And You too, O Lord, have now found in me the most fitting vessel for Your mercy.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka 8.42; Stotra-ratna 26)
