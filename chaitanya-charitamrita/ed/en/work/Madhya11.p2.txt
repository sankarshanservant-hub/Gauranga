@233
at the picnic on the riverbank  as  Kṛṣṇa  in the middle place
on all four sides  friends  say  at me  He looks
= It was just as when Kṛṣṇa sat in the middle at the picnic on the riverbank, and each of the friends around Him said, “He is looking at me!”

@234
dance  doing  whoever  comes  near
Mahāprabhu  gives  him  a firm  embrace
= Whoever came near Him while dancing, Mahāprabhu embraced him tightly.

@235
the great dance  the great love  the great saṅkīrtana
seeing  in the rapture of love  float  the people of Nīlācala
= Seeing the great dancing, the great love and the great *saṅkīrtana*, the people of Nīlācala were swept away in the rapture of love.

@236
Gajapati  the king  hearing  the glory of the kīrtana
the palace  climbing  watches  with his own people
= When King Gajapati heard of the glory of the *kīrtana*, he climbed onto the roof of his palace and watched with his attendants.

@237
the kīrtana  seeing  in the king  arose  wonder
the Lord  to meet  eagerness  grew  boundless
= Seeing the *kīrtana*, the king was struck with wonder, and his eagerness to meet the Lord grew boundless.

@238
at the end of the kīrtana  the Lord  seeing  the flower offering
all  Vaiṣṇavas  taking  the Lord  came  to His lodging  going
= When the *kīrtana* was over, the Lord watched the offering of flowers to Jagannātha and then returned to His lodging with all the Vaiṣṇavas.

@239
the paḍichā  bringing  gave  prasāda  in abundance
to all  distributing  it  gave  the Lord
= The *paḍichā* brought *prasāda* in abundance, and the Lord shared it out among everyone.

@240
to all  leave  He gave  to  go to sleep
in this way  pastimes  performs  of Śacī  the son
= Then He sent everyone off to sleep. In this way the son of Śacī performs His pastimes.

@241
as long as  stayed  all  with Mahāprabhu
every day  in this way  they do  in the joy of kīrtana
= For as long as they all stayed with Mahāprabhu, they spent every day like this in the joy of *kīrtana*.

@242
this  indeed  I have told  of the Lord  the kīrtana pastimes
whoever  this  hears  becomes  Caitanya’s  servant
= Thus I have described the Lord’s pastimes of *kīrtana*. Whoever hears of them becomes a servant of Caitanya.

@243
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
