= At that moment Mahāprabhu came with His own companions and met the Vaiṣṇavas on the road with great delight.

= Both were utterly overcome by the bliss of love; then, seeing the occasion, the Lord composed Himself a little.

@130

= He greeted all the devotees one by one and took them all inside.

@131

@132

= The Lord seated them all close to Himself, and with His own hand He gave everyone garlands and sandal paste.

@133

= Then the Bhaṭṭācārya came to where Mahāprabhu was and greeted everyone as befitted each.

@134

= The Lord spoke sweetly to Advaita: “Today, with Your coming, I am complete.”

@135-136

= Advaita said, “This is the Lord’s nature: although He is complete in Himself and full of all majesty, still He finds joyful delight in the company of His devotees, and with His devotees He eternally enjoys pastimes of every kind.”

@137

@138

= “Though Mukunda has been with Me since childhood, I feel greater joy in seeing you.”

@139

= Vāsu said, “Mukunda obtained Your company; he obtained Your feet — that was his second birth.

@140

Your  mercy’s recipient  therefore  in all qualities  the best
= Though he is younger, Mukunda has now become my elder. He is the recipient of Your mercy, and so he excels in every quality.”

@141

@142

@143

= Every one of the Vaiṣṇavas made a copy for himself, and so, little by little, the two books spread everywhere.

@144

@145

= Śrīvāsa replied, “Why do You say the opposite? With the price of Your mercy You have bought us four brothers.”

@149

@150

= Hearing this, Śivānanda Sena was overwhelmed with love; he fell flat like a rod before the Lord and recited this verse:

@151

for long  by me  the shore  like  You are  found
by You  too  is found  O Lord  now
the best  vessel  this  of mercy
= “O Infinite One, for so long I was sinking in the ocean of worldly existence, and now I have found You, like the shore. And You too, O Lord, have now found in me the most fitting vessel for Your mercy.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka 8.42; Stotra-ratna 26)
