@title The Deliverance of Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka

@1
countless  blessed  of Caitanya’s
associates  by the flood of love
was brought  of unfortunate  people  the hearts’
desert  forever  to the state of a well-watered land
= By the flood of love of Śrī Caitanya’s countless blessed associates, the desert of unfortunate people’s hearts was forever turned into a well-watered land.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the merciful
glory  glory  to Nityānanda  whose heart is compassionate
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, the merciful! Glory, glory to Nityānanda, whose heart is full of compassion!

@3
glory to Advaitācārya  glory  glory  the merciful
glory  to Gaura’s devotees  all  full of rasa
= Glory to Advaitācārya — glory, glory to the merciful one! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura, who are full of *rasa*!

@4
thus  Mahāprabhu  the devotees  with
in Nīlācala  residence  makes  in the joy of love for Kṛṣṇa
= In this way Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala with His devotees, in the joy of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@5
within and without  of separation from Kṛṣṇa  the waves
by various  bhāvas  agitated  the Lord’s  mind  and  body
= Within and without surged the waves of separation from Kṛṣṇa; the Lord’s mind and body were agitated by various *bhāvas* (states of love).

@6
by day  dancing and kīrtana  seeing Jagannātha
at night  Rāya and Svarūpa  with  the relishing of rasa
= By day there was dancing and *kīrtana* and the sight of Jagannātha; by night He relished *rasa* with Rāya and Svarūpa.

@7
of the three worlds  the people  coming  make  darśana
whoever  sees  he  gains  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa
= People of the three worlds came to see Him, and whoever saw Him gained the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@8
of humans  in the guise  devas, gandharvas and kinnaras
of the seven nether worlds  all  the Daityas  the serpents
= In human guise came *devas*, *gandharvas* and *kinnaras*, and all the Daityas and serpents of the seven nether worlds.

@9
on the seven dvīpas  in the nine khaṇḍas  dwell  all  the people
in various guises  coming  make  of the Lord  darśana
= All the people who dwell on the seven *dvīpas* (islands) and in the nine *khaṇḍas* (regions) came in various guises to see the Lord.

@10
Prahlāda  Bali  Vyāsa  Śuka  and other  sages
coming  the Lord  seeing  in love  become  unconscious
= Sages such as Prahlāda, Bali, Vyāsa and Śuka came, and seeing the Lord, they lost consciousness in love.

@11
outside  cry aloud  the people  darśana  not  getting
“Kṛṣṇa  say”  says  the Lord  outside  coming
= Outside, the people cried aloud when they could not see Him; then the Lord would come out and say, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!”

@12
of the Lord  at the sight  all  people  in love  float
in this way  pass  the Lord’s  nights  days
= At the sight of the Lord all the people floated in love. In this way the Lord’s days and nights passed.

@13
one day  people  coming  to the Lord  reported
Gopīnātha  the Baḍa  Jānā  on the cāṅga  has raised
= One day people came and reported to the Lord, “The Baḍa Jānā, the eldest prince, has raised Gopīnātha onto the *cāṅga* (the execution platform).

@14
below  swords  setting  him  from above  they will throw
the Lord  protection  makes  if  then  he will be saved
= They have set swords beneath it and will throw him down onto them from above. Only if You, Lord, protect him will he be saved.

@15
with his whole family  Your  servant  Bhavānanda Rāya
his  son  Your  servant  to protect  it is fitting
= Bhavānanda Rāya and his whole family are Your servants. His son is Your servant, and it is fitting that You protect him.”

@16
the Lord  says  the king  why  does  punishing
then  those  people  told  the whole  account
= The Lord said, “Why is the king punishing him?” Then the people told Him the whole story.

@17
Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka  Rāmānanda’s brother
always  is  he  a keeper of the king’s property  so
= “Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka, Rāmānanda’s brother, has always been a keeper of the king’s property (*rāja-viṣayī*).

@18
in Mālajāṭhyā Daṇḍapāṭa  his  charge
collecting  levying  bringing  the money  he gave  at the king’s court
= His charge was Mālajāṭhyā Daṇḍapāṭa. He collected the revenue there and delivered it to the king’s court.

@19
two lakhs  of kāhanas  him  from  outstanding  became
two lakhs  of kāhanas  of cowries  the king  indeed  demanded
= Two lakhs of *kāhanas* (a *kāhana* is 1,280 cowries) fell due from him, and the king demanded the two lakhs of *kāhanas* of cowries.

@20
he  says  ready wealth  there is not  that  I could give
little by little  selling  buying  the money  I will make good
= He said, ‘I have no ready wealth that I could give at once. Little by little, by selling and buying, I will make good the sum.

@21
horses  ten or twelve  there are  take them  a value  setting
this  saying  the horses  he brings  to the king’s gate  leading
= I have ten or twelve horses — take them at a fair valuation.’ Saying this, he led the horses to the king’s gate.

@22
one  prince  of horses  the value  well  knows
him  sent  the king  ministers and friends  with
= One prince knew well the value of horses, and the king sent him there with ministers and friends.

@23
that  prince  the value  makes  lowering
Gopīnātha’s  anger  arose  the value  hearing
= That prince set the value low, and Gopīnātha grew angry when he heard the price.

@24
of that  prince  the habit  his neck  he turns
with face upward  again and again  here and there  looks
= That prince had a habit: he would turn his neck and, with his face upward, look here and there again and again.

@25
him  mockery  making  he spoke  with proud  words
the king  favor  shows  him  fear  not  feels
= Mocking him, Gopīnātha spoke proud words; the king favoured him, so he felt no fear.

@26
my  horse  its neck  raises  upward  does not  look
therefore  of the horse  the value  low  to make  not  is fitting
= ‘My horse raises its neck, but it does not look upward. So it is not right to set its price low.’

@27
hearing  in the prince’s mind  anger  arose
the king  to  going  much  slander  he made
= Hearing this, the prince grew angry. He went to the king and slandered Gopīnātha at length:

@28
the money  not  will give  this man  goes about  pretexts  making
order  give  on the cāṅga  raising  let me take  the money
= ‘This man will not pay the money; he goes about making excuses. Give the order, and I will raise him onto the *cāṅga* and get the money.’

@29
the king  said  whatever  is good  do  that  going
by which  means  the money  I get  use  that  means
= The king said, ‘Go and do whatever is best. By whatever means I may get the money, use that means.’

@30
the prince  coming  him  on the cāṅga  raised
onto the swords  to throw  below  swords  he set
= The prince came and raised him onto the *cāṅga*, and to throw him onto the swords, he set swords beneath it.”

@31
hearing  the Lord  said  somewhat  showing  affectionate anger
the king’s money  to give  he will not  the king’s  what  fault
= Hearing this, the Lord spoke with a show of affectionate anger: “He will not pay the king’s money — what fault is that of the king’s?

@32
the king’s revenue  collecting  he consumes  no  fear of the king
to harlots and dancers  giving  he makes  various  expenses
= He collects the king’s revenue and consumes it without any fear of the king; he squanders it in various ways, giving it to harlots and dancers.

@33
whoever  is clever  let him  do  the king’s service
the king’s dues  paying off  what he gets  that  let him make  expense
= Let one who is clever do the king’s service: when he has paid off the king’s dues, let him spend what is left to him.”

@34
at this time  another  man  came  running
Vāṇīnātha and the others  with their families  taking  went  binding
= Just then another man came running: “Vāṇīnātha and the others have been bound and taken away with their families!”

@35
the Lord  said  the king  himself  the recorded  money  will take
I  a detached  sannyāsī  in that  what  shall I do
= The Lord said, “The king himself will take the money that stands on the account. I am a detached *sannyāsī* — what can I do in this?”

@36
then  Svarūpa and the others  of Gosāñi  the devotees
of the Lord  at the feet  all  made  entreaty
= Then Svarūpa and the other devotees of the Lord all made an entreaty at His feet:

@37
Rāmānanda Rāya’s  family  all  Your  servants
for You  fitting  is not  such  indifference
= “The family of Rāmānanda Rāya are all Your servants. Such indifference is not fitting for You.”

@38
hearing  Mahāprabhu  spoke  with angry  words
Me  order  you give  all  I should go  to the king’s place
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu spoke angrily: “So you all order Me to go to the king’s court!

@39
of you all  this  opinion  to the king  going
the money  begging  I should take  the end of My cloth  spreading
= This is what you all think: that I should go to the king, spread out the end of My cloth and beg the money from him.

@40
of five gaṇḍās  the recipient  is  a sannyāsī  brāhmaṇa
if he begs  even  why  would he give  two lakhs  of kāhanas
= A *sannyāsī brāhmaṇa* is fit to receive five *gaṇḍās* (twenty cowries). Even if I begged, why would anyone give two lakhs of *kāhanas*?”

@41
at this time  another  man  came  running
of the swords  onto  Gopīnātha  they are  throwing down
= Just then another man came running: “They are throwing Gopīnātha down onto the swords!”

@42
hearing  the Lord’s  associates  the Lord  make  entreaty
the Lord  said  I  a beggar  Me  from  anything  is not
= Hearing this, the Lord’s associates entreated Him, but the Lord said, “I am a beggar; nothing can be done by Me.

@43
therefore  protection  to give  if  it is  in all your  minds
all  together  go  of Jagannātha  to the feet
= So if you all have it in mind to save him, go together to the feet of Jagannātha.

@44
the Lord  Jagannātha  in whose  hands  all  ends
to do  not to do  otherwise  to do  able
= Jagannātha is the Lord, *Īśvara*, in whose hands all things lie. He is able to do, not to do, and to do otherwise.”

@45
here  when  Mahāprabhu  this much  said
Haricandana Pātra  going  to the king  said
= While Mahāprabhu was saying this, Haricandana Pātra went and spoke to the king:

@46
Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka  servant  your
of a servant  the death penalty  is not  proper practice
= “Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka is your servant, and to put a servant to death is not proper conduct.

@47
especially  him  from  money  outstanding  is
life  if you take  what  gain  your own  loss of wealth
= Moreover, money is still owed by him. What gain is there in taking his life? It would only be the loss of your own wealth.

@48
at the proper  value  the horses  take  whatever  outstanding  is
little by little  he will give  needlessly  life  why  take
= Take the horses at their proper value; whatever remains he will pay little by little. Why take his life needlessly?”

@49
the king  said  this  matter  I  not  know
life  why  should I take  his  money  want  I
= The king said, “I know nothing of this. Why should I take his life? It is his money I want.

@50
you  going  make  there  all  settlement
the money  so that  comes  and  remains  his  life
= Go there and settle everything, so that the money comes in and his life is spared.”

@51
then  Haricandana  coming  to the Jānā  told
the cāṅga  from  Gopīnātha  quickly  brought down
= Then Haricandana came and told the Jānā, and Gopīnātha was quickly brought down from the *cāṅga*.

@52
the money  give  the king  demands  the means  he asked
at the proper value  the horses  take  he  indeed  said
= “The king demands the money,” Haricandana said, and asked him how he would pay. Gopīnātha replied, “Take the horses at their proper value.

@53
little by little  I will give  and  whatever  anything  I can
without inquiry  life  you take  what  to say  can I
= The rest I will pay little by little, whatever I can. If you take my life without inquiry, what can I say?”

@54
proper  value  setting  then  the horses  all  they took
of the remaining  money  a fixed term  making  home  they sent
= Then they valued the horses properly and took them all, and, binding him to pay the rest of the money by a fixed date, they sent him home.

@55
here  the Lord  that  man  a question  asked
Vāṇīnātha  what  does  when  binding  they brought
= Meanwhile the Lord asked that man, “What was Vāṇīnātha doing when they bound him and took him away?”

@56
he  said  Vāṇīnātha  fearlessly  takes  Kṛṣṇa’s name
Hare  Kṛṣṇa  Hare  Kṛṣṇa  he says  without rest
= He said, “Vāṇīnātha was fearlessly chanting the name of Kṛṣṇa, saying ‘Hare Kṛṣṇa, Hare Kṛṣṇa’ without rest.

@57
of the count  for the sake  on both hands  on the fingers  marking
a thousand and so on  complete  when  on his body  he scratches  a line
= To keep count, he marked the names on the fingers of both hands, and whenever a thousand or so were complete, he scratched a line on his body.”

@58
hearing  Mahāprabhu  became  supremely  joyful
who  to understand  can  Gaura’s  the design of mercy
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu was supremely joyful. Who can understand the design of Gaura’s mercy?

@59
at this time  Kāśī Miśra  came  to the Lord’s place
the Lord  to him  spoke  some  anxious words
= At that time Kāśī Miśra came to the Lord, and the Lord spoke to him with some anxiety:

@60
here  to stay  I cannot  I will go  to Ālālanātha
many  disturbances  here  not  I get  peace
= “I cannot stay here; I shall go to Ālālanātha. There are many disturbances here, and I find no peace.

@61
Bhavānanda Rāya’s  family  does  the king’s service
in various ways  make  they  the king’s money  expense
= The family of Bhavānanda Rāya is in the king’s service, and in various ways they spend the king’s money.

@62
the king’s  what  fault  the king  his own money  wants
to give  they will not  the money  the punishment  to Me  they report
= What fault is the king’s? The king wants his own money. They will not give the money, and then they report their punishment to Me.

@63
the king  Gopīnātha  when  on the cāṅga  raised
four times  people  coming  to Me  reported
= When the king had Gopīnātha raised onto the *cāṅga*, people came four times to tell Me.

@64
a mendicant  sannyāsī  I  living in solitude
to Me  pain  they give  their own troubles  telling  coming
= I am a mendicant *sannyāsī* who lives in solitude, yet they come and pain Me by telling Me their own troubles.

@65
today  him  Jagannātha  made  protection
tomorrow  who  will protect  if  not  he gives  the king’s wealth
= Today Jagannātha has protected him; but tomorrow who will protect him if he does not pay the king’s money?

@66
of worldly men  the news  hearing  disturbed  becomes  the mind
therefore  here  staying  My  is no  purpose
= Hearing the affairs of worldly men disturbs My mind, and so I have no reason to stay here.”

@67
Kāśī Miśra  said  the Lord’s  holding  feet
You  why  over this  matter  agitation  make  in the mind
= Clasping the Lord’s feet, Kāśī Miśra said, “Why do You let this matter trouble Your mind?

@68
a detached  sannyāsī  Your  with whom  relationship
worldly dealings  for the sake of  You  worships  he  blind to knowledge
= You are a detached *sannyāsī* — what connection have You with anyone? One who worships You for worldly ends is blind to true knowledge.

@69
of Your  worship  as the fruit  for You  the treasure of love
worldly things  for the sake of  You  worships  that  foolish  person
= The fruit of worshipping You is the treasure of love for You. One who worships You for the sake of worldly things is a fool.

@70
You  for the sake of  Rāmānanda  the kingdom  renunciation  made
You  for the sake of  Sanātana  his worldly position  gave up
= For Your sake Rāmānanda gave up his governorship; for Your sake Sanātana abandoned his worldly position.

@71
You  for the sake of  Raghunātha  everything  gave up
here  his  father  wealth  sent
= For Your sake Raghunātha gave up everything, and here his father sent him wealth.

@72
Your  the mercy of the feet  has come  to him
at the alms-house  begging  he eats  the wealth  touch  not  does
= But the mercy of Your feet has come to him: he begs his food at the alms-house and does not touch that wealth.

@73
Rāmānanda’s  brother  the noble Gopīnātha
You  from  desire for worldly gain  his  wish  is not
= The noble Gopīnātha, Rāmānanda’s brother, has no wish for worldly gain from You.

@74
his  distress  seeing  his  servants and others
to You  they reported  since  having no other shelter
= Seeing his distress, his servants and others told You of it, for he has no shelter but You (*ananya-śaraṇa*).

@75
he  a pure devotee  who  You  worships  You  for the sake of
his own  in happiness and distress  is  the sharer of the experience
= He is a pure devotee who worships You for Your own sake; in his own happiness and distress he himself bears his share.

@76
Your  mercy  he seeks  he worships  constantly
soon  is attained  by him  Your  feet
= He longs for Your mercy and worships You constantly; soon he attains Your feet.
