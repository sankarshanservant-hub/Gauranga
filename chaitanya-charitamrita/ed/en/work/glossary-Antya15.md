# English names and terms — CC Antya 15 (stage A; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 15»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 15», Antya15-p1/p2) are used as given there:
the five senses; *rasa-narma* (playful jests); chief wealth (*mūla-dhana*); *cūta*, *priyāla*, *panasa*, *āsana*,
*kovidāra*, *jambu*, *arka*, *bilva*, *bakula*, *kadamba*, *nīpa*; *mālatī*, *mallikā*, *jāti*, *yūthikā*; doe (*eṇa-patnī*);
Rāmarāya; Madana-mohana (the enchanter of Madana); the starlike necklace (*tāra-hāra*, Anubhāṣya 63); ground collyrium;
*indīvara* (blue lotus); *valāhaka* (cloud); a row of cranes (*baka-pā̃ti*); Vaijayantī garland; bait, snare;
the robber chest (*ḍākātiyā vakṣa*).
