@121
of the meal  of the remnants  the plate  Govinda  to him  gave
prasāda  having taken  Sanātana  to the Lord’s side  came
= Govinda gave him the plate with the remnants of the Lord’s meal. Having taken the *prasāda*, Sanātana came to the Lord.

@122
the Lord  says  by which  path  have you come  Sanātana
he  says  by the path along the sea  I have come
= The Lord asked, “By which path did you come, Sanātana?” He replied, “I came by the path along the sea.”

@123
the Lord  says  over the hot  sand  how  did you come
of the Siṁha-dvāra  the path  is cool  why  not  did you come
= The Lord said, “How could you come over the burning sand? The path by the Siṁha-dvāra is cool — why did you not come that way?

@124
from the hot  sand  your  on the feet  have come  blisters
to walk  not  you can  how  did you endure
= The hot sand has blistered your feet; you cannot walk. How did you bear it?”

@125
Sanātana  says  pain  much  not  I felt
on the feet  blisters  have formed  that  not  I knew
= Sanātana said, “I did not feel much pain. I did not even know that blisters had formed on my feet.

@126
by the Siṁha-dvāra  to go  my  there is no  right
especially  of the Ṭhākura  there  of the servants  the movement
= I have no right to go by the Siṁha-dvāra, especially since the servants of the Ṭhākura, Lord Jagannātha, move about there.

@127
the servants  coming and going  do  there is no  pause
their  touch  if it happens  ruin  will be  mine
= The servants come and go without pause, and if I should touch them, it would be my ruin.”

@128
hearing  Mahāprabhu  in mind  satisfaction  felt
pleased  becoming  to him  something  to say  began
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu was satisfied at heart, and, pleased, He began to speak to him.

@129-130
although  you  are  the purifier of the world
by your touch  purified  become  the gods  and sages
nevertheless  of a devotee  the nature  propriety  guarding
of propriety  the keeping  is  of a sādhu  the ornament
= “Although you purify the whole world, and even the gods and sages are purified by your touch, it is still a devotee’s nature to guard propriety (*maryādā*). Keeping propriety is the ornament of a *sādhu*.

@131
of propriety  in the transgression  people  make  mockery
this world  the next world  both  are  destroyed
= When propriety is transgressed, people mock, and both this world and the next are ruined.

@132
propriety  when you keep  pleased  becomes  My  mind
you  so  if not  do  would do  which  person
= When you keep propriety, My mind is pleased. If you do not act this way, who will?”

@133
this  having said  the Lord  him  embrace  made
his  the fluid of the sores  of the Lord  to the holy body  clung
= Saying this, the Lord embraced him, and the fluid from his sores smeared the Lord’s holy body.

@134
again and again  he forbids  still  He makes  embrace
to the body  the fluid  clings  sorrow  feels  Sanātana
= Again and again Sanātana tried to stop Him, yet the Lord kept embracing him. The fluid clung to the Lord’s body, and Sanātana was grieved.

@135
in this way  servant and master  both  home  went
the next  day  Jagadānanda  with Sanātana  met
= In this way servant and master went to their dwellings. The next day Jagadānanda came to see Sanātana.

@136
the two  sitting  about Kṛṣṇa  a talk  made
to the Paṇḍita  Sanātana  his grief  confided
= The two sat together and talked of Kṛṣṇa, and Sanātana confided his grief to the Paṇḍita:

@137
here  I came  the Lord  seeing  my grief  to remove
whatever  in mind  that  the Lord  not  let  me do
= “I came here to see the Lord and be rid of my grief, but the Lord did not let me do what I had in mind.

@138
though I forbid  the Lord  embrace  makes  me
my  the fluid of the sores  clings  of the Lord  to the body
= Though I try to stop Him, the Lord embraces me, and the fluid of my sores smears His body.

@139
offense  is  mine  there is no  deliverance
even Jagannātha  not  I see  this  grief  boundless
= I am committing an offense, and for me there is no deliverance. I cannot even see Jagannātha — this grief is boundless.

@140
for my welfare  came  I  it turned out  the opposite
what  by doing  welfare  comes  I cannot  determine
= I came for my welfare, but the opposite has happened. I cannot decide what I should do for my good.”

@141
the Paṇḍita  says  for you  fit to live in  is Vṛndāvana
the Ratha-yātrā  having seen  there  make  your going
= The Paṇḍita said, “The place fit for you to live is Vṛndāvana. After seeing the Ratha-yātrā, go there.

@142
of the Lord  the order  has been given  to you  two  brothers
in Vṛndāvana  settle  there  all happiness  you will find
= The Lord has ordered you two brothers to settle in Vṛndāvana; there you will find every happiness.

@143
for which purpose  you came  of the Lord  you have seen  the feet
on the chariot  Jagannātha  having seen  make  your going
= You came for the Lord’s feet, and you have seen them. Now see Jagannātha on His chariot and go.”

@144
Sanātana  says  good  you have given  advice
there  I shall go  that  my  the land given by the Lord
= Sanātana said, “You have given good advice. I shall go there: that is my ‘land given by the Lord.’”

@145
this  having said  the two  to their own duties  rising  went
the next  day  Mahāprabhu  to meet  came
= Saying this, the two rose and went about their duties. The next day Mahāprabhu came to meet them.

@146
Haridāsa  made  of the Lord  to the feet  obeisance
Haridāsa  made  the Lord  a loving embrace
= Haridāsa bowed at the Lord’s feet, and the Lord embraced Haridāsa with love.

@147
from afar  prostrate  obeisance  makes  Sanātana
the Lord  calls  again and again  to make  embrace
= Sanātana offered prostrate obeisances from a distance. Again and again the Lord called him, wanting to embrace him.

@148
of offense  in fear  he  to meet  not  came
Mahāprabhu  to meet  to that  place  went
= Fearing to offend, he did not come forward to meet Him, so Mahāprabhu went to that place to meet him.

@149
Sanātana  running away  backward  goes
by force  catching him  the Lord  made  embrace
= Sanātana drew back and fled, but the Lord caught him by force and embraced him.

filled with nirveda  Sanātana  began  to speak
= Taking the two with Him, the Lord sat down on the raised platform (*piṇḍā*), and Sanātana, filled with *nirveda* — detachment — began to speak:
