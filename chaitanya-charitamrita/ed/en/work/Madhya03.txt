@title Śrī Caitanya at the House of Advaita Ācārya

@1
sannyāsa  having accepted  full of love  then  Gaura
to Vṛndāvana  intent on going  in bewilderment  who
in Rāḍha  wandering  Śāntipura  having reached
enjoyed  with the devotees  here  to Him  bowed  am I
= After accepting *sannyāsa*, Gaura, overflowing with love, set out for Vṛndāvana, but in His bewilderment He wandered through Rāḍha. Then He came to Śāntipura and delighted there in the company of His devotees. I bow down to Him.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  of the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
twenty-four  of years  at the end  which  the month of Māgha
of it  in the bright fortnight  the Lord  accepted  sannyāsa
= In the month of Māgha that ended His twenty-fourth year, during the bright fortnight, the Lord accepted *sannyāsa*.

@4
sannyāsa  having accepted  absorbed in love  set out  for Vṛndāvana
in the land of Rāḍha  three  days  did  wandering
= After accepting *sannyāsa*, absorbed in love, He set out for Vṛndāvana, but for three days He wandered in the land of Rāḍha.

@5
this  verse  reciting  the Lord  of ecstasy  in absorption
by wandering  pure  made  all  the land of Rāḍha
= Absorbed in ecstasy and reciting this verse, the Lord wandered and purified the whole land of Rāḍha:

@6
this  fully  having taken up  devotion to the Supreme Self
worshipped  by the most ancient  great souls
I  shall cross  whose far shore is hard to reach
the darkness  of Mukunda’s feet  by service  alone
= “Fully taking up this devotion to the Supreme Self, embraced by the great souls of old, I shall cross the darkness whose far shore is so hard to reach, by service to the feet of Mukunda alone.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.23.57)

@7
the Lord  said  excellent  these  of the mendicant  words
to Mukunda  of service  the vow  has made  firm resolve
= The Lord said, “Excellent are the words of this mendicant: he has firmly resolved on the vow of serving Mukunda.

@8
devotion to the Supreme Self  only for  the dress  wearing
by service to Mukunda  comes about  from saṁsāra  deliverance
= Wearing the dress of renunciation is only for the sake of devotion to the Supreme Self; it is by serving Mukunda that one is delivered from *saṁsāra*.

@9
that  dress  I have taken  now  to Vṛndāvana  having gone
Kṛṣṇa  service  doing  in solitude  sitting
= I have taken that dress; now I shall go to Vṛndāvana and serve Kṛṣṇa, settled in solitude.”

@10
thus  saying  goes  the Lord  of love’s madness  with the signs
of directions  awareness  none  whether  night or day
= Saying this, the Lord went on, showing the signs of love’s madness. He had no sense of direction and did not know whether it was night or day.

@11
Nityānanda  Ācāryaratna  Mukunda  three  persons
the Lord  behind  behind  the three  make  going
= Nityānanda, Ācāryaratna and Mukunda—these three followed close behind the Lord.

@12
whoever  whoever  the Lord  sees  those  those  people
in ecstatic love  “Hari”  say  is cut off  sorrow and grief
= Whoever saw the Lord called out “Hari!” in ecstatic love, and their sorrow and grief were cut away.

@13
cowherd  boys  all  the Lord  seeing
“Hari”  “Hari”  saying  call  loudly  making
= All the cowherd boys, on seeing the Lord, called out loudly, “Hari! Hari!”

@14
hearing  them all  near  went  Gaurahari
“say it”  “say it”  says  of all  on the heads  hand  placing
= Hearing them, Gaurahari went up to them, placed His hand on each one’s head and said, “Say it! Say it!”

@15
them all  praise  does  you  are fortunate
fulfilled  have made  Me  by making Me hear  the name of Hari
= He praised them all: “You are fortunate! You have fulfilled My life by letting Me hear the name of Hari.”

@16
secretly  them all  bringing  Ṭhākura  Nityānanda
instructed  all of them  having made  a plan
= Meanwhile Ṭhākura Nityānanda secretly gathered the boys and, with a plan in mind, instructed them all:

@17
of Vṛndāvana  the way  the Lord  asks  you
along the bank of the Ganges  the path  then  show  Him
= “When the Lord asks you the way to Vṛndāvana, show Him the path along the bank of the Ganges.”

@18
then  the Lord  asked  listen  children
tell  please  by which  path  shall I go  to Vṛndāvana
= Then the Lord asked, “Listen, children! Tell Me, by which path shall I go to Vṛndāvana?”

@19
the children  all  the path along the Ganges’ bank  showed
that  by path  in absorption  the Lord  going  made
= The children showed Him the path along the bank of the Ganges, and the Lord, absorbed in ecstasy, went that way.

@20
to Ācāryaratna  says  Nityānanda Gosāñi
quickly  go  you  of Advaita Ācārya  to the place
= Nityānanda Gosāñi said to Ācāryaratna, “Go quickly to Advaita Ācārya.

@21
the Lord  taking  shall go  I  His  to the house
ready  let Him stay  so that  a boat  having taken  on the bank
= I shall bring the Lord to His house. Let Him wait ready on the bank with a boat.

@22
then  to Navadvīpa  you  make  going
Mother Śacī  taking  come  and  the devotees
= Then go to Navadvīpa and come back with Mother Śacī and the other devotees.”

@23
him  having sent  Nityānanda  the great soul
of Mahāprabhu  before  coming  gave  introduction
= Having sent him off, the great-hearted Nityānanda came before Mahāprabhu and made Himself known.

@24
the Lord  says  Śrīpāda  Your  where  going
Śrīpāda  says  You  with  shall go  to Vṛndāvana
= The Lord said, “Śrīpāda, where are You going?” Śrīpāda answered, “I am going with You to Vṛndāvana.”

@25
the Lord  says  how  far  is  Vṛndāvana
He  says  take  this  of the Yamunā  sight
= The Lord asked, “How far is Vṛndāvana?” He replied, “Behold—here is the Yamunā!”

@26
thus  saying  brought  Him  to the Ganges’  vicinity
in ecstasy  the Lord’s  arose  the Ganges  as the Yamunā perceiving
= Saying this, He brought Him to the Ganges, and in His ecstasy the Lord took the Ganges for the Yamunā.

@27
oh  fortune  of the Yamunā  I have obtained  sight
thus  saying  of the Yamunā  makes  praise
= “Oh, what fortune! I have seen the Yamunā!” Saying this, He began to praise the Yamunā:

@28
of consciousness and bliss  of the sun  always  of Nanda’s son
of supreme love  the object  liquid Brahman  whose body
of sins  the cutter  of the world  welfare  bestower
may she purify  our  bodies  of the sun  the daughter
= “May the daughter of the sun purify our bodies—she who is ever the object of the supreme love of Nanda’s son, the sun of consciousness and bliss; whose body is Brahman in liquid form; who cuts away sins and bestows welfare on the world.” (*Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka* 5.13)

@29
thus  saying  bowing down  did  bath in the Ganges
one  kaupīna  not  second  garment
= Saying this, He bowed down and bathed in the Ganges. He had only a single *kaupīna*, and no other garment.

@30
at such  a time  Ācārya Gosāñi  in a boat  having boarded
came  new  kaupīna and outer cloth  bringing
= Just then Ācārya Gosāñi arrived by boat, bringing a new *kaupīna* and outer cloth.

@31
in front  the Ācārya  coming  stood  obeisance  having made
the Ācārya  seeing  says  the Lord  in mind  doubt  feeling
= The Ācārya came before Him and stood after offering obeisance. Seeing the Ācārya, the Lord spoke, puzzled at heart:

@32
You  surely  Ācārya Gosāñi  here  why  have come
I  in Vṛndāvana  You  how  knew
= “You are Ācārya Gosāñi, aren’t You? Why have You come here? How did You know that I was in Vṛndāvana?”

@33
the Ācārya  says  You  wherever  that  is Vṛndāvana
by My  fortune  to the Ganges’ bank  Your  coming
= The Ācārya said, “Wherever You are, that is Vṛndāvana. It is My good fortune that You have come to the bank of the Ganges.”

@34
the Lord  says  Nityānanda  Me  has deceived
to the Ganges  bringing  to Me  the Yamunā  called it
= The Lord said, “Nityānanda has deceived Me! He brought Me to the Ganges and told Me it was the Yamunā.”

@35
the Ācārya  says  false  is not  Śrīpāda’s words
in the Yamunā  bath  You  took  just now
= The Ācārya said, “Śrīpāda’s words are not false. Just now You did bathe in the Yamunā.

@36
in the Ganges  the Yamunā  flows  becoming  one stream
on the west  the Yamunā  flows  on the east  the Ganges’ stream
= Here the Yamunā flows within the Ganges as one stream: on the west flows the Yamunā, on the east the stream of the Ganges.

@37
in the western stream  the Yamunā  flows  there  You took  bath
wet  kaupīna  giving up  dry  make  wearing
= The Yamunā flows in the western stream, and that is where You bathed. Now take off Your wet *kaupīna* and put on a dry one.

@38
in ecstatic love  three  days  You have been  fasting
today  in My  house  alms  come  to My  dwelling
= Absorbed in love, You have been fasting for three days. Today accept alms at My house—come to My home.

@39
one  handful  of rice  I  have done  cooking
plain  vegetables  I made  dāl  and  greens
= I have cooked a handful of rice and made some plain vegetables, *dāl* and greens.”

@40
thus  saying  in the boat  seating  took  to His own house
of the feet  washing  did  joyful at heart
= Saying this, He seated the Lord in the boat and took Him to His own house, where, joyful at heart, He washed the Lord’s feet.

@41
first  the cooking  had done  the Ācārya’s wife
to Viṣṇu  offering  did  the Ācārya  Himself
= The Ācārya’s wife had done the cooking beforehand, and the Ācārya Himself offered the food to Viṣṇu.

@42
in three  places  the food  served  equal  making
Kṛṣṇa’s  offering  served  on a metal plate
= The food was served in three equal portions. Kṛṣṇa’s offering was served on a metal plate,

@43
of the battiśā-āṭhiyā  banana  whole  on leaves
in two  places  the food  served  in a fine  manner
= and the other two portions were served nicely on whole, uncut leaves of the *battiśā-āṭhiyā* banana tree.

@44
in the middle  with yellow ghee soaked  of fine rice  a heap
on four sides  vegetable cups  and  mung dāl
= In the middle of each was a heap of fine rice soaked in yellow *ghee*, and all around it were leaf cups of vegetables and *mung dāl*.

@45
sārdraka  vāstuka greens  various  kinds
paṭola  pumpkin with baḍi  mānakacu  and
= There was *sārdraka*, various kinds of *vāstuka* greens, *paṭola*, pumpkin with *baḍi*, and *mānakacu*.

@46
cai and pepper sukhta  adding  to all  fruits and roots
nectar-defeating  five kinds  bitter and pungent
= There were five kinds of bitter and pungent dishes, putting nectar to shame, with *sukhta* of *cai* and black pepper added to all the fruits and roots.

@47
tender  nim leaves  with  fried  eggplant
paṭola with phula-baḍi fried  pumpkin mānacāki
= There was eggplant fried with tender *nim* leaves, *paṭola* fried with *phula-baḍi*, and pumpkin *mānacāki*.

@48
coconut pulp  curd  with sugar  sweet
banana-flower ghaṇṭa  pumpkin in milk  all  in abundance
= There was coconut pulp, curd sweetened with sugar, a *ghaṇṭa* of banana flowers and pumpkin cooked in milk—all in abundance.

@49
sweet-and-sour baḍās  sour dishes and others  five or six
all  vegetables  made  among people  as many as  exist
= There were sweet-and-sour *baḍās* and five or six sour dishes. Every vegetable dish known among people was prepared.

@50
mung baḍās  urad baḍās  banana baḍās  sweet
kṣīrapulī  coconut  all  cakes  favourite
= There were *baḍās* of *mung* and of *urad*, sweet banana *baḍās*, *kṣīrapulī*, coconut, and every favourite kind of cake.

@51
of the battiśā-āṭhiyā  banana  the cups  large  large
move  shake  not  cups  very  large  strong
= The cups made of *battiśā-āṭhiyā* banana leaves were very large and strong, so that they did not tip or wobble.

@52
fifty  fifty  cups  with vegetables  filling
of the three  offerings  around  on the sides  placed  arranging
= Fifty such cups each, filled with vegetables, were set all around the three offerings.

@53
with ghee sweet rice  new  earthen pots  filling
in three  vessels  thickened milk  they place  indeed  arranging
= Sweet rice with *ghee* filled new earthen pots, and thickened milk was set out in three vessels.

@54
milk with flat rice and banana  and  milk laklakī
however much  they made  that  to describe  not  I am able
= There was flat rice with banana in milk, and milk *laklakī*. I cannot describe all that they made.

@55
on two  sides  placed  all  earthen pots  filling
campaka bananas, yogurt, sandeśa  to describe  not  I can
= On both sides were placed earthen pots filled with *campaka* bananas, yogurt and *sandeśa*—I cannot describe it all.

@56
over the rice and vegetables  placed  tulasī buds
three  water pots  with scented  water  filling
= On top of the rice and vegetables they placed *tulasī* buds, and three water pots were filled with scented water.

@57
three  white seats  them  upon  cloth
Kṛṣṇa’s  food  directly  to Kṛṣṇa  made to eat
= There were three white seats covered with cloth. The food prepared for Kṛṣṇa was offered to Kṛṣṇa Himself, and He ate it.

@58
of the ārati  at the time  the two  Lords  called
with the Lords  all  coming  the ārati  saw
= At the time of *ārati* the Ācārya called the two Lords, and everyone came with Them to see the *ārati*.

@59
ārati  having done  Kṛṣṇa  made to lie down
the Ācārya  coming  to the Lord  then  made  a request
= After the *ārati*, Kṛṣṇa was laid down to rest, and then the Ācārya came and made a request to the Lord:

@60
of the house  inside  the Lord  may make  going
the two  brothers  came  then  to take  the meal
= “May the Lord come inside the house.” Then the two brothers came in to take Their meal.

@61
Mukunda  Haridāsa  the two  Lords  called
with joined palms  the two  to speak  began
= The two Lords called Mukunda and Haridāsa, but the two of them, with joined palms, began to speak.

@62
Mukunda  says  my  some  duty  not  is finished
later  I  prasāda  shall take  You  go  into the house
= Mukunda said, “Some of my duties are not yet finished. I shall take *prasāda* later; You go inside.”

@63
Haridāsa  says  I  most sinful  lowly
outside  one  handful  later  shall make  meal
= Haridāsa said, “I am most sinful and lowly. Later I shall eat a handful outside.”

@64
the two  Lords  taking  the Ācārya  went  to the inner room
the prasāda  seeing  of the Lord  joy  in the heart
= The Ācārya took the two Lords into the inner room. Seeing the *prasāda*, the Lord felt joy in His heart.

@65
such  food  who  to Kṛṣṇa  makes  eat
birth  after birth  on My head  I hold  his  feet
= “Whoever feeds Kṛṣṇa such food—birth after birth I hold his feet on My head.”

@66
the Lord  knows  three  offerings  Kṛṣṇa’s  naivedya
of the Ācārya  the mind’s  intention  not  to the Lord  known
= The Lord understood that all three offerings were food offered to Kṛṣṇa, but He did not know what the Ācārya had in mind.

@67
the Lord  says  sit  You  to take  the meal
the Ācārya  says  I  shall do  serving
= The Lord said, “Sit down and eat too.” The Ācārya said, “I shall serve.”

@68
in which  place  shall I sit  another  bring  two  leaves
little  making  on them  bringing  give  vegetables and rice
= The Lord said, “Where shall I sit? Bring two more leaves, and put a little rice and vegetables on them.”

@69
the Ācārya  says  sit  both  the seats  upon
thus  saying  by the hands  taking  seated  both
= The Ācārya said, “Both of You, sit on these seats,” and saying this, He took Them by the hand and seated Them.

@70
the Lord  says  of a sannyāsī  food  is not  such fare
this  eating  how  will be  of the senses  restraint
= The Lord said, “Such fare is not fit food for a *sannyāsī*. If he eats this, how will he restrain his senses?”

@71
the Ācārya  says  give up  You  Your own  concealment
I  know  of Your  sannyāsa  the pretence
= The Ācārya said, “Give up Your concealment. I know what lies behind Your *sannyāsa*.

@72
the meal  take  give up  of words  cleverness
the Lord  says  so much  food  to eat  not  I can
= Eat, and give up Your clever words.” The Lord said, “I cannot eat so much food.”

@73
the Ācārya  says  without pretence  take  food
if  to eat  not  You can  will remain  the rest
= The Ācārya said, “Eat without pretence. If You cannot eat it all, the rest will remain.”

@74
the Lord  says  so much  food  I shall not be able  to eat
of a sannyāsī  the duty  is not  remnants  to leave
= The Lord said, “I shall not be able to eat so much food, and it is not the duty of a *sannyāsī* to leave remnants.”

@75
the Ācārya  says  in Nīlācala  You eat  fifty-four times
at one time  food  You eat  hundreds  hundreds  loads
= The Ācārya said, “In Nīlācala You eat fifty-four times, and each time You eat hundreds and hundreds of loads of food.

@76
of three  persons  the food portion  Your  one  mouthful
by that  reckoning  this  food  is not  five mouthfuls
= What three people eat is a single mouthful for You. By that reckoning this food is not even five mouthfuls.

@77
by My  fortune  to My  house  Your  coming
give up  cleverness  Lord  take  the meal
= By My good fortune You have come to My house. Give up Your cleverness, Lord, and eat.”

@78
thus  saying  water  gave  of the two  Gosāñis  on the hands
smiling  began  both  meal  to take
= Saying this, He poured water on the hands of the two Gosāñis, and smiling, the two of Them began to eat.

@79
Nityānanda  says  I did  three  fasts
today  breaking the fast  to do  great  was  hope
= Nityānanda said, “I have fasted for three days, and I had great hope of breaking My fast today.

@80
today  fasting  has come  by the Ācārya’s invitation
half the belly  not  has filled  this  mouthful of  food
= But today, by the Ācārya’s invitation, it has turned into a fast: this mouthful of food has not filled even half My belly.”

@81
the Ācārya  says  You  a pilgrim  sannyāsī
sometimes  fruits and roots  You eat  sometimes  fasting
= The Ācārya said, “You are a pilgrim *sannyāsī*: sometimes You eat fruits and roots, sometimes You fast.

@82
in a poor brāhmaṇa’s  house  what  You have got  a handful of  food
with this  satisfied  be  give up  a greedy mind
= Be satisfied with the handful of food You have received in a poor brāhmaṇa’s house, and give up Your greed.”

@83
Nityānanda  says  since  You made  an invitation
so much  to give  You must  as much as  I eat
= Nityānanda said, “Since You invited Me, You must give Me as much as I eat.”

@84
hearing  Nityānanda’s  words  Ṭhākura  Advaita
speaks  to Him  some  feeling  affection
= Hearing Nityānanda’s words, Ṭhākura Advaita, feeling a surge of affection, said to Him:

@85
fallen  avadhūta  You  the belly  to fill
sannyāsa  You have taken  I think  brāhmaṇas  to punish
= “You are a fallen *avadhūta*! You took *sannyāsa* to fill Your belly, and to punish brāhmaṇas, I think.

@86
You  to eat  can  ten or twenty  māns of  food
I  that  where  shall get  a poor  brāhmaṇa
= You can eat ten or twenty *māns* of rice. Where shall I, a poor brāhmaṇa, get that much?

@87
what  You have got  a handful of  food  that  eating  get up
madness  not  do  not  scatter  leftovers
= Eat the handful of food You have been given and get up. Don’t act like a madman, and don’t scatter Your leftovers about!”

@88
in this  way  in a joking mood  They take  the meal
half  half  eating  the Lord  leaves  the vegetables
= In this way They ate, joking and laughing. The Lord would eat half of each vegetable dish and leave the rest.

@89
those  vegetables  the Ācārya  again  makes  filling
in this  way  again  again  serves  the vegetables
= The Ācārya would fill that dish again, and so He served the vegetables again and again.

@90
cup  with vegetables  filling  makes  entreaty
the Lord  says  more  how much  shall I make  eating
= Filling the cup with vegetables, He would entreat the Lord to eat. The Lord said, “How much more shall I eat?”

@91
the Ācārya  says  what  I have given  that  not  leave
now  what  I give  of that  half  eat
= The Ācārya said, “Do not leave what I have already given You. Of what I give You now, eat half.”

@92
with various  efforts and humility  the Lord  made  eat
the Ācārya’s  desire  the Lord  made  fulfilled
= With many efforts and humble pleas the Ācārya made the Lord eat, and the Lord fulfilled the Ācārya’s desire.

@93
Nityānanda  says  My  belly  not  is filled
taking  go away  your  food  any  not  I have eaten
= Nityānanda said, “My belly is not full! Take it away—I have not eaten any of your food.”

@94
thus  saying  a mouthful of  rice  in His hand  taking
tossing  threw  in front  as if  angry  becoming
= Saying this, He took a handful of rice in His hand and tossed it in front of Him, as if in anger.

@95
grains of rice  two or four  touched  the Ācārya’s  body
the rice  on His body  bearing  the Ācārya  dances  in many ways
= A few grains of rice struck the Ācārya’s body, and with the rice on His body the Ācārya danced about in delight.

@96
of the avadhūta  the leftovers  have touched  My  body
supremely  pure  Me  has made  in this  way
= “The leftovers of the *avadhūta* have touched My body! In this way He has made Me supremely pure.

@97
you  invitation  having made  I have got  its  fruit
your  caste and family  none  by nature  a madman
= I invited you, and I have reaped the fruit of it! You have no caste or family—you are a madman by nature.

@98
Your own  equal  Me  to make  for the sake of
leftovers  you gave  a brāhmaṇa  considering  fear  not  you felt
= To make Me just like you, you threw your leftovers at Me, without fearing that I am a brāhmaṇa.”

@99
Nityānanda  says  this  Kṛṣṇa’s  prasāda
it  ‘leftovers’  having called  You committed  an offence
= Nityānanda said, “This is Kṛṣṇa’s *prasāda*! By calling it ‘leftovers’ You have committed an offence.

@100
a hundred  sannyāsīs  if  You feed
then  this  offence  will be  cancelled
= If You feed a hundred *sannyāsīs*, then this offence will be cancelled.”

@101
the Ācārya  says  not  I shall make  sannyāsī invitation
a sannyāsī  has destroyed  My  all  smṛti-dharma
= The Ācārya said, “I shall never again invite a *sannyāsī*! A *sannyāsī* has ruined all My *smṛti-dharma*.”

@102
thus  saying  the two  persons  made to rinse
on an excellent  bed  taking  made to lie down
= Saying this, He had the two Lords rinse Their hands and mouths, and then took Them to an excellent bed and had Them lie down.

@103
cloves  cardamom seeds  excellent  fragrance
tulasī buds  with  gave  mouth-freshener
= He gave Them cloves and cardamom seeds with *tulasī* buds as a fragrant mouth-freshener.

@104
fragrant  with sandalwood  anointed  made  the bodies
fragrant  flower garlands  bringing  placed  on the chest
