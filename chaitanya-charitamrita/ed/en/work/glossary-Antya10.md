# English names and terms — CC Antya 10, verses 1–82 (stage A, part 1; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 10»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 10») are used as given there: *jhāli* (hamper), “Rāghava’s
*jhālis*”; *munsib* (steward); *purāṇa śuktā* (dried bitter greens); *tailāmra* (mango pickle in mustard oil); *guṇḍā* (powder);
*manuṣya-buddhi* (regarding Him as a human being); *āma*; *kuthalī* (little bag); *bojhāri* (porter); *ājāḍa* (empty);
*nāḍu-gaṅgājali*; *śālikācaṭi*; *ukhḍā*; *huḍum*; *phuṭkalāi* (fried peas); *pāṁc-kuḍi*; *Kirātārjunīya*.
Verse 2 — word for word as in the revised Madhya 4.2; verse 59, line 2 — as in the revised Madhya 13.31 (`ed/en-rev/`).
