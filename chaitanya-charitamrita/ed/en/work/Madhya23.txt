@title Divine Love

@1
for long  not given  His own  hidden treasure
the nectar of His own love and name  the supremely generous
down to the lowest  who  distributed  Gaura
Kṛṣṇa  to the people  in Him  I  take refuge
= I take refuge in Gaura-Kṛṣṇa, the supremely generous, who gave to all people, down to the lowest, the nectar of His own love and His name — His own hidden treasure, not given for a long time.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
now  hear  of bhakti  the fruit  prema  the goal
of which  by hearing  there is  knowledge of bhakti-rasa
= Now hear of the fruit of *bhakti*: *prema*, the ultimate goal (*prayojana*). By hearing of it one comes to know *bhakti-rasa*.

@4
for Kṛṣṇa  rati  deep  when it becomes  prema  is the name
of the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  that  sthāyī-bhāva  is the name
= When *rati* for Kṛṣṇa deepens, it is called *prema*. It is called the abiding emotion (*sthāyī-bhāva*) of the *rasa* of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

@5
śuddha-sattva  special  in its nature
of the sun of prema  to a ray  similar
by tastes  of the heart  softness
producing  this  bhāva  is called
= “That which by nature is a special *śuddha-sattva*, pure spiritual existence, which is like a ray of the sun of *prema*, and which by its tastes (*ruci*) softens the heart, is called *bhāva*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.3.1)

@6
these  two  of bhāva  essential  marginal  characteristics
of prema  the characteristics  now  hear  Sanātana
= These two are the essential (*svarūpa*) and marginal (*taṭastha*) characteristics of *bhāva*. Now, Sanātana, hear the characteristics of *prema*.

@7
completely  having softened  the heart
by the feeling ‘He is mine’  intense  marked
bhāva  that  itself  dense in nature
by the wise  prema  is called
= “When *bhāva* has completely softened the heart, is marked by an intense feeling that ‘He is mine’ (*mamatva*), and has become dense in its nature, the wise call it *prema*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.4.1)

@8
the exclusive feeling ‘He is mine’  toward Viṣṇu
the feeling ‘He is mine’  with prema  joined
bhakti  thus  is called  by Bhīṣma
by Prahlāda, Uddhava and Nārada
= “The exclusive feeling ‘He is mine’ (*ananya-mamatā*) toward Viṣṇu alone, joined with *prema* — this is what Bhīṣma, Prahlāda, Uddhava and Nārada call *bhakti*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.4.2)

@9
by some  good fortune  of some  jīva  faith  if  arises
then  that  jīva  the company of sādhus  keeps
= If by some good fortune faith (*śraddhā*) arises in a *jīva*, that *jīva* seeks the company of *sādhus*.

@10
the company of sādhus  from  arise  hearing and chanting
through sādhana-bhakti  there is  the removal of all anarthas
= From the company of *sādhus* come hearing and chanting (*śravaṇa-kīrtana*), and by *sādhana-bhakti* all *anarthas* are removed.

@11
of anarthas  removal  when there is  in bhakti  steadiness  arises
steadiness  from  for hearing and the rest  taste  is born
= When the *anarthas* have been removed, steadiness (*niṣṭhā*) in *bhakti* arises. From steadiness, taste (*ruci*) for hearing and the other practices is born.

@12
taste  from  for bhakti  there is  attachment  deep
attachment  from  in the heart  is born  for Kṛṣṇa  the sprout of love
= From taste comes deep attachment (*āsakti*) to *bhakti*, and from attachment the sprout of love for Kṛṣṇa is born in the heart.

@13
that  rati  deep  when it becomes  takes  the name prema
that  prema  the goal  of all bliss  the abode
= When that *rati* deepens, it takes the name *prema*. That *prema* is the ultimate goal (*prayojana*), the abode of all bliss.

@14
first  faith  then  with sādhus
association  then  of worship  the practice
then  the removal of anarthas  comes
then  steadiness  taste  then
= “First comes faith, then the company of *sādhus*, and then the practice of worship (*bhajana-kriyā*). From it comes the removal of *anarthas*, then steadiness, and after that taste.

@15
then attachment  then  bhāva
then  prema arises
of practitioners  this  of prema
in the appearance  is  the order
= “Then come attachment and *bhāva*, and then *prema* arises. This is the order in which *prema* appears in those who practise *sādhana*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.4.15–16)

@16
of the saints  in the company  My  prowess  revealing
become  to heart and ear  delightful  talks
by relishing them  quickly  on the path of liberation
faith,  attraction,  love  will follow in turn
= (Lord Kapila to His mother Devahūti:) “In the company of the saints there are talks revealing My power, delightful to the heart and to the ear. By relishing them one quickly comes to faith in Me, the path of liberation, and after it to attraction (*rati*) and love (*prema-bhakti*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.25)

@17
in whose  heart  this  sprout of bhāva  appears
in him  these  signs  all the scriptures  declare
= All the scriptures describe these signs in one in whose heart this sprout of *bhāva* has appeared.

@18-19
forbearance  time not wasted
detachment  freedom from pride
the bond of hope  eager longing
for singing the name  always  taste
attachment  to describing His qualities
love  for the places where He dwelt
these and others  the anubhāvas  are
in whom the sprout of bhāva has arisen  in a person
= “Forbearance, never wasting time, detachment, freedom from pride, firm hope (*āśā-bandha*), eager longing (*samutkaṇṭhā*), a constant taste for singing the holy name, attachment to describing His qualities and love for the places where He dwelt — these and other *anubhāvas* are seen in a person in whom the sprout of *bhāva* has arisen.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.3.25–26)

@20
this  new  sprout of love  in whose  heart  appears
by worldly agitation  his  agitation  not  arises
= One in whose heart this new sprout of love has appeared is not disturbed by anything worldly.

@21
that  me  surrendered  may know  O brāhmaṇas
Gaṅgā  and  the goddess  whose heart is fixed  on the Lord
sent by a brāhmaṇa  a magic creature  Takṣaka  or
let it bite  as it will  sing  the narrations of Viṣṇu
= “O *brāhmaṇas*, and you, goddess Gaṅgā, know me as one who has surrendered, whose heart is fixed on the Lord. Let a magic creature sent by the *brāhmaṇa*, or Takṣaka himself, bite me as it will — you go on singing the narrations of Viṣṇu.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.19.15)

@22
connection with Kṛṣṇa  without  time  wasted  not  goes
worldly enjoyment  mystic powers  sense objects  to him  not  appeal
= Not a moment of his time is wasted without connection with Kṛṣṇa. Worldly enjoyment, mystic powers and the objects of the senses do not appeal to him.

@23
with words  praising  with the mind  remembering
with the body  bowing down  even  constantly  not  satisfied
the devotees  with tears flowing from their eyes  their whole
life  to Hari  alone  offer
= “Constantly praising Him with words, remembering Him with the mind and bowing down to Him with the body, the devotees are still not satisfied. With tears flowing from their eyes, they offer their whole life to Hari alone.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 12.38)

@24
who  hard to give up  wife and sons
friends and kingdom  dear to the heart
gave up  while young  even  like filth
for Uttamaśloka  longing
= “Longing for Uttamaśloka, Bharata gave up, while still young, like so much filth, his wife and sons, his friends and kingdom — all dear to his heart and so hard to give up.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.14.43)

@25
the best of all  himself  low  making  considers
Kṛṣṇa  mercy  will show  firmly  he knows
= Though he is the best of all, he considers himself lowly, and he knows firmly that Kṛṣṇa will show him mercy.

@26
for Hari  rati  bearing  this one
of kings  the crest jewel
alms  wandering for  in the city of his enemies
a dog-eater  even  honours
= “This crest jewel of kings, bearing *rati* for Hari, wandered begging alms in the city of his enemies and offered respect even to a dog-eater.” (Padma Purāṇa)

@27
not  prema  bhakti of hearing and the rest  even  or  yoga  or else  Vaiṣṇava
knowledge  or  pious work  or  any  alas  good birth  even  is  or
to the lowly granting more than their due  in You  yet  with roots unbreakable  being
O  Beloved of the gopīs  torments  alas  alas  my hope  indeed  me
= “I have no *prema*, no *bhakti* of hearing and the rest, no Vaiṣṇava *yoga*, no knowledge, no pious deeds — alas, not even a good birth. Yet, O Beloved of the *gopīs*, my hope in You, who grant the lowly more than they deserve, has roots that cannot be cut, and it torments me — alas, alas!” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.3.35)

@28
eager longing  is  always  craving  dominant
for singing the name  always  taste  takes  Kṛṣṇa’s name
= In eager longing (*samutkaṇṭhā*) craving always predominates. Having a constant taste for singing the name, he chants the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@29
Your boyhood  of the three worlds a wonder  so  know
My restlessness  and  to You  or  to Me  alone  known
so  what  shall I do  in solitude  O flute-player
charming  lotus face  to behold  with My two eyes
= “O flute-player, know that Your boyish charm and My restlessness are wonders of the three worlds, known only to You and Me. What then shall I do to gaze on Your charming lotus face with My own eyes, when We are alone?” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 32)

@30
of tears  drops  like honey  pouring  eyes  blue lotuses  today  O Govinda
Your  with a sweet voice  sings  the string of names  the girl
= “O Govinda, today this young girl, Her blue-lotus eyes shedding drops of tears like honey, sings the string of Your names in a sweet voice.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.3.38)

@31
to describing Kṛṣṇa’s qualities  there is  always  attachment
in the places of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  he makes  always  his dwelling
= He is always attached to describing Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, and he always lives in the places of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.

@32
sweet  sweet  the form  of this  Lord
sweet  sweet  the face  sweet
fragrant with honey  the soft smile  this  oh
sweet  sweet  sweet  sweet
= “Sweet, sweet is the form of this Lord; sweet, sweet is His face — so sweet! And this soft smile, fragrant with honey — oh, it is sweet, sweet, sweet, sweet!” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 92)

@33
when I  on the bank of the Yamunā
the names  Your  chanting
with tears  O lotus-eyed
shall perform  a dance
= “O lotus-eyed Lord, when shall I dance on the bank of the Yamunā, chanting Your names with tears in my eyes?” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.156)

@34
for Kṛṣṇa  of rati  the signs  these  I have given  the description
of love for Kṛṣṇa  the signs  now  hear  Sanātana
= I have described these signs of *rati* for Kṛṣṇa. Now, Sanātana, hear the signs of love for Kṛṣṇa (*kṛṣṇa-prema*).

@35
in whose  heart  love for Kṛṣṇa  makes  its rising
his  words  actions  ways  even the wise  not  understand
= Even the wise cannot understand the words, actions and ways of one in whose heart love for Kṛṣṇa has risen.

@36
of the fortunate  this  new prema
in whose  blossoms  heart
by knowers of the scriptures  even  his
ways  quite  very hard to understand
= “The ways of the fortunate soul in whose heart this new *prema* has blossomed are very hard to understand, even for those who know the scriptures.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.4.17)

@37
one with such a vow  by the chanting of the names of his beloved
in whom love has arisen  with melted heart  loudly
laughs  then  weeps  cries out  sings
like a madman  dances  beyond the world
= “One who has taken this vow, chanting the names of his beloved Lord, finds love awakening within him; his heart melts, and, heedless of the world, he laughs aloud, weeps, cries out, sings and dances like a madman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.2.40)

@38
prema  gradually  increasing  becomes  sneha  māna  praṇaya
rāga  anurāga  bhāva  mahābhāva  are
= As *prema* gradually increases, it becomes *sneha*, *māna*, *praṇaya*, *rāga*, *anurāga*, *bhāva* and *mahābhāva*.

@39
as  the seed  the sugarcane  the juice  molasses  raw sugar  refined sugar
white sugar  sugar candy  rock candy  pure rock candy  and
= It is like the seed, the sugarcane, its juice, molasses, raw sugar, refined sugar, white sugar, sugar candy, rock candy and pure rock candy.

@40
these  as  step by step  become pure  step by step  increases  the taste
of rati, prema and the rest  so  increases  the relish
= Just as these become purer step by step and their taste increases, so the relish of *rati*, *prema* and the rest increases.

@41
by the difference of those qualified  rati  five  kinds
śānta  dāsya  sakhya  vātsalya  madhura  and
= According to the differences among those qualified for it, *rati* is of five kinds: *śānta*, *dāsya*, *sakhya*, *vātsalya* and *madhura*.

@42
these  five  from sthāyī-bhāvas  become  five  rasas
in which rasa  the devotee  happy  Kṛṣṇa  becomes  controlled
= These five *sthāyī-bhāvas* become the five *rasas*, in which the devotee is happy and Kṛṣṇa comes under his control.

@43
prema and the rest  sthāyī-bhāva  with the ingredients  on meeting
devotion to Kṛṣṇa  the form of rasa  attains  in its transformation
= When the *sthāyī-bhāva* — *prema* and the rest — meets with its ingredients (*sāmagrī*), devotion to Kṛṣṇa is transformed into *rasa*.

@44
vibhāva  anubhāva  sāttvika  vyabhicārī
sthāyī-bhāva  rasa  becomes  these  four  meeting
= *Vibhāva*, *anubhāva*, *sāttvika* and *vyabhicārī*: when these four combine with it, the *sthāyī-bhāva* becomes *rasa*.

@45
yogurt  as  with sugar, pepper and camphor  on mixing
called rasālā  a drink  becomes  of unprecedented taste
= When yogurt is mixed with sugar, black pepper and camphor, it becomes the drink called *rasālā*, whose taste is like nothing else.

@46
two kinds  vibhāva  ālambana  uddīpana
the sound of the flute and the rest  uddīpana  Kṛṣṇa and others  ālambana
= *Vibhāva* is of two kinds: the support (*ālambana*) and the stimulus (*uddīpana*). The sound of the flute and the like are *uddīpana*; Kṛṣṇa and others are *ālambana*.

@47
anubhāva  smiling  dancing  singing and the rest  outward signs
being stunned and the rest  sāttvika  of anubhāvas  among
= The *anubhāvas* are smiling, dancing, singing and other outward signs (*udbhāsvara*). Being stunned (*stambha*) and the rest, the *sāttvika* emotions, are counted among the *anubhāvas*.

@48
despondency, joy and the rest  thirty-three  vyabhicārī
all  combining  rasa  becomes  wonder-producing
= Despondency (*nirveda*), joy (*harṣa*) and the rest are the thirty-three *vyabhicārī-bhāvas*. When all these combine, *rasa* becomes wonderful.

@49
of five kinds  rasa  śānta  dāsya  sakhya  vātsalya
in madhura-rasa  of the amorous mood  predominance
= *Rasa* is of five kinds: *śānta*, *dāsya*, *sakhya*, *vātsalya* and *madhura*. In *madhura-rasa* the amorous mood (*śṛṅgāra*) predominates.

@50
in śānta-rasa  śānti-rati  prema  up to  goes
dāsya-rati  rāga  up to  gradually  increases
= In *śānta-rasa*, *śānti-rati* develops as far as *prema*; *dāsya-rati* gradually increases as far as *rāga*.

@51
sakhya- and vātsalya-rati  reaches  anurāga  the limit
of Subala and others  bhāva  up to  of prema  the glory
= *Sakhya-rati* and *vātsalya-rati* reach the limit of *anurāga*. In Subala and his companions the glory of *prema* extends as far as *bhāva*.

@52
of śānta and the rest  of the rasas  union  separation  two  divisions
in sakhya and vātsalya  of union and the rest  many  subdivisions
= The *rasas* beginning with *śānta* have two divisions, union (*yoga*) and separation (*viyoga*). In *sakhya* and *vātsalya*, union and separation have many subdivisions.

@53
rūḍha  adhirūḍha  bhāva  only  in madhura
of the queens  rūḍha  adhirūḍha  in the host of gopīs
= The *bhāvas* called *rūḍha* and *adhirūḍha* are found only in *madhura-rasa*: *rūḍha* in the queens, and *adhirūḍha* in the *gopīs*.

@54
adhirūḍha-mahābhāva  two  indeed  kinds
in union  mādana  in separation  mohana  the name  its
= *Adhirūḍha-mahābhāva* is of two kinds: in union it is called *mādana*, and in separation *mohana*.

@55
in mādana  kissing and the rest  are  endless  varieties
udghūrṇā  citra-jalpa  in mohana  two  divisions
= In *mādana* there are endless varieties, such as kissing. *Mohana* has two divisions: *udghūrṇā* and *citra-jalpa*.

@56
of citra-jalpa  ten  parts  prajalpa and the rest  by name
of the Bhramara-gītā  ten  verses  of this  are the evidence
= *Citra-jalpa* has ten parts, named *prajalpa* and so on. The ten verses of the *Bhramara-gītā*, the Song to the Bee, are the evidence of this.

@57
udghūrṇā  helpless actions  divine madness  by name
in separation  Kṛṣṇa’s appearing  oneself  as Kṛṣṇa  thinking
= *Udghūrṇā* and helpless actions (*vivaśa-ceṣṭā*) are called divine madness (*divyonmāda*). In separation Kṛṣṇa appears before her, and she even takes herself to be Kṛṣṇa.

@58
union  separation  by the division  two kinds  śṛṅgāra
of union  endless  aspects  no  end  of it
= *Śṛṅgāra* is of two kinds, union (*sambhoga*) and separation (*vipralambha*). Union has endless aspects; there is no end to them.

@59
separation  four kinds  pūrva-rāga  māna
called pravāsa  and  called prema-vaicittya
= Separation (*vipralambha*) is of four kinds: longing before meeting (*pūrva-rāga*), loving pique (*māna*), separation by distance (*pravāsa*) and the feeling of separation even in the beloved’s presence (*prema-vaicittya*).

@60
in Rādhikā and others  pūrva-rāga  well known  pravāsa  māna
prema-vaicittya  in the Tenth (Canto)  in the queens
= *Pūrva-rāga*, *pravāsa* and *māna* are well known in Rādhikā and the other *gopīs*; *prema-vaicittya* is seen in the queens, as the Tenth Canto describes.
