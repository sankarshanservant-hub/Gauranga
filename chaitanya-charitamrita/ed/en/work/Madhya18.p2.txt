# CC Madhya 18, stage A, part 2 (verses 115–229); read by build_en.py together with Madhya18.txt

@115
who  fool  says  the jīva  the Lord  is  equal
he  indeed  a pāṣaṇḍī  is  punishes  him  Yama
= “The fool who says that the jīva is ‘equal’ to the Lord is a ‘pāṣaṇḍī,’ and Yama punishes him.

@116
who  but  Nārāyaṇa  the Lord
with Brahmā, Rudra and other  gods
as equal  indeed  sees
he  a pāṣaṇḍī  becomes  surely
= ‘One who regards the Lord Nārāyaṇa as merely equal to Brahmā, Rudra and the other gods is surely a pāṣaṇḍī.’” (Vaiṣṇava-tantra)

@117
people  say  about You  ever  is not  of a jīva  the notion
Kṛṣṇa’s  like  Your  form  nature
= The people said, “We never think of You as a jīva. Your form and nature are like Kṛṣṇa’s.

@118
by form  You  we see  the son of the king of Vraja
of the body  luster  a yellow garment  made  covering
= “By Your features we see that You are the son of the king of Vraja; the luster of Your body has only covered You like a yellow garment.

@119
musk  in cloth  they tie  still  not  hides
the Lord’s  nature  Your  to cover  not  is possible
= “Musk may be tied up in cloth, yet it does not stay hidden; just so, Your nature as the Lord cannot be concealed.

@120
extraordinary  nature  Your  intellect  beyond the reach of
You  seeing  with love for Kṛṣṇa  the world  mad
= “Your nature is extraordinary, beyond the reach of the intellect. Seeing You, the world goes mad with love for Kṛṣṇa.

@121-122
women  children  the old  and  caṇḍālas  yavanas
who  You  once  gets  sight
Kṛṣṇa’s name  takes  dances  becoming  mad
an ācārya  became  he  delivered  the world
= “Women, children and old men, even caṇḍālas and yavanas — whoever sees You even once takes the name of Kṛṣṇa and dances as if mad. He becomes an ācārya and delivers the world.

@123
of seeing  the matter  let it be  who  Your  name  hears
he  with love for Kṛṣṇa  mad  delivers  the three worlds
= “Let alone seeing You — whoever only hears Your name becomes mad with love for Kṛṣṇa and delivers the three worlds.

@124
Your  name  hearing  becomes  a dog-eater  a purifier
extraordinary  power  Your  not  can  be told
= “Hearing Your name, even a dog-eater becomes a purifier of others. Your extraordinary power cannot be described.

@125
whose  name  from hearing and chanting
to whom  from bowing  of whom  from remembering  even  once
a dog-eater  even  at once  for Vedic sacrifice  becomes fit
how much more  then  You  O Lord  indeed  from seeing
= “O Lord, if even a dog-eater hears and chants Your name, bows down to You or remembers You but once, he is instantly made fit to perform Vedic sacrifice. How much more, then, one who sees You!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.33.6)

@126
such  glory  Your  marginal  characteristic
by the essential  characteristic  You  the son of the king of Vraja
= “Such glories are Your marginal characteristics (*taṭastha-lakṣaṇa*); by Your essential characteristic (*svarūpa-lakṣaṇa*) You are the son of the king of Vraja.”

@127
those  all  to people  the Lord  mercy  did
with love for Kṛṣṇa  mad  the people  to their own homes  went
= The Lord showed mercy to all those people, and, mad with love for Kṛṣṇa, they went back to their homes.

@128
in this way  some days  at Akrūra  He stayed
Kṛṣṇa’s name  and love  giving  the people  He delivered
= In this way He stayed some days at Akrūra, delivering the people by giving them the name of Kṛṣṇa and love for Him.

@129
of Mādhava Purī  the disciple  that  very  brāhmaṇa
of Mathurā  in house after house  has made  invitations
= That brāhmaṇa, the disciple of Mādhava Purī, had invitations for the Lord arranged from house to house in Mathurā.

@130
of Mathurā  all  people  brāhmaṇas  the respectable
at Bhaṭṭācārya’s place  coming  make  invitation
= All the people of Mathurā — brāhmaṇas and respectable folk — came to Bhaṭṭācārya to invite the Lord.

@131
in one day  ten  twenty  come  invitations
Bhaṭṭācārya  of one  only  makes  acceptance
= In a single day ten or twenty invitations would come, but Bhaṭṭācārya would accept only one.

@132
chance  not  get  people  invitation  to give
that  brāhmaṇa  entreat  people  invitation  to accept
= People could not find a chance to give their invitations, so they pleaded with that brāhmaṇa to have them accepted.

@133
of Kānyakubja and the south  Vedic  brāhmaṇas
humility  showing  make  Mahāprabhu’s  invitation
= Vedic brāhmaṇas of Kānyakubja and of the south humbly invited Mahāprabhu.

@134
in the morning  to Akrūra  coming  cooking  doing
to the Lord  a meal  give  to the śālagrāma  offering
= They would come to Akrūra in the morning and cook, and after offering the food to the śālagrāma they would give it to the Lord as His meal.

@135
one day  that  of Akrūra-ghāṭa  on top
sitting  Mahāprabhu  something  does  reflection
= One day Mahāprabhu sat at that Akrūra-ghāṭa and reflected:

@136
at this  ghāṭa  Akrūra  Vaikuṇṭha  saw
of Vraja  the people  of Goloka  a vision  had
= “At this ghāṭa Akrūra saw Vaikuṇṭha, and the people of Vraja had a vision of Goloka.”

@137
thus  saying  a leap  gave  the water  onto
sinking  remained  the Lord  the water  within
= With these words He leapt into the water, and the Lord stayed submerged beneath it.

@138
seeing  Kṛṣṇadāsa  weeping  a cry  made
Bhaṭṭācārya  quickly  coming  the Lord  lifted out
= Seeing this, Kṛṣṇadāsa wept and cried out, and Bhaṭṭācārya quickly came and pulled the Lord out.

@139
then  Bhaṭṭācārya  that  brāhmaṇa  taking
counsel  made  some  in seclusion  sitting
= Then Bhaṭṭācārya took that brāhmaṇa aside, and sitting in a secluded place they made a plan.

@140
today  I  was (here)  I lifted out  the Lord
in Vṛndāvana  He sinks  if  who  will lift out  Him
= “Today I was here, and I pulled the Lord out. But if He dives in at Vṛndāvana, who will pull Him out?

@141
of people  the crowding  and  of invitations  the bother
constant  absorption  of the Lord  not  I see  good
= “The crowds of people, the bother of invitations, the Lord’s constant absorption — I do not think it good.

@142
from Vṛndāvana  if  the Lord  we take away
then  good  will be  this  good  plan  is
= “If we take the Lord away from Vṛndāvana, all will be well. This is a good plan.”

@143
the brāhmaṇa  says  to Prayāga  the Lord  taking  let us go
along the Ganges’ bank  by the road  let us go  then  happiness  we get
= The brāhmaṇa said, “Let us take the Lord to Prayāga. If we go by the road along the bank of the Ganges, the journey will be pleasant.

@144
to Soro-kṣetra  first  going  doing  bath in the Ganges
by that  road  the Lord  taking  let us make  the journey
= “Let us first go to Soro-kṣetra and bathe in the Ganges, and then travel on that road with the Lord.

@145
the month of Māgha  has begun  now  if  we go
in Makara  bath at Prayāga  some  days  we get
= “The month of Māgha has begun. If we go now, we shall have some days of bathing at Prayāga during Makara.

@146
your own  distress  some  making  submission
the full moon of Makara  at Prayāga  do  mention
= “Tell Him something of your own distress, and mention the full moon of Makara (*makara-pañcasi*) at Prayāga.

@147
along the Ganges’ bank  by the road  the pleasure  make known  to Him
Bhaṭṭācārya  coming  then  spoke  to the Lord
= “Tell Him too how pleasant the road along the Ganges is.” Then Bhaṭṭācārya came and spoke to the Lord:

@148
to bear  not  can  I  of the people  the commotion
invitation  for the sake of  people  make  a scramble
= “I cannot bear the commotion of these people. They jostle one another to invite You.

@149
in the morning  come  people  You  not  find
You  not  finding  people  my  head  eat
= “People come in the morning and do not find You; and when they do not find You, they pester me to death.

@150
then  happiness  is  when  by the Ganges road  we go
now  if  we go  in Makara  bath in the Ganges  we get
= “I would be happy if we went by the Ganges road. If we go now, we shall be able to bathe in the Ganges during Makara.

@151
troubled  has become  heart  to bear  not  I can
of the Lord  whatever  order  is  that  on the head  I hold
= “My heart is troubled; I cannot bear it. Whatever the Lord commands, I will accept on my head.”

@152
although  Vṛndāvana-leaving  is not  the Lord’s  mind
the devotee’s wish  to fulfill  speaks  sweet  words
= Although the Lord had no wish to leave Vṛndāvana, to fulfill His devotee’s wish He spoke sweet words:

@153
you  Me  bringing  showed  Vṛndāvana
this  debt  I  shall be unable  to make  repayment
= “You brought Me here and showed Me Vṛndāvana. I shall never be able to repay this debt.

@154
what  your  wish  I  that  indeed  will do
where  taking  go  you  there  I will go
= “Whatever you wish, that I will do. Wherever you take Me, there I will go.”

@155
in the morning  Mahāprabhu  the morning bath  took
“Vṛndāvana  I shall leave”  knowing  absorption in love  came
= In the morning Mahāprabhu took His morning bath, and at the thought “I am leaving Vṛndāvana” He was overwhelmed with love.

@156
outward  transformations  are not  absorbed in love  the mind
Bhaṭṭācārya  says  come  let us go  to Mahāvana
= There were no outward transformations, but His heart was absorbed in love. Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Come, let us go to Mahāvana.”

@157
thus  saying  Mahāprabhu  in a boat  seating
crossing  making  Bhaṭṭācārya  went  taking (Him)
= Saying this, Bhaṭṭācārya seated Mahāprabhu in a boat, took Him across the river and set off with Him.

@158
loving  Kṛṣṇadāsa  and  that  very  brāhmaṇa
along the Ganges’ bank  by the road  for going  knowing  the two
= Kṛṣṇadāsa, full of love, and that brāhmaṇa — these two knew the road along the Ganges well.

@159
going  one  under a tree  the Lord  all  taking
sat  everyone’s  fatigue from the road  seeing
= On the way the Lord saw that everyone was tired from walking, and He sat down with them all beneath a tree.

@160
that  near the tree  graze  many  cows
that  seeing  Mahāprabhu’s  joyful  mind
= Many cows were grazing near that tree, and the sight of them filled Mahāprabhu’s heart with joy.

@161
suddenly  a  cowherd  flute  played
hearing  Mahāprabhu’s  great  absorption in love  came
= Suddenly a cowherd played on his flute, and hearing it, Mahāprabhu was overwhelmed with great love.

@162
unconscious  becoming  the Lord  to the ground  fell
from the mouth  foam  comes  in the nostrils  breath  stopped  became
= The Lord fell unconscious to the ground; foam came from His mouth, and the breath in His nostrils stopped.

@163

= Just then ten horsemen arrived there — mleccha Paṭhāns — and dismounted from their horses.

@164

this  sannyāsī  with  was  gold  boundless
= Seeing the Lord, the mlecchas reasoned: “This sannyāsī had boundless gold.

@165

= “These four highwaymen fed him dhuturā, killed him and took all the sannyāsī’s wealth.”

@166

= Then the Paṭhāns bound the four men and wanted to cut them down. The Bengalis all began to tremble.

@167

= Kṛṣṇadāsa was a Rajput and utterly fearless. That brāhmaṇa too was fearless, and very bold of speech.

= “This sannyāsī is my guru, and I am a brāhmaṇa of Mathurā. I have a hundred men at the pādśāh’s court.

= “Because of an illness this sannyāsī sometimes faints. He will soon regain consciousness and come to His senses.

@171

= “Sit here a moment and keep us all bound. Ask Him, and then kill me.”

= The Paṭhān said, “You two are Westerners from Mathurā, and these two Bengali cheats are trembling.”

@173

= Kṛṣṇadāsa said, “My home is in this village. There are two hundred Turks there and a hundred guns.

@174

= “If I give the signal, they will all come at once, kill you all and carry off your horses and saddles.

@175

= “The Bengalis are not highwaymen — you are the highwaymen! You rob pilgrims and want to kill them besides.”
