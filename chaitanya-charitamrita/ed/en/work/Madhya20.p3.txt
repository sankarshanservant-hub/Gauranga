# CC Madhya 20, stage A, part 3 (verses 271–404); read by build_en.py together with Madhya20.txt

@271
of māyā  which  two  functions  māyā  and  pradhāna
māyā  the efficient  cause  of the universe  the material cause  pradhāna
= Māyā has two functions, called māyā and *pradhāna*. Māyā is the efficient cause of the universe, and *pradhāna* is its material cause.

@272
that  Puruṣa  toward māyā  directs  His glance
prakṛti  agitated  making  makes  of the seed  the placing
= That Puruṣa turns His glance toward māyā. Agitating *prakṛti*, He deposits the seed in her.

@273
of His own body  of a particular part  of the reflection  in the form  prakṛti  touching
in the form of the jīvas  the seed  into her  He made  the offering
= In the form of a reflection of a particular part of His own body He touches *prakṛti*, and into her He placed the seed in the form of the *jīvas*.

@274
by time  in her whose qualities were agitated
in His own  womb  the Supreme  Person
placed  the seed  she  gave birth to
the mahat-tattva  golden
= “When by time (*daiva*) the qualities of nature were agitated, the Supreme Person placed the seed in His own womb, and she gave birth to the golden *mahat-tattva*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.26.19)

@275
by the action of time  indeed  in māyā
consisting of the guṇas  Adhokṣaja
through the Puruṣa  His own part
the seed  placed  the potent One
= “Through the Puruṣa, His own part, the potent Adhokṣaja, by the action of time, placed the seed in māyā, which consists of the *guṇas*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.5.26)

@276
then  the mahat-tattva  from  threefold  ego
which  from  of the gods  senses  and elements  the spreading forth
= Then from the *mahat-tattva* came the threefold ego (*ahaṅkāra*), from which the gods, the senses and the elements spread forth.

@277
all  the elements  combining  created  of universes  the multitudes
endless  universes  their  there is no  counting
= All the elements combined and created multitudes of universes — endless universes, beyond all counting.

@278
He  of the mahat  the creator  Puruṣa  Mahā-Viṣṇu  by name
endless  universes  His  in the pores of the skin  abode
= This Puruṣa, the creator of the *mahat*, is named Mahā-Viṣṇu. Countless universes dwell in the pores of His skin.

@279
through a lattice window  flying  as  specks of dust  come  go
the Puruṣa’s  breath  with  the universes  come out
= As specks of dust fly in and out through a lattice window, so the universes come out with the Puruṣa’s breath.

@280
again  with the breath  go  inside
endless  majesty  His  all  beyond māyā
= And with His breath they go back inside again. His majesty is endless, and all of it lies beyond māyā.

@281
whose  one  exhalation’s  span  then  depending on
live  from the pores  born  of the universes  the lords
Viṣṇu  the Great  He  here  whose  kalā  particular
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “The lords of the universes, born from His pores, abide for the span of one of His breaths. That Mahā-Viṣṇu is a particular kalā of Govinda, the original Person, whom I worship.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.48)

@282
of all  the universes  the multitudes  He  the indwelling Lord
Kāraṇābdhiśāyī  of all  the worlds  the master
= He is the indwelling Lord of all the multitudes of universes. Kāraṇābdhiśāyī is the master of all the worlds.

@283
thus  I have told  of the first  Puruṣa  the truth
of the second  Puruṣa  now  hear  the greatness
= Thus I have told you the truth about the first Puruṣa. Now hear of the greatness of the second Puruṣa.

@284
that  Puruṣa  endless millions  of universes  having created
in each one  with a form  entered  many  forms  becoming
= Having created endless millions of universes, that Puruṣa became many forms and entered each universe in a form of His own.

@285
entering  He sees  everything  darkness
to stay  there is no  place  He made  deliberation
= Entering, He saw that all was darkness and there was no place to stay, and He reflected.

@286
with the water of His own body’s perspiration  half the universe  He filled
in that  water  on Śeṣa  as a bed  lying down  He did
= With the water of His own body’s perspiration He filled half the universe, and on those waters He lay down on the bed of Śeṣa.

@287
His  from the navel-lotus  arose  one  lotus
that  lotus  became  of Brahmā  birth  the abode
= From His navel-lotus a lotus arose, and that lotus became the birthplace of Brahmā.

@288
in that  lotus  stem  came to be  the fourteen  worlds
He  Brahmā  becoming  creation  made  bringing forth
= In the stem of that lotus the fourteen worlds came to be, and He, becoming Brahmā, brought forth the creation.

@289
Viṣṇu’s  form  taking  makes  of the world  maintenance
beyond the guṇas  Viṣṇu  touch  there is no  with māyā
= Taking the form of Viṣṇu, He maintains the world. Viṣṇu is beyond the *guṇas*; He has no contact with māyā.

@290
Rudra’s  form  assuming  makes  of the world  destruction
creation  maintenance  dissolution  take place  by the will  of whom
= Assuming the form of Rudra, He destroys the world. Creation, maintenance and dissolution all take place by His will.

@291
Brahmā  Viṣṇu  Śiva  His  guṇa-avatāras
of creation  maintenance  and dissolution  of the three  the charge
= Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva are His *guṇa-avatāras*. The three are in charge of creation, maintenance and dissolution.

@292
of Hiraṇyagarbha  the indwelling Lord  Garbhodakaśāyī
‘thousand-headed’ and so on  calling  in the Vedas  whom  they sing
= Garbhodakaśāyī is the indwelling Lord of Hiraṇyagarbha. The Vedas sing of Him in the hymn that begins “thousand-headed” (*sahasra-śīrṣā*) and in others.

@293
this  second Puruṣa  of the universe  the Lord
of māyā  the shelter  He is  yet  beyond māyā
= This second Puruṣa is the Lord of the universe. He is the shelter of māyā, yet He is beyond māyā.

@294
the third Puruṣa  Viṣṇu  a guṇa-avatāra
two  avatāras  among  the counting  of Him
= The third Puruṣa, Viṣṇu, is a *guṇa-avatāra*; He is counted among both kinds of avatāras.

@295
of the virāṭ  of the individual jīvas  He  the indwelling Lord
Kṣīrodakaśāyī  He  the maintainer  the master
= He is the indwelling Lord of the *virāṭ* and of the individual *jīvas*. He is Kṣīrodakaśāyī, the maintainer and master.

@296
of the Puruṣa-avatāras  this  I have made  the description
the līlā-avatāras  now  hear  Sanātana
= Thus I have described the Puruṣa-avatāras. Now, Sanātana, hear about the *līlā-avatāras*.

@297
the līlā-avatāras  of Kṛṣṇa  not  can be  counted
the chief  taking  I tell  a glimpse
= Kṛṣṇa’s *līlā-avatāras* cannot be counted. I shall give only a glimpse, naming the chief ones.

@298
Matsya  Kūrma  Raghunātha  Nṛsiṁha  Vāmana
Varāha and others  writing  of whom  not  can be  counted
= Matsya, Kūrma, Raghunātha, Nṛsiṁha, Vāmana, Varāha and others — they are too many to write down and count.

@299
as the Fish  the Horse  the Tortoise  Nṛsiṁha  the Boar  the Swan
among kings  brāhmaṇas  and gods  who has descended
You  protect  us  the three worlds  and  so  now  O Lord
the burden  of the earth  remove  O best of the Yadus  obeisance  to You
= “O Lord, You descended among kings, brāhmaṇas and gods, and as the Fish, the Horse, the Tortoise, Nṛsiṁha, the Boar and the Swan, and You protect us and the three worlds. So now, O best of the Yadus, remove the burden of the earth. We bow down to You.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.40)

@300
of the līlā-avatāras  I have given  a glimpse
of the guṇa-avatāras  now  hear  the account
= I have given you a glimpse of the *līlā-avatāras*. Now hear the account of the *guṇa-avatāras*.

@301
Brahmā  Viṣṇu  Śiva  three  guṇa-avatāras
the three guṇas  accepting  they carry out  creation and so on  the business
= Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva are the three *guṇa-avatāras*. Accepting the three *guṇas*, they carry out creation and the rest.

@302
with devotion  mixed  performed  through piety  some  excellent jīva
with rajo-guṇa  imbued  making  his  mind
= Through piety mixed with devotion, some excellent *jīva* has his mind imbued with *rajo-guṇa*,

@303
Garbhodakaśāyī  through  power  infusing
the individual  creation  makes  Kṛṣṇa  Brahmā’s  form  assuming
= and, infusing His power into him through Garbhodakaśāyī, Kṛṣṇa assumes the form of Brahmā and brings forth the individual creation.

@304
the sun  as  in stones  its own  radiance
its own  some  manifests  also  likewise  here
Brahmā  who  this  of the universe  of the ordering  the maker
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “As the sun displays something of its own radiance in certain gems that are its own, so here He becomes Brahmā, who orders the universe. I worship Govinda, that original Person.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.49)

@305
in some  kalpa  if  a qualified  jīva  not  is found
Himself  the Lord  then  by a part  Brahmā  becomes
= If in some *kalpa* no qualified *jīva* is found, then the Lord Himself becomes Brahmā by His part.

@306
whose  feet  lotus  the dust  of all  the worlds  by the guardians
on heads  best  borne  the worshipped  of holy places  the holy place
Brahmā  Śiva  I  also  whose  parts  of a part
Śrī  and  we bear  ever  His  royal throne  what
= (Baladeva said:) “The guardians of all the worlds bear on their crowned heads the dust of His lotus feet, the holiest of holy places; and Brahmā, Śiva, Lakṣmī and I Myself, mere parts of His part, carry it on our heads forever. What then is a king’s throne to Him?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.68.37)

@307
of His own part  by a kalā  Kṛṣṇa  tamo-guṇa  accepts
for destruction  in association with māyā  Rudra’s  form  assumes
= By a *kalā* of His own part, Kṛṣṇa accepts *tamo-guṇa*, and for the sake of destruction, in association with māyā, He assumes the form of Rudra.

@308
of association with māyā  through the transformation  Rudra  different and non-different  a form
jīva-tattva  is  not  Kṛṣṇa’s  svarūpa
= Through the transformation caused by association with māyā, Rudra is a form both different and non-different. He is counted as *jīva-tattva*; he is not Kṛṣṇa’s own form (*svarūpa*).

@309
milk  as  through contact with a sour agent  of curd  the form  takes
other than milk  a thing  is not  milk  to be  cannot
= As milk takes the form of curd through contact with a sour agent — curd is nothing other than milk, yet it cannot be milk.

@310
milk  as  curd  of a particular change  through contact
becomes  not  yet  from that  separate  is  cause
who  the state of Śambhu  also  likewise  attains  for a purpose
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “As milk becomes curd through contact with a particular agent of change, yet the curd is not separate from its cause, so He, for a particular task, assumes the state of Śambhu (Śiva). I worship Govinda, that original Person.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.45)

@311
Śiva  of māyā-śakti  the companion  absorbed in tamo-guṇa
beyond māyā  beyond the guṇas  Viṣṇu  the Supreme Lord
= Śiva is the companion of *māyā-śakti* and is absorbed in *tamo-guṇa*, whereas Viṣṇu, the Supreme Lord, is beyond māyā and beyond the *guṇas*.

@312
Śiva  with śakti  united  eternally
with three marks  by the guṇas  covered
vaikārika  taijasa  and
tāmasa  and  thus  the ego  threefold
= “Śiva is eternally united with *śakti*. Covered by the *guṇas*, he bears three marks: he is the threefold ego — *vaikārika*, *taijasa* and *tāmasa*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.88.3)

@313
Hari  indeed  beyond the guṇas  directly
the Person  to prakṛti  transcendent
He  all-seeing  the witness
Him  worshipping  free of the guṇas  one becomes
= “Hari is directly beyond the *guṇas*: He is the Person transcendent to *prakṛti*. He sees all and witnesses all. One who worships Him becomes free of the *guṇas*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.88.5)

@314
for maintenance  His own part  in the form of Viṣṇu  descent
of sattva-guṇa  the overseer  therefore  the guṇa-māyā  beyond
= For maintenance He descends as His own part (*svāṁśa*) in the form of Viṣṇu. He is the overseer of *sattva-guṇa*, and therefore He is beyond the māyā of the *guṇas*.

@315
His nature  full of majesty  equal to Kṛṣṇa  almost
Kṛṣṇa  the whole  He  a part  in the Vedas  so  they sing
= By nature He is full of majesty, almost equal to Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa is the whole (*aṁśī*), and He is a part (*aṁśa*) — so the Vedas sing.

@316
the flame of a lamp  indeed  surely  another wick  having reached
shines as a lamp  with its manifest cause  of the same qualities
who  just so  indeed  and  as Viṣṇu  shines forth
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “As the flame of one lamp, passing to another wick, shines as a lamp with the same qualities as the lamp it came from, so He shines forth as Viṣṇu. I worship Govinda, that original Person.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.46)

@317
Brahmā  Śiva  orders  carrying out  bhakta-avatāras
for maintenance  Viṣṇu  Kṛṣṇa’s  own  form
= Brahmā and Śiva are *bhakta-avatāras* who carry out His orders, while Viṣṇu, who maintains the world, is Kṛṣṇa’s own form (*svarūpa*).

@318
I create  by Him  appointed  I
Hara  destroys  under His control
the universe  in the form of the Puruṣa
maintains  three  potencies  the bearer of
= “Appointed by Him, I create; under His control Hara (Śiva) destroys; and He, the bearer of the three potencies, maintains the universe in the form of the Puruṣa.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.6.32)

@319
the manvantara-avatāras  now  hear  Sanātana
countless  the number  of them  hear  the reason
= Now, Sanātana, hear about the *manvantara-avatāras*. Their number is countless — hear the reason.

@320
Brahmā’s  in one day  there are  fourteen  manvantaras
these  fourteen  avatāras  there  makes  the Lord
= In one day of Brahmā there are fourteen *manvantaras*, and in them the Lord brings forth these fourteen avatāras.

@321
fourteen  in one  day  in a month  four hundred  twenty
Brahmā’s  in a year  five thousand  forty
= Fourteen in one day, four hundred and twenty in a month, and five thousand and forty in a year of Brahmā.

@322
a hundred  years  is  the life  of Brahmā
five hundred thousand  four thousand  manvantara-avatāras
= Brahmā’s life lasts a hundred years; so there are 504,000 *manvantara-avatāras*.

@323
in the endless  universes  thus  make  the count
of Mahā-Viṣṇu  in one breath  of Brahmā  the life
= Count them in this way in the endless universes — and Brahmā’s whole life lasts but one breath of Mahā-Viṣṇu.

@324
of Mahā-Viṣṇu  of the breaths  there is no  limit
of the manvantara-avatāras alone  see  of the writing  the end
= There is no limit to Mahā-Viṣṇu’s breaths. See, then, how there could be an end to writing of the *manvantara-avatāras* alone!

@325
in Svāyambhuva  Yajña  in Svārociṣa  Vibhu  by name
in Uttama  Satyasena  in Tāmasa  Hari  the name
= In the Svāyambhuva *manvantara* the avatāra is named Yajña; in the Svārociṣa, Vibhu; in the Uttama, Satyasena; and in the Tāmasa, Hari.

@326

= In the Raivata, Vaikuṇṭha; in the Cākṣuṣa, Ajita; in the Vaivasvata, Vāmana; in the Sāvarṇya, Sārvabhauma; and in the Dakṣa-sāvarṇya, Ṛṣabha is counted.

= In the Brahma-sāvarṇya, Viṣvaksena; in the Dharma-sāvarṇya, Dharmasetu; in the Rudra-sāvarṇya, Sudhāmā; and in the Deva-sāvarṇya, Yogeśvara.

@328

= In the Indra-sāvarṇya the name is Bṛhadbhānu. These are the names of the fourteen avatāras in the fourteen *manvantaras*.

@329

= Now, Sanātana, hear about the *yuga-avatāras*. The *yugas* are counted as Satya, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali.

@330

four  colours  assuming  Kṛṣṇa  establishes  the dharma of the age
= White, red, black and yellow — these are the four colours in turn. Assuming these four colours, Kṛṣṇa establishes the dharma of each age.

@331

accepting  in each age  bodies
white  red  likewise  yellow
now  blackness  has attained
= “Your son, who accepts a body in every age, has appeared in three colours — white, red and yellow; now He has become dark (*kṛṣṇa*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.13)

@332

to Kardama  a boon  gave  who  mercy  showing
= In Satya-yuga, assuming a white form, He has people practise the dharma of meditation. It was He who mercifully gave Kardama a boon.

@338

@340
