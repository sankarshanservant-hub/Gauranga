# CC Madhya 20, stage A, part 3 (verses 271–404); read by build_en.py together with Madhya20.txt

@271
of māyā  which  two  functions  māyā  and  pradhāna
māyā  the efficient  cause  of the universe  the material cause  pradhāna
= Māyā has two functions, called māyā and *pradhāna*. Māyā is the efficient cause of the universe, and *pradhāna* is its material cause.

@272
that  Puruṣa  toward māyā  directs  His glance
prakṛti  agitated  making  makes  of the seed  the placing
= That Puruṣa turns His glance toward māyā. Agitating *prakṛti*, He deposits the seed in her.

@273
of His own body  of a particular part  of the reflection  in the form  prakṛti  touching
in the form of the jīvas  the seed  into her  He made  the offering
= In the form of a reflection of a particular part of His own body He touches *prakṛti*, and into her He placed the seed in the form of the *jīvas*.

@274
by time  in her whose qualities were agitated
in His own  womb  the Supreme  Person
placed  the seed  she  gave birth to
the mahat-tattva  golden
= “When by time (*daiva*) the qualities of nature were agitated, the Supreme Person placed the seed in His own womb, and she gave birth to the golden *mahat-tattva*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.26.19)

@275
by the action of time  indeed  in māyā
consisting of the guṇas  Adhokṣaja
through the Puruṣa  His own part
the seed  placed  the potent One
= “Through the Puruṣa, His own part, the potent Adhokṣaja, by the action of time, placed the seed in māyā, which consists of the *guṇas*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.5.26)

@276
then  the mahat-tattva  from  threefold  ego
which  from  of the gods  senses  and elements  the spreading forth
= Then from the *mahat-tattva* came the threefold ego (*ahaṅkāra*), from which the gods, the senses and the elements spread forth.

@277
all  the elements  combining  created  of universes  the multitudes
endless  universes  their  there is no  counting
= All the elements combined and created multitudes of universes — endless universes, beyond all counting.

@278
He  of the mahat  the creator  Puruṣa  Mahā-Viṣṇu  by name
endless  universes  His  in the pores of the skin  abode
= This Puruṣa, the creator of the *mahat*, is named Mahā-Viṣṇu. Countless universes dwell in the pores of His skin.

@279
through a lattice window  flying  as  specks of dust  come  go
the Puruṣa’s  breath  with  the universes  come out
= As specks of dust fly in and out through a lattice window, so the universes come out with the Puruṣa’s breath.

@280
again  with the breath  go  inside
endless  majesty  His  all  beyond māyā
= And with His breath they go back inside again. His majesty is endless, and all of it lies beyond māyā.

@281
whose  one  exhalation’s  span  then  depending on
live  from the pores  born  of the universes  the lords
Viṣṇu  the Great  He  here  whose  kalā  particular
Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
= “The lords of the universes, born from His pores, abide for the span of one of His breaths. That Mahā-Viṣṇu is a particular kalā of Govinda, the original Person, whom I worship.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.48)

@282
of all  the universes  the multitudes  He  the indwelling Lord
Kāraṇābdhiśāyī  of all  the worlds  the master
= He is the indwelling Lord of all the multitudes of universes. Kāraṇābdhiśāyī is the master of all the worlds.

@283
thus  I have told  of the first  Puruṣa  the truth
of the second  Puruṣa  now  hear  the greatness
= Thus I have told you the truth about the first Puruṣa. Now hear of the greatness of the second Puruṣa.

@284
that  Puruṣa  endless millions  of universes  having created
in each one  with a form  entered  many  forms  becoming
= Having created endless millions of universes, that Puruṣa became many forms and entered each universe in a form of His own.

@285
entering  He sees  everything  darkness
to stay  there is no  place  He made  deliberation
= Entering, He saw that all was darkness and there was no place to stay, and He reflected.

@286
with the water of His own body’s perspiration  half the universe  He filled
in that  water  on Śeṣa  as a bed  lying down  He did
= With the water of His own body’s perspiration He filled half the universe, and on those waters He lay down on the bed of Śeṣa.

@287
His  from the navel-lotus  arose  one  lotus
that  lotus  became  of Brahmā  birth  the abode
= From His navel-lotus a lotus arose, and that lotus became the birthplace of Brahmā.

@288
in that  lotus  stem  came to be  the fourteen  worlds
He  Brahmā  becoming  creation  made  bringing forth
= In the stem of that lotus the fourteen worlds came to be, and He, becoming Brahmā, brought forth the creation.

@289
Viṣṇu’s  form  taking  makes  of the world  maintenance
beyond the guṇas  Viṣṇu  touch  there is no  with māyā
= Taking the form of Viṣṇu, He maintains the world. Viṣṇu is beyond the *guṇas*; He has no contact with māyā.

@290
Rudra’s  form  assuming  makes  of the world  destruction
creation  maintenance  dissolution  take place  by the will  of whom
= Assuming the form of Rudra, He destroys the world. Creation, maintenance and dissolution all take place by His will.

@291
Brahmā  Viṣṇu  Śiva  His  guṇa-avatāras
of creation  maintenance  and dissolution  of the three  the charge
= Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva are His *guṇa-avatāras*. The three are in charge of creation, maintenance and dissolution.

@292
of Hiraṇyagarbha  the indwelling Lord  Garbhodakaśāyī
‘thousand-headed’ and so on  calling  in the Vedas  whom  they sing
= Garbhodakaśāyī is the indwelling Lord of Hiraṇyagarbha. The Vedas sing of Him in the hymn that begins “thousand-headed” (*sahasra-śīrṣā*) and in others.

@293
this  second Puruṣa  of the universe  the Lord
of māyā  the shelter  He is  yet  beyond māyā
= This second Puruṣa is the Lord of the universe. He is the shelter of māyā, yet He is beyond māyā.

@294
the third Puruṣa  Viṣṇu  a guṇa-avatāra
two  avatāras  among  the counting  of Him
= The third Puruṣa, Viṣṇu, is a *guṇa-avatāra*; He is counted among both kinds of avatāras.

@295
of the virāṭ  of the individual jīvas  He  the indwelling Lord
Kṣīrodakaśāyī  He  the maintainer  the master
= He is the indwelling Lord of the *virāṭ* and of the individual *jīvas*. He is Kṣīrodakaśāyī, the maintainer and master.

@296
of the Puruṣa-avatāras  this  I have made  the description
the līlā-avatāras  now  hear  Sanātana
= Thus I have described the Puruṣa-avatāras. Now, Sanātana, hear about the *līlā-avatāras*.

@297
the līlā-avatāras  of Kṛṣṇa  not  can be  counted
the chief  taking  I tell  a glimpse
= Kṛṣṇa’s *līlā-avatāras* cannot be counted. I shall give only a glimpse, naming the chief ones.

@298
Matsya  Kūrma  Raghunātha  Nṛsiṁha  Vāmana
Varāha and others  writing  of whom  not  can be  counted
= Matsya, Kūrma, Raghunātha, Nṛsiṁha, Vāmana, Varāha and others — they are too many to write down and count.

@299
as the Fish  the Horse  the Tortoise  Nṛsiṁha  the Boar  the Swan
among kings  brāhmaṇas  and gods  who has descended
You  protect  us  the three worlds  and  so  now  O Lord
the burden  of the earth  remove  O best of the Yadus  obeisance  to You
= “O Lord, You descended among kings, brāhmaṇas and gods, and as the Fish, the Horse, the Tortoise, Nṛsiṁha, the Boar and the Swan, and You protect us and the three worlds. So now, O best of the Yadus, remove the burden of the earth. We bow down to You.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.40)

@300
of the līlā-avatāras  I have given  a glimpse
of the guṇa-avatāras  now  hear  the account
= I have given you a glimpse of the *līlā-avatāras*. Now hear the account of the *guṇa-avatāras*.
