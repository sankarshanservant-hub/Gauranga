@title The Testing of Rūpa Gosvāmī at Siddha-bakula

@1
the lame  makes cross  a mountain
the mute  makes recite  the scriptures (śruti)
whose  mercy  to Him  I  offer obeisance
to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the Lord
= I offer obeisance to the Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, whose mercy makes the lame cross a mountain and the mute recite the scriptures.

@2
on the difficult  path  for me  blind
slipping  of feet  whose gait  constantly
their own  mercy’s  staff  by the gift
the saints  may be  the support
= I am blind, and on this difficult path my feet keep slipping. May the saints give me the staff of their mercy and be my support.

@3
Śrī  Rūpa,  Sanātana,  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha
Śrī  Jīva,  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa,  Dāsa  Raghunātha
= Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha Dāsa —

@4
of these  six  gurus  I make  to the feet  obeisance
from whom  come  of obstacles  destruction,  of the desired  fulfilment
= I bow at the feet of these six gurus, by whose grace obstacles are destroyed and all one longs for is fulfilled.

@5
glory  to the merciful (Pair)  of the lame
of me  of dull mind  the refuge
whose lotus feet are my all
Rādhā  and Madana-mohana
= All glory to the merciful Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Madana-mohana! I am lame and dull of mind, and They are my only refuge; Their lotus feet are all I have.

@6
shining  in Vṛndāvana forest  wish-fulfilling  tree  beneath
in a beautiful  jewel temple  on a lion-throne  seated
the beautiful  Rādhā  Śrīla  Govinda  the Deities
by Their dearest friends  being served  I remember
= I remember the beautiful Rādhā and Śrīla Govindadeva, seated on a throne in a beautiful temple of jewels beneath a wish-fulfilling tree in the radiant forest of Vṛndāvana, served by Their dearest companions.

@7
the beautiful  of the rāsa dance’s  sweetness  the initiator
of Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing
drawing  with the flute’s  sounds  the gopīs
of the gopīs  the Lord  for good  may be  ours
= May the beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha, who begins the sweet rāsa dance, be our good fortune. Standing on the bank by Vaṁśīvaṭa, He draws the gopīs to Himself with the sounds of His flute.

@8
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@9
the Madhya-līlā  in brief  I have made  description
of the Antya-līlā  the description  something  hear,  O devotees
= I have described the Madhya-līlā in brief. Now, O devotees, hear something of the Antya-līlā.

@10
of the Madhya-līlā  within  of the Antya-līlā  the sūtras  all
in the earlier  part of the book  in brief  I have made  description
= Within the Madhya-līlā, in the earlier part of this book, I have briefly described the *sūtras* (summaries) of the Antya-līlā.

@11
I  by old age  seized,  near  knowing  death
of the Antya-līlā  some  sūtras  I have made  description
= I am overcome by old age, and knowing that death is near, I have described some *sūtras* of the Antya-līlā.

@12
earlier  written  of the book  the sūtras  according to
whatever  not  I wrote,  that  I write  in detail
= Following the *sūtras* written earlier in the book, I now describe in detail what I have not written before.

@13
Vṛndāvana  from  the Lord  to Nīlācala  came
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  to Gauḍa  news  sent
= When the Lord returned from Vṛndāvana to Nīlācala, Svarūpa Gosāñi sent word of it to Gauḍa.

@14
hearing  Śacī  was delighted,  all  the devotees
all  gathering  to Nīlācala  made  the journey
= Hearing this, Śacī and all the devotees rejoiced, and together they set out for Nīlācala.

@15
of Kulīnagrāma  the devotees  and  all  of Khaṇḍa  the residents
the Ācārya,  with Śivānanda  met  all  coming
= The devotees of Kulīnagrāma, all the residents of Khaṇḍa and the Ācārya came and joined Śivānanda.

@16
Śivānanda  makes  for all  at the tollgates  settlement
all  care  takes,  gives  lodging  place
= Śivānanda paid the tolls for everyone at the river crossings, looked after them all and found them places to stay.

@17
one  dog  goes  with Śivānanda
food  giving,  taking (it) along  he goes  showing  care
= A dog was traveling with Śivānanda. He fed it and looked after it as he took it along.

@18
one day  in one  place  the river  across  while crossing
an Oriya  boatman  the dog  not  lets board  into the boat
= One day, at a river crossing, an Oriya boatman would not let the dog into his boat.

@19
the dog  remained,  Śivānanda  sad  became
ten  paṇas  of cowries  giving,  the dog  across  he took
= The dog was left behind, and Śivānanda grew sad. He paid ten *paṇas* of cowries and had the dog taken across.

@20
one day  Śivānanda  at the tollgate  they detained
to the dog  rice  to give  the servant  forgot
= One day Śivānanda was detained at a tollgate, and his servant forgot to give the dog its rice.
