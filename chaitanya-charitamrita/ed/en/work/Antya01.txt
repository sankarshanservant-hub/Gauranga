@title The Testing of Rūpa Gosvāmī at Siddha-bakula

@1
the lame  makes cross  a mountain
the mute  makes recite  the scriptures (śruti)
whose  mercy  to Him  I  offer obeisance
to Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the Lord
= I offer obeisance to the Lord, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, whose mercy makes the lame cross a mountain and the mute recite the scriptures.

@2
on the difficult  path  for me  blind
slipping  of feet  whose gait  constantly
their own  mercy’s  staff  by the gift
the saints  may be  the support
= I am blind, and on this difficult path my feet keep slipping. May the saints give me the staff of their mercy and be my support.

@3
Śrī  Rūpa,  Sanātana,  Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha
Śrī  Jīva,  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa,  Dāsa  Raghunātha
= Śrī Rūpa, Sanātana, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa, Śrī Jīva, Gopāla Bhaṭṭa and Raghunātha Dāsa —

@4
of these  six  gurus  I make  to the feet  obeisance
from whom  come  of obstacles  destruction,  of the desired  fulfilment
= I bow at the feet of these six gurus, by whose grace obstacles are destroyed and all one longs for is fulfilled.

@5
glory  to the merciful (Pair)  of the lame
of me  of dull mind  the refuge
whose lotus feet are my all
Rādhā  and Madana-mohana
= All glory to the merciful Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Madana-mohana! I am lame and dull of mind, and They are my only refuge; Their lotus feet are all I have.

@6
shining  in Vṛndāvana forest  wish-fulfilling  tree  beneath
in a beautiful  jewel temple  on a lion-throne  seated
the beautiful  Rādhā  Śrīla  Govinda  the Deities
by Their dearest friends  being served  I remember
= I remember the beautiful Rādhā and Śrīla Govindadeva, seated on a throne in a beautiful temple of jewels beneath a wish-fulfilling tree in the radiant forest of Vṛndāvana, served by Their dearest companions.

@7
the beautiful  of the rāsa dance’s  sweetness  the initiator
of Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing
drawing  with the flute’s  sounds  the gopīs
of the gopīs  the Lord  for good  may be  ours
= May the beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha, who begins the sweet rāsa dance, be our good fortune. Standing on the bank by Vaṁśīvaṭa, He draws the gopīs to Himself with the sounds of His flute.

@8
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@9
the Madhya-līlā  in brief  I have made  description
of the Antya-līlā  the description  something  hear,  O devotees
= I have described the Madhya-līlā in brief. Now, O devotees, hear something of the Antya-līlā.

@10
of the Madhya-līlā  within  of the Antya-līlā  the sūtras  all
in the earlier  part of the book  in brief  I have made  description
= Within the Madhya-līlā, in the earlier part of this book, I have briefly described the *sūtras* (summaries) of the Antya-līlā.

@11
I  by old age  seized,  near  knowing  death
of the Antya-līlā  some  sūtras  I have made  description
= I am overcome by old age, and knowing that death is near, I have described some *sūtras* of the Antya-līlā.

@12
earlier  written  of the book  the sūtras  according to
whatever  not  I wrote,  that  I write  in detail
= Following the *sūtras* written earlier in the book, I now describe in detail what I have not written before.

@13
Vṛndāvana  from  the Lord  to Nīlācala  came
Svarūpa  Gosāñi  to Gauḍa  news  sent
= When the Lord returned from Vṛndāvana to Nīlācala, Svarūpa Gosāñi sent word of it to Gauḍa.

@14
hearing  Śacī  was delighted,  all  the devotees
all  gathering  to Nīlācala  made  the journey
= Hearing this, Śacī and all the devotees rejoiced, and together they set out for Nīlācala.

@15
of Kulīnagrāma  the devotees  and  all  of Khaṇḍa  the residents
the Ācārya,  with Śivānanda  met  all  coming
= The devotees of Kulīnagrāma, all the residents of Khaṇḍa and the Ācārya came and joined Śivānanda.

@16
Śivānanda  makes  for all  at the tollgates  settlement
all  care  takes,  gives  lodging  place
= Śivānanda paid the tolls for everyone at the river crossings, looked after them all and found them places to stay.

@17
one  dog  goes  with Śivānanda
food  giving,  taking (it) along  he goes  showing  care
= A dog was traveling with Śivānanda. He fed it and looked after it as he took it along.

@18
one day  in one  place  the river  across  while crossing
an Oriya  boatman  the dog  not  lets board  into the boat
= One day, at a river crossing, an Oriya boatman would not let the dog into his boat.

@19
the dog  remained,  Śivānanda  sad  became
ten  paṇas  of cowries  giving,  the dog  across  he took
= The dog was left behind, and Śivānanda grew sad. He paid ten *paṇas* of cowries and had the dog taken across.

@20
one day  Śivānanda  at the tollgate  they detained
to the dog  rice  to give  the servant  forgot
= One day Śivānanda was detained at a tollgate, and his servant forgot to give the dog its rice.

@21
at night  coming  Śivānanda  of the meal  at the time
the dog  has received  rice?  the servant  he asked
= Returning at night, at mealtime Śivānanda asked his servant, “Has the dog been given its rice?”

@22
the dog  not  received  rice  hearing  sad  he became
the dog  to search for  ten men  he sent
= Hearing that the dog had not been fed, he grew sad and sent ten men to search for it.

@23
searching  not  found  the dog,  the men  all  came back
sad  becoming  Śivānanda  a fast  observed
= The men searched but could not find the dog, and they all came back. In his sorrow Śivānanda fasted.

@24
in the morning  the dog  searching for  anywhere  not  they found
of all  the Vaiṣṇavas  in the minds  wonder  arose
= In the morning they searched for the dog but found it nowhere, and all the Vaiṣṇavas were astonished.

@25
anxiously  traveling  all  came  to Nīlācala
as before  Mahāprabhu  met  with all
= Anxious at heart, they all traveled on to Nīlācala, and Mahāprabhu met them all as before.

@26
all  taking  He had  of Jagannātha  darśana
all  taking (with Him)  Mahāprabhu  takes  His meal
= He took them all for *darśana* of Jagannātha, and then Mahāprabhu took His meal together with them.

@27
as before  all  the Lord  sent  to their lodgings
to the Lord’s place  the next  day  of all  the coming
= As before, the Lord sent everyone to their lodgings, and the next day they all came to Him.

@28
coming  saw  all  that  very  dog
near the Lord  is sitting  a little  at a short distance
= When they arrived, they all saw that very dog sitting a short distance from the Lord.

@29
prasāda  coconut  pulp  He gives  tossing
“Rāma”  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Hari”  say  He says  smiling
= The Lord was tossing it coconut pulp, His *prasāda*, and smiling He said, “Say ‘Rāma,’ ‘Kṛṣṇa,’ ‘Hari’!”

@30
the pulp  eats  the dog,  “Kṛṣṇa”  says  again  and again
seeing  of the people  in the minds  arose  wonder
= The dog ate the pulp and said “Kṛṣṇa” again and again. Seeing this, the people were filled with wonder.

@31
Śivānanda  the dog  seeing  obeisance  offered
humility  showing  his own  offense  had forgiven
= When Śivānanda saw the dog, he bowed down before it and humbly begged forgiveness for his offense.

@32
the next  day  anyone  its  sight  not  got
a perfected body  attaining  the dog  to Vaikuṇṭha  went
= From the next day no one saw the dog again: it had attained a perfected body and gone to Vaikuṇṭha.

@33
such  divine pastimes  performs  of Śacī  the son
the dog  “Kṛṣṇa”  making say  He made  deliverance
= Such are the divine pastimes of the son of Śacī: He made even a dog say “Kṛṣṇa,” and so He delivered it.

@34
meanwhile  by the Lord’s order  Rūpa  came  to Vṛndāvana
of Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes  a drama  to write  there arose  the wish
= Meanwhile, by the Lord’s order, Rūpa had come to Vṛndāvana, and the wish arose in him to write a drama on Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.

@35
in Vṛndāvana  of the drama  the beginning  he made
the invocation  the nāndī verse  right there  he wrote
= He began the drama in Vṛndāvana and wrote its invocation, the *nāndī* verse, right there.

@36
on the road  traveling  he comes  of the drama  the plot  pondering
a draft  making  something  he began  to write
= As he traveled, he pondered the plot of the drama, and he began to set some of it down in a draft (*kaḍacā*).

@37
in this way  the two brothers  to Gauḍa  came
in Gauḍa  coming  Anupama’s  attaining of the Gaṅgā  took place
= In this way the two brothers came to Gauḍa, and there Anupama left this world on the bank of the Gaṅgā.

@38
Rūpa Gosāñi  to the Lord  made  his way
the Lord  to see  his  longing  heart
= Rūpa Gosāñi set out for the Lord; his heart longed to see Him.

@39
of Anupama  because  his  delay  occurred
to the devotees  he came,  meeting  not  he got
= Because of Anupama he was delayed, and when he came to join the devotees, he could not find them.

@40
in the Oriya land  Satyabhāmā-pura  by name  a village
one  night  in that  village  he made  rest
= In the land of Orissa there is a village called Satyabhāmā-pura. He rested there for one night.

@41
at night  in a dream  he sees,  a  divinely beautiful  woman
before him  coming  an order  she gave  mercy  showing
= That night he saw in a dream a woman of divine beauty. She came before him and graciously gave him an order:

@42
my  drama  separately  make  composition
by my  mercy  the drama  will be  extraordinary
= “Compose a separate drama about me. By my mercy the drama will be extraordinary.”

@43
the dream  seeing  Rūpa Gosāñi  made  reflection
Satyabhāmā’s  order  a separate  drama  to write
= After this dream Rūpa Gosāñi reflected: “Satyabhāmā orders me to write a separate drama.

@44
of Vraja  of the cities  the pastimes  together  I have made  the plot
two  parts  making  now  I shall make  the composition
= I have joined the pastimes of Vraja and of the cities in one plot. Now I shall divide them into two parts and compose them.”

@45
thinking  thinking  quickly  he came  to Nīlācala
coming  he stayed  at Haridāsa’s  dwelling
= Absorbed in these thoughts, he soon reached Nīlācala and went to stay at Haridāsa’s dwelling.

@46
Haridāsa Ṭhākura  to him  great mercy  showed
you  will come  to me  the Lord  that  told
= Haridāsa Ṭhākura received him with great kindness and said, “The Lord told me that you would come.”

@47
the upala-bhoga  seeing  Haridāsa  to see
every day  He comes;  the Lord  came  unexpectedly
= Every day, after seeing the *upala-bhoga* offering, the Lord would come to see Haridāsa. Now He arrived unexpectedly.

@48
Rūpa  obeisance  offers,  Haridāsa  said
with Haridāsa  meeting  the Lord  Rūpa  embraced
= “Rūpa is offering You obeisance,” said Haridāsa. The Lord greeted Haridāsa and then embraced Rūpa.

@49
Haridāsa,  Rūpa  taking  the Lord  sat  in one place
about welfare questions,  talk on Kṛṣṇa  He had  for some time
= The Lord sat down together with Haridāsa and Rūpa. He asked about their welfare, and for some time they spoke together of Kṛṣṇa (*iṣṭa-goṣṭhī*).

@50
of Sanātana  news  when  Gosāñi (the Lord)  asked
Rūpa  says,  him  with  meeting  not  happened
= When the Lord asked for news of Sanātana, Rūpa said, “I did not meet him.

@51
I  by the Gaṅgā route  came,  he  by the main road
therefore  my  meeting  did not happen  with him
= I came by the route along the Gaṅgā, and he by the main road, so we did not meet.

@52
at Prayāga  I heard,  he  went  to Vṛndāvana
of Anupama  the attaining of the Gaṅgā  he made  report
= At Prayāga I heard that he had gone to Vṛndāvana.” Then he told the Lord that Anupama had left this world on the bank of the Gaṅgā.

@53
to Rūpa  there  lodging  giving  Gosāñi (the Lord)  went away
of Gosāñi  the companions  the devotees  Rūpa  met
= The Lord gave Rūpa lodging there and left. Then the devotees who accompanied the Lord came to meet Rūpa.

@54
the next  day  Mahāprabhu  all  the devotees  taking
Rūpa  introduced  to all  mercy  indeed  showing
= The next day Mahāprabhu came with all the devotees and kindly introduced Rūpa to them all.

@55
of all  the feet  Rūpa  made  worship
mercy  showing  Rūpa  all  made  embrace
= Rūpa bowed at the feet of all of them, and they all kindly embraced him.

@56
Advaita,  Nityānanda,  You  both
the Lord  says,  to Rūpa  mercy  show  with body and mind
= The Lord said, “Advaita and Nityānanda, both of You, give Rūpa Your mercy with all Your heart.

@57
of You both  by the mercy  his  may be  the power
so that  to describe  he can  in relation to Kṛṣṇa  in rasa  devotion
= By the mercy of You both, may he gain the power to describe devotion to Kṛṣṇa and its *rasas*.”

@58
Gauḍīya,  Oriya,  all  of the Lord  the devotees
of all  became  Rūpa  of affection  the object
= All the Lord’s devotees, from Gauḍa and from Orissa, came to hold Rūpa dear.

@59
every day  coming  with Rūpa  He makes  meeting
in the temple  whatever  prasāda  He receives  He gives  to the two  persons
= Every day the Lord came to meet Rūpa, and whatever *prasāda* He received in the temple He gave to the two of them.

@60
talk on Kṛṣṇa  with the two  having  for some time
the midday duties  to perform  the Lord  made  departure
= After speaking with them of Kṛṣṇa for a while, the Lord would leave to perform His midday duties.

@61
in this way  every day  of the Lord  the practice
the Lord’s mercy  receiving  of Rūpa  the joy  boundless
= Such was the Lord’s practice every day, and receiving the Lord’s mercy, Rūpa felt boundless joy.

@62
the devotees  taking  He did  of Guṇḍicā  the cleansing
to Āiṭoṭā  coming  He had  a feast in the garden
= With the devotees the Lord cleansed the Guṇḍicā temple; then He came to Āiṭoṭā and held a feast in the garden there.

@63
prasāda  eat,  “Hari”  say  all the devotees
seeing,  of Haridāsa and Rūpa  delighted  the minds
= All the devotees were eating *prasāda* and calling out “Hari!” Seeing this, Haridāsa and Rūpa were delighted.

@64
through Govinda  of the Lord  the remnants of prasāda  they received
in love  intoxicated  the two  to dance  began
= Through Govinda they received the remnants of the Lord’s meal, and the two of them, intoxicated with love, began to dance.

@65
another  day  the Lord  with Rūpa  meeting  sat
of the omniscient the crown jewel  the Lord  to speak  began
= Another day the Lord met Rūpa and sat with him. The Lord, the crown jewel of the omniscient, began to speak:

@66
Kṛṣṇa  outside  not  do take  Vraja  from
Vraja  leaving  Kṛṣṇa  ever  not  goes  anywhere
= “Do not take Kṛṣṇa out of Vraja. Kṛṣṇa never leaves Vraja to go anywhere else.

@67
Kṛṣṇa  another  in the Yadu dynasty  born
who  however  the son of the king of cowherds
Vṛndāvana  leaving
He  anywhere  never at all  goes
= ‘The Kṛṣṇa born in the Yadu dynasty is another; but He who is the son of the king of the cowherds never leaves Vṛndāvana to go anywhere.’” (Yāmala, quoted in Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.5.461)

@68
this  saying  Mahāprabhu  for the midday duties  left
Rūpa Gosāñi  in the mind  some  wonder  felt
= Saying this, Mahāprabhu left for His midday duties, and Rūpa Gosāñi was somewhat astonished.

@69
a separate  drama  to write  Satyabhāmā  the order  gave
I understand,  a separate  drama  to write  the Lord’s order  has come
= “Satyabhāmā ordered me to write a separate drama, and now I understand that the Lord too has ordered me to write a separate drama.

@70
before  two  dramas  were  together  in composition
in two parts  dividing  now  I shall make  the plot
= Before, the two dramas were composed together. Now I shall divide them in two and arrange their plots.

@71
two  nāndīs,  prologues,  two  plots
separately  making  I shall write,  making  reflection
= Two *nāndī* verses, two prologues (*prastāvanā*), two plots: after careful thought I shall write them separately.”

@72
at the Ratha-yātrā  of Jagannātha  darśana  he had
before the chariot  the Lord’s  dancing and kīrtana  he saw
= At the Ratha-yātrā Rūpa had *darśana* of Jagannātha and saw the Lord dancing and singing *kīrtana* before the chariot.

@73
of the Lord  the verse of the dance  hearing  Śrī  Rūpa Gosāñi
that  verse’s meaning  taking  a verse  composed  right there
= Hearing the verse the Lord recited as He danced, Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi took up its meaning and composed a verse on the spot.

@74
before  those  all  matters  I have made  description
still  I tell  something  briefly  narration
= I have described all this before; still I shall tell something of it briefly.

@75
an ordinary  one  verse  the Lord  recites  in kīrtana
why  the verse  He recites  this  anyone  not  knows
= During *kīrtana* the Lord would recite an ordinary verse, and no one knew why He recited it.

@76
only  alone  Svarūpa  Gosāñi  of the verse  the meaning  knows
matching the verse  songs  to the Lord  makes  relish
= Svarūpa Gosāñi alone knew the meaning of the verse, and he would sing songs that matched it for the Lord to relish.

@77
Rūpa Gosāñi  of the Lord  understanding  the intent
in that  sense  a verse  composed  to the Lord  that  appeals
= Rūpa Gosāñi understood the Lord’s intent and composed a verse with that meaning, a verse that the Lord loved.
