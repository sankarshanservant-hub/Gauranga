# CC Madhya 16, stage A, part 2 (verses 146–290); read by build_en.py together with Madhya16.txt

@146
in this  way  the Lord  your  separation  enduring
your  vow  protection  made  effort  making
= “In the same way the Lord endured separation from you and took great pains to protect your vow.”

@147
in this  way  saying  him  consolation  gave
the two  stricken with grief  to Nīlācala  came
= Speaking in this way, he consoled him, and the two of them, stricken with grief, came back to Nīlācala.

@148
the Lord  for the sake of  religious duties and works  give up  the devotees
of the devotees  duty  the loss  for the Lord  not  is  bearable
= For the Lord’s sake the devotees give up their religious duties and works, yet the Lord cannot bear to see a devotee’s duty suffer.

@149
of love  the strange turns  this  hears  whoever  person
soon  meet  him  Caitanya’s feet
= These are the strange turns of love (*premera vivarta*). Whoever hears of them will soon attain the feet of Śrī Caitanya.

@150
two  royal officers  who  with the Lord  were going
to Yājapura  coming  the Lord  to them  gave  leave
= When the Lord came to Yājapura, He bade farewell to the two royal officers who were travelling with Him.

@151
the Lord  leave  gave  Rāya  goes  with Him
talks of Kṛṣṇa  with Rāmānanda  night  day
= The Lord sent them back, but Rāya went on with Him, and night and day the Lord talked of Kṛṣṇa with Rāmānanda.

@152
in every village  by the king’s order  the king’s servants
in a new house  with all kinds of  provisions  do  service
= In every village, by the king’s order, the king’s servants served Him with a new house and all kinds of provisions.

@153
in this  way  traveling  the Lord  to Remuṇā  came
from there  to Rāmānanda Rāya  leave  gave
= Travelling in this way, the Lord came to Remuṇā, and from there He sent Rāmānanda Rāya back.

@154
to the ground  fell  Rāya  there was no  consciousness
Rāya  on the lap  taking  the Lord  does  weeping
= Rāya fell to the ground unconscious, and the Lord took him on His lap and wept.

@155
Rāya’s  feelings at parting  not  can  be borne
to tell  not  I can  this  its  description
= Rāya’s grief at parting was unbearable; I cannot describe it.

@156
then  to the border of the land of Oḍhra  the Lord  traveling  came
there  the royal governor  the Lord  met
= Then the Lord came to the border of the land of Oḍhra, and there the king’s governor met Him.

@157
days  two or four  he  did  service
ahead  for going on  he  tells  the particulars
= He served the Lord for a few days and then explained to Him what lay ahead on the road.

@158
drunken  of the yavana king  ahead  the domain
his  from fear  on the road  anyone  cannot  travel
= “Ahead lies the domain of a drunken *yavana* king. For fear of him no one can travel this road.

@159
Picchaladā  as far as  all  his  domain
his  from fear  the river  anyone  to get  cannot  across
= “All the land as far as Picchaladā is his, and for fear of him no one can cross the river.

@160
days  a few  stay  a truce  making  with him
then  comfortably  by boat  I shall arrange  the journey
= “Stay here a few days. I will make a truce with him, and then I will send You on comfortably by boat.”

@161
at that  time  that  yavana’s  one  follower
to the Oriyā camp  came  making  a change of dress
= At that time one of that *yavana*’s men came to the Oriyā camp in disguise.

@162
the Lord’s  that  wonderful  conduct  seeing
the Hindu spy  says  to that  yavana  going
= Seeing the Lord’s wonderful ways, the Hindu spy went back to the *yavana* and said,

@163
one  sannyāsī  has come  Jagannātha  from
many  perfected men  are  Him  with
= “A *sannyāsī* has come from Jagannātha, and many perfected souls are with Him.

@164
constantly  perform  all  the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa
all  laugh  dance  sing  do  weeping
= “All of them constantly perform the *saṅkīrtana* of Kṛṣṇa; they all laugh, dance, sing and weep.

@165
hundreds of thousands  hundreds of thousands  people  come  it  to see
Him  seeing  again  to go  cannot  home
= “Hundreds of thousands of people come to see it, and once they have seen Him, they cannot go home again.

@166
those  all  people  become  madmen  like
“Kṛṣṇa”  saying  dance  weep  rolling on the ground  go
= “All those people become like madmen: crying ‘Kṛṣṇa!’, they dance, weep and roll on the ground.

@167
for telling  a matter  it is not  if one sees  that  one knows
His  by the power  Him  “God”  as  I accept
= “It is not something one can describe; one knows it only by seeing it. By His power I accept Him as God (*Īśvara*).”

@168
this  saying  that  spy  “Hari”  “Kṛṣṇa”  sings
laughs  weeps  dances  sings  a madman  like
= Saying this, the spy himself began to sing “Hari! Kṛṣṇa!”, laughing, weeping, dancing and singing like a madman.

@169
this  hearing  the yavana’s  mind  turning  went
his own  secretary (*viśvāsa*)  of the Oriyā  to the place  sent
= Hearing this, the *yavana*’s heart turned, and he sent his own secretary (*viśvāsa*) to the Oriyā officer.

@170
the secretary  coming  the Lord’s  feet  worshipped
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  in love  overwhelmed  became
= The secretary came and bowed at the Lord’s feet, and, crying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”, he was overwhelmed with love.

@171
composed  becoming  to the Oriyā  he says  bowing
to you  has sent  the mleccha  governor
= Composing himself, he bowed to the Oriyā officer and said, “The *mleccha* governor has sent me to you.

@172
you  if  permission  give  here  coming
the yavana  governor  will go  the Lord  having met
= “If you give permission, the *yavana* governor will come here, meet the Lord and go back.

@173
great  eagerness  his  he has shown  humility
with you  this  truce  there is no  of war  fear
= “He is very eager and has asked most humbly. This is a truce with you; there need be no fear of war.”

@174
hearing  the Mahāpātra  says  becoming  amazed
of the drunken  yavana  the heart  like this  who  makes
= Hearing this, the Mahāpātra was amazed and said, “Who could make the heart of a drunken *yavana* like this?

@175
Himself  Mahāprabhu  his  mind  has turned
by seeing and remembering  whom  the world  is delivered
= “Mahāprabhu Himself has turned his mind — He by the sight and remembrance of whom the whole world is delivered.”
