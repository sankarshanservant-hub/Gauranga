= “Wherever I stayed, houses and walls were crushed; wherever My eyes fell, I saw the place filled with people.

= “With great difficulty I reached the village of Rāmakeli. There two men named Rūpa and Sanātana came to Me.

= “The two brothers are kings among devotees, vessels of Kṛṣṇa’s mercy; in worldly affairs they are the king’s ministers and high officers.

= “He spoke well. I have so many people with Me; seeing this, people will say of Me, ‘This is just a show!’

@270

= “That Vṛndāvana is rare, hard to reach and solitary. I shall go alone, or with just one companion.

= “Saying ‘Shame, shame on Me!’, I grew restless, and turning back, I came again to the bank of the Gaṅgā.

= “I left the devotees in their various places and came back; only five or six people came with Me.

= “Now, how am I to go to Vṛndāvana without obstacles? All of you together, be gracious and give Me your counsel.

= “I went away leaving Gadādhara behind, and he was grieved. For that reason I could not go to Vṛndāvana.”
