# English names and terms — CC Antya 14, verses 61–123 (stage A, part 2; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 14»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 14», Antya14-p2) are used as given there: *uttāna-nayana*
(eyes turned upward); *tāta* (the warmth of life); *kyā kara kibā* (Hindustani “What are you doing? Why?”); *pāṇi-śaṅkha* /
*pānī-śaṅkha*; Caṭaka-parvata, Caṭaka-giri (sand hill); *śravaṇe* — right at the ear; half-external consciousness
(*ardha-bāhya*); full external consciousness (*nipaṭṭa-bāhya*); *madayati* — “makes me mad with joy” (as in Antya 6.327,
`ed/en-rev`), combining Bhaktivinoda’s “maddens” and Bhaktisiddhānta’s “delights”; Govardhana, the king of hills; Nīlādri;
Purī, Bhāratī (Paramānanda Purī, Brahmānanda Bhāratī); *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*.
Verse 123 (= Antya 2.172) and verse 86 (= Madhya 18.34) — verbatim from `ed/en-rev`.
