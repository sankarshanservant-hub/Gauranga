# English names and terms — CC Antya 9, verses 1–76 (stage A, part 1; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Антья 9»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (section «Антья 9») are used as given there: the Baḍa Jānā (the eldest
prince); *cāṅga* (the execution platform); *kāhana* (1,280 cowries), two lakhs of *kāhanas*; keeper of the king’s property
(*rāja-viṣayī*); harlots and dancers (*dārī*, *nāṭuyā*); Haricandana; Mālajāṭhyā Daṇḍapāṭa; Vāṇīnātha; Ālālanātha.
Verse 2, line 1 — word for word as in the revised Madhya 1.188; verse 4, line 1 — after Madhya 12.69 (`ed/en-rev/`).
