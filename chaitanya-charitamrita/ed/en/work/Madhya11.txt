@title Śrī Caitanya’s Kīrtana Pastimes at the Temple of Jagannātha

@1
very high-leaping  dance  Gauracandra
performing  with the devotees  of Śrī Jagannātha  in the temple
with various moods adorned in His limbs  by His own splendour
made  the world  in the flood of love  submerged
= Performing a wildly leaping dance with His devotees in the temple of Śrī Jagannātha, Gauracandra, His limbs adorned with many ecstatic moods, by His own splendour plunged the whole world into a flood of prema.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
another  day  Sārvabhauma  says  to the Lord
the gift of fearlessness  give  if  I may make  a request
= The next day Sārvabhauma said to the Lord, “If You grant me freedom from fear, I would like to make a request.”

@4
the Lord  says  speak  you  there is not  any  fear
proper  if it is  I shall do  improper  if it is  no
= The Lord said, “Speak without any fear. If it is proper I shall do it; if it is improper, I shall not.”

@5
Sārvabhauma  says  this  Pratāparudra  the king
eager longing  has come  You  to meet  he wants
= Sārvabhauma said, “King Pratāparudra is filled with longing; he wants to meet You.”

@6
in the ears  hands  placing  the Lord  remembers  ‘Nārāyaṇa’
Sārvabhauma  you say  why  improper  words
= Putting His hands over His ears, the Lord called on the name of Nārāyaṇa: “Sārvabhauma, why do you say something so improper?

@7
renounced  sannyāsī  for Me  a king  seeing
seeing a woman  like  of poison  the eating
= For Me, a renounced sannyāsī, to see a king is like seeing a woman — it is like swallowing poison.

@8
of one who owns nothing  toward the worship of the Lord  turned
the shore  far  wishing to cross  of worldly existence  of the ocean
the seeing  of worldly people  and also  of women  and
alas  alas  alas  than drinking poison  even  more evil
= “Alas, alas! For one who owns nothing in this world, who is turned toward the worship of the Lord and longs to cross to the far shore of the ocean of worldly existence, to look upon worldly people and women is worse even than drinking poison.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 8.24)

@9
Sārvabhauma  says  true  Your  words
of Jagannātha  servant  the king  but  the best of devotees
= Sārvabhauma said, “Your words are true. But the king is a servant of Jagannātha and the best of devotees.”

@10
the Lord  says  still  the king  like a deadly black snake
of a wooden woman  by the touch  as  arises  agitation
= The Lord said, “Even so, a king is like a deadly black snake. Just as even the touch of a woman carved of wood can stir agitation in the mind.

@11
of the form  even  one should fear
of women  of worldly people  also
as  of a snake  of the mind  agitation
so  of its  likeness  even
= ‘One should fear even the outward form of women and of worldly people: as the mind is agitated by a snake, so it is agitated even by its likeness.’ (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 8.25)

@12
such  words  again  to the mouth  not  bring
you say  if  then  Me  here  not  you will see
= Never bring such words to your lips again. If you speak of this once more, you will not see Me here any longer.”

@13
fear  getting  Sārvabhauma  to his own house  went
to his residence  having gone  the Bhaṭṭācārya  anxious  became
= Frightened, Sārvabhauma went home, and there the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious.

@14
at that time  Pratāparudra  to Puruṣottama  came
ministers and friends  with  the king  for darśana  set out
= At that time Pratāparudra came to Puruṣottama, and with his ministers and friends the king went for darśana of Jagannātha.

@15
Rāmānanda  Rāya  came  the Gajapati  with
first of all  the Lord  coming  met  with great joy
= Rāmānanda Rāya came with the Gajapati king, and before anything else he came and met the Lord with great joy.

@16
Rāya  obeisance  made  the Lord  made  an embrace
the two  persons  in ecstatic love  make  weeping
= Rāya bowed down, and the Lord embraced him; the two of them wept in ecstatic love.

@17
with Rāya  of the Lord  seeing  affectionate dealings
of all  the devotees  in the mind  arose  wonder
= Seeing the Lord’s affectionate dealings with Rāya, all the devotees were filled with wonder.

@18
Rāya  says  Your  order  to the king  I told
by Your  order  the king  my  worldly duties  took away
= Rāya said, “I told the king Your order, and by Your order the king has released me from my worldly duties.

@19
I  said  by me  not  will be  ‘worldly affairs’
at the feet of Caitanya  let me stay  if  permission  there is
= I told him, ‘I can no longer attend to worldly affairs. If you permit, let me remain at the feet of Caitanya.’

@20
Your  name  hearing  the king  delighted  became
from his seat  rising  me  an embrace  gave
= Hearing Your name, the king was delighted. He rose from his throne and embraced me.

@21
Your  name  hearing  there was  great ecstasy of love
my  hand  taking  shows  affection  special
= Hearing Your name, he was overwhelmed with love; taking my hand, he showed me special affection.

@22
your  which  salary  you  eat  that  salary
free from worry  being  worship  of Caitanya  the feet
= He said, ‘Keep drawing the salary you received before, and free from care worship the feet of Caitanya.’

@23
I  am wretched  fit  not  His  for the sight
Him  whoever  worships  his  fruitful  life
= Then he said, ‘I am wretched, not fit to see Him. But whoever worships Him has made his life fruitful.

@24
supremely merciful  He  the son of the king of Vraja
in some birth  to me  surely  He will give  His sight
= He, the son of the king of Vraja, is supremely merciful; in some birth He will surely let me see Him.’

@25
which  his  anguish of love  I saw  for You
of that  one  particle of love  there is not  in me
= Of the anguish of love for You that I saw in him, not even a particle is in me.”

@26
the Lord  says  you  foremost among Kṛṣṇa’s devotees
you  who  loves  he  fortunate
= The Lord said, “You are the foremost of Kṛṣṇa’s devotees. Whoever loves you is fortunate.

@27
for you  which  so much  love  has arisen  of the king
for this  quality  Kṛṣṇa  him  will accept  as His own
= Because the king has come to love you so much, for this quality Kṛṣṇa will accept him as His own.

@28
who  My  devotees  O Pārtha
not  My  devotees  and  those  people
of My devotees  and  who  devotees
they  by Me  the best of devotees  are considered
= “O Pārtha, those who are devoted only to Me are not truly My devotees; but those who are devoted to My devotees I consider the best of My devotees.” (Ādi-purāṇa; also Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 2.6)

@29
reverence  for My service
with all the limbs  obeisance
worship of My devotees  greater
in all beings  awareness of Me
= “Reverence for My service, obeisance with the whole body, worship of My devotees greater than worship of Me, seeing Me in all living beings,

@30
for My sake  the actions of the body  and
with speech  telling of My qualities
to Me  offering  and  of the mind
of all desires  giving up
= the actions of the body done for My sake, speech that tells of My qualities, a mind offered to Me, and the giving up of all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.19.21–22)

@31
of forms of worship  of all
of Viṣṇu  the worship  highest
than that  higher still  O goddess
of those who are His  the worship
= “O goddess, of all forms of worship the worship of Viṣṇu is the highest; but higher still is the worship of those who belong to Him.” (Padma-purāṇa, 6.253.176)

@32
hard to attain  indeed  for one of little austerity
service  to those who are the path to Vaikuṇṭha
in whom  is sung  constantly
the God of gods  Janārdana
= “For one whose austerity is slight, service is hard to attain to those who walk the path to Vaikuṇṭha — the devotees among whom Janārdana, the God of gods, is constantly glorified.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.7.20)

@33
Purī  Bhāratī Gosāñi  Svarūpa  Nityānanda
Jagadānanda  Mukunda and others  all  the devotees
= Purī, Bhāratī Gosāñi, Svarūpa, Nityānanda, Jagadānanda, Mukunda and all the other devotees were there.

@34
of the four  Gosāñis  made  Rāya  to the feet  obeisance
as befitting  all  with the devotees  made  meeting
= Rāya bowed at the feet of the four Gosāñis and met all the devotees, each as befitted him.

@35
the Lord  says  Rāya  have you seen  the lotus-eyed Lord
Rāya  says  now  going  I shall get  darśana
= The Lord said, “Rāya, have you seen the lotus-eyed Lord?” Rāya said, “I am going now to see Him.”

@36
the Lord  says  Rāya  you  what  deed  have done
the Lord  not  seeing  why  first  here  you came
= The Lord said, “Rāya, what have you done? Why did you come here first, without seeing the Lord?”

@37
Rāya  says  the feet  the chariot  the heart  the charioteer
wherever  taking  it goes  there  goes  the soul who rides
= Rāya said, “The feet are the chariot and the heart is the charioteer. Wherever the heart drives, there the soul who rides in the chariot must go.

@38
I  what  shall do  the mind  here  bringing  came
about seeing Jagannātha  consideration  not  made
= What could I do? My mind brought me here; it gave no thought to seeing Jagannātha first.”

@39
the Lord  says  quickly  going  take  darśana
likewise  home  going  make  with the family  meeting
= The Lord said, “Go quickly and see Jagannātha; then go home in the same way and meet your family.”

@40
of the Lord  the order  getting  Rāya  went  for darśana
of Rāya  of loving devotion  the ways  understands  what  person
= Receiving the Lord’s order, Rāya went to see Jagannātha. Who can understand the ways of Rāya’s loving devotion?

@41
to the Kṣetra  coming  the king  Sārvabhauma  summoned
to Sārvabhauma  bowing  him  asked
= Arriving at the holy Kṣetra, the king summoned Sārvabhauma. Bowing to him, he asked,

@42
me  for the sake of  at the feet of the Lord  did you make  the request
Sārvabhauma  says  I made  much  effort
= “Did you make my request at the Lord’s feet?” Sārvabhauma said, “I tried very hard.

@43
still  not  does  He  meeting a king
the Kṣetra  leaving  He will go  again  if  I make  the request
= Still, He will not see a king. If I ask Him again, He will leave the Kṣetra.”

@44
hearing  of the king  in the mind  sorrow  arose
lamentation  making  something  to speak  he began
= Hearing this, the king was grieved at heart, and in his despair he began to speak.

@45
the sinful and the low  to deliver  His  descent
Jagāi and Mādhāi  He has made  deliverance
= “He has descended to deliver the sinful and the fallen; He delivered even Jagāi and Mādhāi.

@46
Pratāparudra  leaving out  He will make  of the world  deliverance
this  vow  making  He has made  His descent
= He has descended with this vow: He will deliver the whole world, leaving out Pratāparudra alone.

@47
not fit to be seen  even  those of low birth
He looks upon with mercy  alas  yet  not  me
leaving me alone out  He will show mercy  thus
having decided  can it be  He  descended  the Lord
= “He looks with mercy even upon people of low birth, whom one is not supposed even to see — yet, alas, not upon me. Can it be that the Lord descended having resolved, ‘I shall show mercy to everyone but him’?” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 8.29)

@48
His  vow  me  not  He will give  sight
my  vow  without Him  I shall give up  life
= His vow is that He will not let me see Him; my vow is that without Him I shall give up my life.

@49
if  of that  Mahāprabhu  not  I obtain  the treasure of mercy
what  the kingdom  what  the body  all  useless
= If I do not obtain the treasure of that Mahāprabhu’s mercy, what is my kingdom, what is my body? All of it is useless.”

@50
this  hearing  Sārvabhauma  became  thoughtful
of the king  the deep attachment  seeing  became  astonished
= Hearing this, Sārvabhauma grew thoughtful; seeing the king’s deep attachment, he was astonished.

@51
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  Your Majesty  not  do  lamentation
to you  of the Lord  surely  will be  grace
= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Your Majesty, do not despair. You will surely receive the Lord’s grace.

@52
He  subject to love  your  love  very deep
surely  He will show  mercy  you  upon
= He is subject to love, and your love is very deep; He will surely show His mercy to you.

@53
still  I tell  I  one  means
this  means  carry out  the Lord  will see you  by which
= Still, I shall tell you one means. Do as I say, and by this the Lord will see you.

@54
on the day of the Ratha-yātrā  the Lord  all  the devotees  taking
before the chariot  dance  He will  overwhelmed with love  being
= On the day of the Ratha-yātrā the Lord, with all His devotees, will dance before the chariot, overwhelmed with love.

@55
in ecstatic love  into the flower garden  He will make  entrance
at that time  alone  you  giving up  the royal dress
= In that ecstasy of love He will enter the flower garden. At that time you must lay aside your royal dress and come alone.

@56
‘the five chapters on Kṛṣṇa’s rāsa dance’  doing  recitation
alone  going  of Mahāprabhu  you will clasp  the feet
= Reciting the five chapters on Kṛṣṇa’s rāsa dance, go up to Him by yourself and clasp Mahāprabhu’s feet.

@57
external awareness  is not  at that time  Kṛṣṇa’s name  hearing
an embrace  He will give  you  ‘a Vaiṣṇava’  knowing
= He will not be aware of the outer world then; hearing Kṛṣṇa’s name, He will embrace you, taking you for a Vaiṣṇava.

@58
Rāmānanda  Rāya  today  your  qualities of love
before the Lord  telling  the Lord’s  turned  went  mind
= Today, when Rāmānanda Rāya spoke before the Lord of your love and qualities, the Lord’s mind changed.”

@59
hearing  of the Gajapati  in the mind  happiness  arose
the Lord  to meet  this  plan  firm  he made
= Hearing this, the Gajapati king was happy at heart, and he firmly resolved to follow this plan for meeting the Lord.

@60
the Snāna-yātrā  when  will be  he asked  the Bhaṭṭa
the Bhaṭṭa  says  three  days  remain  until the festival
= He asked the Bhaṭṭa when the Snāna-yātrā would be held, and the Bhaṭṭa said, “Three days remain until the festival.”

@61
the king  encouraging  the Bhaṭṭa  went  to his own home
on the day of the Snāna-yātrā  of the Lord  joyful  the heart
= Having encouraged the king, the Bhaṭṭa went home. On the day of the Snāna-yātrā the Lord’s heart was full of joy.

@62
the Snāna-yātrā  seeing  of the Lord  there was  great  happiness
of the Lord  at the ‘anavasara’  He felt  great  sorrow
= Seeing the Snāna-yātrā, the Lord was very happy; but during the Lord’s anavasara He felt great sorrow.

@63
in the mood of the gopīs  in separation  the Lord  distraught  becoming
to Ālālanātha  went  the Lord  everyone  leaving
= Distraught with separation in the mood of the gopīs, the Lord left everyone and went to Ālālanātha.

@64
afterwards  of the Lord  near  came  the devotees
Gauḍa  from  devotees  are coming  made  submission
= Afterwards the devotees came to the Lord and told Him, “Devotees are coming from Gauḍa.”

@65
Sārvabhauma  to Nīlācala  came  the Lord  bringing
the Lord  has come  to the king  he told  going
= Sārvabhauma brought the Lord back to Nīlācala, and then went and told the king, “The Lord has come.”

@66
at that time  came  there  Gopīnātha Ācārya
to the king  blessing  giving  says  listen  Bhaṭṭācārya
= At that time Gopīnātha Ācārya came there. Blessing the king, he said, “Listen, Bhaṭṭācārya.

@67
Gauḍa  from  Vaiṣṇavas  are coming  two hundred
of Mahāprabhu  devotees  all  great devotees
= Two hundred Vaiṣṇavas are coming from Gauḍa — devotees of Mahāprabhu, every one of them a mahā-bhāgavata.

@68
to the Narendra  having come  all  have become  present
for all of them  is needed  lodging  of prasāda  provision
= They have all arrived at the Narendra lake. Lodging must be found for all of them, and prasāda provided.”

@69
the king  says  to the paḍichā  I  the order  will give
lodging  and so on  whatever  is needed  the paḍichā  all  will give
= The king said, “I shall give the order to the paḍichā; the paḍichā will provide lodging and whatever else is needed.

@70
of Mahāprabhu  associates  as many as  have come  Gauḍa  from
Bhaṭṭācārya  one by one  show  to me
= Bhaṭṭācārya, show me one by one all of Mahāprabhu’s associates who have come from Gauḍa.”

@71
the Bhaṭṭa  says  onto the palace roof  make  the ascent
Gopīnātha  knows  everyone  will make you  see
= The Bhaṭṭa said, “Go up onto the roof of the palace. Gopīnātha knows them all; he will point them out to you.

@72
I  anyone  not  know  to know  the wish  is
Gopīnātha Ācārya  all  will introduce
= I do not know any of them, though I would like to. Gopīnātha Ācārya will introduce them all.”

@73
this  saying  three  persons  onto the palace roof  climbed
at that time  the Vaiṣṇavas  all  near  came
= Saying this, the three of them climbed onto the roof of the palace, and at that moment the Vaiṣṇavas drew near.

@74
Dāmodara-svarūpa  Govinda  the two  persons
garlands  prasāda  taking  go  where  the Vaiṣṇavas
= Svarūpa Dāmodara and Govinda went with garlands and prasāda to where the Vaiṣṇavas were.

@75
first  Mahāprabhu  sent  the two
the king  says  these  two  who  make known  to me
= Mahāprabhu had sent these two ahead. The king said, “Who are these two? Tell me.”

@76
the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  this  Svarūpa Dāmodara
of Mahāprabhu  is  he  second  body
= The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “This is Svarūpa Dāmodara. He is Mahāprabhu’s second body.
