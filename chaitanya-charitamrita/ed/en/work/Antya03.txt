@title The Glories of Haridāsa Ṭhākura

@1
I bow down  of my śrī guru  to the beautiful  feet-
-lotus  to the śrī gurus  to the Vaiṣṇavas  and
to Śrī Rūpa  with his elder brother  with the companions
with Raghunātha  to him  with Jīva
with Advaita  with the Avadhūta  with His associates
together  to Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva
of Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa  to the feet  with Their companions
with Lalitā and Śrī Viśākhā  and
= I bow down to the beautiful lotus feet of my śrī guru, to the śrī gurus and to the Vaiṣṇavas; to Śrī Rūpa, together with his elder brother (Sanātana), with Raghunātha and his companions and with Jīva; to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, together with Advaita, with the Avadhūta (Nityānanda) and with His associates; and to the feet of Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, together with Their companions, Lalitā and Śrī Viśākhā.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in Puruṣottama  one  Oriyā  brāhmaṇa  boy
fatherless  very handsome  gentle  in manner
= In Puruṣottama there lived an Oriyā brāhmaṇa boy, fatherless, very handsome and gentle in his manner.

@4
to the Lord’s place  daily  he comes  makes  obeisance
with the Lord  talk  he speaks  the Lord  ‘life’  his
= Every day he came to the Lord and offered Him obeisance. He would talk with the Lord, for the Lord was his very ‘life’.

@5
for the Lord  his  affection  the Lord  kindness  shows
Dāmodara  his  affection  to tolerate  not  could
= The boy loved the Lord, and the Lord was kind to him; but Dāmodara could not tolerate the boy’s affection.

@6
again and again  forbidding  does  the brāhmaṇa  boy
the Lord  not  seeing  he  to remain  not  could
= Again and again he forbade the brāhmaṇa boy to come, but the boy could not stay away without seeing the Lord.

@7
daily  he comes  the Lord  him  treats  with great affection
where  affection  there  he comes  of a child  the way
= He came every day, and the Lord treated him with great affection. A child goes where he is loved — such is the way of children.

@8
that  seeing  Dāmodara  distress  feels  in his mind
to say  not  could  the boy  the prohibition  not  heeds
= Seeing this, Dāmodara was troubled at heart, yet he could say nothing more, for the boy would not heed his prohibition.

@9
another  day  that  boy  to the Lord’s place  came
the Lord  him  affection  showing  news  asked
= One day the boy came to the Lord again, and the Lord affectionately asked him how he was.

@10
after some time  that  boy  getting up  when  left
to bear  not  could  Dāmodara  to speak  began
= After a while, when the boy got up and left, Dāmodara could bear it no longer and began to speak.

@11
in instructing others  a paṇḍita  he says  of the Lord  before
‘Gosāñi’  ‘Gosāñi’  now  we shall know  ‘Gosāñi’
= He said to the Lord, “When it comes to instructing others, You are a *paṇḍita*, and everyone hails You, ‘Gosāñi! Gosāñi!’ Now we shall see what kind of ‘Gosāñi’ You are.

@12
now  of the Gosāñi  the qualities  all  people  will sing
the Gosāñi’s  reputation  all over  in Puruṣottama  will be
= Now all the people will sing the Gosāñi’s qualities, and the Gosāñi’s fame will spread all over Puruṣottama!”

@13
hearing  the Lord  says  what  are you saying  Dāmodara
Dāmodara  says  You  independent  the ‘Lord’
= Hearing this, the Lord said, “What are you saying, Dāmodara?” Dāmodara said, “You are the independent Lord.

@14
as You please  conduct  You do  who  can  to speak
talkative  of the world  the mouth  can You  to cover
= You behave as You please — who can say anything to You? But can You cover the mouth of the talkative world?

@15
a paṇḍita  being  in Your mind  why  consideration  not  You do
of the widowed brāhmaṇī  to the boy  affection  why  You show
= You are a *paṇḍita* — why do You not consider this? Why do You show such affection to the son of a widowed brāhmaṇī?

@16
although  the brāhmaṇī  she  austere  chaste
still  her  fault  beautiful  young
= Although that brāhmaṇī is austere and chaste, she has one fault: she is young and beautiful.

@17
You too  very young  very handsome
to people’s  whispering talk  You give  occasion
= And You too are very young and very handsome. You are giving people occasion for gossip.”

@18
this  saying  Dāmodara  silent  became
within  pleased  the Lord  smiling  reflected
= Having said this, Dāmodara fell silent. The Lord, pleased at heart, smiled and reflected:

@19
this  I call  of pure love  a wave
like Dāmodara  My  there is not  ‘intimate associate’
= “This I call a wave of pure love. I have no intimate associate like Dāmodara.”

@20
thus  reflecting  the Lord  for the midday duties  went
the next  day  Dāmodara  in private  He called
= Reflecting thus, the Lord went to attend to His midday duties. The next day He called Dāmodara to Him in private.

@21
the Lord  says  Dāmodara  go  to Nadīyā
of My mother  near  you  stay  there  going
= The Lord said, “Dāmodara, go to Nadīyā. Go there and stay near My mother.

@22
you  except  her  protector  there is not  other
even Me  since  you  made  cautious
= She has no protector other than you, for you have cautioned even Me.

@23
like you  ‘impartial’  there is not  among My  associates
‘impartial’  without being  ‘dharma’  not  can be  protected
= Among My associates there is no one as impartial (*nirapekṣa*) as you, and without impartiality *dharma* cannot be protected.

@24
Me  from  what  not  happens  that  you  from  happens
Me  you made  chastisement  others  who  are
= What I cannot do, you can. You have chastised even Me — what then of anyone else?

@25
of My mother  in the house  stay  going  of My mother  at the feet
you  before  there is not  of anyone  willful behavior
= Go and stay in My mother’s house, at her feet. Before you no one will dare to behave as he pleases.

@26
from time to time  you will come  sometimes  Me  to see
quickly  doing  again  there  make  going
= From time to time you may come to see Me, but then go back there quickly.

@27
to My mother  convey  My  millions of  obeisances
My  happy news  telling  happiness  give  to her
= Convey to My mother My obeisances millions of times, and give her joy by telling her the happy news of Me.

@28
constantly  His own  news  to you  to tell
for this  reason  the Lord  me  sent  here
= Tell her: ‘The Lord has sent me here to tell you His news constantly.’

@29
this  saying  of My mother  in the mind  satisfaction  produce
and  a confidential  matter  her  remembrance  cause
= Saying this, bring satisfaction to My mother’s heart. And remind her of a confidential matter:

@30
again and again  coming  I  to your  house
sweets  vegetable dishes  all  I do  eating
= ‘Again and again I come to your house and eat all the sweets and vegetable dishes.

@31
eating  do  I  you  that  know
external  in separation  that  a vision  as  you consider
= I do eat them, and you know it; but in the external state of separation you take it to be only a vision (*sphūrti*).

@32
this  at the Māgha-saṅkrānti  you  cooking  did
various  vegetable dishes  condensed milk  cakes  sweet rice  you cooked
= This Māgha-saṅkrānti you did the cooking: you made various vegetable dishes, condensed milk, cakes and sweet rice.

@33
to Kṛṣṇa  the food  offering  when  you did  meditation
of Me  a vision  occurred  tears  filled  the eyes
= When you had offered the food to Kṛṣṇa and were meditating, I appeared to you, and your eyes filled with tears.

@34
hastily  I  going  everything  ate
I  eat  seeing  your  happiness  arose
= I went in haste and ate everything, and when you saw Me eating, joy arose in you.

@35
after a moment  tears  wiping  empty  seeing  the plate
a dream  I saw  as if  Nimāi  ate  the rice
= A moment later, wiping your tears, you saw that the plate was empty and thought, “I have had a dream that Nimāi ate the rice.”

@36
in the state of external separation  again  confusion  came
the food  not  I offered  this  thought  came
= In that state of external separation you were confused again and thought, “I have not offered the food.”

@37
in the cooking pots  you saw  all  the food  is  filled
again  the food  you offered  the place  cleansing  having done
= You looked in the cooking pots and saw that they were all full of food. So, having cleansed the place, you offered the food again.

@38
in this way  again and again  I do  eating
your  by pure love  Me  does  attracting
= In this way I eat again and again; your pure love draws Me.

@39
by your  order  I  am  in Nīlācala
near  taking  you go  Me  of your  love  by the power
= It is by your order that I stay in Nīlācala, yet by the power of your love you draw Me close to you.’

@40
in this way  again and again  cause  remembrance
My  name  taking  her  worship  feet
= Remind her in this way again and again, and worship her feet in My name.”

@41
this  saying  of Jagannātha  the prasāda  had brought
to His mother  to the Vaiṣṇavas  to give  separately  separately  He gave
= Saying this, the Lord had Jagannātha’s *prasāda* brought and gave it to him in separate portions for His mother and for the Vaiṣṇavas.

@42
then  Dāmodara  going  to Nadīyā  came
the mother  meeting  her  at the feet  he remained
= Then Dāmodara set out and came to Nadīyā. He met the Lord’s mother and remained at her feet.

@43
to the Ācārya and others  to the Vaiṣṇavas  mahā-prasāda  he gave
of the Lord  as  the order  the Paṇḍita  that  carried out
= He gave the *mahā-prasāda* to the Ācārya (Advaita) and the other Vaiṣṇavas. Just as the Lord had ordered, so the Paṇḍita did.

@44
Dāmodara  before  independence  not  is  of anyone
of him  out of fear  all  do  restrained  behavior
= Before Dāmodara no one dared to act independently; out of fear of him everyone behaved with restraint.

@45
among the Lord’s associates  of whom  he sees  slight  propriety  transgression
with words  chastisement  making  makes  propriety  establishment
= Whenever he saw any of the Lord’s associates commit even a slight breach of propriety, he chastised him with words and so established proper conduct.

@46
this  indeed  I have told  of Dāmodara  chastisement with words
whose  by hearing  flee  ‘ignorance’  ‘atheism’
= Thus I have told of Dāmodara’s chastisement with words; by hearing of it, ignorance and atheism (*pāṣaṇḍa*) flee.

@47
of Caitanya  the pastimes  deep  millions of oceans  than
for what reason  what  He does  anyone  not  can  understand
= Caitanya’s pastimes are deeper than millions of oceans. No one can understand what He does or why.

@48
therefore  the hidden  meaning  anything  not  I know
external  meaning  to make  I make  great effort
= Therefore I know nothing of their hidden meaning; I struggle just to give them an external meaning.

@49
one day  the Lord  Haridāsa  met
him  with  conversation  making  him  asked
= One day the Lord met Haridāsa, and as they conversed He asked him:

@50
Haridāsa  in the age of Kali  yavanas  countless
to cows and brāhmaṇas  violence  they do  very wicked
= “Haridāsa, in the age of Kali the *yavanas* are countless. They are very wicked and do violence to cows and brāhmaṇas.

@51
of all of them  in what  way  will be  deliverance
of it  the means  not  I see  this  grief  boundless
= How will all of them be delivered? I see no means for it, and this grief of Mine is boundless.”

@52
Haridāsa  says  Lord  worry  not  do
of the yavanas  the worldly bondage  seeing  grief  not  feel
= Haridāsa said, “Lord, do not worry. Do not grieve at the sight of the *yavanas*’ worldly bondage (*saṁsāra*).

@53
of all the yavanas  ‘liberation’  will be  easily
‘hā rāma’  ‘hā rāma’  saying  they speak  in the semblance of the Name
= All the *yavanas* will attain ‘liberation’ easily, for they say ‘hā rāma, hā rāma’ and so utter the semblance of the Name (*nāmābhāsa*).

@54
in great love  a devotee  says  ‘hā rāma’  ‘hā rāma’
of the yavanas  the fortune  see  they take  that  Name
= In great love a devotee cries, ‘Hā Rāma! Hā Rāma!’ See the good fortune of the *yavanas*: they utter that very Name!

@55
although  to something else  by indication  is  nāmābhāsa
still  of the Name  the potency  not  is  destroyed
= Although the Name is only *nāmābhāsa* when it points (*saṅketa*) to something else, its potency is still not destroyed.

@56
by a boar’s  tusk  struck  a mleccha
‘hā rāma’  thus  again  and again
even by saying  liberation  attains
what  then  with faith  one who chants
= “A *mleccha* struck by a boar’s tusk who cries ‘hā rāma’ again and again attains liberation even by saying so. What then of one who chants the Name with faith?” (Nṛsiṁha Purāṇa)

@57
Ajāmila  his son  calls  saying  ‘Nārāyaṇa’
the messengers of Viṣṇu  coming  release  his  bonds
= Ajāmila called his son by saying ‘Nārāyaṇa’, and the messengers of Viṣṇu came and released him from his bonds.

@58
‘Rāma’  two  syllables  these  not  are  separated
expressing love  the ‘hā’  word  with it  adorned
= In ‘hā rāma’ the two syllables of ‘Rāma’ are not separated, and they are adorned with the word ‘hā’, which expresses love.

@59
of the Name  of all the syllables  this  indeed  the nature
separated  even if they become  not  give up  their own power
= Such is the nature of the syllables of the Name: even when separated, they do not give up their power.

@60
the Name  one  whose  on the tongue  of memory  the path
having reached  the root of the ear  having reached  or
pure  or with impure sounds  separation
free from  delivers  certainly  truly
that  if  the body  wealth  followers
greed  godlessness  amid
placed  may be  not  producing fruit
quickly  indeed here  O brāhmaṇa
= “O brāhmaṇa, if even one Name is on someone’s tongue, or has entered the path of his memory, or has reached the root of his ear — whether its sounds are pure or impure — and is free from separation, it certainly delivers him; this is the truth. But if that Name is placed amid concerns for the body, wealth and followers, amid greed or godlessness (*pāṣaṇḍa*), it will not quickly bear its fruit here.” (Padma Purāṇa; Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 11.289)

@61
the semblance of the Name  through  there is  of all sins  destruction
the semblance of the Name  through  there is  of worldly existence  the end
= Through *nāmābhāsa* all sins are destroyed; through *nāmābhāsa* worldly existence comes to an end.

@62
Him  without deceit  worship  O treasure of virtues  the purifier  of purifiers
with faith  delighting  your mind  quickly  of those of highest fame  the crown
rising  of the heart  in the cave  ah  whose Name  of the sun
the faint light  even  destroys  of great sins  of the darkness  the mass
= “O treasure of virtues, with your mind delighting in faith, quickly and without deceit worship Him, the purifier of all purifiers, the crown of those whose glory is sung in the finest verses. Ah! When even the faint light of the sun of His Name rises in the cave of the heart, it dispels the massed darkness of great sins.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.103)

@63
dying  of Hari  the Name
uttering  addressed to his son
Ajāmila  even  went  to the abode
what to speak  then  with faith  one who utters
= “Even Ajāmila reached the Lord’s abode by uttering the Name of Hari as he was dying, though he addressed it to his son. What then of one who utters the Name with faith?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 6.2.49)

@64
by nāmābhāsa  ‘liberation’  there is  in all scriptures  I see
in the Śrī Bhāgavata  of that  Ajāmila  the witness
= In all the scriptures I see that ‘liberation’ comes through *nāmābhāsa*, and in the Śrī Bhāgavata Ajāmila is the witness to it.”

@65
hearing  the Lord’s  happiness  increases  within
again  a feint  making  He asks  him
= Hearing this, the Lord felt His happiness grow within. Yet again, as if testing him, He asked:

@66
on the earth  many beings  nonmoving and moving
of all of them  in what  way  will be  release
= “On the earth there are many living beings, nonmoving and moving. How will all of them be released?”

@67
Haridāsa  says  Lord  that  mercy  Yours
the nonmoving and moving  already  You have made  deliverance
= Haridāsa said, “Lord, that is by Your mercy: You have already delivered the nonmoving and moving beings.

@68
You  that  have done  this  loud  saṅkīrtana
of the nonmoving and moving  that  is  indeed  hearing
= You have performed this loud *saṅkīrtana*, and the nonmoving and moving beings have all heard it.

@69
by hearing  of the moving beings  there is  of worldly existence  the end
on the nonmoving  that  sound  strikes  an echo  arises
= By hearing it, the moving beings are freed from worldly existence; and when that sound strikes the nonmoving beings, an echo arises.

@70
‘an echo’  it is not  they  perform  ‘kīrtana’
of Your  mercy  this  indescribable  account
= It is not an ‘echo’: they are performing ‘*kīrtana*’. This is an indescribable account of Your mercy.

@71
in all  the world  there is  loud  saṅkīrtana
hearing  absorbed in love  dance  the nonmoving and moving
= Throughout the world there is loud *saṅkīrtana*, and hearing it, the nonmoving and moving beings dance, absorbed in *prema*.

@72
as  You did  in Jhārikhaṇḍa  to Vṛndāvana  while going
Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya  has told  me
= Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya has told me how You did this in Jhārikhaṇḍa on Your way to Vṛndāvana.

@73
Vāsudeva  the jīvas  for the sake of  made  a petition
then  acceptance  You made  of the jīvas  the release
= When Vāsudeva petitioned You on behalf of the *jīvas*, You agreed to release them.

@74
the world  to deliver  this  Your  descent
the mood of a devotee  first  for that  You made  acceptance
= This descent of Yours is to deliver the world; for that purpose You first accepted the mood of a devotee.

@75
loud  saṅkīrtana  therefore  You made  propagation
nonmoving and moving  of the jīvas  You cut off  worldly existence
= So You spread loud *saṅkīrtana* and cut off the worldly existence of all *jīvas*, nonmoving and moving.”

@76
the Lord  says  all  jīvas  liberation  when  will attain
this  indeed  universe  then  devoid of jīvas  will be
= The Lord said, “If all the *jīvas* attain liberation, then this universe will be empty of *jīvas*!”

@77-78
Haridāsa  says  Your  as long as  in the mortal world  stay
so long  nonmoving and moving  all species of beings
all  liberated  making  You  to Vaikuṇṭha  will send
the subtle jīvas  again  to karma  awakened  You will make
= Haridāsa said, “As long as You remain in the mortal world, You will liberate all the nonmoving and moving beings of every species and send them to Vaikuṇṭha. Then You will awaken the subtle *jīvas* (*sūkṣma-jīva*) to *karma* once again.

@79
those  jīvas  will become  here  nonmoving and moving
with them  will be filled  the universe  as  like before
= Those *jīvas* will become the nonmoving and moving beings here, and with them the universe will be filled as before.

@80
formerly  as  Raghunātha  all  Ayodhyā  taking
to Vaikuṇṭha  went  with other  jīvas  Ayodhyā  filling
= Just as formerly Raghunātha (Rāmacandra) took all of Ayodhyā with Him to Vaikuṇṭha and filled Ayodhyā with other *jīvas*,

@81
having descended  You  likewise  have set up  a market
anyone  not  to understand  can  Your  hidden  drama
= so You too, having descended, have set up a market in the same way. No one can understand Your hidden drama.

@82
formerly  as  in Vraja  Kṛṣṇa  making  descent
of all  the universe’s  jīvas  cut off  worldly existence
= Formerly, when Kṛṣṇa descended in Vraja, He cut off the worldly existence of all the *jīvas* in the universe.

@83
not  and  thus  wonder  should be made
by you  about the Lord  the unborn
about the Lord of the lords of yoga  Kṛṣṇa
from whom  this (world)  is liberated
= “You should not be astonished at this in the case of Kṛṣṇa, the unborn Lord, the Lord of the lords of *yoga*, by whom this whole world is liberated.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.16)

@84
this  indeed  Bhagavān  seen  glorified  remembered  and
with persistent enmity  even  for all gods, demons and others  hard to attain
the fruit  bestows  what to speak  then  perfectly  of those who have devotion  thus
= “When this Bhagavān is seen, glorified or remembered even with persistent enmity, He bestows a fruit hard to attain for all the gods, demons and others. What then of those who have perfect devotion?” (Viṣṇu Purāṇa, 4.15.17)

@85
likewise  You  in Navadvīpa  making  descent
of all  the universe’s  jīvas  You made  deliverance
= Likewise, You have descended in Navadvīpa and delivered the *jīvas* of the whole universe.

@86
who  says  of Caitanya  the glory  to me  known  is
he  let him know  my  however  this  indeed  conviction
= If someone says, ‘The glory of Caitanya is within my grasp,’ let him know it; but my own conviction is this:

@87
Your  which  pastimes  of great nectar  an ocean
to my  mind  perceptible  is not  of it  one  drop
= Your pastimes are an ocean of great nectar, and not even a single drop of it is within the reach of my mind.”

@88
this  hearing  the Lord’s  in the mind  amazement  was
My  hidden pastimes  Haridāsa  how  has known
= Hearing this, the Lord was amazed at heart: “How has Haridāsa come to know My hidden pastimes?”

@89
of the heart  in satisfaction  him  made  embrace
outwardly  to reveal  that  He made  avoidance
= In the satisfaction of His heart He embraced him, but He avoided revealing this outwardly.

@90
of the Lord  the nature  His majesty  wishes  to cover
before devotees  to hide  cannot  becomes  indeed  known
= It is the Lord’s nature to wish to conceal His majesty, but He cannot hide it from His devotees: to them it becomes known.

@91
transgressing  three kinds of  limits,  equal and superior
the possibility (excluding)  Your  of supremacy  the nature
of māyā  by the power  by You  even  being hidden
see  some  constantly  to You  exclusively devoted
= “Your sovereign nature transcends the three kinds of limits and admits of no equal or superior. Though You hide it by the power of Your māyā, some who are devoted to You alone see it constantly.” (Stotra-ratna, 18)

@92
then  Mahāprabhu  to His own devotees  going
Haridāsa’s  qualities  speaks  hundred-mouthed  becoming
= Then Mahāprabhu went to His own devotees and spoke of Haridāsa’s qualities as if He had a hundred mouths.

@93
of a devotee  qualities  in speaking  the Lord’s  increases  delight
of all the devotees  the best  moreover  Śrī Haridāsa
= The Lord’s delight grows when He speaks of a devotee’s qualities — and Śrī Haridāsa, moreover, is the best of all devotees.

@94
Haridāsa’s  host of qualities  countless  boundless
someone  some  portion  describing  not  reaches  the end
= Haridāsa’s qualities are countless and boundless. One may describe some portion of them, but no one reaches their end.

@95
in the Caitanya-maṅgala  Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa
Haridāsa’s  qualities  some  has made  manifest
= In the *Caitanya-maṅgala* (the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*) Śrī Vṛndāvana dāsa has revealed some of Haridāsa’s qualities.

@96
all  to tell  not  is possible  of Haridāsa  the character
someone  something  tells  to make  himself  pure
= It is impossible to tell all of Haridāsa’s character; one tells a little of it to purify oneself.

@97
Vṛndāvana dāsa  what  not  made  description
Haridāsa’s  qualities  some  hear  O devotees
= O devotees, hear of some of Haridāsa’s qualities that Vṛndāvana dāsa did not describe.

@98
Haridāsa  when  his own home  renunciation  made
of Benāpola  in the forest  for some days  he stayed
= When Haridāsa left his home, he lived for some time in the forest of Benāpola.

@99
in a solitary forest  a hut  making  Tulasī  serving
night and day  three hundred thousand  Names  chanting
