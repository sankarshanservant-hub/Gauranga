Its lotuses are the arts of glorifying Kṛṣṇa aloud, of singing and of dancing. Rows of true devotees sport in it like swans, cakravāka birds and swarms of bees, and its sweet murmur delights the ears.

@5

And Bhagavān, full of the six opulences, is He Himself. In this world there is no Supreme Truth higher than Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa.”

the shelter  to know  I describe  these  nine  categories
of these  nine  of origin  the cause  that  the meaning of shelter
= I describe these nine categories so that the shelter may be known. The cause from which these nine arise — that is what *shelter* means.

@94

Kṛṣṇa's  in the body  of all  the universes  the repose
= Kṛṣṇa alone is the shelter of all; Kṛṣṇa is the abode of all. All the universes rest in His body.

@95

of the sheltered  of the shelter  the embodiment
called Śrī Kṛṣṇa  the supreme  abode
of the world  the abode  I bow to  that
= “The Tenth Canto reveals the tenth subject, the embodiment of the shelter of all who take shelter. I bow to that supreme abode called Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the abode of the universe.” (Śrīdhara Svāmī, Bhāvārtha-dīpikā on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.1.1)

@96
of Kṛṣṇa  the own form  and  of the three potencies  knowledge
whose  is  his  there is not  about Kṛṣṇa  ignorance
= One who knows Kṛṣṇa’s own form and His three potencies is never ignorant about Kṛṣṇa.

@97
of Kṛṣṇa  of the own form  are  six kinds of  pastime forms
prābhava  vaibhava  in the forms  two kinds of  manifestation
= Kṛṣṇa’s own form has six kinds of pastime forms (*vilāsa*). His manifestations are of two kinds, *prābhava* and *vaibhava*.

@98
partial  empowered  in the forms  two kinds of  avatāras
of childhood  of boyhood  the characteristics  two  indeed  kinds
= His avatāras are of two kinds: partial avatāras and empowered avatāras (*śaktyāveśa*). There are two further kinds: His forms of childhood and of boyhood (*paugaṇḍa*).

@99
youthful  own form  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  the source of avatāras
pastimes  performs  in these  six  forms  the universe  filling
= Kṛṣṇa in His own youthful form (*kiśora*) is Himself the source of all avatāras. He sports in these six forms and fills the universe.

@100
in these  six  forms  are  endless  varieties
in endless  forms  one  form  there is not  any  difference
= These six forms have endless varieties. Yet in all these endless forms He is one form; there is no difference at all.

@101
the spiritual potency  His own potency  internal  named
its  opulence  countless  Vaikuṇṭha and other  abodes
= The spiritual potency (*cit-śakti*) is called His very own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*) and the internal potency. Its opulence is the countless abodes such as Vaikuṇṭha.

@102
the potency māyā  external  the cause of the world
its  opulence  countless  of universes  multitudes
= The potency called māyā is external and is the cause of the material world. Its opulence is the countless multitudes of universes.

@103
the potency of the jīvas  called marginal  there is not  whose  end
principal  three  potencies  their  varieties  endless
= The potency of the living beings is called marginal (*taṭastha*), and it has no end. These are the three principal potencies, and their varieties are endless.

@104
these  indeed  of His own forms  the multitude  and  three  potencies
of all  the shelter  Kṛṣṇa  in Kṛṣṇa  of all  the existence
= These are His own forms and the three potencies. Kṛṣṇa is the shelter of them all, and all of them exist in Kṛṣṇa.

@105
although  of the universes  multitude  the Puruṣa  the shelter
of those  the Puruṣa and others  all  Kṛṣṇa  the original shelter
= Although the Puruṣa is the shelter of all the universes, Kṛṣṇa is the original shelter of the Puruṣa and of all the others.

@106
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  the shelter of all
supreme  Lord  Kṛṣṇa  in all scriptures  is said
= Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord Himself; Kṛṣṇa is the shelter of all. All the scriptures declare that Kṛṣṇa is the supreme Lord.

@107
the Lord  supreme  Kṛṣṇa
of eternity  knowledge  bliss  whose form
without beginning  the beginning  Govinda
of all  causes  the cause
= “Kṛṣṇa, who is Govinda, is the supreme Lord. His form is eternity, knowledge and bliss. He has no beginning, yet He is the beginning of all, the cause of all causes.” (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.1)

@108
these  all  conclusions  you  know  well
still  objections  you make  me  to unsettle
= You know all these conclusions well, yet you raise objections to unsettle me.

@109
that  Kṛṣṇa  the source of avatāras  the son of the king of Vraja
Himself  in the form of Caitanya  made  descent
= That Kṛṣṇa, the son of the king of Vraja, the source of the avatāras, has Himself descended in the form of Caitanya.

@110
therefore  Caitanya  Gosāñi  of the Supreme Truth  the utmost limit
Him  Kṣīrodaśāyī  calling  what  His  glory
= Therefore Lord Caitanya is the utmost limit of the Supreme Truth. What glory is there in calling Him Kṣīrodaśāyī?

@111
those  indeed  of a devotee  words  are not  wrong
all  is possible  in Him  since  the source of avatāras
= Yet such words of a devotee are not wrong. Everything is possible in Him, since He is the source of the avatāras.

@112

someone  in some  way  speaks  as  whose  understanding
= All the avatāras exist within the body of Him who is their source. So people speak of Him in different ways, each according to his understanding.

@113

someone  says  Kṛṣṇa  is  directly  Vāmana
= Some call Kṛṣṇa Nara-Nārāyaṇa, and some say that Kṛṣṇa is Vāmana Himself.

@114

impossible  is not  true  the words  of all
= Some say that Kṛṣṇa is an avatāra of Kṣīrodaśāyī. That is not impossible; the words of all of them are true.

@115

all  is possible  in Kṛṣṇa  since  the source of avatāras
= Some call Him Hari, the Nārāyaṇa of the spiritual sky. Everything is possible in Kṛṣṇa, since He is the source of the avatāras.

@116

= I bow at the feet of all my listeners. Hear all these conclusions with undivided attention.

@117
conclusions  thinking (them)  in the heart  not  be  lazy

= Do not let your heart grow lazy, thinking these are mere philosophical conclusions. Through them the mind becomes firmly attached to Kṛṣṇa.

the heart  firm  becoming  becomes attached  of the greatness  from knowing

@119
of Caitanya Mahāprabhu  the greatness  to tell  in order to

= This is the established truth about Lord Caitanya: He is Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Lord Himself, the son of the king of Vraja.
