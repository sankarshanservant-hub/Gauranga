@title The Truth of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu

@1
Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship
a child  even  by whose  mercy
may cross  of various  doctrines  the crocodiles
infested with  of conclusive truths  the ocean
= I worship Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu. By His mercy even a child can cross the ocean of conclusive truths, which is infested with the crocodiles of various doctrines.

@2
of Kṛṣṇa's loud glorification  singing  dancing  the arts  with lotuses  adorned
of true  devotees  rows  swans  cakravāka birds  bees  swarms  the place of sport
delighting the ears  sweet  murmur  may flow  my  of the tongue  desert  in the courtyard
O Śrī Caitanya  of mercy  O ocean  Your  resplendent  of pastimes  of nectar  the heavenly Gaṅgā
= O Śrī Caitanya, ocean of mercy! May the heavenly Gaṅgā of the nectar of Your resplendent pastimes flow through the desert courtyard of my tongue. Its lotuses are the arts of glorifying Kṛṣṇa aloud, of singing and of dancing. Rows of true devotees sport in it like swans, cakravāka birds and swarms of bees, and its sweet murmur delights the ears.

@3
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaita-candra  glory  to Gaura's  devotees  host
= All glory, all glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaita-candra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@4
of the third  verse  the meaning  I make  explanation
the subject  defining  in the form of  invocation
= Now I shall explain the meaning of the third verse, the invocation (*maṅgalācaraṇa*) that defines the subject.

@5
that which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  too  His  the radiance of the body
who  the Self dwelling within  the Puruṣa  thus (called)  he  His  plenary expansion
with the six opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one
not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  Supreme Truth  higher  here
= “What the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is only the radiance of His body. The one called the Puruṣa, the Supersoul dwelling within all, is His plenary expansion. And Bhagavān, full of the six opulences, is He Himself. In this world there is no Supreme Truth higher than Caitanya, who is Kṛṣṇa.”

@6
Brahman  the Supersoul  Bhagavān  subjects  three
the radiance of the body  the portion  His own form  three  predicate  marks
= Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān are the three subjects (*anuvāda*). The radiance of His body, His portion and His own form (*svarūpa*) are the three predicates (*vidheya*) that define them.

@7
the subject  first  after  the predicate  is placed
that  meaning  I tell  hear  of scripture  the explanation
= The subject comes first, and the predicate is placed after it. I shall tell you the meaning of the verse in this way; now hear it explained according to scripture.

@8
Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of Viṣṇu  the Supreme Truth
full  knowledge  full bliss  supreme  greatness
= Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord Himself (*svayaṁ bhagavān*), the Supreme Truth of all Viṣṇu forms. He is full knowledge and full bliss, and His greatness is supreme.

@9
the son of Nanda  calling  whom  in the Bhāgavata  they sing
that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  Caitanya  Gosāñi
= That Kṛṣṇa, whom the Bhāgavatam glorifies as the son of Nanda, has descended as Lord Caitanya.

@10
manifestation  in particular  He  bears  three  names
Brahman  the Supersoul  and  Himself  Bhagavān
= According to His particular manifestations He bears three names: Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān, the Supreme Lord Himself.

@11
declare  that  the knowers of the Truth
the Truth  which  knowledge  non-dual
Brahman as  the Supersoul as
Bhagavān  as  is named
= “The knowers of the Truth declare that the Truth is non-dual knowledge. It is named Brahman, the Supersoul and Bhagavān.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.2.11)

@12
His  of the body  pure  of rays  the halo
the Upaniṣads  call  it  Brahman  utterly pure
= The pure halo of rays from His body is what the Upaniṣads call the utterly pure Brahman.

@13
with fleshly eyes  one sees  as  the sun  featureless
on the path of knowledge  to grasp  cannot  His  distinctive features
= To the physical eye the sun appears featureless. In the same way, on the path of knowledge one cannot grasp His distinctive features.

@14
whose  effulgence  of the mighty one  of universes  of millions
in millions  countless  earths and other  by opulences  differentiated
that  Brahman  undivided  infinite  complete
Govinda  the original Person  Him  I  worship
= “I worship Govinda, the original Person. In the millions upon millions of universes of that mighty Lord, His effulgence appears differentiated by countless opulences, such as earths. That effulgence is Brahman — undivided, infinite and complete.” (Brahma-saṁhitā 5.40)

@15
millions  of millions  in universes  which  of Brahman  the opulence
that  Brahman  of Govinda  is  the luster of the body
= Brahman, whose opulence fills millions upon millions of universes, is the luster of Govinda’s body.

@16
that  Govinda  worship  I  He  my  master
His  by the grace  my  is  the power of creation
= I worship that Govinda; He is my master. By His grace I have the power to create.

@17
sages  wind  clad in
ascetics  upward  drawing (their seed)
called Brahman  the abode  they  reach
peaceful  renunciants  pure
= “Sages clad only in the wind, ascetics who draw their seed upward, peaceful and pure renunciants — they reach the abode called Brahman.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.6.47)

@18
the Self, the indwelling Lord  whom  in the scriptures of yoga  they call
He  of Govinda  portion  manifestation of opulence  indeed  is
= He whom the scriptures of yoga call the Self, the indwelling Lord (*antaryāmī*), is a portion of Govinda and a manifestation of His opulence.

@19
in countless  crystals  as  one  sun  is reflected
so  in the living beings  of Govinda  the portion  is manifest
= Just as one sun is reflected in countless crystals, so Govinda’s portion is manifest in all living beings.

@20
or else  by much  this
what  by knowing  your  O Arjuna
pervading and supporting  I  this  entire
with one portion  abide  the universe
= “But what need is there, Arjuna, for you to know all this in detail? I pervade and support this entire universe with a single portion of Myself.” (Bhagavad-gītā 10.42)

@21
Him  this  I  the unborn  of the embodied
in heart  in heart  abiding  created by Himself
to each  eye  like  in many ways  the sun  one
have realized  I am  dispelled  of difference  the delusion
= “My delusion of difference is gone, and I have realized Him, this unborn Lord. He abides in the heart of every embodied being He has created, just as the one sun appears in many ways to every eye that sees it.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.9.42)

@22
that  indeed  Govinda  directly  Caitanya  Gosāñi
souls  in delivering  so  merciful  another  there is not
= That same Govinda is directly Lord Caitanya. No one else is so merciful in delivering souls.

@23
in the spiritual sky  resides  Nārāyaṇa  named
with the six opulences  full  of Lakṣmī  the beloved  the Lord
= In the spiritual sky resides the Lord named Nārāyaṇa. He is full of the six opulences and is the beloved of Lakṣmī.

@24
the Vedas  the Bhāgavata  the Upaniṣads  the Āgamas
the complete Truth  whom  call  there is not  whose  equal
= The Vedas, the Bhāgavatam, the Upaniṣads and the Āgamas call Him “the complete Truth”; no one is equal to Him.

@25
through bhakti-yoga  the devotee  obtains  whose  sight
the sun  as  in his form  see  the gods
= Through *bhakti-yoga*, the path of devotion, the devotees obtain the sight of Him, just as the gods see the sun in his personal form.

@26
of knowledge  of yoga  on the path  Him  worship  who  all
of Brahman  of the Supersoul  in the form  Him  they  realize
= Those who worship Him on the paths of knowledge and yoga realize Him as Brahman and as the Supersoul.

@27
of worship  by the differences  one knows  of the Lord  the greatness
therefore  of the sun  to Him  I give  indeed  the comparison
= One comes to know the greatness of the Lord according to the different kinds of worship. That is why I compare Him to the sun.

@28
that  Nārāyaṇa  of Kṛṣṇa  His own form  non-different
one and the same  form  but  in shape  difference
= That Nārāyaṇa is non-different from Kṛṣṇa’s own form. They are one and the same divine form (*vigraha*), but Their appearance differs.
