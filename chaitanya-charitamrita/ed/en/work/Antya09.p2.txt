# Sārvabhauma’s reading *bhakti-pade*) — after ed/en-rev, with *mukti-pade* (“liberation”) in line 4.
# Kāśī Miśra’s speech (67–79) and the nested quotations follow the quotation marks of part 1.

@77
therefore  Your  mercy  earnestly awaiting
enduring  indeed  self-made  the fruits
with heart, speech and body  offering  obeisance  to You
lives  who  to the position of liberation  he  is heir
= ‘Therefore one who lives earnestly awaiting Your mercy, enduring the fruits of his own deeds and offering You obeisance with heart, words and body, is an heir to the position of liberation.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.8)

@78
here  You  settled  stay,  why  will You go  to Ālālanātha
anyone  to You  not  will make hear  of worldly men  talk
= Stay settled here. Why should You go to Ālālanātha? No one will make You listen to the talk of worldly men.

@79
if  Your  him  to protect  is  mind
today  who  protected,  He  will do  protection
= If You have a mind to protect him, He who protected him today will protect him still.”

@80
thus  saying  Kāśī Miśra  went  to his own residence
at midday  Pratāparudra  came  his  to the house
= Saying this, Kāśī Miśra went to his own residence, and at midday Pratāparudra came to his house.

@81
of Pratāparudra  one  there is  rule
as many  days  stays  he  in Śrī Puruṣottama
= Pratāparudra kept one rule for as long as he stayed in Śrī Puruṣottama:

@82
daily  coming  does  Miśra’s  of the feet  massage
of Jagannātha’s service  does  the ordered performance  hearing
= every day he would come and massage Miśra’s feet, and hear how the service of Jagannātha was being carried out in all its order and splendor (*bhiyāna*).

@83
the king  Miśra’s  feet  when  to press  began
then  Miśra  to him  something  by hints  said
= When the king began pressing Miśra’s feet, Miśra told him something in a roundabout way:

@84
Your Majesty,  hear  another  one  strange  piece of news
Mahāprabhu  the holy place  leaving  will go  to Ālālanātha
= “Your Majesty, hear one more strange piece of news: Mahāprabhu is going to leave the holy place (*kṣetra*) for Ālālanātha!”

@85
hearing  the king  sorrowful  became,  asked  the reason
then  Miśra  tells  him  all  the details
= Hearing this, the king became sorrowful and asked the reason. Then Miśra told him the whole story:

@86
Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka  onto the cāṅga  they raised
his  servant  coming  to the Lord  told
= “Gopīnātha Paṭṭanāyaka was raised onto the *cāṅga*, and his servant came and told the Lord.

@87
hearing  agitated  became  Mahāprabhu’s  mind
in anger  Gopīnātha  He did  much  rebuking
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu’s mind was agitated, and in anger He rebuked Gopīnātha severely:

@88
not controlling the senses  being  he does  the king’s business
in various  wrong ways  does  the king’s wealth  spending
= ‘He handles the king’s affairs without controlling his senses, and spends the king’s wealth in all sorts of wrong ways.

@89
than a brāhmaṇa’s property  greater  this  is  the king’s wealth
it  stealing  enjoyment  does  a great sinner  person
= The king’s wealth weighs more than a brāhmaṇa’s property; one who steals it to enjoy it is a great sinner.

@90
the king’s  salary  he eats,  and  theft  does
punishable by the king  is  he  of scripture  by the judgment
= He lives on the king’s salary and steals besides; by the judgment of scripture he deserves the king’s punishment.

@91
his own  money  asks for,  the king  not  does  punishment
the king  greatly righteous,  this one  is  sinful  cheat
= The king asks only for his own money and does not punish him. The king is most righteous; this man is a sinful cheat!

@92
the king’s money  not  he gives,  to Me  cries out loudly
this  great  distress  here  who  to bear  can
= He does not pay the king’s money, and he cries out loudly to Me (*phukāre*). Who here can bear such great distress?

@93
to Ālālanātha  going  there  free from care  I shall stay
of worldly men  good  bad  news  not  I shall hear
= I shall go to Ālālanātha and stay there free from care; I will not hear the news of worldly men, good or bad.’”

@94
this  hearing  says  the king  feeling  in the mind  pain
all  the money  I give up,  if  the Lord  stays  here
= Hearing this, the king felt pain in his heart and said, “I give up all the money, if only the Lord will stay here.

@95
for one  moment  the Lord’s  if  I obtain  sight
of millions of cintāmaṇi gems  gain  is not  to it  equal
= If I can have the Lord’s *darśana* for even a moment, gaining millions of *cintāmaṇi* gems is not its equal.

@96
what  paltry  thing  this  two lakh  kāhanas
life  kingdom  I make  at the Lord’s feet  an offering
= What paltry thing are these two lakh *kāhanas*? I offer up my life and my kingdom at the Lord’s feet (*nirmañchana*).”

@97
Miśra  says,  the money  you should give up  is not  the Lord’s  mind
they  suffer  this  not  goes  bearing
= Miśra said, “It is not the Lord’s wish that you give up the money. They are suffering, and that He cannot bear.”

@98
the king  says  to him  I  pain  not  give
onto the cāṅga  raising,  onto the swords  throwing  I  not  know
= The king said, “I mean him no pain. Raising him onto the *cāṅga*, throwing him onto the swords — I knew nothing of it.

@99
at Puruṣottama Jānā  he  made  mockery
that  Jānā  to him  showed  false  terror
= He mocked Puruṣottama Jānā, and that Jānā only gave him a false scare.

@100
you  go,  the Lord  keep  effort  making
here  I  to him  have released  all  the money
= Go and do all you can to keep the Lord here. Here and now I release him from all the money.”

@101
Miśra  says,  the money  you should give up  is not  in the Lord’s  mind
the money  if you give up  the Lord  perhaps  pain  feels
= Miśra said, “Your giving up the money is not what the Lord has in mind. If you give it up, the Lord may perhaps be pained.”

@102
the king  says,  the money  I shall give up  this  not  you should say
naturally  to me  dear  they  this  you should make known
= The king said, “I shall give up the money, but do not tell Him that. Let Him know only this: they are naturally dear to me.

@103
Bhavānanda Rāya  my  worthy of honor and pride
his  sons  my  naturally  affection
= Bhavānanda Rāya is worthy of my honor and my pride (*pūjya-garvita*), and I naturally feel affection for his sons.”

@104
thus  saying  to Miśra  offering obeisance  home  he went
Gopīnātha  the Baḍa Jānā  calling  brought
= Saying this, the king offered obeisance to Miśra and went home. The Baḍa Jānā called Gopīnātha and brought him.

@105
the king  says  all  the money  to you  I have released
that same  Mālajāṭhyā Daṇḍapāṭa  to you  indeed  I have given
= The king said, “I release you from all the money, and I give you that same Mālajāṭhyā Daṇḍapāṭa again.

@106
another  time  like this  not  consume  the king’s wealth
today  from  I have given  to you  double  salary
= Do not consume the king’s wealth like this again. From today I give you double salary.”

@107
thus  saying  silk cloth  on him  he put
the Lord’s permission  taking  go,  leave  to you  was given
= Saying this, he dressed him in silk cloth (*netadhaṭī*): “Take the Lord’s permission and go; you have your leave.”

@108
in the supreme goal  the Lord’s  mercy,  that  let it stay  far off
unlimited  its  fruit,  who  to tell  can
= Leave aside the Lord’s mercy in the supreme goal of life (*paramārtha*): its fruits are unlimited — who can tell them?

@109
of royal wealth  the fruit  this  of mercy  from a semblance
its  reckoning  anyone’s  to the mind  not  comes
= This fruit of royal wealth came from a mere semblance (*ābhāsa*) of His mercy! No one can even conceive of its reckoning!

@110
where  onto the cāṅga  raising  takes  wealth and life
where  everything  releasing  that  kingdom and more  bestowing
= On one hand, he was raised onto the *cāṅga* to lose his wealth and life! On the other, everything was forgiven, and a kingdom and more were bestowed on him!

@111
where  all he owned  selling  takes,  to be paid  not  goes  the money
where  double  salary,  dresses  in silk cloth
= On one hand, all he owned was to be sold off, and still the money could not be paid! On the other, a double salary, and he is dressed in silk!

@112
the Lord’s  wish  is not  him  the money  would release
double  salary  making  again  the post  would give
= The Lord had no wish that his money be remitted, that his salary be doubled, or that he be given his post again.

@113
still  his  servant  coming  made  a petition
by that  disturbed  became  when  Mahāprabhu’s  mind
= Still, his servant came and made his petition, and Mahāprabhu’s mind was disturbed by it.

@114
worldly happiness  to give  of the Lord  is not  inclination
of the petition  even by the power  still  bears  so much  fruit
= The Lord had no inclination to give worldly happiness; yet even by the power of a mere petition such fruit was borne.

@115
who  to tell  can  Gaura’s  wonderful  nature
Brahmā, Śiva and others  whose  not  find  inner intent
= Who can describe the wonderful nature of Gaura? Even Brahmā, Śiva and the others cannot fathom His inner intent.

@116
here  Kāśī Miśra  coming  the Lord’s  at the feet
the king’s  conduct  all  made  report
= Meanwhile Kāśī Miśra came to the Lord’s feet and reported all that the king had done.

@117
the Lord  says  Kāśī Miśra,  what  you  have done
a gift from the king  you  Me  have made accept
= The Lord said, “Kāśī Miśra, what have you done? Have you made Me accept a gift from the king (*rāja-pratigraha*)?”

@118
Miśra  says  hear,  Lord,  the king’s  words
without guile  the king  this  made  request
= Miśra said, “Lord, hear the king’s words. Without any guile the king made this request:

@119
the Lord  so that  not  should think  the king  My  for the sake
two lakh  kāhanas  of money  gave  releasing
= ‘Let the Lord not think that the king has given up two lakh *kāhanas* of money for His sake.

@120
Bhavānanda’s  sons  all  to me  most dear
them all  I  see  as my own self
= All of Bhavānanda’s sons are most dear to me; I regard them all as my own self.

@121
therefore  here  there  I give  posts
they eat,  drink,  plunder,  give away,  not  I make  scrutiny
= So I give them posts here and there. They eat, drink, plunder and give away, and I do not look into it.

@122
in Rājamahendrī  governor  I made  Rāmānanda Rāya
what  he consumed,  what  he gave,  is not  accounting due
= In Rājamahendrī I made Rāmānanda Rāya governor (*rājā*). Whatever he consumed and whatever he gave away, no account was asked of him.

@123
Gopīnātha  in this way  official business  doing
two or four lakh  kāhanas  remains  indeed  having consumed
= In the same way, while handling the king’s business, Gopīnātha goes on consuming two to four lakh *kāhanas*.

@124
some  he gives,  some  not  gives,  not  I make  scrutiny
the Jānā  with  through ill will  trouble  he got  this time
= He pays some and does not pay some, and I do not look into it. This time he came to grief through his ill will with the Jānā.

@125
the Jānā  so much  did  this  I  not  know
Bhavānanda’s  sons all  as my own self  I regard
= I did not know that the Jānā had gone so far. I regard all of Bhavānanda’s sons as my own self.

@126
His  for the sake  the money  I give up  this  let Him not  consider
naturally  my  love  is  with them
= Let Him not consider that I am giving up the money for His sake (*māt*, “not”): my love for them is natural.’”

@127
hearing  the king’s  humility  the Lord’s  joy
at this  time  came  there  Bhavānanda Rāya
= Hearing of the king’s humility, the Lord was pleased. Just then Bhavānanda Rāya arrived there.

@128
five  sons  with  coming  fell  at the feet
raising  the Lord  him  did  embracing
= He came with his five sons and fell at the Lord’s feet. The Lord raised him up and embraced him.

@129
Rāmānanda Rāya  and the others  all  met
Bhavānanda Rāya  then  to speak  began
= Rāmānanda Rāya and all the others met the Lord. Then Bhavānanda Rāya began to speak:

@130
Your  servants  this  whole  my  family
in this  danger  saving,  Lord,  again  You took  for a price
= “This whole family of mine are Your servants. Saving us in this danger, Lord, You have bought us again for a price (*nilā mūla*).

@131
affection for devotees  now  manifest  You have made
formerly  as  the five Pāṇḍavas  from danger  You delivered
= Now You have shown Your affection for Your devotees, as long ago You delivered the five Pāṇḍavas from danger.”

@132
silk cloth  on his head  Gopīnātha  at the feet  fell
the king’s  mercy  story  all  he told
= Gopīnātha, the silk cloth on his head, fell at the Lord’s feet and told the whole story of the king’s kindness:

@133
the outstanding money  remitted,  and  double  salary  he made
again  the post  giving  silk cloth  he dressed
= “He remitted the money I owed and doubled my salary; he gave me my post again and dressed me in silk.

@134
where  of the cāṅga  on top  that  of death  peril
where  silk cloth  again  all this  grace
= On one hand, that peril of death on top of the *cāṅga*! On the other, silk cloth again — all this is Your grace!

@135
of the cāṅga  on top  Your  feet  meditation  I did
of the feet  remembering  by the power  this  fruit  I obtained
= On the *cāṅga* I meditated on Your feet, and by the power of remembering Your feet I obtained this fruit.

@136
people  amazement  my  this  all  seeing
praise  Your  of mercy  the glory  singing
= Seeing all this happen to me, people are amazed; they sing and praise the glory of Your mercy.

@137
but  Your  of remembrance  is not  this  the chief fruit
a semblance of fruit  this  because  worldly things  fickle
= But this is not the chief fruit of remembering You. It is only a semblance of fruit (*phalābhāsa*), for worldly things are fickle.

@138
Rāmānanda Rāya,  Vāṇīnātha  You made  free from worldly affairs
that  mercy  on me  is not,  by which  such  comes about
= You made Rāmānanda Rāya and Vāṇīnātha free from worldly affairs. On me there is not that mercy by which this comes about!

@139
pure  mercy  show,  Gosāñi,  take away  worldly affairs
weary  I have become,  by me  worldly affairs  not  can be done
= Show me pure mercy, Gosāñi: take away my worldly affairs. I have grown weary of them (*nirviṇṇa*); worldly affairs are not for me.”

@140
the Lord  says  sannyāsīs  if  will become  five  persons
large household  your  who  will do  maintaining
= The Lord said, “If all five of you become *sannyāsīs*, who will maintain your large household?

@141
great  worldly affairs  do,  or  detached  indifferent
in birth  after birth  you  five  My  own servants
= Whether you manage great worldly affairs or live detached and indifferent to the world, birth after birth you five are My own servants.

@142
but  My  do  one  order  keeping
spending  not  do  any  of the king’s  principal
= But keep one order of Mine: do not spend any of the king’s principal (*mūla-dhana*).

@143
the king’s  principal  paying  whatever  profit  is
that  money  do  in various  religious and pious works  spending
= After paying the king his principal, spend whatever profit remains on various religious and pious works.

@144
sinful spending  not  do  by which  both worlds  are lost
thus  saying  to all  He gave  leave
= Do not spend on sinful things, by which both worlds are lost.” Saying this, He gave them all leave to go.

@145
in Rāya’s  house  the Lord’s  seeming mercy  I have told
affection for devotees  quality  in which  manifest  became
= I have told of the Lord’s *kṛpā-vivarta* — a seeming mercy — in the house of Bhavānanda Rāya, in which His quality of affection for His devotees was revealed.

@146
all  embracing  leave  when  He gave
the sound of Hari  making  all  devotees  rising  went
= When He embraced them all and gave them leave, all the devotees rose and went away, calling out the name of Hari.

@147
the Lord’s  mercy  seeing  everyone’s  was  amazement
they  to understand  cannot  the Lord’s  behavior
= Seeing the Lord’s mercy, everyone was amazed; they could not understand the Lord’s conduct.

@148
they  all  when  mercy  to show  entreated
Me  from  anything  is not  the Lord  then  said
= When they had all entreated Him to show mercy, the Lord had said, “Nothing will be done by Me.”

@149
Gopīnātha’s  censure,  and  His own  dejection
this  only  He spoke,  of this  not  will understand  the secret
= He spoke only censure of Gopīnātha and His own weariness with the world (*nirveda*); no one will understand the secret of this.

@150
Kāśī Miśra  not  He entreated,  the king  not  He entreated
effort  without  all this  fruit  He gave
= He did not entreat Kāśī Miśra, nor did He entreat the king; yet without any effort He gave all this fruit.

@151
Caitanya’s  conduct  this  supremely  deep
he  understands  His  at the feet  whose  mind  steady
= This conduct of Śrī Caitanya is supremely deep. Only he understands it whose mind is steady (*dhīra*) at His feet.

@152
whoever  this  hears  the Lord’s  affection  display
loving devotion  attains,  his  danger  goes  to destruction
= Whoever hears this display of the Lord’s affection attains loving devotion, and his dangers are destroyed.

@153
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
