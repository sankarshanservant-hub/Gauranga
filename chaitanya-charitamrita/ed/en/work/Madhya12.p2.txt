# CC Madhya 12, stage A, part 2 (verses 112–222); read by build_en.py together with Madhya12.txt

@112
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  they make  for a pot  a request
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  they make  of a pot  the handing over
= Saying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” some asked for a water pot, and saying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” others handed one over.

@113
whoever  whoever  speaks  he  speaks  by Kṛṣṇa’s name
Kṛṣṇa’s name  became  the signal  in all tasks
= Whoever had something to say said only the name of Kṛṣṇa: Kṛṣṇa’s name became the signal for every task.

@114
absorbed in love  the Lord  says  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  the name
alone  absorbed in love  He does  of a hundred people  the work
= Absorbed in love, the Lord chanted the name “Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa,” and in that absorption He alone did the work of a hundred men.

@115
with a hundred hands  He does  as if  washing and cleaning
to each person’s side  going  He gives  instruction
= It was as if He were washing and cleaning with a hundred hands. Going up to each person, He showed him how to do the work.

@116
good  work  seeing  him  He gives  praise
with His mind  not  agreeing  He gives  a pure  rebuke
= When He saw good work, He praised the worker; when the work did not satisfy Him, He gave a rebuke — but a pure, purifying one.

@117
you  well  have done  teach  others
in this way  good  work  they  so that  do
= “You have done well. Now teach the others, so that they too do such good work.”

@118
these words  hearing  all  abashed  becoming
properly  the work  do  all  the mind  giving
= Hearing these words, everyone felt abashed, and all of them set about the work properly, with full attention.

@119
then  washing  they made  of Śrī Jagamohana
the bhoga-mandira  and the rest  then  they made  washing
= Then they washed the Śrī Jagamohana, and after that the *bhoga-mandira* and the other halls.

@120
the Nāṭaśālā  washing  they washed  the courtyard and the yard
the kitchen  and the rest  including  they made  washing
= After washing the Nāṭaśālā, they washed the courtyards, and then the kitchen and all the other rooms.

@121
of the temple  on four sides  washing  they did
all  the inner rooms  thoroughly  they washed
= They washed the temple on all four sides and thoroughly washed all the inner rooms.

@122
at that time  a Gauḍīyā  one  sensible  simple
on the Lord’s  pair of feet  poured  water from a pot
= At that moment a simple and sensible Gauḍīyā (devotee from Bengal) poured water from his pot over the Lord’s feet.

@123
that  water  taking  himself  drinking  did
that  seeing  in Mahāprabhu’s  mind  anger  arose
= Then he took that water and drank it himself. Seeing this, Mahāprabhu became angry.

@124
although  the Lord  with him  had become  pleased
of dharma  the upholding  for the sake of  outwardly  great anger
= Although the Lord was pleased with him, to uphold proper conduct (*dharma*) He showed great anger outwardly.

@125
of teaching  for the sake  Svarūpa  calling  He said  to him
just  see  of your  “Gauḍīyā”  the behavior
= To teach everyone, He called Svarūpa and said to him, “Just look at how your ‘Gauḍīyā’ behaves!

@126
in the Lord’s temple  My  feet  he washed
that  water  himself  taking  drinking  did
= He washed My feet in the temple of the Lord, and then took that water and drank it himself.

@127
for this  offense  My  where  will be  end
your  “Gauḍīyā”  makes  such  misfortune
= What will become of Me for this offense? Your ‘Gauḍīyā’ has brought such misfortune upon Me!”

@128
then  Svarūpa Gosāñi  his  on the neck  hand  placing
a shove  giving  of the temple  outside  he put  taking (him)
= Then Svarūpa Gosāñi took him by the neck, gave him a shove, and put him outside the temple compound.

@129
again  coming  at the Lord’s feet  he made  a humble plea
“of an ignorant man  the offense”  forgiveness  to make  is fitting
= Coming back, he humbly pleaded at the Lord’s feet: “It is fitting to forgive the offense of an ignorant man.”

@130
then  in Mahāprabhu’s  mind  satisfaction  arose
rows  making  on two  sides  everyone  He seated
= Then Mahāprabhu was satisfied, and He seated everyone in rows on two sides.

@131
Himself  sitting  in the middle  with His own  hand
straw  grit  rubbish  He began  to pick up
= Sitting between them, He began to pick up straw, grit and rubbish with His own hand.

@132
who  how much  picks up  all  together  I shall gather
whose  little  from him  cakes and sweet drinks  I shall take
= “I shall gather together all that is collected and see who has picked up how much. Whoever has the least will have to pay Me in cakes and sweet drinks.”

@133
in this way  the whole  temple  He made  cleansing
cool  spotless  He made (it)  like  His own mind
= In this way He cleansed the whole temple and made it cool and spotless, like His own heart.

@134
the drain  letting out by  when  the water  they made flow
a new  river  as if  with the sea  met
= When the water was let out through the drain, it looked like a new river rushing to meet the sea.

@135
in this way  before the temple gate  roads  as many as
all  they cleansed  that  who  will describe  how much
= In the same way they cleansed all the roads in front of the temple gate. Who could describe how much was cleansed?

@136
the Nṛsiṁha temple  inside and outside  He cleansed
a moment  rest  taking  dance  He began
= He cleansed the temple of Nṛsiṁha inside and out, and after resting for a moment He began to dance.

@137
on all sides  the devotees  do  kīrtana
in the middle  dancing  does  the Lord  like a maddened lion
= On all sides the devotees sang *kīrtana*, and in the middle the Lord danced like a maddened lion.

@138
perspiration  trembling  pallor and tears  thrills  roaring
His own body  washing  ahead  flow  streams of tears
= Perspiration, trembling, pallor, tears, thrills of rapture and loud roars appeared in Him. Streams of tears washed His body and ran on ahead of Him.

@139
on all sides  the devotees’ bodies  made  washing
of Śrāvaṇa  a cloud  as if  makes  raining
= On every side they washed the devotees’ bodies, like a cloud pouring rain in the month of Śrāvaṇa.

@140
by the very loud saṅkīrtana  the sky  filled
by the Lord’s  leaping dance  an earthquake  occurred
= The sky was filled with the very loud *saṅkīrtana*, and the earth quaked under the Lord’s high-leaping dance.

@141
Svarūpa’s  loud singing  to the Lord  always  is pleasing
in bliss  leaping  dance  does  Gaurarāya
= Svarūpa’s loud singing always delighted the Lord, and Gaurarāya danced in bliss, leaping high.

@142
in this way  for some time  dance  indeed  having done
rest  took  the Lord  the time  knowing
= After dancing like this for some time, the Lord, aware of the hour, stopped to rest.

@143
of Ācārya Gosāñi  the son  Śrī Gopāla  by name
dance  to do  to him  the order  gave  Gauradhāma
= Ācārya Gosāñi had a son named Śrī Gopāla, and the Lord, Gauradhāma, ordered him to dance.

@144
absorbed in love  dance  having done  he became  faint
unconscious  becoming  he  fell  to the ground
= Dancing in the absorption of love, he fainted and fell unconscious to the ground.

@145
in haste  the Ācārya  him  took  on his lap
breathless  seeing  the Ācārya  became  distraught
= The Ācārya hastily took him on his lap, and seeing that he was not breathing, he became distraught.

@146
Nṛsiṁha’s  mantra  reciting  he casts  sprinkles of water
of the roar  by the sound  the universe  goes  bursting
= Reciting the *mantra* of Nṛsiṁha, he sprinkled him with water, and at the sound of his roar the universe seemed to burst apart.

@147
much  he did  still  not  comes  consciousness
the Ācārya  weeps  weep  all  the devotees
= He tried many things, yet the boy did not regain consciousness. The Ācārya wept, and all the devotees wept with him.

@148
then  Mahāprabhu  his  on the chest  hand  placed
“Rise up  Gopāla”  saying  in a loud voice  He spoke
= Then Mahāprabhu placed His hand on the boy’s chest and called out loudly, “Rise up, Gopāla!”

@149
on just hearing  Gopāla’s  came  consciousness
“Hari”  saying  dance  do  all the devotees
= As soon as he heard this, Gopāla regained consciousness, and all the devotees danced, calling out “Hari!”

@150
this  pastime  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa
therefore  brief  making (it)  I have made  the description
= Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this pastime, and so I have told it only briefly.

@151
then  Mahāprabhu  a little while  rest  having taken
bathing  to do  went  the devotees  taking
= Then, after resting a little, Mahāprabhu went with the devotees to bathe.

@152
on the bank  climbing up  puts on  the Lord  dry  cloth
to Nṛsiṁhadeva  bowing down  He went  to the garden
= Coming up onto the bank, the Lord put on dry cloth, bowed down to Nṛsiṁhadeva, and went into the garden.

@153
in the garden  sat  the Lord  the devotees  with
then  Vāṇīnātha  came  mahā-prasāda  bringing
= The Lord sat down in the garden with the devotees, and then Vāṇīnātha arrived with *mahā-prasāda*.

@154
Kāśī Miśra  Tulasī the temple superintendent  the two
five hundred  people  as much as  would eat
= Kāśī Miśra and Tulasī, the temple superintendent (*paḍichā*), had sent as much food as five hundred people could eat:

@155
that much  rice, cakes and sweet drinks  all  they sent
seeing  in Mahāprabhu’s  mind  satisfaction  arose
= all that rice, all those cakes and sweet drinks. Seeing it, Mahāprabhu was very pleased.

@156
Purī Gosāñi  Mahāprabhu  Bhāratī  Brahmānanda
Advaita Ācārya  and  Prabhu Nityānanda
= Purī Gosāñi, Mahāprabhu, Brahmānanda Bhāratī, Advaita Ācārya and Nityānanda Prabhu;

@157
Ācāryaratna  Ācāryanidhi  Śrīvāsa  Gadādhara
Śaṅkara  Nandanācārya  and  Rāghava  Vakreśvara
= Ācāryaratna, Ācāryanidhi, Śrīvāsa, Gadādhara, Śaṅkara, Nandanācārya, Rāghava and Vakreśvara —

@158
the Lord’s order  receiving  sits  himself  Sārvabhauma
the wooden seat  upon  the Lord  sits  with  the devotees
= at the Lord’s bidding Sārvabhauma, too, sat down; and the Lord sat on a raised wooden seat (*piṇḍā*) together with these devotees.

@159
below that  below that  making  in order
the garden  filling  sit  the devotees  to do  eating
= Below them, row after row, the devotees sat down to eat, filling the whole garden.

@160
“Haridāsa”  saying  the Lord  calls  again and again
at a distance  remaining  Haridāsa  makes  a humble submission
= The Lord called again and again, “Haridāsa!” But Haridāsa stayed at a distance and humbly submitted:

@161
with the devotees  the Lord  may He make  of prasāda  acceptance
in this company  to sit  fit  am not  I  worthless
= “Let the Lord take *prasāda* with the devotees. Worthless as I am, I am not fit to sit in this company.

@162
later  to me  prasāda  Govinda  will give  outside the gate
the mind  knowing  the Lord  again  not  called  to him
= Later Govinda will give me *prasāda* outside the gate.” Knowing his mind, the Lord did not call him again.

@163-164
Svarūpa Gosāñi  Jagadānanda  Dāmodara
Kāśīśvara  Gopīnātha  Vāṇīnātha  Śaṅkara
serving  do  there  these  seven people
from time to time  the sound of “Hari”  make  the devotees
= Svarūpa Gosāñi, Jagadānanda, Dāmodara, Kāśīśvara, Gopīnātha, Vāṇīnātha and Śaṅkara — these seven served the food there, while from time to time the devotees called out “Hari!”

@165
the meal on the riverbank  Kṛṣṇa  formerly  as  made
that  pastime  in Mahāprabhu’s  mind  remembrance  arose
= Mahāprabhu remembered the pastime of long ago when Kṛṣṇa ate with His friends on the riverbank (*pulina-bhojana*).

@166
although  in the absorption of love  the Lord  became  unsteady
the occasion  understanding  the Lord  became  somewhat  composed
= Though the absorption of love shook the Lord, He understood the occasion and composed Himself somewhat.

@167
the Lord  says  to Me  give  lāphrā vegetables
cakes and sweet drinks  amṛta-guṭikā  give  to the devotees
= The Lord said, “Give Me the *lāphrā*, the plain mixed vegetables, and give the devotees the cakes, sweet drinks and *amṛta-guṭikā*.”

@168
all-knowing  the Lord  knows  whom  what  pleases
to each one  that  He has given  through Svarūpa
= The all-knowing Lord knew what each person liked, and through Svarūpa He had each one served just that.

@169
Jagadānanda  goes about  serving  to do
on the Lord’s  plate  choice things  he puts  unexpectedly
= Jagadānanda went about serving, and now and then he would unexpectedly put some choice item on the Lord’s plate.

@170
although  when given  the Lord  at him  shows  anger
by force or by trick  still  he gives  having given  he  is satisfied
= Though the Lord grew angry with him for it, Jagadānanda still gave Him such things, by force or by trick, and only when he had given them was he satisfied.

@171
once more  that  food  he makes  watching
of him  out of fear  the Lord  a little  does  eating
= Then he would keep his eye on that food, and out of fear of him the Lord would eat a little.
