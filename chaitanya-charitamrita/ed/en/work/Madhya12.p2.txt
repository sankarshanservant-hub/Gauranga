# CC Madhya 12, stage A, part 2 (verses 112–222); read by build_en.py together with Madhya12.txt

@112
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  they make  for a pot  a request
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  saying  they make  of a pot  the handing over
= Saying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” some asked for a water pot, and saying “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” others handed one over.

@113
whoever  whoever  speaks  he  speaks  by Kṛṣṇa’s name
Kṛṣṇa’s name  became  the signal  in all tasks
= Whoever had something to say said only the name of Kṛṣṇa: Kṛṣṇa’s name became the signal for every task.

@114
absorbed in love  the Lord  says  “Kṛṣṇa”  “Kṛṣṇa”  the name
alone  absorbed in love  He does  of a hundred people  the work
= Absorbed in love, the Lord chanted the name “Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa,” and in that absorption He alone did the work of a hundred men.

@115
with a hundred hands  He does  as if  washing and cleaning
to each person’s side  going  He gives  instruction
= It was as if He were washing and cleaning with a hundred hands. Going up to each person, He showed him how to do the work.

@116
good  work  seeing  him  He gives  praise
with His mind  not  agreeing  He gives  a pure  rebuke
= When He saw good work, He praised the worker; when the work did not satisfy Him, He gave a rebuke — but a pure, purifying one.

@117
you  well  have done  teach  others
in this way  good  work  they  so that  do
= “You have done well. Now teach the others, so that they too do such good work.”

@118
these words  hearing  all  abashed  becoming
properly  the work  do  all  the mind  giving
= Hearing these words, everyone felt abashed, and all of them set about the work properly, with full attention.

@119
then  washing  they made  of Śrī Jagamohana
the bhoga-mandira  and the rest  then  they made  washing
= Then they washed the Śrī Jagamohana, and after that the *bhoga-mandira* and the other halls.

@120
the Nāṭaśālā  washing  they washed  the courtyard and the yard
the kitchen  and the rest  including  they made  washing
= After washing the Nāṭaśālā, they washed the courtyards, and then the kitchen and all the other rooms.

@121
of the temple  on four sides  washing  they did
all  the inner rooms  thoroughly  they washed
= They washed the temple on all four sides and thoroughly washed all the inner rooms.

@122
at that time  a Gauḍīyā  one  sensible  simple
on the Lord’s  pair of feet  poured  water from a pot
= At that moment a simple and sensible Gauḍīyā (devotee from Bengal) poured water from his pot over the Lord’s feet.

@123
that  water  taking  himself  drinking  did
that  seeing  in Mahāprabhu’s  mind  anger  arose
= Then he took that water and drank it himself. Seeing this, Mahāprabhu became angry.

@124
although  the Lord  with him  had become  pleased
of dharma  the upholding  for the sake of  outwardly  great anger
= Although the Lord was pleased with him, to uphold proper conduct (*dharma*) He showed great anger outwardly.

@125
of teaching  for the sake  Svarūpa  calling  He said  to him
just  see  of your  “Gauḍīyā”  the behavior
= To teach everyone, He called Svarūpa and said to him, “Just look at how your ‘Gauḍīyā’ behaves!

@126
in the Lord’s temple  My  feet  he washed
that  water  himself  taking  drinking  did
= He washed My feet in the temple of the Lord, and then took that water and drank it himself.

@127
for this  offense  My  where  will be  end
your  “Gauḍīyā”  makes  such  misfortune
= What will become of Me for this offense? Your ‘Gauḍīyā’ has brought such misfortune upon Me!”

@128
then  Svarūpa Gosāñi  his  on the neck  hand  placing
a shove  giving  of the temple  outside  he put  taking (him)
= Then Svarūpa Gosāñi took him by the neck, gave him a shove, and put him outside the temple compound.

@129
again  coming  at the Lord’s feet  he made  a humble plea
“of an ignorant man  the offense”  forgiveness  to make  is fitting
= Coming back, he humbly pleaded at the Lord’s feet: “It is fitting to forgive the offense of an ignorant man.”

@130
then  in Mahāprabhu’s  mind  satisfaction  arose
rows  making  on two  sides  everyone  He seated
= Then Mahāprabhu was satisfied, and He seated everyone in rows on two sides.

@131
Himself  sitting  in the middle  with His own  hand
straw  grit  rubbish  He began  to pick up
= Sitting between them, He began to pick up straw, grit and rubbish with His own hand.

@132
who  how much  picks up  all  together  I shall gather
whose  little  from him  cakes and sweet drinks  I shall take
= “I shall gather together all that is collected and see who has picked up how much. Whoever has the least will have to pay Me in cakes and sweet drinks.”

@133
in this way  the whole  temple  He made  cleansing
cool  spotless  He made (it)  like  His own mind
= In this way He cleansed the whole temple and made it cool and spotless, like His own heart.

@134
the drain  letting out by  when  the water  they made flow
a new  river  as if  with the sea  met
= When the water was let out through the drain, it looked like a new river rushing to meet the sea.

@135
in this way  before the temple gate  roads  as many as
all  they cleansed  that  who  will describe  how much
= In the same way they cleansed all the roads in front of the temple gate. Who could describe how much was cleansed?

@136
the Nṛsiṁha temple  inside and outside  He cleansed
a moment  rest  taking  dance  He began
= He cleansed the temple of Nṛsiṁha inside and out, and after resting for a moment He began to dance.

@137
on all sides  the devotees  do  kīrtana
in the middle  dancing  does  the Lord  like a maddened lion
= On all sides the devotees sang *kīrtana*, and in the middle the Lord danced like a maddened lion.

@138
perspiration  trembling  pallor and tears  thrills  roaring
His own body  washing  ahead  flow  streams of tears
= Perspiration, trembling, pallor, tears, thrills of rapture and loud roars appeared in Him. Streams of tears washed His body and ran on ahead of Him.

@139
on all sides  the devotees’ bodies  made  washing
of Śrāvaṇa  a cloud  as if  makes  raining
= On every side they washed the devotees’ bodies, like a cloud pouring rain in the month of Śrāvaṇa.

@140
by the very loud saṅkīrtana  the sky  filled
by the Lord’s  leaping dance  an earthquake  occurred
= The sky was filled with the very loud *saṅkīrtana*, and the earth quaked under the Lord’s high-leaping dance.

@141
Svarūpa’s  loud singing  to the Lord  always  is pleasing
in bliss  leaping  dance  does  Gaurarāya
= Svarūpa’s loud singing always delighted the Lord, and Gaurarāya danced in bliss, leaping high.
