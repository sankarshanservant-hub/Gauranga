@title Śrī Caitanya’s Feast at Sārvabhauma’s House

@1
in the house of Sārvabhauma  eating
His own reviler  Amogha
accepting  manifest  made
Gaura  His own  subjection to His devotees
= While taking His meal at Sārvabhauma’s house, Gaura accepted Amogha, who had reviled Him, and so made plain that He is subject to His devotees.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
glory  to the listeners of the Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta
the Caitanya-caritāmṛta  —  whose  treasure of life
= Glory to the listeners of the *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, for whom the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* is the treasure of their lives!

@4
thus  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees
in Nīlācala  staying  does  dancing and singing  joyfully
= In this way Mahāprabhu stayed in Nīlācala with His devotees, joyfully dancing and singing.

@5
in the first year  Jagannātha  seeing
dancing and singing  does  prostrations  obeisances  prayers
= In that first year He went to see Jagannātha; He danced and sang, fell prostrate, bowed down and offered prayers.

@6
the upala-bhoga  when it begins  makes  outside  going forth
Haridāsa  meeting  comes  to His own dwelling
= When the *upala-bhoga* began, He would come out of the temple, meet Haridāsa, and return to His own dwelling.

@7
at home  sitting  does  the Lord  the saṅkīrtana of the Name
Advaita  coming  does  the Lord’s  worship
= Sitting at home, the Lord would chant the Holy Name, and Advaita would come and worship Him.

@8
with fragrant water  offers  water for the feet  water for sipping
over all the limbs  smears  of the Lord  fragrant  sandalwood
= Advaita offered Him fragrant water to wash His feet and to sip, and smeared fragrant sandalwood paste over all His limbs.

@9
on the neck  a garland  puts  on the head  placed  tulasī buds
joined  with hands  prayers  offers  at the feet  bowing
= He put a garland around the Lord’s neck and placed *tulasī* buds on His head; then he bowed at His feet and offered prayers with joined palms.

@10
on the worship plate  of flowers and tulasī  remnant  that  was
that  all  taking  the Lord  the Ācārya  worshiped
= Taking whatever flowers and *tulasī* were left on the worship plate, the Lord worshiped the Ācārya with them.

@11
who  You are  that  You are  obeisance  be  to You  this  mantra  recites
mouth-music  making  the Lord  makes laugh  the Ācārya
= He recited the *mantra* “Whoever You are, that You are — obeisance to You,” and made the Ācārya laugh with sounds made with His mouth.

@12
thus  to one another  make  obeisance
the Lord  invitation  makes  the Ācārya  time  after time
= So They paid obeisance to one another, and again and again the Ācārya invited the Lord to a meal.

@13
the Ācārya’s  invitation  —  a wonderful story
in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa
= The story of the Ācārya’s invitations is wonderful, and Vṛndāvana dāsa has described it in detail.

@14
repetition  would be  it  not  I made  description
other  devotees  make  to the Lord  invitation
= To tell it again would be repetition, so I have not described it. The other devotees also invited the Lord.

@15
one  by one  day  in one  after another  devotee’s home  a festival
with the Lord  there  eating  do  devotees  all
= Day after day there was a festival in one devotee’s home after another, and all the devotees ate there with the Lord.

@16
four months  stayed  all  with Mahāprabhu
of Jagannātha  various  festivals  see  with great joy
= For four months all the devotees stayed with Mahāprabhu and watched Jagannātha’s various festivals with great joy.

@17
on the day of the festival of Kṛṣṇa’s birth  Nanda’s great festival
in cowherd dress  became  the Lord  taking  all the devotees
= On the festival of Kṛṣṇa’s birth comes Nanda’s great celebration. That day the Lord and all the devotees dressed as cowherds.

@18
a yoke of yogurt and milk  the Lord  on His own shoulder  placing
to the festival place  came  saying  “Hari”  “Hari”
= Carrying a yoke of yogurt and milk on His shoulder, the Lord came to the festival grounds calling out, “Hari! Hari!”

@19
Kānāi Khuṭiyā  was  Nanda’s dress  wearing
Jagannātha Māhāti  had become  the Queen of Vraja
= Kānāi Khuṭiyā was dressed as Nanda, and Jagannātha Māhāti had become Yaśodā, the Queen of Vraja.

@20
himself  Pratāparudra  and  Kāśī Miśra
Sārvabhauma  and  the paḍichā-pātra  Tulasī
= King Pratāparudra himself was there, with Kāśī Miśra, Sārvabhauma and Tulasī, the temple *paḍichā*.

@21
them all  taking  the Lord  does  joyful dancing
yogurt and milk  with turmeric water  fills  everyone’s  body
= With them all the Lord danced joyfully, and everyone was drenched in yogurt, milk and turmeric water.

@22
Advaita  says  —  truth  I speak  not  do  anger
the staff  to twirl  You can  then  I know  a cowherd
= Advaita said, “I am telling You the truth — don’t be angry. Only if You can twirl a staff will I know You are a cowherd.”

@23
then  the staff  taking  the Lord  to twirl  began
time  after time  into the sky  throwing  snatching  caught
= Then the Lord took a staff and began to twirl it, tossing it into the air again and again and catching it.

@24
of the head  above  in front  behind  on both sides
between the feet  twirls  the staff  —  seeing  the people  laugh
= He twirled the staff over His head, in front, behind, on both sides and even between His feet, and the people laughed to see it.

@25
a firebrand’s circle  like  the staff  twirls
seeing  in the hearts of all people  wonder  arises
= He whirled the staff like a circle of fire traced by a firebrand, and all who saw it were struck with wonder.

@26
in the same way  Nityānanda  twirls  a staff
who  will understand  that  of the two  the cowherd mood  hidden
= Nityānanda twirled a staff in the same way. Who can understand the hidden cowherd mood of these two?

@27
by Pratāparudra’s  order  the paḍichā Tulasī
a prasāda cloth of Jagannātha  one  taking  coming
= At Pratāparudra’s order, Tulasī the *paḍichā* came, bringing a cloth that had been offered to Jagannātha.

@28
costly  cloth  the Lord  on the head  tied
the Ācārya and others  the Lord’s  people  clothed
= The Lord tied the costly cloth around His head, and the Ācārya and the Lord’s other companions were given it to wear as well.

@29
Kānāi Khuṭiyā  Jagannātha  —  the two
in ecstasy  gave away  at home  was  whatever  wealth
= Kānāi Khuṭiyā and Jagannātha, the two of them, in their ecstasy gave away all the wealth they had at home.

@30
seeing  Mahāprabhu  great  satisfaction  felt
as mother and father  to both  obeisance  made
= Seeing this, Mahāprabhu was very pleased and bowed down to the two of them as to His mother and father.

@31
in great ecstasy  the Lord  came  to His own home
thus  pastimes  performs  Gaurāṅga-sundara
= In deep ecstasy the Lord returned home. Such were the pastimes of Gaurāṅgasundara.

@32
Vijayā-daśamī  —  of the conquest of Laṅkā  on the day
a monkey army  made  the Lord  taking  the devotees
= On Vijayā-daśamī, the day of the conquest of Laṅkā, the Lord made the devotees into an army of monkeys.

@33
in the mood of Hanumān  the Lord  a tree branch  taking
on the fort of Laṅkā  climbing  throws down  Laṅkā  breaking
= Absorbed in the mood of Hanumān, the Lord took a tree branch, climbed the ramparts of Laṅkā and tore Laṅkā down.

@34
where is  the wretch Rāvaṇa  the Lord  says  in a fit of anger
the Mother of the world  stole  the sinner  I will kill  with all his race
= In a fit of anger the Lord cried, “Where is that wretch Rāvaṇa? The sinner has carried off the Mother of the world! I will kill him and all his race!”

@35
of the Lord  the ecstasy  seeing  people  amazed
all people  “Jaya”  “Jaya”  make  time  after time
= Seeing the Lord’s ecstasy, the people were amazed, and all of them shouted “Jaya! Jaya!” again and again.

@36
in this way  the Rāsa-yātrā  and  Dīpāvalī
the Utthāna-dvādaśī  festival  saw  all
= In the same way they all saw the Rāsa-yātrā, Dīpāvalī and the festival of Utthāna-dvādaśī.

@37
one day  Mahāprabhu  Nityānanda  taking
the two  brothers  counsel  held  in seclusion  sitting
= One day Mahāprabhu took Nityānanda aside, and the two brothers sat together in seclusion and took counsel.

@38
what  counsel  held  the two  anyone  not  knows
from the result  a guess  afterwards  made  the devotees
= What the two decided no one knew; only later could the devotees guess it from what followed.

@39
then  Mahāprabhu  all  devotees  called
to Gauḍa  go  all  farewell  made
= Then Mahāprabhu called all the devotees and bade them farewell, telling them to go back to Gauḍa.

@40
to all  said  —  every year  coming
Guṇḍicā  seeing  you will go  Me  meeting
= He said to them all, “Come every year: see the Guṇḍicā festival, meet with Me, and then go home.”

@41
to the Ācārya  order  gave  showing  respect
down to the caṇḍālas  and others  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  give  as a gift
= With respect He gave this order to the Ācārya: “Give devotion to Kṛṣṇa to everyone, down to the *caṇḍālas*.”

@42
to Nityānanda  order  gave  —  go  to Gauḍa
unrestrained  loving devotion  do  manifest
= To Nityānanda He gave this order: “Go to Gauḍa and reveal loving devotion freely, without restraint.

@43
Rāmadāsa  Gadādhara  and others  some  persons
Your  help  for  I have given  You  with
= To help You I am sending Rāmadāsa, Gadādhara and some others with You.

@44
from time  to time  I  You  near  will go
unseen  remaining  Your  dancing  I will see
= From time to time I will come to You, and, staying unseen, I will watch You dance.”

@45
Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita  the Lord  making  embrace
by the neck  holding  says  to him  sweet  words
= The Lord embraced Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and, with His arm around his neck, spoke sweet words to him:

@46
in your  house  in the kīrtana  I  always  will dance
you  sight  will get  other  anyone  not  will see
= “I will always dance in the *kīrtana* at your house. You will see it, but no one else will.

@47
this  cloth  to Mother  give  this  all  prasāda
prostration  making  My  have forgiven  offense
= Give this cloth and all this *prasāda* to My mother. Fall down before her and beg her to forgive My offense.

@48
her  service  leaving  I  have taken  sannyāsa
dharma  it is not  do  I  My own dharma  destruction
= I gave up serving her and took *sannyāsa*. That is not *dharma*: I am destroying My own *dharma*.

@49
her  love  subject to  I am  her  service  —  dharma
that  leaving  I have done  of a madman  deed
= I am subject to her love, and serving her is My *dharma*. In giving that up I have acted like a madman.

@50
mad  child’s  mother  not  takes  fault
this  knowing  Mother  with Me  not  is  angry
= A mother does not hold her mad child at fault. Knowing this, My mother is not angry with Me.

@51
what  use  in sannyāsa  to Me  love  own wealth
at the time  sannyāsa  I took  deranged  became  mind
= What use is *sannyāsa* to Me? Love is My real wealth. When I took *sannyāsa*, My mind was deranged.

@52
in Nīlācala  am  I  her  by order
from time  to time  I will come  her  feet  to see
= I stay in Nīlācala at her bidding, and from time to time I will come to see her feet.

@53
always  going  see  I  her  feet
a vision  thinking  she  that  true  not  accepts
= Every day I go and see her feet, but she takes it for a vision and does not believe it is real.

@54-55
one day  śāli rice  vegetable dishes  five or seven
greens  banana-flower ghaṇṭa  fried  paṭolas with nīm leaves
lemon and ginger slices  yogurt  milk  sugar candy
to the śālagrāma  offered  many  gifts
= One day she offered the *śālagrāma* many gifts: *śāli* rice, five or seven vegetable dishes, greens, *ghaṇṭa* of banana flowers, fried *paṭolas* with *nīm* leaves, slices of lemon and ginger, yogurt, milk and sugar candy.

@56
the prasāda  taking  in her lap  does  weeping
of Nimāi  favorites  my  —  all these  dishes
= She took the *prasāda* on her lap and wept: ‘All these dishes are my Nimāi’s favorites.

@57
Nimāi  not  here  who  does  eating
on Me  in meditation  with tears  filled  eyes
= Nimāi is not here — who will eat them?’ As she thought of Me, her eyes filled with tears.

@58
quickly  going  I  all  did  eating
the empty plate  seeing  tears  making  wiping
= I went at once and ate everything. Seeing the plate empty, she wiped away her tears.

@59
who  rice and vegetables  ate  empty  why  the plate
Bāla-gopāla  perhaps  ate  all  the rice
= ‘Who has eaten the rice and vegetables? Why is the plate empty? Could Bāla-gopāla have eaten all the rice?

@60
or  my  in the matter  in the mind  delusion  happened
or  some  animal  coming  everything  ate
= Or was my mind deluded about it? Or did some animal come and eat it all?

@61
or  I  on the rice plate  by mistake  not  served
so  thinking  the cooking pots  going  looked at
= Or did I forget to serve the food on the plate?’ Thinking this, she went and looked into the cooking pots.

@62
with rice and vegetables  full  seeing  all  the vessels
seeing  doubt  arose  some  wonder  in the mind
= She saw that all the pots were still full of rice and vegetables, and at the sight she was filled with doubt and wonder.

@63
Īśāna  calling  again  the place  had cleaned
once more  to Gopāla  food  offered
= She called Īśāna, had the place cleaned and plastered again, and once more offered food to Gopāla.

@64
thus  whenever  does  excellent  cooking
Me  to feed  does  in longing  weeping
= Whenever she cooks something excellent like this, she weeps with longing to feed Me.

@65
her  by love  bringing  Me  makes  eat
within  happiness  feels  she  outwardly  not  accepts
= Her love draws Me there and makes Me eat. Within she feels happy, but outwardly she does not accept it.

@66
on this  Vijayā-daśamī  happened  this  event
her  asking  to her  make  conviction
= This happened on this very Vijayā-daśamī. Ask her about it and convince her that it was real.”

@67
so much  saying  the Lord  overwhelmed  became
the people  farewell  to make  the Lord  composure  held
= As He said this, the Lord was overwhelmed; but to send the devotees off He kept His composure.

@68
to Rāghava Paṇḍita  speaks  words  tender
your  steadfast  by love  I  am  your  subject
= To Rāghava Paṇḍita He spoke tender words: “Your steadfast love has brought Me under your control.

@69
his  of service to Kṛṣṇa  the account  hear  all people
supremely pure  service  most  excellent
= Hear, everyone, how he serves Kṛṣṇa. His service is supremely pure, the very best of all.

@70
other  things  let be  hear  of the coconuts  the story
five  gaṇḍās  at  a coconut  sells  there
= Leaving aside his other offerings, hear about the coconuts. There a coconut sells for five *gaṇḍās*.

@71
in his garden  how many  hundreds  on trees  lakhs  upon lakhs  fruits
still  he inquires  where  sweet  coconuts
= In his garden hundreds of trees bear fruit by the hundreds of thousands, yet he still asks where the sweetest coconuts are to be had.

@72
for each  one  fruit’s  price  giving  four  four  paṇas
ten krośas  from  has brought  taking  pains
= He pays four *paṇas* for every single fruit and takes pains to have them brought from ten *krośas* away.

@73
every day  five or seven  fruits  husking
very cool  making  keeps  in water  immersing
= Every day he has five or seven fruits husked and keeps them immersed in water to make them very cool.

@74
of the offering  at the time  again  trimming  preparing
to Kṛṣṇa  offering  makes  the top  a hole  making
= At the time of the offering he trims and prepares them again, cuts an opening in the top, and offers them to Kṛṣṇa.

@75
Kṛṣṇa  that  coconut water  drinking  doing
sometimes  empty  the fruit  leaves  sometimes  with water  full
= Kṛṣṇa drinks the coconut water: sometimes He leaves the fruit empty, sometimes still full of water.

@76
without water  the fruit  seeing  the Paṇḍita  —  delighted
the fruit  breaking  with the pulp  makes  the śata-pātra  filled
= When the Paṇḍita sees a fruit emptied of its water, he is delighted. He breaks it open and fills a plate with the pulp.

@77
the pulp  offering  making  outside  meditation
the pulp  eating  Kṛṣṇa  makes  empty  the vessel
= He offers the pulp and meditates outside, and Kṛṣṇa eats the pulp and leaves the vessel empty.

@78
sometimes  the pulp  eating  again  the vessel  fills  with pulp
faith  grows  the Paṇḍita’s  in the ocean of love  floats
= Sometimes, after eating the pulp, Kṛṣṇa fills the vessel with pulp again. Then the Paṇḍita’s faith grows, and he floats in an ocean of love.

@79
one  day  ten fruits  preparation  making
the offering  to set  a servant  came  bringing
= One day a servant prepared ten coconuts and brought them to be offered.

@80
opportunity  not  is  delay  happened
with the plate of fruits in hand  the servant  at the door  just  stayed
= It was not yet time to offer them; there was a delay, so the servant waited at the door with the plate of fruits in his hand.

@81
of the door  on the upper wall  he  hand  put
that  with hand  the fruits  touched  the Paṇḍita  saw
= He touched the wall above the door with his hand and then touched the fruits with the same hand. The Paṇḍita saw it.

@82
the Paṇḍita  says  —  through the door  people  make  coming and going
their  foot-dust  flying up  sticks  on the upper  wall
= The Paṇḍita said, “People are always coming and going through this door. The dust from their feet flies up and settles on the wall above it.

@83
that  on the wall  hand  putting  the fruits  you touched
for Kṛṣṇa fit  not  the fruits  impure  have become
= You put your hand on that wall and then touched the fruits. They are no longer fit for Kṛṣṇa — they have become impure.”

@84
so  saying  the fruits  throws  the boundary wall  crossing
such  pure  loving service  the world  conquering
= With these words he threw the fruits over the boundary wall. Such is his pure, loving service: it conquers the world.

@85
then  other  coconuts  preparation  had made
supremely  pure  making  offering  set
= Then he had other coconuts prepared and offered them with the utmost purity.

@86
in this way  bananas  mangoes  oranges  jackfruits
whatever  whatever  in a distant village  he has heard  good
= In the same way, whenever he hears of good bananas, mangoes, oranges or jackfruits in some distant village,

@87
great price  paying  bringing  taking  pains
pure  preparation  making  makes  offering
= he pays a great price, takes pains to have them brought, prepares them with purity and offers them.

@88
in the same way  for vegetable dishes  greens  roots  fruits
in the same way  flat rice  huḍuma  sweets  all
= So too with the greens, roots and vegetables for his dishes, and with flat rice, *huḍuma* and all kinds of sweets;

@89
in the same way  cakes and drinks  sweet rice
supremely  pure  and  makes  the best of all
= so too with cakes, drinks and sweet rice — all supremely pure and the very best.

@90
kāśamdi  pickles  and so on  many  kinds
scents  cloth  ornaments  of all things  the best
= He offers *kāśamdi*, pickles and many other things, and the best of everything: scents, cloth and ornaments.

@91
in this way  loving  service  does  incomparable
which  seeing  of all people  are soothed  the eyes
= Such is the incomparable service he renders in love; it is a balm to the eyes of all who see it.”

@92
so  saying  Rāghava  made  embrace
in this way  honored  all  the devotees
= With these words He embraced Rāghava, and in the same way He honored all the devotees.

@93
to Śivānanda Sena  says  showing  respect
of Vāsudeva Datta  you  do  care
= Respectfully He said to Śivānanda Sena, “Please look after Vāsudeva Datta.

@94
supremely  generous  he  on whatever  day  whatever  comes
on that  day  spending  does  not  keeps  remainder
= He is extremely generous: whatever comes to him on any day, he spends that same day and keeps nothing back.

@95
a householder  is  he  is needed  saving
saving  not  if made  the family’s upkeep  not  is
= He is a householder, and a householder needs to save; without savings he cannot support his family.

@96
his  household’s  income and expenses  all  —  with you
the sarakhela  becoming  you  do  managing
= Let all the income and expenses of his household be in your hands. Be his *sarakhela*, his guardian, and manage his affairs.

@97
every year  My  all  devotees  taking
to Guṇḍicā  you will come  all  protection  making
= Every year bring all My devotees to the Guṇḍicā festival, and take care of them all.”

@98
to the residents of Kulīna-grāma  says  respect  showing
every year  you will come  to the festival  silk ropes  bringing
= To the residents of Kulīna-grāma He said respectfully, “Come every year to the festival, and bring silk ropes.

@99
Guṇarāja Khān  composed  the Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vijaya
there  one sentence  his  is  full of love
= Guṇarāja Khān composed the *Śrī Kṛṣṇa-vijaya*, and in it there is a sentence of his that is full of love:

@100
Nanda’s son  Kṛṣṇa  —  my  Lord of life
by this  sentence  I am sold  his  family’s  into the hands
= ‘Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda, is the Lord of my life.’ For this one sentence I have sold Myself into the hands of his descendants.

@101
of you  what  talk  of your  village  the dog
even he  to Me  dear  other people  let be  far
= What to speak of you? Even a dog of your village is dear to Me, not to mention anyone else there.”

@102
then  Rāmānanda  and  Satyarāja Khān
of the Lord  at the feet  something  made  submission
= Then Rāmānanda and Satyarāja Khān humbly put a question at the Lord’s feet:

@103
a householder  worldly  I  what  my  practice
with Your holy mouth  give  the order  I submit  at the feet
= “I am a worldly householder. What should my practice be? Please instruct me with Your own lips — I submit this at Your feet.”

@104
the Lord  says  —  service to Kṛṣṇa  service to the Vaiṣṇavas
constantly  do  saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s Name
= The Lord said, “Serve Kṛṣṇa, serve the Vaiṣṇavas, and always chant the Name of Kṛṣṇa in *saṅkīrtana*.”

@105
Satyarāja  says  —  a Vaiṣṇava  shall I recognize  how
who  a Vaiṣṇava  tell  his  general  marks
= Satyarāja said, “How am I to recognize a Vaiṣṇava? Tell me who is a Vaiṣṇava — what are his general marks?”

@106
the Lord  says  —  from whose  mouth  I hear  once
Kṛṣṇa’s Name  he  worshipable  —  best  of all
= The Lord said, “Anyone from whose mouth I hear the Name of Kṛṣṇa even once is worthy of worship — he is the best of all.

@107
one  Name of Kṛṣṇa  does  of all sins  destruction
ninefold  devotion  complete  the Name  from  becomes
= A single Name of Kṛṣṇa destroys all sins, and through the Name the nine kinds of devotion become complete.

@108
initiation, puraścaryā, rules  dependence  not  does
by touching the tongue  down to the caṇḍāla  all  delivers
= The Name does not wait for initiation, *puraścaryā* or any other rule. Simply by touching the tongue it delivers everyone, down to the *caṇḍāla*.

@109
as a by-product  does  of worldly existence  ending
the heart  attracting  causes  for Kṛṣṇa  the dawning of love
= As a mere by-product it ends worldly existence; and by attracting the heart it awakens love for Kṛṣṇa.

@110
the attraction  of the liberated  of the wise  the uprooting  and  of sins
down to the caṇḍāla  to all people but the mute  easily available  the controller  and  of the wealth of liberation
not  initiation  nor  and  pious rites  nor  and  puraścaryā  in the least  looks for

= “This *mantra*, the Name of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, draws the liberated and the wise and uproots all sins. Down to the *caṇḍāla*, it is within easy reach of everyone who is not mute, and it holds the wealth of liberation in its power. It does not look in the least for initiation, pious rites or *puraścaryā*: merely by touching the tongue it bears fruit.” (Padyāvalī 29)

= Mukunda said, “Raghunandana is my father, and I am his son — that is my conviction.
