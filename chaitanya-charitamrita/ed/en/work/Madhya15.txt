@title Śrī Caitanya’s Feast at Sārvabhauma’s House

@1
in the house of Sārvabhauma  eating
His own reviler  Amogha
accepting  manifest  made
Gaura  His own  subjection to His devotees
= While taking His meal at Sārvabhauma’s house, Gaura accepted Amogha, who had reviled Him, and so made plain that He is subject to His devotees.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
glory  to the listeners of the Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta
the Caitanya-caritāmṛta  —  whose  treasure of life
= Glory to the listeners of the *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, for whom the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* is the treasure of their lives!

@4
thus  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees
in Nīlācala  staying  does  dancing and singing  joyfully
= In this way Mahāprabhu stayed in Nīlācala with His devotees, joyfully dancing and singing.

@5
in the first year  Jagannātha  seeing
dancing and singing  does  prostrations  obeisances  prayers
= In that first year He went to see Jagannātha; He danced and sang, fell prostrate, bowed down and offered prayers.

@6
the upala-bhoga  when it begins  makes  outside  going forth
Haridāsa  meeting  comes  to His own dwelling
= When the *upala-bhoga* began, He would come out of the temple, meet Haridāsa, and return to His own dwelling.

@7
at home  sitting  does  the Lord  the saṅkīrtana of the Name
Advaita  coming  does  the Lord’s  worship
= Sitting at home, the Lord would chant the Holy Name, and Advaita would come and worship Him.

@8
with fragrant water  offers  water for the feet  water for sipping
over all the limbs  smears  of the Lord  fragrant  sandalwood
= Advaita offered Him fragrant water to wash His feet and to sip, and smeared fragrant sandalwood paste over all His limbs.

@9
on the neck  a garland  puts  on the head  placed  tulasī buds
joined  with hands  prayers  offers  at the feet  bowing
= He put a garland around the Lord’s neck and placed *tulasī* buds on His head; then he bowed at His feet and offered prayers with joined palms.

@10
on the worship plate  of flowers and tulasī  remnant  that  was
that  all  taking  the Lord  the Ācārya  worshiped
= Taking whatever flowers and *tulasī* were left on the worship plate, the Lord worshiped the Ācārya with them.

@11
who  You are  that  You are  obeisance  be  to You  this  mantra  recites
mouth-music  making  the Lord  makes laugh  the Ācārya
= He recited the *mantra* “Whoever You are, that You are — obeisance to You,” and made the Ācārya laugh with sounds made with His mouth.

@12
thus  to one another  make  obeisance
the Lord  invitation  makes  the Ācārya  time  after time
= So They paid obeisance to one another, and again and again the Ācārya invited the Lord to a meal.

@13
the Ācārya’s  invitation  —  a wonderful story
in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa
= The story of the Ācārya’s invitations is wonderful, and Vṛndāvana dāsa has described it in detail.

@14
repetition  would be  it  not  I made  description
other  devotees  make  to the Lord  invitation
= To tell it again would be repetition, so I have not described it. The other devotees also invited the Lord.

@15
one  by one  day  in one  after another  devotee’s home  a festival
with the Lord  there  eating  do  devotees  all
= Day after day there was a festival in one devotee’s home after another, and all the devotees ate there with the Lord.

@16
four months  stayed  all  with Mahāprabhu
of Jagannātha  various  festivals  see  with great joy
= For four months all the devotees stayed with Mahāprabhu and watched Jagannātha’s various festivals with great joy.

@17
on the day of the festival of Kṛṣṇa’s birth  Nanda’s great festival
in cowherd dress  became  the Lord  taking  all the devotees
= On the festival of Kṛṣṇa’s birth comes Nanda’s great celebration. That day the Lord and all the devotees dressed as cowherds.

@18
a yoke of yogurt and milk  the Lord  on His own shoulder  placing
to the festival place  came  saying  “Hari”  “Hari”
= Carrying a yoke of yogurt and milk on His shoulder, the Lord came to the festival grounds calling out, “Hari! Hari!”

@19
Kānāi Khuṭiyā  was  Nanda’s dress  wearing
Jagannātha Māhāti  had become  the Queen of Vraja
= Kānāi Khuṭiyā was dressed as Nanda, and Jagannātha Māhāti had become Yaśodā, the Queen of Vraja.

@20
himself  Pratāparudra  and  Kāśī Miśra
Sārvabhauma  and  the paḍichā-pātra  Tulasī
= King Pratāparudra himself was there, with Kāśī Miśra, Sārvabhauma and Tulasī, the temple *paḍichā*.

@21
them all  taking  the Lord  does  joyful dancing
yogurt and milk  with turmeric water  fills  everyone’s  body
= With them all the Lord danced joyfully, and everyone was drenched in yogurt, milk and turmeric water.

@22
Advaita  says  —  truth  I speak  not  do  anger
the staff  to twirl  You can  then  I know  a cowherd
= Advaita said, “I am telling You the truth — don’t be angry. Only if You can twirl a staff will I know You are a cowherd.”

@23
then  the staff  taking  the Lord  to twirl  began
time  after time  into the sky  throwing  snatching  caught
= Then the Lord took a staff and began to twirl it, tossing it into the air again and again and catching it.

@24
of the head  above  in front  behind  on both sides
between the feet  twirls  the staff  —  seeing  the people  laugh
= He twirled the staff over His head, in front, behind, on both sides and even between His feet, and the people laughed to see it.

@25
a firebrand’s circle  like  the staff  twirls
seeing  in the hearts of all people  wonder  arises
= He whirled the staff like a circle of fire traced by a firebrand, and all who saw it were struck with wonder.

@26
in the same way  Nityānanda  twirls  a staff
who  will understand  that  of the two  the cowherd mood  hidden
= Nityānanda twirled a staff in the same way. Who can understand the hidden cowherd mood of these two?

@27
by Pratāparudra’s  order  the paḍichā Tulasī
a prasāda cloth of Jagannātha  one  taking  coming
= At Pratāparudra’s order, Tulasī the *paḍichā* came, bringing a cloth that had been offered to Jagannātha.

@28
costly  cloth  the Lord  on the head  tied
the Ācārya and others  the Lord’s  people  clothed
= The Lord tied the costly cloth around His head, and the Ācārya and the Lord’s other companions were given it to wear as well.

@29
Kānāi Khuṭiyā  Jagannātha  —  the two
in ecstasy  gave away  at home  was  whatever  wealth
= Kānāi Khuṭiyā and Jagannātha, the two of them, in their ecstasy gave away all the wealth they had at home.

@30
seeing  Mahāprabhu  great  satisfaction  felt
as mother and father  to both  obeisance  made
= Seeing this, Mahāprabhu was very pleased and bowed down to the two of them as to His mother and father.
