@title The Advent of Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu

@1
may He  be gracious  Caitanya-
-deva  whose  by the grace
of His pastimes  in the description  fit
at once  may become  fallen  even  this one
= May Śrī Caitanyadeva be gracious to me: by His grace even this fallen soul may at once become fit to describe His pastimes.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  Gauracandra
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  glory  to Nityānanda
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory, glory to Nityānanda!

@3
glory  glory  to Gadādhara  glory  to Śrīnivāsa
glory  to Mukunda  to Vāsudeva  glory  to Haridāsa
= Glory, glory to Gadādhara! Glory to Śrīnivāsa (Śrīvāsa)! Glory to Mukunda and Vāsudeva! Glory to Haridāsa!

@4
glory  to Dāmodara Svarūpa  glory  to Murāri  Gupta
these  all  by their moonrise  the darkness  made  vanish
= Glory to Svarūpa Dāmodara! Glory to Murāri Gupta! Rising like moons, all of them dispelled the darkness.

@5
glory  of Śrī Caitanyacandra  the devotees  the full moons
of all  by the moonlight of love  bright  the three worlds
= Glory to the devotees of Śrī Caitanyacandra, who are full moons! The moonlight of their love illumines the three worlds.

@6
this  indeed  I have told  at the book’s beginning  the preface
now  I tell  of Caitanya’s pastimes  the sequence  in order
= Thus I have given the preface at the beginning of the book. Now I shall tell of Caitanya’s pastimes in their proper sequence.

@7
first  indeed  in the form of sūtras  I make  an enumeration
later  that  expanding  I shall make  a description
= First I shall list them briefly, as *sūtras* (concise threads); afterwards I shall expand them into a full description.

@8
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  in Navadvīpa  descending
forty-eight  years  manifest  sporting
= Descending in Navadvīpa, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya enjoyed His pastimes in the manifest world for forty-eight years.

@9
fourteen hundred  seven  in the Śaka year  of the birth  the record
fourteen hundred  in the fifty-fifth  took place  the disappearance
= His birth is recorded in the Śaka year 1407; His disappearance took place in Śaka 1455.

@10
twenty-four  years  the Lord  made  household life
constantly  made  in it  the sport of kīrtana
= For twenty-four years the Lord lived as a householder, constantly delighting in *kīrtana* all the while.

@11
twenty-four  years  at the end  having taken  sannyāsa
another  twenty-four  years  made  in Nīlācala  residence
= At the end of His twenty-fourth year He took *sannyāsa*, and for another twenty-four years He resided in Nīlācala.

@12
of that  within  six  years  going and coming
sometimes  the South  sometimes  Gauḍa  sometimes  Vṛndāvana
= Of these, six years were spent in travels—sometimes to the South, sometimes to Gauḍa, sometimes to Vṛndāvana.

@13
eighteen  years  remained  in Nīlācala
of love for Kṛṣṇa  in the nectar of pastimes  flooded  everyone
= For eighteen years He stayed in Nīlācala, flooding everyone with the nectar of the pastimes of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@14
in household life  the Lord’s  pastimes  the “Ādi”-līlā  they are called
“Madhya” and “Antya”  by name  of the final pastimes  two  names
= The Lord’s pastimes in household life are called the Ādi-līlā; His final pastimes bear two names—Madhya and Antya.

@15
the Ādi-līlā  within  the Lord’s  all  deeds
in sūtra form  Murāri  Gupta  made  strung together
= All the Lord’s deeds within the Ādi-līlā Murāri Gupta set down in *sūtra* form.

@16
of the Lord  which  final  pastimes  Svarūpa Dāmodara
sūtras  making  strung  a book  within
= The Lord’s final pastimes Svarūpa Dāmodara set down as *sūtras* and strung them together in a book.

@17
these  two  persons’  sūtras  seeing  hearing
description  makes  the Vaiṣṇava  sequence  which  making
= Seeing and hearing the *sūtras* of these two, a Vaiṣṇava describes the pastimes, arranging them in sequence.

@18
childhood  paugaṇḍa  kaiśora  youth  four  divisions
therefore  in the Ādi section  the pastimes  four  divisions
= Childhood, *paugaṇḍa* (boyhood), *kaiśora* (adolescence) and youth—these are four stages; hence the pastimes of the Ādi section fall into four divisions.

@19
all  good qualities  full of  that
I praise  the Phālguna  full moon
on which  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
descended  with the names of Kṛṣṇa
= I praise that full moon of Phālguna, full of every auspicious quality, on which Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya descended together with the names of Kṛṣṇa.

@20
on the Phālguna full moon  at dusk  the Lord’s  birth
at that time  by providence  of the moon  an eclipse  occurs
= The Lord was born at dusk on the full moon of Phālguna; at that very time, by providence, there was an eclipse of the moon.

@21
“Hari!”  “Hari!”  say  the people  joyful  becoming
was born  Lord Caitanya  the Name  causing to be born
= Joyfully the people cried “Hari! Hari!”—Lord Caitanya took birth after first bringing the Name to birth.

@22
at birth  in childhood  paugaṇḍa  kaiśora  youth  in the time
the name of Hari  made to take  the Lord  on various pretexts
= At His birth and in His childhood, boyhood, adolescence and youth, the Lord made people take the name of Hari on various pretexts.

@23
of a child’s mood  on the pretext  the Lord  makes  crying
“Kṛṣṇa”  “Hari”  the names  hearing  stops  weeping
= On the pretext of a child’s moods the Lord would cry, and on hearing the names “Kṛṣṇa” and “Hari” He stopped weeping.

@24
therefore  “Hari!”  “Hari!”  say  the women
to see  come  whoever  all  friends and kin
= So the women—all the friends and kinsfolk who came to see Him—would say, “Hari! Hari!”

@25
“Gaurahari”  calling  Him  laugh  all  the women
therefore  became  His  name  “Gaurahari”
= Calling Him “Gaurahari,” all the women laughed; and so His name became Gaurahari.

@26
childhood  age  until  in the hand  chalk  was given
paugaṇḍa  age  until  marriage  not  made
= Childhood lasts until the chalk is placed in His hand to begin His schooling; *paugaṇḍa* lasts until He marries.

@27
marriage  having made  came  fresh  youth
everywhere  made to take  the Lord  nāma-saṅkīrtana
= With His marriage came fresh youth; everywhere the Lord made people take up *nāma-saṅkīrtana*, the congregational chanting of the Name.

@28
in the paugaṇḍa age  He studies  teaches  the students
everywhere  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  explanation
= In His *paugaṇḍa* years He studied and taught students, and everywhere He explained the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@29
in sūtra, vṛtti and ṭīkā  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the purport
to the students  understood  becomes  of all  the wonder
= He showed that the purport of every *sūtra*, gloss and commentary is the name of Kṛṣṇa; the students understood it, and it was a wonder to all.

@30
whom  He sees  to him  says  chant  Kṛṣṇa’s name
with Kṛṣṇa’s name  flooded  Navadvīpa town
= To everyone He met He said, “Chant the name of Kṛṣṇa!” He flooded the town of Navadvīpa with the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@31
in the kaiśora age  began  saṅkīrtana
night and day  in love  dancing  with  the devotees
= In His *kaiśora* years He began *saṅkīrtana*, dancing in love night and day together with His devotees.

@32
town  to town  wanders  kīrtana  performing
flooded  the three worlds  loving devotion  giving
= Performing *kīrtana*, He wandered from one town to another, and by giving loving devotion He flooded the three worlds.

@33
twenty-four  years  thus  in Navadvīpa town
made to take  all people  love for Kṛṣṇa  the Name
= Thus for twenty-four years in Navadvīpa He led all people to love for Kṛṣṇa and to His name.

@34
twenty-four  years  remained  having taken  sannyāsa
the devotees  taking  made  in Nīlācala  residence
= After taking *sannyāsa* He lived twenty-four years more, residing in Nīlācala with His devotees.

@35
of that  within  from Nīlācala  six  years
dancing  singing  the gift of loving devotion  ceaselessly
= Of these, for six years He ceaselessly danced, sang and gave loving devotion, setting out from Nīlācala.

@36
Setubandha  and  through Gauḍa  Vṛndāvana
love and the Name  preaching  made  travel
= Preaching love and the Name, He traveled to Setubandha, and through Gauḍa to Vṛndāvana.

@37
this  “Madhya-līlā”  the name  of the pastimes  the chief abode
the last  eighteen  years  “Antya-līlā”  the name
= This is called the Madhya-līlā, the principal abode of His pastimes. The last eighteen years are called the Antya-līlā.

@38
of that  within  six  years  with the devotees
loving devotion  made to take  in the delight of dancing and singing
= For six of these years, with His devotees, He led people to loving devotion amid the delight of dancing and singing.

@39
twelve  years  the last  remained  in Nīlācala
the states of love  taught  on the pretext of tasting
= For the last twelve years He remained in Nīlācala and, on the pretext of tasting them Himself, taught the states of love.

@40
night and day  of separation from Kṛṣṇa  the welling up
of a madman  the behavior  makes  delirious  words
= Night and day the pain of separation from Kṛṣṇa welled up in Him; He behaved like a madman and spoke in delirium.

@41
of Śrī Rādhā  the delirious talk  as  on seeing Uddhava
in the same way  mad  delirious talk  makes  night and day
= Just as Śrī Rādhā spoke in delirium on seeing Uddhava, so He spoke in mad delirium night and day.

@42
of Vidyāpati  Jayadeva  Caṇḍīdāsa  the songs
relishes  with Rāmānanda and Svarūpa  together
= Together with Rāmānanda and Svarūpa He relished the songs of Vidyāpati, Jayadeva and Caṇḍīdāsa.

@43
of Kṛṣṇa  in separation  all  the movements of love
tasting  fulfilled  made  His own  desires
= Tasting every movement of love in separation from Kṛṣṇa, He fulfilled His own desires.

@44
endless  Caitanya’s pastimes  a tiny  soul  being
who  to tell  can  them  in detail  making
= The pastimes of Caitanya are endless. Who, being a tiny soul, could tell them in detail?

@45
in sūtras  making  counts  if  Himself  Ananta
with a thousand  mouths  He  not  reaches  the end
= Even if Ananta Himself were to count them out in brief, with His thousand mouths He would not reach their end.

@46
Dāmodara Svarūpa  and  Gupta  Murāri
the very chief pastimes  in sūtras  have written  having considered
= Svarūpa Dāmodara and Murāri Gupta, after careful consideration, wrote down the chief pastimes in *sūtra* form.

@47
that  following  I write  of the pastimes  the sūtras
expanding  has described  them  Vṛndāvana dāsa
= Following them, I write the *sūtras* of the pastimes; Vṛndāvana dāsa has described them in full.

@48
of Caitanya’s pastimes  the Vyāsa  Vṛndāvana dāsa
sweet  making  the pastimes  made  composition
= Vṛndāvana dāsa is the Vyāsa of Caitanya’s pastimes; he composed his account of them and made them sweet.

@49
of the book  expansion  for fear of  left  which  which  places
those  those  places  something  I shall make  explanation
= In whatever places he passed over the pastimes for fear of making his book too long, there I shall give some explanation.

@50
the Lord’s  nectar of pastimes  he  made  tasting
his  leavings of the meal  some  I do  chewing
= He tasted the nectar of the Lord’s pastimes; I only chew a little of the remnants of his meal.

@51
of the Ādi-līlā  the sūtras  I write  hear  O devotees
in brief  I write  fully  not  can  be written
= O devotees, listen: I am writing the *sūtras* of the Ādi-līlā. I write in brief, for it cannot be written in full.

@52
some  desire  fulfilment  for the sake of  the prince of Vraja
to descend  in the mind  made  deliberation
= To fulfil a certain desire, the prince of Vraja resolved in His mind to descend.

@53
before  sent down  which  which  the circle of His elders
in brief  I tell  told  not  can be  in full
= First He sent down the circle of His gurus and elders. I tell of them briefly; they cannot be described in full.

@54-55
Śrī Śacī  Jagannātha  Śrī Mādhava Purī
Keśava  Bhāratī  and  Śrī Īśvara  Purī
Advaita  Ācārya  and  Paṇḍita  Śrīvāsa
Ācāryaratna  Vidyānidhi  Ṭhākura  Haridāsa
= Śrī Śacī and Jagannātha, Śrī Mādhava Purī, Keśava Bhāratī and Śrī Īśvara Purī, Advaita Ācārya and Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, Ācāryaratna, Vidyānidhi and Ṭhākura Haridāsa.

@56
of Śrīhaṭṭa  a resident  Śrī Upendra Miśra  by name
a Vaiṣṇava  learned  wealthy  foremost in virtues
= A resident of Śrīhaṭṭa named Śrī Upendra Miśra was a Vaiṣṇava—learned, wealthy and foremost in virtue.

@57-58
seven  Miśras  his  sons  seven  great sages
Kaṁsāri  Paramānanda  Padmanābha  Sarveśvara
Jagannātha  Janārdana  Trailokyanātha
in Nadīyā  by the Gaṅgā  residence  made  Jagannātha
= His sons, the seven Miśras, were like seven great sages: Kaṁsāri, Paramānanda, Padmanābha, Sarveśvara, Jagannātha, Janārdana and Trailokyanātha. Jagannātha made his home by the Gaṅgā in Nadīyā.

@59
Jagannātha  the best of Miśras  title  “Purandara”
Nanda and Vasudeva  formerly  an ocean of good qualities
= Jagannātha, the best of the Miśras, bore the title Purandara. Formerly he was Nanda and Vasudeva; he was an ocean of good qualities.

@60
his  wife  “Śacī”  by name  devoted to her husband  chaste
whose  father  “Nīlāmbara”  by name  Cakravartī
= His wife was named Śacī; she was chaste and devoted to her husband. Her father was Nīlāmbara Cakravartī.
