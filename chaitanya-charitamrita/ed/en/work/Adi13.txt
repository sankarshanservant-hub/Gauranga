in joy  made  bath in the Gaṅgā
obtaining  the eclipse  as a pretext  by His own  mind’s power
to the brāhmaṇas  gave  various  gifts
= Seeing the eclipse, they laughed, hurried to the bathing place on the Gaṅgā and joyfully bathed. Taking the eclipse as a pretext, Advaita by the power of His mind gave various gifts to the brāhmaṇas.

@101
the world  full of bliss  seeing  in the mind  with wonder
by hints and gestures  says  Haridāsa
Your  such  merriment  my  mind  gladdened
I see  some  in the act  there is  a hint
= Seeing the whole world full of bliss, Haridāsa marveled and said in hints, “This merriment of Yours gladdens my heart. I see there is some purpose behind it.”

@102
Ācāryaratna  Śrīvāsa  became  in the mind  delighted
going  bath  made  in the Gaṅgā water
with joy  overwhelmed  mind  make  hari-saṁkīrtana
various  gifts  made  by the mind’s power
= Ācāryaratna and Śrīvāsa were delighted at heart. They went and bathed in the waters of the Gaṅgā; overwhelmed with joy, they sang *hari-saṁkīrtana* and by the power of the mind gave various gifts.

@103
in this way  devotees and ascetics  of whom  in which  land  the dwelling
there  there  obtaining  by the mind’s power
dance  make  saṁkīrtana  with joy  overwhelmed  mind
gifts  make  on the pretext of the eclipse
= In the same way all the devotees and renunciants, wherever they lived, danced and sang *saṁkīrtana* where they were, overwhelmed with joy, and on the pretext of the eclipse gave gifts by the power of the mind.

@104
brāhmaṇa  respectable  women  with various articles  trays  filling
came  all  gifts  bringing
like  of pure gold  the luster  seeing  the child’s  form
blessings  give  happiness  obtaining
= The wives of brāhmaṇas and of respectable men came, bringing gifts on trays filled with various articles. Seeing the child’s form, lustrous like pure gold, they gave their blessings with delight.

@105
Sāvitrī  Gaurī  Sarasvatī  Śacī  Rambhā  Arundhatī

= Sāvitrī, Gaurī, Sarasvatī, Śacī, Rambhā, Arundhatī and all the other heavenly women filled trays with various articles, assumed the dress of brāhmaṇa women and came to see Him.

@106

praises and dance  make  music and song
dancers  musicians  bards  in Navadvīpa  whose  stage
all  coming  dance  getting  joy
= In the sky the gods, Siddhas, Gandharvas and Cāraṇas offered praises, danced, played and sang. The dancers, musicians and bards whose stage was Navadvīpa all came and danced with delight.

@107
who  comes  who  goes  who  dances  who  sings
make out  cannot  anyone’s  words
dispelled  sorrow and grief  with delight filled  the people
Miśra  became  with joy  overwhelmed
= Who came and who went, who danced and who sang—no one could make out anyone’s words. Sorrow and grief were gone, the people were filled with delight, and Miśra was overwhelmed with joy.

@108
Ācāryaratna  Śrīnivāsa  Jagannātha Miśra’s  to the side
coming  him  make  mindful
had performed  the birth rite  which  was  the prescribed duty
then  Miśra  makes  various  gifts
= Ācāryaratna and Śrīnivāsa came to Jagannātha Miśra and reminded him of his duties. They had the birth ceremony performed according to the prescribed rules, and then Miśra gave many gifts.

@109
gifts  received  as many  in the house  or  was  whatever
all  wealth  to the brāhmaṇas  gave  in charity
all  dancers  singers  bards  destitute  people
wealth  giving  made  of all  honor
= All the gifts he had received and whatever there was in the house—all that wealth he gave away to the brāhmaṇas; and he honored all the dancers, singers, bards and the destitute with gifts.

@110
Śrīvāsa’s  wife  name  her  “Mālinī”
Ācāryaratna’s  wife  together with
vermilion  turmeric  oil  puffed rice  bananas  various  fruits
giving  worship  the women  in joy
= Śrīvāsa’s wife, named Mālinī, together with Ācāryaratna’s wife and the other women, joyfully worshipped the child, offering vermilion, turmeric, oil, puffed rice, bananas and various fruits.

@111
Advaita Ācārya’s  wife  world-worshipped  noble lady
name  her  “Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī”
of the Ācārya  the order  receiving  went  gifts  taking
to see  the crest-jewel of children
= Advaita Ācārya’s wife, the noble lady worshipped by the world, named Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī, received the Ācārya’s permission and went with gifts to see the crest-jewel of children.

@112

on both arms  fine  conch bangles  silver  anklets

= She brought golden bangles, foot ornaments of silver coins, golden armlets and wristlets, fine conch-shell bangles for both arms, silver anklets and various necklaces of gold coins;

@113

colorful  silk sārīs  knitted  frock  silk-bordered

= a tiger’s claw set in gold, a waist cord of silk thread, all kinds of ornaments for the hands and feet, colorful silk sārīs, a knitted child’s frock with a silk border, and much wealth in gold and silver coins.

@114
dūrvā grass  paddy  gorocana  turmeric  kuṅkuma  sandalwood

cloth-covered  palanquin  riding  with  taking  maidservants

= Filling trays with auspicious articles—*dūrvā* grass, paddy, *gorocana*, turmeric, *kuṅkuma* and sandalwood—and baskets with clothes and ornaments, she rode in a curtained palanquin, taking her maidservants with her.

@115

at Śacī’s house  became  arrived
seeing  the child’s  form  directly  Kānu of Gokula
the color  only  sees  different
= She brought many loads of foods and dishes as gifts and arrived at Śacī’s house. In the child’s form she saw Kānu of Gokula Himself—only His color was different.

@116

all  limbs  full of auspicious marks
the child’s  divine  effulgence  seeing  got  much  joy
with parental love  melted  heart
= All His limbs were beautifully formed and shone like a golden image; all were full of auspicious marks. Seeing the child’s divine effulgence, she felt great joy, and her heart melted with motherly love.

@117
dūrvā grass  paddy  placed  on the head  made  many  blessings
long-lived  be  the two  brothers
from witches and evil spirits  fear  arose  in the heart
out of fear  name  gave  “Nimāi”
= She placed *dūrvā* grass and paddy on His head and gave many blessings: “May both brothers live long!” Fearing witches and evil spirits, she named Him “Nimāi.”

@118

of Śacī and Miśra  the worship  accepting  in the mind  joyful  being

= On the day of the mother’s purificatory bath she gave clothes and ornaments, honoring Miśra together with his son. Accepting the honor offered by Śacī and Miśra, Sītā Ṭhākurāṇī returned home, joyful at heart.

@119

with wealth and grain  is filled  the house  honored by people  in person

= Thus Śacī and Jagannātha, having obtained as their son the Lord of Lakṣmī, had all their desires fulfilled. Their house was filled with wealth and grain; honored by all, they grew happier day by day.

@120

in enjoying wealth  no  pride
by the son’s  power  as much  wealth  coming  is received  so much
for Viṣṇu’s pleasure  to the twice-born  gives  in charity
= Miśra was a Vaiṣṇava—peaceful, free from lust, pure and self-controlled—and took no pride in enjoying wealth. Whatever wealth came to him by his son’s power he gave to the brāhmaṇas for the pleasure of Viṣṇu.

@121

of a great personality  the signs  in the ascendant  on the body  various
I see  this one  will deliver  the world
= Having calculated the ascendant, Nīlāmbara Cakravartī joyfully told Miśra in private, “I see the various signs of a great personality in His ascendant and on His body. He will deliver the world.”

@122

= Thus the Lord mercifully descended in Śacī’s house. Whoever hears of it—to him the merciful Lord Gaura becomes kind, and he attains His feet.

@123

= One who has obtained a human birth yet does not hear of Gaura’s qualities has wasted such a birth. He has found a river of nectar yet drinks water from a poisonous pit—having been born, why did he not die?

@124
