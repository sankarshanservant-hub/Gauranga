@title The Residents of Vārāṇasī Become Vaiṣṇavas

@1
having made Vaiṣṇavas  the sannyāsīs
headed by  of Kāśī  the residents
Sanātana  thoroughly  having refined
the Lord  to Nīlādri  went
= Having made Vaiṣṇavas of the residents of Kāśī, headed by the *sannyāsīs*, and having thoroughly refined Sanātana, the Lord went to Nīlādri.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  Mahāprabhu  two  months  for
taught  him  of the conclusions of bhakti  the utmost limit
= In this way, for two months, Mahāprabhu taught him the utmost limit of the conclusions of *bhakti*.

@4
Paramānanda  Kīrtanīyā  Śekhara’s  companion
to the Lord  kīrtana  makes hear  very  great  in delight
= Paramānanda Kīrtanīyā, the companion of Candraśekhara, sang *kīrtana* for the Lord, and he did it with great delight.

@5
of the sannyāsīs  the host  the Lord  when  disregarded
the devotees’ sorrow  to dispel  for them  mercy  arose
= When the *sannyāsīs* disregarded the Lord, mercy toward them arose in Him, so that He might dispel the sorrow of His devotees.

@6
to the sannyāsīs  the mercy  before  I have written  in detail
by way of mention  I tell  here  brief  making (it)
= I have described the Lord’s mercy to the *sannyāsīs* before, in detail. Here I mention it again, briefly.

@7
here  there  of the Lord  blasphemy  do  of the sannyāsīs  the host
hearing  in sorrow  a Mahārāṣṭrian  brāhmaṇa  does  reflection
= Everywhere the *sannyāsīs* blasphemed the Lord. Hearing this, a *brāhmaṇa* from Mahārāṣṭra reflected sorrowfully:

@8
the Lord’s  nature  whoever  sees  near at hand
His true nature  perceiving  Him  as Īśvara  taking  accepts
= “Whoever sees the Lord’s nature at close hand perceives who He truly is and accepts Him as Īśvara, the Supreme Lord.

@9
by some  means  I can  if  together  to bring
this  seeing  the sannyāsīs  will become  His  devotees
= If by some means I can bring them together, the *sannyāsīs* will see this and become His devotees.

@10
in Vārāṇasī  residence  my  is  at all  times
at all  times  sorrow  I will get  this  not  if I do
= I live in Vārāṇasī permanently. If I do not do this, I shall be sorry for ever.”

@11
so  thinking  he invited  of the sannyāsīs  the host
then  that  brāhmaṇa  came  Mahāprabhu’s  to the place
= Thinking thus, he invited all the *sannyāsīs*. Then that *brāhmaṇa* came to the place where Mahāprabhu was staying.

@12
at that time  the blasphemy  hearing  Śekhara  Tapana
sorrow  feeling  at the Lord’s feet  made  a petition
= At that time Candraśekhara and Tapana Miśra, pained to hear the blasphemy, appealed at the Lord’s feet.

@13
the devotees’ sorrow  seeing  the Lord  in His mind  reflected
of the sannyāsīs  the minds  to turn  the wish  arose
= Seeing His devotees’ sorrow, the Lord reflected within Himself, and the wish arose in Him to turn the hearts of the *sannyāsīs*.

@14
at that time  the brāhmaṇa  coming  made  an invitation
much  humility and so on  showing  clasped  the feet
= Just then the *brāhmaṇa* came and invited Him. With great humility he clasped the Lord’s feet.

@15
then  Mahāprabhu  his  invitation  accepted
the next  day  midday duties  performing  to his  house  went
= Mahāprabhu then accepted his invitation, and the next day, after His midday duties, He went to his house.

@16
there  how  made  the Lord  the deliverance of the sannyāsīs
in the account of the Pañca-tattva  that  I have made  in detail
= How the Lord delivered the *sannyāsīs* there I have told in detail in the account of the Pañca-tattva.

@17
the book  grows  again  would be  indeed  a telling
there  what  not  I wrote  that  I make  writing
= The book would grow long, and it would be told again; so I write here only what I did not write there.

@18
on which  day  the Lord  to the sannyāsīs  mercy  showed
that  day  from  in the town  a clamour  arose
= From the day the Lord showed mercy to the *sannyāsīs*, the whole town was astir.

@19
of people  crowds  come  the Lord  to see
in various  scriptures  scholars  come  scripture  to discuss
= Crowds of people came to see the Lord, and scholars learned in various scriptures came to discuss scripture with Him.

@20
all  scriptures  refuting  the Lord  bhakti  makes  the essence
with well-reasoned  words  the minds  turns  of all
= Refuting every argument from the scriptures, the Lord established *bhakti* as their essence, and with well-reasoned words He turned everyone’s mind.

@21
the instruction  accepting  perform  kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana
all  the people  laugh  sing  do  dancing
= Accepting His instruction, they performed *kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana*; all the people laughed, sang and danced.

@22
before the Lord  bowed down  became  of the sannyāsīs  the host
among themselves  discussion  they hold  giving up  study
= The *sannyāsīs* bowed down to the Lord. Giving up their studies, they discussed Him among themselves.

@23
Prakāśānanda’s  disciple  one  to him  equal
the assembly  in the midst of  says  the Lord  showing  honour
= One disciple of Prakāśānanda, his equal, spoke in the assembly, honouring the Lord:

@24
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  is  directly  Nārāyaṇa
of the Vyāsa-sūtra  the meaning  He gives  most  charming
= “Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is Nārāyaṇa Himself. He explains the meaning of the *Vyāsa-sūtra* most charmingly.

@25
of the Upaniṣads  He gives  of the primary meaning  explanation
hearing  of learned  men  are soothed  minds  ears
= He explains the primary meaning of the Upaniṣads, and hearing it soothes the minds and ears of the learned.

@26
of the sūtras and Upaniṣads  the primary meaning  abandoning
the Ācārya  imagination  makes  insistence  showing
= Abandoning the primary meaning of the *sūtras* and the Upaniṣads, the Ācārya (Śaṅkara) insists on his own imaginings.

@27
by the Ācārya  imagined  meaning  which  a scholar  hears
with his mouth  ‘yes’  ‘yes’  he says  the heart  not  accepts
= When a scholar hears the meaning the Ācārya imagined, his mouth says ‘Yes, yes,’ but his heart does not accept it.

@28
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s  words  firmly  true  I accept
in the age of Kali  by sannyāsa  saṁsāra  not  we conquer
= I accept the words of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya as firmly true: in the age of Kali one does not conquer *saṁsāra* by *sannyāsa*.

@29
of the harer nāma verse  which  He gave  explanation
that  true  a delightful meaning  supreme  evidence
= The explanation He gave of the *harer nāma* verse is true; its meaning gives joy, and it is the supreme evidence.

@30
bhakti  without  liberation  there is not  in the Bhāgavata  it says
in the age of Kali  by nāmābhāsa  easily  liberation  comes
= The *Bhāgavata* says that without *bhakti* there is no liberation, and in the age of Kali liberation comes easily by *nāmābhāsa*, the mere semblance of the holy name.

@31
the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord
toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge
for them  this  toil  only  remains
nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks
= “O almighty Lord, bhakti to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)

@32
those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated
toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence
having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there
fall  down  having neglected Your feet
= “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)

@33
‘Brahman’  by the word  is meant  full of the six opulences  Bhagavān
Him  as without attributes  establishing  completeness  suffers  loss
= The word ‘Brahman’ means Bhagavān, full of the six opulences. To establish Him as without attributes (*nirviśeṣa*) is to diminish His completeness.

@34
the śrutis  the Purāṇas  say  Kṛṣṇa’s  of the spiritual potency  the play
that  not  accepting  the scholar  makes  mockery
= The *śrutis* and the Purāṇas speak of the play of Kṛṣṇa’s spiritual potency (*cic-chakti-vilāsa*). Not accepting this, the scholar mocks it.

@35
spiritual and blissful  Kṛṣṇa’s  form  māyic  taking  to regard
this  a great  sin  true  Caitanya’s  words
= To regard Kṛṣṇa’s form of spirit and bliss as *māyic* is a great sin. Caitanya’s words are true.

@36
not  than this  higher  O Supreme  which  Your  form
pure bliss  free of difference  of unobscured radiance
I see  of the universe  the creator  one  distinct from the universe  O Self
the source of the elements and senses  that  Your  sheltered  I am
= “O Supreme Lord, I see nothing higher than this form of Yours, which is pure bliss, free of all difference, its radiance unobscured. You are the one creator of the universe, yet You are distinct from it, O Self, and You are the source of the elements and the senses. I have taken shelter of that form of Yours.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.9.3)

@37
that  indeed  this (form)  O good of the worlds  for the good
in meditation  indeed  to us  shown  by You  to the worshippers
to Him  obeisance  to Bhagavān  we offer  to You
who  is not honoured  by those bound for hell  attached to false arguments
= “O good of all the worlds, this very form You have shown in meditation to us, Your worshippers, for our good. We bow down to You, Bhagavān, whom those bound for hell, attached to false arguments, do not honour.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.9.4)

@38
disregard  Me  fools
humanlike  form  who has assumed
the supreme  nature  not knowing
of all beings  the great Lord
= “Fools disregard Me when I appear in a humanlike form, not knowing My supreme nature as the great Lord of all beings.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 9.11)

@39
those  I  envious  cruel
into the cycles of birth and death  the lowest of men
I cast  perpetually  impure
into demonic  indeed  wombs
= “Those envious and cruel men, the lowest of mankind and impure, I perpetually cast into the cycles of birth and death, into demonic wombs.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 16.19)

@40
of the sūtras  the doctrine of transformation  that  not  accepting
vivarta-vāda  he establishes  ‘Vyāsa  is mistaken’  saying
= Not accepting the doctrine of transformation (*pariṇāma-vāda*) taught in the *sūtras*, he establishes *vivarta-vāda*, saying that Vyāsa was mistaken.

@41
this  indeed  imagined  meaning  to the mind  not  appeals
the scriptures  abandoning  wicked fancies  to the pāṣaṇḍīs  he explains
= Such an imagined meaning does not appeal to the mind. Abandoning the scriptures, he explains his wicked fancies to the *pāṣaṇḍīs*.

@42
inquiry into the highest truth  is gone  we do  only  argument
where  liberation  shall we get  where  Kṛṣṇa’s  grace
= Inquiry into the highest truth is gone; we only argue. How shall we gain liberation, and how Kṛṣṇa’s grace?

@43
of the Vyāsa-sūtra  the meaning  the Ācārya  has  covered
this  is  true  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s  word
= The Ācārya has covered the meaning of the *Vyāsa-sūtra*. This word of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is true.

@44
Caitanya Gosāñi  whatever  says  that  doctrine  is the essence
other  as many  doctrines  they  all  worthless
= Whatever Caitanya Gosāñi says is the essential doctrine; all other doctrines are worthless.”

@45
so  saying  he  performs  kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana
hearing  Prakāśānanda  some  speaks  words
= Having said this, he began *kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana*. Hearing him, Prakāśānanda spoke:

@46
the Ācārya’s  insistence  advaita-vāda  to establish
therefore  of the sūtras  explanation  he gives  in another  way
= “The Ācārya was determined to establish *advaita-vāda*, and so he explained the *sūtras* in another way.

@47
Bhagavattā  if one accepts  advaita  not  can be  established
therefore  all  scriptures  he makes  refutation
= If one accepts that He is Bhagavān (*bhagavattā*), *advaita* cannot be established. Therefore he refutes all the scriptures.

@48
which  of a book  author  wants  his own doctrine  to establish
of the scriptures  the natural  meaning  does not come  from him
= When an author wants to establish his own doctrine, the natural meaning of the scriptures does not come from him.

@49
the Mīmāṁsaka  says  Īśvara  is  of karma  a limb
the Sāṅkhya  says  of the world  prakṛti  is the cause
= The Mīmāṁsaka says that Īśvara is a limb of *karma*. The Sāṅkhya says that *prakṛti* is the cause of the world.

@50
the Nyāya  says  from atoms  the universe  arises
the Māyāvādī  the impersonal Brahman  the cause  calls
= The Nyāya says that the universe arises from atoms. The Māyāvādī calls the impersonal Brahman the cause.

@51
the Pātañjala  says  Īśvara  is  the realisation of one’s own nature
in the Vedic view  they call  Him  Svayam  Bhagavān
= The Pātañjala says that Īśvara is the realisation of one’s own nature. In the view of the Vedas, He is called Svayam Bhagavān, the Lord Himself.

@52
of the six  the six  doctrines  Vyāsa  made  overturning
those  all  sūtras  taking  the description of Vedānta
= Vyāsa overturned the six doctrines of the six schools, and with all those *sūtras* he set forth the Vedānta.

@53
of Vedānta  in the view  Brahman  with form  is defined
nirguṇa  by negation  He  is  indeed  saguṇa
= In the view of Vedānta, Brahman is defined as having form (*sākāra*). By negation, the word *nirguṇa* shows that He is *saguṇa*, possessed of [transcendental] qualities.

@54
as the supreme  cause  Īśvara  no one  not  accepts
each his own  doctrine  establishes  of others’ doctrines  by refutation
= None of them accepts Īśvara as the supreme cause. Each establishes his own doctrine by refuting the doctrines of others.

@55
therefore  the six  philosophies  from  the truth  not  we know
the mahājanas  whatever  say  that  as truth  we accept
= So we do not come to know the truth from the six philosophies. What the *mahājanas* say, that we accept as truth.

@56
argument  is unsettled  the śrutis  are various
there is no such  sage  whose  opinion  not  differs
of dharma  the truth  is hidden  in a cave
the great soul  by which  has gone  that  is the path
= “Argument is inconclusive, the śrutis differ from one another, and there is no sage whose opinion does not differ from the others. The truth of dharma is hidden in the cave of the heart; the path is the one by which the great souls have gone.” (Mahābhārata, Vana-parva, 313.117)

@57
Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s  words  of nectar  a stream
He  which  speaks of  thing  that  the truth  the essence
= The words of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya are a stream of nectar. Whatever He declares is the truth, the very essence.”

@58
these  all  events  hearing  the Mahārāṣṭrian  brāhmaṇa
to the Lord  to tell  happily  made  going
= Hearing all this, the *brāhmaṇa* from Mahārāṣṭra happily went to tell the Lord.

@59
at that time  Mahāprabhu  at Pañcanada  bath  having taken
to see  was going  Bindu-mādhava  Hari
= At that time Mahāprabhu, having bathed at Pañcanada, was on His way to see Lord Bindu-mādhava.

@60
on the way  that  brāhmaṇa  all  the events  told
hearing  Mahāprabhu  happily  slightly  smiled
= On the way the *brāhmaṇa* told Him everything. Hearing it, Mahāprabhu smiled a little with pleasure.
