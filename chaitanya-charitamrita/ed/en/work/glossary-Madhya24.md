# English names and terms — CC Madhya 24, verses 1–117 (stage A, part 1; additions to `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md`, section «Мадхья 24»)

Terms already fixed in `ed/GLOSSARY-commentary.md` (Madhya 24, commentary parts 1–3) are used as given there: *ātmārāma*; the meanings of *ātmā* — Brahman, body, mind, effort (*yatna*), steadfastness (*dhṛti*), intelligence (*buddhi*), nature (*svabhāva*); the meanings of *nir* and *grantha*; *krama*; *parasmaipada* / *ātmanepada*, *svarita*, *ñ*; the seven meanings of *ca* and of *api*; *bṛhattva*, *bṛṁhaṇatva*, *ātatatva*; *kevala-brahmopāsaka*, *mokṣākāṅkṣī*; *sādhaka*, *brahmamaya*, *prāpta-brahma-laya*; *mumukṣu*, *jīvan-mukta*, *prāpta-svarūpa*; the nine Yogendras; *raṅga-kṣetra*.
