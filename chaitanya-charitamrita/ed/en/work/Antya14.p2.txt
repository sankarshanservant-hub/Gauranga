# CC Antya 14, verses 61–123 (stage A, part 2): our English translation from the Bengali and English word-by-word.
# Text of the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (ed/fixes/Antya14.txt): 63 punaḥ, 79 pāni-śaṅkha, 81 dekhe, 95 vaivarṇya,
# 100 suśītala, 109 cāhe keha, 119 kairāchena, 121 bujhite, 122 kariyā … dik daraśana.
# Verse 123 (= Antya 2.172) and verse 86 (= Madhya 18.34) — word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev.
# Madayati (73, 120) — “makes me mad with joy”, as in Antya 6.327 (ed/en-rev).

@61
anxious  became  all  the Lord  not  seeing
the Lord  looking for  wander  all  distraught  becoming
= Not seeing the Lord, everyone grew anxious, and in great distress they all went about looking for Him.

@62
of the Siṁha-dvāra  on the north side  there is  one  place
of it  in the midst  fallen  is  Caitanya  Gosāñi
= On the north side of the Siṁha-dvāra (the Lion Gate) there is a certain spot, and there Caitanya Gosāñi lay fallen.

@63
seeing  Svarūpa Gosāñi and the others  joyful  became
of the Lord  the condition  seeing  again  to worry  began
= Seeing Him, Svarūpa Gosāñi and the others were overjoyed; but when they saw the Lord’s condition, they grew anxious again.

@64
the Lord  fallen  is  long  cubits  five or six
unconscious  the body  in the nostrils  breath  not  flows
= The Lord lay there, five or six cubits long. His body was unconscious, and no breath moved in His nostrils.

@65-66
each  each  arm and leg  —  long  three cubits
of the bones  the joints  separated  the skin  remains  only  warmth
hands  feet  neck  waist  bones  joints  as many as
each  each  a span  separated  had become  so many
= Each of His arms and legs had become three cubits long. The joints of His bones had come apart: only the skin remained, and the warmth of life. His hands, feet, neck and waist, His bones and all His joints had each come apart by a span.

@67
skin  only  above  the joints  are  long  being
grieved  became  all  the Lord  seeing
= Over the joints there was only skin, and they lay drawn out. Seeing the Lord in this state, everyone was stricken with grief.

@68
at the mouth  saliva and foam  the Lord’s  upturned  eyes
seeing  all  the devotees’  body  leaves  life
= Saliva and foam were at the Lord’s mouth, and His eyes were turned upward. Seeing this, all the devotees felt as if life were leaving their bodies.

@69
Svarūpa Gosāñi  then  loud  making
of the Lord  in the ear  Kṛṣṇa’s name  says  the devotees  taking along
= Then Svarūpa Gosāñi, together with the devotees, loudly chanted the name of Kṛṣṇa into the Lord’s ear.

@70
after a long time  Kṛṣṇa’s name  into the heart  entered
Haribol  saying  the Lord  roaring  rose
= After a long while the name of Kṛṣṇa entered His heart. Crying “Haribol!”, the Lord rose up with a roar.

@71
consciousness  on regaining  the bone-joints  joined
as before  just as it was  the body  became
= As He regained consciousness, the joints of His bones came together, and His body became just as it had been before.

@72
this  pastime  of Mahāprabhu  Raghunātha dāsa
in the Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa  has  described
= Raghunātha dāsa has described this pastime of Mahāprabhu in his *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*:

@73
once  in Miśra’s house  of Vraja’s
lord’s  son’s  from intense separation
loosening  beauty and joints  through  bearing
extra  length  of arms and legs
rolling  on the ground  piteously  in utter
distress  with choked voice
weeping  Śrī Gaurāṅga  in the heart
rising  me  maddens
= “Once, in the house of Miśra, in intense separation from the son of the lord of Vraja, the beauty and the joints of His limbs gave way, and His arms and legs grew unnaturally long. Rolling on the ground, in utter distress, He wept piteously in a voice choked with feeling. That Śrī Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (*Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*, 4)

@74
at the Siṁha-dvāra  seeing  the Lord’s  astonishment  was
what  do you do  why  —  this  of Svarūpa  He asked
= Finding Himself at the Siṁha-dvāra, the Lord was astonished, and He asked Svarūpa, “What are you doing? Why?”[^74-1]

@75
Svarūpa  says  —  get up  Prabhu  come  to Your own room
there  to You  all  I shall make  known
= Svarūpa said, “Get up, Prabhu, and come to Your own room. There I shall tell You everything.”

@76
so  saying  the Lord  holding  to the room  taking  went
His  condition  all  to tell  began
= Saying this, he held the Lord and took Him to His room, and there began to tell Him all about the state He had been in.

@77
hearing  Mahāprabhu  greatly  became  astonished
the Lord  says  —  any  memory  is not  Mine
= Hearing this, Mahāprabhu was greatly astonished. The Lord said, “I remember nothing of it!

@78
only  I see  —  is  My  Kṛṣṇa  present
lightning  like  appearance  giving  is  vanishing
= I only see that My Kṛṣṇa is present: He appears like a flash of lightning and vanishes.”

@79
at that time  of Jagannātha  the conch (pāni-śaṅkha)  sounded
bath  taking  Mahāprabhu  for darśana  went
= Just then the conch (*pāni-śaṅkha*) of Jagannātha sounded, and Mahāprabhu bathed and went for *darśana*.[^79-1]

@80
thus  indeed  I have told  of the Lord  wonderful  transformations
of which  in the hearing  in people  arises  wonder
= Thus I have described the Lord’s wonderful transformations; whoever hears of them is struck with wonder.

@81
people  not  see  such  in the scriptures  not  we hear
such  ecstasy  manifest  makes  of sannyāsīs  the crest-jewel
= People have never seen such things, nor do we hear of them in the scriptures; yet the crest-jewel of *sannyāsīs* manifests such ecstasy.

@82
scriptures  world  beyond  whatever  whatever  emotion  is
of ordinary  people  in that  not  is  conviction
= Ordinary people cannot be convinced of emotions that lie beyond the scriptures and beyond this world.

@83
Raghunātha dāsa’s  always  with the Lord  stay
his  from the mouth  hearing  I write  making  conviction
= Raghunātha dāsa always stayed with the Lord. I write what I heard from his own lips, with full faith in it.

@84
one day  Mahāprabhu  to the sea  going
Caṭaka  hill  He saw  suddenly
= One day, as Mahāprabhu was going to the sea, He suddenly saw the sand hill called Caṭaka-parvata.

@85
Govardhana  hill  in the thought  absorbed  became
hill  toward  the Lord  running  went
= Taking it for Govardhana Hill, He was overwhelmed with ecstasy, and the Lord ran toward the hill.

@86
ah  this  hill  O friends  best of Hari’s servants
since  of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  the feet  by the touch  rejoicing
honour  offers  with Their herds of cows  to Them  because
with water  fine grass  caves  bulbs  roots
= “Ah, friends, this hill is the best of Hari’s servants! Thrilled by the touch of the feet of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, it honours Them, together with Their cows and companions, with water to drink, fine grass, caves, and bulbs and roots.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.18)

@87
this  verse  reciting  the Lord  goes  with the speed of the wind
Govinda  ran  behind  not  could  reach
= Reciting this verse, the Lord ran with the speed of the wind. Govinda ran after Him but could not catch up.

@88
a cry  arose  great  uproar  there was
whoever  where  was  he  getting up  ran
= A cry went up, and there was a great uproar. Everyone, wherever he was, got up and ran.

@89
Svarūpa  Jagadānanda  Paṇḍita Gadādhara
Rāmāi  Nandāi  and  Paṇḍita Śaṅkara
= Svarūpa, Jagadānanda, Gadādhara Paṇḍita, Rāmāi, Nandāi and Śaṅkara Paṇḍita all ran.

@90
Purī  Bhāratī  Gosāñi  came  to the seashore
Bhagavān Ācārya  lame  went  slowly  slowly
= Purī Gosāñi and Bhāratī Gosāñi came to the seashore, and the lame Bhagavān Ācārya followed very slowly.

@91
at first  went  the Lord  —  like  the wind’s  speed
stupor  ecstasy  on the way  arose  to move  no  strength
= At first the Lord went like the wind; but on the way the ecstasy of stupor (*stambha*) came over Him, and He had no strength to move.

@92
at every  hair  pore  the flesh  —  of a boil  the shape
it  upon  the hair standing on end  —  kadamba  like
= At every pore the flesh swelled up like a boil, and on it the hairs stood on end, like the filaments of a *kadamba* flower.

@93
at every  hair  perspiration  falls  of blood  a stream
in the throat  gurgling  no  of syllables  utterance
= From every pore sweat poured out with streams of blood. His throat gurgled, and He could not utter a single syllable.

@94
two  eyes  filling  tears  flow  boundless
in the sea  met  as if  Gaṅgā  Yamunā  streams
= His two eyes filled, and tears flowed endlessly, as if the streams of the Gaṅgā and the Yamunā had met the sea.

@95
pallor  conch  like  white  became  the body
then  trembling  rises  —  like  in the sea  waves
= His pallor was like that of a conch, and His body turned white. Then a trembling arose in Him, like waves on the sea.

@96
trembling  trembling  the Lord  on the ground  fell
then  indeed  Govinda  of the Lord  near  came
= Trembling and trembling, the Lord fell to the ground. Then Govinda came up to Him.

@97
of the waterpot  with water  makes  all limbs  sprinkling
the outer cloth  taking  makes  of the body  fanning
= With water from the waterpot (*karaṅga*) he sprinkled the Lord’s whole body, and with the outer cloth he fanned Him.

@98
Svarūpa and others  there  coming  met
of the Lord  the condition  seeing  to weep  began
= Svarūpa and the others came and joined them there; seeing the Lord’s condition, they began to weep.

@99
of the Lord  in the body  they see  eight  sāttvika  transformations
wonderful  sāttvika  seeing  became  amazed
= They saw in the Lord’s body the eight *sāttvika* transformations, and seeing these wonderful *sāttvika* signs, they were amazed.

@100
loud  saṅkīrtana  they make  at the Lord’s  ear
very cool  with water  they make  the Lord’s  body  washing
= They sang loud *saṅkīrtana* right at the Lord’s ear and bathed His body with very cool water.

@101
in this way  many  times  kīrtana  doing
Haribol  saying  the Lord  rises  suddenly
= When they had sung *kīrtana* in this way many times, the Lord suddenly rose, crying “Haribol!”

@102
joyfully  all  Vaiṣṇavas  say  Hari  Hari
arose  auspicious  sound  the four  directions  filling
= Joyfully all the Vaiṣṇavas cried, “Hari! Hari!”, and an auspicious sound arose, filling the four directions.

@103
having risen  Mahāprabhu  astonished  here  there  looks
what  to see  He wants  that  to see  not  can
= Having risen, Mahāprabhu looked here and there in astonishment, but what He wanted to see He could not see.

@104
the Vaiṣṇavas  seeing  of the Lord  half-external consciousness  became
to Svarūpa Gosāñi  something  to say  began
= Seeing the Vaiṣṇavas, the Lord came half into external consciousness and began to say something to Svarūpa Gosāñi:

@105
Govardhana  from  Me  who  here  brought
having found  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes  to see  not  I could
= “Who has brought Me here from Govardhana? I had found Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, but I could not go on seeing them.

@106
from here  today  I  went  to Govardhana
I would see  whether  Kṛṣṇa  does  of cows  grazing
= Today I went from here to Govardhana to see whether Kṛṣṇa was grazing the cows.

@107
on Govardhana  climbing  Kṛṣṇa  played  the flute
of Govardhana  all around  graze  all  the cows
= Kṛṣṇa climbed Govardhana and played His flute, and all around Govardhana the cows were grazing.

@108
of the flute  the sound  hearing  came  Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī
all  the sakhīs  with  making  adornment
= Hearing the sound of the flute, Rādhā Ṭhākurāṇī came, beautifully dressed and adorned, with all Her *sakhīs*.

@109
Rādhā  taking  Kṛṣṇa  entered  into a cave
the sakhīs  want  some  flowers  to gather
= Kṛṣṇa took Rādhā with Him and entered a cave, and some of the *sakhīs* wanted to gather flowers.[^109-1]

@110
at that time  you all  uproar  made
there  from  seizing  Me  here  taking  came
= Just then all of you raised an uproar, seized Me, and brought Me here from there.

@111
why  then  brought  Me  in vain  pain  to give
having found  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes  not  I could  see
= Why did you bring Me here, only to give Me pain for nothing? I had found Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, but I could not see them.”

@112
so  saying  Mahāprabhu  does  weeping
His  condition  seeing  the Vaiṣṇavas  do  crying
= Saying this, Mahāprabhu wept, and seeing His condition, the Vaiṣṇavas wept too.

@113
at that time  came  Purī  Bhāratī  —  the two
both  seeing  Mahāprabhu’s  was  reverence
= Just then the two, Purī and Bhāratī, arrived. Seeing them both, Mahāprabhu was filled with reverence.

@114
full  external consciousness  when it came  the Lord  to both  offered respects
Mahāprabhu  the two  loving embrace  made
= When He came fully into external consciousness, the Lord offered His respects to them both, and the two embraced Mahāprabhu with love.

@115
the Lord  says  —  you two  why  came  so  far
Purī Gosāñi  says  —  Your  dance  to see
= The Lord said, “Why have you two come so far?” Purī Gosāñi said, “To see Your dancing.”

@116
embarrassed  became  the Lord  of Purī  at the words
to the sea’s  bathing place  came  all  Vaiṣṇavas  with
= At Purī’s words the Lord was embarrassed, and He went with all the Vaiṣṇavas to the bathing place at the sea.

@117
bath  taking  Mahāprabhu  home  came
all  taking along  mahā-prasāda  eating  did
= After bathing, Mahāprabhu returned home and, together with everyone, partook of *mahā-prasāda*.

@118
thus  indeed  I have told  of the Lord  divine madness  mood
even Brahmā  to tell  cannot  whose  power
= Thus I have described the Lord’s mood of divine madness (*divyonmāda*), whose power even Brahmā cannot describe.

@119
Caṭaka  hill  going  pastime  Raghunātha dāsa
in the Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa  has  described
= Raghunātha dāsa has described the pastime of the Lord’s running to Caṭaka-giri in his *Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*:

@120
near  of Nīlādri  Caṭaka  hill
the king  from seeing
oh  in Vraja  Govardhana  hills’
king  to see  from here
going  I am  thus  saying  mad
as if  running  followed
by companions  His own  Gaurāṅga  in the heart
rising  me  maddens
= “Near Nīlādri He saw Caṭaka-giri, a king among hills, and cried, ‘Oh, from here I am going to Vraja to see Govardhana, the king of hills!’ Saying this, He ran like a madman, and His own companions followed Him. That Gaurāṅga, rising in my heart, makes me mad with joy.” (*Caitanya-stava-kalpavṛkṣa*, 8)

@121
now  the Lord  all that  did  extraordinary  pastimes
who  to understand  can  that  of Mahāprabhu  play
= Who can understand the extraordinary pastimes the Lord performed in those days — that play of Mahāprabhu?[^121-1]

@122
in brief  making  I do  of direction  showing
whoever  this  hears  attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
= Telling it briefly, I have only pointed the way. Whoever hears this attains the feet of Kṛṣṇa.

@123
Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
“Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.

@notes
[^74-1]: *Kyā kara, kibā* is Hindustani (*keyā karo, keṁo*): “What are you doing? Why?” (*Anubhāṣya*). In half-external consciousness, the Lord asks why people have gathered around Him.
[^79-1]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *pāni-śaṅkha*. The *Anubhāṣya* explains *pāṇi-śaṅkha* as a conch held in the hand and blown (or the clapping of hands when the temple doors are opened), and, by another reading, *pānī-śaṅkha*, a conch [holding water] for *ācamana*.
[^109-1]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *sakhī-gaṇa cāhe keha phula uṭhāite*, “some of the *sakhīs* wanted to gather flowers”; other editions read *sakhī-gaṇa kahe more phula uṭhāite*, “the *sakhīs* told Me to gather flowers”.
[^121-1]: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *ke bujhite pāre*, “who can understand”; other editions read *ke varṇite pāre*, “who can describe”.
