@title The Trunk and Branches of Śrī Caitanya’s Tree

@1
of Śrī Caitanya  of the lotus feet
to the honeybees  obeisances  obeisances
somehow  by taking shelter  of whom
a dog  even  of that fragrance  a sharer  may become
= Again and again I bow to the bees who drink the honey of Śrī Caitanya’s lotus feet. By somehow taking shelter of them, even a dog may come to share in that fragrance.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  and Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  the host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of this gardener  of this tree  indescribable  the description
now  hear  of the main branches  the names  the account
= This gardener and this tree are beyond description. Now hear the names of the main branches.

@4
of Caitanya Gosāñi  as many as  the host of associates
lesser  greater  the status  their  not  is  determined
= Among the many associates of Caitanya Gosāñi, no one can determine who is lesser and who is greater.

@5
as many  as many  great souls  made  of them all  a count
anyone  to make  cannot  senior  junior  an order
= Many great souls have counted them all, yet none could arrange them in order of senior and junior.

@6
therefore  to them all  making  obeisances
names  only  I make  fault  do not  take  my
= Therefore, bowing to them all, I simply give their names. Please do not hold this against me.

@7
I worship  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
of love  the immortal  tree  the dear ones
in the form of branches  the devotees
of love for Kṛṣṇa  the fruit  the givers
= “I worship the dear devotees of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who are the branches of His immortal tree of love and who give the fruit of love for Kṛṣṇa.”

@8
Śrīvāsa  Paṇḍita  and  Śrī Rāma  Paṇḍita
two  brothers  two  branches  in the world  renowned
= Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and Śrī Rāma Paṇḍita: these two brothers are two branches renowned throughout the world.

@9
Śrīpati  Śrīnidhi  their  two  brothers
of the four  brothers  the servants and maidservants  the household
= Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi are their two brothers. The servants and maidservants of the four brothers and their whole household

@10
of the two  branches  among the sub-branches  of them all  the counting
in whose  house  Mahāprabhu’s  always  saṅkīrtana
= are all counted among the sub-branches of these two branches. In their house Mahāprabhu always held saṅkīrtana.

@11
with the whole family  do  who  Caitanya’s  service
Gauracandra  except  not  know  goddess  god
= With their whole family they serve Śrī Caitanya; they know no god or goddess other than Gauracandra.

@12
Ācāryaratna  the name  bears  great  one  branch
his  associates  his  branches  sub-branches
= One great branch bears the name Ācāryaratna; his associates are his branches and sub-branches.

@13
of Ācāryaratna  the name  Śrī Candraśekhara
in whose  house  in the mood of the Goddess  danced  the Lord
= Ācāryaratna’s name is Śrī Candraśekhara. In his house the Lord danced in the mood of the Goddess.

@14
Puṇḍarīka  Vidyānidhi  a great branch  I know
whose  name  uttering  the Lord  wept  Himself
= I know Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi to be a great branch. The Lord Himself wept as He spoke his name.

@15
a great  branch  Gadādhara  Paṇḍita Gosāñi
he  Lakṣmī  in form  to him  equal  anyone  there is not
= A great branch is Gadādhara Paṇḍita Gosāñi. He is Lakṣmī in person, and no one is his equal.

@16
his  disciples  and their disciples  his  sub-branches
in this way  all  of the branches and sub-branches  the record
= His disciples and their disciples are his sub-branches. In this way I record all the branches and sub-branches.

@17
Vakreśvara  Paṇḍita  the Lord’s  very  dear  servant
in one mood  twenty-four  praharas  whose  dancing
= Vakreśvara Paṇḍita is the Lord’s very dear servant. Absorbed in a single mood, he would dance for twenty-four *praharas* — three days and nights.

@18
Himself  Mahāprabhu  sings  whose  at the time of dancing
the Lord’s  feet  clasping  Vakreśvara  says
= While he danced, Mahāprabhu Himself would sing. Clasping the Lord’s feet, Vakreśvara said:

@19
ten  thousand  Gandharvas  to me  give  O moon-faced One
they  sing  I  dance  then  my  happiness
= “O moon-faced One, give me ten thousand Gandharvas! Let them sing while I dance — then I shall be happy.”

@20
the Lord  says  you  My  wing  one  branch
in the sky  flying  I would go  if I got  another  wing
= The Lord said, “You are a branch — one of My wings. If I had another wing, I would fly up into the sky.”

@21
Paṇḍita  Jagadānanda  the Lord’s  life  itself
among people  known  who  of Satyabhāmā  the very self
= Paṇḍita Jagadānanda is the Lord’s very life. People know him as Satyabhāmā herself.

@22
out of love  to do  wants  for the Lord  tender care
renunciation  people  out of fear  the Lord  not  accepts  ever
= Out of love he wanted to care for the Lord and look after His comfort, but the Lord, mindful of His renunciation and of what people would say, never accepted it.

@23
the two  bickering  start  a quarrel
his  love  the story  later  I shall tell  all
= So the two of them would bicker and quarrel. I shall tell the whole story of his love later.

@24
Rāghava Paṇḍita  the Lord’s  original  follower
his  branch  principal  one  Makaradhvaja  Kara
= Rāghava Paṇḍita is the Lord’s first follower. One principal branch from him is Makaradhvaja Kara.

@25
his  sister  Damayantī  the Lord’s  dear  maidservant
the Lord’s  foodstuffs  who  makes  all the year round
= His sister Damayantī is the Lord’s dear maidservant. All year round she prepares foodstuffs for the Lord.

@26
those  all  foodstuffs  as many as  in bags  filling
Rāghava  taking  goes  secret  keeping
= Rāghava fills bags with all those preparations and carries them to the Lord, keeping them hidden.

@27
all year  them  the Lord  makes  acceptance
Rāghava’s bags  called  the fame  of which
= The Lord accepts them all year long, and they became famous as “Rāghava’s bags.”

@28
those  all  foodstuffs  later  I shall make  in detail
of which  on hearing  of the devotees  flow  streams of tears
= I shall describe all those preparations in detail later. Hearing of them, devotees shed streams of tears.

@29
to the Lord  extremely  dear  Paṇḍita  Gaṅgādāsa
of whom  by remembrance  there is  of all  bondage  destruction
= Paṇḍita Gaṅgādāsa is extremely dear to the Lord; by remembering him, one is freed from all bondage.

@30
Caitanya’s  associate  Śrī  Ācārya  Purandara
father  as  whom  calls  Gaurāṅga-sundara
= Śrī Purandara Ācārya is Śrī Caitanya’s associate. Gaurāṅga-sundara called him father.

@31
Dāmodara  Paṇḍita  branch  in love  intense
the Lord  upon  who  made  a punishment of words
= Dāmodara Paṇḍita is a branch fierce in love. He even rebuked the Lord with words.

@32
of the rebuke  the story  I shall tell  later  in detail  making
by the rebuke  pleased  the Lord  him  sent  to Nadīyā
= I shall tell the story of that rebuke in detail later. Pleased by it, the Lord sent him to Nadīyā.

@33
his  younger brother  branch  Śaṅkara  Paṇḍita
the Lord’s  foot-pillow  whose  name  known
= His younger brother, Śaṅkara Paṇḍita, is a branch. He is known as “the Lord’s foot-pillow.”

@34
Sadāśiva  Paṇḍita  whose  at the Lord’s feet  hope
at the very first  Nityānanda’s  in whose  house  residence
= Sadāśiva Paṇḍita’s one hope is the Lord’s feet. When Nityānanda first came, He stayed in his house.

@35
of Śrī Nṛsiṁha  the worshiper  Pradyumna  Brahmacārī
the Lord  his  name  made  Nṛsiṁhānanda  making
= Pradyumna Brahmacārī worshiped Śrī Nṛsiṁha. The Lord gave him the name Nṛsiṁhānanda.

@36
Nārāyaṇa  Paṇḍita  one  very  generous
Caitanya’s  feet  except  not  knows  anything else
= Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍita is another, most generous. He knows nothing but Śrī Caitanya’s feet.

@37
Śrīmān  Paṇḍita  branch  the Lord’s  own  servant
the torch  holds  when  the Lord  does  dancing
= The branch Śrīmān Paṇḍita is the Lord’s personal servant. He holds the torch when the Lord dances.

@38
Śuklāmbara  Brahmacārī  very  fortunate
whose  food  begging  snatching  ate  the Lord
= Śuklāmbara Brahmacārī is most fortunate: the Lord begged for his food, snatched it and ate it.

@39
Nandana  Ācārya  branch  in the world  renowned
hiding  the two  Lords  in whose  house  stayed
= The branch Nandana Ācārya is renowned throughout the world. In his house the two Lords stayed in hiding.

@40
Śrī  Mukunda  Datta  branch  the Lord’s  classmate
at whose  kīrtana  dances  Caitanya Gosāñi
= The branch Śrī Mukunda Datta was the Lord’s classmate. Caitanya Gosāñi dances to his kīrtana.

@41
Vāsudeva  Datta  the Lord’s  servant  a great soul
with a thousand  mouths  whose  qualities  if one tells  not  is possible
= Vāsudeva Datta is the Lord’s servant, a great soul. Even with a thousand mouths one could not describe his qualities.

@42
in the world  as many as  living beings  their  sins  taking
hell  to suffer  he wants  the beings  freeing
= He wanted to take on himself the sins of every living being in the world and suffer in hell, to set them free.

@43
of the branch Haridāsa Ṭhākura  wonderful  the life
three  hundred thousand  names  he  takes  without fail
= The life of the branch Haridāsa Ṭhākura is wonderful. Without fail he chanted three hundred thousand names.

@44
his  endless  qualities  I tell  a mere hint
Ācārya Gosāñi  whom  fed  the śrāddha plate
= His qualities are endless; I give only a hint of them. Ācārya Gosāñi fed him the plate of the *śrāddha* ceremony.

@45
like Prahlāda  his  of qualities  the waves
even when beaten by Muslims  whose  there was not  a knitting of the brows
= The waves of his qualities are like Prahlāda’s: even when the Muslims beat him, he did not so much as frown.

@46
he  perfection  when he attained  his  body  taking  on the lap
danced  Caitanya Prabhu  in great  joy
= When he attained perfection, Caitanya Prabhu took his body in His arms and danced in great joy.

@47
his  pastimes  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa
whatever  remains  later  I shall make  manifest
= Vṛndāvana dāsa has described his pastimes; whatever remains I shall bring to light later.

@48
his  sub-branches  all  of Kulīna-grāma  the people
Satyarāja  and others  his  of mercy  the recipients
= His sub-branches are all the people of Kulīna-grāma, headed by Satyarāja, who received his mercy.

@49
Śrī  Murāri  Gupta  branch  of love  the storehouse
the Lord’s  heart  melts  hearing  the humility  whose
= The branch Śrī Murāri Gupta is a storehouse of love. Hearing of his humility, the Lord’s heart melts.

@50
gifts  not  does  not  takes  anyone’s  wealth
his own  livelihood  making  does  of the family  maintenance
= He accepts no gifts and takes no one’s wealth; he supports his family by his own profession.

@51
treatment  he does  whom  becoming  kind
bodily disease  the disease of worldly existence  both  his  destruction
= Whoever he kindly treats is cured of both diseases — the disease of the body and the disease of worldly existence (*bhava-roga*).

@52
Śrīmān  Sena  the Lord’s  servant  chief
Caitanya’s  feet  except  not  knows  anything else
= Śrīmān Sena is the Lord’s chief servant. He knows nothing but Śrī Caitanya’s feet.

@53
Śrī  Gadādhara  dāsa  branch  above all
of the Kazis  in the mouths  who  made say  Hari
= The branch Śrī Gadādhara dāsa is above all, for he made the Kazis say “Hari.”

@54
Śivānanda  Sena  the Lord’s  servant  intimate
to the Lord’s place  to go  all  take  whose  company
= Śivānanda Sena is an intimate servant of the Lord. Everyone going to the Lord travels in his company.

@55
every year  the Lord’s devotees  with him  taking
to Nīlācala  he goes  on the way  care  taking
= Every year he takes the Lord’s devotees with him to Nīlācala, looking after them on the way.

@56
to the devotees  mercy  shows  the Lord  in these three  forms
in person  by entering  and  by appearing  as
= The Lord shows mercy to His devotees in three ways: in person (*sākṣāt*), by entering someone (*āveśa*), and by appearing (*āvirbhāva*).

@57
in person  all  devotees  see  alike
Nakula  Brahmacārī  in the body  the Lord’s  entering
= In person, all the devotees saw Him alike. In the body of Nakula Brahmacārī the Lord was present by *āveśa*.

@58
Pradyumna  Brahmacārī  his  earlier  name  was
Nṛsiṁhānanda  name  the Lord  later  indeed  gave
= His earlier name was Pradyumna Brahmacārī; later the Lord named him Nṛsiṁhānanda.

@59
in him  was  Caitanya’s  appearance
extraordinary  such  the Lord’s  many  ways
= In him Śrī Caitanya was present by *āvirbhāva*. The Lord has many such extraordinary ways.

@60
tasted  these  all  mellows  Sena  Śivānanda
in detail  I shall tell  later  all this  bliss
= Śivānanda Sena tasted all these mercies. I shall describe all this bliss in detail later.

@61
Śivānanda’s  sub-branches  his  associates
sons  servants  and so on  counting  Caitanya’s  servants
= Śivānanda’s sub-branches are his associates — his sons, servants and others, all servants of Śrī Caitanya.

@62
Caitanya dāsa  Rāmadāsa  and  Karṇapūra
three  sons  of Śivānanda  the Lord’s  devotees  heroes
= Caitanya dāsa, Rāmadāsa and Karṇapūra, the three sons of Śivānanda, are heroic devotees of the Lord.

@63
Śrī Vallabha  Sena  and  Sena  Śrīkānta
through relation to Śivānanda  the Lord’s  devotees  exclusive
= Śrīvallabha Sena and Śrīkānta Sena, Śivānanda’s relatives, are wholeheartedly devoted to the Lord.

@64
dear to the Lord  Govindānanda  a great devotee
the Lord’s  kīrtana singer  chief  Śrī Govinda  Datta
= Govindānanda, dear to the Lord, is a great devotee (*mahā-bhāgavata*). Śrī Govinda Datta is the Lord’s chief kīrtana singer.

@65
Śrī Vijaya dāsa  by name  the Lord’s  scribe
to the Lord  many  books  has given  writing
= Śrī Vijaya dāsa is the Lord’s scribe; he copied many books and gave them to the Lord.

@66
Ratnabāhu  calling  the Lord  gave  his  name
penniless  the Lord’s  dear  Kṛṣṇadāsa  by name
= The Lord gave him the name Ratnabāhu, “Jewel-armed.” Dear to the Lord also is the penniless Kṛṣṇadāsa.

@67
banana-bark  seller  Śrīdhara  the Lord’s  dear servant
with whom  the Lord  makes  always  jokes
= Śrīdhara, the seller of banana bark, is the Lord’s dear servant, with whom the Lord always joked.

@68
the Lord  whose  daily  takes  banana pith  banana flowers  fruits
from whose  broken  iron pot  the Lord  drank  water
= Every day the Lord took his banana pith, banana flowers and fruits, and the Lord drank water from his broken iron pot.

@69
the Lord’s  very dear  servant  Bhagavān  Paṇḍita
in whose  body  Kṛṣṇa  formerly  was  situated
= Bhagavān Paṇḍita is the Lord’s very dear servant. Kṛṣṇa formerly dwelt in his body.

@70
Jagadīśa  Paṇḍita  and  Hiraṇya  Mahāśaya
to whom  mercy  showed  in childhood  the Lord  merciful
= Jagadīśa Paṇḍita and Hiraṇya Mahāśaya received the merciful Lord’s mercy in His childhood:

@71
in these  two houses  the Lord  on Ekādaśī  day
Viṣṇu’s  offering  begging  ate  Himself
= in these two houses, on the day of Ekādaśī, the Lord begged for the food offered to Viṣṇu and ate it Himself.

@72
the Lord’s  students  two  Puruṣottama  Sañjaya
in grammar  two  disciples  two  great souls
= Two of the Lord’s students, Puruṣottama and Sañjaya, were His disciples in grammar — two great souls.

@73
Vanamālī  Paṇḍita  branch  famous  in the world
golden  club  plow  who  saw  the Lord’s  in hand
= The branch Vanamālī Paṇḍita is famous in the world. He saw a golden club and plow in the Lord’s hands.

@74
to Śrī Caitanya  very  dear  Buddhimanta  Khān
from birth  obedient to orders  he  servant  chief
= Buddhimanta Khān is very dear to Śrī Caitanya. From birth he carried out the Lord’s orders; he is one of His chief servants.

@75
Garuḍa  Paṇḍita  takes  the auspicious holy name
by the strength of the name  poison  on whom  not  had  power
= Garuḍa Paṇḍita chants the auspicious holy name; by the strength of the name, poison had no power over him.

@76
Gopīnātha  Siṁha  one  Caitanya’s  servant
Akrūra  calling  the Lord  whom  made  jest
= Gopīnātha Siṁha is a servant of Śrī Caitanya; the Lord jokingly called him Akrūra.

@77
the Bhāgavata reciter  Devānanda  by Vakreśvara’s mercy
of the Bhāgavata  the meaning as devotion  received  the Lord  from
= By Vakreśvara’s mercy, Devānanda, the reciter of the *Bhāgavata*, received from the Lord the meaning of the *Bhāgavata* as devotion.

@78-79
of Khaṇḍa  the resident  Mukunda dāsa  Śrī Raghunandana
Narahari dāsa  Cirañjīva  Sulocana
these  all  great branches  of Caitanya’s mercy  abodes
of love  fruits  flowers  make  everywhere  gift
= From Khaṇḍa: Mukunda dāsa, Śrī Raghunandana, Narahari dāsa, Cirañjīva and Sulocana. All these are great branches, abodes of Śrī Caitanya’s mercy; they give away the fruits and flowers of love everywhere.

@80
of Kulīna-grāma  residents  Satyarāja  Rāmānanda
Yadunātha  Puruṣottama  Śaṅkara  Vidyānanda
= The residents of Kulīna-grāma — Satyarāja, Rāmānanda, Yadunātha, Puruṣottama, Śaṅkara, Vidyānanda,

@81
Vāṇīnātha  Vasu  and others  all  of the village  people
all  Caitanya’s servants  Caitanya  the treasure of their life
= Vāṇīnātha Vasu and all the other villagers — all are servants of Śrī Caitanya, and Śrī Caitanya is the treasure of their life.

@82
the Lord  says  of Kulīna-grāma  which  is  a dog
that  to Me  dear  other  people  let be  far
= The Lord said, “Even a dog of Kulīna-grāma is dear to Me, what to speak of its people!

@83
of the people of Kulīna-grāma  the fortune  to tell  not  possible
pigs  herds  the Ḍom  he too  Kṛṣṇa  sings
= The fortune of the people of Kulīna-grāma cannot be described: even the Ḍom who herds pigs there sings of Kṛṣṇa.”

@84
Anupama  Vallabha  Śrī Rūpa  Sanātana
these  three  branches  of the tree  in the west  counted
= Anupama (Vallabha), Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana are counted as three branches on the western side of the tree.

@85
of them  among  Rūpa  Sanātana  great  branches
Anupama  Jīva  Rājendra and others  sub-branches
= Among them Rūpa and Sanātana are the great branches; Anupama, Jīva, Rājendra and others are sub-branches.

@86
of the gardener  by the will  the branches  greatly  grew
growing  western  lands  all  covered
= By the gardener’s will the branches grew greatly and spread over all the western lands,

@87
up to  the Sindhu river  the banks  and  the Himālayas
Vṛndāvana  Mathurā and others  all  holy places  there are
= as far as the banks of the Sindhu and the Himālayas, with Vṛndāvana, Mathurā and all the holy places.

@88
of the two  branches  by the fruit of love  everything  was flooded
the fruit of love  tasting  people  mad  became
= The fruit of love from these two branches flooded everything, and the people who tasted it went mad with love.

@89
of the west  the people  all  foolish  without proper conduct
there  preached  the two  devotion  good conduct
= The people of the west were all foolish and without proper conduct; there the two brothers preached devotion and right conduct.

@90
by the vision of scripture  did  of lost holy places  recovery
in Vṛndāvana  did  of the Deity  worship  propagation
= Guided by scripture, they recovered the lost holy places, and in Vṛndāvana they spread the worship of the Deity.
