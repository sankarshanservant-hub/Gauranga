@title The Trunk and Branches of Śrī Caitanya’s Tree

@1
of Śrī Caitanya  of the lotus feet
to the honeybees  obeisances  obeisances
somehow  by taking shelter  of whom
a dog  even  of that fragrance  a sharer  may become
= Again and again I bow to the bees who drink the honey of Śrī Caitanya’s lotus feet. By somehow taking shelter of them, even a dog may come to share in that fragrance.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  and Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  the host
= Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of this gardener  of this tree  indescribable  the description
now  hear  of the main branches  the names  the account
= This gardener and this tree are beyond description. Now hear the names of the main branches.

@4
of Caitanya Gosāñi  as many as  the host of associates
lesser  greater  the status  their  not  is  determined
= Among the many associates of Caitanya Gosāñi, no one can determine who is lesser and who is greater.

@5
as many  as many  great souls  made  of them all  a count
anyone  to make  cannot  senior  junior  an order
= Many great souls have counted them all, yet none could arrange them in order of senior and junior.

@6
therefore  to them all  making  obeisances
names  only  I make  fault  do not  take  my
= Therefore, bowing to them all, I simply give their names. Please do not hold this against me.

@7
I worship  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
of love  the immortal  tree  the dear ones
in the form of branches  the devotees
of love for Kṛṣṇa  the fruit  the givers
= “I worship the dear devotees of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who are the branches of His immortal tree of love and who give the fruit of love for Kṛṣṇa.”

@8
Śrīvāsa  Paṇḍita  and  Śrī Rāma  Paṇḍita
two  brothers  two  branches  in the world  renowned
= Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and Śrī Rāma Paṇḍita: these two brothers are two branches renowned throughout the world.

@9
Śrīpati  Śrīnidhi  their  two  brothers
of the four  brothers  the servants and maidservants  the household
= Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi are their two brothers. The servants and maidservants of the four brothers and their whole household

@10
of the two  branches  among the sub-branches  of them all  the counting
in whose  house  Mahāprabhu’s  always  saṅkīrtana
= are all counted among the sub-branches of these two branches. In their house Mahāprabhu always held saṅkīrtana.

@11
with the whole family  do  who  Caitanya’s  service
Gauracandra  except  not  know  goddess  god
= With their whole family they serve Śrī Caitanya; they know no god or goddess other than Gauracandra.

@12
Ācāryaratna  the name  bears  great  one  branch
his  associates  his  branches  sub-branches
= One great branch bears the name Ācāryaratna; his associates are his branches and sub-branches.

@13
of Ācāryaratna  the name  Śrī Candraśekhara
in whose  house  in the mood of the Goddess  danced  the Lord
= Ācāryaratna’s name is Śrī Candraśekhara. In his house the Lord danced in the mood of the Goddess.

@14
Puṇḍarīka  Vidyānidhi  a great branch  I know
whose  name  uttering  the Lord  wept  Himself
= I know Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi to be a great branch. The Lord Himself wept as He spoke his name.

@15
a great  branch  Gadādhara  Paṇḍita Gosāñi
he  Lakṣmī  in form  to him  equal  anyone  there is not
= A great branch is Gadādhara Paṇḍita Gosāñi. He is Lakṣmī in person, and no one is his equal.

@16
his  disciples  and their disciples  his  sub-branches
in this way  all  of the branches and sub-branches  the record
= His disciples and their disciples are his sub-branches. In this way I record all the branches and sub-branches.

@17
Vakreśvara  Paṇḍita  the Lord’s  very  dear  servant
in one mood  twenty-four  praharas  whose  dancing
= Vakreśvara Paṇḍita is the Lord’s very dear servant. Absorbed in a single mood, he would dance for twenty-four *praharas* — three days and nights.

@18
Himself  Mahāprabhu  sings  whose  at the time of dancing
the Lord’s  feet  clasping  Vakreśvara  says
= While he danced, Mahāprabhu Himself would sing. Clasping the Lord’s feet, Vakreśvara said:

@19
ten  thousand  Gandharvas  to me  give  O moon-faced One
they  sing  I  dance  then  my  happiness
= “O moon-faced One, give me ten thousand Gandharvas! Let them sing while I dance — then I shall be happy.”

@20
the Lord  says  you  My  wing  one  branch
in the sky  flying  I would go  if I got  another  wing
= The Lord said, “You are a branch — one of My wings. If I had another wing, I would fly up into the sky.”

@21
Paṇḍita  Jagadānanda  the Lord’s  life  itself
among people  known  who  of Satyabhāmā  the very self
= Paṇḍita Jagadānanda is the Lord’s very life. People know him as Satyabhāmā herself.

@22
out of love  to do  wants  for the Lord  tender care
renunciation  people  out of fear  the Lord  not  accepts  ever
= Out of love he wanted to care for the Lord and look after His comfort, but the Lord, mindful of His renunciation and of what people would say, never accepted it.

@23
the two  bickering  start  a quarrel
his  love  the story  later  I shall tell  all
= So the two of them would bicker and quarrel. I shall tell the whole story of his love later.

@24
Rāghava Paṇḍita  the Lord’s  original  follower
his  branch  principal  one  Makaradhvaja  Kara
= Rāghava Paṇḍita is the Lord’s first follower. One principal branch from him is Makaradhvaja Kara.

@25
his  sister  Damayantī  the Lord’s  dear  maidservant
the Lord’s  foodstuffs  who  makes  all the year round
= His sister Damayantī is the Lord’s dear maidservant. All year round she prepares foodstuffs for the Lord.

@26
those  all  foodstuffs  as many as  in bags  filling
Rāghava  taking  goes  secret  keeping
= Rāghava fills bags with all those preparations and carries them to the Lord, keeping them hidden.

@27
all year  them  the Lord  makes  acceptance
Rāghava’s bags  called  the fame  of which
= The Lord accepts them all year long, and they became famous as “Rāghava’s bags.”

@28
those  all  foodstuffs  later  I shall make  in detail
of which  on hearing  of the devotees  flow  streams of tears
= I shall describe all those preparations in detail later. Hearing of them, devotees shed streams of tears.

@29
to the Lord  extremely  dear  Paṇḍita  Gaṅgādāsa
of whom  by remembrance  there is  of all  bondage  destruction
= Paṇḍita Gaṅgādāsa is extremely dear to the Lord; by remembering him, one is freed from all bondage.

@30

father  as  whom  calls  Gaurāṅga-sundara
= Śrī Purandara Ācārya is Śrī Caitanya’s associate. Gaurāṅga-sundara called him father.

@31
Dāmodara  Paṇḍita  branch  in love  intense
the Lord  upon  who  made  a punishment of words
= Dāmodara Paṇḍita is a branch fierce in love. He even rebuked the Lord with words.

@32
of the rebuke  the story  I shall tell  later  in detail  making
by the rebuke  pleased  the Lord  him  sent  to Nadīyā
= I shall tell the story of that rebuke in detail later. Pleased by it, the Lord sent him to Nadīyā.

@33

the Lord’s  foot-pillow  whose  name  known
= His younger brother, Śaṅkara Paṇḍita, is a branch. He is known as “the Lord’s foot-pillow.”

@34
Sadāśiva  Paṇḍita  whose  at the Lord’s feet  hope
at the very first  Nityānanda’s  in whose  house  residence
= Sadāśiva Paṇḍita’s one hope is the Lord’s feet. When Nityānanda first came, He stayed in his house.

@35

the Lord  his  name  made  Nṛsiṁhānanda  making
= Pradyumna Brahmacārī worshiped Śrī Nṛsiṁha. The Lord gave him the name Nṛsiṁhānanda.

@36
Nārāyaṇa  Paṇḍita  one  very  generous
Caitanya’s  feet  except  not  knows  anything else
= Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍita is another, most generous. He knows nothing but Śrī Caitanya’s feet.

@37

the torch  holds  when  the Lord  does  dancing
= The branch Śrīmān Paṇḍita is the Lord’s personal servant. He holds the torch when the Lord dances.

@38
Śuklāmbara  Brahmacārī  very  fortunate
whose  food  begging  snatching  ate  the Lord
= Śuklāmbara Brahmacārī is most fortunate: the Lord begged for his food, snatched it and ate it.

@39

hiding  the two  Lords  in whose  house  stayed
= The branch Nandana Ācārya is renowned throughout the world. In his house the two Lords stayed in hiding.

@40
Śrī  Mukunda  Datta  branch  the Lord’s  classmate
at whose  kīrtana  dances  Caitanya Gosāñi
= The branch Śrī Mukunda Datta was the Lord’s classmate. Caitanya Gosāñi dances to his kīrtana.

@41

with a thousand  mouths  whose  qualities  if one tells  not  is possible
= Vāsudeva Datta is the Lord’s servant, a great soul. Even with a thousand mouths one could not describe his qualities.

@42
in the world  as many as  living beings  their  sins  taking

= He wanted to take on himself the sins of every living being in the world and suffer in hell, to set them free.

@43

= The life of the branch Haridāsa Ṭhākura is wonderful. Without fail he chanted three hundred thousand names.

@44

= His qualities are endless; I give only a hint of them. Ācārya Gosāñi fed him the plate of the *śrāddha* ceremony.

@45
like Prahlāda  his  of qualities  the waves
even when beaten by Muslims  whose  there was not  a knitting of the brows
= The waves of his qualities are like Prahlāda’s: even when the Muslims beat him, he did not so much as frown.

@46

= When he attained perfection, Caitanya Prabhu took his body in His arms and danced in great joy.

@47

= Vṛndāvana dāsa has described his pastimes; whatever remains I shall bring to light later.

= His sub-branches are all the people of Kulīna-grāma, headed by Satyarāja, who received his mercy.

@50
