# CC Madhya 22, stage A, part 2 (verses 83–164); read by build_en.py together with Madhya22.txt
# Verses 83 (= Ādi 1.60), 91 (= Madhya 8.63 = 9.265), 164 (= Madhya 7.155): word-by-word and translation verbatim from ed/en-rev.

@83
of the saints  in the company  My  prowess  revealing
become  to heart and ear  delightful  talks
by relishing them  quickly  on the path of liberation
faith,  attraction,  love  will follow in turn
= (Lord Kapila to His mother Devahūti:) “In the company of the saints there are talks revealing My power, delightful to the heart and to the ear. By relishing them one quickly comes to faith in Me, the path of liberation, and after it to attraction (*rati*) and love (*prema-bhakti*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.25)

@84
of bad association  the rejection  this  Vaiṣṇava  conduct
one attached to women  one kind  of the unholy  one who is not Kṛṣṇa’s devotee  another
= To give up bad association — this is the conduct of a Vaiṣṇava. One who is attached to women is one kind of unholy person (*asādhu*); one who is not a devotee of Kṛṣṇa is the other.

@85
truthfulness  purity  compassion  silence
intelligence  modesty  beauty  fame  forbearance
control of the mind  control of the senses  fortune  and  thus
by the company of whom  goes  to ruin
= “Truthfulness, purity, compassion, silence, intelligence, modesty, beauty, fame, forbearance, control of the mind and of the senses, and fortune — all go to ruin through the company of such people.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.31.33)

@86
with those  restless  foolish
who have lost sight of the self  unholy
company  not  one should keep  pitiable
of women  play-  pets  and
= “One should never keep company with the restless and the foolish, with the unholy who have lost sight of the self, with those pitiable men who are pets for women to play with.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.31.34)

@87
not  so  for him  arises  delusion
bondage  and  from other  attachment
from the company of women  as  for a man
as  of those attached to them  from the company
= “From no other attachment does a man fall into such delusion and bondage as from the company of women and of those attached to women.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.31.35)

@88
better  of fire  of the flames
inside a cage  to dwell
not  from thought of Kṛṣṇa (Śauri)  turned away
of people  living with  the torment
= “Better to dwell in a cage amid blazing flames than to suffer the torment of living with people who have turned away from the thought of Kṛṣṇa.” (Kātyāyana-saṁhitā)

@89
do not  look at  whose piety is spent  ever  even
for the Lord  devotion  devoid of  people
= “Never even look upon people who are devoid of devotion to the Lord, whose piety is spent.”

@90
these  all  giving up  and  of varṇa and āśrama  the dharma
owning nothing  becoming  he takes  of Kṛṣṇa alone  shelter
= Giving up all this, and the *dharma* of *varṇa* and *āśrama* as well, he becomes *akiñcana*, one who owns nothing, and takes shelter of Kṛṣṇa alone.

@91
all  dharmas  giving up
to Me  alone  for shelter  come
I  you  from all  sins
shall free  do not  grieve
= “Give up all *dharmas* and take shelter of Me alone. I shall free you from all sins; do not grieve.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 18.66)

@92
affectionate to His devotees  grateful  all-capable  generous
such  Kṛṣṇa  leaving  a wise man  does not  worship  another
= Kṛṣṇa is affectionate to His devotees, grateful, all-capable and generous. No wise man would leave such a Kṛṣṇa to worship another.

@93
what  wise man  other than You  shelter  would approach
than the One who loves His devotees  true to His word  the friend  grateful
all  He gives  to His friends  who worship Him  desires
Himself  even  increase and decrease  not  of whom
= “What wise man would approach any shelter but You? You love Your devotees, You are true to Your word, You are their friend and are grateful. To the friends who worship You, You give all they desire, and even Your own self — and You neither increase nor diminish.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.48.26)

@94
of a wise  person  arises  if  of Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  knowledge
others  giving up  he worships (Him)  of that  Uddhava  the proof
= When a wise person comes to know Kṛṣṇa’s qualities, he gives up all else and worships Him. Of this Uddhava is the proof:

@95
ah  Pūtanā (sister of Baka)  whom  from her breast  deadly poison
wishing to kill  made drink  even though  wicked
attained  the destination  befitting a nurse  than Him  other
whom  indeed  merciful  for shelter  should we approach
= “Ah! Wishing to kill Him, the wicked Pūtanā made Him drink deadly poison from her breast — and still she attained the destination befitting His nurse. To whom else so merciful should we go for shelter?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.2.23)

@96
of the surrendered  of the one who owns nothing  the very same  characteristics
of them  within  enters  self-offering
= The surrendered soul (*śaraṇāgata*) and the one who owns nothing (*akiñcana*) have the same characteristics; and within surrender there is also self-offering (*ātma-samarpaṇa*).

@97
of what is favourable  acceptance
of what is unfavourable  rejection
He will protect  thus  faith  as protector  acceptance  likewise
offering of the self  humility  six kinds of  surrender
= “Surrender (*śaraṇāgati*) is of six kinds: the resolve to accept what is favourable to devotion; the rejection of what is unfavourable; faith that He will protect; acceptance of Him as one’s guardian; the offering of oneself; and humility.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 11.417)

@98
‘Yours  I am’  thus  saying  with speech
just so  with the mind  knowing
of Him  the abode  taking shelter of  with the body
rejoices  the surrendered soul
= “Saying with his words, ‘I am Yours,’ knowing it so in his mind, and with his body taking shelter of the Lord’s abode, the surrendered soul rejoices.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 11.418)

@99
shelter  taking  he makes  to Kṛṣṇa  the offering of himself
Kṛṣṇa  him  makes  at that moment  like Himself
= Taking shelter, he offers himself to Kṛṣṇa, and at that very moment Kṛṣṇa makes him like Himself.

@100
a mortal  when  having given up  all  works
having offered himself  whom I wish to exalt  by Me
then  immortality  attaining
with Me  for a nature like Mine  and  becomes fit  indeed
= “When a mortal gives up all works and offers himself to Me, I wish to exalt him. Then he attains immortality and becomes fit to share with Me a nature like My own.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.23.34)

@101
now  of sādhana-bhakti  the characteristics  hear  Sanātana
from which  one obtains  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the great treasure
= Now hear, Sanātana, the characteristics of *sādhana-bhakti*, devotion in practice, by which one obtains the great treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

@102
by action  to be accomplished  becomes  whose goal
is bhāva  it  is called sādhana
of the eternally  perfect  bhāva
the manifestation  in the heart  is the attainment
= “Devotion carried out by the senses, whose goal is *bhāva*, is called *sādhana-bhakti*. *Bhāva* is eternally perfect; to ‘attain’ it is to make it manifest in the heart.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.2)
