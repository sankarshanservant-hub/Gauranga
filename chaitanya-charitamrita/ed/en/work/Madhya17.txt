@title Śrī Caitanya Goes to Vṛndāvana

@1
going  to Vṛndāvana  Gaura
tigers, elephants, deer and birds  in the forest
maddened with love  dancing along with Him
made  chanting “Kṛṣṇa”
= As Gaura went to Vṛndāvana, He made the tigers, elephants, deer and birds of the forest mad with love: they danced along with Him and chanted the name of Kṛṣṇa.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

@3
autumn  came  the Lord’s  to set out  arose  the intention
with Rāmānanda and Svarūpa  in private  consultation
= When autumn came, the Lord resolved to set out, and He took counsel in private with Rāmānanda and Svarūpa.

@4
to Me  help  you give  if  you two  persons
then  I  going  may see  Śrī Vṛndāvana
= “If you two will help Me, I may go and see Śrī Vṛndāvana.

@5
at night  rising  by the forest path  slipping away  I shall go
alone  I shall go  anyone  with Me  not  I shall take
= I shall rise at night and slip away by the forest path. I shall go alone and take no one with Me.

@6
anyone  if  company  to keep  after Me  rising  runs
all  you will hold back  so that  anyone  not  goes
= If anyone gets up and runs after Me to keep Me company, hold them all back, so that no one follows.

@7
pleased  being  leave  give  not  consider it  a sorrow
of you all  in the happiness  on the road  will be  My  happiness
= Give Me leave gladly, and do not take it as a sorrow. If you are all happy, I shall be happy on the road.”

@8
the two  say  —  You  the Lord  independent
whatever  You wish  that  You will do  You are not  dependent
= The two of them said, “You are the independent Lord. Whatever You wish, You will do; You depend on no one.

@9
but  of us two  hear  one  request
in your  happiness  My  happiness  —  You said  Yourself
= But hear one request from the two of us. You Yourself have said, ‘In your happiness is My happiness.’

@10
of us two  in the mind  then  great  happiness  will be
one  request  if  You accept  O merciful one
= Our hearts will be very happy, O merciful Lord, if You grant us one request.

@11
a worthy  brāhmaṇa  one  with You  certainly  we want
alms  collecting  meals  he will give  he will go  the waterpot  carrying
= We certainly want one good brāhmaṇa to go with You. He will collect alms and give You Your meals, and he will carry Your waterpot.

@12
on the forest path  going  there is no  whose food may be accepted  brāhmaṇa
give the order  —  with You  let go  a brāhmaṇa  one
= On the forest path there is no brāhmaṇa whose food You could accept. Permit one brāhmaṇa to go with You.”

@13
the Lord  says  —  of My own companions  anyone  not  I shall take
one person  if I take  of the others  in the mind  sorrow  will be
= The Lord said, “I shall not take any of My own companions. If I take one, the others will be unhappy.

@14
new  the companion  should be  —  loving  whose  mind
such  when  I find  then  I take  one  person
= Let My companion be someone new, whose heart is full of love. If I find such a person, I will take one.”

@15
Svarūpa  says  —  here is  Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya
toward You  affectionate  very  learned  saintly  noble
= Svarūpa said, “Here is Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya. He is full of love for You, and he is learned, saintly and noble.

@16
at the very first  with You  he came  Gauḍa  from
his  desire  is  all the holy places  to visit
= He came with You from Gauḍa at the very start, and he wishes to visit all the holy places.

@17
with him  together  there is  a brāhmaṇa  one  servant
he  on the road  will perform  the duties of service and meals
= With him is a brāhmaṇa servant, and on the road the Bhaṭṭācārya himself will serve You and see to Your meals.

@18
him  with You  You take  if  of everyone  is  happiness
on the forest path  going  Your  will not be  any  hardship
= If You take him with You, everyone will be happy, and You will meet no hardship on the forest path.

@19
that  brāhmaṇa  carrying  will take  the cloth and the waterpot
the Bhaṭṭācārya  meals  will give  doing  the rounds for alms
= That brāhmaṇa will carry Your cloth and waterpot, and the Bhaṭṭācārya will go begging alms and give You Your meals.”

@20
his  words  the Lord  acceptance  made
Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya  as a companion  making  took
= The Lord accepted Svarūpa’s words and took Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya with Him.

@21
the night before  Jagannātha  seeing  permission  taking
at the end of the night  rising  the Lord  set out  in secret
= The night before, He went to see Jagannātha and took His leave. Then, rising at the end of the night, the Lord set out in secret.

@22
in the morning  the devotees  the Lord  not  seeing
search  making  wander about  distressed  becoming
= In the morning the devotees could not find the Lord, and they went about searching for Him in distress.

@23
Svarūpa Gosāñi  everyone  made  restraint
desisting  becoming  remain  all  knowing  the Lord’s  mind
= Svarūpa Gosāñi held them all back, and knowing the Lord’s mind, they all gave up the search.

@24
the well-known  road  leaving  the Lord  by a side path  went
Kaṭaka  on the right  keeping  into the forest  He entered
= Leaving the main road, the Lord went by a side path. Keeping Kaṭaka on His right, He entered the forest.

@25
through the lonely forest  goes  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa’s name  taking
elephants and tigers  the path  leave  the Lord  seeing
= The Lord went through the lonely forest chanting Kṛṣṇa’s name, and elephants and tigers left the path when they saw Him.

@26
herd upon herd  tigers  elephants  rhinoceroses  boars
of them  in the midst  in ecstasy  the Lord  made  His way
= There were herds of tigers, elephants, rhinoceroses and boars, and the Lord, absorbed in ecstasy, made His way through their midst.

@27
seeing  in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind  arises  great fear
by the Lord’s  power  they  to one  side  move
= Seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was terrified, but by the Lord’s power the animals stood aside.

@28
one day  on the path  a tiger  had made  lying down
in ecstasy  its  against the body  the Lord’s  touched  foot
= One day a tiger was lying on the path, and the Lord, absorbed in ecstasy, touched its body with His foot.

@29
the Lord  says  —  say  “Kṛṣṇa”  the tiger  rose
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  saying  the tiger  to dance  began
= The Lord said, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!” The tiger rose and began to dance, saying, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”

@30
on another  day  Mahāprabhu  takes  a bath in the river
a herd of maddened elephants  came  to do  drinking of water
= On another day, while Mahāprabhu was bathing in a river, a herd of maddened elephants came to drink.

@31
the Lord  in the water  His rites  performs  before Him  the elephants  came
“Kṛṣṇa  say”  saying  the Lord  water  throwing  splashed
= While the Lord was performing His rites in the water, the elephants came before Him. Saying, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!” the Lord splashed water at them.

@32
of that water drops sprays  touch  whose  body
he  Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  says  in love  dances  sings
= Whoever was touched by a spray of those drops cried, “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” and danced and sang in love.

@33
some  on the ground  fall  some  make  loud cries
seeing  in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind  arises  wonder
= Some fell to the ground, and some trumpeted loudly. Seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya was struck with wonder.

@34
on the road  going  does  the Lord  loud saṅkīrtana
sweet  the sound of His voice  hearing  come  the does
= As He went along the road, the Lord would sing the Name aloud, and hearing His sweet voice, the does came to Him.

@35
right and left  the sound  hearing  go  with the Lord
the Lord  their  bodies  strokes  a verse  recites  joyfully
= Listening to the sound, they walked with the Lord on His right and left. The Lord stroked them and joyfully recited a verse:

@36

who  to the son of Nanda  wearing a colourful dress
hearing  the sound of the flute  together with the black bucks
worship  offered  arranged  with loving glances
= “Blessed are these does! Though foolish by nature, when they heard the flute they came with their black bucks to the son of Nanda, in His colourful dress, and worshipped Him with loving glances.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.21.11)

@37
at that time  tigers  there  came  five or seven
tigers and does  together  go  Mahāprabhu’s  company
= Just then five or seven tigers came there, and tigers and does walked together with Mahāprabhu.

@38
seeing  in Mahāprabhu  of Vṛndāvana  remembrance  arose
Vṛndāvana’s  qualities  describing  a verse  He recited
= Seeing this, Mahāprabhu remembered Vṛndāvana, and He recited a verse describing its glories:

@39

lived together  men, beasts and others
like friends  the abode of the Unconquered
fled away  anger, thirst and the like
= “There, in the abode of the Unconquered Lord, men and beasts, enemies by nature, lived together like friends, and anger, thirst and the like had fled away.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.13.60)

@40
Kṛṣṇa  Kṛṣṇa  say  thus  the Lord  when  said
Kṛṣṇa  saying  the tigers and deer  to dance  began
= When the Lord said, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!’” the tigers and deer began to dance, saying, “Kṛṣṇa!”

@41
dance  weep  the tigers  together with the does
Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya  watches  with wonder at the unprecedented sport
= The tigers danced and wept together with the does, and Balabhadra Bhaṭṭācārya watched this unprecedented sport.

@42
tigers and deer  one another  do  embracing
to mouth  mouth  putting  do  one another  kissing
= The tigers and deer embraced one another, and putting mouth to mouth, they kissed.

@43

= Seeing this delightful scene, the Lord began to laugh. Then He left them all there and went on ahead.

@44
peacocks and other  birds  the Lord  seeing
along with Him  go  Kṛṣṇa  saying  dance  maddened  becoming
= Seeing the Lord, peacocks and other birds went along with Him, crying “Kṛṣṇa” and dancing as if mad.

@45
“Hari bol”  saying  the Lord  makes  a loud call
trees and creepers  —  rejoice  that  call  hearing
= The Lord called aloud, “Hari bol!” and the trees and creepers rejoiced to hear it.

@46

Kṛṣṇa’s name  giving  He made  with love  mad
= All the moving and nonmoving beings of Jhārikhaṇḍa He made mad with love by giving them Kṛṣṇa’s name.

@47
which  village  through  He goes  where  He makes  a stay
of all those  villages  in the people  arises  loving devotion
= In every village He passed through or stayed in, the people gained loving devotion, *prema-bhakti*.

@48-49

= If anyone heard Kṛṣṇa’s name from His mouth, another heard it from that person’s mouth, and yet another from his. Saying “Kṛṣṇa!” and “Hari!”, they all danced, wept and laughed, and so, one from another, people became Vaiṣṇavas in every land.

@50

= Although the Lord, wary of crowds, kept His love hidden and did not show it outwardly,

@51
still  His  of seeing and hearing  by the power
of all  lands  the people  became  Vaiṣṇavas
= still, by the power of seeing and hearing Him, the people of every land became Vaiṣṇavas.

@52
to Gauḍa  Vaṅga  Utkala  the southern lands  going
of the people  deliverance  He brought  Himself  wandering
= Going to Gauḍa, Vaṅga, Utkala and the South, He delivered the people, wandering there Himself.

@53

almost outsiders  the people  there  great unbelievers
= On the pretext of going to Mathurā He came to Jhārikhaṇḍa, where the people lived almost outside the Vedic order and were great unbelievers.

@54
the Name and love  giving  He made  of all  the deliverance
Caitanya’s  hidden pastimes  to understand  the power  whose
= Giving them the Name and love, He delivered them all. Who has the power to understand the hidden pastimes of Caitanya?

@55

= Seeing a forest, He would take it for Vṛndāvana; seeing a hill, He would think it Govardhana.

@56

in a great trance of love  dances  the Lord  falls  weeping
= Wherever He saw a river, He took it for the Kālindī. Overwhelmed by love, the Lord danced and fell down weeping.

@57

= As they went along the road, the Bhaṭṭācārya gathered whatever greens, roots and fruits he could find.

@58

= In whatever village the Lord stayed, five or seven brāhmaṇas would come and invite Him.

@60

= Where there were no brāhmaṇas, respectable men of the *śūdra* community came and invited the Bhaṭṭācārya.

@61
the Bhaṭṭācārya  cooking  does  of forest vegetables

= The Bhaṭṭācārya cooked vegetables from the forest, and the Lord was delighted with them.

@64

= The Lord was perfectly content with the forest fare, and He was happiest on the days He spent in solitude.

his  brāhmaṇa  carries  the waterpot and the outer cloth
= The Bhaṭṭācārya served Him lovingly, like a servant, and his brāhmaṇa carried the waterpot and the outer cloth.

@66

at both twilights  the warmth of a fire  of wood  without limit
= He bathed three times a day in the warm water of springs, and at dawn and dusk He sat by a fire, for there was no end of firewood.

@67

happiness  feeling  the Lord  speaks  words
= Travelling in solitude, He was constantly absorbed in love. Feeling this happiness, the Lord said:

= “Listen, Bhaṭṭācārya. I have travelled through many lands, but on this forest path I find not a trace of hardship anywhere.

= Kṛṣṇa is merciful. He has shown Me great mercy: bringing Me by the forest path, He has given Me great happiness.

@70

= Earlier, when I set out for Vṛndāvana, I thought, ‘I shall see My mother, the Gaṅgā and the devotees once more.

@71

the devotees  with Me  taking  I shall go  to Vṛndāvana
= I shall surely meet the devotees, and I shall take them with Me to Vṛndāvana.’

@72

My mother  the Gaṅgā  the devotees  seeing  happy  became  My mind
= With this in mind I went to Gauḍa, and My heart was happy to see My mother, the Gaṅgā and the devotees.

@73
the devotees  taking  then  I set out  joyfully
hundreds of thousands  of people  there  became  with Me
= Then I joyfully set out with the devotees, and hundreds of thousands of people there joined Me.

@74
of Sanātana  through the mouth  Kṛṣṇa  Me  taught

= Kṛṣṇa taught Me through Sanātana’s mouth. He put an end to that journey and brought Me by the forest path.

@75

Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  without  any  happiness  not  there is
= He is an ocean of mercy, compassionate to the poor and the fallen. Without Kṛṣṇa’s mercy there is no happiness at all.”

@76

= Embracing the Bhaṭṭācārya, He said to him, “By your kindness I have found so much happiness.”

@77

= He replied, “You are Kṛṣṇa! You are merciful! I am a fallen soul, yet You have been kind to me.

@78

= I am worthless, yet You have brought me with You. In Your mercy, my Lord, You have taken Your meals from my hand.

= You have made a lowly crow equal to Garuḍa. You are the independent Lord, *Svayam Bhagavān* Himself.”

= “I bow down to Mādhava, the supreme bliss, whose mercy makes the mute eloquent and makes the lame cross mountains.”[^80] (Śrīdhara Svāmī, Bhāvārtha-dīpikā on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 1.1.1)

= In this way Balabhadra offered praise, and by his loving service he pleased the Lord.

= So the Lord came joyfully to Kāśī, and there He took His midday bath at Maṇikarṇikā.

= “I heard earlier that the Lord had taken *sannyāsa*,” he thought, and when he was sure it was He, his heart filled with joy.

= Clasping the Lord’s feet, he wept, and the Lord raised him up and embraced him.

= Full of joy, he brought the Lord home, served Him, and danced, waving his cloth.

= He was Miśra’s friend and an old servant of the Lord. A *vaidya* by caste and a scribe by profession, he lived in Vārāṇasī.

= We two constantly meditate on Your feet, and You, the omniscient Lord, have given us Your darśana.
