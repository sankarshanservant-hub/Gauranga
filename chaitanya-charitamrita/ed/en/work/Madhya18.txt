@title Śrī Caitanya in Vṛndāvana

@1
in Vṛndāvana  the nonmoving and moving beings
delighting  with His glances
Himself  and  by the sight of them
Gaurāṅga  all around  wandered
= Delighting the moving and nonmoving beings of Vṛndāvana with His glances, and delighting Himself by the sight of them, Gaurāṅga wandered all around.

@2
glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura![^2]

@3
in this way  Mahāprabhu  dancing  dancing
at Āriṭ-grāma  arriving  outer consciousness  came  suddenly
= Dancing and dancing in this way, Mahāprabhu came to Āriṭ-grāma, and there His outer awareness suddenly returned.

@4
in Ariṣṭa  of Rādhā-kuṇḍa  news  He asks  of the people
anyone  not  tells  accompanying  brāhmaṇa  not  knows
= In Ariṣṭa He asked the people about Rādhā-kuṇḍa, but no one could tell Him, and the brāhmaṇa with Him did not know either.

@5
the holy place  lost  knowing  the Lord  all-knowing  Bhagavān
in two  rice fields  in shallow water  He took  bath
= The Lord, the all-knowing Bhagavān, knew that the holy place had been lost to sight, and He bathed in the shallow water of two rice fields.

@6
seeing  all  of the village people  astonished  became  the mind
with love  the Lord  makes  of Rādhā-kuṇḍa  praise
= Seeing this, all the villagers were astonished. Then, full of love, the Lord praised Rādhā-kuṇḍa:

@7
all  gopīs  than  Rādhā  Kṛṣṇa’s  beloved
so too  Rādhā-kuṇḍa  —  dear  of the beloved  the lake
= Of all the gopīs, Rādhā is Kṛṣṇa’s dearest. So too Rādhā-kuṇḍa is dear to Him: it is the lake of His beloved.

@8
as  Rādhā  dear  to Viṣṇu
Her  pond  dear  so
among all gopīs  She alone
of Viṣṇu  most  beloved
= “As Rādhā is dear to Viṣṇu, so is Her pond dear to Him; among all the gopīs She alone is His most beloved.” (Padma Purāṇa; Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 45)[^8]

@9
in which  kuṇḍa  eternally  Kṛṣṇa  Rādhikā  with
in the water  water sports  performs  on the bank  in the joy of the rāsa
= In this kuṇḍa Kṛṣṇa eternally sports with Rādhikā in the water, and on its bank He delights in the rāsa dance.

@10
in that  kuṇḍa  whoever  once  takes  bath
to him  equal to Rādhā’s  love  Kṛṣṇa  makes  gift
= To anyone who bathes in that kuṇḍa even once, Kṛṣṇa gives love like Rādhā’s own.

@11
of the kuṇḍa  sweetness  —  as if  Rādhā’s  charm
of the kuṇḍa  glory  —  as if  Rādhā’s  glory
= The sweetness of the kuṇḍa is like Rādhā’s own charm, and the glory of the kuṇḍa is like Rādhā’s own glory.

@12
like Śrī Rādhā  to Hari  Her  lake  dearest by wondrous  its own  qualities
in which  the beautiful moon Mādhava  ceaselessly  with love  with Her  sports
love for Him  ah  like Rādhikā  obtains  in which  once  one who bathes
its  indeed  glory  and  sweetness  by whom  could be  described  on earth
= “By its own wondrous qualities Her lake is as dear to Hari as Śrī Rādhā Herself. In it the beautiful moon Mādhava ceaselessly sports with Her in love, and whoever bathes in it even once gains love for Him like Rādhikā’s. Who on earth could describe its glory and its sweetness?” (Govinda-līlāmṛta 7.102)

@13
in this way  praises  He offers  absorbed in love  being
on the bank  dance  He performs  the pastimes of the kuṇḍa  remembering
= Absorbed in love, He offered these praises, and He danced on the bank, remembering the pastimes of the kuṇḍa.

@14
of the kuṇḍa  clay  taking  tilaka  He made
through the Bhaṭṭācārya  clay  with Him  making  He took
= He took clay from the kuṇḍa and marked Himself with tilaka, and He had the Bhaṭṭācārya carry some of the clay along.

@15
then  setting out  came  the Lord  to Sumanaḥ-sarovara
there  Govardhana  seeing  the Lord  became  overwhelmed
= Then the Lord went on to Sumanaḥ-sarovara. There He saw Govardhana and was overwhelmed.

@16
Govardhana  seeing  the Lord  became  prostrate like a rod
one  rock  embracing  He became  mad
= Seeing Govardhana, the Lord fell down like a rod in obeisance. He embraced a rock of Govardhana and became mad with love.

@17
with love  intoxicated  setting out  He came  the village of Govardhana
Harideva  seeing  there  He became  bowed down
= Intoxicated with love, He went on to the village of Govardhana. There He saw Harideva and bowed down before Him.

@18
of the lotus of Mathurā  on the western petal  whose  dwelling
Harideva  Nārāyaṇa  —  the original manifestation
= Harideva, who dwells on the western petal of the lotus of Mathurā, is Nārāyaṇa, the original manifestation.

@19
before Harideva  He dances  with love  intoxicated  being
all  the people  to see  came  the wonder  hearing
= Intoxicated with love, He danced before Harideva, and all the people, hearing of the wonder, came to see.

@20
the Lord’s  love  beauty  seeing  the people  amazed
Harideva’s  servant  the Lord’s  did  honour
= The people were amazed to see the Lord’s love and beauty, and the servant of Harideva received the Lord with honour.

@21
the Bhaṭṭācārya  at Brahma-kuṇḍa  cooking  going  did
in Brahma-kuṇḍa  bath  taking  the Lord  meal  took
= The Bhaṭṭācārya went to Brahma-kuṇḍa and cooked there, and the Lord bathed in Brahma-kuṇḍa and took His meal.

@22
that night  He stayed  of Harideva  in the temple
at night  Mahāprabhu  makes  in His mind  deliberation
= That night He stayed in the temple of Harideva, and in the night Mahāprabhu reflected in His mind:

@23
onto Govardhana  I  ever  not  shall climb
of Gopāla-rāya  darśana  how  shall I get
= “I shall never climb onto Govardhana. How, then, shall I have darśana of Gopāla-rāya?”

@24
so  in mind  thinking  the Lord  silence  keeping  remained
knowing (this)  Gopāla  the pretext of fear of the mlecchas  raised
= Thinking this, the Lord kept silent. Gopāla knew His mind and raised a pretext: fear of the mlecchas.

@25
not wishing to climb  the hill
to Himself  who regarded Himself as a devotee
coming down  from the hill  Kṛṣṇa
to Gaura  Himself  showed
= Gaura — Kṛṣṇa Himself — regarded Himself as a devotee and would not climb the hill; so Kṛṣṇa came down from the hill and showed Himself to Him.

@26
Annakūṭa  named  in the village  Gopāla’s  residence
of Rajput people  in that  village  dwelling
= Gopāla resided in the village called Annakūṭa, and the people of that village were Rajputs.

@27
one  person  coming  at night  to the villagers  said
your  village  to raid  the Turkish troops  have armed
= One night a man came and told the villagers: “Turkish troops have armed themselves to raid your village.

@28
today  at night  flee  not  remain  a single person
the Deity  taking  run away  will come  tomorrow  the yavanas
= Flee tonight, and let not a single person remain. Take the Deity and run, for the yavanas will come tomorrow.”

@29
hearing  of the village  the people  anxious  became
first  Gopāla  taking  in the village of Gāṭholi  they placed
= Hearing this, the villagers grew anxious. First of all they took Gopāla and placed Him in the village of Gāṭholi.

@30
in a brāhmaṇa’s house  Gopāla’s  in secret  service
the village  deserted  became  fled  all the people
= In a brāhmaṇa’s house Gopāla was served in secret. The village was deserted, for everyone had fled.

@31
thus  from fear of the mlecchas  Gopāla  flees  again and again
the temple  leaving  in a grove  He stays  or  in another village
= In this way, for fear of the mlecchas, Gopāla flees again and again: He leaves His temple and stays in a grove or in another village.

@32
in the morning  the Lord  in Mānasa-gaṅgā  taking  bath
on the circumambulation of Govardhana  He made  departure
= In the morning the Lord bathed in Mānasa-gaṅgā and set out to circle Govardhana.

@33
Govardhana  seeing  the Lord  absorbed in love  becoming
dancing  dancing  He went  a verse  reciting
= Seeing Govardhana, the Lord was overcome with love, and He went on, dancing and dancing and reciting this verse:

@34
ah  this  hill  O friends  best of Hari’s servants
since  of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  the feet  by the touch  rejoicing
honour  offers  with Their herds of cows  to Them  because
with water  fine grass  caves  bulbs  roots
= “Ah, friends, this hill is the best of Hari’s servants! Thrilled by the touch of the feet of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, it honours Them, together with Their cows and companions, with water to drink, fine grass, caves, and bulbs and roots.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.21.18)[^34]

@35
at Govinda-kuṇḍa and other  holy places  the Lord  took  bath
there  He heard  Gopāla  —  Gāṭholi  in the village
= The Lord bathed at Govinda-kuṇḍa and other holy places, and there He heard that Gopāla was in the village of Gāṭholi.

@36
to that  village  going  He had  darśana of Gopāla
in the rapture of love  the Lord  performs  kīrtana and dance
= He went to that village and had darśana of Gopāla, and in the rapture of love the Lord sang and danced.

@37
of Gopāla  the beauty  seeing  the Lord’s  rapture
this  verse  reciting  He dances  came  the end of the day
= Seeing the beauty of Gopāla, the Lord was enraptured. Reciting this verse, He danced until the day was over:

@38
the left  of the lotus-eyed Lord
staff-like arm  that  may protect  you
the state of a toy ball  by which
was brought  Govardhana  hill
= “May the left arm of the lotus-eyed Lord protect you — the arm by which Govardhana Hill was made a ball for His play.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu 2.1.62)

@39
in this way  for three days  Gopāla  He beheld
on the fourth day  Gopāla  to His own temple  went
= In this way He beheld Gopāla for three days, and on the fourth day Gopāla went back to His own temple.

@40
with Gopāla  going  He came  dancing and singing  doing
in a joyful uproar  the people  say  Hari  Hari
= He went along with Gopāla, dancing and singing, and in a joyful uproar the people cried, “Hari! Hari!”

@notes
[^2]: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.
[^8]: The same verse as Ādi 4.215 and Madhya 8.98; translation as there.
[^34]: According to the *Anubhāṣya*, the gopīs sing this verse: wandering in longing for Kṛṣṇa, they see before them Govardhana, the king of mountains, nondifferent from Vrajendranandana.
