@title The Lord’s Infant Pastimes

@1
somehow  being remembered  whom
the difficult  easy  becomes
being forgotten  the reverse  becomes
Śrī Caitanya  Him  I worship
= “I worship Śrī Caitanya: when He is remembered in any way at all, the difficult becomes easy, and when He is forgotten, the opposite happens.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa 20.1)

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s devotees  all
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
of the Lord  I have told  this  the sūtra of the birth pastime
Yaśodā’s  son  as  became  Śacī’s son
= I have told the *sūtra* of the Lord’s birth pastime—how Yaśodā’s son became the son of Śacī.

@4
in brief  I have told  of the birth pastime  the sequence
now  I tell  of the childhood pastimes  the sūtras’  enumeration
= I have told the sequence of the birth pastime in brief; now I shall list the *sūtras* of the childhood pastimes.

@5
I praise  of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa
the childhood pastimes  enchanting
humanlike  though  them  by the Lord’s
activity  filled within
= I praise the enchanting childhood pastimes of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa: though they seem humanlike, within they are filled with the deeds of the Lord.

@6
in the childhood pastimes  first  the Lord’s  on His back  lying
to father and mother  showed  the marks  of the feet
= In His childhood pastimes the Lord first lay on His back and showed His father and mother the marks on His feet.

@7
in the house  the two  persons  seeing  of small feet  the marks
in them  flag  thunderbolt  conch  disc  fish
= In the house the two saw the prints of small feet, and in them were a flag, a thunderbolt, a conch, a disc and a fish.

@8
seeing  of both  in the hearts  arose  wonder
whose  footprints  in the house  not  get  certainty
= Seeing them, both were filled with wonder: whose footprints could be in the house? They could not be sure.

@9
Miśra  says  Bāla-gopāla  is  with the śilā
He  taking a form  plays  I know  in the house  for fun
= Miśra said, “Bāla-gopāla is here with our *śālagrāma-śilā*. I think He takes a form and plays about the house for fun.”

@10
at that  moment  waking  Nimāi  makes  crying
on the lap  taking  Śacī  Him  gave to drink  the breast
= At that moment Nimāi woke and began to cry. Śacī took Him on her lap and nursed Him.

@11
the breast  while giving  the son’s  feet  saw
those  marks  on the feet  seeing  Miśra  called
= As she nursed Him she looked at her son’s feet and, seeing those very marks on them, she called Miśra.

@12
seeing  Miśra’s  became  joyful  mind
in secret  called  Nīlāmbara  Cakravartī
= Seeing them, Miśra rejoiced and secretly called Nīlāmbara Cakravartī.

@13
the marks  seeing  Cakravartī  says  smiling
the ascendant  having calculated  before  I  have kept  written
= Seeing the marks, Cakravartī said with a smile, “When I calculated His ascendant earlier, I wrote all this down.

@14
thirty-two  marks  of a great personality  the ornaments
this  child’s body  I see  those  all  marks
= There are thirty-two marks that adorn a great personality, and I see all of them on this child’s body.

@15
five long  five fine
seven red  six raised
three short  broad  deep
with thirty-two marks  great
= “A great personality bears thirty-two marks: five parts of his body are long, five fine, seven reddish and six raised; three are short, three broad and three deep.”

@16
of Nārāyaṇa  bearing the marks  His holy hands and feet
this  child  all  people  will  deliver
= His holy hands and feet bear the marks of Nārāyaṇa. This child will deliver all people.

@17
this one  indeed  will make  of Vaiṣṇava dharma  the preaching
from Him  will be  of both  families  the deliverance
= He will preach Vaiṣṇava *dharma*, and through Him both families will be delivered.

@18
a great festival  hold  all  invite  the brāhmaṇas
today  the day  is good  I shall make  the name-giving
= Hold a great festival and invite all the brāhmaṇas. Today is an auspicious day; I shall perform the naming ceremony.

@19
all people  will make  He  support  nourishment
“Viśvambhara”  the name  His  this  indeed  the reason
= He will support and nourish all people; that is why His name is Viśvambhara, ‘He who sustains the universe.’”

@20
hearing  of Śacī and Miśra  in the mind  joy  increased
brāhmaṇas and their wives  inviting  festival  held
= Hearing this, Śacī and Miśra grew still more joyful. They invited brāhmaṇas and their wives and held a festival.

@21
then  in a few  days  the Lord’s  crawling on the knees
various  wonders  there  caused  to be seen
= After some days the Lord began crawling on His knees, and then He showed many wonders.

@22
of crying  on the pretext  made to say  the name of Hari
the women  all  “Hari”  say  laughs  the abode of Gaura’s radiance
= On the pretext of crying He made people chant the name of Hari: all the women said “Hari,” and the golden Lord laughed.

@23
then  in a few  days  made  walking on His feet
with the children  joining  made  various  games
= After some days more He began to walk, and joining the other children He played many games.

@24
one day  Śacī  puffed rice and sweets  bringing
a dish  filling  giving  says  eat  sitting
= One day Śacī brought puffed rice and sweets, filled a dish, gave it to Him and said, “Sit down and eat.”

@25
so  saying  went  Śacī  in the house  work  to do
hiding  began  the child  earth  to eat
= With these words Śacī went off to do her housework, and the child secretly began to eat earth.

@26
seeing  Śacī  running  came  making  “Alas!”  “Alas!”
the earth  snatching  taking  says  earth  why  You eat
= Seeing this, Śacī came running, crying “Alas! Alas!” She snatched the earth away and said, “Why are You eating earth?”

@27
weeping  says  the child  why  you make  anger
you  earth  to eat  gave  My  what  fault
= Weeping, the child said, “Why are you angry? You gave Me earth to eat—what fault is it of Mine?

@28
puffed rice  sweets  food  all  of earth  transformations
this  earth  that  earth  what  distinction
= Puffed rice, sweets, grains—all are transformations of earth. This is earth, that is earth; what difference is there?

@29
earth  the body  earth  the food  see  considering
without consideration  you give  blame  what  to say  can I
= The body is earth, the food is earth—consider and see. You blame Me without thinking; what can I say?”

@30
inwardly  astonished  Śacī  said  to Him
earth  to eat  the yoga of knowledge  who  taught  You
= Astonished at heart, Śacī said to Him, “Who taught You this yoga of knowledge so that You may eat earth?

@31
of earth  a transformation  food  if eaten  of the body  nourishment  comes
earth  if eaten  disease  comes  the body  goes  to decay
= Food, a transformation of earth, nourishes the body when eaten; but if you eat earth itself, you fall ill and the body wastes away.

@32
of earth  a transformation  in a pot  water  filling  I bring
on a lump of earth  I pour  when  soaking  goes  the water
= In a pot, which is a transformation of earth, I fill and carry water; but when I pour water on a lump of earth, it soaks away.”

@33
Himself  to hide  the Lord  said  to her
before  why  this  Mother  not  taught  Me
= To hide Himself, the Lord said to her, “Mother, why didn’t you teach Me this before?

@34
now  that  I have learned  more  earth  not  will eat
hunger  comes  when  then  your  breast  will drink
= Now I know it, and I will not eat earth any more. When I am hungry, I will drink at your breast.”

@35
so  saying  of the mother  on the lap  climbing
the breast  drinking  does  the Lord  slightly  smiling
= Saying this, the Lord climbed onto His mother’s lap and, smiling slightly, drank at her breast.

@36
in this way  on various pretexts  majesty  shows
childhood mood  manifesting  afterwards  hides
= In this way, on various pretexts, He revealed His majesty, and then hid it again by showing a child’s ways.

@37
of a guest brāhmaṇa  the food  ate  three times
afterwards  in secret  that  brāhmaṇa  made  delivered
= He ate a guest brāhmaṇa’s offering three times, and afterwards, in secret, He delivered that brāhmaṇa.

@38
thieves  taking  went  the Lord  outside  finding
on their  shoulders  riding  came  them  misleading
= Finding the Lord outside, thieves carried Him off; riding on their shoulders, He bewildered them and came back home.

@39
illness  on the pretext  of Jagadīśa and Hiraṇya  in the houses
Viṣṇu’s  offering  ate  on Ekādaśī  day
= On the pretext of illness He ate Viṣṇu’s offering in the homes of Jagadīśa and Hiraṇya on the day of Ekādaśī.

@40
the children  taking  of the neighbors  into the houses
stealing  things  eats  beats  the boys
= He would go with the children into the neighbors’ houses, steal food and eat it, and beat the other boys.

@41
the children  all  to Śacī  made  complaint
hearing  Śacī  the son  some  gave  scolding
= The children all complained to Śacī, and hearing them, she scolded her son a little:

@42
why  steal  You  why  beat  the children
why  to others’ houses  go  what  or  is not  at home
= “Why do You steal? Why do You beat the children? Why do You go to other people’s houses—what is there that we don’t have at home?”

@43
hearing  angry  becoming  the Lord  into the house  going
in the house  all  the pots  there were  threw down  breaking
= Hearing this, the Lord grew angry, went into the house and smashed all the pots that were there.

@44
then  Śacī  on her lap  taking  made  pacified
ashamed  became  the Lord  knowing  His own  fault
= Then Śacī took Him on her lap and soothed Him, and the Lord became ashamed, knowing His fault.

@45
once  with His soft hand  made  to the mother  a blow
the mother  fainted  seeing  makes  crying
= Once He struck His mother with His soft hand; seeing her faint, He began to cry.

@46
the women  say  a coconut  give  bringing
then  well  will be  Your  mother
= The women said, “Bring a coconut, and then Your mother will be well.”

@47
outside  going  brought  two  coconuts
seeing  the extraordinary  became  astonished  all
= He went outside and brought back two coconuts. Everyone was astonished to see such a wonder.

@48
once  with the children  bath  took  in the Gaṅgā
the girls  came  there  gods  to worship
= Once He was bathing in the Gaṅgā with the children, and the girls came there to worship the gods.

@49
in the Gaṅgā  bath  having taken  worship  to do  began
among the girls  the Lord  coming  sat
= After bathing in the Gaṅgā they began their worship, and the Lord came and sat down among the girls.

@50
to the girls  says  Me  worship  I  will give  a boon
Gaṅgā  Durgā  maidservants  Mine  Maheśa  servant
= He said to the girls, “Worship Me, and I will give you a boon. Gaṅgā and Durgā are My maidservants, and Maheśa is My servant.”

@51
Himself  sandalwood  putting on  puts on  flower garlands
the offerings  snatching  eats  sweets  rice  bananas
= He Himself put on the sandalwood paste and the flower garlands, snatched the offerings and ate them—the sweets, the rice and the bananas.

@52
in anger  the girls  say  listen  O  Nimāi
by village  relationship  are  You  of us all  the brother
= The girls said angrily, “Listen, Nimāi! By village relationship You are a brother to us all.

@53
of us all  on the part  this  to say  not  is fitting
do not  take  the gods’ articles  do not  do  wrong
= It isn’t proper for us to say this, but do not take the gods’ offerings, and do not do wrong!”

@54
the Lord  says  to you all  I have given  this  boon
of you all  the husband  will be  supremely  handsome
= The Lord said, “I have given you all this boon: each of you will have a most handsome husband,

@55
learned  clever  young  rich in wealth and grain
seven  seven  sons  will be  long-lived  intelligent
= learned, clever, young and rich in wealth and grain; each of you will have seven sons, long-lived and intelligent.”

@56
the boon  hearing  of the girls  within  delight
outwardly  rebuke  make  making  false  anger
= Hearing the boon, the girls were delighted at heart, but outwardly they scolded Him in feigned anger.

@57
some  girl  ran off  the offerings  taking
to her  calling  says  the Lord  angry  becoming
= One girl ran off with her offerings, and the Lord, angry, called after her:

@58
if  the offerings  not  you give  being  miserly
an old  husband  will be  and  four  co-wives
= “If you are miserly and do not give Me the offerings, you will have an old husband and four co-wives!”

@59
this  hearing  of them all  in the minds  arose  fear
some  thing  He knows  or  by a god possessed  is
= Hearing this, they all grew afraid: “Perhaps He knows something, or a god has entered Him.”

@60
bringing  the offerings  they  before Him  held
eating  the offerings  to them  the desired boons  gave
= They brought the offerings and held them out before Him; He ate the offerings and gave them the boons they desired.

@61
in this way  pranks  all  to the people  shows
sorrow  in anyone’s  mind  is not  all  happiness  get
= In this way He displayed His pranks to everyone; no one was troubled at heart, and all were made happy.

@62
one day  Vallabhācārya’s daughter  “Lakṣmī”  by name
the gods  to worship  came  having taken  bath in the Gaṅgā
= One day Vallabhācārya’s daughter, named Lakṣmī, came to worship the gods after bathing in the Gaṅgā.

@63
her  seeing  the Lord’s  became  longing  mind
Lakṣmī  at heart  happiness  got  of the Lord  at the sight
= Seeing her, the Lord’s heart was drawn to her, and Lakṣmī felt joy at heart on seeing the Lord.

@64
natural  love  of the two  made  arising
in the childhood state  hidden  the body  became  firm
= Their natural love awakened; though their bodies were still veiled in childhood, it became firm.

@65
the two  seeing  of the two  in the hearts  became  joy
the worship of the gods  on the pretext  made  both  manifest
= Seeing each other, both rejoiced at heart, and under the pretext of worshipping the gods they revealed their feelings.

@66
the Lord  says  “Me”  worship  I  am the Supreme Lord
Me  if you worship  will get  the desired  boon
= The Lord said, “Worship Me—I am Maheśvara, the Supreme Lord. If you worship Me, you will obtain the boon you desire.”

@67
Lakṣmī  His  on the body  placed  with flowers  sandalwood
of mallikā  a garland  giving  made  obeisance
= Lakṣmī offered flowers and sandalwood paste on His body, placed a garland of *mallikā* flowers on Him and bowed down.

@68
the Lord  her  worship  receiving  to smile  began
a verse  reciting  her  feeling  acceptance  made
= Receiving her worship, the Lord began to smile, and reciting a verse He accepted her feelings.

@69
the resolve  is known  O chaste ones
your  worship of Me
by Me  approved  that  it
true  to become  deserves
= “O chaste ones, I know your resolve to worship Me. I approve of it, and it deserves to come true.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.22.25)

@70
in this way  the pastime  the two  having made  went  home
deep  Caitanya’s pastime  who  to understand  can
= After this pastime the two went home. Who can understand the deep pastimes of Caitanya?

@71
Caitanya’s  pranks  seeing  out of love  all  people
Śacī and Jagannātha  meeting  give  complaints
= Seeing Caitanya’s pranks, everyone would complain to Śacī and Jagannātha out of love.

@72
one day  Śacī-devī  the son  scolding
to catch  went  the son  went  running away
= One day Śacī-devī scolded her son and went to catch Him, but He ran away.

@73
in the pit for leavings  of discarded pots  on top
sat  happily  the Lord  Viśvambhara
= Lord Viśvambhara sat happily on top of the discarded pots in the refuse pit.

@74
Śacī  coming  says  why  the impure  You touched
bath in the Gaṅgā  take  going  impure  You have become
= Śacī came and said, “Why have You touched impure things? You have become impure—go and bathe in the Gaṅgā.”

@75
this  hearing  to the mother  told  knowledge of Brahman
astonished  becoming  the mother  made  bathe
= Hearing this, He told His mother about the knowledge of Brahman. His mother was astonished, but she made Him bathe.

@76
once  with the son  Śacī  made  lying down
sees  divine beings  coming  filled  the house
= Once, as Śacī lay down with her son, she saw divine beings come and fill the house.

@77
Śacī  says  go  son  call  your father
the mother’s order  receiving  the Lord  went  outside
= Śacī said, “Go, son, call your father.” At His mother’s bidding the Lord went outside.

@78
as He walks  on His feet  ankle-bells  sound  jingling
hearing  amazed  became  of father and mother  the minds
= As He walked, ankle-bells jingled on His feet, and His father and mother were amazed to hear them.

@79
Miśra  says  this  very  wonderful  story
on the child’s  bare feet  why  of ankle-bells  the sound
= Miśra said, “This is very wonderful! Why is there a sound of ankle-bells on the child’s bare feet?”

@80
Śacī  says  another  one  wonder  I saw
divine  divine  beings  coming  the courtyard  filled
= Śacī said, “I saw another wonder: divine beings came and filled the courtyard.

@81
what  clamor  they make  to understand  not  I can
whom  or  praises  they make  conjecture  I make
= I could not understand what they were saying so loudly. I suppose they were praising someone.”

@82
Miśra  says  whatever  may be  worry  any  is not
Viśvambhara’s  well-being  may be  this  only  I want
= Miśra said, “Whatever it may be, there is nothing to worry about. All I want is Viśvambhara’s well-being.”

@83
one day  Miśra  of the son  the pranks  seeing
instruction in dharma  gave  much  rebuke  making
= One day, seeing his son’s pranks, Miśra instructed Him in *dharma* and scolded Him severely.

@84
at night  a dream  sees  a  coming  brāhmaṇa
to Miśra  says  some  angry  words
= That night he saw in a dream a brāhmaṇa who came and spoke angry words to him:

@85
Miśra  you  of the son  the truth  anything  not  know
rebuking and beating  make  a son  considering  regard
= “Miśra, you know nothing of the truth about your son. You scold and beat Him because you consider Him your son.”

@86
Miśra  says  a god  a siddha  a sage  why  not
whoever  great  He may be  now  my  son
= Miśra said, “Let Him be a god, a perfected being or a sage—why not? However great He may be, now He is my son.

@87
of the son  the nurture and teaching  the father’s  own duty
I  not  if I teach  how  will know  the essence of dharma
= To bring up and teach his son is a father’s duty. If I do not teach Him, how will He know the essence of *dharma*?”

@88
the brāhmaṇa  says  He  if  divinely perfect  is
self-perfect knowledge  then  teaching  useless  is
= The brāhmaṇa said, “But if He is divinely perfect, His knowledge is complete by itself, and then teaching is useless.”

@89
Miśra  says  the son  why  is not  Nārāyaṇa
even so  the father’s  dharma  the son  teaching
= Miśra said, “Even if my son is Nārāyaṇa—why not?—still, it is a father’s *dharma* to teach his son.”

@90
in this way  the two  make  of dharma  discussion
pure parental love  Miśra’s  not  knows  anything else
= In this way the two discussed *dharma*. Miśra had pure parental love and knew nothing else.

@91
this  hearing  the brāhmaṇa  went  becoming  joyful
Miśra  waking  became  supremely  astonished
= Hearing this, the brāhmaṇa went away pleased, and Miśra woke up utterly astonished.

@92
of friends and relatives  in the presence  the dream  told
hearing  all  people  astonished  became
= He told the dream to his friends and relatives, and everyone who heard it was astonished.

@93
in this way  child pastimes  makes  Gauracandra
day by day  of father and mother  increased  the joy
= Thus Gauracandra played His childhood pastimes, and day by day His parents’ joy increased.

@94
after some  days  Miśra  of the son  in the hand  chalk  gave
in a few  days  twelve-phalā  letters  learned
= After some days Miśra placed chalk in his son’s hand, and in a few days He learned the letters with their twelve compound signs.

@95
of the childhood pastimes  the sūtra  this  I have told  in sequence
this  has expanded  Vṛndāvana dāsa
= I have told in sequence the *sūtra* of the childhood pastimes; Vṛndāvana dāsa has described them in detail.

@96
therefore  the childhood pastimes  in brief  as sūtra  made
for fear of repetition  expanding  not  told
= Therefore I have given the childhood pastimes briefly, as a *sūtra*; for fear of repetition I have not told them at length.

@97
at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha  whose  hope
Caitanya-caritāmṛta  tells  Kṛṣṇadāsa
= Hoping for the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Raghunātha, Kṛṣṇadāsa tells the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
