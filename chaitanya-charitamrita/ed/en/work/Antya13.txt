@title The Stories of Jagadānanda Paṇḍita and Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī

@1
Kṛṣṇa  from separation  born  by the pain
wasted  and  though  mind  and body
attain  blossoming  through ecstatic emotions
whose  that  Gaura  I take shelter of
= I take shelter of Gaura, whose mind and body, though wasted by the pain born of separation from Kṛṣṇa, blossomed again through His ecstatic emotions.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  Mahāprabhu  with Jagadānanda  in the company
in many  ways  tastes  of love  in the waves
= In this way, in the company of Jagadānanda, Mahāprabhu tasted the waves of love in many ways.

@4
Kṛṣṇa  in separation  by sorrow  wasted  mind  and body
in ecstatic absorption  the Lord  sometimes  blossoming  becomes
= His mind and body were wasted by the sorrow of separation from Kṛṣṇa, yet at times, absorbed in ecstatic emotion, the Lord would blossom again.

@5
of the banana  on the bark  lying  very  thin  the body
against the bark  the bones  press  pain  is  in the body
= He lay on dried banana bark, and His body was very thin. His bones pressed against the bark, and His whole body ached.

@6
seeing  all  the devotees  great  sorrow  feel
to bear  could not  Jagadānanda  devised  a means
= Seeing this, all the devotees were deeply grieved. Jagadānanda could not bear it, and he devised a remedy.

@7
fine  cloth  bringing  with red ochre  with  dyed
of the śimula tree  cotton  with  it  stuffed
= He brought fine cloth, dyed it with red ochre and stuffed it with the cotton of the silk-cotton (*śimula*) tree.

@8
one  mattress  pillow  of Govinda  into the hands  gave
the Lord  have lie down  on this  to him  said
= He put a mattress and a pillow into Govinda’s hands and told him, “Have the Lord lie down on these.”

@9
to Svarūpa Gosāñi  says  Jagadānanda
today  yourself  going  the Lord  have lie down
= Jagadānanda said to Svarūpa Gosāñi, “Today please go yourself and have the Lord lie down on them.”

@10
of lying down  at the time  Svarūpa  right there  stayed
mattress  pillow  seeing  the Lord  filled with anger  became
= At bedtime Svarūpa stayed right there. But when the Lord saw the mattress and pillow, He became angry.

@11
Govinda  He asks  this  had made  which  person
of Jagadānanda  the name  hearing  shrinking  became  the mind
= He asked Govinda, “Who had this made?” But when He heard Jagadānanda’s name, His heart shrank back in apprehension.

@12
to Govinda  telling  that  mattress  away  put
of the banana  the bark  upon  lying down  did
= He had Govinda put the mattress away and lay down on the banana bark.

@13
Svarūpa  says  Your  will  what  to say  can I
the bed  if You reject  the Paṇḍita  sorrow  will feel  heavy
= Svarūpa said, “It is Your will—what can I say? But if You reject the bed, the Paṇḍita will be deeply hurt.”

@14
the Lord  says  a bedstead  one  bring  to set up
Jagadānanda  wants  Me  sense objects  to make enjoy
= The Lord said, “Then bring a bedstead too and set it up! Jagadānanda wants Me to enjoy worldly pleasures.

@15
a sannyāsī  man  My  on the ground  lying
for Me  bedstead  mattress  pillow  of the head  shaving
= I am a *sannyāsī*; I must lie on the ground. For Me to have a bedstead, a mattress and a pillow would be a disgrace—a shaven head (*mastaka-muṇḍana*).”

@16
Svarūpa Gosāñi  coming  to the Paṇḍita  told
hearing  Jagadānanda  great  sorrow  felt
= Svarūpa Gosāñi came and told the Paṇḍita, and when Jagadānanda heard it, he was deeply grieved.

@17
Svarūpa Gosāñi  then  devised  a way
of the banana  dry  leaves  brought  countless
= Then Svarūpa Gosāñi devised a way. He brought a great quantity of dry banana leaves.

@18
with his nails  tearing  tearing  them  very  fine  made
of the Lord  into the outer cloths  them  all  filled
= Tearing them again and again with his nails, he shredded them very fine and filled the Lord’s outer cloths (*bahirvāsa*) with them.

@19
in this way  two  made  covering and bedding
acceptance  made  the Lord  with much  effort
= In this way he made two pieces of bedding (*oḍana-pāḍana*), and only after much persuasion did the Lord accept them.

@20
on them  lying  does  the Lord  seeing  all  happy
Jagadānanda  inwardly and outwardly  very  unhappy
= The Lord lay on them, and seeing this, everyone was happy. But Jagadānanda remained deeply unhappy, inwardly and outwardly.

@21
before  of Jagadānanda  the wish  to Vṛndāvana  to go
the Lord  permission  not  gives  to him  not  could  set out
= Jagadānanda had long wished to go to Vṛndāvana, but the Lord would not give him permission, and so he could not set out.

@22
inner  sorrow  outwardly  display  not  made
to Mathurā  to go  from the Lord  permission  asked
= Without showing his inner sorrow outwardly, he asked the Lord for permission to go to Mathurā.

@23
the Lord  says  to Mathurā  you will go  with Me  anger  making
on Me  blame  laying  you  will become  a beggar
= The Lord said, “You will go to Mathurā angry with Me. Laying the blame on Me, you will become a beggar.”

@24
Jagadānanda  says  the Lord’s  clasping  feet
from long  since  the wish  my  to go  to Vṛndāvana
= Clasping the Lord’s feet, Jagadānanda said, “For a long time I have wished to go to Vṛndāvana.

@25
the Lord’s  permission  there was not  so  not  could  go
now  permission  give  surely  I will go  certainly
= Without Your permission I could not go. Now please give me permission; I will surely go.”

@26
the Lord  out of love  his  going  not  does  accept
he  from the Lord  permission  asks  time  after time
= Out of love, the Lord would not agree to his going, but he asked the Lord for permission again and again.

@27
to Svarūpa Gosāñi  the Paṇḍita  made  a request
from long  since  to Vṛndāvana  to go  my  heart
= The Paṇḍita appealed to Svarūpa Gosāñi: “For a long time my heart has been set on going to Vṛndāvana.

@28
the Lord’s  permission  without  there  to go  not  can
now  permission  not  He gives  me  in anger  go  saying
= Without the Lord’s permission I cannot go there, and now He will not give me permission. He says, ‘You would go in anger.’

@29
naturally  my  there  to go  the heart  is
the Lord’s  permission  obtaining  give  I make  humble request
= My wish to go there is natural. Please obtain the Lord’s permission for me—I humbly request you.”

@30
then  Svarūpa Gosāñi  says  of the Lord  at the feet
of Jagadānanda  the wish  great  to go  to Vṛndāvana
= Then Svarūpa Gosāñi submitted at the Lord’s feet: “Jagadānanda greatly desires to go to Vṛndāvana.

@31
from You  permission  he  asks  time  after time
permission  give  Mathurā  seeing  let him come back  once
= He has asked You for permission again and again. Please give him permission: let him see Mathurā once and come back.

@32
Mother  to see  just as  to the land of Gauḍa  he goes
so  once  Vṛndāvana  seeing  let him come
= Just as he goes to Gauḍa to see Mother Śacī, so let him go once, see Vṛndāvana and return.”

@33
of Svarūpa Gosāñi  at the word  the Lord  permission  gave
Jagadānanda  calling  him  instructed
= At Svarūpa Gosāñi’s word the Lord gave His permission. He called Jagadānanda and instructed him:

@34
Vārāṇasī  as far as  freely  you may go  on the road
beyond  carefully  go  with kṣatriyas and others  together
= “As far as Vārāṇasī you may travel the road freely. Beyond that, go carefully, in the company of *kṣatriyas* and others.

@35
only  a Gauḍīya  if they find  highway robbery  doing  they bind
all  plundering  bound  they keep  to go on  they prevent
= If they find a Gauḍīya alone, highwaymen (*bāṭapāḍa*) waylay him and bind him; they plunder everything, keep him bound and do not let him go on.
