@title The Lord’s Journey to the South

@1
blessed  that  I praise  Caitanya
Vāsudeva  His mind melting with compassion
cured of leprosy  filled with beauty
satisfied in devotion  made  who
= I praise that blessed Caitanya who, His heart melting with compassion, cured Vāsudeva of leprosy, filled him with beauty and made him content in devotion.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  of Sārvabhauma  the deliverance  He accomplished
for going south  of the Lord  the desire  arose
= Having thus delivered Sārvabhauma, the Lord felt the desire to go to the south.

@4
in the bright fortnight of Māgha  the Lord  took  sannyāsa
in Phālguna  coming  made  in Nīlācala  His residence
= The Lord accepted sannyāsa in the bright fortnight of Māgha, and in Phālguna He came to live in Nīlācala.

@5
of Phālguna  at the end  the Dola-yātrā  He  saw
absorbed in love  many kinds of  dancing and singing  He did
= At the end of Phālguna He saw the Dola-yātrā festival and, absorbed in love, danced and sang in many ways.

@6
in Caitra  staying  He made  Sārvabhauma’s deliverance
of Vaiśākha  at the beginning  south  to go  arose  the wish
= Staying on through Caitra, He delivered Sārvabhauma; and at the beginning of Vaiśākha He resolved to go south.

@7
His own associates  bringing  He speaks  humility  showing
embrace  making  all  with His divine hands  holding
= He called His associates together, embraced them all, took them by the hand and humbly spoke to them.

@8
you all  know  I  dearer than life  as
life  leaving  may go  you all  to leave  not  I can
= “I know all of you to be dearer to Me than life itself. My life may leave Me, but I cannot leave you.

@9
you all  friends  My  a friend’s duty  you have done
here  bringing  to Me  Jagannātha  you have shown
= You are My friends, and you have done a friend’s duty: you brought Me here and showed Me Jagannātha.

@10
now  from all  I  beg  one  gift
all  together  permission  give  I shall go  south
= Now I beg one gift from all of you: give Me your permission together, and I shall go south.

@11
in search of Viśvarūpa  certainly  I  shall go
alone  I shall go  anyone  with (Me)  not  I shall take
= I must certainly go in search of Viśvarūpa. I shall go alone and take no one with Me.

@12
Setubandha  from  I  not  return  until
in Nīlācala  you all  will stay  so long
= Until I return from Setubandha, all of you should stay here in Nīlācala.”

@13
Viśvarūpa’s  perfection  attainment  He knows  all
the southern  country  to deliver  He makes  this  pretext
= The Lord knows all things; He knew that Viśvarūpa had attained perfection. He used this pretext only to deliver the southern country.

@14
hearing  of all  in the minds  arose  great sorrow
silent  became  all  withered  the faces
= Hearing this, everyone was stricken with great sorrow. All fell silent, and their faces withered.

@15
Nityānanda Prabhu  says  so  how  can it be
alone  will go  You  who  this  can bear
= Nityānanda Prabhu said, “How can this be? You will go alone — who could bear it?

@16
one or two  with (You)  let go  not  fall  into the hands of rogues
whom  You say  those two  let go  You  with
= Let one or two go with You, so that You do not fall into the hands of rogues. Let any two You name go with You.

@17
of the south  the routes to the holy places  I  all  know
I  with (You)  go  Prabhu  permission  give  You
= I know all the routes to the holy places of the south. Let Me go with You, Prabhu — give Me Your permission.”

@18
the Lord  says  I  a dancer  You  the puppeteer
You  as  make dance  so  the dancing  My
= The Lord replied, “I am a dancer and You are the puppeteer. As You make Me dance, so I dance.

@19
sannyāsa  having taken  I  set out  for Vṛndāvana
You  Me  taking  brought  to Advaita’s house
= After taking sannyāsa I set out for Vṛndāvana, but You led Me instead to Advaita’s house.

@20
to Nīlācala  coming  on the way  You broke  My  daṇḍa
of you all  by the deep affection  My  work  is hindered
= On the way to Nīlācala You broke My daṇḍa. The deep affection all of you bear for Me hinders My work.

@21
Jagadānanda  wants  Me  sense objects  to make enjoy
whatever  he says  that  out of fear  I try  to do
= Jagadānanda wants Me to enjoy worldly comforts, and whatever he says, I try to do out of fear.

@22
ever  if  his  word  I make  otherwise
in anger  three  days  to Me  not  he speaks  a word
= If ever I go against his word, he is angry with Me and does not speak to Me for three days.

@23
Mukunda  becomes  unhappy  seeing  the duties of sannyāsa
three times  in the cold  bathing  on the ground  lying
= Mukunda is unhappy to see the duties of My sannyāsa: bathing three times a day even in the cold, and sleeping on the bare ground.

@24
within  unhappy  Mukunda  not  he says  with his mouth
his  unhappiness  seeing  My  twofold  becomes  unhappiness
= Mukunda grieves within but says nothing; and seeing his grief, I grieve twice as much.

@25
I  indeed  a sannyāsī  Dāmodara  a brahmacārī
always  he stays  Me  over  the rod of instruction  holding
= I am a sannyāsī and Dāmodara is only a brahmacārī, yet he always stands over Me holding the rod of instruction.

@26
him  before  I  not  know  proper conduct
to him  not  is pleasing  independent  nature  My
= In his eyes I know nothing of proper conduct, and My independent ways do not please him.

@27
regard for people  not  his  Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  by
I  regard for people  ever  not  can  give up
= By Kṛṣṇa’s mercy he cares nothing for what people think; but I can never give up regard for what people think.

@28
therefore  you all  stay  in Nīlācala
days  some  I  holy places  shall wander  alone
= Therefore all of you stay in Nīlācala, while for some days I wander alone to the holy places.”

@29
of these all  controlled  the Lord  becomes  by which  qualities
of fault-finding  under the pretext  He does  of qualities  relishing
= The Lord is controlled by the very qualities of these devotees, and under the pretext of finding fault with them He relished their virtues.

@30
Caitanya’s  affection for His devotees  indescribable
Himself  the hardships of renunciation  He does  endure
= Caitanya’s affection for His devotees is beyond words. He Himself endures the hardships of renunciation,

@31
that  hardship  seeing  which  devotee  pain  feels
that  pain  His  by power  to bear  not  is possible
= but when a devotee is pained to see those hardships, that pain He has no power to bear.

@32
virtues  speaking of faults  under the pretext  all  forbidding
alone  He will wander  holy places  renunciation  practising
= So, under the pretext of declaring their virtues to be faults, He forbade them all, intending to wander alone to the holy places in strict renunciation.

@33
then  the four persons  much  pleading  made
independent  Lord  the Lord  at all  not  accepted
= Then the four devotees pleaded with Him at length, but the Lord, the independent Supreme Lord, would not consent.

@34
then  Nityānanda  says  whatever  order  Your
sorrow  happiness  whatever  may be  duty  My
= Then Nityānanda said, “Whatever You order is My duty, whether it brings sorrow or happiness.

@35
but  one  request  I make  once  more
consideration  making  it  make  acceptance
= But I make one more request: consider it, and then accept it.

@36
kaupīna  outer cloth  and  water pot
other  anything  not  will go  all  this  only
= A kaupīna, an outer cloth and a water pot — nothing else will go with You; that is all.

@37
Your  two  hands  bound  in counting the names
water pot  outer cloth  You will carry  how
= Both Your hands are occupied in counting the holy names. How will You carry the water pot and the outer cloth?

@38
absorbed in love  on the way  You  will be  unconscious
these all  things  Your  who  will make  safekeeping
= On the way You will fall unconscious in the ecstasy of love. Who will look after these things of Yours then?

@39
‘Kṛṣṇadāsa’  by name  this  simple  brāhmaṇa
him  with (You)  making  take  accept  the request
= Here is a simple brāhmaṇa named Kṛṣṇadāsa. Please take him with You — grant My request.

@40
the water pot and cloth  carrying  with You  he will go
whatever  Your  wish  do  anything  not  he will say
= He will go with You and carry the water pot and the cloth. Do whatever You wish; he will say nothing.”

@41
then  His  words  the Lord  making  acceptance
them all  taking  went  to Sārvabhauma’s house
= The Lord then accepted His words and, taking them all along, went to Sārvabhauma’s house.

@42
bowing  Sārvabhauma  seats  offered
all  meeting  then  on a seat  sat
= Sārvabhauma bowed down and offered them seats; then, after greeting everyone, he sat down.

@43
various  talks of Kṛṣṇa  the Lord  spoke  to him
to you  I have come  permission  to ask
= The Lord spoke with him of Kṛṣṇa in many ways and then said, “I have come to you to ask your permission.

@44
sannyāsa  having taken  Viśvarūpa  has gone  south
certainly  I shall make  I  his  search
= Viśvarūpa took sannyāsa and went to the south, and I must go in search of him.

@45
permission  give  certainly  I  south  shall go
by your  permission  safely  turning back  I shall come
= Give Me permission, for I must go to the south. With your permission I shall come back safely.”

@46
hearing  Sārvabhauma  became  extremely  distressed
the feet  clasping  he speaks  a sorrowful reply
= Hearing this, Sārvabhauma was deeply distressed. Clasping the Lord’s feet, he replied sorrowfully,

@47
of many births  by the fruit of piety  I obtained  Your  company
such company  Providence  my  has made  broken
= “By the piety of many births I obtained Your company, and now Providence is breaking it off.

@48
on the head  a thunderbolt  falls  if  a son  dying  goes
that  I bear  Your  separation  to bear  not  is possible
= If a thunderbolt struck my head or my son died, I could bear it; but separation from You I cannot bear.

@49
the independent Lord  You  will make  departure
a few days  stay  let me see  Your  feet
= You are the independent Lord, and You will go. But stay a few days, so that I may see Your feet.”

@50
his  by humility  of the Lord  softened  became  the heart
He stayed  a few days  not  made  departure
= His humble plea softened the Lord’s heart, and He stayed on a few days instead of leaving.

@51
the Bhaṭṭācārya  insistence  making  makes  invitation
at home  cooking  doing  the Lord  makes take  the meal
= The Bhaṭṭācārya insistently invited Him, had food cooked at home and fed the Lord.

@52

having cooked  alms  gives  she  wonderful  her  story
= His wife, known as “Ṣāṭhī’s mother”, cooked and served the Lord His meals. Her story is a wonderful one.

@53
later  indeed  I shall tell  that  making  in detail
now  I tell  the Lord’s  southern journey  the account
= I shall tell it in detail later on; now I tell the account of the Lord’s journey to the south.

@54

= After staying five days with the Bhaṭṭācārya, the Lord Himself asked permission to set out.

@55

= At the Lord’s insistence the Bhaṭṭācārya consented, and the Lord took him to the temple of Jagannātha.

@56

= After seeing Jagannātha, He asked the Deity for permission, and the priest brought the Lord a garland from the Deity as prasāda.

@57

= Receiving the permission and the garland, Gaurahari joyfully bowed down and set out in bliss for the south.

@58
the Bhaṭṭācārya  with  and  all  His own  people
Jagannātha  circumambulation  making  He made  departure
= With the Bhaṭṭācārya and all His own people He walked around Jagannātha’s temple and then departed.

@59
along the seashore  on the road to Ālālanātha
Sārvabhauma  said  to Gopīnātha Ācārya
= On the road to Ālālanātha along the seashore, Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya,

@60

them  prasāda  taking  come  by a brāhmaṇa
= “I have kept four sets of kaupīnas and outer cloths at home. Have them brought by a brāhmaṇa, together with prasāda.”

@61

= Then Sārvabhauma said at the Lord’s feet, “Prabhu, please fulfil my request.

@62

governor  is  he  in Vidyānagara
= Rāmānanda Rāya lives on the bank of the Godāvarī; he is the governor in Vidyānagara.

@63

= Do not neglect him, thinking him a śūdra and a worldly man (*viṣayī*). At my request, be sure to meet him.

@64

= He is the one person worthy of Your company. On earth there is no devotee who knows the taste of rasa (*rasika*) to equal him.

@65

= In learning and in the rasa of devotion he has reached the limit of both. When You speak with him, You will know his greatness.

@66

= Not understanding his extraordinary words and actions, I used to mock him, knowing him to be a Vaiṣṇava.

@67

= By Your grace I now understand who he truly is. Speak with him, and You will see how great he is.”

@68

= The Lord accepted his words and, bidding him farewell, embraced him,

@69
at home  Kṛṣṇa  worshipping  Me  make  blessings
to Nīlācala  I may come  so that  by your  grace
= saying, “Worship Kṛṣṇa at home and bless Me, so that by your grace I may return to Nīlācala.”

@70

= With these words Mahāprabhu departed, and Sārvabhauma fell unconscious on the spot.

= Paying no heed to him, the Lord went quickly on His way. Who can understand the heart and mind of Mahāprabhu?

@72

= Such is the nature of the hearts of great souls: they are soft as a flower, and hard as a thunderbolt.

= “Harder than a thunderbolt and softer than a flower are the hearts of those who are beyond this world. Who can understand them?”

= Nityānanda Prabhu lifted the Bhaṭṭācārya up and sent him home with his men.

= The devotees quickly came and joined the Lord, and then Gopīnātha arrived with the cloth and the prasāda.

= Then the Lord came with them all to Ālālanātha, bowed down to Him and offered Him many prayers.

= The sight filled the people with wonder. Of all who came, no one went home.

= In whichever village He stayed and took His meal in someone’s house, all the people of that village came to see Him.

@107
by the Lord’s  mercy  they become  mahā-bhāgavatas

= By the Lord’s mercy they became mahā-bhāgavatas, and all of them, becoming ācāryas, delivered the world.

@108

all lands  Vaiṣṇava  became  through the Lord’s  connection
= He did this all the way to Setubandha, and through their connection with the Lord all the lands became Vaiṣṇava.

@109

that  power  manifesting  He delivered  the southern country
= The power He had not manifested in Navadvīpa He now manifested to deliver the southern country.

@110

= Whoever worships the Lord receives His mercy, and only such a person accepts all these pastimes as true.

@111

= Whoever has no faith in these extraordinary pastimes is ruined both in this world and in the next.

@112
first  I told  the Lord’s  in what manner  journey
in this way  know  as long as  the southern tour
= I have told first how the Lord travelled; know that it was the same throughout His tour of the south.

@113

Kūrma  seeing  He made  to Him  prayers and obeisances
= Travelling on in this way, He came to Kūrma-sthāna. Seeing the Deity of Kūrma, He offered Him prayers and obeisances.

@114
absorbed in love  laughing  weeping  dancing and singing  He did
seeing  of all  the people  in the hearts  wonder  arose
= Absorbed in love, He laughed and wept, danced and sang, and all who saw it were filled with wonder.

@115
the wonder  hearing  people  came  to see
the Lord’s  beauty and love  seeing  became  amazed
= Hearing of this wonder, people came to see, and they were amazed at the Lord’s beauty and love.

@116
by the sight  Vaiṣṇavas  became  say  Kṛṣṇa  Hari
absorbed in love  dance  the people  raised  arms  making
= Just by seeing Him they became Vaiṣṇavas and called out, “Kṛṣṇa! Hari!” Absorbed in love, the people danced with their arms raised.

@117
Kṛṣṇa’s name  from the mouths of people  hearing  ceaselessly
those  people  Vaiṣṇava  made  other  all  villages
= Having heard the name of Kṛṣṇa ceaselessly from others’ lips, these people in turn made all the other villages Vaiṣṇava.

@118
in this way  by succession  the land  Vaiṣṇava  became
by the flood of the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the land  He flooded
= In this way, one from another, the whole land became Vaiṣṇava: He flooded the country with the nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s name.

@119
after a while  the Lord  when  outward consciousness  showed
of Kūrma  the servant  much  honour  showed
= After some time, when the Lord returned to outward consciousness, the servant of the Deity of Kūrma honoured Him greatly.

@120
which  village  He goes  there  this  behaviour
in one  place  I have told  not  I shall tell  again
= In every village He went to, it was the same. I have described it here in one place and shall not describe it again.

@121
‘Kūrma’  by name  in that  village  a Vedic  brāhmaṇa
great  with faith and devotion  made  the Lord’s  invitation
= In that village a Vedic brāhmaṇa named Kūrma invited the Lord with great faith and devotion.

@122
home  bringing  the Lord’s  made  feet  washing

= He brought the Lord home and washed His feet, and he and his whole family drank that water.

@123
of many kinds  with affection  meal  made take

= With great affection he fed the Lord many kinds of food, and he and his family ate the Gosāñi’s remnants.

@124
which  lotus feet  Your  Brahmā  meditation  makes

= “Your lotus feet, on which Brahmā meditates — those same lotus feet have come in person to my house!

@125
my  of good fortune  the limit  not  can be  told

= There is no telling the limit of my good fortune. Today my birth, my family and my wealth have become glorious.

@126
mercy  show  Prabhu  to me  let me go  with You

= Be merciful to me, Prabhu — let me go with You. I shall not be able to bear the waves of separation from You.”

@127
the Lord  says  such  talk  ever  not  you should say
at home  staying  Kṛṣṇa’s name  constantly  you should take
= The Lord said, “Never speak like that. Stay at home and chant the name of Kṛṣṇa constantly.

@128

= Whomever you see, teach him about Kṛṣṇa. By My order become a guru and deliver this land.

@129
ever  not  will obstruct  you  the waves of worldly life
again  in this  place  you will get  My  company
= Then the waves of worldly life will never obstruct you, and here, in this very place, you will have My company again.”

@130
in this way  in whose  house  takes  the Lord  alms

= In whatever house the Lord took His meal, the host spoke in the same way, and the Lord gave him this same teaching.

@131-132

= On His way, in whatever village He stayed at a temple, and with whatever great soul in whose house He took His meal, He did as He had done with Kūrma. So He did everywhere, until Gosāñi came back to Nīlācala.

@133

= That is why I have described it here in detail; know that this is how the Lord acted everywhere.

= He stayed there that night, and in the morning, after bathing, the Lord set out.
