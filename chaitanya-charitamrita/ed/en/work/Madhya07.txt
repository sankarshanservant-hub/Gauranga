@title The Lord’s Journey to the South

@1
blessed  that  I praise  Caitanya
Vāsudeva  His mind melting with compassion
cured of leprosy  filled with beauty
satisfied in devotion  made  who
= I praise that blessed Caitanya who, His heart melting with compassion, cured Vāsudeva of leprosy, filled him with beauty and made him content in devotion.

@2
glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
= Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

@3
in this way  of Sārvabhauma  the deliverance  He accomplished
for going south  of the Lord  the desire  arose
= Having thus delivered Sārvabhauma, the Lord felt the desire to go to the south.

@4
in the bright fortnight of Māgha  the Lord  took  sannyāsa
in Phālguna  coming  made  in Nīlācala  His residence
= The Lord accepted sannyāsa in the bright fortnight of Māgha, and in Phālguna He came to live in Nīlācala.

@5
of Phālguna  at the end  the Dola-yātrā  He  saw
absorbed in love  many kinds of  dancing and singing  He did
= At the end of Phālguna He saw the Dola-yātrā festival and, absorbed in love, danced and sang in many ways.

@6
in Caitra  staying  He made  Sārvabhauma’s deliverance
of Vaiśākha  at the beginning  south  to go  arose  the wish
= Staying on through Caitra, He delivered Sārvabhauma; and at the beginning of Vaiśākha He resolved to go south.

@7
His own associates  bringing  He speaks  humility  showing
embrace  making  all  with His divine hands  holding
= He called His associates together, embraced them all, took them by the hand and humbly spoke to them.

@8
you all  know  I  dearer than life  as
life  leaving  may go  you all  to leave  not  I can
= “I know all of you to be dearer to Me than life itself. My life may leave Me, but I cannot leave you.

@9
you all  friends  My  a friend’s duty  you have done
here  bringing  to Me  Jagannātha  you have shown
= You are My friends, and you have done a friend’s duty: you brought Me here and showed Me Jagannātha.

@10
now  from all  I  beg  one  gift
all  together  permission  give  I shall go  south
= Now I beg one gift from all of you: give Me your permission together, and I shall go south.

@11
in search of Viśvarūpa  certainly  I  shall go
alone  I shall go  anyone  with (Me)  not  I shall take
= I must certainly go in search of Viśvarūpa. I shall go alone and take no one with Me.

@12
Setubandha  from  I  not  return  until
in Nīlācala  you all  will stay  so long
= Until I return from Setubandha, all of you should stay here in Nīlācala.”

@13
Viśvarūpa’s  perfection  attainment  He knows  all
the southern  country  to deliver  He makes  this  pretext
= The Lord knows all things; He knew that Viśvarūpa had attained perfection. He used this pretext only to deliver the southern country.

@14
hearing  of all  in the minds  arose  great sorrow
silent  became  all  withered  the faces
= Hearing this, everyone was stricken with great sorrow. All fell silent, and their faces withered.

@15
Nityānanda Prabhu  says  so  how  can it be
alone  will go  You  who  this  can bear
= Nityānanda Prabhu said, “How can this be? You will go alone — who could bear it?

@16
one or two  with (You)  let go  not  fall  into the hands of rogues
whom  You say  those two  let go  You  with
= Let one or two go with You, so that You do not fall into the hands of rogues. Let any two You name go with You.

@17
of the south  the routes to the holy places  I  all  know
I  with (You)  go  Prabhu  permission  give  You
= I know all the routes to the holy places of the south. Let Me go with You, Prabhu — give Me Your permission.”

@18
the Lord  says  I  a dancer  You  the puppeteer
You  as  make dance  so  the dancing  My
= The Lord replied, “I am a dancer and You are the puppeteer. As You make Me dance, so I dance.

@19
sannyāsa  having taken  I  set out  for Vṛndāvana
You  Me  taking  brought  to Advaita’s house
= After taking sannyāsa I set out for Vṛndāvana, but You led Me instead to Advaita’s house.

@20
to Nīlācala  coming  on the way  You broke  My  daṇḍa
of you all  by the deep affection  My  work  is hindered
= On the way to Nīlācala You broke My daṇḍa. The deep affection all of you bear for Me hinders My work.

@21
Jagadānanda  wants  Me  sense objects  to make enjoy
whatever  he says  that  out of fear  I try  to do
= Jagadānanda wants Me to enjoy worldly comforts, and whatever he says, I try to do out of fear.

@22
ever  if  his  word  I make  otherwise
in anger  three  days  to Me  not  he speaks  a word
= If ever I go against his word, he is angry with Me and does not speak to Me for three days.

@23
Mukunda  becomes  unhappy  seeing  the duties of sannyāsa
three times  in the cold  bathing  on the ground  lying
= Mukunda is unhappy to see the duties of My sannyāsa: bathing three times a day even in the cold, and sleeping on the bare ground.

@24
within  unhappy  Mukunda  not  he says  with his mouth
his  unhappiness  seeing  My  twofold  becomes  unhappiness
= Mukunda grieves within but says nothing; and seeing his grief, I grieve twice as much.

@25
I  indeed  a sannyāsī  Dāmodara  a brahmacārī
always  he stays  Me  over  the rod of instruction  holding
= I am a sannyāsī and Dāmodara is only a brahmacārī, yet he always stands over Me holding the rod of instruction.

@26
him  before  I  not  know  proper conduct
to him  not  is pleasing  independent  nature  My
= In his eyes I know nothing of proper conduct, and My independent ways do not please him.

@27
regard for people  not  his  Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  by
I  regard for people  ever  not  can  give up
= By Kṛṣṇa’s mercy he cares nothing for what people think; but I can never give up regard for what people think.

@28
therefore  you all  stay  in Nīlācala
days  some  I  holy places  shall wander  alone
= Therefore all of you stay in Nīlācala, while for some days I wander alone to the holy places.”

@29
of these all  controlled  the Lord  becomes  by which  qualities
of fault-finding  under the pretext  He does  of qualities  relishing
= The Lord is controlled by the very qualities of these devotees, and under the pretext of finding fault with them He relished their virtues.

@30
Caitanya’s  affection for His devotees  indescribable
Himself  the hardships of renunciation  He does  endure
= Caitanya’s affection for His devotees is beyond words. He Himself endures the hardships of renunciation,

@31
that  hardship  seeing  which  devotee  pain  feels
that  pain  His  by power  to bear  not  is possible
= but when a devotee is pained to see those hardships, that pain He has no power to bear.

@32
virtues  speaking of faults  under the pretext  all  forbidding
alone  He will wander  holy places  renunciation  practising
= So, under the pretext of declaring their virtues to be faults, He forbade them all, intending to wander alone to the holy places in strict renunciation.

@33
then  the four persons  much  pleading  made
independent  Lord  the Lord  at all  not  accepted
= Then the four devotees pleaded with Him at length, but the Lord, the independent Supreme Lord, would not consent.

@34
then  Nityānanda  says  whatever  order  Your
sorrow  happiness  whatever  may be  duty  My
= Then Nityānanda said, “Whatever You order is My duty, whether it brings sorrow or happiness.

@35
but  one  request  I make  once  more
consideration  making  it  make  acceptance
= But I make one more request: consider it, and then accept it.

@36
kaupīna  outer cloth  and  water pot
other  anything  not  will go  all  this  only
= A kaupīna, an outer cloth and a water pot — nothing else will go with You; that is all.

@37
Your  two  hands  bound  in counting the names
water pot  outer cloth  You will carry  how
= Both Your hands are occupied in counting the holy names. How will You carry the water pot and the outer cloth?

@38
absorbed in love  on the way  You  will be  unconscious
these all  things  Your  who  will make  safekeeping
= On the way You will fall unconscious in the ecstasy of love. Who will look after these things of Yours then?

@39
‘Kṛṣṇadāsa’  by name  this  simple  brāhmaṇa
him  with (You)  making  take  accept  the request
= Here is a simple brāhmaṇa named Kṛṣṇadāsa. Please take him with You — grant My request.

@40
the water pot and cloth  carrying  with You  he will go
whatever  Your  wish  do  anything  not  he will say
= He will go with You and carry the water pot and the cloth. Do whatever You wish; he will say nothing.”
