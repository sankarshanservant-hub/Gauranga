= Haridāsa Ṭhākura said, “What can I do? I have made a vow — how can I give it up?”

= I have bewitched all living beings, beginning with Brahmā; you alone I could not bewitch.

@250-251

= You are a great devotee (*mahā-bhāgavata*). By seeing you and hearing you chant Kṛṣṇa’s Name, my heart has become pure and longs to take Kṛṣṇa’s Name. Be merciful to me: instruct me in Kṛṣṇa’s Name.

@252

= In the descent of Caitanya a flood of the nectar of love is flowing. All living beings float in that love, and the earth has become blessed.

= Wretched is the soul who does not float in this flood: for him there is no deliverance even in millions of *kalpas*.

= Long ago I received the Name of Rāma from Śiva; now, by your company, I long to take the Name of Kṛṣṇa.

@255

= The Name of Rāma is the *tāraka-brahma*, the Brahman that delivers, the cause of liberation; the Name of Kṛṣṇa, the *pāraka*, carries one across and gives love.

= Give me Kṛṣṇa’s Name and make me blessed, so that this flood of love may sweep me away too.”

= At the descent of Caitanya, Brahmā, Śiva, Sanaka and the others, eager for love of Kṛṣṇa, took birth on earth.
