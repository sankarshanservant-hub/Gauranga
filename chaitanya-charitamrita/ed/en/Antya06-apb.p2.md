## 155

**Abhyantara** — the inner quarters of the house (*andar-bāṛī*).

## 158

When Gaura's devotees travel to Nīlācala, their company is in plain view and known to everyone. Raghunātha fears that if he goes with them his father will seize him and bring him back, and so he cannot go with them.

## 185

He went by way of small, insignificant villages.

## 195

In relation to Nīlāmbara Cakravartī I regard them as *ājā*, that is, as maternal grandfathers.

## 198

A person may dress and look like a Vaiṣṇava and may serve the Deity and so on, yet that does not make him a pure Vaiṣṇava. Until he shows the marks of pure devotion — *anyābhilāṣitā-śūnyam* (free from other desires) and the rest[^198-1] — he remains *vaiṣṇava-prāya*, almost a Vaiṣṇava, even after receiving initiation (*dīkṣā*) and so on.

## 203

The three Raghunāthas are Vaidya-Raghunātha (Ādi 11.22), Bhaṭṭa-Raghunātha, and Dāsa-Raghunātha.
