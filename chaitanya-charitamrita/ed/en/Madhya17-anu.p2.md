## 127

> Subheading: Since the Māyāvādī does not believe in spiritual play, he utters the holy name with contempt; because of this offense to the name, his utterance must not be heard —

## 126–127

*Doṣa*: reviling. Names such as “Brahman,” “Caitanya,” “Ātmā,” “Paramātmā,” “Jagadīśa,” “Īśvara,” “Virāṭ,” “Vibhu,” “Bhūmā,” “Viśvarūpa” and “Vyāpaka,” as the one who utters them understands them, do not point to Kṛṣṇa's magnanimity (*audārya*) or sweetness (*mādhurya*); they point only partly to His majesty (*aiśvarya*). Therefore these names do not reveal that Kṛṣṇa is the embodiment of spiritual *rasa*, as His principal names do. This is why the Māyāvādīs, the worshipers of *prakṛti*, are great offenders. In the end they hold the Truth to be featureless, devoid of spiritual play: they disbelieve and doubt that the Truth's spiritual name, form, qualities, pastimes and distinctive associates are nondual knowledge (*advaya-jñāna*). Nor do they revere Bhagavān Kṛṣṇa's principal names with faith as the sole goal (*sādhya*) and the sole means (*sādhana*). No one who seeks the eternal, highest good should hear any talk of the highest goal from their mouths.

## 133

> Subheading: The nature of Śrī Kṛṣṇa's name — (a statement of the *Padma Purāṇa* and the *Viṣṇu-dharmottara*)

**nāma-nāminoḥ** (of the name and of the bearer of the name, Kṛṣṇa; that is, of the bearer of the name, Kṛṣṇa, together with the name) **abhinnatvāt** (because there is no difference) (Kṛṣṇa's) **nāma**—**cintāmaṇiḥ** (the giver of everything desired for service), **kṛṣṇaḥ** (directly Kṛṣṇa Himself in His original form), **caitanya-rasa-vigrahaḥ** (the embodiment of spiritual *rasa*, not the abode of the tastelessness of insentient matter, for it is beyond *māyā* and cannot mix with it), **pūrṇaḥ** (whose form cannot be fragmented by *māyā*), **śuddhaḥ** (unmixed with *māyā*, having cast *māyā* off), **nitya-muktaḥ** (ever beyond matter).
