# Anubhāṣya on Madhya-līlā 21

## 1

> Subheading: The author's *maṅgalācaraṇa* as he describes the sweetness and majesty of Gaura-Kṛṣṇa —

**agaty-eka-gatiṁ** (the sole support of those who have no refuge) **hīnārthādhika-sādhakam** (who abundantly fulfils the aims of the destitute, that is, of those poor in love for Kṛṣṇa) **śrī-caitanyaṁ** **natvā** (having bowed down) **asya** (of the Lord, Caitanyadeva) **mādhuryaiśvarya-śīkaram** (a drop of the majesty that lies within sweetness — or a drop of sweetness and majesty) **likhāmi**.

## 3

> Subheading: In Paravyoma are the countless Vaikuṇṭha abodes of all the forms of Viṣṇu —

## 4

Vaikuṇṭha has no measure, as the *māyic* universes have. Vaikuṇṭha extends a hundred, a thousand, ten thousand, a hundred thousand, ten million — that is, countless — *yojanas*. Where there is no limitation (*kuṇṭha-dharma*) tied to any kind of measure, that is “Vaikuṇṭha.”

## 5

> Subheading: In Paravyoma the container and the contained, the abode and the Lord's form, are one; the form of Bhagavān is a spiritual play of pure goodness (*śuddha-sattva*) —

## 7

> Subheading: Goloka is the whorl (*karṇikā*) of Paravyoma, which is like a thousand-petalled lotus —

## 8

> Subheading: Viṣṇu and His abode are both *adhokṣaja*, and so beyond the reach even of Brahmā and others —

The places of Vaikuṇṭha, full of the six opulences, and the limits of the *avatāras* endowed with the six opulences are beyond the reach even of Brahmā, Śiva and the other lords of the *māyic* realm. What then to speak of the *jīva*, who is subject to *māyā*!

## 9

> Subheading: The transcendental Viṣṇu is *adhokṣaja*: the mind cannot grasp Him — (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.21)[^9-1] —

Brahmā stole the calves, and as a result Śrī Kṛṣṇa crushed his pride. Then Brahmā, understanding that Śrī Kṛṣṇa is the Supreme Lord, offers Him praise in the following two verses:

**bhūman** (Virāṭ) **bhagavan**, **parātman**, **yogeśvara**, **bhavataḥ** **ūtīḥ** (pastimes) **kva** **vā**, **kathaṁ** **vā**, **kadā** **vā**, **kati** **vā** **yoga-māyāṁ** **vistārayan** **krīḍasi** — **iti** **tri-lokyāṁ** **kaḥ** **vetti**? (No one knows; and so the glory of Your Yogamāyā is inconceivable — such is the sense.)

## 11

> Subheading: Even the most skilful counter in the universe is unable to measure Viṣṇu's qualities — (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.7) —

**yaiḥ** **su-kalpaiḥ** (by the most skilful people, over many births) **vā** (supposing) **kālena** **bhū-pāṁśavaḥ** (the atoms of the earth), **khe** (in the sky) **mihikāḥ** (the snowflakes), **dyu-bhāsaḥ** (the atoms of the rays of the luminaries in the heavens) **api** **vimitāḥ** (have been counted one by one) — of them **ke** (which people) **asya** (of the universe) **hitāvatīrṇasya** (who appears for its good, or who has descended to maintain it by revealing many qualities) **guṇātmanaḥ** (the master of the three *guṇas*) **te** (Your) **guṇān** **api** (in turn) **vimātum** (to count — “there are so many”) **īśire** (were able? — to say nothing of telling of each one in particular: such is the sense). See Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.7.40 and 11.4.2.

## 12

> Subheading: Even Śeṣa Himself, singing Kṛṣṇa's qualities, finds no end to them —

What to speak of four-faced Brahmā or five-faced Śiva! Ananta-deva sings ceaselessly with a thousand mouths, yet even He does not reach the limit of Kṛṣṇa's qualities. There is another reading: “What to speak of Brahmā and the rest — the thousand-mouthed Ananta sings ceaselessly with His mouths, yet cannot count them.”

## 13

> Subheading: The evidence of scripture — (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 2.7.41) —

Brahmā tells his disciple Nārada of the deeds, purposes and opulences of Viṣṇu's *līlā-avatāras*, and then speaks of His unknowable, immeasurable glory of energies:

**puruṣasya** (of Bhagavān) **viṣṇoḥ** **māyā-balasya** (of the glory of His *māyā*) **antam** **aham** (I, Brahmā) **na** **vidāmi** (do not know; likewise) **te** (your) **agra-jāḥ** (elder brothers) **amī** **munayaḥ** (Sanaka and the others) do not know; **daśa-śatānanaḥ** (thousand-mouthed) **ādi-devaḥ** **śeṣaḥ** (Ananta, who holds up the earth) **api** **asya** (of Bhagavān) **guṇān** **gāyan** (glorifying) **adhunā** (now) **api** **pāram** (the limit) **na** **samavasyati** (does not ascertain, that is, does not reach; therefore) **ye** **apare**[^13-1] (other people — they) **kutaḥ**? (how could they know? — such is the sense).

## 14

> Subheading: Even for Kṛṣṇa Himself Kṛṣṇa's qualities are immeasurable —

*Teṅho rahu*: what to speak of Ananta-deva! Śrī Kṛṣṇa Himself does not reach the limit of His own qualities, and so He is full of longing.

## 15

> Subheading: Having rejected “not this” and described the featureless, the *śrutis* culminate in describing the form full of attributes — (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.87.41) —

In Janaloka, at the Brahma-satra sacrifice, before sages eager to hear, the *brahmarṣi* Sanandana, one of the four Kumāras, recited this praise of Bhagavān offered by the *śrutis*. Later the original sage Nārāyaṇa recounted the same praise to the *devarṣi* Nārada.

**bhagavan**, **dyu-patayaḥ** (the lords of heaven, the lords of the worlds — Brahmā and others) **eva** (even) **te** (Your) **anantatayā** (because You are without end) **antam** (the limit of Your qualities) **na** **yayuḥ** (did not reach; that is, whatever has an end is not You); (what to speak of the lords of heaven,) **yat** (since) **tvam** **api** (You Yourself do not reach Your own limit, being endless); **nanu** (oh!) **yat** (of You) **antarā** (within) **sāvaraṇāḥ** (in seven coverings, each ten times the one before) **aṇḍa-nicayāḥ** (the multitudes of universes) **vayasā** (by the wheel of time) **khe** (in the sky) **rajāṁsi** **iva** **saha** (all at once, not in turn) **vānti** (revolve); **yat** (because) **śrutayaḥ** **atan-nirasanena** (by ceaselessly rejecting matter) **bhavan-nidhanāḥ** (those whose end, whose conclusion, is in You) **tvayi** (in You, who are full of distinct spiritual pastimes) **hi** **phalanti** (culminate).

## 16

> Subheading: The description of Kṛṣṇa's wondrous pastime of herding the cows in Vraja —

## 17

> Subheading: Because of the theft of the calves, Kṛṣṇa manifests a spiritual pastime and destroys Brahmā's pride —

In a single moment Kṛṣṇa created countless transcendental Vaikuṇṭhas with the lords of Paravyoma, and countless material universes with many Brahmās and others.

## 18

> Subheading: The supreme wonder of that pastime —

*Avadhūta*: shaken, stirred, overflowing, overwhelmed, overcome.

[^9-1]: The edition apparently reads “10th canto, ch. 14, verse 29” (the scan is unclear); the verse is Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.14.21.

[^13-1]: The edition reads the verse as *kuto ’pare ye* (checked against the scan), and the *anvaya* follows this reading; our text has *kuto ’varā ye*.
