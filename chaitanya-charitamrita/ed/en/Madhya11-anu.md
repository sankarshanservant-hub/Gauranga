# Anubhāṣya on Madhya-līlā 11

## 1

> Subheading: Dancing, Gaura plunges the world into the flood of *prema* —

**nānā-bhāvālaṅkṛtāṅgaḥ** (whose body is adorned with the ornaments of many *bhāvas*) **gauracandraḥ** **śrī-jagannātha-gehe** (in the temple of Śrī Jagannāthadeva) **bhaktaiḥ** (with the devotees) **sva-dhāmnā** (by His own sweetness, not of this world) **aty-uddaṇḍaṁ tāṇḍavaṁ** (an exceedingly charming dance and so on) **kurvan** **viśvaṁ** (the world, devoid of spiritual *rasa* and given to material *rasa*) **prema-vanyā-nimagnaṁ cakre** (flooded with waves of love for Kṛṣṇa).

## 8

> Subheading: For one who seeks to rise to *prema*, to look on objects of enjoyment as an enjoyer is strictly forbidden — (the Lord's words to Sārvabhauma in the *Caitanya-candrodaya*, Act 8, verse 24) —

**hā hanta hanta** (an exclamation of extreme grief) **bhava-sāgarasya** (of the ocean of *saṁsāra*) **paraṁ pāraṁ** (to the far shore: to Paravyoma, the abode of the Lord beyond Devī-dhāma) **jigamiṣoḥ** (of one who wishes to go) **niṣkiñcanasya** (free of worldly attachments) **bhagavad-bhajanonmukhasya** (devoted to Kṛṣṇa's service) **viṣayiṇām** (of those given to enjoying things other than Kṛṣṇa) **atha yoṣitāṁ** (and of the objects of enjoyment) **sandarśanaṁ** (looking at them as objects of enjoyment, and so on)[^8-1] **viṣa-bhakṣaṇataḥ** (than consuming poison that destroys oneself) **api** **asādhu** (is more harmful).

## 9

## 10

> Subheading: To see and associate with a king, the enjoyer of gold and prestige, or with a woman, through the eyes of enjoyment, brings death: turning away from the Lord —

## 11

> Subheading: Fear, born of absorption in a second thing, arises simply from seeing things externally, as their enjoyer, taking them to be objects of enjoyment — (*Caitanya-candrodaya*, Act 8, verse 25) —

**strīṇāṁ** (of women) **viṣayiṇām** (of those who serve their senses), (that is, of enjoyers and objects of enjoyment) **api** **ākārāt** (even of their outward form [by *sādhakas* who serve Kṛṣṇa alone and are intent on the highest goal]) **api** **bhetavyam** (one should be afraid). **yathā** **aheḥ** (of a snake) **manasaḥ** **kṣobhaḥ** (fear) (arises), **tathā** **tasya** (of the snake) **ākṛteḥ** (of a form like it) **api** (fear arises).
