## 144

> Subheading: Sanātana agrees. He knows that Śrī Vṛndāvana-dhāma is Kṛṣṇa's transcendental abode, yet he sees it above all as “the land the Lord has chosen” — such is Sanātana's incomparable love for Gaura —

**“Prabhu-datta deśa”** (“the land given by the Lord”). The meaning is this: the place that Śrī Hari, guru and the Vaiṣṇavas — whom the *jīva* eternally worships — have chosen and appointed is itself Śrī Vṛndāvana, the abode of service to Kṛṣṇa that the *jīva* eternally longs for. By living there and giving them pleasure, the *jīva* attains his eternal good.

## 163

> Subheading: Sanātana compares the Lord's affection for himself with His affection for the Paṇḍita —

The juice of *nimba* and *niśindā* is bitter, so it is unpleasant to taste. Just as unpleasant it is for one who is lovingly cared for (*lālya*) — one who deserves affection and mercy — to receive respect, obeisances and other honors from the one who cares for him (*lālaka*), whom he himself should serve and worship.

## 166

> Subheading: The Lord describes how His affection for Jagadānanda differs from His affection for Sanātana, and explains why He rebuked Jagadānanda —

Each person has his proper place (*maryādā*). Mahāprabhu did not encourage anyone to overstep it, think himself important and give advice to one who deserves respect. Still less did He approve of such conduct in one younger in years, like Jagadānanda.
