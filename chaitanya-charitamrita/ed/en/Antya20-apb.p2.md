## 90

*Rāṅgā-ṭuni*: a tiny *ṭunṭunī* bird.[^90-1]

## 92

I am as powerless as a wooden doll, and to suppose that I have written this book is vain. The meaning is this: it is Bhagavān and the devotees who are making me write this book.

## 154

Whoever relishes with faith this nectar-like account of the life of Bhagavān Śrī Caitanyadeva, which bestows good fortune and destroys all that is inauspicious — at his pure lotus feet this author becomes a bee and relishes in the highest degree this *rasa*, full of the intoxicating honey of *prema*.[^154-1]

## 155

May this book, the *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, be offered to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva for the pleasure of Śrīman Madana-Gopāla and Śrī Govinda-deva.

## 156

Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet fill the world with fragrance, and with their own *rasa* they madden the bees who know *rasa*. What *rasika* would try to give them up?[^156-1]

## 157

By the power of the dust of the lotus feet of her who is all the treasure of my life, and meditating on the dust of the lotus feet that is dearer than life and all in all to my Mistress Śrīmatī Rādhikā, I surrender to Śrī Śrī Govinda-deva.

## 158

In the Śaka year 1537, in the month of Jyaiṣṭha, on a Sunday, on *kṛṣṇa-pañcamī* (the fifth day of the dark fortnight), in Vṛndāvana, this book was completed.

*Thus ends the twentieth chapter of the “Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya.”*

Hari who sports in the forests, Vipina-vihārī,
sent down His potency: [there appeared] the excellent master Vipina-vihārī.
In the form of Śrī Guru Gosvāmī he saw me in the well of worldly existence
and delivered his servant.

Wishing to carry out his order, I carefully composed,
under the name “Amṛta-pravāha,”
an explanation of the “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”
and offered it to the devotees: read it and be rid of *anarthas*!

May all my sons who labored
to prepare this book
live free of obstacles, and in the festival of the company of *sādhus*
serve with devotion the feet of Śrī Hari.

Holding the feet of the Vaiṣṇavas, I humbly pray:
may the rest of this servant's life
pass in Śrī Godruma, in the company of *sādhus*, in the joy of spiritual bliss,
absorbed in the name of Kṛṣṇa.

This world has no substance; fools sink in it,
but the great soul is indifferent to it:
in the company of *sādhus* he worships Kṛṣṇa, serves Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vraja,
and ever takes shelter of the name of Kṛṣṇa.

In the four hundred tenth year of the Gaura era, in the month of Meṣa, on the Ekādaśī of the bright fortnight,[^158-1]
in the grove of Śrī Surabhi-kuñja,
the commentary was completed, and so my service was fulfilled.
Now I beg forgiveness for my faults.

*The “Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya” on the book “Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta” is complete.*

[^90-1]: *Ṭunṭunī* is the Bengali name of the tiny tailorbird. The verse translation has “a little red-beaked bird.”

[^154-1]: The verse translation reads differently: “Anyone who, like a honeybee, is intoxicated by the nectar of the spotless lotus feet of this Supreme Lord will, by his faith and devotion, be able to taste transcendental relationships with the Supreme…”

[^156-1]: The verse translation reads differently: “Those who sense the fragrance of Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet spreading throughout the world, who are intoxicated by the nectar of their own relationship with Him, who have found their full shelter in these relationships — how could such saintly *rasika* Vaiṣṇavas give all this up?” (the verse text in VCD has a different reading; see the *Anubhāṣya* on 156).

[^158-1]: The 410th year of the Gaura era (Gaurābda) is 1896 CE; Meṣa is a solar month (April–May).
