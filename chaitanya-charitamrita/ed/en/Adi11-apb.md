# Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya on Ādi-līlā 11

## 0

**Summary.** The eleventh chapter describes the associates of Nityānanda Prabhu.

## 1

I bow down to all the bees at Nityānanda's lotus feet, who are maddened by drinking the honey of love, and I will name a few of the chief devotees among them.

## 4

Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the wish-fulfilling tree of *prema*, and Śrī Avadhūta Nityānanda-candra is its high trunk. I bow down to His associates, the branches of that trunk.

## 6

“Of the gardener”: of Śrī Mahāprabhu.

## 9

Vīracandra Prabhu is by nature Payobdhiśāyī, the *vyūha* of Śrī Saṅkarṣaṇa, and so He is the Lord Himself. Yet He thought of Himself as a Vaiṣṇava.

## 13

Rāmadāsa is Abhirāma dāsa.

## 14–15

These two are like Nityānanda's own associates. When Mahāprabhu ordered Nityānanda Prabhu to go to Gauḍa, He sent Rāmadāsa and Gadādhara dāsa with Him. So these two were counted once among Mahāprabhu's associates, and now they are counted among Nityānanda's as well. Mādhava and Vāsu Ghoṣa are likewise counted in both groups.

## 20

Murāri-Caitanyadāsa was born in the village of Sar-Vṛndāvanapura, one *krośa* from Galaśī station in Barddhamāna district. When he came to the village of Modadruma, or Māugāchi, in Navadvīpa-dhāma, he came to be called “Śārṅga” (Sāraṅga) Murāri-Caitanyadāsa. His descendants still live at the *pāṭa* of Sar.

## 24

The descendants of Kamalākara Pippalāi are servants of Śrī Jagannāthadeva at Māheśa.

## 27

“*Pā̃ti*”: eating together in one row (*paṅkti-bhojana*).

*Thus ends the eleventh chapter of the “Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya.”*
