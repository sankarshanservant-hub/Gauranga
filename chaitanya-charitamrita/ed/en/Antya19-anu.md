## 20

> Subheading: A hint that the purpose of Mahāprabhu's descent has been fulfilled and that it is time to wind up His pastimes —

A variant reading: “Tell the madman (*bāul*): people have become *āul*.” The word *āul* means *ātura*, overwhelmed [with feeling] — that is, full of love. Some also take it to mean “slack” or “incoherent.” The word *āul* also means *niṣkiñcana* (one who owns nothing), *ārta* (afflicted), *ātura* (distressed), and so on.

## 21

“**kāye nāhika āula**” (there is no more *āul* in the work) — some explain it this way: there is no longer any disorder in the work of preaching love[^21-1].

## 35

> Subheading: Śrī Rādhā, in separation from Kṛṣṇa, asks where Kṛṣṇa may be found (*citra-jalpa*) —

> (*Lalita-mādhava* 3.25; Śrī Rādhā's words to Viśākhā)

**sakhi** (O friend [Viśākhā]), **nanda-kula-candramāḥ** (Nanda — “he who gladdens”; he is like the ocean of milk, and the moon born in his line is the moon of Nanda's dynasty) **kva** (where [is He])? **śikhi-candrakālaṅkṛtiḥ** (He whose ornament is the peacock feather) **kva** (where does He stay)? **mandra-muralī-ravaḥ** (He whose flute sounds deep) **kva** (where is He)? **surendra-nīla-dyutiḥ** (He whose luster is blue like the *surendra* gem, that is, the sapphire [*indranīla*]) **kva**? **rāsa-rasa-tāṇḍavī** (He who in the *rāsa* sport dances with rapture [*tāṇḍava*, dance]) **kva**? **jīva-rakṣauṣadhiḥ** (He who is like a medicine that guards and saves life) **kva**? **mama** **suhṛt-tamaḥ** (dearest) **nidhiḥ** (the source of all riches) **kva**? **bata** (expresses grief) **hā hanta**, **vidhim** (on the Creator) **dhik**.

## 38

> Subheading: Kṛṣṇacandra, the treasure of the *gopīs'* lives —

The *gopīs'* loving desire (*kāma*) is like the sun (*arka*), and the *gopīs'* hearts are like night-blooming lotuses (*kumudinī*): the *gopīs'* hearts, burned by their desire [to meet] Kṛṣṇa, are like lotuses scorched by the sun's rays. The word *nija* points to Kṛṣṇa's own *kara*, that is, His rays or His hands: Kṛṣṇacandra (Kṛṣṇa, who is like the moon) gives those nectar-like rays — or hands.

## 45

> Subheading: Reproaching the Creator, who brings about separation from Kṛṣṇa —

> (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 10.39.19)

The *gopīs* of Vraja, whose lives were given to Kṛṣṇa and who were Kṛṣṇa's beloveds, heard that Śrī Akrūra had come to Vraja only to take Rāma and Kṛṣṇa to Mathurā. Then, fearing the coming separation from Kṛṣṇa, they weep and lament in great grief:

**aho** (expresses grief) **vidhātaḥ**, **tava** **kvacit** **dayā** **na** (**asti**, there is not, **yataḥ**, because) **maitryā** (by friendship — by acting for their welfare) **praṇayena** (by affection — by tender love) **dehinaḥ** (embodied *jīvas*, one with another) **saṁyojya** **akṛtārthān** (even though they have not yet tasted the happiness [of union]) **tān** **ca** **viyunaṅkṣi** (you separate); **te** (your) **viceṣṭitaṁ** (deeds) **arbhaka-ceṣṭitaṁ** (what a child does out of foolishness) **yathā** (as, [so]) **apārthakaṁ** (are without reason).
