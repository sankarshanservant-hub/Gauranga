## 201

> Subheading: In an assembly of brāhmaṇas the Lord hears a reading of the *Kūrma Purāṇa* —

***Kūrma Purāṇa.*** The present-day *Kūrma Purāṇa* contains only two parts, the Pūrva-khaṇḍa and the Uttara-khaṇḍa. In fact the *Kūrma Purāṇa* does not have six thousand verses: it had seventeen thousand. “That auspicious [Purāṇa] of seventeen thousand [verses] consists of four *saṁhitās*; its seventeen thousand [verses] are connected with the Lakṣmī-kalpa.” (According to the *Bhāgavata*.) It is the fifteenth of the eighteen *mahā-purāṇas*.

## 218

> Subheading: A night in the brāhmaṇa's house and bathing in the Tāmraparṇī —

**Pāṇḍya-deśa**[^218-1] is the region of the Dākṣiṇātya (the South) between the kingdoms of Kerala and Cola. Here many kings bearing the title “Pāṇḍya” ruled in Madurai and Rāmeśvara. The *Rāmāyaṇa* says: “You will cross the great river Tāmraparṇī, where crocodiles dwell, whose islands and waters are hidden by wondrous sandal forests. Going there, O monkeys, you will see the strong gate of the Pāṇḍyas.”[^218-2]

**Tāmraparṇī** is the river on whose left bank Tinnevelly stands.[^218-3] It is called “Porunai.” It rises in the Western Ghats and falls into the Bay of Bengal. “Where the rivers Tāmraparṇī, Kṛtamālā, Payasvinī …” (*Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 11.5.39)

## 219

> Subheading: *Darśana* of Nava Tirupati —

**Naya Tripati** is Ālvār Tirunagarī. This town lies 17 miles southeast of Tinnevelly. Around it stand nine temples of Śrīpati, that is, of Viṣṇu. At festivals all nine Deities gather in this town.

## 220

> Subheading: *Darśana* of Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa at Ciyaḍatalā and of Śiva at Tilakāñcī —

**Ciyaḍatalā**, according to some, is “Cheratalā” near Nagercoil; it is a temple of Śrī Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa.

**Tilakāñcī** is a temple of Śiva. Probably Tenkasi, 30 miles northeast of the town of Tinnevelly, is meant.

## 221

> Subheading: *Darśana* of Viṣṇu at Gajendra-mokṣaṇa and of Rāma at Pānāgaḍi —

**Gajendra-mokṣaṇa.** Some mistakenly give this name to “Sthāṇu-liṅga,” or “Devendra-mokṣaṇa-Śiva,” two miles south of Nagercoil. In truth this is a Deity of Śrī Viṣṇu.
