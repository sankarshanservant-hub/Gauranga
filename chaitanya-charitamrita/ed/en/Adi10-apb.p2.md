## 89

Through contact with the *yavanas*, the people of the western lands had partly lost the sense of their duty, and compared with the good customs of Bengal they lived largely without proper conduct. At that time, under the influence of the Muslims, they had strayed still further from proper conduct. By the mercy of Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana they became inclined to good conduct (*sadācāra*).

## 90

The lost holy places are Śrī Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa and others. The Deities: they spread the worship of seven Deities — Śrī Madana-mohana, Śrī Govinda, Śrī Gopīnātha and others.

## 92–93

“Hidden service”: those services to which outsiders have no access. The intimate services dear to the Lord, needed when He bathes, eats, rests, sings *kīrtana* and so on, are here also called *antaraṅga-sevā*, intimate service.

## 94

“Throwing myself down”: falling from a high mountain peak.

## 100

“Day and night he served Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa within his mind”: together with chanting (*kīrtana*) the holy name, he meditated on the service of the eight periods of the day (*aṣṭa-kālīya sevā*).

## 104

“Later”: for the Lord's meeting with Raghunātha see Antya, Chapter 6.

## 107

“Śrīnātha Paṇḍita”: a resident of Kāṁcaḍāpāḍā.

## 108

“He lived by the Gaṅgā”: he founded a village named “Gaṅgāvāsa” on the bank of the Alakānandā within Śrī Navadvīpa.

## 113

“Bhāgavatācārya” lived in Varāhanagara. Even now his *āśrama* is called “the *pāṭa* of Bhāgavatācārya.” “Ṭhākura Sāraṅga dāsa” lived in Māmagāchi.

## 114

“The brāhmaṇa Vāṇīnātha” lived in Campāhāṭī.

## 115

“Govinda” installed the Deity of Gopīnātha at Agradvīpa.

## 116

“Abhirāma” lived in Khānākula-Kṛṣṇanagara.
