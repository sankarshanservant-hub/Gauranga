## 0
tr: The Meeting with Vallabha Bhaṭṭa

## 1
w: of Caitanya  of the lotus feet
w: the honey  who lick  I worship
w: whose  by the mercy alone
w: a wretch  even  immortal  becomes
tr: I worship those who sip the honey of Śrī Caitanya’s lotus feet: by their mercy alone even a wretched soul becomes immortal.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *pāmara* — a godless person, bereft of bhakti; *amara* — one who possesses a transcendental body. Heading in the edition: obeisance to the devotees of Gaura, the touchstone.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Ādi 4.2 (= Ādi 1.18, 17.2, Antya 2.2, 4.2, 6.2); translation as there.

## 3
w: in the next year  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees  came
w: as before  Mahāprabhu  all  met
tr: The next year all the devotees of Gauḍa came, and Mahāprabhu met them all as before.

## 4
w: in this way  pastimes  of the Lord  the devotees  with
w: at that time  Vallabha Bhaṭṭa  met (Him)  coming
tr: While the Lord was thus enjoying His pastimes with the devotees, Vallabha Bhaṭṭa came to meet Him.

## 5
w: coming  bowed  the Bhaṭṭa  of the Lord  at the feet
w: the Lord  ‘regarding him as a bhāgavata’  gave  an embrace
tr: Coming before the Lord, the Bhaṭṭa bowed at His feet, and the Lord embraced him, regarding him as a *bhāgavata*, a devotee.

## 6
w: honour  showing  the Lord  him  near  seated
w: humility  showing  the Bhaṭṭa  to speak  began
tr: Showing him honour, the Lord seated him near Himself, and the Bhaṭṭa humbly began to speak.

## 7
w: for many days  the desire  You  to see
w: Jagannātha  fulfilled  I have seen  You
tr: “For many days I have longed to see You. Jagannātha has fulfilled my desire: I have seen You.

## 8
w: Your  sight  who  obtains  he  fortunate
w: You  I see  as if  directly  Bhagavān
tr: Whoever obtains Your sight is fortunate. When I see You, it is as if I see Bhagavān Himself.

## 9
w: You  who  remembers  he  becomes  pure
w: by the sight  pure  will become  in this  what  wonder
tr: Whoever remembers You becomes pure. What wonder, then, that one becomes pure by seeing You?

## 10
w: of whom  by the remembrance  of men
w: at once  become pure  indeed  the homes
w: how much more  by seeing  touching
w: washing the feet  offering a seat and so on
tr: ‘When people merely remember such saints, their very homes become pure at once. How much more, then, when they see them and touch them, wash their feet and offer them a seat!’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.19.33)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Parīkṣit, resolved to fast until death, asked the assembled sages what the one duty of a dying man is; when Śrī Śukadeva arrived, Parīkṣit greeted him humbly with these words. “Homes” — even abodes of worldly enjoyment.

## 11
w: of the age of Kali  the dharma  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the saṅkīrtana
w: Kṛṣṇa’s power  without  is not  its  propagation
tr: The dharma of the age of Kali is the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s name. Without Kṛṣṇa’s power no one can propagate it.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: without Kṛṣṇa’s direct will — the power of His mercy — no one can become an ācārya, the guru of the world, and establish the chanting of the pure name; the ācārya wholly devoted to chanting the name is a direct embodiment of Kṛṣṇa’s potency.

## 12
w: that  have propagated  You  this  indeed  ‘the proof’
w: Kṛṣṇa’s power  bear  You  in this  there is not  otherwise
tr: You have propagated it — that is the proof. You bear Kṛṣṇa’s power; of this there is no doubt.

## 13
w: in the world  have made  You  Kṛṣṇa’s name  manifest
w: whoever  You  sees  he  in love for Kṛṣṇa  floats
tr: You have made Kṛṣṇa’s name manifest in the world. Whoever sees You floats in love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 14
w: of prema  the manifestation  is not  Kṛṣṇa’s power  without
w: ‘Kṛṣṇa’  the one  giver of prema  by the evidence of scripture
tr: Without Kṛṣṇa’s power prema is not manifested. Kṛṣṇa alone is the giver of prema — so the scriptures testify.

## 15
w: let there be  avatāras  many
w: of the lotus-naveled One  in every way  auspicious
w: than Kṛṣṇa  other  who  indeed  to the creepers
w: even  love  the giver  becomes
tr: ‘Let there be many all-auspicious avatāras of the Lord whose navel is a lotus; but who other than Kṛṣṇa gives prema even to the creepers?’” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.5.37)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 3.27; translation, transliteration and word-for-word as there (the transliteration lines follow the quarters of the verse). Heading in the edition: *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, verse 303; the words of Bilvamaṅgala. *Anubhāṣya* and *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (on Ādi 3.27): the “creepers” (*latā*) are those who have taken shelter of Him.

## 16
w: Mahāprabhu  says  listen  Bhaṭṭa  great-minded
w: a Māyāvādī  sannyāsī  I  not  know  devotion to Kṛṣṇa
tr: Mahāprabhu said, “Listen, wise Bhaṭṭa. I am a Māyāvādī sannyāsī; I do not know devotion to Kṛṣṇa.
fn: Heading in the edition: the humility of Bhagavān, and His pretence.

## 17
w: Advaitācārya Gosāñi  ‘Īśvara Himself’
w: His  in the company  My  mind  became  pure
tr: Advaitācārya Gosāñi is Īśvara Himself, and in His company My mind became pure.

## 18
w: in all the scriptures  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  there is not  whose  equal
w: therefore  ‘Advaita Ācārya’  His  name
tr: In all the scriptures and in devotion to Kṛṣṇa He has no equal; therefore His name is Advaita Ācārya, the peerless teacher.

## 19
w: by whose  mercy  of mlecchas  arises  devotion to Kṛṣṇa
w: who  to tell  can  His  of Vaiṣṇava nature the power
tr: By His mercy even mlecchas gain devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Who can describe the power of His Vaiṣṇava nature?

## 20
w: Nityānanda Avadhūta  ‘Īśvara Himself’
w: by the madness of bhāva  intoxicated  of love for Kṛṣṇa  the ocean
tr: Nityānanda Avadhūta is Īśvara Himself. Intoxicated with the madness of ecstatic emotion (*bhāva*), He is an ocean of love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 21
w: of the six darśanas  the knower  Bhaṭṭācārya Sārvabhauma
w: in the six darśanas  the world’s guru  the best of bhāgavatas
tr: Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya knows the six systems of philosophy (*darśanas*). In these six systems he is the guru of the world, and he is the best of bhāgavatas.

## 22
w: he  showed  Me  of bhakti-yoga  the farthest limit
w: by his  grace  I came to know  ‘Kṛṣṇa-bhakti-yoga’  the essence
tr: He showed Me the farthest limit of bhakti-yoga; by his grace I came to know that the yoga of devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the essence.

## 23
w: Rāmānanda Rāya  of the rasa of Kṛṣṇa  ‘the treasury’
w: he  made known  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān Himself
tr: Rāmānanda Rāya is a treasury of the rasa of Kṛṣṇa. He made Me understand that Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself.

## 24
w: through that  prema-bhakti  ‘the crown jewel of the goals of life’
w: on the path of rāga  prema-bhakti  ‘the highest of all’  I know
tr: Through him I came to know that prema-bhakti is the crown jewel of the goals of life (*puruṣārthas*), and that prema-bhakti on the path of spontaneous attraction (*rāga*) is the highest of all.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: prema-bhakti is higher than the four goals of life (dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa) — it is the crown jewel of the puruṣārthas; service on the path of rāgānugā is higher than the worship of Kṛṣṇa on the path of rules (*vidhi*).

## 25
w: servitude,  friendship,  parental love,  and  which  the love of the beloved
w: servant,  friend,  elder,  beloved  ‘the shelter’  of which
tr: Servitude, friendship, parental love and the love of the beloved (*śṛṅgāra*) — of these the servant, the friend, the elder and the beloved are the shelter (*āśraya*).

## 26
w: ‘joined with knowledge of majesty’  ‘pure’ bhāva  and
w: by knowledge of majesty  not  one attains  the son of the king of Vraja
tr: There is bhāva joined with the knowledge of majesty, and there is pure (*kevala*) bhāva. By the knowledge of majesty one does not attain the son of the king of Vraja.
fn: Heading in the edition: in the madhura-rasa there are two dispositions — in the cities, “mixed with majesty”; in Vraja, *kevala* (pure); the son of Yaśodā is attained only by the pure disposition of *parakīyā* proper to Vraja, not by the disposition of *svakīyā* mixed with majesty.

## 27
w: not  this  easily attained  Bhagavān
w: for the embodied  of the gopī  the son
w: for the jñānīs  and  for those who realised the self
w: as  for those who have devotion  here
tr: ‘This Bhagavān, the son of the gopī, is not so easily attained by the embodied, nor by the jñānīs, nor by those who have realized the self, as He is by those who have devotion.’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.9.21)
fn: The same verse as Madhya 8.226 (= 9.132, 24.82); translation as there. In this chapter the word *ātma-bhūtānām* is taken to mean “the associates” (v. 28): *Anubhāṣya* on 28 — Lakṣmī and the associates called *ātma-bhūta* are potencies non-different from Kṛṣṇa, yet Lakṣmī is full of the mood of majesty. In the edition the reference is “canto 10, chapter 9, verse 31”; in the standard numbering, 10.9.21.

## 28
w: ‘ātma-bhūta’  by the word  means  ‘the associates’
w: in knowledge of majesty  Lakṣmī  not  attained  the son of the king of Vraja
tr: The word *ātma-bhūta* means His associates. Because of her knowledge of His majesty, Lakṣmī did not attain the son of the king of Vraja.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Lakṣmīdevī and the associates called *ātma-bhūta* are potencies non-different from Kṛṣṇa; even so, Lakṣmī is full of the mood of majesty and therefore did not gain the right to serve Vrajendra-nandana in the madhura-rasa. Heading in the edition: the meaning of a word of the verse; Lakṣmī has no right to take part in the rāsa dance.

## 29
w: not  this  of Śrī  on the chest  indeed  wholly attached  favour
w: of the celestial women  of lotuses  the fragrance  having the lustre  how then  others
w: at the festival of rāsa  His  by the arms  embraced  around the neck
w: who obtained their desire  which  arose  of Vraja  the beautiful women
tr: ‘At the festival of the rāsa dance He embraced the beautiful women of Vraja around their necks with His arms, and they attained their heart’s desire. Such favour was not granted even to Śrī, who is wholly devoted to Him, nor to the celestial women, fragrant and radiant like lotuses — what then of others?’ (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.47.60)
fn: The same verse as Madhya 8.80 (= 8.231, 9.121); translation as there.

## 30
w: in pure bhāva  the friend  does  on the shoulders  climbing
w: in pure bhāva  the queen of Vraja  does  binding
tr: In pure bhāva His friend climbs on His shoulders, and in pure bhāva the queen of Vraja binds Him.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: “in pure bhāva” — not enchanted or bound by the knowledge of majesty, but ruled by pure, unmixed (*kevala*) *rati*. Heading in the edition: the friendship (*sakhya*) and parental love (*vātsalya*) of the people of Vraja are *kevala*, that is, pure *rāgātmikā* bhakti.

## 31
w: ‘my  friend’,  ‘my  son’  this  ‘pure’  mind
w: therefore  Śuka and Vyāsa  do  praise
tr: ‘My friend,’ ‘my son’ — such is the pure mind. Therefore Śuka and Vyāsa praise it.

## 32

fn: The same verse as Madhya 8.75; translation as there. In the edition the reference is “canto 10, chapter 12, verse 21”; in the standard numbering, 10.12.11.

fn: The same verse as Madhya 8.77; translation as there.

tr: Gadādhara Paṇḍita’s bhāva was pure and deep, with a compliant nature (*dakṣiṇa*) like that of Rukmiṇī-devī.
fn: Heading in the edition: Gadādhara is the avatāra of Rukmiṇī; his compliant nature (*dakṣiṇa*) and pure, deep love for Gaura. *Anubhāṣya* (on 140–141): once in Dvārakā Kṛṣṇa jokingly advised Rukmiṇī to choose another husband; she was frightened and, by her compliant nature, fell at His feet. So too in Gaura’s pastimes: Jagadānanda, like Satyabhāmā, is contrary and quarrels out of love, while Gadādhara, like Rukmiṇī, is timid and always follows the Lord in everything.

## 141
w: his  loving  anger  to see  the Lord’s  desire  is
w: by the knowledge of majesty  his  anger  not  arises
tr: The Lord wanted to see his loving anger (*praṇaya-roṣa*), but because Gadādhara was conscious of the Lord’s majesty, anger never arose in him.

## 142
w: this  pretext  getting  the Lord  made  a semblance of anger
w: hearing  in the Paṇḍita’s  heart  arose  fear
tr: Taking this occasion, the Lord feigned anger, and when the Paṇḍita heard of it, fear arose in his heart.
fn: Heading in the edition: on the pretext of goodwill toward Vallabha, the Lord outwardly shows feigned anger and so tests Gadādhara’s love; Gadādhara is frightened.

## 143

w: hearing  in Rukmiṇī’s  mind  fear  arose
tr: So it was long ago: when Kṛṣṇa made fun of Rukmiṇī, fear arose in her mind as she heard Him.

## 144

## 145

## 162

w: firm  of love  the mark  to the world  He made  proclamation

## 163
w: of pride  the mire  washing  the Bhaṭṭa  He purified
w: by that  others  all  people  He taught
tr: Washing away the mire of pride, He purified the Bhaṭṭa, and by this He taught everyone else as well.

## 164
w: within  ‘grace’,  outwardly  ‘like disregard’
w: the outer meaning  whoever  takes,  he  ruin  goes to
tr: Within is grace; outwardly it looks like disregard. Whoever takes only the outer meaning is ruined.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: wishing Vallabha Bhaṭṭa well, the Lord outwardly disregarded him and so cleansed him of the conceit of a scholar and a Vaiṣṇava; and since Gadādhara Paṇḍita had not rejected Vallabha altogether, for some days the Lord showed disregard to him as well. Yet Mahāprabhu was never displeased with Gadādhara, the embodiment of His own *svarūpa-śakti*, nor can He be; whoever fails to grasp the hidden meaning of this pastime and loses faith in Śrī Gadādhara’s feet will surely fall.
