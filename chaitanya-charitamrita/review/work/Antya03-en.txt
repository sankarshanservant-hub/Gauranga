## 243
w: in this way  three days  makes  coming
w: various moods  shows  by which  of Brahmā  is captivated  the mind
tr: In this way she came for three days, displaying moods that could captivate even Brahmā’s mind.

## 244
w: in Kṛṣṇa  with mind absorbed in the name  always  Haridāsa
w: in the forest  a cry  became  the display of feminine charms
tr: Haridāsa’s mind was always absorbed in the name of Kṛṣṇa, so her display of feminine charms was like crying in the wilderness.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: while he chanted, Haridāsa’s mind was always absorbed in Kṛṣṇa’s name, and so Māyā-devī’s charms, which allure men and bewitch conditioned souls, proved as fruitless as weeping in an empty forest.

## 245

tr: When the third night was coming to an end, the woman said to the Ṭhākura:

## 246
w: three  days  you have cheated  me  making  assurances
w: night and day  is not  your  completion of the name
tr: “For three days you have deceived me with promises. Night and day, your chanting of the name never ends.”

## 247

tr: Haridāsa Ṭhākura said, “What can I do? I have made a vow — how can I give it up?”

## 249

## 250–251

tr: You are a great devotee (*mahā-bhāgavata*). By seeing you and hearing you chant Kṛṣṇa’s name, my heart has become pure and longs to take Kṛṣṇa’s name. Be merciful to me: instruct me in Kṛṣṇa’s name.

## 252
w: in Caitanya’s descent  flows  a flood of the nectar of love
w: all  jīvas  in love  float  the earth  became  blessed
tr: With Caitanya’s descent a flood of the nectar of love is flowing. All living beings float in that love, and the earth has become blessed.

## 253
w: in this flood  who  not  floats  that  soul  —  wretched
w: in millions of kalpas  then  his  there is no  deliverance
tr: Wretched is the soul who does not float in this flood: for him there is no deliverance even in millions of *kalpas*.
fn: The edition’s heading to 252: without cultivating devotion to Kṛṣṇa under the shelter of Caitanya, a soul is like dead matter. *Anubhāṣya*: a *mahā-yuga* is 4,320,000 solar years, a thousand *mahā-yugas* make a *kalpa*, and *koṭi-kalpa* is ten million times as long.

## 254

tr: Long ago I received the name of Rāma from Śiva; now, by your company, I long to take the name of Kṛṣṇa.

## 255

## 257

## 258

## 263

w: descending  does  of love and the name  the relishing

fn: The edition reads *prema-nāma āsvādana*, “relishes love and the name” (another reading: *prema-rasa*, “the taste of love”); cf. 262. The edition’s heading: Kṛṣṇa Himself relishes love for His own sweetness.

## 264

fn: The edition’s heading: even Māyā-devī longs to taste love for Kṛṣṇa; love for Kṛṣṇa is attained only by the mercy of the pure name and of one who chants the pure name.
