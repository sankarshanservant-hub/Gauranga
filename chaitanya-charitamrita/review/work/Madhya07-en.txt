## 0
tr: The Journey to the South: The Deliverance of Vāsudeva


## 1
w: blessed  that  I praise  Caitanya
w: Vāsudeva  His mind melting with compassion
w: cured of leprosy  filled with beauty
w: satisfied in devotion  made  who
tr: I praise that blessed Caitanya who, His heart melting with compassion, cured Vāsudeva of leprosy, filled him with beauty and made him content in devotion.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to the Lord, ‘Vāsudevāmṛta-prada’” (the Giver of nectar to Vāsudeva; see verse 150).

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Ādi 4.2; translation as there.

## 3
w: in this way  of Sārvabhauma  the deliverance  He accomplished
w: for going south  of the Lord  the desire  arose
tr: Having thus delivered Sārvabhauma, the Lord felt the desire to go to the south.

## 4
w: in the bright fortnight of Māgha  the Lord  took  sannyāsa
w: in Phālguna  coming  made  in Nīlācala  His residence
tr: The Lord accepted sannyāsa in the bright fortnight of Māgha, and in Phālguna He came to live in Nīlācala.

## 5
w: of Phālguna  at the end  the Dola-yātrā  He  saw
w: absorbed in love  many kinds of  dancing and singing  He did
tr: At the end of Phālguna He saw the Dola-yātrā festival and, absorbed in love, danced and sang in many ways.

## 6
w: in Caitra  staying  He made  Sārvabhauma’s deliverance
w: of Vaiśākha  at the beginning  south  to go  arose  the wish
tr: Staying on through Caitra, He delivered Sārvabhauma; and at the beginning of Vaiśākha He resolved to go south.

## 7
w: His own associates  bringing  He speaks  humility  showing
w: embrace  making  all  with His divine hands  holding
tr: He called His associates together, embraced them all, took them by the hand and humbly spoke to them.

## 8
w: you all  know  I  dearer than life  as
w: life  leaving  may go  you all  to leave  not  I can
tr: “I know all of you to be dearer to Me than life itself. My life may leave Me, but I cannot leave you.

## 9
w: you all  friends  My  a friend’s duty  you have done
w: here  bringing  to Me  Jagannātha  you have shown
tr: You are My friends, and you have done a friend’s duty: you brought Me here and showed Me Jagannātha.

## 10
w: now  from all  I  beg  one  gift
w: all  together  permission  give  I shall go  south
tr: Now I beg one gift from all of you: give Me your permission together, and I shall go south.

## 11
w: in search of Viśvarūpa  certainly  I  shall go
w: alone  I shall go  anyone  with (Me)  not  I shall take
tr: I must certainly go in search of Viśvarūpa. I shall go alone and take no one with Me.
fn: Heading in the edition: the Lord wishes to go alone, so that under the pretext of searching for His elder brother Viśvarūpa He may deliver the south.

## 12
w: Setubandha  from  I  not  return  until
w: in Nīlācala  you all  will stay  so long
tr: Until I return from Setubandha, all of you should stay here in Nīlācala.”

## 13
w: Viśvarūpa’s  perfection  attainment  He knows  all
w: the southern  country  to deliver  He makes  this  pretext
tr: The all-knowing Lord knew that Viśvarūpa had already attained perfection; He made this pretext only to deliver the southern country.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu knew well that Viśvarūpa had attained perfection (*siddhi*) long before; He only pretended to go in search of him in order to deliver the south. *Anubhāṣya*: see Madhya 9.299–300.

## 14
w: hearing  of all  in the minds  arose  great sorrow
w: silent  became  all  withered  the faces
tr: Hearing this, everyone was stricken with great sorrow. All fell silent, and their faces withered.

## 15
w: Nityānanda Prabhu  says  so  how  can it be
w: alone  will go  You  who  this  can bear
tr: Nityānanda Prabhu said, “How can this be? You will go alone — who could bear it?

## 16
w: one or two  with (You)  let go  not  fall  into the hands of rogues
w: whom  You say  those two  let go  You  with
tr: Let one or two go with You, so that You do not fall into the hands of rogues — any two You choose.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *haṭha-raṅge* — “into the clutches of a cheat or swindler”.

## 17
w: of the south  the routes to the holy places  I  all  know
w: I  with (You)  go  Prabhu  permission  give  You
tr: I know all the routes to the holy places of the south. Let Me go with You, Prabhu — give Me Your permission.”

## 18
w: the Lord  says  I  a dancer  You  the puppeteer
w: You  as  make dance  so  the dancing  My
tr: The Lord replied, “I am a dancer and You are the puppeteer. As You make Me dance, so I dance.
fn: Heading in the edition: under the guise of feigned reproach the Lord glorifies the qualities of Nityānanda, Jagadānanda, Dāmodara and others.

## 19
w: sannyāsa  having taken  I  set out  for Vṛndāvana
w: You  Me  taking  brought  to Advaita’s house
tr: After taking sannyāsa I set out for Vṛndāvana, but You led Me instead to Advaita’s house.

## 20
w: to Nīlācala  coming  on the way  You broke  My  daṇḍa
w: of you all  by the deep affection  My  work  is hindered
tr: On the way to Nīlācala You broke My daṇḍa. The deep affection all of you bear for Me hinders My work.

## 21
w: Jagadānanda  wants  Me  sense objects  to make enjoy
w: whatever  he says  that  out of fear  I try  to do
tr: Jagadānanda wants Me to enjoy worldly comforts, and whatever he says, I try to do out of fear.

## 22
w: ever  if  his  word  I make  otherwise
w: in anger  three  days  to Me  not  he speaks  a word
tr: If ever I go against his word, he is angry with Me and does not speak to Me for three days.

## 23
w: Mukunda  becomes  unhappy  seeing  the duties of sannyāsa
w: three times  in the cold  bathing  on the ground  lying
tr: Mukunda is unhappy to see the duties of My sannyāsa: bathing three times a day even in the cold, and sleeping on the bare ground.

## 24
w: within  unhappy  Mukunda  not  he says  with his mouth
w: his  unhappiness  seeing  My  twofold  becomes  unhappiness
tr: Mukunda grieves within but says nothing; and seeing his grief, I grieve twice as much.

## 25
w: I  indeed  a sannyāsī  Dāmodara  a brahmacārī
w: always  he stays  Me  over  the rod of instruction  holding
tr: I am a sannyāsī and Dāmodara a brahmacārī, yet he always stands over Me with the rod of instruction.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: a sannyāsī is the guru of a brahmacārī, so it is not fitting for a brahmacārī to instruct a sannyāsī.

## 26
w: him  before  I  not  know  proper conduct
w: to him  not  is pleasing  independent  nature  My
tr: In his eyes I know nothing of proper conduct, and My independent ways do not please him.

## 27
w: regard for people  not  his  Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  by
w: I  regard for people  ever  not  can  give up
tr: By Kṛṣṇa’s mercy he cares nothing for what people think; but I can never give up regard for what people think.

## 28
w: therefore  you all  stay  in Nīlācala
w: days  some  I  holy places  shall wander  alone
tr: Therefore all of you stay in Nīlācala, while for some time I wander alone to the holy places.”

## 29
w: of these all  controlled  the Lord  becomes  by which  qualities
w: of fault-finding  under the pretext  He does  of qualities  relishing
tr: The Lord is controlled by the very qualities of these devotees, and under the pretext of finding fault with them He relished their virtues.
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *doṣāropa-chale*. *Anubhāṣya*: the very qualities by which the Lord was controlled He deliberately called “faults”, and so made known the greatness of His devotees.

## 30
w: Caitanya’s  affection for His devotees  indescribable
w: Himself  the hardships of renunciation  He does  endure
tr: Caitanya’s affection for His devotees is beyond words. He Himself endures the hardships of renunciation.

## 31
w: that  hardship  seeing  which  devotee  pain  feels
w: that  pain  His  by power  to bear  not  is possible
tr: But when a devotee is pained to see those hardships, that pain He has no power to bear.

## 32
w: virtues  speaking of faults  under the pretext  all  forbidding
w: alone  He will wander  holy places  renunciation  practising
tr: So, under the pretext of declaring their virtues to be faults, He forbade them all, intending to wander alone to the holy places in strict renunciation.

## 33
w: then  the four persons  much  pleading  made
w: independent  Lord  the Lord  at all  not  accepted
tr: Then the four devotees pleaded with Him at length, but the Lord, independent and supreme, would not consent.

## 34
w: then  Nityānanda  says  whatever  order  Your
w: sorrow  happiness  whatever  may be  duty  My
tr: Then Nityānanda said, “Whatever You order is My duty, whether it brings sorrow or happiness.

## 35
w: but  one  request  I make  once  more
w: consideration  making  it  make  acceptance
tr: But I make one more request: consider it, and then accept it.

## 36
w: kaupīna  outer cloth  and  water pot
w: other  anything  not  will go  all  this  only
tr: A kaupīna, an outer cloth and a water pot — nothing else will go with You; that is all.

## 37
w: Your  two  hands  bound  in counting the names
w: water pot  outer cloth  You will carry  how
tr: Both Your hands are occupied in counting the holy names. How will You carry the water pot and the outer cloth?

## 38
w: absorbed in love  on the way  You  will be  unconscious
w: these all  things  Your  who  will make  safekeeping
tr: On the way You will fall unconscious in the ecstasy of love. Who will look after these things of Yours then?

## 39
w: ‘Kṛṣṇadāsa’  by name  this  simple  brāhmaṇa
w: him  with (You)  making  take  accept  the request
tr: Here is a simple brāhmaṇa named Kṛṣṇadāsa. Please take him with You — grant My request.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: this brāhmaṇa, Kālā Kṛṣṇadāsa, is not the Kālā Kṛṣṇadāsa of Ādi 11.37 (an eternal friend from Vraja, one of the twelve gopālas); this brāhmaṇa later went to Gauḍa (Madhya 10.62–74).

## 40
w: the water pot and cloth  carrying  with You  he will go
w: whatever  Your  wish  do  anything  not  he will say
tr: He will go with You and carry the water pot and the cloth. Do whatever You wish; he will say nothing.”

## 41
w: then  His  words  the Lord  making  acceptance
w: them all  taking  went  to Sārvabhauma’s house
tr: The Lord then accepted His words and, taking them all along, went to Sārvabhauma’s house.

## 42
w: bowing  Sārvabhauma  seats  offered
w: all  meeting  then  on a seat  sat
tr: Sārvabhauma bowed down and offered them seats; then, after greeting everyone, he sat down.

## 43
w: various  talks of Kṛṣṇa  the Lord  spoke  to him
w: to you  I have come  permission  to ask
tr: The Lord spoke with him of Kṛṣṇa in many ways and then said, “I have come to you to ask your permission.

## 44
w: sannyāsa  having taken  Viśvarūpa  has gone  south
w: certainly  I shall make  I  his  search
tr: Viśvarūpa took sannyāsa and went to the south, and I must go in search of him.

## 45
w: permission  give  certainly  I  south  shall go
w: by your  permission  safely  turning back  I shall come
tr: Give Me permission, for I must go to the south. With your permission I shall come back safely.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *leuṭi* — the western (Hindi) word *lauṭ*, “returning”.

## 46
w: hearing  Sārvabhauma  became  extremely  distressed
w: the feet  clasping  he speaks  a sorrowful reply
tr: Hearing this, Sārvabhauma was deeply distressed. Clasping the Lord’s feet, he replied sorrowfully,

## 47
w: of many births  by the fruit of piety  I obtained  Your  company
w: such company  Providence  my  has made  broken
tr: “By the piety of many births I obtained Your company, and now Providence is breaking it off.

## 48
w: on the head  a thunderbolt  falls  if  a son  dying  goes
w: that  I bear  Your  separation  to bear  not  is possible
tr: If a thunderbolt struck my head or my son died, I could bear it; but separation from You I cannot bear.

## 49
w: the independent Lord  You  will make  departure
w: a few days  stay  let me see  Your  feet
tr: You are the independent Lord, and You will go. But stay a few days, so that I may see Your feet.”

## 50
w: his  by humility  of the Lord  softened  became  the heart
w: He stayed  a few days  not  made  departure
tr: His humble plea softened the Lord’s heart, and He stayed on a few days instead of leaving.

## 51
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  insistence  making  makes  invitation
w: at home  cooking  doing  the Lord  makes take  the meal
tr: The Bhaṭṭācārya insistently invited Him, had food cooked at home and fed the Lord.

## 52
w: his  brāhmaṇa wife  her  name  ‘Ṣāṭhī’s mother’
w: having cooked  alms  gives  she  wonderful  her  story
tr: His wife, known as “Ṣāṭhī’s mother”, cooked and served the Lord His meals. Her story is a wonderful one.
fn: Her story is told in Madhya 15 (Ṣāṭhī, Sārvabhauma’s daughter, and her husband Amogha).

## 53
w: later  indeed  I shall tell  that  making  in detail
w: now  I tell  the Lord’s  southern journey  the account
tr: I shall tell it in detail later on; now I tell the account of the Lord’s journey to the south.

## 54
w: days  five  staying  the Lord  at the Bhaṭṭācārya’s place
w: for going  permission  asked  Himself
tr: After staying five days with the Bhaṭṭācārya, the Lord Himself asked permission to set out.

## 55
w: by the Lord’s  insistence  the Bhaṭṭa  consenting  became
w: the Lord  him  taking  to the temple of Jagannātha  went
tr: At the Lord’s insistence the Bhaṭṭācārya consented, and the Lord took him to the temple of Jagannātha.

## 56
w: darśana  having had  from the Deity  permission  He asked
w: the priest  to the Lord  the garland-prasāda  bringing  gave
tr: After seeing Jagannātha, He asked the Deity for permission, and the priest brought the Lord a garland from the Deity as prasāda.

## 57
w: permission and garland receiving  joyfully  obeisance  making
w: in bliss  to the southern land  goes  Gaurahari
tr: Receiving the permission and the garland, Gaurahari joyfully bowed down and set out in bliss for the south.

## 58
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  with  and  all  His own  people
w: Jagannātha  circumambulation  making  He made  departure
tr: With the Bhaṭṭācārya and all His own people He walked around Jagannātha’s temple and then departed.

## 59
w: along the seashore  on the road to Ālālanātha
w: Sārvabhauma  said  to Gopīnātha Ācārya
tr: On the road to Ālālanātha along the seashore, Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya,

## 60
w: four  kaupīnas and outer cloths  I have kept  at home
w: them  prasāda  taking  come  by a brāhmaṇa
tr: “I have kept four sets of kaupīnas and outer cloths at home. Have them brought by a brāhmaṇa, together with prasāda.”

## 61
w: then  Sārvabhauma  says  at the Lord’s  feet
w: certainly  You will fulfil  Prabhu  my  request
tr: Then Sārvabhauma said at the Lord’s feet, “Prabhu, please fulfil my request.

## 62
w: Rāmānanda Rāya  is  on the bank of the Godāvarī
w: governor  is  he  in Vidyānagara
tr: Rāmānanda Rāya lives on the bank of the Godāvarī; he is the governor in Vidyānagara.

## 63

w: by my  word  him  certainly  You will meet
tr: Do not neglect him, thinking him a śūdra and a worldly man (*viṣayī*). At my request, be sure to meet him.

## 64
w: of Your  company  worthy  he  the one  person
w: on earth  rasika  devotee  there is no  his  equal
tr: He is the one person worthy of Your company. On earth there is no devotee who knows the taste of rasa (*rasika*) to equal him.

## 65
w: learning  and  the rasa of bhakti  of both  he  the limit
w: speaking with him  will know  You  his  greatness
tr: In learning and in the rasa of devotion he has reached the limit of both. When You speak with him, You will know his greatness.

## 66
w: extraordinary  words  actions  his  not  understanding
w: mockery  I have made  him  a ‘Vaiṣṇava’  knowing
tr: Not understanding his extraordinary words and actions, I used to mock him, knowing him to be a Vaiṣṇava.

## 67
w: by Your  grace  now  I know  his  truth
w: speaking with him  You will know  his  how  great
tr: By Your grace I now understand who he truly is. Speak with him, and You will see how great he is.”

## 68
w: acceptance  making  the Lord  his  words
w: him  farewell  to give  him  made  an embrace
tr: The Lord accepted his words and, bidding him farewell, embraced him,

## 69
w: at home  Kṛṣṇa  worshipping  Me  make  blessings
w: to Nīlācala  I may come  so that  by your  grace
tr: saying, “Worship Kṛṣṇa at home and bless Me, so that by your grace I may return to Nīlācala.”

## 70

## 71

w: who  to understand  can  of Mahāprabhu  the heart and mind

## 72

w: of those beyond this world  the hearts

## 77

## 80

## 96
