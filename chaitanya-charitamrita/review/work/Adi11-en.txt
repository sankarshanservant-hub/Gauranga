# English translation, Ādi-līlā 11 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: The Description of the Branches of the Nityānanda Trunk

## 1
w: of Nityānanda  of the feet  lotus-
w: the bees  by the honey of love  maddened
w: having bowed to  all  among them  the chief
w: are written of  a few  by me
tr: Bowing to all the bees at the lotus feet of Nityānanda, maddened by the honey of love, I shall write of a few, the foremost among them.

## 2
w: glory  glory  Mahāprabhu  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya
w: His  sheltered at the feet  whoever,  he  is blessed
tr: Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever has taken shelter at His feet is truly blessed.

## 3
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Advaita,  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory  glory  of Mahāprabhu  to all the devotees
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Advaita! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory, glory to all the devotees of Mahāprabhu!

## 4
w: of that  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya-
w: eternal love  undying  tree
w: the high  trunk  the Avadhūta  moon
w: as branches  the devotees  we bow to
tr: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the undying tree of eternal love, and its tall trunk is the Avadhūta, Śrī Nityānanda, beautiful as the moon. I bow to His devotees, the branches of that trunk.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The description of the branches of the Nityānanda trunk.” *Anubhāṣya*: the eternal, imperishable tree of love is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Himself, who bears the name Gaura; the tall trunk of that tree is Nityānanda Prabhu, the moon; His associates are the branches of that trunk.

## 5
w: Śrī  Nityānanda  of the tree  trunk  mighty
w: on it  grew  branches  offshoots  in abundance
tr: Śrī Nityānanda is the mighty trunk of the tree, and on it grew branches and offshoots in great number.
fn: Heading in the edition: “By Mahāprabhu’s will the branches of Nityānanda grow and become pre-eminent.”

## 6
w: the gardener’s  by the water of will  grow  the branches
w: with flowers and fruits of love  filled  covered  the world
tr: The gardener waters them with His will, and the branches grow. Laden with the flowers and fruits of love, they have covered the world.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the gardener is Śrī Mahāprabhu.

## 7
w: countless  endless  devotees  who  can  count
w: myself  to purify  I tell  the chief  chief  persons
tr: These devotees are countless, without end — who could ever count them? For my own purification I shall name the foremost among them.

## 8
w: Śrī  Vīrabhadra  Gosāñi  of the trunk  the great branch
w: His  offshoots  as many,  countless  their  record
tr: Śrī Vīrabhadra Gosāñi is the great branch of the trunk, and the offshoots that spring from Him are beyond counting.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The branch of Śrī Vīracandra Gosāñi.” *Anubhāṣya*: Vīrabhadra was the son of Śrī Nityānanda and Vasudhā and the disciple of Jāhnavā Mātā. According to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (67), the vyūha of Saṅkarṣaṇa who lies on the ocean of milk appeared as Vīracandra, non-different from Śrī Caitanya.

## 9
w: the Lord  being  has Himself called  a great devotee
w: beyond Vedic dharma  being  in Vedic dharma  engaged
tr: Though He is the Lord, He calls Himself a great devotee; though He is beyond the dharma of the Vedas, He keeps faithfully to it.
fn: Heading in the edition: “His glory: though Viṣṇu Himself, He acts as a Vaiṣṇava.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Vīracandra Prabhu is the vyūha of Śrī Saṅkarṣaṇa who lies on the ocean of milk; though He is the Lord Himself, He regarded Himself as a Vaiṣṇava.

## 10
w: within  the Lord’s  conduct,  outwardly  without pride
w: of devotion to Caitanya  in the hall  He  the main pillar
tr: Within, He acts as the Lord; outwardly, He shows no pride. He is the main pillar in the hall of devotion to Śrī Caitanya.

## 11
w: even today  whose  of mercy  by the glory
w: Caitanya-Nityānanda  sings  the whole  world
tr: Even today, through the glory of His mercy, the whole world sings of Caitanya and Nityānanda.

## 12
w: that  Vīrabhadra  Gosāñi’s  feet  —  my shelter
w: whose  by the grace  comes  desires’  fulfilment
tr: The feet of that Vīrabhadra Gosāñi are my shelter, for by His grace every desire is fulfilled.

## 13
w: Śrī  Rāmadāsa  and  Gadādhara  dāsa
w: of Caitanya Gosāñi  devotees  stayed  his  at the side
tr: Śrī Rāmadāsa and Gadādhara dāsa, devotees of Caitanya Gosāñi, stayed at Nityānanda’s side.
fn: “His side” is Nityānanda’s: by verse 14, Mahāprabhu sent these two to Gauḍa with Him; so too the edition’s heading to verse 14: “Together with Nitāi both preach in Gauḍa.” Heading in the edition: “Ṭhākura Abhirāma (the first of the twelve gopālas) and Dāsa Gadādhara.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Rāmadāsa is Abhirāma dāsa. *Anubhāṣya*: Abhirāma, whose very life was Nityānanda, is Śrīdāma of Vraja (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 126); at Nityānanda’s command he became an ācārya and preacher of bhakti, and love for Kṛṣṇa awoke in anyone he struck with his whip “Jayamaṅgala”. On Gadādhara dāsa see Ādi 10.53.

## 14
w: to Nityānanda  the order  gave  when  to Gauḍa  to go
w: Mahāprabhu  these  two  gave  Him  with
tr: When Mahāprabhu told Nityānanda to go to Gauḍa, He sent these two with Him.

## 15
w: therefore  in both groups  of both  the counting
w: of Mādhava  Vāsudeva  Ghoṣa  the same  account
tr: Therefore they are counted in both groups, and so it is with Mādhava and Vāsudeva Ghoṣa.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Mādhava and Vāsu Ghoṣa Ṭhākura.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (on 14–15): when Mahāprabhu sent Nityānanda to Gauḍa, He gave Him Rāmadāsa and Gadādhara dāsa as companions; that is why they are named both among Mahāprabhu’s devotees and among Nityānanda’s. The same is true of Mādhava and Vāsu Ghoṣa.

## 16
w: Rāmadāsa  —  a chief branch,  of friendly love  a treasure
w: of sixteen bearers  the log  which  lifting  made  a flute
tr: Rāmadāsa is a chief branch, a treasure of the love of friendship. He lifted a log that it takes sixteen men to carry and made a flute of it.
fn: The *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (126), quoted in the *Anubhāṣya* on 11.13, speaks of a log that only thirty-two men could carry. Heading in the edition: “The pastime of Abhirāma.”

## 17
w: Gadādhara  dāsa  in the mood of a gopī  full of bliss
w: whose  in the house  the toll pastime  performed  Nityānanda
tr: Gadādhara dāsa was full of bliss in the mood of a gopī, and in his house Nityānanda performed the *dāna-keli*.
fn: *Dāna-keli*, “the toll pastime”: Kṛṣṇa and His friends block the gopīs’ way and demand a “toll” from them. Heading in the edition: “The wondrous deeds of Dāsa Gadādhara.”

## 18
w: Śrī  Mādhava  Ghoṣa  —  foremost  among kīrtana singers
w: Nityānanda Prabhu  dance  does  whose  to the singing
tr: Śrī Mādhava Ghoṣa was foremost among the kīrtana singers, and Nityānanda Prabhu would dance to his singing.

## 19
w: Vāsudeva  in songs  makes  of the Lord  description
w: wood  stone  melt  whose  on hearing
tr: Vāsudeva sang of the Lord in his songs, and on hearing them even wood and stone would melt.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 15): into the collections of Vāsu Ghoṣa’s songs the *prākṛta-sahajiyās* have inserted many songs of “Gaura the lover of the women of Nadia” (*gaura-nāgarī*), pleasing to their senses. Vāsu Ghoṣa, who tasted the rasa of separation, never wrote such songs and never could have; the sādhaka should reject them.

## 20
w: of Murāri-caitanya dāsa  wondrous  pastimes
w: the tiger  on the cheek  slap  strikes,  with snakes  play
tr: Wondrous were the pastimes of Murāri-caitanya dāsa: he would slap a tiger on the cheek and play with snakes.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*, quoting the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 5): oblivious of his body, Caitanya dāsa would chase tigers through the forest and ride on them, take a huge python into his lap, and stay under water for two or three days. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: he was born at Sar-Vṛndāvanapura (Bardhamān district), and on coming to Modadruma (Māmgāchi) in Navadvīpa he received the name Śārṅga Murāri-caitanya dāsa.

## 21
w: of Nityānanda  the associates  as many  —  all  friends of Vraja
w: horn  staff  cowherd dress,  on the head  peacock feather
tr: All of Nityānanda’s associates are cowherd friends from Vraja. Each carries a horn and a staff and wears the dress of a cowherd, with a peacock feather on his head.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Nitāi’s associates are pure devotees, the friends of Vraja.” *Anubhāṣya*: all who took shelter of Nityānanda Prabhu belong to the sakhya-rasa of Vraja and wear the dress of cowherd boys. His consort, Īśvarī Jāhnavā Mātā, is Anaṅga-mañjarī, the younger sister of Śrīmatī Rādhikā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 66), and the devotees who took shelter of her are also counted among Nityānanda’s associates.

## 22
w: Raghunātha  Vaidya  Upādhyāya  a great soul
w: whose  at the sight  love and devotion for Kṛṣṇa  arises
tr: Raghunātha Vaidya Upādhyāya was a great soul: the very sight of him awakened loving devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

## 23
w: Sundarānanda  —  Nityānanda’s  branch,  servant  intimate
w: whom  with  Nityānanda  enjoys  the playful pastimes of Vraja
tr: Sundarānanda, a branch of Nityānanda, was His intimate servant, and with him Nityānanda enjoyed the playful pastimes of Vraja.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the second of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Sudāma (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 127); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6) calls him “an ocean of the rasa of love, the chief of Nityānanda’s companions”.

## 24
w: Kamalākara  Pippalāi  —  extraordinary  ways
w: extraordinary  love  his  in the world  known
tr: Kamalākara Pippalāi was extraordinary in his ways, and his extraordinary love is known throughout the world.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the third of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Mahābala (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 128); he installed the Deity of Jagannātha at Māhesh, and his descendants serve Him.

## 25
w: Sūryadāsa  Sarakhela,  his  brother  Kṛṣṇadāsa
w: in Nityānanda  firm  faith,  of love  the abode
tr: Sūryadāsa Sarakhela and his brother Kṛṣṇadāsa had firm faith in Nityānanda and were abodes of love.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Sūryadāsa Sarakhela lived at Śāligrāma near Navadvīpa; he received the title “Sarakhela” in the service of the Muslim ruler of Gauḍa. His daughters Vasudhā and Jāhnavā are the consorts of Śrī Nityānanda (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 65). Kṛṣṇadāsa Sarakhela was the younger brother of Sūryadāsa and of Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita.

## 26
w: Śrī  Gaurīdāsa  in Paṇḍita  devotion of soaring love
w: love for Kṛṣṇa  to give,  to take,  holds  great power
tr: Śrī Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita’s devotion was ablaze with prema; he had great power both to give and to receive love for Kṛṣṇa.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the fourth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Subala (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 128); the brother of Sūryadāsa Sarakhela, he moved from Śāligrāma to Ambikā-Kālnā on the bank of the Gaṅgā.

## 27
w: to Nityānanda  surrendered  caste  family  place at the common meal
w: Śrī  Caitanya-Nityānanda  making  the Lords of his life
tr: Gaurīdāsa surrendered to Nityānanda his caste, his family and his place in society, and made Śrī Caitanya and Nityānanda the Lords of his life.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *pā̐ti* is the common meal (*paṅkti-bhojana*), the right to sit and eat in one row with one’s caste. Heading in the edition: “Gaurīdāsa Paṇḍita, whose life is Gaura and Nitāi.”

## 28
w: of Nityānanda  Prabhu  dear  —  Paṇḍita  Purandara
w: the ocean of love  within  moves  like  Mandara
tr: Purandara Paṇḍita was dear to Nityānanda Prabhu; he moved through the ocean of love like Mount Mandara.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*, quoting the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 5): at Khaḍadaha, at the home of Purandara Paṇḍita, Nityānanda danced tirelessly, and Purandara, frenzied with love, climbed a tree, roared like a lion and leapt down crying, “I am Aṅgada!”

## 29
w: Parameśvara dāsa  —  Nityānanda  his sole shelter
w: devotion to Kṛṣṇa  attains,  him  who  does  remember
tr: Parameśvara dāsa took shelter in Nityānanda alone; whoever remembers him attains devotion to Kṛṣṇa.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the fifth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Arjuna (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 132); he accompanied Jāhnavā Mātā to the festival at Kheturi and at her command installed the Deities of Śrī Rādhā-Gopīnātha at Āṭpur. The *Vaiṣṇava-vandanā* says that he made even jackals utter the holy name.

## 30
w: Śrī  Jagadīśa  Paṇḍita  is  of the world  the purifier
w: the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa  rains,  like  rain  heavy
tr: Śrī Jagadīśa Paṇḍita purifies the whole world; like a heavy monsoon rain, he pours down the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: his śrīpāṭa is Yaśaḍā near Cākdaha; at Mahāprabhu’s command he preached the holy name in Nīlācala, and from there he brought a Deity of Jagannātha, whom he installed at Yaśaḍā.

## 31
w: of Nityānanda  dear  servant  Paṇḍita  Dhanañjaya
w: extremely  detached,  always  full of love for Kṛṣṇa
tr: Dhanañjaya Paṇḍita, a dear servant of Nityānanda, was utterly detached and always full of love for Kṛṣṇa.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the sixth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Vasudāma (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 127); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6) calls him “a wonderful mahānta, in whose heart Nityānanda ever dwells”. His chief śrīpāṭa is Śītalagrāma near Kāṭoyā.

## 32
w: Maheśa  Paṇḍita  —  of Vraja  the generous  cowherd boy
w: to the kettledrum  dance  does  in love  intoxicated
tr: Maheśa Paṇḍita is a generous cowherd boy of Vraja; to the beat of the kettledrum he would dance, drunk with love.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the seventh of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Mahābāhu (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 129); he was with Nityānanda at the festival in Pānihāṭi and then went with Him to Saptagrāma.

## 33
w: in Navadvīpa  Puruṣottama  Paṇḍita  a great soul
w: at Nityānanda’s name  whose  great madness  comes
tr: Puruṣottama Paṇḍita of Navadvīpa was a great soul; at the very name of Nityānanda he was seized by the great madness of love.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the eighth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Stoka-kṛṣṇa (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 130); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6) says he was “born in Navadvīpa, the intimate great servant of Nityānanda”.

## 34
w: Balarāma  dāsa  —  the rasa of love for Kṛṣṇa  tasting
w: at Nityānanda’s name  becomes  supremely  mad
tr: Balarāma dāsa tasted the rasa of love for Kṛṣṇa, and at the name of Nityānanda he was utterly beside himself.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*, quoting the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6): “Balarāma dāsa, drunk with the rasa of love — even a breeze from him destroys all sins.”

## 35
w: the great devotee  Yadunātha  Kavicandra
w: whose  in the heart  dance  does  Nityānanda
tr: Yadunātha Kavicandra was a great devotee, and Nityānanda dances in his heart.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (from the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*): Yadunātha Kavicandra was the son of Ratnagarbha Ācārya, a friend of Nityānanda’s father from the same village; his brothers were Kṛṣṇānanda and Jīva (11.44, 11.50).

## 36
w: in Rāḍha  whose  birth  Kṛṣṇadāsa  the best of brāhmaṇas
w: of Śrī Nityānanda  he  the foremost  servant
tr: Kṛṣṇadāsa, an excellent brāhmaṇa born in Rāḍha, was a foremost servant of Śrī Nityānanda.

## 37
w: Kālā  Kṛṣṇadāsa  great  among Vaiṣṇavas  foremost
w: Nityānanda  the moon  but  not  knows  another
tr: Kālā Kṛṣṇadāsa was great, foremost among Vaiṣṇavas; he knew no one but Nityānanda, the moon.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the ninth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Lavaṅga (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 132); the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6): “Kāliyā Kṛṣṇadāsa is famed in the three worlds; whoever remembers him attains Gauracandra.” His śrīpāṭa is Ākāihāṭ near Kāṭoyā.

## 38
w: Śrī  Sadāśiva  Kavirāja  —  great  soul
w: Śrī  Puruṣottama  dāsa  —  his  son
tr: Śrī Sadāśiva Kavirāja was a great soul, and Śrī Puruṣottama dāsa was his son.
fn: By the heading in the edition, Puruṣottama is the tenth of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: Sadāśiva Kavirāja was formerly, in Vraja, Candrāvalī, Kṛṣṇa’s beloved (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 156); his son is “Nāgara” Puruṣottama, the cowherd boy Dāma of Vraja (ibid., 131); Sadāśiva’s father was Kaṁsāri Sena (11.51). Rarely does one find, as in this family, four generations of perfected devotees of Gaura: Kaṁsāri Sena, Sadāśiva, Puruṣottama and Kānu Ṭhākura.

## 39
w: from birth  immersed  in Nityānanda’s  feet
w: constantly  childhood pastimes  does  Kṛṣṇa  with
tr: From his birth he was absorbed in the feet of Nityānanda, and he played the games of childhood with Kṛṣṇa without cease.
fn: Heading in the edition: “‘Nāgara’ Puruṣottama.”

## 40
w: his  son  —  the great soul  Śrī  Kānu  Ṭhākura
w: whose  in the body  dwells  of the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa  a flood
tr: His son was the great soul Śrī Kānu Ṭhākura, in whose body dwelt a full flood of the nectar of love for Kṛṣṇa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: some count Kānu Ṭhākura among the twelve gopālas. According to his family tradition, his mother left the world soon after his birth, and Nityānanda Prabhu took the infant to Khaḍadaha and named him Śiśu Kṛṣṇadāsa; in Vṛndāvana, Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī and the people of Vraja gave him the name Ṭhākura Kānāi. His śrīpāṭa is Bodhkhānā (Yaśohar district).

## 41
w: of great devotees  the best  Datta  Uddhāraṇa
w: in every way  serves  Nityānanda’s  feet
tr: Uddhāraṇa Datta, the best of great devotees, served the feet of Nityānanda in every way.
fn: By the heading in the edition, the eleventh of the twelve gopālas. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Subāhu (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 129). According to the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 5), Nityānanda stayed in his house at Saptagrāma on the bank of the Triveṇī, and through Uddhāraṇa the whole community of merchants (*vaṇiks*) was purified.

## 42
w: Ācārya  Vaiṣṇavānanda  of devotion  the possessor
w: formerly  name  was  whose  ‘Raghunātha  Purī’
tr: Ācārya Vaiṣṇavānanda was rich in devotion; his former name was Raghunātha Purī.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (97) names Raghunātha Purī among the eight Purīs who embody the eight mystic perfections (*aṇimā* and the rest).

## 43
w: Viṣṇudāsa,  Nandana,  Gaṅgādāsa  —  three  brothers
w: formerly  whose  in the house  stayed  Ṭhākura  Nitāi
tr: Viṣṇudāsa, Nandana and Gaṅgādāsa were three brothers, in whose house Ṭhākura Nitāi once stayed.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the three brothers were brāhmaṇas of Navadvīpa; Viṣṇudāsa and Gaṅgādāsa lived for some time with Mahāprabhu in Nīlācala (Ādi 10.151). In the house of Nandana Ācārya, Mahāprabhu and Advaita once hid, and Nityānanda, on coming to Navadvīpa, stayed there for some time.

## 44
w: Nityānanda’s servant  —  Paramānanda  Upādhyāya
w: Śrī  Jīva  Paṇḍita  Nityānanda’s  qualities  sings
tr: Paramānanda Upādhyāya was a servant of Nityānanda, and Śrī Jīva Paṇḍita sang of Nityānanda’s virtues.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Jīva Paṇḍita was the middle son of Ratnagarbha Ācārya (11.35); in Vraja he is Indirā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 169).

## 45
w: Paramānanda  Gupta  —  Kṛṣṇa’s devotee  great-minded
w: formerly  whose  in the house  Nityānanda’s  dwelling
tr: Paramānanda Gupta was a great-minded devotee of Kṛṣṇa; Nityānanda once lived in his house.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja he is Mañjumedhā (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 194, 199); he composed a garland of hymns to Kṛṣṇa.

## 46
w: Nārāyaṇa,  Kṛṣṇadāsa,  and  Manohara
w: Devānanda  —  four  brothers  Nitāi’s servants
tr: Nārāyaṇa, Kṛṣṇadāsa, Manohara and Devānanda — these four brothers were servants of Nitāi.

## 47
w: Hoḍa  Kṛṣṇadāsa  —  Nityānanda Prabhu  his life
w: Śrī  Nityānanda’s  feet  but  not  knows  another
tr: To Hoḍa Kṛṣṇadāsa, Nityānanda Prabhu was life itself; he knew nothing but the feet of Śrī Nityānanda.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Hoḍa Kṛṣṇadāsa is Rājā Kṛṣṇadāsa of Baḍagāchi, the son of Hari Hoḍa; at his initiative Śrī Nityānanda’s wedding took place in nearby Śāligrāma. The *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Antya 6): “the pious Kṛṣṇadāsa of Baḍagāchi, in whose house Nityānanda showed His pastimes”.

## 50

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Navanī Hoḍa is the son of Hoḍa Kṛṣṇadāsa (11.47); Kṛṣṇānanda is the brother of Yadunātha Kavicandra (11.35).

## 51
w: Kaṁsāri  Sena,  Rāmasena,  Rāmacandra  Kavirāja
w: Govinda,  Śrīraṅga,  Mukunda,  three  kavirājas
tr: Kaṁsāri Sena, Rāmasena and Rāmacandra Kavirāja, and the three physicians — Govinda, Śrīraṅga and Mukunda;
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Kaṁsāri Sena is the father of Sadāśiva Kavirāja (11.38), in Vraja Ratnāvalī (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 194, 200). Rāmacandra Kavirāja was the son of Cirañjīva and Sunandā of Śrīkhaṇḍa, a disciple of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya and the dear friend of Narottama Ṭhākura, who prayed for his company birth after birth. Govinda Kavirāja was his younger brother, also a disciple of Śrīnivāsa Ācārya, the author of the play *Saṅgīta-mādhava* and of the *Gītāmṛta*; both received the title “Kavirāja” from Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī.

## 54
w: Vṛndāvana  dāsa  —  Nārāyaṇī’s  son

tr: Vṛndāvana dāsa, the son of Nārāyaṇī, composed the *Caitanya-maṅgala*.
fn: *Caitanya-maṅgala* was the first name of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (see Ādi 8.33–35). *Anubhāṣya*: according to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (109), Vṛndāvana dāsa was formerly Vedavyāsa, and Kṛṣṇa’s friend Kusumāpīḍa also dwells in him. He was the son of Nārāyaṇī, the niece of Śrīvāsa, and the author of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*.

## 55
