## 6

tr: Bathing, going to see Lord Jagannātha and eating went on of themselves, by the body’s own nature, as a potter’s wheel keeps turning.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: a potter’s wheel goes on turning by the force of the first push, and the potter need not keep his hand on it; so the Lord’s bodily actions went on naturally even when He had no awareness of the outer world. The comparison fits the daily life of the highest mahā-bhāgavata as long as he remains in this world.

w: in Vṛndāvana  dance  peacocks  in flocks
w: spotless  full in all digits
w: of beauty  moonlight  sparkling

tr: Hearing the soft, sweet sound of His flute — the cloud’s sweet thunder — the peacocks of Vṛndāvana dance. And in that cloud a wondrous moon is rising, spotless and full in all its digits, its moonlight of beauty sparkling.

## 68
w: of pastimes  of nectar  by the shower
w: it sprinkles  fourteen  worlds
w: such  a cloud  when  appeared
w: of misfortune  of the storm  by the wind
w: the cloud  was taken  to another  place
w: dies  the cātaka  to drink  not  could
tr: With a shower of the nectar of His pastimes it drenches all fourteen worlds — but when such a cloud appeared, the storm wind of My misfortune carried it away to another place, and the *cātaka* is dying, for it could not drink.”

## 69
w: again  He says  alas  alas
w: recite  recite  Rāmarāya
w: says  the Lord  choked  in speech
w: Rāmānanda  recites  a verse
w: hearing  the Lord’s  joy  and sorrow
w: Himself  the Lord  makes  explanation
tr: “Alas, alas! Recite, recite, Rāmarāya!” the Lord said again, His voice choked with feeling. Rāmānanda recited a verse; hearing it, the Lord felt both joy and sorrow, and He Himself explained it.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: joy, because He hears of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness; sorrow, because He is separated from Him.

## 70
w: having seen  by locks  framed  face  Your  by the beauty of earrings
w: of the cheeks  of the lips  the nectar  smiling  glance
w: granting fearlessness  and  of arms  the pair  having beheld
w: the chest  for Śrī  the sole delight  and  we become  maidservants
tr: “Seeing Your face framed by curling locks and graced by the beauty of Your earrings, Your cheeks, the nectar of Your lips and Your smiling glance, and beholding Your two arms that grant fearlessness and Your chest, the sole delight of Śrī, we have become Your maidservants.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.39)
fn: The verse is Madhya 24.48; translated as there.
