## 1

tr: I take refuge in Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, who by the ropes of His mercy cleverly lifted Raghunātha dāsa out of the blind well of a wretched household life, entrusted him to Svarūpa, and made him His intimate associate.
fn: The last line is as in Madhya 19.54 (*śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanyam amuṁ prapadye*, “I take refuge in this Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya”). *Anubhāṣya*: *ku-gṛha* — a wretched home, full of talk of husbands, wives, sons and the like, a blind well with no way out; *bhaṅgyā* — skilfully; compare Raghunātha dāsa’s *Vilāpa-kusumāñjali*: “I worship Caitanyacandra… by the ropes of His mercy He at once pulled me out of the impassable, waterless great well of endless suffering… and gave me to Śrī Dāmodara.”

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *markaṭa-vairāgya* is also said of a householder who wears the dress and marks of a renunciant. *Anubhāṣya*: before people Raghunātha played the part of a worldly man and wholly gave up the way of the monkey, attached to enjoyment, that makes a show of outward renunciation. Edition heading: at home he practises *yukta-vairāgya* — outwardly like a worldly man, inwardly free of the world, owning nothing and doing everything for Kṛṣṇa.

## 17

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: a *caudhurī* keeps for himself the quarter of the revenue due to him from the tenants and pays the tax to the landlord. Edition heading: the Muslim *caudhurī* of Saptagrāma, who held Saptagrāma with the support of the Nawab’s vizier.

## 18

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *makrari* — *ijārā*, a permanent lease. *Anubhāṣya*: the Muslim *caudhurī* lost all his income, and seeing this he was enraged to the core.

## 19

w: that  Turk  anything  not  getting  became  an enemy
tr: Hiraṇya collected twenty lakhs and paid the king twelve. Getting nothing, that “Turk” became his enemy.
fn: A lakh is a hundred thousand. *Anubhāṣya*: of the twenty lakhs collected, Hiraṇya should have kept back a quarter (five lakhs) and paid the king fifteen; instead he paid twelve, so the “Turk”, that is, the Muslim, lost the share due to him and became their enemy.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *kaiphiyat* — a written report. Edition heading: Hiraṇya and Govardhana flee, and Raghunātha is taken into custody.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Raghunātha was the son of a respected and wealthy man, learned himself and, above all, born among the Kāyasthas, an eminent class close to the brāhmaṇas, so the official could no longer beat him; from of old the Kāyasthas have been the king’s officers, honoured equally with the kṣatriyas.
