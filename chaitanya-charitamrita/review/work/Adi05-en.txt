# English translation, Ādi-līlā 5 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: Determining the Truth of Śrī Nityānanda

## 1
w: I bow  of endless  wondrous  opulence (possessed)
w: Śrī Nityānanda  the Lord
w: by whose  will  His  true nature
w: by an ignorant one  even  is described
tr: I bow to the Lord Śrī Nityānanda, whose divine opulence is endless and wondrous. By His will even a fool can come to know and describe His true nature.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *ajñena* — “by one who does not know the scriptures”, that is, by the author himself; *nirūpyate* — “is described”: by Nityānanda’s mercy even such a person can describe the truth about Him.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: in these  six  verses  I have told  of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  the glory
w: in five  verses  I tell  of the truth of Nityānanda  the full extent
tr: In these six verses I have told the glory of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya; in five verses I now tell the whole truth of Nityānanda.

## 4
w: of all  avatāras the source  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān
w: His  second  body  Śrī Balarāma
tr: Kṛṣṇa, the source of all avatāras, is Bhagavān Himself, and Śrī Balarāma is His second body.

## 5
w: one and the same  nature  of the two  different only  the bodies
w: the first  bodily expansion  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  the helper
tr: The two are one in nature; only Their bodies differ. Balarāma is the first expansion of Kṛṣṇa’s body and His helper in His pastimes.

## 6
w: that  Kṛṣṇa  in Navadvīpa  Śrī Caitanyacandra
w: that  Balarāma  with Him  Śrī Nityānanda
tr: That Kṛṣṇa is Śrī Caitanyacandra in Navadvīpa, and that Balarāma is Śrī Nityānanda, who is with Him.

## 7
w: Saṅkarṣaṇa  Kāraṇodakaśāyī
w: Garbhodakaśāyī  and  Kṣīrodakaśāyī
w: and Śeṣa  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-
w: -nanda named  Rāma  refuge  my  may be
tr: “Saṅkarṣaṇa, the Lord who lies in the Causal Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Garbha Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Ocean of Milk, and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions: may that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my refuge.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Saṅkarṣaṇa is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa of the spiritual sky (Paravyoma); Kāraṇodakaśāyī is the original Puruṣa-avatāra; Garbhodakaśāyī is the second Puruṣa, Hiraṇyagarbha, the Viṣṇu of the whole universe (*samaṣṭi*); Kṣīrodakaśāyī is the third Puruṣa, the Viṣṇu of each being (*vyaṣṭi*); Śeṣa is Ananta. The edition ascribes the verse to the diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara. The same verse as Ādi 1.7.

## 8
w: Śrī Balarāma  Gosāñi  the original  Saṅkarṣaṇa
w: five  forms  assuming  He performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  service
tr: Śrī Balarāma Gosāñi is the original Saṅkarṣaṇa. Assuming five forms, He serves Kṛṣṇa.

## 9
w: Himself  He performs  of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  assistance
w: of the pastime of creation  the work  He does  assuming  four  bodies
tr: He Himself assists Kṛṣṇa in His pastimes, and in four other bodies He does the work of the pastime of creation.

## 10
w: creation  and the rest  service  His  order’s  carrying out
w: Śeṣa  in the form of  He performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  manifold  service
tr: His service of creation and the rest is the carrying out of Kṛṣṇa’s order; and as Śeṣa He serves Kṛṣṇa in many ways.

## 11
w: in all  forms  He tastes  of Kṛṣṇa’s service  the bliss
w: that  Balarāma  Gaura’s companion  Nityānanda
tr: In all these forms He tastes the bliss of serving Kṛṣṇa. And that same Balarāma is Nityānanda, the companion of Gaura.

## 12
w: of the seventh  verse  the meaning  I make  in four verses
w: by which  of Nityānanda  the truth  know  all people
tr: The meaning of the seventh verse I shall explain in four verses, so that all people may know the truth of Nityānanda.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: what is said in the seventh verse is explained in the eighth, ninth, tenth and eleventh verses of the invocation (Ādi 1.8–1.11), which the author now quotes and expounds (5.13, 5.50, 5.93, 5.109).

## 13
w: beyond māyā  in all-pervading  Vaikuṇṭha  world
w: full of opulence  amid the fourfold expansion
w: form  whose  shines forth  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named
w: to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
tr: “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma, whose form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines amid the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha) in the all-pervading world of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence and beyond māyā.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.8.

## 14
w: material nature’s  beyond  Paravyoma  named  an abode
w: Kṛṣṇa’s  form  just as  with opulence  and other  qualities endowed
tr: Beyond material nature lies the abode called Paravyoma. Like Kṛṣṇa’s own form, it is endowed with every opulence and every divine quality.

## 15
w: all-pervading  endless  Brahman  Vaikuṇṭha  and others  abodes
w: of Kṛṣṇa  of Kṛṣṇa’s avatāras  there  the dwelling
tr: There lie endless, all-pervading abodes — Brahman, Vaikuṇṭha and others — and in them Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras dwell.
fn: The edition’s heading: “Brahman and the worlds above it, the abodes of Kṛṣṇa and His avatāras.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in that realm are the all-pervading, endless abode of Brahman and the abodes of Vaikuṇṭha; in all of them dwell Kṛṣṇa Himself and all His avatāras.

## 16
w: its  in the upper  part  Kṛṣṇaloka  renowned
w: Dvārakā  Mathurā  Gokula  in three divisions  it abides
tr: In its highest region is the realm known as Kṛṣṇaloka, which abides in three divisions — Dvārakā, Mathurā and Gokula.

## 17
w: above all  Śrī Gokula  the abode of the world of Vraja
w: Śrī Goloka  Śvetadvīpa  Vṛndāvana  named
tr: Above all is Śrī Gokula, the abode of Vraja, which is also called Śrī Goloka, Śvetadvīpa and Vṛndāvana.

## 18
w: all-pervading  endless  vast  like Kṛṣṇa’s body
w: above and below  it pervades  there is no  limit
tr: Like Kṛṣṇa’s body, it is all-pervading, endless and vast; it extends above and below without any bound.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «নাহিক নিয়ম» (another reading: «নাহি তাঁর সীমা»).

## 19
w: in the universe  manifestation  its  by Kṛṣṇa’s  will
w: one and the same  nature  its  there are not  two  bodies
tr: By Kṛṣṇa’s will it is manifest within the universe, yet it remains one and the same: it has not two bodies.

## 20
w: of cintāmaṇi  the land  of wish-fulfilling trees full  forests
w: with eyes of flesh  one sees  it  the material world  like
tr: Its land is cintāmaṇi, its forests are full of wish-fulfilling trees; yet eyes of flesh see it as no different from the material world.
fn: The edition’s heading: “To the eye of enjoyment it looks like the material world, but to the eye of devotion it is the spiritual land of cintāmaṇi, the field of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: even in the Vraja manifest on earth the land is cintāmaṇi and the forests are of wish-fulfilling trees.

## 21
w: with eyes of love  one sees  its  true form’s  manifestation
w: with the cowherd boys  and girls  together  where  Kṛṣṇa’s  pastimes
tr: With eyes of love one sees it as it truly is — the place where Kṛṣṇa sports with the cowherd boys and girls.

## 22
w: of cintāmaṇi gems  of clusters  in abodes  wish-fulfilling trees
w: by millions  surrounded  the surabhi cows  tending
w: by Lakṣmīs  thousands  hundreds  with reverence  being served
w: Govinda  the original  Person  Him  I  worship
tr: “I worship Govinda, the original Person, who tends the surabhi cows in abodes built of cintāmaṇi gems and surrounded by millions of wish-fulfilling trees, and who is served with reverence by hundreds of thousands of Lakṣmīs.” (Brahma-saṁhitā, 5.29)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lakṣmīs here are the gopīs, the beloved cowherd maidens of Vraja (*lakṣmyaḥ gopa-rāmāḥ*); *abhipālayantam* — “tending them in every way, as befits a cowherd”.

## 23
w: in Mathurā  Dvārakā  His own  form  manifesting
w: in many  forms  He sports  the catur-vyūha  becoming
tr: In Mathurā and Dvārakā He manifests His own form and, becoming the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha), sports in many forms.

## 24
w: Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha
w: of all  catur-vyūhas  the source  transcendent  pure
tr: Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha are the source of every catur-vyūha; They are transcendent (*turīya*) and pure.

## 25
w: in these  three  abodes  Kṛṣṇa  wholly  of pastimes full
w: His own  associates  taking  He plays  endless  time
tr: In these three abodes Kṛṣṇa is wholly given to His pastimes; with His own associates He plays there for endless time.

## 26
w: of Paravyoma  in the midst  making  of His own form  manifestation
w: of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  He performs  various  pastimes
tr: In the midst of Paravyoma He manifests His own form and, as Nārāyaṇa, enjoys pastimes of many kinds.

## 27
w: own  form  Kṛṣṇa’s  only  two-armed
w: of Nārāyaṇa  in the form  that  body  four-armed
tr: Kṛṣṇa’s own form has only two arms; in the form of Nārāyaṇa that same body has four.

## 28
w: conch  disc  club  lotus  of great majesty full
w: Śrī  Bhū  Nīlā  potencies  whose  feet  serve
tr: He holds the conch, disc, club and lotus and is full of supreme majesty; His feet are served by His potencies Śrī, Bhū and Nīlā.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in some Bengali manuscripts “Līlā-śakti” is read in place of “Nīlā”; these three potencies dwell in Vaikuṇṭha beside Nārāyaṇa.

## 29
w: although  only  His  play  merely  nature
w: still  to the jīvas  out of mercy  He does  one  work
tr: Though His nature is simply to play, yet out of mercy to the jīvas He does one work.

## 30
w: sālokya  sāmīpya  sārṣṭi  sārūpya  kinds
w: four  liberations  giving  He makes  of the jīvas  deliverance
tr: Granting the four kinds of liberation — sālokya, sāmīpya, sārṣṭi and sārūpya — He delivers the jīvas.

## 31
w: in Brahman  by merging  of the liberated  there  there is no  access
w: Vaikuṇṭha  outside  is  of them all  the dwelling
tr: Those liberated by merging into Brahman have no access there; they all dwell outside Vaikuṇṭha.

## 32
w: Vaikuṇṭha  outside  one  luminous  sphere
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s  body  the radiance  supremely  bright
tr: Outside Vaikuṇṭha lies a sphere of light — the radiance of Kṛṣṇa’s body, supremely bright.

## 33
w: Siddhaloka  name  its  material nature  beyond
w: spiritual  in nature  there  there are not  of the spiritual potency  transformations
tr: It is called Siddhaloka and lies beyond material nature. It is spiritual, yet the spiritual potency displays no variety there.

## 34
w: the sun’s  disc  as  outside  without variety
w: within  the sun’s  chariot  and the rest  full of variety
tr: It is like the disc of the sun: from outside it is a featureless glow, but within are the sun’s chariot and all its variety.

## 35
w: from desire  from enmity  from fear  from affection
w: as  by devotion  on the Lord  the mind
w: fixing  that  sin  casting off
w: many  His  destination  have attained
tr: “Many have fixed their minds on the Lord — some by devotion, others by desire (*kāma*), enmity, fear or affection — and, casting off the sin in those feelings, have attained Him.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.1.29)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: this is Nārada’s answer to Yudhiṣṭhira’s question how Śiśupāla, who hated Kṛṣṇa, attained *sāyujya*: by *kāma* — the gopīs; by enmity — Dantavakra and Śiśupāla; by fear — Kaṁsa; by affection — the Pāṇḍavas. *Tad agham* is the sin born of desire and the other feelings; *tad-gatim* — the various kinds of liberation. The gopīs’ *kāma* Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī calls prema: “The pure love of the gopīs came to be called *kāma*” (*Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*).

## 36
w: which  of the enemies  of the beloved  and
w: the attainment  one  as if  is spoken of
w: that  of Brahman and Kṛṣṇa  from the oneness
w: like the rays and the sun  of those two
tr: “That the goal of the Lord’s enemies and of His beloved ones is spoken of as if it were one is because Brahman and Kṛṣṇa are one, as the rays are one with the sun.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.278)
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: where the scriptures say that the Lord’s enemies and His beloved ones attain the same, they speak only of the oneness of Brahman, which is like the rays, and Kṛṣṇa, who is like the sun. In truth His beloved ones attain the variety of Vaikuṇṭha, and His enemies Siddhaloka, where there are no pastimes.

## 37
w: so  in Paravyoma  manifold  of the spiritual potency  pastimes
w: featureless  of light  a sphere  outside  is manifest
tr: So in Paravyoma there are manifold pastimes of the spiritual potency, while outside it a featureless sphere of light is manifest.

## 38
w: featureless  Brahman  that  only  luminous
w: for sāyujya  the qualified  there  attain  absorption
tr: That featureless Brahman is light alone, and those who are fit for *sāyujya* are absorbed in it.

## 39
w: Siddhaloka  but  darkness
w: beyond  where  dwell  indeed
w: the siddhas  in the bliss of Brahman  immersed
w: the demons  and  by Hari  slain
tr: “Beyond darkness lies Siddhaloka, where dwell the siddhas, immersed in the bliss of Brahman, and the demons slain by Hari.” (Brahmāṇḍa-purāṇa; Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.2.280)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *tamasaḥ pāre* — beyond the three guṇas; the siddhas are those perfected in the knowledge of featureless Brahman and in *kaivalya-yoga*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: there dwell the Māyāvādīs, immersed in the bliss of Brahman, and asuras such as Kaṁsa, slain by the Lord; the yogīs of Patañjali’s school who attain *kaivalya* also reach that world.

## 40
w: in that  Paravyoma  of Nārāyaṇa  on four  sides
w: of Dvārakā  the catur-vyūha  in a second  manifestation
tr: In that Paravyoma, on the four sides of Nārāyaṇa, the catur-vyūha of Dvārakā is present in a second manifestation.

## 41
w: Vāsudeva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  Pradyumna  Aniruddha
w: the second  catur-vyūha  this  transcendent  pure
tr: Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha — this is the second catur-vyūha, transcendent and pure.

## 42
w: there  which  of Rāma  form  Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa
w: of the spiritual potency  the shelter  He  of causes  the cause
tr: There the form of Rāma is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa. He is the shelter of the spiritual potency, the cause of all causes.

## 43
w: of the spiritual potency  a manifestation  one  śuddha-sattva  named
w: of śuddha-sattva made  all  Vaikuṇṭha and other  abodes
tr: One manifestation of the spiritual potency is called *śuddha-sattva*, pure spiritual existence, and of it all the abodes of Vaikuṇṭha are made.

## 44
w: of six kinds  opulence  there  all  spiritual
w: of Saṅkarṣaṇa  the splendour  all  know  for certain
tr: The six kinds of opulence there are all spiritual; know for certain that all of them are the splendour of Saṅkarṣaṇa.

## 45
w: jīva  named  marginal called  one  potency  there is
w: Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa  of all  jīvas  the shelter
tr: There is a potency called *jīva*, known as the marginal (*taṭasthā*) potency; and Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of all jīvas.

## 46
w: from whom  of the universe  the origin  in whom  the dissolution
w: of that  Puruṣa  Saṅkarṣaṇa  the shelter
tr: Saṅkarṣaṇa is the shelter of the Puruṣa from whom the universe arises and into whom it dissolves.

## 47
w: of all  the shelter  all-wondrous  opulence  boundless
w: Ananta  to describe  cannot  the glory  whose
tr: He is the shelter of all, wondrous in every way, His opulence boundless; even Ananta cannot describe His glory.

## 48
w: transcendent  pure sattva  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named
w: He  whose  part  that  Nityānanda-Rāma
tr: The transcendent Saṅkarṣaṇa, pure spiritual existence (*viśuddha-sattva*), is a part of Nityānanda-Rāma.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the text reads *aṁśa* (“part”), the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* *aṅga*; both readings mean the same. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa is spiritual *viśuddha-sattva*; He is the *aṅga*, that is, the *prakāśa*, of Nityānanda-Rāma.

## 49
w: of the eighth  verse  I gave  a brief  explanation
w: of the ninth  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  the mind
tr: Thus I have briefly explained the eighth verse. Now hear attentively the meaning of the ninth.

## 50
w: māyā’s  master  of universes  multitudes  shelter  whose body
w: lies  in person  of the Causal  Ocean  in the midst
w: whose  one part  the Puruṣa  the original  Lord
w: to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
tr: “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of Him is the original Lord, the Puruṣa, the master of māyā, who lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean, His body the shelter of countless universes.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.9.

## 51
w: Vaikuṇṭha  outside  which  luminous  abode
w: of it  outside  Kāraṇārṇava  named
tr: Outside Vaikuṇṭha is the luminous abode, and beyond it lies what is called the Kāraṇārṇava, the Causal Ocean.

## 52
w: Vaikuṇṭha  encircling  one  there is  an ocean
w: endless  shoreless  its  there is no  limit
tr: Encircling Vaikuṇṭha is an ocean, endless and shoreless; it has no bound.

## 53
w: of Vaikuṇṭha  earth  and the rest  all  spiritual
w: material  of the elements  there  birth  not  is
tr: In Vaikuṇṭha earth and all the elements are spiritual; material elements never arise there.

## 54
w: spiritual  water  that  the supreme  cause
w: of which  one  drop  the Gaṅgā  of the fallen  the purifier
tr: That spiritual water is the supreme cause; a single drop of it is the Gaṅgā, purifier of the fallen.

## 55
w: in that  very  Causal Ocean  that  Saṅkarṣaṇa
w: His own  in one  part  He makes  lying down
tr: In that Causal Ocean the same Saṅkarṣaṇa lies in one part of Himself.

## 56
w: of the mahat  the creator  Puruṣa  He  of the world  the cause
w: the first  avatāra  makes  of māyā  the glance
tr: He is the Puruṣa, creator of the *mahat-tattva*, the cause of the world. This first avatāra casts His glance upon māyā.

## 57
w: māyā  potency  remains  the Causal Ocean  outside
w: the Causal  Ocean  māyā  to touch  cannot
tr: The potency māyā stays outside the Causal Ocean; māyā cannot touch its waters.

## 58
w: of that  very  māyā  twofold  state
w: of the world  the material cause  pradhāna  prakṛti
tr: That māyā exists in two ways; as the material cause of the world it is called *pradhāna* or *prakṛti*.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* and *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the external potency has two parts. The efficient cause (*nimitta*), the guṇas, is called māyā; the material cause (*upādāna*) is *pradhāna*, which is also called *prakṛti* (the first is spoken of in 5.62).

## 59
w: of the world  the cause  is not  prakṛti  inert  in nature
w: potency  infusing  into her  Kṛṣṇa  shows  mercy
tr: Inert prakṛti is not the cause of the world; Kṛṣṇa shows her mercy by infusing her with His potency.

## 60
w: by Kṛṣṇa’s  potency  prakṛti  becomes  the secondary  cause
w: by fire’s  power  iron  as  does  burning
tr: By Kṛṣṇa’s potency prakṛti becomes a secondary cause, just as iron burns by the power of fire.

## 61
w: therefore  Kṛṣṇa  the original  of the world  cause
w: prakṛti  the cause  as  of a goat  on the neck  the teats
tr: Therefore Kṛṣṇa is the original cause of the world. To call prakṛti the cause is like taking the teats on a goat’s neck for an udder.

## 62
w: in the māyā  aspect  I call  her  the efficient  cause
w: even that  is not so  because  the agent  cause  Nārāyaṇa
tr: In her aspect as māyā I call her the efficient cause of the world; but even that is not so, for the true maker and cause is Nārāyaṇa.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Nārāyaṇa is the principal efficient cause of the world, as the potter is of the pot; māyā is a secondary cause, like the potter’s wheel and stick (5.63–5.64).

## 63
w: of the pot  efficient  cause  as  the potter
w: so  of the world  the maker  the Puruṣa-avatāra
tr: As the potter is the efficient cause of the pot, so the Puruṣa-avatāra is the maker of the world.

## 64
w: Kṛṣṇa  the maker  māyā  to Him  gives  help
w: of the pot  cause  wheel  stick  and other  instruments
tr: Kṛṣṇa is the maker, and māyā only helps Him, as the wheel, the stick and other tools are causes of the pot.

## 65
w: from afar  the Puruṣa  makes  on māyā  a glance
w: in the form of jīvas  the seed  into her  He makes  placing
tr: From afar the Puruṣa glances upon māyā and places in her the seed — the jīvas.

## 66
w: by one  semblance of His body  makes  with māyā  union
w: from māyā  are born  then  of universes  the multitudes
tr: By a mere semblance of His body He unites with māyā, and from māyā the hosts of universes are then born.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *aṅgābhāsa* is only a semblance of bodily contact, not a real union with māyā.

## 67
w: countless  endless  as many  universes’  arrangements
w: in so many  forms  the Puruṣa  makes  into all  entrance
tr: Into all those countless, endless universes the Puruṣa enters, taking as many forms.
