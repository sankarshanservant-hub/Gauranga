## 0
tr: A Brief Description of the Pastimes of Young Manhood

## 1
w: I worship  whose acts are free and wondrous  I  Him
w: Caitanya  by whose  grace
w: the yavanas  behave like the good
w: the names of Kṛṣṇa  chanting aloud
tr: “I worship Śrī Caitanya, whose deeds are free and wondrous. By His grace even the yavanas, chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa, become saintly.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *yavanas* are the mlecchas, those outside Vedic society; devoted to chanting the name, they behave like the good (*sumanasaḥ*). The Lord’s deeds are free (*svaira*) and wondrous — not of this world, and beyond the rules of the smārtas.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: of the youthful  pastimes  an outline  I have made  count
w: of young manhood  of the pastimes  an outline  I make  in order
tr: I have given an outline of the pastimes of His youth; now I shall outline, in order, the pastimes of His young manhood.

## 4
w: with learning  beauty  fine attire
w: enjoyment  dancing and kīrtana
w: love and the Name  by giving  and
w: Gaura  plays  in young manhood
tr: “In His young manhood Gaura shone in His play: in learning, beauty and fine attire and their enjoyment, in dancing and kīrtana, and in giving away love and the holy name.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: young manhood — once the fifteenth year was past; learning — knowledge of the supreme truth; enjoyment — of garments, garlands, sandal paste and the like; the Lord gave the name of Kṛṣṇa together with love (prema).

## 5
w: of young manhood  on the entry  of the body  the limbs  adorned
w: splendid  garments  splendid  dress  garlands and sandal
tr: As He entered young manhood, ornaments graced every limb of His body, and He wore splendid garments and attire, garlands and sandal paste.

## 6
w: of learning  in the pride  anyone  not  does  regard
w: all  the paṇḍitas  defeating  does  teaching
tr: In the pride of His learning He took no account of anyone; He defeated all the paṇḍitas and began to teach.

## 7
w: of a disorder of vāyu  under the pretext  made  love  manifest
w: the devotees  taking with Him  made  various  pastimes
tr: Under the pretext of a disorder of the vital airs (*vāyu*), He revealed His love, and with the devotees He enjoyed many pastimes.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: having finished His studies and His teaching, Gauracandra, in order to preach pure devotion, for a time feigned this illness; everywhere He explained the name of Kṛṣṇa to His pupils, showed Kṛṣṇa in every rule of grammar, and tried to turn them away from study. *Anubhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 12.

## 8
w: then  indeed  made  the Lord  to Gayā  the journey
w: Īśvara Purī  with  there  the meeting
tr: Then the Lord went to Gayā, and there He met Īśvara Purī.

## 9
w: initiation  after  there was  of love  the manifestation
w: to His country  return  again  of love  the play
tr: After His initiation His love was revealed, and when He came home again, He gave Himself to the play of love.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu went to Gayā with His pupils to offer śrāddha for His father. On the way He fell ill with fever and was cured by drinking the water that had washed the feet of brāhmaṇas, showing householders that brāhmaṇas must be honoured. In Gayā He received initiation into the Kṛṣṇa mantra from Śrī Īśvara Purī, and from then on His prema began to show itself; returning to Navadvīpa, He began to preach it.

## 10
w: to Śacī  the gift of love  then  with Advaita  meeting
w: Advaita  obtained  of the universal form  the vision
tr: He gave love to Śacī; then He met Advaita, and Advaita beheld His universal form.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mother Śacī had offended Śrī Advaita Ācārya, and Mahāprabhu said that until Advaita forgave her she would not receive prema. When Advaita came, Śacī took the dust of his feet and was forgiven. At that time Advaita asked to see the universal form once shown to Arjuna, and the Lord granted his prayer.

## 11
w: the Lord’s  abhiṣeka  then  performed  Śrīvāsa
w: on the couch  seated  the Lord  made  majesty  manifest
tr: Then Śrīvāsa performed the Lord’s abhiṣeka, and the Lord, seated on the couch, revealed His majesty.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the devotees bathed the Lord in Śrīvāsa’s house; Mahāprabhu sat on the couch of Viṣṇu (*viṣṇu-khaṭṭā*) and revealed the majesty of the King of kings; Advaita and others worshipped Him, and He granted each the boon he desired.

## 12
w: then  of Nityānanda Svarūpa  the arrival
w: the Lord  meeting  obtained  of the six-armed form  the vision
tr: Then Nityānanda Svarūpa came, and when He met the Lord, He was granted the vision of the six-armed form.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śrī Nityānanda Prabhu was born in the village of Ekacakrā in Bīrbhūm, the son of Hāḍāi Paṇḍita and Padmāvatī. While still a boy He left with a sannyāsī, travelled through many lands and lived long in Mathurā; drawn by Mahāprabhu, He came to Navadvīpa and stayed in the house of Nandana Ācārya, from where the Lord and His devotees brought Him to their own place.

## 13
w: first  the six-armed form  to Him  showed  the Lord
w: conch, disc, club, lotus,  Śārṅga bow and flute  holding
tr: First the Lord showed Him the six-armed form, holding conch, disc, club and lotus, the Śārṅga bow and the flute.

## 14
w: afterwards  four-armed  He became  in three places  body  bent
w: in two  hands  the flute  He plays  in two  hands  conch and disc
tr: Then He became four-armed, His body bent in three places; with two hands He played the flute, and in the other two He held the conch and disc.

## 15
w: then  indeed  two-armed  only  the flute at His lips
w: dark  body  yellow  garment  the son of Vraja’s king
tr: And then He was simply two-armed, with the flute at His lips: the son of Vraja’s king, dark of body and dressed in yellow.

## 16
w: then  of Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Vyāsa-pūjā
w: in the mood of Nityānanda  made  the club  holding
tr: Then Nityānanda Gosāñi performed the Vyāsa-pūjā, and the Lord, absorbed in the mood of Nityānanda, took up the club (*muṣala*).
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the night before the Vyāsa-pūjā, during kīrtana in Śrīvāsa’s house, Mahāprabhu, in the mood of Balarāma, sat on the couch of Viṣṇu and asked Nityānanda for the plough and the club, and the devotees saw them in His hands.

## 17
w: then  Śacī  saw  Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  two  brothers
w: then  delivered  the Lord  Jagāi and Mādhāi
tr: Then Śacī saw the two brothers as Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, and then the Lord delivered Jagāi and Mādhāi.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śacī saw this in a dream: Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma, the two Deities of her house, were snatching the offerings with Gaurāṅga and Nityānanda. The next day, as Viśvambhara and Nityānanda were eating in her house, she saw before her eyes that they were Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma.

## 18
w: then  seven  praharas  remained  the Lord  in ecstatic absorption
w: wherever  the devotees  saw  in a special way
tr: Then for seven praharas the Lord remained absorbed in ecstasy, and the devotees, wherever they were, beheld Him in a special way.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on that day Mahāprabhu sat on the couch of Viṣṇu in Śrīvāsa’s house; the devotees bathed Him with Ganges water, reciting the *Puruṣa-sūkta*, and offered Him many foods. For seven praharas He showed the moods of all the avatāras, revealed to the devotees the secrets of their past, removed their doubts and granted boons. This is called the *sāta-prahariyā bhāva*, “the mood of seven praharas”, or the *mahā-prakāśa*, “the great revelation”.

## 19
w: of Varāha  absorption  He had  in Murāri’s  house
w: his  on the shoulders  climbing  the Lord  danced  in the courtyard
tr: In Murāri’s house He became absorbed in the mood of Varāha, and the Lord climbed onto Murāri’s shoulders and danced in the courtyard.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: crying “Boar! Boar!”, the Lord entered Murāri Gupta’s house in the form of Varāha, lifted a water pot with His teeth, as Varāha lifted the earth, and drank. On another day He climbed onto Murāri’s shoulders and danced for a long time.

## 20
w: then  Śuklāmbara’s  made  rice  eating
w: ‘harer nāma’  of the verse  made  meaning  explanation
tr: Then He ate the raw rice that Śuklāmbara had begged, and He explained the meaning of the verse *harer nāma*.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śuklāmbara Brahmacārī lived in Navadvīpa on the bank of the Ganges. Once, while the Lord was dancing, he came with his bag of begged rice, and Mahāprabhu, out of love for His devotee, took the rice from his bag and ate it with great love.

## 21
w: of Hari  the name  of Hari  the name
w: of Hari  the name  indeed  only
w: in Kali  there is not  indeed  there is not  indeed
w: there is not  indeed  way  other
tr: “The name of Hari, the name of Hari, the name of Hari alone! In the age of Kali there is no other way, there is no other way, there is no other way.” (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa, 38.126)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 7.76.

## 22
w: in the age of Kali  in the form of the Name  Kṛṣṇa’s  avatāra
w: the Name  from  comes  of the whole world  deliverance
tr: In the age of Kali, Kṛṣṇa descends in the form of the holy name, and through the name the whole world is delivered.

## 23
w: firmness  for the sake of  ‘harer nāma’  the words  three times
w: dull  people  to make understand  again  the word ‘eva’
tr: For firmness the words *harer nāma* are said three times, and the word *eva* is added again to make dull people understand.

## 24
w: ‘kevala’  by the word  once again  certainty  is made
w: knowledge, yoga, austerity  and the rest  karma  exclusion
tr: The word *kevala* (“alone”) makes it certain once more, and rules out knowledge, yoga, austerity, karma and all the rest.

## 25
w: otherwise  who  holds  for him  there is no  deliverance
w: no, no, no  three times  said  the word ‘eva’
tr: Whoever thinks otherwise has no deliverance: that is why “there is not” is said three times with *eva*.

## 26
w: grass  than  lower  being  always  shall take  the Name
w: oneself  free from pride  to others  shall give  honour
tr: Thinking oneself lower than the grass, one should always chant the name, seeking no honour for oneself and giving honour to others.

## 27
w: like a tree  tolerance  the Vaiṣṇava  shall practise
w: when scolded or beaten  to anyone  anything  not  shall say
tr: A Vaiṣṇava should be as tolerant as a tree; scolded or beaten, he should say nothing to anyone.

## 28
w: even when cut  a tree  just as  anything  not  says
w: drying up  it dies  yet  water  not  asks
tr: A tree says nothing even when it is cut down; it may wither and die, yet it never asks for water.

## 29
w: in this way  the Vaiṣṇava  of anyone  anything  not  shall ask
w: living on what comes unasked  or  greens and fruit  shall eat
tr: So a Vaiṣṇava should ask nothing of anyone; he should live on what comes unasked, or eat greens and fruit.

## 30
w: always  the Name  shall take  with whatever comes  content
w: such  conduct  makes  of bhakti-dharma  nourishment
tr: He should always chant the name and be content with whatever comes. Such conduct nourishes the dharma of devotion.

## 31
w: than grass  even  lower
w: a tree  like  tolerant
w: free from pride  giving honour
w: to be glorified  always  Hari
tr: “Lower than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, seeking no honour and offering honour to others, one should always sing the glories of Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 3)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: lower than grass — lower in every way, free of worldly pride; more tolerant even than a tree; seeking no honour — giving up worldly honour even when one deserves it; offering honour — to everyone, even the unworthy. With these qualities one should always, at every time, sing the name of Hari with lips and tongue.
