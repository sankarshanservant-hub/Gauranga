## 1

tr: “I worship Śrī Caitanya, whose deeds are free and wondrous. By His grace even the yavanas, chanting the names of Kṛṣṇa, become saintly.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *yavanas* are the mlecchas, those outside Vedic society; devoted to chanting the name, they behave like the good (*sumanasaḥ*). The Lord’s deeds are free (*svaira*) and wondrous — not of this world, and beyond the rules of the smārtas.

w: of young manhood  of the pastimes  an outline  I make  in order
tr: I have given an outline of the pastimes of His youth; now I shall outline, in order, the pastimes of His young manhood.

## 4

w: love and the Name  by giving  and

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: young manhood — once the fifteenth year was past; learning — knowledge of the supreme truth; enjoyment — of garments, garlands, sandal paste and the like; the Lord gave the name of Kṛṣṇa together with love (prema).

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: having finished His studies and His teaching, Gauracandra, in order to preach pure devotion, for a time feigned this illness; everywhere He explained the name of Kṛṣṇa to His pupils, showed Kṛṣṇa in every rule of grammar, and tried to turn them away from study. *Anubhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 12.

## 9

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu went to Gayā with His pupils to offer śrāddha for His father. On the way He fell ill with fever and was cured by drinking the water that had washed the feet of brāhmaṇas, showing householders that brāhmaṇas must be honoured. In Gayā He received initiation into the Kṛṣṇa mantra from Śrī Īśvara Purī, and from then on His prema began to show itself; returning to Navadvīpa, He began to preach it.

## 10

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Mother Śacī had offended Śrī Advaita Ācārya, and Mahāprabhu said that until Advaita forgave her she would not receive prema. When Advaita came, Śacī took the dust of his feet and was forgiven. At that time Advaita asked to see the universal form once shown to Arjuna, and the Lord granted his prayer.

## 12

While still a boy He left with a sannyāsī, travelled through many lands and lived long in Mathurā; drawn by Mahāprabhu, He came to Navadvīpa and stayed in the house of Nandana Ācārya, from where the Lord and His devotees brought Him to their own place.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the night before the Vyāsa-pūjā, during kīrtana in Śrīvāsa’s house, Mahāprabhu, in the mood of Balarāma, sat on the couch of Viṣṇu and asked Nityānanda for the plough and the club, and the devotees saw them in His hands.

## 17

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śacī saw this in a dream: Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma, the two Deities of her house, were snatching the offerings with Gaurāṅga and Nityānanda. The next day, as Viśvambhara and Nityānanda were eating in her house, she saw before her eyes that they were Kṛṣṇa and Balarāma.

## 18
w: then  seven  praharas  remained  the Lord  in ecstatic absorption
w: wherever  the devotees  saw  in a special way
tr: Then for seven praharas the Lord remained absorbed in ecstasy, and the devotees, wherever they were, beheld Him in a special way.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on that day Mahāprabhu sat on the couch of Viṣṇu in Śrīvāsa’s house; the devotees bathed Him with Ganges water, reciting the *Puruṣa-sūkta*, and offered Him many foods. For seven praharas He showed the moods of all the avatāras, revealed to the devotees the secrets of their past, removed their doubts and granted boons. This is called the *sāta-prahariyā bhāva*, “the mood of seven praharas”, or the *mahā-prakāśa*, “the great revelation”.

## 31

tr: “Lower than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, seeking no honour and offering honour to others, one should always sing the glories of Hari.” (Śikṣāṣṭaka, 3)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: lower than grass — lower in every way, free of worldly pride; more tolerant even than a tree; seeking no honour — giving up worldly honour even when one deserves it; offering honour — to everyone, even the unworthy. With these qualities one should always, at every time, sing the name of Hari with lips and tongue.

## 32

## 33
w: by the Lord’s order  do  this  verse  practise
w: surely  you will attain  then  Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
tr: Live by this verse, as the Lord has ordered, and you will surely attain the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the thread is the holy name, and what is strung on it are the four qualities of this verse: humility, tolerance, freedom from pride and honouring others. With worldly pride one cannot chant constantly; free of these four kinds of pride, the pure soul can always chant, and by obeying this command one surely attains the feet of Kṛṣṇa. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: one who chants without this qualification gets only a semblance of the name (*nāmābhāsa*) or commits an offence against it (*nāmāparādha*), and does not attain its fruit, love for Kṛṣṇa.

The Lord said He would not soon be pleased with him, for among devotees Mukunda spoke of pure devotion, while among Māyāvādīs he accepted the Māyāvāda of the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha*. Hearing this, Mukunda said, “I am fortunate, for some day He will surely show me mercy!” Seeing how firmly Mukunda had given up the company of Māyāvādīs, the Lord at once called him in, punished him for that association, and gave him His grace — the fruit of the company of pure devotees.

## 68

Grieved by this, Advaita went to Śāntipura and began to explain the path of knowledge to some unfortunate people, in order to be punished. Hearing of it, the Lord came to Śāntipura in anger and gave Him a sound beating. Advaita began to dance: “Today my wish is fulfilled! Mahāprabhu grudgingly honoured me as His guru; today He has treated me as His servant and disciple and saved me from the evil of Māyāvāda.” Seeing this, the Lord was abashed and was pleased with Him. *Anubhāṣya*: this event is not in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*.

tr: Hearing from Murāri Gupta the glories of Rāma, the Lord wrote the name “Rāmadāsa” on his forehead.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord asked Murāri Gupta, a worshipper of Rāma, to recite a hymn to Śrī Rāma, and Murāri recited the *Rāmāṣṭaka* with great love. Then the Lord wrote on his forehead: “By My grace, be Rāmadāsa.” *Anubhāṣya*: according to the *Caitanya-maṅgala*, the Lord placed His foot on Murāri’s head and showed him His form as Rāma.

## 70
w: Śrīdhara’s  from the iron pot  made  water drinking
w: all  to the devotees  gave  desired  boons
tr: He drank water from Śrīdhara’s iron pot, and to all the devotees He granted the boons they desired.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the night of the first city kīrtana, after the Kazi had been delivered, Chand Kazi came with the kīrtana as far as Śrīdhara’s courtyard. There, when the kīrtana stopped to rest, Mahāprabhu mercifully drank the water from Śrīdhara’s cracked iron pot, calling it “water offered by a devotee”. The place, to the north-east of Māyāpura, is still called the resting place of the kīrtana.

## 71
w: Haridāsa Ṭhākura  showed  grace
w: at the Ācārya’s place  of His mother  removed  the offence
tr: He showed His grace to Haridāsa Ṭhākura, and freed His mother from her offence against the Ācārya.
fn: The edition’s heading calls this “the enactment (*abhinaya*) of freeing Śacī from offence”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: on the day of the *mahā-prakāśa* Mahāprabhu embraced Haridāsa, declared him an incarnation of Prahlāda and granted him a boon. Mother Śacī had blamed Advaita Ācārya for Viśvarūpa’s sannyāsa; Mahāprabhu freed her from this offence against a Vaiṣṇava by having her take the dust of the Ācārya’s feet.

## 72
w: to the devotees  the Lord  of the Name  the glory  told
w: hearing  a student  there  explaining it away  made
tr: The Lord told the devotees of the glory of the holy name, and a student who heard it explained it away as mere praise (*arthavāda*).

## 73
w: of the Name  exaggerated praise  hearing  of the Lord  was  pain
w: to all  He forbade  his  not  you shall see  face
tr: Hearing the name called an exaggeration, the Lord was pained, and He forbade everyone to look upon that man’s face.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: to think the glory of the holy name, which is not different from Kṛṣṇa, an exaggeration (*arthavāda*) — something unreal — is sheer unbelief, opposition to the Lord. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this is an offence against the name, and no offence is more terrible; on seeing the face of one who offends the name, one should bathe in one’s clothes.

## 74
w: with His associates  in their clothes  going  made  bath in the Ganges
w: of bhakti  the glory  there  made  explanation
tr: Then He went with His companions and bathed in the Ganges in His clothes, and there He explained the glory of devotion.

## 75
w: by knowledge, karma, yoga, dharma  not  Kṛṣṇa  controlled
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s control  the cause  one  the rasa of love for Kṛṣṇa
tr: Kṛṣṇa is not brought under control by knowledge, karma, yoga or dharma; the one thing that holds Him is the rasa of love for Him.

## 76
w: not  wins  Me  yoga
w: not  sāṅkhya  dharma  O Uddhava
w: not  Vedic study  austerity  renunciation
w: as  devotion  to Me  intense
tr: (Śrī Kṛṣṇa said:) “O Uddhava, neither yoga nor sāṅkhya, neither dharma nor study of the Vedas, neither austerity nor renunciation hold sway over Me as intense devotion to Me does.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.20)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: yoga — restraint, observances, posture, breath control and the rest; sāṅkhya — the enumeration of the elements of existence taught by Kapila (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: knowledge that identifies the self with Brahman); dharma — varṇāśrama-dharma; renunciation — sannyāsa.

## 77
w: to Murāri  says  the Lord  Kṛṣṇa  under control  you have made
w: hearing  Murāri  a verse  to recite  began
tr: The Lord said to Murāri, “You have brought Kṛṣṇa under your control,” and hearing this, Murāri began to recite a verse:
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu told Murāri, “By your loving devotion you have brought Kṛṣṇa under your control,” and Murāri replied with the verse of the brāhmaṇa Sudāmā. His humble meaning was: “I have no power to control Kṛṣṇa; it is Kṛṣṇa who, out of love for His devotees, makes an unworthy servant fortunate.”

## 78
w: where  I  poor  most sinful
w: where  Kṛṣṇa  the abode of Śrī
w: a brāhmaṇa by birth only  thus  indeed  I
w: with both arms  was embraced
tr: “Who am I, a poor and sinful man, and who is Kṛṣṇa, the abode of the goddess of fortune? Yet because I was born a brāhmaṇa, He embraced me in His arms!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.81.16)
fn: The edition’s heading gives *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 10.81.14 (a different numbering of the chapter’s verses). *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī*): Sudāmā marvels that Kṛṣṇa embraced him only because he was born a brāhmaṇa, not out of friendship, of which he thought himself unworthy; thus the Lord’s reverence for brāhmaṇas is glorified.
