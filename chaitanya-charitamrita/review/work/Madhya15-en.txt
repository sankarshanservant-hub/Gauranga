## 0
tr: The Feast at Sārvabhauma’s House

## 1
w: in the house of Sārvabhauma  eating
w: His own reviler  Amogha
w: accepting  manifest  made
w: Gaura  His own  subjection to His devotees
tr: While taking His meal at Sārvabhauma’s house, Gaura accepted Amogha, who had reviled Him, and so made plain that He is subject to His devotees.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Amogha was the husband of Ṣaṣṭhī, Sārvabhauma’s daughter; “accepted” — counted him among His servants. Heading in the edition: “Gaurasundara makes His own Amogha, who had reviled Him.”

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Madhya 10.2 (= Ādi 4.2); translation as there.

## 3
w: glory  to the listeners of the Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta
w: the Caitanya-caritāmṛta  —  whose  treasure of life
tr: Glory to the listeners of the *Śrī Caitanya-caritāmṛta*, for whom the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* is the treasure of their lives!

## 4
w: thus  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees
w: in Nīlācala  staying  does  dancing and singing  joyfully
tr: In this way Mahāprabhu stayed in Nīlācala with His devotees, joyfully dancing and singing.

## 5
w: in the first year  Jagannātha  seeing
w: dancing and singing  does  prostrations  obeisances  prayers
tr: In that first year He went to see Jagannātha; He danced and sang, fell prostrate, bowed down and offered prayers.

## 6
w: the upala-bhoga  when it begins  makes  outside  going forth
w: Haridāsa  meeting  comes  to His own dwelling
tr: When the *upala-bhoga* began, He would come out of the temple, meet Haridāsa, and return to His own dwelling.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *upala-bhoga* is offered at midday in the *bhoga-vardhana* hall. Before leaving the temple, the Lord would stand behind the Garuḍa pillar, fall prostrate and offer prayers; on the way back He met Haridāsa Ṭhākura at Siddha-bakula and returned to His dwelling, the house of Kāśī Miśra.

## 7
w: at home  sitting  does  the Lord  the saṅkīrtana of the name
w: Advaita  coming  does  the Lord’s  worship
tr: Sitting at home, the Lord would chant the holy name, and Advaita would come and worship Him.

## 8
w: with fragrant water  offers  water for the feet  water for sipping
w: over all the limbs  smears  of the Lord  fragrant  sandalwood
tr: Advaita offered Him fragrant water to wash His feet and to sip, and smeared fragrant sandalwood paste over all His limbs.

## 9
w: on the neck  a garland  puts  on the head  placed  tulasī buds
w: joined  with hands  prayers  offers  at the feet  bowing
tr: He put a garland around the Lord’s neck and placed *tulasī* buds on His head; then He bowed at His feet and offered prayers with joined palms.

## 10
w: on the worship plate  of flowers and tulasī  remnant  that  was
w: that  all  taking  the Lord  the Ācārya  worshipped
tr: Taking whatever flowers and *tulasī* were left on the worship plate, the Lord worshipped the Ācārya with them.

## 11
w: who  You are  that  You are  obeisance  be  to You  this  mantra  recites
w: mouth-music  making  the Lord  makes laugh  the Ācārya
tr: He recited the *mantra* “Whoever You are, that You are — obeisance to You,” and, making funny sounds with His mouth, set the Ācārya laughing.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: some give the *mantra* in a fuller form: “O Rādhā, O Kṛṣṇa, O Rāma, O Viṣṇu, O Sītā, O Rāma, O Śiva, O Śiva! Whoever You are, that You are — I always bow to You; whoever You are, that You are — obeisance to You.”

## 12
w: thus  to one another  make  obeisance
w: the Lord  invitation  makes  the Ācārya  time  after time
tr: So They paid obeisance to one another, and again and again the Ācārya invited the Lord to a meal.

## 13
w: the Ācārya’s  invitation  —  a wonderful story
w: in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa
tr: The story of the Ācārya’s invitations is wonderful, and Vṛndāvana dāsa has described it in detail.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see the *Śrī Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya-khaṇḍa, chapter 9.

## 14
w: repetition  would be  it  not  I made  description
w: other  devotees  make  to the Lord  invitation
tr: To tell it again would be repetition, so I have not described it. The other devotees also invited the Lord.

## 15
w: one  by one  day  in one  after another  devotee’s home  a festival
w: with the Lord  there  eating  do  devotees  all
tr: Day after day there was a festival in one devotee’s home after another, and all the devotees ate there with the Lord.

## 16
w: four months  stayed  all  with Mahāprabhu
w: of Jagannātha  various  festivals  see  with great joy
tr: For four months all the devotees stayed with Mahāprabhu and watched Jagannātha’s various festivals with great joy.

## 17
w: on the day of the festival of Kṛṣṇa’s birth  Nanda’s great festival
w: in cowherd dress  became  the Lord  taking  all the devotees
tr: On the day of Kṛṣṇa’s birth festival, at Nanda’s great celebration, the Lord and all His devotees dressed as cowherds.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the day of Kṛṣṇa’s birth festival here is the day after Janmāṣṭamī, the day of the Nandotsava.

## 18
w: a yoke of yogurt and milk  the Lord  on His own shoulder  placing
w: to the festival place  came  saying  “Hari”  “Hari”
tr: Carrying a yoke of yogurt and milk on His shoulder, the Lord came to the festival grounds calling out, “Hari! Hari!”

## 19
w: Kānāi Khuṭiyā  was  Nanda’s dress  wearing
w: Jagannātha Māhāti  had become  the Queen of Vraja
tr: Kānāi Khuṭiyā was dressed as Nanda, and Jagannātha Māhāti had become Yaśodā, the Queen of Vraja.

## 20
w: himself  Pratāparudra  and  Kāśī Miśra
w: Sārvabhauma  and  the paḍichā-pātra  Tulasī
tr: King Pratāparudra himself was there, with Kāśī Miśra, Sārvabhauma and Tulasī, the temple *paḍichā*.

## 21
w: them all  taking  the Lord  does  joyful dancing
w: yogurt and milk  with turmeric water  fills  everyone’s  body
tr: With them all the Lord danced joyfully, and everyone was drenched in yogurt, milk and turmeric water.

## 22
w: Advaita  says  —  truth  I speak  not  do  anger
w: the staff  to twirl  You can  then  I know  a cowherd
tr: Advaita said, “I am telling You the truth — don’t be angry. I will know You are a cowherd only if You can twirl a staff.”

## 23
w: then  the staff  taking  the Lord  to twirl  began
w: time  after time  into the sky  throwing  snatching  caught
tr: Then the Lord took a staff and began to twirl it, tossing it into the air again and again and catching it.

## 24
w: of the head  above  in front  behind  on both sides
w: between the feet  twirls  the staff  —  seeing  the people  laugh
tr: He twirled the staff over His head, in front, behind, on both sides and even between His feet, and the people laughed to see it.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *pāda-sandhye* — in the space between the feet.

## 25
w: a firebrand’s circle  like  the staff  twirls
w: seeing  in the hearts of all people  wonder  arises
tr: He whirled the staff like a circle of fire traced by a firebrand, and all who saw it were struck with wonder.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: a burning brand whirled very fast looks like an unbroken circle of fire; so fast did the Lord whirl the staff that it seemed to be everywhere at once.

## 26
w: in the same way  Nityānanda  twirls  a staff
w: who  will understand  that  of the two  the cowherd mood  hidden
tr: Nityānanda twirled a staff in the same way. Who can understand the hidden cowherd mood of these two?
fn: Heading in the edition: “Nitāi too whirls a staff and shows Himself a cowherd.”

## 27
w: by Pratāparudra’s  order  the paḍichā Tulasī
w: a prasāda cloth of Jagannātha  one  taking  coming
tr: At Pratāparudra’s order, Tulasī the *paḍichā* came, bringing a cloth that had been offered to Jagannātha.

## 28
w: costly  cloth  the Lord  on the head  tied
w: the Ācārya and others  the Lord’s  people  clothed
tr: The Lord tied the costly cloth around His head, and He had the Ācārya and His other companions wear it as well.

## 29
w: Kānāi Khuṭiyā  Jagannātha  —  the two
w: in ecstasy  gave away  at home  was  whatever  wealth
tr: Kānāi Khuṭiyā and Jagannātha, the two of them, in their ecstasy gave away all the wealth they had at home.

## 30
w: seeing  Mahāprabhu  great  satisfaction  felt
w: as mother and father  to both  obeisance  made
tr: Seeing this, Mahāprabhu was very pleased and bowed down to the two of them as to His mother and father.

## 31
w: in great ecstasy  the Lord  came  to His own home
w: thus  pastimes  performs  Gaurāṅga-sundara
tr: In deep ecstasy the Lord returned home. Such were the pastimes of Gaurāṅgasundara.

## 32
w: Vijayā-daśamī  —  of the conquest of Laṅkā  on the day
w: a monkey army  made  the Lord  taking  the devotees
tr: On Vijayā-daśamī, the day of the conquest of Laṅkā, the Lord made the devotees into an army of monkeys.
fn: Heading in the edition: “On Vijayā-daśamī the Lord dresses the devotees as an army of monkeys and Himself plays Hanumān.”

## 33
w: in the mood of Hanumān  the Lord  a tree branch  taking
w: on the fort of Laṅkā  climbing  throws down  Laṅkā  breaking
tr: Absorbed in the mood of Hanumān, the Lord took a tree branch, climbed the ramparts of Laṅkā and tore Laṅkā down.

## 34
w: where is  the wretch Rāvaṇa  the Lord  says  in a fit of anger
w: the Mother of the world  stole  the sinner  I will kill  with all his race
tr: In a fit of anger the Lord cried, “Where is that wretch Rāvaṇa? The sinner has carried off the Mother of the world! I will kill him and all his kin!”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Mother of the world is Sītā-devī.

## 35
w: of the Lord  the ecstasy  seeing  people  amazed
w: all people  “Jaya”  “Jaya”  make  time  after time
tr: Seeing the Lord’s ecstasy, the people were amazed, and all of them shouted “Jaya! Jaya!” again and again.

## 36
w: in this way  the Rāsa-yātrā  and  Dīpāvalī
w: the Utthāna-dvādaśī  festival  saw  all
tr: In the same way they all saw the Rāsa-yātrā, Dīpāvalī and the festival of Utthāna-dvādaśī.

## 37
w: one day  Mahāprabhu  Nityānanda  taking
w: the two  brothers  counsel  held  in seclusion  sitting
tr: One day Mahāprabhu took Nityānanda aside, and the two brothers sat together in seclusion and took counsel.

## 38
w: what  counsel  held  the two  anyone  not  knows
w: from the result  a guess  afterwards  made  the devotees
tr: What the two decided no one knew; only later could the devotees guess it from what followed.

## 39
w: then  Mahāprabhu  all  devotees  called
w: to Gauḍa  go  all  farewell  made
tr: Then Mahāprabhu called all the devotees and bade them farewell, telling them to go back to Gauḍa.

## 40
w: to all  said  —  every year  coming
w: Guṇḍicā  seeing  you will go  Me  meeting
tr: He said to them all, “Come every year: see the Guṇḍicā festival, meet with Me, and then go home.”
fn: Heading in the edition: “The Lord sends off all the devotees of Gauḍa, telling them to come to Him every year for the Guṇḍicā festival.”

## 41
w: to the Ācārya  order  gave  showing  respect
w: down to the caṇḍālas  and others  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  give  as a gift
tr: With respect He gave this order to the Ācārya: “Give devotion to Kṛṣṇa to everyone, down to the *caṇḍālas*.”

## 42
w: to Nityānanda  order  gave  —  go  to Gauḍa
w: unrestrained  loving devotion  do  manifest
tr: To Nityānanda He gave this order: “Go to Gauḍa and reveal loving devotion freely, without restraint.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *prākṛta-sahajiyās* claim that Mahāprabhu sent Nityānanda to Gauḍa to continue a family line; such an idea springs only from offence at His feet. Śrī Nityānanda is non-different from the Lord, the original source of all *viṣṇu-tattva*, and no authoritative book records any such order.

## 43
w: Rāmadāsa  Gadādhara  and others  some  persons
w: Your  help  for  I have given  You  with
tr: I am sending Rāmadāsa, Gadādhara and a few others along to help You.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Nitāi’s companions in preaching — Abhirāma and Gadādhara dāsa.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Gadādhara is Gadādhara dāsa of Eṇḍiyādaha.

## 44
w: from time  to time  I  You  near  will go
w: unseen  remaining  Your  dancing  I will see
tr: From time to time I will come to You, and, staying unseen, I will watch You dance.”
fn: The edition reads তোমার নিকটে (locative: “near You”); the Bengali text and transliteration are corrected accordingly (scan, p. 582).

## 45
w: Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita  the Lord  making  embrace
w: by the neck  holding  says  to him  sweet  words
tr: The Lord embraced Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and, with His arm around his neck, spoke sweet words to him:

## 46
w: in your  house  in the kīrtana  I  always  will dance
w: you  sight  will get  other  anyone  not  will see
tr: “Every day I will dance in the kīrtana at your house. You will see it, but no one else will.

## 47
w: this  cloth  to Mother  give  this  all  prasāda
w: prostration  making  My  have forgiven  offence
tr: Give this cloth and all this *prasāda* to My mother. Fall down before her and beg her to forgive My offence.

## 48
w: her  service  leaving  I  have taken  sannyāsa
w: dharma  it is not  do  I  My own dharma  destruction
tr: I gave up serving her and took *sannyāsa*. That is not *dharma*: I am destroying My own *dharma*.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: by taking *sannyāsa* the Lord failed in the *dharma* of serving His mother.

## 49
w: her  love  subject to  I am  her  service  —  dharma
w: that  leaving  I have done  of a madman  deed
tr: I am subject to her love, and serving her is My *dharma*. In giving that up I have acted like a madman.

## 50
w: mad  child’s  mother  not  takes  fault
w: this  knowing  Mother  with Me  not  is  angry
tr: A mother does not hold her mad child at fault. Knowing this, My mother is not angry with Me.

## 51
w: what  use  in sannyāsa  to Me  love  own wealth
w: at the time  sannyāsa  I took  deranged  became  mind
tr: What use is *sannyāsa* to Me? Love is My real wealth. When I took *sannyāsa*, My mind was deranged.

## 52
w: in Nīlācala  am  I  her  by order
w: from time  to time  I will come  her  feet  to see
tr: I stay in Nīlācala at her bidding, and from time to time I will come to see her feet.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The Lord promises that even now He will come to Māyāpura from time to time to see Śacī.”

## 53
w: always  going  see  I  her  feet
w: a vision  thinking  she  that  true  not  accepts
tr: Every day I go and see her feet, but she takes it for a vision and does not believe it is real.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The Lord has always visited Śacī, but by the power of His *māyā* Śacī doubts it.”

## 54–55
w: one day  śāli rice  vegetable dishes  five or seven
w: greens  banana-flower ghaṇṭa  fried  paṭolas with nīm leaves
w: lemon and ginger slices  yogurt  milk  sugar candy
w: to the śālagrāma  offered  many  gifts
tr: One day she offered the *śālagrāma* many gifts: *śāli* rice, five or seven vegetable dishes, greens, *ghaṇṭa* of banana flowers, fried *paṭolas* with *nīm* leaves, slices of lemon and ginger, yogurt, milk and sugar candy.

## 56
w: the prasāda  taking  in her lap  does  weeping
w: of Nimāi  favourites  my  —  all these  dishes
tr: She took the *prasāda* on her lap and wept: ‘All these dishes are my Nimāi’s favourites.

## 57
w: Nimāi  not  here  who  does  eating
w: on Me  in meditation  with tears  filled  eyes
tr: Nimāi is not here — who will eat them?’ As she thought of Me, her eyes filled with tears.

## 58
w: quickly  going  I  all  did  eating
w: the empty plate  seeing  tears  making  wiping
tr: I went at once and ate everything. Seeing the plate empty, she wiped away her tears.

## 59
w: who  rice and vegetables  ate  empty  why  the plate
w: Bāla-gopāla  perhaps  ate  all  the rice
tr: ‘Who has eaten the rice and vegetables? Why is the plate empty? Could Bāla-gopāla have eaten all the rice?

## 60
w: or  my  in the matter  in the mind  delusion  happened
w: or  some  animal  coming  everything  ate
tr: Or was my mind deluded about it? Or did some animal come and eat it all?

## 61
w: or  I  on the rice plate  by mistake  not  served
w: so  thinking  the cooking pots  going  looked at
tr: Or did I forget to serve the food on the plate?’ Thinking this, she went and looked into the cooking pots.

## 62
w: with rice and vegetables  full  seeing  all  the vessels
w: seeing  doubt  arose  some  wonder  in the mind
tr: She saw that all the pots were still full of rice and vegetables, and at the sight she was filled with doubt and wonder.

## 63
w: Īśāna  calling  again  the place  had cleaned
w: once more  to Gopāla  food  offered
tr: She called Īśāna, had the place cleaned and plastered again, and once more offered food to Gopāla.

## 64
w: thus  whenever  does  excellent  cooking
w: Me  to feed  does  in longing  weeping
tr: Whenever she cooks something excellent like this, she weeps with longing to feed Me.

## 65
w: her  by love  bringing  Me  makes  eat
w: within  happiness  feels  she  outwardly  not  accepts
tr: Her love draws Me there and makes Me eat. Within she feels happy, but outwardly she does not accept it.

## 66
w: on this  Vijayā-daśamī  happened  this  event
w: her  asking  to her  make  conviction
tr: This happened on this very Vijayā-daśamī. Ask her about it and convince her that it was real.”

## 67
w: so much  saying  the Lord  overwhelmed  became
w: the people  farewell  to make  the Lord  composure  held
tr: As He said this, the Lord was overwhelmed; but to send the devotees off He kept His composure.

## 68
w: to Rāghava Paṇḍita  speaks  words  tender
w: your  steadfast  by love  I  am  your  subject
tr: To Rāghava Paṇḍita He spoke tender words: “Your steadfast love has brought Me under your control.

## 69
w: his  of service to Kṛṣṇa  the account  hear  all people
w: supremely pure  service  most  excellent
tr: Hear, everyone, how he serves Kṛṣṇa. His service is supremely pure, the very best of all.

## 70
w: other  things  let be  hear  of the coconuts  the story
w: five  gaṇḍās  at  a coconut  sells  there
tr: Leaving aside his other offerings, hear about the coconuts. In those parts a coconut sells for five *gaṇḍās*.
fn: Money was counted in cowrie shells: a *gaṇḍā* is four cowries, so five *gaṇḍās* are twenty. Heading in the edition: “The wonderful variety of Rāghava’s coconut offerings.”

## 71
w: in his garden  how many  hundreds  on trees  lakhs  upon lakhs  fruits
w: still  he inquires  where  sweet  coconuts
tr: In his garden hundreds of trees bear fruit by the hundreds of thousands, yet he still asks where the sweetest coconuts are to be had.

## 72
w: for each  one  fruit’s  price  giving  four  four  paṇas
w: ten krośas  from  has brought  taking  pains
tr: He pays four *paṇas* for every single fruit and takes pains to have them brought from ten *krośas* away.
fn: A *paṇa* is twenty *gaṇḍās*, or eighty cowries: four *paṇas* are 320 cowries, sixteen times the usual price (verse 70). A *krośa* is about two miles.

## 73
w: every day  five or seven  fruits  husking
w: very cool  making  keeps  in water  immersing
tr: Every day he has five or seven fruits husked and keeps them immersed in water to make them very cool.

## 74
w: of the offering  at the time  again  trimming  preparing
w: to Kṛṣṇa  offering  makes  the top  a hole  making
tr: At the time of the offering he trims and prepares them again, cuts an opening in the top, and offers them to Kṛṣṇa.

## 75
w: Kṛṣṇa  that  coconut water  drinking  doing
w: sometimes  empty  the fruit  leaves  sometimes  with water  full
tr: Kṛṣṇa drinks the coconut water: sometimes He leaves the fruit empty, sometimes still full of water.

## 76
w: without water  the fruit  seeing  the Paṇḍita  —  delighted
w: the fruit  breaking  with the pulp  makes  the śata-pātra  filled
tr: When the Paṇḍita sees a fruit emptied of its water, he is delighted. He breaks it open and fills a plate with the pulp.
fn: The edition reads *śata-pātra* (literally “a hundred vessels”, or a vessel of that name); the commentaries do not explain the word, and it is rendered neutrally as “a plate”.

## 77
w: the pulp  offering  making  outside  meditation
w: the pulp  eating  Kṛṣṇa  makes  empty  the vessel
tr: He offers the pulp and meditates outside, and Kṛṣṇa eats it and leaves the vessel empty.

## 78
w: sometimes  the pulp  eating  again  the vessel  fills  with pulp
w: faith  grows  the Paṇḍita’s  in the ocean of love  floats
tr: Sometimes, having eaten it, Kṛṣṇa fills the vessel with pulp again. Then the Paṇḍita’s faith grows, and he floats in an ocean of love.

## 79
w: one  day  ten fruits  preparation  making
w: the offering  to set  a servant  came  bringing
tr: One day a servant prepared ten coconuts and brought them to be offered.

## 80
w: opportunity  not  is  delay  happened
w: with the plate of fruits in hand  the servant  at the door  just  stayed
tr: It was not yet time to offer them; there was a delay, so the servant waited at the door with the plate of fruits in his hand.

## 81
w: of the door  on the upper wall  he  hand  put
w: that  with hand  the fruits  touched  the Paṇḍita  saw
tr: He touched the wall above the door with his hand and then touched the fruits with the same hand. The Paṇḍita saw it.

## 82
w: the Paṇḍita  says  —  through the door  people  make  coming and going
w: their  foot-dust  flying up  sticks  on the upper  wall
tr: The Paṇḍita said, “People are always coming and going through this door. The dust from their feet flies up and settles on the wall above it.

## 83
w: that  on the wall  hand  putting  the fruits  you touched
w: for Kṛṣṇa fit  not  the fruits  impure  have become
tr: You put your hand on that wall and then touched the fruits. They are no longer fit for Kṛṣṇa — they have become impure.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Rāghava Paṇḍita was neither a man of *karma* afflicted with the worldly “disease of cleanliness” nor a novice devotee who “considers the earth worshipable” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.84.13). He was an eternally perfect servant of Kṛṣṇa, absorbed in transcendental service. Those whose devotion is mixed with *karma* do not prove their pure devotion by imitating his outward conduct and arguing about worldly purity and impurity (see Antya 4.174, 176; Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.28.4).

## 84
w: so  saying  the fruits  throws  the boundary wall  crossing
w: such  pure  loving service  the world  conquering
