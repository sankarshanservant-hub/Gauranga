tr: “He did not give hundreds of thousands, millions of eyes; he gave only two, and on them he put blinking lids. The Creator is a dull ascetic; his mind is void of taste (*rasa*), and he does not know how to create properly.

## 134
w: who  will see  Kṛṣṇa’s face  for him  makes  two eyes
w: the Creator  being  such  injustice
w: my  if  word  he takes  millions  of eyes  for him  he makes
w: then  I know  proper  creation  his
tr: For one who is to see Kṛṣṇa’s face he makes only two eyes — such injustice from the Creator! If he would take my advice and make millions of eyes for such a one, then I would know his creation to be proper.”

## 135
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s body  the sweetness  an ocean  the very sweet  face  the moon
w: the exceedingly sweet  smile  its fine rays
w: to these three  clung  the mind  in greed  makes  tasting
w: a verse  recites  His own hand  moving
tr: The sweetness of Kṛṣṇa’s body is an ocean, His very sweet face the moon, and His exceedingly sweet smile its fine rays. The Lord’s mind clung to these three; greedily He tasted them and, waving His hand, recited a verse.

## 136
w: sweet  sweet  the form  of this  Lord
w: sweet  sweet  the face  sweet
w: honey-fragrant  the gentle smile  this  ah
w: sweet  sweet  sweet  sweet
tr: “Sweet, sweet is the form of this Lord; sweet, sweet is His face, sweet indeed; and this gentle smile, fragrant with honey — ah, it is sweet, sweet, sweet, sweet!” (Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta, 92)

## 137
w: O Sanātana  Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  of nectar  an ocean
w: My  mind  delirious  all  to drink  makes  resolve
w: of ill fate  the physician  not  gives  one  drop
tr: O Sanātana, Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness is an ocean of nectar. My mind, delirious as in a fever (*sannipāta*), resolves to drink it all — but the physician, alas, My ill fate, will not give Me a single drop!

## 138
w: Kṛṣṇa’s body  of loveliness  a flood  than sweet  sweeter
w: on it  which  the face  the moon
w: than sweet  sweeter  than that  sweeter
w: His  which  smile  of moonlight  the fullness
tr: Kṛṣṇa’s body is a flood of loveliness, sweeter than sweetness itself. On it is His moonlike face, sweeter than sweet and sweeter still; and His smile is the full flood of that moon’s light.

## 139
w: than sweet  sweeter  than that  sweeter
w: than that  far  sweeter
w: by its own  one  particle  pervades  all  the three worlds
w: the ten directions  pervades  whose  flood
tr: Sweeter than sweet, sweeter still, and sweeter even than that, the smile pervades all the three worlds with a single particle of itself, and its flood fills the ten directions.

## 140
w: of the smile  the rays’  fine camphor  enters  the lips’ sweetness
w: that  honey  maddens  the three worlds
w: of the flute’s holes  into the sky  its  quality  into sound  enters
w: of sound  in the form  attaining  transformation
tr: The fine camphor of the rays of His smile enters the sweetness of His lips, and that honey maddens the three worlds. It enters the sky within the holes of the flute and passes into sound, the sky’s own quality, transformed into the form of melody.

## 141
w: that  sound  in all directions  runs  the universe  piercing  to Vaikuṇṭha  goes
w: by force  enters  of the world  the ears
w: all  maddened  making  by force  brings  seizing
w: especially  of young women  the host
tr: That sound runs in all directions; piercing the shell of the universe, it reaches Vaikuṇṭha and forces its way into the ears of the world. Maddening all, it seizes them and brings them by force — above all the young women.

## 142
w: the sound  very  insolent  of chaste wives  breaks  the vow
w: from the husband’s lap  pulling  brings
w: of Vaikuṇṭha  the Lakṣmīs  which  makes  attraction
w: of it  before  what to speak of  the gopīs
tr: That sound is most insolent: it breaks the vow of chaste wives and pulls them from their husbands’ laps. It attracts even the Lakṣmīs of Vaikuṇṭha — what then of the gopīs?

## 143
w: the waist-knot  loosens  before the husband  household duty  makes  give up
w: by force  seizing  brings  to Kṛṣṇa’s place
w: social duty  modesty  fear  all  awareness  lost  becomes
w: thus  makes dance  all  the women
tr: It loosens the knots of their skirts before their husbands and makes them give up their household duties; seizing them by force, it brings them to Kṛṣṇa. Social duty, modesty, fear — all awareness is lost. So it makes all the women dance.

## 144
w: of the ears  within  a dwelling  it makes  itself  there  always  sounds
w: other  sound  not  allows  to enter
w: other  talk  not  hear  the ears  one thing  to say  says  another
w: this  of Kṛṣṇa’s  flute  the conduct
tr: It makes its home within the ears and ever sounds there by itself, letting no other sound enter. The ears hear no other talk; meaning to say one thing, one says another. Such is the way of Kṛṣṇa’s flute.

## 145
w: again  says  in outer consciousness  other  to say  I said  other
w: Kṛṣṇa’s mercy  you  upon
w: My  bewilderment of mind  making  of His own majesty  the sweetness
w: through My  mouth  makes hear  you
tr: Then, returning to outer consciousness, the Lord said again, “Meaning to say one thing, I have said another. Kṛṣṇa’s mercy is upon you: bewildering My mind, He has made you hear the sweetness of His own majesty through My mouth.

## 146
w: I  indeed  a madman  other  to say  other  I say
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s  in the stream of sweetness  I  go  carried away
tr: I am a madman: meaning to say one thing, I say another. I am carried away in the stream of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness.”

## 147
w: then  Mahāprabhu  for a moment  silence  keeping  remains
w: the mind  one  making  again  to Sanātana  speaks
tr: Then Mahāprabhu remained silent for a moment; collecting His mind, He spoke to Sanātana again.

## 148
w: Kṛṣṇa’s  sweetness  and  from Mahāprabhu’s  mouth
w: this  whoever  hears  he  floats  in the happiness of love
tr: Whoever hears this — Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness from Mahāprabhu’s own mouth — floats in the happiness of love.

## 149
w: Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
w: “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
