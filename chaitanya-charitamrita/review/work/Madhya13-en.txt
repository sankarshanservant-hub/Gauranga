fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *dayitās* (from *dayita*, “beloved”) are a special class of Jagannātha’s servants. By birth they do not belong to the respected castes, but service to Jagannātha has brought them the honour of the higher *varṇas*. The *Kṣetra-māhātmya* calls them Śabaras: the Deity Nīla-mādhava, who later became Jagannātha, once dwelt among the Śabaras, and so they were given the right of intimate service.

## 79

fn: Heading in the edition: “Glory to the transcendental, ever-new Kāmadeva.” *Anubhāṣya*: *kāma-deva* here is transcendental loving desire, which He kindles with His beautiful smile; *deva* means “transcendental”: this *kāma* is His own self-luminous nature.
