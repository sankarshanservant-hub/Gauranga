## 248
w: countless  avatāras  of Kṛṣṇa  there is no  counting
w: of the branch and the moon  by the logic  I make  an indication
tr: Kṛṣṇa’s avatāras are countless; there is no counting them. As one shows the moon by pointing to a branch, I shall give only an indication.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: by the maxim of “the moon on the branch” one points to a branch of a tree to show where the moon is in the sky — it is only an indication of direction (*dig-darśana*). Though the avatāras can be seen by worldly eyes, they are not worldly: their pastimes are known only to the jīva who humbly follows them, never to worldly senses and logic.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Sūta Gosvāmī speaks to the sages headed by Śaunaka; *sattva-nidhi* is the shelter of all *sattva*, the treasure-house of pure *sattva*.
