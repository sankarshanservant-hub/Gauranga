tr: The sun moves night and day through the circle of the luminaries, crossing the seven islands and their oceans step by step.

## 386
w: night and day  amount to  of sixty daṇḍas  the measure
w: three thousand  six hundred  palas  its  measure
tr: A night and a day measure sixty *daṇḍas*, or three thousand six hundred *palas*.

## 387
w: from sunrise  sixty palas  of gradual rising
w: that  one  daṇḍa  in eight  daṇḍas  a prahara  is
tr: From sunrise the sun climbs step by step through sixty *palas*; that is one *daṇḍa*, and eight *daṇḍas* make a *prahara*.

## 388
w: one  two  three  four  in praharas  setting  occurs
w: four praharas  of night  having passed  again  sunrise
tr: Through one, two, three and four *praharas* it moves on to its setting, and when the four *praharas* of night have passed, the sun rises again.

## 389
w: likewise  Kṛṣṇa’s  of pastimes  the circle  in fourteen  manvantaras
w: of the universes  the circle  pervading  step  by step  revolves
tr: In the same way, over fourteen *manvantaras* the circle of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes revolves step by step through the circle of the universes.

## 390
w: a hundred and twenty-five  years  Kṛṣṇa’s  manifest  presence
w: there  as  in Vraja and the cities  He performed  pastimes
tr: Kṛṣṇa’s manifest presence lasts a hundred and twenty-five years, and during it He enjoys His pastimes in Vraja and in the cities, just as He did here.

## 391
w: a whirling firebrand  like  that  of pastimes  the wheel  revolves
w: all  pastimes  in all  universes  in turn  rising  make
tr: Like a whirling firebrand, that wheel of pastimes revolves, and all the pastimes rise in turn in all the universes.

## 392
w: birth  childhood  boyhood  the manifestation of youth
w: Pūtanā’s killing  beginning with  ending with the Mauṣala  the pastimes
tr: Birth, childhood, boyhood and the manifestation of youth — pastimes beginning with the killing of Pūtanā and ending with the Mauṣala episode.

## 393
w: in some  universe  of some  pastime  there is  the presence
w: therefore  the pastimes  eternal  call  the Vedas  the Purāṇas
tr: In one universe or another some pastime is always present; therefore the Vedas and Purāṇas call the pastimes eternal.

## 394
w: Goloka  the abode of Gokula  all-pervading  equal to Kṛṣṇa
w: by Kṛṣṇa’s will  into the universes  its  passing
tr: Goloka, the abode of Gokula, is all-pervading (*vibhu*), equal to Kṛṣṇa, and by Kṛṣṇa’s will it descends into the universes.

## 395
w: therefore  in Goloka  eternal  pastimes
w: in the universes  in turn  the manifestation  of them
tr: Thus His pastimes go on eternally in Goloka, and in turn they become manifest in the universes.

## 396
w: in Vraja  Kṛṣṇa  by manifesting all majesty  most complete
w: in the two cities  in Paravyoma  more complete  complete
tr: In Vraja, where He manifests all His majesty, Kṛṣṇa is most complete (*pūrṇatama*); in the two cities He is more complete (*pūrṇatara*), and in Paravyoma complete (*pūrṇa*).

## 397
w: Hari  most complete  more
w: complete  complete  thus  in three ways
w: best  middling and the like  by the words
w: in drama  who  is celebrated
tr: “Hari, whom works on drama celebrate with words such as ‘best’ and ‘middling’, is described in three ways: most complete, more complete and complete.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.221)

## 398
w: with all qualities manifest
w: is regarded  as most complete  by the wise
w: not manifesting all  more
w: complete  complete  showing few
tr: “The wise regard Him in whom all qualities are manifest as most complete; Him who does not manifest them all as more complete; and Him who shows only a few as complete.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.222)

## 399
w: Kṛṣṇa’s  most complete fullness
w: was manifest  in Gokula
w: fullness  greater fullness
w: in Dvārakā  Mathurā and the rest
tr: “Kṛṣṇa’s most complete fullness was manifest in Gokula; His fullness and greater fullness, in Dvārakā, Mathurā and elsewhere.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 2.1.223)

## 400
w: this  Kṛṣṇa  in Vraja  most complete  Bhagavān
w: other  all  forms  more complete  complete  are called
tr: This Kṛṣṇa in Vraja is Bhagavān most complete; all His other forms are called more complete or complete.

## 401
w: in brief  I have told  of Kṛṣṇa’s  forms  the analysis
w: Ananta  to tell  cannot  of this  the full extent
tr: Thus I have briefly analysed Kṛṣṇa’s forms (*svarūpa*). Even Ananta cannot tell all of it.

## 402
w: endless  forms  Kṛṣṇa’s  there is no  counting
w: of the branch  and the moon  by the maxim  I give  a glimpse
tr: Kṛṣṇa’s forms are endless, beyond counting. I have only pointed toward them, as one shows the moon by pointing to a branch (*śākhā-candra-nyāya*).”

## 403
w: this  whoever  hears  reads  he  fortunate
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s  forms  of the truth  comes  some  knowledge
tr: Whoever hears or reads this is fortunate: he gains some knowledge of the truth of Kṛṣṇa’s forms.

## 404
w: Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
w: “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
