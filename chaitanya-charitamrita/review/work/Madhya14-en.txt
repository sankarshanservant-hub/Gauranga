fn: Heading in the edition: “Gaurasundara dances as He watches the Herā-pañcamī.” *Anubhāṣya*: *gopī-rasollāsa* is the highest pitch of the gopīs’ *parakīya-rasa*.

## 13

w: to the ear auspicious  full of power  widespread
w: on earth  proclaim  they  most munificent  people
tr: “The nectar of Your words is the very life of those who burn with suffering. The wise have praised it, and it takes away all sin. It is auspicious to hear, full of power and spread far and wide. Those who proclaim it on earth are the most munificent of givers.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.9)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the gopīs sing this verse when Kṛṣṇa has vanished from the *rāsa* dance; *tapta-jīvanam* — the life of those who burn in separation from Him; *kalmaṣāpaham* — taking away the anguish of the fever of separation; *kavi* — those who know the rasa of Kṛṣṇa.
