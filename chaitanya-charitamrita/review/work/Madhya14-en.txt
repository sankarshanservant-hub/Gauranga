## 13

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the gopīs sing this verse when Kṛṣṇa has vanished from the *rāsa* dance; *tapta-jīvanam* — the life of those who burn in separation from Him; *kalmaṣāpaham* — taking away the anguish of the fever of separation; *kavi* — those who know the rasa of Kṛṣṇa. tr: “You be the patrons (*yajamāna*) of these silk ropes. Every year have the ropes made and bring them.”

## 250
w: so  saying  gave  to them  the torn  silk rope
w: this  seeing  make  ropes  very  strong  making
tr: So saying, He gave them the torn silk rope. “Look at this,” He said, “and make the ropes very strong.

## 251
w: in this  silk rope  is  Śeṣa’s abode
w: in ten forms  becoming  who  serves  Bhagavān
tr: In this silk rope resides Śeṣa, who serves Bhagavān by assuming ten forms.”

## 252
w: fortunate  those  Satyarāja  Rāmānanda
w: of service  the order  receiving  became  supreme  bliss
tr: Fortunate indeed were Satyarāja and Rāmānanda: receiving the order to serve, they felt supreme bliss.

## 253
w: every  year  to the Guṇḍicā  with the devotees
w: silk ropes  bringing  they come  with very  great  joy
tr: Every year after that, they came with the devotees to the Guṇḍicā festival with great joy, bringing silk ropes.

## 254
w: then  Jagannātha  going  sat  on His throne
w: Mahāprabhu  home  came  with  the devotees
tr: Then Jagannātha went and sat upon His throne, and Mahāprabhu came home with the devotees.

## 255
w: in this way  to the devotees  the festival  He showed
w: the devotees  with  the pastimes of Vṛndāvana  He enacted
tr: In this way the Lord showed the devotees the Ratha-yātrā festival and, together with them, enacted the pastimes of Vṛndāvana.

## 256
w: of Caitanya Gosāñi  the pastimes  endless  boundless
w: the thousand-mouthed one  of which  not  reaches  the end
tr: The pastimes of Caitanya Gosāñi are endless and boundless; even the thousand-mouthed Śeṣa cannot reach their end.

## 257
w: Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
w: “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
fn: The same colophon as Madhya 7.155; translation as there.
