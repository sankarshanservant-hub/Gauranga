fn: *Dāru-brahma* — the Brahman manifest in a form of sacred wood (*dāru*); to see in the Deity only a wooden image is an offence (*Anubhāṣya*, see 118). Edition heading (147–148): Svarūpa explains the worldly poet’s words as praise of Kṛṣṇa and establishes the non-difference of the Dāru-brahma, Jagannātha, from the moving Brahman (*jaṅgama-brahma*), Gaurahari.

## 149
w: with Him  selfhood  one form  being
w: Kṛṣṇa  the one truth  —  two forms  having become
tr: Caitanya is one with Him in self and form: Kṛṣṇa, the one truth, has appeared in two forms.
fn: Edition heading: to deliver the world, the one Deity is manifest in two forms by the power of His will (*icchā-śakti*).

## 150
w: for deliverance from saṁsāra  that  will-potency
w: of that  union  making  oneness  thus  is attained
tr: Uniting in that will (*icchā-śakti*) whose aim is to deliver souls from *saṁsāra*, They attain such oneness.

## 151
w: all  worldly  people  to deliver
w: of Gaura  in the moving  form  made  descent
tr: To deliver all the people of the world, He descended in the moving form of Gaura.

## 152
w: by the sight of Jagannātha  is cut  saṁsāra
w: of all  lands  all  people  cannot  come
tr: By seeing Jagannātha one is freed from *saṁsāra*, but not all the people of every land can come to Him.

## 153
w: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu  land to land  going
w: all people  delivered  the moving Brahman  becoming
tr: So Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Prabhu, going from land to land as the moving Brahman (*jaṅgama-brahma*), delivered everyone.

## 154
w: Sarasvatī’s  meaning  this  I have told  the explanation
w: this too  fortune  yours  —  thus  you made  the description
tr: This is the explanation I have given of Sarasvatī’s meaning. It is your good fortune, too, that you described Them in this way.

## 155
w: Kṛṣṇa  to abuse  makes  the name  utterance
w: that  name  becomes  his  of ‘liberation’  the cause
tr: One may utter Kṛṣṇa’s name only to abuse Him, yet that very name becomes the cause of his liberation.”
fn: Edition heading: even when Kṛṣṇa is reviled, the name uttered as *stobha* — *nāmābhāsa* — grants liberation. *Stobha* is a kind of *nāmābhāsa*: the name uttered not for its own sake.

## 156
w: then  that  poet  at the feet of all  falling
w: of all  shelter  took  in the teeth  straw  holding
tr: Then the poet fell at everyone’s feet and, holding straw between his teeth, took shelter of them all.

## 157
w: then  all  devotees  him  accepted
w: his  qualities  telling  with Mahāprabhu  introduced
tr: All the devotees then accepted him and, speaking of his good qualities, introduced him to Mahāprabhu.
fn: Edition heading: first the devotees’ mercy, and through it the mercy of Mahāprabhu.

## 158
w: that  poet  everything  giving up  stayed  in Nīlācala
w: of Gaura’s devotees  the mercy  who  to describe  can
tr: The poet gave up everything and remained in Nīlācala. Who can describe the mercy of Gaura’s devotees?
fn: Edition heading: the poet accepts the dharma of sannyāsa and lives in Purī.

## 159
w: thus  indeed  I have told  of Pradyumna Miśra  the account
w: by the Lord’s order  he did  of Kṛṣṇa-kathā  the hearing
tr: Thus I have told the account of Pradyumna Miśra, who by the Lord’s order heard talks about Kṛṣṇa.

## 160
w: within that  I have told  Rāmānanda’s  glory
w: Himself  with His holy mouth  the Lord  describes  whose  limit
tr: Within it I have spoken of the glory of Rāmānanda, the full extent of which the Lord Himself described with His own holy lips.

## 161
w: in passing  I have told  the poet’s  drama  account
w: ignorant  being  by faith  obtained  the Lord’s  feet
tr: In passing I have also related the story of the poet’s drama: ignorant though he was, by faith he attained the Lord’s feet.
fn: Edition heading: through faith in the Vaiṣṇavas even an ignorant poet obtains the Lord’s mercy.

## 162
w: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s pastimes  —  of nectar  the essence
w: of a single pastime  in the stream  flow  hundreds upon hundreds  of currents
tr: The pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya are the essence of nectar: from the stream of a single pastime flow hundreds upon hundreds of currents.

## 163
w: faith  having  this  pastime  whoever  reads  hears
w: Gaura’s pastimes  bhakti  the devotees  the truth of rasa  knows
tr: Whoever reads or hears this pastime with faith comes to know the pastimes of Gaura, bhakti, the devotees and the truth of rasa.

## 164
w: Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
w: “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
