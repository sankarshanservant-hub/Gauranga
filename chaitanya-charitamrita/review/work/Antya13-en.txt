## 0

## 1

tr: I take shelter of Gaura, whose mind and body, though wasted by the pain born of separation from Kṛṣṇa, blossomed again through His ecstatic emotions.

fn: The same verse as Ādi 4.2 (= Ādi 1.18, 17.2, Antya 2.2, 4.2, 6.2); translation as there.

## 3
w: in this way  Mahāprabhu  with Jagadānanda  in the company

## 4

tr: His mind and body were wasted by the sorrow of separation from Kṛṣṇa, yet at times, absorbed in ecstatic emotion, the Lord would blossom again.

## 9

## 10

## 11

tr: He asked Govinda, “Who had this made?” But when He heard Jagadānanda’s name, His heart shrank back in apprehension.

## 12

tr: He had Govinda put the mattress away and lay down on the banana bark.

## 13
w: Svarūpa  says  Your  will  what  to say  can I
w: the bed  if You reject  the Paṇḍita  sorrow  will feel  heavy
tr: Svarūpa said, “It is Your will—what can I say? But if You reject the bed, the Paṇḍita will be deeply hurt.”

## 14
w: the Lord  says  a bedstead  one  bring  to set up
w: Jagadānanda  wants  Me  sense objects  to make enjoy
tr: The Lord said, “Then bring a bedstead too and set it up! Jagadānanda wants Me to enjoy worldly pleasures.
fn: Heading in the edition: considering Himself a renounced ascetic (*yati*), the Lord reproaches him with feigned anger.

## 15
w: a sannyāsī  man  My  on the ground  lying
w: for Me  bedstead  mattress  pillow  of the head  shaving
tr: I am a sannyāsī, and My place is on the ground. A bedstead, a mattress and a pillow would bring Me utter disgrace.”
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *mastaka-muṇḍana*, literally “a shaven head,” is an expression for disgrace.

## 16
w: Svarūpa Gosāñi  coming  to the Paṇḍita  told
w: hearing  Jagadānanda  great  sorrow  felt
tr: Svarūpa Gosāñi came and told the Paṇḍita, and when Jagadānanda heard it, he was deeply grieved.

## 17
w: Svarūpa Gosāñi  then  devised  a way
w: of the banana  dry  leaves  brought  countless
tr: Then Svarūpa Gosāñi devised a way. He brought a great quantity of dry banana leaves.

## 18
w: with his nails  tearing  tearing  them  very  fine  made
w: of the Lord  into the outer cloths  them  all  filled
tr: Tearing them again and again with his nails, he shredded them very fine and filled the Lord’s outer cloths (*bahirvāsa*) with them.

## 19
w: in this way  two  made  covering and bedding
w: acceptance  made  the Lord  with much  effort
tr: In this way he made two pieces of bedding (*oḍana-pāḍana*), and only after much persuasion did the Lord accept them.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *oḍana-pāḍana* means *ota-prota*, “warp and woof”; according to some, a pillow and a mattress.

## 20
w: on them  lying  does  the Lord  seeing  all  happy
w: Jagadānanda  inwardly and outwardly  very  unhappy
tr: The Lord lay on them, and seeing this, everyone was happy. But Jagadānanda remained deeply unhappy, inwardly and outwardly.
fn: Heading in the edition: everyone is pleased; only Jagadānanda is grieved.

## 21
w: before  of Jagadānanda  the wish  to Vṛndāvana  to go
w: the Lord  permission  not  gives  to him  not  could  set out
tr: Jagadānanda had long wished to go to Vṛndāvana, but the Lord would not give him permission, and so he could not set out.

## 22
w: inner  sorrow  outwardly  display  not  made
w: to Mathurā  to go  from the Lord  permission  asked
tr: Without showing his inner sorrow outwardly, he asked the Lord for permission to go to Mathurā.
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1003) reads *duḥkha bāhye*, “his sorrow outwardly”; another reading is *krodha-duḥkha*, “anger and sorrow.”

## 23
w: the Lord  says  to Mathurā  you will go  with Me  anger  making
w: on Me  blame  laying  you  will become  a beggar
tr: The Lord said, “You will go to Mathurā angry with Me. Laying the blame on Me, you will become a beggar.”
fn: Heading in the edition: the Lord consoles him with sweet words.

## 24
w: Jagadānanda  says  the Lord’s  clasping  feet
w: from long  since  the wish  my  to go  to Vṛndāvana
tr: Clasping the Lord’s feet, Jagadānanda said, “For a long time I have wished to go to Vṛndāvana.

## 25
w: the Lord’s  permission  there was not  so  not  could  go
w: now  permission  give  surely  I will go  certainly
tr: Without Your permission I could not go. Now please give me permission; I will surely go.”

## 26
w: the Lord  out of love  his  going  not  does  accept
w: he  from the Lord  permission  asks  time  after time
tr: Out of love, the Lord would not agree to his going, but Jagadānanda begged Him for permission again and again.

## 27
w: to Svarūpa Gosāñi  the Paṇḍita  made  a request
w: from long  since  to Vṛndāvana  to go  my  heart
tr: The Paṇḍita appealed to Svarūpa Gosāñi: “For a long time my heart has been set on going to Vṛndāvana.

## 28
w: the Lord’s  permission  without  there  to go  not  can
w: now  permission  not  He gives  me  in anger  go  saying
tr: Without the Lord’s permission I cannot go there, and now He will not give me permission. He says, ‘You would go in anger.’

## 29
w: naturally  my  there  to go  the heart  is
w: the Lord’s  permission  obtaining  give  I make  humble request
tr: My heart is drawn there of its own accord. Please obtain the Lord’s permission for me—I humbly beg you.”

## 30
w: then  Svarūpa Gosāñi  says  of the Lord  at the feet
w: of Jagadānanda  the wish  great  to go  to Vṛndāvana
tr: Then Svarūpa Gosāñi submitted at the Lord’s feet: “Jagadānanda greatly desires to go to Vṛndāvana.

## 31
w: from You  permission  he  asks  time  after time
w: permission  give  Mathurā  seeing  let him come back  once
tr: He has asked You for permission again and again. Please let him go: let him see Mathurā once and come back.

## 32
w: Mother  to see  just as  to the land of Gauḍa  he goes
w: so  once  Vṛndāvana  seeing  let him come
tr: Just as he goes to Gauḍa to see Mother Śacī, so let him go once, see Vṛndāvana and return.”

## 33
w: of Svarūpa Gosāñi  at the word  the Lord  permission  gave
w: Jagadānanda  calling  him  instructed
tr: At Svarūpa Gosāñi’s word the Lord gave His permission. He called Jagadānanda and instructed him:

## 34
w: Vārāṇasī  as far as  freely  you may go  on the road
w: beyond  carefully  go  with kṣatriyas and others  together
tr: “As far as Vārāṇasī you may travel the road freely. Beyond that, go carefully, in the company of kṣatriyas and others.

## 35
w: only  a Gauḍīya  if they find  highway robbery  doing  they bind
w: all  plundering  bound  they keep  to go on  they prevent
tr: If they find a Gauḍīya alone, highwaymen (*bāṭapāḍa*) waylay him and bind him; they plunder everything, keep him bound and do not let him go on.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the people of Gauḍa, being slight of build, look weak; finding them alone, highwaymen keep them bound, take everything and do not let them go. According to some, the robbers, seeing that the Bengalis are clever, force them into highway robbery and never let them go.

## 36
w: to Mathurā  having gone  with Sanātana  in the company  stay
w: of Mathurā  the lords  all  the feet  honour
tr: When you reach Mathurā, stay with Sanātana, and honour the feet of all the lords of Mathurā.
fn: *Mathurāra svāmī-saba*, “the lords of Mathurā.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Caubes, the residents of Mathurā. Heading in the edition: instructions on how to behave in Mathurā, and a warning about keeping company with devotees on the path of rāga, who are free from awareness of the Lord’s majesty.

## 37
w: from afar  staying  devotion  show  in company  not  stay
w: their  all  conduct  behaviour  to adopt  you cannot
tr: Show them devotion, but from afar, and do not stay in their company: you could never accept their ways and conduct.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in their pure parental love for Kṛṣṇa they behave in ways contrary to the views of the *smārtas*, and you, devoted to the mood of majesty, might come to disrespect them; but their bhakti is *rāgātmikā*, and there must be no disrespect for the residents of Vraja, so honour them with devotion from afar.

## 38
w: with Sanātana  make  of the forests  visiting
w: of Sanātana  the company  not  leave  for one  moment
tr: Visit the forests with Sanātana, and do not leave his side for a moment.

## 39
w: quickly  come back  there  not  stay  for long
w: Govardhana  not  climb  to see  Gopāla
tr: Come back quickly; do not stay there long. Do not climb Govardhana to see Gopāla.
fn: Heading in the edition: the prohibition against climbing Govardhana, who is non-different from Kṛṣṇa. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: if one lives in Vraja for long, one begins to see faults in its residents and one’s faith weakens; those not yet on the path of rāga should visit Vraja and soon return. Govardhana is Bhagavān Himself, so one must not climb it; it is better to have darśana of Gopāla when He goes elsewhere.

## 40
w: I too  am coming  tell  Sanātana
w: for Me  a place  let him make  in Vṛndāvana
tr: Tell Sanātana, ‘I too am coming,’ and let him prepare a place for Me in Vṛndāvana.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: no authoritative book says that the Lord went to Vṛndāvana again; but since in verse 70 Sanātana repairs the place chosen for Mahāprabhu, one may infer that He may later have gone there again.

## 41

tr: Saying this, the Lord embraced Jagadānanda, who bowed at His feet and set out.

## 42
w: all  the devotees  from  permission  asked
w: by the forest  path  going  going  to Vārāṇasī  came
tr: He took leave of all the devotees, and travelling on and on by the forest path, he came to Vārāṇasī.

## 43

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (on 46–47): Sanātana then lived on pieces of flatbread received as *mādhukarī* alms, while Jagadānanda could not do without rice day after day and so went to cook at a temple: the temples of Vraja did not cook rice and dal as prasāda.

## 47

tr: Sanātana would go begging in Mahāvana—sometimes at a temple, sometimes at the homes of brāhmaṇas.

fn: Heading in the edition: wearing on his head the cloth given by a sannyāsī, Sanātana comes to the Paṇḍita’s lodging.

tr: Seeing the red cloth, the Paṇḍita was overwhelmed with love, for he took it to be Mahāprabhu’s gift (prasāda), and he asked him:

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *jāni*, “making him understand,” or “knowing that the Paṇḍita was full of love for Gaura.”

## 56

w: like you  to Caitanya  dear  anyone  is not
tr: Sanātana said, “O saintly Paṇḍita, great soul! No one is as dear to Caitanya as you.

## 59

w: you  not  had shown  this  shall I learn  how
tr: Such steadfast faith in Caitanya befits you. If you had not shown it, how would I ever learn it?
fn: Heading in the edition (on 58): the humility of Sanātana Gosvāmī, who seeks no honour and honours others, and his praise of the Paṇḍita’s steadfast love for Gaura.

## 60
w: which  in order to see  the cloth  on my head  I tied
w: that  unprecedented  love  this  directly  I have seen
tr: I tied the cloth on my head in order to see this, and now I have seen that unprecedented love with my own eyes.

## 61
w: red cloth  for a Vaiṣṇava  to wear  not  is fitting
w: to some  stranger  I shall give  what  use  in it
tr: It is not fitting for a Vaiṣṇava to wear red cloth. I shall give it to some stranger—what use is it to me?”
fn: Heading in the edition: a paramahaṁsa on the path of rāga is forbidden to wear saffron cloth. *Anubhāṣya*: Vaiṣṇavas are paramahaṁsas and *akiñcanas*, possessing nothing but Kṛṣṇa, and need not put on the saffron dress of sannyāsīs to show their status; moreover, Śrī Gaurahari Himself accepted the dress of an *ekadaṇḍī* sannyāsī, and His servants, mindful of the difference between the Lord and His servant, do not think it fitting to resemble Him in dress; while Vaiṣṇavas who accept sannyāsa under the shelter of a guru who is a Vaiṣṇava paramahaṁsa often wear the saffron dress of the fourth āśrama out of humility, thinking themselves unworthy to be called paramahaṁsas.

## 72

## 75

w: of Vṛndāvana  pīlu  in eating  this  one  līlā
tr: Their mouths were stung with pungency, and their tongues burned. Such was the pastime of eating the *pīlu* of Vṛndāvana.

## 77
w: Jagadānanda’s  at the coming  of all  the joy
w: in this way  at Nīlācala  the Lord’s  pastimes

## 78

## 79

w: of the Gīta-govinda  a song  she sings  of the world  the mind  steals
tr: Taking up the Gurjarī *rāgiṇī*, she was singing a song from the *Gīta-govinda* in a very sweet voice, and it captivated the mind of the whole world.

## 80
w: from afar  the song  hearing  the Lord’s  arose  absorption
w: woman,  man,  who  sings  not  knowing  specifically
tr: Hearing the song from afar, the Lord was lost in ecstatic love and could not tell who was singing, a woman or a man.
fn: Heading in the edition: hearing a song about Kṛṣṇa, the Lord, in half-external consciousness and overwhelmed by love, sees in her singing a transcendental service to Kṛṣṇa and runs towards her.

## 81
w: the singer  to meet  the Lord  in ecstasy  ran
w: on the way  sij  hedge  He takes,  piercing through  He went
tr: In His ecstasy the Lord ran to meet the singer. On the way He went straight into a hedge of *sij* shrubs and forced His way through it.
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1006) reads *laya*, “He takes (the way through the hedge)”; another reading is *haya*. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in Utkala a flower garden is called *phul-bāḍī*; *sij* shrubs grow there—*manasā-sij* and thorny *sij*—and so a hedge of them is called *sijera bāḍī*; *bāḍī* here means “a hedge.”

## 82
w: in the body  thorns  pierced,  anything  not  He knew
w: in great haste  Govinda  Him  behind  ran
tr: Thorns pierced His body, but He knew nothing of it! In great haste Govinda ran after Him.

## 83
w: running  goes  the Lord,  the woman  is  a little  away
w: a woman’s  song  saying  Govinda  the Lord  took  in his arms
tr: The Lord was running on, and the woman was only a little way off, when Govinda caught Him in his arms, crying, “It is a woman singing!”

## 84
w: woman  the word  hearing  the Lord’s  external awareness  came

tr: Hearing the word “woman,” the Lord came back to external consciousness and returned the way He had come.
fn: Heading in the edition: the Lord, filled with the mood of the *āśraya* (the abode of love), is the *ācārya*, the teacher of the world; the doctrine of “Gaura-nāgara” is refuted here; the Lord turns back.

## 85
w: the Lord  says  Govinda,  today  you saved  life
w: woman’s  touch  if it had been  My  would have been  death
tr: The Lord said, “Govinda, today you have saved My life. Had I touched a woman, it would have been My death.
fn: Heading in the edition: to touch a woman or keep company with her is death for an *ācārya* and a preacher, and so it must be avoided in every way; by thanking Govinda, the Lord teaches this lesson.

## 86
w: this  debt  to repay  I  shall not be able  to you
w: Govinda  says  Jagannātha  protects  I  what  insignificant one
tr: I shall never be able to repay this debt to you.” Govinda replied, “It is Jagannātha who protects. What am I? I am nothing.”

## 87
w: the Lord  says  Govinda,  Me  with  stay
w: wherever  there  My  in protecting  careful  be
tr: The Lord said, “Govinda, stay with Me. Wherever I go, be watchful and protect Me.”

## 88

tr: Saying this, the Lord turned back and went to His residence. Hearing of it, Svarūpa and the others were greatly alarmed at heart.

tr: Meanwhile Raghunātha Bhaṭṭācārya, the son of Tapana Miśra, left all his affairs and set out to see the Lord.
fn: Heading in the edition: the story of Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosvāmī, who from his youth kept the great vow of a lifelong celibate (*naiṣṭhika-brahmacārī*).

## 90
w: Kāśī  from  set out  he  of Gauḍa  the road  by

tr: He set out from Kāśī by the road through Gauḍa, and a servant went with him, carrying his bag.
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1007) reads *vahiyā*, “carrying”; another reading is *sājāñā*, “having packed.”

## 91
w: on the way  him  met  Viśvāsa  Rāmadāsa
w: of the Viśvāsa-khānā  a kāyastha  he  of the king  trusted officer
tr: On the way he was met by Rāmadāsa Viśvāsa, a *kāyastha* of the *Viśvāsa-khānā* and a trusted officer of the king.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the accounts office of the ruler of Gauḍa was called the *Viśvāsa-khānā*; *kāyasthas* worked there because the king trusted them.

## 92
w: in all  scriptures  expert,  of the Kāvya-prakāśa  teacher
w: a great  Vaiṣṇava,  of Raghunātha  worshipper
tr: He was learned in all the scriptures and taught the *Kāvya-prakāśa*; he was a great Vaiṣṇava and a worshipper of Raghunātha (Rāma).
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Kāvya-prakāśa* is a famous treatise on poetics by Mammaṭa Bhaṭṭa. Heading in the edition: Rāmadāsa belonged to the Rāmānandī *sampradāya* (he was a Rāmāyat). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: one who at heart desires liberation is not counted among pure Vaiṣṇavas; as a worshipper of Rāma, Rāmadāsa was “almost a Vaiṣṇava,” but in those days few could distinguish the degrees of pure Vaiṣṇavas, and so he was known in the world as “a great Vaiṣṇava.”

## 93
w: eight  praharas  Rāma’s  name  he chants  night  and day
w: all  giving up  he set out  Jagannātha  to see
tr: Night and day, through all eight *praharas*, he chanted the name of Rāma. Giving up everything, he had set out to see Jagannātha.

## 94
w: Raghunātha  Bhaṭṭa  with  on the way  he met
w: the Bhaṭṭa’s  bag  on his head  putting  carrying  went
tr: Meeting Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa on the way, he carried the Bhaṭṭa’s bag on his head as they went.

## 95
w: various  services  doing  does  feet  massaging
w: by that  Raghunātha’s  becomes  embarrassed  mind
tr: He rendered various services and massaged his feet, and this made Raghunātha uneasy at heart.

## 96
w: you  a great  man,  a paṇḍita,  a great  bhāgavata
w: service  not  do,  happily  walk  me  with
tr: “You are a great man, a *paṇḍita*, a *mahā-bhāgavata*. Do not serve me; just walk happily with me.”

## 97
w: Rāmadāsa  says  I  a śūdra  lowly
w: of the brāhmaṇa  service  this  my  own  dharma
tr: Rāmadāsa said, “I am a lowly *śūdra*! To serve a brāhmaṇa is my own *dharma*.

## 98
w: hesitation  not  do  you,  I  your  servant
w: your  service  doing  there is  in the heart  joy
tr: Do not feel uneasy: I am your servant. When I serve you, my heart is filled with joy.”

## 99
w: this  saying  the bag  he carries,  does  service
w: Raghunātha’s  delivering  mantra  he chants  night  and day
tr: Saying this, he carried the bag and served him, and night and day he chanted the *tāraka-mantra* of Raghunātha (Rāma), the mantra that delivers.

## 100
w: in this way  Raghunātha  came  to Nīlācala
w: the Lord’s  at the feet  going  met  with eager joy
tr: In this way Raghunātha came to Nīlācala; he went to the Lord’s feet and met Him with eager joy.

## 101
w: rod-like  obeisance  making  the Bhaṭṭa  fell  at the feet
w: the Lord  Raghunātha  saying  did  embrace
tr: The Bhaṭṭa fell flat at His feet in obeisance, and the Lord embraced him, calling him by name: “Raghunātha!”
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1007) reads *bali’*, “saying”; another reading is *jāni*, “knowing (him).”

## 102
w: of Miśra  and  Śekhara  the obeisances  he conveyed
w: Mahāprabhu  of them  all  news  asked
tr: He conveyed the obeisances of Miśra and Śekhara, and Mahāprabhu asked for news of them all.

## 103
w: good  it is  you came,  see  the lotus-eyed (Jagannātha)
w: today  My  here  you will do  prasāda  eating
tr: “It is good that you have come. Go and see the lotus-eyed Lord (Jagannātha), and today you will take prasāda here with Me.”

## 104
w: to Govinda  telling  a  lodging  had given
w: Svarūpa and others  devotees  with  He introduced
tr: Through Govinda the Lord arranged lodging for him and introduced him to Svarūpa and the other devotees.

## 105
w: in this way  the Lord  with  stayed  eight  months
w: day  by day  the Lord’s  mercy  increases  joy
tr: In this way he stayed with the Lord for eight months, and day by day the Lord’s mercy and his joy increased.
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (p. 1007) reads *kṛpā*, “mercy”; another reading is *kṛpāya*, “by the mercy.” Heading in the edition: eight months with the Lord; Raghunātha receives His loving mercy.

## 106
w: from time  to time  Mahāprabhu’s  he makes  invitation
w: home-cooked  rice  he makes,  and  various  vegetable dishes
tr: From time to time he would invite Mahāprabhu and cook rice and various vegetable dishes at his home.

## 107
w: Raghunātha  Bhaṭṭa  in cooking  very  expert
w: whatever  he cooks,  that  becomes  nectar  like
tr: Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa was highly expert in cooking; whatever he cooked became like nectar.

## 108
w: with great  satisfaction  the Lord  does  eating
w: the Lord’s  remnants  plate  the Bhaṭṭa’s  eating
tr: With great satisfaction the Lord would eat, and the Bhaṭṭa would take the remnants left on His plate.

## 109
w: Rāmadāsa  when  first  the Lord  met
w: Mahāprabhu  much  to him  mercy  not  showed
tr: When Rāmadāsa first met the Lord, Mahāprabhu did not show him much mercy.
fn: Heading in the edition: although Rāmadāsa met the Lord, the Lord, the Indweller (*antaryāmī*), remains indifferent to him.

## 110
w: at heart  desiring liberation  he,  of learning  proud
w: all  hearts  knower  the Lord  omniscient  Bhagavān
tr: At heart he desired liberation, and he was proud of his learning. The Lord knows every heart: He is Bhagavān, who knows all.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the desire for liberation and pride of learning—these two faults kept Rāmadāsa from becoming a pure Vaiṣṇava.

## 111
w: Rāmadāsa  made  then  at Nīlācala  residence
w: to the Paṭṭanāyaka  family  teaches  Kāvya-prakāśa
tr: Rāmadāsa then settled at Nīlācala and taught the *Kāvya-prakāśa* to the Paṭṭanāyaka family.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Paṭṭanāyaka family—the descendants of Bhavānanda.

## 112
w: eight  months  staying  the Lord  to the Bhaṭṭa  farewell  gave
w: marriage  not  make  saying  forbade
tr: When he had stayed eight months, the Lord bade the Bhaṭṭa farewell and forbade him, saying, “Do not marry.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu saw that Raghunātha, before entering family life, was already devoted to Kṛṣṇa, and so He forbade him to take a wife—an excess (*atyāhāra*)—and to enter the world of *māyā*, the abode of enjoyment; worldly people ruled by their wives often turn away from the service of Kṛṣṇa, and there is little hope of their attaining devotion to Hari.

## 113
w: aged  mother  father’s  going  do  service
w: a Vaiṣṇava  from  the Bhāgavata  do  study
tr: Go and serve your aged mother and father, and study the *Bhāgavata* under a Vaiṣṇava.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: through Raghunātha the Lord teaches that one should serve a father and mother who are Vaiṣṇavas wholly devoted to Gaura, and serve them only in a way that helps one’s own service to Hari; He does not tell anyone to serve parents averse to Hari, guru and Vaiṣṇavas. The *Bhāgavata* is to be studied only under a *bhāgavata* free from *anarthas*: under a non-Vaiṣṇava grammarian it becomes a worldly poem.

## 114
w: again  once more  come  to Nīlācala
w: this  saying  neck  garland  gave  his  on the neck
tr: Come to Nīlācala once more.” Saying this, the Lord took the garland from His own neck and placed it on him.

## 115
w: embrace  making  the Lord  farewell  to him  gave

tr: Embracing him, the Lord bade him farewell, and the Bhaṭṭa, overflowing with love, began to weep.

## 116

tr: Asking leave of Svarūpa and the other devotees, and having received the Lord’s permission, the Bhaṭṭa came to Vārāṇasī.

## 117
w: four  years  at home  father  mother’s  service  did

tr: For four years he served his father and mother at home, and he studied the *Bhāgavata* under a Vaiṣṇava *paṇḍita*.

## 118

tr: When his father and mother departed this world at Kāśī, he became detached, left home and all, and came to the Lord again.

## 119

## 120
