## 0
tr: The Meeting with the Vaiṣṇavas

## 1
w: that  I worship  Gaura the raincloud
w: His own  who  by the nectar of the sight
w: by the drought of separation  withered
w: the crops of the devotees  brought back to life
tr: I worship that raincloud, Gaura, who by the nectar of His own sight brought back to life the crops of His devotees, withered by the drought of separation.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to Gaura, the treasure of the devotees’ life.”

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Ādi 4.2; translation as there.

## 3
w: earlier  when  Mahāprabhu  went  south
w: Pratāparudra  the king  then  summoned  Sārvabhauma
tr: Earlier, when Mahāprabhu had gone to the south, King Pratāparudra summoned Sārvabhauma.

## 4
w: to sit  a seat  he gave  making  obeisance
w: of Mahāprabhu  the news  then  he asked  him
tr: Bowing to him, the king offered him a seat and then asked him for news of Mahāprabhu.

## 5
w: I have heard  in your  house  a  great soul
w: Gauḍa  from  has come  He  full of great mercy
tr: “I have heard that a great soul has come from Gauḍa and is staying in your house, and that He is supremely merciful.

## 6
w: to you  great  mercy  He showed  say  all people
w: mercy  showing  arrange  for me  His  sight
tr: Everyone says that He has shown you great mercy. Be kind enough to let me see Him.”

## 7
w: the Bhaṭṭa  says  what  you have heard  all  true  is
w: His  sight  for you  happening  not  is
tr: The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “All that you have heard is true. But it is not possible for you to see Him.

## 8
w: renounced  sannyāsī  He  lives  in solitude
w: even in a dream  not  does  He  meeting a king
tr: He is a renounced sannyāsī who lives in solitude; not even in a dream would He meet a king.

## 9
w: still  somehow  for you  I would have arranged  a sight
w: just now  has made  He  to the south  departure
tr: Still, I would have found some way to arrange for you to see Him; but just now He has gone to the south.”

## 10
w: the king  says  Jagannātha  leaving  why  did He go
w: the Bhaṭṭa  says  of a great soul  this  one  pastime
tr: The king asked, “Why did He leave Jagannātha and go away?” The Bhaṭṭācārya replied, “That is a pastime of great souls.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: on verses 10–11 it refers to Madhya 8.39 and *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 4.30.37 — devotees travel to purify the holy places.

## 11
w: the holy places  pure  to make  He makes  a pilgrimage
w: on that  pretext  delivers  worldly  people
tr: To purify the holy places, He travels on pilgrimage, and on that pretext He delivers worldly people.

## 12
w: like you  devotees
w: become holy places  yourselves  O lord
w: make holy  the holy places
w: by Him within  who bears the club
tr: “Devotees such as you are themselves holy places, my lord. By the Lord who dwells within your hearts, you make even the holy places holy.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.13.10)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.63; translation as there. Here the edition reads «তীর্থীভূতাঃ» (in Ādi 1.63, «তীর্থভূতাঃ»).

## 13
w: of a Vaiṣṇava  this  is  one  nature  unchanging
w: He  a jīva  is not  He is  the independent  Lord
tr: That is the unchanging nature of a Vaiṣṇava. But He is no jīva: He is the independent Lord Himself.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: pure devotees purify the holy places by their coming and deliver the worldly people who live there — such is their eternal nature, for they suffer at the suffering of others; but Śrī Mahāprabhu, though He plays the part of a devotee, is the independent Supreme Lord Himself.

## 14
w: the king  says  Him  you  to go  why  did you let
w: at His feet  falling  effort  making  why  not  did you keep (Him)
tr: The king said, “Why did you let Him go? Why did you not fall at His feet and do all you could to keep Him here?”

## 15
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  He  Himself  the Lord  independent
w: directly  Śrī Kṛṣṇa  He  is not  dependent on others
tr: The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “He is the Lord Himself, fully independent — Śrī Kṛṣṇa in person. He depends on no one.

## 16
w: still  to keep  Him  great effort  I made
w: of the Lord  independent  the will  to keep (Him)  I could not
tr: Even so, I tried very hard to keep Him; but the Lord’s will is His own, and I could not hold Him back.”

## 17
w: the king  says  Bhaṭṭa  you  of the learned  the crest-jewel
w: you  Him  Kṛṣṇa  call  therefore  as truth  I accept
tr: The king said, “Bhaṭṭa, you are the crest-jewel of the learned. Since you call Him Kṛṣṇa, I accept it as the truth.

## 18
w: again  here  His  when there is  coming
w: once  seeing  I will make  fruitful  my eyes
tr: When He comes here again, let me see Him just once and make my eyes fruitful.”

## 19
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  He  will come  shortly
w: to stay  for Him  a  place  I want  secluded
tr: The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “He will come back soon. I would like a secluded place for Him to stay.

## 20
w: of the Lord (Jagannātha)  near  and  it should be  in solitude
w: such  a decision  making  give  a  place
tr: It should be close to Jagannātha and yet quiet. Please decide on such a place and grant it.”

## 21
w: the king  says  such  of Kāśī Miśra  the house
w: of the Lord  near  it is  very  secluded
tr: The king said, “Kāśī Miśra’s house is just such a place. It is near the Lord and very secluded.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Kāśī Miśra’s house is the present Śrī Rādhākānta Maṭha in the Bālisāhi quarter, a little south of the temple; in those days the place was secluded.

## 22
w: so  saying  the king  remains  eager  being
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  to Kāśī Miśra  told  coming
tr: Having said this, the king waited eagerly, and the Bhaṭṭācārya went and told Kāśī Miśra.

## 23
w: Kāśī Miśra  says  I  very  fortunate
w: in my  house  of Prabhupāda  will be  the stay
tr: Kāśī Miśra said, “I am most fortunate: Prabhupāda will stay in my house!”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu, Nityānanda and Advaita are called “Prabhupāda” because They are *viṣṇu-tattva*, the eternal Lord of the *jīva*; the Gurudeva is called “Oṁ Viṣṇupāda”, and a pure Vaiṣṇava “Śrīpāda”.

## 24
w: in this way  of Puruṣottama the residents  all  people
w: the Lord  to meet  of all  eager  the minds
tr: In this way all the people of Puruṣottama were eager to meet the Lord.

## 25
w: of all people  the eagerness  when  extremely  increased
w: Mahāprabhu  the south  from  quickly  came
tr: When everyone’s longing had grown intense, Mahāprabhu hastened back from the south.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The longing to serve is the thread that binds the devotee and Bhagavān.”

## 26
w: hearing  joyful  became  of all  the minds
w: all  coming  to Sārvabhauma  made  a request
tr: Hearing of it, everyone rejoiced. They all came to Sārvabhauma and pleaded with him:

## 27
w: the Lord  with  for us all  arrange  a sight
w: by your  grace  may we obtain  of the Lord  the feet
tr: “Arrange for us all to see the Lord. By your grace may we reach His feet.”

## 28
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  tomorrow  of Kāśī Miśra  to the house
w: the Lord  will go  there  I will bring together  you all
tr: The Bhaṭṭācārya said, “Tomorrow the Lord will go to Kāśī Miśra’s house; there I will bring you all to meet Him.”

## 29
w: the next  day  Mahāprabhu  the Bhaṭṭācārya  with
w: Jagannātha’s  sight  He had  with great joy
tr: The next day Mahāprabhu went with the Bhaṭṭācārya and joyfully saw Jagannātha.

## 30
w: mahā-prasāda  giving  there  met (Him)  the servants
w: Mahāprabhu  all of them  gave  embrace
tr: There the servants of the Deity met Him and gave Him *mahā-prasāda*, and Mahāprabhu embraced them all.

## 31
w: the sight  having had  the Lord  went  outside
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  brought  Him  to Kāśī Miśra’s house
tr: Having seen Jagannātha, the Lord came out, and the Bhaṭṭācārya brought Him to Kāśī Miśra’s house.
fn: The edition reads «দরশন করি’ প্রভু» (the earlier text had «মহাপ্রভু»); the Bengali and the transliteration follow the edition (scan, p. 501).

## 32
w: Kāśī Miśra  coming  fell  of the Lord  at the feet
w: the house  together with  himself  to Him  made  offering
tr: Kāśī Miśra came and fell at the Lord’s feet, and offered Him his house and his very self.

## 33
w: the Lord  four-armed form  to him  showed
w: His own  making  him  embrace  He gave
tr: The Lord showed him His four-armed form, made him His own and embraced him.

## 34
w: then  Mahāprabhu  there  sat  on a seat
w: all around  sat  headed by Nityānanda  the devotees
tr: Then Mahāprabhu sat down there, and the devotees headed by Nityānanda sat all around Him.

## 35
w: pleased  became  seeing  the Lord  of the residence  the arrangement
w: in which  residence  is  for the Lord  every provision
tr: The Lord was pleased to see how the residence was arranged: it held everything He would need.

## 36
w: Sārvabhauma  says  Lord  suitable  Your  residence
w: You  acceptance  make  of Kāśī Miśra  the hope
tr: Sārvabhauma said, “Lord, this residence is worthy of You. Kāśī Miśra hopes that You will accept it.”

## 37
w: the Lord  says  this  body  of you all
w: whatever  you  say  that  duty  My
tr: The Lord said, “This body belongs to all of you. Whatever you say, that I must do.”

## 38
w: then  Sārvabhauma  of the Lord  at the right side  sitting
w: to introduce  began  all  of Puruṣottama the residents
tr: Then Sārvabhauma, seated at the Lord’s right, began to introduce to Him all the residents of Puruṣottama:

## 39
w: these  all  people  Lord  live  in Nīlācala
w: eager  they have been  all  You  to meet
tr: “Lord, all these people live in Nīlācala. They have all been longing to meet You.

## 40
w: thirsty  cātaka birds  as  make  cries of distress
w: so  these  all  all  make  acceptance
tr: Just as thirsty *cātaka* birds cry out in distress, so do these people. Please accept them all.

## 41
w: of Jagannātha a servant  this  name  Janārdana
w: during the anavasara  does  of the Lord  service to the divine body
tr: This is Janārdana, a servant of Jagannātha. During the *anavasara* he serves the Lord’s divine body.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *anavasara* is the period after the Snāna-yātrā until the darśana of “Nava-yauvana”.

## 42
w: Kṛṣṇadāsa  name  this  golden  staff-bearer
w: Śikhi Māhiti by name  this  the scribe
tr: This is Kṛṣṇadāsa, who bears the golden staff, and this is Śikhi Māhiti, the scribe.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *likhana-adhikārī* is the official holding the post of *deula-karaṇa*, who keeps the temple chronicle (the “Mādalā-pāñji”). *Anubhāṣya*: on Śikhi Māhiti see Antya 2.104–106.

## 43
w: Pradyumna Miśra  this  of Vaiṣṇavas  the chief
w: of Jagannātha  the chief cook  he  Dāsa  by name
tr: This is Pradyumna Miśra, foremost among the Vaiṣṇavas. He is Jagannātha’s chief cook and is known by the name Dāsa.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *mahā-soyāra* means *mahā-sūpakāra*, the chief cook, the superintendent of the kitchen. *Anubhāṣya*: adding “dāsa” to a brāhmaṇa’s name, as a sign that he is a servant of Viṣṇu, is sanctioned also by Culli Bhaṭṭa.

## 44
w: Murāri Māhiti  this  of Śikhi Māhiti  the brother
w: Your  feet  except  other  refuge  he has not
tr: This is Murāri Māhiti, Śikhi Māhiti’s brother. He has no refuge but Your feet.

## 45
w: Candaneśvara  Siṁheśvara  Murāri  the brāhmaṇa
w: Viṣṇudāsa  these  meditate on  Your  feet
tr: These are Candaneśvara, Siṁheśvara, Murāri the brāhmaṇa and Viṣṇudāsa; they all meditate on Your feet.

## 46
w: the Prahararāja  Mahāpātra  this  of great wisdom
w: Paramānanda  Mahāpātra  his  (name) joined (to the title)
tr: This is the Prahararāja Mahāpātra, a man of great wisdom; his name is Paramānanda Mahāpātra.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *prahararāja* is a hereditary title of a family of royal priests in Utkala: when the king dies, one of them sits on the throne holding the royal sceptre for one *prahara*, until the heir is anointed, so that the throne is never left empty. In the edition this is one person: “(10) Paramānanda”.

## 47

w: with single-minded devotion  think of  all  Your  feet
tr: All these Vaiṣṇavas are the ornaments of this holy place. With one-pointed devotion they all think of Your feet.”

## 48

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the four sons are Rāmānanda Rāya’s brothers — Vāṇīnātha, Gopīnātha, Kalānidhi and Sudhānidhi (see Ādi 10.133).

## 52

tr: “The glory of one whose son is a jewel like Rāmānanda cannot be described in this world.

## 53

tr: You are Pāṇḍu himself, and your wife is Kuntī; your five wise sons are the five Pāṇḍavas.”
