## 0
tr: Beholding Śrī Vṛndāvana

## 1
w: in Vṛndāvana  the nonmoving and moving beings
w: delighting  with His glances
w: Himself  and  by the sight of them
w: Gaurāṅga  all around  wandered
tr: Delighting the moving and nonmoving beings of Vṛndāvana with His glances, and Himself rejoicing at the sight of them, Gaurāṅga wandered everywhere.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

## 3
w: in this way  Mahāprabhu  dancing  dancing
w: at Āriṭ-grāma  arriving  outer consciousness  came  suddenly
tr: Dancing and dancing in this way, Mahāprabhu came to Āriṭ-grāma, and there His outer awareness suddenly returned.

## 4
w: in Ariṣṭa  of Rādhā-kuṇḍa  news  He asks  of the people
w: anyone  not  tells  accompanying  brāhmaṇa  not  knows
tr: In Ariṣṭa He asked the people about Rādhā-kuṇḍa, but no one could tell Him, and the brāhmaṇa with Him did not know either.

## 5
w: the holy place  lost  knowing  the Lord  all-knowing  Bhagavān
w: in two  rice fields  in shallow water  He took  bath
tr: The Lord, the all-knowing Bhagavān, knew that the holy place had been lost to sight, and He bathed in the shallow water of two rice fields.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in this way the Lord showed that these two rice fields are Rādhā-kuṇḍa and Śyāma-kuṇḍa. Edition heading: “Gaura, endowed with Rādhā’s sentiment and lustre, reveals the hidden Śrī Rādhā-kuṇḍa.”

## 6
w: seeing  all  of the village people  astonished  became  the mind
w: with love  the Lord  makes  of Rādhā-kuṇḍa  praise
tr: Seeing this, all the villagers were astonished. Then, full of love, the Lord praised Rādhā-kuṇḍa:

## 7
w: all  gopīs  than  Rādhā  Kṛṣṇa’s  beloved
w: so too  Rādhā-kuṇḍa  —  dear  of the beloved  the lake
tr: Of all the gopīs, Rādhā is Kṛṣṇa’s dearest. So too Rādhā-kuṇḍa is dear to Him: it is the lake of His beloved.

## 8
w: as  Rādhā  dear  to Viṣṇu
w: Her  pond  dear  so
w: among all gopīs  She alone
w: of Viṣṇu  most  beloved
tr: “As Rādhā is dear to Viṣṇu, so is Her pond dear to Him; among all the gopīs She alone is His most beloved.” (Padma Purāṇa; Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, Uttara-khaṇḍa, 45)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 4.215 and Madhya 8.98; translation as there. The edition’s heading here: “the words of the *Padma Purāṇa*”.

## 9
w: in which  kuṇḍa  eternally  Kṛṣṇa  Rādhikā  with
w: in the water  water sports  performs  on the bank  in the joy of the rāsa
tr: In this kuṇḍa Kṛṣṇa eternally sports with Rādhikā in the water, and on its bank He delights in the rāsa dance.

## 10
w: in that  kuṇḍa  whoever  once  takes  bath
w: to him  equal to Rādhā’s  love  Kṛṣṇa  makes  gift
tr: To anyone who bathes in that kuṇḍa even once, Kṛṣṇa gives love like Rādhā’s own.

## 11
w: of the kuṇḍa  sweetness  —  as if  Rādhā’s  charm
w: of the kuṇḍa  glory  —  as if  Rādhā’s  glory
tr: The sweetness of the kuṇḍa is like Rādhā’s own charm, and the glory of the kuṇḍa is like Rādhā’s own glory.

## 12
w: like Śrī Rādhā  to Hari  Her  lake  dearest by wondrous  its own  qualities
w: in which  the beautiful moon Mādhava  ceaselessly  with love  with Her  sports
w: love for Him  ah  like Rādhikā  obtains  in which  once  one who bathes
w: its  indeed  glory  and  sweetness  by whom  could be  described  on earth
tr: “By its own wondrous qualities Her lake is as dear to Hari as Śrī Rādhā Herself. In it the beautiful moon Mādhava ceaselessly sports with Her in love, and whoever bathes in it even once gains love for Him like Rādhikā’s. Who on earth could describe its glory and its sweetness?” (Govinda-līlāmṛta, 7.102)

## 13
w: in this way  praises  He offers  absorbed in love  being
w: on the bank  dance  He performs  the pastimes of the kuṇḍa  remembering
tr: Absorbed in love, He offered these praises, and He danced on the bank, remembering the pastimes of the kuṇḍa.

## 14
w: of the kuṇḍa  clay  taking  tilaka  He made
w: through the Bhaṭṭācārya  clay  with Him  making  He took
tr: He took clay from the kuṇḍa and marked Himself with tilaka, and He had the Bhaṭṭācārya carry some of the clay along.

## 15
w: then  setting out  came  the Lord  to Sumanaḥ-sarovara
w: there  Govardhana  seeing  the Lord  became  overwhelmed
tr: Then the Lord went on to Sumanaḥ-sarovara. There He saw Govardhana and was overwhelmed.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Sumanaḥ-sarovara is the same as Kusuma-sarovara.

## 16
w: Govardhana  seeing  the Lord  became  prostrate like a rod
w: one  rock  embracing  He became  mad
tr: Seeing Govardhana, the Lord fell down like a rod in obeisance. He embraced a rock of Govardhana and became mad with love.

## 17
w: with love  intoxicated  setting out  He came  the village of Govardhana
w: Harideva  seeing  there  He became  bowed down
tr: Intoxicated with love, He went on to the village of Govardhana. There He saw Harideva and bowed down before Him.

## 18
w: of the lotus of Mathurā  on the western petal  whose  dwelling
w: Harideva  Nārāyaṇa  —  the original manifestation
tr: Harideva, who dwells on the western petal of the lotus of Mathurā, is Nārāyaṇa, the original manifestation.

## 19
w: before Harideva  He dances  with love  intoxicated  being
w: all  the people  to see  came  the wonder  hearing
tr: Before Harideva He danced, intoxicated with love, and all the people, hearing of the wonder, came to see.

## 20
w: the Lord’s  love  beauty  seeing  the people  amazed
w: Harideva’s  servant  the Lord’s  did  honour
tr: The people were amazed to see the Lord’s love and beauty, and the servant of Harideva received the Lord with honour.

## 21
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  at Brahma-kuṇḍa  cooking  going  did
w: in Brahma-kuṇḍa  bath  taking  the Lord  meal  took
tr: The Bhaṭṭācārya went to Brahma-kuṇḍa and cooked there, and the Lord bathed in Brahma-kuṇḍa and took His meal.

## 22
w: that night  He stayed  of Harideva  in the temple
w: at night  Mahāprabhu  makes  in His mind  deliberation
tr: He spent that night in the temple of Harideva, and during the night Mahāprabhu reflected:

## 23
w: onto Govardhana  I  ever  not  shall climb
w: of Gopāla-rāya  darśana  how  shall I get
tr: “I shall never climb Govardhana. How, then, shall I have darśana of Gopāla-rāya?”

## 24
w: so  in mind  thinking  the Lord  silence  keeping  remained
w: knowing (this)  Gopāla  the pretext of fear of the mlecchas  raised
tr: Thinking this, the Lord kept silent. Gopāla knew His mind and raised a pretext: fear of the mlecchas.

## 25
w: not wishing to climb  the hill
w: to Himself  who regarded Himself as a devotee
w: coming down  from the hill  Kṛṣṇa
w: to Gaura  Himself  showed
tr: Gaura regarded Himself as a devotee and would not climb the hill; so Kṛṣṇa came down from the hill and showed Himself to Gaura — to His very own Self.
fn: The author’s own verse (edition heading). *Svasmai* — “to Himself”. *Anubhāṣya*: Gaura is Kṛṣṇa’s own form; nondifferent from the One He worships, He regards Himself as His servant.

## 26
w: Annakūṭa  named  in the village  Gopāla’s  residence
w: of Rajput people  in that  village  dwelling
tr: Gopāla resided in the village called Annakūṭa, and the people of that village were Rajputs.

## 27
w: one  person  coming  at night  to the villagers  said
w: your  village  to raid  the Turkish troops  have armed
tr: One night a man came and told the villagers: “Turkish troops have armed themselves to raid your village.

## 28
w: today  at night  flee  not  remain  a single person
w: the Deity  taking  run away  will come  tomorrow  the yavanas
tr: Flee tonight, and let not a single person remain. Take the Deity and run, for the yavanas will come tomorrow.”

## 29
w: hearing  of the village  the people  anxious  became
w: first  Gopāla  taking  in the village of Gāṭholi  they placed
tr: Hearing this, the villagers grew anxious. First of all they took Gopāla and placed Him in the village of Gāṭholi.

## 30
w: in a brāhmaṇa’s house  Gopāla’s  in secret  service
w: the village  deserted  became  fled  all the people
tr: In a brāhmaṇa’s house Gopāla was served in secret. The village was deserted, for everyone had fled.

## 31
w: thus  from fear of the mlecchas  Gopāla  flees  again and again
w: the temple  leaving  in a grove  He stays  or  in another village
tr: In this way, for fear of the mlecchas, Gopāla flees again and again: He leaves His temple and stays in a grove or in another village.

## 32
w: in the morning  the Lord  in Mānasa-gaṅgā  taking  bath
w: on the circumambulation of Govardhana  He made  departure
tr: In the morning the Lord bathed in Mānasa-gaṅgā and set out to circle Govardhana.

## 33
w: Govardhana  seeing  the Lord  absorbed in love  becoming
w: dancing  dancing  He went  a verse  reciting
tr: Seeing Govardhana, the Lord was overcome with love, and He went on, dancing and dancing and reciting this verse:

## 34
w: ah  this  hill  O friends  best of Hari’s servants
w: since  of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa  the feet  by the touch  rejoicing
w: honour  offers  with Their herds of cows  to Them  because
w: with water  fine grass  caves  bulbs  roots
tr: “Ah, friends, this hill is the best of Hari’s servants! Thrilled by the touch of the feet of Rāma and Kṛṣṇa, it honours Them, together with Their cows and companions, with water to drink, fine grass, caves, and bulbs and roots.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.21.18)
fn: According to the *Anubhāṣya*, the gopīs sing this verse: wandering in longing for Kṛṣṇa, they see before them Govardhana, the king of mountains, nondifferent from Vrajendranandana.

## 35
w: at Govinda-kuṇḍa and other  holy places  the Lord  took  bath
w: there  He heard  Gopāla  —  Gāṭholi  in the village
tr: The Lord bathed at Govinda-kuṇḍa and other holy places, and there He heard that Gopāla was in the village of Gāṭholi.

## 36
w: to that  village  going  He had  darśana of Gopāla
w: in the rapture of love  the Lord  performs  kīrtana and dance
tr: He went to that village and had darśana of Gopāla, and in the rapture of love the Lord sang and danced.

## 37
w: of Gopāla  the beauty  seeing  the Lord’s  rapture
w: this  verse  reciting  He dances  came  the end of the day
tr: Seeing the beauty of Gopāla, the Lord was enraptured. Reciting this verse, He danced until the day was over:

## 38
w: the left  of the lotus-eyed Lord
w: staff-like arm  that  may protect  you
w: the state of a toy ball  by which
w: was brought  Govardhana  hill
tr: “May the left arm of the lotus-eyed Lord protect you — the arm with which He made Govardhana Hill a ball for His play.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Dakṣiṇa-vibhāga, Vibhāva-laharī, 26)
fn: The reference follows the edition’s heading.

## 39
w: in this way  for three days  Gopāla  He beheld
w: on the fourth day  Gopāla  to His own temple  went
tr: In this way He beheld Gopāla for three days, and on the fourth day Gopāla went back to His own temple.

## 40
w: with Gopāla  going  He came  dancing and singing  doing
w: in a joyful uproar  the people  say  Hari  Hari
tr: He went along with Gopāla, dancing and singing, and in a joyful uproar the people cried, “Hari! Hari!”

## 41
w: Gopāla  to the temple  went  the Lord  remained  below
w: the Lord’s  desires  fulfilled  all  made  Gopāla
tr: Gopāla went to His temple, and the Lord remained below. Gopāla had fulfilled all the Lord’s desires.

## 42
w: in this way  Gopāla’s  compassionate  nature
w: of those  devotees  to see  there is  longing
tr: Such is Gopāla’s compassionate nature. The hearts of His devotees long for the sight of Him.

## 43
w: to see  eagerness  there is  not  they climb  Govardhana
w: on some  pretext  Gopāla  coming  descends  Himself
tr: Yet, eager as they are, they will not climb Govardhana, and so, on some pretext, Gopāla Himself comes down to them.
fn: Edition heading: “The merciful Gopāla, on some pretext, gives darśana to the devotees.”

## 44
w: sometimes  in a grove  He stays  sometimes  He stays  in another village
w: whichever  devotee  there  coming  sees  Him
tr: Sometimes He stays in a grove, sometimes in another village, and any devotee may come there and see Him.

## 45
w: on the hill  not  climb  the two  Rūpa and Sanātana
w: in this way  to them  He has given  darśana
tr: The two brothers, Rūpa and Sanātana, would not climb the hill, and in this way He gave them darśana.

## 46
w: in old age  Rūpa Gosāñi  not  could  go
w: a desire  arose  of Gopāla  the beauty  to see
tr: In his old age Rūpa Gosāñi could no longer go there, yet a desire arose in him to see the beauty of Gopāla.

## 47
w: from fear of the mlecchas  came  Gopāla  to the city of Mathurā
w: one month  He stayed  in Viṭhṭhaleśvara’s house
tr: For fear of the mlecchas Gopāla came to the city of Mathurā and stayed for a month in the house of Viṭhṭhaleśvara.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: wishing to show mercy to Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī as well, Gopāla again raised the same “pretext”, fear of the mlecchas. *Anubhāṣya*: Viṭhṭhaleśvara (Viṭhṭhalanātha) was the son of Vallabha Bhaṭṭa.

## 48
w: then  Rūpa  Gosāñi  all  his own associates  taking
w: for one month  darśana  had  in Mathurā  staying
tr: Then Rūpa Gosāñi took all his associates with him, stayed in Mathurā for a month and had darśana of Gopāla.

## 49
w: with him  Gopāla Bhaṭṭa  Raghunātha dāsa
w: Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosāñi  and  Lokanātha
tr: With him were Gopāla Bhaṭṭa, Raghunātha dāsa, Raghunātha Bhaṭṭa Gosāñi and Lokanātha,

## 50
w: Bhūgarbha Gosāñi  and  Śrī Jīva Gosāñi
w: Śrī Yādava Ācārya  and  Govinda Gosāñi
tr: Bhūgarbha Gosāñi, Śrī Jīva Gosāñi, Śrī Yādava Ācārya, Govinda Gosāñi,

## 51
w: Śrī Uddhava dāsa  and  Mādhava  the two
w: Śrī Gopāla dāsa  and  Nārāyaṇa dāsa
tr: Śrī Uddhava dāsa and Mādhava, Śrī Gopāla dāsa and Nārāyaṇa dāsa,

## 52
w: Govinda  the devotee  and  Vāṇī Kṛṣṇadāsa
w: Puṇḍarīkākṣa  Īśāna  and  Laghu Haridāsa
tr: the devotee Govinda, Vāṇī Kṛṣṇadāsa, Puṇḍarīkākṣa, Īśāna and Laghu Haridāsa.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this Laghu Haridāsa is not the Choṭa Haridāsa of Mahāprabhu’s time, who left his body at Prayāga.

## 53
w: all these  chief devotees  taking  with himself
w: of Śrī Gopāla  darśana  he had  with great joy
tr: Taking all these chief devotees with him, he had darśana of Śrī Gopāla with great joy.

## 54
w: one month  staying  Gopāla  went  to His own place
w: Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi  came  to Śrī Vṛndāvana
tr: After staying for a month, Gopāla went back to His own place, and Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi returned to Śrī Vṛndāvana.

## 55
w: in passing  I have told  of Gopāla’s mercy  the story
w: then  Mahāprabhu  went  to Śrī Kāmyavana
tr: In passing I have told the story of Gopāla’s mercy. Then Mahāprabhu went to Śrī Kāmyavana.

## 56
w: the Lord’s  manner of travelling  before  which  I described
w: in the same way  in Vṛndāvana  everything  He saw
tr: In the same way as I described before, the Lord travelled through Vṛndāvana and saw all its holy places.

## 57
w: there  the places of the pastimes  seeing  He went  to Nandīśvara
w: Nandīśvara  seeing  with love  He became  overwhelmed
tr: After seeing the places of the pastimes there, He went to Nandīśvara, and on seeing Nandīśvara He was overwhelmed with love.

## 58
w: Pāvana and other  in all  kuṇḍas  bath  taking
w: the people  He asked  onto the hill  going
tr: He bathed in all the kuṇḍas, beginning with Pāvana, and then He went up the hill and asked the people:

## 59
w: any  Deities  are there  on the hill
w: the people  say  —  Deities  there are  of a cave  inside
tr: “Are there any Deities on the hill?” The people said, “There are Deities inside a cave.

## 60
w: on two sides  mother and father  sturdy  of body
w: in the middle  one  child  is  beautiful in three bends
tr: On either side are the mother and father, sturdy of build, and between them is a child, beautiful in His threefold bending form.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (edition heading): in the cave dwell the Deities of Nanda, Yaśodā and Kṛṣṇa.

## 61
w: hearing  Mahāprabhu  in His mind  joy  feeling
w: three  Deities  He saw  that  cave  opening
tr: Mahāprabhu was delighted to hear this. He opened the cave and saw the three Deities.

## 62
w: of the King and Queen of Vraja  He did  of the feet  worship
w: in the rapture of love  Kṛṣṇa’s  He did  touching of the whole body
tr: He bowed to the feet of the King and Queen of Vraja, and in the rapture of love He touched Kṛṣṇa’s whole body.

## 63
w: all  day  in the rapture of love  dance and song  He performed
w: from there  Mahāprabhu  to Khadiravana  came
tr: All day He danced and sang, absorbed in love, and from there Mahāprabhu came to Khadiravana.

## 64
w: the places of the pastimes  seeing  there  He went  to Śeṣaśāyī
w: Lakṣmī  seeing  this  verse  recites  the Lord
tr: He saw the places of the pastimes there and went on to Śeṣaśāyī. There, seeing Lakṣmī, the Lord recited this verse:

## 65
w: which  Your  tender  lotus feet  on the breasts
w: fearful  gently  O dear  we place  hard
w: with them  the forest  You wander  they  are pained  not  whether
w: by fine stones and such  whirls  the mind  of whom You are the life  our
tr: “O dear one, Your tender lotus feet we place gently on our hard breasts, afraid to hurt them — and with those feet You wander through the forest! Are they not hurt by the sharp pebbles? You are our very life, and our minds are reeling.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.31.19)
fn: The same verse as Madhya 8.218; translation as there.

## 66
w: then  Khelā-tīrtha  seeing  to Bhāṇḍīravana  He came
w: the Yamunā  across  going  to Bhadravana  He went
tr: Then, after seeing Khelā-tīrtha, He came to Bhāṇḍīravana, and crossing the Yamunā He went to Bhadravana.

## 67
w: Śrīvana  seeing  again  He went  to Lohavana
w: to Mahāvana  going  He had  darśana of the birthplace
tr: From Śrīvana He went on to Lohavana, and in Mahāvana He saw the place of Kṛṣṇa’s birth.

## 68
w: the breaking of the Yamala-arjuna trees and so on  He saw  those  places
w: in the rapture of love  the Lord’s  mind  became  agitated
tr: He saw the places where the twin Arjuna trees were broken and where other pastimes took place, and the Lord’s heart trembled with love.

## 69
w: Gokula  seeing  He came  to the city of Mathurā
w: the birthplace  seeing  He stays  that  in the brāhmaṇa’s house
tr: From Gokula He came to the city of Mathurā. He saw the place of Kṛṣṇa’s birth and stayed in the house of that same brāhmaṇa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord stayed with the disciple of Mādhavendra Purī of whom the previous chapter tells (Madhya 17).

## 70
w: of people  the crowds  seeing  Mathurā  leaving
w: in solitude  at Akrūra-tīrtha  He stayed  coming
tr: Seeing the crowds of people, He left Mathurā and came to stay in solitude at Akrūra-tīrtha.

## 71
w: the next  day  came  the Lord  to see  Vṛndāvana
w: in Kālīya-hrada  bath  He took  and  in Praskandana
tr: The next day the Lord came to see Vṛndāvana, and He bathed in Kālīya-hrada and in Praskandana.

## 72
w: from Dvādaśāditya  to Keśī-tīrtha  He came
w: the place of the rāsa  seeing  with love  unconscious  He became
tr: From Dvādaśāditya He came to Keśī-tīrtha, and on seeing the place of the rāsa dance He fainted with love.

## 73
w: consciousness  regaining  again  He rolls on the ground
w: He laughs  weeps  dances  falls  in a loud voice  sings
tr: Regaining consciousness, He rolled on the ground again; He laughed, wept, danced, fell down and sang at the top of His voice.

## 74
w: in this joy  that day  there  He spent
w: in the evening  to Akrūra  coming  meal  He took
tr: He spent that day there in this joy, and in the evening He returned to Akrūra and took His meal.

## 75
w: in the morning  in Vṛndāvana  He took  at Cīra-ghāṭa  bath
w: to Tẽtula-talā  coming  He took  rest
tr: In the morning He bathed at Cīra-ghāṭa in Vṛndāvana, and then He came to Tẽtula-talā and rested there.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: this place is now called Āmli-talā (cf. verse 83).

## 76
w: of the time of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes  that  tree  ancient
w: its  beneath  a built-up seat  very smooth
tr: That ancient tree dates from the time of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes, and beneath it is a raised platform, beautifully smooth.

## 77
w: nearby  the Yamunā  flows  cool  breeze
w: the beauty of Vṛndāvana  seeing  of the Yamunā  water
tr: The Yamunā flows nearby, and a cool breeze blows. He gazed at the beauty of Vṛndāvana and at the waters of the Yamunā.

## 78
