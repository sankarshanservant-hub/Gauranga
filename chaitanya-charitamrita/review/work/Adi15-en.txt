# English translation, Ādi-līlā 15 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: A Brief Description of the Boyhood Pastimes

## 1
w: one of evil mind  goodness of mind  surely
w: attains  at whose  lotus feet
w: of flowers  by the offering  merely
w: to that  Caitanya Prabhu  I worship
tr: “I worship Caitanya Prabhu. Merely by offering flowers at His lotus feet, even one whose mind is evil gains a good mind.” (Hari-bhakti-vilāsa, 7.1)
fn: A play on words: *sumanas* means both “flower” and “good mind”. Heading in the edition: “By the worship of Gaura even the evil-minded gain a good mind.” *Anubhāṣya*: an evil mind is one absorbed in anything other than Kṛṣṇa; a good mind is free of other desires, uncovered by knowledge and karma, and wholly given to the service of Kṛṣṇa.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: of the boyhood  pastimes  an outline  I make  enumeration
w: in the boyhood  years  the Lord’s  chief (pastime)  study
tr: Now I shall give a brief account of the Lord’s boyhood pastimes. In those years His chief occupation was study.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 15.4): *paugaṇḍa*, boyhood, is the period from the fifth year of life to the tenth. Heading in the edition: “Among the boyhood pastimes, study is the chief.”

## 4
w: the boyhood  pastimes  of Caitanya-
w: Kṛṣṇa  very extensive
w: with the beginning of learning  as their start  hand-
w: taking (marriage)  as their end  enchanting
tr: “The boyhood pastimes of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa are vast and enchanting. They begin with His first lessons and end with His marriage.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa is Viśvambhara, the Lord whose form is one with Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

## 5
w: Gaṅgādāsa  Paṇḍita’s  at the place  He studies  grammar
w: by hearing  merely  in His throat  He held  the sūtras and commentaries  all
tr: He studied grammar with Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita, and as soon as He heard the sūtras and their commentaries, He knew them all by heart.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord first received His elementary schooling from Viṣṇu and Sudarśana, and then studied grammar with Gaṅgādāsa Paṇḍita.

## 6
w: in a short time  He became  in the Pañjī-ṭīkā  expert
w: of long standing  the students  He defeats  being  new
tr: Within a short time He mastered the *Pañjī-ṭīkā*, and, young as He was, He defeated students who had studied for years.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *Pañjī-ṭīkā* was a well-known commentary on grammar, and Mahāprabhu wrote His own notes (*ṭippanī*) on it.

## 7
w: of study  the pastimes  of the Lord  Vṛndāvana dāsa
w: in the ‘Caitanya-maṅgala’  made  detailed  description
tr: Vṛndāvana dāsa has described the Lord’s pastimes of study in detail in the *Caitanya-maṅgala*.
fn: *Caitanya-maṅgala* is the original title of the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (cf. Ādi 8.33–35). *Anubhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 4 and 6–10.

## 8
w: one  day  at His mother’s  feet  making  obeisance
w: the Lord  says  —  Mother,  to Me  give  one  gift
tr: One day the Lord bowed at His mother’s feet and said, “Mother, grant Me one gift.”

## 9
w: the mother  says  —  that  I will give  whatever  You  will ask
w: the Lord  says  —  on Ekādaśī  grain  not  you will eat
tr: His mother said, “I will give You whatever You ask.” And the Lord said, “Do not eat grain on Ekādaśī.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in the *Bhakti-sandarbha* (299) Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī cites the *Skanda Purāṇa* and the *Agni Purāṇa*: to eat on Ekādaśī is a grave sin, and the fast on that day is the great vow of the Vaiṣṇavas, binding even on those who are not Vaiṣṇavas. Vaiṣṇavas never eat anything but *mahā-prasāda*, and so for them the Ekādaśī fast means giving up grains, even as *mahā-prasāda*.

## 10
w: Śacī  says  —  not  I will eat,  well indeed  You have spoken
w: from then on  Ekādaśī  to observe  she began
tr: Śacī said, “I will not eat it. You have spoken well.” And from that day on she observed Ekādaśī.

## 11
w: then  Miśra  of Viśvarūpa  seeing  the youth
w: a bride  seeking,  marriage  to give  made  up his mind
tr: Then Miśra, seeing that Viśvarūpa had come of age, resolved to find Him a bride and see Him married.

## 12
w: Viśvarūpa  hearing,  home  leaving  fled
w: sannyāsa  taking,  pilgrimage  to make  He went
tr: When Viśvarūpa heard of this, He left home and fled. He took sannyāsa and set out on pilgrimage to the holy places.

## 13
w: hearing,  of Śacī and Miśra  sorrowful  became  the hearts
w: then  the Lord  mother and father  made  consolation
tr: The news filled the hearts of Śacī and Miśra with sorrow, and the Lord set about consoling His mother and father.

## 14
w: good  it is  —  Viśvarūpa  sannyāsa  has taken
w: the father’s line,  the mother’s line  —  both  He has delivered
tr: “It is well that Viśvarūpa has taken sannyāsa: He has delivered both His father’s line and His mother’s.
fn: Heading in the edition: “When a son takes sannyāsa for the worship of Kṛṣṇa, the lines of his mother and father are delivered.”

## 15
w: I  indeed  will do  of you  both  service
w: hearing  content  became  of the father and mother  the hearts
tr: “And I Myself will serve you both.” Hearing this, His father and mother were content at heart.

## 16
w: one day  offered  betel  having eaten
w: to the ground  fell  the Lord  unconscious  becoming
tr: One day, after chewing betel that had been offered to the Deity, the Lord fell to the ground unconscious.

## 17
w: in haste  father and mother  on His face  put  water
w: recovering  tells  the Lord  a wondrous  tale
tr: His father and mother hastily sprinkled water on His face, and when He came to Himself, the Lord told them a wondrous tale.

## 18
w: from here  Viśvarūpa  Me  taking  went
w: sannyāsa  take  You  to Me  He said
tr: “Viśvarūpa took Me away from here and said to Me, ‘You must take sannyāsa.’

## 19
w: I  said  —  My  helpless  father and mother
w: I  am a boy  —  of sannyāsa  what  do I know  the matter
tr: “I answered, ‘My father and mother have no one to look after them, and I am still a boy — what do I know of sannyāsa?

## 20
w: a householder  being  I will do  father’s and mother’s  service
w: by this  pleased  will be  Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa
tr: “ ‘As a householder I shall serve My father and mother, and this will please Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇa.’

## 21
w: then  Viśvarūpa  here  sent  Me
w: to Mother  He sent word  millions  upon millions  of obeisances
tr: “Then Viśvarūpa sent Me back here and bade Me convey His obeisances to Mother, millions upon millions of them.”

## 22
w: in this way  various  pastimes  does  Gaurahari
w: for what  reason  the pastimes  —  this  to understand  not  I can
tr: Thus Gaurahari revels in many pastimes, and why He does so, I cannot understand.

## 23
w: some  days  remaining,  Miśra  went  to the next world
w: mother and son  of both  grew  in the heart  grief
tr: Some time later Miśra departed for the next world, and grief welled up in the hearts of mother and son.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Miśra’s disappearance from the world of men.” The *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Ādi 8): Miśra’s eternally perfect body passed from sight; the Lord wept bitterly for him, as Śrī Rāma wept at the passing of Daśaratha; to tell of this at length is painful, and so the author tells it briefly.

## 24
w: friends and kinsmen  coming  both  consoled
w: the rites for the father  according to the injunctions  the Lord  performed
tr: Friends and kinsmen came and consoled them both, and the Lord performed His father’s funeral rites as the scriptures enjoin.

## 25
w: after some  days  the Lord  in His mind  made  reflection
w: a householder  I have become,  now  I want  household dharma
tr: After a while the Lord thought to Himself, “I have become a householder; now I must take up the duties of household life.

## 26
w: a wife  without  household dharma  not  is  graceful
w: thus  thinking,  marriage  to make  turned  His mind
tr: “Yet without a wife household life has no grace.” Thinking thus, He set His mind on marriage.

## 27
w: not  the house  a home  thus  they call
w: the wife  the home  is called
w: with her  indeed  together  all
w: the goals of life  one attains
tr: “It is not the house that is called the home; the wife is called the home. Together with her one attains all the goals of life.”
fn: In the edition the verse is headed “[A saying of smṛti (of the Lord?)]”; the *Anubhāṣya* refers to the *Mahābhārata* (Śānti-parva, 144.6). The goals of life (*puruṣārtha*), according to the *Anubhāṣya*, are dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa and the rest. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: it is not the dwelling but the wife that is called the home; together with the wife one enjoys all the goals of life.

## 28
w: by providence  one  day  the Lord  from study  while returning
w: Vallabhācārya’s  daughter  He sees  on the path to the Gaṅgā
tr: One day, by providence, as the Lord was coming home from His studies, He saw the daughter of Vallabhācārya on the way to the Gaṅgā.

## 29
w: perfect from of old  the feelings  of both  arising  made
w: by providence  Vanamālī  the matchmaker  to Śacī’s house  came
tr: The love that had been theirs from of old awoke in them both, and, as Providence would have it, Vanamālī the matchmaker came to Śacī’s house.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Vanamālī the matchmaker was a brāhmaṇa of Navadvīpa who arranged the Lord’s marriage. *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (49): Viśvāmitra, the matchmaker at Śrī Rāma’s wedding, and the brāhmaṇa whom Rukmiṇī sent to Śrī Kṛṣṇa are both now Vanamālī, who for this service came to be called Ācārya.

## 30
w: at Śacī’s  sign  the match  made  arrangement
w: Lakṣmī  married  did  Śacī’s  son
tr: At Śacī’s bidding he arranged the match, and the son of Śacī married Lakṣmī.

## 31
w: in detail  described  that  Vṛndāvana dāsa
w: this  indeed  of the boyhood pastimes  the outline  presentation
tr: Vṛndāvana dāsa has described all this in detail; here I have set out the boyhood pastimes in outline.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Ādi 10; Ādi 8 there describes at greater length the Lord’s sacred-thread ceremony (*upanayana*) and the gold He gave His mother.

## 32
w: in the boyhood pastimes  pastimes  many  indeed  kinds
w: Vṛndāvana dāsa  them  has made  elaboration
tr: The Lord’s boyhood holds pastimes of many kinds, and Vṛndāvana dāsa has told them at length.

## 33
w: therefore  a mere glimpse  here  I have shown
w: through the ‘Caitanya-maṅgala’  among all people  fame  came about
tr: Therefore I have given here no more than a glimpse of them; through the *Caitanya-maṅgala* they have become famous throughout the world.

## 34
w: Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
w: “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
