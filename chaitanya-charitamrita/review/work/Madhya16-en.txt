## 0
tr: The Journey to Gauḍa Once Again

## 1
w: the garden of Gauḍa  the Gaura-cloud
w: sprinkling  with the nectar of His glances
w: by the fire of worldly existence  scorched  of the people
w: the creepers  revived
tr: Sprinkling the garden of Gauḍa with the nectar of His glances, the cloud that is Gaura revived the people — creepers scorched by the fire of worldly existence.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Gaurasundara, having come to Gauḍa, devotes Himself to delivering the people.” *Anubhāṣya*: the cloud is Śrī Gaura, the garden is the land of Gauḍa, the creepers are the people scorched by the blaze of worldly existence.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Madhya 4.2, 6.2 and 12.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

## 3
w: of the Lord  arose  the desire  to go  to Vṛndāvana
w: hearing  Pratāparudra  became  downcast
tr: The Lord conceived the desire to go to Vṛndāvana, and when Pratāparudra heard of it, he was downcast.

## 4
w: Sārvabhauma  Rāmānanda  summoning  the two  persons
w: to both  speaks  the king  humble  words
tr: The king summoned the two of them, Sārvabhauma and Rāmānanda, and spoke to them humbly:

## 5
w: Nīlādri  leaving  of the Lord  the mind  elsewhere  to go
w: you  make  effort  Him  to keep
tr: “The Lord has a mind to leave Nīlādri and go elsewhere. Please make every effort to keep Him here.

## 6
w: Him  without  this  kingdom  to me  not  is pleasing
w: the Gosāñi  to keep  employ  various  means
tr: Without Him this kingdom gives me no pleasure. Use every means to keep the Gosāñi here.”

## 7
w: Rāmānanda  Sārvabhauma  with the two
w: then  counsel  takes  the Lord  —  “I will go  to Vṛndāvana”
tr: Then the Lord took counsel with the two of them, Rāmānanda and Sārvabhauma: “I will go to Vṛndāvana.”

## 8
w: the two  say  —  the Ratha-yātrā  do  seeing
w: Kārtika  when it comes  then  make  departure
tr: Both of them said, “See the Ratha-yātrā first; when Kārtika comes, then You may go.”

## 9
w: Kārtika  when it came  they say  —  now  great cold
w: the Dola-yātrā  having seen  go  —  this  good  way
tr: When Kārtika came, they said, “Now it is bitterly cold. See the Dola-yātrā and then go — that is the proper way.”

## 10
w: today–tomorrow  saying  raise  various  pretexts
w: to going  consent  not  give  of separation  out of fear
tr: Saying “today, tomorrow,” they raised one pretext after another; afraid of separation, they would not consent to His going.

## 11
w: although  independent  the Lord  not  is to be stopped
w: the devotees’ wish  without  the Lord  not  makes  departure
tr: Although the Lord is independent and no one can stop Him, He would not leave without the consent of His devotees.

## 12
w: in the third  year  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees
w: to Nīlācala  to go  of all  arose  the wish
tr: In the third year all the devotees of Gauḍa resolved to go to Nīlācala.

## 13
w: all  gathering  went  Advaita Ācārya’s  to the side
w: the Lord  to see  the Ācārya  set out  joyfully
tr: They all gathered and went to Advaita Ācārya, and the Ācārya joyfully set out to see the Lord.

## 14–15
w: although  of the Lord  the order  in Gauḍa  to stay
w: to Nityānanda Prabhu  prema-bhakti  to make manifest
w: nevertheless  set out  Mahāprabhu  to see
w: Nityānanda’s  loving dealings  who  can  understand
tr: Although the Lord had ordered Nityānanda Prabhu to stay in Gauḍa and reveal *prema-bhakti*, Nityānanda set out all the same to see Mahāprabhu. Who can understand the ways of His love?

## 16–17
w: Ācāryaratna  Vidyānidhi  Śrīvāsa  Rāmāi
w: Vāsudeva  Murāri  Govinda and the others  the three brothers
w: Rāghava Paṇḍita  his own bags  having prepared
w: the residents of Kulīna-grāma  go  silk ropes  taking
tr: Ācāryaratna, Vidyānidhi, Śrīvāsa, Rāmāi, Vāsudeva, Murāri, and Govinda with his two brothers set out. Rāghava Paṇḍita packed his bags (*jhāli*) for the journey, and the residents of Kulīna-grāma came bringing silk ropes.
fn: The *jhāli* are Rāghava Paṇḍita’s alone; they are described in Antya 10.

## 18
w: the resident of Khaṇḍa  Narahari  Śrī Raghunandana
w: all the devotees  go  of them  who  makes  a count
tr: Narahari of Khaṇḍa and Śrī Raghunandana came too. All the devotees set out — who can count them?

## 19
w: Śivānanda Sena  does  at the toll stations  the settling
w: all  care  taking  happily  leading  goes
tr: Śivānanda Sena settled the tolls at the crossings; taking care of everyone, he led them happily along.

## 20
w: of all  all the affairs  he does  gives  lodging places
w: Śivānanda  knows  of the Oḍiyā roads  the details
tr: He attended to everyone’s needs and found them places to stay, for Śivānanda knew the roads through Orissa well.

## 21

tr: That year all the ladies went to see the Lord. Acyuta’s mother went with the Ācārya.

## 22
w: with Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita  went  Mālinī
w: with Śivānanda  goes  his  wife
tr: Mālinī went with Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita, and Śivānanda’s wife went with him.

## 23

w: he  set out  the Lord  to see  joyfully

## 24
w: with Ācāryaratna  goes  his  wife
w: of that one  of the love  the account  to tell  not  I know how
tr: Ācāryaratna’s wife went with him. I do not know how to describe her love.
fn: *Tāṅhāra* (“his” or “her”) is taken as referring to the wife: verses 21–25 speak of the devotees’ wives (heading in the edition: “the journey of Candraśekhara’s wife”).

## 25

w: of the Lord  various  favourite  foods  took  from home
tr: To offer Mahāprabhu a meal, all the ladies brought from home many of the Lord’s favourite foods.

## 26
w: Śivānanda Sena  does  all  the arranging
w: the toll collectors  pacifying  gives  to all  lodging places
tr: Śivānanda Sena arranged everything. He pacified the toll collectors (*ghāṭiyāla*) and found lodgings for everyone.

## 27
w: food  giving  does  of all  everywhere  the care
w: in supreme  bliss  goes  of the Lord  to the sight
tr: Everywhere he looked after them all and gave them food, and he went on in supreme bliss to see the Lord.

## 28
w: to Remuṇā  coming  they made  of Gopīnātha  darśana
w: the Ācārya  did  there  kīrtana  dancing
tr: Coming to Remuṇā, they had *darśana* of Gopīnātha, and there the Ācārya sang and danced.

## 29
w: Nityānanda’s  acquaintance  with all the people
w: great  honour  coming  showed  the servants
tr: Nityānanda was known to all the people there, and the temple servants came and showed Him great honour.

## 30
w: that  night  all  the great souls  right there  stayed
w: twelve  pots of kṣīra  bringing  before  the servant  placed
tr: All those great souls stayed there that night, and a servant brought twelve pots of *kṣīra* (sweet rice) and set them before the devotees.

## 31
w: the kṣīra  dividing  to all  gave  Nityānanda Prabhu
w: the kṣīra prasāda  receiving  of all  increased  the bliss
tr: Nityānanda Prabhu divided the *kṣīra* and gave it to everyone, and receiving that *kṣīra-prasāda*, they were all filled with joy.

## 32
w: of Mādhava Purī  the story  of Gopāla  the installation
w: from him  Gopāla  how  asked for  sandalwood
tr: The story of Mādhava Purī, the installation of Gopāla, how Gopāla asked him for sandalwood,
fn: The first line is the second line of Madhya 1.96. *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu had told this story to Nityānanda and His other companions at Remuṇā, on His way to Nīlācala (Madhya 4.19–190).

## 33
w: him  for the sake of  Gopīnātha  the kṣīra  theft  made
w: of Mahāprabhu  from the mouth  before  this  story  they had heard
tr: and how Gopīnātha stole *kṣīra* for his sake — they had heard this story earlier from Mahāprabhu’s own mouth.

## 34
w: that  story  all  among  tells  Nityānanda
w: hearing  in the hearts of the Vaiṣṇavas  increased  the bliss
tr: Now Nityānanda told that story among them all, and the Vaiṣṇavas’ hearts swelled with joy as they listened.

## 35
w: in this way  going on  going on  to Kaṭaka  they came
w: Sākṣi-gopāla  seeing  all  that  day  stayed
tr: Travelling on in this way, they came to Kaṭaka. There they saw Sākṣi-gopāla and stayed that day.

## 36
w: of Sākṣi-gopāla  the story  tells  Nityānanda
w: hearing  in the hearts of the Vaiṣṇavas  increased  the bliss
tr: Nityānanda told the story of Sākṣi-gopāla, and the Vaiṣṇavas’ hearts swelled with joy as they listened.

## 37
w: the Lord  to meet  of all  eagerness  in the heart
w: quickly  making  came  all  to Śrī Nīlācala
tr: Their hearts were all eager to meet the Lord, so they hurried on to Śrī Nīlācala.

## 38
w: at Āṭhāranālā  they have come  the Gosāñi  hearing
w: two garlands  sent  Govinda’s  in the hand  placing
tr: Hearing that they had reached Āṭhāranālā, the Gosāñi placed two garlands in Govinda’s hands and sent him to them.

## 39
w: the two  garlands  Govinda  on the two  persons  put
w: Advaita  the Avadhūta Gosāñi  great  happiness  felt
tr: Govinda put the two garlands on the two of them, and Advaita and the Avadhūta Gosāñi (Nityānanda) were overjoyed.

## 40
w: right there  beginning  made  of Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana
w: dancing  dancing  going  came  the two
tr: Right there they began Kṛṣṇa-saṅkīrtana, and the two of them came on, dancing all the way.

## 41
w: again  garlands  giving  to Svarūpa and the others  to His own men

tr: Then Śacī’s son gave garlands to Svarūpa and His other associates and sent them ahead.

## 42

w: by Mahāprabhu  given  the garlands  on all  they put

## 43

tr: When Gaurarāya heard that they had reached the Siṁha-dvāra, the Lord came Himself and met them all.

## 44

w: all  taking  came  again  to His own residence
tr: Taking them all with Him, He had *darśana* of Jagannātha, and then He brought them all back to His own residence.

## 45
w: Vāṇīnātha  Kāśī Miśra  prasāda  brought
w: with His own hand  all  the Lord  prasāda  fed
tr: Vāṇīnātha and Kāśī Miśra brought *prasāda*, and the Lord served it to everyone with His own hand.

## 46
w: in the previous year  whose  whatever  lodging place
w: there  all  sending  made rest
tr: He sent each of them to the lodging they had had the year before and let them rest.

## 47
w: in this way  the devotees  stayed  four  months
w: the Lord  with  do  the pastimes of kīrtana
tr: In this way the devotees stayed four months, enjoying the pastimes of *kīrtana* with the Lord.

## 48
w: as before  the time of the Ratha-yātrā  when  came

tr: When the time of the Ratha-yātrā came, He took them all, as before, and washed the Guṇḍicā temple.

## 49

tr: The residents of Kulīna-grāma gave silk ropes to Jagannātha, and as before He danced in front of the chariot.

## 50
w: much  dancing  having done  then  went  to the garden
w: on the bank of the pond  there  going  made  rest
tr: After dancing a great deal He went to the garden, and there He rested on the bank of the pond.

## 51
w: from Rāḍha  one  brāhmaṇa  he  —  a servant of Nityānanda
w: most fortunate  he  name  —  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: There was a most fortunate brāhmaṇa from Rāḍha named Kṛṣṇadāsa, a servant of Nityānanda.

## 52
w: a pitcher  filling  of the Lord  he  bathing  did
w: his  by the bathing  the Lord  greatly pleased  became
tr: He filled a pitcher and bathed the Lord, and the Lord was greatly pleased.

## 53
w: of the Balagaṇḍi offering  much  prasāda  came
w: all  with  Mahāprabhu  prasāda  ate
tr: A great deal of *prasāda* came from the Balagaṇḍi offering, and Mahāprabhu honoured the *prasāda* with them all.

## 54
w: as before  the Ratha-yātrā  He made  seeing
w: the Herā-pañcamī festival  sees  taking  the devotees
tr: As before, He watched the Ratha-yātrā, and with His devotees He saw the festival of Herā-pañcamī.

## 55
w: Ācārya Gosāñi  of the Lord  made  invitation
w: that  in the midst of  made  how  a storm of rain
tr: Ācārya Gosāñi invited the Lord, and in the midst of it He brought on a storm of wind and rain.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya-khaṇḍa, chapter 8. Advaita wished the Lord to come without the other sannyāsīs; they had gone to perform their midday rites when the storm broke, and the Lord came alone and ate Advaita’s rice and vegetables.

## 56

w: Śrīvāsa  the Lord  then  made  invitation
tr: Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail. Then Śrīvāsa invited the Lord.
fn: The first line is the same as in Madhya 3.217, 5.140 and 15.13 (and in verse 81 of this chapter).

## 57

w: “in devotion  a maidservant” — such self-conception  “in affection  a mother”
tr: Mālinī cooked all the Lord’s favourite dishes. In devotion she thought of herself as His maidservant; in affection she was His mother.

## 58
w: Ācāryaratna and the others  as many  chief  devotees
w: from time  to time  the Lord  make  invitation
tr: Ācāryaratna and the other leading devotees invited the Lord from time to time.

## 59

tr: At the end of Cāturmāsya the Lord would again take Nityānanda aside every day and sit with Him in private, holding some counsel.

## 60
w: Ācārya Gosāñi  to the Lord  speaks  in hints
w: the Ācārya  riddles  recites  anyone  to understand  not  is able
tr: Ācārya Gosāñi spoke to the Lord in hints; the Ācārya recited riddles (*tarjā*) that no one could understand.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *tarjā* is speech in the *payāra* and other metres that others cannot easily understand.

## 61
w: his  face  seeing  smiles  of Śacī  the son
w: acceptance  knowing  the Ācārya  does  dancing
tr: Śacī’s son looked at Advaita’s face and smiled, and the Ācārya, taking this as the Lord’s assent, began to dance.

## 62
w: whether  a prayer  whether  an order  —  anyone  not  understood
w: embrace  making  the Lord  him  leave  gave
tr: Whether it was a prayer or an instruction, no one understood. Then the Lord embraced Him and bade Him farewell.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: what Śrī Advaita asked in His *tarjā*, and what Śacī’s son meant by His smile, no one else could understand.

## 63
w: to Nityānanda  says  the Lord  —  listen  Śrīpāda
w: this  I  beg  You  grant  the favour
tr: The Lord said to Nityānanda, “Listen, Śrīpāda: I beg this of You — grant Me this favour.

## 64
w: every year  to Nīlācala  You  not  should come
w: in Gauḍa  staying  My  desire  fulfilled  should make
tr: Do not come to Nīlācala every year. Stay in Gauḍa and fulfil My desire.

## 65
w: that  fulfilment  does  —  such  another  not  I see
w: My  difficult  work  You  from  is accomplished
tr: I see no one else who could accomplish it. My difficult work can be done only by You.”

## 66
w: Nityānanda  says  —  I  the body  You  the life
w: the body  the life  different  not are  —  this  indeed  the proof
tr: Nityānanda said, “I am the body and You are the life. Body and life are not separate; that is beyond doubt.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: body and life are never apart; that the Lord lives in Nīlācala and Nityānanda in Gauḍa is due only to the Lord’s inconceivable power.

## 67
w: by inconceivable potency  bring about  You  of that  the accomplishment
w: whatever  You make Me do  that  I do  there is no  rule

## 68
w: Him  leave  gave  the Lord  making  an embrace

## 69

w: Lord  an order  give  —  my  duty  practice
tr: As before, a resident of Kulīna-grāma submitted, “Lord, please instruct me: what is my duty, my practice?”

## 70
w: the Lord  says  —  of the Vaiṣṇavas  service  saṅkīrtana of the name
w: two  do  quickly  you will obtain  the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa
tr: The Lord said, “Serve the Vaiṣṇavas and chant the holy name in *saṅkīrtana*. Do these two, and you will quickly attain the feet of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.”

## 71
w: he  says  —  who  a Vaiṣṇava  what  his  symptom
w: then  smiling  says  the Lord  knowing  his  mind
tr: He asked, “Who is a Vaiṣṇava? What are his marks?” Then the Lord, knowing his mind, smiled and said,

## 72
w: the name of Kṛṣṇa  constantly  whose  in the mouth
w: he  the best of the Vaiṣṇavas  worship  his  feet
tr: “One on whose lips the name of Kṛṣṇa is always present is the best of Vaiṣṇavas. Worship his feet.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: one on whose lips the name of Kṛṣṇa is present “constantly” is the intermediate devotee (*madhyama-bhāgavata*), higher than the beginner (*kaniṣṭha*), who utters the name once (Madhya 15.106). “Constantly” means without any gap — free of other desires, *karma*, *jñāna* and slackness.

## 73
w: after a year  again  they  such  a question  asked
w: of the Vaiṣṇavas  the gradation  the Lord  taught
tr: A year later they asked the same question again, and the Lord taught them the gradation of Vaiṣṇavas:

## 74
w: whose  at the sight  to the mouth  comes  the name of Kṛṣṇa
w: him  know  you  the foremost Vaiṣṇava
tr: “One whose very sight brings the name of Kṛṣṇa to the lips — know him to be the foremost Vaiṣṇava.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: this is the *mahā-bhāgavata* (*uttama-adhikārī*): at the sight of him the name of Kṛṣṇa comes of itself to the lips of the beholder, and by his mercy the intermediate devotee becomes the highest.

## 75
w: in order  making  speaks  the Lord  of a Vaiṣṇava  the marks
w: Vaiṣṇava  a higher Vaiṣṇava  and  the highest Vaiṣṇava
tr: Step by step the Lord described the marks of a Vaiṣṇava: the Vaiṣṇava, the higher Vaiṣṇava (*vaiṣṇavatara*) and the highest Vaiṣṇava (*vaiṣṇavatama*).
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord’s answers to the residents of Kulīna-grāma over three years (Madhya 15.106 and verses 72, 74) show these three grades; to serve all three is the duty of a Vaiṣṇava householder.

## 76
w: in this way  all  the Vaiṣṇavas  to Gauḍa  went
w: Vidyānidhi  that  year  in Nīlādri  stayed
tr: In this way all the Vaiṣṇavas returned to Gauḍa. That year Vidyānidhi stayed on in Nīlādri.

## 77
w: with Svarūpa  his  is  friendly affection
w: of the two  in talks of Kṛṣṇa  together  the abiding
tr: He had a loving friendship with Svarūpa, and the two of them stayed together, absorbed in talks about Kṛṣṇa.

## 78
w: to Gadādhara Paṇḍita  he  again  mantra  gave
w: of Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī  on the day  the festival  which  he saw
tr: He gave the *mantra* to Gadādhara Paṇḍita a second time, and he saw the festival on the day of Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī.

## 79
w: Jagannātha  wears  there  starched  cloth
w: seeing  disgusted  became  Vidyānidhi’s  mind
tr: There Jagannātha is dressed in starched (*māḍuyā*) cloth, and when Vidyānidhi saw this, he felt disgust.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī is the first *ṣaṣṭhī* of the cold season, when Jagannātha is dressed in warm clothing; this is *māḍuyā*, the weaver’s unwashed cloth, stiff with starch. Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi found fault with it and showed some disdain for the devotees of Utkala, and so received his due.

## 80
w: that  night  Jagannātha and Balāi  coming

## 81

w: in detail  has described  Vṛndāvana  dāsa
tr: His cheeks swelled, but the Ācārya (Vidyānidhi) rejoiced at heart. Vṛndāvana dāsa has described this in detail.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya-khaṇḍa, chapters 10 and 11.

## 82
w: in this way  every year  come  of Gauḍa  the devotees
w: with the Lord  staying  do  of the festival  seeing

## 83
w: that  among  in which  which  years  there is  something special

tr: Whatever was special in particular years, I shall relate fully and in detail later on.

## 84

## 268
w: at night  in My mind  I  consideration  made
w: Sanātana  to Me  what  riddle  spoke
tr: That night I pondered in My mind the riddle that Sanātana had told Me.

## 269
w: well  indeed  he spoke  My  so many  people  with Me
w: people  seeing  will say  of Me  “this  is some  show”
tr: He spoke well. I have so many people with Me; seeing this, people will say of Me, ‘This is just a show!’

## 270
w: rare  hard to reach  that  solitary  Vṛndāvana
w: alone  I shall go  or  with  one person
tr: That Vṛndāvana is rare, hard to reach and solitary. I shall go alone, or with just one companion.

## 271
w: Mādhavendra Purī  there  went  alone
w: on the pretext of giving milk  Kṛṣṇa  His direct presence  gave  to him
tr: Mādhavendra Purī went there alone, and on the pretext of bringing him milk, Kṛṣṇa appeared to him in person.

## 272
w: a juggler’s  show  setting up  I went  there
w: with a crowd  to Vṛndāvana  journey  not  one makes
tr: I was travelling there like a juggler with his show. One does not go to Vṛndāvana with a crowd.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *bādiyā*, or *bede*, are wandering jugglers; wherever they set up their show a crowd gathers, and the Lord was going to Vṛndāvana with just such a crowd, which was not good.

## 273
w: alone  I shall go  or  with  servant  one
w: then  indeed  is graceful  to Vṛndāvana  the going
tr: I shall go alone, or with just one servant; only then is a journey to Vṛndāvana as it should be.

## 274
w: to Vṛndāvana  I shall go  where  alone  being
w: an army  with  I have set out  drums  beating
tr: I was to go to Vṛndāvana alone — and here I have set out with an army, beating drums!

## 275
w: shame  shame  to Myself  saying  I became  restless
w: turning back  becoming  again  I came  to the bank of the Gaṅgā
tr: Saying ‘Shame, shame on Me!’, I grew restless, and turning back, I came again to the bank of the Gaṅgā.

## 276
w: the devotees  leaving  I came  in place after place
w: with Me  came  in all  five or six  persons
tr: I left the devotees in their various places and came back; only five or six people came with Me.

## 277
w: without obstacles  now  how  shall I go  to Vṛndāvana
w: all  together  counsel  give  becoming  gracious
tr: Now, how am I to go to Vṛndāvana without obstacles? All of you together, be gracious and give Me your counsel.

## 278
w: Gadādhara  leaving  I went  he  sorrow  felt
w: that  for reason  to Vṛndāvana  to go  I could not
tr: I went away leaving Gadādhara behind, and he was grieved. For that reason I could not go to Vṛndāvana.”

## 279
w: then  Gadādhara Paṇḍita  absorbed in love  becoming
w: the Lord’s feet  clasping  says  humility  showing
tr: Then Gadādhara Paṇḍita, absorbed in love, clasped the Lord’s feet and humbly said,

## 280
w: You  wherever  stay  there  is Vṛndāvana
w: there  the Yamunā  the Gaṅgā  all the holy places
tr: “Wherever You stay, there is Vṛndāvana, and there are the Yamunā, the Gaṅgā and all the holy places.

## 281
w: still  to Vṛndāvana  You go  people  to teach
w: that very thing  You will do  Your  which  takes  heart
tr: Still, You go to Vṛndāvana to teach people. You will do just what Your heart decides.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu’s heart is itself Vṛndāvana, the place of Śrī Rādhākānta’s pastimes; still, to teach people, He goes to the earthly Vṛndāvana.

## 282
w: now  ahead  have come  Lord  of the rains  four  months
w: these  four  months  make  in Nīlācala  residence
tr: Lord, the four months of the rains are almost upon us. Spend these four months in Nīlācala.

## 283
w: afterwards  that  You will do  which  Your  mind
w: by Your own will  go  stay  who  makes  prohibition
tr: Afterwards do whatever You have in mind. Go or stay as You please — who could stop You?”

## 284
w: hearing  all  the devotees  say  the Lord’s  at the feet
w: of everyone  the desire  the Paṇḍita  has made  submission
tr: Hearing this, all the devotees said at the Lord’s feet, “The Paṇḍita has put forward what we all desire.”

## 285
w: everyone’s  by the desire  the Lord  four months  stayed
w: hearing  Pratāparudra  delighted  became
tr: At everyone’s wish the Lord stayed for the four months, and when Pratāparudra heard this, he was delighted.

## 286
w: that  day  Gadādhara  made  invitation
w: there  meal  took  the Lord  taking  the devotees
tr: That day Gadādhara invited Him, and the Lord took His meal there with the devotees.

## 287
w: at the meal  the Paṇḍita’s  affection  the Lord’s  relishing
w: by human  power  the two  not  can  be described
tr: The Paṇḍita’s affection at that meal and the Lord’s relishing of it — neither can be described by human power.

## 288
w: in this  way  the pastimes of Gaura  endless  boundless
w: in brief  I tell;  to tell  not  goes  in full
tr: Such are Gaura’s pastimes, endless and boundless. I tell them in brief, for they cannot be told in full.
fn: The second line is the same as in Ādi 13.53.

## 289
w: with a thousand mouths  tells  Himself  Ananta
w: still  of one  pastime  He  not  reaches  the end
tr: Ananta Himself tells of them with His thousand mouths, yet He cannot reach the end of even one pastime.

## 290
w: Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
w: “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
fn: The verse = Madhya 7.155 (the closing refrain); the translation is the same as there.
