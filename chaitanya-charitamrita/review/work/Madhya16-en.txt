## 0
tr: The Journey to Gauḍa Once Again

## 1

w: sprinkling  with the nectar of His glances
w: by the fire of worldly existence  scorched  of the people
w: the creepers  revived
tr: Sprinkling the garden of Gauḍa with the nectar of His glances, the cloud that is Gaura revived the people — creepers scorched by the fire of worldly existence.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Gaurasundara, having come to Gauḍa, devotes Himself to delivering the people.” *Anubhāṣya*: the cloud is Śrī Gaura, the garden is the land of Gauḍa, the creepers are the people scorched by the blaze of worldly existence.

## 2

w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Madhya 4.2, 6.2 and 12.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

## 4

tr: “The Lord has a mind to leave Nīlādri and go elsewhere. Please make every effort to keep Him here.

## 6

tr: When Kārtika came, they said, “Now it is bitterly cold. See the Dola-yātrā and then go — that is the proper way.”

## 10

tr: Saying “today, tomorrow,” they raised one pretext after another; afraid of separation, they would not consent to His going.

tr: Although the Lord is independent and no one can stop Him, He would not leave without the consent of His devotees.

## 14–15

tr: Although the Lord had ordered Nityānanda Prabhu to stay in Gauḍa and reveal *prema-bhakti*, He nevertheless set out to see Mahāprabhu. Who can understand the ways of Nityānanda’s love?

fn: The first line is the second line of Madhya 1.96. *Anubhāṣya*: Mahāprabhu had told this story to Nityānanda and His other companions at Remuṇā, on His way to Nīlācala (Madhya 4.19–190).

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *tarjā* is speech in the *payāra* and other metres that others cannot easily understand.

tr: Whether it was a prayer or an instruction, no one understood. Then the Lord embraced Him and bade Him farewell.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: what Śrī Advaita asked in His *tarjā*, and what Śacī’s son meant by His smile, no one else could understand.

## 63

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: body and life are never apart; that the Lord lives in Nīlācala and Nityānanda in Gauḍa is due only to the Lord’s inconceivable power.

## 71
w: he  says  —  who  a Vaiṣṇava  what  his  symptom

## 72
w: the name of Kṛṣṇa  constantly  whose  in the mouth
w: he  the best of the Vaiṣṇavas  worship  his  feet
tr: “One on whose lips the name of Kṛṣṇa is always present is the best of Vaiṣṇavas. Worship his feet.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: one on whose lips the name of Kṛṣṇa is present “constantly” is the intermediate devotee (*madhyama-bhāgavata*), higher than the beginner (*kaniṣṭha*), who utters the name once (Madhya 15.106). “Constantly” means without any gap — free of other desires, *karma*, *jñāna* and slackness.

## 73
w: after a year  again  they  such  a question  asked
w: of the Vaiṣṇavas  the gradation  the Lord  taught

## 74
w: whose  at the sight  to the mouth  comes  the name of Kṛṣṇa
w: him  know  you  the foremost Vaiṣṇava
tr: “One whose very sight brings the name of Kṛṣṇa to the lips — know him to be the foremost Vaiṣṇava.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: this is the *mahā-bhāgavata* (*uttama-adhikārī*): at the sight of him the name of Kṛṣṇa comes of itself to the lips of the beholder, and by his mercy the intermediate devotee becomes the highest.

## 75

w: Vaiṣṇava  a higher Vaiṣṇava  and  the highest Vaiṣṇava
tr: Step by step the Lord described the marks of a Vaiṣṇava: the Vaiṣṇava, the higher Vaiṣṇava (*vaiṣṇavatara*) and the highest Vaiṣṇava (*vaiṣṇavatama*).
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord’s answers to the residents of Kulīna-grāma over three years (Madhya 15.106 and verses 72, 74) show these three grades; to serve all three is the duty of a Vaiṣṇava householder.

## 77

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Oḍana-ṣaṣṭhī is the first *ṣaṣṭhī* of the cold season, when Jagannātha is dressed in warm clothing; this is *māḍuyā*, the weaver’s unwashed cloth, stiff with starch. Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi found fault with it and showed some disdain for the devotees of Utkala, and so received his due.

## 85
