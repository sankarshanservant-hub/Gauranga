# English translation, Ādi-līlā 4 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: An Account of the Root Purpose of Caitanya’s Descent

## 1
w: of Śrī Caitanya  by the grace
w: of His  form  the ascertainment
w: a child  even  makes  scripture
w: having seen  of the Enjoyer in Vraja
tr: By the grace of Śrī Caitanya even a child, looking into the scriptures, can ascertain the true form of Him who sports in Vraja.
fn: In the edition the verse is headed “By the grace of Gaura, the nature of Śrī Kṛṣṇa ascertained”. *Anubhāṣya*: “a child” — one who knows nothing; “His form” — the form of Gaura, who is not different from Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa; “the Enjoyer in Vraja” — the son of the king of Vraja; “ascertainment” — pointing out the truth (*tattva*).

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: of the fourth  verse  of the meaning  I have made  the explanation
w: of the fifth  verse  the meaning  hear  giving  the mind
tr: I have explained the meaning of the fourth verse. Now hear with attention the meaning of the fifth.

## 4
w: of the root  verse  the meaning  in order to make  manifest
w: the meaning  in order to apply  first  I speak  an outline
tr: To bring out the meaning of the root verse, I shall first give an outline before applying it.

## 5
w: of the fourth  verse  of the meaning  this  I have made  the essence
w: love and the name  to spread  this  descent
tr: The essence of the fourth verse, as I have explained it, is this: the Lord descended to spread divine love and the holy name.

## 6
w: true  this  reason  but  this indeed  external
w: another  one  reason  hear  there is  internal
tr: This reason is true, but it is external. There is another reason, an inner one — hear it.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «এতো» (another reading: «এহো», “this too”); the sense is the same. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Gaurāṅga descended to preach bhakti and the name of Kṛṣṇa; that reason is true, but it is external, not the hidden one.

## 7
w: formerly  so  of the earth  the burden  to remove
w: Kṛṣṇa  descended  became  in the scriptures  they proclaim
tr: The scriptures proclaim that in the past Kṛṣṇa descended to remove the burden of the earth.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «হেন» (“so”); another reading has «যেন» (“as”); the sense is the same.

## 8
w: of Bhagavān Himself  the work  is not  the removal of the burden
w: the maintainer  Viṣṇu  does  of the world  the protection
tr: But removing the burden is not the work of Bhagavān Himself. It is Viṣṇu, the maintainer, who protects the world.
fn: In the edition the verse is headed: “Viṣṇu’s work is the deliverance of the saints and the destruction of the wicked; it is not the work of Kṛṣṇa Himself.”

## 9
w: but  of Kṛṣṇa  which  is  of descent  the time
w: of the removal of the burden  the time  with it  became  mixed
tr: But the time of Kṛṣṇa’s descent coincided with the time for removing that burden.

## 10
w: the complete  Bhagavān  descends  at which  time
w: other  all  avatāras  in Him  coming  meet
tr: When the complete Bhagavān descends, all the other avatāras come and meet in Him.

## 11–12
w: Nārāyaṇa,  the Catur-vyūha,  Matsya and the other  avatāras
w: of the yugas  of the manvantaras  the avatāras  as many as  there are  others
w: all  coming  in Kṛṣṇa’s  body  become  descended
w: thus  descends  Kṛṣṇa  Bhagavān  complete
tr: Nārāyaṇa, the Catur-vyūha, Matsya and the other avatāras, the avatāras of the yugas and the manvantaras, and as many others as there are — all come and descend within the body of Kṛṣṇa. So does Kṛṣṇa, the complete Bhagavān, descend.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Catur-vyūha are Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna and Aniruddha; Matsya and the others are partial avatāras (*aṁśāvatāra*). All the avatāras are parts of the complete Bhagavān and take shelter in Him, and so Viṣṇu, the maintainer of the world, was then present within the form of Kṛṣṇa.

## 13
w: therefore  Viṣṇu  then  of Kṛṣṇa  in the body
w: through Viṣṇu  Kṛṣṇa  makes  of the demons  the slaying
tr: Therefore Viṣṇu was then within the body of Kṛṣṇa, and through Viṣṇu Kṛṣṇa slew the demons.

## 14
w: secondary  work  this  of the demons  the killing
w: for which  the descent  I tell  that  root  cause
tr: The killing of the demons is a secondary work. Now I shall tell the root cause for which He descends.

## 15–16
w: of the rasa of love  the essence  in order to make  tasting
w: of the path of rāga  devotion  in the world  in order to make  spreading
w: of the relishers of rasa  the crown  Kṛṣṇa  supremely merciful
w: these  two  reasons  from  of the desire  the rising
tr: To taste the very essence of the rasa of love, and to spread in the world devotion on the path of rāga (*rāga-mārga*) — from these two reasons the desire arose in Kṛṣṇa, the crown of all who relish rasa, who is supremely merciful.
fn: In the edition the verse is headed: “Viṣṇu descends to preach *vidhi-bhakti*; Kṛṣṇa comes as Gaura to preach *rāga-bhakti*.”

## 17
w: of majesty  with the awareness  the whole  world  is mixed
w: by majesty  slackened  in love  is not  My  pleasure
tr: The whole world is permeated by the awareness of My majesty, and love slackened by awe gives Me no joy.
fn: The same verse as Ādi 3.16 (the *Anubhāṣya* refers to it). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in love born of the awareness of majesty prema slackens, and Kṛṣṇa takes no pleasure in it; one who thinks himself lowly and Him the Lord loves Him with that awareness, and to such love He is not subject.

## 18
w: Me  as the Lord  considers  himself  as low
w: his  by love  subdued  I  not  become  dependent
tr: One who regards Me as the Lord and himself as lowly — by his love I am not subdued; I do not become dependent on him.

## 19
w: to Me  indeed  whichever  whichever  devotee  worships  in which  mood
w: him  in that  in that  mood  I worship  this  My  nature
tr: In whatever mood a devotee worships Me, in that very mood I reciprocate with him. Such is My nature.

## 20
w: who  as  to Me  surrender
w: them  in that same way  I reward  I
w: My  path  follow
w: men  O Pārtha  in every way
tr: “As people surrender to Me, so do I reward them. O Pārtha, all men follow My path in every way.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 4.11)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord speaks this verse about the cause of His manifest pastimes; “I reward” — “I show favour”; “My path” — the path of perfection. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “O Pārtha, in whatever way one worships Me, in that way I become attainable to him; all men follow the path shown by Me.”

## 21–22
w: my  son  my  friend  my  of life  lord
w: in this mood  whoever  to Me  makes  pure devotion
w: himself  great  considers  Me  equal or lower
w: in that  mood  become  I  his  dependent
tr: Whoever renders Me pure devotion in the mood “He is my son, my friend, the lord of my life”, considering himself great and Me equal or lower — in that mood I become subject to him.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: pure devotion (*śuddha-bhakti*) is the cultivation of Kṛṣṇa with a favourable intent, free of other desires and uncovered by knowledge and fruitive work. *Anubhāṣya*: devotion mixed with other desires, knowledge, work or yoga is “mixed devotion” (*viddha-bhakti*); devotion by rule (*vaidhī*) assists pure devotion, but the word *śuddha-bhakti* points to the service of the *rāgānugā* path — the devotion of Goloka, full of rāga, while *vaidhī-bhakti*, full of reverence, belongs to Vaikuṇṭha.

## 23
w: to Me  devotion  indeed  of beings
w: for immortality  is fit
w: by good fortune  which  arose  for Me  the love
w: of you  bringing Me
tr: “Devotion to Me brings living beings to immortality. And how fortunate it is that you have love for Me — for it brings you to Me!” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.82.44)
fn: Kṛṣṇa’s words to the gopīs at Kurukṣetra (Samanta-pañcaka), where, at a solar eclipse, both the Yādavas from Dvārakā and Nanda Mahārāja with all Vraja had come. *Anubhāṣya*: “immortality” — eternal service as the Lord’s associate; their love “brings Me” — grants direct meeting with Him; “by good fortune” — “by My good fortune”.

## 24
w: mother  Me  in the mood of a son  makes  binding
w: utterly helpless  thinking  does  fondling  caring
tr: My mother binds Me in the mood of a son; thinking Me utterly helpless, she fondles and cares for Me.

## 25
w: a friend  in pure friendship  makes  on the shoulders  climbing
w: You  what  great  person  You  I  equal
tr: My friend, in pure friendship, climbs on My shoulders and says, “What great man are You? You and I are equal!”

## 26
w: the beloved  if  pique  showing  makes  rebuke
w: of the Vedas  the hymns  than  steals  that  My  mind
tr: When My beloved, in her pique (*māna*), rebukes Me, that steals My heart more than the hymns of the Vedas.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the rebuke with which the lover (*āśraya*), in pure attachment, chides the Beloved (*viṣaya*) is a sign of utmost, exclusive love; where the Beloved is honoured as worshipful, natural love slackens, and so such rebuke stands higher than the awe-filled praise of the Vedas.

## 27–28
w: this  pure devotion  taking  I shall make  descent
w: I shall make  of many kinds  wondrous  pastimes
w: in Vaikuṇṭha and elsewhere  there is not  which  which  of pastimes  the display
w: those  those  pastimes  I shall perform  in which  My  wonder
tr: Taking this pure devotion, I shall descend and perform many kinds of wondrous pastimes. I shall perform pastimes such as are not shown even in Vaikuṇṭha — pastimes that astonish even Me.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the pastimes not found in Vaikuṇṭha and the other worlds Kṛṣṇa will show in this descent, and in them He Himself will be astonished. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vaikuṇṭha and the other realms beyond māyā the Lord’s pastimes do not give Him Himself the ever-new joy of wonder; the excellence of the pastimes of Goloka, where He lives for His own joy, He wishes to show in the world to devotees in whom the sense of majesty prevails.

## 29
w: toward Me  of the gopīs  in the mood of a paramour (*upapati*)
w: Yogamāyā  will make  by her own  power
tr: By her own power Yogamāyā will awaken in the gopīs a love for Me as a lover who is not their husband (*upapati*).
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Yogamāyā is Kṛṣṇa’s own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*); by His will and by her inconceivable power she awakens in the hearts of His eternal beloveds, the gopīs, the mood of *upapati* toward Him. *Anubhāṣya*: the tendency to *parakīyā* service, in which the lover, drawn by a sweetness (*mādhurya*) that surpasses majesty, passes beyond all ordained reverence, comes from Yogamāyā; this working of the spiritual (*cinmayī*) māyā is unknown even to the Beloved Himself, and is possible only through Yogamāyā, the embodiment of His grace.

## 30
w: I too  not  know  that  not  know  the gopīs
w: of the two  by beauty and qualities  of the two  always  steals  the mind
tr: Neither I nor the gopīs will know of it, for each of us will ever steal the other’s heart with beauty and virtues.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: for the nourishment of rasa My inconceivable potency will cover My omniscience and give rise to a wondrous rasa, and the gopīs, though they are the Lord’s own potency, will not know it either.

## 31
w: dharma  leaving  in rāga  the two  make  meeting
w: sometimes  meet  sometimes  not  meet  of destiny  the arrangement
tr: Leaving dharma behind, the two will meet in love (*rāga*) — sometimes meeting, sometimes not, as destiny arranges.

## 32
w: these  all  of rasa  essences  I shall make  tasting
w: by this  means  I shall make  to all  devotees  grace
tr: I shall taste the essence of all these rasas, and by this means I shall show grace to all My devotees.

## 33
w: of Vraja  the pure  love  hearing  the devotees
w: on the path of rāga  worship  so that  leaving  religious duties
tr: Hearing of the pure love (*rāga*) of Vraja, the devotees will worship Me on the path of rāga, leaving aside their religious duties (*dharma-karma*).
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the pure rāga that Kṛṣṇa will show in Vraja is the means by which He gives rasa to all devotees: hearing of it, they will leave *dharma-karma* and worship Him on the path of rāga.
