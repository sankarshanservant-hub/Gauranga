## 0
tr: A Brief Description of the Madhya-līlā

## 1
w: whose  by the grace  an ignorant person  even
w: at once  omniscience  attains
w: that  Śrī Caitanyadeva  to me
w: the Lord  may He be fully gracious
tr: May the Lord Śrī Caitanyadeva be fully gracious to me — He by whose grace even an ignorant person at once becomes all-knowing.

## 2
w: I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
w: and Nityānanda  who rose together
w: on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and moon
w: wonderful  bestowing good  dispelling darkness
tr: I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Śrī Nityānanda, who are like a wonderful sun and moon. They have risen together on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa, dispelling the darkness and bestowing good on all.
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.2 and 1.84; translation as there.

## 3
w: glory  to the merciful (Pair)  of the lame
w: of me  of dull mind  the refuge
w: whose lotus feet are my all
w: Rādhā  and Madana-mohana
tr: All glory to the merciful Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Madana-mohana! I am lame and dull of mind, and They are my only refuge; Their lotus feet are all I have.
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.15; translation as there. Edition heading: “Obeisance to the Deity who presides over *sambandha* (the relationship with the Lord)”. *Anubhāṣya* (to Ādi 1.15): *manda-mati* — “a mind absorbed in the world, feeble, unable to follow other paths such as karma or jñāna”; *suratau* — “merciful”, or “deeply attached to each other”.

## 4
w: shining  in Vṛndāvana forest  wish-fulfilling  tree  beneath
w: in a beautiful  jewel temple  on a lion-throne  seated
w: Śrī Śrī  Rādhā  and Śrīla  Govinda  the Deities
w: by Their dearest friends  being served  I remember
tr: I remember Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Śrī Govindadeva, seated on a throne in a beautiful temple of jewels beneath a wish-fulfilling tree in the radiant forest of Vṛndāvana, served by Their dearest companions.
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.16; translation as there. Edition heading: “Obeisance to the Deity who presides over *abhidheya* (the means)”. *Anubhāṣya* (to Ādi 1.16): *preṣṭhālī* — Lalitā and the other *priya-narma-sakhīs*, attended by Śrī Rūpa-mañjarī and others.

## 5
w: the beautiful  of the rāsa dance’s  sweetness  the initiator
w: of Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing
w: drawing  with the flute’s  sounds  the gopīs
w: of the gopīs  the Lord  for good  may be  ours
tr: May the beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha, who begins the sweet rāsa dance, be our good fortune. Standing on the bank by Vaṁśīvaṭa, He draws the gopīs to Himself with the sounds of His flute.
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.17; translation as there. Edition heading: “Obeisance to the Deity who presides over *prayojana* (the goal)”. *Anubhāṣya* (to Ādi 1.17): the flute “loosens every desire in the gopīs other than for Kṛṣṇa and draws them from their homes with the rope of prema”; *śriye* — “for the wealth of prema”.

## 6
w: glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to the ocean of mercy
w: glory  glory  to Śacī’s son  glory  to the friend of the fallen
tr: Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to the ocean of mercy! Glory, glory to the son of Śacī! Glory to the friend of the fallen!

## 7
w: glory  glory  to Nityānanda  glory to Advaitacandra
w: glory  glory  headed by Śrīvāsa  of Gaura  the devotees  to the host
tr: Glory, glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory, glory to all the devotees of Gaura, headed by Śrīvāsa!

## 8
w: earlier  I told  of the Ādi-līlā  the sūtras
w: which  has described in full  Vṛndāvana dāsa
tr: Earlier I told the *sūtras* (brief threads) of the Ādi-līlā, which Vṛndāvana dāsa has described in full.

## 9
w: therefore  of it  I  only the sūtras  made
w: whatever  something  particular  within the sūtras themselves  I told
tr: Therefore I gave only the sūtras of it; whatever particulars there were, I told within the sūtras themselves.

## 10
w: now  I tell  of the final  pastimes  the main  sūtras
w: of the Lord  endless  pastimes  not  can be  described
tr: Now I shall tell the main sūtras of the final pastimes (Śeṣa-līlā). The Lord’s pastimes are endless; they cannot be fully described.

## 11
w: among them  which  portion  Vṛndāvana dāsa
w: in the Caitanya-maṅgala  in detail  made  a description
tr: Of these, the portion that Vṛndāvana dāsa described in detail in his *Caitanya-maṅgala* —

## 12
w: of that  portion  here  the sūtras  only  I shall write
w: there  whatever  particular  something  here  I shall expand
tr: — of that portion I shall write here only the sūtras, and whatever special things were told there only in passing I shall describe here in full.
fn: Edition heading (to 11–12): “A promise: the events described in detail in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* will be given as sūtras, and the chief events that are told there only briefly will be set out in detail.”

## 13
w: of Caitanya’s pastimes  the Vyāsa  —  dāsa  Vṛndāvana
w: by his  order  I do  his  remnants  chewing
tr: Vṛndāvana dāsa is the Vyāsa of Śrī Caitanya’s pastimes. By his order I only chew the remnants of his meal.
fn: The first line is the same as in Ādi 8.34 and 13.48. *Anubhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana Vyāsa wrote of the avatāras and of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes; Vṛndāvana dāsa Ṭhākura, who wrote of Śrī Caitanya’s pastimes, is Vyāsa himself (*vyāsa-svarūpa*); Kṛṣṇadāsa writes of what he left untold as his servant and follower.

## 14
w: with devotion  on my head  holding  his  feet
w: of the final pastimes  the sūtras  now  I make  description
tr: With devotion I hold his feet upon my head, and now I describe the sūtras of the final pastimes.

## 15
w: twenty-four  years  the Lord’s  at home  stay
w: there  which  He performed  pastimes  Ādi-līlā  the name
tr: For twenty-four years the Lord lived at home; the pastimes He showed there are known as the Ādi-līlā.

## 16
w: of the twenty-fourth  year  at the end  which  the month of Māgha
w: in its  bright fortnight  the Lord  took  sannyāsa
tr: At the close of His twenty-fourth year, in the bright fortnight of the month of Māgha, the Lord accepted *sannyāsa*.

## 17
w: sannyāsa  having taken  twenty-four  years  stay
w: there  which  pastimes  their  Śeṣa-līlā  name
tr: After taking sannyāsa He remained for twenty-four years; the pastimes of that time are called the Śeṣa-līlā.

## 18
w: of the Śeṣa-līlā  ‘Madhya’  ‘Anta’  two  names  there are
w: by the difference of pastimes  Vaiṣṇavas  all  difference of names  speak
tr: The Śeṣa-līlā has two names, ‘Madhya’ (middle) and ‘Anta’ (final): the Vaiṣṇavas name it differently according to the difference in its pastimes.

## 19
w: of them  within  six  years  —  going and coming
w: Nīlācala  Gauḍa  Setubandha  Vṛndāvana
tr: Six of those years He spent journeying — to and fro between Nīlācala and Gauḍa, Setubandha and Vṛndāvana.
fn: The first line is the same as in Ādi 13.12; the second differs there (“now to the south, now to Gauḍa, now to Vṛndāvana”). Setubandha is the site of Rāma’s bridge near Rāmeśvaram (cf. 116).

## 20
w: there  which  pastimes  their  Madhya-līlā  name
w: after that  the pastimes  Antya-līlā  the designation
tr: The pastimes of that period are called the Madhya-līlā; the pastimes after it are named the Antya-līlā.

## 21
w: Ādi-līlā  Madhya-līlā  Antya-līlā  and
w: now  the Madhya-līlā  somewhat  I make  expansion
tr: Thus there are the Ādi-līlā, the Madhya-līlā and the Antya-līlā. Now I shall expand somewhat on the Madhya-līlā.

## 22
w: eighteen  years  only  at Nīlācala  residence
w: Himself  practising  the living beings  He taught  devotion
tr: For eighteen years He stayed only at Nīlācala. Practising devotion Himself, He taught it to the living beings.

## 23
w: of them  within  six  years  with the devotees
w: loving devotion  He spread  of dancing and singing  in the joy
tr: For six of those years, in the joy of dancing and singing with His devotees, He spread loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*).
fn: The first line is the same as in Ādi 13.38; in the second, Ādi 13.38 has *laoyāila* (“led people to take”), and this verse *pravartāila* (“spread, established”).

## 24
w: Nityānanda Gosāñi  He sent  to the land of Gauḍa
w: He  the land of Gauḍa  flooded  with the nectar of love
tr: He sent Nityānanda Gosāñi to Gauḍa, and Nityānanda flooded the whole land with the nectar of love.

## 25
w: by nature  Nityānanda  in love for Kṛṣṇa  unrestrained
w: by the Lord’s order  He made  here  and there  the gift of love
tr: Nityānanda is by nature unrestrained in love for Kṛṣṇa, and by the Lord’s order He gave that love away everywhere.

## 26
w: at His  feet  my  millions of  obeisances
w: to Caitanya  dear  He who  made accept  the world
tr: I offer millions of obeisances at the feet of Him who is dear to Caitanya and who made the whole world accept Him.
fn: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: *caitanyera priya yiṅho* (“He who is dear to Caitanya”). Other editions read *caitanyera bhakti yeṅho* (“He who [made the world take] devotion to Caitanya”).

## 27
w: Caitanya Gosāñi  whom  calls  elder  brother
w: He  says  my  Lord  is Caitanya Gosāñi
tr: He whom Caitanya Gosāñi calls His “elder brother” says, “My Lord is Caitanya Gosāñi.”

## 28
w: although  Himself  is  the Lord  Balarāma
w: still  of Caitanya  He holds  of a servant  the self-conception
tr: Although He Himself is Lord Balarāma, He still regards Himself as Caitanya’s servant.

## 29
w: Caitanya  serve  Caitanya  sing of  take  Caitanya’s name
w: to Caitanya  who  devotion  shows  he  My  life
tr: He would say: “Serve Caitanya! Sing of Caitanya! Take Caitanya’s name! Whoever is devoted to Caitanya is My very life.”

## 30
w: thus  people  devotion to Caitanya  He made take up
w: the wretched  the lowly  the blasphemers  all  He delivered
tr: In this way He made people take up devotion to Caitanya and delivered everyone — the wretched, the lowly and even blasphemers.

## 31
w: then  the Lord  to Vraja  sent  Rūpa and Sanātana
w: by the Lord’s order  the two  brothers  came  to Vṛndāvana
tr: Then the Lord sent Rūpa and Sanātana to Vraja, and by His order the two brothers came to Vṛndāvana.

## 32
w: devotion  preaching  all the holy places  they revealed
w: of Madana-gopāla  and Govinda  the service  they spread
tr: Preaching devotion, they revealed all the holy places and spread the service of Madana-gopāla and Govinda.

## 33
w: various  scriptures  gathering  they made  books of devotion  the essence
w: foolish  fallen people  they  made  deliverance
tr: Gathering many scriptures, they composed books of devotion that are their essence, and so delivered foolish and fallen people.

## 34
w: the Lord’s  by order  they made  of all  the scriptures  study
w: of Vraja  the confidential  devotion  they made  preaching
tr: By the Lord’s order they examined all the scriptures and preached the confidential devotion of Vraja.

## 35
w: Hari-bhakti-vilāsa  and  Bhāgavatāmṛta
w: Daśama-ṭippanī  and  Daśama-carita
tr: *Hari-bhakti-vilāsa*, *Bhāgavatāmṛta*, *Daśama-ṭippanī* and *Daśama-carita* —

## 36
w: these  all  books  composed  Gosāñi  Sanātana
w: Rūpa Gosāñi  composed  as many as  who  can make  a count
tr: all these books Sanātana Gosāñi composed. And who can count all that Rūpa Gosāñi composed?

## 37
w: principal  principal  some  I make  enumeration
w: in a hundred thousand  verses  he made  of Vraja’s pastimes  description
tr: I shall name some of the principal ones. In a hundred thousand verses he described the pastimes of Vraja.

## 38
w: Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu  and  Vidagdha-mādhava
w: Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi  and  Lalita-mādhava
tr: *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, *Vidagdha-mādhava*, *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi* and *Lalita-mādhava*;

## 39
w: Dāna-keli-kaumudī  and  many  hymns (Stavāvalī)
w: eighteen  Līlā-cchandas  and  Padyāvalī
tr: *Dāna-keli-kaumudī*, many hymns (*Stavāvalī*), the eighteen *Līlā-cchandas* and *Padyāvalī*;

## 40
w: Govinda-virudāvalī  its  lakṣaṇa (definitions)
w: Mathurā-māhātmya  and  Nāṭaka-varṇana
tr: *Govinda-virudāvalī* with its *lakṣaṇa* (definitions), *Mathurā-māhātmya* and *Nāṭaka-varṇana*.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *Govinda-virudāvalī* is part of the *Stava-mālā*; the *Nāṭaka-varṇana* is the *Nāṭaka-candrikā*.

## 41
w: Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta and others  who  can make  a count
w: everywhere  he made  of Vraja’s pastimes  description
tr: Who can count *Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta* and the rest? Everywhere in them he described the pastimes of Vraja.

## 42
w: his  brother’s son  named  Śrī Jīva Gosāñi
w: as many  books of devotion  he composed  of them  end  there is none
tr: His nephew is named Śrī Jīva Gosāñi. There is no end to the books of devotion he composed.

## 43
w: Śrī Bhāgavata-sandarbha  named  an extensive book
w: of the conclusions of devotion  in it  he has written  the essence
tr: In his extensive work, the *Śrī Bhāgavata-sandarbha*, he has written the essence of the conclusions of devotion.
fn: So in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition (*bhakti-siddhānta tāte likhiyāchena sāra*). Other editions read *…dekhāiyāchena pāra* (“he has shown the utmost reach of the conclusions of devotion”). *Anubhāṣya*: the *Bhāgavata-sandarbha* is also called the *Ṣaṭ-sandarbha*, the six *Sandarbhas*.

## 44
w: Gopāla-campū  named  a book  most mighty
w: of the eternal pastimes  the establishment  in which  with the rasa of Vraja  full
tr: In his renowned *Gopāla-campū*, full of the *rasa* of Vraja, he established the eternity of Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.

## 45
w: in this way  various  books  having made  manifest
w: their community  together with  they made  in Vṛndāvana  residence
tr: In this way they brought out many books and lived in Vṛndāvana together with their community.

## 46
w: in the first  year  headed by Advaita  the devotees
w: the Lord  to see  made  to Nīlādri  journey
tr: In the first year after His sannyāsa the devotees headed by Advaita went to Nīlādri to see the Lord.

## 47
w: the Ratha-yātrā  having seen  there  they stayed  four months
w: with the Lord  dancing  singing  supreme  joy
tr: Having seen the Ratha-yātrā, they stayed there for four months, dancing and singing with the Lord in supreme joy.

## 48
w: of farewell  at the time  the Lord  said  to all
w: every year  come  all  the Guṇḍicā  to see
tr: When they took leave, the Lord told them all, “Come every year, all of you, to see the Guṇḍicā festival.”

## 49
w: by the Lord’s order  the devotees  every year  coming
w: the Guṇḍicā  having seen  they go back  the Lord  having met
tr: By the Lord’s order the devotees came every year, saw the Guṇḍicā festival, met the Lord and went back.

## 50
w: twelve  years  thus  they made  coming and going
w: mutually  of both  each other  without  there is no  existence
tr: For twelve years they came and went like this; neither the Lord nor His devotees could live without each other.

## 51
w: of this  at the end  which  remain  twelve  years
w: of Kṛṣṇa  pastimes of separation  the Lord’s  within the heart
tr: In the twelve years that remained at the end, the Lord’s heart was filled with the pastimes of separation from Kṛṣṇa.

## 52
w: constantly  night and day  of separation  in the madness
w: He laughs  weeps  dances  sings  in great  sorrow
tr: Night and day, in the divine madness of separation, He laughed, wept, danced and sang in great sorrow.

## 53
w: at whatever  time  He takes  of Jagannātha  darśana
w: in the mind  He thinks  at Kurukṣetra  I have found  union
tr: Whenever He took *darśana* of Jagannātha, He thought, “I have found Him again at Kurukṣetra.”

## 54
w: of the Ratha-yātrā  in front  when  He performs  dance
w: there  this  song  only  He makes  singing
tr: When He danced in front of the chariot at the Ratha-yātrā, He sang only this song:

## 55
w: that very  Lord of My life  I have found
w: whom  for  in the fire of Madana  pining  I was wasting away
tr: “I have found that very Lord of My life for whom I was wasting away, burning in the fire of Madana (love).”

## 56
w: to this  refrain song  He dances  the second  prahara
w: Kṛṣṇa  taking  to Vraja  I go  this feeling  within
tr: To this refrain He danced through the second *prahara* (watch) of the day, and in His heart was one feeling: “I am taking Kṛṣṇa back to Vraja.”

## 57
w: in this  mood  of the dance  in the midst  He recites  a  verse
w: of that  verse  the meaning  anyone  not  understands  of the people
tr: In this mood, in the midst of the dance, He recited a verse whose meaning none of the people understood:

## 58
w: who  stole my heart in my girlhood  He  the same  indeed  my beloved  those  same  nights of Caitra
w: those same  and  fragrant with blossoming mālatī  full  breezes from the kadambas
w: she  the same  am I  yet  there  for the pastimes of love
w: of the Revā  on the bank  of the vetasī  tree  beneath  My heart  yearns
tr: “He who stole My heart in My girlhood is now My lord. These are the same moonlit nights of Caitra, and the same full breezes blow from the kadamba groves, fragrant with blossoming *mālatī*. I too am the same. Yet My heart yearns for Our play of love there, beneath the *vetasī* trees on the bank of the Revā.” (Padyāvalī, 386)
fn: The verse is also found in the *Kāvya-prakāśa* (1.4), as the edition heading notes. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* (to 59): it was composed about a worldly hero and heroine; why Mahāprabhu recited it with such love, no one but Svarūpa Dāmodara knew.

## 59
w: of this  verse  the meaning  knows  alone  Svarūpa
w: by providence  that  year  there  had come  Rūpa
tr: Svarūpa alone knew the meaning of this verse. By providence, that year Rūpa had come there.

## 60
w: from the Lord’s mouth  the verse  hearing  Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi
w: of that  verse  of the meaning  a verse  composed  right there
tr: Hearing the verse from the Lord’s own lips, Śrī Rūpa Gosāñi then and there composed a verse that revealed its hidden meaning.

## 61
w: the verse  composing  on a  palm leaf  writing
w: of his own  dwelling  in the thatch  he put  tucking
tr: Having composed the verse and written it on a palm leaf, he tucked it into the thatched roof of his dwelling.

## 62
w: the verse  leaving  he went  bath in the sea  to take
w: at that time  came  the Lord  him  to meet
tr: Leaving the verse there, he went to bathe in the sea, and at that very time the Lord came to meet him.

## 63
w: Haridāsa Ṭhākura  Śrī  Rūpa and Sanātana
w: into Jagannātha’s temple  not  go  the three
tr: Haridāsa Ṭhākura, Śrī Rūpa and Sanātana — these three did not go into the temple of Jagannātha.

## 64
w: Mahāprabhu  Jagannātha’s  upala-bhoga  having seen
w: to His own residence  goes  these  three  having met
tr: After seeing Jagannātha’s *upala-bhoga* offering, Mahāprabhu would meet these three and then go to His own residence.

## 65
w: these  three  among  when  is present  whichever  person
w: him  coming  Himself  He meets  the Lord’s  rule
tr: Whichever of the three was there at the time, the Lord would come and meet him Himself — this was His rule.

## 66
w: by chance  having come  the Lord  when  upward  looked
w: in the thatch  tucked  on the palm leaf  that  verse  He found
tr: When the Lord came and by chance looked up, He found that verse on the palm leaf tucked into the thatch.

## 67
w: the verse  having read  remains  the Lord  absorbed (in ecstasy)  becoming
w: Rūpa Gosāñi  coming  falls  like a rod  becoming
tr: Having read the verse, the Lord stood absorbed in ecstasy. Rūpa Gosāñi came and fell before Him flat like a rod.

## 68
w: getting up  Mahāprabhu  him  a slap  giving
w: to speak  began  something  in His arms  taking
tr: Mahāprabhu rose, gave him an affectionate slap, took him in His arms and began to speak:
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: some readings have *uhāi* for *uṭhi’* (“rising”). Edition heading: “The Lord’s unfeigned affection and mercy towards Rūpa”.

## 69
w: of My  verse  the intent  not  knows  any  person
w: of My  mind  the words  you  knew  how
tr: “No one knows the intent of My verse. How did you know what was in My mind?”

## 70
w: so  saying  to him  much  favour  showing
w: to Svarūpa Gosāñi  the verse  He showed  taking
tr: Saying this, He showed him great favour, and then took the verse and showed it to Svarūpa Gosāñi.

## 71
w: Svarūpa  asks  the Lord  being  astonished
w: of My  mind  the words  Rūpa  knew  how
tr: Astonished, the Lord asked Svarūpa, “How did Rūpa know what is in My mind?”

## 72
w: Svarūpa  says  since  he knew  Your  mind
w: from that  I know  he is  of Your  mercy  a recipient
tr: Svarūpa said, “Since he knew Your mind, I know from this that he has received Your mercy.”

## 73
w: the Lord  says  with him  I  pleased  being
w: embrace  I made  all  potencies  infusing
tr: The Lord said, “Pleased with him, I embraced him and infused all potencies into him.

## 74
w: a worthy  recipient  he is  of confidential  rasa  for discerning
w: you also  speak  to him  in explaining the confidential rasa
tr: He is a worthy vessel for understanding the hidden *rasa*, and you too should instruct him in its secrets.”

## 75
w: this  all  I shall tell  later  in detail  making
w: briefly  mention  I made  occasion  finding
tr: All this I shall tell later in detail; here, finding the occasion, I have mentioned it briefly.

## 76
w: beloved  that  very  Kṛṣṇa  O companion  at Kurukṣetra  met
w: likewise  I  that  Rādhā  that  this  of both  of union  the joy
w: yet  within  playing  sweet  of the flute  the fifth note  delighting in
