# English translation, Ādi-līlā 1 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: The Supreme Principle of the Guru

## 1
w: I worship  the gurus,  the Lord’s devotees
w: the Lord,  the Lord’s avatāras
w: His manifestations  and  His potencies
w: Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  named
tr: I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, His avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies — and the Lord Himself, who is known as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.
fn: According to the «Anubhāṣya» (on 1.34): the gurus are Śrī Nityānanda, Śrī Raghunātha, Śrī Rūpa and others; the devotees — Śrīvāsa and others; the avatāras — Śrī Advaita and others; the manifestations — Śrī Nityānanda and others; the potencies — Śrī Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Jagadānanda and others. “Known as Kṛṣṇa Caitanya” qualifies the Lord Himself (*īśam*). The verse is explained in 1.32–1.82.

## 2
w: I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
w: and Nityānanda  who rose together
w: on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa  the sun and moon
w: wonderful  bestowing good  dispelling darkness
tr: I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Śrī Nityānanda, who are like a wonderful sun and moon. They have risen together on the eastern horizon of Gauḍa, dispelling the darkness and bestowing good on all.

## 3
w: which  non-dual  Brahman  in the Upaniṣads  that  also  His  bodily  effulgence
w: who  the Supersoul  the Indweller  the Puruṣa  thus  He  His  portion  manifestation
w: with six  opulences  full  who  here  Bhagavān  He  Himself  this one
w: not  than Caitanya  Kṛṣṇa  in the world  supreme  truth  higher  here
tr: There is no Truth in the world higher than Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. That which the Upaniṣads call the non-dual Brahman is the effulgence of His body; the Puruṣa, the Supersoul who dwells within every heart, is His partial manifestation; and Bhagavān, full in all six opulences, is He Himself.
fn: «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 2.5): Brahman is the radiance of His transcendental body, and the Paramātmā a particular manifestation of His majesty; both are only two aspects of His form, perceived according to the capacity of the seer — not the fullness of the Lord, as Bhagavān is.

## 4
w: not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in Kali
w: to bestow  of the most exalted radiant rasa  devotion to Himself  the treasure
w: Hari  golden  beautiful  lustre  with a mass  illumined
w: always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  Śacī’s  son
tr: “May the Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, illumined by a mass of lustre more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the inmost cave of your hearts. Out of His mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a very long time — the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted, radiant *ujjvala-rasa*.” (Vidagdha-mādhava, 1.2)
fn: «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 3.4): *unnatojjvala-rasa* is “the exalted *ujjvala-rasa*, that is, *śṛṅgāra-rasa*” — the amorous love of Vraja, which in the gopīs is *parakīyā*; hence not “conjugal”. *Sva-bhakti-śrī* is “the beauty of prema for Him”. Why this gift was withheld for so long is explained by Kavirāja Gosvāmī in Ādi 3.

## 5
w: Rādhā  of Kṛṣṇa’s  love  the transformation  the pleasure  potency  therefore
w: one in self  although  in the world  of old  bodily  distinction  attained  the Two
w: Caitanya named  manifest  now  that  pair  and oneness  attained
w: with Rādhā’s  sentiment  and lustre  adorned  I bow to  Kṛṣṇa  Himself
tr: Śrī Rādhā is the transformation of Kṛṣṇa’s love, His pleasure potency, *hlādinī-śakti*. Therefore, though Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one in Self, from time without beginning They have appeared in the world in two different forms. Now these Two have become one and are manifest as Śrī Caitanya. I bow to Him, Kṛṣṇa Himself, adorned with the sentiment and the lustre of Śrī Rādhā.
fn: «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 4.55): Rādhā is “the transformation of Kṛṣṇa’s love — the *hlādinī-śakti* in the form of the play of prema”; the Two are “of one Self”; Their two forms are the *viṣaya* and the *āśraya* of love, “from beginningless time” (*purā*); in Śrī Caitanya — “Kṛṣṇa within, Gaura without”.

## 6
w: Śrī  Rādhā’s  love’s  greatness  of what kind  or  by Her alone
w: to be tasted  by which  wondrous  sweetness  of what kind  or  My
w: happiness  and Her  from experiencing Me  of what kind  or thus  out of longing
w: with Her sentiment rich  was born  in Śacī’s womb  ocean  the moon Hari
tr: “What is the greatness of Śrī Rādhā’s love? What is My own wondrous sweetness, which She alone tastes through that love? And what is the happiness She feels in experiencing Me?” Longing to know this, the moon Hari, rich with Her sentiment, was born from the ocean of Śacī’s womb.
fn: «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 4.230): *mad-anubhavataḥ* — “from experiencing Me”: Rādhā’s happiness comes from relishing Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness.

## 7
w: Saṅkarṣaṇa  Kāraṇodakaśāyī
w: Garbhodakaśāyī  and  Kṣīrodakaśāyī
w: and Śeṣa  whose  portions and parts  He  Nityā-
w: -nanda named  Rāma  refuge  my  may be
tr: Saṅkarṣaṇa, the Lord who lies in the Causal Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Garbha Ocean, the Lord who lies in the Ocean of Milk, and Śeṣa are His portions and the parts of His portions: may that Rāma who is called Nityānanda be my refuge.
fn: «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 5.7): Saṅkarṣaṇa is Mahā-Saṅkarṣaṇa of the spiritual sky; then the three Puruṣa-avatāras; Śeṣa is Ananta. Ascribed in the edition to the diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara.

## 8
w: beyond māyā  in all-pervading  Vaikuṇṭha  world
w: full of opulence  amid the fourfold expansion
w: form  whose  shines forth  Saṅkarṣaṇa  named
w: to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
tr: I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma, whose form called Saṅkarṣaṇa shines amid the fourfold expansion (catur-vyūha) in the all-pervading world of Vaikuṇṭha, full of every opulence and beyond māyā.

## 9
w: māyā’s  master  of universes  multitudes  shelter  whose body
w: lies  in person  of the Causal  Ocean  in the midst
w: whose  one part  the Puruṣa  the original  Lord
w: to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
tr: I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of Him is the original Lord, the Puruṣa, the master of māyā, who lies in person in the midst of the Causal Ocean, His body the shelter of countless universes.

## 10
w: whose  part of a part  the blessed  Garbhodakaśāyī
w: whose  navel-lotus  of the worlds  totality  as its stem
w: of the creator of the worlds  of birth  the place  of Brahmā
w: to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
tr: “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of His part is the blessed Garbhodakaśāyī, whose navel-lotus, with all the worlds as its stem, is the birthplace of Brahmā, the creator of the worlds.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)
fn: «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 5.7, 5.93): Garbhodakaśāyī is the second Puruṣa, Hiraṇyagarbha, the Viṣṇu of the whole universe (*samaṣṭi*); the stem is the fourteen worlds.

## 11
w: whose  part of a part of a part  the Supersoul  of all
w: the maintainer  Viṣṇu  appears  lying on the Ocean of Milk
w: of the earth  the upholder  whose part  He  also  Ananta
w: to Him  Śrī  Nityānanda-Rāma  I surrender
tr: “I take refuge in Śrī Nityānanda Rāma. A part of a part of His part is Viṣṇu who lies on the Ocean of Milk, the Supersoul and maintainer of all beings; and Ananta, who upholds the earth, is a portion of that Viṣṇu.” (diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara)
fn: «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 5.109): Ananta, upholder of the earth, is a *kalā* of Kṣīrodakaśāyī.

## 12
w: Mahā-Viṣṇu  of the world  the maker
w: by māyā  who  creates  this (world)
w: His  avatāra  indeed  this
w: Advaita Ācārya  the Lord
tr: Mahā-Viṣṇu is the maker of the world, who creates it through māyā; and this Lord Advaita Ācārya is His avatāra.
fn: «Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya» (on Ādi 6.10): one and the same māyā is the material cause as *pradhāna* and the efficient cause as *māyā*; Mahā-Viṣṇu creates through both.

## 13
w: Advaita  from Hari  being non-different
w: Ācārya  bhakti  from teaching
w: as a devotee descended  the Lord  Him
w: Advaita Ācārya  I take shelter of
tr: He is called Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, and Ācārya because He teaches bhakti. I take shelter of Him, the Lord Advaita Ācārya, who has descended as a devotee.

## 14
w: of five truths  composed  Kṛṣṇa
w: in the form of a devotee  in the guise of a devotee
w: the devotee-avatāra  called a devotee
w: I bow to  the devotional potency
tr: “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who is manifest as five truths — the Pañca-tattva: in the form of a devotee, in the guise of a devotee, as the incarnation of a devotee, as the devotee, and as the potency of devotion.” (Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī)
fn: According to the «Anubhāṣya» (on Ādi 7.6): the form of a devotee is Śrī Gaurāṅga, who tastes His own sweetness; the guise of a devotee (as His brother) is Śrī Nityānanda; the devotee-avatāra is Śrī Advaita; the devotee is Śrīvāsa and others; the potency is Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Rāmānanda and others. Ascribed in the edition to Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī.

## 15
w: glory  to the merciful (Pair)  of the lame
w: of me  of dull mind  the refuge
w: whose lotus feet are my all
w: Rādhā  and Madana-mohana
tr: All glory to the merciful Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Madana-mohana! I am lame and dull of mind, and They are my only refuge; Their lotus feet are all I have.
fn: «Anubhāṣya»: *manda-mati* — “a mind absorbed in the world, feeble, unable to follow other paths such as karma or jñāna”; *suratau* — “merciful”, or “deeply attached to each other”.

## 16
w: shining  in Vṛndāvana forest  wish-fulfilling  tree  beneath
w: in a beautiful  jewel temple  on a lion-throne  seated
w: Śrī Śrī  Rādhā  and Śrīla  Govinda  the Deities
w: by Their dearest friends  being served  I remember
tr: I remember Śrī Śrī Rādhā and Śrī Govindadeva, seated on a throne in a beautiful temple of jewels beneath a wish-fulfilling tree in the radiant forest of Vṛndāvana, served by Their dearest companions.
fn: «Anubhāṣya»: *preṣṭhālī* — Lalitā and the other *priya-narma-sakhīs*, attended by Śrī Rūpa-mañjarī and others.

## 17
w: the beautiful  of the rāsa dance’s  sweetness  the initiator
w: of Vaṁśīvaṭa  on the bank  standing
w: drawing  with the flute’s  sounds  the gopīs
w: of the gopīs  the Lord  for good  may be  ours
tr: May the beautiful Śrī Gopīnātha, who begins the sweet rāsa dance, be our good fortune. Standing on the bank by Vaṁśīvaṭa, He draws the gopīs to Himself with the sounds of His flute.
fn: «Anubhāṣya»: the flute “loosens every desire in the gopīs other than for Kṛṣṇa and draws them from their homes with the rope of prema”; *śriye* — “for the wealth of prema”.

## 18
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory  to Advaitacandra  glory  to Gaura’s  devotees  host
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 19
w: these  three  Deities  the Gauḍīyas  have made  Their own
w: of these  three  the feet  I worship;  the Three  my  Lords
tr: These three Deities have made the Gauḍīya devotees Their own. I worship Their feet: these Three are my Lords.
fn: «Anubhāṣya»: Madana-mohana, Govinda and Gopīnātha are the presiding Deities of *sambandha*, *abhidheya* and *prayojana* taught by Śrī Caitanya.

## 20
w: of the book  at the beginning  I make  the auspicious invocation
w: guru,  Vaiṣṇava,  Bhagavān —  of the three  remembrance
tr: At the beginning of the book I make the auspicious invocation — the remembrance of three: the guru, the Vaiṣṇavas and Bhagavān.

## 21
w: of the three  by remembrance  comes  of obstacles  destruction
w: easily  comes  one’s own  desire’s  fulfilment
tr: Remembering these three, one overcomes all obstacles and easily attains one’s heart’s desire.

## 22
w: that  invocation  is  of three  kinds
w: indication of the subject,  benediction,  obeisance
tr: This invocation is of three kinds: indicating the subject, giving blessing and offering obeisance.

## 23
w: in the first  two  verses  to the worshipful Lord  obeisance
w: general  and specific  in form  two  indeed  kinds
tr: In the first two verses is the obeisance to the worshipful Lord, in two ways — general and specific.

## 24
w: in the third  verse  I make  of the subject  indication
w: by which  is known  of the Supreme Truth  the indication
tr: In the third verse I indicate the subject, through which the Supreme Truth is made known.

## 25
w: in the fourth  verse  I give  to the world  blessing
w: everywhere  I beg for  Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’s  grace
tr: In the fourth verse I bless the world, begging everywhere for the grace of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.

## 26
w: in that  verse  I tell  of the external descent  the cause
w: in the fifth  and sixth  verses  I tell  the root  purpose
tr: In that verse I also tell the external reason for His descent; in the fifth and sixth verses I tell its root purpose.

## 27
w: in these  six  verses  of Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  the truth
w: and in five  verses  of Nityānanda  the greatness
tr: In these six verses is the truth of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and in the next five, the greatness of Śrī Nityānanda.

## 28
w: then in two  verses  of Advaita  the truth  the account
w: then in one  verse  of the Pañca-tattva  the explanation
tr: Then two verses tell the truth of Advaita, and one verse explains the Pañca-tattva.

## 29
w: in these  fourteen  verses  I make  the invocation
w: within  them  I tell  the whole  subject’s  description
tr: In these fourteen verses I make the auspicious invocation, and within them I describe the whole subject.

## 30
w: to all  listening  Vaiṣṇavas  offering  obeisance
w: of all these  verses  I make  of the meaning  consideration
tr: Offering obeisance to all the Vaiṣṇavas who hear this, I shall now consider the meaning of all these verses.

## 31
w: all  Vaiṣṇavas,  hear  making  one mind
w: of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa  the scriptures  as  in view  establish
tr: O Vaiṣṇavas, hear with one-pointed mind how the scriptures establish the truth of Caitanya-Kṛṣṇa.
fn: Bengali text as in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «শাস্ত্র যে মত নিরূপণ».

## 32
w: Kṛṣṇa,  the two gurus,  devotees,  avatāras,  manifestations
w: potencies —  in these  six  forms  He enjoys
tr: Kṛṣṇa, the two kinds of guru, the devotees, the avatāras, the manifestations and the potencies — in these six forms He enjoys His pastimes.
fn: «Anubhāṣya»: *guru-dvaya* — the initiating (*dīkṣā*) and the instructing (*śikṣā*) guru; both are one guru-tattva and equally worshipful for the disciple.

## 33
w: of these  six  truths  I make  to the feet  worship
w: first  in general  I make  the invocation
tr: I worship the feet of these six truths; first I make the invocation in general.

## 34
w: I worship  the gurus,  the Lord’s devotees
w: the Lord,  the Lord’s avatāras
w: His manifestations  and  His potencies
w: Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya  named
tr: I worship the gurus, the devotees of the Lord, His avatāras, His manifestations and His potencies — and the Lord Himself, who is known as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.
