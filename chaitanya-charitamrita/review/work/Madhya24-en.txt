## 21

tr: “Who in this world could count the mighty deeds of Viṣṇu — even a sage who has counted the atoms of the earth? His unchecked stride shook the universe from *pradhāna*, where the three *guṇas* rest in balance, up to Satyaloka, and it was He who held it fast.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.7.40)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Brahmā tells Nārada of the *līlā-avatāras* of Viṣṇu and glorifies the immeasurable prowess of Vāmana; *tri-pṛṣṭha* is Satyaloka, *tri-sāmya-sadana* — *pradhāna*, the state of balance of the three *guṇas*. The *Anubhāṣya* also cites the *Ṛg Veda* (1.154): “What mighty deeds of Viṣṇu shall I proclaim? He measured out the earthly realms; wide-striding, He upheld the highest abode in His three steps.”
