## 1

fn: Heading in the edition: “The dancing Gaura plunges the world into a flood of prema.”

tr: Covering His ears with His hands, the Lord uttered the name of Nārāyaṇa and said, “Sārvabhauma, why do you speak such improper words?

## 7
w: renounced  sannyāsī  for Me  a king  seeing
w: seeing a woman  like  of poison  the eating
tr: For Me, a renounced sannyāsī, to see a king is as bad as to see a woman: both are like swallowing poison.

## 8
w: of one who owns nothing  toward the worship of the Lord  turned

w: the seeing  of worldly people  and also  of women  and
w: alas  alas  alas  than drinking poison  even  more evil
tr: “Alas, alas! For one who owns nothing in this world, who is turned toward the worship of the Lord and longs to cross to the far shore of the ocean of worldly existence, to look upon worldly people and women is worse even than drinking poison.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 8.24)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: what is forbidden is to look upon worldly people and women with the eyes of an enjoyer, as objects of enjoyment.

## 9
w: Sārvabhauma  says  true  Your  words
w: of Jagannātha  servant  the king  but  the best of devotees
tr: Sārvabhauma said, “Your words are true. But the king is a servant of Jagannātha and the best of devotees.”

## 10
w: the Lord  says  still  the king  like a deadly black snake
w: of a wooden woman  by the touch  as  arises  agitation
tr: The Lord said, “Even so, a king is like a deadly black snake. Even the touch of a woman carved from wood can disturb the mind.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in the same way, the mere sight of a king, even if he is the best of devotees, may give rise to an anartha in one who is renounced.

## 11
w: of the form  even  one should fear
w: of women  of worldly people  also
w: as  of a snake  of the mind  agitation
w: so  of its  likeness  even
tr: “One should fear even the outward form of women and of worldly people; for as a snake disturbs the mind, so does even its likeness.” (Caitanya-candrodaya-nāṭaka, 8.25)

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “Gajapati” (“lord of elephants”) was the title of the emperors of Orissa, as other kings bore the titles “chatrapati”, “narapati” or “aśvapati”.

## 16

## 18

w: by Your  order  the king  my  worldly duties  took away
tr: Rāya said, “I conveyed Your order to the king, and in obedience to it he has released me from my worldly duties.
fn: The edition reads «tomāra ājñāya» (“by Your order”); the older text has «icchāya» (“by Your wish”). *Anubhāṣya*: see Madhya 8.297–298; following Mahāprabhu’s wish, the king publicly released Rāmānanda from worldly affairs — he gave him leave from his post.

## 20

## 21

w: my  hand  taking  shows  affection  special
tr: Hearing Your name, he was overwhelmed with love; taking my hand, he showed me special affection.

## 22
w: your  which  salary  you  eat  that  salary
w: free from worry  being  worship  of Caitanya  the feet
tr: He said, ‘Keep drawing the salary you received before, and, free from care, worship the feet of Caitanya.’
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Rāmānanda had drawn this salary as governor of southern Kaliṅga; though released from service, he would go on receiving it.

## 23
w: I  am wretched  fit  not  His  for the sight
w: Him  whoever  worships  his  fruitful  life
tr: Then he said, ‘I am wretched, not fit to see Him. But whoever worships Him has made his life fruitful.

## 24
w: supremely merciful  He  the son of the king of Vraja

## 25

## 26

## 27
w: for you  which  so much  love  has arisen  of the king
w: for this  quality  Kṛṣṇa  him  will accept  as His own
tr: The king has come to love you so much, and for this quality Kṛṣṇa will accept him as His own.

## 28
w: who  My  devotees  O Pārtha
w: not  My  devotees  and  those  people
w: of My devotees  and  who  devotees
w: they  by Me  the best of devotees  are considered
tr: “O Pārtha, those who are devoted only to Me are not truly My devotees; but those who are devoted to My devotees I consider the best of My devotees.” (Ādi-purāṇa; also Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 2.6)
fn: Heading in the edition: “The devotee of the devotee is the true devotee of Bhagavān” (*Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, 2.6; the words of the *Ādi-purāṇa*).

## 29
w: reverence  for My service
w: with all the limbs  obeisance
w: worship of My devotees  greater
w: in all beings  awareness of Me
tr: “Reverence for My service, obeisance with the whole body, worship of My devotees greater than worship of Me, seeing Me in all living beings,
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the words of Bhagavān to Uddhava on bhakti-yoga.

## 30
w: for My sake  the actions of the body  and
w: with speech  telling of My qualities
w: to Me  offering  and  of the mind
w: of all desires  giving up
tr: the actions of the body done for My sake, speech that tells of My qualities, a mind offered to Me, and the giving up of all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.19.21–22)

## 31
w: of forms of worship  of all
w: of Viṣṇu  the worship  highest
w: than that  higher still  O goddess
w: of those who are His  the worship
tr: “O goddess, of all forms of worship the worship of Viṣṇu is the highest; but higher still is the worship of those who belong to Him.” (Padma-purāṇa, 6.253.176)
fn: Heading in the edition: “The worship of the Vaiṣṇava is higher than the worship of Viṣṇu, the Lord of all lords” (*Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, 2.4; the words of Śiva to Pārvatī in the *Padma-purāṇa*). *Anubhāṣya*: “those who belong to Him” are Kṛṣṇa’s associates in every rasa — in mādhurya Śrī Rūpa, Vārṣabhānavī and others, in vātsalya Nanda and Yaśodā, in sakhya Śrīdāmā and Subala, in dāsya Citraka and others.

## 32
w: hard to attain  indeed  for one of little austerity
w: service  to those who are the path to Vaikuṇṭha
w: in whom  is sung  constantly
w: the God of gods  Janārdana
tr: “For one whose austerity is slight, it is hard to attain service to those who walk the path to Vaikuṇṭha — the devotees among whom Janārdana, the God of gods, is constantly glorified.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.7.20)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the words of Vidura to Maitreya. Only by serving great souls does one hear hari-kathā; from it love for Hari is born, and with it even concern for the body falls away.

## 33
w: Purī  Bhāratī Gosāñi  Svarūpa  Nityānanda
w: Jagadānanda  Mukunda and others  all  the devotees
tr: Purī, Bhāratī Gosāñi, Svarūpa, Nityānanda, Jagadānanda, Mukunda and all the other devotees were there.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Purī is Paramānanda Purī, Bhāratī is Brahmānanda Bhāratī, Svarūpa is Svarūpa Dāmodara, Nityānanda is Nityānanda Prabhu — the four Gosāñis of the next verse.

## 36

w: the Lord  not  seeing  why  first  here  you came
tr: The Lord said, “Rāya, what have you done? Why did you come here first, without seeing the Lord?”

## 37
w: Rāya  says  the feet  the chariot  the heart  the charioteer
w: wherever  taking  it goes  there  goes  the soul who rides
tr: Rāya said, “The feet are the chariot and the heart is the charioteer. Wherever the heart drives, there the soul who rides in the chariot must go.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the soul is like the rider in a chariot, the feet are the chariot, and the mind is the charioteer; wherever the mind drives, there the soul goes. Compare Kaṭha Upaniṣad 1.3.3–6, 9: the self is the rider, the body the chariot, the intelligence the charioteer and the mind the reins.

tr: The Lord said, “Go quickly and see Jagannātha; then go straight home and meet your family.”
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: having seen Jagannātha, go straight home and meet your relatives.

fn: The words of Pratāparudra to Sārvabhauma; the reference follows the heading in the edition (act 8, verse 29).

## 62

tr: Seeing the Snāna-yātrā, the Lord was very happy; but during Jagannātha’s *anavasara* He felt great sorrow.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: after the Snāna-yātrā the painting of Śrī Jagannātha’s body (*aṅga-rāga*) is renewed, and for that time the Deity stays apart, hidden from those who come for darśana; this period is called *anavasara*.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: during the *anavasara* the Lord could not see Jagannātha and, beside Himself in separation from Kṛṣṇa, went to Ālālanātha and stayed there.

## 66

## 67

w: of Mahāprabhu  devotees  all  great devotees

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the mahā-bhāgavatas are *niṣkiñcanas* above *varṇāśrama*, *paramahaṁsas* whose only shelter is Kṛṣṇa; as Śrī Narottama Ṭhākura writes in his *Prārthanā*, “One who regards the associates of Gaurāṅga as eternally perfect goes to the son of the king of Vraja.”

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Narendra is the pond where the Candana-yātrā festival is held; even today Gauḍīya Vaiṣṇavas coming to Puruṣottama wash their hands and feet in its water before going to the temple.

## 76

## 77
