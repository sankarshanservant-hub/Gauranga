## 1

tr: I bow down to the beautiful lotus feet of my guru, to all the gurus and to the Vaiṣṇavas. I bow down to Śrī Rūpa, together with his elder brother, Sanātana, with his companions and Raghunātha, and with Jīva; to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, together with Advaita, with the Avadhūta, Nityānanda, and with all His associates; and to the feet of Śrī Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, together with Lalitā and Śrī Viśākhā and their companions.
fn: This same verse opens Antya 3. *Anubhāṣya*: the śrī guru is the guru who gave initiation into the mantra, or the guru who instructs in bhajana; the gurus are the whole line of gurus (the parama-guru and those before him), headed by Śrīmad Ānandatīrtha and Śrīman Mādhavendra Purī; the Vaiṣṇavas are the bhāgavatas of all four yugas; Śrī Rūpa is honoured with his elder brother Sanātana, with his own devotees and Raghunātha dāsa, and with Jīva, whom he blessed; the companions of Lalitā and Viśākhā are the sakhīs and mañjarīs.

## 3
w: all  the worlds  to deliver  Gaura’s  descent
w: of deliverance  the means  His  three  kinds
tr: Gaura descended to deliver all the worlds, and the means by which He delivered them were of three kinds.

## 4
w: direct  sight  and  in a worthy  devotee-soul
w: ‘entering’  He makes  somewhere  somewhere  by ‘appearing’
tr: To some He granted the sight of Himself in person; in some places He made His ‘āveśa’, entering a worthy devotee; and in others His ‘āvirbhāva’, appearing before a devotee.
fn: The three means: direct sight (*sākṣāt-darśana*), entering (*āveśa*) and appearance (*āvirbhāva*). The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *kāṅhā, kāṅhā*, “in some places… in others”. The edition’s heading: (1) direct darśana, (2) entering (*āveśa*) a worthy soul, (3) appearance (*āvirbhāva*). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: to one the Lord grants direct darśana, into another worthy devotee He enters, and to yet another He appears.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: on the seven dvīpas see the *Anubhāṣya* on Madhya 20.218 and *Bhāgavatam* 5.16 and 5.20; the nine khaṇḍas of Bhārata-varṣa, according to the *Siddhānta-śiromaṇi* (*Golādhyāya*, *Bhuvana-kośa*), are Aindra, Kaśeru, Tāmraparṇa, Gabhastimat, Kumārikā, Nāga, Saumya, Vāruṇa and Gāndharva.
