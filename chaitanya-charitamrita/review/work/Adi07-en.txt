## 0
tr: Setting Forth the Account of the Pañca-tattva

## 1
w: of the shelterless  the only shelter  having bowed
w: to the deprived  the highest  who grants
w: to Śrī Caitanya  is written  His
w: in giving prema-bhakti  magnanimity
tr: Bowing to Śrī Caitanya, the only refuge of those who have no other refuge, who gives the highest treasure to those bereft of every good, I write of the magnanimity with which He bestows love and devotion for Kṛṣṇa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *agaty-eka-gati* — “the one refuge of those who have no other shelter”; *hīnārthādhika-sādhaka* — “He who grants to those deprived of the supreme good (*paramārtha*) something greater still — the fifth goal of life, love for Kṛṣṇa”.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Mahāprabhu  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya
w: His  at the feet sheltered  he  greatly  blessed
tr: Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Whoever takes shelter at His feet is truly blessed.

## 3
w: earlier  the guru and the rest  six  to the truths  I made  obeisance
w: the truth of the guru  I have told  now  of the five  the consideration
tr: At the beginning I offered obeisance to the six truths, headed by the guru. I have spoken of the truth of the guru; now I shall consider the other five.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in the first chapter the truth of the guru was described — as the initiating and the instructing guru; the six truths are those of the verse *vande gurūn īśa-bhaktān* (Ādi 1.1), and now the remaining five are examined.

## 4
w: the five truths  descended  Caitanya  with
w: the five truths  taking  He performs  of saṅkīrtana  in the joy
tr: The five truths descended together with Śrī Caitanya, and with them He delights in saṅkīrtana.

## 5
w: the five truths  one substance  there is not  any  difference
w: rasa  to taste  the truth  manifold  distinctions
tr: The five truths are one substance; there is no difference between them at all. Yet to taste rasa, the one truth appears in different forms.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in substance the five truths are one, without duality, but for the sake of the pastimes and the tasting of rasa the one truth is fivefold. The devotee in form, the devotee in essence and the incarnation of a devotee are the Lord Himself, His *prakāśa* and His *aṁśa* (*viṣṇu-tattva*); the potency of devotion and the pure devotee are potencies non-different from Him, who serve Him. Without either the worshipped or the worshipper there would be no pastime of tasting rasa.

## 6
w: of five truths  composed  Kṛṣṇa
w: devotee in form  devotee in essence
w: the devotee-avatāra  called a devotee
w: I bow to  the devotional potency
tr: “I bow to Kṛṣṇa, who is manifest as five truths — the Pañca-tattva: as a devotee in form and a devotee in essence, as the incarnation of a devotee, as the one called a devotee, and as the potency of devotion.” (Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī)
fn: According to the *Anubhāṣya*: the devotee in form is Śrī Gaurāṅga, who tastes His own sweetness; the devotee in essence (in the form of His brother) is Śrī Nityānanda; the devotee-avatāra is Śrī Advaita; the one called a devotee is Śrīvāsa and others, established in *śānta*, *dāsya* and the other rasas; the potency is Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara, Rāmānanda and others. The same verse as Ādi 1.14; ascribed in the edition to Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī.

## 7
w: Himself  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  the one  Lord
w: without a second  Nanda’s son  of rasikas  the crown
tr: Kṛṣṇa is Bhagavān Himself, the one Lord of all. He has no equal; He is the son of Nanda and the crown of all who relish rasa.

## 8
w: of the rāsa and other pastimes the enjoyer  of the maidens of Vraja  the lover
w: others  as many  all  you see  His  associates
tr: He enjoys the rāsa dance and His other pastimes and is the beloved of the maidens of Vraja; all the others you see are His associates.

## 9
w: that  Kṛṣṇa  has descended  as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
w: those  associates  with Him  all  blessed
tr: That same Kṛṣṇa has descended as Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, and with Him have come all those associates, every one of them blessed.

## 10
w: alone  the Lord-truth  Caitanya  the Lord
w: of a devotee’s sentiment full  His  pure  body
tr: Lord Caitanya alone is the Supreme Lord, yet His pure, spiritual body is filled with the sentiment of a devotee.
fn: The edition’s heading: “Though He is the Lord of all, Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is full of the mood of one who is subordinate.” *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Caitanya is the one Supreme Lord of all conscious beings (*nityo nityānām…*); though non-different from the son of the king of Vraja, He takes a form full of the mood of serving Kṛṣṇa. That form must not be thought of as a body for worldly enjoyment or counted among the jīvas of this world: it is manifest in pure *sattva* (*viśuddha-sattva*) and is revealed to a heart illumined by it.

## 11
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness  one  wondrous  property
w: Himself  to taste  Kṛṣṇa  takes on  a devotee’s sentiment
tr: Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness has one wondrous property: to taste Himself, Kṛṣṇa takes on the sentiment of His own devotee.
fn: The edition’s heading: “To taste His own sweetness, Kṛṣṇa descends as Gaura — ‘in the form of a devotee’.” *Anubhāṣya*: Kṛṣṇa, the abode of all sweetness, though He is Himself the object of love (*viṣaya*), takes on the sentiment of its subject (*āśraya*) and tastes the joy of serving Himself; yet Śrī Caitanya is not merely a form of the *āśraya*: He is the original Lord Himself (*svayaṁ-rūpa*).

## 12
w: therefore  a devotee’s sentiment  takes  Caitanya  Gosāñi
w: ‘devotee in essence’  His  Nityānanda  the brother
tr: Therefore Caitanya Gosāñi takes on the sentiment of a devotee, and His brother Nityānanda is the devotee in essence (*bhakta-svarūpa*).

## 13
w: ‘the devotee-avatāra’  His  the Ācārya  Gosāñi
w: these  three  truths  all  as Lord  holding  we sing
tr: Advaita Ācārya is His incarnation as a devotee (*bhakta-avatāra*). These three truths we all glorify as the Lord — as Prabhu.

## 14
w: one  Mahāprabhu  and  Prabhus  two
w: the two  Prabhus  serve  Mahāprabhu’s  feet
tr: One is Mahāprabhu, and the other two are Prabhus; the two Prabhus serve the feet of Mahāprabhu.

## 15
w: these  three  truths  ‘worshipable by all’  as  we accept
w: the fourth  which  the devotee-truth  ‘worshipper’  as  we know
tr: These three truths we accept as worshipable by all; the fourth, the truth of the devotee, we know as the worshipper.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Mahāprabhu is the highest Truth, above all, while Śrī Nityānanda and Śrī Advaita are *īśvara-tattvas* subordinate to Him; all three are worshipable by everyone else. The fourth truth is the pure devotees, the fifth the intimate (*antaraṅga*) devotees; both are worshippers. The two worshipable truths, though the last two worship them, are themselves engaged in the service of Śrī Gaurāṅga.

## 16
w: Śrīvāsa and others  all  millions  millions  devotees
w: ‘pure devotee’-truth  within  of them all  the counting
tr: The countless millions of devotees headed by Śrīvāsa are all counted among the pure devotees (*śuddha-bhakta*).

## 17
w: Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others  the Lord’s  ‘potency’-avatāras
w: ‘intimate devotees’  as  counting  of whom
tr: Gadādhara Paṇḍita and others are incarnations of the Lord’s potency (*śakti*); they are counted as His intimate devotees.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the truths of *śakti* — the intimate devotees — abide in *madhura-rasa*, which includes *vātsalya*, *sakhya* and *dāsya*; the servants of Śrī Nityānanda and Śrī Advaita, the pure devotees, usually abide in *vātsalya*, *sakhya*, *dāsya* and *śānta*. When their love for Śrī Gaurasundara grows intense, they take shelter of *madhura-rasa* under the guidance of the intimate devotees. Pure devotees are those who, free from other desires, do not cover their service to Kṛṣṇa with *karma* or *jñāna*; the intimate devotees are those wholly given to *madhura-rasa* alone.

## 18–19
w: with whom all  taking  the Lord’s  eternal  pastimes
w: with whom all  taking  the Lord’s  of kīrtana  spreading
w: with whom all  taking  He does  of prema  tasting
w: with whom all  taking  giving  He makes  prema’s treasure
tr: With them the Lord enjoys His eternal pastimes, with them He spreads the kīrtana, with them He tastes prema, and with them He gives away the treasure of prema.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: “with them” — with all four: with His *prakāśa* (Śrī Nityānanda), with the avatāra of the Puruṣa (Śrī Advaita), with the intimate devotees and with the pure devotees. The edition’s heading: “With the four truths — the Lord’s pastimes, preaching, tasting and giving.”

## 20–21
w: those  five truths  together  to the earth  coming
w: of the ancient storehouse of prema  the seal  breaking open
w: the five  together  plunder  prema  do  tasting
w: as much  as much  they drink  thirst  grows  every moment
tr: Coming to earth together, the five truths broke the seal of the ancient storehouse of prema. Together they plunder that love and taste it, and the more they drink, the more their thirst grows at every moment.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the storehouse of prema is the pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. It had come into the world before, but it was locked and sealed; in the descent of Śrī Caitanya the Pañca-tattva broke the seal, threw open the doors and tasted prema, plundering it.

## 22
w: again  again  giving to drink  become  greatly maddened
w: they dance  weep  laugh  sing  like  the intoxicated
tr: Again and again they give it to others to drink and grow wild with love; they dance, weep, laugh and sing as though intoxicated.

## 23
w: fit and unfit  consideration  there is not  there is not  proper place and improper
w: whoever  wherever  is found  there  they make  of prema  the gift
tr: They make no distinction between the fit and the unfit, nor between a proper place and an improper one: wherever they find anyone, there they give prema.

## 24
w: plundering  eating  giving  the storehouse  they empty
w: wondrous  storehouse  prema  a hundredfold  grows
tr: Plundering, tasting and giving, they empty the storehouse — yet this storehouse is wondrous: the prema in it grows a hundredfold.

## 25
w: surged  of prema the flood  on all sides  spreads
w: women  old men  boys  youths  all  it drowns
tr: The flood of prema surged and spread on every side, drowning everyone — women and old men, boys and youths.

## 26
w: the good  the wicked  the lame  the dull  the blind
w: in the flood of prema  drowned  of the world  the people
tr: The good and the wicked, the lame, the dull and the blind — the flood of prema drowned all the people of the world.

## 27
w: the world  drowned  of the jīvas  became  the seed  destroyed
w: that  seeing  of the five  persons  supreme  joy
tr: The world was drowned, and the seed of the jīvas’ bondage was destroyed. Seeing this, the five felt the highest joy.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in the Lord’s *jīva-śakti*, together with the inclination towards Kṛṣṇa, there lies hidden the seed of the desire to enjoy in turning away from Him. As a seed in flooded earth can put forth no shoot, so in the flood of prema the seed of every desire other than service to Kṛṣṇa perished. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: there perished the seed of the bondage of *avidyā* — the soul’s forgetfulness that it is Kṛṣṇa’s servant.

## 28
w: as much  as much  rain of prema  make  the five persons
w: so much  so much  rises  the water  pervades  the three worlds
tr: The more the five pour down the rain of prema, the higher the waters rise, until they fill the three worlds.

## 29–30
w: Māyāvādīs  those devoted to karma  the false logicians
w: blasphemers  pāṣaṇḍīs  all  students  the lowest
w: they  all  very clever  running  fled
w: that  flood  them all  to touch  could not
tr: But the Māyāvādīs, those devoted to fruitive work, the false logicians, the blasphemers, the pāṣaṇḍīs and the lowest of students — all these, being very clever, ran away, and the flood could not touch them.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Māyāvādīs are the sannyāsīs Prakāśānanda and others; those devoted to karma — Devānanda and other karmīs without bhakti, the *smārtas*, for whom karma and its fruits are the chief goal; the false logicians — Sārvabhauma and other godless logicians; the blasphemers — the one whom the Lord drove away with a stick, Gopāla Cāpāla and others who reviled the Lord and His devotees; the pāṣaṇḍīs — those who place other gods on a level with the Lord; the lowest of students — those for whom learning is only a cause for argument and not a way to God.

## 31–32
w: that  seeing  Mahāprabhu  makes  reflection
w: the world  to drown  I  made  effort
w: some  some  escaped  the vow  became  broken
w: them all  to drown  I shall set  some  play
tr: Seeing this, Mahāprabhu reflected: “I tried to drown the whole world, but some have escaped, and My vow has been broken. To drown them too, I shall set some play in motion.”

## 33
w: thus  saying  in the mind  some  making  deliberation
w: the sannyāsa order  the Lord  made  acceptance
tr: Saying this and deliberating within Himself, the Lord accepted the order of sannyāsa.

## 34
w: twenty-four  years  He remained  in the householder order
w: in the twenty-fifth  year  He took  to the renunciant’s dharma
tr: For twenty-four years He lived as a householder; in His twenty-fifth year He took up the dharma of a renunciant.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Mahāprabhu accepted sannyāsa in Śaka 1432, in the month of Māgha, during the bright fortnight, at Katwa, from the *daṇḍi-svāmī* Śrī Keśava Bhāratī of the school of Śrī Śaṅkara (under the Śṛṅgerī Maṭha in the south).

## 35
w: sannyāsa  having taken  the Lord  made  attraction
w: all who  had fled  the logicians and the rest
tr: Having taken sannyāsa, the Lord drew to Himself all who had fled — the logicians and the rest.

## 36
w: students  pāṣaṇḍīs  karmīs  blasphemers and others  all
w: they  coming  at the Lord’s feet  become  bowed down
tr: Students, pāṣaṇḍīs, karmīs, blasphemers and all the others came and bowed down at the Lord’s feet.

## 37
w: offences  He forgave  they sank  in the waters of prema
w: who  will escape  the Lord’s  in the great net of prema
tr: He forgave their offences, and they sank into the waters of prema. Who can escape the Lord’s great net of love?

## 38
w: all  to deliver  the Lord  the avatāra of mercy
w: all  to deliver  He uses  stratagems  boundless
tr: The Lord is the avatāra of mercy who came to deliver everyone, and to deliver everyone He uses boundless stratagems.

## 39
w: then  His own  devotees  He made  all  mlecchas  and others
w: all  escaped  only  of Kāśī  the Māyāvādīs
tr: He made His devotees of all — even the mlecchas and others; only the Māyāvādīs of Kāśī escaped.

## 40
w: to Vṛndāvana  going  the Lord  stayed  in Kāśī
w: the Māyāvādīs  Him  began  to blaspheme
tr: On His way to Vṛndāvana the Lord stayed in Kāśī, and the Māyāvādīs began to blaspheme Him:

## 41
w: a sannyāsī  being  He does  singing  dancing
w: not  does  of Vedānta  hearing  does  saṅkīrtana
tr: “Though a sannyāsī, He sings and dances. He does not hear Vedānta, but performs saṅkīrtana.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: by the rule of the *smṛti*, a sannyāsī must give up singing, dancing and playing music and always study Vedānta; seeing that Mahāprabhu did not hear Śaṅkara’s Māyāvāda but danced in love, absorbed in singing of Kṛṣṇa, the sannyāsīs of Kāśī took Him to be ignorant of the dharma of sannyāsa.

## 42
w: a foolish  sannyāsī  his own dharma  not  knows
w: sentimental  being  He wanders  with the sentimentalists  together
tr: This foolish sannyāsī does not know His own dharma; a sentimentalist Himself, He wanders about with sentimentalists.”

## 43
w: this  all  hearing  the Lord  smiles  in the mind  in the mind
w: disregard  making  to anyone  not  made  conversation
tr: Hearing all this, the Lord smiled within, and, paying no heed, He spoke with none of them.

## 44
w: disregard  making  He made  to Mathurā  going
w: Mathurā  having seen  again  He made  return
tr: Paying them no heed, He went on to Mathurā, and after seeing Mathurā He came back again.

## 45
w: in Kāśī  the scribe  the śūdra  Śrī Candraśekhara
w: his  in the house  stayed  the Lord  the independent  Īśvara
tr: In Kāśī lived the scribe Śrī Candraśekhara, a śūdra; in his house the Lord stayed, for He is the independent Lord.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Candraśekhara was a *vaidya*, and so by varṇa a śūdra. It is not proper for a sannyāsī to spend the night in a śūdra’s house, but Mahāprabhu stayed with him out of mercy, for He is the independent Lord: before His mercy brāhmaṇa and śūdra are equal.

## 46
w: of Tapana Miśra  in the house  alms  taking
w: of sannyāsīs  in company  not  accepts  invitation
tr: He took His meals at the house of Tapana Miśra and accepted no invitation in the company of the sannyāsīs.

## 47
w: Sanātana  Gosāñi  coming  there  met
w: his  instruction  for the sake of  the Lord  two months  stayed
tr: Sanātana Gosāñi came and met Him there, and to instruct him the Lord stayed two months.

## 48
w: him  He taught  all  of the Vaiṣṇavas  the dharma
w: Śrī Bhāgavata and other  of the scriptures  all  secret  heart
tr: He taught him the whole dharma of the Vaiṣṇavas and all the hidden heart of the *Bhāgavata* and the other scriptures.

## 49
w: meanwhile  Candraśekhara  Miśra Tapana

tr: Meanwhile Candraśekhara and Tapana Miśra, deeply grieved, came to the Lord’s feet with a petition:

## 50

w: not  we can  bear  now  we shall give up  life
tr: “How much longer must we hear You blasphemed, O Lord? We cannot bear it; we would rather give up our lives.

## 51
w: You  blaspheme  all  of sannyāsīs  the company
w: to hear  not  we can  bursts  heart and ears
tr: All the sannyāsīs revile You, and we cannot listen to it: our hearts and ears are bursting.”

## 52
w: this  hearing  remains  the Lord  slightly  smiling
w: at that  time  one  brāhmaṇa  met  coming
tr: Hearing this, the Lord only smiled a little. Just then a brāhmaṇa came to meet Him.

## 53
w: coming  petition  makes  the feet  clasping
w: one  thing  I beg  give  pleased  being
tr: He came and, clasping the Lord’s feet, made his petition: “I beg one thing of You; be pleased and grant it.

## 54
w: all  sannyāsīs  I  have made  invitation
w: You  if  come  fulfilled  becomes  my  heart
tr: I have invited all the sannyāsīs; if You come too, my heart’s desire will be fulfilled.

## 55
w: not  You go  sannyāsī-gatherings  this  I  know
w: to me  mercy  show  the invitation  accepting
tr: I know that You do not go to gatherings of sannyāsīs; yet be merciful to me and accept my invitation.”

## 56
w: the Lord  smiling  the invitation  made  acceptance
w: to the sannyāsīs  mercy  for the sake of  this  gesture  His
tr: Smiling, the Lord accepted the invitation; He made this gesture to show mercy to the sannyāsīs.

## 57
w: that  brāhmaṇa  knows  the Lord  not  goes  to anyone’s  house
w: by His  inspiration  Him  great insistence  makes
tr: The brāhmaṇa knew that the Lord does not go to anyone’s house, but, inspired by the Lord Himself, he pressed Him with great insistence.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: wishing to show mercy to the sannyāsīs, the Lord Himself moved the brāhmaṇa’s heart, and so he begged with such insistence.

## 58
w: on the next  day  went  the Lord  that  brāhmaṇa’s  house
w: He saw  are seated  of sannyāsīs  the company
tr: The next day the Lord went to the brāhmaṇa’s house and saw the sannyāsīs all seated there.

## 59
w: to all  bowing  He went  to wash the feet
w: the feet  having washed  sat  in that  place
tr: Bowing to them all, He went to wash His feet, and when He had washed them He sat down in that very place.

## 60
w: sitting  He made  some  of majesty  display
w: greatly effulgent  body  of millions of suns  the splendour
tr: As He sat there, He revealed a little of His majesty: His body shone with great effulgence, with the splendour of millions of suns.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «কোটীসূর্য্যাভাস» (another reading: «কোটিসূর্য্যাভাস»); the sense is the same.

## 61
w: by the splendour  He attracted  all  of the sannyāsīs  the minds
w: rose  the sannyāsīs  all  leaving  their seats
tr: That splendour drew the minds of all the sannyāsīs, and they all rose from their seats.

## 62
w: Prakāśānanda  by name  one  sannyāsī  chief
w: to the Lord  spoke  something  showing  respect
tr: The chief among the sannyāsīs, Prakāśānanda by name, addressed the Lord with respect:
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 7.149): Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī was then the leader of the sannyāsīs of Śaṅkara’s school in Kāśī. Some mistakenly identify him with Prabodhānanda Sarasvatī, the author of the *Caitanya-candrāmṛta* and the *Rādhā-rasa-sudhā-nidhi*, but they are different persons.

## 63
w: here  come  Gosāñi  listen  Śrīpāda
w: in an impure  place  You sit  what  dejection
tr: “Come here, Gosāñi; listen, Śrīpāda. You are sitting in an impure place — why are You so downcast?”

## 64
w: the Lord  says  I  am  of a lower sampradāya
w: of you all  in the assembly  to sit  not  befits
tr: The Lord replied, “I belong to a lower sampradāya; it is not fitting for Me to sit in your assembly.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: of the ten names of sannyāsīs instituted by Śaṅkarācārya, “Tīrtha”, “Āśrama” and “Sarasvatī” are held higher than the rest. Mahāprabhu had taken sannyāsa in the “Bhāratī” line and therefore called Prakāśānanda Sarasvatī’s school the higher one. In this way He showed the humility of a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī, who seeks no honour for himself and gives honour to others.

## 65
w: himself  Prakāśānanda  by the hand  taking
w: seated  in the midst of the assembly  respect  showing
tr: Then Prakāśānanda himself took Him by the hand and, with respect, seated Him in the midst of the assembly.

## 66
w: he asked  Your  name  ‘Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’
w: of Keśava Bhāratī  the disciple  therefore  You  are glorious
tr: He asked, “Your name is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya, is it not? You are a disciple of Keśava Bhāratī, and so You are worthy of glory.

## 67
w: of the sampradāya  a sannyāsī  You  You live  in this  town
w: for what  reason  us all  not  You make  seeing
tr: You are a sannyāsī of our own sampradāya and You live in this town; why then do You not come to see us?

## 68
w: a sannyāsī  being  You do  dancing and singing
w: sentimentalists  all  with  taking  You do  kīrtana
tr: Though You are a sannyāsī, You dance and sing, and You take all these sentimentalists with You and perform kīrtana.

## 69
w: Vedānta-study  meditation  of a sannyāsī  the dharma
w: that  abandoning  You do  why  of sentimentalists  the work
tr: The study of Vedānta and meditation are the dharma of a sannyāsī; why do You abandon them and do the work of sentimentalists?

## 70
w: by Your splendour  we see  You  directly  Nārāyaṇa

## 71
w: the Lord  says  listen  Śrīpāda  of this  the reason
w: the guru  Me  a fool  seeing  made  chastisement
tr: The Lord said, “Listen, Śrīpāda, to the reason for this. My guru saw that I was a fool, and he chastised Me:
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Caitanya, Himself the sole object of the Vedānta, teaches who is fit to study it: one who is humbler than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, who seeks no honour and gives honour to others. In calling Himself a fool, He, the Lord of the fourteen worlds, shows the humility of a disciple; the real fool is one who judges the object of the Vedānta by the mind and senses.

tr: ‘You are a fool; You are not qualified for Vedānta. Always chant the Kṛṣṇa-mantra — it is the essence of all mantras.

## 73
w: the Kṛṣṇa-mantra  from  will be  from saṁsāra  release
w: Kṛṣṇa’s name  from  You will attain  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
tr: From the Kṛṣṇa-mantra will come release from saṁsāra, and by Kṛṣṇa’s name You will attain Kṛṣṇa’s feet.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-sandarbha*, 284): the mantras too are names of the Lord, adorned with the word *namaḥ* and the like, and the sages have placed a special power in them; the name alone, depending on nothing else, is able to give the highest goal of life. Initiation is needed to curb the impure tendencies of those whose minds are attached to the body and restless.

## 76

w: there is not  indeed  way  other
tr: “The name of Hari, the name of Hari, the name of Hari alone! In the age of Kali there is no other way, there is no other way, there is no other way.” (Bṛhan-nāradīya Purāṇa, 38.126)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in Satya-yuga the way is meditation, in Tretā sacrifice, in Dvāpara the worship of the Deity; in Kali there is none of these — there is only one way, the name of Hari; all other means are fruitless in Kali.

## 83

fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 7.74): “Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare / Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare — these sixteen names destroy the impurity of Kali; no higher means is to be found in all the Vedas” (*Kali-santaraṇa Upaniṣad*). ## 84

## 85
w: the fifth  goal of life  an ocean of the nectar of the bliss of prema
w: of Brahman and the rest  the bliss  of which  is not  one  drop
tr: The fifth goal of life is an ocean of the nectar of the bliss of prema; the bliss of Brahman and all the rest is not equal to a single drop of it.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «ব্রহ্মাদি আনন্দ» (“the bliss of Brahman and the rest”; the heading: “Prema, which puts liberation and the rest to shame, is the fifth goal of life”). *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: even the bliss of Brahman, the first stage of liberation, cannot be compared with a single drop of prema. Other editions read «মোক্ষাদি» (“liberation and the rest”).

## 87
w: by prema’s  nature  it makes  of mind and body  agitation
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s  feet  for the attainment  arises  greed
tr: It is the nature of prema to stir both mind and body and to awaken a burning longing to attain Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

## 88
w: by prema’s  nature  the devotee  laughs  weeps  sings
w: mad  becoming  dances  here and there  runs
tr: By the nature of prema the devotee laughs, weeps and sings; as if mad, he dances and runs here and there.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: when those without prema display laughter, tears, dancing and singing, it is only a sign of their misfortune; one who performs bhajana must reject altogether any artificial agitation of body and senses. The laughter, tears, singing, dancing and longing born of bhāva and prema come when the natural function of the soul awakens; they are the guileless movements of one turned towards service. The presumption of one who, without prema, assumes the high station of a devotee brings only harm into the world.
