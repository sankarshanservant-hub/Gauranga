## 0
tr: The Discourse on the Truth of Bhakti as the Abhidheya

## 1
w: I worship  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
w: the Lord  Him  the ocean of mercy
w: in Kali  even  this most hidden
w: bhakti  by whom  was revealed
tr: I worship Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanyadeva, the ocean of mercy, by whom this most hidden bhakti was revealed even in the age of Kali.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *bhakti*, causeless service to Kṛṣṇa, was unknown even to the knowers of *dharma* in the Satya, Tretā and Dvāpara ages; Mahāprabhu proclaimed it to all in the age of Kali, the age of quarrel.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Ādi 10.2 (= Madhya 13.2); translation as there.

## 3
w: thus  indeed  I have told  of sambandha-tattva  the analysis
w: of the Vedas and śāstras  in the teaching  Kṛṣṇa  the one  essence
tr: “Thus I have explained to you the truth of *sambandha*. In the teaching of the Vedas and the śāstras, Kṛṣṇa is the one essence.
fn: The chapter continues the Lord’s instruction to Sanātana (Madhya 20–21). Heading of the edition: “in all the Vedas and śāstras Kṛṣṇa alone is established as the *sambandha*”.

## 4
w: now  I speak  listen  of abhidheya  the characteristics
w: which  through  one attains  Kṛṣṇa  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa
tr: Now listen as I describe *abhidheya*, the means by which one attains Kṛṣṇa and the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 5
w: devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is the abhidheya  all the scriptures  declare
w: therefore  the sages  have made  the conclusion
tr: All the scriptures declare that devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the *abhidheya*, and so the sages have concluded.

## 6
w: śruti  the mother  when asked  points out  of Your worship  the method
w: as  of the mother  the words  smṛti  too  so  speaks  the sister
w: the Purāṇas and the rest  which  and  the brothers  they  following her
w: therefore  truly  it is known  O slayer of Mura  You  alone  the shelter
tr: “When Mother Śruti is asked, she points out the way to worship You. Smṛti, her sister, speaks just as the mother does, and the Purāṇas and the rest, like brothers, follow her. Therefore, O slayer of Mura, I have truly understood that You alone are my shelter.” (Words of a sage)
fn: Heading of the edition: “words of a sage” (*muni-vākya*); the source of the verse is not named.

## 7
w: the Truth of nondual knowledge  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  Bhagavān
w: of svarūpa-śakti  in the form  His  is  abiding
tr: Kṛṣṇa, the nondual Truth (*advaya-jñāna-tattva*), is Bhagavān Himself. He abides in the form of His own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*).
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the potency and its possessor are non-different. “Potency” here must not be taken as *māyā-śakti*, which covers the nature of the *jīva*: the *svarūpa-śakti*, engaged only in the service of Kṛṣṇa Himself, abides inseparably with Him.

## 8
w: as His own parts and separated parts  in the form  having become  expanded
w: in endless  Vaikuṇṭhas and universes  He performs  pastimes
tr: Expanding Himself as His own parts (*svāṁśa*) and separated parts (*vibhinnāṁśa*), He sports in endless Vaikuṇṭhas and universes.
fn: Heading of the edition: in the countless Vaikuṇṭhas He sports in the forms of His own parts, the Viṣṇu forms, and in the universes in the forms of the jīvas.

## 9
w: the expansion of His own parts  the catur-vyūha  the host of avatāras
w: the separated part  the jīva  His  among the energies  is counted
tr: His own parts are the *catur-vyūha* and the host of avatāras. The jīva is His separated part and is counted among His energies.

## 10
w: those  separated parts  the jīvas  two  indeed  kinds
w: one  eternally liberated  one  eternally in worldly existence
tr: Those jīvas, His separated parts, are of two kinds: some are eternally liberated (*nitya-mukta*), and some are eternally in worldly existence (*nitya-saṁsāra*).

## 11
w: the eternally liberated  ever  toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet  turned
w: Kṛṣṇa’s associates  named  they enjoy  the joy of service
tr: The eternally liberated are ever turned toward Kṛṣṇa’s feet. They are called Kṛṣṇa’s associates (*pārṣadas*), and they enjoy the happiness of serving Him.

## 12
w: the eternally bound  from Kṛṣṇa  eternally turned away
w: eternally in worldly existence  they suffer  the miseries of hell and the like
tr: The eternally bound (*nitya-baddha*) are forever turned away from Kṛṣṇa. Eternally caught in worldly existence, they suffer the miseries of hell and the like.

## 13
w: for that  fault  the witch Māyā  punishment  inflicts  on him
w: beginning with those of body and mind  the threefold miseries  him  wearing down  torment
tr: For this fault the witch Māyā punishes him: the threefold miseries, beginning with those of body and mind (*ādhyātmika*), wear him down and torment him.

## 14
w: of lust and anger  the servant  having become  her  kicks  he suffers
w: wandering  wandering  if  a sādhu-physician  he finds
tr: Having become the servant of lust and anger, he suffers her kicks. But if, wandering on and on, he meets a sādhu, a physician of the soul,

## 15
w: his  by the mantra of instruction  the witch  flees
w: devotion to Kṛṣṇa  he obtains  then  near Kṛṣṇa  he goes
tr: by the mantra of that sādhu’s instruction the witch flees. The soul obtains devotion to Kṛṣṇa and then draws near to Kṛṣṇa.

## 16
w: of lust and the rest  how many  not  in how many ways  were obeyed  the evil commands
w: of them  arose  toward me  no  pity  no  shame  nor peace
w: having abandoned them  now  O Lord of the Yadus  at present  having gained good sense
w: to You  I have come  for shelter  fearless  me  engage in Your service
tr: “How many evil commands of lust and the rest have I not obeyed, and in how many ways! Yet they never took pity on me, nor did I feel shame or find peace. Now, O Lord of the Yadus, I have cast them off; good sense has dawned in me, and I have come to You, the fearless shelter. Engage me in Your service.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, Paścima-vibhāga, Prīti-bhakti-laharī, 6)

## 17
w: devotion to Kṛṣṇa  is  the chief abhidheya
w: on the face of bhakti  looking  karma  yoga  jñāna
tr: Devotion to Kṛṣṇa is the chief *abhidheya*. Karma, yoga and jñāna all look to the face of bhakti for their results.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: only by taking shelter of bhakti can karma and haṭha-yoga give the fruit of worldly enjoyment, and jñāna and rāja-yoga the fruits of liberation and mystic perfection.

## 18
w: of these  all  practices  very  feeble  the strength
w: devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without  they  to give  cannot  fruit
tr: The strength of all these practices is very feeble: without devotion to Kṛṣṇa they cannot give any fruit.

## 19
w: freedom from action  even  love for Acyuta  devoid of
w: not  shines  knowledge  at all  stainless
w: how then  again  ever  inauspicious  to the Lord
w: not  offered  action  which  even  free of motive
tr: “Even stainless knowledge, free from all action (*naiṣkarmya*), does not shine if it is devoid of love for Acyuta. How then can action shine, ever inauspicious as it is, if it is not offered to the Lord, even when it is free of selfish motive?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.5.12)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Nārada speaks thus to Vyāsadeva, who had found no satisfaction of heart, glorifying pure devotion to Hari, which is above all paths — karma, jñāna and the rest.

## 20
w: ascetics  devoted to charity  the renowned
w: the thoughtful  knowers of mantras  of auspicious conduct
w: welfare  not  attain  without  offering to Him
w: to Him  of auspicious fame  obeisance  obeisance
tr: “Ascetics, the generous, the renowned, the thoughtful, the knowers of mantras and those of auspicious conduct — none of them attains true welfare without offering everything to Him. Again and again I bow to Him, whose fame is auspicious.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.4.17)

## 21
w: mere  knowledge  liberation  to give  cannot  bhakti  without
w: to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa  that  liberation  comes  without knowledge
tr: Knowledge alone cannot give liberation without bhakti; but to one turned toward Kṛṣṇa that liberation comes even without knowledge.

## 22
w: the path to the highest good  bhakti  having rejected  Your  O almighty Lord
w: toil  those who  for attaining mere knowledge
w: for them  this  toil  only  remains
w: nothing else  as  for those who pound empty husks
tr: “O almighty Lord, bhakti to You is the path to the highest good. Those who reject it and toil only to gain knowledge are left with nothing but their toil, like those who pound empty husks.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.4)

## 23
w: divine  indeed  this  made of the guṇas
w: My  māyā  hard to overcome
w: to Me  alone  those who  surrender
w: māyā  this  cross over  they
tr: “This divine māyā of Mine, made of the guṇas, is hard to overcome. But those who surrender to Me alone cross beyond this māyā.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 7.14)
fn: The same verse as Madhya 20.121; translation as there.

## 24
w: Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant  the jīva  that  having forgotten  went
w: for that  fault  Māyā  his  around the neck  bound
tr: The jīva is Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant, but he has forgotten it. For that fault Māyā has bound him by the neck.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: māyā blinded the jīva who had forgotten that he is Kṛṣṇa’s eternal servant, and put on his neck the chain of the three guṇas — the desire to enjoy.

## 25
w: therefore  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he renders  the guru’s  service
w: the net of Māyā  falls away  he attains  Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
tr: If he then worships Kṛṣṇa and serves his guru, the net of Māyā falls away and he attains Kṛṣṇa’s feet.

## 26
w: of the four  followers of varṇa and āśrama  if  Kṛṣṇa  do not  worship
w: their own duties  even performing  they  in the hell Raurava  having fallen  sink
tr: If the followers of the four *varṇas* and *āśramas* do not worship Kṛṣṇa, then even while performing their own duties they fall into the hell Raurava and sink there.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: though under worldly pride they may rise high, when their merit is spent they surely fall into the hell Raurava.

## 27
w: from the mouth, arms, thighs and feet
w: of the Puruṣa  with the āśramas  together
w: four  were born  the varṇas
w: by their qualities  brāhmaṇas and the rest  distinct
tr: “From the mouth, arms, thighs and feet of the Puruṣa the four *varṇas*, beginning with the brāhmaṇas, were born together with the *āśramas*, each distinct by its qualities.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.2)
fn: Heading of the edition: *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, 11.5.2–3 (on verses 27–28). *Anubhāṣya*: the Puruṣa is the universal (*vairāja*) Brahmā; the sage Camasa answers King Nimi thus, describing the *daiva-varṇāśrama* favourable to bhakti and the fate of those who depart from it.

## 28
w: who  of them  the Puruṣa  directly
w: their own source  the Lord
w: not  worship  disregard
w: from their position  fallen  they fall  down
tr: “Those among them who do not worship the Puruṣa, their own source and Lord, but disregard Him, fall from their position and go down.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.3)

## 29
w: the jñānī  of liberation in this life  the state  “I have attained”  thus  thinks
w: in truth  the intelligence  pure  is not  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  without
tr: The jñānī thinks, ‘I have attained the state of liberation in this life (*jīvan-mukti*).’ But in truth the intelligence does not become pure without devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

## 30
w: those who  others  O lotus-eyed Lord  who think themselves liberated
w: toward You  for want of love  of impure intelligence
w: having risen  with hardship  to the supreme  position  from there
w: fall  down  having neglected Your feet
tr: “O lotus-eyed Lord, others think themselves liberated, but their intelligence is impure, for they have no love for You. Though they rise with great hardship to the supreme position, they fall down from there, having neglected Your feet.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.2.32)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Brahmā and the gods pray thus to Bhagavān, who has appeared in the womb of Devakī; the “supreme position” (*paraṁ padam*), according to Śrīdhara Svāmī, is the place immediately next to *mokṣa*.

## 31
w: Kṛṣṇa  like the sun  Māyā  is  darkness
w: where  Kṛṣṇa  there  is no  of Māyā  dominion
tr: Kṛṣṇa is like the sun, and Māyā is darkness. Where Kṛṣṇa is, Māyā has no power.
fn: Heading of the edition: where Kṛṣṇa is seen, māyā is not seen; where māyā is seen, Kṛṣṇa is not seen.

## 32
w: ashamed  whose
w: to stand  in the path of the sight  by her
w: bewildered  boast
w: “mine” and “I”  thus  the foolish
tr: “Māyā is ashamed to stand in the path of His sight; yet the foolish, bewildered by her, boast of ‘I’ and ‘mine’.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.5.13)

## 33
w: “Kṛṣṇa  Yours  I am”  if  he says  once
w: from the bondage of Māyā  Kṛṣṇa  him  makes  carried across
tr: If someone says even once, ‘Kṛṣṇa, I am Yours’, Kṛṣṇa carries him across, beyond the bondage of Māyā.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: not by habit, with the lips alone, but from the whole heart — with body, mind and words.

## 34
w: once  only  surrendered  who
w: “I am Yours”  thus  and  begs
w: fearlessness  always  to him
w: I give  this  vow  My
tr: “To one who surrenders to Me even once and prays, ‘I am Yours,’ I always grant fearlessness. This is My vow.” (Rāmāyaṇa, Yuddha-kāṇḍa, 18.33)
fn: Heading of the edition: the words of Śrī Rāmacandra in the *Rāmāyaṇa*, when Vibhīṣaṇa came to Him.

## 35
w: desiring liberation, enjoyment and perfections  truly intelligent  if  he becomes
w: by deep bhakti-yoga  then  Kṛṣṇa  he worships
tr: If one who desires liberation, worldly enjoyment or mystic perfections becomes truly intelligent, he then worships Kṛṣṇa with deep *bhakti-yoga*.

## 36
w: without desire  full of all desires  or
w: desiring liberation  of broad intelligence
w: with intense  bhakti-yoga
w: should worship  the Person  Supreme
tr: “Whether he is without desire, full of every desire, or desirous of liberation, a person of broad intelligence should worship the Supreme Person with intense *bhakti-yoga*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 2.3.10)

## 37
w: one with other desires  if  performs  Kṛṣṇa’s  worship
w: not  even having asked  Kṛṣṇa  to him  gives  His own feet
tr: If one who has other desires worships Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa gives him His own feet even though he has not asked for them.

## 38
w: Kṛṣṇa  says  Me  he worships  he asks for  sense  pleasure
w: nectar  giving up  poison  he asks for  this one  a great  fool
tr: Kṛṣṇa says, ‘He worships Me, yet asks for sense pleasure. Giving up nectar, he asks for poison — he is a great fool.

## 39
w: I  am wise  to this  fool  sense objects  why  should I give
w: the nectar of My own feet  giving  sense objects  I shall make him forget
tr: But I am wise: why should I give sense objects to this fool? I will give him the nectar of My own feet and make him forget sense objects.’

## 40
w: truly  He grants  what is asked  when asked  to men
w: not indeed  giver of that  which  again  asking  from which
w: Himself  He grants  to those who worship  though not desiring
w: covering all desires  His own  feet like tender shoots
tr: “It is true that when men ask, the Lord grants what they ask for; but He does not give what would make them ask again. To those who worship Him, though they do not desire it, He Himself gives His own feet, tender as new shoots, which put an end to all other desires.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.19.26)
fn: Numbered as in the heading of the edition and the *Anubhāṣya* (5.19.26); in other editions — 5.19.27.

## 41
w: desire  for the sake of  Kṛṣṇa  he worships  he attains  the taste of Kṛṣṇa
w: desire  giving up  a servant  to become  there arises  the longing
tr: One who worships Kṛṣṇa for the sake of some desire comes to taste Kṛṣṇa’s sweetness (*kṛṣṇa-rasa*). Then, giving up that desire, he longs to become His servant.

## 42
w: desiring a position  in austerity  engaged  I
w: You  have attained  hidden from the gods and the best of sages
w: glass  searching for  though  a divine jewel
w: O Master  fulfilled  I am  a boon  not  I ask
tr: “Desiring a high position, I engaged in austerities, and I have attained You, who are hidden even from the gods and the greatest sages. I was searching for a piece of glass and found a divine jewel. O Master, my purpose is fulfilled; I ask for no boon.” (Hari-bhakti-sudhodaya, 7.28)

## 43
w: in worldly existence  while wandering  by some  fortune  someone  is delivered
w: of a river  in the current  as  a log  touches  the bank
tr: While wandering in worldly existence, someone by some good fortune is delivered, as a log carried by a river’s current comes to rest on the bank.

## 44
w: not so  of me  lowly  even
w: there will be  surely  the sight of Acyuta
w: being carried away  by the river of time
w: at times  crosses over  someone
tr: “It is not so that I, lowly as I am, cannot see Acyuta: I will surely see Him. For even among those carried away by the river of time, someone at times reaches the shore.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.38.5)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Akrūra reflects thus on the road to Gokula, where he is going to bring Rāma and Kṛṣṇa.

## 45
w: by some  fortune  someone’s  worldly existence  near its end  becomes
w: in the company of sādhus  he is delivered  for Kṛṣṇa  attraction  arises
tr: When by some good fortune someone’s worldly existence nears its end, he is delivered through the company of sādhus, and attraction (*rati*) for Kṛṣṇa awakens in him.

## 46
w: the end of worldly existence  of one wandering  when  comes
w: of a person  then  O Acyuta  meeting with the saints
w: association with the saints  when  at that very time  for the goal of the saints
w: for the Lord of the high and the low  for You  is born  attraction
tr: “O Acyuta, when a wandering soul’s worldly existence comes to its end, he meets the saints. And when he associates with the saints, attraction (*rati*) is born in him for You, the goal of the saints and the Lord of the high and the low.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.51.53)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Mucukunda prays thus to Śrī Kṛṣṇa; the end of worldly existence (*bhavāpavarga*) comes by the Lord’s mercy, when its time arrives.

## 47
w: Kṛṣṇa  if  mercy  shows  to some  fortunate soul
w: in the forms of guru and the Indweller  teaches  Himself
tr: If Kṛṣṇa shows mercy to some fortunate soul, He Himself teaches him in the forms of the guru and the Indweller (*antaryāmī*).

## 48
w: not  attain  repayment  the wise  Your,  O Lord
w: with Brahmā’s lifespan  even  Your deeds  with growing joy  remembering
w: who  within  without  of the embodied  the inauspicious  dispelling
w: as ācārya  and Supersoul  in forms  Your own path  reveal
tr: “O Lord! Even if the wise lived as long as Brahmā and remembered with ever-growing joy all that You have done for them, they could not repay their debt to You. For, dispelling all that is inauspicious in embodied souls, You come to them from without as the ācārya and from within as the Supersoul, and reveal to them the way to Yourself.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.29.6)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.48; translation as there.

## 49
w: in the company of sādhus  in devotion to Kṛṣṇa  faith  if  arises
w: the fruit of bhakti  prema  comes  worldly existence  goes  to its end
tr: If, in the company of sādhus, faith in devotion to Kṛṣṇa arises, then prema, the fruit of bhakti, appears, and worldly existence comes to an end.

## 50
w: by good fortune  in narrations about Me and the rest
w: in whom faith is born  but  who  the person
w: not  disgusted  not overly attached
w: bhakti-yoga  for him  gives perfection
tr: “For one who by good fortune has come to have faith in narrations about Me and the like, and who is neither disgusted with the world nor overly attached to it, *bhakti-yoga* grants perfection.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.8)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *nirviṇṇa* — one who holds to false (*phalgu*) renunciation; just such a person, full of faith, is qualified for bhakti-yoga.

## 51
w: of the great souls  the mercy  without  by any  activity  bhakti  is not
w: devotion to Kṛṣṇa  aside  let it be  worldly existence  does not  end
tr: Without the mercy of great souls no activity yields bhakti. Without it, worldly existence does not even come to an end, let alone devotion to Kṛṣṇa arise.

## 52
w: O Rahūgaṇa  this  by austerity  not  is attained
w: not  and by worship  by renunciation  by household life  or
w: not  by the Vedas  nor  by water, fire and sun
w: without  bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls
tr: “O Rahūgaṇa, this is not attained by austerity, by worship, by renunciation or by household duties, nor by the Vedas, nor by worship of water, fire and the sun, without bathing in the dust of the feet of great souls.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 5.12.12)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *avadhūta* Bharata instructs Rahūgaṇa, king of Sindhu and Sauvīra, thus; *ijyā* — Vedic rites, the worship of the gods; *nirvapaṇa* — *sannyāsa*; *gṛha* — the sacrifices of the householder.

## 53
w: not  their  mind  so long  Urukrama’s feet
w: touches  the removal of anarthas  whose purpose
w: of the great  bathing in the dust of the feet
w: of those who own nothing  not  accepts  as long as
tr: “So long as people do not accept a bath in the dust of the feet of the great, who own nothing (*niṣkiñcana*), their minds cannot touch the feet of Urukrama, whose purpose is to remove all *anarthas*.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.5.32)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Prahlāda answers Hiraṇyakaśipu, who asked where his Vaiṣṇava disposition came from.

## 54
w: sādhu-saṅga  sādhu-saṅga  all the scriptures  declare
w: a mere moment of  sādhu-saṅga  all perfection  comes
tr: ‘The company of sādhus, the company of sādhus!’ — so declare all the scriptures. Even a moment of *sādhu-saṅga* brings every perfection.

## 55
w: we compare  with a moment  even
w: not  heaven  not liberation
w: of association with those who associate with the Lord
w: of mortals  what  then  the blessings
tr: “We cannot compare even heaven or liberation with a moment of association with those who associate with the Lord — what then of the blessings mortals seek?” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.18.13)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the sages headed by Śaunaka glorify thus their association with Sūta, the *mahā-bhāgavata*.

## 56
w: Kṛṣṇa  merciful  Arjuna  the aim  making
w: the world  has protected  instruction  giving

## 57

## 58

w: to Me  surely you will come  truly  to you

## 59
w: the earlier  commands  Vedic dharma  karma  yoga  jñāna
w: all  having established  the final command  is strongest
tr: His earlier commands concerned Vedic *dharma*, karma, yoga and jñāna. Having established all of these, He gave His final command, and it is the strongest.

## 60

w: all activities  giving up  having made  he  Kṛṣṇa  worships
tr: If by the force of this command faith arises in the devotee, he gives up all other activities and worships Kṛṣṇa.

## 61

w: in hearing My narrations and the rest  or

tr: “One should go on performing one’s prescribed duties only so long as one has not grown weary of them, or so long as faith has not been born in hearing about Me and the like.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.20.9)
fn: The same verse as Madhya 9.266; translation as there.

## 62
w: by the word “śraddhā”  trust  is meant  very firm  conviction
w: to Kṛṣṇa  devotion  when one renders  all duties  done  become
tr: The word *śraddhā* means trust: a very firm conviction that by rendering devotion to Kṛṣṇa one has fulfilled all one’s duties.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: one who serves Kṛṣṇa need not discharge the duties of the worldly realm — the debts to the forefathers, to beings, to the gods and others; detachment, the best fruit of karma, accompanies the devotee of itself.

## 63
w: as  of a tree  by watering the root
w: are satisfied  its trunk, branches and twigs
w: by feeding the life-breath  and  as  of the senses
w: just so  the worship of all  is the worship of Acyuta
tr: “As by watering the root of a tree its trunk, branches and twigs are all nourished, and as by feeding the life-breath (*prāṇa*) all the senses are satisfied, so the worship of Acyuta is the worship of all.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 4.31.14)

## 64
w: having faith  a person  is  qualified for bhakti
w: highest  middle  lowest  according to faith
tr: A person who has faith is qualified for bhakti. According to his faith he is of the highest, the middle or the lowest level (*uttama*, *madhyama*, *kaniṣṭha*).

## 65
w: in scripture and reasoning  very expert  firm faith  whose
w: of the highest  qualification  he  delivers  the world
tr: One who is expert in scripture and reasoning and whose faith is firm is an *uttama adhikārī*, of the highest qualification; he delivers the world.

## 66
w: the reasoning of scripture  not  knows  firmly  having faith
w: of middle qualification  he  greatly fortunate
tr: One who does not firmly know the reasoning of scripture but has faith is a *madhyama adhikārī*, of the middle qualification; he is most fortunate.
fn: The *Anubhāṣya* cites Śrī Rūpa: “One who is not so expert in the śāstras and the rest, but has faith, is of the middle level.”

## 67
w: whose  tender  faith  he  a kaniṣṭha  person
w: step by step  step by step  he  a devotee  will become  of the highest
tr: One whose faith is still tender is a *kaniṣṭha*, a beginner. Step by step he too will become a devotee of the highest level.

## 68

w: in the eleventh  canto  their  has been given  description
tr: According to the degree of their *rati* and prema, devotees are higher and highest. The Eleventh Canto has described their characteristics.

## 69

## 70

## 73

## 74

## 75

w: faultless  magnanimous  gentle  pure  owning nothing
tr: A Vaiṣṇava is merciful, harms no one, is devoted to truth, equal to all, faultless, magnanimous, gentle, pure and without possessions (*akiñcana*);
fn: Heading of the edition: “the secondary (*taṭastha*) marks”. *Anubhāṣya*: the essential (*svarūpa*) mark of a Vaiṣṇava is that Kṛṣṇa is his only shelter (*kṛṣṇaika-śaraṇa*, verse 76); all the rest are secondary.
