fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the kāyasthas were officials who served under Sanātana. Seeing that he was inclined to renunciation, some of them, hoping to gain his post, began to show special zeal in the king’s affairs. Tradition says that after Sanātana his post went to his subordinate, Purandara Khān.
