## 0
tr: A Brief Account of the Antya-līlā: The Ravings of Love’s Madness

## 1
w: in chapter  this  of the Lord  final
w: pastimes  sūtras  in describing
w: of Gaura  from Kṛṣṇa  in separation
w: raving and the rest  is described
tr: “In this chapter, as I set out in brief the Lord’s final pastimes, I describe Gaura’s raving (*pralāpa*) and His other states in separation from Kṛṣṇa.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the final pastimes are the Lord’s life in sannyāsa; Gaura is the Lord who has taken on the feelings of the gopīs; *pralāpa* is the speech of madness born of separation from the Beloved. Heading in the edition: “The Lord’s raving and other states in separation from Kṛṣṇa”.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: the last  which  remained  of the Lord  twelve  years
w: of Kṛṣṇa  separation  the feeling  was  constant
tr: Through the last twelve years that remained to Him, the Lord felt His separation from Kṛṣṇa without cease.

## 4
w: of Śrī Rādhikā  the behaviour  as  on seeing Uddhava
w: such  a state  of the Lord  was  night and day
tr: Night and day the Lord was in the very state of Śrī Rādhikā when She met Uddhava.

## 5
w: constant  was  of the Lord  separation  madness
w: bewildered  actions  always  raving  speech
tr: The Lord was constantly in the divine madness of separation: He moved always as one bewildered, and His speech was raving.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: bewildered actions — *udghūrṇā*; raving speech — the ten kinds of mad speech, beginning with *citra-jalpa*.

## 6
w: from the pores  blood oozing  teeth  all  loosened
w: at one moment  the body  thin  becomes  at another  the body  swells
tr: Blood oozed from His pores, and all His teeth shook. At one moment His body grew thin, at another it swelled.

## 7
w: within the Gambhīrā  at night  not  of sleep  a particle
w: against the wall  face and head  rubs  wounds  become  all over
tr: Inside the Gambhīrā He did not sleep a moment at night. He rubbed His face and head against the wall, and they were covered with wounds.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Gambhīrā is the small inner room behind the veranda and the hall.

## 8
w: on three  doors  bolts  the Lord  goes  outside
w: sometimes  at the Lion Gate  falls  sometimes  in the waters of the sea
tr: Though the three doors were bolted, the Lord would go outside. Sometimes He fell at the Lion Gate, sometimes into the waters of the sea.

## 9
w: Caṭaka  hill  seeing  for Govardhana  mistaking
w: running  goes  cries of anguish  making  weeping
tr: Seeing the Caṭaka hill, He mistook it for Govardhana, and ran towards it, weeping and crying out in anguish.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the sand hills on the seashore are called *caṭaka-parvatas*; there is a large one between the Guṇḍicā temple and the sea, and Mahāprabhu often ran there, taking it for Govardhana.

## 10
w: groves and gardens  seeing  as Vṛndāvana  conceiving
w: there  going  dances  sings  at times  unconscious  falls
tr: The groves and gardens He took for Vṛndāvana; He would go there to dance and sing, and at times He fell unconscious.

## 11
w: nowhere  not  heard  which  of ecstasy  transformations
w: those  ecstasies  became  in the Lord’s  body  manifest
tr: Transformations of feeling never heard of anywhere showed themselves in the Lord’s body.

## 12
w: of hands and feet  the joints  all  a span  in length
w: joints  leaving  separate  became  the skin  remained  in place
tr: All the joints of His arms and legs came apart by a span; they left their sockets, and only the skin held them in place.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the bones in the joints come apart, the skin alone stays in place, and the joint stretches to the length of a span (*vitasti*).

## 13
w: hands  feet  head  all  the body  within
w: withdrawn  became  a tortoise’s form  was seen  in the Lord
tr: His hands, feet and head all withdrew into His body, and the Lord was seen in the form of a tortoise.

## 14
w: in this way  wondrous  ecstasies  in the body  manifest
w: in the mind  emptiness  in words  alas  despair
tr: Thus wondrous feelings appeared in His body. His mind was empty, and His words were full of lament and despair:

## 15
w: where  My  of life  Lord  with the flute at His lips
w: what  shall I do  where  shall I find  the son of the king of Vraja
tr: “Where is the Lord of My life, with the flute at His lips? What shall I do? Where shall I find the son of the king of Vraja?

## 16
w: to whom  shall I tell  who  knows  My  sorrow
w: the son of the king of Vraja  without  bursts  My  heart
tr: To whom can I speak? Who knows My sorrow? Without the son of the king of Vraja My heart is breaking.”

## 17
w: thus  lamentation  makes  bewildered  at heart
w: of Rāya  from the drama  verses  recites  constantly
tr: Thus He lamented, bewildered at heart, and constantly recited verses from Rāmānanda Rāya’s drama:

## 18
w: love’s  breaking  the pains  knows  Hari  not  this  not  and  love  either
w: proper and improper place  knows  nor even  Madana  knows  us  weak
w: another  knows  not  and  another’s  sorrow  all  our  life  is  not obedient
w: two or three  only  days  youth  this  alas  alas  of the Creator  what  is the way
tr: (Śrī Rādhā laments:) “This Hari does not know the pains we suffer when love is broken; nor does love know where it should and should not strike; nor does even Madana know how weak we are. No one knows another’s sorrow in full; our very life is not in our control, and this youth lasts only two or three days. Alas, alas, O Creator, what is to become of us?” (Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka, 3.9)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the pain is that of love broken off — Kṛṣṇa has broken the bond, and the gopīs are tormented by the sickness of separation; *āśravam* — “nothing but suffering”, or “in another’s power”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: another reading is *vidhe* (“O Creator!”).

## 19
w: sprouted  of love the sprout  broke (it)  that  of sorrow  flood
w: Kṛṣṇa  it  not  does  drink
w: outwardly  king of lovers  inwardly  of a cheat  the work
w: others’ women  in killing  intent
tr: “In My heart the sprout of love came up, but a flood of sorrow broke it, and Kṛṣṇa pays it no heed. Outwardly He is the king of lovers, but within He does the work of a cheat, intent on killing other men’s wives.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the sprout of love came up when She met Kṛṣṇa; in separation it was wounded, and now a stream of sorrow flows. Kṛṣṇa alone can heal it, yet He makes no effort to protect the sprout.

## 20
w: O friend  not  I understand  the Creator’s  ordinance
w: happiness  for the sake of  I loved  came out  contrary  the course
w: now  goes  not  stays  life
tr: O friend, I do not understand the Creator’s ordinance. I loved for the sake of happiness, but everything turned out the opposite. Now life is leaving Me; it will not stay.

## 21
w: crooked  love  senseless  not  knows  proper and improper place
w: good and bad  cannot  consider
w: of the cruel  cheat  with the rope of qualities  hands and neck  binding  Me
w: keeps  unable  to get free
tr: Love is crooked and senseless: it does not know where it should strike and where not, nor can it tell good from bad. With the rope of that cruel cheat’s qualities it has bound My hands and neck, and I cannot get free.
fn: Bengali *guṇa* means both “quality” and “rope”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: it is love itself that binds Rādhā with the rope of the qualities of that cruel cheat, Kṛṣṇa.

## 22
w: that  Madana  bodiless  in harming others  expert
w: five  arrows  fixes  constantly
w: of a helpless woman  into the body  piercing  has made  riddled
w: pain  gives  not  takes  life
tr: That bodiless Madana, so skilled in harming others, constantly fixes his five arrows to the bow. He has pierced the body of a helpless woman through and through: he gives her pain but does not take her life.

## 23
w: of another  which  sorrow  in the heart  another  that  not  knows
w: true  this  of scripture  the verdict
w: of other  people  why  write  not  knows  the friend dear as life
w: since  says  patience  to hold
tr: What sorrow lies in another’s heart, no one else knows: this verdict of scripture is true. Why speak of others? Even My friend, dear to Me as life, does not know it, for she only tells Me to be patient.

## 24
w: Kṛṣṇa  of mercy  the ocean  some day  will accept
w: friend  your  these  useless  words
w: of the living being  the life  unsteady  like  on a lotus leaf  water
w: so many  days  will live  which  person
tr: ‘Kṛṣṇa is an ocean of mercy; some day He will accept You’ — friend, these words of yours are useless. A living being’s life is as unsteady as water on a lotus leaf: who will live long enough?

## 25
w: a hundred  years  up to  of the living being  life’s  end
w: this  statement  you make  not  considering
w: of a woman  youth’s  wealth  on which  Kṛṣṇa  sets  His mind
w: that  youth  days  two or four
tr: You say without thinking that a living being’s life lasts a hundred years. But the wealth of a woman’s youth, on which Kṛṣṇa sets His mind, lasts only a few short days.

## 26
w: fire  as  its own  light  showing  charming
w: the moth  attracting  kills
w: Kṛṣṇa  so  His own  qualities  showing  steals  the mind
w: afterwards  of sorrow  into the ocean  casts
tr: As fire shows its charming light, draws the moth and kills it, so Kṛṣṇa shows His qualities and steals the heart, and then casts it into an ocean of sorrow.”

## 27
w: so much  lament  making  in dejection  Śrī Gaurahari
w: opening  of sorrow  the door
w: of ecstasy  of the waves  by the force  in many ways  the mind  moves
w: another  one  verse  made  recitation
tr: Lamenting so much in His dejection, Śrī Gaurahari opened the door of His sorrow. Driven by the force of the waves of feeling, His mind moved in many ways, and He recited another verse:

## 28
w: of Śrī Kṛṣṇa  form and the rest  service  without
w: useless  My  days  all senses  utterly
w: of stone  dry wood  burdens  alas
w: I bear  how  them  shameless
tr: “Without serving Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s form and all else that is His, My days and all My senses are utterly useless. Alas, how can I, shameless, go on bearing these senses, burdens of stone and dry wood?
fn: Heading in the edition: “A song of despondency (*nirveda*) that reveals deep love for Kṛṣṇa”; the verse is called the words of the Gosvāmipāda (*gosvāmi-pādokta-śloka*). *Anubhāṣya*: shameless — because She still clings to this living body that does not taste Kṛṣṇa.

## 29
w: of the flute song’s nectar  abode  of beauty’s nectar  birthplace
w: who  not  sees  that moon-like  face
w: of those  eyes  what  use  let fall  his  on the head  a thunderbolt
w: those  eyes  remain  for what  reason
tr: That moon-like face is the abode of the nectar of His flute song and the birthplace of the nectar of beauty. What use are the eyes of one who does not see it? Let a thunderbolt fall on his head! Why do such eyes remain?
fn: For verses 29–34 the *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Bhāgavatam* (2.3.17–24): ears that do not hear of the Lord’s deeds are empty holes; a tongue that does not sing of Him is a frog’s tongue; one who has never smelt the tulasī from His feet is a corpse that breathes. Heading in the edition (29–34): without service to Kṛṣṇa the body and senses are fruitless — the futility of eyes, ears, tongue, nose and skin.

## 30
w: O friend  hear  My  struck  destiny’s power
w: My  body heart mind  all  senses
w: Kṛṣṇa  without  all  fruitless
tr: O friend, hear Me: My fortune is struck down. My body, heart and mind, and all My senses — without Kṛṣṇa all are fruitless.

## 31
w: Kṛṣṇa’s  sweet  words  of nectar  a river
w: their  entry  is not  into which  ears
w: of a holed cowrie shell  a hole  like  know  those  ears
w: their  birth  was  in vain
tr: Kṛṣṇa’s sweet words are a river of nectar. Know that the ears they do not enter are like the hole in a broken cowrie shell: their birth was in vain.

## 32
w: of Kṛṣṇa  the nectar of the lips  Kṛṣṇa’s  qualities  and deeds
w: of the essence of nectar  the taste  surpassing
w: their  taste  who  not  knows  being born  not  died  why
w: that  tongue  a frog’s tongue  like
tr: The nectar of Kṛṣṇa’s lips, and Kṛṣṇa’s qualities and deeds, surpass in taste the very essence of nectar. Why did one who does not know their taste not die at birth? That tongue is like a frog’s tongue.

## 33
w: of musk  and the blue lotus  in mingling  which  fragrance
w: that  steals  its  pride
w: such  Kṛṣṇa’s  body’s  fragrance  whose  not  that  connection
w: that  nose  a bellows  like
tr: The fragrance of Kṛṣṇa’s body steals the pride even of the scent born from mingling musk and the blue lotus. The nose that has no connection with that fragrance is like a bellows.

## 34
w: Kṛṣṇa’s  hands  and feet  the palms and soles  than millions of moons  cooler
w: their  touch  like  a touchstone
w: their  touch  not  whose  he  let go  to ruin
w: that  body  like iron  I know
tr: The palms of Kṛṣṇa’s hands and the soles of His feet are cooler than millions of moons, and their touch is like a touchstone. Let one who has not known that touch go to ruin: I know his body is like iron.”
fn: The touchstone (*sparśa-maṇi*) turns iron into gold at a touch.

## 35
w: making  so much  lament  the Lord  Śrī Śacīnandana
w: opening  of the heart  the grief
w: in humility  despondency  dejection  of the heart  in exhaustion
w: again  recites  one  verse
tr: Lamenting so much, the Lord, Śrī Śacīnandana, laid open the grief of His heart. In humility, despondency and dejection, His heart exhausted, He recited another verse:

## 36
w: when  came  by fortune  the enemy of Madhu  this  of the eyes  on the path
w: then  our  mind  by wretched Madana  stolen  was
w: again  in which  He  a moment  even  of the eyes  comes  on the path
w: we shall make  in it  all the ghaṭikās  with jewels  studded
tr: “When by good fortune Madhu’s enemy came within our sight, our mind was stolen by that wretched Madana. When He comes again into our sight even for a moment, we shall stud all the *ghaṭikās* of that time with jewels.” (Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka, 3.11)

## 37
w: at the time  or  as in a dream  I saw  the one with the flute at His lips
w: at that  time  came  two  enemies
w: bliss  and  Madana  stealing  took  My  mind
w: to see  not  I could  eyes  filling
tr: “Whenever I saw Him with the flute at His lips, even in a dream, two enemies came at once — Bliss and Madana. They stole My mind, and I could not see Him to My eyes’ content.

## 38
w: again  if  some  moment  grants  Kṛṣṇa’s  sight
w: then  those  ghaṭīs  kṣaṇas  and palas
w: giving  garlands  and sandal  various  jewelled  ornaments
w: adorned  I shall make  all
tr: If some moment ever again grants Me the sight of Kṛṣṇa, then I shall adorn all those *ghaṭīs*, *kṣaṇas* and *palas* with garlands and sandal paste and many jewelled ornaments.”

## 39
w: in a moment  external  became  the mind  before  sees  two  persons
w: to them  asks  I  not  Caitanya (conscious)
w: like a dream  what  I saw  what  I  raved
w: you  anything  heard  of humble words
tr: In a moment His mind came to external consciousness, and seeing two persons before Him, He asked them: “Am I not Caitanya? What did I see, as though in a dream? What did I say in My raving? Did you hear any words of misery?
fn: Heading in the edition: “The Lord’s mahābhāva — *citra-jalpa*; in outward consciousness He laments before Svarūpa and Rāmānanda”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the two were Svarūpa Dāmodara and Rāya Rāmānanda; seeing them, the Lord came partly to outward consciousness, set aside the feeling that He was Rādhā, and asked, “Am I not that very Caitanya?” The Bengali *caitanya* also means “consciousness”: “Am I not conscious?”

## 40
w: hear  My  of life  friend
w: not  for Kṛṣṇa  love’s wealth  poor  My  life
w: body and senses  useless  My  all
tr: Hear Me, friend of My life: I have no wealth of love for Kṛṣṇa. My life is poor, and My body and senses are all useless.”

## 41
w: again  says  alas  alas  hear  Svarūpa and Rāmarāya
w: this  My  heart’s  conviction
w: having heard  consider  is  is not  tell  the truth
w: so  saying  a verse  utters
tr: Again He said: “Alas, alas! Hear, Svarūpa and Rāmarāya, what My heart is sure of. Having heard it, consider and tell Me truly whether it is so or not.” Saying this, He uttered a verse:

## 42
w: free of deceit  love  not  indeed  is  in the human  world
w: if  it is  whose  separation  in separation  occurring  who  lives
tr: “Love free of deceit does not exist in the human world. If it exists, how can anyone be separated? And if separation comes, who can live?”
fn: Heading in the edition: a Prakrit verse cited in the *Vaiṣṇava-toṣaṇī* on *Bhāgavatam* 10.31.1. *Anubhāṣya*: love without deceit — without the pretended religion of dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa and the rest.

## 43
w: without deceit  love for Kṛṣṇa  like  of the Jāmbū river  gold
w: that  love  in the human world  not  exists
w: if  is  with it  union  not  is  then  separation
w: separation  occurring  anyone  not  lives
tr: “Love for Kṛṣṇa without deceit is like the gold of the Jāmbū river: such love does not exist in the human world. If one is joined with it, there is no separation; and if separation comes, no one lives.”

## 44
w: so  saying  the son of Śacī  a verse  recites  wondrous
w: hear  both  of one  mind  becoming
w: of My own  heart’s  affairs  to tell  I feel  shame
w: still  I tell  shame’s seed  eating
tr: Having said this, the son of Śacī recited a wondrous verse, and the two listened with one mind: “I feel ashamed to tell what is in My heart, yet I will speak, casting shame aside:
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *lāja-bīja khāñā* (lit. “having eaten the seed of shame”) — casting off all shame.

## 45
w: not  of love a trace  is  even slightly  in Me  for Hari
w: I weep  of good fortune  the greatness  to display
w: of Him who plays the flute  of the face  without seeing
w: I sustain  that  the insects of My life  in vain
tr: There is not even a slight trace of love for Hari in Me. I weep only to display how great My good fortune is. Without seeing the face of Him who plays the flute, I sustain in vain this insect-like life of Mine.
fn: Heading in the edition: a verse of Śrī Mahāprabhu Himself (*śrī-śrī-mahāprabhu-pādokta-śloka*). *Anubhāṣya*: I weep — I shed tears of joy, as though I had prema, to display My great good fortune.

## 46
w: far  of pure love  the scent  of feigned  love  the bond
w: even that  of Mine  is not  at Kṛṣṇa’s feet
w: yet  that  I make  weeping  my good fortune’s  display
w: I make  this  know  for certain
tr: Far from Me is even the scent of pure love; I do not have even a bond of feigned love at Kṛṣṇa’s feet. If I weep, I do it to display My good fortune — know this for certain.

## 47
w: in which  the flute sound’s  joy  not  seeing  that  moon-like  face
w: although  there is not  support (ālambana)
w: My own body  I make  love  only  of kāma  the way
w: the insect of life  I make  maintenance
tr: Without seeing that moon-like face, in which lies the joy of the flute’s sound, I have no support (*ālambana*) for My life; still I love My own body. This is simply the way of *kāma*, desire for oneself: I maintain this insect of a life.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *ālambana* (support) is the meeting of the one served (*viṣaya*) and the servant (*āśraya*); the longing to see Kṛṣṇa’s face shows that this support is absent, and then life is kept up in vain, for the sake of *kāma*, desire for oneself.

## 48
w: love for Kṛṣṇa  spotless  like  pure  Ganges  water
w: that  love  of nectar  an ocean
w: spotless  in that  attachment (anurāga)  not  hides  another  stain
w: on white cloth  like  an ink  drop
tr: Love for Kṛṣṇa is spotless, like the pure water of the Ganges; that love is an ocean of nectar. In that spotless attachment (*anurāga*) no other stain can hide, just as a drop of ink cannot hide on white cloth.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the anurāga of pure love for Kṛṣṇa is like white cloth, and the absence of anurāga is like a drop of ink: against it, it shows at once.

## 49
w: pure love’s  happiness  ocean  if I get  its  one  drop
w: that  drop  the world  drowns
w: to tell  fit  not  yet  the madman  speaks
w: when told  even  who  believes
tr: Pure love is an ocean of joy; if one gets a single drop of it, that drop drowns the world. It is not fit to be told, yet the madman speaks — and when it is told, who believes it?”

## 50
w: in this way  day after day  Svarūpa and Rāmānanda  with
w: His own  feelings  makes  known
w: outwardly  poison’s burning  is  within  blissful
w: love for Kṛṣṇa  wondrous  nature
tr: In this way, day after day, He revealed His feelings to Svarūpa and Rāmānanda. Outwardly there was the burning of poison, but within was bliss: such is the wondrous nature of love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 51
w: this  love’s  tasting  hot  sugarcane  chewing
w: the mouth  burns  not  can be  given up
w: that  love  in whose  heart  its  power  he alone  knows
w: of poison and nectar  in one  meeting
tr: Tasting this love is like chewing hot sugarcane: the mouth burns, yet one cannot give it up. Only he in whose heart that love lives knows its power — poison and nectar meeting in one.

## 52
w: by its pains  of fresh  kālakūṭa poison  pungency’s  pride  banishing
w: by the outpouring  of joys  of nectar  sweetness’s  conceit  humbling
w: love  O beautiful one  for Nanda’s son  directed  awakens  in whose  heart
w: are known  clearly  its  crooked and sweet  by him  alone  powers
tr: “O beautiful one, when love for Nanda’s son awakens in someone’s heart, its pains drive out the pride that fresh *kālakūṭa* poison takes in its sting, and its outpouring of joys humbles the boast of nectar in its sweetness. Its crooked and sweet powers are clearly known to that person alone.” (Vidagdha-mādhava, 2.18)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: these are the words of Paurṇamāsī to Nāndīmukhī.

## 53
w: when  sees  Jagannātha  with Śrī Rāma and Subhadrā
w: then  thinks  I have come  to Kurukṣetra
w: fruitful  has become  life  I have seen  the lotus-eyed One
w: are soothed  body  mind  eyes
tr: When He saw Jagannātha with Śrī Balarāma and Subhadrā, He thought: “I have come to Kurukṣetra! My life has borne fruit: I have seen the lotus-eyed One, and My body, mind and eyes are soothed.”

## 54
w: of Garuḍa  in the presence  staying  takes  darśana
w: that  bliss  what  shall I tell  in words
w: of the Garuḍa pillar  at the base  is  a  low  channel
w: that  channel  filled  with tears
tr: Standing near Garuḍa, He took darśana of the Lord. What can I say of that bliss? At the base of the Garuḍa pillar there was a low channel in the floor, and it filled with His tears.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Garuḍa pillar stands before Śrī Jagannātha’s temple, at the far end of the *jagamohana*; behind it, in the floor, was a low channel, and it filled with the Lord’s tears of love.

## 55
w: from there  home  coming  the ground  on  sitting
w: with His nails  makes  on the earth  writing

tr: Restless feeling arose and His mind grew unsteady; the course of such feeling cannot be understood. His mind burned from not seeing Kṛṣṇa, and He asked Kṛṣṇa Himself how He might see Him:

## 61

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Your *śaiśava* and My *cāpala* (restlessness) are wondrous in all three worlds, and known to the two of Us alone; *viralam* — “in solitude”, or “seldom seen”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “the sweetness of Your *śaiśava*” (lit. “childhood”).

fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*): *sandhi* is the joining of two feelings, alike or different; *śābalya*, their clash with one another; *autsukya* — eagerness, *cāpalya* — restlessness, *dainya* — humility, *roṣa* — anger, *amarṣa* — indignation.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *divyonmāda* is a wondrous state of prema in the *mohana* phase of bhāva, resembling delusion.

## 65

fn: The names of this verse are explained in order in verses 67–71. *Anubhāṣya*: sole Friend of the world — sole guardian of the land of Vraja; Fickle One — He who acts as He pleases; Lover — He who gives joy to the gopīs.

## 66

tr: The signs of madness made Kṛṣṇa appear before Him, and, absorbed in feeling, He was seized by loving pique (*praṇaya-māna*). His words turned mocking, sulky and proud, with praise in the guise of blame: now He censured, now He honoured.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *solluṇṭha* — censure in words of praise. *Anubhāṣya*: *praṇaya* is love untouched even by the scent of reverent awe; *māna* is affection at its height which, to hide itself, outwardly takes on a contrary look.

## 76

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: as a guru, keeping his pupils under strict discipline, teaches them the arts, so the feelings in Mahāprabhu’s heart became His guru and made His body and mind dance in many ways.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Rāya’s drama is the *Jagannātha-vallabha-nāṭaka*; the songs are verses from the works of Caṇḍīdāsa, Vidyāpati, Rāmānanda Rāya, Bilvamaṅgala and Jayadeva.

## 78

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Purī is Śrī Paramānanda Purī; the chief rasa is madhura-rasa. *Anubhāṣya*: in Vraja Paramānanda Purī is Uddhava, and Rāmānanda is Arjuna (or Viśākhā); through these four moods the Lord accepted their worship, their company and their service, and was bound to them.

## 79

tr: Even in Līlāśuka, a mortal man, such feelings arose; what wonder is it in the Lord? Moreover, the Great One has become the abode (*āśraya*) of the chief rasa, and so every feeling rises in Him.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Līlāśuka is Śrī Bilvamaṅgala Gosvāmī, a brāhmaṇa of the South named Śilhaṇa Miśra; renouncing the world at the words of the courtesan Cintāmaṇi, he later received bhakti by the grace of Kṛṣṇa and the Vaiṣṇavas and wrote the *Kṛṣṇa-karṇāmṛta*; seeing his madness of love, people called him Līlāśuka. The chief rasa is madhura-rasa.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: see Ādi 4.230 — the greatness of Śrī Rādhā’s love, His own sweetness that Her love tastes, and the happiness She feels in it.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: like an earthly cintāmaṇi, the cintāmaṇi of prema brings forth other cintāmaṇis and yet remains whole in the Lord’s treasury; and the devotees, from the cintāmaṇi the Lord gave them, spread countless millions of them through the world.

## 82

## 84

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Svarūpa Gosvāmī set down the Lord’s last pastimes in the sūtras of his diary (*kaḍacā*) and placed them in the throat of Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī — had him learn them by heart; so the diary was never written as a separate book, and this *Caitanya-caritāmṛta* is its essence.

## 85

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: describing the Lord’s conduct as it was, the author does not seek the praise of the followers of various doctrines, and for fear of their censure he neither omits, adds, hides nor “corrects” anything.

## 86

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: for the jīva the truth of rāga is natural (*sahaja*), the way of reasoning is not; if rāga is turned towards another doctrine or the conclusions of logic, one is absorbed in them and loses impartiality, and then the nature innate in the jīva cannot be described. *Anubhāṣya*: one who gains the direction of rāga will, absorbed in it, easily grasp these descriptions; the natural reality is lived by the followers of rāga and cannot be conveyed in writing. In another reading, *rāga-dveṣa*: one who has left the service of Kṛṣṇa and is held by attachment and aversion to other things cannot describe the love for Kṛṣṇa innate in the pure soul.
