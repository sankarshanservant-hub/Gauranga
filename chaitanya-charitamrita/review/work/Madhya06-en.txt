## 0
tr: The Deliverance of Sārvabhauma

## 1
w: I bow down  to that  Gauracandra  who
w: by false arguments  whose heart was hardened
w: Sārvabhauma  the Lord of all
w: of devotion  the great abode  made
tr: I bow down to Gauracandra, the Lord of all, who turned Sārvabhauma, his heart hardened by false arguments, into a great abode of devotion.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to Gaura, who conquered Sārvabhauma”. *Anubhāṣya*: *sarva-bhūmā* — He whose greatness surpasses all that is not Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Paramātmā Gauracandra; *kutarka* — false arguments, misconceptions about the nature of the jīva and its activity; *bhakti-bhūmānam ācarat* — He made him a vessel full of pure bhakti, that is, a servant of His feet. The verse plays on the words *sārvabhauma* (“lord of all the earth”), *sarva-bhūmā* and *bhakti-bhūmā*.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

## 3
w: in ecstasy  went  the Lord  to Jagannātha’s temple
w: Jagannātha  seeing  in love  He became  restless
tr: Absorbed in ecstasy, the Lord went to the temple of Jagannātha, and when He saw Jagannātha He was overwhelmed with love.

## 4
w: Jagannātha  to embrace  He went  running
w: in the temple  He fell  with love  overwhelmed  being
tr: He ran forward to embrace Jagannātha, but, overwhelmed with love, He fell down in the temple.

## 5
w: by providence  Sārvabhauma  Him  sees
w: the temple superintendent  from striking  he  made  prevention
tr: By providence Sārvabhauma saw Him there, and he stopped the temple superintendent (*paḍichā*) from striking Him.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *paḍichā* is a temple officer, a kind of overseer; this *paḍichā* was a disciple of Sārvabhauma. Heading in the edition: “Sārvabhauma happens to see the Lord and saves Him from a blow”.

## 6
w: the Lord’s  beauty  and  of love  the transformations
w: seeing  Sārvabhauma  became  amazed  boundlessly
tr: Seeing the Lord’s beauty and the transformations of love in His body, Sārvabhauma was amazed beyond measure.

## 7
w: for a long time  consciousness  is not  of the offering  the time  came
w: Sārvabhauma  in his mind  then  a means  considered
tr: For a long time the Lord did not regain consciousness, and the time came to offer food to Jagannātha. Then Sārvabhauma considered what to do.

## 8
w: by disciples  and temple superintendents  had (Him)  carried
w: to his house  bringing  in a pure  place  he kept (Him)  lying
tr: With the help of his disciples and the temple superintendents he had Him carried to his house and laid Him down in a pure place.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Sārvabhauma then lived south of the temple, in Bālukhaṇḍa, on the bank of the Mārkaṇḍeya-saras pond; the house is now known as the Gaṅgāmātā Maṭha.

## 9
w: breathing in and out  there is not  of the abdomen  movement
w: seeing  anxious  became  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  mind
tr: There was no breath and no movement of the abdomen; seeing this, the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious.

## 10
w: fine  cotton  bringing  at the tip of the nostrils  he held
w: slightly  moves  the cotton  seeing  composure  came
tr: He brought a fine wisp of cotton and held it before the Lord’s nostrils, and when he saw the cotton stir slightly, he grew calmer.

## 11
w: sitting  the Bhaṭṭācārya  in his mind  makes  reflection
w: this  of great love for Kṛṣṇa  the sāttvika  transformation
tr: Sitting there, the Bhaṭṭācārya reflected: “This is a *sāttvika* transformation of great love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 12
w: sūddīpta  sāttvika  this  the name  which  pralaya
w: in eternally  perfect  devotees  that  sūddīpta  bhāva  occurs
tr: This swoon, called *pralaya*, is a *sūddīpta sāttvika*, the ecstatic symptom at its most blazing; such *sūddīpta bhāva* appears only in eternally perfect devotees.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* and the *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi*): the *sāttvika* symptoms may be “smouldering”, “flaming”, “burning” or “blazing” (*uddīpta*); *sūddīpta* is a special kind of the blazing, when all the *sāttvika* symptoms, multiplied many times over, reach the highest limit of prema. An eternally perfect (*nitya-siddha*) devotee is an eternal associate of the Lord.

## 13
w: adhirūḍha-mahābhāva  in whom  his  this  transformation
w: of a human being  in the body  I see  very  astonishing
tr: This transformation belongs to one who has *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva*. That I should see it in a human body is astonishing indeed!”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva* is beyond the reach even of the queens of Dvārakā — only the gopīs of Vraja experience it; its highest stage, *mādana*, is found in Śrī Rādhikā alone. Heading in the edition: “Unworldly *mahābhāva* in the Lord’s body”.

## 14
w: thus  thinking  the Bhaṭṭācārya  remains  sitting
w: Nityānanda and the others  at the Lion Gate  arrived  going
tr: While the Bhaṭṭācārya sat there thinking this, Nityānanda and the others arrived at the Lion Gate.

## 15
w: there  hearing  people  speak  among themselves  the talk
w: such a  sannyāsī  coming  seeing  Jagannātha
tr: There they heard people talking among themselves: “Such a sannyāsī came here and saw Jagannātha.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Nityānanda and His companions come from Āṭhāranālā to Purī and hear from the people that the Lord is in the Bhaṭṭācārya’s house”.

## 16
w: unconscious  He became  consciousness  not  is  in the body
w: Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house
tr: He fell unconscious and showed no sign of life, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.”

## 17
w: hearing  all  understood  this  Mahāprabhu’s  doing
w: at that time  came  there  Gopīnātha Ācārya
tr: Hearing this, they all understood that it was Mahāprabhu’s doing. Just then Gopīnātha Ācārya came there.

## 18
w: of Nadīyā  a resident  of Viśārada  the son-in-law
w: Mahāprabhu’s  devotee  he  of the Lord  the truth  knowing
tr: He was a resident of Nadīyā and the son-in-law of Viśārada, a devotee of Mahāprabhu who knew the truth about the Lord.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Maheśvara Viśārada, a fellow student of Nīlāmbara Cakravartī, lived in Vidyānagara near Samudragaḍa; his sons were Madhusūdana Vācaspati and Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma, and his son-in-law was Gopīnātha Ācārya, the husband of Sārvabhauma’s sister.

## 19
w: with Mukunda  previously  there is  acquaintance
w: Mukunda  seeing  his  became  surprise
tr: He had known Mukunda before, and he was surprised to see him there.

## 20
w: Mukunda  him  seeing  made  obeisance
w: he  embracing (him)  asks  of the Lord  the news
tr: On seeing him, Mukunda bowed down, and Gopīnātha embraced him and asked for news of the Lord.

## 21
w: Mukunda  says  of the Lord  here  has been  the coming
w: we all  have come  Mahāprabhu  with
tr: Mukunda said, “The Lord has come here, and we have all come with Mahāprabhu.”

## 22
w: to Nityānanda Gosāñi  the Ācārya  made  obeisance
w: all  meeting  he asks  of the Lord  news  again  and again
tr: The Ācārya bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi and, meeting them all, asked again and again for news of the Lord.

## 23
w: Mukunda  says  Mahāprabhu  sannyāsa  having taken
w: to Nīlācala  came  with (Him)  us all  taking
tr: Mukunda said, “After taking sannyāsa, Mahāprabhu came to Nīlācala and brought us all with Him.

## 24
w: us all  leaving  ahead  He went  for darśana
w: we all  behind  came  of Him  in search
tr: He left us and went on ahead for darśana, and we have followed in search of Him.

## 25
w: among themselves  of the people  from the mouths  what  talk  we heard
w: in Sārvabhauma’s house  the Lord  inference  we made
tr: From what we heard people saying among themselves, we have gathered that the Lord is in Sārvabhauma’s house.

## 26
w: of the Lord  at the sight  the Lord  in love  unconscious
w: Sārvabhauma  taking (Him)  went  to his own  house
tr: At the sight of Jagannātha the Lord fell unconscious in love, and Sārvabhauma took Him to his own house.

## 27
w: with you  for meeting  when  my  was  mind
w: by providence  at that  moment  I got  your  sight
tr: Just as I was wishing to meet you, by providence I saw you at that very moment.

## 28
w: come  all  let us go  Sārvabhauma’s  to the house
w: the Lord  seeing  afterwards  we shall have  of the Lord  darśana
tr: Come, let us all go to Sārvabhauma’s house. After seeing the Lord, we shall have darśana of Jagannātha.”
fn: Heading in the edition: “Love for the Lord is greater than for Jagannātha”.

## 29
w: this  hearing  Gopīnātha  all  taking
w: to Sārvabhauma’s house  went  delighted  being
tr: Hearing this, Gopīnātha joyfully took them all to Sārvabhauma’s house.

## 30
w: to Sārvabhauma’s place  going  the Lord  he saw
w: the Lord  seeing  of the Ācārya  sorrow and joy  arose
tr: When he came to Sārvabhauma’s house and saw the Lord, the Ācārya felt both sorrow and joy.

## 31
w: Sārvabhauma  informing  all  he took  inside
w: to Nityānanda Gosāñi  he  made  obeisance
tr: The Ācārya informed Sārvabhauma and brought them all inside, and Sārvabhauma bowed down to Nityānanda Gosāñi.

## 32
w: all  with  as befitting  he made  meeting
w: the Lord  seeing  of all  became  joyful  the minds
tr: He greeted each of them as was fitting, and all rejoiced at the sight of the Lord.

## 33
w: Sārvabhauma  sent  all  darśana  to have
w: Candaneśvara  his own son  he gave  of all  along with
tr: Sārvabhauma sent them all for darśana and gave them his own son, Candaneśvara, to go with them.

## 34
w: Jagannātha  seeing  of all  there was  joy
w: in ecstasy  absorbed  became  Prabhu  Nityānanda
tr: Seeing Jagannātha, everyone was filled with joy, and Nityānanda Prabhu was overcome with ecstasy.

## 35
w: all  together  holding  Him  steady  made
w: of the Lord  the servant  garland-prasāda  bringing  gave
tr: Together they held Him and steadied Him, and a servant of Jagannātha brought them His garland and prasāda.

## 36
w: prasāda  receiving  all  became  joyful  in mind
w: once again  came  all  Mahāprabhu’s  to the place
tr: Receiving the prasāda, they all rejoiced, and they came back to Mahāprabhu.

## 37
w: loudly  making  perform  all  of the name  saṁkīrtana
w: in the third  watch  came  of the Lord  consciousness
tr: They all chanted the holy name loudly together, and in the third *prahara* of the day, after noon, the Lord regained consciousness.

## 38
w: a roar  making  He rises  Hari  Hari  saying
w: in joy  Sārvabhauma  His  took  the dust of the feet
tr: With a roar He rose up, crying, “Hari! Hari!”, and Sārvabhauma joyfully took the dust of His feet.

## 39

## 40
w: in the sea  bath  having taken  the Lord  quickly  came
w: the feet  washing  the Lord  on a seat  sat
tr: The Lord bathed in the sea and quickly returned. He washed His feet and sat down on His seat.

## 41
w: much  prasāda  Sārvabhauma  had brought
w: then  Mahāprabhu  happily  eating  did
tr: Sārvabhauma had a great quantity of prasāda brought, and Mahāprabhu happily took His meal.

## 42
w: on golden plates  rice  excellent  vegetables
w: with the devotees  the Lord  does  eating
tr: Rice and excellent vegetable dishes were served on golden plates, and the Lord ate together with His devotees.

## 43
w: Sārvabhauma  serving  does  himself
w: the Lord  says  to Me  give  lāphrā vegetables
tr: Sārvabhauma served them himself. The Lord said, “Give Me the *lāphrā*, the plain mixed vegetables,
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *lāphrā-vyañjana* is a vegetable dish in which various vegetables are stewed together and seasoned with cumin, pepper and mustard.

## 44
w: cakes and sweet drinks  give  you  to all these
w: to all  the Bhaṭṭācārya  says  joining  two  hands
tr: and give the cakes and sweet drinks to all of them.” With folded hands the Bhaṭṭācārya said to them all:

## 45
w: Jagannātha  as  has done  eating
w: today  all  mahā-prasāda  do  tasting
tr: Today you must all relish the mahā-prasāda, just as Jagannātha has eaten it.”

## 46
w: so  saying  cakes and sweet drinks  all  he fed
w: the meal  having given  rinsing of the mouth  he had them do
tr: Saying this, he fed them all the cakes and sweet drinks, and when they had eaten he gave them water to rinse their mouths.

## 47
w: permission  asking  Gopīnātha  Ācārya  taking
w: of the Lord  near  came  eating  having done
tr: Then, taking leave, he went with Gopīnātha Ācārya to eat, and after eating he came back to the Lord.

## 48
w: namo nārāyaṇāya  saying  obeisance  he made
w: on Kṛṣṇa  the mind  may be  saying  Gosāñi  spoke
tr: Saying, “*Namo nārāyaṇāya*”, he bowed down to the Lord, and Gosāñi replied, “*Kṛṣṇe matir astu* — may your mind be fixed on Kṛṣṇa!”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: it is customary to greet sannyāsīs with the words *oṁ namo nārāyaṇāya*. The smṛti rule for sannyāsīs is to give neither blessings nor obeisances, but a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī knows himself to be a servant of Kṛṣṇa and so gives everyone the compassionate blessing, “May your mind be fixed on Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet.”

## 49
w: hearing  Sārvabhauma  in his mind  consideration  made
w: a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī  He  by His words  he knew
tr: Hearing this, Sārvabhauma reflected, and from His words he understood that He was a Vaiṣṇava sannyāsī.

## 50
w: to Gopīnātha  Ācārya  says  Sārvabhauma
w: of Gosāñi  to know  I want  what  the former āśrama
tr: Sārvabhauma said to Gopīnātha Ācārya, “I would like to know about Gosāñi’s life before He took sannyāsa.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *pūrvāśrama* — the name by which He was known and the place where He lived as a householder, before taking sannyāsa.

## 51
w: Gopīnātha Ācārya  says  in Navadvīpa  home
w: Jagannātha  name  title  Miśra Purandara
tr: Gopīnātha Ācārya said, “His home is in Navadvīpa. There lived a man named Jagannātha, whose title was Miśra Purandara.

w: of Viśārada  a fellow student  this  his  fame

tr: Sārvabhauma was glad to find this connection with Nadīyā, and in his pleasure he began to speak to Gosāñi.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: by the excellence of Your nature You are worthy of my worship, and by Your outward āśrama, as a sannyāsī, of the worship of householders like us; so I am Your servant.
