## 1
w: I bow down  to that  Gauracandra  who
w: by false arguments  whose heart was hardened
w: Sārvabhauma  the Lord of all
w: of devotion  the great abode  made
tr: I bow down to Gauracandra, the Lord of all, who turned Sārvabhauma, his heart hardened by false arguments, into a great abode of devotion.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Obeisance to Gaura, who conquered Sārvabhauma”. *Anubhāṣya*: *sarva-bhūmā* — He whose greatness surpasses all that is not Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Paramātmā Gauracandra; *kutarka* — false arguments, misconceptions about the nature of the jīva and its activity; *bhakti-bhūmānam ācarat* — He made him a vessel full of pure bhakti, that is, a servant of His feet. The verse plays on the words *sārvabhauma* (“lord of all the earth”), *sarva-bhūmā* and *bhakti-bhūmā*.

fn: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the *paḍichā* is a temple officer, a kind of overseer; this *paḍichā* was a disciple of Sārvabhauma. Heading in the edition: “Sārvabhauma happens to see the Lord and saves Him from a blow”.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Sārvabhauma then lived south of the temple, in Bālukhaṇḍa, on the bank of the Mārkaṇḍeya-saras pond; the house is now known as the Gaṅgāmātā Maṭha.

## 12
w: sūddīpta  sāttvika  this  the name  which  pralaya
w: in eternally  perfect  devotees  that  sūddīpta  bhāva  occurs
tr: This swoon, called *pralaya*, is a *sūddīpta sāttvika*, the ecstatic symptom at its most blazing; such *sūddīpta bhāva* appears only in eternally perfect devotees.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* and the *Ujjvala-nīlamaṇi*): the *sāttvika* symptoms may be “smouldering”, “flaming”, “burning” or “blazing” (*uddīpta*); *sūddīpta* is a special kind of the blazing, when all the *sāttvika* symptoms, multiplied many times over, reach the highest limit of prema. An eternally perfect (*nitya-siddha*) devotee is an eternal associate of the Lord.

## 13

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *adhirūḍha-mahābhāva* is beyond the reach even of the queens of Dvārakā — only the gopīs of Vraja experience it; its highest stage, *mādana*, is found in Śrī Rādhikā alone. Heading in the edition: “Unworldly *mahābhāva* in the Lord’s body”.
