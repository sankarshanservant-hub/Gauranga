## 50

## 52

## 57

w: by the ways of darkness and passion  of Kṛṣṇa  not  one obtains  the essence
tr: Giving up the body and all such acts are of the nature of darkness (*tamo-dharma*); through the ways of darkness and passion one cannot grasp the essence of Kṛṣṇa.

## 58
w: ‘devotion’  without  for Kṛṣṇa  ever  not  ‘love’s awakening’
w: love  without  attainment of Kṛṣṇa  anything else  from  is not
tr: Without *bhakti*, love for Kṛṣṇa never awakens; and without love, Kṛṣṇa is not attained by any other means.

## 59

w: as  devotion  to Me  intense

## 60

w: the practitioner  not  obtains  by that  of Kṛṣṇa  the feet
tr: Giving up the body and the like are of the nature of darkness and a cause of sin; by them the *sādhaka* does not attain the feet of Kṛṣṇa.

## 61

## 62

tr: Separation in deep *anurāga* cannot be borne; therefore one who is full of *anurāga* longs for his own death.

## 63

w: I shall give up  my life  by vows  emaciated  through a hundred births  it may be

If I do not obtain Your grace, I shall give up my life, emaciated by vows, so that, even after a hundred births, it may be mine.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.52.43)

## 64
w: sprinkle  O dear one  our  Your  lips’  nectar  with the flood

tr: (The *gopīs* said:) “O dear one, with the flood of nectar from Your lips quench the fire of love’s desire that Your smiling glances and sweet song have kindled in our hearts. Otherwise, O friend, our bodies consumed by the fire of separation, we shall reach the place of Your feet by meditation.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.35)

tr: One of low birth is not unfit for the worship of Kṛṣṇa, nor is a *brāhmaṇa* of good family fit for it by that alone.

## 68

Such a devotee purifies his whole family; the brāhmaṇa swollen with pride cannot.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.10)

## 71

## 84

## 89

## 94
w: your  body  calls  the Lord  ‘My  own property’
w: like you  fortunate  there is not  any  person
tr: The Lord calls your body ‘My own property’; no one is as fortunate as you.

## 95
w: by His own body  which  work  not  He can  to do
w: that  work  He will have done  by you  and that  in Mathurā
tr: The work He cannot do in His own body He will have done through you — and in Mathurā.

## 96
w: what  to have done  wishes  the Lord  that  accomplished  is
w: your  good fortune  this  I have told  for certain
tr: Whatever the Lord wishes to have done is accomplished. This is your good fortune, I tell you for certain.

## 97
w: the conclusions of devotion  of scripture  conduct  the determination
w: through you  He will have done  I have understood  His intention
tr: The conclusions of *bhakti* and the determination of conduct according to scripture He will have done through you — I have understood His intention.

## 98
w: my  this  body  of the Lord  in the work  not  was used
w: in the land of Bhārata  born  this  body  futile  has become
tr: But this body of mine has not been used in the Lord’s work. Born in the land of Bhārata, this body has been in vain!”

## 99
w: Sanātana  says  like you  who  is there  other
w: of Mahāprabhu  among the associates  you  most fortunate
tr: Sanātana said, “Who else is there like you? Among Mahāprabhu’s associates you are the most fortunate!

## 100
w: of the descent  the mission  of the Lord  in spreading the Name
w: that  own  work  the Lord  does  you  through
tr: The mission of the Lord’s descent is to spread the Name, and that very work of His the Lord does through you.

## 101
w: daily  you do  three hundred thousand  chanting of the Name
w: of all  before  you do  of the Name  the glory  telling
tr: Every day you chant three hundred thousand names, and before everyone you tell the glory of the Name.

## 102
w: himself  practises  someone  not  does  preaching
w: preaching  does  someone  not  does  practice
tr: Some practise themselves but do not preach; others preach but do not practise.

## 103
w: ‘practice’  ‘preaching’  of the Name  you do  ‘both’  works
w: you  the guru of all  you  of the world  the noble one
tr: You do both works for the Name — practice (*ācāra*) and preaching (*pracāra*). You are the guru of all; you are the noblest in the world.”

## 104
w: in this way  the two  in the joy of many talks
w: talks of Kṛṣṇa  relish  staying  together
tr: In this way the two of them lived together, relishing talks of Kṛṣṇa in the joy of many conversations.

## 105
w: at festival time  came  all  of Gauḍa  the devotees
w: as before  made  all  of the Ratha-yātrā  seeing
tr: At the time of the festival all the devotees of Gauḍa came, and as before they all saw the Ratha-yātrā.

## 106
w: before the chariot  the Lord  likewise  made  dancing
w: seeing  astonished  became  of Sanātana  the mind
tr: Before the chariot the Lord danced as before, and seeing it, Sanātana was filled with wonder.

## 107
w: of the rains  the four months  stayed  all  His own devotees
w: with all  the Lord  made meet  Sanātana
tr: All His devotees stayed for the four months of the rainy season, and the Lord introduced Sanātana to them all.

## 108–110
w: Advaita  Nityānanda  Śrīvāsa  Vakreśvara
w: Vāsudeva  Murāri  Rāghava  Dāmodara
w: (Paramānanda) Purī  (Brahmānanda) Bhāratī  Svarūpa  Paṇḍita Gadādhara
w: Sārvabhauma  Rāmānanda  Jagadānanda  Śaṅkara
w: Kāśīśvara  Govinda and others  all  the devotees
w: with all  of Sanātana  He brought about  the meeting
tr: He brought Sanātana to meet Advaita, Nityānanda, Śrīvāsa and Vakreśvara; Vāsudeva, Murāri, Rāghava and Dāmodara; (Paramānanda) Purī, (Brahmānanda) Bhāratī, Svarūpa and Gadādhara Paṇḍita; Sārvabhauma, Rāmānanda, Jagadānanda and Śaṅkara; Kāśīśvara, Govinda and all the other devotees.

## 111
w: as befitting  He had (him) make  of all  of the feet  worship
w: him  He made  of all  of the mercy  the recipient
tr: He had him bow down at the feet of each of them as befitted each, and made him a recipient of everyone’s mercy.

## 112
w: by his virtues  by his learning  of all  dear  Sanātana
w: as befitting  of mercy  friendship  respect  the recipient
tr: By his virtues and learning Sanātana became dear to all, and received from each, as befitted each, mercy, friendship or respect.

## 113
w: all  the Vaiṣṇavas  when  to Gauḍadeśa  went
w: Sanātana  of Mahāprabhu  at the feet  remained
tr: When all the Vaiṣṇavas went back to Gauḍa, Sanātana remained at the feet of Mahāprabhu.

## 114
w: the Dola-yātrā and so on  of the Lord  in the company  he saw
w: day by day  in the Lord’s company  joy  increased
tr: In the Lord’s company he saw the Dola-yātrā and other festivals, and day by day, in the Lord’s company, his joy increased.

## 115
w: earlier  in the month of Vaiśākha  Sanātana  when  came
w: in the month of Jyaiṣṭha  the Lord  him  test  made
tr: Sanātana had come in the month of Vaiśākha (April–May); in the month of Jyaiṣṭha (May–June) the Lord put him to a test.

## 116
w: in the month of Jyaiṣṭha  the Lord  to Yameśvara-ṭoṭā  came
w: at the devotees’ request  there  His meal  indeed  took
tr: In the month of Jyaiṣṭha the Lord came to Yameśvara-ṭoṭā and, at the request of His devotees, took His meal there.

## 117
w: at the time of the midday meal  Sanātana  He had called
w: the Lord  has called  his  joy  increased
tr: At the time of the midday meal He sent for Sanātana; and since the Lord had called him, Sanātana’s joy increased.

## 118
w: at midday  the sea sand  has become  like fire
w: by that path  Sanātana  made  going
tr: At midday the sand by the sea had become like fire, yet Sanātana went by that path.

## 119
w: the Lord  has called  in this  joyful  mind
w: on the hot sand  the feet  burn  that  not  he knows
tr: His mind was so joyful that the Lord had called him that he did not notice his feet burning on the hot sand.

## 120
w: on both  feet  blisters  appeared  still  he went  to the Lord’s place
w: the meal  having taken  Mahāprabhu  was  resting
tr: Blisters rose on both his feet, yet he went on to the Lord. Mahāprabhu, having taken His meal, was resting.
