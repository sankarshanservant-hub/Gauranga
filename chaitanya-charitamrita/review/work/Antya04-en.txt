fn: *Gaṅgā-prāpti*, “attaining the Gaṅgā”: departing from the world on the bank of the Gaṅgā (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*, summary of the chapter).

## 39

tr: ‘I have sold my head at the lotus feet of Raghunātha. I cannot take my head back; it gives me great pain.

## 41
w: mercy  doing  to me  permission  give  you two
w: birth  after birth  may I serve  of Raghunātha  the feet
tr: Be merciful, both of you, and give me leave to serve the feet of Raghunātha birth after birth.

## 42
w: of Raghunātha  the lotus feet  to give up  not  is possible
w: of giving up  the thought  when it comes  my life  bursting  goes
tr: I cannot give up the lotus feet of Raghunātha; at the very thought of giving them up, my heart breaks.’

## 43
w: then  we two  him  embrace  made

tr: Then the two of us embraced him and praised him, saying, ‘Excellent! Your devotion is firm.’

## 44

w: all  auspiciousness  in it  are cut away  all  troubles
tr: The family upon which there is even a trace of Your mercy has every blessing, and all its troubles are cut away.”

## 45
w: the Lord  says  in this way  Murāri Gupta
w: before  I  tested  his  this  way
tr: The Lord said, “In the same way I once tested Murāri Gupta; this was his way too.

## 46
w: that  devotee  blessed  who  not  leaves  of the master  the feet
w: that  master  blessed  who  not  leaves  his own people
tr: Blessed is the devotee who does not leave the feet of his master, and blessed is the master who does not abandon his own.

## 47
w: by misfortune  a servant  if  goes  to another place
w: that  master  blessed  him  by the hair  seizing  brings back
tr: And if by misfortune a servant goes elsewhere, blessed is the master who seizes him by the hair and brings him back.

## 48
w: good  it was  your  here  took place  coming
w: in this  house  stay  here  Haridāsa  with

## 49

w: Kṛṣṇa’s rasa  relishing  do  take  Kṛṣṇa’s name
tr: You are both supremely eminent in the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa. Relish the sweetness of Kṛṣṇa and chant His name.”

## 50
w: so  saying  Mahāprabhu  rising  departed
w: through Govinda  to both  prasāda  He sent
tr: Having said this, Mahāprabhu rose and left, and He sent prasāda to both of them through Govinda.

## 51
w: in this way  Sanātana  stays  near the Lord
w: of Jagannātha  the wheel  seeing  makes  obeisance
tr: Thus Sanātana stayed near the Lord. Seeing the wheel (the Nīla-cakra) atop Jagannātha’s temple, he would bow down.

## 52
w: the Lord  coming  every day  meets  the two
w: intimate discussion  talks of Kṛṣṇa  speaks  for some time
tr: Every day the Lord came and met them both, and for a while He spoke intimately with them about Kṛṣṇa.

## 53
w: divine  prasāda  receiving  daily  in Jagannātha’s temple
w: that  bringing  daily  without fail  gives  to both
tr: Every day He received the divine prasāda in the temple of Jagannātha, and every day without fail He brought it and gave it to them both.

## 54
w: one day  coming  the Lord  the two  met
w: to Sanātana  suddenly  to speak  began
tr: One day the Lord came and met them both, and suddenly began to speak to Sanātana:

## 55

w: millions of bodies  in a moment  then  to give up  one could
tr: “Sanātana, if Kṛṣṇa could be attained by giving up the body, I would give up millions of bodies in a moment.
fn: *Deha-tyāga* — “giving up the body”. Edition heading to 55: addressing Sanātana, the Lord teaches the sādhaka who is not yet free of anarthas and is led by his own mind: false (*phalgu*) knowledge and false renunciation are no means of attaining Kṛṣṇa.

## 56
w: by giving up the body  Kṛṣṇa  not  one obtains  one obtains  by worship
w: of attaining Kṛṣṇa  means  any  there is not  ‘devotion’  without
tr: One does not attain Kṛṣṇa by giving up the body; one attains Him by worship. There is no means of attaining Kṛṣṇa except bhakti.

## 57
w: giving up the body and the like  whatever  all  the nature of darkness
w: by the ways of darkness and passion  of Kṛṣṇa  not  one obtains  the essence
tr: Giving up the body and all such acts are of the nature of darkness (*tamo-dharma*); through the ways of darkness and passion one cannot grasp the essence of Kṛṣṇa.

## 58
w: ‘devotion’  without  for Kṛṣṇa  ever  not  ‘love’s awakening’
w: love  without  attainment of Kṛṣṇa  anything else  from  is not
tr: Without bhakti, love for Kṛṣṇa never awakens; and without love, Kṛṣṇa is not attained by any other means.

## 59
w: not  wins  Me  yoga
w: not  sāṅkhya  dharma  O Uddhava
w: not  Vedic study  austerity  renunciation
w: as  devotion  to Me  intense
tr: (Śrī Kṛṣṇa said:) “O Uddhava, neither yoga nor sāṅkhya, neither dharma nor study of the Vedas, neither austerity nor renunciation can win Me as intense devotion to Me does.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.14.20)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 17.76 (= Madhya 20.137); translation as there. The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 17.76.

## 60
w: giving up the body and the like  the nature of darkness  the cause of sin
w: the practitioner  not  obtains  by that  of Kṛṣṇa  the feet
tr: Giving up the body and the like are of the nature of darkness and a cause of sin; by them the *sādhaka* does not attain the feet of Kṛṣṇa.

## 61
w: a loving  devotee  in separation  wishes  the body  to give up
w: by love  Kṛṣṇa  is met  he  not  can  die
tr: A loving devotee in separation wishes to give up his body; but by that very love he meets Kṛṣṇa, and he cannot die.

## 62
w: of deep attachment  the separation  not  can be  borne
w: therefore  the one full of attachment  desires  his own  death
tr: Separation in deep *anurāga* cannot be borne; therefore one who is full of *anurāga* longs for his own death.

## 63
w: whose  feet  lotus  in the dust  bathing  the great souls
w: desire  Umā’s husband (Śiva)  like  their own  darkness  for dispelling
w: if  O lotus-eyed one  not  I obtain  Your  grace
w: I shall give up  my life  by vows  emaciated  through a hundred births  it may be
tr: (Rukmiṇī wrote to Kṛṣṇa:) “O lotus-eyed one, great souls like Umā’s lord (Śiva) long to bathe in the dust of Your lotus feet to dispel their own darkness. If I do not obtain Your grace, I shall give up my life, emaciated by vows, so that, even after a hundred births, it may be mine.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.52.43)
fn: Edition heading: “Rukmiṇī reveals her anurāga to Vāsudeva.” *Anubhāṣya*: *vrata-kṛśān* — emaciated by vows, by fasts and the like.

## 64
w: sprinkle  O dear one  our  Your  lips’  nectar  with the flood
w: smiling glance  sweet  song  born of  love’s desire  the fire
w: not  if  we  by the fire born of separation  consumed  bodies
w: by meditation  we shall go  of the feet  to the place  O friend  Your
tr: (The gopīs said:) “O dear one, with the flood of nectar from Your lips quench the fire of love’s desire that Your smiling glances and sweet song have kindled in our hearts. Otherwise, O friend, our bodies consumed by the fire of separation, we shall reach the place of Your feet by meditation.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.29.35)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *hṛc-chaya* — the desire of love born in the heart of the gopīs and dwelling there; *kala-gīta* — the sweet song, the sweet sound of the flute.

## 65
w: wicked intelligence  giving up  do  hearing and chanting
w: soon  you will obtain  then  the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa
tr: Give up this wicked intelligence (*kubuddhi*) and engage in hearing and chanting; then you will soon obtain the treasure of love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 66
w: one of low birth  is not  for the worship of Kṛṣṇa  unfit
w: a brāhmaṇa of good family  is not  for worship  fit
tr: One of low birth is not unfit for the worship of Kṛṣṇa, nor is a brāhmaṇa of good family fit for it by that alone.
fn: Edition heading to 66: refutation of the doctrine of nobility by seed, born of union with a woman; who is fit for the service of Kṛṣṇa; only the pure devotee is the guru, the greatest of men.

## 67
w: whoever  worships  he  is great  the non-devotee  low  contemptible
w: in the worship of Kṛṣṇa  there is not  caste family and the like  consideration
tr: Whoever worships is great; the non-devotee is low and contemptible. In the worship of Kṛṣṇa there is no consideration of caste, family and the like.

## 68
w: to the lowly  greater  kindness  shows  Bhagavān
w: the high-born  the learned  the rich  great  pride
tr: Bhagavān shows greater kindness to the lowly; the high-born, the learned and the rich have great pride.

## 69
w: than a brāhmaṇa  twelve  qualities  endowed with  of the lotus-naveled Lord
w: from the lotus feet  averse  a dog-eater  better
w: I consider  to Him offered  mind  words  deeds  wealth
w: and life  purifies  he  his family  not  but  one full of pride
tr: “I consider a dog-eater whose mind, words, deeds, wealth and very life are offered to the Lord of the lotus navel better than a brāhmaṇa endowed with the twelve qualities who has turned away from His lotus feet. Such a devotee purifies his whole family; the brāhmaṇa swollen with pride cannot.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.9.10)
fn: The same verse as Madhya 20.59; translation as there. The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Madhya 20.59.

## 70
w: of the forms of worship  among  the best  ninefold  devotion
w: ‘love for Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  to give  holds  great power
tr: Among the forms of worship the best is the ninefold bhakti: it has great power to bestow love for Kṛṣṇa and Kṛṣṇa Himself.

## 71
w: of them  among  the best of all  the chanting of the name
w: without offense  the name  if one takes  obtains  the treasure of love
tr: Of these the best of all is *nāma-saṅkīrtana*. One who chants the name without offence obtains the treasure of love.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *nirāparādhe* — free of the ten offences against the name, serving the name constantly and untiringly.

## 72
w: this  hearing  of Sanātana  there was  astonishment
w: to the Lord  not  seems fit  my  resolve to die
tr: Hearing this, Sanātana was astonished: “My resolve to give up my body does not please the Lord.

## 73
w: the omniscient  Mahāprabhu  has forbidden  me
w: of the Lord  the feet  clasping  he says  to Him
tr: The all-knowing Mahāprabhu has forbidden me.” Clasping the Lord’s feet, he said to Him:

## 74
w: omniscient  merciful  You  the Lord  independent
w: as  You make dance  so  I dance  like  a wooden puppet
tr: “You are all-knowing and merciful — the independent Lord. As You make me dance, so I dance, like a wooden puppet.

## 75
w: low  fallen  wretched  I  wretched by nature
w: me  if You keep alive  Your  what  will be  gain
tr: I am low, fallen and wretched, wretched by nature. What will You gain by keeping me alive?”

## 76
w: the Lord  says  your  body  My  own property
w: you  to Me  have made  self-surrender
tr: The Lord said, “Your body is My own property, for you have surrendered yourself to Me.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: you have given Me your body, and you have no right to give it up.

## 77
w: another’s  property  you  why  wish  to destroy
w: right and wrong  discrimination  is it that  not  you can  make
tr: Why do you wish to destroy another’s property? Can you not discern right from wrong?

## 78
w: your  body  My  chief  ‘instrument’
w: by this  body  shall accomplish  I  many  purposes
tr: Your body is My chief instrument (*sādhana*); through this body I shall accomplish many purposes:

## 79
w: of the devotee  devotion  love for Kṛṣṇa  of the truths  the establishing
w: of the Vaiṣṇava  the duties  and  Vaiṣṇava conduct
tr: establishing the truths about the devotee, devotion and love for Kṛṣṇa; the duties of a Vaiṣṇava, and Vaiṣṇava conduct;

## 80
w: devotion to Kṛṣṇa  of service in love for Kṛṣṇa  the introduction
w: of lost holy places  the recovery  and  of renunciation  teaching
tr: introducing devotion to Kṛṣṇa and service in love for Kṛṣṇa; recovering the lost holy places, and teaching renunciation.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 79–81): through Sanātana the Lord established the service of the Deity in maṭhas and temples and the inner service in the mood of Vraja, and by his recovering the lost holy places and by his life He taught the renunciation befitting a devotee (*yukta-vairāgya*).

## 81
w: own  dear  place  My  Mathurā-Vṛndāvana
w: there  these  dharmas  I wish  to make  propagation
tr: My own dear abode is Mathurā-Vṛndāvana; there I wish to propagate all these dharmas.

## 82
w: of My mother  by the order  I  reside  in Nīlācala
w: there  ‘dharma’  to teach  not  by My own strength
tr: By My mother’s order I reside in Nīlācala, and it is not within My own power to teach this dharma there.

## 83
w: these  all  works  I  by which  body  shall do
w: that  to give up  wish  you  how  shall I bear
tr: You wish to give up the very body through which I shall do all these works! How can I bear it?”

## 84
