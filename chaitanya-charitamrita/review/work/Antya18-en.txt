## 85
w: began  water  play
w: at one another  water  throwing back and forth
w: tumult  rain  water  streams
w: for all  victory  defeat
w: not  any  certainty

tr: The water play began. They splashed one another; there was a tumult, and water rained down in streams. No one could tell who was winning and who was losing, and the water battle grew boundless.

## 86
w: rain  steady  lightning flashes
w: sprinkles  dark  new cloud
w: the cloud  rains  the lightning  upon
w: of the sakhīs  the eyes

tr: The steady flashes of lightning rained water on the dark new cloud, and the cloud rained upon the lightning. The eyes of the sakhīs, like thirsty *cātakī* birds, drank that nectar with joy.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the gopīs, like steady flashes of lightning, showered water on Kṛṣṇa, dark as a new cloud, and the cloud — Kṛṣṇa — showered water back upon the lightning.

## 87
w: first  battle  water against water
w: then  battle  hand against hand
w: after that  battle  face to face
w: then  battle  heart to heart
w: then  there was  word against word
w: then  there was  battle  nail against nail
tr: First they fought water against water, then hand against hand, after that face to face, then breast to breast; then came a battle of words, and then a battle of nails.
fn: The *Anubhāṣya* gives a variant reading: *radā-radi* (“tooth against tooth”) for *hṛdā-hṛdi* (“breast to breast”).

## 88

## 90
