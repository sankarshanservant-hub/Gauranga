fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the sun is the lotus’s friend, for at sunrise the lotus opens and the *cakravākas* are reunited; but at sunrise the *utpala* closes, so the sun is its enemy, and the *cakravāka* is a stranger to it, since it blooms at night.
