## 1

tr: Once, seeing the ocean in the light of the autumn moon, Śrī Śacīnandana took it for the Yamunā and ran towards it. He plunged into its waters as if into an ocean of the burning pain of separation from Hari, and lay there unconscious the whole night; at dawn His own devotees found Him. May that son of Śacī protect us here.
fn: Heading in the edition: a prayer for the mercy of the Lord who, in separation from Kṛṣṇa, drifts on the ocean, having taken it for the Yamunā. *Anubhāṣya*: “His own” — His most intimate devotees.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: joy in the pastimes of union (*sambhoga*) of Kṛṣṇa, sorrow in the pastimes of separation (*vipralambha*) of the gopīs.

## 10

fn: Heading in the edition: to understand the love of the gopīs and to know the measure of its taste, Kṛṣṇa accepts the feeling of a gopī.

## 25

Bees followed Him, singing like the lords of the Gandharvas, drawn by His garland, crushed by the contact of their bodies and reddened with the *kuṅkuma* from their breasts.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.33.22)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: “having broken the dikes” (*bhinna-setuḥ*) is said both of the king of elephants and of Kṛṣṇa — Bhagavān Himself, who passes beyond the bounds of worldly convention; the bees sing like the lords of the Gandharvas (*gandharva-pāḥ*).
