## 0
tr: The Cleansing of the Guṇḍicā Temple

## 1
w: of Śrī Guṇḍicā  the temple  with His own  people
w: sweeping  by washing  He  Gaura
w: like His own heart  cool  bright  and
w: for Kṛṣṇa’s sitting  fit  made
tr: Together with His own devotees, Gaura swept and washed the temple of Śrī Guṇḍicā. He made it cool and radiant like His own heart, a fit place for Kṛṣṇa to be seated.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Gaurasundara cleans the Guṇḍicā.” *Anubhāṣya*: “cool” (*śītalam*) means free from the threefold miseries born of the fire of the desire to enjoy; the temple became fit for Kṛṣṇa to be seated there.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Gauracandra  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Madhya 4.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

## 3
w: glory  glory  to Śrīvāsa and the others  Gaura’s devotees
w: strength  give  I may do  so that  of Caitanya  the description
tr: Glory, glory to Śrīvāsa and all the devotees of Gaura! Grant me the strength to describe Śrī Caitanya.
fn: The first line is the same as Ādi 9.3; translation as there.

## 4
w: earlier  the South  from  the Lord  when  came
w: Him  to meet  the Gajapati  eager  became
tr: Earlier, when the Lord returned from the South, the Gajapati king grew eager to meet Him.

## 5
w: Kaṭaka  from  a letter  gave  to Sārvabhauma
w: of the Lord  permission  is  if  to see (Him)  I shall go
tr: From Kaṭaka he sent a letter to Sārvabhauma: “If the Lord permits, I shall come to see Him.”

## 6
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  wrote  of the Lord  permission  not  was
w: again  the king  to him  a letter  sent
tr: The Bhaṭṭācārya wrote back that the Lord had not given permission. Then the king sent him another letter:

## 7
w: of the Lord  near  are  all the  devotees
w: me  for the sake of  to all of them  make  a petition
tr: “Please petition, on my behalf, all the devotees who are with the Lord.

## 8
w: those  all  merciful ones  to me  becoming  kind
w: me  for the sake of  at the Lord’s feet  will make  humble entreaty
tr: May all those merciful souls be kind to me and humbly plead for me at the Lord’s feet.

## 9
w: of all of them  by the grace  one attains  Śrī  of the Lord  the feet
w: the Lord’s mercy  without  my  kingdom  not  pleases
tr: By their grace one reaches the feet of the Lord. Without the Lord’s mercy, my kingdom gives me no joy.

## 10
w: if  to me  mercy  not  will show  Gaurahari
w: the kingdom  leaving  a yogī  becoming  I shall become  a beggar
tr: If Gaurahari will not be merciful to me, I shall give up my kingdom, become a yogī and live as a beggar.”

## 11
w: the Bhaṭṭācārya  the letter  seeing  anxious  becoming
w: to the devotees  went  that  letter  taking
tr: Seeing the letter, the Bhaṭṭācārya grew anxious, and he took it to the devotees.

## 12
w: all  meeting  he told  the king’s  account
w: afterwards  that  letter  to all  caused  to see
tr: Meeting them all, he told them about the king, and then he showed everyone the letter.

## 13
w: the letter  seeing  of all  in the mind  arose  wonder
w: to the Lord’s feet  of the Gajapati  so much  devotion  there is
tr: When they read the letter, everyone was amazed: “What devotion the Gajapati has for the Lord’s feet!”

## 14
w: all  say  the Lord  him  ever  not  will meet
w: we all  speak  if  pain  He  will feel
tr: They all said, “The Lord will never meet him. And if we ask Him, He will be pained.”

## 15
w: Sārvabhauma  says  all  go  once
w: to meet  not  we shall say  we shall tell  the king’s conduct
tr: Sārvabhauma said, “Let us all go to Him, just this once. We will not ask Him to meet the king; we will only tell Him how the king is behaving.”
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: we will tell Mahāprabhu that the king behaves like a true Vaiṣṇava, but we will not ask Him to grant the king His darśana.

## 16
w: so  saying  all  went  of Mahāprabhu  to the place
w: to speak  eager  all  not  say  in words
tr: Saying this, they all went to Mahāprabhu. Everyone was eager to speak, yet no one said a word.

## 17
w: the Lord  says  what  to say  of all  the coming
w: I see  to say  you want  not  you speak  what  reason
tr: The Lord said, “What have you all come to tell Me? I see that you want to say something, yet you do not speak. Why?”

## 18
w: Nityānanda  says  to You  we want  to submit
w: not  having spoken  to remain  we cannot  to speak  fear  in the heart
tr: Nityānanda said, “We want to put something before You. We cannot keep silent, yet our hearts are afraid to speak.

## 19
w: proper or improper  to You  all  we want  to submit
w: You  not  if he meets  the king  wants  a yogī  to become
tr: Proper or improper, we want to tell You everything: if he cannot meet You, the king wants to become a yogī.

## 20
w: in the ears  the earrings  taking  I  shall become  a beggar
w: royal enjoyment  is not  in the heart  without  Gaurahari
tr: He says, ‘I will put the yogī’s rings in my ears and become a beggar. Without Gaurahari, my heart finds no joy in royal pleasures.
fn: Verses 20–21 are the king’s own words (first person in the Bengali: *muñi*, “I”), which Nityānanda repeats. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in the western regions such yogīs are called *kānphaṭā* (“split-eared”) yogīs; they wear a sign made of conch shell in their ears.

## 21
w: I shall see  that  moonlike face  the eyes  filling
w: I shall hold  those  lotus feet  to the heart  lifting
tr: I shall gaze on that moonlike face to my eyes’ fill, and I shall lift those lotus feet and hold them to my heart.’”

## 22
w: although  hearing  of the Lord  soft  became  the heart
w: still  outwardly  He speaks  harsh  words
tr: Though the Lord’s heart softened when He heard this, outwardly He spoke harsh words:
fn: Heading in the edition: “Harsh words of the Lord, faithful to the duty of a sannyāsī, as befits an ācārya.”

## 23
w: of you all  the desire  this  Me  taking
w: the king  make meet  now  to Kaṭaka  going
tr: “You all want to take Me to Kaṭaka and have Me meet the king.

## 24
w: spiritual good  let it be  people  will make  blame
w: people  let them be  Dāmodara  will make  rebuke
tr: Quite apart from the spiritual harm, people will condemn Me; and quite apart from the people, Dāmodara will scold Me.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: from the standpoint of the highest goal a sannyāsī incurs fault by meeting a king; and he must avoid the censure of people because his task is to preach dharma, and the preaching of one who is censured does not go well.

## 25
w: of you all  at the bidding  I  not  meet  the king
w: Dāmodara  says  when  I meet  then  him
tr: I will not meet the king at the bidding of all of you. When Dāmodara tells Me to, then I shall meet him.”
fn: Heading in the edition: “Under the guise of respect, a jibe at Dāmodara’s judging what is not his to judge.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord is won by Dāmodara’s devotion, yet his verbal chastisements are often unfitting toward the Lord; by these words the Lord lets it be understood that Dāmodara will have to give up that habit.

## 26
w: Dāmodara  says  You  independent  the Lord
w: what should and should not be done  all  to You  known
tr: Dāmodara said, “You are the independent Lord. You know everything — what should be done and what should not.

## 27
w: I  what  insignificant soul  to You  rule  shall give
w: Yourself  You will meet  him  that also  I shall see
tr: What tiny soul am I, to give You rules? You will meet him of Your own accord, and that too I shall see.

## 28
w: the king  You  affection  makes  You  controlled by affection
w: his  by the affection  will cause  him  Your  touch
tr: The king holds You in affection, and You are ruled by affection. His affection will win him Your touch.

## 29
w: although  the Lord  You  supremely independent
w: still  by nature  You are  subject to love
tr: Though You are the Lord and supremely independent, by Your very nature You are subject to love.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: You are Īśvara and so are bound to no one; yet by Your own nature You are subject only to the love of Your exclusive (*aikāntika*) devotees.

## 30
w: Nityānanda  says  such  is  what  person
w: who  to You  says  ‘make  the meeting with the king’
tr: Nityānanda said, “Who is there who would tell You, ‘Go and meet the king’?
fn: Heading in the edition: “Agreeing with the Lord, Nityānanda pleads for the king’s love.”

## 31
w: but  of a loving  person  the nature  one  is
w: the beloved  not  if he gets  his own life  he  gives up
tr: But one who loves has this nature: if he cannot reach the one he longs for, he gives up his life.

## 32
w: of the sacrificing brāhmaṇas the wives  all  of that  the proof
w: Kṛṣṇa  for the sake of  before the husband  gave up  life
tr: The wives of the brāhmaṇas who performed sacrifice are proof of this: for Kṛṣṇa’s sake, one of them gave up her life before her husband’s eyes.
fn: The Bengali has a singular verb (*chāḍileka*) and “before her husband” (*pati-āge*): according to *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 10.23.34, one of the wives, held back by her husband, gave up her body. *Anubhāṣya*: see *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam*, Canto 10, Chapter 23.

## 33
w: one  means  there is  if  You give  attention
w: You  not  even if You meet  him  remains  his  life
tr: There is one way, if You will hear it, by which his life may be saved even though You do not meet him.

## 34
w: one  outer cloth  if  You give  mercy  showing
w: it  getting  life  he will keep  of You  hope  holding
tr: If You mercifully give him one of Your outer cloths, he will receive it and keep himself alive, holding on to the hope of seeing You.”
fn: The edition reads *tāhā pāñā prāṇa rākhe, tomāra āśā dhari’* (with a comma after *rākhe*); the Bengali and the transliteration follow the edition.

## 35
w: the Lord  says  you all  very  learned
w: whatever  good  is  that  make  the arrangement
tr: The Lord said, “You are all very learned. Arrange whatever is best.”

## 36
w: then  Nityānanda Gosāñi  of Govinda  from
w: begging  took  of the Lord  one  outer cloth
tr: Then Nityānanda Gosāñi asked Govinda for one of the Lord’s outer cloths and took it.

## 37
w: that  outer cloth  to Sārvabhauma  gave
w: Sārvabhauma  that  cloth  to the king  sent
tr: He gave the cloth to Sārvabhauma, and Sārvabhauma sent it to the king.

## 38
w: the cloth  getting  of the king  became  joyful  the mind
w: as the Lord’s form  regarding  does  of the cloth  worship
tr: Receiving the cloth, the king rejoiced, and he worshipped it as the Lord Himself.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord’s garments, ornaments and all that He uses are eternally non-different from His transcendental body: Śeṣa, a portion of Śrī Baladeva, serves Kṛṣṇa as His bed, garments and so on. Likewise the things used by the guru and the Vaiṣṇavas are non-different from them and eternally worthy of worship.

## 39
w: Rāmānanda  Rāya  when  the South  from  came
w: with the Lord  to stay  the king  he petitioned
tr: When Rāmānanda Rāya returned from the South, he asked the king to let him stay with the Lord.

## 40
w: then  the king  gladly  to him  permission  gave
w: himself  meeting  for the sake of  to speak  began
tr: The king gladly gave him leave, and then began to plead for a meeting with the Lord for himself:

## 41
w: Mahāprabhu  great mercy  shows  to you
w: me  to meet  surely  you will entreat  Him
tr: “Mahāprabhu is very merciful to you. You must entreat Him to meet me.”

## 42
w: together  two  persons  to the Kṣetra  when  came
w: Rāmānanda  Rāya  then  the Lord  met
tr: When the two of them came to the Kṣetra together, Rāmānanda Rāya went to meet the Lord.

## 43
w: at the Lord’s feet  loving devotion  he made known  of the king
w: occasion  finding  thus  he speaks  again and again
tr: At the Lord’s feet he told of the king’s loving devotion, and whenever the occasion arose he spoke of it again and again.

## 44
w: the king’s minister  Rāmānanda  in dealings  skilled
w: the king’s love  telling  melted  of the Lord  the heart
tr: Rāmānanda, the king’s minister, was skilled in dealing with people; by telling of the king’s love he softened the Lord’s heart.

## 45

tr: Pratāparudra could no longer bear his longing, and Rāmānanda entreated the Lord to meet him.

## 46
w: Rāmānanda  at the Lord’s feet  made  a petition
w: once  to Pratāparudra  show  the feet
tr: Rāmānanda petitioned at the Lord’s feet: “Just once, show Your feet to Pratāparudra.”

## 47
w: the Lord  says  Rāmānanda  say  considering
w: the king  to meet  is it fitting  a sannyāsī  being
tr: The Lord said, “Rāmānanda, think it over and tell Me: is it fitting for a sannyāsī to meet a king?

## 48
w: of the king  in the meeting  of a mendicant  both  worlds lost
w: the next world  let it be  people  make  mockery
tr: If a mendicant meets a king, both his worlds are lost. Quite apart from the next world, even here people will mock him.”

## 49
w: Rāmānanda  says  You  the Lord  independent
w: whom  Your  fear  You  are not  dependent on others
tr: Rāmānanda said, “You are the independent Lord. Whom need You fear? You depend on no one.”

## 50
w: the Lord  says  I  a human being  in āśrama  a sannyāsī
w: in body, mind and word  in conduct  fear  I feel
tr: The Lord said, “I am a human being, a sannyāsī by āśrama. In body, mind and word I am fearful about My conduct.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: “I am only a man in the fourth āśrama, not Īśvara; therefore I fear to transgress worldly propriety in body, mind and speech” — that is, “I depend on the opinion of others.” Heading in the edition: “The Lord, as if by a ruse, shows Himself bound by injunctions.”

## 51
w: on white cloth  an ink spot  as  not  is hidden
w: of a sannyāsī  small  fault  all people  sing of
tr: As a spot of ink cannot be hidden on white cloth, so everyone talks of a sannyāsī’s slightest fault.”

## 52
w: Rāya  says  how many  sinners  You have made  delivered
w: the Lord’s servant  Your  devotee  the Gajapati
tr: Rāya said, “You have delivered so many sinners! And the Gajapati is a servant of the Lord and Your devotee.”

## 53
w: the Lord  says  full  as  of milk  a pot
w: a drop of wine  by falling  anyone  not  makes  touch
tr: The Lord said, “It is like a pot full of milk: if a single drop of wine falls into it, no one will touch it.

## 54
w: although  Pratāparudra  possessed of every virtue

tr: Although Pratāparudra has every good quality, the single name ‘king’ has stained him.
fn: The edition reads *eka ‘rājā’ nāma* (without a hyphen); the Bengali and the transliteration follow the edition. Heading in the edition: “The worldly label ‘*viṣayī*’ destroys every virtue.”

## 55
w: still  your  if  great insistence  is
w: then  bringing  have (Me) meet  you  his  son
tr: Still, if you are so insistent, then bring his son to meet Me.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the son is Puruṣottama Jānā (marked with a question mark).

## 56
w: ‘the self  indeed  is born  as the son’  this  the word of scripture
w: of the son  in the meeting  as if  will meet  himself
tr: Scripture says, ‘One’s self is born as one’s son.’ So when his son meets Me, it will be as if the king himself had met Me.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: these are the Lord’s words in *Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam* 10.78.36: “‘One’s self is born as one’s son’ — such is the teaching of the Vedas”; Śrīdhara Svāmī cites the Vedic blessing “You are my very self, bearing the name of son; live a hundred autumns.”

## 57
w: then  Rāya  going  all  to the king  told
w: of the Lord  by the order  his  son  bringing  came
tr: Rāya then went and told the king everything, and at the Lord’s bidding he brought the king’s son.

## 58
w: beautiful  of the king  the son  dark of complexion
w: youthful in age  long  lotus eyes
tr: The king’s son was beautiful and dark-complexioned, in the bloom of youth, with long lotus eyes.

## 59
w: in yellow garments  wears  on the body  jewelled ornaments
w: Śrī  of the remembrance of Kṛṣṇa  he  became  ‘the awakener’
tr: Dressed in yellow and adorned with jewelled ornaments, he was an *uddīpana*, one who awakens the remembrance of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

## 60
w: him  seeing  of Mahāprabhu  remembrance of Kṛṣṇa  arose
w: in loving absorption  him  embracing  to speak  He began
tr: Seeing him, Mahāprabhu remembered Kṛṣṇa. Overwhelmed with love, He embraced him and began to speak:

## 61
w: this one  a mahā-bhāgavata  whose  at the sight
w: of the son of Vraja’s king  remembrance  arises  in all people
tr: “Here is a *mahā-bhāgavata*, at whose sight everyone remembers the son of the king of Vraja.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 59–61): the Lord sees the soul, and has no external vision that looks on body and mind as objects of enjoyment; He does not think of the prince as a worldly man, the son of a *viṣayī*. Himself the *viṣaya-vigraha*, He sees Himself as a gopī and the prince as Vrajendra-nandana Himself; such is the non-dual vision of the pure soul, the “vision of a Vaiṣṇava” (see Madhya 8.277).

## 62
w: fulfilled  have become  I  his  by the sight
w: so  saying  did  him  again  embracing
tr: Seeing him, I have attained My goal.” Saying this, the Lord embraced him again.

## 63
w: by the Lord’s touch  of the prince  arose  ecstatic love
w: sweat  trembling  tears  stupor  horripilation  intense
tr: At the Lord’s touch the prince was overcome with prema: he sweated and trembled, wept, became stunned, and his hair stood on end.

## 64
w: ‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Kṛṣṇa’  he says  he dances  makes  weeping
w: his  fortune  seeing  praise  make  the devotees
tr: He cried “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!”, danced and wept. Seeing his good fortune, the devotees praised him.

## 65
w: then  Mahāprabhu  him  composure  caused
w: daily  coming  Me  meet  this  order  He gave
tr: Then Mahāprabhu calmed him and told him, “Come and see Me every day.”

## 66
w: leave  taking  Rāya  came  the prince  bringing
w: the king  happiness  felt  of the son  the behaviour  seeing
tr: Taking leave, Rāya came back with the prince. The king was delighted to see how his son behaved.

## 67
w: the son  embrace  making  overwhelmed by love  he became
w: direct  touch  as if  of Mahāprabhu  he received
tr: Embracing his son, he was overwhelmed with love, as if he had received Mahāprabhu’s touch directly.

## 68
w: that  from  fortunate  of the king  the son
w: among the Lord’s devotees  became  one
tr: From then on the fortunate prince was one of the Lord’s devotees.

## 69
w: thus  Mahāprabhu  with the devotees
w: constantly  play  does  in the joy of saṅkīrtana
tr: In this way Mahāprabhu constantly enjoyed His pastimes with His devotees in the joy of saṅkīrtana.

## 70
w: the Ācārya and others  devotees  make  the Lord  invitation
w: there  there  alms  He takes  taking  the devotees
tr: Advaita Ācārya and other devotees would invite the Lord, and He would take His meal at each of their homes together with His devotees.

## 71
w: thus  in various  joys  days  some  passed
w: of Jagannātha  the Ratha-yātrā  near  became
tr: So, amid many joys, some days passed, and Jagannātha’s Ratha-yātrā drew near.

## 72
w: first of all  Kāśī Miśra  the Lord  called
w: the paḍichā-pātra  Sārvabhauma  calling  He brought
tr: First the Lord called Kāśī Miśra; then He sent for the *paḍichā-pātra* and Sārvabhauma.

## 73
w: to the three persons  the Lord  smiling  said
w: the service of cleaning the Guṇḍicā temple  begging  He took
tr: Smiling, the Lord spoke to the three of them and begged for the service of cleaning the Guṇḍicā temple.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Guṇḍicā temple stands one *krośa* north-east of the Śrī Mandira; at Ratha-yātrā Śrī Jagannātha goes there for a week and then returns on His chariot. By tradition, Guṇḍicā was the name of King Indradyumna’s queen.

## 74
w: the paḍichā  says  we all  servants  Your
w: what  Your  wish  that  duty  our
tr: The *paḍichā* said, “We are all Your servants. Whatever You wish is our duty.

## 75
w: especially  of the king  the order  has been  to me
w: of the Lord  the order  whatever  that  quickly  to carry out
tr: Moreover, the king has specially ordered me to carry out at once whatever the Lord commands.

## 76
w: for You  fitting  service  is not  the cleaning of the temple
w: this  one  pastime  You perform  as  Your  mind
tr: Cleaning the temple is not a service worthy of You. But if it is Your wish, it will be one of Your pastimes.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The *paḍichā* does not know the hidden meaning of cleaning the Guṇḍicā temple.”

## 77
w: but  pots  brooms  many  are needed
w: the order  give  today  all  here  bringing  I give
tr: Many pots and brooms will be needed, though. Give the order, and I shall bring them all here today.”

## 78
w: new  one hundred  pots  a hundred  brooms
w: the paḍichā  bringing  gave  of the Lord  the wish  knowing
tr: Knowing the Lord’s wish, the *paḍichā* brought a hundred new pots and a hundred brooms.

## 79
w: on the next  day  in the morning  taking  His own people
w: with His divine  hand  of all  on the bodies  smeared  sandal paste
tr: Early the next day the Lord gathered His own devotees and with His own hand anointed them all with sandal paste.

## 80
w: with His divine  hand  gave  to all  one  each  broom
w: all His people  taking  the Lord  went  Himself
tr: With His own hand He gave each of them a broom, and the Lord Himself set out, taking them all with Him.

## 81
w: to the Guṇḍicā temple  He went  to do  cleaning
w: first  brooms  taking  made  cleansing
tr: He went to clean the Guṇḍicā temple, and first of all He took a broom and swept it.
fn: Heading in the edition: “First the Lord Himself sets the example.”

## 82
w: inside  the temple  above  all  He scrubbed
w: the throne  scrubbing  again  placing  He did
tr: He scrubbed everything inside the temple, even the ceiling; He cleaned the throne and set it back in its place.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in the main shrine of the Guṇḍicā temple there is a jewelled altar (*ratna-vedi*) twelve cubits long and two cubits high; this is the throne (*siṁhāsana*).

## 83

w: afterwards  likewise  cleansed  Śrī  Jagamohana
tr: He swept and cleaned the small and large shrines, and afterwards cleaned the Śrī Jagamohana in the same way.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Śrī Jagamohana is the hall between the main shrine and the *nāṭya-mandira*, thirty-two cubits long.

## 84
w: on four sides  a hundred  devotees  sweeping  do
w: Himself  cleans  the Lord  teaches  all
tr: On every side a hundred devotees were sweeping. The Lord Himself cleaned and taught them all.

## 85

## 180
w: where  the Bhaṭṭācārya’s  former  worldly dealings
w: where  this  supreme bliss  make  consideration
tr: “Just consider: where were the Bhaṭṭācārya’s former worldly dealings, and where is this supreme bliss now!”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: formerly Sārvabhauma held to the reasoning of the smārtas and to worldly views and had no transcendental faith in prasāda, in Govinda’s name or in the Vaiṣṇavas; now, by Mahāprabhu’s grace, he had gained that faith and found the highest bliss in honouring prasāda.

## 181

## 184

tr: Where was I then, among my logician disciples who had turned away from the Lord, and where am I now, amid the waves of this nectar ocean of holy company!”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *bahirmukha*, “those turned outward”, are those who think themselves enjoyers of outer forms and tastes, are busy gratifying their senses and have turned away from Kṛṣṇa’s service (cf. Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 7.5.30–31).

## 185

## 190

fn: Heading in the edition: “A sannyāsī is not defiled by food.”

## 191
w: “not  by faults in food  the ascetic”  this  the scripture’s evidence
w: I  indeed  a householder brāhmaṇa  my  a place for fault
tr: ‘An ascetic is not tainted by faults in food’ — this is the evidence of scripture. But I am a householder brāhmaṇa: for Me it is a cause of fault.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the faults of food do not cling to a sannyāsī.

## 192
w: birth, family, character and conduct  not  I know  whose
w: with him  together  one  row  great  impropriety
tr: To eat in the same row with someone whose birth, family, character and conduct are unknown is a great impropriety.”
fn: Heading in the edition: “By calling himself a ‘householder brāhmaṇa’, Advaita feigns to follow the worldly society of the smārtas.”

## 193
w: Nityānanda  says  You  Advaita Ācārya
w: by the “doctrine of non-duality”  is obstructed  the work of pure devotion
tr: Nityānanda replied, “You are Advaita Ācārya, the teacher of non-duality! The ‘doctrine of non-duality’ obstructs the practice of pure devotion.
fn: The words play on Advaita’s name. *Anubhāṣya*: in the guise of blame Nityānanda praises Advaita. Outwardly the non-dual knowledge Śrī Advaita preached may seem one with the māyāvādīs’ doctrine of merging into Brahman; but he is called Ācārya because he proclaims pure bhakti, and his “doctrine of non-duality” is the worship of non-dual knowledge, that is, pure bhakti. What Nityānanda condemns is the doctrine of exclusive non-duality (*kevala-advaita*), which obstructs pure bhakti (see also Madhya 3.93–101).

## 194
w: Your  doctrine’s company  makes  whatever  person
w: “one”  reality  apart from  he  a “second”  not  accepts
tr: Whoever keeps company with Your doctrine accepts nothing ‘second’ besides the ‘one’ reality.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in the guise of blaming Advaita, Nityānanda describes the glory of non-dual knowledge. The supreme reality is the one Śrī Kṛṣṇa, and to see a “second” besides Him is māyā; the *mahā-bhāgavata*, like Prahlāda, sees only the one Kṛṣṇa. Pure Vaiṣṇavas accept the Truth as non-dual, but not as impersonal: it is Bhagavān, full of rasa, eternally with His name, form, qualities, pastimes and associates.

## 195
w: such  You  with  My  together  eating

tr: And here I am eating together with such a person as You! I do not know what will happen to My mind in Your company.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: by this Nityānanda hints that a pure devotee should never eat together with a covert māyāvādī or a *prākṛta-sahajiyā*.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the prasāda was sprinkled with the nectar of the Lord’s mercy, and so it became all the more delicious.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: during the Snāna-yātrā the paint on Jagannātha is washed off, and in the period of *anavasara* the bodies of the three Deities are painted anew (*aṅgarāga*); on the morning of the Nava-yauvana day comes the Netrotsava, the painting of the eyes. *Anubhāṣya*: Netrotsava, “the festival of the eyes”, is the first darśana after the two weeks of *anavasara*, when the eyes of those longing for darśana find their fulfilment.
