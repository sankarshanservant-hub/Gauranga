# English translation, Ādi-līlā 10 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: The Description of the Branches of the Main Trunk

## 1
w: of Śrī Caitanya’s  lotus feet
w: to the bees  obeisance  obeisance
w: in any way  by taking shelter  of whom
w: even a dog  of that fragrance  a partaker  may become
tr: Again and again I bow to the bees that drink the honey of Śrī Caitanya’s lotus feet. Whoever takes shelter of them in any way at all, even one as low as a dog, comes to share in the fragrance of those feet.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the “dog” is one absorbed in enjoyment and without faith in the Lord’s devotees; to “share in the fragrance” of the feet is to receive devotion to Gaura.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  to the host of Gaura’s devotees
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya and Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: of this gardener — of this tree  inexpressible  the account
w: now  hear  of the main branches  of the names  the account
tr: This gardener, this tree, is beyond all telling. Now hear the names of its principal branches.

## 4
w: of Caitanya Gosāñi  as many  associates  the host
w: lesser or greater  status  their  not  is  determinable
tr: Of all the host of Caitanya Gosāñi’s associates, none can determine who is greater and who is lesser.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Among Gaura’s devotees there is no division into higher and lower.”

## 5
w: as many  as many  great souls  made  of them all  an enumeration
w: anyone  to make  is not able  of senior and junior  the order
tr: Many great souls have listed them all, yet none has been able to rank them as senior and junior.

## 6
w: therefore  to them all  having made  obeisance
w: names  only  I give  fault  do not take  my
tr: Therefore, bowing down to them all, I shall simply name them. Please do not count this as a fault in me.

## 7
w: I worship  of Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya,
w: the immortal tree of love,  the dear ones
w: in the form of branches  the devotees
w: of love for Kṛṣṇa  the fruit  who give
tr: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya is the immortal tree of love. I worship His dear devotees, who are the branches of that tree and give everyone the fruit of love for Kṛṣṇa.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the wish-fulfilling tree of love is Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Himself, and His dear devotees are its branches, which give the fruit of love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 8
w: Śrīvāsa  Paṇḍita  and  Śrī  Rāma  Paṇḍita
w: two  brothers —  two  branches  in the world  renowned
tr: Śrīvāsa Paṇḍita and Śrī Rāma Paṇḍita — two brothers, two branches — are renowned throughout the world.
fn: In the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition the branches are listed under numbered headings: (1 — a, b, c, d) Śrīvāsa, Śrī Rāma and their two brothers; (2) Candraśekhara; (3) Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi, and so on; the offshoots of a branch are marked with letters (20a — Satyarāja, an offshoot of Haridāsa’s branch). The ordinal numbers of the branches in the translation follow these headings. *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*): Śrīvāsa was Nārada Muni, and Śrī Rāma the sage Parvata, Nārada’s friend; Śrīvāsa’s wife Mālinī was Ambikā, Kṛṣṇa’s nurse; Śrīvāsa’s niece Nārāyaṇī was the mother of Ṭhākura Vṛndāvana dāsa. After Mahāprabhu took sannyāsa, Śrīvāsa left Navadvīpa and settled in Kumārahaṭṭa.

## 9
w: Śrīpati,  Śrīnidhi —  their  two  brothers
w: of the four  brothers  menservants and maidservants,  household
tr: Śrīpati and Śrīnidhi are their two brothers, and the four brothers had menservants, maidservants and a whole household.

## 10
w: of the two  branches  among the offshoots  of them all  the counting
w: in whose  house  Mahāprabhu’s  constant  saṅkīrtana
tr: All of them are counted among the offshoots of these two branches. In their house Mahāprabhu held saṅkīrtana constantly.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Their undivided devotion to Gaura.”

## 11
w: with their family  render  who  Caitanya  service
w: Gauracandra  besides  not  know  goddess or god
tr: With their whole family they served Śrī Caitanya, and apart from Gauracandra they knew no god or goddess.

## 12
w: ‘Ācāryaratna’  the name  bears  big  one  branch
w: his  associates  his  branches and offshoots
tr: Another great branch bears the name Ācāryaratna; his associates are its branches and offshoots.

## 13
w: of Ācāryaratna  the name  ‘Śrī Candraśekhara’
w: in whose  house  in the mood of the Goddess  danced  the Lord
tr: The name of Ācāryaratna is Śrī Candraśekhara. In his house the Lord danced in the form of the Goddess, the Ādi-śakti.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in Candraśekhara’s house a sacred drama was performed in which Mahāprabhu danced as the Goddess (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 18); the place is now known as Vraja-pattana. Candraśekhara was with Mahāprabhu in Kāṭoyā at His sannyāsa and, returning to Navadvīpa, told everyone of it. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: according to some books, Candraśekhara was the husband of the sister of Mahāprabhu’s mother.

## 14
w: Puṇḍarīka  Vidyānidhi —  a great  branch  I know
w: whose  name  uttering  the Lord  wept  Himself
tr: Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi is a great branch; the Lord Himself would weep as He spoke his name.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*): Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi was Vṛṣabhānu, the father of Śrī Rādhā; Mahāprabhu called him “Father” and gave him the name Premanidhi. A disciple of Śrī Mādhavendra Purī, he was the guru of Gadādhara Paṇḍita and the friend of Svarūpa Dāmodara; his home was in Cāṭigrām.

## 15
w: a great  branch —  Gadādhara  Paṇḍita  Gosāñi
w: he  Lakṣmī in form,  to him  equal  anyone  there is not
tr: Another great branch is Gadādhara Paṇḍita Gosāñi. He is Lakṣmī Herself in form, and none is his equal.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*): Gadādhara Paṇḍita is Śrī Rādhā, the queen of Vṛndāvana; Svarūpa Dāmodara saw in him the Lakṣmī of Vraja. His branch is described at the end of Ādi 12.

## 16
w: his  disciples and their disciples —  his  offshoots
w: in this way  of all  the branches and offshoots  the account
tr: His disciples and their disciples are the offshoots of this branch, and so it is with all the other branches and offshoots.

## 17
w: Vakreśvara  Paṇḍita —  of the Lord  a very  dear  servant
w: in one ecstasy  twenty-four  praharas  whose  dance
tr: Vakreśvara Paṇḍita was a servant very dear to the Lord. In a single unbroken ecstasy he could dance for twenty-four praharas — three days and nights.

## 18
w: Himself  Mahāprabhu  sings  whose  at the time of dancing
w: the Lord’s  feet  clasping  Vakreśvara  says
tr: While he danced, Mahāprabhu Himself would sing, and once Vakreśvara clasped the Lord’s feet and said:

## 19
w: ten  thousand  Gandharvas  to me  give,  O moon-faced One
w: they  sing,  I  dance —  then  my  happiness
tr: “O moon-faced Lord, give me ten thousand Gandharvas! Let them sing while I dance — that would be my joy.”

## 20
w: the Lord  says —  you  My  wing  one  side
w: in the sky  flying  I would go,  if I got  another  wing
tr: The Lord replied, “You are one of My wings. If I had another like you, I would fly up into the sky.”

## 21
w: Paṇḍita  Jagadānanda  of the Lord  the very life
w: among people  famed  who  of Satyabhāmā  the manifestation
tr: Paṇḍita Jagadānanda is the Lord’s very life. He is renowned as a manifestation of Satyabhāmā.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in Navadvīpa he joined the Lord’s kīrtanas at Śrīvāsa’s house and in the home of Candraśekhara; after the Lord’s sannyāsa, on the way to Orissa, he carried His staff and begged alms for Him.

## 22
w: out of love  to do  wants  for the Lord  care and nurture
w: for renunciation  people  fearing  the Lord  not  accepts  ever
tr: Out of love he longed to surround the Lord with care and comfort, but the Lord, guarding His renunciation and wary of what people would say, never accepted it.

## 23
w: the two  little squabbles  start  quarrels
w: of his  love  the account  later  I shall tell  all
tr: So the two of them would fall into little quarrels. I shall tell the whole story of his love later on.

## 24
w: Rāghava  Paṇḍita —  of the Lord  an early  follower
w: his  branch  main  one —  Makaradhvaja  Kara
tr: Rāghava Paṇḍita was one of the Lord’s first followers, and his chief branch is Makaradhvaja Kara.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Rāghava Paṇḍita’s home was in Pānihāṭi on the bank of the Gaṅgā; his samādhi is there, and the Deity Śrī Madana-mohana is still served nearby. Makaradhvaja Kara also lived in Pānihāṭi.

## 25
w: his  sister  Damayantī  of the Lord  dear  maidservant
w: of the Lord  the provisions  who  makes  all year round
tr: His sister Damayantī, a dear maidservant of the Lord, prepared provisions for Him all year round.

## 26
w: those  all  provisions  as many  in bags  filling
w: Rāghava  taking  goes  secretly  keeping
tr: Filling bags with all those provisions, Rāghava would carry them secretly to the Lord.

## 27
w: all the year  them  the Lord  accepts
w: ‘Rāghava’s bags’  calling  the fame  of which
tr: All year round the Lord accepted them, and they became famous as “Rāghava’s bags.”
fn: The bags are described in Antya 10.

## 28
w: those  all  provisions  later  I shall make  in detail
w: of which  in the hearing  of devotees  flow  streams of tears
tr: I shall describe those provisions in detail later. Hearing of them, the devotees shed streams of tears.

## 29
w: of the Lord  extremely  dear —  Paṇḍita  Gaṅgādāsa
w: by whose  remembrance  there is  of all  bonds  destruction
tr: Paṇḍita Gaṅgādāsa was extremely dear to the Lord. By remembering him all bondage is destroyed.

## 30
w: Caitanya’s  associate —  Śrī  Ācārya  Purandara
w: father  calling  whom  addresses  Gaurāṅgasundara
tr: Śrī Purandara Ācārya was an associate of Śrī Caitanya; Gaurāṅgasundara called him Father.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (from the *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 5): when the Lord came to Pānihāṭi, Purandara Ācārya hurried to Him; the Lord, calling him Father, embraced him in ecstatic love, and seeing the Lord, he wept uncontrollably.

## 31
w: Dāmodara  Paṇḍita  the branch  in love  fierce
w: of the Lord  toward  who  made  a chastisement in words
tr: The branch Dāmodara Paṇḍita was fierce in his love; he even rebuked the Lord in words.

## 32
w: of the rebuke  the story  I shall tell  later  in detail  making
w: with the rebuke  pleased  the Lord  him  sent  to Nadīyā
tr: I shall tell the story of that rebuke in detail later. Pleased with it, the Lord sent him to Nadīyā.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the rebuke is described in Antya 3. *Anubhāṣya*: at the Lord’s wish Dāmodara Paṇḍita went to Gauḍa to be with Mother Śacī, and for the Ratha-yātrā he would come to Purī again with the devotees (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 9).

## 33
w: his  younger brother  a branch —  Śaṅkara  Paṇḍita
w: ‘the Lord’s footrest’  whose  name  is known
tr: His younger brother, Śaṅkara Paṇḍita, is a branch known by the name “the Lord’s footrest.”

## 34
w: Sadāśiva  Paṇḍita  whose  at the Lord’s feet  hope
w: at the very first  Nityānanda’s  in whose  house  dwelling
tr: Sadāśiva Paṇḍita set all his hope on the Lord’s feet, and at first Nityānanda lived in his house.

## 35
w: of Śrī Nṛsiṁha  a worshipper —  Pradyumna  Brahmacārī
w: the Lord  his  name  made  ‘Nṛsiṁhānanda’  making
tr: Pradyumna Brahmacārī was a worshipper of Śrī Nṛsiṁha, and the Lord gave him the name Nṛsiṁhānanda.

## 36
w: Nārāyaṇa  Paṇḍita  one  most  generous
w: of Caitanya  the feet  besides  not  knows  anything else
tr: Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍita was a most generous soul who knew nothing but the feet of Śrī Caitanya.

## 37
w: Śrīmān  Paṇḍita  a branch —  of the Lord  own  servant
w: the torch  holds  when  the Lord  dances
tr: The branch Śrīmān Paṇḍita was the Lord’s personal servant; whenever the Lord danced, he held the torch.

## 38
w: Śuklāmbara  Brahmacārī  very  fortunate
w: whose  rice  begging  snatching  ate  the Lord
tr: Śuklāmbara Brahmacārī was most fortunate: the Lord Himself would beg his rice, or snatch it from him, and eat it.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in Navadvīpa Mahāprabhu would joyfully snatch and eat the rice that Śuklāmbara had gathered by begging (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 16 and 25).

## 39
w: Nandana  Ācārya  a branch  in the world  famed
w: hiding  the two  Lords  in whose  house  stayed
tr: The branch Nandana Ācārya is famed in the world, for the two Lords hid in his house.

## 40
w: Śrī  Mukunda  Datta  a branch —  of the Lord  a fellow student
w: in whose  kīrtana  dances  Caitanya Gosāñi
tr: The branch Śrī Mukunda Datta was the Lord’s fellow student; when he sang kīrtana, Caitanya Gosāñi danced.

## 41
w: Vāsudeva  Datta —  of the Lord  a servant  great-souled
w: with a thousand mouths  whose  qualities  telling  cannot be done
tr: Vāsudeva Datta was the Lord’s great-souled servant; even with a thousand mouths one could not tell his virtues.

## 42
w: in the world  as many  beings,  their  sins  taking
w: hell  to suffer  wants  the beings  setting free
tr: He wished to take upon himself the sins of every living being in the world and suffer in hell himself, if only they could be set free.
fn: His prayer is described in Madhya 15.159–180 (*Anubhāṣya*).

## 43
w: of the branch Haridāsa Ṭhākura  wonderful  the character
w: three  hundred thousand  names  he  takes  without fail
tr: Wonderful is the life of the branch Haridāsa Ṭhākura: without fail he chanted three hundred thousand names every day.

## 44
w: his  endless  virtues —  I tell  only a glimpse
w: Ācārya Gosāñi  whom  fed  the śrāddha plate
tr: His virtues are endless; I can give only a glimpse of them. Advaita Ācārya fed him the plate of the śrāddha offering.
fn: The *śrāddha-pātra* is offered to the most worthy brāhmaṇa; Advaita Ācārya offered it to Haridāsa, who was born in a Muslim family (Antya 3).

## 45
w: like Prahlāda  his  of virtues  the waves
w: even when beaten by the Yavanas  whose  not  a frown
tr: The waves of his virtues were like Prahlāda’s: even when the Yavanas beat him, he did not so much as frown.

## 46
w: he  perfection  attained when  his  body  taking  on His lap
w: danced  Caitanya Prabhu  in great  joy
tr: When he attained perfection, Caitanya Prabhu took his body in His arms and danced in great joy.

## 47
w: his  pastimes  has described  Vṛndāvana dāsa
w: whatever  remains,  later  I shall make  known
tr: Vṛndāvana dāsa has described his pastimes; what remains I shall tell later.

## 48
w: his  offshoots —  all the  of Kulīna-grāma  people
w: Satyarāja and others —  of his  mercy  the recipients
tr: All the people of Kulīna-grāma are his offshoots; Satyarāja and others received his mercy.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Satyarāja Khān was the son of Guṇarāja Khān of Kulīna-grāma and the father of Rāmānanda Vasu. Haridāsa Ṭhākura spent a cāturmāsya in Kulīna-grāma and blessed the whole Vasu family. Mahāprabhu asked Satyarāja to bring silk ropes for Jagannātha every year and explained to him who is a Vaiṣṇava, a better Vaiṣṇava and the best of Vaiṣṇavas (Madhya 15–16).

## 49
w: Śrī  Murāri  Gupta  a branch —  of love  a storehouse
w: of the Lord  the heart  melts  hearing  the humility  whose
tr: The branch Śrī Murāri Gupta is a storehouse of love; hearing his humility, the Lord’s heart would melt.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Murāri Gupta, the author of the *Śrī Caitanya-carita*, was born of a physician family of Śrīhaṭṭa and later lived in Navadvīpa; he was older than Mahāprabhu. In his house the Lord showed His form as Varāha, and to him He revealed Himself as Śrī Rāma.

## 50
w: gifts  not  accepts,  not  takes  anyone’s  wealth
w: his own calling  following  makes  of the family  support
tr: He accepted no gifts and took no one’s money, but supported his family by his own calling.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *ātma-vṛtti* — the calling proper to one’s own class: Murāri Gupta was a physician (kavirāja).

## 51
w: treatment  gives  to whom  being  merciful
w: of the body  the disease,  of worldly existence  the disease —  both  his  are destroyed
tr: Whomever he treated with kindness was freed from both diseases — the disease of the body and the disease of worldly existence.

## 52
w: Śrīmān  Sena  of the Lord  a servant  foremost
w: of Caitanya  the feet  besides  not  knows  anything else
tr: Śrīmān Sena was a foremost servant of the Lord and knew nothing but the feet of Śrī Caitanya.

## 53
w: Śrī  Gadādhara  dāsa  a branch  above all
w: of the Kazis  in the mouths  who  made utter  ‘Hari’
tr: The branch Śrī Gadādhara dāsa stands above all; he made even the Kazis utter the name of Hari.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Gadādhara dāsa lived at Eṛiyādaha on the Gaṅgā and was one of Nityānanda’s chief helpers in preaching in Gauḍa. The local Kazi opposed the kīrtana; one night Gadādhara came to him singing and asked him to say the name of Hari. The Kazi replied, “I will say ‘Hari’ tomorrow,” and Gadādhara, full of love, cried out, “Why tomorrow? You have just said ‘Hari’!” He is the radiance of Śrī Rādhā and is counted among the associates of both Gaura and Nityānanda.

## 54
w: Śivānanda  Sena —  of the Lord  a servant  intimate
w: to the Lord’s place  going  all  take  whose  company
tr: Śivānanda Sena was an intimate servant of the Lord; all who went to see the Lord went in his company.

## 55
w: every year  the Lord’s devotees  with him  taking
w: to Nīlācala  goes  on the way  care  taking
tr: Every year he took the Lord’s devotees with him to Nīlācala, caring for them all along the way.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śivānanda Sena lived in Kumārahaṭṭa (Hālisahar); every year he guided the devotees of Gauḍa to Nīlācala and bore all the costs of the journey (Madhya 16.19–26).

## 56
w: to the devotees  mercy  shows  the Lord  in these three  forms
w: ‘sākṣāt’,  ‘āveśa’  and  ‘āvirbhāva’  in the form
tr: The Lord shows His mercy to the devotees in three ways: by His direct presence (*sākṣāt*), by entering into someone with His power (*āveśa*), and by manifesting Himself where He is not present in person (*āvirbhāva*).

## 57
w: in ‘sākṣāt’  all  the devotees  see  without distinction
w: Nakula  Brahmacārī’s  in the body  the Lord’s  ‘āveśa’
tr: In His direct presence all the devotees see Him alike; in the body of Nakula Brahmacārī there was the Lord’s *āveśa*.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: according to the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* (74) it is the other way round: the Lord’s *āvirbhāva* was in Nakula Brahmacārī and His *āveśa* in Pradyumna Miśra. The Lord always manifests Himself (*āvirbhāva*) in four places: in Śacī’s house, in Nityānanda’s dancing, in Śrīvāsa’s kīrtana and in Rāghava’s house (Antya 2.34–35).

## 58
w: ‘Pradyumna  Brahmacārī’  his  earlier  name  was
w: ‘Nṛsiṁhānanda’  the name  the Lord  afterwards  indeed  gave
tr: His earlier name was Pradyumna Brahmacārī; afterwards the Lord gave him the name Nṛsiṁhānanda.

## 59
w: in him  there was  Caitanya’s  ‘āvirbhāva’
w: extraordinary  such  of the Lord  many  ways
tr: In him Śrī Caitanya manifested Himself (*āvirbhāva*). The Lord has many such wonderful, unearthly ways.

## 60
w: tasted  these  all  rasas  Sena  Śivānanda
w: in detail  I shall tell  later  all this  bliss
tr: Śivānanda Sena tasted all these rasas; I shall describe this bliss in detail later.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śivānanda Sena experienced and tasted all three kinds of the Lord’s mercy — *sākṣāt*, *āveśa* and *āvirbhāva* (Antya 2).
