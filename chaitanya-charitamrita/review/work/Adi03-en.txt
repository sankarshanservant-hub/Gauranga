## 0
tr: The Auspicious Invocation of Blessing: The General Cause of Caitanya’s Descent

## 1
w: Śrī Caitanya  the Lord  I worship
w: by whose  feet  of the shelter  by the power
w: gathers  from the mines  from the multitude
w: the ignorant one  of conclusive truth  precious jewels
tr: I bow to the Lord Śrī Caitanya. By the power of shelter at His feet even an ignorant man gathers, from the many mines of scripture, the precious jewels of conclusive truth (*siddhānta*).

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: of the third  verse  of the meaning  I have made  the explanation
w: of the fourth  verse  the meaning  hear  O devotees
tr: I have explained the meaning of the third verse. Now, O devotees, hear the meaning of the fourth.

## 4
w: not given before  for a long time  out of mercy  descended  in Kali
w: to bestow  of the most exalted radiant rasa  devotion to Himself  the treasure
w: Hari  golden  beautiful  lustre  with a mass  illumined
w: always  in the cave of the heart  may He shine  your  Śacī’s  son
tr: “May the Lord Hari, the son of Śacī, illumined by a mass of lustre more beautiful than gold, ever shine in the inmost cave of your hearts. Out of His mercy He has descended in the age of Kali to bestow what had not been given for a very long time — the treasure of devotion to Himself, rich in the most exalted, radiant *ujjvala-rasa*.” (Vidagdha-mādhava, 1.2)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.4. *Anubhāṣya*: with this verse Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī, blessing the world, opens the drama *Vidagdha-mādhava*, and the author, his follower, follows the guru he holds dear. *Unnatojjvala-rasa* is “the exalted *ujjvala-rasa*, that is, *śṛṅgāra-rasa*”; *sva-bhakti-śrī* is “the beauty of prema for Him”; *sphuratu* — “may He reveal Himself”.

## 5
w: complete  Bhagavān  Kṛṣṇa  of Vraja’s king  the son
w: in Goloka  Vraja  together with  eternal  pastimes
tr: Kṛṣṇa, the son of the king of Vraja, is Bhagavān in all His fullness. Together with all Vraja He enjoys His pastimes eternally in Goloka.

## 6
w: of Brahmā  in one  day  He  once
w: descended  having become  performs  manifest  pastimes
tr: Once in a day of Brahmā He descends and displays those pastimes in this world.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in Goloka, the splendour (*vaibhava*) of Gokula, Kṛṣṇa dwells eternally with everything that makes up the rasa of Vraja; these are His unmanifest (*aprakaṭa*) pastimes. Once in every kalpa, that is, in each day of Brahmā, He descends into the world and displays them as manifest (*prakaṭa*) pastimes.

## 7
w: Satya,  Tretā,  Dvāpara,  Kali —  four  ages  we know
w: those  four  ages  divine  one  age  we count
tr: We know the four ages — Satya, Tretā, Dvāpara and Kali; these four together are counted as one divine age (*divya-yuga*).

## 8
w: in seventy-one  cycles of four ages  one  manvantara
w: fourteen  manvantaras  of Brahmā  of the day  within
tr: Seventy-one such cycles make one manvantara, the reign of a Manu, and fourteen manvantaras make up a day of Brahmā.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Sūrya-siddhānta*): Kali-yuga lasts 432,000 solar years, Dvāpara twice as long, Tretā three times and Satya four times; together they make 4,320,000 years — a *mahā-yuga*, or *divya-yuga*. Seventy-one *mahā-yugas* make a manvantara; fourteen manvantaras, with fifteen junctions (*sandhi*) each as long as a Satya-yuga, make a thousand *yugas* — a day of Brahmā, or kalpa.

## 9
w: ‘Vaivasvata’  by name  this  seventh  manvantara
w: twenty-seven  cycles of four ages  when gone  of it  within
tr: The present, seventh manvantara is called Vaivasvata, and twenty-seven cycles of its ages have already passed.

## 10
w: in the twenty-eighth  cycle of four ages  of Dvāpara  at the end
w: Vraja  together with  takes place  of Kṛṣṇa  the manifestation
tr: At the end of the Dvāpara of the twenty-eighth cycle, Kṛṣṇa appears in this world together with His Vraja.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «সহিত» (another reading: «সহিতে»). *Anubhāṣya*: when twenty-seven *mahā-yugas* of the Vaivasvata manvantara have passed, in the twenty-eighth, after Satya and Tretā, at the end of Dvāpara, comes the time of Kṛṣṇa’s appearance.

## 11
w: servitude,  friendship,  parental love,  the love of the beloved —  four  rasas
w: of the four  moods  devotees  all  Kṛṣṇa  of them  under the sway
tr: Servitude, friendship, parental affection and the love of the beloved (*śṛṅgāra*) are the four rasas, and Kṛṣṇa is under the sway of all the devotees who have these four moods.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *śānta-rasa* is not named here because, though in the material world it is held highest, in the spiritual world it stands lowest: in it there is no exchange of feeling between Kṛṣṇa and His devotee. In servitude, friendship, parental love and *madhura* the love for Kṛṣṇa rises step by step. *Śṛṅgāra* is the love of the beloved ones of Vraja, not a conjugal relationship (see Ādi 1.4).

## 12
w: servants  friends  father  mother  beloveds  the company  taking
w: in Vraja  play  makes  Kṛṣṇa  in love absorbed  becoming
tr: With His servants and friends, His father and mother and His beloved ones, Kṛṣṇa sports in Vraja, absorbed in love.

## 13
w: as He wished  having sported  Kṛṣṇa  makes  disappearance
w: disappearance  having made  in the mind  makes  reflection
tr: Having sported as He wished, Kṛṣṇa disappears, and after His disappearance He reflects:

## 14
w: for a long time  not  I make  of loving devotion  the gift
w: devotion  without  of the world  is not  standing
tr: “For a long time I have not given the gift of loving devotion (*prema-bhakti*), and without devotion the world has no ground to stand on.

## 15
w: in all  the world  to Me  they do  devotion by rule
w: by devotion by rule  of Vraja  the mood  to attain  there is not  the power
tr: All over the world they worship Me by the rules of scripture (*vidhi-bhakti*), but devotion by rule has no power to reach the love of Vraja.

## 16
w: of majesty  with the awareness  the whole  world  is mixed
w: by majesty  slackened  in love  is not  My  pleasure
tr: The whole world is permeated by the awareness of My majesty, and love slackened by awe gives Me no joy.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: in *vidhi-bhakti* the awareness of majesty (*aiśvarya*) prevails; in that mood prema slackens and loses its depth, and such love does not please the Lord.

## 17
w: of majesty  in the awareness  by rule  worship  performing
w: to Vaikuṇṭha  they go  of four  kinds  liberation  attaining
tr: Worshipping by rule in the awareness of My majesty, they go to Vaikuṇṭha and attain the four kinds of liberation.

## 18
w: equal opulence,  likeness of form,  and  nearness,  residence in the same world
w: merging  not  accepts  the devotee  in which  with Brahman  oneness
tr: These are *sārṣṭi* (opulence equal to Mine), *sārūpya* (a form like Mine), *sāmīpya* (nearness to Me) and *sālokya* (residence in My world). But *sāyujya*, in which one becomes one with Brahman, the devotee never accepts.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *sārṣṭi* — opulence equal to Viṣṇu’s; *sārūpya* — a form like Viṣṇu’s, with four arms and so on; *sāmīpya* — dwelling near Him; *sālokya* — residence in His world. The *Anubhāṣya* refers to the *Bhāgavatam* (3.29.13): “Even when offered *sālokya*, *sārṣṭi*, *sārūpya*, *sāmīpya* and oneness, My people do not accept them without My service.”

## 19
w: of the age  the religion  I shall set going  of the name  congregational chanting
w: four  moods  devotion  giving  I shall make dance  the world
tr: I shall inaugurate the religion of the age, the congregational chanting of the holy name (*nāma-saṅkīrtana*), and, giving devotion in the four moods, I shall make the whole world dance.

## 20
w: Myself  I shall make  of a devotee  the mood  acceptance
w: Myself  practising  devotion  I shall teach  to all
tr: I Myself shall take on the mood of a devotee, and by practising devotion Myself I shall teach it to all.

## 21
w: oneself  not  having done  dharma  to teach  not  is possible
w: this  indeed  the conclusion  in the Gītā and the Bhāgavata  they sing
tr: Unless one practises dharma oneself, one cannot teach it. This is the conclusion sung in the *Gītā* and the *Bhāgavatam*.

## 22
w: when  when  indeed  of dharma
w: a decline  there is  O descendant of Bharata
w: a rise  of irreligion
w: then  Myself  I send forth  I
tr: “Whenever, O descendant of Bharata, dharma declines and irreligion rises, then I manifest Myself.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 4.7)

## 23
w: for the deliverance  of the saints
w: for the destruction  and  of the wicked
w: of dharma  the establishing  for the sake of
w: I appear  in age  after age
tr: “To deliver the saints, to destroy the wicked and to establish dharma, I appear in age after age.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 4.8)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the saints (*sādhu*) are those devoted to serving the Lord, and He delivers them from the obstacles to their service; the wicked (*duṣkṛt*) are the enemies of the devotees whom none but He can slay — Rāvaṇa, Kaṁsa, Keśī and others; dharma is non-envious service to Bhagavān, whose chief mark is saṅkīrtana.

## 24
w: would perish  these  worlds
w: not  I should do  action  if  I
w: of confusion  and  the maker  I would be
w: I would ruin  these  beings
tr: “If I did not act, these worlds would perish; I would be the cause of confusion among the classes of men and would ruin all these beings.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 3.24)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the Lord, free from any desire for worldly enjoyment, acts to set an example: if He did not act, people taking Him as their model would fall; *upahanyām* — “I would ruin”, that is, “make them impure”.

## 25
w: whatever  whatever  does  the best
w: that  that  indeed  other  people
w: he  whatever  standard  sets
w: the world  that  follows
tr: “Whatever the best of men does, others do the same; whatever standard he sets, the world follows.” (Bhagavad-gītā, 3.21)

## 26
w: of the age  of the religion  the establishing  happens  a portion  from
w: Me  without  others  cannot  of Vraja  love  to give
tr: The religion of the age can be established by My portion (*aṁśa*), but none but Me can give the love of Vraja.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the Lord wishes to appear in order to bring the world two things — the religion of the age, *nāma-saṅkīrtana*, and the love of Vraja. The religion of the age can be established even by a partial avatāra, but the love of Vraja none can give except the complete Bhagavān, Śrī Kṛṣṇa.

## 27
w: let there be  avatāras  many
w: of the lotus-naveled One  in every way  auspicious
w: than Kṛṣṇa  other  who  indeed  to the creepers
w: even  love  the giver  becomes
tr: “Let there be many all-auspicious avatāras of the Lord whose navel is a lotus; but who other than Kṛṣṇa gives prema even to the creepers?” (Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta, 1.5.37)
fn: In the edition — “the words of Bilvamaṅgala” (*Laghu-bhāgavatāmṛta*, Pūrva-khaṇḍa, 5.37). *Anubhāṣya* and *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the “creepers” (*latā*) are those who have taken shelter of Him.

## 28
w: therefore  My own  devotees  having made  companions
w: on earth  having descended  I shall make  various  sports
tr: Therefore, taking My own devotees with Me, I shall descend to earth and enjoy many kinds of pastimes.”

## 29
w: thus  having thought  of the age of Kali  in the first  junction
w: descended  became  Kṛṣṇa  Himself  in Nadīyā
tr: Thinking thus, Kṛṣṇa Himself descended in Nadia in the first junction (*sandhyā*) of the age of Kali.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the first *sandhyā* is the opening period of an age, a twelfth part of it; the first *sandhyā* of Kali-yuga lasts 36,000 solar years. Śrī Gaurasundara appeared when 4,586 years of Kali had passed — that is, in its first *sandhyā* — at Śrī Māyāpur in Navadvīpa.

## 30
w: of the lion Caitanya  in Navadvīpa  the descent
w: lion’s  neck,  lion’s  strength,  of a lion  the roar
tr: So the lion Caitanya descended in Navadvīpa — with the neck of a lion, the strength of a lion and the roar of a lion.

## 31
w: that  lion  may He sit  of the living being  of the heart  in the cave
w: of sin  the elephant  perishes  at whose  roar
tr: May that lion take His seat in the cave of every soul’s heart — the lion at whose roar the elephant of sin perishes.

## 32
w: in the first  pastimes  His  ‘Viśvambhara’  the name
w: with the rasa of devotion  He filled,  He upheld  of beings  the multitude
tr: In His early pastimes His name was Viśvambhara. He filled the world with the nectar of devotion and upheld all living beings.

## 33
w: ‘ḍubhṛñ’  of the root  the meaning —  nourishing,  sustaining
w: He nourished,  He sustained  love  giving  the three worlds
tr: The root *ḍubhṛñ* means “to nourish and to sustain”: giving love, He nourished and sustained the three worlds.

## 34
w: in the last pastimes  the name  He bears  ‘Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya’
w: Śrī Kṛṣṇa  making known  the whole  universe  He made  blessed
tr: In His later pastimes He bears the name Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya. Making Śrī Kṛṣṇa known to all, He blessed the whole world.
fn: In the edition verses 3.32 and 3.34 are headed: the name Viśvambhara is the presiding Deity of *abhidheya* (the means), the name Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya the presiding Deity of *sambandha* (knowledge of the relationship). *Anubhāṣya*: the later pastimes are the twenty-four years after sannyāsa. Mahāprabhu received the name “Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya” when He took sannyāsa from Keśava Bhāratī; in Śaṅkara’s line this is a *brahmacārī* name, and He never called Himself by the sannyāsa title “Bhāratī”: in that line the sannyāsa name carries the claim to be God, whereas the *brahmacārī* name breathes service to the guru and is not opposed to bhakti.

## 35
w: His  as the avatāra of the age  knowing  Garga  the great soul
w: of Kṛṣṇa  at the name-giving  has made  the determination
tr: Knowing Him to be the avatāra of the age, the great Garga declared it at Kṛṣṇa’s name-giving ceremony.

## 36
w: there were  colours  three  indeed  of Him
w: accepting  in each age  bodies
w: white  red  likewise  yellow
w: now  blackness  has attained
tr: “Your son, who accepts a body in every age, has appeared in three colours — white, red and yellow; now He has become dark (*kṛṣṇa*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.8.13)
fn: Garga Muni’s words to Nanda Mahārāja. *Anubhāṣya*: *tathā* (“likewise”) points to the future: He will be yellow in the first *sandhyā* of the Kali-yuga of the twenty-eighth *mahā-yuga* of the Vaivasvata manvantara. Śrī Jīva Gosvāmī (*Krama-sandarbha*, quoted in the *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* on 3.51) adds that Śrī Kṛṣṇa is the complete form, in whom all the avatāras are contained.

## 37
w: white,  red,  yellow  colour —  these  three  lustres
w: in the Satya,  Tretā,  Kali  ages  bears  the Lord of Śrī
tr: White, red and yellow — these three lustres the Lord of Śrī assumes in the Satya, Tretā and Kali ages.

## 38
w: now  in Dvāpara  He  became  dark  in colour
w: this  all  of the scriptures, Āgamas and Purāṇas  the essence
tr: Now, in Dvāpara, He has become dark. This is the essence of all the scriptures — the Āgamas and the Purāṇas.

## 39
w: in Dvāpara  the Lord  dark
w: in yellow garments  with His own weapons
w: by Śrīvatsa and the rest  by marks  and
w: by characteristics  distinguished
tr: “In Dvāpara the Lord is dark, clad in yellow and bearing His own weapons, and He is known by the Śrīvatsa and other marks and characteristics.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.27)

## 40
w: in Kali-yuga  of the age  the religion —  of the name  the spreading
w: for that  sake  yellow  in colour  the Caitanya avatāra
tr: In the age of Kali the religion of the age is the spreading of the holy name. For this the golden-hued avatāra, Caitanya, has come.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Madhvācārya, in his commentary on the *Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad*, quotes the *Nārāyaṇa-saṁhitā*: “In Dvāpara people worship Viṣṇu by the Pañcarātra, but in Kali the Lord Hari is worshipped by the name alone.” The *Kali-santaraṇa Upaniṣad*: “Hare Kṛṣṇa Hare Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Kṛṣṇa Hare Hare / Hare Rāma Hare Rāma Rāma Rāma Hare Hare — these sixteen names destroy the contamination of Kali; no higher means is seen in all the Vedas.”

## 41
w: molten  gold  like  lustre,  mighty  body
w: new  clouds  surpassing  of the throat  the sound  which  deep
tr: His lustre is like molten gold, His body tall and mighty, and His deep voice surpasses the thunder of fresh rain-clouds.

## 42
w: in length  and in breadth  who  of one’s own  hands (cubits)
w: four  cubits  is  ‘great person’  famed
tr: One whose height and arm-span both measure four of his own cubits is famed as a *mahāpuruṣa*, a great person.

## 43
w: ‘nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala’  is  his  the name
w: of nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala  the body  Caitanya  of virtues  the abode
tr: Such a person is called *nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala*, and Caitanya, the abode of all virtues, has such a body.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *nyagrodha-parimaṇḍala* is one whose height and girth are four of his own cubits — a *mahāpuruṣa*; so too is Viṣṇu called, complete in all four of His forms (*catur-vyūha*), who, surpassing all beings, holds them in check by His *māyā*.

## 44
w: to the knees  hanging  arms,  lotus  eyes
w: the sesame flower  surpassing  nose,  the moon  face
tr: His arms reach down to His knees, His eyes are like lotuses, His nose is finer than a sesame flower, and His face is like the moon.

## 45
w: peaceful,  self-controlled,  to devotion to Kṛṣṇa  steadfastly  dedicated
w: to devotees  affectionate,  gentle,  to all  beings  equal
tr: He is peaceful and self-controlled, steadfastly dedicated to devotion to Kṛṣṇa; loving to His devotees, gentle in nature, and equal towards all beings.

## 46
w: of sandalwood  armlets and bracelets,  sandal  ornaments
w: at the time of dancing  having put on  performs  Kṛṣṇa  saṅkīrtana
tr: Adorned with sandal paste and wearing armlets and bracelets of sandalwood, He dances in the saṅkīrtana of Kṛṣṇa’s names.

## 47
w: these  all  qualities  taking  the sage  Vaiśampāyana
w: in the Thousand Names  made  His  of names  the enumeration
tr: Taking all these qualities, the sage Vaiśampāyana counted His names in the *Thousand Names* of Viṣṇu.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *Thousand Names* is the *Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma* of the *Mahābhārata*, Dāna-dharma, chapter 149; commentaries on it were written by Śaṅkarācārya, Śrī Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa and other Vaiṣṇava ācāryas.

## 48
w: two  pastimes  of Caitanya —  early  and  last
w: in the two  pastimes  four  four  names  special
tr: Caitanya’s pastimes are of two parts, the early and the later, and in each of them He has four special names.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the early pastimes are His life as a householder, the first twenty-four years; the later are His sannyāsa, the last twenty-four years (cf. 3.32–34). The eight names are given in the next verse.

## 49
w: of golden  colour  with limbs of gold
w: with beautiful limbs  wearing sandal armlets
w: sannyāsa  taking  self-restrained  peaceful
w: to steadfastness and peace  devoted
tr: “He is of golden colour, His limbs are like gold, His form is beautiful, and He wears armlets of sandal. He takes sannyāsa; He is self-restrained and peaceful, and wholly devoted to steadfastness and peace.” (Mahābhārata, Dāna-dharma, 149; Viṣṇu-sahasra-nāma, 92 and 95)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the first four names belong to the early pastimes, the last four to the later. According to the *Anubhāṣya* and the commentary *Nāmārtha-sudhā* of Śrī Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa which it quotes: *varāṅga* — a body bearing the marks of a *mahāpuruṣa*; *candanāṅgadī* — wearing sandal armlets that gladden the hearts of the devotees; *śama* — free from worldly attraction (Baladeva: contemplating the secrets of Hari); *śānta* — whose mind is fixed on Kṛṣṇa alone; *niṣṭhā* — steadfastness, one-pointedness of mind (Baladeva: in Him the sacrifice of Hari-kīrtana is established); *śānti* — peace (Baladeva: He subdues the opponents of bhakti, the *kevalādvaitins*); *parāyaṇa* — the highest shelter (Baladeva: of all the moods up to *mahābhāva*). The source is given as in the edition.

## 50
w: clearly  making  in the Bhāgavata  it says  time  after time
w: in Kali-yuga —  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  congregational chanting  the essence
tr: The *Bhāgavatam* makes it clear again and again: in the age of Kali the congregational chanting of Kṛṣṇa’s name is the essence of all.

## 51
w: Kṛṣṇa  describing (or: with the syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’)  by lustre  not dark
w: with limbs,  sub-limbs,  weapons  and associates
w: by sacrifices  in which saṅkīrtana  predominates
w: they worship  indeed  the intelligent
tr: “The wise worship Him with sacrifices in which congregational chanting predominates — Him who ever sings of Kṛṣṇa and whose lustre is not dark. He is always surrounded by His intimate companions (*aṅga*), His devotees (*upāṅga*), His weapons and His associates (*pārṣada*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 11.5.32)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *kṛṣṇa-varṇam* — “who sings of Kṛṣṇa”, or “in whom are the two syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’” (3.53); *tviṣākṛṣṇam* — not dark by lustre, that is, golden: of the white, red and yellow (3.36) the third remains; the *aṅgas* are Nityānanda and Advaita, the *upāṅgas* Śrīvāsa and the other devotees, the weapons the holy name and the like, the associates Gadādhara, Svarūpa Dāmodara and others; *saṅkīrtana* is many people singing together the names of Hari and His glories. That He is Kṛṣṇa Himself the author shows below (3.79–80).

## 52
w: hear,  brothers,  this  all  of Caitanya  the glory
w: in this  verse  it tells  His  of glory  the limit
tr: Hear, brothers, of all this glory of Caitanya: this verse tells the very height of His greatness.

## 53
w: ‘Kṛṣṇa’  these  two  syllables  always  in whose  mouth
w: or,  Kṛṣṇa  He  describes  in His own  joy
tr: The two syllables ‘Kṛṣ-ṇa’ are always in His mouth; or else, in His own joy He describes Kṛṣṇa.

## 54
w: of the word ‘kṛṣṇa-varṇa’  the meaning  two  thus  are proved
w: Kṛṣṇa  except  His  to the mouth  not  comes  anything else
tr: Thus the two meanings of the word *kṛṣṇa-varṇa* are established: nothing but Kṛṣṇa ever comes to His lips.

## 55
w: anyone  Him  calls  if  dark in colour
w: the next  by the adjective  that  makes  refutation
tr: And if anyone should say that He is dark in colour, the next adjective rules this out.

## 56
w: by the body’s lustre  is  He  not dark  in colour
w: by ‘not dark in colour’  His  it tells  yellow  colour
tr: By the lustre of His body He is not dark; and by the words “not dark” the verse tells of His yellow colour.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «অকৃষ্ণবরণে তাঁর কহে» (in other copies «তাঁর» is missing).
