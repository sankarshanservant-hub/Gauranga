fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Ācāryaratna is Candraśekhara; Ācāryanidhi is Puṇḍarīka Vidyānidhi, also called Premanidhi.

tr: This is the sign of *anurāga* (deep, spontaneous love): it does not heed rules (*vidhi*). For the sake of His company it breaks His order.

tr: Kṛṣṇa is pleased when His order is kept; but when it is broken out of love, His joy is nourished a millionfold.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *tailāmra* is mango pickle kept in mustard oil. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *purāṇa śuktā* — dried bitter jute greens.

## 17

tr: Do not look down on it because it is only *śuktā*: the joy found in *śuktā* is not found even in *pañcāmṛta*.
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *śuktāya ye sukha haya, nahe pañcāmṛte*; another reading is *sukutāya ye sukha prabhura, tāhā nahe pañcāmṛte* (“the Lord’s joy in *sukutā* is not found in *pañcāmṛta*”).

## 18
w: who accepts the feeling  Mahāprabhu  the affection  alone  accepts
w: in śuktā leaves and kāśandi  great joy  there is
tr: Mahāprabhu receives the feeling (*bhāva-grāhī*); He takes only the affection. So the *śuktā* leaves and *kāśandi* gave Him great joy.
fn: Edition heading: the transcendental Bhagavān accepts the feeling. *Anubhāṣya*: *bhāva* here is a transcendental, causeless movement of the heart, made of pure goodness and aimed at pleasing Kṛṣṇa — not a mundane feeling that seeks its own pleasure.

## 19
w: human  regard  Damayantī  holds  the Lord’s  toward the feet
w: from heavy eating  in the stomach  sometimes  āma  becoming  goes
tr: Damayantī, in her affection, regarded the Lord as a human being (*manuṣya-buddhi*): “From heavy food, *āma* (indigestion) may sometimes form in His stomach.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: to see Him as a human being (*manuṣya-buddhi*) is the consciousness of the residents of Gauḍa and Vraja: their hearts, made of pure goodness, know nothing of His majesty, and they know Gaura-Kṛṣṇa in His human form as won over by their pure love alone. *Āma* is an acid-bilious disorder that comes from indigestion.

## 20
w: śuktā  if He eats  that  āma  will be  destroyed
w: that  affection  in His mind  considering  the Lord’s  delight
tr: If He eats *śuktā*, that *āma* will be cured.” Thinking of her affection, the Lord was delighted.

## 21

w: the garland  not  a certain (woman)  cast off  soiled with water
w: dwell  for  in love  virtues  not  in the thing
tr: “Her beloved strung a garland himself and placed it on her breast in the presence of her rivals; and though it was soiled with water, the full-bosomed maiden did not cast it off — for virtues dwell in love, not in the thing.” (Kirātārjunīya, 8.20)
fn: Edition heading: a verse from Bhāravi’s *Kirātārjunīya* (canto 8, verse 20); only what is offered with love is full of virtue, and in a gift of love outer faults and merits are not weighed. *Anubhāṣya*: only a thing offered with love has virtue; any other, even a good one, is without it — and love has no need to test the thing.

tr: Knowing that the devotees were tired, He brought the kīrtana to an end, went with them all and bathed in the sea.

## 81
w: all  with  the Lord  made  prasāda  eating
w: to all  leave  He gave  to take  rest
tr: The Lord took prasāda with them all and then sent everyone off to rest.

## 82
w: of the Gambhīrā  at the door  makes  Himself  lying down
w: Govinda  coming  does  massaging of the feet
tr: He Himself lay down at the door of the Gambhīrā, and Govinda came to massage His feet.
