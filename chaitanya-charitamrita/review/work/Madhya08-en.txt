fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Brahmā, whose pride Kṛṣṇa had crushed, surrenders to Him and offers this prayer. “Remaining where they are” — on the path of the sādhus; the accounts of You — of Your name, form, qualities and pastimes, flowing naturally from the lips of the great devotees. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: they live keeping to the path of the sādhus with body, mind and words, and He who is hard to reach in all three worlds becomes easily accessible to them.

## 69

fn: Heading of the edition: “Only the devotee’s love wins Kṛṣṇa”; the verse is Rāmānanda Rāya’s own, quoted in the *Padyāvalī* (13).
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: worship with the sixteen offerings melts the devotee’s heart with bliss only when there is prema in it — the bhakti that delights Kṛṣṇa.

## 70

tr: “A mind imbued with the *rasa* of devotion to Kṛṣṇa — buy it at once, if it can be found anywhere! Its only price is eagerness (*laulya*); it cannot be had for the pious deeds of ten million births.” (Padyāvalī, 14)
fn: Heading of the edition: “Love for Kṛṣṇa is attained only on the path of rāga; vaidhī-bhakti, born of piety, cannot reach it”; the verse is Rāmānanda Rāya’s own (*Padyāvalī*, 14).
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: wherever such a mind is found — in whatever place, time, person or practice — it must be bought, and its only price is eagerness. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the first of the two verses (69) points to *vaidhī-bhakti*, rooted in faith, the second to *rāgānugā-bhakti*, rooted in greed (*lobha*); from here on Rāmānanda speaks only of rāga-bhakti.
