# English translation, Ādi-līlā 6 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: Determining the Truth of Śrī Advaita

## 1
w: I bow  to Him  Śrī Advaita-
w: Ācārya  wondrous  (whose) deeds
w: by whose  grace  an ignorant one  even
w: His  true nature  may describe
tr: I bow to Śrī Advaita Ācārya, whose deeds are wondrous. By His grace even an ignorant person may describe His true nature.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī Caitanya  glory  to Nityānanda
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!

## 3
w: in five  verses  I have told  of Śrī Nityānanda  the truth
w: in two verses  I tell  of Advaita Ācārya  the greatness
tr: In five verses I have told the truth of Śrī Nityānanda; now in two verses I tell the greatness of Advaita Ācārya.

## 4
w: Mahā-Viṣṇu  of the world  the maker
w: by māyā  who  creates  this (world)
w: His  avatāra  indeed  this
w: Advaita Ācārya  the Lord
tr: Mahā-Viṣṇu is the maker of the world, who creates it through māyā; and this Lord Advaita Ācārya is His avatāra.
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.12. The edition ascribes verses 6.4–6.5 to the diary of Svarūpa Dāmodara («শ্রীস্বরূপগোস্বামি-কড়চায় শ্লোক»; heading: “explanation of the twelfth and thirteenth of the first fourteen verses of the Ādi”). *Anubhāṣya*: Mahā-Viṣṇu, the maker of the world, is the shelter of the efficient cause (*nimitta*), and His avatāra, the Lord Advaita Ācārya, is the shelter of the material cause (*upādāna*).

## 5
w: Advaita  from Hari  being non-different
w: Ācārya  bhakti  from teaching
w: as a devotee descended  the Lord  Him
w: Advaita Ācārya  I take shelter of
tr: He is called Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, and Ācārya because He teaches bhakti. I take shelter of Him, the Lord Advaita Ācārya, who has descended as a devotee.
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.13. *Anubhāṣya*: He is Advaita because He is non-different from Hari, from the Viṣṇu-tattva; He is Ācārya because He instructs others in bhajana.

## 6
w: Advaita  Ācārya  Gosāñi  Himself  the Lord
w: whose  greatness  not  of the jīva  within the range
tr: Advaita Ācārya Gosāñi is the Lord Himself; His greatness lies beyond the reach of the jīva.

## 7
w: Mahā-Viṣṇu  creation  performs  of the world  and other  works
w: His  avatāra  direct  Advaita  Ācārya
tr: Mahā-Viṣṇu performs the creation of the world and its other works, and Advaita Ācārya is His direct avatāra.

## 8
w: which  Puruṣa  creation and maintenance  performs  through māyā
w: endless  universes  creation  performs  in play
tr: That Puruṣa creates and maintains the world through māyā, and in His play He creates endless universes.

## 9
w: by His will  endless  forms  He makes  manifest
w: in one  one  form (each)  He makes  into a universe  entrance
tr: By His will He manifests endless forms, and in one form each He enters every universe.

## 10
w: of that  Puruṣa  a part  Advaita  there is not  any  difference
w: a particular body  His  there is no  separation
tr: Advaita is a part (*aṁśa*) of that Puruṣa, and there is no difference between Them at all: He is only another body of the Puruṣa, and the two are never apart.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: one and the same māyā is called *pradhāna* as the material cause and *māyā* as the efficient cause. According to these two functions Mahā-Viṣṇu abides in two forms: in one, abiding in prakṛti, He is the efficient cause of the world — the form of Viṣṇu; in the other, abiding in pradhāna, He is Advaita in the form of Rudra. Therefore Advaita is in no way different from the Puruṣa; only Their bodies differ.

## 11
w: help  He gives  to Him  taking  pradhāna
w: millions  of universes  He makes  by His will  the fashioning
tr: Taking pradhāna, the material basis of the world, Advaita assists the Puruṣa, and by His will He fashions millions of universes.
fn: The edition’s heading: “The Deity who presides over pradhāna, the material cause of the world.”

## 12
w: of the world  the good  Advaita  of auspicious  qualities  the abode
w: auspicious  conduct  always  ‘Maṅgala’  whose  name
tr: Advaita is the good of the whole world and the abode of all auspicious qualities. His conduct is ever auspicious, and His name is Maṅgala, “the Auspicious”.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Śrī Advaita is Mahā-Viṣṇu, and He is the Ācārya; His life, turned towards service, brings good to all; He is no material being and no shelter of the material guṇas. There were people of demoniac nature, bewildered by māyā and devoid of bhakti, who could not understand Him as the non-dual Viṣṇu and, clinging to the doctrine of bare non-duality (*kevalādvaita*), only pretended to follow Him; by His māyā the Ācārya let them feed their pride — and even this punishment of the faithless is nothing but auspicious conduct. Some say that Śrī Advaita’s other name was Maṅgala.

## 13
w: millions  of parts  millions  of potencies  millions  of avatāras
w: so many  taking  creates  the Puruṣa  the whole  world
tr: Taking millions of parts, millions of potencies and millions of avatāras, the Puruṣa creates the whole world.

## 14
w: māyā  as  two  aspects  efficient  material
w: māyā  the efficient  cause  the material  pradhāna
tr: Māyā has two aspects, the efficient cause (*nimitta*) and the material cause (*upādāna*): as the efficient cause it is called māyā, and as the material cause, pradhāna.

## 15
w: the Puruṣa  the Lord  likewise  two forms  becoming
w: of the universe  creation  performs  the efficient  the material  taking
tr: In the same way the Lord, the Puruṣa, assumes two forms and creates the universe with these two causes, the efficient and the material.

## 16
w: Himself  the Puruṣa  of the world  the efficient  cause
w: of Advaita  in the form  the material cause  becomes  Nārāyaṇa
tr: The Puruṣa Himself is the efficient cause of the world, and in the form of Advaita, Nārāyaṇa becomes its material cause.

## 17
w: in the efficient aspect  performs  He  upon māyā  the glance
w: as the material cause  Advaita  performs  of the universe  the creation
tr: As the efficient cause He casts His glance upon māyā; as the material cause, Advaita creates the universe.

## 18
w: although  Sāṅkhya  holds  pradhāna  the cause
w: from inert matter  never  is  of the world  the creation
tr: Although Sāṅkhya holds pradhāna to be the cause, the world can never come into being from inert matter.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 6.15) examines the Sāṅkhya doctrine at length and, following the *Vedānta-sūtra* (2.2.1–10) and Baladeva Vidyābhūṣaṇa’s *Govinda-bhāṣya*, shows that inert pradhāna can be neither the material nor the efficient cause of the world, for a varied creation is impossible without a conscious principle. It adds that the Kapila who founded the Sāṅkhya opposed to the Vedas is not Lord Kapila, the son of Kardama and avatāra of Vāsudeva, but another — a jīva of the line of Agni, bewildered by māyā. On 6.18–6.19 the *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 5.58–5.66 and Madhya 20.259–261, 271, 276.

## 19
w: His own  creative power  the Lord  infuses  into pradhāna
w: of the Lord  by the power  then  takes place  indeed  the fashioning
tr: The Lord infuses His own creative power into pradhāna, and then, by the Lord’s power, the world is fashioned.

## 20
w: Advaita  Ācārya  of millions of universes  the creator
w: and  in one  one  form (each)  of the universe  the sustainer
tr: Advaita Ācārya is the creator of millions of universes, and in a form of His within each of them He is its sustainer.
fn: The edition’s heading: “The two forms of Advaita Prabhu.” The form in which the Puruṣa enters each universe (6.9) is Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu.

## 21
w: of that  Nārāyaṇa  the principal  limb  Advaita
w: ‘aṅga’  by the word  part  meaning  says  the Bhāgavata
tr: Advaita is the principal limb (*aṅga*) of Śrī Nārāyaṇa; and in the *Bhāgavatam* the word ‘aṅga’ means ‘aṁśa’, a partial manifestation.
fn: The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 3.67 (“‘aṅga’ means ‘aṁśa’”).

## 22
w: Nārāyaṇa  You  not  indeed  of all  the embodied
w: the Self  You are  O Lord  of all  worlds  the witness
w: Nārāyaṇa  (Your) limb  from Nara  born  waters  abode
w: that  also  true  not  Your  indeed  māyā
tr: “Are You not Nārāyaṇa? For You are the Self of all embodied beings, O Lord, and the witness of all the worlds. Nārāyaṇa is Your portion — so called because His abode is the waters born of Nara; and that too is true, not Your māyā.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 10.14.14)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 2.30 and 3.68 (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “see Ādi 2.30”, where the *Anubhāṣya* explains the three meanings of the name Nārāyaṇa). Here the author cites it for the word *aṅgam*, “part”: Nārāyaṇa is Your *aṅga*, that is, Your *aṁśa* (6.21).

## 23
w: of the Lord  the aṅga  the aṁśa  full of consciousness and bliss
w: with māyā  connection  there is not  this  verse  says
tr: This verse says that the Lord’s aṅga, His aṁśa, is full of consciousness and bliss and has no connection with māyā.
fn: The edition’s heading: “Whether aṅga or aṁśa, beyond māyā.” The *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 3.69–3.70.

## 24
w: ‘aṁśa’  not  saying  why  do you call  Him  ‘aṅga’
w: than ‘aṁśa’  ‘aṅga’  because  is  more intimate
tr: Why call Him ‘aṅga’ and not ‘aṁśa’? Because ‘aṅga’, a limb, is closer and more intimate than ‘aṁśa’, a part.

## 25
w: of Mahā-Viṣṇu  a part  Advaita  of qualities  the abode
w: from the Lord  non-different  therefore  ‘Advaita’  full  name
tr: Advaita, the abode of all good qualities, is a part of Mahā-Viṣṇu. He is non-different from the Lord, and so the name Advaita, “Non-dual”, fits Him in full.
fn: The edition’s heading: “The meaning of the name ‘Advaita’.” *Pūrṇa nāma* — “a full name”, a name true in every respect.

## 26
w: before  as  He performed  of the whole  universe  the creation
w: having descended  He performed  now  of bhakti  the establishment
tr: As once He created the whole universe, so now, having descended, He has established bhakti.

## 27
w: the jīvas  He delivered  devotion to Kṛṣṇa  making  a gift
w: in the Gītā and Bhāgavata  He gave  of bhakti  the explanation
tr: He delivered the jīvas by giving them devotion to Kṛṣṇa, and He explained the *Gītā* and the *Bhāgavatam* in the light of bhakti.

## 28
w: bhakti  teaching  except  His  there is no  work
w: therefore  name  became  ‘Advaita Ācārya’
tr: He has no work but teaching bhakti; therefore His name became Advaita Ācārya.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (on 6.26–6.28): although Advaita Prabhu is Viṣṇu-tattva, the One who is served, He has no work but the good of the souls — awakening in them the desire to serve. Had He shown His pastimes only as the One who is served, the pleasure-seeking people of the world would have imitated Him and become godless adherents of bare non-duality, or worshippers of themselves as God; seeing this, He displayed the pastime of a servant of Viṣṇu, and that is His being the Ācārya. As for the wicked who defile the holy place and dress of the Ācārya and, instead of serving the Lord, dance the dance of sense-pleasure, the Ācārya utterly rejects them.

## 29
w: of the Vaiṣṇavas  the guru  He  of the world  the venerable
w: two names  by joining  became  ‘Advaita Ācārya’
tr: He is the guru of the Vaiṣṇavas and the venerable one of the whole world. Joined together, these two names became Advaita Ācārya.

## 30
w: of the Lotus-eyed  He  because  ‘aṅga’  ‘aṁśa’
w: ‘Kamalākṣa’  calling  He bears  the name  as a crown-jewel
tr: Because He is the limb and the part of the Lotus-eyed Lord, He bears the beautiful name Kamalākṣa, “Lotus-eyed”.
fn: The edition’s heading: “The meaning of the name ‘Kamalākṣa’.” *Avataṁsa* — “a crown, an ornament”: Kamalākṣa is the loveliest of names.

## 31
w: with the Lord  sameness of form  attain  the associates
w: four-armed  yellow-clad  just as  Nārāyaṇa
tr: The Lord’s associates attain a form like His (*sārūpya*): like Nārāyaṇa, they are four-armed and clad in yellow.

## 32
w: Advaita  Ācārya  of the Lord  part  the foremost
w: His  truth  name  qualities  all  wondrous
tr: Advaita Ācārya is the foremost part of the Lord; His truth, His name and His qualities are all wondrous.

## 33
w: by whose  tulasī  leaves  by whose  roar
w: His associates  together with  Caitanya’s  descent
tr: By His offering of tulasī leaves and by His roar, Caitanya descended together with His associates.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «তুলসীদলে» (“with tulasī leaves”; another reading: «তুলসীজলে», “with tulasī and water”). The edition’s heading: “Advaita Prabhu calls Mahāprabhu down.” On 6.33–6.34 the *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 3.91 and 3.95–3.108, where Advaita worships Kṛṣṇa with tulasī leaves and Ganges water.

## 34
w: through whom  made  the Lord  of kīrtana  the spreading
w: through whom  made  the Lord  of the world  the deliverance
tr: Through Him the Lord spread the kīrtana, and through Him the Lord delivered the world.

## 35
w: of Ācārya  Gosāñi  of the qualities  the glory  boundless
w: the worm-like jīva  where  will find  of it  the end
tr: The glory of Ācārya Gosāñi’s qualities is boundless. Where can the jīva, a mere worm, find its end?

## 36
w: Ācārya  Gosāñi  Caitanya’s  principal  limb
w: another  one  limb  His  Prabhu  Nityānanda
tr: Ācārya Gosāñi is the principal limb (*aṅga*) of Caitanya, and His other limb is Nityānanda Prabhu.
fn: The edition’s heading: “One limb of Gaura is Advaita, the other is Nitāi.” On 6.36–6.38 the *Anubhāṣya* refers to Ādi 3.71–3.74.

## 37
w: the Lord’s  lesser limbs  Śrīvāsa and the other  devotees
w: hands  face  eyes  limbs  disc and other  weapons  like
tr: The Lord’s lesser limbs (*upāṅga*) are Śrīvāsa and the other devotees; they are like His hands, face and eyes, and like His disc and other weapons.

## 38
w: with all these  taking  Caitanya Prabhu’s  pastimes
w: with all these  taking  He makes  of His desire  the spreading
tr: With them Caitanya Prabhu performs His pastimes, and with them He spreads what He desires.

## 39
w: of Mādhavendra Purī  He  the disciple  with this  understanding
w: Ācārya Gosāñi  the Lord  as guru  regards
tr: Knowing that He is a disciple of Mādhavendra Purī, the Lord regards Ācārya Gosāñi as His guru.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Advaita Prabhu is a disciple of Mādhavendra Purī, and His godbrother Īśvara Purī is Mahāprabhu’s guru; that is why Mahāprabhu honours the Ācārya as a guru. In truth Śrī Caitanya is the Lord of all and Advaita is His servant. *Anubhāṣya*: Mādhavendra Purī is one of the gurus of the sampradāya of Śrī Madhvācārya, and his disciples are Īśvara Purī and Śrī Advaita; the line of succession is cited from the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*.

## 40
w: in worldly  pastimes  of dharma’s  bounds  the keeping
w: with praise and devotion  He makes  His  to the feet  obeisance
tr: In His worldly pastimes the Lord keeps the bounds of dharma: with praise and devotion He bows at the Ācārya’s feet.

## 41
w: Caitanya Gosāñi  the Ācārya  holds as  ‘Master’
w: Himself  He holds  His  ‘servant’  in self-conception
tr: The Ācārya knows Caitanya Gosāñi as His Master, and He thinks of Himself as His servant.

## 42
w: in that  self-conception’s  happiness  Himself  He forgets
w: ‘Kṛṣṇa’s servant’  become  to the jīvas  instruction  He gives
tr: In the happiness of that feeling He forgets Himself, and He teaches the jīvas, “Become servants of Kṛṣṇa!”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: accepting the mood of a devotee, Mahā-Viṣṇu sets aside the sense of His own majesty and finds bliss in serving the Lord as His duty; in that bliss of service His sense of being the enjoyer recedes. By His own example He moves the souls to serve Kṛṣṇa at every moment.

## 43
w: of Kṛṣṇa’s servant  in the self-conception  which  of bliss  the ocean
w: millions of times  Brahman’s  happiness  is not  of it  one  drop
tr: Such is the ocean of bliss in feeling oneself Kṛṣṇa’s servant that the happiness of Brahman, multiplied ten million times, is not equal to a single drop of it.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: the happiness of Brahman is the happiness of the notion of non-difference, “I am Brahman.” The *Anubhāṣya* cites the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu* (1.1.38): “Even if the bliss of Brahman were multiplied a parārdha times, it would not equal an atom of the ocean of the happiness of bhakti.”

## 44
w: I  indeed  Caitanya’s servant  and  Nityānanda
w: of the servant’s mood  equal  there is not  elsewhere  bliss
tr: “I and Nityānanda are servants of Caitanya. Nowhere is there bliss equal to the mood of a servant.”

## 45
w: supreme  beloved  Lakṣmī  on His heart  dwells
w: she  of service  the happiness  begs  making  entreaty
tr: Lakṣmī, His dearest beloved, dwells upon His heart, yet she too begs with humble entreaty for the happiness of service.

## 46
w: in the mood of service  rejoice  the associates
w: Brahmā  Śiva  Nārada and others  Śuka  Sanātana (and the other Kumāras)
tr: In the mood of service His associates rejoice — Brahmā, Śiva, Nārada and others, Śuka, and Sanātana with his brothers.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «নারদাদি» (“Nārada and others”; another reading: «নারদার»). The edition’s heading: “(2) The associates of Viṣṇu, and Brahmā, Śiva, the four Sanas, Nārada, Śuka and others too, are servants of Kṛṣṇa.”

## 47
w: Nityānanda  Avadhūta  among all  the foremost
w: Caitanya’s  in the love of service  became  mad
tr: Nityānanda Avadhūta is the foremost of all; in His love of serving Caitanya He became mad.
fn: As in the Gauḍīya Maṭha edition: «হইল» (another reading: «হইলা»); the sense is the same. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: *āgala* — “the foremost”.

## 48–49
w: Śrīvāsa  Haridāsa  Rāmadāsa  Gadādhara
w: Murāri  Mukunda  Candraśekhara  Vakreśvara
w: all these  learned  men  of supreme  greatness
w: Caitanya’s  service  all of them  makes  mad
tr: Śrīvāsa, Haridāsa, Rāmadāsa, Gadādhara, Murāri, Mukunda, Candraśekhara and Vakreśvara — all these are learned men of the highest greatness, and service to Caitanya makes every one of them mad.

## 50
w: in this way  they sing  dance  make  loud laughter
w: people  they teach  ‘become  Caitanya’s  servants’
tr: So they sing, dance and laugh aloud, and they teach everyone: “Become servants of Caitanya!”

## 51
w: Caitanya Gosāñi  Me  holds  as guru
w: still  My  is  ‘servant’  self-conception
tr: “Caitanya Gosāñi regards Me as His guru, yet I feel Myself His servant.”

## 52
w: of Kṛṣṇa-prema  this  one  unprecedented  power
w: elders  equals  juniors  makes feel  the mood of service
tr: This is one wondrous power of love for Kṛṣṇa: it gives elders, equals and juniors alike the mood of a servant.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “elders” are those related to Kṛṣṇa in parental love, “equals” — the friends, “juniors” — those beneath them; love for Kṛṣṇa gives all three the mood of service. So the elders, equals and juniors of Kṛṣṇa Caitanya are all His servants.

## 53
w: of this  the proof  hear  of the scriptures  the explanation
w: of great souls  the experience  in which  firm  proof
tr: Hear the proof of this from the scriptures, in which the experience of great souls is firm evidence.

## 54–55
w: of others  what  to speak  in Vraja  Nanda  Mahāśaya
w: his  equal  elder  of Kṛṣṇa  other  anyone  is not
w: in pure  parental love  of God  the knowledge  is not  his
w: him  even  love  makes feel  of service  a semblance
tr: What to speak of others? In Vraja Kṛṣṇa has no elder equal to Nanda Mahāśaya. In his pure parental love he has no thought that Kṛṣṇa is God; yet love makes even him feel something like a servant.
fn: The edition’s heading: “(5) Even in Nanda Mahārāja’s parental love there is service to Kṛṣṇa.” *Guru* here means “elder” (*Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* on 6.52).

## 56
w: he  attraction  devotion  begs  at Kṛṣṇa’s  feet
w: his  holy  lips’  words  of that  the proof
tr: He too begs at Kṛṣṇa’s feet for attachment and devotion; his own words are the proof of it.

## 57–58
w: hear  Uddhava  truly  Kṛṣṇa  my  son
w: He  God  thus  if  your  mind  takes
w: still  in Him  may remain  my  mind’s workings
w: in your  God  Kṛṣṇa  may be  my  thought
tr: “Listen, Uddhava: Kṛṣṇa is truly my son. But if it seems to you that He is God, then still let all the workings of my mind rest in Him; let my thoughts be fixed on your God, Kṛṣṇa.”
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* on 6.58: “O Uddhava, even if you think that Kṛṣṇa is God, may my mind rest in that Kṛṣṇa.” Nanda prays for attachment to the Kṛṣṇa whom Uddhava calls God — this is the “semblance of service” (6.55) of which the author speaks.
