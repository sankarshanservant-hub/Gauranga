## 0
tr: The Description of Śrī Haridāsa’s Departure

## 1
w: I bow  to Haridāsa  to him
w: to Caitanya  to Him  and  his Lord
w: departed  though  whose  body
w: on His own lap  placing  danced  who
tr: I bow to Haridāsa and to his Lord, Caitanya, who took the body of the departed Haridāsa on His lap and danced.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: *saṁsthitām* — gone into *samādhi*. Heading in the edition: obeisance to Gaura, who danced holding Haridāsa’s body in His arms.

## 2
w: glory  glory  to Śrī  Caitanya  glory  to the all-merciful
w: glory to Him dear to Advaita  dear to Nityānanda  glory
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Caitanya! Glory to the all-merciful Lord! Glory to Him who is dear to Advaita and dear to Nityānanda!

## 3
w: glory  to the Lord of Śrīnivāsa  the master of Haridāsa
w: glory  dear to Gadādhara  the Lord of Svarūpa’s life
tr: Glory to the Lord of Śrīnivāsa, the master of Haridāsa! Glory to Him who is dear to Gadādhara, the Lord of Svarūpa’s life!

## 4
w: glory  dear to Kāśīśvara  the Lord of Jagadānanda’s life
w: glory  the Lord of Rūpa, Sanātana and Raghunātha
tr: Glory to Him who is dear to Kāśīśvara, the Lord of Jagadānanda’s life! Glory to the Lord of Rūpa, Sanātana and Raghunātha!
fn: The Gauḍīya Maṭha edition reads *kāśīśvara-priya*, “dear to Kāśīśvara” (a disciple of Īśvara Purī and servant of Mahāprabhu; cf. verse 84); some editions read *kāśī-priya*.

## 5

w: mercy  showing  give  O Lord  of Your own feet  the gift
tr: Glory to Kṛṣṇa of golden form, the Supreme Lord Himself! O Lord, be merciful and grant me the shelter of Your feet.
fn: *Gaura-deha* qualifies Kṛṣṇa. *Anubhāṣya*: *gaura-deha* — He whose body has a golden lustre.

## 6
w: glory  Nityānanda the moon  Caitanya’s  life
w: Your  at the lotus feet  devotion  give  as a gift
tr: Glory to Nityānandacandra, the life of Caitanya! Grant me devotion to Your lotus feet.

## 7
w: glory  glory to Advaitacandra  by Caitanya  honoured
w: at Your own feet  devotion  give  glory to Advaitācārya
tr: Glory to Advaitacandra, whom Caitanya honours! Glory to Advaitācārya! Grant me devotion to Your feet.

## 8
w: glory  Gaura’s  devotees  all  Gaura  whose  life
w: all  devotees  together  to me  devotion  give  as a gift
tr: Glory to all the devotees of Gaura, whose very life is Gaura! All you devotees, together grant me devotion.

## 9
w: glory  to Rūpa  Sanātana  Jīva  Raghunātha
w: Raghunātha  Gopāla  six  my  lords of life
tr: Glory to Rūpa, Sanātana, Jīva, Raghunātha, Raghunātha and Gopāla! These six are the lords of my life.

## 10
w: of all these  by the mercy  I write  Caitanya’s  pastimes  qualities
w: somehow  or other  I write  I make  myself  pure
tr: By the mercy of them all I write of the pastimes and qualities of Śrī Caitanya. However I may write, I do it to purify myself.
fn: The first line is the same as Ādi 9.5; translated as there.

## 11
w: thus  Mahāprabhu’s  in Nīlācala  residence
w: with Him  the devotees  taking  the pastimes of kīrtana
tr: Thus Mahāprabhu lived in Nīlācala, enjoying the pastimes of kīrtana with His devotees.

tr: By day He danced and sang kīrtana and beheld Lord Jagannātha; by night He relished rasa with Rāya and Svarūpa.
fn: The second line is the same as Antya 9.6. Heading in the edition: at night, with Svarūpa and Rāmānanda, the Lord relishes the rasa of Śrī Rādhā’s love for Kṛṣṇa.

tr: Day by day these transformations grew, and at night they became intense: anxiety, agitation, delirious talk and all the others the scriptures describe.

tr: He saw that Haridāsa Ṭhākura was lying down, very slowly chanting his fixed number of names.

## 19

tr: My counted chanting is not complete; how can I eat? Yet you have brought mahā-prasāda; how can I disregard it?”

## 21
w: the next  day  Mahāprabhu  his  place  came to

tr: The next day Mahāprabhu came to him and asked, “Haridāsa, are you well?”

## 22

tr: He bowed down and replied, “My body is well, but my mind and intelligence are not.”

## 23

w: he  says  the counted  chanting  not  is completed
tr: The Lord said, “What is the illness? Tell Me exactly.” He said, “I cannot complete my counted chanting.”

## 24

w: perfect  in body  you are  for sādhana  eagerness  why  do you show
tr: The Lord said, “You have grown old; reduce the number of your names. You are ever perfect, even in body (*siddha-deha*); why are you so intent on sādhana?
fn: Heading in the edition: the Lord tells Haridāsa, whose body is perfect and not of this world, to lessen the sādhana that he only enacts as an example (*sādhana-abhinaya*).

## 25
w: the people  to deliver  this  your  descent
w: of the name  the glory  in the world  you have made  preaching
tr: You have descended to deliver the people, and you have preached the glory of the holy name in the world.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: by the Lord’s will His devotees and associates descend to the earth to serve Him.

## 26
w: now  small  the number  making  do  saṅkīrtana
w: Haridāsa  says  hear  my  submission
tr: Now reduce your number and perform saṅkīrtana.” Haridāsa said, “Please hear my request.

## 27
w: in a low caste  birth  my  contemptible  body
w: in low deeds  engaged  I  the lowest  wretch
tr: I was born in a low caste, and my body is contemptible. I am given to low deeds — I am the lowest of wretches.

## 28
w: unfit to be seen  unfit to be touched  me  acceptance  You made
w: Raurava  from  snatching  me  to Vaikuṇṭha  You raised
tr: I am unfit to be seen or touched, yet You accepted me. You snatched me out of the hell of Raurava and raised me up to Vaikuṇṭha.

## 29
w: independent  Lord  You  are  acting at will
w: the world  You make dance  whom  as  wish  there is
tr: You are the independent Lord and act as You will. You make the world dance — each one as You please.

## 30
w: much  You made dance  me  grace  bestowing
w: of brāhmaṇas  the śrāddha plate  I ate  a mleccha  being
tr: By Your grace You have made me dance in many ways: though a *mleccha*, I ate the *śrāddha* plate meant for brāhmaṇas.
fn: See Antya 3.220. *Anubhāṣya*: by the smṛti the *śrāddha* plate is not given even to a brāhmaṇa by birth if he is unworthy; here the plate meant for a pure brāhmaṇa was given to Haridāsa, a brāhmaṇa by initiation: though born among *mlecchas*, he had the right to it as a servant of Hari (*hari-jana*).

## 31
w: one  desire  there is  my  many  days  since
w: the pastimes  will wind up  You  feels  my  heart
tr: For many days I have had one desire. My heart tells me that You will soon wind up Your pastimes.

## 32
w: that  pastime  O Lord  to me  ever  not  show

tr: Lord, never let me see that pastime. Let my body fall before You.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: “that pastime” — the pastime of Your disappearance (*antardhāna-līlā*). Heading in the edition: Haridāsa wishes to end his pastimes before the Lord’s departure.

## 33
w: on my heart  I shall hold  Your  lotus  feet
w: with my eyes  I shall see  Your  moonlike  face
tr: I shall hold Your lotus feet to my heart and gaze with my eyes upon Your moonlike face.

w: with my tongue  I shall utter  Your  ‘Kṛṣṇa-Caitanya’  name
w: in this way  my  desire  I shall leave  my life
tr: With my tongue I shall utter Your name, “Kṛṣṇa Caitanya.” This is my desire — to give up my life in this way.

## 35
w: my  this  desire  if  by Your  grace  comes true

tr: If by Your grace this desire of mine may come true, then grant this request of mine, O merciful Lord.

## 36
w: this  low  body  my  may it fall  You  before
w: this  fulfilment of desire  my  on You alone  depends

## 37

w: Kṛṣṇa  merciful  that  surely  will do
tr: The Lord said, “Haridāsa, whatever you ask, merciful Kṛṣṇa will surely grant.

## 38

w: for you  fitting  it is not  you will go  Me  leaving
tr: But whatever happiness I have, I have it all with you. It is not fitting for you to go away and leave Me.”

## 39

tr: Clasping His feet, Haridāsa said, “Lord, do not deceive me with *māyā*; surely show this fallen one this mercy (*dayā*).
fn: Heading in the edition: Haridāsa begs the Lord for mercy without guile (*niṣkapaṭa*); *māyā* here is the opposite of *dayā*.

## 40

tr: There are so many great souls, crest jewels far above me; millions of devotees assist in Your pastimes.

tr: If one insect like me is gone, what is lost? When an ant dies, what loss does the earth suffer?

tr: You are affectionate to Your devotees (*bhakata-vatsala*), and I am a mere semblance of a devotee. Surely, Lord, You will fulfil this hope of mine.”

w: whoever  here  dancing  did  whoever  did  kīrtana
w: whoever  on him  sand  to put  did  go
w: among them  at the festival  whoever  did  eat
w: soon  they  all  will have  the attainment of Kṛṣṇa
w: in seeing Haridāsa  there is  such  power
tr: “Whoever saw the festival of Haridāsa’s departure, whoever danced here or sang kīrtana, whoever went to put sand on him, and whoever among them ate at the festival — all of them will soon attain Kṛṣṇa. Such is the power of seeing Haridāsa.

## 94
w: mercy  showing  Kṛṣṇa  to Me  had given  company
w: independent  Kṛṣṇa’s  will  He made  the company broken
tr: Out of mercy Kṛṣṇa gave Me his company. Kṛṣṇa’s will is independent: now He has broken that company.

## 95
w: Haridāsa’s  wish  when  arose  to go
w: My  power  him  could not  keep
tr: When Haridāsa wished to go, My power could not keep him.

## 96
w: by mere wish  he made  his own life  go out
w: in the past  as  we have heard  Bhīṣma’s  departure
tr: By his mere wish he sent his life out of his body, just as we have heard of Bhīṣma’s departure long ago.

## 97
w: Haridāsa  was  of the earth  the crest jewel
w: him  without  bereft of its jewel  has become  the earth
tr: Haridāsa was the crest jewel of the earth; without him the earth has been robbed of its jewel.

## 98
w: glory  glory  Haridāsa  saying  make  the sound of Hari
w: so  saying  Mahāprabhu  dances  Himself
tr: “Call out ‘Glory, glory to Haridāsa!’ and raise the sound of Hari’s name!” Saying this, Mahāprabhu Himself began to dance.

## 99
w: all  sing  glory  glory  glory  Haridāsa
w: of the name  the glory  who  made  manifest
tr: Everyone sang, “Glory, glory, glory to Haridāsa, who revealed the glory of the holy name!”

## 100
w: then  Mahāprabhu  all  the devotees  leave  gave
w: in joy  in sorrow  the Lord  rest  took
tr: Then Mahāprabhu bade farewell to all the devotees and rested, in both joy and sorrow.

## 101
w: thus  indeed  I have told  of Haridāsa’s  departure (vijaya)
w: of which  by the hearing  for Kṛṣṇa  firm devotion  arises
tr: Thus I have told of Haridāsa’s departure (*vijaya*); by hearing of it one gains firm devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

## 102
w: Caitanya’s  affection for His devotees  from this very  we know
w: His devotee’s  desire  fulfilled  made  the crest jewel of sannyāsīs
tr: From this we know Caitanya’s affection for His devotees: He, the crest jewel of sannyāsīs, fulfilled His devotee’s desire.

## 103
w: at the last time  He gave  to him  sight and touch
w: him  on His lap  taking  did  Himself  dancing
tr: At his last hour the Lord let him see and touch Him, and taking him on His lap, He Himself danced.

## 104
w: Himself  with His holy hands  in mercy  on him  sand  He put
w: Himself  prasāda  begging  a festival  He held
tr: In His mercy He Himself put sand on him with His holy hands, and He Himself begged prasāda and held a great festival.

## 105
w: a mahā-bhāgavata  Haridāsa  supremely learned
w: this  good fortune  for the sake of  first  he made  his departure
tr: Haridāsa was a *mahā-bhāgavata* and supremely learned (*parama-vidvān*); for the sake of this good fortune he departed first.

## 106
w: Caitanya’s  deeds  these  of nectar  an ocean
w: ears and mind  satisfied  makes  of which  one  drop
tr: These deeds of Caitanya are an ocean of nectar; a single drop of it satisfies the ears and the mind.

## 107
w: the ocean of worldly existence  to cross  there is  whose  mind
w: faith  having  hear  that  account of Caitanya
tr: Whoever has set his heart on crossing the ocean of worldly existence should hear with faith this account of Caitanya.

## 108
w: Śrī  Rūpa’s,  Raghunātha’s  at the feet  whose  hope
w: “Caitanya-caritāmṛta”  narrates  Kṛṣṇadāsa
tr: Praying at the feet of Śrī Rūpa and Śrī Raghunātha, I, Kṛṣṇadāsa, narrate the *Caitanya-caritāmṛta*.
