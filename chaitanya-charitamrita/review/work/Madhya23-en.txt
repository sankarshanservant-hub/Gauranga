## 1
w: for long  not given  His own  hidden treasure
w: the nectar of His own love and name  the supremely generous
w: down to the lowest  who  distributed  Gaura

tr: I take refuge in Gaura-Kṛṣṇa, the supremely generous, who bestowed on all people, down to the lowliest, His own hidden treasure, not given for so long — the nectar of His love and His name.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: He gave it without distinguishing the pious from the sinful or the worthy from the unworthy. The edition’s heading: “Obeisance to Gaura, the most magnanimous, who gives the Name and prema never given before”.

w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  of Gaura  the devotees  to the multitude
tr: Glory, glory to Gauracandra! Glory to Nityānanda! Glory to Advaitacandra! Glory to all the devotees of Gaura!
fn: The same verse as Madhya 12.2 (the refrain of Ādi 4.2 with *gauracandra* in the first line); translation as there.

## 3
w: now  hear  of bhakti  the fruit  prema  the goal
w: of which  by hearing  there is  knowledge of bhakti-rasa
tr: “Now hear of the fruit of *bhakti*: *prema*, the ultimate goal (*prayojana*). By hearing of it one comes to know *bhakti-rasa*.
fn: The Lord’s instruction to Sanātana continues from Madhya 22. The edition’s heading: “Instructions to Śrī Sanātana — (3) the *prayojana*: the fruit of the *abhidheya*, *sādhana-bhakti*, is the *sādhya*, *prema-bhakti*”.

## 4
w: for Kṛṣṇa  rati  deep  when it becomes  prema  is the name
w: of the rasa of devotion to Kṛṣṇa  that  sthāyī-bhāva  is the name
tr: When *rati* for Kṛṣṇa deepens, it is called *prema*; and that *prema* is the abiding emotion (*sthāyī-bhāva*) of the *rasa* of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (heading): *bhāva*, or *rati*, is the fluid, budding state of *prema*; in its dense, mature state it is *prema* itself.

## 5
w: śuddha-sattva  special  in its nature
w: of the sun of prema  to a ray  similar
w: by tastes  of the heart  softness
w: producing  this  bhāva  is called
tr: “*Bhāva* is in essence a special *śuddha-sattva*, pure spiritual existence; it is like a ray of the sun of *prema*, and by its tastes (*ruci*) it softens the heart.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.3.1)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *śuddha-sattva* here is a function of Kṛṣṇa’s own potency (*svarūpa-śakti*) called *saṁvit*; the tastes are the desire to attain the Lord, to serve Him favourably and to be intimate with Him.

## 6
w: these  two  of bhāva  essential  marginal  characteristics
w: of prema  the characteristics  now  hear  Sanātana
tr: These, then, are the two marks of *bhāva*: its essential (*svarūpa*) and its attendant (*taṭastha*) characteristic. Now, Sanātana, hear the marks of *prema*.

## 7
w: completely  having softened  the heart
w: by the feeling ‘He is mine’  intense  marked
w: bhāva  that  itself  dense in nature
w: by the wise  prema  is called
tr: “When *bhāva* has completely softened the heart, is marked by an intense feeling that ‘He is mine’ (*mamatva*), and has become dense in its nature, the wise call it *prema*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.4.1)

## 8

w: bhakti  thus  is called  by Bhīṣma
w: by Prahlāda, Uddhava and Nārada
tr: “The exclusive feeling ‘He is mine’ (*ananya-mamatā*) toward Viṣṇu alone, joined with *prema* — this is what Bhīṣma, Prahlāda, Uddhava and Nārada call *bhakti*.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.4.2)
fn: The edition’s heading: the definition of *prema* according to the Pañcarātra — words of the *Nārada-pañcarātra*, quoted in the *Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu*, 1.4.2.

## 9

## 10

tr: From the company of *sādhus* come hearing and chanting (*śravaṇa-kīrtana*), and by *sādhana-bhakti* all *anarthas* are removed.

## 11

w: steadiness  from  for hearing and the rest  taste  is born
tr: When the *anarthas* have been removed, steadiness (*niṣṭhā*) in *bhakti* arises. From steadiness, taste (*ruci*) for hearing and the other practices is born.

## 12
w: taste  from  for bhakti  there is  attachment  deep
w: attachment  from  in the heart  is born  for Kṛṣṇa  the sprout of love
tr: From taste comes deep attachment (*āsakti*) to *bhakti*, and from attachment the sprout of love for Kṛṣṇa is born in the heart.

## 13
w: that  rati  deep  when it becomes  takes  the name prema
w: that  prema  the goal  of all bliss  the abode
tr: When that *rati* deepens, it takes the name *prema*. That *prema* is the ultimate goal (*prayojana*), the abode of all bliss.

## 14

tr: “First comes faith, then the company of *sādhus*, and then the practice of worship (*bhajana-kriyā*). From it comes the removal of *anarthas*, then steadiness (*niṣṭhā*), and after that taste (*ruci*).
fn: The verse number is not printed in the edition (only “॥”).

## 15

## 16

By relishing them one quickly comes to faith in Me, the path of liberation, and after it to attraction (*rati*) and love (*prema-bhakti*).” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 3.25.25)
fn: The same verse as Ādi 1.60 (= Madhya 22.83); translation as there.

## 18–19
w: forbearance  time not wasted

tr: “Forbearance, never wasting time, detachment, freedom from pride, firm hope (*āśā-bandha*), eager longing (*samutkaṇṭhā*), a constant taste for singing the holy name, attachment to describing His qualities and love for the places where He dwelt — these and other *anubhāvas* are seen in a person in whom the sprout of *bhāva* has arisen.” (Bhakti-rasāmṛta-sindhu, 1.3.25–26)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: when *rati* has awakened, nine signs are seen in the *sādhaka*; *āśā-bandha* is the firm hope of attaining the Lord, and *samutkaṇṭhā* an intense longing for what one desires.

fn: The edition’s heading: “Explanation of the verse — (1) *kṣānti*, or imperturbability”.

## 21
w: that  me  surrendered  may know  O brāhmaṇas

tr: “O *brāhmaṇas*, and you, goddess Gaṅgā, know me as one who has surrendered, whose heart is fixed on the Lord. Let a magic creature sent by the *brāhmaṇa*, or Takṣaka himself, bite me as it will — you go on singing the narrations of Viṣṇu.” (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.19.15)
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: hearing of the curse of Śṛṅgī, son of the sage Śamīka, Mahārāja Parīkṣit took the vow of fasting until death on the bank of the Gaṅgā; he welcomed the curse as a blessing and asked the assembled sages to chant *hari-kathā* without cease.

fn: The edition’s heading: “(2) never wasting time (*avyartha-kālatva*) and (3) detachment from sense gratification”.

## 27

fn: The edition’s heading: “words of one full of *āśā-bandha* — words of Śrī Prabhupāda, quoted by Śrī Rūpa Gosvāmī”. *Anubhāṣya*: Vaiṣṇava *yoga* — *yoga* full of meditation on Viṣṇu, pure *bhakti-yoga*.

fn: The same verse as Madhya 2.61; translation as there, with the speaker’s pronouns in lower case: here, by the edition’s heading, these are the words of a devotee full of *samutkaṇṭhā* — Bilvamaṅgala. *Anubhāṣya*: *viralam* — “in solitude”, or “seldom seen”.

## 58

w: of union  endless  aspects  no  end  of it
tr: *Śṛṅgāra* is of two kinds, union (*sambhoga*) and separation (*vipralambha*). Union has endless aspects; there is no end to them.

## 59

## 61
