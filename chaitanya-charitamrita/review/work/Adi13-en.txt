# English translation, Ādi-līlā 13 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).

## 0
tr: The Description of the Great Festival of the Lord’s Appearance

## 1
w: He  may be gracious  Caitanya-
w: deva  whose  by the grace
w: in describing His pastimes  fit
w: at once  may become  fallen  even  this one
tr: May Śrī Caitanyadeva be gracious to me, for by His grace even one as fallen as I at once becomes fit to describe His pastimes.

## 2
w: glory  glory  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  Gauracandra
w: glory to Advaitacandra  glory  glory  Nityānanda
tr: Glory, glory to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya Gauracandra! Glory to Advaitacandra, and glory, glory to Nityānanda!

## 3
w: glory  glory  Gadādhara  glory  Śrīnivāsa
w: glory  Mukunda  Vāsudeva  glory  Haridāsa
tr: Glory, glory to Gadādhara, and glory to Śrīvāsa! Glory to Mukunda and Vāsudeva, and glory to Haridāsa!

## 4
w: glory  Dāmodara-svarūpa  glory  Murāri  Gupta
w: these  all  by the moons’ rising  darkness  made  vanish
tr: Glory to Svarūpa Dāmodara, and glory to Murāri Gupta! These moons have risen and driven all darkness away.

## 5
w: glory  of Śrī  Caitanyacandra  the devotees  full moons
w: of all  by the moonlight of love  bright  the three worlds
tr: Glory to the devotees of Śrī Caitanyacandra, who are full moons! The moonlight of their love makes the three worlds shine.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The rays of *hari-bhajana* shining from the devotee-moons destroy the darkness of the living beings’ ignorance.”

## 6
w: this  indeed  I have told  at the book’s beginning  the preface
w: now  I tell  of Caitanya’s pastimes  the sequence  in order
tr: Thus I have given the preface at the beginning of the book. Now I shall tell the pastimes of Śrī Caitanya in their proper order.

## 7
w: first  indeed  in the form of sūtras  I make  the count
w: afterwards  that  expanding  I shall make  the description
tr: First I shall list them briefly, as in sūtras; afterwards I shall describe them in detail.

## 8
w: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya  in Navadvīpa  descending
w: forty-eight  years  manifest  sporting
tr: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya descended in Navadvīpa, and for forty-eight years He enjoyed His pastimes before the eyes of the world.

## 9
w: fourteen hundred  seven  in the Śaka year  of birth  the record
w: in fourteen hundred  fifty-five  took place  the disappearance
tr: His appearance is recorded in Śaka 1407 (1486), and He disappeared from the world in 1455 (1533).

## 10
w: twenty-four  years  the Lord  made  household life
w: constantly  made  there  the delight of kīrtana
tr: For twenty-four years the Lord lived as a householder, and all that time He delighted in kīrtana.

## 11
w: of the twenty-fourth  year  at the end  accepting  sannyāsa
w: another  twenty-four  years  made  in Nīlācala  residence
tr: At the end of His twenty-fourth year He accepted sannyāsa, and for another twenty-four years He lived in Nīlācala.

## 12
w: of them  within  six  years  —  going and coming
w: sometimes  the south,  sometimes  Gauḍa,  sometimes  Vṛndāvana
tr: Six of those years He spent journeying — now to the south, now to Gauḍa, now to Vṛndāvana.

## 13
w: eighteen  years  remained  in Nīlācala
w: in the nectar of the pastimes of love for Kṛṣṇa  flooded  everyone
tr: For eighteen years He stayed in Nīlācala, flooding everyone with the nectar of His pastimes of love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 14
w: in household life  the Lord’s  pastimes  —  ‘ādi’-līlā  is the name
w: ‘madhya’  ‘antya’  by name  —  of the later pastimes  two  names
tr: The Lord’s pastimes as a householder are called the Ādi-līlā, and His later pastimes bear two names, Madhya and Antya.

## 15
w: the Ādi-līlā  within  the Lord’s  all  deeds
w: in the form of sūtras  Murāri Gupta  made  strung together
tr: Murāri Gupta strung together, in the form of sūtras, all the Lord’s deeds in the Ādi-līlā.

## 16
w: the Lord’s  which  later pastimes  Svarūpa-dāmodara
w: sūtras  making  strung  the book  within
tr: Svarūpa Dāmodara set down the Lord’s later pastimes in brief notes, and gathered them in his book.
fn: Heading in the edition to 15–16: Murāri Gupta strung together the Ādi-līlā in his *Caitanya-carita*, and Svarūpa Dāmodara the later pastimes in his *Kaḍacā* (diary).

## 17
w: these  two  persons’  sūtras  seeing  hearing
w: description  make  the Vaiṣṇavas  the sequence  which  keeping
tr: Reading and hearing the notes of these two, the Vaiṣṇavas describe the Lord’s pastimes in their order.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Śrī Murāri Gupta’s notes on the Ādi-līlā still survive; the Vaiṣṇavas read them, and they heard the notes of Śrī Svarūpa Gosvāmī’s diary from the lips of Śrī Raghunātha Dāsa Gosvāmī — and so they describe the Lord’s pastimes.

## 18
w: childhood,  boyhood,  early youth,  youth,  —  four  divisions
w: therefore  in the first part  the pastimes  four  divisions
tr: Childhood (*bālya*), boyhood (*paugaṇḍa*), early youth (*kaiśora*) and youth (*yauvana*) — these are the four ages, and so the pastimes of the first part fall into four divisions.

## 19
w: with all  good  qualities  full  that
w: I bow to  the Phālguna  full moon
w: on which  Śrī  Kṛṣṇa  Caitanya
w: descended  with the names of Kṛṣṇa
tr: I bow to that full moon of Phālguna, rich in every good quality, on which Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya descended together with the names of Kṛṣṇa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: on this full moon Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya — the Lord of Goloka, the original source of all avatāras, in whom Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are one — descended together with the names of Kṛṣṇa from His own abode, the Gaura-prakoṣṭha, into the earthly Navadvīpa; and the full moon itself is no worldly day but a transcendental goddess of the lunar day (*tithi*), who descended into time to serve Him. The *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* cites a verse: “In the twenty-eighth age of Vaivasvata Manu, in the year fourteen hundred and seven, on the lovely bank of the Bhāgīrathī, from the great ocean of Śacī’s womb, on a full moon seized by Rāhu, Gaurāṅga appeared.”

## 20
w: on the Phālguna full moon  in the evening  the Lord’s  birth  arose
w: at that  time  by providence  of the moon  eclipse  takes place
tr: The Lord appeared on the evening of the Phālguna full moon, and at that very hour, as providence would have it, the moon was eclipsed.

## 21
w: ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’  say  the people  joyful  becoming
w: was born  Caitanya Prabhu  the Name  causing to be born
tr: The people were joyfully calling out, “Hari! Hari!” — and Caitanya Prabhu took birth, having first brought the Name to birth.

## 22
w: at birth,  in childhood,  boyhood,  early youth,  youth
w: the name of Hari  made take  the Lord  by various  pretexts
tr: At His birth, in childhood, boyhood, early youth and youth, the Lord found one pretext or another to make people take the name of Hari.

## 23
w: of a child’s mood  on the pretext  the Lord  does  crying
w: ‘Kṛṣṇa’  ‘Hari’  the names  hearing  stops  weeping
tr: On the pretext of being a baby the Lord would cry, and the moment He heard the names “Kṛṣṇa” and “Hari”, He stopped weeping.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Crying on the pretext of making people chant the name — and quieting at the name alone.”

## 24
w: therefore  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’  say  the women
w: to see  come  who  all  friends and kin
tr: So all the women among His friends and kin who came to see Him would chant, “Hari! Hari!”

## 25
w: ‘Gaurahari’  calling  Him  laugh  all  the women
w: therefore  became  His  name  ‘Gaurahari’
tr: Laughing, the women all called Him “Gaurahari”, and so Gaurahari became His name.

## 26
w: childhood  age  —  until  in His hand  chalk  was given
w: boyhood  age  —  until  marriage  not  was made
tr: His childhood lasted until the chalk was put in His hand for His first lesson; His boyhood, until He married.

## 27
w: marriage  having made  began  fresh  youth
w: everywhere  made take  the Lord  the saṅkīrtana of the name
tr: With His marriage began the bloom of youth, and everywhere the Lord led people to chant the name together.

## 28
w: in boyhood  age  He studies,  teaches  the pupils
w: everywhere  makes  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the explanation
tr: In His boyhood He studied and also taught pupils, and everywhere He explained the name of Kṛṣṇa.

## 29
w: in sūtra,  gloss,  commentary  of Kṛṣṇa’s name  the purport
w: to the pupils  clear  becomes,  —  to all  a wonder
tr: He showed that the purport of every sūtra, gloss and commentary was the name of Kṛṣṇa, and His pupils came to see it plainly — a wonder to all.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: while teaching His pupils the sūtras of grammar with their *vṛtti* and *ṭīkā*, the Lord taught them the purport of Kṛṣṇa’s name; following that teaching, the Gosvāmīs (Śrī Jīva) later composed two grammars, the *Laghu-* and the *Bṛhad-Harināmāmṛta-vyākaraṇa*, through which one gains both a knowledge of words and devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

## 30
w: whom  He sees,  to him  says  —  say  Kṛṣṇa’s name
w: with Kṛṣṇa’s name  flooded  Navadvīpa  town
tr: Whomever He met He would tell, “Chant the name of Kṛṣṇa!” — and He flooded the town of Navadvīpa with Kṛṣṇa’s name.
