tr: As the Lord performed His rites in the water, the elephants came before Him. Crying, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!”, the Lord splashed water at them.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *mahā-bhāgavata* has realised non-dual knowledge; he sees nothing “second”, apart from Kṛṣṇa, and so knows neither fear nor enmity. Everywhere he sees Kṛṣṇa and what belongs to Kṛṣṇa, and all beings in turn love him as their own. *Kṛtya* means bathing, chanting the mantra, remembrance and the like.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the gopīs sing this in autumn, when Kṛṣṇa wanders from forest to forest playing His flute; His attire is a garland of forest flowers, a crown of peacock feathers, earrings of guñjā berries; the black bucks (*kṛṣṇa-sāras*) are the does’ mates.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: so Brahmā saw Vṛndāvana when, after he had stolen the calves and cowherd boys, Kṛṣṇa mercifully lifted the veil of His māyā from him: it is the abode of Ajita, and by its own glory anger, greed, thirst and the like had fled from it.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Jhārikhaṇḍa is a well-known forest region on the way to Vṛndāvana — today the hill and forest states of Athgarh, Dhenkanal, Angul, Lahara, Keonjhar, Bamra, Bonai, Gangpur, Chota Nagpur, Jashpur, Surguja and others. Edition heading: “In Jhārikhaṇḍa the Lord delivers all beings, moving and nonmoving — that is, He gives them devotion to Kṛṣṇa.”

fn: *Paramānanda-mādhava*, “Mādhava, the supreme bliss.” *Anubhāṣya*: the words name both Śrī Paramānanda Svāmī of the Viṣṇusvāmī line and Bhagavān Himself, the worshipful Lord of Śrīdhara Svāmī; the mute becomes eloquent in chanting of Kṛṣṇa, and the lame crosses the mountain, doing even the impossible in Kṛṣṇa’s service. Edition heading: the sixth verse of Śrīdhara Svāmī’s invocation at the start of his *Bhāvārtha-dīpikā* on Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam, 1.1.1.

## 95
w: by my own prārabdha  living  in Vārāṇasī
w: māyā  Brahman  words  except  not  I hear  with my ears
tr: By my own *prārabdha*, the karma now bearing fruit, I live in Vārāṇasī, and my ears hear nothing but the words ‘*māyā*’ and ‘Brahman.’
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in Kāśī, chief holy place of the Śaivas and of the worshippers of five deities, there is no talk of serving Hari, and for a devotee of Gaura it is no place to live; so Candraśekhara bitterly says he lives there by his *prārabdha*, for past sins. This is self-reproach: a pure devotee may speak of himself so, but he does not reap the fruits of good and bad deeds; even *sādhana-bhakti* destroys sin, its seed and ignorance. Edition heading: “Kāśī, where there is no talk of serving Hari, is the dwelling of the dry Māyāvādīs.”

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Prakāśānanda was an *ekadaṇḍī sannyāsī* of Śaṅkara’s line who lived in Kāśī in Mahāprabhu’s time; the Lord Himself speaks of him in the *Caitanya-bhāgavata* (Madhya 3 and 20): explaining the Vedas, he rejects His form (*vigraha*) and calls His body, pastimes and abode false.
