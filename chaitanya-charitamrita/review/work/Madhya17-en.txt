## 31

tr: As the Lord performed His rites in the water, the elephants came before Him. Crying, “Say ‘Kṛṣṇa’!”, the Lord splashed water at them.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the *mahā-bhāgavata* has realised non-dual knowledge; he sees nothing “second”, apart from Kṛṣṇa, and so knows neither fear nor enmity. Everywhere he sees Kṛṣṇa and what belongs to Kṛṣṇa, and all beings in turn love him as their own. *Kṛtya* means bathing, chanting the mantra, remembrance and the like.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: the gopīs sing this in autumn, when Kṛṣṇa wanders from forest to forest playing His flute; His attire is a garland of forest flowers, a crown of peacock feathers, earrings of guñjā berries; the black bucks (*kṛṣṇa-sāras*) are the does’ mates.

fn: *Anubhāṣya*: so Brahmā saw Vṛndāvana when, after he had stolen the calves and cowherd boys, Kṛṣṇa mercifully lifted the veil of His māyā from him: it is the abode of Ajita, and by its own glory anger, greed, thirst and the like had fled from it.

fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Jhārikhaṇḍa is a well-known forest region on the way to Vṛndāvana — today the hill and forest states of Athgarh, Dhenkanal, Angul, Lahara, Keonjhar, Bamra, Bonai, Gangpur, Chota Nagpur, Jashpur, Surguja and others. Edition heading: “In Jhārikhaṇḍa the Lord delivers all beings, moving and nonmoving — that is, He gives them devotion to Kṛṣṇa.”
