# English translation, Ādi-līlā 12 — from the Bengali/Sanskrit, following the «Anubhāṣya» (2026 draft).
# Format: tools/build_rev.py. «w:» — word-for-word for each transliteration line, in order.

## 0
tr: The Description of the Branches of the Advaita Trunk

## 1
w: of Advaita’s  feet  lotus  the bees  those
w: the essence and the empty  bearing  all
w: rejecting  the empty  the bearers of the essence
w: I bow to  whose life is Caitanya
tr: Like bees, all the followers of Śrī Advaita hover about His lotus feet, yet some of them hold the essence and others are empty. Rejecting the empty, I bow to those who hold the essence, whose very life is Śrī Caitanya.
fn: Heading in the edition: “The servants of Advaita are of two kinds: those who take the essence (*sāra-grāhī*) and the empty.” *Anubhāṣya*: *sāra*, the essence, are the people of Gaurahari who follow Advaita; *asāra*, the empty, are those who merely fancy themselves His followers but have turned away from Gaurahari — Māyāvādīs devoid of pure devotion; those who hold the essence are the *bhāgavatas*, whose life is Gaura. According to the edition’s *Kathāsāra*, the author calls *sāra-grāhī* the Vaiṣṇavas who follow the view of Śrī Acyutānanda (12.73), and all the rest *asāra*.

## 2
w: glory  glory  Mahāprabhu  Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya
w: glory  glory  Nityānanda  glory to Advaita  the blessed
tr: Glory, glory to Mahāprabhu Śrī Kṛṣṇa Caitanya! Glory, glory to Nityānanda, and glory to the blessed Advaita!

## 3
w: of Śrī  Caitanya  the immortal  tree
w: the second  trunk  who is
w: of the glorious  Advaitacandra
w: who are the branches  the followers  we bow to
tr: The glorious Advaitacandra is the second trunk of the immortal tree of Śrī Caitanya, and His followers are the branches of that trunk. To them we bow down.

## 4
w: of the tree  the second  trunk  —  Ācārya Gosāñi
w: His  as many  branches  grew,  of them  record  there is none
tr: The second trunk of the tree is Ācārya Gosāñi. So many branches grew from Him that there is no writing them all down.

## 5
w: of Caitanya the gardener  of the water of mercy  by the sprinkling
w: by that  water  nourished  the trunk  grows  day by day
tr: Caitanya the gardener sprinkles it with the water of His mercy, and, nourished by that water, the trunk grows day by day.
fn: Heading in the edition: “By Gaura’s mercy only those servants of Advaita who take the essence flourish.”

## 6
w: on that  trunk  as many  fruits of love  grew
w: with those  fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa  the world  was filled
tr: So many fruits of love ripened on that trunk that the whole world was filled with the fruits of love for Kṛṣṇa.

## 7
w: that  water  through the trunk  makes  into the branches  passage
w: with fruit and flower  they grow  —  the branches  became  wide-spreading
tr: Through the trunk that water rises into the branches, and, laden with fruit and flowers, they spread far and wide.

## 8
w: at first  indeed  of one mind  the Ācārya’s  followers
w: later  two views  arose  of fate  by reason
tr: At first the Ācārya’s followers were all of one mind; but later, as fate would have it, two views arose among them.

## 9
w: some  indeed  by the Ācārya’s  order,  some  indeed  independent
w: their own views  invent  to fate  subject
tr: Some kept to the Ācārya’s order, while others went their own way: in the grip of fate, they invented views of their own.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Those who take the essence serve Gaura in loyalty to Advaita; the empty, going their own way, oppose Gaura.”

## 10
w: the Ācārya’s  teaching  which,  that  teaching  the essence
w: His  order  transgressing  goes,  he  indeed  empty
tr: The Ācārya’s teaching is the essence; whoever goes against His order is empty, mere chaff.
fn: Heading in the edition: “Loyalty to the Ācārya is the essence; anything else is empty.” *Sāra* is “essence, kernel”; *asāra*, “empty, without a kernel”. *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: those who walk the path the Ācārya taught are pure Vaiṣṇavas; those who, subject to fate, left it and invented a teaching of their own are *asāra*.

## 11
w: of the empty  in naming  here  there is no  need
w: the difference  to know  I make  together  the count
tr: There is no need here to name the empty ones; I count them all together so that the difference may be known.

## 12
w: a heap of paddy  one measures  just as  chaff  together with
w: afterwards  the chaff  blowing away  cleaning  to do
tr: Just so, one first measures a heap of paddy along with its chaff, and afterwards winnows the chaff away to clean the grain.
fn: Heading in the edition to 11–12: “Why the non-devotees who fancy themselves servants of Advaita are mentioned, and an example.” *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: we have no concern with the names of the *asāra*; yet, to keep the Vaiṣṇavas who take the essence apart from the empty, I name them together, as chaff is winnowed from the grain. Chaff (*pātanā*) is the empty paddy that holds no rice.

## 13
w: Acyutānanda  —  a great  branch,  the Ācārya’s  son
w: from birth  served  he  Caitanya’s  feet
tr: Acyutānanda, the Ācārya’s son, is a great branch. From his very birth he served the feet of Śrī Caitanya.
fn: In the edition the branches of Śrī Advaita are listed under numbered headings: (1) Acyutānanda, (2) Kṛṣṇa Miśra, (3) Gopāla, (4) Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, (5) Yadunandana Ācārya, (6)–(38) in 12.58–65; the offshoots of Gadādhara Paṇḍita are numbered (1)–(33), 12.79–87. *Anubhāṣya*: Acyutānanda was Advaita’s eldest son (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 9). Devoted to Mahāprabhu from childhood, he never married; later, in Purī, he took shelter of Śrī Gadādhara Paṇḍita, and the *Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā* calls him Gadādhara’s disciple (some see in him Kārttikeya, others the gopī Acyutā).

## 14
w: of Caitanya  Gosāñi  the guru  —  Keśava  Bhāratī
w: these  of his father  words  hearing,  pain  he felt  great
tr: When he heard his father say that Keśava Bhāratī was the guru of Caitanya Gosāñi, he was deeply pained.

## 15
w: about the guru of the world  You  give  such  instruction
w: Your  this  by instruction  ruined  is  the land
tr: “How can You teach such a thing about the guru of the world? This teaching of Yours will bring ruin on the whole land.

## 16
w: of the fourteen  worlds  the guru  —  Caitanya Gosāñi
w: His  guru  —  another,  this  in any  scripture  is not
tr: Caitanya Gosāñi is the guru of the fourteen worlds; no scripture anywhere says that anyone else is His guru.”

## 17
w: of five  years  a boy  speaks  of conclusions  the essence
w: hearing  obtained  the Ācārya  satisfaction  boundless
tr: Hearing a boy of five speak the very essence of the scriptures’ conclusions, the Ācārya was boundlessly pleased.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: in the year Mahāprabhu set His heart on going from Nīlācala to Vṛndāvana by way of Rāmakeli (1433–34 of the Śaka era), Acyutānanda was only five years old (*Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Antya 4).

## 18
w: Kṛṣṇa  Miśra  by name  another  the Ācārya’s son
w: Caitanya Gosāñi  dwells  in whose  heart
tr: Another of the Ācārya’s sons was named Kṛṣṇa Miśra; in his heart Caitanya Gosāñi dwelt.
fn: *Anubhāṣya* (after the *Advaita-carita*): of Advaita’s six sons, three — Acyuta, Kṛṣṇa Miśra and Gopāla — served Śrī Gaurāṅga; they are called the three jewels born from the ocean of Sītā’s womb.

## 19
w: Śrī  Gopāla  by name  another  the Ācārya’s  son
w: his  story,  hear,  most  wonderful
tr: Another son of the Ācārya was named Śrī Gopāla. Listen to his story, for it is most wonderful.

## 20
w: in the Guṇḍicā  temple  Mahāprabhu’s  before
w: in the kīrtana  dancing  does  in great  joy of love
tr: When Mahāprabhu was cleansing the Guṇḍicā temple, Gopāla danced before Him in the kīrtana, filled with the great joy of love.
fn: The edition’s *Kathāsāra*: this was in Gopāla’s youth, at the cleansing of the Guṇḍicā temple. *Anubhāṣya*: see Madhya 12.143–149.

## 21
w: many  ecstasies arising  in his body,  wondrous  dancing
w: the two  Gosāñis  ‘Hari’  say,  delighted  at heart
tr: Many ecstasies arose in his body, and his dancing was wondrous; the two Gosāñis, delighted at heart, called out, “Hari!”
fn: The two Gosāñis are Mahāprabhu and Advaita.

## 22
w: dancing  and dancing  Gopāla  became  unconscious
w: on the ground  he fell,  in his body  there was no  awareness
tr: In the midst of his dancing Gopāla fainted and fell to the ground, with no sign of awareness left in his body.

## 23
w: grieved  became  the Ācārya  his son  on His lap  taking
w: protection  makes  of Nṛsiṁha  the mantra  reciting
tr: The Ācārya was grieved. Taking His son on His lap, He recited the mantra of Nṛsiṁha to protect him.

## 24
w: many  mantras  recites  the Ācārya,  not  comes  consciousness
w: at the Ācārya’s  grief  the Vaiṣṇavas  do  weeping
tr: The Ācārya recited mantra after mantra, but the boy did not come to, and the Vaiṣṇavas wept to see the Ācārya’s grief.

## 25
w: then  Mahāprabhu  his  on the chest  hand  placing
w: ‘get up,  Gopāla’  —  say  say  ‘Hari’  ‘Hari’
tr: Then Mahāprabhu placed His hand on the boy’s chest and said, “Get up, Gopāla! Say ‘Hari! Hari!’”

## 26
w: rose  Gopāla  the Lord’s  touch and voice  perceiving
w: delighted  becoming  all  make  the cry of ‘Hari’
tr: Feeling the Lord’s touch and hearing His voice, Gopāla rose, and everyone, overjoyed, raised the cry of “Hari!”

## 27
w: the Ācārya’s  other  son  —  Śrī  Balarāma
w: other  son  —  ‘Svarūpa’  a branch,  ‘Jagadīśa’  by name
tr: The Ācārya had other sons as well: Śrī Balarāma, Svarūpa and one named Jagadīśa.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Balarāma, Svarūpa and Jagadīśa turned away from Gaura; they were *smārtas* or Māyāvādīs, and therefore not Vaiṣṇavas (compare 12.1 and 12.10: “the empty”).

## 28
w: ‘Kamalākānta  Viśvāsa’  by name  the Ācārya’s  servant
w: the Ācārya’s  dealings  all  —  to him  known
tr: The Ācārya had a servant named Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, to whom all the Ācārya’s affairs were known.
fn: The *Anubhāṣya* distinguishes Kamalākānta Viśvāsa, Advaita’s servant, from the brāhmaṇa Kamalākānta of Navadvīpa, whom Paramānanda Purī brought with him to Nīlācala (Madhya 10.94).

## 29
w: in Nīlācala  he  a  letter  writing
w: to Pratāparudra  gave  sending
tr: In Nīlācala he wrote a letter and sent it to King Pratāparudra.

## 30
w: of that  letter  the matter  the Ācārya  does not  know
w: somehow  that  letter  came  to the Lord
tr: The Ācārya knew nothing about the letter, and somehow it found its way to the Lord.

## 31
w: in that  letter  written  is  —  this  indeed  the writing
w: as God  the Ācārya  he has  established
tr: This is what the letter said: he had proclaimed the Ācārya to be God Himself.

## 32
w: but  His  by fate  some  has come about  debt
w: the debt  to pay off  I ask  coins  three hundred
tr: “But by fate He has fallen into some debt, and to pay it off I ask for three hundred coins.”

## 33
w: the letter  reading  the Lord’s  in mind  arose  sorrow
w: outwardly  smiling  something  says  the moon-faced
tr: Reading the letter, the Lord was pained at heart, but outwardly the moon-faced Lord smiled and said:

## 34
w: the Ācārya  he has established  making  God
w: in this  fault  there is none,  the Ācārya  —  truly  God
tr: “He has proclaimed the Ācārya to be God, and in that there is no fault: the Ācārya truly is God.

## 35
w: God’s  poverty  making  he has made  begging
w: therefore  punishment  giving  I shall make  a lesson
tr: But he has made God out to be poor and gone begging for Him. So I shall punish him and teach him a lesson.”
fn: Heading in the edition: “To take Nārāyaṇa, full in all six opulences, for a poor man, as though He were a *jīva*, is Māyāvāda, the creed of the *bāuls*.” *Anubhāṣya*: *īśvarera dainya kari’* — “making the Lord out to be poor”.

## 36
w: to Govinda  order  He gave  —  here  today  from
w: the madcap  ‘Viśvāsa’  here  not  you shall let  come
tr: He ordered Govinda, “From today on, do not let that madcap Viśvāsa come here.”
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: Kamalākānta Viśvāsa was called “the madcap Viśvāsa” because his conclusions were like the ravings of a madman (*bāul*).

## 37
w: of the punishment  hearing  ‘Viśvāsa’  became  most  unhappy
w: hearing  of the Lord’s  punishment  the Ācārya  delighted
tr: Hearing of his punishment, Viśvāsa was deeply distressed; but when the Ācārya heard of the Lord’s punishment, He was delighted.

## 38
w: to Viśvāsa  He says  —  you  very  fortunate
w: you  has made  punishment  the Lord  Bhagavān
tr: He said to Viśvāsa, “You are most fortunate: the Lord, Bhagavān Himself, has punished you.

## 39
w: before  Mahāprabhu  Me  would show  honour
w: pain  feeling  in mind  I  made  a plan
tr: In the past Mahāprabhu used to honour Me. This pained Me, and I thought of a way to change it.

## 40
w: liberation  —  the highest  making  I gave  of the Vāsiṣṭha  an exposition
w: angry  becoming  the Lord  Me  did  dishonour
tr: I expounded the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha*, extolling liberation as the highest goal, and the Lord grew angry and treated Me with dishonour.
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya*: while explaining the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha*, Advaita on some pretext declared liberation higher than devotion. *Anubhāṣya*: the *Yoga-vāsiṣṭha Rāmāyaṇa* teaches Māyāvāda — liberation as *nirvāṇa*, merging into Brahman — and is opposed to devotion to Viṣṇu; pure devotees therefore do not read it.

## 41
w: punishment  receiving  came  My  supreme  joy
w: which  punishment  received  fortunate  Śrī Mukunda
tr: Being punished brought Me the greatest joy. Fortunate Śrī Mukunda received the same punishment,

## 42
w: which  punishment  received  Śrī  Śacī  the fortunate
w: that  punishment-mercy  other  people  will get  where
tr: and so did the fortunate Śrī Śacī. Where else could anyone find such mercy in the guise of punishment?”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: for Advaita’s punishment see *Caitanya-bhāgavata*, Madhya 19; for Mukunda’s, Madhya 10; for Mother Śacī’s, Madhya 22.

## 43
w: so  saying  the Ācārya  him  making  reassurance
w: joyful  becoming  came  to Mahāprabhu
tr: With these words the Ācārya comforted him, and then, joyful, He went to Mahāprabhu.

## 44
w: to the Lord  He says  —  Your  not  I understand  this  pastime
w: than Me  of mercy a vessel  You have made  Kamalā
tr: He said to the Lord, “I do not understand this pastime of Yours. You have made Kamalā a greater vessel of Your mercy than Me.

## 45
w: to Me even  ever  such  not  is  mercy
w: at Your  feet  I  what  did  offence
tr: Such mercy You have never shown even Me. What offence have I committed at Your feet?”

## 46
w: this  hearing  Mahāprabhu  to laugh  began
w: having called  Kamalākānta  gracious  became
tr: Hearing this, Mahāprabhu began to laugh; He sent for Kamalākānta and was gracious to him.

## 47
w: the Ācārya  says  —  to him  why  You gave  audience
w: in two  ways  he makes  of Me  a mockery
tr: The Ācārya said, “Why have You let him see You again? He makes a mockery of Me in two ways.”
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: “He calls Me the transcendent Nārāyaṇa, yet in practice he takes Me for a poor worldly man begging for money.”

## 48
w: hearing  the Lord’s  mind  pleased  became
w: of the two  the inner words  the two  alone  knew
tr: The Lord was pleased at heart to hear this. Only the two of Them knew what lay within each other’s words.

## 49
w: the Lord  says  —  madcap,  thus  why  you do
w: of the Ācārya  honour  dharma  harm  that  you practise
tr: The Lord said, “Madcap, why do you behave like this? What you do harms the Ācārya’s honour and His dharma.

## 50
w: acceptance  ever  not  one should do  of a king’s wealth
w: of the worldly  food  if one eats  defiled  becomes  the mind
tr: A Vaiṣṇava ācārya must never accept a king’s wealth as a gift. If he eats the food of worldly men, his mind is defiled.
fn: Heading in the edition to 50–52: “The Lord sets out the duty of a Vaiṣṇava ācārya.”

## 51
w: the mind  defiled  when it becomes  there is no  of Kṛṣṇa  remembrance
w: of Kṛṣṇa  remembrance  without  becomes  fruitless  life
tr: When the mind is defiled, one cannot remember Kṛṣṇa, and without remembrance of Kṛṣṇa life bears no fruit.

## 52
w: before people  shame  arises,  dharma  and good name  are  harmed
w: such  deeds  not  do  ever  this  knowing
tr: It brings shame before the world and ruins one’s dharma and good name. Know this, and never do such a thing.”
fn: *Amṛta-pravāha-bhāṣya* to 49–53: though the Ācārya is Īśvara, His human pastime as the teacher of the world is well known, and for an ācārya to beg money from a king to pay a debt is shameless. Greed must be shunned in every way; a king is by nature a worldly man, and eating his food defiles the heart; with a defiled heart one does not remember Kṛṣṇa, and life passes without fruit. This is forbidden to all, and to ācāryas above all: one who gives instruction in the holy name for money is no teacher of the name but an offender against it.

## 53
w: this  teaching  for everyone,  all  in mind  took
w: Ācārya Gosāñi  in mind  joy  felt
tr: This teaching was meant for everyone, and everyone took it to heart; and Ācārya Gosāñi rejoiced within.

## 54
w: the Ācārya’s  intention  the Lord  alone  understands
w: the Lord’s  deep  words  the Ācārya  understands
tr: Only the Lord understood the Ācārya’s intention, and the Ācārya understood the Lord’s deep words.

## 55
w: in this  indeed  episode  there is  much  to consider
w: of the book  the lengthening  for fear  I cannot  write
tr: There is much to consider in this episode, but I cannot write of it for fear of making the book too long.

## 56
w: Śrī  Yadunandana Ācārya  —  Advaita’s  branch
w: his  of branches  and offshoots  the host  there is no  record
tr: Śrī Yadunandana Ācārya is a branch of Advaita. His branches and offshoots are past all writing.

## 57
w: of Vāsudeva  Datta  he  of the mercy  recipient
w: in every way  has taken shelter of  Caitanya’s  feet
tr: He received the mercy of Vāsudeva Datta and with all his heart took shelter of Śrī Caitanya’s feet.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Yadunandana Ācārya was the initiating guru of Śrī Raghunātha dāsa Gosvāmī (Antya 6.160–169). Vāsudeva Datta is Madhuvrata, a singer of Vraja (*Gaura-gaṇoddeśa-dīpikā*, 140; see Ādi 10.41).

## 58
w: Bhāgavata Ācārya,  and  Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya
w: Cakrapāṇi  Ācārya,  and  Ananta  Ācārya
tr: Other branches are Bhāgavata Ācārya, Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya, Cakrapāṇi Ācārya and Ananta Ācārya.
fn: *Anubhāṣya*: Bhāgavata Ācārya was first among Advaita’s followers and later among Gadādhara’s; he wrote the *Prema-taraṅgiṇī* (*Śākhā-nirṇayāmṛta*, 6). Viṣṇudāsa Ācārya went with Acyutānanda to the festival at Kheturi. Ananta Ācārya is Sudevī, one of the eight sakhīs of Vraja; later he too joined the branch of Gadādhara (see 12.80).

## 59
w: Nandinī,  and  Kāmadeva,  Caitanya dāsa
w: Durlabha  Viśvāsa,  and  Vanamāli dāsa
tr: Then come Nandinī, Kāmadeva, Caitanya dāsa, Durlabha Viśvāsa and Vanamāli dāsa.

## 60
w: Jagannātha  Kara,  and  Kara  Bhavanātha
w: Hṛdayānanda  Sena,  and  dāsa  Bholānātha
tr: Jagannātha Kara, Bhavanātha Kara, Hṛdayānanda Sena and Bholānātha dāsa.
