# Vilasa 12. The Branches and Sub-branches of Narottama's Tree

*A list of Narottama Thakura's disciples: the pujari Balarama, Ramakrishna, Ganganarayana, Radhavallabha, Santosha and Govinda Kaviraja (the Sangita-madhava), the kings and chaudhuris of Kheturi, kirtaniyas and vairagis; the sub-branches — the disciples of Ramakrishna and Ganganarayana, Krishnacharana Chakravarti*

**1.** Glory to Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita with their associates!
Be merciful, Prabhu, to this wretched beggar.

**2.** Glory, glory to the listeners, oceans of compassion!
Hear what I shall now relate.

**3.** All the disciples Sri Thakura Mahashaya accepted —
who could describe all their deeds?

**4.** Sri Mahashaya's branches and sub-branches are very many;
of them I, an ignorant sinner, shall tell a little.

**5.** Do not find fault if the names are out of order;
knowing me as your servant, be pleased, all of you.

**6.** Glory, glory to Sri Mahashaya's disciples,
whose very life is Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita!

**7.** Glory to the pujari Sri Balarama, full of bhakti,
whose service won the Lord and gladdened His heart!

**8.** Glory, glory to Sri Gopiramana Chakravarti,
whose treasure of life is Gaurachandra with His associates!

**9.** Glory to Sri Ramakrishna Acharya, jewel of virtues,
whose branches and sub-branches spread over the earth!

**10.** Glory to the pujari Sri Ravi Raya, giver of bhakti,
who finds great bliss in serving the Vaishnavas!

**11.** Glory, glory to Ganganarayana Chakravarti,
whose branches and sub-branches spread over the world!

**12.** Glory to the merciful Radhavallabha Chaudhuri,
to whose love Sri Thakura Mahashaya is subject!

**13.** Sri Mahashaya's elder brother was Ramakanta;
his son was the very gentle Sri Radhavallabha.

**14.** Glory to Sri Nava-Gauranga Dasa, store of virtues,
who day and night is intoxicated with the name of Gaurachandra!

**15.** Glory to Narayana Ghosha, full of prema-bhakti,
whose singing intoxicates Sri Thakura Mahashaya!

**16.** Glory to Krishna Simha, famed for lion-like valour,
ever intoxicated with prema, learned in music!

**17.** Glory to Sri Santosha Raya, renowned throughout the world,
whose service delights Mahashaya!

**18.** With Sri Govinda Kaviraja he shares great friendship;
the Kaviraja described his ways in his songs.

**19.** At Sri Santosha's request the Kaviraja gladly
composed the drama called *Sangita-madhava*.

**20.** *Thus in the drama Sangita-madhava:*
*"Sri Santosha Datta, son of the excellent Sri Purushottama Datta, great minister of the king of Gauda, residing at Gopalapura on the bank of the Padmavati, younger brother in the paternal realm of the most excellent Sri Narottama Datta Mahashaya and his disciple — he, having had the drama Sangita-madhava composed, fit for the pastimes of Sri Radha-Madhava beginning with purva-raga, in the manner of the world, following Their manifest lila, and having honoured us with gifts of many jewels, has handed it over to us: so let it be performed."*[^1]

**21.** Glory to the most wise king Sri Govinda Rama,
whose tongue ceaselessly chants the name of Hari!

**22.** Glory to Sri Vinoda Raya, who, intoxicated with prema,
dances in the sankirtana with graceful steps!

**23.** Glory to the very learned Kanta Chaudhuri,[^2]
hearing whose singing the gandharvas count themselves blessed!

**24.** Glory, glory to the great poet Sri Vasanta Raya,
ever immersed in the lilas of Radha-Krishna and Chaitanya!

**25.** Glory to Sri Shitala Raya, cool by nature,
seeing whom all the Vaishnavas are very happy!

**26.** Glory to Prabhu Ramadatta, most steadfast,
from whose eyes tears of prema ceaselessly flow!

**27.** Sri Dharmadasa Chaudhuri, who has fully conquered his senses —
his faith in the Vaishnavas is wholly without guile.

**28.** Glory to Sri Bhakata Dasa, vessel of the taste of bhakti,
whom the Vaishnavas do not leave for a moment!

**29.** Glory to Nityananda Dasa, full of prema-bhakti,
exceedingly intoxicated with Nityananda's virtues!

**30.** Glory to Chandidasa, learned and endowed with every virtue,
skilled in refuting unbelievers and most kind to the poor!

**31.** Glory to Dhiru Chaudhuri, renowned on earth,
hearing of whose virtues beasts and birds weep!

**32.** Glory to Boncha Ramabhadra, a great jester,
seeing whose antics all the Vaishnavas rejoice!

**33.** Glory to Ramabhadra Raya, life of the wretched,
whose constant work is the sankirtana of the name!

**34.** Glory, glory to the merciful Rupanarayana —
whose heart does not melt at hearing his singing?

**35.** Glory to Thakura Janakivallabha Chaudhuri,
seeing whose deeds bliss abundantly grows!

**36.** Glory to Sri Srimanta Datta, the experienced treasurer,
mad day and night with Gaura's virtues!

**37.** Glory to the pujari Thakura Rupanarayana,
hearing of whose virtues the threefold miseries depart!

**38.** Glory, glory to Sri Vaishnavacharana, the renunciant,
ever devoted to singing Gaurachandra's glories!

**39.** Glory to Shivarama Dasa, most magnanimous,
for whom Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita are everything!

**40.** Glory, glory to Thakura Krishnadasa Vairagi,
by whose grace all sorrow departs!

**41.** Glory to King Narasimha, full of power,
to whose love Sri Thakura Mahashaya is subject!

**42.** Glory to Rupamala, wife of Narasimha,
by whose way of bhakti the earth counts itself blessed!

**43.** Glory to Chand Raya, renowned for his lovely deeds,
who serves the Vaishnavas with the greatest love!

**44.** Glory to Narayana Raya, most gentle,
ever intoxicated with seeing Sri Vigraha Radhakanta!

**45.** Glory to Ramachandra Raya, wholly without possessions,
whose treasure of life is Gaurachandra with His associates!

**46.** Glory to Sri Thakura Devidasa the kirtaniya,
at whose kirtana the Vaishnavas become ecstatic!

**47.** Glory to the merciful Radha-Krishna Bhattacharya,
whose home was formerly in Navadvipa!

**48.** Glory to the very wise Sri Thakura Krishnadasa,
whose faith in the Vaishnavas is supreme!

**49.** Glory to Sri Chatuya Ramadasa, vessel of bhakti,
who eats only the remnants on the Vaishnavas' plates!

**50.** Glory to Shankara Bishvasa, dear to the Vaishnavas,
who sings Gaura's glories in great delight!

**51.** Glory to Sri Gandharva Raya, skilled in song,
whose singing puts the gandharvas to shame!

**52.** Glory to Sri Madana Raya, son of Gandharva,
hearing of whose virtues prema arises in all!

**53.** Glory to Gangadasa Raya, embodiment of affection,
whose way of prema-bhakti is wholly extraordinary!

**54.** Glory to Sri Gauranga Dasa, the drummer Thakura,
whose playing of the mridanga drives away all pain!

**55.** Glory to the most wise Sri Acharya Jayakrishna,
an intoxicated bee at the Lord's lotus feet!

**56.** Glory, glory to the wise Sri Baru Chaitanya Dasa,
a lovely embodiment of prema-bhakti!

**57.** Glory to Vraja Raya, wondrous in his way of bhakti,
who at the cost of his life does good to others!

**58.** Glory to Radha-Krishna Dasa, a rasika without equal,
who, propagating bhakti, blessed the fallen!

**59.** Glory to Krishna Raya, overwhelmed with love for Krishna,
from whose eyes tears ceaselessly flow!

**60.** Glory, glory to Thakura Sri Dayarama Dasa,
who serves tulasi in great delight!

**61.** Glory to Sri Purushottama, abode of virtues,
whose love for serving the Vaishnavas is boundless!

**62.** Glory to Sri Gokula, embodiment of the taste of bhakti,
at whose singing the Vaishnavas forget their bodies!

**63.** Glory, glory to Haridasa, delighting in the taste of Gaura,
ever longing to live in Navadvipa!

**64.** Glory to Ganga-Haridasa, who lives on the Ganga's bank,
whose way of bhakti astonishes people!

**65.** Glory, glory to Sri Thakura Jaya-Haridasa,
firm in faith in the service of books of bhakti!

**66.** Glory to Sri Jagat Raya, a great pandita,
who deals out fitting punishment to unbelievers and asuras!

**67.** Glory to Rupa Raya, highly skilled in song,
hearing whose singing even yavanas float in prema!

**68.** Glory to Dhiru Chaudhuri, who takes away sorrow and grief,
seeing whose deeds all people float in happiness!

**69.** Glory, glory to the very learned Srikanta,
who by his own goodness delivers the fallen!

**70.** Glory to the most steadfast Sri Mathura Dasa,
ever humble within and without!

**71.** Glory to Bhagavata Dasa, vessel of the taste of bhakti,
who has not a moment free from sadhana!

**72.** Glory to Jagadisha Raya, renowned in the world,
ever engaged in the Lord's service, pure in conduct!

**73.** Glory, glory to Thakura Sri Mahesha Chaudhuri,
always sweet with tears, trembling and thrilling of the body!

**74.** Glory, glory to Ganesha Chaudhuri, absorbed in song,
who does not notice how day and night pass!

**75.** Glory to Sri Chandrashekhara, giver of the jewel of bhakti,
an intoxicated bee at the Lord's lotus feet!

**76.** Glory to Sri Govinda Raya, treasury of virtues,
who would give his life for one who takes Krishna's name!

**77.** Glory to the very wise Narottama Majumdar,
whom no one calls anything but "Majumdar"!

**78.** Glory to Sri Shankara Bhattacharya, full of virtues,
who crushes the pride of unbelievers!

**79.** Glory to Sri Gosani Dasa, of wondrous heart,
whom Sri Thakura Mahashaya praises!

**80.** Glory to Sri Murari Dasa, most kind to the poor,
with great love for the Vaishnavas' remnants!

**81.** Glory, glory to Vasanta Datta, full of prema,
ever intoxicated with the taste of love for Gaura-Govinda!

**82.** Glory to Sri Thakura Shyamadasa, always happy,
seeing whom the wretched float in the bliss of prema!

**83.** Glory, glory to Sri Jaya-Gopala Datta,
whom the Vaishnavas cannot leave even for half a moment!

**84.** Glory to Ramadeva Datta, kind to the poor,
irresistibly intoxicated with the taste of sankirtana!

**85.** Glory to Gangadasa Datta, life of the wretched,
who ceaselessly performs the sankirtana of the name!

**86.** Glory to Manohara Ghosha, of charming deeds,
who ceaselessly sings Sri Gaurachandra's glories!

**87.** Glory to Sri Mukuta Maitra, most pure in conduct,
firmly attached to the feet of Radha-Krishna and Chaitanya!

**88.** Glory to the most gentle Sri Manohara Bishvasa,
whose all is Gaura and Sri Vallavikanta!

**89.** Glory, glory to the strong Arjuna Bishvasa,
most attentive in the Lord's service!

**90.** Glory to the fortunate treasurer Sri Govardhana,
who settles every affair in every way!

**91.** Glory to Sri Balaka Dasa, the vairagi Thakura,
who always behaves like a child, abundant in compassion!

**92.** Glory to the experienced Sri Gauranga Dasa Vairagi,
who always considers himself the lowliest!

**93.** Glory to Sri Vihari Dasa, the vairagi Thakura,
in the garb of one wholly without possessions, sweet in character!

**94.** Glory to the mighty Sri Gokula Dasa Vairagi,
overwhelmed with longing to live in Navadvipa and Vrindavana!

**95.** Glory to Sri Prasada Dasa, foremost of vairagis,
who knows no dwelling but Sri Kheturi!

**96.** There is no limit to describing the deeds of them all;
the glory of them all has filled the world.

**97.** This is the desire I always cherish:
free from envy, to sing the virtues of them all.

**98.** Briefly I have named these branches;
by hearing these names all desires are fulfilled.

**99.** Glory, glory to the sub-branches, renowned in the world!
I shall name just a few, to purify myself.

**100.** The branch of Ramakrishna Acharya — he has many disciples;
I shall say a little: I cannot describe them in full.

**101.** The Acharya's wife was named Kanakalatika —
bhakti embodied, devoted to her husband, abounding in virtues.

**102.** The Acharya's eldest son was Radha-Krishna Acharya;
this great noble soul withdrew from sight at a young age.

**103.** Radha-Krishna Chakravarti of Betulya
was ardently eager to practise every limb of bhakti.

**104.** Sri Svarupa Chakravarti, wise in every way,
lived in Husanpura, serving Sri Govinda.

**105.** In Kumarapura was Sri Gokula Chakravarti,[^3]
whose glory all people sing.

**106.** Such branches and sub-branches are beyond counting;
the life and soul of all of them is Sri Krishna Chaitanya.

**107.** A branch of Sri Mahashaya is Ganganarayana,
whom all call Sri Thakura Chakravarti.

**108.** Who does not pine for Ganganarayana's virtues?
Even today the wise sing his fame in Vrindavana.

**109.** *Thus in the Stava-amrita-lahari:*
*"He whose fame is still renowned in Vrindavana*
*and sung in the assemblies of the saints —*
*may that Sri Chakravarti, Ganganarayana,*
*ocean of the taste of prema, be merciful to me!"*[^4]

**110.** Very learned, an abode of compassion —
he has many branches; here I name a few.

**111.** Sri Chakravarti's wife was named Narayani;
famed in the world is her daughter Vishnupriya.

**112.** Vishnupriya's daughter was Krishnapriya, a treasury of bhakti,
favoured by Sri Radha, dwelling at Radha-kunda.

**113.** The merciful Sri Krishnacharana Chakravarti
was the younger son of Ramakrishna Acharya.

**114.** Sri Krishnacharana's virtues cannot be described;
how he became a disciple I shall tell briefly.

**115.** Ramakrishna and Ganganarayana were one soul:
only their bodies differed — people regarded them as one.

**116.** Sri Thakura Chakravarti had no children —
who can understand his inexpressible ways?

**117.** The Acharya, knowing his wish, with a glad heart
soon gave his son to Ganganarayana.[^5]

**118.** Sri Krishnacharana, relishing the taste of bhakti,
counts logicians and other unbelievers as nothing.

**119.** *Thus in the Stava-amrita-lahari:*
*"He who, alas, among ascetics, sannyasis, followers of karma*
*and excessive logicians, proud in the splendour of each displaying his own doctrine,*
*shines by the power of bhakti like the sun in darkness —*
*may that great Krishnacharana grant us the nectar of his feet!"*[^6]

**120.** Another branch is Sri Madhusudana Chakravarti,
whose life and soul is Ganganarayana.

**121.** Raghudeva Bhattacharya, highly experienced —
Sri Thakura Chakravarti is subject to his love.

**122.** Sri Chakravarti's branches and sub-branches —
who could describe them? They have filled the world.

**123.** The other branches, the branches of branches and the sub-branches
I have not described, for fear of lengthening the book.

**124.** The lovely branches of Sri Mahashaya
are ceaselessly absorbed in the bliss of sankirtana.

**125.** All these branches fulfilled the desire —
the most wondrous pastimes of Sri Mahashaya.

**126.** To describe this is beyond my power;
my only hope here is the Vaishnava gosanis.

**127.** Hear all this always with care:
Narahari tells the *Narottama-vilasa*.

*Thus ends the twelfth vilasa of the Narottama-vilasa, called "The Description of the Branches and Sub-branches of Thakura Narottama".*

*Thus the Sri Narottama-vilasa is complete.*

[^1]: This Sanskrit passage from the prologue of Govinda Kaviraja's *Sangita-madhava* (the sutradhara's speech) is found only in the 2021 edition; the 1924 edition lacks it. "Younger brother in the paternal realm": Santosha, son of Purushottama Datta, was Narottama's cousin (Narottama being the son of Krishnananda Datta). "In the manner of the world" (laukika-ritya) means the lila is portrayed by theatrical means after the model of a worldly drama; the lila of Radha-Madhava itself, according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, is transcendent and has nothing in common with mundane love.
[^2]: So in the 1924 edition; the 2021 edition reads Phagu Chaudhuri.
[^3]: So in the 1924 edition; the 2021 edition reads Gopala Chakravarti.
[^4]: The *Stava-amrita-lahari* is a collection of prayers by Vishvanatha Chakravarti; this verse is from his praise of Ganganarayana Chakravarti, a predecessor in his line of succession (Ganganarayana — Krishnacharana — Radharamana — Vishvanatha).
[^5]: Krishnacharana, son of Ramakrishna Acharya, became the adopted son and disciple of Ganganarayana; his disciple was Radharamana Chakravarti, the guru of Vishvanatha Chakravarti.
[^6]: From Vishvanatha Chakravarti's *Stava-amrita-lahari*, a praise of Krishnacharana. This verse is found only in the 2021 edition; the 1924 edition lacks it.
