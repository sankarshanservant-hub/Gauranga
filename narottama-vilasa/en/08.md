# Vilasa 8. The Farewell of the Vaishnavas from Kheturi

*The second day of the festival: the feeding of the mahantas and Raja Santosha's gifts; the conversion of the pashandis; the farewell of Jahnava and the mahantas; the crossing of the Padmavati; the night kirtana with Vasu Ghosha's song; the mahantas return home; Srinivasa and Shyamananda leave Kheturi*

**1.** Glory to Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita with their associates!
Be merciful, Prabhu, to this wretched beggar.

**2.** Glory, glory to the listeners, oceans of mercy!
Hear what I shall now relate.

**3.** At the time of Sri Gaurachandra's evening arati
all the mahantas came to Gauranga's house.

**4.** Seeing the arati, all were greatly pleased,
and the pujari gave everyone tulasi and flower garlands.

**5.** All began the sankirtana of Krishna's name,
hearing which the ears and mind are satisfied.

**6.** After a while, finishing the sankirtana of the name,
all went to their lodgings in supreme bliss.

**7.** Meanwhile the pujari, having offered various foods to Prabhu,
joyfully removed the offering

**8.** and sent it to everyone separately;
seeing those foods and smelling their fragrance, all were delighted.

**9.** Though not hungry at all, still, praising them,
they ate, and their hearts overflowed with prema.

**10.** When all had received prasada and grown calm,
the Acharya said respectfully everywhere:

**11.** "This Santosha Raya is the servant of you all;
please fulfil his wish."

**12.** Hearing this, all said with great affection:
"His wish will be fulfilled — of that there is no doubt."

**13.** Sri Acharya Mahashaya, greatly pleased,
came quickly with his people to Prabhu's house.

**14.** The pujari, having finished all Prabhu's service,
joyfully gave everyone tulasi and garlands.

**15.** Sri Acharya, the Mahashaya and Shyamananda, the three,
ate a little prasada together with everyone.

**16.** Sri Acharya instructed all whom he had earlier appointed
to their places to be careful in every way.

**17.** By the time everything was done, much of the night had passed,
and all lay down to sleep in their own places.

**18.** At dawn, having finished their morning duties,
all quickly bathed and so on.

**19.** Meanwhile the mahantas' cooks
first bathed and performed their daily rites,

**20.** circumambulated Sri Tulasi, bowed,
and all made ready in the kitchens.

**21.** Ramachandra Kaviraja and others went there,
each to the storeroom to which he had been appointed.

**22.** Everywhere they gave the storeroom attendants
all the provisions for cooking.

**23.** Those appointed to each place, taking all the provisions,
went joyfully to the mahantas' lodgings

**24.** and handed everything over
to the cooks of each of the mahantas.

**25.** Seeing the various provisions, all were pleased
and handed them to the kitchen helpers.

**26.** They prepared everything — greens and other dishes —
and the cooks quickly set about cooking.

**27.** Ramachandra Kaviraja and the others
remained at their posts with great care.

**28.** Meanwhile Sri Santosha Raya prepared
boxes with betel and other things, most exquisite,

**29.** plates, bowls, jugs and so on of wonderful workmanship,
gold and silver coins, silk cloths, seats.

**30.** All this he meant to present to each of the mahantas,
and for that he arranged everything separately.

**31.** Sri Santosha Raya, going to Sri Ishvari,
told her of this and received her permission.

**32.** Where all the mahantas had gladly gone to bathe,
there Sri Santosha brought all this.

**33.** As soon as he presented it to all the mahantas,
they affectionately put on the silk cloths at once.

**34.** Gladdening Sri Santosha with sweet words,
they sat on those seats to perform their daily rites.

**35.** To all who were with the mahantas
he gave each wonderful cloths, coins and so on.

**36.** Santosha, with great joy at heart,
came to where Sri Acharya Mahashaya was

**37.** and told how all had shown him mercy;
Sri Acharya Mahashaya rejoiced to hear it.

**38.** Prabhu's pujari said: "I have removed the offering
and set everything out separately."

**39.** Hearing this, Sri Acharya went joyfully,
taking fresh butter, curd cheese, various sweets and so on.

**40.** Going to Sri Ishvari and then to every place,
everyone partook of prasada in great happiness.

**41.** There the cooks of all the mahantas,
having cooked, offered the food to Prabhu.

**42.** After a while all removed the offering
and invited the holy mahantas to the meal.

**43.** All the wise ones in their own lodgings
sat in a circle to eat.

**44.** Someone quickly brought with joy
new jugs filled with scented water.

**45.** Whoever had cooked joyfully served
new plates with rice and other things arranged on them,

**46.** carefully served new bowls filled with milk and so on,
and all began the meal in great happiness.

**47.** Such was the fine arrangement of the meal everywhere;
Sri Acharya and others rejoiced greatly to see it.

**48.** In the courtyard of Sri Jahnava Ishvari's house —
I shall only name those who sat down to the meal:

**49.** Krishnadasa Sarakhela, Madhava Acharya,
Raghupati Upadhyaya, the best of Krishna's devotees,

**50.** Sri Minaketana Ramadasa, Mahidhara,
Murari Chaitanya, the charming Jnanadasa,

**51.** Kamalakara Piplai, Nrisimha Chaitanya,
Sri Jiva Pandita, who made the fallen blessed,

**52.** Sri Gauranga Dasa, Vrindavana, Sri Shankara,
Kanai, Nakadi, Krishnadasa, the best of brahmanas,

**53.** Parameshvara Dasa, Balarama, Damodara,
Mukunda and others — lovely was their beauty.

**54.** Where Sri Achyutananda sat to eat —
I shall only name those who sat with him:

**55.** Sri Achyutananda's younger brother Sri Gopala,
full of prema-bhakti, supremely merciful,

**56.** Sri Kanu Pandita, Vishnudasa, Narayana,
Vanamali Dasa, Sri Ananta, Janardana,

**57.** Sri Madhava, Lokanatha, Bhagavata Acharya —
seeing their beauty, who could keep composure?

**58.** Raghunatha Acharya with his companions
ate with great joy at heart.

**59.** Sri Vamshivadana's son Sri Chaitanya Dasa
ate with his people, rejoicing.

**60.** How their wonderful lodging shone!
Seeing the beauty of that circle, who could keep composure?

**61.** Sri Hridaya Chaitanya with everyone
ate merrily in his own lodging.

**62.** What a lovely circle around the courtyard!
Seeing that beauty, eyes and life are soothed.

**63.** Shripati, Shrinidhi, Krishnadasa, Sri Sanjaya,
Kashinatha, Mukunda, full of supreme bliss,

**64.** Shekhara Pandita, Krishnadasa Vaidya,
Shubhananda, the generous Sri Gopala Acharya,

**65.** the kirtaniya Kavichandra, Shashthivara and others
ate in one lodging — the limit of beauty.

**66.** Krishnadasa of Akaihat with his companions
ate in his own lodging, an embodiment of bliss.

**67.** Vaninatha, Shivananda, Vallabha Chaitanya,
the dancer Gopala, whose dancing blesses the earth,

**68.** Bhagavata Acharya, Jita Mishra, Raghu,
Sri Uddhava, the generous Kashinatha Pandita,

**69.** Sri Nayana Mishra, Sri Mangala — in one place
all these ate; there is no limit to that beauty.

**70.** Sri Raghunandana with Sulochana and others
ate in his own lodging in the great festivity of prema.

**71.** Even the gods long to see that circle;
what comparison can I give? The beauty is most wonderful.

**72.** Sri Yadunandana Chakravarti with his people
ate in his own lodging, an embodiment of bliss.

**73.** Acharya Thakura Mahashaya with his people
went about everywhere to see the joy of the meal.

**74.** Deeming himself blessed, he said again and again:
"Shall I ever see such a sight again?"

**75.** Meanwhile all the mahantas finished the meal,
rinsed their mouths and sat on their seats.

**76.** Prasada betel from new boxes
all chewed with gladness of heart.

**77.** Those everywhere who had waited to eat afterwards
ate one after another.

**78.** Ramachandra, Shyamananda and others, wherever they were,
ate at the command of all the mahantas.

**79.** And at the other circles of Vaishnavas in various places
there was no end to the people who ate.

**80.** Meanwhile the rice-prasada of Prabhu, purifier of the worlds,
was served by the pujari, and everyone ate.

**81.** Joyfully countless people, having eaten,
cried "Jaya! Jaya!", greatly intoxicated.

**82.** Even chandalas and others received the highest honour;
everything everywhere was arranged in every way.

**83.** Acharya Thakura and the Mahashaya, the two,
ate last of all, with supreme bliss at heart.

**84.** In every house there was a great festival —
even with a thousand mouths I could not describe it.

**85.** Whoever saw this bliss to his eyes' content —
birth after birth I would die for him.[^1]

**86.** In various places people, with gladness of heart,
in prema said to one another:

**87.** "O brother, this great festival we see
is rare even for the gods — how is it possible for men?"

**88.** Some said: "Who calls them men?
These associates of Chaitanya are worshipped by the gods."

**89.** Some said: "What more can one say, O brother?
Nothing is impossible for the associates of Sri Chaitanya."

**90.** Some said: "O brother, I saw it with my own eyes:
they maddened the pashandis with talk of Krishna."

**91.** Some said: "O brother, all the pashandis
eat the remnants of the Vaishnavas, overwhelmed."

**92.** Some said: "The pashandis say everywhere:
'Be merciful to me, Vaishnava Gosani!'"

**93.** Some said: "A pashandi rolls in the dust,
weeps aloud and goes about singing Gora's virtues."

**94.** Some said: "The pashandis are delivered —
no one is as fortunate as they."

**95.** Some said: "The pashandis who did not come here —
what will become of them? It pains me."

**96.** Some said: "No pashandis will remain:
by Narottama's mercy they will be delivered."

**97.** Some said: "O brother, I said it even then:
through Narottama this land has been blessed."

**98.** "Glory, glory to Narottama and his wondrous greatness,
by whose mercy we have seen this great festival!"

**99.** Thus people said many things with gladness of heart;
for fear of the book's length I do not describe them.

**100.** Meanwhile Srinivasa Acharya, in a secluded room,
having rested a moment, said to the Mahashaya:

**101.** "Tomorrow at daybreak all will leave,
cross the Padmavati and bathe.

**102.** It would be good to send prasada and fried delicacies with them,
so that all may eat on the bank of the Padmavati."

**103.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, hearing this, at once
had various fried delicacies carefully prepared,

**104.** offered them to Prabhu, set them apart
and arranged what was to be sent with them.

**105.** Coming to Sri Acharya, he reported everything;
by the time this was done, evening had come.

**106.** Meanwhile none of the mahantas' minds was at rest,
and they came out of their lodgings.

**107.** Even before Prabhu's arati, eagerly,
all came and stood in Prabhu's courtyard.

**108.** The pujari, giving everyone tulasi and flower garlands,
performed Prabhu's arati with gladness.

**109.** Having seen the arati, all the mahantas
sang the sankirtana of the name, and there was no end to their happiness.

**110.** Hearing it, hearts hard as wood and stone melted;
it was as if rivers of nectar flowed in every direction.

**111.** All the mahantas swam in the ocean of prema,
rolling in the dust, unable to contain themselves.

**112.** Their bodies were lovely to begin with,
and now became charming, grey with dust.

**113.** Whoever saw that beauty had his sufferings go far away,
and prema-bhakti showed mercy to them all.

**114.** Thus, having sung the sankirtana of the name for a watch,
all saw the arati of retiring.

**115.** The pujari again brought and gave garlands and tulasi,
and all took leave and went to their lodgings.

**116.** The Acharya, without composure within but showing steadiness outwardly,
carefully consoled and calmed Narottama.

**117.** Taking all the prasada fried delicacies row on row,
they went quickly to every lodging.

**118.** To all the mahantas he said again and again:
"Tomorrow this village of Kheturi will be plunged into darkness.

**119.** Crossing the Padmavati, on the bank of the Padmavati
all of you will bathe with gladdened hearts.

**120.** There you will eat these prasada delicacies;
by midday you will reach the village of Budhari.

**121.** Govinda and some others will go ahead,
and the cooks will go with them.

**122.** Ramachandra and others will go along with you,
and will come back here from Budhari.

**123.** Then Sri Ishvari will go to Vrindavana."
Having said so much, he made another request:

**124.** "Eat this maha-prasada now —
let this servant of yours see it with his eyes."

**125.** Before Srinivasa all ate the prasada,
their hearts distraught at the coming separation.

**126.** All honoured the Sri maha-prasada,
and the two[^2] fulfilled their own wish.

**127.** All the mahantas, wholly losing composure,
kept silent.

**128.** Acharya Thakura, going to the Ishvari,
told her everything softly.

**129.** Sri Ishvari, seeing the Acharya distraught,
with great care consoled and calmed him.

**130.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, with the greatest affection,
gave the Acharya the remnants of her own meal to eat.

**131.** Eating joyfully, he took some with him
and fed Narottama and the other dear ones.

**132.** How much bliss they felt eating Sri Jahnava Ishvari's prasada
they did not themselves know.

**133.** Acharya Thakura said to Santosha:
"Let the boats be ready very quickly."

**134.** Santosha said: "I sent a messenger in advance;
the boats are ready on the bank of the Padmavati."

**135.** Hearing this, Sri Acharya went joyfully to his lodging,
and all rested in their own places.

**136.** They slept a little, and the night ended;
rising, all performed their morning duties.

**137.** Having seen the mangala-arati,
all the cooks gathered together.

**138.** Sri Govinda Kaviraja and some others
set off quickly, taking them along.

**139.** They crossed the Padmavati quickly
and, reaching Budhari, bathed and so on.

**140.** Meanwhile the mahantas at daybreak
went to the Ishvari's lodging to take leave.

**141.** Sri Achyutananda said, weeping:
"My heart tells me I shall not see such a thing again."

**142.** Sri Gopala and others, in great distress,
said things that make the heart melt to hear.

**143.** Shripati, Shrinidhi and others, wishing to say something,
lost their composure; streams flowed from their eyes.

**144.** The brahmana Vaninatha and others spoke respectfully —
to hear it, hearts hard as wood and stone melt.

**145.** What Raghunatha Acharya and others said with aching hearts —
who could describe it?

**146.** Sri Hridaya Chaitanya entreated:
"Grant that I may soon see your holy feet again."

**147.** Sri Chand Haldar, Mitu Haldar and all the others
could say nothing, but fell to the ground and wept.

**148.** Sri Chaitanya Dasa and others wanted to say something,
but no words came from their mouths, their hearts were so distraught.

**149.** Sri Raghunandana said, most anxiously:
"Be merciful and let us see you again soon."

**150.** Sri Yadunandana said: "Come back soon from Vrindavana
to purify this sinner."

**151.** So, in great distress, the mahantas one by one
took leave and went to Prabhu's courtyard.

**152.** Sri Minaketana Ramadasa, Vrindavana,
Kamalakara Piplai and some others —

**153.** to all these the Ishvari gave the command to go to Khardaha;
having taken leave, no one could be calm.

**154.** When, having taken leave, all were going,
what the Ishvari said cannot be described.

**155.** All, gathered in Prabhu's courtyard,
became intoxicated with prema at the darshan of Prabhu.

**156.** Again and again they bowed, falling to the ground;
everyone's body grew grey with dust.

**157.** Before Prabhu they prayed for the welfare of the Acharya and the others;
all begged for the company of Sri Acharya and Narottama.

**158.** All said: "O Lotus-eyed One,
may we hear such sankirtana birth after birth!"

**159.** So, offering many prayers,
all took leave of Prabhu and went.

**160.** The pujari, in great distress,
at once gave everyone Prabhu's prasada cloths.

**161.** Taking the prasada cloths and placing them on their heads,
all went, wholly losing composure.

**162.** Sri Hridaya Chaitanya, embracing the Acharya,
said softly in prema:

**163.** "Come to Ambika now and then to see us,
and regard Shyamananda as your own."

**164.** The Acharya said: "Shyamananda is my life;
toward Shyamananda I have no other feeling.

**165.** Narottama, Ramachandra and all the others —
for all of them Shyamananda with his people is their life."

**166.** Hridaya Chaitanya, in great tenderness,
entrusted Shyamananda to Srinivasa.

**167.** The mercy Sri Hridaya Chaitanya had for Shyamananda —
what power have I to describe it?

**168.** All the mahantas said many such things
to Narottama and Srinivasa in sweet words.

**169.** When all, leaving Kheturi, had gone some distance,
a tumult of weeping rose in the village of Kheturi.

**170.** Children and old people all cried, "Alas, alas!
Tell us, who goes away like this?"

**171.** All the mahantas, hearing their words,
became such that what can I say?

**172.** After a while all came to the bank of the Padmavati,
and the Acharya and others consoled each one.

**173.** Embracing everyone in the assembly firmly,
all boarded the boats together with Ramachandra and others.

**174.** The helmsman quickly rowed the boats away,
and the Acharya and the others wept, rolling on the ground.

**175.** Seeing their state, all the mahantas
could not check their tears.

**176.** By Prabhu's will all grew calm,
and after a while they had crossed the Padmavati.

**177.** On the bank of the Padmavati all bathed
and, having eaten the prasada, went to the village of Budhari.

**178.** Meanwhile, by Prabhu's will, all, gathering their composure,
went from the bank of the Padmavati to Kheturi.

**179.** Acharya Thakura, Sri Thakura Mahashaya,
Shyamananda and others came to Prabhu's house.

**180.** The pujari came to Acharya Thakura and said:
"The Ishvari has only just bathed.

**181.** When the holy mahantas took leave and went,
she remained in seclusion, bathed in tears.

**182.** Madhava Acharya and others, gathering composure,
have only now bathed — I saw it and came."

**183.** Hearing this, the Acharya could not contain himself
and went to the Ishvari with a distraught heart.

**184.** The Ishvari, her own heart without composure, asked,
and the Acharya told her briefly:

**185.** "Having crossed the Padma, all have gone to Budhari,
and we, having bathed in the Padmavati, have come back."

**186.** Hearing this, the Ishvari looked at the Acharya
and saw that the Acharya's body had almost withered.

**187.** "The grief of separation is unbearable enough,
and on top of that he has eaten almost nothing since yesterday.

**188.** Today they have no thought of eating —
I do not know what will happen; I am afraid.

**189.** If I do not feed them, they will not eat" —
so thinking, she spoke sweet words:

**190.** "You have bathed and come back, and it is already afternoon,
and you have no thought of eating — it pains me.

**191.** Gather your composure, take everyone
and eat in my courtyard today."

**192.** Hearing this, the Acharya felt himself favoured
and called Narottama and all the others.

**193.** Seeing everyone's state, the Ishvari, distraught,
spoke with affection words that cannot be repeated.

**194.** Nrisimha Chaitanya said in sweet words:
"Take them all and sit quickly in the courtyard."

**195.** All sat in a lovely circle;
someone laid out the leaves.

**196.** Someone brought vessels full of water,
and the Ishvari gave everyone various fried delicacies.

**197.** At the Ishvari's command all ate,
and the Ishvari's heart rejoiced greatly.

**198.** Curd cheese, drinks, fresh butter, most sweet,
she gave to all again and again in abundance.

**199.** All ate, and prema surged in their hearts;
in their bliss they did not know who was eating what.

**200.** Having eaten, all rinsed their mouths,
and the Acharya's servants took up the leaves.

**201.** Taking the leaves and so on to another place,
all ate the remnants on the plates and were satisfied.

**202.** The Acharya and all the others, receiving the Ishvari's leave,
went joyfully to Prabhu's courtyard.

**203.** Someone carefully brought and gave them prasada betel;
having chewed it, all went to another house.

**204.** There they saw countless people seated,
joyfully eating Sri maha-prasada.

**205.** When all had honoured the maha-prasada,
all rose with the cry of "Hari!"

**206.** So everywhere they ate prasada;
there was no end to the circles of Vaishnavas.

**207.** Sorrowful at the departure of the Lord's associates,
by the Ishvari's will all became very happy.

**208.** Who can understand the Ishvari's will?
Only he understands to whom mercy is given.

**209.** So in great happiness the day ended,
and Sri Ishvari went to Prabhu's temple.

**210.** As she gazed her fill on the sweetness of Prabhu's form,
the pujari brought and gave her a garland-prasada.


**211.** It was time for the evening arati, and to see it
countless people came into Prabhu's courtyard.

**212.** Having seen Prabhu's lovely arati,
all together began the sankirtana of the name.

**213.** The sound of the sankirtana of the holy name filled the world;
children and old people — all went mad.

**214.** Gods, mingling with men, sang the name,
all rolling in the dust as their hearts desired.

**215.** Some danced with arms upraised,
some roared and thundered with heroic pride,

**216.** some leapt about clapping their hands,
some, embracing someone, were bathed in tears.

**217.** So moment by moment various transformations of bhava appeared;
who could describe the happiness there was in the sankirtana of the holy name?

**218.** Whoever drank the nectar of the sankirtana of the holy name —
who in the world is as fortunate as he?

**219.** All became so absorbed in the holy name
that no one knew how the night came to an end.

**220.** By Prabhu's will all stopped,
and Sri Jahnava Ishvari went joyfully to her lodging.

**221.** At dawn, having finished her morning duties,
she quickly bathed in warm water,

**222.** performed her regular rites with joy at heart,
and prepared to cook in her lodging.

**223.** Meanwhile the Acharya and all the others, having finished their morning duties,
bathed and performed their regular rites.

**224.** Having seen the raja-bhoga arati in the holy temple,
they came joyfully to Sri Ishvari.

**225.** The Ishvari, having cooked and offered to Prabhu,
removed the offering and came out and sat outside.

**226.** To the Acharya and others she said in sweet words:
"Ramachandra and the others have not come back yet."

**227.** Saying this, she looked anxiously in all directions —
and at that moment they all came from Budhari.

**228.** Ramachandra, Govinda and others, bowing to Prabhu,
on being asked, eagerly gave the news:

**229.** "Crossing the Padma, all bathed and did their rites,
ate prasada and went quickly to Budhari.

**230.** There the cooks quickly prepared food
and carefully offered it to Prabhu.

**231.** Prabhu ate, the offering was removed,
and at that moment all the mahantas arrived there.

**232.** After resting a while, all
ate happily, talking of what had happened here.

**233.** By the time the meal and the rest were done, evening had come,
and all sang the sankirtana of the name for a while.

**234.** Eating a little prasada at night,
all lay down to sleep with anxious hearts.

**235.** Rising at dawn, they finished their morning duties
and, knowing us to be their servants, showed us great mercy.

**236.** What everyone was like at the parting —
it will not come from my mouth.

**237.** Our hearts are like stone:
we came back calmly across the Padmavati."

**238.** Saying this, they could say no more,
and the Ishvari with great affection consoled them all.

**239.** All were sprinkled with the nectar of the Ishvari's words,
and suddenly bliss arose in their hearts.

**240.** Manifesting joy in everyone's heart, the Ishvari
carefully fed them rice, dishes and so on.

**241.** Sri Ishvari ate, taking the remnants from those plates,
and the Acharya went on joyfully with everyone.

**242.** He saw many people in Prabhu's courtyard
eating likewise — eating everywhere.

**243.** Honouring everyone, Acharya Mahashaya
spoke words of consolation to Santosha and the others:

**244.** "By the Ishvari's mercy everything has been accomplished;
her mercy has spread everywhere.

**245.** She is eager to go to Vrindavana;
now we cannot keep her here by insisting.

**246.** When she comes back from Vrindavana,
you will see her holy feet at leisure.

**247.** For now do not think of any of this:
the Ishvari will set out tomorrow morning."

**248.** Hearing this, Santosha Raya after a while
went to the Ishvari with a distraught heart.

**249.** Knowing Santosha's heart, Sri Ishvari
spoke words of consolation with great affection.

**250.** Sri Santosha said: "For the sake of this fallen one
come back quickly from Vraja.

**251.** What arises in my heart I cannot say."
Hearing this, the Ishvari consoled him with gentle words.

**252.** Sri Santosha Raya was greatly comforted
and quickly had brought what he would give her to take:

**253.** finest silks and other many-coloured cloths,
gold ornaments set with various jewels,

**254.** to be presented to Sri Govinda, Gopinatha, Madanamohana,
Sri Radha-vinoda and Sri Radharamana,

**255.** and Radha-Damodara; arranging all beautifully,
he carefully placed it before the Ishvari.

**256.** Again he gave many gold and silver coins and things
and arranged everything needed for the journey.

**257.** There is no limit to Sri Santosha Raya's good fortune:
Lakshmi herself accepted his wealth![^3]

**258.** Seeing all ready and nothing left to wait for,
Sri Jahnava Ishvari was very happy.

**259.** For the darshan of the evening arati in the holy temple
the Ishvari went with supreme joy at heart.

**260.** Having seen Prabhu's arati,
she submitted what was in her heart.

**261.** As the garland slipped from Prabhu's neck,
the pujari brought it and put it in the Ishvari's hands.

**262.** The Ishvari placed that garland on her head;
who can understand what was in the Ishvari's heart?

**263.** Before Prabhu there was singing of the name and so on as before;
what can I say of how Sri Ishvari went to her lodging?

**264.** She lay down, and morning came;
all performed their morning duties with distraught hearts.

**265.** Sri Ishvari took leave of Prabhu,
and the pujari brought and gave her many prasada garlands.

**266.** Of those who went with Sri Ishvari
I shall name some:

**267.** Suryadasa's younger brother Sri Pandita Krishnadasa,
Madhava Acharya, whose pastimes are wonderful,

**268.** Murari Chaitanya, Krishnadasa the best of brahmanas,
Nrisimha Chaitanya, Balarama, Mahidhara,

**269.** Kanai, Nakadi Dasa, Gauranga, Shankara,
Sri Parameshvara Dasa, Dasa Damodara,

**270.** Raghupati Vaidya, the charming Upadhyaya,
Jnanadasa, Mukunda and others, oceans of virtue.

**271.** Who could describe their power?
They are supremely skilled in subduing wicked pashandis.

**272.** With these companions, and at the Ishvari's command,
some others went from Kheturi:

**273.** Sri Govinda, Sri Gopiramana, Bhagavan,
Gokula, Nrisimha, Vasudeva and other leading ones.

**274.** With all these Sri Jahnava, at an auspicious moment,
set out from Kheturi at daybreak.

**275.** The people of the village of Sri Kheturi had no composure:
at the Ishvari's departure all wept everywhere.

**276.** Acharya Thakura with Sri Narottama and others,
weeping and weeping, went with her some distance.

**277.** Sri Jahnava, affection embodied,
consoled them with words, though her own heart was without composure.

**278.** Sending them all back with sweet words,
she went quickly on ahead in her palanquin.

**279.** Krishnadasa, Madhava Acharya and all the others
could not check the incessant tears from their eyes.

**280.** For Sri Acharya, the Mahashaya, Shyamananda and the others
sorrow reached its limit.

**281.** Saying many things to one another, they parted —
who could keep composure hearing all that?

**282.** When Sri Govinda and others were taking leave,
the Acharya and Sri Narottama could not be calm.

**283.** Saying many things to all, they sent them off,
and all went, bathed in tears.

**284.** The Acharya and all the others, gazing at the road they had taken,
came back to the village of Kheturi in distress.

**285.** The people of the village of Kheturi, as if dead,
sat in solitude singing Sri Jahnava's virtues.

**286.** Someone, steadying himself with effort, said to another:
"The Ishvari will come back soon from Vrindavana."

**287.** Someone said: "She will go home by another road;
what business will she have to come here again?"

**288.** Someone said: "This Sri Acharya Mahashaya
has surely won her over by the power of bhakti."

**289.** Someone said: "She is subdued by the prema of them all —
you will see it yourselves, let a few days pass."

**290.** So, saying many things to one another, they steadied themselves,
and suddenly happiness came into everyone's heart.

**291.** Meanwhile Sri Acharya, Sri Thakura Mahashaya,
Shyamananda and others came to Prabhu's house.

**292.** By the Ishvari's will all regained composure,
and bliss arose in everyone's heart.

**293.** All happily finished their bathing and daily rites
and saw the raja-bhoga arati.

**294.** Acharya Thakura, going to all the Vaishnavas' lodgings,
called everyone and came.

**295.** Having fed everyone Sri maha-prasada,
he sat with his own people in Prabhu's courtyard.

**296.** What a wondrous beauty, lovely to see:
all their bodies were full of prema-bhakti.

**297.** Prabhu's cooks, with gladness of heart,
carefully served rice, dishes and so on.

**298.** Acharya Thakura, Ramachandra Mahashaya,
Shridasa, Gokulananda, abodes of virtue,

**299.** Shyamananda, Vyasa, Ramakrishna and others merrily
ate greens, soups and so on, praising them in great happiness.

**300.** Having eaten happily and rinsed their mouths,
they carefully chewed prasada betel.

**301.** Acharya Mahashaya sat with everyone,
and all their hearts were immersed in the relish of talks about Krishna.

**302.** Fortunate people heard them;
for fear of the book's length I do not describe them.

**303.** At the end of the day, having finished their tasks,
all came with great joy to Prabhu's courtyard.

**304.** All the Vaishnavas who were in various places
came to Prabhu's courtyard too.

**305.** Understanding that they wished to take leave,
the Acharya said to them all in private:

**306.** "This is your place — what more is there to say?
Come here from time to time.

**307.** See, today the day is already over;
tomorrow morning you will set out for your homes.

**308.** Whatever wish Santosha Raya has,
regard him as your own and fulfil it."

**309.** All were sprinkled with the nectar of the Acharya's words
and, having seen the arati of awakening, went to their lodgings.

**310.** Sri Santosha Raya, going to them,
entreated them humbly in many sweet words.

**311.** Seeing Santosha Raya's conduct,
everyone's heart rejoiced greatly.

**312.** Sri Santosha, with their permission,
carefully gave each one cloths, coins and so on.

**313.** Meanwhile it was time for the evening arati,
and all came again to Prabhu's house.

**314.** They saw the evening arati,
and the lovely sankirtana of the holy name began.

**315.** Greatly rejoicing at drinking the nectar of the name,
having seen the arati of retiring, all went to their lodgings.

**316.** Srinivasa Acharya and others stayed for a while
in Prabhu's courtyard with their own people.

**317.** Having passed part of the night in talk of Prabhu,
they went to their lodgings and lay down.

**318.** At daybreak the Acharya and all the others
came for the darshan of the mangala-arati.

**319.** The Vaishnavas who were going home
also came and saw the arati.

**320.** They all took leave of Prabhu,
and the pujari gave everyone prasada garlands.

**321.** What they were like as they parted from one another —
to see it, a heart like wood melts.

**322.** All went, wholly without composure,
and the Acharya and the others stayed, gazing after them.

**323.** So people from various lands, with distraught hearts,
left Kheturi — and could hardly walk.

**324.** The distinguished brahmanas went to their homes,
telling one another of the glory of the festival.

**325.** The bards took their leave joyfully
and described the great festival at length.

**326.** Players of various instruments, singers, dancers and others
took their leave — their happiness was boundless.

**327.** Thousands upon thousands went in one crowd,
telling of the bliss of the kirtana, bathed in tears.

**328.** The poor and the suffering became happy in every way;
the fame of the great festival spread through the world.

**329.** Seeing the procession of people, someone said to another:
"Who has the power to count so many people?"

**330.** Someone said: "I saw that there was no end to the people —
how did they fit into the village of Kheturi?"

**331.** Someone said, laughing: "It is not impossible:
what cannot happen by Narottama's power?"

**332.** Someone said: "This is proof of Narottama's power;
otherwise how could so many people be provided for?"

**333.** So people said many things in sweet words,
singing Narottama's virtues with gladness of heart.

**334.** Meanwhile, at Narottama's request, Sri Acharya
bathed together with Narottama and others.

**335.** All, having finished their regular rites,
carefully ate a few sweets and so on.

**336.** Narottama and Srinivasa Acharya, the two,
were in seclusion talking of something — I do not know what.

**337.** Both, bathed in the tears of their own eyes,
went with everyone to see Prabhu.

**338.** Having seen the raja-bhoga arati
and eaten Prabhu's rice-prasada and so on,

**339.** having rinsed their mouths, all sat on their seats
and ate prasada roots.

**340.** To Sri Thakura Mahashaya and the Kaviraja
the Acharya, steadying himself with great effort, said:

**341.** "In the morning I shall set out with Shyamananda,
cross the Padma and go to the village of Budhari.

**342.** I shall go to Yajigrama, and from there very soon,
by way of Vana-Vishnupura, I shall hasten back.

**343.** Shyamananda, by way of Navadvipa and Ambika,
will go and stay at Dharenda-Bahadurpura.

**344.** Spreading bhakti in those lands,
he will soon send news by letter.

**345.** From Yajigrama I shall write all the news
and send it quickly by a man.

**346.** When Shrimati Ishvari comes back this way,
send a letter quickly to Yajigrama.

**347.** The Ishvari will travel by that road;
from here we shall all go with her.

**348.** When the Ishvari departs from there,
we shall all come quickly to the village of Kheturi."

**349.** Acharya Thakura said many such things;
as soon as they heard it, everyone's composure went far away.

**350.** Still all steadied themselves,
and Santosha made preparations for the journey.

**351.** He quickly sent a letter to the village of Budhari
and had boats made ready on the bank of the Padma.

**352.** What was to go with Sri Shyamananda
he entrusted to Sri Rasikananda.

**353.** What Sri Acharya Thakura needed to take
he gave to Kaviraja Karnapura.

**354.** So Sri Santosha arranged everything
and, coming before the Thakura, reported it all.

**355.** Hearing this, the Acharya, most pleased at heart,
went with everyone to Prabhu's storeroom.

**356.** He saw all the provisions there full;
so he looked into every storeroom.

**357.** Again and again he said, "Fortunate is Santosha:
he prepared the provisions so that they are inexhaustible."

**358.** Saying many such things, they came to Prabhu's courtyard,
and there was bliss at the darshan of the evening arati.

**359.** The pujari gave everyone garland-prasada,
and a wondrous beauty appeared on everyone's neck.

**360.** Gazing at the sweetness of Prabhu's form,
everyone's eyes ceased to blink.

**361.** Acharya Thakura could not contain himself
and looked again and again at Sri Narottama.

**362.** Knowing the Acharya's heart, the Mahashaya
began the Sri sankirtana, the abode of happiness.

**363.** The singers and musicians came at once
into Prabhu's courtyard with khols and karatalas.

**364.** Devidasa, Gokula, Gauranga and all the others
played the khols and karatalas most wonderfully.

**365.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, with gladness of heart,
began the alapa of a song composed by Vasu Ghosha.

**366.** Thus in the song:[^4]
*Friend, look at Gora's body!*
*His face surpasses so many moons, His lips are lovely.*
*His arms surpass the trunk of the best of elephants,*
*the dance of Gora's eyes surpasses the wagtails.*
*On His lovely forehead shines a tilaka of sandal,*
*His arms reach to His knees, a garland of forest flowers on His neck.*
*A conch-like neck, a broad, full chest,*
*and on it, anointed with sandal, many necklaces of gems.*
*Thighs more lovely than plantain stems, a cloth the colour of dawn,*
*His jewel-like nails surpass a host of full moons.*
*Vasu Ghosha says: where was Gora all this time?*
*The Creator fashioned this beauty to slay young maidens.*

**367.** How the alapa of the song sounded cannot be told;
the mridangas and others played, drawing all hearts.

**368.** The sound of the mridanga is nectar, the alapa sweet;
hearing it, Acharya Thakura became intoxicated with prema.

**369.** He stood in a graceful pose to dance —
who could keep composure seeing that sweet pose?

**370.** How the thrill shines on his body!
By his beauty he destroys the pride of so many golden mirrors.

**371.** A gentle smile on his moon-like face,
red lips, teeth like kunda flowers.

**372.** Lovely lotus eyes reaching to the ears,
brows a line of bees, a nose like a parrot's beak.

**373.** Lovely ears and gleaming cheeks,
arms reaching to the knees, a broad chest.

**374.** A sweet navel, an incomparable waist,
shapely knees, lovely feet.

**375.** What wondrous beauty in the ecstasy of bhava!
He dances, and people watch from all sides.

**376.** Though many people had left Kheturi,
still many distinguished people remained.

**377.** All the residents of Kheturi, gathering together,
came running into Prabhu's courtyard.

**378.** Hundreds of lamps are burning, the earth shines,
and again and again the people cry "Jaya!"

**379.** To see Srinivasa Acharya's dancing
the gods came riding on their aerial cars.

**380.** The gandharvas and kinnaras said to one another:
"Such dancing is never possible for a human being."

**381.** Some said: "There is no such dancing in the world of the gods;
this dancing is possible only for a servant of Chaitanya."

**382.** Some said: "As incomparable as the song and music are,
so incomparable is the dancing that blesses the world."

**383.** So, saying many things and losing composure,
they watched the wondrous dancing, mingling with men.

**384.** Seeing the various turns of the dance,
the gods joyfully showered flowers.

**385.** All sang the glory of the song, dance and music
and, leaving their cars, came and mingled with men.

**386.** Neither gods nor men could be calm:
the song, music and dance stole their hearts.

**387.** The Acharya danced, forgetting himself,
embracing Devidasa, bathed in tears.

**388.** Devidasa played the khol in many ways,
beating the rhythm — who, hearing it, could keep composure?

**389.** Sri Gokula sang, sweetly articulating the words,
and with gestures of the hands and so on abundantly showed the bhavas.

**390.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, embracing him,
cried "Sing, sing!" and was bathed in tears.

**391.** Shyamananda, losing composure in bhava,
was drenched with the streams from both his eyes.

**392.** Ramachandra Kaviraja and others, in prema,
went about all around, grey with dust.

**393.** In the sankirtana the ocean of happiness surged up;
the marvels that took place I cannot describe.

**394.** No one had outer consciousness in the ecstasy of the kirtana;
by Prabhu's will they grew calm at the end of the night.

**395.** Having finished the sankirtana, in Prabhu's courtyard
all rolled in the dust, tears in everyone's eyes.

**396.** Embracing one another firmly,
all bowed to one another as was fitting.

**397.** Going quickly to their lodgings,
having rested, all finished their morning duties.

**398.** Sri Acharya Thakura with some people,
ready for the journey, came to Prabhu's courtyard.

**399.** Shyamananda, ready with his people,
came with them all to Prabhu's courtyard.

**400.** Narottama and Ramachandra, with distraught hearts,
came to Prabhu's house with Santosha and others.

**401.** Hearing of the Acharya's departure, in distress
the people of the village of Kheturi came running.

**402.** In Prabhu's courtyard there was a great crowd;
women and men — all were without composure.

**403.** Acharya Thakura, gazing at Prabhu,
felt his heart breaking as he took leave.

**404.** Shyamananda, bowing to the ground before Prabhu,
took leave, saying many things with love.

**405.** The pujari, bringing a garland and a prasada cloth,
presented them to Acharya Thakura.

**406.** The Acharya gave garlands and cloths to everyone,
and himself carefully placed them on his head.

**407.** Outwardly showing composure and consoling everyone,
he set out from Kheturi at an auspicious moment.

**408.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya became distraught,
and Ramachandra Kaviraja carefully consoled him.

**409.** Coming to the bank of the Padmavati, Acharya Thakura
quickly boarded the boat — composure went far away.

**410.** What Sri Thakura Mahashaya said to Shyamananda —
what power have I to tell it?

**411.** Shyamananda floated in the tears of both his eyes;
Narottama wept, embracing Shyamananda.

**412.** So all wept with one another —
who could keep composure hearing that weeping?

**413.** After a while Ramachandra consoled everyone,
and Shyamananda with his people boarded the boat.

**414.** The helmsman steered the boat quickly;
crossing the Padma, they went quickly to Budhari.

**415.** Meanwhile the Mahashaya, having bathed with everyone,
came to Kheturi with a greatly distraught heart.

**416.** As soon as all reached Prabhu's courtyard,
bliss suddenly arose in their hearts.

**417.** Glory, glory to the holy courtyard full of the bliss of prema,
where the son of Sri Shachi dances with His associates!

**418.** Whoever saw this lovely courtyard
became grey with the dust of this courtyard.

**419.** Whoever meditates on this courtyard —
there is none in the world as fortunate as he.

**420.** In Prabhu's courtyard the pujari came
and respectfully said to Sri Thakura Mahashaya:

**421.** "The raja-bhoga arati was long ago;
please honour the Sri prasada with everyone."

**422.** Hearing this, Sri Thakura Mahashaya joyfully
ate Sri maha-prasada with everyone.

**423.** How much bliss the people of Kheturi felt eating the prasada
they did not themselves know.

**424.** That day many pashandis came,
and they too ate the prasada.

**425.** Eating the prasada, bhakti awoke in them,
and with tears they said to one another:

**426.** "O brother, our lives are in vain:
we have done so much evil it cannot be counted."

**427.** Someone said: "What is to become of us now?
Who will save us from Yama's punishment?"

**428.** Someone said: "This Thakura Narottama
will deliver us, seeing that we are fallen and low."

**429.** Someone said: "My body trembles to go before him."
Someone said: "Let us go and fall at his feet."

**430.** Saying many such things, all, weeping and weeping,
fell before Narottama, rolling on the ground.

**431.** The ocean of mercy, Sri Thakura Mahashaya,
spoke to them in the sweetest words:

**432.** "Check your tears: you are all blessed —
Sri Krishna Chaitanya will deliver you all."

**433.** Hearing the Mahashaya's words, joyfully,
with joined palms, they said softly:

**434.** "O Prabhu, all the evil deeds people speak of
we did without any fear.

**435.** We were not in this land; we had gone to other lands,
and after robbing we came home yesterday.

**436.** Seeing us, our own companions —
what shall we say? — how they reviled us!

**437.** They were great evil-doers and wicked men,
and by Prabhu's mercy they have become Vaishnavas.

**438.** O Prabhu, be merciful to us —
let your spotless fame resound in the world."

**439.** Hearing such words, he was filled with boundless mercy,
and the Thakura instructed them:

**440.** "All of you, keep the company of sadhus continually,
hear and chant in great humility.

**441.** Always be careful with the Vaishnavas,
so that in no way is anyone disrespected."

**442.** Saying many such things, he said again and again:
"Make this mantra of Hari-nama your all."

**443.** Saying this, in prema he spread his arms
and said gently, "Come, come," embracing them.

**444.** Seeing his mercy, all fell to the ground,
touched his feet to their heads and were bathed in tears.

**445.** Their good fortune cannot be told:
without effort prema-bhakti awoke in them.

**446.** Obtaining a treasure rare even for the gods,
they could not contain themselves; their hearts overflowed with bliss.

**447.** Thus he destroys the misdeeds of all the pashandis;
whoever hears of it obtains pure bhakti.

**448.** The giver of prema-bhakti, Sri Thakura Mahashaya,
was anxious, having no news of the Acharya.

**449.** As Ramachandra was going to his lodging to send a man,
at that moment great auspicious signs appeared:

**450.** the Acharya's letter came from Yajigrama,
and reading the letter, they rejoiced at heart.

**451.** The Mahashaya, sending a letter in reply,
spent his time joyfully with Ramachandra.

**452.** They told each other the Acharya's virtues,
hearing which one is freed from sorrow.

**453.** Listen to all this continually and with care:
Narahari tells the *Narottama-vilasa*.

*Thus ends the eighth vilasa of the Narottama-vilasa.*

[^1]: Literally, "birth after birth I will take his misfortunes upon myself and die" — a common Bengali formula of love and blessing.
[^2]: That is, Srinivasa and Narottama, who had longed to see the mahantas eat prasada.
[^3]: That is, Jahnava is a manifestation of the Lord's shakti (according to the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika*, Revati / Ananga Manjari), and to have a gift accepted by her is the greatest good fortune.
[^4]: A pada by Vasu Ghosha (Vasudeva Ghosha), an associate of the Lord, in the mood of nagari-bhava. The last line, "The Creator fashioned this beauty to slay young maidens," is to be taken as praise of Gaura's irresistible beauty; Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati did not accept the "Gaura-nagari" teaching, in which Gaura becomes an object of worldly attraction.
