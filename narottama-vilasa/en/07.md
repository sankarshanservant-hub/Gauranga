# Vilasa 7. The Installation of the Six Deities and the Great Festival in Kheturi

*Talk of the festival; the preparations; on Phalguna-purnima Srinivasa performs the abhisheka of the six Deities; Narottama begins the sankirtana; the Lord with His associates appears in the kirtana; the Holi festival; the feeding of the Vaishnavas*

**1.** Glory to Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita with their associates!
Be merciful, Prabhu, to this wretched beggar.

**2.** Glory, glory to the listeners, oceans of mercy!
Hear what I shall now relate.

**3.** The news of the great festival in the village of Sri Kheturi
spread through every land.

**4.** With great joy at heart people said to one another:
"O brother, what a marvel I have seen with my eyes!

**5.** Blessed on all the earth is the village of Sri Kheturi —
what wondrous beauty, like an abode of bliss!

**6.** Women and men, children and old people there
shed tears ceaselessly at the sight of the Vaishnavas.

**7.** Today many Vaishnavas have come to Kheturi,
and people, forgetting themselves, run from all sides."

**8.** Some, seeing that sweetness,
begged the Creator for countless eyes.

**9.** Some said: "Their radiance is like the sun's;
it destroys the cruel darkness of the sufferings of living beings."

**10.** Some said: "By the mercy of a glance from them
even one who would not say 'Krishna' sings Krishna's virtues."

**11.** Some said: "Wondrous are their ways:
they have the greatest love for the fallen and suffering."

**12.** Some said: "Fortunate is Raja Sri Santosha:
what wonderful lodgings he has made for them!"

**13.** Some said: "In preparing the great festival
the raja and his people are always rejoicing."

**14.** Some said: "The stores he has prepared —
I have no power to describe them."

**15.** Some said: "All the auspicious rites customary among people
the raja performs with care."

**16.** Some said: "On the bright fifth day of Phalguna
he told the musicians to begin playing."

**17.** Some said: "The sound of the music pierced the sky;
singers sing, dancers dance."

**18.** Some said: "The raja told the gardeners
to bring various flowers and carefully make garlands."

**19.** Some said: "The raja told many people
to grind fine sandal carefully."

**20.** Some said: "At the Mahashaya's command
they joyfully made ready the things for the abhisheka.

**21.** Tomorrow is the holy full moon, a wondrous day:
the Deities will be ceremonially brought into the holy temple."

**22.** Some said: "O brother, I cannot say it —
I shall leave everything and go quickly to Kheturi."

**23.** Someone, after a silence, said: "This is what has happened:
Sri Thakura Mahashaya has made this land blessed.

**24.** The people of this land are skilled in banditry;
they do not know what dharma is or what proper deeds are.

**25.** Who could tell all the evil they do?
The blood of goats, sheep and buffaloes is at their doors."

**26.** Some said: "Taking a severed human head,
sword in hand, they dance, intoxicated.

**27.** If at that time anyone goes along that road,
even a brahmana does not escape their hands.

**28.** All are lechers, with no regard for caste;
they never eat without wine and meat.

**29.** O brother, I am firmly convinced:
Narottama will deliver them all."

**30.** Crying "Glory to Narottama! Glory to Narottama!"
they danced with arms upraised, tears streaming from their eyes.

**31.** Taking various gifts, in great excitement,
all came quickly to the village of Sri Kheturi.

**32.** So from many villages came many people;
as soon as they entered Kheturi they forgot all grief.

**33.** Meanwhile Sri Thakura Mahashaya, with sweet words,
dispelled the sorrows of all the people.

**34.** Having arranged everything thus, with gladness of heart,
in the evening he said to the Acharya:

**35.** "Many khols and karatalas have been made and brought here —
please go and look at them once."

**36.** Hearing this, Sri Acharya went gladly,
taking Gauranga, Gokula and Devidasa with him.

**37.** Going there and seeing all the khols and karatalas,
the Acharya said in prema, "Good, good!"

**38.** Remembering Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita,
he at once performed puja of the khols and karatalas.

**39.** With everyone he went to where Sri Ishvari was
and set out in order everything about the abhisheka.

**40.** Receiving her permission, they went everywhere
and told everyone about the abhisheka.

**41.** Hearing it, joy grew in everyone's heart,
and they spent that night in talks about Sri Chaitanya.

**42.** They slept a little, and the night turned to morning;
all performed their morning duties and bathed.

**43.** Meanwhile Sri Acharya and Sri Thakura Mahashaya
went to every lodging with wonderful cloths.

**44.** To all the mahantas and all who were with the mahantas
they offered cloths with great insistence.

**45.** Meanwhile Sri Santosha Raya, with great joy,
was seeing how well the canopy looked over the courtyard.

**46.** The courtyard of the holy temple is very spacious
and adorned beautifully in every way.

**47.** Beneath the canopy were wonderful seats,
on which the holy mahantas came and sat.

**48.** The place where Sri Jahnava Ishvari sat
was well screened, near the assembly.

**49.** Everywhere plantain trees beyond counting,
mango boughs hung with coconuts and other fruits,

**50.** pitchers full of water shining everywhere.
Having seen all this, he went to where the Acharya was

**51.** and reported that everything there was finely arranged.
Hearing it, the Acharya went to where Sri Ishvari was.

**52.** When he informed her, she went
and sat in the screened place.

**53.** When Sri Acharya informed all the mahantas,
all went and sat on the seats in the courtyard.

**54.** A wondrous beauty appeared, surpassing a host of moons;
they showered one another with the nectar of words.

**55.** All gave Acharya Thakura permission
to perform the abhisheka and other rites of the holy Deities.

**56.** Sri Acharya, receiving the permission of the Ishvari and others,
bowed most humbly and went.

**57.** He had the holy Deities brought into the holy temple,
and seeing their beauty the Acharya was overwhelmed.

**58.** Clasping to his heart the Moon of Navadvipa with Lakshmi and Vishnupriya,
he wept, remembering His virtues.

**59.** Who can understand the Acharya's heart?
After a while the wisest one grew calm.

**60.** According to the rules of the books composed by Sri Rupa Gosvami
he performed all the rites with the greatest care.[^1]

**61.** The names Prabhu had made known in the dream
all clearly resounded at the abhisheka:

**62.** Gauranga, Vallavikanta, Sri Vrajamohana,
Sri Krishna, Sri Radhakanta, Sri Radharamana.

**63.** So the holy Deities took their seats on the thrones —
a wondrous beauty together with their Beloveds, dear as life.

**64.** Seeing Their bodies adorned with various ornaments,
the Acharya was filled with great bliss.

**65.** Finishing the puja, he quickly performed arati
and offered food to each separately.

**66.** All those foods were most wonderful:
things to chew, suck, lick and drink, of many kinds.

**67.** The Lords ate in supreme bliss;
after a while the offering was carefully removed.

**68.** Quickly washing the place of the meal,
he offered plates filled with holy garlands and sandal.

**69.** He placed garlands with sandal on the Lords' necks
and, dividing the rest, kept some apart.

**70.** On separate plates the holy garlands and sandal
he offered before all the mahantas.

**71.** All, rejoicing together in prema,
adorned themselves with the holy garlands and sandal.

**72.** Seeing the six holy Deities together,
everyone cried out "Jaya! Jaya!"

**73.** Many instruments played, a tumult arose —
as though all that is inauspicious had left the world.

**74.** Then everyone gave Sri Thakura Mahashaya
permission to begin the sankirtana.

**75.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, with gladness of heart,
told Devidasa to make ready.

**76.** Devidasa, with the singers and musicians,
came joyfully into Gauranga's courtyard.

**77.** Vallabha, Gauranga, Gokula and other dear ones —
their beauty stole everyone's heart.

**78.** Taking them with him, Sri Thakura Mahashaya,
all radiant, stood in the courtyard.

**79.** His body, wrapped in thrills, beautifully formed,
its lustre surpassing golden ketaki.

**80.** A prominent nose, long lotus eyes —
seeing that face, Kandarpa's pride goes far away.

**81.** A pair of lovely arms surpassing an elephant's trunk;
who would not rejoice to see the beauty of his chest?

**82.** How the tilaka gleams on his lovely forehead!
His neck shines with tulasi beads.

**83.** How sweet are his lovely feet, knees and waist!
To look on them takes away the burning of the eyes.

**84.** The supreme, wondrous beauty cannot be told;
what jubilation in his heart at the start of the sankirtana!

**85.** Remembering Nitai, Advaita and Gorachand with their associates,
he overflowed with prema and could not contain himself.

**86.** Bowing to the ground before all the mahantas,
he took the karatalas in his hands and began the alapa.[^2]

**87.** In a gentle smile his teeth shone beautifully;
he was covered with sweat and tears, with great bliss at heart.

**88.** Thus in the *Stavamrita-lahari*:
*To him whose face lit up the quarters with the radiance of his teeth*
*in the gentle smile born of the bliss of sankirtana,*
*who was bathed in streams of sweat and tears —*
*to Sri Narottama, obeisances, obeisances!*

**89.** On Devidasa and others he had earlier bestowed power;
now, at his direction, they became intoxicated in song and music.

**90.** The mridangas sounded, sweet as nectar;
the alapa, the karatalas and the music blended together.

**91.** Sri Raghunandana could not contain himself:
like a maddened lion he roared, remembering Gauranga.

**92.** The Acharya brought holy garlands and sandal
and at once touched them to the khols and karatalas.

**93.** Sri Raghunandana, forgetting himself in prema,
smeared Narottama with sandal with his own hands,

**94.** put a garland on him and embraced him firmly;
in the same way he gave holy garlands and sandal to all.

**95.** All, bowing at Raghunandana's feet,
with all their hearts deemed themselves blessed.

**96.** Sri Gauranga Dasa began to beat the tala;[^3]
at first the instruments sounded very softly,

**97.** and then new and new things swelled moment by moment,
as shoots of nectar grow thick and fast.

**98.** Hearing the wondrous, wondrous music, the gods
with the gandharvas and kinnaras filled the sky,

**99.** and, wholly losing composure, they showered flowers
and, mingling with men, fulfilled their longing.

**100.** Meanwhile all the mahantas said to one another:
"This is Prabhu's wondrous creation through Narottama.

**101.** Such music full of prema we have never heard,
such a mode of singing we have never seen.

**102.** Narottama's voice is a stream of nectar:
whoever drinks it feels his thirst grow ceaselessly.

**103.** What wondrous turns he shows in his singing!
Do the gandharvas and kinnaras know their secret?

**104.** The Moon of Navadvipa, Prabhu, the son of Sri Shachi —
surely it was for this that He drew him long ago.

**105.** Subdued by Narottama's prema, Prabhu
has entrusted to Narottama the treasury of song and music."

**106.** Saying this, they embraced Narottama
and all began to dance like madmen.

**107.** What wondrous tears of bliss in everyone's eyes!
Their bodies shone with holy garlands and sandal.

**108.** Narottama, intoxicated, sang Hari's virtues,
and Gaura Raya with His associates lost His composure.

**109.** Nityananda, Advaita, Srivasa, Gadadhara,
Murari, Svarupa, Haridasa, Vakreshvara,

**110.** Jagadisha, Gauridasa and all the others
joyfully became visible to everyone's eyes.[^4]

**111.** At that moment all forgot themselves —
as if they were sporting joyfully in Navadvipa.

**112.** Sri Achyutananda and others danced,
and with them danced the son of Shachi.

**113.** Prabhu Nityananda, with great gladness of heart,
danced beside his dear Narottama.

**114.** Prabhu Sri Advaita danced in great intoxication,
taking with him Ramachandra, Shyamananda and all the others.

**115.** Pandita Gadadhara danced in the joy of prema,
bringing Srinivasa Acharya to Prabhu.

**116.** In the same great festivity danced Pandita Srivasa,
Sri Murari Gupta, Sri Svarupa, Haridasa,

**117.** Shriman Pandita, the brahmachari Shuklambara,
Vasudeva Datta, Sri Pandita Vakreshvara,

**118.** Gadadhara Dasa, Sri Mukunda, Narahari,
Gauridasa Pandita, Nakula Brahmachari,

**119.** Jagadisha, Suryadasa, Acharya-nandana,
Shrinatha, Mahesha, Yadu, Sri Madhusudana,

**120.** Govinda, Madhava, Vasu, Ramananda Raya,
Sri Vijaya, Dhananjaya, Datta Sri Mukunda,

**121.** Sanatana, Rupa, Raghunatha, Kashishvara —
countless associates of Sri Prabhu danced.

**122.** The turns of their dance intoxicate the world with delight;
at the movement of their feet the earth trembles.

**123.** The manifest and the unmanifest came together in one place —
what a wondrous dance! No one remembers the body.

**124.** At the supremely intoxicating music the heart exults;
all roar, clapping their hands.

**125.** Who can hold himself up, drinking the nectar of the song?
Moment by moment waves of various bhavas arise.

**126.** The Moon of Navadvipa, casting His glance in all directions,
showers the nectar of prema, rare even for the gods.

**127.** Countless people were maddened and could not restrain themselves;
everywhere they wept, crying "Sri Krishna Chaitanya!"

**128.** What Prabhu had told Narottama in the dream
He revealed to all, rejoicing.[^5]

**129.** Who can understand this unearthly pastime of Prabhu?
As He appeared, so He vanished.

**130.** When Prabhu vanished, all were stunned:
in that ecstasy all lost consciousness.

**131.** Though all these wise ones had forgotten everything,
they lamented, overwhelmed by separation:

**132.** "Alas, alas, what a marvel we have just seen!
Where have Gaura and Nityananda, jewels of virtue, gone?

**133.** Where have Advaita, Srivasa, Gadadhara gone?
Where are Sri Murari, Haridasa, Vakreshvara?

**134.** Where are Narahari, Gauridasa and the Lord's other associates?" —
so, taking their names, all wept.

**135.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari could not contain herself:
"Having shown Himself, where has He gone?" she said and wept.

**136.** Sri Achyutananda and all the dear ones
said weeping: "What dream have we seen?"

**137.** Srinivasa and Narottama, when Prabhu disappeared,
at once threw themselves to the ground.

**138.** "Alas, alas, what has happened!" they cried and wept;
hearing that weeping, wood and stone melt.

**139.** Ramachandra, Shyamananda and others all around —
who could keep composure at their weeping?

**140.** Hundreds of thousands of people wept, and the tears from their eyes
flowed in streams over the ground like the current of a river.

**141.** The pashandis who had come to mock —
all their hearts were turned; they wept in distress.

**142.** No one could leave Gauranga's courtyard;
the state they were all in cannot be described.

**143.** The brahmana Vaninatha and others, who had lain unconscious,
after a while came to their senses and grew calm.

**144.** So all grew calm by Prabhu's will
and saw Srinivasa Acharya rolling on the ground,

**145.** and Narottama, Ramachandra, Sri Gokulananda,
Shridasa, Sri Shyamananda, Gokula, Govinda,

**146.** Sri Rasikananda, Devidasa and all the others
lying unconscious on the ground.

**147.** By the efforts of all the mahantas they
came to consciousness, but could not compose themselves.

**148.** After a while, growing calm and checking their weeping,
Sri Acharya and Narottama lamented much.

**149.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, in a sweet, gentle voice,
said privately to Narottama and Srinivasa:

**150.** "Hearing this grief, the heart breaks;
check your grief, remembering Prabhu's command.

**151.** Now is just the time to begin the play with colored powder."[^6]
Hearing and remembering, they rejoiced at heart.

**152.** Bowing at the feet of Sri Jahnava Ishvari,
they went with everyone to the mahantas.

**153.** Acharya Thakura and the Mahashaya with their people
consoled Sri Achyutananda and all the others:

**154.** "Nityananda, Advaita and Gaura with Their associates
are in every way subdued by your prema.

**155.** Birth after birth you are servants of Prabhu;
He is always visible to your eyes."

**156.** They said: "May the bliss we found in your kirtana
remain in our hearts birth after birth."

**157.** Saying this, they embraced everyone,
bathed in tears, unable to contain themselves.

**158.** Srinivasa, Narottama and all the others
bowed in prema at the feet of all.

**159.** The bliss they all shared at that moment —
is it possible to tell it with one mouth?

**160.** Srinivasa Acharya and Thakura Mahashaya
respectfully addressed all the mahantas:

**161.** "Offering colored powder to Prabhu's holy body,
begin the play with powder together with everyone."

**162.** As soon as they heard it, everyone's heart rejoiced;
at that moment Sri Santosha came bringing powder —

**163.** of various kinds, fragrant and lovely,
in separate dishes, delightfully beautiful.

**164.** So much powder was brought that it could not be counted;
everything around became powder, a marvel to see.

**165.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya with Ramachandra
arranged powder beautifully before Sri Ishvari.

**166.** Taking the powder, the Ishvari went into the holy temple,
put powder on Prabhu's body and gazed her fill.

**167.** Losing her composure, she came back into seclusion
and could not check the tears from both her eyes.

**168.** Meanwhile Sri Achyuta, Raghunandana, Shrinidhi,
Vaninatha, Hridaya Chaitanya, Yadu and others —

**169.** all the mahantas, joyfully taking powder,
put it on Gauranga's body and laughed in prema.

**170.** Some, putting it on Radhakanta and Sri Vallavikanta,
spoke with great joy of the pastimes of Vraja.

**171.** Some playfully put powder on Krishna with Radha,
some put powder on Vrajamohana's body.

**172.** Someone, putting powder on Radharamana's body,
lost composure, gazing at that lovely beauty.

**173.** Having thus offered powder to the Lords,
they began, overwhelmed, to throw powder at one another.

**174.** Some recited verses of the Holi-yatra at will,
some sang of the pastimes of Navadvipa and Vrindavana,

**175.** some went about beating drums, some danced,
some, powder in hand, ran after someone.

**176.** Hundreds of thousands of people all around played with powder;
the powder flew upward and covered the sky.

**177.** Gods and men came together in one gathering;
there is nothing in the world to compare with such a powder-play.

**178.** Srinivasa Acharya and others, with gladness of heart,
adorned with powder, went about everywhere.

**179.** The wondrous powder-play went on for a long time —
who has the power to describe it?

**180.** When all the mahantas grew calm, evening had come;
seeing Prabhu's arati they soothed their eyes.

**181.** For a while, intoxicated with the holy name,
all sat again in Prabhu's courtyard.

**182.** The rites of Prabhu's birth-day — abhisheka and so on —
the Acharya performed with all care.[^7]

**183.** All the mahantas, with great bliss at heart,
sang songs of Gauranga's birth in soft voices.

**184.** Cymbals and mridangas sounded, sweet as nectar;
some danced a dance that enchants the world.

**185.** The song, dance and music have no comparison;
the bliss there was — who could describe it?

**186.** So in prema all passed the night,
and when the night turned to morning they performed their morning duties.

**187.** Meanwhile Sri Jahnava Devi, at the very dawn,
having finished her morning duties, bathed in warm water.

**188.** Having performed her daily rites, with gladness of heart
she went to the kitchen, taking Srinivasa with her.

**189.** Seeing all the provisions for cooking ready,
she said to the Acharya with a slight smile:

**190.** "I brought you here
to tell you to fetch various things —

**191.** and you have made everything ready here so quickly!
How did you know that I would cook like this?"

**192.** Saying this, the Ishvari sat on a low stool
and cooked with every care.

**193.** Seeing the fine skill of the servants,
she praised them all with great joy.

**194.** The Ishvari's cooking is an unearthly play:
one cannot follow how she accomplishes it.

**195.** Quickly cooking rice and various dishes,
she carefully arranged the rice on wonderful platters.

**196.** Filling many vessels with various dishes,
the Ishvari hastened to offer the food.

**197.** Admiring the beauty of each separate offering,
she offered the food to the Lords with great joy —

**198.** to Gauranga, Vallavikanta, Sri Radhamohana,
Radhakanta, Radha-Krishna, Sri Vrajamohana.[^8]

**199.** All ate joyfully with various playful ways,
praising all the wonderful, most delicious dishes.

**200.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, seeing that play,
was overwhelmed with prema and could not be calm.

**201.** As is the custom among people, quickly drawing the curtain,
the Ishvari came out of the temple.

**202.** When the play of the meal here was over,
she went, as is the custom, to remove the offering,

**203.** gave water for rinsing and offered betel;
the play there was cannot be described.

**204.** Meanwhile all the mahantas finished their bathing and rites,
and the Acharya came bringing prasada:

**205.** sweets, fried delicacies and so on, sweet as nectar —
all ate them in supreme bliss.

**206.** Acharya Thakura announced everywhere
that the time for the raja-bhoga arati had come.

**207.** Hearing it, all went to Prabhu's courtyard,
and at the sight of the arati there was supreme bliss.

**208.** The pujari, performing arati with joy at heart,
gave everyone prasada garlands and tulasi.

**209.** Garlands of wonderful flowers on everyone's neck —
seeing this, all the people soothed their eyes.

**210.** Meanwhile, preparing lovely beds in various places,
the pujari laid the Lords to rest,

**211.** carefully covering Them with wonderful cloths,
pressing Their feet and waving a lovely chamara.

**212.** Having served thus, he quickly came outside,
closed the doors and bowed to the ground,

**213.** offered many prayers and went away;
how fine the service was I cannot describe.

**214.** Meanwhile Srinivasa Acharya said to everyone:
"We shall eat here, in Prabhu's courtyard."

**215.** Srinivasa swept the dust from the courtyard,
and all the mahantas sat in a circle.

**216.** All asked for plantain leaves to be brought,
and wonderful leaves were brought as everyone wished.

**217.** Some carefully laid out the leaves,
some filled vessels with scented water,

**218.** some brought vessels of ghee, curd and milk,
some put salt on pieces of leaf.

**219.** What Sri Jahnava Ishvari felt on seeing that circle —
who could say?

**220.** Quickly rice and dishes were served row on row;
the fragrance of rice and dishes stole the heart.

**221.** Of greens and fried dishes there was no counting,
soups, sour dishes and sweet rice of many kinds.

**222.** All ate with gladness of heart;
seeing that beauty, life and eyes were soothed.

**223.** Having eaten joyfully and rinsed their mouths,
they said to one another, "We have eaten a great deal."

**224.** Sri Achyutananda and others said softly:
"How we ate so much we cannot understand."

**225.** Shripati, Shrinidhi, Vaninatha and others said:
"Surely we ate so much by the Ishvari's power."

**226.** Sri Raghunandana and others said again and again:
"We shall never again eat with such happiness."

**227.** As soon as they said this, all were bathed in tears,
and with great effort all regained composure.

**228.** Acharya Thakura and Sri Thakura Mahashaya,
going to the Ishvari, said respectfully:

**229.** "You have toiled greatly; now, sitting in seclusion,
please partake of prasada — this is our wish."

**230.** The Ishvari said: "I have a great longing:
to feed all of you, and to eat afterwards myself.

**231.** Take everyone and sit quickly in the courtyard;
if you say anything against it, you are bound by an oath to me."

**232.** Hearing this, the Acharya quickly took everyone,
and they sat in a circle in Prabhu's courtyard.

**233.** Seeing the leaves and the rest laid out as before,
the Ishvari, with joy at heart, served in order.

**234.** With the greatest affection she fed everyone,
and all ate happily, remembering Prabhu's deeds.

**235.** Tasting the supreme flavour, with gladness of heart,
they said to one another in sweet words:

**236.** "This cooking of holy hands is rare even for the gods:
never since we were born have we eaten such greens!"

**237.** So, praising the various dishes, they ate
and with great joy deemed themselves blessed.

**238.** Meanwhile Raghunandana and others, overwhelmed by affection,
having watched the beauty of the meal, went to their lodgings.

**239.** Having finished the meal, there rose Srinivasa,
Narottama, Ramachandra, Govinda, Shridasa,

**240.** Ramakrishna, Kumuda, Gokulananda, Vyasa,
Shyamananda, Sri Krishnavallabha, Devidasa,

**241.** Bhagavan, Nrisimha, Gokula, Karnapura,
Kishora, Rasikananda, Gauranga Thakura,

**242.** Sri Gopiramana and others; having rinsed their mouths,
all chewed prasada betel.

**243.** Sri Acharya went quickly to Sri Ishvari
and carefully prepared a place for her meal in seclusion.

**244.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, with supreme joy at heart,
taking all the dishes, sat down to eat.

**245.** Sri Acharya Thakura with Sri Shyamananda
fed many people, serving them with care.

**246.** The pujari Sri Balarama and some others
ate last of all.

**247.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, having finished her meal,
came into seclusion and bathed in warm water.

**248.** The Ishvari's attendant, a brahmana's wife,
carefully dried her body with a fine cloth.

**249.** Burned incessantly by the fire of separation from Prabhu,
her body, like a golden lotus, had become very thin.

**250.** Drying her body with the greatest care,
she had another bring her clothing.

**251.** Putting on a dry, clean cloth, the Ishvari sat on her seat,
ate a piece of haritaki and rinsed her mouth.

**252.** To Narottama she said affectionately:
"After so many days, today I have eaten my fill."

**253.** Narottama, remembering Nityananda and Chaitanya,
shed streams from both eyes and kept silent.

**254.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, in prema,
calmed Narottama with sweet words.

**255.** Srinivasa Acharya together with Sri Shyamananda
came joyfully to Sri Ishvari.

**256.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, with supreme joy at heart,
said to the Acharya in sweet words:

**257.** "It is not good to delay going to Vrindavana;
I think to set out tomorrow morning."

**258.** The Acharya said: "I can say nothing:
my heart breaks to hear these words.

**259.** What you have willed cannot be otherwise:
you will surely go to Vrindavana.

**260.** Everything needed for the journey has been arranged here."
Hearing this, she smiled slightly.

**261.** The Acharya added humbly:
"It would be good for you to lie down a little."

**262.** Hearing this, she lay down on that same seat,
and the three quickly went elsewhere.

**263.** After resting a while, the three
went to Sri Achyutananda's house.

**264.** All the mahantas were sitting there,
absorbed in talks about Krishna.

**265.** At the coming of these three their happiness increased;
I cannot describe all those talks here.

**266.** After a while all said to the Acharya:
"Asking leave to go — we do not know what our hearts are doing.

**267.** You know everything — what more can we say?
All wish to leave tomorrow morning."

**268.** The Acharya said: "What you have willed —
who has the power to make it otherwise?

**269.** We think that tomorrow, very early,
it would be good to have food cooked in each lodging,

**270.** to finish bathing and so on quickly
and eat with joy — the fortunate will see it."

**271.** Hearing the Acharya's words, all were amused
and said, laughing, "As you wish."

**272.** Saying this and staying a little longer,
all went to their own lodgings.

**273.** Acharya Thakura and Sri Thakura Mahashaya
with Shyamananda came to Prabhu's temple.

**274.** Sri Santosha Raya and others came there,
and the Acharya told them everything.

**275.** Whoever rejoices to hear all these accounts
will surely have his desire fulfilled.

**276.** Listen to all this continually and with care:
Narahari tells the *Narottama-vilasa*.[^9]

*Thus ends the seventh vilasa of the Narottama-vilasa.*
