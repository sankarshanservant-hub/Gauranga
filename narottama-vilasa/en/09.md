# Vilasa 9. Jahnava in Vrindavana; Her Return through Kheturi; the Brahmana Healed of Leprosy

*Jahnava's journey to Vrindavana, her meeting with the Gosvamis, her tour of Vraja; Gopinatha in a dream asks her to send Him Radha; Jahnava's return through Kheturi, Budhari, Katwa, Yajigrama and Shrikhanda; Srinivasa and Narottama teach; the offending brahmana struck with leprosy is forgiven by Narottama*

**1.** Glory to Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita with their associates!
Be merciful, Prabhu, to this wretched beggar.

**2.** Glory, glory to the listeners, oceans of mercy!
Hear what I shall now relate.

**3.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, going from the village of Kheturi to Vrindavana,
performed an unearthly deed.

**4.** What shall I say? Wicked pashandis and yavanas
effortlessly obtained the rare treasure of bhakti.

**5.** Those who kept company with such people —
they too became intoxicated with Sri Krishna Chaitanya's virtues.

**6.** Along whatever road the Ishvari went with everyone,
the people of those lands ran along with her.

**7.** In whatever village she stayed for a day,
the villagers' joy was boundless.

**8.** So, destroying the impurity of so many beings,
she went by way of Prayaga quickly to Madhupuri.[^1]

**9.** Bathing with everyone at the Sri Vishrama-ghata,
she honoured the brahmanas of Sri Mathura.

**10.** Staying there that day and night, and bathing in the morning,
the Ishvari joyfully went on from there.

**11.** That the Ishvari had come to Mathura
everyone everywhere heard.

**12.** All the Gosvamis quickly, with gladness of heart,
came out from Vrindavana to meet her.

**13.** Meanwhile the Ishvari with everyone, from afar,
gazed overwhelmed at the sweetness of the forest.

**14.** Unable to check her tears, drenched with them,
she got down from the palanquin and went on foot.

**15.** Sri Parameshvara Dasa, before the Ishvari,
spoke softly in the sweetest words:

**16.** "Sri Gopala Bhatta, Sri Bhugarbha, Lokanatha,
Sri Jiva, Sri Krishna Pandita and others together —

**17.** all these have come out to meet you."
Saying this, he pointed them all out from afar.

**18.** Seeing them all, Sri Jahnava Ishvari
became such as I cannot tell.

**19.** All the Gosvamis, at the sight of the Ishvari,
lost their composure and could not check their tears.

**20.** Falling to the ground and bowing at the Ishvari's feet,
they could not say anything of all that rose in their hearts.

**21.** With Krishnadasa Sarakhela, Madhava Acharya and all the others
the meeting took place as was fitting.

**22.** Sri Parameshvara Dasa, taking Govinda and others,
brought them together and introduced everyone.

**23.** Sri Govinda Kaviraja and all the others
bowed to the ground at the Gosvamis' feet.

**24.** All, with great mercy toward them,
embraced them with gladness of heart.

**25.** How that meeting was —
for fear of the book's length it is not told in detail.

**26.** Sri Jiva Gosvami, saying many things carefully,
seating the Ishvari in a palanquin carried by men,

**27.** quickly led everyone to a secluded lodging;
people ran from all sides to see the Ishvari.

**28.** From Sri Govinda, Gopinatha, Madanamohana
their servants came from there.

**29.** Who was doing what, no one remembered;
what wondrous bliss there was in Vrindavana!

**30.** When the Ishvari with everyone had settled in the lodging,
all the food was brought there.

**31.** Quickly feeding everyone with various prasada delicacies,
the Ishvari ate a little herself.

**32.** Sri Gopala Bhatta and others, with gladness of heart,
took leave and went to their own dwellings.

**33.** For the darshan of Govinda's raja-bhoga arati
all went together with Sri Jiva Gosvami.

**34.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, entering the temple,
lost her composure on seeing Radha-Govinda.

**35.** Sri Madhava Acharya and others, at the sight of Govinda,
were overwhelmed; tears streamed from their eyes.

**36.** They saw the arati of Sri Govinda
and with great joy honoured the maha-prasada.

**37.** Coming back from there, all rested,
and Sri Jiva Gosvami went joyfully to his dwelling.

**38.** In the afternoon Sri Jahnava Ishvari
quickly bathed with everyone.

**39.** Going to the temples of Madanamohana and Gopinatha,
she had darshan, overwhelmed with prema.

**40.** Sri Radha-vinoda, Sri Radharamana,
Radha-Damodara — she saw them all.

**41.** The transformations of bhava at these darshans —
how can I, a wretch, describe them with one mouth?

**42.** The various cloths and ornaments she had brought
she offered everywhere.

**43.** What shall I say of the bliss in eating the prasada
of Sri Govinda, Gopinatha and Madanamohana?

**44.** Before Lokanatha and the others they told everything:
how the great festival in Kheturi had been,

**45.** how they had come along the road — they told it all,
and hearing it, all the Gosvamis rejoiced.

**46.** All the Gosvamis, gathering their composure,
told what had happened to them.

**47.** Hearing it, the Ishvari's heart was distraught,
and Madhava Acharya and others could not contain themselves.

**48.** After a while, growing calm, all said:
"Please hear some of Govinda's poetry."

**49.** Hearing Govinda's verses, they praised them greatly,
and the title "Kaviraja" was approved by all.[^2]

**50.** Sri Ishvari, with their permission,
went to Sri Kunda by way of Bahulavana.

**51.** Those who had come from Sri Kunda
all went along with the Ishvari.

**52.** Having seen Radha-kunda and Shyama-kunda,
they saw Sri Manasa-Ganga and Govardhana.

**53.** By way of Vrishabhanupura they went to Nandishvara
and saw the lovely village of Sri Javat.

**54.** Seeing the place where Balarama performed the rasa-lila,
they came back to Vrindavana.

**55.** For Sri Govinda, Gopinatha, Madanamohana,
Sri Radha-vinoda and Sri Radharamana,

**56.** and Sri Radha-Damodara — for all of them the Ishvari carefully
cooked and offered food in turn.

**57.** What bliss arose in the hearts of all the Gosvamis
as they ate that prasada — who knows?

**58.** So Sri Jahnava stayed some days,
and Sri Jiva Gosvami read her something from his books.

**59.** Again the Ishvari, taking everyone with her,
went in turn around the twelve forests.

**60.** What bliss there was on which day in which place —
for fear of the book's length it is not described.

**61.** She made ready to travel to Gauda,
and all the Gosvamis gave their consent.

**62.** Sri Govinda, Gopinatha, Madanamohana,
Radha-Damodara and Sri Radharamana,

**63.** Sri Radha-vinoda — from all of them
she took leave, telling what was in her heart.

**64.** What the Ishvari became at the parting
could not be described even with a thousand mouths.

**65.** Madhava Acharya and others calmed themselves with effort,
and that day all stayed in Vrindavana.

**66.** The dearest disciple of Gauridasa Pandita,
named Bara Gangadasa, incomparable in virtue,

**67.** had come to Vrindavana earlier;
he never stayed in one place but always wandered.

**68.** The Ishvari herself, showing him mercy,
commanded: "You will go with me to Gauda."

**69.** Receiving such a command, he made ready.
Meanwhile Govinda and others went to the Gosvamis' dwellings.

**70.** Bowing at the feet of Sri Gopala Bhatta and Lokanatha,
they told what was in their hearts.

**71.** Sri Bhatta and Sri Lokanatha were very pleased
and blessed Srinivasa and Narottama.

**72.** Placing their feet on the heads of all,
what more they said cannot be described.

**73.** From there they went to the dwelling of Bhugarbha Gosvami,
and he showed them all great mercy.

**74.** From there they went to Jiva Gosvami;
Krishnadasa Kaviraja and others were there.

**75.** At once they saw many together,
and falling to the ground they bowed at the feet of all.

**76.** All showed them great mercy;
Sri Jiva Gosvami said affectionately to Govinda:

**77.** "Tell the Acharya the news from here
and send me the nectar of the songs you have composed.

**78.** I shall finish this book very soon
and send the book with a man, together with a letter."

**79.** Saying this, he gave them the *Gopala-virudavali*,[^3]
and Krishnadasa Kaviraja and others praised it.

**80.** So, having visited everywhere,
they came quickly back to the lodging and rested.

**81.** When the Ishvari lay down late at night,
Gopinatha appeared to her in a dream.

**82.** He gave Jahnava the garland from His own neck
and, smiling softly, said slowly:

**83.** "What you resolved on seeing My Beloved,
go to Gauda and quickly send it.

**84.** She will be on the left, and this one on the right;
the beauty that will be — you will see it."[^4]

**85.** Saying many such things, He went into the temple;
when the sleep broke, she saw what she had seen.

**86.** Keeping Sri Gopinatha's garland hidden,
she went for the darshan of the Sri mangala-arati.

**87.** Having seen the arati and offered many prayers,
she came back to the lodging, greatly rejoicing.

**88.** When the night turned to morning, at a very auspicious moment,
Sri Ishvari set out from the lodging.

**89.** All the Gosvamis came there;
what they said cannot be described.

**90.** Having gone some distance, all, at the Ishvari's command,
took leave, bathed in streams of tears.

**91.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari could not be calm:
tears flowed from her eyes like the current of a river.

**92.** Krishnadasa Pandita, Sri Madhava Acharya,
Murari Chaitanya and others lost their composure.

**93.** They wept, and the people of Vraja wept;
having gone some distance, all grew calm.

**94.** The people of Vraja went to their dwellings,
and Sri Ishvari with everyone came to Mathura.

**95.** That day she stayed in Mathura
and carefully fed the brahmanas of Mathura.

**96.** From there she set out for Gauda
and after some days came to the village of Kheturi.

**97.** Hearing from people of the Ishvari's coming,
Narottama forgot himself for joy.

**98.** Calling Ramachandra, he told him the news;
hearing of her coming, all rejoiced.

**99.** They went out to meet her with their people,
and the people of the village of Kheturi ran from all sides.

**100.** Going some distance, they saw a wondrous procession,
and the meeting took place in great bliss.

**101.** They fell rolling at the Ishvari's feet,
and the Ishvari rejoiced to see them all.

**102.** Casting a merciful glance on the people of Kheturi,
she entered the village of Kheturi with everyone.

**103.** Sri Ishvari stayed in her former lodging,
and many people were appointed to serve.

**104.** Sri Govinda Kaviraja and all the others gladly
stayed in their former lodgings.

**105.** Baru Gangadasa and the other wise ones
stayed there, finding a wonderful secluded place.

**106.** Ramachandra Kaviraja with great care
took various provisions to every place.

**107.** Before the Ishvari, Sri Thakura Mahashaya
begged her again and again to bathe.

**108.** Quickly bathing in warm water and doing her rites,
the Ishvari ate a few prasada sweets.

**109.** Quickly cooking, she offered it to Prabhu,
and when He ate, everyone was glad.

**110.** So all the mahantas bathed and so on,
and Sri Santosha dressed them all in new cloths.

**111.** All ate the prasada sweets,
and meanwhile food was quickly cooked in one place.

**112.** The cooks, having offered the food to Krishna,
joyfully fed all the mahantas.

**113.** Sri Govinda Kaviraja and all the others
ate together with the cooks.

**114.** All chewed prasada betel
and lay down for a little while in their own places.

**115.** Baru Gangadasa and others, going to their own place,
rested for a while, happily.

**116.** Sri Ishvari, having rested a while,
quickly bathed and did her rites again.

**117.** Narottama, with Ramachandra, Santosha and others,
came with joyful hearts to Sri Ishvari.

**118.** At the Ishvari's command all sat on their seats;
Narottama wished to ask something.

**119.** Knowing what was in his heart, Jahnava Ishvari
told in detail of her journey to Vrindavana and so on.

**120.** Reflecting on the doings of all the Gosvamis,
she lost her composure; streams flowed from her eyes.

**121.** After a while, growing calm, she consoled everyone
and told by hints of Sri Gopinatha's command —

**122.** that she must go quickly. When she made this known,
Ramachandra Kaviraja said with joined palms:

**123.** "We had hoped you would stay here some days —
our wish has come to nothing."

**124.** The Ishvari said: "I can say nothing;
consider and tell me what is right, and I shall do it."

**125.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya said slowly:
"In two or four days set out for Khardaha.

**126.** It is better that the image be made in your presence;
in such matters there must be no delay.

**127.** On the road you will be delayed some days;
tomorrow morning I shall send a man to Khardaha."

**128.** Saying this, in Sri Jahnava Ishvari's presence
he had a letter written and gave it into Santosha's hand.

**129.** He wrote a letter to Acharya Thakura too
and quickly sent a messenger with the two letters.

**130.** It was time for the darshan of the evening arati,
and they went with Sri Ishvari to Prabhu's courtyard.

**131.** Sri Madhava Acharya and all the others came quickly
and joyfully saw Prabhu's arati.

**132.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, entering the temple,
had darshan — what did she say to Him by a hint?

**133.** For a while she listened to the kirtana,
and Sri Ishvari went to her own lodging.

**134.** Madhava Acharya and all the others went to their lodgings,
and Ramachandra and others remained in Prabhu's courtyard.

**135.** Quickly taking Prabhu's prasada delicacies,
they fed everyone with the greatest care.

**136.** Tired from the road, all lay down,
and Sri Santosha and others massaged their feet.

**137.** Ramachandra went quickly to the Ishvari
and gave her a little prasada milk to drink.

**138.** All the brahmanas' wives who were with Sri Ishvari
he carefully fed a little.

**139.** When Sri Ishvari had lain down, the Mahashaya
came with Ramachandra to Prabhu's house.

**140.** Taking Ramachandra, Govinda and all the others,
the Mahashaya joyfully ate prasada.

**141.** Finding the moment, to Thakura Mahashaya
Sri Govinda Kaviraja carefully reported

**142.** what all the Gosvamis had bidden him say,
and having said it, gave him the *Gopala-virudavali*.

**143.** Hearing it, the Mahashaya fell silent
and lost his composure in a way I cannot tell.

**144.** After a while, consoling himself, he grew calm
and gave the *Gopala-virudavali* to Ramachandra.

**145.** Still he lay down distraught,
and in a dream Lokanatha appeared to him.

**146.** Narottama, falling at the Gosvami's feet,
drenched his lotus feet with the tears of his eyes.

**147.** The Gosvami embraced Narottama
and spoke nectarean words of consolation.

**148.** Giving Narottama great joy,
smiling softly, he vanished.

**149.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya was greatly delighted
and passed the rest of the night chanting the holy name.

**150.** All, having done their morning duties, with Narottama
became absorbed in talk of Sri Vrindavana.

**151.** So in great bliss they spent four days;
as before, the Ishvari cooked and so on.

**152.** The bliss she manifested in those four days —
who could describe it? The fortunate saw it.

**153.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya with Ramachandra
together fixed the time of departure.

**154.** Sri Govinda Kaviraja and some others
they sent with supreme joy to Budhari.

**155.** Sri Santosha said: "The departure is tomorrow morning —
quickly make preparations for the journey."

**156.** To all the pujaris he said with great care:
"Be attentive in the service of Prabhu and the Vaishnavas."

**157.** So he instructed everyone in every task
and reported it all to Sri Ishvari.

**158.** Meanwhile Sri Santosha Raya and some others
quickly made preparations for the journey.

**159.** What was fitting to send with Sri Ishvari
he entrusted to Sri Parameshvara Dasa.

**160.** When the night turned to morning, into Prabhu's courtyard
all came to take leave.

**161.** Seeing Prabhu, all, with gladness of heart,
offered many prayers in soft words.

**162.** The pujari gave everyone prasada garlands and cloths,
and all, bowing to the ground, took leave.

**163.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, losing composure at the darshan,
took leave — what did she say within herself?

**164.** Bowing, placing the garland and cloth on her head,
she went out of the courtyard with everyone.

**165.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya took leave
and bowed, reciting a verse of his own composition.

**166.** Thus:
*O Gauranga, Vallavikanta, Sri Krishna, Vrajamohana,*
*Radharamana, O Radha, Radhakanta — obeisance to You!*

**167.** Taking with him all who were to go,
Ramachandra at the parting was distraught at heart.

**168.** The people of the village of Kheturi, losing their composure,
all went with them to the bank of the Padmavati.

**169.** Sri Ishvari, consoling all the people,
wholly without composure, boarded the boat.

**170.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya said to the helmsman:
"Take the boat quickly across the Padma."

**171.** The helmsman brought the boat across the Padma,
and the people here went back distraught to the village.

**172.** On the bank of the Padmavati the Ishvari with everyone
bathed and quickly came to Budhari.

**173.** The people of the nearby villages there
came running to see her.

**174.** Seeing all the mahantas, they
could not restrain their hearts and were bathed in tears.

**175.** Seeing them so, the wise ones rejoiced
and addressed them in the sweetest words.

**176.** Sri Ishvari with everyone, with gladness of heart,
stayed in a wonderful secluded lodging.

**177.** Sri Govinda Kaviraja asked the cooks
to go and cook.

**178.** They quickly and joyfully cooked,
offered the food to Krishna and removed it.

**179.** Sri Ishvari, cooking very briefly,
offered it to Krishna with milk and so on,

**180.** removed the offering, ate happily,
bathed again and sat on her seat.

**181.** Meanwhile the cooks with great care
fed all the mahantas.

**182.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya and all the others
ate together with the cooks.

**183.** What bliss the Ishvari felt that day:
she arranged the marriage of Baru Gangadasa!

**184.** Baru Gangadasa, the crown of the renounced,
who has no desire even in dreams,

**185.** was very abashed, but
married by the Ishvari's will.[^5]

**186.** How Jahnava Ishvari arranged the marriage
I cannot describe for fear of the book's length.

**187.** A lovely Deity named Shyamaraya —
what a wondrous pose, how lovely in every limb! —

**188.** said to the Ishvari in a dream:
"Now entrust Me to Baru Gangadasa."

**189.** At the command in the dream, the Ishvari with great joy
gave the service to Baru Gangadasa.

**190.** At once she established the order of offerings,
and the next day there was a great festival.

**191.** What the Ishvari told Baru Gangadasa in private
I cannot understand.

**192.** Leaving Baru Gangadasa in the village of Budhari,
she came with everyone to Kantaka-nagara.

**193.** Sri Yadunandana and others, with joyful hearts,
came out to meet them and brought them to Prabhu's house.

**194.** As Prabhu, having eaten, was about to rest,
at that moment all entered the courtyard.

**195.** Seeing Gaurachandra with great joy at heart,
she stayed with everyone in the former lodging.

**196.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya and all the others
were given wonderful lodgings in complete seclusion.

**197.** All went to bathe in the Ganga,
and meanwhile all the provisions were made ready.

**198.** A man was sent quickly to Yajigrama,
and Sri Acharya Thakura came with everyone.

**199.** Meanwhile all, having bathed and done their rites,
ate a few prasada sweets.

**200.** At that moment the Acharya arrived,
and seeing him, all rejoiced.

**201.** Srinivasa Acharya bowed to all,
and all, bowing, embraced Srinivasa.

**202.** Affectionately they asked Srinivasa of his welfare;
Srinivasa said: "This darshan is my welfare."

**203.** All who were with Srinivasa
bowed at the feet of all the mahantas.

**204.** All the mahantas did as was fitting;
the affection they showed I cannot describe.

**205.** Meanwhile the cooks cooked,
offered the food to Krishna and removed it.

**206.** Sri Ishvari, quickly and briefly cooking,
fed Prabhu and carefully ate herself.

**207.** Bathing again, she said to everyone:
"The day is ending; sit down to eat."

**208.** Taking Srinivasa Acharya and all the others,
all the mahantas ate joyfully.

**209.** Having rinsed their mouths, all sat on their seats,
and the Acharya went to have darshan of the Ishvari.

**210.** He bowed to the ground at the Ishvari's feet,
and the Ishvari affectionately asked of his welfare.

**211.** Srinivasa said: "By the darshan of these feet
all ill fortune of so many days has gone far away."

**212.** Sri Ishvari again, in the sweetest words,
told Srinivasa everything from beginning to end.

**213.** Srinivasa listened with gladness of heart
and came to the lodging of his dear Narottama.

**214.** Sri Govinda Kaviraja told him
all that the Sri Gosvamis had bidden him say.

**215.** Hearing it, the Acharya thought within himself:
"Shall I ever see Prabhu's lotus feet again?"[^6]

**216.** After a while Ramachandra Kaviraja
gave the Acharya the *Gopala-virudavali*.

**217.** The Acharya took it and placed it on his head,
and then went quickly to see the evening arati.

**218.** All the mahantas came together into the courtyard,
and at the darshan of the arati there was supreme bliss.

**219.** For a while they sang the sankirtana of the name;
the bliss there was cannot be described.

**220.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, in Prabhu's temple,
lost her composure at the darshan of Prabhu.

**221.** Calming herself with effort, she went to her lodging;
after a while Gauranga retired to rest.

**222.** All the mahantas, taking Srinivasa Acharya,
went to the lodging, overwhelmed with bliss.

**223.** For a while they spoke of Sri Vrindavana;
it grew very late, and they lay down.

**224.** Srinivasa Acharya and others went to their lodging,
and the Acharya lay down with a distraught heart.

**225.** He slept a little, and at the end of the night
Sri Gopala Bhatta appeared to him in a dream.

**226.** Srinivasa fell rolling on the ground
and washed his lotus feet with the tears of his eyes.

**227.** Sri Bhatta Gosvami embraced him firmly
and spoke sweet words to Srinivasa:

**228.** "I am always near you;
birth after birth you are my chief servant."

**229.** Saying many such things, placing his feet on his head,
he vanished — and he came to his senses.

**230.** Meditating on the lotus feet of Sri Gopala Bhatta,
he rose and sat up, remembering Krishna Chaitanya.

**231.** Morning came; all, having done their morning duties,
joyfully bathed in the Suradhuni.

**232.** Having seen Sri Gauranga, they saw Bharati's place too;
in taking leave their hearts were distraught.

**233.** Saying many things to Sri Yadunandana and calming him,
Sri Ishvari came with everyone to Yajigrama.

**234.** Acharya Thakura sent a man to Khanda;
hearing the news, the people of Khanda rejoiced.

**235.** Sri Raghunandana came to Yajigrama
and saw Sri Jahnava Ishvari.

**236.** The gladness there was at meeting everyone
I cannot describe here at length.

**237.** He stayed a while in Yajigrama
and, hearing the accounts of Vraja, lost his composure.

**238.** Again, taking Acharya Srinivasa with him,
he said to the Ishvari in soft words:

**239.** "I have heard everything: there must be no delay;
you must go quickly to Khardaha.

**240.** Tomorrow morning please come to Khanda;
I must go there now."

**241.** Saying this and bowing, he set out for Shrikhanda,
having first asked leave of each of the mahantas.

**242.** Addressing Srinivasa Acharya and all the others,
Sri Raghunandana came joyfully to Khanda.

**243.** He had all the provisions prepared
and the lodgings cleaned at once.

**244.** He saw that everything everywhere was ready;
the people of Khanda were eager for darshan.

**245.** Meanwhile in Yajigrama the Ishvari with everyone
quickly finished the meal and so on.

**246.** The Acharya read from a book for a while,
and after that there was a wondrous sankirtana.

**247.** That day in Yajigrama there was no end to happiness;
what shall I describe? The fortunate saw it.

**248.** At dawn, having done her morning duties,
the Ishvari came with everyone to Shrikhanda.

**249.** The people of Khanda were overwhelmed with bliss,
seeing the two feet of Sri Jahnava.

**250.** The bliss there was at the darshan of all the mahantas —
what shall I describe? Whoever saw it knows.

**251.** Going quickly with everyone to Prabhu's courtyard,
their hearts rejoiced at the darshan of Prabhu.

**252.** Where Prabhu Nityananda danced,
where in prema he drank honey,[^7]

**253.** where Nitai watched Narahari's dancing,
where he became grey with dust —

**254.** seeing all those places with gladness of heart,
all stayed in a most wonderful lodging.

**255.** That day the cooking was finished quickly;
offering it quickly to Prabhu, all ate.

**256.** Knowing the Ishvari's wish, Sri Raghunandana
began a sankirtana that blesses the world.

**257.** In the sankirtana a wondrous flood of prema arose;
all swam, and no one's mind was steady.

**258.** All forgot themselves;
someone clasped another's feet and wept;

**259.** they rolled on the ground, overwhelmed at heart,
and everyone's body grew grey with dust.

**260.** As the song and music were, so was the dancing:
at it a heart like stone melts.

**261.** Someone, standing to one side, said to another:
"There is no comparison for the song, dance and music."

**262.** Someone said: "O brother, I feel I shall die,
taking on myself the misfortunes of this dance, song and music!"

**263.** Someone said: "In the ocean of song, dance and music
only he drowns to whom they give their mercy."

**264.** Saying this, drenched with streams of tears,
they went about like maddened elephants.

**265.** In the wondrous ecstasy of such a sweet kirtana
they remembered nothing — the night came to an end.

**266.** By Prabhu's will, growing calm after a while,
all came to the lodgings and rested.

**267.** They slept a little, and the night turned to morning;
all quickly finished their morning duties and so on.

**268.** The Ishvari quickly bathed and did her rites
and fed Prabhu with wonderful cooking.

**269.** At the Ishvari's command all — Madhava Acharya and others
together with Sri Raghunandana — sat down to eat.

**270.** The Ishvari herself served;
I do not know with what bliss all ate.

**271.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, having fed everyone,
ate last of all, with love.

**272.** At the Ishvari's affection Sri Raghunandana
lost his composure and could not check his tears.

**273.** The people of the village of Shrikhanda, by the Ishvari's virtues,
were overwhelmed; their happiness grew moment by moment.

**274.** Sri Ishvari, having joyfully bathed again,
sat with Srinivasa Acharya and others

**275.** and said in the sweetest words, with great affection:
"From here all of you go quickly to Kheturi.

**276.** I shall set out for Khardaha tomorrow morning;
from there I shall send news quickly."

**277.** Saying many such things, they came to Prabhu's courtyard,
and there was bliss at the darshan of the evening arati.

**278.** After listening for a while to the singing of the name,
she took leave and went to her lodging.

**279.** Sri Raghunandana and others, before the Ishvari,
said something in sweet words:

**280.** "We have heard you will leave tomorrow morning;
we cannot keep you now by insisting.

**281.** You are independent — we are afraid to ask,
but it would be good if you came from time to time.

**282.** There is no one as shameless as I:
burning in such a fire of separation, I still live."

**283.** Hearing such words of Raghunandana,
the Ishvari lost her composure; streams flowed from both her eyes.

**284.** After a while Sri Raghunandana, growing calm,
came, having said many humble words,

**285.** and gave everyone Gauranga's prasada provisions;
though not hungry, all ate.

**286.** What he gave at once to go with Sri Ishvari
he entrusted to Sri Madhava Acharya.

**287.** It grew very late, and they lay down;
when the night turned to morning, all took leave.

**288.** How distraught everyone's heart was at that time,
what tears flowed — who has the power to describe it?

**289.** By the road by which Shrimati Ishvari had come before,
by that road, visiting everyone, she went to Khardaha.

**290.** How the Ishvari went, how people came and went —
what power have I to describe all that?

**291.** Meanwhile in Khanda Sri Thakura Raghunandana,
with the Acharya and others, was overwhelmed with prema.

**292.** That day the Acharya and others stayed there,
and in the morning, taking leave, they came to Yajigrama.

**293.** Staying two or four days in Yajigrama,
he went quickly to Nadia with two companions.

**294.** How they went about Navadvipa
is told at length in the book *Bhakti-ratnakara*.

**295.** From there Sri Acharya, coming back to Yajigrama,
spent that night in sankirtana.

**296.** The next day he set out in the morning
and in four or five days came to the village of Budhari.

**297.** Leaving Sri Govinda Kaviraja and some others there,
the next day he came to Kheturi.

**298.** Hearing of his coming, people ran from all sides
and saw him with great gladness of heart.

**299.** Acharya Thakura and Sri Thakura Mahashaya
gladdened them all with sweet words.

**300.** Going quickly with everyone to Gauranga's courtyard,
they had darshan, wholly losing their composure.

**301.** At that moment a letter came from Khardaha;
reading the letter, they learned that all was well.

**302.** At once writing a letter of supreme good news,
they sent it with great joy to Khardaha.

**303.** After staying there a while, they came to the lodging
and day and night were intoxicated with talk of Krishna.

**304.** Every day was like a great festival —
I cannot describe it for fear of length.

**305.** The Acharya, Sri Mahashaya and Ramachandra, the three —
what they spoke of in private I do not know.

**306.** Sri Acharya, staying fifteen days,
went by way of Budhari to Kanchanagadiya.

**307.** There he stayed five days in supreme bliss
and with many disciples came to Yajigrama.

**308.** Continually he taught everyone the shastras of bhakti;
no one had the power to dispute with him.

**309.** Among all he roared like a maddened lion,
and hearing him, the logicians and others fled far away.

**310.** People came from various lands to study
and went home as teachers of the books of bhakti.

**311.** The great treasure of prema-bhakti, rare even for the gods,
he distributes by Sri Chaitanya's will.

**312.** Sinners and pashandis, by the Acharya's mercy,
sing Sri Krishna Chaitanya's virtues continually.

**313.** Not different in body from such a Sri Acharya,
Sri Thakura Narottama, an ocean of virtues,

**314.** with Ramachandra, dearer than life,
enjoys the festivity of prema in the village of Sri Kheturi.

**315.** The *Srimad-Bhagavata* and the Gosvamis' books
he continually has his disciples study.

**316.** Hearing his explanations of the books of bhakti, karmis and jnanis
become Vaishnavas and condemn karma and jnana.

**317.** Brahmanas and Vaishnavas from other lands, coming together,
study the Gosvamis' books and teach them everywhere.

**318.** So he distributes the jewels of the books of bhakti;
fortunate people hear of this.

**319.** One day Narottama with Ramachandra
sat speaking of Krishna.

**320.** At that moment a distinguished brahmana came
and said to the Mahashaya, weeping:

**321.** "Before my students, in my pride,
I said so much that it cannot be repeated.

**322.** From the day I regarded you as a shudra,
I was struck with leprosy.

**323.** To cure the disease I took many medicines,
performed rites for welfare to Shiva and much else.

**324.** Instead of a cure my torment grew,
and I resolved to enter the Ganga.[^8]

**325.** In a dream Bhagavati, turning away from me,
said in anger: 'A special misfortune awaits you.

**326.** In your pride you regarded Narottama as a shudra;
your study and learning have turned your mind to ashes.

**327.** Whoever regards Narottama as an ordinary man —
that sinner has no deliverance ever.

**328.** But if he, by your good fortune, is kind to you,
then alone you will be saved — know this for certain.'

**329.** Saying this, she vanished;
morning came, and I came here.

**330.** I was afraid to come before you,
but on the way a wise man said that you are merciful.

**331.** Seeing you from afar,
my eyes were soothed — as if I had found life.

**332.** Forgive my offense this once:
I take shelter at these feet of yours."

**333.** Saying this, he was bathed in the tears of both his eyes,
and, distraught, the brahmana fell to the ground.

**334.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya said again and again:
"You have committed no offense against me."

**335.** The brahmana said: "Place your feet on my head;
only then will this sinner's heart be at peace."

**336.** Narottama, remembering Sri Krishna Chaitanya,
embraced the brahmana in prema.

**337.** The brahmana, embracing him and taking the dust of his feet,
began to dance with both arms raised.

**338.** After a while the brahmana grew calm:
the disease had gone, his body had become clean.

**339.** Prema-bhakti arose in the brahmana's heart,
and when the disease was cured, he thought:

**340.** "It would have been better had the disease stayed in my body:
I fear lest pride should arise again."

**341.** So the brahmana thought, and by the power of bhakti
he became a Vaishnava together with his whole family.

**342.** All this became known everywhere,
and the brahmanas' fear grew immensely.

**343.** Someone said to another: "Be careful:
do not regard Sri Narottama as a shudra."

**344.** Someone said: "Brahmanas, intoxicated with pride,
cannot recognize such a jewel as Narottama."

**345.** Someone said: "Narottama is an abode of mercy:
by his own goodness he mercifully destroys the fear of worldly existence."

**346.** Someone said: "I saw with my own eyes:
by singing Sri Narottama's virtues the low became exalted."

**347.** Someone said: "Narottama is an ocean of virtues:
so many virtues are never possible in a human being."

**348.** Someone said: "He only has a human form:
he is a descent of a part of Ishvara to deliver living beings."

**349.** Saying many such things, the old and worthy brahmanas
each warned their own families.

**350.** They sing Sri Narottama's virtues continually;
what shall I say of all his deeds?

**351.** How the Mahashaya from time to time went to Yajigrama
and spent happy times with the Acharya,

**352.** what his meeting with Vira Hambira was like —
is described in the book *Bhakti-ratnakara*.

**353.** Listen to all this continually and with care:
Narahari tells the *Narottama-vilasa*.

*Thus ends the ninth vilasa of the Narottama-vilasa.*
