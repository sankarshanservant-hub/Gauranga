# Vilasa 6. Gauranga in Kheturi and the Gathering of the Vaishnavas for the Festival

*Narottama in Kheturi: in a dream the Lord reveals His Deity in a granary; the beginning of the kirtana; news of Srinivasa (his marriage, Ramachandra, his second journey to Vrindavana); the meeting at Budhari; the invitations and the coming of the Vaishnavas, led by Jahnava*

**1.** Glory to Gaura, Nityananda and Advaita with their associates!
Be merciful, Prabhu, to this wretched beggar.

**2.** Glory, glory to the listeners, oceans of mercy!
Hear what I shall now relate.

**3.** Having crossed the river Padmavati, the Mahashaya
entered the village of Sri Kheturi at an auspicious moment.

**4.** People came from all sides and gazed their fill;
Kheturi was immersed in an ocean of bliss.

**5.** Sri Santosha and others carefully took
Sri Thakura Mahashaya to a very secluded dwelling.

**6.** Still there was no end to the coming and going of people;
the crowd stayed day and night, into the late watches.

**7.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, at night, in seclusion,
thought: "How shall I manifest the service?"

**8.** At the end of the night sleep drew him in,
and in a dream the son of Shachi said:

**9.** "O Narottama, watching the road for you,
I have long been present in a metal Deity.

**10.** In your kingdom there is a prominent householder;
everyone knows him as a very wealthy man.

**11.** In his house are many granaries for grain,
but no one can go there for fear of a huge snake.

**12.** In the biggest of them am I:
open the door and bring Me quickly."

**13.** Having spoken also of the making of other Deities,
He embraced Narottama and vanished.

**14.** In separation from the dream Sri Thakura Mahashaya
awoke in agitation and saw that six dandas of night remained.

**15.** Passing the rest of the night in chanting the holy name,
he quickly cleaned his teeth and bathed.

**16.** With great joy he asked everyone:
"Where is there a householder with a large family,

**17.** who has many granaries for grain,
where no one can go for fear of a snake?"

**18.** All said, "We know him."
The Thakura said, "Then let us go.

**19.** I have a very secret purpose there."
Saying this, the Mahashaya set out.

**20.** He went quickly to that householder's house,
and he with his whole family deemed himself blessed.

**21.** As Sri Thakura Mahashaya went toward the granary,
the householder anxiously fell at his feet,

**22.** and with joined palms said, weeping:
"Everyone knows there is danger there from a huge snake.

**23.** Many snake-charmers came to drive the snake away,
but at the snake's hissing none could stand firm.

**24.** We abandoned it long ago;
there are many valuable goods in it, and we grieve.

**25.** Come what may, we will not let you go there;
whatever your business, we will do it here."

**26.** Smiling, Sri Thakura Mahashaya said:
"Do not worry: the fear of the snake will go far away.

**27.** In your granary is something greatly needed;
you will see it with your own eyes, and your eyes will be fulfilled."

**28.** Saying this, the Thakura Mahashaya went on,
while all the people trembled with fear.

**29.** At the sight of Sri Thakura Mahashaya's approach,
the huge snakes vanished.

**30.** As Narottama, in prema, opened the door,
he saw the Moon of Navadvipa with His Beloved.[^1]

**31.** His body shone, adorned with ornaments —
nowhere could anything be found to compare.

**32.** As he spread his arms to take Him up,
He flashed like lightning and entered his embrace.

**33.** Seeing this, all the people were astonished,
crying "Jaya! Jaya!" with tears in their eyes.

**34.** They said to one another, "We have seen a marvel:
such a deed is never possible for a man."

**35.** Some said: "Others cannot recognize him;
by his mercy this land will be blessed."

**36.** Some said: "If we are fortunate,
we shall surely take shelter of these feet."

**37.** Crying "Glory, glory to Prabhu Narottama!"
all danced with arms upraised.

**38.** As the Mahashaya carried Prabhu to his dwelling,
people ran from all sides; there was a great crowd on the road.

**39.** Reaching his dwelling, he carefully seated
Gaurachandra with His Beloved on a wondrous seat.

**40.** Gazing on that beauty with unblinking eyes,
he was overwhelmed and could not check his tears.

**41.** Suddenly there arose in his heart
all that the science of music speaks of — dance, song and instruments.

**42.** At once the Mahashaya, clapping his hands,
sang of Gaurachandra's virtues with his people.

**43.** What wondrous singing the Mahashaya created!
At the sight of that dancing the pride of the gandharvas was destroyed.

**44.** Thus in the *Stavamrita-lahari*:
*To him who with his own dancing, destroying the pride of the gandharvas,*
*astonished all the people of the age of Kali,*
*who is renowned for the songs he himself created —*
*to Sri Narottama, obeisances, obeisances!*[^2]

**45.** On whomever he casts a merciful glance but once,
he becomes a singer and in his singing showers prema.

**46.** Even the lowest yavana, barbarian or evil-doer
sings, maddened, of Gauranga's pastimes.

**47.** The sound of the kirtana rose and filled the world;
the gods from heaven showered flowers.

**48.** Who could keep composure hearing that lofty singing?
What to speak of others — wood and stone split apart.

**49.** The gandharvas and kinnaras said: "What a marvel!
Who has suddenly made such singing known?

**50.** Such a mode of song is rare even in the world of the gods —
have dance, song and music themselves taken form?"

**51.** Some said: "What is impossible for a devotee of Chaitanya?
Chaitanya's devotees are worshipped by all the gods."

**52.** Saying this, they came in human guise
and fell at Narottama's feet, rolling on the ground.

**53.** What happened — who could describe it?
After a while all were calmed with effort.

**54.** That same day Balarama and many others
received the holy mantra from the Thakura.

**55.** From that day the kirtana had its auspicious beginning;
the other festivities I cannot describe.

**56.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya, with joy in his heart,
beheld Gaurachandra with Lakshmi and Vishnupriya.[^3]

**57.** Having appointed the brahmana Balarama and other disciples
to the service of the Deity of Gaura,

**58.** and manifesting five more services by command in a dream,[^4]
he was anxious, having no news of the Acharya.

**59.** The Mahashaya pondered within himself:
"Without his permission how can I send a man?

**60.** What shall I do now? Many days have passed,
and no one has come here from Yajigrama."

**61.** As he pondered thus, growing anxious,
at that moment a man came from Yajigrama.

**62.** Seeing him, Sri Thakura Mahashaya rejoiced,
seated him and asked after his welfare.

**63.** He said: "All is well; I shall tell it in order.
In Yajigrama everyone is always anxious about you.

**64.** He[^5] mostly stays in Shrikhanda and Kantaka-nagara,
and at times goes about the region of Navadvipa.

**65.** One day Acharya Thakura went to Khanda,
and Sri Sarakara Thakura admonished him at length.

**66.** Again taking his hand, he commanded again and again:
'My child, you must marry.'

**67.** A command given again and again cannot be transgressed —
he married; hearing this, everyone rejoiced.

**68.** Having married, he lives in Sri Yajigrama,
and many learned men come there to become disciples.

**69.** A resident of Khanda, the son of Chiranjiva Sena,
named Ramachandra, expert in all the shastras —

**70.** that he has made him his disciple" — on hearing this,
Narottama felt prema spontaneously arise in his heart.

**71.** He went on: "Thus he has made many disciples
and spread the Gosvamis' books everywhere.

**72.** He sent news to Vrindavana,
and a man came from there with a letter.

**73.** The books that Sri Jiva Gosvami sent again
he quickly spread everywhere.

**74.** A letter came from Navadvipa with news:
many devotees in Nadia have passed from sight.

**75.** In Shantipura and other places the Lord's associates
have almost all passed away, burned by the fire of separation.

**76.** At the passing of Sri Sarakara Thakura and Sri Dasa Gadadhara
the Acharya's heart was burned.

**77.** The state the Acharya fell into cannot be told;
the life in the Acharya's body hung in doubt.

**78.** Hearing his weeping, beasts and birds wept;
the Acharya could not restrain himself even for a moment.

**79.** Without saying anything to anyone he left in the morning
and in a very short time entered Vrindavana.

**80.** Seeing the Acharya, all the Gosvamis rejoiced,
sat with him in seclusion and asked after his welfare.

**81.** How he took the books and how he spread them —
the Acharya told it all from beginning to end.

**82.** When he told of the passing of the Lord's associates,
all, distraught, wept.

**83.** Growing calm and understanding the Acharya's heart,
all spoke many words of consolation to the Acharya.

**84.** As four or five days passed thus,
Ramachandra Sena came and joined them there.

**85.** All learned about Ramachandra —
whose grandson and whose son he is.[^6]

**86.** He showed himself in Vraja as a great logician and poet,
and all the Gosvamis gave him the title 'Kaviraja.'

**87.** How the Acharya had married
all heard from the Kaviraja.

**88.** Sri Jiva Gosvami and the others, with great care,
sent him off, entrusting him with some books,

**89.** and gave him four men of Vraja as companions.
The Acharya set out, weeping much.

**90.** Sri Gopala Bhatta, Lokanatha and the others
were distraught, gazing at the road the Acharya had taken.

**91.** The Thakura came back to Gauda very quickly
and, going to Vishnupura, set the king at ease.

**92.** Hearing that he would come to Yajigrama,
I came alone with all the news."

**93.** Even as he said this, another man came
and gave him a letter in Sri Acharya's own hand.

**94.** Reading the letter, the Thakura Mahashaya
lost his composure, but still told the letter's meaning:

**95.** "Sri Acharya, leaving his house with his people,
came to Kanchanagadiya and made two disciples:

**96.** the brahmana Haridasa, foremost among the Lord's associates —
Shridasa and Gokulananda, his two sons —

**97.** the two brothers became disciples at their father's bidding;
they are great panditas, intoxicated with the rasa of sankirtana.

**98.** From there both have come with joy at heart:
Acharya Thakura came to Budhari yesterday."

**99.** "Today is a blessed morning for me!" — saying this,
he went with the two to the temple of Sri Gaura

**100.** and briefly told everything
to the pujari Balarama and all who were there.

**101.** The pujari Balarama, with supreme joy at heart,
fed the two with Sri maha-prasada.

**102.** Meanwhile the Mahashaya went to see
the storerooms prepared for the great festival.

**103.** Seeing all ready, with gladness in his heart
he set each one to his task.

**104.** Taking with him Devidasa, Gokula and Gauranga,[^7]
he set out at dawn for the village of Budhari.

**105.** As he entered the village, people, seeing him, rejoiced
and went quickly to tell Sri Acharya Thakura.

**106.** Acharya Thakura, with great joy in his heart,
saw at the gate of the house that the Mahashaya had come.

**107.** As the Mahashaya fell to the ground in obeisance,
the Acharya embraced him and could not be calm.

**108.** The ocean of prema surged up immeasurably;
all who saw it were amazed.

**109.** The Acharya himself introduced Sri Thakura Mahashaya
to Ramachandra and everyone else.

**110.** What a meeting it was, full of the bliss of prema —
I have told something of it at length in the *Bhakti-ratnakara*.

**111.** Acharya Thakura told Sri Thakura Mahashaya
all that had happened, in a secluded room:

**112.** how he made Ramachandra and others his disciples,
how, after marrying, he went to Vraja,

**113.** how Ramachandra and others went to Vrindavana,
how he received the title "Kaviraja,"

**114.** how he came back to Gauda, to Vishnupura,
how he came from Yajigrama to Budhari,

**115.** how the title "Kaviraja" was given to Govinda —
all this he told with joy in his heart.

**116.** He asked Sri Thakura Mahashaya how things stood,
and the Mahashaya told everything in order:

**117.** how Sri Santosha Raya and others became disciples,
how Bhakti-devi showed mercy to all,

**118.** what festivity there was at the finding of Sri Gaura's Deity,
how the five other Deities were made,

**119.** how the great festival was prepared,
how the holy temple and the throne were built.

**120.** Having said this, he added: "As soon as I received the letter,
I set my mind on the festival at Phalguna-purnima."

**121.** The Acharya said, "That day is settled,"
and, saying this, had letters of invitation written.

**122.** Wherever devotees lived in Sri Gauda-mandala,
there letters of invitation were sent.

**123.** He quickly sent a man to Utkala
and wrote all this to Shyamananda.

**124.** Having joyfully sent letters everywhere,
he told the Mahashaya something in private — I do not know what.

**125.** Overwhelmed by the relish of talks about Krishna,
he entrusted Narottama to Ramachandra.

**126.** These two have one body, life and mind;
only to the eye are they two separate embodiments of prema.

**127.** The ways of Sri Acharya, Narottama and Ramachandra
became known within a day or two.

**128.** Some said: "These three are not human at all;
the three have appeared to deliver living beings."

**129.** Some said: "O brother, looking at the three,
it seems to me that they are one substance in three."

**130.** Some said: "What arises in my heart
I cannot reveal or tell to anyone."

**131.** People said many such things to one another;
for fear of the book's length I cannot expand on them.

**132.** The Acharya, keeping the Mahashaya four days,
sent him off to go ahead to Kheturi.

**133.** He gave him as companions Ramachandra and other dear ones,
and arriving in Kheturi, all saw Gauranga.

**134.** Shridasa and Gokulananda, treasuries of virtues,
Vyasa Acharya and others — all greatly learned —

**135.** all rejoiced at heart
to see the great abundance for Prabhu's service.

**136.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya together with Ramachandra
gave everyone lodgings, choosing secluded ones.

**137.** Narottama, Ramachandra and all the others
watched the road for the Acharya.

**138.** Meanwhile Sri Acharya, having made some more disciples,
came quickly to Kheturi with Govinda and others.

**139.** What wondrous splendour there was as he entered the village!
All the Vaishnavas came out to receive him.

**140.** The bliss of prema surged in everyone's heart;
they brought the Acharya to a wonderful lodging.

**141.** From the lodging Acharya Thakura with his companions
went quickly to have darshan of Sri Gauranga.

**142.** Seeing Gaura Raya with Lakshmi and Vishnupriya,
he was overwhelmed and bathed in tears.

**143.** Seeing the five other Deities as well,
he entered such prema as cannot be described.

**144.** After a while, growing calm, with his dear ones
he looked over all the provisions prepared in the house.

**145.** Coming back to the lodging with his people, he kept saying:
"Why is Shyamananda so late in coming?"

**146.** At that moment someone suddenly came and said:
"Shyamananda has come from Utkala."

**147.** Hearing this, the Acharya rejoiced at heart,
and the Mahashaya went forward with his people.

**148.** At that moment Shyamananda with his people
came and quickly entered the Acharya's house.

**149.** Shyamananda, seeing the Acharya,
could not hold himself up; tears poured from his eyes.

**150.** Acharya Thakura, out of affection, could not be calm
and embraced Shyamananda as he was bowing.

**151.** He drenched Shyamananda with the tears of his eyes,
and all, seeing it, lost their composure in prema.

**152.** The Acharya, gazing at Shyamananda's face,
asked after his welfare and after a while grew calm.

**153.** What Narottama and Shyamananda were like in prema
cannot be told.

**154.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya introduced
all the Vaishnavas to Sri Shyamananda:

**155.** Shridasa, Gokulananda, Vyasa Chakravarti,
Ramachandra, Govinda and others bearing the title "Kaviraja."

**156.** The joy of meeting Chattaraja, Ramakrishna, Kumuda and others[^8] —
who could describe it?

**157.** Sri Shyamananda's disciples, Rasikananda and others,
Narottama, the treasury of virtues, brought together with all.

**158.** The ways of love and devotion in their meeting —
whoever saw it deems himself virtuous.

**159.** Narottama Mahashaya with Ramachandra
took Shyamananda to a wonderful lodging.

**160.** Giving him lodging there, with great gladness of heart
he said affectionately to Rasikananda:

**161.** "My child, you will see to everything,
so that no one is in any way slighted."

**162.** Hearing this, Rasikananda, with joined palms,
deeming himself blessed, stood silent.

**163.** Seeing Rasikananda's conduct, the Mahashaya
was more pleased than can be told.

**164.** Sri Thakura Mahashaya together with Ramachandra
went to where Sri Acharya Thakura was.

**165.** He sent Sri Govinda Kaviraja,
and he came gladly to Shyamananda.

**166.** The mahanta Shyamananda, with supreme joy at heart,
went quickly to have darshan of Sri Gauranga.

**167.** He saw the sweet form, streams flowed from his eyes,
and again and again he bowed, falling to the ground.

**168.** All over his body a most lovely thrill;
his body was helpless in the ecstasy of prema.

**169.** After a while, growing calm, he said to Sri Govinda:
"Let me have darshan of the five other Deities too."

**170.** When Sri Govinda Kaviraja showed them to him,
what Shyamananda became I cannot describe.

**171.** Where the provisions for the festival were kept,
he showed him that too, and seeing it he was greatly pleased.

**172.** Meanwhile Sri Rasikananda, Sri Purushottama,
Sri Kishora and others, excellent in every way,

**173.** had all the provisions they had brought from their land
placed in Gauranga's storeroom.

**174.** The many people with them, by their care,
were given what was fitting and assigned lodgings.

**175.** In this way Shyamananda's disciples, the life of the Vaishnavas,
arranged everything everywhere.

**176.** Meanwhile Shyamananda went to where the Acharya was
and became absorbed in talks of Gaura and Krishna.

**177.** Having passed that day in supreme bliss,
in the morning all performed their morning duties.

**178.** Having bathed and so on, all thought within themselves:
"Why is Sri Jahnava Devi so late?"

**179.** At that moment a brahmana carefully reported:
"Jahnava Ishvari has crossed the Padmavati."

**180.** As soon as they heard it, all were filled with the bliss of prema
and went forward along the road to the bank of the Padmavati.

**181.** People everywhere ran about, jostling;
all said, "Jahnava, full of prema, has come!"

**182.** One of Sri Jahnava Ishvari's companions
came to announce the Ishvari's coming.

**183.** Seeing the Acharya approach, with great joy at heart
he bowed to the ground and reported the Ishvari's coming.

**184.** Acharya Mahashaya, bowing to him,
asked for particulars, and he related them in detail:

**185.** "Receiving the news from here by letter,
all became eager to come here.

**186.** There were the most generous Krishnadasa,
whose elder brother is Suryadasa Sarakhela,

**187.** Shrila Raghupati Upadhyaya, Mahidhara,
Murari Chaitanya, the charming Jnanadasa,

**188.** Kamalakara Piplai, Sri Jiva Pandita,
Madhava Acharya, whose deeds are well known,

**189.** Nrisimha Chaitanya Dasa, Kanai, Shankara,
Sri Gauranga Dasa, the wise Vrindavana,

**190.** Sri Minaketana Ramadasa Mahashaya,
Nakadi, Sri Balarama and others full of prema.

**191.** All submitted at the feet of the two Ishvaris[^9]
that they wished to go to Kheturi.

**192.** Hearing this, Jahnava Ishvari said with delight:
'Why delay? Let us go there quickly.'

**193.** At the Ishvari's command Sri Parameshvara Dasa
joyfully made everything ready for the journey.

**194.** On the day the Ishvari set out from Khardaha,
Vaishnavas came from afar to see her.

**195.** The Ishvari told them about the journey here,
and hearing it, all became eager.

**196.** All went quickly to their homes and came back;
that is why there was delay, and they set out again.

**197.** At the time of departure a voice spoke from the sky —
it was most wonderful; hear it, Mahashaya.

**198.** In a deep and solemn voice it said again and again:
'Srinivasa and Narottama are dear to Me;

**199.** with their people they are skilled in giving bhakti;
I am ever subdued by the prema of the two.

**200.** In the village of Kheturi, in sankirtana with My associates,
I shall dance, and everyone will see it.

**201.** By the power of My prema all people will be maddened;
no one will be left with any sorrow or grief.

**202.** By your going there everything will be accomplished;
all are watching the road for you.

**203.** From Kheturi you will go to Vrindavana,
and coming back from there you will distribute the treasure of bhakti.'

**204.** Hearing this, the Ishvari was astonished at heart;
she could not be calm, tears flowed from her eyes.

**205.** All the wise who were in the village of Khardaha,
and those who had come from other places —

**206.** all, hearing it, were maddened with gladness of heart;
unable to check their tears, they were bathed in them.

**207.** Sri Jahnava, remembering Gaura and Nityananda,
at once set out with everyone.

**208.** Having told Sri Vasudha Devi something in private,
having carefully settled Ganga and Virachandra,

**209.** having arranged everything in every way,
they travelled some distance by boat.

**210.** As soon as they set out, the cry spread through the land:
"The Ishvari is going to Vraja by way of Kheturi."

**211.** Some way along they left the boat
and went to the house of a fortunate, dear merchant.

**212.** Day and night they are intoxicated with Nityananda's virtues;
at the sight of the Ishvari the bliss of prema surged up.

**213.** Sri Ishvari, showing mercy to all,
stayed there that day with her people.

**214.** Raghunatha, the son of lame Bhagavan,
the dearest disciple of Jagadisha Pandita,

**215.** came and met the Ishvari there.
Rising very early, all came to Ambika.

**216.** Sri Hridaya Chaitanya went out a long way
and brought the Ishvari and everyone to his house.

**217.** What befell them at the sight of Nitai and Chaitanya-chand
cannot be described.

**218.** After a while, regaining composure,
they ate and did their other duties there.

**219.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari told Hridaya Chaitanya
everything, quietly.

**220.** Hearing it, Sri Hridayananda rejoiced
and resolved to go to the village of Kheturi.

**221.** Sri Vamshivadana's son Sri Chaitanya Dasa
at that moment came with his people to Prabhu.[^10]

**222.** Seeing the feet of Sri Jahnava Ishvari,
he deemed himself blessed; streams flowed from both his eyes.

**223.** Again and again he bowed, falling to the ground,
and at the Ishvari's bidding grew calm and sat down.

**224.** With gladness of heart they told him everything,
and hearing it he was overwhelmed with joy.

**225.** Sri Chaitanya Dasa and others resolved:
'We shall go to Kheturi to see the holy festival.'

**226.** With gladness of heart all made ready
and informed Sri Hridaya Chaitanya Thakura.

**227.** From Shantipura came a man
who related the news from there:

**228.** 'Sri Achyutananda Prabhu, Advaita's son,
is so worn by separation that his life hangs in doubt.

**229.** To carry out the command of Mother Sri Sita,
he will set out at dawn to go to Kheturi.'

**230.** Hearing this, the Ishvari's joy grew greatly,
and she quickly sent all the news back by him.

**231.** Sri Jahnava with everyone at the Pandita's house
passed the night with great gladness of heart.

**232.** At dawn she saw the mangala-arati
and offered herself at the feet of Nitai and Chaitanya.

**233.** Having bidden all engaged in the holy service to be careful,
the Ishvari set out with everyone for Navadvipa.

**234.** Gazing from afar toward Sri Navadvipa,
she shed streams from both eyes that washed her breast.

**235.** Remembering all those pastimes of Navadvipa,
she heaved sighs like tongues of flame.

**236.** Her body grew helpless, her heart distraught;
after a while by Prabhu's will she grew calm.

**237.** All those dear to Prabhu who were in Navadvipa
heard of Sri Jahnava Ishvari's coming.

**238.** With gladness of heart all came forward to meet her;
seeing them from afar, the Ishvari got down from her palanquin.

**239.** Having seen the Ishvari, all
extolled their own good fortune:

**240.** 'Today the Creator has given us a blessed morning!' —
saying this, they came near and bowed again and again.

**241.** What Sri Jahnava Devi did, what was in her mind —
who understands the wondrous ways of prema?

**242.** With the dear ones who had come with Sri Ishvari
all met, each as was fitting.

**243.** At the meeting everyone's composure was gone:
some took the dust of another's feet again and again,

**244.** some in prema, clasping one another by the neck,
remembering the Lord's pastimes, wept aloud.

**245.** How wondrous is the glory of prema — who knows it?
After a while those dear to Prabhu grew calm.

**246.** Srivasa Pandita's brother Pandita Shripati
said carefully to Sri Madhava Acharya and others:

**247.** 'It would be good to bathe in the Ganga here.'
Hearing these words, all were pleased.

**248.** All bathed in the Ganga right there,
and Shripati hurried into Navadvipa,

**249.** and quickly brought various provisions;
meanwhile all finished their bathing and daily rites.

**250.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari, with supreme joy at heart,
fed everyone, having eaten a little herself.

**251.** Quickly entering Navadvipa,
the Ishvari came to the house of Srivasa Pandita.

**252.** There came the son of Prabhu Advaita,
named Sri Achyutananda, purifier of the worlds,

**253.** Achyuta's brother Sri Gopala, full of prema,
Sri Kanu Pandita, Vishnudasa Mahashaya,

**254.** Vanamali Dasa and other very wise ones —
their meeting was most wonderful.

**255.** The ocean of prema surged immeasurably;
it cannot all be told with one mouth.

**256.** Shrimati Ishvari, joyfully, in complete seclusion,
told everything to Sri Achyutananda.

**257.** Hearing it, Prabhu Advaita's son, in prema,
lost all composure and roared incessantly.

**258.** When Shripati, Shrinidhi and others were told,
all wished to see the festival.

**259.** The news of the journey to Kheturi spread everywhere,
and all gathered at Srivasa's house.

**260.** The bliss there was that day as all dined there
cannot be described.

**261.** The people of Navadvipa ran from all sides;
there was a great crowd at Srivasa's house.

**262.** Receiving the blessed sight of the Lord's associates,
they soothed hearts burned by the cruel fire of sorrow.

**263.** After part of the night all the people went home,
and the Lord's associates all lay down to sleep.

**264.** Rising at dawn, all set out quickly
and came to Akaihat, to Krishnadasa's house.

**265.** The excellent singer Krishnadasa, in prema,
bringing them to his home, deemed himself blessed.

**266.** Preparing food very quickly,
he made ready to go to Kheturi.

**267.** Rising at dawn, all with joyful hearts
came very quickly to Kantaka-nagara.

**268.** First Krishnadasa Thakura went
and told Sri Yadunandana everything in detail.

**269.** As soon as he heard it, with great gladness of heart
he went forward and quickly brought them to his house.

**270.** There came Sri Raghunandana with his people;
with Shivananda came the brahmana Vaninatha,

**271.** Vallabha, Chaitanya Dasa, Bhagavata Acharya,
the dancer Gopala, the best of brahmanas Jita Mishra,

**272.** Raghu Mishra, Kashinatha, Pandita Uddhava,
Sri Nayanananda Mishra, Mangala Vaishnava —

**273.** so came many dear to Prabhu,
and their meeting was most wonderful.

**274.** Seeing the beauty of Dasa Gadadhara's Gauranga,
all were enraptured, and their eyes were soothed.

**275.** To where Gaurachandra took sannyasa
all went, weeping and weeping.

**276.** The state they all fell into at the mere sight of the place —
all that cannot come from my mouth.

**277.** After a while all grew calm
and quickly bathed in the Ganga.

**278.** Meanwhile Yadunandana and others, with great care,
arranged various sweets on plates,

**279.** offered them row on row to Sri Gaurachandra
and placed them separately in the lodgings.

**280.** All meanwhile finished their bathing and other rites
and partook of Sri maha-prasada with great care.

**281.** That day Jahnava Ishvari herself,
with joy in her heart, went quickly to the kitchen

**282.** and quickly cooked many kinds of dishes;
hearing of it, all were astonished.

**283.** She offered the food to Sri Gaurangachandra,
and Prabhu ate in supreme bliss.

**284.** After a while she carefully removed the offering
and with the greatest care fed everyone.

**285.** Everything was like nectar — what comparison can be given?
Whoever ate forgot himself in bliss.

**286.** Sri Ishvari partook of the prasada,
and the minds of all the mahantas rejoiced.

**287.** Sri Yadunandana Chakravarti and all the rest
ate afterwards, with all care.

**288.** What they felt as they tasted the Sri maha-prasada
cannot be told: streams flowed from both their eyes.

**289.** Remembering his own beloved Dasa Gadadhara,
he grew calm after a while, sitting in seclusion.

**290.** Greatly eager to go to Kheturi,
he made preparations for going there.

**291.** The servants who serve Sri Gaurachandra
he instructed to be careful in every way.

**292.** When everything was done, evening came,
and all the mahantas came to Gauranga's courtyard.

**293.** Having seen the arati of Sri Gaurachandra,
they sang the holy name for a while.

**294.** All passed the night in the relish of talks about Krishna
and slept a little, with gladness of heart.

**295.** At dawn, bowing to Gaurachandra,
they all came along the road this way, addressing everyone.

**296.** Today they quickly crossed the Padmavati
and sent me to bring you the news at once."

**297.** Hearing all this, Acharya Thakura
became such as words cannot express.

**298.** In the hearts of Sri Thakura Mahashaya, Shyamananda and the others
joy reached its limit.

**299.** As they went, they saw before their eyes
everyone coming, radiant as the sun.

**300.** As they gazed, their eyes forgot to blink;
their bodies grew helpless; they could not walk.

**301.** Seeing their state, Jahnava Ishvari,
remembering Prabhu, got down from her palanquin.

**302.** Sri Achyuta and others who were in carriages
gladly and quickly got down to the ground.

**303.** Srinivasa Acharya and others, awash in tears of prema,
fell rolling at the Ishvari's feet.

**304.** Sri Jahnava Ishvari could not be calm;
the mercy she showed — who could tell it?

**305.** To Sri Achyutananda and the others dear to Prabhu
they bowed at the feet one by one.

**306.** Gazing at Srinivasa Acharya and the others,
Sri Achyutananda and the rest could not restrain their hearts.

**307.** One, embracing Srinivasa, wept;
one embraced Narottama again and again;

**308.** one, embracing Ramachandra, would not let him go;
one drenched Sri Gokulananda with tears;

**309.** one, spreading his arms, clasped Shridasa;
one showed great affection to Shyamananda;

**310.** one, gazing at Sri Govinda Kaviraja's face,
embraced him, and streams from his eyes washed his breast.

**311.** Seeing such ways of prema, such a wondrous meeting,
even the gods deemed themselves blessed.

**312.** As they entered the village, people ran from all sides;
the village of Kheturi was drowned in a flood of bliss.

**313.** Acharya Thakura, respectfully asking everyone's leave,
took them to separate lodgings.

**314.** Where the Ishvari's lodging with her people was,
there he appointed Ramachandra Kaviraja.

**315.** To the lodging of Raghunatha Acharya and others
he appointed Kaviraja Karnapura.

**316.** Where Hridaya Chaitanya's lodging was,
there he carefully appointed Shyamananda.

**317.** Where Sri Chaitanya Dasa and others put up,
there he appointed Sri Nrisimha Kaviraja.

**318.** To the lodging of Shripati, Shrinidhi Pandita and others
he appointed Sri Vyasa Acharya.

**319.** To the lodging of Krishnadasa of Akaihat and others
Sri Vallavikanta was appointed.

**320.** In the lodging where Sri Raghunandana was with his people,
Sri Govinda Kaviraja was appointed.

**321.** To the house of the brahmana Vaninatha, Jita Mishra and others
he appointed Rama, Krishna, Kumuda and others.

**322.** To Sri Yadunandana Chakravarti's lodging
he carefully appointed Kaviraja Bhagavan.

**323.** Where the lodgings of other Vaishnavas were,
there he appointed Sri Gopiramana and others.

**324.** Going everywhere and arranging everything,
he gave each a separate storeroom.

**325.** There were so many provisions there that they cannot be counted,
ever full by the will of Krishna Chaitanya.

**326.** Sri Acharya Thakura and Thakura Mahashaya
went about everywhere again and again in prema.

**327.** Such is the coming of the mahantas to the village of Sri Kheturi;
whoever hears of it has his desires fulfilled.

**328.** Listen to all this continually and with care:
Narahari tells the *Narottama-vilasa*.

*Thus ends the sixth vilasa of the Narottama-vilasa.*
