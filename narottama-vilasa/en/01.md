# Vilasa 1. Lokanatha Gosvami

*Opening verses and glorifications; the life of Lokanatha: Talkhari, his going to the Lord in Navadvipa, his search for Mahaprabhu, the dream-vision and the prophecy of Narottama; Lokanatha in Vrindavana with the Gosvamis; in Ramakeli the Lord calls Narottama by name*

**1.** *O you who are dear to those surrendered to the Lord of Sri, O king of dancers,*
*uniquely skilled in bestowing the treasure of your prema,*
*O friend of the life of Sri Gaura-Vishvambhara —*
*O Lokanatha, O Prabhu, be gracious to me!*[^1]

**2.** *I bow to Sri Lokanatha, the associate of Sri Chaitanya,*
*whose one life is Sri Radha-vinoda, who is the life of the people.*

**3.** *I bow to Sri Narottama, dear to Sri Gaura,*
*a bee at the lotus feet of Lokanatha, maddened by the rasa of Radha and Krishna.*

**4.** *I worship the branches of Sri Narottama Prabhu,*
*endowed with every good quality, removers of every anartha.*

**5.** *For the delight of the Vaishnavas and to fulfil my own cherished desire,*
*I briefly relate the book called "Narottama-vilasa."*[^2]

**6.** Glory, glory to Sri Gaura-Govinda, the Lord of all,
whose body, full of prema, enchants the worlds!

**7.** Glory to the son of Shachi and Jagannatha Mishra,
glory, glory to the life of Nityananda and Advaita!

**8.** Glory to the Lord of Gadadhara Pandita's life,
glory to the Lord of Srivasa, renowned in the world!

**9.** Glory to Him who is subdued by the prema of Haridasa and Vakreshvara,
glory to Him who is expert in increasing Murari's joy!

**10.** Glory to the friend of Gauridasa and Gadadhara,
glory to the Beloved of Narahari, full of majesty!

**11.** Glory to the treasury of virtues, dear to Svarupa,
glory to the life of Sanatana, Rupa and Gopala!

**12.** Glory, glory to the Lord together with the host of His devotees!
Reveal to me a little of the pastimes of Your devotees that I long for.

**13.** O listeners, hear the words of a fool such as I;
seeing my destitution, be kind to me, all of you.

**14.** I know neither good nor bad, I have no knowledge at all;
whatever I say, it is only the will of the sadhus that is strong in it.

**15.** This book, the "Narottama-vilasa," is delightful:
correct it and relish it continually.

**16.** In the order in which I made the mangalacharana in the preceding verses,
in that order I shall now speak — listen attentively.[^3]

**17.** Glory, glory to Lokanatha, dear to Sri Chaitanya,
the lamp of a brahmana family, renowned in every respect!

**18.** Here I shall tell a little of his life —
listen: it is known throughout the world.

**19.** In the land of Jessore there is a village named Talkhari:[^4]
there he appeared, incomparable in every way.

**20.** His mother was Sita, his father Padmanabha Chakravarti;
what can I say of how the two of them shone?[^5]

**21.** Padmanabha Chakravarti is known in the world:
Prabhu Advaita had great kindness for him.

**22.** A great Vaishnava, extraordinary in all his deeds,
full of every virtue, the foremost of the brahmanas of Rarh.

**23.** Day and night he was immeasurably intoxicated with sankirtana;
who could keep composure seeing the streams from his eyes?

**24.** By the grace of Sri Advaita, with great joy in his heart,
he would constantly come to Nadia to see Gauranga.

**25.** When he returned home, nothing pleased Padmanabha:
together with his wife he always sang of Gaurachandra's virtues.

**26.** As was Padmanabha, so was his wife Sita —
a great Vaishnavi, devoted to her husband.

**27.** Having obtained such a son as Lokanatha, the virtuous mother
raised him — how, I have no power to tell.

**28.** Having dedicated her son to the feet of Gaurachandra,
she watched her son's doings with great joy.

**29.** Sri Lokanatha had great eagerness on the path of bhakti;
beautiful in every limb, he was like compassion embodied.

**30.** From a young age he was learned in all the shastras
and most diligent in serving his father and mother.

**31.** He worshipped the feet of Krishna constantly
and by the power of bhakti drew all hearts to himself.

**32.** When after some time his father and mother passed from sight,
he told his friends what was in his mind.

**33.** Leaving worldly life and its pleasures like filth,
he set out for Nadia to see Prabhu.

**34.** In Navadvipa he gave himself to Prabhu's feet,
and Prabhu graciously kept him near Himself.

**35.** Prabhu, anxious at heart about the sannyasa He was to take,
quickly sent Lokanatha to Vraja.

**36.** Who can understand Prabhu's doings? They are utterly deep:
having sent Lokanatha away, He Himself was not at ease.

**37.** Lokanatha understood what was in Prabhu's heart:
in two or four days He would leave His home.

**38.** Prabhu is the independent Lord, all is by His will:
what Lokanatha's journey was like, I cannot describe.

**39.** Tears flowed incessantly from his eyes;
a day's journey took him four or five days.

**40.** Some way along he heard that Prabhu had taken sannyasa
and, consoling His dear devotees, had gone to Nilachala.

**41.** Remembering that the hair was no longer to be seen on Prabhu's head,
he wept aloud.

**42.** Half dead, by Prabhu's order,
after some days he entered Vrindavana.

**43.** He stayed some days, gazing at the beauty of Vrindavana,
and there he heard that Prabhu had gone to the south.

**44.** Anxious at heart, Lokanatha
set off for the south, where Sri Gaurasundara was.

**45.** Some way along he learned all the news:
Prabhu had returned from the south to Nilachala.

**46.** Having set out for Vrindavana by the road through Gauda,
He had gone back to the Kshetra at the wish of the devotees.[^6]

**47.** Again Lokanatha heard that Prabhu had come to Vrindavana,
and at once he set out for Vraja.

**48.** Coming to Vrindavana, he learned all the news:
in these very days Prabhu had left for Prayaga.

**49.** Grieved, Lokanatha resolved firmly:
"At dawn I shall go to Prayaga to see Prabhu."

**50.** Remembering Prabhu's virtues, he wept;
he rolled on the ground, and none could hold him.
