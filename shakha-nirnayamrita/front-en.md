# Shakha-nirnayamrita

*Yadunatha Dasa. A Sanskrit vandana of the branches of Gadadhara Pandita. 59 verses*

*Translated from the Sanskrit into English*

---

## Translator's Note

The *Shakha-nirnayamrita* ("The Nectar of Determining the Branches") consists of 59 Sanskrit verses of vandana to the disciples and followers of Gadadhara Pandita. It was not published as a separate book; the translation follows the text printed in Kishori Dasa Babaji's collection *Gaura-bhakta-amrita-lahari* (the editor's notes and verses are not translated). Majumdar uses it in his list of associates; together with the *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Adi 12) and the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* it is one of the main sources on Gadadhara's branch.
