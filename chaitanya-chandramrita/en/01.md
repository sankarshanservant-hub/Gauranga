# Part One

*Praise. Obeisance. Benediction. The glory of Sri Chaitanya's devotees (verses 1–28)*

## Praise (stuti)

**1.** We praise the best of all — Him who has the form of Chaitanya, whose generosity is boundless and supremely wonderful; who appeared in the supreme abode of Navadvipa to give the son of the lord of Vraja — Himself — a taste of the nectar waves of His own pure, maddening, sweet love, and to distribute them to others.

**2.** A man never touched by *dharma*, always sunk in lawlessness, who has never come into the sight of the saints and has never been anywhere among them — intoxicated by the taste of the nectar of Sri-Hari-rasa, the *rasa* of the love of Radha and Krishna, which this Lord has bestowed, he dances, sings aloud and rolls on the ground. I praise this indescribable Lord.

**3.** That secret which those devoted to ritual did not attain, which was not won by austerity, meditation and yoga, which no one even guessed at in spite of renunciation, abandonment of the world, knowledge of the truth and hymns of praise, which even those who love Govinda did not know — that revealed itself of its own accord through the Holy Name alone, when the Supreme descended. I bow to that Gaura.

**4.** I bow to the merciful Lord, Sri Chaitanya, who alone is able to give the essence of love to anyone who has seen Him, touched Him, glorified Him or remembered Him, or even from afar has bowed to Him or honoured Him.

**5.** For those who possess the wealth of His merciful glance, *kaivalya* (liberation) becomes like hell, the city of the gods like a flower in the sky, the pack of untamed senses — those serpents of death — like snakes whose fangs have been pulled out; the whole world becomes full of happiness, and Brahma, Indra and the rest become like worms. Gaura alone we praise.

**6.** His devotees, worshipped even by the gods, having drunk and become intoxicated with the wondrous nectar juices of radiant love and bliss that flow from His lotus feet, laugh at Brahma and the others, do not think much even of great Vaishnavas, and censure the knowers of Brahman and of yoga. We praise that Gaurachandra.

**7.** What is so great in the destruction of the race of Rakshasas and Daityas? What is so great in revealing the path of yoga and other practices? What is so great in the creation of the world and the rest, in the rescue of the earth and so on? We praise only the form of Chaitanya, who has revealed the path of the highest *bhakti* to Bhagavan, radiant with *prema*.

## Obeisance (namaskara)

**8.** Obeisance to Chaitanya-chandra, the Moon, whose face shines like millions of moons — the moon over the ocean of love and bliss, whose smile is like beautiful moonbeams.

**9.** By devotion to His lotus feet alone is attained the highest goal of man, called love (*prema*). To Him, the good of all the good of the world, to Chaitanya-chandra — obeisance, again obeisance!

**10.** He vigorously swings His arms and legs — oh wonder! — raising His arms, which are like pillars of gold; His body sways in a beautiful dance, His eyes are long like lotuses; with indescribable cries of "Hari! Hari!", full of ecstatic bliss, He destroys all that is inauspicious in the universe. I bow to that Chaitanya-chandra, the crest-jewel of the gods, absorbed in incomparable *rasa*.

**11.** To Him whose body is made of pastimes of bliss, who is beautiful with a golden divine lustre, who bestows the *rasa* of great love — to Chaitanya-chandra — obeisance, again obeisance!

**12.** With streams of tears His eyes are like millions of new rain-clouds; by the wealth of His love He laughs at millions of supreme abodes; by the lustre of His body He seems to pour forth the sweetness of millions of oceans of nectar. I bow to that Hari, who has assumed the guise of a *sannyasi*.

## Benediction (ashir-vada)

**13.** With the shoulders of a lion, with cheeks smiling ever so sweetly, with many wondrous transformations born of the inconceivable radiant *rasa*, with a beauty as charming as the heart of a blossoming golden lotus — may the body of Madhava, become one with Radha, protect you.

(I bow to Sri Krishna Chaitanya, Gaura, who is Krishna Himself, but who, longing for the moods of Radha, entirely gave up His own nature.)[^1]

**14.** Seeing a mass of new clouds, He becomes intoxicated; seeing a peacock feather, He becomes utterly overwhelmed; at the sight of a string of *gunja* berries, He trembles; and when He sees a dark youth, He is filled with wonder and awe. Thus Hari in the form of Gaura spreads His own love. May He protect you!

**15.** The ocean of mercy, in a wondrous garment the colour of the red dawn, radiant and full — a pure great light made of the nectar of love — born in the milk ocean of Shachi's womb — the beautiful lord of the digits of the moon, with generous and wondrous rays — may He rise in the sky of your heart!

**16.** To count His own names, "Hare Krishna," the blessing of the whole world, He chants them and ties knots in the cord at His waist with a hand that trembles from the excess of love; His face is bathed in tears; He walks to and fro, longing to see Himself — Jagannatha.[^2] May Hari in the form of Gaura, the delight of the eyes, protect you!

**17.** It suddenly uproots the darkness accumulated within all the worlds, forcefully makes the ocean of the *rasa* of love and bliss overflow without end, and cools the universe, tormented day and night by the threefold miseries — may the radiance of Chaitanya-chandra ever shine in your hearts![^3]

## The glory of Sri Chaitanya's devotees

**18.** On that path where once even the lords of sages went astray, into which no one's mind on the whole earth has penetrated, which even Shuka did not know, which the merciful Shauri (Krishna) never revealed anywhere even to His own intimates — on that radiant path of *bhakti* the beloved devotees of Gaura joyfully play.

**19.** Only so long does talk of Brahman, the path of liberation, not taste bitter; only so long does adherence to the order of the world and the Vedas not slacken; only so long does the noisy dispute of the learned about the many external paths go on — as long as one has not seen one who loves the lotus feet of Sri Chaitanya.

**20.** Where else is such renunciation, where such aversion to talk of sense objects as to hell, where this wave overflowing with humility, where that unearthly power, where that path of great *bhakti* — all that is seen in those whose refuge is Gaura?

**21.** One who has even once seen Gaura's beautiful face, shy with love, with eyes like full-blown lotuses brimming with streams of tears — intoxicated with ever-new loving attachment (*anuraga*), will never wish to leave Gaura's feet, an ocean of unearthly, sparkling sweetness.

**22.** One may practise *dharma*, serve Vishnu, travel to the holy places, study the Vedas — but without serving the feet of Gaura's beloved devotees one does not come to know the abode that is hard to reach even through the Vedas — Vrindavana, the footstool of Sri Radha-Govinda.

**23.** If, by long churning the shoreless ocean of nectar, some supreme, incomparable essence were obtained — even that would taste bitter and harsh to those touched by the radiance of the feet of Madana-Gopala, who has become golden with the lustre of Sri Radha.

**24.** Humility lower than a blade of grass, a naturally gentle and pure appearance, speech sweet as nectar, spitting at even the scent of sense objects, a mind leaning on nothing [external], indescribably overwhelmed by love for Hari — such good qualities are found in this world only in the devotees of Gaura.

**25.** Let them serve millions of the best gurus, let them study millions of *shrutis* and *shastras* — but to those on whom Chaitanya's merciful glance has fallen, the highest secret is revealed at once.

**26.** Let there be millions of kinds of renunciation, millions of kinds of restraint, self-control, patience, friendliness and the rest, millions of meditations on the truth, and even millions of kinds of Vaishnava *bhakti* — all of these together do not equal even a millionth part of the qualities that dwell of themselves in those who delight in the fragrance of the radiance of the toenails of the dear devotees of Sri Chaitanya-chandra.

**27.** Some of them stride over oceans and mountains and dance; others again and again hurl great reproaches at Indra and the other gods; and Advaita-chandra and the others, beside themselves with tumultuous bliss — who did not run wild at this festival of Chaitanya's dance?

**28.** Such a relationship with the *rasa* of the Lord's lotus feet as no one in this world has had, will have or has — all this the non-envious come to know through the outpouring mercy of this Gaura, who sports with the supreme wealth: devotion to Himself.

[^1]: In the 1951 edition this verse stands in brackets without a number; it is probably a later insertion. It could not be found in the OCR copy of the Bengali edition with Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's translation.

[^2]: Mahaprabhu worshipped Jagannatha in Puri as another form of His own (more precisely, as Himself). Here it is said: "longing to see Himself — Jagannatha."

[^3]: In the Bengali edition with Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's translation this verse stands here too (No. 17), and is then repeated in the seventh section (No. 75) with the words "in our hearts" (*asmakam* instead of *yushmakam*).
