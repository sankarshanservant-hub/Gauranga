### 63
THEME: Fidelity to Gaura's lotus feet
Let the *shastras* say what they please, let the learned logicians interpret as they like — but the nectar of Sri Chaitanya's lotus feet alone is my life.

### 64
THEME: Deep attachment to Gaura's lotus feet
Let the extremely rare *siddhis* — *anima* and the rest — come of themselves and lie in my palm like an *amalaki* fruit; let all the gods come of their own accord to serve me; more than that, let even this body of mine become four-armed — still my mind will not swerve a hair's breadth from Sri Gaurachandra.
From the word-for-word gloss: "if this body becomes four-armed" — that is, if even the liberation of *sarupya* (possessing a form like the Lord's) is obtained.

### 65
THEME: Rejection of association with the *asuras* who have turned away from Gaura
If I must live even in a cage full of terrible tongues of flame — so be it, only let me never have association with people who have turned away from Sri Chaitanya's lotus feet. If my mind does not taste even a little of the radiance of love for the pollen of Sri Gaura's lotus feet, it will not desire even the abode of Vaikuntha and the rest, though it come of itself, without any *sadhana*.

### 66
THEME: Without Gaura's lotus feet, enjoyment, liberation and *siddhis* are utterly insignificant
Let my fame spread over the whole earth: "Great, great!"; or let an everlasting, unearthly *siddhi* appear on earth; or let me, worshipping Vishnu, the Lord of the universe, obtain the desired beautiful four-armed form — but without *bhakti* for Gaura my mind never values any of this, never values it.

### 67
THEME: The prayer of Gaura's devotee
"O Chaitanya-chandra, who attract the hearts of all beings, form of eternity, knowledge and bliss!", "O treasury of mercy!", "O most magnanimous!", "O You who have filled the hearts of all beings with love of various kinds!" (that is, You who fill the hearts of all beings with various love — *dasya*, *sakhya* and the rest), "O possessor of a wondrous lustre like that of molten gold!", "O Sri Hari in a golden body!", "O ocean of auspicious qualities!", "O embodied form of the *rasa* of separation (*vipralambha*)!", "O lover of Your own names!" — may I pass this life chanting such names: this alone I pray for.
From the word-for-word gloss: "love of various kinds" — *dasya*, *sakhya*, *vatsalya* and *madhura*.

### 68
THEME: Without *bhakti* for Gaura, *rati* for Radha is impossible
O Krishna, ornament of the Shura dynasty! When will guileless *rati* arise in me for Your Gaura-body, which bestows the elevated, radiant *rasa* of love and is the very life of the *rasika* devotees? And when, by the transcendental (*adhokshaja*) inference that proceeds from this guileless *rati*, will the radiance of the jewel-like toenails of Sri Radhika suddenly rise in my heart?
From the word-for-word gloss: the inference here is this: where the radiance of the jewel-like toenails of Sri Radha has risen, there the feeling for Gaurahari is present too; it is called unearthly because it is beyond the reach of the worldly senses.

### 69
THEME: Meditation on Sri Chaitanya
Adorned with garlands of full-blown *damanaka* flowers, giving complete bliss to multitudes of people, the abode of mercy, with a golden lustre bright as gold — the supreme abode named "Chaitanya," that is, the best shelter, we contemplate.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*arama-rama*" — He who, staying in a secluded place, experiences bliss within Himself; "*avirama-grihita-nama*" — He who incessantly chants the names.

### 70
THEME: Meditation on Gaurahari, immersed in *mahabhava*, dwelling at Nilachala
May my mind remember Sri Gaurasundara — Him whose eyes, like bees, rest on the radiant lotus face of Jagannathadeva, who is ever engaged in pastimes on the festive summit of Nilachala; in whom, in the highest love, which has the nature of *mahabhava*, flow the waves of *rasa* — joy, pride and the other [feelings]; who in beauty is Kandarpa himself to the doe-eyed young women; and who at the same time is supremely detached (that is, enacting the role of an acharya, He is extremely detached from the *rasa* of enjoyment — from looking at women and the like).

### 71
THEME: Meditation on Gaurahari with blazing *sattvika-bhavas*
He whose garment is the colour of the dawn; whose holy body is adorned with lovely drops of tears like pearls, flowing from His lotus eyes; whose body is beautified also by the blazing (*uddipta*) *sattvika-bhavas* — the thrill of the hair born of *mahabhava* and the rest — in that Sri Hari, Gauranga, I take shelter.

### 72
THEME: Gaurahari's beauty, purifying power, coolness and bestowal of sweetness
More beautiful than Kandarpa, purer than the stream of the Ganga, the saviour of the fallen, cooler than the moon, sweeter than nectar, more generous than the wish-fulfilling tree, more tender in love than a mother — when will Gaurahari, becoming the object of my meditation, step lovingly into my heart?
From the word-for-word gloss: Kandarpa can scorn even the pride of Brahma by his beauty and power, but the worldly Kandarpa (Madana) produces agitation and bewilderment, while Gaurahari's beauty destroys them and makes one experience the happiness of the highest bliss of love; the stream of the Ganga destroys sins but cannot purify a sinful heart, while Sri Gaurasundara purifies the heart and causes a stream of the highest love to rise in it — therefore He is purer and more wonderful than the Ganga; the moon can dispel only external darkness and heat, while Sri Gaurasundara completely destroys the darkness of the heart — the gloom of the ignorance of millions of births — and the heat of suffering, flooding the heart with the rays of the bliss of love; the sweetness of nectar only the gods can taste, and from it worldly pride, envy and the like arise in them, while Gaura's sweetness is inaccessible even to the gods, and tasting it completely removes worldly pride, envy and the like and awakens the bliss of love; the wish-fulfilling tree gives fruit only to one who asks it, while Sri Gaurasundara is so generous that He Himself, becoming the petitioner, bestows the wealth of love and so on even on the unworthy and those who do not ask; a mother's love brings poisonous fruits in the future, for it is directed to the perishable body and the like, while Sri Gaurasundara's love is directed to the soul of the *jiva*, and therefore the *jiva* becomes worthy of the highest bliss of love; moreover, a mother loves only her own child, while Sri Gaurasundara sprinkles all living beings with the *rasa* of love.

### 73
THEME: Love for Gaurahari in saffron cloth, the giver of love
He who, again and again giving heaps of the nectar *rasa* of love, sweeter than the sweet, gladdens this world; who wears on His hips a lovely saffron (*gairika*) cloth; whose bodily radiance puts to shame millions of new lightnings — that boundlessly merciful, non-dual Purusha Himself, engaged in spiritual pastimes — may He alone be dear to me.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*eka*" — non-dual, or He who has neither equal nor superior; "*deva*" — the Purusha engaged in pastimes (*lila*); the cloth is dyed with *manjishtha*, a particular red creeper.

### 74
THEME: Gaurahari's incomparable form and qualities
He whose captivating beauty and charm put to shame millions upon millions of Kandarpas, by the splendour of whose most beautiful moon-face the radiance of millions upon millions of rising autumn moons grows dim, whose great generosity belittles millions upon millions of wish-fulfilling trees — will that Gaurasundara step into my heart by virtue of the heaps of pious merits (*sukriti*) of millions upon millions of births?

### 75
THEME: The bodily radiance of Gaurachandra, which makes the ocean of love swell and destroys impurity
That moonlight of the Chaitanya-moon which suddenly uproots the masses of the darkness of ignorance in the hearts of living beings, incessantly makes the ocean of the *rasa* of the bliss of love swell by force, and every moment cools the world scorched by the three kinds of misery — *adhyatmika* and the rest — may that radiance of the Chaitanya-moon shine even for a moment in our hearts.

### 76
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, in whom there is a mingling of feelings (*bhava-shabalya*) — *vibhavas*, *anubhavas* and the rest
Now emaciated, now full, now with eyes full of tears of the bliss of love, now with a smile on His face, now cool, now burning, now running, now stunned, now talkative, now silent — may such a Gaura-body of Krishna appear in my heart.
From the word-for-word gloss: "emaciated" — in prolonged separation (*vipralambha*), that is, in the state in which Srimati Radhika was in separation from Krishna, who had gone to Mathura: Gaurahari, imbued with that feeling, for a moment grew thin from separation from Krishna; "full" — again in separation, [at the appearance] of Krishna […], for a moment full; "cool" — scorched by the fire of separation from Krishna, then, [on meeting] Krishna, for a moment cooled; "smiling" — for a moment with a gentle smile from laughter and jokes with Krishna; "burning" — *prema-vaicittya*, that is, even while near Krishna, again for a moment scorched by the fire of the coming separation from Him; "running" — absorbed in the feeling "Krishna is going away" and for a moment running after Him; "stunned" — again, coming to another place […], for a moment stunned (*stambha*, one of the eight *sattvika-bhavas*); "talkative" — for a moment uttering incoherent speech (*prajalpa*): "O libertine, destroyer of the vows of chaste women, do not touch me!"; "silent" — for a time keeping silence out of offended pride (*mana*).

### 77
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, who does not consider place, time or the worthiness of the recipient, and impartially bestows love
That Lord (the independent Ishvara) who does not consider whether a person is worthy or unworthy, does not look to whether he is His own or another's, does not deliberate on what may be given and what may not, and does not wait for a suitable time — who at once bestows the *rasa* of *bhakti*, hard to attain by hearing, seeing, bowing, meditation and the like — that Bhagavan Gaurasundara is my only refuge.

### 78
THEME: An example for the preceding words
He by whose mercy were delivered even the greatest sinners such as Gopala Chapala, the low-born such as the *yavana* tailor and others, people of evil character who had reached the limit of evil deeds — Jagai, Madhai and their like, given to sin and forbidden acts — beings lower than the dog-eating *candala*, always given to evil tendencies, born in an evil land where there is no Ganga and where the Pandavas never set foot, and living in an evil land, and also those ruined by evil association — in that Gaurasundara I take my only refuge.
From the word-for-word gloss: Gopala Chapala and his like are the greatest sinners, struck with leprosy; "the limit of evil deeds" — the abduction of others' wives, drunkenness and so on; they live in an evil land as a consequence of their evil deeds.

### 79
THEME: Bhagavan Krishna Himself, clothed in the feeling and lustre of Radha, is Gaura
He who left the radiant, beautiful Vrindavana on the bank of the daughter of Kalinda and went to the flowering gardens on the shore of the salt ocean; who left His most beautiful yellow garment and put on a garment the colour of the dawn; who concealed His own colour, that is, His dark colour (with the feeling and lustre of His most beloved Srimati Radhika), and manifested a golden lustre — that Sri Hari is my only refuge.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the daughter of Kalinda" is the Yamuna: Kalinda is the mountain Kalinda or else the sun; Krishna's own colour is dark like a sapphire (*indranila*).

### 80
THEME: To obtain the great love, hard to reach even for the lords of the gods, one should take shelter of Gaurahari's feet
Ho, fools! Seek that secret path of devotion to Hari which formerly even the sages could not know with their far-seeing vision (foresight). And if there is no faith in your heart, if it seems hard to reach — give up all that (your endeavours) entirely and take shelter at Gaurahari's feet.
From the word-for-word gloss: there may be no faith because the path of *bhakti* is extremely secret.

### 81
THEME: Obeisance to Gaurahari, bathed in tears at the sight of Jagannatha
He who holds His joined lotus palms high above His head, who bathes His tender cheeks with flowing tears, who is adorned with a garment having the lustre of the filaments of a fresh lotus — bow down to that Gaurasundara, clothed in the lustre of molten gold, the supreme light.

### 82
THEME: Without Mahaprabhu's mercy it is impossible to obtain the elevated, radiant *rasa*
O brother! Loudly chant the mighty series of names of the lord of Gokula or meditate on His divinely sweet form, which blesses the world — but if Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu's merciful glance has not fallen on you, you cannot have even a hope of that supreme, elevated and radiant *rasa* of love.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the radiant (*ujjvala*) *rasa*" is *shringara*, the *rasa* of conjugal love, in which the greatest *rasa* is contained.

### 83
THEME: Instruction to take shelter of the feet of Gaura, the source of all perfections
Ho, do not rashly throw yourself into any *sadhana* other than the shelter of Sri Chaitanya's feet: Gaura's people, wholly intoxicated by drinking the nectar *rasa* of love for Krishna, laugh at all your endeavours. And now hear one most secret word: that which in all the Vedas and other *shastras* is set forth as the object of love — know for certain: its only object (*vishaya*) is this Sri Gauranga.
From the word-for-word gloss: "Hari" — He whose nature is to steal the heart and its wealth; "intoxicated" — those in whom intoxication, that is, the bliss of love, has arisen — Gaura's devotees.

### 84
THEME: Sri Gaurahari is worthy of worship even for one who lacks pure *bhakti* and […]
"The path of worship of Sri Krishna, which produces distaste for the path of knowledge of the undifferentiated Brahman and the like — that I do not know! And I do not happen to meet true gurus! (What am I to do now?) In whom am I to seek shelter?" — Alas, alas, what a fool you are! (You still cannot decide who your shelter is?) Has not even the name of Gaurahari entered your ears?
From the word-for-word gloss: "knowledge and the like" are the knowledge of the undifferentiated Brahman, the regular and occasional duties (*nitya-naimittika dharma*), *adhyatma-yoga*, dry renunciation and so on; the sense of the last words: quickly bring the worship of Gaurahari to your ears; delay no longer.

### 85
THEME: Leaving […], one should pray only for Gaura's mercy, which fulfils all goals
Cast far away your vain absorption in the regular, occasional and other rites (*karma*); never let even the slightest word about the path of ascending reasoning (*aroha*) come even to the threshold of your ears, and never fall into delusion about the body and everything connected with it — then by the mercy of Sri Gaurasundara you will obtain the crown of the goals of man: love for Krishna, full of supreme wondrous sweetness.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the path of *adhyatma*" — the reasoning "*neti, neti*" ("not this, not this") of the adherents of the ascending method; "the body and the rest" — body, home, family, country, society and everything of the kind; "the crown of the goals of man" — *prema*, the fifth goal of man.

### 86
THEME: The insignificance of talking with women, the pursuit of heaven, […] and the like, and the supremacy of the shelter of Gaura's feet alone
Always beware of association with women, those tigresses; considering heaven (transient) as insignificant as a straw, spit at it; what is the use of studying many *shastras*? — give that up too; and what is the gain of pilgrimages? — renounce them too. (Look here:) Sri Gaura, enacting the role of a sannyasi, dances on the shore of the blue ocean at Nilachala in the bliss of love of His own essence — Krishna! O fortunate devotees, (go, go,) take shelter at His feet.
From the word-for-word gloss: woman is compared to a tigress, and conversation with her is forbidden; to the objection: "How then shall heaven be obtained, since sacrifices and other rites are performed together with one's wife?" — it is said: spit at heaven; it is false, that is, transient. To the objection: "Then we shall study the *shastras* on yoga and practise yoga — that can give every perfection" — it is said: enough of studying *shastras*, for with those who practise yoga, far from perfection, even the fall of those who have reached the heights of yoga is spoken of; where attainment is so uncertain and the practice itself so painful — what need is there of it? To the objection: "Then service of the holy places is more pleasant: serving them, we shall obtain knowledge and become worthy of liberation" — it is said: enough of pilgrimages, for, first, it is hard labour, and second, the *karmis* and *jnanis* with worldly minds retain offences against the holy abode (*dhama-aparadha*), and their wanderings through holy places are only vain endeavours for sense gratification. "*Dhanyah*" — people worthy of obtaining the wealth of love.

### 87
THEME: The essence of the instruction — leaving futile endeavours on the painful paths of yoga and the like, love Gaura alone
O brothers! What is the use of vain searching on the painfully difficult paths of *ashtanga-yoga* and the rest? Practising *bhakti* to Krishna's lotus feet, plunder the wealth of love — everything most valuable. And if you have hope of the festival of the bliss of love, supremely wondrous, inaccessible even to Shiva, Brahma and the others — then love the most magnanimous Gaurahari, whose glory is unfathomable.
From the word-for-word gloss: "everything valuable" (*sarvartha*) is *prema*, the crown of all goals, or the only real need among all things — the wealth of love.

### 88
THEME: The fruit of *bhakti* for Gaura is service to Radha
To the extent that a person possessing heaps of piety (*sukriti*) obtains *bhakti* for Sri Gaura's lotus feet, to that same extent the ocean of the nectar of love for the lotus feet of Sri Sri Radha suddenly surges up in his heart.

### 89
THEME: The supremacy of the shelter of Gaura's feet
Chaitanya's feet, worshipped even by Shiva and Brahma, are the receptacle of the shoreless ocean of the nectar of the secret of the elevated, radiant *rasa*. Therefore in this age of Kali let pious people, [lovingly meditating on them,] take shelter of them and, maddened with love, in this very age sing of them alone (the glory of Chaitanya's feet).
From the word-for-word gloss: the pious (*kritinah*) are people possessing *sukriti*, that is, those who grasp the essence.

### 90
THEME: The method of preaching *bhakti* for Gaura of the *tridandi-gosvamis*: leaving everything, the only duty is love for Gaura's feet
O *sadhus*! I (a *tridandi* mendicant), holding a straw between my teeth and falling at your feet, with hundreds of entreaties say only this (beg this alms): leaving everything (all the sanctity or *dharma* you have invented) far behind (rejecting it), be filled with love for the feet of Sri Chaitanya-chandra.
From the word-for-word gloss: the straw between the teeth indicates the humility of the *kirtana*-preaching *tridandi-gosvami*: the tiniest of the straws that the people of the world trample underfoot he holds between his teeth, that is, shows it the highest respect; falling at the feet indicates his quality of "*amani*" (not demanding respect): he does not think himself, drunk with pride, "Narayana," like a *brahma-sannyasi*, nor is he bound, like the *smartas*, by worldly judgements about the gross and subtle bodies — brahmana or *candala*, senior or junior — being deprived of the vision of the true nature of all beings; therefore, demanding respect from no one, he is ready to proclaim the truth; the hundreds of entreaties indicate his "tolerance like a tree": the people of the world do not at all wish to hear the truth, but the *tridandi-gosvamis*, entreating them, make them hear *hari-katha*; the address "O *sadhus*" indicates his quality of "*manada*" (giving respect to others); "leaving everything": you are, of course, *sadhus*, but everything on account of which you think yourselves *sadhus* or are known as such in the world — *karma*, *jnana*, Sankhya and yoga, *phalgu-vairagya* (false renunciation), the four goals of life, the practice of *dharma* and *adharma*, and all good and bad endeavours or desires opposed to the gratification of Krishna's senses — leave from afar.

### 91
THEME: Gaura's mercy — the tasting of the most secret nectar of love — is hard to attain even in Vaikuntha
Liberation — *brahma-sayujya* or the four kinds of liberation, *salokya* and the rest, attained by *vaidhi-bhakti* mixed with knowledge — is not hard to attain. Service to Narayana, attained by *vaidhi-bhakti*, is not hard to attain either. But Gaura's mercy (Gaura's mercy is threefold: (1) pure — in the form of the removal of *anarthas*; (2) […] — in the form of attaining the *rasa* of the *siddhanta* of *bhakti*; (3) joyful — in the form of attaining the dignity of *madhurya*) is hard to attain even in Vaikuntha, for even the associates of Narayana in Vaikuntha pray for Gaura's mercy.
From the word-for-word gloss: this is said in answer to a possible question: "Why, leaving liberation and *bhakti* (*vaidhi-bhakti*, full of majesty), obtain love for Gaura's feet?"; "Gaurachandra's mercy" — the tasting of the most secret nectar of love, manifested by His mercy.

### 92
THEME: By taking shelter of Gaura, world-attracting qualities become possible — a sweet nature, good fortune, compassion and forbearance
Take shelter of the lotus feet of the Chaitanya-moon, be filled with the supreme, that is, never before given (*anarpita-cari*), elevated, radiant *rasa*, and give the three worlds manifold bliss through such good qualities as sweetness (a sweet nature), good fortune (the tasting of the highest love), compassion (turning averse souls towards Krishna, or preaching the Name) and forbearance (tolerance like a tree).
From the word-for-word gloss: to be filled with such *rasa* is possible only in the shelter of Chaitanya's feet; "compassion" — the distribution of this love to all living beings; "forbearance" and the rest — humility "lower than grass" and the other qualities of a Vaishnava.

### 93
THEME: Without the shelter of Gaura's feet alone it is impossible to cross *samsara*, taste the *rasa* of *sankirtana* and obtain the wealth of love
If there is a desire to cross the ocean of *samsara*, if the mind wishes to delight in the sweetness of the nectar *rasa* of *sankirtana*, if there is a longing to play in the ocean of love — then take shelter of the feet of Sri Chaitanya-chandra.
From the word-for-word gloss: the desire to cross the ocean of *samsara* is the desire, having passed to the other shore of *samsara*, to plunge into the ocean of *bhakti*.

### 94
THEME: *Siddhanta*, *prema-bhakti* and renunciation abide only in Gaura's devotees
Let (people) cultivate knowledge, renunciation, *bhakti* and the rest in whatever way they like — but where [is one to find] that special knowledge, renunciation and *bhakti* which are obtained by serving the lotus feet of the Chaitanya-moon? Nothing like it is to be seen anywhere else.
From the word-for-word gloss: this special knowledge, renunciation and *bhakti* which the devotees who have taken shelter of the Chaitanya-moon's feet possess are the knowledge of the truth of the sweetness of Bhagavan Sri Krishna Himself in everything, detachment [from everything else] and the elevated, radiant *rasa* of *bhakti*.

### 95
THEME: One not devoted to Chaitanya obtains only the round of coming and going in *samsara*
If this world, devoid of *bhakti* for Krishna and devoid of consciousness, does not worship Bhagavan Sri Chaitanya Himself, the all-controlling, worthy of worship even by the best of the gods — Shiva, Brahma and the others — it obtains only death in all its forms, that is, people subject to birth and death merely taste the fruits of *karma* — happiness, misery and the like.
From the word-for-word gloss: "devoid of consciousness (*a-caitanya*)" — that is, devoid of realization of one's own true nature, or devoid of *bhakti* for the all-pervading Consciousness (Chaitanya).

### 96
THEME: A person having even the slightest *bhakti* for the Chaitanya-moon is able to engage even […] the gods in his service
To one who has hope in the two feet of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, even the lord of the gods appears as a servant; what to speak of a king, who is like an insignificant worm?

### 97
THEME: The great souls who have obtained the *cintamani* of the worship of Gaura take no thought for their own maintenance
What need of the royal gate has that petitioner who longs for the wealth of the feet of Sri Krishna-Chaitanya? Is there such a fool who, having obtained a *cintamani*, will go (to distant lands) for silver?

### 98
THEME: In the world, apart from Gaura's devotees, there is no one intoxicated with love
In mountain caves many *jnana-yogis*, given to meditation and the search for the undifferentiated Brahman, abide, having realized Brahman; on earth there are many people devoted to the practice of *ashtanga-yoga* who have obtained *siddhis* — *anima* and the rest; and many more, intoxicated with false objects — learning, strength, wealth and the like — engage in empty disputes. But who in the world today, without Gaurahari's mercy, dances in the intoxication of love?

### 99
THEME: Gaura's devotee […] is free from petty desires
If Sri Gaurasundara, the Lord even of the gods — Brahma, Shiva and the rest — is merciful, then I count the residents of Kashi as nothing; why should I seek the abode of Gaya? If liberation itself seems to me like mother-of-pearl, what is there to say of the three goals — *dharma*, *artha* and *kama*? And if even in Maharaurava not a shadow of fear approaches me, where then is fear for wife, sons and the like?
From the word-for-word gloss: "the residents of Kashi" are those who have settled in Kashi for the sake of undifferentiated liberation; Gaura's devotee does not bustle about the *shraddha* at Gaya, the offering of *pinda* and the like to the ancestors, as the *karmis* seeking fruits do.

### 100
THEME: Description of the supremacy of Gaurahari's form, qualities, sweetness, valour and generosity
A certain indescribable Gaurachandra — mighty as an intoxicated young lion in subduing Kali, more tender than a beautiful, freshly blossomed golden *kandali* bud, striving to flood the world by making the ocean of love surge — shines, surpassing all.
