# Verses 29–56: Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's anvaya (word-for-word gloss), translated from the Bengali. Numbering of the 1951 edition.

### 29
ANVAYA: nigama-cakra-cuda-cayam — Him whom the Upanishads seek ("*nigama-cakra*" — the collection of the *shrutis*, their "*cuda*" — crowns, and "*caya*" — that which is sought by them; that is, Him whom the Upanishads, the head-ornament of the Vedas, seek — Him); nijam pada-ambujam ajanatam — who do not know His own — Gaurahari's — lotus feet; maha-purusha-maninam — who think themselves great personalities; sura-muni-ishvaranam — of the best of the gods and the best of the sages; kimapi garva-nirvasanam — a certain destroyer of pride; shaci-sutam — the son of Shachi; [mat-vidhe jane api — even for a person such as me]; nayana-gocaram — visible to the eye; acikarat — has made; aho iha — ah, in this world; [idrik — such]; bhuri-bhagya-udayah — greatly fortunate; kah — who?

### 30
ANVAYA: sarva-sadhana-hinah api — even devoid of all kinds of *sadhana*; yah — he who; parama-ashcarya-vaibhave gaurange — in Gaurasundara, who possesses supremely wonderful glory; nyasta-bhavah — has placed his *citta-vritti* (the movements of the mind); sah — he; sarva-artha-purnah — (*sarva* — […]; *artha* — *prema*, that is, the highest goal of man, *prema* for Krishna; having obtained it, *purna* — full, that is, one whose desires are all fulfilled); eva [bhavati] — certainly becomes.

### 31
ANVAYA: aganya-maha-punyam — one who has countless great merits — such a person; hareh ananya-sharanam api — even one exclusively surrendered to Hari; anupasita-caitanyam [janam] — the person who has not worshipped Krishna-Chaitanya-chandra; matih — my mind; adhanyam — (*dhana* is the fifth goal of man, *prema*; one worthy of that wealth is "*dhanya*," one who is not "*dhanya*" is "*adhanya*"; that is, no one except the worshippers of Sri Chaitanya-chandra is worthy of obtaining the wealth of *prema*); manyate — considers.

### 32
ANVAYA: kriya-asaktan — (*kriya* — the regular and occasional rites, *sandhya-vandana* and so on) those attached to them, that is, always eager for them, and therefore; jada-matin — dull-witted (*jada* — indifferent to the true search for the highest goal but absorbed in seeking a particle of transient, worldly, illusory happiness — such people); dhik — shame (an indeclinable word expressing contempt); vikata-tapasah — the harsh ascetics (those who in summer endure the heat of the sun, in the rainy season the incessant downpours, in the cold seasons stand in water, grow their nails, beards and hair or hang head downwards, go without food and sleep, performing very laborious austerities, *japa*, meditation and so on — them); dhik — shame; yaminah ca — and the self-restrained (those who try to subdue the senses or the mind by *yama*, *niyama* and the other steps of the eightfold yoga — them too); dhik — shame; brahma aham [iti shabda-uccarana-matrena eva — merely by uttering the words "I am Brahman"]; vadana-pariphullan — those whose faces blossom (who, merely uttering the words "I am Brahman," pride themselves on being "liberated," and whose faces are therefore very joyful — them); dhik astu — shame; vishaya-rasa-mattan — intoxicated with the *rasa* of sense objects (intoxicated with the pride of enjoying sense objects unconnected with Bhagavan: the false renunciants (*phalgu-tyagis*) seeking the impersonal Brahman are intoxicated with the evil *rasa* of aversion to Bhagavan — the thought "I am liberated," while the pleasure-seekers attached to rites are busy enjoying the fruits of this world and the next; so, these); nara-pashun — over these human animals (animals in human form: as village animals eat and amuse themselves, so these, having no connection with Gaura, eat and amuse themselves in every way like them — over them); kim shocamah — why should we grieve?; ahaha — alas!; [etesham — of them]; keshancit — no one; gaura-madhunah — of the honey of Gaura's lotus feet; leshah api — even a drop; na militah — has received.

### 33
ANVAYA: [he] budhah — O learned ones; pashanah — a stone; amrita-rasaih — with streams of nectar; parishecitah [api] — (*pari* — on all sides, abundantly; *secita* — watered) even if watered; [tasya — of it]; ankurah na eva sambhavet — the appearance of a sprout is never possible; langulam vivrinatah — of one stretching out its tail; sarama-pateh — of a dog; asya [langulasya] — of that stretched-out tail; arjavam — straightness; na eva [bhavati] — never comes about; hastau unnayata [janena] — by a person raising his hands; vidhoh mandalam — the disc of the moon; katham dharyam — how can it be held? (that is, merely by raising his hands a person cannot touch the disc of the moon); sarvam sadhanam astu — let there be all the means of practice, but even then; gaura-karuna-abhave — in the absence of Gaurasundara's mercy, that is, without mercy; na bhava-utsavah — the festival [of *prema*] (the OCR is unclear) is never possible.

### 34
ANVAYA: vistirne prema-sagare — in the wide ocean of *prema*; gaura-candre avatirne [sati] — when Gaurachandra has risen; [tena — by Him]; su-prakashita-ratna-oghe — when the multitude of jewels is beautifully illumined (*su* — beautifully, *prakashita* — illumined, *ratna* — jewels, *ogha* — multitude; that is, when the bright radiance of Sri Gaurachandra has beautifully illumined the jewels of the treasury of *prema* — the nine kinds of *bhakti*, or the *bhavas* nourished by the *vibhavas*, *anubhavas*, *sattvika-* and *vyabhichari-bhavas* and the other ingredients, or the necklace of the jewel-names "Hare Krishna" and the rest); yah dinah — whoever remains poor; sah dinah eva — he is truly poor (that is, whoever, leaving the jewels of the ocean of *prema* illumined by the rays of Gaurachandra, wishes to seek rust and slag elsewhere, is never rid of poverty: not having taken shelter of the lotus feet of the Gaura-moon, he will not be freed from poverty, forever labouring for what is not wealth and remaining deprived of the wealth of *prema*).

### 35
ANVAYA: vistirne prema-sagare — in the wide ocean-treasury of *prema*, or in the ocean of the *rasa* of *prema*; gaura-candre avatirne [sati] — when the Gaura-moon has risen; ye [janah tatra] — those people who into that ocean of *rasa* or that treasury; na majjanti — do not plunge; te — they; maha-anartha-sagare — in the ocean of great *anarthas* (when the ocean is explained as a treasury of jewels: *artha* is Krishna, or the highest goal, *prema*, and *anartha* is its opposite: everything that is not Krishna, or the four goals of man; when it is explained as an ocean of *rasa* — in an ocean of donkey's urine, that is, of the *anarthas*: the desire for prestige (*pratishtha-asha*), crookedness (*kutinati*) and the like); majjanti — drown.

### 36
ANVAYA: prasarita-maha-prema-piyusha-rasa-sagare — in the ocean in which (*prasarita* — very wide, *mahan* — excellent) *prema* is *piyusha*, that is, the nectar *rasa* — in it; caitanya-candre prakate [sati] — when the Chaitanya-moon has risen; yah dinah sah dinah eva — whoever is poor is truly poor (whoever has not taken shelter of Gaura's feet is more foolish than the foolish).

### 37
ANVAYA: sarva-shastra-jnah [janah] api — even people learned in all the *shastras*; yadi caitanyam — if Sri Chaitanyadeva; ishvaram na viduh — they do not know as Bhagavan Himself; te — they; acaitanyam idam vishvam — in this unconscious material world; bhramyanti — wander (that is, subject to the fruits of *karma*, they merely travel through heaven, hell and the other worlds, and their comings and goings never cease).

### 38
ANVAYA: sviya-nama-avalinam — of His own names, "Hare Krishna" and the rest; madhurima-bharam — (*madhurima* — sweetness, *bhara* — fullness or excess; that is) the utmost sweetness; svadam svadam — tasting again and again; [tatah — from that]; madam madam — becoming intoxicated again and again; kimapi — in an indescribable way; vivashibhuta-visrasta-gatrah [san] — losing control, and then with His body grown limp; vrajapati-gunan — the qualities of the lord of Vrindavana; varam varam — again and again; gaya gaya — "sing," "sing"; iti — thus; jalpan — saying (that is, He says); gaurah — Gaurasundara; yaih — by those people; sakrit api na drishtah — has not been seen even once, that is, not realized in experience; teshu — in those people; bhaktih kutah [bhavati] — whence *bhakti*? (the possibility of *bhakti* is negligible).

### 39
ANVAYA: yadi hari-rasamaya-ashcarya-vibhave — possessing supremely wondrous wealth, which is the *rasa* of *bhakti* for Hari Himself; shri-caitanye — in Sri Gaurasundara; abhaktanam api — even of those devoid of *bhakti*; para-prema-rabhasah — ("*para*" — the Supreme, Parameshvara; "*prema-rabhasa*" — the bliss of *prema*) the bliss of love for the Supreme; kathamapi — in some way; bhavi — will arise; [tada — then]; bijam vina kim na ankura-jananam [bhavet] — why does a sprout not appear without a seed?; andhah api katham na prapashyet — why does even the blind man not see?; panguh katham na giri-shikharam arohati — why does the lame man not climb the summit of Mount Sumeru? (that is, for those who have not taken shelter of Gaura's lotus feet, to taste the *rasa* of *prema* is utterly impossible).

### 40
ANVAYA: eshu shri-govinda-anucara-saciveshu kritishu — among those pious souls who, as associates, assist the pastimes of Sri Govinda; yah na [ganitah — one who is not counted; tasmai api — even to him]; alaukikya — in an unearthly way; prema-unmada-rasa-vilasa-prathanaya — by spreading the pastimes of *rasa* born of love — joy, pride and the rest; maha-ashcarya-prema-utsavam — the supremely wondrous bliss of *prema*; hathat datari sakshat gaure — towards Gaurasundara Himself, who suddenly bestows; yat-matih na [bhavet] — whose mind does not turn; iha — in this world; sah nara-pashuh — that human animal; parah mudhah — is the greatest fool.

### 41
ANVAYA: shruti-adau — in the Vedas and the rest (in the Vedas, […] the natural commentary on the Vedanta, the *Srimad-Bhagavatam*, […] *shastras*); asankhyah — countless; avatarah — *avataras*; nigaditah — are named; [gaura-harau … — in Gaurahari …] (such wondrous power as is seen in Gaurahari, […]); prabhavam — such power; parama-ishat itaratah — in anyone other than Parameshvara; kah sambhavayatu — who can suppose?; anyat kim [vaktavyam] — what more is to be said?; sva-preshthe — in their beloved Gaurasundara; satam — of the saintly devotees; kati kati anubhavah api na [drishtah] — how many, how many experiences were there not? (the deliverance of Jagai and Madhai, […], the six-armed form shown to Sarvabhauma, […] — did they not realize Him as the Supreme?); hari hari — alas! (in grief); tathapi shri-gaure — still, in Gaurasundara; mudhah hari-dhiyah na — the fools have no understanding that He is Hari (the Supreme).

### 42
ANVAYA: yasya lila-katakshah — the playful glance, full of *bhavas*, of that Gaurasundara; sakala-tanu-bhritam — of all embodied beings; sakshat moksha-adika-arthan — the direct goals of man, liberation and the rest; vividha-vikritibhih — by a multitude of transformations (*vikaras*); tucchatam darshayantam prema-anandam — the bliss of *prema*, which shows their insignificance, that is, their utter worthlessness (that is, the fifth goal, *prema-ananda*, "which makes liberation insignificant"); prasute — gives birth to (manifests); vedeshu gudhah asau gauracandrah — this Gaurachandra, hidden in the Vedas (hidden because He is the hidden *avatara*; that is, although the *shrutis* point to Gaurasundara with the *mantras* "*mahan prabhur vai purushah*" (*Shvetashvatara Upanishad* 3.12), "*rukma-varnam kartaram isham purusham brahma-yonim*" (*Mundaka Upanishad* 3.1.3) and others, for those who lack the realization of the wise this has not become an object of realization); jagati yadi ishvarah na bhavet — if He is not the one Lord in the world; tat anisha-vadah praptah — then atheism (the doctrine of godlessness) has come; shiva shiva — Shiva, Shiva!; [he] gahane vishnu-maye — O Vishnu-maya of inscrutable power; te — to you; namah — obeisance (that is, deluded by Vishnu-maya of inscrutable power, some cannot realize Gaurasundara as "the Supreme").

### 43
ANVAYA: ujjvalam kulam dhik — shame on a noble lineage (shame on birth in a righteous family full of pious conduct); vagmitam api dhik — shame also on eloquence (wordiness); yashah [ca] dhik astu — shame on fame, or honour; adhyayanam dhik — shame on the study of the *shrutis* and the rest; akritim navam vayah shriyam [ca] dhik astu — shame on a beautiful build of hands, feet and other limbs, on full youth (*purna-kaishora*), on inexhaustible wealth; dvijatvam api dhik — shame also on receiving instruction in the Gayatri and so on after accepting the prescribed sacraments, *upanayana* and the rest; param vimalam ashrama-adyam ca dhik — shame also on the highest, pure *brahmacarya* and the other *ashramas* (the word "*adi*" also indicates [sacrifices], the practice of yoga, renunciation and the like — shame on them too); cet — if; kalau — in the age of Kali; prakata-gaura-gopi-patih — the manifest Gaura, the beloved of the *gopis* (Krishna, the beloved of the *gopis*, the embodiment of sweetness (*madhurya*), who, accepting the mood and lustre of "*gaura*," that is, of the golden Sri Radha, has appeared as the embodiment of the *rasa* of *vipralambha*); na paricitah [bhavet] — is not worshipped (does not become the object of worship).

### 44
ANVAYA: aho — oh!; maha-ashcarya-prema-ujjvala-rasa-sada-avesha-vivashikrita-angah — those whose limbs are overpowered by constant absorption in the supremely wondrous radiant *rasa* of *prema*; yat-anucara-vakreshvara-mukhah — the associates of that Gaurasundara headed by Vakreshvara, connoisseurs of *madhura-rasa*, that is, Vakreshvara, Gadadhara, Raya Ramananda and others; vaikuntha-sthaih bhagavat-parshada-varaih api ca — even by the best associates of Narayana, who dwell in Vaikuntha, the abode of majesty (*aishvarya*); sa-romancam drishtah [abhavan] — were seen with gooseflesh (that is, seeing them, even the associates of Narayana in Vaikuntha cannot help getting gooseflesh from [rapture]); akrita-punyah — one devoid of piety; katham — how; tam gauram — that Gaurasundara; pranayatu — will be able to make the object of his love?

### 45
ANVAYA: yah — He who; kam api — to someone; prasadam — grace (favour); [dattva — having given]; smita-shri-mukham — shines with a softly smiling beautiful face; atha — then; sambhashya — having spoken; durat snigdha-drisha nirikshya ca — and having looked from afar with an affectionate glance; maha-prema-utsavam — the highest bliss of *prema*; yacchati — bestows; hanta — alas!; yesham — those who; kutarka-karkasha-dhiya — because of a mind hardened by false arguments; tatra sakshat-purna-rasa-avatarini harau api — even towards that Sri Gaurahari, whose nature is to bring down before our eyes, that is, to manifest, the complete *rasa* of *prema*; na atyadarah — have no great reverence; ami — they; kevalam dushtah — are simply wicked (have the nature of *asuras*).

### 46
ANVAYA: vancitah asmi vancitah asmi vancitah asmi — I am cheated, cheated, certainly cheated; [yatah — because]; vishvam — the whole world; gaura-rase magnam — has plunged into the elevated radiant *rasa*, never given before (*anarpita-cari*), bestowed by Sri Gaurasundara, the embodiment of *vipralambha*; [kintu — but]; mama — of me; [tatra — with it]; sparshah api — even a touch; na abhavat — there was not.

### 47
ANVAYA: gaura-pada-aravinda-rajasa — by the pollen of Sri Gaurasundara's lotus feet; mahi-mandale sprishte [sati] — when the earth was touched; iha — in this world; kaih va — by whom; akrita-ayasaih — without effort; sarva-puma-artha-maulih — the crown of all the goals of man, *prema*; asaditah na asit — was not obtained? (that is, everyone obtained it); ha ha — alas, alas (in grief); mama — my; jivanam dhik — shame on the life; me — my; vidyam api dhik — shame also on the learning (knowledge of the *shastras* and the like); ashramam api dhik — shame also on the *ashrama*, *tridanda-sannyasa*, the fourth *ashrama*; yat — because; daurbhagya-bharat — through extreme misfortune; aho — oh!; mama — of me; tat-sambandha-gandhah api — even the scent of a connection with that [*prema*]; na abhut — there was not.

### 48
ANVAYA: gauracandra-karuna-maha-arnavah — the great ocean of the Gaura-moon's mercy (the OCR is partly lost); jagat purayan eva — completely flooding the world; utsarpa — overflowed its shores (surged up); [kintu — but]; maha-durbhage mayi — on me, extremely unfortunate; bindu-matram api — even a drop of that mercy; na apatat — did not fall; etat kim adbhutam — what a wonder is this? (that is, everyone in the world is flooded with Gaurasundara's mercy; only I, through my extreme misfortune, could not receive even a drop).

### 49
ANVAYA: kalah kalih — the time is Kali (the promoter of *adharma*); indriya-vairi-vargah — the hosts of the enemies, the senses; balinah — are very strong; iha — in this age of Kali; shri-bhakti-margah — the supreme radiant path of *bhakti*; kantaka-koti-ruddhah — is blocked by countless thorns in the form of *karma*, *jnana* and the like; [atah — therefore]; [he] caitanya-candra — O Chaitanya-moon; yadi adya kripam — if today mercy; na karoshi — You do not show; [tada — then]; ha ha — alas, alas; vikalah — bewildered (overwhelmed, that is, afflicted by all these faults of Kali); aham — I; kim karomi — what am I to do?; kva — where; yami — am I to go?

### 50
ANVAYA: sah api ashcaryamayah prabhuh — even that Lord Gaurahari, full of supreme wonders; yat — that through misfortune; nayanayoh — to the eyes; gocarah na abhavat — did not become visible; yat — or that for some reason; hareh pada-ambuja-rasah — the *rasa* of Hari's lotus feet; na asvadi — was not tasted; tat gatam tat gatam [eva] — what has been has passed; [kintu — but]; mama — for me; etavat [eva] — only this; [astu — let there be]; shri-caitanya-pade nikhata-manasah — those who have fixed their minds on Sri Chaitanya's lotus feet; ye anye api — others who; jagatim — the earth; alankurvate — adorn; taih — with them; yat prasanga-utsavah — elevated association (*pra* — excellently, *sanga* — association) and its bliss; tavat astu — let there be.

### 51
ANVAYA: dushkarma-koti-niratasya — of one given to millions of evil deeds; gadham — firmly; duranta-ghora-durvasana-nigada-shrinkhalitasya — bound by the chains of untamable, terrible evil tendencies; klishyat-mateh — with a mind exhausted [by labours for] *karma*, *jnana* and the like; kumati-koti-kadarthitasya — led and overpowered by countless people of evil mind, obstinate in *karma*; mama — to me; gauram vina — except Gaurahari; adya — today; iha — in this world; kah bandhuh bhavita — who will become a friend?

### 52
ANVAYA: ha hanta hanta — alas! alas! alas!; mama — of my; parama-ushara-citta-bhumau — in the supremely barren soil of the heart; sadhana-kotayah api — even millions of practices; vyarthah bhavanti — are lost in vain; tat — therefore; aham — I; sarvatmana — with all my heart; adbhuta-bhakti-bijam — which are the seed of unearthly (*aprakrita*) *bhakti*; shri-gauracandra-caranam — the lotus feet of Sri Gaurachandra; sharanam karomi — take as my shelter.

### 53
ANVAYA: ha hanta — alas!; me — of my; parama-usharayam citta-bhuvi — in the extremely barren soil of the heart; katham — how; sat-bhakti-kalpa-latika-ankurita bhavet — will the sprout of *bhakti* in the form of *prema* — the *sthayi-bhava*, or *rati* — appear?; [kintu — but]; hridi — in the heart; ekam eva — the only; param ashvasaniyam — chief consolation; asti — there is; [yat — that]; caitanya-nama — the name of Chaitanya; kalayan — repeating; kadapi — ever; na shocyah — one is not an object of grief.

### 54
ANVAYA: [he] caitanya-candra — O Chaitanya-moon!; samsara-duhkha-jaladhau patitasya — to one fallen into the ocean of the miseries of *samsara*; kama-krodha-adi-nakra-makaraih kavalikritasya — seized by the crocodiles — lust and anger — and by the *makaras* and the rest; durvasana-nigaditasya — bound by the chains of evil tendencies; nirashrayasya — without support; mama — to me; pada-avalambanam — the shelter of the feet; dehi — give.

### 55
ANVAYA: [he] durbodha-vaibhava-pate caitanya-candra — O Chaitanya-moon, master of inconceivable power (*shakti*); pamare api mayi — even on me, a fallen one; yadi te — if Your; karuna-katakshah — merciful glance; [syat — falls]; [tada — then]; shiva-shuka-uddhava-narada-adyaih api — even by the great souls Shiva, Shuka, Uddhava, Narada and others; mrigya — sought; sa ashcarya-bhakti-padavi — that wondrous path of *bhakti*; nah — from us; na daviyasi — will not be far.

### 56
ANVAYA: [he] shaure — O Krishna of the Shura dynasty; tava gaure atmani — in Your golden form; yadrik — such as; nirankusha-kripa — causeless mercy; sa kva — where else is such?; tat adbhutam vaibhavam kva — where else is such supremely wondrous glory?; sa vatsalata [ca] kva — where else is such *vatsalya* (love for the devotees)?
