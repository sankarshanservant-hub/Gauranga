### 31
THEME: Even an exclusive devotee of Hari, if he has no *bhakti* for Sri Chaitanya, is unfortunate (*a-dhanya*)
Let someone have countless great merits, let him be exclusively surrendered to Hari — but if he has not worshipped Sri Chaitanya-chandra, my mind considers him "*a-dhanya*," that is, unworthy of obtaining the highest goal of man.

From the word-for-word gloss: "*dhana*" (wealth) is *prema*, the fifth goal of man; "*dhanya*" is one who is worthy of that wealth, and one who is not "*dhanya*" is "*a-dhanya*": in other words, without worshipping Sri Chaitanya-chandra no one is worthy of obtaining the wealth of *prema*.

### 32
THEME: Censure of the futile endeavours of the knowers (*jnanis*), the followers of rituals (*karmis*), the ascetics and the yogis
Shame on the *smartas* attached to the regular and occasional rites; shame on the harsh ascetics, shame on the yogis of the eightfold yoga; shame also on the *ahamgraha-upasakas* (who worship themselves as God), who, as soon as they utter the words "*aham brahmasmi*" ("I am Brahman"), blossom with the conceit of the liberated. All of them are intoxicated with the enjoyment of sense objects unconnected with the Lord. Why should one grieve over these two-legged animals? Alas, alas! Not one of them has received even a drop of the nectar of Gaura's lotus feet.

From the word-for-word gloss: "*kriya*" are the regular and occasional rites such as *sandhya-vandana*, and those attached to them are "dull-witted" — indifferent to the true search for the highest goal, but busy seeking a particle of transient, worldly, illusory happiness; "harsh ascetics" are those who in summer endure the heat of the sun, in the rainy season the incessant downpours, in the cold seasons stand in water, grow their nails, beards and hair or hang head downwards, go without food and sleep, performing severe austerities, *japa*, meditation and so on; "those who restrain themselves" (*yamins*) are those who try to subdue the senses and the mind by means of *yama*, *niyama* and the other steps of the eightfold yoga; "intoxicated with the taste of sense objects" — on the one hand, the false renunciants (*phalgu-tyagis*) seeking the impersonal Brahman, intoxicated with the evil *rasa* of aversion to the Lord — the thought "I am liberated"; on the other, the pleasure-seeking followers of rituals, busy with the fruits of this world and the next; "two-legged animals" — like village animals, having no connection with Gaura, they only eat and amuse themselves.

### 33
THEME: Without Gaura's mercy, love (*prema*) is unattainable even for one who possesses all the means of practice
O wise ones! As on a stone, however much one waters it with nectar, a sprout will never appear; as a dog's tail, however much one straightens it, will never become straight; as, raising both hands, no one can touch the disc of the moon — so, alas, even one who possesses all the means of practice will not become worthy of obtaining the bliss of *prema* without the mercy of Sri Gaurasundara.

### 34
THEME: Truly poor is only he who has not taken shelter of Gaura's lotus feet
Over the wide ocean, the treasury of *prema*, the Gaura-moon has risen and revealed heaps of the jewels of the nine kinds of *bhakti* — hearing, chanting and the rest. Whoever has remained poor even with such an opportunity is, without doubt, extremely unfortunate.

From the word-for-word gloss: the jewels of this ocean, brightly lit by the radiance of Sri Gaurachandra, are the nine kinds of *bhakti*, or the *bhavas* nourished by the *vibhavas*, *anubhavas*, *sattvika-* and *vyabhichari-bhavas* and so on, or the necklace of the jewels of the name "Hare Krishna" and the rest; whoever, leaving the jewels of the ocean of *prema* lit by the rays of Gaurachandra, wishes to look elsewhere for rust and slag, will never be rid of poverty: not having taken shelter of the lotus feet of the Gaura-moon, he forever labours for what is not wealth and remains deprived of the wealth of *prema*.

### 35
THEME: Whoever has not plunged into the ocean of *prema* revealed by Gaurachandra drowns in the ocean of great evils (*anarthas*)
Though the Gaura-moon has risen over the wide ocean, the treasury of *prema*, those who have not dived into that ocean of *prema* have remained drowning in the ocean of great evils (*anarthas*)!

From the word-for-word gloss: if the "ocean" is understood as a treasury of jewels, then "*artha*" is Krishna, or the highest goal, *prema*, and "*anartha*" is its opposite: everything that is not Krishna, or the four goals of man (*catur-varga*); but if it is understood as an ocean of *rasa*, then the "*anarthas*" are the desire for prestige (*pratishtha-asha*), crookedness (*kutinati*) and the like, and that is an ocean of donkey's urine.

### 36
THEME: Whoever has turned away from the honey of Gaura's lotus feet is extremely foolish
When the Chaitanya-moon has risen and poured forth an ocean of the nectar *rasa* of great love, one who has remained "poor," that is, deprived of the chance to drink that nectar of *prema*, is truly "poor," that is, extremely unfortunate.

From the word-for-word gloss: "poor" here is one who has not taken shelter of Gaura's feet; he is more foolish than the most foolish.

### 37
THEME: Whoever doubts that Gaurasundara is Bhagavan Himself, even if he knows all the *shastras*, is only a conditioned soul wandering in the material world
If even the knowers of all the *shastras* do not know that Sri Chaitanya-chandra is "Bhagavan Himself," they merely come and go in this unconscious (*a-cit*) world.

From the word-for-word gloss: subject to the fruits of their deeds, they merely wander through heaven, hell and the other worlds, and their comings and goings never cease.

### 38
THEME: One who does not know the glory of Gaura's lotus feet cannot obtain the secret of *rasa*
He, again and again tasting the sweetness of His own names — "Hare Krishna" and the rest — becomes more and more intoxicated by them, indescribably loses control of Himself, and His body grows limp; He calls out to everyone and says again and again: "Chant, chant the qualities of the lord of Vrindavana!" For those who have never once seen such a Sri Gaurasundara, the possibility of obtaining *bhakti* is infinitely remote.

From the word-for-word gloss: "has not seen" means has not realized Him in experience.

### 39
THEME: As without a seed no sprout appears, so without *bhakti* for Gaura it is impossible to obtain the secret love
If those devoid of *bhakti* could, even with the greatest effort, ever awaken the bliss of love for the Supreme — for Sri Chaitanya-chandra, whose supremely wondrous wealth is the *rasa* of *bhakti* for Hari — then why should a sprout not appear without a seed? Why should the blind not see? And why should the lame not climb the summit of Mount Sumeru?

From the word-for-word gloss: "*para*" (in the word "*para-prema*") is the Supreme (Parameshvara); in other words, for those who have not taken shelter of Gaura's lotus feet, to taste the *rasa* of *prema* is utterly impossible.

### 40
THEME: A two-legged animal is he who has not turned his mind to Gaurahari, who gives unearthly love even to those who cling to the insentient
Even one who is not counted among the pious souls who, as associates, assist in the pastimes of Sri Radha and Govinda, He suddenly endows with the wondrous bliss of *prema*, spreading in an unearthly way the pastimes of *rasa* born of love — joy, pride and the rest. Whoever has not turned his mind to this Sri Gaurasundara, manifest before our eyes, is a two-legged animal and the greatest fool in this world.

### 41
THEME: Deprived are those who doubt the divinity of Gaurahari, the crown of all *avataras*
In the Vedas and the other *shastras* countless *avataras* are glorified, but who except Bhagavan Himself can display such wondrous power as is seen in Gaurahari? What more is there to say: did not the devotees see in their beloved Gaurahari six arms and many other wonders? Alas, alas, even seeing and hearing all this, the fools do not recognize the Supreme in Gaurahari.

From the word-for-word gloss: "the *shrutis* and the rest" — the Vedas, the *Srimad-Bhagavatam* (the natural commentary on the Vedanta) and the other *shastras*; among the experiences of the saints are mentioned the deliverance of Jagai and Madhai, […], the six-armed form shown to Sarvabhauma, and so on […].

### 42
THEME: Those who do not believe in Gaurahari, the giver of *bhakti* beside which liberation is insignificant, are atheists bewildered by *maya*
Gaurasundara's playful glance, full of *bhavas*, gives rise in all embodied beings to the bliss of *prema*, which by a multitude of transformations (*vikaras*) shows how insignificant are the four goals of man headed by liberation. If this Sri Gaurachandra, hidden in the Vedas, is not the Lord of the world, that is, if the living beings of the world do not accept that He is "God Himself," then the world is covered by atheism! Shiva, Shiva! O inscrutable Vishnu-maya, how inscrutable is your power! Obeisance to you.

From the word-for-word gloss: this *prema* is the fifth goal of man, "which makes liberation insignificant" ("*moksha-laghuta-krit*"); Gaurachandra is "hidden in the Vedas" because He is the hidden *avatara* (*channa-avatara*): the *shrutis* point to Gaurasundara with *mantras* such as "*mahan prabhur vai purushah*" (*Shvetashvatara Upanishad* 3.12) and "*rukma-varnam kartaram isham purusham brahma-yonim*" (*Mundaka Upanishad* 3.1.3), but for those who lack the realization of the wise this has remained unperceived; deluded by Vishnu-maya, whose power is inscrutable, some cannot realize that Gaurasundara is the Supreme.

### 43
THEME: Shame on the noble lineage, eloquence, learning, wealth and brahminhood of one who has turned away from Gaura
If Sri Gauranga is not worshipped — Krishna, the beloved of the *gopis*, the embodiment of the *rasa* of sweetness (*madhurya*), who appeared in the age of Kali with the feelings and lustre of the golden Sri Radha as the embodiment of the *rasa* of separation (*vipralambha*) — then shame on a righteous noble lineage, shame on eloquence, shame on fame, shame on the study of the Vedas and the other *shastras*, shame on beauty, youth and wealth, shame on brahminhood adorned with the prescribed sacraments such as *upanayana*, shame on the pure *ashramas* such as *brahmacarya*, and also on yoga, sacrifices, renunciation and the rest.

### 44
THEME: Gaurahari, surrounded by associates whom even the associates of Narayana admire, is not an object of love for one devoid of piety
Seeing the great souls headed by Vakreshvara, whose bodies are overpowered by constant absorption in the wondrous radiant *rasa* of *prema*, even the associates of Narayana in Vaikuntha get gooseflesh. How can one devoid of piety make Sri Gaurasundara, whose servants are these great souls, connoisseurs of *madhura-rasa*, the object of his love?

From the word-for-word gloss: "the servants headed by Vakreshvara" are connoisseurs of *madhura-rasa*: Vakreshvara, Gadadhara, Raya Ramananda and others; Vaikuntha is the abode of majesty (*aishvarya*).

### 45
THEME: Whoever disregards Sri Gaurasundara, who gives love even to those who do not ask, is an *asura*
He, having shown His grace to someone, shines with a softly smiling beautiful face, then speaks to him, looks at him from afar with an affectionate glance and bestows the highest love. Alas, those who, because of a mind hardened by false arguments, do not show the highest reverence even to Him — Sri Gaurahari, who manifests before our eyes the complete *rasa* of *prema* — are nothing but wicked *asuras*.

From the word-for-word gloss: "descending with complete *rasa*" — He whose nature is to manifest the complete *rasa* of *prema*; "wicked" — having the nature of *asuras*.

### 46
THEME: The humility of a connoisseur of the *rasa* of love for Gaura
I am cheated, cheated, truly I am cheated! The whole world has plunged into the ocean of the *rasa* of love for Sri Gaura, but me, alas, it has not even touched.

From the word-for-word gloss: "the *rasa* of Gaura" is the elevated radiant *rasa* (*unnata-ujjvala-rasa*), never given before (*anarpita-cari*), bestowed by Sri Gaurasundara, the embodiment of separation (*vipralambha*).

### 47
THEME: Shame on the learning, the *ashrama* and the life of one who, at the time of Gaura's descent, remained deprived even of the scent of the *rasa* of love
When the earth was touched by the pollen of Sri Gaura's lotus feet, who did not obtain "without practice" (without effort) *prema*, the crown of all the goals of man? But alas, alas, through my extreme misfortune I could not obtain even a particle of that *prema*! Shame on my life, shame on my learning, shame on my fourth *ashrama* — *tridanda-sannyasa*!

### 48
THEME: Extremely unfortunate is one deprived of Gaura's merciful glance
The great ocean of Sri Gaurachandra's mercy overflowed its shores and flooded the whole world, but, alas, what a wonder — not a single drop of it fell on an unfortunate one such as me!

### 49
THEME: In the age of Kali, without Gaura's mercy it is impossible to enter the path of *bhakti*, blocked by millions of thorns
The time is Kali; the enemies, the senses, are extremely strong, and the supreme radiant path of *bhakti* is blocked by a net of millions of thorns. Therefore, O Chaitanya-chandra, if You do not show mercy today, what, alas, am I to do, bewildered as I am (that is, having lost my wits in searching for pure *bhakti*, for in the age of Kali the path of *bhakti* is covered by many mental speculations); where am I to go?

From the word-for-word gloss: Kali is he who promotes irreligion (*adharma*); the millions of thorns are *karma*, *jnana* and the like.

### 50
THEME: Only association with Gaura's people, the ornaments of the world, is desirable
Though through my misfortune that Lord Gaurahari, full of supreme wonders, never came before my eyes (that is, even seeing Him, I could not see His true nature for lack of the realization of the wise), though for some reason it did not fall to my lot to taste love for Hari's lotus feet — what has been has been. Now I have only one prayer: may I have the festival of elevated association with those other devotees who, having fixed their minds on Sri Chaitanya's lotus feet, still adorn the earth.

### 51
THEME: In the age of Kali the only saviour of the fallen is Gaurahari
I am wholly given to millions of evil deeds, firmly bound by the chains of untamable, terrible evil tendencies, my mind is exhausted by labours for *karma*, *jnana* and the like, millions of evil thoughts drag me along a false path and overpower me. Who but Gaurahari will today in this world become the friend of one as wretched as I?

### 52
THEME: Without the shelter of Gaura's feet all means of practice are fruitless
Alas, alas! In the extremely hard, barren soil of my heart millions of seeds of practice are lost in vain! Therefore I now with all my heart take shelter of the lotus feet of Sri Gaura — the seed of unearthly *bhakti*.

### 53
THEME: One has only to utter the name of Sri Chaitanya, and even in a hard heart the creeper of the desires of *bhakti* sprouts
Alas, alas! How will the sprout of *prema-bhakti*, that is, the *sthayi-bhava* or *rati*, grow in the extremely barren soil of my heart? There is no hope! But here is the one supreme consolation: whoever utters the name of Sri Chaitanya never has anything to grieve over.

### 54
THEME: Sri Chaitanya is the shelter of the shelterless
O Chaitanya-chandra, I have fallen into the ocean of worldly miseries, my hands and feet are bound by the chains of evil tendencies, I have no support; the crocodiles and sea monsters (*makaras*) of lust, anger and the rest have swallowed me. (O Lord! In such a calamity) save me by giving me shelter in the boat of Your feet!

### 55
THEME: The path of *bhakti*, hard to reach even for the gods, is by Gaura's mercy accessible even to the fallen
O Chaitanya-chandra, You are the master of inconceivable wealth! If You cast Your merciful glance even on a fallen one such as me, then even for us that supreme, wondrous, radiant path of *bhakti*, which even the great souls — Shiva, Shuka, Narada, Uddhava and the others — seek, will not be far away.

### 56
THEME: It is impossible to find anyone other than Gaurahari whose mercy is so boundless
O Krishna, lamp of the Shura dynasty! Where else is to be found such causeless mercy as in Your golden form? Where else such wealth, astonishing all the worlds? Where else such love for the devotees?

### 57
THEME: Leaving […], everyone must take shelter of Gaurahari, hidden in the *shrutis*
O foolish heart! Take shelter of the lotus feet of the indescribable Supreme — Sri Gaurasundara, who by His power has filled the whole world with love for the supreme *rasa* of *prema* of the lotus feet of Sri Krishna, His own form (*sva-svarupa*), and who is secretly present in all the *shrutis*, that is, hidden in the Vedas.

From the word-for-word gloss: the "great *rasa*" of Krishna's lotus feet is the *unnata-ujjvala-rasa* (the elevated radiant *rasa*).

### 58
THEME: The secret of *rasa* given by Gaura is more wondrous than the love for Krishna born of practice according to rules (*vaidhi*)
If someone, worshipping Murari by means of *bhakti-yoga* — hearing, chanting and the rest — attains the highest goal of man, let him attain it; that is good. For me, however, the only object of worship is that secret treasure of love which is hidden in the *rasa* of *bhakti* for Sri Hari in golden lustre — the ocean of the nectar of boundless *prema*.

### 59
THEME: The secret love of Radha and Krishna, unattainable even by the exclusive devotees of Krishna, is obtained only in the shelter of Chaitanya's feet
Let someone worship the Lord of the world in the hope of *dharma*, *artha*, *kama* and *moksha* — let him worship; let someone, leaving all other objects of worship, become an exclusive servant of Sri Hari — let him become so. I, however, longing to taste that extremely rare, secret *rasa* of the love of Sri Radha and Krishna, take shelter of the feet of Sri Chaitanya-chandra.

### 60
THEME: Sri Gaurahari steals away the duties of the body, the properties of the mind and the like
My adherence to worldly and Vedic dealings, my bashfulness at loud laughter, loud chanting, dancing and the like at festivals, and all the natural properties of life and body that I had — all this has been stolen by some thief who has assumed the golden form of Gaura, whose power is irresistible.

From the word-for-word gloss: "the natural properties of life, body and the like" are the natural duties necessary for the maintenance of life and body; "festival" includes comic performances (*prahasana*), loud chanting, dramatic performances and celebrations.

### 61
THEME: Sri Gaurahari forcibly binds the mind to His lotus feet
Who is this indescribable, playful Lord with a golden body like the core of a golden plantain tree, who, pouring from the corners of His eyes, moist with compassion, millions of oceans of the nectar of *prema*, full of dense, blissful, radiant *rasa*, has suddenly bound my mind firmly to His lotus feet?

From the word-for-word gloss: "dense" (*sandra*) — condensed; "radiant" (*ujjvala*) — ever renewing itself.

### 62
THEME: The glory of [Navadvipa], the best abode (*pitha*) of *prema-bhakti*
Where by His mercy the playful Bhagavan, golden like molten gold, the embodiment of *shringara-rasa* in the form of *mahabhava*, has appeared of His own accord, where every house is full of the festivals of the goddess of *prema-bhakti* — in that abode, Navadvipa, full of greater sweetness even than Vaikuntha, my mind roams.
