### 1
THEME: Praise of Gaurahari, who, to taste His own sweetness and to distribute *prema*, accepted the mood and lustre of the *ashraya* (Radha) and appeared in Navadvipa.
To give Himself, non-different from the son of the lord of Vraja, a taste of the nectar waves of sweet feelings such as joy and the rest, rising from the ocean of His own most pure *prema*, and to distribute them to others, He appeared in the supreme abode called "Sri Navadvipa," the seat of the ninefold *bhakti*. That person named Sri Krishna Chaitanya — the best of all *avataras*, the embodiment of boundless and supremely wonderful mercy — we praise.
From the word-for-word gloss: "we" means the Gaudiya *sampradaya*.

### 2
THEME: Praise of Gaurahari, who by the causeless shower of His mercy bestows the intoxication of *prema* even on one who has abandoned *dharma*.
One whom *dharma* has never touched, that is, in whom there is not a trace of piety; who is always sunk in great sin; who has never received the merciful glance of the saints and has never stayed in any holy place established by the righteous and inaccessible to sin — even such a great sinner, intoxicated by the taste of the nectar of the loving *rasa* of Radha and Krishna which He bestowed, He makes dance, sing aloud and roll on the ground. That powerful, indescribable person I praise.
From the word-for-word gloss: in the word "Sri-Hari-rasa," "Sri" is Srimati Radhika, the source of all beauty, and "Hari" is Sri Krishna.

### 3
THEME: Praise of Gaurahari, the giver of the secret *prema* unattainable even by the devotees of Krishna.
That which people devoted to ritual action (*karma*) cannot obtain; that which no one can realize by the power of austerity, meditation and the eightfold yoga; that which no one is able to perceive even through renunciation, abandonment of works, knowledge of the truth, the reciting of hymns of praise and the like; more than that, that which is unattainable even by devotees dedicated to the loving service of Sri Govinda (that is, unattainable even by the devotees of the Nimbarka *sampradaya*, who are dedicated to loving service in the *svakiya* relationship and lack skill in understanding *parakiya-rasa*) — that secret *prema*, upon His appearance, revealed itself of its own accord through *nama-kirtana* alone. That Gaurasundara I praise.

### 4
THEME: Praise of Sri Chaitanya, who bestows the highest *prema* merely by being seen, touched and so on.
He who, becoming the object of sight, touch or glorification, or the object of remembrance, obeisance or honour even of those who are far away, alone is able to give the essence of *prema* (the *rasa* of separation, *vipralambha*) — that merciful Lord, Sri Chaitanya, I praise.

### 5
THEME: Praise of Gaurahari, the master of the devotees who possess incomparable wealth and scornfully reject liberation and the like.
For Gaura's devotees, rich with the treasure of Gaurasundara's merciful glance, *kaivalya*, or merging into Ishvara, attainable by yogis, is like hell; Amarapuri (the city of the gods) — the desired or already obtained fruit of those who perform their *dharma* with selfish desires — is as unreal as a flower in the sky; the hosts of untamed senses, like black serpents of death, are like snakes whose poison fangs have been pulled out; the whole world appears as an abode of complete happiness, that is, […]; and the position of Brahma, the lord of the gods and others appears as the position of a worm. That Sri Gaurasundara we praise.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*tridasha*" are the thirty-three principal gods: the twelve Adityas, the eleven Rudras, the eight Vasus and the two Ashvini-kumaras; their "*pur*" is heaven; "*kala*" means "extremely wrathful" (serpent); "the world full of happiness" — that is, everywhere the presence (*bhava*) of Bhagavan is seen; the positions of Brahma, Indra and the others seem as insignificant as that of a worm.

### 6
THEME: Praise of Gaurahari, the master of the devotees who laugh at the good fortune of Brahma and the others and censure the endeavours of the knowers of undifferentiated knowledge and of the yogis.
Gaura's devotees, honoured by all the gods, having drunk to the full the wondrous nectar *rasa* full of supremely radiant *prema* and bliss which flows from His lotus feet, absorbed in the madness of love, look even at Brahma and the others and lament: "Alas, alas! They are deprived of the honey of Gaurasundara's lotus feet!"; they do not greatly honour even great Vaishnavas who lack devotion to Gaura, and they censure the knowers of Brahman and the followers of the eightfold yoga for their [futile endeavours]. That Sri Gaurasundara we praise.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the knowers of Brahman" are the *jnanis* who worship themselves as Brahman (*ahamgraha-upasaka*).

### 7
THEME: Praise of Sri Chaitanya, who revealed the elevated, radiant *bhakti* never given before.
Is the destruction of the race of Rakshasas and the race of Daityas in the *avataras* Rama, Nrisimha and the others such a beneficial and great deed? Is the showing of the path of action of Sankhya, yoga and so on in the *avataras* Kapila and the others so important? How great is the significance of the pastimes of creation, maintenance and destruction of the *guna-avataras*, Brahma and the others? Is the feat of rescuing the earth sunk in the waters of the deluge in the *avatara* Varaha and the others so beneficial? (We do not attach great importance to all this: beside Gaurasundara's gift of *prema* it is all insignificant.) We praise the form of Sri Chaitanya, the best of all *avataras*, who shows the path of the highest *bhakti* to the Lord, radiant with *prema*.

### 8
THEME: Obeisance to Gaurachandra, the moon over the ocean of *prema* and bliss.
To Him whose radiance of face is more beautiful than the splendour of millions upon millions of full moons, who is the moon over the ocean of *prema* and bliss, whose sweet smile on His lotus face is more charming than the moonbeams — to that Chaitanya-chandra we offer obeisance.

### 9
THEME: Obeisance to Chaitanya-chandra, the good of all the good of the world.
Only from exclusive devotion to Your lotus feet is the highest goal of man — *prema* — obtained. You are that Chaitanya-chandra, the good even of the good of the world; to You we bow again and again.
From the word-for-word gloss: the good of the world is the worship of Sri Krishna; a still greater good is the supreme *prema* obtained from Him; therefore He is "the good of the good of the world."

### 10
THEME: Glorification of Gaurahari, who dances wildly to the loud *nama-kirtana*.
Oh! He who in the mood of Radha is immersed in the most exalted secret *rasa* directed towards Krishna; who, absorbed in the dance, throws up His huge arms, like golden pillars, and swings His arms and legs this way and that; whose beautiful body sways in the wondrous *tandava* dance; who with the cries "Hari! Hari!" — indescribable sounds of loving bliss, full of feelings such as joy and pride — destroys everything inauspicious in the whole world — that Sri Chaitanya-chandra with eyes as wide as lotus petals, the crown of all *avataras*, I glorify.

### 11
THEME: The name, form, qualities and pastimes of Gauranga; obeisance to Gauranga, who shines like molten gold, the embodiment of separation (*vipralambha*), the giver of the secret *prema*.
To that Sri Chaitanya-chandra — the form made of the *rasa* of pastimes of bliss, beautiful with a radiance like gold, the giver of the *rasa* of the elevated, radiant *prema* never given before — I bow again and again.

### 12
THEME: Glorification of Gaurahari, who [laughs at] Vaikuntha and enacts the pastime of sannyasa.
Oh! He whose eyes, in streams of tears, are like millions of new rain clouds; whose wealth of *prema* makes even millions of supreme abodes, that is, Vaikunthas, appear insignificant, like objects of ridicule; whose bodily beauty and sweetness seem to pour forth millions upon millions of oceans of nectar; who (so that people, seeing Him in the dress of a sannyasi and bowing to Him, might obtain *bhakti* to Krishna by the fruit of unknown piety, *ajnata-sukriti*) under a pretext accepted the dress of a sannyasi — that Sri Gaurahari I glorify.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the pretended sannyasi" — that is, He enacts the pastime of sannyasa in order to spread the pastimes of an acharya.

### 13
THEME: Gaurasundara — the body in which Radha and Krishna are united.
He whose neck is raised like the neck of a lion; whose cheeks are lit at their edges by the sweetest gentle smile; who (incomprehensibly even to the *bhagavatas* who worship Sri Krishna, the embodiment of the *rasa* of union, *sambhoga*) from the excess of the *rasa* of separation (*vipralambha*) displays wondrous transformations of feeling, such as the form of a tortoise; whose bodily lustre is more charming than the filaments of a full-blown golden lotus — may that Sri Gaurasundara, the body in which Radha and Madhava are united, protect you.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*ujjvala-rasamaya*" — full of *shringara-rasa* (the *rasa* of love).

### 14
THEME: Krishna, imbued with the mood of Radha, who preaches the longing to seek Krishna.
He who, at the sight of a garland of new clouds that awaken the remembrance of Krishna, becomes frenzied; who at the sight of a peacock feather becomes extremely agitated; whose body trembles at the sight of a string of *gunja* berries coiled like a bracelet; who at the sight of a dark youth starts and assumes a wondrously beautiful appearance, and who has thus spread His own *prema* everywhere — may that Gauranga-Hari protect you.

### 15
THEME: Gaurachandra, who appeared from the ocean of Shachi's womb.
He who is the ocean of mercy, who wears a lovely garment of a red colour like [the sun] at evening twilight — that radiant, complete one, made of the nectar of *prema*, illumined by the lustre of the *sattvika-bhavas* and the rest, spotless, charming and endowed with the wondrous sixty-four arts of *rasa*, beginning with *vaidagdhya* (refinement), born from the milk ocean of Shachi's womb, the most beautiful Sri Chaitanya-chandra — may He rise in the sky of your hearts.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*Shriman*" — the body united with Srimati Radhika, who is full of supreme beauty, or simply "full of supreme beauty"; "*Kalanatha*" — the moon, and in another sense, Svayam Bhagavan, the source of the partial *avataras* such as Matsya; "your" (singular) here means "your" (plural).

### 16
THEME: Gaurahari, who loudly chants the *maha-mantra*.
He who, chanting the *maha-mantra* Hare Krishna, which brings blessing to the whole world, and tying knots on His waist-cord to keep count of the names — while His hands tremble [from the excess of *prema*] — who, longing to see Sri Jagannathadeva, non-different from Himself, His face bathed in tears, walks to and fro and thus increases the joy of people's eyes — may that Gauranga, Sri Hari, protect you.

### 17
THEME: Gaurachandra, who destroys the inner darkness, makes the ocean of His *prema* swell and refreshes the universe.
That moonlight of the body of Sri Chaitanya-chandra which suddenly uproots the masses of the darkness of ignorance in the hearts of all living beings; which with mighty force incessantly makes the ocean of the *rasa* of *prema* and bliss swell and increase; which, sprinkling the nectar of *prema*, deeply refreshes the world of living beings, incessantly tormented by the three kinds of misery — may it ever shine in your hearts.

### 18
THEME: Gaura's devotees, who play in the *rasa* of the *prema* never given before.
On that path of the *bhakti* of sweet *rasa* on which in ancient times even the best of sages and *rishis* went astray, into which no one's intelligence in the whole circle of the earth could penetrate, of which even Shukadeva did not know — more than that, which the all-merciful Sri Krishna Himself did not reveal even to His own devotees — on that elevated, radiant path of *bhakti* the dear devotees of Sri Gaura now play in supreme bliss.

### 19
THEME: As long as there has been no association with a devotee of Gaura, the soul retains a taste for Brahman, liberation and speculative argument.
As long as an intimate associate of Sri Chaitanya — a bee drinking the nectar of His lotus feet — has not come within one's sight, only so long do discussions of the impersonal Brahman and the paths of liberation, such as merging into Ishvara, not taste bitter; only so long do worldly and Vedic norms not fall into disorder, that is, "the mind fixed in the world and in the Vedas" is not given up; only so long are disputes and quarrels about the various paths turned away from the Lord inevitable, that is, […].
From the word-for-word gloss: having learned, in association with Gaura's devotees, of the supremely wondrous *bhakti* radiant with *prema*, a person gives up "the mind fixed in the world and in the Vedas"; "only so long" — that is, until one sees the beauty of the radiance of the toenails of the lotus feet of Gaura's devotees; "the knowers of the *shastras*" are people who think themselves *panditas*; "the external paths" are false paths born of the mind.

### 20
THEME: Only Gaura's devotees are the mine of all virtues.
In those whose only refuge is Gaurahari, causeless renunciation, or attachment to Bhagavan, is seen — where else is such renunciation to be found? Where else is there such fear, as of falling into hell, at worldly talk or village gossip? Where else is there such a wave of the perfect beauty of humility and meekness? Where else is there such unearthly power? And where else is such a wondrous path of *bhakti*, full of *mahabhava*, possible?
From the word-for-word gloss: "*gaura-gati*" — those for whom Gaurahari is the "*gati*," that is, the refuge or the goal; their renunciation is *yukta-vairagya*.

### 21
THEME: For […] Gaura, attachment to anything other than Gaura is impossible.
One who has even once seen the beautiful face of Sri Gaurasundara, full of the *rasa* of separation, shy with love, adorned with lotus eyes bathed in streams of tears — filled with ever-new transformations of feeling, such as joy and pride, born of ever newer attachment (*anuraga*), will never wish to leave the feet of that Gaurahari, the ocean of sweetness manifest in an unearthly way.

### 22
THEME: Even performing *dharma*, serving Vishnu, travelling to holy places or mastering the Vedas, no one comprehends the secret truth of the Vedas without serving the feet of Gaura's devotees.
Even performing the *dharma* of *varnashrama*, worshipping Vishnu, travelling to hundreds of holy places, studying all the Vedas and *shastras* and so on — without service to the lotus feet of Gaura's dear ones no one can know the abode that is hard to reach by the Vedas and the rest (cannot find Sri Dhama Vrindavana, the field of the spiritual pastimes of Sri Radha-Govinda).
From the word-for-word gloss: "*gaura-priya*" — those to whom Gaurasundara is dear, or those who are dear to Gaurahari; "Vishnu" — the *vishnu-tattva*: Rama, Narayana, Nrisimha and the others; "holy places" — Kashi, Kanchi, Gaya and the rest; this abode is hard to reach because in the Vedas and the rest it is described very secretly and is beyond the understanding of ordinary people: that secret place is the footstool of Sri Radha-Govinda.

### 23
THEME: For a devotee attracted by the radiance of the toenails of Gaura's feet, everything best in the world — the best things, the best *dharmas* — is insignificant.
Even if, by churning with the utmost effort the ocean of nectar that has no shores, some indescribable, supreme, incomparable precious essence were extracted — still, to those touched by the rays of the feet of Madana-Gopala, that is, Krishna clothed in the mood and lustre of Radha — Sri Gaurasundara — it will seem extremely bitter.
From the word-for-word gloss: "Sri" is Sri Radhika, the embodiment of all the Lakshmis; by Her lustre Madana-Gopala has become golden.

### 24
THEME: The virtues of body, speech, mind and intelligence proper to Gaura's devotee are found nowhere else.
Humility lower than grass, that is, absence of worldly self-conceit; a naturally gentle, charming appearance; speech sweet as nectar; spitting at the mere smell of sense objects unconnected with Krishna Chaitanya; complete loss of external consciousness from being overwhelmed by love for Hari — all these virtues in the world belong only to Gaura's devotees.

### 25
THEME: The secret *prema* which the devotees of Sri Gaura obtain at once cannot be obtained by millions of famous *karmis*, *jnanis*, yogis and servants of gurus, nor by millions of readers of *shruti* and *smriti*.
Let them (those who have not taken shelter at Gaura's lotus feet) take shelter of millions of the best gurus, that is, receive from them as many […] instructions as they like, or study millions of *shrutis* and *shastras* (the *agamas*, *nigamas* and the rest) — this gives no possibility of obtaining the secret *prema*; but those whom the merciful glance of Sri Chaitanyadeva has touched certainly obtain that secret *prema* at once.

### 26
THEME: Others do not possess even a small part of a millionth part of the natural qualities of Gaura's devotee.
Let there be the strongest renunciation, let there be countless qualities — *shama*, *dama*, *kshanti*, *maitri* and the rest; let millions of reflections on the oneness of […] Brahman and the *jivatma* take possession of the heart, or let there be millions of kinds of *bhakti* connected with Vishnu — still, not one part in a million of those natural qualities which always abide in the people who rejoice in the radiance of the toenails of the dear devotees of Sri Chaitanya-chandra is to be found anywhere else.
From the word-for-word gloss: renunciation is of two kinds, *phalgu* and *yukta*: giving up what is connected with Hari because one considers it worldly is called *phalgu-vairagya*, while accepting, as is proper, what is favourable and connected with Krishna is *yukta-vairagya*; *shama* is fixity in Bhagavan, *dama* is control of the senses, *kshanti* is forbearance, the undisturbed state of the mind even when there is cause for agitation; "*maitri* and the rest" — purity and so on; "contemplation of the truth" — meditation on "*tat tvam asi*," that is, on the oneness of the *jiva* and Brahman.

### 27
THEME: The madness of love of Gaura's devotees at the festival of the dance is incomparable.
Caught in the net of boundless bliss born of tasting the nectar of *prema*, devoid of external consciousness, some of the devotees headed by Murari Gupta seem to stride over mountains and oceans; others of the devotees headed by Srivasa dance, again and again heaping reproaches on Indra and the other gods; and at such a festival of Sri Chaitanya's dance which of the devotees headed by Advaita (as venerable, learned and acharyas as they were) did not become wild!

### 28
THEME: The secret *prema*, impossible for anyone in the past, future and present, is always accessible to Gaura's devotees.
No relationship with the *rasa* of Bhagavan's lotus feet has anyone in this world had, will have or has; but since the mercy of Gaurasundara, who sports with His supreme wealth — devotion to Himself — has become manifest, the non-envious devotees marked by that mercy have come to know all that sweetness of *rasa*. That is, that secret relationship with the *rasa* of Krishna's lotus feet which no one has ever had, will have or has even now, the associates of Gaura constantly realize by Gaura's mercy.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the non-envious" — those full of compassion for all beings.

### 29
THEME: The most fortunate of all is he who gives the opportunity to seek the lotus feet of Gaurahari, whom the Upanishads seek.
Shachinandana — He whom the garland of the Upanishads, the ornament of all the *shrutis*, seeks; the destroyer of the pride of the best of sages and gods who think themselves great personalities and do not know His own lotus feet — who has made Him visible even to one such as me? Oh! Who in this world is such a greatly fortunate one?
From the word-for-word gloss: "*nigama-chakra*" — the collection of the *shrutis*; their "*chuda*" (crowns) are the Upanishads; "*caya*" — that which is sought by them.

### 30
THEME: Even one devoid of all *sadhana* but surrendered to Gaura alone obtains the crown of the goals of man.
Even devoid of all *sadhana*, one who has given his mind to Sri Gaurasundara, who possesses supremely wonderful glory, certainly obtains the highest goal of man — *prema* for Krishna — and becomes fully satisfied; of this [there is no doubt].
