# Verses 1–28: Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's anvaya (word-for-word gloss), translated from the Bengali. Numbering of the 1951 edition.

### 1
ANVAYA: vrajapati-kumaram — [to give a taste] to Himself, non-different from the son of the lord of Vraja; vishuddha-sva-prema-unmada-madhura-piyusha-laharim — the sweet nectar waves in the form of joy and other feelings, rising from His own pure *prema*; rasayitum — to give a taste; anyebhyah ca — and to others; pradatum — to bestow; [yah — He who]; para-pada-navadvipa-prakatam — appeared in the supreme abode called Navadvipa, the seat of the ninefold *bhakti*; tam — Him; varyam — the best of all *avataras*; ati-vimaryada-parama-adbhuta-audaryam — the embodiment of boundless and supremely wonderful mercy; caitanya-akritim — the person named Sri Krishna Chaitanya; [vayam — we, the Gaudiya *sampradaya*]; stumah — praise.

### 2
ANVAYA: [yah janah — the man who]; dharma-asprishtah — untouched by *dharma*, that is, in whom there is not a drop of piety and the like; ati-adharme — in great sin; satata-parama-avishtah eva — constantly, wholly immersed; na hi khalu satam drishtim praptah — of whom it is known that he has not received the merciful glance of the saints; samsrishtishu kvapi ca no san — and has not stayed in any place connected [with the saints]; [tadrisha janah — even such a man]; yat-datta-shri-hari-rasa-sudha-asvada-mattah [san] — intoxicated by tasting the nectar of the loving *rasa* bestowed by Him: "Sri" means Srimati Radhika, the source of all beauty, and "Hari" [Sri Krishna]; intoxicated by tasting the nectar of Their *prema*; pranrityati — dances exceedingly; uccaih gayati — sings aloud; atha viluthati ca — and rolls on the ground; tam kancit isham — that powerful, indescribable person; staumi — I praise.

### 3
ANVAYA: karma-nishthaih janaih — by people attached to *karma*, that is, [the *karmis*]; yat — that which; na aptam — is not obtained; tapo-dhyana-yogaih ca — and by austerity, meditation, yoga and the like; yat na samadhigatam — that which no one can realize; kaishcit api [janaih] vairagyaih tyaga-tattva-stutibhih api yat na tarkitam ca — that which no one can perceive through renunciation, abandonment of *karma*, [knowledge of the truth], [the reciting of hymns of praise] and the like; govinda-prema-bhajam api [jananam] — even of people full of love for Sri Krishna; na ca yat kalitam — that which is not obtained; tat rahasyam — that secret *prema*; yatra pare avatarati [sati] — when the Supreme, Sri Gauranga, descends; namna eva — through *nama-sankirtana* alone; svayam pradur asit — manifested of its own accord; tam gauram — that Gaurasundara; naumi — I praise.

### 4
ANVAYA: yah — He who; drishtah sprishtah kirtitah [san] — becoming the object of sight, touch and glorification; dura-sthaih api [janaih] samsmritah anatah adritah va — or becoming the object of remembrance, obeisance and great honour of people who are far away; premnah saram datum ekah ishah — alone is able to give the essence of *prema*; [tam] dayalum — that merciful; devam shri-caitanyam — Sri Chaitanyadeva; naumi — I praise.

### 5
ANVAYA: yat-karunya-kataksha-vaibhavavatam — for those whose wealth is His mercy, that is, His merciful glance; kaivalyam — liberation in the form of merging into Ishvara; narakayate — seems like hell; tridasha-puh — the city of the *tridashas*: "*tridasha*" is three times ten plus three, that is, the number thirty-three; among [the gods] the chief are the twelve Adityas, the eleven Rudras, the eight Vasus and the two Ashvini-kumaras — these thirty-three gods are the *tridashas*, and their city is heaven (*svarga*); akasha-pushpayate — seems as unreal as a flower in the sky; durdanta-indriya-kala-sarpa-patali — the host of senses, which are like serpents: "*durdanta*" — untamable, "*kala*" — extremely wrathful; protkhata-damshtrayate — behaves like snakes whose poison fangs have been pulled out; vishvam purna-sukhayate — the universe is felt as an abode of complete happiness, that is, everywhere the presence of Bhagavan is seen; vidhi-mahendra-adih ca — and Brahma, Indra and the other gods; kitayate — seem like worms, that is, the positions of the lordship of Brahma, the lordship of Indra and the rest seem as insignificant as that of a worm; tam — that; gauram eva — Gaurasundara alone; [vayam — we]; stumah — praise.

### 6
ANVAYA: suparva-iditah — honoured even by the gods; sarve — all, that is, all of Gaura's devotees; yasya — whose, that is, Gaurachandra's; carana-ambhoja-sravat-projjvala-prema-ananda-maya-amrita-adbhuta-rasan — the wondrous nectar *rasa*, full of *prema* and bliss, supremely radiant, flowing from His lotus feet; paripiya — having drunk; madyantah [santah] — intoxicated by drinking; brahma-adin — at Brahma and the others; hasanti — laugh; maha-vaishnavan ca — and the great Vaishnavas, the *mahabhagavatas*, the devotees of Vishnu; atibahu na manyante — do not honour very much; brahma-yoga-vidushah — the knowers of Brahman, who worship on the principle "I am He" (*ahamgraha-upasaka*), and the yogis of the eightfold yoga; dhik kurvanti — censure; tam gaurachandram — that Gaurachandra; [vayam — we]; stumah — praise.

### 7
ANVAYA: [rama-nrisimha-adibhih — by the *avataras* Rama, Nrisimha and the others]; rakshah-daitya-kulam — the race of Rakshasas and Daityas; hatam — was destroyed; idam kiyat — what is so important in this deed?; [kapiladeva-adibhih — by Kapiladeva and the others]; yoga-adi-vartma-kriya-margah — the path of action on the ways of yoga and the like; va prakatikritah — was revealed; idam kiyat — what is so great in this?; [guna-avatara-brahma-adina kritam — done by the *guna-avataras*, Brahma and the others]; srishti-adikam — the deeds of creation, maintenance, destruction and so on; va kiyat — what is so excellent in this too?; [shri-varaha-adina kritam — done by Sri Varaha and other *avataras*]; medini-uddharana-adikam — the lifting of the earth and other deeds; idam kiyat — what is there in this either?; [vayam tu — we, however]; bhagavatah — to Bhagavan; prema-ujjvalayah maha-bhakteh — of the highest (*para*) *bhakti*, radiant with *prema*; vartma-karim — showing the path; caitanya-murtim — the form of Chaitanya; param stumah — praise exclusively.

### 8
ANVAYA: koti-candra-anana-tvishe — to Him whose radiance of face is more beautiful even than millions of moons; prema-ananda-abdhi-candraya — to Him who is like the moon in the ocean of bliss born of *prema*; caru-candra-amshu-hasine — to Him whose smile is like beautiful moonbeams; caitanya-candraya — to Chaitanya-chandra; namah — obeisance.

### 9
ANVAYA: prema-abhidhanah paramah puma-arthah — the highest goal of man, called *prema*; yasya eva — only by whose; pada-ambuja-bhakti-labhyah — is attainable by devotion to the lotus feet; [bhavati — is]; tasmai — to Him; jagat-mangala-mangalaya — to Him who is the good of the good of the world: the good of the world is the worship (*bhajana*) of Sri Krishna, and still more auspicious is the supreme *prema* obtained from Him; that is, to Him who is the good of the good of the world; caitanya-candraya te — to You, Chaitanya-chandra; namah namah — obeisance again and again.

### 10
ANVAYA: hema-danda-prakandau bahu — huge arms like golden pillars; proddhritya — raising; aho — ah!; kara-caranam — hands and feet; uccaih asphalayantam — Him who, absorbed in the dance, swings them high; sat-tandava-tarala-tanum — Him whose body has become mobile through a beautiful vigorous dance: "*sat*" — sweet, "*tandavena*" — by a vigorous dance, "*tarala*" — the body has become mobile; that is, Him whose body sways in a beautiful vigorous dance; pundarika-ayata-aksham — Him whose eyes are wide as lotuses; kimapi hari hari iti unmada-ananda-nadaih — with indescribable sounds of the bliss of *prema* arising from the words "Hari, Hari"; vishvasya — of the universe; amangala-ghnam — Him who destroys the inauspicious; deva-cudamanim — the crown-ornament of the gods; tam — that; atula-rasa-avishta-caitanya-candram — Chaitanya-chandra, absorbed in incomparable *rasa*; [aham — I]; vande — worship.

### 11
ANVAYA: ananda-lila-maya-vigrahaya — to Him whose form is made of pastimes of bliss; hema-abha-divya-cchavi-sundaraya — beautiful with a divine radiance like gold; maha-prema-rasa-pradaya — the giver of the *rasa* of the most exalted radiant *prema*, never given before; tasmai caitanya-candraya te — to Him, to You, Chaitanya-chandra; namo namah — obeisance again and again.

### 12
ANVAYA: ahaha — oh!; ashrunam pravahaih — with streams of tears; nava-jalada-koti iva drishau dadhanam — Him whose eyes are like millions of new clouds; prema-ardhya — by the wealth of *prema*; parama-pada-koti-prahasanam — Him who laughs at millions of supreme abodes, that is, at millions of Vaikunthas; tanu-cchatabhih madhuryaih va — by the radiance of His body, or by the sweetness of the beauty and charm of His form; amrita-nidhi-kotih iva vamantam — Him who seems to pour forth millions of oceans of nectar; tam sannyasa-kapatam — Him who under a pretext accepted the dress of a sannyasi, that is, enacts the role of a sannyasi in order to spread the example of an acharya; harim — Gaurahari; vande — I worship.

### 13
ANVAYA: simha-skandham — Him whose shoulders are raised like a lion's; madhura-madhura-smera-ganda-sthala-antam — Him at the edges of whose cheeks a sweet, sweet smile blossoms; durvijneya-ujjvala-rasa-maya-ashcarya-nana-vikaram — "*durvijneya*" — hard to comprehend even for the worshippers of Bhagavan, "*ujjvala-rasamaya*" — full of *shringara-rasa*, "*ashcarya-nana-vikaram*" — possessing wondrous transformations of feeling, such as the form of a tortoise, caused by tasting *rasa*; that is, possessing most wondrous transformations, such as the form of a tortoise, caused by tasting the most exalted *ujjvala-rasa*, hard to comprehend even for the worshippers of Bhagavan; vikaca-kanaka-ambhoja-garbha-abhiramam kantim — a lustre as charming as the filaments of a full-blown golden lotus; [yah — He who]; bibhrat — bears; radhayah madhavasya ca — of Radha and Madhava; ekibhutam vapuh — the united body; vah — you; avatu — may protect.

### 14
ANVAYA: [yah — He who]; nutana-ambuda-cayam — a mass of new clouds; drishtva — seeing; madyati — becomes intoxicated; [yah ca — and who]; barham — a peacock feather; samvikshya — seeing; atyantam vikalah bhavet — becomes extremely agitated; valitam — coiled in a ring like a bracelet; gunja-avalim — a string of *gunja* berries; vilokya — seeing; vepate — trembles; shyama-kishorake drishte api — even on seeing a dark youth; cakitam — starting; camatkaritam — wondrousness; dhatte — assumes; ittham — thus; pracarita-nija-prema — He who has spread His own *prema*; gaura-tanuh harih — Gauranga Hari; vah — you; patu — may protect.

### 15
ANVAYA: kripa-sindhuh — the ocean of mercy; sandhya-aruna-ruci-vicitra-ambara-dharah — wearing wondrous garments of a red colour, like the sun at evening twilight; ujjvalah purnah prema-amrita-maya-maha-jyotih — radiant, undivided, full of the nectar of *prema*, possessing the lustre of the *sattvika* and other *bhavas*; amalah — pure; shaci-garbha-kshira-ambudhi-bhavah — born from the milk ocean, the womb of Shachi; udara-adbhuta-kalah — possessing the beautiful and wondrous sixty-four arts of *rasa*, beginning with refined skill (*vaidagdhya*); shriman — the body united with Srimati Radhika, who is full of supreme beauty, or: full of supreme beauty; kalanathah — the moon, or in another sense: Svayam Bhagavan, the source even of the partial *avataras* such as Matsya; tava — in your (here: in your, plural); svanta-nabhasi — in the sky of the heart; udayatu — may He rise.

### 16
ANVAYA: nija-loka-mangala-hare-krishna-iti namnam — of His own names "Hare Krishna," which are the good of the world; sankhyatum — to keep count of the repetitions; kati-dorakaih — with the waist-cord; granthin — knots; badhnan — tying; japan [ca] — and chanting; prema-bhara-prakampita-karah — He whose hands tremble from the excess of *prema*; ashru-snata-mukhah — His face bathed in tears of *prema*; svam eva hi jagannatham — Jagannatha, non-different from Himself; didrikshuh — wishing to see; gata-ayataih — by going to and fro; vilocana-mudam — the joy of the eyes; tanvan — spreading; gaura-tanuh harih — Gauranga Sri Hari; vah — you; patu — may protect.

### 17
ANVAYA: samasta-jagatam — of the whole world; antar-dhvanta-cayam — the mass of the darkness of ignorance abiding in the heart; hathat — suddenly; unmulayanti — uprooting; balat — by force; prema-ananda-rasa-ambudhim — the ocean of the *rasa* of the bliss of *prema*; niravadhi — incessantly; prodvelayanti — increasing exceedingly; tapa-trayena — by the three miseries; anisham — incessantly; vikalam — overwhelmed; vishvam — the universe; ativa — exceedingly; shitalayanti — refreshing by sprinkling the nectar of *prema*; caitanya-candra-cchata — the bodily radiance of Chaitanya-chandra; yushmakam — your; hridaye — in the hearts; satatam — always; cakastu — may it shine.

### 18
ANVAYA: pura — in ancient times; muni-ishvaraih api — even by the lords of sages; yatra [bhakti-marge] — on which [path of *bhakti*]; bhrantam — went astray; kshama-mandale — on the whole earth; kasyapi — no one's; dhishana — intelligence; yasmin — into which [path of *bhakti*]; na pravivesha eva — did not enter at all; shukah va — even Shuka; yat — which [path]; na veda — did not know; kripamayena shaurina — by the merciful Sri Krishna; kvapi — ever; nije api jane — even to His own devotees; na udghatitam — was not revealed; gaura-priyah — the devotees dear to Gaura; tasmin ujjvala-bhakti-vartmani — on that radiant path of *bhakti*; sukham khelanti — joyfully play.

### 19
ANVAYA: yavat — as long as; shri-caitanya-pada-ambuja-priya-janah — one absorbed in drinking the nectar of Sri Chaitanya's lotus feet, that is, an intimate of Gaura; na drik-gocarah [bhavet] — does not become visible; tavat — only so long; brahma-katha — discussion of the impersonal Brahman; tavat — so long; vimukti-padavi — the path of liberation; na tiktibhavet — does not seem bitter; tavat ca — and so long; loka-veda-sthitih api — even the order of worldly and Vedic duties; no vishrinkhalatvam ayate — does not fall into disorder, that is, the respective boundaries are not transgressed; in other words, having learned, in association with Gaura's people, of the supremely wondrous *bhakti* radiant with *prema*, a person gives up the mind fixed in the world and in the Vedas; tavat eva — only so long, that is, only until one sees the beauty of the radiance of the toenails of the lotus feet of Gaura's people; nana-bahih-vartmasu — about the many false external paths born of mental speculation; shastra-vidam — of the knowers of the *shastras*, that is, of those who consider themselves *panditas*; mithah — among themselves; kalakalah [bhavet] — there are disputes and quarrels.

### 20
ANVAYA: gaura-gatishu — among those whose "*gati*," that is, refuge or goal, is Gaurahari; yat avakalitam — that [renunciation and the rest] which is seen, that is, felt; tavat vairagyam — such renunciation, that is, *yukta-vairagya*, or attachment to Bhagavan; kva — where?; kva ca — and where; tavan — such; vishaya-vartasu — towards talk of sense objects; narakeshu iva — as towards hell; udvegah — alarm; kva ca — and where; asau vinaya-bhara-apurya-lahari — this overflowing wave of abundant humility; tavat alaukikam tejah va — such unearthly power; kva — where?; atha sa maha-bhakti-padavi va — and this wondrous path of *bhakti*, full of *mahabhava*; kva sambhavya — where is it possible?

### 21
ANVAYA: [yah — he who]; sakrit-nayana-gocarikrita-tat-ashru-dhara-akula-praphulla-kamala-ikshana-pranaya-katara-shri-mukhah — "*pranaya-katara*" — overcome by love, "*shri*" — supreme beauty, the face endowed with it; what is it like? — "*ashru-dhara-akula*" — full of streams of tears, with eyes like full-blown lotuses; it has "*sakrit*" — even once — become the object of his sight; that is, he who has even once seen the beautiful face overcome by love, with lotus eyes full-blown and full of streams of tears; nava-nava-anuraga-unmadah [san] — filled with transformations of feeling, such as joy, pride and the rest, born of ever newer *anuraga*; lokottara-sphurat-madhurima-arnavam — the ocean of sweetness manifest in an unearthly way; gaura-caranam — Gaura's feet; kadapi — ever; na jihasati — does not wish to leave.

### 22
ANVAYA: gaura-priya-pada-sevam — service to the feet of the *gaura-priyas*: those to whom Gaurasundara is dear, or those who are dear to Gaurahari; vina — without; dharmam — the *dharma* of *varnashrama* and the like; acarya [api] — even performing, properly observing; vishnum — the *vishnu-tattva*: Rama, Narayana, Nrisimha and the others; paricarya [api] — even worshipping excellently; tirthani — hundreds of holy places: Kashi, Kanchi, Gaya and the rest; vicarya [api] — even having wandered through them or made their *parikrama*; vedan — all the Vedas and *shastras*; vicarya [api] — even having studied; veda-adi-dushprapya-padam — the abode hard to reach by means of the Vedas and the rest, or: described very secretly in the Vedas and the rest and therefore incomprehensible to ordinary people — that secret place, the footstool of Sri Radha-Govinda, Sri Dhama Vrindavana; [ko'pi — no one]; na vidanti — can know.

### 23
ANVAYA: [yadyapi — even if]; apara-avaram — shoreless: "*para-avara*" — both shores; that which has neither shore, that is, without shores, endless; amrita-maya-pathodhim — "*pathodhi*" — the ocean, the receptacle of waters; such an ocean whose water is nectar; adhikam — abundantly; vimathya — having thoroughly churned; idam kimapi — some indescribable; atulam — incomparable, without equal; paramam saram — supreme essence; praptam syat — should be obtained; tathapi tat vastu — still that thing; shri-gaura-akriti-madana-gopala-carana-cchata-sprishtanam — for the people touched by the radiance (beauty) of the feet of Madana-Gopala, golden [with the lustre] of Sri: "Sri" is Sri Radhika, the embodiment of all the Lakshmis; Madana-Gopala, who has become golden by Her lustre, that is, Krishna clothed in the mood and lustre of Sri Radha — Sri Gaurasundara; for those whom the radiance of His feet has touched; vikatam eva katutam — a terrible bitterness indeed; vahati — seems to have.

### 24
ANVAYA: trinat api ca su-nicata — humility lower than grass, that is, absence of material pride; sahaja-saumya-mugdha-akritih — a natural, gentle, beautiful appearance; sudha-madhura-bhashita — speech sweet as nectar; vishaya-gandha-thuthut-kritih — spitting at the mere smell of sense objects unconnected with Krishna-Chaitanya; dhih hari-pranaya-vihvala [sati] kimapi analambita — the mind, overwhelmed by love for Hari, entirely devoid of external consciousness; ami sat-gunah — these good qualities; jagati — in the world; kila gaura-bhajam [eva] bhavanti — belong only to the devotees of Gaura.

### 25
ANVAYA: guru-varya-kotih — millions of the best gurus; upasatam — let them take shelter of; va — or; shruti-shastra-kotih — millions of *shrutis* and *shastras*; adhiyatam — let them study; [parantu — but]; caitanya-karunya-kataksha-bhajam — of those who have obtained Chaitanya's merciful glance; hi — certainly; sadyah — immediately; param rahasya-labhah — the attainment of the secret *prema*; syat — takes place.

### 26
ANVAYA: vairagya-kotih — strong or manifold *vairagya*; *vairagya* is of two kinds, *phalgu* and *yukta*: giving up things connected with Hari as worldly is called *phalgu-vairagya*, while the proper acceptance of what is favourable and connected with Krishna is *yukta-vairagya*; astam — let there be; shama-dama-kshanti-maitri-adi-kotih va — or: *shama* — steadiness in Bhagavan, *dama* — control of the senses, *kshanti* — tolerance, the undisturbed state of the mind even when there is cause for agitation, *maitri* and the rest — purity and the rest — all these countless qualities; bhavatu — let there be; tattva-anudhyana-kotih — millions of meditations on "*tat tvam asi*," that is, on the oneness of the *jiva* and Brahman; bhavatu — let there be; vaishnavi-bhakti-kotih va — or millions of *bhaktis* connected with Vishnu; bhavatu — let there be; tat api — still; shrimat-caitanya-candra-priya-carana-nakha-jyotih-amoda-bhajam — of the people who have obtained bliss from the radiance of the toenails of the dear devotees of the all-powerful Chaitanya-chandra; yah — which; svatah-siddhah — naturally inherent; guna-ganah — host of qualities; aste — abides; asya — of it; koti-amshah api — even a millionth part; [anyatra — elsewhere]; na syat — is not.

### 27
ANVAYA: ananda-udbhata-jala-vihvalataya — because of being overwhelmed by the boundless bliss born of tasting the nectar of *prema*; kecit — some of the devotees headed by Murari Gupta; sagara-bhudharan api — even oceans and mountains; parakramanti — stride over; kecit — some of the devotees headed by Srivasa; deva-purandara-adishu — at Indra and the other gods; muhuh — again and again; maha-akshepam — great reproach; kshipantah — hurling; vai nrityanti — dance; idrishi — at such a; caitanya-nritya-utsave — festival of Sri Gauranga's dance; te advaitacandra-adayah — those headed by Sri Advaita Acharya; ke ke — who, who; punah na uddhatavantah — did not become wild?

### 28
ANVAYA: asmin jagat-mandale — in this earthly world; bhagavat-pada-ambuja-rase — in the *rasa* of Bhagavan's lotus feet; kasyapi — of no one; yah ko'pi sambandhah — any relationship whatever; na bhutah — has been; [na] bhavita — will be; [na] bhavati api va — nor is even now; nija-bhakti-rupa-parama-aishvaryena — together with the supreme wealth in the form of devotion to Himself; vikriditah — sporting; asya gaurasya — of this Gaurachandra; kripa-vijrimbhitataya — owing to the manifestation of mercy; [tat-upalakshitah — illumined by that mercy]; nirmatsarah — people merciful to all beings; tat sarvam — all those sweetnesses of *rasa*; jananti — come to know.
