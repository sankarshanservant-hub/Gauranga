# Verses 57–98: Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's anvaya (word-for-word gloss), translated from the Bengali. Numbering of the 1951 edition (Bengali edition: 57–74, 76–99).

### 57
ANVAYA: [he — O]; mudha-cetah — foolish heart; sva-tejasa — by His own power (influence); krishna-pada-aravinda-maha-rasa-aveshita-vishvam — Him who by the elevated radiant (*unnata-ujjvala*) *rasa* of the lotus feet of Sri Krishna, His own form (*sva-svarupa*), has filled the universe with *anuraga* (attachment); ashesha-shruti-gudha-vesham — Him who abides secretly among all the *shrutis*, that is, has entered them in a hidden form; kam api isham gaurangam — that indescribable Supreme, Gaurahari; angikuru — accept, that is, take shelter of His lotus feet.

### 58
ANVAYA: yadi kah api — if someone; murareh — of Murari; shravana-manana-sankirti-adi-bhaktya — by the ninefold *sadhana-bhakti* — hearing, reflection and the rest; parama-puma-artham — the highest goal of man, *prema*; sadhayet — attains by practice; [tarhi — then]; bhadram — good; let him attain it; mama tu — for me, however; apara-prema-piyusha-sindhoh gaura-dhamnah — of Sri Hari, who has a golden lustre (*gaura-kanti*) and is the ocean of the nectar of boundless *prema*; kim api rasa-rahasyam — whatever secret there is in the *rasa* of *bhakti*, that is, the treasure of love; param namasyam — is alone worthy of worship.

### 59
ANVAYA: purusha-artha-catushtaya-ashah — those who long for the four goals — *dharma*, *artha*, *kama* and *moksha* — some of them; isham — the Supreme; bhajantu — let them worship; [anyan — other]; upasyan — objects of worship; vihaya — leaving; hareh dasah ca bhavantu — let them become exclusive servants of Hari; aham tu — I, however; kincit rahasya-pada-lobhita-dhih [san — being] — longing to taste a certain extremely rare, secret love of Radha and Krishna; caitanya-candra-caranam sharanam karomi — take shelter of the feet of the Chaitanya-moon.

### 60
ANVAYA: laukiki-vaidiki-nishtham prapta va vyavahriti-tatih — that series of worldly and Vedic dealings in which I was firmly established; prahasana-samudgana-natya-utsaveshu ya va lajja — that shame which there was at laughter (jokes), loud chanting, dancing, festivals and the like; ahaha — oh!; ye va sahaja-prana-deha-artha-dharmah — those natural properties that are the cause (support) of life and body; abhuvan — that there were; kah api tivra-viryah gaurah caurah — a certain thief, very powerful, who has assumed the form of Gaura; me sakalam aharat — has stolen all this of mine.

### 61
ANVAYA: karuna-snigdha-netra-ancalena — with the corners of His eyes, moist with compassion; sandra-ananda-ujjvala-rasamaya-prema-piyusha-sindhoh — of the ocean of the nectar of *prema*, consisting of *rasa* that is dense (*sandra*), that is, […], and radiant (*ujjvala*), that is, ever renewing itself; kim api — indescribable; kotim varshan — pouring millions; kah ayam — who is this; kanaka-kadali-garbha-gauranga-yashtih devah — the playful (*lilamaya*) Purusha, whose beautiful slender body is golden like the core of a golden plantain tree; akasmat — suddenly; mama cetah — my heart; nija-pade — on His own, that is, His holy feet; gadha-yuktam cakara — has firmly fixed.

### 62
ANVAYA: yatra — where; druta-kanaka-gaurah — golden like molten gold; maha-prema-ananda-ujjvala-rasa-vapuh — possessing a body consisting of *shringara-rasa* in the form of *mahabhava*; devah — the playful Bhagavan; karunaya — out of mercy (subject to mercy); svayam pradurabhavat — appeared of His own accord; tasmin — in that; prati-bhavana-bhakti-utsavamaye — where every house is full of the festival of the goddess of *prema-bhakti*; vaikunthat api ca madhure dhamni navadvipe — in Navadvipa, the abode full of a sweetness greater even than Vaikuntha; me manah — my mind; ramate — delights (abides in pastimes).

### 63
ANVAYA: shastrani — the *shastras*; yat tat vadatu — let them say whatever they please; tarkikah ca — and the logicians; yat yat vyakhyantu — let them interpret as they please; tu — but; caitanya-pada-ambhoja-sudha eva — the nectar of Chaitanya's lotus feet alone; mama jivanam — is my life.

### 64
ANVAYA: durlabhah siddhayah [api] — even extremely rare *siddhis*; yadi kara-tale svayam patanti — if they come of themselves into my hand; surah — the gods; sevaki bhavitum — to become servants; yadi svayam ca agatah syuh — if they come of their own accord; kim anyat — what more (moreover); yadi va idam eva vapuh — if even this very body; catur-bhujam syat — becomes four-armed, that is, if even *sarupya-mukti* is obtained; tathapi mama manah — still my mind; gauracandrat — from Gaurachandra; manak no calati — will not swerve in the least.

### 65
ANVAYA: ghora-dahana-jvala-avali-panjare — in a cage of many tongues of terrible flame; varam me vasah astu — better let my dwelling be; [kintu — but, tathapi — still]; shri-caitanya-pada-aravinda-vimukhaih — with those who have turned away from Chaitanya's lotus feet; kutracit — at any time; sangamah — association; ma — not; [astu — let there be]; yadi gaurasya pada-ambhoja-rajah-chata — if the radiance of the pollen of Chaitanya's lotus feet; manak — even a little; no rasyate — cannot be tasted; [tada — then]; me manah — my mind; svayam militam vaikuntha-adi padam ca — even the abode of Vaikuntha and the rest, obtained of themselves, without *sadhana* and the like; no lipsate — does not wish to obtain.

### 66
ANVAYA: sarva-kshama-mandale — throughout the whole circle of the earth; mahan mahan iti ravah — such a report (fame): "he is a great man," "he is a great man"; astam — let there be; nama — (a particle expressing concession); va — or; loke — on earth; mahati — long-lasting; camatkarini siddhih — a supernatural *siddhi*; prakata astu — let it appear; nama — (a particle expressing concession); [kim va — or else]; vishva-ishvaram — the Lord of the universe, Vishnu; aradhya — worshipping; kamam — desired for oneself; caru-catur-bhujatvam — a charming four-armed form; ayatam — let it come; [kintu — but]; shri-gaura-bhaktim vina — without *bhakti* for Sri Gaura; me cetah — my mind; [etat sarvam — all this]; na hi bahumanyate na hi — truly does not value, truly does not value.

### 67
ANVAYA: [he — O]; caitanya iti — "Chaitanya" (the word "Chaitanya" means the form consisting of being, consciousness and bliss, or He who attracts the hearts of all beings — O such Chaitanya); [he — O]; kripamaya iti — "O merciful one"; [he — O]; parama-udara iti — "O most magnanimous"; [he — O]; nana-vidha-prema-aveshita-sarva-bhuta-hridaya iti — "O You by whom the hearts of all beings are filled with various kinds of *prema* — *dasya*, *sakhya*, *vatsalya* and *madhurya*"; [he — O]; ashcarya-dhaman iti — "O possessor of a supremely wondrous lustre like that of molten gold"; [he — O]; gauranga iti — "O Gaurasundara"; [he — O]; guna-arnava iti — "O ocean of auspicious qualities"; [he — O]; rasa-rupa iti — "O embodied form of *vipralambha-rasa*"; [he — O]; sva-nama-priya iti — "O lover of Your own names" — thus; ashrantam jalpatah — incessantly chanting; mama iyam janih — this life of mine; yayat — may it pass; iti prarthaye — for this alone I pray.

### 68
ANVAYA: [he — O]; shaure — Shauri (Krishna, descendant of Shura); [tava — in Your]; parama-prema-rasade — bestowing the highest *rasa* of *prema*, that is, bestowing the elevated radiant (*unnata-ujjvala*) *rasa* of *prema*; sat-eka-prane — the very life of the *rasika* devotees; gaure vapushi — in the Gaura-body; kada — when; nishkapata-krita-bhavah — guileless feeling; bhavita asmi — will arise; kada va — or when; tasya — of that guileless feeling; alaukika-sat-anumanena — by an unearthly, that is, inaccessible to the material senses, true inference, that is, the inference: "where the radiance of the jewel-like toenails of Sri Radha's feet has risen, there is the feeling for Gaurahari"; mama hridi — in my heart; akasmat — suddenly; shri-radha-pada-nakha-mani-jyotih — the radiance of the jewels, the toenails, on the lotus feet of Sri Radha; udagat — will rise (with the word "*kada*" the past tense is used in the sense of the future).

### 69
ANVAYA: uddama-damanaka-damam — Him who wears a garland of full-blown *damanaka* flowers; gana-abhiramam — *gana* — people, *abhitah* — wholly, *ramayati* — gladdens; that is, Him who wholly gladdens multitudes of people; arama-ramam — Him who, staying in a secluded place, experiences bliss within Himself; avirama-grihita-nama — Him who incessantly chants the names "Krishna" and the rest; karunya-dhama — the abode of mercy; kanaka-ujjvala-gaura-dhama — golden, radiant like gold; caitanya-nama paramam dhama — the supreme abode named Chaitanya, that is, the highest shelter; [vayam — we]; kalayamah — contemplate (meditate on).

### 70
ANVAYA: mama manah — my mind; range nilacala-shikhara-shringe — on the festive summit of Nilachala; sada — always; vilasatah hareh eva — of Hari alone (Jagannathadeva), who sports there; bhrajat-mukha-kamala-bhringa-ikshana-yugam — *bhrajat* — on the radiant, *mukha-kamalam* — lotus of the face, *tasmin* — on it, *bhringa-rupam* — in the form of bees, *ikshana-yugam* — the pair of eyes, *yasya* — whose; that is, Him whose eyes are bees on the radiant lotus of the face; samuttunga-prema-unmada-rasa-tarangam — Him in whom flow the waves of the *rasas* — joy, pride and the rest — born of the most exalted great *prema* in the form of *mahabhava*; mriga-drisham anangam — to doe-eyed women Kandarpa himself; gata-sangam — extremely detached (that is, utterly disinclined to the enjoyment of *sambhoga-rasa* — looking at women and the like — enacting the pastime of an acharya); gaurangam — Gauranga; smaratu — may it remember.

### 71
ANVAYA: aruna-rocishnu-vasanam — Him whose garment is the colour of the red dawn; pankeruha-nayana-nihsyandi-payasam sat-mukta-phala-sulalitaih prishadbhih — by drops of tears flowing from His lotus-like eyes, most charming, like pearls; yasya vapushi alankarah — whose body is adorned; udancat-romancaih api ca — and also by the hairs standing on end, that is, by the blazing (*uddipta*) *sattvika-bhavas* — the thrill born of *prema* and the rest — by which the beauty of His body is produced; tam — that; gauram — golden; harim — Hari; alambe — I take as my shelter.

### 72
ANVAYA: kandarpat api sundarah — more beautiful even than Kandarpa ([…] — he who is able to scorn even him, that is, to crush even Brahma's pride consisting in beauty and power — Kandarpa; more beautiful even than him; the worldly Kandarpa, or Madana, produces agitation and bewilderment, while Gaurahari's beauty, destroying agitation and bewilderment, gives one to taste the highest happiness of the bliss of *prema*); aho — oh! (expresses wonder); sura-sarit-purat pavanah — purifying more than the stream of the heavenly river ("*sura-sarit*" — the Ganga, the best of all purifiers; "*pura*" — her stream; purifying more even than it: the stream of the Ganga destroys sins but cannot purify a sinful heart, while Sri Gaurasundara, having purified the heart, causes a stream of the highest *prema* to rise in it — therefore His purity and wonderfulness surpass those of the Ganga's stream); shita-amshoh api shitalah — cooler even than the moon (he whose rays are cool is the moon; cooler even than it: the moon can remove external darkness, heat and the like, while Sri Gaurasundara completely destroys the darkness of the heart — the gloom of ignorance accumulated over millions of births — and the heat of suffering, and floods it with the […] rays of the bliss of *prema*); madhvika-sarat api sumadhurah — sweeter even than the finest honey (*madhvika-sara*, that is, *amrita*; sweeter even than it: the sweetness of *amrita* only the gods can taste, and as the fruit of tasting it worldly pride, envy and the like arise in them, while Gaura's sweetness is inaccessible even to the gods, and as the result of tasting it worldly pride, envy and the like completely vanish and the bliss of *prema* appears); kalpa-mahiruhat api data — more generous even than the wish-fulfilling tree (the wish-fulfilling tree gives fruit to one who asks it, while Sri Gaurasundara is so generous that He Himself, becoming the petitioner, bestows the wealth of *prema* and the like even on the unworthy and those who do not ask); jananyah api maha-snigdhah — more tender even than a mother (a mother's love brings poisonous fruit in the future, for it is directed to the perishable body and the like, while Sri Gaurasundara's love is directed to the soul of the living being, and therefore it obtains the right to […] the highest bliss of *prema*; besides, a mother loves only her own child, while Sri Gaurasundara sprinkles all living beings with the *rasa* of love); gaura-harih — Gaurahari; dhyatah — becoming the object of meditation; kada nu — when indeed; me hridi — in my heart; premna — with love; padam dhasyati — will step.

### 73
ANVAYA: madhura-madhura-prema-madhvi-rasanam — of the *rasas* of the nectar of *prema*, sweeter than sweet; punjam punjam — in heaps; dattva dattva — giving again and again; etat vishvam — this world; modayan — gladdening; kati-tata-milat-manju-manjishtha-vasah — *manju* — very charming, *manjishtha* — a particular red creeper; He whose garment is dyed with it, that is, who wears on His hips a charming ochre-coloured (*gairika*) garment; bhasa-nirbhartsita-nava-tadit-koti — He who by His radiance has put to shame millions of lightnings, that is, possessing a bodily radiance that puts to shame millions of lightnings; svayam uru-dayah — Himself supremely merciful; ekah devah eva — *eka* — non-dual, or having neither equal nor superior; *deva* — the Purusha devoted to pastimes; me priyah [astu] — may He be dear to me.

### 74
ANVAYA: kantya — by the charming beauty of His form; nindita-koti-koti-madanah — He who puts to shame millions upon millions of Kandarpas; shrimat-mukha-indu-chata-vicchayikrita-koti-koti-sharad-unmilat-tushara-cchavih — *shrimat-mukha-indu* — the moon of the face, full of supreme beauty; *chata* — radiance; *tena* — by it; *vicchayikrita* — eclipsed; *koti-koti sharad-unmilat-tushara-cchavi* — millions upon millions of rising autumn moons, white as snow; that is, He by the radiance of whose most beautiful moon-face millions upon millions of autumn moons are eclipsed; audaryena ca koti-koti-gunitam kalpa-drumam hi alpayan — He by whose great generosity even millions of wish-fulfilling trees are belittled; gaurah — Gaurasundara; koti-koti-janusham — of countless births; bhagyaih — by the fruits of good deeds (*sukriti*); me hridi — in my heart; padam dhasyati — will step; [kim — will He?].

### 75
ANVAYA: kshanam kshinah — for a moment emaciated (the state in which Srimati Radhika was in prolonged *vipralambha*, that is, in the separation of *mathura-viraha* — Gaurahari, imbued with that mood, for a moment emaciated in separation from Krishna); kshanam [ca] pinah — and for a moment full (again in *vipralambha*, […], for a moment full); ahaha — alas!; kshanam sashruh — for a moment full of tears of bliss; atha — then; kshanam shitah — for a moment cooled (scorched by the fire of separation from Krishna, again at […] for a moment cooled); kshanam smerah — for a moment gently smiling (from jokes and laughter with […] Krishna); [kshanam] anala-taptah — scorched by fire (in *prema-vaicittya*, that is, even while near Krishna, again for a moment scorched by the fire of future separation from Him); kshanam dhavan — for a moment running (absorbed in the thought "Krishna is going away," for a moment running after Him); kshanam stabdhah — for a moment stunned (again coming to another place and […], for a moment stunned; *stambha* is the first of the eight *sattvika-bhavas*); aho kshanam adhika-jalpan — oh! for a moment talking much (for a moment uttering *prajalpa*: "O libertine, O destroyer of the vow of chastity, do not touch me!"); kshanam [ca] mukah — and for a moment mute (for some time keeping silent from the weight of offended pride, *mana*); [etadrishah — such]; bhagavatah dehah — the body of Bhagavan; gaurah — Gaurasundara; mama [hridaye — in the heart] sphuratu — may He appear in my heart.

### 76
ANVAYA: yah prabhuh — that Prabhu (*prabhu* — the independent Lord) who; patra-apatra-vicaranam — deliberation on whether a person is worthy or unworthy; na kurute — does not make; na svam param vikshate — does not look to whether one is His own or another's; na hi deya-adeya-vimarshakah — does not reflect or deliberate on what may be given and what may not; na va kala-pratikshah — nor waits for a suitable or unsuitable time; shravana-ikshana-pranamana-dhyana-adina — by hearing, seeing, bowing, meditation and the like; durlabham bhakti-rasam — the *rasa* of *bhakti*, hard to attain; sadyah datte — at once bestows; sah bhagavan gaurah eva — that very Bhagavan Gaurasundara; me param gatih — is my only refuge.

### 77
ANVAYA: papiyan api — even the greatest sinner (like Gopala Chapala and others, struck with leprosy); hina-jatih api — even the low-born (like tailors, *yavanas* and other low castes); duhshilah api — even a man of evil character; dushkarmanam sima api — even one who has reached the limit of extremely evil deeds (the abduction of others' wives, drunkenness and the like), like Jagai, Madhai and their like; shvapaca-adhamah api — even one worse than a *candala*; satatam durvasana-adhyah api — even one forever given to evil desires; durdesha-prabhavah api ca — and even one born in an evil land (where there is no Ganga or where the Pandavas did not go — in such lands devoid of the Ganga and the Pandavas); tatra vihita-avasah api — even one living in those places as a consequence of evil deeds; duhsangatah nashtah api — even one ruined by evil association; yena kripaya eva uddhritah — He by whose mercy alone was delivered; tam gauram eva — that Gaurasundara alone; ashraye — I take as my shelter.

### 78
ANVAYA: kalinda-tanaya-tate — Kalinda is the mountain Kalinda, its *tanaya* — daughter, the Yamuna, *tate* — on the bank; or Kalinda means the sun, his daughter is the Yamuna, on her bank; that is, on the bank of the Yamuna; sphurat-amanda-vrindavanam — the radiant, most charming Vrindavana; vihaya — leaving; lavana-ambudheh — of the salt ocean; pulina-pushpa-vatim — to the flower garden on the shore; gatah — He who went; parihrita-supita-vasah — He who, leaving His most beautiful yellow garment; dhrita-aruna-patah — put on a garment the colour of the dawn; tirohita-nija-cchavih — He who, concealing His own dark colour, like an *indranila* sapphire (in the *anuvada*: covering it with the feeling and lustre of His most beloved Srimati Radhika); prakata-gaurima — manifested a golden lustre; harih — that Sri Hari; me gatih — is my shelter.

### 79
ANVAYA: are mudhah — ho, fools; muni-varaih daviyasya drishtya api aparicita-purvam — that which the sages formerly could not know even with their far-seeing vision; gudham — secret; hareh bhakti-padavim — the path of *bhakti* to Sri Hari; vicinuta — seek; yadi citte na vishrambhah — if there is no faith in the heart (because the path of *bhakti* is too secret); yadi ca daurlabhyam iva — and if it seems hard to reach; tat [sarvam] ashesham parityajya — giving up all that entirely; gaura-caranam sharanam vrajata — take shelter of Gaurahari's feet.

### 80
ANVAYA: mukulita-kara-ambhoja-yugalam — the pair of lotus palms joined (in *anjali*); murdhni — on the head; urdhvam — raised upwards; dadhat — holding; galat-netra-ambhobhih — with the water flowing from the eyes; snapita-mridu-ganda-sthala-yugam — Him whose tender cheeks are bathed; nava-kamala-kinjalka-rucina — with a lustre like that of the filaments of a fresh lotus; dukulena — with a cloth; avitam — adorned; kanaka-ruci-cauram — *kanaka* — gold, *ruci* — its lustre, *caura* — the thief; that is, Him who has taken on the golden lustre; param jyotih — the supreme light; gauram — to Gaurasundara; pranamata — bow down (offer *pranama*).

### 81
ANVAYA: [he bhratah — O brother]; uccaih — loudly; gokula-pateh — of the son of the king of Vraja; uddama-nama-avalim — the series of names possessing the highest power; nama — (an ornament of speech, in the sense of assent or certainty); kirtaya — chant; yat va — or; tasya — His; divya-madhuram — radiant, full of sweetness; jagat-mangalam — bringing good to the world; rupam bhavaya — contemplate the form; yadi shri-caitanya-mahaprabhoh — if Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu's; kripa-drishtih — merciful glance; tvayi — on you; na patet — does not fall; hanta — alas; [tada — then]; prema-maha-rasa-ujjvala-pade — in the object of *ujjvala* (the radiant *rasa*), in which, because of *prema*, there is the "*mahat*," the highest *rasa*; *ujjvala* is *shringara*, "*pada*" — that is, the object; *shringara-rasa* is called *ujjvala-rasa*; te — of you; asha api na sambhavet — not even hope is possible.

### 82
ANVAYA: aye — ho! [bhratah — brother]; [shri-caitanya-pada-ashrayam vina anya-sadhaneshu — in other *sadhanas* apart from the shelter of Sri Chaitanya's feet]; sahasam na kuru — do not act rashly, that is, do not take them up hastily; [yatah — since]; hari-rasa-amrita-asvadinah — those expert in tasting the nectar of the *bhakti-rasa* of Hari, who steals the heart and its wealth, that is, of Sri Krishna; [atah eva — therefore]; paritah unmadah — those in whom intoxication, that is, the bliss of *prema*, has wholly arisen — these devotees of Gaura; tava — at your; sarva-udyamam — every kind of endeavour; hasanti — laugh; idam tu nibhritam shrinu — but hear this secret word; nigameshu — in the Vedas and other *shastras*; yat eva pranaya-vastu prastuyate — that which is set forth as the object (substance) of love; tat-patih — its master, that is, its object (*vishaya*); ayam hi — certainly this; [param — only]; gaurah — Gaura.

### 83
ANVAYA: jnana-adi-vartma-virucim — producing distaste for the paths of knowledge (*jnana* — the knowledge of Brahman without distinctions; "and the rest" — the regular and occasional duties, *adhyatma-yoga*, dry renunciation and the like); vrajanatha-bhakti-ritim — the method of worshipping Sri Krishna; na vedmi — I do not know; na ca mama sat-guravah milanti — and true gurus do not come my way; mama kah sharanam [bhavita — will be] — what is my way (who is my shelter)?; ha hanta hanta — alas, alas!; [are — ho]; vimudha — O, you are a thorough fool!; gaurah harih — Gaurahari; tava karna-patham na gatah asti — has He not entered the path of your ears? — that is, quickly bring the worship of Gaurahari into the path of your ears; delay no longer.

### 84
ANVAYA: karmasu — in the regular and occasional rites; vritha-avesham — vain absorption; apanayata — cast away; adhyatma-saraneh — *adhyatma* — the reasoning "*neti, neti*" of the adherents of the ascending path (*aroha-vadis*), *sarani* — its path; manak vartam api — even the slightest talk; kvacana — ever; karna-abhyarne api — even into the vicinity of the ears; na nayata — do not let come; deha-adau — in the body and the rest (the words "and the rest" mean home, family, country, society and all other things); moham na bhajata — do not fall into delusion; [tada — then]; parama-ashcarya-madhurah — possessing the highest, astonishing, special sweetness; puma-arthanam maulih — the crown of the goals of man, the fifth goal, *prema*; bhavatam — by you; gaura-kripaya — by Gaurasundara's mercy; milati — will be obtained (the present tense in the sense of the near future).

### 85
ANVAYA: ahaha — alas!; sada yoshit-vyaghryah trasata — always beware of the tigress, woman; (if it is asked: "since, comparing woman to a tigress, association with women has been forbidden, how then, without sacrifices and the other rites performed together with one's wife, is heaven to be attained?" — anticipating this question, he says:) vitatham — false, that is, transient; divam — heaven; trinam-manyah — considering it as insignificant as a straw; thutkuru — spit at it; [anticipating the question: "very well, let it be so; then we shall study the *shastras* on yoga and perform yoga and the rest — for by this all goals can be attained," he says:] shastra-abhyasaih alam — the study of *shastras* is in vain; [for with those who practise yoga having studied them, perfection is a distant matter; even the fall of those established in yoga is spoken of; what is the sense of such a practice, whose success is so uncertain and whose very practice is extremely painful?]; [anticipating the question: "if so, then service of the holy places is surely more pleasant? serving the *tirthas*, we shall obtain knowledge and the like, and as a result become worthy of liberation," he says:] tirtha-atanikaya [ca] alam — and what is the use of pilgrimages?; [for, first, pilgrimage is hard labour; second, since the *karmis* and *jnanis* with worldly minds have offences against the holy abode, their going to the *tirthas* is only a vain endeavour for sense gratification; what then should be done — this he now concludes:]; dhanyah — O people worthy of obtaining the wealth of *prema*; [yuyam — you]; nija-rasa-madat ambudhi-tate — from the bliss born of the *rasa* of *prema* of His own form, Krishna, on the shore of the ocean at Nilachala; natantam — dancing; sannyasi-kapatam — enacting the pastime of sannyasa; kila — certainly; gaurangam shrayata — take shelter of Gauranga.

### 86
ANVAYA: bata — alas (expresses regret); aho — ho (an address) [aho bhratarah — O brothers]; durgameshu yoga-adi-margeshu — on the hard-to-travel paths of the eightfold yoga and the rest; kim tavat viphalam [anusandhanam kriyate — is search made] — what is the use of wandering fruitlessly?; krishna-pada-ambuje bhaktim vidadhatah — performing *bhakti* to Krishna's lotus feet; sarva-artham — *prema*, the crown of all goals, or that which among all things is the only goal (*prayojana*) — the wealth of *prema*; aluntata — seize by force, plunder completely; shiva-brahma-adi-alabhye — inaccessible even to Shiva, Brahma and the rest; adbhute — wondrous; prema-maha-utsave — in the great festival of *prema*; yadi asha [vartate] — if there is hope; tada — then; durvigaha-mahima-udare gaure dhamani — to Gaura, the abode, whose glory is unfathomable and who is generous, that is, great in generosity (*maha-vadanya*); rajyatam — be filled with attachment (*anuraga*).

### 87
ANVAYA: krita-punya-rashih — a person who has performed heaps of good deeds (*sukriti*); gaura-pada-aravinde — for Sri Gaurasundara's lotus feet; yatha yatha — to the extent that; bhaktim vindeta — obtains *bhakti*; akasmat — suddenly; [tasya — his]; hridi — in the heart; tatha tatha — to that same extent; radha-pada-ambhoja-sudha-ambu-rashih — the ocean of the nectar of Sri Radha's lotus feet; utsarpati — surges up.

### 88
ANVAYA: caitanya-caranah — Chaitanya's feet; hi — since; brahma-isha-arcitah — are worshipped even by Shiva and Brahma; aparasya — of the shoreless; prema-ujjvala-rasa-rahasya-amrita-nidheh — of the ocean of nectar which is the secret of the radiant *rasa* of *prema*; nidhanam — the receptacle; atah — therefore; kritinah — those possessing *sukriti*, that is, people who accept the essence (*saragrahi*); iha — in this age of Kali; pranaya-bharatah — in the abundance of love; tam — those feet of Chaitanya; dhyayantah — contemplating; tam eva — of them alone; sharanam yantu — let them seek shelter; pronmattah [santah] — filled with the greatest joy; tam — the glory of His lotus feet; kila — certainly; iha — in this age; gayantu — let them sing.

### 89
ANVAYA: dante trinakam nidhaya — taking a tiny straw between the teeth (this indicates the humility "lower than grass" of the preacher of *kirtana*, the *tridandi-gosvami*: for of the grasses that the people of the world trample underfoot, he holds between his teeth the smallest straw, that is, shows respect even to it); padayoh nipatya — falling at the feet (this indicates the quality of "*amani*" — freedom from the desire for respect — of the preacher of the message of Gaura, the *tridandi-gosvami*: unlike the *brahma-sannyasi*, he is not intoxicated with pride and does not consider himself "Narayana," and, unlike the *smartas*, he is not deprived of the vision of the true nature (*svarupa*) of all beings and is not fettered by worldly judgements about the gross and subtle body — brahmana or *candala*, senior or junior; therefore, having become "*amani*" before all, he strives to proclaim the truth); kaku-shatam kritva ca — with a hundred plaintive entreaties (this indicates the "tolerance like a tree" of the preacher of *kirtana*, the *tridandi-gosvami*: the people of the world, turned away from Krishna, do not at all wish to hear the truth, but the *tridandi-gosvami*, entreating them, [tries] to make them hear *hari-katha*); etat aham bravimi — I say this; he sadhavah — O *sadhus* (this indicates the quality of "*manada*" — giving respect to others — of the preacher of *kirtana*, the *tridandi-gosvami*); sakalam eva vihaya durat — leaving everything from afar (you are, of course, *sadhus*, but everything on account of which you have in your heart the pride "I am a *sadhu*" or the fame of a *sadhu* in the world — *karma*, *jnana*, Sankhya and yoga, *phalgu-vairagya*, the four goals, the performance of *dharma* and *adharma*, and all good and bad endeavours or desires opposed to the gratification of Krishna's senses — leaving that from afar); caitanya-candra-carane — for the feet of the Sri Chaitanya-moon; anuragam kuruta — be filled with attachment.

### 90
ANVAYA: [if it is asked: "why, leaving liberation, *bhakti* (*vaidhi-bhakti*, full of majesty) and the rest, become attached to Gaura's lotus feet?" — anticipating this question, he says]; aho — oh!; muktih na durlabha — liberation is not hard to obtain (the four kinds of *mukti* — *salokya* and the rest — are not hard to attain, for they are easily obtained by *vaidhi-bhakti* mixed with *jnana*); bhaktih ca na sudurlabha — and *bhakti* is not extremely hard to attain (for by *vaidhi-sadhana* service to Narayana in Vaikuntha is obtained); gaura-candra-prasadah tu — but the favour of Gaurachandra, that is, the tasting of the extremely secret nectar of *prema*, manifested by Gaura's mercy; vaikunthe api sudurlabhah — is extremely hard to attain even in Vaikuntha.

### 91
ANVAYA: caitanya-pada-aravindam bhajantu — take shelter of the lotus feet of Sri Chaitanyadeva; sat-bhakti-rasena ca purnah bhavantu — and be filled with the highest *rasa* of *bhakti*, that is, be immersed in the *unnata-ujjvala-rasa*, never given before (*anarpita-cari*) (this is possible only in the shelter of Chaitanya's feet); madhurya-saubhagya-daya-kshama-adyaih — *madhurya* — by a sweet nature, *saubhagya* — by the highest tasting of *prema*, *daya* — by the distribution of this *prema* to all living beings, *kshama* — by humility "lower than grass" and the other Vaishnava qualities; tri-jagat — the three worlds; vicitram — in manifold ways; anandayantu — gladden.

### 92
ANVAYA: yadi samsara-sindhu-tarane hridayam syat — if someone has the desire to cross the ocean of worldly existence (that is, the desire, having crossed *samsara*, to plunge "into the ocean of *bhakti*"); manah cet sankirtana-amrita-rase ramate — if the mind wishes to delight in the sweetness of the nectar *rasa* of *sankirtana*; yadi prema-ambudhau viharane citta-vrittih [syat] — if the mind inclines to play in the ocean of the *rasa* of *prema*; [tada — then]; caitanya-candra-carane sharanam prayatu — let him take shelter of the feet of the Chaitanya-moon.

### 93
ANVAYA: jnana-vairagya-bhakti-adi — knowledge, renunciation, *bhakti* and the rest; yatha-tatha — in whatever way; sadhayantu — let them practise (or produce); caitanya-candra-carana-ambhoja-bhakti-labhya-samam — like that special knowledge, renunciation and *bhakti* which are obtained by serving the lotus feet of Chaitanya-chandra, that is, what the devotees of Chaitanya-chandra's feet possess — the knowledge of the truth of the all-embracing sweetness of Svayam Bhagavan Sri Krishna, […] detachment and the *unnata-ujjvala bhakti-rasa*; kutah — where else? (that is, it is found nowhere else).

### 94
ANVAYA: idam — this visible; acaitanyam — devoid of consciousness, that is, devoid of realization of one's own *svarupa*, or devoid of *bhakti* for the all-pervading Consciousness (*vibhu-caitanya*); vishvam — whole world; amara-uttamaih — by the best of the gods; upasyam — worshipped; caitanyam ishvaram — the Lord Chaitanya, that is, Chaitanya who rules all; yadi na bhajet — if it does not worship; [tada — then]; sarvatah mrityuh — death in every respect; [bhavati — there is] (in the *anuvada*: death of every kind, that is, subject to birth and death, they taste the fruits of *karma* — happiness, misery and the like).

### 95
ANVAYA: caitanyasya mahaprabhoh — of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu; pada-dvandve — in the two feet; yasya asha — one who has hope; tasya — to him; indrah [api] — even Indra; dasavat bhati — appears as a servant; [anye — of others]; nripa-kitake — of a king who is like an insignificant worm; ka katha — what to speak.

### 96
ANVAYA: yasya arthinah — of the petitioner who; krishna-caitanye asha [vartate] — has the hope of obtaining Sri Krishna-Chaitanya; [tasya — of him]; nripa-dvari — at the door of a king; kim — what need?; cintamanim prapya — having obtained a *cintamani*; kah mudhah — who is such a fool; rajatam vrajet — that he would go (to distant lands) for silver?

### 97
ANVAYA: giri-kandareshu — in mountain caves; bahavah — many people; [dhyayantah] — meditating on the impersonal Brahman; brahma anubhuya — having realized Brahman; asate — abide; ca — and; mahi-mandale — in the circle of the earth; yoga-abhyasa-parah — devoted to the practice of the eightfold yoga; bahavah siddhah — many who have obtained *siddhis* (*anima* and the rest); santi — there are; vidya-shaurya-dhana-adibhih ca — and with learning, strength, wealth and the like; mithya-uddhatah — intoxicated with unreal things; bahavah — many; jalpanti — indulge in idle talk; [kintu — but]; gaura-kripam vina — without Gaura's mercy; jagati — in the world; kah va — who indeed; adya — today; prema-unmadah [san — being] — intoxicated with *prema*; nrityati — dances?

### 98
ANVAYA: yadi devadevah — if He who is worshipped even by the gods; sah gaurah — that Gaurasundara; kripayate — is merciful; [tada — then]; kashi-vasin api — even the residents of Kashi (that is, those who have settled in Kashi for the sake of undifferentiated liberation); na ganaye — I count as nothing; kim gayam margayamah — why seek Gaya? (that is, Gaura's devotee, unlike the *karmis* seeking fruits, does not bustle about the *shraddha* at Gaya in order to offer *pindas* to the ancestors); yadi me muktih — if for me even liberation; shukti bhavati — becomes a shell; [tada — then]; kah para-artha-prasangah — what to speak of the fruits of the three other goals and the rest!; maha-raurave api — even in the great hell Raurava; [yadi — if]; trasa-abhasah na sphurati — there is not a shadow of fear; [tada — then]; stri-putra-adi — with regard to wife, sons and the rest; kva bhitih — where is fear?
