### 101
THEME: The supreme excellence of the beauty, gladdening power, loving tenderness, generosity, depth and sweetness of Krishna clothed in the mood and lustre of Radha.
He who in beauty is millions of Kamadevas, in gladdening all living beings millions of moons, in loving tenderness millions of mothers, in the height of generosity millions of wish-fulfilling trees, in depth millions of oceans, in sweetness millions of essences of nectar, millions of [milk drinks] and millions of essences of honey, and who in the field of *shringara-rasa* displays millions of wonders (variety of *rasa*) — may that Gaurahari, who sports in pastimes, be glorious!
From the word-for-word gloss: His beauty puts to shame countless Madanas, and His refreshing power the coolness of countless moons; "*madhvika*" — an intoxicating drink made from honey; the word "*sudha*" (nectar) here indicates immortality and an unprecedented taste, the word "*kshira*" (milk) satisfaction and [nourishment], the word "*madhvika*" the madness of love; the *pranaya-rasa* manifested by Sri Gaurasundara is millions of times more wondrous than the sweetness of *shringara-rasa* shown by the former connoisseurs of *rasa*.

### 102
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, who displays […] that astonish even Shiva, Brahma and the others.
May Bhagavan Sri Gaurahari Himself be glorious in supreme excellence — He who manifested the most wondrous glory of the devotees abiding in *sadhana-bhakti*, the means to obtain the highest wave of *prema-bhakti* for His lotus feet, and of the devotees who, immersed in *mahabhava*, dance wondrously as if mad, causing the greatest astonishment even to Shiva, Brahma and the others.
From the word-for-word gloss: "Prabhu" — the independent Lord.

### 103
THEME: Description of Gaurahari's supremacy through the display of His great might, radiance, beauty, coolness, the sweetness of His gait and […].
Possessing a voice deep as the roar of millions of intoxicated lions; bearing a radiance brighter than that of millions of suns; cooler than millions of moons; with a gait more beautiful than the stride of millions of maddened king-elephants; delivering millions of the distressed by the *sankirtana* of the names "Hare Krishna" and the rest; the master of millions of Brahmas and others; worshipped by millions of *advaita-vadis*, the highest summit [of the realization] of the impersonal Brahman — Parabrahman, that is, the embodiment of the complete realization of *advaya-jnana* (non-dual knowledge) — may Sri Sri Shachinandana be especially glorious!
From the word-for-word gloss: "by the name" — by the *maha-mantra* "Hare Krishna" and so on, or by the names "Krishna-Chaitanya," "Vishvambhara" and the rest; "the crown of millions of Advaitas" — Parabrahman, the highest summit of the impersonal perception of Brahman, that is, the condensed Parabrahman in human form.

### 104
THEME: The lamp of Gaura, which destroys […] darkness and illumines the path of tasting the secret *rasa* of *prema*.
The path of *bhakti* is inaccessible to the material intellect and devoid of the external pomp of *karma* and the like; alas, it is blocked by the thorns of dry knowledge and attachment to *karma* and is therefore extremely hard to travel; it misleads, making one take the false for the true, and at once brings a stream of the *rasa* of the bliss of *prema*. Glory to the indescribable Purusha, the lamp of Navadvipa full of tender love, who, instantly illumining that path of *bhakti*, destroys the darkness of ignorance in the cave of the heart and manifests the glory of *bhakti*!
From the word-for-word gloss: "far" — inaccessible to the material intellect; "[far from] emptiness" — devoid of the gross material aspect of the endeavours of *karma* and the like; "misleads by false aims" — that is, makes even the wise (*suris*) consider false *bhakti*, pretended *bhakti* and the like to be "*bhakti*"; "*snehavan*" — full of love, and with reference to a lamp "*sneha*" means oil.

### 105
THEME: The lamp of Navadvipa, ever burning with a bright flame.
That unearthly (*aprakrita*) lamp which even from afar burns the moths of false logic; which is cooler than millions of moons and is the abode of a mass of light; supremely sweet; destroying darkness outer and inner; from whose wick of the heart, soaked in the oil of love (*sneha*), a divine radiance issues; whose splendour is like gold — that lamp of Navadvipa (Gaurasundara) shines in this world out of mercy.
From the word-for-word gloss: the words of the verse have a double meaning — "*sneha*" means both love and ghee or other oil; "*ashaya*" — both the heart and a vessel; "*varti*" — a wick; that is, His radiance issues when the wick of the heart, which abides together with the oil of love, is kindled.

### 106
THEME: Gaurahari, adorned with the *vibhavas* and the rest and the *sattvika-bhavas*.
In extreme agitation from joy and sorrow making the ten directions resound with loud cries, with waves of the radiance of pealing loud laughter making the vault of the sky bright as if with full-blown *kunda* and *kumuda* flowers, possessing a body trembling like an *ashvattha* tree shaken by the wind — intoxicated with joy, pride and the other states born of the *rasa* of *prema*, Gaurahari, dancing the *uddanda* (with high leaps), abides in supreme excellence.
From the word-for-word gloss: "cries" — sounds uttered with extreme longing from joy and sorrow; "*apluta-gati*" — among the various kinds of movement, the *uddanda-nritya*, a particular dance in which, intoxicated with the joy and pride born of the *rasa* of *prema*, He dances leaping high.

### 107
THEME: Gaurachandra, risen in the ocean of Shachi's womb and pouring the nectar of *prema*.
Spotless and ever risen, dancing wondrously, devoid of dimness and crookedness, pouring the nectar of *prema* to remove the threefold miseries of all living beings, the indescribable Sri Gaurachandra, the sweetness of whose rays the *cakora* birds of the devotees' hearts taste (that is, the sweetness of His rays the *cakoras* of the devotees' hearts taste), having risen from the milk ocean of the womb of the fortunate and most beautiful Sri Shachidevi, shines.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*nirdosha*" — without a spot, or "He who has no need of night (*dosha*) or darkness," that is, ever shining.

### 108
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, immersed in the mood of Srimati Radhika, overwhelmed by the prolonged *rasa* of separation (*vipralambha*).
He who, immersed in the ocean of the nectar of the *mahabhava* of Srimati Radhika, suffering in Vraja from separation from Krishna, again and again wets with tears the edges of His pale cheeks; ah! — He who, every moment heaving long sighs, weeps aloud and utters the sounds "ha! ha!", full of an extremely piteous *rasa* — such an indescribable Purusha, Sri Gaurasundara, manifesting His mood, abides in supreme excellence.

### 109
THEME: Sri Gaurahari — the united body [of Radha and Krishna].
Assuming a certain indescribable colour like the essence of molten gold that has passed through fire, He displays the pastimes of Bala-Gopala, and sometimes, in a certain indescribable spiritual form, reveals the most wondrous pastimes of youth (*kaishora*) — Sri Gauranga shines, the visible united body of Radha and Madhava.
From the word-for-word gloss: as Srila Svarupa Gosvami also says, Radha and Krishna are a transformation of love, [a manifestation] of the *hladini* potency; in their essence They are one, but since the *tattva* of pastimes is eternal, They eternally abide in two forms; now these two have appeared in one form — as the *tattva* of Chaitanya.

### 110
THEME: The glory of the Gaura-*avatara*, who descended together with the name of Krishna, which melts […] hard [hearts].
When He descended into the world of men, ah, even for embodied beings surrounded by heaps of grievous sins a wave of the name of Sri Krishna suddenly appeared, and even the heart grown hard as stone through offences became soft and melted like fresh butter — may that Gaurasundara be my only refuge.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the wave of the name of Bhagavan" — "Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna" and so on, the sequence of holy names; the heart had grown hard through *karma*, *jnana* and the like, or through various *anarthas*.

### 111
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara* [even] those devoid of *sadhana* and given to forbidden conduct [obtain] […] and the bliss of *prema*.
When the supremely merciful Sri Chaitanyadeva suddenly descended into this world, even a man who had neither yoga, nor meditation, nor *japa*, nor austerity, nor renunciation, nor vows, nor study of the Vedas, nor good conduct — alas, who had not even given up sinful and other deeds — even such a man in great joy plunders the highest *prema*, the crown of the goals of human life!

### 112
THEME: The glory of the Gaura-*avatara*, which melts even the hard hearts of the *smartas* and yogis immersed in *karma*.
When Sri Chaitanyadeva, possessing wondrous splendour, descended to earth, the mind of the people of *karma*, though fallen into the stream of great *karma*, became steady, having obtained Gaura's [love], and even the mind harder than a huge rock melted in the *rasa* of *bhakti*. Even the mind of those whose consciousness was directed to the *sadhana* of the great yoga and the like, turning away from the *sadhana* of yoga and the like (connected with the senses), dances high, that is, tastes *prema* in the kingdom of the spiritual pastimes of Adhokshaja.

### 113
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara*, people attached to worldly things, people of *karma*, *jnanis* and yogis, each leaving his own *dharma*, became intoxicated with the *rasa* of *prema*.
When Sri Chaitanya-chandra revealed the path of the highest *bhakti-yoga*, people immersed in the taste of material objects gave up talk of wives, sons and the like; the *panditas* gave up disputes and quarrels about the *shastras*; the best of yogis entirely abandoned the torments of *sadhana* for the restraint of the life-air; the ascetics gave up their austerities; the [sannyasis] gave up the search for the undifferentiated Brahman; and then no "*rasa*" other than *bhakti-rasa* was to be seen in the world.
From the word-for-word gloss: "the highest path of *bhakti-yoga*" — the path of *raganuga* worship; "the wise" — philosophers, experts in poetics (*alankarikas*), logicians (*naiyayikas*) and the like; "the torments of restraining the air" — the hardships of *sadhana* in holding the inhalation and exhalation by *pranayama*, *kumbhaka* and so on; "*yatis*" — sannyasis, adherents of the knowledge of the undifferentiated [Brahman]; neither the worldly *rasa* of those attached to objects, nor the worldly *rasa* of the *shastras* of the *panditas* — the *alankarikas*, philosophers and the rest — nor the *rasa* of Paramatma of the yogis, nor the *rasa* of the impersonal Brahman of the seekers of undifferentiated knowledge — none seemed "*rasa*" beside the *rasa* of *advaya-jnana* (the non-dual Absolute Truth), for beside the wondrous *bhakti-rasa* produced by the union of the four ingredients — *rati*, that is, the inclination towards Bhagavan, or the *sthayi-bhava*, together with the *vibhavas*, *anubhavas*, *sattvika[-bhavas]* and *vyabhichari[-bhavas]* — a worldly or partial *rasa* cannot be called "*rasa*" at all.

### 114
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara*, the preaching of the elevated *ujjvala-rasa*, hidden in the Vedas, and the attainment of *prema* by all people.
When Sri Gaurachandra descended into the world, in every house rose the noise of a tumultuous Hari-*sankirtana*, in every body shone abundant thrills, tears and the other [*sattvika-bhavas*], and with the growing intensification of the density of *prema-bhakti* even the highest, sweetest, best stage, inaccessible to the *shrutis*, was revealed.
From the word-for-word gloss: *sneha* is matured *prema*; "*amnaya*" — the *shrutis*, the Vedas.

### 115
THEME: The glory of the Chaitanya-*avatara*, which flooded the world with a deluge of the *rasa* of *prema*.
When Bhagavan Sri Krishna Himself, the attractor of all hearts, descended, assuming a charming golden lustre, this whole world was suddenly and entirely flooded by the deluge of *rasa* from the ocean of great *prema* and came to the greatest astonishment at transformations of *prema* never seen or heard of before.

### 116
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara* even those who think themselves learned, [the people of *karma*] and the ascetics obtain *prema*.
Some people, full of irrepressible pride, collected [the knowledge of] all the *shastras* from every side, that is, thought: "I know all the *shastras*; there is no one better than I." Others considered themselves blessed. Of these people, who thought they had attained the goal and stood on the higher and lower rungs of the paths of the regular and occasional rites enjoined by the *smritis*, and of austerity, Sankhya, yoga and the like, some only two or three times chanted the names of Hari, yet their hearts remained full of duplicity. So it was before; but now, when Gaurachandra has descended, *prema* too has become common property, that is, all are endowed with it, from the highest to the lowest.
From the word-for-word gloss: "collected all the *shastras*" — or else they prided themselves: "We have studied all the *shastras*; who is above us?"; "with crooked hearts" — devoted to the path of *karma*.

### 117
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara*, children, the old, […], the blind, the deaf and others become intoxicated with the *rasa* of *prema*.
When Sri Chaitanyadeva, the Purusha of pastimes, service to whose lotus feet the demigods long for, descended into this world and spread far and wide the universal, sweetest wave of the nectar of *prema* — whether child or old man, woman or dull-witted, or a wretched low person — all in this world gained the capacity to obtain *bhakti*, and in each awoke for the feet of Sri Hari a certain unprecedented, wondrous *rasa* of *advaya-jnana* (the non-dual Absolute Truth).
From the word-for-word gloss: "*deva*" — the Purusha of pastimes, or He who, accepting Radhika's mood, wishes to taste His own sweetness.

### 118
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara*, the appearance of […] the demigods and the eternal associates of Bhagavan.
When Bhagavan Gaurachandra Himself, the crown of the connoisseurs of the *rasa* of *prema*, descended to earth, all came — Shankara, Narada and the others (in the form of the devotees Advaita, Srivasa and the rest). Lakshmi Herself appeared (as Sri Lakshmipriya and Sri Vishnupriya). Baladeva — His manifest form, non-different from Bhagavan Himself — was present (in the form of Nityananda Raya, the crusher of the godless). The Yadavas too appeared (in Shachi, Jagannatha and others); what more is there to say — Nanda and the other residents of Vraja, the servants Raktaka, Citraka and the rest, the friends headed by Subala and the *shaktis* headed by the *gopis* — all the eternally perfect associates of Krishna's pastimes descended into Gaura's pastimes.
From the word-for-word gloss: the words "and the rest" also indicate Yogamaya and the other *shaktis*.

### 119
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara* the eternal associates obtain a greater bliss of *prema* than before.
When Sri Gaurasundara, shining like molten gold, was distributing His unearthly *prema* on earth, the servants, the friends and the beloveds — the eternally perfect maidservants of *madhura-rasa* alone — all of them, having descended to His lotus feet, obtained a wealth of the nectar of great *prema* surpassing their former (in Krishna's pastimes) tasting of *prema*.
From the word-for-word gloss: "exceedingly sweet" — of a sweetness free from the idea of majesty (*aishvarya*); "*projjvala*" — of the elevated *ujjvala[-rasa]*; "*udara*" — charming; that is, the beloveds who serve exclusively the sweet, elevated, charming *ujjvala-rasa*.

### 120
THEME: Description of the essence of the glory of the Gaura-*avatara* through the attainment of *prema* even by the hard-hearted, those given to evil sense objects, and the ignorant.
When the supremely unearthly Sri Chaitanya, endowed with the greatest glory, descended to earth, even noble married women (leaving shame, in love for Krishna) laugh aloud; even hearts hard as the stone of evil objects serving sense gratification melt completely; even the ignorant, devoid of knowledge of the truth (having obtained knowledge of the truth by Chaitanya's mercy), put to shame the whole society of the knowers of the *shastras* (that is, censure the *shastric* knowledge of those who think themselves *panditas*, skilled in the lower knowledge).

### 121
THEME: Before the Chaitanya-*avatara* people in general did not obtain the elevated *ujjvala-rasa*.
Before Chaitanya's appearance in this world, even in the knowers of all the *shastras* who thought themselves *panditas*, the function of consciousness — service to Krishna — was almost covered. They did not aspire to *krishna-prema*, the crown of all the goals of human life, since their intelligence was extremely petty and inclined to doubt. But now that Sri Gaurachandra has mercifully descended into the world, who has not entered the inconceivable *prema-bhakti*, full of the elevated *ujjvala-madhura-rasa* and nourished by the most wondrous ingredients — *vibhava*, *anubhava* and the rest!
From the word-for-word gloss: "*caitanya*" — the function of consciousness, service to Krishna; "the essence of all goals" — the fifth goal of human life, *krishna-prema*, the essence of the four goals (*caturvarga*); "*bhava*" — *vibhava* and *anubhava*; "*ujjvala-rasa*" — *shringara-rasa*.

### 122
THEME: The Gaura-*avatara* for the sake of revealing the meaning of the *Srimad-Bhagavatam* and the *rasa* of Radha.
Even the son of Vyasa, Srila Shukadeva, in the account of the *rasa-lila* only touched on the secret meaning of the *Srimad-Bhagavatam* without describing it in detail, for it cannot be grasped by the study of the *shastras* alone. To reveal two things — this meaning of the *Srimad-Bhagavatam* and the one best vessel for tasting the sweetness of the *rasa-lila* and the other pastimes of Sri Krishna, the crown of the supreme connoisseurs of *rasa*, in the love-sports of the *nikunja* — Sri Krishna descended into this world in the body of Gaura.
From the word-for-word gloss: the highest meaning of the *Bhagavatam* is *prema*, the highest meaning of the *rasa* of the pastimes of Sri Radha and Krishna; "because of the difficulty of comprehension" — that is, because there was no vessel able to taste the *rasa* of these pastimes and know their truth; the "*rati-keli*" of Srimati Radhika — the love-sports in the *nikunja*; "*nagara*" — Sri Krishna, the crown of the supreme connoisseurs of *rasa*.

### 123
THEME: At the Gaura-*avatara* all obtain the wealth of *prema* in *dasya*, *sakhya* and the other [*rasas*], and [the most chosen] service to Radha.
What riches did people not obtain by the mercy of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu? Of the devotees headed by Uddhava (all the devotees of Krishna's pastimes headed by Uddhava descended into Gaura's pastimes), some obtained the Vraja mood of servants, like that of Raktaka, Patraka and others; others the praiseworthy position of confidential friends, like that of Shridama and others; others again obtained the mood of the *gopis* of Vraja; and still others, the most blessed and wise, taste [the sweetness of] the lotus feet of Sri Radha.

### 124
THEME: The difference between the doctrines born of speculation and preached by the sages, and the religion of *bhakti* preached [by Gaura] — the real truth; the *prema-bhakti* preached by Gaura is the highest goal established by the Vedas.
Although the omniscient best of sages expounded their doctrines [on the basis of] arguments, formerly no one was firmly convinced by these partial arguments. But now that Sri Gaurachandra, incomparable in power, has risen, who has not again become convinced that *hari-bhakti* alone is the highest goal established by the Vedas?
From the word-for-word gloss: that is, although the mutually contradictory doctrines of the sages, born of speculation, were established by casuistic arguments and the like, their very authors doubted their doctrines; but the eternal religion of *bhakti* preached by the Eternal Purusha, Bhagavan Sri Gaurasundara Himself, is the real truth established by the *shrutis*, free from all deceit.

### 125
THEME: The glory of Gaurahari, the ocean of the nectar of the *rasa* of *prema*, who immerses the whole world [in the ocean of *prema*].
I remember the indescribable, wondrous power of the radiance of the moonstones of Gaurachandra's toenails, which immerses the whole world in the supreme nectar ocean of *mahapranaya*.
From the word-for-word gloss: as *prema* matures, *sneha*, *mana* and *pranaya* arise; with the growth of *pranaya*, *raga*, *anuraga*, *bhava* and [*mahabhava*] gradually arise; therefore "*mahapranaya*" means *prema* in the form of *mahabhava*.

### 126
THEME: Sri Gaurahari is the only one who has flooded the world with a deluge of *prema*.
Through the ancient great personalities, distinguished by good conduct and the highest righteousness, some people, reaching Vaikuntha and other worlds, obtained the fulfilment of their goal; but as Sri Chaitanya-chandra has immersed the whole world in the ocean of *prema*, no one did before.

### 127
THEME: The glory of the mercy of Gaurahari, who gives *prema* to the unworthy without waiting to be asked.
Even possessing incomparable firmness in *dharma* and having wholly taken shelter of the highest *bhakti* to Vishnu, people live on earth with hearts hard as iron; (but by the mercy of Sri Gaurahari) ah! even a man more sinful than a cow-killer (wholly turning away from sinful tendencies) floods the world with streams of tears. Ah! Who can comprehend the inscrutable play of the golden Sri Gaurangasundara!

### 128
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, who astonishes the world by the pastimes of childhood, *pauganda* and the rest and by manifesting the mood of Sri Radhika, immersed in the *rasa* of separation.
Sri Gaurasundara, possessing vast and inscrutable power, astonishes the universe. Because of His absorption in Sri Krishna, He now, manifesting a child's [mood], crawls on His knees, now, displaying the nature of a cowherd boy, now, enacting many poses, dances, and now, seized by the mood of Sri Radha in separation from Krishna, weeps with the pain born of the suffering of separation: "Hari! Hari!! Hari!!!"
From the word-for-word gloss: "*gambhira*" — unfathomable by logic, or hard to penetrate.

### 129
THEME: Devotion to Gaurahari, who immerses the world in the elevated *ujjvala-rasa*.
That form of Gaurahari which by the power of its own radiance, even in childhood games, is the only enchanter of the universe that has taken shelter of Him, and which immerses this world [that has taken] shelter in the pastime manifesting through the essence of the former *bhava* of the sweetness of Sri Krishna, the crown of the connoisseurs of *rasa*, the receptacle of the *rasa* of Sri Varshabhanavi, the best of the young [cowherd girls] (that is, through the essence of the supreme wondrous *prema* of Srimati Radhika, the *ashraya-vigraha*, for Krishna, the *vishaya-vigraha*) — that is, in the pastime of the divine madness of Krishna, the king of *rasa*, full of *mahabhava*, in the mood of Radha — that wondrous form of molten gold on the shore of the salt ocean is pleasing to my heart.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*nava-ballavi*" — the young Sri Radhika, the best of the *gopis*.

### 130
THEME: Sri Gaurahari is the only one who revealed *ujjvala-rasa*, the sweetness of Vrindavana and the glory of Radha.
Who had ever heard of the highest goal of human life called "*prema*"? Who knew the glory of the holy name? Who had penetrated the impenetrable mass of the great sweetnesses of the forest of Vrindavana? Who knew (as the object of worship) Sri Varshabhanavi, the highest limit of the sweetness of the most wondrous *adhirudha-mahabhava*? Chaitanya-chandra alone, displaying the pastime of supreme generosity, revealed all this.
From the word-for-word gloss: the "*vipina*" of Vrindavana — thickets hard to penetrate; Radha — Srimati Radhika, in whom alone the highest stage […] of the divine madness full of unearthly service to Krishna is manifest in its fullness; the word "*candra*" (moon) indicates the destruction of the darkness of duplicity and […]; Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakura, quoting this verse in the conclusion of the fifth chapter of the book *Sri Mahaprabhu-shiksha*, wrote: "The nectarean *prema* was brought into this world by Sri Chaitanyadeva alone; no one brought it before; to show this Srila Prabodhananda Sarasvati has presented this verse."

### 131
THEME: […], beyond the reach of logic, […], the wondrously dancing Supreme Brahman, Sri Gaurahari.
He who has covered the universe with millions of rays of His golden lustre, set trembling by the waves of the ocean of the nectar of the *rasa* of perfect *prema*, and whom the wise — Shiva, Brahma, Vyasa and the others — incessantly praise — that wondrous Supreme Brahman, Sri Gaurasundara, manifesting a world-enchanting form hidden from millions of *shrutis*, Himself dances on the shore of the salt ocean.
From the word-for-word gloss: "Ishvara" — Maheshvara, the god of gods; "Vidhi" — Brahma.

### 132
THEME: Sri Gaura-Krishna, dancing to the *sankirtana* of His own names.
Who is this golden Purusha, the supreme *nagara*, the crown of the supreme connoisseurs of *rasa*, who, clothed in silk at the waist, with bracelets on His arms, a necklace on His chest, earrings in His ears, ankle-bells on His feet and a crown of full-blown garlands of jasmine (*mallika*) on His high-bound curling hair, plays, dancing to the *kirtana* of His names?
From the word-for-word gloss: "Gaura" — golden in colour; "*nagara-vara*" — the crown of the supreme connoisseurs of *rasa*; that is, Gaura is Krishna Himself, the crown of the connoisseurs of *rasa*, enacting the pastime on the side of the *ashraya*.

### 133
THEME: Gaurahari's dance gives joy and wonder to the gods, the Gandharvas and the Siddhas.
When Sri Gaurachandra, maddened with His own *prema*, began His dance on earth, the demigods began to beat the *dundubhis*, the chief Gandharvas began *sankirtana*, the Siddhas covered the earth with an incessant rain of flowers, and hosts of great *rishis*, skilled in reciting charming hymns, began lovingly to offer praise.

### 134
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, maddened by *mahabhava*.
Immersed in the nectar *rasa* of *mahabhava*, Sri Gaurahari now laughs, now weeps, now loses consciousness, now rolls on the ground, now runs swiftly and now dances again, now heaves long sighs and now utters loud cries of "ha! ha!" — (thus, in various states) He sports in this world.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*mahapranaya*" — *mahabhava*; "*sidhu*" — the nectar *rasa*.

### 135
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, who gives rise to […] attachment to the service of Radha.
Sri Krishna Himself, having accepted the *bhava* on the side of the *ashraya*, in the form of Gauranga sits on the shore of the salt ocean, clothed in saffron garments, and, resting on His palm His cheek, slightly pale like a *badari* fruit (which indicates *vaivarnya*, change of colour), turning the ground before Him into mud with streams of tears, spreads *rati* for Radha's lotus feet.
From the word-for-word gloss: "*shona*" — red; "*amshuka*" — garment; in the *shastras* it is said that Srimati Radhika too wears garments of a red colour — the colour of *anuraga* for Krishna.

### 136
THEME: Sri Gaurahari, dancing the *uddanda* and displaying […] and many other *anubhavas*.
Ah! What Purusha of pastimes is this, shining golden like the moon, in charming saffron garments hanging from His beautiful waist, who dances in the flowering garden on the shore of the salt ocean, making the ten directions resound with the sound of the stamping of His feet, turning the ground into mud with drops of tears and making the sky white with peals of loud laughter?
From the word-for-word gloss: "the strokes of the feet" — stamping in the dance; "in joy" — He dances with bliss.

### 137
THEME: The *prema* which the Upanishads and the rest seek and which is hard to comprehend even for the four *sampradayas* — of Sri, Brahma, Rudra and [Sanaka] — is easily accessible to Gaura's devotees.
He whose essence even the Upanishads — the jewelled necklace on the crown of all the *shrutis* — cannot fully indicate; He whose never-before-given, supremely relishable path is hard to comprehend even for Sri, Brahma, Rudra and the others — that is, the path of the elevated *ujjvala-prema* is unknown not only to the founders of the *sampradayas* of Sri, Brahma, Rudra and Sanaka but even to their original gurus — but which is very easy [to walk] for those who have received His merciful glance, that is, which is very accessible to Gaura's devotees; He who suddenly intoxicated this world with the wine of the *rasa* of the *prema* of Sri Radha and Krishna — will that Chaitanya-chandra, radiating supreme beauty, become accessible to my words and mind?
From the word-for-word gloss: "Sri" — Lakshmi, the original guru of the [Sri-]*sampradaya*, worshipped by Sri Ramanujacharya, the teacher of *vishishta-advaita*; "Isha" — Rudra, worshipped by Sri Vishnusvami, the teacher of *shuddha-advaita*; "Brahma" — the original guru of the Brahma-*sampradaya*, worshipped by Sriman Madhvacharya, the teacher of *shuddha-dvaita*; "and the rest" — Sanaka, Sanandana and the others, the original gurus of the Catuhsana-*sampradaya*, worshipped by Nimbaditya; "Sri-Hari": "Sri" — Srimati Radhika, the embodiment of all the Lakshmis, "Hari" — Krishna.

### 138
THEME: With the concealment of Gaurahari's pastimes, the path of *bhakti* again fell into disorder.
O Sri Chaitanya! Where have You gone! Your pure, secret path of the *bhakti* of the elevated *ujjvala-rasa* is no longer seen in any *sampradaya*. In one *sampradaya* there is the inertness of *karma*, in another austerity, *japa*, yoga and the like, in a third the rules of the worship of Govinda on the path of *arcana*, in a fourth *bhakti* mixed with knowledge, and somewhere even *ujjvala-bhakti* abides only in words, without practice.
From the word-for-word gloss: that is, no one practises *bhakti* in deed.

### 139
THEME: The heartache of the devotees at the concealment of Gaurahari's pastimes.
At the time when Sri Gaurahari, shining like molten gold, became visible in this world, by His power the earth was immersed in the *rasa* of *prema*, and a tumultuous Krishna-*sankirtana* resounded loudly. Alas! Will that sweet time ever return?

### 140
THEME: Separation from Gaura at the sight of […], Sri Navadvipa, the holy name of Sri Hari and the other things that awaken the mood of Gaurahari.
On earth there is the same blessed city of Gauda, the same shore of the ocean with its gardens and groves, the same Sri Purushottama, and the same names of Sri Krishna exist — "Hare Krishna" and the rest. Hari! Hari! But nowhere is that festival of the bliss of *prema* to be seen. O Chaitanya, O treasury of mercy, will my eyes behold Your splendour again?
From the word-for-word gloss: Sri Purushottama — Sri Jagannathadeva; "Hari! Hari!" — a cry of grief.

### 141
THEME: Gaurahari is not a partial *avatara* but the complete Bhagavan Himself, the son of the lord of Vraja.
If you say that Gaurachandra is like the partial *avataras* — the Fish and the others of whom the *shrutis* speak — in fact He is not so, for the partial *avataras*, Matsya and the others, are only the manifestation of some particular potency and pastime. But this Gaurachandra, whose greatness is unfathomed, displaying a wondrous pastime endowed with the incomparable fullness of all potencies, has certainly descended in fullness.

### 142
THEME: Bhagavan Gaurahari Himself is more primeval even than Shiva, Brahma and the rest, the possessor of inconceivable power.
Sriman Mahaprabhu, whose might, having neither equal nor superior, is more wondrous even than Sri Narayana — the original cause of Shiva and Brahma — on hearing these words befitting a foolish child, will surely become affectionate, that is, will mercifully bestow His tender love on me.

### 143
THEME: The only thing worth praying for is Gaurahari's mercy.
I have not seen [the *shastras*], I have not received instruction from many teachers, I have not discussed the *shastras* with *panditas* learned in them; and yet, however my words, spoken in foolishness, may describe Gaurahari, may Sri Gaurahari be pleased with me.
From the word-for-word gloss: "gurus" — teachers of many *sampradayas*.

### END
Thus ends the *Sri Sri Chaitanya-chandramrita* in twelve sections, composed by Srimat Prabodhananda Sarasvati-pada, the crest-jewel of the line of *tridandi-gosvamis*, the best of wandering acharyas, the foremost of Gaura's associates.

Thus ends the *Gaudiya-bhashya* on the *Sri Chaitanya-chandramrita*.
