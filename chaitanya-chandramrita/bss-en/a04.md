# Verses 99–142: Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati's anvaya (word-for-word gloss), translated from the Bengali. Numbering of the 1951 edition (Bengali edition: 100–143).

### 99
ANVAYA: matta-keshari-kishora-vikramah — mighty as an intoxicated young lion, that is, in subduing Kali possessing valour like that of an intoxicated young lion; prema-sindhu-jagat-aplava-udyamah — striving to flood the world by making the ocean of love surge; divya-nava-hema-kandali-komalah — (*divya* — beautiful; *nava-hema-kandali-komala* — tender as a freshly blossomed golden *kandali* [flower]); kah api gaura-candramah — a certain indescribable Gaurachandra; jayati — shines, surpassing all.

### 100
ANVAYA: [yah — He who]; saundarye — in beauty; kama-kotih — millions of Kamadevas, that is, He whose wealth of beauty puts to shame countless Madanas; sakala-jana-samahladane — in giving delight (refreshing tenderness) to all people; candra-kotih — millions of moons, that is, belittling the coolness of countless moons; vatsalye — in loving tenderness; matri-kotih — millions of mothers, that is, belittling the loving tenderness of countless mothers; audarya-sare — in the height of generosity; tridasha-vitapinam kotih — millions of the trees of the gods, that is, putting to shame the generosity of countless wish-fulfilling trees; gambhirye — in depth; ambhodhi-kotih — millions of oceans, that is, He whose [depth] surpasses the depth of thousands upon thousands of oceans; madhurimani — in sweetness; sudha-kshira-madhvika-kotih — millions of essences of nectar, [of milk drinks] and of essences of honey ("*madhvika*" — an intoxicating drink made from honey; here the word "*sudha*" indicates immortality and an unprecedented taste, the word "*kshira*" satisfaction and [nourishment], the word "*madhvika*" the madness of love); pranaya-rasa-pade — with regard to *shringara-rasa*; darshita-ashcarya-kotih — who has displayed millions of wonders, that is, the *pranaya-rasa* manifested by Sri Gaurasundara is millions of times more wondrous than the sweetness of *shringara-rasa* shown by the former connoisseurs of *rasa*; sah devah gaurah — that Gaurasundara who sports in pastimes (*lilamaya*); jiyat — may He be glorious.

### 101
ANVAYA: sva-pada-ambhoja-eka-pranaya-lahari-sadhana-bhritam — (*sva-pada-ambhoja* — His own lotus feet; *eka* — surpassing all; *pranaya-lahari* — the wave of *bhava*; *sadhana* — the means to manifest it in the heart; of the *sadhana-bhaktas*, that is, of those *sadhana-bhaktas* who abide [in *sadhana*] in order to manifest in the heart the all-surpassing wave of *prema* for His lotus feet); shiva-brahma-adinam api ca — even of Shiva, Brahma and the others; su-maha-vismaya-bhritam — who give boundless astonishment; maha-prema-aveshat — because of absorption in *maha-prema*; unmadah iva — as if mad; kim api natam — of the devotees who dance wondrously; prakatita-parama-ashcarya-mahima — He who has manifested their supremely wondrous glory; prabhuh — the independent Lord; gaurah — Gaurasundara; jiyat — may He be glorious.

### 102
ANVAYA: matta-koti-mrigendra-hunkriti-ravah — He whose voice is like the roar of millions of intoxicated lions; tigmamshu-koti-chavih — possessing a bodily radiance brighter than that of millions of suns; koti-indu-udbhata-shitalah — cooler than millions of moons; gati-jita-pronmatta-koti-dvipah — with a gait more beautiful than the stride of millions of intoxicated elephants; namna — by the name (the *maha-mantra* "Hare Krishna" and the rest, or the names "Krishna-Chaitanya," "Vishvambhara" and the rest); durgata-koti-nishkriti-karah — the deliverer of millions of the distressed; brahma-adi-koti-ishvarah — the master of millions upon millions of Brahmas and others; koti-advaita-shiromanih — (the crown of millions upon millions of *advaita-vadis*, worshipped by them, the highest summit of the perception of the impersonal Brahman — Parabrahman, that is, the condensed Parabrahman in human form); shri-shri-shacinandanah — Sri Sri Shachinandana; vijayate — shines with supreme excellence.

### 103
ANVAYA: iha — in this world; yah dura-shunyah — that which is "*dura*" — inaccessible to the material intellect — and "*shunya*" — devoid of the gross material aspect of the endeavours of *karma* and the like; [yo va — or which]; bata — alas (in regret); balavat-kantakah — blocked by thorns — dry knowledge and attachment to regular and occasional *karma*; [atah — therefore]; yah ati-durgah — hard to travel, extremely difficult; yah mithya-artha-bhramakah — (*mithya-artha* — a false, unreal object; producing the knowledge of it as real, that is, making even the wise (*suris*) consider false *bhakti*, pretended *bhakti* and the like to be "*bhakti*"); yah sapadi rasa-maya-ananda-nihsyandakah — which at once brings a stream of the *rasa* of the bliss of *prema*; margah — the path; tam — that [path of *bhakti*]; sadyah — at once; pradyotayan — […] illumining; [prakatita-mahima — not legible in the OCR of the *anvaya*; in the *anuvada*: "who manifests the glory of *bhakti*"]; hrit-guhah — of the cave of the heart; antar-dhvanta-hanta — destroying the darkness of ignorance; snehavan — full of tender love (with reference to a lamp, "*sneha*" is oil); navadvipa-dipyat-pradipah — the lamp shining in Navadvipa; ko 'pi — a certain indescribable Purusha; sah jayati — may He be glorious.

### 104
ANVAYA: kutarka-shalabhan — the moths of false logic; durat eva — even from afar; dahan — burning; koti-indu-samshitalah — cooler than millions of moons; jyotih-kandala-sadma — the abode of a mass of light; sat-madhurima — (*sat* — excellent, *madhurima* — sweetness; that is, supremely sweet); bahya-antara-dhvanta-hrit — destroying darkness outer and inner; sa-sneha-ashaya-varti-divya-visarat-tejah — (*sa-sneha* — abiding together with *sneha* (love), and in another sense with ghee or other oil; *ashaya* — the heart, and in another sense a vessel; that is the *varti* — the wick; *divya-visarat-tejah* — He from whom a radiance issues divinely, beautifully; that is, He whose radiance issues when the wick of the heart, which abides together with the oil of love, is kindled); suvarna-dyutih — possessing a golden lustre; adbhutah — unearthly (*aprakrita*); sah navadvipa-pradipah — that lamp of Navadvipa; karunyat — out of mercy; iha — in this world (*prapanca*); jajvaliti — shines brightly.

### 105
ANVAYA: citkaraih — with cries (sounds uttered with extreme longing from joy and sorrow); dasha-dig-mukham — the ten directions; mukharayan — making resound, making echo; atta-atta-hasa-cchata-vicibhih — with waves of the radiance of pealing laughter; nabhah — the sky; sphuta-kunda-kairava-gana-prodbhasi — ([making it] extremely bright as if with full-blown *kunda* and *kumuda* flowers); pavana-uccalat-caladala-praya-prakampam — (*pavana-uccala* — shaken by the wind; *caladala* — the *ashvattha* tree; *praya* — like it; *prakampa* — trembling); sarva-angam — the whole body; dadhat — having; prema-rasa-unmada-apluta-gatih — (*prema-rasa-unmada* — the intoxication born of the *rasa* of *prema*; by it *apluta-gati*: among the various kinds of movement, the *uddanda*, a particular dance; that is, intoxicated with the joy, pride and the rest born of the *rasa* of *prema*, dancing with high leaps); mattah gaurah harih — the intoxicated Gaurahari; shobhate — shines with supreme excellence.

### 106
ANVAYA: nirdoshah — (without a spot, or: He who has no need of "*dosha*" — night, darkness — that is, ever shining); caru-nrityah — dancing beautifully; vidhuta-malinata-vakra-bhavah — free of dimness and crookedness; nihshesha-prani-tapa-traya-harana-maha-prema-piyusha-varshi — pouring the nectar of *prema* to remove the threefold miseries of all living beings; bhaktanam hrit-cakora-svadita-pada-rucih — (*bhaktanam* — of the devotees; *hrit-cakora* — the *cakora* birds of the hearts; *svadita* — tasted; *pada* — rays; *ruci* — sweetness; that is, He the sweetness of whose rays the *cakoras* of the devotees' hearts taste); ko 'pi gauranga-candrah — a certain indescribable Purusha, Sri Gaurachandra; bhagya-udaya-rucira-shaci-garbha-dugdha-ambu-rasheh — (*bhagya-udaya* — the rising of good fortune, *rucira* — most beautiful; from the milk ocean of the womb of Shachidevi); udbhutah — risen; [san — being]; bhati — shines.

### 107
ANVAYA: vraja-virahini-bhava-magnah [san] — immersed in the ocean of the nectar of the *mahabhava* of Srimati Radhika, suffering from separation from Krishna; nayana-payasa — with streams of tears; pandu-ganda-sthala-antam — the edge of the pale cheeks; sincan sincan — wetting again and again; aho — ah!; dirgha-nihshvasa-jatam — many long sighs; pratimuhuh — every moment; muncan muncan — heaving again and again; uccaih krandan — weeping aloud; karuna-karuna-udgirna-ha-ha-iti-ravah — He who utters the sounds "ha! ha!", expressing an extremely piteous *rasa*; kah api gaurah — a certain indescribable Purusha, Sri Gaurasundara; cakasti — manifesting His mood, shines with supreme excellence.

### 108
ANVAYA: dahana-uttirna-sauvarna-saram — like the essence of molten gold that has passed through fire; kim api varnam — a certain indescribable colour; bibhrat — assuming; dripta-gopala-balah [san] — (*dripta* — illumined, that is, manifested; *gopala-bala* — the pastime of Bala-Gopala; that is, displaying the pastime of Bala-Gopala); kim api divya-akaram — a certain indescribable spiritual form (*cinmaya-vigraha*); kalayan — assuming; kvacit avasare — sometimes; tat-tat-ashcarya-lilam — those various most wondrous pastimes (in the *anuvada*: the pastimes of youth, *kaishora*); avishkurvan — revealing; sakshat-radha-madhuripu-vapuh — the visible united body of Radha and Madhava; gauranga-candrah — Sri Gauranga; bhati — shines with supreme beauty; [as Srila Svarupa Gosvami also says, Radha and Krishna are a transformation of love, [a manifestation] of the *hladini* potency; in their essence They are one, but since the *tattva* of pastimes is eternal, They eternally abide in two forms; now these two have appeared in one form — as the *tattva* of Chaitanya].

### 109
ANVAYA: nrinam loke — in the world of men, that is, in the *prapanca*; yasmin avatarati [sati] — when He descends; aho — ah!; purubhih papaih — by heaps of grievous sins; paritanam — surrounded; esham tanubhritam api — even for these embodied beings; bhagavat-nama-lahari — the wave of the name of Sri Krishna, that is, the sequence of the holy names "Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna" and so on; akasmat eva — suddenly; avirbhavati — appeared; vajra-prayam hrit api — even the heart grown hard as stone through *karma*, *jnana* and the like, or through various *anarthas*; navanitayitam abhut — became soft and melted like fresh butter; sah gaurah — that Gaurasundara; mama gatih [astu] — may He be my refuge.

### 110
ANVAYA: akasmat daya-sara-hridaye caitanye iha avatarati [sati] — when the supremely merciful Sri Chaitanyadeva suddenly descended into this world; [yasya — one who]; na yogah na dhyanam na ca japa-tapah-tyaga-niyamah [santi] — has neither yoga, nor meditation, nor *japa*, austerity, renunciation and vows; na vedah na acarah — has neither study of the Vedas nor good conduct; nishiddha-adi-uparatih bata kva nu — alas, where is the abstention from forbidden and other deeds! — that is, who has not even abstained from sinful deeds; [tadrishah] janah — such a man; puma-arthanam maulim param — the crown of the goals of man, the highest *prema*; muda — with a joyful heart; lunthati — plunders.

### 111
ANVAYA: citra-vibhave — (*citra* — wondrous, *vibhava* — splendour; the locative in the sense of state; endowed with wondrous splendour); shri-caitanye bhuvi avatarati [sati] — when Sri Chaitanyadeva descended to earth; [karminam — of the people of *karma*]; manah — the mind; maha-karma-srotah-nipatitam api — though fallen into the stream of great *karma*; sthairyam ayate — attains steadiness, having obtained Gaurasundara's *prema*; [evam — and]; maha-pashanebhyah api ati-kathinam api — even if harder than huge rocks; [manah] drava-dasham eti — the mind comes to a state of fluidity through the *rasa* of *bhakti*; maha-yoga-manasam api — even of those whose mind, that is, consciousness, is directed to the *sadhana* of the great yoga and the like; [manah — the mind]; nihsadhanam — turning away from yoga and the like; urdhvam natati — dances high, that is, leaving the *sadhana* of yoga and the like, connected with the senses, tastes the bliss of *prema* in the kingdom of the spiritual pastimes of Adhokshaja.

### 112
ANVAYA: caitanya-candre param bhakti-yoga-padavim avishkurvati [sati] — when Sri Chaitanya-chandra revealed the highest path of *bhakti-yoga*, that is, the path of *raganuga* worship; vishayinah — people immersed in the taste of material objects; stri-putra-adi-katham — worldly talk about the maintenance of wives, sons and the rest; jahuh — gave up; budhah — the *panditas*, that is, philosophers, experts in poetics (*alankarikas*), logicians (*naiyayikas*) and the rest; shastra-pravadam — disputes and quarrels about the *shastras*; [jahuh — gave up]; yogindrah — the best of yogis; marut-niyamaja-klesham — (*marut* — the air, *niyama* — subjugation, *ja-klesha* — the hardships born of it; that is, the hardships of *sadhana* in holding the inhalation, exhalation and the rest of the life-air by *pranayama*, *kumbhaka* and so on); vijahuh — entirely gave up; tapasah — the ascetics; tapah — austerity; [jahuh — gave up]; yatayah ca — and the *yatis*, that is, sannyasis, adherents of the knowledge of the undifferentiated; jnana-abhyasa-vidhim — the search for the undifferentiated Brahman; jahuh — gave up; [tada — then]; anyah rasah na eva asit — there was no *rasa* other than *bhakti-rasa* (that is, neither the worldly *rasa* of those attached to objects, nor the worldly *rasa* of the *shastras* of the *panditas* — the *alankarikas*, philosophers and the rest — nor the *rasa* of Paramatma of the yogis, nor the *rasa* of the impersonal Brahman of the seekers of undifferentiated knowledge — none seemed "*rasa*" beside the *rasa* of *advaya-jnana* (the non-dual Absolute Truth), for beside the wondrous *bhakti-rasa* produced by the union of the four ingredients — *rati*, that is, the inclination connected with Bhagavan, or the *sthayi-bhava*, together with the *vibhava*, *anubhava*, *sattvika[-bhavas]* and *vyabhichari[-bhavas]* — a worldly or partial *rasa* cannot be called "*rasa*" at all).

### 113
ANVAYA: jagati gaure avatarati [sati] — when Gaurasundara descended into the world; gehe gehe — in every house; tumula-hari-sankirtana-ravah — the noise of a tumultuous Hari-*sankirtana*; abhut — rose; dehe dehe — in every body; vipula-pulaka-ashru-vyatikarah — (*vipula* — abundant, that is, full; thrills, tears and the other *sattvika* transformations; *vyatikara* — multitude); babhau — shone; snehe snehe — (matured *prema* is *sneha*; with the growing intensification of the density of *prema-bhakti*); amnayat api — (*amnaya* — the *shrutis*, the Vedas; even from them); daviyasi — remote, that is, inaccessible; parama-madhura-utkarsha-padavi api — even the highest, sweet, best stage; [prakatabhuta — was revealed].

### 114
ANVAYA: kanaka-rucira-ange krishne avatarati [sati] — when Bhagavan Sri Krishna Himself, the attractor of all hearts, descended, assuming a charming golden lustre; maha-prema-ambhodheh — of the ocean of great *prema*; kim api rasa-vanyabhih — by certain floods of *rasa*; akhilam etat bhuvanam — this whole world; abhitah — on all sides; akasmat eva plavitam abhut — was suddenly flooded; akasmat ca adrishta-ashruta-cara-vikaraih — and suddenly by transformations of *prema* never seen or heard of before; alam — extremely; camatkarah abhut — was astonished.

### 115
ANVAYA: [kecit janah — some people]; durvara-garvayitah — full of irrepressible pride; samasta-shastram — all the *shastras*; abhitah — from every side; udgrihnanti — collected, or else prided themselves: "We have studied all the *shastras*; who is above us?"; dhanyam-manya-dhiyah ca — and those who considered themselves blessed; karma-tapasa-adi-uccavaceshu sthitah — standing on the higher and lower rungs of the paths of the regular and occasional rites enjoined by the *smritis*, of austerity, Sankhya, yoga and the like; kecana — some [of them]; dvi-trani eva — only two or three times; hareh namani — the names of Hari; japanti — chanted; [tatrapi te — but even then they]; vama-ashayah — with crooked hearts, that is, devoted to the path of *karma*; [asan — were]; [evam — so]; purvam [asit] — it was before; samprati — now; gauracandre udite [sati] — when Gaurachandra has descended; prema api sadharanah [abhut] — *prema* too has become common, that is, all, from the highest to the lowest, have obtained it.

### 116
ANVAYA: sura-prarthya-pada-abja-seve — He service to whose lotus feet the gods long for; caitanya-namani — bearing the name Chaitanya; deve — (the Purusha of pastimes, or He who, accepting Radhika's mood, wishes to taste His own sweetness); avatarati [sati] — when He descended; vishvadricih — all-pervading; sumadhura-prema-piyusha-vicih — the sweetest waves of the nectar of *prema*; pravistarayati [sati] — when He spread far and wide; kah balah kah ca vriddhah — whether child or old man; kah jadamatih ka vadhuh — whether dullard or woman; kah varakah — whether a wretched low person; iha — in this world (*samsara*); sarvesham bhakti-bhajam — in all those endowed with *bhakti*; hari-pade — for the feet of Sri Hari; kim api aikarasyam babhuva — a certain unprecedented *rasa* arose, that is, in all, for the feet of Sri Hari, one unprecedented *rasa* of *advaya-jnana* (the non-dual Absolute Truth) awoke.

### 117
ANVAYA: purne prema-rasa-ishvare shri-gauracandre — when Bhagavan Gaurachandra Himself, the crown of the connoisseurs of the *rasa* of *prema*; bhuvi — to earth; avatarati [sati] — descended; sarve shankara-narada-adayah — all — Shankara, Narada and the others (in the form of Advaita, Srivasa and others); iha — into this world (*prapanca*); ayatah — came; svayam shrih api — Lakshmi Herself (in the form of Sri Lakshmipriya and Vishnupriya); prapta — appeared; deva-halayudhah api — and Baladeva, His manifest form, non-different from Bhagavan Himself; militah — was present (in the form of Nityananda); te vrishnayah — the Yadavas (in the form of Shachi, Jagannatha Mishra and others); jatah — descended; kim bhuyah — what more is to be said; vraja-vasinah gopala-gopi-adayah api — and the residents of Vraja — Nanda and the rest, the servants Raktaka, Citraka and the rest, the friends headed by Subala, the *gopis* of Vraja (the words "and the rest" also mean Yogamaya and the other *shaktis*) — all; prakatah — appeared, that is, all the eternally perfect devotees of Krishna's pastimes appeared in Gaura's pastimes.

### 118
ANVAYA: hema-gaure — Sri Gaurasundara, shining like molten gold; adbhutam — unearthly; sva-premanam — His own *prema*; jagati — on earth; vitarati [sati] — when He was distributing; bhrityah — the servants; snigdhah — the friends; ati-sumadhura-projjvala-udara-bhajah — (*ati-sumadhura* — sweetness free from the idea of *aishvarya*; *projjvala* — the elevated *ujjvala*; *udara* — charming; that is, also the beloveds who serve exclusively the sweet, elevated, charming *ujjvala-rasa*); sarve eva — all; tat-pada-abja-dvitaya-savidhe — near His two lotus feet; avatirnah [santah] — having descended; purva-adhikatara-maha-prema-piyusha-lakshmim — a wealth of the nectar of great *prema* greater than [the tasting of *prema*] in Krishna's pastimes; prapuh — obtained.

### 119
ANVAYA: adbhuta-mahima-sare — supremely unearthly, endowed with the highest greatness; shri-caitanye — Sri Chaitanya; kshitau — to earth; avatarati [sati] — when He descended; ahaha — ah!; kula-vadhvah api — even noble married women; uccaih uccaih hasanti — laugh very loudly; ku-vishaya-grava-ghatitah api — (*ku-vishaya* — evil objects; *grava* — stone; *ghatita* — hearts made of it; that is, hard hearts made of the stones of evil objects of enjoyment); paritah — wholly; dravibhavam gacchanti — melt; ajnah api — even the ignorant; sakala-shastrajna-samitim — the society of the knowers of all the *shastras*; tiraskurvanti — put to shame.

### 120
ANVAYA: iha — in this world (*prapanca*); purvam — before Chaitanya's appearance; sakala-vidam api — even in the *panditas*, the knowers of all the *shastras*; prayah caitanyam — (the function of consciousness, service to Krishna, is "*caitanya*"); na asit — was almost absent; yat — since; esham — their; kharva kunthita [ca] — extremely petty and inclined to doubt; buddhi-vrittih — the function of the intelligence; sarva-artha-sare api — (*sarva* — all, *artha* — the goals of man; the essence of the four goals is the fifth goal, *krishna-prema*) even in it; padam — (an object, that is, an inclination); na hi akrita — did not have; [kintu] idanim — but now; gauracandre — Gaurachandra; karunaya — out of mercy; jagati — into the world; avatirne [sati] — having descended; kesham — in whom; gambhira-udara-bhava-ujjvala-rasa-madhura-prema-bhakti-praveshah — (*gambhira* — inscrutable; *udara* — extremely wondrous; *bhava* — *vibhava* and *anubhava*; *ujjvala-rasa* — *shringara-rasa*; *madhura-prema-bhakti*; entry into it; that is, entry into the inscrutable *prema-bhakti*, full of the elevated *ujjvala-madhura-rasa* and nourished by the most wondrous ingredients — *vibhava*, *anubhava* and the rest); na asit — was there not?

### 121
ANVAYA: shri-vaiyasakina api — even by the son of Vyasa, Shukadeva; yatra rasa-prasange — in the account of the *rasa-lila*; shrimat-bhagavatasya — of the *Srimad-Bhagavatam*; yat paramam tatparyam — that highest meaning (*prema*, the highest meaning of the *rasa* of the pastimes of Sri Radha and Krishna); duranvayataya — because of the difficulty of comprehension, that is, because there was no vessel able to taste the *rasa* of these pastimes and know their truth; uttankitam — was only indicated, not described in detail; yat [ca] — and that which; radha-rati-keli-nagara-rasa-asvada-eka-sat-bhajanam — (the *rati-keli* of Srimati Radhika — the love-sports in the *nikunja*; *nagara* — Sri Krishna, the crown of the supreme connoisseurs of *rasa*; the one best vessel for tasting the sweetness of His *rasa* and other pastimes); tat-vastu-prathanaya — to spread these two things; harih — Sri Krishna; gaura-vapusha — in the body of Gaura; loke — into this world; avatirnah — descended.

### 122
ANVAYA: shri-caitanya-mahaprabhoh — of Sri Krishna-Chaitanya Mahaprabhu; karunaya — by the mercy; lokasya kah sampadah — what riches did people [not obtain]?; uddhava-mukhah kecit dasyam avapuh — of the devotees headed by Uddhava, some obtained the mood of service, like that of Raktaka, Patraka and others; pare — others; shlaghyam shridama-adi-padam lebhire — obtained the praiseworthy position of the friends Shridama and the rest; pare ca vraja-ambuja-drisham bhavam bhejuh — and others obtained the mood of the *gopis* of Vraja; anye dhanyatamah sudhiyah — still others, the most blessed and wise; radha-pada-ambhoruham — the sweetness of the lotus feet of Sri Radha; dhayanti — taste.

### 123
ANVAYA: sarvajnaih — by the omniscient; muni-pungavaih — best of sages; tat-tat-mate — their own doctrines; yuktibhih — by arguments and reasonings; pravitate [sati] — when they were widely spread; purvam — formerly; kah janah api — no person at all; ekataratra — in a one-sided doctrine; sudridham vishvastah — firmly convinced; na asit — was; samprati — now; apratima-prabhave gauranga-candre — when Sri Gaurachandra, incomparable in power; udite [sati] — has risen; punah — again; paramah shruti-arthah — the highest goal established by all the *shrutis*; hari-bhaktih eva — *hari-bhakti* alone; kaih va — by whom; na nirdharyate — has it not been determined?; [that is, although the mutually contradictory doctrines of the sages, born of speculation, were established by casuistic arguments and the like, their very authors doubted their doctrines; but the eternal religion of *bhakti* preached by the Eternal Purusha, Bhagavan Sri Gaurasundara Himself, is the real truth established by the *shrutis*, free from all deceit].

### 124
