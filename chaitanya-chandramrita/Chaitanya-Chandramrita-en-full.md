# Sri Chaitanya-chandramrita

*"The Nectar of the Moon Chaitanya"*

*Prabodhananda Sarasvati*

*Translated from the Sanskrit*

*Sanskrit text, transliteration and word-for-word translation*

---

## Translator's note

The *Sri Chaitanya-chandramrita* is a Sanskrit collection of a little over a hundred verses glorifying Sri
Chaitanya Mahaprabhu. Its author is Prabodhananda Sarasvati, a contemporary of Mahaprabhu who later lived in
Vrindavana (tradition regards him as the uncle and teacher of Gopala Bhatta Gosvami). The book is divided
into twelve sections: praise, obeisance, benediction, the glory of Gaura's devotees, censure of the
non-devotees, humility, fidelity to the object of worship, instruction to the world, the supremacy of
Chaitanya, the glory of His descent, the beauty and dance of Gaura, and finally lament over His
disappearance.

**Source.** The Sanskrit text is taken from the edition *Sri Chaitanya-chandramritam tatha Sri
Sangita-madhavam*, Mathura, 1951 (ed. Baba Krishnadasa, with a Hindi translation by Gosvami Krishna
Chaitanya; eGangotri scan at archive.org). The verse numbering follows that edition (142 verses and one
verse in brackets without a number). Poorly recognized passages have been checked against the Bengali
edition with the translation of Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati (which has 143 verses: one verse stands in a
different place, so from the seventh section on its numbers are one higher). **Only Prabodhananda's
Sanskrit text has been translated.** The Hindi and Bengali translations and commentaries of the editions
helped to make out corrupt passages but are not reproduced.

**Verification.** After the first translation the Sanskrit text was restored line by line from both
editions (checking the metre and relying on Bhaktisiddhanta's word-for-word analysis), and the translation
of every verse was compared afresh with it; places where the translation departed from Bhaktisiddhanta's
interpretation in meaning were corrected according to him. The English translation was made from the
Sanskrit, in agreement with the Russian translation (same numbering and notes).

---

**Full version.** For each verse are given: the Sanskrit text in Devanagari, restored from the 1951 edition
and Bhaktisiddhanta's Bengali edition (where they differ, Bhaktisiddhanta's reading is preferred; this is our
own corrected text, not a reprint of any single edition); an IAST transliteration; a word-for-word
translation in the order of the text, with Bhaktisiddhanta's glosses marked "B:"; and the translation.

# Part One

*Praise. Obeisance. Benediction. The glory of Sri Chaitanya's devotees (verses 1–28)*

## Praise (stuti)

### Verse 1

स्तुमस्तं चैतन्याकृतिमतिविमर्यादपरमा-
द्भुतौदार्यं वर्यं व्रजपतिकुमारं रसयितुम् ।
विशुद्धस्वप्रेमोन्मदमधुरपीयूषलहरीं
प्रदातुं चान्येभ्यः परपदनवद्वीपप्रकटम् ॥ १ ॥

*stumastaṁ caitanyākṛtimativimaryādaparamā-*
*dbhutaudāryaṁ varyaṁ vrajapatikumāraṁ rasayitum |*
*viśuddhasvapremonmadamadhurapīyūṣalaharīṁ*
*pradātuṁ cānyebhyaḥ parapadanavadvīpaprakaṭam || 1 ||*

*Word for word:* stumah — we praise; tam — Him; caitanya-akritim — who has the form of Chaitanya (B: the person named Sri Krishna Chaitanya); ati-vimaryada-parama-adbhuta-audaryam — whose generosity is boundless and supremely wonderful; varyam — the best (B: of all avataras); vrajapati-kumaram — the son of the lord of Vraja (B: Himself, non-different from the son of Nanda); rasayitum — to give a taste; vishuddha-sva-prema-unmada-madhura-piyusha-laharim — the waves of the sweet nectar of His own pure, maddening love (B: of joy and other states born of pure love); pradatum — to give; ca — and; anyebhyah — to others; para-pada-navadvipa-prakatam — who appeared in the supreme abode of Navadvipa (B: Navadvipa, the seat of the nine kinds of bhakti).

**1.** We praise the best of all — Him who has the form of Chaitanya, whose generosity is boundless and supremely wonderful; who appeared in the supreme abode of Navadvipa to give the son of the lord of Vraja — Himself — a taste of the nectar waves of His own pure, maddening, sweet love, and to distribute them to others.

### Verse 2

धर्मास्पृष्टः सततपरमाविष्ट एवात्यधर्मे
दृष्टिं प्राप्तो न हि खलु सतां सृष्टिषु क्वापि नो सन् ।
यद्दत्तश्रीहरिरससुधास्वादमत्तः प्रनृत्य-
त्युच्चैर्गायत्यथ विलुठति स्तौमि तं कञ्चिदीशम् ॥ २ ॥

*dharmāspṛṣṭaḥ satataparamāviṣṭa evātyadharme*
*dṛṣṭiṁ prāpto na hi khalu satāṁ sṛṣṭiṣu kvāpi no san |*
*yaddattaśrīharirasasudhāsvādamattaḥ pranṛtya-*
*tyuccairgāyatyatha viluṭhati staumi taṁ kañcidīśam || 2 ||*

*Word for word:* dharma-asprishtah — untouched by dharma (B: without a drop of piety); satata — always; parama-avishtah — deeply sunk; eva — indeed; ati-adharme — in the greatest lawlessness (B: in great sin); drishtim — the sight; praptah — obtained; na hi khalu — never; satam — of the saints; srishtishu — in the places (B: where the sadhus dwell); kvapi — anywhere; no — not; san — being; yat-datta-shri-hari-rasa-sudha-asvada-mattah — intoxicated by the taste of the nectar of the rasa of Sri and Hari bestowed by Him (B: Sri is Radha, Hari is Krishna); pranrityati — dances; uccaih — loudly; gayati — sings; atha — and; viluthati — rolls on the ground; staumi — I praise; tam — that; kancit — indescribable; isham — Lord.

**2.** A man never touched by *dharma*, always sunk in lawlessness, who has never come into the sight of the saints and has never been anywhere among them — intoxicated by the taste of the nectar of Sri-Hari-rasa, the *rasa* of the love of Radha and Krishna, which this Lord has bestowed, he dances, sings aloud and rolls on the ground. I praise this indescribable Lord.

### Verse 3

यन्नाप्तं कर्मनिष्ठैर्न च समधिगतं यत्तपोध्यानयोगै-
र्वैराग्यैस्त्यागतत्त्वस्तुतिभिरपि न यत्तर्कितञ्चापि कैश्चित् ।
गोविन्दप्रेमभाजामपि न च कलितं यद्रहस्यं स्वयं त-
न्नाम्नैव प्रादुरासीदवतरति परे यत्र तं नौमि गौरम् ॥ ३ ॥

*yannāptaṁ karmaniṣṭhairna ca samadhigataṁ yattapodhyānayogai-*
*rvairāgyaistyāgatattvastutibhirapi na yattarkitañcāpi kaiścit |*
*govindapremabhājāmapi na ca kalitaṁ yadrahasyaṁ svayaṁ ta-*
*nnāmnaiva prādurāsīdavatarati pare yatra taṁ naumi gauram || 3 ||*

*Word for word:* yat — what; na — not; aptam — attained; karma-nishthaih — by those devoted to ritual; na ca — nor; samadhigatam — realized; yat — what; tapah-dhyana-yogaih — by austerity, meditation and yoga (B: ashtanga-yoga); vairagyaih — by renunciation; tyaga-tattva-stutibhih — by abandonment of works, knowledge of the truth and hymns; api — even; na — not; yat — what; tarkitam — guessed; ca api — and even; kaishcit — by anyone; govinda-prema-bhajam — of those who love Govinda; api — even; na ca — nor; kalitam — known; yat — which; rahasyam — secret (B: the secret prema); svayam — of itself; tat — that; namna — through the Name (B: through nama-kirtana); eva — alone; pradurasit — became manifest; avatarati — when He descends; pare — the Supreme; yatra — in whom; tam — that; naumi — I bow to; gauram — Gaura.

**3.** That secret which those devoted to ritual did not attain, which was not won by austerity, meditation and yoga, which no one even guessed at in spite of renunciation, abandonment of the world, knowledge of the truth and hymns of praise, which even those who love Govinda did not know — that revealed itself of its own accord through the Holy Name alone, when the Supreme descended. I bow to that Gaura.

### Verse 4

दृष्टः स्पृष्टः कीर्तितः संस्मृतो वा
दूरस्थैरप्यानतो वादृतो वा ।
प्रेम्णः सारं दातुमीशो य एकः
श्रीचैतन्यं नौमि देवं दयालुम् ॥ ४ ॥

*dṛṣṭaḥ spṛṣṭaḥ kīrtitaḥ saṁsmṛto vā*
*dūrasthairapyānato vādṛto vā |*
*premṇaḥ sāraṁ dātumīśo ya ekaḥ*
*śrīcaitanyaṁ naumi devaṁ dayālum || 4 ||*

*Word for word:* drishtah — seen; sprishtah — touched; kirtitah — glorified; samsmritah — remembered; va — or; dura-sthaih — by those far away; api — even; anatah — bowed to; va — or; adritah — honoured; va — or; premnah — of love; saram — the essence (B: vipralambha-rasa); datum — to give; ishah — able; yah — who; ekah — alone; shri-caitanyam — Sri Chaitanya; naumi — I bow to; devam — the Lord; dayalum — merciful.

**4.** I bow to the merciful Lord, Sri Chaitanya, who alone is able to give the essence of love to anyone who has seen Him, touched Him, glorified Him or remembered Him, or even from afar has bowed to Him or honoured Him.

### Verse 5

कैवल्यं नरकायते त्रिदशपूराकाशपुष्पायते
दुर्दान्तेन्द्रियकालसर्पपटली प्रोत्खातदंष्ट्रायते ।
विश्वं पूर्णसुखायते विधिमहेन्द्रादिश्च कीटायते
यत्कारुण्यकटाक्षवैभववतां तं गौरमेव स्तुमः ॥ ५ ॥

*kaivalyaṁ narakāyate tridaśapūrākāśapuṣpāyate*
*durdāntendriyakālasarpapaṭalī protkhātadaṁṣṭrāyate |*
*viśvaṁ pūrṇasukhāyate vidhimahendrādiśca kīṭāyate*
*yatkāruṇyakaṭākṣavaibhavavatāṁ taṁ gaurameva stumaḥ || 5 ||*

*Word for word:* kaivalyam — kaivalya (B: merging into Ishvara); narakayate — becomes like hell; tridasha-puh — the city of the thirty-three gods, heaven; akasha-pushpayate — is like a flower in the sky (unreal); durdanta-indriya-kala-sarpa-patali — the pack of untamed senses, those deadly serpents; protkhata-damshtrayate — is like snakes with their fangs pulled out; vishvam — the universe; purna-sukhayate — becomes full of happiness; vidhi-mahendra-adih — Brahma, the great Indra and the rest; ca — and; kitayate — are like worms; yat-karunya-kataksha-vaibhava-vatam — for those whose wealth is His merciful glance; tam — that; gauram — Gaura; eva — alone; stumah — we praise.

**5.** For those who possess the wealth of His merciful glance, *kaivalya* (liberation) becomes like hell, the city of the gods like a flower in the sky, the pack of untamed senses — those serpents of death — like snakes whose fangs have been pulled out; the whole world becomes full of happiness, and Brahma, Indra and the rest become like worms. Gaura alone we praise.

### Verse 6

माद्यन्तः परिपीय यस्य चरणाम्भोजस्रवत्प्रोज्ज्वल-
प्रेमानन्दमयामृताद्भुतरसान् सर्वे सुपर्वेडिताः ।
ब्रह्मादींश्च हसन्ति नातिबहु मन्यन्ते महावैष्णवान्
धिक्कुर्वन्ति च ब्रह्मयोगविदुषस्तं गौरचन्द्रं नुमः ॥ ६ ॥

*mādyantaḥ paripīya yasya caraṇāmbhojasravatprojjvala-*
*premānandamayāmṛtādbhutarasān sarve suparveḍitāḥ |*
*brahmādīṁśca hasanti nātibahu manyante mahāvaiṣṇavān*
*dhikkurvanti ca brahmayogaviduṣastaṁ gauracandraṁ numaḥ || 6 ||*

*Word for word:* madyantah — intoxicated; paripiya — having drunk; yasya — whose; carana-ambhoja-sravat-projjvala-prema-ananda-maya-amrita-adbhuta-rasan — the wondrous nectar juices of radiant love and bliss flowing from the lotus feet; sarve — all (B: all the devotees of Gaura); suparva-iditah — praised by the gods; brahma-adin — Brahma and the others; ca — and; hasanti — laugh at; na ati-bahu manyante — do not think much of; maha-vaishnavan — great Vaishnavas (B: those not devoted to Gaura); dhikkurvanti — censure; ca — and; brahma-yoga-vidushah — the knowers of Brahman and of yoga; tam — that; gaura-candram — Gaurachandra; numah — we praise.

**6.** His devotees, worshipped even by the gods, having drunk and become intoxicated with the wondrous nectar juices of radiant love and bliss that flow from His lotus feet, laugh at Brahma and the others, do not think much even of great Vaishnavas, and censure the knowers of Brahman and of yoga. We praise that Gaurachandra.

### Verse 7

रक्षोदैत्यकुलं हतं कियदिदं योगादिवर्त्मक्रिया-
मार्गो वा प्रकटीकृतः कियदिदं सृष्ट्यादिकं वा कियत् ।
मेदिन्युद्धरणादिकं कियदिदं प्रेमोज्ज्वलाया महा-
भक्तेर्वर्त्मकरीं परं भगवतश्चैतन्यमूर्तिं स्तुमः ॥ ७ ॥

*rakṣodaityakulaṁ hataṁ kiyadidaṁ yogādivartmakriyā-*
*mārgo vā prakaṭīkṛtaḥ kiyadidaṁ sṛṣṭyādikaṁ vā kiyat |*
*medinyuddharaṇādikaṁ kiyadidaṁ premojjvalāyā mahā-*
*bhaktervartmakarīṁ paraṁ bhagavataścaitanyamūrtiṁ stumaḥ || 7 ||*

*Word for word:* rakshah-daitya-kulam — the race of Rakshasas and Daityas; hatam — destroyed; kiyat — how great; idam — this; yoga-adi-vartma-kriya-margah — the path of the practices of yoga and the rest; va — or; prakatikritah — revealed (B: by Kapila and others); kiyat idam — how great is this; srishti-adikam — creation and the rest (B: creation, maintenance and destruction by the guna-avataras); va — or; kiyat — how great; medini-uddharana-adikam — the rescue of the earth and so on (B: by Varaha); kiyat idam — how great is this; prema-ujjvalayah — radiant with prema; maha-bhakteh — of great bhakti; vartma-karim — who makes the path; param — only; bhagavatah — to Bhagavan; caitanya-murtim — the form of Chaitanya; stumah — we praise.

**7.** What is so great in the destruction of the race of Rakshasas and Daityas? What is so great in revealing the path of yoga and other practices? What is so great in the creation of the world and the rest, in the rescue of the earth and so on? We praise only the form of Chaitanya, who has revealed the path of the highest *bhakti* to Bhagavan, radiant with *prema*.

## Obeisance (namaskara)

### Verse 8

नमश्चैतन्यचन्द्राय कोटिचन्द्राननत्विषे ।
प्रेमानन्दाब्धिचन्द्राय चारुचन्द्रांशुहासिने ॥ ८ ॥

*namaścaitanyacandrāya koṭicandrānanatviṣe |*
*premānandābdhicandrāya cārucandrāṁśuhāsine || 8 ||*

*Word for word:* namah — obeisance; caitanya-candraya — to Chaitanya-chandra; koti-candra-anana-tvishe — whose face shines [more beautifully than] millions of moons; prema-ananda-abdhi-candraya — the moon of the ocean of love and bliss; caru-candra-amshu-hasine — whose smile is beautiful moonbeams.

**8.** Obeisance to Chaitanya-chandra, the Moon, whose face shines like millions of moons — the moon over the ocean of love and bliss, whose smile is like beautiful moonbeams.

### Verse 9

यस्यैव पादाम्बुजभक्तिलभ्यः
प्रेमाभिधानः परमः पुमर्थः ।
तस्मै जगन्मङ्गलमङ्गलाय
चैतन्यचन्द्राय नमो नमस्ते ॥ ९ ॥

*yasyaiva pādāmbujabhaktilabhyaḥ*
*premābhidhānaḥ paramaḥ pumarthaḥ |*
*tasmai jaganmaṅgalamaṅgalāya*
*caitanyacandrāya namo namaste || 9 ||*

*Word for word:* yasya — whose; eva — alone; pada-ambuja-bhakti-labhyah — attainable by devotion to the lotus feet; prema-abhidhanah — called prema; paramah — the highest; pumarthah — goal of man; tasmai — to Him; jagan-mangala-mangalaya — the good of the good of the world; caitanya-candraya — to Chaitanya-chandra; namah — obeisance; namah — obeisance; te — to You.

**9.** By devotion to His lotus feet alone is attained the highest goal of man, called love (*prema*). To Him, the good of all the good of the world, to Chaitanya-chandra — obeisance, again obeisance!

### Verse 10

उच्चैरास्फालयन्तं करचरणमहो हेमदण्डप्रकाण्डौ
बाहू प्रोद्धृत्य सत्ताण्डवतरलतनुं पुण्डरीकायताक्षम् ।
विश्वस्यामङ्गलघ्नं किमपि हरिहरीत्युन्मदानन्दनादै-
र्वन्दे तं देवचूडामणिमतुलरसाविष्टचैतन्यचन्द्रम् ॥ १० ॥

*uccairāsphālayantaṁ karacaraṇamaho hemadaṇḍaprakāṇḍau*
*bāhū proddhṛtya sattāṇḍavataralatanuṁ puṇḍarīkāyatākṣam |*
*viśvasyāmaṅgalaghnaṁ kimapi hariharītyunmadānandanādai-*
*rvande taṁ devacūḍāmaṇimatularasāviṣṭacaitanyacandram || 10 ||*

*Word for word:* uccaih — vigorously; asphalayantam — swinging (B: moving to and fro in the rapture of dance); kara-caranam — arms and legs; aho — oh!; hema-danda-prakandau — huge like golden staffs; bahu — both arms; proddhritya — raising; sat-tandava-tarala-tanum — whose body sways in a beautiful vigorous dance; pundarika-ayata-aksham — with eyes long like lotuses; vishvasya — of the universe; amangala-ghnam — destroying the inauspicious; kimapi — indescribable; hari-hari-iti — "Hari, Hari!"; unmada-ananda-nadaih — with cries of ecstatic bliss; vande — I praise; tam — that; deva-cuda-manim — crest-jewel of the gods (B: of all avataras); atula-rasa-avishta-caitanya-candram — Chaitanya-chandra absorbed in incomparable rasa.

**10.** He vigorously swings His arms and legs — oh wonder! — raising His arms, which are like pillars of gold; His body sways in a beautiful dance, His eyes are long like lotuses; with indescribable cries of "Hari! Hari!", full of ecstatic bliss, He destroys all that is inauspicious in the universe. I bow to that Chaitanya-chandra, the crest-jewel of the gods, absorbed in incomparable *rasa*.

### Verse 11

आनन्दलीलामयविग्रहाय
हेमाभदिव्यच्छविसुन्दराय ।
तस्मै महाप्रेमरसप्रदाय
चैतन्यचन्द्राय नमो नमस्ते ॥ ११ ॥

*ānandalīlāmayavigrahāya*
*hemābhadivyacchavisundarāya |*
*tasmai mahāpremarasapradāya*
*caitanyacandrāya namo namaste || 11 ||*

*Word for word:* ananda-lila-maya-vigrahaya — whose body is made of pastimes of bliss; hema-abha-divya-chavi-sundaraya — beautiful with a divine lustre like gold; tasmai — to Him; maha-prema-rasa-pradaya — who bestows the rasa of great love (B: the sublime ujjvala-rasa never given before); caitanya-candraya — to Chaitanya-chandra; namah namah — obeisance, obeisance; te — to You.

**11.** To Him whose body is made of pastimes of bliss, who is beautiful with a golden divine lustre, who bestows the *rasa* of great love — to Chaitanya-chandra — obeisance, again obeisance!

### Verse 12

प्रवाहैरश्रूणां नवजलदकोटी इव दृशौ
दधानं प्रेमर्द्ध्या परमपदकोटीप्रहसनम् ।
वमन्तं माधुर्यैरमृतनिधिकोटीरिव तनु-
च्छटाभिस्तं वन्दे हरिमहह सन्न्यासकपटम् ॥ १२ ॥

*pravāhairaśrūṇāṁ navajaladakoṭī iva dṛśau*
*dadhānaṁ premarddhyā paramapadakoṭīprahasanam |*
*vamantaṁ mādhuryairamṛtanidhikoṭīriva tanu-*
*cchaṭābhistaṁ vande harimahaha sannyāsakapaṭam || 12 ||*

*Word for word:* pravahaih — with streams; ashrunam — of tears; nava-jalada-koti — millions of new clouds; iva — like; drishau — eyes; dadhanam — having; prema-riddhya — by the wealth of love; parama-pada-koti-prahasanam — laughing at millions of supreme abodes (Vaikunthas); vamantam — pouring forth; madhuryaih — with sweetness; amrita-nidhi-kotih — millions of oceans of nectar; iva — as if; tanu-chatabhih — by the lustre of His body; tam — that; vande — I bow to; harim — Hari; ahaha — ah!; sannyasa-kapatam — who has assumed the guise of a sannyasi.

**12.** With streams of tears His eyes are like millions of new rain-clouds; by the wealth of His love He laughs at millions of supreme abodes; by the lustre of His body He seems to pour forth the sweetness of millions of oceans of nectar. I bow to that Hari, who has assumed the guise of a *sannyasi*.

## Benediction (ashir-vada)

### Verse 13

सिंहस्कन्धं मधुरमधुरस्मेरगण्डस्थलान्तं

# Part Two

*Censure of the non-devotees. Humility (verses 29–56)*

## Censure of those not devoted to Chaitanya

### Verse 29

महापुरुषमानिनां सुरमुनीश्वराणां निजं
पदाम्बुजमजानतां किमपि गर्वनिर्वासनम् ।
अहो नयनगोचरं निगमचक्रचूडाचरं
शचीसुतमचीकरत् क इह भूरिभाग्योदयः ॥

*mahāpuruṣamānināṁ suramunīśvarāṇāṁ nijaṁ*
*padāmbujamajānatāṁ kimapi garvanirvāsanam |*
*aho nayanagocaraṁ nigamacakracūḍācaraṁ*
*śacīsutamacīkarat ka iha bhūribhāgyodayaḥ ||*

*Word for word:* maha-purusha-maninam — who think themselves great persons; sura-muni-ishvaranam — of the lords of the gods and sages; nijam — His own; pada-ambujam — lotus feet; ajanatam — not knowing; kim api — indescribably; garva-nirvasanam — who drives out the pride; aho — oh!; nayana-gocaram — visible to the eyes; nigama-cakra-cuda-caram — sought by the crowns (the Upanishads) of the whole host of the Vedas; shaci-sutam — the son of Shachi; acikarat — has made; kah — who; iha — here, in this world; bhuri-bhagya-udayah — a great rising of fortune (an exceedingly fortunate one).

**29.** Oh, who in this world is that great fortunate one who has made visible even to one such as me the son of Shachi — He whom the crowns of the whole host of the Vedas seek! He drives out, in an indescribable way, the pride of the lords of the gods and of the sages, who think themselves great and do not know His lotus feet.

### Verse 30

सर्वसाधनहीनोऽपि परमाश्चर्यवैभवे ।
गौराङ्गे न्यस्तभावो यः सर्वार्थपूर्ण एव सः ॥

*sarvasādhanahīno'pi paramāścaryavaibhave |*
*gaurāṅge nyastabhāvo yaḥ sarvārthapūrṇa eva saḥ ||*

*Word for word:* sarva-sadhana-hinah — devoid of all means of practice; api — even; parama-ashcarya-vaibhave — whose glory is supremely wonderful; gaurange — in Gauranga; nyasta-bhavah — who has placed his heart; yah — who; sarva-artha-purnah — fulfilled in every goal (has attained the highest goal, prema); eva — surely; sah — he.

**30.** Even if a man is devoid of every means of spiritual practice, if his heart is given to Gauranga, whose glory is wonderful beyond all, he has attained every goal.

### Verse 31

अप्यगण्यमहापुण्यमनन्यशरणं हरेः ।

*Word for word:* api — even; aganya-maha-punyam — possessing countless great merits; ananya-sharanam — having no other refuge; hareh — (than) Hari; anupasita-caitanyam — who has not worshipped Chaitanya; adhanyam — unfortunate (unworthy of the wealth of love); manyate — considers; matih — (my) mind.

**31.** Even if someone possesses countless great merits and seeks no refuge other than Hari, my mind still considers him unfortunate if he has not worshipped Chaitanya.

### Verse 32

किमेतान् शोचामो विषयरसमत्तान्नरपशून्

*dhigastu brahmāhaṁ vadanapariphullān jaḍamatīn |*
*kimetān śocāmo viṣayarasamattānnarapaśūn*

*Word for word:* kriya-asaktan — those attached to rituals; dhig dhig — shame, shame; vikata-tapasah — on harsh ascetics; dhik — shame; ca — and; yaminah — on self-restraining yogis (by yama and the rest); dhik astu — let there be shame; brahma aham — "I am Brahman"; vadana-pariphullan — (on those whose) faces blossom (at these words); jada-matin — dull-witted; kim — why; etan — over them; shocamah — should we grieve; vishaya-rasa-mattan — intoxicated with the taste of sense objects; nara-pashun — two-legged beasts; na — not; keshancit — to any of them; leshah api — even a drop; ahaha — alas!; militah — has come; gaura-madhunah — of the honey (of the feet) of Gaura.

**32.** Shame, shame on those attached to rituals and therefore dull-witted; shame on harsh ascetics and self-restraining yogis! Shame on those whose faces blossom at the mere words "I am Brahman"! And why should we grieve over these two-legged beasts intoxicated with the taste of sense pleasure? Alas, not one of them has received even a drop of the honey of Gaura.

### Verse 33

*Word for word:* pashanah — a stone; parishecitah — however watered; amrita-rasaih — with nectar juices; na eva — never; ankurah — a sprout; sambhavet — will appear; langulam — the tail; sarama-pateh — of a dog; vivrinatah — of one straightening; syat — will be; asya — its; na eva — never; arjavam — straightness; hastau — the hands; unnayata — (by one) raising; budhah — O wise ones; katham — how; aho — oh!; dharyam — can be held; vidhoh — of the moon; mandalam — the disc; sarvam — every; sadhanam — means of practice; astu — let there be; gaura-karuna-abhave — without Gaura's mercy; na — there is no; bhava-utsavah — festival of feeling (love).

**33.** However much a stone is watered with nectar, no sprout will appear; however much one straightens a dog's tail, it will never become straight; O wise ones, however high one raises one's hands, how can one hold the disc of the moon? Let there be every means of practice — but without Gaura's mercy there is no festival of *bhava* (love).

### Verse 34

**34.** When Gaurachandra has descended and the ocean of love has spread wide, its multitude of jewels (the nine kinds of *bhakti* and the rest) shining brightly — whoever has remained poor is poor indeed.

### Verse 35

**35.** When Gaurachandra has descended and the ocean of love has spread wide, those who do not plunge into it drown in the ocean of great misfortunes.

### Verse 36

प्रसारितमहाप्रेमपीयूषरससागरे ।

**36.** When Chaitanya-chandra has appeared and the ocean of the nectar *rasa* of great love has spread forth — whoever has remained poor is poor indeed.

### Verse 37

न विदुः सर्वशास्त्रज्ञा ह्यपि भ्राम्यन्ति ते जनाः ॥

**37.** If people, even those who know all the shastras, do not know that Chaitanya is God, they merely wander about in this world devoid of consciousness (*a-chaitanya*).

### Verse 38

*vāraṁ vāraṁ vrajapatiguṇān gāya gāyeti jalpan*

*Word for word:* svadam svadam — again and again tasting; madhurima-bharam — the fullness of sweetness; sviya-nama-avalinam — of His own names; madam madam — again and again becoming intoxicated; kim api — indescribably; vivashibhuta-visrasta-gatrah — losing control, His body gone limp; varam varam — again and again; vraja-pati-gunan — the qualities of the lord of Vraja; gaya gaya — "sing, sing"; iti — thus; jalpan — murmuring; gaurah — Gaura; drishtah — seen; sakrit api — even once; na — not; yaih — by whom; durghata — hard to attain; teshu — for them; bhaktih — bhakti.

**38.** Again and again tasting the fullness of the sweetness of His own names, again and again becoming intoxicated, indescribably losing control of Himself so that His body goes limp, again and again murmuring, "Sing, sing of the qualities of the lord of Vraja!" — such is Gaura. Those who have never once seen Him — how can they have *bhakti*?

*Word for word:* vina bijam — without a seed; kim na — could not; ankura-jananam — the appearance of a sprout; andhah api — even a blind man; na katham — why not; prapashyet — see; no — not; panguh — a lame man; giri-shikharam — a mountain peak; arohati — climbs; katham — why; yadi — if; shri-caitanye — towards Sri Chaitanya; hari-rasa-maya-ashcarya-vibhave — whose wondrous wealth is made of Hari-rasa (the rasa of bhakti); api — even; abhaktanam — of non-devotees; bhavi — will arise; katham api — somehow; para-prema-rabhasah — the impulse of the highest love (love for the Supreme).

### Verse 40

**40.** He who, by an unearthly unfolding of the pastimes of the intoxicating *rasa* of love, suddenly gives the festival of great and wondrous love even to one who is not among these virtuous companions and ministers of Sri Govinda — whoever does not turn his mind to Gaura Himself is here the greatest of fools, a two-legged beast.

### Verse 41

*prabhāvaṁ kaḥ sambhāvayatu parameśāditarataḥ |*

*Word for word:* kva — where (else); sa — such; nirankusha — unrestrained; kripa — mercy; kva — where; tat — such; vaibhavam — majesty; adbhutam — wondrous; kva — where; sa — such; vatsalata — tenderness (love for the devotees); shaure — O Shauri (Krishna); gaure — in the form of Gaura; yadrik — as; tava — Your; atmani — own self.

**56.** O Shauri (Krishna)! Where else is such unrestrained mercy, where such wondrous majesty, where such tenderness as in Yourself in the form of Gaura?

# Part Three

*Fidelity to the object of worship. Instruction to the world (verses 57–98)*

## Fidelity to the object of worship (upasya-nishtha)

### Verse 57

स्वतेजसा कृष्णपदारविन्दमहारसावेशितविश्वमीशम् ।
कमप्यशेषश्रुतिगूढवेशं गौराङ्गमङ्गीकुरु मूढचेतः ॥ ५७ ॥

*svatejasā kṛṣṇapadāravindamahārasāveśitaviśvamīśam |*
*kamapyaśeṣaśrutigūḍhaveśaṁ gaurāṅgamaṅgīkuru mūḍhacetaḥ || 57 ||*

*Word for word:* sva-tejasa — by His own splendour; krishna-pada-aravinda-maha-rasa-aveshita-vishvam — who has immersed the world in the great rasa of Krishna's lotus feet; isham — the Lord; kam api — indescribable; asesha-shruti-gudha-vesham — whose form is hidden in all the shrutis; gaurangam — Gauranga; angikuru — accept; mudha-cetah — O foolish heart.

**57.** O foolish heart, accept Gauranga — the Lord who by His own splendour has immersed the whole world in the great *rasa* of Krishna's lotus feet, the indescribable One whose form is hidden in all the *shrutis*!

### Verse 58

श्रवणमननसङ्कीर्त्यादिभक्त्या मुरारे-
र्यदि परमपुमर्थं साधयेत् कोऽपि भद्रम् ।
मम तु परमपारप्रेमपीयूषसिन्धोः
किमपि रसरहस्यं गौरधाम्नो नमस्यम् ॥ ५८ ॥

*śravaṇamananasaṅkīrtyādibhaktyā murāre-*
*ryadi paramapumarthaṁ sādhayet ko'pi bhadram |*
*mama tu paramapārapremapīyūṣasindhoḥ*
*kimapi rasarahasyaṁ gauradhāmno namasyam || 58 ||*

*Word for word:* shravana-manana-sankirti-adi-bhaktya — by devotion consisting of hearing, reflection, sankirtana and the rest; murareh — to Murari; yadi — if; parama-pum-artham — the highest goal of man; sadhayet — attains; kah api — someone; bhadram — good (for him); mama — for me; tu — but; parama-apara-prema-piyusha-sindhoh — of the shoreless ocean of the nectar of supreme love; kim api — indescribable; rasa-rahasyam — the secret of rasa; gaura-dhamnah — of (Hari) with a golden radiance; namasyam — is to be worshipped.

**58.** If someone attains the highest goal of man through devotion to Murari — hearing, reflection, *sankirtana* and the rest — good for him. But for me the object of worship is the indescribable secret of the *rasa* of Hari clothed in a golden radiance,[^03-1] a shoreless ocean of the nectar of supreme love.

### Verse 59

ईशं भजन्तु पुरुषार्थचतुष्टयाशा
दासा भवन्तु च विहाय हरेरुपास्यान् ।
किञ्चिद्रहस्यपदलोभितधीरहं तु
चैतन्यचन्द्रचरणं शरणं करोमि ॥ ५९ ॥

*īśaṁ bhajantu puruṣārthacatuṣṭayāśā*
*dāsā bhavantu ca vihāya harerupāsyān |*
*kiñcidrahasyapadalobhitadhīrahaṁ tu*
*caitanyacandracaraṇaṁ śaraṇaṁ karomi || 59 ||*

*Word for word:* isham — the Lord; bhajantu — let them worship; purusha-artha-catushtaya-ashah — hoping for the four goals of man; dasah — servants; bhavantu — let them become; ca — and; vihaya — giving up; hareh — (other than) Hari; upasyan — objects of worship; kincit — a certain; rahasya-pada-lobhita-dhih — whose mind is enticed by a secret state; aham — I; tu — but; caitanya-candra-caranam — the feet of Chaitanya-chandra; sharanam karomi — take as refuge.

**59.** Let some worship the Lord in the hope of the four goals of man; let others, giving up other objects of worship, become servants of Hari. But my mind is enticed by a certain secret state, and I take refuge in the feet of Chaitanya-chandra.

### Verse 60

निष्ठां प्राप्ता व्यवहृतिततिर्लौकिकी वैदिकी वा
या वा लज्जा प्रहसनसमुद्गाननाट्योत्सवेषु ।
ये वाभूवन्नहह सहजप्राणदेहार्थधर्मा
गौरश्चौरः सकलमहरत् कोऽपि मे तीव्रवीर्यः ॥ ६० ॥

*niṣṭhāṁ prāptā vyavahṛtitatirlaukikī vaidikī vā*
*yā vā lajjā prahasanasamudgānanāṭyotsaveṣu |*
*ye vābhūvannahaha sahajaprāṇadehārthadharmā*
*gauraścauraḥ sakalamaharat ko'pi me tīvravīryaḥ || 60 ||*

*Word for word:* nishtham prapta — in which I was established; vyavahriti-tatih — the series of duties; laukiki — worldly; vaidiki — Vedic; va — or; ya — which; va — or; lajja — bashfulness; prahasana-samudgana-natya-utsaveshu — at jesting, loud singing, dancing and festivals; ye — which; va — or; abhuvan — were; ahaha — alas; sahaja-prana-deha-artha-dharmah — the natural duties concerning life and body; gaurah — Gaura; caurah — thief; sakalam — all; aharat — stole; kah api — a certain; me — my; tivra-viryah — of irresistible might.

**60.** The whole series of worldly and Vedic duties in which I was established; my bashfulness at jesting, loud singing, dancing and festivals; the natural duties tied to life and body — alas! all of this has been stolen by a certain thief, Gaura, whose might is irresistible.

### Verse 61

सान्द्रानन्दोज्ज्वलरसमयप्रेमपीयूषसिन्धोः
कोटिं वर्षन् किमपि करुणास्निग्धनेत्राञ्चलेन ।
कोऽयं देवः कनककदलीगर्भगौराङ्गयष्टि-
श्चेतोऽकस्मान्मम निजपदे गाढयुक्तं चकार ॥ ६१ ॥

*sāndrānandojjvalarasamayapremapīyūṣasindhoḥ*
*koṭiṁ varṣan kimapi karuṇāsnigdhanetrāñcalena |*
*ko'yaṁ devaḥ kanakakadalīgarbhagaurāṅgayaṣṭi-*
*śceto'kasmānmama nijapade gāḍhayuktaṁ cakāra || 61 ||*

*Word for word:* sandra-ananda-ujjvala-rasa-maya-prema-piyusha-sindhoh — of the ocean of the nectar of love made of the dense and radiant rasa of bliss; kotim — millions; varshan — pouring forth; kim api — indescribable; karuna-snigdha-netra-ancalena — with the corner of His eyes moist with compassion; kah — who; ayam — this; devah — Lord; kanaka-kadali-garbha-gaura-anga-yashtih — whose slender body is golden like the heart of a golden plantain tree; cetah — mind; akasmat — suddenly; mama — my; nija-pade — to His feet; gadha-yuktam — firmly bound; cakara — made.

**61.** Pouring forth millions of oceans of the nectar of love made of the dense and radiant *rasa* of bliss through the indescribable corner of His eyes moist with compassion — who is this Lord, His slender golden body like the heart of a golden plantain tree, who has suddenly bound my mind fast to His feet?

### Verse 62

स्वयं देवो यत्र द्रुतकनकगौरः करुणया
महाप्रेमानन्दोज्ज्वलरसवपुः प्रादुरभवत् ।
नवद्वीपे तस्मिन् प्रतिभवनभक्त्युत्सवमये
मनो मे वैकुण्ठादपि च मधुरे धाम्नि रमते ॥ ६२ ॥

*svayaṁ devo yatra drutakanakagauraḥ karuṇayā*
*mahāpremānandojjvalarasavapuḥ prādurabhavat |*
*navadvīpe tasmin pratibhavanabhaktyutsavamaye*
*mano me vaikuṇṭhādapi ca madhure dhāmni ramate || 62 ||*

*Word for word:* svayam — Himself; devah — God; yatra — where; druta-kanaka-gaurah — golden as molten gold; karunaya — out of mercy; maha-prema-ananda-ujjvala-rasa-vapuh — whose body is the radiant rasa of great love and bliss; pradurabhavat — appeared; navadvipe — in Navadvipa; tasmin — in that; prati-bhavana-bhakti-utsava-maye — where every house is full of the festival of bhakti; manah — mind; me — my; vaikunthat — than Vaikuntha; api ca — even; madhure — sweet; dhamni — in the abode; ramate — delights.

**62.** Where God Himself, golden as molten gold, His body the radiant *rasa* of great love and bliss, appeared out of mercy — in Navadvipa, where every house is a festival of *bhakti* — in that sweet abode my mind delights even more than in Vaikuntha.

### Verse 63

यत्तद्वदन्तु शास्त्राणि यत्तद्व्याख्यान्तु तार्किकाः ।
जीवनं मम चैतन्यपादाम्भोजसुधैव तु ॥ ६३ ॥

*yattadvadantu śāstrāṇi yattadvyākhyāntu tārkikāḥ |*
*jīvanaṁ mama caitanyapādāmbhojasudhaiva tu || 63 ||*

*Word for word:* yat tat — whatever; vadantu — let them say; shastrani — the shastras; yat tat — whatever; vyakhyantu — let them explain; tarkikah — the logicians; jivanam — life; mama — my; caitanya-pada-ambhoja-sudha — the nectar of the lotus feet of Chaitanya; eva tu — only.

**63.** Let the shastras say what they will, let the logicians explain as they will — my life is only the nectar of the lotus feet of Chaitanya.

### Verse 64

पतन्ति यदि सिद्धयः करतले स्वयं दुर्लभाः
स्वयं च यदि सेवकीभवितुमागताः स्युः सुराः ।
किमन्यदिदमेव वा यदि चतुर्भुजं स्याद्वपु-
स्तथापि मम नो मनाक् चलति गौरचन्द्रान्मनः ॥ ६४ ॥

*patanti yadi siddhayaḥ karatale svayaṁ durlabhāḥ*
*svayaṁ ca yadi sevakībhavitumāgatāḥ syuḥ surāḥ |*
*kimanyadidameva vā yadi caturbhujaṁ syādvapu-*
*stathāpi mama no manāk calati gauracandrānmanaḥ || 64 ||*

*Word for word:* patanti — fall; yadi — if; siddhayah — siddhis; kara-tale — into the palm; svayam — of themselves; durlabhah — rare; svayam — themselves; ca — and; yadi — if; sevaki-bhavitum — to become servants; agatah syuh — should come; surah — the gods; kim anyat — what more; idam eva va — or even this; yadi — if; catur-bhujam — four-armed; syat — there were; vapuh — a body; tathapi — still; mama — my; no — not; manak — in the least; calati — moves; gaura-candrat — from Gaurachandra; manah — mind.

**64.** Should rare *siddhis* fall into my palm of themselves, should the gods themselves come to become my servants, should I even — what more? — have a four-armed body, still my mind would not stir for a moment from Gaurachandra.

### Verse 65

वासो मे वरमस्तु घोरदहनज्वालावलीपञ्जरे
श्रीचैतन्यपदारविन्दविमुखैर्मा कुत्रचित् सङ्गमः ।
वैकुण्ठादिपदं स्वयं च मिलितं नो मे मनो लिप्सते
पादाम्भोजरजश्छटा यदि मनाक् गौरस्य नो रस्यते ॥ ६५ ॥

*vāso me varamastu ghoradahanajvālāvalīpañjare*
*śrīcaitanyapadāravindavimukhairmā kutracit saṅgamaḥ |*
*vaikuṇṭhādipadaṁ svayaṁ ca militaṁ no me mano lipsate*
*pādāmbhojarajaśchaṭā yadi manāk gaurasya no rasyate || 65 ||*

*Word for word:* vasah — dwelling; me — for me; varam astu — let it be better; ghora-dahana-jvala-avali-panjare — in a cage of the rows of flames of a terrible fire; shri-caitanya-pada-aravinda-vimukhaih — with those averse to the lotus feet of Sri Chaitanya; ma — let there not be; kutracit — anywhere; sangamah — association; vaikuntha-adi-padam — the abode of Vaikuntha and the rest; svayam — of itself; ca — and; militam — come; no — not; me — my; manah — mind; lipsate — desires; pada-ambhoja-rajah-chata — the lustre of the dust of the lotus feet; yadi — if; manak — a little; gaurasya — of Gaura; no — not; rasyate — is tasted.

**65.** Better for me to live in a cage of the tongues of a terrible fire than to associate anywhere with those who have turned away from the lotus feet of Sri Chaitanya. Even if the abode of Vaikuntha and the rest came to me of itself, my mind would not desire it if it did not taste even a little of the lustre of the dust of Gaura's lotus feet.

### Verse 66

लोके वा प्रकटास्तु नाम महती सिद्धिश्चमत्कारिणी ।
कामं चारुचतुर्भुजत्वमयतामाराध्य विश्वेश्वरं
चेतो मे बहु मन्यते नहि नहि श्रीगौरभक्तिं विना ॥ ६६ ॥

*āstāṁ nāma mahān mahāniti ravaḥ sarvakṣamāmaṇḍale*
*loke vā prakaṭāstu nāma mahatī siddhiścamatkāriṇī |*
*kāmaṁ cārucaturbhujatvamayatāmārādhya viśveśvaraṁ*
*ceto me bahu manyate nahi nahi śrīgaurabhaktiṁ vinā || 66 ||*

*Word for word:* astam — let there be; nama — indeed; mahan mahan iti — "great, great"; ravah — the cry; sarva-kshama-mandale — over the whole circle of the earth; loke — in the world; va — or; prakata astu — let be manifested; nama — indeed; mahati — great; siddhih — siddhi; camatkarini — wondrous; kamam — let it be; caru-catur-bhujatvam — a beautiful four-armed form; ayatam — let me attain; aradhya — having worshipped; vishva-ishvaram — the Lord of the universe; cetah — mind; me — my; bahu manyate — values; nahi nahi — not at all; shri-gaura-bhaktim vina — without devotion to Sri Gaura.

**66.** Let the cry go round me over the whole earth: "Great, great!" Let a great and wondrous *siddhi* be manifested to the world. Let me, worshipping the Lord of the universe, attain a beautiful four-armed form — no, no, without devotion to Sri Gaura my mind values none of this!

### Verse 67

प्रेमावेशितसर्वभूतहृदयेत्याश्चर्यधामन्निति ।
गौराङ्गेति गुणार्णवेति रसरूपेति स्वनामप्रिये-
त्यश्रान्तं मम जल्पतो जनिरियं यायादिति प्रार्थये ॥ ६७ ॥

*caitanyeti kṛpāmayeti paramodāreti nānāvidha-*
*premāveśitasarvabhūtahṛdayetyāścaryadhāmanniti |*
*gaurāṅgeti guṇārṇaveti rasarūpeti svanāmapriye-*

*Word for word:* caitanya iti — "Chaitanya!"; kripa-maya iti — "Merciful One!"; parama-udara iti — "Supremely generous One!"; nana-vidha-prema-aveshita-sarva-bhuta-hridaya iti — "You who have filled the hearts of all beings with love of many kinds!"; ashcarya-dhaman iti — "You of wondrous radiance!"; gauranga iti — "Gauranga!"; guna-arnava iti — "Ocean of virtues!"; rasa-rupa iti — "Embodiment of rasa!"; sva-nama-priya iti — "You who love Your own names!"; ashrantam — ceaselessly; mama — my; jalpatah — repeating; janih — life; iyam — this; yayat — may pass; iti — thus; prarthaye — I pray.

**67.** "Chaitanya! Merciful One! Supremely generous One! You who have filled the hearts of all beings with love of many kinds! You whose wondrous radiance is like molten gold! Gauranga! Ocean of virtues! Embodiment of *rasa*! You who love Your own names!" — I pray that, ceaselessly repeating this, I may pass this life.

### Verse 68

कदा शौरे गौरे वपुषि परमप्रेमरसदे
सदेकप्राणे निष्कपटकृतभावोऽस्मि भविता ।
कदा वा तस्यालौकिकसदनुमानेन मम हृ-
द्यकस्मात् श्रीराधापदनखमणिज्योतिरुदगात् ॥ ६८ ॥

*kadā śaure gaure vapuṣi paramapremarasade*
*sadekaprāṇe niṣkapaṭakṛtabhāvo'smi bhavitā |*
*kadā vā tasyālaukikasadanumānena mama hṛ-*
*dyakasmāt śrīrādhāpadanakhamaṇijyotirudagāt || 68 ||*

*Word for word:* kada — when; shaure — O Shauri; gaure — of Gaura; vapushi — towards the body; parama-prema-rasa-de — which gives the rasa of supreme love; sat-eka-prane — the one life of the saints; nishkapata-krita-bhavah — having a guileless feeling; asmi bhavita — shall I be; kada — when; va — or; tasya — from that; alaukika-sat-anumanena — by an unearthly true inference; mama — my; hridi — in the heart; akasmat — suddenly; shri-radha-pada-nakha-mani-jyotih — the radiance of the jewel-like toenails of Sri Radha; udagat — will rise.

**68.** O Shauri! When will I have a guileless feeling for Your body of Gaura, which gives the *rasa* of supreme love, the one life of the saints? Or when, by an unearthly true inference from that feeling, will the radiance of the jewel-like toenails of Sri Radha suddenly rise in my heart?

### Verse 69

उद्दामदामनकदामगणाभिराम-
मारामराममविरामगृहीतनाम ।
कारुण्यधाम कनकोज्ज्वलगौरधाम
चैतन्यनाम परमं कलयाम धाम ॥ ६९ ॥

*uddāmadāmanakadāmagaṇābhirāma-*
*mārāmarāmamavirāmagṛhītanāma |*
*kāruṇyadhāma kanakojjvalagauradhāma*
*caitanyanāma paramaṁ kalayāma dhāma || 69 ||*

*Word for word:* uddama-damanaka-dama-gana-abhiramam — charming with garlands of luxuriant damanaka flowers; arama-ramam — delighting people (sporting in gardens); avirama-grihita-nama — ceaselessly taking the Name; karunya-dhama — the abode of compassion; kanaka-ujjvala-gaura-dhama — the abode of radiant golden lustre; caitanya-nama — named Chaitanya; paramam — supreme; kalayama — we contemplate; dhama — abode.

**69.** Charming with garlands of luxuriant *damanaka* flowers, delighting all people, sporting in secluded gardens, ceaselessly taking the Name, the abode of compassion, the abode of Gaura's golden radiance — the supreme abode named Chaitanya we contemplate.

### Verse 70

हरेरेव भ्राजन्मुखकमलभृङ्गेक्षणयुगम् ।
समुत्तुङ्गप्रेमोन्मदरसतरङ्गं मृगदृशा-
मनङ्गं गौराङ्गं स्मरतु गतसङ्गं मम मनः ॥ ७० ॥

*sadā raṅge nīlācalaśikharaśṛṅge vilasato*
*harereva bhrājanmukhakamalabhṛṅgekṣaṇayugam |*
*samuttuṅgapremonmadarasataraṅgaṁ mṛgadṛśā-*
*manaṅgaṁ gaurāṅgaṁ smaratu gatasaṅgaṁ mama manaḥ || 70 ||*

*Word for word:* sada — always; range — in festivity; nilacala-shikhara-shringe — on the summit of Nilachala; vilasatah — sporting; hareh — of Hari; eva — indeed; bhrajat-mukha-kamala-bhringa-ikshana-yugam — whose two eyes are bees on the shining lotus face; samuttunga-prema-unmada-rasa-tarangam — in whom rise high waves of the intoxicating rasa of love; mriga-drisham — of the doe-eyed; anangam — Ananga (Kamadeva); gaurangam — Gauranga; smaratu — may remember; gata-sangam — detached; mama — my; manah — mind.

**70.** May my mind remember Gauranga, detached from all: His eyes are bees on the shining lotus face of Hari, who ever sports on the festive summit of Niladri;[^03-2] in Him rise high waves of the intoxicating *rasa* of love; to the doe-eyed He is Ananga (the bodiless Kamadeva) himself.

### Verse 71

अलङ्कारः पङ्केरुहनयननिःस्यन्दिपयसां
पृषद्भिः सन्मुक्ताफलसुललितैर्यस्य वपुषि ।
उदञ्चद्रोमाञ्चैरपि च परमा यस्य सुषमा
तमालम्बे गौरं हरिमरुणरोचिष्णुवसनम् ॥ ७१ ॥

*alaṅkāraḥ paṅkeruhanayananiḥsyandipayasāṁ*
*pṛṣadbhiḥ sanmuktāphalasulalitairyasya vapuṣi |*

**84.** Put away your vain absorption in rituals; do not let even talk of the path of contemplation of the Self come anywhere near your ears; do not cherish the delusion of the body and the rest — and by Gaura's mercy the crown of the goals of man, supremely wondrous and sweet, will come to you.

*alaṁ śāstrābhyāsairalamahaha tīrthāṭanikayā*
*sadā yoṣidvyāghryāstrasata vitathāṁ thūtkuru divam |*

O fortunate ones, take refuge in Gauranga, who has assumed the guise of a *sannyasi* and dances on the ocean shore, intoxicated with His own *rasa*.

### Verse 86

### Verse 87

यथा यथा गौरपदारविन्दे विन्देत भक्तिं कृतपुण्यराशिः ।

*rnidhānaṁ brahmeśārcita iha hi caitanyacaraṇaḥ |*

*Word for word:* aparasya — shoreless; prema-ujjvala-rasa-rahasya-amrita-nidheh — of the ocean of the nectar of the secret of the rasa radiant with love; nidhanam — the treasury; brahma-isha-arcitah — worshipped by Brahma and Shiva; iha — here; hi — indeed; caitanya-caranah — the feet of Chaitanya; atah — therefore; tam — them; dhyayantah — meditating on; pranaya-bharatah — in the fullness of love; yantu — let them go; sharanam — for refuge; tam eva — them alone; pronmattah — as if mad; tam — them; iha — here; kila — indeed; gayantu — let them sing; kritinah — the wise.

**88.** The feet of Chaitanya, worshipped by Brahma and Shiva, are the treasury of the shoreless ocean of the nectar of the secret of the *rasa* radiant with love. Therefore let the wise, meditating on them, take refuge in them in the fullness of love, and let them, as if mad, sing of them alone here.

### Verse 89

### Verse 90

*Word for word:* aho — oh; na — not; durlabha — hard to attain; muktih — liberation; na ca — nor; bhaktih — bhakti; su-durlabha — very hard to attain; gaura-candra-prasadah — the grace of Gaurachandra; tu — but; vaikunthe — in Vaikuntha; api — even; su-durlabhah — very hard to attain.

### Verse 91

*Word for word:* bhajantu — let them worship; caitanya-pada-aravindam — the lotus feet of Chaitanya; bhavantu — let them be; sat-bhakti-rasena — with the rasa of true bhakti; purnah — filled; anandayantu — let them delight; tri-jagat — the three worlds; vicitram — manifold; madhurya-saubhagya-daya-kshama-adyaih — with sweetness, good fortune, compassion, forbearance and the rest.

**91.** Worship the lotus feet of Chaitanya, be filled with the *rasa* of true *bhakti*, and delight the three worlds with manifold sweetness, the highest fortune of tasting *prema*, compassion, forbearance and the rest.

### Verse 92

*saṁsārasindhutaraṇe hṛdayaṁ yadi syāt*
*saṅkīrtanāmṛtarase ramate manaścet |*
*premāmbudhau viharaṇe yadi cittavṛtti-*

*Word for word:* samsara-sindhu-tarane — for crossing the ocean of worldly existence; hridayam — the heart; yadi — if; syat — is (eager); sankirtana-amrita-rase — in the nectar rasa of sankirtana; ramate — delights; manah — the mind; cet — if; prema-ambudhau — in the ocean of love; viharane — for sporting; yadi — if; citta-vrittih — the inclination of thought; caitanya-candra-carane — at the feet of Chaitanya-chandra; sharanam — refuge; prayatu — let it take.

**92.** If the heart longs to cross the ocean of worldly existence, if the mind delights in the nectar *rasa* of *sankirtana*, if thought wishes to sport in the ocean of love — let it take refuge in the feet of Chaitanya-chandra.

### Verse 93

**93.** Let them practise knowledge, renunciation, *bhakti* and the rest as they may — but where is anything equal to what is attained by devotion to the lotus feet of Chaitanya?[^03-3]

*acaitanyamidaṁ viśvaṁ yadi caitanyamīśvaram |*

*Word for word:* acaitanyam — devoid of consciousness (of devotion to Krishna); idam — this; vishvam — world; yadi — if; caitanyam — Chaitanya; ishvaram — the Lord; na bhajet — does not worship; sarvatah — on every side; mrityuh — death; upasyam — worshipped; amara-uttamaih — by the best of the gods.

### Verse 95

*Word for word:* asha — hope; yasya — whose; pada-dvandve — in the two feet; caitanyasya mahaprabhoh — of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu; tasya — to him; indrah — Indra; dasavat — like a servant; bhati — appears; ka katha — what to speak; nripa-kitake — of a worm of a king.

**95.** For one whose hope is in the two feet of Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, Indra himself seems like a servant — what to speak of a worm of a king!

### Verse 96

### Verse 97

# Part Four

*The supremacy of Sri Chaitanya. The glory of His descent. The beauty and dance of Gaura. Lament (verses 99–142)*

## The supremacy of Sri Chaitanya (utkarsha)

### Verse 99

मत्तकेशरिकिशोरविक्रमः प्रेमसिन्धुजगदाप्लवोद्यमः ।
कोऽपि दिव्यनवहेमकन्दलीकोमलो जयति गौरचन्द्रमाः ॥

*mattakeśarikiśoravikramaḥ premasindhujagadāplavodyamaḥ |*
*ko'pi divyanavahemakandalīkomalo jayati gauracandramāḥ ||*

*Word for word:* matta-keshari-kishora-vikramah — with the valour of a young lion in rut; prema-sindhu-jagat-aplava-udyamah — eager to flood the world with the ocean of love; kah api — indescribable; divya-nava-hema-kandali-komalah — tender as a wondrous, newly blossomed golden kandali flower; jayati — glory to; gaura-candramah — Gaura-chandra, the Moon.

**99.** With the valour of a young lion in rut, eager to flood the world with the ocean of love, tender as a wondrous, newly blossomed golden *kandali* flower — glory to the indescribable Gaura-chandra, the Moon!

### Verse 100

*saundarye kāmakoṭiḥ sakalajanasamāhlādane candrakoṭi-*
*rvātsalye mātṛkoṭistridaśaviṭapināṁ koṭiraudāryasāre |*

*Word for word:* saundarye — in beauty; kama-kotih — millions of Kamadevas; sakala-jana-samahladane — in delighting all people; candra-kotih — millions of moons; vatsalye — in tenderness; matri-kotih — millions of mothers; tridasha-vitapinam — of the trees of heaven; kotih — millions; audarya-sare — in the essence of generosity; gambhirye — in depth; ambhodhi-kotih — millions of oceans; madhurimani — in sweetness; sudha-kshira-madhvika-kotih — millions of nectars, milks and honeys; gaurah — Gaura; devah — the Lord; sah — He; jiyat — glory to; pranaya-rasa-pade — in the realm of the rasa of love; darshita-ashcarya-kotih — who has shown millions of wonders.

**100.** In beauty — millions of Kamadevas; in delighting all people — millions of moons; in tenderness — millions of mothers; in the essence of generosity — millions of trees of heaven; in depth — millions of oceans; in sweetness — millions of nectars, milks and honeys; in the realm of the *rasa* of love He has shown millions of wonders. Glory to the Lord Gaura!

**139.** This is the same blessed capital of Gauda, the same shore of the ocean, the same Sri Purushottama (Jagannatha), the same names of Madhupati — but, Hari, Hari! — such a festival of love is seen nowhere. Ah, Chaitanya, treasury of mercy, shall I ever see Your glory again?

### Verse 140

यदि निगदितमीनाद्यंशवद्गौरचन्द्रो
न तदपि स हि कश्चिच्छक्तिलीलाविकाशः ।
अतुलसकलशक्त्याश्चर्यलीलाप्रकाशै-
रनधिगतमहत्त्वः पूर्ण एवावतीर्णः ॥

*yadi nigaditamīnādyaṁśavadgauracandro*
*na tadapi sa hi kaścicchaktilīlāvikāśaḥ |*
*atulasakalaśaktyāścaryalīlāprakāśai-*
*ranadhigatamahattvaḥ pūrṇa evāvatīrṇaḥ ||*

*Word for word:* yadi — if; nigadita-mina-adi-amshavat — like the named partial incarnations, the Fish and the rest; gaura-candrah — Gaurachandra; na — not; tat api — so; sah — He; hi — for; kashcit — some; shakti-lila-vikashah — manifestation of potency and pastime; atula-sakala-shakti-ashcarya-lila-prakashaih — with the manifestation of wondrous pastimes with the matchless fullness of all potencies; anadhigata-mahattvah — whose greatness is unfathomable; purnah — complete; eva — indeed; avatirnah — descended.

**140.** If one says that Gaurachandra is like the partial incarnations — the Fish and the rest — He is not so: for they are only the manifestation of some potency and pastime. But He, whose greatness is unfathomable, revealing wondrous pastimes with the matchless fullness of all potencies, descended as the complete [Lord].

### Verse 141

ब्रह्मेशादिमहाश्चर्यमहिमापि महाप्रभुः ।
मुग्धबालोदितं श्रुत्वा स्निग्धोऽवश्यं भविष्यति ॥

*brahmeśādimahāścaryamahimāpi mahāprabhuḥ |*
*mugdhabāloditaṁ śrutvā snigdho'vaśyaṁ bhaviṣyati ||*

*Word for word:* brahma-isha-adi-maha-ashcarya-mahima — of glory more wondrous than (the source of) Brahma, Shiva and others; api — though; mahaprabhuh — Mahaprabhu; mugdha-bala-uditam — the prattle of an innocent child; shrutva — hearing; snigdhah — tender; avashyam — surely; bhavishyati — will be.

**141.** Though Mahaprabhu has a glory more wondrous even than that of the Original One, the source of Brahma and Shiva, on hearing the prattle of an innocent child He will surely be tender.

### Verse 142

दृष्टं न शास्त्रं गुरवो न दृष्टा
विवेचितं नापि बुधैः सुबुद्ध्या ।
यथा तथा जल्पतु बालभावा-
त्तथैव मे गौरहरिः प्रसीदतु ॥

*dṛṣṭaṁ na śāstraṁ guravo na dṛṣṭā*
*vivecitaṁ nāpi budhaiḥ subuddhyā |*
*yathā tathā jalpatu bālabhāvā-*
*ttathaiva me gaurahariḥ prasīdatu ||*

*Word for word:* drishtam — seen; na — not; shastram — the shastras; guravah — gurus; na — not; drishtah — seen; vivecitam — examined; na api — nor; budhaih — by the wise; su-buddhya — with keen intellect; yatha tatha — however; jalpatu — may prattle; bala-bhavat — like a child; tatha eva — even so; me — with me; gaura-harih — Gaura-Hari; prasidatu — may be pleased.

**142.** I have not seen the shastras, I have not seen gurus, the wise have not examined [my words] with their keen intellect — so however I may prattle like a child, may Gaura-Hari be pleased with me.

*Thus ends the Sri Sri Chaitanya-chandramrita in twelve sections, composed by Shripada Srimat Prabodhananda Sarasvati — the jewel in the crown of the family of the tridandi-gosvamis, the best of wandering teachers, an eminent associate of Gaura.*

[^04-1]: "Knowers of non-duality" (*advaita*) — the experts in non-dualism (Advaita Vedanta); this is also how Bhaktisiddhanta explains the word.

[^04-2]: *Nagara-vara* — according to Bhaktisiddhanta, "the crown of the highest knowers of *rasa*": Gaura is Krishna Himself, enacting His pastime on the side of the *ashraya* (tasting the feelings of Radha). It does not mean a worldly "gallant lover," nor does it support the doctrine of *gaura-nagari*, which Bhaktisiddhanta rejected.
