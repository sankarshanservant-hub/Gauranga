# Chapter 19. Nama-rahasya-patala

*On a moonlit night, by the samadhi of Haridasa, Mahaprabhu explains to the devotees the "Nama-rahasya-patala" ("The Section on the Secret of the Name", Sanatkumara's conversation with Narada from the Padma Purana): the chanting of the Name is the only dharma of Kali; the ten offences to the Name and how to avoid them; the service of the Name in the footsteps of Haridasa Thakura (pp. 69–87)*

*[p. 69]*

**1.** Once, when the moon had risen, Gaurangachand
came with the devotees to the shore of the ocean.

**2.** Sitting by the samadhi of Haridasa,[^1]
moonlike Gaura speaks to all the Vaishnavas:

**3.** "Listen, devotees! The dharma of the Kali age:
besides Sri Krishna-kirtana there is no other work.

**4.** Karma, jnana, yoga and meditation are weak means:
they are not the way to gain transcendent wealth.

**5.** Vows, renunciation, homa — all of it is material:
it does no good for attaining the transcendent.

**6.** By uttering, remembering and hearing Krishna's name
transcendent perfection is attained — so say the shrutis.

**7.** You will find the secret of Sri Nama in all the scriptures:
by merely uttering the Name you will gain spiritual happiness.

**8.** *Sri Shaunaka said: "It is heard that the glory of uttering the Name is great and wondrous: by merely uttering it a man may reach the supreme abode. Tell us now, Suta, the rule for chanting the Name."*[^2]

*[p. 70]*

**9.** *Suta said: "Listen, Shaunaka, I shall tell the conversation that is the means to liberation, which Narada once asked of Kumara; I will tell it to you. Once on the bank of the Yamuna Narada, with folded palms, asked Sanatkumara, who sat with a peaceful mind — having heard of the various dharmas and of transgressions of dharma:*

**10.** *Sri Narada said: 'That transgression of dharma by men of which Bhagavan has spoken — how can it be destroyed? Tell me, dear to the Lord.'"*

**11.** Explaining in particular something of the meaning of this patala,
I speak — Svarupa, Ramananda, listen attentively.

**12.** By the word "uttering" understand Sri Nama-kirtana,
which the devotees count on beads.

**13.** Sometimes, leaving the count, one chants countless names —
know for certain: that too is "uttering".

**14.** Uttered softly it is "japa", aloud — kirtana;
remembrance and kirtana are all included.

**15.** How the Name should be chanted so as to be perfect —
the rule for that is surely given in Sri Nama-kirtana.

*[p. 71]*

**16.** Sri Nama-kirtana is the eternal dharma of the jiva:
both in this world and in Vaikuntha it is the jiva's chief work.

**17.** For the jiva bound by maya it is the means of liberation;
for the liberated jiva it remains the goal itself.

**18.** All dharma without devotion described in the dharma-shastras,
all vows other than those prescribed for devotees,

**19.** all renunciation other than the detachment born of devotion,
sacrifices unfavourable to devotion — the worldly division;

**20.** all these good works, on consideration of sambandha,
the shastras proclaim [useful only as] favourable to devotion.

**21.** In the Kali age all this has become material dharma:
having lost its service to devotion, dharma has perished.

**22.** Therefore in the Kali age, apart from nama-sankirtana,
there is no other dharma — listen, devotees.

**23.** Whatever you see that transgresses this dharma,
carefully avoid it by the power of devotion.

**24.** *Sri Sanatkumara said: "Listen, Narada, dear to Govinda, knower of Govinda's dharma: you have asked about the cause of the world's liberation, which lies beyond darkness."*

*[p. 72]*

**25.** You, Narada, know the dharma of Sri Govinda,
are dear to Govinda, and cut the bonds of maya.

**26.** Your question is for the liberation of the world:
by your question and its answer the jiva will pass beyond darkness.

**27.** In Kali all dharma and adharma are full of darkness:
without the dharma of the Name the jiva's samsara does not end.

**28.** *"Those devoid of all good conduct, crooked-minded, fallen from the rites, deceivers of the world, given to hypocrisy, pride, drinking and slander, sinners, outcastes, the cruel, and others attached to wealth, wives and sons — all the lowest — even they, O twice-born, taking refuge at the lotus feet of Sri Govinda, become pure."*

**29.** Whoever takes refuge at the lotus feet of Sri Govinda —
all his sins are destroyed by the mercy of the Name.

**30.** Taking Krishna's name, he weeps and confesses his faults —
very soon, by the power of devotion, his sin departs.

**31.** What fruit has atonement by karma and jnana?
Its fruit is weak; it has no power.

**32.** As many sins as a sinner destroys by one name of Krishna,
so many he cannot commit even in many births.

**33.** There is no sin described in the smarta scriptures
that is not destroyed by one name of Krishna.

*[p. 73]*

**34.** Why then do the smarta people perform atonements?
For lack of piety their minds are drawn to karma.

**35.** By karmic atonement, tell me, desire does not go;
atonement by jnana purifies desire in the heart,

**36.** but after some days it grows gross again;
by devotion, however, avidya — the root of desire — departs.

**37.** Whoever, taking refuge at Govinda's feet,
takes the Name with entreaty and weeps —

**38.** for him are the sweet words from the Lord's own lips,
for the good of the jivas — see how many there are in the *Gita*:

**39.** *"Abandoning all dharmas, take refuge in Me alone: I shall free you from all sins; do not grieve. Even if a man of the worst conduct serves Me with undivided devotion, he must be considered a sadhu, for he has rightly resolved. Quickly he becomes righteous and attains lasting peace. O son of Kunti, declare it: My devotee never perishes."*[^3]

**40.** Therefore, giving up atonements and the rest that belong to karma,
the intelligent person serves Hari, the Lord of life.

*[p. 74]*

**41.** *"Even Him — the divine, the mine of mercy, the supreme liberator of moving and unmoving beings — people given to offences transgress; yet even them the holy Name protects here."*

**42.** Krishna's name is merciful, full of Krishna's splendour,
truly the giver of liberation to moving and unmoving beings.

**43.** The offender against the Name commits offence against it:
transgression comes and obstructs the dharma of the Name.

**44.** Even that great offender's sin is destroyed by the Name:
besides the Name, the jiva has no friend in the world.

**45.** *Sri Narada said: "What are those offences against the Name of Bhagavan, O best of brahmanas, which ruin people's work and drag it down into the material?"*

**46.** "O guru Sanatkumara, mercifully tell me:
what are all the kinds of offences against the Name?

**47.** The Name is truly the jiva's great work;
[tell me] that by which the practitioner's work is ruined,

**48.** that by which, making the Name material in practice,
one is thrown back to ordinary material fruits."

**49.** *Sri Sanatkumara said: "Blasphemy of the saints is the greatest offence to the Name: how can It bear the reviling of those through whom It has gained renown?…"*

*[p. 75]*

**50.** Hari, the supreme truth, is Vishnu, auspiciousness itself;
in His transcendent form He is Sri Vraja-vihari.

**51.** *"…Whoever here sees in his mind the qualities, names and all else of Shiva as separate from Sri Vishnu is indeed an enemy of Hari's name."*[^4]

**52.** Distinguishing the ten offences to the Name one by one
and understanding them, one overcomes the offences to the Name.

**53.** In this verse two offences are considered:
considering them, purify your service of the Name.

**54.** One who has undivided refuge in the Name
is called a sadhu: he delivers from samsara.

**55.** Giving up material dharma, knowledge and endeavour,
such a person utters the Name in the mood of pure devotion.

**56.** Through him alone the Name is spread:
Krishna's name will never bear blasphemy against him.

**57.** Whoever blasphemes such a sadhu or thinks him insignificant
certainly commits a great offence against the Name.

**58.** Carefully avoiding this offence,
by the strength of association with such a sadhu perform bhajana.

*[p. 76]*

**59.** His name, form, qualities and lila are transcendent:
in truth they are not different from His own form.

**60.** The Name and the Possessor of the name are one truth, a transcendent law:
in this material world its secret is unknown.

**61.** This pure knowledge is gained by the power of devotion:
it is far from logic — know this for certain.

**62.** By the union of two — one's own pure sadhana and the strength of the sadhu-guru —
this auspicious knowledge of the truth is gained.

**63.** As long as one is not established in this truth,
so long one will never give up the material conception;

**64.** so long, chanting the Name, one will not attain its true form:
there will be only namabhasa, contrary to bhajana.

**65.** With great effort, brother, attain perfection in the true form [of the Name]:
uttering the pure Name, you will gain understanding of the supreme abode.

**66.** If one chants with effort, unceasingly, even namabhasa, Hari
will mercifully grant perfection in the true form of the Name.

**67.** Know for certain: Krishna is the Lord of all;
Shiva and the other gods are His parts.

**68.** The names, qualities and forms of each of those gods —
know that their perfection is given by Krishna's energy.

*[p. 77]*

**69.** Whoever thus understands [the relation of] Shiva and Vishnu in their non-difference,
in him true understanding will arise — so sing all the Vedas.

**70.** Carefully give up the offence of the idea of difference;
then, by the strength of the guru's mercy, you will serve Sri Nama.

**71.** *"Disregard of the guru, blasphemy of the shruti and the scriptures, taking [the glory of the Name] as exaggeration, inventing interpretations of Hari's name; and one who has the inclination to sin relying on the strength of the Name — him no yamas (restraints) can purify."*

**72.** He who in mercy revealed Hari to me
and acquainted me with Hari's name —

**73.** he is my helmsman, the guru, a great soul:
to disregard him is an offence to the Name.

**74.** "He is of low birth, without learning, without mantra" —
such a judgment about the guru of the Name is foolish.

**75.** The shruti-scriptures that show the Name to be Brahman
and proclaim to the world the boundless glory of the Name —

**76.** whoever disregards them and praises karma and the like,
of him all the scriptures say: "he blasphemes the shruti".

*[p. 78]*

**77.** The Name is an eternal treasure, ever spiritual, fathomless:
to think [its glory] imaginary is a grave offence.

**78.** One in whose heart there constantly arises
the inclination to sin relying on the Name's strength — he is a hollow man.

**79.** Descriptions of fruits meant to arouse taste belong to the path of karma;
on the path of devotion the fruits of the Name are eternal at all times.

**80.** The glory of the transcendent Name is boundless:
whoever sees "exaggeration" (arthavada) in it is a fool.

**81.** These five offences avoid with care —
then in your practice you will gain the mercy of the Name.

**82.** *"To equate [the Name] with dharma, vows, renunciation, homa and all good works is also negligence; and to instruct [in the Name] one who is faithless, averse, unwilling to hear — is an offence against the auspicious Name."*

**83.** The dharma of varnas and ashramas described in the dharma-shastras,
the new-moon and full-moon rites and other vows full of darkness,

**84.** the kinds of renunciation — with staff, with shaven head, sannyasa and the rest,
the business of daily and occasional homa and the like,

*[p. 79]*

**85.** eightfold and sixfold yoga and other good works —
all are material principles: this is the true essence.

**86.** As means they lead to the goal;
if they do not, they are nothing but matter.

**87.** But the Name is a transcendent, spiritual matter:
in sadhana it is the means, in attainment the essence of the goal.

**88.** Therefore the truth of the Name is pure and spiritual:
it is never equal to karma, a material means.

**89.** Where the Name is thought equal to karma and jnana,
there a grave offence to the Name is committed.

**90.** One who through lack of fortune has not gained faith in the Name —
by instructing him in the Name you will commit an offence.

**91.** These two offences, brother, by the mercy of a true guru
are given up with great effort, and one gains the treasure of the Name.

**92.** *"Whoever, even having heard the glory of the Name, is without love, low, absorbed in 'I' and 'mine' — he too offends the Name."*

**93.** Having heard from the scriptures all the glory of the Name,
whoever still has in no way gained attachment to it,

*[p. 80]*

**94.** who, taking the body as "I" and "mine",
remains immersed in gain, honour and fame,

**95.** who, engaged in sin, cannot give up sin
and is unable to make an effort in the Name,

**96.** whose mind does not incline to the company of sadhus, but in the affairs of non-sadhus
finds joy, having abandoned discrimination and detachment —

**97.** with him offence to the Name occurs:
he gains no taste for the Name and does not enter Krishna's household.

**98.** These ten offences are the nama-aparadhas:
they obstruct the dharma of the Name and destroy great good.

**99.** *"Even one who has committed all offences is saved by refuge in Hari; and one, a disgrace among bipeds, who offends even Hari, if he at some time takes refuge in the Name, is saved by the Name. But one who offends the Name — the friend of all — falls down."*

**100.** All the sins, sufferings and offences of the jiva
are at once destroyed by refuge in Sri Hari.

**101.** Whoever, leaving the household of Kali, [makes] the household of Krishna
guilelessly — he has no offences.

**102.** All the sins committed before in many births
are overcome by initiation into the service of Hari alone.

*[p. 81]*

**103.** When one guilelessly surrenders oneself,
Krishna destroys one's former sins.

**104.** He need perform no atonements:
by initiation alone sins are destroyed, say all the scriptures.

**105.** Whoever takes refuge in Hari without guile —
all his offences are then destroyed.

**106.** He no longer has any taste for sins and sufferings:
again sin goes away, and he conquers maya.

**107.** Then at times an offence in service (seva-aparadha) occurs:
through that offence there is obstruction in the acts of devotion.

**108.** In the company of sadhus he takes refuge in Krishna's name:
by refuge in the Name the seva-aparadha is destroyed.

**109.** When the Name is merciful, the jiva gains purity,
comes to Krishna and takes refuge in pure service.

**110.** But if he commits an offence to the Name,
then he will surely fall again.

**111.** The Name is the friend of all jivas; an offence to It
is in no way destroyed — it only meets obstruction.

**112.** Giving up offences to the Name with great effort,
the jiva gains every perfection and attains Hari.

*[p. 82]*

**113.** *"Thus, Narada, by Shankara's mercy the highest [teaching] among the sages was spoken [to me]: the Name of Bhagavan brings happiness; [offences to it] must always be carefully avoided. Whoever, even knowing, does not at once give up the ten offences to the Name suffers like children who, angry with their mother, refuse food."*

**114.** Formerly, in the world of Shiva, before Shankara,
I asked, O sage, about the offences to the Name.

**115.** Among many sages Shambhu in mercy
instructed me on Kailasa.

**116.** The Name of Bhagavan brings happiness to all jivas;
an offence to It brings every misfortune.

**117.** Whoever has in his mind the desire to gain good
should always carefully avoid offences to the Name.

**118.** Before a sadhu-guru, in great humility,
let him learn the truth of the ten offences.

**119.** Carefully learning the offences, let him give them up —
and soon prema for Sri Harinama will awaken.

**120.** Whoever, having received the Name, does not avoid offences
is suddenly seized by the ten offences.

**121.** Whoever, understanding the offences, is indifferent to avoiding them
suffers unceasingly — a fool.

*[p. 83]*

**122.** Angry with his mother, a child will not eat:
for lack of proper food he always suffers.

**123.** So the fool, not avoiding offences,
chants the Name, rejecting his own good.

**124.** *"Freed from offences, always practise japa of the Name, O divine sage: by the Name alone all will be accomplished for you, not otherwise."*

**125.** Sanatkumara says: "O best of divine sages,
always practise japa of the Name without offence.

**126.** Apart from the Name no other path is needed:
in the Name is all perfection, O ascetic."

**127.** *Sri Narada said: "O dear Sanatkumara, how can offences not overpower those who are rash, devoid of discrimination and detachment, devoted to the body, to dear ones, to wealth and to self?"*

**128.** "O Sanatkumara! You are a perfected servant of Hari:
you have easily revealed the secret of the Name.

**129.** We sadhakas have great fear:
how are we to strive to give up offences?

**130.** The worldly is our friend: relying on it,
we do all our deeds, bound in the noose of maya.

*[p. 84]*

**131.** Devoid of discrimination and detachment, [loving] the body and dear ones,
we are always devoted to wealth and self.

**132.** How to make the ten offences
not arise in the mind of a sadhaka — reveal this."

**133.** *Sri Sanatkumara said: "If through negligence an offence to the Name should at any time occur, let one always chant the Name, taking refuge in It alone. The Names themselves take away the sin of those who have offended the Name; chanted unceasingly, they alone bring the goal."*

**134.** At the very moment one takes refuge in the Name,
the offences to the Name are at once destroyed.

**135.** And yet, if through negligence an offence arises,
that too obstructs devotion.

**136.** When through negligence an offence occurs,
then perform nama-sankirtana unceasingly.

**137.** Make your refuge in the Name very firm:
unceasingly, by the strength of the Name, the offences will go.

**138.** By the Name alone the offences to the Name will be destroyed:
no one else has the power to destroy the offence.

**139.** I shall tell you the root truth in this matter:
understand, Narada, what the Vedas sing.

*[p. 85]*

**140.** *"If even one Name has come upon the lips, onto the path of memory, or entered the ear — with pure or impure syllables — and is not screened, it truly delivers. But if it is placed amid [attachment to] body, wealth, people, greed and heretics, it does not quickly bear fruit here, O brahmana."*[^5]

**141.** One on whose lips one name of Krishna is uttered,
on whose path of memory is one Name — the abode of virtues,

**142.** into whose ears it enters —
if it is unscreened, it delivers at once.

**143.** The word "screened" (*vyavahita*) has two meanings:
the Name is screened by the interval of other syllables;

**144.** and by the covering of avidya — by material perception;
[knowing] the Name and its Possessor as one, one destroys avidya.

**145.** When there is no screen, the pure Name rises:
from purity or impurity of syllables there is neither flaw nor fear.

**146.** Into the transcendent Name Krishna has put all power:
for the Name there is neither proper nor improper time, neither purity nor impurity.

**147.** At all times, in every state, chant the pure Name:
all good will rise and all evil will depart.

**148.** Such an unprecedented Name, when joined with [bad] company,
in no way bears good fruit quickly.

*[p. 86]*

**149.** From attachment to body, wealth, people, from greed and the company of heretics,
a screen arises, and the jiva falls into great delusion.

**150.** Therefore first of all give up [bad] company
and, taking undivided refuge, serve the Name alone.

**151.** By the strength of the Name's mercy you will become free of negligence:
the offences will go far away, and there will be good.

**152.** Freed from offences, one takes Krishna's name —
and prema comes and abides together with the Name.

**153.** The mark of the offender's "name" is surely deceit:
carefully giving up such company, take refuge in the Name.

**154.** *"This supreme secret, Narada, I heard formerly from Shankara: it removes all evil and prevents offences. Even men given to offences who know the name of Vishnu attain liberation merely by reciting it, O Narada."*

**155.** Sanatkumara says: "O best of divine sages,
formerly Sri Shankara, merciful to me,

**156.** proclaimed the secret of Sri Nama, which destroys all evil
and averts offences.

**157.** A person given to offences, knowing the name of Vishnu,
merely by reciting this attains liberation — accept it."

**158.** "O Svarupa! Ramaraya! This 'Nama-rahasya-patala'
you must carefully spread.

*[p. 87]*

**159.** In Kali the jivas have no other remedy:
by the secret of the Name they will cross samsara.

**160.** The truth I spoke before in the 'Shikshashtaka'
I have now shown again in the words of Vyasa.

**161.** Carefully spread the 'Rahasya-patala' among all:
discussing it constantly, they will take the Name.

**162.** Haridasa was the crown of the earth:
he revealed all this secret of the Name.

**163.** He preached and himself practised this dharma of the Name:
Haridasa is the acharya of the Name — know the essence.

**164.** Whoever, following Haridasa, serves Sri Nama
has his desires eternally fulfilled."

[^1]: *Haridasa-samaj* — the samadhi (tomb) of Haridasa Thakura on the shore of the ocean in Puri (cf. CC Antya 11). The conversation is thus placed after Haridasa's departure. The scene itself is known only from the *Prema-vivarta*.
[^2]: The Sanskrit verses of this chapter (numbered 1–18 in the edition) are the "Nama-rahasya-patala", according to the edition from the *Padma Purana*, Svarga-khanda, ch. 48. The verses on the ten offences to the Name (here 49, 51, 71, 82, 92) are known from the *Padma Purana* and are quoted in the *Hari-bhakti-vilasa* (11) and by Jiva Gosvami (*Bhakti-sandarbha*). They are translated afresh here; the Bengali stanzas are a paraphrase-commentary placed in Mahaprabhu's mouth.
[^3]: *Gita* 18.66, 9.30–31.
[^4]: That is, to regard Shiva and the other gods as independent lords, equal to or separate from Vishnu. The Bengali commentary (verses 67–70) explains: the gods are Krishna's parts; their qualities are given by His energy. This agrees with the teaching of Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati: Shiva is not an independent God, nor yet an ordinary jiva.
[^5]: The verse is quoted by Jiva Gosvami in the *Bhakti-sandarbha*. *Vyavahita* — "screened": the Name broken up by extraneous syllables, or the Name perceived as material sound.
