# Chapter 13. Longing for Gaura

*A song: having set out for Vrindavana, the author cannot bear separation from Gaura; memories of Puri — Tota-Gopinatha, Gaura and Gadadhara; he can neither go on nor return (pp. 36–37)*

*[p. 36]*

**1.** Gauranga, leaving Your feet
I set out for Sri Vrindavana:
"I shall see Your former lila" —
so it came into my mind.[^1]

**2.** Why did this desire arise in me?
Now I am dying of weeping.
Not seeing You, my life is leaving me;
I do not know what to do now.

**3.** Those reddish feet, the treasure of my life,
I left on the sand by the ocean.
What did I come to see? I have ruined myself:
the bird of my life is about to fly away.

**4.** The farther I walk, the less my mind will go —
and still I go, out of stubbornness.
The vivarta of love makes me dance;
not understanding it, I die.

**5.** Gauranga's play I could not understand —
and I have fallen into an ocean of sorrow.
What I want I do not get;
how restless my mind is!

**6.** For Gauranga I go to give up my life,
yet death does not come:
resolved to die, I throw myself into the ocean —
and only flounder in the waves.

**7.** Greedy to see that moonlike face,
I hurry up onto the ocean shore;
again I do not see Him — my life flies away;
again I take the road to the Tota.

*[p. 37]*

**8.** In Gopinatha's courtyard, seeing Gora's face,
I fall unconscious;
Pandita Gosai takes me and keeps me —
seeing [Him], I come to my senses.[^2]

**9.** Gaura and Gadadhara, sitting together,
speak of me;
at once, weeping, I roll on the ground,
not minding where I am.

**10.** Even a moment's separation I cannot bear:
my Gaura dances in my heart.
He will not let me die, and to live means quarrels:
how then shall my life survive?

**11.** In such a state — to leave Gaura's feet
and come to Vrindavana:
this idea came to me, I know not why,
ruinous for this world and the next.

**12.** I took leave to go; if I do not obey the command
it will be an offence;
and not seeing Gorachand's face, I die:
on every side I am blocked.

**13.** Whoever has love for Gora is in a plight:
his life is torn to and fro.
Gadadhara's people
all whisper: "See what he has come to."

[^1]: According to CC Antya 13, Jagadananda went to Vrindavana with the Lord's permission (obtained through Svarupa Damodara), stayed there for some time with Sanatana and returned to Puri. The song conveys his feelings on the way.
[^2]: Pandita Gosai — Gadadhara Pandita, who served Tota-Gopinatha.
