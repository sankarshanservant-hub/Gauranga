# Chapter 18. Sri Ekadashi

*Ekadashi in the Jagannatha-vallabha garden: the Lord and the devotees sing the Name all night; the mahaprasada brought to Him He honours with obeisance and eats only the next morning; His ruling: on Ekadashi — fasting and vigil, on other days — prasada; the service of the Name and the Ekadashi vow are one (pp. 65–68)*

*[p. 65]*

**1.** One day Gaurahari, leaving Sri Gundicha,
stayed in the Jagannatha-vallabha;[^1]
on the day of pure Ekadashi, in the sankirtana of Krishna's name,
He passed the day and the night.

**2.** With Him were Svarupa Damodara, Ramananda, Vakreshvara
and all the residents of the holy place.
The Lord says: "With all your heart, in the sankirtana of Krishna's name,
giving up sleep and food —

**3.** some chant the name by count, some offer prostrate obeisances,
some speak of Rama and Krishna."
Falling down anywhere, all, with cries of "Govinda, Govinda!",
became utterly intoxicated with great prema.

**4.** Just then Gopinatha the parichha, together with Sarvabhauma,
came bringing prasada from Gundicha:[^2]
rice and vegetables, cakes, drinks, sweet rice, yogurt, curd —
and set it all before Mahaprabhu.

**5.** At the Lord's command all fell down flat
and bowed again and again to the mahaprasada;
and the whole three-watch night, absorbed in great prema,
they passed guilelessly in the Name.

*[p. 66]*

**6.** Taking the Lord's command on their heads, all bathed at dawn
and broke the fast by honouring the mahaprasada;
then, with joyful hearts, at the Lord's feet,
with folded palms, they petitioned Him:

**7.** "We know that Sri Hari-vasara is the crown of all vows,
and we keep vigil without food;
yet Jagannatha's rice-prasada, honoured in the Kshetra at all times,
should be eaten as soon as it is received.

**8.** In this dilemma, living in the Kshetra, we are in great fear:
we pray for a clear command.
Your command is all the Vedas; Brahma and Shiva honour it:
give it and relieve our distress."

**9.** The Lord says: "If one breaks the honour of Ekadashi —
one of the limbs of devotion —
that tithi cannot be had back the next day.

**10.** On the day of Sri Hari-vasara, drinking the rasa of Krishna's name,
the good Vaishnava is satisfied;
he takes no other rasa, speaks no other talk,
and gives up all enjoyment.

*[p. 67]*

**11.** Eating prasada is the constant duty of a pure Vaishnava:
he eats nothing that is not prasada.
But when it is pure Ekadashi, then he is without food,
and at the breaking of the fast he eats prasada.

**12.** Only in the case of a substitute (anukalpa) — know it for certain —
does the Vaishnava, abstaining from grain, take prasada.
But non-Vaishnavas, on the pretext of prasada,
indulge in enjoyment day and night:
in the company of the Papa-purusha they eat grain with relish
and do not honour the vow of Hari-vasara.[^3]

**13.** Good conduct is a limb of devotion: honour devotion,
and you will gain the mercy of the goddess Bhakti.
Give up the company of non-Vaishnavas, keep the Ekadashi vow —
then Ekadashi too becomes the vow of the Name.

**14.** Eating prasada and Sri Hari-vasara
never contradict each other — understand this in your heart.

**15.** Whoever honours one limb [of devotion] and slights another
is, know it well, a fool.

**16.** Whatever rule of place and time belongs to a given limb —
in that be wholly engaged in devotion.

**17.** The Lord of all the limbs is Vrajendra-nandana:
do whatever pleases Him.

*[p. 68]*

**18.** On the day of Ekadashi give up sleep and food;
on other days gladly take prasada and nirmalya."

**19.** Hearing this, all the Vaishnavas, with joyful cries of "Govinda!",
fell down flat;
Svarupa and the others, and Ramananda, felt great joy —
all the devotees, "Oriya" and "Gaudiya".

**20.** O brother! Gauranga is the treasure of my life:
serve Him guilelessly, and you will cross the ocean of existence,
and body and mind will grow cool.

**21.** The service of Sri Nama and the Ekadashi vow —
know them as one eternal truth and be devoted to them.[^4]

[^1]: Jagannatha-vallabha — a garden in Puri where the Lord rested with the devotees during the Ratha-yatra days, while Jagannatha stays in the Gundicha temple (cf. CC Madhya 14).
[^2]: *Parichha* — a temple superintendent in Puri. Gopinatha Acharya, Sarvabhauma's brother-in-law (CC Madhya 6), may be meant. The episode of the mahaprasada on Ekadashi is not described in the CC or CB; it is known from the *Prema-vivarta* and widely retold in the Gaudiya tradition.
[^3]: According to the Puranas, on Ekadashi all sins (the Papa-purusha) take refuge in grain. *Anukalpa* — the permitted substitute for those unable to keep a full fast: food without grain.
[^4]: Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati and the Gaudiya Math strictly observe the fast from grain on Ekadashi, including grain mahaprasada — cf. this chapter.
