# Chapter 8. Give Up Crookedness

*Gora must be served guilelessly: service for show and sinning on the strength of the Name ruin bhajana; the ease and power of the Name; Kavi Karnapura and his father Shivananda, the author's guardian (pp. 22–24)*

*[p. 22]*

**1.** Serve Gora, serve Gora, serve Gora, brother:
apart from Gora there is no other guru in this world.

**2.** If you would serve Gora, make your mind guileless:
giving up crookedness, serve Gora's feet.

**3.** Gora knows what is in your mind — how will you deceive Him?
If you are guileless, you will understand Gora's teaching.

**4.** Trying to please others, you will deceive yourself:
Gora knows the mind — how can you hide your heart?

**5.** Gora says: "Behave as I do;
follow My command if you want your own good."

**6.** "I am Gora's, I am Gora's" — words alone will not do:
it bears fruit if one adopts Gora's conduct and Gora's teaching.

**7.** Whoever serves Gora for show, wearing only tilaka,
and secretly commits misdeeds — Gora will catch the thief.

**8.** You will fall, brother, if you play crooked:
through offences to the Name your bhajana will turn to dust.

*[p. 23]*

**9.** Whoever sins relying on the Name commits an offence:[^1]
is there any greater obstacle to devotion than this?

**10.** Chanting the Name is no hardship; the Name is an easy treasure:
by the mere movement of the lips the kirtana of the Name takes place;
and if even that does not happen, there is remembrance of the Name.

**11.** When the mouth is closed and the mind distracted, there is still hearing:
all sins are destroyed, and the jiva gains the chief fruit.

**12.** This fruit ripens from worship over many births:
then Krishna's name dances unceasingly on the lips.[^2]

**13.** Karma, jnana, yoga and the rest have no such power:
through faults in following the rules they are fruitless — so the scriptures say.

**14.** Leave all that, brother; make the Name the essence:
in a very few days you will conquer samsara.

**15.** Blessed is Kavi Karnapura, who lives in my village:[^3]
he revealed something of the glory of the Name.

**16.** Whom Gaura favours is blessed in the world:
at seven years of age He made him honoured as a great poet.[^4]

**17.** Blessed is Shivananda, Kavi Karnapura's father:
in my childhood he taught me the *Bhagavatam* and the *Gita*.

**18.** Taking me to Nadia, he left me at the Lord's feet;
Shivananda is my protector in good times and bad.[^5]

*[p. 24]*

**19.** In his house, cooking offerings, I learned to cook,
and, cooking well, I served Sri Gauranga.

**20.** Jagai says: he whose days pass in the company of sadhus —
he alone takes the Name unceasingly.

[^1]: "Sinning on the strength of the Name" is one of the ten offences to the holy name (cf. ch. 19).
[^2]: Cf. Rupa Gosvami's verse "Tunde tandavini…" (CC Antya 1): Krishna's name dances on the lips.
[^3]: Kavi Karnapura (Puri Dasa, Paramananda Sena), son of Shivananda Sena of Kumarahatta (Kanchanapalli), author of the GGD and the *Chaitanya-chandrodaya*. The CC and CB do not make Jagadananda his fellow villager; this is known only from the *Prema-vivarta*.
[^4]: Cf. CC Antya 16: Shivananda's seven-year-old son composed a verse by the Lord's mercy, and the Lord gave him the name "Kavi Karnapura".
[^5]: That Shivananda Sena brought Jagadananda up and took him to the Lord in Nadia is known only from the *Prema-vivarta*.
