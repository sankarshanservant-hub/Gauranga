# Chapter 7. The Name Is for All

*Without the company of sadhus there is no Name — only the sounds of the name, namabhasa or offences; how to chant the name; instructions to renunciants: Chhota Haridasa and Haridasa Thakura; for householder and renunciant alike — not a day without the Name (pp. 19–21)*

*[p. 19]*

**1.** In the company of non-sadhus, brother, Krishna's name does not arise:
the syllables of the name are uttered, yet it is never the Name.

**2.** Sometimes there is namabhasa, and constantly offences to the Name:
know, brother, that all this obstructs devotion to Krishna.[^1]

*[p. 20]*

**3.** If you would take Krishna's name, seek the company of sadhus;
cast far away the desire for enjoyment, liberation and mystic perfections.

**4.** Give up the "ten offences",[^2] the concern for honour and dishonour;
use what you need without attachment, and take Krishna's name.[^3]

**5.** Accept everything favourable to devotion to Krishna;
reject everything unfavourable to it.[^4]

**6.** Give up the efforts of jnana and yoga and attachment to karma;
renounce "monkey renunciation", in which the body is indulged.[^5]

**7.** Know that Krishna always guards and protects me;
by the humility of self-surrender dispel the entanglement.

**8.** Knowing that for the jivas it is very hard to find a sadhu,
Krishna came to Nadia in the form of a saintly devotee.

**9.** O wise one, take shelter of Gora's feet:
apart from Gora, who else is sadhu and guru?

**10.** Renunciant brother, do not listen to worldly talk,
and when you meet others, do not speak of worldly matters;

**11.** even in dreams, brother, do not converse with women:
you have left your wife at home, brother, and come to the forest.[^6]

*[p. 21]*

**12.** If you wish to keep Gauranga's love,
remember the story of Chhota Haridasa.[^7]

**13.** Do not eat well and do not dress well;
in your heart always serve Radha and Krishna.

**14.** Like the elder Haridasa, repeat Krishna's name with your lips;
in the eight periods of the day serve Radha and Krishna in the kunja groves.[^8]

**15.** To householder and renunciant alike Gora Raya says:
"See, brother, that not a day passes without the Name."

**16.** There is no need, brother, to practise many kinds of sadhana:
taking shelter of Krishna's name, purify your life.

**17.** In mercy to the bound jivas Krishna became the Name;
in compassion for the jivas of Kali Krishna became radiant Gaura.[^9]

**18.** With a guileless, undivided heart serve Gaura's people —
then, brother, you will attain Sri Krishna's feet.

**19.** Keep company with Gaura's people, saying "Gauranga",
and, dancing and dancing, chant the name "Hare Krishna".

**20.** Soon, brother, you will gain the treasure of love for the Name,
to distribute which the Lord came to Nadia.

**21.** The Lord's quarrelsome Jaga, weeping and weeping, says:
"Serve the Name, sing the Name, all you devotees!"

[^1]: *Namabhasa* — the "shadow of the name", not yet the pure Name; *nama-aparadha* — offences to the Name. See ch. 19 for details.
[^2]: The ten offences to the holy name (*Padma Purana*); in the *Prema-vivarta* they are listed in ch. 19, in the words of Sanatkumara.
[^3]: Cf. CC Madhya 16.238: use the objects of the senses as is fitting, without attachment.
[^4]: The beginning of the six kinds of self-surrender (sharanagati), cf. CC Madhya 22.97 (by the numbering of our CC text).
[^5]: "Monkey renunciation" (*markata-vairagya*) — show renunciation; cf. CC Madhya 16.238, Antya 2.120.
[^6]: Verses 10–11 and 13 are close to the Lord's instruction to Raghunatha Dasa in CC Antya 6.236–237.
[^7]: Chhota Haridasa, according to CC Antya 2, was rejected by the Lord for begging rice from an elderly woman, Madhavi.
[^8]: Haridasa Thakura ("the elder Haridasa"), who chanted 300,000 names a day. *Ashta-kala* — the eight periods of the day of Radha and Krishna's lila, in which remembrance is practised.
[^9]: *Gaura-dhama* — literally "golden radiance"; "abode of Gaura" is also possible.
