# Chapter 20. The Glory of the Name

*In Kashi Mishra's house Krishnadasa asks to hear the glory of Krishna's name; Mahaprabhu sets it out, supporting it with verses of scripture: the Name destroys all sins, diseases and fears, is above all vows, atonements, holy places and Vedas, knows no rules of place and time, grants liberation and prema; the fruits come according to faith; disbelief and "arthavada" are an offence (pp. 88–110)*

*[p. 88]*

**1.** One day Krishnadasa, in Kashi Mishra's house,
made a request to the Lord:[^1]

**2.** "Permit me to hear the glory of Krishna's name —
the glory whose limit neither Brahma nor Shiva knows."

**3.** The Lord says: "The glory of Krishna's name is boundless:
Krishna Himself does not know it — what will a wretched jiva know?

**4.** What I have heard in the scriptures I shall tell you:
listen with faith — you will cross the ocean of existence.

**5.** The Name calms all sins, destroys all diseases,
destroys all sorrows, lessens the obstacles of Kali;

**6.** it delivers the dwellers in hell, cuts off prarabdha-karma,
destroys all offences — all this is in the Name.

**7.** The fulfilment of all good works is the play of the Name;
the manifestation of the Name is above all the Vedas.

**8.** The Name is above all holy places — so say all the scriptures;
the excellence of all good works dawns in the Name.

**9.** The Name grants all goals and is full of all powers;
to gladden the world is the Name's nature.

**10.** Taking the Name, anyone becomes honoured by the world;
the Name is the refuge of the helpless, the purifier of the fallen.

*[p. 89]*

**11.** It is served everywhere and always, it grants every kind of liberation;
the Name brings one to Vaikuntha and gives the love of Hari.

**12.** The Name itself is the supreme goal of life, chief among [the limbs of] devotion:
in shruti, smriti and shastra there is much proof of it."

**13.** To destroy all sins is one quality of the Name:
first hear the proof of this and its essence.

**14.** Look at the sinner Ajamila: helplessly
he uttered the name of Hari, saying "Narayana" —

**15.** and from all the sins committed in millions of births
he was at once set free.

**16.** *"He has already atoned even for the sins of ten million births, for, though helplessly, he uttered the name of Hari, which brings well-being" (*Bhagavatam *6.2.7).*[^2]

**17.** The killer of a woman, a king, a cow or a brahmana, the drunkard,
the defiler of the guru's bed, the betrayer of a friend, the thief —

**18.** their sins and other heaps of sins
are all cleansed by uttering the name of Hari.

**19.** When sin is thoroughly atoned, the mind turns to Krishna:
thus through the Name the jiva gains a good destination.

**20.** *"The thief, the drunkard, the betrayer of a friend, the killer of a brahmana, the defiler of the guru's bed, the killer of a woman, king, father or cow, and other sinners — for all of them this is the best atonement: uttering the name of Vishnu, for by it the mind turns to Him" (*Bhagavatam *6.2.9–10).*

*[p. 90]*

**21.** The chandrayana vow and others, performed according to scripture,
do not free the sinner from sin so well

**22.** as when Krishna's name is uttered even once:
then the jiva in the world is freed from all sins.

**23.** *"The sinner is not so purified by atonements — vows and the like prescribed by the knowers of Brahman — as by uttering the syllables of Hari's name, which call to mind the qualities of Him whose fame is holy" (*Bhagavatam *6.2.11).*

**24.** By way of indication, in jest, as filler in a song, or with neglect —
if one says "Krishna", "Hari" even as namabhasa,

**25.** his countless sins go far away,
and he is led to Sri Vaikuntha, the Yamadutas defeated.

**26.** *"It is known: uttering the name of Vaikuntha — by indication, in jest, as filler in a song, or with neglect — destroys countless sins" (*Bhagavatam *6.2.14).*

**27.** Having fallen, slipped, broken [a bone], been bitten, burned or wounded,
one who helplessly cries out "I am done for!" —

**28.** *"A man who, having fallen, slipped, broken a bone, been bitten, burned or wounded, helplessly says 'Hari' does not deserve [the torments of hell]" (*Bhagavatam *6.2.15).*

**29.** Whoever calls with his lips the name "Krishna", "Hari", "Narayana" —
torments will never cling to him.

*[p. 91]*

**30.** In ignorance or knowingly — in the sankirtana of Krishna's name
all sins turn to ashes, like wood cast into fire.

**31.** *"Whether in ignorance or knowingly, the name of Him whose fame is holy, when chanted, burns a man's sin as fire burns fuel" (*Bhagavatam *6.2.18).*

**32.** Present sins and those accumulated in former births,
and those that will be in the future — all of them

**33.** are easily destroyed by the sankirtana of Krishna's name:
besides the Name the jiva has no friend in life.

**34.** *"Present sin, past and future — all this the fire of Govinda's kirtana quickly burns up" (*Laghu-bhagavata*).*

**35.** People on earth who behave sinfully toward the righteous
gain liberation by the kirtana of the Name.

**36.** *"One who on earth is always hostile to the righteous becomes pure and blessed by chanting the name of Hari" (*Laghu-bhagavata*).*

**37.** In the scriptures there are tens of millions of atonements,
but none equals the kirtana of Krishna.

**38.** *"The tens of millions of purifying means on earth do not equal [even a fraction of] the chanting of Krishna's name" (*Kurma Purana*).*

*[p. 92]*

**39.** As many sins as the name of Hari destroys,
so many sins a sinner can never commit.

**40.** *"As much power as Hari's name has to remove sin, so much sin a sinful man is unable to commit" (*Kurma Purana*).*

**41.** There is no sin — of mind, speech or body —
that is not destroyed in Kali by the name of Govinda.

**42.** *"There is no sin in the world — born of deed, of speech or of mind — which the kirtana of Govinda does not destroy in Kali" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**43.** By the Name all diseases are destroyed — so sing all the scriptures;
O Sthaneshvari, devotee, I tell you:[^3]

**44.** truly, truly I say — accept it with faith:
uttering the names "Achyuta", "Ananta", "Govinda",

**45.** weeping, weeping, call on Sri Madhusudana:
Sri Nama-kirtana destroys all diseases.

**46.** *"By the medicine of uttering the names 'Achyuta', 'Ananta', 'Govinda' all diseases perish: truly, truly I say it" (*Brihan-naradiya Purana*).*

**47.** Even a great sinner, singing of Hari day and night,
becomes pure and is counted among those who purify the row of diners.[^4]

**48.** *"Even one guilty of great sins, glorifying the Lord Hari, becomes pure of heart and a purifier of the row" (*Brahmanda Purana*).*

*[p. 93]*

**49.** Whether fear of grave disease or fear of royal punishment —
by the sankirtana of Narayana one becomes free of fear.

**50.** *"Stricken by grave disease, oppressed by royal punishment, a man who glorifies 'Narayana' becomes free of fear" (*Vahni Purana*).*

**51.** All diseases, all troubles, calamities,
misfortunes and the rest are destroyed by uttering "Hari".

**52.** *"The chanting of Hari's name calms all diseases, destroys all calamities and pacifies all misfortunes" (*Brihad-vishnu Purana*).*

**53.** As a strong wind drives away the clouds
and at sunrise darkness surely perishes,

**54.** so the chanted Name by its own power
removes the jiva's distress — these are Vyasa's words.

**55.** *"When the infinite Bhagavan is glorified or His power is heard of, He enters the heart and entirely removes a man's distress, as the sun removes darkness or a strong wind the clouds" (*Bhagavatam *12.12.47).*

**56.** The afflicted, the dejected, the faint-hearted, the frightened,
those in the agony of terrible diseases who see no good —

**57.** if they perform sankirtana, saying "Narayana", "Hari",
they will surely be freed from sorrow and be happy.

*[p. 94]*

**58.** *"The afflicted, the dejected, the weakened, the frightened, those in terrible diseases — glorifying the single word 'Narayana', they are freed from sorrow and become happy" (*Vishnu-dharmottara*).*

**59.** By the kirtana of Vishnu of boundless might
yakshas, rakshasas, vetalas, bhutas and pretas,

**60.** vinayakas, dakinis and all harmful beings
take to flight, and all sorrow ends.

**61.** Hari-nama-sankirtana destroys all anarthas,
destroys calamities — hunger, thirst, falls.

**62.** Where there is doubt about this,
there the might of the Name never shows itself.

**63.** With faith — the Name's mercy; without faith — none:
this is a secret, devotee; know it for certain.

**64.** *"By the kirtana of Vishnu, God of gods, of boundless splendour, yakshas, rakshasas, vetalas, bhutas, pretas, vinayakas, dakinis and other harmful beings flee; the chanting of His name is declared to remove all anarthas. Chanting the Name in hunger, thirst, on falling and the like, one is soon parted from all calamities — there is no doubt" (*Vishnu-dharmottara*).*

**65.** Seeing the sharp fang of the evil serpent of the Kali age,
do not be so afraid; listen with faith:

*[p. 95]*

**66.** the forest fire of Krishna's name, blazing up,
will burn that serpent's fang.

**67.** *"Do not fear the sharp-fanged evil serpent of the Kali age: scorched by the forest fire of Govinda's name it will turn to ashes" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**68.** In this terrible age of Kali, the devotees who have taken refuge in Hari's name
and given up other refuges have done all that must be done.

**69.** "Hare Keshava Govinda Vasudeva Jaganmaya" —
from the sankirtana of this Name comes great happiness.

**70.** Whoever always sings the Name with faith —
Kali is no obstacle to him; his heart is always pure.

**71.** *"Those men in the terrible age of Kali who are devoted to Hari's name — they alone have done their duty, and Kali does not trouble them. Those who daily say 'Hare Keshava Govinda Vasudeva Jaganmaya' — Kali does not trouble them" (*Vishnu-dharmottara*).*

**72.** The dwellers in hell, chanting and saying "Hari", "Krishna",
became devotees of Hari and went to the divine abode.

**73.** *"As the dwellers in hell chanted the name of Hari, so, gaining devotion to Hari, they went to heaven" (*Narasimha Purana*).*

**74.** Prarabdha is cut off only by the name of Hari:
from jnana and karma such a fruit never comes.

**75.** Without the kirtana of Hari the bonds of karma are never
cut, and [true] desire for liberation is not gained —

*[p. 96]*

**76.** for that liberation, gaining which one is no longer attached to karma,
free from the faults of rajas and tamas, without contact with maya.

**77.** *"For those who seek liberation there is no means of cutting the root of karma higher than the chanting of Him whose feet are holy: after it the mind is no more attached to karma; otherwise [by atonements] it remains defiled by rajas and tamas" (*Bhagavatam *6.2.46).*

**78.** One who is dying, suffering, falling or stumbling,
if somehow he helplessly says "Krishna" —

**79.** freed from the bolt of karma, he reaches the supreme goal,
which in the Kali age people of another mind do not reach.

**80.** *"A man who, dying, suffering, falling or stumbling, helplessly utters His name is freed from the bolt of karma and attains the highest goal; yet people in Kali will not worship Him" (*Bhagavatam *12.3.44).*

**81.** If one takes the Name with faith, millions of offences
Krishna forgives, if there is no crookedness.

**82.** Whoever does not believe this
is most unfortunate: there is no deliverance for him.

**83.** *"Whoever in this world chants My names with faith — I forgive him millions of offences; there is no doubt" (*Vishnu-yamala*).*

**84.** Flaws of mantra, rite, place, time and articles
vanish in the sankirtana of the Name, and supreme satisfaction is gained.

*[p. 97]*

**85.** The Name is the chief of good works: taking refuge in It,
you will surely gain perfection in other good works too.

**86.** *"Flaws of mantra, rite, place, time and articles — the chanting of Your name makes all flawless" (*Bhagavatam *8.23.16).*

**87.** The Name is above all the Vedas: whoever doubts this
will never have good fortune.

**88.** The pranava is Krishna's name; from it the Veda was born
from Brahma's mouth — understand the distinction of truth.

**89.** He has studied the Rig, Yajur, Sama and Atharva
from whose lips "Hari, Hari" is heard unceasingly.

**90.** *"He has studied the Rig-veda, Yajur-veda, Sama-veda and Atharva-veda who has uttered the two syllables 'Ha-ri'" (*Vishnu-dharmottara*).*

**91.** Why read the Rig, Yajur, Sama and Atharva?
Chant the name "Govinda, Govinda".

**92.** *"Do not read the Rik, my dear, nor the Yajus, nor the Saman: always sing Hari's name 'Govinda'" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**93.** Each name of Vishnu is above all the Vedas;
know that the name "Rama" is above a thousand names.

**94.** *"Even a single name of Vishnu is above all the Vedas; the name 'Rama' is held equal to a thousand such names" (*Padma Purana*).*

**95.** The fruit of reciting the thousand names three times
is gained by one name of Krishna.

*[p. 98]*

**96.** "Krishna Krishna Krishna Krishna Krishna Krishna Krishna he!" —
chant this name all the time, devotees.[^5]

**97.** "Hare Krishna Hare Krishna Krishna Krishna Hare Hare
Hare Rama Hare Rama Rama Rama Hare Hare" —

**98.** in these sixteen names everything is preserved;
the perfection of all fruits is gained by these sixteen names.[^6]

**99.** *"The fruit of reciting the holy thousand names three times is given by a single recitation of one name of Krishna" (*Brahmanda Purana*).*

**100.** What fruit comes from the toils of pilgrimage?
Singing "Hare Krishna" daily, you will gain all fruits.

**101.** Why wander to Kurukshetra, Kashi, Pushkara
for one on the tip of whose tongue is Hari's name every moment?

**102.** *"What is Kurukshetra to him, what Kashi or Pushkara, on the tip of whose tongue are the two syllables 'Ha-ri'?" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**103.** What a man [gains] from thousands of millions of holy places,
that fruit the kirtana of Hari's name gives.

**104.** *"Thousands of millions of holy places, hundreds of millions of holy places — all this [is given] by the kirtana of Vishnu's name" (*Vamana Purana*).*

**105.** Sitting at Kurukshetra, the sage Vishvamitra says:
"I have heard the names of many holy places on earth —

**106.** none equals even a ten-millionth part
of the kirtana of Hari's name": this word is most precious.

*[p. 99]*

**107.** *"Many famous holy places are heard of; they do not equal even a ten-millionth part of the kirtana of Hari's name" (*Vishvamitra-samhita*).*

**108.** What need is there of the vastness of Vedas, Agamas and shastras?
Why do people wander to many holy places?

**109.** Whoever desires his own liberation, let that good man
chant: "Govinda, Govinda".

**110.** *"What use, my dear, is the vastness of the words of the Vedas and Agamas, what use the many holy places? If you desire the cause of your own liberation, say clearly 'Govinda, Govinda'."*

**111.** Know for certain: the Name is above all good works;
whoever believes this has fulfilled all dharma.

**112.** The gift of millions of cows at every eclipse,
kalpa-vasa at Prayaga in the month of Magha according to rule,

**113.** ten thousand sacrifices, the gift of a golden Mount Meru —
do not equal a hundredth part of Hari's name.

**114.** *"The gift of ten million cows at an eclipse, kalpa-vasa by the waters of the Ganga at Prayaga, ten thousand sacrifices, the gift of a mountain of gold — do not equal a hundredth part of the kirtana of Govinda" (*Laghu-bhagavata*).*

**115.** Though very many ishta and purta works be done,
the scriptures say: all of them are causes of worldly existence.

**116.** Hari's name easily grants the boon of release from existence:
the fruits of karma are insignificant before the Name.

*[p. 100]*

**117.** *"Ishta and purta works, though performed in great number, are the cause of worldly existence; but Hari's name grants liberation" (*Baudhayana-samhita*).*

**118.** What hope do you place in sankhya, ashtanga and other yoga?
If you want liberation, always perform the kirtana of Govinda.

**119.** Even liberation is a small fruit beside the Name:
even by the Name chanted with neglect the jiva is liberated.

**120.** *"What will sankhya do, what the yogas, O leader of men? If you desire liberation, O king of kings, perform the kirtana of Govinda" (*Garuda Purana*).*

**121.** Though he be an eater of dogs, he is called the best of the twice-born
if Krishna's name dances on the tip of his tongue.

**122.** He has performed all austerities, bathed in all holy places,
studied all the Vedas — he is an Arya and wise;

**123.** by the power of these practices, fortunate,
he always sings Hari's name with his tongue.

**124.** *"Oh, how great is even a dog-eater on the tip of whose tongue is Your name! Those who utter Your name have performed austerities, offered sacrifices, bathed; they are Aryas and have studied the Vedas" (*Bhagavatam *3.33.7).*

**125.** Hari's name is the great mantra that grants all goals:
all the Vedas, Agamas and Tantras proclaim it aloud.

**126.** By the power of Hari's name the six [enemies] are subdued,
foes are restrained and spiritual practice is accomplished.

*[p. 101]*

**127.** *"The chanting of Vishnu's name is the supreme means of subduing the six [enemies] and restraining foes; truly it is the root of the spiritual" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**128.** The Aryas who know qualities and take the essence honour Kali:
in Kali all goals are gained by nama-sankirtana.

**129.** *"The Aryas who know true worth and take the essence honour the Kali age, in which every goal is attained by sankirtana alone" (*Bhagavatam *11.5.36).*

**130.** The Name is all-powerful, equal to Krishna:
all Krishna's powers are present in the Name.

**131.** Whatever powers there were in gifts, vows, austerities and holy places,
distributed among the gods in the karma-kanda,

**132.** in the rajasuya, the ashvamedha, in spiritual knowledge —
drawing them all in, Krishna placed them in His own name.

**133.** *"The powers that resided in gifts, vows, austerities, holy places and sacred fields, the purifying powers of gods and saints, the powers of the rajasuya and ashvamedha and the knowledge of the spiritual — Hari drew them all in and placed them in His names" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**134.** All the meanings and powers of Sri Krishna, God of gods,
are placed in all the names; for whichever one has attraction —

**135.** apply that name to every purpose:
from all meanings and powers everything will be gained.

**136.** *"Of the God of gods, the Bearer of the discus, endowed with all meanings and powers — whatever name one likes, apply it to all purposes" (*Brahmanda Purana*).*

*[p. 102]*

**137.** By the sankirtana of Hrishikesha the world rejoices;
with love, with a joyful heart, it is ever content;

**138.** daityas and rakshasas flee in fear,
and the hosts of siddhas always bow at His feet.

**139.** As Krishna is, so is the Name: the might of the Name
is fitting; there is no lack in it.

**140.** *"Rightly, O Hrishikesha, the world rejoices and is attached in glorifying You; the rakshasas flee in fear in all directions, and all the hosts of siddhas bow down" (*Gita *11.36).*

**141.** In Sri Nama-kirtana there is no consideration of varna and the like:
the rules of diksha and purashcharya are no hindrance to it.

**142.** One from whose lips "Narayana, Jagannatha, Vasudeva, Janardana" is always heard —
he is worthy of honour, he is a guru.

**143.** Lying down, sleeping, walking and sitting —
whoever repeats Krishna's name is honourable in every way.

**144.** *"Those who daily say 'Narayana, Jagannatha, Vasudeva, Janardana' are honoured everywhere. Sleeping, eating, walking, standing, rising and speaking — to those who utter Hari's name, ever obeisance, obeisance" (*Brihan-naradiya Purana*).*

**145.** Be it a woman, a shudra, a pulkasa, a yavana or anyone —
if he sings Krishna's name, he too is an honoured guru.

*[p. 103]*

**146.** *"Women, shudras, pulkasas and others born of sinful wombs — if they glorify Hari with devotion, to them obeisance, obeisance" (*Narayana-vyuha-stava*).*

**147.** One with no other refuge, a pleasure-seeker, a tormentor of others,
devoid of knowledge, detachment and brahmacharya, a sinner,

**148.** devoid of all dharma — if he repeats the Name,
the destination he reaches is not reached by all the righteous.

**149.** *"Mortals with no other refuge, pleasure-seekers, tormentors of others, devoid of knowledge and detachment, of brahmacharya and the like, having abandoned all dharma, whose one thing is the name of Vishnu alone, easily reach a destination that not even all the righteous reach" (*Padma Purana*).*

**150.** For taking Hari's name there is no rule of place and time;
I find no prohibition for one unclean after eating or in impurity.

**151.** *"There is no rule of place for it, nor rule of time; there is no prohibition for the unclean and the like in the name of Sri Hari, O hunter" (*Vishnu-dharma*).*

**152.** Always and everywhere chant Krishna's name,
do not regard impurity: the Name is independent and purifying.

**153.** *"Let one always and everywhere chant the names of the Bearer of the discus: there is no impurity in His chanting, for He is the purifier" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**154.** For sacrifice, gift, bathing and japa there are rules of time;
in the kirtana of Krishna to think of proper and improper time is a great error.

**155.** Rules of place, time and the rest never apply to the Name:
all of you, always perform the kirtana of Krishna.

*[p. 104]*

**156.** *"There is no rule of place, O king, nor rule of time — there is no doubt of it — for the chanting of Vishnu's name. There is a time for gifts, a time for bathing, a time for japa; but for the sankirtana of Vishnu there is no [fixed] time anywhere on earth" (*Vaishnava-chintamani*).*

**157.** One whose heart is weary of samsara and who desires fearlessness —
for such a yogi the Name is the only means.

**158.** *"For those weary [of the world], who desire fearlessness, for the yogis, O king, it is established: the chanting of Hari's name" (*Bhagavatam *2.1.11).*

**159.** Besides Hari's name there is in the three worlds
no other easy giver of liberation: the Name is the jiva's saviour.

**160.** Whoever has once uttered with his lips the two syllables "Ha-ri"
has girded his loins for the journey to liberation.

**161.** *"He by whom the two syllables 'Ha-ri' have been uttered even once has girded his loins for the journey to liberation" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**162.** Whoever, having overcome drowsiness, says "Narayana" even once,
becoming pure of heart, sets out on the path of nirvana.

**163.** *"Whoever tirelessly utters 'Narayana' even once becomes pure of heart and attains nirvana" (*Padma Purana*).*[^7]

**164.** Whoever in this terrible samsara helplessly says "Hare, Hare"
is at once set free: fear itself fears him.

**165.** *"One fallen into terrible samsara who helplessly utters His name is at once set free — He whom fear itself fears" (*Bhagavatam *1.1.14).*

*[p. 105]*

**166.** Whoever at the hour of death helplessly utters [the names]
that reflect His avataras, qualities, deeds and lilas —

**167.** he, quickly casting off the sins of many births,
hastens to the supreme abode and serves Hari.

**168.** *"Those who at the hour of departure helplessly utter the names that reflect His avataras, qualities and deeds at once cast off the defilement of many births and attain the uncovered Truth; I take refuge in Him, the Unborn" (*Bhagavatam *3.9.15).*

**169.** Walking, sitting, dreaming, eating, lying down,
filling one's speech with the utterance of Krishna, the tamer of Kali —

**170.** even chanting the Name with neglect, one gains one's own true form
and goes fearlessly to the supreme abode, Vaikuntha.

**171.** *"Walking, standing, sleeping, eating, breathing, filling one's speech — having performed the chanting of the name of Vishnu, the tamer of Kali, even with neglect, one gains one's own true form and, joined with devotion, goes to the Supreme" (*Vishnu Purana*).*

**172.** In whatever way one takes Krishna's name,
Krishna, the fountain of mercy, loves him.

**173.** In drunkenness, possessed by spirits, suffering from a wind disorder —
uttering Hari's name, he holds liberation in his palm.

**174.** *"Glorifying Vasudeva, even a drunkard or a sick man is surely liberated; Maha-Vishnu is pleased" (*Varaha Purana*).*

**175.** Hari's name in itself is the supreme goal of life,
the essence of the goal and of good, full of supreme wealth.

*[p. 106]*

**176.** "The fruit of life" — so the *Kashi-khanda* calls it,
and the *Padma Purana* says so in many places:

**177.** *"This alone is good fortune, this alone the gaining of wealth, this alone the fruit of life — the chanting of Damodara" (*Padma Purana*).*

**178.** The supreme good of all goods,
spiritual by nature, the fruit of the creeper of all the Vedas —

**179.** Krishna's name, taken with faith or with neglect,
delivers every human being — so sing all the Vedas.

**180.** *"Sweeter than the sweetest, the good of all goods, the eternal fruit of the creeper of all the Vedas, spiritual by nature — Krishna's name, sung even once, with faith or with neglect, O best of the Bhrigus, delivers every human being" (*Prabhasa-khanda*).*

**181.** Of all the kinds of devotion seen in the scriptures,
refuge in the Name is sung as the best.

**182.** With hardship, by eightfold yoga, one achieves remembrance of Vishnu;
but the better way — kirtana — shines by the mere movement of the lips.

**183.** *"Unbroken remembrance of Vishnu is achieved with great labour; kirtana by the mere movement of the lips — therefore it is better" (*Vaishnava-chintamani*).*

**184.** If one worships [the Lord] for a hundred births, having received initiation,
Hari's name always shines on his tongue.

**185.** *"By whom Vasudeva was worshipped before for a hundred births — in his mouth the names of Hari always dwell, O Bharata" (*Vaishnava-chintamani*).*

*[p. 107]*

**186.** What was gained in Satya-yuga by long meditation,
what fruit was drawn in Treta by performing sacrifices,

**187.** what fruit was gained in Dvapara by worship —
in Kali all this is gained by Hari's name.

**188.** *"What one attains by meditation in Krita, by sacrifice in Treta, by worship in Dvapara, that one attains in Kali by glorifying Keshava" (*Vishnu Purana*).*

**189.** In the Kali age he is called a maha-bhagavata
who serves Hari by kirtana in this world.

**190.** *"The maha-bhagavatas in Kali always perform kirtana" (*Skanda Purana*).*

**191.** Whoever utters even once the spiritual name of Hari —
the fruit of that neither Shiva nor Brahma can describe.

**192.** The glory of uttering the Name is sung as wondrous:
by merely uttering it a man reaches the supreme abode.

**193.** *"The fruit of one who utters even once the spiritual name of Hari not even thousand-mouthed Vidhi can describe. The glory of uttering the Name is great and wondrous: by merely uttering it a man may reach the supreme abode" (*Brihan-naradiya Purana*).*

**194.** Krishna says: "Listen, Arjuna, I shall tell you:
the jivas who sing My name with faith or with neglect —

**195.** that name is always present in My heart.
There is no vow equal to the Name, no knowledge equal to the Name,

**196.** no meditation equal to the Name, no fruit equal to the Name,
no renunciation equal to the Name, no strength equal to the Name,

*[p. 108]*

**197.** no merit equal to the Name, no path equal to the Name:
even the Vedas cannot sing the power of the Name.

**198.** The Name is the highest liberation, the Name the highest path,
the Name the highest peace, the Name the highest abode;

**199.** the Name is the highest devotion, the Name the highest understanding,
the Name the highest love, the Name the highest remembrance;

**200.** the Name is the jiva's cause, the Name the jiva's lord,
the Name the highest object of worship, the Name is guru and master."

**201.** *"Those beings who repeat My name with faith or with neglect — their name, O Partha, is ever in My heart. There is no knowledge like the Name, no vow like the Name, no meditation like the Name, no fruit like the Name; no renunciation like the Name, no calm like the Name, no merit like the Name, no path like the Name. The Name alone is the highest liberation, the Name alone the highest path, the Name alone the highest peace, the Name alone the highest abode; the Name alone is the highest devotion, the Name alone the highest understanding, the Name alone the highest love, the Name alone the highest remembrance; the Name alone is the cause of a being, the Name alone its master, the Name alone the highest object of worship, the Name alone the highest guru" (*Adi Purana*).*

**202.** There is no limit to the glory of Hari's name:
hearing the Name, even a pulkasa is at once delivered.

**203.** *"By hearing His name even once, even a pulkasa is freed from samsara" (*Bhagavatam *6.16.44).*

*[p. 109]*

**204.** Whoever in sleep and waking murmurs Krishna's name
becomes in Kali "like Krishna" — such is Krishna's law.[^8]

**205.** *"Whoever in Kali constantly repeats 'Krishna, Krishna, Krishna' — sleeping, waking or walking — becomes like Krishna in form" (*Varaha Purana*).*

**206.** Whoever in the ocean of samsara always remembers [Me], saying "Krishna" —
him I lift out of hell like a lotus rising from the water.

**207.** *"Whoever always remembers Me, saying 'Krishna, Krishna, Krishna' — him I lift out of hell as a lotus parts the water" (*Narasimha Purana*).*

**208.** Krishna's name is the chief of all names, the jiva's refuge:
it takes away countless sins and frees from sin at once.

**209.** *"Of My names the foremost is the name 'Krishna', O scorcher of foes: it is the supreme atonement, freeing from all sins" (*Prabhasa-khanda*).*

**210.** The Name is a chintamani, Krishna Himself, spiritual by nature,
full, pure, eternally free: the Name and its Possessor are one.

**211.** *"The Name is a chintamani gem, Krishna Himself, the embodiment of spiritual rasa, full, pure, eternally free, for the Name and its Possessor are non-different" (*Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu *1.2.108, from the* Padma Purana*).*

**212.** Whoever knows the name of Vishnu as His spiritual power
prays with transcendent knowledge for good understanding.

**213.** *"O Vishnu, knowing Your name to be spiritual, we utter it… we worship good understanding. Om tat sat" (*Rig-veda *1.156.3).*[^9]

*[p. 110]*

**214.** Sthaneshvari Krishnadasa, with folded palms, says:
"Lord, I have one request.

**215.** Such glory of the Name I have heard with my own ears —
why then is the fruit not the same everywhere?"

**216.** The Lord says: "Faith and trust are the root of everything:
without trust no one gains the fruit."

**217.** The Lord says: "The Name is the indwelling Lord, Bhagavan:
He gives the fruit according to one's trust.

**218.** Whoever does not fully trust the glory of the Name
does not gain the Name's fruit and perishes by offence to the Name.

**219.** Giving up faith in the fruits, he takes them as exaggeration (arthavada):
he gains no fruit and remains fallen in hell.

**220.** *"The man who regards [the glory of] Hari's name as exaggeration — the most sinful of men — surely falls into hell" (*Katyayana-samhita*).*

**221.** *"The man who, having heard of the various fruits of chanting My name, does not believe and thinks it exaggeration — him I cast here into a heap of miseries, his body racked by the many terrible torments of samsara" (*Brahma-samhita*)."*

*End of the* Sri Sri Prema-vivarta.
