# Chapter 4. The Greatness of Gaura

*Gaura's eternal dance in Navadvipa and Puri; all the gods are Gauranga's servants; unwavering loyalty to Gaura: one cannot serve Gaura and worldly opinion at once (pp. 14–15)*

*[p. 14]*

**1.** Brother, serve Gauranga, my life:
without Gaura all of life's amusements are in vain.

**2.** In Navadvipa, in Mayapura, in Shachi's courtyard,
Gaura dances eternally with Nitai and Advaita.

**3.** In Srivasa's courtyard He dances and sings, full of rasa:
whoever saw it once will never forget it.

**4.** That dance, established in my heart,
keeps my heart weeping without end.

**5.** When I see the dancing in Jagannatha's temple,
endless waves of feeling rise in my mind.

**6.** Shall I see again the Lord's wondrous dancing and kirtana
on the bank of the Jahnavi in this wretched life?

*[p. 15]*

**7.** With firm faith, brother, serve the feet of Gauranga;
never worship other gods and goddesses.

**8.** Know all the gods to be Gauranga's servants;
never consider Gaura different from Krishna.

**9.** Know your own gurudeva as the chosen recipient of Gaura's mercy;
know Gauranga's associates as Gaura's body and limbs.

**10.** Know only this: even Gaura's enemies nourish His rasa;[^1]
all are Gauranga's servants — accept this.

**11.** Never blame others or gossip about them;
firmly and wholeheartedly serve Sri Gaura's feet.

**12.** Sing the great name "Gaura":
the glory of all other names you will find in that name.[^2]

**13.** There is no guru but Gaura in this world;
teach everyone guileless devotion to Gauranga.

**14.** Give up crookedness, make your mind guileless:
to serve Gaura and please people at the same time is fruitless.

**15.** Either serve Gora or serve people, brother:
two never stay together in one vessel.

**16.** Jagai says: if you are not faithful to one,
you will meet the plight of one who crosses a river in two boats.[^3]

[^1]: That is, even the Lord's opponents take part in His lila and set off its rasa (cf. Jagai and Madhai, whose deliverance revealed His mercy).
[^2]: The teaching that Gaura's name contains the glory of all names is a feature of the *Prema-vivarta*; in the Gaudiya Math tradition chanting Gaura's names does not replace the Hare Krishna maha-mantra, which Gaura Himself gave (cf. chs. 19–20).
[^3]: Jagai is the author's pet name (cf. ch. 5: "Kondale Jagai" — "Jagai the quarrelsome").
