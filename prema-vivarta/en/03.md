# Chapter 3. The First Obeisance

*Obeisance to Sri Radha; Sri Chaitanya with Gadadhara in Navadvipa and Puri; the service of Tota-Gopinatha and of Giridhari; the author writes by the devotees' command (pp. 13–14)*

*[p. 13]*

**1.** She whose manifestation is Satyabhama, dwelling in Dvaraka —
to the feet of that Radha my countless obeisances.[^1]

**2.** Sri Nandanandana is now Sri Krishna Chaitanya:
bringing Gadadhara with Him, He blessed Nadia.

**3.** With Gadadhara He came to Sri Purushottama,
in the form of Gadai and Gauranga performed a hidden lila,
and entrusted Gadadhara with the service of Tota-Gopinatha.[^2]

**4.** To me He gave the service of Giridhari on the shore of the ocean;
all the devotees from Gauda are near me.[^3]

*[p. 14]*

**5.** Damodara and Rupa are as dear to me as life:
Sri Krishna Chaitanya is their mind and life.[^4]

**6.** Bowing to the feet of Gaura, my life, prostrate with my whole body,
I write this *Prema-vivarta*, having received the devotees' command.

[^1]: According to Gaudiya Vaishnava theology the queens of Dvaraka, Satyabhama among them, are manifestations of Sri Radha (cf. CC Adi 4). The author's obeisance to Radha as the source of Satyabhama echoes GGD 51 (Jagadananda is a manifestation of Satyabhama).
[^2]: Tota-Gopinatha — the Deity in the garden (*tota*) by the sea at Puri, served, according to tradition, by Gadadhara Pandita, who lived in Puri with the Lord.
[^3]: The CC and CB say nothing of Jagadananda serving Giridhari in Puri — this is known only from the *Prema-vivarta*. According to the CC (Antya 12), the Lord sent Jagadananda from Puri to Navadvipa to Mother Shachi.
[^4]: Probably Svarupa Damodara and Rupa Gosvami; the reading "Damodara-svarupa" (one person) is also possible.
