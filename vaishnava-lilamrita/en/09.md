# Madhava Pattanayak. *Vaishnava-lilamrita*, Chapter 9
## The ishtagoshthi of Nilachala; Rupa brings his drama

*Translated from Odia without a native speaker; doubtful places are marked (?) and (??). The original, transliteration and word-for-word translation are in `full/09.md`.*

*Chapter Nine*

**9.1.** The ishtagoshthi of Nilachala gathers on the northern (?) side [of the temple].[^ishtagoshthi-1]  
**9.2.** Rama Raya is the chief in it; Prabhu and the Swami sit in the middle.[^ramaray-2]  
**9.3.** Balarama [Das] is beside the Swami; [there too are] Ananta, Achyuta, Yashovanta.[^panchasakha-3]  
**9.4.** Svarupa [and] Raghunatha Das are distinguished scholars.[^raghunatha-4]  
**9.5.** Sarvabhauma sits in the middle; he conducts the deliberation on the shastras.[^sarvabhauma-5]  
**9.6.** From Vrindavana comes Rupa — in the forty-fourth anka of the king.[^rupa-6]  
**9.7.** Rupa showed his drama; Prabhu bids (?) [him] read [it].[^nataka-7]  
**9.8.** [All] came to the ishtagoshthi; Rupa showed the drama.  
**9.9.** Prabhu handed [it] to Rama Raya: "Examine [it]," [He] said.[^pariksha-9]  

## Ramananda tests Rupa's drama; the passing of Svarupa Damodara; Gadadhara is sent to Gauda; the passing of Balarama Das


**9.10.** Raya said: "Recite the shlokas," in order to test [his] poetic gift.[^raya-10]  
**9.11.** Rupa recites all the shlokas, from the nandi to Radha's mana.[^nandi-11]  
**9.12.** Having heard Rupa, Raya said: "Your poetry is a treasure-trove of nectar.[^amrita-12]  
**9.13.** Such (?) a drama of rasa you have made! We have tasted (?) the rasa of bhakti (?).[^edeka-13]  
**9.14.** Your drama is excellent; may it become known in all lands."  
**9.15.** This drama, the *Vidagdha-madhava*, became known among the people.[^vidagdha-15]  
**9.16.** Rupa stayed [in Puri] four months; at the beginning of Karttika he departs.[^rupa-16]  
**9.17.** Raghunatha [Das] set out with Rupa on the road to Vrindavan.[^raghunatha-17]  
**9.18.** But Svarupa, remaining here, endures many sufferings.[^svarupa-18]  
**9.19.** In the torment (??) of this breathing disease he tosses about [in pain].[^bejjara-19]  
**9.20.** On the ekadashi of the bright fortnight of Magha came his leaving [of the body] in the Gambhira.[^svarupa-20]  
**9.21.** Ananta, Achyuta [and I], we gathered; we called Yashovanta.[^we-21]  
**9.22.** We carried the body to the sea; we gave the saint to the waves.[^sea-22]  
**9.23.** No word comes forth (?) from Prabhu's mouth; Raya stood stunned (?).[^stunned-23]  
**9.24.** Together with the Swami [and] Balarama they grieved deeply there.  
**9.25.** Prabhu calls Gadadhara: "Go from here (?) to Gauda;[^gadadhara-25]  
**9.26.** Svarupa has passed away (?) here: inform the devotees properly."[^marigala-26]  
**9.27.** Gadadhara went to Gauda at the beginning of the month of Phalguna.[^phalguna-27]  
**9.28.** Balarama [Das] is an excellent pandit, devoted to the doctrine of the Vedanta shastra.[^balarama-28]  
**9.29.** He composed a *Ramayana* [and] an exposition of the *Bhagavad-gita*.[^ramayana-29]  
**9.30.** His mind is firm in brahma-jnana; in bhakti (?) … (??) … (??).[^bhagati-30]  
**9.31.** He always performs kirtana; he says nothing (?) to anyone.[^kahu-31]  
**9.32.** He chants the Name unceasingly. The time of [his] passing came.[^bhajai-32]  
**9.33.** He said: "I will give up food," calling (?) it dharma (?) [and] austerity (?).[^anna-33]  
**9.34.** He sits in seclusion (?) in a mandapa; on the tenth day (?) of the bright fortnight of Phalguna[^phalguna-34]  
**9.35.** the breath leaves (?) the body; … (??) falls.[^pinda-35]  
**9.36.** Prabhu, the Swami, Yashovanta, Kanhai, Achyuta, Ananta[^kanhai-36]  
**9.37.** [and] Rama Raya came; all recalled [his] virtues.  
**9.38.** He, dear to Jagannatha, enters Jagannatha's body.[^merge-38]  
**9.39.** In the sand (?) of the seashore they buried the body there (??).[^burial-39]  
**9.40.** The devotees heard the news, with heads bowed, shedding tears.[^bhagate-40]  
**9.41.** Prabhu [and] the Swami did not rise (?); they spoke not a word of it.[^uthile-41]  

## Prabhu injures His foot while dancing; the night in the northern mandapa, the Swami beside Him


**9.42.** Listen attentively, good people: Prabhu's body is holy.[^dehi-42]  
**9.43.** Prabhu's body is a pure body; [hear] how in time it came to decline.[^kshaya-43]  
**9.44.** One day [He] was dancing and did not leave His unrestrained bhava.[^uddama-44]  
**9.45.** To the khol and kartal [His] feet moved incessantly.  
**9.46.** A brick lay on the path — He did not notice it.[^brick-46]  
**9.47.** [The brick] struck the big toe of [His] left foot; blood flows without stopping (?).[^toe-47]  
**9.48.** The foot twisted at the joint (?); with a loud cry [He] fell to the ground.[^khanja-48]  
**9.49.** The Swami, Kashishvara Mishra, Yashovanta, Srivatsa, Ananta —[^kashishvara-49]  
**9.50.** all of them together lift Prabhu from the ground.  
**9.51.** Prabhu has lost consciousness: He lies on the ground without awareness.  
**9.52.** They lifted Prabhu onto their shoulders — [it was] the evening of the Rukmini new moon.[^rukmini-52]  
**9.53.** In the mandapa on the north side they laid [Him] down on the floor (??).[^mandapa-53]  
**9.54.** Breath (?) comes from His nostrils; [He] lies with eyes closed (?).[^anila-54]  
**9.55.** They sprinkled water on His face; after some time consciousness awakens.  
**9.56.** [He] opened His eyes and lay [so]; breath entered His body.  
**9.57.** All rejoiced; the Swami gazes at Prabhu's face.  
**9.58.** He says: "Prabhu, speak a word, give life back to my body!"[^prana-58]  
**9.59.** In time Prabhu came to Himself (?); all the people went off to their own (?) places.[^sastama-59]  
**9.60.** At about the first watch of the night Rama Raya withdrew (?).[^bije-60]  
**9.61.** In the temple the khol and tal sound; a deep, mighty roar arose (?).[^mandira-61]  
**9.62.** Prabhu says: "Carry Me there — I shall watch the dancing, staying a moment."  
**9.63.** But the foot cannot be set down; the Swami, giving his hand, seats [Him].[^swami-63]  
**9.64.** [The Swami] said: "Prabhu, do not go; sit still in this place.  
**9.65.** There is pain in Your foot; do not move from here even a cubit.  
**9.66.** Drink, food, bathing (?) for You — I shall stay [here] and provide it all (?)."[^nai-66]  
**9.67.** Thus the night passed; dawn came (?).[^thula-67]  

## The fever and Prabhu's last words to the Swami; Prabhu's disappearance on Akshaya Tritiya; Jagannatha Dasa beside the body; word sent to Rama Raya


**9.68.** Prabhu's body is burning with fever — as though puffed rice would pop on it (?).[^khai-68]  
**9.69.** By evening (?) the foot had swollen; the toe of the left foot gave pain.[^sanja-69]  
**9.70.** The Swami heated water and dipped a rag (?) in it while it was hot (?)[^kania-70]  
**9.71.** [and] laid it on Prabhu's left foot: "Has it become warm?" — he asked (?).[^ushuma-71]  
**9.72.** Receiving the warmth of the hot rag, [Prabhu] was soothed.  
**9.73.** Yet the fever (?) did not let go; at night (?) shivering seized the body.[^tapa-73]  
**9.74.** Collecting [Himself] (?), [Prabhu] said to the Swami: "While you are [here], there is no term (kala) for Me.[^kala-74]  
**9.75.** Would I ever leave your company? [But] Hari's will is being fulfilled.  
**9.76.** All Hari's servants in the world — not for an instant (?) does dying befall them.[^labe-76]  
**9.77.** You are the servant of Jagannatha; I am your servant, [and it was] unseemly (?):[^sevaka-77]  
**9.78.** I received service from your hands. Jagabandhu knows all.[^jagabandhu-78]  
**9.79.** Without Him there is no other refuge; He decides (?) the lot of living beings.[^artha-79]  
**9.80.** Taking [Me] to His feet, will He take Me to His own abode?[^nijadhama-80]  
**9.81.** You, Swami, are My life (?) — wise, learned, knowing.[^parana-81]  
**9.82.** You showed [Me] the path of bhakti; you bound Me with ties of friendship.[^bhagati-82]  
**9.83.** Leaving you, where shall I go? [There] I shall be watching (?) the road by which you will come."[^bata-83]  
**9.84.** Tears are in Prabhu's eyes; the Swami gazes at [His] moon-like face (?).[^chandra-84]  
**9.85.** Tears mingled with tears and flowed in an overflowing (?) stream.[^upaca-85]  
**9.86.** The Swami stays close beside Him; he serves Prabhu, never taking his eyes off [Him].  
**9.87.** The bond of feeling [as] of a faithful wife, when [she] gives (??) her life to the moon (??).[^sadhvi-87]  
**9.88.** The fever does not leave the body; the whole body has swollen.  
**9.89.** Akshaya Tritiya arrived — [the time of] the chandana, the garlands (?) [and] the abakasha.[^akshaya-89]  
**9.90.** The hour of the brahma-muhurta came — the breath of life left Prabhu's body.[^antardhana-90]  
**9.91.** Jagannatha Dasa alone was beside Him in the temple.[^mandire-91]  
**9.92.** He weeps aloud: "O Prabhu, where have You gone?"[^kene-92]  
**9.93.** Tears stream from his eyes; [he] turns over (??) Prabhu's holy body.[^oltai-93]  
**9.94.** Having laid the body down carefully (?), he weeps, unblinking (?).[^shaba-94]  
**9.95.** A crow came and cried out; the east (?) grew bright.[^indradiga-95]  
**9.96.** [It was] the time of the morning abakasha; the temple lies still [and silent].[^abakasha-96]  
**9.97.** One or two servitors came to the temple.  
**9.98.** The Swami said: "Go quickly, inform Rama Raya.[^ramaray-98]  
**9.99.** Let him come here without delay; go now, at a run (?)."[^jhapata-99]  
**9.100.** Receiving the message, Rama Raya at once (?) arrived at the temple.[^takshane-100]  

## Rama Raya and the Gajapati; the secret burial in Koili Vaikuntha


**9.101.** Seeing Prabhu's grievous state, [Rama Raya] for a moment stood frozen in deep shock (?).[^sugambhira-101]  
**9.102.** Tears stream from [his] eyes; he took [Prabhu's] body onto his lap.[^shaba-102]  
**9.103.** He stroked Prabhu's face and passed his hand over [His] whole body.  
**9.104.** "Prabhu is no more!" — [thinking] thus, he let go of (?) the body and rose.[^nahiki-104]  
**9.105.** Hastily (??) calling the servants, he gave an order aloud (?):[^turite-105]  
**9.106.** "Today the gates will be bolted; the Deity's rites will not be performed."[^niti-106]  
**9.107.** Summoning (?) a horseman, he at once sent a letter.  
**9.108.** As it happened, [it was the day of] Akshaya Tritiya, the joyful (?) Chandana Yatra.[^chandana-108]  
**9.109.** Gajapati Rudradeva, as it happened (?), had set out [for the temple] for darshan.[^rudradeva-109]  
**9.110.** Meeting the horseman on the way, he opens the letter and reads it.  
**9.111.** With grief [his] face was clouded with sorrow. It was about midday (?).[^yugaprahara-111]  
**9.112.** Rama Raya informed [him] and says: "What are we to do with Prabhu?"  
**9.113.** The Gajapati stood stupefied (?): truly, not a word comes from his mouth.[^darubhuta-113]  
**9.114.** Rama Raya says: "Listen: if this body is carried out, [it will bring] harm.[^akarana-114]  
**9.115.** The Gaudiyas will stir up trouble and speak hostile words to the mleccha king.[^gaudiya-115]  
**9.116.** Great strife will arise; out of empty talk [they will make] both lies and truth.  
**9.117.** They will piece together many rumours — and in the end ill fame (?) will fall to us.[^apaninda-117]  
**9.118.** Prabhu departed (?) in the temple — here we shall commit Him to the earth.[^marigala-118]  
**9.119.** Your Majesty, at the time of the Ratha Yatra you called [Him] 'Prabhu' before the pilgrims;[^mahima-119]  
**9.120.** [so] there is ground for saying: 'This Prabhu became absorbed in that Prabhu.'[^lina-120]  
**9.121.** It is fitting to commit the body to the earth at the place (?) of Koili Vaikuntha,[^koili-121]  
**9.122.** where the worn-out [forms] of the Deity Jagannatha are buried — carry it there."  
**9.123.** The Gajapati, with face cast down, agreed to what was fitting.  
**9.124.** Taking the body to Koili Vaikuntha, having a pit dug, they committed [it] to the earth —  
**9.125.** Rama Raya, the revered Swami, and one or two servants besides.[^swamipada-125]  
**9.126.** No one else knows of this. The bolted gates are opened.[^secret-126]  
**9.127.** They ordered a purification (?) — the whole temple was washed.[^samskara-127]  
**9.128.** With camphor, sandal [and] aguru the servants sprinkled [the temple] inside.  

## The news of Prabhu's disappearance; the grief of the Panchasakha; a Ratha Yatra without Prabhu


**9.129.** At that time, seeing that the festival of Chandana was beginning,  
**9.130.** the devotees came [and] gathered; [then] they set off, [making] for Markanda.[^markanda-130]  
**9.131.** On the festival day there is sorrow: on no one's face is there joy.  
**9.132.** Receiving the news of Prabhu's disappearance, all [were] in dismay:[^antardhana-132]  
**9.133.** "He has merged into Jagannatha's body — as lightning hides in a cloud."[^vijuli-133]  
**9.134.** They look (?) into one another's faces; at the talk of Prabhu [all] were beside themselves.[^heranti-134]  
**9.135.** All grew sorrowful at heart, remembering Prabhu's qualities:  
**9.136.** "Prabhu is Bhagavan Himself; He appeared (lit. "was born") in order to dance (?).[^bhagavan-136]  
**9.137.** And now, alas, He has merged into the holy body!" Hearing [this], all were overwhelmed with feeling.[^shrianga-137]  
**9.138.** Achyuta, Kanhai, Yashovanta and Ananta came to know [of it].[^panchasakha-138]  
**9.139.** Hearing it, all were grieved; embracing Swami, they weep.  
**9.140.** Achyuta says to Swami: "Why did you let Prabhu go?[^tu-140]  
**9.141.** We could not see [Him]. Why did you not bind [Him and] keep [Him]?"[^bandhi-141]  
**9.142.** Yashovanta says this: "What sort of talk is [this] boy talking!"[^baluta-142]  
**9.143.** The child Ananta does not understand — he weeps like (?) a little child.[^parae-143]  
**9.144.** Truly, all recalled Prabhu's deeds day and night.  
**9.145.** The Gaudiya devotees arrived; on the chariot the king proclaimed (?) [the news].[^gaudiya-145]  
**9.146.** The Gaudiyas all departed; going to Gauda (?), they told [it].[^gaude-146]  
**9.147.** The news of Prabhu's departure they all learned together at the Ratha Yatra.[^prayana-147]  
**9.148.** Not finding Prabhu at the chariot, the king grieved deeply.  
**9.149.** The golden broom fell: from the king's hand — this had never been (?).[^broom-149]  
**9.150.** During the Ratha Yatra the people (?) learned this news.[^laukike-150]  
**9.151.** The month of Bhadra, Krishnashtami: knowing [it to be] the festival of Krishna's birth,[^janmashtami-151]  

## Rama Raya's departure; the circle disperses; the end of the poem


**9.152.** There was no joyful kirtan; in every heart there was dejection.[^birasa-152]  
**9.153.** To the mandapa on the northern side Rama Raya came and sat down.[^parushe-153]  
**9.154.** Remembering Prabhu's bhava, [he weeps] — tears brim over from his eyes.  
**9.155.** With the Swami he talks fondly, remembering the kirtan.[^swami-155]  
**9.156.** The Swami said: "I will not come [any more]; I will live on the seashore."[^samudra-156]  
**9.157.** Achyuta went (?) to Nemala (?); Ananta went to his own place.[^nemala-157]  
**9.158.** On that [same] day, at evening time, Rama Raya departed to heaven.[^svarga-158]  
**9.159.** As the prana was leaving (?) [his] body, he uttered: "O Krishna!"[^hakrishna-159]  
**9.160.** At once (?) a stir arose; people hastily gathered.[^khene-160]  
**9.161.** On every lip was the name of Hari; they say: "Rama's name is truth."[^satya-161]  
**9.162.** Good or bad, a living being speaks its word at the hour of passing (??); knowing this,[^marane-162]  
**9.163.** Jagannatha Dasa says: "He is a perfected one (a siddha)."[^siddha-163]  
**9.164.** This is so; it cannot be otherwise (?), — says Madhava, a shudra (?).[^shudra-164]  

*Thus the* Vaishnava-lilamrita *is complete.*

[^ishtagoshthi-1]: The ishtagoshthi is a gathering of intimate devotees for talks about Krishna; in the CC it is the usual expression for Mahaprabhu's discussions with His associates. Nilachala is Puri. Second half-line: printed ଉ + the ligature ତ୍ତ — we read ଉତ୍ତ, apparently a shortened ଉତ୍ତର, "northern" (?); ଉଚ୍ଚ, "high", is also possible. *Pāruśeṇa* is from *pāruśa* = *pārśwa*, "side". Printed ନୀଲାଚଲର, ବସନ୍ତ.
[^ramaray-2]: Rama Raya is Ramananda Raya. The Swami is Jagannatha Dasa. The Swami's place beside Prabhu at the centre of the gathering is Madhava's Odia tradition. According to the CC (Antya 6, 14–20), Mahaprabhu's closest companions in conversation at Puri were Svarupa Damodara and Ramananda Raya. Jagannatha Dasa is not mentioned in the CC or CB. Printed ମଧେ (= ମଧ୍ୟେ), ବସନ୍ତ.
[^panchasakha-3]: Balarama Das, Ananta, Achyuta (Achyutananda), Yashovanta and the Swami are the Panchasakha (cf. 8.4, 8.33). Printed ବଲରାମ.
[^raghunatha-4]: Svarupa Damodara and Raghunatha Das (Gosvami). According to the CC (Antya 6), Mahaprabhu entrusted Raghunatha Das to Svarupa Damodara, and he lived for many years in Puri under his care. Printed ଅଟନ୍ତ, ବଶେଷ.
[^sarvabhauma-5]: Vasudeva Sarvabhauma, the Vedantist scholar of Puri converted by Mahaprabhu (CC, Madhya 6). Printed ମଧେ, ବସନ୍ତ, କରନ୍ତ.
[^rupa-6]: Rupa Gosvami. "The forty-fourth anka of the king" is the regnal year of Prataparudra counted in ankas, ≈1531/32 (±1 year). According to the CC (Antya 1), Rupa came to Puri a second time from Vrindavana by way of Gauda, soon after meeting Mahaprabhu at Prayaga (Madhya 19). In the chronology accepted by Gaudiya Vaishnavas this was long before the 1530s. Printed ଚଉରାଲିସେ.
[^nataka-7]: Madhava names the drama below, in 9.15 (leaf 114): *Vidagdha-madhava*. According to the CC (Antya 1), in Puri Rupa was writing two dramas, *Vidagdha-madhava* and *Lalita-madhava*. The verb is printed ≈ ବୋଲି + a sign ଇ (OCR ବୋଲଇ); we read ବୋଲଇ, "says, bids". *Paṛhibāka* = ପଢ଼ିବାକୁ, "to read" (printed ପଢ଼ବାକ).
[^pariksha-9]: Cf. CC (Antya 1): Mahaprabhu came with Ramananda Raya, Sarvabhauma and other associates to where Rupa was staying (with Haridasa Thakura) and asked Rupa to read verses from the dramas. Ramananda questioned Rupa about the drama and praised his poetry. In Madhava the examination takes place in the ishtagoshthi. Printed ପରୀକ୍ଷ (= ପରୀକ୍ଷା).
[^raya-10]: Raya is Ramananda Raya (cf. 9.2, 9.9). According to the CC (Antya 1), Ramananda questioned Rupa in detail about his dramas, and Rupa recited verses from them in the presence of Mahaprabhu, Sarvabhauma and other associates. The word ଶ୍ଳୋକ is printed with a subscript ଲ (ଶ୍ଲୋକ); *kabitwa* in 9.10 is printed ≈ କବତ୍ୱ (the ‑i broken). *Paramāṇibāka* means "in order to test" (a purposive infinitive).
[^nandi-11]: The nandi is the benedictory verse at the opening of a Sanskrit drama. Mana is Radha's loving pique towards Krishna. "From the nandi to Radha's mana" means from the first verse of the drama to one of its last scenes. According to the CC (Antya 1), Ramananda began his questions with the nandi verse.
[^amrita-12]: Cf. CC, Antya 1: Ramananda says that Rupa's verses are not mere poetry but a stream of nectar. We take *rūpe śuṇi* as "having heard Rupa"; an address, "Rupa, listen", is also possible.
[^edeka-13]: The first word is printed ≈ ଏ + e + a sign like ହ/ଡ଼ + କ (OCR ଏଡ଼େକ); we read ଏଡ଼େକ, "such, so great". *Bhakatā* (printed ଭକତା) is either "bhakti" or "of the devotees" (?). The form *āswādilu* can mean either "you tasted" or "we tasted"; we choose the latter, since Ramananda speaks for the listeners. Printed ≈ ଅଃସ୍ୱାଦଲୁ.
[^vidagdha-15]: The *Vidagdha-madhava* is Rupa Gosvami's Sanskrit drama on Krishna's lilas in Vrindavan. According to the CC (Antya 1), Rupa first planned a single drama. Then, at Satyabhama's instruction in a dream and with Mahaprabhu's approval, he divided it into two: the *Vidagdha-madhava* on the Vrindavan lilas and the *Lalita-madhava* on the Dvaraka lilas. Madhava names only the first.
[^rupa-16]: Karttika falls in October–November. According to the CC (Antya 1), Rupa stayed in Puri until the Dola-yatra, a spring festival, and then returned to Vrindavan through Gauda. The four-month stay and the departure in Karttika are Madhava's account. Printed ରହଲେ and ≈ ଚାଶମାସେ ("ri" looks like ଶ).
[^raghunatha-17]: According to the CC (Antya 6), Raghunatha Das Gosvami lived in Puri for sixteen years in the care of Svarupa Damodara. He went to Vrindavan only after the disappearance of Mahaprabhu and Svarupa (cf. CC, Adi 10), and he did not leave with Rupa. Madhava's account differs; cf. also 9.4.
[^svarupa-18]: This is Svarupa Damodara. Madhava has already spoken of his frailty and sufferings in 8.93–94.
[^bejjara-19]: The last word of the first half-line is printed ≈ ବେ + an unclear ligature with a subscript + ର (OCR ବେଭ୍ର); we read ବେଜ୍ଜର (??), perhaps from ବେଜାର, "worn out". *Kaḷabaḷa* (printed କଲବଲ) means "tosses about in agony". The breathing disease is apparently asthma.
[^svarupa-20]: Magha falls in January–February. No year is named; in the sequence of the narrative this is the Magha after Rupa's arrival (9.6), i.e. ≈1532/33 (±1 year). *Deharakṣā*, literally "keeping the body", is a respectful expression for leaving the body. The Gambhira is the room in Kashi Mishra's house where Mahaprabhu lived (cf. 5.20). According to the CC (Antya 14–20), Svarupa Damodara served Mahaprabhu in the Gambhira without leaving Him until the end of His lila. In Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, Svarupa left this world soon after Mahaprabhu's disappearance. That Svarupa passed away while Mahaprabhu was still present is the account of Madhava's Odia tradition; it is not in the CC/CB.
[^we-21]: We take the verbs in ‑*ilu* in 9.21–22 as first person plural ("we"). Madhava apparently counts himself among those who carried the body. Printed ≈ ମିଳଲୁ, ହକାରଲୁ. Ananta, Achyuta(nanda) and Yashovanta belong to the Panchasakha (cf. 9.3).

[^gaudiya-115]: The "Gaudiyas" are Mahaprabhu's devotees from Gauda (Bengal); the "mleccha king" is the Muslim ruler of Gauda, the Gajapati's adversary (cf. CC, Madhya 16). The CC and CB report no such conflict. *Khara* means "sharp, hostile [words]"; *kadartha* means "distortion, disgrace, trouble".

[^marigala-118]: Printed ≈ ମା + a sign like ରି/ବ୍ର + ଗଲ. We read, as in 9.26, ମରିଗଲ, the ordinary verb for the end of a person's earthly life. The words belong to Rama Raya in the Odia account. We translate "departed" (see the notes to 9.42 and 9.102).
[^mahima-119]: *Mahimā*, "greatness", is here a form of address to the king. Rama Raya reminds him that the Gajapati once, before the pilgrims, called Sri Chaitanya "Prabhu", that is, the Lord. Jagannatha too is called "Prabhu"; hence the play on words in 9.120. Madhava does not say which occasion is meant. According to the CC (Madhya 13–14), at the Ratha Yatra Prataparudra served by sweeping the road before the chariot and then received Mahaprabhu's mercy. ଯାତ୍ର is printed without ‑ā (the metre has 9 syllables).
[^lina-120]: "This Prabhu became absorbed in that Prabhu": Sri Chaitanya in Jagannatha. In Madhava these are the words Rama Raya proposes to announce to the people to conceal the burial. Cf. 9.38 and the note to 9.38 on the expression "to enter Jagannatha's body". Gaudiya Vaishnavas have different accounts of Mahaprabhu's disappearance. According to Lochana Dasa's *Chaitanya-mangala*, He entered the Deity of Jagannatha; according to another tradition, the Deity of Tota-Gopinatha. For Gaudiya Vaishnavas Sri Chaitanya is Krishna Himself (CC, Adi 1), non-different from Jagannatha. His "entering" is the Lord's disappearance, not the merging of a soul into God, nor the burial of a material body.
[^koili-121]: Koili Vaikuntha is a place in the northern part of the Jagannatha temple compound in Puri. There, at the Navakalevara (renewal of the Deities' forms), the former wooden forms of Jagannatha, Baladeva, Subhadra and Sudarshana are buried (cf. 9.122). Printed କୋଇଲ (‑i lost); *purate* is printed ≈ ପୂରତେ. We take it as "at the place, before" (?), from Skt *puratas* or ପୁର + ‑ତେ.
[^swamipada-125]: The Swami is Jagannatha Dasa (cf. note to 9.49); *swāmī pāda*, the honorific "the Swami's feet", means "the revered Swami". The Gajapati is not named among those who performed the burial. 9.124–125 form one sentence.
[^secret-126]: Only those named in 9.125 know of it: this is how Madhava explains why the account did not become known. *Paṛila duāra* means "the closed ('fallen') doors" (printed ପଡ଼ଲ): after the burial the temple is opened again.
[^samskara-127]: The first word is printed ≈ ସ + ସ୍କା/ସ୍ଥା + ର; the same sign appears in the editor's note on this leaf. We read ସଂସ୍କାର, "purification" (?) (no anusvara is visible). [^markanda-130]: The festival of Chandana is the Chandana Yatra, which begins on Akshaya Tritiya (see note to 9.108). Markanda is apparently the sacred Markandeya tank in Puri, one of the five tirthas of the town. [^antardhana-132]: *Antarddhāna* means "disappearance, withdrawal from view". Here Madhava himself calls Prabhu's departure by this word — the same word Gaudiya Vaishnavas use for it (see note to 9.90). Verses 9.132–137 concern the news that the devotees and the people learned; only a few knew of the burial in Koili Vaikuntha (9.124–126).
[^vijuli-133]: This is the news that Rama Raya proposed to announce in 9.120: "this Prabhu became absorbed in that Prabhu". The simile of lightning: lightning does not perish in a cloud, it only passes from view. On how Gaudiya Vaishnavas understand Mahaprabhu's "entering" Jagannatha, see the note to 9.120. Printed ଲୁଚଗଲ (= ଲୁଚିଗଲ).
[^heranti-134]: The last word of the first half-line is printed ≈ ହେ + ର + a sign resembling ଶ (or ‑ନ୍ତି). We read ହେରନ୍ତି, "they look" (?); ହେରଇ is also possible. The sense of the half-line is clear. *Bhoḷa* (printed ≈ ଭ୍ରଳ) means "bewildered, beside oneself".
[^bhagavan-136]: In 9.136–137a the devotees speak, remembering Prabhu. They call Him "Bhagavan Himself" (*sākṣāte bhagabāna*). The second half-line is unclear: we take *nṛtya kalaku* as "in order to dance" (= *karibāku*?) (?). Mahaprabhu's dancing in kirtan is a recurring motif of the poem (cf. 5.8–13).

[^panchasakha-138]: Achyuta (Achyutananda), Yashovanta and Ananta belong to the Panchasakha; for Kanhai see the note to 9.36. The same persons are named together in 9.36. [^tu-140]: Swami is Jagannatha Dasa. He was with Prabhu to the end and at the burial (9.63–66, 9.125). [^baluta-142]: Yashovanta checks Achyuta: such reproaches are childish. Madhava calls Achyuta a "boy" (*bāḷuta*) and Ananta (9.143) a "child"; apparently they were younger than the others (?). Printed ≈ କଂବା (= କିବା).

[^janmashtami-151]: Krishnashtami (Janmashtami) is the festival of Krishna's appearance, the eighth day of the dark fortnight of Bhadra (August–September). The narrative thus runs from Akshaya Tritiya (Vaishakha) through the Ratha Yatra (Ashadha) to Janmashtami — about four months. The sentence continues in 9.152 (beyond this portion).
[^birasa-152]: The account of Krishna Janmashtami continues (cf. 9.151). No year is named; in the course of the story it is the same year, ≈1533/34 (±1 year). Formerly on this day the ishtagoshthi sang and danced together with Prabhu; now, without Him, the kirtan gives no joy. *Birasa* means "tasteless, devoid of rasa", i.e. "joyless".
[^parushe-153]: The mandapa on the northern side [of the temple] is the place of the ishtagoshthi (cf. 9.1, 9.53 and the notes there). Nemala (Nemal, Cuttack district) is, by Odia tradition, the place where Achyutananda lived and where his samadhi is. [^svarga-158]: *Swarga gale*, "went to heaven", is a respectful expression for leaving this life (cf. 3.119 on Madhavendra Puri). [^siddha-163]: *Siddha puruṣa* is a "perfected" devotee, one who has reached the goal. We understand that Jagannatha Dasa bears witness to Rama Raya. [^colophon-164]: Three unnumbered lines after the last couplet. *Iti … sampūrṇṇa*, "thus is complete…", is the usual formula at the end of a manuscript. *Lekhanakāra* means "writer, copyist"; the request not to blame him for errors is a common scribal addition, so these lines apparently belong not to Madhava but to the copyist. They give no date of copying, no anka and no copyist's name. *Biśeṣa* (?) in the second line means "special" and also "abundant, more besides". A possible sense: "the account here is extensive [and may have been copied with errors]", or "there is much else [about this]".
