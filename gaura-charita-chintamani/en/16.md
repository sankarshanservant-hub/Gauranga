# Kirana 16. The Deeds of the Morning: The Description of the Various Conversations and So On of the Hosts of the Wives of the Nagas[^1]

*Morning: the wives of the nagas, bowing at Gaura's feet, in prema praise the good fortune of the people of Navadvipa and lament their own lot, and then one after another tell their friends of the beauty and deeds of Gaura that they have been able to see*

*Again, in the morning period — the various conversations and so on of the hosts of the wives of the nagas.*

*Raga bhairava*

**16.1.** At the end of the night, at dawn — who could describe [this] happiness?
Beautifully adorned, the hosts of naga women bow at the feet of the wondrous Gaura. *(Refrain)*
Overpowered by the rasa of prema, the movement of all their bodies has stopped; filled with abundant waves of thrills of ecstasy, they shine ceaselessly;
like rows of creepers shaken by a mighty wind, [their] hearts tremble ceaselessly; they have neither composure nor steadiness.
Again and again they say: "The present dwellers of Navadvipa — their birth is fulfilled, with their own mouths they sing [His] qualities —
those before whose eyes is always the lovely radiance of Gaura!" — saying so much, they shed tears from their eyes.
They lament in many ways, revile the Creator, and then joyfully, fervently praise their own good fortune;
seeing the sweetness of the moon-like face of the Lord of servant Narahari, they offer themselves; the heart longs for a touch.[^2]

*Chaupadi*

**16.2.** The naga women at the end of the night, seeing the Lord's face — their bodies thrilled with ecstasy.
Who can understand the way of new prema? They tell one another of Gaura's deeds.[^3]

*One [of them] says — raga vibhasha*

**16.3.** "Listen, listen, O playful friend,
I will tell you how I saw the Moon of Nadiya.
With what playfulness He walks along the road,
smiling and smiling, floating in rasa — how much He talks with His friends!
The play of His eyes — how sweet!
Seeing [it], hundreds of thousands of women, like madwomen — in what rasa! — are intoxicated by Madana.
Ah, ah! What beauty of body!
How elegant His attire! His garments and ornaments draw the world.
Lovely, curly, glossy hair,
half bound with a fresh garland of malati — [seeing it,] not a trace of composure can be kept.
He has entered my heart.
Narahari knows: this story I cannot tell for shame."[^4]

*And another — raga lalita*

**16.4.** "Listen, friend! He is the Moon of Nadiya,
a form that crushes Manmatha's pride, a net of the rasa of love.
With His beauty He has immersed the land of Nadiya [in Himself].
On the day of Bhratri-dvitiya, at dawn, I saw [His] attire that enchants the world.
Without laughing, that face laughs.
On His forehead a lovely dot of sandal paste destroys the pride of the moon.
Ah, ah! What more can I say!
Narahari knows what [He] does to my heart: may I be [His] nirmanchhana!"[^5]

*And another — raga bhairava*

**16.5.** "O friend! That beautiful Gora —
the embodiment of rasa, conquering Ratipati, the thief of young women's lives.
With the play of His eyes He has bewitched the land.
On the day of Akshaya-tritiya I saw [Him] — what new, sweet attire!
His body anointed with lovely sandal paste —
as if moonlight had covered a pure new golden mountain.
That beauty cannot be forgotten.
Narahari knows: for my whole life I have sold myself to His two feet."[^6]

*And another — raga todi*

**16.6.** "Gaurasundara is a well of rasa;
His beauty subdues kunkuma, ketaki, gold, haritala and lightning.
Friend! On the day of Vijaya-dashami
on the bank of the Suradhuni I saw Him playing with His friends.
What attire that enchants the world!
Out of shame Manmatha gave up his body on seeing those curly locks.
Narahari, what merriment there was!
All — women and men alike — leaving [everything], rushed in a throng to where Gauranga was."[^7]

*And another — raga dhanashi*

**16.7.** "Friend! What enchantment Gora knows!
Moment by moment His beauty grew ever greater — I saw [it] on the day of [His] wedding.
Who would not lose himself seeing Him?
Lighting up the world with His beauty, He goes seated in a lovely chaudola palanquin.
What enchanting attire at the wedding!
Women of good families, faithful wives, leave their husbands and do not count it the slightest disgrace.
Narahari, what sweetness is His!
The people of Nadiya are intoxicated: day and night they know nothing but Him."[^8]

*Raga mangala*

**16.8.** The hosts of naga women again and again speak in many ways;
tears brim over from their eyes, their breasts are filled with great joy.
Overwhelmed by prema, they shine; on their bodies — new, abundant thrills of ecstasy;
offering their lives [to Him] in nirmanchhana, they are ever at the feet of Narahari's Lord.[^9]

*Thus [ends] the sixteenth kirana of the Shrimad Gaura-charita-chintamani, called "The Description of the Various Conversations and So On of the Hosts of the Wives of the Nagas", in the description of the deeds of the morning period.*[^10]

[^2]: Raga bhairava is a morning raga (cf. 15.1). pdf 158 (printed p. 153). Lines 1–2 are the refrain (*dhru*; the mark "॥ধ্রু॥" stands after line 2). The author's words. "Beautifully adorned" — *parama ramaṇīya bani*. "Naga women" — *nāga-ramaṇī*. "The wondrous Gaura" — *bara-gaura*. "The movement of their bodies has stopped" — *gāta gati thakita*. "Filled with abundant waves of thrills of ecstasy" — *subipula pulaka-kula-balita*. "Ceaselessly" — *niratā*. "A mighty wind" — *pracuratara bāta*. "Rows of creepers" — *latāli*. "Neither composure nor steadiness" — *dhṛti na dhiratā*. "The present dwellers of Navadvipa" — *naba nabadbīpa-bāsī jana*: *naba*, literally "new"; taken as "present" (living in Navadvipa during Gaura's lila); a sound play with *nabadbīpa* is also possible. "Their birth is fulfilled" — *janama sāphalya*. "With their mouths" — *bayane*. "The lovely radiance of Gaura" — *gaura-ruci rucira*. "Revile the Creator" — *bihi prati deta gāri*: Brahma, for not letting them be born in Navadvipa. "They offer themselves" — *tanu nichai*: see note 4 of kirana 12. "The heart longs for a touch" — *cita cāha paraśe*: from the context (line 2: they bow at His feet), probably the touch of His feet; the text does not specify. Here a new kind of the mood begins: the naga women, seeing Gaura, are filled with prema for Him. Gaudiya Vaishnavas, following Shrila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati and the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Adi 15: in His lila the Lord did not jest with women and did not even look at them), reject gaura-nagari-vada — the worship of Gaura as an enjoying lover (*nāgara*) and of oneself as His nagari: Gaura is Krishna in the mood of Radha, tasting Her love, not an enjoyer. Cf. the main notes of kiranas 11 (note 2), 12 (note 10), 14 (note 2) and 15 (note 5). Further notes in this kirana refer to this note. Signature: "the Lord of servant Narahari" (*dāsa narahari pahũka*).
