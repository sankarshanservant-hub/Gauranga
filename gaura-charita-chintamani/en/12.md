# Kirana 12. The Deeds of the Morning: The Description of the Anuraga and So On of the Hosts of Gods[^1]

*Morning: the hosts of gods, motionless in the sky above Shachi's courtyard, behold Gaura amid His associates; they tell one another of the love of the people of Nadiya for Him and of His childhood plays, glorify His beauty — the union of Gauri and Shyama — dancing, sing garlands of His names, and at last, taking human form, descend to earth*

*Again, in the morning period — the anuraga and so on of the hosts of gods.*

*Raga bhairava*

**12.1.** Today night has turned to dawn — Shachi's courtyard shines, perfectly pure, wondrously lovely.
There the beautiful Gaurachanda sports, surrounded by His associates, the Remover of others' sorrow.
Who can describe this delight? There is no end to it.
The hosts of gods with their retinues, on the path of the sky, motionless, are immersed in boundless happiness. *(Refrain)*
The charming face that subdues the pride of hundreds of millions of Madanas, the nectarean sweet smile.
They gaze — their eyes do not blink, their thoughts tremble, in their hearts ever more rejoicing.
They deem [their lives] fulfilled, they blossom [with joy]; again and again a strong trembling, a fresh thrill of rapture in lovely rows.
Intoxicated with the highest rasa, they worship the pair of lotuses — the hands and feet of Narahari's Lord.[^2]

*Keshari-chhandah*

**12.2.** The hosts of gods, absorbed [in bliss], beautiful, glitter in the midst of the sky;
throng upon throng, in heart-delighting circles, they sway, brimming with rasa.
From their eyes, as from the clouds of the month of Shravana, tears of prema pour unceasingly;
in incomparable rejoicing they again speak sweet words to one another.[^3]

*Someone says — raga lalita*

**12.3.** "Look: the supreme, incomparable mystery — in Mishra's house!
In the lovely morning hour, cries of 'Glory, glory!' resound there.
Seeing Gaura's face, the people rejoice and cannot keep their composure;
in dense thrills of rapture, trembling ceaselessly, they shed streams of tears from their eyes.
Carried away by the stream of the nectar of [His] beauty, they give their very lives for Him;
ceaselessly telling of His charming virtues, lost to themselves, they speak of nothing else.
Some come and go, but they have no strength to stay at home;
all surround on all four sides the Lord of Narahari, His servant."[^4]

*And another*

**12.4.** "Who can tell of the incomparable love of the men and women of the town of Nadiya?
Gaurahari is their life and their all; without Him they have not the least composure.
The old men, most eager, come taking staffs in their hands;
wishing [Him] well, their hearts overflowing, on seeing [Him] they become like young men.
The women full of parental love perform various auspicious rites with great tenderness;
having gazed [at Him] to their eyes' content, walking around [Him] many times, they give abundant blessings.
Never do they think that He is the Supreme Lord; they remember nothing else — incomparable [is their] way.
Constantly they speak to one another of the childhood plays of Narahari's Lord."[^5]

*And another — raga vibhasha*

**12.5.** "Many lovely, wondrous deeds were hidden in this age;
He reveals them, ever compassionate — He keeps nothing secret.
The devotees constantly sing His glory — who, hearing it, would not be filled with anuraga?
Knowing Him as their own chosen Deity, all the virtuous cling to His feet.
Whatever feeling has risen in anyone's heart, they never give it up;
they guard it as a miser guards his wealth, and cannot forget it even for a moment.
In the Lord of Narahari, His servant, this avatara has become full of rasa;
now this intoxicating form, entering the heart, has taken away everything."[^6]

*And another — raga vibhasha*

**12.6.** "The complexion of Gaurasundara, which steals people's minds — I have grasped [its secret]:
in it is the union of Gauri and Shyama; that is why it has become so intoxicating.
In the world there are many various comparisons — none of them ever fits it.
It is ever new and new — who can describe [it]? There is no such poet.
Trying to convey even a drop of [its] lovely sweetness, I rejoice at heart,
but all the many comparisons, on beholding it, are helpless — they show only a little.
He whose fortune is great, great, is immersed in this beauty;
Narahari, His servant, filling his eyes again and again, has drunk in the beauty of such a Lord."[^7]

*And another — raga lalita*

**12.7.** "Amid His associates the youthful Gaura shines in incomparable attire;
His royal splendour surpasses crushed kunkuma, gold and heaps of champakas.
His beautiful curling hair gleams — glossy, with the sheen of kajal;
the orb of His face is spotless and radiant; His teeth are a row of pearls.
Lotus eyes, broad brows, ears, nose, soft cheeks;
a lion's neck, fresh grace, incomparable, most lovely arms;
broad chest, slender waist, knees — [all] steal [the hearts of] the sages;
by the rays of the toenails of the Lord of Narahari, His servant, the world is illumined."[^8]

*Ramanaka-chhandah*

**12.8.** Speaking thus, the hosts of gods
are greatly intoxicated — how much rises in their hearts!
Gracefully moving their arms, they dance
and sing a sweet garland of names:[^9]

*Someone says — raga karnata*

**12.9.** "Glory [to You], O Vishvambhara, Krishna, son of Shachi, golden of form, full of compassion!
O Madhava, Crusher of Mura, Giver of honour, Increaser of joy, Abode of all rasas!
O Plunderer of the bodices on the breasts of the hosts of gopis, whose deeds are lovely, Delighter of the people!
O You whose feet are caressed by Kamala's hands, tender as new shoots, Destroyer of the mighty threefold miseries!
O Keshava, Slayer of Kamsa, Fulfiller of desires, King of the brahmanas, Remover of fear!
O You who are eager for sports and plays on the bank of Jahnu's daughter, revered by the universe, of auspicious form!
O Holder of the mountain, praised by Dharanidhara, new Dancer, rare for the three worlds!
O You who are skilled in giving the treasure — the jewel of prema, Ocean of virtues, Beloved of Narahari!"[^10]

*And another — raga deshapala*

**12.10.** "Glory, glory [to You], O Shri Jagadisha, son of Shachi, beautiful youthful Gaura, ocean of rasa,
O Keshava, [word unclear], kajal for the eyes of the daughters of the cowherds of Gokula!
Remover of the burden of the age of Kali, incomparable Preacher of prema, lovely of body,
most Complete One, Moon of the line of the twice-born, Dispeller of the darkness of a mass of afflictions!
Skilled in unprecedented dance, Ocean of virtues, Nagara, Increaser of the joy of the rasikas, Madhava,
devoted to playful quarrels, Fulfiller of desires, Beloved, You who put [the beauty of] Kama to shame!
Ornament of the bank of Jahnu's daughter, Krishna, foremost in water-sports in the daughter of Mount Kalinda,
lovely One, Lord of Narahari, Delighter of the people of the world!"[^11]

*Raga vibhasha*

**12.11.** The incomparable, sweetest name "Gaura", surpassing [even] the fullness of the intoxication of nectar,
the hosts of gods in the sky repeat again and again; water streams from their eyes.
Descending to the earth, stirred, they at once took on human form;
no one can recognize them — they enter the precincts of [His] abode.
Staying near the skilled associates, at the sight of the Lord they are full of sweet delight;
offering body and mind, joining their palms, how many longings they cherish!
Counting [this] their good fortune, they spread out in obeisance at the feet of the Lord and [His] associates.
The servant Narahari gives himself up to this anuraga [and exclaims]: "I offer myself, I offer myself!"[^12]

*Thus [ends] the twelfth kirana of the Shrimad Gaura-charita-chintamani, called "The Description of the Anuraga and So On of the Hosts of Gods", in the description of the deeds of the morning period.*[^13]
