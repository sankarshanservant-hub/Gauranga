# Vilasa 8. The Prema Left for Narottama

*The glory of sankirtana; Mahaprabhu turns back from Kanai Natashala; in kirtana he calls Narottama; his talk with Nityananda about Garerhat; Mahaprabhu entrusts prema to the river Padmavati for the future Narottama*

**1.** Glory, glory to Sri Chaitanya, glory to the Merciful One!
Glory, glory to Nityananda, the shelter of devotees!

**2.** Glory, glory to Vishvambhara, the embodiment of compassion!
Glory, glory to Advaita-chandra, grace to the world!

**3.** Glory, glory to Virachandra, the ocean of prema!
Glory, glory to Gaura's devotees, crest-jewels of the connoisseurs of rasa!

**4.** Glory, glory to Gauranga with the host of his devotees!
Whoever hears the story of Chaitanya obtains bhakti.

**5.** Listen, listen, O hearers, attentively;
hear with faith a tale of prema.

**6.** The ornament of Gauda is the great sankirtana:
at the mere hearing prema arises — this I say firmly.

**7.** Hari-nama-sankirtana is this great power;
in the Kali age there is nothing else — all else is false.

**8.** From a single name of Hari comes every perfection;
by sankirtana prema is born in one's body.

**9.** In the body of one who has no attraction to Hari's name,
there is no prema — it flies off who knows where.

**10.** Whoever has the desire to attain Krishna,
let him take Hari's name with great joy.

**11.** Whatever one's attachment, let all take Hari's name;
taking the Name by count fulfils the heart's desire.

**12.** Now hear the account of Narottama's birth;
hearing it, you will find joy, and your heart will turn to kirtana.

**13.** The Prabhu was to go from Gauda to Vrindavana,
but he did not go to Vrindavana — he turned back from Kanai Natashala.

**14.** That story is told at length in earlier writings;[^1]
[here] I only describe the virtues of Narottama.

**15.** At the ghat of Tartipur[^2] he crossed the Padma,
and seeing the beauty of the Padmavati he rejoiced.

**16.** Clasping Nityananda round the neck, he sat down right there:
"I shall not go to Vrindavana; I shall stay here."

**17.** Hearing the Prabhu, Nityananda laughed:
"Leaving Navadvipa, you took sannyasa,

**18.** and now you have conceived the intention [to live] on the bank of the Padmavati!"
"Good, good," said the Sripada,[^3] and began to laugh.

**19.** The Prabhu said: "Sripada, pay attention:
where one sits happily, that is one's birthplace.

**20.** The reason for which I left Nilachala —
when you meet him, you will hear it all."[^4]

**21.** The Prabhu said: "That is the truth, this is no lie;
I shall explain in detail, listen if you wish.

**22.** When I am together with Sanatana and Rupa,
then all the Acharyas will hear all of this."

**23.** "Good, good," and the Prabhu rose quickly
and came to a village near Gauda,

**24.** named Chaturpura, not far [from there];
there was a meeting with Sanatana, full of prema.

**25.** Having accomplished the purpose of that meeting,
from there he went on and arrived at Natashala.

**26.** Hearing the village's name, "Krishna's Natashala,"
prema surged in his body, mistaking it for Vrindavana.

**27.** Nityananda said: "Prabhu, ever since you left the Padmavati,
you have had a mind to live on a river bank."[^5]

**28.** Listen, listen, O hearers, attentively:
the Prabhu's intentions cannot be understood at all.

**29.** One day Mahaprabhu, dancing in kirtana,
suddenly called out the name "Narottama!"

**30.** Leaning his whole body against Nityananda,
he wept, and hundreds of streams flowed from his eyes.

**31.** Seeing these transformations of prema, [Nityananda] reflected
and, seized with fear, stopped the kirtana.

**32.** Surrounding the Prabhu, all the kirtaniyas
sing Krishna's name in sweet voices.

**33.** "Sing, sing!" cried the Prabhu, and fell to the ground,
and Nityananda Prabhu could no longer hold him.

**34.** Crying "Mathura, Mathura!" he weeps;
hearing it, the devotees' hearts break.

**35.** At the name of Mathura he lost all sense of direction;
the village of Natashala quaked with prema.

**36.** The Prabhu weeps aloud, calling "Mathura!";
Nityananda sat down, holding the Prabhu round the neck.

**37.** Everyone cried out, a great tumult arose;
the housewives and others came to see.

**38.** Crying "Mathura, Mathura!" he rolls on the ground;
the Prabhu's golden body lies sprawled on the earth.

**39.** Seeing the Prabhu's beauty, the sweetness of prema,
men and women gaze at his form without blinking.

**40.** He roars "hum, hum," saying "Mathura!";
the Prabhu became intoxicated with prema like a mad baul.

**41.** "Where is Radha? Radha!" he says again and again,
"My body burns when I do not see you!

**42.** Where are Lalita and Vishakha, where is Champakalata?
Ah, show me — where is my friend, dear as life?

**43.** Show yourself and save my life — why do you give me sorrow?
I shall throw myself into the Yamuna; I cannot go on living.

**44.** Come quickly, friend Lalita, let us go to Madhupuri;
why can I no longer see the lord of my life?"

**45.** Seeing him distraught, the Prabhu[^6] sat holding him
and began to think: "What am I to do, what will happen?

**46.** Let us go — why did we come to the village of Natashala?
We have lost Gorachand!" he thinks to himself.

**47.** The Sripada found a means in sankirtana:
he loudly raised the cry "Jagannatha!"

**48.** At the name of Jagannatha the Prabhu regained consciousness
and for a while wandered here and there.

**49.** Calling "Narottama!", the Prabhu weeps unceasingly;
he looks in every direction, but does not see Narottama.

**50.** All say: "Let us take the Prabhu to Nilachala;
then all my good fortune will be complete.

**51.** If somehow we can turn the Prabhu's mind,
once we have crossed the Padmavati we shall have everything."

**52.** At that moment he again calls out "Narottama!"
[They thought:] "It seems some best of bhagavatas is to come."

**53.** Clasping the Sripada, the Prabhu embraced him,
and Nitai's body was drenched with tears.

**54.** With effort they calmed the Prabhu,
and, knowing the time had come, Nityananda too grew composed.

**55.** Nityananda said: "Prabhu, I beg of you:
there is great need to go to Jagannatha.

**56.** Krishna forbade [this road] through Sanatana's mouth:
he told of the crowds of people and the dangers of the way."

**57.** "Whatever you think best, do that;
whatever you do will be — I am not my own master."

**58.** The Prabhu said: "Sripada, listen attentively:
there is a reason why we came to Natashala."

**59.** "What business is there, Prabhu? Tell me, let me hear;
if it seems right to me, I shall take [you] — then I shall agree."

**60.** Nityananda said: "Prabhu, I beg of you:
in the sankirtana you remembered Narottama.

**61.** So shall I take you, or shall I not go with you?[^7]
I have no strength to hold you in the waves of feeling.

**62.** Seeing the pain of separation, I cannot bear to look;
I think: better that I should die this very moment."

**63.** The Prabhu said: "Garerhat is a most delightful place;
as soon as you see it, you will want to stay.

**64.** Listen, listen, Sripada, I shall explain in detail:
I wish to preserve here the sankirtana, the treasure of my life.

**65.** In Navadvipa the sankirtana was made manifest,
but I have left Gauda and live in Nilachala.

**66.** From now on I wish to preserve the sankirtana:
I shall lay up prema at Garerhat — so I tell you."

**67.** "How will you keep prema at Garerhat, Prabhu?
Who is the vessel to whom you will give such prema?"

**68.** The Prabhu said: "As long as you are present [in the world],
so long my prema will not disappear.

**69.** If this prema is kept with a worthy vessel, it remains;
if it falls on an unworthy one, prema is wasted.

**70.** The earth, drunk with prema, does not know proper place from improper;
give prema to such a person that all may drink of it.

**71.** So come, brother, let us go to Garerhat:
giving prema to such a person, [I] shall make the ghats and the roads weep."

**72.** "Good, good," said the Sripada, and fell silent,
and began to think how such a vessel would be born.

**73.** The Prabhu said: "Sripada, I see you are pondering;
you yourself do not know your own virtues.

**74.** All that you wept on the way to Nilachala —
that prema I have gathered and bound up day by day.

**75.** That prema I shall lay up on the bank of the Padmavati
and give it to a vessel named Narottama.

**76.** His birth will be through prema while I am still [in the world];
for now I shall leave it in the keeping of the Padmavati."

**77.** Nityananda said: "Prabhu, where is Garerhat?
Take me with you and go there."

**78.** "Both banks of the Padmavati are very cool,
and between them the Padmavati flows in a pure stream."

**79.** Hearing this, Nityananda's heart rejoiced:
"Make haste, Prabhu, and go there."

**80.** On the pretext of [the journey to] Vrindavana he came to Garerhat;
thus he turned back from Natashala.

**81.** Holding Nityananda's hand, laughing and laughing,
he began to speak, seeing the beauty of the Padmavati.

**82.** So he came to the village of Kudodarapura,[^8]
and, seeing the beauty of the bank, was full of joy.

**83.** There he stayed, in talk about Krishna,
and at dawn the Prabhu went to the Padmavati.

**84.** Having bathed, the Prabhu began kirtana on the bank;
with roars of "hum, hum," overflowing with prema, he shook violently.

**85.** How shall I behold that prema, what is its meaning?
A thousand people were not able to hold him.

**86.** At that moment he cried out "Narottama!"
The Sripada said: "[He is] stealing the play of prema.[^9]

**87.** Listen, listen, devotees, be on your guard:
at this moment, I surmise, he is going to take the prema."

**88.** At Nityananda's words the devotees were startled,
and Nityananda stopped the kirtana.

**89.** With the kirtaniyas the Prabhu began his bath,
and the Padmavati, drunk with prema, began to rise.

**90.** At the touch of the Prabhu's body the current stood still,
and all the water was filled with prema.

**91.** Rising and rising, the water flooded the village;
we understood that in this way he flooded [it] with prema.

**92.** Houses and homesteads floated away; there was an uproar in the village;
"This is not the rains," but no one could understand.

**93.** The Sripada said: "Prabhu, keep the prema properly;
the village is being laid waste — I have never seen the like."

**94.** The Prabhu said: "Padmavati, hold it, take the prema;
give this prema to a vessel named Narottama.

**95.** In Nityananda's presence I have placed prema with you;
keep it carefully and in secret."

**96.** The Padmavati said: "Prabhu, I beg of you:
how shall I know who is named Narottama?"

**97.** "At whose touch you overflow the more,
he is Narottama — give him the prema."

**98.** The Prabhu said: "All is as you have said;
keep the prema at this ghat — I command it."

**99.** The joyful Padmavati kept it by the bank:
in seclusion she hid the prema at a secluded ghat.

**100.** The Prabhu stood up to take leave of the Padmavati,
and with Nityananda gazed in that direction.

**101.** The current moved on, flooding [the nearby] villages,[^10]
then let go of the water, and the people rejoiced.

**102.** The Sripada said: "Prabhu, seeing such beauty,
I too have been tempted to stay here.

**103.** You will cause Narottama to be born and give him prema;
I think you will [abide] near Narottama."[^11]

**104.** The Prabhu said: "Sripada, it is as you have said:
near Narottama alone do I abide."

**105.** Then the Prabhu crossed the Padmavati,
and, going on step by step, the Prabhu came to Nilachala.

**106.** All say: "What sort of thing is this prema?
Who would call dancing and singing prema?

**107.** Weeping and falling down too we do not call prema;
who can describe it, who has the capacity?"

**108.** In the form of prema is Krishna's very self;
this Sri Rupa himself has explained.

**109.** I write only a particle of it, that it may be known;
by the strength of the Prabhu's command I write this with my hand.

**110.** […][^12] —
and how many more tales there are of her virtues!

**111.** Thus I have told the sweetness of Garerhat;
with great longing I shall tell of the prema of kirtana.

**112.** The Vaishnava who hears of this prema with faith
will soon obtain this treasure of prema.[^13]

**113.** He whose hope is the feet of Sri Jahnava and Virachandra,
Nityananda Dasa, tells the Prema-vilasa.

*Thus ends the eighth vilasa of the Prema-vilasa.*

[^1]: Cf. the Chaitanya-bhagavata and the Chaitanya-charitamrita, which describe how Mahaprabhu turned back from Kanai Natashala.
[^2]: Both editions have "tartibapura" (Tartipur); the reading of the name is uncertain.
[^3]: Sripada, a respectful title of Nityananda.
[^4]: The sense is unclear; perhaps Narottama or Sanatana is meant (cf. couplet 24).
[^5]: This line is found only in the 1913 edition; the second line of the couplet is lost in the 1999 edition.
[^6]: Nityananda.
[^7]: Variant: "So tell him that I shall not go with you."
[^8]: Variant: "Kutubpur".
[^9]: The sense of the remark is unclear.
[^10]: The line is corrupt ("srota calila … jākti-/jābhigrāma chāilā"); it may contain a village name.
[^11]: Variant: "why, I wonder, will you [abide] near Narottama?"
[^12]: The first line of the couplet ("nava-putra deva rati kanyā tāra mātā" in the 1913 edition) is unclear; perhaps Narottama's kin (son, daughter, mother) are listed.
[^13]: This couplet is found only in the 1913 edition.
