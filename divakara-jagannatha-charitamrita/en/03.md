# Divakara Dasa. *Jagannatha-charitamrita*, Chapter 3
## Jagannatha Dasa Serves Sri Chaitanya; Mahaprabhu Asks about His Former Form

*Translated from Odia without a native speaker; doubtful places are marked (?) and (??). The original, transliteration and word-for-word translation are in `full/03.md`.*[^intro-3-1]

*Chapter Three*

**3.1.** Listen attentively, kinsfolk, to the deeds of Atibada Gosai.  
**3.2.** Thus some days passed. And that Jagannatha Dasa,  
**3.3.** day by day, with ever-new love, served Sri Chaitanya with devotion.[^seva-3-3]  
**3.4.** Rising at dawn (?), [he offered Him] full prostration, and gave [Him water] to wash His holy face;  
**3.5.** he cleaned the bed for sleeping (?); [he gave] clay to cleanse the holy hands;  
**3.6.** the tooth-stick for cleaning the teeth; the bath, the rubbing down, drinking water;  
**3.7.** sprinkling the holy body; putting on new cloth;  
**3.8.** the daily duties of sannyasa; cleaning the staff and kamandalu.  
**3.9.** All such tasks — service to Prabhu — were [his] daily duty;  
**3.10.** [also] the darshan of Jagannatha, and the routine of serving [Prabhu] in [His] retinue.  
**3.11.** Seeing this service, Chaitanya put a question to Dasa:  
**3.12.** "O Dasa, seeing your service, We have become very happy at heart.  
**3.13.** About your former divine form — tell Me that story."[^purva-3-13]  
**3.14.** Hearing this command, Dasa was amazed and rejoiced.  
**3.15.** That (?) Lord is the embodiment of bhakti, the lamp (?) … (??) of the Kali age.[^pradipa-3-15]  
**3.16.** "[You are] the Purusha, the Lord of all (?); nothing is hidden from You.  

## Jagannatha Dasa's Account of His Former Form; Mahaprabhu Calls Him Atibada; the Vaishnavas Murmur


*Shloka*  
**3.17.** *[Skt.]* "The hidden (?) supreme abode (?) of Vishnu is the glorious, wondrous Vaikuntha; it spreads out [like] a lotus with its pericarp — this is declared a secret.[^shloka-3-17][^intro-3-17]  
**3.18.** Above it, on a golden throne, adorned with a jewelled pavilion, in the pericarp is the great abode where Govinda dwells (?), great and imperishable.  
**3.19.** Its southern petal is called the most secret of secrets, the highest. Then [He] entered the rasa on the great throne, which is hard to reach.  
**3.20.** In its midst dwells Krishna, intent on embracing Radha; with one self, with mind and intellect, He manifests (?) the eternal lila."  
**3.21.** "In eternal Vaikuntha, [His] own abode, Bhagavan [was] together … (??) [with Radha].[^rabe-3-21]  
**3.22.** [They] abode as one self, and with great happiness cast a glance [at each other].  
**3.23.** Seeing Radha's face, the Life of the world laughed.[^hasa-3-23]  
**3.24.** That laughter by which crores upon crores of worlds are born in a single moment —  
**3.25.** from that laughter You appeared (lit. "were born"), and You stand in front of the Lord.  
**3.26.** From Radha's laughter I was born, and I stand behind You.  
**3.27.** Looking [at You], [the Lord] gave You the command: 'Becoming incarnate in the mortal world —  
**3.28.** this age of Kali is mighty (?) — perform the kirtan of Hari's Name.'  
**3.29.** You make the unconscious conscious; therefore Your name is Chaitanya.[^caitanya-3-29]  
**3.30.** Then Sri Krishna, looking at me, said: 'Be born on earth.[^mahi-3-30]  
**3.31.** You will worship this form, and at the end you will attain that same goal.'  
**3.32.** Therefore, apart from the love of the Couple, my mind finds taste in nothing else."  
**3.33.** Hearing this, Sri Chaitanya became absorbed in meditation (?) on His own abode.[^dhyana-3-33]  
**3.34.** Attaining recognition (?) of the former form, the Gosai rejoiced.  
**3.35.** He removed His own wrap (??) from His sacred body with His sacred hand[^pacheri-3-35]  
**3.36.** and tied it on Dasa's head, saying: "[You are] very great (ati bada)!  
**3.37.** You have told this most rare account today before Me.  

*Shloka*  
**3.38.** *[Skt.]* "Hard to attain even for yogis, true, accessible [only] to the Purusha (?)."[^yogi-3-38]  
**3.39.** You have told a very great account; therefore you have become Atibada."  
**3.40.** [Since then] the world calls Dasa "Sri Atibada Jagannatha":  
**3.41.** having heard it from Mahaprabhu's mouth, everyone speaks this word.  
**3.42.** Thus some days passed. Sri Chaitanya in Purushottama  
**3.43.** every day, with an affectionate heart, constantly calls [him] Atibada.  
**3.44.** When [He] called [him] Atibada, the Vaishnavas grieved at heart.[^vaishnava-3-44]  
**3.45.** Looking at the Odia brahmana, [they] said: "You (?) are Atibada!"  
**3.46.** "Until today we have served, and all of us have fallen to a lower position.  
**3.47.** If we stay with Him, we shall hear nothing but such reproach."  
**3.48.** He indeed is the Lord over all (?); He accomplishes everything [by a mere] word.  

*Shloka*  
**3.49.** *[Skt.]* "The Maker, the maker of the two makers, the cause of the cause of makers; God is all-pervading — what then can I think of Him?"[^karta-3-49]  
**3.50.** Such is the glory of the Lord that there is no gainsaying it (?):  
**3.51.** what is done He makes undone, what is not done He does;  
**3.52.** at times He establishes what is done, at times He does not do what is not done;  
**3.53.** at times He delivers great sinners, at times He destroys the pious.  
**3.54.** Since He abides in all bodies, He is called the greatest of all.  
**3.55.** Every day Sri Chaitanya calls [Dasa] by the name Atibada.  
**3.56.** Thus some days passed; the name "Atibada" took hold (?).[^katila-3-56]  
**3.57.** Unable to bear [it], the devotees went and reported [it] before the Prabhu.  

[^intro-3-1]: Beginning of Chapter 3: PDF page 15 (printed p. 14). The end of Chapter 2 (2.167–2.171) and its colophon are on the same page (files `ch02/DD-02-142-171-*`). The heading "ତୃତୀୟ ଅଧ୍ୟାୟ" is separated from the colophon by a printer's ornament. The first line of the chapter is indented, like the first line of Chapter 2. On Divakara Dasa, the Atibadi line and the Gaudiya Vaishnava position see Ch. 1, the note to 1.1. Features of the type are as in Chapter 2 (notes to 2.17, 2.58 and 2.142). On this page many signs are filled with ink: the first signs of 3.5 (ଶୟନ), 3.15 (ସେ), 3.16 (ସର୍ବଙ୍କ, ଈଶ୍ୱର). The distinction between ଅ and ଆ is unreliable here (ଆଗେ 3.13, ଆଜ୍ଞା 3.14, ଅଗୋଚର 3.16 are given from the sense). The sign ି is often printed as a stroke inside the letter (ଚିତ୍ତ, ଦିନ, ପ୍ରୀତି, ବିଧାନ, ନିତ୍ୟ, ପରିଧାନ, ମୃତିକା). The ligature ରା resembles ସ୍ୱ (କରାଇ 3.4). "Kinsfolk" (*swajane*, 3.1) addresses listeners of the same faith; ସ୍ୱ and ରା look alike here, but ରାଜନେ makes no sense. "Atibada Gosai" is Jagannatha Dasa; cf. the colophon of Chapter 2 ("Jagannatha Gosai") and VL 5 (the name "Atibada").
[^seva-3-3]: 3.3–3.10 present Jagannatha Dasa as Mahaprabhu's personal servant. He carries out the Lord's morning routine: the prostration, washing the face, cleaning the bed, clay for the hands, the tooth-stick, the bath and change of cloth, cleaning the sannyasi's staff and kamandalu. He also accompanies the Lord to the darshan of Jagannatha. According to the CC, Mahaprabhu's personal servant in Puri was Govinda, together with Kashishvara (CC, Madhya 10; cf. MP 1.95 and the note to it). Jagannatha Dasa is not mentioned in the CC or CB (cf. the note to VL 9.63). ପ୍ରଭାତୁଁ (*prabhātu̐*, "from dawn", 3.4) is a tentative reading: the second sign is filled with ink and resembles ଭା/ସ୍ୱ. ମୃତିକା (*mṛtikā*, 3.5) = *mṛttikā*, "clay". *Karāi … dhaüta* (3.4) is understood as "giving [the Lord water] to wash the face". ଗହଣ ଖଟିବା (*gahaṇa khaṭibā*, 3.10) means "to serve in the retinue, to attend".
[^purva-3-13]: The "former divine form" (*pūrba dibya aṅga*) is apparently a question about who Jagannatha Dasa was in Krishna's eternal pastimes. The answer apparently follows on the next page. Cf. 1.27 and 1.75 (he is a part of Radha) and 1.61, 1.68 (he was born from Radha's laughter). Establishing Jagannatha Dasa's pre-eminence is a running theme of the Atibadi line; for the Gaudiya Vaishnava position see the note to 2.22. Mahaprabhu speaks of Himself as "We" (*hoilu̐*, 1st pers. pl., 3.12), the honorific plural.
[^pradipa-3-15]: The reading and sense of 3.15 are unclear. The first sign is filled with ink and taken as ସେ, "that". If so, 3.15 is the author's description of Mahaprabhu, and Jagannatha Dasa's speech begins in 3.16 ("nothing is hidden from You"). Another possibility is that Dasa already begins to answer in 3.15. The word ଅଙ୍ଗ (*aṅga*, "body, limb") is unclear in sense; ଅନ୍ଧ (*andha*) would be expected: "a lamp for the blind of the Kali age". ପ୍ରଦୀପ (*pradīpa*, "lamp") is read from the indistinct signs ପ୍ର + ଦୀ + ପ. ସର୍ବଙ୍କ (*sarbaṅka*, "of all", 3.16): the third sign is smudged. Gaudiya Vaishnavas also worship Mahaprabhu as the Supreme Lord, Krishna Himself (CC, Adi 1–2). The speech continues on the next page.
[^intro-3-17]: Continuation of Chapter 3: PDF pages 16–17 (printed pp. 15–16). 3.17–3.32 are Jagannatha Dasa's answer to Mahaprabhu's question about his "former divine form" (3.13). The shlokas 3.17–3.20 open the answer. The couplets 3.21–3.32 are his speech ("You" is Mahaprabhu). In 3.33–3.39 Mahaprabhu speaks. Features of the type on these pages: on many letters the sign ି is printed as a stroke or small tail inside or below the letter, not as an arc above. Read thus are ବି (ବିଷ୍ଣୁ, ବିସ୍ତାରଂ, ବିନେ), ତି (ଅତିବଡ଼, ପ୍ରତି), ଦି (ଦିନ, ଦିବସ), ଚି (ଚିତ୍ତେ, ଚିନ୍ତୟାମି), ରି (ଚରିତ, ପାରି), ହି (ସହି, ମହିମା), ନି (ନିତ୍ୟ), ଳି (କଳି). Such ି are given without brackets. After ର and ଚ the sign ା merges into a tail, so ରା and ଚା look like ସ୍ୱ (ରାଧା, ରାସେ, କରାଅ, ଚାହିଁ, ଚାଇ). ଅ and ଆ are poorly distinguished. The narrow sign without a flag in ଅଜ୍ଞା (3.27) and ଅଜ (3.37, 3.46) is given with the correction [ଆଜ୍ଞା], [ଆଜ], as in 2.138. The wide sign in ଆପଣା (3.35) is read as ଆ. The shloka lines end in a single "।" in the print; the "॥" at the end of each shloka is ours.
[^shloka-3-17]: 3.17–3.20 are eight lines (four shlokas in anushtubh metre) after the mark "Shloka". The supreme abode is described as a lotus: in the pericarp, a golden throne under a jewelled pavilion and Govinda's abode; the southern petal is the most secret; in the centre Krishna, embraced by Radha, manifests the eternal lila. The source has not been found. The imagery is close to *Brahma-samhita* 5.2–5.5: Gokula is a thousand-petalled lotus, and its pericarp is Krishna's abode. The reading of the first words of 3.17 is uncertain. ବିଷ୍ଣୁ: the first sign is ବ with a lower stroke, as in ବିସ୍ତାରଂ. ଛଦ୍ମ: the first sign is round with a ring below, like ଛ in ପଛରେ (3.26); the OCR gives ବଞ୍ଚ ର୍ଷ. ପରଧାନ: the last sign is ନ, not ମ; taken as *para-dhāma*, "supreme abode" (or *pradhāna*, "chief"). ମଦ୍ଭୁତଂ = *-m adbhutam*. In 3.18 the signs ସ୍ଥ and ୟ look alike (ଗୋବିନ୍ଦସ୍ଥଂ, ମହାବ୍ୟୟଂ); ପ୍ରକାଶୟେତ୍ (3.20) is a tentative reading. As in 1.62, Divakara places Radha and Krishna in Vaikuntha (see the note to 1.62). For Gaudiya Vaishnavas, Goloka-Vrindavana is above Vaikuntha (CC, Adi 5).
[^rabe-3-21]: Printed ରାବେ (*rābe*): the first sign is ରା or ଚା with a tail (see the note to 3.17), the second a clear ବେ. No word with this meaning exists. Possibly a misprint for ରାଧେ (*rādhe*, "with Radha"). ଭାବେ (*bhābe*, "in bhava") is also possible, but ଭା in the next line (ଭାବରେଣ, 3.22) looks different. The translation follows the context; cf. 1.62–1.63: "Sri Radha and Krishna on one seat… abide in a single bhava".
[^hasa-3-23]: 3.23–3.30 repeat the account of 1.61–1.75, but here it is put in the mouth of Jagannatha Dasa himself, as in VL 5.113–5.121. Sri Chaitanya appeared from Krishna's laughter (3.24–3.25), Jagannatha Dasa of Radha's laughter (3.26); both are sent to earth (3.27, 3.30). On this Atibadi teaching and the Gaudiya Vaishnava position see the notes to 1.27 and 1.61 and to VL 5.117. For Gaudiya Vaishnavas, Sri Chaitanya is Krishna Himself, who has taken Radha's mood and complexion (CC, Adi 1.5; Adi 4). He descends Himself to give the Name (CC, Adi 3); He is not born of Krishna's laughter. "The Lord" (*prabhu*) in 3.25 is Krishna. "In front of the Lord… behind You" (3.25–3.26) apparently describes their order in the eternal abode.
[^caitanya-3-29]: An explanation of the name: "You make the unconscious (*aceta*) conscious (*caitanya*)". According to CB (Madhya 28), the name "Krishna-Chaitanya" was given to the Lord at His sannyasa by Keshava Bharati: He awakens Krishna consciousness in the world.
[^mahi-3-30]: Krishna's command to Jagannatha Dasa to be born on earth: cf. 1.75 and the note there (where ତୁହି / ମହୀ also occur). "This form" (*ehi swarūpa*, 3.31) is apparently the Couple, Radha and Krishna (cf. 3.32). Gaudiya Vaishnavas do not accept the teaching that Jagannatha Dasa is a portion of Radha (note to 1.27).
[^dhyana-3-33]: Printed ଧାନ (*dhāna*). Taken as *dhyāna*, "meditation"; the ୍ୟ is not visible. ଧାମରୁ (*dhāmaru*) literally means "from the abode"; translated "[meditation] on His own abode". ଚିହ୍ନାଙ୍ଗ (*cihnāṅga*, 3.34; printed ଚି + ହ୍ନ + ା + ଙ୍ଗ) is understood tentatively as "recognition of the form" (*cihnā*, "recognition", + *aṅga*). The sense of 3.33–3.34: in meditation Mahaprabhu recognizes His former form and confirms Dasa's account. Cf. 3.16: "for You nothing is hidden".
[^pacheri-3-35]: The word has not been identified. It is printed ପା + େ + a dark sign like ଛ (cf. ପଛରେ, 3.26) + ଡ଼; no ି is visible. Tentatively ପାଛେଡ଼ି (*pācheṛi*), "wrap, shawl" (cf. Beng. *pāchṛā*, "cloth"); the rhyme with କାଢ଼ି supports this. The OCR gives ପାଶ୍ମେଡ଼. In the parallel VL 5.122 the Lord ties His uttariya on the Swami's head (see the note there). The episode of the title "Atibada" (3.35–3.41): cf. VL 5.119–5.129. It is absent from CC and CB. See the notes to 1.20 and to VL 5.119.
[^yogi-3-38]: A single line (half-shloka); the source has not been found. *Puruṣa-gocaram* is taken as "accessible [only] to the Purusha", i.e. to the Lord; cf. 3.16. It apparently refers to Dasa's account, the secret of his origin. 3.39 is almost word for word VL 5.121 and 5.129: "you have told a very great account; from today you have become Atibada". A pun: *atibaṛa kathā*, "a very great account", and *atibaṛa*, "very great".
[^vaishnava-3-44]: The "Vaishnavas" (3.44) and the "devotees" (3.57) are apparently Mahaprabhu's companions, the Gaudiyas. In VL 5.124–5.128, by contrast, they follow Svarupa and accept the form of address "Atibada". In Divakara they murmur and complain that they "have fallen to a lower position" (3.46): the author thus stresses Jagannatha Dasa's superiority over the Gaudiyas. Gaudiya Vaishnavas see Mahaprabhu's companions (Svarupa Damodara, Ramananda Raya and others) as His eternal associates, free from envy. A claim to the title "very great" contradicts the Lord's instruction to be "humbler than a blade of grass" (*Shikshashtaka* 3; CC, Antya 20.21). This is why Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati counts the "ativadi" among the apasampradayas (see the note to 1.27). ତୁହି (*tuhi*, familiar "you", 3.45) is read as in 1.75; here it apparently carries mockery. Who speaks in 3.48, the Vaishnavas or the author, is unclear. Given the link with 3.49–3.54, these are the author's words on the Lord's sovereignty.
[^karta-3-49]: The source of the shloka has not been found. Printed କର୍ତ୍ତ (without ା and ୃ) three times and ଯଥାଦେବ ସ୍ତସ୍ୟ; restored by metre and sense: *kartā kartṛ-dvayoḥ kartā… devas tasya kiṁ cintayāmy aham*. Who the "two makers" are is unclear (Brahma and Shiva? prakriti and purusha?). 3.50–3.54 are an Odia gloss: the Lord is almighty; He can make the done undone and the undone done. Cf. the well-known phrase *kartum akartum anyathā kartuṁ samarthaḥ*, "able to do, not to do, and to do otherwise". With this the author apparently justifies the exaltation of Jagannatha Dasa above the Lord's former servants (3.46). ମହିମାଟି (3.50; printed ମହ + ମା + a sign like ଣ୍ଟ) and ତହୁଁକ (printed ତ + ହୁଁ + କ) are tentative readings.
[^katila-3-56]: କାଟିଲା (*kāṭilā*) is understood by the Odia idiom *nā̃ kāṭibā*, "to make a name, to become renowned". The second sign of the word is indistinct. 3.57 leads into the devotees' complaint; it continues on the next page.
