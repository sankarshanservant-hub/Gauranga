# Divakara Dasa. *Jagannatha-charitamrita*, Chapter 3
## Jagannatha Dasa Serves Sri Chaitanya; Mahaprabhu Asks about His Former Form

*Translated from Odia without a native speaker; doubtful places are marked (?) and (??). The original, transliteration and word-for-word translation are in `full/03.md`.*[^intro-3-1]

*Chapter Three*

**3.1.** Listen attentively, kinsfolk, to the deeds of Atibada Gosai.  
**3.2.** Thus some days passed. And that Jagannatha Dasa,  
**3.3.** day by day, with ever-new love, served Sri Chaitanya with devotion.[^seva-3-3]  
**3.4.** Rising at dawn (?), [he offered Him] full prostration, and gave [Him water] to wash His holy face;  
**3.5.** he cleaned the bed for sleeping (?); [he gave] clay to cleanse the holy hands;  
**3.6.** the tooth-stick for cleaning the teeth; the bath, the rubbing down, drinking water;  
**3.7.** sprinkling the holy body; putting on new cloth;  
**3.8.** the daily duties of sannyasa; cleaning the staff and kamandalu.  
**3.9.** All such tasks — service to Prabhu — were [his] daily duty;  
**3.10.** [also] the darshan of Jagannatha, and the routine of serving [Prabhu] in [His] retinue.  
**3.11.** Seeing this service, Chaitanya put a question to Dasa:  
**3.12.** "O Dasa, seeing your service, We have become very happy at heart.  
**3.13.** About your former divine form — tell Me that story."[^purva-3-13]  
**3.14.** Hearing this command, Dasa was amazed and rejoiced.  
**3.15.** That (?) Lord is the embodiment of bhakti, the lamp (?) … (??) of the Kali age.[^pradipa-3-15]  
**3.16.** "[You are] the Purusha, the Lord of all (?); nothing is hidden from You.  

## Jagannatha Dasa's Account of His Former Form; Mahaprabhu Calls Him Atibada; the Vaishnavas Murmur


*Shloka*  
**3.17.** *[Skt.]* "The hidden (?) supreme abode (?) of Vishnu is the glorious, wondrous Vaikuntha; it spreads out [like] a lotus with its pericarp — this is declared a secret.[^shloka-3-17][^intro-3-17]  
**3.18.** Above it, on a golden throne, adorned with a jewelled pavilion, in the pericarp is the great abode where Govinda dwells (?), great and imperishable.  
**3.19.** Its southern petal is called the most secret of secrets, the highest. Then [He] entered the rasa on the great throne, which is hard to reach.  
**3.20.** In its midst dwells Krishna, intent on embracing Radha; with one self, with mind and intellect, He manifests (?) the eternal lila."  
**3.21.** "In eternal Vaikuntha, [His] own abode, Bhagavan [was] together … (??) [with Radha].[^rabe-3-21]  
**3.22.** [They] abode as one self, and with great happiness cast a glance [at each other].  
**3.23.** Seeing Radha's face, the Life of the world laughed.[^hasa-3-23]  
**3.24.** That laughter by which crores upon crores of worlds are born in a single moment —  
**3.25.** from that laughter You appeared (lit. "were born"), and You stand in front of the Lord.  
**3.26.** From Radha's laughter I was born, and I stand behind You.  
**3.27.** Looking [at You], [the Lord] gave You the command: 'Becoming incarnate in the mortal world —  
**3.28.** this age of Kali is mighty (?) — perform the kirtan of Hari's Name.'  
**3.29.** You make the unconscious conscious; therefore Your name is Chaitanya.[^caitanya-3-29]  
**3.30.** Then Sri Krishna, looking at me, said: 'Be born on earth.[^mahi-3-30]  
**3.31.** You will worship this form, and at the end you will attain that same goal.'  
**3.32.** Therefore, apart from the love of the Couple, my mind finds taste in nothing else."  
**3.33.** Hearing this, Sri Chaitanya became absorbed in meditation (?) on His own abode.[^dhyana-3-33]  
**3.34.** Attaining recognition (?) of the former form, the Gosai rejoiced.  
**3.35.** He removed His own wrap (??) from His sacred body with His sacred hand[^pacheri-3-35]  
**3.36.** and tied it on Dasa's head, saying: "[You are] very great (ati bada)!  
**3.37.** You have told this most rare account today before Me.  

*Shloka*  
**3.38.** *[Skt.]* "Hard to attain even for yogis, true, accessible [only] to the Purusha (?)."[^yogi-3-38]  
**3.39.** You have told a very great account; therefore you have become Atibada."  
**3.40.** [Since then] the world calls Dasa "Sri Atibada Jagannatha":  
**3.41.** having heard it from Mahaprabhu's mouth, everyone speaks this word.  
**3.42.** Thus some days passed. Sri Chaitanya in Purushottama  
**3.43.** every day, with an affectionate heart, constantly calls [him] Atibada.  
**3.44.** When [He] called [him] Atibada, the Vaishnavas grieved at heart.[^vaishnava-3-44]  
**3.45.** Looking at the Odia brahmana, [they] said: "You (?) are Atibada!"  
**3.46.** "Until today we have served, and all of us have fallen to a lower position.  
**3.47.** If we stay with Him, we shall hear nothing but such reproach."  
**3.48.** He indeed is the Lord over all (?); He accomplishes everything [by a mere] word.  

*Shloka*  
**3.49.** *[Skt.]* "The Maker, the maker of the two makers, the cause of the cause of makers; God is all-pervading — what then can I think of Him?"[^karta-3-49]  
**3.50.** Such is the glory of the Lord that there is no gainsaying it (?):  
**3.51.** what is done He makes undone, what is not done He does;  
**3.52.** at times He establishes what is done, at times He does not do what is not done;  
**3.53.** at times He delivers great sinners, at times He destroys the pious.  
**3.54.** Since He abides in all bodies, He is called the greatest of all.  
**3.55.** Every day Sri Chaitanya calls [Dasa] by the name Atibada.  
**3.56.** Thus some days passed; the name "Atibada" took hold (?).[^katila-3-56]  
**3.57.** Unable to bear [it], the devotees went and reported [it] before the Prabhu.  

## The Vaishnavas Leave for Yajanagara; Mahaprabhu's Letter "More Humble than a Blade of Grass"; the Lord Goes After Them


**3.58.** "You brought an Odia brahmin and called [him] by the title 'Atibada' (?).[^intro-3-58]  
**3.59.** All the servants have become small, [while] his name is 'Atibada'."  
**3.60.** Sri Chaitanya commanded: "Know [this place] as Purushottama.[^kshetra-3-60]  

*Shloka*  
**3.61.** *[Skt.]* 'Even from insects, moths, wood, stones and shells (?) [here] the best (?) on earth is born — what then to speak of a human!'  
**3.62.** The pebbles (?) and stones of this Kshetra, the shells (??) and the wood — regard them thus:  
**3.63.** for other realms they are gods. What then to say of the people!  
**3.64.** Take this into account: the word 'Atibada' is not at all too great for the Dasa.  
**3.65.** As many men and women as there are [here], [all] attain bhakti without sadhana:  
**3.66.** it enters [them] of itself; in other realms this is unattainable."  
**3.67.** Hearing such words from the Prabhu's mouth, all the Vaishnavas were aggrieved.  
**3.68.** They deliberated (?) in their hearts: "It is not proper to stay [here]."  
**3.69.** So they all decided, and cast away their attachment to the Prabhu.  
**3.70.** In despair over the title 'Atibada', they gave up hope of the best of kshetras.  
**3.71.** They left Purushottama and went away, saying: "[We go] to Yajanagara."[^yajanagara-3-71]  
**3.72.** For six months they stayed there. Thus Chaitanya Gosai,  
**3.73.** together with six Vaishnavas, remained in Purushottama.[^shadmurti-3-73]  
**3.74.** They worshipped (?) Jagannatha and, [apart from] service, knew nothing else.  
**3.75.** With affection for the host of devotees in His heart, [the Lord] bore compassion for them deep in His heart.  
**3.76.** [There was] a Vaishnava named Haridasa, dearest to all the mahantas.[^haridasa-3-76]  
**3.77.** The Merciful One sent him: "Go and bring all the Vaishnavas."  
**3.78.** He wrote a shloka in a letter and gave [it] into Haridasa's hand.  

*Shloka*  
**3.79.** *[Skt.]* 'Being more humble than a blade of grass, more tolerant than a tree, seeking no honour (?) and giving honour [to others], one should always chant [the name of] Hari.'[^trinad-3-79]  
**3.80.** At the command Haridasa went and entered Yajanagara.  
**3.81.** Paying respects to all, he conveyed (?) [the message] to the assembly in detail.  
**3.82.** With the consent of all the mahantas, he handed over the letter before [them].  
**3.83.** Jagadananda Chakravarti reads out in the assembly the words of the holy mouth:[^jagadananda-3-83]  
**3.84.** "One should be lower than a blade of grass, endure everything like a tree,  
**3.85.** not rejoice at honour, not grieve at dishonour:  
**3.86.** then a Vaishnava becomes perfect. Where there is division, bhakti does not hold."[^bheda-3-86]  
**3.87.** Hearing this, the Vaishnavas were pleased and said, looking at Haridasa:  
**3.88.** "If He has affection for us, then let Him [Himself] take [us] away from here.  
**3.89.** Without seeing Chaitanya's feet, we shall not go to the best of kshetras again."  
**3.90.** Hearing [this], Haridasa went and told these words to the Prabhu.  
**3.91.** Hearing it, Chaitanya Gosai, looking at the Atibada Dasa,  
**3.92.** said: "Let us go to Yajapura and bring back all the Vaishnavas."[^yajapura-3-92]  
**3.93.** Jagannatha Dasa said: "Because of me they have all taken offence.  
**3.94.** How shall I go there? There will be anger in their hearts."  
**3.95.** Hearing this, the Prabhu commanded, uniting [him] with the gods (?):[^devata-3-95]  
**3.96.** "Our word will stand, [and] between them and you there will be love."  
**3.97.** Together with the six (?) Vaishnavas, the Prabhu went to Yajanagara.  
**3.98.** Arriving in Yajanagara, where the host of Vaishnavas was, He met [them].  
**3.99.** Hearing [of Him], the Vaishnavas were pleased and went out some distance to receive [Him] (?).  
**3.100.** Welcome, greeting, feasting — thus some days passed.  
**3.101.** One day Chaitanya joyfully called [him]: "Atibada Dasa!"  
**3.102.** Hearing the former word, all the Vaishnavas grieved:  
**3.103.** "The very words on account of which [we went] so far — the same reply He has spoken now.  
**3.104.** If we go to Purushottama, we shall hear only these words."[^atibada-3-104]  

## The Vaishnavas Leave for Vishnupur; Mahaprabhu's Letter about Purushottama; the Gaudiyas Go to Vrindavana


**3.105.** "Let us leave the company of the Odia and go to the land of Gauda," [they] said.[^intro-3-105]  
**3.106.** Looking at Chaitanya, they said: "Truly, [one should] not stay in one kingdom.[^gauda-3-106]  
**3.107.** [Take] bath at Gaya (?), in the Ganga, [at] the sea — make a pilgrimage to the tirthas!"  
**3.108.** Hearing these words, Sri Chaitanya — this is how He spoke:  

*Shloka*  
**3.109.** *[Skt.]* "Attachment to Krishna, mind on Krishna, wandering (?) along every bank of the Ganga — [yet] in life and at departure [from the body] [My] refuge is the Lord of Nilachala."[^ratih-3-109]  
**3.110.** "My mind, intellect and feeling seek refuge with Jagannatha.  
**3.111.** Whether I live or depart [from this world], apart from Jagannatha I have no other.  
**3.112.** Why then do you grieve at heart? This is Atibada Jagannatha.[^atibada-3-112]  
**3.113.** From My [word] there will be no other release (?): this befits the very great one."  
**3.114.** Hearing these words, the Vaishnavas then left the Prabhu and went away.  
**3.115.** Coming to Bana-Vishnupur, they set up a matha there and stayed [there].[^vishnupur-3-115]  
**3.116.** The Prabhu graciously returned and remained in Purushottama.  
**3.117.** And at the end of a year all the Vaishnavas, pure in heart,  
**3.118.** because they had no darshan of Purushottama, were dejected (?) at heart.  
**3.119.** They chose (?) one Vaishnava [and] said: "You will tell the Prabhu:  
**3.120.** [we have had] darshan of the holy places and bathing in the holy places."  
**3.121.** Hearing [this], the Vaishnava set out and bowed down to the Prabhu.  
**3.122.** Seeing [him], Sri Chaitanya asked: "Are the chief Vaishnavas well?"  
**3.123.** That Madho Dasa speaks: "By the grace of Your feet,[^madho-3-123]  
**3.124.** all is well, there is success in all; they practise bhakti to perfection."  
**3.125.** Hearing [this], the Prabhu was joyful: from prema (?) His tears flowed.[^premasha-3-125]  
**3.126.** Thus, after some days, Madho Dasa speaks before [the Prabhu]:  
**3.127.** "The Vaishnavas say: [let Him] make a pilgrimage to the tirthas.  
**3.128.** [You] have stayed in the Odia kingdom, [You] have taken to observing Odia practices."[^odia-3-128]  
**3.129.** Hearing [this], Sri Chaitanya, lovingly embracing the Vaishnava, said:  
**3.130.** "Madho Dasa, go and tell [them], and give this letter there."  

*Shloka*  
**3.131.** *[Skt.]* "I have seen the city of Kashi and the Yamuna, beheld (?) the nearness of Madhupuri, seen Gangadvara, many roads (?) … (??) the city of Ayodhya. But in Sri Purushottama — remembering, remembering day and night the world-enchanting form of the Foe of Mura, my mind stays nowhere [else]."[^kashi-3-131]  
**3.132.** That Madho Dasa took the command and gave [the letter] in the assembly of Vaishnavas.  
**3.133.** In it was written thus: "I have seen Kashi,  
**3.134.** Madhupura, the town of cowherds, Vrindavana, the city of Dvaraka,  
**3.135.** Gangadvara, the confluence of the Ganga, Ayodhya, Badarikashrama.  
**3.136.** In these Krishna formerly abode; at the end of the yuga He left them.  
**3.137.** Leaving all the kshetras and tirthas, Jagannatha in Nila-kandara[^kandara-3-137]  
**3.138.** enchants the world by [His] form — therefore I have taken shelter [in Him].  
**3.139.** As long as I live [I shall be] here: I will never leave this [place]."  
**3.140.** Hearing these words, the Vaishnavas all deliberated again:  
**3.141.** "We thought in our hearts: the Son of Shachi, showing mercy,  
**3.142.** will abolish the title 'Atibada', [and] He (?) will take [us] to Purushottama.[^sehi-3-142]  
**3.143.** We shall not go to Purushottama, then! By what shelter would we gain bhakti [there]?  
**3.144.** The former place of Govinda's lila — let us go away to Sri Vrindavana."[^vrindavana-3-144]  
**3.145.** [Though] this word [is given] in the Vedas and Puranas, they rejected [it — took up] another injunction.  
**3.146.** Whatever customs Purushottama had — those matters they made into another custom.  
**3.147.** They abandoned "Hare Rama Krishna" and began to say "Hare Krishna Rama".[^harerama-3-147]  
**3.148.** They cast away the honouring of nirmalya [and] began to worship (?) the dust of Vrindavana.[^nirmalya-3-148]  

## The Gaudiyas in Vrindavana; Rupa and Sanatana at the Gundicha; End of the Chapter


**3.149.** Leaving bathing in the sea, they turned their minds to the waters of the Yamuna;[^intro-3-149]  
**3.150.** leaving the form of Jagannatha, [they turned their] love to Madana-mohana.[^madanamohana-3-150]  
**3.151.** They cast off the 'Hari-mandira' [tilaka] [and] took to honouring (?) a black (?) tilaka.[^tilaka-3-151]  
**3.152.** Leaving the hope of Purushottama, they settled in Vrindavana.  
**3.153.** They cast off acts by rule [and] took to worshipping Krishna by the path of raga.[^raga-3-153]  
**3.154.** They reasoned in their minds: "Bhagavan is subject to bhakti.  
**3.155.** Since Hari is subject to feeling, He delivers and takes away [one] who thinks [of Him].  
**3.156.** He is pleased by the devotee's feeling (?) [and] for feeling grants the hard-to-attain abode.  
**3.157.** Where devotees dwell — know that [place] as Purushottama."[^bhava-3-157]  
**3.158.** Having reasoned thus in their minds, they took up residence in Vrindavana.  
**3.159.** Circling the kshetra, they wander round the hill of Govardhana,[^govardhana-3-159]  
**3.160.** bathe in the Yamuna, [have] darshan of the kadamba tree.  
**3.161.** Thus the glory of the six Gosais became manifest there.[^shad-3-161]  
**3.162.** Hearing this news, Sri Chaitanya, looking (?) at Mathura Dasa,  
**3.163.** said [to him]: "Take the order, go [and] bring [them]" (?).[^mathura-3-163]  
**3.164.** Mathura Dasa set off and arrived in Vrindavana.  
**3.165.** He told [them] Prabhu's orders. Hearing [them], Rupa [and] Sanatana  
**3.166.** came to the Gundicha-yatra together (?) with twenty companions.  
**3.167.** They saw Chaitanya Gosai pushing the chariot with [His] head.[^ratha-3-167]  
**3.168.** They made full prostrations [to Him], and Prabhu Himself embraced [them].  
**3.169.** Overwhelmed by prema, they did not know their own from others.  
**3.170.** First — Prabhu Nityananda; Sri Sanatana — [full of] joy (?);[^six-3-170]  
**3.171.** Sri Rupa Gosai; Sri Raghunatha Dasa — [also] there;  
**3.172.** Sri Jiva Gosai; Sri Gopala Dasa — [also] abiding [with them].  
**3.173.** [These are] the six Vaishnavas of liberation (?). Who will write more of that?  
**3.174.** Having had darshan of Jagannatha, [they] go to Vrindavana.  
**3.175.** Every year they come [and] serve in the Gundicha procession;[^yearly-3-175]  
**3.176.** having completed the darshan of Gundicha, they go again to Vrindavana.  
**3.177.** Sri Chaitanya showed [them] honour, [but] they did not take it to heart.  
**3.178.** Whoever stays near [something great] does not esteem it, though it be great.[^near-3-178]  
**3.179.** [Bhrigu], not acknowledging [Him] as Vishnu, struck the Srivatsa with his foot;  
**3.180.** Nanda beat [Krishna], seizing Him by the tuft of hair; the cowherd boys leapt [onto Him].  
**3.181.** [Those who] share the company of the Lord — [such] devotees do not speak of awe (??).[^kampa-3-181]  
**3.182.** Whatever and wherever the devotees speak, there the Lord is found as such.  
**3.183.** With them Sri Chaitanya, saying nothing at all, [remained] silent.  
**3.184.** Offended at the title 'Atibada', they returned (?) [and] went to Vrindavana.[^atibada-3-184]  
**3.185.** But this Sri Chaitanya Gosai remains abiding in Purushottama;  
**3.186.** He chants 'Hare Rama Krishna', bearing love for Sri Jagannatha.[^harerama-3-186]  
**3.187.** Says the brahmin Divakara: "At Chaitanya's feet is my head.  
**3.188.** That Prabhu is the ocean of bhakti: in the boat of bhakti He will carry [us] across."  

*Thus [ends], in the Sri Jagannathamrita, [the life of] Sri Ati Bada Jagannatha, the third chapter, called 'Anukramani' (?).*[^colophon-3-188]

[^seva-3-3]: 3.3–3.10 present Jagannatha Dasa as Mahaprabhu's personal servant. He carries out the Lord's morning routine: the prostration, washing the face, cleaning the bed, clay for the hands, the tooth-stick, the bath and change of cloth, cleaning the sannyasi's staff and kamandalu. He also accompanies the Lord to the darshan of Jagannatha. According to the CC, Mahaprabhu's personal servant in Puri was Govinda, together with Kashishvara (CC, Madhya 10; cf. Jagannatha Dasa is not mentioned in the CC or CB (cf. the note to VL 9.63). ପ୍ରଭାତୁଁ (*prabhātu̐*, "from dawn", 3.4) is a tentative reading: the second sign is filled with ink and resembles ଭା/ସ୍ୱ. ମୃତିକା (*mṛtikā*, 3.5) = *mṛttikā*, "clay". *Karāi … dhaüta* (3.4) is understood as "giving [the Lord water] to wash the face". ଗହଣ ଖଟିବା (*gahaṇa khaṭibā*, 3.10) means "to serve in the retinue, to attend".
[^purva-3-13]: The "former divine form" (*pūrba dibya aṅga*) is apparently a question about who Jagannatha Dasa was in Krishna's eternal pastimes. The answer apparently follows on the next page. Cf. 1.27 and 1.75 (he is a part of Radha) and 1.61, 1.68 (he was born from Radha's laughter). Establishing Jagannatha Dasa's pre-eminence is a running theme of the Atibadi line; for the Gaudiya Vaishnava position see the note to 2.22. Mahaprabhu speaks of Himself as "We" (*hoilu̐*, 1st pers. pl., 3.12), the honorific plural.
[^pradipa-3-15]: The reading and sense of 3.15 are unclear. The first sign is filled with ink and taken as ସେ, "that". If so, 3.15 is the author's description of Mahaprabhu, and Jagannatha Dasa's speech begins in 3.16 ("nothing is hidden from You"). Another possibility is that Dasa already begins to answer in 3.15. The word ଅଙ୍ଗ (*aṅga*, "body, limb") is unclear in sense; ଅନ୍ଧ (*andha*) would be expected: "a lamp for the blind of the Kali age". ପ୍ରଦୀପ (*pradīpa*, "lamp") is read from the indistinct signs ପ୍ର + ଦୀ + ପ. ସର୍ବଙ୍କ (*sarbaṅka*, "of all", 3.16): the third sign is smudged. Gaudiya Vaishnavas also worship Mahaprabhu as the Supreme Lord, Krishna Himself (CC, Adi 1–2). The speech continues on the next page.

The shlokas 3.17–3.20 open the answer. The couplets 3.21–3.32 are his speech ("You" is Mahaprabhu). In 3.33–3.39 Mahaprabhu speaks. Features of the type on these pages: on many letters the sign ି is printed as a stroke or small tail inside or below the letter, not as an arc above. [^vaishnava-3-44]: The "Vaishnavas" (3.44) and the "devotees" (3.57) are apparently Mahaprabhu's companions, the Gaudiyas. In Divakara they murmur and complain that they "have fallen to a lower position" (3.46): the author thus stresses Jagannatha Dasa's superiority over the Gaudiyas. Gaudiya Vaishnavas see Mahaprabhu's companions (Svarupa Damodara, Ramananda Raya and others) as His eternal associates, free from envy. [^karta-3-49]: The source of the shloka has not been found. Gangadvara is Haridvar; 'the confluence of the Ganga' is evidently Prayaga (or Ganga-sagara); 'the town of cowherds' is probably Gokula. According to CC (Madhya 17–19), Mahaprabhu visited Kashi, Prayaga, Mathura and Vrindavana. According to CC, He did not go to Dvaraka, Ayodhya or Badarikashrama. The words 'I have seen' here may refer to the pilgrimages of those same Vaishnavas (3.120).
[^kandara-3-137]: Nila-kandara ('the Blue Cave') is a name of Puri, like Nilachala ('the Blue Mountain'). 3.136–3.137 give an Odia teaching: Krishna left the former places of His pastimes at the end of the yuga and now abides in Puri as Jagannatha. Gaudiya Vaishnavas worship Jagannatha as Krishna Himself. Mahaprabhu saw Krishna in Him and longed to take Him to Vrindavana (CC, Madhya 13). But they hold Krishna's pastimes in Vrindavana to be eternal, and Vrindavana to be the highest abode, which Krishna never leaves (CC, Adi 5).
[^sehi-3-142]: 3.141–3.142 are understood tentatively. *Nebe sehi* is 'He will take' or 'that one will take', and *puruṣottame* is 'to Purushottama' or 'in Purushottama'. The sense adopted: the Vaishnavas hoped that Mahaprabhu would abolish the title 'Atibada' for their sake and bring them back to Puri. The letter, however, only repeats that He will not leave Jagannatha. Hence the decision of 3.143–3.144.
[^vrindavana-3-144]: From 3.145 the author speaks. According to Divakara, the Gaudiyas, offended with Puri, go to Vrindavana. There they 'reject' the injunctions of the Vedas and Puranas and the customs of Purushottama (3.145–3.148). According to CC, Mahaprabhu Himself sent Rupa and Sanatana Gosvami to Vrindavana to uncover the lost places of Krishna's pastimes and to write books on bhakti (CC, Madhya 19, 23; Antya 4). Gaudiya Vaishnavas, following Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati, hold their teaching to be grounded in shastra. Their guides are the *Hari-bhakti-vilasa* of Sanatana and Gopala Bhatta and the works of the Gosvamis.
[^harerama-3-147]: The dispute over the beginning of the mahamantra: 'Hare Rama…' (the Odia custom) or 'Hare Krishna…' (the Gaudiyas). Cf. VL 6.1–6.18 and the note to VL 6.15: there Mahaprabhu Himself decides that in Nilachala the mantra begins with 'Hare Rama' and in Gauda with 'Hare Krishna'. In Divakara the Gaudiyas abandon the order of Purushottama on their own. On the 'sixteen [names]' see the note to 2.160. Gaudiya Vaishnavas, following Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati, chant the mahamantra beginning with 'Hare Krishna'.
[^nirmalya-3-148]: Nirmalya is the dried mahaprasad of Jagannatha, and also the flowers and other items offered to the Deity. In Puri it is honoured and sent out with pilgrims. Divakara's charge differs from CC: there Mahaprabhu and His Gaudiya associates receive Jagannatha's mahaprasad with reverence (CC, Madhya 12; Antya 16). ଭଜିଲେ (?): printed ଭ + a sign ଜ or ବ + ଲେ, with ି not visible. ରଜ is taken as 'dust' (*raja*). 'Vraja' may also be meant.
[^intro-3-149]: End of Chapter 3: PDF pages 22–23 (printed pp. 21–22). 3.149–3.153 continue the list of 3.145–3.148: here the author speaks and accuses the Gaudiyas who went to Vrindavana. On p. 23, after the colophon, Chapter 4 begins. Features of the type on these pages. ଆ is printed as a bold sign with a flag: ଆଶ, ଆଜ୍ଞା, ଆଣିବ, ଆଇଲେ, ଆଲିଙ୍ଗନ, ଆପଣା, ଆଦ୍ୟେଣ, ଆନନ୍ଦ (3.170), ଆସନ୍ତି, ଆଦର. A light ଅ without a flag stands in ଅଧିକ (3.173) and in the colophon (ଅତିବଡ଼, ଅନୁକର୍ମଣି). But in 3.184 ଅତିବଡ଼ is printed with the bold flagged sign, so the distinction is not absolute. ଆନନ୍ଦ in 3.170 is therefore given without correction (cf. ଅନନ୍ଦ[ଆନନ୍ଦ] in 3.125). The sign ି is often invisible or printed as a stroke inside the letter: so in ଛଡ଼ିଣ, ଭଜିଲେ, ହରି, ଭାବିଲେ, ନିଅନ୍ତି, କୋଡ଼ିଏ, ଚଳିଗଲେ, ଘେନିଲେ, ବିଷ୍ଣୁ, ଧରି, ସାରି. Such ି are given without brackets. ରା, ଚା resemble ସ୍ୱ (ରାଗ, ମଥୁରା, ଗୁଣ୍ଡିଚା, ରାମ), ଭା resembles ଭ୍ୱ (ଭାବ). ଛଡ଼ିଣ, ଛଡ଼ି (3.149, 3.150, 3.152) = ଛାଡ଼ିଣ, ଛାଡ଼ି, 'leaving', like ଛଡ଼ିଲେ in 3.70. ସ୍ନାହାନ (3.149, 3.160) is a stretched *snāna*, as in 3.120. ପୀରତି (3.150) = *prīti*, 'love'. ଲ and ଳ are hardly distinguished in this type; the usual spelling is given (ତିଳକ, ଗୋପାଳ, ଚୂଳ). 3.159 prints ଟିରି and 3.185 ଚୋସାଇଁ: misprints for ଗିରି and ଗୋସାଇଁ.
[^madanamohana-3-150]: Madana-mohana ('He who enchants even Cupid') is Krishna, the Deity of Sanatana Gosvami in Vrindavana. Gaudiya Vaishnavas honour Madana-mohana, Govinda and Gopinatha as the principal Deities of Vrindavana, installed by the Gosvamis. The 'abandoning' of Jagannatha's form is Divakara's accusation. According to the CC, the Gaudiyas worshipped Jagannatha as Krishna Himself (see the note to 3.137).
[^tilaka-3-151]: On the 'Hari-mandira' tilaka see the note to 2.150. The second half-line is read with doubt. କଳା (?) is printed as କ + ଲା; understood as *kaḷā*, 'black'. *Kalā* is also possible. The last word is printed as flagged ଅ + a sign like ସ୍ୱ (= ରା) + ଧ + ଲେ; understood as ଆରାଧିଲେ, 'they honoured'. It may be ଆଦରିଲେ, 'they adopted with regard'. Which tilaka Divakara calls 'black' is unclear. Gaudiya Vaishnavas wear the same urdhva-pundra (the 'temple of Hari'), of gopi-chandana or Vraja clay (*Hari-bhakti-vilasa*, ch. 4). The charge of abandoning the 'Hari-mandira' is the author's view.
[^raga-3-153]: *Baidha karma* is service by the rules of scripture (vaidhi-bhakti); *rāga-mārga* is the path of spontaneous love (raganuga-bhakti). According to the CC (Madhya 22) and Rupa Gosvami's *Bhakti-rasamrita-sindhu* (1.2), Mahaprabhu Himself taught both paths. Raganuga-bhakti follows the example of the eternal residents of Vraja but does not reject scripture. Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati warned against imitating raga without purity of heart. He did not approve of abandoning the rules: the Gosvamis themselves compiled the *Hari-bhakti-vilasa* on vaidhi-bhakti. Divakara, however, presents the Gaudiyas as rejecting the rules (cf. 3.145).
[^bhava-3-157]: 3.154–3.157 give the Gaudiyas' reasoning as Divakara reports it: the Lord is subject to the devotee's feeling; where devotees dwell, there is Purushottama. Cf. the well-known verse ascribed to the *Padma Purana*: 'I abide where My devotees sing.' This argument answers 3.60–3.66, where Mahaprabhu glorifies the Purushottama-kshetra. In 3.178–3.182 Divakara apparently grants the first half of the argument: the Lord 'is found there' where devotees speak of Him. ଭବେ (3.156) is printed without ା; understood as ଭାବେ, 'by feeling', as in the second half-line.
[^govardhana-3-159]: Govardhana Hill, the Yamuna and the kadamba trees are places of Krishna's pastimes in Vraja. The parikrama of Vraja is the circuit of its holy places. According to the CC (Madhya 17–18), Mahaprabhu Himself went round Vrindavana, Govardhana and the forests of Vraja. Here this is described as the doing of the Gaudiyas who turned away from Purushottama.
[^shad-3-161]: The 'six Gosais' (*ṣaṛa gosāi̐*) are the six Gosvamis of Vrindavana. Their list is given in 3.170–3.172 (see the note to 3.170). *Emantare* is understood as 'thus'; possibly 'meanwhile'.
[^mathura-3-163]: Mathura Dasa is not identified; no such companion appears in the CC/CB. The reading ଚାଇଣ (3.162) is tentative: printed a sign like ସ୍ୱ + ଇଣ (cf. ଚାହିଁ in 3.91, 3.106). 3.163b literally reads 'you will bring Mathura Dasa'. It is understood thus: -ku here marks the addressee — '[He said] to Mathura Dasa' (cf. 'they said, looking at Chaitanya', 3.106) — and those to be brought are the Gosvamis (3.165–3.166). According to the CC, Mahaprabhu Himself sent Rupa and Sanatana to Vrindavana (Madhya 19, 23). They came to Puri separately, not at a summons: Rupa through Bengal (Antya 1), Sanatana through the Jharikhanda forest at the time of the Ratha-yatra (Antya 4; cf. VL 5.40, 5.73–5.76).
[^ratha-3-167]: The Gundicha-yatra is the Ratha-yatra: the Deities ride on chariots to the Gundicha temple (cf. the note to VL 5.75). According to the CC (Madhya 14), when Jagannatha's chariot stopped and the elephants could not move it, Mahaprabhu pushed it with His head, and the chariot moved. ସଙ୍ଗତୁଲେ (3.166) is read tentatively: printed ସଙ୍ଗ + a ligature like ଭୁ or ତୁ, + ଲେ (cf. ତୁଲେ, 'together with', 3.73). 'Twenty persons' (*koṛie mūrtti*): cf. 'six persons' in 3.73.
[^six-3-170]: The syntax of 3.170–3.172 is uncertain. 3.170b literally reads 'Sri Sanatana's joy' (or 'Sri Sanatana — joy'). *Yeu̐ gosāi̐* means 'who [is] Gosai'. Divakara's six are Nityananda, Sanatana, Rupa, Raghunatha Dasa, Jiva and Gopala Dasa. Gaudiya Vaishnavas name as the six Gosvamis Rupa, Sanatana, Raghunatha Bhatta, Raghunatha Dasa, Jiva and Gopala Bhatta. Nityananda is not among them: according to the CC (Madhya 15) and CB (Antya 5), Mahaprabhu sent Him to preach in Gauda. Divakara lacks Raghunatha Bhatta; 'Gopala Dasa' is presumably Gopala Bhatta. Jiva Gosvami, the nephew of Rupa and Sanatana, came to Vrindavana, by Gaudiya tradition, only after Mahaprabhu's disappearance. ମୋକ୍ଷ (3.173) is printed clearly; it may be a misprint for ମୁଖ୍ୟ, 'chief'.
[^yearly-3-175]: 3.174–3.176: the Gosvamis come every year to the Gundicha and return to Vrindavana. According to the CC, it was the Gaudiyas from Bengal who came every year for the Ratha-yatra (Madhya 16; Antya 10, 12; see the note to 3.71). The Gosvamis from Vrindavana, by the CC, came to Puri only once each — Rupa (Antya 1) and Sanatana (Antya 4).
[^near-3-178]: 3.177–3.183 give the author's explanation: the Gaudiyas did not value Mahaprabhu's honour because they were close to Him. Examples of such closeness follow. Bhrigu struck Vishnu on the chest with his foot (*Bhagavata* 10.89); tradition links this blow with a mark on Vishnu's chest (here the Srivatsa). Nanda beats Krishna, seizing Him by the tuft: in the *Bhagavata* it is Yashoda who punishes Krishna (10.9). In their games the cowherd boys leap onto Krishna's shoulders (10.18). Gaudiya Vaishnavas see in such closeness the higher rasas of sakhya and vatsalya, free from reverential awe (CC, Adi 4). With Divakara it serves as a reproach.
[^kampa-3-181]: The word କମ୍ପା (??) is unclear: printed କ + ମ with a subscript resembling ପ or ଭ, + ା. Understood as *kampa*, 'trembling', i.e. reverential fear. The translation is tentative.
[^atibada-3-184]: 3.184 sums up the chapter: the Gaudiyas finally went to Vrindavana because of the title 'Atibada'. ନେଉଟି is understood as *leuṭi*, 'returning' (cf. ଲେଉଟି 3.116). Mahaprabhu, however, remains in Puri with Jagannatha. On the Gaudiya Vaishnava position see the notes to 3.44 and 3.79. According to the CC, the Gaudiyas did not leave Mahaprabhu; the Gosvamis lived in Vrindavana by His order.
[^harerama-3-186]: On the order of the mahamantra ('Hare Rama Krishna' among the Odias, 'Hare Krishna Rama' among the Gaudiyas) see the note to 3.147. According to Divakara, Mahaprabhu Himself chants it in the Odia order.
[^colophon-3-188]: The colophon is set in two lines, as at the end of Chapters 1 and 2 (see the note to the colophon of Ch. 2). Chapter 3 is also called 'Anukramani'; printed ଅନୁକର୍ମଣି, understood as ଅନୁକ୍ରମଣି. After Jagannatha's name there is here neither 'Dasa' (Ch. 1) nor 'Gosai' (Ch. 2): 'Sri Atibada Jagannatha'.
