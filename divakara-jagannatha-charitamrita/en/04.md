# Divakara Dasa. *Jagannatha-charitamrita*, Chapter 4
## Mahaprabhu Goes with the Swami to Sarvabhauma

*Translated from Odia without a native speaker; doubtful places are marked (?) and (??). The original, transliteration and word-for-word translation are in `full/04.md`.*[^intro-4-1]

*Chapter Four*

**4.1.** Listen attentively, good people, to the doings of Prabhu Chaitanya.  
**4.2.** Taking the Swami (?) with Him, He met Sarvabhauma.[^svami-4-2]  
**4.3.** That Sarvabhauma Bhattacharya [performed] his duties by Vedic injunctions;  
**4.4.** he did not know devotion to Hari [and] abode in pride of learning.[^sarvabhauma-4-4]  
**4.5.** In the hope of delivering him, Prabhu graciously went to him.  

## Sarvabhauma Asks about Mahaprasada; Unoffered Food and the Naivedya to the Three Deities


**4.6.** With Him is Jagannatha Dasa, in the hope of hearing.[^intro-4-6]  
**4.7.** Seeing [Prabhu], Sarvabhauma came and fell prostrate before Prabhu.[^sarvabhauma-4-7]  

*Shloka*  
**4.8.** *[Skt.]* "Hard to obtain is the sight of You, [the fruit] of the good fortune of ten million kalpas. At Your feet (??) I was purified in soul that very moment."[^koti-4-8]  
**4.9.** "[The fruit] of the good fortune of my ten million kalpas is Your darshan, now attained (?).  
**4.10.** What I had heard before [has come true]: I have been purified in soul."  

*Shloka*  
**4.11.** *[Skt.]* "And the unoffered (?) savoury foods — in which (??) vessel should one feed with them? In what (?) vessel, offering the unoffered, does it become mahaprasada?"[^amoha-4-11]  
**4.12.** [In this] wave of the truth of Krishna-prema, Sarvabhauma asks (?) this:  
**4.13.** "What food is unoffered (amaneha)? With what (?) vessel is it filled?  
**4.14.** In what manner is the offering made? When does [the food] become mahaprasada?"  

*Shloka*  
**4.15.** *[Skt.]* "And the unoffered savoury foods — in which (??) vessel should one feed with them? Be pleased to tell this, O Lord of mercy! Protect the three worlds (?), O Protector!"[^vaktum-4-15]  
**4.16.** "The unoffered savoury foods that Jagannatha enjoys —  
**4.17.** out of mercy tell me [of them], O Lord: You constantly (?) keep the three worlds (?)."  
**4.18.** Hearing this, Sri Chaitanya speaks a word to him:  

*Shloka*  
**4.19.** *[Skt.]* "Amoha destroys (?) all karma; amoha is rare [even] for Brahma. Through the exposition (?) of guru to disciple — that supreme, by the attainment of Brahman (?)."[^amoham-4-19]  
**4.20.** "O Sarvabhauma, hear the exposition [that] the guru [gives] to disciples.  
**4.21.** Calling it amaneha, [know]: it is itself perfect Brahman (?)."  

*Shloka*  
**4.22.** *[Skt.]* "The kshetra is Sri Purushottama, and in it abides (?) the amoha; mahaprasada is only there, in the kshetra of Sri Purushottama."[^kshetra-4-22]  
**4.23.** "Amaneha [and] mahaprasada — [this is] the excellent kshetra, a distinct liberation (?).  
**4.24.** This kshetra of liberation (?) is here; in other kshetras no such abiding is found."  

*Shloka*  
**4.25.** *[Skt.]* "Balabhadra, Subhadra, Jagannatha [and] Sudarshana — the manifestation in a fourfold form; so long as (?) Your story is told…"[^chaturdha-4-25]  
**4.26.** "Hear: Balabhadra, Subhadra, Jagannatha and Sudarshana —  
**4.27.** in this kshetra the fourfold form has become manifest and abides.  
**4.28.** The unoffered (amoha) naivedya becomes renowned [as mahaprasada] in just this way."  

*Shloka*  
**4.29.** *[Skt.]* "'Shrim' — this is the manifestation of Shesha; 'hrim' — this is Bhadra, likewise; 'klim' — Krishna Jagannatha. With these three [bijas] one should duly feed [Them] that food (?)."[^bija-4-29]  
**4.30.** "The bija 'shri' (?) is Shesha-deva; with the bija 'hri' they call Subhadra;  
**4.31.** the bija 'kli' is Jagannatha-deva. Understand the naivedya thus.  
**4.32.** Whatever naivedya you prepare, offer [it] to Sri Balabhadra;  
**4.33.** he will pass [it] to Subhadra; she will give [it] into the holy mouth of Prabhu.  
**4.34.** What Prabhu has tasted — all that is mahaprasada."  

*Shloka*  
**4.35.** *[Skt.]* "Partaking [of the nectar] of the lips of Shri (?), [with the bija] 'klim' one should feed the brahmanas (??). One who does not know prema thus — [and yet passes for] a sadhu and a Vaishnava!"[^adhara-4-35]  
**4.36.** "In this mood make the naivedya; eating the remnants, cross over samsara.  
**4.37.** People do not know this — and [yet] are called sadhus [and] Vaishnavas.  
**4.38.** This mood is hard to attain [even] for Brahma; O sadhus, cultivate this mood!"  
**4.39.** Again Sarvabhauma says: "How then does bhakti arise in this?"  

## Sarvabhauma Asks about Bhakti; the Mantra, the Name, and Radha in Jagannatha's Body


*Shloka*  
**4.40.** *[Skt.]* "The divisions of bhakti must be told; [let one] practise (??) the truth of bhakti (?). Mercifully tell me of bhakti (?), O Mahaprabhu (?)."[^bhakti-4-40][^intro-4-40]  
**4.41.** "Shall I obtain all these (??) [kinds of] bhakti? O Prabhu, give me instruction."  
**4.42.** Hearing the devotee's words, Sri Chaitanya gives instruction.  

*Shloka*  
**4.43.** *[Skt.]* "In the common tongue (?) — [the sense] of the king of mantras, which has the form of mantra and yantra: Jagannatha is the highest truth, and wholly (??), as is fitting."[^mantra-4-43]  
**4.44.** Rendering the king of mantras into the common tongue, worship the body of Jagannatha.  
**4.45.** Contemplate [Him] in mantra, yantra [and] tantra — with little [effort] you will attain bhakti.  

*Shloka*  
**4.46.** *[Skt.]* "For one who always sings the name of Hari, the devising of mantras is blunted (?); [his] always are attachment and love, and the joy of hearing and relishing."[^nama-4-46]  
**4.47.** Singing the name of Hari [and] casting contrivances far away,  
**4.48.** contemplate attachment [and] love in hearing — you will obtain the boat of the joy of bhakti.  

*Shloka*  
**4.49.** *[Skt.]* "She whose body is prema, named Radhika, is the form of sweet prema. 'Klim' — [She] who knows the rasa of Krishna (?); [Her] mark is called [that of] a Vaishnavi (?)."[^radha-4-49]  
**4.50.** Radhika is the sweet form; in the body of prema (?) abides the form of Krishna.  
**4.51.** Knowing the love of Radha [and] Krishna, count Her among the Vaishnavas.  

*Shloka*  
**4.52.** *[Skt.]* "The Parabrahman Jagannatha is higher than the highest (?) above the avatars; ten million avatars are born (??) [from Him]; … (??) is released."[^avatari-4-52]  
**4.53.** The Supreme Brahman is Jagannatha: beyond the highest is the Highest — such (?) [is He].  
**4.54.** … (??) tens of millions of avatars come out of Jagannatha's body.  

*Shloka*  
**4.55.** *[Skt.]* "Appearing (lit. 'born') (?) in Radha's body, the gopi named (?) Radhika comes to be; this arose in Sri Krishna, who sports in Vrindavana."[^radha-4-55]  
**4.56.** [She] who appeared (lit. 'is born') from Jagannatha's body is the nectar of the Govardhana (?) pastimes in Vraja.  
**4.57.** She appeared (lit. 'was born') from Him [and] sported in Vrindavana.  
**4.58.** This Radha arose from Radha; [She] is renowned [as] the daughter (lit. 'joy') of Vrishabhanu.  
**4.59.** *[Skt.]* "'Shrim' … (??) is shakti; 'hrim' is declared to be Radha; 'klim' is Krishna, Kamaraja; childhood, boyhood [and] youth."[^bija-4-59]  
**4.60.** The syllable 'shri' is the supreme king (?) [of syllables]; the syllable 'hri' is called the king (?) of Rama.  
**4.61.** The syllable 'klim' is the essence of Kamaraja. Childhood, boyhood [and] youth —  
**4.62.** five, ten [and] sixteen years: such is the age of the three forms.[^age-4-62]  
**4.63.** 'Shri' (?), which is the supreme king (?) — in it are the syllables of the name Hari.[^harerama-4-63]  
**4.64.** Know that 'hrim' is the king (?) of Rama: in it, they expound, is the name Rama.  
**4.65.** 'Klim', which is Kamaraja — know it as 'Krishna'.  
**4.66.** Red, white [and] dark hue — [thus] distinguish (?) the three names.  

*Shloka*  
**4.67.** *[Skt.]* "Jagannatha in the form of mantra is upon the head of Shesha-deva — [the form] of the yantra, of the king of mantras, joined (?) with the sixteen-syllabled mantra."[^yantra-4-67]  
**4.68.** This Jagannatha in the form of mantra abides upon Shesha-deva.  
**4.69.** Yantra, mantra, sixteen syllables — this is the utterance (?) of the king of mantras.  

*Shloka*  
**4.70.** *[Skt.]* "And in the body of Sri Jagannatha is Radha, ever (?) white; and in the middle (?) of the white chita Krishna [and] Radhika are seen."[^chita-4-70]  
**4.71.** In this body of Jagannatha abides Radha, full of prema.  
**4.72.** Shining (?), the white chita of the Lord joins the bodies (?) of Radha [and] Krishna.  

## The Glory of Radha; Radha and Jagannatha; the King of Mantras and Harinama


*Shloka*  
**4.73.** *[Skt.]* "The round (?), youthful and reddish [form] of Krishna [and] Radha (?), unseen by Brahma and Rudra — about that you asked."[^vartula-4-73][^intro-4-73]  
**4.74.** Very youthful, beautifully round — [such is] the body of Radha [and] Krishna.  
**4.75.** The truth (?) you asked about (?) is beyond the reach of Brahma [and] Rudra.  

*Shloka*  
**4.76.** *[Skt.]* "Not in pastimes (?) [only] — the absorption of Krishna [and] Radha in rasa is always. Mercifully tell me of another pastime of Radha, O Prabhu."[^vihara-4-76]  
**4.77.** Finding happiness in Radha's rasa, He, Jagannatha, becomes subject [to Her].  
**4.78.** From here appeared (lit. 'were born') Radha [and] Krishna, who perform pastimes in the world.  

*Shloka*  
**4.79.** *[Skt.]* "As the substance of the body of prema (?), so Radha too is in the form of substance. Radha, the yantra (??) of the king of mantras, abides Herself."[^dravya-4-79]  
**4.80.** Radha is the body of prema; She is called the excellent substance (?).  
**4.81.** Radha's [mantra] is the king of mantras; Radha is called the very form of the yantra.  

*Shloka*  
**4.82.** *[Skt.]* "Radhika is equal to Krishna, [She is] Krishna's body (?); in Radha is the highest essence. In Radha are bhakti and liberation, in Radha are all desires (?)."[^radhika-4-82]  
**4.83.** Radha's form is equal to Krishna; Radha abides beyond the highest (*parapara*).  
**4.84.** Radha grants bhakti [and] liberation and fulfils all desires.  

*Shloka*  
**4.85.** *[Skt.]* "In Radha are the mantra (?), the worship [and] the purashcharana — Radhika; in Radha are ten million worlds (??), in Radha are ten million mantras."[^koti-4-85]  
**4.86.** By Radha's mantra [the Deities] receive worship (?); in the invocation (avahana) (?) Radha is present.  
**4.87.** Worlds, tens of millions of expanses, are born from Radha's glance.  

*Shloka*  
**4.88.** *[Skt.]* "Radha is Jagannatha Himself; Radhika is in visible (lit. 'gross') form; Radhika is beyond prakriti; Radhika is on the head (?) of Shesha."[^jagannatha-4-88]  
**4.89.** Radha (?) is Jagannatha Himself; Radha (?) abides in visible form.  
**4.90.** Radha is beyond prakriti; Radha (?) is [on] the head of Shesha-deva.  
**4.91.** Thus the guru (?) Vishvakarma told the glory of Radha.[^vishvakarma-4-91]  
**4.92.** Radha is the giver of bhakti: say 'Radha', and bhakti arises.  
**4.93.** Jagannatha is subject to Radha; from Radha the world has flowed forth (?).  
**4.94.** The expanse of worlds, all the lands there are — all these are born from Radha.  

*Shloka*  
**4.95.** *[Skt.]* "Brahma, Vishnu and Mahesha, Indra and the [other] gods of the quarters (?), Rama and the [other] avataras (?) — dwellers (?) … (??)."[^devata-4-95]  
**4.96.** These Brahma, Vishnu, Maheshvara, Indra and the [other] guardians of the ten quarters,  
**4.97.** all the hosts of gods, all the divine places (?) of worship,  
**4.98.** Rama and all the [other] avataras — their home is in Purushottama.  
**4.99.** This king of mantras of sixteen syllables (?) came out of Jagannatha's body.  
**4.100.** By half (??) — three bijas (?); doubled — six bijas (?), they say.[^bija-4-100]  
**4.101.** As the king of mantras is, so visibly is this Jagannatha.  
**4.102.** Again Sri Chaitanya says: "Now listen about Harinama."  

*Shloka*  
**4.103.** *[Skt.]* "The mantra 'Hare Rama Krishna' is hard to obtain [even] among all mantras. This maha-mantra is desired above (?) all and grants every perfection."[^harinama-4-103]  
**4.104.** This king of mantras is Harinama; these two (?) are the abode of the fulfilment of desires.  
**4.105.** What is called Harinama is hard to obtain [even] among all mantras.  

## The King of Mantras and the Five States; the Glory of Purushottama-kshetra


*Shloka*  
**4.106.** *[Skt.]* "Receive (lit. 'make by you') the instruction in the sixteen-syllabled (?) king of mantras. The king of mantras … (??); there is no steadfastness (?) in any other bhakti (?)."[^mantraraja-4-106][^intro-4-106]  
**4.107.** You, O, do as I say: keep the king of mantras on your lips (lit. 'in the throat').  
**4.108.** When such instruction is given, it destroys all desires.  
**4.109.** All bhakti will arise, [and] at the end you will obtain the service of [His] feet.  
**4.110.** Without this mantra, by another way, the devotee will never obtain [bhakti].  
**4.111.** Jagannatha, who is the king of mantras — that truth is 'Hare Rama Krishna'.  
**4.112.** [He] who is in the form of Bala-Mukunda — that is the Gopala mantra.  
**4.113.** Even if people [merely] receive the instruction in the Shri king of mantras,  
**4.114.** they at once obtain the increase (?) of rare (?) bhakti in this way (?).  
**4.115.** When [the Name] sounds (?) in a loud voice (?) among animals, insects (?) [and] worms (?),  
**4.116.** by [its] power their bhakti grows; at the end they attain the supreme goal (?).[^kirtana-4-115]  
**4.117.** The external (?) state is [the state] of the supreme (??) bija (??); the state of absorption (?) is the ardha-matra.  
**4.118.** The principal state that is spoken of — that is Shri Jagannatha's body.  
**4.119.** These five states are Jagannatha; hence (?) [He] is called the Pancha-tattva.  
**4.120.** Such is the unfolding (?) of the king of mantras, and such [is] the Harinama:  
**4.121.** the state of the jiva is [the name] 'Hari'; the inner state is called 'Rama';  
**4.122.** the supreme state is called 'Krishna'; 'Hare' is the external state;  
**4.123.** and the principal state that is spoken of is the form of the mantra 'Om'.[^avastha-4-117]  
**4.124.** Fifty crores [of yojanas] — the load of the earth — [rest] on Ananta's head.  
**4.125.** In the midst of that are five kroshas: Purushottama, the abode of bhakti.[^ananta-4-124]  
**4.126.** Dharma, artha, kama [and] moksha — [all] perfection Purushottama grants.  
**4.127.** Whether one desires bhakti [or] liberation, for beings (?) [it] is fulfilled by the power [of this place].  
**4.128.** Do not strive (??) for dharma, artha, kama [and] moksha: [they come from] the perfection of this city.  
**4.129.** At the end (?) all the perfections, as many as there are, perforce dance (?), having come to the feet.  
**4.130.** They say: 'We shall come to your feet; we shall become [your] siddhis in Purushottama.'[^siddhi-4-126]  

*Shloka*  
**4.131.** *[Skt.]* "Purushottama is the best (??) and the chief of kshetras on earth; [in it are] likewise Mathura, Dvaraka, Ayodhya, Kashi [and] Vrindavana."  
**4.132.** Whoever abides (??) in Purushottama, being born in the kshetra, obtains the truth.  
**4.133.** Being born from this kshetra, at the end, having passed away (?), they obtain liberation (?).  
**4.134.** Mathura, Dvaraka and Kashi, Ayodhya, Vrindavan — [are here] together (?).[^kshetra-4-131]  

*Shloka*  
**4.135.** *[Skt.]* "North of the Kalpavriksha, beside [it], is Shri Jagannatha … (??). Those who enter the middle region — a man (?) will obtain bhakti and liberation."  
**4.136.** The Kalpavriksha [and] Jagannatha: the devotee who enters between [them] —  
**4.137.** whether he entered (?) knowingly or unknowingly — into him bhakti enters, even by mistake (?).[^kalpavata-4-135]  
**4.138.** The unknowing obtains prema-bhakti; the knowing obtains every [kind of] bhakti.  
**4.139.** This [place] surpasses in all qualities; its [Lord] is the Lord of all kshetras.  
**4.140.** Since Brahma [and] Shiva (?) know [this], they therefore contemplate this city.  
**4.141.** Vyasa, Narada and the others, as many as there are, contemplate this city in the heart (?).  
**4.142.** Whoever does not accept this understanding — know him to be born an animal.  
**4.143.** One whose desire for the Name is gone (?) [and who], leaving (?) this city, desires something else —  
**4.144.** the Creator will write him down — has written (?) him down for birth as an asura.  
**4.145.** I (?), leaving (?) all kshetras [and] tirthas, abide (lit. 'abides') in Purushottama-kshetra.  
**4.146.** Who is the greatest fool, so foolish [as to] leave this kshetra [and] go elsewhere?[^asura-4-143]  
**4.147.** Pushkara and the other tirthas, as many as there are, all abide in the ocean.  
**4.148.** All intelligence [and] perfections — [these are granted by] Yameshvara (?), the one who … (??).  
**4.149.** Mastery of all Vedic learning [is granted by] Ganesha, who abides beneath the banyan.[^yameshvara-4-148]  

## The Goddesses of Purushottama; Mahaprabhu's Ashtaka before Sarvabhauma; End of the Chapter


**4.150.** With all words of praise Sarasvati abides [here] (?).[^devi-4-150][^intro-4-150]  
**4.151.** Over pleas (?) of the heart's pain that Vimalakshi holds charge.  
**4.152.** With all sovereignty Varuna's daughter abides [here].  
**4.153.** With dominion (?) [abides] Mangala (?), through whom [comes] the wooden body [of the Deities].[^mangala-4-153]  
**4.154.** A being who becomes wise — why (?) would he go to other kshetras?  
**4.155.** He whom (?) the Creator … (??) wanders in the ocean of samsara.[^vidhata-4-155]  
**4.156.** Before Sarvabhauma Prabhu Chaitanya uttered  
**4.157.** the longing (??) for Krishna-prema — the ashtaka from Prabhu's holy mouth (?).[^ashtaka-4-157]  
**4.158.** All the Vaishnavas heard [it], [and] Jagannatha Dasa, who were [there].  
**4.159.** They heard [the words] from Prabhu's holy mouth [and] pondered these verses in their hearts.  
**4.160.** The brahmin Divakara says, bowing his head at Chaitanya's feet:  
**4.161.** "Pandits (?), do not hold it against me: I am the servant of your servants."[^bhanita-4-161]  

*Thus [ends], in the Sri Jagannathamrita, [the life of] Sri Ati Bada Jagannatha Gosain, the fourth chapter, called 'Anukramani' (?).*[^colophon-4-161]

The heading 'ଚତୁର୍ଥ ଅଧ୍ୟାୟ' is separated from the colophon by a printer's ornament. On Divakara Dasa, the Atibadi line and the Gaudiya Vaishnava position see Ch. 1, the note to 1.1. [^svami-4-2]: The Swami is Jagannatha Dasa, as the Odia authors call him (cf. VL). Printed ସ୍ୱାନିଙ୍କି: ସ୍ୱା + ନି + ଙ୍କି. Understood as ସ୍ୱାମିଙ୍କି (ନ for ମ), with the ending ‑ṅki for ‑ṅku. According to the CC (Madhya 6), Mahaprabhu met Sarvabhauma immediately on arriving in Puri. On seeing Jagannatha He fell unconscious in the temple, and Sarvabhauma had Him carried to his house. Jagannatha Dasa does not appear in that account. In Divakara the meeting with Sarvabhauma comes after the departure of the Gaudiyas (Ch. 3), and Mahaprabhu comes to him together with Jagannatha Dasa.
[^sarvabhauma-4-4]: Here Divakara is close to the CC (Madhya 6): Sarvabhauma Bhattacharya is a great scholar and teacher of Vedanta, proud of his learning. Mahaprabhu turned him to bhakti by explaining the *ātmārāma* verse in eighteen ways and showed him His six-armed form.
[^intro-4-6]: Continuation of Chapter 4: PDF pages 24–25 (printed pp. 23–24). These two pages hold eight Sanskrit shlokas, each followed by an Odia paraphrase. The sources of the shlokas have not been found. The Sanskrit is irregular in places, and several words are read uncertainly. Each shloka is preceded by an unnumbered "ଶ୍ଲୋକଃ" (I1–I8). Shloka 4.25 ends in print with a single "।", the others with "॥". Features of the type. A bold ଆ with a flag occurs in ଆଶେ (4.6, rhyming with ଦାସେ), ଆଇଲେ, ଆତ୍ମା, ଆସ୍ୱାଦ. The distinction between ଅ and ଆ is unreliable, so ଅ/ଆ is nowhere corrected. The sign ି is not visible in ତିଷ୍ଠତି (4.22), ନିରତେ (4.17), ସ୍ଥିତି (4.24), ମୂରତି (4.27); such ି are given without brackets. A two-loop sign occurs in ତ୍ରିଲୋକଂ (4.15, with a ି arc) and is taken as ତ୍ର. The same sign occurs in ପାତ୍ରେ (4.11) and ପାତ୍ରେଣ (4.13). In other words ତ୍ର has its ordinary form (ସ୍ତତ୍ରୈବ, କ୍ଷେତ୍ର). ଚା and ରୀ resemble ସ୍ୱ (ପଚାରି, ଲହରୀ). The ligature ତୁ resembles ଭୁ (ତୁମ୍ଭର, ତୁମ୍ଭେ).
[^sarvabhauma-4-7]: Mahaprabhu has come to Sarvabhauma, and Jagannatha Dasa listens nearby (4.6). Sarvabhauma bows at once and glorifies the Lord (4.8–4.10).
