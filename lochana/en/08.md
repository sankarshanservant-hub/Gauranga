# Sutra-khanda, part 8 (portion 08)

*Narada glorifies Gaura, who sends him to Balarama in Shvetadvipa; the Lord reveals to His associates why He will be born on earth; Narada with Balarama, who on hearing the command bids his own to take birth on earth; the descent of the associates — Advaita, Nityananda, Sita and many others; praise of Narahari Sarakara, Raghunandana and Mukunda Dasa; the author's humility; the end of the Sutra-khanda*

**531.** The sage says: "Mahaprabhu, such a wonder I have never
seen nor heard of.
Today my life has borne fruit — I have seen a treasury of nectar,
and I count myself blessed, blessed.

**532.** Brahma and the others do not know the truth; Your coming is unknown [to them];
I call You inconceivable.
Some call [You] effulgence — but even they cannot express [You] in words:
there is no comparison to tell it.

**533.** Some say, 'Higher than the highest, the chief, the best Purusha' —
but by reasoning they cannot determine [You].
Seeing that Your shakti is in all, they do not attain liberation;
Your deeds are beyond reach.

**534.** Thousand-headed Ananta, finding no end to [Your] virtues,
has taken two tongues in every mouth;
finding no limit to Your virtues — such a Lord are You, Gaura —
by good fortune I have seen You!

**535.** And those who long [for You], following Your feet,
are of various understanding, not of one opinion:
some say, 'The All-pervading' — these are the Sankhya-yogis who speak of the subtle;
the devotees serve [Your] visible form.

**536.** Others, following the Vedas, perform the eternal dharma and rites,
adhering to the dharma of varna and ashrama;
and the conclusion of Vedanta I do not find [with them]:
not understanding, they say many things.

**537.** Why there is contradiction among them they do not consider,
and again they say, 'All is one, non-dual'.
Not understanding Your heart, each holding to his side, they perform their rites —
Your word is unknown to all.

**538.** Now by the grace of [Your] feet my heart weeps unceasingly:
leaving this perishable form,
may I be born again, make Krishna my whole world
and practise this prema-bhakti."[^1]

**539.** Hearing such words of Narada, the Jewel of virtues says:
"Go, go, go, king of sages!
I shall deliver the people of Kali, spread devotion to Myself,
I shall be born among the people of Nadia.

**540.** Go [down] to the earth: in Shvetadvipa I abide
[in the form of] a brother named Balarama;
Ananta is his portion, the eleven Rudras are his line,
Lord Mahesha serves him.[^2]

**541.** With his beloved Revati he dwells in joyful play
amid the land in the Ocean of Milk;
whatever avataras there are, he alone is their helper;
in every work of Mine I place him first.

**542.** Go, go, king of sages, make this task known to him,
tell him all in due order:
'Taking all your portions, go and take birth on earth,
and bear the name Nityananda.'"

**543.** In bliss Narada Muni, hearing the Lord's words,
cries "Haribol!" from the joy of his heart.
Says Lochana Dasa: whoever hears the conversation of these two,
in him a wave of bliss arises.

*Raga dhanashi, short metre; disha: "I give myself to the crimson lotus feet! Go, go, give out prema, madden the world with prema!"*

**544.** Having sent Narada off, the Lord sat down,
and the words of His heart sprang up like a shoot.

**545.** For what reason He will be born on earth —
I shall tell the essence; listen, all, attentively.

**546.** Gathering His own, the Lord speaks of Himself:
the Supreme Lord of lords is thinking of the earth.

**547.** On the right is Radhika, on the left the goddess Sri Rukmini,
nearest to them all the chief companions of play,

**548.** after them all the dear associates,
after them all the other followers.

**549.** Longing to hear the dear words of the Lord of their life,
hundreds of thousands of eyes [are fixed] on one beautiful face:

**550.** like many chakoras hoping for one moon,
they drink heaps of nectar from the shining face.

**551.** "Yuga after yuga I take birth on earth
to deliver the righteous and to protect dharma.[^3]

**552.** I establish dharma — but no one understands,
and sin grows ever more: what a calamity!

**553.** More than in the Satya-yuga sin grows in the Treta,
in the Dvapara still more: great is the sorrow!

**554.** Kali is pitch darkness, not a trace of dharma is there;
seeing the sufferings of all, I am filled with compassion.

**555.** My descent is for the destruction of adharma;
if adharma grows again, what then is My work?

**556.** Knowing this, compassion arose in My heart:
I shall take birth to reveal My prema.

**557.** Revealing such rare prema-bhakti,
I shall make people understand, discerning dharma from adharma.

**558.** I shall be born in Navadvipa, of Shachi,
near the Ganga, in the house of Jagannatha Mishra.

**559.** The other avataras are not like this avatara:
they come to earth to slay the asuras.

**560.** Huge great asuras, with My great weapons,
in great battles I struck down and crushed.

**561.** But now all people are asuras in their hearts:
they cannot be cut by a sword — what can be done by force of arms?

**562.** The name, the virtues, sankirtana — this is the Vaishnava's power;
and further I shall reveal prema-bhakti for Myself.

**563.** In this way I shall destroy the sin of Kali;
come, all, do not consider who goes before and who after.

**564.** Now, taking the sharp sword of the sankirtana of the name,
I shall cut down the asuras within the hearts of living beings.

**565.** If a sinner, abandoning dharma, goes to a distant land,
My generals, the devotees, will go there.

**566.** With My prema I shall flood this whole universe
and not leave a single drop of grief and sorrow.

**567.** I shall flood the moving and the unmoving, and the gods."
Hearing this, joyfully speaks this servant Lochana.

*Raga varadi, tripadi metre*

**568.** Narada Muni set out, the sound of his vina arose;
his hands and feet no longer obey him;
he walks but does not see the way — tears of prema flood his eyes —
he staggers as if drunk.

**569.** He goes two or four steps and falls again on the same spot,
half uttering the name of the Lord;
with great effort he rises, clutching at the ground,[^4]
and a river of tears flows from his eyes.

**570.** Now great madness, cries of "hum!", a lion's roar,
in his heart meditation on Gora's form;
he has no outer awareness, does not tell himself from others —
in all there is only the awareness of Gaura.

**571.** Like the splendour of millions of suns — such is the radiance of his body;
Narada flies through the sky
and descended at that abode where Lord Balarama is —
Shvetadvipa was lit up.

**572.** Stopping at the city's bounds, he looks in amazement on every side:
the radiance of hundreds of thousands of moons;
a soft, soft breeze blows, [bearing] the divine fragrance of flowers,
pearls hang at every gate.

**573.** All the people there are in sattva; there is no old age, death or sorrow;
everyone is the friend of all;
wherever one looks, all the people are lovely;
the Ocean of Milk is full of Baladeva.

**574.** Seeing this, Narada Muni says in his mind, "Blessed, blessed!"
and counts himself blessed, blessed:
"The Lord of the three worlds, the Master, I shall see with my eyes
and fall weeping at His feet.

**575.** This Lord Balarama is the helper yuga after yuga:
making him [His helper], Krishna descends;
playing games of every kind, in endless joyful lilas
He destroys the asuras.

**576.** This Lord Balarama, by his portions in three abodes,[^5]
abides and serves Krishna with love;
at the beginning, the middle and the end — his portion is Ananta,
who holds the earth upon one hood.

**577.** Himself being the Lord, dwelling in Shvetadvipa,
he enjoys pastimes in many ways;
his highest fulfilment is service
to that Mahaprabhu, in wondrous ways.

**578.** When [the Lord] walks he is the umbrella, when He sits the seat and garments,
when He lies down the bed;
at the dissolution the banyan leaf, in great battle the divine weapon:
in many forms he serves.

**579.** With one portion he serves, with another portion he holds the earth —
such is my Lord Balarama.
The supreme King of the three worlds I shall see amid the Ocean of Milk
and convey to him the Lord's command."

**580.** These two Lords are like a king and his chief minister:
with one counsel they protect the earth;
and all the others of the Rudras' line — they too, portions of his portions —
will descend to earth.

**581.** Absorbed in the rasa of these thoughts, the sage, beside himself,
entered the city in great bliss
and beheld the Lord of the three worlds with all His associates —
the wondrous moon-like Balarama.

**582.** Like a mountain of nectar he sits on a white throne,[^6]
smiling softly, sweet as nectar;
his eyes red lotuses, seen drooping languidly,
half-words come from his lips.

**583.** The pupils, like bees, are half covered [by the lids],
both eyes half drowsy;
gems, pearls, coral, divine necklaces of jewels —
the limbs cannot be seen for the ornaments.

**584.** Leaning idly on a pillow, his left hand under his head,
with his right he holds Revati's hand;
Revati, betel in hand, puts it to his lips
and gazes lovingly at his face.

**585.** Maidservants on every side, smiling, wave chamaras;
the tinkling of bracelets and girdle-bells is heard;
some play the vina and the flute, some sing,
the loveliest women keep the beat.

**586.** Beyond them are hundreds and hundreds of followers,
each at his appointed service.
At that moment the sage sounded his vina,
and the Lord suddenly saw him.

**587.** Overwhelmed, Narada Muni staggers and falls to the ground;
the Lord lifted him and took him on His lap,
and, with long-held love, looked on that great fortunate one
and comforted him with cool, great words.

**588.** Greeting him with a smile, the Lord says: "Where have you come from?
I feel you will tell [Me] a secret.
Tell Me, what is the task? As I listen, within My heart
bliss rises beyond counting."

**589.** Respectfully the sage says: "What can I know how to say?
You, Lord, are the Indweller of all.
What I can say, I shall say as I understand;
do what is fitting, Lord.

**590.** 'In the sinful Kali-yuga I see no deliverance for people' —
compassion arose in the Lord's heart —
'to protect the devotees and establish dharma
I shall be born on earth.

**591.** For the destruction of adharma, what other dharma is there?' —
so I understood from His bearing and signs.
He commanded me to make the proclamation,
and on hearing it people rejoiced.

**592.** With the bhava of Radha within and the colour of Radha without,
filled with Radha inside and out,
with His friends and girl-companions and endless devotees
He will madden the whole world with the bhava of Vraja.[^7]

**593.** 'With limbs, sub-limbs and associates take birth on earth
and bear the name Nityananda' —
the heart of His deeds and form is not unknown to you —
this is the command that Gaurachandra spoke."

**594.** Hearing this, Lord Balarama looks around in bliss
and laughs aloud with great peals;
again and again he roars "hum!", displays wonders,
forgets himself in the bliss of prema.

**595.** He commanded his own: "Go to the earth
to carry out the Lord's command.
Go, Narada: I shall take birth
and make the unseen seen."

**596.** Such a wondrous tale, the story of Gaura's virtues — hear it,
all of you, be attentive:
the essence of all avataras is Gora, the avatara of Kali —
consider this, all, in your minds.

**597.** Holding a straw between my teeth, with a contrite heart I say:
do not neglect Gora's virtues;
do not give your mind to worldly life, love Krishna —
this is the raft for crossing the ocean of worldly existence.

**598.** What has never been — that Gora-avatara
will be fully manifest;
the lifeless will gain life, the blind will discern the path —
so sings His virtues this Lochana Dasa.

*Raga bhatiyari; disha: "Brothers, sing, sing the tale of the virtues of Nitai and Chaitanya!"*

**599.** When Mahaprabhu had so commanded,
all took birth by their portions.

**600.** Mahesha Thakura went forth before all:
he was born in a brahmana family, named Kamalaksha.

**601.** By study and hearing he became accomplished in virtue
and received the title "Advaita Acharya".[^8]

**602.** This great Maheshvara is endowed with sattva —
yet the world proclaims him [the embodiment of] tamo-guna.

**603.** No one considers what he is within and without:
seeing his outer conduct, they say "tamo-guna".

**604.** [Shiva] is Krishna's very self; their name is Hari-Hara;
the worldly tamo-guna is set aside among his qualities.[^9]

**605.** Whoever, taking him for a worldly devotee, calls him "tamoguni" —
when I know of him, I shall call him low, a petty man.

**606.** How can you say that Hari-Hara is tamo-guna?
Show no disrespect, if you listen to my words.

**607.** Reflect in your minds and consider:
this is why I say that Gora is the essence of all avataras.

**608.** In all the avataras the companion of His play,
Balarama, took birth on this earth —

**609.** in a brahmana family, as befits the dharma of the yuga:
the eternal (*nitya*) root of bliss (*ananda*) is his innate form.

**610.** One portion of him bears a thousand hoods,
and on one hood holds the earth to preserve creation.

**611.** Balarama was born of Padmavati;
his father was Hado Ojha, by name also Paramananda.[^10]

**612.** His father and mother named him Kuvera Pandita;
in the ashrama of sannyasa he is Nityananda of wondrous deeds.[^11]

**613.** Under an auspicious conjunction, on the bright thirteenth of the month of Magha,
he took birth on earth in great joy.

**614.** Katyayani took birth on earth
and bears the name Sita in the society of brahmanas.

**615.** She lives together with Advaita Thakura,
and the two together reveal prema-bhakti.

**616.** I am small of mind — what do I know of who is what?
How can I expound whose avatara anyone is?

**617.** Even what I have heard with my ears from the mouths of mahantas
I cannot repeat: my heart shrinks back.

**618.** I have no power to determine it —
I only name those who are.

**619.** Let no one consider in his mind who is named before and who after:
for the sake of the metre the book keeps no order [of rank].

**620.** Shachi-devi and Jagannatha Mishra Purandara,
in whose house the Lord Himself took birth;

**621.** Gopinatha by name, and Kashi Mishra Thakura,[^12]
on the path approved by Chaitanya, in abundant bliss;

**622.** Pandita Sri Gadadhara, Gadadhara Dasa,
Murari, Mukunda Datta and Srinivasa;

**623.** Raya Ramananda and Vasudeva Datta,
Haridasa Thakura and Govinda the attendant;

**624.** Ishvara [Puri], Madhava Puri and Vishnupuri,
Vakreshvara, Paramananda Puri of pure conduct;

**625.** Pandita Jagadananda and Vishnupriya,
Raghava Pandita and others came to earth;

**626.** Ramadasa, Gauridasa and Sundara,
Krishnadasa, Purushottama, Sri Kamalakara,

**627.** Kala Krishnadasa and Uddharana Datta —
the twelve cowherd boys (*gopalas*) of Vraja: such is their greatness;[^13]

**628.** Parameshvara Dasa and Vrindavana Dasa,
Kashishvara, Srila Rupa and Sanatana appeared;

**629.** Govinda, Madhava Ghosha and Vasu Ghosha —
all came together and spread [bhakti] over the earth.

**630.** Together with Damodara Pandita, five brothers
took birth on earth in one place.[^14]

**631.** Purandara Pandita and Paramananda Vaidya —
all who were, from the first to the last, came to earth.

**632.** Sri Narahari Dasa is my Thakura:
of his deeds I shall say something in particular.

**633.** What can I know how to tell of his deeds?
[I tell] only what I grasp by the power of my mind.

**634.** Let no one harbour any resentment:
I bow at the feet of my own guru,

**635.** by the grace of whose feet I, such a wretch,
tell you all of the Lord's virtues.

**636.** Sri Narahari Dasa is my Thakura,
whose power is the glory of the vaidya lineage.

**637.** Unchecked is his love for Krishna, his body is full of Krishna;
to his followers he teaches nothing but prema.

**638.** His heart aches with compassion for countless beings,
his soul is ever restless with love for Krishna.

**639.** His body seems moulded of the rasa of Radha and Krishna:
bhavas rise in him, now this, now that.

**640.** In the rasa of Radha and Krishna lies his pure fame;[^15]
he dwells in the land of Shrikhanda.

**641.** "Narahari of Chaitanya" — so this master is renowned;
apart from his feet I have no other refuge.

**642.** Now Krishna, now Radha — rapt in their bhavas,
he displays the rasa of Radha and Krishna embodied.

**643.** On the path approved by Chaitanya his judgement is pure;
peerless and full of rasa are his bhavas, [as of] all the avataras.

**644.** To all Vaishnavas due honour and love;
through all the world his pure fame.

**645.** In Vrindavana he was named Madhumati,
a dear companion of Radha, a treasury of sweetness.[^16]

**646.** Now, in the Kali age, Narahari, companion of Gaura,
is the keeper of the treasury of the prema of Radha and Krishna.

**647.** His nephew, Sri Raghunandana Thakura,
is widely proclaimed throughout the world.

**648.** He who fed the Sri Murti a sweet ball (*laddu*) —[^17]
what fool would think him of little understanding?

**649.** A Vaishnava by nature is beyond the bounds of varna;
he who converses with Krishna has Krishna alone.[^18]

**650.** He whose constant vow is converse with the Sri Murti —
how can you know what he is, what his greatness is?

**651.** He to whom Chaitanya said, "You are My life",
whom Abhirama Gosvami revealed,

**652.** making known that he was the avatara of Madana —
and all saw him so on Chaitanya's lap.[^19]

**653.** When he dances absorbed in Krishna he enchants the world's heart;
he has no "mine" and "another's" — the same love for all.

**654.** Always sweet words come from his lips;
no one ever heard a harsh word from him.

**655.** Skill, sweetness, lila, play, loveliness —
his body, full of rasa, is blessed in this world.

**656.** His father is the wise Sri Mukunda Dasa,
pure in faith on the path approved by Chaitanya.

**657.** Seeing a peacock feather in the king's presence,
he fell senseless, the form of Krishna drawing his mind.[^20]

**658.** Who knows what the rasa of Chaitanya's companions is?
Ananta and the others know it — His limbs and companions.

**659.** Can [an ordinary] being see the glory of Krishna?
He alone sees in whom there is realization of Krishna.

**660.** What more shall I say? All His warriors and associates
came to earth — how many shall I name?

**661.** If I could measure the water of the ocean with a pitcher,
if I could count the dust of the earth grain by grain,

**662.** if I could count the stars of the sky —
still I could not describe the avatara of Gora.

**663.** I am very small of mind — what more shall I say?
A fool, I venture to reason on the Vedas.

**664.** As a blind man without sight wants to see a divine jewel,
as a dwarf stretches out his arms to catch the moon,

**665.** as a lame man boasts that he will stride across the earth,
as a tiny ant wants to carry a mountain —

**666.** such a desire has arisen in my heart:
to proclaim the tale of the avatara of Gora.

**667.** With joined palms I say: listen, all!
Gora makes this mute one eloquent.

**668.** The tongueless speaks clear and sweet words,
the unlettered fool tells the tale of Brahman.

**669.** Great, great devotees, born on earth,
make manifest the secret words of Krishna.

**670.** Without cause they are merciful to all beings,
as a mother nurses a wayward son.

**671.** Seeing such boundless mercy of the Lord,
I, though low, long for nectar.

**672.** Sri Narahari Dasa, whose body is mercy itself,
seeing a sinner, has boundless mercy and love.

**673.** Me, an incorrigible sinner, blind, of evil conduct,
seeing me helpless, he showed mercy.

**674.** By the power of his mercy and by the grace of the Vaishnavas —
in this I trust — the book will be completed without hindrance.

**675.** With joined palms I say in a humble voice:
I give myself to the feet of the Vaishnavas.

**676.** There is no one on earth lower than I;
by the power of the Vaishnavas' mercy may the work succeed.

**677.** Holding a straw between his teeth, this Lochana Dasa
bows and entreats: fulfil my hope!

**678.** The book of the Sutra-khanda is ended — listen, all:
now in the Adi-khanda I shall tell of the descent.

**679.** The telling of the "sutra" is ended; now come the pastimes of prema —
with joy in his heart says this Lochana Dasa.

*Thus ends the Sutra-khanda of the* Sri Chaitanya-mangala *composed by Sri Lochana Dasa Thakura.*

[^1]: Narada, the Lord's eternal associate, speaks thus out of humility, as a devotee who longs for service.
[^2]: In the 1983 edition line 1 reads: "Go, Narada: in Shvetadvipa I abide." Balarama is the Lord's second body; according to Gaudiya teaching, from him (as Sankarshana) come Ananta-Shesha, and Shiva with the Rudras (*Chaitanya-charitamrita*, Adi 5).
[^3]: Cf. *Bhagavad-gita* 4.8.
[^4]: The line is damaged in A ("ধরিয়া ব্রণী-কোডি"); after the 1983 edition, "leaning on the earth".
[^5]: Probably the three abodes of the Purusha-avataras — Karanodakashayi, Garbhodakashayi and Kshirodakashayi Vishnu — who, according to the *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Adi 5), are expansions of Sankarshana-Balarama.
[^6]: "Mountain of nectar" after the 1903 edition ("অমৃত-পর্বত"); in A and the 1983 edition the first word is corrupt.
[^7]: Cf. *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Adi 4.55 ff.): Krishna appears as Gaura, taking the bhava and lustre of Radha, to taste her love.
[^8]: Lochana Dasa calls Advaita an avatara of Mahesha (Shiva). According to the *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Adi 6.4–27) Advaita is Maha-Vishnu and also Sada-Shiva; Bhaktisiddhanta in the summary of this section says: "Shiva [appears] as Advaita Prabhu."
[^9]: The line admits two readings: *parakrita* (Sanskrit "rejected, set aside") — tamo-guna is set aside in him; or *prakrita* ("worldly") — the worldly tamo-guna [appears only] among his outward qualities. The thought is the same in either case: Shiva (and Advaita) is not under the sway of tamas; cf. *Bhag.* 12.13.16: "Shambhu is the best of Vaishnavas."
[^10]: Nityananda's father is usually called Hadai Pandita (Hadai Ojha), and his mother Padmavati. The name "Paramananda" is Lochana Dasa's.
[^11]: The 1983 edition has "with renunciation in his heart — Nityananda of wondrous deeds". The name "Kuvera Pandita" is found only in Lochana Dasa.
[^12]: Probably Gopinatha Acharya, Sarvabhauma's brother-in-law, and Kashi Mishra, in whose house the Lord lived at Puri; the line can also be read as one person: "Kashi Mishra Thakura, named Gopinatha".
[^13]: Cf. the *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Adi 11) and the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika*: the twelve gopalas are cowherd boys of Vraja who appeared as associates of Nityananda (among them Abhirama, Sundarananda, Gauridasa Pandita, Kala Krishnadasa, Purushottama, Kamalakara, Uddharana Datta, Parameshvara Dasa and others).
[^14]: The sense is unclear: perhaps Damodara Pandita and his brothers (so in A and in the 1983 edition).
[^15]: In A the beginning of the line is corrupt ("ক্ষণে…"); in the 1983 edition stanzas 640–644 are in a different order and lack the second line of v. 641.
[^16]: So also the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* (177): Narahari Sarakara is Madhumati, a companion of Radha in Vraja.
[^17]: A well-known tradition: the boy Raghunandana, entrusted by his father with the worship, persuaded the Deity of Gopinatha at Shrikhanda to eat a laddu, and the Deity ate it.
[^18]: The line also admits the reading "he is Krishna himself"; we follow the understanding consistent with Gaudiya teaching: the devotee does not become Krishna, but for him there is Krishna alone.
[^19]: Abhirama Thakura (Ramadasa), one of the twelve gopalas, is said to have recognized the Lord's eternal associates in people. "Madana" is Kamadeva; according to the *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika* Raghunandana is Pradyumna (Kandarpa) of Dvaraka.
[^20]: Cf. *Chaitanya-charitamrita* (Madhya 15.121–125): Mukunda Dasa, a court physician, on seeing a fan of peacock feathers above the king's head, remembered Krishna and fell senseless.
