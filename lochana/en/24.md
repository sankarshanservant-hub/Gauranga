# Madhya-khanda, part 1 (portion 24)

*The beginning of the Madhya-khanda: the Lord teaches His students that true knowledge is devotion to Krishna; in the mood of Radha He weeps in separation, and by His word Shachi attains prema; prema in the house of Shuklambara; the Lord's associates gather; the sound of the flute and the mood of Gandharvika; the heavenly voice: "You are Ishvara Himself"; the mood of the Boar in Murari's house, the talk about the Vedas, the instruction to worship Radha and Krishna, Murari sees Rama; the explanation of the verse on the holy name*

## Prema-dana-lila (The Lord bestows prema)

> *Summary by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.* The author, having praised the good fortune of those who were Sriman Mahaprabhu's students in His lila of teaching, describes the lila of the Lord's mercy to mother Shachi. One day, when Sriman Mahaprabhu was resting in His house, He suddenly, Himself absorbed in the mood of Srimati Radhika in separation [when Krishna had gone] to Mathura, began to weep, crying: "O Krishna, O Krishna!" Hearing the weeping, Shachi-devi came there and begged Gaurasundara for prema, and Gaurasundara bestowed prema on her. Then in the house of Shuklambara Brahmachari He enacted the lila of the great manifestation of prema. Bereft of all outward awareness, He wept all the time, crying "O Krishna!"; and hearing someone sing the name of Krishna, He fainted in bliss. Moment by moment the eight sattvika-bhavas arose all over His body, and anubhavas such as dancing, singing and rolling on the ground appeared. Gaurasundara, though Bhagavan Himself, accepted the mood of a devotee and revealed the glory of bhakti and of the devotees — therefore He is the crown jewel of all avataras.
>
> When Sriman Mahaprabhu began the lila of preaching prema, the devotees headed by Gadadhara and devotees from various lands and countries all gathered together. By Gaurahari's mercy all were maddened with prema. One day the Lord was walking with Srivasa Pandita and his brothers, and suddenly, hearing the sound of Krishna's flute, He attained the state that Srimati Gandharvika had attained and, like a madman, began to display loud laughter, weeping, silence, humility and other anubhavas. Then a heavenly voice resounded: "O Vishvambhara! You are Bhagavan Himself, descended to the earth to preach prema." When in Murari's house the Lord manifested the form of the Boar, Murari praised Him and begged the Lord for prema; the Lord told him to worship the son of the king of Vraja, served by the cowherd men and women. When Murari wished to see the form of Ramachandra, the Lord showed him that form and glorified the greatness of the name of Krishna. Further are described the coming of Brahma and the other gods to Gaura with prayers for prema, their attaining prema by Gaura's mercy and their dancing with cries of "O Radha, O Govinda!", the mercy to Shuklambara Brahmachari, the garland from the Lord's body given to Gadadhara, the wondrous beauty of the pair Gaura and Gadadhara, and so on[^1].

*Raga karuna-shri*

**1.** Glory to You, Lord of the life of Narahari and Gadadhara!
Be merciful, Lord — cast on us Your auspicious glance.

**2.** The Adi-khanda is ended — the beginning of the Madhya-khanda:
whoever hears it will at once obtain the treasure of prema.

**3.** I tell the story of the Madhya-khanda — the essence of nectar,
where are the pastimes in Nadia and the preaching of prema,

**4.** where the sinners Jagai and Madhai were delivered,
where prema, rare even for Brahma, was given to one and all,

**5.** where the sankirtana of the name of Hari was manifested,
where — for the deliverance of the fallen — sannyasa.

**6.** I shall tell all this — morsels of nectar:
whoever hears it, from his heart godlessness will vanish.

**7.** Having returned to Nadia, the Lord with a joyful heart
lives happily together with His relatives and friends.

**8.** All the brahmana youths living in Navadvipa
are born in good families and very pure in conduct.

**9.** Great is their fortune, they are blessed in the three worlds:
the Thakura Himself bestowed knowledge on them.

**10.** One day Gaurahari, the Lord, showing His favour,
said to all His students:

**11.** "Study the one true reality — the feet of Krishna:
that is knowledge in which there is the mark of devotion to Hari.

**12.** Without it, say the shastras, all is ignorance;
without devotion to Radha and Krishna no one is our companion.

**13.** By pride of learning, birth and wealth Krishna is not attained,
but by devotion one easily attains the Lord of the Yadus.

**14.** Consider: Krishna is subdued by the rasa of devotion" —
saying this, He recites a shloka in accordance with the shastras.

*Thus in the Padyavali (the words of a southern poet):*

**15.** "What was the conduct of the hunter, what the age of Dhruva, what the learning of Gajendra, what the name and beauty of Kubja, what the wealth of Sudama,
what the lineage of Vidura, what the prowess of Ugrasena, lord of the Yadavas? Madhava, who loves devotion, is pleased by devotion alone, not by qualities"[^2].

**16.** Thus the Thakura taught His students,
[intending] to reveal His prema — abundant bliss.

**17.** One day, lying in His house,
He weeps, overwhelmed by the bliss of love for Krishna.

**18.** Agitated by the mood of Radha, the Lord calls out
and in separation from [Krishna gone to] Mathura beats His breast with His hand:

**19.** "Ah, Akrura! You have carried off my Krishna!" —
saying this, the Lord weeps and laments.

**20.** "Kubja, with her base mind, has taken my Krishna —
the cheat in love, the rake, the thief of young women's hearts!"[^3]

**21.** Saying this, the Lord weeps and cries aloud;
His body thrills, His hair stands on end — a wondrous bhava.

**22.** Astonished, Shachi asks Vishvambhara:
"Why are You weeping, my son? What is Your sorrow?"

**23.** Hearing His mother's words, the Lord gave no answer
and weeps, beside Himself with bliss.

**24.** Then Shachi-devi, reflecting within herself,
understood by the signs: this is prema — Krishna's grace.

**25.** Great is Shachi's fortune — she knows the whole truth;
before her son she speaks sweet words:

**26.** "Listen, listen, my son, my golden son:
I see in You a wondrous [treasure], rare in the world.

**27.** Wherever You went, whatever wealth You obtained,
You brought it and gave it to me.

**28.** In Gaya You obtained such wealth — love for Krishna,
a thing rare even for the gods, a priceless jewel.

**29.** If there is any compassion for me in Your heart,
give me the wealth of love for Krishna — this I ask."

**30.** When Shachi-devi spoke such words,
the Lord's heart melted, and He looked [at her]:

**31.** "By the mercy of the Vaishnavas you will obtain prema, mother —
know it for certain: I am telling you.

**31+1.** The Vaishnava gosanis can give prema and take it away;
apart from them no one else can give prema"[^4].

**32.** Hearing these words, Shachi was overjoyed
and at once, unexpectedly, obtained prema-bhakti.

**33.** Her whole body thrills, her body trembles,
streams of tears flow ceaselessly from her eyes.

**34.** "Krishna, Krishna!" she calls, her heart rejoicing. —
Thus Lochana tells of Gora's first manifestation.

*Raga shri*

**35.** Then the Lord Vishvambhara, overwhelmed with love, —
[in Navadvipa] there lived a brahmana, the brahmachari Shuklambara, —

**36.** in his house the Lord weeps, lost in prema;
streams of tears flow ceaselessly from His eyes.

**37.** Mucus flows ceaselessly from His nose,
and the brahmana Shuklambara keeps wiping it away.

**38.** Rolling on the ground, He weeps day and night;
at dusk, beside Himself, He asks [the time]:

**39.** in the daytime the Lord asks: "How much of the night has passed?"
All say: "It is day now, not night."

**40.** Thus the Lord, overpowered by prema,
weeps again, helpless with bliss.

**41.** When a watch of the night has passed, He asks, [thinking it] day;
"It is not yet day," say all who are near.

**42.** Lost in prema, He does not know day from night;
hearing the name of Krishna from anyone's lips, He falls to the ground.

**43.** If anyone sings a song of Krishna's names and qualities,
He, hearing it, at once weeps, rolling on the ground.

**44.** Now He prostrates Himself and bows down,
now He sings the name of Krishna in a loud voice.

**45.** Now His voice is full of entreaty, now His body trembles,
His hair stands on end like the filaments of the kadamba.

**46.** All the time He is beside Himself and only for a moment comes to His senses —
then, at people's request, He bathes and gives gifts.

**47.** Then too He performs worship, offers food [to the Deity]
and partakes of mahaprasada.

**48.** Thus in joy and delight the day passes,
and all night long He, happy, dances and sings.

**49.** Thus in delight — day and night:
the Lord teaches the people and tastes the rasa of prema.

**50.** He Himself tastes His own rasa —
this is the chief reason; listen, all of you.

**51.** The deliverance of souls I consider a secondary reason;
that is why He is called the crown jewel of avataras[^5].

**52.** In His body the whole series of avataras is manifest;
the associates of all the avataras are with Him, all [His] servants.

**53.** Gaurachandra has risen in Navadvipa
and dispelled the blindness in the hearts of the people of the world.

**54.** By the rays of His compassion the age of Kali was lit up,
the burning in the hearts of all people was quenched.

**55.** All the chakora-devotees came and gathered together;
drinking the nectar of prema, all lost themselves.

**56.** Gadadhara Pandita Gosani came,
and Narahari came and remained with him.

**57.** Srinivasa, Murari, Mukunda, Vakreshvara,
Shridhara Pandita, whose home is in Navadvipa,

**58.** Shriman, Sanjaya, Pandita Dhananjaya,
Shuklambara, Nilambara and other great souls,

**59.** Sri Rama Pandita and Mahesha Pandita,
Haridasa, Nandana Acharya — the righteous,

**60.** Rudra Pandita and Pandita Damodara —
many followers of Gauranga gathered.

**61.** It is impossible to list them all by name:
the book could not be finished — it would become endless.

**62.** All the devotees who lived in various lands
came and gathered at the Lord's feet.

**63.** The devotees were maddened with great prema
and intoxicated all the people, giving them the wealth of prema.

**64.** Equally compassionate to all living beings,
Gora goes about with the devotees, delighting in prema.

**65.** And then one day Vishvambhara,
with Srivasa Pandita and his brothers —

**66.** with all of them the Lord walks along the road
and hears the sound of a flute — no one knows who is playing.

**67.** In the mood of Gandharvika, hearing the sound of the flute,
He wanders, weeping and weeping, calling and calling[^6].

**68.** Beside Himself, He prostrates Himself on the ground
and weeps in many ways, overflowing with prema.

**69.** The Lord is senseless, wholly in the grip of bhava;
He blesses His own and laughs aloud.

**70.** To His students He at times says unearthly things;
now He is in a frenzy, now He keeps silent.

**71.** Srivasa Pandita, Rama and Narayana,
together with Mukunda, came [with Him] to Srivasa's house.

**72.** All around are the devotees, and in the midst is Gaurahari,
intoxicated like the Youthful Couple[^7].

**73.** Now He rises, now falls and rolls on the ground,
cries "Hari, Hari!" and weeps aloud.

**74.** Day and night His body thrills with the bliss of prema;
there is no other talk than talk of Krishna.

**75.** Once, when He was at home, absorbed in prema,
tears flowed from His eyes in seven or five streams:

**76.** "What shall I do? Where shall I go? What means shall I find
so that My mind may somehow turn to Sri Krishna?"

**77.** Saying this, He sobs with anguished cries;
hearing His plaintive words, all the people weep.

**78.** Then a heavenly voice resounded tenderly:
"You are Ishvara Himself; listen, Vishvambhara!

**79.** You have descended to the earth to reveal prema;
by Your own mercy You will preach prema.

**80.** You will establish dharma on earth and perform kirtana;
do not grieve — perform the kirtana of Krishna.

**81.** By Your mercy in Kali the people will be delivered;
giving them Your prema, You will dispel all their sorrows.

**82.** There is no doubt in this — listen to these words:
cast off Your grief and perform Your sankirtana."

**83.** When He heard these words from the lips of the gods,
His heart rejoiced, but He did not say a word[^8].

**84.** On the next day — listen to a wondrous tale,
the nectar-steeped account of Vishvambhara's glories.

**85.** One day He came to the house of Murari Gupta;
His whole body thrills — signs of bhava.

**86.** He entered the shrine
and, beside Himself in bhava, began to say something.

**87.** From the oceans of His eyes flow streams of tears of love,
like the heavenly Ganga from the peak of Sumeru.

**88.** He says to all: "Look — a wonder:
before Me is a boar as large as a mountain!

**89.** At great speed he rushes on — look, the boar!
Baring his tusks, he rushes on, he wants to kill Me.

**90.** Baring his two tusks, this boar will kill Me!" —
saying this, He entered the shrine.

**91.** Then the mood of the Boar again took hold of Him:
on hands and feet He moves about the ground.

**92.** His form is round, His eyes are red,
great might, a loud roar and bellowing.

**93.** A brass vessel stood there;
raising His snout, He seized it for a moment in His teeth.

**94.** Letting go of the brass vessel and opening His mouth,
He asks Murari to declare His true nature.

**95.** In the form [of the Boar] who rescued the Vedas, Bhagavan,
the Lord, the Supreme Person, sitting down, says:

**96.** "Tell Me, what do you know of My svarupa?"
Murari says: "Lord, what do I know?"

**97.** Prostrate on the ground, Murari [said]:
"[Even] Shambhu does not know Your deeds, Lord."

**98.** Saying this, he recited a shloka of the Gita;
I shall render it in plain speech — listen, all.

*Thus in the Srimad Bhagavad-gita (10.15):*

**99.** "You alone know Yourself by Yourself, O Supreme Person,
Source of beings, Lord of beings, God of gods, Lord of the world!"

**100.** "You Yourself know Yourself, Mahaprabhu:
apart from You no one knows You."

**101.** Then Gaurahari speaks again:
"Have the Vedas the power to know Me?"

**102.** Murari again speaks plaintively:
"Even the Thousand-mouthed One does not know Your truth.

**103.** How could the Vedas know the truth of Your deeds?
No one knows Your greatness, Lord."

**104.** Hearing this, Gaura Bhagavan speaks again:
"The Vedas mock Me — listen to the account."

*Thus in the Shvetashvatara Upanishad (3.19):*

**105.** "Without hands and feet, He is swift and grasps; without eyes, He sees; without ears, He hears.
He knows all that is to be known, but none knows Him; they call Him the first, primeval Person."

**106.** "The Vedas say: 'I have no hands and feet' —
no one else mocks Me like this."

**107.** Saying this, the Lord laughs, His face serene:
"The Vedas do not know Me" — so He said[^9].

**108.** Then the vaidya [Murari], bowing down, said:
"Be merciful, Lord, grant me prema."

**109.** The Thakura said again: "Listen, Murari:
love Me — that is your prema.

**110.** Worship the Parabrahman in human form:
the colour of sapphire, bending in three places, a flute in His hand, —

**111.** [and Her] whose radiance humbles the pride of fresh gorochana,
by name the daughter of Vrishabhanu, the original prakriti[^10].

**112.** Many young beauties of Vraja give themselves
to the Beloved of the cowherd girls, the son of Nanda, [staying] by His side.

**113.** The ground [there] is chintamani, a beautiful temple of jewels,
a wish-fulfilling tree, an altar, and on it a seat.

**114.** There are kamadhenu cows of inconceivable power:
they fulfil the desire of one who meditates [on this].

**115.** The radiance of His body is called the formless Brahman.
Know: all this truth is the sweetness of Krishna"[^11].

**116.** Thus the Thakura spoke to all the devotees;
hearing it, the hearts of all were filled with great joy.

**117.** Hearing this, Murari says at the Lord's feet:
"Lord, [let] me see with my eyes the form of Raghunatha."

**118.** As soon as he said this, that very moment he sees:
Rama, dark as durva grass, the life of Janaki,

**119.** Lakshmana, Bharata, Shatrughna and all the rest.
Seeing this, Murari was filled with bliss.

**120.** Losing consciousness, he fell to the ground and rolls about;
the Lord, placing [on him] His lotus hand, calmed him.

**121.** He gave him a blessing: "Be full of prema:
you are Hanuman, and I am that same Ramachandra"[^12].

**122.** Saying this, the Lord went home.
On another day in the house of Srinivasa Pandita,

**123.** gathering all His associates,
He sat and speaks, revealing His prema.

**124.** Calling "Hari, Hari!", with delight in His heart,
He says to His own: "Listen, listen to a wonder:

**125.** how in Kali one attains Radha and Krishna —
this I shall tell you; listen with undivided attention."

**126.** Saying this, He recited a shloka from the Naradiya;
its hidden meaning people do not know.

*Thus in the Brihan-naradiya Purana (38.126):*

**127.** "The name of Hari, the name of Hari, only the name of Hari!
In Kali there is no, no, no other way."

**128.** "The Name is [the Lord's] form: the Name is the one original Person;
in Kali He is present embodied — the fool does not know this.

**129.** Know: 'only' means Bhagavan in the form of the Name;
to dispel doubt Vyasa says it three times.

**130.** Besides the three times there is one more ['only'],
to bring all ill-intentioned sinners to understanding.

**131.** By the name of Hari alone one attains kaivalya:
consider — 'kevala' means 'kaivalya'.

**132.** The plain sense of this: 'by the Name alone' is namabhasa;
its chief [fruit] is kaivalya, as the shastras proclaim.

**133.** Namabhasa gives liberation — true is the word of the shastras;
but when the Name rises [in the heart] — the bliss of prema, as the Puranas explain.

**134.** Whoever, besides this, honours another god
has no way — that is [the sense of] the threefold word.

**135.** Chant the name of Hari, meditating [on the Lord] with the cows, the gopis and the cowherd boys;
know that all this meaning is confirmed by the Vedas"[^13].

**136.** So spoke Gora in the mood of the Boar;
He performs sankirtana of the Name and dances, overwhelmed by prema[^14].

**137.** Whoever hears of Gora's glories and His pastimes in Nadia —
in him love for Krishna at once arises.

**138.** Holding a straw between his teeth, Lochana says:
I have no other wealth than the feet of Gaura.

[^1]: The summary covers the whole section; its end (the coming of the gods, the mercy to Shuklambara, Gadadhara) is in the next portion. Cf. Murari 2.1–2.3.
[^2]: A shloka from Rupa Gosvami's Padyavali. The hunter, Dhruva, Gajendra, Kubja, Sudama, Vidura, Ugrasena — devotees whom the Lord accepted for their devotion, not for their qualities. Bhaktisiddhanta's prose renderings of the shlokas (anvaya and translation) are not translated here or below.
[^3]: The Lord speaks in the mood of Radha separated from Krishna, whom Akrura has taken away to Mathura (cf. Bhaktisiddhanta's summary). The reproaches to Krishna ("cheat", "rake") are the language of the gopis' love in separation, not blasphemy.
[^4]: V. 31+1 is found only in the 1983 edition (absent from Bhaktisiddhanta's edition); the 1903 edition does not have it. Cf. Murari 2.1.9–18; in Vrindavana Dasa too (Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 22) Shachi obtains prema only by the mercy of a Vaishnava (Advaita).
[^5]: Vv. 50–51 anticipate the teaching of Krishnadasa Kaviraja (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 4): the chief reason for Gaura's appearance is the tasting of His own rasa (Radha's love), while the deliverance of souls is a secondary reason.
[^6]: Gandharvika — a name of Radha. Hearing the flute, the Lord experiences what Radha experienced (so in Bhaktisiddhanta's summary). Cf. Murari 2.2.1–6.
[^7]: Lit. "like Kishora and Kishori" — the youthful Krishna and Radha. Gaudiya Vaishnavas see in Gaura both of Them in one form.
[^8]: Cf. Murari 2.2.7–10, where the voice from above likewise calls the Lord Bhagavan, descended to give people prema.
[^9]: Cf. Murari 2.2.11–24. The Upanishadic verse denies the Lord material hands and feet, but not a spiritual form: that is why He is "swift" and "grasps". The Lord jokingly calls it "mockery" when these words are taken as a denial of all form: the Vedas cannot describe Him directly (vv. 101–107). So also in Gaudiya Vaishnava theology: the Lord is a person with a spiritual body, and the impersonal Brahman is the radiance of His body (v. 115).
[^10]: "The original prakriti" (mula-prakriti) here means the Lord's original energy (shakti): Radha is the source of all His shaktis, not material nature.
[^11]: Cf. the Brahma-samhita (5.29, 5.40): Krishna's abode with ground of chintamani stones, wish-fulfilling trees and surabhi cows; the impersonal Brahman is the radiance of His body. In Bhaktisiddhanta's summary: the Lord told Murari to worship the son of the king of Vraja, served by the cowherd men and women.
[^12]: In the Rama lila Murari Gupta is Hanuman. Cf. Murari 2.7.13–18, where the Lord names him Ramadasa.
[^13]: Vv. 128–135 are an explanation of the verse "harer nama", following Murari (2.2.29–33). According to Lochana Dasa, the word "kevalam" ("only") points to kaivalya — liberation; but liberation is given already by namabhasa (the "shadow of the Name"), whereas the pure Name bestows prema (v. 133); so too Gaudiya Vaishnavas, following Haridasa Thakura (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Antya 3), teach that liberation is only a by-product of namabhasa. Krishnadasa Kaviraja (Adi 17.21–25) explains differently: the threefold "name of Hari" is for emphasis, and "kevala" excludes knowledge, yoga, austerity and karma.
[^14]: Cf. Murari 2.2.34.
