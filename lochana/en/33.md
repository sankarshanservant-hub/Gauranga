# Madhya-khanda, part 10 (portion 33)

*The section "The Leper's Release from Sin and the Bhava of Baladeva" (end): a brahmana who was not admitted to see the Lord's dancing curses Him — "Be outside samsara!" — and the Lord accepts the curse as a blessing. The section "Shachi's Grief over the Brahmana's Curse" (only in the 1983 edition): Shachi in despair, the Lord consoles her. The section "Mahaprabhu Distributes Prema in His Bhavas": the Lord in the bhava of Balarama demands honey; the vision of Halayudha and His words to Gadadhara; the fragrance of varuni and Balarama's divine companions; the Lord tells of Varaha and Halayudha and asks for His flute*

**45.** Then on another day the Lord was dancing,
and at the door stood a brahmana.

**46.** At that very time another brahmana came:
he wished to see Gaurachandra dance.

**47.** The one who stood at the door did not let him in,
and the brahmana was grieved at not being able to see.[^1]

**48.** Grieved, the brahmana went home;
the Lord danced in joy and knew nothing of it.

**49.** The next day, as the Lord was bathing in the Ganga,
that brahmana suddenly saw the Lord.

**50.** He saw the Lord Vishvambhara bathing in the Ganga —
and the brahmana glares in anger, his whole body trembling.

**51.** Seeing the Lord, he speaks angry words:
"I came to Your house wishing to see You.

**52.** I greatly longed to see Your dancing,
but a sinful brahmana barred the way.

**53.** He did not let me in, [kept me] outside the door —
so You too shall be outside samsara!"

**54.** Saying this, he tore his sacred thread in anger;
beside himself with rage, the brahmana heeds no persuasion.[^2]

**55.** "I was put outside the door — I will not bear it!" —
and he cursed: "May You be outside samsara!"

**56.** Hearing this, the Lord rejoiced at heart:
"The brahmana's curse has become a great blessing for Me!"

**57.** When Bhagavan accepted the curse,
the brahmana, hearing it, grew greatly afraid at heart:

**58.** "What could I do, Lord? It is You who made me say it:
You are all-perfect, the Inner Witness of all.

**59.** You will deliver all who are given to idle argument:
taking sannyasa, You will give them prema.

**60.** Seeing You a sannyasi, they will call You 'guru',
and through that humility You will give them prema.

**61.** Gaurahari, the crest-jewel of the wise,
will open the storehouse of the prema of former times and distribute it.

**62.** You have vowed to flood the universe [with prema] —
You will leave out no one, neither the wicked nor the good.

**63.** I alone have been left out of the flood of Your prema:
what will become of me, O Purifier of the fallen?"[^3]

**64.** Hearing this, the Lord says: "It is not a curse — it is a blessing for Me:
you have fulfilled My desire; do not fear."

**65.** Hearing this, the brahmana fell at the Lord's feet,
and Mahaprabhu, raising him, embraced him.

**66.** In the Lord's embrace the brahmana is beside himself with prema:
overflowing with prema for Krishna, he melted.

**67.** Bhagavan fulfilled the brahmana's desire —
He gave him prema, hard even for Brahma to obtain.

**68.** Such wondrous pastimes Gaurangasundara performs —
scoundrels with wicked hearts cannot understand them.

**69.** Thus Mahaprabhu rejoiced at heart —
and Lochana Dasa happily sings of Gora's qualities.

## Shachi's Grief over the Brahmana's Curse

*Raga — any (yatha-raga)*

**69+1.** Hearing from people's lips of the brahmana's curse on the Lord,
Shachi suddenly trembled to the depths of her soul[^4].

**69+2.** Her heart pounds and burns — she does not know what has happened;
she cannot hold back her tears — both eyes are full.

**69+3.** In distress Shachi asks everyone —
and on everyone's lips is the brahmana's curse on the Lord.

**69+4.** Hearing this, she fell there senseless;
coming to herself, Shachi weeps aloud.

**69+5.** Weeping, she returned home;
a little later Vishvambhara came home too.

**69+6.** Seeing Gaura's face, the mother's grief welled up,
and, weeping, Shachi began to ask:

**69+7.** "Listen, my son Nimai, what is this I hear?
They say a brahmana has pronounced a curse on you?

**69+8.** What offence did you commit against him?
What sort of brahmana is he — how hard his heart!

**69+9.** Looking at your face, he felt no pity!
Who will be guilty of my death?

**69+10.** All this house and household of mine is only for you;
the heart of unfortunate Shachi is breaking.

**69+11.** You are everyone's darling, the apple of my eye:
may I not lose you by some evil turn of fate!

**69+12.** Looking at your face, I bathe as if in nectar —
and hearing cruel words, my breast is bursting.

**69+13.** I know not what fate awaits unfortunate Shachi:
if harm comes to you, I shall die."

**69+14.** Hearing these words, beautiful Gauranga
gives His mother this answer in consolation:

**69+15.** "Listen, mother, to My word:
why do you weep for nothing?

**69+16.** I have committed no offence against the brahmana:
the brahmana cursed Me without cause.

**69+17.** And how can a curse take effect without an offence?
Know for certain, mother: this is a true word"[^5].

**69+18.** Saying this, the Lord went to the bank of the Jahnavi
and, bathing in the heavenly river, came back home.

**69+19.** Returning home, Mahaprabhu with great reverence
and joy in His heart performs the worship of Krishna.

**69+20.** Having worshipped, He recited hymns for a while
and, absorbed in prema, watered the tulasi.

**69+21.** Having bowed down, the Lord took refreshment,
and Shachi gazes tenderly at her son's face.

**69+22.** The radiance of Gora's face outshines millions of moons;
of Gauranga's deeds this Lochana Dasa tells.

## Mahaprabhu Distributes Prema in His Bhavas

> *Summary by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.* Sriman Mahaprabhu [one day] danced together [with the devotees] in the bhava of Varaha. Then, [saying] that Shiva, Shuka, Narada, Brahma and other sages worship God by song, that the best [dharma] of the Kali age is the sankirtana of the name, and that [Gaurahari] has descended to distribute this dharma at the door of every living being, Sriman Mahaprabhu ordered the devotees headed by Advaita Acharya [to preach it] and, singing with His own the stories of the gopis, in the bhava of a gopi He went with sankirtana to the house of Chandrashekhara Acharya; [Srivasa] Pandita was filled with the bhava of Narada. In His bhava Sriman Mahaprabhu glorified before all the greatness of Gadadhara Pandita and revealed that he is Radhika, [worshipped by] Brahma, Shiva, Narada and others. Then Thakura Haridasa [came], and all the Vaishnavas joined them in the sankirtana of the name and prema. [Dancing] in the bhava of a gopi, [the Lord] suddenly became filled with the bhava of His majesty; then the devotees saw Him in the form of Lakshmi and offered praise to Lakshmi. [In the bhava of Lakshmi the Lord] distributed the prema of service; at that time someone came and loudly called Sriman Mahaprabhu "Prabhu!" — and [the Lord, giving up] that bhava, in the bhava of Ishvara distributed prema to all.[^6]

*Raga vibhasa; disha: "Glory, glory to Gaurachand, risen in Nadia in the Kali age!" (murchha); refrain: "Hear the story of my Lord, whose qualities have lit up the three worlds! Hear the story of Gaurangachand!"*

**1.** I shall tell another story — listen, it is most wondrous:
in the town of Nadia every day there is a new marvel.

**2.** The Lord sits in His house, joyful at heart,
and all His own sit around Him.

**3.** Suddenly a cry rose to the sky:
in a voice like a thundercloud He calls: "Give Me honey!"

**4.** At that very moment the Lord assumes the form of Halayudha —
clad in blue, like a white mountain,[^7]

**5.** with beautiful feet and lotus eyes;
seeing this marvel, all rejoiced at heart.

**6.** The Giver of prema to all enjoys prema:
in His bhava the Great Soul dances.

**7.** Singing the name of Hari with all His own,
thus He went to Advaita and Murari.[^8]

**8.** Going there, the Lord speaks in a faltering voice,
crying "Give, give Me honey!" — and laughs aloud.

**9.** The colour of His body is like the rising sun;
"Give, give Me honey!" He says, holding out His hand.

**10.** Taking in His hand a vessel full of water,
He drinks [it as] honey and belches with relish.[^9]

**11.** Reeling, He dances, intoxicated with prema,
and belches with relish: "heu, heu!"

**12.** Now He falls, now rises, now weeps, now laughs,
now, smacking His lips, laughs aloud.

**13.** Seeing this, all the people offer praise,
and some, crying "Haladhara!", clasp His feet.

**14.** Then Mahaprabhu, playing Balarama,
speaks incomparable, nectarean words:

**15.** "I am not Sri Krishna, to be pleased with words —
bring Me wonderful, sweet honey!"[^10]

**16.** A brahmana was standing there;
"Here is a wrestler!" [the Lord] said, and pushed him with a finger.

**17.** At the push of a finger the brahmana flew far off;
the brahmana was ashamed: the Thakura had thrown him down.

**18.** This bhava began in the morning [and lasted] until evening:
the Great Soul sports, playing Balarama.

**19.** The lotus feet of Narahari are the ornament of my head;
this Lochana Dasa sings of Gora's blessed qualities.

**20.** Hear one more wonder — on the following day
the Thakura dances, imitating Baladeva.

**21.** Suddenly, in grief, He fainted;
remembering Balarama, He sheds tears.

**22.** Mahaprabhu rolls on the ground with His hair loose;
they sprinkle water on His face — all are in distress.

**23.** In a moment, seeing Gadadhara, He came to Himself
and, understanding [his] silent sign, spoke anguished words:

**24.** "You are My friend — I know you are as dear to Me as life;
I am conquered by your love, listen, jewel of brahmanas.

**25.** I am your Lord, you are My life;
[people] call Me 'Gadai's Gauranga' — mark this.

**26.** None of My bhavas is hidden from you:
your body is My inner shakti.[^11]

**27.** Day and night you do not leave Me for a moment:
who but you truly knows My ways?

**28.** All My dear friends, the Vaishnavas —
bring them; I wish to see them now."

**29.** Receiving the command, Gadadhara Pandita
brought them all — Acharyaratna and the others.[^12]

**30.** Coming, all those great souls saw [Him] —
and all were overwhelmed, their eyes filled with tears.

**31.** Acharyaratna spoke gentle words:
"Tell us Yourself, dear child, what is the cause?"

**32.** Hearing his words, Vishvambhara speaks,
but cannot speak — His voice chokes in His throat.

**33.** In great agitation He speaks in broken words:
"I saw Halayudha, like a white mountain — beside Me.

**34.** The radiance of His body, golden, is like the sun,
His ornaments flash brilliantly."

**35.** As He spoke, the Lord again
saw Baladeva, like a white mountain.

**36.** Then Mahaprabhu Vishvambhara-raya
again, in that bhava, dances and sings.

**37.** All the Vaishnavas are overwhelmed with bliss:
in prema for Balarama all of them reel.

**38.** Bliss filled all the quarters;
for two days the Lord's bhava did not break.

**39.** Then on the following day, at the time of dancing,
all the great devotees surrounded Him on every side.

**40.** Under the weight of His feet the earth shakes;
He rolls His reddened eyes and speaks in broken words.

**41.** His gait is slow like that of an intoxicated elephant:
He cannot walk, absorbed in prema.

**42.** As the Lord is given over to His bhava, so are His companions:
beside Himself, He dances, delighting in the play of Balarama.

**43.** With dancing and singing evening came,
and suddenly from His mouth there wafted the fragrance of varuni.

**44.** Everything was filled with the divine fragrance of varuni;
the people look about in amazement.

**45.** All ten directions are fragrant with the scent of varuni;
the devotees grew intoxicated in the madness of prema.[^13]

**46.** At that time Sri Rama Pandita, the best of brahmanas,
saw — hear what was revealed to him:

**47.** suddenly there appeared there
divine, divine jewels among men in divine dress:

**48.** each had a lotus in one ear, lotus eyes,
an earring in the other ear, blue garments;

**49.** on their heads yellow turbans, loosely tied —
I cannot describe the beauty and elegance of their forms and dress.

**50.** There was a brahmana there named Vanamali —
I shall tell of him; listen, all brothers:

**51.** he saw a body made of gold,
adorned with gems like the peak of Sumeru.[^14]

**52.** Seeing it, he rejoiced at heart, his body thrilled,
and all the people, seeing it, were amazed.

**53.** In the form of Halayudha the Lord of the three worlds dances,
and all the devotees together dance with Him.

**54.** The gods in the sky, rejoicing at heart,
went back content to their own abodes.

**55.** Thus, having spent the whole day and night,
the Lord, smiling, goes to bathe in the heavenly river.

**56.** All the Vaishnavas together
dip and bathe in the waters of the heavenly river.

**57.** The Lord laughs and jests with His own
and plays merrily, delighting in their company.

**58.** Finishing His bath, the Lord quickly came out [of the water];
all bowed to the Lord and went to their homes.

**59.** The Lord, going home, remains in great happiness;
in the morning all came before the Lord.

**60.** And Mahaprabhu said: "Hear one word" —
but His voice choked, and only half was distinct:

**61.** "Lord Varaha embraced Me,
Halayudha entered My heart.

**62.** He who holds the flute to His lips has become the collyrium of My eyes."[^15]
Thus He told a nectarean story — hear, His own!

**63.** Seeing Srivasa, Mahaprabhu said:
"Give Me My flute!" — and holds out His divine hand.

**64.** Then Shrinivasa Pandita Thakura,
a clever devotee, answered Him:

**65.** "Listen, listen, Mahaprabhu: here, in Your house,
the daughter of Bhishmaka keeps Your flute for You.[^16]

**66.** At night the doors of the house were locked —
You will get the flute at once, I tell You."

**67.** Thus every moment there is joy and merriment:
most wondrous are these pastimes in Nadia.

**68.** Whoever knows the rasa of Krishna knows their essence:
the story of the pastimes in Nadia is a great treasure.

**69.** And those who do not know — I implore them:
do not neglect it, turn your minds to Gora's qualities.

**70.** Consider with your heart, brothers, what lies in this:
you will get into your hands Krishna, the Lord of the three worlds.

**71.** Without serving [Him] there is no, no, no deliverance —
so says Lochana Dasa again and again.

[^1]: Vv. 45–69 — cf. Murari 2.13.18–23; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17. The kirtana in Srivasa's house was held behind closed doors, and outsiders were not admitted.
[^2]: Tearing the sacred thread is the sign of a curse pronounced by an angry brahmana.
[^3]: "Outside samsara" means one who has renounced the world, a sannyasi; the Lord Himself wished to take sannyasa, so the curse is a blessing for Him (cf. Murari 2.13.21–22). The brahmana, coming to his senses, himself foretells the Lord's sannyasa and its fruit: the disputants and logicians who did not honour Him as a householder will bow before the sannyasi and receive prema (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17).
[^4]: This section is not in Bhaktisiddhanta's edition (1922); it is translated from Haridasa Shastri's edition (1983), where it stands without a heading of its own (only with the indication "যথা রাগ" and a fresh numbering, vv. 1–22) right after the story of the brahmana's curse (vv. 45–69); the heading is ours. The 1903 edition lacks it. Shachi, full of maternal love (vatsalya), fears for her son as any mother would; the Lord consoles her.
[^5]: Consoling His mother, the Lord says that a curse without an offence will not take effect; but He Himself, as said above (vv. 56, 64), accepted this curse as a blessing, since it coincided with His wish to take sannyasa (see note 3). Then (vv. 69+19 — 69+20) the Lord, playing the role of a devotee, Himself worships Krishna and the tulasi, teaching this to others.
[^6]: This summary concerns pastimes told in the next portion (the bhava of Varaha, the sankirtana, the play in Chandrashekhara's house and so on); the pastimes in the bhava of Balarama with which the section begins (vv. 1–58) are already described in the summary of the previous section (en/32). The OCR of the summary is badly garbled; gaps are filled by sense and from the table of contents in the edition's preface ("the Lord's bhava of Varaha, the order to Advaita Acharya and other devotees about sankirtana and preaching, the singing of the gopis' qualities in the bhava of a gopi, the visit to Chandrashekhara's house, Srivasa's bhava of Narada, the glorification of Gadadhara and the revelation that he is Radhika, the arrival of Thakura Haridasa, the bliss of sankirtana, the Lord's bhava of majesty, the distribution of the prema of service in the form of Lakshmi, and finally the bhava of Ishvara").
[^7]: Halayudha ("armed with the plough"), Haladhara and Baladeva are Balarama. The form and bhava of Balarama that Gaura reveals are His own: Balarama is the first manifestation of Krishna Himself. Cf. Murari 2.14.
[^8]: So in Bhaktisiddhanta's edition ("অদ্বৈত-মুরারির স্থানে"; the OCR is not entirely clear); the 1983 edition has "to Advaita Acharya", Murari (2.14.4) the house of Murari; Bhaktisiddhanta's summary, the house of Advaita Acharya.
[^9]: Balarama loves the intoxicating drink varuni; the Lord in His bhava drinks water as honey (cf. Murari 2.14.5). This is not worldly intoxication but rapture in prema (cf. en/28, note 12).
[^10]: Cf. Murari 2.14.7: "I am not that Krishna whom it is enough to please with words."
[^11]: Gadadhara Pandita is, in the Gaudiya Vaishnava understanding, Sri Radha (en/25), the inner (antaranga) shakti of the Lord; hence all His bhavas are known to him. "Gadai's Gauranga" means "Gadadhara's Gauranga".
[^12]: Acharyaratna is Chandrashekhara Acharya. Vv. 20–38 — cf. Murari 2.14.10–15.
[^13]: Vv. 43–54 — cf. Murari 2.14.17–22. Balarama's divine varuni is no worldly wine; its fragrance is the sign of the presence of Balarama and His companions, whom Sri Rama Pandita, Srivasa's brother, sees.
[^14]: In Murari (2.14.20) Vanamali sees on the ground Balarama's golden club, Saunanda. Whether this is the same Vanamali as in en/31 (v. 6 of the section) is not stated.
[^15]: That is, the Lord ceaselessly beholds Krishna with the flute. Cf. Murari 2.15.1: the Lord tells of the vision of Varaha and Halayudha and asks for His flute.
[^16]: The daughter of Bhishmaka is Rukmini; thus Srivasa jokingly refers to the Lord's wife, Vishnupriya. (According to the Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika, Rukmini appeared in Gaura-lila as Lakshmipriya, and Vishnupriya is Bhu-shakti.)
