# Madhya-khanda, part 9 (portion 32)

*The section "Wondrous Pastimes of Mahaprabhu in the Bhava of Bhagavan" (end): a brahmana takes the dust of the Lord's feet, and the Lord leaps into the Ganga; Nityananda brings Him out onto the bank; the Lord goes off to the northern bank and returns at the devotees' entreaties; in Srivasa's house — words on serving Krishna and on His departure; Murari, embraced by the Lord, recites the verse of Sudama; the Lord reveals His radiance, and Srivasa bathes Him; the Lord and the devotees clean the temple; Lochana Dasa calls on all to serve Gaura. The section "The Leper's Release from Sin and the Bhava of Baladeva": a leper who had offended Srivasa begs the Lord for deliverance; the Lord rejects him and delivers him only when Srivasa forgives*

*Refrain: "Who does not weep at the qualities of my Gauranga? He has bound the minds of all living beings with prema"*

**64.** Hear one more wonder — on the next day,
when the dancing with His own had ended,

**65.** the Lord fell flat on the ground like a rod,
and all the people in joy cry: "Hari, Hari!"

**66.** At that very moment a certain brahmana came up[^1]
and, smiling, took the dust of the Lord's lotus feet.

**67.** Seeing this, Gaura Bhagavan quickly rose:
seeing the brahmana's deed, He was grieved.

**68.** In great remorse, His face downcast,
in displeasure He breathes heavily and fast.

**69.** Quickly rising, the Lord suddenly ran
and swiftly leapt into the waters of the Jahnavi.

**70.** The Lord sank under the water — He cannot be seen;
all His own leapt in after Him.

**71.** All the people of Nadia thought a calamity had happened:
all weep, all are in sorrow.

**72.** Crying "My son! My son!" runs Shachi, His mother,
wanting to leap in where Vishvambhara Hari is.

**73.** Shachi, distraught, wails aloud,
weeps and sobs, rolls on the ground.

**74.** Seeing such calamity, the Avadhuta-raya
leapt into the Ganga in search of the Lord.

**75.** Diving into the water, He caught the Lord by the hand
and, holding Him, suddenly brought Him out onto the bank of the Ganga.

**76.** Seeing this, all the people were overjoyed;
all [the Lord's] own, coming to their senses, weep.

**77.** Shachi-devi weeps, holding Vishvambhara in her arms;
[weep] Srivasa, Murari, Mukunda, Shuklambara,

**77+1.** Gadadhara and Narahari weep, clasping [His] feet,
Vasudeva and Jagadananda weep, gazing at [His] face[^2],

**78.** Haridasa and all, all His own —
they weep as they look at Gaura's face, still shaken with fear.

**79.** All the other people, who had grieved greatly,
seeing Gaura's face, went home happy.

**80.** Then the Lord Vishvambhara, with everyone,
quickly went to the house of Murari Gupta.

**81.** After staying there a little, the Lord swiftly left
and unexpectedly came to the house of Vijaya Mishra.[^3]

**82.** Having spent the night, the Lord rose at dawn
and unexpectedly went to the northern bank of the Ganga.

**83.** He wanders there — His mind is past understanding;
all who were with Him were frightened.

**84.** The brahmanas, the good people and all His own
together made a humble plea to Him:

**85.** "Be gracious, Lord Gaura, treasury of virtues!" —
so all these offenders say in anguish. —

**86.** "Have mercy, Mahaprabhu, give up Your great anger:
how many such faults of Your servants will You hold against them?

**87.** The Lord is an ocean of mercy, mercy embodied;
You descended out of mercy, in grace towards the people.

**88.** Why have You Yourself so turned away [from us]?
How can we know what is in Your heart, what Your ways are?

**89.** Come home, Lord, avert this calamity;
look upon Your followers — be gracious!"

**90.** When His own thus humbly pleaded,
the Lord's compassionate heart then melted.

**91.** The Lord returned home with a joyful heart
and with His followers sings of His own qualities.

**92.** In the town of Nadia there arose jubilation —
Lochana Dasa happily sings of Gora's qualities.

*Raga varadi; disha: "Haya re haya, are haya!" (murchha); refrain: "I take upon myself all misfortunes of Gora's beauty! He has given away the treasure of prema, filling the world with it"*

**93.** Leaving grief behind, with a joyful heart Gaurahari
went with His own to Srivasa's house.

**94.** Srinivasa, Haridasa and all the others
sit near the Thakura and gaze at His face.

**95.** Then Mahaprabhu, before the whole assembly,
speaks words from His heart, and all listen:

**96.** "Wealth, kin, youth — all is in vain:
I have not served the true treasure — the feet of Krishna.

**97.** My heart burns ceaselessly in samsara:
having obtained such a body, I did no work for Krishna.

**98.** In this world the human body is rare;
and for serving Krishna, what does it matter — man or woman?

**99.** If one does not serve Krishna, this whole body is in vain,
husband, son, father, mother — all is in vain, and the home.

**100.** Leaving My mother, I shall go to distant lands" —
so He revealed to all His heart's word.[^4]

**101.** "All the people say that I act wrongly."
Murari says: "To hear this is death to me.

**102.** No one says so, listen, Mahaprabhu:
we have never heard it from anyone's lips."

**103.** Hearing this, Gaura Bhagavan
embraced Murari.

**104.** Embracing Murari, He went [with him] into the house;
in the Lord's embrace the vaidya forgot himself.

**105.** His whole body thrilled from head to foot;
he recited an ancient verse:

**106.** "Where am I — a poor man, most sinful — and where is Krishna, the abode of Shri!
Yet me, a brahmana only by birth, He embraced with His arms."[^5]

**107.** Hearing this, He revealed His lordship —
a radiance surpassing the rays of millions of suns.

**108.** Sitting on His seat, He speaks sweet words:
"Here I am — consciousness and bliss; do not think I am far away."[^6]

**109.** Hearing this, all were overwhelmed with bliss,
the bodies of all were filled with thrill.

**110.** Srivasa Pandita, excellent in conduct,
bathes Him with the water of the Ganga.

**111.** He performed the bathing and worship according to rule,
and the Treasury of virtues was pleased with his worship.

**112.** In joy all the people sing of Hari's qualities;
looking at the faces of the devotees, Gora-raya dances.

**113.** Placing on his head the lotus feet of Narahari,
Lochana Dasa speaks of the sweetness of Gauranga.

**114.** Hear an unheard-of story of the following day:
listen attentively — I shall tell it now.

**115.** The Teacher of the world teaches all people —
the ocean of mercy, the wish-fulfilling tree of prema-bhakti.

**116.** All He does is to enlighten His own.
Taking with Him Advaita Acharya and the others —

**117.** Srinivasa, Haridasa, Murari, Mukunda,
Gadadhara, Shuklambara, Rama and the rest,

**118.** Narahari, Raghunandana, Sri Mukunda Dasa,
Vasu Ghosha, Jagadananda and all the servants —

**119.** taking all the devotees with Him,
the Lord joyfully goes to the temple.[^7]

**120.** He wears a loincloth, a hoe on His shoulder,
a broom in His hand, and all are together with Him.

**121.** All His companions wear the same dress:
brooms in their hands, hoes on their shoulders, their hair tied up high.

**122.** The Lord goes to clean the temple —
never have I heard such a wondrous story.

**123.** Becoming Krishna's sweeper, He goes from door to door;
all the Vaishnavas together sweep.[^8]

**124.** Thus the Thakura teaches the people:
serve [Him], all people, whoever is wise!

**125.** There is no other Giver of prema-bhakti —
knowing this, serve the feet of Sri Gauranga.

**126.** In age after age come so many avataras —
and they return service for service in like measure.

**127.** No one else has shown such lordship:
to teach bhakti He puts a hoe on His shoulder.

**128.** In what age was there one who serves those who do not serve Him,
who goes from house to house begging [them to accept] devotion to Himself?

**129.** "He who returns service for service is already a great lord" —
so say the devotees; and others say [God] is far away.[^9]

**130.** Without considering who is worthy or unworthy, in any land,
He gives all the treasure of Vrindavana and gladdens all.

**131.** Prema beyond dharma and adharma He offers to all —
the son of Shachi, the Lord, has delivered all.

**132.** Brahma, Maheshvara, Lakshmi and Ananta
cannot call it their own — so hard is it to obtain.

**133.** There is no [other] lord who calls his own those who do not serve him —
this is why my heart longs for Gora's qualities.

**133+1.** Mighty is the remembrance of the glories of Gauranga's feet:
this power alone carries everyone across samsara[^10].

**134.** Brothers, serve Gora's qualities, do not neglect them:
to cross samsara, this alone is the raft.

**135.** There will never be such a Thakura again —
says Lochana: Gora's avatara alone.

## The Leper's Release from Sin and the Bhava of Baladeva

> *Summary by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.* One day, when Sriman Mahaprabhu was walking with the devotees, absorbed in prema, a brahmana afflicted with leprosy on that road appealed to Sriman Mahaprabhu for his deliverance. Sriman Mahaprabhu at first rejected him as an offender against a Vaishnava, then told him to beg Srivasa Pandita's forgiveness for his offence, and at last, freeing him from the offence against the Vaishnava, bestowed prema on him. A certain brahmana wished to see Gaurasundara's dancing, but the devotees of Gaura headed by Srivasa did not admit him, and his desire was not fulfilled; so one day, seeing Sriman Mahaprabhu bathing in the Ganga, he angrily cursed Him: "May Your worldly happiness be destroyed!" Sriman Mahaprabhu accepted the brahmana's curse with joy. Then the brahmana came to his senses and in fear began to praise Sriman Mahaprabhu, and Sriman Mahaprabhu consoled him, revealing that the brahmana's curse was what He Himself desired. Then are described wondrous pastimes: Sriman Mahaprabhu in the bhava of Balarama cries out "Give Me honey!"; with the devotees, performing kirtana, He goes to the house of Sri Advaita Acharya; the next day, when He has fainted in the bhava of Baladeva, Gadadhara comes and He restrains His bhava; the devotees headed by Acharyaratna arrive; Srivasa and the other devotees see Sriman Mahaprabhu in the form of Baladeva; He goes with the devotees to bathe in the Ganga, and so on.[^11]

*Refrain: "Hari, Rama, Narayana — Shachi's darling, golden Gora!"*

**1.** Hear another wondrous story of Gauranga's deeds:
hearing it, you will gain great love.

**2.** The Lord walks along the road with His own;
from the taste of talk of Krishna His body sways in rapture.

**3.** On that road there was a leper;
humbly he addresses the Lord's feet.

**4.** Falling to the ground, he bows down
and in anguish humbly speaks:

**5.** "All people say, Lord: You are Janardana,
You are Purushottama, You are the Eternal.

**6.** You are the Lord of the lords of the gods, the Friend of the three worlds;
deliver me, O ocean of mercy!

**7.** Hearing that You are the Purifier of the fallen, I have come to You:
deliver me — You are the Master of all.

**8.** O Lord of the destitute, Shachi's darling,
deliver me, Lord Gauranga-Gopala!

**9.** In the three worlds there is no greater sinner than I;
free [me] from this unbearable leprosy."

**10.** Hearing this, the Lord grew angry at heart
and looked wrathfully at the leper.

**11.** The Thakura says: "Listen, sinful wretch:
why did you, worthless one, revile a Vaishnava?

**12.** All living beings in the world are My friends;
whoever hates a Vaishnava is My enemy.

**13.** At blasphemy against Myself I am never grieved,
but how can I be happy when Srivasa Pandita is reviled?

**14.** You spoke unspeakable words to him:
even if you suffer a hundred births, [the sin] will not leave you.

**15.** Whoever offends a Vaishnava,
him I never deliver.

**16.** See: this body of Mine is [My] life outside,
and the Vaishnava is [My] life within — there is no doubt.[^12]

**17.** Whoever serves the Vaishnava, even if he hates Me,
him I deliver, removing his distress.

**18.** The fool who does harm to a Vaishnava
falls into hell, and he has no refuge.

**19.** You are a sinner, the greatest scoundrel, incorrigible:
how long you will suffer in hell — there is no end!"

**20.** Hearing this, the leper falls down and weeps,
weeps in distress and finds no peace:

**21.** "In other avataras You were merciful, discerning who was a devotee —
but now, Lord, in Kali [You deliver] scoundrels in every home.

**22.** If You strike down those who do not serve You,
how will You bear the name of Purifier of the fallen?

**23.** Glory to You whose name, Vishvambhara, is the welfare of all!
Glory to You, Mighty-armed, the support of the bridge of dharma!

**24.** By Your bridge people cross the ocean of existence —
do not cast me away, Lord, son of Shachi!"

**25.** Though, seeing him, [the Lord] had softened at heart,
yet He is subject to the Vaishnavas — not independent.[^13]

**26.** Knowing this, the Lord went to Srivasa's house
and, sitting down, the Great Soul tells him everything:

**27.** "On the road I saw a leper:
for his offence he will suffer many births.

**28.** For his offence against you his whole body is rotting,
and seeing him, I felt no pity for him.

**29.** 'Deliver me!' the leper cries;
but who will deliver one who has offended you?

**30.** Only if you yourself look on him with kindness
will the sinner be delivered by your mercy."

**31.** Hearing this, Srivasa Pandita
began to laugh, hearing of the Lord's conduct:

**32.** "I am the lowest of the low — and You say such things to me!
Using me as a pretext, You deliver the sinner.

**33.** His fault towards me is entirely gone;
I am pleased — relieve him of his suffering."

**34.** Hearing this, the Lord cried aloud "Hari!" —
and the leper was delivered: such [was] the grace.

**35.** There, on the bank of the Ganga, that very moment the leper
received the supreme medicine — the mercy of Srivasa.[^14]

**36.** That very moment his body became divine;
crying "Gauranga!", he runs, overcome with longing:

**36+1.** Intoxicated with great prema, he gives thunderous cries:
now he swoons, now he raves without end[^15].

**37.** "Where has Gaurachandra gone, the moon of my heart?
Who else so frees one from the great blindness — the disease of existence?"

**38.** Meanwhile Gaurachandra left Srinivasa's house
and hurried to see the leper.

**39.** On the road they met,
and [he], clasping the Lord's feet, fell to the ground.

**40.** Raising him, the Lord embraced him
and that very moment gave him prema, hard even for Brahma to obtain.

**41.** He laughs, weeps, dances, sings, rolls on the ground,
whirls about in dance, crying: "Friend of Gadadhara!"[^16]

**42.** Seeing this, all the devotees rejoiced,
and all Nadia, seeing it, was amazed.

**43.** Hear, all of you, of Vishvambhara's deeds:
hearing of them, you will soon obtain prema-bhakti.

**43+1.** Then Mahaprabhu, rejoicing at heart,
dances — and prema became manifest in the brahmana's body[^17].

**43+2.** Seeing this, Mahaprabhu loudly cries "Hari!":
He delivered the leper, He showed him grace.

**44.** Most wondrous is this manifestation in Nadia —
hearing of it, Lochana Dasa is lost in joy.

[^1]: The text has "a brahmana" (so also the 1983 edition); in Bhaktisiddhanta's summary (en/31, at the head of the section "Wondrous Pastimes of Mahaprabhu in the Bhava of Bhagavan") and in Murari (2.11.21) it is a brahmana woman. The Lord, playing the role of a devotee, grieves at having received an honour befitting the Lord. Vv. 64–79 — cf. Murari 2.11.19–25.
[^2]: V. 77+1 is found only in the 1983 edition; the 1903 edition gives it in a footnote as an additional reading (in another form: "Gadadhara and Narahari weep, holding the Lord; Vasudeva and Jagadananda weep, gazing at the Lord").
[^3]: Vv. 80–92 — cf. Murari 2.12.1–4. Vijaya is probably Vijaya Dasa, a devotee of Navadvipa and copyist of books.
[^4]: The Lord is already thinking of sannyasa. Vv. 93–102 — cf. Murari 2.12.5–8.
[^5]: Bhagavata 10.81.16 — the words of the poor brahmana Sudama whom Krishna embraced; Murari applies them to himself. In the edition the verse is followed by Bhaktisiddhanta's anvaya and translation: "Where am I, a sinful pauper, and where is Krishnachandra, full of all opulence! Only because I am the lowest of brahmanas did Bhagavan embrace me with both arms." Cf. Murari 2.12.9–11.
[^6]: Literally "I am Chidananda": the Lord's body is not material — it is consciousness and bliss (cf. Murari 2.12.13: "this body is the highest concentration of being and consciousness"). Vv. 107–111 — cf. Murari 2.12.12–15.
[^7]: Vv. 114–123 — cf. Murari 2.13 (the Lord and the devotees clean the temple). Narahari, Raghunandana and Mukunda Dasa in this list appear only in Lochana Dasa (cf. en/30, note 7); "Rama" is Sri Rama Pandita, Srivasa's brother.
[^8]: A haddipa is a sweeper of the lowest caste of scavengers. The Lord Himself takes on the lowliest service, teaching humility and service to the Deity; cf. the cleaning of the Gundicha temple (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 12).
[^9]: Vv. 126–133: the other avataras return service for service (cf. Gita 4.11: "as they surrender to Me, so I reciprocate"), while Gaura Himself goes from house to house offering prema even to those who do not serve Him.
[^10]: V. 133+1 is found only in the 1983 edition (the 1903 edition lacks it).
[^11]: The summary covers both this section (the leper, vv. 1–44; the brahmana's curse, vv. 45–69) and the pastimes in the bhava of Balarama, which in the text of the edition already form the next section. The OCR of the summary is garbled in places; gaps are filled from the table of contents in the edition's preface. Cf. Murari 2.13; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17. In the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Adi 17) this is the brahmana Gopala Chapala, who slandered Srivasa and was struck with leprosy; the Lord refused him and later told him to beg Srivasa's forgiveness.
[^12]: Vv. 11–19 speak of the gravity of offence against a Vaishnava (vaishnava-aparadha): the Lord does not forgive it Himself until the offended Vaishnava forgives. The words "the Vaishnava is My life within" convey the teaching that the devotee is dearer to the Lord than His own body (cf. Bhagavata 9.4.68: "the sadhus are My heart, and I am the heart of the sadhus").
[^13]: The Lord is the independent Master, but of His own will He submits to the love of His devotees (bhakta-adhina); therefore He places the Vaishnava's forgiveness above His own.
[^14]: In the 1983 edition this story is given in a different redaction (the 1903 edition agrees with Bhaktisiddhanta; we follow Bhaktisiddhanta's edition). Instead of vv. 20–24 it has: "Hearing this, the brahmana was filled with anguish and, falling to the ground, began to plead piteously: 'Glory, glory to Mahaprabhu, be merciful to me! The Vedas call You the Purifier of the fallen. If You bear the name Purifier of the fallen, You will surely deliver me. How many great sinners You have delivered — deliver me too, O lotus-eyed One! There is no sinner in the three worlds equal to me; I am suffering — free [me] from leprosy.'" V. 25 there reads: "Then compassion awoke in the Lord's heart, yet He is subject to the Vaishnavas, not independent." Instead of v. 34: "The Lord says: 'Srinivasa, listen to Me: take everyone and go to where the leper is.' Then all went there together joyfully and sprinkled him with the water of Srivasa's feet. As soon as the drops of that water touched his body, his body shone like gold and the disease vanished. Then his heart was filled with great bliss, and he joyfully began to dance, crying 'Hari, Hari!'" From v. 35 on the two redactions agree. The sense is the same: an offence against a Vaishnava is removed only by the mercy of the offended Vaishnava himself (the water of his feet).
[^15]: V. 36+1 is found only in the 1983 edition (the 1903 edition lacks it).
[^16]: "Friend of Gadadhara" — a name of Gaura, inseparable from Gadadhara Pandita (in the Gaudiya Vaishnava understanding, Radha; en/25, note 7).
[^17]: Vv. 43+1 — 43+2 are found only in the 1983 edition (the 1903 edition lacks them).
