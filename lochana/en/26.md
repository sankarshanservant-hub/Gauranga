# Madhya-khanda, part 3 (portion 26)

*The mango tree — a lesson on maya and on offering all acts to Krishna; instruction to Mukunda on worshipping two-armed Krishna; Murari begs for mercy; Srivasa and Sri Rama; the blaspheming brahmana and the Lord's bath; the section "The Truth about Advaita": the Lord with Advaita Acharya*

*Raga varadi; refrain: "Hari, Rama, Narayana — Gora, Shachi's darling!"*

**25.** Hear yet another wonderful story of another day:
every day the son of Shachi reveals something new.

**26.** This story is most unusual, unknown to people;
in the hearts of low people faith in it does not arise.

**27.** Only in secret does He reveal His lordship;
to His own He says: "See: this world is illusory"[^1].

**28.** Saying this, He spoke of something else;
all forgot [about it] and repeat the name of Hari.

**29.** [His] heart became intoxicated with the sankirtana of His own name;
overpowered by prema, He rolls on the ground and weeps.

**30.** Suddenly He rises and speaks, clapping His hands —
He reveals His lordship to His own:

**31.** "Look, look: I have planted a mango stone —
the tree I have raised will grow by itself!"

**32.** Then all suddenly say:
"He has only just planted the stone — and it has sprouted already!"

**33.** Before their eyes the tree put out shoots,
fine branches grew, the tree was covered with buds.

**34.** "Look, look, all you people — another wonder:
see, My little tree is covered with buds!"

**35.** At once fruits appeared and at that very moment ripened;
pointing with His finger, the Lord shows them to all.

**36.** They plucked the fruits and brought them — all the people see it —
and, making an offering, placed them before the Ishvara.

**37.** In a moment none of it is to be seen:
only the fruits remain, and the tree has proved illusory.

**38.** Such maya He showed, and He tells all the people:
"Knowing this, do not die of the grief of this world.

**39.** By the power of My maya the whole world is created;
not understanding this, all people call it their own.

**40.** Who can tear the rope of My maya?
There is only one way to conquer maya[^2]:

**41.** if all the acts that people perform as duties of the body,
all their acts, people dedicate to Me,

**42.** then the body is given to Krishna's feet,
and there is no more bondage of action or inaction, of good or evil.

**43.** This bhakti is the highest truth: reckon it as dedication;
when one gives [all] to Krishna, separateness of itself does not remain[^3].

**44.** Having dedicated all, one surely attains Krishna —
so sing all the Puranas, the Gita and the Bhagavata.

**45.** Otherwise all this is fruitless;
if one dedicates everything to the Ishvara, life in the world becomes meaningful"[^4].

**46.** Such is the wondrous manifestation of Gorachand.
Hearing of it, Lochana Dasa speaks in joy.

*Raga shri; refrain: "O-ki ho-re, Gauranga, jaya, jaya!"*

**47.** At that time, seeing the vaidya Mukunda,
Mahaprabhu spoke with a slight smile:

**48.** "You, they say, honour the knowledge of Brahman — so I have heard.
Well, Mukunda Datta, I praise you!"

**49.** Saying this, the Thakura recited this shloka;
hearing it, everyone's heart trembled.

*Thus in the Padma-purana (quoted in Kavi-karnapura's Chaitanya-charitamrita, 6.36):*

**50.** "The yogis delight in the Infinite — in the Truth, the Bliss, the One whose self is consciousness;
therefore the Supreme Brahman is called by the word 'Rama'"[^5].

**51.** Then Bhagavan Gaurahari
again, showing favour, said to the vaidya:

**52.** "You think highly of worshipping the Four-armed,
but in meditation on the Two-armed your knowledge is small.

**53.** If you want all riches and your own good,
worship two-armed Krishna, immersing your heart in Him.

**54.** Narayana is a manifestation of Krishna, say the shastras;
there is no word that Krishna comes from Narayana"[^6].

**55.** Such compassionate words Vishvambhara speaks;
hearing them, the vaidya respectfully bowed his head:

**56.** "I have bathed my fill in the waters of the heavenly river;
the dust of the Vaishnavas' feet is [for me] the highest grace.

**57.** Let Your lotus feet be an umbrella over my head:
perform my anointing as a servant — that alone I ask.

**58.** What do I know, Lord, of my own good and ill?
Always, within and without, I am blinded by pride.

**59.** When by Your own goodness You show me mercy,
then grant me the grace of serving You.

**60.** You are the Lord of the lords of all, the embodiment of bliss;
You are that very son of Nanda, the root of the avataras."

**61.** Hearing these words, the Lord, pleased at heart,
touched his head with His lotus foot.

**62.** [Mukunda's] whole body thrilled, his eyes filled with tears,
the vaidya's speech faltered — signs of prema.

**63.** In a faltering voice he offered long praise:
"Glory to the Great Maheshvara, the One beyond all causes!"[^7]

**64.** Then Mahaprabhu Vishvambhara Hari,
seeing Murari, began to speak:

**65.** "Hear, hear, vaidya, My word:
give up [your] song — the speculation about the Self that occupies your mind[^8].

**66.** If you have the desire to live,
and if you still have the wish [to taste] the bliss of love for Krishna,

**67.** then give up speculation about the Self
and sing the sankirtana of His qualities with love for Krishna.

**68.** The crown of the best of dancers, beautiful, dark of body,
radiant as a sapphire, with a choice flute in His hand,

**69.** in yellow garments, with a forest garland on His neck —
yet that Lord, surrounded by the gopis, you do not worship!"

**70.** Hearing the Lord's commanding words, Murari the vaidya
fell to the ground in distress and weeps.

**71.** At the Lord's feet he humbly pleads at length:
"Lord, I cannot cross the impassable samsara.

**72.** Neither Brahma nor Maheshvara, nor Lakshmi nor Ananta
can conquer maya — so invincible is she[^9].

**73.** Who can conquer the all-powerful maya
without Your grace? — hear, Vishvambhara.

**74.** I am the lowest of the low; what power have I
to conquer samsara and serve Your feet?

**75.** If, seeing me wretched, You show me mercy,
[then], Lord, embodiment of compassion, I shall serve You.

**76.** For so long the treasure of prema was hidden —
[and now] You, Lord, have revealed it out of compassion.

**77.** Let my mind, like a bee,
drink the nectar-prema of Your lotus feet.

**78.** Give me this boon, ocean of compassion;
do not despise me — I am most wretched."

**79.** Hearing such humble words, the Thakura
was filled with compassion and great joy.

**80.** Smiling, the Lord said: "Hear, Murari:
soon your desire will be fulfilled"[^10].

**81.** And Srinivasa Pandita Thakura
is a devotee of the purest mind and very wise.

**82.** Every day he serves Krishna together with his brothers;
with all his heart he worships Vishvambhara's feet.

**83.** Every day he performs sankirtana of names and qualities;
with his younger brother Rama he has great love.

**84.** Sri Rama Pandita is devoted to serving his elder brother;
the two brothers together sing songs of Hari's qualities.

**85.** Srivasa and Sri Rama are two favourites of the Lord;
with them He plays with a joyful heart.

**86.** In their house the Lord dances with them all,
like Kapila Thakura in the circle of sages[^11].

**87.** Thus in joy and merriment the days pass;
hundreds of students He Himself teaches.

**88.** The students reason among themselves;
among them was one great ignoramus.

**89.** "The one they call Sri Krishna is but maya" —
so said the foolish son of a brahmana.

**90.** Hearing this, the Thakura covered His ears with His hands
and at once went to bathe in the heavenly river.

**91.** In His clothes, together with the students, He bathes in the Ganga,
thrilling, repeating the name of Hari again and again:

**92.** "Sinful, low, wretched, godless in character —
with his evil words he has defiled My ears!"[^12]

**93.** Saying this, He repeats the name of Hari again and again.
Lochana says: Gora is the abode of all virtues.

## The Truth about Advaita

> *Summary by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.* One day Sriman Mahaprabhu [with the devotees] came to the house of Sri Advaita Prabhu to see Him; Advaita Prabhu fell at the feet of Sriman Mahaprabhu. Mahaprabhu lovingly embraced Advaita Prabhu and in conversation glorified the pre-eminence of prema-bhakti in the age of Kali. Seeing a certain godless brahmana, Srivasa Pandita grew alarmed, and by the will of Sriman Mahaprabhu that brahmana was bewildered by maya. Then [the Lord], having enjoyed kirtana and a meal in Advaita's house, returned home. Afterwards Sriman Mahaprabhu, [having expounded the doctrine of the Self,] spoke of the greatness of supreme bhakti.
>
> Sri Advaita Acharya Prabhu came to Navadvipa and prostrated Himself fully at the feet of Sriman Mahaprabhu. At that time Sriman Mahaprabhu, in Srivasa's house, was worshipping His weapon, the club, in order to destroy the godless. Sriman Mahaprabhu, [greatly pleased] with Acharya Gosani, declared before the people, "Bhagavan descends by Advaita's desire," mounted a couch and ordered Advaita Prabhu to dance and sing kirtana. When Srivasa Pandita asked about the truth of Advaita, Sriman Mahaprabhu told the truth about Advaita and instructed everyone to serve Bhagavan[^13].

*Raga bhatiyari; refrain: "Hari, Narayana, Gorachand, Shachi's darling — with the noose of prema He has bound the hearts of living beings!"*

**1.** Now I shall tell another wondrous story:
listen, all, attentively, setting other thoughts aside.

**2.** When one tells of Gora's qualities, the body thrills:
such is the power of the unending nectar of Gora's qualities.

**3.** Together with Srinivasa and the other disciples
He set out joyfully to see Advaita Acharya[^14].

**4.** Some sing songs, some repeat the name of Hari,
they cry "Hari, Hari!" — beyond compare.

**5.** The Thakura Himself dances, the devotees sing;
by the power of prema they do not know themselves.

**6.** From head to foot [all] thrill, their eyes are red;
gazing at Gora's face, they sway.

**7.** The Lord slaps His arms like a wrestler and roars "hum!";
rolling on the ground, all the associates weep.

**8.** Thus in joy they go along the road,
longing to see Advaita Acharya Gosani.

**9.** Arriving, He saw Advaita Acharya Gosani
and, falling to the ground, prostrated Himself.

**10.** In dismay Acharya Gosani fell at His feet
and with much humility speaks piteously,

**11.** bowing and rolling on the ground:
"[You are] the crown of millions of Advaitas like me!"[^15]

**12.** They embrace each other
and bathe each other with the tears of their eyes.

**13.** Sitting down, the Lord speaks His words —
captivating, sin-removing, bestowing prema-bhakti.

**14.** Having heard, Acharya Gosani speaks;
His eyes redden as He rebukes the godless:

**15.** "The godless say, 'In the Kali-yuga there is no bhakti' —
let them see with their own eyes my Chaitanya Gosani!"

**16.** Hearing these words, the Lord, His lips trembling,
began to reply in a voice deep as thunder:

**17.** "'There is no bhakti in the Kali-yuga' — what else is there, then?
There is bhakti alone — by it the world lives.

**18.** One who says, 'There is no bhakti in the Kali-yuga' —
his birth is in vain; hear, all of you.

**19.** In the Kali-yuga there is devotion to Krishna, [given] by [the Lord's] gracious love:
in no other yuga is there such mercy as in Kali"[^16].

**20.** At this time Pandita Srivasa,
with fear in his heart, began to speak:

**21.** "See, Lord, there in front — a godless brahmana:
now he will disrupt Krishna's festival.

**22.** He is a great godless man, of very wicked conduct;
proud of his learning, he is exceedingly arrogant."

**23.** Then Mahaprabhu said to him:
"This wicked scoundrel will not come here."

**24.** And the brahmana, bewildered by maya, did not come[^17],
while Mahaprabhu, joyful at heart, gives Himself to play:

**25.** placing one arm on Srinivasa's arm,
with His left hand He holds Gadadhara's hand;

**26.** leaning His body on Narahari,
He gazes at Sri Raghunandana's face and weeps;

**27.** placing His lotus feet on Sri Rama Pandita,
Mahaprabhu plays before the Acharya.

**28.** All around the Vaishnavas sing the sankirtana of His qualities,
and in the middle dances the Lord, the son of Shachi,

**29.** as at the festival of the rasa, surrounded by the gopis —
so beautiful was [His play] in the midst of the kirtana.

**30.** Thus after a while, when the dance was over,
Advaita Acharya, together with Sita, rejoiced.

**31.** Then in His house the Lord took His meal;
His body was anointed with fragrant sandal paste and adorned with a garland.

**32.** Advaita Acharya considered Himself blessed:
"Now I have known the Lord's mercy to me!"

**33.** Advaita's people weep, fallen at His feet;
Vishvambhara lifts them all up and embraces them.

**34.** Dancing and singing His own names and qualities,
the Lord returned home with His own.

**35.** Thus day by day [His] manifestation grows.
Hearing of it, Lochana Dasa rejoices at heart.

[^1]: "Illusory" (michha) in the sense of transient and deceptive: the world is created by the Lord's energy and is real, but it is false to regard it as one's own and to seek happiness in it (v. 39). Cf. Murari 2.4.6–11, where the same miracle with the tree is called "a conjurer's trick".
[^2]: Cf. Bhagavad-gita 7.14: the Lord's maya is hard to overcome, but those who surrender to Him overcome it. Here maya is the Lord's external energy, which He controls.
[^3]: Not merging with Krishna: one who has given everything to Krishna has no interests left apart from His will (cf. Bhagavata 7.5.23 on atma-nivedana, and 11.29.34).
[^4]: Cf. Murari 2.4.10–11: everything done for the Master bears fruit.
[^5]: A verse of the Padma-purana; it is quoted by Murari (2.4.14) and in the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 9.29). The Lord jokingly "praises" Mukunda for his knowledge of Brahman and shows that the Supreme Brahman is Rama Himself, a Person.
[^6]: So also in Gaudiya Vaishnava theology: Krishna is the original Personality of Godhead Himself ("krishnas tu bhagavan svayam", Bhagavata 1.3.28), and Narayana is His manifestation. Cf. Murari 2.4.15–17.
[^7]: Cf. Murari 2.4.18–21.
[^8]: Lit. "give up the gita (song)…". In Murari (2.4.22–23) the Lord reproaches Murari for his song on the doctrine of the Self and tells him to compose a shloka about Hari. The vaidya is Murari Gupta, who like Mukunda was of the vaidya community.
[^9]: Murari's humble exaggeration: in Gaudiya Vaishnava theology Lakshmi is the Lord's internal energy and Ananta His expansion, and maya has no power over them. The point is that no one can conquer maya by his own strength (cf. Gita 7.14).
[^10]: Cf. Murari 2.4.22–27; there the Lord's blessing is understood as permission for Murari to tell of His descent.
[^11]: Cf. Murari 2.4.27–32. Kapila is the sage, a descent of Vishnu, teacher of sankhya.
[^12]: Cf. Murari 2.4.33–34. The words "Krishna is maya" express the Mayavada view, in which Krishna's form is a product of maya; for Vaishnavas this is the gravest offence: Krishna's form is eternal and spiritual (cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 17.129–133).
[^13]: The summary covers the whole section; this portion contains vv. 1–35 (the Lord at Advaita's), the rest is in the next portion. Some places in the summary are illegible in the OCR of the edition and are restored by sense (in square brackets).
[^14]: Cf. Murari 2.5.1–19: the Lord goes to Advaita in Shantipur.
[^15]: Cf. Murari 2.5.3–5: the Lord first prostrates Himself before Advaita, "honouring the Vaishnava as Vishnu Himself", and Advaita in dismay falls at His feet. In Gaudiya Vaishnava theology Advaita is Maha-Vishnu and the Lord's eternal servant; His humility is an example for devotees.
[^16]: "Gracious love" — in the original "prasanna maya": the Bengali word maya is used here in its ordinary sense of "tenderness, love, compassion", not illusion. Cf. Murari 2.5.7–10.
[^17]: Cf. Murari 2.5.11–13: "blinded by the maya of Vishnu". Maya here is the Lord's external energy, which by His will keeps non-devotees away from the kirtana.
