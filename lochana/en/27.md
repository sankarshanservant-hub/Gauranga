# Madhya-khanda, part 4 (portion 27)

*The Lord expounds the path of knowledge and proclaims what is highest — bhakti; meditation on Radha and Krishna in Vrindavana; Advaita comes to Navadvipa; the Lord with His club threatens the haters of devotees; Advaita's dance; the truth about Advaita; the ban on speculating about the Self; a song of the beauty of dancing Gaura*

*Raga varadi; refrain: "I take upon myself all misfortunes of Gora's beauty! He has given away the treasure of prema, filling the world with it"*

**36.** Then Mahaprabhu, sitting in His own house,
the Ishvara, speaks of the truth of the Self (adhyatma-tattva):

**37.** "Krishna alone is the Master, the creation and maintenance [of the world];
He Himself, as the one Soul, abides [in all] on earth."

**38.** Saying this, He opened His hand and again closed it into a fist,
showing everyone: "Such is My creation."

**39.** And again He says: "The Truth is by nature pure being;
hear, all of you, what absorption in it is.

**40.** And yet people strive for that true being:
without knowledge of the One there is no liberation — that is why.

**41.** [Without it] one cannot conquer the grievous bonds of samsara;
if one gains knowledge of the One, the bonds fall away.

**42.** Without liberation there is never knowledge of Krishna;
therefore I say: hear — the Lord is reached through knowledge[^1].

**43.** Here, look at these five fingers of My hand:
one is smeared with honey, four with something repulsive.

**44.** Because of the stench one does not even want to look at them,
but the finger with honey one carefully licks with the tongue.

**45.** The One, the Imperishable is Bhagavan alone;
apart from Him there is nothing by which to be liberated."

**46.** Thus in many ways He set forth the path of knowledge (jnana-yoga)
and for a moment fell silent — the Ocean of virtues.

**47.** Then, out of mercy, He again speaks the essence of all truths:
"Without devotion to Sri Krishna there is nothing else."

**48.** He explained to all: "Krishna is reached through knowledge,
[but] prema-bhakti for Krishna's lotus feet is the essence of all.

**49.** When this knowledge comes, the mind turns firmly to Krishna;
when the mind is firm, causeless bhakti arises"[^2].

**50.** Then He became absorbed in meditation on Krishna's lotus feet
and, crying "Hari, Hari!", remembered His lotus feet:

**51.** together with Radha — dark, thrice-bent, [the embodiment of] consciousness and bliss,
the enchanter of Kamadeva, the best of dancers, of many sports,

**52.** in Vrindavana, in a new temple of jewels,
captivating, surrounded by the beautiful cowherd girls.

**53.** Cuckoos, peacocks, sarikas, parrots, swarms of bees;
Vrindavana in full bloom is adorned with many flowers.

**54.** The ground is chintamani, everywhere wish-fulfilling trees,
the kamadhenu cows like herds of surabhis.

**55.** Girdled by the Yamuna, captivating, full of beauty —
seeing that rasa and loveliness, Lakshmi is filled with longing[^3].

**56.** A stream of prema rose and flows from both His eyes,
His body thrills, His eyes are red.

**57.** Now He laughs, now weeps, now dances and sings;
in a faltering voice the Lord said to all:

**58.** "Such are all My devotees:
by their virtues they purify the three worlds."

**59.** Saying this, the Lord, joyful, with His devotees
makes them all dance and dances Himself.

**60.** Thus He lives happily in Navadvipa
together with His devotees, by the Ganga.

**61.** And then on another day Advaita Acharya Gosani
came to Navadvipa to see Vishvambhara.

**62.** Mahaprabhu had gone to Srinivasa's house;
the Acharya, about to come [to Him], performs His bath and worship.

**63.** In Srinivasa's house the Lord, with a joyful heart,
placing a flower on the tip of a staff, said:

**64.** "I have worshipped the club to destroy these wicked ones —
all who do harm to My devotees.

**65.** With it I shall destroy all such people" —
so the Lord spoke in the presence of all.

**66.** "There is one wicked man, a hater of My devotees:
he will suffer leprosy for many births.

**67.** I shall make him dwell in the hell of the pishachas;
he himself will become a hog that eats filth.

**68.** I shall punish his disciples too;
with My club I shall destroy all the godless[^4].

**69.** I had thought to go to the forest —
but here for Me is that great forest:

**70.** some are like tigers, others like stones,
some are like trees, others like grass,

**71.** and some I count as cattle —
that is why I say: this is a great forest for Me[^5].

**72.** I have heard that Advaita Acharya has come here;
if He does not come here, I shall go there Myself."

**73.** At that very moment the Acharya suddenly came
and appeared before the Lord.

**74.** Placing gifts near the lotus feet,
He fell to the ground and prostrated Himself.

**75.** Taking His hand, the Lord says:
"I have come here for Your sake.

**76.** You held My lotus feet on Your head
and, weeping, worshipped them, offering tulasi buds.

**77.** You, whose heart is that of a bhagavata, brought [Me] with Your loud calls:
thanks to Your love all have obtained Me"[^6].

**78.** Saying this, Mahaprabhu sat on a couch
and ordered the Acharya: "Dance!"

**79.** Then Advaita Acharya, the best of brahmanas,
danced long to songs of the ten avataras.

**80.** Srivasa Pandita and all the other devotees,
beside themselves with joy, sing the sankirtana of His qualities.

**81.** Seeing this, Mahaprabhu Gaura Bhagavan,
pleased, said to Him with a serene face:

**82.** "All these children of Yours beg Me for prema;
I shall give them the gift of prema-bhakti — I tell You"[^7].

**83.** Hearing these words, the Acharya rejoiced;
in His heart He knew: "My work is accomplished."

**84.** The Acharya says: "Lord, hear me:
all these people are devoted to Your feet.

**85.** You love Your devotees, You are an ocean of compassion:
giving them the treasure of prema, protect Your devotees."

**86.** Then all of them came to the Lord
and sat down around the Thakura.

**87.** The night was moonlit, the quarters shone;
looking at the Acharya, [the Lord] spoke again:

**88.** "Kamalaksha, You are My greatest devotee:
for Your sake I have come, for Your sake I have appeared.

**89.** Be happy singing My qualities and dancing;
may all people become devotees, seeing this."

**90.** Hearing these words, Srivasa Pandita,
with a serene heart, speaks before the Thakura:

**91.** "I wish to ask something — hear me;
I am afraid to speak, yet my heart is restless.

**92.** I have one doubt, and that is what I ask:
is this Advaita Acharya really Your devotee?"

**93.** Hearing this, Gaura Bhagavan, His face angry,
His eyes red with anger, began to rebuke him:

**94.** "Uddhava and Akrura are two dear ones of Mine —
and you think the Acharya lower than they?

**95.** Is there in Bharata-varsha
a single devotee of Mine equal to the Acharya?

**96.** Since you speak so, you are an ignorant brahmana:
I have no other devotee equal to the Acharya.

**97.** He is the king of the Vaishnavas, I call Him My own soul;
the creator of the world has come to deliver [the people of] Kali.

**98.** The shastras describe Him as Maha-Vishnu;
know that this Advaita is the avatara in the form of a devotee.

**99.** Therefore I tell you firmly:
always glorify the Acharya and be devoted to Him"[^8].

**100.** Hearing this, the brahmana was frightened at heart;
the brahmana fell silent, unable to utter a word.

**101.** Then Gaurahari says again:
"You will no longer speculate about the Self.

**102.** If I see or hear you speculating about the Self,
I shall no longer give you prema.

**103.** For one who disregards knowledge and karma, love for Krishna comes;
knowing this, do not take refuge in knowledge and karma."

**104.** Hearing this, Srivasa Pandita says:
"Give me this boon: let my heart forget it."

**105.** Murari said: "I do not know the doctrine of the Self."
The Lord says: "You know it — from Kamalaksha![^9]

**106.** With a pure heart be firmly devoted to Krishnachandra:
before the rasa of bhakti, liberation is a maidservant."

**107.** Hearing these words, all rejoiced
and in their hearts accepted the order: "We shall carry it out."

**108.** Intoxicated with the honey of the lotus feet of Hari-Hara,
they dance in joy like gods[^10].

**109.** Such is the wondrous story of the pastimes in Nadia:
Lochana has told how Gora preached prema.

*Raga sindhura*

**110.** His eyes are red lotuses, the pupils bees
drowning in the nectar of compassion.
His face is the full moon: at its radiance the heart weeps;
how many beginnings of prema are in it!

*Refrain: "The town of Nadia rejoices: swaying under the weight of prema, the moon, Shachi's darling, dances. They cry 'Jaya, jaya!' and auspicious words; seeing Him, all are amazed — the King of dancers, the enchanter of Madana!"*

**111.** His body is full of thrill, with drops of sweat upon it;
the hairs on end — like a golden kadamba.
At the onset of prema His body is like the morning sun;
His conch-like throat holds back half-spoken words.

**112.** In the fragrance of His lotus feet, ringed by ten moons of nails,
above them shine golden anklets.
When He walks, shining, lightning flashes;
the assembly of the gods is astonished.

**113.** In the midst of the earth of seven continents Navadvipa is adorned,
and in it a new manifestation of prema;
there the new Gaurahari sings the kirtana of Hari's qualities —
earth and sky rejoice.

**114.** Like a lion cub, He roars deeply again and again;
"hum!" — the waves of the ocean of prema.
He cries "Hari, Hari!" — and the world fell into rapture,
and the wives of noble houses forgot the honour of both houses[^11].

**115.** By the radiance of His body the sun is like a lamp;
in it is the play of lila and attire.
His charming body surpasses millions of flower-bowed Kamadevas;
in it He reveals prema.

**116.** The form of His face surpasses hundreds of thousands of full moons,
and on it are lovely moon-marks of sandal.
When the corners of His eyes move, nectar streams forth:
even those deluded from birth obtain prema.

**117.** His gait is that of an intoxicated elephant, He is wholly in the grip of bhava;
now He laughs, now starts and looks around.
His appearance enchants women; seeing Him, the whole land lost its head,
seeing [Him], they feel the pain of love.

**118.** To what shall I compare Him — the essence of compassion embodied?
Such is my Gora-raya.
In prema the people of Nadia know neither night nor day;
joyfully sings Lochana Dasa.

[^1]: Vv. 36–46 (as in Murari, 2.5.20–28) set out the path of knowledge (jnana-yoga) as those seeking liberation understand it; the Lord expounds it in order then to place bhakti above everything (vv. 47–49) and to forbid speculation about the Self (vv. 101–106). In v. 42 the 1983 edition has "without bhakti" instead of "without liberation". According to the teaching of the Gaudiya Vaishnavas (and of Bhaktisiddhanta), Krishna is reached by bhakti alone ("bhaktyaham ekaya grahyah", Bhagavata 11.14.21), and liberation is not a precondition but a by-product of devotion.
[^2]: "Knowledge" here is the understanding that Krishna is Bhagavan Himself; it leads to firm faith and to causeless, uninterrupted bhakti (cf. Bhagavata 1.2.7). Cf. Murari 2.5.29–30.
[^3]: Cf. the Brahma-samhita (5.29, 5.56) and en/24, note 11. On Lakshmi's longing for the rasa of Vraja see Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 9. Vv. 56–58 — cf. Murari 2.5.30–33.
[^4]: Cf. Murari 2.6.1–6. The hater of devotees is perhaps the brahmana Gopala Chapala, struck with leprosy for offending Srivasa (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17).
[^5]: Cf. Murari 2.6.7–9. "To go to the forest" hints at the coming sannyasa.
[^6]: According to Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, the Lord descended in answer to the loud calls (hunkaras) and worship of Advaita, who offered tulasi leaves and Ganga water to the Shalagrama (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 3). Cf. Murari 2.6.10–13.
[^7]: The "children" are Advaita's sons (Achyutananda and others) or His disciples. Cf. Murari 2.6.14–15.
[^8]: Cf. Murari 2.6.16–22. So too in Gaudiya Vaishnava theology: Advaita is Maha-Vishnu, who has taken the form of a devotee (bhakta-avatara); Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 6. Kamalaksha is Advaita's worldly name.
[^9]: That is, "you heard it from Advaita". According to the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 19), Advaita for a time deliberately preached the doctrine of the Self (the Yoga-vasishtha) so as to provoke the Lord's punishment. Cf. Murari 2.6.23–26.
[^10]: "Hari-Hara" — so also in Murari (2.6.27); probably Hari is Gaura, and Hara is Advaita, revered as one with Sadashiva.
[^11]: That is, they cast aside the honour of both their fathers' and their husbands' houses — an image from the songs of the gopis; cf. Lochana Dasa's "nagari-bhava" (en/09, note 9).
