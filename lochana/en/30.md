# Madhya-khanda, part 7 (portion 30)

*The section "Pastimes with the Devotees and the Deliverance of Jagai and Madhai": the devotees place on their heads the water of Nityananda's feet; Haridasa in the form of Brahma; the arrival of Advaita; the Lord speaks of the reasons for His descent; He bids the devotees give the holy name from house to house; the sins of Jagai and Madhai; sankirtana through the town; the brothers wound Nityananda; the Lord summons Sudarshana, and Nityananda pleads for the offenders*

## Pastimes with the Devotees and the Deliverance of Jagai and Madhai

> *Summary by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.* One day, when Sriman Mahaprabhu was sporting in the bliss of prema with the devotees headed by Srivasa, Haridasa, Murari and Mukunda, Sriman Nityananda Prabhu came there. At Sriman Mahaprabhu's command the devotees placed on their heads the water of Sriman Nityananda Prabhu's feet and [began] to dance in the bliss of prema. When Haridasa and Sriman Advaita Prabhu came there, [Haridasa] offered Him praise; then Sriman Mahaprabhu rose together with the devotees and showed him due honour, fed him and told him of preaching prema without distinction between the worthy and the unworthy, and commanded His devotees to go from door to door and distribute the holy name and prema. But when the devotees refused, [fearing] two great sinful brahmanas named Jagai and Madhai, Sriman Mahaprabhu Himself glorified before the devotees the greatness of the holy name — for example, the deliverance of the great sinner Ajamila by namabhasa, [the name uttered while intending another,] — and, to deliver these two sons of brahmanas, expressed His wish to go round the town Himself with the devotees, performing kirtana with mridangas and karatalas. Delighted at Sriman Mahaprabhu's words, the devotees all gathered together and began kirtana; all around resounded with the holy name. Hearing the kirtana, Jagai and Madhai in great anger began to abuse Sriman Mahaprabhu in every way; [Sriman Mahaprabhu and] Nityananda by Their own will [paid no heed] and [looked on them with mercy]. At last they struck Sri Nityananda on the head with a shard of a pot. Sriman Mahaprabhu, unable to bear [the insult and pain] of His own [devotee], summoned Sudarshana to destroy those two. Sri Nityananda Prabhu forgave Jagai and Madhai all their offences and begged for their lives, [and Mahaprabhu] cast a merciful glance on them. [By the grace] of Sri Nityananda, Jagai and Madhai were delivered and became great devotees. Then the author glorified the compassion of Nityananda and Mahaprabhu.[^1]

*Raga dhanashi; refrain: "What a wonder in the midst of Nadia! Golden Gauranga dances — a great wonder!"*

**1.** Thus the Thakura sports in Navadvipa:
forgetting Himself, He reveals prema abundantly.

**2.** Independent as He is, He becomes subject to His devotees
and, as if most humble, begs everyone to take prema.[^2]

**3.** The Lord moves in His lila unnoticed by people;
only His own understand His hints.

**4.** Shrinivasa, Haridasa, Murari, Mukunda,
understanding His hint, sing, and the bliss of prema grows.

**5.** Overwhelmed with joy, His associates dance and sing;
at that time the Avadhuta-raya came again.

**6.** "The Avadhuta has come!" — "Jaya, jaya!" rings out,
and all the people joyfully sing auspicious songs.

**7.** His gait is slow, like a maddened elephant's;
hearing the cry "Hari, Hari!", He is not master of Himself.

**8.** Swaying, He walks, blocking the road;
after two steps He stops and looks all around.

**9.** His whole body thrills from feet to head:
each hair standing on end surpasses the kadamba's filaments.

**10.** With bent neck He looks to both sides with red eyes;
at times He runs in rapture, calling loudly.

**11.** So, with hundreds of people running behind Him,
absorbed in bliss He came to where Gora-raya was.

**12.** Seeing Nityananda, the Lord Gaurasundara [embraces Him]:
the two embrace, overflowing with prema.

**13.** Tears of prema's bliss flow from the eyes of both;
both are absorbed in bliss, Their bodies unsteady.

**14.** Both dance in joy, and with Them Their own,
like the cowherd boys with Krishna and Balarama.

**15.** When the dance ended, the Lord told everyone
to wash Nityananda's feet:

**16.** "Place on your heads the water of Nityananda's feet —
you will attain supreme prema, waves of bliss."

**17.** When Mahaprabhu gave this command,
the joy in the hearts of all who heard it grew.

**18.** They longed for one thing and received more — the Lord's command;
they placed on their heads the water that had washed [His] feet.

**19.** Then Avadhuta Prabhu, hearing the Lord's command —
tears brimmed in His reddened eyes.

**20.** Rising, He joyfully embraces everyone:
the ocean of prema surged in waves of bliss.

**21.** Overwhelmed by prema, all weep
and clasp the Avadhuta's feet to their hearts.

**22.** The great festival of prema grew boundlessly:
within — radiance, without — the transformations of ecstasy.

**23.** Seeing this, the Lord Gaura Bhagavan
looks on with a serene face, rejoicing at heart.[^3]

**24.** All, surrounding [Him] on every side, offer praise;
at that time Haridasa suddenly came:

**25.** on his neck a necklace of pure crystal,
golden bells tinkling on his red feet;

**26.** his whole body thrilled, his eyes full of tears,
his body swaying with prema, he roars "hum!" and calls aloud.

**27.** In the fullness of prema he dances before the Lord —
the universe cannot contain his happiness in prema's bliss.

**28.** [The Lord], by the custom of hospitality [offering] water for the feet, arghya and water for sipping,
Himself bade [him] take food.[^4]

**29.** At that time Advaita Acharya suddenly
came to the Lord.

**30.** The Thakura rose and honoured him,
and all, rising, bowed to him.

**31.** Dancing and dancing, [Haridasa], in whom Brahma manifested,
falls at the Lord's feet and prostrates;

**32.** with four mouths, reciting the Vedas, he offers praise.
"Be calm!" says the Lord, lifts him up and embraces him.

**33.** Becoming calm, Haridasa dances, weeps and laughs,
knowing no directions, floating in prema's bliss.[^5]

**34.** Then Acharya Gosani was filled with reverent awe
and, taking the command on his head, ate there.

**35.** So the Lord with all His own
sits secluded in the house, smiling gently.

**36.** Among His own the Lord speaks of Himself —
of the reasons for which He came to the earth:

**37.** "To taste My own bhava, to destroy irreligion,
to establish dharma and to reveal the kirtana of the name.

**38.** In every land, in every home, I will reveal
the bhava of Vraja — servitude, friendship, parental love and amorous love.

**39.** Above all I will give to taste the treasure of prema for Radha and Krishna:
I Myself will taste it and make the three worlds taste it.

**40.** To gods and asuras I will give this treasure of prema,
to chandalas, yavanas, the ignorant, women and children.

**41.** Bringing the happiness of Vrindavana to Nadia,
together with all of you I will give it to taste in every land."[^6]

**42.** A most wondrous story — the pastimes in Nadia;
all these stories I shall proclaim together.

**43.** Gadadhara and Narahari sit on both sides,
Sri Raghunandana delights near His feet;

**44.** Advaita Acharya and Nityananda-raya —
and the Thakura Himself sings the song of His own qualities;

**45.** Murari, Mukunda Datta and Shrinivasa,
Haridasa and the rest — all abodes of prema;

**46.** Shuklambara, Vakreshvara, Shriman, Sanjaya,
Shridhara Pandita and the other great souls.[^7]

**47.** Who can tell the glory of even one of them?
Gauradeva Himself has descended to the earth!

**48.** The manifestation in Nadia has no comparison —
so says with a joyful heart this Lochana Dasa.

*Raga gurjari; disha: "Gorachand is my life!" (with vocables); refrain: "Hari, Rama, Narayana — golden Gora, Shachi's darling!"*

**49.** I shall tell an unprecedented story — hear, all:
whoever hears it is freed from all sins.

**50.** In Navadvipa, in His own home,
Gaurachandra is in joyful play with His disciples.

**51.** Gathering all His devotees together,
He was lost in prema's bliss in the sankirtana of His own qualities.

**52.** Laughing, the Lord said to all the devotees:
"Give this name of Hari of Mine in every home.

**53.** All who live in Navadvipa — children and old people,
chandalas and the wretched, the good and the wicked —

**54.** teach them all the name of Hari, tying it fast,
so that all people may easily cross the ocean of existence."[^8]

**55.** Hearing this, all the devotees said to the Lord:
"We cannot give the name of Hari in every home.

**56.** In this Navadvipa there is one calamity:
most wicked [men], whose sins have no end —

**57.** two brothers, greatly sinful brahmanas,
the masters of Navadvipa, Jagai and Madhai.[^9]

**58.** They spare neither brahmana women, nor Muslim women, nor the wives of gurus;
getting liquor, they abandon all duties.

**59.** They constantly do violence to gods, gurus and brahmanas;
when they go out, they do not return home without a killing.

**60.** Killings of brahmanas, cows, women — by hundreds;
I cannot write how many sins they have committed.

**61.** They live on the bank of the Ganga, yet do not bathe in the Ganga;
never in their lives have they worshipped the gods.

**62.** They constantly punish their own kin and friends;
towards the sankirtana of Krishna's qualities they are utter atheists."

**63.** One day, when the Lord was among His own,
in the course of conversation talk of them arose.

**64.** Everyone told [of them] before the Lord;
hearing it, the Lord grew angry, pondering within.

**65.** His face turned red, both eyes reddened:
"What you have said, My heart too bears witness to.

**66.** There was a sinful brahmana named Ajamila;
at the hour of death he uttered the name "Narayana".

**67.** Out of love for his son he uttered the name "Narayana" —
and the brahmana, receiving a divine body, attained Vaikuntha.[^10]

**68.** Jagai and Madhai are more sinful than he:
by what means will they be delivered?

**69.** For their sake My heart is pained;
all of you, hear what I say in answer.

**70.** The sankirtana of Hari's name is the dharma of the age of Kali:
by the sankirtana of the name and qualities I will accomplish the whole task.

**71.** Bring your friends, wherever they may be;
gathering all together, perform sankirtana.

**72.** Taking singers and players, mridangas and karatalas,
sing aloud the sweet kirtana of the name.

**73.** I will go round the town performing kirtana."
Hearing these words, all came:

**74.** Advaita Acharya and his people,
the Avadhuta Nityananda with a serene face,

**75.** Haridasa, Shrinivasa with his four brothers,
Murari, Mukunda Datta, Pandita Gadai,

**76.** Sri Chandrashekhara Acharya and Shuklambara —
all together came to the Thakura's house.

**77.** All the devotees, wherever they were,
at the Lord's command gathered together.

**78.** When all had gathered and sankirtana began,
the Lord Vishvambhara Hari set forth.

**79.** In the town of Nadia waves of bliss arose;
the cry "Hari, Hari!" rises to the sky.

**80.** Meanwhile Jagai and Madhai lie in their house:
drunk on their liquor, both brothers sleep.

**81.** Along that very road the Lord goes performing kirtana,
and all the people of Nadia run to see.

**82.** In the roar of the kirtana — karatalas, mridangas and the rest —
on all sides one hears only "Hari, Hari!".

**83.** At the roar of the kirtana the two brothers awoke;
raising their heads they look and angrily shout: "Seize, seize!"

**84.** With reddened eyes they glare in anger:
"What noise is this we hear? As if a spear had struck our ears!

**85.** Every sound is like a spear in the heart.
If they value their lives, let them be silent!"

**86.** Their attendants say to them:
"Restrain yourselves, masters — with whom are you angry?

**87.** Give the order and we will go at once and forbid them;
who would then dare to come this way again?

**88.** It is Jagannatha's son, the brahmana Nimai Pandita,
performing kirtana surrounded by brahmanas."

**89.** "Forbid them: let them go another way!
Let them be silent if they value their lives.

**90.** They go about making a pointless din, not knowing the root of things —
at my hand they will lose caste, life and family!"

**91.** Saying this, they sent their messenger,
and he gave [their message] to the Thakura; Shachi's son listens —

**92.** and sings the sankirtana of Hari's name all the louder:
raising His arms, again and again He cries "Hari, Hari!".

**93.** Doubling the prema, He increases the exultation:
the cry "Hari, Hari!" touches the sky.

**94.** The sinful hearts cannot bear it:
the two brothers went out to the outer gate.

**95.** Their eyes red with anger, their faces crimson,
their clothes slipping again and again from their bodies.

**96.** Staggering, blind with anger, they go
and with threats and roars shout: "Stop, stop!"

**97.** Standing in front, they look all around
and shout loudly: "Know your place — get out!

**98.** Hey you, wretched brahmana, Shani hangs over your life!" —
and with these words they hurl foul abuse.[^11]

**99.** Seeing their anger, the people of Nadia were frightened:
looking around, all were terrified.

**100.** Advaita Acharya Gosani and Nityananda,
Haridasa, Shrinivasa, Murari, Mukunda —

**101.** and the Thakura Himself, Vishvambhara-raya,
together with His associates, sings Hari's qualities.

**102.** Happily, without weariness, they sing Hari's qualities,
while Jagai and Madhai in anger make mischief.

**103.** In anger both brothers run with sticks in their hands
and find before them a shard of a broken pot.

**104.** In anger they hurl that rim of the pot —
and it struck Nityananda's head with full force.

**105.** The shard struck with full force, blood flows in a stream;
seeing it, all [the Lord's] own cry out in dismay.[^12]

**106.** Seeing it, the Thakura was deeply grieved at heart
and called out, [standing] before those sinners:

**107.** "There are none more wicked than you two:
the earth spreads your names as the very name of sin.

**108.** You had done everything but one thing —
and now you have done that too; see it plainly!"[^13]

**109.** Saying this, Mahaprabhu went to Nityananda
and bound His head with His own cloth.

**110.** He knows the greatness of Nityananda Shripada:
lest His blood fall on the ground —

**111.** knowing that misfortune would then befall the earth —
the Lord, fearing this, bound His head with the cloth.[^14]

**112.** In anger Gaurahari summons Sudarshana,
and Sudarshana stood [before Him] with joined palms:

**113.** "Why, O Ishvara, have You summoned me?
Glory, glory to Mahaprabhu, the son of Shachi!"

**114.** The Lord says: "Destroy Jagai and Madhai:
by striking Nityananda they have wounded My heart."

**115.** Hearing this, Sudarshana, terrible as the world's dissolution,
rushed towards Jagai and Madhai.

**116.** Jagai and Madhai saw Sudarshana:
their bodies began to tremble, their minds were seized with fear.

**117.** Seeing Sudarshana, Nityananda Prabhu laughs:
"What has Bhagavan done by this great manifestation?

**118.** In Your compassion You have delivered the three worlds —
the poor and lowly, the fallen, the vile and the wicked.

**119.** Deliver Jagai and Madhai — be the Friend of the fallen,
keep the glory of the name 'Purifier of the fallen'."

**120.** Saying this, Nityananda fell at His feet
and spoke humbly at the Lord's feet:

**121.** "Lord, give these two fallen ones to Me:
let the name 'Purifier of the fallen' be renowned.

**122.** In other ages You destroyed the demons —
deliver these two in their very bodies."[^15]

**123.** Hearing Nityananda's words, the merciful Lord [says]:
"Blessed, blessed is Nityananda, the son of Rohini![^16]

**124.** All the scriptures say that I am under Your control:
what You have said I will surely do.

**125.** Whoever, having taken birth, even once says 'Nityananda'
is purified — that person is Mine."

**126.** Mahaprabhu went home with His own,
and Jagai and Madhai remained, astonished.

[^1]: The summary covers the whole section (vv. 1–169); this portion contains vv. 1–126, the rest is in the next. In the OCR of the edition many places are garbled or illegible (part of the summary ended up among the verses); what is restored by sense is in square brackets. Cf. Murari 2.10 and 2.13; Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 13; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.
[^2]: In the 1983 edition, after v. 2 there are 7 more couplets not found in Bhaktisiddhanta's edition: one evening the Lord playfully takes away His associates' clothes and, amused by their embarrassment, gives them back (cf. Murari 2.10.16–17, where this play is understood as a repetition of Krishna's play with the gopis' garments).
[^3]: Vv. 5–23 — cf. Murari 2.10.18–21. Nityananda's swaying gait, red eyes and loud cries are signs of His intoxication with prema (cf. en/28, note 12). The washing of His feet at the Lord's command teaches honour to the Vaishnava: the water of a devotee's feet purifies and bestows prema.
[^4]: In the 1983 edition this couplet stands after Advaita's arrival (vv. 29–30), and the honours and the meal refer to him (cf. v. 34); in Bhaktisiddhanta's edition it comes before his arrival, so that they may also be referred to Haridasa (cf. Murari 2.10.4, where the Lord feeds Haridasa mahaprasada). We keep the order of the edition.
[^5]: According to Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, Brahma and Prahlada are united in Haridasa Thakura (Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika); here Brahma manifests in him and praises the Lord with four mouths. Cf. Murari 2.10.24.
[^6]: Vv. 36–41 — the reasons for the Lord's descent: to taste His own bhava and to establish the dharma of the age, the sankirtana of the name (cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 3–4, where the inner reason is to taste Radha's love, and the outer one to spread the holy name and the prema of Vraja). Cf. Gita 4.8 on "destroying irreligion and establishing dharma".
[^7]: Vv. 43–46 — a list of associates; the presence of Narahari and Raghunandana, as above (en/29, note 7), is mentioned only by Lochana Dasa.
[^8]: "Tying it fast" — literally "making a knot (granthi)": perhaps counting the names on knots of a string is also meant.
[^9]: "Masters (thakuras) of Navadvipa" — according to the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 13), Jagai and Madhai, brothers of a noble brahmana family, were the town's keepers of order (kotwals) and were sunk in vice.
[^10]: The story of Ajamila is Bhagavata 6.1–2: the dying sinner called his son named Narayana, and even such an utterance of the name — namabhasa, the "shadow of the name", the name directed to another — saved him. Bhaktisiddhanta's summary stresses precisely this.
[^11]: Shani is Saturn, the planet-deity who brings misfortune; "Shani hangs over your life" is a threat: "you are done for".
[^12]: In Lochana Dasa both brothers throw the shard. According to the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 13), only Madhai struck Nityananda, while Jagai held him back from a second blow and was the first to receive mercy; so too in the 1983 edition, which adds couplets about how Nityananda, heedless of the pain, asks the brothers to sing the name of Hari. In Murari (2.13) the Lord promises to deliver Jagai and Madhai at Srivasa's request.
[^13]: That is, they have offended a saint — Nityananda, a sannyasi and a Vaishnava; offence to a Vaishnava (vaishnava-aparadha) is the gravest of sins.
[^14]: Nityananda is Balarama, the Lord Himself (Sankarshana, the source of Ananta-Shesha); to spill His blood on the earth is a great offence that threatens the world with calamity.
[^15]: The Lord's anger at the offenders of His devotee is also a manifestation of His love; and in the age of Kali the Lord takes up no weapons, but delivers by the holy name and prema (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 3, 17). Nityananda's intercession for His offenders is an image of His unparalleled mercy: according to Gaudiya Vaishnava teaching, Nityananda's grace makes no distinction between the worthy and the unworthy (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 5).
[^16]: The son of Rohini is Balarama; thus Lochana Dasa directly calls Nityananda Balarama, as does the whole Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition.
