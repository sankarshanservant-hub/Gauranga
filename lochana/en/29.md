# Madhya-khanda, part 6 (portion 29)

*The section "Meeting with Advaita and Haridasa": the Lord weeps after seeing Krishna in a dream; Nityananda beholds the Lord's forms; Nityananda at Advaita's; Advaita worships the Lord in Srivasa's house; the arrival of Haridasa; Nityananda's kaupina; the Lord vanishes with Srivasa and returns; the greatness of prema*

## Meeting with Advaita and Haridasa

> *Summary by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.* One night, in the third watch, Sriman Mahaprabhu suddenly began to weep; Shachi-devi in alarm asked the reason for His weeping, and Mahaprabhu told His mother how He had seen Krishna in a dream. Sriman Nityananda Prabhu [beheld] the six-armed and other forms of Mahaprabhu; then at Mahaprabhu's command the devotees took Him to Advaita's [house], and there They spent two days in great bliss. [When] Murari [told the Lord] of Nityananda Prabhu's manifestations of prema, [the Lord laughed]. When Advaita [Prabhu], seeing Sriman Mahaprabhu in the house of Srivasa Pandita, worshipped Him, the Vaishnavas [danced in bliss]. [Thakura] Haridasa came [and met the Lord]. When Nityananda Prabhu [was taking leave] of Sriman Mahaprabhu, Mahaprabhu took Nityananda's kaupina [and distributed it] to the devotees, [and they tied it] on their heads. [When the Lord disappeared,] the devotees lamented, and when He returned, they were seized with boundless bliss.[^1]

*Raga turi; refrain: "Gora has remembered the past — that is why Gora weeps!"*[^2]

**1.** Now I shall tell another wondrous story —
of such deeds of Gora as none has seen or heard.

**2.** The Lord of all worlds has descended on earth —
why do you not count this your good fortune?

**3.** Cunning does not leave the vile hearts of the godless:
their hearts are entangled in this maya of Vishnu.[^3]

**4.** They will become pure when they hear of Gora's qualities:
this is the means that cures the disease of worldly existence.

**5.** One day, as the third watch of the night was passing,
Vishvambhara suddenly began to weep.

**6.** Astonished, Shachi asks her son:
"Why are you weeping, child? Tell me.

**7.** Hearing your weeping, I burn all over;
I cannot hold my heart — an arrow pierces my breast."

**8.** Hearing His mother's words, He is silent,
and then, sitting up on the bed, tells the dream He saw:

**9.** "I saw a Person shining like a fresh raincloud,
with a crest of peacock feathers, wondrously radiant;

**10.** bracelets, armlets, a necklace, bells on His feet,
on His forehead a moon of sandal, richly shining;

**11.** clad in yellow, with a flute in His left hand —
such a Beautiful One I saw, and My heart rejoiced."

**12.** He weeps, streams of tears flow from His eyes:
"Do not tell anyone, so that no one else may hear."

**13.** Hearing such words, the nectarean speech
risen from Vishvambhara's lips, Shachi rejoiced.

**14.** Vishvambhara's whole body is filled with thrills,
and the whole house glitters with the radiance of His body.[^4]

**15.** At that time Nityananda the Avadhuta-raya
came there from Shrinivasa's house.

**16.** Coming, He saw the Lord's beautiful body:
radiant, mighty-armed, with a deep navel;

**17.** in His right hand a club, in His left hand a flute,
in His palm a lotus, in His left palm a bow;

**18.** His lustre like molten gold, the Kaustubha on His breast,
two makara-shaped earrings adorning His cheeks;

**19.** a necklace with the lustre of emerald adorning His neck —
such wondrous attire the Avadhuta-raya beholds.

**20.** Then He sees Him four-armed — without the bow and flute:
the form all the same, with four excellent arms.

**21.** A moment later He sees Him two-armed —
in a form and deeds gracious to the world.[^5]

**22.** Seeing this form, the Avadhuta-raya
embraces His own, dances and sings.

**23.** In rapture He dances, beside Himself,
plunged into the great ocean of prema.

**24.** [The Lord said:] "Shrinivasa, Narayana, Rama, Murari,
go, the four of you, with Him.

**25.** The Avadhuta will go to the house of Advaita Acharya —
let him know of it: He is a great wonder."

**26.** When Mahaprabhu gave this command,
the hearts of all who heard it rejoiced.

**27.** All set off quickly with Nityananda
and with joyful hearts came to the Acharya's house.

**28.** Bowing down, they told everything;
hearing it, the Acharya dances in bliss, beside himself.

**29.** Both joyfully embrace each other:
the Acharya dances happily, Nityananda dances.

**30.** Happily They sank wholly into the ocean of bliss:
again and again roars of "hum!" rise up in waves.

**31.** Both speak confidentially of Gauranga's deeds;
telling and hearing, both are enraptured at heart.

**32.** So they spent two days in joy;
all the Vaishnavas joyfully sang of Gora's qualities.

**33.** Again respectfully asking leave at Advaita's feet,
they hurried to see Gaurahari.

**34.** Coming before the Lord and bowing down,
with joined palms Murari tells everything:

**35.** all the confidential play that took place in the Acharya's house;
hearing it, the Lord was delighted and laughed.[^6]

**36.** The next day the Acharya himself, the best of brahmanas,
came to see His lotus feet.

**37.** In the house of Shrinivasa Pandita Mahaprabhu
sits in the shrine room, smiling gently.

**38.** The Lord sits in great joy on a divine seat;
the room glitters with the radiance of His body.

**39.** The beauty of His holy body is like molten gold,
ruddy with prema, like the morning sun.

**40.** Divine ornaments, garlands, fragrant sandal;
His beautiful face surpasses the full moon.

**41.** Gadadhara and Narahari stand on both sides,
and Sri Raghunandana gazes at the moon of His face.[^7]

**42.** All around Him are the devotees,
like the shining moon in a ring of stars.

**43.** Nityananda, sitting before Him in the bliss of prema,
gazes at His face and again and again laughs and weeps.

**44.** At that very time, seeing [Him], the Acharya, the moon among brahmanas,
again and again roars "hum!" and lets out a lion's roar.

**45.** His body from feet to head filled with thrills;
the universe cannot contain the joy of his heart.

**46.** The brahmana offered various gifts
and laid new divine garments at His lotus feet.

**47.** With tulasi blossoms he worshipped His feet,
with a fragrant garland of malati and with fragrant sandal.

**48.** He prostrates, falling on the ground like a rod —
and Mahaprabhu Himself lifted him up.

**49.** Accepting the worship, Gaura Bhagavan
gave the remnants as a gift to His devotees.

**50.** Those garments and ornaments adorn His holy body;
crying "Hari, Hari!", He dances with them all.

**51.** Advaita Acharya and Nityananda-raya,
Shrinivasa, Murari, Mukunda sing His qualities.

**52.** All the Vaishnavas together in joyful exultation
forget themselves, absorbed in rasa.

**53.** All praise one another: "Blessed, blessed!" —
and deem the happiness of kaivalya and nirvana insignificant.

**54.** In the happiness of prema's bliss they know neither day nor night,
ever overwhelmed with inner joy.

**55.** The dance begins at sunrise and lasts into the night;
from evening they dance until the next dawn.[^8]

**56.** So night and day they are immersed in the bliss of prema.
When the dance ended, Gora commanded all:

**57.** "Bathe and worship the Deity, all of you, in your own homes,
and after your meal come again."

**58.** So all, having completed their rites,
came and assembled at His lotus feet.

**59.** At that very time the great-souled Haridasa,
whose heart ceaselessly exults in the name of Krishna —

**60.** a bee intoxicated with the honey of Krishna's lotus feet,
who in absorption in rasa becomes a young lion —

**61.** suddenly arrived in Navadvipa.
"Come, come!" the Lord greets him, laughing.

**62.** With full love He embraced him tightly,
and Mahaprabhu bade him sit down.

**63.** The wise Haridasa bows down,
and the Thakura Himself, taking his hand, raises him.

**64.** He anointed his body with fragrant sandal
and gave him His own prasada garland from His body.

**65.** The Thakura bade him eat,
and he partook of abundant mahaprasada.[^9]

**66.** So Mahaprabhu with a joyful heart
delights in the sankirtana of Hari's name and qualities,

**67.** [with Him] Haridasa, Advaita Acharya, Nityananda,
Shrinivasa and all the rest of His own.

**68.** Days and nights they passed in joyful play of prema's bliss.
[At last the Lord] dismissed the Acharya: "Go home today."

**69.** Receiving the command, Advaita Acharya went home,
lost in happiness at all he had seen and heard.

**70.** Then Nityananda the Avadhuta-raya
took leave of the Lord.

**71.** The Thakura went along to see Him off;
from love He cannot turn back and went far.

**72.** The Avadhuta-raya cannot bring Himself to leave [Him] and go;
with great effort He took His leave.

**73.** At the parting the Lord says:
"Give them all one kaupina."

**74.** At the Lord's word the Thakura Avadhuta
gave them all a wondrous kaupina.

**75.** The Lord Himself, laughing, took the kaupina,
divided it and gave it to all His devotees.

**76.** Receiving this kaupina joyfully as grace,
all, rejoicing, tied it on their heads.[^10]

**77.** Taking leave at Nityananda's lotus feet,
they go home together with the Lord.

**78.** All came home with sorrowful hearts
and sit in their houses, their eyes brimming with tears.

**79.** After a while, having bathed and worshipped the Deity,
in the evening all came to see Gaurahari.

**80.** And Nityananda went to Acharya Gosani
and joyfully speaks of Gora day and night.

**81.** Hear, all of you, the story of the next day —
then you will attain prema-bhakti at the feet of Sri Krishna.

**82.** It is a wondrous story, beyond the reach of the world and the Vedas;
this song of Gora's qualities is the essence of nectar.

**83.** Seeing His own, the Lord embraces them
and, hearing of His own qualities, moves about dancing.

**84.** All around, everyone happily dances and sings,
and in the middle, absorbed in bliss, Gora-raya dances.

**85.** Suddenly the Lord Vishvambhara, taking Shrinivasa by the hand,
went off — no one knows where.

**86.** All around, everyone is dancing and singing,
but Mahaprabhu is not in the middle — He cannot be seen.

**87.** Fear arose in everyone's heart;
all the people weep, reckoning all lost.

**88.** Rolling on the ground they weep and find no calm;
all the people of Nadia weep, recalling His qualities.

**89.** Everyone runs about searching from house to house,
unable to open their eyes for the tears.

**90.** "We shall take poison and all die!
Why, where has our Lord Gora gone?"

**91.** So lament all His own.
Hearing it, Shachi ran [there], beside herself.

**92.** Without arranging her clothes, without tying her hair,
beating her breast, she runs, frantic with grief.

**93.** "Child, child!" she calls aloud to Vishvambhara. —
"Come home: it is already the second watch of the day!

**94.** Lamp of my family, moon of Nadia,
pupil of my eyes — who has blinded me?"

**95.** Seeing the extreme anguish of all,
the Lord, affectionate to His devotees, suddenly came.

**96.** As the sun rises in pitch darkness,
so the Lord illumined the hearts of the Vaishnavas.

**97.** Some, falling at His feet, weep with cries of anguish;
some, seeing His holy face, dance in rapture.

**98.** Some say: "Mahaprabhu, without Your feet
there is darkness in all ten directions — we see nothing with our eyes."

**99.** Shachi, beside herself with love, takes her son in her arms
and showers His lotus face with hundreds of thousands of kisses:

**100.** "You are my staff — the blind woman's — the pupil of both my eyes,
the soul of this body; apart from You I have no one.

**101.** My whole world had become empty —
with the rising of Gorachand the darkness vanished."[^11]

**102.** Murari, Mukunda Datta and Haridasa
humbly say: "Hear, Shrinivasa:

**103.** the Lord has no servant dearer than you;
by your grace these feet have appeared [to us].

**104.** What can all of us say to you?
Considering [us] your own, be kind to us yourself."[^12]

**105.** Saying this, all together sing Hari's qualities,
and Gora-raya, intoxicated with love, dances.

**106.** Hear, all of you, this wondrous story:
in Navadvipa the jewel of love has been proclaimed.

**107.** The Lord's prema-bhakti is rare in the three worlds —
who has the power to attain it?

**108.** Lakshmi, Ananta, Shiva or Sanatana —
none of them knows the inner essence of this prema-bhakti.[^13]

**109.** Such prema-bhakti the Lord reveals —
so says with a joyful heart this Lochana Dasa.

[^1]: The summary covers the whole section (vv. 1–109). In the OCR of the edition it is almost illegible (lines of two columns are mixed); it has been restored from the surviving words and from the table of contents in Bhaktisiddhanta's preface (the fifth chapter of the Madhya-khanda); what is restored by sense is in square brackets. The section as a whole follows Murari Gupta (2.9–2.10).
[^2]: The raga is given only in the 1983 edition. The refrain is read after the 1903 edition (in the OCR of Bhaktisiddhanta's edition it is garbled); it refers to the Lord's weeping in vv. 5–12: Gora remembers His former pastimes — the pastimes of Krishna.
[^3]: Vishnu-maya here is the Lord's external energy, which by His will keeps the godless away from Him (cf. en/26, note 17); the Lord Himself is never subject to maya.
[^4]: Vv. 5–14 — cf. Murari 2.9.2–6, where the Lord sees the Boy Krishna in a dream. The request to tell no one: the Lord conceals His intimate bhava.
[^5]: Vv. 15–23 — cf. Murari 2.9.6–11: Nityananda sees the form of Hari with club, discus, conch, flute, bow and lotus, then the four-armed and two-armed forms. The translation of the second line of v. 20 is tentative.
[^6]: Vv. 24–35 — cf. Murari 2.9.13–16. Advaita lived in Shantipura, on the bank of the Ganga. Rama is Sri Rama Pandita; Narayana is an associate of the Lord in Navadvipa.
[^7]: Gadadhara is Radha (en/25, note 8). Narahari Sarakara is Lochana Dasa's guru, and Raghunandana his nephew, both of Shrikhanda; only Lochana Dasa mentions their presence in this scene. In Murari (2.9.17–18) Advaita sees the Lord on the seat of honour in Srivasa's shrine room.
[^8]: Vv. 44–55 — cf. Murari 2.9.17–22. Kaivalya and nirvana are liberation as merging into Brahman; for the devotees it is insignificant beside prema.
[^9]: Vv. 59–65 — cf. Murari 2.10.2–5. Haridasa Thakura is the great devotee of the holy name; the Lord Himself feeds him mahaprasada.
[^10]: Vv. 68–76 — cf. Murari 2.10.6–9. The kaupina is a renunciant's loincloth; the Lord gave the pieces of Nityananda's kaupina to the devotees as a holy relic.
[^11]: Vv. 83–101 — cf. Murari 2.10.10–13: the Lord carries Srivasa away from the circle of dancers and, seeing the devotees' alarm, appears again of Himself. Shachi's lament is only in Lochana Dasa.
[^12]: The devotees ascribe the Lord's return to the grace of Srivasa, whom He had taken with Him: thus the Lord's mercy comes through His devotee.
[^13]: Ananta (Shesha) is an expansion of the Lord; Sanatana is one of the four Kumaras (possibly also an epithet of Shiva, "the eternal Shiva"). Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 8–9: even Lakshmi did not attain the rasa of the gopis; the prema that Gaura bestowed is the highest treasure, rare even for the greatest.
