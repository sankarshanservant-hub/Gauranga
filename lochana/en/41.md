# Madhya-khanda, part 18 (portion 41)

*The section "The Lord's Arrival at Shantipura": the lament of Shachi and Vishnupriya on Chandrashekhara's return; the devotees of Navadvipa chant the Name, and the Lord, bound by it, sends Nityananda for them; Nityananda in Navadvipa; Shachi and all the people of Nadia go to Advaita's house; the Lord comes to Shantipura; the meal and kirtana*

## The Lord's Arrival at Shantipura

> *Summary by Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.* When Chandrashekhara Acharya returned to Nadia, at the sight of him the fire of Shachi's and Vishnupriya's grief blazed up twice as fierce; lamenting bitterly, they began to question the Acharya about Sriman Mahaprabhu. Meanwhile Bhagavan Gaurahari, the Knower of hearts, [seeing] the anguish of the people of Nadia and wishing to show Himself to them, resolved to come to Shantipura and sent them word of it through Sri Nityananda Prabhu. As soon as Nityananda Prabhu arrived in Nadia, at the sight of Him life returned to the bodies of the people of Nadia, who were wasting away in separation. When Shachidevi began to weep, Nityananda Prabhu consoled her and told her of the Lord's coming to Shantipura. Hearing this news, all the people of Nadia in great joy [came] to the house of Sri Advaita [Acharya], to the feet of Sriman Mahaprabhu, and the Lord received them fittingly. Thus that night passed in great bliss.[^1]

*Raga karunashri; disha: "Oh, alas, alas!"*

**1.** As Acharya Shekhara entered Navadvipa,
tears flowed ceaselessly from his eyes.

**2.** All the people of Navadvipa, seeing him,
burn within, their hearts pounding.

**3.** All the Vaishnavas came and gathered there;
unable to hold back their tears, with stricken faces,

**4.** they cannot ask anything — no word comes from their lips.
Hearing [of his return], Shachidevi runs with her hair dishevelled.

**5.** Frantic with grief, she calls, "Acharya!" —
and, not seeing Gauranga, she is thrown into turmoil:

**6.** "Where have you left my Nimai and come back?
How did He shave His head, in what land?

**7.** What wretched sannyasi with a cruel heart
felt no pity when giving the mantra to Vishvambhara![^2]

**8.** Seeing such loveliness of beautiful hair, —
what wretched barber with a pitiless heart!

**9.** What sinner put a razor to such hair?
How did he survive, that pitiless, cruel one?

**10.** In whose house did my Nimai take alms?
How does my child look with His head shaven?

**11.** I shall see your face no more, my son:
my whole world has become dark.

**12.** No more shall I cook rice to serve you,
no more shall I touch your beautiful body,

**13.** no more shall I kiss your lovely face.
Who will know when you are hungry?"

**14.** While Shachidevi was lamenting thus,
some people went to console Vishnupriya.

**15.** At Vishnupriya's weeping the earth splits;
beasts, birds, creepers, trees — even the stones weep.[^3]

**15+1.** "Alas, alas, Lord of my life, You have left Nadia,[^4]
cruelly forsaking helpless Vishnupriya!

**15+2.** The joy of kirtana with Srivasa and the other devotees,
Your dancing — I shall not see them to my eyes' fill again.

**15+3.** Not hearing the words from Your beautiful lips, faltering with prema,
how will this unfortunate one live?

**15+4.** In what land are You, and how, Lord of my life?
I remember and remember — and my life is worn away.

**15+5.** Alas, hard life, why do you not depart?
Kindle a fire — I shall die at once!

**15+6.** In my anguish each day has become as millions of yugas:
I do not see, Lord of my life, Your moon-like face.

**15+7.** A saint causes no anguish to any living being, —
yet in grief for You Mother Shachi is giving up her life.

**15+8.** Unfortunate, I did not know how to serve You with devotion —
surely for that offence I have been left forlorn.

**15+9.** No more shall I find solace, Lord,
sitting at Your feet and gazing and gazing at Your beauty,

**15+10.** raising betel with camphor to Your lips,
touching with my fingers the pearl-like row of Your teeth.

**15+11.** You would look tenderly from the corners of Your reddish eyes
and, smiling, speak sweet, sweet words.

**15+12.** The reddish lips, redder still with betel,
the gleam of the teeth — all rise up in my heart,

**15+13.** and on the face a smile steeped in nectar:
only by the hope of it do my ears and eyes live.

**15+14.** All Your virtues, Lord, are sweeter than nectar, —
but now, when I remember them, they have turned to fire.

**15+15.** Delightful pastimes and their sweetness, the happy bed, —
remembering all this, Vishnupriya is giving up her life.

**16.** Alas, alas, what fate has befallen me!
Without Gaura all is darkness for me.

**17.** I shall see that smile, that loveliness of body no more,
I shall not hear the clever words, the essence of nectar.

**18.** Leaving me forlorn, where have You gone?
I remember Your virtues and pour out my grief.

**19.** What fortunate woman who had seen You
has not reproached me with tears?

**20.** From the embrace of what unfortunate woman have You gone?
Why did she not die, the unfortunate one whose vow is broken?[^5]

**21.** I worshipped Your face with loving eyes —
how shall I hold my heart without seeing You?

**22.** All the noble women who love You are dying in separation,[^6]
and I, unfortunate, have kept this body so long.

**23.** I die, I die — where has beautiful Gauranga gone?
I am a woman, forlorn and weak by nature.

**24.** To what land shall I go, where shall I find You?
No one will let me go — I shall die right here.

**25.** Leaving Your mother forlorn, to what land have You gone?
How will she live, burning with longing for You?

**26.** Life will not leave my sinful body!" —
rolling on the ground, the goddess cries, "Alas, alas!"

**27.** Breath hot with the fire of separation flows ceaselessly;
her lips dry up, her body trembles.

**28.** Lying in the dust, she tends neither hair nor clothes;
now her body grows faint, now it seems to swell.

**29.** Now she swoons, meditating on His reddish feet,
now with great effort she comes to herself.

**30.** Now she calls with a wail, "Lord, Lord!" —
and at Vishnupriya's weeping everyone weeps.

**31.** All who went to console her —
seeing Vishnupriya, their hearts began to burn.

**31+1.** They call into her ear, "Gauranga, Gauranga!"[^7] —
and after a while Vishnupriya came to herself.

**32.** Everyone says: "Listen, Vishnupriya,
what consolation can we give you? Steady your heart.

**32+1.** Your Lord told you:[^8]
'Wherever I go, I am always near you.'

**33.** The doings of your Lord are not unknown to you:
understand this and console your own heart."

**34.** Having consoled her, all the devotees gathered together
and took counsel about Gorachand:

**35.** "He has taken sannyasa, causing grief to us all,
and pitilessly left us here.

**36.** How shall we live without Him?
Pitilessly Gora has left us all.

**37.** But His great name is more merciful than He Himself:
through the name we shall attain Him — this is the chief thing.[^9]

**38.** There is His word, spoken to us before:
'Whoever takes My name will attain Me.'"

**39.** Thinking thus, all sat down to chant the name —
Shachi, Vishnupriya and all the others,

**40.** children and the old, young men and women —
sat down to chant the name, taking Gauranga as their refuge.

**41.** With the noose of the name they bound Gauranga, the maddened lion:
Mahaprabhu stopped, His going was broken off.[^10]

**42.** Leaning on Nityananda, He stood
and wept, His eyes reddened.

**43.** "Go to Navadvipa today, Nityananda,
so that I may see everyone at Shantipura."

**44.** Hearing this, Nityananda rejoiced at heart,
and [the Lord] gave His word: "I will show Myself to all."

**45.** With a grieving heart Lochana Dasa says:
then the Lord Gorachand set out.

**46.** Sri Nityananda Prabhu goes along with Him;[^11]
the Lord, smiling, sent Him off:

**47.** "Go to Navadvipa, hear My word:
to all My dear ones in the town of Nadia

**48.** give My greeting, 'Narayana':[^12]
I shall stay at the house of Advaita Acharya.

**49.** Bring everyone and come there —
we shall all meet together at the Acharya's house."

**50.** Saying this, Mahaprabhu went on quickly,
and Nityananda goes to the town of Nadia.

**51.** The people of the town of Nadia are dead while living:
cut them, chop them — there is no blood or flesh in them;

**52.** no food in their stomachs, their bodies totter —
without Gorachand all is darkness to them.

**53.** Suddenly — Nityananda in the town of Nadia!
Strength came into their bodies, and all ran to Him quickly.

**54.** They cannot walk, they totter on the road,
they cannot see the way for their tears.

**55.** All the Vaishnavas weep, falling at His feet;
they cannot ask anything — they only gaze at His face.

**56.** Shachi, utterly frantic, runs with her face turned to the sky;
Shachi's grief filled earth and sky.

**57.** With a wail Shachi calls: "O Avadhuta,
where have you left my Nimai, my golden son, and come back?"

**58.** Saying this, Shachi weeps and beats her breast;
she totters, not looking to the road.

**59.** Seeing Shachi, Thakura rose to greet her.[^13]
Shachi says: "How far off is my son?"

**60.** Nityananda says: "Do not grieve at heart:
He has sent me to bring you all.

**61.** The Lord will stay at Advaita Acharya's house.
Do not grieve — you will see Him soon.

**62.** Come, everyone, to see the Lord!" —
and at that very moment everyone set out:

**63.** from children to the old, young women, the mute, the sober,
the ignorant and the ascetics — everyone set out.

**64.** Shachi runs ahead — strength has come into her body;
joyfully all the Vaishnavas go along.

**65.** Arriving at Advaita Acharya's house
and not seeing the Lord, they lost heart.[^14]

**66.** Nityananda says to Advaita Acharya:
"The Lord has resolved to stay at Your abode.

**67.** He sent me to bring all these;
what more is in His heart I do not know."

**68.** Saying this, the two embrace;
hearing of Gauranga's sannyasa, Advaita is distraught:

**69.** "Unfortunate that I am, I was not with Him.
When shall I see His moon-like face again?"

**70.** Frantic, Shachi keeps asking again and again,
and everyone says: "The Lord will come any moment."

**71.** Eagerness grew in everyone's heart —
and at that very time Mahaprabhu came.[^15]

**72.** The radiance of His body was millions of times greater than before,
and on it a bright, long mark of sandal.

**73.** On the golden body the reddish cloth shines,
surpassing in colour the morning sun.

**74.** Staff in hand, the Lord comes with a lion's gait;
seeing Him, all the people fell at His feet.

**75.** Seeing the radiance of His body, their hearts were soothed,
and all forgot hundreds of thousands of sorrows.

**75+1.** Hearts filled with joy — no sorrow, no grief;[^16]
all gaze fixedly at Vishvambhara's face.

**75+2.** As a man who has lost his life regains life,
as a rich man who has lost his wealth regains his wealth,

**75+3.** as faithful wives who have lost their husbands
are happy to see them again,

**75+4.** as a fish that struggles out of water
rejoices when suddenly it finds water —

**75+5.** so all the people, seeing Gauranga,
thrill in every limb, their hearts rejoicing.

**76.** Shachi's heart filled with prema — no sorrow, no grief;
she gazes fixedly at Vishvambhara's face.

**76+1.** "Come, come, my son, son of a mother bereft of her children![^17]
Leaving me forlorn, where did you go?

**76+2.** I shall take you home and keep you safe,
removing all your sannyasi dress."

**76+3.** Seeing His mother weep, the Lord of the world,
Vishvambhara, fell before her like a staff

**76+4.** and says to His mother: "Weep no more:
your weeping pains My heart."

**76+5.** Saying this, Bhagavan dispelled her grief,
and Shachi checked her sorrow.

**77.** All the grief there had been left their hearts:
His face, as they gazed at it, sprinkled them with nectar.

**78.** Advaita Acharya Gosani, with a joyful heart,
seated the Lord Gora Raya on a splendid seat.

**79.** Washing His feet and wiping them with cloth,
all His own people drank the water from His feet.

**80.** Cries of "Jaya, jaya!" and "Hari, Hari!" resound —
waves of bliss in the hearts of all the Vaishnavas.

**81.** Seeing His effulgence, Haridasa rejoiced,
and Murari, Mukunda Datta and Srinivasa.

**82.** Falling on the ground, they prostrate like staffs;
seeing His face, their eyes brim with tears.

**83.** Their voices are choked with joy, their bodies thrill:
life suddenly returned to dead bodies.

**84.** Thus Gora Raya, seeing His own,
looks on them with a merciful glance — compassion swells in His heart.

**85.** Some the Lord touches with His own hand,
some He greets with a smile, some He clasps to His breast —

**86.** the Lord fulfils each one's desire,
and prema grew abundantly in everyone's heart.

**87.** Everyone was delighted, grief went far away;
joyfully the people raise the auspicious cry of "Hari!"

**88.** At the abode of the wise devotee Advaita Acharya Gosani
the Lord took His meal.[^18]

**88+1.** Mother Shachi, the mother of the world, cooked;[^19]
Sita-devi and Narayani floated in bliss.

**88+2.** Advaita serves [the Lord] with great care:
all the dishes, most delicious, He placed on His leaf.

**88+3.** The Lord, the King of the gods, eats,
and, seeing this, all the devotees rejoice at heart.

**89.** And all the others, each as befitted him,
ate in joy and merriment.

**90.** No one remembers that the Lord has taken sannyasa:
joyfully they spend days and nights in sankirtana.

**91.** Absorbed in sankirtana, the Lord sings His own glories;[^20]
with a joyful heart He dances Himself and makes others dance.

**91+1.** Nityananda dances, and Haridasa dances,[^21]
Murari, Mukunda and Srinivasa dance;

**91+2.** beside Him Gadadhara and Narahari dance,
Vasudeva Ghosha and Gadadhara Dasa dance.

**91+3.** All the devotees dance around my Gauranga —
I cannot count them, naming them one by one.

**91+4.** Countless are Gauranga's companions — who can name them?
All dance around the Lord Vishvambhara.

**92.** All the devotees dance in the delight of prema's rasa;
Advaita Acharya dances with His son.[^22]

**93.** In everyone's heart prema grew boundlessly —
tears, trembling, thrill and the other sattvika transformations.

**94.** In everyone's heart arose joy and exultation.
Hearing this, Lochana Dasa is happy.

[^3]: Vishnupriya is the Lord's eternal consort, His Bhu-shakti (en/21, note 9); her separation from the Lord is not worldly grief (cf. en/38, note 5).
[^4]: Vv. 15+1 — 15+15 are found only in the 1983 edition; the 1903 edition does not have them.
[^5]: Lit. "whose vow is broken": Vishnupriya apparently reproaches herself for having failed in the vow of a faithful wife — to keep her husband and serve him.
[^6]: Lit. "all Your worthy women" — the women of Nadia devoted to the Lord; cf. en/09, note 9.
[^7]: V. 31+1 is found only in the 1983 edition; the 1903 edition does not have it.
[^8]: V. 32+1 is found only in the 1983 edition; the 1903 edition does not have it. Cf. en/38 (the Lord consoles Vishnupriya before His sannyasa).
[^9]: The Lord's name is not different from Him, yet it is more merciful than He, for it is open to all — a common teaching of the Gaudiya Vaishnavas.
[^10]: The independent Lord submits to the love of His devotees and to their chanting of His name.
[^11]: In the 1983 edition a new chapter, "Pastimes at Shantipura", begins at v. 46. Vv. 46–64 — cf. Murari 3.4.3–10.
[^12]: The customary greeting of sannyasis (en/39, note 7); cf. Murari 3.3.13. The 1983 edition reads "Namo narayanaya".
[^13]: Thakura here is Nityananda.
[^14]: Lit. "their backs broke". In Bhaktisiddhanta's edition the number of v. 65 is printed as "1"; the numbers of vv. 60 and 85 are missing, and vv. 62 and 92 are printed as 61 and 91 — restored.
[^15]: Vv. 71–90 — cf. Murari 3.4.12–23; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 3.
[^16]: Vv. 75+1 — 75+5 are found only in the 1983 edition; in the 1903 edition vv. 75+2 — 75+5 are in a footnote, as an additional reading of one manuscript. V. 75+1 is close to v. 76.
[^17]: Vv. 76+1 — 76+5 are found only in the 1983 edition; the 1903 edition does not have them.
[^18]: Bhiksha is a sannyasi's meal, taken in a householder's home.
[^19]: Vv. 88+1 — 88+3 are found only in the 1983 edition; in the 1903 edition they are in a footnote, as an additional reading of one manuscript. The Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 3) also says that Shachi cooked at Advaita's house.
[^20]: The Lord is Himself Krishna; singing of Krishna, He sings of Himself.
[^21]: Vv. 91+1 — 91+4 are found only in the 1983 edition; in the 1903 edition they are in a footnote, as an additional reading of one manuscript.
[^22]: The 1983 edition has another redaction of this couplet: "Shachi watches happily together with Sita and Narayani; Advaita Acharya dances merrily with His son."
