# Sutra-khanda, part 3 (portion 03)

*Narada comes to Dvaraka; Rukmini washes Krishna's feet and weeps, glorifying the greatness of His feet and the love of Radha; Krishna resolves to taste prema and to appear in the Kali-yuga, and reveals to Narada His form as Gaura; at Naimisharanya Narada tells everything to Uddhava*

**33.** The world is full of those who live only for belly and lust;
all people lie senseless, having forgotten Krishna.

**34.** With greed, delusion, lust, anger, intoxication and pride
they ceaselessly sprinkle their hearts as though with nectar.[^1]

**35.** Saying "I" and "mine", they perish for nothing;
who they themselves are, what is their own — they know nothing.

**36.** Seeing such sorrow of the people, the great sage
grew anxious at heart and pondered within himself.

**37.** Seeing no deliverance for people in the terrible age of Kali,
wandering on, he came to the gates of Dvaraka.

**38.** The Thakura of Dvaraka, God of gods, the crest-jewel of all,[^2]
had happily passed the night in the apartments of Satyabhama;

**39.** rising in the morning, He did all that was fitting
and let it be known that He would go to Rukmini's apartments.

**40.** Realizing what good fortune had come to her, Rukmini-devi
cannot contain herself — her body trembles.

**41.** She cleans the house, adorns herself;
instruments of many kinds are playing — joy without end;

**42.** auspicious jars full of water, lamps of ghee burning —
and at that time the Lord graciously arrived.

**43.** Mitravinda, Nagnajiti, Sushila, Subala[^3]
in bliss wave [lamps] around the Lord.[^4]

**44.** Bringing fragrant water to the Lord,
Sri Rukmini washes His feet.

**45.** Holding the feet, her own treasure, to her breast,
she gazes [at them] with love, now presses them to her face.

**46.** Pressing the holy feet to her heart, Rukmini weeps;
amazed, the Holder of the Discus asks:

**47.** "I cannot understand why you weep;
what are you weeping for, goddess? Tell Me what has happened.

**48.** You are dearer to Me than life — the whole world knows it;
who is dearer to Me than you? — tell Me yourself.

**49.** Has someone, out of neglect, failed to carry out your order?
Tell Me plainly, goddess, who has done wrong?

**50.** Only once before did I tease you[^5] —
is that grievance still in your heart today?

**51.** How much I pleaded with you then, humbly and piteously —
and still your hard heart did not soften."

**52.** Hearing such pitiless words from the Lord's mouth,
Rukmini answers tenderly, with a gentle reproach:

**53.** "My heart is hard; I cannot understand [Your words];
I have but one great good fortune — You are my life.

**54.** Your lotus feet are greater than You Yourself:
even now Shiva dances, drunk on their honey.

**55.** All that is in the world is known to You;
only the secret answer of love for [Your] feet You do not know.

**56.** If You place in Your heart the bhava of Radha,
then You will know what love for You is."[^6]

**57.** Hearing these words, the Lord was amazed at heart:
"What did you say, what did you say, goddess? Say it again.

**58.** I did not quite hear what you said;
can there be something that I do not know?

**59.** Hearing these wondrous words, I feel in My heart
a longing and some amazement.

**60.** Is there in the three worlds anything so hard to reach
that people marvel at seeing it and hearing of it?

**61.** From your lips I hear that there is something beyond My reach —
for joy My heart does not know what to do.

**62.** Tell Me, tell Me, tell Me, goddess — so I may be sure"; —
the greatness of the feet is sung by Lochana Dasa.

*Raga dhanashi; dirgha metre*

**63.** The goddess Rukmini says: "Listen, Lord, jewel of virtues,
think nothing else in Your heart:
what I weep for — that You do not know,
though all else You know.

**64.** What lies hidden in Your lotus feet, how great their power —
this You do not know well;
these feet will leave my house and go to another —
that is why my heart weeps.

**65.** Wherever, to the ends of the earth, the fragrance of these lotus feet is carried,
old age and death leave those regions;
and for those who live drinking the honey of these feet,
what are day and night, what are the seasons?

**66.** He who lovingly holds the lovely pollen of the lotus feet —
his feet I might touch only by great good fortune;
weeping I speak of all the pain in my heart —
I lay it all before You.

**67.** You are the master of all; is there in the whole world
anyone who is master over You?
In love for Your feet this rasa is tasted —
this, Lord, is what I am telling You.

**68.** Radha alone knows this, having found that rasa of love —
all its joy, all its tenderness;
the devotees marvel at it, [speaking of it] day and night:
what rasa, what prema, what anuraga!

**69.** Brahma and the other gods and goddesses serve the feet of Lakshmi,
and she herself, out of her own love —
Kamala, a lotus in her lotus hand, beside herself with longing —
begs for service to these lotus feet.

**70.** Though Rama dwells on Your breast, not lying [apart] upon a bed,
face to face with You,
yet, longing for the sweetness of these feet, she does not value even that:[^7]
who then can tell the greatness of the feet?

**71.** Lakshmi, for all her own happiness, looks [at them] in supplication:
such is the grace of love for the feet;
Radha alone knows this — she who tasted [it] in Vrindavana:
there is no limit to her good fortune.

**72.** And here is a riddle for the world: You are bound by her virtues,
and even now Your heart repeats [her name];
when You utter the name of Radha, Your eyes, I see, brim with tears:
such is the might of love for the feet.

**73.** These feet are in my house — and my heart rejoices,
yet I weep again for fear of separation;
Your holy lotus feet are stronger than You Yourself —
feel it and judge.

**74.** You are the object of his meditation, You are the knowledge of his samadhi,
You alone are his support in all things;
and such a servant of Yours sets his hope on Your feet[^8] —
this is what amazes me.

**75.** The feet whose maidservant Lakshmi is — why does she long for them?
Such is Your lordship:
though You are her master, You do not think of her good;
without a thought You pierce her with a spear.

**76.** Whoever with longing tastes the rasa of the honey of these feet
has a treasury inexhaustible and imperishable;
what then of Vani and Lakshmi — they think themselves blessed
by a mere touch of it.

**77.** The four liberations, beginning with salokya, follow at his heels,
yet he will not glance at them even from the corner of his eye;
he who has sunk into the rasa of prema — what else is dear to him?
He counts even Vaikuntha as nothing.

**78.** With joined palms I pray, Lord: of those lotus feet
make me a bee — grant me this boon;
from fear of separation from those feet this sinful life is ebbing away:
never leave my house."

**79.** Listen: Rukmini told the glories of the lotus feet —
it is love itself made manifest;
and so, by the Lord's mercy, with a trembling heart,
Lochana Dasa sings [His] glories.

*Raga dhanashi; madhya metre; disha: "Ah, ah, oh! — Murchha: — Here is a wondrous tale, its very name a blessing to the ear; hear further the tale of Gora's virtues"*

**80.** Hearing Rukmini's words, rejoicing at heart,
[the Lord] — His eyes, red lotuses, swimming in tears of compassion —

**81.** drew her to Him and speaks very softly,
sitting on the throne with Rukmini on His lap.

**82.** Taking her by the chin with His right hand, He looks into her face —
and the ocean of prema heaves in waves of nectar.

**83.** "Never have I heard anything so wondrous!
I Myself shall taste the happiness of prema," He said.[^9]

**84.** At that time Narada suddenly arrived;
the sage's face was joyless, his heart troubled.

**85.** Rising hastily, the goddess offered him water for his feet and arghya,
seated him on a splendid seat and asked after his welfare.

**86.** The Thakura, rising, embraced him tightly
and spoke to Narada with tender words.

**87.** For love both his eyes brim with tears;
the sage babbles, choking, he sways.

**88.** As he gazes [at the Lord], his eyes swim in tears of prema;
he wants to say something and cannot.

**89.** The Lord asked: "Sage, tell Me truly:
why are you so weak, why so troubled at heart?

**90.** You are dearer to Me than life, and I am your life;
seeing you in sorrow, I lose My senses."

**91.** Narada says: "Lord, what shall I say?
You are the Lord of lords, You are the inner witness of all.

**92.** To sing Your virtues is my nectar food;
thirsting for Your virtues, I go round the whole world.

**93.** Going round the world, I did not hear the name of Krishna:
people are drunk with their own intoxication, having forgotten You.

**94.** All people lie senseless, blinded by pride;
seeing people without Krishna — that is my grief.

**95.** I see no way to save the people;
my mind thinks only of this, unceasingly.

**96.** I have laid before You all the grief that was in my heart;
by the grace of Your feet all else is happiness."

**97.** Smiling, the Lord says: "Listen, great sage:
you have forgotten all that happened before —

**98.** how Katyayani made a vow
in her talk with Mahesha for the sake of mahaprasada.[^10]

**99.** And Rukmini has said something unprecedented;
hearing it, I was moved and made a vow:

**100.** I shall taste the happiness of prema and give it to the people to taste;
I shall show a humble mood in the Kali-yuga;

**101.** together with the devotees, practising bhakti,
I, though I am Ishvara, shall distribute My prema;

**102.** I shall reveal the sankirtana of My virtues and names,
I shall be born in the house of Shachi in Navadvipa;

**103.** My body will be golden and tall, My arms reaching the knees,
beautiful as Sumeru, beyond compare."

**104.** As He said this, the Lord became golden;
seeing it, Narada was filled with great longing.

**105.** A body beautiful as Sumeru, in the rapture of prema —
so Lochana tells of Gora's first manifestation.

*Shri-raga; disha: "Ah, Gauranga, glory, glory! — Murchha: — Ah, my prema for Gauranga — nectar! Ah, my [treasure], glory, glory!"*

**106.** Gazing [at Him] with a jubilant heart, the sage Narada
sheds tears in a thousand streams.

**107.** A radiance surpassing millions of moons, a brilliance of millions of suns,
a beauty surpassing millions of Kamadevas: so shines the Best of the Gauras.

**108.** The dazzling radiance of the body — one cannot look at it;
the sage sits with eyes shut, trembling all over.

**109.** Restraining His radiance, the Lord looks at Narada
and, seeing him powerless, calls him in a loud voice.

**110.** Coming to himself, the sage [is lost] in meditation on that form,
and his eyes thirst to see [it] again.

**111.** The Thakura says: "Sage, listen, greatly fortunate one:
your path is barred by nothing, everywhere you are honoured.

**112.** Proclaim in the worlds of Shiva, Brahma and the others:
I shall appear in the Kali-yuga as Gaura.

**113.** I shall reveal the sankirtana of virtues and name,
I shall spread the happiness of the rasa of bhakti and prema for Me.

**114.** Hundreds upon hundreds of branches — the paths of bhakti are without limit;
turning people towards one, I shall spread prema.[^11]

**115.** Together with My own devotees and associates,
born on earth, [I shall fulfil] the longing for prema-bhakti."

**116.** Hearing such words from the holy mouth, Narada,
by the grace of [the Lord's] feet, was freed from all sorrow.

**117.** The sage Narada went on, playing his vina,
absorbed in these thoughts:

**118.** "What a wondrous form of Gora I have seen!
What red eyes full of compassion I have seen!

**119.** What a radiance I have seen — sweeter than nectar!
What a sweet smile on the holy lips I have seen!

**120.** Of all the descents there have been, this is the essence;
never have I seen such a treasure-house of prema.

**121.** Blessed is my birth, blessed are my eyes:
I have seen the form of Gora, His serene face.

**122.** Never have I seen such an ocean of mercy:
the heart cannot forget, my eyes are satisfied."

**123.** Pondering thus, the sage goes on his way,
and in the forest of Naimisha he meets Uddhava.

**124.** Uddhava, rising respectfully, offered water for the feet and arghya
and lay prostrate on the ground, falling at his feet.

**125.** He thinks the day auspicious and himself fortunate:
"At an auspicious hour the sage has come to the forest of Naimisha."

**126.** Narada lifted him up, embraced him tightly,
kissed him and smelled the crown of his head.

**127.** Uddhava brought him a seat,
[Narada sat,] and, smiling, [Uddhava] tells what is in his heart:

**128.** "Blessed is my birth, special is this day;
let me lay before you one long-standing pain of my heart.

**129.** Long ago Vyasa, here in the forest of Naimisha,
having studied the Vedas, was not freed from the heaviness of his mind;

**130.** by your grace he heard the hidden truth
and for the deliverance of people composed the *Bhagavata*.[^12]

**131.** You alone know the truth, you know the truth about the Lord;
understanding the Lord's purpose, reveal the future:

**132.** how will people be saved in the Kali-yuga —
wrapped in sin, the eyes of their hearts blind?

**133.** I know what the dharma of people is in the Satya, Treta and Dvapara yugas;
but in the terrible Kali-yuga there is nothing but sin.

**134.** Be merciful, tell me, dispel my doubt:
who is more merciful than you?"

**135.** Rejoicing at heart, the sage says, smiling:
"Well have you asked, Uddhava, servant of Hari!

**136.** I will tell you the deepest secret:
the very same grief was in my heart too.

**137.** Now I have understood: the Kali-yuga is blessed;
apart from the people of Kali, there is no one else so blessed.

**138.** The dharma of the Satya and the other yugas is a severe discipline;
the dharma of the Kali-yuga is the excellent Hari-nama.[^13]

**139.** By the sankirtana of the name and virtues they are freed from bondage;
they go about dancing and singing, free from the fear of Yama.

**140.** And here is another wonder — listen attentively —
which I saw with my own eyes in Dvaraka.

**141.** Speaking of this with Rukmini, the Lord
resolved in His heart: 'I will unfold My prema.'

**142.** He was sitting on the throne with Rukmini on His lap —
at that time I arrived, troubled at heart.

**143.** Seeing me in sorrow, the Lord asked me:
'Why do I see you like this?'

**144.** Falling at His feet, I told Him what was in my heart;
the Lord, His face serene, said smiling:

**145.** 'Rukmini has told the greatness of love for [My] feet;
hearing it, I am overwhelmed — so great is [this] longing.

**146.** I shall taste the happiness of prema and give it to the people to taste;
I shall show a humble mood in the Kali-yuga.

**147.** The terrible Kali-yuga is sinful, without dharma;
to bring people to understanding, I shall become very humble.'

**148.** A body full of prema, golden, tall, of lovely hue,
a broad chest, arms reaching the knees —

**149.** as He said this, the Lord became golden
and vowed to give away His prema.

**150.** What I saw, what I heard, I have told you;
I shall go and proclaim it throughout the world.

**151.** Taking birth on earth out of longing for prema-bhakti,
so wondrous [the Lord] will appear in the Kali-yuga."

**152.** Hearing Narada's words, Uddhava, beside himself,
weeps, fallen at his feet, overwhelmed with joy:

**153.** "You have told me such a wondrous thing —
as though life were breathed into a lifeless body.

[^1]: So in Bhaktisiddhanta's text (A: "amiya"): people take these passions for nectar. The 1983 edition has "sprinkle their hearts with poison".
[^2]: Literally "the Thakura of Dvaraka, crest-jewel of the God of gods".
[^3]: Mitravinda and Nagnajiti are among Krishna's principal queens (*Bhag.* 10.58). Sushila and Subala may also be names of queens, or epithets ("good-natured, youthful"); we take them as names.
[^4]: *Nirmanchhana*: the rite of waving a lamp or other object around a beloved person to ward off all harm.
[^5]: Cf. *Bhag.* 10.60: Krishna in jest told Rukmini that she should have chosen a king equal to her as husband, and she fainted from grief.
[^6]: Literally "then You will know the marks of Your own love", i.e. the love directed to You. This directly echoes how Gaudiya Vaishnavas understand the reason for Sri Chaitanya's appearance: Krishna wishes to know the greatness of Radha's love, the sweetness of Himself that she tastes, and her bliss, and therefore comes, accepting her bhava and lustre (*Chaitanya-charitamrita*, Adi 1.6 and ch. 4). Bhaktisiddhanta says the same in his summary of this section: Krishna descends as Gaura "accepting the bhava of Radha".
[^7]: Rama is Lakshmi. Cf. *Bhag.* 10.16.36: Lakshmi performed austerities desiring to touch the dust of Krishna's feet.
[^8]: Probably Shiva is meant (cf. v. 54): he contemplates the Lord in samadhi and himself longs for His feet.
[^9]: The 1983 edition likewise: "'I shall taste the happiness of prema,' He Himself said." This is the first statement of the vow spoken in full in v. 100.
[^10]: The story of the mahaprasada, Shiva and Katyayani (Parvati) is told by Lochana Dasa further on, in the account of Narada's visit to Kailasa.
[^11]: Literally "let people be turned in one direction" — towards the one prema-bhakti.
[^12]: In the *Bhagavata-purana* (1.4–1.7) Narada instructs the dejected Vyasa at his ashrama on the bank of the Sarasvati (Shamyaprasa), while it is Suta who recites the *Bhagavata* to the sages at Naimisharanya; Lochana Dasa, following his source, joins these accounts.
[^13]: Cf. *Bhag.* 12.3.52: what was attained in the Satya-yuga by meditation, in the Treta by sacrifice and in the Dvapara by worship is attained in the Kali-yuga by the chanting of Hari's name.
