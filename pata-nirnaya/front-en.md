# Pata-nirnaya · Pata-paryatana · Shakha-nirnaya of Abhiramachandra

*Ramagopala Dasa; Abhirama Dasa. Bengali, 17th century*

*Translated from the Bengali into English*

---

## Translator's Note

Three short Bengali lists of the holy places ("patas") and branches of Sri Chaitanya's associates. The **Pata-nirnaya** of Ramagopala Dasa (chronogram 1597 Shaka, c. 1675) names the dhamas of the Lord and the shripatas of His associates — the places of their birth and pastimes in Bengal and Orissa. The **Pata-paryatana** of Abhirama Dasa is an extract from it: five dhamas, the twelve shripatas of the gopalas with their names in Vraja, the villages of other associates. The **Shakha-nirnaya of Abhiramachandra** by the same author lists the disciples of Abhirama Thakura. The translation follows the edition of Kishori Dasa Babaji and the 1911 publication in the *Sahitya-parishat-patrika*; the editors' notes are not translated.
