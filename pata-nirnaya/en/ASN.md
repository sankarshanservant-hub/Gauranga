# The *Shakha-nirnaya of Abhiramachandra* by Abhirama Dasa

*The disciples of Abhirama Thakura — twenty-four and a half "branches" — and their villages; the author's signature (couplets 1–17)*

**1.** However many disciples Abhiramachandra[^1] had —
their names and villages I write down truly.

**2.** At Khanakul is the residence of Krishnadasa Thakura;
in the village of Kaiyar Vedagarbha appeared[^2].

**3.** At Budhana is the residence of Haridasa[^3];
at Helagrama stays Pakhiya Gopaladasa[^4].

**4.** At Pakmalyati lives Gumphya-Narayana[^5];
at Sitanagar lives Thakura Mohana.

**5.** Everyone calls him Dariya-Mohana[^6]:
for what a handsome beard he has — quite extraordinary!

**6.** At Mahinnamuri lives [a disciple] named Satyaraghava[^7];
at Salika, one called Rajanikara Pandita.

**7.** At Bhajamora lives [a disciple] named Sundarananda[^8] —
a supremely learned brahmana, called Pandita.

**8.** At Dvipagrama stays Krishnananda Avadhuta;
at Sonatola, in Ranga-desha, Rangana Krishnadasa — this is certain[^9].

**9.** At Maldah lives Murari Dasa[^10];
at Panihati stays Thakura Mohana[^11].

**10.** At Radhanagar lives Yadu Haldar;
Hira Madhava Dasa stays at Anantanagar.

**11.** At Maheshagrama lives [a disciple] named Gopaladasa;
at Kotra lives one called Achyuta Pandita.

**12.** At Patlagrama is Lakshminarayana the doorkeeper[^12];
at Nilachala stays one called Gopinathadasa.

**13.** A dweller of Chunakhali is Dasa Nandakishora;
at Patagrama Vidura Brahmachari constantly roams[^13].

**14.** A dweller of Vishnupara, named Ramakrishnadasa[^14];
at Gaurangapur stays one called Kamalakaradasa[^15].

**15.** The disciple of Gopala Bhatta is Acharya Shrinivasa:
the Acharya is a limb-branch (anga-shakha) — know it truly[^16].

**16.** At Vishvagrama lives Thakura Balarama.
[Thus] I have told the names and villages of the twenty-four and a half branches[^17].

**17.** Meditating on the lotus feet of Sri Ratneshvara,
Dasa Abhirama composed [this] in brief[^18].

*Thus ends the* Shakha-nirnaya of Abhiramachandra.

[^1]: Abhiramachandra — Abhirama Thakura (Ramadasa; @abhirama), one of the twelve gopalas, whose shripat is Khanakul (#khanakul; cf. *Pata-paryatana*, v. 8). "Shakha" (branch) — a disciple.
[^2]: Kaiyar (কৈয়ড়) is a new place. Krishnadasa Thakura and Vedagarbha are new names.
[^3]: The text has বুঢ়নগ্রাম. Cf. Budhana (#budhana), the birthplace of Haridasa Thakura (CB Adi 2); whether this is the same village and the same Haridasa is unclear from the text — here he is named as a disciple of Abhirama.
[^4]: Helagrama is a new place. "Pakhiya" (পাখীয়া) — apparently a nickname (from পাখী, "bird"); the interpretation is uncertain.
[^5]: "Pakmalyati" (পাকমাল্যাটি) — reading unclear; a new place. "Gumphya-Narayana" (গুম্ফ্যানারায়ণ) — apparently "moustached Narayana" (গুম্ফ, "moustache"), a nickname like the "bearded Mohana" of the next verse; the reading is uncertain.
[^6]: "Dariya-Mohana" (দাড়িয়ামোহন) — "bearded Mohana". Sitanagar is a new place.
[^7]: The reading of the name is unclear (মহিন্নামুড়ি?); a new place. Salika (সালিকা) is a new place.
[^8]: The reading of the name is unclear (ভজমোড়া? ভঞ্জমোড়া?); a new place. This Sundarananda is a disciple of Abhirama, not Sundarananda of the twelve gopalas (*Pata-paryatana*, v. 9).
[^9]: The line is not quite clear: সোনাতোলা রঙ্গাদেশে রঙ্গনকৃষ্ণ দাসনিশ্চিত. Understood as "at Sonatola, in Ranga-desha, Rangana Krishnadasa"; "Ranga-desha" and "Rangana" are uncertain readings. Dvipagrama and Sonatola are new places.
[^10]: Maldah (#maldah).
[^11]: Panihati (#panihati). Another Thakura Mohana (cf. v. 4).
[^12]: "Doorkeeper" — দ্বারী; possibly a nickname or an office. Patlagrama is a new place.
[^13]: Chunakhali and Patagrama are new places. "Roams" — বিহার, literally "spends [his time], sports".
[^14]: The journal has বিন্নুপাড়া (?); probably Vishnupara (বিষ্ণুপাড়া). Reading unclear; a new place.
[^15]: Gaurangapur is a new place; cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 41 (a Gaurangapur near Khanakul?). Kamalakaradasa is a disciple of Abhirama, not Kamalakara Pippalai.
[^16]: Shrinivasa Acharya (@srinivasa-acharya), disciple of Gopala Bhatta (@gopala-bhatta). "Anga-shakha" (অঙ্গশাখা) — so the journal; the reading of the image is clear. It may be a corruption of "ardha-shakha" ("half-branch"): then Shrinivasa would be the "half" in the count "twenty-four and a half" (v. 16) — without him the list contains exactly twenty-four disciples.
[^17]: Vishvagrama is a new place. The count: vv. 2–16 name twenty-four disciples and Shrinivasa.
[^18]: Ratneshvara — apparently the author's guru; a new name. The author, Abhirama Dasa, is not Abhirama Thakura (v. 1).
