# The *Pata-paryatana* of Abhirama Dasa

*An extract from the Pata-nirnaya: the five dhamas of the Lord, Nityananda and Advaita; the twelve shripats of the gopalas with their names in Vraja; the villages of other associates and the great shripats Agradvipa and Shrikhanda; the author's signature (couplets 1–57)*

## Introduction

**1.** The shripats one should go round (parikrama)[^1] —
briefly, merely pointing the way, I write them down truly.

**2.** Five dhamas[^2] and twelve shripats — that makes seventeen;
together with the seventeen [shripats] of the devotees, thirty-four shripats, it is said[^3].

**3.** In which villages the thirty-four shripats are — I will name them;
hear all the names in order, [and] be sure of them.

**4.** In which village each one lived — that is established;
writing the names and villages, I humbly beg [your indulgence][^4].

## The five dhamas

**5.** Sri Navadvipa is the dhama [where] the Lord was born;
Katwa is a dhama of the Lord — know this for certain[^5].

**6.** Ekachakra is the birthplace, Khardaha the residence:
the two dhamas of Sri Nityananda — know it truly[^6].

**7.** The dhama of Sri Advaita is at Shantipura;
know these five dhamas, all of you, for certain.

## The twelve shripats

**8.** Abhirama, formerly Sudasa[^7], dwelt at Khanakul;
the village is famed by the name Khanakul-Krishnanagar.

**9.** Halda-Maheshpur is the residence of Sundarananda;
Sundarananda was formerly Sudama — know this for certain[^8].

**10.** Kanchrapara is the birthplace, Jalandi the residence:
Dhananjaya is Vasudama — know it truly[^9].

**11.** At Ambika is the residence of Gauridasa Pandita;
Gauridasa was formerly Subala — know it truly[^10].

**12.** Born at Akna-Mahesh, dwelling at Jageshvara:
such is Kamalakara Pippalai, this is certain[^11].

**13.** Kamalakara's former name is Mahabala.
The residence of Uddharana Datta is said to be Krishnapur.

**14.** Near Hugli is the village of Krishnapur;
Uddharana's former name is Subahu — know this[^12].

**15.** Saguna [and] Sardanga, near Sukhasagar:
[there] is the residence of Mahesha Pandita, I say with joined palms[^13].

**16.** Mahesha was formerly Mahabahu — know this name.
At Badagachhi lived [a devotee] named Sri Krishnadasa[^14].

**17.** Parameshvara Dasa was formerly Stoka-Krishna[^15];
at Bodhkhana Nagara Purushottama was born.

**18.** Bodhkhana is in the pargana of Halda, know this, all;
Purushottama was formerly called the friend Sudama[^16].

**19.** At Sanchra is the residence of Parameshvara Dasa;
Parameshvara was formerly the friend Arjuna — such is his fame[^17].

**20.** This [Arjuna] is Madhava's friend, not the Pandava.
Hiranga, Sanchra-Panchra — so all people call [the village][^18].

**21.** At Akai Hat is the residence of Kala Krishnadasa,
formerly famed by the name of the friend Lavanga[^19].

**22.** Shridhara the Kholavecha[^20] lived in Navadvipa;
formerly he was Madhumangala — know it truly.

**23.** Thus the twelve shripats have been written down.
[Now] hear the account of the villages where devotees lived.

## The villages of the devotees

**24.** Sri Gadadhara Pandita was born in Shrihatta;
coming to the Lord, he lived in Navadvipa[^21].

**25.** The Pandita's nephew is his branch,
by name Nayanananda Mishra; he lives at Bharatpur[^22].

**26.** At Ariadaha is the residence of Gadadhara Dasa;
Svarupa Gosvami always stayed in Navadvipa.

**27.** Svarupa was formerly Lalita — know this name;
Ramananda is Vishakha — know this, all[^23].

**28.** Ramananda Raya lived on the bank of the Godavari —
in the southern country, at Sri Vidyanagara.

**29.** [That place] is not counted in the round of the shripats;
if you go to Nilachala, perhaps you will visit [it too][^24].

**30.** At Kanchrapara [and] Kumarahatta Shivananda stayed;
formerly he is famed by the name Suchitra[^25].

**31.** At Kulinagrama stayed Vasu Ramananda,
formerly famed by the name Champakalatika.

**32.** Agradvipa is a great shripat (mahapat)[^26] — know this, devotees:
because two or three devotees lived there, it is called a mahapat.

**33.** In Agradvipa the three Ghoshas took birth;
for this reason the devotees call [it] a mahapat.

**34.** Govinda Ghosha is Ranga-devi, Vasu is Sudevi, it is said;
Madhava Ghosha is Tungavidya — know this for certain[^27].

**35.** At Kongarhatta is the residence of Govindananda Thakura;
formerly he was the sakhi Indurekha — know it truly[^28].

**36.** Names with an identification (anuvada and vidheya) are only these;
from here on, identifying names (vidheya) are not written[^29].

## Shrikhanda and the other shripats

**37.** The [places] one should go round —
those villages I write down; know this for certain.

**38.** Writing down the villages and the names of the devotees, [I pray]:
forgive my offences, all you devotees.

**39.** The glorious great shripat — know this, all:
in Shrikhanda many devotees took birth[^30].

**40.** Sri Mukunda, Narahari, Sri Raghunandana,
Chiranjiva Kaviraja and Sulochana.

**41.** The devotees glorify Sarakara Thakura[^31];
because many devotees were born [here], [Shrikhanda] is called a mahapat.

**42.** Kulia and Paharpur — two [villages], this is established:
Vamshivadana, Kavidatta, Saranga Thakura[^32].

**43.** In these two villages the three [of them] always live;
[so] the names of Kulia and Paharpur are famed.

**44.** Hear the account of Kanchrapara [and] Kumarahatta:
[there] appeared Shrikanta Sena, Kavikarna, Sri Rama Pandita[^33].

**45.** In the village of Panihati is the abode of Raghava [and] Damayanti[^34];
there is a story called "Raghava's bags".

**46.** At Bodhkhana is the residence of Sadashiva Kaviraja;
Sadashiva's son is Nagara Purushottama Dasa[^35].

**47.** Four incomparable services are at Vallabhapur[^36];
I will tell the names of the devotees who were [there]:

**48.** Kashishvara, Shankararanya and Shrinatha,
Sri Rudra Pandita and others — the residence of them all[^37].

**49.** At Belun is Ananta Puri, great is [his] glory;
the dweller of Baghnapara is Sri Ramai Thakura[^38].

**50.** At Guptipara Satyananda Sarasvati
serves Vrindavanachandra with love[^39].

**51.** At Jirat are Madhava Acharya and Ganga-devi;
at Jashra is Jagadisha, ever rejoicing[^40].

**52.** Halisahar, Natigrama — Narayani's son,
named Thakura Vrindavana, known to the world.

**53.** Natigrama is his birthplace, his residence is at Dendura;
he spread the *Sri Chaitanya-bhagavata*[^41].

**54.** At Varahanagara is the residence of Bhagavatacharya;
at Naihati truly lived Rupa [and] Sanatana[^42].

## Conclusion

**55.** The villages where one should perform parikrama —
those villages are written down here, truly.

**56.** In the book *Pata-nirnaya* [this is given] in detail;
having looked at it, I have made this extract[^43].

**57.** Thus the *Pata-paryatana* is complete;
Abhirama Dasa composed it.

*Thus ends the "Pata-parikrama", the* Pata-paryatana.

[^1]: "Pat" (পাট) — shripat, the place where an associate of the Lord lived and served; "parikrama" (পরিক্রমা) — going round holy places. "Merely pointing the way" (দিঙ্মাত্র) — i.e. briefly; the *Pata-nirnaya* says the same of itself (v. 2).
[^2]: "Dhama" (ধাম) — an abode of the Lord (here also of Nityananda and Advaita). The five dhamas are named in vv. 5–7.
[^3]: The count: five dhamas and the twelve shripats of the gopalas (vv. 8–22) make seventeen; with the seventeen shripats of other devotees (vv. 24–54), thirty-four. The text does not number the shripats, and the exact list of the last seventeen cannot be derived from it. The *Pata-nirnaya* counts differently (vv. 15, 44, 54).
[^4]: "I make parihara" (করি পরিহার) — in Old Bengali, "I humbly entreat"; cf. v. 38. In the journal the word "name" is misprinted (মাম for নাম); Kishori Dasa has নাম.
[^5]: Katwa (কাটোয়া; #katwa), the place of the Lord's sannyasa; in the *Pata-nirnaya* (v. 14), Kantakanagara.
[^6]: Ekachakra (#ekachakra; journal একচাকা, Kishori Dasa একচাক্রা), Nityananda's birthplace; Khardaha (#kharadaha), His abode.
[^7]: So the journal (সুদাস). Kishori Dasa has "Shridama" (শ্রীদাম), as in GGD 126 (Abhirama is Shridama); the journal's reading is probably corrupt. Khanakul (#khanakul); Khanakul-Krishnanagar is not the town of Krishnanagar in Nadia (cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 40).
[^8]: GGD 127: Sundarananda is Sudama. Halda is a pargana (v. 18); Maheshpur — #maheshpur (cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 37: Halda-Maheshpur and Bodhkhana).
[^9]: Jalandi (#jalandi): journal জলণ্ডি (?), Kishori Dasa জলন্দী; neither print is fully clear. GGD 127: Dhananjaya Pandita is Vasudama. Other sources give Jaragrama (#jaragrama) as his birthplace; Kanchrapara is a new place.
[^10]: GGD 128: Gauridasa Pandita is Subala. Ambika — Ambika-Kalna (#ambika-kalna).
[^11]: Akna-Mahesh (আকনা মাহেশ) — apparently Mahesh on the Ganga (#mahesh) with the neighbouring village of Akna; Jageshvara is a new place, which we do not identify. GGD 128: Kamalakara Pippalai is Mahabala (v. 13).
[^12]: GGD 129: Uddharana Datta is Subahu. Krishnapur near Hugli — cf. #krishnapura-saptagrama (Krishnapur in Saptagram, Hooghly district); the identity is unverified.
[^13]: Saguna (সাগুণা) and Sukhasagar are new places. "Sardanga": journal সরডেঙ্গা (Sardenga), Kishori Dasa সরডাঙ্গা; cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 63: Mahesha Pandita's home is at Sardanga-Sultanpur. GGD 129: Mahesha Pandita is Mahabahu (v. 16).
[^14]: Badagachhi (#badagachhi); Krishnadasa — @krishnadasa-badagachhi. No Vraja name is given for him.
[^15]: So in both prints. Below (v. 19) Parameshvara Dasa is called the friend Arjuna, as in GGD 132; here there seems to be a slip in the manuscript (perhaps it concerns Krishnadasa of Badagachhi, who is left without a Vraja name). Stoka-Krishna is a friend of Krishna in Vraja.
[^16]: So in both prints ("সুদাম সখা"). Sudama has been named above (v. 9) as Sundarananda; possibly a corruption of the name of another friend of Krishna. Nagara Purushottama — @purushottama-nagara (v. 46).
[^17]: Sanchra: journal সাচড়া, Kishori Dasa সাঁচড়া; Sanchra-Panchra — #sanchra-panchra (by other accounts Dhananjaya's shripat). GGD 132: Parameshvara Dasa is Arjuna.
[^18]: Madhava is Krishna: the Arjuna meant here is the cowherd friend of Vraja, not Arjuna the Pandava. "Hiranga" (হিরণগাঁ; Kishori Dasa হিরনগাঁ) — reading unclear; possibly a village name (Hirangaon). A new place.
[^19]: Akai Hat (#akai-hat); Kala Krishnadasa — @kaliya-krishnadasa; GGD 132: Lavanga.
[^20]: "Kholavecha" — "seller of khola", banana bark (@shridhara). According to GGD 133 Shridhara is the cowherd boy Kusumasava; Madhumangala is Krishna's brahmana friend.
[^21]: So in both prints. By other accounts Gadadhara Pandita's family came from Beleti (Chittagong), and he himself grew up in Navadvipa; Shrihatta (#shrihatta) is the land of the Lord's forefathers.
[^22]: Nayanananda Mishra — @nayanananda-mishra. Bharatpur (ভরতপুর) is a new place; cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 48 ("Mishra Thakura" at Bharatpur).
[^23]: Journal "Visakhika" (বিসখিকা), Kishori Dasa "Vishakha" (বিশাখা). GGD 120–124 sees in Ramananda Raya the union of two Arjunas; the correspondence of Svarupa and Ramananda to Lalita and Vishakha is not given uniformly in the sources.
[^24]: Vidyanagara (#vidyanagara) on the Godavari; Nilachala — Puri (#puri). Kishori Dasa: "the round of the shripats does not go (গমন) there"; the journal: "is not counted (গণন)".
[^25]: Kanchrapara is a new place (cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 28); Kumarahatta (#kumarahatta). "His" renders ইঞির, an Old Bengali form.
[^26]: "Mahapat" (মহাপাট) — great shripat, where many devotees lived. Agradvipa — #agradvipa.
[^27]: GGD 188 calls the Ghosha brothers singers of Vraja (Vasu Ghosha is Gunatunga); here the identification is different.
[^28]: "Kongarhatta" (কোঙরহট্ট; so in both prints): কোঙর is an Old Bengali form of "kumara"; apparently this is Kumarahatta (#kumarahatta). Whether this is the Govindananda whom GGD 91 calls Sugriva (@govindananda) is unclear. Indurekha is a sakhi of Radha (usually Indulekha).
[^29]: Anuvada and vidheya are terms of poetics (cf. CC Adi 2.74–75): anuvada is the known (the associate's name), vidheya what is predicated of him (his name in Vraja). That is, from here on the Vraja names are not given.
[^30]: So the journal: "শ্রীমন্ত মহাপাট" ("the glorious great shripat"). Kishori Dasa: "শ্রীখণ্ড মহাপাট" ("Shrikhanda is a great shripat"). Shrikhanda — #shrikhanda.
[^31]: Narahari Sarakara (Thakura) — @narahari-sarakara. Mukunda is his brother Mukunda Dasa (@mukunda-dasa), Raghunandana is Mukunda's son; Chiranjiva — @chiranjiva-sena; Sulochana — @sulochana-shrikhanda.
[^32]: Kulia (#kulia); Paharpur is a new place (cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 22: Kulia-Paharpur). Vamshivadana — @vamshivadana; Kavidatta — not identified (cf. *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 23); Saranga Thakura — @saranga-thakura.
[^33]: Shrikanta Sena — @shrikanta-sena; "Kavikarna" (কবিকর্ণ) — apparently Kavi Karnapura (@kavi-karnapura), son of Shivananda Sena; Sri Rama Pandita — @srirama-pandita.
[^34]: Journal দয়মন্ত (corrupt), Kishori Dasa "Raghava-Damayanti" (রাঘব-দময়ন্তী). Raghava Pandita — @raghava-pandita; Damayanti is his sister (@damayanti). "Raghava's bags" (রাঘবের ঝালি) — the foodstuffs he brought to the Lord in Puri every year (CC Antya 10).
[^35]: Sadashiva Kaviraja — @sadashiva-kaviraja; his son is Nagara Purushottama (CC Adi 11; v. 17).
[^36]: So the journal: "চারটা" ("four"). Kishori Dasa: "চাতরা বল্লভপুরে" ("at Chatra [and] Vallabhapur"), as in the *Pata-nirnaya* (v. 42: Chatra-Vallabhapur). Vallabhapur and Chatra are new places.
[^37]: Kashishvara — @kashishvara; Shankararanya — @shankararanya (identification unclear); Shrinatha — in the *Pata-nirnaya* (v. 42) "Shrinatha Pandita"; Rudra Pandita served the Deity Radhavallabha (*Pata-nirnaya*, v. 43).
[^38]: Belun is a new place; Ananta Puri — @ananta-puri. "Baghnapara": journal বগনপাড়া, Kishori Dasa বাঘনাপাড়া; a new place. Ramai Thakura — apparently Ramai (Ramachandra) Gosvami (@ramai-jahnava).
[^39]: Guptipara (#guptipara): journal গোপ্‌তিপাড়া, Kishori Dasa গোপতিপাড়া. Vrindavanachandra is a Deity. Satyananda Sarasvati is a new name (not to be confused with @satyananda-bharati).
[^40]: Jirat is a new place; Madhava Acharya is the husband of Ganga (@madhava-acharya-ganga), Ganga-devi is Nityananda's daughter (@ganga-nityananda). Jashra (যশড়া) — #jashra. "Ever rejoicing" (নিত্য বিনোদী) — so the journal; Kishori Dasa has "rejoicing in dance" (নৃত্য বিনোদী); GGD 143: Jagadisha Pandita is a dancer of Vraja (@jagadisha-pandita).
[^41]: Vrindavana Dasa (@vrindavana-dasa), son of Narayani (@narayani). Halisahar — cf. #kumarahatta; Natigrama is a new place; "Dendura" (দেন্দুড়া; in the *Pata-nirnaya*, v. 51, "Dendudi") — possibly Denur, but we do not identify it.
[^42]: Varahanagara (#varahanagara); Bhagavatacharya — @bhagavatacharya. Naihati (#naihati), the village of the Gosvamis' forefathers.
[^43]: The *Pata-nirnaya* is the work of Ramagopala Dasa (1675). "Extract" — চুম্বক, "a brief digest".
