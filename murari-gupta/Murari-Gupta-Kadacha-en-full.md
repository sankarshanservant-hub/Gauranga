# Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charitamrita

*"The Nectar of Sri Krishna Chaitanya's Acts" (Murari Gupta's Kadacha)*

*Murari Gupta*

*Translated from the Sanskrit into English*

*Sanskrit text, transliteration, word-for-word and translation*

---

## Translator's Note

*Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charitamrita* is the Sanskrit poem of Murari Gupta, a fellow-countryman and companion of
Sri Chaitanya and an eyewitness of his life in Navadvipa; tradition calls it Murari Gupta's *Kadacha* ("notes"). It
is the earliest biography of Mahaprabhu, drawn upon by Vrindavana Dasa, Kavikarnapura, Lochana Dasa and Krishnadasa
Kaviraja. The poem is divided into four *prakramas* (parts) and 78 *sargas* (cantos).

**Source.** The Sanskrit text has been reconstructed from three OCR copies: two scans of the edition of Haridasa
Shastri (Vrindavana) and the edition of Haridasa Dasa (Calcutta, 1945, Bengali script); readings were checked against
metre and grammar. Verse numbering follows Haridasa Shastri's edition. Only Murari Gupta's Sanskrit text has been
translated; the translations and commentaries of the editions were not used. Interpretation follows Srila
Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati.

## Prakrama 1, Sarga 1. The Sequence of the Descent

*Benediction; the appearance of Vishvambhara; Srivasa bids Murari tell of the Lord; Navadvipa and its devotees; Jagannatha Mishra*

### Verse 1

स जयत्यतिशुद्धविक्रमः कनकाभः कमलायतेक्षणः।
वरजानुविलम्बिसद्भुजो बहुधा भक्तिरसाभिनर्तकः॥१॥

*sa jayatyatiśuddhavikramaḥ kanakābhaḥ kamalāyatekṣaṇaḥ|*
*varajānuvilambisadbhujo bahudhā bhaktirasābhinartakaḥ||1||*

*Word for word:* sah — he; jayati — be victorious; ati-shuddha-vikramah — whose prowess is utterly pure; kanaka-abhah — shining like gold; kamala-ayata-ikshanah — with eyes wide as lotuses; vara-janu-vilambi-sad-bhujah — whose fine arms hang down to his beautiful knees; bahudha — in many ways; bhakti-rasa-abhinartakah — dancer in the rasa of devotion.

**1.** Glory to Him! His prowess is utterly pure; He shines like gold; His eyes are wide as lotus petals; His shapely arms reach down to His fine knees; and in many ways He dances in the rasa of devotion.

### Verse 2

स जगन्नाथसुतो जगत्पतिर्जगदादिर्जगदार्तिहा विभुः।
कलिपाता कलिभारहारकोऽजनि शच्यां निजभक्तिमुद्वहन्॥२॥

*sa jagannāthasuto jagatpatirjagadādirjagadārtihā vibhuḥ|*
*kalipātā kalibhārahārako'jani śacyāṁ nijabhaktimudvahan||2||*

*Word for word:* sah — he; jagannatha-sutah — the son of Jagannatha; jagat-patih — the lord of the world; jagat-adih — the origin of the world; jagat-arti-ha — remover of the world's distress; vibhuh — the all-powerful; kali-pata — protector in the Kali age; kali-bhara-harakah — remover of the burden of Kali; ajani — was born; shachyam — of Shachi; nija-bhaktim — devotion to himself; udvahan — bearing.

**2.** He — the son of Jagannatha, Lord and origin of the world, the all-powerful one who takes away the world's distress, protector in the age of Kali and remover of its burden — was born of Shachi, bringing with Him devotion to Himself.

### Verse 3

स नवद्वीपवतीषु भूमिषु द्विजवर्यैरभिनन्दितो हरिः।
निजपित्रोः सुखदो गृहे सुखं निवसन् वेदषडङ्गसंहिताम्॥३॥

*sa navadvīpavatīṣu bhūmiṣu dvijavaryairabhinandito hariḥ|*
*nijapitroḥ sukhado gṛhe sukhaṁ nivasan vedaṣaḍaṅgasaṁhitām||3||*

*Word for word:* sah — he; navadvipa-vatishu — of Navadvipa; bhumishu — in the lands; dvija-varyaih — by the best of the twice-born; abhinanditah — welcomed; harih — Hari; nija-pitroh — to his parents; sukha-dah — giving joy; grihe — at home; sukham — happily; nivasan — living; veda-shad-anga-samhitam — the collection of the Vedas with their six limbs (object of the verb in v. 4).

**3.** Welcomed by the best of the twice-born in the lands of Navadvipa, Hari lived happily at home, a joy to His parents; and the Veda with its six limbs[^101-1]

### Verse 4

निपपाठ गुरोर्गृहे वसन् परिचर्याभिरतः शुचिव्रतः।
स च विश्वम्भरसंज्ञको हरिर्युगधर्माचरणाय धर्मिणाम्॥४॥

*nipapāṭha gurorgṛhe vasan paricaryābhirataḥ śucivrataḥ|*
*sa ca viśvambharasaṁjñako hariryugadharmācaraṇāya dharmiṇām||4||*

*Word for word:* nipapatha — studied; guroh — of the guru; grihe — in the house; vasan — living; paricharya-abhiratah — devoted to service; shuchi-vratah — pure in his vows; sah — he; cha — and; vishvambhara-samjnakah — called Vishvambhara; harih — Hari; yuga-dharma-acharanaya — for the practice of the dharma of the age; dharminam — of the righteous.

**4.** He studied while living in His guru's house, devoted to serving him and pure in His vows. And this same Hari, called Vishvambhara, so that the righteous might follow the dharma of the age,

### Verse 5

हरिकीर्तनमादिशत् स्मरन् पुरुषार्थाय हरेरतिप्रियम्।
स गयासु पितृक्रियां चरन् हरिपादाङ्कितभूमिषु स्वयम्॥५॥

*harikīrtanamādiśat smaran puruṣārthāya hareratipriyam|*
*sa gayāsu pitṛkriyāṁ caran haripādāṅkitabhūmiṣu svayam||5||*

*Word for word:* hari-kirtanam — the kirtana of Hari; adishat — enjoined; smaran — remembering; purusha-arthaya — for the goal of human life; hareh — to Hari; ati-priyam — very dear; sah — he; gayasu — in Gaya; pitri-kriyam — the rite for the ancestors; charan — performing; hari-pada-ankita-bhumishu — in the lands marked by Hari's feet; svayam — himself.

**5.** enjoined the kirtana of Hari for the sake of man's highest goal, remembering that it is dear beyond measure to Hari. And when He Himself was performing the rite for His ancestors at Gaya, in the land marked by Hari's feet,

### Verse 6

निजसंस्मृतिमात्रसम्पदः पुलकप्रेमजडो बभूव ह।
स तदा निजमेव मन्दिरं समगादशरीरया गिरा॥६॥

*nijasaṁsmṛtimātrasampadaḥ pulakapremajaḍo babhūva ha|*
*sa tadā nijameva mandiraṁ samagādaśarīrayā girā||6||*

*Word for word:* nija-samsmriti-matra-sampadah — from the wealth of the mere remembrance of himself; pulaka-prema-jadah — stunned with love, his hair standing on end; babhuva ha — became; sah — he; tada — then; nijam eva — to his own; mandiram — home; samagat — went back; ashariraya — by a bodiless; gira — voice.

**6.** the mere remembrance of Himself — that treasure — left Him stunned with love, the hair of His body standing on end. Then, at the word of a bodiless voice[^101-2], He returned to His own home.

### Verse 7

भक्तवर्गमुखवेष्टितः प्रभुः प्रेमपाकपरिपूर्णविग्रहः।
हरिकीर्तनसत्कथासुखं मुमुदे दानवसिंहमर्दनः॥७॥

*bhaktavargamukhaveṣṭitaḥ prabhuḥ premapākaparipūrṇavigrahaḥ|*
*harikīrtanasatkathāsukhaṁ mumude dānavasiṁhamardanaḥ||7||*

*Word for word:* bhakta-varga-mukha-veshtitah — surrounded by the foremost of the band of devotees; prabhuh — the Lord; prema-paka-paripurna-vigrahah — whose form was full of ripened love; hari-kirtana-sat-katha-sukham — in the joy of the kirtana of Hari and of holy talks; mumude — rejoiced; danava-simha-mardanah — the crusher of the lion-like Danavas.

**7.** Surrounded by the foremost of His devotees, the Lord — the crusher of the lions among the Danavas, whose form was filled with ripened love — gave Himself joyfully to the kirtana of Hari and to holy talk of Him.

### Verse 8

अथास्य कीर्तिं श्रवणामृतं सतामुदारकीर्तेः श्रुतिभिः पिपासुभिः।
विगाहितुं श्रीयुतसत्कथां शुभामुवाह हर्षाश्रुविलोललोचनः॥८॥

*athāsya kīrtiṁ śravaṇāmṛtaṁ satāmudārakīrteḥ śrutibhiḥ pipāsubhiḥ|*
*vigāhituṁ śrīyutasatkathāṁ śubhāmuvāha harṣāśruvilolalocanaḥ||8||*

*Word for word:* atha — then; asya — of him; kirtim — the fame; shravana-amritam — nectar for the ears; satam — of the virtuous; udara-kirteh — of generous fame; shrutibhih — with ears; pipasubhih — thirsting; vigahitum — to plunge into; shri-yuta-sat-katham — the splendid true narrative; shubham — auspicious; uvaha — took up; harsha-ashru-vilola-lochanah — his eyes trembling with tears of joy.

**8.** And then, so that thirsting ears might plunge into the glory of Him whose fame is noble — a glory that is nectar to the ears of the virtuous — one whose eyes were brimming with trembling tears of joy took up this splendid, auspicious and true narrative[^101-3].

### Verse 9

भक्तः श्रीवासनामा द्विजकुलकमलप्रोल्लसच्चित्रभानुः
प्राहेदं श्रीमुरारिं त्वमिह वद हरेः श्रीचरित्रं नवीनम्।
तस्याज्ञामाकलय्य प्रकटकरपुटैस्तं नमस्कृत्य भूयः
श्रीमच्चैतन्यमूर्तेः कलिकलुषहरां कीर्तिमाह स्वयं सः॥९॥

*bhaktaḥ śrīvāsanāmā dvijakulakamalaprollasaccitrabhānuḥ*
*prāhedaṁ śrīmurāriṁ tvamiha vada hareḥ śrīcaritraṁ navīnam|*
*tasyājñāmākalayya prakaṭakarapuṭaistaṁ namaskṛtya bhūyaḥ*
*śrīmaccaitanyamūrteḥ kalikaluṣaharāṁ kīrtimāha svayaṁ saḥ||9||*

*Word for word:* bhaktah — the devotee; shrivasa-nama — named Srivasa; dvija-kula-kamala-prollasat-chitrabhanuh — the sun that makes the lotus of the twice-born race bloom; praha — said; idam — this; shri-murarim — to Sri Murari; tvam — you; iha — here; vada — tell; hareh — of Hari; shri-charitram — the holy life; navinam — new; tasya — his; ajnam — command; akalayya — accepting; prakata-kara-putaih — with openly joined palms; tam — to him; namaskritya — bowing; bhuyah — again and again; shrimach-chaitanya-murteh — of the embodied Sriman Chaitanya; kali-kalusha-haram — removing the stain of Kali; kirtim — the glory; aha — told; svayam — himself; sah — he.

**9.** A devotee named Srivasa, the sun who makes the lotus of the twice-born race bloom, said to Sri Murari: "Tell here a new and holy account of Hari's life." Accepting his command, Murari openly joined his palms, bowed to him again and again, and himself began to tell the glory of Sriman Chaitanya, which washes away the stain of Kali.

### Verse 10

अथ स चिन्तयामास वैद्यसूनुर्मुरारिकः।
कथं वक्ष्यामि बह्वर्थां चैतन्यस्य कथां शुभाम्॥१०॥

*atha sa cintayāmāsa vaidyasūnurmurārikaḥ|*
*kathaṁ vakṣyāmi bahvarthāṁ caitanyasya kathāṁ śubhām||10||*

*Word for word:* atha — then; sah — he; chintayam asa — reflected; vaidya-sunuh — the physician's son; murarikah — Murari; katham — how; vakshyami — shall I tell; bahu-artham — rich in meaning; chaitanyasya — of Chaitanya; katham — the story; shubham — auspicious.

**10.** Then Murari, the physician's son, reflected: "How shall I tell the auspicious story of Chaitanya, so rich in meaning,

### Verse 11

यद्वक्तुं नैव शक्नोति वाचस्पतिरपि स्वयम्।
तथापि वैष्णवादेशं कर्तुं युक्तं मतिर्मम॥११॥

*yadvaktuṁ naiva śaknoti vācaspatirapi svayam|*
*tathāpi vaiṣṇavādeśaṁ kartuṁ yuktaṁ matirmama||11||*

*Word for word:* yat — which; vaktum — to tell; na eva — not at all; shaknoti — is able; vachaspatih — Vachaspati; api — even; svayam — himself; tatha api — yet; vaishnava-adesham — the command of a Vaishnava; kartum — to carry out; yuktam — is fitting; matih — the thought; mama — my.

**11.** which even Vachaspati[^101-4] himself cannot tell? And yet it is right to carry out a Vaishnava's command — so I have resolved;

### Verse 12

निर्मला भाति सततं कृष्णस्मरणसम्पदा।
वैष्णवाज्ञा हि फलदा भविष्यति न चान्यथा॥१२॥

*nirmalā bhāti satataṁ kṛṣṇasmaraṇasampadā|*
*vaiṣṇavājñā hi phaladā bhaviṣyati na cānyathā||12||*

*Word for word:* nirmala — pure (my thought); bhati — shines; satatam — ever; krishna-smarana-sampada — by the wealth of remembering Krishna; vaishnava-ajna — the Vaishnava's command; hi — surely; phala-da — fruitful; bhavishyati — will be; na — not; cha — and; anyatha — otherwise.

**12.** and my resolve shines ever pure through the treasure of remembering Krishna. A Vaishnava's command is sure to bear fruit — it cannot be otherwise."

### Verse 13

इत्युक्त्वा वक्तुमारेभे भगवद्भक्तिबृंहिताम्।
कथां धर्मार्थकामाय मोक्षाय विष्णुभक्तये॥१३॥

*ityuktvā vaktumārebhe bhagavadbhaktibṛṁhitām|*
*kathāṁ dharmārthakāmāya mokṣāya viṣṇubhaktaye||13||*

*Word for word:* iti — thus; uktva — having said; vaktum — to tell; arebhe — began; bhagavad-bhakti-brimhitam — enriched with devotion to Bhagavan; katham — the story; dharma-artha-kamaya — for dharma, wealth and desire; mokshaya — for liberation; vishnu-bhaktaye — for devotion to Vishnu.

**13.** With these words he began to tell the story, steeped in devotion to Bhagavan — for the sake of dharma, wealth and pleasure, for liberation, and for devotion to Vishnu.

### Verse 14

नमामि चैतन्यमजं पुरातनं
चतुर्भुजं शङ्खगदाब्जचक्रिणम्।
श्रीवत्सलक्ष्माङ्कितवक्षसं हरिं
सद्भालसंलग्नमणिं सुवाससम्॥१४॥

*namāmi caitanyamajaṁ purātanaṁ*
*caturbhujaṁ śaṅkhagadābjacakriṇam|*
*śrīvatsalakṣmāṅkitavakṣasaṁ hariṁ*
*sadbhālasaṁlagnamaṇiṁ suvāsasam||14||*

*Word for word:* namami — I bow; chaitanyam — to Chaitanya; ajam — the unborn; puratanam — the primeval; chatur-bhujam — four-armed; shankha-gada-abja-chakrinam — bearing conch, mace, lotus and disc; shrivatsa-lakshma-ankita-vakshasam — whose chest is marked with the sign of Shrivatsa; harim — Hari; sad-bhala-samlagna-manim — with a gem set on his fair forehead; su-vasasam — finely clad.

**14.** I bow to Chaitanya — the unborn, the ancient Hari, four-armed, bearing conch, mace, lotus and disc. His chest is marked with the sign of Shrivatsa, a gem rests on His fair brow, and His garments are fine.

### Verse 15

वदामि काञ्चिद्भगवत्कथां सतां
हर्षाय किञ्चित्स्खलनं यदा भवेत्।
तदात्र संशोधयितुं महत्तमाः
प्रमाणमेवात्र परोपकारिणः॥१५॥

*vadāmi kāñcidbhagavatkathāṁ satāṁ*
*harṣāya kiñcitskhalanaṁ yadā bhavet|*
*tadātra saṁśodhayituṁ mahattamāḥ*
*pramāṇamevātra paropakāriṇaḥ||15||*

*Word for word:* vadami — I shall tell; kanchit — some; bhagavat-katham — story of Bhagavan; satam — of the virtuous; harshaya — for the delight; kinchit — some; skhalanam — slip; yada — if; bhavet — should occur; tada — then; atra — here; samshodhayitum — to correct; mahattamah — the greatest souls; pramanam — the authority; eva — indeed; atra — in this; para-upakarinah — who do good to others.

**15.** For the delight of the virtuous I shall tell something of the stories of Bhagavan. Should some slip occur, to correct it is the task of the greatest souls, those who do good to others: in this they alone are the measure.

### Verse 16

नवद्वीप इति ख्याते क्षेत्रे परमवैष्णवे।
ब्राह्मणाः साधवः शान्ता वैष्णवाः सत्कुलोद्भवाः॥१६॥

*navadvīpa iti khyāte kṣetre paramavaiṣṇave|*
*brāhmaṇāḥ sādhavaḥ śāntā vaiṣṇavāḥ satkulodbhavāḥ||16||*

*Word for word:* navadvipe — Navadvipa; iti — thus; khyate — known as; kshetre — in the holy place; parama-vaishnave — supremely Vaishnava; brahmanah — brahmanas; sadhavah — saintly; shantah — peaceful; vaishnavah — Vaishnavas; sat-kula-udbhavah — born in good families.

**16.** In the holy land called Navadvipa, devoted wholly to Vishnu, live brahmanas — saintly, peaceful Vaishnavas of good families,

### Verse 17

महान्तः कर्मनिपुणाः सर्वे शास्त्रार्थपारगाः।
अन्ये च सन्ति बहुशो भिषक्शूद्रवणिग्जनाः॥१७॥

*mahāntaḥ karmanipuṇāḥ sarve śāstrārthapāragāḥ|*
*anye ca santi bahuśo bhiṣakśūdravaṇigjanāḥ||17||*

*Word for word:* mahantah — great; karma-nipunah — skilled in rites; sarve — all; shastra-artha-paragah — masters of the meaning of the scriptures; anye — others; cha — and; santi — there are; bahushah — in great numbers; bhishak-shudra-vanig-janah — physicians, shudras and merchants.

**17.** great souls, skilled in rites, every one a master of the meaning of the scriptures; and many other people live there too — physicians, shudras and merchants.

### Verse 18

स्वाचारनिरताः शुद्धाः सर्वे विद्योपजीविनः।
तत्र देवव्रताः सर्वे वैकुण्ठभवनोपमे॥१८॥

*svācāraniratāḥ śuddhāḥ sarve vidyopajīvinaḥ|*
*tatra devavratāḥ sarve vaikuṇṭhabhavanopame||18||*

*Word for word:* sva-achara-niratah — devoted to their own duties; shuddhah — pure; sarve — all; vidya-upajivinah — living by learning; tatra — there; deva-vratah — vowed to God; sarve — all; vaikuntha-bhavana-upame — (in that place) like the abode of Vaikuntha.

**18.** All of them are true to their own duties and pure; all live by learning; all have vowed themselves to God — in that place, which is like the abode of Vaikuntha.

### Verse 19

श्रीवासो यत्र रेजे हरिपदकमलप्रोल्लसन्मत्तभृङ्गः
प्रेमार्द्रोद्धूतबाहुः परमरसमदैर्गायतीशं सदोत्कः।
गोपीनाथो द्विजाग्र्यः श्रवणपथगते नाम्नि कृष्णस्य मत्तो-
ऽत्युच्चै रौति स्म भूयो लयतरलकरो नृत्यति स्मातिवेलम्॥१९॥

*śrīvāso yatra reje haripadakamalaprollasanmattabhṛṅgaḥ*
*premārdroddhūtabāhuḥ paramarasamadairgāyatīśaṁ sadotkaḥ|*
*gopīnātho dvijāgryaḥ śravaṇapathagate nāmni kṛṣṇasya matto-*
*'tyuccai rauti sma bhūyo layataralakaro nṛtyati smātivelam||19||*

*Word for word:* shrivasah — Srivasa; yatra — where; reje — shone; hari-pada-kamala-prollasan-matta-bhringah — an intoxicated bee sporting at Hari's lotus feet; prema-ardra-uddhuta-bahuh — moist with love, with arms flung up; parama-rasa-madaih — in the rapture of the highest rasa; gayati — sings; isham — the Lord; sada-utkah — ever eager; gopinathah — Gopinatha; dvija-agryah — foremost of the twice-born; shravana-patha-gate — having reached the path of hearing; namni — the name; krishnasya — of Krishna; mattah — intoxicated; ati-uchchaih — very loudly; rauti sma — cried; bhuyah — again; laya-tarala-karah — his hands quivering in rhythm; nrityati sma — danced; ati-velam — beyond measure.

**19.** There shone Srivasa, an intoxicated bee sporting at Hari's lotus feet: wet with the tears of love, his arms flung up[^101-5], ever eager, he sings of the Lord in the rapture of the highest rasa. **23.** There flows the river born from Vishnu's foot — swift, and brimming with the waters of compassion; vying with the Yamuna, daughter of the Sun, and with the Sarayu, she has received Hari, shining like gold[^101-8].

*'bhavadvedācāryaḥ sakalaguṇayukto gurusamaḥ|*

*viśuddhaḥ premārdro navaśaśikalevāśu vavṛdhe||24||*

*Word for word:* jagannathah — Jagannatha; tasmin — in it (Navadvipa); dvija-kula-payodhi-indu-sadrishah — like the moon in the ocean of the twice-born race; abhavat — there was; veda-acharyah — a teacher of the Vedas; sakala-guna-yuktah — endowed with all virtues; guru-samah — equal to Guru (Brihaspati); sah — he; krishna-anghri-dhyana-prabala-tara-yogena — by the mighty yoga of meditation on Krishna's feet; manasa — in mind; vishuddhah — purified; prema-ardrah — moist with love; nava-shashi-kala — the crescent of the new moon; iva — like; ashu — swiftly; vavridhe — grew.

**24.** There lived Jagannatha — like the moon risen from the ocean of the twice-born race — a teacher of the Vedas, endowed with all virtues, equal to Brihaspati. His mind purified by the mighty yoga of meditation on Krishna's feet, his heart melting with love, he swiftly waxed like the crescent of the new moon.

*kathayasva kathāṁ divyāmadbhutāṁ lokapāvanīm|*
*yāṁ śrutvā mucyate lokaḥ saṁsārādghorakilbiṣāt||16||*

*Word for word:* kathayasva — tell; katham — the story; divyam — divine; adbhutam — wondrous; loka-pavanim — purifying the world; yam — which; shrutva — having heard; muchyate — is freed; lokah — the world; samsarat — from samsara; ghora-kilbishat — from dreadful sin.

**16.** "Tell the divine and wondrous story that purifies the world: by hearing it people are freed from the round of births and from dreadful sin.

### Verse 17

श्रीकृष्णचरणाम्भोजे परमाः प्रेमसम्पदः।
जायन्ते सर्वलोकस्य तद्वदस्व हरेः कथाम्॥१७॥

*śrīkṛṣṇacaraṇāmbhoje paramāḥ premasampadaḥ|*
*jāyante sarvalokasya tadvadasva hareḥ kathām||17||*

*Word for word:* shri-krishna-charana-ambhoje — for the lotus feet of Sri Krishna; paramah — supreme; prema-sampadah — treasures of love; jayante — arise; sarva-lokasya — in all people; tat — therefore; vadasva — tell; hareh — of Hari; katham — the story.

**17.** Through it the supreme treasures of love for the lotus feet of Sri Krishna arise in all people. So tell us of Hari!

### Verse 18

कस्य हेतोः पृथिव्यां स जातः सर्वेश्वरो विभुः।
कृतं किमिह तेनैव जगतामीश्वरेण च॥१८॥

*kasya hetoḥ pṛthivyāṁ sa jātaḥ sarveśvaro vibhuḥ|*
*kṛtaṁ kimiha tenaiva jagatāmīśvareṇa ca||18||*

*Word for word:* kasya hetoh — for what reason; prithivyam — on earth; sah — he; jatah — was born; sarva-ishvarah — the lord of all; vibhuh — the all-powerful; kritam — was done; kim — what; iha — here; tena eva — by him; jagatam — of the worlds; ishvarena — by the lord; cha — and.

**18.** For what reason was He, the all-powerful Lord of all, born on earth? What did He, the Lord of the worlds, accomplish here?

### Verse 19

वक्तुमर्हसि भद्राणि कर्माणि मङ्गलानि च।
जगतां तापशान्त्यर्थं प्रेमार्थं सुमहात्मनाम्॥१९॥

*vaktumarhasi bhadrāṇi karmāṇi maṅgalāni ca|*
*jagatāṁ tāpaśāntyarthaṁ premārthaṁ sumahātmanām||19||*

*Word for word:* vaktum arhasi — please tell; bhadrani — auspicious; karmani — deeds; mangalani — blessed; cha — and; jagatam — of the worlds; tapa-shanti-artham — for relieving the distress; prema-artham — for the love; su-maha-atmanam — of the very great souls.

**19.** Be pleased to tell of His auspicious and blessed deeds — to relieve the sufferings of the worlds, and for the love of the very great souls."

### Verse 20

तच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं तस्य पण्डितस्य महात्मनः।
उवाच वचनं प्रीतो मुरारिः श्रूयतामिति॥२०॥

*tacchrutvā vacanaṁ tasya paṇḍitasya mahātmanaḥ|*
*uvāca vacanaṁ prīto murāriḥ śrūyatāmiti||20||*

*Word for word:* tat — that; shrutva — having heard; vachanam — speech; tasya — of that; panditasya — pandita; maha-atmanah — the great soul; uvacha — spoke; vachanam — the word; pritah — pleased; murarih — Murari; shruyatam — let it be heard; iti — thus.

**20.** Hearing the words of the great-hearted pandita, Murari answered with pleasure: "Listen!

### Verse 21

साधु ते कथयिष्यामि यथाशक्त्या द्विजोत्तम।
संक्षेपाद्विस्तरान्नालं वक्तुं शक्नोति भार्गवः॥२१॥

*sādhu te kathayiṣyāmi yathāśaktyā dvijottama|*
*saṁkṣepādvistarānnālaṁ vaktuṁ śaknoti bhārgavaḥ||21||*

*Word for word:* sadhu — well; te — to you; kathayishyami — I shall tell; yatha-shaktya — as far as I can; dvija-uttama — O best of the twice-born; samkshepat — in brief; vistarat — in full; na — not; alam — able; vaktum — to tell; shaknoti — can; bhargavah — Bhargava.

**21.** Well then, O best of the twice-born, I shall tell you as far as I am able — in brief, for not even Bhargava[^102-6] could tell it in full.

### Verse 22

अथ नारदो धर्मात्मा वर्षे भारतसंज्ञके।
वैष्णवाग्र्यो महातेजाः पूर्णचन्द्रसमप्रभः॥२२॥

*atha nārado dharmātmā varṣe bhāratasaṁjñake|*
*vaiṣṇavāgryo mahātejāḥ pūrṇacandrasamaprabhaḥ||22||*

*Word for word:* atha — once; naradah — Narada; dharma-atma — righteous; varshe — in the land; bharata-samjnake — called Bharata; vaishnava-agryah — the foremost of Vaishnavas; maha-tejah — of great power; purna-chandra-sama-prabhah — radiant as the full moon.

**22.** Once, in the land called Bharata, Narada — the righteous soul, foremost of the Vaishnavas, of great power, radiant as the full moon,

### Verse 23

कैलासशिखराकारो मेखलावरभूषणः।
ऐणचर्मधरो विष्णोरंशः सर्वजनप्रियः॥२३॥

*kailāsaśikharākāro mekhalāvarabhūṣaṇaḥ|*
*aiṇacarmadharo viṣṇoraṁśaḥ sarvajanapriyaḥ||23||*

*Word for word:* kailasa-shikhara-akarah — like the peak of Kailasa in appearance; mekhala-vara-bhushanah — adorned with a fine girdle; aina-charma-dharah — wearing a deerskin; vishnoh — of Vishnu; amshah — a portion; sarva-jana-priyah — dear to all people.

**23.** white as the peak of Kailasa, adorned with a fine girdle, clad in a deerskin, a portion of Vishnu, beloved of all people —

### Verse 24

सर्वेषामुपकाराय बभ्रामाकाशमण्डले।
महतीं रणयन् प्रीतो हरिनाम प्रगायतीम्॥२४॥

*sarveṣāmupakārāya babhrāmākāśamaṇḍale|*
*mahatīṁ raṇayan prīto harināma pragāyatīm||24||*

*Word for word:* sarvesham — of all; upakaraya — for the good; babhrama — wandered; akasha-mandale — through the vault of the sky; mahatim — the Mahati (his vina); ranayan — sounding; pritah — joyful; hari-nama — the name of Hari; pragayatim — singing forth.

**24.** was roaming the vault of the sky for the good of all, joyfully sounding his Mahati[^102-7], which sings the name of Hari.

### Verse 25

द्रक्ष्यामि वैष्णवं कुत्र तत्र वत्स्यामि साम्प्रतम्।
इति सञ्चिन्त्य मनसा ददर्श पृथिवीमिमाम्॥२५॥

*drakṣyāmi vaiṣṇavaṁ kutra tatra vatsyāmi sāmpratam|*
*iti sañcintya manasā dadarśa pṛthivīmimām||25||*

*Word for word:* drakshyami — I shall see; vaishnavam — a Vaishnava; kutra — where; tatra — there; vatsyami — I shall dwell; sampratam — now; iti — thus; sanchintya — reflecting; manasa — in his mind; dadarsha — saw; prithivim — earth; imam — this.

**25.** 'Where shall I find a Vaishnava? There I shall now make my dwelling,' he thought, and surveyed this earth with his mind.

### Verse 26

कलिना पापमित्रेण प्रथितां मलपङ्किलाम्।
गामेव म्लेच्छहस्तस्थां प्रचण्डकरशोषिताम्॥२६॥

*kalinā pāpamitreṇa prathitāṁ malapaṅkilām|*
*gāmeva mlecchahastasthāṁ pracaṇḍakaraśoṣitām||26||*

*Word for word:* kalina — by Kali; papa-mitrena — the friend of sin; prathitam — the wide (earth); mala-pankilam — muddied with filth; gam — the earth (the cow); eva — indeed; mlechchha-hasta-stham — in the hands of the mlechchhas; prachanda-kara-shoshitam — drained by cruel taxes.

**26.** And he saw that Kali, the friend of sin, had soiled the wide earth with filth, and that the earth-cow herself was in the hands of the mlechchhas, drained by cruel taxes[^102-8].

### Verse 27

जनांश्च ददृशे तत्र पापव्याधिसमाकुलान्।
परापवादनिरतान् शठान् ह्रस्वायुषः कृशान्॥२७॥

*janāṁśca dadṛśe tatra pāpavyādhisamākulān|*
*parāpavādaniratān śaṭhān hrasvāyuṣaḥ kṛśān||27||*

*Word for word:* janan — people; cha — and; dadrishe — he saw; tatra — there; papa-vyadhi-samakulan — beset by sins and diseases; para-apavada-niratan — given to slandering others; shathan — deceitful; hrasva-ayushah — short-lived; krishan — emaciated.

**27.** He saw there, too, people beset by sins and diseases, eager to slander others, deceitful, short-lived and wasted;

### Verse 28

राज्ञश्च पापनिपुणान् शूद्रान् स यवनान् खलान्।
म्लेच्छान् विकर्मनिरतान् प्रजासर्वस्वहारकान्॥२८॥

*rājñaśca pāpanipuṇān śūdrān sa yavanān khalān|*
*mlecchān vikarmaniratān prajāsarvasvahārakān||28||*

*Word for word:* rajnah — kings; cha — and; papa-nipunan — skilled in sin; shudran — shudras; sah — he; yavanan — Yavanas; khalan — villains; mlechchhan — mlechchhas; vikarma-niratan — given to forbidden deeds; praja-sarvasva-harakan — robbing their subjects of all they have.

**28.** he saw kings skilled in sin, shudras, villainous Yavanas, mlechchhas given to forbidden deeds and robbing their subjects of all they had,

### Verse 29

शास्त्रज्ञानपि साधूनां निन्दकानात्ममानिनः।
एतान् बहुविधान् दृष्ट्वा चिन्तयामास नारदः॥२९॥

*śāstrajñānapi sādhūnāṁ nindakānātmamāninaḥ|*
*etān bahuvidhān dṛṣṭvā cintayāmāsa nāradaḥ||29||*

*Word for word:* shastra-jnan — knowers of scripture; api — even; sadhunam — of saints; nindakan — revilers; atma-maninah — proud; etan — these; bahu-vidhan — of many kinds; drishtva — having seen; chintayam asa — pondered; naradah — Narada.

**29.** and even men learned in scripture — proud, and reviling the saints. Seeing all these people, so many and so various, Narada fell to pondering."

*Thus ends the second sarga, "Narada's Sorrow," of the first prakrama of the great poem "Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charita."*

[^102-2]: The epithet kula-kricchada is the same in all the sources, but its meaning is unclear; the translation is conjectural.
[^102-3]: That is, like Indra, who won his consort Shachi.
[^102-4]: Upendra is Vishnu as Vamana, born of the sage Kashyapa and Aditi as Indra's younger brother.
[^102-5]: From this verse the poem is framed as a dialogue: Damodara Pandita asks, and Murari tells.

[^102-7]: The Mahati is Narada's vina.

## Prakrama 1, Sarga 3. Narada's Inquiry

*Narada goes to Vaikuntha; the vision of Vaikuntha and of Krishna; Narada pleads for the world perishing in Kali; the Lord agrees to descend*

### Verse 1

कलेः प्रथमसन्ध्यायां निमग्नेयं वसुन्धरा।
सर्वेषां पापदग्धानां हरिनामरसायनः॥१॥

*kaleḥ prathamasandhyāyāṁ nimagneyaṁ vasundharā|*
*sarveṣāṁ pāpadagdhānāṁ harināmarasāyanaḥ||1||*

*Word for word:* kaleh — of Kali; prathama-sandhyayam — in the first twilight (opening period); nimagna — is sunk; iyam — this; vasundhara — earth; sarvesham — for all; papa-dagdhanam — scorched by sin; hari-nama-rasayanah — the elixir of Hari's name.

**1.** "'In the first twilight of Kali[^103-1] this earth has sunk (into sin). For all who are scorched by sin, the name of Hari is the healing elixir;

### Verse 2

तारकोऽयं भवत्येव वैष्णवद्वेषिणं विना।
आत्मसम्भाविता ये च ये च वैष्णवनिन्दकाः॥२॥

*tārako'yaṁ bhavatyeva vaiṣṇavadveṣiṇaṁ vinā|*
*ātmasambhāvitā ye ca ye ca vaiṣṇavanindakāḥ||2||*

*Word for word:* tarakah — a deliverer; ayam — it; bhavati eva — surely becomes; vaishnava-dveshinam — one who hates Vaishnavas; vina — except; atma-sambhavitah — self-conceited; ye — who; cha — and; ye — who; cha — and; vaishnava-nindakah — revilers of Vaishnavas.

**2.** it surely delivers everyone except those who hate the Vaishnavas — the self-conceited and the revilers of Vaishnavas.

### Verse 3

ये कृष्णनाम्नि देहेषु निन्देयुर्मन्दबुद्धयः।
तेऽनित्या इति वक्ष्यन्ते तेषां निरय एव हि॥३॥

*ye kṛṣṇanāmni deheṣu nindeyurmandabuddhayaḥ|*
*te'nityā iti vakṣyante teṣāṁ niraya eva hi||3||*

*Word for word:* ye — who; krishna-namni — Krishna's name; deheshu — (his) forms; nindeyuh — would revile; manda-buddhayah — the dull-witted; te — they; anityah — non-eternal; iti — thus; vakshyante — will say; tesham — for them; nirayah — hell; eva hi — alone.

**3.** But those dull-witted ones who would revile the name of Krishna and His forms, calling them non-eternal[^103-2] — for them there is only hell.

### Verse 4

अत्र किं स्यादुपायोऽयमिति निश्चित्य शुद्धधीः।
वैकुण्ठाख्यं परं धाम जगाम करुणानिधिः॥४॥

*atra kiṁ syādupāyo'yamiti niścitya śuddhadhīḥ|*
*vaikuṇṭhākhyaṁ paraṁ dhāma jagāma karuṇānidhiḥ||4||*

*Word for word:* atra — here; kim — what; syat — could be; upayah — the remedy; ayam — this; iti — thus; nishchitya — having considered; shuddha-dhih — pure of mind; vaikuntha-akhyam — called Vaikuntha; param — the supreme; dhama — abode; jagama — went; karuna-nidhih — the treasury of compassion.

**4.** What remedy can there be for this?' Having pondered thus, the sage, pure of mind, a treasury of compassion, went to the supreme abode called Vaikuntha.

### Verse 5

अथ त्रिवेदीपरिगीयमानं
ददर्श वैकुण्ठमखण्डधिष्ण्यम्।
स्वतेजसा ध्वस्तरजःसमूहं
दिशां दशामाप गुणात्परां मुनिः॥५॥

*atha trivedīparigīyamānaṁ*
*dadarśa vaikuṇṭhamakhaṇḍadhiṣṇyam|*
*svatejasā dhvastarajaḥsamūhaṁ*
*diśāṁ daśāmāpa guṇātparāṁ muniḥ||5||*

*Word for word:* atha — then; tri-vedi-parigiyamanam — sung by the three Vedas; dadarsha — saw; vaikuntham — Vaikuntha; akhanda-dhishnyam — the undivided abode; sva-tejasa — by its own radiance; dhvasta-rajah-samuham — that has dispelled the dust (rajas); disham — of the quarters; dasham — a state; apa — attained; gunat — the gunas; param — beyond; munih — the sage.

**5.** And the sage beheld Vaikuntha, the undivided abode sung by the three Vedas, which by its own radiance has scattered all the dust (of rajas) from every quarter; and he attained a state beyond the gunas.

### Verse 6

मधुव्रतानां निवहैर्हरेर्यशः
प्रगीयमानं कमलावलीषु।
विराजितं रत्नतटाभिराम-
वापीभिरामुक्तलतासुगन्धिभिः॥६॥

*madhuvratānāṁ nivahairhareryaśaḥ*
*pragīyamānaṁ kamalāvalīṣu|*
*virājitaṁ ratnataṭābhirāma-*
*vāpībhirāmuktalatāsugandhibhiḥ||6||*

*Word for word:* madhu-vratanam — of bees; nivahaih — by swarms; hareh — Hari's; yashah — fame; pragiyamanam — being sung; kamala-avalishu — among rows of lotuses; virajitam — adorned; ratna-tata-abhirama-vapibhih — with ponds lovely with jewelled banks; amukta-lata-sugandhibhih — fragrant with hanging creepers.

**6.** There swarms of bees sing Hari's fame among rows of lotuses; it is adorned with ponds lovely with jewelled banks and fragrant with hanging creepers.

### Verse 7

माणिक्यगेहैर्वलभीभिरन्वितं
गजेन्द्रमुक्तावलिभूषिताभिः।
सार्वर्तवैः शाखिभिरन्वितं खगै-
र्विकूजितं चन्द्रशिलापथाढ्यम्॥७॥

*māṇikyagehairvalabhībhiranvitaṁ*
*gajendramuktāvalibhūṣitābhiḥ|*
*sārvartavaiḥ śākhibhiranvitaṁ khagai-*
*rvikūjitaṁ candraśilāpathāḍhyam||7||*

*Word for word:* manikya-gehaih — with ruby houses; valabhibhih — with turrets; anvitam — furnished; gajendra-mukta-avali-bhushitabhih — adorned with strings of pearls from lordly elephants; sarvartavaih — (bearing) in all seasons; shakhibhih — with trees; anvitam — furnished; khagaih — by birds; vikujitam — filled with song; chandra-shila-patha-adhyam — rich in paths of moonstone.

**7.** There are ruby houses with turrets adorned with strings of pearls from the heads of lordly elephants[^103-3]; there are trees that bear fruit in every season; there birds fill the air with song, and paths of moonstone abound.

### Verse 8

तत्र श्रिया जुष्टमजं पुरातनं
लसत्किरीटद्युतिरञ्जितालकम्।
विकासिदिव्याब्जजितेक्षणं लसत्-
सुधाकराराधितसन्मुखोल्लसम्॥८॥

*tatra śriyā juṣṭamajaṁ purātanaṁ*
*lasatkirīṭadyutirañjitālakam|*
*vikāsidivyābjajitekṣaṇaṁ lasat-*
*sudhākarārādhitasanmukhollasam||8||*

*Word for word:* tatra — there; shriya — by Shri (Lakshmi); jushtam — attended; ajam — the unborn; puratanam — the primeval; lasat-kirita-dyuti-ranjita-alakam — whose locks were lit by the radiance of a gleaming crown; vikasi-divya-abja-jita-ikshanam — whose eyes surpassed blooming celestial lotuses; lasat — shining; sudhakara-aradhita-san-mukha-ullasam — radiant with a fair face worshipped by the moon.

**8.** There he saw the Unborn, the Primeval One, whom Shri attends: His locks are lit by the radiance of a gleaming crown, His eyes outshine blooming celestial lotuses, and His fair, shining face is worshipped by the moon itself;

### Verse 9

लसन्महाकुण्डलगण्डशोभितं
सुकम्बुकण्ठं कनकोज्ज्वलांशुकम्।
कृष्णं चतुर्भिः परिघोपमैर्भुजै-
र्नीलाद्रिशृङ्गं सुरपादपैरिव॥९॥

*lasanmahākuṇḍalagaṇḍaśobhitaṁ*
*sukambukaṇṭhaṁ kanakojjvalāṁśukam|*
*kṛṣṇaṁ caturbhiḥ parighopamairbhujai-*
*rnīlādriśṛṅgaṁ surapādapairiva||9||*

*Word for word:* lasan-maha-kundala-ganda-shobhitam — whose cheeks were graced by gleaming great earrings; su-kambu-kantham — with a fine conch-like neck; kanaka-ujjvala-amshukam — in garments bright as gold; krishnam — Krishna; chaturbhih — with four; parigha-upamaih — like door-bars; bhujaih — arms; nila-adri-shringam — the peak of a blue mountain; sura-padapaih — with trees of the gods (wish-fulfilling trees); iva — like.

**9.** great gleaming earrings grace His cheeks, His neck is fine as a conch, His garments shine like gold — Krishna, whose four arms are like door-bars: He was like a blue mountain peak with wish-fulfilling trees.

### Verse 10

विराजमानं कनकाङ्गदादिभि-
र्मुक्तावलीभिर्वरहेमसूत्रैः।
सकिङ्किणीजालनिबद्धचेलो-
ल्लसन्नितम्बं वरपादपङ्कजम्॥१०॥

*virājamānaṁ kanakāṅgadādibhi-*
*rmuktāvalībhirvarahemasūtraiḥ|*
*sakiṅkiṇījālanibaddhacelo-*
*llasannitambaṁ varapādapaṅkajam||10||*

*Word for word:* virajamanam — resplendent; kanaka-angada-adibhih — with golden armlets and the like; mukta-avalibhih — with strings of pearls; vara-hema-sutraih — with fine golden cords; sa-kinkini-jala-nibaddha-chela-ullasan-nitambam — whose hips shone with a cloth bound by a net of little bells; vara-pada-pankajam — with beautiful lotus feet.

**10.** He was resplendent with golden armlets and other ornaments, with strings of pearls and fine golden cords; on His hips shone a cloth bound with a net of little bells, and His feet were lovely lotuses.

### Verse 11

तदीयपादाब्जमनोज्ञगन्ध-
माघ्राय हर्षाश्रुतनूरुहोद्गमैः।
विसंज्ञ एवाशु पपात भूमौ
स दण्डवत्कृष्णसमीपतो मुनिः॥११॥

*tadīyapādābjamanojñagandha-*
*māghrāya harṣāśrutanūruhodgamaiḥ|*
*visaṁjña evāśu papāta bhūmau*
*sa daṇḍavatkṛṣṇasamīpato muniḥ||11||*

*Word for word:* tadiya-pada-abja-manojna-gandham — the charming fragrance of his lotus feet; aghraya — having inhaled; harsha-ashru-tanu-ruha-udgamaih — with tears of joy and bristling hair; visamjnah — senseless; eva — as if; ashu — at once; papata — fell; bhumau — on the ground; sah — he; danda-vat — like a rod; krishna-samipatah — near Krishna; munih — the sage.

**11.** Inhaling the enchanting fragrance of His lotus feet, the sage, with tears of joy and his hair standing on end, at once fell to the ground near Krishna, stretched out like a rod, as if senseless.

### Verse 12

ततः प्रसार्याशु करं कृतज्ञो
रत्नाङ्गुरीभिन्ननखप्रभं प्रभुः।
मुदा स्पृशन्मूर्ध्नि मुनेर्मनोहरं
बभाष ईषत्स्मितशोभिताननः॥१२॥

*tataḥ prasāryāśu karaṁ kṛtajño*
*ratnāṅgurībhinnanakhaprabhaṁ prabhuḥ|*
*mudā spṛśanmūrdhni munermanoharaṁ*
*babhāṣa īṣatsmitaśobhitānanaḥ||12||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; prasarya — stretching out; ashu — at once; karam — his hand; kritajnah — grateful; ratna-anguri-bhinna-nakha-prabham — its nail-radiance variegated by jewelled rings; prabhuh — the Lord; muda — joyfully; sprishan — touching; murdhni — on the head; muneh — of the sage; manoharam — charmingly; babhasha — spoke; ishat-smita-shobhita-ananah — his face graced by a gentle smile.

**12.** Then the grateful Lord at once stretched out His hand, the radiance of its nails mingling with the gleam of jewelled rings, joyfully touched the sage's head and, with a gentle smile on His face, spoke enchantingly:

### Verse 13

स्वायम्भुवोत्तिष्ठ मुने महात्मन्
यन्नो वदस्यद्य करोमि तत्ते।
ममैव कालोऽयमुपागतः स्वयं
युगेषु धर्माचरणाय धर्मिणाम्॥१३॥

*svāyambhuvottiṣṭha mune mahātman*
*yanno vadasyadya karomi tatte|*
*mamaiva kālo'yamupāgataḥ svayaṁ*
*yugeṣu dharmācaraṇāya dharmiṇām||13||*

*Word for word:* svayambhuva — O son of the Self-born (Brahma); uttishtha — rise; mune — O sage; maha-atman — O great soul; yat — what; nah — to us; vadasi — you say; adya — today; karomi — I do; tat — that; te — for you; mama — my; eva — indeed; kalah — time; ayam — this; upagatah — has come; svayam — of itself; yugeshu — in the ages; dharma-acharanaya — for the practice of dharma; dharminam — of the righteous.

**13.** 'Rise, O son of the Self-born[^103-4], O sage, O great soul! Whatever you tell Me today, that I shall do for you. For My time has come of itself — the time when, in the ages, the righteous must practise dharma.'

### Verse 14

ततः समुत्थाप्य महर्षिसत्तमं
महत्तमैकान्तपरायणो हरिः।
समादिदेशासनमाशु तस्मै
तस्मिन्निविष्टो मुनिराज्ञया हरेः॥१४॥

*tataḥ samutthāpya maharṣisattamaṁ*
*mahattamaikāntaparāyaṇo hariḥ|*
*samādideśāsanamāśu tasmai*
*tasminniviṣṭo munirājñayā hareḥ||14||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; samutthapya — raising; maharshi-sattamam — the best of the great rishis; mahattama-ekanta-parayanah — wholly devoted to the greatest souls; harih — Hari; samadidesha — indicated; asanam — a seat; ashu — at once; tasmai — to him; tasmin — on it; nivishtah — sat; munih — the sage; ajnaya — at the command; hareh — of Hari.

**14.** Then Hari, wholly devoted to the greatest souls, raised up the best of the great rishis and at once showed him to a seat; and at Hari's bidding the sage sat down.

### Verse 15

अथान्वपृच्छद्भगवान्मुने कथं
सम्प्राप्तवान्मामिह किं तवेप्सितम्।
पूर्णस्य कार्यं करवाणि साधो
परोपकाराय महद्विचेष्टितम्॥१५॥

*athānvapṛcchadbhagavānmune kathaṁ*
*samprāptavānmāmiha kiṁ tavepsitam|*
*pūrṇasya kāryaṁ karavāṇi sādho*
*paropakārāya mahadviceṣṭitam||15||*

*Word for word:* atha — then; anvaprichchhat — asked; bhagavan — Bhagavan; mune — O sage; katham — how (why); sampraptavan — have come; mam — to me; iha — here; kim — what; tava — your; ipsitam — wish; purnasya — (though I am) complete; karyam — the task; karavani — let me do; sadho — O holy one; para-upakaraya — for the good of others; mahad-vicheshtitam — the deeds of the great.

**15.** Then Bhagavan asked: 'O sage, why have you come here to Me? What is your wish? Though I am complete, let Me do your task, O holy one: the great exert themselves for the good of others.'

### Verse 16

इत्थं सतोयाम्बुदतुल्यघोषं
वचोऽमृतं कृष्णदयामृताब्धेः।
उवाच पूर्णस्मितवीक्षया हरे-
र्नमामि लोकान्परिपाहि दुःखितान्॥१६॥

*itthaṁ satoyāmbudatulyaghoṣaṁ*
*vaco'mṛtaṁ kṛṣṇadayāmṛtābdheḥ|*
*uvāca pūrṇasmitavīkṣayā hare-*
*rnamāmi lokānparipāhi duḥkhitān||16||*

*Word for word:* ittham — such (was); sa-toya-ambuda-tulya-ghosham — resonant as a rain-laden cloud; vachah — speech; amritam — nectar; krishna-daya-amrita-abdheh — of Krishna, the ocean of the nectar of mercy; uvacha — said; purna-smita-vikshaya — under the glance full of smiles; hareh — of Hari; namami — I bow; lokan — the worlds; paripahi — protect; duhkhitan — suffering.

**16.** Such were the nectarean words of Krishna, the ocean of the nectar of mercy, resonant as a rain-laden cloud. And under Hari's glance, full of smiles, the sage said: 'I bow to You! Protect the suffering worlds!

### Verse 17

क्षितिः क्षिणोत्यद्य समाकुला विभो
जनस्य पापौघयुतस्य धारणात्।
जनाश्च सर्वे कलिकालदष्टाः
पापे रतास्त्यक्तभवत्प्रसङ्गाः॥१७॥

*kṣitiḥ kṣiṇotyadya samākulā vibho*
*janasya pāpaughayutasya dhāraṇāt|*
*janāśca sarve kalikāladaṣṭāḥ*
*pāpe ratāstyaktabhavatprasaṅgāḥ||17||*

*Word for word:* kshitih — the earth; kshinoti — is wasting away; adya — now; samakula — distressed; vibho — O mighty one; janasya — of people; papa-augha-yutasya — laden with floods of sin; dharanat — from bearing; janah — people; cha — and; sarve — all; kali-kala-dashtah — bitten by the time of Kali; pape — in sin; ratah — delighting; tyakta-bhavat-prasangah — having abandoned talk of you.

**17.** Now, O mighty Lord, the earth is wasting away in distress under the weight of people laden with floods of sin. All the people, bitten by the time of Kali, delight in sin and have abandoned talk of You.

### Verse 18

तान्पाहि नाथ त्वदृते न तेषा-
मन्योऽस्ति पाता निरयात्तु सद्गतिः।
एवं विचार्य कुरु सर्वलोक-
नाथ स्वयं सद्गतिरीश नान्यः॥१८॥

*tānpāhi nātha tvadṛte na teṣā-*
*manyo'sti pātā nirayāttu sadgatiḥ|*
*evaṁ vicārya kuru sarvaloka-*
*nātha svayaṁ sadgatirīśa nānyaḥ||18||*

*Word for word:* tan — them; pahi — protect; natha — O master; tvat-rite — apart from you; na — not; tesham — for them; anyah — another; asti — is; pata — protector; nirayat — from hell; tu — indeed; sat-gatih — good refuge; evam — thus; vicharya — having considered; kuru — act; sarva-loka-natha — O lord of all worlds; svayam — yourself; sat-gatih — the good refuge; isha — O Lord; na — not; anyah — another.

**18.** Protect them, O Master: apart from You they have no other protector from hell, no other good refuge. Consider this and act, O Lord of all worlds: You Yourself are the good refuge, O Lord, and no other.'

### Verse 19

इत्थं समाकर्ण्य मुनेर्वचो हरि-
र्विदन्नपि प्राह किमाचरिष्ये।
केनाप्युपायेन भवेद्धि शान्ति-
स्तद्ब्रूहि तं प्राह पुनः स्वभूसुतः॥१९॥

*itthaṁ samākarṇya munervaco hari-*
*rvidannapi prāha kimācariṣye|*
*kenāpyupāyena bhaveddhi śānti-*
*stadbrūhi taṁ prāha punaḥ svabhūsutaḥ||19||*

*Word for word:* ittham — such; samakarnya — having heard; muneh — of the sage; vachah — words; harih — Hari; vidan — knowing; api — though; praha — said; kim — what; acharishye — shall I do; kena api — by what; upayena — means; bhavet — may come; hi — indeed; shantih — peace; tat — that; bruhi — tell; tam — to him; praha — said; punah — again; svabhu-sutah — the son of the Self-born.

**19.** Having heard these words of the sage, Hari, though He knew all, said: 'What shall I do? By what means can peace come about? Tell Me.' And the son of the Self-born spoke to Him again:

### Verse 20

स्वयं सुशीतः शतचन्द्रमा यथा
भूदेववंशेऽप्यवतीर्य सत्कुले।
वात्स्ये जगन्नाथसुतेति विश्रुतिं
समाप्नुहि त्वं कुरु शं धरण्याः॥२०॥

*svayaṁ suśītaḥ śatacandramā yathā*
*bhūdevavaṁśe'pyavatīrya satkule|*
*vātsye jagannāthasuteti viśrutiṁ*
*samāpnuhi tvaṁ kuru śaṁ dharaṇyāḥ||20||*

*Word for word:* svayam — yourself; su-shitah — cooling; shata-chandrama — a hundred moons; yatha — like; bhu-deva-vamshe — in a lineage of brahmanas ("gods of the earth"); api — indeed; avatirya — descending; sat-kule — in a good family; vatsye — of the Vatsya line; jagannatha-suta — "son of Jagannatha"; iti — thus; vishrutim — renown; samapnuhi — attain; tvam — you; kuru — make; sham — welfare; dharanyah — of the earth.

**20.** 'Descend Yourself, cooling as a hundred moons, into a good family of the brahmana race, of the Vatsya line[^103-5]; win renown as "the son of Jagannatha," and bring welfare to the earth.

### Verse 21

रामादिरूपैर्भगवन्कृतं हि य-
त्पापात्मनां राक्षसदानवानाम्।
वधादिकं कर्म न चेह कार्यं
मनो नराणां परिशोधयस्व॥२१॥

*rāmādirūpairbhagavankṛtaṁ hi ya-*
*tpāpātmanāṁ rākṣasadānavānām|*
*vadhādikaṁ karma na ceha kāryaṁ*
*mano narāṇāṁ pariśodhayasva||21||*

*Word for word:* rama-adi-rupaih — in the forms of Rama and others; bhagavan — O Bhagavan; kritam — done; hi — indeed; yat — which; papa-atmanam — of the sinful; rakshasa-danavanam — of Rakshasas and Danavas; vadha-adikam — slaying and the like; karma — the deed; na — not; cha — and; iha — here; karyam — to be done; manah — the mind; naranam — of men; parishodhayasva — purify.

**21.** What You did, O Bhagavan, in the forms of Rama and others — the slaying of sinful Rakshasas and Danavas and the like — is not to be done here: purify the minds of men.

### Verse 22

तानासुरं भावमुपागतान्हि
यदा हनिष्ये क्व तदास्ति लोकः।
एवं व्यवस्य स्वधियात्मनो यशः
प्रख्याहि लोकाः सुखिनो भवन्तु॥२२॥

*tānāsuraṁ bhāvamupāgatānhi*
*yadā haniṣye kva tadāsti lokaḥ|*
*evaṁ vyavasya svadhiyātmano yaśaḥ*
*prakhyāhi lokāḥ sukhino bhavantu||22||*

*Word for word:* tan — them; asuram — demoniac; bhavam — state; upagatan — who have entered; hi — indeed; yada — if; hanishye — I kill; kva — where; tada — then; asti — is; lokah — the world; evam — thus; vyavasya — resolving; sva-dhiya — by your own understanding; atmanah — your own; yashah — glory; prakhyahi — make known; lokah — people; sukhinah — happy; bhavantu — may become.

**22.** "If I slay those who have fallen into the demoniac state, where then will the world be?" — resolving thus in Your own mind, make Your glory known, and may the people be happy.

### Verse 23

तत्रैव रुद्रेण मुनिप्रवीराः
कर्तुं हि साहाय्यमवातरिष्यन्।
तथेति तं प्राह हरिः सुरर्षिं
सोऽपि प्रणम्याशु जगाम हृष्टः॥२३॥

*tatraiva rudreṇa munipravīrāḥ*
*kartuṁ hi sāhāyyamavātariṣyan|*
*tatheti taṁ prāha hariḥ surarṣiṁ*
*so'pi praṇamyāśu jagāma hṛṣṭaḥ||23||*

*Word for word:* tatra eva — there too; rudrena — with Rudra; muni-pravirah — the foremost of sages; kartum — to give; hi — indeed; sahayyam — help; avatarishyan — would descend; tatha — so be it; iti — thus; tam — to him; praha — said; harih — Hari; sura-rshim — to the divine rishi; sah — he; api — too; pranamya — bowing; ashu — at once; jagama — went; hrishtah — joyful.

**23.** And there too, together with Rudra, the foremost of sages will descend to help You'[^103-6]. 'So be it,' Hari said to the divine rishi; and he, bowing, at once departed joyfully."

*Thus ends the third sarga, "Narada's Inquiry," of the first prakrama of the great poem "Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charita."*

[^103-1]: The "twilight" (sandhya) is the opening period of a yuga; the Kali age has only just begun.
[^103-2]: The Lord's name and form are eternal and not material; to call them non-eternal is an offence.
[^103-3]: According to poetic convention, pearls form in the heads of lordly elephants.
[^103-4]: Narada is the son of Brahma, the Self-born (Svayambhu).
[^103-5]: Vatsya is the brahmana clan (gotra) to which Jagannatha Mishra belonged.

## Prakrama 1, Sarga 4. On the Descents

*The Lord's companions as portions of the Lord, of gods and of sages; the story of Haridasa; the two kinds of avatara: the avataras of the ages and the avataras for a particular purpose*

### Verse 1

अथ श्रुत्वा तु तत्सर्वं श्रीदामोदरपण्डितः।
उवाच परमप्रीतः कथ्यतां नृहरेः कथाम्॥१॥

*atha śrutvā tu tatsarvaṁ śrīdāmodarapaṇḍitaḥ|*
*uvāca paramaprītaḥ kathyatāṁ nṛhareḥ kathām||1||*

*Word for word:* atha — then; shrutva — having heard; tu — indeed; tat — that; sarvam — all; shri-damodara-panditah — Sri Damodara Pandita; uvacha — said; parama-pritah — greatly pleased; kathyatam — let be told; nrihareh — of Nrihari (the Lord); katham — the story.

**1.** Having heard all this, Sri Damodara Pandita, greatly pleased, said: "Tell us, then, the story of Nrihari[^104-1].

### Verse 2

के के तत्रावतारेषु स्ववतीर्णा महीतले।
अवताराश्च कतिधा तान्वदस्वानुपूर्वशः॥२॥

*ke ke tatrāvatāreṣu svavatīrṇā mahītale|*
*avatārāśca katidhā tānvadasvānupūrvaśaḥ||2||*

*Word for word:* ke ke — who and who; tatra — there; avatareshu — in the descents; su-avatirnah — descended; mahi-tale — to the earth; avatarah — the descents; cha — and; katidha — of how many kinds; tan — them; vadasva — tell; anupurvashah — in order.

**2.** Who came down to the earth in these descents? And of how many kinds are the descents? Tell me of them in order."

### Verse 3

इति श्रुत्वा द्विजाग्र्यस्य वचनं श्रीमुरारिकः।
उवाच परमप्रीत्या श्रूयतामिति सादरम्॥३॥

*iti śrutvā dvijāgryasya vacanaṁ śrīmurārikaḥ|*
*uvāca paramaprītyā śrūyatāmiti sādaram||3||*

*Word for word:* iti — thus; shrutva — having heard; dvija-agryasya — of the foremost of the twice-born; vachanam — the words; shri-murarikah — Sri Murari; uvacha — said; parama-pritya — with great affection; shruyatam — listen; iti — thus; sa-adaram — respectfully.

**3.** Hearing these words of the foremost of the twice-born, Sri Murari answered with great affection and respect: "Listen!

### Verse 4

अथ ते कथयाम्यन्यत्स्वांशावतरणं हरेः।
शुद्धभक्ततया ख्यातान्भक्तानीश्वररूपिणः॥४॥

*atha te kathayāmyanyatsvāṁśāvataraṇaṁ hareḥ|*
*śuddhabhaktatayā khyātānbhaktānīśvararūpiṇaḥ||4||*

*Word for word:* atha — now; te — to you; kathayami — I tell; anyat — another thing; sva-amsha-avataranam — the descent of the own portions; hareh — of Hari; shuddha-bhaktataya — as pure devotees; khyatan — renowned; bhaktan — the devotees; ishvara-rupinah — who are forms of the Lord.

**4.** Now I shall tell you of something else — of the descent of Hari's own portions, the devotees who are forms of the Lord, though renowned as pure devotees.

### Verse 5

आदौ जातो द्विजश्रेष्ठः श्रीमाधवपुरी प्रभुः।
ईश्वरांशो द्विधा भूत्वाद्वैताचार्यश्च सद्गुणः॥५॥

*ādau jāto dvijaśreṣṭhaḥ śrīmādhavapurī prabhuḥ|*
*īśvarāṁśo dvidhā bhūtvādvaitācāryaśca sadguṇaḥ||5||*

*Word for word:* adau — first; jatah — was born; dvija-shreshthah — the best of the twice-born; shri-madhava-puri — Sri Madhava Puri; prabhuh — the master; ishvara-amshah — a portion of the Lord; dvidha — twofold; bhutva — having become; advaita-acharyah — Advaita Acharya; cha — and; sad-gunah — of good qualities.

**5.** First was born the best of the twice-born, the master Sri Madhava Puri. A portion of the Lord, dividing in two, appeared both as him and as Advaita Acharya, rich in virtues[^104-2].

### Verse 6

तयोः शिष्योऽभवद्देवश्चन्द्रांशश्चन्द्रशेखरः।
स आचार्यरत्न इति ख्यातो भुवि महायशाः॥६॥

*tayoḥ śiṣyo'bhavaddevaścandrāṁśaścandraśekharaḥ|*
*sa ācāryaratna iti khyāto bhuvi mahāyaśāḥ||6||*

*Word for word:* tayoh — of the two; shishyah — the disciple; abhavat — became; devah — the divine; chandra-amshah — a portion of Chandra (the Moon); chandrashekharah — Chandrashekhara; sah — he; acharya-ratnah — Acharyaratna; iti — thus; khyatah — renowned; bhuvi — on earth; maha-yashah — of great fame.

**6.** The disciple of them both was the divine Chandrashekhara, a portion of the Moon; of great fame, he is renowned on earth as Acharyaratna.

### Verse 7

श्रीनारदांशजातोऽसौ श्रीमच्छ्रीवासपण्डितः।
गन्धर्वांशोऽभवद्वैद्यः श्रीमुकुन्दः सुगायनः॥७॥

*śrīnāradāṁśajāto'sau śrīmacchrīvāsapaṇḍitaḥ|*

**7.** Sriman Srivasa Pandita was born as a portion of Sri Narada, and Sri Mukunda of the Vaidya (physician) caste, the fine singer, as a portion of a Gandharva.

### Verse 8

श्रीमच्छ्रीहरिदासोऽभून्मुनेरंशः शृणुष्व तत्।
कथितं नागदष्टेन ब्राह्मणेन यथा पुरा॥८॥

### Verse 9

आदौ मुनिवरः श्रीमान् रामो नाम महातपाः।

**9.** Long ago in Dravida, in a Vaishnava holy place, there lived an illustrious sage named Rama, the best of sages, a great ascetic, who loved his son dearly.

### Verse 10

**10.** His son washed some tulasi and placed it in a clean vessel, but it fell to the ground, and without washing it again

### Verse 11

पित्रेऽददात्पुनः सोऽपि श्रीरामाख्यो महामुनिः।

### Verse 12

स धर्मात्मा सुधीः शान्तः सर्वज्ञानविचक्षणः।

**12.** He is righteous, wise, peaceful and versed in all knowledge; moreover, he is a portion of Brahma[^104-4], and therefore the illustrious Haridasa is beyond doubt a true devotee.

### Verse 13

अवधूतो महातेजा नित्यानन्दो महत्तमः।
बलदेवांशतो जातो महायोगी स्वयं प्रभुः॥१३॥

*avadhūto mahātejā nityānando mahattamaḥ|*

**13.** The Avadhuta Nityananda, the greatest of all, of mighty power, was born as a portion of Baladeva; he is the great yogi, the Lord Himself.

### Verse 14

न तस्य कुलशीलानि कर्माणि वक्तुमुत्सहे।
अपि वर्षशतेनापि बृहस्पतिरपि स्वयम्॥१४॥

*na tasya kulaśīlāni karmāṇi vaktumutsahe|*

*Word for word:* na — not; tasya — his; kula-shilani — family and character; karmani — deeds; vaktum — to describe; utsahe — I venture; api — even; varsha-shatena — in a hundred years; api — even; brihaspatih — Brihaspati; api — even; svayam — himself.

**14.** I do not venture to describe his family, character and deeds: even Brihaspati himself, in a hundred years,

### Verse 15

वक्तुं नेशेऽपरे किं वा वयं हि क्षुद्रजन्तवः।
श्रीकृष्णद्वितीयश्चापि गौराङ्गप्राणवल्लभः॥१५॥

*vaktuṁ neśe'pare kiṁ vā vayaṁ hi kṣudrajantavaḥ|*

*Word for word:* vaktum — to tell; na ishe — is not able; apare — others; kim va — how much less; vayam — we; hi — indeed; kshudra-jantavah — petty creatures; shri-krishna-dvitiyah — the second (form) of Sri Krishna; cha api — and also; gauranga-prana-vallabhah — dear as life to Gauranga.

**15.** could not tell of them — what then of others, of petty creatures like us! He is the second form of Sri Krishna, dear to Gauranga as life itself.

### Verse 16

अन्ये च शतशो जाता देवाश्च मुनिपुङ्गवाः।

*anye ca śataśo jātā devāśca munipuṅgavāḥ|*

*Word for word:* anye — others; cha — and; shatashah — by hundreds; jatah — were born; devah — gods; cha — and; muni-pungavah — eminent sages; prithivyam — on earth; amsha-bhavena — as portions; tan — them; na — not; samkhyatum — to enumerate; utsahe — I venture.

**16.** And many others — hundreds of gods and eminent sages — were born on earth as portions; I do not venture to count them.

### Verse 17

अथावतारो द्विविधः पुरुषस्य प्रकीर्तितः।
युगावतारः प्रथमः कार्यार्थेऽपरसम्भवः॥१७॥

*athāvatāro dvividhaḥ puruṣasya prakīrtitaḥ|*

*Word for word:* atha — next; avatarah — the descent; dvi-vidhah — of two kinds; purushasya — of the Purusha (the Supreme Person); prakirtitah — is declared; yuga-avatarah — the avatara of the age; prathamah — the first; karya-arthe — for a (particular) task; apara-sambhavah — the appearance of the other.

**17.** Next: it is declared that the descents of the Purusha are of two kinds. The first is the avatara of the age (yuga-avatara); the other appears for a particular task.

### Verse 18

युगावताराः कथ्यन्ते ये भवन्ति युगे युगे।

*dharmaṁ saṁsthāpayanti ye tān śṛṇuṣva yathākramam||18||*

*Word for word:* yuga-avatarah — avataras of the ages; kathyante — are called; ye — those who; bhavanti — appear; yuge yuge — in age after age; dharmam — dharma; samsthapayanti — establish; ye — who; tan — of them; shrinushva — hear; yatha-kramam — in order.

**18.** Those are called avataras of the ages who appear in age after age and establish dharma. Hear of them in order.

### Verse 19

सत्ये युगे ध्यान एकः पुरुषस्यार्थसाधकः।
तदर्थेऽवतरच्छुक्लश्चतुर्बाहुर्जटाधरः॥१९॥

*satye yuge dhyāna ekaḥ puruṣasyārthasādhakaḥ|*

*Word for word:* satye yuge — in the Satya age; dhyanah — meditation; ekah — alone; purushasya — of man; artha-sadhakah — accomplishes the goal; tat-arthe — for that purpose; avatarat — descended; shuklah — the White One; chatur-bahuh — four-armed; jata-dharah — wearing matted locks.

**19.** In the Satya age meditation alone leads a man to the goal. For this purpose the White One descended, four-armed, with matted locks;

### Verse 20

सहस्रचन्द्रसदृशः सदा ध्यानरतो मुनिः।
सर्वेषामेव जन्तूनां ध्यानाचार्यो बभूव ह॥२०॥

**20.** like a thousand moons, a sage always absorbed in meditation, He became the teacher of meditation for all living beings.

### Verse 21

त्रेतायां यज्ञ एवैको धर्मः सर्वार्थसाधकः।

**21.** In the Treta age the one dharma that achieves every aim is sacrifice. Then Yajna Himself was born, with the sacrificial ladles and the other implements.

### Verse 22

**22.** Together with brahmanas who performed sacrifice, He, Janardana, the enjoyer of sacrifice, the Victorious One, Himself performed sacrifice and taught it to all the people.

### Verse 23

### Verse 24

*pūjāṁ cakāra dharmātmā lokānāṁ cānuśāsanam|*

**24.** Righteous, He Himself performed worship and instructed the people in it, and the minds of all turned to worship.

### Verse 25

### Verse 26

### Verse 27

### Verse 28

मात्स्ये तु वेदोद्धरणं कौर्मे मन्दारधारणम्।

### Verse 29

### Verse 30

### Verse 31

**31.** And in the descent of Sriman Krishna, Hari Himself, endowed with all powers, removed the burden of the earth.

### Verse 32

बौद्धे तु मोहनं चक्रे वेदानां भगवान्परः।

**32.** As the Buddha the Supreme Bhagavan bewildered (people) regarding the Vedas, and in the form of Kalki He destroyed the mlechchhas.

### Verse 33

एवंविधान्यनेकानि कर्माणि बहुरूपिणः।
कार्यावतारा नृहरेः कथिताः परमर्षिभिः॥३३॥

*evaṁvidhānyanekāni karmāṇi bahurūpiṇaḥ|*
*kāryāvatārā nṛhareḥ kathitāḥ paramarṣibhiḥ||33||*

*Word for word:* evam-vidhani — of this kind; anekani — many; karmani — deeds; bahu-rupinah — of the many-formed; karya-avatarah — the avataras for a task; nrihareh — of Nrihari; kathitah — are described; parama-rshibhih — by the great rishis.

**33.** Such are the many deeds of the many-formed Nrihari; these avataras who descend for a particular task have been described by the great rishis."

*Thus ends the fourth sarga, "On the Descents" (Avataranukarana), of the first prakrama of the great poem "Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charitamrita."*

[^104-1]: Nrihari ("Man-Hari") is usually a name of Nrisimha; here, as in v. 33, it names the Lord in general.
[^104-2]: The sense is that a single portion of the Lord appeared in two ways — as Madhavendra Puri and as Advaita Acharya.
[^104-3]: The brahmana's account itself is not given in the poem; Murari relates only its substance (vv. 9–11). Possibly "Bitten by a Snake" (Nagadashta) is the brahmana's name.
[^104-4]: According to tradition, Haridasa is a portion of Brahma, who appeared as a devotee to atone for an offence.
[^104-5]: The avatara of the Dvapara age is usually named otherwise; here Murari Gupta names Prithu, the king who first established the order of worship.
[^104-6]: Bhargava is Parashurama, the descendant of Bhrigu.

## Prakrama 1, Sarga 5. The Appearance of Sri Chaitanya

*Shachi bears in her womb a portion of Hari; the hymn of the gods to Shachi; the Lord's birth on the full-moon night of Phalguna during a lunar eclipse; the prediction of Nilambara Chakravarti; the rites after the birth*

### Verse 1

शृणुष्वावहितं ब्रह्मन्चैतन्यस्यावतारकम्।
नवीनं जगदीशस्य करुणावारिधेर्विभोः॥१॥

*śṛṇuṣvāvahitaṁ brahmancaitanyasyāvatārakam|*
*navīnaṁ jagadīśasya karuṇāvāridhervibhoḥ||1||*

*Word for word:* shrinushva — hear; avahitam — attentively; brahman — O brahmana; chaitanyasya — of Chaitanya; avatarakam — (the account of) the descent; navinam — new; jagad-ishasya — of the Lord of the world; karuna-varidheh — the ocean of compassion; vibhoh — the all-powerful.

**1.** "Hear attentively, O brahmana, the new story of the descent of Chaitanya — the all-powerful Lord of the world, the ocean of compassion.

### Verse 2

गते देवर्षिवर्ये तु स्वाश्रमे भगवान्परः।
जगन्नाथस्य विप्रर्षेर्मनस्याविशदच्युतः॥२॥

*gate devarṣivarye tu svāśrame bhagavānparaḥ|*
*jagannāthasya viprarṣermanasyāviśadacyutaḥ||2||*

*Word for word:* gate — when had gone; devarshi-varye — the best of divine sages (Narada); tu — and; sva-ashrame — to his own hermitage; bhagavan — Bhagavan; parah — the Supreme; jagannathasya — of Jagannatha; vipra-rsheh — the brahmana sage; manasi — into the mind; avishat — entered; achyutah — Achyuta (the Infallible).

**2.** When the best of the divine sages[^105-1] had gone to his own hermitage, the Supreme Bhagavan Achyuta entered the mind of Jagannatha, the brahmana sage.

### Verse 3

तेनाहितं महत्तेजो दधार समये सती।
एतस्मिन्नन्तरे साध्वी शची पतिपरायणा॥३॥

*tenāhitaṁ mahattejo dadhāra samaye satī|*
*etasminnantare sādhvī śacī patiparāyaṇā||3||*

*Word for word:* tena — by him (Jagannatha); ahitam — placed; mahat — great; tejah — splendour; dadhara — received; samaye — in due time; sati — the chaste one; etasmin antare — meanwhile; sadhvi — the virtuous; shachi — Shachi; pati-parayana — devoted to her husband.

**3.** In due time the chaste wife received the great splendour he had placed in her. Thus the virtuous Shachi, devoted to her husband,

### Verse 4

लेभे गर्भं हरेरंशं गङ्गेव शाम्भवं शुभा।
तस्यास्तेजोऽतिववृधे शुक्लपक्षे यथा शशी॥४॥

*lebhe garbhaṁ hareraṁśaṁ gaṅgeva śāmbhavaṁ śubhā|*
*tasyāstejo'tivavṛdhe śuklapakṣe yathā śaśī||4||*

*Word for word:* lebhe — conceived; garbham — an embryo; hareh — of Hari; amsham — a portion; ganga — the Ganga; iva — like; shambhavam — (the seed) of Shambhu; shubha — the auspicious one; tasyah — her; tejah — splendour; ati-vavridhe — greatly increased; shukla-pakshe — in the bright fortnight; yatha — as; shashi — the moon.

**4.** auspicious, conceived a child — a portion of Hari — as the Ganga once received the seed of Shambhu[^105-2]; and her radiance waxed like the moon in the bright fortnight.

### Verse 5

तां दृष्ट्वा रूपसम्पन्नां तप्तचामीकरप्रभाम्।
श्रिया युक्तो जगन्नाथो मुमुदे हृष्टमानसः॥५॥

*tāṁ dṛṣṭvā rūpasampannāṁ taptacāmīkaraprabhām|*
*śriyā yukto jagannātho mumude hṛṣṭamānasaḥ||5||*

*Word for word:* tam — her; drishtva — seeing; rupa-sampannam — endowed with beauty; tapta-chamikara-prabham — shining like molten gold; shriya — with splendour (good fortune); yuktah — endowed; jagannathah — Jagannatha; mumude — rejoiced; hrishta-manasah — with a delighted heart.

**5.** Seeing her beauty and her glow like molten gold, Jagannatha, graced with good fortune, rejoiced with all his heart.

### Verse 6

अथ तां तादृशीं दृष्ट्वा देवा ब्रह्मादयोऽपरे।
गन्धर्वा अमरा ये च ये च सेन्द्रा नभोगताः॥६॥

*atha tāṁ tādṛśīṁ dṛṣṭvā devā brahmādayo'pare|*
*gandharvā amarā ye ca ye ca sendrā nabhogatāḥ||6||*

*Word for word:* atha — then; tam — her; tadrishim — such; drishtva — seeing; devah — the gods; brahma-adayah — headed by Brahma; apare — others; gandharvah — the Gandharvas; amarah — the immortals; ye cha — and those who; ye cha — and those who; sa-indrah — together with Indra; nabhah-gatah — dwelling in the sky.

**6.** Then, seeing her so, Brahma and the other gods, the Gandharvas, the immortals and the dwellers of the sky together with Indra

### Verse 7

कृताञ्जलिपुटा हर्षात्साश्रुकण्ठविलोचनाः।
तुष्टुवुर्मुदिताः सर्वे प्रणामानतकन्धराः॥७॥

*kṛtāñjalipuṭā harṣātsāśrukaṇṭhavilocanāḥ|*
*tuṣṭuvurmuditāḥ sarve praṇāmānatakandharāḥ||7||*

*Word for word:* krita-anjali-putah — with joined palms; harshat — out of joy; sa-ashru-kantha-vilochanah — with tears in their throats and eyes; tushtuvuh — praised; muditah — joyful; sarve — all; pranama-anata-kandharah — with necks bent in obeisance.

**7.** joined their palms in joy; with tears in their voices and eyes, their heads bowed in obeisance, they all joyfully sang her praise:

### Verse 8

नमामि त्वां सदागर्भामदितिं जननीं हरेः।
चन्द्रार्काग्निप्रभागर्भां सत्त्वगर्भां धृतिं क्षमाम्॥८॥

*namāmi tvāṁ sadāgarbhāmaditiṁ jananīṁ hareḥ|*
*candrārkāgniprabhāgarbhāṁ sattvagarbhāṁ dhṛtiṁ kṣamām||8||*

*Word for word:* namami — I bow; tvam — to you; sada-garbham — ever bearing (Him) in the womb; aditim — Aditi; jananim — the mother; hareh — of Hari; chandra-arka-agni-prabha-garbham — bearing in the womb the radiance of moon, sun and fire; sattva-garbham — bearing pure being (sattva) in the womb; dhritim — steadfastness; kshamam — forbearance (the earth).

**8.** 'We bow to you who ever bear Him in your womb! You are Aditi, the mother of Hari; you bear in your womb the radiance of moon, sun and fire, you bear pure being in your womb; you are steadfastness and forbearance;

### Verse 9

अद्वेषगर्भां संसिद्धिं वेदगर्भां स्वयं हरेः।
देवकीं रोहिणीं चैव यशोदां सर्वथाभवाम्॥९॥

*adveṣagarbhāṁ saṁsiddhiṁ vedagarbhāṁ svayaṁ hareḥ|*
*devakīṁ rohiṇīṁ caiva yaśodāṁ sarvathābhavām||9||*

*Word for word:* advesha-garbham — bearing freedom from malice in the womb; samsiddhim — perfection; veda-garbham — bearing the Veda in the womb; svayam — himself; hareh — of Hari; devakim — Devaki; rohinim — Rohini; cha eva — and also; yashodam — Yashoda; sarvatha-bhavam — the source (of being) in every way.

**9.** you bear freedom from malice in your womb; you are perfection; you bear in your womb the Veda — Hari Himself. You are Devaki, Rohini and Yashoda — in every guise the source of being[^105-3].

### Verse 10

तं वै बिभर्षि गर्भे त्वं यो यज्ञं प्रथयिष्यति।
कीर्तनाख्यं महापुण्यं यद्यज्ञैर्नोपपद्यते॥१०॥

*taṁ vai bibharṣi garbhe tvaṁ yo yajñaṁ prathayiṣyati|*
*kīrtanākhyaṁ mahāpuṇyaṁ yadyajñairnopapadyate||10||*

*Word for word:* tam — him; vai — indeed; bibharshi — you bear; garbhe — in the womb; tvam — you; yah — who; yajnam — the sacrifice; prathayishyati — will spread; kirtana-akhyam — called kirtana; maha-punyam — supremely holy; yat — which; yajnaih — by (ordinary) sacrifices; na upapadyate — is not attained.

**10.** You bear in your womb Him who will spread the holiest of sacrifices — kirtana, whose fruit no (ordinary) sacrifice can attain.

### Verse 11

कीर्तनं नृहरेः श्रुत्वा निमिषार्धेन या भवेत्।
प्रीतिरस्मादृशां सा तु कोटियज्ञैर्भवेन्न हि॥११॥

*kīrtanaṁ nṛhareḥ śrutvā nimiṣārdhena yā bhavet|*
*prītirasmādṛśāṁ sā tu koṭiyajñairbhavenna hi||11||*

*Word for word:* kirtanam — the glorification; nrihareh — of Nrihari; shrutva — hearing; nimisha-ardhena — in half a moment; ya — which; bhavet — arises; pritih — joy; asmadrisham — for such as us; sa — that; tu — indeed; koti-yajnaih — by millions of sacrifices; bhavet — would arise; na hi — not at all.

**11.** Not even millions of sacrifices could give the joy that arises in such as us from half a moment of hearing the kirtana of Nrihari.

### Verse 12

अहो महत्पुरा दत्तममृतं हरिणा स्वयम्।
समुद्रमन्थनं कृत्वा ततः कोटिगुणाधिकम्॥१२॥

*aho mahatpurā dattamamṛtaṁ hariṇā svayam|*
*samudramanthanaṁ kṛtvā tataḥ koṭiguṇādhikam||12||*

*Word for word:* aho — oh; mahat — great; pura — once; dattam — given; amritam — the nectar of immortality; harina — by Hari; svayam — himself; samudra-manthanam — the churning of the ocean; kritva — having performed; tatah — than that; koti-guna-adhikam — millions of times greater.

**12.** Oh! Great was the nectar of immortality that Hari Himself once gave after churning the ocean[^105-4] — yet a sweetness a million times greater

### Verse 13

रसं पश्याम एवात्र शृण्वन्तः श्रीहरेर्यशः।
मोक्षमप्यनृतं चेतो मन्यते कीर्तनाद्धरेः॥१३॥

*rasaṁ paśyāma evātra śṛṇvantaḥ śrīhareryaśaḥ|*
*mokṣamapyanṛtaṁ ceto manyate kīrtanāddhareḥ||13||*

*Word for word:* rasam — a sweetness (rasa); pashyamah — we behold (taste); eva — indeed; atra — here; shrinvantah — hearing; shri-hareh — of Sri Hari; yashah — the glory; moksham — liberation; api — even; anritam — false (worthless); chetah — the mind; manyate — considers; kirtanat — because of the kirtana; hareh — of Hari.

**13.** we taste here, hearing the glory of Sri Hari: for the sake of the kirtana of Hari the mind counts even liberation as nothing.'

### Verse 14

एवमुक्त्वा ततो देवाः सेन्द्रा जग्मुः प्रणम्य ताम्।
ब्रह्माणमग्रतः कृत्वा गायन्तः श्रीहरेर्यशः॥१४॥

*evamuktvā tato devāḥ sendrā jagmuḥ praṇamya tām|*
*brahmāṇamagrataḥ kṛtvā gāyantaḥ śrīhareryaśaḥ||14||*

*Word for word:* evam — thus; uktva — having spoken; tatah — then; devah — the gods; sa-indrah — with Indra; jagmuh — went away; pranamya — bowing; tam — to her; brahmanam — Brahma; agratah — in front; kritva — placing; gayantah — singing; shri-hareh — of Sri Hari; yashah — the glory.

**14.** Having spoken thus, the gods with Indra bowed to her and, placing Brahma at their head, departed, singing the glory of Sri Hari,

### Verse 15

स्वां पुरीं श्रीपतेरंशो जातो भुव्यतिहर्षिताः।
कलेर्भाग्यं प्रशंसन्तो नृत्यन्तः प्रेमविह्वलाः॥१५॥

*svāṁ purīṁ śrīpateraṁśo jāto bhuvyatiharṣitāḥ|*
*kalerbhāgyaṁ praśaṁsanto nṛtyantaḥ premavihvalāḥ||15||*

*Word for word:* svam — to their own; purim — city; shri-pateh — of the Lord of Shri; amshah — a portion; jatah — is born; bhuvi — on earth; ati-harshitah — (saying this) overjoyed; kaleh — of Kali; bhagyam — the good fortune; prashamsantah — praising; nrityantah — dancing; prema-vihvalah — overwhelmed with love.

**15.** to their own city. Overjoyed that a portion of the Lord of Shri had been born on earth, they praised the good fortune of (the age of) Kali and danced, overwhelmed with love.

### Verse 16

ततः पूर्णे निशानाथे निशीथे फाल्गुने शुभे।
काले सर्वगुणोत्कर्षे शुद्धगन्धवहान्विते॥१६॥

*tataḥ pūrṇe niśānāthe niśīthe phālgune śubhe|*
*kāle sarvaguṇotkarṣe śuddhagandhavahānvite||16||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; purne — when was full; nisha-nathe — the lord of the night (the moon); nishithe — at midnight; phalgune — in (the month of) Phalguna; shubhe — auspicious; kale — at a time; sarva-guna-utkarshe — excellent in every quality; shuddha-gandha-vaha-anvite — filled with a pure breeze.

**16.** And then at midnight, in the auspicious month of Phalguna, when the moon, the lord of the night, was full, at an hour rich in every excellence and fanned by a pure breeze,

### Verse 17

मनःसु देवसाधूनां प्रसन्नेषु च शीतले।
स्वर्नद्याः शुद्धसलिले जाते जातः स्वयं हरिः॥१७॥

*manaḥsu devasādhūnāṁ prasanneṣu ca śītale|*
*svarnadyāḥ śuddhasalile jāte jātaḥ svayaṁ hariḥ||17||*

*Word for word:* manahsu — when the minds; deva-sadhunam — of gods and saints; prasanneshu — were serene; cha — and; shitale — cool; svar-nadyah — of the heavenly river (Ganga); shuddha-salile — when the water had become pure; jate — had become; jatah — was born; svayam — himself; harih — Hari.

**17.** when the minds of the gods and the saints were serene and the waters of the heavenly river cool and pure — Hari Himself was born.

### Verse 18

तं विकाशिकमलेक्षणं लसत्पूर्णचन्द्रवदनं कनकाभम्।
तेजसा वितिमिरा दिशः स्वयं कारयन्तमुपलभ्य सुतं सः॥१८॥

*taṁ vikāśikamalekṣaṇaṁ lasatpūrṇacandravadanaṁ kanakābham|*
*tejasā vitimirā diśaḥ svayaṁ kārayantamupalabhya sutaṁ saḥ||18||*

*Word for word:* tam — him; vikashi-kamala-ikshanam — with eyes like blossoming lotuses; lasat-purna-chandra-vadanam — with a face like the shining full moon; kanaka-abham — golden; tejasa — by his splendour; vitimirah — free of darkness; dishah — the quarters; svayam — himself; karayantam — making; upalabhya — having obtained; sutam — the son; sah — he.

**18.** The son he had obtained was golden; His eyes were like blossoming lotuses, His face like the shining full moon, and by His own splendour He drove the darkness from every quarter.

### Verse 19

प्रीतिसागररसस्य न पारं प्राप पद्मनिधिना यथाऽधनः।
श्रीजगन्नाथमिश्रपुरन्दरः प्रेमगद्गदमुखं सदा दधे॥१९॥

*prītisāgararasasya na pāraṁ prāpa padmanidhinā yathā'dhanaḥ|*
*śrījagannāthamiśrapurandaraḥ premagadgadamukhaṁ sadā dadhe||19||*

*Word for word:* priti-sagara-rasasya — of the waters of the ocean of joy; na — not; param — the further shore; prapa — reached; padma-nidhina — (on gaining) the padma treasure; yatha — as; adhanah — a poor man; shri-jagannatha-mishra-purandarah — Sri Jagannatha Mishra Purandara; prema-gadgada-mukham — a mouth faltering with love; sada — constantly; dadhe — had.

**19.** And Sri Jagannatha Mishra Purandara, like a poor man who has found the padma treasure[^105-5], could not reach the further shore of his ocean of joy, and his voice kept faltering with love.

### Verse 20

तस्य जन्मसमयेऽनु शशाङ्कं राहुरग्रसदलं त्रपयेव।
कृष्णपद्मवदनेन निर्जितः प्राविशत्सुररिपोर्मुखं विधुः॥२०॥

*tasya janmasamaye'nu śaśāṅkaṁ rāhuragrasadalaṁ trapayeva|*
*kṛṣṇapadmavadanena nirjitaḥ prāviśatsuraripormukhaṁ vidhuḥ||20||*

*Word for word:* tasya — his; janma-samaye — at the time of birth; anu — then; shashankam — the moon; rahuh — Rahu; agrasat — swallowed; alam — completely; trapaya — out of shame; iva — as if; krishna-padma-vadanena — by the lotus face of Krishna; nirjitah — defeated; pravishat — entered; sura-ripoh — of the enemy of the gods; mukham — the mouth; vidhuh — the moon.

**20.** At the hour of His birth Rahu swallowed the moon entirely: defeated by the lotus face of Krishna, the moon, as if out of shame, hid in the mouth of the enemy of the gods[^105-6].

### Verse 21

तत्र पुण्यसमये मनुजानां कीर्तनं नरहरेः कृतं जनैः।
पूजनं सपदि जाह्नवीजले स्नानदानमघमार्जनं शुचौ॥२१॥

*tatra puṇyasamaye manujānāṁ kīrtanaṁ narahareḥ kṛtaṁ janaiḥ|*
*pūjanaṁ sapadi jāhnavījale snānadānamaghamārjanaṁ śucau||21||*

*Word for word:* tatra — at that; punya-samaye — holy time; manujanam — by men; kirtanam — the kirtana; narahareh — of Narahari; kritam — was performed; janaih — by the people; pujanam — worship; sapadi — at once; jahnavi-jale — in the waters of the Jahnavi; snana-danam — bathing and giving of gifts; agha-marjanam — cleansing from sin; shuchau — in purity.

**21.** At that holy hour the people sang the kirtana of Narahari and offered worship, and then, keeping themselves pure, they bathed in the waters of the Jahnavi, gave gifts and performed the rite of cleansing from sin.

### Verse 22

जहृषुः सुरगणाः समहेन्द्राः पद्मसम्भवमहेशपुरोगाः।
अप्सरोभिरतिनृत्यपराभिर्नायकाश्च सुमनांसि ववर्षुः॥२२॥

*jahṛṣuḥ suragaṇāḥ samahendrāḥ padmasambhavamaheśapurogāḥ|*
*apsarobhiratinṛtyaparābhirnāyakāśca sumanāṁsi vavarṣuḥ||22||*

*Word for word:* jahrishuh — rejoiced; sura-ganah — the hosts of gods; sa-mahendrah — with Mahendra; padma-sambhava-mahesha-purogah — led by the Lotus-born (Brahma) and Mahesha; apsarobhih — with the apsaras; ati-nritya-parabhih — absorbed in dancing; nayakah — the leaders (of the Gandharvas); cha — and; sumanamsi — flowers; vavarshuh — rained down.

**22.** The hosts of gods with Mahendra, led by the Lotus-born and Mahesha, rejoiced; the apsaras gave themselves to dancing, and the leaders (of the Gandharvas) rained down flowers.

### Verse 23

नीलाम्बरचक्रवर्ती जन्मना तस्य हर्षितः।
आजगामाश्रमं तूर्णं जामातुः सर्वशास्त्रवित्॥२३॥

*nīlāmbaracakravartī janmanā tasya harṣitaḥ|*
*ājagāmāśramaṁ tūrṇaṁ jāmātuḥ sarvaśāstravit||23||*

*Word for word:* nilambara-chakravarti — Nilambara Chakravarti; janmana — by the birth; tasya — of him; harshitah — delighted; ajagama — came; ashramam — to the home; turnam — quickly; jamatuh — of his son-in-law; sarva-shastra-vit — knower of all scriptures.

**23.** Delighted by His birth, Nilambara Chakravarti, the knower of all scriptures, hurried to the home of his son-in-law.

### Verse 24

जगन्नाथं समाहूय शचीं सम्बोधयन्सुधीः।
दौहित्रजन्मकालज्ञ इदं वचनमब्रवीत्॥२४॥

*jagannāthaṁ samāhūya śacīṁ sambodhayansudhīḥ|*
*dauhitrajanmakālajña idaṁ vacanamabravīt||24||*

*Word for word:* jagannatham — Jagannatha; samahuya — calling; shachim — Shachi; sambodhayan — addressing; sudhih — the wise man; dauhitra-janma-kala-jnah — knowing the time of his grandson's birth; idam — these; vachanam — words; abravit — spoke.

**24.** The wise man, who knew (from the stars) the hour of his grandson's birth, called Jagannatha and, addressing Shachi, spoke these words:

### Verse 25

अये पुरुषसिंहोऽयं जातः प्रोच्चे बृहस्पतौ।
असौ सर्वस्य लोकस्य पाता नित्यं भविष्यति॥२५॥

*aye puruṣasiṁho'yaṁ jātaḥ procce bṛhaspatau|*
*asau sarvasya lokasya pātā nityaṁ bhaviṣyati||25||*

*Word for word:* aye — oh; purusha-simhah — a lion among men; ayam — this; jatah — is born; procche — when in exaltation; brihaspatau — Brihaspati (Jupiter); asau — he; sarvasya — of all; lokasya — the world; pata — the protector; nityam — always; bhavishyati — will be.

**25.** 'Oh! This lion among men is born when Brihaspati (Jupiter) stands in exaltation[^105-7]. He will be the eternal protector of the whole world;

### Verse 26

सुशीलः सर्वधर्माणामाश्रयो न्यासिनां वरः।
प्रीतिदः सर्वभूतानां पूर्णामृतकरो यथा॥२६॥

*suśīlaḥ sarvadharmāṇāmāśrayo nyāsināṁ varaḥ|*
*prītidaḥ sarvabhūtānāṁ pūrṇāmṛtakaro yathā||26||*

*Word for word:* sushilah — of good character; sarva-dharmanam — of all dharmas; ashrayah — the refuge; nyasinam — of renunciants (sannyasis); varah — the best; priti-dah — giving joy; sarva-bhutanam — to all beings; purna-amrita-karah — the full moon (whose rays are nectar); yatha — like.

**26.** of noble character, the refuge of all dharmas, the best of sannyasis, He will gladden all beings like the full moon pouring nectar.

### Verse 27

समुद्धर्ता सदैवायं पितृमातृकुलद्वयम्।
एवमुक्ते द्विजे तस्मिन्सर्वे प्रमुदिता जनाः॥२७॥

*samuddhartā sadaivāyaṁ pitṛmātṛkuladvayam|*
*evamukte dvije tasminsarve pramuditā janāḥ||27||*

*Word for word:* samuddharta — the deliverer; sada — always; eva — indeed; ayam — he; pitri-matri-kula-dvayam — of both lineages, father's and mother's; evam — thus; ukte — when had spoken; dvije — the brahmana; tasmin — that; sarve — all; pramuditah — rejoiced; janah — the people.

**27.** He will forever deliver both lineages — his father's and his mother's.' When the brahmana had spoken thus, all the people rejoiced.

### Verse 28

माता हर्षमतीवाप श्रुत्वा तत्पितृभाषितम्।
वात्स्यश्चकार पुत्रस्य जातकर्ममहोत्सवम्॥२८॥

*mātā harṣamatīvāpa śrutvā tatpitṛbhāṣitam|*
*vātsyaścakāra putrasya jātakarmamahotsavam||28||*

*Word for word:* mata — the mother; harsham — joy; ativa — great; apa — obtained; shrutva — hearing; tat — that; pitri-bhashitam — speech of her father; vatsyah — the Vatsya (Jagannatha Mishra); chakara — performed; putrasya — of the son; jatakarma-maha-utsavam — the great festival of the birth rite (jatakarma).

**28.** Hearing these words of her father, the mother was filled with great joy, and the Vatsya[^105-8] held a great festival — the jatakarma, the birth rite of his son.

### Verse 29

ताम्बूलं चन्दनं माल्यं गन्धं प्रादाद्द्विजातये।
क्रमेणोत्थानकर्मादिमङ्गलानि चकार सः॥२९॥

*tāmbūlaṁ candanaṁ mālyaṁ gandhaṁ prādāddvijātaye|*
*krameṇotthānakarmādimaṅgalāni cakāra saḥ||29||*

*Word for word:* tambulam — betel; chandanam — sandal paste; malyam — garlands; gandham — fragrances; pradat — gave; dvijataye — to the twice-born; kramena — in due order; utthana-karma-adi-mangalani — the auspicious rites beginning with the rite of rising (after childbirth); chakara — performed; sah — he.

**29.** He gave the brahmanas betel, sandal paste, garlands and fragrances, and in due order performed the auspicious rites beginning with the utthana, the rite of "rising"[^105-9]."

*Thus ends the fifth sarga, "The Appearance of Sri Chaitanya" (Sri Chaitanya-avirbhava), of the great poem "Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charitamrita."*

[^105-1]: Narada, at whose entreaty the Lord agreed to descend (Sarga 1.03).
[^105-2]: An allusion to the birth of Skanda: the seed of Shiva (Shambhu) was received by the Ganga.
[^105-3]: The reading of the last word of the verse is uncertain; the translation is tentative.
[^105-4]: The churning of the Milk Ocean by the gods and asuras, from which the nectar of immortality (amrita) was obtained.
[^105-5]: Padma is one of the nine treasures (nidhi) of Kubera.
[^105-6]: The Lord's birth coincided with a lunar eclipse; Rahu is the demon, "the enemy of the gods," who swallows the moon.
[^105-7]: The "exalted position" (uccha) of a planet in astrology is the sign of its greatest strength.
[^105-8]: The Vatsya is Jagannatha Mishra, of the Vatsya clan.
[^105-9]: Probably the rite marking the end of the lying-in period; another understanding is possible — the child's first standing up.

## Prakrama 1, Sarga 6. The Description of the Pastimes from Birth among the Childhood Plays

*Vishvambhara's infancy: the naming, His form, childhood plays; the wandering brahmana; the broken pots; the teaching on pure and impure; the brick thrown at His mother; the two coconuts; the sound of ankle bells*

### Verse 1

ततः कालेन कियता जानुचंक्रमणं शिशोः।
दृष्ट्वा प्रहर्षमाप्तौ तौ दम्पती कलभाषिणः॥१॥

*tataḥ kālena kiyatā jānucaṁkramaṇaṁ śiśoḥ|*
*dṛṣṭvā praharṣamāptau tau dampatī kalabhāṣiṇaḥ||1||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; kalena — after a time; kiyata — some; janu-chankramanam — the crawling on knees; shishoh — of the infant; drishtva — seeing; praharsham — great joy; aptau — obtained; tau — the two; dampati — husband and wife; kala-bhashinah — sweetly babbling.

**1.** Some time passed, and the husband and wife, watching their sweetly babbling infant crawl about on His knees, were filled with great joy.

### Verse 2

शोणपद्माभवदने द्विजराजस्य रश्मयः।
सुस्मिते भान्ति साधूनां मनोध्वान्तापहारिणः॥२॥

*śoṇapadmābhavadane dvijarājasya raśmayaḥ|*
*susmite bhānti sādhūnāṁ manodhvāntāpahāriṇaḥ||2||*

*Word for word:* shona-padma-abha-vadane — on the face like a red lotus; dvija-rajasya — of the king of the twice-born (the moon; also — of the teeth); rashmayah — the rays; su-smite — smiling; bhanti — shine; sadhunam — of the saints; manah-dhvanta-apaharinah — dispelling the darkness of the mind.

**2.** On His smiling face, like a red lotus, shone the rays of "the king of the twice-born"[^106-1], dispelling the darkness in the minds of the saints.

### Verse 3

पुरा बिभर्त्यसौ विश्वमिति चक्रे पिता स्वयम्।
श्रीमद्विश्वम्भर इति नाम तस्य सुशोभनम्॥३॥

*purā bibhartyasau viśvamiti cakre pitā svayam|*
*śrīmadviśvambhara iti nāma tasya suśobhanam||3||*

*Word for word:* pura — of old (and ever); bibharti — sustains; asau — he; vishvam — the universe; iti — thus (thinking); chakre — gave; pita — the father; svayam — himself; shrimad-vishvambharah — Sriman Vishvambhara; iti — thus; nama — the name; tasya — to him; su-shobhanam — most beautiful.

**3.** "He has sustained the universe from of old," thought the father, and he himself gave Him the most beautiful name Sriman Vishvambhara[^106-2].

### Verse 4

तप्तकाञ्चनगौराङ्गो लसत्पद्मायतेक्षणः।
प्रभञ्जनाम्बरो रौप्यहारी मालालको हरिः॥४॥

*taptakāñcanagaurāṅgo lasatpadmāyatekṣaṇaḥ|*
*prabhañjanāmbaro raupyahārī mālālako hariḥ||4||*

*Word for word:* tapta-kanchana-gaura-angah — with a body golden like molten gold; lasat-padma-ayata-ikshanah — with eyes wide like shining lotuses; prabhanjana-ambarah — clad in the wind (naked); raupya-hari — wearing a silver necklace; mala-alakah — with a garland in his curls; harih — Hari.

**4.** Hari's body gleamed like molten gold, His eyes were wide as shining lotuses; clad only in the wind, He wore a silver necklace and a garland in His curls.

### Verse 5

राकासुधाकरमुखः कलवागमृतान्वितः।
मधुराकृतिरामुक्तकङ्कणाङ्गदभूषणः॥५॥

*rākāsudhākaramukhaḥ kalavāgamṛtānvitaḥ|*
*madhurākṛtirāmuktakaṅkaṇāṅgadabhūṣaṇaḥ||5||*

*Word for word:* raka-sudhakara-mukhah — with a face like the full moon; kala-vag-amrita-anvitah — endowed with the nectar of sweet babble; madhura-akritih — charming in form; amukta-kankana-angada-bhushanah — adorned with bracelets and armlets put on him.

**5.** His face was like the full moon, His babble sweet as nectar; bracelets on His wrists and armlets on His arms adorned the charming child.

### Verse 6

भङ्गहिङ्गुलरक्ताब्जकरपादतलः शुचिः।
ववृधे कलया नित्यं शुक्लपक्ष इव द्युराट्॥६॥

*bhaṅgahiṅgularaktābjakarapādatalaḥ śuciḥ|*
*vavṛdhe kalayā nityaṁ śuklapakṣa iva dyurāṭ||6||*

*Word for word:* bhanga-hingula-rakta-abja-kara-pada-talah — with palms and soles red as a lotus or ground vermilion; shuchih — pure; vavridhe — grew; kalaya — digit by digit; nityam — constantly; shukla-pakshe — in the bright fortnight; iva — like; dyurat — the king of the sky (the moon).

**6.** Pure, His palms and soles red as a lotus or ground vermilion, He grew day by day, waxing digit by digit like the moon in the bright fortnight.

### Verse 7

ततः कालेन शोणाभ्यां पादाभ्याममितद्युतिः।
अटन्विरहजं तापं मेदिन्याः संजहार सः॥७॥

*tataḥ kālena śoṇābhyāṁ pādābhyāmamitadyutiḥ|*
*aṭanvirahajaṁ tāpaṁ medinyāḥ saṁjahāra saḥ||7||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; kalena — in time; shonabhyam — with red; padabhyam — feet; amita-dyutih — of boundless radiance; atan — walking; viraha-jam — born of separation; tapam — the burning; medinyah — of the earth; samjahara — removed; sah — he.

**7.** In time the infant of boundless radiance began to walk, and treading with His red feet He soothed the earth's burning, born of separation (from Him)[^106-3].

### Verse 8

तीर्थभ्रमणशीलस्य द्विजस्यान्नं जनार्दनः।
भुक्त्वा तं स्मारयामास नन्दगेहकुतूहलम्॥८॥

*tīrthabhramaṇaśīlasya dvijasyānnaṁ janārdanaḥ|*
*bhuktvā taṁ smārayāmāsa nandagehakutūhalam||8||*

*Word for word:* tirtha-bhramana-shilasya — given to wandering among holy places; dvijasya — of the brahmana; annam — the food; janardanah — Janardana; bhuktva — having eaten; tam — him; smarayamasa — reminded of; nanda-geha-kutuhalam — the wonder in the house of Nanda.

**8.** Janardana ate the food of a brahmana who wandered among the holy places, and so reminded him of the wonder in the house of Nanda[^106-4].

### Verse 9

वयस्यैर्बालकैः सार्धं विहरंस्तरुपल्लवैः।
आहताः शिशवः सर्वे विचक्रुः पुरतो मुदा॥९॥

*vayasyairbālakaiḥ sārdhaṁ viharaṁstarupallavaiḥ|*
*āhatāḥ śiśavaḥ sarve vicakruḥ purato mudā||9||*

*Word for word:* vayasyaih — with companions; balakaih — boys; sardham — together; viharan — playing; taru-pallavaih — with shoots of trees; ahatah — struck (with them); shishavah — the children; sarve — all; vichakruh — romped; puratah — before (him); muda — joyfully.

**9.** He played with the boys of His age, (swinging) shoots of trees, and the children, struck by them, all romped joyfully before Him.

### Verse 10

भुवि तिष्ठन्पदैकेन जानुनान्यस्य जानुकम्।
पस्पर्श मर्कटीं लीलां कुर्वन्मायार्भको हरिः॥१०॥

*bhuvi tiṣṭhanpadaikena jānunānyasya jānukam|*
*pasparśa markaṭīṁ līlāṁ kurvanmāyārbhako hariḥ||10||*

*Word for word:* bhuvi — on the ground; tishthan — standing; pada-ekena — on one foot; januna — with the knee; anyasya — of the other; janukam — the knee; pasparsha — touched; markatim — monkey; lilam — play; kurvan — performing; maya-arbhakah — a child by his maya (appearing as a child); harih — Hari.

**10.** Hari, a child by His own maya, played like a monkey: standing on one foot, He would touch another boy's knee with His knee.

### Verse 11

एकदा धर्तुमात्मानमुद्यतां जननीं रुषा।
वीक्ष्य कोपपरिपूर्णो भाजनानि बभञ्ज सः॥११॥

*ekadā dhartumātmānamudyatāṁ jananīṁ ruṣā|*
*vīkṣya kopaparipūrṇo bhājanāni babhañja saḥ||11||*

*Word for word:* ekada — once; dhartum — to seize; atmanam — him; udyatam — ready; jananim — the mother; rusha — in anger; vikshya — seeing; kopa-paripurnah — full of anger; bhajanani — the pots; babhanja — broke; sah — he.

**11.** Once, seeing His mother angrily about to seize Him, He grew angry Himself and smashed the pots.

### Verse 12

पुरा भग्ने च भाण्डे यं यशोदा पशुरज्जुभिः।
बबन्ध वेपिता तस्य भयाद्वीक्ष्य मुखं शची॥१२॥

*purā bhagne ca bhāṇḍe yaṁ yaśodā paśurajjubhiḥ|*
*babandha vepitā tasya bhayādvīkṣya mukhaṁ śacī||12||*

*Word for word:* pura — once; bhagne — when was broken; cha — and; bhande — the pot; yam — whom; yashoda — Yashoda; pashu-rajjubhih — with cattle ropes; babandha — bound; vepita — trembling; tasya — his; bhayat — from fear; vikshya — looking at; mukham — the face; shachi — Shachi.

**12.** He whom Yashoda once bound with cattle ropes for a broken pot[^106-5] — Him Shachi now feared: looking at His face, she trembled.

### Verse 13

उपर्युपरि विन्यस्तत्यक्तमृद्भाण्डसंहतौ।
उपविश्याशुचौ देशे मातुरग्रे जहास सः॥१३॥

*uparyupari vinyastatyaktamṛdbhāṇḍasaṁhatau|*
*upaviśyāśucau deśe māturagre jahāsa saḥ||13||*

*Word for word:* upari-upari — one upon another; vinyasta-tyakta-mrid-bhanda-samhatau — on a heap of discarded clay pots piled; upavishya — sitting down; ashuchau — impure; deshe — in a place; matuh — of the mother; agre — in front; jahasa — laughed; sah — he.

**13.** Then He sat down in an impure place, on a heap of discarded clay pots piled one upon another, and laughed in front of His mother.

### Verse 14

तं दृष्ट्वा सा शची प्राह त्यज तात जुगुप्सितम्।
स्थानं शुद्धं पुनः स्नात्वा ममाङ्कारोहणं कुरु॥१४॥

*taṁ dṛṣṭvā sā śacī prāha tyaja tāta jugupsitam|*
*sthānaṁ śuddhaṁ punaḥ snātvā mamāṅkārohaṇaṁ kuru||14||*

*Word for word:* tam — him; drishtva — seeing; sa — she; shachi — Shachi; praha — said; tyaja — leave; tata — child; jugupsitam — the loathsome; sthanam — place; shuddham — cleanly; punah — again; snatva — having bathed; mama — my; anka-arohanam — climbing onto the lap; kuru — do.

**14.** Seeing this, Shachi said: "Child, come away from that loathsome place! Bathe again, be clean, and climb onto my lap."

### Verse 15

एवमुक्ते तु तां प्राह भगवान्सर्वतत्त्ववित्।
दत्तात्रेयस्य भावैकपूर्णः सर्वज्ञपूरकः॥१५॥

*evamukte tu tāṁ prāha bhagavānsarvatattvavit|*
*dattātreyasya bhāvaikapūrṇaḥ sarvajñapūrakaḥ||15||*

*Word for word:* evam — thus; ukte — when was spoken; tu — and; tam — to her; praha — said; bhagavan — Bhagavan; sarva-tattva-vit — knower of all truths; dattatreyasya — of Dattatreya; bhava-eka-purnah — wholly filled with the mood; sarva-jna-purakah — completing (the knowledge) of the all-knowing.

**15.** At these words Bhagavan, the knower of all truths, wholly imbued with the mood of Dattatreya[^106-6], who completes (the knowledge) of the all-knowing, answered her:

### Verse 16

शृणु शुचिरशुचिर्वा कल्पनामात्रमेतत्क्षितिजलपवनाग्निव्योमचित्तं जगद्धि।
विततविभवपूर्वाद्वैतपादाब्ज एको हरिरिह करुणाब्धिर्भाति नान्यत्प्रतीहि॥१६॥

*śṛṇu śuciraśucirvā kalpanāmātrametatkṣitijalapavanāgnivyomacittaṁ jagaddhi|*
*vitatavibhavapūrvādvaitapādābja eko haririha karuṇābdhirbhāti nānyatpratīhi||16||*

*Word for word:* shrinu — listen; shuchih — pure; ashuchih — impure; va — or; kalpana-matram — a mere notion; etat — this; kshiti-jala-pavana-agni-vyoma-chittam — (made of) earth, water, air, fire, ether and mind; jagat — the world; hi — for; vitata-vibhava-purva-advaita-pada-abjah — whose lotus feet are non-dual and prior to all the spread-out manifestation; ekah — the one; harih — Hari; iha — here; karuna-abdhih — the ocean of compassion; bhati — shines; na — not; anyat — anything else; pratihi — know.

**16.** "Listen: pure and impure are mere notions of the mind. For the world is (only) earth, water, air, fire, ether and mind; and here shines only the one Hari, the ocean of compassion, whose lotus feet are non-dual and prior to all the outspread manifestation. Know that there is nothing else.

### Verse 17

अतः पवित्र एवास्मि नापवित्रः कथंचन।
जानीहि मातर्नान्यां त्वं शङ्कां कर्तुमिहार्हसि॥१७॥

*ataḥ pavitra evāsmi nāpavitraḥ kathaṁcana|*
*jānīhi mātarnānyāṁ tvaṁ śaṅkāṁ kartumihārhasi||17||*

*Word for word:* atah — therefore; pavitrah — pure; eva — indeed; asmi — I am; na — not; apavitrah — impure; kathamchana — in any way; janihi — know; matah — O mother; na — not; anyam — other; tvam — you; shankam — doubt; kartum — to have; iha — here; arhasi — should.

**17.** Therefore I am pure and in no way impure. Know this, mother, and harbour no other doubt here."

### Verse 18

एवमुक्ते सुते सा तं करे संगृह्य सत्वरा।
आनीय स्नापयामास स्वर्णदीस्वच्छवारिभिः॥१८॥

*evamukte sute sā taṁ kare saṁgṛhya satvarā|*
*ānīya snāpayāmāsa svarṇadīsvacchavāribhiḥ||18||*

*Word for word:* evam — thus; ukte — when had spoken; sute — the son; sa — she; tam — him; kare — by the hand; samgrihya — taking; satvara — hastily; aniya — bringing; snapayamasa — bathed; svarnadi-svachchha-varibhih — with the clear waters of the heavenly river (Ganga).

**18.** When her son had spoken thus, she hastily took Him by the hand, led Him (to the river) and bathed Him in the clear waters of the heavenly river.

### Verse 19

अथ कतिपये काले मुक्तमृद्भाण्डसंहतौ।
उपविष्टं सुतं वीक्ष्य शची वाग्भिरताडयत्॥१९॥

*atha katipaye kāle muktamṛdbhāṇḍasaṁhatau|*
*upaviṣṭaṁ sutaṁ vīkṣya śacī vāgbhiratāḍayat||19||*

*Word for word:* atha — then; katipaye — after some; kale — time; mukta-mrid-bhanda-samhatau — on a heap of thrown-away clay pots; upavishtam — seated; sutam — the son; vikshya — seeing; shachi — Shachi; vagbhih — with words; atadayat — scolded.

**19.** After some time Shachi again saw her son seated on a heap of thrown-away clay pots and scolded Him:

### Verse 20

अपवित्रे निषिद्धेऽपि स्थाने त्वं मन्दधीः कथम्।
तिष्ठसीति वचः श्रुत्वा मातुः क्रोधसमन्वितः॥२०॥

*apavitre niṣiddhe'pi sthāne tvaṁ mandadhīḥ katham|*
*tiṣṭhasīti vacaḥ śrutvā mātuḥ krodhasamanvitaḥ||20||*

*Word for word:* apavitre — impure; nishiddhe — forbidden; api — even; sthane — in a place; tvam — you; manda-dhih — foolish one; katham — why; tishthasi — stay; iti — thus; vachah — the words; shrutva — hearing; matuh — of the mother; krodha-samanvitah — filled with anger.

**20.** "Foolish one! Why do you sit in an impure, forbidden place?" Hearing these words of His mother, filled with anger,

### Verse 21

श्रीमद्विश्वम्भरः प्राह मूढे नास्त्यशुचिः क्वचित्।
उक्तं मयैतत्पूर्वं ते तत्किं मां त्वं विगर्हसि॥२१॥

*śrīmadviśvambharaḥ prāha mūḍhe nāstyaśuciḥ kvacit|*
*uktaṁ mayaitatpūrvaṁ te tatkiṁ māṁ tvaṁ vigarhasi||21||*

*Word for word:* shrimad-vishvambharah — Sriman Vishvambhara; praha — said; mudhe — foolish woman; na asti — there is not; ashuchih — impurity; kvachit — anywhere; uktam — told; maya — by me; etat — this; purvam — before; te — to you; tat — so; kim — why; mam — me; tvam — you; vigarhasi — reproach.

**21.** Sriman Vishvambhara said: "Foolish woman! There is nothing impure anywhere. I told you this before — so why do you reproach Me?"

### Verse 22

इत्युक्त्वा वदने तस्या इष्टकां प्राहिणोद्रुषा।
तदाघातेन व्यथिता मूर्च्छिता निपपात सा॥२२॥

*ityuktvā vadane tasyā iṣṭakāṁ prāhiṇodruṣā|*
*tadāghātena vyathitā mūrcchitā nipapāta sā||22||*

*Word for word:* iti — thus; uktva — having spoken; vadane — at the face; tasyah — her; ishtakam — a brick; prahinot — threw; rusha — in anger; tat-aghatena — by that blow; vyathita — pained; murchchhita — fainting; nipapata — fell; sa — she.

**22.** With these words He angrily threw a brick at her face. Stricken by the blow, she fainted and fell.

### Verse 23

तदा सर्वाः समागत्य स्त्रियस्तां शीतलैर्जलैः।
सिषिचुः स्म तदा तत्र हरिर्मानुषकर्मकृत्॥२३॥

*tadā sarvāḥ samāgatya striyastāṁ śītalairjalaiḥ|*
*siṣicuḥ sma tadā tatra harirmānuṣakarmakṛt||23||*

*Word for word:* tada — then; sarvah — all; samagatya — gathering; striyah — the women; tam — her; shitalaih — with cool; jalaih — water; sishichuh sma — sprinkled; tada — then; tatra — there; harih — Hari; manusha-karma-krit — acting in human ways.

**23.** Then all the women gathered and sprinkled her with cool water. And then Hari, who acts in human ways, came there

### Verse 24

आगत्य प्ररुरोदाशु मातर्मातरिति स्वयम्।
श्रीहस्तं तन्मुखे न्यस्य सर्वदुःखापहारकम्॥२४॥

*āgatya prarurodāśu mātarmātariti svayam|*
*śrīhastaṁ tanmukhe nyasya sarvaduḥkhāpahārakam||24||*

*Word for word:* agatya — coming; praruroda — burst into tears; ashu — at once; matah matah — mother, mother; iti — thus; svayam — himself; shri-hastam — his beautiful hand; tat-mukhe — on her face; nyasya — placing; sarva-duhkha-apaharakam — which removes every sorrow.

**24.** and at once burst into tears Himself: "Mother, mother!" — and laid on her face His beautiful hand, which takes away every sorrow.

### Verse 25

ततः प्रबुद्धा सा सद्यः क्रोडे कृत्वा सुतं शची।
मुमोद वत्सलातीव पुत्रस्नेहातिविह्वला॥२५॥

*tataḥ prabuddhā sā sadyaḥ kroḍe kṛtvā sutaṁ śacī|*
*mumoda vatsalātīva putrasnehātivihvalā||25||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; prabuddha — come to her senses; sa — she; sadyah — at once; krode — on her lap; kritva — placing; sutam — the son; shachi — Shachi; mumoda — rejoiced; vatsala — loving; ativa — greatly; putra-sneha-ati-vihvala — overwhelmed with love for her son.

**25.** Shachi came to herself at once, took her son onto her lap and, beside herself with love for him, rejoiced with all her heart.

### Verse 26

ततो जगद्गुरुं प्राह काचिद्धर्षपरायणा।
परिहासपरा मात्रे नारिकेलफलद्वयम्॥२६॥

*tato jagadguruṁ prāha kāciddharṣaparāyaṇā|*
*parihāsaparā mātre nārikelaphaladvayam||26||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; jagad-gurum — to the teacher of the world; praha — said; kachit — a certain (woman); harsha-parayana — merry; parihasa-para — fond of jest; matre — to the mother; narikela-phala-dvayam — two coconuts.

**26.** Then a merry woman, fond of a joke, said to the Teacher of the world: "Go and fetch two coconuts for your mother,

### Verse 27

समानीय प्रयच्छास्यै तदा स्वस्था भविष्यति।
न चेन्मरिष्यति तदा किमुपायं करिष्यसि॥२७॥

*samānīya prayacchāsyai tadā svasthā bhaviṣyati|*
*na cenmariṣyati tadā kimupāyaṁ kariṣyasi||27||*

*Word for word:* samaniya — bringing; prayachchha — give; asyai — to her; tada — then; svastha — well; bhavishyati — she will be; na chet — if not; marishyati — she will die; tada — then; kim — what; upayam — remedy; karishyasi — will you take.

**27.** bring them and give them to her — then she will be well. If not, she will die — and what will you do then?"

### Verse 28

इति तस्या वचः श्रुत्वा मातुरङ्का[…]।
निर्गत्यानीय स ददौ नारिकेलफलद्वयम्॥२८॥

*iti tasyā vacaḥ śrutvā māturaṅkā[…]|*
*nirgatyānīya sa dadau nārikelaphaladvayam||28||*

*Word for word:* iti — thus; tasyah — her; vachah — words; shrutva — hearing; matuh ankat — from the mother's lap; […] — (rising?); nirgatya — going out; aniya — bringing; sah — he; dadau — gave; narikela-phala-dvayam — two coconuts.

**28.** Hearing her words, He (slipped down) from His mother's lap[^106-7], went out, brought and gave two coconuts.

### Verse 29

तत्कालपातनाम्बुयुक्तवृन्तयुगं हरिः।
तद्दृष्ट्वा विस्मिताः प्रोचुः कुतः प्राप्तं त्वया फलम्॥२९॥

*tatkālapātanāmbuyuktavṛntayugaṁ hariḥ|*
*taddṛṣṭvā vismitāḥ procuḥ kutaḥ prāptaṁ tvayā phalam||29||*

*Word for word:* tat-kala-patana-ambu-yukta-vrinta-yugam — a pair (of fruits) whose stalks were moist with sap from having just fallen; harih — Hari; tat — that; drishtva — seeing; vismitah — amazed; prochuh — said; kutah — from where; praptam — obtained; tvaya — by you; phalam — the fruit.

**29.** Hari brought a pair of coconuts whose stalks still glistened with sap, as if they had just fallen. The astonished women asked: "Where did you get this fruit?"

### Verse 30

ततो हुंकृतिभिः सर्वा वारयित्वा महामनाः।
वत्सगोत्रध्वजो मात्रे ददौ स्मेरमुखाम्बुजम्॥३०॥

*tato huṁkṛtibhiḥ sarvā vārayitvā mahāmanāḥ|*
*vatsagotradhvajo mātre dadau smeramukhāmbujam||30||*

*Word for word:* tatah — then; humkritibhih — with cries of "hum"; sarvah — all (the women); varayitva — silencing; maha-manah — the great-hearted; vatsa-gotra-dhvajah — the banner of the Vatsa clan; matre — to the mother; dadau — gave; smera-mukha-ambujam — his smiling lotus face.

**30.** Then the great-hearted (boy), the banner of the Vatsa clan[^106-8], silenced them all with a fierce "hum!" and turned His smiling lotus face to His mother.

### Verse 31

अथान्यच्छृणु वीर्याणि विचित्राणि महात्मनः।
लोकोत्तराणि साधूनि मायिनः परमात्मनः॥३१॥

*athānyacchṛṇu vīryāṇi vicitrāṇi mahātmanaḥ|*
*lokottarāṇi sādhūni māyinaḥ paramātmanaḥ||31||*

*Word for word:* atha — now; anyat — further; shrinu — hear; viryani — of the mighty deeds; vichitrani — wonderful; maha-atmanah — of the great soul; loka-uttarani — beyond the worldly; sadhuni — excellent; mayinah — of the Lord of maya; parama-atmanah — of the Supreme Self.

**31.** Now hear more of the wonderful deeds of the Great Soul — the Supreme Self, the Lord of maya: excellent deeds, beyond all that is worldly.

### Verse 32

रात्रौ कदाचित्संसुप्ता शची पूर्णं जनैरिव।
पुरमालक्ष्य संविग्ना क्रोडस्थं स्वसुतं शची॥३२॥

*rātrau kadācitsaṁsuptā śacī pūrṇaṁ janairiva|*
*puramālakṣya saṁvignā kroḍasthaṁ svasutaṁ śacī||32||*

*Word for word:* ratrau — at night; kadachit — once; samsupta — having fallen asleep; shachi — Shachi; purnam — full; janaih — of people; iva — as if; puram — the house; alakshya — noticing; samvigna — alarmed; kroda-stham — lying on her lap; sva-sutam — her own son; shachi — Shachi.

**32.** One night Shachi, having fallen asleep, noticed that the house seemed full of people; alarmed, she took her son, who lay on her lap,

### Verse 33

शङ्किता प्रेषयामास पतिगेहे त्वरान्विता।
पूजितं पथि देवैश्च श्रीमद्विश्वम्भरं हरिम्॥३३॥

*śaṅkitā preṣayāmāsa patigehe tvarānvitā|*
*pūjitaṁ pathi devaiśca śrīmadviśvambharaṁ harim||33||*

*Word for word:* shankita — frightened; preshayamasa — sent; pati-gehe — to her husband's room; tvara-anvita — hastily; pujitam — worshipped; pathi — on the way; devaih — by the gods; cha — and; shrimad-vishvambharam — Sriman Vishvambhara; harim — Hari.

**33.** and in fear hastily sent Sriman Vishvambhara Hari to her husband's room; and on the way the gods worshipped Him[^106-9].

### Verse 34

पथि प्रयातस्य सुतस्य पादयोः सुरिक्तयोर्नूपुरनिस्वनं मुहुः।
श्रुत्वा सशङ्कः किमिदं कुतः स्वनं वात्स्यः शचीं प्राह शची च वात्स्यम्॥३४॥

*pathi prayātasya sutasya pādayoḥ suriktayornūpuranisvanaṁ muhuḥ|*
*śrutvā saśaṅkaḥ kimidaṁ kutaḥ svanaṁ vātsyaḥ śacīṁ prāha śacī ca vātsyam||34||*

*Word for word:* pathi — on the way; prayatasya — going; sutasya — of the son; padayoh — on the feet; su-riktayoh — quite bare (of ornaments); nupura-nisvanam — the jingling of ankle bells; muhuh — again and again; shrutva — hearing; sa-shankah — alarmed; kim — what; idam — this; kutah — whence; svanam — sound; vatsyah — the Vatsya; shachim — Shachi; praha — asked; shachi — Shachi; cha — and; vatsyam — the Vatsya.

**34.** As the son walked there, the jingling of ankle bells came again and again from His bare feet. Hearing it, the alarmed Vatsya asked Shachi, and Shachi the Vatsya: "What is this? Where does this sound come from?"

### Verse 35

गते समीपं तनयेऽतिविस्मितो दृष्ट्वा सुरिक्तं सुतपादपङ्कजम्।
कुतः श्रुतं नूपुरमञ्जुलस्वनं सुतं समालिङ्ग्य मुदं ययौ द्विजः॥३५॥

*gate samīpaṁ tanaye'tivismito dṛṣṭvā suriktaṁ sutapādapaṅkajam|*
*kutaḥ śrutaṁ nūpuramañjulasvanaṁ sutaṁ samāliṅgya mudaṁ yayau dvijaḥ||35||*

*Word for word:* gate — when had come; samipam — near; tanaye — the son; ati-vismitah — greatly amazed; drishtva — seeing; su-riktam — quite bare; suta-pada-pankajam — the son's lotus feet; kutah — whence; shrutam — heard; nupura-manjula-svanam — the sweet jingling of ankle bells; sutam — the son; samalingya — embracing; mudam — joy; yayau — attained; dvijah — the brahmana.

**35.** When the son came near, the brahmana saw in great amazement that his son's lotus feet were quite bare. "Then where did the sweet jingling of ankle bells come from?" (he wondered) — and, embracing his son, he was filled with joy.

*Thus ends the sixth sarga, "The Description of the Pastimes from Birth among the Childhood Plays" (Balya-krida: janmadi-lila-varnana), of the first prakrama of the great poem "Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charitamrita."*

[^106-1]: "The king of the twice-born" (dvijaraja) is the moon; but "the twice-born" (dvija) are also the teeth: a play on words — the shining of His teeth in His smile.
[^106-2]: Vishvambhara means "He who sustains the universe."
[^106-3]: The earth, according to tradition, suffered in separation from Krishna's feet after His departure.
[^106-4]: The story of the pilgrim brahmana whose food, offered to the Lord, was eaten by the infant Nimai; so too the infant Krishna once ate the food of a wandering brahmana in the house of Nanda.
[^106-5]: An allusion to the Bhagavata Purana (10.9): Yashoda bound the child Krishna when He broke the pot of curd.
[^106-6]: Dattatreya is an incarnation of the Lord, an avadhuta who made no distinction between pure and impure.
[^106-7]: The end of the line is corrupt in the sources; the reading is tentative.
[^106-8]: The Vatsa clan (gotra) is the clan of Jagannatha Mishra (cf. "the Vatsya").

## Prakrama 1, Sarga 7. The Childhood Plays

*The story of Vishvarupa: his sannyasa and the parents' blessing; Vishvambhara's promise to serve his parents; His pranks and the dream of Jagannatha Mishra; Vishvambhara asks His mother not to eat on Ekadashi; the question of Damodara*

### Verse 1

इति श्रुत्वा हरेः पादपङ्कजध्याननिर्वृतः।
दामोदरः पर्यपृच्छद्धरेर्ज्येष्ठस्य सत्कथाम्॥१॥

*iti śrutvā hareḥ pādapaṅkajadhyānanirvṛtaḥ|*
*dāmodaraḥ paryapṛcchaddharerjyeṣṭhasya satkathām||1||*

*Word for word:* iti — thus; shrutva — having heard; hareh — of Hari; pada-pankaja-dhyana-nirvritah — blissful in meditation on the lotus feet; damodarah — Damodara; paryaprichchhat — inquired; hareh — of Hari; jyeshthasya — of the elder (brother); sat-katham — the holy story.

**1.** Having heard this, and blissful in meditation on Hari's lotus feet, Damodara asked to hear the holy story of Hari's elder brother:

### Verse 2

कथयस्व महत्ख्यातं विश्वरूपस्य तत्त्वतः।
तच्छ्रुत्वा प्राह भो ब्रह्मञ्छ्रूयतां कथयामि ते॥२॥

*kathayasva mahatkhyātaṁ viśvarūpasya tattvataḥ|*
*tacchrutvā prāha bho brahmañchrūyatāṁ kathayāmi te||2||*

*Word for word:* kathayasva — tell; mahat — the great; khyatam — renowned (story); vishvarupasya — of Vishvarupa; tattvatah — truly; tat — that; shrutva — hearing; praha — said; bhoh brahman — O brahmana; shruyatam — listen; kathayami — I shall tell; te — to you.

**2.** "Tell me truly the great and renowned story of Vishvarupa." Hearing this, (Murari) replied: "Listen, O brahmana, and I shall tell you."

### Verse 3

इत्युक्त्वा वक्तुमारेभे वैद्यो हृद्यां कथां शुभाम्।
बलदेवांशकस्यापि विश्वरूपस्य पावनीम्॥३॥

*ityuktvā vaktumārebhe vaidyo hṛdyāṁ kathāṁ śubhām|*
*baladevāṁśakasyāpi viśvarūpasya pāvanīm||3||*

*Word for word:* iti — thus; uktva — having spoken; vaktum — to tell; arebhe — began; vaidyah — the physician (Murari); hridyam — heart-charming; katham — the story; shubham — auspicious; baladeva-amshakasya — of the portion of Baladeva; api — also; vishvarupasya — of Vishvarupa; pavanim — purifying.

**3.** With these words the physician[^107-1] began the heart-charming, auspicious and purifying story of Vishvarupa, the portion of Baladeva.

### Verse 4

श्रीमच्छ्रीविश्वरूपः सकलगुणनिधिः षोडशाब्दोऽतिशुद्धः
प्रापाचार्यत्वमात्मश्रवणमननतः शक्तधीः प्रेमभक्तः।
सर्वज्ञः सर्वदासौ नरहरिचरणासक्तचित्तोऽतिहृष्टः
शान्तः सन्तोषयुक्तो जगति न रतिमान्वेदवेत्ता रसज्ञः॥४॥

*śrīmacchrīviśvarūpaḥ sakalaguṇanidhiḥ ṣoḍaśābdo'tiśuddhaḥ*
*prāpācāryatvamātmaśravaṇamananataḥ śaktadhīḥ premabhaktaḥ|*
*sarvajñaḥ sarvadāsau naraharicaraṇāsaktacitto'tihṛṣṭaḥ*
*śāntaḥ santoṣayukto jagati na ratimānvedavettā rasajñaḥ||4||*

*Word for word:* shrimach-chhri-vishvarupah — Sriman Sri Vishvarupa; sakala-guna-nidhih — a treasury of all virtues; shodasha-abdah — sixteen years old; ati-shuddhah — utterly pure; prapa — attained; acharyatvam — the rank of acharya; atma-shravana-mananatah — by hearing and reflecting on the Self; shakta-dhih — of powerful intellect; prema-bhaktah — a devotee full of love; sarva-jnah — all-knowing; sarvada — always; asau — he; narahari-charana-asakta-chittah — with mind attached to the feet of Narahari; ati-hrishtah — very joyful; shantah — peaceful; santosha-yuktah — contented; jagati — to the world; na ratiman — not attached; veda-vetta — knower of the Vedas; rasa-jnah — knower of rasa.

**4.** Sriman Sri Vishvarupa, a treasury of every virtue, utterly pure, became an acharya at sixteen by hearing of the Self and reflecting upon it. Of powerful intellect, a devotee full of love, all-knowing, he was ever joyful, his heart clinging to the feet of Narahari; peaceful and content, unattached to the world, he knew the Vedas and knew rasa.

### Verse 5

जनको विजने विचिन्त्य तत्तनयस्योद्वहनोचितां वधूम्।
मनसा परिचिन्तयन्स्वयं बुबुधे तत्सकलं द्विजात्मजः॥५॥

*janako vijane vicintya tattanayasyodvahanocitāṁ vadhūm|*
*manasā paricintayansvayaṁ bubudhe tatsakalaṁ dvijātmajaḥ||5||*

*Word for word:* janakah — the father; vijane — in private; vichintya — having considered; tat — that; tanayasya — of the son; udvahana-uchitam — fit for marriage; vadhum — a bride; manasa — in his mind; parichintayan — pondering; svayam — himself; bubudhe — knew; tat — that; sakalam — all; dvija-atmajah — the brahmana's son.

*hareścaritramevātra hitāya jagatāṁ bhavet||27||*

*Word for word:* kim — is it; maya — the maya; jagad-ishasya — of the Lord of the world; tat — that; vaktum — to explain; tvam — you; iha — here; arhasi — should; hareh — of Hari; charitram — the deeds; eva — indeed; atra — here; hitaya — for the good; jagatam — of the worlds; bhavet — may be.

**27.** Is this the maya of the Lord of the world? Please explain it — for the deeds of Hari are done for the good of the world."

*Thus ends the seventh sarga, "The Childhood Plays" (Balya-krida), of the first prakrama of the great poem "Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charitamrita."*

## Prakrama 1, Sarga 8. The Passing of Jagannatha Mishra

*Murari's answer: the Lord enters the devotee's heart, and the devotee takes on His form (the examples of Prahlada, the gopis and Narada); Vishvambhara's studies; the illness and passing of Jagannatha Mishra on the bank of the Ganga; the funeral rites*

### Verse 1

इति श्रुत्वा वचस्तस्य चिन्तयित्वा विचार्य च।

### Verse 3

तस्यानुकारं चक्रे स तत्तेजस्तत्पराक्रमम्।

**4.** So it is for a time; and then he returns to outer consciousness and does his ordinary acts — as once Prahlada did:

### Verse 5

तादात्म्योऽभूत्तोयनिधौ पुनर्देहस्मृतिस्तटे।

**6.** The Lord did this to teach the world a lesson: for a devotee of Krishna there can be such identity of form (with Him).

### Verse 7

यथात्र न विमुह्यन्ति जना इत्यभ्यशिक्षयत्।

**8.** Krishna, having slain Keshi, showed Narada His glory and His power; and then the best of sages

### Verse 9

### Verse 10

एवं रामो जगद्योनिर्विश्वरूपमदर्शयत्।

*Word for word:* vimanah — downcast; iva — as if; sanchitaih — with the accumulated; dhanaih — wealth; pitri-yajnam — the sacrifice to the ancestors (shraddha); pitri-vatsalah — loving his father; akarot — performed; dvija-pujana-sat-kriyam — the holy rite of honouring brahmanas; kramat — in due order; vidadhe — performed; tam — that; sah — he; dhara-adi-bhajanaih — with gifts of land and other things (?).

**23.** As if downcast, loving His father, He performed with the accumulated wealth the sacrifice to the ancestors, and then in due order the holy rite of honouring the brahmanas, presenting them with land and other gifts[^108-8].

### Verse 24

*iti yo vadati prabhoḥ piturdivasaṁsthānamatandrito naraḥ|*
*labhate dyunadīṁ hareḥ purīṁ parihāyāśu malaṁ sa gacchati||24||*

*Word for word:* iti — thus; yah — who; vadati — tells; prabhoh — of the Lord; pituh — of the father; diva-samsthanam — the departure to heaven; atandritah — tirelessly; narah — a person; labhate — obtains; dyu-nadim — the heavenly river (Ganga); hareh — of Hari; purim — to the abode; parihaya — casting off; ashu — swiftly; malam — impurity; sah — he; gachchhati — goes.

**24.** Whoever tirelessly tells thus of the departure to heaven of the Lord's father will attain the heavenly river and, swiftly casting off impurity, will go to the abode of Hari."

*Thus ends the eighth sarga, "The Passing of Jagannatha Mishra" (Jagannatha-mishra-samsiddhi), of the first prakrama of the great poem "Sri Krishna-chaitanya-charitamrita."*
