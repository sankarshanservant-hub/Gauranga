# Part 10

*The Dhanvidhara thickets. Prabhasa. Dvaraka. The return journey: Baroda, the Narmada, farewell to Bharga, Dohad, Kukshi — the poor brahmana and the merchant's dream (pp. 183–202)*

*[p. 183]*

**2000.** Lions, tigers and all kinds of beasts live here;
thinking of it, I was very much afraid.

**2001.** Understanding my mind from a hint,
the Lord smiled and said, "Why are you afraid for nothing?

**2002.** If the Name of Hari removes the fear of Yama,
why are you afraid at the sight of these thickets?"

**2003.** With our whole party we were sixteen people;
the son of Shachi entered the thickets.

**2004.** The beauty of the jungle is most charming;
what can I say of its beauty — it would take long to tell.

**2005.** How many wild flowers have bloomed, scenting the air,
how many hundreds of trees and creepers sway in the wind!

**2006.** Sitting on the branches, birds of many kinds are singing;
hearing that song, the heart is overwhelmed.

**2007.** Through the middle runs a single path, jungle on both sides,
and now and then bands of sannyasis appear.

**2008.** I see the sun overhead,
and at once, for hunger, I look this way and that.

**2009.** "To whose door shall I go now for alms?
There are no village people here — where shall I get alms?"

**2010.** On both sides various trees are laden with fruit;
seeing the fruit, my eagerness grew.

*[p. 184]*

**2011.** I cannot describe the wondrous fruit;
so many fruits have ripened and stand out in rows.

**2012.** The fruit is shaped like a star-fruit;
never have I tasted fruit like it.

**2013.** All around the fruit lies in heaps;
all the sannyasis are eating the fruit with both hands.

**2014.** Without permission I cannot eat the fruit,
but seeing the fruit, greed seized me.

**2015.** I take some fruit for the Lord,
and in the afternoon the Lord offers the fruit.

**2016.** Having tasted two or four fruits,
Gora, the delight of hearts, tells us to eat.

**2017.** I eat my fill of fruit, as much as I can,
and put as many more as I find into the bag.

**2018.** Govinda Charana gobbles fruit after fruit,
while Ramananda tastes them slowly.

**2019.** All saw the wondrous property of the fruit:
that wild fruit takes away both hunger and thirst.

**2020.** On a tree like a four-sided cactus,
wondrous fruit — I eat it with great greed.

**2021.** The more I eat, the more fruits I see;
I put more into the bag and eat till I am stuffed.

*[p. 185]*

**2022.** In the dense jungle there is no smell of man;
now and then everyone raises the cry of "Hari".

**2023.** Before evening had come it grew dark on the path;
the son of Shachi sat down beneath a tree.

**2024.** Here and there the king has made halting-places;
we go down to one with the Lord.

**2025.** A place fenced with forest logs, without house or door;
all the sannyasis sat down here.

**2026.** Clapping his hands, the Lord began the Name,
and hearing the Name, the sannyasis grew excited.

**2027.** Gathering wood, they lit a fire;
Bhargadeva chants the Name, clapping his hands.

**2028.** In the middle of that jungle we feel no fear;
chanting the Name of Hari, we all pass the night.

**2029.** Next morning, raising the cry of "Hari",
Gora set out, remembering Sri Hari.

**2030.** The further we go, the denser the jungle;
seeing those thickets, the body trembles.

**2031.** Going a long way, we find a small stream,
and there Shachi's darling bathes.

**2032.** Having bathed, we hurry on ahead
and, after going some distance, all sit down.

*[p. 186]*

**2033.** The Lord tells Ramananda to bring fruit,
and Ramananda brings fruit and puts it down there.

**2034.** He gathers and brings fruits of every kind,
and Gora, the delight of hearts, worships and makes the offering.

**2035.** Never have I seen such sweet fruit;
all together we fill our bellies with fruit.

**2036.** Food for thousands of people lies on the road;
to whom shall I tell how great God's mercy is!

**2037.** Finishing our tasks by midday, we go on,
and in the afternoon we all find another halting-place.

**2038.** A place at the edge of the jungle, fenced with stakes;
there Gora, the delight of hearts, entered.

**2039.** Bringing wood, the sannyasis lit a fire,
and the Lord, clapping his hands, began the song:

**2040.** "Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna, Hare" —
this song the Lord sings at any hour.

**2041.** As he sang I saw that he was beside himself;
the Lord was filled with thrills and his body trembled.

**2042.** Gora Raya began to weep aloud,
and seeing his state Bharga cried out.

**2043.** Next day, rising in the morning, we go on;
on the road a band of pilgrims comes back towards us.

*[p. 187]*

**2044.** When the two bands met on the road,
all joyfully raised the cry of "Hari".

**2045.** Thus in seven days the Dhanvidhara thickets
we all crossed, spreading joy.

**2046.** Nearby is Amarapuri, named Gopitala;
we all go there, to the abode of joy.

**2047.** Everyone calls this the holy place of Prabhasa;
seeing Prabhasa, I felt great delight.

**2048.** Where the Yadus gave up their bodies,
there the Lord went and wept a great deal.

**2049.** Drunk on mead, all the Yadu heroes
fought one another and gave up their bodies.

**2050.** Without counting who was kin and who a stranger,
by Krishna's will the Yadu heroes died.

**2051.** Charudeshna, Sura, Satyaki, Yuyudhana,
Samba, Gada and all the other wise ones

**2052.** died there, fighting one another;
with a sorrowful face the Lord weeps at that place.

**2053.** No one gains so much joy from weeping:
weeping, the Lord scatters bliss over the earth.

**2054.** To take away the grief and sorrow of the world,
he preaches the Name of Hari at all times.

*[p. 188]*

**2055.** The Name of Hari, love, devotion, the worship of Hari —
these the Lord teaches the people of the world unceasingly.

**2056.** Day or night, he goes from door to door
and distributes the Name of Hari throughout the world:

**2057.** "Who will take the Name of Hari? Come forward!
I give everyone this jewel without price.

**2058.** All of you, take this priceless jewel with care,
and you will cross the ocean of worldly existence without effort.

**2059.** If once you utter the Name of Hari with your lips,
your bonds will be cut and all will go to the eternal abode.

**2060.** Very tight are the knots of the rope of maya;
who can cut them but with the sword of the Name of Hari?"

**2061.** Saying this, the Lord goes from door to door
and spreads the rasa of love through the world.

**2062.** The people of Amarapuri gathered together
and all were delighted to see the Lord.

**2063.** Like a madman he runs this way and that,
roaming about intoxicated with ecstasy.

**2064.** Sometimes he runs breathlessly, as if senseless,
the pupils of his eyes rolled upwards.

**2065.** The mass of his matted hair has fallen loose over his back,
and tears fall ceaselessly onto his breast.

*[p. 189]*

**2066.** Dressed like a madman, his garment slipping,
chalk flying all over his body, grey with dust.

**2067.** "Where is the sacrificial pit?" says Gora Raya,
and the pandas, staying with him, show him Prabhasa.

**2068.** We go to the southern part of Prabhasa
and there we see the pit.

**2069.** Having dug this pit, the Lord of the Yadus performed a sacrifice,
and at that sacrifice the Yadus fought and died.

**2070.** Where Satyabhama made a garden
and sometimes came there with Krishna,

**2071.** where the virtuous lady lived in supreme joy —
seeing that place, the great-souled Gauranga

**2072.** burst into tears, crying aloud;
crying out again and again, the Lord weeps without end.

**2073.** Gradually ten pandas gathered
and one by one began showing all the places.

**2074.** After three days of spiritual conversation here,
he then told us to go on to the city of Dvaraka.

**2075.** At Prabhasa there are no more tirthas to see;
on the first of Ashvin we go to Dvaraka.

**2076.** The Lord goes chanting Krishna's name;
along the seashore we go to Dvaraka.

*[p. 190]*

**2077.** After four days we come to an inlet of the sea;
it has to be crossed on a rope.

**2078.** Over the rope we cross to Dvaraka
and see a mountain called Raivataka.

**2079.** Seeing the mountain, Gora sways with feeling,
and the Lord smiled quietly.

**2080.** Who knows why, with a radiant face,
Mahaprabhu looked at me with a smile.

**2081.** Looking at me he said, "When you reach Dvaraka,
bow down, all of you, and be fulfilled.

**2082.** With great devotion smear the dust all over your bodies;
by the fruit of your merit you have seen the city of Dvaraka.

**2083.** By the strength of good deeds in former lives
you have all seen the city of Dvaraka today."

**2084.** Hearing this, all of us bowed down together,
and Gora's well of joy overflowed.

**2085.** Saying "Hari bol, Hari bol",
the Lord gradually came down, swaying.

**2086.** The ocean of feeling surged, overstepping all bounds;
who could hold the Lord back now?

**2087.** Rolling over and over, he falls on the ground,
and gradually he entered the city.

*[p. 191]*

**2088.** His hair stood on end and he began to tremble,
and tears seemed to burst from his eyes.

**2089.** "Where are you, Lord of Dvaraka?" — saying this,
he flooded the land of Dvaravati with tears.

**2090.** Everything in disarray, his matted hair came loose,
and Gora burst into very loud weeping.

**2091.** What can I say of his state — it is beyond words;
again and again the Lord cries out, "Krishna!"

**2092.** When he entered the house of the Lord of Dvaraka,
at once he grew twice as drunk with joy.

**2093.** His body bristled like a kadamba,
rolling over and over he falls, grey with dust.

**2094.** Drunk with feeling, the Lord gazes languidly
and lies rolling on the ground before the Lord of Dvaraka.

**2095.** All around, devotion seems to overflow;
my Gora, the delight of hearts, weeps and sobs.

**2096.** Sometimes, closing his eyes, he looks within,
as if he were seeing something in his heart.

**2097.** Sometimes he stands gazing upwards,
tears streaming down from his eyes.

**2098.** Seeing Krishna, his body was filled with thrills,
and he gazed at him fixedly.

*[p. 192]*

**2099.** He circles the holy temple three times,
bowing humbly each time.

**2100.** Gora, the delight of hearts, bows with all eight limbs,
and seeing this Bhargadeva falls prostrate.

**2101.** Gradually word spread through Dvaraka,
and everyone whispered about the Lord.

**2102.** Some say, "Come and see the sannyasi, brother;
no one has ever seen such a sannyasi.

**2103.** What can I say of him — it is beyond words;
surely there is no other sannyasi like him on earth.

**2104.** Never have I seen such a wondrous state;
I would give my life for the sannyasi's beauty and virtues!

**2105.** Seeing him, devotion arises in the heart,
and tears come into the eyes of themselves.

**2106.** I long to go away with the sannyasi;
if you see him, brother, your bonds are cut.

**2107.** Seeing him, one loses all taste for worldly life;
seeing that form, a sinner becomes pure at once.

**2108.** No one so merciful is to be seen;
in his mercy he gives the Name of Hari to all.

**2109.** A head full of matted hair, an outer cloth for clothing;
seeing his form, one's desires are fulfilled.

*[p. 193]*

**2110.** Seeing him, one feels he is an incarnation of God;
his heart is always full of the rasa of devotion.

**2111.** The young sannyasi is always drunk with this ecstasy;
he has stirred up the people of Dvaraka.

**2112.** He has no lust, no anger, no desire;
his faith in the Lord of Dvaraka is unshakeable.

**2113.** He calls the sinners and gives them the Name of Hari;
seeing him, the heart overflows."

**2114.** Gora Raya stayed a fortnight in Dvaraka
and went every day to the Lord of Dvaraka.

**2115.** Every day he went and had darshan to his heart's content,
and made the city of Dvaraka drunk with the rasa of devotion.

**2116.** All the people of Dvaraka who were devoted to God
come to see the Lord.

**2117.** The Lord holds spiritual conversation with all,
and all, singing kirtana, dance full of love.

**2118.** The city sways with the spirit of dharma;
everyone's heart seems to have become pure.

**2119.** A gentle breeze always began to blow,
and everything around was fragrant with lotus.

**2120.** All the people rejoice in the Lord's company;
women and men alike come running.

*[p. 194]*

**2121.** On all sides auspicious signs appeared;
through the Name of Hari every quarter grew bright.

**2122.** Pandas and householders all together
listen to the teaching on dharma with attentive ears.

**2123.** He explains to anyone who does not understand;
speaking in many tongues, the Lord inspires him.

**2124.** Sometimes my Lord speaks "khai mai",
speaks "jhi mai"[^138] and explains to everyone.

**2125.** How he makes the people understand — he is all-powerful;
hearing his teaching, all were beside themselves.

**2126.** Wise men and fools alike all came,
and seeing the Lord, all were thrilled.

**2127.** One evening the Lord slowly
went to the temple of Krishna.

**2128.** Many people follow behind the Lord,
but Shachi's son says nothing, good or bad.

**2129.** At the temple door he made the eight-limbed obeisance,
and seeing this all the people rolled on the ground.

**2130.** With folded hands the Lord sings many hymns,
and at once tears pour from his eyes.

**2131.** The Lord's heart brims with the rasa of love:
wherever he looks he sees everything full of Krishna.

*[p. 195]*

**2132.** Closing his eyes, he began to call, "Krishna!"
and his body thrilled with love.

**2133.** One who has not seen that state with his own eyes —
how shall I, a great fool, make him understand?

**2134.** Where there was no [water], a desert,
there Chaitanya Gosai made a river flow.

**2135.** In the whole country no sinner remained;
by giving devotion, the Lord delivered the sinners.

**2136.** One day the pandas, rejoicing,
held a great festival, making an offering.

**2137.** Inviting guests and Vaishnavas,
they distribute thickened milk, yogurt, puris and the rest.

**2138.** Gora, the jewel of virtues, going among the pandas,
distributes the prasada himself.

**2139.** At night we all go together to the hut
and pass the night in supreme joy.

**2140.** Thus, after a fortnight of spiritual conversation,
the next day the Lord leaves the city of Dvaraka.

**2141.** The Lord said, "This time we shall go to Nilachala,
and in Nilachala we shall all live together in joy.

**2142.** Come, let us all go together to Vidyanagara;
I will not go to Puri alone, leaving Rama Raya behind.

*[p. 196]*

**2143.** Ramananda is a great lover of worship;
when I think of him my heart is soothed.

**2144.** Ramananda Raya is the crest-jewel of practitioners;
sitting in solitude, Raya sings the glories of Krishna.

**2145.** One whose tears flow at the name of Hari-Krishna
the Bhagavata calls an eternal Vaishnava.

**2146.** If even a fool calls on the treasure that is Krishna with devotion,
I bow millions of times at his feet.

**2147.** Ramananda, the devotee of Krishna, is worthy of worship;
Ramananda Raya is dearer to me than life.

**2148.** I love Ramananda as my own life;
he is a supreme Vaishnava, a detached sannyasi.

**2149.** Rama Raya is unattached to worldly things;
every day Raya gets to see Radha and Krishna.

**2150.** Ramananda counts wealth as straw
and calls on Krishna with love day and night.

**2151.** I have seen that, as he calls out "Krishna",
Raya, drunk with love, keeps trembling.

**2152.** At Krishna's name he sheds tears of love
and falls unconscious on the ground.

**2153.** My heart can bear separation from Raya no longer;
let us all go quickly to Raya."

*[p. 197]*

**2154.** Saying this, the Lord set out,
and hundreds of people followed him.

**2155.** Sending the village people back with sweet words,
my Gora Raya goes towards the inlet.

**2156.** Bhargadeva with his party
comes to the bank of the inlet to cross.

**2157.** One by one, all having crossed,
my sannyasi of Nadia comes into Gujarat.

**2158.** On the last day of Ashvin, coming back to the city of Baroda,
my Lord chants the Name of Hari.

**2159.** Govinda Charana and I, to beg alms,
presented ourselves at householders' doors.

**2160.** Fruits, roots, flour, parched grain — whatever alms I receive,
I bring and offer with a pure heart.

**2161.** Settling beneath a tree, the Lord made the offering,
and receiving the prasada, all were fulfilled.

**2162.** Next day, setting out from Baroda,
the Lord began to go south.

**2163.** After sixteen days we came to the bank of the Narmada,
and all of us bathed in the waters of the Narmada.

**2164.** The Lord said, "Bhargadeva, where will you go?
Will you come with me to Nilachala?"

*[p. 198]*

**2165.** Bharga, before the Lord, rubbing his hands,
said, "I shall go south.

**2166.** In the city of Bom-bom[^139] the mahanta Aditya Raj
serves Ranchhodji with devotion.

**2167.** Accept my obeisance, Lord;
mercifully place your foot on my head."

**2168.** Saying this, Bhargadeva fell prostrate
and clasped both feet tightly with both hands.

**2169.** Bharga said, "You are Krishna, you are my Hari;
give me this alms: that I may die remembering your feet.

**2170.** To see your own lilas and play yourself,
you went to Dvaraka, deceiving people.

**2171.** Be that as it may, put your foot on my head;
you will not be able to deceive me again — I will not be deceived."

**2172.** The Lord said, "Bharga, why do you say such things?
Why do you speak such words to me?

**2173.** I wander the roads as a renunciant,
without food, without clothing, utterly poor and lowly.

**2174.** For alms I go from door to door;
never speak such words to me again.

**2175.** Always say 'Krishna, Krishna' with faith;
faith in Krishna will bring you to Krishna.

*[p. 199]*

**2176.** Hari is a mass of consciousness and bliss, the highest of the high;
meditate on his lotus feet and you will cross the ocean of existence.

**2177.** With love and devotion meditate on Hari's feet,
and he will surely show himself to you.

**2178.** Hari is very kind to his devotees;
think of him — he is the refuge of the helpless."

**2179.** Saying this, the Lord touched Bhargadeva,
and at once Bharga's body was purified.

**2180.** Standing on the bank, Bhargadeva gazes at him
and, fulfilled, at last took his leave.

**2181.** All the other sannyasis who were with Bharga
came and stood before the Lord.

**2182.** One by one they bowed at the Lord's feet,
and the Lord sent them all off with sweet words.

**2183.** Bhargadeva went away to the south,
and the Lord sets out lovingly for Nilachala.

**2184.** I, Ramananda and Govinda Charana
spent that day on the bank of the Narmada.

**2185.** Next day we go along the bank of the Narmada
and all arrive at the town of Dohad.

**2186.** I brought some flour that I had begged,
and Lord Gaura-Hari made rotis and offered them.

*[p. 200]*

**2187.** We pass the night in the town of Dohad;
beneath a tree Gora-chand raises the cry of "Hari".

**2188.** Rising in the morning, we go to the town of Kukshi,
and here we see many Vaishnavas.

**2189.** Wherever we go we see tulasi groves;
every villager, I see, is devoted to Krishna.

**2190.** In the evening all the people raise the cry of "Hari";
seeing this, my Lord thrills with joy.

**2191.** In this place lives a poor brahmana,
and in his house there is Lakshmi-Janardana.

**2192.** The brahmana worships Lakshmi-Janardana with devotion;
hearing of this, the Lord goes to his house.

**2193.** The brahmana, seeing the Lord, offers him hospitality,
welcoming him warmly, regarding him as a guest.

**2194.** The brahmana says, "I am a poor brahmana;
why have you set foot in my house?

**2195.** How shall I serve a sannyasi?
I fear my dharma will be ruined in my house."

**2196.** The Lord said, "Have no anxiety, thakura:
he whose creation it is will give food in plenty.

**2197.** Who worries for whom — that is the error of Kali;
hear the essence: it is Krishna who always cares.

*[p. 201]*

**2198.** The householder says, 'I feed everyone';
but who thinks how the homeless man living under a tree is fed?

**2199.** Who gives food to the tiny insects in the forest?
Why then, brahmana, do you worry for nothing?"

**2200.** Just then a vaishya[^140] came to the brahmana's door
bringing milk, sugar and flour to fill his store.

**2201.** The vaishya said, "Listen, listen, brahmana thakura:
the Lord has shown me his mercy.

**2202.** I saw your Janardana in a dream:
he asks me for sweet rice.

**2203.** In the form of a man Narayana is your guest;
Narayana showed me this in a dream.

**2204.** Last night, before dawn, I saw it in a dream,
and I have brought milk and sugar for his sake.

**2205.** Cook sweet rice and offer it to Narayana, brahmana."
Hearing this, the brahmana began to weep.

**2206.** The brahmana said, "Where have the milk and sugar come from?"
The Lord said, "Narayana himself has provided them."

**2207.** [Here came] milk and sugar [the line is corrupt in the edition].
The brahmana said, "I am a poor man — this is a marvel!"

**2208.** The Lord said, "Narayana…" [the line breaks off].
The brahmana said, "I was worrying about you."

**2209.** The Lord said, "Narayana has given and provided."[^141]

*[p. 202]*

**2210.** The vaishya, gazing at the Lord's face,
sees something, losing consciousness again and again.

**2211.** The brahmana said, "Vaishya, what are you seeing, brother?"
The vaishya said, "I am bewildered, that is why I look.

**2212.** Listen, best of brahmanas, what shall I tell you:
in my dream I saw him in human form."

**2213.** Hearing this, the Lord said to the vaishya,
"Why do you keep making a fuss for nothing?

**2214.** Whom did you see in an empty dream?
Why then do you make a fuss without reason?

**2215.** Brother vaishya, you are a holy man and very fortunate;
that is why the Lord Bhagavan appeared to you in a dream.

**2216.** I am an ordinary sannyasi, come
to a brahmana's house for the sake of food."

**2217.** The brahmana said, "What need is there for such talk?
Narayana takes care of the service of guests."

**2218.** Then the brahmana, weeping, begged the Lord,
"Cook the sweet rice yourself and make the offering."

**2219.** With a slight smile the Lord cooked the sweet rice,
and the brahmana, staying near, waited upon him.

**2220.** All received the prasada with joy;
the Lord distributed the prasada with his own hands.

### Translator's notes

[^138]: "Khai mai", "jhi mai": Govinda imitates the sound of languages unknown to him (Gujarati, Kathiawari).
[^139]: "Bom-bom nagar" is apparently Bombay (Mumbai), as D. C. Sen suggests.
[^140]: A vaishya is a member of the merchant class.
[^141]: Lines 2207–2209 are badly damaged in the edition (some words are missing); the translation gives the surviving sense.
