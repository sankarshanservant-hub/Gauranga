# Part 11

*Back through Central India: Amjhora, Mandu, Devghar (the healing of the leper), Vidyanagara and Ramananda Raya, Sambalpur, Rasalkunda (the Marwari brahmana and his son). The return to Puri (pp. 203–222)*

*[p. 203]*

**2221.** There was a great festival in the brahmana's house;
next morning the Lord sets out.

**2222.** The excellent brahmana, coming up lovingly,
pleaded with him long, with folded hands.

**2223.** Taking leave of the brahmana,
my Gora Raya set out in the morning.

**2224.** The vaishya, who had been waiting in hiding,
followed the Lord along the road.

**2225.** Clasping his feet, the vaishya began to weep,
and merciful Chaitanya raised him up.

**2226.** The Lord said, "Holy man, what are you doing, brother?"
The vaishya said, "Have mercy on me, Gosai.

**2227.** I will not let you go — I have recognised you;
give me the dust of your feet and be merciful to me."

**2228.** Smiling, Lord Chaitanya
poured the nectar of the Name of Hari into his ears once.

**2229.** By the Lord's mercy his sins were destroyed,
and the vaishya, greatly blessed, went away.

**2230.** By the Lord's grace the vaishya gave up worldly things,
went far away and lives there, having made a tulasi grove.

**2231.** He does not converse with anyone
and always meditates on Krishna with the flute at his lips.

*[p. 204]*

**2232.** With his lips he says, "O Hari, have mercy on me;
in your kindness take away all this sinner's suffering."

**2233.** He lives as a hermit and does not go home,
and saying "Hari" he begs alms from door to door.

**2234.** Having shown mercy to the vaishya, Lord Vishvambhara
left the town and went on through the jungle.

**2235.** We all go on through dense jungle,
and for two days we see no town or village.

**2236.** After two days, leaving the jungle,
we reach the town of Amjhora.

**2237.** We writhe in the pangs of hunger,
while my Lord, unperturbed, says "Hari, Hari".

**2238.** The Lord said, "Hari provides food for all."
That same day food came to us of itself.

**2239.** I brought two handfuls of flour I had begged,
and the Lord himself made sixteen rotis.

**2240.** Just then a woman with a child
said, "Give me something — I am dying of hunger.

**2241.** I have no food, no clothing, nothing to eat;
I go along the roads with my child, living on alms."

**2242.** Hearing her words, the merciful Lord
gave her his own share.

*[p. 205]*

**2243.** The wretched woman went away content,
and the Lord passed the day without food.

**2244.** At night I brought some fruit I had begged,
and the Lord, partaking of the fruit, passed the night.

**2245.** There is a tank of Lakshmana here;
hearing of it in the morning, we go there to bathe.

**2246.** The tank at the edge of the town is most charming,
surrounded by hills, of small extent.

**2247.** When Janaki's throat was parched with thirst,
Lakshmana dug this tank by shooting an arrow.

**2248.** It became famous as the tank of Lakshmana;
Janaki called this tank a great tirtha.

**2249.** A most delightful tank, very deep;
bathing in it, our bodies were cooled.

**2250.** Having bathed in this tirtha, merciful Gora
raises the cry of "Hari" — hearing it, the fear of Yama goes.

**2251.** Next day we go up onto the Vindhya mountains,
where the town of Mandura[^142] stands in splendour.

**2252.** In the midst of the mountains, inside a cave,
an ascetic lives and performs austerities.

**2253.** Hearing of the ascetic, my Gora Raya
goes there to see him.

*[p. 206]*

**2254.** The sannyasi thakura is sitting in meditation;
the ascetic's form is most lovely.

**2255.** His body is the colour of molten gold,
and rays of radiance stream out all around.

**2256.** His long, long nails have curled over,
and his white beard covers his chest.

**2257.** Only skin and bone remain, his body emaciated;
his ribs can be seen.

**2258.** He sits motionless, naked;
seeing that form, devotion arose in me.

**2259.** He looks like a wooden image;
opening my eyes wide, I look at his face again and again.

**2260.** Mahaprabhu went and stood before him,
and the ascetic, breaking his meditation, began to look.

**2261.** The moment their eyes met,
the excellent ascetic smiled.

**2262.** Having conversed with the ascetic,
the Lord comes down to the foot of the mountain, to the town of Mandul.

**2263.** On the left the Vindhya range stands in splendour, on the right the Narmada;
from there we reach Devghar in three days.

**2264.** There was a leper in Devghar;
this sick man was named Adi Narayana.

*[p. 207]*

**2265.** Adi Narayana is the foremost of merchants;
he has much wealth, but his mind is always troubled.

**2266.** Outside the village there is a banyan tree;
the merciful Lord went and sat beside it.

**2267.** The Lord's beauty lights up everything around,
and gradually word spread in the town.

**2268.** Two or four people come to see the sannyasi,
and I go into the town to beg.

**2269.** Ramananda goes to bring flowers,
Govinda Charana went to the riverbank.

**2270.** That day I received unboiled rice as alms,
and Ramananda brings flowers of every kind.

**2271.** Having bathed, my Lord began the worship,
and Govinda Charana brought dry wood.

**2272.** After making the offering, Gora Raya began the Name,
and as he chanted the Name his thrills increased.

**2273.** His body choked with love, he began to dance
and at last fell senseless to the ground.

**2274.** Hearing of this, many people come there,
and that leper came and stood close by.

**2275.** Adi Narayana weeps with folded hands,
and, saying "Save me", wept a great deal.

*[p. 208]*

**2276.** Adi Narayana is a supreme Vaishnava;
they gave him prasada to eat.

**2277.** Partaking of the prasada with devotion,
he was at once rid of his leprosy.

**2278.** Receiving prasada, his leprosy left him;
hearing this news, many sick people come.

**2279.** Seeing the difficulty, the Lord began to look around,
then, looking at me, made a sign.

**2280.** I took the sandals and set out,
and on that pretext the Lord leaves the town.

**2281.** Then Adi Narayana follows along.
The Lord said, "By Krishna's mercy you are freed —

**2282.** why then do you come with me?
Go home and always meditate on Krishna's feet."

**2283.** Adi Narayana said, "I will not go home;
I will wander from land to land with you."

**2284.** The Lord said, "Go home and enjoy your wealth."
Narayana said, "What use have I for wealth?

**2285.** If you will not take me with you, merciful one,
I shall build a hut and pass my time there."

**2286.** The Lord said, "Go and make a tulasi grove,
and sitting there, pass your time."

*[p. 209]*

**2287.** Hearing the Lord's words, Adi Narayana gave up everything
and says "Hari, Hari" with his lips.

**2288.** Adi Narayana became the best of holy men;
he passes his time chanting Krishna's name.

**2289.** Bowing at his feet, Adi Narayana
took his leave of the Lord.

**2290.** Thirty kroshas away is the town of Shivana;
in two days Vishvambhara hurries there.

**2291.** East of Shivana is the Mahal mountain;
there the Lord goes, full of love for Krishna.

**2292.** Having seen the Mahal mountain,
the Lord comes to the town of Chandapur.

**2293.** Having had darshan of the goddess Chandi in Chandapur,
Gora goes on to Raipur, remembering Hari.

**2294.** Many people in Raipur, wishing to see him,
came into Chaitanya's presence.

**2295.** Seeing the wretched state of living beings, my Gora Raya
joyfully distributes the Name of Hari from house to house.

**2296.** Then the Lord came to Vidyanagara,
and Ramananda meets him with folded hands.

**2297.** Ramananda Raya came and bowed down,
and the Lord, raising him by the hand, embraced him.

*[p. 210]*

**2298.** The supreme Vaishnava Raya, drawing far back,
began to weep with great humility.

**2299.** The Lord said, "Raya, come with me;
let us go together and behold Lord Jagannatha.

**2300.** You, I and the Bhatta, going to Nilachala,
will chant the Name of Hari to our hearts' content.

**2301.** With you I shall find great joy in discussing the truth;
come, you shall go with me to Nilachala."

**2302.** Raya said, "You go on ahead;
in a few days I shall go to Nilachala."

**2303.** Hearing this, Mahaprabhu smiled a little,
and, rising in the morning, went north.

**2304.** That day we covered a great distance,
and in six days the four of us reach Ratnapur.

**2305.** Leaving Ratnapur we come to the Mahanadi,
and all go east along its bank.

**2306.** A little further on Mahaprabhu came to Svarnagarh
and gazed attentively at the beauty of the town.

**2307.** Wondrous is the beauty of the fort — what more can I say?
Looking all around, I was astonished.

**2308.** A king named Shantishvara lives in this fort;
a messenger went and told this to the king.

*[p. 211]*

**2309.** Hearing of us, the worthy king
comes humbly to see the Lord.

**2310.** The most righteous king, seeing the Lord,
with folded hands lay prostrate on the ground.

**2311.** The king said, "Listen, worthy sannyasi,
sanctify my house today.

**2312.** Today, in your kindness, take alms in my house."
Saying this, the king sang many praises.

**2313.** Hearing this, the Lord looked at me,
and I asked the king for alms.

**2314.** The maharaja brought and gave full alms,
and having given the alms, remained standing with folded hands.

**2315.** In the afternoon the maharaja took his leave,
and Mahaprabhu passed the night beneath a tree.

**2316.** In the morning we all go to Sambalpur
and arrive there at dusk.

**2317.** The town, surrounded by hills, is very beautiful;
the Lord passes the night joyfully in Sambalpur.

**2318.** Ten kroshas away is the town of Bhramara;[^143]
there Mahaprabhu hastened on.

**2319.** Many Vaishnavas live in the town of Bhramara;
here the Lord stayed four days.

*[p. 212]*

**2320.** An Oriya brahmana named Vishnu Rudra
lives here and serves Krishna.

**2321.** The brahmana Vishnu Rudra is very devoted;
the Lord went to see him.

**2322.** Having conversed with Vishnu Rudra,
the Lord goes joyfully on to the town of Pratapnagari.

**2323.** Having given the Name of Hari to the people of this town,
he went on to the town of Dasapalla.

**2324.** Unbelievers, Mayavadis and sufferers, wherever they were,
the Lord made drunk by giving the Name of Hari.

**2325.** Gora is always drunk with joy;
how many sinners he delivered by giving the Name of Hari!

**2326.** Next day we go to Rasalkunda[^144]
and there we see the deity Kurma.

**2327.** Seeing Kurmadeva, the Lord was drunk with love;
tears streamed from both his eyes.

**2328.** With folded hands he sings many hymns to Kurmadeva
and falls crashing to the ground.

**2329.** The people of Rasalkunda are very lacking in devotion;
seeing this, the Lord stays there three days.

**2330.** Calling everyone, men and women alike,
the Lord delivers them by giving the Name of Hari.

*[p. 213]*

**2331.** By the Lord's mercy everyone grew excited
and began to chant the Name of Hari with devotion.

**2332.** There was a Marwari brahmana here;
his son met the Lord.

**2333.** The brahmana's son said, "Have mercy on me;
give me the dust of your feet, Lord, and take away my sorrow.

**2334.** I am a great unbeliever and know nothing;
give me devotion and save me yourself.

**2335.** My father cannot bear the name of Krishna;
in your mercy give devotion to his heart.

**2336.** This is my great sorrow, that my father hates Krishna;
give devotion to his mind, merciful Lord.

**2337.** When my father sees a Vaishnava he abuses him;
in your mercy remove all his sins.

**2338.** I have heard that you are an abode of mercy;
give me this alms, O merciful one."

**2339.** Hearing this, the Lord put his hand on the boy's back,
and at once devotion arose in his heart.

**2340.** Hearing of this, the brahmana, blind with rage,
ran up to the Lord with a stick in his hand.

**2341.** The brahmana said, "Listen, you hypocrite, you villain!
You have ruined my only son!

*[p. 214]*

**2342.** I will strike you with this stick;
I will see right now who will protect you!"

**2343.** With folded hands, weeping, the brahmana's son said,
"Merciful one, forgive my father's offence.

**2344.** My father is utterly ignorant and does not recognise you;
count me as a servant of your feet and forgive me."

**2345.** Hearing this, two or four people,
rebuking the Marwari, went for him.

**2346.** The Marwari brahmana heeded no one's words
and, stick in hand, rose up to beat Chaitanya.

**2347.** The brahmana said, "Having made my son a Vaishnava,
you will lead him away with you, beguiling him!

**2348.** Where will you go, having beguiled the boy?
This time I will punish you and teach you a lesson.

**2349.** I have seen many sannyasis with my own eyes;
this time you will get a lesson from me."

**2350.** Smiling, Chaitanya said, "Listen, my brother:
if you want to beat me, you must say the Name of Hari.

**2351.** As many times as you utter the Name of Hari,
so many blows of the stick you may give.

**2352.** If in your anger you want to beat me,
then say the name 'Hare Krishna' with your lips.

*[p. 215]*

**2353.** Look, here I have bared my back to you;
say 'Hari' once and strike me."

**2354.** Hearing this again, the brahmana's son,
with folded hands, spoke again before the Lord:

**2355.** The boy said, "Lord, forgive my father,
save him from hell.

**2356.** This alms I beg at your lotus feet,
that people may not say, 'Cruel Nimai'."

**2357.** Then the Lord, with a slight smile, said to him,
"In the family into which you have been born

**2358.** no one has any fear of hell;
millions of generations will have a place in Vaikuntha."

**2359.** Saying this, he looked at the brahmana:
"Say, brahmana, say 'Hari' and strike me.

**2360.** Your hard heart is like a desert;
may it become full of sap today by Krishna's mercy.

**2361.** Strike me — that does no harm, brahmana;
just once say 'Hare Krishna' with your lips, brother."

**2362.** Hearing such words, the brahmana began to tremble
and in his fear let his head sink onto his chest.

**2363.** Shrinking with fear, the brahmana cannot see;
weeping in distress, he fell to the ground.

*[p. 216]*

**2364.** Overwhelmed by the Lord's power, the brahmana
clasped both his feet tightly with both hands.

**2365.** The brahmana said, "Merciful one, I beseech you:
save me from hell.

**2366.** Having committed a great offence, I am afraid;
in your mercy forgive my offence, merciful one.

**2367.** Without understanding, I said so many things to you;
punish or protect me, as you judge best."

**2368.** Seeing the brahmana's humility, Gora, the delight of hearts,
poured the nectar of the Name of Hari into his ears.

**2369.** The brahmana was fulfilled, his mind became pure,
and at last he took his leave, clasping the Lord's feet.

**2370.** Having delivered the unbelieving brahmana, the Lord
went on to the bank of the river Rishikulya.

**2371.** Many rishis live on both banks of the river;
all together they welcomed Gora.

**2372.** When the Lord came to the river Rishikulya,
this news gradually reached Puri.

**2373.** Staying three nights at the abode of Rishikulya,
the Lord sanctified Rishikulya with the Name of Hari.

**2374.** When the Lord came near Alalanatha,
Gadadhara and Murari came running to him.

*[p. 217]*

**2375.** Khanjan Acharya[^145] comes with deep love;
lame as he is, he comes ahead of everyone.

**2376.** Sarvabhauma comes, beating two kettle-drums,
and Narahari appears carrying a banner.

**2377.** Haridasa, Ramadasa and Krishnadasa
come eagerly, breathing hard.

**2378.** Jagannatha Dasa and Devaki-nandana,
Junior Haridasa and the singer Lakshmana,

**2379.** Vishnudasa, Puridasa and Damodara,
Narayana Tirtha and Giridhara Dasa —

**2380.** giris, puris, sarasvatis,[^146] countless brahmanas —
all come to see Gora.

**2381.** A great master at playing the shinga horn,
Balarama Dasa comes, full of thrills.

**2382.** Hundreds of learned gosais appeared,
and my heart began to dance with joy.

**2383.** Some dance, some laugh, some sing songs;
with one mouth that joy cannot be told.

**2384.** Thousands upon thousands, surrounding the Lord,
began the Name, drunk with joy.

**2385.** The Lord went to embrace Murari and Mukunda,
and Gupta[^147] collapsed near his knees.

*[p. 218]*

**2386.** Siddha Krishnadasa came and bowed down,
and the Lord, raising him by the hand, embraced him.

**2387.** Gathering together, all the other devotees too
come to take the Lord with them.

**2388.** All the bands of Vaishnavas play their madals;
in his joy the Lord's eyes grow moist.

**2389.** All the Vaishnavas together sing kirtana,
and Gora, the delight of hearts, dances, swaying his head.

**2390.** Seeing Khanjan, the Lord, crying "Hari bol",
spread his arms and embraced him.

**2391.** Gora began to dance with arms outstretched,
and Sarvabhauma fell prostrate at his feet.

**2392.** With folded hands Sarvabhauma began to say,
"The arrow of separation from you has pierced my heart.

**2393.** I am a great fool; that is why I have borne separation from you
and kept my life in me all these days.

**2394.** In your mercy trample my body with your feet;
then I shall know, Lord, that you love me."

**2395.** Saying this, Sarvabhauma rolls on the ground,
and Gora Raya, raising him, embraces him.

**2396.** Thus, raising the cry of "Hari",
they all set off to Puri, taking the Lord.

*[p. 219]*

**2397.** Hundreds of white, blue and many-coloured banners,
how many kettle-drums sound, rumbling!

**2398.** Some dance, some sing, drunk with joy;
how many people stand pressing close, gazing fixedly!

**2399.** Swaying, Shachi's darling goes along,
while sweet mridangas play, delightful to hear.

**2400.** Raising his arms, Gadadhara began to dance;
Raghunatha Dasa dances, and Damodara too.

**2401.** The Lord asks after Raghunatha affectionately:
"I am very happy to see you."

**2402.** Gora Raya goes to embrace Raghunatha,
but Raghunatha falls and lies prostrate at his feet.

**2403.** On the third day of Magha my Gora Raya[^148]
with all his companions reaches Puri.

**2404.** In the afternoon Mahaprabhu reached Puri,
and millions of people thronged there.

**2405.** With the dust of the road on his feet, the Lord, with many people,
entered the temple and beheld Jagannatha.

**2406.** As he gazed fixedly at Mahavishnu,
tears of love began to stream down.

**2407.** All at once losing consciousness, Gora Raya
immediately fell crashing to the ground.

*[p. 220]*

**2408.** His matted locks came loose, his kaupina slipped,
his body grey with dust, like the poorest of the poor.

**2409.** All around, the devotees raise the cry of "Hari",
and Sarvabhauma lifted him up and held him.

**2410.** His hair bristled like a kadamba,
and sweat flowed in streams.

**2411.** Regaining consciousness, the Lord stood up
and began to gaze fixedly at Mahavishnu.

**2412.** Sarvabhauma says, "Lord, seeing your own form,
you have made the well of your feeling overflow.

**2413.** Seeing your own image, to teach the people,
you began to weep, drunk with great emotion.

**2414.** Before you is the immovable Vishnu, and you are the moving one;[^149]
why then do you weep, Lord, and play so many tricks?

**2415.** You are Krishna in person, the son of the king of Vraja;
why do you blind my eyes with your moods?

**2416.** Put on airs before someone who does not understand,
but do not hide your own form from me.

**2417.** You are the lifter of Govardhana, the lord of Vrindavana,
the life of the gopis, the refuge of the helpless.

**2418.** You were born in the Yadu race, yet they did not recognise you;
the unfortunate Yadavas understood nothing.

*[p. 221]*

**2419.** Having got you in my hands, I will not let you go;
take up the flute and show me your own form.

**2420.** A golden doll is painted on your breast,
by whose radiance your dark form cannot be seen."[^150]

**2421.** The Lord said, "Sarvabhauma, talk of something else.
Why do you talk such idle nonsense?

**2422.** Why do you get excited for nothing and say all sorts of things?
Hearing your words, I put my hands over my ears.

**2423.** Calling me the son of the king of Vraja,
why do you make me an offender without cause?

**2424.** Talk of Krishna in your mouth is like nectar;
speak of Krishna, Bhatta, let my soul be soothed."

**2425.** The Bhatta said, "What you, Lord, make me say —
what else can I say, a beast in human form?"

**2426.** The Lord said, "There is no need for more words;
let us all go together today to our place."[^151]

**2427.** Having seen the arati, to the house of Kashi Mishra
he came with all his companions.

**2428.** Just then Sarvabhauma, bringing prasada,
arrived at that place.

**2429.** Gora, the delight of hearts, distributes the prasada,
and all rejoice on receiving the prasada.

*[p. 222]*

**2430.** In Kashi Mishra's house there is a huge courtyard;
many people come to see the Lord.

**2431.** Staying in Mishra's house, merciful Gora
sings Krishna's glories every day in supreme joy.

**2432.** How can I tell how many people come and go?
Every day there are new joys in Mishra's house.

**2433.** Hearing from people of the Lord's arrival,
how many people of Gauda come to see him!

**2434.** The worthy Bhatta brings prasada every day,
and receiving the prasada, the Lord is filled with joy.

**2435.** Gora, the delight of hearts, joyfully taking the prasada,
distributes the prasada into everyone's hands.

**2436.** Before the prasada there is nama-sankirtana,
and all eat the prasada with loving attachment.

**2437.** "Today we have become blessed" — saying this,
all dance joyfully, clapping their hands.

**2438.** Ramananda, Vasanta and Govinda Charana
took their leave and went to Gauda.

**2439.** Again, to see Gauranga,
hundreds of people come, full of love.

**2440.** Srivasa, Keshava Dasa and Siddha Haridasa
all together come to Chaitanya.

### Translator's notes

[^142]: Mandura (Mandua) is Mandu (Mandavgarh), the fortress-city on the spurs of the Vindhyas above the Narmada; below it is called Mandul.
[^143]: Bhramara is Bamra (Orissa).
[^144]: Rasalkunda is Russellkonda (Bhanjanagar) in southern Orissa; nearby is Kurmakshetra (Srikurmam).
[^145]: Khanjan ("the lame") Acharya is apparently Khanja Bhagavan Acharya, one of Chaitanya's companions at Puri.
[^146]: Giri, puri and sarasvati are names of branches of the order of sannyasis (Dashanami).
[^147]: Murari Gupta.
[^148]: That is, a little more than two years after leaving Puri (the departure was on 7 Vaishakha). The traditional chronology (the *Charitamrita*) allows about two years for the southern pilgrimage.
[^149]: A play on words: Jagannatha is the *achala* (immovable) Vishnu, Chaitanya the *chala* (moving) one.
[^150]: Another formula of the doctrine of Chaitanya as Krishna concealed by the "golden doll", Radha.
[^151]: That is, to the place where they will stay (Kashi Mishra's house).
