# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Five — verses 1–129 (printed pp. 121–161)

**Title of the sarga (from the colophon):** *Navadvīpa-vihāra-varṇana*, "The Description of the Pastimes in Navadvipa".
**Metre:** *praharṣiṇī* (13 syllables per pada); verse 126 — *śikhariṇī*; verse 127 — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita*; verses 128–129 — *drutavilambita*.
**Parallels:** Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 1–2 ("The beginning of sankirtana").

**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–9 — after Gaya: Nimai weeps with love; Shachi asks her son for *prema*; the blessing of the brahmanas.
- 10–14 — He loses the sense of time; at the sound of the Name He falls to the ground; four months of kirtana and teaching.
- 15–21 — **the manifestation of Varaha in the house of Murari Gupta**.
- 22–31 — at Srivasa's: "Do you see Shiva, Skanda and Brahma?"; Advaita comes, and the Lord reveals Himself to Him just as He had resolved; straw between the teeth.
- 32–37 — summer comes; Gaura, radiant, goes to Srivasa.
- 38–50 — the **mahabhisheka** in the house of Srivasa: the bathing with the waters of the Ganga; the Lord sits on the throne of the Deities; light breaks through the chinks.
- 51–52 — all recognise in Him the Lord of the three worlds; a flute sounds.
- 53–60 — the Lord passes into the living quarters; the brothers adorn the house, Gadadhara prepares the bed; the town shines brighter than Ilavrita; a festival for all.
- 61–68 — Gadadhara weaves flower ornaments, dresses and anoints the Lord.
- 69–80 — the wives of the brahmanas are admitted to the Lord: "Always think of Me"; gifts; the Lord gives away cloth, chews betel, accepts offerings.
- 81–90 — Advaita's dance; **Shachi** before the Lord: "He is not your son!"; the touch of His foot, her ecstasy.
- 91–99 — a night of pastimes; the round of the houses of the four brothers; "I am leaving" — the devotees' threat; the Lord falls senseless.
- 100–110 — dawn, a long swoon; kirtana brings Him back; the effulgence withdrawn: "the summer sun has become the autumn moon".
- 111–118 — "I remember nothing"; Srivasa's jest about maya; eleven watches without sleep or food.
- 119–124 — the bath, worship in the house of Srivasa, the return to His mother.
- 125–127 — eight months (from Jyaishtha to Pausha) of dancing in the house of Srivasa.
- 128–129 — Gadadhara is constantly at the Lord's side.

**Textual cruxes:** 52 (*aśobhad aṅgam*), 59 (*pramatham*), 84 (*yogyaṁ ced vayam*), 89 (*spṛṣṭe*, *deha-yaṣṭi*), 112 (the missing *manā*), 129 (the missing *-m a-*) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
आगत्य स्वगृहमथ स्वकीर्त्तनाद्यैः
संरेजे निरवधि रोदनैर्विभिन्नः ।
दृष्ट्वैवंविधमनिशं सविस्मयासी-
दित्येतत्किमिति किमित्यथ प्रसूः सा ॥१॥
```
*āgatya sva-gṛham atha sva-kīrtanādyaiḥ
saṁreje niravadhi rodanair vibhinnaḥ |
dṛṣṭvaivaṁ-vidham aniśaṁ sa-vismayāsīd
ity etat kim iti kim ity atha prasūḥ sā ||1||*

**Word for word:** *āgatya* — having come; *sva* — His own; *gṛham* — to the home; *atha* — then; *sva* — with His own; *kīrtana* — kirtana; *ādyaiḥ* — and the like; *saṁreje* — shone; *niravadhi* — without limit; *rodanaiḥ* — by sobs; *vibhinnaḥ* — shaken; *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *evaṁvidham* — such; *aniśam* — constantly; *sa* — with; *vismayā* — wonder; *āsīt* — was; *iti* — thus; *etat* — this; *kim* — what; *iti* — thus; *kim* — what; *iti* — thus; *atha* — and; *prasūḥ* — the mother; *sā* — she.

**Translation.** Having then come to His home, He shone without limit with His kirtana and the like, shaken by sobs. Seeing Him constantly so, His mother was filled with wonder: "What is this? What is it?"

### 2

**2.**

```
यामिन्यां शयितवतः शची कदाचित्
पुत्रस्य प्रथममवेक्ष्य रोदनं सा ।
ब्रूहीत्थं किमहह तात रोदिषि त्वं
साशङ्कं तमिति जगाद भूरिभाग्या ॥२॥
```
*yāminyāṁ śayitavataḥ śacī kadācit
putrasya prathamam avekṣya rodanaṁ sā |
brūhītthaṁ kim ahaha tāta rodiṣi tvaṁ
sāśaṅkaṁ tam iti jagāda bhūri-bhāgyā ||2||*

**Word for word:** *yāminyām* — at night; *śayitavataḥ* — who was lying [in bed]; *śacī* — Shachi; *kadācit* — once; *putrasya* — of the son; *prathamam* — for the first time; *avekṣya* — having seen; *rodanam* — the weeping; *sā* — she; *brūhi* — tell; *ittham* — so; *kim* — why; *ahaha* — ah; *tāta* — O dear child; *rodiṣi* — weep; *tvam* — You; *sa* — with; *āśaṅkam* — anxiety; *tam* — to Him; *iti* — thus; *jagāda* — said; *bhūri* — of great; *bhāgyā* — [she] of fortune.

**Translation.** Once at night Shachi, rich in great fortune, seeing for the first time the weeping of her son as He lay [in bed], said to Him anxiously: "Ah, O dear child, tell me — why do You weep so?"

### 3

**3.**

```
तच्छ्रुत्वा न किमपि चेदुवाच नाथः
प्रेमार्द्रो नयनजलाभिषिक्तसर्व्वगात्रः ।
सात्यन्तं निरवधि चिन्तिता तदासी-
त्प्रेमेत्येतदपि विवेद दैवयोगात् ॥३॥
```
*tac chrutvā na kim api ced uvāca nāthaḥ
premārdro nayana-jalābhiṣikta-sarva-gātraḥ |
sātyantaṁ niravadhi cintitā tadāsīt
premety etad api viveda daiva-yogāt ||3||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — that; *śrutvā* — having heard; *na* — not; *kim api* — anything; *cet* — when; *uvāca* — answered; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *prema* — by love; *ārdraḥ* — softened; *nayana* — of the eyes; *jala* — by the water; *abhiṣikta* — bathed; *sarva* — [with] the whole; *gātraḥ* — body; *sā* — she; *atyantam* — extremely; *niravadhi* — boundlessly; *cintitā* — anxious; *tadā* — then; *āsīt* — was; *prema* — "[it is] prema"; *iti* — thus; *etat* — this; *api* — too; *viveda* — understood; *daiva* — of providence; *yogāt* — by the dispensation.

**Translation.** When, hearing that, the Lord answered nothing — softened by love, His whole body bathed in the water of His eyes — she became extremely, boundlessly anxious; and then, by the dispensation of providence, she understood this too: "It is *prema*, love."

### 4

**4.**

```
ज्ञात्वैतद्विमलमनाः शची तनूजं
सार्द्राक्षी विनयपरा भृशं ययाचे ।
मह्यं यद्धनमखिलं प्रयच्छसि त्वं
प्रेमाख्यं किमु न ददासि साम्प्रतं तत् ॥४॥
```
*jñātvaitad vimala-manāḥ śacī tanūjaṁ
sārdrākṣī vinaya-parā bhṛśaṁ yayāce |
mahyaṁ yad dhanam akhilaṁ prayacchasi tvaṁ
premākhyaṁ kimu na dadāsi sāmprataṁ tat ||4||*

**Word for word:** *jñātvā* — having understood; *etat* — this; *vimala* — [with] a pure; *manāḥ* — heart; *śacī* — Shachi; *tanūjam* — the son; *sa* — with; *ārdra* — moist; *akṣī* — [she] with eyes; *vinaya* — to humility; *parā* — devoted; *bhṛśam* — earnestly; *yayāce* — begged; *mahyam* — to me; *yat* — which; *dhanam* — wealth; *akhilam* — all; *prayacchasi* — give; *tvam* — You; *prema* — prema; *ākhyam* — called; *kimu* — why; *na* — not; *dadāsi* — give; *sāmpratam* — now; *tat* — that.

**Translation.** Understanding this, pure-hearted Shachi, her eyes moist, humbly and earnestly begged her son: "You give me all the wealth [You have] — why then do You not now give [me] that [wealth] called *prema*?"

### 5

**5.**

```
देवानामविदितमेतदत्यलभ्यं
प्रेमेदं यदवगतं त्वया गयायाम् ।
दीनायै तदिह हि मे प्रयच्छ तात
स्नेहस्ते यदि मयि तिष्ठति क्षणञ्च ॥५॥
```
*devānām aviditam etad aty-alabhyaṁ
premedaṁ yad avagataṁ tvayā gayāyām |
dīnāyai tad iha hi me prayaccha tāta
snehas te yadi mayi tiṣṭhati kṣaṇañ ca ||5||*

**Word for word:** *devānām* — to the gods; *aviditam* — unknown; *etat* — this; *ati* — exceedingly; *alabhyam* — hard to attain; *prema* — love; *idam* — this; *yat* — which; *avagatam* — obtained; *tvayā* — by You; *gayāyām* — at Gaya; *dīnāyai* — to the wretched; *tat* — that; *iha* — here; *hi* — indeed; *me* — to me; *prayaccha* — give; *tāta* — O dear child; *snehaḥ* — affection; *te* — Your; *yadi* — if; *mayi* — for me; *tiṣṭhati* — remains; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment; *ca* — even.

**Translation.** "This love which You obtained at Gaya — unknown to the gods, exceedingly hard to attain — give it here to me, a wretched woman, O dear child, if Your affection for me remains even for a moment."

### 6

**6.**

```
इत्यस्या गिरमधिगम्य गौरचन्द्रः
स्नेहार्द्रः प्रतिवचनं ददौ जनन्यै ।
तन्मातस्तव भविता चिरेण नूनं
यत्ते स्याद्गुरुतरवैष्णवानुकम्पा ॥६॥
```
*ity asyā giram adhigamya gauracandraḥ
snehārdraḥ prativacanaṁ dadau jananyai |
tan mātas tava bhavitā cireṇa nūnaṁ
yat te syād gurutara-vaiṣṇavānukampā ||6||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *asyāḥ* — her; *giram* — speech; *adhigamya* — having heard; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra; *sneha* — by affection; *ārdraḥ* — softened; *prativacanam* — a reply; *dadau* — gave; *jananyai* — to the mother; *tat* — that; *mātaḥ* — O Mother; *tava* — yours; *bhavitā* — will be; *cireṇa* — in time; *nūnam* — surely; *yat* — when; *te* — to you; *syāt* — there will be; *gurutara* — very great; *vaiṣṇava* — of the Vaishnavas; *anukampā* — the mercy.

**Translation.** Hearing her words thus, Gaurachandra, softened by affection, gave His mother a reply: "O Mother, it will surely be yours in time, when the very great mercy of the Vaishnavas comes to you."

**Note.** An important theological motif: even the mother of God receives prema not from Him directly but by the mercy of His devotees. Cf. Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 22 ("The forgiveness of Shachidevi"): Shachi receives forgiveness for her offence against Advaita.

### 7

**7.**

```
तच्छ्रुत्वा मुदमधिकां ययौ ततः सा
तन्नूनं मम भवितेति हृष्टचित्ता ।
गौराङ्गोऽपि तदधिगम्य मातृचित्तं
विप्रेन्द्रान् विनयपरो जगाद भूयः ॥७॥
```
*tac chrutvā mudam adhikāṁ yayau tataḥ sā
tan nūnaṁ mama bhaviteti hṛṣṭa-cittā |
gaurāṅgo 'pi tad adhigamya mātṛ-cittaṁ
viprendrān vinaya-paro jagāda bhūyaḥ ||7||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — that; *śrutvā* — having heard; *mudam* — joy; *adhikām* — great; *yayau* — attained; *tataḥ* — then; *sā* — she; *tat* — that; *nūnam* — surely; *mama* — mine; *bhavitā* — will be; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *hṛṣṭa* — [with] a delighted; *cittā* — heart; *gaurāṅgaḥ* — Gauranga; *api* — and; *tat* — that; *adhigamya* — perceiving; *mātṛ* — of the mother; *cittam* — the heart; *vipra* — of brahmanas; *indrān* — to the best; *vinaya* — to humility; *paraḥ* — devoted; *jagāda* — spoke; *bhūyaḥ* — further.

**Translation.** Hearing that, she attained great joy, her heart delighted: "Then it will surely be mine!" And Gauranga, perceiving His mother's heart, humbly spoke further to the best of the brahmanas:

### 8

**8.**

```
प्रेमायं निरवधि मृग्यते जनन्या
भक्तिश्च प्रभुचरणे गरीयसीयम् ।
ते स्यातां सपदि यथाशिषो भवद्भि-
र्युज्यन्तां तदनु तथोचुरेवमेते ॥८॥
```
*premāyaṁ niravadhi mṛgyate jananyā
bhaktiś ca prabhu-caraṇe garīyasīyam |
te syātāṁ sapadi yathāśiṣo bhavadbhir
yujyantāṁ tad anu tathocur evam ete ||8||*

**Word for word:** *premā* — love; *ayam* — this; *niravadhi* — boundlessly; *mṛgyate* — is sought; *jananyā* — by [My] mother; *bhaktiḥ* — devotion; *ca* — and; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *caraṇe* — to the feet; *garīyasī* — most weighty; *iyam* — this; *te* — these two; *syātām* — may be; *sapadi* — soon; *yathā* — so that; *āśiṣaḥ* — blessings; *bhavadbhiḥ* — by you; *yujyantām* — let be bestowed; *tat anu* — after that; *tathā* — accordingly; *ūcuḥ* — spoke; *evam* — thus; *ete* — they.

**Translation.** "My mother seeks this love boundlessly, and this most weighty devotion to the feet of the Lord. Let blessings be bestowed by you, so that both may soon be hers." And thereupon they spoke accordingly.

### 9

**9.**

```
इत्येवं क्वचन रुदन्विलोचनाभ्यां
धाराणां शतशतमादधात्युरःसु ।
श्लेष्माणं क्षिपति मुहुर्मुहुः स्थविष्ठं
नासाभ्यां भुवि विलुठन्क्वचित्स नाथः ॥९॥
```
*ity evaṁ kvacana rudan vilocanābhyāṁ
dhārāṇāṁ śata-śatam ādadhāty uraḥsu |
śleṣmāṇaṁ kṣipati muhur muhuḥ sthaviṣṭhaṁ
nāsābhyāṁ bhuvi viluṭhan kvacit sa nāthaḥ ||9||*

**Word for word:** *iti evam* — thus; *kvacana* — sometimes; *rudan* — weeping; *vilocanābhyām* — from the eyes; *dhārāṇām* — of streams; *śata-śatam* — hundreds upon hundreds; *ādadhāti* — pours; *uraḥsu* — on the breast; *śleṣmāṇam* — mucus; *kṣipati* — expels; *muhuḥ muhuḥ* — again and again; *sthaviṣṭham* — copious; *nāsābhyām* — from the nose; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *viluṭhan* — rolling; *kvacit* — sometimes; *saḥ* — He; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord.

**Translation.** Thus the Lord, sometimes weeping, would pour from His eyes hundreds upon hundreds of streams on His breast; sometimes, rolling on the ground, He would again and again expel copious mucus from His nose.

**Note.** Kavi Karnapura is not afraid of the "unlovely" bodily details of ecstasy — tears, mucus from the nose, rolling on the ground. This is deliberate realism: the Chaitanya-bhagavata and the Chaitanya-charitamrita write in the same way. *Uraḥsu* is plural, literally "on the breasts" — a poetic plural, or "on the breasts [of those beside Him]".

### 10

**10.**

```
प्रत्यूषप्रभृति दिनं समस्तमेव
प्रेमाश्रुप्रचुररवै रुदन्विनीय ।
यामिन्या भवति सति प्रभुः प्रबोधे
वैकल्याद्दिनमिति तर्कयाम्बभूव ॥१०॥
```
*pratyūṣa-prabhṛti dinaṁ samastam eva
premāśru-pracura-ravai rudan vinīya |
yāminyā bhavati sati prabhuḥ prabodhe
vaikalyād dinam iti tarkayāmbabhūva ||10||*

**Word for word:** *pratyūṣa* — from dawn; *prabhṛti* — onward; *dinam* — the day; *samastam* — whole; *eva* — indeed; *prema* — of love; *aśru* — with tears; *pracura* — abundant; *ravaiḥ* — with cries; *rudan* — weeping; *vinīya* — having passed; *yāminyā* — night ⟨?⟩; *bhavati sati* — [when] it had come; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *prabodhe* — on awakening; *vaikalyāt* — from agitation; *dinam* — day; *iti* — thus; *tarkayāmbabhūva* — supposed.

**Translation.** Having passed the whole day from dawn onward weeping, with loud cries and abundant tears of love, the Lord, when night had come and He awoke [to outer consciousness], in His agitation supposed that it was day.

**Note.** *Yāminyā bhavati sati* is irregular; the sense of a locative absolute is required: "when night had come".

### 11

**11.**

```
सन्ध्यायां किमपि रुदन्विमुक्तकण्ठः
प्रातः स्यात्कथमपि चेद्बहिःप्रबोधः ।
तन्नक्तं व्रजति कियत्कदेति गौरो
वैकल्याद्वदति न तस्य कालभेदः ॥११॥
```
*sandhyāyāṁ kim api rudan vimukta-kaṇṭhaḥ
prātaḥ syāt katham api ced bahiḥ-prabodhaḥ |
tan naktaṁ vrajati kiyat kadeti gauro
vaikalyād vadati na tasya kāla-bhedaḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *sandhyāyām* — at twilight; *kim api* — somehow; *rudan* — weeping; *vimukta* — [with] unrestrained; *kaṇṭhaḥ* — voice; *prātaḥ* — in the morning; *syāt* — there should be; *katham api* — somehow; *cet* — if; *bahiḥ* — outer; *prabodhaḥ* — consciousness; *tat* — then; *naktam* — the night; *vrajati* — is passing; *kiyat* — how much; *kadā* — when; *iti* — thus; *gauraḥ* — Gaura; *vaikalyāt* — from agitation; *vadati* — says; *na* — not; *tasya* — for Him; *kāla* — of time; *bhedaḥ* — distinction.

**Translation.** At twilight He wept somehow with unrestrained voice; and if in the morning outer consciousness somehow returned to Him, Gaura in His agitation would ask: "So the night is passing? How much [is left]? When [will it end]?" For Him there was no distinction of time.

**Note.** In the edition the first pada is a syllable short of the metre; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *kim api rudan vimukta-kaṇṭhaḥ* ("somehow weeping with unrestrained voice"), and the missing *rudan* is restored.

### 12

**12.**

```
नामैकं श्रवणपथं यदैव गच्छे-
त्तत्सोऽयं भुवि विलुठन्बलप्रकामम् ।
द्राघिष्ठैः श्वसनसमीरणैः सकम्पै-
र्नेत्रान्तप्रसृमरधारया च रेजे ॥१२॥
```
*nāmaikaṁ śravaṇa-pathaṁ yadaiva gacchet
tat so 'yaṁ bhuvi viluṭhan bala-prakāmam |
drāghiṣṭhaiḥ śvasana-samīraṇaiḥ sa-kampair
netrānta-prasṛmara-dhārayā ca reje ||12||*

**Word for word:** *nāma* — Name; *ekam* — a single; *śravaṇa* — of hearing; *patham* — the path; *yadā eva* — as soon as; *gacchet* — entered; *tat* — then; *saḥ* — He; *ayam* — this; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *viluṭhan* — rolling; *bala* — of strength; *prakāmam* — to the full; *drāghiṣṭhaiḥ* — with very long; *śvasana* — of breath; *samīraṇaiḥ* — with the blasts; *sa* — with; *kampaiḥ* — trembling; *netra* — of the eyes; *anta* — from the corners; *prasṛmara* — flowing forth; *dhārayā* — with a stream; *ca* — and; *reje* — shone.

**Translation.** As soon as a single Name entered the path of His hearing, He would roll on the ground with all His strength, and shone with long, trembling sighs and with a stream flowing from the corners of His eyes.

### 13

**13.**

```
सोत्कण्ठं निरवधि कृष्ण कृष्ण कृष्णे-
त्याजल्पन्क्वचन विभिन्नसन्नकण्ठः ।
हर्षोद्भवैस्तनुरुहसञ्चयैर्विभाति
प्रायोऽयं प्रतिदिनमेवमेव भूत्वा ॥१३॥
```
*sotkaṇṭhaṁ niravadhi kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇety
ājalpan kvacana vibhinna-sanna-kaṇṭhaḥ |
harṣodbhavais tanuruha-sañcayair vibhāti
prāyo 'yaṁ pratidinam evam eva bhūtvā ||13||*

**Word for word:** *sa* — with; *utkaṇṭham* — longing; *niravadhi* — without end; *kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇa* — "Krishna, Krishna, Krishna"; *iti* — thus; *ājalpan* — murmuring; *kvacana* — sometimes; *vibhinna* — [with] broken; *sanna* — choked; *kaṇṭhaḥ* — voice; *harṣa* — from joy; *udbhavaiḥ* — risen; *tanuruha* — of the hairs of the body; *sañcayaiḥ* — with the masses; *vibhāti* — shines; *prāyaḥ* — almost; *ayam* — He; *pratidinam* — every day; *evam eva* — just so; *bhūtvā* — being.

**Translation.** Murmuring with longing, without end, "Krishna, Krishna, Krishna!", sometimes with a broken and choked voice, He shone with the hairs of His body standing on end in masses from joy. Such He was almost every day.

### 14

**14.**

```
स स्नात्वा दिवसमुखे करोति पूजा-
मश्नाति प्रतिदिवसं मुदा निवेद्य ।
सद्विप्रानपि परिपाठयन्नुदारान्
माघाद्यानिति चतुरो निनाय मासान् ॥१४॥
```
*sa snātvā divasa-mukhe karoti pūjām
aśnāti pratidivasaṁ mudā nivedya |
sad-viprān api paripāṭhayann udārān
māghādyān iti caturo nināya māsān ||14||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — He; *snātvā* — having bathed; *divasa* — of the day; *mukhe* — at the beginning; *karoti* — performs; *pūjām* — worship; *aśnāti* — eats; *pratidivasam* — every day; *mudā* — with joy; *nivedya* — having offered [to God]; *sat* — worthy; *viprān* — brahmanas; *api* — also; *paripāṭhayan* — teaching; *udārān* — noble; *māgha* — with Magha; *ādyān* — beginning; *iti* — thus; *caturaḥ* — four; *nināya* — spent; *māsān* — months.

**Translation.** Bathing at the beginning of the day, He performed worship; every day He ate with joy what had been offered [to God]; and teaching also the noble and worthy brahmanas, He thus spent four months, beginning with Magha.

**Note.** The four months are Magha, Phalguna, Chaitra and Vaishakha (January–May).

### 15

**15.**

```
प्रेमार्द्रः सपुलकमेकदा मुरारे-
र्वैद्यस्यालयमगमत्कृपासमुद्रः ।
तत्रासौ सपदि निवेश्य देवगेहे
संभिन्नो नयनजलैः समध्यवात्सीत् ॥१५॥
```
*premārdraḥ sa-pulakam ekadā murārer
vaidyasyālayam agamat kṛpā-samudraḥ |
tatrāsau sapadi niveśya deva-gehe
saṁbhinno nayana-jalaiḥ samadhyavātsīt ||15||*

**Word for word:** *prema* — by love; *ārdraḥ* — softened; *sa* — with; *pulakam* — hairs standing on end; *ekadā* — once; *murāreḥ* — of Murari; *vaidyasya* — of the physician; *ālayam* — to the house; *agamat* — went; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *samudraḥ* — the ocean; *tatra* — there; *asau* — He; *sapadi* — at once; *niveśya* — having entered; *deva* — of God; *gehe* — in the house; *saṁbhinnaḥ* — convulsed; *nayana* — of the eyes; *jalaiḥ* — with the waters; *samadhyavātsīt* — settled.

**Translation.** Once the ocean of mercy, softened by love, His hairs standing on end, went to the house of Murari the physician. There He at once entered the shrine of the house and settled there, convulsed with tears.

**Note.** Murari Gupta is a physician (*vaidya*), Nimai's boyhood friend and the author of the earliest biography (the Kadacha). The episode of Varaha's manifestation in Murari's house is found in the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 3) and in Murari Gupta himself.

### 16

**16.**

```
आश्चर्यं दशनयुगेन गां बलीया-
न्वाराहं वपुरिदमावहन्क एषः ।
मर्म्मस्पृक्तुदति महामहीध्रतुल्यो
भूयोऽसाविति निगदन्ससर्प पश्चात् ॥१६॥
```
*āścaryaṁ daśana-yugena gāṁ balīyān
vārāhaṁ vapur idam āvahan ka eṣaḥ |
marma-spṛk tudati mahā-mahīdhra-tulyo
bhūyo 'sāv iti nigadan sasarpa paścāt ||16||*

**Word for word:** *āścaryam* — a wonder; *daśana* — of tusks; *yugena* — with the pair; *gām* — the earth; *balīyān* — mighty; *vārāham* — of a boar; *vapuḥ* — the body; *idam* — this; *āvahan* — bearing; *kaḥ* — who; *eṣaḥ* — this; *marma* — the vitals; *spṛk* — reaching; *tudati* — pierces; *mahā* — a great; *mahīdhra* — mountain; *tulyaḥ* — like; *bhūyaḥ* — ever more; *asau* — He; *iti* — thus; *nigadan* — saying; *sasarpa* — crept; *paścāt* — backwards.

**Translation.** "What a wonder! Who is this mighty One who bears this boar's body, [holding up] the earth on a pair of tusks? Like a great mountain, He pierces [Me] ever more, reaching to the very vitals!" Saying this, He crept backwards.

**Note.** **The manifestation of Varaha:** Mahaprabhu takes on the mood of Varaha, the boar avatara who raised the Earth from the waters on His tusks. The edition has *bhūye*; we read *bhūyo* ("ever more, again") with the Gosvami edition (GGM).

### 17

**17.**

```
इत्युक्त्वा सपदि तथा तदीयभावं
संगृह्णन्भुवि भुजजानुभिर्व्रजन्सः ।
घूर्णाभिस्तरलतरेण दृग्युगेन
द्राघिष्ठामपि विदधे च हूंकृतिं ताम् ॥१७॥
```
*ity uktvā sapadi tathā tadīya-bhāvaṁ
saṅgṛhṇan bhuvi bhuja-jānubhir vrajan saḥ |
ghūrṇābhis taralatareṇa dṛg-yugena
drāghiṣṭhām api vidadhe ca hūṅkṛtiṁ tām ||17||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *uktvā* — having said; *sapadi* — at once; *tathā* — so; *tadīya* — His [Varaha's]; *bhāvam* — mood; *saṅgṛhṇan* — taking on; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *bhuja* — on the arms; *jānubhiḥ* — and knees; *vrajan* — moving; *saḥ* — He; *ghūrṇābhiḥ* — with rollings; *taralatareṇa* — very restless; *dṛk* — of eyes; *yugena* — with the pair; *drāghiṣṭhām* — very long; *api* — also; *vidadhe* — made; *ca* — and; *hūṅkṛtim* — the roar "hum"; *tām* — that.

**Translation.** Having said this, He at once took on His mood: moving over the ground on His arms and knees, His very restless eyes rolling, He also let out that long roar, "Hum!"

### 18

**18.**

```
दन्ताग्रैः सपदि स पैत्तलाम्बुपात्रं
धृत्वासौ बहुभयमुन्मुखोऽतिदूरे ।
संक्षिप्यंस्तदनु मुरारिगुप्तमूचे
रूपं मे सहजमुदीरयेति शश्वत् ॥१८॥
```
*dantāgraiḥ sapadi sa paittalāmbu-pātraṁ
dhṛtvāsau bahu-bhayam unmukho 'ti-dūre |
saṅkṣipyaṁs tad-anu murāri-guptam ūce
rūpaṁ me sahajam udīrayeti śaśvat ||18||*

**Word for word:** *danta* — of the teeth; *agraiḥ* — with the tips; *sapadi* — at once; *saḥ* — He; *paittala* — brass; *ambu* — for water; *pātram* — a vessel; *dhṛtvā* — having seized; *asau* — He; *bahu* — great; *bhayam* — fear [inspiring]; *unmukhaḥ* — raising His head; *ati* — very; *dūre* — far; *saṅkṣipyan* — flinging; *tat anu* — after that; *murāri-guptam* — to Murari Gupta; *ūce* — said; *rūpam* — the form; *me* — My; *sahajam* — natural; *udīraya* — describe; *iti* — thus; *śaśvat* — insistently.

**Translation.** At once He seized a brass water-vessel with the tips of His teeth and, inspiring great fear, raised His head and flung it far away. After that He said insistently to Murari Gupta: "Describe My natural form!"

**Note.** The vessel lifted with the teeth is the image of Varaha raising the Earth on His tusks.

### 19

**19.**

```
तच्छ्रुत्वा भुवि निपतन्स भीतभीतो
नो विद्मो वयमिह ते स्वरूपमेतत् ।
आत्मानं स्वयमेवमात्मनैव वेत्थे-
त्यूचेऽसौ प्रतिवचनैश्च गीतयोक्तैः ॥१९॥
```
*tac chrutvā bhuvi nipatan sa bhīta-bhīto
no vidmo vayam iha te svarūpam etat |
ātmānaṁ svayam evam ātmanaiva vettha
ity ūce 'sau prativacanaiś ca gītayoktaiḥ ||19||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — that; *śrutvā* — having heard; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *nipatan* — falling; *saḥ* — he; *bhīta-bhītaḥ* — terror-stricken; *na u* — not; *vidmaḥ* — know; *vayam* — we; *iha* — here; *te* — Your; *svarūpam* — own form; *etat* — this; *ātmānam* — Yourself; *svayam* — Yourself; *evam* — thus; *ātmanā* — by Yourself; *eva* — alone; *vettha* — know; *iti* — thus; *ūce* — said; *asau* — he; *prativacanaiḥ* — with a reply; *ca* — and; *gītayā* — by the Gita; *uktaiḥ* — spoken.

**Translation.** Hearing that, Murari fell to the ground, terror-stricken: "We do not know this own form of Yours. You Yourself know Yourself thus, by Yourself alone." So he answered, with words spoken in the Gita.

**Note.** Murari quotes the Bhagavad-gita (10.15): *svayam evātmanātmānaṁ vettha tvaṁ puruṣottama* — "You Yourself know Yourself by Yourself, O Supreme Person."

### 20

**20.**

```
भूयोऽसौ स हसितवन्मधुद्रवैस्तैः
प्रत्यूचे प्रतिवचनैः प्रभुस्तमेनम् ।
वेदोऽयं ननु किमु वेत्त्ययं विमुग्ध-
संमोहादवचिनुतेऽन्धवत्स नित्यम् ॥२०॥
```
*bhūyo 'sau sa hasitavan madhu-dravais taiḥ
pratyūce prativacanaiḥ prabhus tam enam |
vedo 'yaṁ nanu kimu vetty ayaṁ vimugdha-
saṁmohād avacinute 'ndhavat sa nityam ||20||*

**Word for word:** *bhūyaḥ* — again; *asau* — He; *saḥ* — that; *hasitavat* — smiling; *madhu* — of honey; *dravaiḥ* — moist; *taiḥ* — with those; *pratyūce* — replied; *prativacanaiḥ* — with words; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *tam enam* — to him; *vedaḥ* — the Veda; *ayam* — this; *nanu* — indeed; *kimu* — what; *vetti* — knows; *ayam* — it; *vimugdha* — of the bewildered; *saṁmohāt* — out of confusion; *avacinute* — gropes; *andhavat* — like a blind man; *saḥ* — it; *nityam* — eternally.

**Translation.** Then the Lord, smiling, replied to him in words moist with honey: "What, indeed, does this Veda know? Out of bewildered confusion it eternally gropes [for Me] like a blind man."

### 21

**21.**

```
इत्युक्त्वा श्रुतिगदितं निपठ्य भूयः
सोत्प्रासं स परिहसन्नुवाच नाथः ।
वेदानामिह खलु नास्ति शक्तिरेषा
ज्ञातुं मामिति निगदन्ययौ स्वगेहम् ॥२१॥
```
*ity uktvā śruti-gaditaṁ nipaṭhya bhūyaḥ
sotprāsaṁ sa parihasann uvāca nāthaḥ |
vedānām iha khalu nāsti śaktir eṣā
jñātuṁ mām iti nigadan yayau sva-geham ||21||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *uktvā* — having spoken; *śruti* — by the Shruti; *gaditam* — what is declared; *nipaṭhya* — having recited; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *sa* — with; *utprāsam* — mockery; *saḥ* — He; *parihasan* — laughing; *uvāca* — said; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *vedānām* — of the Vedas; *iha* — here; *khalu* — truly; *na asti* — there is not; *śaktiḥ* — power; *eṣā* — this; *jñātum* — to know; *mām* — Me; *iti* — thus; *nigadan* — saying; *yayau* — went; *sva* — to His own; *geham* — home.

**Translation.** Having spoken thus and recited once more what the Shruti declares, the Lord, laughing, said with mockery: "Truly, the Vedas have no such power to know Me." And saying this, He went to His own home.

**Note.** Which text of the Shruti is meant is not specified. A probable parallel is Taittiriya Upanishad 2.4: *yato vāco nivartante aprāpya manasā saha* ("from which words turn back, together with the mind, without reaching it").

### 22

**22.**

```
अन्येद्युः स्वगृहमभि क्षपेशकोटि-
श्रीयुक्तः परपरभागभाक्प्रतीकः ।
श्रीवासं निजपुरतः स्थितं महस्वा-
नभ्यूचे सह बलहुङ्कृतैर्वचोभिः ॥२२॥
```
*anyedyuḥ sva-gṛham abhi kṣapeśa-koṭi-
śrī-yuktaḥ para-para-bhāga-bhāk pratīkaḥ |
śrīvāsaṁ nija-purataḥ sthitaṁ mahasvān
abhyūce saha bala-huṅkṛtair vacobhiḥ ||22||*

**Word for word:** *anyedyuḥ* — on the next day; *sva* — His own; *gṛham abhi* — at the house; *kṣapeśa* — of moons; *koṭi* — of millions; *śrī* — with the beauty; *yuktaḥ* — endowed; *para-para* — the very highest; *bhāga* — excellence; *bhāk* — possessing; *pratīkaḥ* — [with] a form; *śrīvāsam* — to Srivasa; *nija* — Himself; *purataḥ* — before; *sthitam* — standing; *mahasvān* — radiant; *abhyūce* — spoke; *saha* — with; *bala* — mighty; *huṅkṛtaiḥ* — with roars; *vacobhiḥ* — with words.

**Translation.** The next day, at His own house, He — endowed with the beauty of millions of moons, His form possessing the very highest excellence, all radiant — spoke to Srivasa, who stood before Him, in words accompanied by mighty roars:

**Note.** *Kṣapeśa*, "lord of the night", is the moon.

### 23

**23.**

```
त्वं भोः पश्यसि न किमत्र पञ्चवक्त्रान्
षड्वक्त्रानपि च चतुर्मुखान्समेतान् ।
सोऽप्यूचे न खलु विलोक्यते मयासौ
षड्वक्त्रप्रभृतिजनः समागतोऽयम् ॥२३॥
```
*tvaṁ bhoḥ paśyasi na kim atra pañca-vaktrān
ṣaḍ-vaktrān api ca catur-mukhān sametān |
so 'py ūce na khalu vilokyate mayāsau
ṣaḍ-vaktra-prabhṛti-janaḥ samāgato 'yam ||23||*

**Word for word:** *tvam* — you; *bhoḥ* — O [friend] (hey); *paśyasi* — see; *na kim* — do not; *atra* — here; *pañca* — five; *vaktrān* — the faced ones; *ṣaṭ* — six; *vaktrān* — the faced ones; *api ca* — and also; *catuḥ* — four; *mukhān* — the faced ones; *sametān* — assembled; *saḥ* — he; *api* — for his part; *ūce* — said; *na khalu* — not at all; *vilokyate* — is seen; *mayā* — by me; *asau* — this; *ṣaṭ* — six; *vaktra* — faced; *prabhṛti* — and the rest; *janaḥ* — company; *samāgataḥ* — assembled; *ayam* — this.

**Translation.** "Hey, do you not see the five-faced, the six-faced and the four-faced assembled here?" And he replied: "No, I do not see this company assembled here — the six-faced and the rest."

**Note.** The five-faced is Shiva, the six-faced Kartikeya (Skanda), the four-faced Brahma: the gods have come to see the Lord. Compare 2.86, where the invisible guests in the house are seen by Shachi alone.

### 24

**24.**

```
इत्युक्ते सति तदनूपतस्थिवांसं
नाम्ना श्रीपतिमनुजं ददर्श विप्रः ।
सोऽभ्येत्य श्रुतिनिकटेषु धीरमूचे-
ऽद्वैतस्यागमनकथां प्रभुं दिदृक्षोः ॥२४॥

'dvaitasyāgamana-kathāṁ prabhuṁ didṛkṣoḥ ||24||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *ukte sati* — when it had been said; *tat* — behind him; *anūpatasthivāṁsam* — standing; *nāmnā* — by name; *śrīpatim* — Shripati; *anujam* — the younger brother; *dadarśa* — saw; *vipraḥ* — the brahmana; *saḥ* — he; *abhyetya* — having approached; *śruti* — to the ears; *nikaṭeṣu* — close; *dhīram* — softly; *ūce* — told; *advaitasya* — of Advaita; *āgamana* — of the arrival; *kathām* — the news; *prabhum* — the Lord; *didṛkṣoḥ* — wishing to see.

**Translation.** When this had been said, the brahmana saw his younger brother, Shripati by name, standing behind him. Shripati came up and softly, close to his ear, told him the news that Advaita had arrived, wishing to see the Lord.

**Note.** Shripati is one of Srivasa's brothers (Srivasa, Sri Rama, Shripati, Shrinidhi). The arrival of Advaita: Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 6 ("The meeting with Sri Advaita").

### 25

**25.**

```
आचार्यः किमिह समागतोऽस्ति तस्यै
तज्ज्ञात्वा सपदि समुत्थितोऽजिरेषु ।
आगत्य प्रतिपदहूंकृतां स वाणीं
प्रत्यूचे महितमहामहःसमूहः ॥२५॥
```
*ācāryaḥ kim iha samāgato 'sti tasyai
taj jñātvā sapadi samutthito 'jireṣu |
āgatya pratipada-hūṅkṛtāṁ sa vāṇīṁ
pratyūce mahita-mahā-mahaḥ-samūhaḥ ||25||*

**Word for word:** *ācāryaḥ* — the Acharya; *kim* — whether; *iha* — here; *samāgataḥ* — come; *asti* — has; *tasyai* — ⟨?⟩; *tat* — that; *jñātvā* — having learned; *sapadi* — at once; *samutthitaḥ* — risen; *ajireṣu* — into the courtyard; *āgatya* — having come; *pratipada* — at every word; *hūṅkṛtām* — with roars; *saḥ* — He; *vāṇīm* — speech; *pratyūce* — spoke in reply; *mahita* — of glorious; *mahā* — great; *mahaḥ* — effulgence; *samūhaḥ* — a mass.

**Translation.** "Has the Acharya come here?" Learning that it was so, He at once rose, came out into the courtyard and — a mass of glorious, great effulgence — made reply in words broken by a roar at every one:

**Note.** ⟨?⟩ *Tasyai* (dative feminine) is unclear; perhaps it stands for *tasmai*, "to him". The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text. Grammatically *samutthitaḥ*, *āgatya* and *pratyūce* share one subject, *saḥ … mahita-mahā-mahaḥ-samūhaḥ*, that is, the Lord; the reply follows in verse 26.

### 26

**26.**

```
ते ज्ञास्यन्त्यहह सपद्यमुत्र ये ये
यास्यन्ति क्ष्मामधुनाधिकारहीनाः ।
इत्युक्त्वा गुरुतरहूंकृतैर्विभिन्नः

```
*te jñāsyanty ahaha sapady amutra ye ye
yāsyanti kṣmām adhunādhikāra-hīnāḥ |
ity uktvā gurutara-hūṅkṛtair vibhinnaḥ
śrīvāsālayam agamad drutaṁ prabhuḥ saḥ ||26||*

**Word for word:** *te* — they; *jñāsyanti* — will know; *ahaha* — ah; *sapadi* — soon; *amutra* — there (in the next world); *ye ye* — all who; *yāsyanti* — will go; *kṣmām* — to the earth; *adhunā* — now; *adhikāra* — of qualification; *hīnāḥ* — devoid; *iti* — thus; *uktvā* — having said; *gurutara* — with heavy; *hūṅkṛtaiḥ* — with roars; *vibhinnaḥ* — shaken; *śrīvāsa* — of Srivasa; *ālayam* — to the house; *agamat* — went; *drutam* — swiftly; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** ⟨?⟩ "Ah, soon they will know there — all those who, now devoid of qualification, will go to the earth…" Having said this, the Lord, shaken by heavy roars, went swiftly to the house of Srivasa.

**Note.** ⟨?⟩ The sense of the first half is obscure. Perhaps it refers to those who are not yet worthy to see His divinity. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 27

**27.**

```
तत्रैव द्रुतमधिगत्य गाढबन्धं
सम्बध्यार्गलमवरद्वये विकुर्वन् ।
बह्वाविष्कृतसहजप्रकाशभास्वा-
नावासे रहसि रराज गौरचन्द्रः ॥२७॥
```
*tatraiva drutam adhigatya gāḍha-bandhaṁ
sambadhyārgalam avara-dvaye vikurvan |
bahv-āviṣkṛta-sahaja-prakāśa-bhāsvān
āvāse rahasi rarāja gauracandraḥ ||27||*

**Word for word:** *tatra eva* — there itself; *drutam* — swiftly; *adhigatya* — having entered; *gāḍha* — firmly; *bandham* — fastened; *sambadhya* — having shot; *argalam* — the bolt; *avara* — of the door; *dvaye* — on both leaves; *vikurvan* — transforming ⟨?⟩; *bahu* — abundantly; *āviṣkṛta* — revealed; *sahaja* — with His innate; *prakāśa* — radiance; *bhāsvān* — shining; *āvāse* — in the chamber; *rahasi* — secluded; *rarāja* — shone forth; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra.

**Translation.** Swiftly entering there, He shot the bolt firmly across both leaves of the door; and, transforming ⟨?⟩, brilliant with His innate radiance abundantly revealed, Gaurachandra shone forth in the secluded chamber.

**Note.** ⟨?⟩ *Vikurvan*, literally "transforming, undergoing change", may refer to the ecstatic transformations of His body or to the manifestation of His own form; the meaning is uncertain.

---

### 28

**28.**

```
अद्वैतो निजनिलयात्समागतोऽसौ
सम्प्रादुष्कृतसहजो विलोकितव्यः ।
इत्येवं मनसि विधाय सत्प्रतिज्ञां
तत्काले वहिरुदभूत्कवाटयोस्तत् ॥२८॥
```
*advaito nija-nilayāt samāgato 'sau
samprāduṣkṛta-sahajo vilokitavyaḥ |
ity evaṁ manasi vidhāya sat-pratijñāṁ
tat-kāle bahir udabhūt kavāṭayos tat ||28||*

**Word for word:** *advaitaḥ* — Advaita; *nija* — from His own; *nilayāt* — home; *samāgataḥ* — come; *asau* — He; *samprāduṣkṛta* — having manifested; *sahajaḥ* — [His] innate [form]; *vilokitavyaḥ* — is to be seen; *iti evam* — thus; *manasi* — in the mind; *vidhāya* — having made; *sat* — firm; *pratijñām* — a vow; *tat* — at that; *kāle* — time; *bahiḥ* — outside; *udabhūt* — appeared; *kavāṭayoḥ* — at the two leaves of the door; *tat* — then.

**Translation.** Advaita, who had come from his own home, made a firm vow in His mind: "He must be seen [by me] with His innate [form] fully manifest." And at that very time He appeared outside, at the leaves of the door.

**Note.** The motif of a test: Advaita wants the Lord Himself to reveal to Him His divine form. In the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 6) Advaita sees the Lord in the form for which He had prayed.

---

### 29

**29.**

```
श्रीवासद्विजकुलचन्द्रमः कनीया-
नेषः श्रीपतिरथ तत्समागमं तम् ।
साशङ्कं सपदि निवेदयाञ्चकार
ज्ञात्वैवैतत्स्वयममुचत्प्रभुः कवाटम् ॥२९॥
```
*śrīvāsa-dvija-kula-candramaḥ kanīyān
eṣaḥ śrīpatir atha tat-samāgamaṁ tam |
sāśaṅkaṁ sapadi nivedayāñcakāra
jñātvaivaitat svayam amucat prabhuḥ kavāṭam ||29||*

**Word for word:** *śrīvāsa* — of Srivasa; *dvija* — of brahmanas; *kula* — of the race; *candramaḥ* — the moon; *kanīyān* — the younger [brother]; *eṣaḥ* — this; *śrīpatiḥ* — Shripati; *atha* — then; *tat* — his; *samāgamam* — arrival; *tam* — that; *sa* — with; *āśaṅkam* — timidity; *sapadi* — at once; *nivedayāñcakāra* — announced; *jñātvā* — having learned; *eva* — just; *etat* — this; *svayam* — Himself; *amucat* — opened; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *kavāṭam* — the door.

**Translation.** Then Shripati, the younger brother of Srivasa, the moon of the brahmana race, timidly announced his arrival at once; and, just on learning this, the Lord Himself unbolted the door.

### 30

**30.**

```
सङ्कल्पो मनसि कृतो यथैव तेन
श्रीभाजं प्रभुमवलोक्य तं तथैव ।
अद्वैतस्तृणनिचयं रदैर्गृहीत्वा
सुस्निग्धो भुवि निपपात दण्डवत्सः ॥३०॥
```
*saṅkalpo manasi kṛto yathaiva tena
śrī-bhājaṁ prabhum avalokya taṁ tathaiva |
advaitas tṛṇa-nicayaṁ radair gṛhītvā
susnigdho bhuvi nipapāta daṇḍavat saḥ ||30||*

**Word for word:** *saṅkalpaḥ* — the resolve; *manasi* — in the mind; *kṛtaḥ* — made; *yathā eva* — just as; *tena* — by Him; *śrī* — splendour; *bhājam* — possessing; *prabhum* — the Lord; *avalokya* — having beheld; *tam* — Him; *tathā eva* — exactly so; *advaitaḥ* — Advaita; *tṛṇa* — of straw; *nicayam* — a bunch; *radaiḥ* — with the teeth; *gṛhītvā* — having taken; *su* — deeply; *snigdhaḥ* — loving; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *nipapāta* — fell; *daṇḍavat* — like a rod; *saḥ* — he.

**Translation.** Beholding the Lord in His splendour exactly as He had resolved in His mind, the deeply loving Advaita took a bunch of straw between His teeth and fell on the ground like a rod.

**Note.** Straw between the teeth is an ancient Indian sign of complete submission: "I am like a cow, your beast". Rupa Gosvami does the same on meeting Mahaprabhu (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 1).

### 31

**31.**

```
तं दृष्ट्वा प्रभुरपि दोर्द्वयेन शीघ्रं
श्रीभाजं स्वयमिव मुन्निनाय पश्चात् ।
हर्षेणाशिथिलितमाश्लिषद्विशेषं
प्रेमाश्रुस्रवणझरैः सिषेच भूयः ॥३१॥
```
*taṁ dṛṣṭvā prabhur api dor-dvayena śīghraṁ
śrī-bhājaṁ svayam iva munninnāya paścāt |
harṣeṇāśithilitam āśliṣad viśeṣaṁ
premāśru-sravaṇa-jharaiḥ siṣeca bhūyaḥ ||31||*

**Word for word:** *tam* — him; *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *api* — too; *doḥ* — of arms; *dvayena* — with the pair; *śīghram* — swiftly; *śrī-bhājam* — the glorious one; *svayam* — [His] own self; *iva* — as if; *unninnāya* — raised up; *paścāt* — then; *harṣeṇa* — with joy; *aśithilitam* — without loosening; *āśliṣat* — embraced; *viśeṣam* — especially; *prema* — of love; *aśru* — of tears; *sravaṇa* — of the flowing; *jharaiḥ* — with torrents; *siṣeca* — bathed; *bhūyaḥ* — again and again.

**Translation.** Seeing Him, the Lord too swiftly raised the glorious [Advaita] up with both His arms, as if [He were] His own self; then, in His joy, He embraced Him with special closeness, never loosening His hold, and bathed Him again and again with torrents of flowing tears of love.

**Note.** The edition reads *svayam iva munninnāya*, the Gosvami edition (GGM) *svayam iva munnināya*: in both cases the *m* is a linking sound before *unni-* ("raised up"), so the sense is clear.

### 32

**32.**

```
इत्येवंविधविविधोल्लसद्विहारै-
र्विश्रान्तोऽभवदृतुनायकोऽवसन्तः ।
अत्रान्ते प्रभुनटनावलोकहृष्टः
किं ग्रीष्मः प्रहसति मल्लिकाविकासैः ॥३२॥
```
*ity evaṁ-vidha-vividhollasad-vihārair
viśrānto 'bhavad ṛtu-nāyako vasantaḥ |
atrānte prabhu-naṭanāvaloka-hṛṣṭaḥ
kiṁ grīṣmaḥ prahasati mallikā-vikāsaiḥ ||32||*

**Word for word:** *iti evaṁvidha* — of such kinds; *vividha* — various; *ullasat* — exultant; *vihāraiḥ* — by the pastimes; *viśrāntaḥ* — wearied; *abhavat* — became; *ṛtu* — of the seasons; *nāyakaḥ* — the leader; *vasantaḥ* — spring; *atra* — here; *ante* — at the end; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *naṭana* — the dancing; *avaloka* — at seeing; *hṛṣṭaḥ* — delighted; *kim* — is it not that; *grīṣmaḥ* — summer; *prahasati* — laughs; *mallikā* — of jasmine; *vikāsaiḥ* — with the blossoming.

**Translation.** By such various, exultant pastimes spring, the leader of the seasons, was wearied. And now, at its end, does not summer, delighted at seeing the Lord's dancing, laugh with the blossoming of jasmine?

**Note.** The change of seasons marks the passage of time: spring gives way to summer (*grīṣma*, the hot season, May–June). The blossoming of jasmine as "laughter" is a classical image.

### 33

**33.**

```
विच्छेदादिव सुरभेर्दिनान्यमूनि
प्रत्यग्रादतिविधुराणि संश्रयन्ते ।
उद्दीप्यद्दिनकरजातवेदसः किं
ज्वालाभिर्निरवधि देहदाहवर्त्म ॥३३॥
```
*vicchedād iva surabher dināny amūni
pratyagrād ati-vidhurāṇi saṁśrayante |
uddīpyad-dinakara-jāta-vedasaḥ kiṁ
jvālābhir niravadhi deha-dāha-vartma ||33||*

**Word for word:** *vicchedāt* — from the separation; *iva* — as if; *surabheḥ* — from spring; *dināni* — the days; *amūni* — these; *pratyagrāt* — from the fresh; *ati* — utterly; *vidhurāṇi* — bereaved; *saṁśrayante* — take to; *uddīpyat* — of the blazing; *dinakara* — of the sun; *jātavedasaḥ* — of the fire; *kim* — is it not that; *jvālābhiḥ* — with the flames; *niravadhi* — without end; *deha* — of the body; *dāha* — of burning; *vartma* — the path.

**Translation.** These days, utterly bereaved, as it were, by their fresh separation from spring — do they not take, without end, the path of burning their bodies in the flames of the blazing fire that is the sun?

**Note.** *Surabhi*, "the fragrant one", is spring. *Jātavedas* is Agni, fire. The image is of a bereaved wife who enters the fire after her husband.

### 34

**34.**

```
उद्दामद्युमणिरुचो मुहुर्ज्वलन्त्यो
यद्यप्याश्रयमयमम्बु शोषयन्ति ।
पद्मिन्यास्तदपि दधात्यतीव सौख्यं
दुःखञ्च प्रियविहितं प्रियं तनोति ॥३४॥
```
*uddāma-dyumaṇi-ruco muhur jvalantyo
yady apy āśraya-mayam ambu śoṣayanti |
padminyās tad api dadhāty atīva saukhyaṁ
duḥkhañ ca priya-vihitaṁ priyaṁ tanoti ||34||*

**Word for word:** *uddāma* — unrestrained; *dyumaṇi* — of the sun; *rucaḥ* — the rays; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *jvalantyaḥ* — blazing; *yadi api* — although; *āśraya* — the support; *mayam* — forming; *ambu* — the water; *śoṣayanti* — dry up; *padminyāḥ* — to the lotus; *tat api* — even so; *dadhāti* — gives; *atīva* — very great; *saukhyam* — happiness; *duḥkham* — pain; *ca* — and; *priya* — by the beloved; *vihitam* — caused; *priyam* — dear; *tanoti* — becomes.

**Translation.** Although the unrestrained rays of the sun, blazing again and again, dry up the water that is the lotus's support, even so [the sun] gives the lotus very great happiness: for pain too, when caused by the beloved, becomes dear.

**Note.** The sun is the "beloved" of the day-lotus. A thought important for Gaudiya aesthetics: pain from the beloved is sweet (compare Shikshashtaka 8).

### 35

**35.**

```
नैदाघं निजमहसा निदाघरश्मिं
न्यक्कुर्वन्सततं नवनवेन गौरः ।
अन्येद्युर्द्विजतनुजान्विपाठयन्स
प्रोद्भिन्नप्रकटनिजप्रकाश आसीत् ॥३५॥
```
*naidāghaṁ nija-mahasā nidāgha-raśmiṁ
nyakkurvan satataṁ nava-navena gauraḥ |
anyedyur dvija-tanujān vipāṭhayan sa
prodbhinna-prakaṭa-nija-prakāśa āsīt ||35||*

**Word for word:** *naidāgham* — of summer; *nija* — with His own; *mahasā* — radiance; *nidāgha* — of heat; *raśmim* — the [one with] rays (the sun); *nyakkurvan* — putting to shame; *satatam* — constantly; *nava-navena* — ever new; *gauraḥ* — Gaura; *anyedyuḥ* — on the next day; *dvija* — of brahmanas; *tanujān* — the sons; *vipāṭhayan* — teaching; *saḥ* — He; *prodbhinna* — burst forth; *prakaṭa* — manifest; *nija* — His own; *prakāśaḥ* — [with] radiance; *āsīt* — was.

**Translation.** Constantly putting to shame the hot-rayed summer sun with His own ever-new radiance, Gaura, on the next day, while teaching the sons of brahmanas, had His own radiance burst forth and become manifest.

### 36

**36.**

```
इत्येतद्विधसहजप्रकाशभास्वा-
न्निर्भिन्नः सदरुणसर्व्वगात्रयष्टिः ।
प्रत्यग्रोन्मिषदरुणोत्पलाङ्घ्रियुग्मः
श्रीवासालयमगमद्विमुक्तसङ्गः ॥३६॥
```
*ity etad-vidha-sahaja-prakāśa-bhāsvān
nirbhinnaḥ sad-aruṇa-sarva-gātra-yaṣṭiḥ |
pratyagronmiṣad-aruṇotpalāṅghri-yugmaḥ
śrīvāsālayam agamad vimukta-saṅgaḥ ||36||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *etat-vidha* — with such; *sahaja* — innate; *prakāśa* — radiance; *bhāsvān* — shining; *nirbhinnaḥ* — overwhelmed; *sat* — beautifully; *aruṇa* — rosy; *sarva* — whole; *gātra* — of the body; *yaṣṭiḥ* — [whose] slender stem; *pratyagra* — fresh; *unmiṣat* — opening; *aruṇa* — red; *utpala* — [like] lotuses; *aṅghri* — of feet; *yugmaḥ* — [whose] pair; *śrīvāsa* — of Srivasa; *ālayam* — to the house; *agamat* — went; *vimukta* — having left; *saṅgaḥ* — company.

**Translation.** Thus, shining with such innate radiance, overwhelmed, the slender stem of His whole body a beautiful rosy hue, His two feet like fresh, opening red lotuses, He left all company and went to the house of Srivasa.

### 37

**37.**

```
उन्मीलद्द्युमणिगणप्रकाशभाजं
प्रत्यग्रस्फुटतरशोणसारसाक्षम् ।
गच्छन्तं द्रुतमरुणाङ्घ्रिपद्मयोस्तै-
र्विन्यासैः पथि ददृशुर्जनाः सचित्रम् ॥३७॥
```
*unmīlad-dyumaṇi-gaṇa-prakāśa-bhājaṁ
pratyagra-sphuṭatara-śoṇa-sārasākṣam |
gacchantaṁ drutam aruṇāṅghri-padmayos tair
vinyāsaiḥ pathi dadṛśur janāḥ sa-citram ||37||*

**Word for word:** *unmīlat* — of rising; *dyumaṇi* — of suns; *gaṇa* — of a host; *prakāśa* — the radiance; *bhājam* — possessing; *pratyagra* — fresh; *sphuṭatara* — fully opened; *śoṇa* — red; *sārasa* — [like] lotuses; *akṣam* — [whose] eyes; *gacchantam* — going; *drutam* — swiftly; *aruṇa* — red; *aṅghri* — of feet; *padmayoḥ* — of the lotuses; *taiḥ* — with those; *vinyāsaiḥ* — with the steps; *pathi* — on the road; *dadṛśuḥ* — saw; *janāḥ* — the people; *sa* — with; *citram* — wonder.

**Translation.** The people on the road watched with wonder as He went swiftly with the steps of His red lotus feet — possessing the radiance of a host of rising suns, His eyes like fresh, fully opened red lotuses.

**38.**

```
तत्पुर्य्यां सपदि निवेश्य देवगेह-
स्यालिन्दोपरि परितस्थिवान्परेशः ।

ālindopari paritasthivān pareśaḥ |
dhyāyantaṁ gṛham adhi nirbharaika-tānaṁ
śrīvāsaṁ prakaṭa-prakāśam ājuhāva ||38||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — his; *puryām* — into the house; *sapadi* — at once; *niveśya* — having entered; *deva* — of God; *gehasya* — of the shrine; *alinda* — the veranda; *upari* — upon; *paritasthivān* — took His place; *pareśaḥ* — the Supreme Lord; *dhyāyantam* — meditating; *gṛham adhi* — within the house; *nirbhara* — wholly; *eka* — on one [object]; *tānam* — fixed; *śrīvāsam* — Srivasa; *prakaṭa* — manifest; *prakāśam* — [He with] radiance; *ājuhāva* — called.

**Translation.** At once entering his house, the Supreme Lord took His place upon the veranda of the shrine and, with His radiance manifest, called Srivasa, who was within the house, meditating, wholly fixed on one object.

### 39

**39.**

```
तच्छ्रुत्वा सपदि गृहाद्बहिर्बभूव
ध्यानादि प्रकटमपोह्य विप्रमुख्यः ।
उन्मीलन्गुरुमहसं महायताङ्गं

**Word for word:** *tat* — that; *śrutvā* — having heard; *sapadi* — at once; *gṛhāt* — from the house; *bahiḥ* — outside; *babhūva* — came; *dhyāna* — meditation; *ādi* — and the rest; *prakaṭam* — openly; *apohya* — having set aside; *vipra* — of brahmanas; *mukhyaḥ* — the foremost; *unmīlan* — opening [his eyes]; *guru* — [with] mighty; *mahasam* — effulgence; *mahā* — majestic; *āyata* — tall; *aṅgam* — [with] a body; *saḥ* — he; *adrākṣīt* — saw; *nija* — himself; *purataḥ* — before; *sthitam* — standing; *pareśam* — the Supreme Lord.

**Translation.** Hearing that, the foremost of brahmanas at once openly set aside his meditation and the rest and came out of the house. Opening his eyes, he saw standing before him the Supreme Lord, of mighty effulgence, His body tall and majestic.

### 40

**40.**

```
उद्भ्रान्तः प्रकटनिजप्रकाशवेगै-
रज्यद्भिर्महिततनूर्नवैर्महोभिः ।
पाथोभिः सुरसरितो ममाभिषेकं

*udbhrāntaḥ prakaṭa-nija-prakāśa-vegai-
rajyadbhir mahita-tanur navair mahobhiḥ |
pāthobhiḥ sura-sarito mamābhiṣekaṁ
śīghraṁ kurv iti nijagāda gauracandraḥ ||40||*

**Word for word:** *udbhrāntaḥ* — agitated; *prakaṭa* — of the manifest; *nija* — of His own; *prakāśa* — radiance; *vegaiḥ* — by the surges; *rajyadbhiḥ* — with reddening; *mahita* — glorious; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body; *navaiḥ* — with fresh; *mahobhiḥ* — with rays; *pāthobhiḥ* — with the waters; *sura* — of the gods; *saritaḥ* — of the river; *mama* — My; *abhiṣekam* — ceremonial bathing; *śīghram* — quickly; *kuru* — perform; *iti* — thus; *nijagāda* — declared; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra.

**Translation.** Agitated by the surges of His own manifest radiance, His glorious body [aglow] with fresh, reddening rays, Gaurachandra declared: "Quickly perform My ceremonial bathing with the waters of the river of the gods!"

**Note.** The **mahabhisheka** is the royal bathing of the Lord in the house of Srivasa, the beginning of the *maha-prakasha* (Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 9–10, "The completion of the maha-prakasha-lila").

### 41

**41.**

```
तच्छ्रुत्वा सपदि सहोदरैरमुष्य

इत्युक्तः स च सकलं निनाय तत्र

### 56

**56.**

```
सच्चन्द्रातपमुपरि प्रतत्य तूर्णं
तस्यान्ते सुरुचिरचामराणि तेने ।
पर्य्यङ्कोपरि कशिपूत्तमं निपात्य

```
*sac-candrātapam upari pratatya tūrṇaṁ
tasyānte su-rucira-cāmarāṇi tene |

**Word for word:** *sat* — a fine; *candrātapam* — canopy; *upari* — above; *pratatya* — having spread; *tūrṇam* — quickly; *tasya* — of it; *ante* — along the edge; *su* — very; *rucira* — beautiful; *cāmarāṇi* — chamaras; *tene* — hung; *paryaṅka* — the couch; *upari* — upon; *kaśipu* — a mattress; *uttamam* — the best; *nipātya* — having laid; *śrīmadbhiḥ* — with splendid; *vara* — excellent; *vasanaiḥ* — with cloths; *atha* — then; *anuvavre* — covered.

### 57

**57.**

```
गौराङ्गस्तदथ गृहं व्रजन् विरेजे
तेजोभिर्लघु तिरयन् विवस्वदोजः ।
शम्पानां शतशतकोटिकोटिवत् स

**Word for word:** *gaurāṅgaḥ* — Gauranga; *tat* — that; *atha* — then; *gṛham* — house; *vrajan* — going to; *vireje* — shone; *tejobhiḥ* — with rays; *laghu* — easily; *tirayan* — eclipsing; *vivasvat* — of the sun; *ojaḥ* — the might; *śampānām* — of lightning flashes; *śata* — hundreds; *śata* — of hundreds; *koṭi* — of crores; *koṭivat* — like crores; *saḥ* — He; *pronmīlya* — having burst open; *kṣitim* — upon the earth; *iva* — as if; *saṁśritaḥ* — come to rest; *cakāsti* — shines.

**58.**

पादाम्भोरुहयुगलं विलासपूर्व्वं
विन्यस्य क्षितिषु चलन्महामहस्वान् ।

### 59

**59.**

```
सद्गौरैः परममहोभिरुन्मिषद्भिः
सर्व्वा पूः परिमिलिता तदा तदीयैः ।
बभ्राज प्रमथमिव प्रजेशसृष्टां
न्यक्कुर्व्वन्त्यनिशमिलावृतस्य शोभाम् ॥५९॥
```
*sad-gauraiḥ parama-mahobhir unmiṣadbhiḥ
sarvā pūḥ parimilitā tadā tadīyaiḥ |
babhrāja pramatham iva prajeśa-sṛṣṭāṁ
nyak-kurvanty aniśam ilāvṛtasya śobhām ||59||*

**Word for word:** *sat* — pure; *gauraiḥ* — golden; *parama* — supreme; *mahobhiḥ* — with rays; *unmiṣadbhiḥ* — flashing; *sarvā* — the whole; *pūḥ* — city; *parimilitā* — suffused; *tadā* — then; *tadīyaiḥ* — with His; *babhrāja* — shone; *pramatham* — crushingly; *iva* — as if; *prajeśa* — by the Lord of creatures; *sṛṣṭām* — created; *nyak* — to nothing; *kurvantī* — reducing; *aniśam* — unceasingly; *ilāvṛtasya* — of Ilavrita; *śobhām* — the beauty.

**Translation.** Then the whole city, suffused with His pure, golden, supreme, flashing rays, shone as if crushingly and unceasingly reducing to nothing the beauty of Ilavrita, created by the Lord of creatures.

**Note.** Ilavrita is the central region (*varsha*) of Jambudvipa, around the golden mountain Meru; the Lord of creatures (*Prajesha*, Prajapati) is Brahma. The edition (and the Gosvami edition) reads *pramatham*, "crushingly"; an earlier conjecture *prathamam* ("for the first time") is unnecessary. The edition also has *पुः*, corrected to *पूः* (nominative of *pur*, "city"). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 60

**60.**

```
कैश्चिद्वा परिपिपिषे न गन्धसार-
स्ताम्बूलं न हि कति सज्जितं प्रचक्रे ।
आजह्रे कुसुमशतं तदा न कैश्चि-

*kaiś cid vā paripipiṣe na gandha-sāras

ājahre kusuma-śataṁ tadā na kaiścit
pūrṇā bhūḥ kim iva mahotsavais tadānīm ||60||*

**Word for word:** *kaiḥ cit* — by whom; *vā* — then; *paripipiṣe* — was ground; *na* — not; *gandha* — fragrant; *sāraḥ* — essence (sandalwood); *tāmbūlam* — betel; *na* — not; *hi* — indeed; *kati* — how many; *sajjitam* — prepared; *pracakre* — made; *ājahre* — was brought; *kusuma* — of flowers; *śatam* — a hundred; *tadā* — then; *na* — not; *kaiścit* — by whom; *pūrṇā* — filled; *bhūḥ* — the earth; *kim iva* — with what [not]; *mahā* — great; *utsavaiḥ* — with festivities; *tadānīm* — then.

**Translation.** By whom was sandalwood not ground then? How many did not prepare betel? By whom were hundreds of flowers not brought then? With what great festivities was the earth not filled at that time!

### 61

**61.**

```
कर्पूरैर्मरिचसिताभिरप्यखण्डा-
नन्दस्यानुभवसहोदरं समन्तात् ।
कैर्नो वा सपदि पयोविभावनादि-
व्यापारै रस इव सम्मदात् प्रसस्रे ॥६१॥
```
*karpūrair marica-sitābhir apy akhaṇḍā-
nandasyānubhava-sahodaraṁ samantāt |
kair no vā sapadi payo-vibhāvanādi-
vyāpārai rasa iva sammadāt prasasre ||61||*

**Word for word:** *karpūraiḥ* — with camphor; *marica* — with pepper; *sitābhiḥ* — with sugar; *api* — and; *akhaṇḍa* — of unbroken; *ānandasya* — of bliss; *anubhava* — to the experience; *sahodaram* — own brother (akin); *samantāt* — all around; *kaiḥ* — by whom; *no* — not; *vā* — indeed; *sapadi* — at once; *payaḥ* — of milk; *vibhāvana* — the preparation; *ādi* — and the like; *vyāpāraiḥ* — by the activities; *rasaḥ* — rasa; *iva* — as it were; *sammadāt* — out of joy; *prasasre* — was made to flow.

**Translation.** By whom, out of joy, was a *rasa* not at once set flowing all around — own brother, as it were, to the experience of unbroken bliss — through the work of preparing milk and the like, with camphor, pepper and sugar?

**Note.** The syllable *-nu-* in the second line is poorly printed in the edition; *anubhava* is restored by metre and sense. A play on words from the theory of *rasa*: *vibhāvana* ("preparation", but also *vibhāva*, the cause of an emotion), *anubhava* (echoing *anubhāva*, its outward expression), and *rasa* itself — both "drink, juice" and "aesthetic relish". Camphor and pepper are the usual spices for sweetened milk in Bengal.

### 62

**62.**

माल्यौघः प्रवणतरेण सौष्ठवेन

**Word for word:** *sa* — with; *utkaṇṭham* — eagerness; *sapadi* — at once; *gadādhareṇa* — by Gadadhara; *puṣpaiḥ* — from flowers; *sa* — with; *āmodaiḥ* — fragrance (joy); *ati* — very; *ruciraiḥ* — lovely (pleasing); *svayam* — himself; *tadānīm* — then; *mālya* — of garlands; *oghaḥ* — a multitude; *pravaṇatareṇa* — most devoted; *sauṣṭhavena* — with skill; *sva* — of his own; *svāntaiḥ* — heart; *iva* — as if; *saḥ* — that; *manorathaiḥ* — from the desires; *jagumphe* — was woven.

**64.**

```
हारञ्च ग्रथनसुकौशलातिमुग्धं
केयूरे वलययुगञ्च कङ्कणे च ।

### 68

**68.**

```
तैरेतैः कुसुमविभूषणैः समस्तै-

**Word for word:** *taiḥ etaiḥ* — with these very; *kusuma* — flower; *vibhūṣaṇaiḥ* — ornaments; *samastaiḥ* — all; *taiḥ etaiḥ* — with these very; *malayaja* — of sandalwood; *kuṅkumasya* — and of kunkuma; *paṅkaiḥ* — with the pastes; *tejobhiḥ* — with the rays; *nija* — of His own; *vapuṣaḥ* — body; *nisarga* — by nature; *gauraiḥ* — golden; *saṁbhinnaḥ* — commingled; *kaḥ* — whom; *iva* — like; *babhūva* — became; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra.

**Translation.** …with all these flower ornaments, with these pastes of sandalwood and kunkuma, and with the naturally golden rays of His own body — commingled with all these, whom did Gaurachandra come to resemble?

**Note.** *Malayaja* ("born on Malaya") is sandalwood from the Malaya hills. The rhetorical question "whom did He resemble?" means: no one.

**Translation.** Then Gauranga laughed and at once said: "What is this? What is this word you are saying?" Having said this, He stood still for a moment — and then, with roars, fell to the ground. What a wonder!

**Note.** A *hunkara* is a thunderous cry of "hum!", a sign of ecstasy; compare Advaita's hunkaras.

svaṁ gehaṁ madhura-mukho yayau tato 'yaṁ
mātus tāṁ mudam ati-nirbharāṁ vitanvan ||124||*

**Word for word:** *iti evam* — thus; *sahaja* — innate; *nija* — His own; *prakāśa* — of effulgence; *tejaḥ* — the fire; *sandarśya* — having shown; *sthira* — steady; *karaṇaḥ* — [with] senses; *ciram* — long; *vilasya* — having sported; *svam* — to His own; *geham* — home; *madhura* — sweet; *mukhaḥ* — [with] a face; *yayau* — went; *tataḥ* — then; *ayam* — He; *mātuḥ* — of [His] mother; *tām* — that; *mudam* — joy; *ati* — exceedingly; *nirbharām* — full; *vitanvan* — spreading.

**Translation.** Thus, having displayed the innate fire of His own effulgence and having sported for a long time, He, steady in His senses, His face sweet, then went to His own home, spreading for His mother that brimming joy.

**Note.** *Sthira-karaṇa* — "with steady, collected senses".

### 125

**125.**

```
इत्येवं प्रचुरकृपामृतं वितन्वन्
ज्यैष्ठाद्यष्टभिरतिसम्मदेन मासैः ।
पौषान्तं नटनरसैर्निदाघवर्षै-
र्हेमन्तं सह शरदा निनाय नाथः ॥१२५॥
```
*ity evaṁ pracura-kṛpāmṛtaṁ vitanvan
jyaiṣṭhādy-aṣṭabhir ati-sammadena māsaiḥ |
pauṣāntaṁ naṭana-rasair nidāgha-varṣair
hemantaṁ saha śaradā nināya nāthaḥ ||125||*

**Word for word:** *iti evam* — thus; *pracura* — abundant; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *amṛtam* — the nectar; *vitanvan* — spreading; *jyaiṣṭha* — with Jyaishtha; *ādi* — beginning; *aṣṭabhiḥ* — eight; *ati* — with great; *sammadena* — delight; *māsaiḥ* — months; *pauṣa* — of Pausha; *antam* — to the end; *naṭana* — of dancing; *rasaiḥ* — in the rasas; *nidāgha* — with the summer; *varṣaiḥ* — and the rains; *hemantam* — the winter; *saha* — together with; *śaradā* — the autumn; *nināya* — spent; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord.

**Translation.** Thus, spreading the abundant nectar of mercy, the Lord, with great delight, in the rasas of dancing, spent eight months, from Jyaishtha to the end of Pausha — the summer and the rains, and the winter together with the autumn.

**Note.** Jyaishtha is May–June, Pausha December–January. The edition has *-atī-* and *वर्षैं*; we read *ati-* (as the metre requires) and *nidāgha-varṣair hemantam* (the fourth line begins with *र्हे*).

### 126

**126.**

```
ऋतूनामेतेषां प्रतिदिनमथानुक्षणमसौ
प्रभुर्मासं मासं प्रति यदकरोन्नर्त्तनरसम् ।
तदेतन्नैवायं कथयितुमलं किं पुनरहो
मनुष्यास्तु क्षुद्राः सुरगुरुसहस्रं क्व नु पुनः ॥१२६॥
```
*ṛtūnām eteṣāṁ prati-dinam athānukṣaṇam asau
prabhur māsaṁ māsaṁ prati yad akaron nartana-rasam |
tad etan naivāyaṁ kathayitum alaṁ kiṁ punar aho
manuṣyās tu kṣudrāḥ sura-guru-sahasraṁ kva nu punaḥ ||126||*

**Word for word:** *ṛtūnām* — of the seasons; *eteṣām* — of these; *prati* — every; *dinam* — day; *atha* — and; *anukṣaṇam* — every moment; *asau* — this; *prabhuḥ* — Lord; *māsam māsam prati* — month by month; *yat* — which; *akarot* — made; *nartana* — of dancing; *rasam* — the rapture; *tat etat* — that; *na eva* — not at all; *ayam* — He [Himself]; *kathayitum* — to tell; *alam* — is able; *kim punaḥ* — how much less; *aho* — ah; *manuṣyāḥ* — humans; *tu* — but; *kṣudrāḥ* — petty; *sura* — of the gods; *guru* — of preceptors; *sahasram* — a thousand; *kva nu punaḥ* — how then.

**Translation.** The rapture of dancing that the Lord displayed through these seasons, month by month, every day and every moment — not even He Himself could tell it; how much less, ah, petty humans! And how then could a thousand preceptors of the gods?

**Note.** The metre is *śikhariṇī* (17 syllables). The preceptor of the gods is Brihaspati.

### 127

**127.**

```
श्रीवासालय एव नृत्यति सदा तद्भ्रातृभिर्निर्भरं
गायद्भिर्हरिकीर्त्तनामृतरसं श्रीगौरचन्द्रः प्रभुः ।
तत्सङ्गामृतदीर्घिका निरवधि स्नातास्तदास्योद्गतं
वाक्पीयूषममी निपीय बहुधा नित्यं विजह्रुस्तथा ॥१२७॥
```
*śrīvāsālaya eva nṛtyati sadā tad-bhrātṛbhir nirbharaṁ
gāyadbhir hari-kīrtanāmṛta-rasaṁ śrī-gauracandraḥ prabhuḥ |
tat-saṅgāmṛta-dīrghikā niravadhi snātās tad-āsyodgataṁ
vāk-pīyūṣam amī nipīya bahudhā nityaṁ vijahrus tathā ||127||*

**Word for word:** *śrīvāsa* — of Srivasa; *ālaye* — in the house; *eva* — only; *nṛtyati* — dances; *sadā* — always; *tat* — his; *bhrātṛbhiḥ* — with the brothers; *nirbharam* — wholeheartedly; *gāyadbhiḥ* — singing; *hari* — of Hari; *kīrtana* — of the praise; *amṛta* — of the nectar; *rasam* — the rasa; *śrī-gauracandraḥ* — Sri Gaurachandra; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *tat* — of His; *saṅga* — company; *amṛta* — of the nectar; *dīrghikā* — in the pond; *niravadhi* — endlessly; *snātāḥ* — bathed; *tat* — His; *āsya* — from the mouth; *udgatam* — come forth; *vāk* — of words; *pīyūṣam* — the nectar; *amī* — they; *nipīya* — having drunk; *bahudhā* — in many ways; *nityam* — always; *vijahruḥ* — sported; *tathā* — likewise.

**Translation.** Sri Gaurachandra, the Lord, always dances in the house of Srivasa alone, while his brothers wholeheartedly sing the nectarean rasa of the praise of Hari. Endlessly bathed in the pond of the nectar of His company and drinking again and again the nectar of the words that came from His mouth, they too constantly sported in the same way.

**Note.** The metre is *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* (19 syllables).

### 128

**128.**

```
स तु गदाधरपण्डितसत्तमः
सततमस्य समीपसुसङ्गतः ।
अनुदिनं भजते निजजीवितं
प्रियतमं तमभिस्पृहया युतः ॥१२८॥
```
*sa tu gadādhara-paṇḍita-sattamaḥ
satatam asya samīpa-susaṅgataḥ |
anudinaṁ bhajate nija-jīvitaṁ
priyatamaṁ tam abhispṛhayā yutaḥ ||128||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — he; *tu* — but; *gadādhara* — Gadadhara; *paṇḍita* — Pandita; *sattamaḥ* — best of the virtuous; *satatam* — always; *asya* — His; *samīpa* — near; *susaṅgataḥ* — closely abiding; *anudinam* — day after day; *bhajate* — serves; *nija* — his own; *jīvitam* — life; *priyatamam* — the most beloved; *tam* — Him; *abhispṛhayā* — with ardent longing; *yutaḥ* — filled.

**Translation.** And Gadadhara Pandita, best of the virtuous, always abiding close beside Him, day after day, filled with ardent longing, serves Him — his own life, his most beloved.

**Note.** The metre is *drutavilambita* (12 syllables).

### 129

**129.**

```
निशि तदीयसमीपगतः स्थिरः
शयनमुत्सुक एव करोति सः ।
विहरणामृतमस्य निरन्तरं
सदुपभुक्तमनेन निरन्तरम् ॥१२९॥
```
*niśi tadīya-samīpa-gataḥ sthiraḥ
śayanam utsuka eva karoti saḥ |
viharaṇāmṛtam asya nirantaraṁ
sad upabhuktam anena nirantaram ||129||*

**Word for word:** *niśi* — at night; *tadīya* — Him; *samīpa* — near; *gataḥ* — being; *sthiraḥ* — steadfast; *śayanam* — sleep; *utsukaḥ* — full of longing; *eva* — indeed; *karoti* — takes; *saḥ* — he; *viharaṇa* — of the pastimes; *amṛtam* — the nectar; *asya* — His; *nirantaram* — uninterrupted; *sat* — well; *upabhuktam* — was enjoyed; *anena* — by him; *nirantaram* — unceasingly.

**Translation.** At night, too, he stays steadfastly near Him and sleeps there, full of longing. Thus the uninterrupted nectar of His pastimes was well enjoyed by him unceasingly.

**Note.** The edition has *विहरणामृतस्य*, a syllable short; *viharaṇāmṛtam asya* is restored by the metre. The repetition of *nirantaram* at the end of two lines is in the original.

इति श्रीकृष्णचैतन्यचरितामृते महाकाव्ये नवद्वीपविहारवर्णनं नाम पञ्चमः सर्गः ॥
```
