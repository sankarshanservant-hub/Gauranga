# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Twelve — verses 1–134 (printed pp. 340–381)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:** *upajāti* (a mixture of *indravajrā* ——⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—— and *upendravajrā* ⏑—⏑——⏑⏑—⏑——; 11 syllables per pada); verse 87 — *śālinī*; verses 133–134 — *mālinī*.
**Total verses:** 134 (verses 49 and 112 have six padas; 112 opens with a quotation of Bhagavatam 10.81.16 in the *anuṣṭubh* metre; 87 — *śālinī*; 120 — the chant "Rāma Rāghava…"; 133–134 — *mālinī*).

**Contents of the passage:** the Lord in Puri with Sarvabhauma; the departure for the south as far as the Godavari.
- 1–9 — the Lord in Puri with Sarvabhauma; the darshan of Jagannatha.
- 10–35 — Sarvabhauma's plan to instruct the Lord in Vedanta; the disputation; the Lord refutes the doctrine of non-duality; the four-armed form; hymns of praise.
- 36–58 — the manuscript in Gopinatha's hand with Sarvabhauma's verse containing the word "Krishna"; the Lord, in ecstasy, returns to Puri; Gopinatha on the mercy shown to Sarvabhauma.
- 59–77 — the mahaprasada: Sarvabhauma eats it without first washing; both are in ecstasy; Sarvabhauma is humbled.
- 78–93 — eighteen interpretations of two stanzas from the Eleventh Canto; the verses *vairāgya-vidyā…* and *kālān naṣṭaṁ…* (the Lord tears up the leaf; Mukunda had copied them onto the wall); "bhakti-pade" instead of "mukti-pade".
- 94–106 — eighteen days in Puri; the departure for the south; Sarvabhauma advises the Lord to meet Ramananda; Kurma-kshetra, the brahmana Kurma.
- 107–117 — the leper Vasudeva is healed by an embrace; Kurma's lament.
- 118–121 — Nrisimha-kshetra; the Lord's gait; the chant "Rāma Rāghava… Kṛṣṇa Keśava…".
- 122–129 — the forests by the Godavari: peacocks, deer, parrots, cicadas, fearsome thickets and torrents.
- 130–132 — hesitation: whether to speak with Ramananda; the Lord passes by and goes on south in ecstasy.
- 133–134 (*mālinī*) — the wonder of the southerners: "A golden elephant? Meru set in motion? Lightning that does not fade?"

**Textual cruxes:** 1 (*jñāna-*/*jagan-manojñam*), 4, 11–13, 14 (metre), 22, 25, 30, 32, 34, 35–36, 66 and 119 (wrong division of the lines), 42, 45, 65 (*jagante*), 73, 84, 112, 124 (*kva ca*), 126, 127–128 (*krakacai rasadbhiḥ*, *juṣṭe*), 130 (*saṁbhāṣitavyaḥ*), 131 (divergence from the Chaitanya-charitamrita on the meeting with Ramananda), 134 (*satoṣam*, *vinimiṣam*) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
प्रविश्य सत्क्षेत्रमदभ्रलीलः
श्रीसार्वभौमालयमाययौ सः ।
आकस्मिकं वीक्ष्य जगन्मनोज्ञं
सन्न्यासिनं सोऽथ ननन्द विप्रः ॥१॥
```
*praviśya sat-kṣetram adabhra-līlaḥ
śrī-sārvabhaumālayam āyayau saḥ |
ākasmikaṁ vīkṣya jagan-manojñaṁ [jñāna-manojñaṁ]
sannyāsinaṁ so 'tha nananda vipraḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *praviśya* — having entered; *sat* — the holy; *kṣetram* — Kshetra; *adabhra* — abundant; *līlaḥ* — [He whose] pastimes [are]; *śrī-sārvabhauma* — of Sri Sarvabhauma; *ālayam* — to the house; *āyayau* — came; *saḥ* — He; *ākasmikam* — unexpected; *vīkṣya* — having seen; *jagat* — to the world; *manojñam* — charming; *sannyāsinam* — the sannyasi; *saḥ* — that; *atha* — then; *nananda* — rejoiced; *vipraḥ* — brahmana.

**Translation.** Having entered the holy Kshetra, He whose pastimes are boundless came to the house of Sri Sarvabhauma. And that brahmana, seeing the unexpected sannyasi who charms the world, rejoiced.

**Note.** The edition has *ज्ञानमनोज्ञं* ("charming by knowledge"), but the metre requires ⏑⏑ at the sixth and seventh syllables; we read *jagan-manojñam*. The Kshetra is Purushottama-kshetra (Puri). Sarvabhauma Bhattacharya was a celebrated Vedantist and logician at the court of Puri.

### 2

**2.**

```
उत्थाय पाद्यादि समर्प्य भक्त्या
पुरो निवेद्यासनमप्युदारम् ।
कृतप्रणामो नु सुधीरमङ्ग
पप्रच्छ सर्वं विनयेन विप्रः ॥२॥
```
*utthāya pādyādi samarpya bhaktyā
puro nivedyāsanam apy udāram |
kṛta-praṇāmo nu sudhīram aṅga
papraccha sarvaṁ vinayena vipraḥ ||2||*

**Word for word:** *utthāya* — having risen; *pādya* — water for [washing] the feet; *ādi* — and the rest; *samarpya* — having offered; *bhaktyā* — with devotion; *puraḥ* — before [Him]; *nivedya* — having presented; *āsanam* — a seat; *api* — also; *udāram* — excellent; *kṛta* — having made; *praṇāmaḥ* — obeisance; *nu* — indeed; *su-dhīram* — very composedly; *aṅga* — (a particle); *papraccha* — asked; *sarvam* — about everything; *vinayena* — with humility; *vipraḥ* — the brahmana.

**Translation.** Rising, the brahmana with devotion offered water for [washing] the feet and the rest, set an excellent seat before [Him] as well, and, having made obeisance, very composedly and humbly asked about everything.

### 3

**3.**

```
कुतः समेतोऽसि कुतो नु यासि
हृद्यो भवान्निर्भरशान्तदान्तः ।
इत्थं प्रभौ तेन यथार्थपृष्टे
तदेकनाथाः सकलं तदूचुः ॥३॥
```
*kutaḥ sameto 'si kuto nu yāsi
hṛdyo bhavān nirbhara-śānta-dāntaḥ |
itthaṁ prabhau tena yathārtha-pṛṣṭe
tad-eka-nāthāḥ sakalaṁ tad ūcuḥ ||3||*

**Word for word:** *kutaḥ* — from where; *sametaḥ asi* — have You come; *kutaḥ nu* — where then; *yāsi* — are You going; *hṛdyaḥ* — charming; *bhavān* — You [are]; *nirbhara* — wholly; *śānta* — peaceful; *dāntaḥ* — [and] self-controlled; *ittham* — thus; *prabhau* — when the Lord; *tena* — by him; *yathārtha* — in substance; *pṛṣṭe* — had been asked; *tat* — Him; *eka* — as their only; *nāthāḥ* — [those who had as] Master; *sakalam* — all; *tat* — that; *ūcuḥ* — related.

**Translation.** "From where have You come, and where are You going? You are charming, wholly peaceful and self-controlled." When the Lord had been asked thus by him in earnest, those whose only Master He was related all of it.

### 4

**4.**

```
यथा-तथा तत् सकलं विदित्वा
ज्ञातं तदाज्ञातमिति प्रहृष्टः ।
ननन्द वृन्दारकवृन्दवन्द्य-
पादारविन्दस्य पुरः स विप्रः ॥४॥
```
*yathā-tathā tat sakalaṁ viditvā
jñātaṁ tadā 'jñātam iti prahṛṣṭaḥ |
nananda vṛndāraka-vṛnda-vandya-
pādāravindasya puraḥ sa vipraḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *yathā-tathā* — just as it was; *tat* — that; *sakalam* — all; *viditvā* — having learned; *jñātam* — [is] known; *tadā* — now; *ajñātam* — the unknown; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *prahṛṣṭaḥ* — delighted; *nananda* — rejoiced; *vṛndāraka* — of the gods; *vṛnda* — by the host; *vandya* — worshipped; *pāda* — feet; *aravindasya* — of [Him whose] lotus; *puraḥ* — before; *saḥ* — that; *vipraḥ* — brahmana.

**Translation.** Having learned all that just as it was, and delighted [at the thought] "Now the unknown has become known", that brahmana rejoiced before Him whose lotus feet are worshipped by the host of the gods.

**Note.** The edition has *तदाज्ञातम्*; we read *tadā 'jñātam*, "now the unknown [has become] known" (the metre is not affected).

### 5

**5.**

```
ज्ञात्वाथ तस्याशयमेव सद्यः
स्वयं स्वपुत्रेण सदादरेण ।
प्रस्थापयामास सितेतराद्रौ
प्रभुं जगन्नाथदिदृक्षुमञ्जः ॥५॥
```
*jñātvātha tasyāśayam eva sadyaḥ
svayaṁ sva-putreṇa sadādareṇa |
prasthāpayāmāsa sitetarādrau
prabhuṁ jagannātha-didṛkṣum añjaḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *jñātvā* — having understood; *atha* — then; *tasya* — His; *āśayam* — intention; *eva* — indeed; *sadyaḥ* — at once; *svayam* — himself; *sva* — with his own; *putreṇa* — son; *sat* — with due; *ādareṇa* — respect; *prasthāpayāmāsa* — sent; *sita-itara* — other than white (blue); *adrau* — to the mountain; *prabhum* — the Lord; *jagannātha* — Jagannatha; *didṛkṣum* — wishing to see; *añjaḥ* — straight.

**Translation.** Then, having understood His intention, he at once, with due respect, himself sent the Lord, who wished to see Jagannatha, straight to the Blue Mountain together with his own son.

**Note.** *Sitetarādri* ("the mountain other than white") is Nilachala, the "Blue Mountain", the hill of Jagannatha's temple.

### 6

**6.**

```
स तेन सार्धं समुपेत्य तत्र
सुखं ततः स्वैरमपि प्रविश्य ।
ददर्श नीलाचलमौलिरत्नं
तदातिसौख्याम्बुधिमग्न आसीत् ॥६॥
```
*sa tena sārdhaṁ samupetya tatra
sukhaṁ tataḥ svairam api praviśya |
dadarśa nīlācala-mauli-ratnaṁ
tadāti-saukhyāmbudhi-magna āsīt ||6||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — He; *tena* — with him; *sārdham* — together; *samupetya* — having come; *tatra* — there; *sukham* — joyfully; *tataḥ* — then; *svairam* — freely; *api* — and; *praviśya* — having entered; *dadarśa* — beheld; *nīlācala* — of Nilachala; *mauli* — of the crown; *ratnam* — the jewel; *tadā* — then; *ati* — of the greatest; *saukhya* — bliss; *ambudhi* — in an ocean; *magnaḥ* — immersed; *āsīt* — was.

**Translation.** Having come there together with him, and then joyfully and freely entering, He beheld the crest-jewel of Nilachala, and was then immersed in an ocean of the greatest bliss.

### 7

**7.**

```
विलोक्य भूयो नतिभिः स्तवैश्च
नेत्राम्बुभिः स्वामभिषिच्य मूर्तिम् ।
प्रदक्षिणीकृत्य च पञ्चकृत्वः
कृच्छ्रेण तस्माद्बहिराययौ सः ॥७॥
```
*vilokya bhūyo natibhiḥ stavaiś ca
netrāmbubhiḥ svām abhiṣicya mūrtim |
pradakṣiṇīkṛtya ca pañca-kṛtvaḥ
kṛcchreṇa tasmād bahir āyayau saḥ ||7||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — beholding; *bhūyaḥ* — again and again; *natibhiḥ* — with obeisances; *stavaiḥ* — with hymns; *ca* — and; *netra* — of the eyes; *ambubhiḥ* — with the waters; *svām* — His own; *abhiṣicya* — having bathed; *mūrtim* — form; *pradakṣiṇīkṛtya* — having circumambulated; *ca* — and; *pañca-kṛtvaḥ* — five times; *kṛcchreṇa* — with difficulty; *tasmāt* — from there; *bahiḥ* — out; *āyayau* — came; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** Beholding [Him] again and again with obeisances and hymns, having bathed His own form with the waters of His eyes, and having circumambulated five times, He came out from there with difficulty.

**Note.** *Svām mūrtim*, "His own form", may mean His own body, drenched with tears, or Jagannatha, the Lord's own Deity form ⟨?⟩.

### 8

**8.**

```
इति प्रभुस्तत्र विलोक्य कान्तं
ननन्द नीलाचलमौलिरत्नम् ।
स्वनामरत्नेन विधाय हारं
कण्ठे वहन्नेव रराज नित्यम् ॥८॥
```
*iti prabhus tatra vilokya kāntaṁ
nananda nīlācala-mauli-ratnam |
sva-nāma-ratnena vidhāya hāraṁ
kaṇṭhe vahann eva rarāja nityam ||8||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *tatra* — there; *vilokya* — beholding; *kāntam* — lovely; *nananda* — rejoiced; *nīlācala* — of Nilachala; *mauli* — of the crown; *ratnam* — the jewel; *sva* — of His own; *nāma* — name; *ratnena* — from the jewel; *vidhāya* — having made; *hāram* — a necklace; *kaṇṭhe* — on the neck; *vahan* — wearing; *eva* — indeed; *rarāja* — shone; *nityam* — always.

**Translation.** Thus the Lord rejoiced, beholding there the lovely crest-jewel of Nilachala. Having made a necklace of the jewel of His own name, and ever wearing it on His neck, He shone.

**Note.** That is, He chanted the Name unceasingly.

### 9

**9.**

```
मुकुन्ददत्तादिभिरात्मलोकैः
स तत्र नाथः कतिचिद्दिनानि ।
विलोकयन्नीलगिरीन्द्ररत्नं
निनाय कौतूहलपूर्णचित्तः ॥९॥
```
*mukunda-dattādibhir ātma-lokaiḥ
sa tatra nāthaḥ katicid dināni |
vilokayan nīla-girīndra-ratnaṁ
nināya kautūhala-pūrṇa-cittaḥ ||9||*

**Word for word:** *mukunda-datta* — with Mukunda Datta; *ādibhiḥ* — and others; *ātma* — His own; *lokaiḥ* — with people; *saḥ* — He; *tatra* — there; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *katicit* — some; *dināni* — days; *vilokayan* — beholding; *nīla* — of the Blue; *giri-indra* — king of mountains; *ratnam* — the jewel; *nināya* — spent; *kautūhala* — with eagerness; *pūrṇa* — filled; *cittaḥ* — [with] heart.

**Translation.** With Mukunda Datta and others of His own, the Lord spent some days there beholding the jewel of the Blue King of mountains, His heart filled with eagerness.

### 10

**10.**

```
स एकदा चेतसि सार्वभौमो
महीसुराग्र्यः कलयांचकार ।
प्रभावमैश्वर्यमिदं समस्तं
मनुष्यभावादविदन् कृपालोः ॥१०॥
```
*sa ekadā cetasi sārvabhaumo
mahīsurāgryaḥ kalayāṁ cakāra |
prabhāvam aiśvaryam idaṁ samastaṁ
manuṣya-bhāvād avidan kṛpāloḥ ||10||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — that; *ekadā* — once; *cetasi* — in the mind; *sārvabhaumaḥ* — Sarvabhauma; *mahī-sura* — of the gods of the earth (brahmanas); *agryaḥ* — the foremost; *kalayāṁ cakāra* — considered; *prabhāvam* — the power; *aiśvaryam* — the majesty; *idam* — this; *samastam* — all; *manuṣya* — human; *bhāvāt* — because of the condition; *avidan* — not knowing; *kṛpāloḥ* — of the Merciful One.

**Translation.** Once Sarvabhauma, the foremost of the brahmanas, reflected in his mind, not knowing, because of [His] human condition, all this power and majesty of the Merciful One.

**Note.** Verses 10–16 are Sarvabhauma's reflections.

### 11

**11.**

```
अखण्डपाण्डित्यसमुद्रवीचि-
प्रवाहकल्लोलकुलैरमन्दै-
र्यस्य प्रकामं बधिरीकृतोऽभू-
द्बृहस्पतिर्जाड्यमयं समेतः ॥११॥
```
*akhaṇḍa-pāṇḍitya-samudra-vīci-
pravāha-kallola-kulair amandair
yasya prakāmaṁ badhirīkṛto 'bhūd
bṛhaspatir jāḍyamayaṁ sametaḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *akhaṇḍa* — of unbroken; *pāṇḍitya* — learning; *samudra* — of the ocean; *vīci* — of the waves; *pravāha* — of the streams; *kallola* — of the billows; *kulaiḥ* — by the masses; *amandaiḥ* — mighty; *yasya* — by whose; *prakāmam* — utterly; *badhirīkṛtaḥ* — deafened; *abhūt* — became; *bṛhaspatiḥ* — Brihaspati; *jāḍya* — stupor; *mayam* — consisting of; *sametaḥ* — having fallen into.

**Translation.** He by whose mighty masses of billows, by whose streams of waves from the ocean of unbroken learning, Brihaspati himself was utterly deafened and fell into a stupor —

**Note.** *Yasya* ("he by whose") is taken up by *sa eva* ("that very one") in verse 12, which points to Sarvabhauma ⟨?⟩. Brihaspati is the preceptor of the gods, the very model of learning. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 12

**12.**

```
स एव सम्भावितदम्भराशि-
र्गभीरधीर्यत्प्रभुपादपद्मम् ।
न वेद तन्नो खलु चित्रमेत-
न्न वेत्ति पाण्डित्यकुलादिलेशम् ॥१२॥
```
*sa eva sambhāvita-dambha-rāśir
gabhīra-dhīr yat prabhu-pāda-padmam |
na veda tan no khalu citram etan
na vetti pāṇḍitya-kulādi-leśam ||12||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ eva* — that very one; *sambhāvita* — esteemed; *dambha* — of pride; *rāśiḥ* — a heap; *gabhīra* — deep; *dhīḥ* — [of] intellect; *yat* — that; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *pāda* — of the feet; *padmam* — the lotus; *na veda* — did not know; *tat* — that; *no khalu* — not at all; *citram* — a wonder; *etat* — this; *na vetti* — does not know; *pāṇḍitya* — of the learning; *kula* — of the lineage; *ādi* — and so on; *leśam* — a particle.

**Translation.** — he, that very one, a heap of esteemed pride, deep of intellect, did not know the lotus feet of the Lord. That is no wonder at all: he does not know even a particle of His learning, lineage and so on.

**Note.** The edition has *yan na … na veda*, a double negative; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *yat prabhu-pāda-padmaṁ na veda*, and so we read: "that he did not know the feet of the Lord is no wonder".

### 13

**13.**

```
असौ महात्मा पुरुषप्रधानो
वयस्थ एव न्यसनं चकार ।
यदीदृशं स्वान्तरलं तदालं
विचिन्तितैर्नस्त्वयि कष्टमेतत् ॥१३॥
```
*asau mahātmā puruṣa-pradhāno
vayastha eva nyasanaṁ cakāra |
yadīdṛśaṁ svāntaralaṁ tad ālaṁ ⟨?⟩
vicintitair nas tvayi kaṣṭam etat ||13||*

**Word for word:** *asau* — this; *mahātmā* — great soul; *puruṣa* — among men; *pradhānaḥ* — the foremost; *vayasthaḥ* — while young; *eva* — indeed; *nyasanam* — renunciation; *cakāra* — has made; *yadi* — if; *īdṛśam* — such; *sva-antaram* — His heart; *alam* — enough; *tat* — then; *ālam* ⟨?⟩ — enough; *vicintitaiḥ* — with deliberations; *naḥ* — our; *tvayi* — for You; *kaṣṭam* — hard; *etat* — this.

**Translation.** "This great soul, the foremost among men, has embraced renunciation while still young. If such is His heart, then enough of our deliberations. But for You this is hard!

**Note.** The second half is obscure. The edition has *स्वान्तरलं तदालं*, perhaps corrupt ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *yad īdṛśaṁ svāntar alaṁ tadā laṁ / vicintitair na tvayi kaṣṭam etat*, with *na*: "this is not hard for You".

### 14

**14.**

```
अनेकधा पुरुषरत्नचिह्नै-
र्मनोरमः सर्वजगज्जनस्य ।
कथं नु कालं गमयिष्यतीमं
सन्न्यासधर्मप्रतिपालनेन ॥१४॥
```
*anekadhā puruṣa-ratna-cihnair
manoramaḥ sarva-jagaj-janasya |
kathaṁ nu kālaṁ gamayiṣyatīmaṁ
sannyāsa-dharma-pratipālanena ||14||*

**Word for word:** *anekadhā* — manifold; *puruṣa* — among men; *ratna* — of a jewel; *cihnaiḥ* — by the marks; *manoramaḥ* — charming; *sarva* — of the whole; *jagat* — world; *janasya* — to the people; *katham nu* — how indeed; *kālam* — time; *gamayiṣyati* — will He pass; *imam* — this; *sannyāsa* — of sannyasa; *dharma* — of the duties; *pratipālanena* — by the observance.

**Translation.** He who by the manifold marks of a jewel among men charms the people of the whole world — how indeed will He pass this time in observing the duties of sannyasa?

**Note.** In the first pada the fifth syllable (*pu-* of *puruṣa*) is light, where both *indravajrā* and *upendravajrā* require a heavy one; perhaps the poetic form *pūruṣa* was intended ⟨?⟩.

### 15

**15.**

```
असौ महावंशसमुद्भवश्च
महाशयश्चाल्पवयोविकाशः ।
कलौ तदर्हां यतितां सुदुर्गां
कथं तरिष्यत्यहहातिकष्टम् ॥१५॥
```
*asau mahā-vaṁśa-samudbhavaś ca
mahāśayaś cālpa-vayo-vikāśaḥ |
kalau tad-arhāṁ yatitāṁ sudurgāṁ
kathaṁ tariṣyaty ahahāti-kaṣṭam ||15||*

**Word for word:** *asau* — He; *mahā* — of a great; *vaṁśa* — family; *samudbhavaḥ* — born; *ca* — and; *mahā-āśayaḥ* — magnanimous; *ca* — and; *alpa* — scarcely; *vayaḥ* — of age; *vikāśaḥ* — [whose] blossoming; *kalau* — in Kali; *tat* — for it; *arhām* — befitting; *yatitām* — asceticism; *su-durgām* — most arduous; *katham* — how; *tariṣyati* — will He get through; *ahaha* — alas; *ati* — exceedingly; *kaṣṭam* — hard.

**Translation.** He is born of a great family, He is magnanimous, and His youth has scarcely blossomed. How will He get through, in [the age of] Kali, the most arduous asceticism befitting it? Alas, how exceedingly hard!

### 16

**16.**

```
तदेतमत्यन्तसुशान्तचित्तं
संश्राव्य वेदान्तमजस्रमेव ।
करोमि वैराग्यरसेन भास्व-
ज्ज्ञानैकतानेन च मोक्षपान्थम् ॥१६॥
```
*tad etam atyanta-suśānta-cittaṁ
saṁśrāvya vedāntam ajasram eva |
karomi vairāgya-rasena bhāsvaj-
jñānaika-tānena ca mokṣa-pāntham ||16||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — therefore; *etam* — Him; *atyanta* — wholly; *su-śānta* — very peaceful; *cittam* — [whose] heart; *saṁśrāvya* — having made to hear; *vedāntam* — the Vedanta; *ajasram* — unceasingly; *eva* — indeed; *karomi* — I shall make; *vairāgya* — of detachment; *rasena* — by the taste; *bhāsvat* — to radiant; *jñāna* — knowledge; *eka-tānena* — [and] by whole-hearted devotion; *ca* — and; *mokṣa* — to liberation; *pāntham* — a wayfarer.

**Translation.** Therefore, by making Him, whose heart is wholly at peace, hear the Vedanta unceasingly, I shall make Him a wayfarer on the road to liberation, through the taste of detachment and through whole-hearted devotion to radiant knowledge."

**Note.** Sarvabhauma means to instruct the Lord in Advaita Vedanta. Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 6: for seven days Sarvabhauma expounds the Brahma-sutras.

### 17

**17.**

```
इत्यस्य हृन्मन्त्रितमाकलय्य
प्रभुः प्रफुल्लाम्बुजमञ्जुलास्यः ।
व्याप्य त्रिलोकीं स्फुरितानुकम्पा-

```
*ity asya hṛn-mantritam ākalayya

vyāpya trilokīṁ sphuritānukampā-
vilola-cetā manasā jahāsa ||17||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *asya* — his; *hṛt* — of the heart; *mantritam* — the design; *ākalayya* — having perceived; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *praphulla* — full-blown; *ambuja* — [like] a lotus; *mañjula* — charming; *āsyaḥ* — [with] face; *vyāpya* — pervading; *trilokīm* — the three worlds; *sphurita* — with flashing forth; *anukampā* — compassion; *vilola* — stirred; *cetāḥ* — [with] heart; *manasā* — in the mind; *jahāsa* — laughed.

**Translation.** Having perceived this design of his heart, the Lord, whose face was charming as a full-blown lotus, and whose heart was stirred by a compassion that flashed forth and pervaded the three worlds, laughed inwardly.

### 18

**18.**

```
अन्येद्युरुद्दामखरांशुराजि-
राजद्द्युतिः स्वैश्चरणानुरक्तैः ।
जगाम तस्यालयमात्तजोषो
दोषाकराकारमनोहरास्यः ॥१८॥
```
*anyedyur uddāma-kharāṁśu-rāji-
rājad-dyutiḥ svaiś caraṇānuraktaiḥ |
jagāma tasyālayam ātta-joṣo
doṣākarākāra-manoharāsyaḥ ||18||*

**Word for word:** *anyedyuḥ* — on the next day; *uddāma* — of the unrestrained; *khara* — hot; *aṁśu* — [sun's] rays; *rāji* — with the array; *rājat* — shining; *dyutiḥ* — [with] splendour; *svaiḥ* — with His own; *caraṇa* — to [His] feet; *anuraktaiḥ* — devoted; *jagāma* — went; *tasya* — his; *ālayam* — to the house; *ātta* — having taken; *joṣaḥ* — contentment; *doṣākara* — of the moon; *ākāra* — with the form; *manohara* — charming; *āsyaḥ* — [with] face.

**Translation.** On the next day, His splendour shining like the array of the rays of the fierce, unrestrained sun, His face charming with the form of the moon, the Lord, content, went to his house with His own [devotees], who were devoted to His feet.

**Note.** *Doṣākara* ("maker of the night") is the moon.

### 19

**19.**

```
विलोक्य नाथं सह शिष्यवृन्दैः
समुत्थितः स प्रणनाम शश्वत् ।
सदासनं चाथ निवेद्य तस्मिं-
स्तत्र स्थितोऽभूत् स्वयमासनस्थः ॥१९॥
```
*vilokya nāthaṁ saha śiṣya-vṛndaiḥ
samutthitaḥ sa praṇanāma śaśvat |
sad-āsanaṁ cātha nivedya tasmiṁs
tatra sthito 'bhūt svayam āsana-sthaḥ ||19||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *nātham* — the Lord; *saha* — together; *śiṣya* — of disciples; *vṛndaiḥ* — with the crowds; *samutthitaḥ* — having risen; *saḥ* — he; *praṇanāma* — bowed; *śaśvat* — again and again; *sat* — a fine; *āsanam* — seat; *ca* — and; *atha* — then; *nivedya* — having offered; *tasmin* — to Him; *tatra* — there; *sthitaḥ abhūt* — settled; *svayam* — himself; *āsana* — on a seat; *sthaḥ* — seated.

**Translation.** Seeing the Lord, he rose together with the crowds of his disciples and bowed down again and again. Then, having offered Him a fine seat, he himself settled there on a seat.

### 20

**20.**

```
उवाच विप्रो विनयेन नाथं
वेदान्त एतैः परिपठ्यतेऽत्र ।
भवादृशा योग्यतमाः शृणुध्वं
मनःकषायो यत आशु याति ॥२०॥
```
*uvāca vipro vinayena nāthaṁ
vedānta etaiḥ paripaṭhyate 'tra |
bhavādṛśā yogyatamāḥ śṛṇudhvaṁ
manaḥ-kaṣāyo yata āśu yāti ||20||*

**Word for word:** *uvāca* — said; *vipraḥ* — the brahmana; *vinayena* — with humility; *nātham* — to the Lord; *vedāntaḥ* — the Vedanta; *etaiḥ* — by these; *paripaṭhyate* — is studied; *atra* — here; *bhavādṛśāḥ* — those like You; *yogyatamāḥ* — [are] most worthy; *śṛṇudhvam* — listen; *manaḥ* — of the mind; *kaṣāyaḥ* — the impurity; *yataḥ* — whereby; *āśu* — quickly; *yāti* — goes away.

**Translation.** The brahmana humbly said to the Lord: "Here the Vedanta is studied by these [disciples]. Such as You are the most worthy [of it]. Listen, then, for by it the impurity of the mind quickly goes away."

**Note.** *Kaṣāya*, literally "astringent, dark", is in Yoga and Vedanta the residual impurity of the mind.

### 21

**21.**

```
अधीतमध्यापितमेतदुच्चै-
रनेकशस्तत् पुनरप्यमुष्य ।
प्रभोः समीपे धरणीसुराग्र्यो
बभूव संपाठयितुं प्रमत्तः ॥२१॥
```
*adhītam adhyāpitam etad uccair
anekaśas tat punar apy amuṣya |
prabhoḥ samīpe dharaṇī-surāgryo
babhūva saṁpāṭhayituṁ pramattaḥ ||21||*

**Word for word:** *adhītam* — [has been] studied; *adhyāpitam* — taught; *etat* — this; *uccaiḥ* — thoroughly; *anekaśaḥ* — many times; *tat* — therefore; *punaḥ api* — once again; *amuṣya* — of this; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *samīpe* — in the presence; *dharaṇī-sura* — of the gods of the earth (brahmanas); *agryaḥ* — the foremost; *babhūva* — set about; *saṁpāṭhayitum* — to teach; *pramattaḥ* — heedlessly.

**Translation.** "This I have thoroughly studied and taught many times" — and so the foremost of the brahmanas heedlessly set about teaching it once again in the presence of this Lord.

**Note.** *Pramatta*, "intoxicated, heedless": Sarvabhauma does not know whom he is teaching.

### 22

**22.**

```
साक्षान्महीगीष्पतिरेष चञ्च-
त्प्रागल्भ्यसंयुक्तवचा यथाधि ।
निर्वक्ति तत्तत् स निशम्य नाथः
शनैस्तदोद्ग्राहविधिं चकार ॥२२॥
```
*sākṣān mahī-gīṣpatir eṣa cañcat-
prāgalbhya-saṁyukta-vacā yathādhi |
nirvakti tat tat sa niśamya nāthaḥ
śanais tadodgrāha-vidhiṁ cakāra ||22||*

**Word for word:** *sākṣāt* — in person; *mahī* — of the earth; *gīṣpatiḥ* — the Brihaspati; *eṣaḥ* — this; *cañcat* — flashing; *prāgalbhya* — with boldness; *saṁyukta* — filled; *vacāḥ* — [with] speech; *yathā-adhi* — as he understood; *nirvakti* — expounds; *tat tat* — this and that; *saḥ* — He; *niśamya* — having heard; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *śanaiḥ* — slowly; *tadā* — then; *udgrāha* — of objection; *vidhim* — the procedure; *cakāra* — began.

**Translation.** This Brihaspati of the earth in person expounded this and that as he understood it, with speech full of flashing boldness. Having heard him, the Lord then slowly began, in due order, to raise objections.

**Note.** *Udgrāha* in debate is the putting forward of a thesis or an objection. *Yathādhi* — "according to [his] understanding" (*yathā* + *adhi*) ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 23

**23.**

```
किमुच्यते कः खलु पूर्वपक्षः
किं वास्य राद्धान्तितमातनोषि ।
वेदान्तशास्त्रस्य न चायमर्थ-
स्तच्छ्रूयतां यत्तु निरूपयामः ॥२३॥
```
*kim ucyate kaḥ khalu pūrva-pakṣaḥ
kiṁ vāsya rāddhāntitam ātanoṣi |
vedānta-śāstrasya na cāyam arthas
tac chrūyatāṁ yat tu nirūpayāmaḥ ||23||*

**Word for word:** *kim* — what; *ucyate* — is said; *kaḥ* — what; *khalu* — indeed; *pūrva-pakṣaḥ* — [is] the initial thesis; *kim* — what; *vā* — or; *asya* — its; *rāddhāntitam* — conclusion; *ātanoṣi* — do you set forth; *vedānta* — of the Vedanta; *śāstrasya* — of the scripture; *na* — not; *ca* — and; *ayam* — this; *arthaḥ* — [is] the meaning; *tat* — therefore; *śrūyatām* — let it be heard; *yat* — what; *tu* — however; *nirūpayāmaḥ* — I shall set forth.

**Translation.** "What is being asserted here? What indeed is the initial thesis? Or what do you set forth as its conclusion? This is not the meaning of the Vedanta scripture. So hear what I shall set forth."

**Note.** *Pūrva-pakṣa* is the opponent's thesis, *siddhānta* (*rāddhānta*) the final conclusion: terms of Sanskrit disputation. *Nirūpayāmaḥ* is plural ("We shall set forth").

### 24

**24.**

```
इत्यस्य पक्षप्रतिपक्षरूपं
स पक्षमेकं स तु सज्जयित्वा ।
अद्वैतवादं विनिरस्य भक्ति-
संस्थापकं स्वीयमतं जगाद ॥२४॥
```
*ity asya pakṣa-pratipakṣa-rūpaṁ
sa pakṣam ekaṁ sa tu sajjayitvā |
advaita-vādaṁ vinirasya bhakti-
saṁsthāpakaṁ svīya-mataṁ jagāda ||24||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus [having spoken]; *asya* — his; *pakṣa* — of the thesis; *pratipakṣa* — of the counter-thesis; *rūpam* — in the form; *saḥ* — He; *pakṣam* — a thesis; *ekam* — one; *saḥ tu* — He; *sajjayitvā* — having made ready; *advaita* — of non-duality; *vādam* — the doctrine; *vinirasya* — having refuted; *bhakti* — bhakti; *saṁsthāpakam* — establishing; *svīya* — His own; *matam* — view; *jagāda* — expounded.

**Translation.** Having spoken thus, He made ready a thesis of His own in the form of a counter-thesis to his thesis, refuted the doctrine of non-duality and expounded His own view, which establishes bhakti.

### 25

**25.**

```
इत्थं प्रमाणैरखिलैश्च शक्त्या
तात्पर्यतो लक्षणया च गौण्या ।
मुख्याजहत्स्वार्थतदन्यमिश्र-
स्वरूपया स्वं मतमाबभाषे ॥२५॥
```
*itthaṁ pramāṇair akhilaiś ca śaktyā
tātparyato lakṣaṇayā ca gauṇyā |
mukhyā-jahat-svārtha-tad-anya-miśra-
svarūpayā svaṁ matam ābabhāṣe ||25||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *pramāṇaiḥ* — by the means of valid knowledge; *akhilaiḥ* — all; *ca* — and; *śaktyā* — by the [denotative] power [of words]; *tātparyataḥ* — by intention; *lakṣaṇayā* — by indication; *ca* — and; *gauṇyā* — by the figurative [sense]; *mukhyā* — primary; *jahat-svārtha* — abandoning its own meaning; *tat-anya* — the other than that [not abandoning it]; *miśra* — mixed; *svarūpayā* — by [indication] of the kinds; *svam* — His own; *matam* — view; *ābabhāṣe* — expounded.

**Translation.** Thus, by all the means of valid knowledge and by the power of words — by intention (*tātparya*), by indication (*lakṣaṇā*) and by the figurative sense (*gauṇī*), by the primary sense (*mukhyā*), and by indication that abandons its own meaning, that does not abandon it, and that is mixed — He expounded His own view.

**Note.** The categories of Sanskrit semantics are listed here: *mukhyā vṛtti*, the primary sense; *gauṇī*, the figurative sense based on similarity; *lakṣaṇā*, indication, of which there are three kinds: *jahat-svārthā* (the word's own meaning is abandoned), *ajahat-svārthā* (it is retained) and the mixed *jahad-ajahat-svārthā*; *tātparya*, the overall intention of a statement. *Śakti* is the denotative power of a word. The form *mukhyā* at the beginning of a compound is unusual ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 26

**26.**

```
असौ वितण्डाच्छलनिग्रहाद्यै-
र्निरस्तधीरप्यथ पूर्वपक्षम् ।
चकार विप्रः प्रभुना स चाशु
स्वसिद्धसिद्धान्तवता निरस्तः ॥२६॥
```
*asau vitaṇḍā-cchala-nigrahādyair
nirasta-dhīr apy atha pūrva-pakṣam |
cakāra vipraḥ prabhunā sa cāśu
sva-siddha-siddhāntavatā nirastaḥ ||26||*

**Word for word:** *asau* — this; *vitaṇḍā* — by cavilling; *chala* — by quibbles; *nigraha* — by "points of defeat"; *ādyaiḥ* — and the like; *nirasta* — refuted; *dhīḥ* — [whose] intellect; *api* — though; *atha* — then; *pūrva-pakṣam* — a thesis; *cakāra* — put forward; *vipraḥ* — the brahmana; *prabhunā* — by the Lord; *saḥ* — he; *ca* — and; *āśu* — quickly; *sva* — by itself; *siddha* — established; *siddhānta* — conclusion; *vatā* — by [Him] possessing; *nirastaḥ* — was refuted.

**Translation.** Though his intellect had been refuted, the brahmana then put forward a thesis [again] by cavilling, quibbles, "points of defeat" and the like; and he was quickly refuted by the Lord, whose conclusion is self-established.

**Note.** *Vitaṇḍā* (cavilling: disputation without a thesis of one's own), *chala* (verbal quibble) and *nigraha-sthāna* (a ground for declaring the opponent defeated) are terms of Nyaya.

### 27

**27.**

```
अद्वैतवादी प्रथमः पदाब्ज-
वादी प्रभुश्च प्रतिभैकसिन्धुः ।
तौ भक्तसेव्यौ बहु दीर्घकालं
```

vādī prabhuś ca pratibhaika-sindhuḥ |
tau bhakta-sevyau bahu dīrgha-kālaṁ
vadāvadair ninyatur anyathaiva ||27||*

**Word for word:** *advaita* — of non-duality; *vādī* — a proponent; *prathamaḥ* — the first; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotus; *vādī* — a proponent; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *ca* — and; *pratibhā* — of genius; *eka* — the one; *sindhuḥ* — ocean; *tau* — the two; *bhakta* — by adherents; *sevyau* — attended; *bahu* — very; *dīrgha* — long; *kālam* — time; *vadāvadaiḥ* — in disputations; *ninyatuḥ* — spent; *anyathā* — differently; *eva* — indeed.

**Translation.** The first was a proponent of non-duality, and the Lord, the one ocean of genius, a proponent of the lotus feet. Attended by their adherents, the two of them spent a very long time in disputations, each [arguing] in his own way.

**Note.** *Padābja-vādī* ("proponent of the lotus feet") — a defender of personal devotion to God.

### 28

**28.**

```
अथैष विस्मेरमना द्विजाग्र्यो
हृदा हृदि व्याकुलितो जगाद ।
क एष मत्प्रातिभखण्डनार्थ-
```

hṛdā hṛdi vyākulito jagāda |
ka eṣa mat-prātibha-khaṇḍanārtham
ihāvatīrṇaḥ kim u gīṣpatiḥ syāt ||28||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *eṣaḥ* — this; *vismera* — astonished; *manāḥ* — [with] mind; *dvija* — of the brahmanas; *agryaḥ* — the foremost; *hṛdā hṛdi* — in the depth of his heart; *vyākulitaḥ* — troubled; *jagāda* — said; *kaḥ* — who; *eṣaḥ* — this; *mat* — my; *prātibha* — genius; *khaṇḍana* — of shattering; *artham* — for the sake; *iha* — here; *avatīrṇaḥ* — has descended; *kim u* — can it be that; *gīṣpatiḥ* — Brihaspati; *syāt* — He is.

**Translation.** Then the foremost of the brahmanas, his mind astonished, troubled in the depth of his heart, said [to himself]: "Who is He who has descended here to shatter my genius? Can He be Brihaspati?

### 29

**29.**

```
इतीह तर्को मम सर्वदासी-
द्बृहस्पतिर्मत्प्रतिभासमुद्रे ।
न पारमासादयिता कदापि
सदोद्यतः सन्नपि बुद्धिनावा ॥२९॥
```
*itīha tarko mama sarvadāsīd
bṛhaspatir mat-pratibhā-samudre |
na pāram āsādayitā kadāpi
sadodyataḥ sann api buddhi-nāvā ||29||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *iha* — here; *tarkaḥ* — reasoning; *mama* — my; *sarvadā* — always; *āsīt* — was; *bṛhaspatiḥ* — Brihaspati; *mat* — of my; *pratibhā* — genius; *samudre* — in the ocean; *na* — not; *pāram* — the farther shore; *āsādayitā* — will reach; *kadā api* — ever; *sadā* — always; *udyataḥ* — striving; *san api* — even though being; *buddhi* — of intellect; *nāvā* — in the boat.

**Translation.** My reasoning has always been this: Brihaspati, though ever striving in the boat of his intellect, will never reach the farther shore of the ocean of my genius.

### 30

**30.**

```
अयं तु कैशोरवयाः कियद्वा-
प्यधीतमास्ते वद पाठितं किम् ।
```

**Word for word:** *ayam* — He; *tu* — but; *kaiśora* — of boyhood; *vayāḥ* — [is of] age; *kiyat* — how much; *vā api* — indeed; *adhītam āste* — has [He] studied; *vada* — say; *pāṭhitam* — has [He] taught; *kim* — what; *tathā api* — even so; *śaktiḥ* — power; *mama* — my; *na eva* — not at all; *bhūtā* — served; *parābhavāya* — for the defeat; *asya* — His; *mama* — my; *eva* — only; *sā* — it; *abhūt* — served [for].

### 31

**31.**

```
तदेष कृष्णः खलु नान्यथैव
```

### 32

**32.**

```
निर्यद्विलोलाक्षिसराः स रेमे
```

**Translation.** With streams flowing from his trembling eyes, he rejoiced; with his hair standing on end, he propitiated the Almighty with praises and obeisances. And He, the one ocean of mercy, then became gracious [to him].

**Note.** *Samudgama* (literally "rising") here probably means the bristling of the hair on the body. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 33

**33.**

```
प्रदर्शयामास चतुर्भुजत्वं
दिवाकराणां शतकोटिभास्वत् ।
ततोऽधिकं सोऽपि ननन्द विप्र-
```

divākarāṇāṁ śata-koṭi-bhāsvat |

**Word for word:** *pradarśayāmāsa* — revealed; *catuḥ* — four-; *bhujatvam* — armed form; *divākarāṇām* — of suns; *śata-koṭi* — a thousand million; *bhāsvat* — shining [like]; *tataḥ* — thereupon; *adhikam* — still more; *saḥ api* — he too; *nananda* — rejoiced; *vipraḥ* — the brahmana; *tataḥ* — than that; *adhikam* — more exalted; *ca* — and; *stavam* — a hymn; *api* — also; *akārṣīt* — composed.

**Translation.** He revealed His four-armed form, shining like a thousand million suns. Thereupon the brahmana rejoiced still more, and composed a hymn more exalted still.

**Note.** In the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 6.202) the Lord shows Sarvabhauma first His four-armed form and then His own form, dark-complexioned and holding the flute; in the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Antya 3) He shows him a six-armed form. Karnapura speaks of the four-armed form.

### 34

**34.**

```
यद्यत् स भूमीसुरसङ्घमुख्य-
स्तुष्टाव तुष्टः सुमहाप्रगल्भः ।
```

**Word for word:** *yat yat* — whatever; *saḥ* — that; *bhūmī-sura* — of brahmanas; *saṅgha* — of the assembly; *mukhyaḥ* — chief; *tuṣṭāva* — praised; *tuṣṭaḥ* — delighted; *su-mahā* — most; *pragalbhaḥ* — eloquent; *tat tat* — that; *na* — not; *vācaspatiḥ* — Vachaspati (Brihaspati); *api* — even; *abhīkṣṇam* — again and again; *prayāsataḥ* — with effort; *api* — even; *prabhavet* — could master; *bhaviṣṇuḥ* — capable.

**Translation.** Whatever [hymns] that chief of the assembly of brahmanas, delighted and most eloquent, offered in praise, not even Vachaspati, capable though he is, could master, even striving again and again.

**Note.** The edition has *प्रभवेद्धविष्णुः*; perhaps a corruption of *prabhaviṣṇuḥ*, "capable" ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**35.**


**36.**


**Translation.** When all had gone, the Lord, walking alone, sat down comfortably at the foot of some tree, untied the manuscript and looked at it for a long time with delight.

**39.**


**Translation.** Examining it all round, the Lord saw there a poem of Sri Sarvabhauma, and then among its words He noticed the word "Krishna".

**Note.** Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 6.252–255: Sarvabhauma's verses glorifying Chaitanya as Krishna (*vairāgya-vidyā-nija-bhakti-yoga…*); Mukunda copied them onto a wall, and the Lord, having read them, tore up the leaf.

**40.**


**41.**


**42.**


### 44

**44.**

```

```

**Word for word:** *amuṣya* — his; *sevā* — of service; *vidhim* — the rite; *antareṇa* — apart from; *na kiñcana api* — nothing whatever; *prayataḥ* — diligently; *kariṣye* — shall I do; *iti* — thus [resolving]; *eva* — indeed; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *payodhiḥ* — the ocean; *kṣetram* — to the Kshetra; *samāyāt* — came back; *prahara* — of a watch; *eka* — of one; *madhye* — within.

### 45

**45.**

```
आचार्यवर्यानयनाय कञ्चि-

स तु त्वरावान् समुपेत्य गोपी-

```
*ācārya-varyānayanāya kañcit

sa tu tvarāvān samupetya gopī-
nāthaṁ tam ācārya-varaṁ jagāda ||45||*

**Word for word:** *ācārya* — of acharyas; *varya* — the best; *ānayanāya* — to fetch; *kañcit* — someone; *saṁpreṣayāmāsa* — [He] sent; *jhaṭiti* — at once; *atha api* — then; *saḥ* — he; *tu* — and; *tvarāvān* — hastening; *samupetya* — having come up; *gopīnātham* — to Gopinatha; *tam* — to that; *ācārya* — of acharyas; *varam* — the best; *jagāda* — said.

**Translation.** Then He at once sent someone to fetch the best of acharyas. And that [man], hastening, came up to Gopinatha, that best of acharyas, and said:

**Note.** The subject is not named in the text. By the context it is the Lord, who has just returned to the Kshetra (44): the messenger announces that "Krishna-Chaitanya has come here" (46), and Gopinatha hastens "to Him" (48), while Sarvabhauma does not yet know of the Lord's return.

### 46

**46.**

```
आचार्य शीघ्रं समुपेहि कृष्ण-
चैतन्यदेवोऽयमिहागतोऽस्ति ।
किमात्थ रे किं वितथं समस्तं

```
*ācārya śīghraṁ samupehi kṛṣṇa-

gataḥ saharṣo diśi dakṣiṇasyām ||46||*

**Word for word:** *ācārya* — O Acharya; *śīghram* — quickly; *samupehi* — come; *kṛṣṇa-caitanya* — Krishna-Chaitanya; *devaḥ* — the divine; *ayam* — this; *iha* — here; *āgataḥ asti* — has come; *kim* — what; *āttha* — are you saying; *re* — hey; *kim* — can it be; *vitatham* — false; *samastam* — all; *gataḥ* — [He] went; *sa* — with; *harṣaḥ* — joy; *diśi* — in the direction; *dakṣiṇasyām* — southern.

**Translation.** "O Acharya, come quickly: the divine Krishna-Chaitanya has come here!" — "What are you saying, fellow? Can it all be false? [Why,] He joyfully went off to the south!

**Note.** The Lord's sannyasa name, Krishna-Chaitanya, is mentioned for the first time in this sarga.

### 47

**47.**

```
अस्माभिरेवायमनुव्रजद्भि-
र्विदूरतस्त्यक्त इतः कथं स्यात् ।
इत्युक्तवानेष पुनश्च तेन

```

**Word for word:** *asmābhiḥ* — by us; *eva* — ourselves; *ayam* — He; *anuvrajadbhiḥ* — accompanying [Him]; *vidūrataḥ* — far away; *tyaktaḥ* — was left; *itaḥ* — from here; *katham* — how; *syāt* — could [He] be [here]; *iti* — thus; *uktavān* — spoke; *eṣaḥ* — he; *punaḥ* — again; *ca* — and; *tena* — by that [man]; *satyam* — the truth; *bravīmi* — I speak; *iti* — thus; *asakṛt* — repeatedly; *saḥ* — he; *uktaḥ* — was told.

**Translation.** We ourselves accompanied Him and took leave of Him far from here — how could He be here?" Thus [Gopinatha] spoke, and the other told him again and again: "I am speaking the truth!"

**48.**

```
त्वरान्वितस्तन्निकटं स गोपी-

अवेक्ष्य तं हृष्टमना महात्मा
```

**Translation.** Gopinatha, the best of worthy acharyas, hastened to Him. Seeing Him, the great soul rejoiced at heart and said with wonder and affection:

### 49

**49.**

```
किमेतदाश्चर्यमतीव देव
कथं गतो वा कथमागतो वा ।
ततः प्रभुर्दन्तविसूनरोचि-
श्छटासमापृक्तविलोहितौष्ठः ।
उवाच माध्वीकरसाप्लुतेन
वचोविलासेन विलासवान् सः ॥४९॥
```
*kim etad āścaryam atīva deva
kathaṁ gato vā katham āgato vā |
tataḥ prabhur danta-visūna-roci-
cchaṭā-samāpṛkta-vilohitauṣṭhaḥ |
uvāca mādhvīka-rasāplutena
vaco-vilāsena vilāsavān saḥ ||49||*

**Word for word:** *kim* — what; *etat* — this; *āścaryam* — wonder; *atīva* — very great; *deva* — O Lord; *katham* — how; *gataḥ* — [did You] go; *vā* — indeed; *katham* — how; *āgataḥ* — [have You] come back; *vā* — indeed; *tataḥ* — then; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *danta* — of [His] teeth; *visūna* — [like] blossomed [flowers]; *roci* — of the radiance; *chaṭā* — with the lustre; *samāpṛkta* — mingled; *vilohita* — crimson; *oṣṭhaḥ* — [with] lips; *uvāca* — spoke; *mādhvīka* — of honey-wine; *rasa* — with the juice; *āplutena* — steeped; *vacaḥ* — of speech; *vilāsena* — with the play; *vilāsavān* — graceful; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** "What is this very great wonder, O Lord? How did You go, and how have You come back?" Then the graceful Lord, His crimson lips mingled with the lustre of the radiance of His teeth, which were like blossomed flowers, spoke with a play of words steeped in the juice of honey-wine:

**Note.** A verse of six padas. *Visūna*, "blossomed": the usual *prasūna* ("flower") would break the metre.

**50.**


### 51

**51.**

```
असौ महात्मा भगवत्स्वरूपो
जगत्त्रयीत्राणपरः सदीहः ।
यदस्य वक्त्रादुदभूत् स कृष्ण-
```

*asau mahātmā bhagavat-svarūpo

### 52

**52.**


### 53

**53.**

```
इत्यस्य वाचं परमां दुरूहां
श्रुतिस्मृतीनामपि सारभूताम् ।
अथैव मृग्यां परिमृग्य विप्रः
```

*ity asya vācaṁ paramāṁ durūhāṁ

### 54

**54.**

```
अहो महाकारुणिकस्य चेष्टां
```

**Word for word:** *aho* — ah; *mahā-kāruṇikasya* — of the supremely compassionate; *ceṣṭām* — the conduct; *paśya* — see; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *dīna* — to the wretched; *jane* — people; *kṛpāloḥ* — merciful; *kaḥ vā* — who indeed; *asya* — His; *jānātu* — can know; *mahā* — very; *durāpam* — hard to attain; *māhātmyam* — greatness; *ete* — these [people]; *khalu* — indeed; *kīṭa* — to worms; *kalpāḥ* — similar.

**Translation.** "Ah, see the conduct of the supremely compassionate Lord, merciful to wretched people! Who can know His greatness, so very hard to attain? These people are indeed like worms.

### 55

**55.**


**Word for word:** *aho* — ah; *mahā-kāruṇikasya* — of the supremely compassionate [Lord]; *paśya* — see; *jagat* — the world; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *pūra* — with a flood; *bhṛtam* — filled; *cikīrṣoḥ* — wishing to make; *anugraham* — the favour; *samprati* — now; *sārvabhaume* — to Sarvabhauma; *deva-īśa* — the lord of the gods; *kalpaiḥ* — by those like; *api* — even; *yaḥ* — which; *durāpaḥ* — [is] hard to attain.

**Translation.** Ah, see the favour now [shown] to Sarvabhauma by the supremely compassionate [Lord], who wishes to fill the world with a flood of mercy — [a favour] hard to attain even for those like the lord of the gods!

### 56

**56.**


**Word for word:** *vedāntinām* — of the Vedantists; *maṇḍala* — of the circle; *sārvabhaumaḥ* — the emperor; *saḥ* — that; *sārvabhaumaḥ* — Sarvabhauma; *gata* — devoid; *bhakti* — of devotion; *gandhaḥ* — [even of] the scent; *daivena* — by destiny; *padya* — in a verse; *udgata* — having appeared; *kṛṣṇa* — of Krishna; *nāmā* — [with] the name; *babhūva* — became; *yuṣmat* — Your; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *adhipātram* — the supreme vessel.

### 61

**61.**

```
ततो बहिश्चेलकटीरसूत्रे
प्रगृह्य नामग्रहणोत्ककण्ठः ।
प्रासादमध्ये प्रविवेश नाथो
```

*tato bahiś-cela-kaṭīra-sūtre

**Translation.** Then, having put on the outer cloth and the waist-cord, His throat eager to repeat the name, the Lord entered the temple, as the autumn moon [ascends] the Mountain of Rising.

```
इत्यादि यस्योक्तिर्मधु प्रसिद्धं
स सार्वभौमः कथया न कथ्यः ॥९३॥
```
*tathāpy asabhya-smṛti-hetukatvād
aślīla-doṣo 'yam iti bravīmi |
ity ādi yasyoktir madhu prasiddhaṁ
sa sārvabhaumaḥ kathayā na kathyaḥ ||93||*

**Word for word:** *tathā api* — even so; *asabhya* — of something indecent; *smṛti* — the recollection; *hetukatvāt* — because of causing; *aślīla* — of indecorum; *doṣaḥ* — the fault; *ayam* — this [is]; *iti* — thus; *bravīmi* — I say; *iti ādi* — such and the like; *yasya* — whose; *uktiḥ* — sayings; *madhu* — sweet; *prasiddham* — [are] well known; *saḥ* — that; *sārvabhaumaḥ* — Sarvabhauma; *kathayā* — by an account; *na* — not; *kathyaḥ* — describable.

**Translation.** "Even so, because it calls something indecent to mind, I say: this [word has] the fault of indecorum." That Sarvabhauma, whose sweet sayings such as this are well known, cannot be described by any account.

**Note.** *Aślīla-doṣa* is, in poetics, the fault of a word that evokes shameful, repulsive or inauspicious associations. For a devotee "mukti" (merging into Brahman) is such a word. Cf. the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 6.266–268.

```

प्रतिध्वनिग्रस्तदिशः क्वचापि ।
क्वच प्रसुप्तोरुकरालसत्त्व-
श्वासाग्निदीप्ता वनभूमिभागाः ॥१२४॥
```
*niṣkūja-śāntāḥ kva ca caṇḍa-śabda-
pratidhvani-grasta-diśaḥ kvacāpi |
kva ca prasuptoru-karāla-sattva-
śvāsāgni-dīptā vana-bhūmi-bhāgāḥ ||124||*

**Word for word:** *niṣkūja* — without the chirping [of birds]; *śāntāḥ* — silent; *kva ca* — somewhere; *caṇḍa* — of fierce; *śabda* — sounds; *pratidhvani* — by the echo; *grasta* — seized; *diśaḥ* — [whose] quarters [were]; *kva api* — somewhere; *kva ca* — somewhere; *prasupta* — sleeping; *uru* — of huge; *karāla* — fearsome; *sattva* — beasts; *śvāsa* — by the breath; *agni* — fiery; *dīptāḥ* — heated; *vana* — of the forest; *bhūmi* — of the land; *bhāgāḥ* — the stretches.

**Translation.** In some places the stretches of forest land were silent, without the chirping of birds; in others all their quarters were seized by the echo of fierce cries; and in others they were heated by the fiery breath of huge, fearsome beasts lying asleep there…

**Note.** *क्वच* (twice in the edition) is evidently *kva ca* ("and somewhere"), a variant of *kvacit*; the metre allows this reading. The verse is linked syntactically with the next (*yugmaka*).

### 125

**125.**

```
गोदावरीवेगमहानिनादा
भीमा गिरिप्रस्रवणा रवेण ।
श्रीगौरचन्द्रस्य वितेनुरुच्चैः
सुकोमलं चित्तमनाप्तधैर्यम् ॥१२५॥
```
*godāvarī-vega-mahā-ninādā
bhīmā giri-prasravaṇā raveṇa |
śrī-gauracandrasya vitenur uccaiḥ
sukomalaṁ cittam anāpta-dhairyam ||125||*

**Word for word:** *godāvarī* — of the Godavari; *vega* — of the rapids; *mahā* — great; *ninādāḥ* — [having] the roar; *bhīmāḥ* — fearsome; *giri* — mountain; *prasravaṇāḥ* — [having] torrents; *raveṇa* — with [their] din; *śrī-gauracandrasya* — of Sri Gaurachandra; *vitenuḥ* — made; *uccaiḥ* — greatly; *su-komalam* — most tender; *cittam* — the heart; *anāpta* — not retaining; *dhairyam* — composure.

**Translation.** …[stretches] full of the great roar of the Godavari's rapids, [full of] mountain torrents, fearsome with their din — [these] utterly robbed the most tender heart of Sri Gaurachandra of its composure.

**Note.** The subject of *vitenuḥ* is the stretches of forest land of v. 124. The plural forms in *-āḥ* can be taken as qualifying them ("[having] the great roar of the Godavari's rapids", "[having] mountain torrents"), or as further subjects: the great roar of the Godavari's rapids and the mountain torrents.

### 126

**126.**

```
क्षणात् स्खलत्पादविकम्प्रपक्षै-
श्चञ्चूपतद्बीजचयैः प्रपूर्णैः ।
शुकैर्दलद्दाडिमचुम्बवद्भि-
र्गोदावरीतीरवने स रेमे ॥१२६॥
```
*kṣaṇāt skhalat-pāda-vikampra-pakṣaiś
cañcū-patad-bīja-cayaiḥ prapūrṇaiḥ |
śukair dalad-dāḍima-cumbavadbhir
godāvarī-tīra-vane sa reme ||126||*

**Word for word:** *kṣaṇāt* — every moment; *skhalat* — slipping; *pāda* — [whose] feet [were]; *vikampra* — fluttering; *pakṣaiḥ* — [whose] wings [were]; *cañcū* — from the beaks; *patat* — falling; *bīja* — of seeds; *cayaiḥ* — [with] heaps; *prapūrṇaiḥ* — sated; *śukaiḥ* — in the parrots; *dalat* — split; *dāḍima* — pomegranates; *cumbavadbhiḥ* — kissing; *godāvarī* — of the Godavari; *tīra* — on the bank; *vane* — in the forest; *saḥ* — He; *reme* — took delight.

**Translation.** In the forest on the bank of the Godavari He took delight in the parrots "kissing" split pomegranates — sated, heaps of seeds tumbling from their beaks, their feet slipping every moment and their wings fluttering.

**Note.** Verses 126–129 form one description: the qualifiers in the locative case in vv. 127–129 refer to the "forest" (*vane*). *Skhalat-pāda* ("with slipping feet") is taken as referring to the birds.

### 127

**127.**

```
ताम्बूलवल्लीदलवृन्दमुच्चै-
र्भिन्दद्भिरुग्रैः क्रकचै रसद्भिः ।
अजस्रदीर्घेण विमुग्धझिल्ली-
झङ्काररावेण निकामरम्ये ॥१२७॥
```
*tāmbūla-vallī-dala-vṛndam uccair
bhindadbhir ugraiḥ krakacai rasadbhiḥ |
ajasra-dīrgheṇa vimugdha-jhillī-
jhaṅkāra-rāveṇa nikāma-ramye ||127||*

**Word for word:** *tāmbūla* — of betel; *vallī* — of the vines; *dala* — of leaves; *vṛndam* — the clusters; *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *bhindadbhiḥ* — tearing; *ugraiḥ* — by fierce; *krakacaiḥ* — "saws"; *rasadbhiḥ* — screeching; *ajasra* — ceaseless; *dīrgheṇa* — long-drawn; *vimugdha* — enchanting; *jhillī* — of cicadas; *jhaṅkāra* — of the chirring; *rāveṇa* — with the sound; *nikāma* — exceedingly; *ramye* — in [the forest] delightful.

**Translation.** [That forest was] exceedingly delightful: fierce, screeching "saws" loudly tore through the clusters of betel-vine leaves, and the ceaseless, long-drawn, enchanting chirring of cicadas [filled it].

**Note.** The edition has *क्रकचैरसद्भिः* (*krakacair asadbhiḥ*, "non-existent"?). We read *krakacai rasadbhiḥ* ("screeching"), cf. the same word in v. 128; the script allows this division as it stands. *Krakaca* means "a saw"; evidently parrots are meant, their beaks likened to a saw.

### 128

**128.**

```
ज्योतिर्गणाचुम्बिभिरम्बुदाभै-
स्तमालमालार्जुनकोविदारैः ।
नानाविधैः पत्ररथै रसद्भि-
श्चमूरुवृन्दैश्चमरैश्च जुष्टे ॥१२८॥
```
*jyotir-gaṇā-cumbibhir ambudābhais
tamāla-mālārjuna-kovidāraiḥ |
nānā-vidhaiḥ patra-rathai rasadbhiś
camūru-vṛndaiś camaraiś ca juṣṭe ||128||*

**Word for word:** *jyotiḥ* — of the stars; *gaṇa* — the hosts; *ā-cumbibhiḥ* — touching; *ambuda* — clouds; *ābhaiḥ* — resembling; *tamāla* — of tamalas; *mālā* — by rows; *arjuna* — by arjunas; *kovidāraiḥ* — by kovidaras; *nānā-vidhaiḥ* — of many kinds; *patra-rathaiḥ* — by birds; *rasadbhiḥ* — crying; *camūru* — of chamuru deer; *vṛndaiḥ* — by herds; *camaraiḥ* — by yaks; *ca* — and; *juṣṭe* — in [the forest] frequented.

**Translation.** [It was] frequented by rows of tamalas, by arjunas and kovidaras that touched the hosts of stars and resembled clouds, by crying birds of many kinds, by herds of chamuru deer and by yaks.

**Note.** The edition has *जुष्टैः*; we read *juṣṭe*, a locative agreeing with "forest" (v. 126), like *nikāma-ramye* in v. 127. *Patra-ratha* ("whose chariot is wings") is a bird.

### 129

**129.**

```
अर्कप्रभापक्वविहीनसान्द्र-
स्निग्धातिसच्छीतलचारुभूमौ ।
अकृत्रिमालेपनिपीतमूले
वापीतडागादिनिरन्तराले ॥१२९॥
```
*arka-prabhā-pakva-vihīna-sāndra-
snigdhāti-sac-chītala-cāru-bhūmau |
akṛtrimālepa-nipīta-mūle
vāpī-taḍāgādi-nirantarāle ||129||*

**Word for word:** *arka* — of the sun; *prabhā* — by the rays; *pakva* — scorching; *vihīna* — free from; *sāndra* — dense; *snigdha* — moist; *ati* — very; *sat* — pleasantly; *śītala* — cool; *cāru* — lovely; *bhūmau* — [whose] ground [was]; *akṛtrima* — with natural; *ālepa* — unguent; *nipīta* — saturated; *mūle* — [whose] roots [were]; *vāpī* — by step-wells; *taḍāga* — by ponds; *ādi* — and the like; *nirantarāle* — [whose] intervals [were] filled without a break.

**Translation.** Its ground, free from the scorching of the sun's rays, was dense [with growth], moist, very pleasantly cool and lovely; the roots [of its trees] were saturated with a natural unguent; and step-wells, ponds and the like filled it without a break.

**Note.** *Pakva* ("ripened, baked") here means "scorched" by the sun.

### 130

**130.**

```
ततः स गोदावरिकामुपेत्य
मनस्यथान्दोलिततां जगाम ।
संभाषितव्यः किमसौ नवेति
श्रीमद्भवानन्दसुतो महात्मा ॥१३०॥
```
*tataḥ sa godāvarikām upetya
manasy athāndolitatāṁ jagāma |
saṁbhāṣitavyaḥ kim asau na veti
śrīmad-bhavānanda-suto mahātmā ||130||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *saḥ* — He; *godāvarikām* — to the Godavari; *upetya* — having come; *manasi* — in mind; *atha* — then; *āndolitatām* — a wavering; *jagāma* — came to; *saṁbhāṣitavyaḥ* — is to be spoken with; *kim* — whether; *asau* — he; *na vā* — or not; *iti* — thus; *śrīmat* — of the illustrious; *bhavānanda* — of Bhavananda; *sutaḥ* — the son; *mahātmā* — great-souled.

**Translation.** Then, having come to the Godavari, He began to waver in His mind: should He speak with the great-souled son of the illustrious Bhavananda, or not?

**Note.** The edition has *संभाषिव्यः* — a misprint; the metre requires *saṁbhāṣitavyaḥ*. The son of Bhavananda is Ramananda Raya (see v. 99).

### 131

**131.**

```
तथाप्यभिव्यज्य विभुर्विरागं
न तं विलोक्यैव ययाववाचीम् ।
नानावनालोकनकोमलात्मा
क्वचित् प्रविश्यातिशयं रुरोद ॥१३१॥
```
*tathāpy abhivyajya vibhur virāgaṁ
na taṁ vilokyaiva yayāv avācīm |
nānā-vanālokana-komalātmā
kvacit praviśyātiśayaṁ ruroda ||131||*

**Word for word:** *tathā api* — and yet; *abhivyajya* — displaying; *vibhuḥ* — the Almighty; *virāgam* — detachment; *na* — not; *tam* — him; *vilokya* — having seen; *eva* — even; *yayau* — went; *avācīm* — to the south; *nānā* — various; *vana* — of forests; *ālokana* — by the sight; *komala* — softened; *ātmā* — [whose] heart [was]; *kvacit* — at times; *praviśya* — having entered [a thicket]; *atiśayam* — beyond measure; *ruroda* — wept.

**Translation.** And yet the Almighty, displaying detachment, went on to the south without even seeing him. His heart softened by the sight of the various forests, at times He would enter [a thicket] and weep beyond measure.

**Note.** In Krishnadasa Kaviraja (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 8) the conversation with Ramananda on the Godavari takes place on the way south. Karnapura in the Mahakavya transfers it to the return journey: 13.34–49 (Ramananda recites verses, the Lord answers "this is external" (*bāhya*), then the Bengali song *pahilahi rāga nayana-bhaṅga bhela* is sung — the same as in the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 8.192–193, in the numbering of the project's edition). Cf. Karnapura's own drama *Chaitanya-chandrodaya*, Act 7: there, as in the Chaitanya-charitamrita, the meeting with Ramananda takes place on the way south (with the same answer "this is external"), so the Mahakavya diverges even from Karnapura's own drama.

### 132

**132.**

```
क्वचित्क्वचिद्गायति मुक्तकण्ठं
क्वचित् क्वचिन्नृत्यति च स्वयं सः ।
क्वचित् क्वचिद्रोदिति हृष्टरोमा
रात्रिन्दिवं नैव विवेद गच्छन् ॥१३२॥
```
*kvacit kvacid gāyati mukta-kaṇṭhaṁ
kvacit kvacin nṛtyati ca svayaṁ saḥ |
kvacit kvacid roditi hṛṣṭa-romā
rātrin-divaṁ naiva viveda gacchan ||132||*

**Word for word:** *kvacit kvacit* — here and there; *gāyati* — sings; *mukta* — with open; *kaṇṭham* — throat; *nṛtyati* — dances; *ca* — and; *svayam* — Himself; *saḥ* — He; *roditi* — weeps; *hṛṣṭa* — standing on end; *romā* — [whose] hairs [were]; *rātrim-divam* — night and day; *na eva* — not at all; *viveda* — distinguished; *gacchan* — going.

### 133

**133.**

```
कनककरिवरोऽयं किं चिरोन्मुक्तबन्धः
किमु झटिति चरिष्णुर्मेरुरेषः प्रभाति ।
अथ किमु चिररोचिःपुञ्ज एष प्रकामं
स्फुरति चिरविलासः को नु वायं प्रपञ्चः ॥१३३॥
```
*kanaka-kari-varo 'yaṁ kiṁ cironmukta-bandhaḥ
kim u jhaṭiti cariṣṇur merur eṣaḥ prabhāti |
atha kim u cira-rociḥ-puñja eṣa prakāmaṁ
sphurati cira-vilāsaḥ ko nu vāyaṁ prapañcaḥ ||133||*

**Word for word:** *kanaka* — golden; *kari-varaḥ* — the best of elephants; *ayam* — this; *kim* — is it; *cira* — from long; *unmukta* — freed; *bandhaḥ* — [whose] bonds [are]; *kim u* — or; *jhaṭiti* — suddenly; *cariṣṇuḥ* — moving; *meruḥ* — Meru; *eṣaḥ* — this; *prabhāti* — shines; *atha kim u* — or else; *cira* — lasting; *rociḥ* — of light; *puñjaḥ* — a mass; *eṣaḥ* — this; *prakāmam* — freely; *sphurati* — flashes; *cira* — lasting; *vilāsaḥ* — play; *kaḥ nu vā* — or what indeed; *ayam* — this; *prapañcaḥ* — marvel.

**Translation.** "Is this a golden king of elephants, freed from long bondage? Or is this Meru, suddenly set in motion, that shines [here]? Or else is this a mass of lasting light that flashes so freely — a play [of lightning] that lasts? Or what indeed is this marvel?"

**Note.** The metre is *mālinī* (⏑⏑⏑ ⏑⏑⏑ ——— ⏑—— ⏑——). A play on words: lightning is *acira-ruc*, "light of a brief moment", whereas here we have *cira-rociḥ*, "lasting light" — lightning that does not fade.

### 134

**134.**

```
इति सकलनृलोको दाक्षिणात्यः सतोषं
विनिमिषमनुवेलं लोचनाभ्यां पिबन् सः ।
जडिमजडितचेता दूरमप्यत्र देवे
गतवति यतिचन्द्रे स्थाणुवत्तत्र तस्थौ ॥१३४॥
```
*iti sakala-nṛ-loko dākṣiṇātyaḥ satoṣaṁ
vinimiṣam anuvelaṁ locanābhyāṁ piban saḥ |
jaḍima-jaḍita-cetā dūram apy atra deve
gatavati yati-candre sthāṇuvat tatra tasthau ||134||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus [thinking]; *sakala* — all; *nṛ* — of people; *lokaḥ* — the folk; *dākṣiṇātyaḥ* — southern; *sa-toṣam* — with delight; *vinimiṣam* — without blinking; *anuvelam* — constantly; *locanābhyām* — with the eyes; *piban* — drinking; *saḥ* — they; *jaḍima* — by stupor; *jaḍita* — numbed; *cetāḥ* — [whose] minds [were]; *dūram* — far; *api* — even; *atra* — here; *deve* — when the Lord; *gatavati* — had gone; *yati* — of ascetics; *candre* — the moon; *sthāṇu-vat* — like posts; *tatra* — there; *tasthau* — stood.

**Translation.** Thus [thinking], all the southern folk drank Him in with their eyes, with delight, constantly and without blinking; their minds numbed with stupor, even when the Lord, the moon among ascetics, had gone far from there, they stood there like posts.

**Note.** *Mālinī*. The edition has *सन्तोषं*, but the metre requires a short syllable here (⏑—— at the end of the pada): *satoṣam*. *Vinimiṣam* means "without blinking"; one would expect *animiṣam*, and the edition's form may be a corruption ⟨?⟩.
