# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Four — verses 1–77 (printed pp. 97–120)

**Metre:** *drutavilambita* (12 syllables per pada); verses 73–74 — *toṭaka*; verse 76 — *śikhariṇī*; verse 77 — *vaṁśastha*.
**Parallels:** Chaitanya-bhagavata, Adi 12–13 (Nimai the teacher; the victory over Keshava Kashmiri); Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 16.

**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–4 — married life with Vishnupriya; the beauty of Gauranga.
- 5–20 — Gauranga as a teacher among His students (the verses are built on *yamaka*, the repetition of syllables; many passages are obscure).
- 21–24 — the journey to Gaya with Chandrashekhara Acharya.
- 25–49 — a poetic interlude: a lotus pond, sunset, night, morning, mountains and deer along the way (the story stands still; continuous *yamaka*).
- 50–55 — the river Chora: the fever, the cure with the water from the brahmanas' feet; Rajagiri (Rajagriha); the entry into Gaya.
- 56–61 — **the meeting with Ishvara Puri; the initiation**.
- 62–65 — the shraddha at Gaya (the Phalgu, Pretashila, Manasa); at the footprint of Vishnu — the first ecstasy.
- 66–68 — the impulse to go to Madhuvana (Vraja); a voice from the sky bids Him return.
- 69–75 — the return home, music; Shachi distributes gifts.
- 76–77 — the beginning of kirtana (the month of Magha).

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
अथ कृपारसवारिनिधीन्दुना
स्वजनमानसकैरवबन्धुना ।
दयितया सह तत्र विराजिता
निजगृहे जगृहे गृहमेधिता ॥१॥
```
*atha kṛpā-rasa-vārinidhīndunā
sva-jana-mānasa-kairava-bandhunā |
dayitayā saha tatra virājitā
nija-gṛhe jagṛhe gṛhamedhitā ||1||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *rasa* — of the liquid; *vārinidhi* — of the ocean; *indunā* — by the moon; *sva* — of His own; *jana* — people; *mānasa* — of the hearts; *kairava* — of the white lilies; *bandhunā* — by the friend; *dayitayā* — the beloved; *saha* — with; *tatra* — there; *virājitā* — splendid; *nija* — in His own; *gṛhe* — in the house; *jagṛhe* — was accepted; *gṛhamedhitā* — the householder's life.

**Translation.** Then, there in His own house, together with His beloved, He — the moon risen from the ocean of the nectar of mercy, the friend of the white lilies of His own people's hearts — accepted the splendid life of a householder.

**Note.** The moon rose from the churned Milk Ocean; the white lily (*kairava*) opens under the moon. A play of sounds: *gṛhe jagṛhe gṛhamedhitā*.

### 2

**2.**

```
द्रुतसुवर्णसुवर्णरुचः शुचे-
र्मधुरकोमलशीतलविभ्रमः ।
श्रियमसौ मधुरामतिसुन्दरी-
मवपुषो वपुषोऽनुरुचाहरत् ॥२॥
```
*druta-suvarṇa-suvarṇa-rucaḥ śucer
madhura-komala-śītala-vibhramaḥ |
śriyam asau madhurām ati-sundarīm
avapuṣo vapuṣo 'nurucāharat ||2||*

**Word for word:** *druta* — of molten; *suvarṇa* — gold; *su* — of beautiful; *varṇa* — colour; *rucaḥ* — [of the body] having the lustre; *śuceḥ* — of the pure; *madhura* — of sweet; *komala* — tender; *śītala* — cooling; *vibhramaḥ* — [He] of charm; *śriyam* — the beauty; *asau* — He; *madhurām* — sweet; *ati* — exceedingly; *sundarīm* — lovely; *avapuṣaḥ* — from the Bodiless One; *vapuṣaḥ* — of the body; *anurucā* — by the radiance; *aharat* — stole.

**Translation.** He, full of sweet, tender and cooling charm, by the radiance of His pure body, which had the lustre of the beautiful colour of molten gold, stole the sweet and exceedingly lovely beauty of the Bodiless One.

**Note.** A play on words: *avapuṣo vapuṣo*, "from the Bodiless One … of the body". The Bodiless One is Kama, the god of love (cf. 3.30).

### 3

**3.**

```
अमूमवेक्ष्य हृदा हृदयेशयः
सपदि निश्चितमित्थममन्यत ।
इममृते मम मन्मथता जनै-
रनुकथं नु कथं न हसिष्यते ॥३॥
```
*amūm avekṣya hṛdā hṛdayeśayaḥ
sapadi niścitam ittham amanyata |
imam ṛte mama manmathatā janair
anukathaṁ nu kathaṁ na hasiṣyate ||3||*

**Word for word:** *amūm* — Him; *avekṣya* — having seen; *hṛdā* — with the heart; *hṛdayeśayaḥ* — "he who lies in the heart", the god of love; *sapadi* — at once; *niścitam* — firmly; *ittham* — thus; *amanyata* — thought; *imam* — Him; *ṛte* — beside; *mama* — my; *manmathatā* — fame as "the churner of minds"; *janaiḥ* — by people; *anukatham* — at every telling; *nu* — indeed; *katham* — how; *na* — not; *hasiṣyate* — will be laughed at.

**Translation.** The god of love, seeing Him with his heart, at once firmly concluded: "Beside Him, how will my fame as 'the churner of minds' not be laughed at by people whenever it is spoken of?"

**Note.** *Hṛdayeśaya*, "he who lies in the heart", and *manmatha*, "churner of the mind", are both names of Kamadeva. The edition has *amūm* (feminine); the sense requires *amum* (masculine, "Him"). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 4

**4.**

```
निजपदाब्जरसैरतिशीतलै-
र्जगदपूरयदात्तकृपारसः ।
य इह तत्कथने विरमन्त्यहो
तनुधरा नु धरासु वसन्ति ते ॥४॥
```
*nija-padābja-rasair ati-śītalair
jagad apūrayad ātta-kṛpā-rasaḥ |
ya iha tat-kathane viramanty aho
tanu-dharā nu dharāsu vasanti te ||4||*

**Word for word:** *nija* — of His own; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotuses; *rasaiḥ* — with the juices; *ati* — very; *śītalaiḥ* — cooling; *jagat* — the world; *apūrayat* — filled; *ātta* — having taken up; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *rasaḥ* — the taste; *ye* — who; *iha* — here; *tat* — about Him; *kathane* — from speaking; *viramanti* — cease; *aho* — ah; *tanu* — bodies; *dharāḥ* — bearing; *nu* — whether; *dharāsu* — on the earth; *vasanti* — live; *te* — they.

**Translation.** Filled with the taste of mercy, He filled the world with the very cooling juices of His lotus feet. Ah, those here who cease from speaking of Him — do they [truly] live on the earth? They are mere bearers of bodies.

**Note.** A play on words: *tanu-dharā*, "body-bearers" — *dharāsu*, "on the earth".

### 5

**5.**

```
अथ गुरुत्वमुपेत्य विकस्वरा-
म्बुजविलोलविलोचनखेलनैः ।
द्विजगणं समपाठयदेष य-
त्प्रतिभयातिभयाकुलितो गुरुः ॥५॥
```
*atha gurutvam upetya vikasvarā-
mbuja-vilola-vilocana-khelanaiḥ |
dvija-gaṇaṁ samapāṭhayad eṣa yat
pratibhayāti-bhayākulito guruḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *gurutvam* — the position of teacher; *upetya* — having assumed; *vikasvara* — of blossoming; *ambuja* — [like] lotuses; *vilola* — of restless; *vilocana* — of the eyes; *khelanaiḥ* — with the play; *dvija* — of brahmanas; *gaṇam* — the throng; *samapāṭhayat* — taught; *eṣaḥ* — He; *yat* — so that; *pratibhayā* — by [His] genius; *ati* — with great; *bhaya* — fear; *ākulitaḥ* — confounded; *guruḥ* — Guru [Brihaspati].

**Translation.** Then, having become a teacher, He taught the throng of brahmanas with the play of His restless eyes, like blossoming lotuses — so that Guru himself, [the teacher of the gods,] was confounded by His genius, in great fear.

**Note.** A pun: *pratibhayāti-bhaya*, "by genius — great fear". *Guru* is both "teacher" and Brihaspati, the preceptor of the gods and the standard of learning.

### 6

**6.**

```
विविधशिष्यसदस्यपि राजतः
कनकगौरतनोर्मधुरद्युतेः ।
सुखवतः परिपाठयतोऽस्य सा
सुरुचिरा रुचिरास सुधारसम् ॥६॥
```
*vividha-śiṣya-sadasy api rājataḥ
kanaka-gaura-tanor madhura-dyuteḥ |
sukhavataḥ paripāṭhayato 'sya sā
su-rucirā rucir āsa sudhā-rasam ||6||*

**Word for word:** *vividha* — of various; *śiṣya* — of students; *sadasi* — in the assembly; *api* — even; *rājataḥ* — [of Him] shining; *kanaka* — [like] gold; *gaura* — golden; *tanoḥ* — [of Him] whose body [is]; *madhura* — sweet; *dyuteḥ* — [of Him] whose effulgence [is]; *sukhavataḥ* — joyful; *paripāṭhayataḥ* — teaching; *asya* — of Him; *sā* — that; *su* — most; *rucirā* — lovely; *ruciḥ* — lustre; *āsa* — cast forth; *sudhā* — of nectar; *rasam* — the juice.

**Translation.** As He — His body golden as gold, His effulgence sweet — shone even in the assembly of His various students and joyfully taught them, that most lovely lustre of His cast forth the juice of nectar.

**Note.** A play of sounds: *su-rucirā rucir āsa*. *Āsa* is taken as the perfect of *as*, "to throw, to cast forth", not as "was".

### 7

**7.**

```
दशनरश्मिभिरच्छरदच्छदौ
स्नपयता सततं वदनेन्दुना ।
स्मितसुधामधुरेण महाप्रभु-
र्घनरुचा नवचारुरुचिर्बभौ ॥७॥
```
*daśana-raśmibhir accha-radacchadau
snapayatā satataṁ vadanendunā |
smita-sudhā-madhureṇa mahāprabhur
ghana-rucā nava-cāru-rucir babhau ||7||*

**Word for word:** *daśana* — of the teeth; *raśmibhiḥ* — with the rays; *accha* — clear; *radacchadau* — the two lips; *snapayatā* — bathing; *satatam* — constantly; *vadana* — of the face; *indunā* — with the moon; *smita* — of the smile; *sudhā* — with the nectar; *madhureṇa* — sweet; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *ghana* — with dense; *rucā* — lustre; *nava* — fresh; *cāru* — lovely; *ruciḥ* — [He] of radiance; *babhau* — shone.

**Translation.** With the moon of His face, sweet with the nectar of His smile, constantly bathing His clear lips in the rays of His teeth, Mahaprabhu shone with dense lustre, His radiance fresh and lovely.

**Note.** The edition has *घनरुधा*; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *ghana-rucā*, "with dense lustre", and we read so.

### 8

**8.**

```
सकलशिष्यमुखानि महाप्रभोः
कलयतः ककुभः सततारुणाः ।
विदधिरे बहुविभ्रमयावलत्-
करुणयारुणया नयनश्रिया ॥८॥
```
*sakala-śiṣya-mukhāni mahāprabhoḥ
kalayataḥ kakubhaḥ satatāruṇāḥ |
vidadhire bahu-vibhramayā valat-
karuṇayāruṇayā nayana-śriyā ||8||*

**Word for word:** *sakala* — of all; *śiṣya* — the students; *mukhāni* — the faces; *mahāprabhoḥ* — of Mahaprabhu; *kalayataḥ* — [of Him] looking upon; *kakubhaḥ* — the quarters; *satata* — ever; *aruṇāḥ* — red; *vidadhire* — were made; *bahu* — of much; *vibhramayā* — [full] of playfulness; *valat* — surging; *karuṇayā* — with compassion; *aruṇayā* — reddish; *nayana* — of the eyes; *śriyā* — by the beauty.

**Translation.** As Mahaprabhu looked upon the faces of all His students, the quarters were made ever red by the reddish beauty of His eyes, full of playfulness and surging with compassion.

**Note.** A play of sounds: *karuṇayā aruṇayā*.

### 9

**9.**

```
करतलेन गिरां गुरुविभ्रमै-
र्भ्रमवता स बभौ परितः स्फुरन् ।
कनकशैल इवोद्गतगैरिको-
दयलता लयताण्डवखेलनाम् ॥९॥
```
*kara-talena girāṁ guru-vibhramair
bhramavatā sa babhau paritaḥ sphuran |
kanaka-śaila ivodgata-gairikodaya-latā-
laya-tāṇḍava-khelanām ||9||*

**Word for word:** *kara* — of the hand; *talena* — with the palm; *girām* — of speech; *guru* — with great; *vibhramaiḥ* — graces; *bhramavatā* — moving in circles; *saḥ* — He; *babhau* — shone; *paritaḥ* — all around; *sphuran* — flashing; *kanaka* — a golden; *śailaḥ* — mountain; *iva* — like; *udgata* — risen; *gairika* — of red ochre; *udaya* — emerging; *latā* — of the veins; *laya* — rhythmic; *tāṇḍava* — of the tandava dance; *khelanām* — [displaying] the play.

**Translation.** With the palm of His hand moving in circles with the great graces of His speech, He shone, flashing all around, like a golden mountain [displaying] the play of a rhythmic tandava dance of risen, emerging veins of red ochre.

**Note.** The red palm against the golden body is like veins of red ochre (*gairika*) on a golden mountain. *Tāṇḍava* is a vigorous, rhythmic dance (the dance of Shiva). ⟨?⟩ The end of the verse is syntactically obscure; it is rendered according to the sense. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 10

**10.**

```
अयमयं नु किमस्य कमस्य वा
किमयमर्थ उतस्विदयं त्विति ।
कलकलोऽस्य बभूव सुखाय स
त्रिचतुरैश्चतुरैः परितः कृतः ॥१०॥
```
*ayam ayaṁ nu kim asya kam asya vā
kim ayam artha utasvid ayaṁ tv iti |
kala-kalo 'sya babhūva sukhāya sa
tri-caturaiś caturaiḥ paritaḥ kṛtaḥ ||10||*

**Word for word:** *ayam* — this; *ayam* — this; *nu* — is it; *kim* — what; *asya* — of this; *kam* — which; *asya* — of this; *vā* — or; *kim* — is it; *ayam* — this; *arthaḥ* — sense; *utasvit* — or perhaps; *ayam* — this; *tu* — indeed; *iti* — thus; *kala-kalaḥ* — the clamour; *asya* — to Him; *babhūva* — was; *sukhāya* — for delight; *saḥ* — that; *tri* — three; *caturaiḥ* — or four; *caturaiḥ* — by clever [students]; *paritaḥ* — all around; *kṛtaḥ* — raised.

**Translation.** "Is it this? Or this? What is [the meaning] of this? Or which [word] of this? Is this the sense — or perhaps this?" — the clamour that three or four clever [students] raised all around Him was His delight.

**Note.** A lively scene: the students vie with one another over an interpretation. A play of sounds: *tri-caturaiś caturaiḥ*, "by three or four clever ones".

### 11

**11.**

```
प्रभुमुखे युगपत्पतयालुभि-
र्विसृमरैरलिभिर्विदधे मुहुः ।
विततपक्षविधूननसक्षणै-
र्दिगबलागवलावलिविभ्रमा ॥११॥
```
*prabhu-mukhe yugapat patayālubhir
visṛmarair alibhir vidadhe muhuḥ |
vitata-pakṣa-vidhūnana-sakṣaṇair
dig-abalā-gavalāvali-vibhramā ||11||*

**Word for word:** *prabhu* — of the Lord; *mukhe* — on the face; *yugapat* — all at once; *patayālubhiḥ* — alighting; *visṛmaraiḥ* — scattering; *alibhiḥ* — by bees; *vidadhe* — was produced; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *vitata* — of outspread; *pakṣa* — wings; *vidhūnana* — in the fluttering; *sa-kṣaṇaiḥ* — exultant; *dik* — of the quarters; *abalā* — for the maidens; *gavala* — of buffalo horn; *āvali* — of rows; *vibhramā* — the charm.

**Translation.** Again and again the bees, now alighting all at once on the Lord's face, now scattering, exultant in the fluttering of their outspread wings, lent the maidens of the quarters the charm of rows of [ornaments black as] buffalo horn.

**Note.** The bees take the Lord's face for a lotus (cf. v. 12). In the edition the last pada reads *dig-abalā abalāvali-vibhramāḥ*; we read with the Gosvami edition (GGM) *dig-abalā-gavalāvali-vibhramā*: *gavala* is the horn of the wild buffalo, a byword for blackness.

### 12

**12.**

```
किसलयं सलयं किमु वारुणं
सकमलं कमलं किमु वेत्यलिः ।
इह तदा हतदाक्ष्य इव प्रभोः
करदलं रदलङ्घनयाभजत् ॥१२॥
```
*kisalayaṁ salayaṁ kimu vāruṇaṁ
sa-kamalaṁ kamalaṁ kimu vety aliḥ |
iha tadā hata-dākṣya iva prabhoḥ
kara-dalaṁ rada-laṅghanayābhajat ||12||*

**Word for word:** *kisalayam* — a young shoot; *salayam* — swaying; *kimu* — is it; *vāruṇam* — or red; *sa* — with; *kamalam* — water; *kamalam* — a lotus; *kimu* — is it; *vā* — or; *iti* — thus [wondering]; *aliḥ* — the bee; *iha* — here; *tadā* — then; *hata* — lost; *dākṣyaḥ* — [having] its cleverness; *iva* — as if; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *kara* — of the hand; *dalam* — the petal; *rada* — of the teeth; *laṅghanayā* — with the touch ⟨?⟩; *abhajat* — resorted to.

**Translation.** "Is it a swaying, red young shoot? Or is it a lotus in its water?" — so wondering, the bee, as if it had lost its wits, then fell upon the petal of the Lord's hand ⟨?⟩ with a touch of its teeth.

**Note.** A play of sounds: *kisalayaṁ salayaṁ*, *sa-kamalaṁ kamalam* (*kam* — "water"); *vāruṇam* is *vā aruṇam*, "or red". ⟨?⟩ *Rada-laṅghanayā* is obscure (literally "by the overstepping of the teeth"). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 13

**13.**

```
नखसुधांशुसुधां शुचिमुक्षता
सुरुचिरेण चिरेण मधुव्रतः ।
करदलेन दलेन रुचाप्यभू-
द्धवलता वलता सुचिरोषितः ॥१३॥
```
*nakha-sudhāṁśu-sudhāṁ śucim ukṣatā
su-rucireṇa cireṇa madhuvrataḥ |
kara-dalena dalena rucāpy abhūd
dhavalatā-valatā su-ciroṣitaḥ ||13||*

**Word for word:** *nakha* — of the nails; *sudhāṁśu* — of the moons; *sudhām* — the nectar; *śucim* — pure; *ukṣatā* — sprinkling; *su* — most; *rucireṇa* — lovely; *cireṇa* — long; *madhuvrataḥ* — the bee; *kara* — of the hand; *dalena* — by the petal; *dalena* — by the petal; *rucā* — by the lustre; *api* — also; *abhūt* — became; *dhavala* — from the white; *latā* — creepers; *valatā* — turned away; *su* — very; *cira* — long; *uṣitaḥ* — having dwelt.

**Translation.** Because of the most lovely petal of His hand, which long sprinkled the pure nectar of the moons of His nails, and because of its lustre, the bee, which had long dwelt on the white creepers, turned away from them.

**Note.** The verse is built on *yamaka*, the repetition of identical syllables with different meanings: *rucireṇa cireṇa*, *kara-dalena dalena*, *dhavalatā valatā*. The translation follows the sense; many words in such verses cannot be analysed unambiguously. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 14

**14.**

```
स परितः परिलक्ष्य धुरीणतां
मधुरिमा धुरि मानवतां श्रियाम् ।
भुवि हितं विहितं रचयन्सता-
मगमदागमदाक्ष्यममुष्य किम् ॥१४॥
```
*sa paritaḥ parilakṣya dhurīṇatāṁ
madhurimā dhuri mānavatāṁ śriyām |
bhuvi hitaṁ vihitaṁ racayan satām
agamad āgama-dākṣyam amuṣya kim ||14||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — that; *paritaḥ* — on every side; *parilakṣya* — having perceived; *dhurīṇatām* — pre-eminence; *madhurimā* — sweetness; *dhuri* — at the head; *mānavatām* — of human beings; *śriyām* — of the beauties; *bhuvi* — on the earth; *hitam* — the good; *vihitam* — prescribed; *racayan* — arranging; *satām* — for the virtuous; *agamat* — came to know; *āgama* — in the scriptures; *dākṣyam* — the skill; *amuṣya* — His; *kim* — did not…? ⟨?⟩.

**Translation.** ⟨?⟩ Sweetness, perceiving on every side His pre-eminence at the head of the beauties of human beings — did it not also come to know His skill in the scriptures, as He arranged on earth the prescribed good for the virtuous?

**Note.** ⟨?⟩ The verse is very obscure (*yamaka*: *dhurīṇatāṁ … dhuri*, *agamad āgama*). The translation is conjectural. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 15

**15.**

```
प्रभुमुखेन्दुगलद्वचनामृतं
मृतजनस्य च जीवितदायि तत् ।
श्रुतिपथेन निपीय चिरेण ते
मुदमिता दमिताखिलकल्मषाः ॥१५॥
```
*prabhu-mukhendu-galad-vacanāmṛtaṁ
mṛta-janasya ca jīvita-dāyi tat |
śruti-pathena nipīya cireṇa te
mudam itā damitākhila-kalmaṣāḥ ||15||*

**Word for word:** *prabhu* — of the Lord; *mukha* — of the face; *indu* — from the moon; *galat* — flowing; *vacana* — of words; *amṛtam* — the nectar; *mṛta* — to a dead; *janasya* — person; *ca* — even; *jīvita* — life; *dāyi* — giving; *tat* — that; *śruti* — of hearing; *pathena* — by the path; *nipīya* — having drunk; *cireṇa* — long; *te* — they; *mudam* — joy; *itāḥ* — attained; *damita* — subdued; *akhila* — all; *kalmaṣāḥ* — [they whose] sins [were].

**Translation.** Having long drunk through the path of their ears the nectar of words flowing from the moon of the Lord's face — a nectar that gives life even to the dead — they, all their sins subdued, attained joy.

**Note.** A play of sounds: *mudam itā damitā*.

### 16

**16.**

```
धवलपक्षसपक्षरुगंशुकः
शुकचञ्चुरुचं चुलुकीकृताम् ।
मधुरयोर्नु दधेऽधरयोरसौ
मधुरयोर्यदयं परिजृम्भते ॥१६॥
```
*dhavala-pakṣa-sapakṣa-rug-aṁśukaḥ
śuka-cañcu-rucaṁ culukī-kṛtām |
madhurayor nu dadhe 'dharayor asau
madhurayor yad ayaṁ parijṛmbhate ||16||*

**Word for word:** *dhavala* — of the bright; *pakṣa* — fortnight; *sapakṣa* — like; *ruk* — [whose] lustre [is]; *aṁśukaḥ* — [He whose] garment [is]; *śuka* — of the parrot; *cañcu* — of the beak; *rucam* — the beauty; *culukī* — a mouthful; *kṛtām* — made into; *madhurayoḥ* — in the sweet; *nu* — did not…?; *dadhe* — held; *adharayoḥ* — in the lips; *asau* — He; *madhurayoḥ* — over the sweet [lips]; *yat* — since; *ayam* — it; *parijṛmbhate* — spreads.

**Translation.** His garment had the lustre of the bright fortnight of the moon. Did He not take the beauty of the parrot's beak in a single sip and hold it in His sweet lips? — for it spreads over those sweet [lips].

**Note.** The edition gives *nudadhe* and *madhu-rayo* ("a stream of honey"); we read with the Gosvami edition (GGM) *nu dadhe* and *madhurayor*: the Lord's lips are red like a parrot's beak.

### 17

**17.**

```
नवविकस्वरपङ्कजभास्वरं
स्मितमधुद्रवविश्वविलोभनम् ।
जहसुरस्य मुखेन्दुमवेक्ष्य ते
रसमयं समयन्तमशोणताम् ॥१७॥
```
*nava-vikasvara-paṅkaja-bhāsvaraṁ
smita-madhu-drava-viśva-vilobhanam |
jahasur asya mukhendum avekṣya te
rasamayaṁ samayantam aśoṇatām ||17||*

**Word for word:** *nava* — freshly; *vikasvara* — blossomed; *paṅkaja* — [like] a lotus; *bhāsvaram* — resplendent; *smita* — of the smile; *madhu* — of honey; *drava* — with the liquid; *viśva* — the world; *vilobhanam* — enchanting; *jahasuḥ* — laughed; *asya* — His; *mukha* — of the face; *indum* — the moon; *avekṣya* — having beheld; *te* — they; *rasa* — of rasa; *mayam* — full; *samayantam* — bringing to; *aśoṇatām* — paleness.

**Translation.** Beholding the moon of His face — resplendent as a freshly blossomed lotus, enchanting the world with the honeyed liquid of its smile, full of rasa, bringing [red lotuses] to paleness — they laughed for joy.

**Note.** *Samayantam aśoṇatām*, literally "bringing to un-redness": beside His face the red lotus loses its colour.

### 18

**18.**

```
विधुरसौष्ठवतां लभतां मुहु-
र्विधुरसौ वलता वदनांशुना ।
मधुरसान्वितपुष्पमनोरमो
मधुरसाधुरसावभिवर्त्तताम् ॥१८॥
```
*vidhur asauṣṭhavatāṁ labhatāṁ muhur
vidhur asau valatā vadanāṁśunā |
madhu-rasānvita-puṣpa-manoramo
madhur asādhu-rasāv abhivartatām ||18||*

**Word for word:** *vidhuḥ* — the moon; *asauṣṭhavatām* — unloveliness; *labhatām* — let it obtain; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *vidhuḥ* — the moon; *asau* — this; *valatā* — turning [towards it]; *vadana* — of the face; *aṁśunā* — by the ray; *madhu* — of honey; *rasa* — with the juice; *anvita* — filled; *puṣpa* — with flowers; *manoramaḥ* — charming; *madhuḥ* — spring; *asādhu* — of poor; *rasau* — taste ⟨?⟩; *abhivartatām* — let it become.

**Translation.** Let this moon, [touched] by the ray of His face turned towards it, again and again lose its loveliness; and let spring, charming with flowers filled with the juice of honey, become insipid.

**Note.** Again a *yamaka*: *vidhur asauṣṭhavatāṁ … vidhur asau*, *madhu-rasānvita … madhur asādhu-rasau*. ⟨?⟩ The form *rasau* is unclear. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 19

**19.**

```
इति जनः परिपाठयति प्रभौ
प्रभवता प्रतिभानरसाब्धिना ।
मधुरिमाणमवेक्ष्य समुज्जगौ
नवसुधा वसुधामिव किं श्रिता ॥१९॥
```
*iti janaḥ paripāṭhayati prabhau
prabhavatā pratibhāna-rasābdhinā |
madhurimāṇam avekṣya samujjagau
nava-sudhā vasudhām iva kiṁ śritā ||19||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *janaḥ* — the people; *paripāṭhayati* — [when] was teaching; *prabhau* — the Lord; *prabhavatā* — with mighty; *pratibhāna* — of inspired insight; *rasa* — of rasa; *abdhinā* — with the ocean; *madhurimāṇam* — the sweetness; *avekṣya* — beholding; *samujjagau* — cried aloud; *nava* — a new; *sudhā* — nectar; *vasudhām* — to the earth; *iva* — as it were; *kim* — has not…?; *śritā* — come.

**Translation.** When the Lord was teaching thus with His mighty ocean of the rasa of inspired insight, the people, beholding His sweetness, cried aloud: "Has not a new nectar, as it were, come down to the earth?"

**Note.** A play of sounds: *nava-sudhā vasudhām*.

### 20

**20.**

```
इति कियन्ति दिनानि महाप्रभुः
समनयत्परिपाठ्य कृपानिधिः ।
निजतनोर्महसा स दिनन्दिनं
प्रभवता भवतापचयानपि ॥२०॥
```
*iti kiyanti dināni mahāprabhuḥ
samanayat paripāṭhya kṛpā-nidhiḥ |
nija-tanor mahasā sa dinandinaṁ
prabhavatā bhava-tāpa-cayān api ||20||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *kiyanti* — a number of; *dināni* — days; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *samanayat* — spent / quelled; *paripāṭhya* — teaching; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *nidhiḥ* — the treasure-house; *nija* — of His own; *tanoḥ* — of the body; *mahasā* — by the splendour; *saḥ* — He; *dinandinam* — day by day; *prabhavatā* — mighty; *bhava* — of worldly existence; *tāpa* — of the torments; *cayān* — the heaps; *api* — also.

**Translation.** Thus Mahaprabhu, the treasure-house of mercy, spent a number of days teaching; and day by day, by the mighty splendour of His own body, He also quelled the heaps of the torments of worldly existence.

**Note.** The verb *samanayat* works twice: "spent [the days]" and, echoing *śamanayat*, "quelled [the torments]".

### 21

**21.**

```
स जननीभगिनीपतिना गयां
सममुपैतुमनास्तदनन्तरम् ।
निजमनोरमचेष्टितविभ्रमैः
सुमनसां मनसां मुदमावहत् ॥२१॥
```
*sa jananī-bhaginī-patinā gayāṁ
samam upaitu-manās tad-anantaram |
nija-manorama-ceṣṭita-vibhramaiḥ
sumanasāṁ manasāṁ mudam āvahat ||21||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — He; *jananī* — of the mother; *bhaginī* — of the sister; *patinā* — with the husband; *gayām* — to Gaya; *samam* — together; *upaitu* — to go; *manāḥ* — minded (wishing); *tat* — that; *anantaram* — after; *nija* — by His own; *manorama* — charming; *ceṣṭita* — of movements; *vibhramaiḥ* — by the graces; *sumanasām* — of good people; *manasām* — to the hearts; *mudam* — joy; *āvahat* — brought.

**Translation.** After that, wishing to go to Gaya together with the husband of His mother's sister, He brought joy to the hearts of good people by the graces of His own charming movements.

**Note.** The edition reads *nija-yato raya-* (obscure); we read *nija-manorama-* with the Gosvami edition (GGM). The husband of Shachi's sister is Chandrashekhara Acharya (Acharyaratna). The pilgrimage to Gaya is a turning point: there Nimai meets Ishvara Puri and receives initiation (Chaitanya-bhagavata, Adi 17; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17).

### 22

**22.**

```
प्रथममुल्लसितो विजयोद्यमे
परिसमाप्तविधिर्महितो मुहुः ।
द्विजगणेन सुखैर्ववृधे जय-
स्वनवता नवतामरसेक्षणः ॥२२॥
```
*prathamam ullasito vijayodyame
parisamāpta-vidhir mahito muhuḥ |
dvija-gaṇena sukhair vavṛdhe jaya-
svanavatā nava-tāmarasekṣaṇaḥ ||22||*

**Word for word:** *prathamam* — first; *ullasitaḥ* — exultant; *vijaya* — of setting out; *udyame* — in the undertaking; *parisamāpta* — having completed; *vidhiḥ* — the rites; *mahitaḥ* — honoured; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *dvija* — of brahmanas; *gaṇena* — by the crowd; *sukhaiḥ* — with joys; *vavṛdhe* — grew; *jaya* — of "Jaya!"; *svanavatā* — by [the crowd] resounding with cries; *nava* — fresh; *tāmarasa* — [like] lotuses; *īkṣaṇaḥ* — [He whose] eyes.

**Translation.** First, exultant at the undertaking of setting out, He completed the rites; and, honoured again and again by the crowd of brahmanas resounding with cries of "Jaya!", He whose eyes were like fresh lotuses grew ever fuller of joy.

### 23

**23.**

```
द्विजगणैर्भगणैरिव संक्षरन्
द्युतिसुधा वसुधासु शशीव सः ।
सुकथितैः पथि तैर्विलसन्प्रभू

```
*dvija-gaṇair bha-gaṇair iva saṅkṣaran
dyuti-sudhā vasudhāsu śaśīva saḥ |
sukathitaiḥ pathi tair vilasan prabhū
rasamayaṁ samayaṁ tam amanyata ||23||*

**Word for word:** *dvija* — of brahmanas; *gaṇaiḥ* — with the throngs; *bha* — of stars; *gaṇaiḥ* — with the hosts; *iva* — as; *saṅkṣaran* — pouring forth; *dyuti* — of radiance; *sudhāḥ* — the nectars; *vasudhāsu* — upon the lands; *śaśī* — the moon; *iva* — like; *saḥ* — He; *sukathitaiḥ* — with good conversations; *pathi* — on the way; *taiḥ* — with them; *vilasan* — delighting; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *rasa* — with sweetness; *mayam* — filled; *samayam* — time; *tam* — that; *amanyata* — considered.

### 24

**24.**

```
क्वचन वीक्ष्य मनोज्ञतमां स्थलीं
स्थलपयोरुहपादपयोरुहाम् ।
उपतरङ्गिणि तेन विशद्विभ्रमे
न मधुपा मधु पातुमनुत्सुकाः ॥२४॥
```
*kvacana vīkṣya manojñatamāṁ sthalīṁ
sthala-payoruha-pāda-payoruhām |
upataraṅgiṇi tena viśad-vibhrame
na madhupā madhu pātum anutsukāḥ ||24||*

**Word for word:** *kvacana* — somewhere; *vīkṣya* — having seen; *manojñatamām* — most charming; *sthalīm* — a spot; *sthala* — land; *payoruha* — lotuses; *pāda* — of the feet; *payoruhām* — of the lotuses; *upataraṅgiṇi* — near a river; *tena* — by Him; *viśat* — being entered (sat in); *vibhrame* — in grace; *na* — not; *madhupāḥ* — the bees; *madhu* — the honey; *pātum* — to drink; *anutsukāḥ* — unwilling.

**Translation.** Somewhere near a river He saw a most charming spot, and when He in His grace sat down there, the bees were far from unwilling to drink the honey of His lotus feet, [taking them for] land lotuses.

**Note.** *Na anutsukāḥ*, "not unwilling", that is, greedily eager: a double negative. *Sthala-padma*, the "land lotus", is the hibiscus.

### 25

**25.**

```
निभृतनीलमधुव्रतलोचनै-
र्ललितकेशरदन्तविकस्वरैः ।
विकसिताम्बुरुहाननमण्डलै-
र्मधुरसाधुरसा मधुशालिनी ॥२५॥
```
*nibhṛta-nīla-madhuvrata-locanair
lalita-keśara-danta-vikasvaraiḥ |
vikasitāmburuhānana-maṇḍalair
madhura-sādhu-rasā madhu-śālinī ||25||*

**Word for word:** *nibhṛta* — quiet; *nīla* — dark; *madhuvrata* — bees; *locanaiḥ* — [as] eyes; *lalita* — lovely; *keśara* — filaments; *danta* — [as] teeth; *vikasvaraiḥ* — with gleaming; *vikasita* — of opened; *amburuha* — lotuses; *ānana* — [as] faces; *maṇḍalaiḥ* — with the circles; *madhura* — of sweet; *sādhu* — excellent; *rasā* — [full of] juice; *madhu* — with honey; *śālinī* — rich.

**Translation.** [The row of lotuses in the pond] — with quiet dark bees for eyes, gleaming with lovely filaments for teeth, with the circles of opened lotuses for faces, full of sweet and excellent juice, rich in honey…

**Note.** Verses 25–26 are a *yugmaka*, one sentence. The subject is *kamalinī-tati* (v. 26), "the row of lotuses" in the pond, described as a beautiful woman.

### 26

**26.**

```
मदनमन्थरहंसवधूगति-
प्रतिपदोल्लसिता मधुराकृतिः ।
कमलिनीततिरस्य मुदं दधे
सरसि को रसिको विलसेन्न हि ॥२६॥
(युग्मकम्)
```
*madana-manthara-haṁsa-vadhū-gati-
pratipadollasitā madhurākṛtiḥ |
kamalinī-tatir asya mudaṁ dadhe
sarasi ko rasiko vilasen na hi ||26||*

**Word for word:** *madana* — with love; *manthara* — slow; *haṁsa* — of swans; *vadhū* — of the females; *gati* — by the gait; *pratipada* — at every step; *ullasitā* — enlivened; *madhura* — lovely; *ākṛtiḥ* — [whose] form; *kamalinī* — of lotuses; *tatiḥ* — the row; *asya* — to Him; *mudam* — joy; *dadhe* — gave; *sarasi* — at a lake; *kaḥ* — what; *rasikaḥ* — connoisseur; *vilaset* — would delight; *na hi* — not.

**Translation.** …enlivened at every step by the gait of the female swans, slow with love, and lovely in form — this row of lotuses gave Him joy. For what connoisseur of beauty would not delight at a lake?

**Note.** Here begins the descriptive insertion traditional in a mahakavya: the lake, the sunset and the night (descriptions of forest and twilight). The story stands still; this is a poetic exercise. Verses 25–40 are saturated with *yamaka* (repetition of syllables) and are deliberately ambiguous.

### 27

**27.**

```
मधुकरा मधुपानमदोन्मदाः
किमिदमेव गदन्ति मुहुर्मुहुः ।
स्फुटसरोजवनीष्ववनीतले

```
*madhukarā madhu-pāna-madonmadāḥ
kim idam eva gadanti muhur muhuḥ |
sphuṭa-saroja-vanīṣv avanī-tale
kalabhatāṁ labhatāṁ rasiko janaḥ ||27||*

**Word for word:** *madhukarāḥ* — the bees; *madhu* — of honey; *pāna* — from the drinking; *mada* — by the intoxication; *unmadāḥ* — maddened; *kim* — is it not; *idam* — this; *eva* — just; *gadanti* — say; *muhuḥ muhuḥ* — again and again; *sphuṭa* — of opened; *saroja* — lotuses; *vanīṣu* — in the groves; *avanī* — of the earth; *tale* — on the surface; *kalabhatām* — the state of a young elephant; *labhatām* — let him attain; *rasikaḥ* — connoisseur; *janaḥ* — the man.

**Translation.** Is it not just this that the bees, maddened by the intoxication of drinking honey, say again and again: "Let the connoisseur on the face of the earth become a young elephant in the groves of opened lotuses"?

**Note.** The humming of the bees is presented as their speech. A young elephant in a lotus pond is an image of carefree enjoyment. A play of sounds: *kalabhatāṁ labhatām*.

### 28

**28.**

```
सरसि का रसिको विरतो भवे-
दुरसि को रसिकोऽधृततत्कुचः ।
ननु कथन्नु कथञ्चन तौ मतौ
```

urasi ko rasiko 'dhṛta-tat-kucaḥ |
nanu kathan nu kathañcana tau matau
kamala-komala-korakavan nayau ||28||*

**Word for word:** *sarasi* — at a lake; *kaḥ* — what; *rasikaḥ* — connoisseur; *virataḥ* — indifferent; *bhavet* — would be; *urasi* — to the chest; *kaḥ* — what; *rasikaḥ* — connoisseur; *adhṛta* — not having pressed; *tat* — her; *kucaḥ* — breasts; *nanu* — surely; *katham nu* — how then; *kathañcana* — in any way; *tau* — those two; *matau* — [not] esteemed; *kamala* — of the lotus; *komala* — tender; *korakavat* — like buds; *nayau* — ⟨?⟩.

**Translation.** ⟨?⟩ "What connoisseur would remain indifferent at a lake? What connoisseur would not press her breasts [— the lotus buds —] to his chest? How then could those two, tender as lotus buds, in any way not be esteemed?"

**Note.** ⟨?⟩ The verse is obscure and erotically coloured (lotus buds are the stock comparison for a woman's breasts); it continues the speech of the bees, and "she" is the row of lotuses of vv. 25–26. The translation is tentative; *nayau* is unclear (perhaps to be divided *na yau*, "which… not"). The text is the same in the Gosvami edition (GGM).

### 29

**29.**

```
इह दृशां सुदृशां सुषमां समां
तुलयितुं लयितुं च मुहुर्मुहुः ।
```

kuvalayaṁ valayaṁ pavanair vanair
akati vā kati kā madhurā dhurāḥ ||29||*

**Word for word:** *iha* — here; *dṛśām* — of the eyes; *sudṛśām* — of fair-eyed women; *suṣamām* — the beauty; *samām* — equal; *tulayitum* — to rival; *layitum* — to merge ⟨?⟩; *ca* — and; *muhuḥ muhuḥ* — again and again; *kuvalayam* — the blue lotuses; *valayam* — [in] a ring; *pavanaiḥ* — by the winds; *vanaiḥ* — by the waters ⟨?⟩; *akati* — swayed ⟨?⟩; *vā* — or; *kati* — how many; *kā* — what; *madhurāḥ* — sweet; *dhurāḥ* — ⟨?⟩.

**Translation.** ⟨?⟩ Here the blue lotuses, swaying in a ring with the winds and the waters, again and again [strove] to rival and to merge with the equal beauty of the eyes of fair-eyed women — how many, and what sweet ⟨?⟩…

**Note.** ⟨?⟩ The verse is almost pure *yamaka* (*dṛśāṁ sudṛśām*, *tulayituṁ layitum*, *kuvalayaṁ valayam*, *pavanair vanair*, *kati vā kati*) and cannot be analysed with certainty. The translation is tentative. The text is the same in the Gosvami edition (GGM).

### 30

**30.**

```
तनुतरङ्गतरङ्गमवीक्ष्य सा-
ऽतनुतरङ्गगतं प्रियमाकुला ।
तनुतरङ्गमयन्त्यखिलं पयो-
ऽतनुतरङ्गममुष्य सितच्छदी ॥३०॥
```
*tanu-taraṅga-taraṅgam avīkṣya sā-
'tanu-taraṅga-gataṁ priyam ākulā |
tanu-taraṅgam ayanty akhilaṁ payo-
'tanu-taraṅgam amuṣya sitacchadī ||30||*

**Word for word:** *tanu* — of fine; *taraṅga* — ripples; *taraṅgam* — amid the wave; *avīkṣya* — not seeing; *sā* — she; *atanu* — by a great; *taraṅga* — wave; *gatam* — carried off; *priyam* — the beloved; *ākulā* — agitated; *tanu* — the fine; *taraṅgam* — ripples; *ayantī* — going over; *akhilam* — all; *payaḥ* — the water; *atanu* — greatly; *taraṅgam* — billowing ⟨?⟩; *amuṣya* — of him (towards him); *sitacchadī* — the female swan ("white-winged").

**Translation.** The white-winged [swan], not seeing amid the fine ripples her beloved, who had been carried off by a great wave, went in agitation over the fine ripples [towards] him, making all the water billow greatly ⟨?⟩.

**Note.** The whole verse is built on the repetition *tanu-taraṅga / atanu-taraṅga* ("fine ripples / great wave"). *Atanu*, "the Bodiless One", is also a name of Kama, so *atanu-taraṅga* can be heard as "a wave of love". The translation follows the sense.

**33.**


**34.**


**35.**


**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *vilokya* — having seen; *gatam* — gone; *carama* — to the western; *acale* — to the mountain; *pipatiṣum* — about to fall; *paripakva* — of a ripe; *phala* — fruit; *ākṛtim* — [having] the form; *dinakaram* — the sun; *bhramaraiḥ* — with the bees; *saha* — together; *niḥsṛtaiḥ* — that flew out; *gata* — lost; *rasā* — [whose] sap; *tarasā* — at once; *abhavat* — became; *abjinī* — the lotus pond.

**36.**


**Word for word:** *apatatā* — by [the swarm] not alighting; *kvacana api* — anywhere; *ca* — and; *niryatā* — flying out; *mada* — with intoxication; *kala* — sweetly [humming]; *ali* — of bees; *kulena* — by the swarm; *samantataḥ* — on all sides; *sahaja* — inborn; *vaira* — enmity; *vatī* — possessing; *iva* — as if; *babhūva* — became; *sā* — that; *kumudinī* — with night-lilies; *mudinī* — joyful; *rajanī* — night; *tadā* — then.

**37.**


**Word for word:** *nava* — for a new; *vikāśa* — blossoming; *parā* — intent; *api* — though; *kumudvatī* — the pond of night-lilies; *madhukaraiḥ* — by the bees; *sutarām* — thoroughly; *paribodhitā* — was awakened; *balavatā* — by an ardent; *dayitena* — by a lover; *yathā* — as; *bhavet* — happens; *priyatamā* — of the beloved; *āyata* — long; *māna* — of the sulking; *virāmataḥ* — upon the end.

**Note.** *Māna*, the "sulking" or jealous estrangement of the beloved, is an important notion in the aesthetics of love.

**38.**


**Word for word:** *aviratam* — ceaselessly; *nalinī* — of the day lotuses; *madhu* — of the honey; *mādhurī* — by the sweetness; *mada* — with intoxication; *madāḥ* — intoxicated; *api* — though; *puṣpa* — of flowers; *lihaḥ* — the lickers (bees); *muhuḥ* — again; *kumudinīm* — to the night-lilies; *abhajan* — resorted; *nirata* — with unceasing; *spṛhāḥ* — [having] desire; *nava* — new; *rasāḥ* — [seeking] taste; *vara* — the best; *sādhu* — good; *janāḥ* — people; *khalu* — indeed.

### 41

**41.**


**Word for word:** *sapadi* — at once; *saṅkucatā* — contracting; *dalatā* — opening; *navam* — anew; *dala* — of petals; *cayena* — by the mass; *tataḥ* — then; *sama* — equal; *vibhramā* — [having] charm; *udayatā* — rising; *viśatā* — entering; *ali* — of bees; *kulena* — by the swarm; *ca* — and; *pravasatā* — flying away; *vasatā* — settling; *rajasā* — by the pollen; *api ca* — and also.

**Word for word:** *amum* — this; *avāpya* — having received; *manum* — mantra; *vraja* — of Vraja; *bhāvinī* — of the lovely; *jana* — women; *pateḥ* — of the Lord; *pulaka* — of hairs standing on end; *aṅkura* — with the sprouts; *śobhinā* — adorned; *vigalat* — streaming; *aśru* — tears; *bhṛtā* — bearing; *vinayāt* — with humility; *ayam* — this; *nijagade* — was spoken; *jagat* — of the world; *eka* — by the one; *kṛpāvatā* — merciful [friend].

**Translation.** Having received this mantra of the Lord of the lovely women of Vraja, He, the one merciful [friend] of the world — adorned with the sprouts of hairs standing on end and shedding streaming tears — humbly spoke these [words]:

**Note.** "The mantra of the Lord of the women of Vraja" is the mantra of Krishna (the Gopala mantra). These signs of ecstasy are the first in the poem: from this moment Nimai the scholar becomes Gauranga the devotee. A play of sounds: *nijagade jagad-eka*.


### 64

**64.**

```

अथ विधाय हरेः पदपद्धतिम् ।
प्रभुरवेक्ष्य मुदं हृदि निर्भरां

```

**Note.** *Gayā-śiras*, "the head of Gaya" (by tradition, the head of the demon Gayasura). The Vishnupada is the temple at Gaya with the footprint of Vishnu in stone. According to all the biographies (Chaitanya-bhagavata, Adi 17), it is at Gaya that Nimai is first seized by the ecstasy of love; in Karnapura — at the sight of the print of Hari's feet. A play of sounds: *sahasā saha*.

### 65

**65.**

```
कथमभून्न हरेः पदपद्धतिं
समवलोकयतो मृदुतैव सः ।
इति विचिन्तयतोऽस्य दृशोर्झरो

```
*katham abhūn na hareḥ pada-paddhatiṁ
samavalokayato mṛdutaiva saḥ |
iti vicintayato 'sya dṛśor jharo
vipulakaḥ pulakaś ca tadābhavat ||65||*

**Word for word:** *katham* — how; *abhūt* — has become; *na* — not; *hareḥ* — of Hari; *pada* — of the feet; *paddhatim* — the print; *samavalokayataḥ* — while gazing on; *mṛdutā* — softness; *eva* — itself; *saḥ* — he [i.e. I]; *iti* — thus; *vicintayataḥ* — as [He] was thinking; *asya* — His; *dṛśoḥ* — from the eyes; *jharaḥ* — a torrent; *vipulakaḥ* — abundant; *pulakaḥ* — the bristling of the hairs; *ca* — and; *tadā* — then; *abhavat* — arose.

**Translation.** "How is it that I, gazing on the print of Hari's feet, have not become softness itself?" As He thought thus, a torrent poured from His eyes, and an abundant bristling of His hairs arose.


**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *divaḥ* — from the sky; *samabhūt* — arose; *aśarīriṇī* — bodiless; *sapadi* — at once; *gīḥ* — a voice; *nava* — of a new; *megha* — cloud; *vara* — excellent; *ākṛtiḥ* — having the form ⟨?⟩; *punaḥ* — again; *upaiṣyati* — will come; *tatra* — there; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *sva* — Your own; *bhavanam* — home; *bhava* — be [there now] ⟨?⟩; *nandayitum* — to gladden; *punaḥ* — again.

### 73

**73.**

```
सुघनं सुततं सुघनं सुततं
सहसा शुषिरैः सहसा शुषिरैः ।
अथ वाद्यमभूदथ वाद्यमभू-
द्रभसोद्यमभू रभसोद्यमभूः ॥७३॥
```
*sughanaṁ sutataṁ sughanaṁ sutataṁ
sahasā śuṣiraiḥ sahasā śuṣiraiḥ |
atha vādyam abhūd atha vādyam abhūr
rabhasodyama-bhū rabhasodyama-bhūḥ ||73||*

**Word for word:** *su* — well; *ghanam* — "solid" [instruments] (struck instruments of metal); *su* — well; *tatam* — "stretched" [instruments] (stringed); *su* — well; *ghanam* — solid; *su* — well; *tatam* — stretched; *sahasā* — all at once; *śuṣiraiḥ* — with "hollow" [instruments] (wind); *sahasā* — all at once; *śuṣiraiḥ* — with hollow; *atha* — and then; *vādyam* — music; *abhūt* — arose; *atha* — and then; *vādyam* — music; *abhūt* — arose; *rabhasa* — of impetuous; *udyama* — ardour; *bhūḥ* — the source; *rabhasa-udyama-bhūḥ* — the source of impetuous ardour.

**Translation.** All at once the music rang out — of fine metal instruments and fine stringed instruments, together with the wind instruments; music arose, music arose — the source of impetuous ardour, the source of impetuous ardour!

**Note.** The verse is sound-painting: each pada is repeated twice, like the echo of an orchestra. *Ghana* (solid, i.e. metal), *tata* (stretched, i.e. stringed) and *śuṣira* (hollow, i.e. wind) are classes of instruments in Indian musical theory; the fourth class is *avanaddha*, drums covered with skin (v. 70). *Sughanam sutatam* may also be heard adverbially: "densely, unbrokenly". The end of the verse is read according to the Gosvami edition (GGM): *abhūd rabhasodyama-bhū rabhasodyama-bhūḥ* (our edition has *abhūr abhasodyama-*). In the edition the verse is wrongly numbered 74.

muditām uditām atha vīkṣya tanuṁ
vasu tasya sutasya sasarja śacī ||74||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *kāñcana* — some ⟨?⟩; *kāñcana* — golden; *navya* — new; *latām* — [like] a creeper; *mṛdulām* — tender; *mṛdula* — with soft; *añcita* — adorned; *śubhra* — white; *paṭām* — with cloth; *muditām* — joyful; *uditām* — radiant; *atha* — and; *vīkṣya* — seeing; *tanum* — the body; *vasu* — wealth; *tasya* — of that; *sutasya* — of the son; *sasarja* — gave away [for the sake]; *śacī* — Shachi.

**Translation.** Then, seeing the body of her son — like some new golden creeper, tender, adorned with soft white cloth, joyful and radiant — Shachi gave away wealth for His sake.

**Note.** Again *yamaka*: *kāñcana-kāñcana*, *mṛdulāṁ mṛdula-*, *muditām uditām*. The first *kāñcana* is probably *kāṁcana*, "some, a certain", agreeing with *latām*.

### 75

**75.**

```
द्विजगणाय सनर्त्तकवादक-
प्रभृतयेऽपि च भिक्षुगणाय सा ।
निजसुतागमनोल्लसिता ददौ
निभृतसंभृतसम्पदिजं वसु ॥७५॥
```
*dvija-gaṇāya sa-nartaka-vādaka-
prabhṛtaye 'pi ca bhikṣu-gaṇāya sā |
nija-sutāgamanollasitā dadau
nibhṛta-saṁbhṛta-sampadijaṁ vasu ||75||*

**Word for word:** *dvija* — of brahmanas; *gaṇāya* — to the throng; *sa* — together with; *nartaka* — dancers; *vādaka* — musicians; *prabhṛtaye* — and others; *api ca* — and also; *bhikṣu* — of mendicants; *gaṇāya* — to the throng; *sā* — she; *nija* — of her own; *suta* — son; *āgamana* — by the arrival; *ullasitā* — gladdened; *dadau* — gave; *nibhṛta* — secretly; *saṁbhṛta* — stored up; *sampat* — of [her] means; *ijam* — born from; *vasu* — the wealth.

**Translation.** Gladdened by her son's arrival, she gave to the throng of brahmanas, together with the dancers, musicians and others, and also to the throng of mendicants, the wealth she had secretly stored up from her means.

### 76

**76.**

```
गयाया इत्येवं स्वगृहमगमद्भूरिकरुण-
प्रभुः पौषस्यान्ते सकलतनुभृत्तापशमनः ।
ततो माघस्यादौ निरवधि निजैः कीर्त्तनरसैः
प्रकाशं चावेशं भुवि विकिरति स्मानुदिवसम् ॥७६॥
```
*gayāyā ity evaṁ sva-gṛham agamad bhūri-karuṇa-
prabhuḥ pauṣasyānte sakala-tanu-bhṛt-tāpa-śamanaḥ |
tato māghasyādau niravadhi nijaiḥ kīrtana-rasaiḥ
prakāśaṁ cāveśaṁ bhuvi vikirati smānudivasam ||76||*

**Word for word:** *gayāyāḥ* — from Gaya; *iti evam* — thus; *sva* — His own; *gṛham* — home; *agamat* — returned to; *bhūri* — of abundant; *karuṇa* — compassion; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *pauṣasya* — of the month of Pausha; *ante* — at the end; *sakala* — of all; *tanubhṛt* — embodied beings; *tāpa* — the torments; *śamanaḥ* — who soothes; *tataḥ* — then; *māghasya* — of the month of Magha; *ādau* — at the beginning; *niravadhi* — without limit; *nijaiḥ* — with His own; *kīrtana* — of kirtana; *rasaiḥ* — with the rasas; *prakāśam* — the manifestation [of His divinity]; *ca* — and; *āveśam* — the inspired ecstasy; *bhuvi* — over the earth; *vikirati sma* — scattered; *anudivasam* — day after day.

**Translation.** Thus the Lord of abundant compassion, who soothes the torments of all embodied beings, returned home from Gaya at the end of the month of Pausha. Then, from the beginning of the month of Magha, day after day, without limit, He scattered over the earth, with the rasas of His own kirtana, the manifestation [of His divinity] and inspired ecstasy.

**Note.** The metre changes to *śikhariṇī* — the close of the sarga. Pausha is December–January, Magha January–February. **The beginning of kirtana** in Navadvipa is the opening of the Madhya-lila (Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 1).

### 77

**77.**

```
इति क्षणोत्क्षिप्तसमस्तचेष्टितः
प्रतिक्षणं गायति निर्भरं मुहुः ।
पदे पदे रोदिति रोमहर्षणै-
र्विमुक्तकण्ठं करुणापयोनिधिः ॥७७॥
```
*iti kṣaṇotkṣipta-samasta-ceṣṭitaḥ
pratikṣaṇaṁ gāyati nirbharaṁ muhuḥ |
pade pade roditi roma-harṣaṇair
vimukta-kaṇṭhaṁ karuṇā-payonidhiḥ ||77||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *kṣaṇa* — in a moment; *utkṣipta* — cast off; *samasta* — all; *ceṣṭitaḥ* — [He] with activities; *pratikṣaṇam* — at every moment; *gāyati* — sings; *nirbharam* — fully; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *pade pade* — at every step; *roditi* — weeps; *roma* — of the hairs; *harṣaṇaiḥ* — with the bristling; *vimukta* — with unrestrained; *kaṇṭham* — voice; *karuṇā* — of compassion; *payonidhiḥ* — the ocean.

**Translation.** Thus the ocean of compassion, having in a moment cast off all [other] activities, sings at every moment, again and again, with all His heart; at every step He weeps with unrestrained voice, the hairs of His body bristling.

**Note.** The metre of the last verse is *vaṁśastha*.

*Iti śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-caritāmṛte mahākāvye caturthaḥ sargaḥ samāptaḥ* — Thus ends the fourth sarga of the mahakavya "Sri Krishna-Chaitanya-charitamrita".
