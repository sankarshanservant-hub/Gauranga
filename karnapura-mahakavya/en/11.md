# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Eleven — verses 1–89 (printed pp. 308–339)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:** *śikhariṇī* (17 syllables per pada: ⏑————— ⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑—— ⏑⏑⏑—); verse 66 — *upajāti* (a quotation from the Bhagavatam); verses 87–89 — *mandākrāntā*.
**Total verses:** 89 (verse 20 has six padas).

**Contents of the passage:** the night-time play in the house of the Acharya; the sannyasa at Katwa; the journey to Puri.
- 1–6 — Gaura arranges a night-time play in the house of the Acharya (cf. Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 18): the roles are assigned; the wives of the brahmanas watch in secret together with Shachi; a guard stands at the door.
- 7–18 — the entrances: Srivasa as Narada, Shuklambara as his disciple, Gadadhara in a woman's role (the bathing in the month of Magha); Narada on the power of the Name (Ajamila); Srivasa's *nāndī*; Haridasa, staff in hand, rouses the three worlds and proclaims the command of Vaikuntha.
- 19–23 — Advaita in the costume of Krishna (with the Lord's flute and peacock feather); Nityananda as an old woman; Gadadhara in a woman's attire.
- 24–34 — a *kulaka* of eleven verses: the Lord Himself, in the guise of the Beloved (Lakshmi, then Girija), enters the shrine room.
- 35–37 — the Lord on the couch, Haridasa on His lap; seven days of dancing.
- 38–39 — Srivasa's question about the fruit of the Name; the answer about the four yugas.
- 40–48 — the intention to leave; the dream of the "great saying" and Murari's interpretation (the "genitive *tatpuruṣa*": *tasya tvam asi*); Keshava Bharati; the Lord departs across the Ganga.
- 49–57 — the lament of the townspeople and the women; the initiation (the Lord whispers the mantra to the guru), the tonsure (the barber weeps; "Hare Krishna"), the saffron cloth and the staff; the departure into the Radha country.
- 58–68 — three days in ecstasy; boys crying "Hari!" save Him from the river; Nityananda summons everyone to Advaita's house; the meeting (a quotation of Bhagavatam 11.23.57).
- 69–77 — at Advaita's house; the farewell (Haridasa with a straw between his teeth); the journey to Puri with Nityananda, Gadadhara and Mukunda.
- 78–86 — Remuna (the crown of Gopinatha), Sakshi-gopala, the broken staff, Yajapura (Jajpur), Bhubaneshvara, Kamalapura; Jagannatha.
- 87–89 (*mandākrāntā*) — conclusion: the well-being of Navadvipa while the Lord was present there.

**Textual cruxes:** 4 (*sahabhayaiḥ*), 9 (*avanamyā vada*), 10, 16, 20 (the subject), 30 (*rucyad-alake*), 32 (*kara-mitam*), 38, 40, 52 (*sva-puram*/*sva-gurum*), 57, 73 (*calitaḥ*), 79, 81 (*jagade tena*), 84 (whose temple) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
इतीदं तत्सर्वं विलसितमनुस्मृत्य सुदृशां
ससन्तोषं वृन्दावनमदन एषोऽतिमधुरः ।
विहर्तुं तद्भावैरकृत पुरतः स्वाङ्घ्रिदयितैः
समन्तादारम्भं द्रुतकनकगौरोज्ज्वलतनुः ॥१॥
```
*itīdaṁ tat sarvaṁ vilasitam anusmṛtya sudṛśāṁ
sasantoṣaṁ vṛndāvana-madana eṣo 'timadhuraḥ |
vihartuṁ tad-bhāvair akṛta purataḥ svāṅghri-dayitaiḥ
samantād ārambhaṁ druta-kanaka-gaurojjvala-tanuḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *idam* — this; *tat* — that; *sarvam* — all; *vilasitam* — play; *anusmṛtya* — having recalled; *sudṛśām* — of the fair-eyed [maidens]; *sa* — with; *santoṣam* — joy; *vṛndāvana* — of Vrindavana; *madanaḥ* — the Madana; *eṣaḥ* — this; *ati* — exceedingly; *madhuraḥ* — sweet; *vihartum* — to sport; *tat* — in their; *bhāvaiḥ* — in the moods; *akṛta* — made; *purataḥ* — before [Him]; *sva* — to His own; *aṅghri* — feet; *dayitaiḥ* — with [those] dear; *samantāt* — on every side; *ārambham* — preparation; *druta* — of molten; *kanaka* — gold; *gaura* — fair; *ujjvala* — radiant; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body.

**Translation.** Thus, having joyfully recalled all that play of the fair-eyed [gopis], this exceedingly sweet Madana of Vrindavana, whose radiant body is fair as molten gold, made preparations on every side, with those to whom His feet are dear [gathered] before Him, to sport in those same moods.

**Note.** The sarga opens with the night-time play in the house of the Acharya; cf. Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 18 (verses 28–31: the play is staged in the house of Chandrashekhara Acharya); so too Murari 2.15.7 ff. The "Acharya" here is evidently Chandrashekhara Acharya himself.

### 2

**2.**

```
क्रमादेतां रात्रिं प्रहरनियमेनैव विभजन्
दिदेश प्रायेण प्रियजनमसौ योग्यललितम् ।
विचिन्त्याथो नृत्यस्थलमनिशमाचार्यनिलये
मुदा रङ्गी चक्रे प्रसृमरतरं चत्वरमथ ॥२॥
```
*kramād etāṁ rātriṁ prahara-niyamenaiva vibhajan
dideśa prāyeṇa priya-janam asau yogya-lalitam |
vicintyātho nṛtya-sthalam aniśam ācārya-nilaye
mudā raṅgī cakre prasṛmarataraṁ catvaram atha ||2||*

**Word for word:** *kramāt* — in due order; *etām* — this; *rātrim* — night; *prahara* — of the watches; *niyamena* — by the rule; *eva* — indeed; *vibhajan* — dividing; *dideśa* — assigned; *prāyeṇa* — for the most part; *priya* — to [His] dear; *janam* — people; *asau* — He; *yogya* — suitable; *lalitam* — roles; *vicintya* — having considered; *atho* — then; *nṛtya* — for dancing; *sthalam* — a place; *aniśam* — unceasingly; *ācārya* — of the Acharya; *nilaye* — in the house; *mudā* — joyfully; *raṅgī* — the lover of spectacle; *cakre* — made; *prasṛmarataram* — very spacious; *catvaram* — the courtyard; *atha* — then.

**Translation.** Dividing this night in due order according to the watches, He assigned suitable roles, for the most part, to His dear ones. Then, having pondered [it] unceasingly, the lover of spectacle joyfully made the very spacious courtyard in the Acharya's house the place for the dancing.

### 3

**3.**

```
ततो रम्ये स्थाने परिनियमिते निर्वृतिकरे
गुरूत्कण्ठाभाजो द्विजवररमण्योऽतिनिभृताः ।
समं शच्या देव्या प्रभुमतमभिज्ञाय समयो-
चितं धैर्यारम्भं गृहमभिदधत्यः प्रविविशुः ॥३॥
```
*tato ramye sthāne pariniyamite nirvṛti-kare
gurūtkaṇṭhā-bhājo dvija-vara-ramaṇyo 'tinibhṛtāḥ |
samaṁ śacyā devyā prabhu-matam abhijñāya samayo-
citaṁ dhairyārambhaṁ gṛham abhidadhatyaḥ praviviśuḥ ||3||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *ramye* — when the charming; *sthāne* — place; *pariniyamite* — had been arranged; *nirvṛti* — delight; *kare* — giving; *guru* — great; *utkaṇṭhā* — eagerness; *bhājaḥ* — feeling; *dvija-vara* — of the best of brahmanas; *ramaṇyaḥ* — the wives; *ati* — very; *nibhṛtāḥ* — quiet; *samam* — together; *śacyā devyā* — with Shachi-devi; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *matam* — the wish; *abhijñāya* — having learned; *samaya* — to the occasion; *ucitam* — befitting; *dhairya* — of restraint; *ārambham* — a show; *gṛham* — the house; *abhidadhatyaḥ* — displaying; *praviviśuḥ* — entered.

**Translation.** When the charming, delightful place had been arranged, the wives of the best of brahmanas, full of great eagerness, learned the Lord's wish and, together with Shachi-devi, very quietly entered the house, showing a restraint befitting the occasion.

### 4

**4.**

```
अलिन्दे गेहस्य प्रभुनटनसन्दर्शनमहो-
त्सुका लीना आसन्निभृतमुपविष्टाः सभयैः ।
अमूश्चित्रोत्कीर्णा विनिमिषगतस्पन्दवपुषो
नवै रागैः कान्ताः फलकभुवि ता मूर्तय इव ॥४॥
```
*alinde gehasya prabhu-naṭana-sandarśana-mahot-
sukā līnā āsan nibhṛtam upaviṣṭāḥ sabhayaiḥ ⟨?⟩ |
amūś citrotkīrṇā vinimiṣa-gata-spanda-vapuṣo
navai rāgaiḥ kāntāḥ phalaka-bhuvi tā mūrtaya iva ||4||*

**Word for word:** *alinde* — on the veranda; *gehasya* — of the house; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *naṭana* — the dancing; *sandarśana* — to behold; *mahā* — greatly; *utsukāḥ* — eager; *līnāḥ* — hidden; *āsan* — were; *nibhṛtam* — secretly; *upaviṣṭāḥ* — seated; *sa-bhayaiḥ* ⟨?⟩ — with timidity; *amūḥ* — they; *citra* — in a painting; *utkīrṇāḥ* — depicted; *vinimiṣa* — unblinking; *gata* — having lost; *spanda* — movement; *vapuṣaḥ* — [with] bodies; *navaiḥ* — with fresh; *rāgaiḥ* — colours; *kāntāḥ* — lovely; *phalaka* — of a panel; *bhuvi* — on the surface; *tāḥ* — those; *mūrtayaḥ* — figures; *iva* — like.

**Translation.** Greatly eager to behold the Lord's dancing, they hid on the veranda of the house and sat there secretly and timidly. Unblinking, their bodies motionless, they were like lovely figures painted in fresh colours on the surface of a panel.

**Note.** The edition has *सहभयैः* (*saha-bhayaiḥ*); the form is unclear ⟨?⟩ and is taken here as "timidly". It is this reading of the edition that gives the second pada its seventeen syllables. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 5

**5.**

```
नियम्यैकं द्वारे दृढललितशौटीर्यवलितं
यथैकोऽप्यायाति क्षणमपि न तत्रेति निरतः ।
जनानाप्तानाप्तान् पुरमभिनिवेश्यैवमसकौ
महत्या निर्वृत्या जयति सततं गौरशशभृत् ॥५॥
```
*niyamyaikaṁ dvāre dṛḍha-lalita-śauṭīrya-valitaṁ
yathaiko 'py āyāti kṣaṇam api na tatreti nirataḥ |
janān āptān āptān puram abhiniveśyaivam asakau
mahatyā nirvṛtyā jayati satataṁ gaura-śaśabhṛt ||5||*

**Word for word:** *niyamya* — having stationed; *ekam* — one [man]; *dvāre* — at the door; *dṛḍha* — steadfast; *lalita* — graceful; *śauṭīrya* — with valour; *valitam* — endowed; *yathā* — so that; *ekaḥ api* — even one; *āyāti* — comes; *kṣaṇam api* — even for a moment; *na* — not; *tatra* — there; *iti* — thus; *nirataḥ* — intent; *janān* — people; *āptān āptān* — only the very closest; *puram* — into the house; *abhiniveśya* — having admitted; *evam* — thus; *asakau* — this; *mahatyā* — in great; *nirvṛtyā* — bliss; *jayati* — is victorious; *satatam* — ever; *gaura* — Gaura; *śaśabhṛt* — the moon.

**Translation.** Having stationed at the door one [man] — steadfast, graceful and full of valour — intent that not a single [outsider] should come in there even for a moment, and having thus admitted into the house only those closest [to Him], this moon Gaura is ever victorious in great bliss.

**Note.** The guard at the door is not named in the text. In the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 18.40–45) Haridasa, staff in hand, is the "constable of Vaikuntha" (*vaikuṇṭha-koṭāla*), who warns everyone.

### 6

**6.**

```
गृहैकं नेपथ्यस्थलमथ विधायाविशदसौ
प्रभुर्वेशं कर्तुं नटनकुतुकी प्रेमललितः ।
जनेनात्मीयेन स्वपदपरमप्रेमवहता
समारेजे श्रीमानतिशयकृपापूरसुभगः ॥६॥
```
*gṛhaikaṁ nepathya-sthalam atha vidhāyāviśad asau
prabhur veśaṁ kartuṁ naṭana-kutukī prema-lalitaḥ |
janenātmīyena sva-pada-parama-prema-vahatā
samāreje śrīmān atiśaya-kṛpā-pūra-subhagaḥ ||6||*

**Word for word:** *gṛha* — a room; *ekam* — one; *nepathya* — for dressing; *sthalam* — the place; *atha* — then; *vidhāya* — having made; *āviśat* — entered; *asau* — He; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *veśam* — costume; *kartum* — to put on; *naṭana* — for dancing; *kutukī* — eager; *prema* — with love; *lalitaḥ* — charming; *janena* — with the people; *ātmīyena* — His own; *sva* — for His own; *pada* — feet; *parama* — supreme; *prema* — love; *vahatā* — bearing; *samāreje* — shone; *śrīmān* — the beautiful [Lord]; *atiśaya* — of boundless; *kṛpā* — mercy; *pūra* — with a flood; *subhagaḥ* — lovely.

**Translation.** Then, having made one room the dressing-place, the Lord, eager for the dance and charming in His love, entered it to put on His costume. The beautiful [Lord], lovely with a flood of boundless mercy, shone among His own people, who bore supreme love for His feet.

**Note.** *Nepathya* is the backstage, the place where actors put on their costumes.

### 7

**7.**

```
गृहीत्वासौ वेशं पुरत ऋषिवर्यस्य परमं
निशादौ श्रीवासः प्रभुचरणपद्मप्रियतमः ।
जटाभिः पिङ्गाभिः स्फटिकमणिमालां कलयता
करेणोर्वीदेवप्रवर इव तत्राविशदथ ॥७॥
```
*gṛhītvāsau veśaṁ purata ṛṣi-varyasya paramaṁ
niśādau śrīvāsaḥ prabhu-caraṇa-padma-priyatamaḥ |
jaṭābhiḥ piṅgābhiḥ sphaṭika-maṇi-mālāṁ kalayatā
kareṇorvī-deva-pravara iva tatrāviśad atha ||7||*

**Word for word:** *gṛhītvā* — having assumed; *asau* — he; *veśam* — the guise; *purataḥ* — first; *ṛṣi-varyasya* — of the best of sages; *paramam* — splendid; *niśā* — of the night; *ādau* — at the beginning; *śrīvāsaḥ* — Srivasa; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *padma* — to the lotuses; *priyatamaḥ* — most dear; *jaṭābhiḥ* — with matted locks; *piṅgābhiḥ* — tawny; *sphaṭika* — of crystal; *maṇi* — beads; *mālām* — a rosary; *kalayatā* — telling; *kareṇa* — with a hand; *urvī* — of the earth; *deva* — of the gods (brahmanas); *pravaraḥ* — the foremost; *iva* — like; *tatra* — there; *āviśat* — entered; *atha* — then.

**Translation.** At the beginning of the night Srivasa, most dear to the lotus feet of the Lord, came in there first, having assumed the splendid guise of the best of sages: with tawny matted locks, telling a rosary of crystal beads with his hand, he entered like the foremost of brahmanas.

**Note.** The best of sages is Narada (cf. *devarṣi*, verses 9 and 11). *Urvī-deva*, "a god of the earth", is a brahmana.

### 8

**8.**

```
अथास्यैको दासो धृतकरकदर्भासनवरो
द्युवापीमृच्छुक्लो वपुषि भुवि शुक्लाम्बर इति ।
स देवेनाविष्टस्तमृषिवरमानम्य सहसा
गदाभृन्नामानं प्रभुदयितमूचे सुमधुरम् ॥८॥
```
*athāsyaiko dāso dhṛta-karaka-darbhāsana-varo
dyu-vāpī-mṛc-chuklo vapuṣi bhuvi śuklāmbara iti |
sa devenāviṣṭas tam ṛṣi-varam ānamya sahasā
gadābhṛn-nāmānaṁ prabhu-dayitam ūce sumadhuram ||8||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *asya* — his; *ekaḥ* — one; *dāsaḥ* — servant; *dhṛta* — holding; *karaka* — a water-pot; *darbha* — of *darbha* grass; *āsana* — a seat; *varaḥ* — excellent; *dyu* — of the heavenly; *vāpī* — stream (the Ganga); *mṛt* — with the clay; *śuklaḥ* — white; *vapuṣi* — on the body; *bhuvi* — on earth; *śuklāmbaraḥ* — Shuklambara; *iti* — [so called]; *saḥ* — he; *devena* — by the divinity; *āviṣṭaḥ* — possessed; *tam* — to that; *ṛṣi* — of sages; *varam* — best; *ānamya* — having bowed; *sahasā* — at once; *gadābhṛt* — Gadadhara; *nāmānam* — by name; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *dayitam* — to the beloved; *ūce* — spoke; *su-madhuram* — most sweetly.

**Translation.** Then came one of his servants, holding a water-pot and an excellent seat of *darbha* grass, his body white with the clay of the heavenly river — on earth he was called Shuklambara. Possessed by the divinity, he at once bowed to the best of sages and spoke most sweetly to the Lord's beloved one named Gadadhara.

**Note.** *Dyu-vāpī*, "the heavenly pool", is the Ganga. With the clay of the Ganga Vaishnavas draw the marks of *tilaka* on the body.

### 9

**9.**

```
अये त्वं देवर्षिश्चरणमवनम्यावद इदं
कलौ भूयां श्रीमत्प्रभुचरणसेवासु निरता ।
इतीदं श्रुत्वासौ मुनिरवददेतत् सुवदने
सुरस्रोतःस्वत्यां स्नपनमधिमाघं कुरु सदा ॥९॥
```
*aye tvaṁ devarṣiś caraṇam avanamyāvada idaṁ
kalau bhūyāṁ śrīmat-prabhu-caraṇa-sevāsu niratā |
itīdaṁ śrutvāsau munir avadad etat su-vadane
sura-srotaḥsvatyāṁ snapanam adhimāghaṁ kuru sadā ||9||*

**Word for word:** *aye* — O; *tvam* — you; *devarṣiḥ* — [of] the divine sage; *caraṇam* — to the feet; *avanamya* — having bowed; *āvada* — say; *idam* — this; *kalau* — in Kali; *bhūyām* — may I be; *śrīmat* — of the beautiful; *prabhu* — Lord; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *sevāsu* — to the service; *niratā* — devoted; *iti* — thus; *idam* — this; *śrutvā* — having heard; *asau* — this; *muniḥ* — sage; *avadat* — said; *etat* — this; *su-vadane* — O fair-faced one; *sura* — of the gods; *srotaḥsvatyām* — in the river; *snapanam* — bathing; *adhimāgham* — in the month of Magha; *kuru* — perform; *sadā* — always.

**Translation.** "O you, bow down to the feet of the divine sage and say this: 'May I in [the age of] Kali be devoted to the service of the feet of the beautiful Lord.'" Hearing this, the sage said: "O fair-faced one, always bathe in the river of the gods in the month of Magha."

**Note.** Gadadhara is playing a woman's role; hence *niratā* (feminine) and the address "fair-faced one". Magha (January–February) is the month of auspicious bathing in the Ganga. The edition has *अवनम्या वद इदं*; by the metre we read *avanamyāvada idam* (*ā-vada*, "say"), with hiatus before *idam*. *Devarṣiś* is formally a nominative; by the sense it is "[the feet] of the divine sage".

### 10

**10.**

```
तदा तत्पुण्येन प्रभुचरणपाथोजमिलनं
भविष्यत्येवं ते तदनु भवतीभिः कृतमिदम् ।
इदानीं तेन त्वं मुनिवरवरेण प्रभुपद-
प्रियो भूत्वा यातः किमु न विदितं तत् सुवदने ॥१०॥
```
*tadā tat-puṇyena prabhu-caraṇa-pāthoja-milanaṁ
bhaviṣyaty evaṁ te tad anu bhavatībhiḥ kṛtam idam |
idānīṁ tena tvaṁ muni-vara-vareṇa prabhu-pada-
priyo bhūtvā yātaḥ kim u na viditaṁ tat su-vadane ||10||*

**Word for word:** *tadā* — then; *tat* — of that; *puṇyena* — by the merit; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *pāthoja* — of the lotuses; *milanam* — meeting; *bhaviṣyati* — will be; *evam* — thus; *te* — for you; *tat anu* — after that; *bhavatībhiḥ* — by you [ladies]; *kṛtam* — done; *idam* — this; *idānīm* — now; *tena* — by that; *tvam* — you; *muni* — of sages; *vara* — of the best; *vareṇa* — by the blessing; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *pada* — to the feet; *priyaḥ* — dear; *bhūtvā* — having become; *yātaḥ* — have come; *kim u* — is it; *na* — not; *viditam* — known; *tat* — that; *su-vadane* — O fair-faced one.

**Translation.** "Then, by that merit, you will thus meet the lotus feet of the Lord. And afterwards this was done by you [ladies]. Now, by the blessing of that best of sages, you have become dear to the Lord's feet and have come [here] — is that not known [to you], O fair-faced one?"

**Note.** Who is speaking (the sage or Shuklambara) and how *tad anu bhavatībhiḥ kṛtam idam* should be understood are unclear ⟨?⟩. *Yātaḥ* is masculine despite the address "fair-faced one" — a play on the fact that the woman's role is played by Gadadhara. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 11

**11.**

```
ततोऽसौ देवर्षिः स्वयमवददुच्चैः सुललितं
महत्त्वं तद्भक्तेरविदितगुरुत्वं पुलकितः ।
वदामः किं नाम्नः परममहिमानं यदघकृ-
द्द्विजाभासो दासीपतिरपि च मुक्तोऽघनिवहात् ॥११॥
```
*tato 'sau devarṣiḥ svayam avadad uccaiḥ sulalitaṁ
mahattvaṁ tad-bhakter avidita-gurutvaṁ pulakitaḥ |
vadāmaḥ kiṁ nāmnaḥ parama-mahimānaṁ yad agha-kṛd
dvijābhāso dāsī-patir api ca mukto 'gha-nivahāt ||11||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *asau* — this; *devarṣiḥ* — divine sage; *svayam* — himself; *avadat* — proclaimed; *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *sulalitam* — gracefully; *mahattvam* — the greatness; *tat* — to Him; *bhakteḥ* — of devotion; *avidita* — unknown; *gurutvam* — [whose] weight; *pulakitaḥ* — thrilling with rapture; *vadāmaḥ* — shall we say; *kim* — what; *nāmnaḥ* — of the Name; *parama* — supreme; *mahimānam* — about the glory; *yat* — since; *agha* — sins; *kṛt* — committing; *dvija* — of a brahmana; *ābhāsaḥ* — a semblance; *dāsī* — of a maidservant; *patiḥ* — the husband; *api ca* — even; *muktaḥ* — was freed; *agha* — of sins; *nivahāt* — from a heap.

**Translation.** Then the divine sage himself, thrilling with rapture, loudly and gracefully proclaimed the greatness of devotion to Him, whose weight is unknown: "What shall we say of the supreme glory of the Name? For even a sinner, a brahmana in semblance only, the husband of a maidservant, was freed [by it] from a heap of sins!"

**Note.** An allusion to Ajamila (Bhagavatam 6.1–2): a fallen brahmana who lived with a maidservant was saved by uttering the name of Narayana at his last hour.

### 12

**12.**

```
इतीवोक्ते तस्मिन् परममुदिताः सर्वमनुजा
हरेर्नाम्नामुच्चैः किमपि विदधुः कीर्तनमथ ।
सहर्षं श्रीवासः पुलकिततनुस्तत्र कुतुका-

```
*itīvokte tasmin parama-muditāḥ sarva-manujā
harer nāmnām uccaiḥ kim api vidadhuḥ kīrtanam atha |
saharṣaṁ śrīvāsaḥ pulakita-tanus tatra kutukāt

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *iva* — as it were; *ukte* — when it had been said; *tasmin* — by him; *parama* — supremely; *muditāḥ* — delighted; *sarva* — all; *manujāḥ* — the people; *hareḥ* — of Hari; *nāmnām* — of the names; *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *kim api* — a wondrous; *vidadhuḥ* — performed; *kīrtanam* — kirtana; *atha* — then; *sa* — with; *harṣam* — joy; *śrīvāsaḥ* — Srivasa; *pulakita* — thrilling; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body; *tatra* — there; *kutukāt* — eagerly; *puraḥ* — in front; *nṛtyam* — a dance; *cakre* — performed; *prathamam* — first; *iva* — as if; *nāndīm* — the *nāndī*; *viracayan* — performing.

**Translation.** When he had spoken thus, all the people, supremely delighted, loudly performed a wondrous kirtana of the names of Hari. And Srivasa, his body thrilling, joyfully and eagerly danced there in front, as if first performing the *nāndī*.

**Note.** The *nāndī* is the benedictory prelude with which a Sanskrit drama opens.

### 13

**13.**

```
ततोऽस्मिन्निष्क्रान्ते कृतनटनसङ्कीर्तनरसे
विवेशासौ श्रीमान् धृतपरमवेशः सुमधुरः ।
प्रविष्टोऽसौ रेजे हिमकरसमूहप्रतिकृति-

```
*tato 'smin niṣkrānte kṛta-naṭana-saṅkīrtana-rase

praviṣṭo 'sau reje hima-kara-samūha-pratikṛtir

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *asmin* — when he; *niṣkrānte* — had gone out; *kṛta* — having tasted; *naṭana* — of dancing; *saṅkīrtana* — [and] of sankirtana; *rase* — the rasa; *viveśa* — entered; *asau* — this; *śrīmān* — beautiful; *dhṛta* — wearing; *parama* — a splendid; *veśaḥ* — costume; *su-madhuraḥ* — most sweet; *praviṣṭaḥ* — having entered; *asau* — he; *reje* — shone; *hima-kara* — of moons; *samūha* — of a host; *pratikṛtiḥ* — the likeness; *gṛhītvā* — having taken; *sat* — a fine; *vetram* — staff; *sapadi* — at once; *haridāsaḥ* — Haridasa; *aṅgana* — of the courtyard; *bhuvi* — on the ground.

**Translation.** When he, having tasted the rasa of dancing and sankirtana, had gone out, the beautiful, most sweet Haridasa entered, wearing a splendid costume. Having entered, he at once took up a fine staff and shone in the courtyard like a host of moons.

**Note.** *Vetra* — the staff of a doorkeeper or watchman. Cf. Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 18.40–45, 100, where Haridasa plays the role of the watchman (*koṭāla*): staff in hand, he goes round everyone and rouses them with the cry "Wake up!"

```

```

### 16

**16.**

```
विना नाम्नां गाथामृतरसधुनीस्रोतसि सदा

```

**Word for word:** *vinā* — without; *nāmnām* — of the names; *gāthā* — of the songs; *amṛta* — of nectar; *rasa* — of the juice; *dhunī* — of the river; *srotasi* — in the stream; *sadā* — always; *kṛta* — having made; *snānān* — ablutions; *lokān* — people; *iti* — thus; *tat* — that; *adhunā* — now; *vāñchitam* — [is] desired; *iha* — here; *tat* — therefore; *etat* — this; *viśvasmin* — throughout the whole world; *iha* — here; *saḥ* — He; *vikiran* — scattering; *ājigamiṣuḥ* — wishing to come; *bhavadbhiḥ* — by you; *kīrtyantām* — let be glorified; *guṇa* — of qualities; *samudayāḥ* — the multitudes; *śrī-bhagavataḥ* — of the beautiful Bhagavan.

**Note.** The syntax of the first half (*vinā … lokān iti*) is rendered according to the sense ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 17

**17.**

**18.**

**19.**

```

### 20

**20.**

```
प्रदायामुं चक्रे कलितकलधौतोज्ज्वलतनु-
र्यथेच्छं नृत्येऽस्मिन् धृतपरमसन्नायकरुचिः ।
ततस्तैस्तैः सर्वैः परममधुराकारकमनः
प्रविष्टो भूत्वासौ किमपि कमलाक्षः परिषदि ।

**Word for word:** *pradāya* — having given; *amum* — Him (Advaita); *cakre* — made [act]; *kalita* — having acquired; *kaladhauta* — [like] pure gold; *ujjvala* — radiant; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body; *yathā-iccham* — at will; *nṛtye* — in the dance; *asmin* — this; *dhṛta* — having assumed; *parama* — of the supreme; *sat* — noble; *nāyaka* — hero; *ruciḥ* — [whose] splendour; *tataḥ* — then; *taiḥ taiḥ sarvaiḥ* — with all these; *parama* — supremely; *madhura* — sweet; *ākāra* — in form; *kamanaḥ* — enchanting; *praviṣṭaḥ bhūtvā* — having entered; *asau* — He; *kim api* — indescribably; *kamala* — lotus-; *akṣaḥ* — eyed; *pariṣadi* — into the assembly; *laghu* — swiftly; *udyat* — rising; *mārdaṅga* — of mridangas; *dhvani* — with the sound; *su-madhuram* — most sweet; *nṛtyam* — a dance; *akarot* — performed; *layaiḥ* — with tempos; *tālaiḥ* — rhythms; *mānaiḥ* — [and] measures; *malayaja* — of sandalwood; *rasaiḥ* — with the pulp; *carcita* — anointed; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body.

**Note.** A verse of six padas. By the grammar (*pradāya amuṁ cakre*; "with all these", *taiḥ taiḥ sarvaiḥ*) the dancer of the second half is evidently Advaita in the role of Krishna. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 21

**21.**

```

**Word for word:** *priyā* — of the Beloved; *veśa* — into the guise; *āveśa* — from absorption; *sphurita* — flashing forth; *ruciḥ* — [whose] splendour; *udyat* — rising; *smita* — of a smile; *rucā* — by the radiance; *paridhvasta* — dispelled; *dhvāntā* — [by whom] darkness; *nibhṛta* — with secret; *rabhasa* — ardour; *āsvāda* — of relishing; *vivaśā* — overpowered; *ghana* — of thick; *snigdha* — glossy; *ābhugna* — curving; *ullasita* — luxuriant; *kabarī* — of braids; *bhāra* — from the mass; *vilasat* (*vigalat*) — shining (falling); *prasūnaiḥ* — with flowers; *ambhoda* — from a cloud; *udgata* — emerging; *bha* — of stars; *gaṇa* — of a host; *śobhām* — the beauty; *vidadhatī* — displaying.

```

वलीभङ्गैर्भङ्गीगरिम नटयन्ती करमितम् ।
श्लथन्नीवीबन्धच्छुरितविमलद्योतिकलया
नितम्बस्वेदार्द्रं घनजघनमन्यादृशमिव ॥३२॥
```
*skhalad-vakṣaḥ-kṣaumāñcala-hati-lasan-madhyam alasaṁ
valī-bhaṅgair bhaṅgī-garima naṭayantī kara-mitam |
ślathan-nīvī-bandha-cchurita-vimala-dyoti-kalayā
nitamba-svedārdraṁ ghana-jaghanam anyādṛśam iva ||32||*

**Word for word:** *skhalat* — slipping; *vakṣaḥ* — from the bosom; *kṣauma* — of the linen [garment]; *añcala* — of the hem; *hati* — by the strokes; *lasat* — gleaming; *madhyam* — the waist; *alasam* — languid; *valī* — of the folds; *bhaṅgaiḥ* — with the curves; *bhaṅgī* — of grace; *garima* — with weight; *naṭayantī* — making dance; *kara* — by the hand; *mitam* — measurable; *ślathat* — loosening; *nīvī* — of the sash; *bandha* — of the knot; *churita* — glinting; *vimala* — of pure; *dyoti* — radiance; *kalayā* — with a gleam; *nitamba* — of the hips; *sveda* — with perspiration; *ārdram* — moist; *ghana* — full; *jaghanam* — the hips; *anyādṛśam* — of another kind; *iva* — as if.

**Translation.** He made the languid waist dance — so slender that a hand could span it — gleaming under the strokes of the hem of the linen garment as it slipped from the bosom, and weighty in grace with the curves of its folds; and the full hips, moist with perspiration, in the gleam of pure radiance glinting from beneath the loosening knot of the sash, seemed as if of quite another kind.

**Note.** *Kara-mitam*, "measured by the hand", is taken here of the slender waist that a hand can span ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 33

**33.**

```
मुहुश्चक्रप्रायभ्रमणविगलत्केशकुसुमै-
स्तथा भ्राम्यद्भृङ्गीललितपरभागैः प्रसृमरैः ।
स्वयं नृत्योल्लासादुपरि मुखचन्द्रस्य नु दधे
सितच्छत्रं चित्रं मरकतसुरेखाविलसितम् ॥३३॥
```
*muhuś cakra-prāya-bhramaṇa-vigalat-keśa-kusumais
tathā bhrāmyad-bhṛṅgī-lalita-parabhāgaiḥ prasṛmaraiḥ |
svayaṁ nṛtyollāsād upari mukha-candrasya nu dadhe
sitac-chatraṁ citraṁ marakata-surekhā-vilasitam ||33||*

**Word for word:** *muhuḥ* — again and again; *cakra* — a wheel; *prāya* — like; *bhramaṇa* — from whirling; *vigalat* — falling; *keśa* — from the hair; *kusumaiḥ* — with flowers; *tathā* — and; *bhrāmyat* — circling; *bhṛṅgī* — by bees; *lalita* — charmingly; *parabhāgaiḥ* — set off; *prasṛmaraiḥ* — scattering; *svayam* — Himself; *nṛtya* — of the dance; *ullāsāt* — out of the rapture; *upari* — above; *mukha* — of the face; *candrasya* — the moon; *nu* — did not …?; *dadhe* — hold; *sita* — white; *chatram* — a parasol; *citram* — wondrous; *marakata* — with emerald; *su-rekhā* — fine stripes; *vilasitam* — adorned.

**Translation.** Again and again, whirling like a wheel, He scattered the flowers falling from the hair, charmingly set off by the circling bees. Did He not Himself, in the rapture of the dance, hold above the moon of His face a wondrous white parasol adorned with fine emerald stripes?

**Note.** The white flowers circling above the head are the white parasol (an emblem of royalty); the dark bees are the emerald stripes upon it.

### 34

**34.**

```
तथा नृत्योन्मादप्रमदमधुरिम्णातिमहता
नताङ्गी सङ्गीतोज्ज्वलरुचिररोचिःपटलिका ।
ततो लक्ष्मीभावं तदनु गिरिजाभावमपि सा
क्रमादाविष्कृत्य प्रकटमविशद्देवभवनम् ॥३४॥
(एकादशभिः कुलकम्)
```
*tathā nṛtyonmāda-pramada-madhurimṇātimahatā
natāṅgī saṅgītojjvala-rucira-rociḥ-paṭalikā |
tato lakṣmī-bhāvaṁ tad-anu girijā-bhāvam api sā
kramād āviṣkṛtya prakaṭam aviśad deva-bhavanam ||34||

**Word for word:** *tathā* — and; *nṛtya* — of the dance; *unmāda* — of the madness; *pramada* — of the rapture; *madhurimṇā* — by the sweetness; *ati-mahatā* — exceedingly great; *nata* — bowed; *aṅgī* — [whose] body; *saṅgīta* — by the music; *ujjvala* — illumined; *rucira* — lovely; *rociḥ* — of radiance; *paṭalikā* — [with] a veil; *tataḥ* — then; *lakṣmī* — of Lakshmi; *bhāvam* — the mood; *tat-anu* — after that; *girijā* — of Girija (Parvati); *bhāvam* — the mood; *api* — also; *sā* — that (form); *kramāt* — in turn; *āviṣkṛtya* — having manifested; *prakaṭam* — openly; *aviśat* — entered; *deva* — of the Deity; *bhavanam* — the chamber.

**Translation.** And, His body bowed by the exceedingly great sweetness of the rapture and madness of the dance, veiled in a lovely radiance illumined by the music, that form manifested in turn first the mood of Lakshmi and then that of Girija as well — and openly entered the shrine room. *(A kulaka of eleven verses.)*

**Note.** Verses 24–34 form one *kulaka*. Cf. Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 18: the Lord in the mood of Rukmini (verses 70–98), then He enters in the form of the Original Shakti (Adya-shakti, verses 120–154) and sits as Mahalakshmi (verse 163). Girija, "the daughter of the mountain", is Parvati.

### 35

**35.**

```
ततस्तां ते नत्वा स्तुतिवचनभङ्गीविरचनै-
र्महत्याः खट्वाया उपरि सरसाङ्गीं स्थितवतीम् ।
विधेहि प्रेमाणं भगवति समन्तादिति जगु-

```
*tatas tāṁ te natvā stuti-vacana-bhaṅgī-viracanair
mahatyāḥ khaṭvāyā upari sarasāṅgīṁ sthitavatīm |
vidhehi premāṇaṁ bhagavati samantād iti jagus

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *tām* — to Her; *te* — they; *natvā* — having bowed; *stuti* — of praise; *vacana* — of words; *bhaṅgī* — with artful; *viracanaiḥ* — compositions; *mahatyāḥ* — large; *khaṭvāyāḥ* — a couch; *upari* — upon; *sa-rasa* — full of rasa; *aṅgīm* — [whose] form; *sthitavatīm* — seated; *vidhehi* — bestow; *premāṇam* — love; *bhagavati* — O Goddess; *samantāt* — everywhere; *iti* — thus; *jaguḥ* — sang; *tataḥ* — then; *aṅke* — on the lap; *sā* — She; *cakre* — placed; *jhaṭiti* — at once; *haridāsam* — Haridasa; *śiśum* — a child; *iva* — like.

**Translation.** Then they bowed to Her, who was seated upon a large couch, Her form full of rasa, and with artful words of praise they sang: "O Goddess, bestow love everywhere!" Then She at once took Haridasa on Her lap like a child.

**Note.** The edition has *खट्टायाः*; the usual form is *khaṭvāyāḥ* ("couch"). Cf. the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 18.203–208): the Lord in the form of the Mother of the universe feeds the devotees at Her breast like Her children.

### 36

**36.**

```
इतीदं सा नानाविधकुतुकचेष्टाविलसितै-
र्निशां नीत्वा प्रातः स्वभवनमगाच्चित्रचरितः ।
तदा भूयस्तस्मिन्नकृत बहु नृत्यं सुमधुरं

```
*itīdaṁ sā nānā-vidha-kutuka-ceṣṭā-vilasitair
niśāṁ nītvā prātaḥ sva-bhavanam agāc citra-caritaḥ |

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *idam* — this; *sā* — She; *nānā-vidha* — various; *kutuka* — playful; *ceṣṭā* — with acts; *vilasitaiḥ* — [and] pastimes; *niśām* — the night; *nītvā* — having spent; *prātaḥ* — in the morning; *sva* — to His own; *bhavanam* — home; *agāt* — went; *citra* — wondrous; *caritaḥ* — [whose] deeds; *tadā* — then; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *tasmin* — there; *akṛta* — performed; *bahu* — many; *nṛtyam* — dances; *su-madhuram* — most sweet; *mahasvān* — the radiant [Lord]; *saptāham* — for seven days; *malayaja* — of sandalwood; *rasaiḥ* — with the pulp; *carcita* — anointed; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body.

**Translation.** Having thus spent the night in various playful acts and pastimes, She — He whose deeds are wondrous — went home in the morning. And then for seven days the radiant [Lord], His body anointed with sandal-paste, again performed many most sweet dances there.

**Note.** The change of gender (*sā*, "She" — *citra-caritaḥ*, "He") reflects the Lord's stepping out of the role.

### 37

**37.**

```
समन्तादुच्चैर्दिशि दिशि मृदङ्गादिनिनदा
मदोन्मत्ताः सर्वे कति कति रसाढ्यं न जगदुः ।

```
*samantād uccair diśi diśi mṛdaṅgādi-ninadā
madonmattāḥ sarve kati kati rasāḍhyaṁ na jagaduḥ |

**38.**

```

**Word for word:** *tathā* — then; *sapta-aha* — of the seven days; *ante* — at the end; *dinakara* — of suns; *śata* — a hundred; *prāya* — like; *mahasā* — with effulgence; *sphurantam* — to [Him] shining; *śrīvāsaḥ* — Srivasa; *sa* — with; *bhaya* — awe; *cakita* — wonder; *ullāsam* — [and] joy; *avadat* — said; *kalau* — in Kali; *nāmnām* — of the names; *gāthā* — the chanting; *yat* — which; *iha* — here; *vihitā* — has been ordained; *tatra* — in it; *nanu* — pray; *kim* — whether; *phalam* — fruit; *nūnam* — surely; *śāṭhye* — with guile; *bhavati* — comes; *kim u vā* — or; *na* — not; *iti* — thus; *vada* — tell; *tat* — that.

**Translation.** Then, at the end of the seven days, Srivasa, with awe, wonder and joy, said to Him who shone with the effulgence of a hundred suns: "The chanting of the names that has been ordained here in [the age of] Kali — pray, does it surely bear fruit when [performed] with guile, or not? **Note.** *Śāṭhye*, "with guile", is taken as "when [the names are chanted] insincerely" ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 39

**39.**

```

कलौ तत्राशक्तिं स्वयमिह विलोक्य प्रकटितं
प्रभुर्नामाख्योऽभूत्तदिह किमिव न्यूनफलता ॥३९॥
```
*kṛte tretāyāṁ ca dvija tad-anu dvāpara-yuge
samastaṁ dhyānādyair bhavati nitarāṁ sādhitatamam |
kalau tatrāśaktiṁ svayam iha vilokya prakaṭitaṁ
prabhur nāmākhyo 'bhūt tad iha kim iva nyūna-phalatā ||39||*

**Word for word:** *kṛte* — in the Krita; *tretāyām* — in the Treta; *ca* — and; *dvija* — O brahmana; *tat-anu* — after that; *dvāpara-yuge* — in the Dvapara-yuga; *samastam* — everything; *dhyāna* — by meditation; *ādyaiḥ* — and the rest; *bhavati* — is; *nitarām* — fully; *sādhitatamam* — accomplished; *kalau* — in Kali; *tatra* — for that; *aśaktim* — the incapacity; *svayam* — Himself; *iha* — here; *vilokya* — having seen; *prakaṭitam* — manifestly; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *nāma* — the Name; *ākhyaḥ* — called; *abhūt* — became; *tat* — so; *iha* — here; *kim iva* — how then; *nyūna* — small; *phalatā* — fruitfulness.

### 42

**42.**

```
इति श्रुत्वा गुप्तः सपदि स मुरारिः समवद-
त्प्रभो तत् षष्ठीतत्पुरुषवचनं तत्र कुरु भोः ।
तथा श्रुत्वा नाथः समुदितमनाः साम्प्रतमभू-

```
*iti śrutvā guptaḥ sapadi sa murāriḥ samavadat
prabho tat ṣaṣṭhī-tatpuruṣa-vacanaṁ tatra kuru bhoḥ |
tathā śrutvā nāthaḥ samudita-manāḥ sāmpratam abhūt
tathā te ca śrutvā vyathita-manaso gāḍham abhavan ||42||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *śrutvā* — having heard; *guptaḥ* — Gupta; *sapadi* — at once; *saḥ* — that; *murāriḥ* — Murari; *samavadat* — said; *prabho* — O Lord; *tat* — that; *ṣaṣṭhī* — a genitive; *tatpuruṣa* — *tatpuruṣa*; *vacanam* — word; *tatra* — there; *kuru* — make; *bhoḥ* — O [Lord]; *tathā* — thus; *śrutvā* — having heard; *nāthaḥ* — the Master; *samudita* — gladdened; *manāḥ* — [whose] heart; *sāmpratam* — then; *abhūt* — became; *tathā* — but; *te* — they; *ca* — and; *śrutvā* — having heard; *vyathita* — pained; *manasaḥ* — [whose] hearts; *gāḍham* — deeply; *abhavan* — became.

**Translation.** Hearing this, Murari Gupta at once said: "O Lord, take that word there as a genitive *tatpuruṣa*!" Hearing this, the Master was then gladdened at heart; but the others, hearing [it all], were deeply pained at heart.

**Note.** Murari proposes a Vaishnava reading of *tat tvam asi*: not "you are That", but, as a genitive *tatpuruṣa*, *tasya tvam asi*, "you are His", that is, you are the servant of the Lord. The devotees grieve, understanding that the Lord is thinking of sannyasa.

**43.**

**Translation.** Then by the will of providence there came before the Lord a certain sannyasi, known on earth as Keshava, with the title Bharati. Seeing the Master weeping thus endlessly, he said again and again in astonishment: "You are Shuka or Prahlada!"

**Note.** Keshava Bharati is the sannyasi from whom the Lord accepted sannyasa at Katwa. Shuka and Prahlada are the models of great devotees.

**Translation.** Hearing His own praise, He, doubly distraught, again wept bitterly. And this [sannyasi], greatly astonished, said again: "You are the Lord Vishnu — this I have truly understood!" Hearing this, the beautiful [Lord] desired to accept renunciation here.

**Note.** *Nyasana* (renunciation) is sannyasa.

मुकुन्दोऽथ प्रोचे विनिमिषममुं पश्यत मुहुः

**Translation.** Then Mukunda said: "Look at Him constantly, without blinking, while the Lord dwells at home and has not yet departed!" Then the Lord said to Srivasa: "Let Me tell you this: from here I shall wander ceaselessly in every direction for the sake of love."

**46.**

```
पुनः श्रीवासोऽयं सभयमवदत्त्वद्विरहितैः

**Word for word:** *punaḥ* — again; *śrīvāsaḥ* — Srivasa; *ayam* — this; *sa* — with; *bhayam* — fear; *avadat* — said; *tvat* — from You; *virahitaiḥ* — by [us,] separated; *katham* — how; *sthātum* — to live; *śakyam* — is it possible; *niravadhi* — without end; *vibho* — O mighty one; *dhakṣyati* — will burn; *manaḥ* — the heart; *bhavat* — your; *gehe* — in the house; *sthāsyāmi* — shall stay; *aham* — I; *iti* — thus; *jagāda* — said; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *atho* — then; *tathā* — so be it; *iti* — thus [saying]; *eṣaḥ* — he; *sthairyam* — steadiness; *manasi* — in the mind; *labhamānaḥ* — gaining; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment; *abhūt* — became.

**Note.** Cf. the Lord's promise to abide in Srivasa's house — Murari 2.18.17–23.

**47.**

```
ततः सायं गत्वा गृहमभि मुरारेरुपदिश-
ञ्जगादाद्वैते संश्रयितुमभिधायास्य चरितम् ।

```
*tataḥ sāyaṁ gatvā gṛham abhi murārer upadiśañ

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *sāyam* — in the evening; *gatvā* — having gone; *gṛham abhi* — to the house; *murāreḥ* — of Murari; *upadiśan* — instructing [him]; *jagāda* — told [him]; *advaite* — in Advaita; *saṁśrayitum* — to take shelter; *abhidhāya* — having related; *asya* — His [Advaita's]; *caritam* — life; *tataḥ* — then; *anyedyuḥ* — on the next day; *śrīmān* — the beautiful; *kva ca* — to a certain; *janapade* — district; *bhūri* — immensely; *karuṇaḥ* — merciful; *prabhuḥ* — Lord; *pāre-gaṅgam* — beyond the Ganga; *saḥ* — He; *sapadi* — at once; *titikṣuḥ* — forbearing; *calitavān* — set out.

**Note.** The district beyond the Ganga is Katwa, where Keshava Bharati lived.

ततस्ते ते सर्वे निरवधि बलद्दुःखदलिताः
समुद्विग्ना नाथ क्व गत इति तेपुः सकरुणम् ।

**50.**

```
स्त्रियः प्रोचुर्हा हा बत शिव शिवात्यन्तकठिनो
विधातुर्वैचित्र्यं कथमशनिपातोऽयमसकृत् ।
अहो रूपं शीलं मधुरिमसुलावण्यमहह
क्व सन्न्यासो वा क्व प्रतिमुहुरिदं मुह्यति मनः ॥५०॥
```
*striyaḥ procur hā hā bata śiva śivātyanta-kaṭhino
vidhātur vaicitryaṁ katham aśani-pāto 'yam asakṛt |
aho rūpaṁ śīlaṁ madhurima-su-lāvaṇyam ahaha
kva sannyāso vā kva pratimuhur idaṁ muhyati manaḥ ||50||*

**Word for word:** *striyaḥ* — the women; *procuḥ* — said; *hā hā* — alas, alas; *bata* — ah; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *atyanta* — utterly; *kaṭhinaḥ* — hard-hearted [is he]; *vidhātuḥ* — of the Creator; *vaicitryam* — the caprice; *katham* — how; *aśani* — of a thunderbolt; *pātaḥ* — the stroke; *ayam* — this; *asakṛt* — again and again; *aho* — ah; *rūpam* — the beauty; *śīlam* — the character; *madhurima* — the sweetness; *su-lāvaṇyam* — the exquisite loveliness; *ahaha* — alas; *kva* — where; *sannyāsaḥ* — sannyasa; *vā* — and; *kva* — where; *pratimuhuḥ* — again and again; *idam* — this; *muhyati* — is bewildered; *manaḥ* — mind.

**Translation.** The women said: "Alas, alas! Ah, Heaven forbid! Utterly hard-hearted [is he]: [such is] the caprice of the Creator! How [can] this thunderbolt strike again and again? **Note.** The turn *kva … kva* ("where the one — and where the other") expresses incompatibility. *Kaṭhinaḥ* (masculine) does not agree with *vaicitryam* (neuter): it evidently refers to the Creator.

**Word for word:** *guroḥ* — of the guru; *geham* — to the house; *taiḥ taiḥ* — with them; *vinaya* — to humility; *nirataḥ* — devoted; *abhyetya* — having come; *bahudhā* — many times; *praṇāmam* — obeisance; *cakre* — made; *asau* — He; *prativihita* — who had assumed; *śiṣya* — for a disciple; *ucita* — befitting; *ruciḥ* — a manner; *tataḥ* — then; *vaidhyam* — what was prescribed; *kṛtvā* — having performed; *sva* — His own; *gurum* — guru; *abhivādya* — having greeted; *asya* — his; *niratam* — earnestly; *śrutau* — into the ear; *svapna* — in a dream; *prāptam* — received; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *mahā-vākyam* — the great saying; *avadat* — spoke.

### 53

**53.**

```
समाहूयाथैकं क्षुरिणमतिधन्यातिसुभगं
दिदेशासौ श्रीमानहह निजकेशापहरणे ।

र्भयात् किञ्चित् कर्तुं शिव शिव शशाकाथ न खलु ॥५३॥
```
*samāhūyāthaikaṁ kṣuriṇam ati-dhanyāti-subhagaṁ
dideśāsau śrīmān ahaha nija-keśāpaharaṇe |
sa tu premāviṣṭo niravadhi rudan kampita-tanur
bhayāt kiñcit kartuṁ śiva śiva śaśākātha na khalu ||53||*

**Word for word:** *samāhūya* — having summoned; *atha* — then; *ekam* — a certain; *kṣuriṇam* — barber; *ati* — supremely; *dhanya* — blessed; *ati* — supremely; *subhagam* — fortunate; *dideśa* — ordered; *asau* — He; *śrīmān* — the beautiful; *ahaha* — alas; *nija* — His own; *keśa* — hair; *apaharaṇe* — to remove; *saḥ* — he; *tu* — but; *prema* — by love; *āviṣṭaḥ* — overwhelmed; *niravadhi* — without end; *rudan* — weeping; *kampita* — trembling; *tanuḥ* — [with] a body; *bhayāt* — out of fear; *kiñcit* — anything; *kartum* — to do; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *śaśāka* — was able; *atha* — then; *na khalu* — not at all.

**Translation.** Then the beautiful [Lord] summoned a certain barber, supremely blessed and supremely fortunate, and — alas! — ordered him to remove His hair. But he, overwhelmed by love, weeping without end, his body trembling, out of fear — Heaven forbid! — could not do anything at all.

### 54

**54.**

```
ततः श्रीगौराङ्गः समवददतीवप्रमुदितो
हरेकृष्णेत्युच्चैर्वद मुहुरिति श्रीमयतनुः ।
ततोऽसौ तत् प्रोच्य प्रतिवलितरोमाञ्चललितो
रुदंस्तत्तत्कर्मारभत बहुदुःखैर्विदलितः ॥५४॥
```
*tataḥ śrī-gaurāṅgaḥ samavadad atīva-pramudito
hare kṛṣṇety uccair vada muhur iti śrīmaya-tanuḥ |
tato 'sau tat procya prativalita-romāñca-lalito
rudaṁs tat-tat-karmārabhata bahu-duḥkhair vidalitaḥ ||54||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *śrī-gaurāṅgaḥ* — Sri Gauranga; *samavadat* — said; *atīva* — exceedingly; *pramuditaḥ* — joyful; *hare kṛṣṇa* — "Hare Krishna"; *iti* — thus; *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *vada* — say; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *iti* — thus; *śrī-maya* — splendid; *tanuḥ* — [with] a body; *tataḥ* — then; *asau* — he; *tat* — that; *procya* — having uttered; *prativalita* — bristling; *romāñca* — with hair standing on end; *lalitaḥ* — lovely; *rudan* — weeping; *tat-tat* — that; *karma* — work; *ārabhata* — began; *bahu* — by many; *duḥkhaiḥ* — sorrows; *vidalitaḥ* — crushed.

**Translation.** Then Sri Gauranga, splendid of form, said exceedingly joyfully: "Say 'Hare Krishna' loudly, again and again!" Then he, uttering it, lovely with his hair bristling on end, weeping, crushed by many sorrows, began that work.

**Note.** In the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Madhya 28.140–152) the barber is not named: he weeps and delays, and the Lord bids everyone sing the Name. The name "Madhu" (Madhava the barber) is a later tradition; it is not found in our sources.

**55.**

```
तदानीं ये तत्र क्षणमपि च तस्थुः शिव शिव
प्रकामं ते मातः पितरिति गदन्तोऽतिकरुणम् ।

**Word for word:** *tadānīm* — then; *ye* — who; *tatra* — there; *kṣaṇam api* — even for a moment; *ca* — and; *tasthuḥ* — stood; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *prakāmam* — unrestrainedly; *te* — they; *mātaḥ* — O Mother; *pitaḥ* — O Father; *iti* — thus; *gadantaḥ* — crying; *ati* — most; *karuṇam* — piteously; *karau* — the hands; *dattvā* — having placed; *mūrdhni* — on the head; *pratimuhuḥ* — again and again; *adhikṣepa* — to reproach; *niratāḥ* — given; *sva* — their own; *jīvam* — life; *nindantaḥ* — cursing; *kati* — how many; *na hi* — not; *vilāpam* — laments; *vyaracayan* — composed.

**Translation.** Then those who stood there even for a moment — Heaven forbid! — crying unrestrainedly and most piteously "O Mother! O Father!", placing their hands on their heads, given again and again to reproach, cursing their own lives — how many laments did they not compose!

### 56

**56.**

```
गुरुर्भूत्वा व्याजात् स्वयमिव पुरा शिष्यविधिना
ततो मन्त्रं लेभे जगति करुणामेव विकिरन् ।
ततो रोमाञ्चाढ्यं जिगमिषुमवेक्ष्य प्रभुमसौ
गृहाणेत्यह्नायारुणवसनदण्डादिकमदात् ॥५६॥
```
*gurur bhūtvā vyājāt svayam iva purā śiṣya-vidhinā
tato mantraṁ lebhe jagati karuṇām eva vikiran |
tato romāñcāḍhyaṁ jigamiṣum avekṣya prabhum asau
gṛhāṇety ahnāyāruṇa-vasana-daṇḍādikam adāt ||56||*

**Word for word:** *guruḥ* — guru; *bhūtvā* — having become; *vyājāt* — under a pretext; *svayam* — Himself; *iva* — as it were; *purā* — first; *śiṣya* — of a disciple; *vidhinā* — in the manner; *tataḥ* — then; *mantram* — the mantra; *lebhe* — received; *jagati* — over the world; *karuṇām* — mercy; *eva* — only; *vikiran* — scattering; *tataḥ* — then; *romāñca* — with thrills of ecstasy; *āḍhyam* — filled; *jigamiṣum* — wishing to go; *avekṣya* — having seen; *prabhum* — the Lord; *asau* — he (Keshava Bharati); *gṛhāṇa* — take; *iti* — thus [saying]; *ahnāya* — at once; *aruṇa* — saffron; *vasana* — cloth; *daṇḍa* — staff; *ādikam* — and the rest; *adāt* — gave.

**Translation.** First He Himself, under a pretext, as it were became the guru; then He received the mantra in the manner of a disciple, scattering nothing but mercy over the world. Then, seeing the Lord filled with thrills of ecstasy and wishing to depart, he [Keshava Bharati] at once said "Take [these]!" and gave Him the saffron cloth, the staff and the rest.

**Note.** Cf. v. 52: the Lord first spoke the "great saying" into His guru's ear, and only then received the mantra from him.

**57.**

```
गृहीत्वा दण्डाद्यं गुरुवचनसंपालनवशा-
दनैषीद्गौराङ्गो दिवसमवशात्मातिचतुरः ।
अथानुज्ञाप्यैनं सुकृतशतगाढं जनपदं
ययौ राढं गूढोपमपरमलोकोत्तरकृतिः ॥५७॥
```
*gṛhītvā daṇḍādyaṁ guru-vacana-saṁpālana-vaśād
anaiṣīd gaurāṅgo divasam avaśātmāti-caturaḥ |
athānujñāpyainaṁ sukṛta-śata-gāḍhaṁ janapadaṁ
yayau rāḍhaṁ gūḍhopama-parama-lokottara-kṛtiḥ ||57||*

**Word for word:** *gṛhītvā* — having taken; *daṇḍa* — the staff; *ādyam* — and the rest; *guru* — of the guru; *vacana* — the word; *saṁpālana* — of keeping; *vaśāt* — for the sake; *anaiṣīt* — spent; *gaurāṅgaḥ* — Gauranga; *divasam* — the day; *avaśa* — not under control; *ātmā* — [with] Himself; *ati* — supremely; *caturaḥ* — wise; *atha* — then; *anujñāpya* — having taken leave; *enam* — of him; *sukṛta* — of pious deeds; *śata* — with hundreds; *gāḍham* — filled; *janapadam* — to the country; *yayau* — went; *rāḍham* — to Radha; *gūḍha* — hidden; *upama* — incomparable; *parama* — supreme; *lokottara* — beyond this world; *kṛtiḥ* — [whose] deeds.

**Translation.** Having taken the staff and the rest, Gauranga — supremely wise, though not master of Himself — spent the day [there] for the sake of keeping His guru's word. Then, having taken leave of him, He whose deeds are hidden, incomparable, supreme and beyond this world went to the Radha country, filled with hundreds of pious deeds.

**Note.** Radha (*rāḍha*; not to be confused with Sri Radha) is a region of western Bengal. The edition has *गूढोपम*: possibly *gūḍhopama-*, "like something hidden", or *gūḍha-anupama-*; we take it as the latter. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 58

**58.**

```
पथि ध्यायं ध्यायं स्वचरितमसौ सौख्यविवशः
स्वनामप्रेमार्द्रः प्रतिपदमशक्तः स्खलति सः ।
क्वचिद्गायत्यार्तः क्वचिदपि नदत्यार्तनिनदं

*pathi dhyāyaṁ dhyāyaṁ sva-caritam asau saukhya-vivaśaḥ

kvacid gāyaty ārtaḥ kvacid api nadaty ārta-ninadaṁ

**Word for word:** *pathi* — on the way; *dhyāyam dhyāyam* — meditating again and again; *sva* — on His own; *caritam* — deeds; *asau* — He; *saukhya* — by bliss; *vivaśaḥ* — overwhelmed; *sva* — for His own; *nāma* — name; *prema* — with love; *ārdraḥ* — melted; *pratipadam* — at every step; *aśaktaḥ* — powerless; *skhalati* — stumbles; *saḥ* — He; *kvacit* — now; *gāyati* — sings; *ārtaḥ* — in distress; *kvacit api* — now; *nadati* — cries out; *ārta* — of anguish; *ninadam* — a cry; *kvacit* — now; *mandam* — slowly; *yāti* — goes; *kvacit api* — now; *mṛga-indra* — of the king of beasts (the lion); *druta* — swift; *gatiḥ* — [with] a gait.

**Translation.** On the way, meditating again and again on His own deeds, overwhelmed by bliss, melted with love for His own name, powerless, He stumbled at every step. **59.**

```
प्रभुस्तस्मिन् देशे क्षणमपि न संश्रुत्य विवशः
स्वनाम त्यक्ष्यामि स्वतनुमिति गत्वोपतटिनि ।
जले मज्जन् डिम्भैर्वद हरिमिति ध्वानमुखरै-
रदर्शि प्रेमार्द्रः प्रतिपदपतद्बाष्पजडितः ॥५९॥
```
*prabhus tasmin deśe kṣaṇam api na saṁśrutya vivaśaḥ
sva-nāma tyakṣyāmi sva-tanum iti gatvopataṭini |
jale majjan ḍimbhair vada harim iti dhvāna-mukharair
adarśi premārdraḥ pratipada-patad-bāṣpa-jaḍitaḥ ||59||*

**Word for word:** *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *tasmin* — in that; *deśe* — country; *kṣaṇam api* — even for a moment; *na* — not; *saṁśrutya* — having heard; *vivaśaḥ* — in despair; *sva* — His own; *nāma* — name; *tyakṣyāmi* — I shall give up; *sva* — My own; *tanum* — body; *iti* — thus [resolving]; *gatvā* — having gone; *upa-taṭini* — to the river; *jale* — in the water; *majjan* — sinking; *ḍimbhaiḥ* — by children; *vada* — say; *harim* — "Hari"; *iti* — thus; *dhvāna* — with cries; *mukharaiḥ* — noisy; *adarśi* — was seen; *prema* — with love; *ārdraḥ* — melted; *pratipada* — at every step; *patat* — falling; *bāṣpa* — by tears; *jaḍitaḥ* — numbed.

**Translation.** Not hearing His own name in that country even for a moment, the Lord in despair [resolved]: "I shall give up My body," and went to the river. As He was sinking into the water, He was seen by children noisily crying "Say 'Hari'!" — and the Lord, melted with love, became numb with the tears that fell at every step.

**Note.** "His own name" is the name of Hari. Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 3.13–18: in the Radha country the cowherd boys, seeing the Lord, cry "Hari! Hari!", and He rejoices (the same in Chaitanya-bhagavata, Antya 1).

**61.**

**62.**

**Translation.** Then, when by providence His course thus turned to the south, the beautiful [Lord] came to Himself: "Where indeed am I going?" Having reflected thus in His mind, He resolved to go to Advaita's house, and spoke very sweetly to Nityananda Prabhu:

### 64

**64.**

```
ततो गत्वा तत्र प्रमुदितमना नाथगदितं
निगद्य प्रत्येकं समनयदमुत्रैव सहसा ।

```
*tato gatvā tatra pramudita-manā nātha-gaditaṁ
nigadya pratyekaṁ samanayad amutraiva sahasā |

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *gatvā* — having gone; *tatra* — there; *pramudita* — joyful; *manāḥ* — [with] a heart; *nātha* — of the Lord; *gaditam* — the words; *nigadya* — having told; *pratyekam* — to each one; *samanayat* — brought [them]; *amutra* — there; *eva* — indeed; *sahasā* — at once; *śacī* — Shachi; *ca* — and; *ati* — greatly; *vyagrā* — agitated; *parama* — supremely; *muditā* — glad; *tatra* — there; *calitā* — set out; *kim* — what; *anyat* — more; *vaktavyam* — is to be said; *gatam* — departed; *iva* — as if; *navadvīpam* — Navadvipa; *abhavat* — became.

**Translation.** Then, going there, He [Nityananda], joyful at heart, told each one the words of the Lord and at once brought [them all] there. And Shachi too, greatly agitated and supremely glad, set out there. What more is there to say? Navadvipa itself, as it were, had departed.

**Translation.** Then on the next day they suddenly saw on a certain road the beautiful moon, Gaura, holding a water-pot and staff, wearing a pair of fine saffron garments, the likeness of the dense radiance of lightning — like the best of golden mountains, lofty-peaked and clad in red ochre.

**Note.** A play on words: *guru-śikha* means "with a lofty peak" (of a mountain) and also "with a long tuft of hair"; *gairika* is the red ochre on a mountain's slopes and also the ochre (saffron) cloth of a sannyasi.

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *evam* — thus; *gacchantam* — to [Him] going; *prabhum* — to the Lord; *ahaha* — ah; *kaścit* — a certain; *dvija-varaḥ* — excellent brahmana; *avadat* — said; *paśyāmi* — let me see; *etat* — this; *prabhu-vara* — O best of lords; *vapuḥ* — body; *te* — Your; *ati* — most; *madhuram* — sweet; *saḥ* — He; *ittham* — thus; *gātrebhyaḥ* — from the limbs; *vasanam* — the cloth; *apakṛṣya* — having drawn off; *eva* — indeed; *karuṇaḥ* — merciful; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *megha* — of the clouds; *apāye* — at the departure; *śaśabhṛt* — the moon; *iva* — like; *reje* — shone; *ati* — utterly; *vimalaḥ* — spotless.

**Translation.** Then, as the Lord was going thus — ah! — a certain excellent brahmana said [to Him]: "O best of lords, let me see this most sweet body of Yours." And the merciful Lord, drawing the cloth off His limbs, shone thus utterly spotless, like the moon when the clouds depart.

**Note.** Grammatically, it is the merciful Lord Himself who draws off the cloth (*sa … karuṇaḥ prabhuḥ*).

### 76

**76.**

```
पुरो नित्यानन्दं मुदितहृदयं भूरिकरुणो
विधायासौ गच्छन्निजचरणपङ्केरुहरतैः ।
गदाधृग्विप्राद्यैरहह समुकुन्दैः परिवृत-
स्तदा तैस्तैः सर्वैः कथमपि हि दुःखेन ददृशे ॥७६॥
```
*puro nityānandaṁ mudita-hṛdayaṁ bhūri-karuṇo
vidhāyāsau gacchan nija-caraṇa-paṅkeruha-rataiḥ |
gadādhṛg-viprādyair ahaha sa-mukundaiḥ parivṛtas

**Word for word:** *puraḥ* — in front; *nityānandam* — Nityananda; *mudita* — joyful; *hṛdayam* — at heart; *bhūri* — immensely; *karuṇaḥ* — merciful; *vidhāya* — having placed; *asau* — He; *gacchan* — going; *nija* — His own; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *paṅkeruha* — to the lotus; *rataiḥ* — by those devoted; *gadādhṛk* — by Gadadhara; *vipra* — by the brahmanas; *ādyaiḥ* — and others; *ahaha* — ah; *sa* — with; *mukundaiḥ* — Mukunda; *parivṛtaḥ* — surrounded; *tadā* — then; *taiḥ taiḥ sarvaiḥ* — by all of them; *katham api* — with difficulty; *hi* — indeed; *duḥkhena* — through grief; *dadṛśe* — was seen.

**Translation.** Placing Nityananda, joyful at heart, in front, the immensely merciful Lord went on, surrounded by those devoted to His lotus feet — Gadadhara, the brahmanas and others, together with Mukunda. Ah, all of them could then see Him only with difficulty, through their grief!

**Note.** *Gadādhṛk* ("bearer of the mace") is a synonym of the name Gadadhara. The compound *gadādhṛg-viprādyaiḥ* may also be read "by the brahmana Gadadhara and others".

**77.**

```

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — He; *ittham* — thus; *gaurāṅgaḥ* — Gauranga; *pathi* — on the way; *nija* — His own; *guṇam* — qualities; *nāma* — name; *ca* — and; *gṛṇan* — chanting; *priyaiḥ* — with those dear; *sārdham* — together; *sva* — to His; *aṅghreḥ* — feet; *niravadhi* — ceaselessly; *rudan* — weeping; *eva* — indeed; *ruruce* — shone; *amī* — they; *dāna* — of tolls; *ādāna* — in the taking; *ullasita* — rejoicing; *hṛdayaiḥ* — [with] hearts; *dāni* — of toll-collectors; *nivahaiḥ* — by the crowds; *na* — not; *kutra api* — anywhere; *śrīmat* — of the beautiful [Lord]; *paridṛḍha* — steadfast; *kṛpā* — in the mercy; *āḍhyāḥ* — rich; *rurudhire* — were stopped.

And nowhere were they, rich in the steadfast mercy of the beautiful [Lord], stopped by the crowds of toll-collectors whose hearts rejoiced in the taking of tolls.

**Note.** *Dānin* is a collector of tolls at ferries and checkpoints. Cf. the episodes of the journey to Puri: Murari 3.5.7–9, 3.7 (the toll-collectors, the detention of Mukunda); Chaitanya-bhagavata, Antya 2.

**78.**

```
ततो गोपीनाथं स्ववसतिलसद्रेमुणमति-

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *gopīnātham* — Gopinatha; *sva* — by His own; *vasati* — residence; *lasat* — making shine; *remuṇam* — Remuna; *ati* — great; *prabhāvam* — [of] power; *tam* — that; *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *kṣiti* — the ground; *milita* — touching; *maulī* — [with] the head; *praṇamataḥ* — as [He] bowed down; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *śīrṣe* — on the head; *śīrṣāt* — from the head; *api* — indeed; *bhagavataḥ* — of Bhagavan; *tasya* — of that; *calitā* — slipping; *prasūnānām* — of flowers; *cūḍā* — a crown; *apatat* — fell; *akhile* — all; *paśyati* — as [they] looked on; *jane* — the people.

**Note.** Remuna (near Balasore in Orissa) is the site of the temple of Gopinatha Kshirachora.

```

**Word for word:** *kare* — into the hand; *dattvā* — having given; *daṇḍam* — the staff; *pathi* — on the way; *tam* — that; *avadhūtasya* — of the avadhuta; *purataḥ* — ahead; *svayam* — Himself; *śrī-gaurāṅgaḥ* — Sri Gauranga; *sukha* — by bliss; *vivaśa* — overwhelmed; *cittaḥ* — [with] a mind; *calitavān* — went; *asau* — He; *paścāt* — behind; *gacchan* — walking; *manasi* — in the mind; *paricintya* — having pondered; *pratimuhuḥ* — again and again; *babhañja* — broke; *enam* — this; *daṇḍam* — staff; *kṛta* — having played; *kutuka* — playful; *ceṣṭaḥ* — a prank; *ati* — greatly; *muditaḥ* — pleased.

**Note.** A famous episode: Nityananda breaks Chaitanya's sannyasa staff.

**81.**

```

*athāsau nedīyān ahaha jagade tena cakitaṁ
kva me daṇḍo brūhi prativacanam eṣo 'pi vidadhe |

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *asau* — He; *nedīyān* — having come nearer; *ahaha* — ah; *jagade* — was addressed; *tena* — by Him; *cakitam* — in alarm; *kva* — where; *me* — My; *daṇḍaḥ* — staff; *brūhi* — tell; *prativacanam* — a reply; *eṣaḥ* — He; *api* — in turn; *vidadhe* — gave; *kṣitau* — on the ground; *daivāt* — by chance; *aṅghri* — of the foot; *skhalanam* — a slip; *abhavat* — happened; *tena* — because of that; *samabhūt* — became; *asau* — it; *bhagnaḥ* — broken; *tat* — so; *kim* — what [is to be done]; *tat anu* — after that; *ca* — and; *saḥ* — He; *cukrodha* — grew angry; *bahudhā* — greatly.

**Translation.** Then, when [the avadhuta] had come nearer, the Lord — ah! — said to Him in alarm: "Where is My staff? Tell Me!" And He in turn replied: "By chance My foot slipped on the ground, and so it broke. What [can be done]?" And after that the Lord grew very angry.

तथा क्षुब्धो भूत्वा मनसि बहु संचिन्त्य स ययौ

According to tradition, it was here that the Lord, seeing the banner of the temple, was overcome with ecstasy ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

```

jagannātho 'py enaṁ nimiṣa-rahitair akṣi-kamalair
vilokya premābdhau niravadhi nimagno 'bhavad iva ||86||*

**Word for word:** *muhuḥ* — again and again; *dṛṣṭvā* — gazing; *tasya* — His; *ānana* — of the face; *śaśinam* — on the moon; *atyanta* — most; *madhuram* — sweet; *galat* — streaming; *netra* — of the eyes; *ambhobhiḥ* — with the waters; *sva* — His own; *tanum* — body; *abhiṣiktām* — bathed; *aracayat* — made; *jagannāthaḥ* — Jagannatha; *api* — too; *enam* — at Him; *nimiṣa* — blinking; *rahitaiḥ* — without; *akṣi* — of the eyes; *kamalaiḥ* — with the lotuses; *vilokya* — looking; *prema* — of love; *abdhau* — in the ocean; *niravadhi* — endlessly; *nimagnaḥ* — immersed; *abhavat* — became; *iva* — as if.

**Translation.** Gazing again and again on the most sweet moon of His [Jagannatha's] face, He bathed His own body with the streaming tears of His eyes. And Jagannatha too, looking at Him with unblinking lotus eyes, became as if endlessly immersed in an ocean of love.

**Note.** Unblinking eyes are a mark of the gods and of the very form of Jagannatha.

### 87

**87.**

```
इत्थं चक्रे परमरभसं श्रीनवद्वीपभूमौ
गन्धैर्माल्यैर्मलयजरसैर्भूरिकर्पूरपूरैः ।
श्रीमद्वेशोद्गतमधुरिमाप्लावितशेषदेशः
स्वैः स्वैर्लोकैर्नटनकलया स्वैरमेष प्रकामम् ॥८७॥
```
*itthaṁ cakre parama-rabhasaṁ śrī-navadvīpa-bhūmau
gandhair mālyair malayaja-rasair bhūri-karpūra-pūraiḥ |
śrīmad-veśodgata-madhurimāplāvitāśeṣa-deśaḥ
svaiḥ svair lokair naṭana-kalayā svairam eṣa prakāmam ||87||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *cakre* — performed; *parama* — with the greatest; *rabhasam* — ardour; *śrī-navadvīpa* — of Sri Navadvipa; *bhūmau* — in the land; *gandhaiḥ* — with perfumes; *mālyaiḥ* — with garlands; *malayaja* — of sandal; *rasaiḥ* — with the essences; *bhūri* — abundant; *karpūra* — of camphor; *pūraiḥ* — with streams; *śrīmat* — splendid; *veśa* — from the appearance; *udgata* — arising; *madhurimā* — with the sweetness; *āplāvita* — who flooded; *aśeṣa* — all; *deśaḥ* — regions; *svaiḥ svaiḥ* — with His own; *lokaiḥ* — people; *naṭana* — of dancing; *kalayā* — with the art; *svairam* — freely; *eṣaḥ* — He; *prakāmam* — to His heart's content.

**Translation.** Thus in the land of Sri Navadvipa He who flooded all regions with the sweetness arising from His splendid appearance performed, with the greatest ardour, freely and to His heart's content, the art of dancing together with His own people — with perfumes, garlands, essences of sandal and abundant streams of camphor.

**Note.** Verses 87–89 are in the *mandākrāntā* metre: a summary of the Navadvipa period.

### 88

**88.**

```
गेहे गेहे समजनि सदा मूर्तिमत्येव लक्ष्मीः
स्थाने स्थाने सुखसमुदयो मूर्तिमानेव भूतः ।
नित्यं नित्यं नवनवमभूत् प्रेम सर्वस्य नाथे
स्वैरं स्वैरं विलसति तदा श्रीनवद्वीपभूमौ ॥८८॥
```
*gehe gehe samajani sadā mūrtimaty eva lakṣmīḥ
sthāne sthāne sukha-samudayo mūrtimān eva bhūtaḥ |
nityaṁ nityaṁ nava-navam abhūt prema sarvasya nāthe
svairaṁ svairaṁ vilasati tadā śrī-navadvīpa-bhūmau ||88||*

**Word for word:** *gehe gehe* — in every house; *samajani* — appeared; *sadā* — always; *mūrtimatī* — embodied; *eva* — herself; *lakṣmīḥ* — Lakshmi; *sthāne sthāne* — in every place; *sukha* — of happiness; *samudayaḥ* — the abundance; *mūrtimān* — embodied; *eva* — as if; *bhūtaḥ* — became; *nityam nityam* — day after day; *nava-navam* — ever new; *abhūt* — was; *prema* — the love; *sarvasya* — of everyone; *nāthe* — for the Lord; *svairam svairam* — quite freely; *vilasati* — when [He] sported; *tadā* — then; *śrī-navadvīpa* — of Sri Navadvipa; *bhūmau* — in the land.

**Translation.** When He sported quite freely in the land of Sri Navadvipa, in every house Lakshmi herself, embodied, was always present; in every place the abundance of happiness became as if embodied; and day after day everyone's love for the Lord was ever new.

### 89

**89.**

```
नासीन्निद्रा न भयमभवन्नाभवत् क्षुत्पिपासा
न स्वैरत्वं न च यमगता कालदण्डादिभीतिः ।
एकस्यापि प्रभुकरुणया यस्य कस्यापि तस्मि-
न्नेवं क्रीडत्यतिसुललितं श्रीनवद्वीपभूमौ ॥८९॥
```
*nāsīn nidrā na bhayam abhavan nābhavat kṣut-pipāsā
na svairatvaṁ na ca yama-gatā kāla-daṇḍādi-bhītiḥ |
ekasyāpi prabhu-karuṇayā yasya kasyāpi tasminn
evaṁ krīḍaty atisulalitaṁ śrī-navadvīpa-bhūmau ||89||*

**Word for word:** *na āsīt* — there was not; *nidrā* — sleep; *na* — not; *bhayam* — fear; *abhavat* — was; *na abhavat* — there was not; *kṣut* — hunger; *pipāsā* — [and] thirst; *na* — not; *svairatvam* — self-will; *na ca* — nor; *yama* — from Yama; *gatā* — coming; *kāla* — of Time; *daṇḍa* — of the rod; *ādi* — and the like; *bhītiḥ* — fear; *ekasya api* — of anyone at all; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *karuṇayā* — by the mercy; *yasya kasya api* — whoever he might be; *tasmin* — while He; *evam* — thus; *krīḍati* — sported; *ati-sulalitam* — most charmingly; *śrī-navadvīpa* — of Sri Navadvipa; *bhūmau* — in the land.

**Translation.** By the mercy of the Lord no one at all, whoever he might be, had sleep, or fear, or hunger and thirst, or self-will, or fear of Yama's rod of Time and the like, while He thus sported most charmingly in the land of Sri Navadvipa.

**Note.** *Kāla-daṇḍa*, "the rod of Time", is the staff of Yama, the lord of the departed.
