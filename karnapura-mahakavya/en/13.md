# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Thirteen — verses 1–148 (printed pp. 382–428)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:**
- 1–63 — *upajāti* (a mixture of *indravajrā* ——⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—— and *upendravajrā* ⏑—⏑——⏑⏑—⏑——; 11 syllables per pada); 12–13 — *anuṣṭubh* (8 syllables per pada; a quotation from the Kurma Purana); 39 — *mandākrāntā* (————⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑——⏑——⏑——; 17 syllables); 42 — *vasantatilakā* (——⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑——; 14 syllables); 46 — Ramananda's Bengali song (*rāga bhairavī*).
- 64–114 — *svāgatā* (—⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑⏑——) intermixed with *rathoddhatā* (—⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—); 11 syllables per pada.
- 115–125, 128–134, 138–144 — *viyoginī* (odd padas ⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—, 10 syllables; even padas ⏑⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—, 11 syllables); 119, 126–127, 135–137, 147 — *upajāti*.
- 145, 148 — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* (———⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑——⏑——⏑—; 19 syllables); 146 — *vasantatilakā*.

**Total verses:** 148.

**Contents of the passage:** the southern pilgrimage; the conversation with Ramananda; the return to Puri; the gathering of the devotees.
- 1–8 — the way south; Shrirangam: the chaturmasya in the house of Trimalla Bhatta; autumn.
- 9–13 — a brahmana devoted to Rama grieves over the abduction of Sita; the Lord shows him the verses of the Kurma Purana about the shadow Sita.
- 14–16 — the meeting with Paramananda Puri; he goes to Puri, the Lord to the Setu.
- 17–19 — seven palmyra palms are embraced and go to heaven.
- 20–30 — heretics try to allure the servant Krishnadasa; the Lord wins him back.
- 31–33 — Rameshvara at the Setu; the Lord turns back.
- **34–49 — the conversation with Ramananda on the way back, on the Godavari.** The verse on renunciation — "this is external"; the verse *nānopacāra…* — "external"; the song *pahilahi rāga…* on the highest oneness of the Two; the embrace; several days of conversation "on the path of realisation".
- 50–59 — Puri: the Snana-yatra; the anavasara; the Lord goes away to Alalanatha, and the devotees search for Him.
- 60–61 — **again on the Godavari**: four more months with Ramananda; at the onset of winter the Lord returns to Puri together with him.
- 62–71 — Kashi Mishra (he sees the Lord four-armed), Vishnudasa, Pradyumna Mishra.
- 72–77 — a grove reminiscent of Vrindavana: the creepers as dancers; the Lord's dance.
- 78–88 — Prataparudra in the grove: he glorifies the rasa dance; the embrace; "Who are you?" — "Your servant."
- 89–109 — Shikhi Mahiti, his brother Murari and his sister Madhava-devi (Madhavi-devi); Shikhi's dream; the meeting in the Jaganmohana; the embrace.
- 110–126 — the arrival of the Bengalis. The line of Madhavendra Puri and Ishvara Puri; Paramananda Puri in Navadvipa with Shachi and Acharyaratna; Kamalananda; the journey to Puri with Gadadhara and Jagadananda; the Lord bows to Paramananda; Vidyanidhi, Acharyaratna.
- 127–136 — Shivananda with Murari Gupta; Raghava; Govinda becomes [the Lord's] servant; Bhavananda ("you are like Pandu") and his five sons; Vaninatha.
- 137–144 — Purushottama Acharya becomes Svarupa Damodara.
- 145–148 — Vakreshvara, Vasudeva Datta, Bhagavan Acharya; the Lord's fame spreads; Satyaraja, Raghunandana, Narahari and Mukunda arrive.

**Divergences from the Chaitanya-charitamrita:**
- the conversation with Ramananda takes place only on the way back (34–49);
- a second visit to the Godavari after the anavasara, and four more months there (60–61);
- the Lord shows the leaf of the Kurma Purana at once (11), not on the way back;
- the seven palms come before the Setu (17–18);
- Prataparudra glorifies the rasa dance rather than reciting the Gopi-gita (82).

**Textual cruxes:** 3 (metre), 5 (*-saṅgam*), 7 (*tat*), 13 (*udanīnamat*), 14 (an extra syllable), 15 (*kṛpālū*), 24, 29, 34 (*tathaiva*), 38 (*sakaitavam*), 39–40 (syntax), 46 (the *bhaṇitā*), 57, 70 (metre), 74 (*priyām*), 80, 83 (*samudāse*), 86, 89, 95, 101, 106 (division of the lines), 113–114 (Ishvara/Paramananda Puri), 118, 126 (*ācāryaratnam*), 138, 140 (*atha*), 144, 146, 148 (*pratyāśam*) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
एवं स तीर्थाटनकौतुकेन
दीनैकबन्धुः करुणैकसिन्धुः ।
ततो ययौ भाग्यवतीमवाचीं
स्वनामरत्नग्रहणोत्सवोत्कः ॥१॥
```
*evaṁ sa tīrthāṭana-kautukena
dīnaika-bandhuḥ karuṇaika-sindhuḥ |
tato yayau bhāgyavatīm avācīṁ
sva-nāma-ratna-grahaṇotsavotkaḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *evam* — thus; *saḥ* — He; *tīrtha* — to the holy places; *aṭana* — for wandering; *kautukena* — with eagerness; *dīna* — of the wretched; *eka* — the only; *bandhuḥ* — friend; *karuṇā* — of compassion; *eka* — the one; *sindhuḥ* — ocean; *tataḥ* — from there; *yayau* — went; *bhāgyavatīm* — to the fortunate; *avācīm* — south; *sva* — of His own; *nāma* — name; *ratna* — of the jewel; *grahaṇa* — of the acceptance; *utsava* — for the festival; *utkaḥ* — eager.

**Translation.** Thus, eager to wander to the holy places, He — the only friend of the wretched, the one ocean of compassion — went from there to the fortunate south, longing for the festival of [people's] accepting the jewel of His own name.

### 2

**2.**

```
विलोक्य तं वर्त्मनि कृष्णसारा-
स्तृष्णावताक्ष्णोर्युगलेन भूयः ।
रूपामृतं पातुमिवाप्तधैर्याः
समं समन्तात् सविधं समीयुः ॥२॥
```
*vilokya taṁ vartmani kṛṣṇa-sārās
tṛṣṇāvatākṣṇor yugalena bhūyaḥ |
rūpāmṛtaṁ pātum ivāpta-dhairyāḥ
samaṁ samantāt savidhaṁ samīyuḥ ||2||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *tam* — Him; *vartmani* — on the road; *kṛṣṇa-sārāḥ* — the black antelopes; *tṛṣṇāvatā* — with the thirsting; *akṣṇoḥ* — of eyes; *yugalena* — with the pair; *bhūyaḥ* — again [and again]; *rūpa* — of [His] form; *amṛtam* — the nectar; *pātum* — to drink; *iva* — as if; *āpta* — having gained; *dhairyāḥ* — courage; *samam* — all together; *samantāt* — from all sides; *savidham* — close [to Him]; *samīyuḥ* — came.

**Translation.** Seeing Him on the road, the black antelopes took courage, as if to drink again and again the nectar of His form with their thirsting pairs of eyes, and all together came close to Him from every side.

### 3

**3.**

```
श्रीरङ्गक्षेत्रमसौ दयालुः
कावेरिकावेष्टितमुच्चदेशम् ।
आसाद्य तत्रत्यमवेक्ष्य देवं
प्रियाकरोऽसौ मुदमाप तुङ्गाम् ॥३॥
```
*śrī-raṅga-kṣetram asau dayāluḥ
kāverikā-veṣṭitam ucca-deśam |
āsādya tatratyam avekṣya devaṁ
priyākaro 'sau mudam āpa tuṅgām ||3||*

**Word for word:** *śrī-raṅga-kṣetram* — Sri Ranga-kshetra; *asau* — He; *dayāluḥ* — the Merciful One; *kāverikā* — by the Kaveri; *veṣṭitam* — encircled; *ucca* — elevated; *deśam* — a place; *āsādya* — having reached; *tatratyam* — present there; *avekṣya* — having beheld; *devam* — the Deity; *priya* — of love (?); *ākaraḥ* — the mine; *asau* — He; *mudam* — joy; *āpa* — attained; *tuṅgām* — lofty.

**Translation.** The Merciful One reached Sri Ranga-kshetra, an elevated place encircled by the Kaveri. Beholding the Deity there, He, the mine of love, attained lofty joy.

**Note.** *Priyākara*, literally "a mine of what is dear", that is, a treasury of love; or perhaps "one who does what is pleasing" ⟨?⟩. Shrirangam stands on an island between the arms of the Kaveri. The first pada has only ten syllables (*śrī-raṅga-kṣetram asau dayāluḥ*: ————⏑⏑—⏑——) instead of the eleven of the *upajāti*; a syllable seems to have dropped out ⟨?⟩.

### 4

**4.**

```
त्रिमल्लभट्टस्य महाशयस्य
गृहे कृतावासविधिः कृपालुः ।
कुतूहलेनैव निनाय चातु-
र्मास्यं स आवश्यककर्म कुर्वन् ॥४॥
```
*trimalla-bhaṭṭasya mahāśayasya
gṛhe kṛtāvāsa-vidhiḥ kṛpāluḥ |
kutūhalenaiva nināya cātur-
māsyaṁ sa āvaśyaka-karma kurvan ||4||*

**Word for word:** *trimalla-bhaṭṭasya* — of Trimalla Bhatta; *mahāśayasya* — the noble-minded; *gṛhe* — in the house; *kṛta* — having made; *āvāsa* — of residence; *vidhiḥ* — the arrangement (having settled); *kṛpāluḥ* — the Merciful One; *kutūhalena* — with eager delight; *eva* — indeed; *nināya* — spent; *cāturmāsyam* — the chaturmasya; *saḥ* — He; *āvaśyaka* — obligatory; *karma* — duties; *kurvan* — performing.

**Translation.** Settling in the house of the noble-minded Trimalla Bhatta, the Merciful One spent the chaturmasya there with eager delight, performing the obligatory duties.

**Note.** In the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 9.82–86) the host is Venkata Bhatta, but in the brief summary (Madhya 1.108–109) the Lord spends the chaturmasya with Trimalla Bhatta, as in Karnapura; according to Gaudiya tradition, Trimalla and Venkata were brothers (the third being Prabodhananda Sarasvati). The chaturmasya is the four months of the rainy season, during which sannyasis do not travel.

### 5

**5.**

```
कावेरिकायां विहिताप्लवोऽयं
चकार तस्या बहुपावनत्वम् ।
श्रीरङ्गसङ्गं प्रविलोक्य देवं
निनाय मासांश्चतुरः कृपालुः ॥५॥
```
*kāverikāyāṁ vihitāplavo 'yaṁ
cakāra tasyā bahu-pāvanatvam |
śrī-raṅga-saṅgaṁ [-saṁjñaṁ?] pravilokya devaṁ
nināya māsāṁś caturaḥ kṛpāluḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *kāverikāyām* — in the Kaveri; *vihita* — having performed; *āplavaḥ* — bathing; *ayam* — He; *cakāra* — made; *tasyāḥ* — its; *bahu* — manifold; *pāvanatvam* — purifying power; *śrī-raṅga* — Sri Ranga; *saṅgam* (*saṁjñam*?) — named (?); *pravilokya* — beholding; *devam* — the Deity; *nināya* — spent; *māsān* — months; *caturaḥ* — four; *kṛpāluḥ* — the Merciful One.

**Translation.** Bathing in the Kaveri, He multiplied its purifying power. Beholding the Deity named Sri Ranga, the Merciful One spent four months [there].

**Note.** *श्रीरङ्गसङ्गं* in the edition is unintelligible. We conjecture *śrī-raṅga-saṁjñam*, "called Sri Ranga" ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 6

**6.**

```
वर्षाशरन्मध्यगतः स कालः
परिष्वजन्तीं शरदं चुचुम्बे ।
कान्ताद्वयान्तःशयितो विलासी
पार्श्वाविवृत्ताविव वीतनिद्रः ॥६॥
```
*varṣā-śaran-madhya-gataḥ sa kālaḥ
pariṣvajantīṁ śaradaṁ cucumbe |
kāntā-dvayāntaḥ-śayito vilāsī
pārśvāvivṛttāv iva vīta-nidraḥ ||6||*

**Word for word:** *varṣā* — of the rains; *śarat* — and of autumn; *madhya* — in the middle; *gataḥ* — situated; *saḥ* — that; *kālaḥ* — season; *pariṣvajantīm* — embracing [it]; *śaradam* — autumn; *cucumbe* — kissed; *kāntā* — of beloveds; *dvaya* — two; *antaḥ* — between; *śayitaḥ* — lying; *vilāsī* — a lover; *pārśva* — onto [his] side; *āvivṛttau* — on turning; *iva* — like; *vīta* — rid of; *nidraḥ* — sleep.

**Translation.** That season lying between the rains and autumn kissed Autumn as she embraced him — like a lover lying between two beloveds who, waking and turning onto his side, [kisses the one who embraces him].

**Note.** That is, autumn set in. The edition has *स काल* without the visarga.

### 7

**7.**

```
ततो नवोत्फुल्लसरोरुहास्या
नवोत्पलाक्षी गतपङ्कजाला ।
सुजीवना तत्करुणाप्तिकामा
दासीव भेजे शरदीश्वरं तत् ॥७॥
```
*tato navotphulla-saroruhāsyā
navotpalākṣī gata-paṅka-jālā |
sujīvanā tat-karuṇāpti-kāmā
dāsīva bheje śarad īśvaraṁ tat ||7||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *nava* — freshly; *utphulla* — blossomed; *saroruha* — a lotus; *āsyā* — [whose] face [is]; *nava* — fresh; *utpala* — blue lotuses; *akṣī* — [whose] eyes [are]; *gata* — gone; *paṅka* — of mud (of sin); *jālā* — [whose] film [is]; *su-jīvanā* — [having] pure water (a good life); *tat* — His; *karuṇā* — mercy; *āpti* — to attain; *kāmā* — desiring; *dāsī* — a maidservant; *iva* — like; *bheje* — served; *śarat* — autumn; *īśvaram* — the Lord; *tat* — then (?).

**Translation.** Then Autumn — her face a freshly blossomed lotus, her eyes fresh blue lotuses, the film of mud gone from her, her waters pure — served the Lord like a maidservant who desires to attain His mercy.

**Note.** A double meaning (*śleṣa*): *paṅka* is both "mud" and "sin", *jīvana* both "water" and "life". The maidservant is free of sin, lives righteously and serves her master for the sake of his favour. The final *tat* is unclear; one would expect *tam* ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 8

**8.**

```
अथात आनन्दसमूहमग्नो
ययौ प्रहृष्टो दिशि दक्षिणस्याम् ।
महाप्रभुः स्वीयगुणानुगाथा-
निरन्तरोत्कीर्तनमुग्धवक्त्रः ॥८॥
```
*athāta ānanda-samūha-magno
yayau prahṛṣṭo diśi dakṣiṇasyām |
mahāprabhuḥ svīya-guṇānugāthā-
nirantarotkīrtana-mugdha-vaktraḥ ||8||*

**Word for word:** *atha ataḥ* — then from here; *ānanda* — of bliss; *samūha* — in the mass; *magnaḥ* — immersed; *yayau* — went; *prahṛṣṭaḥ* — rejoicing; *diśi dakṣiṇasyām* — in the southern direction; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *svīya* — of His own; *guṇa* — qualities; *anugāthā* — of songs; *nirantara* — by unceasing; *utkīrtana* — loud chanting; *mugdha* — charming; *vaktraḥ* — [whose] face [was].

**Translation.** Then Mahaprabhu, immersed in a mass of bliss, went on from here, rejoicing, in the southern direction, His face charming with the unceasing loud chanting of songs about His own qualities.

**Note.** "His own qualities", that is, the qualities of Krishna.

### 9

**9.**

```
तत्र क्वचित् श्रीरघुनाथभक्तं
प्रशान्तचित्तं द्विजपुङ्गवं सः ।
सीता दशास्यापहृतेति शोका-
द्बहिर्व्रजत्प्राणमिवालुलोके ॥९॥
```
*tatra kvacit śrī-raghunātha-bhaktaṁ
praśānta-cittaṁ dvija-puṅgavaṁ saḥ |
sītā daśāsyāpahṛteti śokād
bahir-vrajat-prāṇam ivāluloke ||9||*

**Word for word:** *tatra kvacit* — somewhere there; *śrī-raghunātha* — of Sri Raghunatha; *bhaktam* — a devotee; *praśānta* — peaceful; *cittam* — [whose] mind [was]; *dvija-puṅgavam* — a best of brahmanas; *saḥ* — He; *sītā* — Sita; *daśa-āsya* — by the Ten-faced one; *apahṛtā* — has been abducted; *iti* — that; *śokāt* — from grief; *bahiḥ* — out; *vrajat* — going; *prāṇam* — [whose] life [was]; *iva* — as if; *āluloke* — saw.

**Translation.** Somewhere there He saw a best of brahmanas, a devotee of Sri Raghunatha, peaceful in mind, whose life, from grief that Sita had been abducted by the Ten-faced one, seemed to be departing.

**Note.** Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 9.179–215: in Southern Mathura a brahmana worshipper of Rama (Ramadasa) grieves over the abduction of Sita; later the Lord brings him the leaves of the Kurma Purana. The Ten-faced one is Ravana.

### 10

**10.**

```
लक्ष्मीरियं राक्षसहस्तयाता
किमेतदित्यस्य मनो विदित्वा ।
आश्वासयन्नेव तमब्रवीद्भो
मैवं स्वरूपं शृणु यद्ब्रवीमि ॥१०॥
```
*lakṣmīr iyaṁ rākṣasa-hasta-yātā
kim etad ity asya mano viditvā |
āśvāsayann eva tam abravīd bho
maivaṁ svarūpaṁ śṛṇu yad bravīmi ||10||*

**Word for word:** *lakṣmīḥ* — Lakshmi; *iyam* — she; *rākṣasa* — of a Rakshasa; *hasta* — into the hands; *yātā* — fallen; *kim* — what; *etat* — is this; *iti* — thus; *asya* — his; *manaḥ* — mind; *viditvā* — having understood; *āśvāsayan* — comforting; *eva* — indeed; *tam* — to him; *abravīt* — said; *bho* — O [brahmana]; *mā evam* — not so; *svarūpam* — the truth; *śṛṇu* — hear; *yat* — which; *bravīmi* — I tell.

**Translation.** Understanding his mind — "She is Lakshmi, and she has fallen into the hands of a Rakshasa? What is this?" — He comforted him and said: "O [brahmana], not so! Hear the truth, which I shall tell you.

### 11

**11.**

```
यद्वा मदीये वचसि प्रतीति-
र्न ते भवित्री तदिदं नु पश्य ।
पुराणपद्यद्वयमित्यकस्मा-
ददर्शयत् स्वाञ्चलतो विकृष्य ॥११॥
```
*yadvā madīye vacasi pratītir
na te bhavitrī tad idaṁ nu paśya |
purāṇa-padya-dvayam ity akasmād
adarśayat svāñcalato vikṛṣya ||11||*

**Word for word:** *yadvā* — or if; *madīye* — in My; *vacasi* — word; *pratītiḥ* — faith; *na* — not; *te* — of you; *bhavitrī* — will be; *tat* — then; *idam* — this; *nu* — now; *paśya* — look at; *purāṇa* — from a Purana; *padya* — of verses; *dvayam* — a pair; *iti* — thus [saying]; *akasmāt* — suddenly; *adarśayat* — showed; *sva* — of His own; *añcalataḥ* — from the hem of the cloth; *vikṛṣya* — having drawn out.

**Translation.** Or if you will not have faith in My word, then look at this now." So saying, He suddenly drew out from the hem of His cloth two verses from a Purana and showed [them to him].

**Note.** In the Chaitanya-charitamrita the order is different: the Lord hears the Kurma Purana in Rameshvaram, takes the old leaves with these verses and, returning to Southern Mathura, gives them to the brahmana Ramadasa (Madhya 9.200–215). In Karnapura the leaf is already with the Lord.

**12.**

```
सीतयाराधितो वह्नि-
श्छायासीतामजीजनत् ।
तां जहार दशग्रीवः
सीता वह्निपुरं गता ॥१२॥
```
*sītayārādhito vahniś

**Word for word:** *sītayā* — by Sita; *ārādhitaḥ* — propitiated; *vahniḥ* — Agni; *chāyā* — a shadow; *sītām* — Sita; *ajījanat* — produced; *tām* — her; *jahāra* — carried off; *daśa-grīvaḥ* — the Ten-necked one; *sītā* — Sita; *vahni* — of Agni; *puram* — to the abode; *gatā* — went.

### 13

**13.**

```
परीक्षासमये वह्निं
छायासीता विवेश सा ।
वह्निः सीतां समानीय
स्वपुरादुदनीनमत् ॥१३॥
```
*parīkṣā-samaye vahniṁ
chāyā-sītā viveśa sā |
vahniḥ sītāṁ samānīya
sva-purād udanīnamat ||13||*

**Word for word:** *parīkṣā* — of the ordeal; *samaye* — at the time; *vahnim* — into the fire; *chāyā-sītā* — the shadow Sita; *viveśa* — entered; *sā* — she; *vahniḥ* — Agni; *sītām* — Sita; *samānīya* — having brought; *sva* — from his own; *purāt* — abode; *udanīnamat* (read *udanīnayat*) — led out.

**Translation.** At the time of the ordeal the shadow Sita entered the fire, and Agni, bringing [the real] Sita from his own abode, led her forth [to Rama]."

**Note.** The edition has *उदनीनमत्*; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *sva-purād udanīnayat*, "led out from his abode" (the aorist of the causative of *nī*), and so we read. In the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 9.212) the last pada is different: *tat-purastād anīnayat*, "brought [her] before him", that is, before Rama.

### 14

**14.**

```
अथात्र कथञ्चिद्यतिनां वरिष्ठं
ददर्श नाथो बहुहृष्टचित्तम् ।
महानुभावं परमं पुरस्ता-
दानन्दमध्यं च पुरीं तदन्तम् ॥१४॥
```
*athātra kathañcid yatināṁ variṣṭhaṁ
dadarśa nātho bahu-hṛṣṭa-cittam |
mahānubhāvaṁ paramaṁ purastād
ānanda-madhyaṁ ca purīṁ tad-antam ||14||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *atra* — here; *kathañcit* (read *kañcit*, as GGM) — a certain; *yatinām* — of ascetics; *variṣṭham* — the best; *dadarśa* — saw; *nāthaḥ* — the Master; *bahu* — very; *hṛṣṭa* — joyful; *cittam* — [whose] heart [was]; *mahā-anubhāvam* — magnanimous; *paramam* — "Parama"; *purastāt* — at the beginning; *ānanda* — "ananda"; *madhyam* — [having] in the middle; *ca* — and; *purīm* — "Puri"; *tat-antam* — [having] at its end.

**Translation.** Then here the Master saw a certain best of ascetics, magnanimous and very joyful at heart, [whose name had] "Parama" at the beginning, "ananda" in the middle and "Puri" at its end.

**Note.** A riddle-name: Paramananda Puri, a disciple of Madhavendra Puri. Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 9.168–174: the Lord meets Paramananda Puri at Rishabha hill; he goes on to Puri, and the Lord to the Setu. In our edition the first pada has twelve syllables (*athātra kathañcid*); the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *athātra kañcid*, "a certain [best of ascetics]", which is metrically correct, and so we read.

### 15

**15.**

```
विलोक्य संभाष्य सुजातहर्षौ
बभूवतुस्तौ परमप्रभावौ ।
अन्योन्यसंप्रीतिवशौ कृपालू
तस्मात् प्रयातुं दधतुश्च चेतः ॥१५॥
```
*vilokya saṁbhāṣya sujāta-harṣau
babhūvatus tau parama-prabhāvau |
anyonya-saṁprīti-vaśau kṛpālū
tasmāt prayātuṁ dadhatuś ca cetaḥ ||15||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen [each other]; *saṁbhāṣya* — having conversed; *sujāta* — arisen; *harṣau* — [in whom] joy [had]; *babhūvatuḥ* — became; *tau* — the two; *parama* — supreme; *prabhāvau* — [possessing] power; *anyonya* — of mutual; *saṁprīti* — love; *vaśau* — under the sway; *kṛpālū* — the two merciful ones; *tasmāt* — from there; *prayātum* — to depart; *dadhatuḥ* — set; *ca* — and; *cetaḥ* — [their] minds.

**Translation.** Having seen each other and conversed, the two, endowed with supreme power, were filled with joy. Under the sway of mutual love, both merciful, they resolved to depart from there.

**Note.** The edition has *कृपालु*; the dual *kṛpālū* is required.

### 16

**16.**

```
एको गतो गौरशशी त्ववाची-
मन्यः समागात् पुरुषोत्तमं च ।
सेतुं समुद्दिश्य चलन्नथासौ
रराज राजीवदलायताक्षः ॥१६॥
```
*eko gato gaura-śaśī tv avācīm
anyaḥ samāgāt puruṣottamaṁ ca |
setuṁ samuddiśya calann athāsau
rarāja rājīva-dalāyatākṣaḥ ||16||*

**Word for word:** *ekaḥ* — the one; *gataḥ* — went; *gaura* — the golden; *śaśī* — moon; *tu* — but; *avācīm* — to the south; *anyaḥ* — the other; *samāgāt* — set out; *puruṣottamam* — for Purushottama; *ca* — and; *setum* — the Setu; *samuddiśya* — heading for; *calan* — walking; *atha* — then; *asau* — He; *rarāja* — shone; *rājīva* — of a lotus; *dala* — [like] the petals; *āyata* — long; *akṣaḥ* — [whose] eyes [are].

**Translation.** The one, the golden moon, went south, and the other set out for Purushottama. And He whose eyes are long like lotus petals shone as He walked on, heading for the Setu.

**Note.** In the edition, as in the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Paramananda Puri goes to Puri. The Setu (Setubandha) is Rama's "bridge" in the south, near Rameshvaram.

### 17

**17.**

```
गच्छन् पथि प्रेमविभिन्नचेता
हसत्यलं रोदिति निर्भरार्तः ।
विभिन्नधैर्यश्चलितस्ततोऽसौ
ददर्श सप्तोच्छ्रिततालवृक्षान् ॥१७॥
```
*gacchan pathi prema-vibhinna-cetā
hasaty alaṁ roditi nirbharārtaḥ |
vibhinna-dhairyaś calitas tato 'sau
dadarśa saptocchrita-tāla-vṛkṣān ||17||*

**Word for word:** *gacchan* — going; *pathi* — along the road; *prema* — by love; *vibhinna* — broken; *cetāḥ* — [whose] heart [was]; *hasati* — laughs; *alam* — abundantly; *roditi* — weeps; *nirbhara* — utterly; *ārtaḥ* — afflicted; *vibhinna* — shattered; *dhairyaḥ* — [whose] composure [was]; *calitaḥ* — moving on; *tataḥ* — then; *asau* — He; *dadarśa* — saw; *sapta* — seven; *ucchrita* — tall; *tāla* — palmyra; *vṛkṣān* — trees.

**Translation.** Going along the road, His heart broken by love, He laughed abundantly and wept, utterly afflicted. His composure shattered, He moved on and then saw seven tall palmyra trees.

**Note.** The "Sapta-tala" are the seven palms connected with the story of Rama and Sugriva. Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 9.312–315 (the Lord embraces the seven palms and they vanish, "sent to Vaikuntha"); there this episode takes place on the way back.

### 18

**18.**

```
विलोक्य तांस्तालतरून् कृपालुः
प्रत्येकमेवाश्लिषदात्तहर्षः ।
अत्रान्तरे ते दिवमीयिवांसः
शून्या स्थली सा सहसैव याता ॥१८॥
```
*vilokya tāṁs tāla-tarūn kṛpāluḥ
pratyekam evāśliṣad ātta-harṣaḥ |
atrāntare te divam īyivāṁsaḥ
śūnyā sthalī sā sahasaiva yātā ||18||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *tān* — those; *tāla-tarūn* — palm trees; *kṛpāluḥ* — the Merciful One; *pratyekam* — each one; *eva* — indeed; *āśliṣat* — embraced; *ātta* — seized; *harṣaḥ* — [by] joy; *atra antare* — at that very moment; *te* — they; *divam* — to heaven; *īyivāṁsaḥ* — went; *śūnyā* — empty; *sthalī* — ground; *sā* — that; *sahasā* — suddenly; *eva* — indeed; *yātā* — became.

**Translation.** Seeing those palm trees, the Merciful One, seized with joy, embraced each one of them. At that very moment they went to heaven, and that ground suddenly became empty.

### 19

**19.**

```
क एष गौराङ्गमहाप्रभोस्तत्
विचित्रनानानुभवस्य लोके ।
अतर्कनीयो महिमा कृपालो-
श्चित्रं कृपायाः किमशक्यमास्ते ॥१९॥
```
*ka eṣa gaurāṅga-mahāprabhos tat
vicitra-nānānubhavasya loke |
atarkanīyo mahimā kṛpāloś
citraṁ kṛpāyāḥ kim aśakyam āste ||19||*

**Word for word:** *kaḥ* — what [wonder]; *eṣaḥ* — is this; *gaurāṅga-mahāprabhoḥ* — for Gauranga Mahaprabhu; *tat* — then; *vicitra* — wondrous; *nānā* — manifold; *anubhavasya* — [whose] manifestations [are]; *loke* — in the world; *atarkanīyaḥ* — inconceivable; *mahimā* — the greatness; *kṛpāloḥ* — of the Merciful One; *citram* — a wonder; *kṛpāyāḥ* — for mercy; *kim* — what; *aśakyam* — impossible; *āste* — is there.

**Translation.** What wonder is this, then, for Gauranga Mahaprabhu, whose manifestations in the world are wondrous and manifold? The greatness of the Merciful One is inconceivable. A wonder [indeed] — yet what is impossible for mercy?

**Note.** *Tat* at the end of the pada, before a vowel, stands in the edition without sandhi.

### 20

**20.**

```
अथ व्रजन् दक्षिणदिग्विभागे
विलोकयन् कौतुकचेष्टितानि ।
अखण्डपाषण्डपथप्रविष्टा-
न्ददर्श नानाविधलिङ्गसंघान् ॥२०॥
```
*atha vrajan dakṣiṇa-dig-vibhāge
vilokayan kautuka-ceṣṭitāni |
akhaṇḍa-pāṣaṇḍa-patha-praviṣṭān
dadarśa nānā-vidha-liṅga-saṅghān ||20||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *vrajan* — going; *dakṣiṇa* — of the southern; *dik* — direction; *vibhāge* — in the region; *vilokayan* — observing; *kautuka* — curious; *ceṣṭitāni* — doings; *akhaṇḍa* — utter; *pāṣaṇḍa* — of heresy; *patha* — upon the path; *praviṣṭān* — who had entered; *dadarśa* — saw; *nānā-vidha* — of various kinds; *liṅga* — [with] marks; *saṅghān* — crowds.

**Translation.** Then, going on through the southern region and observing curious customs, He saw crowds bearing sectarian marks of various kinds, who had entered upon the path of utter heresy.

**Note.** *Liṅga* — the outward marks of a sect: dress, tilaka, staff and so on.

### 21

**21.**

```
निकामवामे पथि वर्तमानाः
पाषण्डिनस्ते परिलोक्य नाथम् ।
नानाविधेन स्वमतेन शश्व-
द्विलोभयाञ्चक्रुरदभ्रपापाः ॥२१॥
```
*nikāma-vāme pathi vartamānāḥ
pāṣaṇḍinas te parilokya nātham |
nānā-vidhena sva-matena śaśvad

**Word for word:** *nikāma* — utterly; *vāme* — on a perverse; *pathi* — path; *vartamānāḥ* — abiding; *pāṣaṇḍinaḥ* — heretics; *te* — those; *parilokya* — having seen; *nātham* — the Master; *nānā-vidhena* — of various kinds; *sva* — with their own; *matena* — doctrine; *śaśvat* — constantly; *vilobhayāñcakruḥ* — tried to allure; *adabhra* — not slight; *pāpāḥ* — [whose] sins [were].

**Translation.** Those heretics, great sinners abiding on an utterly perverse path, saw the Master and constantly tried to allure Him with their own doctrines of various kinds.

### 22

**22.**

```
यदीयमायैकविजृम्भितेन
स्वं चातिपाषण्डपथप्रवृत्तम् ।
पश्यन्ति नैते तमिमं कथं वा
कुर्वन्तु नानाकुहकैर्विमुग्धम् ॥२२॥
```
*yadīya-māyaika-vijṛmbhitena
svaṁ cāti-pāṣaṇḍa-patha-pravṛttam |
paśyanti naite tam imaṁ kathaṁ vā
kurvantu nānā-kuhakair vimugdham ||22||*

**Word for word:** *yadīya* — whose; *māyā* — of the maya; *eka* — by the mere; *vijṛmbhitena* — unfolding; *svam* — themselves; *ca* — and; *ati* — of extreme; *pāṣaṇḍa* — heresy; *patha* — on the path; *pravṛttam* — set out; *paśyanti* — see; *na* — not; *ete* — they; *tam imam* — Him, this very one; *katham* — how; *vā* — then; *kurvantu* — could they make; *nānā* — various; *kuhakaiḥ* — by tricks; *vimugdham* — bewildered.

**Translation.** By the mere unfolding of His maya they do not see that they themselves have set out on the path of extreme heresy; how then could they bewilder Him with their various tricks?

### 23

**23.**

```
अथास्य सङ्गे जगदीश्वरस्य
व्रजन्तमेकं परिलोलचित्तम् ।
तं कृष्णदासाख्यममी विलोक्य
विलोभयाञ्चक्रुरतीवमन्दाः ॥२३॥
```
*athāsya saṅge jagad-īśvarasya
vrajantam ekaṁ parilola-cittam |
taṁ kṛṣṇadāsākhyam amī vilokya
vilobhayāñcakrur atīva-mandāḥ ||23||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *asya* — this; *saṅge* — in the company; *jagat* — of the world; *īśvarasya* — of the Lord; *vrajantam* — walking; *ekam* — one [man]; *parilola* — unsteady; *cittam* — [whose] mind [was]; *tam* — him; *kṛṣṇadāsa* — Krishnadasa; *ākhyam* — named; *amī* — they; *vilokya* — having seen; *vilobhayāñcakruḥ* — began to allure; *atīva* — extremely; *mandāḥ* — dull-witted.

**Translation.** Then those extremely dull-witted people saw a man of unsteady mind named Krishnadasa, who was walking in the company of the Lord of the world, and began to allure him.

**Note.** This is Kala Krishnadasa, the Lord's servant on the southern pilgrimage. In the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 9.226–231) he is lured away by the women of the Bhattatharis in Malara-desha, and the Lord rescues him.

### 24

**24.**

```
अरे कुतो गच्छसि दुःखमात्र-
साध्यं तदस्मासु कुरुष्व मैत्रीम् ।
ततस्त्वनेनैव शरीरकेण
स्वर्गं गमिष्यस्यथ नो विचारः ॥२४॥
```
*are kuto gacchasi duḥkha-mātra-
sādhyaṁ tad asmāsu kuruṣva maitrīm |
tatas tv anenaiva śarīrakeṇa
svargaṁ gamiṣyasy atha no vicāraḥ ||24||*

**Word for word:** *are* — hey; *kutaḥ* — where; *gacchasi* — are you going; *duḥkha* — suffering; *mātram* (ed. *mātra-*) — mere; *sādhyam* — to be gained; *tat* — therefore; *asmāsu* — with us; *kuruṣva* — make; *maitrīm* — friendship; *tataḥ* — then; *tu* — indeed; *anena eva* — in this very; *śarīrakeṇa* — little body; *svargam* — to heaven; *gamiṣyasi* — you will go; *atha* — and; *no* — no; *vicāraḥ* — doubt.

**Translation.** "Hey, where are you going? [There] only suffering is to be gained. So make friends with us, and then you will go to heaven in this very little body of yours — there is no doubt about it.

**Note.** In the edition *duḥkha-mātra-sādhyam* agrees with nothing; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *duḥkha-mātraṁ sādhyam*, "[there] only suffering is to be gained", and so we read.

### 25

**25.**

```
अस्त्येक एवात्र स कोऽपि पन्थाः
```

**Translation.** There is a certain path here, a little way off, inaccessible to all people. So come: by that very path we shall surely bring you from here to heaven."

### 26

**26.**

```
इत्येष पाषण्डपथप्रविष्टै-
स्तैर्मोहितो दोलितचित्तवृत्तिः ।
```

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *eṣaḥ* — he; *pāṣaṇḍa* — of heresy; *patha* — upon the path; *praviṣṭaiḥ* — by those who had entered; *taiḥ* — by them; *mohitaḥ* — beguiled; *dolita* — swaying; *citta* — of mind; *vṛttiḥ* — [whose] disposition [was]; *śaithilyam* — slackness; *īśasya* — while the Lord; *pathi* — along the road; *prayāte* — went; *cakāra* — showed; *kiñcit* — somewhat; *kramataḥ* — gradually; *vimugdhaḥ* — bewildered.

**Translation.** Thus beguiled by those who had entered upon the path of heresy, his mind wavering, he grew bewildered and, as the Lord went along the road, gradually began to lag somewhat behind.

### 27

**27.**

```
प्रभुस्तदाज्ञाय दुरात्मभाजां
विचेष्टितं तस्य च लोलतां च ।
```

**Word for word:** *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *tat* — that; *ājñāya* — having perceived; *durātma* — wickedness; *bhājām* — of those possessing; *viceṣṭitam* — the machinations; *tasya* — his; *ca* — and; *lolatām* — fickleness; *ca* — and; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *eka* — the one; *sindhuḥ* — ocean; *jagat* — of the world; *eka* — the one; *bandhuḥ* — friend; *durāśayaiḥ* — with the evil-minded; *taiḥ* — with those; *akarot* — entered into; *vivādam* — a dispute.

**Translation.** Perceiving the machinations of those wicked people and his fickleness, the Lord — the one ocean of mercy, the one friend of the world — entered into a dispute with those evil-minded men.

### 28

**28.**

```
भो न्यासिनः किं मम दास एष
प्रलोभ्य बालः खलु नीयते क्व ।
नैतच्छिवं वो न च साधुचेष्टा

```
*bho nyāsinaḥ kiṁ mama dāsa eṣa
pralobhya bālaḥ khalu nīyate kva |
naitac chivaṁ vo na ca sādhu-ceṣṭā
tat tyajyatām eṣa vidūram ādhvam ||28||*

**Word for word:** *bho* — hey; *nyāsinaḥ* — O renunciants; *kim* — what [is this]; *mama* — My; *dāsaḥ* — servant; *eṣaḥ* — he; *pralobhya* — having enticed; *bālaḥ* — a boy; *khalu* — indeed; *nīyate* — is being led; *kva* — where; *na* — not; *etat* — this; *śivam* — good; *vaḥ* — for you; *na ca* — nor; *sādhu* — of the virtuous; *ceṣṭā* — the conduct; *tat* — so; *tyajyatām* — let [him] be left; *eṣaḥ* — he; *vidūram* — far away; *ādhvam* — stay.

**Translation.** "Hey, renunciants, what is this? He is My servant, a mere boy — where are you leading him, having enticed him? This bodes no good for you, nor is it the conduct of the virtuous. So leave him and keep far away!"

**Note.** *Ādhvam* is the imperative, second person plural, of *ās*, "to sit, to stay".

### 29

**29.**

```
इत्थं विवादी न चिरं कृतेन
कथं कथञ्चिद्विमुखीचकार ।
निजप्रभावेन कृपामयाब्धि-
स्तं सुप्रसन्ने हि विधौ तथा स्यात् ॥२९॥
```
*itthaṁ vivādī na ciraṁ kṛtena
kathaṁ kathañcid vimukhīcakāra |
nija-prabhāvena kṛpāmayābdhis
taṁ suprasanne hi vidhau tathā syāt ||29||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *vivādī* — disputing; *na ciram* — not long; *kṛtena* — by what was done (?); *katham kathañcit* — somehow or other; *vimukhīcakāra* — turned away; *nija* — by His own; *prabhāvena* — power; *kṛpā-maya* — of mercy; *abdhiḥ* — the ocean; *tam* — him; *su-prasanne* — when well pleased; *hi* — for; *vidhau* — destiny; *tathā* — so; *syāt* — it happens.

**Translation.** Thus, after disputing for no long time, the ocean of mercy by His own power somehow or other turned him away [from them]; for so it happens when destiny is well pleased.

**Note.** *Tam* is singular, so Krishnadasa is meant, not the heretics ⟨?⟩. The syntax of *na ciraṁ kṛtena* is unclear. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 30

**30.**

```
इत्थं विलोक्यापथवर्तिनस्तत्
कुचेष्टितं किञ्चिदसौ विहस्य ।
न किञ्चिदूचे खलु कृष्णदासं

```
*itthaṁ vilokyāpatha-vartinas tat

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *vilokya* — having seen; *apatha* — on the wrong road; *vartinaḥ* — of the one standing; *tat* — that; *kuceṣṭitam* — misconduct; *kiñcit* — slightly; *asau* — He; *vihasya* — smiling; *na* — not; *kiñcit* — anything; *ūce* — said; *khalu* — at all; *kṛṣṇadāsam* — to Krishnadasa; *setum* — the Setu; *samuddiśya* — heading for; *tataḥ* — from there; *jagāma* — went.

### 31

**31.**

```
पथि प्रभुः स्वैर्गुणनामधेयै-

प्रेमाश्रुभिर्धौतसमस्तदेह-
```

**Translation.** On the way the Lord unceasingly performed kirtana of His own qualities and names; His whole body washed by tears of love, He purified the whole forest.

### 32

**32.**

```
एवं स सेतुं प्रययौ कृपालुः
कृपापरिप्लावितसर्वदेशः ।
रामेश्वरं रामसमर्हितं तं
दृष्ट्वा ननाम स्तवमप्यकार्षीत् ॥३२॥
```
*evaṁ sa setuṁ prayayau kṛpāluḥ
kṛpā-pariplāvita-sarva-deśaḥ |
rāmeśvaraṁ rāma-samarhitaṁ taṁ
dṛṣṭvā nanāma stavam apy akārṣīt ||32||*

**Word for word:** *evam* — thus; *saḥ* — He; *setum* — to the Setu; *prayayau* — came; *kṛpāluḥ* — the Merciful One; *kṛpā* — with mercy; *pariplāvita* — flooded; *sarva* — all; *deśaḥ* — [by whom] the lands [were]; *rāmeśvaram* — Rameshvara; *rāma* — by Rama; *samarhitam* — worshipped; *tam* — that; *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *nanāma* — bowed down; *stavam* — a hymn; *api* — also; *akārṣīt* — offered.

**Translation.** Thus the Merciful One, who had flooded all the lands with mercy, came to the Setu. Seeing that Rameshvara worshipped by Rama, He bowed down and offered a hymn as well.

**Note.** Rameshvara is the Shiva-linga that, according to tradition, Rama worshipped before His campaign against Lanka.

### 33

**33.**

```
विलोक्य सेतुं रघुनाथकीर्तिं
सेतोस्ततः श्रीमयगौरचन्द्रः ।
निवर्तितुं तत्र कृपासमुद्र-
श्चकार चित्तं परमप्रभावः ॥३३॥
```
*vilokya setuṁ raghunātha-kīrtiṁ
setos tataḥ śrīmaya-gauracandraḥ |
nivartituṁ tatra kṛpā-samudraś

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *setum* — the Setu; *raghunātha* — of Raghunatha; *kīrtim* — the glory; *setoḥ* — from the Setu; *tataḥ* — then; *śrī-maya* — full of beauty; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra; *nivartitum* — to turn back; *tatra* — there; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *samudraḥ* — the ocean; *cakāra cittam* — resolved; *parama* — supreme; *prabhāvaḥ* — [whose] power [is].

**Translation.** Having seen the Setu, the glory of Raghunatha, the beautiful Gaurachandra, the ocean of mercy, whose power is supreme, then resolved there to turn back from the Setu.

### 34

**34.**

```
स तेन तेनैव पथा विलोक्य
श्रीरङ्गदेवं पुनरार्द्रचित्तः ।
गोदावरीमेत्य तथैव रामा-
नन्दस्य सन्दर्शनमेष चक्रे ॥३४॥
```
*sa tena tenaiva pathā vilokya
śrī-raṅga-devaṁ punar ārdra-cittaḥ |
godāvarīm etya tathaiva rāmā-
nandasya sandarśanam eṣa cakre ||34||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — He; *tena tena eva* — by that very same; *pathā* — road; *vilokya* — having seen; *śrī-raṅga-devam* — the Deity Sri Ranga; *punaḥ* — again; *ārdra* — melted; *cittaḥ* — [whose] heart [was]; *godāvarīm* — to the Godavari; *etya* — having come; *tathā eva* — likewise; *rāmānandasya* — of Ramananda; *sandarśanam* — a meeting; *eṣaḥ* — He; *cakre* — had.

**Translation.** [Returning] by that very same road, He saw the Deity Sri Ranga again, His heart melting; and, having come to the Godavari, He likewise had a meeting with Ramananda.

**Note.** Whether *tathaiva*, "likewise", implies that a meeting had already taken place on the way south (contrary to 12.131: *na taṁ vilokyaiva*) is not clear from the text ⟨?⟩. More probably *tathaiva* simply means "likewise [as He had seen Sri Ranga]". The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**35.**

```
उपेत्य गोदावरिकां स नाथः

जगाम तद्वेश्मनि शीतरश्मि-
```

### 36

**36.**

```
विलोक्य नाथं स तु कृष्णचित्तो
ननाम हर्षाद्भुवि संनिपत्य ।
अनन्तरे कोटिगुणप्रवृद्धा-
माह्लादलक्ष्मीमुदितां बभार ॥३६॥
```
*vilokya nāthaṁ sa tu kṛṣṇa-citto
nanāma harṣād bhuvi saṁnipatya |
anantare koṭi-guṇa-pravṛddhām
āhlāda-lakṣmīm uditāṁ babhāra ||36||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *nātham* — the Master; *saḥ tu* — he, for his part; *kṛṣṇa* — [absorbed] in Krishna; *cittaḥ* — [whose] mind [was]; *nanāma* — bowed down; *harṣāt* — out of joy; *bhuvi* — to the ground; *saṁnipatya* — having fallen; *anantare* — thereupon; *koṭi-guṇa* — ten-millionfold; *pravṛddhām* — increased; *āhlāda* — of delight; *lakṣmīm* — the wealth; *uditām* — arisen; *babhāra* — bore.

**Translation.** And he, whose mind was [absorbed] in Krishna, seeing the Master, fell to the ground out of joy and bowed down. Thereupon he bore a wealth of delight that had arisen [in him], increased ten-millionfold.

**Note.** In the edition the verse number lacks the second danda: *॥३६*.

### 37

**37.**

```
ईशस्तु तद्दर्शनमात्रतोऽसौ
द्रुतोऽभवच्चेतसि हर्षभारैः ।
अथोपरिष्टाज्जगदेककान्ति-

```

druto 'bhavac cetasi harṣa-bhāraiḥ |
athopariṣṭāj jagad-eka-kāntir

**Word for word:** *īśaḥ* — the Lord; *tu* — and; *tat* — of him; *darśana* — by the sight; *mātrataḥ* — merely; *asau* — He; *drutaḥ* — melted; *abhavat* — became; *cetasi* — in heart; *harṣa* — of joy; *bhāraiḥ* — by the weight; *atha* — then; *upariṣṭāt* — above (on a raised [seat]); *jagat* — of the world; *eka* — the sole; *kāntiḥ* — beauty; *babhrāja* — shone; *kandarpa* — of Kandarpas; *samūha* — [like] a host; *kamraḥ* — lovely.

**Translation.** And the Lord, at the mere sight of him, melted in heart under the weight of joy. Then He, the sole beauty of the world, lovely as a host of Kandarpas, shone forth on a raised [seat].

### 38

**38.**

```
उवाच किञ्चित् स्तनयित्नुधीरं
सकैतवं भोः कवितां पठेति ।
तदा तदाकर्ण्य महारसज्ञः
पपाठ वैराग्यरसाढ्यपद्यम् ॥३८॥
```
*uvāca kiñcit stanayitnu-dhīraṁ
sakaitavaṁ bhoḥ kavitāṁ paṭheti |
tadā tad ākarṇya mahā-rasajñaḥ
papāṭha vairāgya-rasāḍhya-padyam ||38||*

**Word for word:** *uvāca* — said; *kiñcit* — a little; *stanayitnu* — like thunder; *dhīram* — deep; *sa-kaitavam* — with guile; *bhoḥ* — O [friend]; *kavitām* — poetry; *paṭha* — recite; *iti* — thus; *tadā* — then; *tat* — that; *ākarṇya* — having heard; *mahā* — the great; *rasa-jñaḥ* — knower of rasa; *papāṭha* — recited; *vairāgya* — of renunciation; *rasa* — in the flavour; *āḍhya* — rich; *padyam* — a verse.

**Translation.** In a voice deep as thunder, and with guile, He said a few words: "O [friend], recite some poetry!" Hearing that, the great knower of rasa recited a verse rich in the flavour of renunciation.

**Note.** The edition clearly has *सकैतवं*, "with guile", that is, to test him. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 39

**39.**

```
वैराग्यं चेज्जनयतितरां पापमेवास्तु यस्मात्
सान्द्रं रागं जनयति न चेत् पुण्यमस्मासु भूयात् ।
वैराग्येण प्रमुदितमनोवृत्तिरभ्येति रागं
रागेण स्त्रीजठरकुहरे ताम्यति ब्राह्मणोऽपि ॥३९॥
```
*vairāgyaṁ cej janayatitarāṁ pāpam evāstu yasmāt
sāndraṁ rāgaṁ janayati na cet puṇyam asmāsu bhūyāt |
vairāgyeṇa pramudita-mano-vṛttir abhyeti rāgaṁ
rāgeṇa strī-jaṭhara-kuhare tāmyati brāhmaṇo 'pi ||39||*

**Word for word:** *vairāgyam* — renunciation; *cet* — if; *janayatitarām* — produces abundantly; *pāpam* — sin; *eva* — even; *astu* — let it be; *yasmāt* — from which; *sāndram* — dense; *rāgam* — attachment; *janayati* — produces; *na cet* — if not; *puṇyam* — merit; *asmāsu* — in us; *bhūyāt* — may there be; *vairāgyeṇa* — by renunciation; *pramudita* — gladdened; *manaḥ* — of the mind; *vṛttiḥ* — [one whose] disposition [is]; *abhyeti* — comes; *rāgam* — to attachment; *rāgeṇa* — through attachment; *strī* — of a woman; *jaṭhara* — of the womb; *kuhare* — in the hollow; *tāmyati* — languishes; *brāhmaṇaḥ* — a brahmana; *api* — even.

**Translation.** "If [a thing] abundantly produces renunciation, let it be even sin; may merit be ours [only] if dense attachment does not arise from it. Through renunciation the mind is gladdened; [but if one] comes to attachment, then through attachment even a brahmana languishes in the hollow of a woman's womb."

**Note.** Metre *mandākrāntā*. The rendering of the syntax is conjectural ⟨?⟩; the second half might also be construed "one whose mind is gladdened by renunciation comes to *rāga*", with *rāga* in the third pada understood as love [for the Lord], in contrast to the worldly *rāga* of the fourth ⟨?⟩. The sense: better a sin that leads to renunciation than a merit that breeds attachment, for attachment leads to a new birth. This is the first, "external" stage of the conversation; cf. the stages in the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 8.57–67): varnashrama, offering the fruits of action, giving up one's own dharma, devotion mixed with knowledge — each of which the Lord calls "external". The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 40

**40.**

```
इतीदमाकर्ण्य स गौरचन्द्रो
बाह्यातिबाह्यं बत बाह्यमेतत् ।
इति स्फुरद्वाग्विभवोत्थतापोद्-
गमान्तकृन्नातिमुदं प्रपेदे ॥४०॥
```
*itīdam ākarṇya sa gauracandro
bāhyāti-bāhyaṁ bata bāhyam etat |
iti sphurad-vāg-vibhavottha-tāpod-
gamānta-kṛn nāti-mudaṁ prapede ||40||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *idam* — this; *ākarṇya* — having heard; *saḥ* — He; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra; *bāhya* — external; *ati-bāhyam* — exceedingly external; *bata* — alas; *bāhyam* — external; *etat* — this [is]; *iti* — thus [saying]; *sphurat* — flashing; *vāk* — of eloquence; *vibhava* — from the wealth; *uttha* — arisen; *tāpa* — of heat; *udgama* — to the rising; *anta* — an end; *kṛt* — making; *na* — not; *ati* — great; *mudam* — joy; *prapede* — attained.

**Translation.** Hearing this, Gaurachandra [said]: "This is external — external, exceedingly external, alas, external!" And He who puts an end to the rising of the heat born of the wealth of flashing eloquence did not attain great joy.

**Note.** The compound in the third and fourth padas is unclear ⟨?⟩. Cf. the famous *eho bāhya, āge kaha āra* ("this is external; speak further"), Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 8.59–66, where the Lord repeats this after each of Ramananda's answers. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.
