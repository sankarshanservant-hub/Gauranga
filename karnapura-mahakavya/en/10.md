# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Ten — verses 1–80 (printed pp. 281–307)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:** *svāgatā* (11 syllables per pada: —⏑— ⏑⏑⏑ —⏑⏑ ——); verses 74–80 — *mālinī*.
**Total verses:** 80.

**Contents of the passage:** the continuation of Srivasa's account — the night of Radha, Krishna and the gopis in the jewelled pavilion (classical themes of the mahakavya: sunset, moonrise, adornment, the drinking party, love's play, daybreak).
- 1–3 — the gopis prepare a couch of *mālatī* flowers and perfume the pavilion with incense; the sun sets so as not to stand in their way.
- 4–9 — sunset: the sun draws in its rays; the West holds the sun like red-hot iron (an ordeal); the darkness is the smoke of the serpents' breath; the elephants and the winged mountains of the quarters.
- 10–15 — the darkness deepens; Night lifts her veil; the swan of the moon emerges from the eastern lake; the moon is a silver casket.
- 16–27 — adornment: garments, unguents, coiffures, mirrors; some refuse ornaments ("the body is the ornament of ornaments"); Krishna gazes at His own attire.
- 28–45 — moonlight and the drinking party: the circling cup, tipsy speech and movements, kisses and bites, the "relish" (*vidaṁśa*).
- 46–73 — love's play (after the manner of the *kāma-śāstra*); 65–66 — Kama makes Radha and Krishna exchange roles.
- 74–80 (*mālinī*) — fulfilment and sleep; the lamp shakes its head: "there is nothing like this anywhere"; the sun puts out the night-lamp; the friends' morning jests ("the lake", "the bee and the lotus").

**Textual cruxes:** 4, 11 (*pura-*), 19, 24 (*kuvalasya* — a syllable has dropped out), 31, 32 (*smarakeṇa*), 35, 36 (*saurabham*/*sauratam*), 42 (*khañja*), 44, 54, 64, 66 (*vikraya*/*vikrama*), 72 (*sampad-amattaḥ*), 74, 76 (*vidhurati*), 77 — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
मालतीकुसुमतल्पमनल्पं
सोपबर्हमभिसंभृतवाञ्छाः ।
मण्डनार्थमथ मन्दिरमध्यं
मण्डितं व्यरचयन्मदिराक्ष्यः ॥१॥
```
*mālatī-kusuma-talpam analpaṁ
sopabarham abhisambhṛta-vāñchāḥ |
maṇḍanārtham atha mandira-madhyaṁ
maṇḍitaṁ vyaracayan madirākṣyaḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *mālatī* — of *mālatī* (jasmine); *kusuma* — of flowers; *talpam* — a couch; *analpam* — spacious; *sa* — with; *upabarham* — a pillow; *abhisambhṛta* — filled; *vāñchāḥ* — [whose] longings; *maṇḍana* — of adornment; *artham* — for the sake; *atha* — then; *mandira* — of the pavilion; *madhyam* — the interior; *maṇḍitam* — adorned; *vyaracayan* — made; *madira* — intoxicating; *akṣyaḥ* — [maidens with] eyes.

**Translation.** Then the maidens with intoxicating eyes, full of longing, made a spacious couch of *mālatī* flowers with a pillow, and for the sake of [their] adornment made the interior of the pavilion beautifully adorned.

### 2

**2.**

```
सौरभोद्यदसितागुरुधूपै-
र्धूपितं निरवकाशविकाशैः ।
सञ्चरत्तरशशित्रसरेणु-
व्याप्तमाप्तगुरुगौरवगन्धम् ॥२॥
```
*saurabhodyad-asitāguru-dhūpair
dhūpitaṁ niravakāśa-vikāśaiḥ |
sañcarat-tara-śaśi-trasareṇu-
vyāptam āpta-guru-gaurava-gandham ||2||*

**Word for word:** *saurabha* — with fragrance; *udyat* — rising; *asita* — of black; *aguru* — aloe-wood; *dhūpaiḥ* — with incense; *dhūpitam* — perfumed; *niravakāśa* — leaving no gap; *vikāśaiḥ* — spreading; *sañcarat-tara* — darting to and fro; *śaśi* — of the moon[light]; *trasareṇu* — with motes; *vyāptam* — filled; *āpta* — having acquired; *guru* — heavy; *gaurava* — majestic; *gandham* — fragrance.

**Translation.** [They] perfumed it with incense of black aloe-wood, whose clouds rose fragrantly and spread, leaving no gap; it was filled with motes darting to and fro in the moonbeams, and acquired a heavy, majestic fragrance.

**Note.** *trasareṇu* — a mote of dust visible in a beam of light.

### 3

**3.**

```
इत्थमुत्थितवती रतिभूमौ
वीक्ष्य गोकुलवधूर्दिननाथः ।

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *utthitavatīḥ* — risen up; *rati* — of love; *bhūmau* — on the ground (place); *vīkṣya* — seeing; *gokula* — of Gokula; *vadhūḥ* — the young women; *dina* — of the day; *nāthaḥ* — the lord; *vardhatām* — let it increase; *nidhuvana* — of love's play; *unnatiḥ* — the rising; *āsām* — their; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *apāsta* — having laid aside; *paṭimā* — his strength; *astam* — to his setting; *iyāya* — went.

**Translation.** Seeing the young women of Gokula thus risen up [ready] in the place of love, the lord of the day thought, "Let their love's play rise ever higher!" — and, laying aside his might, went to his setting.

### 4

**4.**

```
सर्वतः प्रसृमरास्तपतो मे
निर्भरं य इह ते यदि हेयाः ।
कीदृशैरहह तद्भवितव्यं
कर्षतीति किरणान्नु पतङ्गः ॥४॥
```
*sarvataḥ prasṛmarās tapato me

kīdṛśair ahaha tad bhavitavyaṁ
karṣatīti kiraṇān nu pataṅgaḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *sarvataḥ* — in all directions; *prasṛmarāḥ* — spreading; *tapataḥ* — blazing; *me* — from me; *nirbharam* — abundantly; *ye* — which; *iha* — here; *te* — they; *yadi* — if; *heyāḥ* — are to be abandoned; *kīdṛśaiḥ* — of what kind; *ahaha* — alas; *tat* — then; *bhavitavyam* — [they] will have to become; *karṣati* — draws in; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *kiraṇān* — the rays; *nu* — is it not; *pataṅgaḥ* — the sun.

**Translation.** "The rays that spread abundantly in all directions from me as I blaze — if they are to be abandoned here, alas, what will become of them?" Is it not with this thought that the sun draws in its rays?

**Note.** The syntax of *kīdṛśair … bhavitavyam* is rendered according to the sense ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 5

**5.**

```
चन्द्रमाः स्वपिति तारकगेहे
कीदृशी त्वमिति वादशमाय ।
वारुणीदिगबलारुणमर्कं
लोहपिण्डमिव तप्तमधत्त ॥५॥
```
*candramāḥ svapiti tāraka-gehe
kīdṛśī tvam iti vāda-śamāya |
vāruṇī-dig-abalāruṇam arkaṁ
loha-piṇḍam iva taptam adhatta ||5||*

**Word for word:** *candramāḥ* — the moon; *svapiti* — sleeps; *tāraka* — of the stars; *gehe* — in the house; *kīdṛśī* — what sort [of wife]; *tvam* — you; *iti* — such; *vāda* — talk; *śamāya* — to silence; *vāruṇī* — of Varuna; *dik* — the quarter (the west); *abalā* — the woman; *aruṇam* — red; *arkam* — the sun; *loha* — of iron; *piṇḍam* — a ball; *iva* — like; *taptam* — red-hot; *adhatta* — took up.

**Translation.** "The moon sleeps in the house of the stars — what sort of wife are you?" To silence such talk, the woman West, the quarter of Varuna, took up the red sun like a red-hot ball of iron.

**Note.** An allusion to the ordeal (*divya*): a woman accused of infidelity proves her innocence by holding red-hot iron.

### 6

**6.**

```
द्योतितानि विरचय्य तथान्य-
द्वीपवर्तिनि दिवाकररत्ने ।
अभ्यपूरि जगदुच्चतमिस्रं
श्वासधूमपटलैर्भुजगानाम् ॥६॥
```
*dyotitāni viracayya tathānya-
dvīpa-vartini divākara-ratne |
abhyapūri jagad ucca-tamisraṁ
śvāsa-dhūma-paṭalair bhujagānām ||6||*

**Word for word:** *dyotitāni* — illumined; *viracayya* — having made; *tathā* — so; *anya* — on another; *dvīpa* — continent; *vartini* — when [it] abode; *divākara* — of the sun; *ratne* — the jewel; *abhyapūri* — was filled; *jagat* — the world; *ucca* — deeply; *tamisram* — darkened; *śvāsa* — of the breath; *dhūma* — of smoke; *paṭalaiḥ* — with clouds; *bhujagānām* — of the serpents.

**Translation.** When the jewel of the sun, having made [those lands] bright, had gone to abide on another continent, the world, plunged in deep darkness, was filled [as it were] with clouds of the smoky breath of the serpents.

**Note.** *dvīpa* — a continent of Puranic cosmology. The darkness is compared to the venomous, smoky breath of the Nagas of the nether world.

### 7

**7.**

```
दिग्गजाः किमु परस्परयुक्ताः
किं पुनर्दिगचलाश्चलपक्षाः ।
इत्थमूहितविकारविशेषं

```
*dig-gajāḥ kim u paraspara-yuktāḥ
kiṁ punar dig-acalāś cala-pakṣāḥ |
ittham ūhita-vikāra-viśeṣaṁ
dhvāntam atra na mamau jagad-aṇḍe ||7||*

**Word for word:** *dik* — of the quarters; *gajāḥ* — the elephants; *kim u* — have …?; *paraspara* — with one another; *yuktāḥ* — come together; *kim punaḥ* — or else; *dik* — of the quarters; *acalāḥ* — the mountains; *cala* — moving; *pakṣāḥ* — [with] wings; *ittham* — thus; *ūhita* — conjectured; *vikāra* — transformation; *viśeṣam* — [whose] particular; *dhvāntam* — the darkness; *atra* — here; *na* — not; *mamau* — was contained; *jagat* — of the universe; *aṇḍe* — in the egg.

**Translation.** "Have the elephants of the quarters come together with one another? Or have the mountains of the quarters set their wings in motion?" So [people] conjectured about its strange transformation; the darkness could no longer be contained here in the egg of the universe.

**Note.** According to the myth, the mountains once had wings, until Indra cut them off with his thunderbolt (*vajra*).

### 8

**8.**

```
किं तमालतरुभिर्जगदेत-
न्निर्मितं ननु किमञ्जनपुञ्जैः ।
रञ्जितं नु हरकण्ठमयूखैः

```
*kiṁ tamāla-tarubhir jagad etan

**Word for word:** *kim* — whether; *tamāla* — *tamāla*; *tarubhiḥ* — from trees; *jagat* — world; *etat* — this; *nirmitam* — fashioned; *nanu kim* — or else; *añjana* — of collyrium; *puñjaiḥ* — from heaps; *rañjitam* — dyed; *nu* — whether; *hara* — of Hara (Shiva); *kaṇṭha* — of the throat; *mayūkhaiḥ* — by the rays; *kim nu* — or; *abhūt* — has come about; *iha* — here; *dik* — of the quarters; *antara* — of the intervals; *lopaḥ* — the vanishing.

**Translation.** Has this world been fashioned from *tamāla* trees? Or from heaps of collyrium? Has it been dyed by the rays of Hara's [blue] throat? Or have the intervals between the quarters vanished here?

**Note.** Shiva's throat turned blue from the *hālāhala* poison that he drank.

**9.**

```

**Translation.** Scorched by separation from his beloved lotuses, the lord of the day plunged into the ocean — and the world was filled with darkness rising from there like dense clouds of smoke.

**Note.** A red-hot body plunged into water gives off steam and smoke.

**10.**

### 11

**11.**

```
सम्मदादिव परस्परमाशा-
योषितो मृगमदोत्करचूर्णैः ।
मन्मथोन्मथितमुग्धवधूनां

```
*sammadād iva parasparam āśā-
yoṣito mṛgamadotkara-cūrṇaiḥ |
manmathonmathita-mugdha-vadhūnāṁ
rañjayanti pura-keli-vanāntam ||11||*

**Word for word:** *sammadāt* — out of merriment; *iva* — as if; *parasparam* — one another; *āśā* — of the quarters; *yoṣitaḥ* — the women; *mṛgamada* — of musk; *utkara* — with heaps; *cūrṇaiḥ* — of powder; *manmatha* — by Manmatha; *unmathita* — agitated; *mugdha* — artless; *vadhūnām* — of the young women; *rañjayanti* — colour; *pura* — before (?); *keli* — of play; *vana* — of the grove; *antam* — the depths.

**Translation.** As though out of merriment [pelting] one another with heaps of musk powder, the women of the quarters colour [with darkness] the depths of the pleasure grove before the artless young women agitated by Manmatha.

**Note.** The darkness is compared to the dark musk powder that people throw at one another at a festival. *pura-* may be understood as *puraḥ* ("in front"); possibly "the pleasure grove of the town" ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 12

**12.**

```
आगतः किमु न वेत्यथ पत्यु-
र्वीक्षणोत्कमनसा रभसेन ।
पूर्वदिक्तटमुखात् स्मितमुग्धा-
च्छ्यामया तिमिरचेलमुदासे ॥१२॥
```
*āgataḥ kim u na vety atha patyur
vīkṣaṇotka-manasā rabhasena |
pūrva-dik-taṭa-mukhāt smita-mugdhāc
chyāmayā timira-celam udāse ||12||*

**Word for word:** *āgataḥ* — has come; *kim u* — whether; *na vā* — or not; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *atha* — then; *patyuḥ* — the husband; *vīkṣaṇa* — to see; *utka* — eager; *manasā* — [with] heart; *rabhasena* — hastily; *pūrva* — of the eastern; *dik* — quarter; *taṭa* — of the edge; *mukhāt* — from the face; *smita* — with a smile; *mugdhāt* — lovely; *śyāmayā* — by Night; *timira* — of darkness; *celam* — the veil; *udāse* — was lifted.

**Translation.** "Has he come or not?" So thinking, Night, her heart eager to see her husband, hastily lifted the veil of darkness from the face of the eastern horizon, lovely with a smile.

**Note.** Night's husband is the moon; before moonrise the east grows pale. *śyāmā* — "the dark one", an epithet of the night.

### 14

**14.**

```

**Word for word:** *aṅka* — of the mark (the moon's spot); *śaivala* — with the waterweed; *vibhūṣita* — adorned; *pṛṣṭhaḥ* — [with] back; *bibhrat* — bearing; *alpatara* — slender; *bhānu* — of rays; *mṛṇālam* — the lotus-stalk; *pūrva* — of the eastern; *dik* — quarter; *taṭa* — of the edge; *sarovara* — of the lake; *madhyāt* — from the middle; *unmamajja* — emerged; *śanakaiḥ* — slowly; *śaśi* — the moon; *haṁsaḥ* — swan.

**Note.** Swans are believed to feed on lotus stalks.

**16.**

```
वासितानि पटवासविमर्दै-
र्निर्भरं तनुसुखानि तनूनि ।
अंशुकानि दधिरे मदिराक्ष्यो
मान्मथानि किमु शुद्धयशांसि ॥१६॥
```
*vāsitāni paṭavāsa-vimardair
nirbharaṁ tanu-sukhāni tanūni |
aṁśukāni dadhire madirākṣyo
mānmathāni kim u śuddha-yaśāṁsi ||16||*

**Word for word:** *vāsitāni* — perfumed; *paṭavāsa* — of scented powder; *vimardaiḥ* — by rubbing; *nirbharam* — richly; *tanu* — to the body; *sukhāni* — pleasant; *tanūni* — fine; *aṁśukāni* — garments; *dadhire* — put on; *madira* — intoxicating; *akṣyaḥ* — [maidens with] eyes; *mānmathāni* — of Manmatha; *kim u* — or was it; *śuddha* — pure; *yaśāṁsi* — the fame.

**Translation.** The maidens with intoxicating eyes put on fine garments, pleasant to the body and richly perfumed by rubbing with scented powder — or was it the pure fame of Manmatha [that they put on]?

**Note.** In kavya fame (*yaśas*) is imagined as white; the white garments are the embodied fame of the god of love.

### 17

**17.**

```

**Translation.** The firm thighs of the fair-browed [maidens], gleaming beneath the edges of their garments and charming with a golden loveliness, far surpassed the pretensions of the flag-decked pillars of Manmatha's city.

**Note.** The thighs are golden pillars; the edges of the garments are the flags upon them.

**18.**

**19.**

**22.**

```
भूषणस्य च विभूषणमङ्गं

### 23

**23.**

```
स्पर्शनव्यवधिरेव किमन्य-

### 24

**24.**

```
लोचनद्वयरुचैव समीपं

```

prāptayā śravaṇayor atiśobhā |

nādadhe kuvala[ya]sya vataṁsam ||24||*

**Word for word:** *locana* — of eyes; *dvaya* — of the pair; *rucā* — by the lustre; *eva* — already; *samīpam* — near; *prāptayā* — which has reached; *śravaṇayoḥ* — of the ears; *atiśobhā* — exceeding beauty; *jāyate* — arises; *kim* — what [use]; *amunā* — of this; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *kayācit* — by a certain [maiden]; *na* — not; *ādadhe* — was put on; *kuvalayasya* — of a blue lotus; *vataṁsam* — the ear-ornament.

**30.**

**31.**

```

**Word for word:** *sādhu* — finest; *rītiḥ* — quality (manner); *iyam* — this; *eva* — very; *vadhūnām* — of young women; *mādhurī* — sweetness; *iti* — thus [reasoning]; *madhuraḥ* — sweet; *madhu* — of wine; *vāraḥ* — the round (circling cup); *tām* — it; *punaḥ* — moreover; *prathayati sma* — increased; *viśeṣam* — especially; *mānmathaiḥ* — born of Manmatha; *nava* — with new; *vikāra* — changes; *vibhaṅgaiḥ* — [and] graceful turns.

**Translation.** "This very sweetness is the finest quality of young women!" — [so reasoning], the sweet round of wine increased it all the more with new changes and graceful turns born of Manmatha.

### 32

**32.**

```
लोहितोत्पलदलं प्रति खेल-
च्चञ्चरीकघटयेव चिराय ।
अन्वरञ्जि नयनाञ्जनलक्ष्म्या

```
*lohitotpala-dalaṁ prati khelac-
cañcarīka-ghaṭayeva cirāya |
anvarañji nayanāñjana-lakṣmyā
subhruvāṁ priya-manaḥ smarakeṇa ⟨?⟩ ||32||*

**Word for word:** *lohita* — of a red; *utpala* — lotus; *dalam prati* — over the petal; *khelat* — playing; *cañcarīka* — of bees; *ghaṭayā* — by a swarm; *iva* — as if; *cirāya* — for long; *anvarañji* — was captivated (coloured); *nayana* — of the eyes; *añjana* — of the collyrium; *lakṣmyā* — by the beauty; *subhruvām* — of the fair-browed; *priya* — of the beloved; *manaḥ* — the heart; *smarakeṇa* ⟨?⟩ — by love.

**Translation.** The heart of their beloved was long held captive — by love ⟨?⟩ — by the beauty of the collyrium in the eyes of the fair-browed [maidens], as though by a swarm of bees playing over the petal of a red lotus.

**Note.** The eyes, reddened by wine, are the red lotus; the dark pupils and collyrium are the bees. The form *smarakeṇa* is unclear ⟨?⟩; it may perhaps refer to the maidens, "stricken by Smara". The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 33

**33.**

```
क्लान्तकान्तरमणीमुखबिम्बैः

पातुमाहितरसस्य दृशाभूत्

```

pātum āhita-rasasya dṛśābhūt

**Word for word:** *klānta* — of the languid; *kānta* — lovely; *ramaṇī* — beauties; *mukha* — of the faces; *bimbaiḥ* — by the reflections; *padma* — with lotuses; *vāsitaḥ* — perfumed; *iva* — as if; *āsavaḥ* — wine; *eṣaḥ* — this; *pātum* — to drink; *āhita* — who had conceived; *rasasya* — a relish; *dṛśā* — by the eye; *abhūt* — was; *preyasaḥ* — of the beloved; *aruṇa* — red; *rucā* — [whose] lustre; *api ca* — even; *pītaḥ* — drunk.

**Translation.** This wine, [mirroring] the faces of the lovely, languid beauties, was as if perfumed with lotuses. The beloved, who had conceived a relish for drinking it, drank it even with His eye, ruddy in its lustre.

### 34

**34.**

```
आययुः किमु परस्परयोगा-
त्सुभ्रुवोऽधरमधूनि मधूनि ।
स्वादुमिष्टमधिकं यदमादी-

```

**Word for word:** *āyayuḥ* — became; *kim u* — have …?; *paraspara* — mutual; *yogāt* — from contact; *subhruvaḥ* — of the fair-browed one; *adhara* — of the lips; *madhūni* — the honey; *madhūni* — the wines; *svādum* — sweet; *iṣṭam* — cherished; *adhikam* — the more; *yat* — for; *amādīt* — became intoxicated; *tat* — her; *mukhāt* — from the mouth; *paripiban* — drinking deeply; *hṛdaya* — of hearts; *īśaḥ* — the lord.

**Translation.** Had the wines, through mutual contact, become the honey of the fair-browed one's lips? For the lord of hearts, drinking deeply from her lips the sweet, cherished [draught], became all the more intoxicated.

### 35

**35.**

```
यद्वचः श्रवणवर्त्म न यातं
प्रेयसः सपदि सापि नवीना ।
वारुणीमदवशादवदंशं
तत्तदोष्ठमतनिष्ट निकामम् ॥३५॥
```
*yad vacaḥ śravaṇa-vartma na yātaṁ
preyasaḥ sapadi sāpi navīnā |
vāruṇī-mada-vaśād avadaṁśaṁ
tat tad oṣṭham ataniṣṭa nikāmam ||35||*

**Word for word:** *yat* — what; *vacaḥ* — [in] speech; *śravaṇa* — of hearing; *vartma* — the path; *na* — not; *yātam* — reached; *preyasaḥ* — of the beloved; *sapadi* — at once; *sā* — she; *api* — even; *navīnā* — young; *vāruṇī* — of wine; *mada* — of the intoxication; *vaśāt* — under the sway; *avadaṁśam* — a bite (a relish); *tat tat* — again and again; *oṣṭham* — the lip; *ataniṣṭa* — made; *nikāmam* — to her heart's content.

**Translation.** What had never reached the path of hearing even in speech, that the young maiden at once did, under the sway of the intoxication of wine: to her heart's content she made her beloved's lip, again and again, a morsel to bite.

**Note.** The edition has *श्रवणवर्त्मनि* ("on the path of hearing"); the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *śravaṇa-vartma na yātam* ("which has not reached the ear", that is, unheard of). We accept the reading with the negative (the metre is unchanged). *avadaṁśa* means "a bite" and also "a relish taken with drink" (cf. *vidaṁśa*, verse 38).

### 36

**36.**

```
या शिरीषकुसुमादपि मृद्वी
सौरभं सुखमुवाह सदैव ।
दुःखवन्मदवशादवसादः
कीदृगित्यपि न वेद चिरं सा ॥३६॥
```
*yā śirīṣa-kusumād api mṛdvī
saurabhaṁ [sauratam?] sukham uvāha sadaiva |
duḥkhavan mada-vaśād avasādaḥ
kīdṛg ity api na veda ciraṁ sā ||36||*

**Word for word:** *yā* — she who; *śirīṣa* — of the *śirīṣa*; *kusumāt* — than the flower; *api* — even; *mṛdvī* — more delicate; *saurabham* — fragrance (or *sauratam* — [the bliss] of love's play?); *sukham* — happiness; *uvāha* — bore; *sadā eva* — always; *duḥkhavat* — painful; *mada* — of intoxication; *vaśāt* — from the sway; *avasādaḥ* — exhaustion; *kīdṛk* — what kind; *iti* — this; *api* — even; *na* — not; *veda* — knew; *ciram* — for long; *sā* — she.

**Translation.** She who is more delicate even than a *śirīṣa* flower, and who had always known only fragrance and happiness, for long did not even know what the painful exhaustion that comes under the sway of intoxication is like.

**Note.** Perhaps *sauratam* ("[the happiness] of love's play") should be read in place of *saurabham* ⟨?⟩. The *śirīṣa* flower is the standard of delicacy. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 37

**37.**

```
वारुणीमभिगतो द्रवभावं
मन्मथः प्रविशतीव वधूषु ।
अङ्गमङ्गमभितः किल सर्व-
ग्रन्थयः शिथिलतां यदुपेयुः ॥३७॥
```
*vāruṇīm abhigato drava-bhāvaṁ
manmathaḥ praviśatīva vadhūṣu |
aṅgam aṅgam abhitaḥ kila sarva-

**Word for word:** *vāruṇīm* — [as] wine; *abhigataḥ* — having assumed; *drava* — liquid; *bhāvam* — form; *manmathaḥ* — Manmatha; *praviśati* — enters; *iva* — as if; *vadhūṣu* — into the young women; *aṅgam aṅgam abhitaḥ* — in every limb; *kila* — indeed; *sarva* — all; *granthayaḥ* — the knots (joints); *śithilatām* — slackness; *yat* — since; *upeyuḥ* — attained.

**Translation.** Manmatha, assuming the liquid form of wine, seemed himself to enter into the young women: for in every limb all the knots grew slack.

**Note.** *granthi* means both "joint" and "knot" (of a girdle or garment).

### 38

**38.**

```
दत्तमात्ममुखतो मधु भूयः
किं जिघृक्षुरभिपीड्य रदाग्रैः ।
भर्तुरोष्ठदलदंशपरापि

```
*dattam ātma-mukhato madhu bhūyaḥ
kiṁ jighṛkṣur abhipīḍya radāgraiḥ |
bhartur oṣṭha-dala-daṁśa-parāpi
preyasī racayatīva vidaṁśam ||38||*

**Word for word:** *dattam* — given; *ātma* — her own; *mukhataḥ* — from the mouth; *madhu* — wine; *bhūyaḥ* — back; *kim* — as if; *jighṛkṣuḥ* — wishing to take; *abhipīḍya* — pressing; *rada* — of the teeth; *agraiḥ* — with the tips; *bhartuḥ* — of her lord; *oṣṭha* — of the lip; *dala* — the petal; *daṁśa* — on biting; *parā* — intent; *api* — and; *preyasī* — the beloved; *racayati* — prepares; *iva* — as if; *vidaṁśam* — a relish (a bite).

**Translation.** The beloved, as though wishing to take back the wine she had given from her own mouth, pressed the petal of her lord's lip with the tips of her teeth; intent on biting it, she seemed to be preparing a relish [to go with the wine].

**Note.** A pun: *vidaṁśa* is both "a relish taken with wine" and "a bite".

**39.**

```
वारुणीमदवशादवशाङ्गी
भ्रश्यदप्यभिविवेद न वासः ।
पाणिरेव तदरुद्ध नितान्त-

**Word for word:** *vāruṇī* — of wine; *mada* — of the intoxication; *vaśāt* — from the sway; *avaśa* — powerless; *aṅgī* — [with] limbs; *bhraśyat* — slipping; *api* — even; *abhiviveda* — noticed; *na* — not; *vāsaḥ* — the garment; *pāṇiḥ* — the hand; *eva* — by itself; *tat* — it; *aruddha* — held back; *nitānta* — constant; *nyāsataḥ* — from practice; *kila* — truly; *tat eva* — that very thing; *vicitram* — the wonder.

**Translation.** One [maiden], her limbs powerless from the intoxication of wine, did not even notice her garment slipping; her hand held it back by itself, from long habit — that indeed was the wonder!

**40.**

```
एकमस्ति मनसीतरदुक्तं

**41.**

```
अर्धमर्धमिव भाषितमासा-

**Translation.** Their speech came as if in halves and halves; His movements came as if in halves and halves. Was it to steal the hearts of the fair-browed [maidens] that Manmatha created the round of wine?

### 42

**42.**

```
उज्जगाम हृदयादनुरागो
लोचने मधुमदारुणशोभे ।
सुभ्रुवः किमिह यद्भरभारा-

```
*ujjagāma hṛdayād anurāgo
locane madhu-madāruṇa-śobhe |
subhruvaḥ kim iha yad-bhara-bhārād

**Word for word:** *ujjagāma* — rose; *hṛdayāt* — from the heart; *anurāgaḥ* — love (redness); *locane* — into the eyes; *madhu* — of wine; *mada* — from the intoxication; *aruṇa* — red; *śobhe* — [with] beauty; *subhruvaḥ* — of the fair-browed one; *kim* — whether; *iha* — here; *yat* — of which; *bhara* — of the weight; *bhārāt* — from the burden; *ghūrṇayā* — with giddiness; *bhramati* — roam; *khañjam iva* — like wagtails; *etat* — they.

**Translation.** Did love rise from the heart of the fair-browed one into her eyes, beautiful with the redness of wine's intoxication? For under the burden of its weight they roam about here giddily, like wagtails.

### 43

**43.**

```
दष्टवत्यभिमते दयितोष्ठं

```

**Word for word:** *daṣṭavati* — when [she] had bitten; *abhimate* — the beloved [maiden]; *dayita* — of the dear one; *oṣṭham* — the lip; *rañjitatvam* — colour; *agaman* — took on; *daśana* — of the teeth; *antāḥ* — the tips; *svacchatām* — the purity; *avakalayya* — having perceived; *nu* — as it seems; *gacchan* — departing; *yāvakaḥ* — the lac; *sthitim* — a dwelling; *iyeṣa* — desired; *tat* — therefore; *eṣu* — in them.

**44.**

```
चुम्बति प्रियतमेऽक्षि मृगाक्ष्याः
पानपाटलितमञ्जनहीनम् ।

**45.**

```
दष्टवत्यतितरां दशनाग्रै-

**46.**

```

**Word for word:** *oṣṭha* — of the lips; *pallava* — of the shoot; *puṭam* — the cup; *dayitāyāḥ* — of the beloved; *daṣṭavati* — when [He] had bitten; *atitarām* — hard; *madhu* — of Madhu; *marde* — the Crusher; *pāṇi* — of the hand; *pallavam* — the shoot; *api* — too; *pracakampe* — trembled; *sakhyam* — friendship; *eka* — one [and the same]; *sukha* — joy; *duḥkha* — [and] sorrow; *gam* — sharing; *eva* — indeed.

**47.**

### 48

**48.**

```
गण्डयुग्ममलिकं किमु किंवा
लोचने किमधरः किमु वान्यत् ।
चुम्बनेन रमणो रमणीनां
भिन्नभिन्नरसपूर्णमबुद्ध ॥४८॥
```
*gaṇḍa-yugmam alikaṁ kim u kiṁ vā
locane kim adharaḥ kim u vānyat |

बाधिते निधुवने प्रमदानां

**Word for word:** *bādhite* — when hindered [by weariness]; *nidhuvane* — love's play; *pramadānām* — of the young women; *kāmaḥ* — Kama; *eva* — himself; *khalu* — truly; *kāma-daḥ* — a granter of desires; *eṣaḥ* — this; *vyatyayam* — an exchange [of roles]; *yat* — that; *akarot* — made; *atha* — then; *rādhā-kṛṣṇayoḥ* — of Radha and Krishna; *ati* — most; *vicitram* — wondrous; *idam tat* — this.

**Translation.** When the love's play of the young women was hindered [by weariness], Kama himself truly proved a granter of desires: that he then exchanged the roles of Radha and Krishna — this was most wondrous!

**Note.** This refers to *viparīta-rata*, love's play with the roles reversed, known from the *kāma-śāstra*.

### 72

**72.**

```
प्रेयसीचरितसाधुसुधाभि-

**Word for word:** *preyasī* — of the beloved; *carita* — of the doings; *sādhu* — with the excellent; *sudhābhiḥ* — nectar; *tṛpta* — sated; *citta* — of the heart; *madhupaḥ* — [whose] bee; *madhu* — of Madhu; *ghātī* — the Slayer; *vibhramat* — circling; *bhramara* — of a bee; *sammada* — with the rapture; *mattaḥ* — intoxicated; *sat* — excellent; *dvitīya* — second; *surate* — for love's play; *prababhūva* — became ready.

### 73

**73.**

```
ओषधिः समधुराधरसीधु-
स्तद्वचो मनुवरः कुचकुम्भौ ।
तौ मणी व्रजवधूरिह कृष्णं
किं न मोहयतु जीवयताद्वा ॥७३॥
```
*oṣadhiḥ sa-madhurādhara-sīdhus
tad-vaco manu-varaḥ kuca-kumbhau |
tau maṇī vraja-vadhūr iha kṛṣṇaṁ
kiṁ na mohayatu jīvayatād vā ||73||*

**Word for word:** *oṣadhiḥ* — the [healing] herb; *saḥ* — that; *madhura* — of the sweet; *adhara* — lips; *sīdhuḥ* — the wine; *tat* — her; *vacaḥ* — speech; *manu* — of mantras; *varaḥ* — the best; *kuca* — of the breasts; *kumbhau* — the jars; *tau* — those; *maṇī* — the two gems; *vraja* — of Vraja; *vadhūḥ* — the young woman; *iha* — here; *kṛṣṇam* — Krishna; *kim* — why; *na* — not; *mohayatu* — should enchant; *jīvayatāt* — should bring to life; *vā* — or.

**Translation.** Her [healing] herb is that wine of her sweet lips; her speech is the best of mantras; the jars of her bosom are the two gems. Why then should the young woman of Vraja not enchant Krishna here, or bring Him to life?

**Note.** The three classical means of enchantment and healing: the herb (*oṣadhi*), the mantra and the gem (*maṇi*).

### 74

**74.**

```
अशिथिलपरिरम्भैश्चुम्बनैर्दन्तपातै-
रजनि रजनिमध्ये कान्तयोर्याथ तृप्तिः ।
नवनिधुवनलक्ष्मीलक्ष्मभाजोस्तथासौ
समधितपदमङ्गे सापराधा व्यरंसीत् ॥७४॥
```
*aśithila-parirambhaiś cumbanair danta-pātair
ajani rajani-madhye kāntayor yātha tṛptiḥ |
nava-nidhuvana-lakṣmī-lakṣma-bhājos tathāsau
samadhita-padam aṅge sāparādhā vyaraṁsīt ||74||*

**Word for word:** *aśithila* — close; *parirambhaiḥ* — by embraces; *cumbanaiḥ* — by kisses; *danta* — of the teeth; *pātaiḥ* — by the nips; *ajani* — arose; *rajani* — of the night; *madhye* — in the middle; *kāntayoḥ* — in the two lovers; *yā* — which; *atha* — then; *tṛptiḥ* — satiety; *nava* — of new; *nidhuvana* — love's play; *lakṣmī* — of the splendour; *lakṣma* — the marks; *bhājoḥ* — bearing; *tathā* — so; *asau* — it; *samadhita* — firmly set; *padam* — [its] foot; *aṅge* — in the body; *sa* — with; *aparādhā* — guilt; *vyaraṁsīt* — ceased.

**Translation.** The satiety that arose in the middle of the night in the two lovers, bearing the marks of the splendour of new love's play, through close embraces, kisses and the nips of teeth — it, though it had firmly set its foot in their bodies, ceased, guilty [as it was].

**Note.** The metre of verses 74–80 is *mālinī*. The sense of the second half is unclear ⟨?⟩: either satiety, having firmly settled in their bodies, brought the play to an end and is "guilty" of this, or satiety itself, as an offender against love, withdrew. The Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *rajani rajani-madhye*.

**75.**

```

धनुरिव चिरसज्यं ज्याविहीनं विधत्ते

```
*smara-samara-samāptau vīta-bhaṅgī-bhavad-bhrūḥ

**Word for word:** *smara* — of Smara; *samara* — of the battle; *samāptau* — at the close; *vīta* — lost; *bhaṅgī* — curve; *bhavat* — having; *bhrūḥ* — [whose] brows; *kim api kim api* — a little, a little; *mandam mandam* — very slowly; *āmīlita* — half-closed; *akṣī* — [whose] eyes; *dhanuḥ* — a bow; *iva* — as it were; *cira* — long; *sajyam* — strung; *jyā* — of the string; *vihīnam* — bereft; *vidhatte* — makes; *śaram* — an arrow; *iva* — as it were; *cira* — long since; *muktam* — loosed; *tūṇa* — of the quiver; *madhye* — within; *karoti* — puts.

### 76

**76.**

```
मदनरणविरामे कान्तयोः श्रान्तिभाजो-

*madana-raṇa-virāme kāntayoḥ śrānti-bhājor

**77.**

```

**Word for word:** *ayam ayam* — here, here; *uditaḥ* — risen; *aham* — I [am]; *vartase* — do you remain; *kim nu* — why then; *idānīm* — now; *iti* — thus; *pariṇata* — ripened; *kopāt* — from anger; *lohitaḥ* — red; *tigma* — hot; *bhānuḥ* — [whose] rays [are] (the sun); *atha* — then; *rajani* — of the night; *virāme* — at the end; *prerayan* — thrusting; *jāla* — of the window lattice; *randhre* — into the opening; *karam* — the hand (ray); *iva* — as it were; *kim u* — whether; *naiśam* — the night; *nāśayāmāsa* — put out; *dīpam* — lamp.

**78.**

### 79

**79.**

```
वपुरतुलपरागैर्धूसरं नास्ति शक्ति-

```
*vapur atula-parāgair dhūsaraṁ nāsti śaktir
lavam api nija-pakṣa-kṣepaṇe ghūrṇasīva |
parikalitam idaṁ tat ko 'pi te nāsti doṣo
madhukara kamalinyā eva ko 'pi prabhāvaḥ ||79||*

**Word for word:** *vapuḥ* — the body; *atula* — with incomparable; *parāgaiḥ* — pollen; *dhūsaram* — grey; *na asti* — there is not; *śaktiḥ* — strength; *lavam api* — even a particle; *nija* — Your own; *pakṣa* — the wings; *kṣepaṇe* — for beating; *ghūrṇasi* — You reel; *iva* — as it were; *parikalitam* — understood; *idam* — this [is]; *tat* — so; *kaḥ api* — any; *te* — Your; *na asti* — there is not; *doṣaḥ* — fault; *madhukara* — O bee; *kamalinyāḥ* — of the lotus; *eva* — only; *kaḥ api* — some; *prabhāvaḥ* — power.

**Translation.** "Your body is grey with incomparable pollen; You have not a particle of strength to beat Your wings; You seem to reel… This is understood: there is no fault of Yours at all, O bee — it is only some power of the lotus."

**Note.** The friends tease Krishna (the "bee") and Radha (the "lotus").

### 80

**80.**

```
इति रहसि दिनादौ सानुतर्षं समन्ता-
न्मसृणवचनलक्ष्मीलक्ष्यहासोपहासा ।
निभृतनिभृतलीलालोलमन्योन्यमासी-
दभिसहचरि भूयः कान्तयोः कापि चेष्टा ॥८०॥
```
*iti rahasi dinādau sānutarṣaṁ samantān
masṛṇa-vacana-lakṣmī-lakṣya-hāsopahāsā |
nibhṛta-nibhṛta-līlā-lolam anyonyam āsīd
abhi-sahacari bhūyaḥ kāntayoḥ kāpi ceṣṭā ||80||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *rahasi* — in seclusion; *dina* — of the day; *ādau* — at the beginning; *sa* — with; *anutarṣam* — longing; *samantāt* — on all sides; *masṛṇa* — of soft; *vacana* — words; *lakṣmī* — by the beauty; *lakṣya* — marked; *hāsa* — [with] laughter; *upahāsā* — [and] jests; *nibhṛta-nibhṛta* — most secret; *līlā* — for play; *lolam* — eager; *anyonyam* — for each other; *āsīt* — there was; *abhi* — in the presence of; *sahacari* — the companions; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *kāntayoḥ* — of the two lovers; *kā api* — a certain [indescribable]; *ceṣṭā* — conduct.

**Translation.** Thus, in seclusion, at the beginning of the day, in the presence of their companions, there was again between the two lovers a certain indescribable conduct: full of longing, with laughter and jests on all sides marked by the beauty of soft words, eager for each other in most secret play.
