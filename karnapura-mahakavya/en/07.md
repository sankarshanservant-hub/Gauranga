# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Seven — verses 1–107 (printed pp. 203–235)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:** *pramitākṣarā* (12 syllables per pada: ⏑⏑— ⏑—⏑ ⏑⏑— ⏑⏑—); verse 98 — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita*; verses 99 and 102 — *upajāti*; verse 100 — *praharṣiṇī*; verse 101 — *vasanta-tilakā*; verses 103–106 — *śālinī*; verse 107 — *sragdharā*.
**Total verses:** 107 (plus an unnumbered quotation after verse 100).

**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–2 — at night Gaura weeps in His sleep; Shachi asks: "Why are You weeping?" — "I have seen the Sweet One."
- 3–12 — a description of Krishna as seen in the dream, from the peacock feathers to the hem of His garment; Shachi's joy.
- 13–20 — some days later, in the house of "the best of brahmanas" (cf. 6.118–123), Gaura reveals to Nityananda the six-armed form (conch, disc, club; flute, lotus, bow), then the four-armed and the two-armed; Nityananda showers Him with flowers and dances.
- 21–24 — Nityananda's dance; He is Balarama.
- 25–29 — the Lord sends the devotees with Nityananda to Kamalaksha (Advaita, "Rudra"); Advaita exults on hearing of the six-armed form.
- 30–33 — Advaita comes and roars like a lion; the Lord reveals His splendour in the shrine of Srivasa ("Narada"); worship, the distribution of gifts.
- 34–37 — liberation is insignificant for the devotees; a night of dancing, the morning bath.
- 38–40 — the beginning of the rains: the clouds cover the moon and the sun, put to shame by Gaura.
- 41–47 — pictures of the rains: the clouds weep for joy, the lotuses hide, the clouds are a parasol over the dancing Gaura, the earth grows soft and turns red from His feet.
- 48–49 — Haridasa comes; the Lord embraces him and bids that he be given a seat of honour.
- 50–52 — Haridasa sits on the ground; the Lord anoints and feeds him; Haridasa dances in the Lord's house.
- 53–56 — Advaita is sent home; Nityananda receives garments as *prasāda* and distributes them among the devotees.
- 57–62 — the evening kirtana; Nityananda whirls "like a wheel", rolls on the ground and disappears together with Srivasa; the search.
- 63–69 — Gaura appears saddened; dancing; play with the devotees' garments; Nityananda dances — the Lord bids [them] drink the water from His feet.
- 70–75 — the joy of the gods; Haridasa's dance; the Lord performs *pūjā* to Advaita and gives Him mahaprasada.
- 76–78 — a wandering mendicant with his son sees Gaura dancing and falls unconscious.
- 79–82 — **Nrisimha**: after the *śrāddha* Srivasa recites the "Thousand Names"; Gaura, club in hand, runs to him; the people flee.
- 83–85 — the Lord calms down: "Have I wronged the people?" — "This is only the greatness of Your mercy."
- 86–90 — a singer of songs about Shiva; Gaura dances on his shoulders like Shiva; Srivasa and Mukunda recite hymns to Shiva.
- 91–93 — a brahmana woman takes the dust of His feet — Gaura throws Himself into the Ganga; Nityananda pulls Him out.
- 94–97 — the lament of the devotees; the night at a brahmana's house; in the morning, the crossing of the Ganga; He is brought home.
- 98–102 — "If I leave My mother…"; Murari: "No one will blame [You]"; Murari recites the verse of Sudama (Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.81.16); the Lord reveals His majesty: "This body is *sac-cid-ānanda*."
- 103–106 — Gaura is bathed again with the waters of the Ganga; He Himself sheds tears; kirtana.
- 107 — Gaura with the devotees cleanses the world of false knowledge and sweeps the temples.

**Textual cruxes:** 3 (*prasṛta*), 4 (*sura-sūna*), 11 and 18 (*kamala* as an epithet), 13 (the subject of the second half), 22 (*jana-*), 41 (*vāsitavatīḥ*), 56 (*nijagāda*), 60 (*karāmbuja*), 76 (*anusaṅgatayā*), 79 (metre), 81 (*valad-*), 93 (*payaso*, the verb), 97 (*vinayaiḥ*) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
अपरेद्युरेष निशि सुप्तिमितो
विरुरोद निर्भरमतिप्रकटम् ।
तनयं तथाविधमवेक्ष्य शची
सभयं जगाद जगदेकपतिम् ॥१॥
```
*aparedyur eṣa niśi suptim ito
viruroda nirbharam ati-prakaṭam |
tanayaṁ tathā-vidham avekṣya śacī
sa-bhayaṁ jagāda jagad-eka-patim ||1||*

**Word for word:** *aparedyuḥ* — on another day; *eṣaḥ* — He; *niśi* — at night; *suptim* — sleep; *itaḥ* — having entered; *viruroda* — wept; *nirbharam* — unrestrainedly; *ati* — very; *prakaṭam* — loudly; *tanayam* — the son; *tathā* — such; *vidham* — [being] of a kind; *avekṣya* — having seen; *śacī* — Shachi; *sa* — with; *bhayam* — fear; *jagāda* — said; *jagat* — of the world; *eka* — to the sole; *patim* — Lord.

**Translation.** On another day, at night, as He lay asleep, He wept unrestrainedly and very loudly. Seeing her son in such a state, Shachi in fear spoke to the sole Lord of the world:

**Note.** Cf. the beginning of Sarga Five (verse 2): the same motif of weeping at night, now with the cause explained.

**2.**

```

स तथेति मातरमुवाच ततः ।
```

**Translation.** "Why then are You weeping, dear child?" she said. And He then answered His mother thus: "O Mother, overwhelmed by sleep, I have seen Him — the Sweet One, the Sweet One!"

### 3

**3.**

```
नवनीलनीरदसमूहरुचि-
र्नवनीलकण्ठदलमण्डनकः ।
घनमेदुरातिकुटिलप्रसृत-
कचसञ्चयप्रसृतभालतलः ॥३॥
```
*nava-nīla-nīrada-samūha-rucir
nava-nīlakaṇṭha-dala-maṇḍanakaḥ |
ghana-medurāti-kuṭila-prasṛta-
kaca-sañcaya-prasṛta-bhāla-talaḥ ||3||*

**Word for word:** *nava* — of fresh; *nīla* — dark-blue; *nīrada* — clouds; *samūha* — of a bank; *ruciḥ* — [with] the lustre; *nava* — with fresh; *nīlakaṇṭha* — peacock; *dala* — feathers; *maṇḍanakaḥ* — adorned; *ghana* — thick; *medura* — lush; *ati* — very; *kuṭila* — curling; *prasṛta* — spreading; *kaca* — of locks; *sañcaya* — [with] a mass; *prasṛta* — overspread; *bhāla* — of the forehead; *talaḥ* — [with] the expanse.

**Translation.** "[He had] the lustre of a bank of fresh dark-blue clouds and was adorned with fresh peacock feathers; a mass of thick, lush, very curling, spreading locks overspread His forehead;

**Note.** Verses 3–10 are a portrait of Krishna "from head to foot" (*keśādi-pādānta*), all one sentence in the nominative ("He was…"). *Nīlakaṇṭha* ("blue-throated") is the peacock. The edition has *प्रसरत-*; the metre requires *prasṛta*.

### 4

**4.**

```
सुरसूनसञ्चयवतंसरस-
प्रमदभ्रमद्भ्रमरविभ्रमभृत् ।
अलसोल्लसन्मधुरचिल्लिलतः
श्रवणान्तसञ्चरितनेत्रयुगः ॥४॥
```
*sura-sūna-sañcaya-vataṁsa-rasa-
pramada-bhramad-bhramara-vibhrama-bhṛt |
alasollasan-madhura-cilli-lataḥ
śravaṇānta-sañcarita-netra-yugaḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *sura* — celestial; *sūna* — of flowers; *sañcaya* — of a cluster; *vataṁsa* — of the crest-garland; *rasa* — by the nectar; *pramada* — intoxicated; *bhramat* — circling; *bhramara* — of bees; *vibhrama* — the charm; *bhṛt* — bearing; *alasa* — languidly; *ullasat* — playing; *madhura* — sweet; *cilli* — of eyebrows; *lataḥ* — [with] the creepers; *śravaṇa* — of the ears; *anta* — to the edges; *sañcarita* — extending; *netra* — of eyes; *yugaḥ* — [with] a pair.

**Translation.** He bore the charm of the bees circling about, intoxicated by the nectar of His crest-garland of clustered celestial flowers; the creepers of His sweet eyebrows played languidly, and His pair of eyes extended to His very ears;

**Note.** The edition has *सुरसुन*; the metre requires *sura-sūna* ("celestial flowers"). *Cilli* — eyebrow (late Sanskrit).

### 5

**5.**

```
अरुणारुणाक्षिकमलः प्रमदो
घनसान्द्रदृष्टिलहरीमधुरः ।
सदपाङ्गभङ्गिमजगन्मदनः
स्मितगण्डमण्डललसन्मुकुरः ॥५॥
```
*aruṇāruṇākṣi-kamalaḥ pramado
ghana-sāndra-dṛṣṭi-laharī-madhuraḥ |
sad-apāṅga-bhaṅgima-jagan-madanaḥ
smita-gaṇḍa-maṇḍala-lasan-mukuraḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *aruṇa* — like the dawn; *aruṇa* — red; *akṣi* — of eyes; *kamalaḥ* — [with] the lotuses; *pramadaḥ* — joyful; *ghana* — of dense; *sāndra* — deep; *dṛṣṭi* — of glances; *laharī* — with the waves; *madhuraḥ* — sweet; *sat* — of lovely; *apāṅga* — sidelong glances; *bhaṅgima* — by the curves; *jagat* — of the world; *madanaḥ* — the intoxicator (Madana); *smita* — with a smile; *gaṇḍa* — of cheeks; *maṇḍala* — [with] the circles; *lasat* — gleaming; *mukuraḥ* — [like] mirrors.

**Translation.** His lotus eyes were red as the dawn; joyful, He was sweet with dense, deep waves of glances; by the curves of His lovely sidelong glances He was Madana to the whole world; the circles of His cheeks, touched by a smile, gleamed like mirrors;

**Note.** *Madana* is Kamadeva, the god of love; *jagan-madana*, "intoxicating the world", i.e. Madana-mohana. The edition has *प्रमदो*; possibly *pramada* is part of the next word ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**6.**

```
तपनीयकुण्डलविलासलस-
च्छ्रवणद्वयीहृतजगद्धृदयः ।
नवविद्रुमद्रुमकडम्बलस-
```

**7.**

```
दशनप्रसूनरुचिमञ्जरिका-
धरपल्लवारुणिमकम्रमुखः ।
मधुमाधुरीमधुरसच्चिबुकः
शुचिकम्बुकण्ठतटहारधरः ॥७॥
```
*daśana-prasūna-ruci-mañjarikā-

madhu-mādhurī-madhura-sac-cibukaḥ

**Word for word:** *daśana* — of teeth; *prasūna* — of flowers; *ruci* — of lustre; *mañjarikā* — with the cluster of blossoms; *adhara* — of lips; *pallava* — of shoots; *aruṇima* — with the redness; *kamra* — lovely; *mukhaḥ* — [with] a face; *madhu* — of honey; *mādhurī* — with the sweetness; *madhura* — sweet; *sat* — lovely; *cibukaḥ* — [with] a chin; *śuci* — pure; *kambu* — [like] a conch; *kaṇṭha* — of the neck; *taṭa* — on the slope; *hāra* — a necklace; *dharaḥ* — wearing.

**Translation.** His face was lovely with the blossom-cluster of the lustre of His flower-like teeth and with the redness of His shoot-like lips; His lovely chin was sweet with the sweetness of honey; on the slope of His neck, pure as a conch, He wore a necklace;

**Note.** A neck "like a conch" — with three folds, a mark of beauty.

**8.**

```
नवमौक्तिकप्रकरहारलता-
विलसद्गलो विलसदंसतटः ।
तपनीयसूत्रपरिकॢप्तलस-
द्वरकौस्तुभस्फुरदुरःसरणिः ॥८॥
```
*nava-mauktika-prakara-hāra-latā-
vilasad-galo vilasad-aṁsa-taṭaḥ |
tapanīya-sūtra-parikḷpta-lasad-
vara-kaustubha-sphurad-uraḥ-saraṇiḥ ||8||*

**Word for word:** *nava* — of fresh; *mauktika* — pearls; *prakara* — of a multitude; *hāra* — of a necklace; *latā* — with the creeper; *vilasat* — gleaming; *galaḥ* — [with] a neck; *vilasat* — gleaming; *aṁsa* — of the shoulders; *taṭaḥ* — [with] the slopes; *tapanīya* — golden; *sūtra* — on a thread; *parikḷpta* — fastened; *lasat* — shining; *vara* — by the excellent; *kaustubha* — Kaustubha; *sphurat* — glittering; *uraḥ* — of the chest; *saraṇiḥ* — [with] the expanse.

**Translation.** His neck gleamed with the creeper of a necklace of many fresh pearls, the slopes of His shoulders gleamed; the expanse of His chest glittered with the shining, excellent Kaustubha fastened on a golden thread;

**Note.** The *Kaustubha* is the jewel on the chest of Vishnu/Krishna, obtained at the churning of the ocean. The edition has *परिकलृप्त*; we read *parikḷpta*.

**9.**

```
अमरप्रसूननवमाल्यकला-
ललितोरुपीनसदुरो मधुरः ।
वरजानुलम्बिमृदुपीनभुजा-

```
*amara-prasūna-nava-mālya-kalā-
lalitoru-pīna-sad-uro madhuraḥ |
vara-jānu-lambi-mṛdu-pīna-bhujā-
vilasad-varāṅgada-su-kaṅkaṇakaḥ ||9||*

**Word for word:** *amara* — celestial; *prasūna* — of flowers; *nava* — of a fresh; *mālya* — garland; *kalā* — by the elegance; *lalita* — charming; *uru* — broad; *pīna* — full; *sat* — lovely; *uraḥ* — [with] a chest; *madhuraḥ* — sweet; *vara* — fine; *jānu* — the knees; *lambi* — reaching; *mṛdu* — tender; *pīna* — mighty; *bhujā* — on the arms; *vilasat* — gleaming; *vara* — with excellent; *aṅgada* — armlets; *su* — with lovely; *kaṅkaṇakaḥ* — wrist-bracelets.

**Translation.** sweet, with a broad, full, lovely chest made charming by the elegance of a fresh garland of celestial flowers; on His tender, mighty arms, which reached to His fine knees, gleamed excellent armlets and lovely bracelets;

### 10

**10.**

```
करमेयमध्यमविलासलस-
द्वरबन्धुरोदरकटीरतटः ।
अभिनाभिवीततपनीयधटी-
लसदञ्चलाञ्चितपदाग्रतटः ॥१०॥
```
*kara-meya-madhyama-vilāsa-lasad-
vara-bandhurodara-kaṭīra-taṭaḥ |
abhi-nābhi-vīta-tapanīya-dhaṭī-
lasad-añcalāñcita-padāgra-taṭaḥ ||10||*

**Word for word:** *kara* — by the palm; *meya* — measurable; *madhyama* — [with] a waist; *vilāsa* — with grace; *lasat* — shining; *vara* — lovely; *bandhura* — undulating; *udara* — of the belly; *kaṭīra* — and of the hips; *taṭaḥ* — [with] the curves; *abhi* — up to; *nābhi* — the navel; *vīta* — wrapped; *tapanīya* — of the golden; *dhaṭī* — loincloth; *lasat* — shining; *añcala* — by the hem; *añcita* — graced; *pada* — of the feet; *agra* — of the tips; *taṭaḥ* — [with] the region.

**Translation.** His waist could be spanned by a palm; the lovely, undulating curves of His belly and hips shone with grace; He was wrapped up to the navel in a golden cloth, whose shining hem graced the tips of His feet;

**Note.** An "undulating" belly — with three folds (*trivali*), a canon of beauty. The golden cloth is the *pītāmbara*, Krishna's yellow garment.

### 11

**11.**

```
स्मितदीधितिस्नपितदिग्वलयः
करुणाकटाक्षमधुरः कमलः ।
इति तं विलोक्य सहसाविरभूत्
सुखसञ्चयैर्मम सुविह्वलता ॥११॥
```
*smita-dīdhiti-snapita-dig-valayaḥ
karuṇā-kaṭākṣa-madhuraḥ kamalaḥ |
iti taṁ vilokya sahasāvirabhūt
sukha-sañcayair mama su-vihvalatā ||11||*

**Word for word:** *smita* — of the smile; *dīdhiti* — with the rays; *snapita* — bathing; *dik* — of the directions; *valayaḥ* — the circle; *karuṇā* — compassionate; *kaṭākṣa* — with sidelong glances; *madhuraḥ* — sweet; *kamalaḥ* — [tender as a] lotus; *iti* — such; *tam* — Him; *vilokya* — having seen; *sahasā* — suddenly; *āvirabhūt* — arose; *sukha* — of happiness; *sañcayaiḥ* — from the abundance; *mama* — in Me; *su* — complete; *vihvalatā* — bewilderment.

**Translation.** with the rays of His smile He bathed the whole circle of the directions; sweet with compassionate sidelong glances, [tender as a] lotus. Seeing Him so, I was suddenly overcome by complete bewilderment from the abundance of happiness.

**Note.** *Kamalaḥ* (masculine, "lotus") as an attribute is unusual; perhaps *komalaḥ* ("tender") should be read. As an adjective *kamala* also means "rose-coloured, lotus-hued" (cf. verse 18). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 13

**13.**

```
प्रभुरप्यसौ नयनवारिझरै-
र्जलधिद्वयं किमदधादुरसि ।
कियता दिनेन समुपेत्य बभौ
द्विजपुङ्गवालयवरं तदिदम् ॥१३॥
```
*prabhur apy asau nayana-vāri-jharair
jaladhi-dvayaṁ kim adadhād urasi |
kiyatā dinena samupetya babhau
dvija-puṅgavālaya-varaṁ tad idam ||13||*

**Word for word:** *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *api* — and; *asau* — He; *nayana* — of the eyes; *vāri* — of water; *jharaiḥ* — with the torrents; *jaladhi* — of oceans; *dvayam* — a pair; *kim* — as if; *adadhāt* — bore; *urasi* — on the chest; *kiyatā* — after some; *dinena* — days; *samupetya* — having come to; *babhau* — shone; *dvija* — of brahmanas; *puṅgava* — of the best; *ālaya* — the house; *varam* — excellent; *tat idam* — that very.

**Translation.** And the Lord, with the torrents of tears from His eyes, seemed to bear two oceans on His chest. After some days He came to that excellent house of the best of brahmanas and shone [there].

**Note.** No subject is named in the second half; we take it, as the grammar suggests, to be the Lord (*prabhuḥ* of the first half), with *tad idam* (neuter accusative) going with the house. One may also understand Nityananda, who in verse 14 is "there" (*tatra*); Nityananda's name is not in the verse. "The best of brahmanas" is not named either; in 6.118 Srivasa is called "the foremost of brahmanas", and the revelation of 6.119–122 takes place in his house. Verses 14–22 evidently repeat and expand the episode at the end of Sarga Six (6.120–123): the revelation of the six-armed form to Nityananda. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

*karuṇālayasya caraṇābja-rajaḥ
parigṛhya tat-pada-yugānugatāḥ |
aparedyur apy ayam amanda-guṇaḥ
kamalākṣa-deva udiyāya tataḥ ||29||*

**Word for word:** *karuṇā* — of mercy; *ālayasya* — of the abode; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotus; *rajaḥ* — the dust; *parigṛhya* — having taken; *tat* — His; *pada* — of feet; *yuga* — the pair; *anugatāḥ* — following; *aparedyuḥ* — on the next day; *api* — and; *ayam* — this; *amanda* — not slight; *guṇaḥ* — [whose] virtues; *kamalākṣa-devaḥ* — Kamalaksha Deva; *udiyāya* — came; *tataḥ* — then.

**Translation.** Taking the dust of the lotus feet of that abode of mercy, they followed His pair of feet. And on the next day this Kamalaksha Deva, whose virtues are not slight, came [to the Lord].

**Note.** The "abode of mercy" here is evidently Kamalaksha himself (in verse 28 the same epithet refers to Gaura). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 30

**30.**

```
अवलोक्य गौरशशिनं च तदा
मदसिंहनादरुचिरः समभूत् ।
समुपागतेऽत्र महनीयगुणे
```

**Word for word:** *avalokya* — having seen; *gaura* — Gaura; *śaśinam* — the moon; *ca* — and; *tadā* — then; *mada* — of an intoxicated; *siṁha* — lion; *nāda* — with the roar; *ruciraḥ* — splendid; *samabhūt* — became; *samupāgate* — when [He] had arrived; *atra* — here; *mahanīya* — worthy of honour; *guṇe* — [with] qualities; *giriśa* — Girisha; *prabhau* — the master; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *asau* — He; *jagatām* — of the worlds.

### 31

**31.**

```
सहसाविरातनुत भूरिदयः
प्रकटप्रकाशमथ गौडशशी ।
```

**Word for word:** *sahasā* — at once; *āviḥ ātanuta* — revealed; *bhūri* — immensely; *dayaḥ* — kind; *prakaṭa* — manifest; *prakāśam* — splendour; *atha* — then; *gauḍa* — of Gauda; *śaśī* — the moon; *bhuvi* — on earth; *nāradaḥ* — Narada; *ayam* — this; *iti* — as; *yaḥ* — who; *prathitaḥ* — is renowned; *bhavaneṣu* — among the dwellings; *tasya* — of him; *saḥ* — He; *tu* — and; *deva-gṛhe* — in the shrine.

**Note.** Srivasa is an incarnation of Narada (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 10.8; *Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika*). Gauda is Bengal.

### 32

**32.**

```
प्रकटप्रकाशमवदर्श्य तदा
सुखमस्य भूरिकरुणोऽतनुत ।
अथ तं तथाविधमवेक्ष्य भृशं

परिपूज्य पुष्पफलपूगधनै-

```
*prakaṭa-prakāśam avadarśya tadā
sukham asya bhūri-karuṇo 'tanuta |

**Word for word:** *prakaṭa* — manifest; *prakāśam* — splendour; *avadarśya* — having shown; *tadā* — then; *sukham* — happiness; *asya* — on him; *bhūri* — immensely; *karuṇaḥ* — merciful; *atanuta* — bestowed; *atha* — and; *tam* — Him; *tathā* — such; *vidham* — in form; *avekṣya* — having seen; *bhṛśam* — intensely; *nanṛtuḥ* — danced; *jaguḥ* — sang; *mumudire* — rejoiced; *bahu* — much; *te* — they; *paripūjya* — having worshipped; *puṣpa* — with flowers; *phala* — fruits; *pūga* — betel nuts; *dhanaiḥ* — and riches; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *daṇḍavat* — like rods; *bahu* — with great; *sukhaiḥ* — happiness; *anaman* — bowed down.

**Translation.** Having then shown him the manifest splendour, the immensely merciful one bestowed happiness on him. And they, seeing Him in such a form, danced intensely, sang and rejoiced much; having worshipped Him with flowers, fruits, betel nuts and riches, with great happiness they bowed down on the ground like rods.

**Note.** The verse has six padas (so in the edition).

**33.**

```
परितस्तदर्च्चनमसौ कृपया

वसनं प्रसूनमपि कारुणिकः

```
*paritas tad-arcanam asau kṛpayā

vasanaṁ prasūnam api kāruṇikaḥ

**Word for word:** *paritaḥ* — from all sides; *tat* — their; *arcanam* — offerings; *asau* — He; *kṛpayā* — mercifully; *parigṛhya* — having accepted; *tebhyaḥ* — to them; *idam eva* — these very [things]; *dadau* — gave; *vasanam* — cloths; *prasūnam* — flowers; *api* — and; *kāruṇikaḥ* — the compassionate one; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *ālayasya* — of the abode; *karuṇā* — the mercy; *mahatī* — [is] great.

**Translation.** Having mercifully accepted their offerings from all sides, the compassionate [Lord] gave these very things — cloths and flowers — to them. Great is the mercy of the abode of mercy!

### 34

**34.**

```
जगतीत्रयस्य जनताभिरति-
प्रतिमृग्यमत्यसुलभं बहुधा ।
अपवर्गमप्यतिलघुं सहसा
सुखतन्मया विदधुरित्थममी ॥३४॥
```
*jagatī-trayasya janatābhir ati-
pratimṛgyam aty-asulabhaṁ bahudhā |
apavargam apy ati-laghuṁ sahasā
sukha-tanmayā vidadhur ittham amī ||34||*

**Word for word:** *jagatī* — of worlds; *trayasya* — of the three; *janatābhiḥ* — by the peoples; *ati* — eagerly; *pratimṛgyam* — sought; *ati* — very; *asulabham* — hard to attain; *bahudhā* — in many ways; *apavargam* — liberation; *api* — even; *ati* — very; *laghum* — trifling; *sahasā* — at once; *sukha* — in happiness; *tanmayāḥ* — absorbed; *vidadhuḥ* — deemed; *ittham* — thus; *amī* — they.

**Translation.** Thus they, absorbed in happiness, at once deemed very trifling even liberation, which the peoples of the three worlds eagerly seek in many ways and which is so hard to attain.

**Note.** *Apavarga* is liberation (moksha); cf. Srimad-Bhagavatam 3.29.13: pure devotees do not accept even liberation.

### 35

**35.**

```
न दिनं न रात्रिमथ तेऽथ विदु-
र्न सुखं न दुःखमपि ते परमाः ।
किमनीप्सितापि सतनूनभज-
ज्जडतामिषेण भुवि मुक्तिरमून् ॥३५॥
```
*na dinaṁ na rātrim atha te 'tha vidur
na sukhaṁ na duḥkham api te paramāḥ |
kim anīpsitāpi sa-tanūn abhajaj
jaḍatā-miṣeṇa bhuvi muktir amūn ||35||*

**Word for word:** *na* — neither; *dinam* — day; *na* — nor; *rātrim* — night; *atha* — and; *te* — they; *atha* — and; *viduḥ* — knew; *na* — neither; *sukham* — happiness; *na* — nor; *duḥkham* — sorrow; *api* — even; *te* — they; *paramāḥ* — the supreme; *kim* — did not?; *anīpsitā* — unwanted; *api* — though; *sa* — with; *tanūn* — bodies; *abhajat* — serve; *jaḍatā* — of stupor; *miṣeṇa* — in the guise; *bhuvi* — on earth; *muktiḥ* — liberation; *amūn* — them.

**Translation.** Those supreme [souls] knew neither day nor night, nor even happiness or sorrow. Did not liberation itself, though unwanted, serve them on earth, while they were still in their bodies, in the guise of their stupor?

**Note.** Stupor (*jaḍatā*, *stambha*) is one of the ecstatic symptoms; the poet compares it with the insensibility of one liberated in this life (*jīvan-mukta*).

**36.**


### 37

**37.**

```
द्युनदीजलं समवगाह्य ततः
प्रथमं दिनस्य मुदितास्त इमे ।
असुधारणैकपरमौषधिव-
```

prathamaṁ dinasya muditās ta ime |

**Word for word:** *dyu* — of the heavenly; *nadī* — river; *jalam* — in the water; *samavagāhya* — having bathed; *tataḥ* — then; *prathamam* — at the beginning; *dinasya* — of the day; *muditāḥ* — joyful; *te ime* — they; *asu* — life; *dhāraṇa* — for sustaining; *eka* — the one; *parama* — supreme; *auṣadhi* — a medicine; *vat* — like; *caraṇam* — the feet; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *mṛdutaram* — most tender; *dadṛśuḥ* — beheld.

**Translation.** Then, at the beginning of the day, having bathed in the water of the heavenly river, they joyfully beheld the most tender feet of the Lord, the one supreme medicine for sustaining their life.

**Note.** The heavenly river is the Ganga.

### 38

**38.**

```
अथ तस्य नर्त्तनविलासमिमं
परिलोकितुं सरभसं मुदितः ।
मुदिरः शनैर्नभसि किं विदधौ
सहसोद्गमं मधुरमेदुररुक् ॥३८॥
```
*atha tasya nartana-vilāsam imaṁ

mudiraḥ śanair nabhasi kiṁ vidadhau

**Word for word:** *atha* — and then; *tasya* — His; *nartana* — of dancing; *vilāsam* — play; *imam* — this; *parilokitum* — to behold; *sa* — with; *rabhasam* — eagerness; *muditaḥ* — joyful; *mudiraḥ* — the cloud; *śanaiḥ* — slowly; *nabhasi* — in the sky; *kim* — did not?; *vidadhau* — make; *sahasā* — suddenly; *udgamam* — a rising; *madhura* — sweet; *medura* — dense; *ruk* — [full of] lustre.

**Translation.** And then, to behold this play of His dancing, did not the joyful cloud, full of a sweet, dense lustre, eagerly rise in the sky — suddenly, yet slowly?

**Note.** A play on *mudita* ("joyful") and *mudira* ("cloud"). Verses 38–40 are *utprekṣā* (poetic fancy): nature responds to Gaura. The rainy season begins.

### 39

**39.**

```
भुवि भाति गौरहिमरश्मिरयं
मधुरद्युतिः किमधुना भवता ।
इति भूरिशो नभसि चन्द्रमसं

```
*bhuvi bhāti gaura-hima-raśmir ayaṁ

iti bhūriśo nabhasi candramasaṁ
jaladodgamaḥ sapadi kiṁ pidadhe ||39||*

**Word for word:** *bhuvi* — on earth; *bhāti* — shines; *gaura* — Gaura; *hima-raśmiḥ* — the cool-rayed (the moon); *ayam* — this; *madhura* — sweet; *dyutiḥ* — [whose] radiance; *kim* — what [use]; *adhunā* — now; *bhavatā* — of you; *iti* — [thinking] thus; *bhūriśaḥ* — wholly; *nabhasi* — in the sky; *candramasam* — the moon; *jalada* — of clouds; *udgamaḥ* — the rising; *sapadi* — at once; *kim* — did not?; *pidadhe* — cover.

**Translation.** "This moon, Gaura, sweet in radiance, shines on earth — what use is there of you now?" Was it [thinking] thus that the rising clouds at once wholly covered the moon in the sky?

**40.**

```
इह गौरचन्द्रमहसा महता
```

### 41

**41.**

```
विकसत्कदम्बनवगन्धरसै-
रतिचारुवासितवतीः ककुभः ।
```

**Word for word:** *vikasat* — of blossoming; *kadamba* — kadambas; *nava* — with the fresh; *gandha* — fragrance; *rasaiḥ* — and sap; *ati* — very; *cāru* — charmingly; *vāsitavatīḥ* — perfumed; *kakubhaḥ* — the quarters; *parirabhya* — having embraced; *harṣa* — from joy; *bhavam* — born; *aśru* — of tears; *bharam* — a flood; *jalada* — of clouds; *udgamaḥ* — the rising; *kṣaṇa* — of the moment; *vaśāt* — by force; *amucat* — shed.

**42.**


### 43

**43.**

```
हरिणीदृशां कुटिलमेदुरस-
त्कचपाशभासुररुचो जलदाः ।
चपलाचयैर्मधुरतां दधिरे
```

capalā-cayair madhuratāṁ dadhire
sphuṭa-ketakāṅkita-tamāla-taroḥ ||43||*

**Word for word:** *hariṇī* — of a doe; *dṛśām* — of [women with] eyes; *kuṭila* — curling; *medura* — thick; *sat* — lovely; *kaca* — of hair; *pāśa* — the tresses; *bhāsura* — gleaming; *rucaḥ* — [with] lustre [like]; *jaladāḥ* — the clouds; *capalā* — of lightning; *cayaiḥ* — with swarms; *madhuratām* — the charm; *dadhire* — took on; *sphuṭa* — blossoming; *ketaka* — with ketakas; *aṅkita* — marked; *tamāla* — tamala; *taroḥ* — of a tree.

**Translation.** The clouds, with a gleaming lustre like the curling, thick, lovely tresses of doe-eyed women, took on with their swarms of lightning the charm of a tamala tree marked with blossoming ketakas.

**44.**

```
लघु नृत्यतोऽस्य चरणाम्बुरुहं
```

**Word for word:** *laghu* — lightly; *nṛtyataḥ* — of [Him] dancing; *asya* — His; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *amburuham* — the lotus; *kṣiti* — with the earth; *saṅgamaḥ* — contact; *vyathayate* — pains; *bahuśaḥ* — greatly; *iti* — thus; *cintayā* — with the thought; *jala-mucaḥ* — the clouds; *salilaiḥ* — with waters; *mṛdulām* — soft; *sadā eva* — always; *dharaṇīm* — the earth; *vidadhuḥ* — made.

### 45

**45.**

```
स यदा सुखेन तनुते नटनं
विलसत्पदाम्बुजविलासरसः ।
जलदास्तदैव करुणैकनिधे-
र्ललितातपत्रसुषमां दधति ॥४५॥
```
*sa yadā sukhena tanute naṭanaṁ
vilasat-padāmbuja-vilāsa-rasaḥ |
jaladās tadaiva karuṇaika-nidher
lalitātapatra-suṣamāṁ dadhati ||45||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — He; *yadā* — when; *sukhena* — joyfully; *tanute* — performs; *naṭanam* — the dance; *vilasat* — shining; *pada* — of the feet; *ambuja* — of the lotuses; *vilāsa* — of the play; *rasaḥ* — [savouring] the delight; *jaladāḥ* — the clouds; *tadā eva* — at that very moment; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *eka* — of the one; *nidheḥ* — of the treasury; *lalita* — of a charming; *ātapatra* — parasol; *suṣamām* — the beauty; *dadhati* — take on.

**Translation.** When He, savouring the delight of the play of His shining lotus feet, joyfully performs His dance, at that very moment the clouds take on the beauty of a charming parasol over the one treasury of mercy.

**Note.** The parasol (*ātapatra*) is an emblem of royalty.

### 46

**46.**

```
नवविद्रुमद्रुमकदम्बरुचा
पदपल्लवस्य मधुरच्छटया ।
धरणीं चकार करुणाब्धिरसा-

```
*nava-vidruma-druma-kadamba-rucā
pada-pallavasya madhura-cchaṭayā |
dharaṇīṁ cakāra karuṇābdhir asāv
aruṇāyitām aruṇa-pāṇi-talaḥ ||46||*

**Word for word:** *nava* — of fresh; *vidruma* — coral; *druma* — trees; *kadamba* — of a grove; *rucā* — with the lustre; *pada* — of the feet; *pallavasya* — of the sprouts; *madhura* — with the sweet; *chaṭayā* — radiance; *dharaṇīm* — the earth; *cakāra* — made; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *abdhiḥ* — the ocean; *asau* — He; *aruṇāyitām* — reddened; *aruṇa* — reddish; *pāṇi* — of the hands; *talaḥ* — [with] the palms.

**Translation.** With the sweet radiance of His sprout-like feet, lustrous as a grove of fresh coral trees, He, the ocean of mercy, whose palms are reddish, made the earth red.

**Note.** Here *kadamba* means "multitude, grove" (cf. verse 6, where the edition has *कड़म्ब*).

### 47

**47.**

```
तपनीयगौरवपुषो महसा
नटतोऽस्य वारिदबलवत्तिमिराः ।
ककुभो विभिन्नरुचयो मिलितां
मृगनाभिकुङ्कुमरुचं विदधुः ॥४७॥
```
*tapanīya-gaura-vapuṣo mahasā
naṭato 'sya vārida-balavat-timirāḥ |
kakubho vibhinna-rucayo militāṁ
mṛganābhi-kuṅkuma-rucaṁ vidadhuḥ ||47||*

**Word for word:** *tapanīya* — as refined gold; *gaura* — golden; *vapuṣaḥ* — of [Him with] a body; *mahasā* — by the splendour; *naṭataḥ* — dancing; *asya* — of Him; *vārida* — of the clouds; *bala* — by the might; *vat* — possessing; *timirāḥ* — darkness; *kakubhaḥ* — the quarters; *vibhinna* — mixed; *rucayaḥ* — [with] lustre; *militām* — blended; *mṛganābhi* — of musk; *kuṅkuma* — and kunkuma; *rucam* — the lustre; *vidadhuḥ* — took on.

**Translation.** By the splendour of Him dancing, His body golden as refined gold, the quarters, dark with the might of the clouds, their lustre mixed [with His], took on the blended lustre of musk and kunkuma.

**Note.** Musk is dark, kunkuma golden-red: the darkness of the clouds and the gold of Gaura. *Vārida-balavat-timirāḥ* is understood as "with darkness from the might of the clouds" (*balavat-timira* may also be simply "deep darkness"). The Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *militāḥ* ("blended", of the quarters).

### 48

**48.**

```
तत आगतश्च हरिदासमहा-
महिताशयं सुमहनीयगुणम् ।
निजपादपङ्कजमधून्मदस-
```

mahitāśayaṁ su-mahanīya-guṇam |
nija-pāda-paṅkaja-madhūnmada-sad-
bhramaraṁ vilokya mumude sa vibhuḥ ||48||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *āgataḥ* — having come; *ca* — and; *haridāsa* — Haridasa; *mahā* — very; *mahita* — exalted; *āśayam* — [of] heart; *su* — most; *mahanīya* — worthy of honour; *guṇam* — [with] qualities; *nija* — of His own; *pāda* — of the feet; *paṅkaja* — of the lotus; *madhu* — by the honey; *unmada* — maddened; *sat* — fine; *bhramaram* — a bee; *vilokya* — having seen; *mumude* — rejoiced; *saḥ* — that; *vibhuḥ* — all-pervading Lord.

**Translation.** Then, having come, the all-pervading Lord saw Haridasa — of a most exalted heart, with qualities most worthy of honour, a fine bee maddened by the honey of His own lotus feet — and rejoiced.

**Note.** Haridasa Thakura, the "namacharya" (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Antya 3, 11). *Āgataś ca* formally refers to the Lord; by the sense it may also be understood: "seeing that Haridasa had come".

**49.**

```
परिरभ्य निर्भरममुं सहसा

```

**Word for word:** *parirabhya* — having embraced; *nirbharam* — tightly; *amum* — him; *sahasā* — at once; *sva* — of His own; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotus; *bhaktam* — the devotee; *anuraktatamam* — most devoted; *varam* — an excellent; *āsanam* — seat; *karuṇayā* — mercifully; *sva* — by His own; *janaiḥ* — people; *nayana* — of the eyes; *śriyā* — by the grace; *anayat* — had brought; *aneka* — of manifold; *kṛpaḥ* — [the Lord] of mercy.

**Translation.** At once tightly embracing him, the most devoted servant of His own lotus feet, the Lord of manifold mercy, by the grace of His eyes, mercifully had His own people bring an excellent seat.

**Note.** "By the grace of His eyes": by a sign of His glance.

### 50

**50.**

```
अभिवाद्य तत्तु शिरसा प्रणतो
वरमासनं भुवि चकार पदम् ।
प्रभुपादपङ्कजपरागचयं

```
*abhivādya tat tu śirasā praṇato

prabhu-pāda-paṅkaja-parāga-cayaṁ
parigṛhya bhakti-parayā sa-dhiyā ||50||*

**Word for word:** *abhivādya* — having saluted; *tat* — that (seat); *tu* — but; *śirasā* — with his head; *praṇataḥ* — bowing; *varam* — an excellent; *āsanam* — seat; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *cakāra* — made; *padam* — [his] place; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *pāda* — of the feet; *paṅkaja* — of the lotus; *parāga* — of pollen; *cayam* — a heap; *parigṛhya* — having taken; *bhakti* — on devotion; *parayā* — intent; *sa-dhiyā* — with a mind.

**Translation.** But he, bowing, saluted that seat with his head and made his place on the bare ground his excellent seat, having taken, with a mind intent on devotion, a heap of pollen from the lotus feet of the Lord.

**Note.** Haridasa's humility: a Muslim by birth, he does not sit on the seat of honour (cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Antya 11).

**51.**

```
तनुमस्य चन्दनरसेन तदा
परिलिप्य माल्यमवयोज्य हृदि ।
स चतुर्विधं मधुरमन्नमतः

```

**Word for word:** *tanum* — the body; *asya* — his; *candana* — of sandal; *rasena* — with the paste; *tadā* — then; *parilipya* — having anointed; *mālyam* — a garland; *avayojya* — having placed; *hṛdi* — on the chest; *saḥ* — He; *catuḥ* — of four; *vidham* — kinds; *madhuram* — sweet; *annam* — food; *ataḥ* — then; *paribhojya* — having fed; *bhūri* — immensely; *karuṇaḥ* — merciful; *mumude* — rejoiced.

**Translation.** Then the immensely merciful [Lord] anointed his body with sandal paste, placed a garland on his chest, and then fed him sweet food of four kinds — and rejoiced.

**Note.** The four kinds of food are those that are chewed, sucked, licked and drunk (*carvya*, *coṣya*, *lehya*, *peya*).

**52.**

```
अनुनृत्य सोऽपि हरिकीर्त्तनतः
```

**53.**

```
अथ तत्र तेन सह देवघटा-
मुकुटार्घ्यरत्नरुचिराजिपदः ।
```

mukuṭārghya-ratna-ruci-rāji-padaḥ |
gamanāya geham abhitaḥ sahasā

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *tatra* — there; *tena* — him; *saha* — with; *deva* — of gods; *ghaṭā* — of the host; *mukuṭa* — of the crowns; *arghya* — precious; *ratna* — of the gems; *ruci* — of lustre; *rāji* — [lit by] a line; *padaḥ* — [whose] feet; *gamanāya* — to go; *geham abhitaḥ* — home; *sahasā* — at once; *gatavantam* — who had come; *āha* — told; *giriśam* — Girisha; *saḥ* — that; *vibhuḥ* — all-pervading Lord.

**Translation.** Then the all-pervading Lord, whose feet [are lit by] a line of lustre from the precious gems in the crowns of the host of gods, told Girisha, who had come there together with him [Haridasa], to go home at once.

**Note.** Girisha is Advaita (verse 30). The gods touch the Lord's feet with their crowns as they bow.

**54.**

**56.**

```
इति तद्बहिर्वसनमेकमसौ
परिगृह्य कारुणिकतां रचयन् ।
निजपादजीवनधनेभ्य ऋते
```

parigṛhya kāruṇikatāṁ racayan |

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *tat* — of them; *bahiḥ* — outer; *vasanam* — garment; *ekam* — one; *asau* — He; *parigṛhya* — having taken; *kāruṇikatām* — compassion; *racayan* — showing; *nija* — His own; *pāda* — feet; *jīvana* — of life; *dhanebhyaḥ* — to [those whose] treasure; *ṛte* — except; *kamalākṣa-devam* — Kamalaksha Deva; *adadāt* — gave; *karuṇaḥ* — compassionate.

**59.**

**60.**

```

विलुठन् करणाम्बुजयुगेन मुदं
```

viluṭhan karaṇāmbuja-yugena mudaṁ

**Word for word:** *naṭana* — of dancing; *antare* — in the intervals; *nija* — His own; *janān* — people; *paritaḥ* — on all sides; *parirabhya* — having embraced; *nirbharam* — tightly; *atho* — then; *saha* — together; *taiḥ* — with them; *viluṭhan* — rolling; *kara* — of hands; *ambuja* — of lotuses; *yugena* — with the pair ⟨?⟩; *mudam* — with joy; *prahasan* — laughing aloud; *mṛga-indraḥ* — the king of beasts (a lion); *iva* — like; *saṁprababhau* — shone forth.

**Translation.** In the intervals of dancing He tightly embraced His own people on all sides, and then, rolling [on the ground] together with them, [with] His pair of lotus hands [outspread], laughing aloud with joy, He shone forth like a lion.

**Note.** The edition has *करणाम्बुज-*, a syllable too many for the metre; we read *karāmbuja-* ("lotus hands") ⟨?⟩. The verse might be referred to the Lord, but verse 61 shows that it concerns Nityananda.

uccair uccaiś cakrur unmatta-cittāḥ
śrī-gaurāṅga-prema-mātrāvalambāḥ ||106||*

**Word for word:** *evam* — thus; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *kautukam* — wonder; *te* — they; *vilokya* — having seen; *prema* — from love; *udbhrāntāḥ* — maddened; *kīrtanam* — kirtana; *nartanam* — dancing; *ca* — and; *uccaiḥ uccaiḥ* — louder and louder; *cakruḥ* — performed; *unmatta* — intoxicated; *cittāḥ* — [with] hearts; *śrī-gaurāṅga* — for Sri Gauranga; *prema* — love; *mātra* — the only; *avalambāḥ* — [whose] support.

**Translation.** Seeing such a wonder again, they — maddened by love, their hearts intoxicated, they whose only support is love for Sri Gauranga — performed kirtana and dancing louder and louder.

### 107

**107.**

```
अन्येद्युर्गौरचन्द्रो निजजनसहितो भक्तिशिक्षां वितन्व-
न्नत्यन्ताश्चर्य्यचेष्टः कमलभवभवाद्यैर्भृशं भावनीयः ।
कुज्ञानाद्यैः समन्तात् सकलमनुपुरं देवतानां निकेतं
सन्मार्जन्या च चक्रे जगति सुविदितो मार्ज्जितं शश्वदेव ॥१०७॥
```
*anyedyur gauracandro nija-jana-sahito bhakti-śikṣāṁ vitanvann
atyantāścarya-ceṣṭaḥ kamala-bhava-bhavādyair bhṛśaṁ bhāvanīyaḥ |
kujñānādyaiḥ samantāt sakalam anupuraṁ devatānāṁ niketaṁ
sanmārjanyā ca cakre jagati suvidito mārjitaṁ śaśvad eva ||107||*

**Word for word:** *anyedyuḥ* — on another day; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra; *nija* — His own; *jana* — people; *sahitaḥ* — together with; *bhakti* — of devotion; *śikṣām* — the teaching; *vitanvan* — spreading; *atyanta* — extremely; *āścarya* — wondrous; *ceṣṭaḥ* — [whose] deeds; *kamala-bhava* — by Brahma (born from the lotus); *bhava* — by Shiva; *ādyaiḥ* — and the others; *bhṛśam* — incessantly; *bhāvanīyaḥ* — to be contemplated; *kujñāna* — of false knowledge; *ādyaiḥ* — [from] and the rest; *samantāt* — everywhere; *sakalam* — everything; *anupuram* — in every town; *devatānām* — of the deities; *niketam* — the abodes; *sanmārjanyā* — with a good broom; *ca* — and; *cakre* — made; *jagati* — in the world; *suviditaḥ* — renowned; *mārjitam* — cleansed; *śaśvat eva* — constantly.

**Translation.** On another day Gaurachandra, whose deeds are extremely wondrous, who is incessantly contemplated by Brahma, Shiva and the others, spreading the teaching of devotion together with His own people, cleansed everything everywhere of false knowledge and the rest — and with a good broom He constantly swept clean the abodes of the deities in every town, becoming renowned for this in the world.

**Note.** The metre is *sragdharā* (21 syllables). A twofold cleansing: of the world from false knowledge (*kujñāna*), and of the temples with a broom; cf. the later cleansing of the Gundicha temple (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 12), where the broom is an image of the cleansing of the heart. The syntax of *kujñānādyaiḥ* (instrumental instead of ablative) is not entirely clear ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.
