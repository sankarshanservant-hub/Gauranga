# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Sixteen — verses 1–49 (printed pp. 492–510)

**Title of the sarga:** *Guṇḍicā-nṛtyādi-varṇana*, "The Description of the Dance [on the Way] to the Gundicha and Other [Events]" (from the colophon).
**Metre:**
- 1–3, 5 — *mālinī* (⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑———⏑——⏑——; 15 syllables per pada). In 5 the first pada is one syllable short.
- 4, 48 — *mandākrāntā* (————⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑——⏑——⏑——; 17 syllables per pada).
- 6–8, 33–36 — *vasanta-tilakā* (——⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑——; 14 syllables per pada); 6–8 form a *viśeṣaka* (three verses, one sentence). In 34 the fourth pada has fifteen syllables (*mūrdhani*, where the metre requires *mūrdhni*).
- 9–11, 22–23, 26–27, 32, 49 — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* (———⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑———⏑——⏑—; 19 syllables per pada). In 9 the second pada has only seventeen syllables; in 10 the first pada has a light syllable (*saha*) in the sixteenth place.
- 12–16, 24, 28–30 — *sragdharā* (————⏑——⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑——⏑——⏑——; 21 syllables per pada). In 28 the printed first pada has nineteen syllables (restored from the Gosvami edition); in 30 the fourth pada has a light syllable (*pu-* of *-purī*) in the seventeenth place.
- 17–19, 31 — *pṛthvī* (⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑——⏑—; 17 syllables per pada).
- 20–21 — *hariṇī* (⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑—————⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—; 17 syllables per pada). In 20 the fourth pada has a light syllable (*-la* of *lola-*) in the eighth place.
- 25 — *praharṣiṇī* (———⏑⏑⏑⏑—⏑—⏑——; 13 syllables per pada).
- 37–47 — *bhujaṅga-prayāta* (⏑——⏑——⏑——⏑——; 12 syllables per pada); 37–46 form a *kulaka*.

**Total verses:** 49.

**Word-for-word conventions:** compound words are divided into their members, each with its own meaning; case is rendered by an English preposition ("of", "with", "in", "by", "to", "from"); the vocative is given with "O". Square brackets enclose a word that is understood (for example, [whose], [like]).

**Contents of the passage:** the Ratha-yatra — the Lord's dancing before the chariot of Jagannatha on the way to the Gundicha.
- 1–5 — raising His arm, the Lord recites three verses: Mukunda-mala 2, Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.90.48 and Padyavali 63 (*nāhaṁ vipro…*); cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 13.
- 6–8 — a portrait of Gaura dancing (a *viśeṣaka*).
- 9–16 — the chariot: its height; "a new king of mountains"; the festival of the people of Puri; the earth beneath the wheels; the rivalry of the chariots; the dust up to the heavens; the sufferings of Kurma and Shesha; "but the dancing Gauranga has captured the hearts of all".
- 17–23 — the dance: the sound of the kirtana pierces the shell of the universe; whirling like a wheel, the tears form a ring like a halo; foam at the mouth; tears like a breaking string of pearls.
- 24 — three circles: the singers; the devotees headed by Kashi Mishra, holding one another's hands; Prataparudra outside.
- 25–29 — no one blinks; the Lord in a swoon, the chariot stands still; the devotees carry Him aside; He leans on Advaita; Prataparudra serves His feet (28).
- 30–33 — Paramananda Puri; a play on the name of Svarupa Damodara (31); Damodara embraces the Lord's feet; Kashishvara, Kashi Mishra, Govinda.
- 34–36 — the Lord places His foot on the heads of the devotees, presses His own foot to His heart and "tastes" it.
- 37–47 — the *kulaka* (37–46): an extended portrait of the Lord dancing; Jagannatha cannot blink.
- 48–49 — the miracle of the garland; immersion in the ocean of bliss.

**Textual cruxes:** 9 (*auttuṅgena*), 10 (*rājeka*; *saha-nirbharaṁ*), 14 (*payodhe*), 15 (*jīrṇa-maṇḍam*), 17 (*kalānidhiḥ*, *-malaj-*), 18 (*uccavaccaram*), 19, 20 (*paraḥśataiḥ*), 24 (*pramatha-*), 25 (*vidhi*), 28 (two syllables omitted), 32 (*anunā*), 34, 38, 40 — see the notes. Other metrical irregularities of the printed text: 5, 9, 20, 30, 34 (see above).

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
अथ पुलकसमूहभ्राजमानं प्ररोह-
न्मुकुलकुलविराजत्काञ्चनद्रुप्रकाशम् ।
मधुरमपठदुच्चैः पीनमुन्नीय बाहुं
कनकगिरिरिवासौ शृङ्गलग्नान्तरीक्षः ॥१॥
```
*atha pulaka-samūha-bhrājamānaṁ prarohan-
mukula-kula-virājat-kāñcana-dru-prakāśam |
madhuram apaṭhad uccaiḥ pīnam unnīya bāhuṁ
kanaka-girir ivāsau śṛṅga-lagnāntarīkṣaḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *pulaka* — of hairs standing on end; *samūha* — with the mass; *bhrājamānam* — shining; *prarohat* — of opening; *mukula* — of buds; *kula* — with clusters; *virājat* — resplendent; *kāñcana* — a golden; *dru* — tree; *prakāśam* — resembling; *madhuram* — sweetly; *apaṭhat* — recited; *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *pīnam* — mighty; *unnīya* — having raised; *bāhum* — [His] arm; *kanaka* — golden; *giriḥ* — a mountain; *iva* — like; *asau* — He; *śṛṅga* — to [whose] peak; *lagna* — clinging; *antarīkṣaḥ* — [with] the sky.

**Translation.** Then, raising His mighty arm — which shone with a mass of hairs standing on end and resembled a golden tree resplendent with clusters of opening buds — He, like a golden mountain with the sky clinging to its peak, loudly and sweetly recited [these verses]:

**Note.** Metre — *mālinī*; verses 1–5 are in *mālinī*, except 4 (*mandākrāntā*). Before the chariot the Lord now recites three verses; cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 13.78–80, where the same verses are cited before His dance at the chariot (2 — from the Mukunda-mala, 3 — Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.90.48, 4 — Padyavali 63).

**2.**

```
जयति जयति देवो देवकीनन्दनोऽसौ
जयति जयति कृष्णो वृष्णिवंशप्रदीपः ।
जयति जयति मेघश्यामलः कोमलाङ्गो
जयति जयति पृथ्वीभारनाशो मुकुन्दः ॥२॥
```
*jayati jayati devo devakī-nandano 'sau
jayati jayati kṛṣṇo vṛṣṇi-vaṁśa-pradīpaḥ |
jayati jayati megha-śyāmalaḥ komalāṅgo
jayati jayati pṛthvī-bhāra-nāśo mukundaḥ ||2||*

**Word for word:** *jayati jayati* — glory, glory [to]; *devaḥ* — God; *devakī* — of Devaki; *nandanaḥ* — the son; *asau* — this; *kṛṣṇaḥ* — Krishna; *vṛṣṇi* — of the Vrishnis; *vaṁśa* — of the dynasty; *pradīpaḥ* — the lamp; *megha* — like a cloud; *śyāmalaḥ* — dark; *komala* — tender; *aṅgaḥ* — [whose] body [is]; *pṛthvī* — of the earth; *bhāra* — of the burden; *nāśaḥ* — the remover; *mukundaḥ* — Mukunda.

**Translation.** Glory, glory to this God, the son of Devaki! Glory, glory to Krishna, the lamp of the Vrishni dynasty! Glory, glory to Him who is dark as a cloud, whose body is tender! Glory, glory to Mukunda, the remover of the burden of the earth!

**Note.** A verse of Kulashekhara from the Mukunda-mala (v. 2); there the reading is *jayatu jayatu* ("may He be glorious"), here *jayati* ("He is glorious").

### 3

**3.**

```
जयति जननिवासो देवकीजन्मवादो
यदुवरपरिषत् स्वैर्दोर्भिरस्यन्नधर्मम् ।
स्थिरचरवृजिनघ्नः सुस्मितश्रीमुखेन
व्रजपुरवनितानां वर्द्धयन् कामदेवम् ॥३॥
```
*jayati jana-nivāso devakī-janma-vādo
yadu-vara-pariṣat svair dorbhir asyann adharmam |
sthira-cara-vṛjina-ghnaḥ susmita-śrī-mukhena
vraja-pura-vanitānāṁ vardhayan kāma-devam ||3||*

**Word for word:** *jayati* — glorious is; *jana* — of [all] beings; *nivāsaḥ* — the abode; *devakī* — from Devaki; *janma* — about [whose] birth; *vādaḥ* — [there is only] a manner of speaking; *yadu* — of the Yadus; *vara* — the best; *pariṣat* — [whose] retinue [are]; *svaiḥ* — with His own; *dorbhiḥ* — arms; *asyan* — casting away; *adharmam* — irreligion; *sthira* — of the unmoving; *cara* — [and] the moving; *vṛjina* — of the sin; *ghnaḥ* — the destroyer; *su-smita* — beautifully smiling; *śrī* — with the beautiful; *mukhena* — face; *vraja* — of Vraja; *pura* — and of the city (Mathura); *vanitānām* — of the women; *vardhayan* — increasing; *kāma-devam* — the god of love.

**Translation.** Glorious is He, the abode of all beings, whose birth from Devaki is only a manner of speaking; whose retinue are the best of the Yadus; who with His own arms casts away irreligion; the destroyer of the sin of all that moves and does not move; who with His beautiful smiling face increases the god of love in the women of Vraja and of the city!

**Note.** Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.90.48; the text is identical. *Yadu-vara-pariṣat* — "whose retinue are the best of the Yadus", or "surrounded by the best of the Yadus". We take *vraja-pura-vanitā* as "the women of Vraja and of Mathura and Dvaraka", following the widespread interpretation. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 4

**4.**

```
नाहं विप्रो न च नरपतिर्नापि वैश्यो न शूद्रो
नाहं वर्णी न च गृहपतिर्नो वनस्थो यतिर्वा ।
किन्तु प्रोद्यन्निखिलपरमानन्दपूर्णामृताब्धे-
र्गोपीभर्तुः पदकमलयोर्दासदासानुदासः ॥४॥
```
*nāhaṁ vipro na ca nara-patir nāpi vaiśyo na śūdro
nāhaṁ varṇī na ca gṛha-patir no vana-stho yatir vā |
kintu prodyan-nikhila-paramānanda-pūrṇāmṛtābdher
gopī-bhartuḥ pada-kamalayor dāsa-dāsānudāsaḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *na aham* — I [am] not; *vipraḥ* — a brahmana; *na ca* — nor; *nara-patiḥ* — a king (a kshatriya); *na api* — nor; *vaiśyaḥ* — a vaishya; *na śūdraḥ* — nor a shudra; *varṇī* — a brahmachari; *gṛha-patiḥ* — a householder; *no* — not; *vana-sthaḥ* — a forest-dweller (a vanaprastha); *yatiḥ* — an ascetic (a sannyasi); *vā* — or; *kintu* — but; *prodyat* — surging; *nikhila* — of complete; *parama* — supreme; *ānanda* — bliss; *pūrṇa* — full; *amṛta* — of nectar; *abdheḥ* — of the ocean; *gopī* — of the gopis; *bhartuḥ* — of the Lord; *pada* — of the feet; *kamalayoḥ* — of the lotuses; *dāsa* — of the servant; *dāsa* — of the servant; *anudāsaḥ* — the servant.

**Translation.** I am not a brahmana, nor a king, nor a vaishya, nor a shudra; I am not a brahmachari, nor a householder, nor a forest-dweller, nor an ascetic. But I am the servant of the servant of the servant of the lotus feet of the Lord of the gopis, the ocean of nectar full of surging, complete, supreme bliss.

**Note.** Metre — *mandākrāntā*. Padyavali 63, where it is ascribed to Sri Chaitanya Himself; in the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 13.80, the Lord recites this verse at the chariot.

### 5

**5.**

```
इति नटनकलादौ श्रीवृन्दावनेन्दोः
परममहिमवत्त्वं निर्भरार्त्तो निरूप्य ।
अतिशयकरुणार्द्रः प्रेमभक्तिं वितन्व-
न्नयमतिमधुराङ्गो हर्षपूर्णो बभूव ॥५॥
```
*iti naṭana-kalādau śrī-vṛndāvanendoḥ
parama-mahimavattvaṁ nirbharārtto nirūpya |
atiśaya-karuṇārdraḥ prema-bhaktiṁ vitanvann
ayam ati-madhurāṅgo harṣa-pūrṇo babhūva ||5||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *naṭana* — of the dance; *kalā* — of the art; *ādau* — at the beginning; *śrī-vṛndāvana* — of Sri Vrindavana; *indoḥ* — of the moon; *parama* — the supreme; *mahimavattvam* — greatness; *nirbhara* — deeply; *ārttaḥ* — stirred; *nirūpya* — having set forth; *atiśaya* — by boundless; *karuṇā* — compassion; *ārdraḥ* — softened; *prema* — loving; *bhaktim* — devotion; *vitanvan* — spreading; *ayam* — He; *ati* — exceedingly; *madhura* — charming; *aṅgaḥ* — [whose] body [is]; *harṣa* — with joy; *pūrṇaḥ* — filled; *babhūva* — became.

**Translation.** Thus at the beginning of His artful dance, having set forth, deeply stirred, the supreme greatness of the Moon of Sri Vrindavana, He — whose body is exceedingly charming — softened by boundless compassion and spreading loving devotion, became filled with joy.

**Note.** The first pada has fourteen syllables instead of fifteen: after *śrī-* (the ninth syllable) a light syllable is missing, so that the heavy *vṛn-* falls in the tenth place, which requires a light one ⟨?⟩. The Sanskrit is given as printed.

### 6–8

**6.**

```
आस्फोट्य वामकरकक्षतटीं करेण
रज्यद्वपुर्मधुरकोमलतातिरम्यः ।
लीलाविलोलमुखचन्द्रमयूखरोचिः-
श्रीमच्छटाझलमलायितदिक्समूहः ॥६॥
```
*āsphoṭya vāma-kara-kakṣa-taṭīṁ kareṇa

līlā-vilola-mukha-candra-mayūkha-rociḥ-

**Word for word:** *āsphoṭya* — having slapped; *vāma* — of the left; *kara* — arm; *kakṣa* — of the armpit; *taṭīm* — the side; *kareṇa* — with [His] hand; *rajyat* — reddening; *vapuḥ* — [whose] body [is]; *madhura* — by sweetness; *komalatā* — [and] tenderness; *ati-ramyaḥ* — most lovely; *līlā* — in play; *vilola* — moving; *mukha* — of the face; *candra* — of the moon; *mayūkha* — of the rays; *rociḥ* — of the radiance; *śrīmat* — with the beautiful; *chaṭā* — streams; *jhalamalāyita* — made to glitter; *dik* — of the directions; *samūhaḥ* — [by whom] the whole host.

**7.**

```
उच्चैर्मुहुर्जयजयेति विमुक्तकण्ठ-
मुच्चारयन् सह तनूरुहवृन्दहर्षैः ।
मुष्टिप्रमेयतनुमध्यविलासबद्ध-
रक्ताम्बरद्युतिविडम्बितबन्धुजीवः ॥७॥
```
*uccair muhur jaya-jayeti vimukta-kaṇṭham
uccārayan saha tanūruha-vṛnda-harṣaiḥ |
muṣṭi-prameya-tanu-madhya-vilāsa-baddha-
raktāmbara-dyuti-viḍambita-bandhujīvaḥ ||7||*

**Word for word:** *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *jaya-jaya* — "Glory, glory!"; *iti* — thus; *vimukta* — released (i.e. at the top of His voice); *kaṇṭham* — [with] throat; *uccārayan* — crying out; *saha* — together with; *tanūruha* — of hairs; *vṛnda* — of the multitude; *harṣaiḥ* — with the bristling; *muṣṭi* — by a fist; *prameya* — measurable; *tanu* — slender; *madhya* — on the waist; *vilāsa* — gracefully; *baddha* — tied; *rakta* — of the crimson; *ambara* — of the cloth; *dyuti* — by the lustre; *viḍambita* — [by whom] mocked; *bandhujīvaḥ* — the bandhujiva flower.

**8.**

```
श्रीमद्विलोचनजलाप्लुतगौरदेहः
प्रत्यग्रघर्मकणिकाखचितास्यचन्द्रः ।
उद्दामताण्डवकलाकुलिताङ्गभङ्गः
श्रीमानथ स्वजनमध्यमलंचकार ॥८॥
(विशेषकम्)
```
*śrīmad-vilocana-jalāpluta-gaura-dehaḥ
pratyagra-gharma-kaṇikā-khacitāsya-candraḥ |
uddāma-tāṇḍava-kalākulitāṅga-bhaṅgaḥ
śrīmān atha svajana-madhyam alaṁcakāra ||8||
(viśeṣakam)*

**Word for word:** *śrīmat* — of the beautiful; *vilocana* — of the eyes; *jala* — with the water; *āpluta* — bathed; *gaura* — golden; *dehaḥ* — [whose] body [is]; *pratyagra* — with fresh; *gharma* — of perspiration; *kaṇikā* — with drops; *khacita* — studded; *āsya* — of the face; *candraḥ* — [whose] moon [is]; *uddāma* — of the unrestrained; *tāṇḍava* — of the tandava; *kalā* — by the art; *ākulita* — agitated; *aṅga-bhaṅgaḥ* — [whose] bendings of the body [are]; *śrīmān* — the beautiful one; *atha* — then; *svajana* — of His own people; *madhyam* — the midst; *alaṁcakāra* — adorned.

**Translation (6–8).** Slapping the side of His left armpit with His hand; His body reddening; most lovely in His sweetness and tenderness; making the whole host of the directions glitter with the beautiful streams of radiance from the rays of the moon of His face, moving in play; again and again loudly crying out "Glory, glory!" at the top of His voice, together with the bristling of the multitude of hairs on His body; mocking the bandhujiva flower with the lustre of the crimson cloth gracefully tied about His slender waist, which a fist could measure; His golden body bathed in the water of His beautiful eyes; the moon of His face studded with fresh drops of perspiration; the bendings of His body agitated by the art of the unrestrained tandava — the beautiful [Gaura] then adorned the midst of His own people.

**Note.** Metre — *vasanta-tilakā*; the three verses form a *viśeṣaka* (so marked in the edition). Slapping the armpit (*āsphoṭana*) is a wrestler's gesture, a challenge before a bout; here it is a sign of exultation before the dance. *Jhalamalāyita* is a denominative from the onomatopoeic *jhalamala*, "glitter, twinkling". The *bandhujīva* is a crimson flower (*Pentapetes phoenicea*).

### 9

**9.**

```
औत्तुङ्ग्येन नभस्थलं तरलयन्मार्त्तण्डबिम्बं मुहु-
श्चुम्बन् देवसभाजनविधिं संपादयन्निर्भरम् ।
ब्रह्माण्डान्तरसंस्थितस्य नयनानन्दोत्सवोत्साहकः
साटोपं मुरवैरिणो विजयते लक्ष्मीमयः स्यन्दनः ॥९॥
```
*auttuṅgyena nabhas-thalaṁ taralayan mārttaṇḍa-bimbaṁ muhuś
cumban deva-sabhājana-vidhiṁ saṁpādayan nirbharam |
brahmāṇḍāntara-saṁsthitasya nayanānandotsavotsāhakaḥ
sāṭopaṁ mura-vairiṇo vijayate lakṣmī-mayaḥ syandanaḥ ||9||*

**Word for word:** *auttuṅgyena* — by [its] loftiness; *nabhaḥ-sthalam* — the expanse of the sky; *taralayan* — making tremble; *mārttaṇḍa* — of the sun; *bimbam* — the disc; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *cumban* — kissing; *deva* — of the gods; *sabhājana* — of honouring; *vidhim* — the rite; *saṁpādayan* — performing; *nirbharam* — fully; *brahmāṇḍa* — the universe; *antara* — within; *saṁsthitasya* — of [all that] abides; *nayana* — for the eyes; *ānanda* — of joy; *utsava* — of the festival; *utsāhakaḥ* — the inspirer; *sāṭopam* — majestically; *mura-vairiṇaḥ* — of the Enemy of Mura; *vijayate* — triumphs; *lakṣmī* — splendour; *mayaḥ* — full of; *syandanaḥ* — the chariot.

**Translation.** Majestically triumphs the chariot of the Enemy of Mura, full of splendour: by its loftiness it makes the expanse of the sky tremble; again and again it kisses the disc of the sun; it fully performs the rite of honouring the gods; and it inspires a festival of joy for the eyes of all that abides within the universe.

**Note.** Metre — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita*; verses 9–11 are in *śārdūla*. The edition prints औत्तुङ्गेन (*auttuṅgena*, which is not a word); we read *auttuṅgyena*, "by its loftiness" (the metre is the same). *Deva-sabhājana* may also be understood through *deva-sabhā*, "the assembly of the gods": "it brings full joy to the assembly of the gods". The second pada has only seventeen syllables instead of nineteen and does not scan from the ninth syllable on (*-na* of *-sabhājana-* stands where a heavy syllable is required): some two syllables seem to have dropped out ⟨?⟩.

### 10

**10.**

```
कैलासं नमयन्नशेषविधिना मेरुं सहनिर्भरं
सोत्कण्ठं किल विन्ध्यकं विकलयन् गौरीगुरुं ग्लापयन् ।
अन्यः कोऽप्यधुनावनौ शिखरिणां राजेव किं निर्मितो
धात्रा स्यन्दन इत्यसौ मुररिपुश्रीमूर्त्तिपीयूषभृत् ॥१०॥
```
*kailāsaṁ namayann aśeṣa-vidhinā meruṁ saha-nirbharaṁ
sotkaṇṭhaṁ kila vindhyakaṁ vikalayan gaurī-guruṁ glāpayan |
anyaḥ ko 'py adhunāvanau śikhariṇāṁ rājeva kiṁ nirmito
dhātrā syandana ity asau mura-ripu-śrī-mūrtti-pīyūṣa-bhṛt ||10||*

**Word for word:** *kailāsam* — Kailasa; *namayan* — bowing down; *aśeṣa* — in every; *vidhinā* — way; *merum* — Meru; *saha* — together with; *nirbharam* — [its] excess [of pride] (?); *sa-utkaṇṭham* — with longing; *kila* — indeed; *vindhyakam* — the paltry Vindhya; *vikalayan* — distressing; *gaurī* — of Gauri; *gurum* — the father (the Himalaya); *glāpayan* — dejecting; *anyaḥ* — another; *kaḥ api* — some; *adhunā* — now; *avanau* — on the earth; *śikhariṇām* — of mountains; *rājā iva* — a king, as it were; *kim* — whether; *nirmitaḥ* — has been made; *dhātrā* — by the Creator; *syandanaḥ* — the chariot; *iti* — thus; *asau* — this; *mura-ripu* — of the Enemy of Mura; *śrī* — of the beautiful; *mūrtti* — form; *pīyūṣa* — the nectar; *bhṛt* — bearing.

**Translation.** Bowing Kailasa down in every way, [humbling] Meru together with all its pride, indeed throwing the paltry Vindhya into longing distress and dejecting the father of Gauri — "Has the Creator now made on earth some other king, as it were, of the mountains?" — [so people thought of] this chariot, which bore the nectar of the beautiful form of the Enemy of Mura.

**Note.** The edition prints राजेक (*rājeka*), which makes no sense; we read *rājeva* (= *rājā iva*), as does the Gosvami edition (GGM). *Meruṁ saha-nirbharaṁ* is obscure, and the sense given is conjectural; moreover, *saha* puts a light syllable in the sixteenth place, where the *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* requires a heavy one. The variant *meruṁ hasan nirbharaṁ*, "laughing heartily at Meru", scans correctly and gives a clear sense ⟨?⟩. The diminutive *vindhyaka* is contemptuous ("some Vindhya or other"). The father of Gauri (Parvati) is Himavan, the Himalaya.

**11.**

```
उत्पक्ष्माक्षिसरोरुहाञ्जलिपुटैर्नीलाद्रिचूडामणेः
श्रीमूर्त्तिच्छुरितामृतानि पिबतामुल्लासधन्यात्मनाम् ।
निष्पन्दं पुलकावलीविलसतामानन्दमन्दाकिनी-
कल्लोलैः किल तत्र तत्र भवतामासीन्महानुत्सवः ॥११॥
```
*utpakṣmākṣi-saroruhāñjali-puṭair nīlādri-cūḍāmaṇeḥ
śrī-mūrtti-cchuritāmṛtāni pibatām ullāsa-dhanyātmanām |
niṣpandaṁ pulakāvalī-vilasatām ānanda-mandākinī-
kallolaiḥ kila tatra tatra bhavatām āsīn mahān utsavaḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *utpakṣma* — with lashes raised; *akṣi* — of the eyes; *saroruha* — of the lotuses; *añjali-puṭaiḥ* — with the cupped hands; *nīla-adri* — of the Blue Mountain; *cūḍā-maṇeḥ* — of the crest-jewel; *śrī* — from the beautiful; *mūrtti* — form; *churita* — radiating; *amṛtāni* — the nectars; *pibatām* — of [those] drinking; *ullāsa* — by exultation; *dhanya* — blessed; *ātmanām* — [whose] souls [were]; *niṣpandam* — motionless; *pulaka* — of hairs standing on end; *āvalī* — with rows; *vilasatām* — of [those] shining; *ānanda* — of bliss; *mandākinī* — of the Mandakini; *kallolaiḥ* — with the waves; *kila* — indeed; *tatra tatra* — everywhere there; *bhavatām* — among the worthy [people]; *āsīt* — there was; *mahān* — a great; *utsavaḥ* — festival.

**Translation.** Among the worthy [people of Puri] — whose souls were blessed by exultation, who with the cupped hands of their lotus eyes, their lashes raised, drank the nectars radiating from the beautiful form of the Crest-Jewel of the Blue Mountain, and who shone motionless with rows of hairs standing on end — there was indeed everywhere a great festival, with waves of the Mandakini of bliss.

**Note.** The Mandakini is the heavenly Ganga. *Utpakṣma*, "with lashes raised", i.e. without blinking.

**12.**

```
भूयो भूयः समन्तात् सरभसमनसामागतानां विशेषं
तत्तत्सीमन्तिनीनामलिकविकलितैः कम्रसिन्दूरपूरैः ।
सैन्दूरीकर्त्तुमासीद्रथपरिसरभूश्चक्रनिष्पीडनेन
क्षुब्धापि प्रायशः सा प्रमुदितमनसात्मानमुत्कण्ठितेव ॥१२॥
```
*bhūyo bhūyaḥ samantāt sarabhasa-manasām āgatānāṁ viśeṣaṁ
tat-tat-sīmantinīnām alika-vikalitaiḥ kamra-sindūra-pūraiḥ |
saindūrī-karttum āsīd ratha-parisara-bhūś cakra-niṣpīḍanena

**Word for word:** *bhūyaḥ bhūyaḥ* — again and again; *samantāt* — from all sides; *sa-rabhasa* — eager; *manasām* — [with] hearts; *āgatānām* — of [those] who had come; *viśeṣam* — especially; *tat-tat* — of these and those; *sīmantinīnām* — of the women; *alika* — from the foreheads; *vikalitaiḥ* — fallen; *kamra* — of lovely; *sindūra* — vermilion; *pūraiḥ* — with the streams; *saindūrī-karttum* — to colour with vermilion; *āsīt* — was; *ratha* — of the chariot; *parisara* — surrounding; *bhūḥ* — the ground; *cakra* — of the wheels; *niṣpīḍanena* — by the pressure; *kṣubdhā* — tormented; *api* — although; *prāyaśaḥ* — almost [all]; *sā* — it; *pramudita* — with a gladdened; *manasā* — heart; *ātmānam* — itself; *utkaṇṭhitā* — longing; *iva* — as if.

**Translation.** The ground around the chariot, though almost all of it was tormented by the pressure of the wheels, with a gladdened heart seemed to long to colour itself with vermilion — with the lovely streams of sindura fallen especially from the foreheads of the many women who came again and again from all sides with eager hearts.

**Note.** Metre — *sragdharā*; verses 12–16 are in *sragdharā*. The edition has *saindūrī-* (the *vṛddhi* derivative of *sindūra*), which is admissible. Sindura on the forehead (in the parting of the hair) is the mark of a married woman.

### 13

**13.**

```
नृत्यन्तं गौरचन्द्रं चरणसरसिजद्वन्द्वविन्यासरम्यं
दृष्ट्वा दृष्ट्वा प्रकामं सरभसमनसो भ्रातरस्ते रथस्थाः ।
भूयोऽहंपूर्विकाभिः प्रसृमरगतयः कौतुकेनाग्रतोऽमी
जङ्घालास्तत्र तत्र प्रमदमदभरान्नर्त्तनं कुर्वतेव ॥१३॥
```
*nṛtyantaṁ gauracandraṁ caraṇa-sarasija-dvandva-vinyāsa-ramyaṁ
dṛṣṭvā dṛṣṭvā prakāmaṁ sarabhasa-manaso bhrātaras te ratha-sthāḥ |
bhūyo 'haṁ-pūrvikābhiḥ prasṛmara-gatayaḥ kautukenāgrato 'mī
jaṅghālās tatra tatra pramada-mada-bharān narttanaṁ kurvateva ||13||*

**Word for word:** *nṛtyantam* — dancing; *gauracandram* — Gaurachandra; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *sarasija* — of the lotuses; *dvandva* — of the pair; *vinyāsa* — by the placing; *ramyam* — charming; *dṛṣṭvā dṛṣṭvā* — looking and looking; *prakāmam* — to their hearts' content; *sa-rabhasa* — eager; *manasaḥ* — [with] hearts; *bhrātaraḥ* — the brothers; *te* — those; *ratha-sthāḥ* — standing on the chariots; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *ahaṁ-pūrvikābhiḥ* — with rivalry ("I first!"); *prasṛmara* — swift; *gatayaḥ* — [whose] going [was]; *kautukena* — with eagerness; *agrataḥ* — forward; *amī* — these; *jaṅghālāḥ* — swift-footed ones; *tatra tatra* — here and there; *pramada* — of joy; *mada* — of the intoxication; *bharāt* — from the excess; *narttanam* — a dance; *kurvata* — performed; *iva* — as if.

**Translation.** Looking and looking to their hearts' content at Gaurachandra as He danced, charming in the placing of His pair of lotus feet, those brothers standing on the chariots grew eager at heart; and again, vying with one another — "I first!" — moving swiftly and eagerly forward, these swift-footed ones here and there, as it were, danced from the excess of their joyful intoxication.

**Note.** The "brothers" are Jagannatha, Balarama and Subhadra (the brothers and their sister). Grammatically the subject of the whole verse is one — *bhrātaraḥ … amī jaṅghālāḥ* — that is, the chariots themselves, overtaking one another ⟨?⟩; the second half may also be referred to the people running before the chariots, vying to go first. *Kurvata* is an epic form without the augment (= *akurvata*). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 14

**14.**

```
आरुन्धन् दिक्करीन्द्रोत्करकरविवरं साम्बु चक्षुःसहस्रं
कुर्वन्नैरावणेशं पलितविलसिता देवतास्तत्र कुर्वन् ।
सेतुभ्रान्तिं पयोधे पुनरपि रचयन्नेवमुज्जृम्भतेऽसौ
प्रोन्मीलच्चक्रचक्रोद्दलनविसृमरोद्धूतधूलीप्रवाहः ॥१४॥
```
*ārundhan dik-karīndrotkara-kara-vivaraṁ sāmbu cakṣuḥ-sahasraṁ
kurvann airāvaṇeśaṁ palita-vilasitā devatās tatra kurvan |
setu-bhrāntiṁ payodhe(ḥ) punar api racayann evam ujjṛmbhate 'sau
pronmīlac-cakra-cakroddalana-visṛmaroddhūta-dhūlī-pravāhaḥ ||14||*

**Word for word:** *ārundhan* — blocking; *dik* — of the directions; *karī-indra* — of the lordly elephants; *utkara* — of the host; *kara* — of the trunks; *vivaram* — the openings; *sa-ambu* — tearful (literally "with water"); *cakṣuḥ* — of eyes; *sahasram* — [whose] thousand [are]; *kurvan* — making; *airāvaṇa* — of Airavana; *īśam* — the lord (Indra); *palita* — with grey hair; *vilasitāḥ* — shining; *devatāḥ* — the gods; *tatra* — there; *kurvan* — making; *setu* — of a bridge; *bhrāntim* — the illusion; *payodheḥ* — over the ocean; *punaḥ api* — once again; *racayan* — creating; *evam* — thus; *ujjṛmbhate* — spreads; *asau* — this; *pronmīlat* — rolling (literally "opening"); *cakra* — of the wheels; *cakra* — by the circles; *uddalana* — from the crushing; *visṛmara* — spreading; *uddhūta* — raised; *dhūlī* — of dust; *pravāhaḥ* — the stream.

**Translation.** The stream of dust, raised and spreading from the crushing [of the ground] by the circles of the rolling wheels, spreads so that it blocks the openings of the trunks of the host of lordly elephants of the directions, makes the Lord of Airavana tearful in all His thousand eyes, makes the gods there shine with grey hair, and once again creates the illusion of a bridge over the ocean.

**Note.** The bridge over the ocean is the one built by Rama's army (the Setu). The edition has *payodhe* without the visarga; we read *payodheḥ*. Airavana (= Airavata) is Indra's elephant.

### 15

**15.**

```
कूर्मो मर्मव्यथोऽभूत् फणिपतिरसकौ शीर्षतः शीर्षमध्यं
भूयोभूयो धरित्रीं नयति नतशिरा जीर्णमण्डं बभूव ।
वेलालोलैः पयोभिश्चिरमिव जलधिः क्षीणमर्याद आसी-
त्तत्रौत्सुक्येन नीलक्षितिधरतिलके प्रस्थिते गुण्डिचायाम् ॥१५॥
```
*kūrmo marma-vyatho 'bhūt phaṇi-patir asakau śīrṣataḥ śīrṣa-madhyaṁ
bhūyo-bhūyo dharitrīṁ nayati nata-śirā jīrṇa-maṇḍaṁ babhūva |
velā-lolaiḥ payobhiś ciram iva jaladhiḥ kṣīṇa-maryāda āsīt
tatrautsukyena nīla-kṣitidhara-tilake prasthite guṇḍicāyām ||15||*

**Word for word:** *kūrmaḥ* — the Tortoise; *marma* — in [his] vital parts; *vyathaḥ* — [feeling] pain; *abhūt* — became; *phaṇi* — of the serpents; *patiḥ* — the lord; *asakau* — this; *śīrṣataḥ* — from head; *śīrṣa* — of head; *madhyam* — to the middle; *bhūyaḥ-bhūyaḥ* — again and again; *dharitrīm* — the earth; *nayati* — moves; *nata* — bowed; *śirāḥ* — [with] heads; *jīrṇa* — worn out; *maṇḍam* — ornament (?); *babhūva* — became; *velā* — at the shore; *lolaiḥ* — tossing; *payobhiḥ* — with waters; *ciram* — for long; *iva* — as if; *jaladhiḥ* — the ocean; *kṣīṇa* — lost; *maryādaḥ* — [whose] bounds [had]; *āsīt* — was; *tatra* — then; *autsukyena* — eagerly; *nīla* — of the Blue; *kṣitidhara* — Mountain; *tilake* — [when] the ornament; *prasthite* — had set out; *guṇḍicāyām* — for the Gundicha.

**Translation.** When the Ornament of the Blue Mountain eagerly set out for the Gundicha, the Tortoise felt pain in his vital parts; this lord of the serpents, his heads bowed, again and again moved the earth from head to head — [and each head] became worn; and the ocean, its waters tossing at the shore, seemed for a long time to have lost its bounds.

**Note.** The Tortoise (Kurma) and Shesha are the supports of the earth. *Jīrṇa-maṇḍam babhūva* is obscure ⟨?⟩: perhaps "[each head] became worn" (as if *jīrṇa-maṇḍala*), or "he grew exhausted". The metre confirms *-maṇḍaṁ*. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 16

**16.**

```
राजन्तां तत्र तास्ताः सुरपतिपरिषत्कम्रलक्ष्मीरधोऽधः
कृत्वा कृत्वा मुरारेरथ रथविजये भूतयो रत्नभाजः ।
तत्तादृग्भूषणाढ्यः स्वयमपि भगवान् सम्यगुज्जृम्भतां सः
श्रीमान् किन्त्वेष नृत्यन्नखिलजनमनोरुद्धगौराङ्गचन्द्रः ॥१६॥
```

**Word for word:** *rājantām* — let shine; *tatra* — there; *tāḥ tāḥ* — all those; *surapati* — of the lord of the gods (Indra); *pariṣat* — of the assembly; *kamra* — the lovely; *lakṣmīḥ* — splendour; *adhaḥ adhaḥ kṛtvā kṛtvā* — again and again putting below (surpassing); *murāreḥ* — of Murari; *atha* — now; *ratha* — of the chariot; *vijaye* — at the triumphal setting out; *bhūtayaḥ* — riches; *ratna* — jewels; *bhājaḥ* — bearing; *tat* — those; *tādṛk* — such; *bhūṣaṇa* — in ornaments; *āḍhyaḥ* — rich; *svayam api* — Himself too; *bhagavān* — the Lord; *samyak* — fully; *ujjṛmbhatām* — let display [His] splendour; *saḥ* — He; *śrīmān* — beautiful; *kintu* — but; *eṣaḥ* — this; *nṛtyan* — dancing; *akhila* — of all; *jana* — the people; *manaḥ* — the hearts; *ruddha* — [who has] captured; *gaurāṅga* — Gauranga; *candraḥ* — the moon.

**Note.** *Ratha-vijaya*, "the triumphal setting out", is the procession of the chariot.

**17.**


**Word for word:** *śacī* — of Shachi; *suta* — of the son; *kalā-nidhiḥ* — the moon (literally "the treasury of digits"); *kim api* — indescribable; *sa-aṅga-bhaṅgam* — with bendings of the body; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *vilāsa* — playful; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *krama* — by the step; *unmathita* — [by whom] outdone; *ramya* — the lovely; *paṅkeruhaḥ* — lotuses; *nirantara* — unceasingly; *digantara* — through the directions; *churitam* — spreading; *accha* — of pure; *kānti* — radiance; *chaṭā* — of the streams; *malat* (?) — [obscure]; *jhalamalāyitam* — the glittering; *manasi* — in the heart; *vaḥ* — your; *samujjṛmbhatām* — may expand.

**Translation.** May that indescribable glittering of the streams of pure radiance of the Moon, the son of Shachi, which spreads unceasingly through the directions, expand in your hearts — [the glittering] of Him who, again and again bending His body, has outdone the lovely lotuses by the playful steps of His feet.

The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 18

**18.**

```
अमन्दतरमन्दिरानिनदसङ्गिसङ्गीतक-
ध्वनिर्ध्वनितदिग्वधूवदनपङ्कजैः पूजितः ।
विभिद्य मुहुरुच्चवच्चरमखण्डखण्डान्तरं
प्रयाति कति दूरतः स खलु मीयतां कैः पुनः ॥१८॥
```
*amanda-tara-mandirā-ninada-saṅgi-saṅgītaka-
dhvanir dhvanita-dig-vadhū-vadana-paṅkajaiḥ pūjitaḥ |
vibhidya muhur uccavac carama-khaṇḍa-khaṇḍāntaraṁ
prayāti kati dūrataḥ sa khalu mīyatāṁ kaiḥ punaḥ ||18||*

**Word for word:** *amanda-tara* — of the loudest; *mandirā* — of hand-cymbals; *ninada* — with the ringing; *saṅgi* — blending; *saṅgītaka* — of the singing; *dhvaniḥ* — the sound; *dhvanita* — echoing; *dik* — of the directions; *vadhū* — of the maidens; *vadana* — of the faces; *paṅkajaiḥ* — by the lotuses; *pūjitaḥ* — worshipped; *vibhidya* — having pierced; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *uccavat* — loud; *carama* — of the last; *khaṇḍa* — layer; *khaṇḍa-antaram* — the partition; *prayāti* — goes; *kati* — how; *dūrataḥ* — far; *saḥ* — it; *khalu* — indeed; *mīyatām* — may be measured; *kaiḥ* — by whom; *punaḥ* — then.

**Translation.** The sound of the singing, blending with the ringing of the loudest hand-cymbals and worshipped by the lotus faces of the maidens of the directions, who echo it, loudly and again and again pierces the partition of the last layer [of the universe] — and how far it goes, by whom then can that be measured?

**Note.** *Mandirā* (Bengali *mandira*) are small hand-cymbals (karatalas). *Uccavaccaram* in the edition is obscure. The Gosvami edition (GGM) divides the same letters as *uccavac carama-khaṇḍa-khaṇḍāntaram*, "the loud [sound, piercing] the partition of the last layer"; we accept this division. The metre confirms the syllables of the printed text.

**20.**

```
र्मुखरमुखरीभूताः सर्वा दिशः किमकुर्वत ।
निरवधि दृशौ तासु क्षिप्त्वा यदेष विलोहिते
```

**Translation.** What could all the directions do, made to resound deafeningly by hundreds upon hundreds of very loud cries of "Glory, glory, may He be glorious!"? For He, unceasingly casting His reddened eyes on them, by the art of His dance made the surface of the world trembling and crimson.

### 21

**21.**

```
मुखशशिसमुद्गीर्णैः फेनैर्हसन्निव शारदं
सततविजितं लक्ष्म्या लक्ष्माकुलं हिमदीधितिम् ।
पुलकपटलैरत्युद्भिन्नैः सुमेरुमिवोद्गता-
ङ्कुरशतपरिच्छेदातीतः स एष विराजते ॥२१॥
```
*mukha-śaśi-samudgīrṇaiḥ phenair hasann iva śāradaṁ
satata-vijitaṁ lakṣmyā lakṣmākulaṁ hima-dīdhitim |

**Word for word:** *mukha* — of the face; *śaśi* — from the moon; *samudgīrṇaiḥ* — poured forth; *phenaiḥ* — with the foam; *hasan* — laughing; *iva* — as if; *śāradam* — [at] the autumn; *satata* — ever; *vijitam* — defeated; *lakṣmyā* — by [His] beauty; *lakṣma* — with spots; *ākulam* — filled; *hima-dīdhitim* — the cool-rayed (moon); *pulaka* — of hairs standing on end; *paṭalaiḥ* — with the masses; *ati-udbhinnaiḥ* — thickly sprouted; *sumerum* — Sumeru; *iva* — like; *udgata* — sprouted; *aṅkura* — of shoots; *śata* — of hundreds; *pariccheda* — the counting; *atītaḥ* — beyond; *saḥ eṣaḥ* — He; *virājate* — shines.

**Translation.** With the foam poured forth from the moon of His face He seems to laugh at the cool-rayed autumn moon, full of spots and ever defeated by [His] beauty; with the thickly sprouted masses of hairs standing on end He is like Sumeru, with sprouted shoots in hundreds beyond all counting: so He shines.

**Note.** Foam at the mouth is one of the signs of ecstatic love. The golden Lord with His hairs standing on end is compared to the golden mountain Sumeru covered with sprouting shoots.

**23.**

```

```
*viśvaṁ plāvayateva tatra luṭhatā bhūmīṣu vakṣaḥ-sthale

**Word for word:** *viśvam* — the universe; *plāvayatā* — flooding; *iva* — as if; *tatra* — there; *luṭhatā* — rolling; *bhūmīṣu* — over the ground; *vakṣaḥ-sthale* — on the chest; *garbha* — from the depths; *udyat* — emerging; *kanaka* — golden; *aśma* — with stones; *ramya* — lovely; *taṭinī* — of a river; *śobhām* — the beauty; *tiraskurvatā* — eclipsing; *akṣṇoḥ* — in the eyes; *magna* — submerged; *saroja* — with lotuses; *sundara* — beautiful; *saraḥ* — of a lake; *śobhena* — having the beauty; *gaura-prabhoḥ* — of Lord Gaura; *ānanda* — of bliss; *aśru* — of tears; *bhareṇa* — by the flood; *tena* — by that; *jagatām* — on the worlds; *ānandaḥ* — bliss; *ādhīyatām* — may be bestowed.

### 24

**24.**


śrī-kāśīmiśra-mukhyaiḥ parama-sumatibhis tat-padābja-prapannaiḥ |
hasta-grāhaṁ pramodāt satata-valayite tad-bahiś ca pratāpa-

**Word for word:** *gāyadbhiḥ* — singing; *gāyanaiḥ* — by the singers; *taiḥ* — by those; *prathama* — first [the edition has *pramatha*]; *valayite* — formed into a ring; *maṇḍale* — [when] the circle; *tat* — of that; *bahiḥ* — outside; *ca* — and; *śrī-kāśīmiśra* — by Sri Kashi Mishra; *mukhyaiḥ* — headed; *parama* — supremely; *sumatibhiḥ* — by the right-minded; *tat* — His; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — at the lotuses; *prapannaiḥ* — who had taken refuge; *hasta-grāham* — holding hands; *pramodāt* — out of joy; *satata* — unbroken; *valayite* — ringed; *tat-bahiḥ ca* — and outside that; *pratāpa-prāk* — with "Pratapa" before [his name]; *śrī-śrī-rudradeve* — [when] Sri Sri Rudradeva; *nibhṛtam* — unobtrusively; *itaḥ itaḥ* — here and there; *veṣṭite* — encircled [it]; *bhāti* — shines; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord.

**26.**

```
सानन्दं सेविताङ्घ्रिद्वयसरसिरुहो राजते गौरचन्द्रः ॥२८॥
```
*ānandotsāha-mūrcchāgata iva bhavati spanda-niśvāsa-mande
rohad-romāñca-pūrair vikalita-vapuṣānanda-mandīkṛtena |
syandan-netrāravinda-dvaya-salila-juṣā rudradevena bhūyaḥ
sānandaṁ sevitāṅghri-dvaya-sarasiruho rājate gauracandraḥ ||28||*

**Word for word:** *ānanda* — of bliss; *utsāha* — from the surge; *mūrcchā* — into a swoon; *āgate* — fallen; *iva* — as if; *bhavati* — [when He] was; *spanda* — in movement; *niśvāsa* — [and] breathing; *mande* — slowed; *rohat* — rising; *romāñca* — of hairs standing on end; *pūraiḥ* — by the floods; *vikalita* — shaken; *vapuṣā* — [whose] body; *ānanda* — by bliss; *mandīkṛtena* — made slow; *syandat* — streaming; *netra* — of eyes; *aravinda* — of lotuses; *dvaya* — of the pair; *salila* — the water; *juṣā* — having; *rudradevena* — by Rudradeva; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *sa-ānandam* — with bliss; *sevita* — served; *aṅghri* — of feet; *dvaya* — of the pair; *sarasiruhaḥ* — [whose] lotuses; *rājate* — shines; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra.

**Translation.** When He, as if fallen into a swoon from the surge of bliss, was slow in movement and breath, Gaurachandra shines, the pair of His lotus feet served again with bliss by Rudradeva — whose body was shaken by floods of rising hairs standing on end, who was slowed by bliss, and whose pair of lotus eyes streamed with water.

**Note.** Metre: *sragdharā* (28–30). In the edition the first pada has nineteen syllables instead of twenty-one; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *mūrcchāgata iva bhavati* (*-gate iva* with sandhi), and the gap is filled from it. Rudradeva is King Prataparudra; cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 14.4–18 (the king, dressed as a Vaishnava and with the devotees' permission, serves the feet of the Lord, who lies in the garden with closed eyes — 14.6–7).

*ānandaṁ netra-randhrair niravadhi-paramānanda-sandoha-dhārā-
dhauta-pratyaṅga-lakṣmī-madhurima-vibhavo rāmaṇīyotka-cittaḥ |

**Word for word:** *ānandam* — bliss; *netra* — of the eyes; *randhraiḥ* — through the openings; *niravadhi* — boundless; *parama-ānanda* — of supreme bliss; *sandoha* — of the abundance; *dhārā* — by the streams; *dhauta* — washed; *pratyaṅga* — of every limb; *lakṣmī* — of the beauty; *madhurima* — the sweetness; *vibhavaḥ* — [whose] wealth; *rāmaṇīya* — for loveliness; *utka* — yearning; *cittaḥ* — [whose] heart; *pītvā pītvā* — having drunk and drunk; *yadā* — when; *ayam* — he; *naṭana* — of the dance; *rasa* — of the rasa; *dhunī* — of the river; *pūram* — the flood; *ullāsa* — with exultation; *lolaḥ* — trembling; *nispandaḥ* — motionless; *bobhavīti* — becomes again and again; *prathayati* — spreads; *paramānanda-purī* — Paramananda Puri; *sa-harṣam* — joyfully.

**Translation.** Paramananda Puri — whose wealth is the sweetness of the beauty of every limb washed by streams of an abundance of boundless supreme bliss, and whose heart yearns for loveliness — when, having drunk again and again the flood of the river of the rasa of the dance, he trembles with exultation and again and again becomes motionless, joyfully spreads bliss through the openings of his eyes.

**Note.** *Bobhavīti* is an intensive of *bhū*. Bliss "through the openings of the eyes" means tears streaming from the eyes. *Paramānanda-sandoha*, "an abundance of supreme bliss", echoes the name Paramananda. Paramananda Puri is a senior companion of the Lord, a disciple of Madhavendra Puri. In the fourth pada the seventeenth syllable (*pu-* of *-purī*) is light where the *sragdharā* requires a heavy one: the name does not fit the metre ⟨?⟩.

**Translation.** Is it because the Lord wore a waist-cord that this best of renunciants is proclaimed here "Damodara", as though he were His "Svarupa", His own form? — he who at the festival of the dance, intensely longing, with all the workings of heart, body and speech, as it were enters into the Moon that is the son of Shachi.

**Note.** Metre: *pṛthvī*. Svarupa Damodara is meant.

**Word for word:** *sphurat* — quivering; *muṣṭi* — by a fist; *meya* — measurable; *avalagne* — on the waist; *nitānta* — closely; *śrita* — clasping; *śrī* — beautiful; *kaṭī* — waist; *sūtra* — of the cord; *kāntyā* — by the lustre; *ati-kāntaḥ* — most lovely; *guru* — heavy; *sveda* — of sweat; *vāri* — of the water; *pravāha* — by the stream; *āpluta* — bathed; *uraḥ-sthalīkaḥ* — [whose] chest; *sat* — beautiful; *uddāma* — unrestrained; *romāñca* — of hairs standing on end; *pūraḥ* — [whose] flood.

**40.**

```
तदानन्दधारां वहन् क्षीरवारां-
निधेः सानुकारां विकारिप्रचाराम् ।
विलोलालिखेलाविलासाक्षिलीला-
रसैः साधु कुर्वन् जनस्याब्जगर्भम् ॥४०॥
```
*tad-ānanda-dhārāṁ vahan kṣīra-vārāṁ-
nidheḥ sānukārāṁ vikāri-pracārām |
vilolāli-khelā-vilāsākṣi-līlā-
rasaiḥ sādhu kurvan janasyābja-garbham ||40||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — of that; *ānanda* — bliss; *dhārām* — the stream; *vahan* — carrying; *kṣīra* — of milk; *vārāṁnidheḥ* — the ocean; *sa-anukārām* — resembling; *vikāri* — transforming; *pracārām* — [whose] course; *vilola* — darting; *ali* — of bees; *khelā* — [like] the play; *vilāsa* — with grace; *akṣi* — of the eyes; *līlā* — of the play; *rasaiḥ* — with the relishes; *sādhu* — wonderfully; *kurvan* — making; *janasya* — for the people; *abja* — of a lotus; *garbham* — the heart (?).

**41.**

```
अलंकुर्वदानन्दमूर्च्छाप्रकाश-
श्रितस्तम्भरोमाञ्चकम्पप्रकाशः ।
अनिर्वार्यभावप्रकाशातिरेक-
स्फुरद्देहकान्तिच्छटाच्छन्नलोकः ॥४१॥
```
*alaṁkurvad-ānanda-mūrcchā-prakāśa-
śrita-stambha-romāñca-kampa-prakāśaḥ |
anirvārya-bhāva-prakāśātireka-
sphurad-deha-kānti-cchaṭā-cchanna-lokaḥ ||41||*

**Word for word:** *alaṁkurvat* — adorning; *ānanda* — of bliss; *mūrcchā* — of swoon; *prakāśa* — by the manifestation; *śrita* — accompanied; *stambha* — of stupor; *romāñca* — of hairs standing on end; *kampa* — of trembling; *prakāśaḥ* — [whose] manifestation; *anirvārya* — irresistible; *bhāva* — of feeling; *prakāśa* — of manifestation; *atireka* — from the excess; *sphurat* — flashing; *deha* — of the body; *kānti* — of the radiance; *chaṭā* — with the streams; *channa* — who has covered; *lokaḥ* — the world.

**42.**

```
त्रिलोकीस्फुरत्कीर्त्तिपीयूषधारः
प्रकाशीकृतप्रेमभक्तिप्रचारः ।
लसत्तप्तकार्त्तस्वरश्रीमदङ्ग-
च्छटाच्छन्नलावण्यतारुण्यभङ्गः ॥४२॥
```
*trilokī-sphurat-kīrtti-pīyūṣa-dhāraḥ
prakāśīkṛta-prema-bhakti-pracāraḥ |
lasat-tapta-kārttasvara-śrīmad-aṅga-
cchaṭā-cchanna-lāvaṇya-tāruṇya-bhaṅgaḥ ||42||*

**Word for word:** *trilokī* — over the three worlds; *sphurat* — spreading; *kīrtti* — of fame; *pīyūṣa* — of nectar; *dhāraḥ* — [whose] stream; *prakāśīkṛta* — who has made manifest; *prema-bhakti* — of loving devotion; *pracāraḥ* — the propagation; *lasat* — shining; *tapta* — molten; *kārttasvara* — [like] gold; *śrīmat* — beautiful; *aṅga* — of the body; *chaṭā* — by the streams; *channa* — covered; *lāvaṇya* — of loveliness; *tāruṇya* — of youth; *bhaṅgaḥ* — [whose] waves.

**43.**

```
नदन्मन्दिरावृन्दरिङ्गन्मृदङ्गैः
समुद्यन्महोल्लासपाथोधिभङ्गैः ।
मुहुर्गायनैर्मुग्धसङ्गीतभङ्गी-
समुत्कण्ठकण्ठैः सदानन्दसङ्गी ॥४३॥
```
*nadan-mandirā-vṛnda-riṅgan-mṛdaṅgaiḥ
samudyan-mahollāsa-pāthodhi-bhaṅgaiḥ |
muhur gāyanair mugdha-saṅgīta-bhaṅgī-
samutkaṇṭha-kaṇṭhaiḥ sadānanda-saṅgī ||43||*

**Word for word:** *nadat* — ringing; *mandirā* — of cymbals; *vṛnda* — with a host; *riṅgat* — rumbling; *mṛdaṅgaiḥ* — with mridangas; *samudyat* — surging; *mahā* — of great; *ullāsa* — exultation; *pāthodhi* — of the ocean; *bhaṅgaiḥ* — with the waves; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *gāyanaiḥ* — with singers; *mugdha* — of charming; *saṅgīta* — melodies; *bhaṅgī* — in the modulations; *samutkaṇṭha* — ardent; *kaṇṭhaiḥ* — [whose] voices; *sadā* — always; *ānanda* — with bliss; *saṅgī* — accompanied.

**44.**

```
जगन्नाथदेवं विमुग्धं स्वलास्यै-
र्विलोक्यातिहर्षाश्रुघर्माम्बुहासैः ।
रसोत्कर्षतो निःसहश्रीमदङ्गः
सदा रज्यदाकुञ्चितापाङ्गभङ्गैः ॥४४॥
```
*jagannātha-devaṁ vimugdhaṁ sva-lāsyair
vilokyāti-harṣāśru-gharmāmbu-hāsaiḥ |
rasotkarṣato niḥsaha-śrīmad-aṅgaḥ
sadā rajyad-ākuñcitāpāṅga-bhaṅgaiḥ ||44||*

**Word for word:** *jagannātha-devam* — Lord Jagannatha; *vimugdham* — enchanted; *sva* — by His own; *lāsyaiḥ* — dances; *vilokya* — having seen; *ati* — excessive; *harṣa* — of joy; *aśru* — with tears; *gharma-ambu* — with sweat; *hāsaiḥ* — [and] with laughter; *rasa* — of rasa; *utkarṣataḥ* — from the excess; *niḥsaha* — exhausted; *śrīmat* — beautiful; *aṅgaḥ* — [whose] body; *sadā* — always; *rajyat* — reddening; *ākuñcita* — narrowed; *apāṅga* — of the corners of the eyes; *bhaṅgaiḥ* — with the curves.

**45.**

```
पुरस्थेन नीलाद्रिमौलीश्वरेण
स्वलास्यावलोकास्थिरात्यस्थिरेण ।
निमेषं दृशोः कर्त्तुमप्यक्षमेण
प्रमत्तीकृतो भूरिहर्षोद्गमेन ॥४५॥
```
*purasthena nīlādri-maulīśvareṇa
sva-lāsyāvalokāsthirāty-asthireṇa |
nimeṣaṁ dṛśoḥ karttum apy akṣameṇa
pramattīkṛto bhūri-harṣodgamena ||45||*

**Word for word:** *purasthena* — standing in front; *nīla-adri* — of the Blue Mountain; *mauli* — the Crown; *īśvareṇa* — by the Lord; *sva* — His; *lāsya* — dance; *avaloka* — to behold; *asthira* — impatient; *ati-asthireṇa* — exceedingly restless; *nimeṣam* — a blink; *dṛśoḥ* — of the eyes; *karttum* — to make; *api* — even; *akṣameṇa* — unable; *pramattīkṛtaḥ* — made intoxicated; *bhūri* — abundant; *harṣa* — of joy; *udgamena* — by the upsurge.

**46.**

```
विलोलाननाम्भोजलीलाविलासः
स्फुरच्छीत्कृतोद्भासिरोमप्रकाशः ।
अपूर्वं त्रिलोकीं प्रति प्रेमपाथः-
प्रदो गुण्डिचायां नरीनर्त्ति नाथः ॥४६॥ (कुलकम्)
```
*vilolānanāmbhoja-līlā-vilāsaḥ
sphurac-chītkṛtodbhāsi-roma-prakāśaḥ |
apūrvaṁ trilokīṁ prati prema-pāthaḥ-
prado guṇḍicāyāṁ narīnartti nāthaḥ ||46|| (kulakam)*

**Word for word:** *vilola* — mobile; *ānana* — of the face; *ambhoja* — of the lotus; *līlā* — of the play; *vilāsaḥ* — [whose] grace; *sphurat* — bursting forth; *śītkṛta* — [with] the hissing intakes of breath; *udbhāsi* — shining; *roma* — of the hairs; *prakāśaḥ* — [whose] manifestation; *apūrvam* — in unprecedented measure; *trilokīm prati* — to the three worlds; *prema* — of love; *pāthaḥ* — the water; *pradaḥ* — giving; *guṇḍicāyām* — towards the Gundicha; *narīnartti* — dances and dances; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord.

**Translation (37–46).** As He sets down the pair of His lotus feet, their sweetness flashing forth washes away the beauty of the red lotus; His sturdy thighs have the grace of swaying, lovely plantain trees, and the rounded curves of His hips shine. With the redness of the pure splendour of His cloth, vividly red like clusters of opening hibiscus buds, He has coloured the three worlds red; the play of bliss has risen in Him, and in the flashing tandava His staff-like arms are raised in play. He is most lovely with the lustre of the beautiful waist-cord that closely clasps His quivering waist, which a fist could span; His chest is bathed in a heavy stream of sweat, and [over His body is] a beautiful, unrestrained flood of hairs standing on end. He carries a stream of that bliss resembling the ocean of milk, whose course brings transformations; with the relishes of the play of His eyes, graceful as the play of darting bees, He wonderfully makes for the people the heart of a lotus. In Him the manifestation of stupor, hairs standing on end and trembling is accompanied by the adorning manifestation of a swoon of bliss; from the excess of the manifestation of irresistible feeling, the flashing streams of the radiance of His body have covered the world. The nectar stream of His fame spreads over the three worlds; He has made manifest the propagation of loving devotion; the waves of His loveliness and youth are covered by the streams of radiance of His beautiful body, shining like molten gold. Accompanied by mridangas rumbling amid a host of ringing cymbals, by the surging waves of an ocean of great exultation, and by singers whose voices again and again are ardent in the modulations of charming melodies, He is ever full of bliss. Seeing Lord Jagannatha enchanted by His dances, He [is overcome] with tears of excessive joy, with sweat and with laughter; from the excess of rasa His beautiful body is exhausted, and the curves of the corners of His eyes are ever narrowed and reddening. Intoxicated by the Lord, the Crown of the Blue Mountain, who stands before Him, exceedingly restless in His impatience to behold His dance and unable even to blink His eyes — [intoxicated] by an abundant upsurge of joy; with the grace of the play of His mobile lotus face, with the shining manifestation of His hairs [standing on end] amid bursting hissing intakes of breath, giving in unprecedented measure the water of love to the three worlds, the Lord dances and dances [on the way] to the Gundicha.

**Note.** Metre of 37–47: *bhujaṅga-prayāta* (⏑——×4; 12 syllables per pada). Verses 37–46 are a *kulaka*: one sentence, with the verb *narīnartti* (an intensive of *nṛt*) in 46. In 38 the edition breaks *raktāṁ / śukaṁ* at the junction of the padas; we read the compound *raktāṁśuka-svaccha-…* (the metre is the same); *anuraktām*, "coloured red", also means "filled with love". In 40 *abja-garbham*, "the heart of a lotus", is unclear ⟨?⟩: perhaps His bee-like glances make the people, as it were, the heart of a lotus in which they sport; another understanding is that the devotees see His eyes as the heart of a lotus. *Rāma-rambhā* (37) — "a lovely plantain tree". In 45 the instrumentals (*purasthena … īśvareṇa … akṣameṇa*) are taken as the agent of *pramattīkṛtaḥ*, together with *bhūri-harṣodgamena*; *-avalokāsthirāty-asthireṇa* may also be divided *-avalokāt sthirāty-asthireṇa*. *Śītkṛta* (46) — drawing in the breath through the teeth, a sign of rapture. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 47

**47.**

```
विलोक्यास्य लास्यं ललन्माधुरीकं
क्षमो नैष कर्त्तुं निमेषौ दृशोः किम् ।
यदुत्फुल्लपाथोरुहाक्षोऽयमासीत्
समस्तात्मना तत्र मग्नः प्रकामम् ॥४७॥
```
*vilokyāsya lāsyaṁ lalan-mādhurīkaṁ
kṣamo naiṣa karttuṁ nimeṣau dṛśoḥ kim |
yad utphulla-pāthoruhākṣo 'yam āsīt
samastātmanā tatra magnaḥ prakāmam ||47||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *asya* — His; *lāsyam* — dance; *lalat* — playful; *mādhurīkam* — [full of] sweetness; *kṣamaḥ* — able; *na* — not; *eṣaḥ* — He; *karttum* — to make; *nimeṣau* — the blinking; *dṛśoḥ* — of the eyes; *kim* — was it [because]; *yat* — for; *utphulla* — full-blown; *pāthoruha* — [like] lotuses; *akṣaḥ* — [whose] eyes; *ayam* — He; *āsīt* — was; *samasta* — with whole; *ātmanā* — self; *tatra* — in it; *magnaḥ* — immersed; *prakāmam* — utterly.

**Translation.** Was it because He had seen His dance, full of playful sweetness, that He was unable to blink His eyes? For He, whose eyes are full-blown lotuses, was utterly immersed in it with His whole self.

**Note.** "He" is Jagannatha, whose eyes have no lids in any case: the poet plays on His unblinking eyes.

### 48

**48.**

```
अङ्गुल्यग्रैः स्रजमनुपमां चक्रवद्भ्रामयित्वा
हर्षोत्कर्षात् क्षिपति स तथा मण्डले तत्र नृत्यन् ।
इच्छापूर्वं यमनु चकमे चेतसा तस्य कण्ठे
दूरस्थस्यापि च बत तथा राजते चित्रमेतत् ॥४८॥
```
*aṅguly-agraiḥ srajam anupamāṁ cakravad bhrāmayitvā
harṣotkarṣāt kṣipati sa tathā maṇḍale tatra nṛtyan |
icchā-pūrvaṁ yam anu cakame cetasā tasya kaṇṭhe
dūrasthasyāpi ca bata tathā rājate citram etat ||48||*

**Word for word:** *aṅguli* — of the fingers; *agraiḥ* — with the tips; *srajam* — the garland; *anupamām* — incomparable; *cakra-vat* — like a wheel; *bhrāmayitvā* — having whirled; *harṣa* — of joy; *utkarṣāt* — from the excess; *kṣipati* — throws; *saḥ* — He; *tathā* — so; *maṇḍale* — in the circle; *tatra* — there; *nṛtyan* — dancing; *icchā-pūrvam* — with desire; *yam* — whom; *anu cakame* — wished for; *cetasā* — in heart; *tasya* — of him; *kaṇṭhe* — on the neck; *dūrasthasya* — standing far off; *api ca* — even though; *bata* — ah!; *tathā* — so; *rājate* — shines; *citram* — a wonder; *etat* — this.

**Translation.** Dancing there in the circle, He whirls the incomparable garland like a wheel with His fingertips and, from the excess of joy, throws it so that it shines on the neck of the one whom He had wished for in His heart, even though that one stood far off. Ah, what a wonder this is!

**Note.** Metre: *mandākrāntā*. Another understanding: the garland falls on the neck of Jagannatha Himself.

### 49

**49.**

```
इत्येवं बहुधा विधाय नटनं रम्यं शचीनन्दनः
श्रीनीलाचलमौलिनीलतिलकस्याग्रे पथि प्रेमवान् ।
दृष्ट्वा तन्मुखचन्द्रसुन्दररुचिं पीयूषवच्छीतल-
मानन्दाम्बुनिधौ ममज्ज सुभृशं सार्द्धं निजाङ्घ्रिप्रियैः ॥४९॥
```
*ity evaṁ bahudhā vidhāya naṭanaṁ ramyaṁ śacīnandanaḥ
śrī-nīlācala-mauli-nīla-tilakasyāgre pathi premavān |
dṛṣṭvā tan-mukha-candra-sundara-ruciṁ pīyūṣavac chītalam
ānandāmbunidhau mamajja subhṛśaṁ sārdhaṁ nijāṅghri-priyaiḥ ||49||*

**Word for word:** *iti evam* — thus; *bahudhā* — in many ways; *vidhāya* — having performed; *naṭanam* — the dance; *ramyam* — lovely; *śacī-nandanaḥ* — the son of Shachi; *śrī-nīlācala* — of Sri Nilachala; *mauli* — on the crown; *nīla* — blue; *tilakasya* — of the ornament; *agre* — in front; *pathi* — on the road; *premavān* — full of love; *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *tat* — His; *mukha* — of the face; *candra* — of the moon; *sundara* — beautiful; *rucim* — the radiance; *pīyūṣa-vat* — like nectar; *śītalam* — cool; *ānanda* — of bliss; *ambunidhau* — in the ocean; *mamajja* — sank; *subhṛśam* — deeply; *sārdham* — together; *nija* — His own; *aṅghri* — the feet; *priyaiḥ* — with those to whom [are] dear.

**Translation.** Having thus performed in many ways a lovely dance on the road in front of the Blue Ornament on the crown of Sri Nilachala, the son of Shachi, full of love, beheld the beautiful radiance of His moonlike face, cool as nectar, and sank deep into the ocean of bliss together with those to whom His feet are dear.

**Note.** Metre: *śārdūla-vikrīḍita*.
