# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Eighteen — verses 1–63 (printed pp. 532–545)

**Title of the sarga:** *Śrī-puruṣottama-vihāra-ratha-yātrā-samāptiḥ*, "The Pastimes in Sri Purushottama and the Conclusion of the Ratha-yatra" (from the colophon).
**Metre:**
- 1–62 — *viyoginī* (an *ardha-sama* metre: odd padas *sa-sa-ja-ga*, ⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—, 10 syllables; even padas *sa-bha-ra-la-ga*, ⏑⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—, 11 syllables); in the edition each printed line is a half-verse. In 35 the printed fourth pada has twelve syllables (*satato 'dhikaṇṭhita-*; *satatotkaṇṭhita-* scans); in 40 the first pada has nine syllables (*na ca nirvṛte vilokya taṁ* gives ⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—⏑—: a light syllable is missing in the fourth or fifth place); in 61 the fourth pada has twelve syllables (*vyacakiraj*; the imperfect *vyakirat*, "scattered", scans).
- 63 — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* (*ma-sa-ja-sa-ta-ta-ga*: ———⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑———⏑——⏑—; 19 syllables per pada). The printed Devanagari has *alaṅkṛtimati*, with a light syllable in the twelfth place of the fourth pada, where a heavy one is required; the transliteration gives the metrical *alaṅkṛtimatīṁ*.

**Total verses:** 63.

**Word-for-word conventions:** compound words are divided into their members, each with its own meaning; case is rendered by an English preposition ("of", "with", "in", "by", "to", "from"); the vocative is given with "O". Square brackets enclose a word that is understood (for example, [whose], [like]).

**Contents of the passage:** the end of the Ratha-yatra and the yearly round of the Lord's life in Puri.
- 1–5 — eight days in the garden by the Gundicha; the longing for Vrindavana assuaged.
- 6 — the return procession of Jagannatha.
- 7–15 — the water pastimes in Narendra Lake:
  - the devotees around the Lord form a "lotus" (11);
  - the Lord dozes, lying upon Advaita (13–14).
- 16 — Jagannatha returns to the temple.
- 17–45 — the daily routine:
  - rising in the last watch of the night, bathing, the red cloth;
  - darshan from the first service until the *dhupa*, with Govinda and Svarupa Damodara; tears and unblinking eyes;
  - Advaita and the Lord worship each other (30–32); Srivasa, "Narada" (33);
  - visitors received when Govinda announces them; the midday rites and the meal.
- 46–51 — the twelve yearly festivals; the festival of Makara: the Lord in the guise of a cowherd with a carrying-pole, twirling a staff (48–50).
- 52–59 — the Dola-yatra: coloured powders; Govinda, the festival Deity, is swung on the swing, and the Lord dances.
- 60–62 — every year at the Ratha-yatra He dances before the chariot of Baladeva, "the support of the world"; twenty years.
- 63 — during this time He goes to the bank of the Bhagirathi in order to go to Mathura (the transition to Sarga Nineteen).

**Textual cruxes:** 10 (*-lālasa-*), 11 (*sthitā*), 16 (*bhramadam*), 23 (*-vadanenduḥ*), 34 (*sunivāritāḥ*), 35 (*satato 'dhikaṇṭhita-*), 37 (*dadṛśu*), 38 (*kayāca netarān*), 40, 43 (*-kriyāḥ*), 57, 61 (*vyacakirat*), 63 (*alaṅkṛtimati*) — see the notes. Other metrical irregularity of the printed text: 40 (see above).

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
अथ तत्र रथोत्सवे प्रभुः स्वजनेनैव विलस्य भूयशः ।
मुहुरष्टसु वासरेषु च प्रमुमोदोपवने स कौतुकी ॥१॥
```
*atha tatra rathotsave prabhuḥ svajanenaiva vilasya bhūyaśaḥ |
muhur aṣṭasu vāsareṣu ca pramumodopavane sa kautukī ||1||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *tatra* — there; *ratha* — of the chariot; *utsave* — at the festival; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *sva-janena eva* — with His own people; *vilasya* — having sported; *bhūyaśaḥ* — abundantly; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *aṣṭasu* — for eight; *vāsareṣu* — days; *ca* — and; *pramumoda* — rejoiced; *upavane* — in the garden; *saḥ* — He; *kautukī* — full of delight.

**Translation.** Then, having sported abundantly there at the Chariot Festival with His own people, the Lord, full of delight, rejoiced again and again in the garden for eight days.

**Note.** Metre — *viyoginī* (odd padas ⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—, even padas ⏑⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—); in the edition each printed line is a half-verse. The eight days are the days during which Jagannatha stays in the Gundicha temple; according to the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 14.104–105) He stayed there nine days, and for all nine days the Lord sported in the large garden by the Gundicha.

### 2

**2.**

```
इह रम्यसरःसु सस्पृहं विहितस्नानविधिर्यथायथम् ।
अवलोक्य सितेतराचलद्युमणिं राजति तत्र तत्र सः ॥२॥
```
*iha ramya-saraḥsu saspṛhaṁ vihita-snāna-vidhir yathāyatham |
avalokya sitetarācala-dyumaṇiṁ rājati tatra tatra saḥ ||2||*

**Word for word:** *iha* — here; *ramya* — lovely; *saraḥsu* — in the ponds; *sa-spṛham* — eagerly; *vihita* — [who had] performed; *snāna* — of bathing; *vidhiḥ* — the rite; *yathāyatham* — as is proper; *avalokya* — having beheld; *sita-itara* — of the not-white (the Dark); *acala* — Mountain; *dyumaṇim* — the Sun; *rājati* — shines; *tatra tatra* — here and there; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** Here, having eagerly performed the rite of bathing in the lovely ponds as is proper, and having beheld the Sun of the Not-white Mountain, He shines here and there.

**Note.** "The Not-white Mountain" is a periphrasis for the Dark Mountain (Nilachala).

### 3

**3.**

```
प्रतिभूरुहमूलमुल्लसन् प्रतिवल्लि प्रतिकुञ्जमञ्जसा ।
प्रतिसैकतरञ्जितस्थलं विलसन् भ्राजति तत्र तत्र सः ॥३॥
```
*prati-bhūruha-mūlam ullasan prati-valli prati-kuñjam añjasā |
prati-saikata-rañjita-sthalaṁ vilasan bhrājati tatra tatra saḥ ||3||*

**Word for word:** *prati* — at every; *bhūruha* — of tree; *mūlam* — the root; *ullasan* — rejoicing; *prati-valli* — by every creeper; *prati-kuñjam* — in every bower; *añjasā* — freely; *prati* — on every; *saikata* — with sand; *rañjita* — adorned; *sthalam* — spot; *vilasan* — sporting; *bhrājati* — shines; *tatra tatra* — here and there; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** Rejoicing at the root of every tree, by every creeper, in every bower, and freely sporting on every spot adorned with sand, He shines here and there.

### 4

**4.**

```
विलसत्कलकण्ठकाकलीं कलयन् कोमलचित्तवृत्तिकः ।
मधुरं मधुपोत्करध्वनिं श्रवणेनैव पिबन् विराजते ॥४॥
```
*vilasat-kalakaṇṭha-kākalīṁ kalayan komala-citta-vṛttikaḥ |
madhuraṁ madhupotkara-dhvaniṁ śravaṇenaiva piban virājate ||4||*

**Word for word:** *vilasat* — playful; *kalakaṇṭha* — of the cuckoos; *kākalīm* — the soft warbling; *kalayan* — listening to; *komala* — tender; *citta* — of heart; *vṛttikaḥ* — [whose] disposition [was]; *madhuram* — sweet; *madhupa* — of bees; *utkara* — of the swarm; *dhvanim* — the hum; *śravaṇena eva* — with the very ear; *piban* — drinking; *virājate* — shines.

**Translation.** Listening to the playful warbling of the cuckoos, His heart tenderly disposed, and drinking with His very ears the sweet hum of the swarms of bees, He shines.

### 5

**5.**

```
इह तत्तददभ्रविभ्रमैर्भ्रममाणः स इतस्ततो मुहुः ।
विजहौ हृदयस्य कर्षणं चिरवृन्दावनविप्रयोगजम् ॥५॥
```
*iha tat-tad-adabhra-vibhramair bhramamāṇaḥ sa itas tato muhuḥ |
vijahau hṛdayasya karṣaṇaṁ cira-vṛndāvana-viprayogajam ||5||*

**Word for word:** *iha* — here; *tat-tat* — with these and those; *adabhra* — no small; *vibhramaiḥ* — with charms; *bhramamāṇaḥ* — wandering; *saḥ* — He; *itaḥ tataḥ* — hither and thither; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *vijahau* — cast off; *hṛdayasya* — of the heart; *karṣaṇam* — the torment; *cira* — long; *vṛndāvana* — from Vrindavana; *viprayoga* — from separation; *jam* — born.

**Translation.** Wandering here hither and thither again and again amid all those charms of no small degree, He cast off the torment of His heart born of long separation from Vrindavana.

**Note.** For the Lord the garden by the Gundicha is Vrindavana; cf. the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 14.73–75, 96: in these days the Lord feels that Krishna has returned to Vrindavana, and with the devotees He manifests the pastimes of Vrindavana in the gardens around the Gundicha temple (Sundarachala, 14.113).

### 6

**6.**

```
अथ तस्य बहिर्विहारतो विजये नीलगिरौ जगत्पतेः ।
स तथैव परिच्छदोत्करैरभवत् सर्वजनप्रमोदकृत् ॥६॥
```
*atha tasya bahir-vihārato vijaye nīla-girau jagat-pateḥ |
sa tathaiva paricchadotkarair abhavat sarva-jana-pramoda-kṛt ||6||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *tasya* — of Him; *bahiḥ* — outside [the temple]; *vihārataḥ* — after the excursion; *vijaye* — at the triumphal return; *nīla-girau* — to the Blue Mountain; *jagat-pateḥ* — of the Lord of the world; *saḥ* — He; *tathā eva* — in the same way; *paricchada* — of His retinue; *utkaraiḥ* — with the throngs; *abhavat* — became; *sarva* — of all; *jana* — the people; *pramoda* — of joy; *kṛt* — the maker.

**Translation.** Then, at the triumphal return of the Lord of the world to the Blue Mountain after His excursion outside [the temple], He, in the same way, with throngs of His retinue, became the source of joy for all the people.

**Note.** Apparently the return procession of the chariots (*bahuda-yatra*). *Paricchada* can also be understood as "paraphernalia" ("He gladdened everyone with various paraphernalia"). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 7

**7.**

```
नववासरमध्यतः प्रभुः स नरेन्द्राख्यसरोवरे ततः ।
स्वजनैः सह तोयखेलनं सममद्वैतमहात्मनाकरोत् ॥७॥
```
*nava-vāsara-madhyataḥ prabhuḥ sa narendrākhya-sarovare tataḥ |
svajanaiḥ saha toya-khelanaṁ samam advaita-mahātmanākarot ||7||*

**Word for word:** *nava* — of nine; *vāsara* — days; *madhyataḥ* — in the course; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *saḥ* — He; *narendra* — Narendra; *ākhya* — called; *sarovare* — in the lake; *tataḥ* — then; *svajanaiḥ saha* — with His own people; *toya* — in the water; *khelanam* — sporting; *samam* — together; *advaita* — with Advaita; *mahā-ātmanā* — the great soul; *akarot* — performed.

**Translation.** Then, in the course of the nine days, the Lord sported in the water of the lake called Narendra with His own people and together with the great soul Advaita.

**Note.** Cf. the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 14.76–91 — the water pastimes (in the Chaitanya-charitamrita, in a lake by the Gundicha, and in Narendra Lake, 14.102).

### 8

**8.**

```
उपगम्य नरेन्द्रसंज्ञकां सरसीं तां सरसीरुहेक्षणः ।
कुतुकेन निदाघशान्तये स ललम्बे निजभक्तवत्सलः ॥८॥
```
*upagamya narendra-saṁjñakāṁ sarasīṁ tāṁ sarasīruhekṣaṇaḥ |
kutukena nidāgha-śāntaye sa lalambe nija-bhakta-vatsalaḥ ||8||*

**Word for word:** *upagamya* — having approached; *narendra* — Narendra; *saṁjñakām* — named; *sarasīm* — the lake; *tām* — that; *sarasīruha* — like lotuses; *īkṣaṇaḥ* — [whose] eyes [are]; *kutukena* — with delight; *nidāgha* — the summer heat; *śāntaye* — to allay; *saḥ* — He; *lalambe* — descended [into it]; *nija* — to His own; *bhakta* — devotees; *vatsalaḥ* — affectionate.

**Translation.** Approaching that lake named Narendra, He, the lotus-eyed, affectionate to His own devotees, descended [into it] with delight to allay the summer heat.

**Note.** A play on sound (*yamaka*): *sarasīṁ tāṁ sarasīruhekṣaṇaḥ*.

### 9–10

**9.**

```
अरुणारुणपादपङ्कजो द्रुतचामीकरगौरविग्रहः ।
करुणारुणलोचनद्वयस्त्रिविधोत्तापविरामकृत् सदा ॥९॥
```
*aruṇāruṇa-pāda-paṅkajo druta-cāmīkara-gaura-vigrahaḥ |
karuṇāruṇa-locana-dvayas trividhottāpa-virāma-kṛt sadā ||9||*

**Word for word:** *aruṇa-aruṇa* — deep red (lit. red-red); *pāda* — of the feet; *paṅkajaḥ* — [whose] lotuses [are]; *druta* — molten; *cāmīkara* — like gold; *gaura* — golden; *vigrahaḥ* — [whose] form [is]; *karuṇā* — with compassion; *aruṇa* — reddened; *locana* — of eyes; *dvayaḥ* — [whose] pair [is]; *trividha* — threefold; *uttāpa* — of the burning; *virāma* — the cessation; *kṛt* — bringing about; *sadā* — always.

**10.**

```
अवलम्ब्य स इत्थमञ्जसा सरसीं सारससालसेक्षणः ।
क्षणवान् जलकेलिकौतुके सह तैस्तैरमृतांशुवद्बभौ ॥१०॥ (युग्मकम्)
```
*avalambya sa ittham añjasā sarasīṁ sārasa-sālasekṣaṇaḥ |
kṣaṇavān jala-keli-kautuke saha tais tair amṛtāṁśuvad babhau ||10|| (yugmakam)*

**Word for word:** *avalambya* — having entered (lit. having resorted to); *saḥ* — He; *ittham* — thus; *añjasā* — at once; *sarasīm* — the lake; *sārasa* — like lotuses; *sa-alasa* — languid; *īkṣaṇaḥ* — [whose] eyes [were]; *kṣaṇavān* — celebrating a festival; *jala* — in the water; *keli* — of sporting; *kautuke* — in the delight; *saha* — together; *taiḥ taiḥ* — with all of them (lit. with these and those); *amṛtāṁśu-vat* — like the moon; *babhau* — shone.

**Translation (9–10).** His lotus feet deep red, His form golden like molten gold, His two eyes reddened with compassion, ever bringing to an end the threefold burning [of suffering] — having thus at once entered the lake, He, His eyes languid like lotuses, celebrating the delight of sporting in the water together with all of them, shone like the moon.

**Note.** A *yugmaka* (so marked in the edition). The edition reads *सारसलालसेक्षणः* (*-lālasa-*, "longing"?), which is unclear; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *sārasa-sālasekṣaṇaḥ* ("with languid, lotus-like eyes"), and this reading is adopted (the metre is the same). Note the rhyme *aruṇāruṇa / karuṇāruṇa*. The threefold suffering: that caused by one's own body and mind, by other beings, and by the forces of nature.

### 11

**11.**

```
कतरे दलसञ्चयाः परे नवकिञ्जल्कचया इव स्थिता(ः) ।
स्वयमेव वराटकाकृतिः स बभौ गौरशशी च पद्मवत् ॥११॥
```
*katare dala-sañcayāḥ pare nava-kiñjalka-cayā iva sthitāḥ |
svayam eva varāṭakākṛtiḥ sa babhau gaura-śaśī ca padmavat ||11||*

**Word for word:** *katare* — some; *dala* — of petals; *sañcayāḥ* — clusters; *pare* — others; *nava* — of fresh; *kiñjalka* — filaments; *cayāḥ* — clusters; *iva* — like; *sthitāḥ* — stood; *svayam eva* — Himself; *varāṭaka* — of the seed-pod; *ākṛtiḥ* — [having] the form; *saḥ* — He; *babhau* — shone; *gaura-śaśī* — Gaurachandra (the golden moon); *ca* — and; *padma-vat* — like a lotus.

**Translation.** Some stood [around Him] like clusters of petals, others like clusters of fresh filaments, and Gaurachandra Himself, in the form of the seed-pod, shone [with them] like a lotus.

**Note.** *Varāṭaka* is the seed-pod (*karṇikā*) in the centre of a lotus. The edition prints *sthitā(ḥ)*, the visarga supplied in brackets; the plural *sthitāḥ* is required.

### 12

**12.**

```
करवारिभिरस्य के च ते सिषिचुस्तत्पदपङ्कजं मृदु ।
कतरे नयनाब्जरन्ध्रकैरिह तद्रूपसुधाः समापिबन् ॥१२॥
```
*kara-vāribhir asya ke ca te siṣicus tat-pada-paṅkajaṁ mṛdu |
katare nayanābja-randhrakair iha tad-rūpa-sudhāḥ samāpiban ||12||*

**Word for word:** *kara* — from the hands; *vāribhiḥ* — with water; *asya* — His; *ke ca te* — some; *siṣicuḥ* — sprinkled; *tat* — His; *pada* — foot; *paṅkajam* — lotus; *mṛdu* — tender; *katare* — others; *nayana* — of the eyes; *abja* — of the lotuses; *randhrakaiḥ* — through the openings; *iha* — here; *tat* — His; *rūpa* — of beauty; *sudhāḥ* — the nectars; *samāpiban* — drank.

**Translation.** Some sprinkled His tender lotus feet with water from their hands; others here drank the nectar of His beauty through the openings of their lotus eyes.

### 13

**13.**

```
स तु भूरिविलासकौतुकं रचयन्निन्दुमुखः कृपानिधिः ।
शयितं कुतुकेन संश्रितः सुखमद्वैततनुं व्यरोचत ॥१३॥
```
*sa tu bhūri-vilāsa-kautukaṁ racayann indu-mukhaḥ kṛpā-nidhiḥ |
śayitaṁ kutukena saṁśritaḥ sukham advaita-tanuṁ vyarocata ||13||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ tu* — but He; *bhūri* — abundant; *vilāsa* — of pastimes; *kautukam* — the delight; *racayan* — creating; *indu* — moon[-like]; *mukhaḥ* — [whose] face [is]; *kṛpā-nidhiḥ* — the treasure-house of mercy; *śayitam* — lying; *kutukena* — playfully; *saṁśritaḥ* — resting upon; *sukham* — comfortably; *advaita* — of Advaita; *tanum* — the body; *vyarocata* — shone.

**Translation.** But He, the moon-faced Treasure-house of Mercy, creating the abundant delight of His pastimes, playfully and comfortably rested upon the body of Advaita, who lay [on the water], and shone.

**Note.** Cf. the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 14.88–90: Advaita lies on the water like Shesha, and the Lord reclines upon Him (the pastime of the Lord lying upon Shesha).

### 14

**14.**

```
सुनिपात्य कृपानिधिस्तदा प्रभुमद्वैतमधो जलान्तरे ।
तदुपर्यपि सालसः स्वयं परिसुप्तः स ययौ सनिद्रताम् ॥१४॥
```
*sunipātya kṛpānidhis tadā prabhum advaitam adho jalāntare |
tad-upary api sālasaḥ svayaṁ parisuptaḥ sa yayau sanidratām ||14||*

**Word for word:** *sunipātya* — having laid down well; *kṛpā-nidhiḥ* — the treasure-house of mercy; *tadā* — then; *prabhum* — the lord; *advaitam* — Advaita; *adhaḥ* — beneath; *jala-antare* — in the water; *tat-upari* — upon Him; *api* — and; *sa-alasaḥ* — languid; *svayam* — Himself; *parisuptaḥ* — lying down; *saḥ* — He; *yayau* — went; *sa-nidratām* — into a doze.

**Translation.** Then, having laid Lord Advaita down beneath [Him] in the water, the Treasure-house of Mercy, Himself languid, lay down upon Him and fell into a doze.

**Note.** Advaita is called *prabhu*, the usual title of Advaita Prabhu.

### 15

**15.**

```
इति भूय इहैव विभ्रमं रचयित्वा तटमुद्ययौ प्रभुः ।
विगलज्जलबिन्दुसुन्दरं वसनं बिभ्रदुपात्तकौतुकः ॥१५॥
```
*iti bhūya ihaiva vibhramaṁ racayitvā taṭam udyayau prabhuḥ |
vigalaj-jala-bindu-sundaraṁ vasanaṁ bibhrad upātta-kautukaḥ ||15||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *bhūyaḥ* — abundantly; *iha eva* — right here; *vibhramam* — play; *racayitvā* — having created; *taṭam* — onto the bank; *udyayau* — came up; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *vigalat* — trickling; *jala* — of water; *bindu* — with drops; *sundaram* — beautiful; *vasanam* — cloth; *bibhrat* — wearing; *upātta* — [who had] gained; *kautukaḥ* — delight.

**Translation.** Having thus played abundantly right here, the Lord, full of delight, came up onto the bank, wearing His cloth, beautiful with trickling drops of water.

---

### 16

**16.**

```
अथ नीलगिरीन्द्रचन्द्रमा विदधेऽन्तर्विजयं तथैव सः ।
स तथैव शचीतनूभवः परिलोच्य भ्रमदं ययौ मुहुः ॥१६॥
```
*atha nīla-girīndra-candramā vidadhe 'ntar-vijayaṁ tathaiva saḥ |
sa tathaiva śacī-tanūbhavaḥ parilocya bhramadaṁ (pramadaṁ?) yayau muhuḥ ||16||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *nīla* — Blue; *giri-indra* — of the king of mountains; *candramāḥ* — the Moon; *vidadhe* — made; *antaḥ* — inward; *vijayam* — the triumphal procession; *tathā eva* — in the same way; *saḥ* — He; *saḥ* — He; *tathā eva* — likewise; *śacī* — of Shachi; *tanūbhavaḥ* — the son; *parilocya* — beholding [it]; *bhramadam* (read *pramadam*) — joy; *yayau* — attained; *muhuḥ* — again and again.

**Translation.** Then the Moon of the Blue King of Mountains in the same way made His triumphal procession inward [into the temple], and the son of Shachi, likewise beholding it, was filled with joy again and again.

**Note.** The edition has भ्रमदं (*bhramadam*); we read *pramadam* ("joy"), which gives the same metre ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text. "The procession inward" is Jagannatha's return to the main temple.

---

### 17

**17.**

```
प्रथमावसरं जगत्पतेः प्रयतो द्रष्टुमसौ शचीसुतः ।
शयनात् प्रहरे समुद्ययौ क्षणदायाश्चरमे कृपानिधिः ॥१७॥
```
*prathamāvasaraṁ jagat-pateḥ prayato draṣṭum asau śacī-sutaḥ |
śayanāt prahare samudyayau kṣaṇadāyāś carame kṛpā-nidhiḥ ||17||*

**Word for word:** *prathama* — the first; *avasaram* — service; *jagat-pateḥ* — of the Lord of the world; *prayataḥ* — intent; *draṣṭum* — to see; *asau* — He; *śacī-sutaḥ* — the son of Shachi; *śayanāt* — from His bed; *prahare* — in the watch; *samudyayau* — would rise; *kṣaṇadāyāḥ* — of the night; *carame* — in the last; *kṛpā-nidhiḥ* — the treasure-house of mercy.

**Translation.** Intent on seeing the first service of the Lord of the world, the son of Shachi, the Treasure-house of Mercy, would rise from His bed in the last watch of the night.

**Note.** From here begins the Lord's daily routine in Puri. *Avasara* is a temple term: one of the successive periods of service to Jagannatha; a *prahara* (watch) is about three hours.

---

### 18

**18.**

```
शयनात् स तथा शचीसुतः प्रभुरुत्थाय विभोर्दिदृक्षया ।
विविधं विदधे विधानतः स तु दैनन्दिनकर्म निर्मलम् ॥१८॥
```
*śayanāt sa tathā śacī-sutaḥ prabhur utthāya vibhor didṛkṣayā |
vividhaṁ vidadhe vidhānataḥ sa tu dainandina-karma nirmalam ||18||*

**Word for word:** *śayanāt* — from bed; *saḥ* — He; *tathā* — thus; *śacī-sutaḥ* — the son of Shachi; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *utthāya* — having risen; *vibhoḥ* — the all-pervading Lord; *didṛkṣayā* — with the desire to see; *vividham* — various; *vidadhe* — performed; *vidhānataḥ* — according to the rules; *saḥ tu* — He; *dainandina* — daily; *karma* — duties; *nirmalam* — pure.

**Translation.** Having thus risen from bed with the desire to see the All-pervading Lord, the Lord, the son of Shachi, would perform His various pure daily duties according to the rules.

---

### 19

**19.**

```
विमलैः सलिलैः परिष्कृतैर्विहितस्नानविधिर्महाप्रभुः ।
कटिसूत्रसमेतमञ्जसा वरवासः स दधार लोहितम् ॥१९॥
```
*vimalaiḥ salilaiḥ pariṣkṛtair vihita-snāna-vidhir mahāprabhuḥ |
kaṭi-sūtra-sametam añjasā vara-vāsaḥ sa dadhāra lohitam ||19||*

**Word for word:** *vimalaiḥ* — with clear; *salilaiḥ* — waters; *pariṣkṛtaiḥ* — purified; *vihita* — [who had] performed; *snāna* — of bathing; *vidhiḥ* — the rite; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *kaṭi* — waist; *sūtra* — cord; *sametam* — together with; *añjasā* — at once; *vara* — fine; *vāsaḥ* — cloth; *saḥ* — He; *dadhāra* — put on; *lohitam* — red.

**Translation.** Having bathed in clear, purified water, Mahaprabhu would at once put on a fine red cloth together with a waist-cord.

---

### 20

**20.**

```
मदवारणराजविभ्रमो निजनामग्रहणे कृतक्षणः ।
अरुणाम्बरसंवृताङ्गको बहिरेषोऽतिसुखेन निर्ययौ ॥२०॥
```
*mada-vāraṇa-rāja-vibhramo nija-nāma-grahaṇe kṛta-kṣaṇaḥ |
aruṇāmbara-saṁvṛtāṅgako bahir eṣo 'ti-sukhena niryayau ||20||*

**Word for word:** *mada* — intoxicated; *vāraṇa* — of elephants; *rāja* — of the king; *vibhramaḥ* — [with] the grace; *nija* — of His own; *nāma* — name; *grahaṇe* — in the taking; *kṛta* — [who] made; *kṣaṇaḥ* — a festival; *aruṇa* — with crimson; *ambara* — cloth; *saṁvṛta* — covered; *aṅgakaḥ* — [whose] body [was]; *bahiḥ* — outside; *eṣaḥ* — He; *ati-sukhena* — most joyfully; *niryayau* — would go out.

**Translation.** With the grace of an intoxicated king of elephants, making a festival of chanting His own name, His body clad in crimson cloth, He would go out most joyfully.

**Note.** "His own name" is the name of Krishna, which is His own.

---

### 21

**21.**

```
करकं परिगृह्य पाणिना स तु गोविन्दमहामतिस्ततः ।
सततं प्रभुसङ्गसङ्गतः स तु दामोदर इत्यसौ यतिः ॥२१॥
```
*karakaṁ parigṛhya pāṇinā sa tu govinda-mahāmatis tataḥ |
satataṁ prabhu-saṅga-saṅgataḥ sa tu dāmodara ity asau yatiḥ ||21||*

**Word for word:** *karakam* — the water-pot; *parigṛhya* — having taken; *pāṇinā* — in his hand; *saḥ tu* — he; *govinda* — Govinda; *mahā-matiḥ* — the wise (lit. great-minded); *tataḥ* — then; *satatam* — always; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *saṅga* — in the company; *saṅgataḥ* — remaining; *saḥ tu* — and he; *dāmodaraḥ iti* — named Damodara; *asau* — that; *yatiḥ* — renunciant.

**Translation.** Then the wise Govinda, taking the water-pot in his hand, and that renunciant named Damodara, who always remained in the Lord's company, [accompanied Him].

**Note.** The *karaka* is a sannyasi's water-pot (kamandalu). Damodara is Svarupa Damodara.

---

### 22

**22.**

```
निजनामसुधापयोनिधेः सततास्वादलसन्मनोरथः ।
समुपेत्य ततः प्रभोः पुरं प्रविवेश प्रणतिं विधाय सः ॥२२॥
```
*nija-nāma-sudhā-payonidheḥ satatāsvāda-lasan-manorathaḥ |
samupetya tataḥ prabhoḥ puraṁ praviveśa praṇatiṁ vidhāya saḥ ||22||*

**Word for word:** *nija* — of His own; *nāma* — name; *sudhā* — of nectar; *payonidheḥ* — of the ocean; *satata* — constant; *āsvāda* — in the tasting; *lasat* — shining; *manorathaḥ* — [whose] desire [was]; *samupetya* — having approached; *tataḥ* — then; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *puram* — the abode; *praviveśa* — entered; *praṇatim* — obeisance; *vidhāya* — having made; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** Then He, whose heart's desire shone in constantly tasting the nectar-ocean of His own name, approaching the abode of the Lord [Jagannatha] and offering obeisance, would enter [it].

**Note.** Verses 22–25 could also be taken as referring to Damodara ("the renunciant Damodara, tasting the nectar of Hari's name, entered…"). But in verse 26 the same actions are explicitly ascribed to the son of Shachi, and we take the Lord as the subject. "The abode of the Lord" is the temple of Jagannatha.

---

### 23

**23.**

```
असिताचलमौलिचन्द्रमोवदनेन्दुः परिलोक्य निर्भरम् ।
विगलन्नयनाम्बुधारया परिधौताङ्गलतो विराजते ॥२३॥
```
*asitācala-mauli-candramo-vadanenduḥ (-induṁ?) parilokya nirbharam |
vigalan-nayanāmbu-dhārayā paridhautāṅga-lato virājate ||23||*

**Word for word:** *asita* — of the Dark; *acala* — Mountain; *mauli* — on the crown; *candramaḥ* — of the Moon; *vadana* — of the face; *induḥ* (read *indum*) — the moon; *parilokya* — gazing at; *nirbharam* — to the full; *vigalat* — streaming; *nayana* — from the eyes; *ambu* — of water; *dhārayā* — by the stream; *paridhauta* — washed; *aṅga* — of the body; *lataḥ* — [whose] creeper [was]; *virājate* — shines.

**Translation.** Gazing to the full at the moon of the face of the Moon on the crown of the Dark Mountain, He shines, the creeper of His body washed by the stream of tears flowing from His eyes.

**Note.** The edition has the nominative *-vadanenduḥ*; with *parilokya* the accusative *-induṁ* is needed (the metre is the same). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

---

### 24

**24.**

```
ननु नीलगिरीन्द्रचन्द्रमाः परिलोक्यैनमदभ्रविभ्रमम् ।
अभिषिञ्चति तद्विलोचनद्वयनीरैरतिहर्षधर्षितः ॥२४॥
```
*nanu nīla-girīndra-candramāḥ parilokyainam adabhra-vibhramam |
abhiṣiñcati tad-vilocana-dvaya-nīrair ati-harṣa-dharṣitaḥ ||24||*

**Word for word:** *nanu* — is it not that; *nīla* — of the Blue; *giri-indra* — king of mountains; *candramāḥ* — the Moon; *parilokya* — beholding; *enam* — Him; *adabhra* — no small; *vibhramam* — [having] charm; *abhiṣiñcati* — ceremonially bathes; *tat* — His; *vilocana* — of eyes; *dvaya* — of the pair; *nīraiḥ* — with the waters; *ati* — excessive; *harṣa* — by joy; *dharṣitaḥ* — overpowered.

**Translation.** Is it not that the Moon of the Blue King of Mountains, beholding Him so full of charm and overpowered by excessive joy, ceremonially bathes Him with the waters of His own two eyes?

**Note.** A poetic fancy (*utprekṣā*): Gaura's tears are, as it were, a ceremonial bath (*abhiṣeka*) given Him by Jagannatha Himself.

---

### 25

**25.**

```
निमिषेण दुनोति मानसं बहुधेत्यस्य विलोकने प्रभुः ।
असिताचलरत्नमञ्जसा नयने निर्निमिषे चकार किम् ॥२५॥
```
*nimiṣeṇa dunoti mānasaṁ bahudhety asya vilokane prabhuḥ |
asitācala-ratnam añjasā nayane nirnimiṣe cakāra kim ||25||*

**Word for word:** *nimiṣeṇa* — by blinking; *dunoti* — pains; *mānasam* — the heart; *bahudhā* — greatly; *iti* — because; *asya* — of Him; *vilokane* — in the beholding; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *asita* — of the Dark; *acala* — Mountain; *ratnam* — the Jewel; *añjasā* — at once; *nayane* — the [two] eyes; *nirnimiṣe* — unblinking; *cakāra* — made; *kim* — did [He]?

**Translation.** Because, while one beholds Him, blinking greatly pains the heart, did the Lord, the Jewel of the Dark Mountain, at once make [Gaura's] eyes unblinking?

**Note.** Another poetic fancy: unblinking eyes are a mark of the gods.

---

### 26

**26.**

```
स शचीतनुजो निजां तनूमभिषिच्याक्षिपयोभरैर्मुहुः ।
पुलकैर्द्विगुणीभवत्तनुर्मुमुदे हर्षवशस्तथा तथा ॥२६॥
```
*sa śacī-tanujo nijāṁ tanūm abhiṣicyākṣi-payo-bharair muhuḥ |
pulakair dviguṇībhavat-tanur mumude harṣa-vaśas tathā tathā ||26||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — He; *śacī* — of Shachi; *tanujaḥ* — the son; *nijām* — His own; *tanūm* — body; *abhiṣicya* — bathing; *akṣi* — of the eyes; *payaḥ* — of the water; *bharaiḥ* — with floods; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *pulakaiḥ* — with hairs standing on end; *dviguṇībhavat* — becoming doubled; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body [was]; *mumude* — rejoiced; *harṣa* — of joy; *vaśaḥ* — in the power; *tathā tathā* — more and more.

**Translation.** Again and again bathing His own body with floods of tears, His body swelling to double [its size] with hairs standing on end, the son of Shachi, overcome by joy, rejoiced more and more.

---

### 27

**27.**

```
प्रथमावसरं प्रभृत्यथो स तु धूपावधि तत्र सुस्थितः ।
बहुधा प्रणतिप्रदक्षिणान्यपि कृत्वा निजमालयं ययौ ॥२७॥
```
*prathamāvasaraṁ prabhṛty atho sa tu dhūpāvadhi tatra susthitaḥ |
bahudhā praṇati-pradakṣiṇāny api kṛtvā nijam ālayaṁ yayau ||27||*

**Word for word:** *prathama* — the first; *avasaram* — service; *prabhṛti* — beginning with; *atho* — then; *saḥ tu* — He; *dhūpa* — the *dhūpa*; *avadhi* — until; *tatra* — there; *susthitaḥ* — remaining; *bahudhā* — many times; *praṇati* — obeisances; *pradakṣiṇāni* — [and] circumambulations; *api* — also; *kṛtvā* — having made; *nijam* — to His own; *ālayam* — dwelling; *yayau* — went.

**Translation.** Remaining there from the first service until the *dhupa*, and making obeisances and circumambulations many times, He then went to His own dwelling.

**Note.** *Dhūpa* ("incense") is the morning offering to Jagannatha, which follows the first service.

---

### 28

**28.**

```
समुपेत्य निजालयं ततो निजनामानि मुहुर्मुहुर्जपन् ।
उपविश्य रराज चन्द्रवज्जगदाह्लादकरः प्रकाशवत् ॥२८॥
```
*samupetya nijālayaṁ tato nija-nāmāni muhur muhur japan |
upaviśya rarāja candravaj jagad-āhlāda-karaḥ prakāśavat ||28||*

**Word for word:** *samupetya* — having come; *nija* — to His own; *ālayam* — dwelling; *tataḥ* — then; *nija* — His own; *nāmāni* — names; *muhuḥ muhuḥ* — again and again; *japan* — chanting; *upaviśya* — sitting down; *rarāja* — shone; *candra-vat* — like the moon; *jagat* — to the world; *āhlāda* — delight; *karaḥ* — giving; *prakāśa-vat* — radiant.

**Translation.** Then, coming to His own dwelling and sitting down, chanting His own names again and again, He shone like the moon, radiant and giving delight to the world.

---

### 29

**29.**

```
अथ तत्र सुखं गृहान्तरे स्थितवन्तं करुणालयं प्रभुम् ।
परिलोकितुमञ्जसा मुहुः परितः स्वैर्मुदिताः समाययुः ॥२९॥
```
*atha tatra sukhaṁ gṛhāntare sthitavantaṁ karuṇālayaṁ prabhum |
parilokitum añjasā muhuḥ paritaḥ svair muditāḥ samāyayuḥ ||29||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *tatra* — there; *sukham* — at ease; *gṛha* — the house; *antare* — within; *sthitavantam* — staying; *karuṇā* — of compassion; *ālayam* — the abode; *prabhum* — the Lord; *parilokitum* — to see; *añjasā* — straight away; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *paritaḥ* — from all sides; *svaiḥ* — with their own [people]; *muditāḥ* — joyful; *samāyayuḥ* — came together.

**Translation.** Then, to see the Lord, the abode of compassion, who stayed there at ease within the house, [devotees] joyfully came together again and again from all sides, straight away, with their own [people].

---

### 30

**30.**

```
प्रथमं परिगृह्य सादरं प्रभुपूजार्थमुपायनं बहु ।
पुलकाश्रुझराकुलः सुखं प्रभुरद्वैत इहागमत्तदा ॥३०॥
```
*prathamaṁ parigṛhya sādaraṁ prabhu-pūjārtham upāyanaṁ bahu |
pulakāśru-jharākulaḥ sukhaṁ prabhur advaita ihāgamat tadā ||30||*

**Word for word:** *prathamam* — first; *parigṛhya* — having taken; *sa-ādaram* — reverently; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *pūjā* — of the worship; *artham* — for the sake; *upāyanam* — gifts; *bahu* — many; *pulaka* — with hairs standing on end; *aśru* — of tears; *jhara* — [and] with streams; *ākulaḥ* — overwhelmed; *sukham* — joyfully; *prabhuḥ advaitaḥ* — Lord Advaita; *iha* — here; *agamat* — came; *tadā* — then.

**Translation.** Then Lord Advaita came here first, joyfully, reverently bearing many gifts for the worship of the Lord, overwhelmed, His hairs standing on end and His tears streaming.

**Note.** Cf. the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 15.9–11: Advaita adorns the Lord with a garland and tulasi flowers, and the Lord worships Advaita with the remaining tulasi, saying, "Whatever You are, I worship You."

---

### 31

**31.**

```
पदयोर्विनिवेद्य भक्तितः सलिलं शुद्धतमं सुवासितम् ।
मलयोद्भवपङ्कसञ्चयैरथ भालस्थलमालिलेप सः ॥३१॥
```
*padayor vinivedya bhaktitaḥ salilaṁ śuddhatamaṁ suvāsitam |
malayodbhava-paṅka-sañcayair atha bhāla-sthalam ālilepa saḥ ||31||*

**Word for word:** *padayoḥ* — to the feet; *vinivedya* — having offered; *bhaktitaḥ* — with devotion; *salilam* — water; *śuddhatamam* — most pure; *su-vāsitam* — fragrant; *malaya-udbhava* — of [sandalwood] born on Malaya; *paṅka* — of the paste; *sañcayaiḥ* — with an abundance; *atha* — then; *bhāla-sthalam* — the forehead; *ālilepa* — anointed; *saḥ* — He [Advaita].

**Translation.** Having with devotion offered most pure and fragrant water to [the Lord's] feet, He [Advaita] then anointed His forehead with an abundance of the paste of [sandalwood] born on Malaya.

---

### 32

**32.**

```
कुसुमानि मनोहराण्यथो शुचिदूर्वाक्षतसञ्चयं ततः ।
विलिलेप कृपानिधिस्तदा प्रभुरद्वैतविभुं विशेषतः ॥३२॥
```
*kusumāni manoharāṇy atho śuci-dūrvākṣata-sañcayaṁ tataḥ |
vililepa kṛpā-nidhis tadā prabhur advaita-vibhuṁ viśeṣataḥ ||32||*

**Word for word:** *kusumāni* — flowers; *manoharāṇi* — charming; *atho* — then; *śuci* — pure; *dūrvā* — durva grass; *akṣata* — [and] unbroken rice; *sañcayam* — an abundance; *tataḥ* — then; *vililepa* — anointed (= honoured); *kṛpā-nidhiḥ* — the Treasure-house of Mercy; *tadā* — then; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *advaita-vibhum* — the master Advaita; *viśeṣataḥ* — especially.

**Translation.** Then the Lord, the Treasure-house of Mercy, [in His turn] especially honoured the master Advaita with charming flowers, and then with an abundance of pure durva grass and unbroken rice.

**Note.** Durva grass and *akṣata* (unbroken rice) are auspicious offerings. *Vililepa*, "anointed", is used here in the broad sense "honoured".

---

### 33

**33.**

```
अथ भूसुरवंशचन्द्रमाः प्रथितो नारद इत्यसौ भुवि ।
विहितप्रणिपातसंहतिर्नयनाब्जेन तथा समर्चयत् ॥३३॥
```
*atha bhūsura-vaṁśa-candramāḥ prathito nārada ity asau bhuvi |
vihita-praṇipāta-saṁhatir nayanābjena tathā samarcayat ||33||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *bhūsura* — of brahmanas; *vaṁśa* — of the race; *candramāḥ* — the moon; *prathitaḥ* — renowned; *nāradaḥ iti* — as Narada; *asau* — he; *bhuvi* — on earth; *vihita* — having made; *praṇipāta* — of obeisances; *saṁhatiḥ* — a multitude; *nayana* — of the eyes; *abjena* — with the lotus; *tathā* — likewise; *samarcayat* — worshipped.

**Translation.** Then the moon of the race of brahmanas, he who is renowned on earth as Narada, having made a multitude of obeisances, likewise worshipped [the Lord] with the lotuses of his eyes.

**Note.** This is Srivasa Pandita, who is regarded as Narada.

### 34

**34.**

```
अथ ये प्रभुपादपल्लवप्रियभृत्याः सुनिवारिताश्च ते ।
समयात् समुपेत्य सस्पृहं नयनैस्तद्वदनं पपुर्मुहुः ॥३४॥
```
*atha ye prabhu-pāda-pallava-priya-bhṛtyāḥ sunivāritāś ca te |
samayāt samupetya saspṛhaṁ nayanais tad-vadanaṁ papur muhuḥ ||34||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *ye* — who; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *pāda* — of the feet; *pallava* — of the [tender] shoots; *priya* — dear; *bhṛtyāḥ* — servants; *sunivāritāḥ* — [usually] kept back; *ca* — and; *te* — they; *samayāt* — at the [proper] time; *samupetya* — having approached; *sa-spṛham* — eagerly; *nayanaiḥ* — with the eyes; *tat* — His; *vadanam* — face; *papuḥ* — drank; *muhuḥ* — again and again.

**Translation.** Then those dear servants of the Lord's feet, tender as fresh shoots, who were [usually] kept back, approached at the [proper] time and eagerly drank His face again and again with their eyes.

**Note.** The sense of *sunivāritāḥ*, "well restrained, kept back", is unclear ⟨?⟩: perhaps those who [otherwise] held themselves back from the Lord. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 35–36

**35.**

```
इतरे बहवोऽपि सर्वतः समुपेताः प्रभुदर्शनोत्सुकाः ।
सभयस्पृहकौतुकत्रयं सततोऽधिकण्ठितचित्तवृत्तयः ॥३५॥
```
*itare bahavo 'pi sarvataḥ samupetāḥ prabhu-darśanotsukāḥ |
sabhaya-spṛha-kautuka-trayaṁ satato 'dhikaṇṭhita- (satatotkaṇṭhita-) citta-vṛttayaḥ ||35||*

**Word for word:** *itare* — others; *bahavaḥ* — many; *api* — also; *sarvataḥ* — from all sides; *samupetāḥ* — having come; *prabhu* — the Lord; *darśana* — to see; *utsukāḥ* — eager; *sa-bhaya* — with fear; *spṛha* — longing; *kautuka* — [and] curiosity; *trayam* — [with] the triad; *satata* — constantly; *utkaṇṭhita* — yearning; *citta* — of the heart; *vṛttayaḥ* — [whose] movements.

**36.**

```
बहिरेव चिरं सुखोत्करैः स्थितवन्तः सुमहाकृपालयम् ।
ददृशुः क्रमशोऽतिसाध्वसादपि गोविन्दनिवेदनान्तरे ॥३६॥ (युग्मकम्)
```
*bahir eva ciraṁ sukhotkaraiḥ sthitavantaḥ sumahā-kṛpālayam |
dadṛśuḥ kramaśo 'ti-sādhvasād api govinda-nivedanāntare ||36|| (yugmakam)*

**Word for word:** *bahiḥ eva* — just outside; *ciram* — for a long time; *sukha* — of happiness; *utkaraiḥ* — with abundance; *sthitavantaḥ* — having stood; *su-mahā* — the very great; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *ālayam* — abode; *dadṛśuḥ* — saw; *kramaśaḥ* — in turn; *ati* — great; *sādhvasāt* — out of timidity; *api* — though; *govinda* — by Govinda; *nivedana* — the announcement; *antare* — after.

**Translation (35–36).** And many others too, who had come from all sides eager to see the Lord — with the triad of fear, longing and curiosity, the movements of their hearts constantly yearning — having stood just outside for a long time with an abundance of happiness, saw in turn the very great abode of mercy, though in great timidity, after Govinda had announced them.

**Note.** A *yugmaka* (so marked in the edition). In 35 the printed *satato 'dhikaṇṭhita-* gives the fourth pada twelve syllables (⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—) instead of eleven; the reading *satatotkaṇṭhita-*, given in brackets in the IAST, scans correctly (⏑⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—) and is adopted. Govinda is the Lord's door-keeper and personal servant.

### 37

**37.**

```
इति ते प्रहरद्वयावधि प्रथिता भावशतेन भूयसा ।
ददृशु(ः) प्रभुमात्तकौतुकं वरकल्पद्रुमवन्मनोरमम् ॥३७॥
```
*iti te prahara-dvayāvadhi prathitā bhāva-śatena bhūyasā |
dadṛśuḥ prabhum ātta-kautukaṁ vara-kalpa-drumavan manoramam ||37||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *te* — they; *prahara* — of watches; *dvaya* — two; *avadhi* — for the duration; *prathitāḥ* — renowned; *bhāva* — of feelings; *śatena* — with a hundred; *bhūyasā* — abundant; *dadṛśuḥ* — saw; *prabhum* — the Lord; *ātta* — who had taken; *kautukam* — joy; *vara* — an excellent; *kalpa-druma* — wish-fulfilling tree; *vat* — like; *manoramam* — charming.

**Translation.** Thus for two watches those renowned [devotees], with an abundant hundred feelings, saw the joyful Lord, charming like an excellent wish-fulfilling tree.

**Note.** Two watches (*praharas*) — about six hours. *Prathitāḥ* may also be taken as "unfolded, displayed": "their abundant hundred feelings unfolding". The edition has *dadṛśu* without the visarga (hence ददृशु(ः)).

**38.**


**Word for word:** *hasitaiḥ* — with smiles; *api* — also; *kāṁścit* — some; *añjasā* — at once; *vacanena* — with a word; *api* — also; *tathā* — likewise; *itarān* — others; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *kṛpayā* — with mercy; *ca* — and; *kayācana* — with some [indescribable]; *itarān* — others; *akarot* — made; *ātta* — having obtained; *manoratha* — the desire; *utsukān* — the eager ones.

**Translation.** Some with smiles, others likewise with a word, and others again with some [indescribable] mercy — the Lord at once made [all these] eager ones obtain the desire [for which they longed].

**39.**

### 40

**40.**

```
न च निर्वृते विलोक्य तं न च दृष्टी रहिताश्रुवाहिता ।
प्रपदान्तगमश्रुनोज्झितं मनुजेनास्य समीपतस्तदा ॥४०॥
```
*na ca nirvṛte vilokya taṁ na ca dṛṣṭī rahitāśru-vāhitā |
prapadānta-gam aśrunojjhitaṁ manujenāsya samīpatas tadā ||40||*

**Word for word:** *na ca* — and not; *nirvṛte* — satisfied; *vilokya* — having seen; *tam* — Him; *na ca* — and not; *dṛṣṭī* — the two eyes; *rahita* — devoid; *aśru* — of tears; *vāhitā* — of the flowing; *prapada* — of the toes; *anta* — to the tips; *gam* — reaching; *aśrunā* — with tears; *ujjhitam* — [were] shed; *manujena* — by a person; *asya* — Him; *samīpataḥ* — near; *tadā* — then.

**Translation.** Having seen Him, the eyes were not satisfied, nor were the eyes free from the flowing of tears; then by every person near Him tears were shed that reached down to the tips of the toes.

**Note.** The syntax is obscure ⟨?⟩: *nirvṛte* and *dṛṣṭī* are feminine duals ("the two eyes … satisfied"), but *rahitāśru-vāhitā* is singular and does not agree with *dṛṣṭī*; it is understood loosely, as an abstract "the state of being devoid of the flow of tears". The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text. Metre: the first pada has only nine syllables (*na ca nirvṛte vilokya taṁ*: ⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—⏑—), where *viyoginī* requires ten (⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—): a light syllable is missing within *nirvṛte*.

### 43

**43.**

```
उपयुज्य च शुद्धमोदनं कृतशुद्धाचमनादिकक्रियाः ।
परिधाय च भिन्नमंशुकं शुचिकान्तिर्ववृधे श्रिया प्रभुः ॥४३॥
```
*upayujya ca śuddham odanaṁ kṛta-śuddhācamanādika-kriyaḥ |
paridhāya ca bhinnam aṁśukaṁ śuci-kāntir vavṛdhe śriyā prabhuḥ ||43||*

**Word for word:** *upayujya* — having eaten; *ca* — and; *śuddham* — pure; *odanam* — rice; *kṛta* — having performed; *śuddha* — the pure; *ācamana* — rinsing of the mouth; *ādika* — and other; *kriyaḥ* — [whose] acts; *paridhāya* — having put on; *ca* — and; *bhinnam* — another; *aṁśukam* — garment; *śuci* — pure; *kāntiḥ* — [whose] lustre; *vavṛdhe* — increased; *śriyā* — in beauty; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord.

**Translation.** Having eaten pure rice, having performed the pure rinsing of the mouth and the other [rites], and having put on another garment, the Lord, pure in lustre, increased in beauty.

**Note.** The edition has *-kriyāḥ* (plural), as in the Devanagari above; as a qualifier of the Lord *-kriyaḥ* is required (so the IAST). The metre does not decide, the syllable standing at the end of the pada.

**46.**


**47.**


**50.**

```
क्षणमुत्क्षिपति क्षणं पदा क्षिपति भ्रामयति क्षणं तु तम् ।
भुजकक्षतटोरुजानुपत्कमलाधोऽध इतस्ततः प्रभुः ॥५०॥
```
*kṣaṇam utkṣipati kṣaṇaṁ padā kṣipati bhrāmayati kṣaṇaṁ tu tam |
bhuja-kakṣa-taṭoru-jānu-pat-kamalādho 'dha itas tataḥ prabhuḥ ||50||*

**Word for word:** *kṣaṇam* — now; *utkṣipati* — tosses up; *kṣaṇam* — now; *padā* — with the foot; *kṣipati* — strikes; *bhrāmayati* — whirls; *kṣaṇam tu* — and now; *tam* — it; *bhuja* — of the arms; *kakṣa-taṭa* — of the armpits; *ūru* — of the thighs; *jānu* — of the knees; *pat-kamala* — of the lotus feet; *adhaḥ adhaḥ* — beneath; *itaḥ tataḥ* — hither and thither; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord.

**Translation.** The Lord now tosses it up, now strikes it with His foot, and now whirls it hither and thither beneath His arms, armpits, thighs, knees and lotus feet.

### 51

**51.**

```
इति कौतुकचेष्टया नृणां नयनानन्दमतीव सान्द्रकम् ।
विदधत् सकलोत्सवेषु स प्रभुरानन्दममन्दमाययौ ॥५१॥
```
*iti kautuka-ceṣṭayā nṛṇāṁ nayanānandam atīva sāndrakam |
vidadhat sakalotsaveṣu sa prabhur ānandam amandam āyayau ||51||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *kautuka* — playful; *ceṣṭayā* — with action; *nṛṇām* — for people; *nayana* — of the eyes; *ānandam* — joy; *atīva* — very; *sāndrakam* — dense; *vidadhat* — providing; *sakala* — at all; *utsaveṣu* — the festivals; *saḥ* — He; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *ānandam* — bliss; *amandam* — no small; *āyayau* — attained.

**Translation.** Thus, by His playful actions providing for people at all the festivals a very dense joy for the eyes, the Lord [Himself] attained no small bliss.

### 52

**52.**

```
अथ दोल इतीरितो हरेः सुमहानुत्सव एक उत्तमः ।
विविधैः खलु कौतुकेहितैः पुरतो नृत्यति गौरविग्रहः ॥५२॥
```
*atha dola itīrito hareḥ sumahān utsava eka uttamaḥ |
vividhaiḥ khalu kautukehitaiḥ purato nṛtyati gaura-vigrahaḥ ||52||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — now; *dolaḥ iti* — as "the Dola"; *īritaḥ* — known; *hareḥ* — of Hari; *su-mahān* — very great; *utsavaḥ* — festival; *ekaḥ* — one; *uttamaḥ* — supreme; *vividhaiḥ* — with various; *khalu* — indeed; *kautuka* — playful; *īhitaiḥ* — actions; *purataḥ* — in front; *nṛtyati* — dances; *gaura* — golden; *vigrahaḥ* — [whose] form.

**Translation.** Now there is one very great and supreme festival of Hari, known as the Dola: [at it] the One whose form is golden dances in front with various playful actions indeed.

**Note.** The Dola-yatra (the swing festival, Holi) falls on the full moon of Phalguna.

### 53

**53.**

```
अरुणैश्च सितैश्च कोमलैरथ हारिद्ररजोभिरुत्तमैः ।
मलयोद्भवरेणुभिश्च तैर्भगवांश्चित्रितविग्रहो बभौ ॥५३॥
```
*aruṇaiś ca sitaiś ca komalair atha hāridra-rajobhir uttamaiḥ |
malayodbhava-reṇubhiś ca tair bhagavāṁś citrita-vigraho babhau ||53||*

**Word for word:** *aruṇaiḥ* — with crimson; *ca* — and; *sitaiḥ* — white; *ca* — and; *komalaiḥ* — soft; *atha* — and; *hāridra* — of turmeric; *rajobhiḥ* — with powders; *uttamaiḥ* — excellent; *malaya-udbhava* — of [sandalwood] born on Malaya; *reṇubhiḥ* — with the dust; *ca* — and; *taiḥ* — with those; *bhagavān* — the Lord; *citrita* — painted; *vigrahaḥ* — [whose] form; *babhau* — shone.

**Translation.** The Lord shone, His form painted with those soft powders — crimson and white, and excellent [powders] of turmeric — and with the dust of [sandalwood] born on Malaya.

**Note.** Coloured powders (*abira*, *gulal*) are the custom of the Dola festival.

### 54–55

**54.**

```
सफलक्रमुकद्रुमोच्चयैः फलनम्रैः कदलीद्रुमैरपि ।
सुमनोभरनिष्पतच्छिखैस्तरुभिश्चाधिकमण्डलीकृते ॥५४॥
```
*saphala-kramuka-drumoccayaiḥ phala-namraiḥ kadalī-drumair api |
sumano-bhara-niṣpatac-chikhais tarubhiś cādhika-maṇḍalīkṛte ||54||*

**Word for word:** *sa-phala* — with fruits; *kramuka* — of areca; *druma* — trees; *uccayaiḥ* — with clusters; *phala* — with fruit; *namraiḥ* — bowed; *kadalī* — banana; *drumaiḥ* — with trees; *api* — also; *sumanaḥ* — of flowers; *bhara* — under the weight; *niṣpatat* — drooping; *śikhaiḥ* — [whose] tops; *tarubhiḥ* — with trees; *ca* — and; *adhika* — richly; *maṇḍalīkṛte* — [in a place] encircled.

**55.**

```
वरमञ्चविभूषिते लसद्वरपर्यङ्कतटोपरि प्रभौ ।
निजभक्तगणेन दोलिते सति गौराङ्गशशी च नृत्यति ॥५५॥ (युग्मकम्)
```
*vara-mañca-vibhūṣite lasad-vara-paryaṅka-taṭopari prabhau |
nija-bhakta-gaṇena dolite sati gaurāṅga-śaśī ca nṛtyati ||55|| (yugmakam)*

**Word for word:** *vara* — with a fine; *mañca* — platform; *vibhūṣite* — [in a place] adorned; *lasat* — of the resplendent; *vara* — fine; *paryaṅka* — couch; *taṭa* — the surface; *upari* — upon; *prabhau* — [when] the Lord; *nija* — His own; *bhakta* — of devotees; *gaṇena* — by the host; *dolite sati* — is swung; *gaurāṅga-śaśī* — the moon Gauranga; *ca* — and; *nṛtyati* — dances.

**Translation (54–55).** When, in a place richly encircled by clusters of areca palms bearing fruit, by banana trees bowed with fruit and by trees whose tops droop under the weight of flowers, [a place] adorned with a fine platform, the Lord upon a resplendent fine couch is swung by the host of His devotees — the moon Gauranga dances.

**Note.** A *yugmaka* (so marked in the edition). The Lord who is swung is the festival Deity, named Govinda in 56.

---

### 56–57

**56.**

```
कनकाचलकान्तविग्रहौ मुहुरन्योन्यविलोकनोत्सुकौ ।
अभिदोलननृत्यचञ्चलावथ गोविन्दशचीसुतौ प्रभू ॥५६॥
```
*kanakācala-kānta-vigrahau muhur anyonya-vilokanotsukau |
abhidolana-nṛtya-cañcalāv atha govinda-śacīsutau prabhū ||56||*

**Word for word:** *kanaka-acala* — [like] a golden mountain; *kānta* — lovely; *vigrahau* — the two [whose] forms; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *anyonya* — at each other; *vilokana* — to look; *utsukau* — the two eager; *abhidolana* — with swinging; *nṛtya* — [and] dancing; *cañcalau* — the two in motion; *atha* — then; *govinda* — Govinda; *śacī-sutau* — [and] the son of Shachi; *prabhū* — the two Lords.

**57.**

```
निजचेष्टितवैभवश्रिया जनतानां निबिडं सुखोत्करम् ।
अविरामरसादकुर्वतामधिदोलोत्सवमुत्सुकात्मना ॥५७॥ (युग्मकम्)
```
*nija-ceṣṭita-vaibhava-śriyā janatānāṁ nibiḍaṁ sukhotkaram |
avirāma-rasād akurvatām adhi-dolotsavam utsukātmanā ||57|| (yugmakam)*

**Word for word:** *nija* — of Their own; *ceṣṭita* — activities; *vaibhava* — of the splendour; *śriyā* — by the beauty; *janatānām* — for the people; *nibiḍam* — dense; *sukha* — of happiness; *utkaram* — an abundance; *avirāma* — unceasing; *rasāt* — from rasa; *akurvatām* — the two created; *adhi-dola-utsavam* — at the Dola festival; *utsuka* — eager; *ātmanā* — with heart.

**Translation (56–57).** Then the two Lords, Govinda and the son of Shachi — both with forms lovely as a golden mountain, again and again eager to look at each other, in motion — [Govinda] in swinging, [the son of Shachi] in dancing — by the beauty of the splendour of Their own activities, out of unceasing rasa, with eager hearts created at the Dola festival a dense abundance of happiness for the people.

**Note.** A *yugmaka* (in the edition the mark is garbled). Govinda is the festival Deity, Dola-Govinda, who is swung on the swing. The epithet "lovely as a golden mountain", in the dual, is apparently extended to both by inertia. *Akurvatām* stands for the classical *akurutām* (imperfect, dual); the metre requires the printed form. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 58

**58.**

```
इतरेषु महोत्सवेषु स प्रथितो दोल इतीह यः सदा ।
सम एव रथस्य कीर्त्तितो मधुमासप्रथमे स राजते ॥५८॥
```
*itareṣu mahotsaveṣu sa prathito dola itīha yaḥ sadā |
sama eva rathasya kīrttito madhu-māsa-prathame sa rājate ||58||*

**Word for word:** *itareṣu* — among the other; *mahā-utsaveṣu* — great festivals; *saḥ* — that; *prathitaḥ* — renowned; *dolaḥ iti* — as "the Dola"; *iha* — here; *yaḥ* — which; *sadā* — always; *samaḥ eva* — equal; *rathasya* — to [the festival] of the chariot; *kīrttitaḥ* — proclaimed; *madhu-māsa* — of the month of Madhu; *prathame* — at the beginning; *saḥ* — it; *rājate* — shines.

**Translation.** That [festival] which among the other great festivals is always renowned here as the Dola and is proclaimed equal to [the festival] of the chariot shines at the beginning of the month of Madhu.

**Note.** Madhu is Chaitra (March–April); the full moon of Phalguna falls at the beginning of Chaitra when the months are reckoned from full moon to full moon.

### 59

**59.**

```
ननु तत् किमिदं जगत्पतेरिह दोलोत्सवकौतुकं जनैः ।
कथनीयममुं महाप्रभुः पुरतः पश्यति निर्भरैः सुखैः ॥५९॥
```
*nanu tat kim idaṁ jagat-pater iha dolotsava-kautukaṁ janaiḥ |
kathanīyam amuṁ mahāprabhuḥ purataḥ paśyati nirbharaiḥ sukhaiḥ ||59||*

**Word for word:** *nanu* — indeed; *tat* — then; *kim* — how; *idam* — this; *jagat-pateḥ* — of the Lord of the world; *iha* — here; *dola-utsava* — of the Dola festival; *kautukam* — delight; *janaiḥ* — by people; *kathanīyam* — [is] to be described; *amum* — it; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *purataḥ* — before [Him]; *paśyati* — beholds; *nirbharaiḥ* — with full; *sukhaiḥ* — happiness.

**Translation.** How then can people describe this delight of the Dola festival of the Lord of the world here — [the festival] that Mahaprabhu beholds before Him with full happiness?

**Note.** *Amum* (masculine) may refer either to the festival (*utsava*) or to the Lord of the world: "whom Mahaprabhu beholds before Him".

### 60

**60.**

```
पुनरप्यथ तैः समागतै रथयात्रासमये महाप्रभुः ।
विलसत्यनिशं तथा तथा निजसङ्कीर्त्तननर्त्तनादिभिः ॥६०॥
```
*punar apy atha taiḥ samāgatai ratha-yātrā-samaye mahāprabhuḥ |
vilasaty aniśaṁ tathā tathā nija-saṅkīrttana-narttanādibhiḥ ||60||*

**Word for word:** *punaḥ api* — again; *atha* — then; *taiḥ* — with those; *samāgataiḥ* — who have come; *ratha-yātrā* — of the Ratha-yatra; *samaye* — at the time; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *vilasati* — sports; *aniśam* — unceasingly; *tathā tathā* — in such and such ways; *nija* — with His own; *saṅkīrttana* — sankirtana; *narttana* — dancing; *ādibhiḥ* — and so on.

**Translation.** And then again, at the time of the Ratha-yatra, Mahaprabhu sports unceasingly in such and such ways with those who have come [from Bengal], with His sankirtana, dancing and so on.

### 61

**61.**

```
इति विंशतिहायनैः प्रभुर्बलदेवस्य रथाग्रतो मुहुः ।
नटनानि विधाय कीर्त्तनैरिदमेतद्व्यचकिरज्जगत्तले ॥६१॥
```
*iti viṁśati-hāyanaiḥ prabhur baladevasya rathāgrato muhuḥ |
naṭanāni vidhāya kīrttanair idam etad vyacakiraj jagat-tale ||61||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *viṁśati* — for twenty; *hāyanaiḥ* — years; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *baladevasya* — of Baladeva; *ratha* — the chariot; *agrataḥ* — before; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *naṭanāni* — dances; *vidhāya* — performing; *kīrttanaiḥ* — with kirtanas; *idam etat* — all this; *vyacakirat* (read *vyakirat*) — scattered; *jagat-tale* — over the world.

**Translation.** Thus for twenty years the Lord again and again performed dances before the chariot of Baladeva, and with kirtanas scattered all this over the world.

**Note.** "Twenty years" is Karnapura's figure (so also in 63); according to the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 1.17–21), after sannyasa the Lord lived twenty-four years: six years of travels and eighteen years in Puri. Metre: the fourth pada has twelve syllables instead of eleven (*-ir idam etad vyacakiraj jagat-tale*: ⏑⏑——⏑⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—): *vyacakirat* has a superfluous light syllable. The regular imperfect of *vi-√kṝ*, *vyakirat* ("scattered"), restores the metre (⏑⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—⏑—) and the grammar.

### 62

**62.**

```
स तु सर्वजनान्तरस्थितो जगदाधार इति प्रकीर्त्तितः ।
इति तस्य पुरो मुहुर्मुहुर्नटनं कीर्त्तनमाततान सः ॥६२॥
```
*sa tu sarva-janāntara-sthito jagad-ādhāra iti prakīrttitaḥ |
iti tasya puro muhur muhur naṭanaṁ kīrttanam ātatāna saḥ ||62||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ tu* — He; *sarva* — of all; *jana* — beings; *antara* — within; *sthitaḥ* — dwelling; *jagat* — of the world; *ādhāraḥ* — the support; *iti* — thus; *prakīrttitaḥ* — [is] glorified; *iti* — therefore; *tasya* — Him; *puraḥ* — before; *muhuḥ muhuḥ* — again and again; *naṭanam* — dance; *kīrttanam* — [and] kirtana; *ātatāna* — spread out; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** He [Baladeva], dwelling within all beings, is glorified as the support of the world; therefore again and again He [the Lord] spread out dance and kirtana before Him.

**Note.** The verse explains the preceding one: why the Lord dances before the chariot of Baladeva in particular. "The support of the world" is an epithet of Baladeva-Sankarshana (Shesha).

### 63

**63.**

```
इत्थं श्रीपुरुषोत्तमे विहरणं कृत्वा शचीनन्दनो
हर्षाद्विंशतिवत्सरेण विहितक्रीडो बभौ निर्भरम् ।
एतन्मध्यमधि प्रयाणकुतुकादागत्य भागीरथी-
तीरे श्रीमथुरामलङ्कृतिमति कर्त्तुं स विक्रीडति ॥६३॥
```
*itthaṁ śrī-puruṣottame viharaṇaṁ kṛtvā śacīnandano
harṣād viṁśati-vatsareṇa vihita-krīḍo babhau nirbharam |
etan-madhyam adhi prayāṇa-kutukād āgatya bhāgīrathī-
tīre śrī-mathurām alaṅkṛtimatīṁ karttuṁ sa vikrīḍati ||63||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *śrī-puruṣottame* — in Sri Purushottama; *viharaṇam* — pastimes; *kṛtvā* — having performed; *śacī-nandanaḥ* — the son of Shachi; *harṣāt* — joyfully; *viṁśati* — for twenty; *vatsareṇa* — years; *vihita* — having performed; *krīḍaḥ* — [whose] play; *babhau* — shone; *nirbharam* — fully; *etat* — of this [period]; *madhyam adhi* — within; *prayāṇa* — to journey; *kutukāt* — out of eagerness; *āgatya* — having come; *bhāgīrathī* — of the Bhagirathi; *tīre* — to the bank; *śrī-mathurām* — Sri Mathura; *alaṅkṛtimatīm* — adorned; *karttum* — to make; *saḥ* — He; *vikrīḍati* — sports.

**Translation.** Thus the son of Shachi, having sported in Sri Purushottama and having joyfully performed His play for twenty years, shone in fullness. And within this [period], out of eagerness to journey, He came to the bank of the Bhagirathi and sports [there], in order to adorn Sri Mathura [with His coming].

**Note.** Metre — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* (———⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑———⏑——⏑—; 19 syllables per pada). The edition has *alaṅkṛtimati* (so the Devanagari above); the metre requires a heavy syllable: *alaṅkṛtimatīṁ* (so the IAST). The verse leads into the next sarga: the journey towards Mathura by way of Bengal (cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 16: the Lord longs for Vrindavana and goes by way of Gauda).

**Colophon:** इति श्रीकृष्णचैतन्यचरितामृते महाकाव्ये श्रीपुरुषोत्तमविहाररथयात्रासमाप्तिरष्टादशः सर्गः । (*iti śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-caritāmṛte mahākāvye śrī-puruṣottama-vihāra-ratha-yātrā-samāptir aṣṭādaśaḥ sargaḥ*) — "Thus [ends] the eighteenth sarga, 'The Pastimes in Sri Purushottama and the Conclusion of the Ratha-yatra', in the mahakavya 'Sri Krishna-Chaitanya-charitamrita'."
