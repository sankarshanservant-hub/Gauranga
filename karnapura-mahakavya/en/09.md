# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Nine — verses 1–95 (printed pp. 249–280)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:** *svāgatā* (11 syllables per pada: —⏑— ⏑⏑⏑ —⏑⏑ ——); verse 94 — *praharṣiṇī*; verse 95 — *mandākrāntā*.
**Total verses:** 95.

**Contents of the passage:** Srivasa's account of Krishna's pastimes with the gopis — the walk in the forest, the bathing in the Yamuna, the entry into the jewelled pavilion.
- 1 — Srivasa begins his account (continuing 8.60–63).
- 2–20 — the gopis enter the forest with Krishna: lightning beside a cloud; their first shyness yields to Manmatha; gestures and gaits; bees alight on the creepers of their bodies; the gopis walk embracing Krishna.
- 21–31 — the Lakshmi of the forest brings gifts; the friends tease a proud one (*kulaka* 22–26: the *mādhavī* and the jasmine tormented by bees); a gopi embraces a creeper as a friend; "the cuckoo calls 'kuhū' — it is darkness you desire".
- 32–47 — gathering flowers: Krishna is adorned with a flower, lifts the gopis to the high branches, calls Radha lotus-faced (34); a bee on Krishna; reproaches to the "bee" (*kulaka* 37–39); "there is a bower!"; balls of flowers; coaxing the proud one.
- 48–50 — Krishna turns to another; the gopis are bathed in perspiration from the forest play.
- 51–78 — the bathing in the Yamuna: splashing, the "water drum", the *śapharī* fish, lotuses and stalks hold back the garments; the washed-off colours and ornaments are gifts to the Yamuna; wet braids and garments.
- 79–93 — a *kulaka* of fifteen verses: the jewelled pavilion (walls like mirrors, moonstones and sunstones, emerald "grass", ruby "pomegranates", a crystal pond).
- 94–95 — the singing of the birds; the wish-fulfilling creepers perform *nīrājana* over the gopis with cries of "Victory!"

**Textual cruxes:** 1 (*yathā* — superfluous syllables), 2 (*śreyasā*/*preyasā*; 12 syllables), 3 (*praviśantaḥ*), 9, 13 (wrong division of lines), 20 (*sakhibhiḥ*), 23 (*mādhurī*/*mādhavī*), 26 (a syllable is missing), 27 (*balottara*), 28 (*rabhasābhiḥ*), 34 (*vadanā*/*radanā*), 46, 57 (*aparasya*), 73 (*alase*), 82, 89, 92 — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
इत्थमुद्भटसुखाम्बुधिमग्नं
गौरचन्द्रमथ यथा सोऽभिजगाद ।
श्रूयतां प्रभुवर स्वविहारं
प्राक्कृतं स्वयमहं कथयामि ॥१॥
```
*ittham udbhaṭa-sukhāmbudhi-magnaṁ
gauracandram atha yathā so 'bhijagāda |
śrūyatāṁ prabhu-vara sva-vihāraṁ
prāk-kṛtaṁ svayam ahaṁ kathayāmi ||1||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *udbhaṭa* — of mighty; *sukha* — happiness; *ambudhi* — in the ocean; *magnam* — immersed; *gauracandram* — to Gaurachandra; *atha* — then; *yathā* ⟨?⟩ — (as); *saḥ* — he (Srivasa); *abhijagāda* — said; *śrūyatām* — let it be heard; *prabhu* — of lords; *vara* — O best; *sva* — Your own; *vihāram* — pastime; *prāk* — formerly; *kṛtam* — performed; *svayam* — myself; *aham* — I; *kathayāmi* — shall tell.

**Translation.** Then he said to Gaurachandra, thus immersed in an ocean of mighty happiness: "Listen, O best of lords: I myself shall tell [You] of Your own pastimes, performed of old."

**Note.** The second pada in the edition (*गौरचन्द्रमथ यथा सोऽभिजगाद*) has 13 syllables instead of 11; without *yathā* (*gauracandram atha so 'bhijagāda*) the *svāgatā* metre is restored exactly, so *yathā* is probably an interpolation or a misprint ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 2

**2.**

```
वीक्ष्य तद्वदनमनिर्वचनीयं
रम्यरम्यमपि वल्गुमनोभिः ।
श्रेयसा सह विलासवतीभिः
स्वाङ्गवल्लिभिरकारि विचित्रम् ॥२॥
```
*vīkṣya tad-vadanam anirvacanīyaṁ
ramya-ramyam api valgu-manobhiḥ |
śreyasā [preyasā] saha vilāsavatībhiḥ
svāṅga-vallibhir akāri vicitram ||2||*

**Word for word:** *vīkṣya* — having seen; *tat* — His; *vadanam* — face; *anirvacanīyam* — indescribable; *ramya-ramyam* — the most charming of the charming; *api* — and; *valgu* — tender; *manobhiḥ* — with hearts; *preyasā* — with the beloved; *saha* — together; *vilāsavatībhiḥ* — by the playful [maidens]; *sva* — of their own; *aṅga* — limbs; *vallibhiḥ* — with the creepers; *akāri* — was made; *vicitram* — a wonder.

**Translation.** Seeing His indescribable face, the most charming of all that is charming, the playful [maidens], tender-hearted, wrought wonders with the creepers of their limbs together with their beloved.

**Note.** The edition has *श्रेयसा* ("with the good"); cf. *preyasā* in verse 3: we read *preyasā* ⟨?⟩. The first pada has 12 syllables instead of 11; with *tan-mukham* in place of *tad-vadanam* the *svāgatā* metre is exact — perhaps a gloss has displaced the original word ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 3

**3.**

```
प्रेयसा सह नवीनतमाल-
श्यामलेन विपिनं प्रविशन्तः ।
तत्पुरो नवघनेन विलासं
विद्युतां दधुरमूर्व्रजवध्वः ॥३॥
```
*preyasā saha navīna-tamāla-
śyāmalena vipinaṁ praviśantaḥ [praviśantyaḥ] |
tat-puro nava-ghanena vilāsaṁ
vidyutāṁ dadhur amūr vraja-vadhvaḥ ||3||*

**Word for word:** *preyasā* — with the beloved; *saha* — together; *navīna* — young; *tamāla* — [as] a tamala tree; *śyāmalena* — dark; *vipinam* — the forest; *praviśantyaḥ* — entering; *tat* — Him; *puraḥ* — before; *nava* — new; *ghanena* — with a cloud; *vilāsam* — the play; *vidyutām* — of lightnings; *dadhuḥ* — displayed; *amūḥ* — these; *vraja* — of Vraja; *vadhvaḥ* — the young women.

**Translation.** Entering the forest together with their beloved, dark as a young tamala tree, these young women of Vraja displayed before Him the play of lightnings with a new rain cloud.

**Note.** The edition has *praviśantaḥ*, a masculine form with a feminine subject; the sense requires *praviśantyaḥ* (the metre is unchanged) ⟨?⟩. The golden gopis before Krishna, dark as a cloud, are like lightning on a cloud. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 4

**4.**

```
रामणीयकमवेक्ष्य रमण्यो
मानसेन मनसिजेन लसन्त्यः ।
चेष्टया रुचिरयालसभाजो
भाविताः समभवन्नधिनाथम् ॥४॥
```
*rāmaṇīyakam avekṣya ramaṇyo
mānasena manasijena lasantyaḥ |
ceṣṭayā rucirayālasa-bhājo
bhāvitāḥ samabhavann adhinātham ||4||*

**Word for word:** *rāmaṇīyakam* — the loveliness; *avekṣya* — having beheld; *ramaṇyaḥ* — the beautiful women; *mānasena* — heartfelt; *manasijena* — with love; *lasantyaḥ* — shining; *ceṣṭayā* — with movement; *rucirayā* — charming; *ālasa* — languor; *bhājaḥ* — possessing; *bhāvitāḥ* — filled with feeling; *samabhavan* — became; *adhinātham* — towards their master.

**Translation.** Beholding His loveliness, the beautiful women shone with love born in the heart; charming in their movements, full of languor, they became wholly filled with feeling for their master.

**Note.** *Rucirayālasa-* = *rucirayā* (agreeing with *ceṣṭayā*) + *ālasa*. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 5

**5.**

```
साभिलासमथ भाववतीनां
कृष्णचन्द्रमभिमुग्धवधूनाम् ।
साध्वसं प्रथमजं तिरयित्वा
मन्मथेन हृदये समुदासे ॥५॥
```
*sābhilāṣam atha bhāvavatīnāṁ
kṛṣṇacandram abhi mugdha-vadhūnām |
sādhvasaṁ prathamajaṁ tirayitvā
manmathena hṛdaye samudāse ||5||*

**Word for word:** *sa* — with; *abhilāṣam* — longing; *atha* — then; *bhāvavatīnām* — full of feeling; *kṛṣṇacandram* — Krishnachandra; *abhi* — towards; *mugdha* — innocent; *vadhūnām* — of the young women; *sādhvasam* — shyness; *prathama* — of the first [meeting]; *jam* — born; *tirayitvā* — having dispelled; *manmathena* — by Manmatha; *hṛdaye* — in the heart; *samudāse* — it was risen (he arose).

**Translation.** Then, in the hearts of the innocent young women, full of feeling and longing for Krishnachandra, Manmatha arose, dispelling the shyness born of the first [meeting].

**Note.** The edition has *साभिलास* for the usual *sābhilāṣa*. *Samudāse* is an impersonal passive perfect (*bhāve prayoga*): "it was risen by Manmatha", that is, Manmatha arose.

**6.**

### 7

**7.**

```
कापि मन्दमयता परिवृत्ते
मारुतेन कुचयुग्मकचेले ।
सम्भ्रमात् प्रियसखीजनमुच्चै-
रालिलिङ्ग परिपश्यति कृष्णे ॥७॥
```
*kāpi mandam ayatā parivṛtte
mārutena kuca-yugma-kacele |
sambhramāt priya-sakhī-janam uccair
āliliṅga paripaśyati kṛṣṇe ||7||*

**Word for word:** *kā api* — a certain [maiden]; *mandam* — gently; *ayatā* — blowing; *parivṛtte* — when [it] was turned aside; *mārutena* — by the breeze; *kuca* — of the breasts; *yugma* — of the pair; *kacele* — the covering; *sambhramāt* — in confusion; *priya* — dear; *sakhī* — friend; *janam* — from the circle; *uccaiḥ* — tightly; *āliliṅga* — embraced; *paripaśyati* — while looking on; *kṛṣṇe* — Krishna.

**Translation.** When the gently blowing breeze turned aside the covering of another's bosom while Krishna looked on, she in confusion tightly embraced a dear friend.

**Note.** *Kacela* is a rare word, apparently a covering for the bosom or a bodice; possibly a corruption. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**8.**

### 9

**9.**

```
पीवरोरसिजकुड्मलकान्तां
पाणिधूतनवपल्लवकान्तिम् ।
प्रोज्झ्य काननलतां वरनारी-

```
*pīvarorasija-kuḍmala-kāntāṁ
pāṇi-dhūta-nava-pallava-kāntim |
projjhya kānana-latāṁ vara-nārī-

**Word for word:** *pīvara* — full; *urasija* — of breasts; *kuḍmala* — with the buds; *kāntām* — lovely; *pāṇi* — by the hands; *dhūta* — put to shame; *nava* — of young; *pallava* — shoots; *kāntim* — [whose] beauty; *projjhya* — having abandoned; *kānana* — of the forest; *latām* — the creeper; *vara* — of a beautiful; *nārī* — woman; *deha* — of the body; *vallim* — the creeper; *abhajat* — resorted to; *madhupa* — of bees; *oghaḥ* — a swarm.

**Translation.** Abandoning the forest creeper, a swarm of bees resorted to the creeper of the body of a beautiful maiden — lovely with the buds of her full bosom, her hands putting to shame the beauty of young shoots.

### 10

**10.**

```
सुभ्रुवां तनुलतासु लतानां
श्रीरियं सपरितोषमभूत् किम् ।
सर्वतः सपदि तासु यदेतन्-
मञ्जुगुञ्जदलिनां कुलमासीत् ॥१०॥
```
*subhruvāṁ tanu-latāsu latānāṁ
śrīr iyaṁ sa-paritoṣam abhūt kim |
sarvataḥ sapadi tāsu yad etan
mañju-guñjad-alināṁ kulam āsīt ||10||*

**Word for word:** *subhruvām* — of the fair-browed; *tanu* — of the bodies; *latāsu* — into the creepers; *latānām* — of the creepers; *śrīḥ* — beauty; *iyam* — this; *sa* — with; *paritoṣam* — delight; *abhūt* — passed (came to dwell); *kim* — did not?; *sarvataḥ* — from all sides; *sapadi* — at once; *tāsu* — around them; *yat* — since; *etat* — this; *mañju* — sweetly; *guñjat* — humming; *alinām* — of bees; *kulam* — swarm; *āsīt* — appeared.

**Translation.** Had not the beauty of the creepers joyfully passed into the creepers of the bodies of the fair-browed [maidens]? For at once, on all sides, this swarm of sweetly humming bees appeared around them.

### 11

**11.**

```
एकयौष्ठपतनेऽमृतपत्वं
प्रेप्सुरुन्मदतरो मधुपायी ।
ओष्ठदंशनरतस्य सतोषं
प्रेयसः स्मरणतो न निरासे ॥११॥
```
*ekayauṣṭha-patane 'mṛta-pa-tvaṁ
prepsur unmadataro madhu-pāyī |
oṣṭha-daṁśana-ratasya satoṣaṁ
preyasaḥ smaraṇato na nirāse ||11||*

**Word for word:** *ekayā* — by one; *oṣṭha* — on the lip; *patane* — on falling; *amṛta* — of nectar; *pa* — of a drinker; *tvam* — the state; *prepsuḥ* — desiring to obtain; *unmadataraḥ* — greatly intoxicated; *madhu* — honey; *pāyī* — drinking (the bee); *oṣṭha* — the lips; *daṁśana* — to biting; *ratasya* — given; *satoṣam* — gladly; *preyasaḥ* — of the beloved; *smaraṇataḥ* — from remembrance; *na* — not; *nirāse* — was driven away.

**Translation.** A greatly intoxicated honey-drinking bee, longing to become a drinker of nectar, fell on the lip of one [maiden]; and she gladly did not drive it away, remembering her beloved, who is given to biting the lips.

**Note.** *Amṛta-pa-tvam* — "the state of a drinker of nectar" (*amṛta-pa* + *-tva*). *Nirāse* is an impersonal passive: "by one [maiden] there was no driving away".

### 12

**12.**

```
मन्थरं मदनविह्वलहंसी-
लास्यशंसि मधुरक्रमरम्यम् ।
आदधुश्चरणपङ्कजरम्यं
सुभ्रुवोऽथ लघु तत्र विहर्तुम् ॥१२॥
```
*mantharaṁ madana-vihvala-haṁsī-
lāsya-śaṁsi madhura-krama-ramyam |
ādadhuś caraṇa-paṅkaja-ramyaṁ
subhruvo 'tha laghu tatra vihartum ||12||*

**Word for word:** *mantharam* — slow; *madana* — by love; *vihvala* — overwhelmed; *haṁsī* — of a female swan; *lāsya* — the dance; *śaṁsi* — recalling; *madhura* — with sweet; *krama* — steps; *ramyam* — charming; *ādadhuḥ* — displayed; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *paṅkaja* — of the lotuses; *ramyam* — the loveliness; *subhruvaḥ* — the fair-browed; *atha* — then; *laghu* — lightly; *tatra* — there; *vihartum* — in order to play.

**Translation.** Then, in order to play there, the fair-browed [maidens] displayed the loveliness of their lotus feet — a light, unhurried [gait], charming with its sweet steps, recalling the dance of a female swan overwhelmed by love.

**Note.** The syntax is unclear: the attributes *mantharam … madhura-krama-ramyam* imply "gait" (*gatim*), while the second *ramyam* is probably a noun, "loveliness". The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 13

**13.**

```
उल्लसन्मदनमन्थरपाद-
न्यासभाजि गमने रमणीनाम् ।
श्रोणिबिम्बकुचयोः परिणाहः

```
*ullasan-madana-manthara-pāda-

śroṇi-bimba-kucayoḥ pariṇāhaḥ
khedayann api babhūva sukhāya ||13||*

**Word for word:** *ullasat* — swelling; *madana* — by love; *manthara* — slowed; *pāda* — of the feet; *nyāsa* — placing; *bhāji* — having; *gamane* — in the gait; *ramaṇīnām* — of the beautiful women; *śroṇi* — of the hips; *bimba* — rounded; *kucayoḥ* — [and] of the breasts; *pariṇāhaḥ* — the fullness; *khedayan* — wearying; *api* — though; *babhūva* — became; *sukhāya* — for happiness.

**Translation.** As the beautiful women walked, their steps slowed by swelling love, the fullness of their rounded hips and bosoms, though it wearied them, became a source of happiness.

**Note.** In the edition the lines are wrongly divided (*…पादन्यास / भाजि…*): the boundary of the padas falls inside the word *pāda-nyāsa*.

### 15

**15.**

```
कोमलं चरणपद्ममशक्तं
मास्म गा द्रुततरं मदिराक्षि ।
इत्यतीव विवशौ रुदतः किं

ity atīva vivaśau rudataḥ kiṁ
nūpurau praṇayato ramaṇīnām ||15||*

**Word for word:** *komalam* — tender; *caraṇa* — of the foot; *padmam* — the lotus; *aśaktam* — without strength; *mā sma gāḥ* — do not go; *drutataram* — too quickly; *madirā* — intoxicating; *akṣi* — O [maiden] with eyes; *iti* — thus; *atīva* — utterly; *vivaśau* — helpless; *rudataḥ* — cry; *kim* — do [they] not?; *nūpurau* — the two anklets; *praṇayataḥ* — out of love; *ramaṇīnām* — of the beautiful women.

**Translation.** "Tender is the lotus of your foot, it has no strength — do not go so quickly, O maiden with intoxicating eyes!" — is it not this that the anklets of the beautiful women, utterly helpless, cry out of love?

### 16

**16.**

```
तत्तदङ्घ्रिकमलस्य विलासे
सस्पृहं कथयतीव महान्तम् ।
स्वानुरागमनुरागवतीनां
यावकैररुणिता वनभूमिः ॥१६॥
```
*tat-tad-aṅghri-kamalasya vilāse
sa-spṛhaṁ kathayatīva mahāntam |
svānurāgam anurāgavatīnāṁ
yāvakair aruṇitā vana-bhūmiḥ ||16||*

**Word for word:** *tat-tat* — of each of them; *aṅghri* — of the feet; *kamalasya* — of the lotus; *vilāse* — for the play; *sa* — with; *spṛham* — longing; *kathayati* — tells; *iva* — as if; *mahāntam* — great; *sva* — its own; *anurāgam* — love (redness); *anurāgavatīnām* — of the loving [maidens]; *yāvakaiḥ* — with lac; *aruṇitā* — reddened; *vana* — of the forest; *bhūmiḥ* — the ground.

**Translation.** Reddened with the lac from the feet of the loving [maidens], the forest ground seemed to tell longingly of its own great love for the play of the lotus feet of each of them.

**Note.** A play on words: *anurāga* means both "love" and "redness". The earth, reddened by *yāvaka* (the red lac for the feet), seems to confess its love.

### 17

**17.**

```
कृष्णपृष्ठतटलग्नकुचाग्रा
तत्तदंसविलसद्भुजमूला ।
साचितद्वदनचुम्बितवक्त्रा

```
*kṛṣṇa-pṛṣṭha-taṭa-lagna-kucāgrā
tat-tad-aṁsa-vilasad-bhuja-mūlā |
sāci-tad-vadana-cumbita-vaktrā
kāpi tatra ruruce 'nucalantī ||17||*

**Word for word:** *kṛṣṇa* — of Krishna; *pṛṣṭha* — of the back; *taṭa* — to the slope; *lagna* — pressed; *kuca* — of the breasts; *agrā* — [whose] tips; *tat-tat* — now on one, now on the other of His; *aṁsa* — shoulders; *vilasat* — resting; *bhuja* — of the arms; *mūlā* — [whose] upper parts; *sāci* — turned sideways; *tat* — by His; *vadana* — face; *cumbita* — kissed; *vaktrā* — [whose] face; *kā api* — a certain [maiden]; *tatra* — there; *ruruce* — shone; *anucalantī* — following.

**Translation.** There one [maiden] shone as she followed [Him], her bosom pressed to Krishna's back, her arms resting now on one, now on the other of His shoulders, her face kissed by His face as He turned it sideways.

**Note.** The compound *sāci-tad-vadana-cumbita-vaktrā* admits two readings: either Krishna, turning His face sideways, kisses her, or she, turning her face, kisses Him.

### 18

**18.**

```
पृष्ठतः प्रियतमेन भुजाभ्यां
श्लिष्टवक्षसिरुहाम्बुरुहाक्षी ।
इन्द्रनीलमणिहारमिवास्याः

```

**Word for word:** *pṛṣṭhataḥ* — from behind; *priyatamena* — by the dearest one; *bhujābhyām* — with both arms; *śliṣṭa* — embraced; *vakṣasi-ruha* — [whose] breasts ("growing on the chest"); *amburuha* — lotus-; *akṣī* — eyed; *indranīla* — of sapphire; *maṇi* — of gems; *hāram* — a necklace; *iva* — like; *asyāḥ* — her; *kaṇṭha* — of the throat; *sīmni* — at the border; *dadhatī* — bearing; *calitā āsīt* — walked.

**Translation.** Another, lotus-eyed, whose bosom her dearest one embraced from behind with both arms, walked on bearing them at her throat like a necklace of sapphires.

### 19

**19.**

```
केशवांसतटराजिभुजाया
मन्थरालसगतेः सह यान्त्याः ।
तन्नितम्बभुवि लग्नविलग्नो

*keśavāṁsa-taṭa-rāji-bhujāyā

### 20

**20.**

```
प्राणनाथमधि कापि सखिभि-
र्बिभ्रती गतिमनङ्गविभङ्गा ।
साङ्गभङ्गमनुगांसतटेऽधाद्
बाहुमूलमुदयत्कुचमूलम् ॥२०॥
```

bibhratī gatim anaṅga-vibhaṅgā |
sāṅga-bhaṅgam anugāṁsa-taṭe 'dhād
bāhu-mūlam udayat-kuca-mūlam ||20||*

**Word for word:** *prāṇa* — of life; *nātham* — the lord; *adhi* — towards; *kā api* — a certain [maiden]; *sakhībhiḥ* — with friends; *bibhratī* — having; *gatim* — a gait; *anaṅga* — by Ananga (love); *vibhaṅgā* — [whose] curves; *sa* — with; *aṅga* — of the body; *bhaṅgam* — a bending; *anugā* — of the [friend] following; *aṁsa* — of the shoulder; *taṭe* — on the slope; *adhāt* — placed; *bāhu* — of the arm; *mūlam* — the root; *udayat* — rising; *kuca* — of the bosom; *mūlam* — [where is] the base.

**Translation.** One, walking with her friends towards the lord of her life, her gait curved by Ananga, bending her body, laid her arm on the shoulder of the friend who followed her — and near the root of her arm her bosom rose.

**Note.** The edition has *सखिभिः*; the metre requires *sakhībhiḥ* (the tenth syllable is long).

### 21

**21.**

```
तत्क्षणे क्षणत एव वधूनां
मन्मथेन बहुधा विवशानाम् ।
आययौ सपदि काननलक्ष्मीः
सा यथेप्सितमुपायनभारम् ॥२१॥
```
*tat-kṣaṇe kṣaṇata eva vadhūnāṁ
manmathena bahudhā vivaśānām |
āyayau sapadi kānana-lakṣmīḥ
sā yathepsitam upāyana-bhāram ||21||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — at that; *kṣaṇe* — moment; *kṣaṇataḥ* — in an instant; *eva* — very; *vadhūnām* — to the young women; *manmathena* — by Manmatha; *bahudhā* — in many ways; *vivaśānām* — rendered helpless; *āyayau* — came; *sapadi* — at once; *kānana* — of the forest; *lakṣmīḥ* — the Lakshmi (the beauty); *sā* — she; *yathā* — as; *īpsitam* — [they] desired; *upāyana* — of gifts; *bhāram* — [with] a load.

**Translation.** At that very moment, in an instant, the Lakshmi of the forest herself at once came to the young women, whom Manmatha had rendered helpless in many ways, bringing a load of gifts — all that they desired.

**Note.** The "Lakshmi of the forest" is its beauty personified. Verses 22–26 that follow (a *kulaka*) are the teasing speech of one of the gopis.

### 22

**22.**

```
मास्म मानिनि कृथाः श्रममुच्चै-
स्त्यज्यतां विवशतां सरसाक्षि ।
हेमगौरि गरिमाणमुपेतो
मान एष भवितैव चरिष्णुः ॥२२॥
```
*mā sma mānini kṛthāḥ śramam uccais
tyajyatāṁ vivaśatāṁ sarasākṣi |
hema-gauri garimāṇam upeto
māna eṣa bhavitaiva cariṣṇuḥ ||22||*

**Word for word:** *mā sma kṛthāḥ* — do not make; *mānini* — O proud one; *śramam* — effort; *uccaiḥ* — great; *tyajyatām* — let [it] be given up; *vivaśatām* — helplessness; *sarasa* — moist; *akṣi* — O [maiden] with eyes; *hema* — like gold; *gauri* — O fair one; *garimāṇam* — heaviness; *upetaḥ* — having attained; *mānaḥ* — pique; *eṣaḥ* — this; *bhavitā* — will become; *eva* — certainly; *cariṣṇuḥ* — fleeting.

**Translation.** "Do not torment yourself so greatly, O proud one; give up this helplessness, O moist-eyed one! O you who are fair as gold, heavy as this pique of yours has grown, it will certainly prove fleeting.

**Note.** *Māna* is the loving pique, the jealous anger of the beloved — one of the moods of the pastimes of love.

### 23

**23.**

```
पश्य मत्तहरिणाक्षि धुनाना
पल्लवं तव करस्य समानम् ।
माधवी कुसुमयौवनरम्या
बाध्यते मधुकरैरतिलुब्धैः ॥२३॥
```
*paśya matta-hariṇākṣi dhunānā
pallavaṁ tava karasya samānam |
mādhavī kusuma-yauvana-ramyā
bādhyate madhukarair ati-lubdhaiḥ ||23||*

**Word for word:** *paśya* — look; *matta* — of an intoxicated; *hariṇa* — doe; *akṣi* — O [maiden] with the eyes; *dhunānā* — waving; *pallavam* — a shoot; *tava* — to your; *karasya* — hand; *samānam* — similar; *mādhavī* — the *mādhavī* creeper; *kusuma* — of blossoms; *yauvana* — by the youth; *ramyā* — lovely; *bādhyate* — is tormented; *madhukaraiḥ* — by bees; *ati* — exceedingly; *lubdhaiḥ* — greedy.

**Translation.** Look, O you whose eyes are like an intoxicated doe's: the *mādhavī* creeper, lovely with the youth of her blossoms, waves a shoot like your hand — and the exceedingly greedy bees torment her.

**Note.** The edition has माधुरी ("sweetness"); we read *mādhavī*, the *mādhavī* creeper (the metre is unchanged), as the image requires (cf. the jasmine creeper in verse 24). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**24.**

```
धर्षितापि मधुपैरिह मल्ली-
वल्लिरुल्लसितकुड्मलरम्या ।

**Translation.** And here is the jasmine creeper, lovely with her opening buds: assailed by the bees, she waves a young shoot like a hand — but could she ever quell their onslaught?

### 25

**25.**

```

नैव नैव मदभाजि विरंसौ
सुभ्रुवो मनसि तिष्ठति मानः ॥२५॥
```
*paśya bhṛṅga-lulitā dala-kampair

**Word for word:** *paśya* — look; *bhṛṅga* — by bumblebees; *lulitā* — ruffled; *dala* — of the leaves; *kampaiḥ* — by the trembling; *evam eva* — just this; *parivakti* — proclaims; *latā* — creeper; *iyam* — this; *na eva na eva* — by no means, by no means; *mada* — intoxication; *bhāji* — experiencing; *viraṁsau* — longing to sport; *subhruvaḥ* — of a fair-browed girl; *manasi* — in the heart; *tiṣṭhati* — remains; *mānaḥ* — pique.

**Translation.** Look: this creeper, ruffled by the bumblebees, proclaims just this by the trembling of her leaves: in the heart of a fair-browed girl, intoxicated and longing for the play [of love], pique by no means, by no means remains.

### 26

**26.**

```
आश्रवं तमिमाश्लिष कान्तं
मुञ्च मुञ्च सखि मानमसन्तम् ।
कापि भावचतुरा परिहासैः
प्राणनाथमभि काञ्चिदवादीत् ॥२६॥ (कुलकम्)
```
*āśravaṁ tam imam āśliṣa kāntaṁ
muñca muñca sakhi mānam asantam |
kāpi bhāva-caturā parihāsaiḥ
prāṇa-nātham abhi kāñcid avādīt ||26|| (kulakam)*

**Word for word:** *āśravam* — obedient; *tam imam* — this very; *āśliṣa* — embrace; *kāntam* — beloved; *muñca muñca* — give up, give up; *sakhi* — O friend; *mānam* — pique; *asantam* — empty; *kā api* — a certain [gopi]; *bhāva* — in the moods [of love]; *caturā* — expert; *parihāsaiḥ* — with jests; *prāṇa-nātham abhi* — in the presence of the lord of [her] life; *kāñcit* — to a certain [proud girl]; *avādīt* — spoke.

**Translation.** Here is your obedient beloved — embrace Him! Give up, O friend, give up this empty pique!" So, in the presence of the lord of her life, a certain [maiden], expert in the moods [of love], spoke jestingly to a certain [proud girl].

**Note.** *Kulaka*: verses 22–26 form a single sentence. In the edition the first pada (*आश्रवं तमिमाश्लिष कान्तं*) has 10 syllables instead of 11 — one short syllable is missing; the transliteration *tam imam āśliṣa* restores it, and the *svāgatā* metre is then exact (the syllable *ma* was probably lost before *mā*) ⟨?⟩. *Āśliṣa* is an irregular imperative of *ā-śliṣ* (the regular form is *āśliṣya*); the poet uses it again in verse 28, where the metre confirms it. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**27.**

```

### 28

**28.**

```
स्वागतं सखि चिरादसि दृष्टा
माश्लिषेति विकसत्कुचमूलम् ।

**Word for word:** *svāgatam* — welcome; *sakhi* — O friend; *cirāt* — after a long time; *asi* — you are; *dṛṣṭā* — seen; *mām* — me; *āśliṣa* — embrace; *iti* — thus [saying]; *vikasat* — rising; *kuca* — of the breasts; *mūlam* — [so that] the base; *kā api* — a certain [gopi]; *bhāva* — by emotion; *vivaśā* — overpowered; *rabhasābhiḥ* — impetuously; *tatra* — there; *kām api* — some; *latām* — creeper; *parirebhe* — embraced.

### 29

**29.**

```
सुभ्रु वल्लिविटपेन विकृष्टं
वक्षसोऽञ्चलमलङ्कुरु मुग्धे ।

mā pated iha saroruha-kośa-

**Word for word:** *subhru* — O fair-browed one; *valli* — of a creeper; *viṭapena* — by a branch; *vikṛṣṭam* — pulled away; *vakṣasaḥ* — from the breast; *añcalam* — the edge of the garment; *alaṅkuru* — set right; *mugdhe* — O artless one; *mā patet* — may not alight; *iha* — here; *saroruha* — of a lotus; *kośa* — for the bud; *bhrāntitaḥ* — by mistake; *madhukaraḥ* — a bee; *sakhi* — O friend; *mugdhaḥ* — foolish.

**Note.** *Mugdha* is said of both: the "artless" girl and the "foolish" bee.

### 30

**30.**

```
चन्द्रिकाः किमिह तेन हि रम्या
वाञ्छितं तिमिरमेव भवत्योः ।
यत् कुहूरिति मुहुर्निगदन्तं
कोकिलं कलयसीह सतृष्णम् ॥३०॥
```
*candrikāḥ kim iha te na hi ramyāḥ
vāñchitaṁ timiram eva bhavatyoḥ |
yat kuhūr iti muhur nigadantaṁ
kokilaṁ kalayasīha satṛṣṇam ||30||*

**Word for word:** *candrikāḥ* — the moonlight; *kim* — is it that; *iha* — here; *te* — to you; *na hi* — not indeed; *ramyāḥ* — pleasing; *vāñchitam* — desired; *timiram* — darkness; *eva* — only; *bhavatyoḥ* — by you two; *yat* — since; *kuhūḥ* — "kuhu" (the new moon); *iti* — thus; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *nigadantam* — calling; *kokilam* — the cuckoo; *kalayasi* — you listen to; *iha* — here; *sa* — with; *tṛṣṇam* — thirst.

**Translation.** "Is the moonlight here not pleasing to you? It is darkness alone that you two desire — for here you listen so thirstily to the cuckoo that keeps calling 'Kuhū!'"

**Note.** A pun: *kuhū* is both the call of the cuckoo (*kokila*) and the moonless night of the new moon, favourable for meetings. The edition (like the Gosvami edition) has *tena hi*, which is syntactically obscure; we read *te na hi*, "not … to you".

### 31

**31.**

```
तद्व्रजाम इत एव विदूरं
तिष्ठ साम्प्रतमभिप्रियमेका ।
इत्यलीकवचनारचनाभि-
र्गन्तुमिष्टमतनिष्ट ततोऽन्या ॥३१॥
```
*tad vrajāma ita eva vidūraṁ
tiṣṭha sāmpratam abhipriyam ekā |
ity alīka-vacanā-racanābhir
gantum iṣṭam atanisṭa tato 'nyā ||31||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — so; *vrajāmaḥ* — let us go; *itaḥ* — from here; *eva* — indeed; *vidūram* — far away; *tiṣṭha* — stay; *sāmpratam* — now; *abhi* — with; *priyam* — the beloved; *ekā* — alone; *iti* — thus; *alīka* — feigned; *vacanā* — of words; *racanābhiḥ* — by contrivances; *gantum* — to go; *iṣṭam* — the wish; *ataniṣṭa* — made known; *tataḥ* — then; *anyā* — another.

**Translation.** "So let us go far away from here, and you stay now alone with your beloved." With such contrivances of feigned words another [friend] then made known her wish to leave.

**Note.** The edition has तिष्ट, a misprint for तिष्ठ.

### 32

**32.**

```
एतदेव कुसुमं तव रम्यं
कर्णयोरिति समुन्नतबाहुः ।
कृष्णवक्षसि मिलत्कुचकुम्भा
काचनामुमभिभूषयति स्म ॥३२॥
```
*etad eva kusumaṁ tava ramyaṁ
karṇayor iti samunnata-bāhuḥ |
kṛṣṇa-vakṣasi milat-kuca-kumbhā
kācanāmum abhibhūṣayati sma ||32||*

**Word for word:** *etat* — this; *eva* — very; *kusumam* — flower; *tava* — on You; *ramyam* — beautiful; *karṇayoḥ* — over the ears; *iti* — thus [saying]; *samunnata* — raised; *bāhuḥ* — [with] arms; *kṛṣṇa* — of Krishna; *vakṣasi* — the chest; *milat* — touching; *kuca* — of the breasts; *kumbhā* — [with] the pitchers; *kācana* — a certain [gopi]; *amum* — Him; *abhibhūṣayati sma* — adorned.

**Translation.** "This very flower looks beautiful on You — over Your ears!" And so saying, a certain [maiden], raising her arms, adorned Him, the pitchers of her bosom touching Krishna's chest.

### 33

**33.**

```
ऊरुमूलमभिबध्य भुजाभ्या-
मुच्चकैः सुमनसोऽवचिचीषुः ।
काप्युरःस्थलविलग्ननितम्बा
माधवेन कृतहर्षमुदासे ॥३३॥
```
*ūru-mūlam abhibadhya bhujābhyām
uccakaiḥ sumanaso 'vacicīṣuḥ |
kāpy uraḥ-sthala-vilagna-nitambā
mādhavena kṛta-harṣam udāse ||33||*

**Word for word:** *ūru* — of the thighs; *mūlam* — the upper part; *abhibadhya* — having clasped; *bhujābhyām* — with both arms; *uccakaiḥ* — [growing] high up; *sumanasaḥ* — flowers; *avacicīṣuḥ* — wishing to pick; *kā api* — a certain [gopi]; *uraḥ-sthala* — to the chest; *vilagna* — pressed; *nitambā* — [whose] hips; *mādhavena* — by Madhava; *kṛta* — with shown; *harṣam* — delight; *udāse* — was lifted up.

**Translation.** A certain [maiden] wished to pick flowers growing high up, and Madhava, delighted, clasped her with both arms below the hips and lifted her up, her hips pressed to His chest.

**Note.** *Udāse* is the passive perfect of *ud-as*, "to lift up"; cf. *samudāse* in verse 5.

### 34

**34.**

```
अम्बुजं मुखमिदं तव राधे
कुन्ददामवदना कुसुमैः किम् ।
इत्थमुन्नयता चिबुकाग्रं
प्रेयसी प्रियतमेन चुचुम्बे ॥३४॥
```
*ambujaṁ mukham idaṁ tava rādhe
kunda-dāma-vadanā [-radanā] kusumaiḥ kim |
ittham unnayatā cibukāgraṁ
preyasī priyatamena cucumbe ||34||*

**Word for word:** *ambujam* — a lotus; *mukham* — face; *idam* — this; *tava* — Your; *rādhe* — O Radha; *kunda* — of kunda (jasmine); *dāma* — [with] a garland; *radanā* — [You, whose] teeth; *kusumaiḥ* — with flowers; *kim* — what [need]; *ittham* — thus [saying]; *unnayatā* — lifting; *cibuka* — of the chin; *agram* — the tip; *preyasī* — the darling; *priyatamena* — by the most beloved; *cucumbe* — was kissed.

**Translation.** "This face of Yours is a lotus, O Radha, and Your teeth a garland of *kunda* jasmine — what need have You of flowers?" Saying this, the most beloved lifted the tip of His darling's chin and kissed Her.

**Note.** Here Radha is named in the sarga for the first time. The edition has *°vadanā* ("with a face"); we read *°radanā*, "with teeth": a garland of white kunda flowers is the standard image for teeth. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 35

**35.**

```
आनता कुचभरैर्मुहुरुच्चैः
पुष्पसंग्रहपरा विकलापि ।
उत्करादगलदुरःस्थलचेला
कापि कृष्णहृदये विजहार ॥३५॥
```
*ānatā kuca-bharair muhur uccaiḥ
puṣpa-saṅgraha-parā vikalāpi |
ut-karād galad-uraḥ-sthala-celā
kāpi kṛṣṇa-hṛdaye vijahāra ||35||*

**Word for word:** *ānatā* — bowed down; *kuca* — of the breasts; *bharaiḥ* — by the weight; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *uccaiḥ* — up high; *puṣpa* — flowers; *saṅgraha* — on gathering; *parā* — intent; *vikalā* — flustered; *api* — though; *ut* — raised; *karāt* — because of the hand; *galat* — slipping; *uraḥ-sthala* — from the bosom; *celā* — [whose] cloth; *kā api* — a certain [gopi]; *kṛṣṇa* — of Krishna; *hṛdaye* — on the heart; *vijahāra* — sported.

**Translation.** Another, though bowed down by the weight of her bosom and flustered, again and again reached up, intent on gathering flowers; and as the cloth slipped from her bosom because her hand was raised, she sported upon Krishna's heart.

**Note.** The edition has उत्करा द्गलद्, a wrong division of words; we read *utkarād galad-*.

### 36

**36.**

```
लीनमप्यलिमवेक्ष्य हरन्ती
केशवं कररुहैरथ वीक्ष्य ।
संभ्रमभ्रमिवशादवशाङ्गी
निर्ममज्ज दयितोरसि काचित् ॥३६॥
```
*līnam apy alim avekṣya harantī
keśavaṁ kara-ruhair atha vīkṣya |
sambhrama-bhrami-vaśād avaśāṅgī
nirmamajja dayitorasi kācit ||36||*

**Word for word:** *līnam* — nestled (merged); *api* — even; *alim* — a bee; *avekṣya* — having noticed; *harantī* — removing; *keśavam* — Keshava; *kara-ruhaiḥ* — with her fingernails; *atha* — then; *vīkṣya* — having seen; *sambhrama* — of confusion; *bhrami* — of the giddiness; *vaśāt* — under the sway; *avaśa* — helpless; *aṅgī* — [whose] limbs; *nirmamajja* — sank; *dayita* — of the beloved; *urasi* — on the chest; *kācit* — a certain [gopi].

**Translation.** A certain [maiden] noticed a bee nestled [on Krishna] and went to remove it with her fingernails — and then saw [that she was touching] Keshava. In the whirl of her confusion her limbs grew helpless, and she sank upon her beloved's chest.

**Note.** The bee, dark like Krishna, merges with Him, and in removing it the gopi touches Keshava Himself with her nails.

### 37

**37.**

```
प्रोज्झ्य फुल्लकुसुमावलिमेतां
कुड्मलेषु निपतिष्यति मुग्धः ।
भृङ्गरागपरवानसि तत्त्वं
रज्यतां मनसि को हि विवेकः ॥३७॥
```
*projjhya phulla-kusumāvalim etāṁ
kuḍmaleṣu nipatiṣyati mugdhaḥ |
bhṛṅga-rāga-paravān asi tat tvaṁ
rajyatāṁ manasi ko hi vivekaḥ ||37||*

**Word for word:** *projjhya* — having abandoned; *phulla* — of full-blown; *kusuma* — flowers; *āvalim* — the row; *etām* — this; *kuḍmaleṣu* — on the buds; *nipatiṣyati* — will alight; *mugdhaḥ* — the fool; *bhṛṅga* — of a bee; *rāga* — by the ardour (colour); *paravān* — enslaved; *asi* — are; *tat* — therefore; *tvam* — you; *rajyatām* — of those who are dyed [with ardour]; *manasi* — in the mind; *kaḥ* — what; *hi* — indeed; *vivekaḥ* — discernment.

**Translation.** "Abandoning this row of full-blown flowers, the fool will alight on the buds! You are enslaved by a bee's *rāga* — and what discernment, indeed, is there in the minds of those who are dyed [with it]?

**Note.** The beginning of a *kulaka* (37–39): the gopi addresses the bee but aims at Krishna. A pun: *rāga* means both "ardour" and "colour"; *bhṛṅga-rāga* is both "a bee's ardour" and "blackness". The syntax of *tat tvaṁ rajyatām* is unclear; we take *rajyatām* as the genitive plural of the participle, "of those who are dyed" ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 38

**38.**

```
श्यामलोऽसि सततं मधुमत्तः
पद्मिनीषु निरतश्चपलोऽसि ।
चञ्चरीकसदृशोऽसि ततस्त्वं
कस्यचिन्ननु समस्तगुणेन ॥३८॥
```
*śyāmalo 'si satataṁ madhu-mattaḥ
padminīṣu nirataś capalo 'si |
cañcarīka-sadṛśo 'si tatas tvaṁ
kasyacin nanu samasta-guṇena ||38||*

**Word for word:** *śyāmalaḥ* — dark; *asi* — you are; *satatam* — always; *madhu* — with honey (wine, spring); *mattaḥ* — intoxicated; *padminīṣu* — to lotus plants (to *padminīs*); *nirataḥ* — attached; *capalaḥ* — fickle; *asi* — you are; *cañcarīka* — O bee; *sadṛśaḥ* — similar; *asi* — you are; *tataḥ* — therefore; *tvam* — you; *kasyacit* — to a certain someone; *nanu* — surely; *samasta* — in every; *guṇena* — quality.

**Translation.** You are dark, ever drunk with honey, attached to lotuses, fickle — so surely, O bee, you resemble a certain someone in every quality!

**Note.** *Padminī* is both "lotus plant" and a *padminī*, the highest of the types of women in the classical typology.

### 39

**39.**

```
संविमर्दनसहासहतां नो
वेत्सि मुग्धतम रागपरोऽसि ।
इत्थमात्तकुतुकं कृतरोषा
कापि कृष्णमपदिश्य जगाद ॥३९॥ (कुलकम्)
```
*saṁvimardana-sahāsahatāṁ no
vetsi mugdhatama rāga-paro 'si |
ittham ātta-kutukaṁ kṛta-roṣā
kāpi kṛṣṇam apadiśya jagāda ||39|| (kulakam)*

**Word for word:** *saṁvimardana* — the crushing onslaught; *saha* — the ability to bear; *asahatām* — [and] the inability; *no* — not (or *naḥ* — our); *vetsi* — you know; *mugdhatama* — O most foolish one; *rāga* — to ardour; *paraḥ* — given; *asi* — you are; *ittham* — thus; *ātta* — having assumed; *kutukam* — playfulness; *kṛta* — feigning; *roṣā* — anger; *kā api* — a certain [gopi]; *kṛṣṇam* — at Krishna; *apadiśya* — hinting; *jagāda* — spoke.

**Translation.** You do not know whether we can bear your onslaught or not, O most foolish one — you are given to ardour alone!" So, playfully feigning anger, a certain [maiden] spoke, hinting at Krishna.

**Note.** *No* may be taken as *na u*, "not", or as *naḥ*, "our"; in the latter case the line is a reproachful question: "Do you know what we can bear and what we cannot?"

**40.**

```

śrotum eva na nirāsa karābhyāṁ

### 41

**41.**

```
मन्थरा तव गतिः सहजैषा
तत्र चेत् प्रतिपदं रमणेन ।
प्रस्थिता तदिह किं चलितव्यं
पश्य सुन्दरि तदत्र निकुञ्जम् ॥४१॥
```
*mantharā tava gatiḥ sahajaiṣā
tatra cet pratipadaṁ ramaṇena |
prasthitā tad iha kiṁ calitavyaṁ

**Word for word:** *mantharā* — slow; *tava* — your; *gatiḥ* — gait; *sahajā* — natural; *eṣā* — this; *tatra* — moreover; *cet* — if; *prati-padam* — at every step; *ramaṇena* — with the beloved; *prasthitā* — [you] have set out; *tat* — then; *iha* — here; *kim* — why; *calitavyam* — [should one] go on; *paśya* — look; *sundari* — O beautiful one; *tat* — that; *atra* — here; *nikuñjam* — bower.

**Translation.** "Your gait is slow by nature; and if, besides, you have set out with your beloved, [lingering] at every step, why go any further? Look, O beautiful one: there is a bower right here!

**Note.** Verses 41–42 form a single speech.

### 42

**42.**

```
यः श्रुतौ लपति ते भ्रमरोऽयं
श्यामलोत्पलदलान्तरितः सन् ।
नावगच्छसि किमेतदितीदं

```
*yaḥ śrutau lapati te bhramaro 'yaṁ
śyāmalotpala-dalāntaritaḥ san |
nāvagacchasi kim etad itīdaṁ
kāpi kāñcid iti sasmitam ūce ||42||*

**Word for word:** *yaḥ* — which; *śrutau* — at the ear; *lapati* — whispers; *te* — your; *bhramaraḥ* — bee; *ayam* — this; *śyāmala* — of the dark; *utpala* — lotus; *dala* — by the petal; *antaritaḥ* — hidden; *san* — being; *na* — not; *avagacchasi* — do you understand; *kim* — what; *etat* — this; *iti* — thus; *idam* — this; *kā api* — a certain [gopi]; *kāñcit* — to another; *iti* — thus; *sa* — with; *smitam* — a smile; *ūce* — said.

**Translation.** This bee, hiding behind the petal of the dark lotus at your ear and whispering into it — do you not understand what it is saying?" So, with a smile, one [maiden] said to another.

**Note.** The dark lotus is an ornament worn over the ear. *Ava-gam* means both "to understand" and "to go"; the question may also be heard as "why do you not go?"

### 43

**43.**

```
कीदृशीं स्रजमहं रचयेयं
कम्बुकण्ठि तव कण्ठतटाय ।
इत्यसौ सकुतुकं दयिताया

```
*kīdṛśīṁ srajam ahaṁ racayeyaṁ
kambu-kaṇṭhi tava kaṇṭha-taṭāya |
ity asau sa-kutukaṁ dayitāyā
vakṣaso vasanam āśu jahāra ||43||*

**Word for word:** *kīdṛśīm* — what kind of; *srajam* — garland; *aham* — I; *racayeyam* — should weave; *kambu* — [like] a conch; *kaṇṭhi* — O [maiden] whose neck; *tava* — your; *kaṇṭha* — of the neck; *taṭāya* — for the slope; *iti* — thus [saying]; *asau* — He; *sa* — with; *kutukam* — mischief; *dayitāyāḥ* — of the beloved; *vakṣasaḥ* — from the bosom; *vasanam* — the cloth; *āśu* — swiftly; *jahāra* — took away.

**Translation.** "What kind of garland should I weave for the slope of your neck, O you whose neck is like a conch?" Saying this, He mischievously, in an instant, took away the cloth from His beloved's bosom.

**Note.** A neck "like a conch" has three folds, a mark of beauty.

### 44

**44.**

```
कापि पुष्पमयकन्दुकवृन्दं
प्राहिणोदघरिपुं परि शश्वत् ।
चन्द्रमोभिरिव तन्मधुरिम्णा-

```

candramobhir iva tan-madhurimṇām

**Word for word:** *kā api* — a certain [gopi]; *puṣpa-maya* — made of flowers; *kanduka* — of balls; *vṛndam* — a multitude; *prāhiṇot* — threw; *agha* — of Agha; *ripum* — at the Enemy; *pari* — all around; *śaśvat* — incessantly; *candramobhiḥ* — with moons; *iva* — as if; *tat* — His; *madhurimṇām* — to the sweetnesses; *aupahārikam* — an offering; *amandam* — abundant; *akārṣīt* — made.

**Translation.** A certain [maiden] incessantly threw a multitude of flower-balls all around the Enemy of Agha, as though making an abundant offering of moons to His sweetness.

**Note.** The edition has अघदिपुं, a misprint for अघरिपुं. The white flower-balls are compared to moons. The sense of the second half is rendered approximately. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 45

**45.**

```
फुल्लचूतलतिकापरिरम्भैः
पिञ्जरः पिकयुवा मधुमत्तः ।
मन्मथं कलयतीव विशेषं
मन्मनो विकलमेव बभूव ॥४५॥
```
*phulla-cūta-latikā-parirambhaiḥ
piñjaraḥ pika-yuvā madhu-mattaḥ |
manmathaṁ kalayatīva viśeṣaṁ
man-mano vikalam eva babhūva ||45||*

**Word for word:** *phulla* — of blossoming; *cūta* — mango; *latikā* — branches; *parirambhaiḥ* — by the embraces; *piñjaraḥ* — turned yellow; *pika* — cuckoo; *yuvā* — young; *madhu* — with spring (with honey); *mattaḥ* — intoxicated; *manmatham* — Manmatha; *kalayati* — summons; *iva* — as if; *viśeṣam* — especially; *mat* — my; *manaḥ* — heart; *vikalam* — distraught; *eva* — utterly; *babhūva* — has become.

**Translation.** "The young cuckoo, intoxicated with spring and turned golden-yellow by the embraces of the blossoming mango branches, seems to be summoning Manmatha in earnest — and my heart has become utterly distraught.

**Note.** With this verse there seems to begin the speech of one of the gopis to the proud girl (45–47). The speaker is not named, however: the *iti* of verse 48, whose subject is Krishna, may also point to Krishna Himself as the speaker, "the beloved" and "the lord" (46–47) then being His words about Himself ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 46

**46.**

```
मा कृथाः कथमपि प्रथयानं
मानमानय मधूनि ददस्व ।
मानयोऽयमभिनाथमजस्रं
मानिनि प्रकटमानवशत्वम् ॥४६॥
```
*mā kṛthāḥ katham api prathayānaṁ
mānam ānaya madhūni dadasva |
mā[a]nayo 'yam abhinātham ajasraṁ
mānini prakaṭa-māna-vaśatvam ||46||*

**Word for word:** *mā kṛthāḥ* — do not make; *katham api* — in any way; *prathayānam* (*prathamānam*?) — growing; *mānam* — pique; *ānaya* — bring; *madhūni* — honey (sweet drink); *dadasva* — give; *anayaḥ* — bad policy; *ayam* — this; *abhi-nātham* — towards the lord; *ajasram* — constant; *mānini* — O proud one; *prakaṭa* — open; *māna* — to pique; *vaśatvam* — subjection.

**Translation.** Do not in any way let your pique grow, O proud one; bring honey and offer it [to Him]! To be constantly and openly in the power of pique towards your lord is bad policy.

**Note.** The verse plays on the sounds of *māna*: *mānam ānaya*, *mānayo*, *mānini*, *māna-*. The beginning of the third pada can also be understood as *mā* + *anayaḥ*, "let there not be this folly". *Prathayānam* is an irregular form; one would expect *prathamānam*. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**48.**

```

**49.**

```

**51.**

```

**52.**

```
स्निग्धसान्द्रघननीलतरङ्गै-

### 54

**54.**

```
चुम्बितानि नखदन्तनिपातान्
प्रायशः सरभसं विलपय्य ।
तौ परस्परजयोत्सुकचित्तौ

```
*cumbitāni nakha-danta-nipātān
prāyaśaḥ sarabhasaṁ vilapayya |
tau paraspara-jayotsuka-cittau
siñcataḥ kara-jalair hṛdayeśau ||54||*

**Word for word:** *cumbitāni* — kisses; *nakha* — of nails; *danta* — [and] of teeth; *nipātān* — the marks; *prāyaśaḥ* — almost all; *sa* — with; *rabhasam* — eagerness; *vilapayya* — having washed away (?); *tau* — the two; *paraspara* — over each other; *jaya* — for victory; *utsuka* — eager; *cittau* — [whose] hearts; *siñcataḥ* — splash; *kara* — of the hands; *jalaiḥ* — with the water; *hṛdaya* — of hearts; *īśau* — the two lords.

**Translation.** Eagerly washing away almost all [the traces of] kisses and the marks of nails and teeth, the two lords of each other's hearts, each eager for victory over the other, splashed one another with handfuls of water.

**Note.** The edition (like the Gosvami edition) has *vilapayya*. We take it as a gerund of the causative of *vi-lī*, "to dissolve, wash away" (*vilāpayati*): in the water the traces of the pastimes are washed off ⟨?⟩. A conjectural reading *vipalāya*, "having fled [from each other]", is also possible.

**55.**

```

**Word for word:** *vāri* — in the water; *vāritatamā* — utterly repulsed; *kara* — of the hands; *nīraiḥ* — by the water; *preyasā* — by the beloved; *kim api* — a certain [gopi]; *nitya* — ever; *navīnā* — youthful; *vāribhiḥ* — with the water; *milati* — having merged; *sūkṣma* — fine; *dukūle* — the silk; *kūlam* — to the bank; *ujjigamiṣuḥ* — wishing to go up; *kim* — was it [because]; *udasthāt* — rose.

**Translation.** A certain ever-youthful [maiden], utterly repulsed in the water by the handfuls of water [thrown by] her beloved, rose up wishing to go up onto the bank — was it because her fine silk had merged with the water?

**56.**

śliṣyatā priyatamena salīlam |
srotasāpahṛtam aṁśukam acche
vāri gopitum ivāṅgam abhāji ||56||*

**Word for word:** *subhruvaḥ* — of a fair-browed [gopi]; *adhi-yamunam* — in the Yamuna; *ślatha* — loosened; *nīvyāḥ* — [whose] waistband knot; *śliṣyatā* — embracing; *priyatamena* — by the most beloved; *salīlam* — playfully; *srotasā* — by the current; *apahṛtam* — carried off; *aṁśukam* — [her] garment; *acche* — in the clear; *vāri* — water; *gopitum* — to hide; *iva* — as if; *aṅgam* — the body; *abhāji* — was taken [into His arms].

**Translation.** In the Yamuna a fair-browed [maiden's] waistband came loose, and the current carried off her garment; her most beloved, playfully embracing her, took her body into His arms, as though to hide it in the clear water.

**62.**

```
सर्वतः करदलाहतिरोह-

**79.**

```
रत्नभित्तिषु निजप्रतिबिम्बै-
र्भूयसीं तनुरुचिं कलयन्त्यः ।

```
*ratna-bhittiṣu nija-pratibimbair
bhūyasīṁ tanu-ruciṁ kalayantyaḥ |
yatra vismaya-vaśaṁ rabhaseṣu
prāpnuvanti cakitaiṇa-dṛśas tāḥ ||79||*

**Word for word:** *ratna* — jewelled; *bhittiṣu* — on the walls; *nija* — by their own; *pratibimbaiḥ* — reflections; *bhūyasīm* — multiplied; *tanu* — of [their] bodies; *rucim* — the radiance; *kalayantyaḥ* — perceiving; *yatra* — where; *vismaya* — of wonder; *vaśam* — into the power; *rabhaseṣu* — in [their] eagerness; *prāpnuvanti* — come; *cakita* — of a startled; *eṇa* — doe; *dṛśaḥ* — [with] the eyes; *tāḥ* — they.

**Translation.** There, perceiving on the jewelled walls the radiance of their bodies multiplied by their own reflections, they, with eyes like a startled doe's, in their eagerness fell under the sway of wonder.

**Note.** With this verse begins the description (through *yatra*, "where") of the jewelled pavilion which the gopis enter after bathing in order to adorn themselves. The word to which all these "where"s refer is *ālayam* ("pavilion") in verse 93, which concludes the *kulaka* (79–93).

### 80

**80.**

```
प्रेयसा परिहृते तनुचेले

**Word for word:** *preyasā* — by the beloved; *parihṛte* — when [it] was drawn away; *tanu* — thin; *cele* — the garment; *dīpaḥ* — a lamp; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *abhinirīkṣya* — having looked; *pidhitsuḥ* — wishing to cover; *yatra* — where; *ratna* — of the gems; *kiraṇān prati* — the rays; *bālā* — a young maiden; *sa* — with; *trapā* — shame; *ajani* — became; *ciram* — for long; *pratibimbaiḥ* — because of the reflections.

**Translation.** There a young maiden, when her beloved drew away her thin garment, looked at the rays of the gems, took them for a lamp and wished to cover them — and for a long time was abashed by her reflections.

**Note.** A familiar motif of kavya: the shy heroine tries to put out or cover the gems, taking them for a lamp.

### 81

**81.**

```
सुस्मितैर्हिममयूखमयूखैः

**Word for word:** *su-smitaiḥ* — smiling; *hima* — cool-; *mayūkha* — rayed [moon]; *mayūkhaiḥ* — by the rays; *saṁsravantī* — oozing; *aniśam* — ceaselessly; *indu* — moon-; *maṇīnām* — of stones; *vediḥ* — the dais; *aikṣi* — was seen; *kila* — as it were; *yatra* — where; *payobhiḥ* — of waters; *sphāṭikaiḥ* — crystal; *viracitā* — built; *iti* — thus; *vadhūbhiḥ* — by the young women.

**82.**

```

**Word for word:** *śoṇa* — red; *ratna* — gems; *maya* — made of; *vīthiṣu* — in the galleries; *kāścit* — some; *bhūṣaṇāya* — for adornment; *muditāḥ* — delighted; *svam* — themselves; *abhīkṣya* — having seen; *yatra* — where; *kuṅkuma* — of kunkuma; *rasena* — with the liquid; *kadācit* — ever; *na* — not; *aṅga* — of the body; *rāgam* — the colouring; *anurāgataḥ* — from the redness (from love); *īṣuḥ* — wished.

### 94

**94.**

```
श्रीमद्भिः परभृतबर्हिकीरहंसैः
सत्पारावतमधुपावलीकपोतैः ।

```

**Word for word:** *śrīmadbhiḥ* — by splendid; *parabhṛta* — cuckoos; *barhi* — peacocks; *kīra* — parrots; *haṁsaiḥ* — [and] swans; *sat* — by fine; *pārāvata* — turtle-doves; *madhupa* — of bees; *āvalī* — swarms; *kapotaiḥ* — [and] pigeons; *anyonya* — mutually; *sva* — their own [voices]; *parivardhataḥ* — from increasing; *api* — and; *apūrvam* — unprecedented; *sambheje* — attained; *śravaṇa* — for the ears; *rasāyanatvam* — the quality of an elixir; *uccaiḥ* — in the highest degree.

**Translation.** Through the splendid cuckoos, peacocks, parrots and swans, the fine turtle-doves, the swarms of bees and the pigeons, each raising its own voice above the others', [the pavilion] became in the highest degree an unprecedented elixir for the ears.

**Note.** The metre is *praharṣiṇī*. The subject of *sambheje* is not named; by the sense it is the "pavilion" of verse 93 ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 95

**95.**

```
उद्गच्छन्तीष्वथ वरवधूष्वालयान्तेषु जाता
नानापुष्पैः सुरभिमधुरैः कल्पवल्ल्यः समन्तात् ।
चक्रुर्नीराजनमिव मुहुः कूजितैः कोकिलानां
संकुर्वत्यो जय जय जयेत्युच्चकैर्हर्षनादम् ॥९५॥
```
*udgacchantīṣv atha vara-vadhūṣv ālayānteṣu jātā
nānā-puṣpaiḥ surabhi-madhuraiḥ kalpa-vallyaḥ samantāt |
cakrur nīrājanam iva muhuḥ kūjitaiḥ kokilānāṁ
saṅkurvatyo jaya jaya jayety uccakair harṣa-nādam ||95||*

**Word for word:** *udgacchantīṣu* — as [they] went up; *atha* — then; *vara* — lovely; *vadhūṣu* — the young women; *ālaya* — of the pavilion; *anteṣu* — within; *jātāḥ* — grown; *nānā* — with various; *puṣpaiḥ* — flowers; *surabhi* — fragrant; *madhuraiḥ* — sweet; *kalpa* — wish-fulfilling; *vallyaḥ* — creepers; *samantāt* — on every side; *cakruḥ* — performed; *nīrājanam* — the rite of *nīrājana*; *iva* — as it were; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *kūjitaiḥ* — with the singing; *kokilānām* — of cuckoos; *saṅkurvatyaḥ* — raising; *jaya jaya jaya* — victory, victory, victory; *iti* — thus; *uccakaiḥ* — loudly; *harṣa* — of joy; *nādam* — a cry.

**Translation.** Then, as the lovely young women went up [into the pavilion], the wish-fulfilling creepers grown within it on every side performed, as it were, *nīrājana* over them with various fragrant, sweet flowers, and with the singing of the cuckoos again and again loudly raised a joyful cry: "Victory, victory, victory!"

**Note.** The metre is *mandākrāntā*. *Nīrājana* is a rite of welcome: a lamp, flowers and the like are waved before one who is honoured.
