# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Eight — verses 1–63 (printed pp. 236–248)

**Title of the sarga:** *Śrī-vṛndāvana-vihāra-varṇana*, "The Description of the Pastimes in Vrindavana" (from the colophon).
**Metre:** *anuṣṭubh* (*śloka*); verse 61 — *śikhariṇī*; verses 62–63 — *vasanta-tilakā*.
**Total verses:** 63.

**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–9 — a leper begs to be healed; the Lord refuses: "You hated Srivasa"; the Vaishnavas are His life-airs moving outside [the body].
- 10–16 — a brahmana who was not admitted to the kirtana breaks his sacred thread and curses Gaura: "May Your samsara be cut off!"; the Lord rejoices at the curse as at a blessing.
- 17–26 — Gaura in the mood of **Balarama**: "Give [Me] honey!"; He turns white like a mountain of silver; with a plough, in a blue garment; in the house of Murari Gupta.
- 27–36 — He hurls away a strong brahmana; the days of absorption; "I have seen a man with a plough, like a mountain of silver"; Chandrashekhara Acharya sees Balarama in Gaura.
- 37–46 — the dance in the mood of Balarama; an intoxicating fragrance, swarms of bees; Sri Rama [Pandita] sees Balarama's associates descended from the sky; Vanamali sees a golden plough as large as a mountain.
- 47–55 — a rain of flowers from the gods; dawn; bathing and water-play in the Ganga.
- 56–60 — "Give Me the flute!" — "The gopis are guarding it"; the Lord in bliss: "Speak, speak!" — Srivasa tells of the pastimes in Vrindavana and on the Yamuna.
- 61–63 — Srivasa's speech on Krishna's ever-new pastimes in Vrindavana; "Speak more!"

**Textual cruxes:** 22 (*dadhānam*), 24 (*uktvāmbu-*), 25 (*śauklyam*/*śaityam*), 32, 35 (the cases), 36 (*āveśāveśa*), 40 (*śrī-yute*, *samapūrayat*), 52 (*samādhunvan*), 53 (*upoṣivān*) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
कदाचिदथ तं प्रीत्या गच्छन्तं परमं प्रभुम् ।
प्रणम्य विनयात् कश्चित् प्रोवाच मधुराक्षरम् ॥१॥
```
*kadācid atha taṁ prītyā gacchantaṁ paramaṁ prabhum |
praṇamya vinayāt kaścit provāca madhurākṣaram ||1||*

**Word for word:** *kadācit* — once; *atha* — then; *tam* — to Him; *prītyā* — with affection; *gacchantam* — walking; *paramam* — to the Supreme; *prabhum* — Lord; *praṇamya* — having bowed down; *vinayāt* — humbly; *kaścit* — someone; *provāca* — spoke; *madhura* — sweet; *akṣaram* — with words.

**Translation.** Then once, as the Supreme Lord was walking [along the road], someone humbly bowed down to Him with affection and spoke to Him in sweet words:

### 2

**2.**

```
सर्व्वे त्वां देवदेवेशं सच्चिद्घनशरीरिणम् ।
पुरुषं परमं प्राहुस्तन्नोद्धरसि किं नु माम् ॥२॥
```
*sarve tvāṁ deva-deveśaṁ sac-cid-ghana-śarīriṇam |
puruṣaṁ paramaṁ prāhus tan noddharasi kiṁ nu mām ||2||*

**Word for word:** *sarve* — all; *tvām* — You; *deva* — of the gods; *deva* — of the gods; *īśam* — the Lord; *sat* — of being; *cit* — of consciousness; *ghana* — concentrated; *śarīriṇam* — possessing a body; *puruṣam* — the Person; *paramam* — Supreme; *prāhuḥ* — call; *tat* — so; *na* — not; *uddharasi* — You deliver; *kim nu* — why then; *mām* — me.

**Translation.** "All call You the Lord of the gods of gods, the Supreme Person whose body is concentrated being and consciousness. Why then do You not deliver me?

### 3

**3.**

```
त्राहि मां सर्व्व सर्व्वेश कुष्ठात् परमगर्हितात् ।
दोधूयमानहृदयं कृपां कुरु कृपानिधे ॥३॥
```
*trāhi māṁ sarva sarveśa kuṣṭhāt parama-garhitāt |
dodhūyamāna-hṛdayaṁ kṛpāṁ kuru kṛpā-nidhe ||3||*

**Word for word:** *trāhi* — save; *mām* — me; *sarva* — O All; *sarva* — of all; *īśa* — O Lord; *kuṣṭhāt* — from leprosy; *parama* — utterly; *garhitāt* — loathsome; *dodhūyamāna* — tormented; *hṛdayam* — [me,] whose heart; *kṛpām* — mercy; *kuru* — show; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *nidhe* — O treasure-house.

**Translation.** O All, O Lord of all, save me from this utterly loathsome leprosy! My heart is tormented — show mercy, O treasure-house of mercy!"

**Note.** This is the same leper, a hater of Srivasa, as in Sarga Six, verse 73 (by tradition, Gopala Chapala; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.37–58).

### 4

**4.**

```
श्रुत्वेदं तद्वचः श्रीमान् क्रोधारुणितलोचनः ।
जगाद वदनव्याजाद्द्विजराजेन शोभितः ॥४॥
```
*śrutvedaṁ tad-vacaḥ śrīmān krodhāruṇita-locanaḥ |
jagāda vadana-vyājād dvija-rājena śobhitaḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *śrutvā* — having heard; *idam* — these; *tat* — his; *vacaḥ* — words; *śrīmān* — the beautiful one; *krodha* — with anger; *aruṇita* — reddened; *locanaḥ* — whose eyes; *jagāda* — spoke; *vadana* — of the face; *vyājāt* — in the guise; *dvija-rājena* — by the king of the twice-born (the moon); *śobhitaḥ* — adorned.

**Translation.** Having heard these words of his, the beautiful [Lord], adorned with the king of the twice-born (the moon) in the guise of His face, spoke, His eyes reddened with anger:

**Note.** *Dvija-rāja*, "king of the twice-born", is both the moon (which is "born twice", rising anew) and the best of brahmanas. Gaura's face is the moon.

### 5

**5.**

```
आः पापात्मन् दुराचार मद्भक्तद्वेषकारक ।
त्वामुद्धरिष्ये चेन्नाहमुद्धरिष्यामि कं ततः ॥५॥
```
*āḥ pāpātman durācāra mad-bhakta-dveṣa-kāraka |
tvām uddhariṣye cen nāham uddhariṣyāmi kaṁ tataḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *āḥ* — ah; *pāpa* — sinful; *ātman* — O soul; *durācāra* — O evil-doer; *mat* — of My; *bhakta* — devotees; *dveṣa* — hatred; *kāraka* — O you who harbour; *tvām* — you; *uddhariṣye* — I shall deliver; *cet* — if; *na* — not; *aham* — I; *uddhariṣyāmi* — shall deliver; *kam* — whom; *tataḥ* — then.

**Translation.** "Ah, O sinful soul, O evil-doer who harbour hatred for My devotees! If I were to deliver you, then whom would I not deliver?

**Note.** The edition has *kiṁ tataḥ* ("what of it?"), and the line is obscure; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *kaṁ tataḥ*, "whom then [would I not deliver]", and so we read.

### 6

**6.**

```
श्रीवासस्य सदा द्वेषं यतस्त्वं कृतवानसि ।
अतएव प्रतिभवं कुष्ठी खलु भविष्यसि ॥६॥
```
*śrīvāsasya sadā dveṣaṁ yatas tvaṁ kṛtavān asi |

**Word for word:** *śrīvāsasya* — towards Srivasa; *sadā* — always; *dveṣam* — hatred; *yataḥ* — since; *tvam* — you; *kṛtavān asi* — have harboured; *ataḥ eva* — therefore; *prati* — in every; *bhavam* — birth; *kuṣṭhī* — a leper; *khalu* — certainly; *bhaviṣyasi* — you will be.

**Translation.** Since you have always harboured hatred towards Srivasa, you will therefore certainly be a leper in every birth.

### 7

**7.**

```
अस्मिन् देहे तु ये प्राणास्ते न लक्ष्याः कदाचन ।
बहिश्चरा इव प्राणा वैष्णवा इति विद्धि मे ॥७॥
```
*asmin dehe tu ye prāṇās te na lakṣyāḥ kadācana |
bahiś-carā iva prāṇā vaiṣṇavā iti viddhi me ||7||*

**Word for word:** *asmin* — in this; *dehe* — body; *tu* — but; *ye* — which; *prāṇāḥ* — life-airs; *te* — they; *na* — not; *lakṣyāḥ* — visible; *kadācana* — ever; *bahiḥ* — outside; *carāḥ* — moving; *iva* — as if; *prāṇāḥ* — life-airs; *vaiṣṇavāḥ* — the Vaishnavas; *iti* — thus; *viddhi* — know; *me* — My.

**Translation.** The life-airs that are in this body are never visible; but know that the Vaishnavas are My life-airs, moving, as it were, outside [the body].

**Note.** *Bahiś-cara prāṇa*, "life moving outside the body": the devotees are the very life of the Lord (cf. Srimad-Bhagavatam 9.4.68: *sādhavo hṛdayaṁ mahyam*). The edition has *लक्ष्या* without the visarga.

### 8

**8.**

```
ये ये येन प्रकारेण तान् द्विषन्ति मम प्रियान् ।
तेषां तेषां प्रतिभवं नरके पतनं भवेत् ॥८॥
```
*ye ye yena prakāreṇa tān dviṣanti mama priyān |
teṣāṁ teṣāṁ prati-bhavaṁ narake patanaṁ bhavet ||8||*

**Word for word:** *ye ye* — whoever; *yena* — in whatever; *prakāreṇa* — way; *tān* — those; *dviṣanti* — hate; *mama* — My; *priyān* — dear ones; *teṣām teṣām* — of each of them; *prati* — in every; *bhavam* — birth; *narake* — into hell; *patanam* — a fall; *bhavet* — there will be.

**Translation.** Whoever, in whatever way, hates those dear ones of Mine — each of them will fall into hell in every birth.

### 9

**9.**

```
वैष्णवेभ्यो नता ये च ये तदाज्ञापरायणाः ।
ते त एव तरिष्यन्ति संसारार्णवमुत्कटम् ॥९॥
```

**Word for word:** *vaiṣṇavebhyaḥ* — before the Vaishnavas; *natāḥ* — bow down; *ye* — who; *ca* — and; *ye* — who; *tat* — to their; *ājñā* — commands; *parāyaṇāḥ* — devoted; *te te* — they; *eva* — alone; *tariṣyanti* — will cross; *saṁsāra* — of samsara; *arṇavam* — the ocean; *utkaṭam* — fearsome.

**10.**

```
तेन सार्द्धं तदा रेमे भगवान् भक्तभक्तिमान् ॥१०॥
```

### 11

**11.**

```
एकदा नृत्यसमये द्रष्टुं गौराङ्गसुन्दरम् ।
चलितो द्वारपालेन वारितो धरणीसुरः ॥११॥
```
*ekadā nṛtya-samaye draṣṭuṁ gaurāṅga-sundaram |
calito dvāra-pālena vārito dharaṇī-suraḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *ekadā* — once; *nṛtya* — of the dancing; *samaye* — at the time; *draṣṭum* — to see; *gaurāṅga* — Gauranga; *sundaram* — the beautiful; *calitaḥ* — having set out; *dvāra* — of the door; *pālena* — by the keeper; *vāritaḥ* — was stopped; *dharaṇī-suraḥ* — a brahmana (lit. "a god on earth").

**Translation.** Once, at the time of the dancing, a brahmana set out to see the beautiful Gauranga, but was stopped by the doorkeeper.

**Note.** The kirtanas in Srivasa's house were held behind closed doors (Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 8 and 26; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.41).

### 12

**12.**

```
क्रुद्धोऽपरदिने सोऽयं गङ्गायास्तटसन्निधौ ।
दृष्ट्वा जगत्प्रभुं तत्र दुर्मुखो रोषलोहितः ॥१२॥
```
*kruddho 'para-dine so 'yaṁ gaṅgāyās taṭa-sannidhau |
dṛṣṭvā jagat-prabhuṁ tatra durmukho roṣa-lohitaḥ ||12||*

**Word for word:** *kruddhaḥ* — angry; *apara* — on another; *dine* — day; *saḥ ayam* — this man; *gaṅgāyāḥ* — of the Ganga; *taṭa* — the bank; *sannidhau* — near; *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *jagat* — of the world; *prabhum* — the Lord; *tatra* — there; *durmukhaḥ* — foul-mouthed; *roṣa* — with fury; *lohitaḥ* — red.

**Translation.** On another day this foul-mouthed [brahmana], [still] angry, saw the Lord of the world there, near the bank of the Ganga, and turned red with fury.

### 13

**13.**

```
उपवीतं द्विधा छित्त्वा शापं दास्यन्निदं जगौ ।
त्वां नृत्यसमये द्रष्टुं गतवानहमेकदा ॥१३॥
```
*upavītaṁ dvidhā chittvā śāpaṁ dāsyann idaṁ jagau |
tvāṁ nṛtya-samaye draṣṭuṁ gatavān aham ekadā ||13||*

**Word for word:** *upavītam* — the sacred thread; *dvidhā* — in two; *chittvā* — having broken; *śāpam* — a curse; *dāsyan* — about to pronounce; *idam* — this; *jagau* — said; *tvām* — You; *nṛtya* — of the dancing; *samaye* — at the time; *draṣṭum* — to see; *gatavān* — went; *aham* — I; *ekadā* — once.

**Translation.** Breaking his sacred thread in two, about to pronounce a curse, he said this: "Once I went to see You at the time of the dancing,

**Note.** Breaking the sacred thread (*upavīta*) is the gesture of a brahmana's curse (cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.33–35: "May Your worldly life be destroyed!").

### 14

**14.**

```
तवैव द्वारपालेन वारितस्तेन दुःखितः ।
शापं ददामि तत्तुभ्यं संसारच्छित्तिरस्तु ते ॥१४॥
```
*tavaiva dvāra-pālena vāritas tena duḥkhitaḥ |
śāpaṁ dadāmi tat tubhyaṁ saṁsāra-cchittir astu te ||14||*

**Word for word:** *tava* — by Your; *eva* — own; *dvāra* — of the door; *pālena* — by the keeper; *vāritaḥ* — stopped; *tena* — by that; *duḥkhitaḥ* — grieved; *śāpam* — a curse; *dadāmi* — I give; *tat* — therefore; *tubhyam* — to You; *saṁsāra* — of samsara; *chittiḥ* — a cutting off; *astu* — let there be; *te* — for You.

**Translation.** and, stopped by Your very own doorkeeper, I was grieved by it. Therefore I give You a curse: let Your samsara be cut off!"

**Note.** A double meaning: "may Your worldly (household) life be ruined" — and "may Your round of rebirth cease" (liberation). Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.34.

### 15

**15.**

```
तच्छ्रुत्वा भगवान्नाथो ननन्द मनसा मुहुः ।
रुष्टस्य शापो विप्रस्य वरोऽभूदिति हर्षितः ॥१५॥
```
*tac chrutvā bhagavān nātho nananda manasā muhuḥ |
ruṣṭasya śāpo viprasya varo 'bhūd iti harṣitaḥ ||15||*

**Word for word:** *tat* — that; *śrutvā* — having heard; *bhagavān* — Bhagavan; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *nananda* — rejoiced; *manasā* — in His heart; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *ruṣṭasya* — of the angry; *śāpaḥ* — the curse; *viprasya* — of the brahmana; *varaḥ* — a blessing; *abhūt* — has become; *iti* — [thinking] thus; *harṣitaḥ* — delighted.

**Translation.** Hearing that, the Lord, Bhagavan, rejoiced in His heart again and again, delighted [at the thought]: "The curse of the angry brahmana has become a blessing!"

**Note.** A foreshadowing of *sannyāsa*: the "cutting off of samsara" is the leaving of home.

### 16

**16.**

```
इति श्रुत्वा हरौ शापं ब्रह्मशापाद्विमुच्यते ।
तदिदं श्रद्धया लोकैः श्रोतव्यं शुद्धबुद्धिभिः ॥१६॥
```
*iti śrutvā harau śāpaṁ brahma-śāpād vimucyate |
tad idaṁ śraddhayā lokaiḥ śrotavyaṁ śuddha-buddhibhiḥ ||16||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *śrutvā* — having heard; *harau* — on Hari; *śāpam* — of the curse; *brahma* — of a brahmana; *śāpāt* — from the curse; *vimucyate* — is freed; *tat idam* — this; *śraddhayā* — with faith; *lokaiḥ* — by people; *śrotavyam* — should be heard; *śuddha* — pure; *buddhibhiḥ* — whose intelligence.

**Translation.** Whoever hears thus of the curse [laid] on Hari is freed from a brahmana's curse. Therefore this should be heard with faith by people of pure intelligence.

**Note.** A *phala-śruti*, "the fruit of hearing".

### 17

**17.**

```
अन्येद्युः पद्मिनीं मुद्रां करेणार्कोऽपसारयन् ।
उदयाद्रेः समुत्तस्थौ विलासी शयनादिव ॥१७॥
```
*anyedyuḥ padminīṁ mudrāṁ kareṇārko 'pasārayan |
udayādreḥ samuttasthau vilāsī śayanād iva ||17||*

**Word for word:** *anyedyuḥ* — on the next day; *padminīm* — of the lotuses; *mudrām* — the seal; *kareṇa* — with its ray (hand); *arkaḥ* — the sun; *apasārayan* — removing; *udaya* — of sunrise; *adreḥ* — from the mountain; *samuttasthau* — rose; *vilāsī* — a lover; *śayanāt* — from his bed; *iva* — like.

**Translation.** On the next day the sun, removing with its ray-hand the seal from the lotuses, rose from the mountain of sunrise like a lover from his bed.

**Note.** At night the lotuses are closed, as if sealed; *kara* means both "ray" and "hand". *Padminī* is both "a lotus pond" and a type of beautiful woman.

**18.**

### 19

**19.**

```
क्षणाद्वैह्वल्यसम्भिन्नः स्खलत्सर्व्वतनुः प्रभुः ।
मधूनि देहि देहीति बभाषे मधुराननः ॥१९॥
```
*kṣaṇād vaihvalya-sambhinnaḥ skhalat-sarva-tanuḥ prabhuḥ |
madhūni dehi dehīti babhāṣe madhurānanaḥ ||19||*

**Word for word:** *kṣaṇāt* — suddenly; *vaihvalya* — by agitation; *sambhinnaḥ* — overcome; *skhalat* — staggering; *sarva* — whole; *tanuḥ* — whose body; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *madhūni* — honey (an intoxicating drink); *dehi dehi* — give, give; *iti* — thus; *babhāṣe* — said; *madhura* — sweet; *ānanaḥ* — whose face.

**Translation.** Suddenly the Lord, overcome by agitation, His whole body staggering, His face sweet, began to say: "Give [Me] honey, give!"

**Note.** *Madhu* is honey and also an intoxicating drink (*vāruṇī*), beloved of Balarama. The episode: Murari 2.14; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.115–120 ("Give Me honey!" — Nityananda brings Ganga water; Chandrashekhara, *ācārya-śekhara*, sees Balarama in the Lord; Vanamali sees the golden plough). In the Chaitanya-bhagavata there are similar scenes — Madhya 3.145–162 and 5.37–50 ("Bring wine!"; the Lord asks Nityananda for the plough and the club) — but not in the house of Murari.

**20.**


### 21

**21.**


**22.**


**23.**

### 24

**24.**

```
मधूनि देहि देहीति तत्रापि मधुराक्षरम् ।

```
*madhūni dehi dehīti tatrāpi madhurākṣaram |
uktvāmbu-pātraṁ hastena dhṛtvāmbūni bhṛśaṁ papau ||24||*

**Word for word:** *madhūni* — honey; *dehi dehi* — give, give; *iti* — thus; *tatra api* — there too; *madhura* — sweet; *akṣaram* — in words; *uktvā* — having said; *ambu* — of water; *pātram* — a vessel; *hastena* — with His hand; *dhṛtvā* — having taken; *ambūni* — the water; *bhṛśam* — eagerly; *papau* — drank.

### 25

**25.**

```
मदघूर्णितलोलाक्षः क्षणदानाथसुन्दरः ।
शुक्लैर्महोभिर्गेहस्य शैत्यं कुर्व्वन्ननर्त्त सः ॥२५॥
```
*mada-ghūrṇita-lolākṣaḥ kṣaṇadā-nātha-sundaraḥ |
śuklair mahobhir gehasya śaityaṁ kurvan nanarta saḥ ||25||*

**Word for word:** *mada* — from intoxication; *ghūrṇita* — rolling; *lola* — restless; *akṣaḥ* — whose eyes; *kṣaṇadā* — of the night; *nātha* — [like] the lord (the moon); *sundaraḥ* — beautiful; *śuklaiḥ* — with white; *mahobhiḥ* — rays; *gehasya* — of the house; *śaityam* — coolness; *kurvan* — making; *nanarta* — danced; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** His restless eyes rolling from intoxication, beautiful as the lord of the night, He danced, bringing coolness to the house with white rays [like the moon].

### 26

**26.**

```
नाहं स कृष्णो वचसा योऽसौ शीघ्रं सुखी भवेत् ।
तदानयानय भृशं मधून्यद्य समर्पय ॥२६॥
```
*nāhaṁ sa kṛṣṇo vacasā yo 'sau śīghraṁ sukhī bhavet |
tad ānayānaya bhṛśaṁ madhūny adya samarpaya ||26||*

**Word for word:** *na* — not; *aham* — I; *saḥ* — that; *kṛṣṇaḥ* — Krishna; *vacasā* — by words; *yaḥ asau* — who; *śīghram* — quickly; *sukhī* — satisfied; *bhavet* — becomes; *tat* — therefore; *ānaya ānaya* — bring, bring; *bhṛśam* — plentifully; *madhūni* — honey; *adya* — now; *samarpaya* — offer.

**Translation.** "I am not that Krishna who is quickly satisfied by words. So bring, bring — offer [Me] plenty of honey now!"

**Note.** The sense is unclear ⟨?⟩. Probably Gaura, as Balarama, says: "I am not Krishna, for whom it is enough to be glorified — I want the intoxicating drink." The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 27

**27.**

```
इत्युक्त्वैकेन हस्तेन द्विजैकं प्राक्षिपत् प्रभुः ।
आरादेव पपातासौ मल्लोऽपि बलवत्तरः ॥२७॥
```

### 28

**28.**

```
प्रातरेव बलावेशविवशो रजनीमुखे ।
प्रबुद्धः स्यात्तदा स्नानं करोति कमलेक्षणः ॥२८॥
```
*prātar eva balāveśa-vivaśo rajanī-mukhe |
prabuddhaḥ syāt tadā snānaṁ karoti kamalekṣaṇaḥ ||28||*

**Word for word:** *prātaḥ* — from the morning; *eva* — indeed; *bala* — of Balarama; *āveśa* — by the absorption; *vivaśaḥ* — overpowered; *rajanī* — of the night; *mukhe* — at the beginning; *prabuddhaḥ* — come to Himself; *syāt* — would be; *tadā* — then; *snānam* — a bath; *karoti* — takes; *kamala* — lotus; *īkṣaṇaḥ* — eyed.

**Translation.** Overpowered from the very morning by His absorption in Balarama, the lotus-eyed [Lord] would come to Himself only at nightfall — and then He would take His bath.

**Note.** *Āveśa*, literally "entering", is the state in which another form of the Lord manifests in Gaura.

**29.**

```
अपरेद्युर्दीप्यमानस्तेजोभिरतिदुःसहैः ।
```

### 30

**30.**

```
बलदेवावेशरम्यं मत्तद्विरदगामिनम् ।
मत्तसिंहसमोल्लासं मदघूर्णितलोचनम् ॥३०॥
```
*baladevāveśa-ramyaṁ matta-dvirada-gāminam |
matta-siṁha-samollāsaṁ mada-ghūrṇita-locanam ||30||*

**Word for word:** *baladeva* — in Baladeva; *āveśa* — by the absorption; *ramyam* — beautiful; *matta* — of a maddened; *dvirada* — elephant; *gāminam* — having the gait; *matta* — a maddened; *siṁha* — lion; *sama* — like; *ullāsam* — whose exultation; *mada* — from intoxication; *ghūrṇita* — rolling; *locanam* — whose eyes.

**31.**

```
रज्यद्गण्डस्थलं चण्डरश्मिकोटिसमप्रभम् ।
वैह्वल्यनुन्नहृदयं दृष्ट्वेत्थं ते तदावदन् ॥३१॥
```
*rajyad-gaṇḍa-sthalaṁ caṇḍa-raśmi-koṭi-sama-prabham |
vaihvalya-nunna-hṛdayaṁ dṛṣṭvetthaṁ te tadāvadan ||31||*

### 32

**32.**

```
किमिदं नाथ को वायं वेशः किम्वा परं महः ।
किमत्र कारणं ब्रूहि भगवान् सर्वभावनः ॥३२॥
```
*kim idaṁ nātha ko vāyaṁ veśaḥ kiṁ vā paraṁ mahaḥ |
kim atra kāraṇaṁ brūhi bhagavān sarva-bhāvanaḥ ||32||*

**Word for word:** *kim* — what; *idam* — this; *nātha* — O Lord; *kaḥ* — what; *vā* — or; *ayam* — this; *veśaḥ* — guise; *kim vā* — or what; *param* — supreme; *mahaḥ* — splendour; *kim* — what; *atra* — here; *kāraṇam* — the cause; *brūhi* — tell; *bhagavān* — [You are] Bhagavan; *sarva* — of all; *bhāvanaḥ* — the creator.

**Translation.** "What is this, O Lord? What is this guise, what this supreme splendour? Tell [us] what the cause of it is — for You are Bhagavan, the creator of all!"

**Note.** The edition writes *कोवायं* as one word. *Bhagavān* is nominative where the vocative *bhagavan* would be expected; the metre is the same either way. We take it as "[You are] Bhagavan"; it may also be understood as an address. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 33

**33.**

```
एवं बलावेशलीलाललितो ललितास्पदम् ।
उवाच स्खलितं शश्वद्वचनं मदघूर्णितः ॥३३॥
```
*evaṁ balāveśa-līlā-lalito lalitāspadam |
uvāca skhalitaṁ śaśvad vacanaṁ mada-ghūrṇitaḥ ||33||*

**Word for word:** *evam* — thus; *bala* — in Balarama; *āveśa* — of absorption; *līlā* — by the play; *lalitaḥ* — charming; *lalita* — of charm; *āspadam* — the abode; *uvāca* — spoke; *skhalitam* — faltering; *śaśvat* — again and again; *vacanam* — speech; *mada* — by intoxication; *ghūrṇitaḥ* — reeling.

**Translation.** Thus He, charming in the play of His absorption in Balarama, reeling with intoxication, spoke words that were an abode of charm, faltering again and again:

**Note.** *Lalitāspadam* (neuter) may be taken with *vacanam*, "words that were an abode of charm", or, as a noun in apposition, with the Lord: "He, the abode of charm".

### 34

**34.**

```
दृष्टो मया सीरपाणिर्नीलाम्बरधरः पुमान् ।
```

### 35

**35.**

```
श्रीचन्द्रशेखराचार्यरत्नं तत्र जगाद तम् ।
यस्त्वया नाथ दृष्टोऽसौ कुत्रास्ते बलिनां वरः ॥३५॥
```
*śrī-candraśekharācārya-ratnaṁ tatra jagāda tam |
yas tvayā nātha dṛṣṭo 'sau kutrāste balināṁ varaḥ ||35||*

**Word for word:** *śrī-candraśekhara* — Sri Chandrashekhara; *ācārya-ratnam* — Acharyaratna (the jewel among acharyas); *tatra* — then; *jagāda* — said; *tam* — to Him; *yaḥ* — who; *tvayā* — by You; *nātha* — O Lord; *dṛṣṭaḥ* — has been seen; *asau* — that one; *kutra* — where; *āste* — is; *balinām* — of the strong; *varaḥ* — the best.

**Translation.** Then Sri Chandrashekhara Acharyaratna said to Him: "O Lord, where is that best of the strong whom You have seen?"

**Note.** Chandrashekhara Acharya (Acharyaratna) is Gaura's maternal uncle. The cases in the first line look confused: *ratnam* and *tam* are both accusative in form, and *ratnaḥ … tam* would be expected. Since *ratna* is a neuter noun, however, *-ratnam* may also be the nominative ("Chandrashekhara, the jewel among acharyas"), with *tam*, "to Him", as the object of *jagāda*. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**36.**

**Translation.** Even as he spoke thus, he saw that very [one] — beautiful Gaura, bearing the appearance of the Lord, the Plough-bearer, in absorption upon absorption.

**Note.** *Āveśāveśa* is probably a dittography (a misprint). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 39

**39.**

```
एवं दिनं स नृत्येन निनाय परमप्रभुः ।
कीर्त्तनामृतवापीषु स्नातैस्तैः स्वजनैः सह ॥३९॥
```
*evaṁ dinaṁ sa nṛtyena nināya parama-prabhuḥ |
kīrtanāmṛta-vāpīṣu snātais taiḥ sva-janaiḥ saha ||39||*

**Word for word:** *evam* — thus; *dinam* — the day; *saḥ* — He; *nṛtyena* — in dancing; *nināya* — spent; *parama* — the Supreme; *prabhuḥ* — Lord; *kīrtana* — of kirtana; *amṛta* — of the nectar; *vāpīṣu* — in the ponds; *snātaiḥ* — who had bathed; *taiḥ* — with those; *sva* — His own; *janaiḥ* — people; *saha* — together.

**Translation.** Thus the Supreme Lord spent the day in dancing together with His own people, who had bathed in the ponds of the nectar of kirtana.

### 40

**40.**

```
ततोऽपराह्णे भूयोऽस्मिन् नृत्यति श्रीयुते मरुत् ।
मदगन्धैर्दिशः सर्व्वाः समन्तात् समपूजयत् ॥४०॥
```
*tato 'parāhṇe bhūyo 'smin nṛtyati śrī-yute marut |
mada-gandhair diśaḥ sarvāḥ samantāt samapūjayat ||40||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *aparāhṇe* — in the afternoon; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *asmin* — when He; *nṛtyati* — was dancing; *śrī-yute* — splendid; *marut* — the wind; *mada* — intoxicating (of musk); *gandhaiḥ* — with fragrances; *diśaḥ* — the quarters; *sarvāḥ* — all; *samantāt* — all around; *samapūjayat* — honoured.

**Translation.** Then in the afternoon, when the splendid [Lord] was dancing again, the wind honoured all the quarters all around with intoxicating fragrances.

**Note.** The edition has *श्रीयते* and *समपूजयत्*; we read *śrī-yute* (as the edition itself prints in verse 50); *samapūjayat* ("honoured") is confirmed by the Gosvami edition (GGM) as well, so it is kept. *Mada-gandha* is the fragrance of musk, or of *vāruṇī*, the intoxicating drink beloved of Balarama (cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.112–118: the scent of *vāruṇī* in the house of Srivasa).

**41.**

**42.**

```
श्रीरामनामा विप्राग्र्यो ददर्शाकाशमण्डलात् ।
```

*śrīrāma-nāmā viprāgryo dadarśākāśa-maṇḍalāt |

**Translation.** The foremost of brahmanas, Sri Rama by name, saw great beings who had come down from the vault of the sky — of great beauty, of great radiance,

**Note.** Sri Rama [Pandita] is Srivasa's brother (6.6). Verses 42–44 form one sentence.

### 43

**43.**

```
दिव्यगन्धानुलिप्ताङ्गान् दिव्याभरणभूषितान् ।

```
*divya-gandhānuliptāṅgān divyābharaṇa-bhūṣitān |
divya-srag-vasanān divyān divya-rūpa-guṇāśrayān ||43||*

**Translation.** their bodies anointed with heavenly fragrances, adorned with heavenly ornaments, wearing heavenly garlands and garments — heavenly [beings], abodes of heavenly beauty and qualities,

### 44

**44.**

```
एककर्णधृताम्भोजकर्णपूरमनोहरान् ।

```
*eka-karṇa-dhṛtāmbhoja-karṇa-pūra-manoharān |
uṣṇīṣa-paṭṭa-saṁśliṣṭa-mastakān hṛṣṭa-mānasān ||44||*

**Word for word:** *eka* — in one; *karṇa* — ear; *dhṛta* — worn; *ambhoja* — of lotus; *karṇa-pūra* — by an earring; *manoharān* — captivating; *uṣṇīṣa* — with turbans; *paṭṭa* — silken; *saṁśliṣṭa* — wrapped; *mastakān* — whose heads; *hṛṣṭa* — joyful; *mānasān* — whose hearts.

**Translation.** captivating with a lotus earring worn in one ear, their heads wrapped in silken turbans, their hearts joyful.

**Note.** An earring in one ear is a mark of Balarama (*eka-kuṇḍala*); these are evidently His associates.

### 45

**45.**

```
अन्ये तस्य मुखाच्छ्रुत्वा ननृतुर्जगुरञ्जसा ।

```
*anye tasya mukhāc chrutvā nanṛtur jagur añjasā |
kīrtanena harer nāmnām āmnāya-sudhiyo bhṛśam ||45||*

**Word for word:** *anye* — others; *tasya* — his; *mukhāt* — from the mouth; *śrutvā* — having heard; *nanṛtuḥ* — danced; *jaguḥ* — sang; *añjasā* — at once; *kīrtanena* — with the kirtana; *hareḥ* — of Hari; *nāmnām* — of the names; *āmnāya* — in the Vedas; *sudhiyaḥ* — learned; *bhṛśam* — ardently.

**Translation.** Others, learned in the Vedas, hearing this from his mouth, at once danced and sang ardently, with the kirtana of the names of Hari.

### 46

**46.**

```
तत्रैव कश्चिद्विप्राग्र्यो वनमाली महाशयः ।
अपश्यत् पर्वताकारं हलं काञ्चननिर्मितम् ॥४६॥
```
*tatraiva kaścid viprāgryo vanamālī mahāśayaḥ |
apaśyat parvatākāraṁ halaṁ kāñcana-nirmitam ||46||*

**Word for word:** *tatra eva* — right there; *kaścit* — a certain; *vipra* — of brahmanas; *agryaḥ* — the foremost; *vanamālī* — Vanamali; *mahā* — great; *āśayaḥ* — [whose] soul; *apaśyat* — saw; *parvata* — of a mountain; *ākāram* — of the size; *halam* — a plough; *kāñcana* — of gold; *nirmitam* — made.

**Translation.** Right there a certain high-souled foremost of brahmanas, Vanamali, saw a plough made of gold, the size of a mountain.

**Note.** Vanamali Acharya; cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.119: "Vanamali Acharya saw a golden plough in the hand of Balarama." Probably the same Vanamali who arranged the marriage with Lakshmipriya (Murari 1.9); the father of Gadadhara is Madhava Mishra, not Vanamali.

### 47

**47.**

```
दृष्ट्वा सविस्मयो भूत्वा लोचनाश्रुझराकुलः ।
पुलकौघपरीताङ्गो न सस्मार तदा तनुम् ॥४७॥
```
*dṛṣṭvā sa-vismayo bhūtvā locanāśru-jharākulaḥ |
pulakaugha-parītāṅgo na sasmāra tadā tanum ||47||*

**Word for word:** *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *sa* — with; *vismayaḥ* — wonder; *bhūtvā* — having become; *locana* — from the eyes; *aśru* — of tears; *jhara* — by streams; *ākulaḥ* — overwhelmed; *pulaka* — of hairs standing on end; *ogha* — with a flood; *parīta* — covered; *aṅgaḥ* — whose body; *na* — not; *sasmāra* — remembered; *tadā* — then; *tanum* — the body.

**Translation.** Seeing this, he was filled with wonder; overwhelmed by streams of tears from his eyes, his body covered with a flood of hairs standing on end, he then forgot his body.

**48.**

```
ततो ननर्त्त तैः सार्द्धं निजकीर्त्तनमङ्गलैः ।

```
*tato nanarta taiḥ sārdhaṁ nija-kīrtana-maṅgalaiḥ |
halāyudhāveśa-ramyo ramya-gaurāṅga-sundaraḥ ||48||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *nanarta* — danced; *taiḥ* — with them; *sārdham* — together; *nija* — of His own; *kīrtana* — of the kirtana; *maṅgalaiḥ* — auspicious; *halāyudha* — of Halayudha (Balarama); *āveśa* — by the absorption; *ramyaḥ* — charming; *ramya* — charming; *gaurāṅga* — Gauranga; *sundaraḥ* — beautiful.

**Note.** *Halāyudha*, "He whose weapon is the plough", is Balarama.

### 49

**49.**

```
दिवि देवगणाः सर्वे समहेन्द्राः सपद्मजाः ।
प्रणेमुः कुसुमस्तोमं वर्षन्तो नतकन्धराः ॥४९॥
```
*divi deva-gaṇāḥ sarve sa-mahendrāḥ sa-padmajāḥ |
praṇemuḥ kusuma-stomaṁ varṣanto nata-kandharāḥ ||49||*

**Word for word:** *divi* — in the sky; *deva* — of gods; *gaṇāḥ* — the hosts; *sarve* — all; *sa* — with; *mahā-indrāḥ* — Mahendra; *sa* — with; *padma-jāḥ* — the Lotus-born; *praṇemuḥ* — bowed down; *kusuma* — of flowers; *stomam* — a multitude; *varṣantaḥ* — showering; *nata* — bent; *kandharāḥ* — whose necks.

**Translation.** In the sky all the hosts of the gods, with Mahendra and the Lotus-born, bowed down with bent necks, showering a multitude of flowers.

**Note.** Mahendra is Indra; the Lotus-born is Brahma.

### 50

**50.**

```
एवं निशावशेषोऽभून्नृत्यति श्रीयुते प्रभौ ।
चन्द्रश्चरमशैलान्तं चुचुम्ब शनकैरिव ॥५०॥
```
*evaṁ niśāvaśeṣo 'bhūn nṛtyati śrī-yute prabhau |
candraś carama-śailāntaṁ cucumba śanakair iva ||50||*

**Word for word:** *evam* — thus; *niśā* — of the night; *avaśeṣaḥ* — the end; *abhūt* — came; *nṛtyati* — while dancing; *śrī-yute* — splendid; *prabhau* — the Lord; *candraḥ* — the moon; *carama* — of the last (western); *śaila* — mountain; *antam* — the edge; *cucumba* — kissed; *śanakaiḥ* — softly; *iva* — as if.

**Translation.** Thus, while the splendid Lord was dancing, the night drew to its end, and the moon, as it were, softly kissed the edge of the western mountain.

**Note.** *Carama-śaila* is the mountain of sunset.

### 51

**51.**

```
नृत्यतस्तस्य नटनदर्शनार्थमियं किमु ।
पुरन्दराशा तरुणी बभूवात्यनुरागिणी ॥५१॥
```
*nṛtyatas tasya naṭana-darśanārtham iyaṁ kim u |
purandarāśā taruṇī babhūvāty-anurāgiṇī ||51||*

**Word for word:** *nṛtyataḥ* — of the dancing; *tasya* — of Him; *naṭana* — the dance; *darśana* — to see; *artham* — in order; *iyam* — this; *kim u* — was it not?; *purandara* — of Purandara; *āśā* — the quarter (the east); *taruṇī* — a young [maiden]; *babhūva* — became; *ati* — exceedingly; *anurāgiṇī* — enamoured (crimson).

**Translation.** Was it not in order to see the dance of the dancing [Lord] that this young [maiden], the quarter of Purandara, became so enamoured and crimson?

**Note.** The quarter of Purandara (Indra) is the east. *Anurāga* means both "love" and "redness" (the dawn).

### 52

**52.**

```
मन्दगन्धवहः शश्वज्ज्योत्स्नयाभ्युपगूहितः ।
कुमुदानि समाधुन्वन् गौराङ्घ्रिमभजत्ततः ॥५२॥
```
*manda-gandhavahaḥ śaśvaj jyotsnayābhyupagūhitaḥ |
kumudāni samādhunvan gaurāṅghrim abhajat tataḥ ||52||*

**Word for word:** *manda* — gentle; *gandhavahaḥ* — the wind (bearer of fragrance); *śaśvat* — constantly; *jyotsnayā* — by the moonlight; *abhyupagūhitaḥ* — embraced; *kumudāni* — the night lotuses; *samādhunvan* — stirring; *gaura* — of Gaura; *aṅghrim* — the feet; *abhajat* — served; *tataḥ* — then.

**Translation.** Then the gentle wind, constantly embraced by the moonlight, stirring the night lotuses, served the feet of Gaura.

**Note.** The edition has *सनाधुन्वन्*; we read *samādhunvan*.

### 53

**53.**

```
ततस्तैः स्वजनैः सार्द्धं स्वर्णद्यां जगतां प्रभुः ।
उपेयिवान् बभौ नाथो यथा मेरुः सहाद्रिभिः ॥५३॥
```
*tatas taiḥ sva-janaiḥ sārdhaṁ svar-ṇadyāṁ jagatāṁ prabhuḥ |
upeyivān babhau nātho yathā meruḥ sahādribhiḥ ||53||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *taiḥ* — with those; *sva* — His own; *janaiḥ* — people; *sārdham* — together; *svaḥ* — heavenly; *nadyām* — to the river; *jagatām* — of the worlds; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *upeyivān* — having come; *babhau* — shone; *nāthaḥ* — the Master; *yathā* — as; *meruḥ* — Meru; *saha* — with; *adribhiḥ* — the mountains.

**Translation.** Then the Lord of the worlds, the Master, having come with His own people to the heavenly river, shone like Meru among the mountains.

**Note.** The edition has *उपोथिवान्*; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *upeyivān* ("having come", the perfect participle of *upa-√i*), and so we read. The heavenly river is the Ganga.

### 54

**54.**

```
अवगाह्य ततो गङ्गां गाङ्गेयाचलसुन्दरः ।
करवारिभिरन्योन्यं चकार जलखेलनम् ॥५४॥
```
*avagāhya tato gaṅgāṁ gāṅgeyācala-sundaraḥ |
kara-vāribhir anyonyaṁ cakāra jala-khelanam ||54||*

**Word for word:** *avagāhya* — having plunged into; *tataḥ* — then; *gaṅgām* — the Ganga; *gāṅgeya* — golden; *acala* — than a mountain; *sundaraḥ* — more beautiful; *kara* — of the hands; *vāribhiḥ* — with the water; *anyonyam* — one another; *cakāra* — made; *jala* — water; *khelanam* — play.

**Translation.** Then, having plunged into the Ganga, He who is more beautiful than a golden mountain made water-play, [all] splashing one another with water from their hands.

**Note.** *Gāṅgeya*, "born of the Ganga", is gold (from the myth of Kartikeya). A play on *gaṅgā* and *gāṅgeya*.

### 55

**55.**

```
एवं नानाप्रकाराणि क्रीडितानि समापयन् ।
ययौ गेहं निजं गौरो यथेन्दुरुदयाचलम् ॥५५॥
```

*evaṁ nānā-prakārāṇi krīḍitāni samāpayan |

**Word for word:** *evam* — thus; *nānā* — of various; *prakārāṇi* — kinds; *krīḍitāni* — pastimes; *samāpayan* — concluding; *yayau* — went; *geham* — to the house; *nijam* — His own; *gauraḥ* — Gaura; *yathā* — as; *induḥ* — the moon; *udaya* — of rising; *acalam* — to the mountain.

**Translation.** Thus concluding pastimes of various kinds, Gaura went to His own house, as the moon [goes] to the mountain of rising.

**Note.** The text has *udayācala*, the mountain of rising (in the east), not the mountain of setting.

### 56

**56.**

```
हसन्नसौ सुमधुरं श्रीवासमवदत् प्रभुः ।
वेणुं प्रयच्छ मे शीघ्रं क्व सोऽस्ति न तु दृश्यते ॥५६॥
```
*hasann asau sumadhuraṁ śrīvāsam avadat prabhuḥ |
veṇuṁ prayaccha me śīghraṁ kva so 'sti na tu dṛśyate ||56||*

**Word for word:** *hasan* — laughing; *asau* — He; *sumadhuram* — very sweetly; *śrīvāsam* — to Srivasa; *avadat* — said; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *veṇum* — the flute; *prayaccha* — give; *me* — to Me; *śīghram* — quickly; *kva* — where; *saḥ* — it; *asti* — is; *na* — not; *tu* — but; *dṛśyate* — is seen.

**Translation.** Laughing very sweetly, the Lord said to Srivasa: "Give Me the flute, quickly! Where is it? It is nowhere to be seen."

**Note.** From the mood of Balarama — to the mood of Krishna.

### 57

**57.**

```
ततोऽयं विप्रप्रवरो हसन्निदमभाषत ।
वेणुस्तवास्ति गोपीभिः परितः परिरक्षितः ॥५७॥
```
*tato 'yaṁ vipra-pravaro hasann idam abhāṣata |

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *ayam* — this; *vipra* — of brahmanas; *pravaraḥ* — the best; *hasan* — laughing; *idam* — this; *abhāṣata* — said; *veṇuḥ* — the flute; *tava* — Your; *asti* — is; *gopībhiḥ* — by the gopis; *paritaḥ* — on all sides; *parirakṣitaḥ* — guarded (hidden).

**Translation.** Then this best of brahmanas, laughing, said: "Your flute is guarded on all sides by the gopis."

**Note.** An echo of the *vaṁśī-caurya* — the stealing of Krishna's flute by Radha and the gopis.

**58.**

```

```
*vṛndāvana-krīḍitāni smṛtvā smṛtvā kṛpā-nidhiḥ |

**Word for word:** *vṛndāvana* — in Vrindavana; *krīḍitāni* — the pastimes; *smṛtvā smṛtvā* — remembering again and again; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *nidhiḥ* — the treasury; *sāndra* — of deep; *ānanda* — bliss; *eka* — unbroken; *sandoha* — in the flood; *magnaḥ* — immersed; *tūṣṇīm* — silent; *abhūt* — became; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment.

### 59

**59.**

```
ततश्चातिशयाविष्टो हृष्टरोमा महाप्रभुः ।
ब्रूहि ब्रूहीति सततमुच्चैस्तं निजगाद सः ॥५९॥
```
*tataś cātiśayāviṣṭo hṛṣṭa-romā mahāprabhuḥ |
brūhi brūhīti satatam uccais taṁ nijagāda saḥ ||59||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *ca* — and; *atiśaya* — exceedingly; *āviṣṭaḥ* — absorbed; *hṛṣṭa* — standing on end; *romā* — whose hairs; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *brūhi brūhi* — speak, speak; *iti* — thus; *satatam* — incessantly; *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *tam* — to him; *nijagāda* — said; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** And then Mahaprabhu, exceedingly absorbed, His hairs standing on end, loudly and incessantly said to him: "Speak, speak!"

---

### 60

**60.**

```
वृन्दावनक्रीडनञ्च यमुनाक्रीडनं तथा ।
सर्वं ततोऽसौ श्रीवासो वर्णयामास भूरिशः ॥६०॥
```
*vṛndāvana-krīḍanañ ca yamunā-krīḍanaṁ tathā |
sarvaṁ tato 'sau śrīvāso varṇayām āsa bhūriśaḥ ||60||*

**Word for word:** *vṛndāvana* — in Vrindavana; *krīḍanam* — the play; *ca* — and; *yamunā* — on the Yamuna; *krīḍanam* — the play; *tathā* — also; *sarvam* — all; *tataḥ* — then; *asau* — this; *śrīvāsaḥ* — Srivasa; *varṇayām āsa* — described; *bhūriśaḥ* — at length.

**Translation.** Then this Srivasa described everything at length — the play in Vrindavana and also the play on the Yamuna.

---

### 61

**61.**

```
पुरा वृन्दारण्ये तरुणहरिणाक्षीभिरनिशं
त्वयि प्रेमाविष्टे विलसति य आसीत् स विभवः ।
त्वयैवातृप्तेनाजनि न यदि तन्नाथ रभसः
कथङ्कारं नित्यं नव नव इवायं समभवत् ॥६१॥
```
*purā vṛndāraṇye taruṇa-hariṇākṣībhir aniśaṁ
tvayi premāviṣṭe vilasati ya āsīt sa vibhavaḥ |
tvayaivātṛptenājani na yadi tan nātha rabhasaḥ
kathaṅkāraṁ nityaṁ nava nava ivāyaṁ samabhavat ||61||*

**Word for word:** *purā* — once; *vṛndā-araṇye* — in the forest of Vrinda; *taruṇa* — young; *hariṇa* — of does; *akṣībhiḥ* — with [maidens] with the eyes; *aniśam* — incessantly; *tvayi* — when You; *prema* — by love; *āviṣṭe* — absorbed; *vilasati* — sported; *yaḥ* — which; *āsīt* — there was; *saḥ* — that; *vibhavaḥ* — splendour; *tvayā* — by You; *eva* — Yourself; *atṛptena* — never satiated; *ajani* — was born; *na* — not; *yadi* — if; *tat* — that; *nātha* — O Lord; *rabhasaḥ* — rapture; *katham-kāram* — how; *nityam* — eternally; *nava nava* — new and new; *iva* — as if; *ayam* — this; *samabhavat* — would have become.

**Translation.** "Once, in the forest of Vrinda, when You, absorbed in love, sported incessantly with young doe-eyed [maidens], there was a splendour. If that had not been born of You Yourself, O Lord, who are never satiated, how could this rapture have become eternally new, as if ever new and new?"

**Note.** The metre is *śikhariṇī*. The thought: it is the insatiability of Krishna Himself that makes His pastimes eternally new. The syntax of *ajani* in the third line is not entirely clear ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 62

**62.**

```
आमञ्जुगुञ्जदलिपुञ्जनिकुञ्जरम्यं
वृन्दावनं निरुपमं स पुरा प्रविश्य ।
क्रीडां चकर्थ रसकौतुककामतन्त्र-
मन्त्रस्वरूप इव यत्त्वमतिप्रियं तत् ॥६२॥
```
*ā-mañju-guñjad-ali-puñja-nikuñja-ramyaṁ
vṛndāvanaṁ nirupamaṁ sa purā praviśya |
krīḍāṁ cakartha rasa-kautuka-kāma-tantra-
mantra-svarūpa iva yat tvam ati-priyaṁ tat ||62||*

**Word for word:** *ā* — exceedingly; *mañju* — sweetly; *guñjat* — humming; *ali* — of bees; *puñja* — with swarms; *nikuñja* — with bowers; *ramyam* — charming; *vṛndāvanam* — Vrindavana; *nirupamam* — incomparable; *saḥ* — that [same One]; *purā* — once; *praviśya* — having entered; *krīḍām* — play; *cakartha* — You performed; *rasa* — of rasa; *kautuka* — of delight; *kāma* — of love; *tantra* — of the science; *mantra* — of the mantra; *svarūpaḥ* — the embodiment; *iva* — as if; *yat* — which; *tvam* — You; *ati* — exceedingly; *priyam* — dear; *tat* — that.

**Translation.** "Once You entered incomparable Vrindavana, charming with its bowers where swarms of bees hum very sweetly, and played there as if You were the very embodiment of the mantra of the science of love, delight and rasa. That is what is dearest to You!"

**Note.** The metre is *vasanta-tilakā*.

---

### 63

**63.**

```
एवं निशम्य मदमत्तमृगेन्द्रनादं
भूयो वदेति मधुरं निजगाद नाथः ।
अत्रान्तरे द्विजवरः स च तत्कृपाभिः
सर्वं तदीयचरितं प्रकटं जगाद ॥६३॥
```
*evaṁ niśamya mada-matta-mṛgendra-nādaṁ
bhūyo vadeti madhuraṁ nijagāda nāthaḥ |
atrāntare dvija-varaḥ sa ca tat-kṛpābhiḥ
sarvaṁ tadīya-caritaṁ prakaṭaṁ jagāda ||63||*

**Word for word:** *evam* — this; *niśamya* — having heard; *mada* — with intoxication; *matta* — maddened; *mṛga-indra* — of a lion; *nādam* — [with] a roar; *bhūyaḥ* — more; *vada* — speak; *iti* — thus; *madhuram* — sweetly; *nijagāda* — said; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *atra antare* — thereupon; *dvija* — of the twice-born; *varaḥ* — the best; *saḥ* — he; *ca* — and; *tat* — His; *kṛpābhiḥ* — by the mercy; *sarvam* — all; *tadīya* — His; *caritam* — deeds; *prakaṭam* — clearly; *jagāda* — recounted.

**Translation.** Hearing this, the Lord, with a roar like that of a lion maddened by intoxication, sweetly said: "Speak more!" And thereupon that best of the twice-born, by His mercy, clearly recounted all His deeds.

**Note.** The metre is *vasanta-tilakā*.

```
इति श्रीकृष्णचैतन्यचरितामृते महाकाव्ये श्रीवृन्दावनविहारवर्णनं नाम अष्टमः सर्गः ॥
```
*iti śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-caritāmṛte mahā-kāvye śrī-vṛndāvana-vihāra-varṇanaṁ nāma aṣṭamaḥ sargaḥ ||*

Thus ends the eighth sarga, named "The Description of the Pastimes in Vrindavana", in the mahakavya "Sri Krishna-Chaitanya-charitamrita".
