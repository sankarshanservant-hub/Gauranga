# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga One — verses 1–29 (printed pp. 1–11)

**Text:** the edition of Haridasa Shastri (Vrindavana); the Sanskrit is taken from the scan and checked by hand.
**Metre:** verses 1–24 — *mandakranta* (17 syllables to the pada); verses 25–29 — *shikharini* (17 syllables).
**Date of the poem:** c. 1542. The author is Paramananda Sena (Kavi Karnapura), son of Shivananda Sena, a younger contemporary of Mahaprabhu.

**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–7 — mangalacharana, the auspicious invocation: Gauranga as Krishna who has become golden.
- 8–12 — the poet's self-abasement: why and how he undertakes to write.
- 13–29 — **the frame narrative:** Mahaprabhu has disappeared from the world, and the devotees lament their separation from Him. The poem opens not with His birth but with grief after His disappearance: the story is told from the point of loss.

**Word-for-word conventions:** compound words are divided into their members, each with its own meaning; case is rendered by an English preposition ("of", "with", "in", "by", "to", "from"). Square brackets enclose a word that is understood (for example, [whose], [like]).

**Symbols:** square brackets [ ] — words added for coherence; ⟨?⟩ — an obscure place; "Note" — the translator's note.

### 1

**1.**

```
यः श्रीवृन्दावनभुवि पुरा सच्चिदानन्दसान्द्रो
गौराङ्गीभिः सदृशरुचिभिः श्यामधामा ननर्त ।
तासां शश्वद्दृढतरपरीरम्भसम्भेदतः किं
गौराङ्गः सन् जयति स नवद्वीपमालम्बमानः ॥१॥
```
*yaḥ śrī-vṛndāvana-bhuvi purā sac-cid-ānanda-sāndro
gaurāṅgībhiḥ sadṛśa-rucibhiḥ śyāma-dhāmā nanarta |
tāsāṁ śaśvad-dṛḍhatara-parīrambha-sambhedataḥ kiṁ
gaurāṅgaḥ san jayati sa navadvīpam ālambamānaḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *yaḥ* — who; *śrī* — of beautiful; *vṛndāvana* — of Vrindavana; *bhuvi* — on the land; *purā* — once; *sat* — with being; *cit* — with consciousness; *ānanda* — with bliss; *sāndraḥ* — dense; *gaurāṅgībhiḥ* — with golden-limbed [maidens]; *sadṛśa* — alike; *rucibhiḥ* — with [those having] radiance; *śyāma* — dark; *dhāmā* — in radiance; *nanarta* — danced; *tāsām* — of them; *śaśvat* — of constant; *dṛḍhatara* — ever firmer; *parīrambha* — of embraces; *sambhedataḥ* — from the union; *kim* — is it not that; *gaurāṅgaḥ* — golden-limbed; *san* — being; *jayati* — is victorious; *saḥ* — He; *navadvīpam* — Navadvipa; *ālambamānaḥ* — taking as His support.

**Translation.** He who once, on the land of beautiful Vrindavana, dense with being, consciousness and bliss, dark in His radiance, danced with golden-limbed maidens alike in their radiance — was it not from the union of their constant, ever firmer embraces that He Himself became golden? Glory to Him, to Gauranga, who has taken Navadvipa for His support!

**Note.** *Kim* is not a question of perplexity but a poetic conjecture (*utpreksha*): the poet offers an explanation of Chaitanya's golden colour. This is the principal theological image of the whole poem: Gauranga is Krishna, who has taken on the form and colour of His beloved gopis.

### 2

**2.**

```
यस्याङ्गश्रीमधुरिमपरीणाहपीयूषसेकै-
र्भास्वच्चामीकरजलमयैः शान्तनिःशेषतापै-
र्यस्य श्रीमत्पदजलरुहान्माकरन्दप्रवाहैः
साक्षात्प्रक्षालितमिव जगच्छश्वदानम्यतां सः ॥२॥
```
*yasyāṅga-śrī-madhurima-parīṇāha-pīyūṣa-sekair
bhāsvac-cāmīkara-jala-mayaiḥ śānta-niḥśeṣa-tāpair
yasya śrīmat-pada-jalaruhān mākaranda-pravāhaiḥ
sākṣāt prakṣālitam iva jagac chaśvad ānamyatāṁ saḥ ||2||*

**Word for word:** *yasya* — of whose; *aṅga* — of the body; *śrī* — of the beauty; *madhurima* — of the sweetness; *parīṇāha* — of the abundance; *pīyūṣa* — of nectar; *sekaiḥ* — with the sprinklings; *bhāsvat* — of shining; *cāmīkara* — gold; *jala* — of liquid; *mayaiḥ* — consisting; *śānta* — which have quenched; *niḥśeṣa* — without remainder; *tāpaiḥ* — the burning; *yasya* — of whose; *śrīmat* — beautiful; *pada* — of the feet; *jalaruhāt* — from the lotus; *mākaranda* — of honey; *pravāhaiḥ* — with the streams; *sākṣāt* — before one's eyes; *prakṣālitam* — washed; *iva* — as if; *jagat* — the world; *śaśvat* — always; *ānamyatām* — let [Him] be bowed to; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** By the sprinklings of the nectar of His bodily beauty, so abundant in sweetness — sprinklings that are like liquid shining gold and quench all burning without remainder — and by the streams of honey from the lotus of His beautiful feet, the world seems washed before one's very eyes. To Him let there ever be obeisance.

### 3

**3.**

```
जानुप्रान्तप्रसृमरभुजादण्डमुद्दण्डचण्ड-
द्योतश्रेणीपटुतरमहोमण्डलीमण्डिताङ्गम् ।
आकर्णान्तःस्खलितललितापाङ्गमत्यन्तरज्यद्-
गण्डाभोगं मृगपतिशताक्रीडमानं भजामः ॥३॥
```
*jānu-prānta-prasṛmara-bhujā-daṇḍam uddaṇḍa-caṇḍa-
dyota-śreṇī-paṭutara-maho-maṇḍalī-maṇḍitāṅgam |
ākarṇāntaḥ-skhalita-lalitāpāṅgam atyanta-rajyad-
gaṇḍābhogaṁ mṛgapati-śatākrīḍamānaṁ bhajāmaḥ ||3||*

**Word for word:** *jānu* — of the knees; *prānta* — to the edge; *prasṛmara* — extending; *bhujā* — arms; *daṇḍam* — [having] staffs; *uddaṇḍa* — of unrestrained; *caṇḍa* — fierce; *dyota* — of radiance; *śreṇī* — of rows; *paṭutara* — with most brilliant; *mahaḥ* — of light; *maṇḍalī* — with a circle; *maṇḍita* — adorned; *aṅgam* — [having] a body; *ā* — up to; *karṇa* — the ears; *antaḥ* — as far as; *skhalita* — slipping; *lalita* — playful; *apāṅgam* — [having] a sidelong glance; *atyanta* — deeply; *rajyat* — reddening; *gaṇḍa* — of the cheeks; *ābhogam* — [having] the breadth; *mṛgapati* — of lions; *śata* — a hundred; *ākrīḍamānam* — sporting [like]; *bhajāmaḥ* — we worship.

**Translation.** We worship Him whose arms, like staffs, reach to the edge of His knees; whose body is adorned with a most brilliant circle of light, rows of fierce, unrestrained radiance; whose playful sidelong glance slips all the way to His ears; whose broad cheeks flush a deep red; and who sports like a hundred lions.

### 4

**4.**

```
यस्य श्रीमन्नखमणिसुधारश्मिरम्यप्रकाशै-
स्त्रैलोक्यान्तर्जटितजडिमक्षालनायोन्मिषद्भिः ।
स्वीयप्रेमाम्बुधिलहरिकापूरपूरेण भूयो
जाड्यं चक्रे तमिह तदहो सेवतां जीवलोकः ॥४॥
```
*yasya śrīman-nakha-maṇi-sudhā-raśmi-ramya-prakāśais
trailokyāntar-jaṭita-jaḍima-kṣālanāyonmiṣadbhiḥ |
svīya-premāmbudhi-laharikā-pūra-pūreṇa bhūyo
jāḍyaṁ cakre tam iha tad aho sevatāṁ jīva-lokaḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *yasya* — of whose; *śrīmat* — beautiful; *nakha* — of the nails; *maṇi* — of the gems; *sudhā* — nectarean; *raśmi* — of rays; *ramya* — with the charming; *prakāśaiḥ* — light; *trailokya* — of the three worlds; *antaḥ* — within; *jaṭita* — ingrained; *jaḍima* — the dullness; *kṣālanāya* — for washing away; *unmiṣadbhiḥ* — flashing forth; *svīya* — of His own; *prema* — of love; *ambudhi* — of the ocean; *laharikā* — of the waves; *pūra* — of the flood; *pūreṇa* — by the inundation; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *jāḍyam* — stupor; *cakre* — produced; *tam* — Him; *iha* — here; *tat* — therefore; *aho* — oh, wonder; *sevatām* — let serve; *jīva* — of living beings; *lokaḥ* — the world.

**Translation.** The charming light of the gems of His beautiful nails, soft as nectar-rays, flashed forth to wash away the dullness ingrained within the three worlds; and then, with the inundation of the flooding waves of His own ocean of love, He again produced a stupor. Oh, wonder! Therefore let the world of living beings serve Him here.

**Note.** A play on words: *jaḍimā* is the dull inertness of the material world; *jāḍya* is the stupor of ecstasy, one of the eight *sattvika-bhavas* (immobility from an excess of feeling). The one is washed away by the light; the other comes with love.

### 5

**5.**

```
स्वीयैर्लीलाविलसितरसैः पादसेवाविलासै-
र्लास्योल्लासैर्यदयमकरोत्पूर्णपूर्णां त्रिलोकीम् ।
मन्ये भूयस्तदिह करुणा सैव नित्यं नवीना
भूयो भूयः प्रणमतुतरां तामिमां जीवलोकः ॥५॥
```
*svīyair līlā-vilasita-rasaiḥ pāda-sevā-vilāsair
lāsyollāsair yad ayam akarot pūrṇa-pūrṇāṁ trilokīm |
manye bhūyas tad iha karuṇā saiva nityaṁ navīnā
bhūyo bhūyaḥ praṇamatutarāṁ tām imāṁ jīva-lokaḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *svīyaiḥ* — with His own; *līlā* — of pastimes; *vilasita* — of the sports; *rasaiḥ* — with the tastes; *pāda* — to the feet; *sevā* — of service; *vilāsaiḥ* — with the joys; *lāsya* — of dance; *ullāsaiḥ* — with the raptures; *yat* — that; *ayam* — He; *akarot* — made; *pūrṇa-pūrṇām* — filled to overflowing; *trilokīm* — the three worlds; *manye* — I think; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *tat* — that; *iha* — here; *karuṇā* — mercy; *sā* — that; *eva* — very; *nityam* — eternally; *navīnā* — new; *bhūyaḥ bhūyaḥ* — again and again; *praṇamatu* — let bow; *tarām* — ever more; *tām* — to that; *imām* — to this; *jīva* — of living beings; *lokaḥ* — the world.

**Translation.** That He filled the three worlds to overflowing with the tastes of His own playful pastimes, with the joys of service to the feet, with the raptures of dance — this, I think, is that very mercy, eternally new, [shown] here once again. Let the world of living beings bow to it again and again, ever more deeply.

**Note.** *Pāda-sevā-vilāsa* can be understood both as "the play of service to the feet" (the devotees' service to Him) and as "the play of the feet" in dance.

### 6

**6.**

```
यत्र श्रीमन्मधुरिममयी कान्तिरेषा जगाम
व्याहारान्तं गुरुकरुणता पूर्णतामागतासीत् ।
वैदग्धीयं निखिलसुभगा हन्त निर्वाहमाप्ता
गौरांगस्य प्रणम तदिदं पादपाथोजयुग्मम् ॥६॥
```
*yatra śrīman-madhurima-mayī kāntir eṣā jagāma
vyāhārāntaṁ guru-karuṇatā pūrṇatām āgatāsīt |
vaidagdhīyaṁ nikhila-subhagā hanta nirvāham āptā
gaurāṅgasya praṇama tad idaṁ pāda-pāthoja-yugmam ||6||*

**Word for word:** *yatra* — in which; *śrīmat* — of beautiful; *madhurima* — sweetness; *mayī* — full; *kāntiḥ* — loveliness; *eṣā* — this; *jagāma* — reached; *vyāhāra* — of speech; *antam* — the limit; *guru* — weighty; *karuṇatā* — compassion; *pūrṇatām* — fullness; *āgatā* — having reached; *āsīt* — was; *vaidagdhī* — artfulness; *iyam* — this; *nikhila* — to all; *subhagā* — charming; *hanta* — ah; *nirvāham* — consummation; *āptā* — attained; *gaurāṅgasya* — of Gauranga; *praṇama* — bow; *tat* — to that; *idam* — to this; *pāda* — of the feet; *pāthoja* — of lotuses; *yugmam* — to the pair.

**Translation.** In them this loveliness, full of beautiful sweetness, went beyond the limit of speech; in them profound compassion reached its fullness; in them this artfulness, charming to all — ah! — attained its consummation. Bow down to that pair of lotus feet of Gauranga.

**Note.** *Vaidagdhī* is not merely "cleverness" but refinement in art, play and love. It is one of Krishna's qualities in Gaudiya aesthetics.

### 7

**7.**

```
चित्रं तावद्गुणजलनिधेस्तस्य लावण्यधाम्नो
वैदग्ध्यादेर्लवमपि सुधीर्भाषितुं कः समर्थः ।
स्वीयां शक्तिं द्विगुणगुणितां चेद्विधायैष वक्तुं
शक्तः शक्तः स्वयमपि नहि श्रीलगौरांगचन्द्रः ॥७॥
```
*citraṁ tāvad guṇa-jala-nidhes tasya lāvaṇya-dhāmno
vaidagdhyāder lavam api sudhīr bhāṣituṁ kaḥ samarthaḥ |
svīyāṁ śaktiṁ dviguṇa-guṇitāṁ ced vidhāyaiṣa vaktuṁ
śaktaḥ śaktaḥ svayam api na hi śrīla-gaurāṅga-candraḥ ||7||*

**Word for word:** *citram* — wonderful; *tāvat* — indeed; *guṇa* — of virtues; *jala* — of waters; *nidheḥ* — of the reservoir; *tasya* — of Him; *lāvaṇya* — of loveliness; *dhāmnaḥ* — of the abode; *vaidagdhya* — of artfulness; *ādeḥ* — and the rest; *lavam* — a particle; *api* — even; *sudhīḥ* — wise man; *bhāṣitum* — to express; *kaḥ* — who; *samarthaḥ* — is able; *svīyām* — His own; *śaktim* — power; *dvi* — two; *guṇa* — -fold; *guṇitām* — multiplied; *cet* — if; *vidhāya* — having made; *eṣaḥ* — He; *vaktum* — to tell; *śaktaḥ* — the Mighty One; *śaktaḥ* — able; *svayam* — Himself; *api* — even; *na hi* — not at all; *śrīla* — Srila; *gaurāṅga* — Gauranga; *candraḥ* — the moon.

**Translation.** Wonderful indeed! What wise man is able to express even a particle of the artfulness and other [virtues] of Him, the ocean of virtues, the abode of loveliness? Even if He, Srila Gaurangachandra, were to multiply His own power twofold in order to tell of it — even He Himself, the Mighty One, would not be able.

**Note.** The repetition *śaktaḥ śaktaḥ* rests on two meanings of the word: "mighty" (of Him) and "able" (to tell).

### 8

**8.**

```
अस्य श्रीमद्व्रजवरवधूप्राणनाथस्य लीला-
लावण्याढ्यं तरुणिमसुधासम्भृतं तं विलासम् ।
ये तत्पादाम्बुजमधुकरा वक्त्रतो हन्त तेषां
श्रुत्वा कोऽपि प्रचलहृदयश्चापलादेष वक्ति ॥८॥
```
*asya śrīmad-vraja-vara-vadhū-prāṇa-nāthasya līlā-
lāvaṇyāḍhyaṁ taruṇima-sudhā-sambhṛtaṁ taṁ vilāsam |
ye tat-pādāmbuja-madhukarā vaktrato hanta teṣāṁ
śrutvā ko 'pi pracala-hṛdayaś cāpalād eṣa vakti ||8||*

**Word for word:** *asya* — of this; *śrīmat* — of the beautiful; *vraja* — of Vraja; *vara* — of the best; *vadhū* — of the maidens; *prāṇa* — of the life; *nāthasya* — of the Lord; *līlā* — of pastimes; *lāvaṇya* — in loveliness; *āḍhyam* — rich; *taruṇima* — of youth; *sudhā* — with the nectar; *sambhṛtam* — filled; *tam* — that; *vilāsam* — play; *ye* — who; *tat* — of His; *pāda* — of the feet; *ambuja* — of the lotuses; *madhukarāḥ* — bees; *vaktrataḥ* — from the mouths; *hanta* — ah; *teṣām* — of them; *śrutvā* — having heard; *kaḥ* — some-; *api* — -one; *pracala* — with trembling; *hṛdayaḥ* — heart; *cāpalāt* — out of rashness; *eṣaḥ* — this; *vakti* — speaks.

**Translation.** He is the Lord of the life of the beautiful, best maidens of Vraja. Having heard of that play of His — rich in the loveliness of pastimes and filled with the nectar of youth — from the mouths of those who are bees at His lotus feet, ah, this someone, his heart trembling, speaks of it out of rashness.

**Note.** "This someone" (*ko 'pi eṣaḥ*) is the poet himself. In the edition the first word is printed with an old form of अ that resembles ग्र.

### 9

**9.**

```
क्वासौ तत्तद्विबुधनगरीचक्रचूडामणीनां
ब्रह्मादीनां मुकुटपदवीरत्ननीराजिताङ्घ्रिः ।
चापल्यैकप्रवणहृदयः क्वाहमत्यन्तमुग्ध-
स्तत्कारुण्यं महदिति कदाप्येष सद्भिर्न हेयः ॥९॥
```
*kvāsau tat-tad-vibudha-nagarī-cakra-cūḍāmaṇīnāṁ
brahmādīnāṁ mukuṭa-padavī-ratna-nīrājitāṅghriḥ |
cāpalyaika-pravaṇa-hṛdayaḥ kvāham atyanta-mugdhas
tat kāruṇyaṁ mahad iti kadāpy eṣa sadbhir na heyaḥ ||9||*

**Word for word:** *kva* — where; *asau* — He; *tat-tat* — of all those; *vibudha* — of the gods; *nagarī* — of the cities; *cakra* — of the circle; *cūḍāmaṇīnām* — of the crest-jewels; *brahma* — of Brahma; *ādīnām* — and the others; *mukuṭa* — of the crowns; *padavī* — in the settings; *ratna* — by the jewels; *nīrājita* — honoured with lamps; *aṅghriḥ* — [whose] feet; *cāpalya* — to frivolity; *eka* — solely; *pravaṇa* — inclined; *hṛdayaḥ* — with heart; *kva* — where; *aham* — I; *atyanta* — utterly; *mugdhaḥ* — foolish; *tat* — His; *kāruṇyam* — compassion; *mahat* — great; *iti* — therefore; *kadā* — ever; *api* — at all; *eṣaḥ* — this one; *sadbhiḥ* — by the virtuous; *na* — not; *heyaḥ* — to be rejected.

**Translation.** Where is He, whose feet are honoured, as with the lamps of *arati*, by the jewels set in the crowns of Brahma and the others, the crest-jewels of the circle of all the cities of the gods? And where am I, utterly foolish, my heart inclined to frivolity alone? His compassion is great — therefore let the virtuous never reject this one.

### 10

**10.**

```
यद्यद्दृष्टं श्रुतमपि च यत्तस्य लीलाविलासै-
स्तत्तत्प्राणैरतिशयमहामूढचित्ताय यन्मे ।
भूयो भूयः कथितमिति यद्यद्धृतं तत्र तत्र
क्षुद्रोऽयं तत्कथयति कियत्तत्कृपाया वशः सन् ॥१०॥
```
*yad yad dṛṣṭaṁ śrutam api ca yat tasya līlā-vilāsais
tat-tat-prāṇair atiśaya-mahā-mūḍha-cittāya yan me |
bhūyo bhūyaḥ kathitam iti yad yad dhṛtaṁ tatra tatra
kṣudro 'yaṁ tat kathayati kiyat tat-kṛpāyā vaśaḥ san ||10||*

**Word for word:** *yat yat* — whatever; *dṛṣṭam* — was seen; *śrutam* — heard; *api* — also; *ca* — and; *yat* — what; *tasya* — of Him; *līlā* — of pastimes; *vilāsaiḥ* — with the sports; *tat-tat* — with those very; *prāṇaiḥ* — [whose] life; *atiśaya* — exceedingly; *mahā* — greatly; *mūḍha* — foolish; *cittāya* — to [one] of mind; *yat* — what; *me* — to me; *bhūyaḥ bhūyaḥ* — again and again; *kathitam* — was told; *iti* — thus; *yat yat* — whatever; *dhṛtam* — retained; *tatra tatra* — here and there; *kṣudraḥ* — insignificant; *ayam* — this; *tat* — that; *kathayati* — tells; *kiyat* — a little; *tat* — of His; *kṛpāyāḥ* — of the mercy; *vaśaḥ* — under the sway; *san* — being.

**Translation.** Whatever I saw, and what I also heard; what was told again and again to me, so exceedingly foolish of mind, by those whose very life is the play of His pastimes; whatever [I] retained here and there — of that this insignificant one tells a little, being under the sway of His mercy.

**Note.** An important testimony to the sources of the poem: part of it the author saw himself (he was a child while Mahaprabhu was present in this world), part he heard from His close companions. *Līlā-vilāsais tat-tat-prāṇaiḥ* — the analysis is tentative: "by those whose life is these pastimes."

### 11

**11.**

```
संपूर्णोऽयं भवति यदि वा नोद्यमस्तेन किं मे
यावत्तावत्प्रभुविलसितोत्कीर्त्तने भूरि भाग्यम् ।
यद्वा शक्तेः सममनुवदन्नैव हास्याय सोऽयं
यस्मान्नैतच्चरितमखिलं ब्रह्मणोऽपि प्रमेयम् ॥११॥
```
*sampūrṇo 'yaṁ bhavati yadi vā nodyamas tena kiṁ me
yāvat tāvat prabhu-vilasitotkīrtane bhūri bhāgyam |
yad vā śakteḥ samam anuvadan naiva hāsyāya so 'yaṁ
yasmān naitac caritam akhilaṁ brahmaṇo 'pi prameyam ||11||*

**Word for word:** *sampūrṇaḥ* — complete; *ayam* — this; *bhavati* — becomes; *yadi* — if; *vā* — or; *na* — not; *udyamaḥ* — effort; *tena* — with that; *kim* — what; *me* — to me; *yāvat* — as much as; *tāvat* — so much; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *vilasita* — of the pastimes; *utkīrtane* — in the glorification; *bhūri* — great; *bhāgyam* — good fortune; *yat vā* — or else; *śakteḥ* — to [one's] power; *samam* — in proportion; *anuvadan* — retelling; *na* — not; *eva* — at all; *hāsyāya* — for laughter; *saḥ* — that; *ayam* — this [one]; *yasmāt* — because; *na* — not; *etat* — this; *caritam* — life-story; *akhilam* — in its entirety; *brahmaṇaḥ* — for Brahma; *api* — even; *prameyam* — measurable.

**Translation.** Whether this effort is completed or not — what is that to me? In glorifying the pastimes of the Lord, however much [is told], so much is great good fortune. Or else: one who retells them in proportion to his power is no cause for laughter at all, since this life-story in its entirety cannot be measured even by Brahma.

### 12

**12.**

```
यद्येतस्मिन्नहह भविता दूषणं न प्रमादात्
किञ्चित्तस्मिन्न खलु सुधियामाग्रहो जातु भावी ।
यत्ते श्रीमच्चरणकमलद्वन्द्वगाथानुमत्ता-
स्तस्मादेषु क्षणमपि न मे वर्त्तते काप्यपेक्षा ॥१२॥
```
*yady etasminn ahaha bhavitā dūṣaṇaṁ na pramādāt
kiñcit tasminn na khalu sudhiyām āgraho jātu bhāvī |
yat te śrīmac-caraṇa-kamala-dvandva-gāthānumattās
tasmād eṣu kṣaṇam api na me vartate kāpy apekṣā ||12||*

**Word for word:** *yadi* — if; *etasmin* — in this; *ahaha* — alas; *bhavitā* — there will be; *dūṣaṇam* — a flaw; *na* — not; *pramādāt* — from carelessness; *kiñcit* — some; *tasmin* — on it; *na* — not; *khalu* — surely; *sudhiyām* — of the wise; *āgrahaḥ* — insistence; *jātu* — ever; *bhāvī* — will be; *yat* — because; *te* — they; *śrīmat* — of the beautiful; *caraṇa* — feet; *kamala* — of the lotuses; *dvandva* — of the pair; *gāthā* — with the songs; *anumattāḥ* — intoxicated; *tasmāt* — therefore; *eṣu* — about them; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment; *api* — even; *na* — not; *me* — to me; *vartate* — is; *kā* — any; *api* — at all; *apekṣā* — concern.

**Translation.** If in this [work], alas, there should be some flaw — though not from carelessness — the wise will surely never dwell on it, for they are intoxicated with songs of the pair of [His] beautiful lotus feet. Therefore I have no concern about them even for a moment.

**Note.** *Na pramādāt* in the first line — so also in the Gosvami edition (GGM); the sense "though not from carelessness" is rendered literally.

### 13

**13.**

```
श्रीमद्वृन्दावनवरवधूप्राणनाथः समस्तं
विश्वं प्रेमामृतलहरिभिर्निर्भरं प्लावयित्वा ।
तल्लीलामृतमपि मुहुः स्वादयित्वा विशेषं
भूयस्तासां निकटमगमत्तद्वियोगाक्षमोऽसौ ॥१३॥
```
*śrīmad-vṛndāvana-vara-vadhū-prāṇa-nāthaḥ samastaṁ
viśvaṁ premāmṛta-laharibhir nirbharaṁ plāvayitvā |
tal-līlāmṛtam api muhuḥ svādayitvā viśeṣaṁ
bhūyas tāsāṁ nikaṭam agamat tad-viyogākṣamo 'sau ||13||*

**Word for word:** *śrīmat* — of beautiful; *vṛndāvana* — of Vrindavana; *vara* — of the best; *vadhū* — of the maidens; *prāṇa* — of the life; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *samastam* — the whole; *viśvam* — universe; *prema* — of love; *amṛta* — of the nectar; *laharibhiḥ* — with the waves; *nirbharam* — to the brim; *plāvayitvā* — having flooded; *tat* — of those; *līlā* — of pastimes; *amṛtam* — the nectar; *api* — also; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *svādayitvā* — having given to taste; *viśeṣam* — especially; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *tāsām* — of them; *nikaṭam* — to the nearness; *agamat* — went; *tat* — from them; *viyoga* — separation; *akṣamaḥ* — unable to bear; *asau* — He.

**Translation.** The Lord of the life of the beautiful, best maidens of Vrindavana flooded the whole universe to the brim with waves of the nectar of love; again and again He gave [all] a special taste of the nectar of those pastimes; and then, unable to bear separation from those maidens, He went back to be near them once more.

**Note.** Here the poet explains **Mahaprabhu's disappearance**: Krishna returned to the gopis, without whom He could not bear to be. The link with verse 14 ("He disappeared") supports this reading.

### 14

**14.**

```
इत्थं तत्तद्विलसितसुधापूरमास्वाद्य भूयः
शिक्षाव्याजात्प्रथितकरुणे हन्त हान्तर्दधाने ।
एतत्प्राणाः कथमपि इहा जीवनैः संविसृष्टाः
केचिद्भूमौ करुणकरुणाः सन्ति केचित्प्रयाताः ॥१४॥
```
*itthaṁ tat-tad-vilasita-sudhā-pūram āsvādya bhūyaḥ
śikṣā-vyājāt prathita-karuṇe hanta hāntardadhāne |
etat-prāṇāḥ katham api ihā jīvanaiḥ saṁvisṛṣṭāḥ
kecid bhūmau karuṇa-karuṇāḥ santi kecit prayātāḥ ||14||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *tat-tat* — of those; *vilasita* — of pastimes; *sudhā* — of nectar; *pūram* — the flood; *āsvādya* — having tasted; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *śikṣā* — of teaching; *vyājāt* — under the pretext; *prathita* — made manifest; *karuṇe* — [He whose] compassion; *hanta* — alas; *hā* — alas; *antardadhāne* — [when He] disappeared; *etat* — He; *prāṇāḥ* — [whose] life; *katham* — some-; *api* — -how; *ihā* — here; *jīvanaiḥ* — by life; *saṁvisṛṣṭāḥ* — abandoned; *kecit* — some; *bhūmau* — on the earth; *karuṇa-karuṇāḥ* — most pitiable; *santi* — remain; *kecit* — others; *prayātāḥ* — departed.

**Translation.** Thus, having once more tasted the flood of the nectar of those pastimes, He whose compassion had been made manifest under the pretext of teaching — alas, alas! — disappeared. Those whose life He was remain here somehow, as if abandoned by life itself: some, most pitiable, are on the earth; others have departed [after Him].

**Note.** *Ihā* — the vowel is lengthened for the metre (the *mandakranta* requires a long syllable at this point). *Śikṣā-vyāja*, "the pretext of teaching": Chaitanya's preaching is only the outward reason for His coming; the inner reason is the tasting of His own pastimes (verse 13).

### 15

**15.**

```
हा गौरांग प्रियतम हहा हा प्रभो दीनबन्धो
हा हा कष्टं निजधनजनप्राणजातिस्वरूप ।
इत्थं भूयः करुणकरुणः क्रन्दतां वाक्प्रबन्ध-
श्चित्तं भित्तीरपि च शतधा हन्त सद्यः करोति ॥१५॥
```
*hā gaurāṅga priyatama hahā hā prabho dīna-bandho
hā hā kaṣṭaṁ nija-dhana-jana-prāṇa-jāti-svarūpa |
itthaṁ bhūyaḥ karuṇa-karuṇaḥ krandatāṁ vāk-prabandhaś
cittaṁ bhittīr api ca śatadhā hanta sadyaḥ karoti ||15||*

**Word for word:** *hā* — O; *gaurāṅga* — Gauranga; *priyatama* — dearest; *hahā hā* — alas; *prabho* — O Lord; *dīna* — of the wretched; *bandho* — O friend; *hā hā* — oh; *kaṣṭam* — woe; *nija* — our own; *dhana* — of wealth; *jana* — of kin; *prāṇa* — of life; *jāti* — of lineage; *svarūpa* — O very embodiment; *ittham* — thus; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *karuṇa-karuṇaḥ* — most piteous; *krandatām* — of those weeping; *vāk* — of words; *prabandhaḥ* — the stream; *cittam* — the heart; *bhittīḥ* — walls; *api* — even; *ca* — and; *śatadhā* — into a hundred pieces; *hanta* — ah; *sadyaḥ* — at once; *karoti* — makes.

**Translation.** "O Gauranga, dearest! Alas, alas, O Lord, friend of the wretched! Oh, oh, woe — You who were the very embodiment of our wealth, our kin, our life and our lineage!" Thus, again and again, the most piteous stream of words of those who wept at once broke the heart, and even walls, into a hundred pieces — ah!

### 16

**16.**

```
केचित्केचिद्बहुविकलितास्तद्वियोगाग्नितापै-
र्दृष्ट्वा विश्वं प्रलयसमयप्रायशून्यातिशून्यम् ।
अन्तर्बाष्पव्रणशतकृतां वेदनां तैर्विलापै-
र्दूरीकर्त्तुं रुरुदुरसकृद्धाहहेत्युच्चनादैः ॥१६॥
```
*kecit kecid bahu-vikalitās tad-viyogāgni-tāpair
dṛṣṭvā viśvaṁ pralaya-samaya-prāya-śūnyātiśūnyam |
antar-bāṣpa-vraṇa-śata-kṛtāṁ vedanāṁ tair vilāpair
dūrīkartuṁ rurudur asakṛd dhā hahety ucca-nādaiḥ ||16||*

**Word for word:** *kecit kecit* — some; *bahu* — greatly; *vikalitāḥ* — weakened; *tat* — from Him; *viyoga* — of separation; *agni* — of the fire; *tāpaiḥ* — by the heat; *dṛṣṭvā* — seeing; *viśvam* — the world; *pralaya* — of the dissolution of the universe; *samaya* — at the time; *prāya* — as; *śūnya* — empty; *atiśūnyam* — utterly empty; *antar* — of inner; *bāṣpa* — tears; *vraṇa* — of wounds; *śata* — by a hundred; *kṛtām* — caused; *vedanām* — the pain; *taiḥ* — with those; *vilāpaiḥ* — laments; *dūrī* — away; *kartum* — to drive; *ruruduḥ* — wept; *asakṛt* — incessantly; *hā* — ah; *haha* — ah; *iti* — thus; *ucca* — with loud; *nādaiḥ* — cries.

**Translation.** Some, utterly weakened by the heat of the fire of separation from Him, saw the world as empty, utterly empty, as at the time of the dissolution of the universe; and to drive away with those laments the pain caused by a hundred wounds of inward tears, they wept incessantly with loud cries of "Ah! Ah!"

### 17

**17.**

```
हाहा लीना भवति सततं क्षोभशोकाग्निपूरे
हाहा प्राणप्रियतम भवद्विप्रयोगे धरित्री ।
पूर्वं यासौ तव चरणयोः स्निग्धमुग्धैर्विहारैः
स्निग्धैरासीत् सुकृतसुकृता धन्यधन्यातिपुण्या ॥१७॥
```
*hāhā līnā bhavati satataṁ kṣobha-śokāgni-pūre
hāhā prāṇa-priyatama bhavad-viprayoge dharitrī |
pūrvaṁ yāsau tava caraṇayoḥ snigdha-mugdhair vihāraiḥ
snigdhair āsīt sukṛta-sukṛtā dhanya-dhanyāti-puṇyā ||17||*

**Word for word:** *hāhā* — alas; *līnā* — sunk; *bhavati* — becomes; *satatam* — constantly; *kṣobha* — of agitation; *śoka* — of grief; *agni* — of the fire; *pūre* — in the flood; *hāhā* — alas; *prāṇa* — than life; *priyatama* — O dearer; *bhavat* — from You; *viprayoge* — in separation; *dharitrī* — the earth; *pūrvam* — formerly; *yā* — who; *asau* — she; *tava* — of Your; *caraṇayoḥ* — of the feet; *snigdha* — with tender; *mugdhaiḥ* — charming; *vihāraiḥ* — wanderings; *snigdhaiḥ* — tender; *āsīt* — was; *sukṛta-sukṛtā* — most blessed; *dhanya-dhanyā* — most fortunate; *ati* — exceedingly; *puṇyā* — holy.

**Translation.** "Alas, alas, O You who are dearer than life! In separation from You the earth is constantly sunk in a flood of the fire of agitation and grief — she who formerly, through the tender, charming, tender wanderings of Your feet, was most blessed, most fortunate, exceedingly holy."

### 18

**18.**

```
किं किं तस्मादहह सुकृतं दीर्घदीर्घं समन्ता-
च्चक्रे पृथ्वी तव पदरसैर्यत्प्रकृष्टा रसासीत् ।
हाहा संप्रत्यपि विरहिता हन्त सर्वंसहेति
स्वीयं नाम प्रकरणवशादन्वितार्थं चकार ॥१८॥
```
*kiṁ kiṁ tasmād ahaha sukṛtaṁ dīrgha-dīrghaṁ samantāc
cakre pṛthvī tava pada-rasair yat prakṛṣṭā rasāsīt |
hāhā sampraty api virahitā hanta sarvaṁsaheti
svīyaṁ nāma prakaraṇa-vaśād anvitārthaṁ cakāra ||18||*

**Word for word:** *kim kim* — what; *tasmāt* — before that; *ahaha* — ah; *sukṛtam* — pious deeds; *dīrgha-dīrgham* — long, long; *samantāt* — everywhere; *cakre* — performed; *pṛthvī* — the earth; *tava* — of Your; *pada* — of the feet; *rasaiḥ* — with the juices; *yat* — that; *prakṛṣṭā* — supreme; *rasā* — Rasa; *āsīt* — was; *hāhā* — alas; *samprati* — now; *api* — but; *virahitā* — separated; *hanta* — ah; *sarvaṁsahā* — "All-enduring"; *iti* — thus; *svīyam* — her own; *nāma* — name; *prakaraṇa* — of circumstance; *vaśāt* — by the force; *anvita* — conforming; *artham* — to the meaning; *cakāra* — made.

**Translation.** "Ah, what long, long pious deeds did the earth perform everywhere before, that through the juices of Your feet she was Rasa in the highest sense, 'full of taste'? Alas, now, separated [from You] — ah! — by force of circumstance she has made her other name, Sarvamsaha, 'All-enduring', true to its meaning."

**Note.** A play on two Sanskrit names of the Earth: *rasā* ("earth", but also "full of *rasa*, of taste") and *sarvaṁsahā* ("earth", literally "bearing everything").

### 19

**19.**

```
हाहा नाथ प्रियतम मनोनाथ कारुण्यसिन्धो
निःसीमागःशमन दयित प्रेष्ठ हाहा हताः स्मः ।
```

*hāhā nātha priyatama mano-nātha kāruṇya-sindho

**Word for word:** *hāhā* — alas; *nātha* — O Lord; *priyatama* — O dearest; *manaḥ* — of the heart; *nātha* — O lord; *kāruṇya* — of compassion; *sindho* — O ocean; *niḥsīma* — of boundless; *āgaḥ* — offences; *śamana* — O extinguisher; *dayita* — O beloved; *preṣṭha* — O dearest of all; *hāhā* — alas; *hatāḥ* — lost; *smaḥ* — we are; *sarvaḥ* — the whole; *lokaḥ* — world; *tava* — of Your; *caraṇayoḥ* — of the feet; *viprayoge* — in separation; *ati* — most; *durge* — impassable; *līnaḥ* — sunk; *dīnaḥ* — wretched; *śvasiti* — sighs; *paramaiḥ* — with the greatest; *duṣkṛtānām* — of misdeeds; *samūhaiḥ* — with heaps.

### 20

**20.**

```
ये ये स्निग्धाः परमसुहृदस्ते त एव प्रयाता-
स्ते ते धन्याः प्रभुचरणयोः प्रेममात्रैकसाध्याः ।
हा धिक् कष्टं प्रभुमपि च तं तं संगं समेत्य
प्राणान्तस्तद्विरहविकलाः सन्ति हा धिक् कठोराः ॥२०॥
```
*ye ye snigdhāḥ parama-suhṛdas te ta eva prayātās
te te dhanyāḥ prabhu-caraṇayoḥ prema-mātraika-sādhyāḥ |
hā dhik kaṣṭaṁ prabhum api ca taṁ taṁ saṅgaṁ sametya
prāṇāntas tad-viraha-vikalāḥ santi hā dhik kaṭhorāḥ ||20||*

**Word for word:** *ye ye* — those who; *snigdhāḥ* — tender; *parama* — closest; *suhṛdaḥ* — friends; *te te* — they; *eva* — alone; *prayātāḥ* — departed; *te te* — they; *dhanyāḥ* — blessed; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *caraṇayoḥ* — for the feet; *prema* — love; *mātra* — only; *eka* — sole; *sādhyāḥ* — [whose] goal; *hā* — oh; *dhik* — shame; *kaṣṭam* — woe; *prabhum* — the Lord; *api* — and; *ca* — also; *tam tam* — that very; *saṅgam* — association; *sametya* — having attained; *prāṇāḥ* — the life-breaths; *antaḥ* — within; *tat* — from Him; *viraha* — by separation; *vikalāḥ* — tormented; *santi* — remain; *hā* — oh; *dhik* — shame; *kaṭhorāḥ* — hard.

**Translation.** "Those who were tender, His closest friends — they alone have departed. Blessed are they, whose sole goal was love alone for the feet of the Lord. Oh, shame and woe! We met the Lord Himself and had that very association with Him, and yet our life-breaths, tormented by separation from Him, remain within. Oh, shame — how hard they are!"

**Note.** *Prāṇāntas* is parsed as *prāṇāḥ antaḥ* ("the breath of life within"). Compare verse 26.

### 21

**21.**

```
ये तच्छ्रीमत्पदकमलयोः सौरभीं माधुरीं वा
तामासाद्य क्षणमपि न यत्सर्वमेव त्यजन्ति ।
ते वा कष्टं किमुत पशवः किं नु वृक्षा विरूढाः
किं ग्रावाणः शिव शिव न वा चेतनाभिर्विहीनाः ॥२१॥
```
*ye tac-chrīmat-pada-kamalayoḥ saurabhīṁ mādhurīṁ vā
tām āsādya kṣaṇam api na yat sarvam eva tyajanti |
te vā kaṣṭaṁ kim uta paśavaḥ kiṁ nu vṛkṣā virūḍhāḥ
kiṁ grāvāṇaḥ śiva śiva na vā cetanābhir vihīnāḥ ||21||*

**Word for word:** *ye* — who; *tat* — His; *śrīmat* — beautiful; *pada* — of the feet; *kamalayoḥ* — of the lotuses; *saurabhīm* — the fragrance; *mādhurīm* — the sweetness; *vā* — or; *tām* — that; *āsādya* — having obtained; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment; *api* — even; *na* — not; *yat* — so that; *sarvam* — everything; *eva* — at once; *tyajanti* — abandon; *te* — they; *vā* — then; *kaṣṭam* — alas; *kim uta* — are they; *paśavaḥ* — beasts; *kim nu* — or; *vṛkṣāḥ* — trees; *virūḍhāḥ* — rooted; *kim* — or; *grāvāṇaḥ* — stones; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *na vā* — are they not; *cetanābhiḥ* — of consciousness; *vihīnāḥ* — devoid.

**Translation.** "Those who, having known even for a moment that fragrance or sweetness of His beautiful lotus feet, do not at once abandon everything — alas, what are they then? Beasts? Or trees rooted [in the ground]? Or stones? Heaven forbid — are they not devoid of consciousness?"

**Note.** *Śiva śiva* is an exclamation of horror and aversion, like "Heaven forbid!" It occurs many times in this passage.

### 22

**22.**

```
यत्पादाम्भोरुहयुगरसास्वादनेनैव तृप्ता-
स्त्यक्त्वैकान्तं धनजनगृहं प्रेममात्रैकसाध्याः ।
दीनाः सन्तः परमकृतिनो हन्त सन्तः समन्तात्
कान्तारान्तर्गिरिषु विपिनेष्वेवमेवं चरन्ति ॥२२॥
```
*yat-pādāmbhoruha-yuga-rasāsvādanenaiva tṛptās
tyaktvaikāntaṁ dhana-jana-gṛhaṁ prema-mātraika-sādhyāḥ |
dīnāḥ santaḥ parama-kṛtino hanta santaḥ samantāt
kāntārāntar giriṣu vipineṣv evam evaṁ caranti ||22||*

**Word for word:** *yat* — whose; *pāda* — of the feet; *ambhoruha* — of the lotuses; *yuga* — of the pair; *rasa* — of the juice; *āsvādanena* — by the tasting; *eva* — alone; *tṛptāḥ* — satisfied; *tyaktvā* — having abandoned; *ekāntam* — utterly; *dhana* — wealth; *jana* — kin; *gṛham* — home; *prema* — love; *mātra* — only; *eka* — sole; *sādhyāḥ* — [whose] goal; *dīnāḥ* — poor; *santaḥ* — being; *parama* — supremely; *kṛtinaḥ* — accomplished; *hanta* — ah; *santaḥ* — saints; *samantāt* — everywhere; *kāntāra* — of the wilderness; *antaḥ* — within; *giriṣu* — on the mountains; *vipineṣu* — in the forests; *evam evam* — just so; *caranti* — wander.

**Translation.** "Satisfied by the mere tasting of the juice of the pair of His lotus feet, having utterly abandoned wealth, kin and home, their sole goal love alone, these saints, supremely accomplished — ah! — wander about like this everywhere, poor: in the depths of the wilderness, on the mountains, in the forests."

**Note.** A play on the two meanings of *santaḥ*: "being" (poor) and "saints".

### 23

**23.**

```
श्रीमत्पादाम्बुजयुगरसं चक्षुषापीय गन्धं
तस्याघ्राय प्रणयमधुरं प्रेमसीधुं च पीत्वा ।
आस्वाद्यैतद्वचनमधुरं हन्त को जीवलोक-
स्तद्विच्छेदं शिव शिव हहा हा कथं हन्त सोढा ॥२३॥
```
*śrīmat-pādāmbuja-yuga-rasaṁ cakṣuṣāpīya gandhaṁ
tasyāghrāya praṇaya-madhuraṁ prema-sīdhuṁ ca pītvā |
āsvādyaitad-vacana-madhuraṁ hanta ko jīva-lokas
tad-vicchedaṁ śiva śiva hahā hā kathaṁ hanta soḍhā ||23||*

**Word for word:** *śrīmat* — of beautiful; *pāda* — feet; *ambuja* — of the lotuses; *yuga* — of the pair; *rasam* — the juice; *cakṣuṣā* — with the eye; *āpīya* — having drunk; *gandham* — the fragrance; *tasya* — of it; *āghrāya* — having inhaled; *praṇaya* — with affection; *madhuram* — sweet; *prema* — of love; *sīdhum* — the wine; *ca* — and; *pītvā* — having drunk; *āsvādya* — having tasted; *etat* — His; *vacana* — of words; *madhuram* — the sweetness; *hanta* — ah; *kaḥ* — what; *jīva* — of the living; *lokaḥ* — person; *tat* — from Him; *vicchedam* — separation; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *hahā hā* — alas; *katham* — how; *hanta* — ah; *soḍhā* — will bear.

**Translation.** "Having drunk with the eyes the juice of the pair of His beautiful lotus feet, having inhaled their fragrance, having drunk the wine of love, sweet with affection, having tasted the sweetness of His words — ah, who among the living will bear separation from Him? Heaven forbid! Alas, alas — how, ah, will one bear it?"

**Note.** The verse lists all the senses: sight, smell, taste and hearing. The very fullness of that association makes the separation unbearable.

### 24

**24.**

```
अद्याप्येतच्चरणकमलद्वन्द्वगन्धेन सर्वे
त्यक्तासङ्गा निरवधिगलत्सर्वबन्धाः समन्तात् ।
स्वैरं स्वैरं नटनरभसैः कीर्त्तनैः सञ्चरन्तो
वर्त्तन्ते तद्विरहदहनं कः सहेतास्य तस्य ॥२४॥
```
*adyāpy etac-caraṇa-kamala-dvandva-gandhena sarve
tyaktāsaṅgā niravadhi-galat-sarva-bandhāḥ samantāt |
svairaṁ svairaṁ naṭana-rabhasaiḥ kīrtanaiḥ sañcaranto
vartante tad-viraha-dahanaṁ kaḥ sahetāsya tasya ||24||*

**Word for word:** *adya* — today; *api* — even; *etat* — His; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *kamala* — of the lotuses; *dvandva* — of the pair; *gandhena* — by the fragrance; *sarve* — all; *tyakta* — having given up; *āsaṅgāḥ* — attachments; *niravadhi* — endlessly; *galat* — falling away; *sarva* — all; *bandhāḥ* — [whose] bonds; *samantāt* — everywhere; *svairam svairam* — quite freely; *naṭana* — of dancing; *rabhasaiḥ* — with the raptures; *kīrtanaiḥ* — with kirtanas; *sañcarantaḥ* — wandering; *vartante* — continue; *tat* — from Him; *viraha* — of separation; *dahanam* — the fire; *kaḥ* — who; *saheta* — could bear; *asya* — of Him; *tasya* — of Him.

**Translation.** "Even today, by the fragrance of the pair of His lotus feet, all [people] everywhere give up their attachments; all their bonds fall away without end, and they go on wandering quite freely, in the raptures of dancing, with kirtanas. Who could bear the fire of separation from Him — from Him Himself?"

**Note.** "Even today" means the time when the poem was written, after Mahaprabhu's disappearance: the kirtana goes on.

### 25

**25.**

```
कथं वा दृष्टौ तौ परमकरुणौ हन्त चरणौ
कथं वा दम्भोलीप्रकरकठिनोऽयं बत जनः ।
कथं वा तत्प्रेम्णः पदमयमहो तिष्ठति च वा
कथं तद्विच्छेदे शिव शिव विधेर्वैशसमिदम् ॥२५॥
```
*kathaṁ vā dṛṣṭau tau parama-karuṇau hanta caraṇau
kathaṁ vā dambholī-prakara-kaṭhino 'yaṁ bata janaḥ |
kathaṁ vā tat-premṇaḥ padam ayam aho tiṣṭhati ca vā
kathaṁ tad-vicchede śiva śiva vidher vaiśasam idam ||25||*

**Word for word:** *katham* — how; *vā* — then; *dṛṣṭau* — were seen; *tau* — those; *parama* — with supreme; *karuṇau* — [full of] compassion; *hanta* — ah; *caraṇau* — feet; *katham* — how; *vā* — then; *dambholī* — of thunderbolts; *prakara* — a heap; *kaṭhinaḥ* — hard [as]; *ayam* — this; *bata* — alas; *janaḥ* — person; *katham* — how; *vā* — then; *tat* — for Him; *premṇaḥ* — of love; *padam* — the abode; *ayam* — he; *aho* — oh; *tiṣṭhati* — remains; *ca* — and; *vā* — then; *katham* — how; *tat* — from Him; *vicchede* — in separation; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *vidheḥ* — of fate; *vaiśasam* — slaughter; *idam* — this.

**Translation.** "How then were those supremely compassionate feet ever seen, ah? And how, alas, is this person as hard as a heap of thunderbolts? And how, oh, does he still remain an abode of love for Him? And how — Heaven forbid! — [has come] this slaughter wrought by fate, in separation from Him?"

**Note.** *Dambholi* is the vajra, Indra's thunderbolt weapon; in Sanskrit it is the standard of indestructible hardness (the same word is used of diamond). *Prakara* means "heap, multitude": the man is harder not than one vajra but than a whole heap of them. With this verse the metre changes to *shikharini*. The syntax of the verse is broken — a deliberate device: speech falters from grief.

### 26

**26.**

```
जगच्छून्यं मन्ये क्षितिरपि च दुःखाग्निनिवहे
विलीना लीयन्ते सकल मनुजास्तत्र विकलाः ।
तथाप्येते प्राणाः शिव शिव न गच्छन्ति विधुरा
अहो चित्रं शिव शिव विधिर्वामचरितः ॥२६॥
```
*jagac chūnyaṁ manye kṣitir api ca duḥkhāgni-nivahe
vilīnā līyante sakala-manujās tatra vikalāḥ |
tathāpy ete prāṇāḥ śiva śiva na gacchanti vidhurā
aho citraṁ śiva śiva vidhir vāma-caritaḥ ||26||*

**Word for word:** *jagat* — the world; *śūnyam* — empty; *manye* — I think; *kṣitiḥ* — the earth; *api* — also; *ca* — and; *duḥkha* — of grief; *agni* — of the fire; *nivahe* — in the mass; *vilīnā* — melted; *līyante* — melt; *sakala* — all; *manujāḥ* — people; *tatra* — there; *vikalāḥ* — exhausted; *tathā api* — and yet; *ete* — these; *prāṇāḥ* — life-breaths; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *na* — not; *gacchanti* — depart; *vidhurāḥ* — bereaved; *aho* — oh; *citram* — wonder; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *vidhiḥ* — fate; *vāma* — perverse; *caritaḥ* — in conduct.

**Translation.** "The world, I think, is empty; the earth too has melted in the mass of the fire of grief, and all people, exhausted, are melting there. And yet these bereaved life-breaths — Heaven forbid! — do not depart. Oh, what a wonder! Heaven forbid! How perverse are the ways of fate!"

**Note.** The last line is metrically incomplete: 15 syllables instead of 17. So also in the Gosvami edition (GGM); the lacuna apparently goes back to a common manuscript tradition ⟨?⟩.

### 27

**27.**

```
अहो अद्याप्यस्य प्रियगुणगणानां लवमपि
क्षणं संशृण्वन्तः कति कति न देहत्यज इह ।
सदा श्रुत्वा दृष्ट्वा सततमनुभूयापि च सुखं
विना तं जीवामः शिव शिव महद्दुष्कृतमिदम् ॥२७॥
```
*aho adyāpy asya priya-guṇa-gaṇānāṁ lavam api
kṣaṇaṁ saṁśṛṇvantaḥ kati kati na deha-tyaja iha |
sadā śrutvā dṛṣṭvā satatam anubhūyāpi ca sukhaṁ
vinā taṁ jīvāmaḥ śiva śiva mahad duṣkṛtam idam ||27||*

**Word for word:** *aho* — oh; *adya* — today; *api* — even; *asya* — of His; *priya* — dear; *guṇa* — of qualities; *gaṇānām* — of the multitude; *lavam* — a particle; *api* — even; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment; *saṁśṛṇvantaḥ* — hearing; *kati kati* — how many; *na* — not; *deha* — the body; *tyajaḥ* — [who have] given up; *iha* — here; *sadā* — always; *śrutvā* — having heard; *dṛṣṭvā* — having seen; *satatam* — constantly; *anubhūya* — having experienced; *api* — also; *ca* — and; *sukham* — happiness; *vinā* — without; *tam* — Him; *jīvāmaḥ* — we live; *śiva śiva* — Heaven forbid; *mahat* — great; *duṣkṛtam* — sin; *idam* — this.

**Translation.** "Oh, even today how many here, hearing for a single moment even a particle of the multitude of His dear qualities, have given up the body! And we, who always heard [Him], saw [Him] and constantly experienced happiness, live without Him. Heaven forbid! This is a great sin."

**Note.** The sense: others give up the body out of love as soon as they hear of Him, while we, who saw Him, remain alive — and in this lies our guilt. The contrast in the second half of the verse and the link with verses 20 and 26 support this reading. A misprint in the edition: संश्रृण्वन्तः → संशृण्वन्तः.

### 28

**28.**

```
अहो धन्यैवेयं क्षितिरतितरां श्रीचरणयो-
रसैः पूर्णा नाम्ना गुणगणमहिम्ना च महता ।
तदेतद्विच्छेदानलविदलितेयं दलति नो
न जानीमः सीमां विधिविलसितस्य क्षणमपि ॥२८॥
```
*aho dhanyaiveyaṁ kṣitir atitarāṁ śrī-caraṇayo
rasaiḥ pūrṇā nāmnā guṇa-gaṇa-mahimnā ca mahatā |
tad etad-vicchedānala-vidaliteyaṁ dalati no
na jānīmaḥ sīmāṁ vidhi-vilasitasya kṣaṇam api ||28||*

**Word for word:** *aho* — oh; *dhanyā* — fortunate; *eva* — truly; *iyam* — this; *kṣitiḥ* — earth; *atitarām* — beyond measure; *śrī* — of beautiful; *caraṇayoḥ* — of the feet; *rasaiḥ* — with the juices; *pūrṇā* — full; *nāmnā* — with the Name; *guṇa* — of qualities; *gaṇa* — of the multitude; *mahimnā* — with the glory; *ca* — and; *mahatā* — great; *tat* — yet; *etat* — from Him; *viccheda* — of separation; *anala* — by the fire; *vidalitā* — torn apart; *iyam* — she; *dalati* — splits; *no* — not; *na* — not; *jānīmaḥ* — we know; *sīmām* — the limit; *vidhi* — of fate; *vilasitasya* — of the play; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment; *api* — even.

**Translation.** "Oh, truly fortunate beyond measure [was] this earth, full of the juices of His beautiful feet, of His Name, and of the great glory of the multitude of His qualities! Yet now, torn apart by the fire of separation from Him, she does not split asunder. We do not know, even for a moment, the limit of the play of fate."

**Note.** A paradox: the earth is "torn apart" (*vidalitā*) yet "does not split" (*dalati no*). It is the same thought as that of the life which does not depart (verses 20 and 26).

### 29

**29.**

```
इतीहोष्णं दीर्घं श्वसितमिदमुच्चैः प्रलपितं
वपुः क्षीणं क्षीणं नयनजलमत्यन्तबहुलम् ।
वहन्तोऽमी स्मृत्वा प्रियगुणगणं भूरि करुणं
रुदन्तोऽविश्रान्तं बत मुमुहुराश्चर्यमिति तत् ॥२९॥
```
*itīhoṣṇaṁ dīrghaṁ śvasitam idam uccaiḥ pralapitaṁ
vapuḥ kṣīṇaṁ kṣīṇaṁ nayana-jalam atyanta-bahulam |
vahanto 'mī smṛtvā priya-guṇa-gaṇaṁ bhūri karuṇaṁ
rudanto 'viśrāntaṁ bata mumuhur āścaryam iti tat ||29||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *iha* — here; *uṣṇam* — hot; *dīrgham* — long; *śvasitam* — breathing; *idam* — this; *uccaiḥ* — loud; *pralapitam* — lamentation; *vapuḥ* — body; *kṣīṇam kṣīṇam* — ever more wasted; *nayana* — of the eyes; *jalam* — water; *atyanta* — extremely; *bahulam* — abundant; *vahantaḥ* — bearing; *amī* — they; *smṛtvā* — having remembered; *priya* — dear; *guṇa* — of qualities; *gaṇam* — the multitude; *bhūri* — most; *karuṇam* — piteously; *rudantaḥ* — weeping; *aviśrāntam* — without rest; *bata* — alas; *mumuhuḥ* — swooned; *āścaryam* — a wonder; *iti* — thus; *tat* — that.

**Translation.** Thus, here, bearing these hot, long sighs and loud lamentations, a body wasting away more and more, and a most abundant water of the eyes, they remembered the multitude of His dear qualities, wept most piteously without rest, and — alas — swooned again and again. That was a wonder.

**Note.** The edition has *rudanto viśrāntam*; we read it with the omitted avagraha sign: *rudanto 'viśrāntam* ("without rest"). In the first word the anusvara is almost worn away in the print; *-ṣṇaṁ* is restored from the metre.

*Iti śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-caritāmṛte mahākāvye prathamaḥ sargaḥ* — Thus ends the first sarga of the mahakavya "Sri Krishna-Chaitanya-charitamrita".
