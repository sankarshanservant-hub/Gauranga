# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Six — verses 1–123 (printed pp. 162–201)

**Title of the sarga:** *Bhakta-sammelana*, "The Gathering of the Devotees" (from the colophon).
**Metre:** *vasanta-tilakā* (14 syllables per pada); verses 3 and 36 and the quotation after verse 101 — *anuṣṭubh*; the quotation after verse 69 — *upajāti*; verses 122–123 — *śikhariṇī*.
**Total verses:** 123 (plus two unnumbered quotations: after 69 and after 101).
**Parallels:** Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.21–25 (the explanation of *harer nāma*); Shuklambara Brahmachari ("Are there no jackals there?") — Murari 2.3.5–9; cf. Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 1.78–108 (prema in the house of Shuklambara) and 16.109 ff. (the Lord eats his rice).

**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–5 — in the house of Srivasa the Lord explains the glory of the Name: *harer nāma…* — the threefold repetition, *kevalam*, *eva*.
- 6–7 — He touches Sri Rama Pandita and the others with His feet and bestows *bhakti*.
- 8–11 — Shuklambara Brahmachari: "I have gone to Dvaraka and Mathura" — "And are there no jackals there?"; the promise of *prema*; his tears.
- 12–13 — at night the Lord bids Gadadhara give away the *nirmalya*; in the morning Gadadhara distributes the mahaprasada.
- 14 — Gadadhara adorns the Lord.
- 15–22 — clouds over the kirtana; the Lord sings with *mandiras* — the clouds disperse, the moon rises; dancing, the women's ululation.
- 23–26 — Shuklambara: "You have made Navadvipa a new Mathura"; dancing on the shoulders of friends; gifts.
- 27–34 — the miracle of the mango seed; "all this is maya — but, given to service, it becomes real".
- 35–36 — to Mukunda: what is Brahman? — the verse on "Rama".
- 37–41 — anger with Mukunda for preferring the four-armed form; "anoint me as Your servant" — His feet on Mukunda's head.
- 42–46 — to Murari Gupta: "give up 'spiritual' songs, sing of the Lord's feet"; the request of Narayana the physician (*vaidya*); the blessing on Murari to write about Gaura.
- 47–48 — Srivasa Pandita.
- 49–52 — a student: "the glory of the Name is *arthavāda*"; the Lord stops His ears and bathes in the Ganga in His clothes.
- 53–62 — at Advaita's: the embrace ("which of them is Advaita?"); "whoever says there is no bhakti in Kali is a sinner"; a brahmana who is not a Vaishnava does not come at the mental summons.
- 63–69 — "difficult knowledge" about the Atma; then: "My devotee purifies the three worlds" (+ Srimad-Bhagavatam 11.14.24).
- 70–75 — the club for the wicked; the leper who hated devotees; "this town is an impassable forest"; Advaita comes.
- 76–82 — "I have come for your sake"; Advaita's dance; Srivasa — "who then is a devotee?" — "apart from Him, there is neither Uddhava nor Shuka here"; Advaita is equal to the Lord.
- 83–85 — the ban on "spiritual" speculation; Murari: "I never even knew it".
- 86–88 — the Lord's summer dances; His attire.
- 89–97 — a portrait of Gaura dancing (arms, chest, girdle, hips, feet, lips); mridangas, mandiras, ululation; 96–97 — a *yugmaka*.
- 98–100 — the etymology of the name "Srivasa"; Murari recites verses about Rama — the Lord's foot on his head.
- 101–105 — "Ramadasa" on Murari's forehead; Srimad-Bhagavatam 11.14.20; "obey Srivasa"; to Sri Rama — "serve your elder brother"; the distribution of remnants.
- 106–108 — news of the arrival of the avadhuta Nityananda; the Lord sends for Him.
- 109–116 — the search; "He is at Nandana Acharya's"; the meeting, praise of Nityananda (detachment and love); the dust of His feet on the heads of the devotees.
- 117–123 — Nityananda is fed; at Srivasa's the Lord shows Him the six-armed, four-armed and two-armed forms; Nityananda's dance and laughter.

**Textual cruxes:** 15 (*udite*), 22 (*avakḷpta*), 23 (*dvīpam*), 34 (*bādhyata eva*), 37 (*nyūnam*), 41 (metre broken), 43 (*jīvitam ena*), 58 (*tasyaiva*), 60 (metre broken; *āgamat*), 64 (*jagad-eka-gatiḥ*), 112 (the variant *paścāt*), 116 (the numbering in the edition) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
श्रीवासगेहमुपगम्य कदाचिदेष
व्याख्यां चकार तदनन्तरमेव नाम्नाम् ।
माहात्म्यमुद्भटमिदं पुरुषार्थसर्व्व-
श्रेष्ठं श्रुतिप्रकरदुर्ल्लभमोदमादौ ॥१॥
```
*śrīvāsa-geham upagamya kadācid eṣa
vyākhyāṁ cakāra tad-anantaram eva nāmnām |
māhātmyam udbhaṭam idaṁ puruṣārtha-sarva-
śreṣṭhaṁ śruti-prakara-durlabha-modam ādau ||1||*

**Word for word:** *śrīvāsa* — of Srivasa; *geham* — to the house; *upagamya* — having come; *kadācit* — once; *eṣaḥ* — He; *vyākhyām* — an explanation; *cakāra* — made; *tat* — that; *anantaram* — immediately after; *eva* — indeed; *nāmnām* — of the Names; *māhātmyam* — the glory; *udbhaṭam* — mighty; *idam* — this; *puruṣa* — of human; *artha* — aims; *sarva* — of all; *śreṣṭham* — the highest; *śruti* — of the Shrutis; *prakara* — for the host; *durlabha* — hard to attain; *modam* — [bringing] a joy; *ādau* — first of all.

**Translation.** Once, soon after that, He came to the house of Srivasa and first of all explained the glory of the Names — this mighty [glory], the highest of all the aims of human life, bringing a joy hard to attain even for the whole host of the Shrutis.

**Note.** The four aims of human life (*puruṣārtha*) are dharma, artha, kama and moksha.

### 2

**2.**

```
स्वीये विलासरसनव्यमहाम्बुराशौ
नित्यं कुतूहलपरो विजिहीर्षुरेषः ।
आदौ स्वनाममहिमामृतरम्यपूरं
हर्षाद्वचोऽञ्जलिपुटैर्जगति व्यकारीत् ॥२॥
```
*svīye vilāsa-rasa-navya-mahāmbu-rāśau
nityaṁ kutūhala-paro vijihīrṣur eṣaḥ |
ādau sva-nāma-mahimāmṛta-ramya-pūraṁ
harṣād vaco-'ñjali-puṭair jagati vyakārīt ||2||*

**Word for word:** *svīye* — in His own; *vilāsa* — of pastimes; *rasa* — of the rasa; *navya* — new; *mahā* — great; *ambu-rāśau* — in the ocean; *nityam* — ever; *kutūhala* — to playfulness; *paraḥ* — given; *vijihīrṣuḥ* — wishing to sport; *eṣaḥ* — He; *ādau* — first; *sva* — of His own; *nāma* — of the Name; *mahima* — of the glory; *amṛta* — of the nectar; *ramya* — charming; *pūram* — the flood; *harṣāt* — out of joy; *vacaḥ* — of words; *añjali-puṭaiḥ* — with cupped handfuls; *jagati* — over the world; *vyakārīt* — scattered.

**Translation.** Wishing to sport in the new, great ocean of the rasa of His own pastimes, He, ever given to playfulness, first joyfully scattered over the world, with cupped handfuls of words, the charming flood of the nectar of the glory of His own Name.

### 3

**3.**

```
हरेर्नाम हरेर्नाम हरेर्नामैव केवलम् ।
कलौ नास्त्येव नास्त्येव नास्त्येव गतिरन्यथा ॥३॥
```
*harer nāma harer nāma harer nāmaiva kevalam |
kalau nāsty eva nāsty eva nāsty eva gatir anyathā ||3||*

**Word for word:** *hareḥ* — of Hari; *nāma* — the Name; *hareḥ* — of Hari; *nāma* — the Name; *hareḥ* — of Hari; *nāma* — the Name; *eva* — alone; *kevalam* — only; *kalau* — in Kali; *na asti* — there is not; *eva* — truly; *na asti* — there is not; *eva* — truly; *na asti* — there is not; *eva* — truly; *gatiḥ* — way; *anyathā* — other.

**Translation.** The Name of Hari, the Name of Hari, the Name of Hari alone — only that! In Kali there is no other way — truly none, truly none, truly none.

**Note.** The verse is from the Brihan-naradiya Purana (38.126); Mahaprabhu explains it in the Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.21–25. Verses 4–5 are Karnapura's explanation, close to the Chaitanya-charitamrita.

### 4

**4.**

```
नाद्यः पुमानयमुदेति सदैव भूमौ
नामस्वरूपमिति तत्तु कलौ विदन्तु ।
बारत्रये च पुनरुक्तिरथैवकारो
दार्ढ्याय सर्व्वजगतो बहुजाड्यभाजः ॥४॥
```
*nādyaḥ pumān ayam udeti sadaiva bhūmau
nāma-svarūpam iti tat tu kalau vidantu |
bāra-traye ca punar-uktir athaiva-kāro
dārḍhyāya sarva-jagato bahu-jāḍya-bhājaḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *na* — not; *ādyaḥ* — original; *pumān* — Person; *ayam* — this; *udeti* — rises; *sadā eva* — always; *bhūmau* — on the earth; *nāma* — of the Name; *svarūpam* — in the form; *iti* — thus; *tat* — that; *tu* — but; *kalau* — in Kali; *vidantu* — let [all] know; *bāra* — times; *traye* — in three; *ca* — and; *punaḥ* — repeated; *uktiḥ* — statement; *atha* — and also; *eva* — "eva"; *kāraḥ* — the word; *dārḍhyāya* — for the firmness; *sarva* — of the whole; *jagataḥ* — world; *bahu* — great; *jāḍya* — dullness; *bhājaḥ* — possessing.

**Translation.** This Original Person in the form of the Name does not rise on the earth at all times: let all know that [He rises] in Kali. And the statement repeated three times, and the word *eva* too, are for the firmness of the whole world, sunk in great dullness.

### 5

**5.**

```
कैवल्यमेव तदिदं त्विति केवलस्य
शब्दस्य दार्ढ्यमनने प्रतिपादनं तत् ।
यस्त्वन्यथा वदति तस्य गतिर्हि नास्ति
नास्त्येव निश्चितमिदं पुनरेवकारात् ॥५॥
```
*kaivalyam eva tad idaṁ tv iti kevalasya
śabdasya dārḍhya-manane pratipādanaṁ tat |
yas tv anyathā vadati tasya gatir hi nāsti
nāsty eva niścitam idaṁ punar eva-kārāt ||5||*

**Word for word:** *kaivalyam* — exclusiveness; *eva* — indeed; *tat* — that; *idam* — this; *tu* — but; *iti* — thus; *kevalasya* — "kevalam"; *śabdasya* — of the word; *dārḍhya* — firm; *manane* — for understanding; *pratipādanam* — the declaration; *tat* — that; *yaḥ* — who; *tu* — but; *anyathā* — otherwise; *vadati* — speaks; *tasya* — for him; *gatiḥ* — way; *hi* — indeed; *na asti* — there is not; *na asti* — there is not; *eva* — truly; *niścitam* — made certain; *idam* — this; *punaḥ* — again; *eva* — "eva"; *kārāt* — by the word.

**Translation.** "This alone, exclusively" — that is what the word *kevalam* declares, so that it may be firmly understood. But for one who speaks otherwise there is no way — truly none: this is made certain by the word *eva*, [repeated] again.

**Note.** Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.23–25: *kevala* excludes knowledge, yoga, austerity and ritual; *anyathā* — "for one who does otherwise there is no deliverance"; *eva* three times — for firmness. *Kaivalya* here is not "liberation" but "exclusiveness" (an abstract noun from *kevala*). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 6

**6.**

```
इत्यूचिवानथ कृतप्रकटप्रकाशः
श्रीमद्वरासनमुपेत्य कृपासमुद्रः ।
पादारविन्दयुगलेन मनोरमेण
श्रीरामपण्डितमुखान् सममस्पृशद्द्राक् ॥६॥
```
*ity ūcivān atha kṛta-prakaṭa-prakāśaḥ
śrīmad-varāsanam upetya kṛpā-samudraḥ |
pādāravinda-yugalena manorameṇa
śrīrāma-paṇḍita-mukhān samam aspṛśad drāk ||6||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *ūcivān* — having spoken; *atha* — then; *kṛta* — having made; *prakaṭa* — manifest; *prakāśaḥ* — [His] effulgence; *śrīmat* — splendid; *vara* — excellent; *āsanam* — seat; *upetya* — having taken; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *samudraḥ* — the ocean; *pāda* — of the feet; *aravinda* — of lotuses; *yugalena* — with the pair; *manorameṇa* — charming; *śrīrāma-paṇḍita* — Sri Rama Pandita; *mukhān* — headed by; *samam* — all together; *aspṛśat* — touched; *drāk* — at once.

**Translation.** Having spoken thus, the ocean of mercy then openly manifested His effulgence, took the splendid seat of honour and at once touched them all together, headed by Sri Rama Pandita, with the charming pair of His lotus feet.

**Note.** Sri Rama Pandita is Srivasa's brother.

### 7

**7.**

```
तेऽपि प्रणम्य सहसा नतकन्धरेण
प्रेमस्वरूपभजनं मुदिताः समीयुः ।
तेभ्यो ददावभिमतं भगवान् प्रकामं
श्रीमान् स्वभक्तजनवत्सलतातिरम्यः ॥७॥
```
*te 'pi praṇamya sahasā nata-kandhareṇa
prema-svarūpa-bhajanaṁ muditāḥ samīyuḥ |
tebhyo dadāv abhimataṁ bhagavān prakāmaṁ
śrīmān sva-bhakta-jana-vatsalatāti-ramyaḥ ||7||*

**Word for word:** *te* — they; *api* — too; *praṇamya* — having bowed down; *sahasā* — at once; *nata* — with bent; *kandhareṇa* — neck; *prema* — love; *svarūpa* — whose very nature; *bhajanam* — service; *muditāḥ* — joyful; *samīyuḥ* — attained; *tebhyaḥ* — to them; *dadau* — gave; *abhimatam* — what was desired; *bhagavān* — Bhagavan; *prakāmam* — abundantly; *śrīmān* — beautiful; *sva* — to His own; *bhakta* — devoted; *jana* — people; *vatsalatā* — by affection; *ati* — exceedingly; *ramyaḥ* — charming.

**Translation.** They too at once bowed down with bent necks and joyfully attained service whose very nature is love. The beautiful Bhagavan, exceedingly charming in His affection for His devotees, abundantly gave them what they desired.

### 8

**8.**

```
शुक्लाम्बरो द्विजवरः सुभगोऽथ कश्चि-
दूचे प्रभुं प्रकटितातिशयप्रकाशम् ।
द्वारावतीञ्च मथुराञ्च सदैव गत्वा
मां दुःखिनं क्षणमवेक्ष्य दयस्व नाथ ॥८॥
```
*śuklāmbaro dvija-varaḥ subhago 'tha kaścid
ūce prabhuṁ prakaṭitātiśaya-prakāśam |
dvārāvatīñ ca mathurāñ ca sadaiva gatvā
māṁ duḥkhinaṁ kṣaṇam avekṣya dayasva nātha ||8||*

**Word for word:** *śuklāmbaraḥ* — Shuklambara; *dvija* — of the twice-born; *varaḥ* — the best; *subhagaḥ* — fortunate; *atha* — then; *kaścit* — a certain; *ūce* — said; *prabhum* — to the Lord; *prakaṭita* — who had manifested; *atiśaya* — an extraordinary; *prakāśam* — effulgence; *dvārāvatīm* — to Dvaravati; *ca* — and; *mathurām* — to Mathura; *ca* — and; *sadā eva* — constantly; *gatvā* — having gone; *mām* — at me; *duḥkhinam* — wretched; *kṣaṇam* — for a moment; *avekṣya* — having looked; *dayasva* — have mercy; *nātha* — O Lord.

**Translation.** Then a certain fortunate man, Shuklambara, best of the twice-born, said to the Lord, who had manifested an extraordinary effulgence: "I have constantly gone to Dvaravati (Dvaraka) and Mathura — look upon me, wretched as I am, for a moment, and have mercy, O Lord!"

**Note.** Shuklambara Brahmachari is Gaura's neighbour in Navadvipa, who lived by begging alms. The Lord's answer in verse 9 shows that the pilgrim is Shuklambara himself.

### 9

**9.**

```
किं तत्र सन्ति न शृगालचयास्ततः किं
तेषां भवेत् किमथ ते न पुनः शृगालाः ।
इत्युक्तवत्यथ विभौ द्विजपुङ्गवोऽय-
मुच्चैः पपात भुवि दण्डवदुत्सुकात्मा ॥९॥
```
*kiṁ tatra santi na śṛgāla-cayās tataḥ kiṁ
teṣāṁ bhavet kim atha te na punaḥ śṛgālāḥ |
ity uktavaty atha vibhau dvija-puṅgavo 'yam
uccaiḥ papāta bhuvi daṇḍavad utsukātmā ||9||*

**Word for word:** *kim* — whether; *tatra* — there; *santi* — are; *na* — not; *śṛgāla* — of jackals; *cayāḥ* — packs; *tataḥ* — from that; *kim* — what; *teṣām* — for them; *bhavet* — would come; *kim* — whether; *atha* — then; *te* — they; *na* — not; *punaḥ* — still; *śṛgālāḥ* — jackals; *iti* — thus; *uktavati* — having spoken; *atha* — then; *vibhau* — the all-pervading Lord; *dvija* — of the twice-born; *puṅgavaḥ* — the bull (the best); *ayam* — this; *uccaiḥ* — heavily; *papāta* — fell; *bhuvi* — on the ground; *daṇḍavat* — like a stick; *utsuka* — longing; *ātmā* — [with] a soul.

**Translation.** "Are there not packs of jackals there? And what comes to them of that? Are they not jackals still?" When the all-pervading Lord had spoken thus, this bull among the twice-born, his soul full of longing, fell heavily to the ground like a stick.

**Note.** The sense: pilgrimage by itself, without bhakti, does not change one's nature — jackals too live in holy places. *Daṇḍavat*, "like a stick", is a full prostration.

### 10

**10.**

```
भूयश्च भूरिकरुणो निजगाद विप्रं
दीनानुकम्पितहृदयो हृदयैकवेत्ता ।
अद्यैव तेऽत्र भविता प्रभुपादपद्मे
सप्रेमभक्तिरिति गौरसुधामयूखः ॥१०॥
```
*bhūyaś ca bhūri-karuṇo nijagāda vipraṁ
dīnānukampita-hṛdayo hṛdayaika-vettā |
adyaiva te 'tra bhavitā prabhu-pāda-padme
sa-prema-bhaktir iti gaura-sudhā-mayūkhaḥ ||10||*

**Word for word:** *bhūyaḥ* — again; *ca* — and; *bhūri* — abundantly; *karuṇaḥ* — merciful; *nijagāda* — said; *vipram* — to the brahmana; *dīna* — towards the wretched; *anukampita* — compassionate; *hṛdayaḥ* — [whose] heart; *hṛdaya* — of hearts; *eka* — the sole; *vettā* — knower; *adya eva* — this very day; *te* — for you; *atra* — here; *bhavitā* — there will be; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *pāda* — of the feet; *padme* — for the lotus; *sa* — with; *prema* — love; *bhaktiḥ* — devotion; *iti* — thus; *gaura* — Gaura; *sudhā* — nectarean; *mayūkhaḥ* — [the moon] with rays.

**Translation.** And again Gaura — the moon of nectarean rays, abundantly merciful, His heart compassionate to the wretched, the sole knower of hearts — said to the brahmana: "This very day, here, you will have devotion with love for the lotus feet of the Lord."

**Note.** The Lord speaks of Krishna in the third person. *Sudhā-mayūkha*, "nectar-rayed", is an epithet of the moon.

### 11

**11.**

```
सद्योऽथ तस्य चरणेषु निपत्य भूयः
स्विद्यन्मनाः पुलकसञ्चयपूरिताङ्गः ।
उच्चैःस्वरेण बहुलाश्रुभरैर्विभिन्नो
वाग्गद्गदेन च रुरोद महानुभावः ॥११॥
```
*sadyo 'tha tasya caraṇeṣu nipatya bhūyaḥ
svidyan-manāḥ pulaka-sañcaya-pūritāṅgaḥ |
uccaiḥ-svareṇa bahulāśru-bharair vibhinno
vāg-gadgadena ca ruroda mahānubhāvaḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *sadyaḥ* — at once; *atha* — then; *tasya* — His; *caraṇeṣu* — at the feet; *nipatya* — having fallen; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *svidyat* — melting; *manāḥ* — [with] a heart; *pulaka* — of bristling hairs; *sañcaya* — with masses; *pūrita* — covered; *aṅgaḥ* — [with] a body; *uccaiḥ* — with a loud; *svareṇa* — voice; *bahula* — of abundant; *aśru* — of tears; *bharaiḥ* — by floods; *vibhinnaḥ* — shaken; *vāk* — with speech; *gadgadena* — faltering; *ca* — and; *ruroda* — wept; *mahā* — great; *anubhāvaḥ* — soul.

**Translation.** Then the great soul at once fell again at His feet. His heart melting, his body covered with masses of bristling hairs, shaken by floods of abundant tears, he wept aloud, his voice faltering.

### 12

**12.**

```
श्रीमान् गदाधरमहामतिरत्युदार-
शीलः स्वभावमधुरो बहुशान्तमूर्त्तिः ।
ऊचे समीपशयितः प्रभुना रजन्यां
निर्म्माल्यमेतदुरसि प्रतिसार्य्यमेभ्यः ॥१२॥
```
*śrīmān gadādhara-mahāmatir aty-udāra-
śīlaḥ svabhāva-madhuro bahu-śānta-mūrtiḥ |
ūce samīpa-śayitaḥ prabhunā rajanyāṁ
nirmālyam etad urasi pratisāryam ebhyaḥ ||12||*

**Word for word:** *śrīmān* — splendid; *gadādhara* — Gadadhara; *mahāmatiḥ* — great-minded; *ati* — exceedingly; *udāra* — noble; *śīlaḥ* — [of] character; *svabhāva* — by nature; *madhuraḥ* — gentle; *bahu* — deeply; *śānta* — peaceful; *mūrtiḥ* — [whose] form; *ūce* — was told; *samīpa* — nearby; *śayitaḥ* — lying; *prabhunā* — by the Lord; *rajanyām* — at night; *nirmālyam* — the nirmalya; *etat* — this; *urasi* — on the chest; *pratisāryam* — is to be taken off [and given]; *ebhyaḥ* — to these.

**Translation.** The splendid, great-minded Gadadhara — exceedingly noble in character, gentle by nature, his form deeply peaceful — as he lay near [the Lord] at night, was told by the Lord: "This nirmalya on My chest is to be taken off [and given] to these [devotees]."

**Note.** Nirmalya — garlands and ornaments that have already been on the Deity (here, on the Lord); the highest prasada.

---

### 13

**13.**

```
इत्थं स यद्यददात् प्रमदेन यस्मै
यस्मै जनाय तदिदञ्च गदाधरोऽपि ।
प्रातर्ददौ सततमुल्लसिताय तस्मै
तस्मै महाप्रभुविमुक्तमहाप्रसादम् ॥१३॥
```
*itthaṁ sa yad yad adadāt pramadena yasmai
yasmai janāya tad idañ ca gadādharo 'pi |
prātar dadau satatam ullasitāya tasmai
tasmai mahāprabhu-vimukta-mahā-prasādam ||13||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *saḥ* — He; *yat yat* — whatever; *adadāt* — gave; *pramadena* — joyfully; *yasmai yasmai* — to whichever; *janāya* — person; *tat idam* — that very thing; *ca* — and; *gadādharaḥ* — Gadadhara; *api* — too; *prātaḥ* — in the morning; *dadau* — gave; *satatam* — always; *ullasitāya* — rejoicing; *tasmai tasmai* — to that very one; *mahāprabhu* — by Mahaprabhu; *vimukta* — left; *mahā* — great; *prasādam* — prasada.

**Translation.** Thus, whatever He joyfully gave to whichever person, that very thing Gadadhara always gave in the morning to that same person, who rejoiced — the mahaprasada left by Mahaprabhu.

### 14

**14.**

```
संग्रथ्य माल्यनिचयं विरचय्य यत्नात्
सद्गन्धसारघनसारवरादिपङ्कम् ।
अङ्गेषु तस्य परियोजयति स्म नित्यं
सोत्कण्ठमत्र स गदाधरपण्डिताग्र्यः ॥१४॥
```
*saṁgrathya mālya-nicayaṁ viracayya yatnāt
sad-gandhasāra-ghanasāra-varādi-paṅkam |
aṅgeṣu tasya pariyojayati sma nityaṁ
sotkaṇṭham atra sa gadādhara-paṇḍitāgryaḥ ||14||*

**Word for word:** *saṁgrathya* — having strung; *mālya* — of garlands; *nicayam* — a mass; *viracayya* — having prepared; *yatnāt* — carefully; *sat* — of fine; *gandhasāra* — sandal; *ghanasāra* — camphor; *vara* — excellent [substances]; *ādi* — and so on; *paṅkam* — a paste; *aṅgeṣu* — on the limbs; *tasya* — His; *pariyojayati sma* — applied; *nityam* — always; *sa* — with; *utkaṇṭham* — eager longing; *atra* — here; *saḥ* — he; *gadādhara* — Gadadhara; *paṇḍita* — among panditas; *agryaḥ* — the foremost.

**Translation.** Gadadhara, foremost among panditas, having strung a mass of garlands and carefully prepared a paste of fine sandal, camphor and other excellent [substances], always applied them to His limbs with eager longing.

### 15

**15.**

```
सायं कदाचिदथ तैः स्वपदाब्जभक्तैः
श्रीगौरचन्द्र उदिते निजकीर्त्तनाब्धौ ।
आकस्मिकैर्गगनमण्डलमम्बुवाहै-
र्व्याप्तं निरीक्ष्य करुणोऽजनि विघ्नभीत्या ॥१५॥
```
*sāyaṁ kadācid atha taiḥ sva-padābja-bhaktaiḥ
śrī-gauracandra udite nija-kīrtanābdhau |
ākasmikair gagana-maṇḍalam ambu-vāhair
vyāptaṁ nirīkṣya karuṇo 'jani vighna-bhītyā ||15||*

**Word for word:** *sāyam* — in the evening; *kadācit* — once; *atha* — then; *taiḥ* — with those; *sva* — of His own; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotus; *bhaktaiḥ* — with the devotees; *śrī-gauracandraḥ* — Sri Gaurachandra; *udite* — having risen; *nija* — His own; *kīrtana* — of kirtana; *abdhau* — the ocean; *ākasmikaiḥ* — by sudden; *gagana* — of the sky; *maṇḍalam* — the vault; *ambu-vāhaiḥ* — by clouds; *vyāptam* — covered; *nirīkṣya* — having seen; *karuṇaḥ* — sorrowful; *ajani* — became; *vighna* — of an obstacle; *bhītyā* — from fear.

**Translation.** Then once, in the evening, when the ocean of His kirtana had risen with those devotees of His lotus feet, Sri Gaurachandra, seeing the vault of the sky suddenly covered with clouds, became sorrowful, fearing an obstacle.

**Note.** The edition has *उदित*; the metre requires *udite* (locative absolute). The episode of the Lord dispersing the clouds over the kirtana: Murari 2.3.18–27; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.89 (the Chaitanya-bhagavata does not have it).

### 16

**16.**

```
आदाय पाणिकमलेष्वथ मन्दिराग्र्यं
रागान् स्वरांश्च सकलान् स कृतार्थयित्वा ।
उच्चैर्जगौ स्वगुणसञ्चयमेव हृष्टः
श्रीमाननङ्ग इव विग्रहवान् पृथिव्याम् ॥१६॥
```
*ādāya pāṇi-kamaleṣv atha mandirāgryaṁ
rāgān svarāṁś ca sakalān sa kṛtārthayitvā |
uccair jagau sva-guṇa-sañcayam eva hṛṣṭaḥ
śrīmān anaṅga iva vigrahavān pṛthivyām ||16||*

**Word for word:** *ādāya* — having taken; *pāṇi* — of the hands; *kamaleṣu* — in the lotuses; *atha* — then; *mandirā* — mandiras; *agryam* — the finest; *rāgān* — the ragas; *svarān* — the notes; *ca* — and; *sakalān* — all; *saḥ* — He; *kṛtārthayitvā* — having made fulfilled; *uccaiḥ* — aloud; *jagau* — sang; *sva* — His own; *guṇa* — of qualities; *sañcayam* — the multitude; *eva* — indeed; *hṛṣṭaḥ* — joyful; *śrīmān* — beautiful; *anaṅgaḥ* — Ananga; *iva* — like; *vigrahavān* — possessing a body; *pṛthivyām* — on earth.

**Translation.** Then, taking the finest *mandiras* in His lotus hands and making all the ragas and notes fulfilled, He joyfully sang aloud the multitude of His own qualities — beautiful, like Ananga, the bodiless, having taken a body on earth.

**Note.** A *mandira* is a pair of small hand cymbals (*karatāla*). Ananga ("the bodiless") is Kamadeva, the god of love, burnt by Shiva. The edition has *रागात्*; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *rāgān* ("the ragas"), and so we read.

### 17

**17.**

```
सद्यस्तदा जलमुचो मरुता प्रकीर्णा
भेजुर्दिशं दिशममी सह चित्तखेदैः ।
व्योमातिनिर्म्मलमभूदुदियाय चन्द्रः
सार्द्धं समस्तभगणेन तमोऽपहत्यै ॥१७॥
```
*sadyas tadā jala-muco marutā prakīrṇā
bhejur diśaṁ diśam amī saha citta-khedaiḥ |
vyomāti-nirmalam abhūd udiyāya candraḥ
sārdhaṁ samasta-bha-gaṇena tamo-'pahatyai ||17||*

**Word for word:** *sadyaḥ* — at once; *tadā* — then; *jala-mucaḥ* — the clouds; *marutā* — by the wind; *prakīrṇāḥ* — scattered; *bhejuḥ* — went; *diśam diśam* — in every direction; *amī* — these; *saha* — together with; *citta* — of the hearts; *khedaiḥ* — the sorrows; *vyoma* — the sky; *ati* — perfectly; *nirmalam* — clear; *abhūt* — became; *udiyāya* — rose; *candraḥ* — the moon; *sārdham* — together with; *samasta* — the whole; *bha* — of stars; *gaṇena* — host; *tamaḥ* — of darkness; *apahatyai* — for the dispelling.

**Translation.** At once the clouds, scattered by the wind, went off in every direction — and with them the sorrows of [all] hearts. The sky became perfectly clear, and the moon rose with the whole host of stars to dispel the darkness.

### 18

**18.**

```
रज्यन् प्रसारितकरः परिरभ्य गाढं
रम्यां क्षपानववधूं वितमोऽन्तरीयाम् ।
आनन्दसिन्धुलहरीचयमुच्छलन्तं
ज्योत्स्नामिषादिव रमत्ययमोषधीशः ॥१८॥
```
*rajyan prasārita-karaḥ parirabhya gāḍhaṁ
ramyāṁ kṣapā-nava-vadhūṁ vitamo-'ntarīyām |
ānanda-sindhu-laharī-cayam ucchalantaṁ
jyotsnā-miṣād iva ramaty ayam oṣadhīśaḥ ||18||*

**Word for word:** *rajyan* — reddening (falling in love); *prasārita* — outstretched; *karaḥ* — [with] rays (hands); *parirabhya* — having embraced; *gāḍham* — tightly; *ramyām* — the charming; *kṣapā* — the night; *nava* — young; *vadhūm* — bride; *vi* — gone; *tamaḥ* — of darkness; *antarīyām* — [whose] lower garment; *ānanda* — of bliss; *sindhu* — of the ocean; *laharī* — of the waves; *cayam* — a mass; *ucchalantam* — surging; *jyotsnā* — of moonlight; *miṣāt* — under the guise; *iva* — as if; *ramati* — sports; *ayam* — this; *oṣadhīśaḥ* — lord of herbs (the moon).

**Translation.** Reddening with love, his ray-hands outstretched, the moon, lord of herbs, has tightly embraced the charming young bride, Night, whose garment of darkness has slipped away; and he sports, as it were, [pouring out], under the guise of moonlight, a surging mass of the waves of an ocean of bliss.

**Note.** The whole verse is built on double meanings: *rajyan* — "reddening" (of the rising moon) and "falling in love"; *kara* — "ray" and "hand"; *antarīya* — "lower garment", here the darkness. *Oṣadhīśa* is the moon, which nourishes the herbs. The accusative *laharī-cayam ucchalantam* has no verb to govern it; we supply [pouring out].

### 19

**19.**

```
गीर्वाणवर्त्मनि तदा विमले सदृक्षैः
पीयूषमुद्गिरति तत्र सुधामयूखे ।
श्रीगौरशीतकिरणोऽप्यवनौ स्वलोके
सङ्कीर्त्तनामृतरसे रमति स्म भूयः ॥१९॥
```
*gīrvāṇa-vartmani tadā vimale sad-ṛkṣaiḥ
pīyūṣam udgirati tatra sudhā-mayūkhe |
śrī-gaura-śīta-kiraṇo 'py avanau sva-loke
saṅkīrtanāmṛta-rase ramati sma bhūyaḥ ||19||*

**Word for word:** *gīrvāṇa* — of the gods; *vartmani* — on the path (in the sky); *tadā* — then; *vimale* — clear; *sat* — beautiful; *ṛkṣaiḥ* — with the stars; *pīyūṣam* — nectar; *udgirati* — while pouring out; *tatra* — there; *sudhā* — nectarean; *mayūkhe* — [the moon] with rays; *śrī-gaura* — Sri Gaura; *śīta* — cool; *kiraṇaḥ* — [the moon] with rays; *api* — too; *avanau* — on the earth; *sva* — with His own; *loke* — people; *saṅkīrtana* — of sankirtana; *amṛta* — of the nectar; *rase* — in the rasa; *ramati sma* — sported; *bhūyaḥ* — again.

**Translation.** While there, on the clear path of the gods, the nectar-rayed moon with the beautiful stars was pouring out nectar, Sri Gaura, the cool-rayed moon, here on earth with His own people, again delighted in the nectarean rasa of sankirtana.

**Note.** A parallel: the moon with the stars in the sky — the Gaura-moon with the devotees on earth.

### 20

**20.**

```
श्रीमत्पदाब्जपदवीवरहंसकाद्यैः
पाणिप्रवालयुगलं वलयैर्लयैश्च ।
लास्योद्गमे सपदि मन्मथमन्मथस्य
श्रीगौरशीतकिरणस्य रराज भूयः ॥२०॥
```
*śrīmat-padābja-padavī-vara-haṁsakādyaiḥ
pāṇi-pravāla-yugalaṁ valayair layaiś ca |
lāsyodgame sapadi manmatha-manmathasya
śrī-gaura-śīta-kiraṇasya rarāja bhūyaḥ ||20||*

**Word for word:** *śrīmat* — splendid; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotuses; *padavī* — on the path; *vara* — with excellent; *haṁsaka* — anklets; *ādyaiḥ* — and the rest; *pāṇi* — of the hands; *pravāla* — of corals; *yugalam* — the pair; *valayaiḥ* — with bracelets; *layaiḥ* — with rhythms; *ca* — and; *lāsya* — of the dance; *udgame* — at the beginning; *sapadi* — at once; *manmatha* — Manmatha; *manmathasya* — of the bewilderer; *śrī-gaura* — of Sri Gaura; *śīta-kiraṇasya* — of the moon; *rarāja* — shone; *bhūyaḥ* — again.

**Translation.** As soon as the dance of Sri Gaura, the cool-rayed moon, the bewilderer of Manmatha, began, His splendid lotus feet [shone] with excellent anklets and the rest, and the pair of His coral-like hands shone once more with bracelets and with rhythms.

**Note.** *Manmatha-manmatha* — "the bewilderer of [even] the bewilderer of minds", that is, of Kamadeva; an epithet of Krishna (cf. Madana-mohana). *Lāsya* is a graceful dance.

### 21

**21.**

```
विप्राङ्गनागणमुखेन्दुविनिर्गतैस्तै-
रुच्चैरुलूलुनिनदैर्जयनादमिश्रैः ।
खेऽवस्थितस्य दिविषन्निचयस्य हर्ष-
स्वानैरतीव तुमुलः सुमहोत्सवोऽभूत् ॥२१॥
```
*viprāṅganā-gaṇa-mukhendu-vinirgatais tair
uccair ulūlu-ninadair jaya-nāda-miśraiḥ |
khe 'vasthitasya diviṣan-nicayasya harṣa-
svānair atīva tumulaḥ su-mahotsavo 'bhūt ||21||*

**Word for word:** *vipra* — of the brahmanas; *aṅganā* — of the wives; *gaṇa* — of the host; *mukha* — of the faces; *indu* — from the moons; *vinirgataiḥ* — issuing; *taiḥ* — with those; *uccaiḥ* — loud; *ulūlu* — "ulu-ulu"; *ninadaiḥ* — with the cries; *jaya* — of "jaya"; *nāda* — with shouts; *miśraiḥ* — mingled; *khe* — in the sky; *avasthitasya* — of [the host] standing; *diviṣat* — of the gods; *nicayasya* — of the host; *harṣa* — joyful; *svānaiḥ* — with the sounds; *atīva* — exceedingly; *tumulaḥ* — tumultuous; *su* — very; *mahā* — great; *utsavaḥ* — festival; *abhūt* — became.

**Translation.** With those loud cries of "ulu-ulu", mingled with shouts of "Jaya!", that issued from the moonlike faces of the host of brahmanas' wives, and with the joyful sounds of the host of gods standing in the sky, the very great festival became exceedingly tumultuous.

**Note.** *Ulu-dhvani* is the auspicious ululation of Bengali women.

### 22

**22.**

```
कुन्दारविन्दकरवीरनवीनमल्लि-
जात्यादिपुष्पनिवहैरवकॢप्तमाल्यः ।
श्रीखण्डकुङ्कुमलसन्मृगनाभिपङ्कै-
रालिप्य सर्व्वतनुमेष रराज नृत्यन् ॥२२॥
```
*kundāravinda-karavīra-navīna-malli-
jāty-ādi-puṣpa-nivahair avakḷpta-mālyaḥ |
śrīkhaṇḍa-kuṅkuma-lasan-mṛganābhi-paṅkair
ālipya sarva-tanum eṣa rarāja nṛtyan ||22||*

**Word for word:** *kunda* — of kunda jasmine; *aravinda* — of lotus; *karavīra* — of oleander; *navīna* — of fresh; *malli* — mallika; *jāti* — of jati jasmine; *ādi* — and others; *puṣpa* — of flowers; *nivahaiḥ* — with masses; *avakḷpta* — adorned; *mālyaḥ* — [with] garlands; *śrīkhaṇḍa* — of sandal; *kuṅkuma* — of kunkuma; *lasat* — of lustrous; *mṛganābhi* — musk; *paṅkaiḥ* — with pastes; *ālipya* — having anointed; *sarva* — whole; *tanum* — body; *eṣaḥ* — He; *rarāja* — shone; *nṛtyan* — dancing.

**Translation.** Adorned with garlands made of masses of flowers — kunda, lotus, oleander, fresh mallika, jati and others — and having anointed His whole body with pastes of sandal, kunkuma and lustrous musk, He shone as He danced.

**Note.** *Kunda*, *mallikā* and *jāti* are different kinds of jasmine. *Mṛganābhi* ("deer's navel") is musk. The edition has *रवकलृ…*; we read *avakḷpta* with the vowel *ḷ*, as the metre requires.

### 23

**23.**

```
शुक्लाम्बरः स तु निपत्य धरातलान्तः
श्रीगौरचन्द्रमवदत् सभयं महात्मा ।
हे नाथ सम्प्रति कृता भवता नवीन-
द्वीपं नवैव मधुरा विविधैर्विहारैः ॥२३॥
```
*śuklāmbaraḥ sa tu nipatya dharā-talāntaḥ
śrī-gauracandram avadat sa-bhayaṁ mahātmā |
he nātha samprati kṛtā bhavatā navīna-
dvīpaṁ navaiva madhurā vividhair vihāraiḥ ||23||*

**Word for word:** *śuklāmbaraḥ* — Shuklambara; *saḥ* — that; *tu* — and; *nipatya* — having fallen; *dharā* — of the earth; *tala* — the surface; *antaḥ* — on; *śrī-gauracandram* — to Sri Gaurachandra; *avadat* — said; *sa* — with; *bhayam* — awe (fear); *mahātmā* — the great soul; *he nātha* — O Lord; *samprati* — now; *kṛtā* — has been made; *bhavatā* — by You; *navīna* — the New; *dvīpam* — Island; *navā* — a new; *eva* — truly; *madhurā* — Mathura (the sweet one); *vividhaiḥ* — by various; *vihāraiḥ* — pastimes.

**Translation.** But the great-souled Shuklambara fell upon the ground and said in awe to Sri Gaurachandra: "O Lord, by Your various pastimes You have now made the New Island a new Mathura indeed!"

**Note.** A play on words: *navīna-dvīpa*, "the New Island", is Navadvipa; *madhurā* is the Bengali pronunciation of "Mathura" and at the same time means "sweet". The edition has *दीपं*, a misprint for *द्वीपं*. This answers verse 8: Mathura has come to Shuklambara himself.

### 24

**24.**

```
इत्युक्तवान् बहलगद्गदगद्यपद्य-
वाक्येन भूमिमभितो गलदश्रुपूरः ।
वैह्वल्यदैन्यहृदयः सततं विमुक्त-

```
*ity uktavān bahala-gadgada-gadya-padya-
vākyena bhūmim abhito galad-aśru-pūraḥ |
vaihvalya-dainya-hṛdayaḥ satataṁ vimukta-
kaṇṭhaṁ ruroda bahuśaḥ stavanena tasya ||24||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *uktavān* — having spoken; *bahala* — greatly; *gadgada* — faltering; *gadya* — in prose; *padya* — and in verse; *vākyena* — with speech; *bhūmim* — the ground; *abhitaḥ* — all around; *galat* — flowing; *aśru* — of tears; *pūraḥ* — [with] a flood; *vaihvalya* — of agitation; *dainya* — and of humility; *hṛdayaḥ* — [with] a heart [full]; *satatam* — incessantly; *vimukta* — released (full); *kaṇṭham* — throat; *ruroda* — wept; *bahuśaḥ* — much; *stavanena* — with praising; *tasya* — of Him.

**Translation.** Having spoken thus, he praised Him at length in speech greatly faltering, now in prose, now in verse; a flood of tears flowed from him over the ground all around; his heart was full of agitation and humility, and he wept incessantly at the top of his voice.

### 25

**25.**

```
नृत्यन् वयस्यरुचिरांसतटेऽतिपीनं
दोस्तम्भमर्पयति स क्षणमप्युदारम् ।
उद्दामवेपथुचलत्सकलाङ्गयष्टि-
र्भूमौ स्खलत्यनुपदं विवशः क्षणञ्च ॥२५॥
```
*nṛtyan vayasya-rucirāṁsa-taṭe 'ti-pīnaṁ
dos-stambham arpayati sa kṣaṇam apy udāram |
uddāma-vepathu-calat-sakalāṅga-yaṣṭir
bhūmau skhalaty anupadaṁ vivaśaḥ kṣaṇañ ca ||25||*

**Word for word:** *nṛtyan* — dancing; *vayasya* — of a friend; *rucira* — lovely; *aṁsa* — of the shoulder; *taṭe* — on the slope; *ati* — very; *pīnam* — massive; *doḥ* — of the arm; *stambham* — the pillar; *arpayati* — places; *saḥ* — He; *kṣaṇam api* — now for a moment; *udāram* — majestic; *uddāma* — with unrestrained; *vepathu* — trembling; *calat* — shaking; *sakala* — whole; *aṅga* — of the body; *yaṣṭiḥ* — [with] the slender stem; *bhūmau* — on the ground; *skhalati* — stumbles; *anupadam* — at every step; *vivaśaḥ* — helpless; *kṣaṇam* — now for a moment; *ca* — and.

**Translation.** As He dances, now for a moment He lays the massive, majestic pillar of His arm on the slope of a friend's lovely shoulder; now for a moment, His whole slender frame shaking with unrestrained trembling, He helplessly stumbles on the ground at every step.

**26.**

```

भूयो ददाति सदयं सदयैकसिन्धुः ।
नानाविधैरतिकृपारससिन्धुचन्द्रो
```

**Word for word:** *tebhyaḥ* — to them; *varān* — boons; *kṣaṇam api* — now for a moment; *īśvara* — of the Lord; *bhāva* — in the mood; *ramyaḥ* — charming; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *dadāti* — gives; *sa* — with; *dayam* — compassion; *sadaya* — of mercy; *eka* — the sole; *sindhuḥ* — ocean; *nānā* — many; *vidhaiḥ* — of kinds; *ati* — of great; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *rasa* — of the nectar; *sindhu* — of the ocean; *candraḥ* — the moon; *lokān* — the people; *aśikṣayat* — taught; *aśeṣa* — by countless; *vilāsa* — of pastimes; *bhāvaiḥ* — manifestations.

**Translation.** Now for a moment, charming in the mood of the Lord, He — the sole ocean of mercy — again compassionately bestows boons on them. Thus He, the moon of the ocean of the nectar of great mercy, taught the people by countless manifestations of His pastimes, of many kinds.

**Note.** "The moon of the ocean": the moon raises the tide, and Gaura makes the ocean of mercy swell.

**Translation.** Now for a moment Mahaprabhu — lovelier than millions of moons, exceedingly beautiful in His [ecstatic] transformations — climbs onto the shoulder of a devotee of His lotus feet and sports in the night, making the tide of the ocean of His own people's joy surge.

**28.**


**Translation.** On the next day, shining like a thousand rising suns, He sat on the ground and, making all the quarters around ring with echoes through the claps of His two palms, said to the devotees of His lotus feet:

**Note.** *Ahimāṁśu* ("the not-cool-rayed") is the sun. Verses 28–34 tell of the miracle of the mango tree in Srivasa's courtyard; cf. Murari 2.4.6–11; Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 17.79–88.

### 29

**29.**

```
भोः पश्य पश्य भुवि रोपितमाम्रबीजं
```

**Translation.** "Hey, look, look: a mango seed planted in the ground! Look — now here is the mango's sprout! Look — already it is a span high! And look again — quickly a bough has grown on it!

**Note.** A *vitasti*, a span, is about 22 cm.

**30.**

```
शाखा बभूवुरिह पश्य निमेषमात्रात्
```

```
मायाकृतं सकलमेव कुतोऽपि नास्ति ।
शैलूषचेष्टितमिदं वितथं यदेत-
```

śailūṣa-ceṣṭitam idaṁ vitathaṁ yad etat
tat prāpta-vaikṛtam anarthakatāṁ prayāti ||31||*

**Word for word:** *vṛkṣaḥ* — the tree; *ca* — and; *sarva* — all; *viṭapaḥ* — branches; *ca* — and; *phalam* — fruit; *ca* — and; *sarvam* — all; *māyā* — by maya; *kṛtam* — made; *sakalam* — all; *eva* — indeed; *kutaḥ api* — anywhere; *na asti* — is not; *śailūṣa* — of a conjurer; *ceṣṭitam* — a trick; *idam* — this; *vitatham* — false; *yat* — since; *etat* — this; *tat* — that; *prāpta* — having undergone; *vaikṛtam* — change; *anarthakatām* — to meaninglessness; *prayāti* — comes.

**32.**


kautūhalāya purataḥ kurute janaughaḥ |
prāpnoti sad-rasanam ṛktham ati prakāmaṁ

**Word for word:** *etat* — this; *tat* — that; *api* — yet; *amṛtam* — nectar; *eva* — truly; *yat* — if; *īśvarasya* — of the Lord; *kautūhalāya* — for the amusement; *purataḥ* — before [Him]; *kurute* — does; *jana* — of people; *oghaḥ* — a multitude; *prāpnoti* — obtains; *sat* — excellent; *rasanam* — relish; *ṛktham* — wealth; *ati* — very; *prakāmam* — abundantly; *māyā* — by maya; *kṛtena* — from what is made; *ca* — and; *phalam* — fruit; *labhate* — gains; *vicitram* — wondrous.

**Translation.** "Yet that very thing becomes nectar if people do it before the Lord for His amusement: then they obtain in great abundance a wealth of excellent relish, and from what maya has made they gain a wondrous fruit.

**Note.** A play on *phala* — "fruit [of the mango]" and "result". In the Chaitanya-charitamrita the devotees eat these mangoes.

**33.**


**Translation.** "So it is with this whole false universe: if it always serves for service to the Lord, it becomes fully meaningful. The untrue becomes true, and what is impure becomes pure.

### 34

**34.**

```
तस्माज्जनैः सकलमेव परेश्वरस्य
सेवार्थमप्यनृतमेतदिहावचेयम् ।
संसार एष नहि तस्य भवेद्विरोधि
```

sevā-paras tu nahi bādhyata eva kaiścit ||34||*

**Word for word:** *tasmāt* — therefore; *janaiḥ* — by people; *sakalam* — everything; *eva* — indeed; *para* — of the Supreme; *īśvarasya* — Lord; *sevā* — of service; *artham* — for the sake; *api* — though; *anṛtam* — false; *etat* — this; *iha* — here; *avaceyam* — should be gathered; *saṁsāraḥ* — samsara; *eṣaḥ* — this; *nahi* — not; *tasya* — to him; *bhavet* — will be; *virodhi* — an obstacle; *sevā* — to service; *paraḥ* — one devoted; *tu* — for; *nahi* — not; *bādhyate* — is hindered; *eva* — at all; *kaiścit* — by anyone.

**Translation.** "Therefore people should gather everything here, false though it is, for the sake of service to the Supreme Lord. This samsara will be no obstacle to such a one, for one who is devoted to service is hindered by no one at all."

**36.**

**Word for word:** *ramante* — take delight; *yoginaḥ* — yogis; *anante* — in the infinite; *satya* — true; *ānande* — in bliss; *cit* — spiritual; *ātmani* — in the Self; *iti* — therefore; *rāma* — "Rama"; *padena* — by the word; *asau* — this; *param* — supreme; *brahma* — Brahman; *abhidhīyate* — is called.

**37.**

```
भूयोऽपि तं समनुशिष्य जगाद नाथः
```

**38.**


**Word for word:** *yadi* — if; *ātmanaḥ* — your own; *hitam* — good; *avaiṣi* — you understand; *tadā* — then; *parasmāt* — than the other; *tat* — that; *dvaibhujam* — two-armed [form]; *varam* — better; *iti* — thus; *pratikīrtaya* — proclaim; *tvam* — you; *śrutvā* — having heard; *eṣaḥ* — he; *tat* — those; *nigaditam* — words; *karuṇā* — with mercy; *vilāsi* — playing; *bhūmau* — on the ground; *nipatya* — having fallen; *nijagāda* — said; *sa* — with; *harṣa* — joy; *śaṅkam* — and fear.

**Translation.** "If you understand your own good, proclaim: 'That two-armed form is better than the other!'" Hearing those words of His, in which mercy played, [Mukunda] fell on the ground and said with joy and fear:

### 42

**42.**

```

आध्यात्मिकं किमु कृतं नु तवास्ति गीतं
```

**Word for word:** *bhūyaḥ* — then; *jagāda* — said; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *eka* — the sole; *nidhiḥ* — treasure-house; *murārim* — to Murari; *śrī-gauracandraḥ* — Sri Gaurachandra; *idam* — this; *udbhaṭa* — in an intense; *bhāva* — mood; *ramyaḥ* — charming; *ādhyātmikam* — "spiritual"; *kim u* — whether; *kṛtam* — composed; *nu* — then; *tava* — of you; *asti* — there is; *gītam* — a song; *satyam* — the truth; *vada* — tell; *āśu* — quickly; *tat idam* — this; *yadi vā* — whether or not; *kṛtam* — composed; *bhoḥ* — O [Murari] (hey).

**Translation.** Then Sri Gaurachandra, the sole treasure-house of mercy, charming in an intense mood, said this to Murari: "Hey, is there then some 'spiritual' song composed by you? Tell the truth quickly: have you composed one or not?"

**50.**

**Word for word:** *nāmnaḥ* — of the Name; *yaḥ* — which; *eṣaḥ* — this; *mahimā* — greatness; *khalu* — surely; *saḥ* — it; *arthavādaḥ* — mere praise; *ittham* — such; *khalasya* — of the scoundrel; *vacanam* — words; *parikarṇya* — having heard out; *sarvam* — all; *karṇau* — the ears; *pidhāya* — having stopped; *saha* — together with; *tena* — him; *puraḥsareṇa* — going ahead; *gaṅgā* — of the Ganga; *taṭam* — to the bank; *samagamat* — went; *ghṛṇayā* — with revulsion; *mahatyā* — great.

sa premṇi nāmni nitarāṁ bhavati pralīnaḥ ||51||*

**Word for word:** *snātvā* — having bathed; *sa* — with; *celaḥ* — [His] clothes; *udagāt* — came out; *saha* — with; *cela* — of clothes; *vṛndaiḥ* — sets (many); *śuddhaiḥ* — clean; *śuciḥ* — purified; *nija* — to His own; *gṛham* — home; *muditaḥ* — joyful; *jagāma* — went; *yaḥ* — who; *kīrtayati* — recounts; *anudinam* — every day; *yaḥ* — who; *idam* — this; *śṛṇoti* — hears; *saḥ* — he; *premṇi* — in love; *nāmni* — in the Name; *nitarām* — wholly; *bhavati* — becomes; *pralīnaḥ* — absorbed.

**Translation.** Having bathed in His clothes, He came out [of the water] and, purified, in clean clothes, joyfully went to His own home. Whoever recounts this every day, whoever hears it, becomes wholly absorbed in love and in the Name.

**Note.** Bathing in one's clothes (*sacela-snāna*) is purification after contact with defilement — here, after the words of an offender. *Cela-vṛndaiḥ* — literally "with a multitude of clothes" ⟨?⟩. The edition has *प्रेम्नि*, a Bengali spelling. The text is the same in the Gosvami edition (GGM).

**52.**

**53.**

**55.**


**58.**


**63.**

```
अध्यात्मतत्त्वमभि गौरमहाप्रभुः स
```

**Word for word:** *adhyātma* — of the higher Self; *tattvam* — the truth; *abhi* — concerning; *gaura-mahāprabhuḥ* — Gaura Mahaprabhu; *saḥ* — He; *vyākhyām* — an explanation; *cakāra* — gave; *bahu* — very; *durgama* — difficult; *bodham* — to understand; *anyaiḥ* — for others; *ekaḥ* — one; *avaśiṣyate* — remains; *iha* — here; *aviratam* — unceasingly; *saḥ* — that; *ātmā* — Atma; *sṛṣṭau* — in creation; *saḥ* — it; *eva* — the same; *punaḥ* — again; *ekakaḥ* — alone; *eva* — indeed; *bhāti* — shines.

The translation of the philosophical lines of verses 63–67 is provisional.

### 64

**64.**

```
इत्थं प्रसार्य्य स्वकरौ करुणासमुद्रो

```
*itthaṁ prasārya sva-karau karuṇā-samudro
muṣṭī-cakāra ca punar drutam eva nṛtyan |
sac-cit-svarūpam atha tattva-nirūpaṇaṁ tad

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *prasārya* — having opened; *sva* — His own; *karau* — hands; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *samudraḥ* — the ocean; *muṣṭī-cakāra* — clenched into fists; *ca* — and; *punaḥ* — again; *drutam* — quickly; *eva* — indeed; *nṛtyan* — dancing; *sat* — of being; *cit* — of consciousness; *svarūpam* — [having] the nature; *atha* — then; *tattva* — of the truth; *nirūpaṇam* — the exposition; *tat* — that; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *jagāda* — set forth; *jagat* — of the world; *eka* — the only; *gatiḥ* — refuge; *prakāmam* — at length.

**Translation.** Thus [speaking], the ocean of mercy, dancing, opened His hands and at once clenched them again into fists. Then He, the only refuge of the world, again set forth at length that exposition of the truth whose nature is being and consciousness.

**Note.** The gesture apparently depicts the unfolding and folding up of the world (creation and dissolution) ⟨?⟩. The edition has *जयदेकगतिः*; we read *jagad-eka-gatiḥ*. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**Translation.** Then He quickly raised Advaita, took Him by the hand and said: "Surely it is for your sake that I have come here." Having spoken thus, the ever-merciful [Lord] sat down together with Him on a couch and thereupon shone openly.

**Note.** The edition has *तन्तु* = *taṁ tu*. "I have come for your sake": Advaita called Gaura down to earth by His prayers (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 3).

**80.**

**Word for word:** *na* — not; *sādhayati* — wins over; *mām* — Me; *yogaḥ* — yoga; *na* — not; *sāṅkhyam* — Sankhya; *dharmaḥ* — dharma; *uddhava* — O Uddhava; *na* — not; *svādhyāyaḥ* — study of the Vedas; *tapaḥ* — austerity; *tyāgaḥ* — renunciation; *yathā* — as; *bhaktiḥ* — devotion; *mama* — to Me; *ūrjitā* — grown strong.

**Translation.** "Neither yoga nor Sankhya nor dharma, O Uddhava, neither study of the Vedas nor austerity nor renunciation wins Me over as does devotion to Me that has grown strong."

**Note.** The metre is *anuṣṭubh*. Srimad-Bhagavatam 11.14.20. Unnumbered in the edition.

**115.**

```
इत्यूचिवान् सह निजाङ्घ्रिसरोजभक्तैः
सङ्कीर्त्तनं समकरोन्नटनञ्च भूयः ।
तत्रावधूतपदधूलिभिरात्मलोक-
शीर्षं चकार परिपूततमं परं सः ॥११५॥
```
*ity ūcivān saha nijāṅghri-saroja-bhaktaiḥ
saṅkīrtanaṁ samakaron naṭanañ ca bhūyaḥ |
tatrāvadhūta-pada-dhūlibhir ātma-loka-
śīrṣaṁ cakāra paripūtatamaṁ paraṁ saḥ ||115||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *ūcivān* — having spoken; *saha* — together with; *nija* — His own; *aṅghri* — feet; *saroja* — lotus; *bhaktaiḥ* — with the devotees; *saṅkīrtanam* — sankirtana; *samakarot* — performed; *naṭanam* — dancing; *ca* — and; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *tatra* — there; *avadhūta* — of the avadhuta; *pada* — feet; *dhūlibhiḥ* — with the dust; *ātma* — of His own; *loka* — people; *śīrṣam* — the heads; *cakāra* — made; *paripūtatamam* — most purified; *param* — utterly; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** Having spoken thus, He again performed sankirtana and dancing together with the devotees of His lotus feet; and there, with the dust of the avadhuta's feet, He made the heads of His own people utterly, supremely pure.

### 116

**116.**

```
इत्थं व्रजन् पथि शचीतनयः स तैस्तै-
स्तस्यावधूतपरमस्य कथां जगाद ।
ज्ञानं पुरो भवति भक्तिरथो विरक्ति-
रित्थं वदत्ययमतः परमोऽयमेव ॥११६॥
```
*itthaṁ vrajan pathi śacī-tanayaḥ sa tais tais
tasyāvadhūta-paramasya kathāṁ jagāda |
jñānaṁ puro bhavati bhaktir atho viraktir
itthaṁ vadaty ayam ataḥ paramo 'yam eva ||116||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *vrajan* — going; *pathi* — along the road; *śacī* — of Shachi; *tanayaḥ* — the son; *saḥ* — He; *taiḥ taiḥ* — with those [devotees]; *tasya* — about that; *avadhūta* — avadhuta; *paramasya* — supreme; *kathām* — a talk; *jagāda* — spoke; *jñānam* — knowledge; *puraḥ* — first; *bhavati* — is; *bhaktiḥ* — devotion; *atho* — then; *viraktiḥ* — detachment; *ittham* — thus; *vadati* — declares; *ayam* — He; *ataḥ* — therefore; *paramaḥ* — supreme; *ayam* — He; *eva* — indeed.

**Translation.** Going thus along the road with those [devotees], the son of Shachi spoke about that supreme avadhuta: "First comes knowledge, then devotion, then detachment — so He [Himself, by His life,] declares. Therefore He indeed is supreme."

**Note.** The sense of the second half is unclear. Cf. Srimad-Bhagavatam 11.2.42: *bhaktiḥ pareśānubhavo viraktiḥ*. In the edition this verse is mistakenly numbered "117" (the next one is also numbered 117). The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 117

**117.**

```
इत्थं विचिन्त्य करुणाब्धिरथापरेद्यु-
र्भिक्षार्थमस्य नियतं निरतो बभूव ।
सद्भोजितं तदनु चन्दनकुङ्कुमाद्यैः
प्रत्यङ्गमेवमनुलिप्य ननन्द नाथः ॥११७॥
```
*itthaṁ vicintya karuṇābdhir athāparedyur
bhikṣārtham asya niyataṁ nirato babhūva |
sad-bhojitaṁ tad-anu candana-kuṅkumādyaiḥ
praty-aṅgam evam anulipya nananda nāthaḥ ||117||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *vicintya* — having reflected; *karuṇā* — of compassion; *abdhiḥ* — the ocean; *atha* — then; *aparedyuḥ* — on the next day; *bhikṣā* — of a meal; *artham* — for the sake; *asya* — His; *niyatam* — diligently; *nirataḥ* — engaged; *babhūva* — was; *sat* — well; *bhojitam* — fed; *tat* — that; *anu* — after; *candana* — with sandal; *kuṅkuma* — with kunkuma; *ādyaiḥ* — and the rest; *prati* — each; *aṅgam* — limb; *evam* — thus; *anulipya* — having anointed; *nananda* — rejoiced; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord.

**Translation.** Having reflected thus, the ocean of compassion on the next day diligently busied Himself with a meal for Him [Nityananda]. When He had been well fed, the Lord then anointed each of His limbs with sandal, kunkuma and the rest, and rejoiced.

**Note.** *Bhikṣā* — alms; a meal offered to a sannyasi or an avadhuta.

### 118

**118.**

```
अन्येद्युरेष भगवानवधूतवेशः
श्रीवासगेहमगमत् क्षुधितः प्रकामम् ।
आमन्त्र्य सोऽनुमुमुदे धरणीसुराग्र्यो
भिक्षां ददौ तदनु चन्दनकैर्लिलेप ॥११८॥
```
*anyedyur eṣa bhagavān avadhūta-veśaḥ
śrīvāsa-geham agamat kṣudhitaḥ prakāmam |
āmantrya so 'numumude dharaṇī-surāgryo
bhikṣāṁ dadau tad-anu candanakair lilepa ||118||*

**Word for word:** *anyedyuḥ* — on another day; *eṣaḥ* — this; *bhagavān* — divine one; *avadhūta* — of an avadhuta; *veśaḥ* — in the attire; *śrīvāsa* — of Srivasa; *geham* — to the house; *agamat* — came; *kṣudhitaḥ* — hungry; *prakāmam* — very; *āmantrya* — having invited; *saḥ* — he; *anumumude* — rejoiced; *dharaṇī-sura* — of the gods on earth (brahmanas); *agryaḥ* — the foremost; *bhikṣām* — a meal; *dadau* — gave; *tat* — that; *anu* — after; *candanakaiḥ* — with sandal; *lilepa* — anointed.

**Translation.** On another day this divine one, in the attire of an avadhuta, came to the house of Srivasa, very hungry. The foremost of brahmanas [Srivasa] rejoiced, invited Him, gave Him a meal, and afterwards anointed Him with sandal.

**Note.** *Bhagavān* here refers to Nityananda.

### 119

**119.**

```
विश्राममत्र स चकार तथैव भुक्त्वा
तत्रैव सोऽपि करुणानिधिरुद्गतोऽभूत् ।
आगत्य देवनिलये वरकम्बलेन
रम्यं वरासनमुपेत्य रराज नाथः ॥११९॥
```
*viśrāmam atra sa cakāra tathaiva bhuktvā
tatraiva so 'pi karuṇā-nidhir udgato 'bhūt |
āgatya deva-nilaye vara-kambalena
ramyaṁ varāsanam upetya rarāja nāthaḥ ||119||*

**Word for word:** *viśrāmam* — rest; *atra* — here; *saḥ* — He; *cakāra* — took; *tathā eva* — likewise; *bhuktvā* — having eaten; *tatra eva* — there too; *saḥ api* — He also; *karuṇā* — of compassion; *nidhiḥ* — the treasury; *udgataḥ abhūt* — appeared; *āgatya* — having come; *deva* — of God; *nilaye* — into the abode; *vara* — with a fine; *kambalena* — blanket; *ramyam* — made charming; *vara* — excellent; *āsanam* — a seat; *upetya* — having taken; *rarāja* — shone; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord.

**Translation.** Having eaten, He [Nityananda] rested there. And there too appeared the treasury of compassion: coming into the shrine, the Lord took an excellent seat, made charming by a fine blanket, and shone.

### 120

**120.**

```
ऊचेऽवधूतमथ गौरसुधाकरोऽसौ
मां पश्य पश्य कृतवानसि यच्छ्रमं त्वम् ।
इत्युक्त एष नहि किञ्चन तस्य देहे
प्रैक्षिष्ट नैव तदबुद्ध महानुभावः ॥१२०॥
```
*ūce 'vadhūtam atha gaura-sudhākaro 'sau
māṁ paśya paśya kṛtavān asi yac chramaṁ tvam |
ity ukta eṣa nahi kiñcana tasya dehe
praikṣiṣṭa naiva tad abuddha mahānubhāvaḥ ||120||*

**Word for word:** *ūce* — said; *avadhūtam* — to the avadhuta; *atha* — then; *gaura* — Gaura; *sudhākaraḥ* — the moon; *asau* — this; *mām* — at Me; *paśya paśya* — look, look; *kṛtavān asi* — You have taken; *yat* — since; *śramam* — trouble; *tvam* — You; *iti* — thus; *uktaḥ* — addressed; *eṣaḥ* — He; *nahi* — not; *kiñcana* — anything; *tasya* — His; *dehe* — in the body; *praikṣiṣṭa* — saw; *na eva* — not at all; *tat* — that; *abuddha* — understood; *mahānubhāvaḥ* — the great soul.

**Translation.** Then that moon, Gaura, said to the avadhuta: "Look, look at Me, since You have taken the trouble [to come]!" Thus addressed, He saw nothing [special] in His [the Lord's] body: the great soul did not understand it at all.

**Note.** The division of *naiva tad abuddha* is unclear. We take *na eva* with *abuddha* and the great soul (*mahānubhāvaḥ*) as Nityananda: "did not understand it at all"; then *jñātvā* in verse 121 refers to the Lord, who "understood this". One may also take *mahānubhāvaḥ* as the Lord, who "understood this" already here. The verse prepares verses 121–122.

### 121

**121.**

```
ज्ञात्वा स इत्थमतिकारुणिकस्ततस्ता-
नूचे बहिर्व्रजत शीघ्रमितो भवन्तः ।
गच्छत्सु तेषु स च तत्र ददर्श तस्य
देहे दिनेशशतकोटिमहो महीयः ॥१२१॥
```
*jñātvā sa ittham ati-kāruṇikas tatas tān
ūce bahir vrajata śīghram ito bhavantaḥ |
gacchatsu teṣu sa ca tatra dadarśa tasya
dehe dineśa-śata-koṭi-maho mahīyaḥ ||121||*

**Word for word:** *jñātvā* — having understood; *saḥ* — He; *ittham* — thus; *ati* — very; *kāruṇikaḥ* — compassionate; *tataḥ* — then; *tān* — to them; *ūce* — said; *bahiḥ* — outside; *vrajata* — go; *śīghram* — quickly; *itaḥ* — from here; *bhavantaḥ* — you; *gacchatsu teṣu* — when they had gone; *saḥ* — He; *ca* — and; *tatra* — then; *dadarśa* — saw; *tasya* — His; *dehe* — in the body; *dina-īśa* — of suns; *śata* — of hundreds; *koṭi* — of crores; *mahaḥ* — a radiance; *mahīyaḥ* — most mighty.

**Translation.** Understanding this, the most compassionate [Lord] then said to those [present]: "Go outside quickly, all of you." And when they had gone, He [Nityananda] then saw in His [the Lord's] body a most mighty radiance, [like that] of hundreds of crores of suns.

### 122

**122.**

```
पुरः षड्भिर्दोर्भिः परमरुचिरं तत्र च पुन-
श्चतुर्णां बाहूनां परमललितत्वेन मधुरम् ।
तदीयं तद्रूपं सपदि परिलोच्याशु सहसा
तदाश्चर्य्यं भूयो द्विभुजमथ भूयोऽप्यकलयत् ॥१२२॥
```
*puraḥ ṣaḍbhir dorbhiḥ parama-ruciraṁ tatra ca punaś
caturṇāṁ bāhūnāṁ parama-lalitatvena madhuram |
tadīyaṁ tad-rūpaṁ sapadi parilocyāśu sahasā
tad āścaryaṁ bhūyo dvi-bhujam atha bhūyo 'py akalayat ||122||*

**Word for word:** *puraḥ* — first; *ṣaḍbhiḥ* — with six; *dorbhiḥ* — arms; *parama* — supremely; *ruciram* — beautiful; *tatra* — there; *ca* — and; *punaḥ* — then; *caturṇām* — of four; *bāhūnām* — arms; *parama* — with the supreme; *lalitatvena* — grace; *madhuram* — sweet; *tadīyam* — His; *tat* — that; *rūpam* — form; *sapadi* — at once; *parilocya* — having beheld; *āśu* — swiftly; *sahasā* — suddenly; *tat* — that; *āścaryam* — wondrous; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *dvi* — two-; *bhujam* — armed [form]; *atha* — then; *bhūyaḥ api* — again and again; *akalayat* — contemplated.

**Translation.** Having at once beheld that form of His — first supremely beautiful with six arms, and then sweet with the supreme grace of four arms — He swiftly, suddenly [beheld] again that wondrous two-armed [form], and then contemplated it again and again.

**Note.** The metre is *śikhariṇī* (17 syllables). The revelation of the *ṣaḍ-bhuja* (the six-armed form) to Nityananda — Chaitanya-bhagavata, Madhya 5.92–94: in the hands are the conch, disc, mace, lotus, plough and pestle (the form of Vishnu and Balarama); Nityananda loses consciousness. The iconography of "the arms of Rama, Krishna and Gaura" is not given by the Chaitanya-bhagavata here. In Karnapura the six-armed form is followed by the four-armed and the two-armed forms.

### 123

**123.**

```
विलोक्येत्थं तत्तत् परमरमणीयं सुमधुरं
कृपासिन्धो रूपामृतमिदममन्दं प्रमुदितः ।
जहासोच्चैर्नृत्यन्नतिशयसुखास्फालनपरो
भृशं नित्यानन्दः सुखजलधिसंप्लाविततनुः ॥१२३॥
```
*vilokyetthaṁ tat tat parama-ramaṇīyaṁ su-madhuraṁ
kṛpā-sindho rūpāmṛtam idam amandaṁ pramuditaḥ |
jahāsoccair nṛtyann atiśaya-sukhāsphālana-paro
bhṛśaṁ nityānandaḥ sukha-jaladhi-saṁplāvita-tanuḥ ||123||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having beheld; *ittham* — thus; *tat tat* — all this; *parama* — supremely; *ramaṇīyam* — enchanting; *su* — very; *madhuram* — sweet; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *sindhoḥ* — of the ocean; *rūpa* — of the form; *amṛtam* — nectar; *idam* — this; *amandam* — immensely; *pramuditaḥ* — delighted; *jahāsa* — laughed; *uccaiḥ* — loudly; *nṛtyan* — dancing; *atiśaya* — from excess; *sukha* — of happiness; *āsphālana* — to clapping [His hands]; *paraḥ* — given; *bhṛśam* — wildly; *nityānandaḥ* — Nityananda; *sukha* — of happiness; *jaladhi* — by the ocean; *saṁplāvita* — flooded; *tanuḥ* — [with] a body.

**Translation.** Having thus beheld all this — this supremely enchanting, very sweet nectar of the form of the ocean of mercy — Nityananda, immensely delighted, His body flooded by an ocean of happiness, danced wildly, given to clapping His hands from excess of happiness, and laughed loudly.

**Note.** The metre is *śikhariṇī*. The edition has *संप्लावितनुः*; we read *saṁplāvita-tanuḥ*.

```
इति श्रीकृष्णचैतन्यचरितामृते महाकाव्ये भक्तसम्मेलनं नाम षष्ठः सर्गः ॥
```
