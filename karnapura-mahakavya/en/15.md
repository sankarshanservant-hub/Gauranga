# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Fifteen — verses 1–110 (printed pp. 456–491)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:**
- 1–104 — *puṣpitāgrā* (odd padas ⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑—⏑—⏑——, 12 syllables; even padas ⏑⏑⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—⏑——, 13 syllables).
- 105 — *sragdharā* (————⏑——⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑——⏑——⏑——; 21 syllables per pada).
- 106 and 108 — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* (———⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑———⏑——⏑—; 19 syllables per pada); 106 is a verse of six padas.
- 107 — *pṛthvī* (⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑—⏑——⏑—; 17 syllables per pada).
- 109 — *hariṇī* (⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑—————⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—; 17 syllables per pada), with rhyme at the ends of the padas.
- 110 — *mālinī* (⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑⏑———⏑——⏑——; 15 syllables per pada).

**Total verses:** 110.

**Word-for-word conventions:** compound words are divided into their members, each with its own meaning; case is rendered by an English preposition ("of", "with", "in", "by", "to", "from"); the vocative is given with "O". Square brackets enclose a word that is understood (for example, [whose], [like]).

**Contents of the passage:** the anavasara; the cleansing of the Gundicha temple; the Netrotsava; Jagannatha mounts the chariot.
- 1–11 — the anavasara: Jagannatha remains hidden with Lakshmi; the Lord's longing (the separation of the gopis); His consolation — the Lord of the gopis on the swing, with the dancing of the devadasis (9–10, *yugmaka*).
- 12–23 — the preparations: the brooms, the bath before dawn, the crimson cloth; the Lord gives out a hundred brooms; the run to the Gundicha, like an elephant broken loose.
- 24–34 — the sweeping: the devotees forget themselves; "you — here, you — there"; blows with brooms for latecomers; the Lord cleans the throne and is covered with dust; "Krishna, Krishna, Krishna".
- 35–54 — the washing: water from the well passed along a chain, "a game of ball with pitchers"; the Lord washes the veranda; the drenched crimson cloth ("Meru in the evening clouds").
- 55–65 — gathering blades of grass and pebbles in the courtyard, a contest of who gathers more (57–58); kirtana and dancing.
- 66–70 — bathing in the tank; obeisance to Nrisimha.
- 71–77 — **the Netrotsava**: the first darshan after the anavasara; the Lord and Jagannatha are "alike": neither of them blinks; at the bhoga-mandapa.
- 78–90 — Jagannatha with Lakshmi; the journey to the Gundicha as "a journey to Vrindavana"; the descent from the throne over cushions.
- 91–99 — the procession: the "game of ball" of Jagannatha and Gaura; Prataparudra sweeps with a golden broom (95–96); Jagannatha mounts the chariot.
- 100–110 — the Lord bathes Advaita, Srivasa and the singers and anoints them with sandal paste (the list, 105–106); the setting out; He falls prostrate before the chariot of Baladeva.

**Textual cruxes:** 13 (*śaci-*), 18 (*raho 'ti*), 21 (*-gamya-*), 26 (a pada three syllables short), 36 and 41 (11 syllables in the first pada), 37, 46, 51 (*-'bhitimyat*), 57 (*naikati*), 68, 76, 81, 86 (an omitted verb), 87 (*kṣitikṣit*), 90, 91, 97 (*marātapa*), 108, 109 — see the notes. Other metrical irregularities of the printed text are noted at 14, 15, 30 and 36.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
असितगिरिपतिस्ततोऽयमन्तः-
पुरपरिचारिकया श्रिया समेतः ।
अनवसरमुपेत्य गूढवेशो
वसति जनस्य विलोचनातिदूरः ॥१॥
```
*asita-giri-patis tato 'yam antaḥ-
pura-paricārikayā śriyā sametaḥ |
anavasaram upetya gūḍha-veśo
vasati janasya vilocanāti-dūraḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *asita* — of the Dark; *giri* — Mountain; *patiḥ* — the Lord; *tataḥ* — then; *ayam* — this; *antaḥ-pura* — of the inner apartments; *paricārikayā* — with the attendant; *śriyā* — with Lakshmi; *sametaḥ* — united; *anavasaram* — the anavasara; *upetya* — having reached; *gūḍha* — hidden; *veśaḥ* — [whose] guise [is]; *vasati* — dwells; *janasya* — of the people; *vilocana* — from the eyes; *ati-dūraḥ* — far away.

**Translation.** Then, when the anavasara has come, this Lord of the Dark Mountain, together with Lakshmi, the attendant of the inner apartments, dwells in hidden guise, far away from the eyes of the people.

**Note.** The *anavasara* is the fortnight after the Snana-yatra during which Jagannatha is not shown to the people. The Lord of the Dark Mountain is Jagannatha (the Dark Mountain is Nilachala).

### 2

**2.**

```
असितगिरिनिवासिभक्तलोका-
नतिशयितार्तिपरान् विधातुकामः ।
स निभृतमथवा श्रिया विहर्तुं
रहसि निलीय रराज देव एषः ॥२॥
```
*asita-giri-nivāsi-bhakta-lokān
atiśayitārti-parān vidhātu-kāmaḥ |
sa nibhṛtam athavā śriyā vihartuṁ
rahasi nilīya rarāja deva eṣaḥ ||2||*

**Word for word:** *asita-giri* — on the Dark Mountain; *nivāsi* — dwelling; *bhakta* — devoted; *lokān* — people; *atiśayita* — to boundless; *ārti* — anguish; *parān* — given over; *vidhātu* — to make; *kāmaḥ* — desiring; *saḥ* — He; *nibhṛtam* — in seclusion; *athavā* — or else; *śriyā* — with Lakshmi; *vihartum* — to sport; *rahasi* — in secret; *nilīya* — having hidden; *rarāja* — shone; *devaḥ* — God; *eṣaḥ* — this.

**Translation.** Whether desiring to give the devoted people who dwell on the Dark Mountain over to boundless anguish, or else to sport with Lakshmi in seclusion, this God hid Himself and shone in secret.

### 3

**3.**

```
अथ तदनवलोकनातिदुःख-
क्षुभिततमानि मनांसि बिभ्रतस्ते ।
असितगिरिनिवासिनो महान्तो
भृशमतपन् प्रभुदर्शनेन हीनाः ॥३॥
```
*atha tad-anavalokanāti-duḥkha-
kṣubhitatamāni manāṁsi bibhratas te |
asita-giri-nivāsino mahānto
bhṛśam atapan prabhu-darśanena hīnāḥ ||3||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *tat* — of Him; *anavalokana* — from not seeing; *ati-duḥkha* — by great sorrow; *kṣubhitatamāni* — utterly agitated; *manāṁsi* — hearts; *bibhrataḥ* — bearing; *te* — they; *asita-giri* — on the Dark Mountain; *nivāsinaḥ* — dwelling; *mahāntaḥ* — great souls; *bhṛśam* — intensely; *atapan* — were tormented; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *darśanena* — of the sight; *hīnāḥ* — deprived.

**Translation.** Then those great souls who dwelt on the Dark Mountain, bearing hearts utterly agitated by the great sorrow of not seeing Him, were intensely tormented, deprived of the sight of the Lord.

### 4

**4.**

```
प्रभुरपि स शचीसुतोऽथ दुःखी
भृशमभवद्विकलो न तं विलोक्य ।
प्रकटयति च तच्छलेन वृन्दा-
वनरमणीजनविप्रयोगदुःखम् ॥४॥
```
*prabhur api sa śacī-suto 'tha duḥkhī
bhṛśam abhavad vikalo na taṁ vilokya |
prakaṭayati ca tac-chalena vṛndā-
vana-ramaṇī-jana-viprayoga-duḥkham ||4||*

**Word for word:** *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *api* — also; *saḥ* — He; *śacī-sutaḥ* — the son of Shachi; *atha* — then; *duḥkhī* — sorrowful; *bhṛśam* — greatly; *abhavat* — became; *vikalaḥ* — distraught; *na* — not; *tam* — Him; *vilokya* — seeing; *prakaṭayati* — reveals; *ca* — and; *tat* — of this; *chalena* — under the pretext; *vṛndāvana* — of Vrindavana; *ramaṇī* — of the beautiful women; *jana* — of the folk; *viprayoga* — of separation; *duḥkham* — the sorrow.

**Translation.** And the Lord, the son of Shachi, not seeing Him, then became sorrowful and greatly distraught. And under this pretext He reveals the sorrow of separation of the beautiful women of Vrindavana.

### 5

**5.**

```
निरवधि हृदयस्थितानि वृन्दा-
वनरमणीविरहस्य दुःखितानि ।
अनुभवति स तच्छलेन लब्धा-
वसरमुदेति हि चेतसो विकारः ॥५॥
```
*niravadhi hṛdaya-sthitāni vṛndā-
vana-ramaṇī-virahasya duḥkhitāni |
anubhavati sa tac-chalena labdhā-
vasaram udeti hi cetaso vikāraḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *niravadhi* — unceasingly; *hṛdaya* — in the heart; *sthitāni* — abiding; *vṛndāvana* — of Vrindavana; *ramaṇī* — of the beautiful women; *virahasya* — of separation; *duḥkhitāni* — the pains; *anubhavati* — experiences; *saḥ* — He; *tat* — of this; *chalena* — under the pretext; *labdha* — having found; *avasaram* — an opportunity; *udeti* — rises; *hi* — for; *cetasaḥ* — of the heart; *vikāraḥ* — the agitation.

**Translation.** Under this pretext He experiences the pains of separation of the beautiful women of Vrindavana, which abide unceasingly in His heart; for the agitation of the heart rises up as soon as it has found an opportunity.

### 6

**6.**

```
निरवधिगलदश्रुणोऽवतारै-
रुरसि सुसंभृतहारविभ्रमाढ्यः ।
कृशिमभिरवशिष्टशिष्टनामा
चिरविरहाद्विषसाद गौरचन्द्रः ॥६॥
```
*niravadhi-galad-aśruṇo 'vatārair
urasi susaṁbhṛta-hāra-vibhramāḍhyaḥ |
kṛśimabhir avaśiṣṭa-śiṣṭa-nāmā
cira-virahād viṣasāda gauracandraḥ ||6||*

**Word for word:** *niravadhi* — unceasingly; *galat* — flowing; *aśruṇaḥ* — of tears; *avatāraiḥ* — by the descents; *urasi* — on the chest; *su-saṁbhṛta* — well formed; *hāra* — of a necklace; *vibhrama* — with the charm; *āḍhyaḥ* — rich; *kṛśimabhiḥ* — through emaciation; *avaśiṣṭa* — remaining; *śiṣṭa* — only; *nāmā* — [whose] name [was]; *cira* — long; *virahāt* — from separation; *viṣasāda* — languished; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra.

**Translation.** Rich with the charm of a necklace well formed on His chest by the streams of tears flowing unceasingly, so wasted that only His name was left, Gaurachandra languished from long separation.

### 7

**7.**

```
विकिरति बहुदीर्घमुष्णमुच्चैः
श्वसितसमीरणमम्बु लोचनाभ्याम् ।
सदरुणकमलद्वयारुणाभ्यां
कृशतनुरन्वहमेवमेव भूतः ॥७॥
```
*vikirati bahu-dīrgham uṣṇam uccaiḥ
śvasita-samīraṇam ambu locanābhyām |
sad-aruṇa-kamala-dvayāruṇābhyāṁ
kṛśa-tanur anvaham evam eva bhūtaḥ ||7||*

**Word for word:** *vikirati* — sends forth; *bahu-dīrgham* — very long; *uṣṇam* — hot; *uccaiḥ* — heavily; *śvasita* — of sighs; *samīraṇam* — the wind; *ambu* — water; *locanābhyām* — from the eyes; *sat-aruṇa* — beautiful red; *kamala* — of lotuses; *dvaya* — like a pair; *aruṇābhyām* — from red; *kṛśa* — emaciated; *tanuḥ* — [whose] body [is]; *anvaham* — day after day; *evam eva* — just so; *bhūtaḥ* — having become.

**Translation.** Heavily He sends forth the very long, hot wind of sighs, and water from His eyes, red as a pair of beautiful red lotuses. Thus, day after day, His body grew thin.

### 8

**8.**

```
असितगिरिपतेरदर्शनेन
द्विगुणितदुःखदवानलः कृपाब्धिः ।
कियदिव स जगाम तत्र गोपी-
पतिविजयं परिलोच्य चित्तधैर्यम् ॥८॥
```
*asita-giri-pater adarśanena
dvi-guṇita-duḥkha-davānalaḥ kṛpābdhiḥ |
kiyad iva sa jagāma tatra gopī-
pati-vijayaṁ parilocya citta-dhairyam ||8||*

**Word for word:** *asita-giri* — of the Dark Mountain; *pateḥ* — of the Lord; *adarśanena* — by not seeing; *dvi-guṇita* — doubled; *duḥkha* — of sorrow; *dava-analaḥ* — [whose] forest fire [was]; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *abdhiḥ* — the ocean; *kiyat iva* — somewhat; *saḥ* — He; *jagāma* — attained; *tatra* — there; *gopī-pati* — of the Lord of the gopis; *vijayam* — the procession; *parilocya* — beholding; *citta* — of heart; *dhairyam* — steadiness.

**Translation.** The ocean of mercy, whose forest fire of sorrow was doubled by not seeing the Lord of the Dark Mountain, attained some steadiness of heart on beholding there the procession of the Lord of the gopis.

**Note.** Evidently during the anavasara the Lord watched the festival of Jagannatha's representative — Krishna with the flute on the swing (verses 9–10). In the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Madhya 11.62–63) the Lord goes to Alalanatha during the anavasara; such a festival is not mentioned in our sources. *Vijaya* can also mean "triumph".

### 9

**9.**

```
सुललितमुरलीकरः स दोला-
मति मधुरामधिरुह्य राजमानः ।
निरवधि वरवारनागरीणां
नटनकलाकुतुकी त्रिसन्ध्यमेव ॥९॥
```
*sulalita-muralī-karaḥ sa dolām
ati madhurām adhiruhya rājamānaḥ |
niravadhi vara-vāra-nāgarīṇāṁ
naṭana-kalā-kutukī tri-sandhyam eva ||9||*

**Word for word:** *su-lalita* — very graceful; *muralī* — a flute; *karaḥ* — [in whose] hand [is]; *saḥ* — He; *dolām* — a swing; *ati* — very; *madhurām* — charming; *adhiruhya* — having mounted; *rājamānaḥ* — shining; *niravadhi* — unceasingly; *vara* — of the finest; *vāra-nāgarīṇām* — of the temple dancers; *naṭana* — of dance; *kalā* — in the art; *kutukī* — delighting; *tri-sandhyam* — at the three junctures of the day; *eva* — indeed.

**Translation.** With a very graceful flute in His hand, mounted on a most charming swing, He shines, and at the three junctures of the day He delights unceasingly in the art of dance of the finest temple dancers…

**Note.** Verses 9–10 form a pair (*yugmaka*). The dancers are the devadasis of Jagannatha's temple.

### 10

**10.**

```
विलसति पटहप्रकृष्टभेरी-
मधुरमृदङ्गविभङ्गरम्यगीतैः ।
निरवधि सुमनःसमूहवृष्ट्या
```

madhura-mṛdaṅga-vibhaṅga-ramya-gītaiḥ |
niravadhi sumanaḥ-samūha-vṛṣṭyā
guru-dhavalī-kṛta-veśma-madhya-bhūmau ||10|| (yugmakam)*

**Word for word:** *vilasati* — sports; *paṭaha* — with drums; *prakṛṣṭa* — excellent; *bherī* — kettledrums; *madhura* — sweet; *mṛdaṅga* — of mridangas; *vibhaṅga* — with the rolls; *ramya* — charming; *gītaiḥ* — and with songs; *niravadhi* — unceasing; *sumanaḥ* — of flowers; *samūha* — of masses; *vṛṣṭyā* — by a shower; *guru* — thickly; *dhavalī-kṛta* — whitened; *veśma* — of the hall; *madhya* — in the middle; *bhūmau* — on the floor.

### 11

**11.**

```
इति विरहविषण्णचित्तवृत्ति-
र्निजजनवीक्षणकिञ्चिदात्तधैर्यः ।
निरवधि विरुदन् विमुक्तकण्ठं

```
*iti viraha-viṣaṇṇa-citta-vṛttir
nija-jana-vīkṣaṇa-kiñcid-ātta-dhairyaḥ |
niravadhi virudan vimukta-kaṇṭhaṁ
kati divasāni nināya gauracandraḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *viraha* — by separation; *viṣaṇṇa* — dejected; *citta* — of mind; *vṛttiḥ* — [whose] state [was]; *nija* — of His own; *jana* — people; *vīkṣaṇa* — by the sight; *kiñcit* — a little; *ātta* — having gained; *dhairyaḥ* — steadiness; *niravadhi* — unceasingly; *virudan* — weeping; *vimukta* — opened; *kaṇṭham* — throat; *kati* — some; *divasāni* — days; *nināya* — passed; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra.

**Translation.** Thus, His mind dejected by separation, and gaining only a little steadiness from the sight of His own people, Gaurachandra passed some days weeping unceasingly and aloud, with open throat.

### 12

**12.**

```
अथ निजचरणाम्बुजैकभक्तैः
सह स तु गौरशशी समुद्यतोऽभूत् ।
रचयितुमभिमार्जनां समन्तात्
प्रथितवतो भवनस्य गुण्डिचेति ॥१२॥
```
*atha nija-caraṇāmbujaika-bhaktaiḥ
saha sa tu gaura-śaśī samudyato 'bhūt |
racayitum abhimārjanāṁ samantāt
prathitavato bhavanasya guṇḍiceti ||12||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *nija* — His own; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *ambuja* — to the lotuses; *eka* — exclusively; *bhaktaiḥ* — with devotees; *saha* — together; *saḥ tu* — He; *gaura* — golden; *śaśī* — moon; *samudyataḥ* — prepared; *abhūt* — became; *racayitum* — to perform; *abhimārjanām* — a cleansing; *samantāt* — complete; *prathitavataḥ* — of [that] known; *bhavanasya* — of the temple; *guṇḍicā* — Gundicha; *iti* — as.

**Translation.** Then the golden Moon, together with the devotees exclusively devoted to His own lotus feet, made ready to perform a complete cleansing of the temple known as the Gundicha.

**Note.** Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 12.79–151 — the famous scene of the cleansing of the Gundicha (the brooms — 12.80).

### 13

**13.**

```
अथ सकलजनैश्चकार पूर्वे-
ऽहनि शचितनुजो विधाय युक्तिम् ।
झटिति रुचिरमार्जनीसमूह-
मुदितमना भवनस्य मार्जनार्थम् ॥१३॥
```
*atha sakala-janaiś cakāra pūrve
'hani śaci-tanujo vidhāya yuktim |
jhaṭiti rucira-mārjanī-samūham
udita-manā bhavanasya mārjanārtham ||13||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — and; *sakala* — with all; *janaiḥ* — with the people; *cakāra* — prepared; *pūrve* — on the previous; *ahani* — on the day; *śaci* — of Shachi; *tanujaḥ* — the son; *vidhāya* — having made; *yuktim* — a plan; *jhaṭiti* — quickly; *rucira* — of beautiful; *mārjanī* — brooms; *samūham* — a multitude; *udita* — elated; *manāḥ* — [whose] heart [was]; *bhavanasya* — of the temple; *mārjana* — of the cleansing; *artham* — for the sake.

**Translation.** And on the previous day the son of Shachi, having made a plan with all the people, quickly prepared, with an elated heart, a multitude of beautiful brooms for the cleansing of the temple.

**Note.** The even pada as printed has 12 syllables instead of 13, and with the short *śaci-* it does not scan in any case. It becomes regular if the *a* of *ahani*, elided across the pada boundary (*pūrve 'hani*), is counted and *śacī-* is read with a long *ī*: *ahani śacī-tanujo vidhāya yuktim* (⏑⏑⏑⏑—⏑⏑—⏑—⏑——). The edition writes *-समूहमुदितमना* continuously; we divide *samūham udita-manā(ḥ)* (the visarga is dropped by sandhi before *bh-*); *mudita-manāḥ*, "joyful at heart", is also possible, with the same sense. The text is the same in the Gosvami edition (GGM).

### 14

**14.**

```
अथ रजनीविरामकालपूर्वं
रभसवशादुदियाय तल्पमध्यात् ।
विमलसलिलसञ्चयैर्विधातुं
स्नपनमथो भगवान् समुद्यतोऽभूत् ॥१४॥
```
*atha rajanī-virāma-kāla-pūrvaṁ
rabhasa-vaśād udiyāya talpa-madhyāt |
vimala-salila-sañcayair vidhātuṁ
snapanam atho bhagavān samudyato 'bhūt ||14||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *rajanī* — of the night; *virāma* — of the end; *kāla* — the time; *pūrvam* — before; *rabhasa* — of eagerness; *vaśāt* — under the sway; *udiyāya* — rose; *talpa* — the bed; *madhyāt* — from; *vimala* — of pure; *salila* — water; *sañcayaiḥ* — with abundance; *vidhātum* — to perform; *snapanam* — bathing; *atho* — and; *bhagavān* — the Lord; *samudyataḥ* — prepared; *abhūt* — became.

**Translation.** Then, before the time of the night's end, the Lord, under the sway of eagerness, rose from His bed and made ready to bathe with an abundance of pure water.

**Note.** In the first pada the fifth syllable (*-nī-*) is long, whereas the metre requires a short one; with the stem *rajani-* (an equally correct form) the pada would be regular.

### 15

**20.**


**21.**


**Word for word:** *druta* — swift; *gatiḥ* — [whose] going [was]; *atha* — then; *guṇḍicā* — of Gundicha; *ālayasya* — of the temple; *prabhu-vara* — by the supreme Lord; *gamya* (?) — to be gone to (?); *samīpam* — to the vicinity; *utka* — eager; *cittaḥ* — [whose] heart [was]; *sukha* — of happiness; *jaladhim* — into the ocean; *iva* — as if; *āviśat* — entered; *puram* — the enclosure; *tat* — that; *cira-samayena* — after a long time; *tu* — but; *te* — they; *samīpam* — near; *īyuḥ* — came.

We take *prabhu-vara-gamya-* as "to be gone to by the supreme Lord", that is, the Gundicha temple as the destination of Jagannatha's journey (cf. 14.3–5); it may also be understood of Gaura Himself: "the best of lords [reached] the vicinity…". **30.**

```
क्षणमपि भगवान् स्वयं विधत्ते
```

### 31

**31.**

```
सकलजनसमीपमेव गच्छ-
```

### 32

**32.**

```
स्वयमपि कतिभिर्जनैः स सिंहा-
```

**33.**

```
असकृदसकृदापतद्भिरेभि-
र्निरवधिवर्धितमार्जनीरजोभिः ।
```

**35.**

**36.**

```
क्वचिदथ गृहीतरज्जुकुम्भाः
कटितटपरिनद्धतरोत्तरीयवस्त्राः ।
```

**Word for word:** *kvacit* — in one place; *atha* — then; *gṛhīta* — having taken; *rajju* — ropes; *kumbhāḥ* — [and] pots; *kaṭi* — of the hips; *taṭa* — on the slope; *parinaddhatara* — tied very firmly; *uttarīya* — upper; *vastrāḥ* — [whose] garments [were]; *katicit* — some; *api* — also; *tat* — to them; *antike* — near; *su-sajjāḥ* — fully ready; *kati* — some; *ca* — and; *tathā eva* — likewise; *tat* — to those; *antike* — near; *atha* — then; *tasthuḥ* — stood.

### 37

**37.**

```
अथ जननिचयः स कोऽपि रज्ज्वा
घटघटया हरति स्म वारिपूरम् ।
```

atha katham api kasyacic ca ko 'pi
vyadadad atha kramataś ca ko 'pi ninye ||37||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *jana* — of people; *nicayaḥ* — the crowd; *saḥ* — that; *kaḥ api* — someone; *rajjvā* — with a rope; *ghaṭa* — of pitchers; *ghaṭayā* — with a multitude; *harati sma* — drew; *vāri* — of water; *pūram* — a flood; *atha* — and; *katham api* — somehow; *kasyacit* — to someone; *ca* — and; *kaḥ api* — someone; *vyadadat* — handed; *atha* — and; *kramataḥ* — in succession; *ca* — and; *kaḥ api* — someone; *ninye* — carried on.

**38.**


**Word for word:** *katicit* — some; *atha* — and; *samunnayanti* — raise up; *pūrṇān* — full; *katicit* — some; *adhaḥ* — down; *ca* — and; *ghaṭān* — pitchers; *nayanti* — carry; *apūrṇān* — empty; *pariṇatiḥ* — the outcome; *ubhayoḥ* — of both; *iyam* — this; *hi* — indeed; *ramyā* — delightful; *na* — not; *khalu* — surely; *viparyayam* — reversal; *eti* — goes to; *hi* — for; *svabhāvaḥ* — nature.

**39.**


**Word for word:** *sukha* — of happiness; *bhara* — from the fullness; *parama* — supremely; *ullasadbhiḥ* — rejoicing; *ebhiḥ* — by them; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *itara-itara* — by turns; *rikti* — emptiness; *pūrti* — and fullness; *bhājām* — of [those] having; *ghaṭana* — by passing on; *vighaṭanaiḥ* — and handing back; *ghaṭī* — of pitchers; *ghaṭānām* — of multitudes; *ghaṭa-maya* — made of pitchers; *kanduka* — of ball; *keliḥ* — a game; *anvaghāṭi* — was set going.

**40.**

```

ध्वनिरसकौ रसकौतुकात् समुत्थः ।
```

sukha-bharataḥ pṛthu-vepathūttha-bhaṅgāḥ |

katicana nirvṛtim eva tatra nāpuḥ ||45||*

**Word for word:** *pulaka* — of hairs standing on end; *paṭala* — with masses; *pūrita* — filled; *akhila* — all; *aṅgāḥ* — [with] limbs; *sukha* — of happiness; *bharataḥ* — from the fullness; *pṛthu* — violent; *vepathu* — by trembling; *uttha* — caused; *bhaṅgāḥ* — [with] swayings; *prabhu* — of the Lord; *kara* — of the hand; *kamale* — into the lotus; *ghaṭa* — of pots; *pradānāt* — from the giving; *katicana* — some; *nirvṛtim* — satiety; *eva* — at all; *tatra* — there; *na āpuḥ* — did not attain.

**Translation.** Some — all their limbs covered with masses of hairs standing on end, their bodies swaying with the violent trembling caused by the fullness of happiness — could find no satiety at all in giving pots into the lotus hand of the Lord.

### 46

**46.**

```
कतिचन दयितस्य पादपद्म-
द्वयमभि निर्भरमुत्सुका जलानि ।
रहसि परिकिरन्ति केवलं स्म

```

**Word for word:** *katicana* — some; *dayitasya* — of the Beloved; *pāda* — of the feet; *padma* — of the lotuses; *dvayam abhi* — upon the pair; *nirbharam* — copiously; *utsukāḥ* — eager; *jalāni* — water; *rahasi* — in secret; *parikiranti sma* — poured; *kevalam* — only; *kva* — where; *ca* — then; *gṛha* — of the chambers; *dhauta* — of the washing; *vidhiḥ* — the business; *sthitaḥ* — remained; *tat* — then; *eṣām* — for them.

**Translation.** Some, eager [for Him], secretly poured water copiously on the pair of the Beloved's lotus feet alone. Where, then, did the business of washing the chambers remain for them?

**Note.** The rhetorical question means that they quite forgot about washing the chambers. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 47

**47.**

```
अवकिरति मुहुः स्वलोकवृन्दे
```

### 48

**48.**

```
श्रमजलकणिकाविकाशभास्व-
द्वदनविधुस्तिमितारुणांशुकान्तः ।

```

**Word for word:** *śrama* — of exertion; *jala* — of water (sweat); *kaṇikā* — of the droplets; *vikāśa* — with the glistening; *bhāsvat* — shining; *vadana* — of the face; *vidhuḥ* — [whose] moon; *timita* — wet; *aruṇa* — of the red; *aṁśuka* — garment; *antaḥ* — [whose] border; *itaḥ itaḥ itaḥ* — from every side; *ukṣita* — splashed; *ambu* — by water; *sa-ārdraḥ* — drenched; *snapana* — of bathing; *kalā* — from the sport; *utthita-vat* — like one risen; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *rarāja* — shone.

**Translation.** The moon of His face shining with the glistening of droplets of sweat, the border of His red garment wet, drenched by the water splashed from every side, the Lord shone as though He had just come out of a sport of bathing.

### 49

**49.**

```
स्वयमपि निजभक्तपाणिपद्मा-
द्घटमपि गृह्य जलेन पूर्णपूर्णम् ।
सरभसमवकीर्य चावकीर्य

```
*svayam api nija-bhakta-pāṇi-padmād
ghaṭam api gṛhya jalena pūrṇa-pūrṇam |
sarabhasam avakīrya cāvakīrya

**Word for word:** *svayam* — Himself; *api* — too; *nija* — of His own; *bhakta* — devotee; *pāṇi* — of the hand; *padmāt* — from the lotus; *ghaṭam* — a pot; *api* — and; *gṛhya* — taking; *jalena* — with water; *pūrṇa-pūrṇam* — full to the brim; *sa-rabhasam* — impetuously; *avakīrya ca avakīrya* — pouring and pouring; *praghaṇam* — the veranda; *apūri* — flooded; *ghanam* — densely; *ghanaḥ* — a raincloud; *yathā* — like; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** And He Himself, taking from the lotus hand of His devotee a pot full to the brim with water, impetuously poured and poured, and flooded the veranda densely, like a raincloud.

**Note.** *Praghaṇa* is a covered veranda before the entrance.

### 50

**50.**

```
क्वचन जलकणाभिचुम्बिताङ्गः
क्वचन च कर्दमखेलया विमुग्धः ।
अभिनवसरसीविलोडनोत्थः

```
*kvacana jala-kaṇābhicumbitāṅgaḥ

**Word for word:** *kvacana* — here; *jala* — of water; *kaṇa* — by drops; *abhicumbita* — kissed; *aṅgaḥ* — [whose] body; *kvacana* — there; *ca* — and; *kardama* — with mud; *khelayā* — by play; *vimugdhaḥ* — enraptured; *abhinava* — fresh; *sarasī* — a pond; *viloḍana* — after churning up; *utthaḥ* — emerged; *saḥ tu* — He; *jala* — in the water; *kuñjara-vat* — like an elephant; *tadā* — then; *rarāja* — shone.

**Translation.** Here His body kissed by drops of water, there enraptured by play with the mud, He shone then like an elephant in the water that has come out after churning up a fresh pond.

### 51

**51.**

```
सलिलपटलसेकतोऽभितिम्यत्-
सदरुणचेललसन्नितम्बशोभः ।
दिनकरभयमग्नसान्ध्यमेघा-
वृत इव मेरुरयं तदा रराज ॥५१॥
```
*salila-paṭala-sekato 'bhitimyat-
sad-aruṇa-cela-lasan-nitamba-śobhaḥ |
dinakara-bhaya-magna-sāndhya-meghā-
vṛta iva merur ayaṁ tadā rarāja ||51||*

**Word for word:** *salila* — of water; *paṭala* — by the masses; *sekataḥ* — from the drenching; *abhitimyat* — wet through; *sat-aruṇa* — bright red; *cela* — through the cloth; *lasat* — shining; *nitamba* — of the hips; *śobhaḥ* — [whose] beauty; *dinakara* — of the sun; *bhaya* — out of fear; *magna* — sheltering; *sāndhya* — by evening; *megha* — clouds; *āvṛtaḥ* — enveloped; *iva* — like; *meruḥ* — Meru; *ayam* — He; *tadā* — then; *rarāja* — shone.

**Translation.** The beauty of His hips shining through the bright red cloth, wet through from the drenching with masses of water, He shone then like Mount Meru enveloped in evening clouds that have taken shelter [on it] out of fear of the sun.

**Note.** The edition has *सेकतोऽभिताम्यत्*. By sense we read *-'bhitimyat*, "wet through"; cf. v. 47 ⟨?⟩. Metrically the two readings are equivalent. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text as the edition.

### 52

**52.**

```
कति कति न घटास्तदा बभञ्जुः
कति कति नो पुनराययुश्च तत्र ।
कति कति न जलानि चाहृतानी-

```
*kati kati na ghaṭās tadā babhañjuḥ
kati kati no punar āyayuś ca tatra |
kati kati na jalāni cāhṛtānī-
ta ita itaḥ kati vābhavan na nadyaḥ ||52||*

**Word for word:** *kati kati* — how many; *na* — not [i.e. how very many]; *ghaṭāḥ* — pots; *tadā* — then; *babhañjuḥ* — broke; *kati kati* — how many; *no* — not; *punaḥ* — again; *āyayuḥ* — came back; *ca* — and; *tatra* — there; *kati kati* — how much; *na* — not [i.e. how very much]; *jalāni* — water; *ca* — and; *āhṛtāni* — was brought; *ita itaḥ* — here and there; *kati* — how many; *vā* — and; *abhavan* — arose; *na* — not [i.e. how very many]; *nadyaḥ* — rivers.

**Translation.** How many pots broke then, how many never came back there again, how much water was brought — and how many rivers sprang up here and there!

**Note.** *चाहृतानीत* = *cāhṛtāni + ita* (sandhi across the pada boundary).

### 53

**53.**

```
निरवधि कलसैश्च लोचनैश्च
प्रसृमरहर्षभरैः किरन्त आपः ।
बभुरतिरहसान्तरान्तरा च
```

**Word for word:** *niravadhi* — ceaselessly; *kalasaiḥ* — with pitchers; *ca* — and; *locanaiḥ* — with eyes; *ca* — and; *prasṛmara* — overflowing; *harṣa* — of joy; *bharaiḥ* — from the fullness; *kirantaḥ* — pouring; *āpaḥ* — water; *babhuḥ* — shone; *ati-rahasā* — impetuously; *antarā antarā* — now and again; *ca* — and; *sphuṭa* — loud; *jaya* — of "jaya"; *nāda* — cries; *juṣaḥ* — giving forth; *ghanāḥ* — clouds; *iva* — like; *ete* — they.

**Translation.** Ceaselessly pouring water from pitchers and from their eyes out of the fullness of overflowing joy, and now and again impetuously raising loud cries of "Jaya!", they shone like [thundering] clouds.

### 54

**54.**

```
निरवधिसलिलाभिषेकतिम्यत्-
करनिकरा वरवारणा इवैते ।
उपपुरि विबभुः प्रभोः समीपे

```
*niravadhi-salilābhiṣeka-timyat-
kara-nikarā vara-vāraṇā ivaite |
upapuri vibabhuḥ prabhoḥ samīpe
na sukha-cayas tu mamau jagaty amīṣām ||54||*

**Word for word:** *niravadhi* — ceaseless; *salila* — with water; *abhiṣeka* — by the drenching; *timyat* — wet; *kara* — of hands (trunks); *nikarāḥ* — [with] multitudes; *vara* — noble; *vāraṇāḥ* — elephants; *iva* — like; *ete* — they; *upapuri* — by the enclosure; *vibabhuḥ* — shone; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *samīpe* — near; *na* — not; *sukha* — of happiness; *cayaḥ* — the mass; *tu* — but; *mamau* — was contained; *jagati* — in the world; *amīṣām* — of theirs.

**Translation.** Their many hands wet from the ceaseless drenching with water, they shone by the enclosure near the Lord like noble elephants with their trunks; and the mass of their happiness could not be contained in the world.

**Note.** A play on words: *kara* means both "hand" and "trunk".

**56.**

```
अथ सुविहितपङ्क्तिषूपविश्य
```

### 57

**57.**

```
अधिधरणि निपात्य भूरिलीलो
ललितबहिर्वसनं त्वरायुतः सः ।
विहितपणफलं बलाज्जिगीषु-
र्न कति तृणानि शर्कराश्च जह्रे ॥५७॥
```
*adhi-dharaṇi nipātya bhūri-līlo
lalita-bahir-vasanaṁ tvarāyutaḥ saḥ |
vihita-paṇa-phalaṁ balāj jigīṣur
na kati tṛṇāni śarkarāś ca jahre ||57||*

**Word for word:** *adhi-dharaṇi* — on the ground; *nipātya* — spreading; *bhūri* — rich; *līlaḥ* — [in] pastimes; *lalita* — graceful; *bahiḥ-vasanam* — outer garment; *tvarā-yutaḥ* — in haste; *saḥ* — He; *vihita* — fixed; *paṇa* — of the wager; *phalam* — the prize; *balāt* — at all costs; *jigīṣuḥ* — wishing to win; *na kati* — how many … not [i.e. how very many]; *tṛṇāni* — blades of grass; *śarkarāḥ* — pebbles; *ca* — and; *jahre* — gathered.

**Translation.** Rich in pastimes, He hastily spread His graceful outer garment on the ground and, wishing to win the prize of the fixed wager at all costs — how many blades of grass and pebbles did He not gather!

**Note.** The edition has *नैकति*; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *na kati* ("how many … not"), and so we read. The second pada is metrically correct; it is the fourth pada that is one syllable short (twelve instead of thirteen), with either reading ⟨?⟩. Cf. Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 12.89–91: the devotees gather the dust and rubbish into their cloths, and the Lord, looking at the heaps, judges who has worked best.

**60.**

```
सहजपरमसुस्वरास्त एते
```

### 61

**61.**


**Word for word:** *nayana* — of the eyes; *jala* — of water; *jharaiḥ* — with torrents; *pada* — of the feet; *aravinda* — of the lotuses; *dvaya* — of the pair; *nakha* — of the nails; *candramasaḥ* — the moons; *pavitrayan* — purifying; *saḥ* — He; *na hi* — for there is not; *jagati* — in the world; *durāpam* — hard to attain; *etat* — than this; *anyat* — anything else; *kim* — whether; *iti* — [thinking] thus; *tadā* — then; *abhisiṣeca* — bathed; *saḥ* — He; *aṅghri* — of the feet; *padmam* — the lotus.

**Translation.** With torrents of tears He purified the moons of the nails of the pair of lotus feet. Was it [with the thought] "For in the world there is nothing else so hard to attain as this" that He then bathed the lotus feet?

**Note.** Whose feet are meant — His own or Jagannatha's — is unclear ⟨?⟩. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**Word for word:** *upari* — above [Him]; *paridhṛta* — held up; *ātapatra* — of parasols; *vṛndaiḥ* — by the multitude; *mukha* — of the face; *śaśi* — the moon; *sevana* — to serving; *tat-para* — devoted; *indu* — of moons; *rūpaiḥ* — in the form; *niravadhi* — unceasing; *sumanaḥ* — of flowers; *samūha* — of masses; *vṛṣṭyā* — by the rain; *sita* — white; *raṇa* — of battle; *bhūḥ* — a field; *api* — even; *nīla-śaila* — of the Blue Mountain; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord.

**Note.** The meaning is unclear ⟨?⟩. The play is on colours: the blue Lord, covered by white parasols and white flowers, becomes white; the procession is the victor's "march" (*vijaya*), hence the "field of battle". The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

**Word for word:** *gaja-pati* — of the king of elephants; *kara* — of the trunk; *daṇḍa* — with the staff; *khaṇḍa-khaṇḍī-kṛta* — hacked to pieces; *sakala* — all; *ariḥ* — [by whom] the foes; *aśeṣa* — of all; *vighna* — obstacles; *hartā* — the remover; *nṛpati* — of kings; *gaṇa* — of the host; *patiḥ* — the lord; *pratāparudraḥ* — Prataparudra; *raviḥ* — the sun; *iva* — like; *yaḥ* — who; *pratapati* — blazes (shines with might); *asau* — he; *sadā eva* — ever.

```
श्रीकान्तो मकरध्वजः सुमधुरः शुद्धः शुभानन्दकः
काशीनाथकवल्लभौ च हरिदासाख्यो रघुः शुद्धधीः ।
एतांस्तान् सहसैव चन्दनरसैर्लिप्त्वा स स्वयं श्रीमता
गौराङ्गेन दृढं निबध्य वसनं श्रीमत्कटीरोधसि ।
आजानुद्वयलम्बिपीवरभुजद्वन्द्वेन मन्दोल्लस-
द्रोमाञ्चाञ्चितविग्रहेण परमाविष्टेन तैर्निर्यये ॥१०६॥
```
*śrīkānto makaradhvajaḥ sumadhuraḥ śuddhaḥ śubhānandakaḥ
kāśīnāthaka-vallabhau ca haridāsākhyo raghuḥ śuddha-dhīḥ |
etāṁs tān sahasaiva candana-rasair liptvā sa svayaṁ śrīmatā
gaurāṅgena dṛḍhaṁ nibadhya vasanaṁ śrīmat-kaṭī-rodhasi |
ājānu-dvaya-lambi-pīvara-bhuja-dvandvena mandollasad-
romāñcāñcita-vigraheṇa paramāviṣṭena tair niryaye ||106||*

**Word for word:** *śrīkāntaḥ* — Shrikanta; *makaradhvajaḥ* — Makaradhvaja; *su-madhuraḥ* — most sweet; *śuddhaḥ* — pure; *śubhānandakaḥ* — Shubhananda; *kāśīnāthaka* — Kashinatha; *vallabhau* — and Vallabha; *ca* — and; *haridāsa* — Haridasa; *ākhyaḥ* — named; *raghuḥ* — Raghu; *śuddha* — pure; *dhīḥ* — [whose] mind; *etān tān* — all these; *sahasā eva* — at once; *candana* — of sandal; *rasaiḥ* — with the juice; *liptvā* — having anointed; *saḥ* — He; *svayam* — Himself; *śrīmatā* — by the beautiful; *gaurāṅgena* — by Gauranga; *dṛḍham* — firmly; *nibadhya* — having tied; *vasanam* — the cloth; *śrīmat* — beautiful; *kaṭī* — of the waist; *rodhasi* — on the slope; *ājānu* — to the knees; *dvaya* — both; *lambi* — reaching; *pīvara* — stout; *bhuja* — of arms; *dvandvena* — [with] the pair; *manda* — gently; *ullasat* — thrilling; *romāñca* — with hairs standing on end; *añcita* — adorned; *vigraheṇa* — [with] a body; *parama* — wholly; *āviṣṭena* — absorbed; *taiḥ* — with them; *niryaye* — there was a setting out.

**Translation.** …Shrikanta, Makaradhvaja, the most sweet and pure Shubhananda, Kashinatha and Vallabha, the one named Haridasa, Raghu of pure mind — all these He Himself at once anointed with sandal juice. And then the beautiful Gauranga, having firmly tied His cloth on the slope of His beautiful waist — with His pair of stout arms reaching to both knees, His body adorned with gently thrilling hairs standing on end, wholly absorbed [in ecstasy] — set out with them.

**Note.** A verse of six padas; the metre is *śārdūla-vikrīḍita*. Note the passive *niryaye* (literally "there was a setting out [by Gauranga]"). The third pada has 20 syllables instead of 19 ⟨?⟩: without *sa* (*…liptvā svayaṁ śrīmatā*) it would be regular, and *sa* is awkward in any case beside the instrumental *gaurāṅgena* of the passive *niryaye*.

### 107

**107.**

```
अमन्दकरतालकप्रकररम्यसन्मन्दिरा-
स्वलङ्कृतकराम्बुजाः पुलकवृन्दसान्द्राङ्गकाः ।
अमी तदनु सत्वरं प्रतिपदं पदं निर्भरं
स्खलत्पदसरोरुहाः सुखसमुद्रमग्ना ययुः ॥१०७॥
```
*amanda-kara-tālaka-prakara-ramya-san-mandirā-
svalaṅkṛta-karāmbujāḥ pulaka-vṛnda-sāndrāṅgakāḥ |
amī tad-anu satvaraṁ pratipadaṁ padaṁ nirbharaṁ
skhalat-pada-saroruhāḥ sukha-samudra-magnā yayuḥ ||107||*

**Word for word:** *amanda* — loud; *kara-tālaka* — of karatalas; *prakara* — with a multitude; *ramya* — lovely; *sat* — fine; *mandirā* — with mandiras; *su-alaṅkṛta* — well adorned; *kara* — of hands; *ambujāḥ* — [whose] lotuses; *pulaka* — of thrills of rapture; *vṛnda* — with masses; *sāndra* — thickly [covered]; *aṅgakāḥ* — [whose] bodies; *amī* — they; *tat-anu* — after [Him]; *satvaram* — quickly; *pratipadam* — at every step; *padam* — step; *nirbharam* — helplessly; *skhalat* — stumbling; *pada* — of feet; *saroruhāḥ* — [whose] lotuses; *sukha* — of happiness; *samudra* — in the ocean; *magnāḥ* — sunk; *yayuḥ* — went.

**Translation.** Their lotus hands well adorned with a multitude of loud karatalas and fine, lovely mandiras, their bodies thickly covered with the thrills of rapture, they went quickly after [Him]; at every step their lotus feet stumbled helplessly, and they were sunk in an ocean of happiness.

**Note.** The metre is *pṛthvī*. Karatalas and mandiras are hand cymbals.

### 108

**108.**

```
गोविन्दस्त्वरितं समेत्य नितरां नैकट्यमासादितः
पार्श्वस्थः सुखसागरेषु सततं मज्जन् प्रतस्थे ततः ।
एते ये च समागताः प्रतिपदोल्लासाकुलाः श्रीयुजो
नैषां हर्षसुधाम्बुधिर्निरवधिर्ब्रह्माण्डमध्येऽञ्चितुम् ॥१०८॥
```
*govindas tvaritaṁ sametya nitarāṁ naikaṭyam āsāditaḥ
pārśvasthaḥ sukha-sāgareṣu satataṁ majjan pratasthe tataḥ |
ete ye ca samāgatāḥ pratipadollāsākulāḥ śrīyujo
naiṣāṁ harṣa-sudhāmbudhir niravadhir brahmāṇḍa-madhye 'ñcitum ||108||*

**Word for word:** *govindaḥ* — Govinda; *tvaritam* — quickly; *sametya* — having come up; *nitarām* — very; *naikaṭyam* — nearness; *āsāditaḥ* — having attained; *pārśva-sthaḥ* — standing at [His] side; *sukha* — of happiness; *sāgareṣu* — in the oceans; *satatam* — constantly; *majjan* — immersed; *pratasthe* — set out; *tataḥ* — then; *ete ye* — those who; *ca* — and; *samāgatāḥ* — had come; *pratipada* — at every step; *ullāsa* — by exultation; *ākulāḥ* — agitated; *śrī-yujaḥ* — splendid; *na* — not; *eṣām* — their; *harṣa* — of joy; *sudhā* — of nectar; *ambudhiḥ* — the ocean; *niravadhiḥ* — boundless; *brahmāṇḍa* — of the universe; *madhye* — within; *añcitum* (?) — to be contained (?).

**Translation.** Govinda quickly came up and, having drawn very near, standing at [His] side and constantly immersed in oceans of happiness, then set out [with the Lord]. And as for those splendid ones who had come, agitated by exultation at every step — the boundless ocean of the nectar of their joy [could] not be contained within the universe.

**Note.** The metre is *śārdūla-vikrīḍita*. Govinda is the Lord's servant. The ending (*'ñcitum*) is read tentatively. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 109

**109.**

```
अथ मदमृगेन्द्रालीलीलाविलासिपदक्रमः
प्रमदविगलद्घर्मस्नानप्रचायकपदक्रमः ।
अनुपमसुखारोहाद्रोमोद्गमाञ्चितविग्रहः
पथि लघु ययौ गौरस्तेजोनिरस्तरविग्रहः ॥१०९॥
```
*atha mada-mṛgendrālī-līlā-vilāsi-pada-kramaḥ
pramada-vigalad-gharma-snāna-pracāyaka-pada-kramaḥ |
anupama-sukhārohād romodgamāñcita-vigrahaḥ
pathi laghu yayau gauras tejo-nirasta-ravi-grahaḥ ||109||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *mada* — maddened; *mṛga-indra* — of lions; *ālī* — of a troop; *līlā* — playful; *vilāsi* — graceful; *pada* — of steps; *kramaḥ* — [whose] gait; *pramada* — from joy; *vigalat* — streaming; *gharma* — of sweat; *snāna* — a bath; *pracāyaka* — scattering; *pada-kramaḥ* — [whose] gait; *anupama* — of incomparable; *sukha* — happiness; *ārohāt* — from the surge; *roma-udgama* — with hairs standing on end; *añcita* — adorned; *vigrahaḥ* — [whose] body; *pathi* — along the road; *laghu* — swiftly; *yayau* — went; *gauraḥ* — Gaura; *tejaḥ* — by [His] effulgence; *nirasta* — outshone; *ravi* — of the sun; *grahaḥ* — [by whom] the orb.

**Translation.** Then Gaura — His gait playful and graceful [like that] of a troop of maddened lions, His steps scattering a bath of sweat that streamed from joy, His body adorned with hairs standing on end from the surge of incomparable happiness — went swiftly along the road, outshining with His effulgence the orb of the sun.

**Note.** The metre is *hariṇī*, with rhyme at the ends of the padas (*pāda-anta-yamaka*: *pada-kramaḥ / pada-kramaḥ; vigrahaḥ / ravi-grahaḥ*). The second pada is read according to the Gosvami edition (GGM): *…pracāyaka-pada-kramaḥ* (our edition has *-mada-kramaḥ*). In either reading the second pada has 18 syllables instead of 17 ⟨?⟩: at its end *-yaka-pada-kramaḥ* gives ⏑⏑⏑—⏑— where the metre requires ⏑⏑—⏑—, i.e. one light syllable is superfluous.

### 110

**110.**

```
रथमभि बलदेवस्याग्रतो गौरचन्द्रः
प्रमदमदमनोज्ञः श्रीविराजत्तनूकः ।
द्रुतकनकमहीध्रो दण्डवद्भूमिपृष्ठं
सह नयनजलेन प्रेमतः प्राप भूयः ॥११०॥
```
*ratham abhi baladevasyāgrato gauracandraḥ
pramada-mada-manojñaḥ śrī-virājat-tanūkaḥ |
druta-kanaka-mahīdhro daṇḍavad bhūmi-pṛṣṭhaṁ
saha nayana-jalena premataḥ prāpa bhūyaḥ ||110||*

**Word for word:** *ratham abhi* — toward the chariot; *baladevasya* — of Baladeva; *agrataḥ* — in front; *gauracandraḥ* — Gaurachandra; *pramada* — of joy; *mada* — by the intoxication; *manojñaḥ* — charming; *śrī* — with beauty; *virājat* — shining; *tanūkaḥ* — [whose] body; *druta* — molten; *kanaka* — of gold; *mahīdhraḥ* — a mountain; *daṇḍa-vat* — like a rod; *bhūmi* — of the earth; *pṛṣṭham* — the surface; *saha* — together; *nayana* — of the eyes; *jalena* — with the water; *premataḥ* — out of love; *prāpa* — reached; *bhūyaḥ* — again.

**Translation.** In front of the chariot of Baladeva, Gaurachandra — charming in the intoxication of joy, His body shining with beauty, [like] a mountain of molten gold — out of love again fell to the surface of the earth like a rod, together with [a flood of] tears.

**Note.** The metre is *mālinī*.
