# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Fourteen — verses 1–135 (printed pp. 429–455)

**Title of the sarga:** none — the colophon of the edition gives no title for this sarga.
**Metre:**
- 1–129 — *anuṣṭubh* (*śloka*; 8 syllables per pada: odd padas ××××⏑——×, even padas ××××⏑—⏑×; the odd padas also admit the usual *vipulā* variants). Verses of six or more padas: 5, 37, 77, 81, 87, 102, 108, 109, 117; verses of two padas: 69, 82, 99. In the printed text the first line of 50 and the second line of 93 are one syllable short (in 93 the reading of the Gosvami edition, *stavana-*, restores the metre).
- 130–132, 134–135 — *śārdūla-vikrīḍita* (———⏑⏑—⏑—⏑⏑⏑———⏑——⏑—; 19 syllables per pada).
- 133 — *upajāti* (a mixture of *indravajrā* ——⏑——⏑⏑—⏑—— and *upendravajrā* ⏑—⏑——⏑⏑—⏑——; 11 syllables per pada).

**Total verses:** 135. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has 141: there the long stanzas of this edition (e.g. 37, 87, 108–109, 117) are divided into couplets, and number 71 is skipped; the text is the same, and nothing is missing.

**Contents of the passage:** the first coming of the Bengali devotees for the festival of the chariots; the Snana-yatra.
- 1–10 — the Lord calls Advaita and Srivasa to come every year for the chariot festival.
- 11–35 — the preparations. Srivasa, Vasudeva and Shivananda fix the day; with Shachi in Navadvipa; Advaita, Haridasa, Rama Pandita, Mukunda, Murari; the journey with kirtana as far as Remuna.
- 36–41 — Gopinatha in Remuna; the stream of devotees — "an ocean without shores" (*kulaka*).
- 42–56 — **Sarvabhauma in Jayapura.** He is going to Kashi to preach bhakti against the Lord's prohibition; he glorifies Advaita and Haridasa.
- 57–66 — Jajpur and the Vaitarani. After a dream Prataparudra summons Advaita to Cuttack (Advaita fears the Lord's displeasure); the others go by way of Hamseshvara; all are together again at Kamalapura.
- 67–82 — the temple of Jagannatha in the distance; garlands from the Lord; at the Narendra a message: a fast, and the meeting in Mishra's house; Murari, in his humility, stays behind by the tank.
- 83–114 — the darshan of Jagannatha and the meeting with the Lord. The description of His form (*kulaka*, 85–92); the mutual obeisances with Advaita (Bhagavatam 10.14.1); Srivasa, Rama Pandita, Vasudeva; two vessels of Ganga water; Murari comes with a blade of grass between his teeth.
- 115–135 — the wedding festival on the Ekadashi; the Snana-yatra on the full moon: the platform; the procession of Baladeva, Subhadra and Jagannatha; the bathing; the hyperbole of "the hero's march".

**Divergences from the Chaitanya-charitamrita:** the episode of Sarvabhauma on his way to Kashi (42–56); Advaita summoned to Prataparudra (58–61); the Bengalis arrive before the Snana-yatra rather than before the Ratha-yatra.

**Textual cruxes:** 10 (*aviśeṣataḥ*), 41 (*vakṣāṁsi*), 60 (*bhītyā*), 64 (*tiṣṭhan*), 66 (a dropped syllable), 77, 78 (whose authority), 86 (*pañcīndra*), 93, 101, 109 (*samuccayaiḥ*), 117 (*sañcarati*), 120 (*karaiḥ*), 126 (a superfluous *ka*), 132 (*'sita-giri*) — see the notes.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

**1.**

```
एकदा प्राह नाथोऽयं निजपादपयोरुहम् ।
```

**Translation.** Once the Lord said to His own people, headed by Advaita, who had come there to see His lotus feet:

### 2

**2.**

```
आचार्य हे महाबुद्धे हे पण्डित महाशय ।
यद्वदामि शृणु श्रीमज्जगन्नाथविचेष्टितम् ॥२॥
```
*ācārya he mahā-buddhe he paṇḍita mahāśaya |
yad vadāmi śṛṇu śrīmaj-jagannātha-viceṣṭitam ||2||*

**Word for word:** *ācārya* — O Acharya; *he* — O; *mahā-buddhe* — great in intellect; *he* — O; *paṇḍita* — Pandita; *mahāśaya* — noble-hearted; *yat* — what; *vadāmi* — I [shall] say; *śṛṇu* — hear; *śrīmat* — of the glorious; *jagannātha* — of Jagannatha; *viceṣṭitam* — about the doings.

**Translation.** "O Acharya, great in intellect! O noble-hearted Pandita! Hear what I shall say about the doings of the glorious Jagannatha.

**Note.** The Acharya is Advaita, the Pandita is Srivasa (cf. verses 11, 24).

### 4

**4.**

```
गुण्डिचागारगमने वर्त्मनः पार्श्वयोर्द्वयोः ।
य एष पुष्पितारामो रामणीयकवानिह ॥४॥
```
*guṇḍicāgāra-gamane vartmanaḥ pārśvayor dvayoḥ |
ya eṣa puṣpitārāmo rāmaṇīyakavān iha ||4||*

**Word for word:** *guṇḍicā* — of Gundicha; *āgāra* — to the house; *gamane* — on the way; *vartmanaḥ* — of the road; *pārśvayoḥ* — on the sides; *dvayoḥ* — on both; *yaḥ* — which; *eṣaḥ* — this; *puṣpita* — blossoming; *ārāmaḥ* — garden; *rāmaṇīyakavān* — full of loveliness; *iha* — here.

**Translation.** This blossoming garden, full of loveliness, which [lies] here on both sides of the road on the way to the Gundicha temple —

**Note.** The relative clause (*ya eṣa…*) is taken up by *enam*, "it", in verse 5.

### 5

**5.**

```
वृन्दारण्यस्मृतिकरमेनं विद्धि विशेषतः ।
तत्र गत्वा जगन्नाथो गुण्डिचामण्डपे प्रभुः ।
एकाधिकाष्टदिवसं विहरंस्तत्र तिष्ठति ॥५॥
```
*vṛndāraṇya-smṛti-karam enaṁ viddhi viśeṣataḥ |
tatra gatvā jagannātho guṇḍicā-maṇḍape prabhuḥ |
ekādhikāṣṭa-divasaṁ viharaṁs tatra tiṣṭhati ||5||*

**Word for word:** *vṛndāraṇya* — of Vrindavana; *smṛti* — remembrance; *karam* — causing; *enam* — it; *viddhi* — know; *viśeṣataḥ* — especially; *tatra* — there; *gatvā* — having gone; *jagannāthaḥ* — Jagannatha; *guṇḍicā* — of Gundicha; *maṇḍape* — in the pavilion; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *eka* — by one; *adhika* — exceeding; *aṣṭa* — eight; *divasam* — days; *viharan* — sporting; *tatra* — there; *tiṣṭhati* — stays.

**Translation.** — know that it especially awakens the remembrance of Vrindavana. Going there, Lord Jagannatha stays in the pavilion of the Gundicha for eight days and one more, sporting there.

**Note.** A verse of six padas. "Eight days and one more" means nine days.

**6.**

```
तदिमां परमां यात्रां देवाद्यैरपि दुर्लभाम् ।
द्रष्टुं प्रत्यब्दमेवात्रागन्तव्यं हि भवादृशैः ॥६॥
```
*tad imāṁ paramāṁ yātrāṁ devādyair api durlabhām |

**Translation.** Therefore people such as you should surely come here every year to see this supreme procession, hard to attain even for the gods and others."

### 7

**7.**

```
इति स्वीयविलासानां दर्शनाय महाप्रभुः ।
तानुवाच कृपाम्भोधी रथयात्राच्छलेन सः ॥७॥
```
*iti svīya-vilāsānāṁ darśanāya mahāprabhuḥ |
tān uvāca kṛpāmbhodhī ratha-yātrā-cchalena saḥ ||7||*

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *svīya* — of His own; *vilāsānām* — of the pastimes; *darśanāya* — for the seeing; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *tān* — to them; *uvāca* — spoke; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *ambhodhiḥ* — the ocean; *ratha-yātrā* — of the Ratha-yatra; *chalena* — on the pretext; *saḥ* — He.

**Translation.** Thus Mahaprabhu, the ocean of mercy, spoke to them on the pretext of the Ratha-yatra, [so that they might come] to see His own pastimes.

**Note.** The edition's *कृपाम्भोधी* is the regular sandhi form of *kṛpāmbhodhiḥ* before *r*.

**8.**

```
ततः प्रभृत्येवमेते रथस्य समये प्रभुम् ।
श्रीगौरचन्द्रं द्रष्टुं तं प्रत्यब्दं यान्ति सस्पृहम् ॥८॥
```
*tataḥ prabhṛty evam ete rathasya samaye prabhum |

### 9

**9.**

```
यत् प्रत्यब्दं प्रयान्त्येते द्रष्टुं गौराङ्गसुन्दरम् ।
तत्कथां किं सुरगुरोः शतं कथयितुं भवेत् ॥९॥
```
*yat pratyabdaṁ prayānty ete draṣṭuṁ gaurāṅga-sundaram |
tat-kathāṁ kiṁ sura-guroḥ śataṁ kathayituṁ bhavet ||9||*

**Word for word:** *yat* — that; *prati-abdam* — every year; *prayānti* — go forth; *ete* — they; *draṣṭum* — to see; *gaurāṅga-sundaram* — the beautiful Gauranga; *tat* — of that; *kathām* — the story; *kim* — whether; *sura-guroḥ* — of the preceptor of the gods; *śatam* — a hundred; *kathayitum* — to tell; *bhavet* — could be [able].

**Translation.** The story of how they go forth every year to see the beautiful Gauranga — could even a hundred Brihaspatis, preceptors of the gods, tell it?

**Note.** The preceptor of the gods is Brihaspati, the type of eloquence.

### 10

**10.**

```
तथाप्युत्कण्ठया शश्वत् प्रथयन्नविशेषतः ।
एकवारस्य गमनं समन्ताद्वर्णयामहे ॥१०॥
```
*tathāpy utkaṇṭhayā śaśvat prathayann aviśeṣataḥ |
eka-vārasya gamanaṁ samantād varṇayāmahe ||10||*

**Word for word:** *tathā api* — nevertheless; *utkaṇṭhayā* — out of longing; *śaśvat* — constant; *prathayan* — setting forth; *aviśeṣataḥ* — without distinction (in general); *eka* — of one; *vārasya* — time; *gamanam* — the journey; *samantāt* — in full; *varṇayāmahe* — we shall describe.

**Translation.** Nevertheless, out of constant longing, setting it forth in a general way, we shall describe one such journey in full.

**Note.** *Aviśeṣataḥ*, "without distinction", i.e. in general: a single journey is described as representative of all of them, while *samantāt* means "in full", from beginning to end. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 12

**12.**

```
प्रवृत्ते माधवे मासि वहन्मलयमारुते ।
रुते कोकिलभृङ्गाद्यैश्चारु ते गन्तुमुद्यताः ॥१२॥
```
*pravṛtte mādhave māsi vahan-malaya-mārute |
rute kokila-bhṛṅgādyaiś cāru te gantum udyatāḥ ||12||*

**Word for word:** *pravṛtte* — when [it] had begun; *mādhave* — Madhava; *māsi* — the month; *vahat* — blowing; *malaya* — from Malaya; *mārute* — when the breeze; *rute* — when [the air] resounded; *kokila* — with cuckoos; *bhṛṅga* — with bees; *ādyaiḥ* — and others; *cāru* — charmingly; *te* — they; *gantum* — to go; *udyatāḥ* — made ready.

**Translation.** When the month of Madhava had begun, when the Malaya breeze was blowing and [the air] resounded charmingly with cuckoos, bees and the rest, they made ready to go.

**Note.** Madhava is the month of Vaishakha (April–May). The Malaya breeze is the spring wind from the Malaya mountains in the south. Note the sound-play *-mārute | rute … cāru te*.

**28.**


### 34

**34.**

```
अस्ताद्रिमस्तके न्यस्य समस्तकरमेव सः ।
```

**Note.** A play on *kara*, "ray" and "hand": the sun in despair "props its head on its hand" — and sets.

### 37

**37.**

```
प्रातः प्रतस्थिरे सर्वे सर्वदोत्सुकचेतसः ।
श्रीगौरचन्द्रचरणदर्शनार्ता महाशयाः ।
तेषामोघः स परमः सततं सुखतन्मयः ।
पारावार इवारेजे पारावारविवर्जितः ॥३७॥
```
*prātaḥ pratasthire sarve sarvadotsuka-cetasaḥ |
śrī-gauracandra-caraṇa-darśanārtā mahāśayāḥ |
teṣām oghaḥ sa paramaḥ satataṁ sukha-tan-mayaḥ |
pārāvāra ivāreje pārāvāra-vivarjitaḥ ||37||*

**Word for word:** *prātaḥ* — in the morning; *pratasthire* — set out; *sarve* — all; *sarvadā* — ever; *utsuka* — eager; *cetasaḥ* — [with] hearts; *śrī-gauracandra* — of Sri Gaurachandra; *caraṇa* — of the feet; *darśana* — for the sight; *ārtāḥ* — pining; *mahāśayāḥ* — the noble-hearted; *teṣām* — their; *oghaḥ* — stream; *saḥ* — that; *paramaḥ* — great; *satatam* — constantly; *sukha* — with happiness; *tat-mayaḥ* — filled; *pārāvāraḥ* — the ocean; *iva* — like; *āreje* — shone; *pāra-avāra* — of the further and nearer shores; *vivarjitaḥ* — devoid.

**Translation.** In the morning they all set out, the noble-hearted ones, their hearts ever eager, pining for the sight of the feet of Sri Gaurachandra. That great stream of theirs, constantly filled with happiness, shone like the ocean (*pārāvāra*) — but an ocean without either shore.

**Note.** A verse of six padas. A play on words: *pārāvāra*, "the ocean", literally "[that which has] a further and a nearer shore", while *pārāvāra-vivarjitaḥ* means "devoid of a further and a nearer shore". Verses 38–41 develop the comparison (*kulaka*, so marked in the edition after verse 41).

### 38

**38.**

```
अद्वैतोऽयं निधिरभूत् श्रीवासो भक्तिपर्वतः ।

```
*advaito 'yaṁ nidhir abhūt śrīvāso bhakti-parvataḥ |
amṛtaṁ kīrtanam abhūt haridāso mahā-maṇiḥ ||38||*

**Word for word:** *advaitaḥ* — Advaita; *ayam* — this; *nidhiḥ* — the treasure; *abhūt* — was; *śrīvāsaḥ* — Srivasa; *bhakti* — of bhakti; *parvataḥ* — the mountain; *amṛtam* — the nectar; *kīrtanam* — the kirtana; *abhūt* — was; *haridāsaḥ* — Haridasa; *mahā* — the great; *maṇiḥ* — jewel.

**Translation.** Advaita was its treasure, Srivasa the mountain of bhakti, the kirtana its nectar, Haridasa its great jewel.

**Note.** The images come from the churning of the ocean: the treasures, Mount Mandara, the nectar, the Kaustubha jewel.

### 39

**39.**

```
तेषामन्योन्यसंप्रीतिर्लक्ष्मीरभवदुत्तमा ।
हिण्डीरो यशसां राशिस्तेजश्च बडवानलः ॥३९॥
```
*teṣām anyonya-saṁprītir lakṣmīr abhavad uttamā |
hiṇḍīro yaśasāṁ rāśis tejaś ca baḍavānalaḥ ||39||*

**Word for word:** *teṣām* — their; *anyonya* — mutual; *saṁprītiḥ* — love; *lakṣmīḥ* — Lakshmi; *abhavat* — was; *uttamā* — the supreme; *hiṇḍīraḥ* — the foam; *yaśasām* — of glories; *rāśiḥ* — the heap; *tejaḥ* — splendour; *ca* — and; *baḍavā-analaḥ* — the submarine fire.

**Translation.** Their mutual love was the supreme Lakshmi; the heap of their glories, the foam; and their splendour, the submarine fire.

**Note.** Lakshmi too rose from the churned ocean; glory is white in poetic convention, hence the foam. *Baḍavānala*, "the mare's fire", is the fire at the bottom of the ocean.

### 40

**40.**

```
कल्लोलो जयनिस्वानस्तरङ्गो निर्भराप्लुतिः ।
मीनाश्च पादाङ्गुलयो मुक्तास्तन्नखपङ्क्तयः ॥४०॥
```
*kallolo jaya-nisvānas taraṅgo nirbharāplutiḥ |
mīnāś ca pādāṅgulayo muktās tan-nakha-paṅktayaḥ ||40||*

**Word for word:** *kallolaḥ* — the billows; *jaya* — of "Jaya!"; *nisvānaḥ* — the cry; *taraṅgaḥ* — the waves; *nirbhara* — unrestrained; *āplutiḥ* — leaping; *mīnāḥ* — the fish; *ca* — and; *pāda* — of the feet; *aṅgulayaḥ* — the toes; *muktāḥ* — the pearls; *tat* — of them; *nakha* — of the nails; *paṅktayaḥ* — the rows.

**Translation.** The cries of "Jaya!" were its billows, their unrestrained leaping [in the dance] its waves, their toes its fish, and the rows of their toenails its pearls.

**Note.** *Āpluti* is taken as "leaping" (from *ā-plu*, "to leap; to plunge"); it may also be understood as "overflowing, flood" [of rapture].

*kecit tat-saṅgato jagmur advaitānugatā janāḥ |
kaṭakasya pathā te ca śrī-gaura-caraṇāśrayāḥ ||61||*

**Word for word:** *kecit* — some; *tat* — with Him; *saṅgataḥ* — together; *jagmuḥ* — went; *advaita* — of Advaita; *anugatāḥ* — followers; *janāḥ* — people; *kaṭakasya* — of Kataka; *pathā* — by the road; *te* — they; *ca* — and; *śrī-gaura* — of Sri Gaura; *caraṇa* — at the feet; *āśrayāḥ* — having taken shelter.

**Translation.** Some people, followers of Advaita who had taken shelter at the feet of Sri Gaura, went together with Him by the road through Kataka (Cuttack).

**Note.** Kataka (Cuttack) was the capital of Prataparudra.

**67.**

**Word for word:** *tejasā* — in splendour; *koṭi* — of myriads; *sūrya* — of suns; *ābhaḥ* — having the likeness; *sudhayā* — with nectar (with plaster); *ca* — and; *samanvitaḥ* — endowed; *saḥ* — that; *nīla* — of the Blue; *parvata* — Mountain; *pateḥ* — of the Lord; *prāsādaḥ* — the temple; *sukha* — joyful; *darśanaḥ* — to behold.

### 77

**77.**


**Word for word:** *bhaviṣyati* — will be; *hi* — for; *tatra eva* — right there; *puṇḍarīka-akṣaḥ* — the Lotus-eyed One; *īkṣyatām* — let be seen; *aham* — I; *tatra eva* — to that very place; *yāsyāmi* — shall come; *vilambena* — after a while; *suniścitam* — certainly; *bhaviṣyati* — will be; *samālāpaḥ* — conversation; *tatra* — there; *miśra* — of Mishra; *ālaya* — of the house; *antare* — inside.

**78.**

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *śrutvā* — having heard; *advaitaḥ* — Advaita; *īśaḥ* — the lord; *māyā* — a stratagem; *eṣā* — this [is]; *iti* — thus; *vitarkayan* — surmising; *tathā eva* — just so; *anumatim* — consent; *cakre* — gave; *tat* — of Him; *vaśaḥ* — under the control; *asau* — He; *yataḥ* — since; *svayam* — Himself.

**Translation.** Hearing this, Lord Advaita surmised, "This is a stratagem," yet He gave His consent just so, since He Himself is under [the Lord's] control.

**Note.** *Tad-vaśo 'sau* can also be taken the other way: "since He [the Lord] Himself is under [Advaita's] control" ⟨?⟩. Grammatically both readings are possible; we keep ours. The Gosvami edition (GGM) has the same text.

### 81

**81.**

```

```
*na pāraye 'haṁ vrajituṁ na śaktir mama vartate |
na sāhasaṁ me 'sti tāvad draṣṭuṁ jagad-adhīśvaram |
bhavadbhir jñāpite paścād gantuṁ śaktir bhaviṣyati ||81||*

**Translation.** Knowing the wish of both, the Lord spoke again: "Divide them: first let half be given to Jagannatha, and then let the other half be kept right here."

**Note.** A verse of six padas.

*kūrmaḥ sīdati śeṣa eṣa calitaḥ sarvaiḥ phaṇā-maṇḍalaiḥ
kṣauṇī kṣubhyati bhūbhṛto vidalitā brahmāṇḍam utkhaṇḍitam |
maryādām api sāgaro 'py atigato dudrāva bhāsvān asau
prasthāne mura-vairiṇo vijayino nīlādri-cūḍāmaṇeḥ ||135||*

**Word for word:** *kūrmaḥ* — the Tortoise; *sīdati* — sinks; *śeṣaḥ* — Shesha; *eṣaḥ* — this; *calitaḥ* — shaken; *sarvaiḥ* — with all; *phaṇā* — of hoods; *maṇḍalaiḥ* — with the circles; *kṣauṇī* — the earth; *kṣubhyati* — quakes; *bhūbhṛtaḥ* — the mountains; *vidalitāḥ* — split; *brahmāṇḍam* — the egg of Brahma; *utkhaṇḍitam* — cracked; *maryādām* — the bounds; *api* — even; *sāgaraḥ* — the ocean; *api* — and; *atigataḥ* — has overstepped; *dudrāva* — has fled; *bhāsvān* — the sun; *asau* — yonder; *prasthāne* — at the setting out; *mura-vairiṇaḥ* — of the Enemy of Mura; *vijayinaḥ* — of the victorious; *nīla-adri* — of the Blue Mountain; *cūḍā-maṇeḥ* — of the crest-jewel.

**Translation.** At the setting out of the victorious Enemy of Mura, the crest-jewel of the Blue Mountain, the Tortoise sinks, this Shesha is shaken with all the circles of his hoods, the earth quakes, the mountains are split, the egg of Brahma is cracked, the ocean has overstepped even its bounds, and the sun yonder has taken to flight!

**Note.** The classical hyperbole of "the hero's march". The Tortoise and Shesha uphold the earth. The Enemy of Mura is Krishna.

**Colophon:** इति श्रीकृष्णचैतन्यचरितामृते महाकाव्ये चतुर्दशः सर्गः । (*iti śrī-kṛṣṇa-caitanya-caritāmṛte mahākāvye caturdaśaḥ sargaḥ*) — "Thus [ends] the fourteenth sarga in the mahakavya 'Sri Krishna-Chaitanya-charitamrita'." (The colophon gives no title for the sarga.)
