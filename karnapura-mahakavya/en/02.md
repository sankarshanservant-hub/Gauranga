# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Two — verses 1–122 (printed pp. 12–50)

**Metre:** *vaṁśastha* (12 syllables per pada); verses 120–122 — *viyoginī*.
**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–8 — the glory of Navadvipa and the Ganga. Verses 5–8 form a *kalāpaka*: four verses, one sentence.
- 9–11 — the inhabitants of Navadvipa; Advaita.
- 12–18 — Srivasa, Jagannatha Mishra and Shachi; their eight daughters pass away.
- 19–21 — Vishvarupa.
- 22–24 — Shachi's pregnancy of thirteen months.
- 25–31 — spring comes before its time and adorns herself for the Lord's coming (27–31 are a *kulaka*).
- 32–37 — the full-moon night of Phalguna; the signs before the birth.
- 38–44 — the appearance of the Lord; the eclipse and the cries of "Hari!"; the horoscope.
- 45–55 — the beauty of the world serves each feature of His form.
- 56–59 — Nilambara Chakravarti; the birth rites.
- 60–62 — infancy; the name Vishvambhara.
- 63–67 — childhood play, dancing, first steps.
- 68–79 — Shachi binds Him; Nimai, seated on a heap of pots, teaches His mother non-duality; He throws a clod of earth.
- 80–85 — Shachi falls senseless; the wonder of the two coconuts.
- 86–89 — night: unseen guests, the jingling of anklets from bare feet.
- 90–100 — Vishvarupa takes sannyasa; the parents' grief; Nimai consoles them.
- 101–107 — play with His companions; Jagannatha Mishra's dream.
- 108–112 — Nimai teaches His mother to keep Ekadashi; "I am going" — He falls senseless.
- 113–116 — He comes to Himself; study in the teacher's house.
- 117–122 — Jagannatha Mishra's illness and passing on the bank of the Ganga; Shachi's lament.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
इयं मही भाग्यवती महीयसी
दिवोऽपि दिव्यादपि निर्मलैर्गुणैः ।
महान्ति रत्नानि यदा दधात्यतो
दधौ नवद्वीपमतीव दुर्लभम् ॥१॥
```
*iyaṁ mahī bhāgyavatī mahīyasī
divo 'pi divyād api nirmalair guṇaiḥ |
mahānti ratnāni yadā dadhāty ato
dadhau navadvīpam atīva durlabham ||1||*

**Word for word:** *iyam* — this; *mahī* — earth; *bhāgyavatī* — fortunate; *mahīyasī* — greatest; *divaḥ* — than heaven; *api* — even; *divyāt* — than the heavenly [world]; *api* — even; *nirmalaiḥ* — by pure; *guṇaiḥ* — by qualities; *mahānti* — great; *ratnāni* — jewels; *yadā* — since; *dadhāti* — bears; *ataḥ* — therefore; *dadhau* — bore; *navadvīpam* — Navadvipa; *atīva* — exceedingly; *durlabham* — rare.

**Translation.** This earth is fortunate: by the purity of her qualities she is greater than heaven, greater even than all that is heavenly. Since she is wont to bear great jewels, she therefore bore Navadvipa, a jewel exceedingly rare.

### 2

**2.**

```
अनेकधा सञ्चितभाग्यसञ्चयं
समस्तमेकत्र विधाय सर्वतः ।
महीरुहैरुत्पुलकेयमुत्सुका
दधौ नवद्वीप इति प्रथां किमु ॥२॥
```
*anekadhā sañcita-bhāgya-sañcayaṁ
samastam ekatra vidhāya sarvataḥ |
mahīruhair utpulakeyam utsukā
dadhau navadvīpa iti prathāṁ kimu ||2||*

**Word for word:** *anekadhā* — in many ways; *sañcita* — of accumulated; *bhāgya* — of good fortune; *sañcayam* — the store; *samastam* — entire; *ekatra* — in one place; *vidhāya* — having put; *sarvataḥ* — from everywhere; *mahīruhaiḥ* — with trees; *utpulakā* — with hair standing on end; *iyam* — she; *utsukā* — eager; *dadhau* — took; *navadvīpaḥ* — "Navadvipa"; *iti* — thus; *prathām* — the fame, the name; *kimu* — was it not…?

**Translation.** Was it not because this eager earth, her hair standing on end in the form of trees, gathered from everywhere into one place the whole store of good fortune she had amassed in many ways, that she won the renowned name "Navadvipa"?

**Note.** *Mahīruha*, "that which grows from the earth", is a tree. Trees as the *pulaka* (the hairs that stand on end in ecstasy) on the body of the Earth are a classical image of Sanskrit poetry.

### 3

**3.**

```
प्रभुः कदा वावतरिष्यतीत्यदो
विचिन्तयन्त्या मनसि प्रफुल्लया ।
मनोरथाक्रान्तिवशादनेकशः
सतां पदाब्जानुगतिर्यया दधे ॥३॥
```
*prabhuḥ kadā vāvatariṣyatīty ado
vicintayantyā manasi praphullayā |
manorathākrānti-vaśād anekaśaḥ
satāṁ padābjānugatir yayā dadhe ||3||*

**Word for word:** *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *kadā* — when; *vā* — indeed; *avatariṣyati* — will descend; *iti* — thus; *adaḥ* — this; *vicintayantyā* — by her who was pondering; *manasi* — in the mind; *praphullayā* — by her who was blossoming; *manoratha* — of desire; *ākrānti* — of the onset; *vaśāt* — by the force; *anekaśaḥ* — many times; *satām* — of the saintly; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotuses; *anugatiḥ* — the following (the tread); *yayā* — by whom; *dadhe* — was borne.

**Translation.** "When indeed will the Lord descend?" — so she pondered, blossoming in her heart; and, carried away by the force of this longing, again and again she bore the tread of the lotus feet of the saintly.

**Note.** The earth, awaiting the Lord, "practises" on the saints in the meantime: she receives their steps as a foretaste of His.

### 4

**4.**

```
इयं नवद्वीपमिषेण मेदिनी
दधार भूयो मथुरामिवापराम् ।
वदेदमुष्याः सुकृतानि को नु वा
प्रभोः पदस्पर्शरसाकुलात्मनः ॥४॥
```
*iyaṁ navadvīpa-miṣeṇa medinī
dadhāra bhūyo mathurām ivāparām |
vaded amuṣyāḥ sukṛtāni ko nu vā
prabhoḥ pada-sparśa-rasākulātmanaḥ ||4||*

**Word for word:** *iyam* — this; *navadvīpa* — of Navadvipa; *miṣeṇa* — in the guise; *medinī* — earth; *dadhāra* — bore; *bhūyaḥ* — again; *mathurām* — Mathura; *iva* — as if; *aparām* — another; *vadet* — could tell; *amuṣyāḥ* — of her; *sukṛtāni* — the pious deeds; *kaḥ* — who; *nu vā* — indeed; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *pada* — of the feet; *sparśa* — of the touch; *rasa* — with the delight; *ākula* — overwhelmed; *ātmanaḥ* — [of her] whose soul.

**Translation.** In the guise of Navadvipa this earth bore once again, as it were, a second Mathura. Who indeed could tell the pious deeds of her whose soul is overwhelmed with the delight of the touch of the Lord's feet?

**Note.** Mathura is the birthplace of Krishna; Navadvipa, the birthplace of Gauranga, is a "second Mathura".

### 5

**5.**

```
आप्लाव्य या धूर्जटिसज्जटातटीं
कपालमालाच्छटया समन्विताम् ।
शशाङ्कलेखाप्रतिबिम्बरूपिणी-
मलब्धपूर्वां शफरीं समासदत् ॥५॥
```
*āplāvya yā dhūrjaṭi-saj-jaṭā-taṭīṁ
kapāla-mālā-cchaṭayā samanvitām |
śaśāṅka-lekhā-pratibimba-rūpiṇīm
alabdha-pūrvāṁ śapharīṁ samāsadat ||5||*

**Word for word:** *āplāvya* — having bathed; *yā* — who; *dhūrjaṭi* — of Dhurjati; *sat* — of the beautiful; *jaṭā* — of the matted locks; *taṭīm* — the slope; *kapāla* — of skulls; *mālā* — of the garland; *chaṭayā* — with the gleam; *samanvitām* — endowed; *śaśāṅka* — of the moon; *lekhā* — of the crescent; *pratibimba* — of the reflection; *rūpiṇīm* — having the form; *alabdha* — not obtained; *pūrvām* — before; *śapharīm* — a *śapharī* fish; *samāsadat* — obtained.

**Translation.** She bathed the slope of Shiva's beautiful matted locks, where the garland of skulls gleams, and there caught a little *shaphari* fish such as she had never caught before — the reflection of the crescent moon.

**Note.** *Dhūrjaṭi*, "he who wears heavy matted locks", is a name of Shiva. The Ganga, falling from heaven, passes through Shiva's hair; the crescent moon on his head is reflected in her water and looks like a silvery *śapharī* fish. The subject, "she" (the Ganga), is named outright only in verse 6: verses 5–8 are one sentence.

### 6

**6.**

```
प्रभोः पदाम्भोजयुगस्य पावनी
धारा मनोज्ञा मधुनो महीयसः ।
चकार यत्रास्पदमुत्सुका सती
समन्ततोऽसौ विमलाम्बुवाहिनी ॥६॥
```
*prabhoḥ padāmbhoja-yugasya pāvanī
dhārā manojñā madhuno mahīyasaḥ |
cakāra yatrāspadam utsukā satī
samantato 'sau vimalāmbu-vāhinī ||6||*

**Word for word:** *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *pada* — of the feet; *ambhoja* — of the lotuses; *yugasya* — of the pair; *pāvanī* — purifying; *dhārā* — stream; *manojñā* — charming; *madhunaḥ* — of honey; *mahīyasaḥ* — of great; *cakāra* — made; *yatra* — where; *āspadam* — an abode; *utsukā* — eager; *satī* — being; *samantataḥ* — on all sides; *asau* — she; *vimala* — pure; *ambu* — waters; *vāhinī* — carrying.

**Translation.** She is the purifying, charming stream of the great honey of the Lord's two lotus feet; and here [in Navadvipa], full of eagerness and carrying pure waters, she made her abode, [embracing it] on all sides.

**Note.** According to the Puranas, the Ganga flows from the foot of Vishnu; therefore her water is "the honey of His lotus feet".

---

### 7

**7.**

```
द्रवस्वरूपापि भवाब्धिशोषिणी
शुभ्रापि यासीद्धृतकृष्णविग्रहा ।
क्षित्याश्रितापि द्युनदीति विश्रुता
भ्रमापहापि भ्रमिविभ्रमावहा ॥७॥
```
*drava-svarūpāpi bhavābdhi-śoṣiṇī
śubhrāpi yāsīd dhṛta-kṛṣṇa-vigrahā |
kṣity-āśritāpi dyunadīti viśrutā
bhramāpahāpi bhrami-vibhramāvahā ||7||*

**Word for word:** *drava* — liquid; *svarūpā* — by nature; *api* — though; *bhava* — of worldly existence; *abdhi* — the ocean; *śoṣiṇī* — drying up; *śubhrā* — white; *api* — though; *yā* — who; *āsīt* — was; *dhṛta* — having assumed; *kṛṣṇa* — a dark; *vigrahā* — body; *kṣiti* — on the earth; *āśritā* — resting; *api* — though; *dyu* — heavenly; *nadī* — river; *iti* — thus; *viśrutā* — famed; *bhrama* — delusion; *apahā* — removing; *api* — though; *bhrami* — of whirlpools; *vibhrama* — the play; *āvahā* — bringing.

**Translation.** Though liquid by nature, she dries up the ocean of worldly existence; though white, she has assumed a dark body; though resting on the earth, she is famed as the heavenly river; though she dispels delusion, she brings the whirling play of eddies.

**Note.** The figure of *virodhābhāsa*, "apparent contradiction": each is resolved by a double meaning. *Kṛṣṇa-vigraha* is "a dark body", but also "the body of Krishna": the Ganga issues from the foot of Vishnu. *Bhrama* is "delusion", *bhrami* "whirling, an eddy".

### 8

**8.**

```
सेयं नवद्वीपभुवो महीयसीं
शोभामिवाधाय तदन्तवासिनी ।
प्रभोः पदाम्भोजयुगस्य सौरभ-
प्राप्त्यै बभूवोत्कलिकाकुलीकृता ॥८॥
(चतुर्भिः कलापकम्)
```
*seyaṁ navadvīpa-bhuvo mahīyasīṁ
śobhām ivādhāya tad-anta-vāsinī |
prabhoḥ padāmbhoja-yugasya saurabha-
prāptyai babhūvotkalikākulīkṛtā ||8||*

**Word for word:** *sā* — she; *iyam* — this; *navadvīpa* — of Navadvipa; *bhuvaḥ* — to the land; *mahīyasīm* — the greatest; *śobhām* — beauty; *iva* — as if; *ādhāya* — having imparted; *tat* — of it; *anta* — at the edge; *vāsinī* — dwelling; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *pada* — of the feet; *ambhoja* — of the lotuses; *yugasya* — of the pair; *saurabha* — of the fragrance; *prāptyai* — for the obtaining; *babhūva* — became; *utkalikā* — with waves (with longing); *ākulī-kṛtā* — agitated.

**Translation.** And she, dwelling at the edge of Navadvipa as if to impart the greatest beauty to that land, became all astir with waves of longing to obtain the fragrance of the Lord's two lotus feet.

**Note.** The edition prints «शोभामिवाधायत दन्तवासिनी», which makes no sense. We divide: *iva ādhāya tad-anta-vāsinī*, "as if imparting…, dwelling at its edge". A play on words: *utkalikā* is both "wave" and "longing". *Caturbhiḥ kalāpakam* — "a *kalāpaka* of four [verses]".

### 9

**9.**

```
वसन्ति यत्र क्षितिदेवसत्तमाः
सदा सदाचारपराः परायणाः ।
निरन्तरं वेदविधानकर्म्मसु
श्रुतिस्मृतीनां विधयः शरीरिणः ॥९॥
```
*vasanti yatra kṣiti-deva-sattamāḥ
sadā sad-ācāra-parāḥ parāyaṇāḥ |
nirantaraṁ veda-vidhāna-karmasu
śruti-smṛtīnāṁ vidhayaḥ śarīriṇaḥ ||9||*

**Word for word:** *vasanti* — live; *yatra* — where; *kṣiti* — of the earth; *deva* — gods; *sattamāḥ* — the best; *sadā* — always; *sat* — to good; *ācāra* — conduct; *parāḥ* — devoted; *parāyaṇāḥ* — given over to the Supreme; *nirantaram* — unceasingly; *veda* — by the Vedas; *vidhāna* — enjoined; *karmasu* — in rites; *śruti* — of shruti; *smṛtīnām* — and of smriti; *vidhayaḥ* — the injunctions; *śarīriṇaḥ* — embodied.

**Translation.** There live the best of the brahmanas, the "gods on earth". Ever devoted to righteous conduct, given over to the Supreme, unceasingly [engaged] in the rites enjoined by the Vedas, they are the injunctions of shruti and smriti in bodily form.

**Note.** *Kṣiti-deva* ("god of the earth") is a traditional designation of a brahmana.

### 10

**10.**

```
प्रभावभाजां भिषजां महत्तमाः
स्वधर्म्मनिष्ठाश्च विशां वराः परे ।
प्रतिष्ठया निःसहशुभ्रया सदा
समन्विता यत्र वसन्ति मानवाः ॥१०॥
```
*prabhāva-bhājāṁ bhiṣajāṁ mahattamāḥ
sva-dharma-niṣṭhāś ca viśāṁ varāḥ pare |
pratiṣṭhayā niḥsaha-śubhrayā sadā
samanvitā yatra vasanti mānavāḥ ||10||*

**Word for word:** *prabhāva* — power; *bhājām* — of those possessing; *bhiṣajām* — of physicians; *mahattamāḥ* — the greatest; *sva* — to their own; *dharma* — dharma; *niṣṭhāḥ* — faithful; *ca* — and; *viśām* — of the vaishyas; *varāḥ* — the best; *pare* — others; *pratiṣṭhayā* — with renown; *niḥsaha* — unsurpassed; *śubhrayā* — with bright; *sadā* — always; *samanvitāḥ* — endowed; *yatra* — where; *vasanti* — live; *mānavāḥ* — people.

**Translation.** There live the greatest of able physicians, the best of the vaishyas, faithful to their own dharma, and other people too — all ever endowed with bright, unsurpassed renown.

**Note.** *Niḥsaha*, "irresistible, unsurpassed": so in both editions (Goswami's, GGM, included), so no conjecture is needed. *Bhiṣaj* — physicians, the *vaidya* caste, to which Murari Gupta and Kavi Karnapura himself belonged.

### 11

**11.**

```
यमेतमद्वैतमहाशयः स्वयं
सतां महिम्ना महितो महीयसा ।
अलञ्चकारैतद् यदीयभावतः
प्रभुर्धरण्यां मनुजैर्विलोकितः ॥११॥
```
*yam etam advaita-mahāśayaḥ svayaṁ
satāṁ mahimnā mahito mahīyasā |
alañcakāraitad yadīya-bhāvataḥ
prabhur dharaṇyāṁ manujair vilokitaḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *yam* — which; *etam* — this; *advaita* — Advaita; *mahāśayaḥ* — the great-hearted; *svayam* — Himself; *satām* — by the saintly; *mahimnā* — for the greatness; *mahitaḥ* — honoured; *mahīyasā* — for the supreme; *alañcakāra* — adorned; *etat* — this; *yadīya* — whose; *bhāvataḥ* — through the love; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *dharaṇyām* — on earth; *manujaiḥ* — by people; *vilokitaḥ* — was beheld.

**Translation.** This [town] the great-hearted Advaita Himself adorned, He who is honoured by the saintly for His supreme greatness; and it was through His love that people beheld the Lord on earth.

**Note.** According to tradition, Mahaprabhu descended in answer to the prayers of Advaita Acharya (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Adi 3). The syntax *yam etam … etad* is rough (so too in GGM): the masculine *yam etam* refers to Navadvipa.

### 12

**12.**

```
उवास यत्रानिशमत्युदारधी-
रधीतसर्वागमवेदकोविदः ।
सतां वरिष्ठः परमो महाशयः
श्रीवासनामा द्विजवंशचन्द्रमाः ॥१२॥
```
*uvāsa yatrāniśam atyudāra-dhīr
adhīta-sarvāgama-veda-kovidaḥ |
satāṁ variṣṭhaḥ paramo mahāśayaḥ
śrīvāsa-nāmā dvija-vaṁśa-candramāḥ ||12||*

**Word for word:** *uvāsa* — lived; *yatra* — where; *aniśam* — constantly; *ati* — very; *udāra* — generous; *dhīḥ* — [of] mind; *adhīta* — having studied; *sarva* — all; *āgama* — the agamas; *veda* — the Vedas; *kovidaḥ* — expert; *satām* — of the saintly; *variṣṭhaḥ* — the best; *paramaḥ* — supreme; *mahāśayaḥ* — great-souled; *śrīvāsa* — Srivasa; *nāmā* — by name; *dvija* — of the twice-born; *vaṁśa* — of the race; *candramāḥ* — the moon.

**Translation.** There constantly lived one named Srivasa — exceedingly generous of mind, expert, having studied all the agamas and Vedas, the best of the saintly, supremely great-souled, the moon of the race of the brahmanas.

### 13

**13.**

```
बभौ महावंशसमुद्भवः सुधी-
रनेकविद्याम्बुधिपारपण्डितः ।
द्विजातिवंशैकवतंसवद्यतः
श्रीमान् जगन्नाथ इतीह विश्रुतः ॥१३॥
```
*babhau mahā-vaṁśa-samudbhavaḥ sudhīr
aneka-vidyāmbudhi-pāra-paṇḍitaḥ |
dvijāti-vaṁśaika-vataṁsavad yataḥ
śrīmān jagannātha itīha viśrutaḥ ||13||*

**Word for word:** *babhau* — shone; *mahā* — of a great; *vaṁśa* — family; *samudbhavaḥ* — born; *sudhīḥ* — wise; *aneka* — of many; *vidyā* — branches of learning; *ambudhi* — of the oceans; *pāra* — [gone] to the far shore; *paṇḍitaḥ* — a scholar; *dvijāti* — of the twice-born; *vaṁśa* — of the race; *eka* — the one; *vataṁsa* — crest-ornament; *vat* — like; *yataḥ* — self-controlled; *śrīmān* — illustrious; *jagannāthaḥ* — Jagannatha; *iti* — thus; *iha* — here; *viśrutaḥ* — renowned.

**Translation.** There shone the illustrious one renowned in these parts as Jagannatha. Born of a great family, wise, a scholar who had crossed to the far shore of the oceans of many sciences, self-controlled, he was like the one crest-ornament of the race of the brahmanas.

### 14

**14.**

```
गुणैः समस्तैरयमेव शुद्धधी-
रधीतवेदो वरणीय एव हि ।
इतीह नीलाम्बरचक्रवर्त्तिना
वराय यस्मै सुधिया सुतार्पिता ॥१४॥
```
*guṇaiḥ samastair ayam eva śuddha-dhīr
adhīta-vedo varaṇīya eva hi |
itīha nīlāmbara-cakravartinā
varāya yasmai sudhiyā sutārpitā ||14||*

**Word for word:** *guṇaiḥ* — with qualities; *samastaiḥ* — with all; *ayam* — he; *eva* — alone; *śuddha* — of pure; *dhīḥ* — mind; *adhīta* — having studied; *vedaḥ* — the Vedas; *varaṇīyaḥ* — worthy to be chosen; *eva hi* — truly; *iti* — thus [judging]; *iha* — here; *nīlāmbara* — by Nilambara; *cakravartinā* — Chakravarti; *varāya* — to the bridegroom; *yasmai* — to whom; *sudhiyā* — by the wise; *sutā* — the daughter; *arpitā* — was given.

**Translation.** "He alone — with all virtues, pure of mind, learned in the Vedas — is truly worthy to be chosen": so judged the wise Nilambara Chakravarti, and gave him his daughter in marriage.

### 15

**15.**

```
शचीति नाम्नातिशुचेरचीक्लृपद्-
गुणेन सौशील्यरसेन तेऽनया ।
प्रतिष्ठया शुद्धतमां गरिष्ठतां
शची हि यां नाप पुरन्दरप्रिया ॥१५॥
```
*śacīti nāmnātiśucer acīkḷpad-
guṇena sauśīlya-rasena te 'nayā |
pratiṣṭhayā śuddhatamāṁ gariṣṭhatāṁ
śacī hi yāṁ nāpa purandara-priyā ||15||*

**Word for word:** *śacī* — Shachi; *iti* — thus; *nāmnā* — by name; *atiśuceḥ* — because of great purity; *acīkḷpat* — named; *guṇena* — by virtue; *sauśīlya* — of good nature; *rasena* — by the charm; *te* — ⟨?⟩; *anayā* — by her; *pratiṣṭhayā* — by renown; *śuddhatamām* — the purest; *gariṣṭhatām* — eminence; *śacī* — Shachi; *hi* — indeed; *yām* — which; *na* — not; *āpa* — attained; *purandara* — of Purandara; *priyā* — the beloved.

**Translation.** Because of her great purity she was given the name Shachi. By her virtue, by the charm of her good nature ⟨?⟩ and by her renown [she reached] the purest eminence — one that even Shachi, the beloved of Purandara, did not attain.

**Note.** ⟨?⟩ The first half of the verse is obscure; Goswami's edition (GGM) gives the same text. *Acīkḷpat* is the causative aorist of *kḷp*, "arranged, named"; cf. 2.62: *nāma acīkḷpat*, "gave a name". *Atiśuceḥ* is best taken as ablative, "because of her great purity", with a play on *śuci* ("pure") and *Śacī*. *Te 'nayā* remains unclear, and the text may be corrupt; the translation follows the general sense. The second half is clear. To be checked against another edition.

### 16

**16.**

```
उपेत्य तं मिश्रपुरन्दराह्वयं
निसर्गयोग्यं पदवीमुपाश्रितम् ।
बभौ शची चन्द्रकलेव नित्यशः
शची समासाद्य पुरन्दरं यथा ॥१६॥
```
*upetya taṁ miśra-purandarāhvayaṁ
nisarga-yogyaṁ padavīm upāśritam |
babhau śacī candra-kaleva nityaśaḥ
śacī samāsādya purandaraṁ yathā ||16||*

**Word for word:** *upetya* — having come to; *tam* — him; *miśra* — Mishra; *purandara* — Purandara; *āhvayam* — called; *nisarga* — by nature; *yogyam* — worthy; *padavīm* — the path; *upāśritam* — following; *babhau* — shone; *śacī* — Shachi; *candra* — of the moon; *kalā* — the crescent; *iva* — like; *nityaśaḥ* — day by day; *śacī* — Shachi; *samāsādya* — having obtained; *purandaram* — Purandara; *yathā* — as.

**Translation.** Having come [as wife] to him who was called Mishra Purandara — worthy by nature and walking the [righteous] path — Shachi shone day by day like the crescent of the moon, as Shachi shines on obtaining Purandara.

**Note.** A double play on names: *Purandara* is the second name of Jagannatha Mishra and a name of Indra; *Shachi* is the name of Gauranga's mother and of Indra's consort.

### 17

**17.**

```
तयोर्गृहे संवसतोः सतोः सदा
गृहस्थधर्म्मः सदुदारमासदत् ।
क्रमेण चाष्टौ तनुजाः पुरोऽभवं-
स्तथैव पञ्चत्वमुपाययुश्च ताः ॥१७॥
```
*tayor gṛhe saṁvasatoḥ satoḥ sadā
gṛhastha-dharmaḥ sad-udāram āsadat |
krameṇa cāṣṭau tanujāḥ puro 'bhavaṁs
tathaiva pañcatvam upāyayuś ca tāḥ ||17||*

**Word for word:** *tayoḥ* — of the two; *gṛhe* — in the house; *saṁvasatoḥ* — living together; *satoḥ* — righteous; *sadā* — always; *gṛhastha* — of householders; *dharmaḥ* — the duty; *sat* — well; *udāram* — generously; *āsadat* — was fulfilled; *krameṇa* — one after another; *ca* — and; *aṣṭau* — eight; *tanujāḥ* — daughters; *puraḥ* — first; *abhavan* — were born; *tathā* — in the same way; *eva* — indeed; *pañcatvam* — to the fivefold state; *upāyayuḥ* — went; *ca* — and; *tāḥ* — they.

**Translation.** As the righteous couple lived together, the duty of householders was always fulfilled in their home, well and generously. First eight daughters were born to them one after another — and in the same way, one after another, they went to the fivefold state.

**Note.** *Pañcatvam upāyayuḥ*, "they went to the fivefold state", that is, the body returned to the five elements: a euphemism for passing away. The eight daughters who passed away are mentioned also in the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Adi 13).

### 18

**18.**

```
ततश्च तौ सन्ततमेव दम्पती
बभूवतुर्दुःखितमौ महत्तमौ ।
प्रयत्नमाधाय सुतार्थमीयतुः

```

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *ca* — and; *tau* — those two; *santatam* — constantly; *eva* — indeed; *dampatī* — husband and wife; *babhūvatuḥ* — became; *duḥkhitamau* — most unhappy; *mahattamau* — most noble; *prayatnam* — effort; *ādhāya* — having applied; *suta* — of a son; *artham* — for the sake; *īyatuḥ* — went; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *pada* — of the feet; *abjam* — to the lotus; *śaraṇam* — to the refuge; *kṛpā* — with mercy; *mayam* — filled.

**Translation.** And then that most noble couple became the most unhappy of people, their grief unceasing. With all their effort, for the sake of a son, they went for refuge to the merciful lotus feet of the Lord.

### 19

**19.**

```
ततोऽतिभाग्येन तयोरभूत्सुतः
स विश्वरूपः शुभरूपशोभितः ।
मुदं ययौ सा सुमुखी पिताप्यसौ
व्यडम्बयच्चाधनमात्तसद्वसुम् ॥१९॥
```
*tato 'tibhāgyena tayor abhūt sutaḥ
sa viśvarūpaḥ śubha-rūpa-śobhitaḥ |
mudaṁ yayau sā sumukhī pitāpy asau

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *ati* — by great; *bhāgyena* — by good fortune; *tayoḥ* — to them; *abhūt* — was born; *sutaḥ* — a son; *saḥ* — he; *viśvarūpaḥ* — Vishvarupa; *śubha* — with a beautiful; *rūpa* — with form; *śobhitaḥ* — adorned; *mudam* — joy; *yayau* — attained; *sā* — she; *sumukhī* — fair-faced; *pitā* — the father; *api* — also; *asau* — he; *vyaḍambayat* — resembled; *ca* — and; *adhanam* — a poor man; *ātta* — who has obtained; *sat* — good; *vasum* — wealth.

**Translation.** Then, by great good fortune, a son was born to them — Vishvarupa, adorned with a beautiful form. The fair-faced [mother] rejoiced, and the father became like a poor man who has come into great wealth.

### 20

**20.**

```
स विश्वरूपः शुभरूपगर्वितां
तनुं वहंश्चन्द्र इव प्रकाशवान् ।
निपठ्य कालेन लघीयसाप्यसौ

*sa viśvarūpaḥ śubha-rūpa-garvitāṁ

**21.**

```
शिशुः स आसीद्वयसा लघीयसा

सरस्वतीयं रसनाग्रनर्त्तकी

**Word for word:** *śiśuḥ* — a child; *saḥ* — he; *āsīt* — was; *vayasā* — in age; *laghīyasā* — very young; *sudhīḥ* — wise; *adhīta* — having studied; *āgama* — of the agamas; *veda* — of the Vedas; *sañcayaḥ* — the collection; *sarasvatī* — Sarasvati; *iyam* — this; *rasanā* — of the tongue; *agra* — on the tip; *nartakī* — a dancer; *babhūva* — became; *vaśyā* — obedient; *iva* — as if; *sadā* — always; *asya* — his; *nirbharam* — wholly.

### 22

**22.**

```
ततश्च कालेन शुभेन सुन्दरी
शची विशेषं शुशुभे शुभेक्षणा ।
भविष्यदिन्दूदयशंसिनीं पुरः
पुरन्दराशां सदृशीं चकार सा ॥२२॥
```
*tataś ca kālena śubhena sundarī
śacī viśeṣaṁ śuśubhe śubhekṣaṇā |
bhaviṣyad-indūdaya-śaṁsinīṁ puraḥ
purandarāśāṁ sadṛśīṁ cakāra sā ||22||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *ca* — and; *kālena* — in time; *śubhena* — auspicious; *sundarī* — beautiful; *śacī* — Shachi; *viśeṣam* — especially; *śuśubhe* — shone; *śubha* — beautiful; *īkṣaṇā* — [having] eyes; *bhaviṣyat* — coming; *indu* — of the moon; *udaya* — the rising; *śaṁsinīm* — announcing; *puraḥ* — beforehand; *purandara* — of Purandara; *āśām* — the quarter; *sadṛśīm* — like; *cakāra* — made; *sā* — she.

**Translation.** Then, at an auspicious time, the lovely Shachi of beautiful eyes shone with a special radiance: she made [herself] like the quarter of Purandara [— the east —] which announces beforehand the coming rise of the moon.

**Note.** The east is the quarter of Indra-Purandara. Shachi is the wife of "Purandara" (Mishra), and her radiance before the birth of her son is compared to the eastern sky before moonrise: Gauranga is the "moon" (Gaurachandra).

### 23

**23.**

```
शची सती भाग्यमही महीयसी
सुकुक्षिपीयूषपयोनिधौ मुदा ।
मनोरमां दोहदलक्षणश्रियं
क्षपाकरस्येव नवां कलां दधौ ॥२३॥
```
*śacī satī bhāgya-mahī mahīyasī
su-kukṣi-pīyūṣa-payonidhau mudā |
manoramāṁ dohada-lakṣaṇa-śriyaṁ
kṣapākarasyeva navāṁ kalāṁ dadhau ||23||*

**Word for word:** *śacī* — Shachi; *satī* — virtuous; *bhāgya* — of good fortune; *mahī* — the earth; *mahīyasī* — greatest; *su* — of the beautiful; *kukṣi* — of the womb; *pīyūṣa* — of nectar; *payonidhau* — in the ocean; *mudā* — with joy; *manoramām* — charming; *dohada* — of pregnancy; *lakṣaṇa* — of the signs; *śriyam* — the beauty; *kṣapākarasya* — of the moon; *iva* — like; *navām* — a new; *kalām* — crescent; *dadhau* — bore.

**Translation.** The virtuous Shachi, great as the very earth of good fortune, joyfully bore in the nectar ocean of her lovely womb the charming beauty of the signs of pregnancy, like a new crescent of the moon.

**Note.** *Kṣapākara*, "maker of the night", is the moon. According to the Puranas the moon was born from the churning of the Milk Ocean. Here Shachi's womb is the "ocean of nectar", and the child within it the young crescent.

### 24

**24.**

```
क्रमेण मासा दश ते त्रयोऽधिकाः
समीयुरासन्नतराः समाप्तताम् ।
तपस्यमासश्चरमः सुमङ्गलो
बभूव तेषां जगतः सुखैकभूः ॥२४॥
```

samīyur āsannatarāḥ samāptatām |
tapasya-māsaś caramaḥ sumaṅgalo
babhūva teṣāṁ jagataḥ sukhaika-bhūḥ ||24||*

**Word for word:** *krameṇa* — gradually; *māsāḥ* — months; *daśa* — ten; *te* — those; *trayaḥ* — three; *adhikāḥ* — more; *samīyuḥ* — came; *āsannatarāḥ* — very near; *samāptatām* — to completion; *tapasya* — Tapasya; *māsaḥ* — month; *caramaḥ* — the last; *sumaṅgalaḥ* — most auspicious; *babhūva* — was; *teṣām* — of them; *jagataḥ* — of the world; *sukha* — of happiness; *eka* — the sole; *bhūḥ* — source.

**Translation.** Gradually those months — ten and three more — drew very near to their completion. The last of them was the most auspicious month of Tapasya, the sole source of happiness for the world.

**Note.** *Tapasya* is an old name of the month Phalguna (February–March): Gauranga appeared on the full moon of Phalguna. The thirteen months of pregnancy are a tradition, recorded also in the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Adi 13). The edition has *āsannatarā* without visarga before *s-*; we read *āsannatarāḥ*.

### 25

**25.**

```
असावृतूनां पतिरग्रतस्तदा
प्रभोः प्रकाशो भवितेति हर्षितः ।
स्वकालमुल्लङ्घ्य निजं पदं दधा-

sva-kālam ullaṅghya nijaṁ padaṁ dadhāv

**Word for word:** *asau* — that; *ṛtūnām* — of the seasons; *patiḥ* — lord; *agrataḥ* — ahead of time; *tadā* — then; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *prakāśaḥ* — the manifestation; *bhavitā* — will be; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *harṣitaḥ* — delighted; *sva* — his own; *kālam* — time; *ullaṅghya* — having overstepped; *nijam* — his own; *padam* — foot; *dadhau* — set; *ārtiḥ* — impatience; *tathā* — such; *tat* — in that; *viṣaye* — in matter; *hi* — indeed; *śobhate* — is becoming.

**Note.** *Ārti* usually means "pain, distress"; here it is the aching impatience of expectation.

### 26

**26.**

```
उपैतुकामा सहकारनायकं
नवप्ररोहामवलम्ब्य वीरुधम् ।
क्वणद्भ्रमद्भृङ्गसमूहनूपुरं

**Word for word:** *upaitu* — to approach; *kāmā* — wishing; *sahakāra* — the mango; *nāyakam* — the lover; *nava* — new; *prarohām* — [having] shoots; *avalambya* — leaning on; *vīrudham* — a creeper; *kvaṇat* — humming; *bhramat* — whirling; *bhṛṅga* — of bees; *samūha* — a swarm; *nūpuram* — [having] as an anklet; *vasanta* — of spring; *lakṣmīḥ* — the beauty; *vipine* — in the forest; *padam* — foot; *dadhau* — set.

**Translation.** Wishing to approach her lover, the mango tree, the Beauty of Spring leaned on a creeper with fresh shoots and set foot in the forest; a humming, whirling swarm of bees was her anklet.

**Note.** *Sahakāra* is the fragrant mango. In Sanskrit poetry the mango is the "husband", and the flowering creeper that twines about it his "wife".

**27.**

```
स्ववेशविन्यासमिवाकरोदियं
प्रभोः प्रकाशो भवितेति सन्ततम् ।

**Word for word:** *sva* — her own; *veśa* — of attire; *vinyāsam* — the arrangement; *iva* — as if; *akarot* — made; *iyam* — she; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *prakāśaḥ* — the manifestation; *bhavitā* — will be; *iti* — thus [thinking]; *santatam* — constantly; *vasanta* — of spring; *lakṣmīḥ* — the beauty; *satata* — ever; *utsukā* — eager; *satī* — being; *satī* — a faithful wife; *iva* — like; *kānta* — of the beloved; *āgamane* — at the coming; *śuci* — with a bright; *smitā* — smile.

**28.**

### 29

**29.**

```
तमालमालादलमाधुरीमयं
बबन्ध धम्मिल्लभरं मनोहरम् ।
मधुव्रतालीमयचिल्लिवल्लरीं

**Word for word:** *tamāla* — of tamala trees; *mālā* — of rows; *dala* — of the foliage; *mādhurī* — with the sweetness; *mayam* — filled; *babandha* — bound up; *dhammilla* — of braided hair; *bharam* — the mass; *manoharam* — captivating; *madhuvrata* — of bees; *ālī* — of lines; *maya* — consisting; *cilli* — of the brows; *vallarīm* — the creepers; *pranartayāmāsa* — made dance; *sukham* — happily; *mada* — with intoxication; *alasām* — languid.

**Note.** The tamala is a tree with dark, almost black foliage; in Gaudiya poetry it is a constant image of Krishna's complexion. *Madhuvrata*, "one vowed to honey", is a bee.

**33.**

```
तदा शशाङ्कः परिपूर्णमण्डलः
स पौर्णमासीपरिरम्भहर्षितः ।

**Word for word:** *tadā* — then; *śaśāṅkaḥ* — the moon; *paripūrṇa* — full; *maṇḍalaḥ* — [having] a disc; *saḥ* — he; *paurṇamāsī* — of the Full-moon Night; *parirambha* — by the embrace; *harṣitaḥ* — delighted; *vyarocata* — shone; *atīva* — exceedingly; *jagat* — of the world; *manoramaḥ* — the delight; *cumban* — kissing; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *pūrva* — of the eastern; *dik* — quarter; *aṅganā* — of the fair lady; *mukham* — the face.

**34.**

```
असावृतूनां पतिरग्रतोऽभव-

**35.**

```
वनप्रियास्तत्समये मधून्मदा-
स्तदादि चक्रुः सकलं जयध्वनिम् ।

tadādi lāsyaṁ vidadhur madhuvratāḥ
sa dakṣiṇas tat prathamaṁ vavau marut ||35||*

**Word for word:** *vanapriyāḥ* — the cuckoos; *tat* — at that; *samaye* — time; *madhu* — by spring; *unmadāḥ* — intoxicated; *tadādi* — from then on; *cakruḥ* — raised; *sakalam* — universal; *jaya* — "Victory!"; *dhvanim* — the cry; *tadādi* — from then on; *lāsyam* — dance; *vidadhuḥ* — took up; *madhuvratāḥ* — the bees; *saḥ* — that; *dakṣiṇaḥ* — southern; *tat* — then; *prathamam* — for the first time; *vavau* — blew; *marut* — wind.

### 36

**36.**

```
स निर्भरस्तम्बभरेण मन्थरो
लतां लतां प्रत्युपगूहनैर्नवैः ।
पयोजमाध्वीकनिदाघवारिभृ-

```
*sa nirbhara-stamba-bhareṇa mantharo
latāṁ latāṁ praty-upagūhanair navaiḥ |
payoja-mādhvīka-nidāgha-vāri-bhṛd
vavau marud candana-śaila-nandanaḥ ||36||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — that; *nirbhara* — heavy; *stamba* — of clusters; *bhareṇa* — by the burden; *mantharaḥ* — slowed; *latām latām* — each creeper; *prati* — in turn; *upagūhanaiḥ* — with embraces; *navaiḥ* — fresh; *payoja* — of lotuses; *mādhvīka* — the honeyed nectar; *nidāgha* — of heat; *vāri* — the drops; *bhṛt* — carrying; *vavau* — blew; *marut* — the wind; *candana* — of sandal; *śaila* — of the mountains; *nandanaḥ* — the son.

**Translation.** That wind, the son of the sandal mountains, blew slowly, weighed down by the burden of heavy flower clusters, with fresh embraces for each creeper in turn, carrying the honeyed nectar of lotuses as drops of sweat from the heat.

**37.**

```
ततो जगन्मङ्गलमङ्गलोदये

### 39

**39.**

```
तदोपरागः समभूत्तथा मुहु-

स्वनामसंकीर्त्तनमन्यथा नहि

prakāśa-mātreṇa bhavet prakāśitam ||39||*

**Word for word:** *tadā* — then; *uparāgaḥ* — an eclipse; *samabhūt* — occurred; *tathā* — and; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *harim* — "Hari"; *vada* — say; *iti* — thus; *dhvaniḥ* — the cry; *uccakaiḥ* — loud; *nṛṇām* — of people; *sva* — of His own; *nāma* — of the name; *saṅkīrtanam* — the congregational chanting; *anyathā* — otherwise; *na hi* — not at all; *prakāśa* — by the appearance; *mātreṇa* — by mere; *bhavet* — would be; *prakāśitam* — made manifest.

**Translation.** Then an eclipse took place, and again and again rose the loud cry of the people: "Say 'Hari'!" For otherwise, by His mere appearance, the sankirtana of His own name would not have been made manifest.

**Translation.** By His mere appearance the planets at once became most favourable to Him and stood in high exaltation. The sign was the one called Simha (Leo), and the chief nakshatra was Purva-phalguni.

**Note.** *Tuṅga* in jyotisha is "exaltation", the position in which a planet has its greatest strength. The horoscope of Mahaprabhu is discussed in detail in the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Adi 3, "The Lord's horoscope") and the Chaitanya-charitamrita (Adi 13).

dine dine tad-vayasā samaṁ sukhaṁ
babhūva pitror ati-bhūmim āgatam ||59||*

**Word for word:** *kramāt* — in due course; *atha* — then; *utthāna* — of the rising (going out); *vidhāna* — of the rite; *maṅgalam* — the auspicious [celebration]; *cakāra* — performed; *hṛṣṭaḥ* — joyful; *jagat* — by the world; *eka* — above all; *pūjitaḥ* — honoured; *dine dine* — day by day; *tat* — His; *vayasā* — with the age; *samam* — together; *sukham* — the happiness; *babhūva* — became; *pitroḥ* — of the parents; *ati* — beyond; *bhūmim* — the limit; *āgatam* — gone.

**Translation.** Then in due course [Mishra], joyful and honoured above all by the world, performed the auspicious rite of the "rising". Day by day, together with His age, the happiness of the parents grew until it passed all bounds.

**Note.** *Utthāna*, "rising", is evidently the rite in which the infant is first brought out of the lying-in chamber (*sūtikā-gṛha*).

**61.**

```

### 62

**62.**

```
भविष्यतीदं निजकीर्त्तनादिभि-
र्विलासलावण्यसुधामयैर्जगत् ।

**Word for word:** *bhaviṣyati* — will be [filled]; *idam* — this; *nija* — with His own; *kīrtana* — with kirtana; *ādibhiḥ* — and the rest; *vilāsa* — of pastimes; *lāvaṇya* — of loveliness; *sudhā* — with nectar; *mayaiḥ* — full; *jagat* — world; *iti* — thus; *iva* — as if [thinking]; *viśvambharaḥ* — "Vishvambhara"; *iti* — thus; *udāra* — generous; *dhīḥ* — [whose] mind; *acīkḷpat* — gave; *nāma* — the name; *manorama* — lovely; *āśayaḥ* — [whose] intentions.

**Note.** *Viśvam-bhara* — "He who bears, sustains and fills the universe". The edition, like GGM, has *भविष्यति* ("will be"), and the phrase is elliptical. The reading *भरिष्यति* ("will fill") would explain the name directly, but the edition does not support it.

**63.**

```
प्रतप्तकार्त्तस्वरशैलभासुर-

**64.**

```

```
झणज्झणत्कारमनोज्ञकङ्कण-

**66.**

```

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *eṣaḥ* — He; *kālena* — in time; *śanaiḥ śanaiḥ* — little by little; *kṣitau* — on the earth; *pada* — of the foot; *aravindam* — the lotus; *madhu* — of honey; *mādhurī* — with the sweetness; *mayam* — full; *nyasyan* — placing; *amuṣyāḥ* — her; *cira* — from long; *viprayoga* — separation; *jam* — born; *jahāra* — took away; *tāpam* — the burning distress; *karuṇā* — of mercy; *payonidhiḥ* — the ocean.

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *tām* — her; *ittham* — thus; *asau* — He; *ruṣā* — with anger; *anvitaḥ* — filled; *babhañja* — broke; *bhāṇḍāni* — pots; *bahūni* — many; *santatam* — one after another; *tam* — Him; *īdṛśam* — such; *tatra* — there; *vilokya* — having seen; *sā* — she; *śacī* — Shachi; *babandha* — bound; *bhītā* — frightened; *svayam* — herself; *api* — even; *ati* — quite; *sphuṭam* — openly.

**70.**

```

sa eka ātmaiva sadāvaśiṣyate
tad-anyad etat sakalaṁ hi vibhramaḥ ||74||*

**Word for word:** *yataḥ* — since; *hi* — indeed; *anānātve* — in the absence of multiplicity; *iha* — here; *etat* — of this; *ātmanaḥ* — of the Self; *ghaṭeta* — is fitting; *na eva* — not at all; *idam* — "this"; *aham* — "I"; *mama* — "mine"; *iti* — thus; *api* — even; *saḥ* — that; *ekaḥ* — one; *ātmā* — Self; *eva* — alone; *sadā* — always; *avaśiṣyate* — remains; *tat* — from it; *anyat* — other; *etat* — this; *sakalam* — all; *hi* — indeed; *vibhramaḥ* — delusion.

**Translation.** "For since there is no multiplicity here in this Self, there is no room at all even for 'this', 'I' and 'mine'. That one Self alone always remains; all this, which is other than it, is delusion."

**Note.** This is the teaching of Advaita Vedanta (absolute non-dualism). The Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition does not share it, and later Chaitanya Himself refutes it in His debates with the Mayavadis (Chaitanya-charitamrita, Madhya 6, 25). Here the child is playing the sage: the Chaitanya-bhagavata (Adi 7) and Murari state plainly that He taught "in the mood of Dattatreya". The comic side of the scene: a little boy sitting on scraps of leftover food lectures His mother that purity does not exist.

paridevanayānayā muhur
bahudhā netra-jalair asecayat ||120||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *sā* — she; *pati* — of the husband; *pāda* — of the feet; *paṅkaja* — of the lotuses; *dvayam* — the pair; *āliṅgya* — having embraced; *sa* — with; *gadgada* — faltering; *svaram* — [with] voice; *paridevanayā* — with lament; *anayā* — with this; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *bahudhā* — abundantly; *netra* — of the eyes; *jalaiḥ* — with the waters; *asecayat* — bathed.

**Translation.** Then she [Shachi] embraced her husband's two lotus feet and, in a faltering voice, with this lament, again and again bathed them abundantly with the water of her eyes:

**Note.** From verse 120 the metre changes to *viyoginī*: by the custom of the mahakavya, the closing verses of a sarga are written in a different metre.

### 121

**121.**

```
अपि मां परिहाय दुःखिता-
मतिदीनां कुररीमिव प्रभो ।
क्व नु सम्प्रति यासि नीयतां
निजदासी बहुदुःखकर्षिता ॥१२१॥
```
*api māṁ parihāya duḥkhitām
ati-dīnāṁ kurarīm iva prabho |
kva nu samprati yāsi nīyatāṁ
nija-dāsī bahu-duḥkha-karṣitā ||121||*

**Word for word:** *api* — can it be that; *mām* — me; *parihāya* — leaving; *duḥkhitām* — grieving; *ati* — utterly; *dīnām* — forlorn; *kurarīm* — an osprey; *iva* — like; *prabho* — O lord; *kva* — where; *nu* — then; *samprati* — now; *yāsi* — are you going; *nīyatām* — let be taken; *nija* — your own; *dāsī* — maidservant; *bahu* — by many; *duḥkha* — sorrows; *karṣitā* — worn down.

**Translation.** "O my lord, will you really leave me — grieving, utterly forlorn, like a *kurari* bird? Where are you going now? Take with you your own maidservant, worn down by many sorrows."

**Note.** *Kurarī* is a bird of prey (the osprey); in Sanskrit poetry it is the symbol of a plaintive, lonely cry.

### 122

**122.**

```
दिवि देवगणे निरन्तरं
सुमनोवर्षिणि भूरिशः सुखात् ।
भुवि कीर्त्तनतत्परे जने
द्युनदीमध्यगतः स निर्ववौ ॥१२२॥
```
*divi deva-gaṇe nirantaraṁ
sumano-varṣiṇi bhūriśaḥ sukhāt |
bhuvi kīrtana-tatpare jane
dyunadī-madhya-gataḥ sa nirvavau ||122||*

**Word for word:** *divi* — in the sky; *deva* — of gods; *gaṇe* — the host [being]; *nirantaram* — unceasingly; *sumanaḥ* — flowers; *varṣiṇi* — raining; *bhūriśaḥ* — abundantly; *sukhāt* — from joy; *bhuvi* — on the earth; *kīrtana* — to kirtana; *tatpare* — devoted; *jane* — the people [being]; *dyunadī* — of the heavenly river; *madhya* — in the midst; *gataḥ* — situated; *saḥ* — he; *nirvavau* — passed away (lit. was extinguished).

**Translation.** While in the sky the hosts of gods, out of joy, rained down flowers abundantly and unceasingly, and on earth the people were absorbed in kirtana, he, standing in the midst of the heavenly river, passed away.

**Note.** *Nir-vā*, "to go out, to be extinguished", that is, to pass away. To leave the body in the waters of the Ganga amid kirtana is held to be a blessed end; hence the gods' rain of flowers.

*Iti śrī-caitanya-caritāmṛte mahākāvye dvitīyaḥ sargaḥ* — Thus ends the second sarga of the mahakavya "Sri Chaitanya-charitamrita".
