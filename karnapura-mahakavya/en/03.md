# Kavi Karnapura. Sri Chaitanya-charita-mahakavya
## Sarga Three — verses 1–144 (printed pp. 51–96)

**Metre:** *upajāti* (11 syllables per pada) and *vaṁśastha* (12 syllables) in alternation; verses 143–144 — *praharṣiṇī*.
**Parallel:** Chaitanya-bhagavata, Adi 10 ("The Lord's marriage to Lakshmipriya").

**Contents of the passage:**
- 1–4 — Gauranga's youth; His teachers.
- 5–11 — the meeting with Lakshmipriya on the way to the Ganga.
- 12–24 — the match: Shachi keeps silent, Nimai reproaches His mother, Vanamali obtains her consent.
- 25–39 — Vanamali at the house of Vallabhacharya; the praise of the bridegroom; the consent of the bride's father.
- 40–49 — the preparations: the fixing of the date, the *adhivasa*, the *nandimukha-shraddha*, music and the cries of "ulu-ulu".
- 50–58 — Shachi, being a widow, cannot perform the rites; Nimai weeps for His father and consoles His mother.
- 59–62 — the bride is adorned; the procession with the palanquin to Vallabha's house.
- 63–70 — the wedding rite: the *varana*, the exchange of flowers and the first glance, the giving away of the bride, *arghya* and *madhuparka*.
- 71–74 — the return home; Shachi welcomes the newly-weds.
- 75–81 — married life; a description of Lakshmipriya's beauty (*nakha-śikha*, "from the toenails to the crown of the head").
- 82–95 — the journey to East Bengal: the people are enchanted by Him; the river Padmavati; He teaches there for several months.
- 96–101 — at home Lakshmipriya serves Shachi and pines for her husband; the snakebite.
- 102–105 — the physicians are powerless; on the bank of the Ganga she leaves her body.
- 106–118 — Shachi's lament over her daughter-in-law; the funeral rites.
- 119–125 — Nimai returns and consoles His mother: "She was not merely a human being."
- 126–144 — the second marriage: the matchmaking of Kashinatha, the consent of Sanatana Mishra, Vishnupriya; the *adhivasa*, the procession, the rite.

Format and conventions as in Sarga One.

### 1

**1.**

```
नवीनलावण्यसुधाम्बुधारा-
भृता नवीनेन सदङ्गकेन ।
तं यौवराज्ये सकलस्य यूनः
प्रसूनचापोऽभिषिषेच भूयः ॥१॥
```
*navīna-lāvaṇya-sudhāmbu-dhārā-
bhṛtā navīnena sad-aṅgakena |
taṁ yauvarājye sakalasya yūnaḥ
prasūna-cāpo 'bhiṣiṣeca bhūyaḥ ||1||*

**Word for word:** *navīna* — of fresh; *lāvaṇya* — of loveliness; *sudhā* — of nectar; *ambu* — of the water; *dhārā* — with streams; *bhṛtā* — filled; *navīnena* — with youthful; *sat* — beautiful; *aṅgakena* — with the body; *tam* — Him; *yauvarājye* — to the rank of crown prince (to the kingdom of youth); *sakalasya* — of all; *yūnaḥ* — of young men; *prasūna* — of flowers; *cāpaḥ* — [he whose] bow [is]; *abhiṣiṣeca* — anointed; *bhūyaḥ* — again.

**Translation.** With His youthful and beautiful body, filled with streams of the nectar-water of fresh loveliness, the god of the flower bow anointed Him once more as crown prince — sovereign of youth over all young men.

**Note.** "The god of the flower bow" is Kama, the god of love. A play on words: *yauvarājya* is "the rank of heir to the throne", but also "the kingdom of youth" (*yuvan*, a young man). The body full of streams of loveliness is itself, as it were, the vessel of the anointing.

### 2

**2.**

```
पपाठ सत्पण्डितविष्णुनाम्नः
सुदर्शनादप्यतिहर्षभाजः ।
गुरुत्वमाकल्प्य महानुकम्पां
चकार हर्षादनयोः किमेषः ॥२॥
```
*papāṭha sat-paṇḍita-viṣṇu-nāmnaḥ
sudarśanād apy ati-harṣa-bhājaḥ |
gurutvam ākalpya mahānukampāṁ
cakāra harṣād anayoḥ kim eṣaḥ ||2||*

**Word for word:** *papāṭha* — studied; *sat* — of the worthy; *paṇḍita* — of the pandita; *viṣṇu* — Vishnu; *nāmnaḥ* — by name; *sudarśanāt* — from Sudarshana; *api* — also; *ati* — of great; *harṣa* — joy; *bhājaḥ* — [from him] partaking; *gurutvam* — the position of teacher; *ākalpya* — having conferred; *mahā* — great; *anukampām* — kindness; *cakāra* — did; *harṣāt* — out of joy; *anayoḥ* — to these two; *kim* — was it not that; *eṣaḥ* — He.

**Translation.** He studied under the worthy pandita named Vishnu and also under Sudarshana, who was filled with great joy. Was it not out of joy that He did these two a great kindness, granting them the position of His teachers?

**Note.** There are two teachers here, Vishnu and Sudarshana.

### 3

**3.**

```
ततश्च वैयाकरणात्स गङ्गा-
दासादभूत्प्रत्यनुभूतविद्यः ।
यदेष विद्यामददाद्द्विजेभ्य-
स्तेनैव पुण्येन पपाठ सोऽत्र ॥३॥
```
*tataś ca vaiyākaraṇāt sa gaṅgā-
dāsād abhūt pratyanubhūta-vidyaḥ |
yad eṣa vidyām adadād dvijebhyas
tenaiva puṇyena papāṭha so 'tra ||3||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *ca* — and; *vaiyākaraṇāt* — from the grammarian; *saḥ* — He; *gaṅgādāsāt* — from Gangadasa; *abhūt* — became; *pratyanubhūta* — [one by whom] was fully realized; *vidyaḥ* — learning; *yat* — because; *eṣaḥ* — that [teacher]; *vidyām* — knowledge; *adadāt* — used to give; *dvijebhyaḥ* — to the brahmanas; *tena* — by that; *eva* — very; *puṇyena* — by the merit; *papāṭha* — studied; *saḥ* — He; *atra* — here (with him).

**Translation.** Then He mastered learning under the grammarian Gangadasa. Because that teacher used to give knowledge to the brahmanas, it was by that very merit [of his] that the Lord studied with him.

**Note.** Gangadasa Pandita was Nimai's principal teacher of grammar; all the biographies mention him.

### 4

**4.**

```
सतीर्थवृन्दैः परिहासवद्भि-
र्हसन् विशेषं सवदावदेन ।
ततान लीलाप्रतिभानवार्त्ता-
मुर्वीसदुर्वीसुरवंशरत्नम् ॥४॥
```
*satīrtha-vṛndaiḥ parihāsavadbhir
hasan viśeṣaṁ sa-vadāvadena |
tatāna līlā-pratibhāna-vārttām
urvī-sad-urvīsura-vaṁśa-ratnam ||4||*

**Word for word:** *satīrtha* — of fellow students; *vṛndaiḥ* — with throngs; *parihāsavadbhiḥ* — with jesting; *hasan* — laughing; *viśeṣam* — especially; *sa* — with; *vadāvadena* — eloquence; *tatāna* — spread; *līlā* — of playful; *pratibhāna* — wit; *vārttām* — talk; *urvī* — on the earth; *sat* — dwelling; *urvīsura* — of the brahmanas; *vaṁśa* — of the line; *ratnam* — the jewel.

**Translation.** Laughing with throngs of jesting fellow students, and with especial eloquence, He — the jewel of the line of the brahmanas who dwell on the earth — spread talk full of playful wit.

**Note.** *Urvī-sura*, "god of the earth", is a brahmana (cf. *kṣiti-deva*, 2.9).

### 5

**5.**

```
कदाचनासौ वनमालिनाम्नो
गृहे सदाचार्यवरस्य नाथः ।
जगाम सम्भाषरसेन हर्षा-
द्यदृच्छया श्रीमयगौरदेहः ॥५॥
```
*kadācanāsau vanamāli-nāmno
gṛhe sad-ācārya-varasya nāthaḥ |
jagāma sambhāṣa-rasena harṣād
yadṛcchayā śrī-maya-gaura-dehaḥ ||5||*

**Word for word:** *kadācana* — once; *asau* — He; *vanamāli* — Vanamali; *nāmnaḥ* — by name; *gṛhe* — to the house; *sat* — of the worthy; *ācārya* — of the acharya; *varasya* — of the excellent; *nāthaḥ* — the Lord; *jagāma* — went; *sambhāṣa* — of conversation; *rasena* — for the relish; *harṣāt* — with joy; *yadṛcchayā* — by chance; *śrī* — beauty; *maya* — full of; *gaura* — golden; *dehaḥ* — [whose] body.

**Translation.** Once the Lord, whose golden body was full of beauty, went by chance, joyfully, to the house of the worthy and excellent acharya named Vanamali, for the relish of conversation.

### 6

**6.**

```
निवर्त्तमानेन ततः सुखेन
संभाष्य तं वर्त्मनि तेन तत्र ।
अकारि पीयूषमिव क्षरन्ती
नेत्रातिथिः काचनहेमवल्ली ॥६॥
```
*nivartamānena tataḥ sukhena
saṁbhāṣya taṁ vartmani tena tatra |
akāri pīyūṣam iva kṣarantī
netrātithiḥ kācana hema-vallī ||6||*

**Word for word:** *nivartamānena* — by [Him] returning; *tataḥ* — then; *sukhena* — happily; *saṁbhāṣya* — having conversed; *tam* — with him; *vartmani* — on the road; *tena* — by Him; *tatra* — there; *akāri* — was made; *pīyūṣam* — nectar; *iva* — as if; *kṣarantī* — pouring forth; *netra* — of the eyes; *atithiḥ* — the guest; *kā cana* — a certain; *hema* — golden; *vallī* — creeper.

**Translation.** Having conversed with him, He was returning happily, and there on the road He made a certain golden creeper, pouring forth nectar, as it were, the guest of His eyes.

### 7

**7.**

```
सा वल्लभाचार्यसुता चलन्ती
स्नातुं सखीभिः सुरदीर्घिकायाम् ।
लक्ष्मीरनेनैव सहावतीर्णा
प्रभोर्ययौ लोचनवर्त्म तत्र ॥७॥
```
*sā vallabhācārya-sutā calantī
snātuṁ sakhībhiḥ sura-dīrghikāyām |
lakṣmīr anenaiva sahāvatīrṇā
prabhor yayau locana-vartma tatra ||7||*

**Word for word:** *sā* — she; *vallabha* — of Vallabha; *ācārya* — of the acharya; *sutā* — the daughter; *calantī* — going; *snātum* — to bathe; *sakhībhiḥ* — with her friends; *sura* — of the gods; *dīrghikāyām* — in the pond; *lakṣmīḥ* — Lakshmi; *anena* — with Him; *eva* — indeed; *saha* — together; *avatīrṇā* — descended; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *yayau* — came; *locana* — of the eyes; *vartma* — into the path; *tatra* — there.

**Translation.** She was the daughter of Vallabhacharya — Lakshmi herself, who had descended together with Him. Going with her friends to bathe in the river of the gods, she there came into the path of the Lord's eyes.

**Note.** *Sura-dīrghikā*, "the pond of the gods", is the Ganga. This scene of the meeting by the river is found also in the Chaitanya-bhagavata, Adi 10.

### 8

**8.**

```
विलोक्य स प्राक्तनवल्लभां तां
सुखाम्बुधौ मज्जनमाततान ।
नैसर्गिकं प्रेम यथावकाशं
प्रसह्य नामोदयतीह कं वा ॥८॥
```
*vilokya sa prāktana-vallabhāṁ tāṁ
sukhāmbudhau majjanam ātatāna |
naisargikaṁ prema yathāvakāśaṁ
prasahya nāmodayatīha kaṁ vā ||8||*

**Word for word:** *vilokya* — having seen; *saḥ* — He; *prāktana* — of old; *vallabhām* — beloved; *tām* — her; *sukha* — of happiness; *ambudhau* — in the ocean; *majjanam* — immersion; *ātatāna* — performed; *naisargikam* — natural; *prema* — love; *yathāvakāśam* — as occasion offers; *prasahya* — forcibly; *na* — not; *āmodayati* — delights; *iha* — here; *kam* — whom; *vā* — indeed.

**Translation.** Seeing her, His beloved of old, He plunged into the ocean of happiness. Whom indeed in this world does natural love not overpower with delight when the occasion offers?

**Note.** *Prāktana*, "former, of old": Lakshmipriya is the eternal consort of the Lord, Lakshmi.

### 9

**9.**

```
तथाविधां तामवलोक्य रामां
मनस्यभूदुल्लसितः कृपाब्धिः ।
मणिं विना दुर्लभमाभिराम्यं
न हैमनी हारलता प्रयाति ॥९॥
```
*tathā-vidhāṁ tām avalokya rāmāṁ
manasy abhūd ullasitaḥ kṛpābdhiḥ |
maṇiṁ vinā durlabham ābhirāmyaṁ
na haimanī hāra-latā prayāti ||9||*

**Word for word:** *tathāvidhām* — such; *tām* — her; *avalokya* — having seen; *rāmām* — lovely woman; *manasi* — in the heart; *abhūt* — became; *ullasitaḥ* — joyful; *kṛpā* — of mercy; *abdhiḥ* — the ocean; *maṇim* — a gem; *vinā* — without; *durlabham* — rare; *ābhirāmyam* — charm; *na* — not; *haimanī* — golden; *hāra* — of a necklace; *latā* — the strand; *prayāti* — attains.

**Translation.** Seeing such a lovely woman, the ocean of mercy rejoiced in His heart. Without a gem, a golden necklace strand does not attain a rare charm.

### 10

**10.**

```
सा शैशवादेकपदेन बाला
समागता यौवनसीम्नि किञ्चित् ।
परित्रुटच्चापलजायमान-
त्रपा तमालोक्य ननन्द शश्वत् ॥१०॥
```
*sā śaiśavād eka-padena bālā
samāgatā yauvana-sīmni kiñcit |
parituṭac-cāpala-jāyamāna-
trapā tam ālokya nananda śaśvat ||10||*

**Word for word:** *sā* — she; *śaiśavāt* — from childhood; *eka* — with one; *padena* — with a step; *bālā* — the girl; *samāgatā* — having come; *yauvana* — of youth; *sīmni* — to the border; *kiñcit* — a little; *paritruṭat* — breaking off; *cāpala* — [whose] restlessness; *jāyamāna* — being born; *trapā* — [whose] shyness; *tam* — Him; *ālokya* — having seen; *nananda* — rejoiced; *śaśvat* — continually.

**Translation.** The girl had come with a single step from childhood a little way over the border of youth: her childish restlessness was breaking off and shyness was being born. Seeing Him, she rejoiced continually.

### 11

**11.**

```
अथाजगामैष निरीक्ष्य कान्तां
तैस्तैर्वयस्यैर्विहरंस्तथैव ।
पठन्सदोद्ग्राहपरः परेशो
रराज गूढस्थिररम्यलीलः ॥११॥
```
*athājagāmaiṣa nirīkṣya kāntāṁ
tais tair vayasyair viharaṁs tathaiva |
paṭhan sadodgrāha-paraḥ pareśo
rarāja gūḍha-sthira-ramya-līlaḥ ||11||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *ājagāma* — returned; *eṣaḥ* — He; *nirīkṣya* — having gazed at; *kāntām* — the beloved; *taiḥ taiḥ* — with those [same]; *vayasyaiḥ* — with friends; *viharan* — sporting; *tathā eva* — just as before; *paṭhan* — studying; *sadā* — always; *udgrāha* — on going over [lessons]; *paraḥ* — intent; *pareśaḥ* — the Supreme Lord; *rarāja* — shone; *gūḍha* — hidden; *sthira* — steady; *ramya* — charming; *līlaḥ* — [whose] play.

**Translation.** Having gazed at His beloved, He returned, sporting just as before with those friends of His. Studying, ever intent on going over His lessons, the Supreme Lord shone, His charming play hidden and steady.

### 12

**12.**

```
अथापरेद्युर्वनमालिनामा
प्रभोः य आचार्य उपेत्य वेश्म ।
नमश्चकार प्रणतो महात्मा
शचीं शुचिः संकथयन्विधिज्ञः ॥१२॥
```
*athāparedyur vanamāli-nāmā
prabhoḥ ya ācārya upetya veśma |
namaś cakāra praṇato mahātmā
śacīṁ śuciḥ saṅkathayan vidhi-jñaḥ ||12||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *aparedyuḥ* — on the next day; *vanamāli* — Vanamali; *nāmā* — by name; *prabhoḥ* — of the Lord; *yaḥ* — who; *ācāryaḥ* — the acharya; *upetya* — having come to; *veśma* — the house; *namaḥ cakāra* — paid homage; *praṇataḥ* — bowing down; *mahātmā* — the great soul; *śacīm* — to Shachi; *śuciḥ* — pure; *saṅkathayan* — conversing; *vidhi* — the proper rules; *jñaḥ* — knowing.

**Translation.** Then on the next day the acharya named Vanamali — a great soul, pure and versed in the proper rules of conduct — came to the Lord's house, bowed low and paid homage to Shachi, and entered into conversation [with her].

**Note.** Vanamali Acharya acts as the matchmaker (*ghaṭaka*), as in the Chaitanya-bhagavata, Adi 10.

### 13

**13.**

```
सुताय ते देवि वृतास्ति काचित्
कन्यातिधन्या गुणरूपशीलैः ।
सा वल्लभाचार्यसुता वराङ्गी
मूर्त्तेव लक्ष्मीः क्षितितोऽवतीर्णा ॥१३॥
```
*sutāya te devi vṛtāsti kācit
kanyāti-dhanyā guṇa-rūpa-śīlaiḥ |
sā vallabhācārya-sutā varāṅgī
mūrteva lakṣmīḥ kṣitito 'vatīrṇā ||13||*

**Word for word:** *sutāya* — for the son; *te* — your; *devi* — O lady; *vṛtā* — chosen; *asti* — there is; *kācit* — a certain; *kanyā* — maiden; *ati* — very; *dhanyā* — blessed; *guṇa* — with qualities; *rūpa* — with beauty; *śīlaiḥ* — and with character; *sā* — she; *vallabha* — of Vallabha; *ācārya* — of the acharya; *sutā* — the daughter; *varāṅgī* — fair of form; *mūrtā* — embodied; *iva* — like; *lakṣmīḥ* — Lakshmi; *kṣititaḥ* — to the earth ⟨?⟩; *avatīrṇā* — descended.

**Translation.** "O lady, for your son a certain maiden has been chosen, most blessed in qualities, beauty and character: that fair daughter of Vallabhacharya, like Lakshmi embodied, descended to the earth."

**Note.** *Vṛtā* ("chosen") can be understood in two ways: "chosen [as a bride] for your son", or "[the maiden] who has chosen your son". ⟨?⟩ *Kṣititaḥ* is an ablative ("from the earth"); the sense requires *kṣitau* ("on, to the earth"). The text is the same in Goswami's edition (GGM).

### 14

**14.**

```
विधीयतां तत्र लघुप्रयत्न-
स्तनूजरत्नस्य विवाहकार्य्ये ।
यदीच्छसि श्रीमति तां सदङ्गां
श्रियं वधूरत्नमनिन्द्यशीलाम् ॥१४॥
```
*vidhīyatāṁ tatra laghu-prayatnas
tanūja-ratnasya vivāha-kārye |
yadīcchasi śrīmati tāṁ sad-aṅgāṁ
śriyaṁ vadhū-ratnam anindya-śīlām ||14||*

**Word for word:** *vidhīyatām* — let be made; *tatra* — in that; *laghu* — slight; *prayatnaḥ* — effort; *tanūja* — of the son; *ratnasya* — of the jewel; *vivāha* — of the marriage; *kārye* — in the matter; *yadi* — if; *icchasi* — you desire; *śrīmati* — O glorious lady; *tām* — her; *sat* — beautiful; *aṅgām* — [whose] limbs; *śriyam* — Lakshmi; *vadhū* — of a bride; *ratnam* — a jewel; *anindya* — blameless; *śīlām* — [whose] character.

**Translation.** "If you desire, O glorious lady, that Lakshmi of lovely limbs, a jewel of a bride of blameless character, then let but a slight effort be made in the matter of the marriage of your jewel of a son."

### 15

**15.**

```
इत्यस्य संश्रुत्य वचोऽमृतं सा
तूष्णीमभून्नैव किमप्युवाच ।
अश्रद्दधाना वचनेऽस्य तस्मिन्
सुतेऽपि तल्लक्षणलक्षणार्था ॥१५॥
```
*ity asya saṁśrutya vaco 'mṛtaṁ sā
tūṣṇīm abhūn naiva kim apy uvāca |
aśraddadhānā vacane 'sya tasmin
sute 'pi tal-lakṣaṇa-lakṣaṇārthā ||15||*

**21.**

```

### 24

**24.**

```

### 32

**32.**

```
यदास्यचन्द्रं विधिना विधाय तं

```

**34.**

```

**35.**

```
भाग्यातिभाग्येन महानुभाव मे

**36.**

```
यदीश्वरः स्यान्मयि सुप्रसादभाक्
भाग्योदयो वा यदि मे महान् भवेत् ।

### 37

**37.**

```
यथा गुणैः काञ्चनहारवल्ली

**Word for word:** *yathā* — as; *guṇaiḥ* — by threads; *kāñcana* — of gold; *hāra* — of a necklace; *vallī* — the strand; *ratnena* — by a gem; *sat* — fine; *nāyakatām* — the place of the central stone; *gatena* — having taken; *niṣpannatām* — completeness; *yāti* — attains; *tathā* — so; *tvadīyaiḥ* — by your; *guṇaiḥ* — by virtues; *tayoḥ* — of the two; *saṅghaṭanā* — the union; *ghaṭeta* — may come about.

### 38

**38.**

```
इत्यूचिवांस्तां विनयोक्तिवत्तया

**Word for word:** *iti* — thus; *ūcivān* — having spoken; *tām* — with that; *vinaya* — modest; *ukti* — of speech; *vattayā* — by the quality; *tayā* — by that; *mahā* — greatly; *prītaḥ* — pleased; *imam* — to him; *jagāda* — said; *saḥ* — he; *tava* — your; *īdṛśā* — by such; *sat* — true; *vinayena* — by humility; *satvaram* — quickly; *sampatsyate* — will come to pass; *sarvam* — all; *aśeṣa* — complete; *maṅgalam* — auspiciousness.

**39.**

इत्थं स संभाष्य मिथो द्विजाग्र्यो

**42.**

**46.**

rejus tadā te sakalā mahāntaḥ

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *dvijebhyaḥ* — to the brahmanas; *pradaduḥ* — they gave; *prakāmam* — abundantly; *tāmbūla* — betel; *mālyāni* — garlands; *api* — and; *candanāni* — sandal; *rejuḥ* — shone; *tadā* — then; *te* — they; *sakalāḥ* — all; *mahāntaḥ* — the venerable; *smera* — smiling; *ānanāḥ* — [with] faces; *harṣa* — of joy; *samudra* — in the ocean; *magnāḥ* — immersed.

**Translation.** Then they gave the brahmanas betel, garlands and sandal in abundance, and all those venerable men shone with smiling faces, immersed in an ocean of joy.

**47.**

```
स वल्लभोऽभ्येत्य तदा सदुर्व्वी-
गीर्व्वाणरत्नैर्द्विजसुन्दरीभिः ।
महाप्रभोर्गन्धसुगन्धिमाल्यैः

*sa vallabho 'bhyetya tadā sad-urvī-
gīrvāṇa-ratnair dvija-sundarībhiḥ |
mahāprabhor gandha-sugandhi-mālyaiḥ
śubhādhivāsaṁ vidadhe vidhi-jñaḥ ||47||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — that; *vallabhaḥ* — Vallabha; *abhyetya* — having come; *tadā* — then; *sat* — worthy; *urvī* — of the earth; *gīrvāṇa* — of the gods; *ratnaiḥ* — with the jewels; *dvija* — of the brahmanas; *sundarībhiḥ* — with the beautiful wives; *mahāprabhoḥ* — of Mahaprabhu; *gandha* — with fragrances; *sugandhi* — sweet-scented; *mālyaiḥ* — with garlands; *śubha* — the auspicious; *adhivāsam* — adhivasa; *vidadhe* — performed; *vidhi* — rites; *jñaḥ* — knowing.

**Translation.** Then Vallabha, the knower of rites, came with the jewels among the worthy gods of the earth and with the beautiful wives of the brahmanas, and performed the auspicious adhivasa of Mahaprabhu with fragrances and sweet-scented garlands.

**Note.** *Gīrvāṇa* ("god"), like *urvīsura*, here means a brahmana.

### 48

**48.**

```
अथ प्रभाते विमलार्कभूषिते
स्वयं कृतस्नानविधिर्यथाविधि ।
प्रभुः पितॄनर्च्चयितुं यथा तथा
नान्दीमुखश्राद्धमथाकरोदसौ ॥४८॥
```
*atha prabhāte vimalārka-bhūṣite
svayaṁ kṛta-snāna-vidhir yathā-vidhi |
prabhuḥ pitṝn arcayituṁ yathā tathā
nāndīmukha-śrāddham athākarod asau ||48||*

**Word for word:** *atha* — then; *prabhāte* — in the morning; *vimala* — by the spotless; *arka* — sun; *bhūṣite* — adorned; *svayam* — Himself; *kṛta* — having performed; *snāna* — of bathing; *vidhiḥ* — the rite; *yathāvidhi* — according to the rules; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *pitṝn* — the forefathers; *arcayitum* — to honour; *yathā tathā* — as is fitting; *nāndīmukha* — the nandimukha; *śrāddham* — shraddha; *atha* — then; *akarot* — performed; *asau* — He.

**Translation.** Then, in the morning adorned by the spotless sun, the Lord Himself performed His bath according to the rules, and then, to honour the forefathers as is fitting, He performed the nandimukha shraddha.

**Note.** *Nāndīmukha-śrāddha* — a rite in honour of the forefathers performed before a joyful occasion, a wedding among others.

### 49

**49.**

```
ततो द्विजातिश्रुतिपाठनादै-
र्मृदङ्गनादैः पणवस्वनैश्च ।
वराङ्गनावक्त्रविनिर्गतैस्तै-
रुलूलशब्दैस्तुमुलो महोऽभूत् ॥४९॥
```
*tato dvijāti-śruti-pāṭha-nādair
mṛdaṅga-nādaiḥ paṇava-svanaiś ca |
varāṅganā-vaktra-vinirgatais tair
ulūla-śabdais tumulo maho 'bhūt ||49||*

**Word for word:** *tataḥ* — then; *dvijāti* — of the brahmanas; *śruti* — of the Vedas; *pāṭha* — of the recitation; *nādaiḥ* — with the sounds; *mṛdaṅga* — of mridangas; *nādaiḥ* — with the sounds; *paṇava* — of panava drums; *svanaiḥ* — with the roar; *ca* — and; *vara* — of lovely; *aṅganā* — women; *vaktra* — from the mouths; *vinirgataiḥ* — issuing; *taiḥ* — with those; *ulūla* — of "ulu-ulu"; *śabdaiḥ* — with the sounds; *tumulaḥ* — tumultuous; *mahaḥ* — festival; *abhūt* — there was.

**Translation.** Then there was a tumultuous festival: with the sound of the brahmanas reciting the Vedas, with the sound of mridangas and the roar of panava drums, and with those cries of "ulu-ulu" issuing from the mouths of lovely women.

**Note.** *Ulūla* — the jubilant cry of women (*ulu-dhvani*), without which no Bengali wedding takes place to this day.

### 50

**50.**

```
शची द्विजानां महिला यथायथं
तत्तत्सपर्य्याग्रहिलास्तदावदत् ।
अलं मया भर्त्तृपदाब्जहीनया
कर्त्तव्यमेतद्भवतीभिरेव हि ॥५०॥
```
*śacī dvijānāṁ mahilā yathā-yathaṁ
tat-tat-saparyā-grahilās tadāvadat |
alaṁ mayā bhartṛ-padābja-hīnayā
kartavyam etad bhavatībhir eva hi ||50||*

**Word for word:** *śacī* — Shachi; *dvijānām* — of the brahmanas; *mahilāḥ* — to the wives; *yathāyatham* — each in her turn; *tat-tat* — for those various; *saparyā* — rites of worship; *grahilāḥ* — eager; *tadā* — then; *avadat* — said; *alam* — enough; *mayā* — with me; *bhartṛ* — of the husband; *pada* — of the feet; *abja* — of the lotuses; *hīnayā* — bereft; *kartavyam* — to be done; *etat* — this; *bhavatībhiḥ* — by you; *eva* — yourselves; *hi* — indeed.

**Translation.** Then Shachi said to the wives of the brahmanas, each of whom was eager to perform those various rites of worship: "It is not for me, bereft of my husband's lotus feet. This must be done by you yourselves."

**Note.** By custom a widow takes no part in the auspicious wedding rites. A bitter detail amid the festivity: at her son's wedding Shachi stands aside. From verse 50 the metre is *vaṁśastha*.

### 51

**51.**

```
स मातुरित्थं करुणोदितं प्रभु-
र्निशम्य तातस्मृतिदुःखविह्वलः ।
मुक्ताफलस्थूलविलोचनाम्भसां

```
*sa mātur itthaṁ karuṇoditaṁ prabhur
niśamya tāta-smṛti-duḥkha-vihvalaḥ |
muktāphala-sthūla-vilocanāmbhasāṁ
bindūn uvāha pravaroru-vakṣasi ||51||*

**Word for word:** *saḥ* — that; *mātuḥ* — of the mother; *ittham* — thus; *karuṇa* — piteously; *uditam* — spoken; *prabhuḥ* — the Lord; *niśamya* — having heard; *tāta* — of the father; *smṛti* — at the memory; *duḥkha* — with grief; *vihvalaḥ* — overwhelmed; *muktāphala* — [like] pearls; *sthūla* — large; *vilocana* — of the eyes; *ambhasām* — of the water; *bindūn* — drops; *uvāha* — bore; *pravara* — splendid; *uru* — broad; *vakṣasi* — on the chest.

### 52

**52.**

```
तथाविधं तत्समये विलोक्य सा
सुतं सुदीनाह सहाङ्गनागणैः ।
पितः कथं मङ्गलकर्म्म कुर्वता
विमुच्यते वारि दृशोरमङ्गलम् ॥५२॥
```
*tathā-vidhaṁ tat-samaye vilokya sā
sutaṁ su-dīnāha sahāṅganā-gaṇaiḥ |
pitaḥ kathaṁ maṅgala-karma kurvatā
vimucyate vāri dṛśor amaṅgalam ||52||*

**Word for word:** *tathāvidham* — such; *tat* — at that; *samaye* — time; *vilokya* — having seen; *sā* — she; *sutam* — the son; *su* — very; *dīnā* — sad; *āha* — said; *saha* — together with; *aṅganā* — of women; *gaṇaiḥ* — with the crowd; *pitaḥ* — O father (i.e. dear son); *katham* — how; *maṅgala* — an auspicious; *karma* — rite; *kurvatā* — by one performing; *vimucyate* — is shed; *vāri* — water; *dṛśoḥ* — from the eyes; *amaṅgalam* — inauspicious.

**Translation.** Seeing her son like this at that time, [Shachi,] very sad herself, said, together with the crowd of women: "Dear son, how can You, while performing an auspicious rite, shed tears from Your eyes? It is inauspicious!"

**Note.** *Pitaḥ*, literally "O father": the affectionate address of a Bengali mother to her son (Beng. *bābā*, "daddy").

### 53

**53.**

```
स मातुरित्थं वचनेन नाथो
द्राघीयसा निश्वसितेन तेन ।

**54.**

```
धनानि किम्वा मनुजा न सन्ति मे

### 55

**55.**

```
त्वयैव दृष्टं द्विजसज्जनेभ्यः
प्रकाममृक्थं रभसाद्विकीर्णम् ।

### 56

**56.**

```
पित्रापि हीनोऽहमकुण्ठशक्तिः
किं मातरित्थं पुरतो ममोक्तम् ।
अमर्त्यकार्य्येषु सदैव शक्ता-

```

kiṁ mātar itthaṁ purato mamoktam |
amartya-kāryeṣu sadaiva śaktās
tathāpi yal laukikam eva kurmaḥ ||56||*

**Word for word:** *pitrā* — of the father; *api* — though; *hīnaḥ* — bereft; *aham* — I; *akuṇṭha* — unimpaired; *śaktiḥ* — [with] power; *kim* — why; *mātaḥ* — O Mother; *ittham* — thus; *purataḥ* — before; *mama* — Me; *uktam* — spoken; *amartya* — of immortals; *kāryeṣu* — in the deeds; *sadā* — always; *eva* — indeed; *śaktāḥ* — capable; *tathā api* — yet; *yat* — that which; *laukikam* — worldly; *eva* — only; *kurmaḥ* — We do.

**Translation.** "Though I am bereft of My father, My power is unimpaired. Why, O Mother, do you speak thus before Me? We are always capable even of the deeds of immortals — and yet We do only what is customary in the world."

**Note.** The Lord half-reveals His divinity: "We are capable of the deeds of immortals." The plural is the majestic "We".

**57.**

**58.**

**59.**

### 62

```

**63.**

```

**Word for word:** *pādya* — with water for the feet; *ādinā* — and the rest; *tam* — Him; *varayāmbabhūva* — chose [as bridegroom]; *saḥ* — that; *dvijaḥ* — brahmana; *navadvīpa* — of Navadvipa; *mahā* — the great; *oṣadhī* — of herbs; *īśvaram* — lord; *babhau* — shone; *vṛtaḥ* — chosen; *tena* — by him; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *tadā* — then; *trailokya* — of the three worlds; *lakṣmī* — with the beauty; *lalitām* — lovely; *tanum* — a body; *śritaḥ* — having assumed.

**64.**

**66.**

### 67

**67.**

```
परस्परं तौ सुमनःसमूहौ
विचक्रतुः प्रेमरसेन सार्द्धम् ।
तयोरभिक्षा सममाविरासी-
त्तदैव चित्रा शशिनोरिवासौ ॥६७॥
```
*parasparaṁ tau sumanaḥ-samūhau
vicakratuḥ prema-rasena sārdham |
tayor abhīkṣā samam āvirāsīt
tadaiva citrā śaśinor ivāsau ||67||*

**Word for word:** *parasparam* — at each other; *tau* — the two; *sumanaḥ* — of flowers; *samūhau* — handfuls; *vicakratuḥ* — cast; *prema* — of love; *rasena* — with the rapture; *sārdham* — together with; *tayoḥ* — their; *abhīkṣā* — glance; *samam* — at once; *āvirāsīt* — appeared; *tadā eva* — at that very moment; *citrā* — Chitra; *śaśinoḥ* — of the moon ⟨?⟩; *iva* — as; *asau* — that.

**Translation.** With the rapture of love the two cast handfuls of flowers at each other — and at that very moment their glances appeared together, as [the meeting] of Chitra and the moon.

**Note.** The exchange of flowers and the first glance of bride and groom (Beng. *śubha-dṛṣṭi*, "the auspicious glance") are rites of the Bengali wedding. ⟨?⟩ The edition has *abhikṣā*; we read *abhīkṣā* ("glance"). The dual *śaśinoḥ* is unclear; the text is the same in the Gosvami edition (GGM). Possibly: "that [glance] of the two moons", i.e. bride and groom are two moons, and the wondrous (*citrā*) glance between them is like the bright star Chitra.

**68.**

**71.**

**Word for word:** *viśvambharaḥ* — Vishvambhara; *viśva* — to all; *janāya* — to the people; *kautukam* — delight; *vikīrya* — having scattered; *viśva* — of the world; *ārti* — of the sorrows; *bharaiḥ* — ⟨?⟩ (read *haraiḥ* — with [the acts] removing); *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *lakṣmīm* — Lakshmi; *samādāya* — having taken; *śarīriṇīm* — embodied; *śriyam* — the beauty; *saundarya* — of loveliness; *sārasya* — of the essence; *jagāma* — went; *veśmani* — home.

**76.**

lāvaṇya-sindhoḥ kimu koka-śāvakau
manoja-dantāvala-kumbhakau kimu ||77||*

**Word for word:** *uraḥ* — of the bosom; *taḍāgasya* — of the pond; *kim* — are they?; *abja* — of the lotus; *korakau* — two buds; *manohare* — charming; *hāra* — of the necklace; *latā* — of the creeper; *phale* — two fruits; *kimu* — or; *lāvaṇya* — of loveliness; *sindhoḥ* — of the ocean; *kimu* — or; *koka* — of the chakravaka; *śāvakau* — two chicks; *manoja* — of the god of love; *dantāvala* — of the elephant; *kumbhakau* — the two frontal globes; *kimu* — or.

**Translation.** [Her young breasts —] are they two lotus buds in the pond of her bosom? Or two charming fruits on the creeper of her necklace? Or two chakravaka chicks in the ocean of loveliness? Or the two frontal globes of the elephant of the god of love?

**Note.** Verses 77–80 are the description of the heroine's beauty (*nakha-śikha*) traditional in a mahakavya; all the comparisons are conventional images of Sanskrit poetry. *Koka* — the chakravaka, the ruddy shelduck; a pair of these birds is the emblem of conjugal love.

**80.**

```

**Word for word:** *daivāt* — by fate; *atho* — and then; *mandira* — of the house; *madhyam* — into the interior; *āgataḥ* — having come; *cakṣuḥ* — with the eyes; *śravāḥ* — hearing (a serpent); *krūrataraḥ* — most cruel; *su* — utterly; *pāmaraḥ* — vile; *vadhvāḥ* — of the daughter-in-law; *padam* — the foot; *śārada* — of an autumn; *padma* — of a lotus; *saurabham* — having the fragrance; *bheje* — seized; *kaṭhoraiḥ* — with hard; *daśanaiḥ* — with fangs; *kaṭhora* — hard; *dhīḥ* — [whose] mind.

**Word for word:** *gataḥ* — has gone; *sutaḥ* — son; *me* — my; *bhavatīm* — you; *samarpya* — having entrusted; *priyaḥ* — beloved; *tava* — your; *asau* — he; *mayi* — to me; *duḥkha* — sorrow; *bhāji* — to [one] who has; *hīnā* — deprived; *tvayā* — of you; *tasya* — his; *mukham* — face; *katham* — how; *vā* — then; *drakṣyāmi* — shall I see; *duḥkha* — of sorrow; *eka* — sole; *nivāsa* — of dwelling; *bhūmiḥ* — the place.

**Translation.** "My son, your beloved, went away having entrusted you to me, the sorrowful one. How, then, deprived of you, shall I see His face — I, now the dwelling-place of sorrow alone?"

**108.**

```
त्वया कृता प्रीतिरतीव गौरवं

### 109

**109.**

```
आहूतमात्रैव मया ददासि

**111.**

```
असौ तव प्राणपतिः प्रियङ्करो

**113.**

```
या त्वं त्रपायै मयि साध्वसाय

**114.**

```

**Word for word:** *sā* — that one; *tvam* — you; *tadīya* — his; *āsya* — of the face; *sudhā* — of nectar; *mayūkham* — [the moon] with rays; *tava* — of your; *eva* — own; *cetaḥ* — of the heart; *kumuda* — of the night-lily; *eka* — the only; *kāntam* — beloved; *kaṭhora* — hard; *citte* — O you whose heart [is]; *tam* — him; *avīkṣya* — without seeing; *sākṣāt* — face to face; *katham* — how; *kutaḥ* — whither; *vā* — and; *vrajasi* — are you going; *prasahya* — forcibly (suddenly).

**115.**

```

```

**Word for word:** *yat* — which; *aṅgam* — body; *etat* — this; *kusumaiḥ* — by flowers; *sudūyate* — is hurt; *bāṣpa* — of breath; *ūṣmaṇā* — by the warmth; *ca api* — and even; *śirīṣa* — [like the flower of] the *śirīṣa*; *komalam* — tender; *katham* — how; *nu vā* — then; *te* — your; *asahata* — endured; *ati* — utterly; *duḥsaham* — unbearable; *viṣa* — of poison; *agni* — of the fire; *tejaḥ* — the heat; *tat* — that; *idam* — this; *hatā* — struck down; *asmi* — I am; *tat* — therefore.

```

tadīya-netra-dvaya-nirbharodgataiḥ
payobhir ākhyātam iva sākṣāt ||123||*

**Word for word:** *ittham* — thus; *samastam* — everything; *bubudhe* — understood; *mahāprabhuḥ* — Mahaprabhu; *tat* — that; *api* — although; *anuktam* — unspoken; *sahasā* — at once; *hasan* — smiling; *muhuḥ* — again and again; *tadīya* — her; *netra* — of eyes; *dvaya* — of the pair; *nirbhara* — abundantly; *udgataiḥ* — welling up; *payobhiḥ* — by the waters; *ākhyātam* — told; *iva* — as if; *sākṣāt* — plainly.

**Translation.** Thus Mahaprabhu, smiling again and again, understood everything at once, though it was left unspoken: it was as if the waters welling up abundantly from her two eyes had told it plainly.

**Note.** Mahaprabhu's smile here is not indifference but knowledge: for Him Lakshmipriya's departure is no loss (vv. 124–125). ⟨?⟩ In the edition the last pada is a syllable short of the metre; the Gosvami edition (GGM) reads *ākhyānitam eva*, which also does not give the full metre.

**139.**

```
ऊचुश्च साधो विजयस्व साधु
साधुर्विवाहस्य बभूव कालः ।
इत्थं निशम्यारचयत्कृपालु-
र्यात्रां समारुह्य मनोज्ञदोलाम् ॥१३९॥
```
*ūcuś ca sādho vijayasva sādhu
sādhur vivāhasya babhūva kālaḥ |
itthaṁ niśamyāracayat kṛpālur
yātrāṁ samāruhya manojña-dolām ||139||*

**Word for word:** *ūcuḥ* — said; *ca* — and; *sādho* — O virtuous One; *vijayasva* — be victorious; *sādhu* — well; *sādhuḥ* — auspicious; *vivāhasya* — of the wedding; *babhūva* — has come; *kālaḥ* — the time; *ittham* — thus; *niśamya* — having heard; *aracayat* — made; *kṛpāluḥ* — the merciful one; *yātrām* — the procession; *samāruhya* — having mounted; *manojña* — a charming; *dolām* — palanquin.

**Translation.** And they said: "O virtuous One, may You be victorious! It is well: the auspicious time for the wedding has come." Hearing this, the merciful Lord mounted a charming palanquin and set out in procession.

### 140

**140.**

```
सन्तप्तचामीकरगौरदेहो
दोलामुपेतः शरदभ्रशुभ्राम् ।
दुग्धाम्बुराशेरुपरि प्ररूढं
शृङ्गं सुमेरोः स जिगाय सद्यः ॥१४०॥
```
*santapta-cāmīkara-gaura-deho
dolām upetaḥ śarad-abhra-śubhrām |
dugdhāmbu-rāśer upari prarūḍhaṁ
śṛṅgaṁ sumeroḥ sa jigāya sadyaḥ ||140||*

**Word for word:** *santapta* — of heated; *cāmīkara* — gold; *gaura* — golden; *dehaḥ* — [He whose] body [was]; *dolām* — the palanquin; *upetaḥ* — having taken; *śarat* — of autumn; *abhra* — [like] a cloud; *śubhrām* — white; *dugdha* — of milk; *amburāśeḥ* — of the ocean; *upari* — above; *prarūḍham* — risen; *śṛṅgam* — the peak; *sumeroḥ* — of Sumeru; *saḥ* — He; *jigāya* — surpassed; *sadyaḥ* — at once.

**Translation.** His body golden as molten gold, seated in a palanquin white as an autumn cloud, He at once surpassed the peak of Sumeru rising above the Milk Ocean.

**Note.** The image: Gauranga's golden body in the white palanquin is like the golden mountain Sumeru above the white Milk Ocean.

### 141

**141.**

```
जामातरं वीक्ष्य समीपमागतं
प्रोद्गम्य हर्षेण तनूरुहैः समम् ।
पाद्यासनाद्यैर्वरयाम्बभूव
क्षणेन कन्याञ्च ददौ स कूकुदः ॥१४१॥
```
*jāmātaraṁ vīkṣya samīpam āgataṁ
prodgamya harṣeṇa tanūruhaiḥ samam |
pādyāsanādyair varayāmbabhūva
kṣaṇena kanyāñ ca dadau sa kūkudaḥ ||141||*

**Word for word:** *jāmātaram* — the son-in-law; *vīkṣya* — seeing; *samīpam* — near; *āgatam* — arrived; *prodgamya* — having risen to meet; *harṣeṇa* — with joy; *tanūruhaiḥ* — with the hairs of the body; *samam* — together; *pādya* — with water for the feet; *āsana* — with a seat; *ādyaiḥ* — and the rest; *varayāmbabhūva* — chose [as bridegroom]; *kṣaṇena* — in a moment; *kanyām* — the daughter; *ca* — and; *dadau* — gave; *saḥ* — he; *kūkudaḥ* — the giver of the daughter.

**Translation.** Seeing the son-in-law arrive near, he joyfully rose to meet Him — and the hairs of his body rose together with him; with water for the feet, a seat and the rest he performed the choosing of the bridegroom, and in a moment the giver of the bride gave away his daughter.

**Note.** *Prodgamya* is a play on words: Sanatana "rose" to meet the bridegroom, and his hairs "rose" from joy. The choosing of the bridegroom is the *varaṇa*. *Kūkuda* is a rare word: "one who gives an adorned daughter in marriage".

### 142

**142.**

```
द्विजस्त्रियः स्वस्तिकधूपदीपै-
रमुष्य निर्मञ्छनमादरेण ।
चक्रुः समानीय ततः स कन्यां
प्रादाद्द्विजस्तस्य पदाम्बुजेभ्यः ॥१४२॥
```
*dvija-striyaḥ svastika-dhūpa-dīpair
amuṣya nirmañchanam ādareṇa |
cakruḥ samānīya tataḥ sa kanyāṁ
prādād dvijas tasya padāmbujebhyaḥ ||142||*

**Word for word:** *dvija* — of the brahmanas; *striyaḥ* — the wives; *svastika* — with svastika signs; *dhūpa* — with incense; *dīpaiḥ* — with lamps; *amuṣya* — for Him; *nirmañchanam* — the rite of protection; *ādareṇa* — with reverence; *cakruḥ* — performed; *samānīya* — having brought; *tataḥ* — then; *saḥ* — that; *kanyām* — daughter; *prādāt* — gave; *dvijaḥ* — brahmana; *tasya* — His; *pada* — of the feet; *ambujebhyaḥ* — to the lotuses.

**Translation.** The wives of the brahmanas reverently performed for Him the rite of protection with svastika signs, incense and lamps; then the brahmana brought his daughter and gave her to His lotus feet.

**Note.** *Nirmañchana* is the rite in which lamps and incense are waved around the bridegroom to ward off the evil eye.

### 143

**143.**

```
उन्मीलत्पटुपटहप्रकृष्टढक्का-
निस्वानैः स्फुटरटितैश्च मर्द्दलानाम् ।
श्रीमद्भिर्जयनिनदैः प्रसूनवृष्ट्या
रेजाते स्मितसुमुखौ परस्परं तौ ॥१४३॥
```
*unmīlat-paṭu-paṭaha-prakṛṣṭa-ḍhakkā-
nisvānaiḥ sphuṭa-raṭitaiś ca mardalānām |
śrīmadbhir jaya-ninadaiḥ prasūna-vṛṣṭyā
rejāte smita-sumukhau parasparaṁ tau ||143||*

**Word for word:** *unmīlat* — breaking forth; *paṭu* — of clear-toned; *paṭaha* — of pataha drums; *prakṛṣṭa* — of mighty; *ḍhakkā* — of dhakka drums; *nisvānaiḥ* — with the roar; *sphuṭa* — with distinct; *raṭitaiḥ* — with the beating; *ca* — and; *mardalānām* — of mardalas; *śrīmadbhiḥ* — with splendid; *jaya* — of "Jaya!"; *ninadaiḥ* — with cries; *prasūna* — of flowers; *vṛṣṭyā* — with a rain; *rejāte* — shone; *smita* — smiling; *sumukhau* — [both] with lovely faces; *parasparam* — at each other; *tau* — the two.

**Translation.** Amid the roar, breaking forth, of clear-toned pataha drums and mighty dhakkas, the distinct beating of the mardalas, splendid cries of "Jaya!" and a rain of flowers, the two shone, smiling at each other with lovely faces.

**Note.** The metre changes to *praharṣiṇī* (13 syllables) for the closing verses of the sarga. Pataha, dhakka and mardala are kinds of Bengali drums.

### 144

**144.**

```
इत्येवं गृहमनयद्वधूं महद्भि-
र्वादित्रध्वनिसहितैर्जयध्वनैश्च ।
सा हृष्टा सपदि निवेशयाञ्चकार
स्त्रीरत्नं मुदितमनाः शची स्वगेहम् ॥१४४॥
```
*ity evaṁ gṛham anayad vadhūṁ mahadbhir
vāditra-dhvani-sahitair jaya-dhvanaiś ca |
sā hṛṣṭā sapadi niveśayāñcakāra
strī-ratnaṁ mudita-manāḥ śacī sva-geham ||144||*

**Word for word:** *iti evam* — thus; *gṛham* — home; *anayat* — brought; *vadhūm* — the bride; *mahadbhiḥ* — loud; *vāditra* — of instruments; *dhvani* — sounds; *sahitaiḥ* — accompanied by; *jaya* — of "Jaya!"; *dhvanaiḥ* — with cries; *ca* — and; *sā* — she; *hṛṣṭā* — joyful; *sapadi* — at once; *niveśayāñcakāra* — led in; *strī* — among women; *ratnam* — the jewel; *mudita* — delighted; *manāḥ* — [with] a heart; *śacī* — Shachi; *sva* — her own; *geham* — into the house.

**Translation.** Thus, with cries of "Jaya!" accompanied by the loud sounds of instruments, He brought the bride home; and Shachi, joyful, her heart delighted, at once led that jewel among women into her house.
