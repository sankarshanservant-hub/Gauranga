# Chapter 15. Śrī Vaikuṇṭhapur, Śrī Rudradwīp, and Śrī Pulina

**1.** All glory to Śrī Gaurāṅga, the Pañcha Tattva, and the abode of Śrī Gaurāṅga, Śrī Nabadwīp!

**2.** Arriving in Śrī Vaikuṇṭhapur, Nityānanda Prabhu smiled gently and spoke to Śrī Jīva.

**3.** “Please listen carefully. Here, on one side of the eight-petalled lotus of Nabadwīp, is Vaikuṇṭhapur.

**4.** “Śrī Vaikuṇṭha, the spiritual world, the abode of Nārāyaṇ, is situated beyond the Virajā River. This is its position.

**5.** “Māyā never enters there, where Nārāyaṇ is served by the śrī, bhū, and līlā energies.

**6.** “The effulgence of this spiritual land is Brahma. With fleshy eyes, everyone sees it to be material.

**7.** “Nārad once saw this pure, eternal abode of Nārāyaṇ with his spiritual eyes.

**8.** “He saw Nārāyaṇ, and then saw Him as Gaurāṅgasundar. Thereafter, Nārad, the best of the sages, stayed here for some time.

**9.** “There is another ancient, confidential subject. Once, Rāmānuja Āchārya came to Jagannāth Kṣetra.

**10.** “He pleased Lord Jagannāth with numerous prayers, and Jagannāth mercifully came before him.

**11.** “Coming before him, the Lord said, ‘Visit Nabadwīp Dhām.

**12.** “‘Very soon, I will appear in Nadia in the home of Jagannāth Miśra.

**13.** “‘Nabadwīp is My dearmost abode. The spiritual world of Vaikuṇṭha is situated within one portion of it.

**14.** “‘You are My eternal servant, and foremost amongst My devotees. You must see Nabadwīp Dhām.

**15.** “‘Let your disciples remain here immersed in dāsya-rasa. You must go.

**16.** “‘O wise Rāmānuja! The birth of those who attain a body but do not see Nabadwīp is meaningless.

**17.** “‘Śrī Raṅgam, Śrī Veṅkaṭa, and Yādavāchal are simply minor parts of Nabadwīp.

**18.** “‘Therefore, O Rāmānuja, go to Nabadwīp and see the form of Gaurāṅga.

**19.** “‘You came to the earth to preach devotion. May your birth be successful by the mercy of Gaura.

**20.** “‘See Nabadwīp and then go to Kūrma Sthān. You will meet your disciples there.’

**21.** “Hearing this, Rāmānuja Āchārya joined his palms and prayed to Jagannāth.

**22.** “‘O Lord, by Your mercy I have now heard about Gaurāṅga, but I do not know who He is.’

**23.** “Jagannāth mercifully replied to Rāmānuja, ‘Everyone knows that Kṛṣṇa is the Lord of Goloka.

**24.** “‘Lord Nārāyaṇ is a form Kṛṣṇa assumes to perform Pastimes. Kṛṣṇa is the supreme being, and His abode is Vṛndāvan.

**25.** “‘Kṛṣṇa also exists fully and eternally as Gaurahari, and Vṛndāvan also exists fully and eternally as Nabadwīp.

**26.** “‘In Nabadwīp, I am eternally present as Gaurāṅgasundar. Nabadwīp is the best abode in the world.

**27.** “‘By My grace, Nabadwīp Dhām is present on the earth, and all the scriptures say that there is no trace of māyā there.

**28.** “‘If you think that Nabadwīp is degraded because it is present on the earth, then your devotion will diminish day by day.

**29.** “‘By My will, My inconceivable energy has manifested this spiritual abode within the realm of Māyā.

**30.** “The scriptures do not ascertain truth that is above reason. Only devotees understand it, by My grace.’

**31.** “Hearing the words of Jagannāth, the sober Rāmānuja became overwhelmed with divine love for Śrī Gaurāṅga.

**32.** “He said, ‘O Lord, Your Pastimes are extremely astonishing. The Vedic scriptures do not know Your glories.

**33.** “‘O Lord Jagannāth, why did the scriptures not specifically describe the Pastimes of Śrī Gaurāṅga?

**34.** “‘Only when I look at the Vedas, Purāṇas, and other scriptures in a deeper way do I find revelation of Gaurāṅga.

**35.** “‘Receiving Your order, I have left behind my doubts. The ecstasy of the Pastimes of Gaura has arisen in my heart.

**36.** “‘If You order me, I will go to Nabadwīp and then preach about the Pastimes of Gaura throughout the three worlds.

**37.** “‘I will reveal hidden scriptures, enlighten everyone, and make every soul within the three worlds a devotee of Gaura! Please speak.’

**38.** “Seeing the eagerness of Rāmānuja, Jagannāth said, ‘O Rāmānuja, do not speak this way.

**39.** “‘My Pastimes as Gaura are very confidential; keep them secret. Only after they are unmanifest will everyone receive them.

**40.** “‘Preach dāsya-rasa, and always worship Gaura within your heart.’

**41.** “Receiving this instruction, Rāmānuja Āchārya secretly came to Nabadwīp.

**42–43.** “So that the Pastimes of Gaura were not manifest prematurely, Nārāyaṇ took Rāmānuja and kept him in Śrī Vaikuṇṭhapur. Seeing this place, Rāmānuja was charmed.

**44.** “Nārāyaṇ, who is served by His śrī, bhū, and līlā energies, then very mercifully revealed Himself to Rāmānuja.

**45.** “Seeing His worshippable Lord, Rāmānuja felt himself to be most fortunate.

**46.** “Rāmānuja then suddenly saw the beautiful, enchanting, golden form of the son of Jagannāth Miśra.

**47.** “Rāmānuja fainted in the lustre of the Lord’s form, and Śrī Gaura put His feet on Rāmānuja’s head.

**48.** “With divine knowledge, Rāmānuja then prayed, ‘May I behold Your manifest Pastimes in Nadia.’

**49.** “Saying this, Rāmānuja cried with divine love. Then he said, ‘I will not leave Nabadwīp.’

**50.** “Mercifully, Gaurahari said, ‘O Rāmānuja, your desires will be fulfilled.

**51.** “‘When My Pastimes in Nadia manifest, you will be born again in Nabadwīp.’

**52.** “Saying this, Gaurahari disappeared. Satisfied, Rāmānuja departed.

**53.** “Eventually, Rāmānuja arrived in Kūrma Sthān and met his disci - ples there.

**54.** “He proceeded south and preached about dāsya-rasa while thinking of Nabadwīp and Śrī Gaurāṅga within His heart.

**55.** “By the mercy of Gaurāṅga, Rāmānuja took birth in this eternal abode as Śrī Ananta Āchārya.”

**56.** The party then went to the home of Vallabha Āchārya and saw the site of Lakṣmī and Gaurāṅga’s marriage.

**57.** “O devotees, see the site of Ananta Āchārya’s home. Here, there were many devotees of Nārāyaṇ.

**58.** “Everyone knows that the king of that time established worship of Nārāyaṇ in this place.”

**59.** Seeing the Niḥśreyasa forest on the bank of the Virajā River, the devotees felt unlimited joy.

**60.** While discussing this history, the devotees arrived in Mahatpur.

**61.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “Kāmyavan is present here. Visit it with great devotion.

**62.** “Previously, there were five banyan trees here, but by the will of the Lord, they have since disappeared.

**63.** “This place is now called Mātāpur. The scriptures state that its previous name was Mahatpur.

**64.** “Once, the five Pāṇḍavas came to Gauḍa with Draupadī to live incognito.

**65.** “Understanding the glories of Nadia through a dream in Ekachakrā, Mahārāj Yudhiṣṭhir became amazed.

**66.** “Desiring to see Nabadwīp, the Pāṇḍavas came here the next day with great joy.

**67.** “Seeing the beauty of Nabadwīp, the Pāṇḍavas praised the fortune of the residents of Gauḍa.

**68.** “They stayed here for some time and killed various demons.

**69.** “Everyone, see there Yudhiṣṭhir Ṭilā. See here the pond of Draupadī.

**70.** “Understanding the glories of this place, Mahārāj Yudhiṣṭhir stayed here for some time.

**71.** “One day, in a dream, he saw the extraordinary form of Gaurāṅga illuminate all directions.

**72.** “Smiling, Gaurāṅga said, ‘Behold this very secret form.

**73.** “‘I am Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda, and I always stay in your house as your friendly companion.

**74.** “‘Nabadwīp Dhām is the best of all abodes. It will manifest during Kali-yuga and dispel all darkness.

**75.** “‘You are all My eternal servants, and when I appear, you will also take birth.

**76.** “‘I will live with you permanently in Puruṣottam Kṣetra on the bank of the ocean in Odisha.

**77.** “‘From here, go to Odisha, purify the country, and remove the sorrow of the people.’

**78.** “After having this dream, Yudhi ṣṭ hir spoke to his brothers. They consulted with Draupadī and then went to Odisha.

**79.** “They felt great sorrow to leave Nabadwīp. Still, they followed the order of the Lord.

**80.** “Madhva Āchārya also stayed here with his disciples for some time as a resident of the Dhām.

**81.** “Gaurāṅgasundar mercifully revealed His most enchanting form to Madhva Āchārya in a dream.

**82.** “Smiling, Gaurachandra said to Madhva Āchārya, ‘Everyone knows that you are My eternal servant.

**83.** “‘When I appear in Nabadwīp, I will follow your lineage.

**84–85.** “‘Now, carefully eradicate illusionism and false scriptures from all regions, and establish the glory of the Lord’s Deity form. Later, I will develop your pure conception.’

**86.** “Saying this, Gaurachandra disappeared. Madhva Āchārya awoke and then fainted.

**87.** “Madhva Āchārya then cried, wondering, ‘Will I ever see that beautiful golden form again?’

**88.** “Then a divine voice came from the clear sky, ‘Serve Me secretly, and you will come to Me.’

**89.** “Madhva Āchārya became resolute and defeated the māyāvādīs in all directions.”

**90.** As Nityānanda Prabhu spoke about all this history, the devotees suddenly arrived in Rudradwīp.

**91.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “This is Rudradwīp. It has been divided into two parts by the Gaṅgā.

**92.** “By the will of the Lord, people do not live here. See how this western island has moved to the east bank of the river.

**93.** “From here, see Śrī Śaṅkarpur. See how far it shines along the bank of the Gaṅgā.

**94.** “When Śaṅkar Āchārya was conquering all directions, he came there to conquer Nabadwīp.

**95.** “At heart, Śaṅkar Āchārya was a great Vaiṣṇava; only externally was he acting as a nondualist and servant of Māyā.

**96.** “He was a highly empowered manifestation of Śiva who was expert at preaching a conception of veiled Buddhism.

**97–98.** “According to the order of the Lord, Śiva performs this duty. When Śaṅkar Āchārya came to Nadia, Gaurachandra revealed Himself to Śaṅkar Āchārya in a dream and mercifully spoke sweet words to him.

**99.** “‘You are My servant, and following My order, you are very cleverly preaching illusionism.

**100.** “‘Nabadwīp Dhām is very dear to Me, and illusionism will never have a place here.

**101.** “‘Here, Vṛddha Śiva and Prauḍhā Māyā propagate imaginary interpretations of the scriptures.

**102.** “‘They deceive, however, only those who oppose My devotees.

**103.** “‘In general, My devotees reside here. This place is not for preaching misconception.

**104.** “‘Therefore, you should go elsewhere. Do not trouble the residents of Nabadwīp.’

**105.** “Understanding the position of Nabadwīp through this dream, Śaṅkar Āchārya went elsewhere, inspired with devotion.

**106.** “The eleven Rudras reside here in Rudradwīp and chant the glories of Gaura.

**107.** “Śrī Nīlalohita Śiva, the leader of the Nīlalohita Rudras, always dances here with great joy.

**108.** “Seeing the dancing of the Rudras, the gods in the sky joyfully shower flowers upon them.

**109.** “Once, Viṣṇu Swāmī, while conquering all directions, came to Rudradwīp with his disciples and stayed the night.

**110.** “The disciples danced, chanting, ‘Hari! Hari!’, and Viṣṇu Swāmī recited prayers from the scriptures.

**111.** “Seeing this devotional discussion, Śrī Nīlalohita Śiva was pleased and mercifully revealed himself.

**112.** “Upon seeing that Śiva had arrived at a Vaiṣṇava assembly, Viṣṇu Swāmī became completely amazed.

**113.** “He immediately joined his palms and prayed to Śiva. Melting with compassion, Śiva spoke to him.

**114.** “‘All you Vaiṣṇavas are very dear to me, and my heart is delighted to see your discussion of devotion.

**115.** “‘Request a boon, and I will kindly give it to you. There is nothing that I do not give to Vaiṣṇavas.’

**116.** “Filled with divine love, Viṣṇu Swāmī bowed down, joined his palms, and requested a boon.

**117.** “‘O Lord, give us the boon that hereafter we will establish a school of devotion.’

**118.** “With great joy, Rudra granted this boon and named the school after himself.

**119.** “Viṣṇu Swāmī thus named his school the Śrī Rudra sampradāya, and danced and sang.

**120.** “By Śiva’s mercy, Viṣṇu Swāmī stayed here and worshipped Śrī Gaurachandra, aspiring for divine love.

**121.** “Śrī Gaurāṅga came to Viṣṇu Swāmī in a dream and said, ‘My devotee Śiva blessed you.

**122.** “‘You are fortunate to have attained the wealth of devotion in Nabadwīp. For now, preach pure nondualism.

**123.** “‘Later, at the time of My appearance, you will appear as Śrī Vallabha Bhaṭṭa.

**124.** “‘You will meet Me in Śrī Kṣetra, later go to Mahāvan, and establish your school.’

**125.** “O Jīva, Śrī Vallabha is now in Gokula. When you go there, you will meet him.”

**126.** Saying this, Nityānanda happily walked southbound towards Pāraḍāṅgā through Śrī Pulina.

**127.** Passing through Śrī Pulina, Nityānanda Prabhu showed the devotees Śrī Rāsa Maṇḍal and Dhīra Samīra.

**128.** He said, “O Jīva, see here eternal Vṛndāvan and its Pastimes.”

**129.** Hearing of Vṛndāvan, Jīva became overwhelmed with divine love, and tears of love streamed from his eyes.

**130.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “Śrī Gaurāṅga chanted verses about the Rāsa-līlā here with the devotees.

**131.** “O Jīva, this place on the bank of the Gaṅgā is the place of the great Rāsa-līlā in Vṛndāvan.

**132.** “Daily, the Rāsa-līlā happens here with the gopīs. Sometimes fortunate souls see this.

**133.** “See Śrī Dhīra Samīra to the west. O wise one, hear about this place of worship.

**134.** “The place known as Dhīra Samīra on the bank of the Yamunā in Vraja is present here on the bank of the Gaṅgā.

**135.** “Actually, you do not see the bank of the Gaṅgā here. The Yamunā flows on the west side of the Gaṅgā.

**136.** “This beautiful bank is on the shore of the Yamunā. Thus, Viśvambhar called this place Vṛndāvan.

**137.** “O Jīva, know that all the places of the Pastimes in Vṛndāvan are present here.

**138.** “There is no difference between Vṛndāvan and Nabadwīp, and one should never differentiate between Gaura and Kṛṣṇa.”

**139.** Exuberant with intense ecstasy, Nityānanda Rāy revealed Vṛndāvan to Jīva and then took him ahead.

**140.** They went a little ways north and spent that night in Rudradwīp.

**141.** Bhakti Vinod, whose wealth is the feet of Nitāi and Jāhnavā, chants the glories of Nadia.
