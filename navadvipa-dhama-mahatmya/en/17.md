# Chapter 17. Śrī Jīva Goswāmī’s Questions and Śrīla Nityānanda Prabhu’s Answers

**1.** All glory to GorāchXd, Nityānanda, Advaita, Gadādhar, and the ecstatic joy of divine love!

**2.** All glory to Śrīvās, the devotees, and Nabadwīp! All glory to Nām-saṅkīrtan, the abode of divine love!

**3.** Nityānanda sat in the courtyard of Śrīvās, and in divine love for Gaura, streams of tears flowed from His eyes.

**4.** There were countless Vaiṣṇava sādhus in all directions, and everyone was immersed in the ocean of divine love for Gaura.

**5.** At that time, Śrī Jīva Goswāmī became mad with love for the Divine Couple.

**6.** He prostrated himself at the feet of Nityānanda and rolled on the ground in the courtyard of Śrīvās.

**7.** Affectionately, Nityānanda Prabhu asked, “When will you go to Vṛndāvan?”

**8.** Jīva answered, “The order of the Lord is above all. When I receive Your order, I will take shelter in Vṛndāvan.

**9.** “I have one or two questions to ask You. O Lord, please answer them for the benefit of this servant.

**10.** “Nabadwīp Dhām is Vṛndāvan. So, why should I endeavour to go to Vṛndāvan?”

**11.** Hearing Jīva’s question, Nityānanda Prabhu answered, “This is a very confidential subject. Now listen.

**12.** “As long as the Pastimes of the Lord remain manifest, see to it that averse souls do not know about this.

**13.** “Nabadwīp and Vṛndāvan are one; neither one is lesser or greater than the other.

**14.** “Vṛndāvan Dhām is an abode of rasa, but unqualified souls do not attain that rasa.

**15.** “Vṛndāvan Dhām mercifully manifests as Nabadwīp, and here, souls develop the qualification to attain rasa.

**16.** “The Pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are the highest of all rasas, and quali fication for them does not come immediately.

**17.** “Performing austerities for many births results in knowledge, and when knowledge matures, souls discover rasa.

**18.** “There are always many obstacles involved, and thus, the supreme wealth of rasa is very difficult to attain.

**19.** “Not everyone who goes to Vraja attains rasa, and as a result of offences, rasa becomes tasteless.

**20.** “During the darkness of Kali-yuga, offences are constant, and the life of a soul is short and extremely troublesome.

**21.** “Even if souls desire it, the rasa of Vraja is not attainable. The mercy of Kṛṣṇa is therefore the only cause of attaining rasa.

**22.** “Being merciful to the souls, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa have again appeared with Vṛndāvan.

**23–24.** “Descending in Nabadwīp through the womb of Śachī Devī in one form, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, as Lord Gaurahari, now give souls qualification for rasa. Offences are never an obstacle in Nabadwīp.

**25.** “If souls reside here and take shelter of the Name, their qualification for rasa develops and their offences are eradicated.

**26.** “Within a short time, divine love for Kṛṣṇa shines within them, and they become captivated by discussion of the Divine Couple’s rasa.

**27.** “Attaining the mercy of Gaura, souls then reach V ṛ nd ā van, the abode of the Divine Couple’s rasa.

**28.** “Do not tell anyone this confidential truth. There is no difference between Nabadwīp and Vṛndāvan.

**29.** “Your shelter is now that abode of rasa. Therefore, take shelter in Vṛndāvan.

**30.** “Vṛndāvan is present here. Still, let Vraja Dhām be your shelter.

**31.** “O Jīva, the duty of the soul is always to take shelter in Nabadwīp to become qualified for the rasa of Vraja.

**32.** “In the place to attain the rasa of Vraja (Nabadwīp), souls reside in Vraja and experience the joy of its rasa.

**33.** “When sādhus attain the grace of Nabadwīp, they easily attain Vṛndāvan Dhām.”

**34.** Hearing the conclusions of Nityānanda Prabhu, Jīva grasped His feet with great joy.

**35.** Grasping His feet, Jīva said, “I have another question. Please hear it, O Supreme Lord!

**36.** “Why do many souls who reside in Nabadwīp not attain devotion to Kṛṣṇa?

**37.** “Why do souls reside in the Dhām but commit offences? This is now my troubling doubt.

**38.** “How will devotees of the Lord become free from anxiety about this? Please answer me, O Lord, O pure, eternal abode of the universe!”

**39.** So speaks the poor and fallen Bhakti Vinod, whose aspiration is the shade of Nitāi and Jāhnavā’s feet.
