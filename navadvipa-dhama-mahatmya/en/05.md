# Chapter 5. Śrī Māyāpur and Antardwīp

**1.** All glory to the son of Śachī, Śrī Chaitanya! All glory to the life of Jāhnavā, Śrī Nityānanda!

**2.** All glory to Nabadwīp Dhām, the best of all abodes, where Gaura descended during the Age of Kali!

**3.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “Listen to My words! The thirty-two miles of Nabadwīp are nondifferent from Vṛndāvan.

**4.** “Within these thirty-two miles, there are nine islands, which form an eight-petalled lotus that floats on water.

**5.** “In the middle of the eight petals—the eight islands—is Antardwīp. At its centre is Māyāpur.

**6.** “The circular Yoga Pīṭh in Māyāpur is eternal. There, Śrī Chaitanya performs various eternal Pastimes.

**7.** “Its circumference is six miles, and its diameter two miles.

**8.** “The Pañcha Tattva reside at the Yoga Pīṭh, whose greatness exceeds that of all other places.

**9.** “Very soon it will be hidden by the will of the Lord. The waters of the Gaṅgā will almost entirely conceal it.

**10.** “Someday, when the desire of the Lord becomes strong, the Yoga Pīṭh will be revealed and the Dhām will shine again.

**11.** “The eternal Dhām is never lost at any time—it only becomes hidden and then emerges again.

**12.** “My Lord is eternally present in Māyāpur on the eastern bank of the Gaṅgā.

**13.** “In the vision of common people, Viśvambhar became a sannyāsī, left Nabadwīp, and toured other places.

**14.** “In truth, however, My Gaurāṅga never leaves Nabadwīp Dhām or the village of Māyāpur.

**15.** “The devotees see His Pastimes here daily, and you too, Jīva, will see His dancing.

**16.** “Māyāpur shines within Antardwīp, where Brahmā saw Gaurāṅga.

**17.** “O Jīva, if you want to see everything, then circumambulate the Dhām, and you will be successful.”

**18.** Hearing the words of Nityānanda Prabhu, with tears in his eyes Jīva bowed at His feet.

**19.** “O Lord, if You are merciful to this poor soul, then please personally take me with You on a circumambulation of the Dhām.”

**20.** Hearing Jīva’s prayer, Nityānanda Rāy answered, “So be it.”

**21.** He said, “O Jīva, today see Māyāpur. Tomorrow we will tour extensively.”

**22.** Having said this, Nityānanda arose, and with a joyous heart Jīva rose after Him.

**23.** Nityānanda Rāy moved slowly, His body completely overwhelmed by divine love for Gaurāṅga.

**24.** Nityānanda Prabhu’s enchanting form swayed to and fro in ecstasy, and the ornaments all over His body shone.

**25.** The feet that Brahmā and Śiva cannot attain by meditation mercifully walked in front of Śrī Jīva.

**26.** Remaining behind, Jīva collected dust from their footprints, smeared it all over his body, and proceeded with great delight.

**27.** They entered the home of Jagannāth Miśra and spoke carefully at the holy feet of Mother Śachī.

**28.** “O Mother, listen! This is Jīva, a dear servant of Śrī Gaurāṅga. He is very intelligent and very fortunate.”

**29.** As Nityānanda Prabhu spoke, Jīva threw himself down like a tree uprooted by an intense storm.

**30.** He bowed at the feet of Śachī and rolled on the ground. Divine ecstasy surged through his body.

**31.** Śachī Devī mercifully blessed him, and that day he received prasād in her home.

**32.** Receiving the order of Śachī Devī, Viṣṇu Priyā cooked rice, various vegetables, and other things.

**33.** Śrī Vaṁśī Vadanānanda Prabhu then carefully offered the preparations to Śrī Gaurāṅga.

**34.** Īśān Ṭhākur then prepared a place and fed Nityānanda, with joy in his heart.

**35.** With motherly affection, Śachī Devī said to Nityānanda, “Eat, my child. Nityānanda, You are in the home of Your mother.

**36.** “Privately, I fed this to Gaurachandra. If You eat it, I will be very happy at heart.”

**37.** At the request of Mother Śachī, Nityānanda Rāy joyfully ate, and Jīva received His remnants.

**38.** Jīva said, “I am so fortunate to have received prasād at the home of Mahāprabhu in Māyāpur.”

**39.** After eating, Nityānanda Rāy took leave of Śachī Devī’s holy feet.

**40.** As He was leaving, He took Vaṁśī Vadanānanda with Him. Śrī Jīva bowed at the feet of Vaṁśī.

**41.** Nityānanda said to Jīva, “This is Vaṁśī Vadanānanda. The devotees know him to be Śrī Kṛṣṇa’s dear flute.

**42.** “By his mercy, souls become attracted to Kṛṣṇa and desire to join the Rāsa Dance.

**43.** “Jīva, look! Lord Chaitanya performed many Pastimes with us in this house.

**44.** “See here. This is the Temple of Jagannāth Miśra, where Gaura would earnestly worship Viṣṇu each day.

**45.** “In this house, the Lord would serve guests. See here His tulasī courtyard.

**46.** “When Śrī Gaurachandra, the maintainer of the devotees, was present at home, He would perform His father’s duties.

**47.** “Īśān Ṭhākur now performs these duties each day under the direction of Vaṁśī Ṭhākur.

**48.** “In this place, there was a great neem tree that disappeared upon being touched by the Lord.”

**49.** Nityānanda wept as He described all of this, as did Jīva and Vaṁśī.

**50.** Just then, Śrīvās arrived, and the four of them then left Jagannāth Miśra’s home.

**51.** Three hundred and twenty metres to the north, Nityānanda Prabhu joyfully showed Śrīvās Aṅgan to Jīva.

**52.** Jīva rolled on the ground in Śrīvās Aṅgan. Remembering the Pastimes of the Lord there, he was overwhelmed with divine love.

**53.** Jīva immediately had a vision: he saw Gaurāṅga dancing with His intimate devotees.

**54.** Jīva saw the Lord’s mahāsaṅkīrtan and wonderful dancing amidst all the devotees.

**55.** Advaita Prabhu, Nityānanda Rāy, Gadādhar, and Haridās were dancing and singing.

**56.** Seeing Śuklāmbar and hundreds of other devotees dancing, Jīva fainted in divine love.

**57–58.** When he regained consciousness and this vision disappeared, Jīva Goswāmī wept and lamented, “Why was I not born a little earlier? I was not fortunate enough to be part of such ecstatic kīrtan.

**59.** “The mercy of Nityānanda Prabhu is unlimited and infinite, and by its influence I have attained a moment of good fortune.

**60.** “I want to stay in Māyāpur forever. Here, the miseries of material existence will be completely dispelled.

**61.** “The order of the Lord, however, is greater than the desire of His servant, and my heart is palpitating at the thought of leaving Māyāpur.”

**62.** From there, Nityānanda took Jīva thirty-two metres north to the house of Advaita.

**63.** The Lord said, “O Jīva, see the house of Advaita. The devotees met here every day.

**64.** “Here, Advaita worshipped Kṛṣṇa and brought down our wealth, Śrī Gaurāṅga, with His cries.”

**65.** There, the four of them rolled on the ground. Then they walked sixteen metres east to the house of Gadādhar.

**66.** Thereafter, Nityānanda showed Jīva the houses of all the Lord’s associates throughout Māyāpur.

**67.** The four of them visited the homes of some brāhmaṇs and then joyfully walked to the bank of the Gaṅgā.

**68.** Along the outer boundary of Māyāpur on the bank of the Gaṅgā, Jīva saw the Temple of Vṛddha Śiva.

**69.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “In Māyāpur, Vṛddha Śiva is the protector of the Dhām. His consort, Prauḍhā Māyā, resides with him eternally.

**70.** “When the Lord disappears, then, by His will, the Gaṅgā will swell.

**71.** “She will almost entirely cover Māyāpur with water for one hundred years, and then she will withdraw.

**72.** “At that time, only the land will emerge; no houses will remain. Māyāpur will stay uninhabited for a long time.

“Again, when the Lord strongly desires it, Māyāpur will be inhabited like this again.

**73.** “All these ghāṭs will appear on the banks of the Gaṅgā again, and the devotees will make Temples for the Lord.

**74.** “One wonderful Temple will manifest, and there daily worship of Gaurāṅga will flourish.

**75.** “By the will of the Lord, Prauḍhā Māyā and Vṛddha Śiva will return and perform their duties (of revealing and protecting the Dhām).”

**76.** Hearing this, Jīva joined his palms, questioned Nityānanda Prabhu, and then grasped the Lord’s feet.

**77.** “O Lord, You are the basis of the ultimate truth. You manifest Yourself as the Dhām and the Holy Name.

**78.** “Although You act according to the will of Mah ā prabhu, You are the Guru of all souls and the controller of all energies.

**79.** “The wise consider anyone who distinguishes between You and Gaurāṅga to be a sinner.

**80.** “You are the all-knowing Lord, who descends to perform Pastimes. A doubt has arisen in my heart.

**81.** “O Lord, please tell me, where will Vṛddha Śiva and Prauḍhā Māyā go when the Gaṅgā hides Māyāpur?”

**82.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “Jīva, listen to My words and look at the land to the west of the Gaṅgā.

**83.** “See there the sand dune known as Pāraḍāṅgā. In that place, there is a village of brāhmaṇs.

**84.** “The wise know the bank of the Gaṅgā to its north as Chhinnaḍeṅgā.

**85.** “A village will be established on this bank. Vṛddha Śiva and Prauḍhā Māyā will remain there for some time.

**86.** “Who can describe the glories of that bank? The Rāsa Sthalī is there beside the Gaṅgā.

**87.** “It appears like a beach to fleshy eyes, but the Lord’s divine Pastimes take place in that eternal abode of gemstone.

**88.** “Māyāpur is Śrī Gokula Mahāvan, and Pāraḍāṅgā is considered Saṭṭīkāra.

**89.** “Śrī Rāsa Maṇḍal of Vṛndāvan is present there, and in the future there will be joyous singing at that place.

**90.** “O wise soul, know that Māyāpur and the Gaṅgā at Śrī Pulina are both part of Śrī Gaura’s Dhām.

**91.** “One who visits this abode of ten miles will see Māyāpur and Śrī Pulina.

**92.** “One who visits these ten miles with devotees on the full moon day in the month of Phālgun attains the eternal wealth.

**93.** “O Jīva, listen to My confidential words. Śrī Viṣṇu Priyā’s Deity of Śrī Gaurāṅga shines here.

**94.** “In the future, brāhmaṇs in the lineage of Jagannāth Miśra will bring this jewel-like Deity to Saṭṭīkāra Dhām.

**95.** “Four hundred years after the appearance day of Śrī Gaura, the worship of the Deity will be set to the highest standard.

**96.** “For now, keep all these matters secret and circumambulate the Dhām with a joyful heart.

**97.** “O best of the wise, see Śrī Gaurāṅga’s ghāṭ ten metres north of Vṛddha Śiva’s ghāṭ.

**98.** “On the pretext of childhood Pastimes, Gaurahari played here with the Gaṅgā and filled her heart with joy.

**99.** “Seeing the fortune of the Yamunā, Gaṅgā Devī had performed many austerities to become an associate of the Lord in His Pastimes.

**100.** “Kṛṣṇa mercifully appeared before her and said, ‘As Gaura, I will play in your waters.’

**101.** “The Lord of the three worlds performed this Pastime here. Upon seeing this place, fortunate souls feel great joy.

**102.** “The ghāṭ one hundred and sixty metres to the north is known by everyone as Mādhāi’s ghāṭ.

**103.** “Sixteen metres to its north is a beautiful ghāṭ that always attracts the minds of all the villagers.

**104.** “This extremely beautiful ghāṭ is known as Bārakoṇā Ghāṭ. Viśvakarmā constructed it on the order of the Lord.

**105.** “O Jīva, see the five Śiva Temples at this ghāṭ. Five effulgent liṅgams always reside here in these five holy places.

**106.** “These four ghāṭs beautify Māyāpur. Bathing at them removes all sorrow.

**107.** “To the east of Māyāpur is a place known as Antardwīp.

**108.** “Now, by the will of the Lord, it is uninhabited. It will remain this way for some time.

**109.** “Eventually, people will live here again, and the place will manifest as the glory of Nadia.

**110.** “O Jīva, today stay in Māyāpur. Tomorrow I will take you to Sīmantadwīp.”

**111.** Hearing this, Jīva said, “A doubt has arisen within me. Please hear it.

**112–113.** “When Gaṅgā Devī uncovers Māyāpur and it no longer remains hidden, by what signs will the devotees discover the hidden places? Please explain this.”

**114.** Hearing Jīva’s words, Nityānanda Rāy gave a nectarean answer.

**115.** “Listen, Jīva, when Gaṅgā Devī covers Māyāpur, one edge of it will remain manifest.

**116.** “Many uncivilised people will reside there, but the name of the place will remain Māyāpur.

**117–118.** “One thousand six hundred metres southwest, past this remaining place, a raised area always covered with grass will be visible. That place will be the home of Jagannāth Miśra.

**119.** “Sixteen metres from there is the Vṛddha Śiva Temple. According to these measurements, devotees will ascertain the lost places.

**120.** “A pond known as Śiva Ḍobā will be visible. Devotees will recognise that pond as the previous bank of the Gaṅgā.

**121.** “Know for certain that the devotees will reveal the lost places in this way by the will of the Lord.

**122.** “Four hundred years after the appearance of the Lord, the endeavour to reveal the lost places will be completed.”

**123.** Śrī Jīva said, “O Lord, now please tell me the true reason for Antardwīp’s name.”

**124.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “At the end of Dvāpar-yuga, Brahmā performed austerities in this place, aspiring for the mercy of Gaura.

**125.** “Previously, using his illusion, he tried to trick Govinda by stealing the calves and cowherd boys.

**126.** “When he saw his illusion overpowered by Govinda, Brahmā became very sad, understanding his offence.

**127.** “He profusely praised Kṛṣṇa and prayed to Him. The Lord of Vṛndāvan then forgave Brahmā’s offence.

**128.** “Brahmā considered within his mind, ‘My mentality of being Brahmā is completely worthless.

**129.** “‘Because of my wrong mentality, I am devoid of divine love for Kṛṣṇa and deprived of relishing the rasa of Pastimes in Vraja.

**130.** “‘If I had been born as a cowherd boy, I would have easily served the Lord of the gopīs.

**131.** “‘I did not attain entrance into the rasa of those Pastimes, but now, with Śrī Gaurāṅga, I will not have any misconceptions.’

**132.** “Thinking in this way, Brahmā performed austerities and meditated for a long time in Antardwīp.

**133.** “Eventually, Gaurachandra mercifully came before Brahmā and spoke to him.

**134.** “‘O Brahmā, I am pleased by your austerities, and I have come to give you whatever you desire.’

**135.** “Opening his eyes and seeing Gaura Rāy, Brahmā fell to the ground unconscious.

**136.** “The Lord placed His foot on Brahmā’s head. Receiving divine knowledge, Brahmā praised the Lord.

**137.** “‘I am very lowly and fallen. Controlled by pride, I forgot Your feet and turned to material pleasures.

**138.** “‘The scriptures say that myself, Śiva, Indra, and the other gods are all servants under Your control.

**139.** “‘But we do not have the fortune of being Your pure servants, and thus Māyā covers us with her net of illusion.

**140.** “‘The first one hundred and fifty-five trillion years of my lifespan have passed. Now I am contemplating my final days.

**141.** “‘How will I pass the second one hundred and fifty-five trillion years of my lifespan? I know that if I remain averse to You, I will suffer greatly.

**142.** “‘My only prayer at Your feet is that I may become one of Your associates in Your manifest Pastimes.

**143.** “‘May I attain a birth in which my mentality of being Brahmā leaves me, and I live in Your company, chanting Your glories.’

**144.** “Hearing Brahmā’s prayer, the Supreme Lord Gaura said, ‘So be it’, and gave him the boon he desired.

**145.** “‘When My Pastimes manifest, you will be born in a home of uncivilised people.

**146.** “‘You will consider yourself lowly. You will be known as Haridās, and you will be free from pride.

**147.** “‘The Name will dance on the tip of your tongue three hundred thousand times a day, and you will see Me at the time of your passing away.

**148.** “‘As a result of your practice, at the end of the second one hundred trillion years of your lifespan, you will attain Nabadwīp Dhām and relish eternal rasa.

**149.** “‘O Brahmā, listen to My heartfelt words, but do not express them in the scriptures anywhere at any time.

**150.** “‘Taking the mood of a devotee, I will relish the rasa of devotion and reveal the most rare form of saṅkīrtan.

**151.** “‘I will completely madden all the devotees of My previous Avatārs with the rasa of Vraja.

**152.** “‘My heart is bound by the love of Śrī Rādhikā. I will take Her heart and halo, and appear.

**153.** “‘What sort of happiness does Rādhā feel by serving Me? Taking Her heart, I will relish that happiness.

**154.** “‘From today, you are My disciple, and as Haridās, you will serve Me eternally.’

**155.** “Saying this, Mahāprabhu disappeared, and Brahmā fell heavily to the ground unconscious.

**156.** “‘O Gaurāṅga! O Friend of the fallen! O affectionate Lord of the devotees! When will I reach Your lotus feet?’

**157.** “After crying and crying in this way for some time, Brahmā returned to his abode to perform his duties.”

**158.** This lowly, fallen, insignificant soul, who aspires only for the feet of Nitāi and Jāhnavā, chants the glories of Nadia.
