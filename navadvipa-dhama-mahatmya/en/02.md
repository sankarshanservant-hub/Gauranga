# Chapter 2. The Nature and Dimensions of the Dhām

**1.** All glory to the son of Śachī, the moon of Nabadwīp! All glory to the avadhūt Śrī Nityānanda Rāy!

**2.** All glory to Nabadwīp, the best of all abodes! Who can describe its glory?

**3.** Nabadwīp Dhām shines eternally within Gauḍa Maṇḍal, and is served by Gaṅgā Devī.

**4.** Gauḍa Maṇḍal is one hundred and sixty-eight miles in circumference. Gaṅgā Devī continuously flows throughout it.

**5.** Gauḍa Maṇḍal is a one-hundred-petalled lotus, and at its centre is the extremely beautiful Nabadwīp.

**6.** In circumference, its whorl is ten miles in circumference, and its fragrant bloom is thirty-two miles.

**7.** Its outer petals form a one-hundred-petalled lotus that extends for one hundred and sixty-eight miles.

**8.** The circumference of Gauḍa Maṇḍal is that size. The scriptures state that its diameter is fifty-six miles.

**9.** Its radius, measured from its centre point, is twenty-eight miles.

**10.** The centre of Nabadwīp Dhām is the pure, spiritual Yoga Pīṭh.

**11.** Śrī Gauḍa Maṇḍal is made of wish-fulfilling gemstone. It is spiritual and joyful: everything within it is made of spiritual energy.

**12.** The water, land, trees, and so on, are all spiritual. Kṛṣṇa’s three energies exist there eternally.

**13.** The Dhām is a manifestation of the sandhinī aspect of the Lord’s personal energy.

**14.** The Dhām is the eternal site of the Lord’s Pastimes. It is a manifestation of the Lord’s inconceivable energy; it is not material.

**15.** Still, the conditioned souls are bewildered by illusion and see the Dhām to be material.

**16.** Eyes covered by clouds see that the sun is covered, but the sun is never actually covered by clouds.

**17.** Similarly, worldly people see the spiritual form of Śrī Gauḍa Maṇḍal to be a formation of matter.

**18.** But once Nityānanda blesses someone, they see the joyful, spiritual Dhām everywhere.

**19.** The Gaṅgā, Yamunā, and other holy rivers exist there eternally. The seven holy cities, Prayāg, and other holy places are present throughout the Dhām.

**20.** Śrī Gauḍa Maṇḍal is Vaikuṇṭha itself; fortunate souls see this clearly.

**21–22.** On the order of the Lord, Māyā, the shadow of His personal energy, extends her influence and covers the eyes of the averse souls. Thus, they do not see the glory of the Lord’s spiritual abode.

**23.** Souls who always reside in Śrī Gauḍa Maṇḍal are the most fortunate souls in this world.

**24.** The gods living in heaven see that they have beautiful four-armed forms with a cloud-like lustre.

**25.** The residents of the thirty-two miles of Śrī Nabadwīp Dhām have a golden lustre and are always engaged in chanting the Name.

**26.** From the heavens, Brahmā and the other gods worship the residents of Nabadwīp in various ways.

**27–28.** Brahmā says, “When will I attain the fortune of becoming a blade of grass in Nabadwīp and receiving the foot dust of the servants of Śrī Gaura’s feet?

**29.** “Alas! Gaurachandra deceived me and made me the emperor of the universe.

**30.** “When will my entanglement in karma be cut away? When will my heart give up its pride and become pure?

**31.** “When will my mentality of being a master be destroyed? When will I become a pure servant and attain the shelter of Śrī Gaura’s feet?”

**32.** The gods, sages, and Rudras live throughout Nabadwīp eternally.

**33.** They spend their lives performing austerities for long periods, but they do not receive the mercy of Nityānanda.

**34–35.** As long as their identification with the body does not go away and humility does not appear within their hearts, even Brahmā and Śiva do not attain the wealth of Gaura and Nitāi’s mercy, although they strive for it.

**36.** All these points will be described ahead. Brother! Faithfully and carefully listen.

**37.** Brother! Give up argument in these matters—inappropriate argument is very inauspicious.

**38.** The Pastimes of Śrī Chaitanya are a deep ocean. Argument over them, like a banana flower’s husk, is simply troublesome.

**39.** The endeavour of those who want to cross over this world by argument is fruitless. Such persons do not attain anything.

**40.** Those who forgo argument and follow the sādhus and scriptures quickly attain Śrī Chaitanya.

**41.** The śrutis, smṛtis, and tantras always sing the glories of Nadia on the order of Nityānanda Prabhu.

**42.** Read all these scriptures and honour the words of the sādhus; then you will understand Nabadwīp.

**43.** During the Age of Kali, all the holy places are very weak. Only Nabadwīp is greatly powerful.

**44.** By the will of the Lord, this holy place was unmanifest for a long time, and its greatness was not revealed.

**45.** When the influence of Kali strongly increased, the other holy places naturally became powerless.

**46.** The Supreme Lord reflected on this and made an arrangement for the welfare of all souls.

**47.** “Understanding a patient’s disease, a good doctor prescribes medicine. For a strong disease, he gives strong medicine.

**48.** “The Age of Kali has become severe, and the disease of the souls has become serious. Without strong medicine, I cannot check it.

**49–50.** “If I do not reveal the Abode, Name, and Form that I have kept completely hidden, then how will the souls be delivered?

**51.** “The souls are My servants, and I am their master. If I do not deliver them, then they will never be delivered.”

**52.** Thinking thus, Śrī Chaitanya appeared with His Name, His abode, and His servants.

**53.** This promise of the Lord is eternal: “I will deliver all souls and end their suffering.

**54.** “In this appearance, I will distribute throughout the world wealth that is difficult for even Brahmā to attain, and I will not consider who is fit or unfit.

**55.** “I will see how Kali devastates the souls, and I will reveal Nabadwīp Dhām.

**56.** “In the Dhām, I will break Kali’s poisonous fangs, perform kīrtan, and collect the souls.

**57.** “Kali will be subdued to the extent that My Name is chanted.”

**58.** Deciding this, Gaurahari revealed Nabadwīp by His own power at the beginning of Kali-yuga.

**59.** Gaurachandra held back Māyā’s darkness and revealed His eternal Pastimes in the land of Gauḍa.

**60–61.** I do not see anyone in the world during the Age of Kali as unfortunate, pitiable, and fallen as someone who does not serve such a merciful Lord and rejects such an inconceivable abode.

**62.** Therefore, brother! Give up all other desires and attachments, and devote yourself exclusively to Nabadwīp Dhām.

**63.** Bhakti Vinod, whose aspiration is the shade of Jāhnavā and Nitāi’s feet, reveals the glories of Nadia.
