# Chapter 4. Śrī Jīva Hears the Glories of the Dhām

**1.** All glory to the son of Śachī, the moon of Nabadwīp! All glory to the avadhūt Nityānanda Rāy!

**2.** All glory to the best of all abodes, Nabadwīp, where Śrī Chaitanya appeared!

**3.** Brother, souls attain in Nabadwīp in one day the result that others attain by residing in all the holy places.

**4.** O sādhus, listen as I discuss the scriptures and sing about circumambulating Nabadwīp.

**5.** The writing of the scriptures, the words of the devotees, and the order of the Lord are my life and wealth.

**6.** Having taken shelter of them, I will describe the procedure for circumambulating Nadia. Everyone listen.

**7.** When Śrī Jīva Goswāmī left home, he was eager at heart, calling out, “Nadia! Nadia!”

**8.** As he left Chandradwīp and walked down the road, his eyes filled with tears.

**9–10.** Again and again, he called out, “O Gaurāṅga! O Nityānanda! O Life of the soul! When will You mercifully appear before me? O Nabadwīp Dhām, best of all abodes! When will I see you?”

**11.** Jīva was young and had a beautiful figure. His extreme detachment was astonishing to see.

**12.** After walking for many days, always filled with divine love, Jīva arrived in Nabadwīp.

**13.** Seeing Nabadwīp from a distance, he bowed and fell almost unconscious.

**14.** Eventually he steadied his heart and entered Nabadwīp, the hair on his body standing on end.

**15.** He came to Bārakoṇā Ghāṭ and asked everyone, “Where is Nityānanda Prabhu? Please show me.”

**16.** Seeing Śrī Jīva’s ecstasy, a great soul immediately brought him to Nityānanda Prabhu.

**17.** Knowing that Śrī Jīva would come, Nityānanda Prabhu was delighted at heart and roaring with laughter.

**18.** He ordered His servants to bring Śrī Jīva, and many devotees went to call him.

**19.** Upon seeing the body of Jīva filled with divine ecstasy, Nityānanda Prabhu’s servants identified him.

**20.** Some of them, filled with intense divine love, went forward and conveyed the order of Nityānanda Prabhu to Jīva.

**21.** Hearing the Name of Nityānanda Prabhu, Jīva fell to the ground unconscious.

**22.** Immediately, he arose and said, “It is my great fortune that I, a fallen soul, have received the mercy of Nityānanda Prabhu.”

**23.** Jīva prostrated himself before all the devotees with a jubilant heart.

**24.** He said, “All the scriptures say that if you all are merciful to me, then I will attain the feet of Nityānanda Prabhu.”

**25.** Seeing Jīva’s fortune, some of the devotees joyously took dust from his feet.

**26.** They all joined together and took Jīva to Nityānanda Prabhu, where He was speaking about Kṛṣṇa, surrounded by devotees.

**27.** Seeing the divine form of Nityānanda Prabhu, extraordinary ecstasy arose in Jīva’s body.

**28.** Thinking, “What a wonderful form I have seen today!”, Jīva fell to the ground unconscious.

**29.** Moved by intense compassion, Nityānanda Prabhu raised Jīva to His feet.

**30.** Śrī Jīva Goswāmī quickly stood up, joined his palms, and began to speak to Nityānanda.

**31.** “You are Balarām, the original form and shelter of the world. I am just a soul. How can I understand Your glories?

**32.** “You are my Master, and I am Your eternal servant. The shade of Your feet is my one and only aspiration.

**33.** “Those whom you bless easily attain the feet of Śrī Chaitanya and float in the waters of divine love.

**34.** “Without Your grace, souls do not attain Śrī Gaura, even if they serve Him within their heart for one hundred births.

**35.** “If Gaura punishes someone, You protect them, and Gaura rejects whomever You punish.

**36.** “Therefore, O Lord, by my good fortune, I have taken shelter at Your lotus feet.

**37.** “O Lord, mercifully give Your consent that I may see Śrī Gaura and develop devotion to Him.

**38.** “When Śrī Gaurāṅga Rāy was in the village of Rāmakeli, my uncles took shelter at His feet.

**39.** “At that time, I was a child. With tearful eyes, I saw Śrī Gaura’s form, and still now that form constantly arises in my heart.

**40.** “Bowing at Śrī Gaurāṅga’s feet, I offered my obeisance. Touching His divine form, I felt great joy.

**41.** “At that time, Gaura said to me, ‘O J ī va, study the scriptures.

**42.** “‘Complete your study and then go to Nabadwīp. There, at the holy feet of Nityānanda, you will attain everything.’

**43.** “Holding this order on my head, I, a poor soul, have learned as much as possible.

**44.** “I studied literature and other subjects in Chandradwīp, but I did not find an expert teacher of Vedānta.

**45.** “The Lord ordered me to study Vedānta and prove that devotion to Kṛṣṇa is its purport.

**46.** “I have come to Your feet in Nabadwīp, and I will do whatever You order me.

**47.** “If You order me, I will go to the feet of the Lord in Puruṣottam Kṣetra and study Vedānta at the school of Sārvabhauma Bhattāchārya.”

**48.** Moved by Jīva’s sweet words, Nityānanda Rāy embraced Jīva and cried, unable to restrain Himself.

**49.** Nityānanda said, “O Jīva, listen to My confidential words. Rūpa and Sanātan know everything.

**50.** “The Lord ordered Me to tell you, ‘Do not go to Puruṣottam Kṣetra or stay here.’

**51.** “Everyone knows that you and the brothers Rūpa and Sanātan are exclusive servants of the Lord.

**52–53.** “The Lord’s order to you is that you go to Vārāṇasī, study Vedānta with Madhusūdan Vāchaspati, and then go straight to Vṛndāvan from there. Rūpa and Sanātan will bless you in Vṛndāvan.

**54.** “Serve the Divine Couple as a follower of Śrī Rūpa, and discuss Ved ā nta and other scriptures there.

**55.** “Preach that *Śrīmad Bhāgavatam* is the best of all the scriptures and the natural commentary on the *Vedānta-sūtra*.

**56.** “Śrī Gaurāṅga Hari mercifully explained the *Brahma-sūtra* to Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭāchārya on the basis of *Śrīmad Bhāgavatam*.

**57.** “Sārvabhauma very carefully taught this knowledge to Śrī Madhusūdan in Śrī Kṣetra Dhām.

**58.** “Go and see Madhusūdan Vāchaspati, who is in Vārāṇasī Dhām on the order of the Lord.

**59.** “Externally, he is a traditional Vedāntic scholar, and Śaṅkarite san nyāsīs study with him.

**60.** “In a gradual way, he mercifully teaches the sannyāsīs Gaurāṅga’s explanations of the sūtras.

**61.** “There is no need for a separate commentary on the *Brahma-sūtra* now. The *Bhāgavatam* explains the sūtras and is considered the sūtras’ natural commentary.

**62.** “In time, when there is a need for another commentary, the *Śrī Govinda-bhāṣya* will be revealed.

**63.** “Sārvabhauma’s relative, Gopīnāth Āchārya, heard the Lord’s commentary with Sārvabhauma.

**64.** “In time, by the will of the Lord, he will take birth as Baladev Vidyābhūṣaṇ and conquer Jaipur.

**65.** “There, he will compose a commentary spoken by Śrī Govinda, serve the feet of Gaurāṅga, and deliver the souls.

**66.** “Rūpa and Sanātan will explain all these confidential matters to you.”

**67.** Upon hearing the words of Nityānanda, Śrī Jīva Goswāmī rolled on the ground crying and lost consciousness.

**68.** Mercifully, the Lord placed His feet on Śrī Jīva’s head and empowered him.

**69.** Chanting, “All glory to Śrī Gaurāṅga! All glory to Nityānanda Rāy!”, Jīva then danced amidst the assembly of Vaiṣṇavas.

**70–71.** Śrīvās and the other great souls there, seeing the mercy of Nityānanda upon Jīva, danced, chanting, “Śrī Gaurāṅga! Nityānanda!”, and the loud sound of women cheering arose.

**72.** Eventually everyone stopped dancing, and Nityānanda sat down with Jīva.

**73.** Jīva stayed in the courtyard of Śrīvās, and in the evening he came to see Nityānanda Prabhu again.

**74.** Nityānanda Prabhu was sitting alone chanting the glories of Śrī Gaura. Jīva came and bowed at His feet.

**75.** Nityānanda Prabhu carefully sat Jīva nearby. Jīva joined his palms and humbly prayed to the Lord.

**76.** Jīva said, “O Lord, mercifully describe the glories of Nabadwīp Dhām for me.”

**77.** The Lord said, “O Jīva, I will describe the most confidential truths to you. Hold them in your heart.

**78.** “Do not reveal them anywhere now. They will be revealed after the manifest Pastimes of the Lord.

**79–80.** “Nabadwīp is the best of all the Lord’s abodes. Beyond Śrī Virajā, Brahmaloka, and Vaikuṇṭha is Śvetadwīp, Śrī Goloka, and lastly Gokula Vṛndāvan, Kṛṣṇa’s abode.

**81.** “That abode manifests in two ways: its rasa appears as either mādhurya or audārya.

**82.** “Aud ā rya is fully present in m ā dhurya, and m ā dhurya is fully present in audārya.

**83.** “Still, fortunate souls know that Vṛndāvan is where mādhurya is predominant.

**84.** “All the Vedas say that Nabadwīp Dhām is where audārya is always predominant.

**85.** “There is no difference between Vṛndāvan and Nabadwīp other than the difference made by their respective manifestations of rasa.

**86.** “This abode is eternally perfect, spiritual, and infinite. Materialists cannot even reach its edge.

**87.** “Only by the influence of the pleasure potency of the Lord do souls give up material nature and realise the true nature of the Dhām through eternal, perfect knowledge.

**88.** “The whole of Nabadwīp is a spiritual manifestation, and Śrī Gaurāṅga performs His Pastimes there.

**89.** “With fleshy eyes, people see a material form of the Dhām—Māyā keeps the eternal abode covered.

**90.** “In Nabadwīp, there is no illusory matter, space, or time. Souls undergo no suffering there.

**91.** “Souls bound by karma and bewildered by Māyā, however, enter Nabadwīp Dhām in a material way.

**92.** “When souls develop divine love by good fortune and the association of the sādhus, they then see the spiritual world.

**93.** “Souls easily and uninterruptedly see with their own eyes the supramundane space, time, light, and objects in the Dhām.

**94.** “Thus, I have described the glory of Nabadwīp. O Jīva, reflect on it with a pure heart.”

**95.** Bhakti Vinod, whose eternal aspiration is the feet of Nitāi and Jāhnavā, reveals the hidden glories of Nadia.
