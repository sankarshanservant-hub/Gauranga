# Chapter 6. Visiting Śrī Gaṅgā Nagar, Śrī Pṛthu Kuṇḍa, Śrī Sīmantadwīp, Śrī Viśrām Sthān, and Other Places

**1.** All glory to the son of Śachī, Śrī Chaitanya! All glory to the life of Jāhnavā, Nityānanda Prabhu!

**2.** All glory to the Lord of Sītā Devī, Advaita Āchārya! All glory to Gadādhar, Śrīvās, and all the Lord’s associates!

**3.** The next day in the morning, Nityānanda Prabhu left the Temple with Śrīvās and Śrī Jīva.

**4.** Rāmadās and other devotees accompanied them, and they chanted the Name of Gaura as they walked.

**5.** When the Lord came to the boundary of Antardwīp, He showed Śrī Gaṅgā Nagar to Jīva.

**6.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “Listen Jīva, this is Gaṅgā Nagar. It was established by Mahārāj Bhagīrath, a forefather of Lord Rāmachandra.

**7.** “When the Gaṅgā, who is also known as Bhāgīrathī, came here, Mahārāj Bhagīrath went ahead of her blowing a conch.

**8.** “Arriving in Nabadwīp Dhām, the Gaṅgā stopped, and Mahārāj Bhagīrath saw that she would not proceed.

**9.** “Overwhelmed with fear, Mahārāj Bhagīrath retraced his course and came before the Gaṅgā.

**10.** “He stayed in Gaṅgā Nagar and began to perform austerities. Pleased by them, Gaṅgā personally appeared before him.

**11.** “Mahārāj Bhagīrath said, ‘O Mother, if you do not continue, my ancestors will never be delivered.’

**12.** “Gaṅgā said, ‘Listen, my child, O heroic Bhagīrath! Peacefully stay here for some time.

**13.** “‘We have come to Nabadwīp Dhām in the month of Māgh. At the end of Phālgun, I will deliver your ancestors.

**14.** “‘O Bhagīrath, I am the water that washes the feet of the Lord. Here in His abode, I will first fulfil my own desires.

**15.** “‘The full moon day in Phālgun is the birthday of the Lord, and I should perform a rite on that day.

**16.** “‘I will complete this rite, and then certainly I will go with you. Do not be afraid.’

**17.** “Mahārāj Bhagīrath resided in Gaṅgā Nagar until the full moon day in Phālgun.

**18–19.** “Anyone who stays in Gaṅgā Nagar on the full moon day in Phālgun, bathes in the Gaṅgā, fasts, and worships Śrī Gaurāṅga crosses over material existence, as do their ancestors.

**20.** “Along with one thousand of their ancestors, they attain Śrī Goloka, regardless of how or where they died.

**21.** “O Jīva, the glories of this place are unlimited. Śrī Chaitanya danced here many times.

**22.** “See there the homes of Gaṅgā Dās Paṇḍit and Sañjay Dās, which are always filled with joy.

**23.** “O best of the wise, hear now about the glories of the beautiful lake to the east.

**24.** “It has now become known as Ballāl Dīrghikā, but it was described in many other ways during Satya-yuga.

**25–26.** “When Mahārāj Pṛthu was cutting and levelling all the high and low places on the earth, he came to level this place, and an extremely bright light appeared in all directions.

**27.** “The workmen informed the king, and Mahārāj Pṛthu came and saw the light.

**28.** “Mahārāj Pṛthu, a Śaktyāveś-avatār, through meditation understood this place to be Nabadwīp.

**29.** “To keep the glories of this place hidden, he ordered, ‘Make a beautiful lake here.’

**30.** “The lake he made, known as Pṛthu Kuṇḍa, became famous all over Nabadwīp Dhām.

**31.** “How can I describe how much joy the villagers felt when they drank the pure water of this lake?

**32.** “Later, King Lakṣmaṇ Sen excavated, deepened, and enlarged the lake.

**33.** “Desiring to deliver his ancestors, he named the lake Ballāl Dīrghikā (after his father, Ballāl Sen).

**34.** “See there that large beautiful hill. There stood the house of Lakṣmaṇ Sen. It has since fallen into ruin.

**35.** “Various kings made all these ornaments at this extremely holy place to continuously accrue good fortune.

**36.** “Later, an uncivilised king desecrated this place, and thus devotees do not worship it.

**37.** “The land in this place is pure, but fearing interaction with the uncivilised people, devotees do not reside here.

**38.** “Offences were made against Deities in this place, and thus devotees left.”

**39.** Saying this, Nityānanda roared. He then came to Simuliyā Grām.

**40.** Seeing Simuliyā, Nityānanda Prabhu said to Jīva, “Know for certain that this is Sīmantadwīp.

**41.** “The sādhus call the island on the border of Nabadwīp and the southern bank of the Gaṅgā ‘Sīmanta’.

**42.** “In time, the Gaṅgā will completely cover this island, and only one pure place will remain.

**43.** “Hear now about how materialistic people will worship Pārvatī in that place, which is known as Simulī.

**44.** “Once, during Satya-yuga, the god of gods, Lord Śiva, was chanting the Name of Śrī Gaurāṅga and dancing.

**45.** “Pārvatī asked him, ‘Who is this golden Lord? Please tell me.

**46.** “‘Seeing your wonderful dancing and hearing the Name ‘Gaurāṅga’, my heart melts.

**47.** “‘I have understood that all the mantras and tantra I have heard about for so long are simply misery for the soul.

**48.** “‘So, please tell me about Lord Gaurāṅga. Serving Him, I will attain new life.’

**49.** “Hearing Pārvatī’s words and remembering Śrī Gaurāṅga, Lord Śiva spoke to Pārvatī.

**50.** “‘You are the primordial energy and an expansion of Śrī Rādhā. I will tell you the crown jewel of truths.

**51.** “‘This time, during Kali-yuga, Kṛṣṇa will take the heart of Rādhā and appear in Māyāpur in the womb of Śachī Devī.

**52.** “‘Maddened by His Pastimes of kīrtan, Lord Gorāmaṇi will distribute the jewel of divine love without judging its recipients.

**53.** “‘O goddess, shame on the life, enjoyment, and fortune of the souls who do not float in the waters of this flood of divine love!

**54.** “‘Remembering the promise of the Lord, I float in divine love. My heart has lost its composure, and thus I have left Kāśī.

**55.** “‘I will stay in a hut on the bank of the Gaṅgā in Māyāpur and worship Gaurāṅga.’

**56.** “Hearing Śiva’s words, beautiful Pārvatī quickly came to Sīmantadwīp.

**57.** “She constantly meditated on the form of Śrī Gaurāṅga. Chanting Gaura’s Name she floated in divine love, and her heart became unsteady.

**58.** “Eventually, Gaurachandra bestowed His mercy; He came with His associates and appeared to Pārvatī.

**59.** “His long form was the colour of molten gold, and His limbs and the wavy hair on His head appeared very beautiful.

**60.** “He wore a dhotī tucked in three places, and a wonderful flower garland swung on His neck.

**61.** “Stammering in divine love, Gaura Rāy said, ‘O Pārvatī! Why have you come here?’

**62.** “Pārvatī bowed at the feet of the Lord of the universe, and with an unsteady mind, explained her sadness.

**63.** “‘O Master! O Lord and Life of the universe! You are merciful to everyone, but cruel to me.

**64.** “‘O Saviour of the fallen, You have engaged me in imprisoning the souls who are averse to You.

**65.** “‘I perform this duty and manage the material world, but I am deprived of Your infinite divine love.

**66.** “‘People say that Māyā is absent wherever Kṛṣṇa is present. Thus, I have become completely estranged from You.

**67.** “‘O Lord, how will I see Your Pastimes? If You do not make a way for me, I will be hopeless.’

**68.** “Saying this, chaste Pārvatī Devī desperately rubbed Gaura’s foot-dust into the parting in her hair.

**69.** “Since then, this place has been known as Sīmantadwīp, though the ignorant call it Simuliyā.

**70.** “Moon-like Śrī Gaurāṅga was pleased and said, ‘O Pārvatī, listen to My words attentively.

**71.** “‘You are not separate from Me. You are My energy and the goddess of all. My consort, My singular energy, has two forms.

**72.** “‘As My personal energy, you are Rādhikā. As My external energy, Rādhā expands as you.

**73.** “‘Without you, My Pastimes are not complete. As Yogamāyā, you are certainly involved in My Pastimes.

**74.** “‘In Vraja, you are eternally present as Paurṇamāsī, and in Nabadwīp, you are present as Prauḍhā Māyā with Lord Śiva, the guardian of the Dhām.’

**75.** “Saying this, Śrī Gaurāṅga disappeared, and Pārvatī’s heart became overwhelmed with divine love.

**76.** “Out of love for Śrī Gaurāṅga, Pārvatī remains here as the goddess of Sīmantadwīp and in Māyāpur as Prauḍhā Māyā.”

**77.** Saying this, Nityānanda quickly entered Kāzī Nagar with Jīva.

**78.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “O Jīva, listen to My words. Kāzī Nagar is Mathurā.

**79.** “Here, Śrī Gaurāṅga performed kīrtan, delivered the Kāzī, and gave him the jewel of divine love.

**80.** “He who was Kaṁsa during Kṛṣṇa’s Pastimes in Mathurā became known as Chā̐d Kāzī during Gaurāṅga’s Pastimes.

**81.** “The Lord thus called the Kāzī His uncle. Out of fear, the Kāzī took shelter at Gaurāṅga’s feet.

**82.** “When Gaurāṅga began performing kīrtan, the Kāzī broke a mṛdaṅga and made a disturbance on the order of Nawab Hussein Shah.

**83.** “Hussein Shah, the emperor of Gauḍa at that time, was previously Jarāsandha. He influenced the Kāzī, who was his relative.

**84.** “In the form of Nṛsiṁha, the Lord frightened the Kāzī, and like Kaṁsa, the Kāzī cringed in fear.

**85.** “The Lord then gave him divine love and made him a great devotee. Those who hear of the Kāzī’s deliverance are fortunate.

**86–87.** “See the difference between Vraja and Nabadwīp: in Vraja, those who offend Kṛṣṇa attain static liberation, but in Nabadwīp, offenders attain the jewel of divine love. Thus, the Pastimes of Gaura are supreme.

**88.** “Gaura’s Abode, Name, Form, and Qualities do not consider offences and are adept at delivering souls.

**89.** “If offences are present in the mind of a practitioner, Kṛṣṇa’s Name and abode deliver them after a long time.

**90.** “Gaura’s Name and abode, however, immediately give souls divine love; offences do not obstruct them.

**91.** “O Jīva, see there the samādhi of the Kāzī. Seeing it dispels sickness and sorrow.”

**92.** Saying this, Nityānanda, smouldering with divine love, quickly went to Śaṅkha Vaṇik Nagar.

**93.** Arriving there, He said to Śrī Jīva, “See there the wonderful sight of Śaraḍāṅgā.

**94.** “The name Śaraḍāṅgā is also very beautiful. Jagannāth resides there with the śabaras.

**95.** “Previously, when a demon named Raktabāhu was on a rampage, the Lord came here with the śabaras.

**96.** “This place is nondifferent from Śrī Puruṣottam Kṣetra; Jagannāth certainly resides here eternally.”

**97.** They then passed through Tantuvāya Grām and saw the cottage of Kholāvechā Śrīdhar.

**98.** Nityānanda Prabhu said, “Here, Śrī Gaurāṅga chanted, rested, and blessed the devotees.

**99.** “This place thus became known as Viśrām Sthān. You may rest here in Śrīdhar’s cottage.”

**100.** When Śrīdhar heard of Nityānanda Prabhu’s arrival, he came, prostrated himself, and worshipped the Lord.

**101.** He said, “O Lord, be most merciful to this servant and rest here. This is my request.”

**102.** Nityānanda Prabhu replied, “You are very fortunate: Lord Gaurāṅga has bestowed His mercy upon you.

**103.** “Today, we will rest here.” Hearing this, Śrīdhar was satisfied.

**104.** With great care, Śrīdhar collected suitable ingredients and engaged a Vaiṣṇava brāhmaṇ to cook.

**105.** Once Nitāi and Śrīvās had been served, Śrī Jīva joyfully took their prasād.

**106.** Śrīdhar laid Nityānanda on a bed, and then he and his family massaged the Lord’s feet.

**107.** In the afternoon, Śrīvās took Śrī Jīva and joyfully showed him Ṣaṣṭi Tīrtha.

**108–109.** Śrīvās said, “Listen, O pure-hearted Jīva! Previously, when the gods heard that Mahāprabhu would descend in Nabadwīp, Viśvakarmā came to Nadia.

**110–111.** “To prevent a scarcity of water along the paths where the Lord would perform saṅkīrtan, Viśvakarmā dug sixty large ponds in one night, the last of which he made in the village of the Kāzī.

**112.** “Śrīdhar’s banana grove is beautiful to see. See also the lake nearby.

**113.** “Mahāprabhu sometimes played in the water there and took Śrīdhar’s bananas.

**114.** “Still today, Śrīdhar collects banana flowers and stems, and gives them to Mother Śachī with joy in his heart.

**115.** “Near here is a place called Mayāmāri. O Jīva, see that it still exists there today.

**116–117.** “Please hear a story from the Purāṇas: once when Baladev was on a pilgrimage, He came to Nabadwīp and rested. At that time, some brāhmaṇs told Him about a demon named Maya.

**118.** “Hearing of the havoc raised by Mayāsura, Baladev furiously confronted him in a field.

**119.** “The demon fought with Baladev ferociously, and finally Baladev killed him.

**120.** “Since then, this place has become known as Mayāmāri. Today, I have told you a very old story.

**121.** “This place is known as Tālavan in Vraja and always appears before the eyes of the fortunate souls.”

**122.** That night, they stayed there, and the next day they continued on, chanting, “Hari! Hari!”

**123.** This servant, whose aspiration is the shade of Nitāi and Jāhnavā’s feet, reveals the glories of Nadia.
