# Chapter 18. The Removal of Śrī Jīva Goswāmī’s Doubts and His Travelling to Vṛndāvan

**1.** All glory to the son of Śachī, Śrī Gaurāṅga, and the son of Padmāvatī, the life of Jāhnavā!

**2.** All glory to Advaita, Gadādhar, Śrīvās, and all the associates of the Lord!

**3.** Hearing Jīva’s question, Nityānanda Rāy explained a highly confidential truth in the assembly of Vaiṣṇavas.

**4.** “O Jīva, listen! Vṛndāvan Dhām and Nabadwīp Dhām are shelters for countless joyful souls.

**5.** “Pure souls are beyond material nature; they reside here in the Dhām eternally as associates of Kṛṣṇa.

**6.** “The Dhām is eternal, pure, and spiritual. Material time and space are suppressed here.

**7.** “Time and space in the Dhām are spiritual and joyful. Everything here always appears the opposite of material nature.

**8.** “Know that all the homes, gates, rivers, streams, forests, and courtyards here are spiritual and extremely beautiful.

**9.** “This is the abode of joy beyond material nature, the grand manifestation of Kṛṣṇa’s inconceivable energy.

**10.** “Kṛṣṇa’s greatest desire is for the souls’ deliverance, and thus the Dhām has descended here by Kṛṣṇa’s inconceivable energy.

**11.** “Matter is never present within the Dhām, and conditioned souls do not receive entrance here.

**12.** “The material energy spreads a covering over the Dhām and keeps it covered forever.

**13.** “Souls who have no relationship with Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya blindly reside on the surface of this covering.

**14.** “They think, ‘I am in Nabadwīp Dhām’, but Prauḍhā Māyā bewilders them and keeps them far away.

**15.** “If, by some fortune, souls attain the association of the sādhus, then their relationship with Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya develops.

**16.** “O Jīva, such relationships are a highly confidential subject, a fortune that conditioned souls do not easily understand.

**17.** “Someone may say, ‘Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya is my Lord’, but have no relationship with Him at heart and be constantly engrossed in māyā.

**18.** “Such persons reside on the surface of Māyā’s covering and never attain pure devotion. O Lord! O Lord!

**19.** “They are religious pretenders. They are always deceitful and devoid of humility. Because of their pride, they consider themselves wise.

**20.** “By the grace of the sādhus, however, they abandon their pride and willingly consider themselves lower than grass.

**21.** “Their tolerance becomes greater than that of a tree, and they become prideless and expert at honouring others.

**22.** “Enriched with these four qualities, they chant the glories of Kṛṣṇa, and their relationship with Śrī Chaitanya develops within the heart.

**23.** “There are five relationships with Śrī Kṛṣṇa: those of adoration, servitorship, friendship, affectionate guardianship, and paramour love.

**24.** “Sādhus serve Gaurāṅga in the relationships of adoration and servitorship and thereby attain relationships of affectionate guardianship and paramour love with Kṛṣṇa.

**25.** “The soul’s perfected mood that arises from their particular relationship with the Lord predominates in their service.

**26.** “Souls who differentiate between Gaura and Kṛṣṇa are wicked, and they never develop a relationship with Kṛṣṇa.

**27.** “Souls who develop, through the association of the sādhus, the qualities of humility, tolerance, pridelessness, and respectfulness, serve Gaurāṅga in a relationship of servitorship.

**28.** “The relationship of servitorship is full-fledged in the service of Gaurāṅga; the sādhus call Śrī Gaurāṅga ‘Mahāprabhu’.

**29.** “Souls who are qualified for a relationship of paramour love serve Gaura as Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa.

**30.** “Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa are united as our Śrī Gaurāṅga Rāy. The Pastimes of the Divine Couple, however, do not manifest in Him.

**31.** “When the relationship of servitorship matures in the heart of the soul, then the relationship of paramour love emerges.

**32–33.** “At that time, the worshippable Lord, Gaurahari, appears in Vraja as Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa and immerses the devotee in the ecstasy of the eternal Pastimes. In this way, the devotee attains the eternal Pastimes of Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vraja Dhām.

**34.** “This is the highly confidential relationship between Nabadwīp and Vraja: they are one, and yet they are also two, but the blind do not see this.

**35.** “Know this to be the essence of the relationship between Gaura and Kṛṣṇa. In the relationship of paramour love, Gaura’s form is the Divine Couple.

**36.** “O Jīva, soon Rūpa and Sanātan will teach you all these truths.

**37.** “The Lord has given you the qualification to stay in Vṛndāvan. O Jīva, do not delay going to Vraja any longer!”

**38.** Saying this, Nityānanda Prabhu placed His feet on Jīva’s head and empowered him.

**39.** In intense divine love, Śrī Jīva Goswāmī remained unconscious at the feet of Nityānanda for some time.

**40.** He rolled on the ground in the courtyard of Śrīvās, and all the symptoms of divine ecstasy shone in his body.

**41.** Crying, he said, “My misfortune is that I did not see the Pastimes of the Lord in Nadia with these eyes.

**42.** “Gaura Rāy performed Pastimes to deliver all souls. Having not seen those Pastimes, my days have passed in vain.”

**43.** Hearing that Śrī Jīva would go to Vraja, all the sādhus came to the courtyard of Śrīvās.

**44.** The elder devotees blessed Jīva, and the junior devotees prayed for his grace.

**45.** Joining his palms, Jīva said to all the devotees, “Please do not take any of my offences.

**46.** “You are all servants of Śrī Chaitanya and Gurus of this world. O wish-fulfilling trees, please bless this insignificant soul.

**47.** “May I have love and devotion to Śrī Kṛṣṇa Chaitanya, and may Nityānanda Prabhu be my shelter, birth after birth.

**48.** “I left home in my youth without understanding anything, and since then, you all have been the well-wishers in my life.

**49.** “Without the grace of the Vaiṣṇavas, I cannot attain Kṛṣṇa. O brothers, please give me the dust of the Vaiṣṇavas’ feet.”

**50–51.** Saying this, Jīva bowed and prayed to everyone, and took permission from Nityānanda Prabhu. Then he went to the feet of Śachī in the home of Jagannāth Miśra and with an eager heart took permission to go to Vraja.

**52.** Śachī Devī gave Jīva dust from her holy feet, blessed him, and bade him farewell.

**53.** Crying, Jīva crossed the Gaṅgā chanting, “O Gaurāṅga!”, and departed, knowing the order of the Lord to be all-in-all.

**54.** Walking ahead, Jīva soon crossed the border of Nabadwīp and left that infinitely glorious abode.

**55.** As he left Nabadwīp Dhām, Jīva prostrated himself fully and then departed for Vṛndāvan.

**56.** Vraja Dhām, the Yamunā, and Rūpa and Sanātan then arose in his heart.

**57.** At night along the path, Jīva saw Gaura Rāy in a dream. The Lord said to Jīva, “Go to Mathurā.

**58.** “You, Rūpa, and Sanātan are very dear to Me, and together you will write scriptures on devotion.

**59.** “Service to Me as the Divine Couple will be your life, and you will always see Their Pastimes in Vraja.”

**60.** After this dream, Jīva felt intense joy and swiftly ran towards Vraja Dhām.

**61.** The services Śrī Jīva Goswāmī performed after arriving in Vraja cannot be described.

**62.** Later, a fortunate soul will describe them, and the sādhus will hear about them with joy in their hearts.

**63.** The unfit and wicked-minded Bhakti Vinod has described the subject of visiting the Holy Dhām.

**64.** This is my prayer at the feet of the Vaiṣṇavas: may I have a relationship with Śrī Gaura.

**65.** May I soon reside in Nabadwīp and have a relationship with Śrī Gaura. This is my desire.

**66.** I am a worm in the ditch of mundanity and very misbehaved. I have no devotion. I am lustful and mad with anger.

**67.** How will a fallen soul and servant of māyā like me develop a relationship with Śrī Gaura?

**68.** May Nabadwīp Dhām bless me and appear in my heart. Then I will be delivered.

**69.** The genuine grace of Prauḍhā Māyā, the protectress of the Dhām, is my only hope to overcome the dangers of ignorance.

**70.** May Vṛddha Śiva, the protector of the Dhām, be merciful. May the divine Dhām appear before my eyes.

**71.** O residents of Nabadwīp and devotees of Gaurāṅga! Place your holy feet on the head of this sinner.

**72.** Everyone, please hear this prayer of mine so that I may quickly attain the feet of Śrī Chaitanya.

**73.** Receiving the order of Nityānanda and Śrī Jāhnavā, I have described Nabadwīp although I am very fallen.

**74.** Certainly this book has been written so as to be filled with the names ‘Nabadwīp’, ‘Gaura’, and ‘Nityānanda’.

**75.** Thus, this book is greatly purifying, and I will not be to blame for any faults in its composition.

**76.** May the devotees of Gaurāṅga always study this book and attain the result of circumambulating the Dhām.

**77.** When devotees discuss this book while circumambulating the Dhām, they attain a result a hundred times greater. This is stated by the scriptures.

**78.** This insignificant, lowly, and humble soul, whose aspiration is the shade of Nitāi and Jāhnavā’s feet, chants the glories of Nadia. Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur-kṛta Śrī-Navadvīpa-Dhāma-Māhātmyera Parikramā-khaṇḍa samāpta Thus ends the Parikramā Khaṇḍa of *Śrī Navadvīpa-dhāma-māhātmya* by Śrīla Bhakti Vinod Ṭhākur.
