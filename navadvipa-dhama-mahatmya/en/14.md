# Chapter 14. Śrī Modadrumadwīp and Śrī Rāma-Līlā

**1.** All glory to the life of the Pañcha Tattva, Śrī Gaurahari, and the best of all abodes, Śrī Nabadwīp!

**2.** Proceeding to Māmgāchhi Grām, Nityānanda Rāy said, “This is Modadrumadwīp. Ayodhyā is present here.

**3–4.** “In a previous manifestation of the universe, while He was residing in the forest, Rāma came here with Lakṣmaṇ and Sītā, built a cottage under a massive banyan tree, and lived here joyfully for some time.

**5.** “Seeing the lustre of Nabadwīp, Rāma smiled slightly.

**6.** “His lotus eyes, His beautiful bow in hand—how enchanting was His dark grass-green form!

**7.** “He wore the dress of a brahmachārī, and dreadlocks shone atop His head. Seeing Him captivated all living beings.

**8.** “Seeing His smiling face, Sītā Devī asked Śrī Rāma about the reason for His smile.

**9.** “Rāma replied, ‘O Sītā, listen! There is a very confidential answer.

**10.** “‘When the glorious Kali-yuga takes place, souls will see My golden form here in Nadia.

**11.** “‘I will soon take birth as Gaurāṅga from the womb of Śachī Devī in the home of Jagannāth Miśra.

**12.** “‘The fortunate souls will see My childhood Pastimes, and I will give them all the highest form of divine love.

**13.** “‘Dear Sītā, at that time, I will perform Pastimes of scholarship and reveal the glories of My Name.

**14.** “‘Later, I will take sannyās and go to Nīlāchal. My mother will embrace My wife and cry.’

**15.** “Hearing this, Sītā said, ‘O lotus-eyed Lord, why will You make Your mother cry?

**16.** “‘Why will You take sannyās and leave Your wife? What happiness is there in making Your wife unhappy? I do not understand.’

**17.** “Śrī Rāma answered, ‘My dear, You actually understand everything, but to teach the souls, You now act as if You were unaware.

**18.** “‘Loving devotion to Me is relished in two ways. O Sītā, please hear My words.

**19.** “‘The joy felt during union with Me is called *sambhoga*. The joy felt during separation from Me is called *vipralambha*.

**20.** “‘My devotees, My eternal associates, desire sambhoga, and by My grace, they experience vipralambha.

**21.** “‘Devotees know that the sadness felt for My sake during vipralambha is actually the greatest joy.

**22.** “‘When sambhoga occurs after vipralambha, the happiness felt is ten million times greater than before.

**23.** “‘Separation from Me is the cause of that happiness. Accept this as truth; the four Vedas affirm it.

**24.** “‘During My Pastimes as Śrī Gaurāṅga, Mother Kauśalyā, who is known in the Vedas as Aditi, will appear as Śachī Devī.

**25.** “‘You will serve Me as Viṣṇu Priyā. In separation from Me, You will make a Deity of My golden form.

**26.** “‘In separation from You, I will make a golden Sītā and worship You in Ayodhyā.

**27.** “‘In exchange, You will make a Deity of My golden form and worship Me in Nadia.

**28.** “‘O Sītā, these profound topics are very confidential. They will not be revealed to the public now.

**29.** “‘Nabadwīp is very dear to Me: neither Ayodhyā nor any other place is equal to it.

**30.** “‘O Sītā, when Kali-yuga arrives, this banyan tree, Rāma Vaṭa, will disappear but invisibly remain.’

**31.** “In this way Rāma, Sītā, and Lakṣmaṇ resided here for some time.

**32.** “Eventually, They went to the Daṇḍaka forest to perform Their Pastimes. See here the place of Rāma’s cottage.

**33.** “By the will of the Lord, Rāma’s friend Guhaka took birth here as the son of a brāhmaṇ.

**34.** “His name became Sadānanda Vipra Bhaṭṭāchārya. He knew nothing other than Rāma within the three worlds.

**35.** “On the day that our Lord took birth in Māyāpur, Sadānanda was present in the home of Jagannāth Miśra.

**36.** “At the time of the Lord’s birth, all the gods observed the child in the home of Jagannāth Miśra.

**37.** “The brāhmaṇ, a great devotee, recognised the gods and understood that our Lord had taken birth there.

**38.** “With great delight, the brāhmaṇ returned to his home. In meditation on his worshippable Lord, he saw Gaurāṅgasundar.

**39.** “Śrī Gaurāṅga Rāy was sitting on a throne, and Brahmā and the other gods were waving chāmaras beside Him.

**40.** “Then, the brāhmaṇ saw the dark, grass-green Rāmachandra and heroic Lakṣmaṇ, the abode of Ananta, nearby.

**41.** “On His left was Sītā, and His devotee Hanumān sat in front of Him. Seeing this, the brāhmaṇ understood the identity of the Lord.

**42.** “With great joy, the brāhmaṇ went to Māyāpur and joyfully saw Gaurāṅga to the satisfaction of his eyes.

**43.** “‘I am blessed! I am blessed!’ said the brāhmaṇ again and again. ‘Rāmachandra has appeared before me as Gaurāṅga!’

**44.** “Later, when the saṅkīrtan Pastimes of the Lord started, Sadānanda would dance and chant the Name of Gaura.

**45.** “O Jīva, here pure devotees see Śrī Bhāṇḍīravan.”

**46.** Hearing this and seeing the eternal abode, the devotees surrounded Nityānanda and danced.

**47.** Divine ecstasy manifested in the body of Śrī Jīva, and he exclaimed, “O Gaurāṅga!”

**48.** With a jubilant heart, Nityānanda stayed in that village in the home of Nārāyaṇī that day.

**49.** The perfectly pure and chaste Nārāyaṇī, the mother of Vyāsa, personally served the devotees.

**50.** The next day in the morning, the devotees walked some distance and peacefully entered Śrī Vaikuṇṭhapur.

**51.** To fulfil the order of Nitāi and Jāhnavā, this poor, lowly soul chants the glories of Nadia.
