# Chapter 8. Śrī Harihara Kṣetra, Śrī Vārāṇasī, and Śrī Godruma

**1.** All glory to the son of Śachī! All glory to the Avadhūt!

**2.** All glory to Advaita Āchārya, the leader of the devotees! All glory to Gadādhar and all the devotees!

**3.** All glory to beautiful Nabadwīp Dhām! All glory to the Name of Gaura!

**4.** Nitāi and the devotees then departed, chanting “Hari! Hari!”

**5.** Swaying to and fro in ecstasy, Nitāi walked and stammered with divine love.

**6.** Tears streamed from His eyes. Chanting, “Gorā! Gorā!”, He became overwhelmed.

**7.** His ornaments and garlands sparkled, and His form illumined the ten directions.

**8.** Śrīvās danced with Jīva. Sometimes they cried, and sometimes they danced continuously.

**9.** All the other devotees danced as they walked.

**10.** Coming to the Alakānandā River, Nitāi spoke, floating in joy.

**11.** “Flowing west of Bilvapakṣa Grām, the Mandākinī comes and surrounds Nadia.

**12.** “Then she separates from the Alakānandā where we saw Suvarṇa Bihār.

**13.** “On the eastern shore of the Alakānandā, beside the Gaṇḍaka River, is Harihara Kṣetra.

**14.** “In time, a Deity will manifest there, and a beautiful forest will shine gloriously.

**15.** “See Kāśī to the west of the Alakānandā. There the followers of Śiva and Śakti worship the maidservant Liberation.

**16–17.** “This place is superior to Vārāṇasī because here Lord Śiva constantly chants, “Gaura! Gaura!”, dances holding his trident, and offers his followers devotion to Gaura.

**18–19.** “Here, devotees chant Gaurāṅga’s Name, dance, and kick away the liberation that sannyāsīs attain by residing in Kāśī and practising jñāna-yoga for a thousand years.

**20.** “Here, when souls pass away, Śiva chants the Name of Gaura into their ears and delivers them.

**21.** “This place is thus Mahāvārāṇasī. Souls have no fear of death here.”

**22.** Saying this, Nitāi danced and offered Jīva divine love for Gaurahari.

**23.** Lord Śiva then invisibly bowed at the feet of Nitāi.

**24.** Chanting the Name of Gaurāṅga, Śiva and Pārvatī fulfilled their desires.

**25.** Then the independent Lord, Nitāi, left with the devotees.

**26.** Arriving in Gādigāchhā Grām, Nityānanda came over and spoke, smiling.

**27.** “This island is called Godruma. Surabhi resides here eternally.

**28–29.** “When Indra, bewildered by Kṛṣṇa’s illusion, flooded Gokula with his powerful rains, Kṛṣṇa lifted Govardhan Hill and carefully protected Gokula.

**30.** “After Indra’s pride was crushed, Indra recognised K ṛṣṇ a.

**31.** “To clear his offence, he bowed and grasped the feet of Kṛṣṇa.

**32.** “Kṛṣṇa, the ocean of mercy, forgave Indra and gave him the boon of fearlessness.

**33.** “Still, Indra remained fearful. He then spoke to Surabhi.

**34.** “‘I could not understand the Pastimes of Kṛṣṇa, and I committed a very heavy offence.

**35.** “‘I have heard that during Kali-yuga, Kṛṣṇa will perform wonderful Pastimes in Nadia.

**36.** “‘At that time, I will become bewildered and be an offender again.

**37.** “‘O Surabhi, you know everything. Please advise me about this.’

**38.** “Surabhi said, ‘Let us go and serve Nimāi in Nabadwīp Dhām.’

**39.** “Indra and Surabhi moved here and worshipped Gaurāṅga.

**40.** “Worshipping Gaurāṅga is very easy, and the results of doing so are easily distributed.

**41.** “They cried, chanting the Name of Gaurāṅga, and immediately saw Him.

**42–43.** “How extraordinary is His beauty and form! They saw the figure of Gaurāṅga—the embodiment of benevolence, the well of rasa—smiling and stammering, choked with divine love.

**44.** “Smiling, My Lord said, ‘I know your desire.

**45.** “‘I will appear in Nadia soon, and you will see Me fully then.

**46.** “‘During those Pastimes, you will serve Me, and the net of māyā will no longer bind you.’

**47.** “Saying this, the Lord disappeared. Beautiful Surabhi remained there.

**48.** “She stayed near a banyan tree and constantly worshipped the feet of Gaura.

**49.** “This place thus became known as Godrumadwīp. The Lord fulfils the desires of the devotees here.

**50.** “Anyone who builds a hut and serves here easily becomes immersed in the feet of Gaura.

**51.** “There is a wonderful story about Mārkaṇḍeya Muni, who was present on this island during the destruction of the universe.

**52.** “Mārkaṇḍeya Muni attained a lifetime of seven kalpas, and during the destruction of the universe, he underwent great difficulty.

**53.** “Everything became submerged in water, and he searched for a place to stay.

**54.** “Floating and floating about, he thought, ‘Alas! Why have I taken this boon?’

**55.** “During the destruction, only the thirty-two miles of Nabadwīp Dhām arise and give shelter to the devotees.

**56.** “Floating in the waves of the water, the sage suddenly lost consciousness.

**57.** “Being very merciful to him, Surabhi carefully rescued the sage.

**58.** “Regaining consciousness, Mārkaṇḍeya Muni saw the wonders of Godrumadwīp.

**59.** “He saw an abode spanning two billion miles around, resplendent with rivers and streams.

**60.** “Numerous trees and vines were shining, and birds were chanting the glories of Śrī Gaura.

**61.** “He saw a banyan tree that was eight miles wide, and he saw Surabhi beneath it.

**62.** “Stricken with hunger, the sage spoke to Surabhi.

**63.** “‘O goddess! Please save my life. Rescue me by giving me some milk.’

**64.** “Surabhi mercifully fed the sage with her milk.

**65.** “Revitalised, Mārkaṇḍeya Muni spoke to Surabhi again.

**66.** “‘O goddess, you are my mother, and the world is absorbed in your illusion.

**67.** “‘Not understanding the consequences, I accepted a boon of living for seven kalpas without dying.

**68.** “‘During the destruction of the universe, there are numerous miseries and extreme distress. There is no happiness.

**69.** “‘O Mother, tell me, what should I do? How can I overcome this distress?’

**70.** “Surabhi said, ‘Serve the feet of Śrī Gaura.

**71.** “‘This abode of Nabadwīp is beyond material nature—it is never destroyed.

**72.** “‘To fleshy eyes, it is only thirty-two miles, but it is the supreme abode Vaikuṇṭha and eternally faultless.

**73.** “‘Here, time and space are supramundane, and no one knows of material illusion.

**74.** “‘See the great wonder of Nabadwīp! The chaste Virajā flows all around it.

**75.** “‘Every part of Nabadwīp measures two billion miles, and at its centre is the grand city of Māyāpur.

**76.** “‘The eight islands of Nabadwīp are petals, and Antardwīp is their whorl.

**77.** “‘All the holy places, gods, and sages reside here and worship Gaurāṅga.

**78.** “‘O Mārkaṇḍeya, understand your fortune and take shelter at the feet of Gaurāṅga.

**79.** “‘Take shelter of the true dharma, and keep desires for enjoyment and liberation far away.

**80.** “‘By taking shelter of Gaurāṅga and serving Him, you will attain divine love in madhura-rasa.

**81.** “When that divine love appears in your heart, it will set you afloat in the rasa of the arts within the Pastimes of the Lord.

**82.** “‘You will take shelter at the feet of Rādhā in Vraja, and your heart will be engaged in the service of the Divine Couple.

**83.** “‘Know that the joy of service is unparalleled, and knowledge of static liberation is useless.’

**84.** “Hearing the words of Surabhi, the sage immediately joined his palms and spoke.

**85.** “‘When I serve the feet of Śrī Gaura, what will become of my destiny?’

**86.** “Surabhi then spoke the ultimate conclusion: ‘There is no judgement in the service of Śrī Gaura.

**87.** “‘When you call the Name of Śrī Gaura, all your karma will be destroyed.

**88.** “‘No reactions will remain any longer, and your attachment to material existence will disappear.

**89.** “‘And not only your karma, but the consequences of your pursuing liberation will also be eradicated.

**90.** “‘You will become immersed in the ecstasy of Gaurāṅga, living on this island and serving Him.’

**91.** “Hearing this, Mārkaṇḍeya floated in joy. He chanted the Name of Gaura, cried, and sometimes laughed.

**92.** “Jīva, see here the wonderful place where Mārkaṇḍeya received life.”

**93.** Hearing the glories of Gaurāṅga from the mouth of Nitāi, Jīva floated in the greatest joy.

**94.** They spent the day in that place and then went to Madhyadwīp, chanting “Hari!”

**95–96.** Knowing Nitāi and Jāhnavā’s feet to be all-in-all, the fallen Bhakti Vinod holds the order of Nitāi on his head and describes the glories of Nadia.
