# Chapter 1. The Glories of the Dhām

**1.** All glory to the son of Śachī, the moon of Nabadwīp! All glory to the avadhūt Nityānanda Rāy!

**2.** All glory to the worshippable Śrī Advaita Prabhu! All glory to Gadādhar Paṇḍit and Śrīvās Paṇḍit!

**3.** All glory to Nabadwīp Dhām, the best of all abodes! All glory to the residents of Nabadwīp, the associates of Śrī Gaura!

**4.** Bowing at the feet of all the devotees, I will briefly describe Nabadwīp Dhām.

**5.** The glories of Nabadwīp Maṇḍal are unlimited. Brahmā and the other gods do not know them. So, who can describe them?

**6.** Ananta Śeṣa is unable to describe them with thousands of mouths. So, how will I, a tiny soul, be able to?

**7.** It is true that the glories of Nabadwīp are unlimited. The god of gods, Lord Śiva, cannot reach their end.

**8.** Still, the will of the moon-like Śrī Chaitanya is all-powerful, and under its control, the devotees give orders.

**9.** By the will of Śrī Chaitanya, the devotees have ordered me, and so I describe the glories of Nadia by their mercy.

**10.** There is another very confidential subject, which I do not want to mention, yet cannot but mention.

**11.** When Śrī Chaitanya disappeared, He ordered the devotees to reveal His abode and Pastimes.

**12.** Of all Avatārs, our moon-like Śrī Chaitanya is the most confidential known in this world.

**13.** His confidential Pastimes are mentioned confidentially in the scriptures. They are not revealed in the hearts of non-devotees.

**14.** For a long time, Māyā Devī hid the confidential scriptures that existed about His Pastimes.

**15–16.** Māyā Devī covered the eyes of the scholars and at all times kept secret all the descriptions of Śrī Chaitanya throughout both the manifest and unmanifest scriptures.

**17.** When the deep Pastimes of Śrī Gaura became unmanifest, Māyā understood the will of the Lord and withdrew her deception.

**18.** Māyā lifted her veil from the eyes of the souls and revealed the truth about Śrī Gaura to the material world.

**19.** The hidden scriptures then appeared clearly and dispelled the doubts of all souls.

**20.** The extremely merciful Nityānanda Prabhu revealed the truth about Śrī Gaura in the hearts of the souls.

**21.** Receiving His order, Māyā removed her veil, and the wise devotees discovered the wealth of the scriptures.

**22.** Those whose doubts are not dispelled by this are unfortunate. Why do such persons meaninglessly continue their lives?

**23.** Whenever and howsoever the Lord bestows His mercy, the fortunate souls become extremely happy.

**24–25.** Everyone should know the symptoms of misfortune: to consider your own intelligence to be great and not accept the mercy of the Lord. Through false arguments, souls fall into Māyā’s traps again and again.

**26.** “O souls of Kali-yuga, come! Give up duplicity, and accept pure divine love for Śrī Gaurāṅga in full!”

**27.** Saying this, Nityānanda calls out again and again. Still, unfortunate souls do not accept.

**28.** Let us consider carefully why souls disregard divine love.

**29.** In various ways, all souls pursue happiness. In this world, some souls study logic, and others practise yoga.

**30.** For the sake of happiness, some leave home and go to the forest, and others wage wars between nations.

**31.** For the sake of happiness, some run after women and wealth, and others pursue the arts and sciences.

**32.** For the sake of happiness, some give up on happiness and learn to be miserable, and others drown themselves in the ocean.

**33.** Raising His arms, Nityānanda calls, “O souls! Give up the misery of exploitation and renunciation, and come with Me!

**34.** “You are searching for happiness. I will give that to you, and I will not take anything from you in return.

**35.** “You will not undergo any trouble, loss, or pain. Simply chant, ‘Śrī Gaurāṅga’ and dance without a care.

**36.** “The happiness I will give you is unparalleled. It is pure, eternal joy, free from all illusion.”

**37.** In this way, Nityānanda Prabhu begs everyone to accept divine love, but the unfortunate, as a result of their sins, do not want it.

**38.** The unlimited sins of anyone who once chants, “Gaurāṅga! Nitāi!”are destroyed.

**39.** Everyone, please listen to another confidential subject. The wealth of Gaura’s Pastimes is appropriate for the souls of Kali-yuga.

**40.** Gaurahari, as Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa, plays with the sakhīs eternally in Vṛndāvan.

**41.** Through the scriptures, the souls understood the nature of Pastimes in Vraja, and the greatness of Vraja and Rādhā-Kṛṣṇa’s eternal Pastimes there.

**42.** Through the scriptures, the whole world understood the unlimited glories of Kṛṣṇa’s Name and Kṛṣṇa’s abode.

**43.** Still, in general, souls did not attain divine love for Kṛṣṇa. What is the reason for this? Consider it within your heart.

**44.** In this regard, there is a highly confidential subject that souls bewildered by Māyā do not consider.

**45.** When souls serve Kṛṣṇa for many births but do not develop divine love, then certainly they have made offences.

**46.** When souls, free from offences, chant the Name of Kṛṣṇa, they quickly attain divine love for Kṛṣṇa.

**47.** The special characteristic of Śrī Chaitanya’s Pastimes is that souls attain the wealth of divine love even while they commit offences.

**48.** Pure divine love for Kṛṣṇa searches for souls who call out “Nitāi-Chaitanya!”

**49.** Offences do not obstruct such souls, and their eyes cry with pure love for Kṛṣṇa.

**50.** Within a short time, offences leave on their own. The souls’ hearts become purified, and divine love develops within them.

**51.** The offences of the souls in Kali-yuga are unlimited and unavoidable. There is no deliverance for the soul without the Name of Gaura.

**52.** Thus, in Kali-yuga, I do not see any way without Gaura, and the scriptures proclaim this.

**53.** Gaurachandra appeared in Nabadwīp, the crown atop all the holy places.

**54.** In other holy places, offenders are punished, but in Nabadwīp offences are always forgiven.

**55.** The brothers Jagāi and Mādhāi are evidence of this. They committed offences but attained Nitāi and Chaitanya.

**56.** Brother! Let alone other holy places, offenders and demons are punished even in Vraja.

**57.** In Nabadwīp, souls who commit hundreds and hundreds of offences are easily delivered by the mercy of Nitāi.

**58.** The sages say that the land of Śrī Nabadwīp Dhām in Śrī Gauḍa Maṇḍal is most glorious.

**59.** Brother, souls who reside in Nabadwīp are very fortunate. They attain devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

**60.** Souls who once visit Nabadwīp are freed from all offences.

**61.** The scriptures say that by remembering Nabadwīp you attain that which pilgrims attain by visiting all the other holy places.

**62.** Souls who see Nabadwīp attain the wealth of divine love for Kṛṣṇa birth after birth.

**63.** Even souls who go to Nabadwīp with a materialistic motivation do not take a human birth again.

**64.** All the scriptures say that while walking through Nabadwīp, such souls attain the result of ten million horse sacrifices with each step.

**65.** Those who reside in Nabadwīp and chant their mantra—that holy mantra comes alive and easily delivers them.

**66.** That which yogīs attain after ten years in other holy places is attained in three nights in Nabadwīp.

**67.** The liberation attained through knowledge of Brahma in other holy places is attained in Nabadwīp simply by bathing in the Gaṅgā.

**68.** In Nabadwīp, even without knowledge of Brahma, seekers of liberation can attain residence in the Lord’s abode, a form like the Lord’s, opulence like the Lord’s, association with the Lord, and absorption into the Lord.

**69.** Enjoyment and liberation personified bow at the feet of the pure devotees in Nabadwīp and always wait upon them as maidservants.

**70.** Although devotees kick them and drive them away, these maidservants do not leave the feet of the devotees or run away.

**71.** Brother, by spending one night in Nabadwīp, souls attain that which is gained by residing in the seven principal holy places for one hundred years.

**72.** Thus, Nabadwīp Dhām is the best of all abodes. Souls who take shelter here in Kali-yuga are delivered.

**73.** The two forms of knowledge—that which liberates and that which nourishes—constantly and perfectly serve the residents of Nabadwīp.

**74.** Bhakti Vinod, whose aspiration is the shade of Nitāi and Jāhnavā’s feet, sings the glories of Nadia and rejoices.
