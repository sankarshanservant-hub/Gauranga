# Chapter 16. Śrī Bilvapakṣa and Śrī Bharadvāja Ṭilā

**1.** All glory to the reveller of Nadia, Gaurachandra! All glory to the Lord of Ekachakrā, Nityānanda Prabhu!

**2.** All glory to the Lord of Śāntipur, Advaita Āchārya, and the resident of Rāmachandrapur Gadādhar Paṇḍit!

**3.** All glory to the land of Gauḍa, made of pure spiritual gemstone, where Kṛṣṇa performed Pastimes during Kali-yuga!

**4.** Nityānanda Prabhu crossed the Gaṅgā, went a little ways ahead, and said, “O devotees, look!

**5.** “This enchanting place is named Bilvapakṣa. Everyone calls it Bel Pukhariyā.

**6.** “See here in Nabadwīp the place which the scriptures call Bilvavan in Vraja Dhām.

**7–8.** “Śiva, the lord of bael, resides here. After a group of noble brāhmaṇs satisfied him, having worshipped him with bael leaves for a fortnight, Śiva gave them the boon of devotion to Kṛṣṇa.

**9.** “Amongst the br ā hma ṇ s was Nimb ā ditya, who worshipped Ś iva exceptionally well.

**10.** “Mercifully, Śiva said to him, ‘On the edge of this village is a divine bael forest.

**11.** “‘The four Kumāras are meditating in that forest, and by their grace, you will attain divine knowledge.

**12.** “‘The four Kumāras are your Gurus, and through their service, you will attain everything here.’

**13.** “Saying this, Śiva disappeared. Nimbāditya then searched and found the place.

**14–16.** “In the bael forest, he saw the four Kumāras—the sages Sanak, Sananda, Sanātan, and Sanat Kumār—seated on a beautiful platform beside Śiva, unseen by others. They were unclothed, very young, and of noble character.

**17.** “Greatly excited upon seeing them, Nimbāditya Āchārya happily called out, ‘Hare Kṛṣṇa! Hare Kṛṣṇa!’

**18.** “Hearing the Name of the Lord, their meditation broke, and they saw the form of a Vaiṣṇava in front of them.

**19.** “Seeing the Vaiṣṇava, they were pleased, and one by one they embraced Nimbāditya.

**20.** “‘Who are you? Why have you come here? Please introduce yourself. We will certainly fulfil your prayers.’

**21.** “Hearing their words, Nimbāditya prostrated and humbly introduced himself.

**22.** “Upon hearing Nimbārka’s introduction, Śrī Sanat Kumār spoke with a smiling face.

**23.** “‘Knowing that Kali-yuga would be dark, the merciful Lord decided to preach devotion.

**24.** “‘He empowered four devotees and sent them to preach devotion throughout the world.

**25.** “‘Rāmānuja, Madhva Āchārya, and Viṣṇu Swāmī are three of them. You are the fourth of these great devotees.

**26.** “‘Lakṣmī Devī accepted Rāmānuja, Brahmā accepted Madhva Āchārya, and Śiva accepted Viṣṇu Swāmī.

**27.** “‘Today, we have met you personally. We will make you our disciple and thus become fortunate. This is our purpose.

**28.** “‘Previously, we meditated on nonduality, but by the grace of the Lord, such sin has left us.

**29.** “‘We now understand that pure devotion is supremely relishable, and we have written a scripture about it.

**30.** “‘Its name is *Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā*. You will certainly be initiated into its teachings.’

**31.** “Seeing the grace of his Guru, the wise Nimbārka immediately bathed in the Gaṅgā and returned.

**32.** “He prostrated himself and humbly said, ‘O lord! Saviour of the fallen! Deliver this lowly soul.’

**33.** “The four Kumāras gave him a mantra to worship the Divine Couple and taught him how to worship on the path of love.

**34.** “Receiving the mantra, Nimbāditya performed worship in this holy place according to the teachings of the *Sanat-kumāra-saṁhitā*.

**35.** “Mercifully, Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa revealed Themselves to him, and the lustre of Their forms illuminated the four directions.

**36.** “Gently smiling, They said, ‘O Nimbāditya, you are fortunate: you have performed worship here.

**37.** “‘Nabadwīp is very dear to Us. Here, We are present in one form as the son of Śachī Devī.’

**38.** “As They spoke, They revealed Their form as Gaurāṅga. Seeing this form, Nimbāditya was overwhelmed.

**39.** “He said, ‘I have never seen or heard of this extraordinary form anywhere.’

**40.** “Mahāprabhu then mercifully said, ‘O great soul, for now, keep this form a secret.

**41.** “‘Preach devotion to Kṛṣṇa and the Pastimes of the Divine Couple. Their Pastimes are My greatest joy.

**42.** “‘When I appear in the form of Gaura, I will find great pleasure in Pastimes of scholarship.

**43.** “‘At that time, you will take birth in Kāśmīr and travel throughout the land of Bhārat, conquering all directions.

**44.** “‘Everyone, everywhere, will call you Keśava Kāśmīrī and praise you for being highly learned.

**45.** “‘While travelling, you will come to Nabadwīp Dhām and stay in the village of Māyāpur.

**46.** “‘Hearing your name, the greatest scholars in Nabadwīp will flee.

**47.** “‘At that time, immersed in Pastimes of scholarship, I will defeat you and feel great pleasure.

**48.** “‘By the grace of Saraswatī, you will understand My identity, take shelter of Me, and give up your pride.

**49.** “‘Then, I will give you devotion and send you to preach devotion again.

**50.** “‘So for now, satisfy Me by preaching the conception of dualistic nondualism and keep My identity secret.

**51.** “‘When I start My saṅkīrtan Pastimes, I will preach the essence of your conception.

**52.** “‘I will accept two essential principles from Madhva Āchārya. One is his refutation of exclusive nondualism.

**53.** “‘O great soul, know the second essential principle to be serving Kṛṣṇa’s Deity form, knowing the Deity to be eternal.

**54.** “‘From Rāmānuja, I will take two essential principles: exclusive devotion and service to the devotees.

**55.** “‘From Viṣṇu Swāmī, I will accept two essential principles: surrender to the Lord and the path of love.

**56.** “‘From you, I will accept two most essential principles: taking shelter of Rādhārāṇī exclusively and serving in the mood of the gopīs.’

**57.** “Saying this, Gaurachandra disappeared, and Nimbāditya cried profusely with divine love.

**58.** “Nimbāditya then bowed at the lotus feet of his Guru and travelled elsewhere, intent upon preaching devotion to Kṛṣṇa.”

**59–60.** From afar, Nityānanda Prabhu showed Rāma Tīrtha to Jīva, where Balarām killed Kolāsura and bathed in the Gaṅgā with the Yadus. This place is now known as Rukmapur.

**61.** It is at one end of the route to circumambulate Nabadwīp. It is especially glorious during the month of Kārtik.

**62.** Leaving Bilvapakṣa, Nityānanda Prabhu and the devotees went to Bharadvāja Ṭilā.

**63.** Nityānanda said, “The best of the sages, Bharadvāja Muni, visited Śrī Gaṅgā Sāgar and then came here.

**64.** “The great sage stayed here for some time worshipping Śrī Gaurāṅgachandra.

**65.** “Satisfied by the worship of the sage, Viśvambhar revealed His form to the sage with a compassionate heart.

**66.** “He said to the sage, ‘Your desire will be fulfilled: you will see Me at the time of My appearance.’

**67.** “Saying this, the Lord disappeared, and Bharadvāja Muni fainted with intense divine love.

**68.** “The great sage stayed here for some time on this hill and then went to visit other holy places.

**69.** “People call this place Bhāruiḍāṅgā. The scriptures say that it is a great holy place.”

**70.** While speaking, the party arrived in Māyāpur. Īśān Ṭhākur came forward and received them.

**71.** Nityānanda Prabhu danced with intense divine love, and all the Vaiṣṇavas joined together and chanted.

**72–73.** The home of Jagannāth Miśra, where Śrī Gaurāṅga descended with His Name, is the best of all holy places. That day, the Lord’s mother personally fed the devotees in the Lord’s home.

**74.** What sort of joy arose there? It was indescribable, and the Nām-saṅkīrtan erupted tumultuously.

**75.** Bhakti Vinod, whose aspiration is the shade of Nitāi and Jāhnavā’s feet, chants the Pastimes of the Lord in Nadia.
