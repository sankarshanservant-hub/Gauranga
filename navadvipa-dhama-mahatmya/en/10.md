# Chapter 10. Visiting Śrī Brāhmaṇa Puṣkara, Śrī Uchcha Haṭṭa, and Other Places

**1.** All glory to Gaurāṅga, Nityānanda, and Advaita! All glory to Gadādhar and Śrīvās Paṇḍit!

**2.** All glory to Nabadwīp, the abode of pure love and devotion! All glory to the Names of Gaura and Nityānanda!

**3.** O souls of Kali-yuga, listen! Abandon renunciation and exploitation, reject religion and irreligion, and serve Nitāi and Chaitanya!

**4.** They are oceans of mercy. Brother! They will freely give you the essence of Vraja’s joy.

**5.** At dawn, Nityānanda Rāy took Jīva and went out to tour the Dhām.

**6.** He said, “Jīva, look at this beautiful village. Everyone now calls it Brāhmaṇpur.

**7.** “All the scriptures call it Brāhmaṇ Puṣkara. A very confidential Pastime took place here.

**8.** “During Satya-yuga, a brāhmaṇ named Diva Dās left home and visited all the holy places.

**9.** “He felt the greatest joy at Puṣkara Tīrtha. Still, he travelled, and eventually he came to Nabadwīp.

**10.** “Here, during the night, he had a dream in which he was told, ‘O brāhmaṇ, reside here and you will attain eternal wealth.’

**11.** “Diva Dās built a cottage and resided here into his old age.

**12.** “In his old age, the great brāhmaṇ was unable to travel, but he desired to see Puṣkara.

**13.** “Unable to travel, the brāhmaṇ cried, ‘I will never see Puṣkara again.’

**14.** “The Lord of Puṣkara felt compassion and revealed himself to Diva Dās in the form of a brāhmaṇ.

**15.** “He said to Diva Dās, ‘O brāhmaṇ, do not cry. There is a beautiful pond in front of you.

**16.** “‘Bathe in this pond once, and you will see Puṣkara Tīrtha.’

**17.** “Hearing this, the great brāhmaṇ bathed in the pond. Attaining divine eyes, he saw Puṣkara before him.

**18.** “Crying, the brāhmaṇ said to Puṣkara, ‘You have taken great trouble for me.’

**19.** “Puṣkara said, ‘Listen, O fortunate brāhmaṇ! I have not come from far away. I live here.

**20.** “‘Nabadwīp Dhām contains all holy places; all holy places reside here and serve Nabadwīp.

**21.** “‘Although a form of mine exists in the west, I myself live here eternally.

**22.** “‘Souls attain here the result attained by bathing a hundred times there.

**23.** “‘Thus, anyone who leaves Nabadwīp desiring to visit other holy places is a foolish, unfortunate soul.

**24.** “‘When souls attain the result of visiting all other holy places, then they can reside in Nabadwīp.

**25.** “‘See there the raised area that resembles a market. Kurukṣetra and Brahmāvarta exist there.

**26.** “‘With the Saraswatī and Dṛṣadvatī Rivers on its two sides, it shines brightly and bestows good fortune.

**27.** “‘O brāhmaṇ, I will tell you a secret. Very soon there will be great joy here.

**28.** “‘Gaurāṅgasundar will appear in the home of Śachī in Māyāpur and broadly distribute divine love.

**29.** “‘In all these places, the Lord will dance in the ecstasy of saṅkīrtan with the devotees and become mad.

**30.** “‘The Lord will perform kīrtan with all the devotees of all His previous Avatārs.

**31.** “‘He will inundate the entire world with a flood of divine love. Everyone except the speculators will attain intense divine love.

**32.** “‘O Diva Dās, souls who are faithful to the Dhām and reside here will reach the feet of Gaura.

**33.** “Unfortunate souls worship Kṛṣṇa for billions of years but do not attain attraction to His Name.

**34.** “‘If souls worship Gaurāṅga, their wickedness goes away, and they quickly attain Rādhā and Kṛṣṇa in Vraja Dhām.

**35.** “‘They attain their spiritual body and the shelter of the sakhīs, and serve the Divine Couple in their own grove.

**36.** “‘O brāhmaṇ, stay and serve here. You will see Śrī Gaurāṅga with His associates.’

**37.** “Saying this, the Lord of Puṣkara left. The brāhmaṇ then heard a voice come from the sky, ‘Glory to the Age of Kali!

**38.** “‘O brāhmaṇ, you will be born again at that time and swim in the divine love of Śrī Gaura’s kīrtan.’

**39.** “Hearing this, Diva Dās became fearless. He resided on the bank of the pond and performed worship.”

**40.** After telling Śrī Jīva these ancient stories, Nityānanda entered Uchcha Haṭṭa, Kurukṣetra.

**41.** Nityānanda said, “All the gods came here with Kurukṣetra Tīrtha.

**42.** “All the holy places in Brahmāvarta and Kurukṣetra came and resided here.

**43.** “Pṛthūdaka and the other holy places resided here and served Nabadwīp without difficulty.”

**44.** By staying here for one night, souls attain the full result of residing in Kurukṣetra for a hundred years.

**45.** Nityānanda Prabhu continued, “The gods resided here, established a market, and discussed Gaurāṅga.

**46.** “The name of this place thus became Haṭṭaḍāṅgā. By seeing this place, souls enter an ocean of divine love.

**47.** “O Jīva, see here one of the boundaries of Nadia. Let us now cross the Gaṅgā.”

**48.** Crossing the Gaṅgā, Nityānanda Prabhu arrived in Koladwīp at midday.

**49.** On the way to Kuliyāpāhāḍapur, Nitāichā̐d spoke to Śrī Jīva.

**50.** “The way we have come, crossing the Gaṅgā, is the proper way to circumambulate the Dhām.

**51–52.** “One evening, while performing saṅkīrtan with fourteen mṛdaṅgas, Śrī Chaitanya left Māyāpur with His associates and came to purify the Kāzī.

**53.** “That night, like a night of Brahmā, did not end quickly. Mahāprabhu toured His abode in this way.

**54.** “Thereafter, My Lord toured performing saṅkīrtan on every Ekādaśī.

**55.** “Sometimes He toured the ten miles of Antardwīp, and sometimes, as He desired, He toured for sixteen miles.

**56.** “From His home, He would leave Bārakoṇā Ghāt and go around Ballāl Dīrghikā to Śrīdhar’s house.

**57.** “From there, He would tour around the boundary of Antardwīp and then return. In this way, He would easily circumambulate for ten miles.

**58.** “Other times, passing Simuliyā, He would go to the house of the Kāzī, speak with Śrīdhar, and then come to Gādigāchhā.

**59.** “From Mājidā, He would cross the Gaṅgā and tour Pāraḍāṅgā and Chhināḍāṅgā along the broad riverbank.

**60.** “Then He would cross the Gaṅgā again and return to His home, thus touring for sixteen miles.

**61.** “A complete circumambulation is the full thirty-two miles. The Lord is pleased if someone completes this.

**62.** “I am taking you on this complete circumambulation. There is no circumambulation equal to it.

**63.** “During this circumambulation, you will see all thirty-two miles of the Dhām and the twelve forests of Vṛndāvan.

**64.** “This circumambulation is completed over the course of nine nights; the scriptures call it the ‘nava-rātra parikramā’.

**65.** “Devotees perform the ten-mile circumambulation in one day and complete the sixteen-mile circumambulation over the course of three nights.

**66.** “One night is in Māyāpur, the second is in Godrumadwīp, and the third night is in Śrī Pulina. Devotees tour in this way.”

**67.** Hearing about the process of circumambulation, Śrī Jīva Goswāmī became overwhelmed with divine love for some time.

**68.** This poor, fallen soul, whose aspiration is the shade of Nitāi and Jāhnavā’s feet, describes the glories of Nadia.
