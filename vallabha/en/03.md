# 3. Chaitanya in the Bhaktamals (Nabhadas, Dhruvadas, Raghavdas)

*The Bhaktamal ("Garland of Devotees") is a genre of North Indian hagiography in Braj-bhasha: short verses of praise
on saints of many schools. Translated here are all the verses found in three seventeenth-century Bhaktamals that name
Sri Krishna-Chaitanya, Nityananda and the Lord's associates: those of **Nabhadas** (Ramanandi, c. 1600), **Dhruvadas**
(Radhavallabhi, 1620s–1640s) and **Raghavdas** (Dadu-panthi, 1660). These are testimonies from outside the Gaudiya
sampradaya — the earliest from outside Bengal; but they are praise, not narratives of lilas, — **in the lila database
they are category D.** The original is given from early twentieth-century editions (see the head of each section); the
verse number is that of the edition, and differences of numbering are noted. The editors' commentaries and notes were
not translated. The translation follows the text; the Gaudiya position and comparison with the Chaitanya-charitamrita
(CC) and the Chaitanya-bhagavata (CB) are in the notes.*

## 3.1. Nabhadas, Bhaktamal

*Nabhadas (Narayanadas) was a disciple of Agradas of Galta (Rajasthan), of the school of Ramananda; the Bhaktamal was
composed about 1600 (in any case before the 1630s) in 214 chhappays (six-line stanzas).[^1] Text from the edition of
Hariprasad Bhagirathji (Bombay, Samvat 1970 [1914]; the text of Nabhadas and Priyadas without commentary), checked
against the Naval Kishore lithograph (Lucknow, 1883). The stanzas are given in the order of the book.*

### Chhappay 47. Vishnupuri

भागवतधर्म उतंग आनधर्म अनन देखा ॥ पीतरपटतर बिगत निकष ज्यों कुंदनरेषा ॥ कृष्णकृपा कहि बेल फलित सतसंग दिखायो ॥ कोटिग्रंथको अर्थ तेरह बिरंचनमें गायो ॥ महासमुद्र भागवत ते भक्तिरत्नराजी रची ॥ कलिजीव जंजाली कारणे, विष्णुपुरी बड़ि निधि सची ॥ ४७ ॥

**47.** He held the Bhagavata-dharma to be the highest, and on other dharmas he did not even look:[^2] as on the
touchstone the streak of pure gold bears no comparison with brass. He told of the vine of Krishna's grace and showed
its fruit — the company of saints. The meaning of millions of books he sang in thirteen "strands": from the great ocean
of the Bhagavata he composed the Bhakti-ratnavali, the "Necklace of the Jewels of Bhakti". For the souls of the
Kali-yuga, caught in snares, Vishnupuri gathered a great treasure.[^3]

### Chhappay 71. Raghunatha Gosai

शीतकाल सकलात विदित पुरुषोत्तम दीनी ॥ शौच गए हरिसंग कृत्य सेवककी कीनी ॥ जगन्नाथपद प्रीति निरंतर करत खवासी ॥ भगवतधर्मप्रधान प्रसन्न नीलाचलवासी ॥ उत्कलदेश उड़ीसा नगर, वैनतेय सबकोउ कहै ॥ श्रीरघुनाथ गुसांई गरुड़ज्यों, सिंहपौरि ठाढ़े रहै ॥ ७१ ॥

**71.** All know how in the cold season Purushottama gave [him] broadcloth. When he went to cleanse himself, Hari went
with him and did a servant's work.[^4] With unbroken love for Jagannatha's feet he rendered personal service; foremost
in the Bhagavata-dharma, a joyful dweller in Nilachala. In the land of Utkala, in the city of Orissa, all call him
Vainateya: Sri Raghunatha Gosai, like Garuda, kept standing at the Lion Gate.[^5]

### Chhappay 72. Nityananda and Krishna-Chaitanya

गौड़देश पाखंड मेंटि कियो भजनपरायण ॥ करुणासिंधु कृतज्ञ भये अगतिन गतिदायन ॥ दशधारस आक्रांत महतजनचरण उपासे ॥ नाम लेत निष्पाप दुरित तिहि नरके नासे ॥ अवतार विदित पूरव मही, उभै महत देही धरी ॥ श्रीनित्यानंद कृष्णचैतन्यकी, भक्ति दशोंदिशि विस्तरी ॥ ७२ ॥

**72.** Uprooting impiety in the land of Gauda, They turned [the people] to bhajana. Oceans of mercy, grateful [for the
least service],[^6] They became the refuge of those who had no refuge. Great souls, overwhelmed by the rasa of ten
kinds, served Their feet.[^7] Whoever takes [Their] name becomes sinless: the sins of such a man are destroyed. It is
well known: in the eastern land avataras appeared, both taking the form of great saints.[^8] The bhakti of Sri
Nityananda and Krishna-Chaitanya spread in all ten directions.[^9]

### Chhappay 75. Keshava Bhatta Kashmiri

काशमीरकी छाप पाप तापन युग मंडन ॥ दृढ़ हरिभक्ति कुठार आन धर्म विटपविहंडन ॥ मथुरामध्य मलेच्छ वाद करि बवरबट जीते ॥ काजी अजित अनेक देखि परचौ भै भीते ॥ विदित बात संसार सब, संत साखि नाहिन दुरी ॥ श्रीकेशवभट नरमुकटमणि, जिनकी प्रभुता बिस्तरी ॥ ७५ ॥

**75.** Bearing the name "Kashmiri",[^10] scorcher of sins, ornament of the age, — with the firm axe of Hari-bhakti he
felled the trees of other dharmas. In Mathura, entering into dispute with the mlecchas, he overcame the impious;[^11]
many qazis, never before defeated, seeing his wondrous power, were filled with fear. This is known to the whole world;
the saints are witnesses, it is not hidden. Sri Keshava Bhatta — the crown jewel among men, whose greatness spread
[everywhere].[^12]

### Chhappay 89. Rupa and Sanatana

गौड़देश बंगाल हुते सबही अधिकारी ॥ हय गज भवन भंडार विभव भूभुज अनुहारी ॥ यह सुख अनित विचार बास वृंदावन कीनों ॥ यथालाभ संतोष कुंजकर वामन दीनो ॥ व्रजभूमि रहस्य राधाकृष्ण, भक्ति तोष उद्धार कियो ॥ संसारस्वाद सुख वांत ज्यों, दुहुं रूप सनातन त्यागि दियो ॥ ८९ ॥

**89.** In the land of Gauda, in Bengal, both were high officials: horses, elephants, palaces, treasury — wealth like
that of kings. Considering this happiness transient, they settled in Vrindavana. Content with whatever came of itself,
they gave their minds to the kunjas and the water-pot.[^13] The secret of the land of Vraja — Radha-Krishna — they
revealed [to the world], to the joy of bhakti.[^14] The taste of worldly pleasures, like vomited food, both — Rupa and
Sanatana — gave up.[^15]

### Chhappay 93. Jiva Gosai

वेला भजन सुपक्व कषाय न कबहूं न लागी ॥ वृंदावन दृढ़ वास युगलचरणणि अनुरागी ॥ पोथीलेखन पानि अघट अक्षर चित दीनो ॥ सद्ग्रंथनको सार सबै हस्तामल कीनो ॥ संदेहग्रंथछेदनसमर्थ, रसरासउपासक परमधीर ॥ श्रीरूपसनातन भक्ति जल, श्रीजीव गुसांई सर गंभीर ॥ ९३ ॥

**93.** The bank [of this lake] — his bhajana — is firmly built, and no scum ever touched it. He dwelt steadfastly in
Vrindavana, loving the feet of the Divine Couple. In his hand was the copying of books; his mind he gave to flawless
letters. The essence of all good books he made [clear] as an amalaka fruit on the palm. Able to cut the knots of doubt;
a worshipper of the rasa of the rasa, supremely steady. The bhakti of Sri Rupa and Sanatana is the water, and Sri Jiva
Gosai the deep lake.[^16]

### Chhappay 94. Gopala Bhatta and the Vrindavana saints

सर्वसु राधारमण भट्ट गोपाल उजागर ॥ हृषीकेश भगवान विपुल विठ्ठल रससागर ॥ थानेश्वरी जगन्नाथ लोकनाथ महानि मधु श्रीरंग ॥ कृष्णदास पंडित उभै अधिकारी हरिअंग ॥ घमंडी युगलकिशोर भृत्य भूगर्भ जीव दृढ़व्रत लियो ॥ श्रीवृंदावनकी माधुरी, इन मिलि आस्वादन कियो ॥ ९४ ॥

**94.** The illustrious Gopala Bhatta, whose whole wealth is Radha-ramana; Hrishikesha, Bhagavan, Vipula Vitthala —
an ocean of rasa; Jagannatha Thaneshvari, Lokanatha, the great sage Madhu, Shriranga; Krishnadas and Pandit — both
worthy servants, close to Hari; Ghamandi, Yugalakishora-bhritya, Bhugarbha, Jiva — who took a firm vow: all of them
together tasted the sweetness of Sri Vrindavana.[^17]

### Chhappay 96. Kashishvara Avadhuta (in a list)

सोझा सींव आधार धीर हरिनाम त्रिलोचन ॥ आशाधर द्योराज नीर सधना दुखमोचन ॥ काशीश्वर अवधूत कृष्णकिंकर कटहरिया ॥ सोभू ऊदाराम नाम डुंगर व्रत धरिया ॥ पद्म पदारथ रामदास, विमलानंद अमृत स्रये ॥ भवप्रवाह निसतार हित, आवलंबन येजन भये ॥ ९६ ॥

**96.** Sojha, Siva, steady Adhara, Harinama, Trilochana, Ashadhara, Dyoraja,[^18] Sadhna — the remover of sorrows,
Kashishvara Avadhuta, Krishna-kinkara, Katahariya, Sobhu, Udarama, Dungar who took the vow [of remembering] the Name,
Padma, Padartha, Ramadas, Vimalananda — who poured forth nectar: to bring [souls] across the current of existence,
these men became a support.[^19]

### Chhappay 101. The servants of Badrinatha, Orissa and Dvaraka (Gajapati)

केशव पुनि हरिनाथ भीम खेता गोविंद ब्रह्मचारी ॥ बालकृष्ण बड़भरत अच्युत अपया व्रतधारी ॥ पंडा गोपीनाथ मुकुंद गजपत्ति महायश ॥ गुणनिधि यश गोपाल दये भक्तनिको सरवस ॥ श्रीअंग सदा सानिधि रहे, कृतपुण्यपुंज भल भाग भर ॥ बद्रीनाथ उडीसा द्वारिका, सेवक सब हरिभजनपर ॥ १०१ ॥

**101.** Keshava, and also Harinatha, Bhima, Kheta, Govinda Brahmachari, Balakrishna, Bada-Bharata, Achyuta, Apaya —
keepers of vows; the panda Gopinatha, Mukunda, the Gajapati of great renown, Gunanidhi, Yasha-Gopala — who gave the
devotees all they had. They always remained near the sacred body [of the Lord] — a mass of merit, full of good fortune.
The servants of Badrinatha, Orissa and Dvaraka — all devoted to the bhajana of Hari.[^20]

### Chhappay 160. Nath-bhat (in the 1914 edition "Raghunath-bhat")

आगम निगम पुराण सार शास्त्रजु बिचार्यो ॥ ज्यों पारो दे पुटहिं सबनिको सार उधार्यो ॥ श्रीरूप सनातन जीव भट्ट नारायण भाख्यो ॥ सो सर्वस उर सांच जतन करि नीके राख्यो ॥ फनी वंस गोपाल सुव रागाको अयन ॥ रसराशिउपाशिक भक्तराज स रघुनाथ भट, निर्मल बयन ॥ १६० ॥

**160.** He pondered the essence of the agamas, nigamas, puranas and shastras; like quicksilver calcined again and
again, he drew out the essence of them all.[^21] All that Sri Rupa, Sanatana, Jiva and Narayana Bhatta set forth he
kept carefully in his heart as a true treasure. The son of Gopala of the Phani line, an abode of raga;[^22] a worshipper
of the very essence of rasa, a king among devotees — this is Nath-bhat,[^23] pure of speech.

### Chhappay 182. The crown-jewel sannyasis

चित्सुखिटीकाकार भक्ति सर्बोपरि राखी ॥ श्रीदामोदरतीर्थ राम आर्चनबिधि भाखी ॥ चंद्रोदय हरिभक्ति नृसिंहारण्यहि कीनी ॥ माधो मधुसूदन सरस्वती परमहंसकीरति लीनी ॥ प्रबोधानंद रामभद्र, जगदानंद कलियुग धनि ॥ परमधर्म प्रतिपोषि कै संन्यासी मुकुटमनि ॥ १८२ ॥

**182.** Chitsukha the commentator placed bhakti above all; Sri Damodara Tirtha set forth the order of the worship of
Rama; the Hari-bhakti-chandrodaya was composed by Nrisimharanya; Madho and Madhusudana Sarasvati won the fame of
paramahamsas; Prabodhananda, Ramabhadra, Jagadananda — blessed in the Kali-yuga. Having nurtured the highest dharma,
[these] sannyasis are crown jewels [among saints].[^24]

## 3.2. Dhruvadas, Bhakta-namavali

*Dhruvadas was a poet of the Radhavallabha school of Vrindavana (the school of Hit Harivamsha); his works are dated
Samvat 1681–1698 (1624–1641). The Bhakta-namavali ("List of the Names of Devotees") is 116 dohas on the saints of
Vraja and other lands; dohas 17–34 are on Chaitanya, Nityananda, the Gosvamis and the Vrindavana saints. Text from the
edition of Radhakrishna Das (Kashi, Nagari-pracharini Sabha, 1901), pp. 2–3; the editor's notes were not
translated.*

गौड देस सब उद्धर्यो प्रगटे कृष्ण चैतन्य ॥ तैसेहि नित्यानन्द हू रसमय भए अनन्य ॥ १७ ॥

**17.** The whole land of Gauda was delivered: Krishna-Chaitanya appeared; and likewise Nityananda — full of rasa,
devoted to none other.[^25]

पावत हीं तिनकौ दरस उपजै भजनानन्द ॥ विनहीं स्रम छुटि जाहिं सब जे माया के फन्द ॥ १८ ॥

**18.** As soon as one obtains Their darshan, the bliss of bhajana arises, and without any labour all the nooses of
maya fall away.

रूप सनातन मन बढ़्यो राधाकृष्णअनुराग ॥ जानि विस्व नस्वर सबै तब उपज्यो वैराग ॥ १९ ॥

**19.** In the hearts of Rupa and Sanatana love for Radha-Krishna grew; knowing the whole world to be perishable,
they gained detachment.

विष समान तजि विषय सुख देस सहित परिबार ॥ वृन्दाबन कौं चले यौं ज्यौं सावन जलधार ॥ २० ॥

**20.** Casting off sense pleasures like poison, together with homeland and family, they rushed to Vrindavana like
the downpours of Shravana.

तृन तें नीचौ आपकौं जानि बसे बन मांहि ॥ मोह छांड़ि ऐसे रहे मनौं चिन्हारिहु नाहिं ॥ २१ ॥

**21.** Thinking themselves lower than grass, they settled in the forest; leaving attachments, they lived as if they
had never known anyone.[^26]

रघुनन्दन सारङ्ग जी जीवति पाछैं आप ॥ कृष्ण कृपा करि सबै आनि निज धाम बसाए ॥ २२ ॥

**22.** Raghunandana, Saranga-ji [and] Jiva[?] came afterwards; Krishna in His mercy gathered them all and settled them
in His own abode.[^27]

भजनरासि रघुनाथ जी राधाकुण्ड स्थान ॥ लोन तक्र व्रज को लयौ परस्यो नहिं कछु आन ॥ २३ ॥

**23.** Raghunatha-ji is a treasury of bhajana; his place is Radha-kunda. He took [only] the salted buttermilk of
Vraja — he touched nothing else.[^28]

बन्दन करि कै चिन्तवन गौर स्याम अभिराम ॥ सोवत हूं रसना रटै राधाकृष्ण सुनाम ॥ २४ ॥

**24.** Bowing down, he contemplated the lovely Gaura and Shyama;[^29] even in sleep his tongue repeated the holy names
of Radha-Krishna.

श्रीविलास व्रजनाथ अरु श्री चन्द मुकुन्द प्रवीन ॥ मदनमोहन पद कमल सों अधिक प्रीति जिन कीन ॥ २५ ॥

**25.** Shrivilasa, Vrajanatha, and also Shri Chand and the skilled Mukunda — who bore special love for the lotus feet
of Madana-mohana.[^30]

महापुरुष नन्दा भए करि तन सकल सिँगार ॥ सखी रूप चिन्तत फिरैं गौर स्याम सुकुवार ॥ २६ ॥

**26.** Nanda became a mahapurusha: adorning his whole body, he wandered in the form of a sakhi, contemplating the
tender Gaura and Shyama.

नैन सजल तिहि रङ्ग मैं चित पायो विस्राम ॥ बिबस बेगि ह्वै जात सुनि लाल लाड़िली नाम ॥ २७ ॥

**27.** His eyes are full of tears; in that love his mind found rest; he at once loses control of himself on hearing
the names of Lal and Ladili.[^31]

कृष्णदास हुते जङ्गली तेऊ तैसी भांति ॥ तिनके उर झलकत रहै हेम नील मनि कांति ॥ २८ ॥

**28.** Krishnadas was a forest hermit, a "jangali"[?] — he too is the same: in his heart the lustre of the golden and
the sapphire gems ever shines.[^32]

जुगल प्रेम रस अब्धि मैं परयो प्रबोध मन जाइ ॥ वृन्दाबन रस माधुरी गाई अधिक लड़ाइ ॥ २९ ॥

**29.** The mind of Prabodha plunged into the ocean of the rasa of the Divine Couple's love; the sweetness of
Vrindavana's rasa he sang with great tenderness.[^33]

अति विरक्त संसार तें बसे विपिन तजि भौन ॥ प्रीति सहित गोपाल भट सेये राधारौन ॥ ३० ॥

**30.** Utterly detached from the world, leaving his home, Gopala Bhatt settled in the forest — and lovingly served
Radha-raun.[^34]

घमँडी रस मैं घमँड़ रह्यौ वृन्दाबन निज धाम ॥ बंसीबट तट रास कै सेये स्यामा स्याम ॥ ३१ ॥

**31.** Ghamandi was ever proud of [his] rasa; Vrindavana is his abode; at Vamshivata, staging the rasa, he served
Shyama and Shyam.[^35]

भट्टनरायन अति सरस व्रज मण्डल सों हेत ॥ ठौर ठौर रचना करी प्रगट किये संकेत ॥ ३२ ॥

**32.** Bhatt Narayan — all juicy with [rasa], with love for the Vraja-mandala: in many places he established
[shrines] and revealed the signs [of the lilas].[^36]

वर्द्धमान श्रीभट्ट अरु गङ्गल व्रज वृन्दाबन आयौ ॥ करि प्रतीति सर्वोपरि जान्यो तातें चित्त लगायौ ॥ ३३ ॥

**33.** Varddhaman, Shribhatt and Gangal came to Vraja, to Vrindavana; having gained faith, they knew [it] to be the
highest and so gave [it] their hearts.[^37]

भट्ट गदाधर नाथभट विद्या भजन प्रवीन ॥ सरस कथा बानी मधुर सुनि रुचि होत नवीन ॥ ३४ ॥

**34.** Bhatt Gadadhar and Nath-bhat — skilled in learning and bhajana; one hears their juicy tales and sweet words,
and the taste [for them] is ever new.[^38]

## 3.3. Raghavdas, Bhaktamal

*Raghavdas was a saint of the Dadu-panth (the school of Dadu Dayal, Rajasthan); his Bhaktamal was completed in Samvat
1717 (1660) and largely reworks Nabhadas; the saints are arranged by sampradaya. Only Raghavdas's own text (of 1660) is
translated, from the edition of Agarchand Nahta (Jodhpur, 1965), pp. 105–108; Chaturdas's commentary (late 18th c.) and
the editor's notes were not translated.[^39] Chaitanya and the Gosvamis are placed in the section of the **Madhva
sampradaya**.*

अथ माध्वाचारिज संप्रदा ॥ रघवा प्रणवत रांमजी, मम दोषो नहीं दीयते ॥ आदि बृक्ष बिधि नमो, निगम नृमल रस छाते ॥ मध्वाचारय मधुर पीवत, अमृत रस माते ॥ तास पथित भू प्रगट, संत अरु महंत निसतरे ॥ हरि पूजैं हरि भजैं, तिनहि संग बहुत निसतरे ॥ मैं बपुरौ बरनौं कहा, जांणीं जाइ न जीय ते ॥ रघवा प्रणवत रांमजी, मम दोषो नहीं दीयते ॥ २१७ ॥

**217.** *The Madhvacharya sampradaya.* Raghava bows to Ramaji: may my faults not be counted against me.[^40] Obeisance
to Brahma, the first tree, who shades the pure rasa of the Vedas. Madhvacharya drinks [its] sweetness, intoxicated
with the rasa of nectar. Those who walk his path appeared on earth — saints and mahants — and were saved; they worship
Hari, they serve Hari — and with them many were saved. What can I, poor wretch, describe? It cannot be grasped by the
mind.

ये पांच महंत परसिध भये, ज्ञांनी गौड़ बंगाल मधि ॥ नित्यानंद श्रीकृष्ण-चैतन्य, भजि लाहो लीयो ॥ रूप सनातन रांम रटत, उमग्यौ अति हीयौ ॥ जीउ-गुसांई खीर-नीर, निति निरनौं कीयौ ॥ जै जै जै त्रिलोक ध्यांन, ध्रुव ज्यूं नहीं बीयौ ॥ राघो रीति बड़ेन की, सब जानैं बोलै न बधि ॥ ये पांच महंत परसिध भये, ज्ञांनी गौड़ बंगाल मधि ॥ २१८ ॥

**218.** These five mahants became renowned — knowers [of the Truth], in Gauda-Bengal. Nityananda [and] Sri
Krishna-Chaitanya served [the Lord] and won the [highest] gain. Rupa and Sanatana repeated [the name of] Rama — their
hearts overflowed.[^41] Jiva Gosai always separated milk from water.[^42] Glory, glory, glory! Their contemplation [is
known] in the three worlds — steadfast like Dhruva, they have no second. Ragho [says]: the way of the great all know,
[but] its measure cannot be told[?].[^43] These five mahants became renowned — knowers, in Gauda-Bengal.

उभै भ्रात कलिजुग प्रगट, भक्ति सथापन कारनै ॥ नित्यानंद बलिभद्र, कृष्णचैतन्य कृष्णघन ॥ कीयो दूरि अधर्म्म, धरम बर थप्यौ भजन-पन ॥ प्रेम रसांइन मत बड़े, जन अंघ्री सेवत ॥ जो नर लेवै नांव, ताहि उत्म गति देवत ॥ पूरब गौड़ बंगाल के, तारे जन औतार नैं ॥ उभै भ्रात कलिजुग प्रगट, भक्ति सथापन कारनैं ॥ २१९ ॥

**219.** Two brothers appeared in the Kali-yuga to establish bhakti: Nityananda — Balabhadra, Krishna-Chaitanya —
Krishna in His fullness.[^44] They drove away irreligion and established the best dharma — the vow of bhajana. Great is
the teaching of the elixir of prema;[^45] people serve Their feet. Whoever takes [Their] name, to him They give the
highest destination. The people of Purva, Gauda and Bengal were saved by [these] avataras. Two brothers appeared in
the Kali-yuga to establish bhakti.[^46]

श्री रूप सनातन तज्ञ दुहु, बिषै स्वाद कीयो बंवन ॥ पूरब गौड़ बंगाल, तहां कौ सूबौ होई ॥ बिभौ भूप परमांन, खजांनां असु गज जोई ॥ मिथा सब सुख मांनि, चालि बृन्दाबन आये ॥ प्रापति मैं संतोष कुंज, करवां मन भाये ॥ संत तोष राघो रिदै, भक्ति करी राधा-रवन ॥ श्रीरूप सनातन तज्ञ दुहु, बिषै स्वाद कीयो बंवन ॥ २२० ॥

**220.** Sri Rupa and Sanatana, both knowers, vomited out the taste of sense pleasures. In Purva, Gauda-Bengal, they
were governors.[^47] Wealth on a king's scale: treasury, horses, elephants. Deeming all [that] happiness false, they
went to Vrindavana; content with what came, they came to love the kunjas and the water-pot. The saints are pleased,
[and] in Ragho's heart [there is joy]: they served Radha-ramana.

श्रीजीव गुसांई अध्व बड़, श्री रूप सनातन भजन जल ॥ प्रेम पालि परपक्क, आंन बिधि फूटै नांहीं ॥ जुगल-रूप सूं प्रीति, बसत बृन्दाबन मांहीं ॥ अखंड अक्षर मन लग्यौ, कलम पुस्तक कर राजै ॥ सास्त्र बेद पुरांन सार, उर मधो बिराजै ॥ राघो रसिक उपासना, संसा काटन अति सबल ॥ श्रीजीव गुसांई अध्व बड़, श्री रूप सनातन भजन जल ॥ २२१ ॥

**221.** Sri Jiva Gosai is the great [reservoir],[^48] and the water is the bhajana of Sri Rupa and Sanatana. He
nurtured prema to ripeness; no other ways sprouted in him. Loving the Divine Couple, he lived in Vrindavana. His mind
was set on imperishable letters; pen and book adorn his hand; the essence of the shastras, Vedas and Puranas shines in
his heart. Ragho [says]: his upasana is [the path] of the rasikas; in cutting through doubts he is very strong.[^49]

[^1]: Nabhadas's date: the Bhaktamal is usually placed between Samvat 1642 and 1680 (1585–1623). The numbering of the
chhappays differs by 1–3 between editions: here it follows the 1914 edition; in the 1883 lithograph the chhappay on the
sannyasis is no. 183, in Rupkala's edition no. 181; the one on Nath-bhat is no. 158 in Rupkala.

[^2]: "आनधर्म अनन देखा" — literally "did not see the face of another dharma", i.e. he did not even look at other paths.

[^3]: Vishnu Puri's Bhakti-ratnavali is an anthology of verses of the Bhagavata on bhakti in 13 sections ("viracana",
"strand" of the necklace); the Gaudiyas revere it. Vishnu Puri is named in CC Adi 9 among the sannyasis who are the
roots of the tree of bhakti. Priyadas tells of his link with Mahaprabhu (kavitt 177, ch. 4.3).

[^4]: Who serves whom is ambiguous in the verse; Priyadas (kavitt 327, ch. 4.3) explains this "custom of accompanying
for cleansing" by reference to the story of Madhodas, whom Jagannatha Himself served in his illness. So the servant
here is Hari (Jagannatha). These two stories (the broadcloth, the accompanying) are not in the Gaudiya sources.

[^5]: Vainateya is Garuda, son of Vinata; Garuda's image stands before Jagannatha. The Lion Gate (Simha-dvara) is the
main entrance of Jagannatha's temple. Priyadas identifies this Raghunatha with Raghunatha Dasa Gosvami (kavitts
327–328). Cf. CC Antya 6: in Puri Raghunatha Dasa stood at the Simha-dvara begging alms.

[^6]: "कृतज्ञ" — "mindful of good done, grateful" (literally "knowing what was done").

[^7]: "दशधा रस" — "rasa of ten kinds": probably the nine kinds of bhakti and prema (the tenth), as often in Braj
poetry. Another reading is possible: "overwhelmed by the rasa of ten kinds, They served the feet of the great" (of the
humility of the Lords Themselves).

[^8]: "महत देही" — "the bodies of mahants, great saints". Cf. CC Adi 1.14: Krishna in five aspects, who has taken the
form of a devotee. According to Gaudiya teaching (Srila Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati), Sri Chaitanya is Krishna Himself in
the mood and lustre of Radha, and Nityananda is Balarama (CC Adi 5); Nabhadas calls Them avataras who appeared as
saints.

[^9]: In the 1883 lithograph the stanza opens with this line (the refrain) and closes with it; in the 1914 edition the
refrain is given once, at the end. Nabhadas writes "Nityananda and Krishna-Chaitanya" in one chhappay, as of two equally
great. This is the earliest known mention of Mahaprabhu from outside Bengal (c. 1600).

[^10]: "छाप" — "seal, signature", i.e. a by-name; "Kashmiri" — from Kashmir. Of Keshava Kashmiri and Mahaprabhu
Nabhadas says nothing; the story of their meeting in Nadia was added by Priyadas (ch. 4.2).

[^11]: "बवरबट" (Rupkala "बरबट") is a rare word, by context "the impious, the violent". The story of Keshava's victory
over the Muslims at Vishram-ghat in Mathura is in Priyadas (kavitt 337; not translated).

[^12]: According to CC Adi 16 and CB Adi 13, this digvijayi pandit was defeated by the young Nimai in Navadvipa and
humbled. The Nimbarka tradition regards Keshava Kashmiri as its acharya, author of the Kaustubha-prabha, and is silent
about the meeting.

[^13]: "कुंजकर वामन दीनो" in the 1914 edition is apparently a corruption; Rupkala has "कुंज करवा मन दीन्हो" — "gave
their minds to the kunjas and the water-pot [karva]"; so too Raghavdas (ch. 220: "कुंज, करवां मन भाये"). Translated by
this reading.

[^14]: "भक्ति तोष" (Rupkala "भक्त तोष", "to the joy of the devotees"). Cf. CC Madhya 19, 23: Mahaprabhu charges Rupa and
Sanatana with finding the lost places of the lilas of Vraja and writing books on bhakti. Nabhadas does not name
Mahaprabhu; Priyadas (kavitt 358) does.

[^15]: According to CC (Madhya 1, 19, 20), Rupa and Sanatana were ministers of the sultan of Gauda, Husain Shah (Dabir
Khas and Sakara Mallik), who left his service for the Lord.

[^16]: The image: the bhakti of Rupa and Sanatana is the water, Jiva is the deep lake, and its "bank" ("वेला", ghat) is
his bhajana, mature and firm; "kashaya" is scum, murk. "Hastamala" — "an amalaka on the palm", i.e. full clarity.
"रसरास उपासक" — "worshipper of the rasa of the rasa" or "of the essence of rasa". Jiva Gosvami was the nephew of Rupa
and Sanatana, author of the Shat-sandarbha; Nabhadas especially notes his copying of books and his learning.

[^17]: The 1914 edition's "महानि" is probably a misprint for "महामुनि", "the great sage". Of those named, the following
belong to Mahaprabhu's circle: Gopala Bhatta Gosvami (the Deity Radha-ramana), Lokanatha Gosvami, Madhu Pandit (the
Deity Gopinatha), Krishnadas Brahmachari (servant of Madana-mohana), Krishnadas Pandit (servant of Govinda), Bhugarbha —
according to Priyadas's sections on this chhappay (Krishnadas and Pandit are two different persons: "Krishnadas
[Brahmachari] and [Krishnadas] Pandit"), Jagannatha Thaneshvari (in Priyadas a disciple of Mahaprabhu, kavitt 378), and
Jiva Gosvami. Bhagavan (Ali Bhagavan), Vipula Vitthala (a disciple of Svami Haridas), Ghamandi (founder of the rasa-lila
performances of Vraja) and others belong to other schools of Vrindavana.

[^18]: Names as in the 1914 edition; Rupkala's readings differ ("सीवां", "योराजनीर" and others). The names are rendered
tentatively.

[^19]: A list of saints without stories. Kashishvara, according to CC (Adi 10; Madhya 10), was a disciple of Ishvara Puri
and a servant of Mahaprabhu at Puri; according to Priyadas (kavitt 398, ch. 4.3), at Mahaprabhu's command he went to
Vrindavana to serve Govinda. "Avadhuta" in Nabhadas is probably a by-name ("Kashishvara Avadhuta"); in Rupkala it is a
separate name.

[^20]: A list of the servants of three holy places. "Gajapati" is the title of the kings of Orissa; Priyadas (kavitt 409,
ch. 4.3) understands Prataparudra here and tells of Mahaprabhu's mercy to him. "The panda Gopinatha" and "Govinda
Brahmachari" are temple servants; identification with persons in CC is not established.

[^21]: "ज्यों पारो दे पुटहिं" — "like quicksilver subjected to puta (repeated calcination with herbs)" — an image of
alchemical extraction of the essence.

[^22]: Rupkala reads "रागानुगा को अयन", "an abode of raganuga" (the bhakti that follows the residents of Vraja); the 1914
edition has "रागा को अयन".

[^23]: The 1914 edition reads "रघुनाथ भट" ("Raghunath-bhat"); Rupkala has "नाथभट", "Nath-bhat", as do Dhruvadas
(doha 34) and Raghavdas (ch. 222: "श्रीनाथ भट"). Translated by this reading. He is a descendant of Narayana Bhatta (of
the Phani line, of Unchagaon in Vraja), not Raghunatha Bhatta Gosvami of the six Gosvamis.

[^24]: Madhusudana Sarasvati was an Advaitin sannyasi, author of the Advaita-siddhi and the Bhagavad-bhakti-rasayana;
sixteenth- and seventeenth-century sources say nothing of a meeting between him and Mahaprabhu (see
`sources/external/README.md`). Prabodhananda, according to Priyadas (kavitt 612, ch. 4.3), was "a dear associate of Sri
Chaitanya-chandra"; the Gaudiyas revere him as the author of the Sri Chaitanya-chandramrita; the Radhavallabhis regard
him as a disciple of Hit Harivamsha. Nabhadas places both in one row of sannyasi-devotees.

[^25]: "Ananya" — devoted to the one Lord alone (a term especially prized by the Radhavallabhis).

[^26]: Cf. Mahaprabhu's Shikshashtaka (verse 3): "humbler than a blade of grass". "चिन्हारि" — "acquaintance".

[^27]: The reading "जीवति" is unclear: either "Jiva" (Jiva Gosvami came to Vrindavana after Rupa and Sanatana) or "while
living". Raghunandana and Saranga are not identified.

[^28]: "लोन तक्र" — "salted buttermilk". Cf. CC Adi 10 and Antya 6: Raghunatha Dasa's austerity in eating; in Vraja he
lived at Radha-kunda and drank only a little buttermilk a day.

[^29]: "गौर स्याम" in Braj poetry is usually Radha (golden) and Krishna (dark), as also in doha 26; Dhruvadas
apparently speaks of Radha-Krishna.

[^30]: Madana-mohana is the Deity of Sanatana Gosvami in Vrindavana. The names (Shrivilasa, Vrajanatha, Shri Chand,
Mukunda) are as in the edition; identification with Gaudiya devotees is not established.

[^31]: "Lal" and "Ladili" are endearing names of Krishna and Radha.

[^32]: Gold and sapphire are the colours of Radha and Krishna. Which Krishnadas this is (Kaviraja? Brahmachari?) cannot be
determined from the text.

[^33]: "Prabodh" is Prabodhananda Sarasvati (see note 24); the Vrindavana-mahimamrita is his poem on the rasa of
Vrindavana.

[^34]: "Radha-raun" is Radha-ramana, the Deity of Gopala Bhatta Gosvami.

[^35]: A play on words, "घमँडी… घमँड़": Ghamandi ("the proud") was "proud" of rasa. Vamshivata is the place of the rasa
in Vrindavana.

[^36]: Narayana Bhatta, a southerner, lived in Vraja from the mid-sixteenth century; according to Priyadas (kavitt 381),
he became a disciple of Krishnadas Brahmachari of the Gosvamis' circle; he is known for the Vraja-bhakti-vilasa,
describing the places of the lilas.

[^37]: Shribhatt is a Nimbarki poet (the Yugala-shataka); Gangal is Gangal Bhatt, according to Hariram Vyas "the servant
of Rupa and Sanatana". "It" is by sense Vrindavana.

[^38]: Gadadhara Bhatta is a sixteenth-century Vrindavana poet; for Nath-bhat see note 23.

[^39]: Chaturdas's commentary (kavitts on ch. 219) reworks Priyadas's kavitts on Nityananda and Chaitanya (ch. 4.1).
Further in the same section Raghavdas has chhappays on Nath-bhat (222), Narayana Bhatta (223–224), Raghunatha Gosai
(228), and lists with Gopala Bhatta, Lokanatha, Kashishvara and the sannyasis with Prabodhananda — reworkings of the
corresponding chhappays of Nabhadas (3.1); they are not translated here.

[^40]: "दीयते" — "may [they] not be given (imputed)"; the refrain of the stanza.

[^41]: "Ram" for the Dadu-panthis is the name of the Lord in general (cf. Kabir, Dadu); the Gaudiya sources speak of
the repetition of the names of Krishna.

[^42]: The image of the hamsa that can separate milk from water — discrimination of the essential from the
inessential; i.e. Jiva is a master of siddhanta.

[^43]: "बोलै न बधि" — the meaning is unclear; translated tentatively.

[^44]: "कृष्णघन" — "Krishna-ghana": "concentrated, full Krishna" or "Krishna dark as a cloud". Raghavdas directly calls
Chaitanya Krishna and Nityananda Balarama, as the Gaudiyas do (CC Adi 5).

[^45]: "Rasayana" — elixir; "prema-rasayana" — the elixir of love of God.

[^46]: The stanza reworks Nabhadas's ch. 72. Raghavdas's placing of Chaitanya and the Gosvamis in the **Madhva
sampradaya** (1660) is one of the early testimonies to this succession from outside Bengal, long before Baladeva
Vidyabhushana (1720s). According to Gaudiya tradition (CC Adi 9; Gaura-ganoddesha-dipika), Mahaprabhu received
initiation from Ishvara Puri — in the line of Madhavendra Puri, going back to Madhvacharya.

[^47]: "सूबौ" — "suba", a governor of a province. According to CC they were ministers of the sultan of Gauda.

[^48]: "अध्व" means "road"; by sense and by Nabhadas ("सर गंभीर", "a deep lake", ch. 93) one would expect "reservoir";
perhaps a corruption ("अब्धि", "ocean"?). Translated by sense.

[^49]: "संसा" — "doubt" (Sanskrit samshaya). The stanza reworks Nabhadas's ch. 93.
